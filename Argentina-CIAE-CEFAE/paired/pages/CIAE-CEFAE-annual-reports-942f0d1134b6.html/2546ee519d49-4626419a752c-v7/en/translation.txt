The Aerospace Identification Center was created on April 4, 2019 as a result of a restructuring of the Aerospace Study Commission (CEFAE) established on May 6, 2011, elevating its organizational level and readjusting its capabilities to fulfill the new task: "Organize, coordinate, and carry out the research and analysis of events, activities, or elements present or originating in the aerospace domain of interest; identify their causes and report the conclusions to the relevant agencies."
"It requires".

The CIAE is composed of the same personnel from the original CEFAE, including its internal and external advisors, and will continue, as a secondary task, to provide the public service of resolving sighting cases and its corresponding annual publication.

In order to reach certain and precise conclusions, in addition to evidence (material remains, photos or videos), it is fundamental for the investigation to have a truthful testimony, which describes with the greatest certainty the observed phenomenon.
If you possess information about a documented sighting through digital photos or videos, complete the form and send it to [email protected]
