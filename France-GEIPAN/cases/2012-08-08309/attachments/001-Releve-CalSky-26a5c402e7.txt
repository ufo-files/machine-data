          The Calendar-Sky
          The astronomical calendar contains thousands of events per day for every point on Earth. We know that you only
          care for a very few of these events and hence we let you personalize your own Astro-Calendar. You may primarily do
          so by switching to your appropriate user level, and by selecting some of the three dozens categories.

          In parentheses are forced limits for the maximum calculation interval. The celestial calendar is to be found further
          below on this page and will appear within some seconds after pressing the Go!-Button (depending on the complexity
          of your selections). The calendar is created especially for you. The higher your user level, the more complex objects
          you selected, the longer it does take to calculate. Please do not press the reload-button; the calculations will take
          significantly longer.
