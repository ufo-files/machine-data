Dear Sir
This is to bring to your attention the following report, quoted
hereunder, submitted by the crew of KMC714 of 6th June 1983,
related to the abovementioned subject.
QUOTE:
AT 2055 GMT, WHILST PASSING OVER NICE, A VERY UNUSUAL SIGHT WAS
SEEN PASSING FROM RIGHT TO LEFT OF OUR TRACK, ON A NORTH
EASTERLY HEADING ABOVE US. (OUR TRACK APPROX 130°M). THE OBJECT
SEEMED TO BE OF CYLINDRICAL FORM WITH A REFLECTIVE SURFACE. TWO
AND PERHAPS THREE BRIGHT LIGHTS WERE LOCATED DOWN THE SIDE.
THERE WAS A VERY BRIGHT STREAM BEHIND THE OBJECT ABOUT TWICE AS
LONG AS THE OBJECT ITSELF, LOOKING AS THOUGH A SEARCHLIGHT WAS
BEING PLAYED ONTO AN AIRCRAFT'S CONDENSATION TRAIL.
THE OBJECT PASSED AT THE SPEED ONE WOULD EXPECT ANOTHER JETLINER
TO PASS AT SOME 4/5000 FEET ABOVE. THE TRAIL IT LEFT IN THE
DARK (BUT STARRY) SKY WAS STILL VISIBLE 10 MINUTES LATER AS WE
PASSED UNDER THE TRAIL, SHOWING THAT ITS ALTITUDE MUST HAVE BEEN
MUCH HIGHER THAN WE THOUGHT. THE ANGLE AT WHICH WE SAW IT PASS
AHEAD WAS ABOUT 25º - 30º ABOVE THE HORIZONTAL.
THE INFORMATION WAS PASSED TO FRANCE CONTROL ON 135. 4MC/S AND
CONFIRMED BY ANOTHER AIRCRAFT SHORTLY AFTERWARDS. I INFORMED
FRANCE CONTROL THAT I WOULD FILE AN UNIDENTIFIED OBJECT REPORT
ON ARRIVAL AT MALTA. FRANCE REPORTED AT 21.07 THAT A SIGHTING
HAD BEEN MADE FROM THE GROUND, BUT THAT HE HAD SEEN NOTHING ON
HIS RADAR.
D. N. A.
ARRIVCE
