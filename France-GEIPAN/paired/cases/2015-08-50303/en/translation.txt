**Description:**

From his room in Avolsheim (67), the witness observed during the night of Saturday, August 1st to Sunday, August 2nd, 2015, around 2:25 am, 10 to 20 white "balls" that shimmered and "moved very slightly on themselves" in a clear sky. The general positioning of the elements relative to each other also changed while remaining grouped. The observation was made with the naked eye and also with binoculars. Enlarged in binoculars, the balls resembled blinking lights. Their light was white and bright. The witness ended his observation around 3:00 am to go to sleep. The witness reported his observation on September 18, 2016. No other testimonies were received. The consistency* of the case is low, with a single witness and no photos or videos (see the investigation report).

*According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. Based on the information provided by the witness, the analysis points towards the hypothesis of a group of LED balloons, relying on the following elements:

- Punctual shape with the naked eye and spherical with binoculars, the presence of a halo, likely the envelope of the balloons,
- White color, common for LED balloons. Blinking is also possible for some models,
- Weather conditions were entirely favorable for a balloon release: no or little wind, no clouds,
- The period was also suitable: a summer night, on the weekend,
- Weak and probably variable wind, including at altitude, explains why the PAN appeared stationary throughout the observation (approximately 35 minutes), without preventing them from being swayed and changing position relative to each other, while remaining generally in the same space.

The case is classified as "B": probable observation of light balloons equipped with LEDs.
