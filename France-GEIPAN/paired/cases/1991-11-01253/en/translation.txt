Summary: Likely observations of a major atmospheric reentry event.
--- seg-99c4a7f01af2d904a65b8ecd [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 28, 1991, around 10:30 PM, at least 25 witnesses across the Atlantic coast and southwest France observed a very bright, green ball traveling at high speed without sound for approximately ten seconds. According to some accounts, the ball disintegrated into a spray of sparks. The general direction inferred from the testimonies appears to be southeast to northwest. It is highly likely that this was the atmospheric reentry of a space debris fragment.
