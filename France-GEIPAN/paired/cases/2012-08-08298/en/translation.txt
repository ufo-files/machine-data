Description: On August 26, 2012, around 0:40 a.m., a witness observed and photographed a luminous phenomenon in the sky that intrigued them: several dozen red objects, not flashing, moved silently along a north-south trajectory. All followed each other at a regular distance and at a constant speed. The observation lasted approximately 5 minutes. The witness's description immediately suggested Thai lanterns: horizontal passage of red balls, in groups, without noise. The wind was blowing from the north at 11 p.m., then from the northeast at midnight, which is consistent with the observed north-south movement. The GEIPAN classified this case as "B," a probable observation of the passage of Thai lanterns.
