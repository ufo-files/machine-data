Summary: Observations of the silent movement of several yellow-orange lights in the night sky: very probable observations of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-97818adafcf11bfb3de05bd0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 8, 2015, between 11:00 PM and midnight, two witnesses observed in the sky the silent movement of several bright, yellow-orange lights that followed each other and disappeared into the altitude. The observation of orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, clearly resembles Thai lanterns, probably released nearby after a private party. The movement is observed from south to north, which is consistent with the light southeast wind recorded at Rodez, 15 km away: Meteorology. The area being very hilly, air currents at low altitude follow the valleys more than the dominant wind. The gendarmerie investigation specifies that: - The detection and control center of Saint-Mars-La-Pile (37), responsible for tracking radar aircraft paths, reported no anomalies in the affected area on the day of the testimony. - The same search, conducted at the Military Operational Surveillance Center of Space Objects (COSMOS), of the Air Force, found no trace of any phenomenon in the observed data. One of the witnesses well describes the successive and regular ascent of this series of hot air balloons. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": very probable observation of Thai lanterns.
