--- seg-630dd56fb86036e391290849 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 30, 2013, at 10:33 a.m., a witness in the right rear seat of a car noticed a white and gray triangle in the sky through the side window. He took a photograph with his smartphone a few seconds later as the car passed a row of trees along the road. Only one testimony was collected. The strangeness of this case is low. The hypothesis of an optical reflection phenomenon was raised from the beginning of the investigation and it was shown to be probable (see the investigation report). The testimony is moderately consistent, with relatively little data provided by the witness and exploitable, but this may be due to both the brevity of the observation and the witness's young age. However, there is a photograph that could be exploited and supports the hypothesis with relative certainty. This case is to be classified as "A," as an optical reflection phenomenon in a car window.
