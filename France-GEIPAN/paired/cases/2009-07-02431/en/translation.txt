Description: On the night of July 4 to 5, 2009, several people observed, from a garden, lights in the sky. These lights moved slowly and silently in a V formation. They suddenly disappeared. A gendarmerie investigation determined that these lights were from Chinese lanterns (approximately fifty) released for a wedding from a nearby golf course.
