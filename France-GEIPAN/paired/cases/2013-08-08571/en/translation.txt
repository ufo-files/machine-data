Summary: Observation of unusual light movements on the horizon: observation of flight AT514 during landing phase.
--- seg-e827a2e4bed8a901bfa7feda [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 5, 2013, around 11:50 PM, several witnesses were intrigued by the silent north-south movements of five bright points of light on the horizon above the sea. Following the civil aviation investigation, only one hypothesis was considered: the passage of a long-haul commercial aircraft, the Paris-Bastia flight of AIR CORSICA, flight 514 AT. This plane had a very significant delay as it was supposed to land at 23:00, but it landed exactly at 00:02 at BASTIA-PORETTA airport, south of BASTIA (therefore passing over BASTIA between 23:40 and 23:55). The 5 white lights seen by the witnesses could be the position lights and wing lights of this Airbus A320, which lights moved in their position as the plane turned and began its descent to align with the landing strip. It may be possible that there was a landing light illumination during approach to the airport, which could have altered the witnesses' perception of the "geometry" of the lights. If the observed phenomenon was not the AT 514 flight, it would have caught the attention of the airport control tower, as AT 514 was the last flight approaching Bastia-Poretta Airport. The air transport gendarmerie noticed nothing unusual and reported nothing. No articles appeared in the local press in the following days. We were unable to find any other witnesses to this phenomenon. In conclusion, the GEIPAN classifies this case as 'A': observation of the AT514 flight during landing phase. The witness was surprised by the quite unusual observation conditions, on the roof of a building, a few kilometers from the plane's landing trajectory.
