Description: On March 25, 1976, around 2:30 PM, a motorist and his passengers observed in the sky a bright object moving from east to west. Of large size and oval shape, topped with a dome, it remained in their visual field for one minute before disappearing suddenly. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. This case is classified as D1 (see investigation report).
