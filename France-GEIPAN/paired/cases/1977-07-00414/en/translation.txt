Description: The gendarmerie reports letters describing the observation of a luminous phenomenon observed on July 2, 1977 by two individuals. A couple observed between 7:30 PM and 8:20 PM two yellow-orange lights, first stationary, then moving across the sky. One of the two lights disappeared instantly, and the witnesses left their observation post before the disappearance of the second light. The witnesses never responded to the gendarmerie's summons, and no other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, leaving us with a lack of information.
