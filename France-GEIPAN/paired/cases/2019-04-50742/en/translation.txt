Description: At 23:00 on March 28, 2019, a witness observed in a clear sky a bright red rectangle followed by several orange disks forming a pyramid-like structure. This structure rose and disappeared into the clouds, leaving for a few seconds traces resembling "snow spotted with small pink dots." Only one testimony was collected.

Due to its high strangeness and weak consistency, this case is deemed unexploitable due to a lack of data (see investigation report 2024).

Indeed, although the case presents a notable strangeness, the witness provided meteorological and astronomical details in their questionnaire that do not correspond to the conditions verified on the date of observation.

The witness, in an email dated 11/05/2024, asserts they are certain of the observation date.

Since the witness is unique, it is impossible to further verify the data.

According to the GEIPAN methodology, the higher the strangeness, the more consistent the case should be.

*Consistency, according to GEIPAN criteria, refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.

The GEIPAN cannot reclassify this case, which remains in 'C', unexplained due to a lack of information.
