--- seg-eed889a5fa8b32f6e0f767e5 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 12, 2010, between 2:00 and 2:30 PM, several people gathered around a barbecue noticed in the sky a black, triangular object at a high altitude. The object appeared stationary, then took an ascending trajectory before disappearing behind a cloud. There was a north/northwestern wind that day. The witness was observing in the north/northwestern direction, which is into the wind: therefore, an ascending balloon following the wind would appear to move very little to the observer. The triangular shape of the object suggests a tetrahedral balloon, black because seen in backlighting by the sun at the zenith. This balloon could be a weather balloon launched from Blagnac or Meteo-France, or a toy balloon released from the city center (many end-of-year celebrations were taking place in Toulouse that day). The object's movement, color, and shape are consistent with a hydrogen or helium-filled balloon carried by the wind. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of a balloon.
