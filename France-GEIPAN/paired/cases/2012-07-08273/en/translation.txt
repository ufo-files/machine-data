--- seg-d0b744e44bbc296cd03fe6b2 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 27, 2012, at 8:23 a.m., a witness observed in the sky the silent, constant-speed movement of a large, dark-colored object. The investigation shows that the object's speed and direction of travel could be consistent with wind conditions at the time and day of the observation. Furthermore, no other testimonies, despite the object flying over a densely populated area during a "peak" hour, were collected. The object observed by the witness is likely just a wind-carried debris. This testimony is of good consistency: precise, but from a single witness without a photo. The observation is moderately strange. The observed object has some dynamic characteristics consistent with those of a passive object carried by the wind; it could be an object with a mundane behavior. This case is classified as "B" as a probable observation of a wind-carried flying debris.
