Description: On November 19, 2023, around 9:35 PM, a witness, accompanied by their children, observed from their home white strobe-like lights. These lights appeared to be reflecting off a cloud layer extending along Onnaing's main street (59). The phenomenon, stationary throughout the observation, persisted for approximately two to three minutes before suddenly disappearing due to extinction. The witness was able to record two video sequences of the event, providing visual evidence that adds consistency and interest to the case. The case's consistency is good*, despite only one official testimony being recorded, three distinct witnesses, and two videos corroborate the observation of PAN, enhancing the case's credibility. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The investigation determined that the observed lights had all the characteristics of terrestrial light sources reflecting off a low cloud layer (see investigation report). Furthermore, analysis identified the probable source of these lights: lighting from the autumn fair installed on Mons' Grand-Place (Belgium), located in the observation axis, about 27 km from the Onnaing viewpoint. It was also confirmed that some rides at this fair were equipped with powerful lighting devices capable of producing a strobe-like effect. The weather conditions recorded at the time of the incident—particularly very good horizontal visibility and the presence of low clouds—are compatible with the appearance of light reflections visible from Onnaing (59). The case is classified as 'A', observation of reflections on clouds from ride headlights at Mons' Autumn Fair.
