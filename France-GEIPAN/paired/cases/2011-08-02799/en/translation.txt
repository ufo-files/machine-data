Summary: Observation of a luminous phenomenon in the sky: aircraft approaching Cannes Airport.
--- seg-d3726153a76bc1d5070ffddd [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 1, 2011, around 12:25, a driver and their passenger noticed a bright object in the sky. The object appeared stationary before suddenly disappearing. The phenomenon was observed for 4 to 5 seconds. Temporary bright points observed during the day are usually explained by flashes from Iridium satellites or by airplanes approaching head-on. In this case, the Air Navigation Service reported a Falcon 900 approaching the Cannes airport, exactly where the witness saw it and at the stated time. It is possible that the observed light, in addition to the plane's projector, could also be a reflection of the sun off the plane. When the plane turned to align with the runway, the witness was no longer in the path of the projectors and the light appeared to disappear. When a bright point disappears from the sky (as in the case of satellites passing into the Earth's shadow), witnesses often interpret its disappearance as a sudden departure. Given the identification of the plane as the source of the luminous phenomenon, this case is classified "A": perfectly identified.
