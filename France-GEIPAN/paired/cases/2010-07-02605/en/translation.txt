Description: On July 4, 2010, a witness filmed a beach from his vehicle parked facing the sea. The next day, he discovered details in the footage that he had not noticed during the previous day's filming. The witness does not wish for his footage to be made public. The video and photos submitted contain many standard details (poor quality video taken with a mobile phone partially through a car windshield facing the sun, resulting in multiple reflections and artifacts, very blurry dark spot that could classically be due to an insect near the lens, various post-combustion traces of planes...). Furthermore, the witness saw nothing in person and does not describe any particular phenomenon to analyze, but rather several 'assumed' phenomena and trajectories a posteriori. These 'observations' lacking any degree of strangeness are classified C as they are unusable.
