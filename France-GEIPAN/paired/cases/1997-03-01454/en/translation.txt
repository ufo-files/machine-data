Summary: Numerous very short observations of the movement of a colored light phenomenon in the morning sky: likely atmospheric reentry observations.
--- seg-97ca99130d3b1668bf979a57 [needs-review] ---
Description: Following the publication in regional press on March 18, 1997, of various reports of an unidentified aerial phenomenon (UAP) observed on March 17, 1997, a gendarmerie investigation is requested. Witnesses in several departments in southern France, as well as several air crews and a pilot performing training flights over the Mediterranean, reported observing, early in the morning, a very fast passing of a luminous phenomenon with a (green) color in the sky. The indicated trajectory was descending towards the Pyrenees. Various gendarmerie stations took statements from the witnesses. The descriptions from the witnesses correspond to a probable observation of a meteoroid atmospheric re-entry in the morning of March 17, 1997. Note that on the same day, but in the evening, other observations were reported to the gendarmerie: each corresponds to a different observation case (available on the GEIPAN site for this same date).
