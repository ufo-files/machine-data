Description: On the evening of April 23, 2023, a resident (T1) of OFFRANVILLE (76) is in their garden observing a aurora borealis. The sky is clear, he is taking pictures. Clouds form, the witness then spots a PAN in the sky. At first, he doesn't pay attention. Simultaneously, at his home, the witness's son (T2) also spots the PAN and takes photos of it, which he sends to his father via his mobile phone. Surprised T1, the initial witness in turn takes pictures of the PAN. Only one testimony will be collected on this phenomenon. Of very low strangeness and very good consistency* (4 witnesses, but only one report to GEIPAN, illustrated by numerous photos), this case turns out to be a misperception with the shadow of the church bell tower projected onto low clouds and formed by a spotlight located at the base of the building (see the investigation report). *According to GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony. The visual aspect of the PAN corresponds perfectly with the shape of the twisted bell tower of Offranville (76), recognizable in particular thanks to the small bells present on one side, corresponding to the dark structures of the PAN visible on one of the photographs taken by the witnesses. The immobility of PAN, its significant angular height, and the alignment between the spotlight, the bell tower, and the witness strengthen the explanatory hypothesis. In addition, the identification of the bell tower was later confirmed by the witness himself, who stated that he had seen the PAN several times since the observation. The case is classified A, observation of the shadow of the church tower projected onto low clouds, formed by a spotlight located at the base of the building.
