Description: A witness observed around 11 PM a bright light rising from the ground into the sky, then flying west at high speed. A second phenomenon appeared a few minutes later and followed the same trajectory. The observation of bright yellow lights on a Saturday night, moving successively with the wind (light east wind, recorded in Blagnac: weather) suggests Thai lanterns released after a festival. Given the relatively low strangeness of this observation case, the GEIPAN did not require an on-site investigation. The GEIPAN classified this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
