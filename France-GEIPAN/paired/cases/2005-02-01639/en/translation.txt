Summary: Reports of a probable atmospheric reentry by aircraft pilots and ground witnesses.
--- seg-866c105c610dae0b6c5203c7 [needs-review] ---
Description: Several observation reports were filed on February 18, 2005, with the CRNA/East. Pilots described the very fast passage at approximately 22:23 of a very bright, blue-white colored phenomenon in the sky. The described trajectory was descending. Other ground witnesses also reported observing the very fast passage of a luminous phenomenon. A few days later, the press reported that many people in the northeast departments of France observed the fall of a small meteorite.
