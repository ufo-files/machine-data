Description: On May 24, 2012, at 11:55 PM, two witnesses, located in the Dieppe port area (76), observed the movement of a luminous flying object at low altitude in the clear, starry sky. The object was elongated and white in color. It passed in front of the castle, then headed over the beach and slowly towards the sea and out to sea. The trajectory was straight. The observation lasted a total of 30 minutes without any particular noise being noticed. Only one testimony was collected. Based on the objective elements gathered (see the investigation report), namely:

- An object slowly flying at very low altitude over the Dieppe agglomeration.
- No wind, or weak and contrary to the object's direction of movement.
- Some factual elements suggest a possible drone.
- The hypothesis of a helicopter was considered but could not be confirmed.

We can conclude that the PAN observed by the witnesses is not explicable with the available elements, but presents characteristics compatible with the plausible hypotheses considered. This testimony is of average consistency: precise but little detailed and without a photo, from a single witness. One may regret the absence of testimony from witness #2, and especially the absence of response to a request for an interview. The observation is strange as it involves an object with characteristics that do not fully conform to known flying objects, but also does not present extraordinary flight characteristics. Due to a lack of information to support either hypothesis, notably the absence of the second testimony and the witness's hearing, the GEIPAN classifies this case as 'C' due to a lack of information.
