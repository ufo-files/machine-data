Description: On January 12, 1989, a woman driving her car observed a "huge" cylindrical shape approximately 30 meters above the marshland. At the top was a dome emitting yellow light, and a series of red and white glowing portholes at its base. As it passed overhead, the object went dark and reignited a few seconds later much higher in the sky. The observation lasted only a few seconds in total. Investigations were unable to gather any additional information or identify the phenomenon.
