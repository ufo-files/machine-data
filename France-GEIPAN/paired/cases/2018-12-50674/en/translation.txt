Summary: Very brief observation of the passage of a luminous phenomenon in the sky: observation of a bolide.
--- seg-99253f89b7883234558c7627 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 6, 2018, at approximately 8:20 PM, the driver of a car, in the company of his wife, observed the very rapid passage from southwest to east of a luminous phenomenon. The witness reported the presence in the sky of a very powerful blinking white light at an angle of 15° or 20° from the horizon. The witness also noticed a much weaker red light (laser-like) constantly present above the blinking. He had the impression that there was a large dark mass (like a large carrier) surrounding these two points. His wife did not see the red light. The observation by the witnesses lasted between 3 and 5 seconds. No noise was heard (even with the window open). No other testimony was collected. This case has an average consistency: 2 witnesses, but a unique testimony given to the gendarmerie, no Technical Questionnaire, no photos, nor videos. The description of the PAN is quite evocative of a meteor, since the PAN was very fast and bright, and the observation lasted only a few seconds. The blinking described by the witness resembles the jerks of flashes of a meteor when it fragments. At the exact time indicated by the main witness, a very bright meteor was observed in the west direction by 7 observers located in southern Germany, Austria, Slovenia, and Italy, as indicated on the International Meteor Organization website: the movement of this meteor was from southwest to northeast or from west to east, which is consistent with what the witness indicates (movement of the PAN from southwest to east). The angular height corresponds well to what the witness described. On the IMO website, the 7 witnesses of the passage of this meteor indicate that it was green, light blue, or yellow, which does not correspond to the colors indicated by the witness, but we know that these colors can be perceived differently by different witnesses (10 different witnesses could describe 10 different colors) and it is possible that this meteor generated several different colors during its entry into the atmosphere. The case is classified A, observation of a meteor.
