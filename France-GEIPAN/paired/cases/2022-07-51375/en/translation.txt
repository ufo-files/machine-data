Description: On July 16, 2022, at 01:45, a witness, present on rural land in Frambouhans (25) during a music festival, observed for several minutes a white glowing point, similar to a star, moving along a straight trajectory at a constant speed. Initially detected in the northwest sector, the phenomenon continued its course towards the east or northeast, covering an estimated angular portion of 90 to 100 degrees of the sky, at an elevation between 45° and 60°, before disappearing from visual range. This case has average consistency, with only one official witness report to the GEIPAN and two available photographs in the form of copies, nevertheless deemed exploitable for analysis.

According to GEIPAN criteria, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The analysis concluded that the witness observed the International Space Station (ISS). Indeed, the appearance and behavior of the PAN are very similar to those of the ISS (see investigation report):

- Point-like shape
- White color
- Straight trajectory
- Movement from northwest to east or northeast, very close to that of the ISS moving from northwest-west to east.
- Initial and final elevations of the PAN are within the order of magnitude indicated by the witness for the PAN.
- The observation time, 01:45, is the beginning of the ISS's visibility, with a slight offset compared to its passage according to the elevation mentioned by the witness.
- The observation duration of the PAN, of several minutes, is fully compatible with the total visibility duration of the ISS, which is 6 minutes 35 seconds.

The witness also indicated that they initially thought it was a satellite, while noting an oddity related to the appearance of the PAN in the photographs. Image analysis demonstrated that the distortions and colorations observed resulted from classic effects of streaking and defocusing, typical of shots taken under these conditions. These are well-known photographic artifacts. The GEIPAN classifies this observation as 'A': ISS observation and photographic artifacts.
