Description: On September 12, 2021, at 01:15, two witnesses observed from their home the passage from north to southeast of a dozen "orange points." The PAN "all dim to about the same place." A photo is taken. Only one witness statement will be collected for this phenomenon. The witness description points to an observation of Thai lanterns, specifically:

- **Appearance:** The description of points, orange color,
- **Trajectory consistent with the direction and wind speed (see weather document) reported by stations in Bouguenais, 9km from the witness, and Saint-Philibert-de-Grand-Lieu, 18km from the witness (10° and 355°).
- **Additionally, a lantern release can only occur with light winds, which is the case: 4km/h and 0km/h.**
- **Disappearance of the phenomenon:** The dimming is typical of a lantern going out.
- **Finally, the observation took place on a Saturday night to Sunday, a weeknight conducive to lantern releases, and the 5-minute duration is a typical length for a lantern observation.** However, no specific element (gendarmerie release statement) allowed to confirm the release, so the GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
