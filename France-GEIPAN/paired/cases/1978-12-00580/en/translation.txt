Description: On Tuesday, December 19, 1978, at 8:23 a.m., a witness observing from their balcony noticed a phenomenon they did not recognize. In the clear sky towards the southeast of Meaux, a glowing point oscillated. The object disappeared around 9:45, hidden by the sun. The gendarmes, also from the apartment, observed a glowing point in the same direction at approximately a 35° elevation. The previous day in Paris, two witnesses were intrigued by the presence in the sky of a particularly bright object, and a police team also noted the object's presence in the sky. The Paris police investigation concluded that it was an astronomical observation. Astronomical verification (see celestial map) clearly indicates Venus, located between 25° and 30° southeast at the indicated time. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": astronomical misperception with Venus.
