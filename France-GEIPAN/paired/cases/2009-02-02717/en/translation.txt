Summary: Observation of two glowing objects in a mountainous area.
--- seg-f3aa0a4dc708921bf8ba0427 [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 28, 2009, between 6:30 PM and 7:35 PM, individuals at a ski resort observed, from various locations, two spherical lights of differing sizes. The observations were made from a great distance, but the witnesses noted the movement of these bright spheres in the mountain range. Numerous photos and videos of the phenomenon were taken before the lights disappeared. This observation was reported very late (March 4, 2011) to GEIPAN through a questionnaire and a very large number of photos. Without conducting a thorough investigation, the reconstruction of the observation from two of the photos (IMG_0114 and IMG_0105) places the phenomenon ("2 glowing balls") on the mountain's slope, at the level of the ski runs. The observation period corresponds to the peak of the ski season. The hypothesis that the observed lights were the headlights/projectors (one of the two balls) of a ski piste maintenance vehicle (a "dameuse") and an inflatable lighting balloon (a commonly used technique) is probable.
