Description: On August 29, 2024, at approximately 10:40 PM, in Antran (86), two witnesses observing from their property's courtyard saw a series of bright points quickly crossing a clear sky. The passage was characterized by points moving in pairs, following two parallel trajectories that, due to perspective, appeared to converge towards the horizon. A low strangeness and medium consistency: two witnesses for a unique yet complete account, without photographic or video evidence. This case turned out to be an observation of the lights of the Futuroscope's nighttime show, "The Key of Dreams." These lights, projected from Poitiers, reflected off the cloud layer above Antran (86) (as per the investigation report). According to the GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The cloud layer at altitude above the Poitiers sector served as a reflecting surface for the projectors and lasers. The absence of particles in the air between the ground and the clouds made the beams invisible from Antran (86), leaving only the light impact on the cloud layer visible. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A," observation of the lights of a sound and light show, with reflections off clouds.
