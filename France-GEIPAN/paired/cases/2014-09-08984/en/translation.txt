Summary: Multiple observations of the passage of a luminous phenomenon in the sky: observations of a large fireball, consequence of the atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
--- seg-1a9061d5d93d692044b091a6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 7, 2014, around 7 a.m., numerous people in southern France observed the passage of a luminous phenomenon in the sky. These observations were the subject of articles in local press. The GEIPAN received several testimonies. This phenomenon had all the characteristics of an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid (small rocky body in orbit around the Sun). The phenomenon occurred vertically over Spanish Catalonia, with a strong inclination (around 65°), taking place between 60 and 25 km in altitude. According to astronomers, the meteoroid responsible for this phenomenon was not on a trajectory close to asteroid 2014RC, which passed near Earth at less than 40,000 km on the same day. The two phenomena are not related. This observation case has a consistency of 1 based on multiple testimonies and recordings by several cameras. After analysis, this case is very unlikely. Therefore, the GEIPAN classifies this case as 'A': observations of a large bolide, consequence of the atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
