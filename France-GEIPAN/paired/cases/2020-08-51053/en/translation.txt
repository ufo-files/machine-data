Summary: Observation of silent movements in the sky of a PAN black matte oval: probable observation of a fantasy balloon.
--- seg-04041f205748c3b0751e8d41 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 8, 2020, around 9:10 PM, while in their garden at Uzès (Gard), the main witness (T1) and his wife (T2) notice the presence of a point in the sky. Observation conditions seem good, with sufficient visibility to distinguish the stars. T1 then conducts a more detailed observation using binoculars, allowing him to specify the shape and behavior of the PAN. The PAN has a perfectly oval shape, dark matte color, with no apparent light emission. It moves at a low speed, making slight random vertical movements while following a general straight trajectory, evolving in steps. The observation lasts about fifteen minutes until the PAN disappears from the witnesses' field of vision. The case consistency is judged as average: 2 witnesses but a single testimony, a photo that proved unusable. However, the testimony remains precise and reported shortly after the observation.

According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The analysis considered the hypothesis of a confusion with a fantasy balloon moving under the effect of local low winds (see investigation report). This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- The shape and color of the PAN are consistent with certain fantasy balloon models.
- The slow movement, generally straight, and at a constant speed, is compatible with the trajectory of a light object carried by a weak wind. The main perceived oddity, namely the movement of the PAN in a direction different from the winds felt on the ground by the witnesses, can be explained by the geographical and altitudinal difference between them and the PAN. Indeed, the witnesses are on the ground, in an area surrounded by hills, where local topography significantly influences the direction and perception of the wind, which is perceived as coming from the north.

The analysis, in particular based on AROME* data provided by Météo France, confirmed that the PAN was moving according to the general west wind. The slow random vertical movements observed can be explained by specific meteorological conditions at the time of the observation, particularly temperature and humidity variations occurring during twilight, as well as by the weakness and variability of the wind.

The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B", probable observation of a fantasy balloon.

AROME: (*Application of Research to Operations at MEsoscale) is a model with a very fine mesh (1.3 km mesh) for forecasting in France (METEO France).
