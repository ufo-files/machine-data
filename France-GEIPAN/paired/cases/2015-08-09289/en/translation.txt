Description: On August 15, 2015 at 10:36 PM, a resident of NIMES (30) was stargazing with his wife and daughter in his garden when he observed a very bright, glowing point in the southeast direction for about ten seconds before it faded away. The witness attempted to identify the PAN using the GEIPAN website, and believes it could be an Iridium flare. Of average strangeness but sufficient consistency, this case turns out to be a classic misidentification involving a satellite reflection phenomenon with the Metop A satellite. The timing, trajectory, and position of this satellite perfectly match the witness's description (see the investigation report). This case is classified as A, misidentification with a satellite reflection phenomenon, involving the Metop A satellite.
