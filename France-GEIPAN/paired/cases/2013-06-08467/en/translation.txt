Description: On June 2, 2013 at 12:09 AM, four people in a living room of an apartment observed the silent N-SSE passage of 6 luminous shapes in the sky. The observation lasted at least one minute. Only one statement was collected. The observation of luminous shapes crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from North to South-Southwest, which is consistent with the North wind reported in Lille (Météociel). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of Thai lanterns. The witness, informed of this hypothesis, did not retain it.
