Summary: Brief observation of a black streak in the sky followed by a bright flash: observation of the volumetric shadow seen in cross-section produced by an airplane condensation trail.
--- seg-1392ea230dc656ea13d90ba6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 7, 2014, around 7 PM, a driver observed in a clear sky, just above some white trails left by planes, a horizontal black line very far away, followed by a bright white flash. The witness looked at the road and lost sight of the phenomenon. Only one testimony was collected. The conjunction in the sky of a black line and plane condensation trails is characteristic of the natural phenomenon of "volume shadow seen edge-on" (or "edge shadow" in English). This phenomenon becomes visible when a straight plane trail passes in front of the sun and an alignment between the sun, the trail, and the observer occurs. A projected shadow then appears over the entire volume of atmosphere contained in the planar slice containing the trail, the observer, and the sun. This atmospheric slice behaves like a slightly tinted glass that is not noticeable when observed face-on, but creates a dark stripe when observed from the side (or edge-on), the darker the stripe, the longer the glass, that is, the longer the straight portion of the trail. The dark stripe created is present over the entire field of view of the witness, including the sky. This phenomenon requires precise geometric conditions (witness, plane trail, sun in the same plane). It can therefore appear and disappear suddenly, especially for a witness in a car, as in this case. The bright flash in the sky is likely a reflection of the sun on the plane, an intense and brief reflection (the plane acts as a mirror for the sun) that often occurs but is rarely noticed unless one is looking at the sky and planes (which was the case here, as the witness had their attention drawn to the black line). There is no necessary link between the two phenomena, just a temporal coincidence; there were several planes at that time (multiple trails), and the reflective plane could have been the one causing the black line or another. The strangeness leading to the testimony is the black line. We do not have a photo in this case. Analysis of another testimony (HYERES (83) 27.05.2016) with photos allows for a more precise exposure of the phenomenon with a detailed report. In this case, it is not possible to provide proof of the explanation, but the probability is very high. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B: Plane trail (volume shadow).
