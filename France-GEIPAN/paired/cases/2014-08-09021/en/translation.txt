--- seg-85c873bb566d187aaa387080 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 22, 2014, around 1:10 a.m., a witness was surprised to hear two cries in the night. Approaching a roof window, they saw for a few seconds, directly above them, a metallic-colored disk surrounded by small red-pink light beams. The witness silently followed the phenomenon's movements in the cloudy sky for approximately 20 seconds in total. No other testimonies were collected. This testimony is of good consistency: precise, but from a single witness without a photo. Given the objective elements defined in the investigation report (see attached document), namely: - Circular shape of the PAN - Presence of beams - Very high speed - Presence of a cloudy ceiling - Absence of noise, we can conclude that the observation made by the witness is likely that of a set of "Skytrackers" lasers, moving rapidly, projected at the base of the low cloud layer. The observation is not particularly strange as it involves objects with ultimately banal behavior. This case is classified as "B" for a probable observation of a set of lasers impacting the low cloud layer.
