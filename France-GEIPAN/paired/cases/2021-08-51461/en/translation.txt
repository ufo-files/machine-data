Description: On August 20, 2021, the main witness was on a balcony in La Garde (83) when, at 8:18 PM, he observed a PAN with a round shape and a light color moving across the sky on a straight, horizontal trajectory. His son (T2) managed to record a short video with his smartphone showing the PAN for a few seconds. According to the GEIPAN criteria, the case's consistency is average: while there were two witnesses, only one report was made to the GEIPAN. The quality and duration of the provided video offer little in the way of analysis for the PAN (__See the definition of consistency according to GEIPAN criteria on the GEIPAN website__). In this investigation, the appearance and behavior of the PAN suggest a wind-carried balloon (see the investigation report). The round shape and color align with this hypothesis. The straight, horizontal trajectory does as well. Additionally, a recreation of local winds at ground level and at low altitude shows that the apparent trajectory of the PAN aligns with the wind direction. The hypothesis that the witness observed a possibly lost hot air balloon from Toulon, a city not far to the west of the observation location or its tourist surroundings, is probable. However, the investigation did not determine the precise location where the balloon was released. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A," observation of a balloon.
