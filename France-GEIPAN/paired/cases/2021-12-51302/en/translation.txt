Summary: A driver observed a bright, stationary PAN in the sky, which then moved; observation of a plane (BGA121J Beluga flight) reflecting the sun brightly.
--- seg-0dfaaff9baa9bdb7d494ad88 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 17, 2021, at 5:15 PM, the witness is returning from work when, upon approaching the roundabout separating the "Tridome" from the "Centre Leclerc" in Marmande, they spot a static bright point in the sky, approximately to the NNO. The object shines brightly and the witness thinks it is the setting sun reflecting on it. The motorist then heads in that direction along the Leclerc center and parks near the "Point P" store, still in Marmande. He gets out of the car and observes the bright point moving towards Marmande to the east. He then spots a shape composed of two white blocks connected by a curved part of gray metallic color. During the observation, the witness will take several photos and a short video of the phenomenon before the PAN disappears towards Marmande and he loses sight of it at the Intermarché (SSE). The observation lasted approximately three minutes. Only one testimony will be collected. For this case, the consistency is good: well-documented testimony, accompanied by photos and a video. The witness is available, which greatly aided the investigator in their inquiry (see the investigation report). The strangeness is mainly that felt by the witness who finds himself face to an unexplained phenomenon: "It was moving slowly, I was already sure it wasn't an airplane." At that speed, the plane would crash." The witness perceives a "bright" massive object moving at very low speed. After studying the file and the data obtained with various aeronautical applications, the GEIPAN concludes to the observation of the BGA121J Beluga flight – regular flight Saint-Nazaire-Toulouse – which passes at the same time, in the observation azimuths and at the correct angular height. With its 63 m length and a height of ~19 m, the Beluga is an unconventional aircraft. The surface it presented to the setting sun that day was expected to reflect significantly, greatly intriguing the witness. The GEIPAN classifies this observation as A: observation of an airplane (flight BGA121J Beluga) reflecting the sun brightly.
