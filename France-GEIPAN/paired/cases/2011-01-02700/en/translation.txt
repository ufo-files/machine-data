Summary: Observation of successive appearances of orange-colored light phenomena in the sky: Lack of information.
--- seg-05537fb63d67b3fac5239d87 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Sunday, January 9, 2011, around 6:50 PM, a witness observed at low altitude several orange lights that successively appeared and moved silently and slowly in the sky. The witness began filming his observation as soon as the third PAN appeared. No other testimonies were collected. The description of the phenomenon closely resembles the successive passages of Thai lanterns (color, speed of movement, low altitude, successive appearances from the same place). However, in this sparsely populated area, the origin and location of the release could not be determined by a nearby investigation, and some elements seem contradictory (passing behind clouds while the object flies quite low). The hypothesis of images due to a laser projector was also considered, but no beam was seen or filmed. Given all these uncertainties, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C".
