Summary: Observations of the slow passage of a bright orange object with a trail: probable observations of an atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-3ed5a6aae3fdc7de9d5db2e1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: At 3:00 AM on June 16, 1982, witnesses observed in the sky a spherical object with a halo, followed by a cluster of smaller points of yellow-orange color forming a glowing trail. This ensemble moved slowly in a straight line from northeast to southeast. The observation lasted less than a minute, and no noise associated with the phenomenon was heard. Based on descriptions of the phenomenon, these individuals likely witnessed an atmospheric reentry.
