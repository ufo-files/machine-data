Summary: Observation of 4 white, silent, oval objects in the sky that dissipate one by one: lack of information and cross-references.
--- seg-d8092d25ec47d25150bd1bd0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 14, 2014, around 9:20 a.m., a witness looking at the Moon was intrigued by four grouped oval-shaped white objects in the clear sky. They appear static and disappear one by one. No particular noise is heard during the observation, which lasts one to two minutes. No other testimonies have been collected. The witness observes white, oval-shaped objects in the sky towards the West, with little movement. The gendarmerie conducted investigations into aircraft flights in this area, which yielded negative results. These could be balloons: the light northwest wind, which was clearly intensifying (the Mistral is rising), would have then pushed them towards the witness, making them appear almost stationary. However, releasing a balloon on a weekday at this time of day seems unusual, although it is the peak of summer holidays. Due to the lack of precise information to support this hypothesis and the absence of cross-referencing with other testimonies, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to lack of information.
