Description: On Saturday, March 7, 2015 at 9:33 PM, a witness observed four very pale white lights in the sky. The phenomenon moved slowly and silently on a straight trajectory before disappearing into a cloud. No other testimonies were collected. The observation of glowing orbs crossing the sky in just a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from West to North, which is consistent with the South wind reported in Quimper (see Météociel record). The observation was very brief, and the available information is imprecise, making this case weak. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of Thai lanterns.
