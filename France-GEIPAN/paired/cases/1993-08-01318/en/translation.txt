Description: On August 7, at 10:45 PM, a witness driving their car observed a very bright, stationary white light for approximately one minute. Upon the light's disappearance, a whistling sound was heard, and a black shape seemed to disappear at a very high speed. The following day, the witness experienced muscle pain, and upon returning to the location of the observation, they suffered from severe headaches. The investigation revealed no air traffic in the area, and the phenomenon remains unexplained.
