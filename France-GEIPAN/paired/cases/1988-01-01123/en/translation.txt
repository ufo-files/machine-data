Description: On January 26, 1988, at 21:15, a witness observed several times a large object flying at low altitude, whose shape reminded him of a helmet with two wings. No noise was heard during the object's movements. A white light emanated from inside, which turned orange. The object disappeared as it quickly moved away. No further information could be gathered about this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
