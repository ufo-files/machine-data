Description: On September 17, 2021, around 4 a.m., the witness, near a beach of the Loire in the commune of Bou (45), observes in the sky a static glowing sphere that seems to grow. He describes the object as "not a ball in the strict sense, but rather two golden crescents, back to back, with a less luminous distinction of the sphere's contours, similar to an eclipse." The phenomenon then appears to shrink into a simple golden sphere. At its maximum, the glowing object had an estimated diameter of 3 to 4 times that of the sun, without a notable increase in ambient light. Despite several attempts to follow up with the witness to obtain additional information via the technical questionnaire, no response was received. The lack of decisive data therefore limits the possibility of a thorough analysis of this observation (see the investigation report). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C," investigation abandoned due to lack of data.
