--- seg-ac8e5c214a9bd0d252db7114 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the evening of March 9, 2022, a resident of VIEILLE-TOULOUSE (31) observed a round, white/blue, stationary light phenomenon in the sky near the constellation of Aries. The observation lasted 5 to 6 seconds, after which the PAN went out. The residual strangeness was of average to good consistency* (single witness, no photo of the PAN, but a fairly complete and precise testimony), and this case turned out to be the certain observation of the flash of an orbiting object. This phenomenon was caused by a brief reflection of the Sun - already set for the observer - on the reflective surface of an artificial satellite (see the investigation report). The Operational Orbitography Center of CNES (COO), questioned on this matter, confirmed the passage of a space debris (the body of the DELTA 2 R/B (2) [PAM-D] rocket - registered N28876). Its trajectory, according to the azimuth and elevation, as well as the timing, perfectly matched the data provided by the witness. The description of the PAN indeed very precisely recalls the observation of such a phenomenon: • point-like appearance (like a star), • white color, high brightness, • extinction in broad daylight and observation for a few seconds. The case is classified as "A": observation of a reflection of the Sun on an orbiting space debris, called a satellite flash.
