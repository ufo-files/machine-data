Description: While reviewing his photographs on his computer, a person discovers a strange image in one of his photos. The photographs were taken at a distance of approximately 1800 meters with a 100-300 zoom and a doubler. After examining the series of photos, it is quite obvious that it is a large bird taking flight at the moment of the shot. The conditions of the shot (distance, zoom, and positioning in a car) led the photographer to not notice the bird, which was not yet in flight on the first photo and was completely gone on the third. This type of misidentification is particularly common, and we are very regularly led to identify birds in photos that the photographer did not notice. Seeing a bird fly by is so mundane that the brain, focused on the shot, completely ignores this information.
