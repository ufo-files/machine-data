Summary: Observations by two groups of witnesses of two red, round phenomena in the sky: lack of information.
--- seg-802853d7114c8534693a9dce [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 8, 1956, around 23:40-23:45, three witnesses in the commune of La Londe (76) observed a red ball in the direction of the West on a clear night without a moon. The phenomenon appears stationary for several minutes then seems to move during the witnesses' movements. The phenomenon disappears behind the trees. The observation lasted about twenty minutes. The gendarmerie will gather three additional witnesses for the same date and time, 23:30-23:45: These witnesses, in a car in Elbeuf (76), describe a red, moving, round object. The observation will be very short (4 to 5 seconds) in the direction of Orival (76). The available information does not confirm that the two groups saw the same phenomenon. Astronomical verification indicates for the first group Venus close to the horizon at approximately 45° north of the observation direction, and for the second group looking towards the North no particular star is visible. This case is not very strange and the information is too imprecise: the GEIPAN classifies this case as C.
