Summary: Observations by two groups of witnesses of two red, round phenomena in the sky: lack of information.
--- seg-802853d7114c8534693a9dce [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 8, 1956, around 11:40-11:45 PM, three witnesses in the commune of La Londe (76) observed a red ball in the clear, moonless night sky heading west. The phenomenon appeared stationary for several minutes, then seemed to move as the witnesses moved. It disappeared behind trees. The observation lasted approximately twenty minutes. The gendarmerie collected three additional witness statements for the same date and time (23:30-23:45): these witnesses, in a car in Elbeuf (76), described a red, round object in motion. The observation was very brief (4-5 seconds) heading towards Orival (76). The available information does not confirm that the two groups observed the same phenomenon. Astronomical verification indicates that for the first group, Venus was near the horizon at approximately 45° north of the observation direction, and for the second group, looking north, no particular stars were visible. This case is not very strange and the information is too imprecise: the GEIPAN classifies this case as C.
