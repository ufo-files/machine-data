--- seg-71270119add2aeb83ec36c7f [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 8, 2011, around 11:30 PM, a witness driving a car observed a bright light in the sky. He stopped and opened his window, noticing a large craft equipped with blinding white lights parked approximately 50 meters above a house. No noise was heard. The lights went out, and the craft turned on itself to leave: the witness then saw that the craft had a triangular shape. Due to the oddness of the case, the GEIPAN wanted to conduct a field investigation, but this was only requested in 2014. As the witness lives relatively far from the observation location and is not very available, this interview could not be organized. The delay of over 3 years since the observation also seemed too long to the witness. With high strangeness, the consistency of this observation case is too low at this stage to allow for an unexplained D classification: a very brief unique testimony with many inaccuracies, written a year after the facts. Without additional data, the GEIPAN classifies this case as C.
