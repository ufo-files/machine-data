Description: On October 5, 2017, at 1:30 PM, a witness, lying on the ground during a physical exercise, was surprised by the fast west-to-east passage of an opaque white ball. The witness noticed no trace in the sky left by the phenomenon. No noise was heard. The observation lasted between 15 and 20 seconds. Only one testimony for this phenomenon was collected. The witness's observation could correspond to the passage of a bolide (a very bright meteor) although we did not find formal evidence of this passage. Daytime bolides are harder to see; some can be identified (by cameras or radar) without ever having been the subject of human testimony. The witness's lying position, calm (respiratory exercise), likely favored the observation. The described aspect could correspond, but the indicated duration seems long and would have required clarifications. The witness did not follow up on our request to fill out our questionnaire. As a result, the GEIPAN is unable to explore this hypothesis or any other further. The GEIPAN classifies this case as C: lack of reliable information.
