--- seg-8ff9b01825303cc68f643ff5 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Saturday, November 16, 2013 at 23:35, a witness observed in the cloudy sky a stationary light above a stadium, persisting after the stadium lights went out. This Palois, looking in the direction of Jurançon, thought it was a laser but did not identify its source. That evening, there was an electronic culture festival - Digital Sun with a green laser installation on the Guindalos Hill in Gelos, south of Pau. The program is available on the Access(s) festival website. The case is classified as A: observation of a laser animation.
