Summary: Observation of the movement in the night sky of a luminous phenomenon moving at a constant speed: mistaken for the passage of a satellite.
--- seg-69487ddebb9182015708164a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 23, 2015, at 10:25 PM, two witnesses observed in the sky the movement of a luminous phenomenon moving at a constant speed. However, the PAN slightly changed direction several times (North then NW then finally North), which intrigued the observers. Only one questionnaire was completed. Of average strangeness, but with sufficient consistency (many coherent and precise details allowing the case to be credible), this case turns out to be a classic misperception with a satellite passage. A more in-depth investigation allowed two satellites to be identified that are very likely the source of this misperception: IGS 5 H2A rocket, whose brightness matches that of the PAN, and whose trajectory is very close, and Iridium 16, an out-of-control satellite whose trajectory and apparent speed match those of the PAN. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A, misperception with a satellite, although there is some doubt as to the exact identity of that satellite.
