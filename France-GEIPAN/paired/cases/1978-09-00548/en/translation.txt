Summary: Observation of two bright flying objects in the sky.
--- seg-f5d67fbfe63a5cf949e8ded3 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 27, 1978, at approximately 7:35 PM, a telephone call to the Sète semaphore reported two stationary bright lights in the sky. The on-duty military personnel confirmed the observation and the presence of a first object with a cylindrical shape, a conical and shiny front part, and a second object resembling a crescent that appeared and disappeared. The post commander was contacted and also confirmed the presence of the two objects. The objects disappeared around 8:20 PM behind clouds. These individuals likely observed balloons.
