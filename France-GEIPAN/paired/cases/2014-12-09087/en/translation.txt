Summary: Sky observation of the movement of a bright yellow phenomenon in the shape of a tear, then a dark, matte circular shape: probable observation of a short condensation trail from an airplane.
--- seg-49c1b9c32d436a2dc641f98c [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 21, 2015, around 6:00 PM, three individuals observed through the living room window and then on the terrace a phenomenon they could not explain. Initially, from inside, the observed phenomenon appeared as a water droplet and was yellow in color, moving across the sky. The witnesses went to the terrace and then saw the PAN as a circular, matte black object that quickly disappeared. The observations were made with the naked eye and binoculars. Only one questionnaire was received, but an on-site investigation allowed for refining or correcting certain data (see the investigation report). What made the observation strange was the juxtaposition in two sequences, at first glance unrelated, of the same phenomenon. Following the investigators' on-site movement and based on the witnesses' descriptions, it appears likely that a short condensation trail from an airplane was involved. The three witnesses observed the phenomenon intermittently as night fell. Initially, the sun's rays illuminated the trail; in the second phase of observation, the sun's rays may no longer have directly illuminated the area of sky traversed by the plane. An airplane condensation trail can thus quickly go from a bright spot to a dark spot. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of an airplane condensation trail.
