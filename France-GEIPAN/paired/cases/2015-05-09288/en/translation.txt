Description: In August 2015, a witness reported their observation made during the night of May 9-10, 2015 at 3:40 a.m. This person observed in the sky 12 to 15 bright objects. These objects were round in shape and light in color. They were arranged in a semi-circle. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted 2 minutes. Only one testimony was collected. This testimony provides too little information for a complete analysis, although the observation of multiple orbs on a weekend night (including here according to a semi-circle arrangement) does suggest Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The GEIPAN classifies the case as C: Lack of Information.
