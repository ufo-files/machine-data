Description: On August 23, 1980, around 9:30 a.m., a witness observed from their residence a flash of light. They then made an intriguing observation: a very bright, spherical flying object moved across the sky. As it approached a mountain range, the object rolled on its side, turned red, and quickly disappeared behind the landscape towards the North. No other testimonies were collected regarding this phenomenon, for which we have limited information.
