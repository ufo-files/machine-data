Description: On October 31, 2006, a driver observed a peculiar luminous phenomenon behind the clouds. A flickering white light appeared to move slowly. That evening, an electric garland placed on a viaduct was active. The gendarmerie investigation determined that the witness, misled by poor weather conditions, mistook this luminous animation for a OVNI.
