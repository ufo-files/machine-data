Description: On December 1, 1995, around 7:30 PM, a witness observed from his home a glowing ball that slowly descended over his roof and, after a brief stop, resumed moving at a slow speed. The witness's testimony was prompted by a radio show about extraterrestrials asking any witnesses to come forward. No other information was gathered.
