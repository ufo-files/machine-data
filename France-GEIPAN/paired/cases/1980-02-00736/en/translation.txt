Description: On February 16, 1980, between 1:00 and 1:30 a.m., three military personnel on duty reported observing an unidentified flying object (UFO). The UFO flew over the village. According to the witnesses, who differ in their descriptions of the object's shape, it projected an intense orange light. It remained stationary for one minute before quickly ascending and moving in steps. The intensity of the light gradually diminished until it became just a point. No other information was gathered from civilians in the area. Since no radar surveillance was conducted over the weekend, the presence of an aircraft could not be confirmed. This case is classified as C due to a lack of data.
