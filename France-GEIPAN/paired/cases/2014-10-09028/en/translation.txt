Description: On October 19, 2014, around 8:30 PM, a witness and his brother-in-law observed the silent passage of a dark triangular shape with red lights at each end. This phenomenon passed over the garden. The three points moved in a straight line, then formed a dark triangle. The witnesses left the observation site. Only one testimony was collected. Given the objective elements defined in the investigation report (see attached document), namely:

- The possibility that the PAN is not a single, solid object but rather three independent objects.
- The straight trajectory, like that of an object pushed by a steady and regular wind.
- The red color, similar to that of lanterns.
- The absence of noise.
- The observation duration compatible with the lifespan of a lantern.
- The observation date conducive to lantern releases, on weekends, for holidays, weddings, etc...

Confusion with a group of three Thai lanterns is possible. However, we lack the azimuthal data for the movement of the PAN. This testimony is of low consistency: it is imprecise and lacks essential data for analysis (direction of movement), coming from a single witness without a photo. The observation is not particularly strange as the objects have a mundane behavior and appearance. The GEIPAN classifies this case as Category C due to lack of information and cross-references.
