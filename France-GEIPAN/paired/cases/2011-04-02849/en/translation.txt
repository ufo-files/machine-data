Description: On April 16, 2011, around 2:20 a.m., a motorist and their passenger were intrigued by the horizontal movement of about a dozen orange glowing orbs. These objects moved silently from northeast to southwest before gradually disappearing. The witnesses' account (moving orange lights, fading after a few minutes) and the wind direction (northeast) are entirely consistent with the observation of Thai lanterns. The time of day and hour (late evening on a weekend) are also typical for the release of lanterns following a celebration. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
