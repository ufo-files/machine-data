Description: On August 02, 2019, in the late afternoon, the witness and 3 other people are at the edge of a swimming pool. The witness is intrigued by the silent passage across a clear sky of 3 objects. He describes them as having an atypical shape (round center and something resembling wings on each side) and a color that is rather gray. The 3 objects are identical and the same size. They move at equal distances from each other on a straight trajectory. After 6 to 7 seconds, these 3 objects separate, taking different directions. The objects are out of sight. Only one testimony is collected. Of oddity and average consistency (multiple witnesses, but unique testimony, short observation, no photos), this case could be a possible misperception with drones or birds, the second hypothesis being favored. The description of the PAN does indeed correspond to these two explanatory hypotheses. The one of a misidentification with birds is more likely with the description of the PAN (round and gray objects with wings, absence of noise), although it is obviously impossible to verify the presence of birds on the trajectory of the PAN at the time of the observation. However, it is not explained why the witness does not recognize birds in broad daylight and with a clear sky, so the hypothesis does not seem to be able to be retained. The case is classified C, lack of reliable information.
