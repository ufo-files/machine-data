--- seg-7418cf7a8b3359626fcdebdc [needs-review] ---
Description: In the evening of January 21, 1985, between 11:30 PM and 12:00 AM, witnesses will observe flashes of light in a ruined house next to their home. The investigation will very quickly demonstrate that it is a short circuit on an electric fence. In the same evening, between 8:45 PM and 9:15 PM, in the same area, a motorist and his wife will be witnesses to a yellow, triangular, luminous phenomenon that even seems to follow them for a moment before disappearing abruptly. None of the patrols in the area observed the phenomenon, and no other information could be gathered.
