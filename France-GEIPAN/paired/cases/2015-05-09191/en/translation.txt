Summary: Long video observation of a luminous phenomenon in the evening: hoax.
--- seg-20827f20858693bf57c55251 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 12, 2015, a witness decides to look at the sky following the publication in a local newspaper of a mention of a meteor having crossed the sky on May 9. From his garden around 10:45 PM, he observes, along with his mother and a neighbor, the presence of a luminous phenomenon. The observation lasts approximately 15 minutes. Only one testimony is collected. A video is made by the witness. The strangeness of this case is very low. Apart from the witness's statements, which are not confirmed by anything, what is visible in the video has no strange character (see the investigation report and the witness's video). Indeed, the available video seems to be the film of a streetlamp, shot in front of his house, through the foliage of a tree. Moving the camera produces an effect making the lampbulb of the streetlamp sometimes visible and sometimes masked, partially or totally, by the foliage. The comments on the video strengthen doubt about its validity. The testimony is very weakly consistent, with very little exploitable data provided by the witness (except the video). The questionnaire was not completed correctly or very succinctly. This case is classified as "A", as a hoax.
