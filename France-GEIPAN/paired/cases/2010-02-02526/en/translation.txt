Summary: Observation of flying lanterns.
--- seg-3aed9640d9116d57d74e8e93 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of February 14, 2010, around midnight, numerous witnesses located around Lorient observed the passage of glowing balls. No noise was heard during the observations, which intrigued the witnesses. Several consistent testimonies were reported to a major daily newspaper in Western France and to the GEIPAN. Following the publication in the Press of these testimonies, a owner of a Vietnamese restaurant reported having released, to celebrate with his guests the Chinese New Year, about thirty flying lanterns at midnight on the night of February 13 to 14, 2010. The time, the launch site, and the direction of movement of the event, which was filmed, correspond perfectly to the testimonies reported to the GEIPAN.
