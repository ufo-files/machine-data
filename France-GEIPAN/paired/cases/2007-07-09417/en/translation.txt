Description: Recent (2016) testimony of an old observation (July 2007). On July 7, 2007, around 3 a.m., a driver and his friend, traveling on N118 towards Nantes from Bordeaux, were surprised to see a luminous, spherical phenomenon with an incandescent green color and a light green halo moving randomly in the air with frequent changes in direction. The observation at close range lasted about twenty seconds before the PAN disappeared. The witness initially thought it was a ball lightning but dismissed this hypothesis due to the absence of stormy weather. He noted the proximity of the CEA Saclay. Only one testimony was collected. According to the expert consulted, the observation is compatible, in terms of the phenomenon's behavior, its lifespan, and its color, with a case of globular lightning. However, the testimony is late (11 years after the observation) and thus does not allow access to meteorological data (including stormy conditions?) that could support this hypothesis. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as C (near a case B: globular lightning).
