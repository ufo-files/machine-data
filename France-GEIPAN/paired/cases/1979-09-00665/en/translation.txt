Summary: Observation of a 11-meter-wide mushroom-shaped formation on the ground.
--- seg-0e2ce297353b4a452885625f [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 30, 1979, a farmer went to a barley field and noticed a 10.50-meter-diameter burned circle. He observed that the grass inside the circle was greener than that in the surrounding meadow. Around September 20, the same witness discovered that pink field mushrooms had grown both inside and outside the circle. This phenomenon had already been observed in the meadows of the area.
