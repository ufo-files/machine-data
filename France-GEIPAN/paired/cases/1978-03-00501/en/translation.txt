Description: On March 24, 1978, around 10 PM, a witness observed from their residence a dazzling crimson red light. They then saw an object with a rectangular shape bathed in this light. Inside the object, the witness distinguished two yellow circles that could be windows. The object moved horizontally before disappearing abruptly, leaving a faint pink glow in its wake. No other information was gathered about this phenomenon.
