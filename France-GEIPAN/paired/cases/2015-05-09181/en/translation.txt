Description: On Friday, May 15, 2015, at approximately 10 PM, a driver and their passenger observed numerous flashes of white light with a slight trail in the night sky. The observation lasted for 7 minutes over an 8-kilometer stretch of rural roads. Only one report of this phenomenon was collected. (The witness incorrectly identified this as a meteor) This observation took place around an hour after sunset, during the last light of twilight, just 10 kilometers from the Camargue lakes and its thousands of large birds: it was the end of the "evening passage" when the last birds leave the lakes and fly to their "sleeping quarters" on land. The witnesses did consider the possibility of birds, but dismissed it as they thought it was nocturnal birds, which is unlikely, especially given the frequency of the passages. However, the GEIPAN did not gather concrete evidence to confirm this hypothesis, and the witness description is quite brief (white light with a slight trail), so the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to lack of corroboration.
