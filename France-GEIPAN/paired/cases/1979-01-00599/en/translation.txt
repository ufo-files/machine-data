--- seg-a2d75ff250f1bfba9badf06e [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 29, 1979, between 8:30 PM and 8:45 PM, a witness observed, following a lightning flash, a bright orange light point. Initially stationary, the point began a slow descent towards the ground while growing in size. Then it changed direction and left at a very high speed horizontally and disappeared. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted approximately 2 to 3 minutes. An hypothesis raised by the Florac Observatory, contacted by the gendarmerie for this observation, was that of a "ball lightning" phenomenon, but we lack data to validate or refute this hypothesis.
