Summary: Observations of a phenomenon in the sky: probable airplane trails.
--- seg-c9b34bb528fc222bccad4fd6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 17, 2013 at 10:04 AM, several people in a car were intrigued by the presence in the clear sky of a phenomenon resembling white streaks. Two photographs are attached to the testimony. The strange aspect of this observation lies in two factors: first, some of the streaks are not straight, and second, there are many of them. This can be explained by the particular meteorological conditions of that day: the strong rising wind, which is correlated with strong winds at high altitude, deforming very quickly airplane trails, and particular temperature and humidity conditions that cause the trails to persist for a relatively long time. This case is classified as "B": very probable observation of airplane trails, numerous and deformed due to the summer wind (low pressure system, strong high-altitude winds).
