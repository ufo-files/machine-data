Summary: Observations of the movement of two opaque white masses in the sky: probable observations of light displays with a Sky Rose-type projector.
--- seg-9676b56c978d6b423359ff18 [needs-review] ---
Description: Several people in a vehicle observed on May 17, 2008, around 1:45 a.m., a phenomenon in the sky that intrigued them: two opaque white light masses moved across the sky. They changed shape, appearing and disappearing for a few seconds. Curiosity, piqued by a recent local press article, excellent visibility, and the increasing sophistication of animation lights surprised the witnesses, prompting them to come forward—certainly just a misperception with a Sky Rose-type light. Another witness very likely saw the same thing the following night (case Cesson-Sévigné (35) 5/17/2008). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Skyrose-type light animations.
