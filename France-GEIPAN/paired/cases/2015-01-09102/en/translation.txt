Description: On January 10, 2015, at 6:08 PM, a driver was intrigued by two very bright points relatively low in the sky. He took a photo. No other testimonies were collected. This testimony of two bright points separated by a dark mass suggests a medium or low-altitude aircraft: the Flight Radar record (at 5:01 PM UTC) indicates that the Nantes-Lyon flight was overflying Communay at less than 2500 meters altitude (see screenshot). The direction changes noted by the witness could also be due to observing another aircraft, given the fairly heavy traffic at that location, even at medium and low altitudes. For example, one hour later, three HOP airplanes succeeded each other within minutes on a northwest to southeast trajectory at less than 2500 meters at the same location. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," probable observation of low-altitude aircraft.
