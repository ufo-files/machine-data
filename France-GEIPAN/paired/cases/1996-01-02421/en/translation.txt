Description: Recent testimony (2 September 2009) of an old observation (in 1996, plus or minus two years, the exact date and month provided here are arbitrary as they were not precisely indicated by the witness). Around 7 PM, a driver observed with his daughter a white halo moving at a constant speed, measuring 80 cm by 40 cm. The witness noticed a particular trajectory composed of straight segments at a constant speed and 1.5 meters above ground. Moving from North to South, the object made a sudden turn towards the West. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted about a minute. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon, for which, given its antiquity, any investigation is difficult, if not impossible.
