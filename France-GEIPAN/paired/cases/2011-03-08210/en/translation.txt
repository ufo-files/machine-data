--- seg-75505220da911f7fa206f4fd [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 20, 2011, around 8:30 PM, a driver observed a light in the sky that intrigued and frightened her. The object witnessed by the witness is ultimately always in the same direction. As it is very far away, it seems to follow the observer. Vertical movements are an illusion, as they are perceived relative to the horizon of the landscape, which of course varies along the journey. The change in color of a bright point on the horizon is due to atmospheric turbulence. The direction indicated by the witness, to the left of her road direction E/NE, points to N/NE. At this point in the sky, the star Deneb, one of the brightest stars in the sky (one of the 3 in the summer triangle), was shining. This case is classified as "A": Confusion with the star Deneb.
