Summary: observation of an unusual object resembling a kite
--- seg-2ad4e881bd7787aa89ecb79a [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 14, 1979, between 21:50 and 21:55, two witnesses observed in the sky at an altitude of 100 to 150 meters a rounded object shaped like a cauldron and colored stainless steel or aluminum. Its horizontal trajectory was NE-SO, and it moved regularly and silently in oscillating motions. Reflections appeared during its movement. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected. According to the description and the date of July 14, many unusual objects were moving at low altitude: these witnesses likely saw a kite pass by.
