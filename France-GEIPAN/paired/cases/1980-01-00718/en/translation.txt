Description: On January 24, 1980, between 6:05 PM and 6:25 PM, a witness claims to have witnessed the arrival and departure of an unidentified flying object (UFO). At the beginning of the observation, the witness describes the object as cylindrical with two small, dark fins on the sides. As the object approached, it stabilized at an altitude of 200 meters and was then approximately 300 meters east of a water tower. At this point, the witness realizes the object is oval-shaped, illuminated by a bright yellow-orange light, and the fins have disappeared. During the 7-minute observation, this person notices their radio-telephone static abnormally and performs several sweeps with a portable flashlight. The object suddenly departs, quickly gaining altitude. Frightened, the witness goes to alert the gendarmerie. A malfunction of the vehicle's dashboard electrical circuit will be noted. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
