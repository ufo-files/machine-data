Description: On January 1, 1976, around 7:45 PM, several people in a public transportation vehicle observed a bright light passing through the sky. In the pitch black night, a circular shape bathed in a vivid light quickly crossed the sky on a NE-SO trajectory. No sound was heard during the brief passage of this phenomenon, for which we lack information.
