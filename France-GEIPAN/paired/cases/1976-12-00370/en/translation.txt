Description: On Tuesday, December 14, 1976, between 01:15 and 01:27, a motorist observes an unusual light above the airport. A bright white sphere moves laterally and slowly, followed by a second sphere. They initially remain stationary before quickly ascending and disappearing. A gendarmerie investigation determines that this person witnessed the release of a weather balloon: all his observations perfectly describe the balloon's maneuvers.
