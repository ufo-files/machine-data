Description: On May 10, 1982, a witness observed a peculiar flying object in the sky. For 45 seconds, they were able to see a shimmering rectangular panel according to their exposure to the sun. Its North-South trajectory was constant. The object disappeared into the landscape. Despite the investigations conducted, no additional information (trace or testimony) was gathered about this phenomenon.
