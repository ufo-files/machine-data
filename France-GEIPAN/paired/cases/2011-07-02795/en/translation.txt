Description: On July 22, 2011, at 22:15, a witness observes, at 10-minute intervals, the silent movement in the sky of reddish-orange glowing objects. The objects will disappear in a southerly direction. Each observation will last 3 minutes. Of low strangeness and average to low consistency due to the uniqueness of the testimony, this observation case has all the characteristics of a misperception of the passage of Thai lanterns. This case is classified as B, observation of Thai lanterns.
