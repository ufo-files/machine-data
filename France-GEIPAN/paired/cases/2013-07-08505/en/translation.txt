--- seg-db631b5881817da95009a08c [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 27, 2013, at 11:47 PM, a couple observed the passage of a flickering bright object in the clear sky. Viewed through binoculars, the object was described as being round in shape, with regular flickering, and amber in color. No noise was heard. Approaching from the south of Nice, the object moved towards the north before making a smooth, linear turn towards the west of the city and the Var. Only one testimony was collected. The witness's description has all the characteristics of an airplane: the multiple flickering lights (anti-collision and strobe lights at 1 Hz) that are not synchronized often give the illusion of flickering twice a second. The trajectory of the phenomenon is compatible with that of an airplane. The GEIPAN processed this case too late to verify this radar trace hypothesis. Therefore, and since the witness clearly refutes the airplane hypothesis, the GEIPAN classifies this case as 'C' due to a lack of information.
