Description: On Monday, April 13, 2015, around 10:45 a.m., a person, on the terrace of a café with friends, observed the silent passage of a very bright object in the clear sky. Only one testimony is collected. A bright object in the sun, appearing metallic, rotating and crossing a portion of the sky in less than a minute, suggests a mylar balloon (metallized fairground balloon) that has escaped from the hands of its owner. Since the witness did not identify the object, he cannot accurately estimate its distance or size; as is often the case, there is an overestimation of these factors: an object 50 cm in size seen from 100 meters away appears strictly the same to the observer as an object of the same shape that is 5 meters long seen from 1000 meters away. The witness looks in the direction of S/SO (azimuth 220°) and notices a movement from his left to his right: the object can fly from SE to NO, as well as from East to West. The distance would not be perceptible in such a short observation. The Météociel report for Orléans indicates a variable wind from the northeast to the east (see the Météociel report . Although not exactly in the SE -> NO axis as perceived by the witness, an easterly or even northeasterly wind could push a balloon from the left to the right of the observer. The end of the observation (spiral descent) may correspond to the deflation of the balloon due to overpressure, caused by the altitude. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a mylar balloon.
