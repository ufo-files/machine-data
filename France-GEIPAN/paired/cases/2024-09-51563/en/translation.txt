Description: On September 12, 2024, at 01:35, in Carentan-les-Marais (50), a witness observed from their home an unusual nocturnal light phenomenon. Their attention was drawn to a fixed, bright, white light that appeared twice in succession. A second, fainter light then moved towards this main source. As the two lights seemed to converge, the brighter object suddenly accelerated and disappeared in less than two seconds. Approximately five seconds after the end of the event, a commercial airliner passed in the same direction, slightly higher in the sky. The case consistency is judged low due to a single testimony and the absence of photos or videos for this specific date. Note that the photos and videos used in the investigation report are from an observation made by the witness on November 12, 2024, an event they consider similar to the first reported observation at GEIPAN. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. Due to its low elevation angle, the phenomenon could correspond to reflections on an obstacle (tree, pole, or building) or directly on the witness's window. However, the available information does not allow for a deeper analysis of possible causes. GEIPAN classifies the case as C, unidentified phenomenon due to lack of data.
