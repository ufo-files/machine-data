Description: Media reports an observation of an unknown object's movement made by several people on January 19, 1975 at 6:45 PM, and the gendarmerie is seeking witnesses. Only two people wish to testify, but only anonymously. The object appeared to rotate on itself and slowly move away from the horizon on a descending trajectory before quickly ascending and disappearing. Another observation of the phenomenon was made on January 20, 1975 at the same time. Despite the consistency of the testimonies, this case is classified C due to a lack of precise information.
