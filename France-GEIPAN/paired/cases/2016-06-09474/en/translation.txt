Description: On June 14, 2016, at 10:35 PM, a witness in their garden observed the successive passage of several orange glowing orbs, moving silently, before disappearing towards the southeast. Only one testimony was collected. The witness likely saw Thai lanterns. The shape, color, arrangement, and trajectory in the sky indicate a release of lanterns, especially since the direction of movement of the orbs recorded that night at the location is exactly that of the wind from 130° Cap -140° (see Infoclimat). Consequently, GEIPAN is classified as B: probable Thai lanterns.
