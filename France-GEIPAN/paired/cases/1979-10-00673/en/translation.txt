Description: On October 20, 1979, several people in the Vosges and Haut-Rhin regions witnessed a flickering light phenomenon in the sky. The object, spherical in shape, appeared to move in a zigzag pattern without making any noise. A similar event had been reported by witnesses one year earlier. This case is classified as C due to a lack of information.
