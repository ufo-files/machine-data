--- seg-f09b8d717d90997f813154e8 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 7, 2008, at 10:55 PM, a group of 6 high school students observed in the sky two glowing orbs that moved silently, then appeared stationary before quickly departing on an upward trajectory. The cartographic representation of the scene and the weather report (light easterly wind in Nantes) suggest that they might have been Thai lanterns released from one of the houses on the forest's edge to the east. However, the testimony does not fully align with this hypothesis: white and blue orbs, a glowing thread, not quite linear movement. Given the low strangeness of the case and its age, the GEIPAN did not reopen an investigation on this case. The GEIPAN classified this case as "C" due to lack of information, although the Thai lantern hypothesis remains fairly probable.
