--- seg-421f8efd3c6712ec2d07f838 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 15, 2011, between 11:30 PM and 11:45 PM, two witnesses were intrigued by the rapid passage in the sky of a very intense and flickering white light. The phenomenon crossed the entire sky. Of average strangeness, but insufficiently precise (directions, time), this observation case suggests phenomena related to reflections of the sun on an unlisted satellite (or a phenomenon of atmospheric reentry type, according to a completely exceptional scenario). Since the GEIPAN could not confirm this hypothesis, this case is classified C due to lack of more precise information.
