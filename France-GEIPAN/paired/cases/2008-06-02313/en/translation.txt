Description: On June 13, 2008, at 23:30, witnesses observed from their homes the movement of luminous phenomena. A video will show bright lights moving from the northeast to the northwest. Other people on the street will spot the lights, and many internet users will testify on various internet sites. The large number of luminous objects moving on the same trajectory and with low brightness is likely due to the release of Thai lantern balloons during a festive event. Identifying the responsible parties and launch sites for these lantern balloons is extremely difficult, and we encourage individuals engaging in such activities to come forward to the GEIPAN.
