Description: On Tuesday, August 23, 2016, around 9:45 PM, a family observed the silent passage of several bright points in the sky. A flickering was noticed before trees blocked the phenomenon. Only one statement was collected. The witnesses very likely saw the passage of LED balloons. These helium-filled balloons are luminous, with variable colors (including during flight), stationary or blinking. The description aligns with characteristics found on merchant sites (such as Lacheballon). It is summer vacation season, conducive to festive events, and notably, the observed movement (towards NW) corresponds to the wind direction reported at that time in the area on Infoclimat. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of LED balloon passage.
