Description: On May 12, 2008, between 10:40 PM and 10:45 PM, a witness observed a bright light object crossing the sky from south to northeast. This white and very bright object moved quickly, making a curve in its trajectory. No noise was heard. After investigation and confirmation by the French Meteor Observation Network (REFORME), this phenomenon was identified as the passage of the International Space Station (ISS) visible in Aubagne between 10:42 PM and 10:47 PM on that date, and it can now be particularly bright since the installation of the four pairs of solar panels (its magnitude can be significantly negative, down to about -4).
