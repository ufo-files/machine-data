--- seg-c7f12f5d45dabd5033635b89 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness seated on a café terrace observed the slow and silent passage of a peculiar spherical craft with fins moving from east to west and ascending vertically into the sky. The witness could not continue tracking the object as it was directly in the sun. No other witnesses were found. The object described by the witness does indeed resemble, as the witness states, an inflatable advertising module. The witness reported a west wind felt at ground level, which contradicts this hypothesis, but weather records in nearby Lille indicate an east wind, which would be consistent with the object's movement. It is possible that the wind felt in the square is very different from the wind at 100 meters altitude. The object's rotation and chaotic movements are not surprising for an object of this shape flying at the mercy of the wind. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of a helium-filled balloon, likely escaped from its owner.
