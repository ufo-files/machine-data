Description: On February 2, 2012, at 12:45 AM, several people were intrigued by a loud, deep noise that seemed to come from a dark mass with no lighting that was moving slowly and at a low altitude in the sky. The main testimony corresponds with the observation of a relatively classic, unlit powered flying object, possibly an unauthorized flight (dirigible, ULM, or possibly a helicopter) or a military exercise. (see investigation report) This case is classified "B": probable observation of an unlit aircraft.
