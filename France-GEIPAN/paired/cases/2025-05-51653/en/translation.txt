Description: On the evening of May 17, 2025, a resident (T1) of TOURNEFEUILLE (31) was at home when they observed, from a south-facing window, an intermittent light point in the sky. The object visually resembled an altitude airliner, but its movement did not correspond to a standard flight. It initially followed a straight trajectory, then abruptly made several right deviations before resuming a straight motion. Its brightness remained stable, although the blinking was irregular. When the object temporarily disappeared behind the roof, T1 moved to another north-facing window and continued observing until the object was no longer visible, likely due to its distance. Another person present also noticed the phenomenon, but did not provide a statement. With average strangeness and consistency (2 witnesses, but a single statement, no photo of PAN), this case appears to be a probable observation of a satellite (see the investigation report).

According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. T1's description of PAN aligns well with this type of observation, as PAN is a bright point advancing in a straight line. The described irregular movements can be explained by a perception illusion due to unconscious eye movements of the witness (autocinematic or auto-stasis) well described on the Misperceptions of the Sky website. At least two satellites passed on a trajectory consistent with PAN during the time slot indicated by T1, with a passage duration matching that of PAN. However, without a precise timestamp of the observation, we cannot determine which one was observed by the witness(es). GEIPAN classifies the case as "B", probable observation of a satellite.
