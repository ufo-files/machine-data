Description: Starting on March 8, 1985, and for several days, a witness will observe from the window of their 14th-floor apartment a light. This "star-like" light moves slowly. On March 18, 1985, while filming the light, it suddenly appears to head towards the witness. The witness becomes frightened and seeks refuge with neighbors. After investigation, it seems the witness observed Jupiter, particularly visible during this time of year.
