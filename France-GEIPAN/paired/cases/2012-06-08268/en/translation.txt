--- seg-18b60ea17dacae10f41d60c2 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 17, 2012, at 12:25 AM, a couple observed the silent passage of a luminous phenomenon consisting of red balls. The witnesses filmed their observation. This case is typical of Thai lanterns: orange or red balls moving erratically according to local wind (from southwest to northeast), released during the night from Saturday to Sunday. The video confirms this hypothesis (the video shows white objects, probably due to the white balance of the camcorder that should have been in automatic mode). The witness who witnesses a lantern release thinks they only move vertically, and did not accept this hypothesis. A press article from the Républicain Lorrain of August 30, 2012 reports that the author of this lantern release has been identified. The case is classified as A: observation of Thai lanterns.
