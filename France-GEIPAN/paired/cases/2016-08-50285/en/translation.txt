--- seg-5051dbc29c9bc504e83f47d7 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 25, 2016, around 5:30 PM, three witnesses outside were intrigued by the slow, low-altitude movement of a glowing object whose shape seemed to change during its movement. The PAN disappeared behind a hedge of cypresses. The witnesses drove to follow it but did not find it again. Photographs were taken. Only one statement was collected. The strangeness of this case is low and is mainly due to the unusual aspect of the PAN, which the witness could not relate to a known phenomenon, probably having never observed it before (see the investigation report). The statement is consistent, with enough data provided by the witness and exploitable. There are also fifteen photographs, which could be partially exploited for the case analysis. The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'A', as a very likely observation of a fantasy Mylar balloon carried by the wind.
