Description: On the night of August 2, 2007, around 1 a.m., two witnesses observed the movement of a green glowing ball. Spiraling downwards in its trajectory, the phenomenon disappeared from their view behind a hangar. No noise was heard during the observation of the phenomenon. No other testimony about this phenomenon in the area was collected. The IPN (French National Police Institute) investigation was unsuccessful due to the lack of contact with the witness. The characteristics of the observation suggest an unlisted meteor (duration = 2 seconds, green ball) but any investigation is impossible due to the lack of contact and thus information.
