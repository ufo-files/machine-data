Description: On the night of July 27 to 28, 1979, around midnight, a person is awakened by a peculiar brightness in their room. The witness then observes through the window a white glowing ball. It moves very slowly in a northwest to northeast direction without any noise. This ball is larger than the moon. It will disappear, hidden by buildings. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
