Description: On June 27, 2013 (confirmed by email) at 11:45 PM, a witness observed for 3 to 4 seconds the silent movement of three luminous phenomena in a clear sky. Two phenomena in rectangular shape are green in color, and the third is white. White or green glowing points crossing the horizon in a few seconds in a straight line could be due to the atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid, after fragmentation upon entering the atmosphere. To verify this hypothesis, the GEIPAN searched for other testimonies or video recordings on the Base des Observateurs Amateurs de Météores (BOAM) but nothing matched this event. Without overlapping evidence to support this hypothesis, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C".
