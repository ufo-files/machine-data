Summary: A long observation by a driver of a luminous phenomenon throughout her journey: mistaken for the Moon.
--- seg-d20bdac1912e0d63386c249a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Sunday, September 19, 2021, a driver was traveling on D986L to return home to MONTFRIN (30). It departs from REMOULINS (30) a few kilometers further north. It is approximately midnight when she observes a bright spot coming from behind the clouds in front of her. During its journey, this spot moves by oscillating back and forth across the road, always in front of the vehicle, at a distance of a few meters and at the level of trees, according to the witness's estimates. Upon arriving at the roundabout known as BEAUCAIRE (30), the motorist takes the third exit (heading north), and the "object" disappears behind him and moves away. When she arrives at the level of the parking lot of MONTFRIN (30), the "spot" is again in front of her, facing south. The observation lasted approximately 30 minutes, a picture of the phenomenon was taken at the very end of the observation. The testimony is precise and consistent, particularly thanks to the photograph. The witness was cooperative, responding quickly to the investigator's requests. The examination of the case file and the photograph leads to the conclusion of a misidentification with the Moon (see the investigation report). The Moon was in the line of sight of the witness at every stage of the journey and was never mentioned. Moreover, the perceived light is that of the Moon reflected on clouds. The movement perceived could have been caused by the slow change of clouds. The strangeness perceived by the witness is due to the impression that the PAN was preceding him and oscillating. This strangeness is explained by the respective positions of the Moon and the witness during his movement, the condition of the road, and the concentration required for driving. The UFO GEIPAN classifies the case as A: lunar mirage.
