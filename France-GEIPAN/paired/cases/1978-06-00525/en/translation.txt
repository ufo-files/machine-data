Description: On June 19, 1978, between 11:00 PM and 12:45 AM, several people heard a loud, deep noise and then observed a glowing object above the urban area. The orange-colored object was oval-shaped. Electrical power outages in the street were noted as the object was present. Gradually, the object disappeared from the witnesses' view as it gained altitude. Additionally, motorists on the RN113 heading towards Bordeaux and at the height of La Réole also observed a slow-moving luminous phenomenon in the stormy sky. The gendarmerie and GEPAN investigation was unable to clearly establish a correlation between these two observations due to a lack of precision and information. The witnesses likely observed a meteorological phenomenon related to the threatening stormy weather in the area.
