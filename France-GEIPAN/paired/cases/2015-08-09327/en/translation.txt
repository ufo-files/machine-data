Summary: Observation of several very distant bright phenomena at sunrise: Lack of information.
--- seg-2db32bc6193ecc15cb10ef59 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: This complex testimony describes a sequence of luminous phenomena in the sky, just before sunrise on August 12, 2015 at 6:38 AM. The witness specified during a phone conversation that their window was oriented precisely southwest (225°). The observed glowing circle appeared 3 times smaller than the Moon. The first observation could correspond to the flash (flare, reflection of the sun) of an Iridium satellite. The other observed phenomena could correspond to reflections of the sun on planes, as the proximity of sunrise is very conducive to this type of phenomenon. Since the observation report is complex and imprecise regarding observation directions, and the testimony arrived too late to allow checks with air traffic, the GEIPAN cannot confirm this last hypothesis. No other testimony was collected. This case is classified "C" due to lack of information.
