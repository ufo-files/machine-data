--- seg-6e198f65c5ab90a886024bfe [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Wednesday, July 3, 2013, around 10 PM, a witness, closing his shutters, observed bright objects moving in the sky in waves for 15 minutes. He took photographs. The observed movement towards the southwest is consistent with the local wind (see record). The repetition of the trajectories strongly confirms this hypothesis of objects carried by the wind. The appearance of the objects does not directly evoke the widely-spread Thai lanterns, as the described and photographed light is rather white (like the Moon, says the witness). It could rather be glowing balloons (LED) inflated with readily available helium for sale. Note that the observation at twilight modifies the perception of colors compared to usual nocturnal observations, and Wednesdays are less prone to festive releases than Saturdays. The GEIPAN classifies this atypical observation as "B": probable observation of helium-filled glowing balloons.
