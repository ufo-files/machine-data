--- seg-a8e528bbbc06f1463f82f657 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 30, 2008, at approximately 10:05 PM, two individuals from their 6th-floor apartment observed an unusual object to the left of the Eiffel Tower: a dark, lenticular mass, initially static, then moving towards Charles de Gaulle Airport, blending into the horizon before disappearing in a flash of light. The witness filmed their observation at 10:05 PM and again at 10:08 PM. With a low to moderate strangeness rating and strong consistency due to the video, this observation case strongly suggests a small balloon accidentally released into the Paris sky, especially due to its movement, which is entirely consistent with an object carried by the wind. Although the testimony does not fully align with this hypothesis (flash), this case is classified as B: probable observation of a balloon.
