Description: On July 20, 1985, a witness stated that he and others saw a peculiar luminous phenomenon above a group of trees. Around 10:45 PM, in a clear and starry sky, a shape resembling an upside-down plate topped with a dome emitted a light of white and yellow colors. This stationary object gradually disappeared only to reappear again. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted 40 minutes. The witness initially alerted a radio station the next day, which then relayed the information to local press. The gendarmes' investigation failed to confirm the young girl's statements on several points: one of the cited witnesses was not present, and the other did not confirm. Additionally, the then-current meteorological conditions do not match the witness's description. It appears that this testimony is not very credible and is likely the fabrication of a teenager.
