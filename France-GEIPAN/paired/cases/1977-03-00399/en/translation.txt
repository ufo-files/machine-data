Description: On March 18, 19, and 20, 1977, several people, including gendarmes, observed an unusual light in the sky starting at 8:30 PM. The light moved slowly and appeared to grow larger or smaller at times. Its color varied from white to orange-yellow. The light disappeared around 9:00 PM. The hypothesis of an astronomical observation could not be confirmed due to a lack of information.
