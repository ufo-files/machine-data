Description: On January 6, 1979, around 10 PM, an individual was walking around their farm with their dog. Suddenly, the dog became very frightened, started barking, and took refuge under a tree. The witness then saw a very bright, immobile object 15 meters high. An object in a circular shape, topped with a dome, emitting blinding lights and a slight whooshing sound. The witness observed the object for 2 minutes from a building. After a swaying motion, the object disappeared at an extremely high speed towards the West at 10:04 PM. No other testimonies were collected, and no ground traces were observed the following day. Based on the noise and swaying motion observed, the witness likely saw a helicopter.
