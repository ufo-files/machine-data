Summary: Observation of the silent movement of three orange light points: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-b00b695c350b84a74bbc73b4 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Thursday, March 20, 2014, at approximately 10:45 PM, two individuals observed three orange glowing points in the sky. These points moved silently but quickly in formation before disappearing successively. Only one statement was collected. The observation of orange glowing objects crossing the sky in a matter of minutes, in the direction of a light south wind (as indicated by evening weather reports), clearly resembles Thai lanterns. These releases most often occur on Saturdays or Sundays. However, spring or carnival may have provided an opportunity for a local celebration: the Clessé municipality received no reports of such events. The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'B': probable observation of Thai lanterns.
