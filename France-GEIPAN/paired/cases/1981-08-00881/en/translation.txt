--- seg-cc803a63e05f4bcb8be5189e [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 3, 1981, around 10:45 PM, several people observed a zigzagging object at an extremely high altitude. The trajectory was east-west and it was moving at a very high speed. The bright object emitted flashing white and red lights. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted less than five minutes. No explanation for the phenomenon could be formulated due to a lack of data.
