Description: On July 1, 2010, around 11 PM, a witness observed the passage from west to east of a luminous phenomenon. The movement was slow and at a constant speed, without any particular noise. The predictions for the passage of the International Space Station (ISS) had been calculated to make summer 2010 sky news on the Geipan website ( http://www.cnes-geipan.fr/geipan/actualites/01072010-Ciel-dete-Attention-aux-meprises-.html ). These predictions show that the witness likely observed the passage of the ISS.
