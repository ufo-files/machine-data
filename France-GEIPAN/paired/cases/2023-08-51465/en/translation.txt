Summary: Sky observations of a bright PAN with a shape resembling "two parallel structures like the rungs of a ladder": observation of a Starlink satellite train.

Description: On August 12, 2023, a family gathered on their terrace at their residence in CUXAC-D’AUDE (11) witnessed, at 10:18 PM, the passage of a PAN. Of the four present, only one took the initiative to report this observation to GEIPAN, describing it as follows: the PAN resembled "a ladder whose posts, perfectly parallel and identical, were connected by right-angle crossbeams; the lower ends of both posts and the upper ends were also connected, giving the object the appearance of about 6 or 7 perfectly aligned rectangles; without any difference in thickness between the posts and the crossbeams." The color of the PAN was uniform, "bright orange"; it moved in a longitudinal direction and crossed the entire sky without noise in about twenty seconds.

According to Geipan's criteria, the consistency of the testimony* is good, with a well-documented observation by the main witness (recommended postal with photos and precisely annotated drawings for localization), and during the investigation, confirmation of the observation by three people from the witness's surroundings. Despite the witness's statement in the GEIPAN questionnaire that the PAN is in no way similar to online images of Starlink, it should be noted that a train of Starlink satellites was indeed launched the day before August 12, with the deployment of 22 satellites [reference number G6-9]. Furthermore, it should be emphasized that this train of satellites was passing through the observation area of the witnesses at 10:14 PM, while they were scanning the sky in anticipation of shooting stars. The strangeness reported by the witness is due to their perception of the shape of the satellite train ("ladder shape"). A more conventional description of a satellite train was reported by the other witnesses contacted during the investigation. This observation is also corroborated by numerous other people present during the evening of August 12, 2023, during which the Perseids, a meteor shower, were visible. Some witnesses took photos of the event.

Given these elements, GEIPAN classifies the case as "A", observation of a Starlink satellite train.
