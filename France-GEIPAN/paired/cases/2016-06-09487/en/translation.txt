Summary: Observation of the slow and silent passage of a bright red/orange light point and disappearance behind trees in a forest: observation of the passage of a Thai lantern.
--- seg-c9eee072aa7b41d7b1299e6d [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 26, 2016, around midnight to 12:15 a.m., a witness observed from their window facing west a bright red/orange point of light crossing the sky. This point moved silently at a low speed towards the northwest before being hidden by trees. The observation lasted between 1 to 2 minutes. Only one testimony was collected in this commune. The gendarmerie investigation reports another observation of about thirty points passing in the same time frame that evening in Nanteuil-la-Forêt (51), very far from the observation location. This confirms the release of lanterns in different parts of France during the summer season. The witness's observation is characteristic of a Thai lantern passage, both in color, shape, and type of trajectory. The witness, looking west, describes a movement towards the north, rather slow, "It was moving at a low speed," "about 1 to 2 minutes before this light moved to the right"). This is consistent with the wind measured at 3 km/h in Hohrod (68) (see the Infoclimat record), coming from the north. The occurrence on a weekend night, favorable for festive events, strengthens this hypothesis. Releasing many night lanterns is spectacular but very expensive; the use of a single lantern remains very common, especially by individuals. Private parties are hard to detect as the people releasing lanterns do not always declare it to the authorities (municipality, prefecture). Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: observation of a Thai lantern passage.
