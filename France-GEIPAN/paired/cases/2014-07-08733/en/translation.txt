Description: On July 13, 2014, around 3:15 PM, three family members on the beach in Argeles-Sur-Mer (66) were intrigued by the passage of a dark, fuzzy disk moving across a cloudy sky. No trail or noise was observed during the 3 to 4-minute observation. The object disappeared as it moved away from the shore. Only one questionnaire was collected. An investigator was tasked to meet with the witnesses. Three conclusions emerge from this investigation (see the investigation report): 1. Consistency: This testimony is reliable, with no hallucinations or vision problems to consider given the presence of multiple witnesses. Furthermore, the quantity and consistency of the collected information is good. This case is therefore entirely consistent (0.8). 2. Strangeness: The strangeness of this phenomenon is relativized by the lack of external testimony from this family (0.45). 3. Hypotheses: Plausible explanation proposals were made, but we were unable to provide formal evidence. In conclusion, the GEIPAN classifies this case as Category B, a probable observation of a dirigible or free balloon (publicity or surveillance dirigible, or amateur balloon).
