Summary: Observation and photographs of a dark object spinning and then disappearing into a cumulonimbus: observation of a captive balloon or helikite (kite balloon).
--- seg-566368f71ccc8d087d2832e6 [needs-review] ---
Description: The witness, a passenger in a car traveling between Saint-Sauveur-Sur-Ecole (77) and Tours (37) on National Road 152 (now Departmental Road 1152), observed on November 1st, 2007, between 11:15 and 11:30, an object in the sky near Orleans (45). This object appeared to be a delta wing, but changed shape and entered a cloud nearby where it disappeared. The object was spinning and did not seem to be held by a cable. The witness managed to take five digital photos. The driver of the vehicle saw nothing. A child in the back of the vehicle saw the object, but will not provide a statement. The witness and his father, a former pilot, cannot explain the phenomenon. No other testimony regarding this PAN was collected. The witness reported the observation to the Gendarmerie; the GEIPAN questionnaire was not completed. The photos were analyzed by the Gendarmerie, which provided the following details:

- The object's appearance changed between the photos: dome, parallelogram;
- It was dark, almost black, with "metallic" reflections on its upper part, not visible in all shots;
- There was a cumulonimbus cloud, the witness specified that the object entered this cloud;
- No aircraft or parachute were found on the ground, and no accidents were reported;
- Air Base 123 in Brecy-Orléans confirmed that no air movement occurred at that level during that time;
- The Orleans departmental weather services specified that no meteorological air vehicles were deployed in the previous days.

**Hypothesis: Piloted Object**
The object's entry into the cumulonimbus cloud contradicts the hypothesis of a civilian aircraft (delta wing, light transport aircraft). Indeed, electrical manifestations, wind shears, and reduced visibility in a cumulonimbus cloud prevent aircraft from passing through. Additionally, if an aircraft had penetrated this cloud, it would have been carried by descending gusts and grounded, but no object was reported on the ground. Information from Air Base 123 rules out the option of a military aircraft. Therefore, the hypothesis of a "piloted object" is not supported.

**Hypothesis: Unpiloted Object**
The accepted hypothesis is that of a captive balloon or a helikite (a mix of kite and captive balloon). They can be used for scientific activities, photography, film production, and communication, among other things. The various shapes described by the witness and the Gendarmerie, such as a delta wing, dome, or parallelogram, are typically those of such objects like helikites, as seen in this video starting at 2'10": Helikites vs Other Aerostats Video. The apparent disappearance of the object into the cloud can be explained by its destruction, the object passing behind the cloud, and the witness perceiving the movement of both the object and the cloud in the same plane, or finally, the user bringing the object back to the ground using the tether, a movement not perceived by the witness as it was obscured by the cloud. The presence of this object at such a high altitude (2000m) in an urban area can be explained by a specific event allowing its presence (celebrations, etc.) or by the tether breaking, causing the object to leave its initial use area.

The GEIPAN classifies the case as B: captive balloon or helikite (captive kite balloon).
