Description: On October 5th and 6th, 1976, between 6:30 and 6:40 a.m., two witnesses observed a bright light in the sky that intrigued them. A luminous object in an oval shape, red-orange in color, appeared stationary to the north during the first observation and moved slowly east-west during the second observation. The presence of this object lasted less than a minute before the phenomenon suddenly disappeared. No other testimonies about this event were collected, and we have limited information about it.
