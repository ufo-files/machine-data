Summary: Sudden night-time observation of a green light beam in an apartment: probable observation of a manually operated green laser spot at a great distance.
--- seg-77884d3502bc4af286923947 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 6, 2015, between 12:15 AM and 12:20 AM, a witness watching television was surprised by the sudden appearance of a very powerful green light ray that penetrated his apartment. The phenomenon moved along the walls for a few seconds. The witness ruled out the possibility of a laser, being on the fourth floor with no line of sight. No other testimonies were collected. A very bright green spot that moves very quickly and randomly has all the characteristics of a handheld green laser spot operated by a prankster. This very powerful laser has a range of several kilometers. At a great distance, the movements of the light spot are of course extremely fast: a hand movement of only a few degrees by the operator translates into a significant beam shift over a kilometer. This type of laser is prohibited for sale in France, but unfortunately quite common. Users of these lasers face severe punishment if they aim them at aircraft in flight. The GEIPAN does not have the means to track down the operators of these devices. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a manually operated green laser spot at a great distance.
