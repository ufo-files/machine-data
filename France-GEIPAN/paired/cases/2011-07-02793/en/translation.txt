Summary: Observations of the silent passage of 7 red balls in the sky.
--- seg-07099844f31f86fcc95b2650 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: At 11:30 PM, several people observed from the terrace of their home overlooking the city of Limoux, the successive appearances of several red balls in the sky at intervals of a few minutes. No noise was heard during the slow passage under the clouds of these 7 balls in total. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon. The description of the phenomenon corresponds exactly to Thai lanterns. The date and time (saturday late evening), the direction of the wind (from West to East) which allows to bring towards the observer lanterns launched near Limoux, are as many elements reinforcing this hypothesis. Note: The witness notices that the clouds come from the East: if these are high altitude clouds, this is not contradictory with a ground wind of West-East direction.
