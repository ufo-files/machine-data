--- seg-3cb314ddc31f9ba31a7f6c18 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: While in a patrol car, the witnesses quickly observed a dark mass in the sky, equipped with two small lights that dimly illuminated. The mass suddenly moved at a breathtaking speed and disappeared. A field investigation confirmed the witnesses' observation reports, and several hypotheses were considered (see the investigation report), but none fully correspond to the observations. In conclusion, this is an unidentified phenomenon (PAN), observed by two highly reliable witnesses under ideal observation conditions, which seems to defy all hypotheses, particularly the explanation of a meteor (bolide) passage. However, uncertainty about the appearance trajectory of PAN, its brief duration, and the interdependence of the two witnesses who immediately discussed their observation prevent its classification as D2, in the absence of photography or video. The GEIPAN classifies this case as D1, an unexplained case with high consistency due to the two high-quality testimonies, and average strangeness (the materiality of the object is possible but not certain).
