Description: A witness reports an observation made on February 15, 2013 at 6:00 p.m. (local time in Martinique): for approximately half an hour, this person observes the slow passage in the sky of a bright, fiery-red light phenomenon. No particular noise is heard by the witness, who had time to take two photographs of the phenomenon. This observation was shared by numerous witnesses in Martinique, who were closely watching the sky following the coincidence between the passage of asteroid 2012DA14 and the meteor that fell in the Ural Mountains earlier that morning. It was the subject of a report on the ATV channel. Airplane contrails at sunset (or sunrise) are often orange, very bright, and contrasting against the dim sky at twilight. Certain weather conditions, particularly near the tropics, produce spectacular contrails, even http://contrailscience.com/short-sunlit-contrails-look-like-ufos. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the condensation trail of an airplane.
