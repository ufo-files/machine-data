Summary: Probable observation of a disco light projector
--- seg-ffec3503e8063e0091e37c19 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the 11th of August 1996, between 12:30 AM and 1:30 AM, a witness and his spouse observed a circular, glowing phenomenon moving slowly from right to left. The witnesses observed this phenomenon for approximately 45 minutes and went to bed while it was still present. The investigation will reveal that the direction of observation corresponds to that of a nightclub using a public laser projector. Therefore, the observed phenomenon was likely the effect of this projector on the clouds.
