Description: On March 10, 2014, between 7:30 PM and 7:45 PM, a driver traveling at 110 km/h on A63 was intrigued by the movement of a tubular object of black color detaching from the blue sky. The object had proportions similar to a "thick fountain pen". The witness observed a small green light beneath the object, which seemed to accompany it during its straight-line path. No unusual noises were heard during the continuous observation of less than three seconds. The object disappeared from the witness's field of vision, obscured by landscape elements. The observation lasted 1 to 2 seconds. No other testimonies have been reported. Based on the information gathered in the file, namely: - The PAN was moving against the dominant wind direction. - Its appearance was consistent with a plane seen from the side. - There was a fixed green light not in line with the direction of movement of a plane. We can only conclude that the PAN observed by the witness cannot be simply characterized. Due to its similarity to a plane and its straight trajectory, the strangeness of this observation is relatively low; the brevity of the observation, the uniqueness of the testimony, and the fact that the witness was driving a car weaken the consistency of this case. This case is classified as "C" because there are no overlapping pieces of evidence to support or refute any of the hypotheses (see the investigation report).
