Description: A witness reports two observations. On vacation in Aude around July 22, 2009, around 11 PM (the witness cannot recall the exact date), they observe in the sky the movement from west to east of a very bright, slightly oval sphere. Suddenly changing trajectory, the phenomenon disappears into space. Hypotheses such as ball lightning or meteors could not be verified due to uncertainties about the exact date and time of the observation. A 'meteor' activity occurred on July 25, 2009, around 11:45 PM local time, but does not seem to correspond to the observation. On September 24, 2009, at 7:55 AM at his home, this witness sees the north-south movement of a flying object in the sky that intrigues him. The object disappears quickly. A probable hypothesis would be the observation of a sunlit airplane. These observation cases are classified "C" due to lack of precision and information.
