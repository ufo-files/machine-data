Description: On September 22, 1981, at 6:15 a.m., a witness saw from the window of his room a flying object moving in a northwest to southeast trajectory. During the observation, which lasted one minute, the witness saw an elliptical-shaped object with blurred contours, having a kind of dome at its upper level. The object emitted a yellow-orange light. No other person in the vicinity was a witness to this appearance for which we lack information.
