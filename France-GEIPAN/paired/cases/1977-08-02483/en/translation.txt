Description: Recent testimony (12/07/2009) of an old observation: during the summer of 1977 (the witness cannot remember the exact date between June and August 1977), people observed a glowing object in the sky at night. Initially stationary, it then moved at irregular speeds, changing direction. It subsequently disappeared at high speed towards the West. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon. Any investigation today is difficult, if not impossible, on this phenomenon for which we lack information.
