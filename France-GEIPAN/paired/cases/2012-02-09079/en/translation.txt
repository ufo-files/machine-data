Summary: Twilight observation of a yellow-tinted, oblong luminescent phenomenon slowly traversing a straight path in the sky: probable aircraft trail.
--- seg-f1a9291c11f3fbace59e17c9 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Recent testimony (14/01/2015) of an old observation. On February 3, 2012, at 6:04 PM, a witness observed in the sky a luminous, oblong phenomenon, slowly and silently following a straight trajectory. The object disappeared after a few minutes. The observation lasted 2 minutes during which the witness took the time to take pictures. No other testimony was collected. The witness observed from the North/Northwest a very bright, oblong yellow shape moving slowly in a straight line. The sun had been set for approximately 30 minutes. This phenomenon corresponds very well to a condensation trail of a distant plane (>50 km) illuminated by the setting sun. A clear sky is enough to observe this type of phenomenon in the half-hour following sunset (or before sunrise). See an explanation of "strange aircraft trails" on page 3 of the news on the website www.cnes-geipan.fr. The GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable observation of an aircraft trail.
