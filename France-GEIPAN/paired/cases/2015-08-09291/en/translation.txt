Summary: Observation of a silent passage across the night sky of 3 red-orange circles: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-782f58d3e60c7e7f11dc0fe7 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 4, 2015, around 11 PM/11:15 PM, several people on a terrace observed in the sky two large, reddish-orange, circular lights moving in a straight line at the same speed and without sound. The objects passed over the roof of the house and were lost from view. A third object then appeared, with a light intensity that seemed to be decreasing. The witnesses walked around the house but the object was gone. Only one testimony was collected. The witness's descriptions (colors, shapes, grouped flight) strongly suggest a flight of Thai lanterns. The described movement has a strong north-to-south component (constant and strong elevation of 60° and an azimut varying from northwest to southeast), which is consistent with the north-to-south wind direction recorded very close to Blagnac (see the weather report). The different aspect (at the end of the observation) of the third object can be explained by the extinction (including slow, as described) of the lantern, causing it to gradually change from a bright round shape to a gray round shape. The latter is still distinguishable when the environment is not too dark, which is often the case in cities (a lantern flies at 50 or 200 meters altitude, not far from urban lighting). The lantern's descent (which can be rapid once it reaches the minimal heat threshold for its sustentation) may have occurred just after the observation when it became hidden by the house. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
