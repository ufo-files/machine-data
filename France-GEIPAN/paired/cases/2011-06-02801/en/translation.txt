Description: A witness reports observing the silent and straight passage of a very bright white ball in the sky on June 15, 2011, shortly after 11:30 PM. He cannot explain the observed phenomenon. A passage of the International Space Station (ISS) occurred between 11:08 PM and 11:18 PM, moving from West to East and visible from Aiglemont. Although the witness indicated that he had researched on the Internet and concluded that the ISS was not visible that evening (note: such retrospective research may be inaccurate), and that the time does not exactly match the witness's statement, the ISS hypothesis is retained as the trajectory and brightness closely match an ISS passage.
