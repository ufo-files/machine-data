Summary: Observations of the straight and silent passage of a luminous phenomenon in the sky: atmospheric reentry of a stage of the Soyuz launcher SL-4 R/B.
--- seg-ad801f634302b0b0aeaf7135 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 13, 2013, around 9:15 PM, witnesses in Alsace and Lorraine (including 6 people in the control tower of Strasbourg-Entzheim) observed in the sky the silent passage of a luminous phenomenon followed by a trail. The phenomenon moved on a horizontal trajectory. Additional testimonies will be collected later. These testimonies describe the passage of a meteor identified as the atmospheric reentry of an stage of the Soyuz launcher SL-4 R/B (registered 39083, or 2013-007B), launched on February 11 to orbit a Progress cargo spacecraft bound for the International Space Station (see the graph of the ground projection of the modeled trajectory of the object). Note that on that evening, only Alsace and southeastern France had clear skies; this explains why this phenomenon, though very spectacular, was relatively unnoticed in France. This case is classified "A" as perfectly identified as the atmospheric reentry of (large) space debris.
