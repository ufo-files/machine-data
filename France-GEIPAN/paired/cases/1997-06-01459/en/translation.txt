Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archives. Among its publications are older cases classified at the time as Type "D" cases, which are now being re-examined. This re-examination is not intended to diminish or increase any particular category of classification, but to make the conclusions more relevant. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over many years, this re-examination sometimes leads to new observations or even an explicit change in classification noted in an investigation report. This observation case is part of those being re-examined. On June 23, 2007, a resident of BEZIERS (34) reported to the gendarmerie that his son and daughter-in-law had observed a PAN for a long time during the night of June 21, 1997, around midnight. Driving on N609 from Béziers, the motorist and his passenger observed a flat, oval-shaped craft for 45 minutes; white, non-glowing lights radiated from the object. Throughout the journey, the PAN maintained a position above the vehicle, seemingly following it. According to the passenger, the PAN appeared and disappeared regularly. The witnesses heard no unusual noises. The PAN was approximately 50 meters above the ground. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. The gendarmerie's investigations were unable to identify an aircraft that could correspond to this observation. Furthermore, the two direct witnesses, residing in the Alpes de Haute Provence (04), could not be interviewed as they had returned home at the time of the investigation. Since the two direct witnesses could not be interviewed, there is no direct testimony to re-examine the case. It is only reported by a third person who saw nothing. The consistency of this case is therefore very low, as it is not direct testimony (see investigation notes). Consequently, in the current classification of GEIPAN, this case is classified as PAN C due to a lack of information (complete absence of direct testimony).
