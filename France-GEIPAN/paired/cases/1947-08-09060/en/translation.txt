Description: Recent testimony (December 2014) of an old observation in August 1947 (the witness does not remember the day; the date of August 1st is arbitrary). The witness was leading his cows to the pasture when he saw, very high in the sky, a bright object moving from east to west at extremely high speed, stopping abruptly for 30 seconds before taking off again at great speed and disappearing. This testimony was reported to GEIPAN in December 2014, over 67 years after the events. The witness's memory seems very precise, but experience shows that we should fear memory alteration over time. The reported observation resembles a Transient Lightning-like Phenomenon (T.L.P.), that is, a phenomenon similar to a ball lightning, possibly occurring during stormy weather (the observation is in August, a month often prone to storms), even if the sky is clear: sudden stops and high-speed takeoffs are sometimes observed in this type of phenomenon. However, the witness describes a phenomenon of cylindrical or parallelepiped shape, a priori incompatible with these phenomena generally spherical in shape. The antiquity of the case and the lack of precise date prevent verifications with weather services. Consequently, this case is classified "C" due to lack of sufficiently precise information (dates, weather).
