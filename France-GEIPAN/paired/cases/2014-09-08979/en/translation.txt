Summary: Observation of a straight-line passage of a bright point in the sky: Lack of information.
--- seg-7d6fdf5f1d04c4909d43c31a [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 1, 2014, at 12:55, a witness, attracted by the sound of a plane, observed a straight-line passage of a bright point in the sky. No other testimonies were collected. The observation of bright points crossing the sky in broad daylight can result from sun reflections on planes or satellites. Flightradar (attached) records numerous commercial aircraft flying over the witness from NNW to SSE, but not in the indicated direction of Northeast to Southwest. A high-altitude military aircraft not listed on Flightradar cannot be ruled out. The Calsky (attached) satellite overpass log for the area also lists a large number of satellites, presumably invisible during the day. At least four have northeast to southwest trajectories consistent with the witness's observation: SPOT4, 5, ARGOS, KOMPSAT. However, these satellites are not known to perform "flares" in broad daylight like Iridium satellites, though this is not physically impossible. Given these various possibilities that the GEIPAN could not eliminate or confirm, this peculiar case is classified C due to a lack of information and cross-corroboration.
