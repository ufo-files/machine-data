Description: On April 1, 1976, around 5:20 PM, several people observed in the sky a white streak that seemed to plunge into the sea. They then saw a gray smoke mass that persisted on the water for a quarter of an hour before completely disappearing. According to the gendarmerie investigation, no aircraft from the airport was in flight after 5:00 PM. It did not collect any object or debris on the coastline. It is likely that the witnesses witnessed the fall into the sea of an object, but we lack information to identify it.
