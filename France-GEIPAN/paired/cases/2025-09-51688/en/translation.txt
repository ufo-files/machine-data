Description: On September 14, 2025, several witnesses observed from their homes intermittent appearances and disappearances of bright lights in various parts of the sky. One observer was able to record a video during their last observation around 8:08 PM. This case of good consistency (3 witnesses, video of PAN) turns out to be an observation of landing lights from aircraft (see the investigation report). According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The description of PAN and its visual aspect are characteristic of this type of observation: white color, high brightness, immobility, low angular height, and an observation duration of a few moments. A precise reconstruction of air traffic confirms that the position of the observed phenomenon coincides with that of two aircraft approaching head-on at the time of the events. These aircraft were then in final approach phase, landing lights on, to reach Nice Airport. This explanation significantly reduces the perceived strangeness by the witnesses accordingly. The case is classified as "A", observation of aircraft landing lights.
