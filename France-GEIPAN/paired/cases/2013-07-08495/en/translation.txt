Description: On July 17, 2013, at approximately 11:45 PM, a witness observed the passage of a fireball followed by a trail on a descending oblique trajectory. The description of a "fireball" lasting 2 to 3 seconds perfectly matches a bolide (atmospheric entry of a meteoroid). The witness saw it to the East, moving from North to South. There was no automatic camera recording, but another similar testimony was received at GEIPAN, with a reported time very close: in Plaisir (78), observed in the South-West, moving towards the horizon (from 60° elevation to 45°). The observed undulation could be a physiological illusion of vision known as: autocinetic illusion (see: kinetic illusion). It is reminded that these bolide phenomena, often perceived by witnesses as very close, actually occur at altitudes of 100 to 50 km, and are therefore visible from the ground over an area of approximately 200 km in diameter. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a bolide.
