Description: Recent testimony (20/03/1975) of an old observation (the witness does not remember the exact date; the case is arbitrarily dated 01/08/1951). In August 1951 around midnight, a witness in Biarritz at the edge of a cliff was surprised by the passage along the coast of a flying mass shaped like two soup bowls joined together, with areas around its perimeter resembling portholes emitting a verdant fluorescence. The trajectory given by the witness is N-S then E-W. The object was seen for 10 to 15 seconds. Frightened, the witness jumped to the ground, dragging his wife who also saw the mass move. No noise was heard. The witnesses stood up but the object had disappeared. Only one testimony was collected. Some aspects of the testimony suggest a meteor observation: the duration of observation of the phenomenon, the feeling of proximity, the verdant fluorescence. However, some other details do not fit this hypothesis (flying mass, change in direction). The lack of precise date knowledge, the uniqueness of the testimony, and the significant delay between the event and the report make this testimony unreliable. The GEIPAN classifies this case as C due to lack of essential information and corroboration.
