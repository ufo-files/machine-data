Summary: Observations of the silent passage of a bright red-orange ball in the sky: lack of information.
--- seg-564bb199d95267fabf93d51d [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Monday, June 2, 2014, around 10:45 PM, three friends observed through a window a sphere of red-orange color passing at a constant speed in a clear sky. No noise was heard during the observation which lasted three to four minutes. This report of a bright light, appearing to be a ball of fire, for 3 to 4 minutes, suggests a Thai lantern carried by the wind. The Meteociel weather report for Besançon indicates a very light north wind. However, lantern releases on weekdays are rare, and the fact that this luminous object was unique does not allow this hypothesis to be confirmed. The unstable and weak wind does not allow either to confirm that the object is flying with the wind. The low strangeness of the case (similarity with a known phenomenon), and the delay of more than 8 months between the events and the receipt at GEIPAN, do not justify a field investigation. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C".
