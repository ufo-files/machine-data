Description: The witness sees two flashing red and white lights approaching her face in the night. The object turns right, and while the witness is getting a camera, it disappears. The witness's description of an aircraft with flashing red and white lights of course evokes an airplane. These very powerful flashing lights are quite blinding, making the green and red position lights (fixed, fairly weak) and the aircraft's fuselage imperceptible. Upon landing, the cabin is poorly lit. The Flightradar record notes that the TK1829 / Turkish Airlines Airbus A321 on approach to Roissy made a loop slightly north of Deuil-la-Barre (95), probably to wait its turn to land at Roissy. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of an airplane in a waiting loop for landing.
