--- seg-d31ae21148d576005d81b1b4 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: The witness observed a very bright, green light, followed by an orange streak, which crossed the sky in 3 to 4 seconds on February 22, 2014 at 8:10 PM. This phenomenon, called a bolide, was very likely caused by the atmospheric entry of a meteoroid. The press reported the event: they saw the meteorite in the sky, but no other witness reported the observation to the GEIPAN. Nothing was reported on the BOAM site (cameras), but France was under cloudy conditions except in the south-east. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": observation of atmospheric entry of a meteoroid (meteorite fall).
