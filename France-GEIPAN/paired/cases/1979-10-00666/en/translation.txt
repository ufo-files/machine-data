Summary: Observation of colorful glowing points in the sky.
--- seg-d0d03fecf2bf003d6d731f14 [needs-review] ---
Description: During the month of September and until October 4, 1979, a witness and his wife observed in the sky bright points that intrigued them. These points are visible on clear nights between 7 PM and 11:30 PM. They are red, green, and white and blink. All these points form a ball. The gendarmes, during various night shifts, did not see any similar events. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
