Summary: Observation of the movement of colored lights forming a PAN in the shape of a triangle with a deep, muffled noise: misidentification with a military aircraft, CUJAS95 type A400M
--- seg-caeba2f80d8fe5fd334e4ca3 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 25, 2021, at 10:15 PM, a witness is intrigued by the presence and movement of colored lights in the night sky. These lights form a large PAN triangle. A weak, muffled, and subdued noise is heard. Only one testimony is collected. The aeronautical context of the observation leaves no doubt about the explanation of the case (see the investigation report): - The witness was intrigued by the large size of PAN, as well as a noise and speed that he found curious. However, it must be known that an aircraft such as the CUJAS95 type A400M has a wingspan of over 42 meters for a length of 45 meters and a height of 15 meters.
- The map of the National Center for Air Operations (CNOA) as well as the FlightAware site confirm the nature of the PAN observed that evening around 22:10/15.
- Internet searches have allowed other witnesses to be found. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": mistaking it for a military aircraft, CUJAS95 type A400M.
