--- seg-41e07bb0306efe94bd299675 [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 29, 1977, at 9:15 PM and 10:15 PM, two individuals observed the slow movement of a cigar-shaped craft. This craft emitted lights of various colors and moved parallel to the Maine. Despite a ten-minute observation, the testimonies remain brief. A third witness, located by the gendarmerie, reported a three-minute observation on the same day around 9:00 PM, describing the movement of a red ball with a "tail" that eventually "faded away." The hypothesis of an atmospheric reentry was not confirmed due to a lack of information.
