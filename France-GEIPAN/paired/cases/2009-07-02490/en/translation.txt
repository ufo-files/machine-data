Description: On July 25, 2009, around 7 PM, several witnesses observed the passage of a white flying object. One of the witnesses, intrigued, described a white ball moving steadily north-south in the valley with a regular speed and height. The object suddenly gained altitude while accelerating and immediately disappeared into the sky. The slow movement of a white ball, without noise, the object's dimensions, the trajectory in the valley, and the acceleration likely due to wind capture suggest the probable observation of a balloon (of a weather balloon type, as described by the witness).
