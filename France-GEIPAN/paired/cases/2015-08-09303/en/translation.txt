Summary: Observation of silent movements of two orange lights in the sky: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-a1be432e703b17aa4dc8d41b [needs-review] ---
Description: On Friday, August 28, 2015, between 9:00 PM and 9:15 PM, four individuals in a garden observed two orange lights in the sky. One light appeared fixed facing West, while the second was moving from the Northwest. The lights disappeared one after the other towards the Southwest. No noise was heard during the one-minute observation. The witness had time to take four photographs. Only one statement was collected at the gendarmerie. The analyzed photo series (see the investigation note) shows, with a high probability, two Thai lanterns moving with the wind. The observation took place on a Friday evening, a weeknight conducive to weekend festivities (weddings, various parties...) during which such lanterns can be released. Consequently, the GEIPAN is classified as B: probable Thai lanterns.
