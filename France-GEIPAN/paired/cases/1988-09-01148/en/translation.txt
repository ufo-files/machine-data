Description: This observation case, formerly known as TRUN (61) 06.09.1988, is classified as 'D' by the SEPRA. It was recently reviewed (see the Investigation Notes document). Starting on September 6, 1988, several individuals observed in the sky an unknown, moving light phenomenon. Subsequent lengthy observations on September 7 and 8 took place between 10:00 PM and 11:00 PM. This case is consistent (four witnesses, multiple photographs), and the description of the PAN observed is accurate. The sincerity and credibility of the witnesses have never been questioned. The described phenomenon shares many common characteristics (observation duration, shape, size, color) with a perfectly known astronomical object: the planet Mars. Furthermore, Mars was visible in the direction indicated by the witnesses, and was particularly bright at that time. The issue is not the witnesses' visual perception, but the interpretation they make of their observation through their feelings (fatigue, fear, driving at night). In the current GEIPAN classification, this weak strangeness case is classified as PAN A, an observation related to a misperception with Mars.
