Description: On December 19, 2008, the GEIPAN received an email regarding an attached photograph: a week before Christmas, a witness observed and photographed the rapid and zigzag movement of a luminous phenomenon. The date on the photograph, extracted from EXIF data, indicates it was taken on December 19, 2008 at 21:24. Cases of observation on Réunion of the movement of Thai lanterns were reported to the GEIPAN on December 14 and 16, 2008. The witness will provide no further details. No other testimony of observation of this phenomenon that evening will be reported.
