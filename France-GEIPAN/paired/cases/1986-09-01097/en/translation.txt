Description: On September 26, 1986, around 9:30 PM, a couple observed a very bright ball for approximately 4 minutes. This ball, about the size of a bocce ball, moved from east to west at the speed of an airplane. After expelling red-brown projections, it suddenly disappeared. Despite the somewhat lengthy observation duration, this observation exhibits characteristics of an atmospheric reentry.
