Summary: Observation of a stationary and silent light phenomenon in the sky: lack of information.
--- seg-1d3f7f8419e7bded04ce2422 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 5, 2012, around 9:45 PM, a witness from their 6th-floor apartment observed a very bright phenomenon in the clear sky that obscured a dark "craft." This object remained stationary for about ten minutes without any noise being heard before disappearing. No other witnesses to this phenomenon were found. This observation is quite unusual, and it is remarkable that it was only reported by one person, given the high population density of the city. Following a gendarmerie investigation, which carefully verified information with aviation and security authorities, the GEIPAN decided to open an on-site investigation with the witness but they refused. Due to the impossibility of conducting this investigation, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of information.
