Description: The witness observes, in the starry sky, a bright point accompanied by a red point to its right, under which was a green point. These bright points move together and resemble an airplane until they separate and depart in opposite directions. The beginning of the observation (a bright point above the horizon, with red and green points) is entirely consistent with the perception of a star or planet with the twinkling due to the atmosphere. The second part of the testimony ("the two points separated, each going in an opposite direction") does not correspond to any known phenomenon, except for an optical illusion, following the object's disappearance. Of low strangeness at the beginning of the observation and average for the end, and of low consistency (single witness, inconsistencies and imprecision in the testimony), this observation case cannot be concluded due to lack of reliable information or cross-referencing. This case is classified as C.
