--- seg-d0601ea2e06890e5c2485c58 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of July 20, 2010, between 12:30 AM and 5 AM, three individuals were intrigued by a bright phenomenon in the starry sky, with a trail, appearing to move silently from O-E on a straight trajectory. Of average strangeness but very good consistency (three amateur astronomer witnesses, precise measurements, photo), the case appears to be a probable misperception with the beam of a ground projector. Numerous potential fixed spot sources exist near the observation location (buildings, summer commercial animation), and the sky was overcast at high altitude. The witnesses' visual perception is not in question, but the interpretation they make of their observation through their feelings (surprise, initial poor interpretation of the phenomenon, considered as a comet fragment, fatigue). This case is classified B, probable misperception with the beam of a fixed projector.
