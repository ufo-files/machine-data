Summary: Probable observations of Thai lantern flights.
--- seg-f8f4b93159084625fd739eb7 [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reports successive observations on August 23 and 24, 2009, during which he observes the movement of red light phenomena in the sky. Other testimonies will be collected. The witness's detailed description corresponds exactly to Thai lanterns. Although the witness states that their movement speed is not consistent with the wind on that day, this rapid movement can be explained by very local atmospheric movements: the atmosphere can be disturbed during summer nights for various reasons, particularly thermal ascents above certain buildings or fields.
