Description: In the garden of a friend, in the commune of Sacy-le-Petit (60), on August 10, 2023, the main witness (T1), accompanied by his wife and son, observed, between 4:20 PM and 5:30 PM (exact time undetermined), a well-defined white disk moving rapidly in the sky. The object followed a straight trajectory, rising from the zenith and disappearing over the horizon after approximately 7 seconds. No trail was present, and no noise was perceived by the witness. The observation was intermittent due to clouds intermittently obscuring visibility of the PAN. Two hypotheses were considered: that of a commercial airliner and that of a bolide (entry into the atmosphere of a solid extraterrestrial natural celestial body of varying size). Although a commercial airliner was in service at the time of the observation, the apparent speed of the PAN and the absence of a trail make this hypothesis unlikely. With average strangeness and consistency (4 witnesses, but unique testimony, no photos of the PAN, imprecise timing), this case appears to be a probable daytime observation of a bolide. The description of the PAN is consistent with that of a daytime bolide observed, notably due to the brief observation duration, combined with a very fast apparent speed and high altitude, as it was temporarily obscured by clouds. The observed round shape is relatively consistent, although bolide witnesses typically describe an elongated shape. The white color is frequently reported. The absence of a trail is not uncommon in daytime observations, as daylight brightness prevents its distinction, especially when the bolide does not have a significant mass at the time of its atmospheric entry. No corresponding bolide was found in AMS (American Meteor Society) and FRIPON (Fireball Recovery and Interplanetary Observation Network - automatic video capture systems activation) databases, which is not surprising for a daytime observation with even partial cloud cover. The lack of other testimony and the imprecise observation timing make further investigations difficult. The case is classified B, probable observation of a bolide. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.
