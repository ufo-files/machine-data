Description: On April 20, 2016, around 2:50 a.m., a driver on D786 before La Couture (commune of Erquy (22)) was surprised by a flash in the clear sky, then observed a large white trail with green material at its end. The observation of the object heading towards the sea lasted three seconds. No other testimonies were collected. As the witness considered, it was indeed an observation of a meteor, an event reported in several places in Normandy and Paris. See the IMO (International Meteor Organization) website. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A: observation of a meteor.
