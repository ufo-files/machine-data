Description: On Tuesday, February 10, 2015, at 9:05 PM, a witness observed for 5 to 7 minutes five orange balls seemingly approaching silently, with a brighter halo surrounding them. These balls disappeared one after the other. The observation of orange balls in the sky in just a few minutes strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The slight movement was observed coming from the northeast, with a perceived closing in, which is consistent with a northeast wind of 15 to 20 km/h recorded in Limoges (see the Meteociel report for the observation date). However, it should be noted that lantern releases typically occur on weekends; it's also worth mentioning that this day was during school vacation and conducive to carnival celebrations. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
