Summary: Probable observation of an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
--- seg-7c62b224dfe374b748c52fc1 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of April 16 to 17, 2010 at 1:10 a.m. near Bourg-en-Bresse, a driver and her daughter observed a very fast-moving bright phenomenon in the sky. During its descending trajectory, a green ball split in two and then disappeared. According to the information received (questionnaire, drawings, and photo of the view from the car), these individuals likely observed the atmospheric reentry of a meteor. This bolide was observed and photographed by the amateur station of Chaligny (south of Nancy) of the BOAM (Amateur Meteor Observation Database) network. The description (precise date and time of the observation, direction, characteristic green light of atmospheric ionization, movement and trajectory at high altitude appearing "parallel" to the horizon, distance greater than 100 km…) and the drawings of the observation by the witness correspond well to the BOAM station's record of the bolide.
