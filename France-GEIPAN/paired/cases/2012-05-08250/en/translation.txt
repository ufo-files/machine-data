Description: On May 30, 2012, starting at 9 PM, a witness observed from their balcony a dark, stationary object at a low altitude. The object irregularly blinked. The object remained in the same location until the witness' departure, who took the time to record a video with their smartphone. Given the objective evidence collected, namely: - Favorable weather conditions for the practice of captive ballooning: calm to light winds. - A lighting system consistent with regulations for the use of captive aircraft. - Suitable terrain for the use of a captive aircraft, at the azimuth where the object is visible. We can conclude that the observed object is very likely a captive balloon. This testimony is of good consistency due to the video, but it lacks essential data (azimuth, estimates of size and height...) that could, however, be partially reconstructed thanks to the video. The observation is not particularly strange as it concerns an object with ultimately banal behavior. This case is classified as "B" as a probable observation of a captive balloon.
