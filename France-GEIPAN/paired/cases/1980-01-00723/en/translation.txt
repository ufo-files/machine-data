Description: On January 27, 1980, between 21:05 and 21:10, several witnesses observed in a clear sky a bright orange ball of light. Located low on the horizon, it seemed to blink at times. The observation lasted 10 to 15 minutes for some witnesses. A similar observation occurred the following day. Verification of the sky map shows that these testimonies are likely related to the observation of Venus, visible in the southwest with a significant magnitude of -3.9 and setting on the horizon around 21:10.
