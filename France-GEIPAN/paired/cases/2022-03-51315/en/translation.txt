Description: On March 4, 2022, at approximately 4:50 AM, a resident of the commune of SAULTAIN (59) reported seeing, for about 90 to 120 seconds, a long line with lights that varied and were unstable along its length. This line moved regularly and moved away, giving the impression of ascending. A video was submitted via wetransfer on March 22, 2022, but the format was not recognized and the video was unreadable. The GEIPAN requested the video again via email on April 8, 2022, but no response was received. The case consistency is low: one unique witness and no photos or videos of PAN.

On March 3, 2022, at 2:25 PM Coordinated Universal Time (2:25 PM local time), SpaceX launched its 39th batch of 47 Starlink internet satellites using a Falcon 9 rocket from the Kennedy Space Center in Florida. The satellites were subsequently deployed one after the other, and in the hours following the launch, these satellites appeared "close" to each other and aligned, creating the illusion of a line of lights. Starlink satellites are regularly visible over France, particularly in the 15 days following their launch, until they reach their final orbit. For more information, see the GEIPAN website under "What I Saw" and the article on Starlink satellites on the Méprises du ciel website.

The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A": observation of the Starlink satellite train from the March 3, 2022 launch.
