Summary: Observation of the fall of a human-made object.
--- seg-7f9d08b7a469f007855d0e93 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 25, 1978, around 4:30 PM, three individuals witnessed the fall of a spherical or oval object over one meter in diameter, smooth and glowing. This multi-colored sphere ignited upon impact with the ground. The gendarmerie investigation found traces of charred soil and various fragments (spring, aluminum plate, rolled and burned "chatterton"). The nature of the debris collected at the crash site unequivocally indicates a human-made craft, its exact type remaining undeterminable (nacelle, balloon, kite). It is likely an amateur experiment.
