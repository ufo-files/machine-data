Description: On September 22, 2022, in the late afternoon, from their garden in Anthon (Isère, 38), two residents heard a high-pitched whistle. When they looked up, they saw a dark mass quickly cross their field of vision. The estimated duration of the observation was about one second. Only one witness accounts for this observation. The case consistency is judged as average: one witness, no photos of the PAN were taken, and it was a fleeting observation. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered from a witness account. The description of the PAN suggests, based on its shape, certain drone models (see the investigation report). The fact that the witnesses clearly perceived the high-pitched whistle emitted by the PAN strongly suggests it was close by. Moreover, the sound emitted by the PAN is very consistent with that of a drone. The very short duration of the observation does not allow for a detailed description of the PAN, but it was flying along the border of an area authorized for model aircraft, although flying over private property is prohibited. The case is classified as "B," probable observation of a drone.
