Summary: Observation of a glowing object appearing to follow the witnesses' vehicle: astronomical misidentification with Venus.
--- seg-febbedac94ea7e5f2bc3c6c7 [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 13, 1980, at 00:05, two gendarmes on patrol on the A1 highway spotted a luminous object above the Roye toll booth (80). Intrigued, they continued their route to a rest area parking lot and then contacted their station via radio, where another gendarme confirmed the object's presence in the sky. One of the witnesses took two photos of the PAN, but the film development proved negative. As they drove north, the gendarmes noticed that the PAN seemed to be following their car. The observation lasted nearly an hour. This observation case was classified as "C" in 1980 by the GEPAN. It is currently under review (see Investigation Notes). This case is consistent (2 primary witnesses and 2 secondary witnesses), and the description of the observation location and the PAN observed is accurate. The sincerity and credibility of the witnesses have never been questioned. The phenomenon described shares many common characteristics (observation duration, shape, size, color...) with a known astronomical object: setting Venus. Moreover, Venus was indeed present in the observed portion of the sky, something the witnesses did not mention. It is not the visual perception of the witnesses that is in question, but the interpretation they make of their observation through their feelings (pursuit sensation, driving at night). In the current classification of GEIPAN, this case of weak strangeness is classified as PAN A, an observation related to a misidentification with Venus.
