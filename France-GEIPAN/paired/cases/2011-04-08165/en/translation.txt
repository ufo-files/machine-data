Description: A witness observes from their balcony the movement of pollen from surrounding trees. Suddenly, a bright point quickly crosses the sky between two clouds. No noise is heard during the observation, and the point disappears into the cloudy sky. The witness observes, during the day and almost facing the sun, a white ball crossing the blue sky between two clouds. The witness noticed that there were many pollen particles flying in the air. It is probably one of these cottony pollen (pollen or seed?) that, facing the sun, became silvery. The movement in the direction of the wind (East wind of 15 to 31 km/h, southerly wind) confirms this hypothesis. (For information, the investigator has observed this phenomenon several times from their home, in May on southerly wind days). This case is therefore classified as "B": probable observation of tree pollen or seed (platanus?).
