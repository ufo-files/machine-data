Description: On the morning of July 28, 2013, at 3:20 AM, two witnesses observed from the first floor of their home two circular, flattened red-orange light phenomena. They moved across the sky on a slightly ascending, straight trajectory. The observation lasted one to two minutes, during which no particular noise was heard. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of several red-orange lights moving in a straight line, on a Saturday night to Sunday morning, for 2 minutes, strongly resembles the observation of Thai lanterns, likely released after a festival (over 400,000 Thai lanterns are sold annually in France). The weather report for Charleville indicates a weak, unstable wind: meteo The weather report shows a very variable wind at that time, which does not fully validate this hypothesis, but it remains compatible with the observation for now. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
