Summary: Observations of a stationary luminous phenomenon in the sky then disappearance at high speed.
--- seg-0ca8aeba2e1d13ecb23912ae [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 28, 1994, in the late afternoon, witnesses in Gard and Hérault saw a stationary luminous phenomenon. For some witnesses with cameras equipped with zoom, the object had a particular shape given by bright points. A less bright light could be distinguished at the center. The witnesses then saw the object split in two. The observation ended with the very fast disappearance of the luminous phenomenon. No photos were taken. No other information could be gathered.
