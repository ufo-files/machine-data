Summary: Brief observation of the movements of two superimposed spheres in the sky: observation of a Mylar-type fantasy balloon, carried by the wind.
--- seg-cedf32af72513b2914c95d58 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 17, 2021, at approximately 7:06 PM, the witness, with his friend in a car, observed the appearance of a flat light in the clouds followed by two superimposed gray spheres. The initially straight trajectory suddenly became perpendicular and then accelerated until it disappeared from their field of vision. Only one testimony was collected. The consistency is average, with two witnesses, but only one provided a statement. No video was provided, and the photo excerpt is not exploitable (see the investigation report). The investigation concluded that the observation was likely of a sophisticatedly shaped fantasy balloon, possibly of a "Mylar" type. This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- Fantasy shape, common for balloons of this type,
- The color changes from white to gray as the sun sets,
- No noise,
- Rectilinear movement in the direction of the wind,
- Disappearance by distance and loss of sight.

The main strangeness of the observation is the perception of the trajectory as consisting of two successive turns. These apparent changes in the trajectory were likely caused by two brightness variations induced by two successive rotations of the balloon on itself, alternately exposing a less reflective (and thus appearing dimmer) part and then a more reflective part, giving the witnesses the impression of a distance followed by a rapprochement.

The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A": observation of a Mylar-type fantasy balloon carried by the wind.
