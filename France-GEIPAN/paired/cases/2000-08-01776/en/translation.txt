--- seg-307ee0d8dd865d8e47ead0c9 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 1, 2000 at 1:20 AM, two people from an apartment observed in the sky a green, oval-shaped object moving very quickly to the southeast on a straight trajectory. The observation lasted 6 seconds before the object split into two parts and was hidden by the urban landscape. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. Based on the given indications, these people likely observed an atmospheric reentry.
