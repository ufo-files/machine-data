Description: On April 16, 2024, at 05:09 local time (03:09 UTC), from his residence in Trouville-sur-Mer (Calvados, 14) facing the sea in a northwest direction, the witness observed a luminous phenomenon moving along an apparent trajectory from west to east. This phenomenon, evolving just above the horizon at an estimated very high speed, abruptly disappeared after approximately 15 seconds of observation. The consistency* of the case is judged medium, due to a single witness and the absence of photos or videos of the PAN. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.

The hypothesis that the observed phenomenon could result from the reflection of a projector on a cloud base is confirmed by the following elements:

- Round, luminous shape, compatible.
- Yellow color, possible for sky-tracers.
- Horizontal and very low movement across the horizon, totally compatible with the presence of low clouds, which is confirmed.
- Very high speed, entirely consistent with a rapid and fortuitous movement of the projector.
- Short observation duration, possible if the projector is accidentally directed towards the sky.

The absence of a visible beam is explained by the combination of a very low elevation, limited observation duration, and exceptional horizontal visibility conditions resulting from an atmosphere particularly devoid of suspended particles capable of reflecting the projector's light.

Calculations suggest a probable use of the projector from a boat positioned offshore, although the investigations conducted did not allow for a precise identification of its origin (see the investigation report).

The GEIPAN classifies this observation case as "B": probable observation of a sky-tracer.
