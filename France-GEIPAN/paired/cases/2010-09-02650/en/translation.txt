--- seg-8705fcff58b162b388e0e16c [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 10, 2010, at 20:49, a witness from their home was intrigued by the silent movement of a luminous phenomenon in the sky. Of average strangeness given the possible sequence of misperceptions of different origins, and of good consistency thanks to precise geographic references on the sketch in 4.1, this observation case is explained for its first part by a misperception with the passage of the International Space Station (ISS), leading to a suggested partial classification as A. For the second part of the observation, new hypotheses must be introduced, namely a possible plane with its landing lights on, or a perception illusion (perception of 2 straight segments instead of a circle arc). These hypotheses, being unable to be confirmed, remain weak. The case is consistent (one eyewitness with a quality testimony), and its strangeness low, as it can be fairly explained with known phenomena. Given the uncertainty about the second part of the observation, this case is classified as "B": probable observation of the International Space Station (ISS).
