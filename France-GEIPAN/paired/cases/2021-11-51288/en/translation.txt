Description: On November 25, 2021, at 06:36 local time, during his early morning run along the Ourcq Canal near Meaux, the witness, alerted by a strange noise, looked up and saw what he thought was an airplane with unusual lights (like flames). The witness saw the light but not really the object, as it was nighttime. He observed a short, slightly zigzagging smoke trail about 1 cm wide. After approximately 4 seconds, the object seemed to catch fire on what the witness perceived as its left side, without any explosion sound, while continuing its straight trajectory. At the end of his run, around 07:30, on the Meaux beach in the Pâtis Park, looking towards the East, the witness observed a smoke trail in the sky. Only one testimony was collected. This testimony is of average consistency, one testimony only, no photos, but the witness answered the investigator's questions precisely (see the investigation report). The witness's description points towards the observation of a meteor. It turns out that on November 25, 2021 at 06:36:12 local time, a meteor was indeed observed and reported. Its trajectory matches the testimony perfectly, both in the overall description and in the range of observation directions, the direction of movement, and the duration of observation. The strange elements perceived by the witness, namely: lights like flames, a fireball, a smoke trail, can be explained by the meteor hypothesis. The only lingering odd noise perceived at the same time and which caught the witness's attention, this transient noise could come from the environment, notably one of the two railway lines located 1.5 km and 3.3 km from the witness. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A: observation of a meteor.
