Summary: Observation of the silent movement of a luminous phenomenon.
--- seg-f92f24448e7bc1bf4779b972 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 5, 1977, around 6:30 PM, two people in a car observed a red-orange light phenomenon that intrigued them. It appeared to come from a mass moving from southeast to northwest without any particular noise being heard. The observation lasted about ten minutes during which sparks were noticed. The phenomenon gradually and slowly diminished before completely fading away. On several occasions, people driving on the road taken by these automobilists noticed sparks coming from an electric line oriented from southeast to northwest. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
