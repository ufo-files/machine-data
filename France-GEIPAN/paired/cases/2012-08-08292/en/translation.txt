Description: On August 4, 2012, around 11 PM, a witness observed the silent, slow passage across the sky of five orange-colored oval shapes. The witness noted that the shapes gathered together before beginning a slow ascent. The witness lost sight of the shapes as their brightness gradually faded. This observation closely matches the sighting of Thai lanterns. The release of flying lanterns is very common on summer Saturday nights: many local festivals or weddings. Given the complex local mountain winds, especially late in the day in the summer, the wind observed by the nearby weather station (Geneva) does not exactly match the observed movement. The GEIPAN did not conduct a local investigation to identify the perpetrators of this release. The witness is reluctant to accept this hypothesis, putting forward arguments to which the GEIPAN has responded (see letters). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
