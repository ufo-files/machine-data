Description: The crew of an A320 flight from Lisbon to Orly observed on October 16, 2002, at approximately 7:30 PM UTC, the very brief passage of two very bright lights above their aircraft at a great distance, estimated at over 35 km. The trajectory was fast and descending. The observation is likely that of an atmospheric reentry, indeed identified as such by the crew.
