Description: On the nights of December 8 and 9, 1976, around 11 PM, military personnel observed for a few seconds several round, unidentified flying objects (UFOs) in the sky that they did not recognize. The UFOs moved slowly and silently from the southeast towards the northwest. About ten kilometers away, a dog handler noticed that on the same nights, his ten dogs had unusually barked at death. Another testimony was collected regarding an observation made six weeks earlier: a witness saw a red light rising into the sky at the foot of the ridges before disappearing behind a peak. Surveillance was established from December 12 to 30 following the military's deposition, but no other observations were reported.
