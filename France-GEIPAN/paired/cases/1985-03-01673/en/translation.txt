Description: On March 2, 1985, at approximately 10 PM, a witness and his entire family observed a flame-colored ball with a 1-meter diameter for 15 minutes. The object left trails of the same color as it moved slowly at an altitude of about 800 meters, following the terrain. No noise was noticed. No other information about this phenomenon could be obtained.
