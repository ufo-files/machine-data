--- seg-2e34ecf5e04ba42104015393 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 16, 2013, at 1:33 PM, three people were intrigued by the silent passage of a gray-metal object in the sky. Only one testimony was collected. According to the statements of the witness who observed towards the "Baou" (Baou des Blancs summit) located to the west of La Gaude, he saw the object fly from his left to his right, therefore from South to North; he saw it ascend. The observation lasted approximately 4 minutes. This direction of movement is compatible with a South wind blowing at around 18 km/h. If it is an object carried by the wind, it would have traveled a little over 1 kilometer. If we take the hypothesis of a 2-meter balloon (e.g., a solar balloon), the geometric analysis of the photo positions it at approximately 700 meters (see schema). It could also be 4 meters and 1.4 kilometers away. The origin of this balloon could not be determined by the GEIPAN: no notable events in the South environment of the witness. It could be an experimental object made by an amateur, or a festive object (one can imagine the shape of a "1"). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of an unknown origin balloon.
