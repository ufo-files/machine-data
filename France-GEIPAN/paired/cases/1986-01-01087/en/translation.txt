Description: On January 3, 1986, as night fell, a couple observed from their vehicle a large ball that descended gradually and slowly towards the horizon. From this orange-colored ball, black smoke was escaping. This observation lasted between 2 and 3 minutes without the object changing direction. No other information was gathered about this phenomenon.
