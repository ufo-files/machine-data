Summary: Observation of 9 bright points forming triangular patterns: Insufficient information.
--- seg-71babfa69b6effbf86f9ca17 [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reports a five-second observation made on July 31, 2008 at 23:15 in a private garden. The witness observes nine non-flashing lights moving silently from East to West across the starry sky. These points, grouped in three triangles, delineate a triangular formation that suddenly begins an upward motion before disappearing. No other testimony about this phenomenon will be collected. The witness, who clearly saw a strange phenomenon, was very impressed by their observation. The lights' movement does not conform to wind direction, ruling out the Thai lantern hypothesis. This case was submitted to the GEIPAN experts. The testimony contains few concrete elements, and there is only one witness, making it a case of low consistency. Consequently, this case is classified "C": non-exploitable observation due to lack of information.
