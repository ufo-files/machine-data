Summary: Possible observations of an atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-1247eadf2e84e0881b8e40c2 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 15, 1983, between 5 a.m. and 6 a.m., witnesses in several departments (Yonne, Seine-et-Marne, Paris, Essonne, Seine-Saint-Denis, and Pas-de-Calais) observed a peculiar phenomenon. All witnesses saw an intense, colored light mass pass at high speed. No noise was noticed by the witnesses during the observations, which lasted approximately ten seconds. These individuals may have witnessed an atmospheric reentry.
