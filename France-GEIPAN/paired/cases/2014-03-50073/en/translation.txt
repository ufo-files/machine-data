Description: On February 6, 2014, at 9:15 PM, a witness closing their shutters noticed several bright objects silently moving across the sky. The observation of bright objects succeeding each other in the sky, in the direction of the wind, evokes Thai lanterns. The witness observed the objects flying from north to south. The Meteociel report for Bordeaux indicates a northwest wind of 22 to 33 km/h, partly cloudy (5/8), fair weather. Given the wind direction, these lanterns could have been launched from the Chartrons quay. The Tet holiday was celebrated in Bordeaux on February 8. The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
