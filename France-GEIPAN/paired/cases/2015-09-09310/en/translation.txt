Summary: Observation of unusual movements of a bright point in the sky: probable misidentification with a satellite coinciding with the observation of a meteor.
--- seg-a6792399865bc08fa249e44f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 06, 2015, around 10:45 PM, a witness observed from their garden a bright point moving across the sky from South to North, initially mistaking it for a satellite. The PAN paused for a few seconds, resumed its trajectory, then made a half-circle in the opposite direction. After another stop, the PAN disappeared at high speed. Only one testimony was collected. Of average strangeness but low consistency (single witness, no photography, missing details), this case appears to be a possible misidentification with the observation of an artificial satellite passing vertically over the witness, immediately followed by a meteor likely belonging to the Kappa Cygnides swarm. However, the witness's lack of precision prevents formally confirming this hypothesis (see the investigation report). The unusual and immediate sequence of a satellite pass and a meteor could have been interpreted by the witness as the movement of a single, unique object. Furthermore, the pauses and rotations of the PAN described by the witness may be a misinterpretation of the observation, caused by an especially uncomfortable observation position, as the event occurred vertically. This case is classified B, probable misidentification with a satellite coinciding with the observation of a meteor.
