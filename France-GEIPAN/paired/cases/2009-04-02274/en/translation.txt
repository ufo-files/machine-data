Description: On April 9, 2009, in the early afternoon, four witnesses at an airport observed the slow movement of white, spherical objects in the sky. No noise was heard during the observation. The phenomenon gradually faded from view. Following the testimony of two individuals and an investigation, no formal explanation for this observation was found, but the possibility of scientific balloons is favored despite a lack of confirmation from Swiss weather services and laboratories. The case is classified as "B."
