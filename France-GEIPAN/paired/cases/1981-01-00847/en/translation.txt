--- seg-bdd0dc099b2fa1376727813f [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 1, 1981, around 9:40 PM, two police officers witnessed a luminous phenomenon that intrigued them. Between 100 and 150 meters in the sky, a yellow-orange glow flickered. As they observed the light's progression, they saw a circular craft spinning and descending at a steady, slow, and silent pace. The craft emitted blinking lights and left a short trail behind. The observation, which lasted about a minute, ceased when the object was obscured by the landscape. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
