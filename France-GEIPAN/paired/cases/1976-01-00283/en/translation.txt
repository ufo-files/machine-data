Description: On January 17, 1976, around 6:00 PM, a farmer observed an unrecognized craft moving slowly from east to west above the woods. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted about a minute. The witness noted the activation of a powerful beacon that scanned the plain. The witness left to get his son, but upon their return, the craft had disappeared. Despite a gendarmerie investigation, no other testimonies or information were gathered.
