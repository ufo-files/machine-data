Description: On May 29, 1979, around 11:30 PM, a driver traveling on a rural road was surprised to see a bright orange crescent-shaped object approaching him. This low-altitude object seemed to follow the driver as he moved northwest. No noise was heard. The object disappeared from view. The following day, the witness returned to the same location and noticed the presence of the same object, but this time it was white. It disappeared behind the trees heading northwest. The sky map for that evening indicates the presence of the Moon at low altitude and in the northwest with a magnitude of -8.91: this person made an astronomical observation of the Moon. The sensation of its movement when one is in motion is well-known.
