--- seg-9df27a22a59bc2ce2d685269 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 7, 2015, at 11:15 PM, a witness observed in the sky a particularly bright spherical object that intrigued them. The PAN moved on a descending vertical trajectory. The observation lasted for an hour and stopped when the PAN was obscured by clouds or fog. Only one testimony was collected. The witness reported observing the PAN in the east-southeast, "towards St. Martin Basilica," which is contradictory. That basilica is located northwest of their position (see the investigation report). Based on the objective evidence gathered, we can conclude that this case should be classified as 'A' as a certain observation of Venus, located very low on the horizon.
