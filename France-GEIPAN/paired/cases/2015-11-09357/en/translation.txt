Description: On November 10, 2015, at 7 PM, a witness outside their residence had their attention drawn to a bright light in the clear sky. The witness observed for 2 to 3 seconds the straight and silent passage of a luminous object with two vivid colors (fluorescent green and pastel orange). The object, at the height of the top of a pine tree at the back of the garden, then passed over the house and disappeared from the witness's sight. No trail was noticed. Only one testimony was collected. The observation is strange. The possibility of a drone cannot be ruled out, but it would still be surprising given the time (darkness) and the isolated nature of the few nearby homes. On the other hand, the level of information about the observation is not sufficient in light of its strangeness and does not allow for a proper investigation (the fleeting nature of the observation (3 seconds), lack of additional information (photo, other testimony)). Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case in category C: lack of information.
