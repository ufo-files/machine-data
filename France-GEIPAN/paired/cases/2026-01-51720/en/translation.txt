Description: On January 5, 2026, at 6:13 PM, in Colmar (68), from his home, the witness observes a very intense white light point placed above the crest of the Vosges mountains. The object, apparently the size of a star, is at a very low altitude relative to the terrain and displays frequent changes in direction, with deviations to the south, producing fine light trails. The observation continues continuously and is recorded via a smartphone through a window. The phenomenon suddenly disappears. The witness is unable to identify the source and suggests it could be a drone, balloon, or other aerial device. No other witnesses come forward. The consistency* of the testimony is considered satisfactory: an exploitable video provided by the sole witness strengthens the consistency of the testimony despite the limited number of statements (see the report). *CONSISTENCY: According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The witness observed the phenomenon with the naked eye for 1 to 2 minutes, then filmed it with his phone for 3 minutes and 44 seconds, always from the inside through a double-glazed window. At first, the object was almost motionless. Ten seconds into the recording, the witness activated a strong zoom which he maintained for 3 minutes and 34 seconds. Prolonged use of the phone's zoom on a screen requires constant concentration to follow the light: any tremors of the hand then create the illusion of a jerky trajectory and light trails. Analysis of the video frame by frame demonstrates that it is solely technical movements, the light source returning to its normal shape as soon as the movement stops. By combining these camera movements with the phenomenon's very slow speed, its colorimetric signature, and its evolution in a restricted field, the hypothesis of a lantern becomes compelling, even though the release was not found or claimed. Given these data, the GEIPAN classifies this case as 'A': very probable observation of a celestial lantern.
