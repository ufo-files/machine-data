Description: On May 26, 1980, around 10:45 PM, several witnesses, including gendarmes, observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky. Two red, circular objects descended slowly towards the ground at a constant speed. Then, red lights appeared rising into the air before disappearing. Some witnesses reported seeing reddish smoke. The investigation revealed that individuals launched distress flares for boats that evening, without malicious intent, because they were no longer up to standard and could not be used at sea.
