Summary: Observations of a bright light in the sky: likely astronomical observations of Mercury
--- seg-3558674beb3d52024f55f3fb [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 14, 1980, at 4:45 a.m., witnesses observed a low-lying bright light on the horizon. Emitting a sparkling yellow light, moving very slowly, this phenomenon was observed for several minutes before fading away in intensity and volume. During the gendarmerie investigation, other witnesses were found: it is likely that these individuals made an astronomical observation of Mercury, which was particularly visible during this time of year.
