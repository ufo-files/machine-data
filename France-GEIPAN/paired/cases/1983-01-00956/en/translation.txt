Summary: Ten-minute observations of the silent passage of a gray-black "V"-shaped phenomenon: likely misidentification with an aircraft.
--- seg-4674d550df3a1568f8fca3d0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 23, 1983, between 5:15 PM and 5:30 PM, several witnesses observed a gray-black phenomenon in the sky at an estimated altitude of 2500 meters. The object was elongated or in the shape of a "V" with no shine. It moved horizontally and silently. Observation through binoculars did not allow for object identification, but according to one witness, a former air navigator, it resembled a trail left by a Boeing 747. The "V" shape and the estimated speed (500 to 600 km/h) by the former navigator could support the hypothesis of a plane seen from behind during a descent. It is likely that the witnesses were victims of a misperception with an aircraft, but the latter could not be precisely identified.
