Summary: Unidentified radar echoes from a patrol boat in the Pacific Ocean.
--- seg-882b3b9a8e5b8b3f1dc9ca49 [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 26, 1979, at 2:30 local time, two witnesses on a patrol boat observed for 2 to 3 minutes a group of four radar echoes side by side, forming a large echo with clear contours on a V 22 radar. The nature of the echo does not correspond to weather interference or multiple reflections from hulls. The vessel was navigating in the North Pacific Ocean, north of the Chimborazo volcano in Ecuador.
