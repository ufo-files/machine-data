Summary: Two successive observations of two flashes in the night sky: very likely misperception with two satellite flashes.
--- seg-2e4ffeca816a28923613e4da [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 21, 2015, at 2:00 AM, a witness observing the stars noticed a flash intensifying then diminishing. He observed the same effect two minutes later. Only one testimony was collected. Due to its low strangeness and consistency (single witness, no photos), the case appears to be a very probable misperception with two satellite flashes (see the investigation notes document). The PAN indeed presents all typical characteristics of such a phenomenon: increasing then decreasing brightness, slow flash, static position in the sky. Furthermore, several uncontrolled satellites known for displaying powerful flashes passed through a nearby area to where the PAN was observed. However, given the lack of precision in the testimony, it is not possible to formally identify the satellite(s) responsible for the observation. This case is classified B, very probable misperception with two satellite flashes.
