Description: On Tuesday, October 29, 2024, around 5:35 PM, the witness, alerted by his dog, observed and filmed with his phone a PAN (Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon) using his phone. The PAN moved from its left to its right on a southwest to northeast trajectory, then distanced itself towards northwest. The consistency* is good, despite only one witness, as there are photos and videos, and an interview with the witness. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. Two hypotheses are considered: one of a wind-carried object, and one of a radio-controlled drone or helicopter (see the investigation report). The hypothesis of a radio-controlled drone or helicopter, which could correspond to the trajectory, does not match the shape of the phenomenon. Furthermore, despite the proximity of the PAN, no noise was perceived by the witness or local aeromodeling club officials, who confirmed never flying in that area. The hypothesis of a wind-carried object is retained. Its movement is consistent with the wind direction indicated by weather reports. Its shape and color are entirely compatible with a fantasy balloon with a mylar envelope. Its movement (rotation, ascents and descents) with the wind is understandable in this case where a dissymmetric shape of the envelope is detected on the video produced by the witness. The investigation did not allow the origin of the balloon release to be found. The GEIPAN classifies the case as 'A': observation of a fantasy balloon with a mylar envelope carried by the wind.
