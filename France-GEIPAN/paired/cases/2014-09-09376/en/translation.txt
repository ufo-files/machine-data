--- seg-61247635d44d28d0ee32c2b0 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Recent testimony (12/18/2015) of an old observation made on September 12, 2014, between 6 a.m. and 7 a.m. On that morning, the witness's attention was drawn to a glowing point moving in circles in the sky before disappearing in a straight line into a cloud. The object is oval-shaped, gray-white in color. The witness had time to photograph the PAN. No other testimony was collected. Analysis of the photos shows a bright shape followed by a light white trail: the hypothesis of observing an airplane was prioritized, with the following elements: - At sunrise and sunset, airplanes are illuminated laterally: the wing planes are in shadow, less bright than the blue sky background, and are therefore invisible. It also happens that the sun reflects brightly on a part of the fuselage or windows, and creates a very bright point. The resulting shape, visible to the eye or in a photograph, can then become a cigar, an oval, or a trapezoid. - The white trail is characteristic of an airplane. Note that the movements described by the witness are surprising for an airplane. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation and photography of an airplane at sunrise. NOTE: The witness states that he/she took the photographs around 6:00 AM on 09/12/2014. The time noted on the photo is 8:16 PM: The witness will specify that his camera was not set to the correct time, and it might have been a little later. Given the brightness of the sky, it must have been around 6:50 AM.
