Summary: Probable observation of light effects due to lasers.
--- seg-616c158ef852d90262fb3545 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 11, 2010 at 8:15 PM, a witness observed a luminous phenomenon consisting of three white lights rotating in the sky, successively appearing and disappearing, from their car. The phenomenon abruptly disappeared. The observation of three white lights rotating in the sky was made by a single witness who compared them to laser lights from a disco. The direction of observation corresponds to that of a 'night club' where a 'fluo night party' was scheduled for February 12. A letter regarding possible laser functionality tests or a laser animation on February 11, 2010, was sent. Due to the lack of response from the 'night club' to this question, this observation without a gendarmerie report could be classified as C due to a lack of information. However, the witness's strong conviction in observing a 'laser-like luminous phenomenon', without being able to provide formal proof of this hypothesis, allows this case to be classified as B, as a probable observation of disco lights.
