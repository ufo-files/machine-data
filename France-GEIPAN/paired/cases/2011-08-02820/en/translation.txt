Summary: Observation of a glowing phenomenon that disappears then reappears in the sky: probable observation of aircraft.
--- seg-f7ea0b48796fa837b5850646 [failed-protected-token-check] ---
Description: After a submarine dive at 10:00 AM on August 20, 2011 at the "Punta d'Arco" site in the Bastia region, witnesses were surprised to observe for five minutes in the sky a stationary luminous phenomenon that suddenly disappeared and then reappeared with a different form, accompanied by a small light to its right. The phenomenon then disappeared definitively. The investigation revealed that on that day, there was intense air traffic in the observed area. The witnesses, who were facing the sun, likely saw successively several planes pass by, which would only have been visible for the brief time they reflected the sun, or when their landing lights were directed towards the witnesses. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of planes.
