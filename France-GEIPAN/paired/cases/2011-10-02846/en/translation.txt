Summary: Observations of a probable passage of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-940019d44c90899b55ea5d0e [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 1, 2011, around 10:30 PM, witnesses observed the silent movement in the sky of several yellow-orange glowing orbs. One witness recorded video from his smartphone. After analyzing the video, it appears that the light points move slightly relative to each other. The weather that day indicates a very light wind whose direction changes hourly; it is not possible to verify if the movement is consistent with the wind. The overall appearance of the video, the accounts of the witnesses, the time and date (Saturday evening), lead to favoring the hypothesis of Thai lanterns. No search for a possible launch site was conducted: there are too many possibilities in the peri-urban area. This case is therefore classified as "B": probable passage of Thai lanterns.
