Description: On December 4, 2011, the GEIPAN received by email a Land Questionnaire (LQ) from a witness of an orange light in the shape of a saucer observation on the night of September 24 to 25, 2011. As the witness contacted us nearly four months after the event, filing a report with the Gendarmerie was no longer possible. The observation lasted between 10 and 15 minutes and took place in the presence of another witness initially, then three other people who were able to see the end of the observation as the object moved away. However, none of the other witnesses provided a report to the GEIPAN. Although the Thai lantern hypothesis is favored, the usual clues reinforcing this hypothesis are not strong enough: the wind direction is imprecise, and the description of the object's movement leaves room for doubt in its interpretation. The helicopter, likely taking off from the hospital, only drew the witness's attention to the sky. Unremarkable and inconsistent (one witness, not very precise testimony), this case is classified as 'C' due to a lack of information or other testimonies.
