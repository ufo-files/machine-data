Description: A walker in the Fontainebleau forest reports observing on August 22, 2009, at approximately 10:15 PM, a red glowing point at a 30° angle above the horizon. The witness then noticed the object's movement before it disappeared. The observation lasted about 6 minutes. The witness did not respond to the various letters sent to them, thus providing insufficient information for the GEIPAN to conduct an investigation. This case is classified as C due to a lack of information.
