Description: On September 11, 2015, around 3 a.m., two people in a car were intrigued by a white glowing phenomenon in the sky. The witnesses stopped and the object changed direction while emitting a humming sound. The witness took three photos. Only one testimony was collected. The case contains elements of strangeness. The stable glowing ball in the sky eventually moved and departed with a humming sound. There is no radar recording. One hypothesis could be a helicopter in maneuver (not seen by the local gendarmerie radar due to its low altitude), although this seems unlikely at 3 a.m. in a rural area without knowledge of the local gendarmerie. Furthermore, the case contains inconsistencies in the testimony, the first being that the witness did not respond to repeated requests for a meeting for an on-site investigation. Previously, he also did not respond to requests for clarification on the PAN's position in the sky, nor to the request for a testimony from the witness's spouse who was also present in the vehicle. An on-site investigation would have allowed the testimony to be completed and perhaps resolve the elements of strangeness. Consequently, the GEIPAN is classified as C: no response from the witness.
