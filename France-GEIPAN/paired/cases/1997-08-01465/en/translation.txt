Summary: Observation of two successive luminous phenomena by the crew of a sailboat
--- seg-1c3de8fac88432566a10511c [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of July 3rd to 4th, 1997 at 4:00 a.m., a witness observing from the deck of their sailboat saw a bright light descend rapidly for 2 to 3 seconds, stabilize for a moment, then quickly depart horizontally and disappear in approximately 2 to 3 seconds. The witness called their two sleeping crewmates to share their observation when they witnessed a second phenomenon, a yellow ocher diamond-shaped object with lights on its peaks. The object moved very quickly at cloud level and disappeared after 10 to 15 seconds. The observed phenomena remain unexplained.
