Description: A witness observed two distinct phenomena in the Bordeaux sky on two separate occasions: first, in the evening of August 3, 2011, starting at 9:00 PM; and second, in the night of August 6-7, 2011, at 3:15 AM. The second observation, which closely resembles a misidentification with Thai lanterns, will not be addressed in this report. Regarding the first observation, while the movement of the PAN appears consistent with the wind (assuming correct interpretation of positions and directions), its appearance does not suggest anything unusual. A hot air balloon, no matter how unique, should have been identifiable, especially during sunset and without clouds. Our best hypothesis remains the possible observation of a lenticular cloud, a rare phenomenon and therefore inherently surprising. However, sunset should have revealed its nature, unless one considers that the horizon was obscured by clouds. Of average strangeness but too weak in consistency to conclude, this case is provisionally classified as C, awaiting new elements.
