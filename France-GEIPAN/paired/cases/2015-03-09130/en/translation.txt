Description: On March 12, 2015, at 7:45 PM, a witness observed two lights, first stationary, then the first took a direction and disappeared behind buildings, soon followed by the second which followed exactly the same trajectory. The witness observed from Courbevoie, at 85° to the right of the Total Tower in La Défense (see map), which gives a northwest direction. A check of civil air traffic (see Flightradar record) shows that at this exact time, flights AFR1159 and AF1281, descending towards Roissy, were in the witnesses' line of sight, then these flights slightly turned towards the north. The video taken by the witness fully aligns with this explanation. It is noted that an airplane seen preparing to land, therefore seen face-on, can be seen over 20 km away thanks to its landing lights, which make the blinking and position lights (green and red) nearly invisible to the eye. When the airplane's axis is no longer in the observer's direction, these powerful lights, which have a relatively narrow beam, are no longer visible by the observer. See the article on airplanes? The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of descending airplanes.
