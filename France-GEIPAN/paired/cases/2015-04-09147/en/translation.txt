Summary: Long observation of bright points in the sky moving rapidly: observation of a series of passes by military aircraft training for night flight.
--- seg-5c985d2f9a751a9a64a8c747 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 7, 2015, at 10:30 PM, a witness spotted several bright points in the sky that appeared to move very quickly. The phenomenon remained visible for over an hour, after which the witness stopped observing. She submitted her video to the Sud-Ouest newspaper. A northwest wind of 20-30 km/h was recorded in Cognac: weather. The GEIPAN inquired with the military air control, who provided a series of radar traces of aerial activities corresponding to "circuit track" turns at the Cognac-Chateaubernard military base (see the map showing the aircraft's trajectory). The Air Base 709 (Cognac-Chateaubernard) confirmed a significant volume of night flight training that evening (the same evening of the observation, the witness had inquired at this same base about traffic that evening, and received a negative response: the interlocutor may not have been qualified to answer the public about military activities). The GEIPAN classifies this case as category "A", as perfectly identified by a series of passages of military aircraft training for night flight.
