Summary: Observation of varied lights likely from a nighttime auto race
--- seg-564c74583f448c34839c12c9 [needs-review] ---
Description: During the night of September 20 to 21, 1980, numerous witnesses, including military personnel, repeatedly observed glowing balls moving with varying trajectories, suddenly disappearing and reappearing. The sky was very cloudy and the weather stormy. That night, an automobile race was taking place on the slopes of the nearby Mt. Ventoux. Although the investigation did not prove it, the hypothesis of reflections in the night and on the low clouds from car headlights, combined with the varied trajectories and successive appearances and disappearances, can be seriously considered due to the topography of the automobile circuit.
