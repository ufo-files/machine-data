Description: On May 11, 2013, around 10:30 PM, a witness observed five yellow-orange glowing points appearing in the northeast of the city, moving slowly. The intensity of these points gradually diminished and they disappeared. This observation on a Saturday night is perfectly consistent with the observation of Thai lanterns released during a festival, moving with the wind, which was very light at that time and variable (see St. Etienne's Meteorology report). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
