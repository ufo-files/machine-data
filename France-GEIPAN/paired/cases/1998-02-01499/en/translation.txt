Description: On February 8, 1998, around 3:00 PM, a witness reports observing a black object in the sky. The investigation will quickly reveal that the witness observed a kite used that same day by a professional photographer to take aerial views. A demonstration is provided to the witness, who will confirm that the kite matches what he observed.
