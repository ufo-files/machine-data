Description: In April 2015, a witness reports an observation made on November 23, 2014, at 21:40. That evening, from the window of his apartment, the witness was surprised by the slow and silent movement in the sky of three lights, two white and one red, forming a triangle. The phenomenon followed a straight trajectory. Only one testimony was collected. Given the elements analyzed and reported in the attached investigation: - the lighting signal similar to that of an aircraft. - the area frequented by numerous aircraft near Nice Airport. - the movement consistent with local wind. Confusion with an aircraft or with 3 balloons or glowing lanterns is possible. However, there is a lack of many angular, azimuthal, and testimonial data. We can therefore conclude that, considered as a whole and pending clarifications from the witness regarding the appearance, movement, position, and disappearance of PAN, this case should be provisionally classified as "C": observation of an unidentified phenomenon at this stage, the significant lack of elements not allowing for a solid opinion.
