Summary: Observation of a very bright stationary object on a roof: unexplained phenomenon.
--- seg-a07aa593bc9d584595ccbad0 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 4, 1989, around 4:30 a.m., a man suffering from insomnia sees through his open window a bright light. He gets up and notices, below in a clover field, a square of light approximately 10 meters in side length. The light emanates from an object stationary above the roof of his home, an object shaped like a spinning top with multiple facets. After about 30 seconds, the phenomenon disappears abruptly, without any sound or smell. The witness checks the next day and finds that the tiles (of canal type) are discolored in a brassy brown at the location where the phenomenon was stationary, and they have shifted, creating a gutter. The contractor who repairs the roof will confirm that the tiles "were twisted clockwise over a length of 3 to 5 meters" and that the ridge was sagged at the location of the observation of the craft. The SEPRA investigation (see the investigation report) was unable to find an explanation for this observation.
