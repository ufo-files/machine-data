Summary: Observation of a white, spherical object in the sky that appears stationary: observation of a gas balloon from the 56th Gordon Bennett Cup aeronautical race.
--- seg-fccefa952f0add970165d810 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 2, 2012, around 12:25, during a family gathering in Montenay-sur-Allier (03), a witness (T1) observed a stationary, white object relatively high in the sky. It attracts the attention of the other present individuals, who show little interest, considering it likely to be a hot air balloon or ordinary phenomenon. The witness then retrieves his camera and takes two pictures, the second with a higher zoom level. He then resumes his activities, without continuously following the object. The observation lasts for approximately ten minutes, during which the object does not appear to have changed position significantly. Only one testimony was collected by the GEIPAN regarding this phenomenon. The witness reported his observation to the GEIPAN on January 2, 2016. The case consistency is judged good, due to the presence of two photographs supporting the narrative. Based on the observation parameters, analyses conducted using the IPACO* tool, and the experience of the GEIPAN with this type of event, the investigation leans towards the hypothesis of a gas balloon competition, specifically the 56th Gordon Bennett Cup, which took place during the observed period (see the investigation report). With the start of the competition given in Switzerland, the dominant winds led the majority of the seventeen crews to fly over central France, several of them approaching the area of Montenay-sur-Allier (03), the location of the observation, as illustrated by the map of trajectories. The Geipan classifies the case as "A" - observation of a gas balloon from the 56th Gordon Bennett Cup aeronautical race. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. IPACO: GEIPAN image analysis and processing software (IPACO.fr).
