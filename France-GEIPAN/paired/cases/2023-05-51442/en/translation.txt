Description: The sole witness of this phenomenon was in his garden in ROUEN (76) on 24.05.2023. For approximately three minutes, he observed a PAN that appeared as a large white glowing point, moving silently in a regular and straight line from northwest to southeast. Only one testimony was collected, as mentioned previously. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, the consistency of this case is good (consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony): although there is only one witness, a video was captured to reinforce the collected data. We demonstrate in this investigation that the physical and dynamic characteristics of PAN are consistent with the explored hypothesis, namely the observation of the International Space Station (ISS). This conclusion is based on the following elements: - The point-like shape, color, and brightness, comparable to those of a star, are in every way consistent with the hypothesis - The straight trajectory and constant speed also confirm this correspondence. - The azimuths of appearance and disappearance, maximum elevation, observation start time, and visibility duration of PAN were verified with the CNES Space Surveillance Service and correspond to the orbital data of the ISS observed from Rouen. The video made by the witness further confirms the appearance and straight movement of PAN, consistent with the ISS. GEIPAN classifies this case as A: observation of the ISS.
