--- seg-3bf984ddc09fdb72447a4a64 [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 10, 2008, an amateur photographer sent to the GEIPAN a high-quality image taken from his window in Paris, part of a series of 5 photos taken in rapid succession. The image shows an object with an elliptical, almost round shape, dark at the bottom and light above. The object is quite clear and appears to be illuminated by the sun. The investigation was entrusted to an investigator in Paris (see the IPN report). Reordering the photos, particularly photos 4 and 5, clearly shows that a pigeon coming from the left of the witness entered the field of view of photo 4 before diving towards a window and being captured in the field of view of photo 5. The taken photos are therefore indeed those of a pigeon, whose very round shape and "metallic" appearance misled the witness.
