Description: On October 9, 2023, at 12:00, a witness and his companion, driving on the Nestes road outside the village of Sarrancolin (65), observed at 12:00 a white PAN "similar in size to an airplane but not in shape." This PAN moved slowly and steadily along a straight trajectory. No noise was audible. Although only one witness reported the observation to GEIPAN, the consistency of the case is good, with the associated video allowing the location and azimuth of the observation to be identified (according to GEIPAN's criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony).

The explored hypothesis, that of observing an airplane, specifically a A330-700 Beluga aircraft, is supported by the following elements:

- An elongated and wide shape, characteristic of this aircraft with its distinctive design. The witness considered the possibility of an airplane but, not recognizing the Beluga, did not make the connection.

- The white color, consistent with that of the Beluga. It is noted that from the witnesses' position, the wings, being in the plane of the fuselage, are not visible.

- The straight and regular movement is entirely consistent with the hypothesis. Despite a lack of precise data on the witnesses' position and the absence of elements regarding the direction of observation, we were able, thanks to geographic references identified in the video, to retrieve these elements. These elements allowed for the establishment of an observation direction oriented towards the north.

By analyzing the PAN's movement from left to right in the video and its size reduction, it is likely that its trajectory was from southwest to northeast. The analysis of aerial maps (CNOA, adsbexchange, and Flightradar24) conclusively confirmed the presence of a A-330-700 Beluga airplane to the north of the witnesses around noon (10 UTC), flying at an altitude of approximately 20,000 feet (6,100 m). This altitude is perfectly coherent with the absence of condensation trail, being above the minimal threshold required for its formation (see attached documents showing the black track SC202=20,200 feet Beluga on the CNOA map and the FR24 aerial map).

GEIPAN classifies the case as A: observation of an Airbus A330-700 Beluga airplane.
