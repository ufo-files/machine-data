Summary: Observation of the movement of an orange-colored light phenomenon: probable Thai lantern.
--- seg-88a407869fbcd71b40ba6ec1 [needs-review] ---
Description: The witness observed for one or two minutes a bright orange point of light moving from the East to the North at approximately 45° elevation (see simulation on the sky map, based on the witness's account) at a steady speed, and then suddenly disappeared. A gendarmerie police report will be forwarded to us in March 2012. The described characteristics are compatible with those of a Thai lantern; the weather conditions for the day indicate a light and unstable wind from South to North at 20h and 22h (but from North to South at 21h!). This could explain the observed movement, but with no certainty. Given the holiday season, releasing a lantern that night would not be surprising. This is the hypothesis that the GEIPAN adheres to: this case is classified as "B": probable Thai lantern.
