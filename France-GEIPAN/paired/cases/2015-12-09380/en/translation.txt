Description: On December 25, 2015, at 6:40 PM, two witnesses observed a passage of a red-orange light in a clear sky. The trajectory was irregular and no particular noise was heard. The observation lasted 1 to 2 minutes before the phenomenon disappeared towards the North. Only one testimony was collected, along with a video taken from a mobile phone. The witness was intrigued as it did not resemble a meteor, a star, or an airplane or helicopter.

1) The observation was conducted from the outside by an immobile witness. A video was recorded in close-up, which does not allow visualizing the environment.
2) The phenomenon was unique, luminous, red-orange in color, circular or star-shaped, small in size, moving across the sky from East to West without noise or trail, then disappearing towards the North.
3) The weather conditions recorded at the Tarbes-Lourdes-Pyrénées Airport (about ten kilometers North) on December 25, 2015, at 6:00 PM and 7:00 PM: the weather report by Météociel mentions a light south wind of 6 km/h on average, no precipitation, and an unknown cloud cover.
4) The UFO event occurred on a Friday evening, the night of Christmas, December 25, 2015.
5) The observation was made from a suburban, scattered neighborhood, bordering Lourdes from the Southeast, looking towards the North which presents a similar type of environment. All these classic elements lead us to favor the hypothesis of a Thai lantern release.

Appearance: The video and description indicate a luminous phenomenon, of high intensity comparable to that of stars, red-orange in color, moving across the sky, then disappearing, all perfectly compatible with a lantern before its extinction.

Date and Location: This occurred during a festive evening, the night of Christmas, making it conducive to a playful event of this type.

Trajectory: It was described by the witness as moving from East to West, then towards the North. The wind measured ten kilometers away was 6 km/h on average, oriented from South to North. We cannot gain knowledge of the wind at the assumed level of the UFO, but we know that in a rugged terrain, as in this case, the orientation of the wind can vary at a small scale. The configuration of the site even allows us to establish a probable mechanism of wind evolution and a good compatibility between the wind measured at ten kilometers and the trajectory of the UFO. Indeed,  les contreforts des Pyrénées montrent un relief avec des collines. La carte jointe (relief.pdf is more explicit. The observer's position is pointed out, the reliefs are circled in green, the south wind is indicated with a red arrow, the trajectory of the phenomenon is reported in blue. It is understood that the wind can cause an aspiration in the Lézignan valley (to the right),  ce qui produit une trajectoire de droite à gauche (d'Est en Ouest) avant de reprendre le vent dans la vallée d'Adé du Sud vers le Nord. La trajectoire peut ainsi être parfaitement expliquée. Le fichier joint (trajectoire_Capture.pdf reconstructs the probable trajectory of the phenomenon as seen by the witness.

All these elements reinforce the hypothesis of a Thai lantern launched during a festive Christmas evening in 2015. The consistency of the testimony is average, the video is little exploitable, the information on the position in the sky is incomplete, but sufficient given the good probability of the retained hypothesis.

GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of a Thai lantern.
