Description: Recent testimony (15/03/2011) of an old observation made on May 13, 2009 at 2 a.m. for 5 to 6 seconds. A witness observes the sky in front of his home and sees a silent, dark orange object in the shape of a V pass very quickly. The object emits no apparent blinking and disappears quickly. The witness will make a similar observation in September, but he will observe two similar objects flying together and still silent. This observation case is relatively strange, but the short observation duration, the lack of details due to a very late report, as well as the absence of independent testimonies make its consistency very low and prevent any conclusion. This case is classified C due to the lack of independent testimonies and cross-checks.
