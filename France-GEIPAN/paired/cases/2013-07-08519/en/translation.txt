Description: On the night of July 20, 2013, at midnight, a witness and his wife noticed that the street lamp was out. They then observed the silent passage of a craft with five red-orange and white lights rotating counterclockwise beneath it. The craft's trajectory was from south-east to north-east at low altitude, moving at a slow, steady speed. The lamppost illuminated again as the craft moved away. The observation lasted for 10 minutes. Only one testimony was collected. The arrangement of lights described by the witness and the movement suggest an airplane on final approach, which is often relatively quiet. Aircraft have multiple flashing lights (red anti-collision lights, white strobe lights, fixed white, green, and red lights). However, there is no major airport nearby. The GEIPAN did not process the testimony quickly enough to obtain radar traces of aircraft overflying the area: this hypothesis cannot be verified. The north-to-north-east wind reported by Météociel contradicts the observed movement: it cannot be a wind-driven object. The correlation between the lamppost outages and the phenomenon is intriguing but not proven. Due to the lack of very precise elements, aside from the testimony, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" (unclassified) due to a lack of information.
