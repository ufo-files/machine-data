Description: On the night of July 18 to 19, 2020, three people observing a perfectly clear sky were intrigued by the passage of a "flashing" object with particularly bright flashes. The witness reports: "There were 3 or 4 flashes during the entire time the object crossed the horizon, and between the moments of brightness, the object was invisible to the naked eye. The trajectory seemed straight, with no deviations." The witness thinks of a satellite, but none of their internet searches yield results. Only one testimony will be collected for this phenomenon. The description of the PAN provided by the witness strongly suggests a rapidly rotating satellite, flashing at a more or less regular pace. This explanation is indeed considered by the witness. The parameters are very consistent with the passage of the Telkom 3 satellite: the trajectory, orientation, angular height, and the passage time. The powerful flashes strongly resemble those of the Telkom 3 satellite reported in July 2020. The satellite is in rapid rotation (see the investigation report). This case has a low strangeness rating and is of very good consistency (unique testimony but complete and precise). The case is classified as A: observation of flashes created by the Telkom 3 satellite.
