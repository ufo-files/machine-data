Description: Recent testimony (7/07/2013) of an older case from 07/23/2009. At 23:00 on 07/23/2009, a person lying on a chaise longue to watch shooting stars was surprised by the appearance behind a barn of a very bright ball. This ball silently moves up and down alternately about 2.50m from the ground. The ball then passes over the witness still lying in his chaise longue. The ball, of a size comparable to a child's balloon, is white and pale yellow. The phenomenon disappears behind trees. The phenomenon shines like the sun, emitting rays of light. The witness's description evokes a ball lightning. The witness does not mention any thunderstorm episode, but it was hot, and there had been a thunderstorm in the morning. The testimony was submitted to a ball lightning specialist (Lightning Laboratory: GEIPAN) who unequivocally recognized a ball lightning phenomenon; lightning impact recordings in Aquitaine were numerous that evening, reinforcing this hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of ball lightning.
