Description: On June 13, 1977, at approximately 00:05, motorists observed the road brightening as if in broad daylight for 4 to 5 seconds, followed by a sphere accompanied by a trail moving from west to east. The sphere emitted a series of sparks. It quickly disappeared, hidden by the landscape. Other witnesses in the area reported hearing a detonation or seeing a flash. The gendarmerie found no evidence of a crash in the area. These individuals were likely witnesses to an atmospheric reentry.
