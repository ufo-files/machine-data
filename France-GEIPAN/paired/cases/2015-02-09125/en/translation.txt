Description: On Thursday, February 19, 2015, around 2:30-3:00 PM, a driver observes while driving a craft resembling a commercial airliner but larger, flying much lower. The object is uniformly white and appears stationary. It disappears suddenly. This brief observation of a white, X-shaped light form could have several origins:

- an airplane, as the witness believes, but in a highly improbable position, and the object's disappearance remains difficult to explain. There is indeed an air corridor in the direction indicated by the witness, but planes are supposed to fly at such an altitude that the witness would not have seen it as large. The GEIPAN investigated this observation too late to verify the presence of an airplane in the witness's field of view.

- a temporary, bright cloud caused by the sun.

- a reflection of a white or reflective object placed inside the car's cabin, temporarily illuminated by the sun according to the car's orientation. This last hypothesis would explain the phenomenon's immobility and subsequent disappearance.

Unable to validate any of these hypotheses and lacking any cross-references or feasible investigations, GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of information. This case is not particularly strange as it's a distant phenomenon whose behavior resembles known occurrences; it is inconsistent as it is based on a single human testimony, and lacks photographs.
