Description: On Saturday, May 9, 2015, around 10:30 PM, a witness observed for 10 seconds the slow movement of an orange sphere in the night sky. The sphere lost intensity as it moved away. No other testimonies were collected. The observation of an orange ball crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests a Thai lantern, likely released nearby after a private party (possibly a release from the leisure base: see map). The movement was observed from the West towards the South-West, which is consistent with the light West wind recorded in Albi (see map and Météociel record). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of a Thai lantern.
