Description: On October 19, 1976, around 11:40 PM, a family in a car witnessed in the sky the movement of a peculiar object with light beams at each end. This object emitted lights towards the ground, then rose after turning off its beams and disappeared towards the East. A person whose home is located in the valley where the observation took place noticed disturbances on their television around 11:30 PM but made no direct observations. No information was gathered from the various services contacted (Weather, radar service, EDF, water treatment plants and balancing stations located nearby and operating day and night) to validate any hypothesis.
