Description: On Wednesday, August 8, 2014, around 2:40 PM, several people observed in the clear sky the movement of numerous black shapes that seemed to float and fall. One witness had time to take photographs and two videos of the objects, which appeared at times to be streaking. The observation lasted 5 minutes without any particular noise being heard. Only one testimony was collected. This testimony is of very good consistency: precise and accompanied by photographic and video documents, but from a single witness. Given the objective elements presented in the investigation report, and in particular the movement of the UFOs in the general direction of the wind as well as their appearance, it is very likely that these objects are merely debris from a balloon that burst in flight or more likely debris from a nearby fire. We can conclude that this case should be classified as "B" as a probable observation of light and scattered debris carried by the wind.
