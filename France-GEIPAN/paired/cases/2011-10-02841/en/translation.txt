Description: On October 10, 2011, around 6:20 a.m., a driver observes a moving orange ball in the sky. The witness photographs the luminous phenomenon which alternately follows an ascending and descending trajectory. No noise is heard during the observation. No other testimony about this phenomenon is collected. The witness, after receiving information about Thai lanterns (videos, photos, articles), credits the likelihood of this hypothesis. Although the early morning hour is unusual for this type of observation, this probability is also considered by the GEIPAN. The weather conditions (very light wind, unstable) are compatible with the observation. This case is classified as a "B" observation, probable passage of a Thai lantern.
