**Description:** On April 1st, 2023, around 11:00 AM, the witness, returning on foot from a large retail store located in La Talaudière (42), observed an object in the sky with a "delta-wing" shape, featuring a square front and rear sections. The PAN moved slowly and silently, slightly oscillating, at a constant altitude, following a linear trajectory from southwest to northeast. The observation lasted between 3 and 4 minutes. The case consistency is rated as average, as there is only one witness and the PAN does not appear on the photograph. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.* The description of the PAN could correspond to a light object carried by the wind, as its apparent trajectory is consistent with wind direction. However, the relatively long observation duration over an angular displacement distance of 40° is not compatible with this hypothesis, unless one imagines a large object farther away.

No other observations were recorded by GEIPAN, and no additional traces were found despite the presence of an urbanized area. Another hypothesis considered is that of a drone, mentioned by the witness themselves, but dismissed due to the absence of noise and the visibility of propellers. Note that noise could have been masked by the sound of nearby national road traffic, and drone propellers are not visible during flight due to their rapid rotation. Although flying drones over the commune is prohibited, the investigation aimed to determine if a drone could have still flown over the area at the time of the observation, without success. Unfortunately, the photograph sent by the witness is unusable, preventing the identification of the nature of the PAN. Investigations did not allow for any explanatory hypothesis to be prioritized. The case is classified as "C", an unidentified case due to a lack of data.
