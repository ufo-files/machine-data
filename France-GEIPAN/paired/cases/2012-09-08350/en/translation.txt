--- seg-e4a74d8102f9b2683d625ce2 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 15, 2012, at 10:30 PM, a witness observed from the open window of his bathroom multiple multicolored light points forming a kind of trapezoid. The points did not flicker. The shape appeared stationary, the size of a large building. The height was compared to that of a helicopter flight. The witness took several photos. The GEIPAN disappeared while the witness had turned away. The strangeness of the phenomenon can be considered low (static phenomenon, lasting, not very bright) with a possible explanation but without confirmation. The testimony is of average consistency: single witness and no correspondence between what is visible on the photographs and the witness's description of the phenomenon. Furthermore, the investigation took place long after the observation. The GEIPAN classifies this case in category "C" due to lack of overlapping information (other witnesses, high-quality photograph, reports of animations). The most probable hypothesis, the observation of a light projection event, could not be confirmed.
