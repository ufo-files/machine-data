Summary: Observation of the movement of a very bright phenomenon: lack of information.
--- seg-adcb3e3b5d47be4e3251c724 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 19, 2012, around 10:40 PM, a witness spotted in the sky a very bright point heading in a northwest direction. This initially stationary point then appeared to move. The direction indicated by the observer (O/NW) corresponds to the position of the planet Venus in the sky, at 15° elevation above the horizon. The object's movements as reported by the observer are obviously inconsistent with this hypothesis; the hypothesis of an airplane was considered, but radar traces could not be obtained in time. Given these insufficient information, the GEIPAN classified this case as "C" (unremarkable) due to lack of information.
