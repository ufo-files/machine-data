Description: Recent (2014) testimony of an old observation. On December 5, 2003, between 8:00 and 8:30 PM, a witness called a friend facing a window when he observed in the clear sky a stationary, white oval shape. This shape was very close to the Moon, almost the same size, and had the same color and intensity. The glow disappeared quickly. The observation lasted between 15 and 20 seconds. Only one testimony was collected on this phenomenon. Due to its strangeness and low consistency (short 20-second observation, single witness, no photo, late testimony, inconsistency between the reported position of the Moon and its actual position, atmospheric conditions), this case is a probable observation of an atmospheric optical phenomenon called paraselene (see investigation notes). It is also worth noting that the late testimony, nearly 10 years after the facts, has certainly led to memory errors (starry sky and Moon's position). This case is classified B, probable observation of paraselene.
