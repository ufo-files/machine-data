Summary: Observation of an atmospheric reentry
--- seg-cc251b43275a5357e5b748df [needs-review] ---
Description: During the night of September 23 to 24, 1997, two independent witnesses observed the passage of a very bright, oval-shaped light with a trail. The phenomenon was very fast and disappeared after a few seconds. No noise was detected. The characteristics of the observation lead to favoring the hypothesis of an atmospheric reentry.
