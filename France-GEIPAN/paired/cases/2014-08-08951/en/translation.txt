Description: The witness was walking his dogs at 23:25 when he observed two white glowing points moving across the sky at a high speed. Only one statement was collected. The witness's description of the phenomenon clearly suggests the passage of two satellites, one of which was large. A check on Calsky confirms that the International Space Station (ISS) was indeed visible at that time, accompanied by the European Space Cargo ATV5, which was preparing for its docking on August 12, 2014. This perfectly identified observation case is classified "A": observation of the ISS and cargo ATV5.
