Summary: Probable observation of balloon flight.
--- seg-64ef8f9ff701f6ab07304b04 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 19, 2010, around 4:30 PM, several individuals observed five to seven bright white points that intrigued them. No trails or noises were noticed during the slow northeast movement of the objects. During the forty-minute observation, photographs were taken. Only one person came forward and sent us the original and commented photographs. The characteristics of the observation (2 then several white points drifting quite slowly "as if carried by the wind") as well as photos P1070304 and P1070306 very likely indicate a balloon flight. The witness himself mentions the hypothesis of "weather probe balloons." The date of the observation (Sunday afternoon) could correspond to a flight of small festive balloons. This case presents a low level of strangeness. It is very likely explained by a balloon flight.
