Description: On October 21, 1977, around 6:30 PM, a witness observed for a few seconds the passage of a luminous phenomenon. A yellow, flickering ball moved on a west-east trajectory. No trail or noise was observed. The gendarmerie investigation did not gather any other testimony about this phenomenon. A particularly stormy day with numerous lightning strikes affected the entire region that day.
