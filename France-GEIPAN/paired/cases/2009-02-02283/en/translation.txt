Description: On February 15, 2009, between 10:20 PM and 10:30 PM, a person is intrigued by a sudden, sharp, high-pitched noise that abruptly stops. Joined by her husband, the couple observes through the window as a red, then orange, incandescent object passes straight across the sky. The object takes a descending trajectory and is quickly hidden by vegetation. The gendarmerie investigates with various sky surveillance organizations: no incidents or sudden altitude losses were reported on the night in question. This case presents a certain strangeness, but it's difficult to go beyond the investigation conducted by the Gendarmerie. The duration of observation seems too long for a meteoroid fall resulting in a bolide, and an atmospheric reentry of space debris from East to West is highly improbable. The noise heard inside a house is the most disturbing element and we cannot associate it with any usual hypothesis. This case is classified "C" due to lack of information.
