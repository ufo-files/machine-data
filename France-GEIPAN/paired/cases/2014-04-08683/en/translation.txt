Description: On April 16, 2014, around 8:00 PM, a witness taking bird photos noticed a peculiar, rapidly flickering light. He then observed through his camera a black, translucent object that did not appear in the photos. No other testimonies were collected. Based on the objective elements gathered in the investigation report, namely favorable conditions for the creation of strong light reflections from components of a commercial airliner towards the witness, the presence of anti-collision and/or navigation lights, the speed and distance of the PAN compatible with those of a commercial airliner, we can conclude that the case concerns the probable observation of a commercial airliner whose fuselage or a part of it was strongly illuminated by the setting sun and reflected towards the witness. This case is classified as "B," a probable observation of a sun-lit commercial airliner.
