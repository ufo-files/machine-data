Summary: Observation of the passage of several "lights" or "bright circles" in a V-formation in the sky: observation of a flight of migratory birds.
--- seg-0b6f46bbad2badd88889fc0f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 30, 2020 (not September 30 as incorrectly indicated in the free-form text of the questionnaire) at 04:38 in the morning, a witness observed from their garden the silent movement of several "lights" or "bright circles" in a V-formation in the sky. No particular noise is heard during the phenomenon's movement towards the southeast. Only one testimony is collected. The strangeness of the observation lies in the luminous aspect of the "circles" in a "V" formation, seen and described by the witness. These "lights" suggest a passage of migratory birds. The witness is located only 5 km from the Saint-Nazaire Lake, an important ornithological observation center. Located on a major migratory axis, it is the first stop for many migratory birds after crossing the Pyrenees range at the Albères Massif. It is one of the last natural sites on the Roussillon coast" (source of the GOR site). The head of the Ornithological Group of Roussillon, contacted by email, responded that it was likely a flight of white storks in this place and at this time (see the article on the pond of Canet-Saint-Nazaire). In an email exchange with the GEIPAN, the witness indicates an area and a direction in which she observes "strange" things (East / Southeast). Or from his home, the direction (East / SE) corresponds to the ornithological zone of the Étang de Canet-Saint-Nazaire. This area is described by the Ornithological Group of Roussillon as follows: "For raptors, the 'largest' populations are provided by: the Common Buzzard, the Black Kite, the Marsh Hawk, the European Sparrowhawk, the Variable Buzzard, and the Common Kestrel." Many fish hawks take a feeding break on the pond. On the side of the Storks, the white ones are at home here, and there are springs where nearly 2000 individuals are recorded. Some of them regularly stop near the city, very close to the observation site, and sometimes in the company of Grey Herons. The laridae are also well represented: gulls (including the Caspian gull), terns, and skimmers. On the side of passerines, tens of thousands of birds pass "close to" this hill, using it as shelter from the strong winds sweeping the coastline: martins, swallows, chaffinches, serins, wren, goshawks, roller, etc... According to migraction.net, the urban lighting of the municipalities of Cabestany (<1km) or Saleilles (<3km) to the southeast of the witness's residence is likely the source of the luminous reflection on the white storks (see the attached ornithologist's letter). The arguments supporting this hypothesis are: - Shape: The "circular lights, circles" described by the witness are consistent with descriptions of birds in nocturnal flight from other testimonies ("bright points" "lights" "of silver objects in formation"). - Color(s): The light reflected off the feathers can appear "white" or "yellowish" or "orangey" depending on the light source. - Brightness: Birds flying overhead at night above urban areas can appear bright due to reflections of city lights on the birds' light-colored feathers. - Trajectory: Described by the witness as "horizontal flight" and in a "V" formation, it is consistent with the flight of migratory birds. - Location: The witness is located only 5 km from the Étang de Canet-Saint-Nazaire, an important ornithological observation center. The witness is in the area of white stork flights. - plausibility: migration period, likely of a flight of white storks. The GEIPAN classifies this observation as A: observation of a flight of migratory birds.
