Summary: Observation from a moving vehicle and through a smartphone of a small circular green fluorescent patch in the landscape: photographic artifact.
--- seg-006e969b4edcf98fa328f863 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 07, 2017, at 15:12, a witness in a taxi scanned and photographed the clouds with a smartphone. A circular, fluorescent green patch that doesn't seem to move or moves at the same speed as the taxi particularly intrigues him. The witness takes several photographs then a little later on his journey makes a video. Only one testimony is collected. The witness is searching for "elementals" or "beings of life" and believes he has seen one following him in the landscape and in bushes along his taxi ride. In fact, it is evident that the witness is misled by a sun reflection created by his smartphone camera. In the photo at 15:16:29, the sun is offset from the center, and the spot or PAN slightly green with halo is the classic reflection created by imperfect optics that replicate (less brightly) in symmetry from the center of the image any extremely bright point, here the sun. In the 15:16:42 photo where the sun is not centered either, the reflection also exists but falls into an already very bright area of the image (the bright cloud) and therefore does not appear. In the photo at 15:16:18, the reflection falls into the bush in the foreground first. We verify the same phenomenon on the video segment. The GEIPAN no longer accepts testimonies of strangeness derived from what is only seen on a device screen and not with the eyes. One could have thought that the witness had checked with his eyes since he writes "I then checked by shifting my screen to look at this curious green patch in the bushes and that the patch indeed existed." The analysis of the entire testimony leads us to not accredit this verification: - on the one hand, the witness makes no distinction between the PAN that would be seen outside the optical (with the eyes) and the one that really exists on the screen and is created by the optics - on the other hand, the witness shows such eagerness to find something abnormal in the landscape that one may doubt their critical thinking. Perhaps unconsciously, he believes he saw a fleeting, naked-eye glow in the bush without attempting to verify it. The photo traces provided by this testimony unequivocally translate the observation of an artifact. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as A: photographic artifact (sun).
