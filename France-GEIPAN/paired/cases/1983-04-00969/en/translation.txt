--- seg-69291987807ac836a75dd420 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Following the discovery of an unknown origin crater in a wheat field, an investigation was conducted between March and April 1983. After discarding the hypothesis of a underground explosion due to the presence of World War II era munitions, investigators turned to the possibility of a natural phenomenon. The investigation demonstrates that the formation of this crater occurred on March 15, the day of a significant storm with a violent thunderclap: the crater was likely formed following the impact of a lightning strike to the ground.
