Description: During a trip on the A4 highway towards Strasbourg, a witness observes for several minutes a light comparable to Venus that seems to accompany the vehicle. The light maintains a constant altitude and apparent speed from Vendenheim to Strasbourg, where the witness loses sight of it behind buildings. This observation case is slightly strange and very likely corresponds to a misidentification with the landing lights of a commercial aircraft, the aircraft being observed face-on. This case is classified as B: probable observation of an aircraft.
