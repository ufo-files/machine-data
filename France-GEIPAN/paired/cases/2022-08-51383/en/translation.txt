Description: On August 16, 2022, at 7:30 PM, the witness operated a drone over the Rhône River in the Feyssine Park in Lyon. He spotted another flying object about one hundred meters away, moving upstream. The witness immediately thought it could be another drone, as its size, speed, and altitude were similar to his drone. It flew approximately twenty meters above water level at a constant speed of around 30 km/h, following the river's course with a horizontal trajectory. The two objects crossed paths at about ten meters from each other. The witness was convinced he was dealing with another drone. However, what surprised him was that this object had a perfectly spherical shape, a uniform dark gray/black color, a matte and metallic appearance, a diameter of about 15 cm, and no wings, which made it unclear how it could fly. Unfortunately, the camera on his drone was not pointing in the right direction to capture video of the object. Only one testimony was collected. The witness clearly observed a drone, but one with a peculiar shape, leading to the perception of oddity. It is very likely a spherical drone of the PITTA type. Attached are some images of this PITTA drone and a video demonstrating its capabilities. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: drone.
