Description: On May 8, 1980, at 11:30 PM, gendarmes were called to the scene of an unusual phenomenon. A witness, deeply impressed, claims his room was suddenly illuminated by a bright light, that he felt the presence of a humanoid shape in the room, and that through the window, he and his sister observed the movement of a glowing object. The gendarmes found no traces or other witnesses in the area confirming these accounts. Other witnesses present in their homes in well-oriented rooms should have seen the phenomenon given the extent described, but they did not. The guard dogs of the witnesses, usually very aggressive at night at the slightest light, did not move during this phenomenon. Finally, the gendarmerie investigation revealed certain fragility in the witness. The most probable hypothesis is therefore that the witness was likely deceived by light phenomena from vehicles easily visible from the bedroom window while he was probably in a state of heightened sensitivity to supernatural phenomena.
