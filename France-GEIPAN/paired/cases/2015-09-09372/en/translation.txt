Summary: Post-observation of a photograph without direct visual of bright points in the blue sky: confusion with the reflection in a window of lights located behind the photographer witness.
--- seg-67eda03654c4f90603d2cf37 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 20, 2015, at 4:52 PM, while visiting the Ballon Keep (72) during Heritage Day, a witness took several photos of the site, including one of the landscape through a window pane, without noticing anything unusual. But, upon reviewing the photos upon returning home, he observes three very clear bright points on the landscape photo, standing out against the blue sky, and describes them as: "I noticed three bright points in the blue sky on one of them." I zoomed in on the photo and to my great surprise, I noticed a formation of three sets of six bright points, forming a hexagon in each photo." No other testimony was collected. The series of photos analyzed (see investigation notes) show reflections, in a window, of one of the groups of LED lights present and turned on in the room where the witness was when he took his photographs. An on-site investigation confirmed the existence of these luminaries and allowed the effect observed by the witness to be photographed (see photos GEIPAN). As a result, the case is classified as 'A', confusion with a reflection in a window of lights behind the photographer witness.
