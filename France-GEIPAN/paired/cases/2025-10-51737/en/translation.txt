Summary: Observation of a luminous phenomenon and a dark rectangular shape: observation of the degassing of a stage of the SpaceX launcher - Falcon 9 - Starlink 10-37 - SLC-40 - Cape Canaveral SFS - October 29, 2025.
--- seg-f7d8c31ca5aa1a36a8d2084c [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Wednesday, October 29, 2025 at 7:27 PM, a witness outdoors is looking south/south-west. A fixed white light suddenly appears to the west, "emerging behind the foliage of a chestnut tree." Without flickering, its "brightness is comparable to that of Venus or an airplane." Referring to a "lunar reflection," the witness states that the light "seemed to reveal a larger shape: a dark gray to black rectangle." He then attempts to film the scene. The consistency* of the testimony is considered good: the main witness shows precision and provides sketches of the phenomenon seen from several angles. However, the technical exploitation of the provided video proved to be impossible. The main hypothesis under consideration is that of a fuel dump (degazage) from the second stage of a Falcon 9 rocket launched on the same day. Timeline and orbital dynamics: On October 29, 2025, SpaceX launched a Falcon 9 rocket from Florida at 4:35 pm UTC (5:35 pm French local time), carrying 29 Starlink® satellites into an inclined orbit at 53°. The satellite deployment occurs approximately 55 minutes after launch, around 17:30 UTC. The second stage then begins its second revolution around the Earth. The observation from Argol (Finistère) occurs around 18:27 UTC, which is 1:55 after takeoff. This schedule corresponds to the usual passivation operations (emptying of residual fuel). These degasings create very bright plumes at high altitude, visible from a great distance when the satellite is illuminated by the Sun while the ground is in shadow, a condition met that evening. Concordance with other observations: The same phenomenon was reported in Ireland between 18:25 and 18:35 UTC (bright point, diffuse halo, slow west-east movement, silence). It was also observed in Finistère, at an equal distance from Sizun and Landerneau (about thirty kilometers from Argol), on the same day at the same time. Geometric concordance (visual aspect and direction): From Argol (29), the phenomenon appears to the west and moves away towards east-southeast. This trajectory corresponds to an object in a tilted orbit at 53° passing north of the witness: generally oriented from west to east, its projection in the Breton sky forms an arc from northwest to southeast. In Ireland, closer to the ground track, the movement is described from west to east, which remains consistent. The geometric aspect described by the witness - in particular, the perception of a "dark rectangle" accompanied by a fixed bright point - appears compatible with several known perceptual mechanisms involved in the nocturnal observation of unusual light phenomena. "Structured" descriptions of Falcon 9 objects at twilight are extremely frequent and well-documented. The sketches provided by the witness are consistent with these known descriptions. However, the computer reconstruction performed by the witness enhances the shapes and increases the strangeness. The consistency of the observation with other nearby (less than 50 km) and distant (>500 km) reports, to the minute, with a common trajectory and similar azimuths, indicates that it is indeed a phenomenon located in the orbital zone. The perceived strangeness by the witness was strong. The residual strangeness after confronting this hypothesis appears weak for the GEIPAN and, as a result, this observation case is classified "A": observation of the degassing of a stage of the SpaceX launcher - Falcon 9 - Starlink 10-37 - SLC-40 - Cape Canaveral SFS - October 29, 2025. *CONSISTENCY: According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of information considered reliable and objectified, gathered for a testimony.
