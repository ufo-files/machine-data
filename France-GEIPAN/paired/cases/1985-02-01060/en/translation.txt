Description: On February 21, 1985, around 10 PM, two witnesses observed a peculiar luminous phenomenon: a bright yellow glowing ball that changed color to become a vivid red. For 20 minutes, the witnesses observed a stationary and silent phenomenon that gradually disappeared. The main witness, as well as several other people, were also able to make this observation in the following nights. It is likely the observation of a particularly visible star in the clear winter sky.
