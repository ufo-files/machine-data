Description: At 10:10 PM, the witness looks out the window and sees an orange, silent, glowing point recede, pause, and then disappear quickly. The observation of an orange ball on a Saturday night, moving with the wind (light southwest to south wind, recorded in Marignane: weather), suggests a Thai lantern released after a party. A plane seen from the front, close to landing, can also appear as a yellow or orange ball, but the witness, though near an airport, is not in the runway axis. Furthermore, they are well-acquainted with the aviation world. The Thai lantern hypothesis does not fit well with two elements of the testimony: the object stops and starts again. However, we note that the wind is light, and local relief can create wind irregularities. The object disappears at an extraordinary speed: the rapid and progressive dimming of the lantern's flame is often interpreted by witnesses as the object receding. Given the relatively strange nature of this observation case, the GEIPAN did not require an on-site investigation. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
