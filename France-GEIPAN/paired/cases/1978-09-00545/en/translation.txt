Description: On September 15, 1978, between 9:00 PM and 10:00 PM, a witness observed in their garden the passage from west to east of a bright flying object at a very high altitude. The object had the shape of a cigar and was illuminated by white lights. No noise was heard during the observation. No other testimonies were collected on this phenomenon for which we lack information.
