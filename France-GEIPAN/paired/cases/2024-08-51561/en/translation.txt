Summary: Observation of several orange lights appearing and suddenly disappearing in the stormy night sky: probable observation of anti-hail balloons.
--- seg-e9a26fdbfd19fa43abe0ffeb [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 31, 2024, between 10:35 PM and 10:55 PM, a witness observing from their garden in Porchères (33) saw several fast-appearing and disappearing orange lights in the low-altitude sky between south and west. The observation occurred as a thunderstorm was approaching from the south. With average strangeness and consistency (single witness, no photo of PAN), this case appears to be a probable observation of hail balloons, particularly during the burning of the salt bags they carry (see investigation report). According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The witness's description of PAN aligns with this type of observation, as the observed lights were orange and of limited duration. Furthermore, the directions of observation correspond to the location of Bordeaux vineyards, situated to the west and south. The timing of the observation coincides with a storm front approaching about thirty kilometers from the observation site, which corresponds to the time when hail balloons are typically launched. Contacted by email, the local winegrowers' union confirmed that 30 hail balloons had been launched the evening of the observation, although their exact positions are unknown. GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of hail balloons.
