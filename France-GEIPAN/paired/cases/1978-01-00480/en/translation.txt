Description: This observation case is part of previously published cases in "D" under the name GUITRES (33) 27.01.1978. Following a correspondent's remark, GEIPAN consulted its archives and found in this case an investigation report by GEPAN concluding in a "A" classification. On January 27, 1978, around 5:45 PM, a couple (T1 and T2) residing in SAINT-CIERS-D’ABZAC (33) observed through their house windows a strange object in the sky. The PAN, a flat, oval object of pale red color, appeared to hover in the direction of the commune of GUITRES (33). After 4 or 5 minutes of observation, the PAN accelerated and then disappeared into the sky. The gendarmerie conducted an on-site investigation as well as in neighboring communes between January 29 and February 3, 1978. GEPAN then mandated two investigators from CNES who visited the site on April 12 and initially concluded to a "D" (unexplained) classification, a classification that unfortunately remained in GEIPAN's database until this document. The responsible party of GEPAN at that time completed the report with his own conclusion in category "A"; this complementary report serves as a lesson in scientific ufology! This case is very consistent (three witnesses, one independent of the other two), the description of the PAN observed is quite precise. The sincerity and credibility of the witnesses have never been questioned. The described phenomenon shares many common characteristics (observation duration, shape, color) with the planet Mars, very close to the indicated axis. In GEIPAN's current classification, this medium strangeness case is classified as PAN A, an observation related to a misperception with the planet Mars.
