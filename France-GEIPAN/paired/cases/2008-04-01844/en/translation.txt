Description: On April 28, 2008, around 4:10 AM, a witness heard a rolling, deep noise coming from the northeast. This noise transformed into a thunder sound that lasted for 5 to 6 seconds as it passed over the residence, frightening the witness. The noise then moved away towards the southwest. That day, a significant thunderstorm activity was recorded in the residential area. The witness heard a thunder clap due to a severe storm.
