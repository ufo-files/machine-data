Description: On August 11, 2015, at 1:15 PM, a family was intrigued by the silent passage across the sky of two black, boomerang-shaped objects with a triangular front. The objects quickly disappeared behind trees. The trajectory of the first object was straight, while the second suddenly changed direction. No other witnesses were found. The weather for that day, recorded in Nimes Garons, was unusual: a wind shift from North to South was observed throughout the day. The moment of the observation fell right in the middle of this inversion, making it impossible to determine the local wind at that exact time (see the Météociel report). The boomerang shape and non-linear trajectory suggest a kite, a remote-controlled model plane, or a drone (see a video of a craft with this shape). The local environment (a residential area) seems very unlikely for kite flying, especially if the wind is unstable. The remote-controlled wing hypothesis remains, which seems consistent with the observation, but the GEIPAN lacks the means to verify this without additional testimony, photos, or videos. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of corroboration.
