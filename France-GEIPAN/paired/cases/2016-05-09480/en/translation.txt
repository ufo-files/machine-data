Summary: Sky observation of a dark streak starting from the ground up to the sky: observation of the volumetric shadow seen in cross-section produced by an airplane condensation trail in front of the sun.
--- seg-2959892a83e79f7cd7182450 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 27, 2016, at 4:26 PM, a witness lying on a beach at Les Salins was intrigued by a peculiar phenomenon in the sky, traced by a condensation trail from an airplane. The witness described a straight-line, very dark black or gray shape resembling "a kind of smoke curtain" visible from the ground to the sky. The phenomenon was stationary. The witness heard no unusual noises. He filmed the phenomenon. Only one testimony was collected. Analysis of the witness's testimony, photographs, and video indicates that the strange nature of the observation results from a known natural phenomenon called a "volumetric shadow seen edge-on" (or "edge shadow" in English). This phenomenon occurs and becomes visible when a straight-line airplane condensation trail passes in front of the sun and there is an alignment between the sun, the trail, and the observer. This results in a projected shadow on the entire volume of atmosphere contained within the planar slice defined by the trail, the observer, and the sun (see the investigation report). This atmospheric slice behaves like a slightly tinted glass that is not noticeable when observed face-on but creates a dark stripe for an observer viewing it from the side (or edge-on). The stripe is darker when the length of the glass is greater, meaning here, the straight portion of the trail is longer. As soon as the observer is not within this slice, the dark character disappears along with the phenomenon. This last point requires specific conditions that were all present for the witness. The dark stripe created is present over the entire field of view of the witness, including the sky. A witness noticing the airplane trail in the sky might imagine the direct (or classic) shadow cast on the ground by this trail, but much less a shadow on the sky. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Airplane trail, volumetric shadow seen edge-on.
