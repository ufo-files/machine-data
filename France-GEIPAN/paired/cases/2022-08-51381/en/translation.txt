Summary: Three successive observations of PAN round and bright objects coming from the same direction and moving on the same trajectory: observation of celestial lanterns.
--- seg-d9b1243e86e1d4d4e1d36edd [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 14, 2022, between 11:00 PM and 11:10 PM, the witness observed from their home in Pouillon (40), a very bright, enormous, round, yellow light with a hint of red on the outside, approaching in their direction and not very high up. He observes it with his wife for approximately 20 seconds, then as the brightness fades, the object eventually disappears into the black night. About 30 seconds later, another identical phenomenon appears at the same location, following the same trajectory, then a third one. Only one testimony is collected. The witness very likely observed three celestial lanterns. - description: yellow with red, - disappearance mode: dimming (corresponding to the gradual extinction of the flame), - direction and sense of movement consistent with the direction and sense of the wind, reported by the Dax station 11 km from the observation point - weak wind (4 km/h): because lanterns cannot be released in strong winds, - an observation made the day before a holiday. All these elements reinforce the hypothesis of observing celestial lanterns. The case is classified A: observation of celestial lanterns.
