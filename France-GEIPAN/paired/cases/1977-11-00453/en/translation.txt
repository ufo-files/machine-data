Description: On November 10, 1977, at 7:15 a.m., two individuals, from their home, observed a stationary bright point in the sky that intrigued them. Around 7:35 a.m., the object began to move, and the witnesses then observed a gray-colored, oblong object with a perfectly smooth surface, followed by a quickly fading puff. The object's speed increased, and it quickly disappeared behind a hill around 7:40 a.m. No sounds were heard during the entire observation. No unusual observations were made at the air base control tower or the Dijon-Longvic weather station, where visibility conditions at that time were quite poor.
