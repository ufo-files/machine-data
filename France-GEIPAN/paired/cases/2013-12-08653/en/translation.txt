--- seg-bcad8e0c3e1db1cd385e8a35 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 10, 2013, between 10:06 PM and 10:13 PM, a driver observed "3 large orange lights forming a triangle, above a small forest." He observes "between these 3 lights, a dark mass." The lights one by one go out, the witness describes that "The OVNI was also dimming." Another motorist will be taken as a witness, but only one testimony is collected. The observation of orange lights for several minutes clearly evokes Thai lanterns. The fact that the witness sees a dark object between the lights is a common visual perception illusion (Gestalt theory). It will be noted that the witness sees this black shape disappear as the lights go out: this confirms this perception illusion. The GEIPAN inquired with the gendarmerie, the town hall, and the Château de Vaux le Vicomte to potentially identify the authors of the lantern release; however, no celebrations were listed in the neighborhood that evening. It should be noted that the lights were located above a large private park; the GEIPAN did not contact the owners of this park; but the landing must have taken place further east. The wind was blowing very lightly from the East (as noted by Meteociel), which meant the witness had the wind at his back and saw the objects move slightly away, almost stationary. Although releases of lanterns are quite rare during weekdays, and the GEIPAN did not identify the author of this release, the witnesses' account remains consistent with this hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns. The witness did not approve this hypothesis.
