--- seg-d1c379e35117c993a1cad0e9 [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 19, 2011, at 21:37, two people observed from the terrace of a residence, the slow, straight passage of a bright white sphere above the mountain crest of the Risoux massif. The main witness took a video of his observation. Unfortunately, the quality of the video did not allow the gendarmerie to make a particular interpretation and does not account for the observation made by the witness (strong shaking due to maximum zoom and artifacts). The directions and observation times correspond exactly to a passage of the International Space Station (ISS): this case is classified "A": perfectly identified.
