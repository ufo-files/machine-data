Description: On September 9, 2015, at 21:30, a witness observed for 3 seconds a pale yellow, rectangular shape with a black band down the middle, moving above the Belledone mountain range. No noise is heard during the movement of this shape on a descending straight trajectory. No change in course is noticed. Only one testimony is collected. The observation location of PAN is located just 3km from an extremely active free flight (paragliding) site. The described aspect is compatible with a paraglider, which would have performed a night flight (aeronautical night begins 30 minutes after local sunset time). Night flying (paragliding, hang gliding) is regulated. The device must have two lights (one green and one red). Additionally, a night flight must be declared to the prefecture and can only take place three days before or after a full moon. This is not the case here. This night flight was illegal, which explains its lack of proper lighting. The 7th Alpine Hunter Regiment (located in Varces) states that there were no military exercises that night. Based on the evidence, we can conclude that this observation likely involves an unauthorized night flight of a civilian paraglider (see the attached investigation report). This GEIPAN report states:

"This testimony has an average consistency (some discrepancies between questionnaire data and on-site data, unique testimony) but is sufficient in light of its strangeness to classify the case as B: probable misidentification with a paraglider."
