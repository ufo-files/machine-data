--- seg-31d9364dd4d67235927b6fa7 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 9, 2013, at 10:02 PM or 10:03 PM, a witness observed for 5 seconds the movements of several white light phenomena that silently moved across the cloudy sky in a linear fashion, then turned in a counterclockwise direction, and eventually disappeared. Only one testimony was collected. White circles resembling projections onto the clouds and spinning suggest an animation projector used in nightclubs. Friday evenings at 10:00 PM are typical for this type of promotion; however, there does not appear to be a nightclub within 10 kilometers of the observation location (there are some in the nearby suburbs of Bordeaux at 12 kilometers, or around Blaye at 18 kilometers, which is quite far for projections to be clearly visible). Despite the strong resemblance to the effect of a 'skyrose' projector, and lacking other testimonies or cross-references, the GEIPAN classifies this case as C: Lack of Information.
