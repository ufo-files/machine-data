Summary: Observation of silent movements of a very bright phenomenon in the sky: probable confusion with a drone.
--- seg-95b45a66620a52c2d251ab0c [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 4, 2017, around 4:35 PM, a witness is called by a friend to observe a luminous phenomenon in the sky. Indeed, from his garden, the witness sees a kind of star that he takes for Venus. However, the witness suddenly realizes that the phenomenon is moving in the sky. The witness has time to take photographs and film the PAN. No other testimony will be collected. The PAN observed, photographed, and filmed by the witness is probably just a white recreational or professional drone, controlled from a field or meadow nearby (see the investigation report). This conclusion is based on the following points: - identical color to some models - movements consistent with drone capabilities - plausible calculated distances - possible visual use from clear locations nearby. Although in the observation axis is a structure that could have been filmed and photographed from a professional drone and a company is known to have produced such documents using a white drone at about the time of the observation, it is not certain that it is the same drone. It could therefore be another drone used for leisure, or the same drone used for other purposes over the village, closer to the witness. In view of this hypothesis, the strangeness is ultimately quite low, the residual strangeness being mainly that one cannot distinguish on the photos the precise shape of the PAN for visual identification of a drone. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": probable confusion with a drone.
