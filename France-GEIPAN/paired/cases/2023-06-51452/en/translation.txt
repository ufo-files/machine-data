Description: On June 22, 2023, at 10:50 PM, a resident of PIERRELATTE (26) is on his terrace at his home when he observes a PAN in the shape of a "large cigar," approximately 25 meters long, with glowing portholes. The PAN moves slowly and silently at an altitude equivalent to that of a helicopter, following a northwest/southeast trajectory. Suddenly, it disappears. Only one testimony is collected for this event. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, the consistency of this case is average: one witness, no photo, no video. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The investigation ruled out the hypothesis of aeronautical origin and confirmed that it was likely the observation of a Starlink satellite train (see investigation report for more details). The launch of Starlink satellites by a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket, carried out that same morning, resulted in the formation of the satellite train "G5-7." This train was observed from southeastern France during its notable passage just before 11:00 PM. The still-significant proximity between the satellites created the illusion of a single cigar-shaped object, the "portholes" actually corresponding to individual satellites illuminated by sunlight, even though the sun was already below the horizon from the witness's perspective. The disappearance is explained by the fact that either the satellite train left the witness's visibility zone or, due to their movement, they are no longer illuminated by sunlight. The case is classified as A, observation of a Starlink satellite train, specifically the train "G5-7."
