Description: On November 26th around 6 a.m., a farmer in a field observed a very bright white light source at a distance of seven to eight hundred meters from him. This light had a vertical movement of low amplitude at five or six meters above the ground. A dark, spherical area was seen under the light. On-site investigation revealed no ground traces. No military flight exercises were reported at that time on the day of the observation. No other testimonies were collected on this phenomenon for which we lack information. The photographs taken by the gendarmes were not found in the gendarmerie file.
