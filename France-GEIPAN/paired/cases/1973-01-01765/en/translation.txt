--- seg-86afeb187bd8179643223a86 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: One evening in 1973 around 11 PM, a child with his parents observed multicolored lights above the Magenta Bay in Nouméa. These lights were stationary above the sea. Thinking it was an airplane movement, the witnesses were very surprised when passing the airport to see it completely closed. After an investigation the next day, no airplane or helicopter movements were recorded on the entire island. The witness mentions other people making similar observations that night, but no other information was gathered about this phenomenon for which we lack details.
