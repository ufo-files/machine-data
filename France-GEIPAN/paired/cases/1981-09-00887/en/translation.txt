Summary: Observation of lights at low altitude with rapid movements.
--- seg-19f0edb32d8de91cd467e438 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 22, 1981, starting at 3:30 a.m., several witnesses, including gendarmes, spotted different-colored lights at low altitude. The weather was rainy and stormy, which did not allow the witnesses to provide a precise description of the lights or their origin. The white light was quickly identified as coming from a radio relay. However, the intermittent red light and the constant green light were not clearly identified. These lights moved very quickly and chaotically. No noise was heard during the entire observation. Furthermore, the main witness stated that he felt a strong, hot wind, for which we have no other information.
