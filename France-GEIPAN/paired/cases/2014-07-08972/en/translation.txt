--- seg-61c851903cd6d511a256d7c6 [needs-review] ---
Description: An automobilist's observation at 23:58 of a flickering red-orange sphere in the sky for less than a few minutes on a weekend night strongly suggests a Thai lantern, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was perceived from south to north, opposite the direction of a car traveling north to south. This apparent movement is not compatible with the light north wind recorded in Dinard: Weather. It is difficult to say if the perceived movement against the car's movement is real or a perspective effect, with an object seemingly stationary during the minute of observation, likely perceived much farther away than it actually was. The witness refutes the hypothesis of a Thai lantern. No other testimonies were collected. No atmospheric reentry of space debris or meteoroid was recorded in this area at that time (sources: aerospace and BOAM). Due to this uncertainty (real movement and brightness), the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" for lack of precise information (stationary witness) or corroboration.
