Description: On April 8, 1985, several witnesses observed a cone-shaped object with an irregular outline and a crimson red color plummeting vertically behind a mountain. Fearing it might be a struggling aircraft, the gendarmerie was immediately notified of the observation and also witnessed the event. The investigation will demonstrate that it was an expired marine distress rocket launched without particular intent by a resident of a nearby hamlet.
