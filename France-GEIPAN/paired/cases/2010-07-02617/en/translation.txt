Description: On the 10th of July, 2010, at 2:30 AM, a witness and his wife observed between 15 and 20 orange glowing orbs in the sky. They moved silently on a south-north trajectory. The observation lasted for a quarter of an hour during which the witness took photographs. No other testimony was collected by the gendarmerie. The witness's photos leave no ambiguity about the very probable observation of flying lanterns.
