Summary: Observations of the movement of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-794dd3c3c8cc7ac0c18fcfde [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness observed for a few seconds around 11 PM the silent and straight movement towards the N-NW of a luminous phenomenon composed of five bright halos of white and yellow colors. Following the classification and publication of the case of Tournan-en-Brie (77) from the same date, the witness contacted the GEIPAN and believes his observation could correspond to the same phenomenon: a release of lanterns. Another testimony was collected on a similar observation made from the Île Saint-Louis between 10:50 PM and 11:13 PM on June 5, 2010. The case is classified in category A.
