Summary: Probable observation of glowing balloons illuminating a ski slope.
--- seg-e022c0b4ed43f0c7efc7eaa2 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 1, 2009, at 20:00, several people observed from their balcony unusual white lights moving on the other side of Lake Geneva. Intrigued, the witnesses took photos and observed them with a telescope. At 21:45, the lights disappeared. Using photographs taken at daylight, the witnesses precisely located the place of their observation: it was the Boré Peak, specifically the area where a ski lift is located. The initial hypothesis by the GEIPAN was that the white lights could have been mistaken for headlights of a snowgroomer in operation that evening. The witnesses later provided a likely explanation for the phenomenon: illuminated balloons were used to light up the slope in the evening of January 1, 2009. Although this observation occurred outside French territory, the proximity to the French border (Haute-Savoie department) and the well-documented questionnaire prompt us to publish this testimony for information purposes.
