Summary: Possible successive observations of the ISS
--- seg-f263b56f91e71ae7f27c87ae [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reports successive observations on September 21, 2008, and July 10, 2009, of silent-moving bright spheres. A set of photographs taken by the witness is attached to his description. This case concerns observations made by the same witness on several dates without a gendarmerie report. These observations are described as single or less bright light points structured in pairs in the questionnaire sent by the witness to GEIPAN. Due to the multitude of observations, GEIPAN commissioned an IPN (Inquiry into Non-Identified Phenomena) which focused on the first observation on September 21, 2008. The reported elements reflect a probable observation of the International Space Station (ISS). The description of "spheres" and a "central core" is due to the use of optical devices (binoculars...). This case is classified as B.
