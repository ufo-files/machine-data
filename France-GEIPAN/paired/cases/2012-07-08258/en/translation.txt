--- seg-6675d2236aa4f990770111f7 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 5, 2012, at 10:22 PM, a witness (T1) and his spouse in their living room heard the sound of a jet plane passing by. T1 went outside his home. He was surprised by the presence in the sky of two white lights followed at a distance by three "fighter" planes flying at a high altitude. The PANs (presumed aerial phenomena) were lost from view, obscured by the landscape. Other testimonies regarding the passage of planes were collected (see the investigation report). A witness (T3) made a statement at the gendarmerie. Given the information gathered in the attached investigation report, the only hypothesis considered is the confusion with the rear position lights of fighter planes, exacerbated by poor observation conditions, and possibly by several passages of the same planes, of which the first two had not yet turned on their landing lights. This testimony is of very good quality (concise, very precise, neutral, and written very quickly after the observation), and of good consistency as, although coming from a single witness without a photo, we were able to rely on a second testimony submitted to the gendarmerie to study the hypothesis. The observation is ultimately not very strange because the PANs are, after investigation, easily characterizable. The strangeness was initially mostly created by the fact that witness #1 did not observe the first planes during their initial approach phase, their landing lights possibly being off and the observation conditions being unfavorable. The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'B', probable confusion with the navigation lights of fighter planes.
