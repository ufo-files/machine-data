Description: On April 5, 2010, at 3:49 a.m. or 5:49 a.m., a witness observing from their balcony saw a very bright, elongated, and stationary white light. Then, they noticed three glowing balls of yellow and red light separating from the main light and moving in different directions. The main light suddenly disappeared. Only one testimony was collected. The description (appearance of the glowing points, weekend night) could correspond to Thai lanterns. However, the cigar-shaped element is harder to explain with the given information alone. Further investigation and exploration of possibilities (such as clouds for cigars, sky position, wind compliance for balloons) would have required additional information that the witness never provided despite several requests. Consequently, the GEIPAN is classified as C: Lack of Information.
