Description: On March 31, 2013, at 5:54 PM, a witness, while driving, observed above a field a black, faceted diamond-shaped object spinning. No wind was perceptible, and the sky was overcast. No other testimonies were collected. A field investigation was conducted with the witness. In conclusion, though the witness displays great sincerity, a proper hearing and reconstruction, due to the lack of corroboration or additional testimonies, this observation lacks consistency. The strangeness of the case is average to low as plausible hypotheses can be advanced, but these have neither been confirmed nor disproven. The GEIPAN classifies this case as Category C due to the lack of reliable information.
