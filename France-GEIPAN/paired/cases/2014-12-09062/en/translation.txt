Description: On December 25, 2014, between 01:20 and 01:29, a witness observed successive movements of 8 bright lights in a clear sky. With a slightly red center and yellow outer edges, these points moved silently towards the East (towards Hyeres). No other reports were collected on this phenomenon. The observation of orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes on the same trajectory, during a major holiday night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby for Christmas. The movement was observed from North to East, which is consistent with the unstable North to North-West wind reported in Hyères (see the Météociel report). The GEIPAN classifies this case as B: very probable observation of Thai lanterns.
