Description: Two people observed from their homes, over several dates, luminous phenomena in the sky and the movement of a dark mass and a beam of light illuminating the ground. No other testimony regarding these phenomena for the indicated dates has been collected. Some of the luminous phenomena can be explained by the passage of planes and satellites, and especially by the observation of the star Arcturus, which was affected by strong twinkling during those days, this case could be classified as totally explained (Class A). However, the white light illuminating the ground with a beam, as well as the dark "object" and the white and purple light directed at the witness, remain unexplained at this time, with too few concrete elements to continue the investigation.
