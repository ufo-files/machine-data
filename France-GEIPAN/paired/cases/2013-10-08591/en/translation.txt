Summary: Observation of a very bright and fixed white point in the sky: Venus observation.
--- seg-9bf5ff1d48a99e16c0f3048c [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 25, 2013, at 8:00 PM, two individuals observed a fixed, bright white point in the sky. In the southwest direction at the time of observation, the planet Venus, particularly bright at that time (magnitude -5, its maximum), was noted, in conjunction with a very clear sky (visibility 35 km) despite clouds (see Meteociel record). This created exceptional conditions for the notable observation of this planet. A cloud passage subsequently obscured the planet from the witnesses. The GEIPAN classified this case as "A": observation of Venus. Note: The witness reported a 45° height on the horizon, while the photo shows a much lower elevation, consistent with 8°30'  adjustments for the actual elevation of the celestial body.
