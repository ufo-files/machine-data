Description: On June 7, 1978, between 7:30 PM and 8:00 PM, three fishermen on a boat observed for several minutes a luminous phenomenon that intrigued them. A white light moved slowly along the coast, then came towards them. When it reached their position, the witnesses reported seeing a craft with colored flames coming out of the rear without any noise. It suddenly changed direction abruptly to return towards the coast and disappeared, leaving the witnesses very impressed. Other witnesses made an observation on the same evening, but according to the description given to the gendarmerie, it is likely the observation of a shooting star. The investigation determined that for the observation made by the fishermen, it could not have been an airplane, a boat, a weather balloon, or signaling lights. Despite a thorough investigation, this case remains unidentified.
