Summary: Observation of green and red-orange stationary phenomena for a few minutes above a farm (apple orchard and building): probable observation of a fireworks display.
--- seg-93ecb9c90d5f45898e40ebdb [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 19, 1990, around 10:45 PM, the witness, in a farm courtyard, observed for one minute an oval green shape above a building. Then, with his wife, from their home, they saw red-orange lights above the barn for five minutes. The witness calls the Gendarmerie but the PAN has disappeared by the time they arrive. In October 1990, the gendarmes conducted various tests, including one with low-power fireworks rockets: the witness did not recognize the PAN but was not in the same conditions (he knew what he was going to see). A firework was launched on 19/08/1990 at CHARCHIGNE (53), which is in the direction of observation and during the same time frame, which may be the cause of the observation. The firework hypothesis remains privileged. The following year, to validate this hypothesis, the gendarmes propose to return to the farm to observe the effects of the fireworks.
