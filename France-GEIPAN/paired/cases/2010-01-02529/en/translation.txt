Description: On January 1st, 2010, around 8:10 PM, numerous people witnessed a bright phenomenon in the sky. Eight testimonies were received by the gendarmerie: two of these (T4 and T5 in the gendarmerie report) likely describe the observation on the continent of a lantern (seen from Fouras and St. Augustin) but are unrelated to the object observed on the island by the other six witnesses. The author of the release in Saint-Pierre d'Oléron spontaneously went to the gendarmerie to declare that he had launched a 'wishing balloon' traditionally used in holidays and birthdays in Holland on January 1st, 2010, between 8:00 PM and 8:15 PM. The case is classified as A.
