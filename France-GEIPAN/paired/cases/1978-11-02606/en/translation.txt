Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archive on its public website www.geipan.fr. Among its publications are old cases classified at the time as Type "C" cases, which are now being reassessed with the sole purpose of being more relevant in their conclusions. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over all these years, this reassessment sometimes leads to new observations or even an explicit change in classification in an investigation note. This observation case (formerly named Saint-Calais (72) after the gendarmerie) is part of cases classified "C" by the GEPAN. On November 21, 1978, shortly after 6:15 a.m., two people observed a circular and red light phenomenon rising on the horizon. This phenomenon moved slowly then faded. The gendarmerie, dispatched to the scene at 8 a.m., concluded it was an astronomical observation of Venus; this was not confirmed by the GEIPAN until 2013, due to doubt about the precise observation time as Venus did not rise that day until 6:29 a.m.
