--- seg-61873197f02ef62aaa2a5a9b [needs-review] ---
Description: The witness observes, very early in the morning, a very bright object moving silently. The trajectory resembles that of a shooting star. The observation of a "very bright thing" crossing the sky at high speed, appearing much lower than a shooting star, clearly suggests a bolide. The BOAM network (see attached document) reports at 02:27 UT (04:27 civil time) the video recording of a phenomenon apparently similar, by a camera located in Gretz-Armainvilliers, Seine-et-Marne, in a northeasterly direction at an elevation of approximately 50°. This time of year is quite conducive to observing this type of phenomenon (Eta Aquariid meteor shower), the GEIPAN has received other similar series of testimonies during the same period. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of a bolide, the result of the atmospheric entry of a meteoroid, probably from the Eta Aquariid shower (coming from the direction of the constellation of Aquarius).
