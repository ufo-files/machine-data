Summary: Observation of red lights at night preceded by a whistling sound.
--- seg-ceb89404dd079bd336f34341 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 30, 1978, around 2:30 a.m., two campers, half-asleep, heard a whistling sound, followed by a red glow observed from inside their tent for 15 to 20 seconds. Simultaneously, they felt the ground warming. An hour later, the campers heard another whistling sound, unusual for the rural night. The young women did not look outside their tent. At dawn, they noticed a burned area on the ground a little further away and thought that a craft might have landed there. A Gendarmerie investigation found no other witnesses who heard these suspicious sounds or saw the lights. However, a witness claimed to have burned wood from brush clearing along the hedge and embankment bordering the field at this location a year ago; two other similar locations are found along the hedge. Around midnight, the campers attempted to start a fire with wet twigs and toilet paper, but the paper burned rather than flared up. It's likely that during the night, a sudden gust of wind caused the paper and grass to catch fire abruptly, creating a glow for 15 to 20 seconds and giving a feeling of warmth. The fire was located between the tent and the presumed landing spot of the craft.
