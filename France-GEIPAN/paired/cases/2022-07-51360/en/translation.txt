Summary: Nighttime observation of the passage of 2 PAN bright objects in the sky: observation of a military aircraft patrol.
--- seg-250a9f0a5c6045ac8543c2f2 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the evening of July 5, 2022, a resident of BEZENET (03) was at his home, standing on his doorstep facing south, when he observed two bright light points passing overhead, consisting of raw white strobe flashes. These two points come from the west direction east. They are moving at the same time, side by side, at a speed, according to the witness, equivalent to that of a traditional satellite. The spacing, according to the witness, "corresponds" to the ends of the wings of a glider passing a few meters above the ground. There is a slight sound of a commercial jet, but its movement did not correspond to the movement of the light points. Of weak strangeness and good consistency* (single witness, no photo of the PAN but fairly precise testimony), this case turns out to be the observation of a military aircraft patrol returning from a mission (see the investigation report). *According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The description of the PAN strongly suggests an aeronautical observation: the lights described by the witness as "stroboscopic" are comparable to the white flash lights of aircraft collision avoidance systems, which may be the only ones illuminated, depending on the mission needs. On the other hand, a light sound of a troop transport aircraft was perceived by the witness. The Air Defense Operations Planning and Command Center of the Air and Space Force [CAPCODA] was also able to confirm that a military aircraft patrol, returning from a mission, passed over the observation location at the time indicated by the witness and according to a trajectory perfectly consistent with that of the PAN. Two of these three aircraft are without a doubt the ones that the witness observed. The case is classified "A", observation of a military aircraft patrol.
