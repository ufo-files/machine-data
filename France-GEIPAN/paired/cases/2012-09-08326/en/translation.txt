Description: On September 16, 2012, at 22:16, a witness observed a very bright yellow object coming from the southwest, moving quickly towards the mountain, above the horizon. The very bright object fragmented into two pieces that disappeared behind the northeast horizon. The witness's description perfectly matches the observation of a meteoroid with fragmentation, very likely caused by re-entry into the atmosphere. This case is classified "B."
