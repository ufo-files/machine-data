Summary: Observation of the movement of a white light point in the sky: probable Thai lantern.
--- seg-415a92a65b32b46f53d0d69c [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 1, 2008, between 11:00 PM and 1:00 AM, a fisherman on a beach observed the slow movement of a white glowing point in the sky that appeared to come from the Isle of Yeu and was heading east. The witness lost sight of the glowing point in the clouds. This observation case has the characteristics of a Thai lantern: movement according to the wind (east) of a glowing ball. The date and time are consistent with this hypothesis: one can think of a release from a boat at sea. The observed deviation towards the north is probably an effect of the coast that modifies the flow of the wind at low altitude. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
