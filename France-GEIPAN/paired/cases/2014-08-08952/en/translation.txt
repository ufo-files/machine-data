Summary: Observation of the movement of a white light phenomenon in the sky: ISS observation.
--- seg-d6bc7e55f5a0fc0e321d06bf [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Sunday, August 17, 2014, at approximately 10:40 PM, a witness observed for 5 minutes the movement of a white glowing phenomenon in the sky that intrigued them. Other witnesses also observed the phenomenon. Only one testimony was collected. The witness's description of the phenomenon clearly suggests the passage of a large satellite. A check on Calsky confirms that the International Space Station (ISS) was indeed visible at that time, in the direction indicated by the witness: from Northwest to East. This perfectly identified observation case is classified "A": observation of the ISS.
