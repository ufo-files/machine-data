--- seg-6180598b3a65f41420b41d76 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness notices, through photographs, the presence of four lights previously unobserved at the time of the shot. The 4 bright points in the sky are lens flare artifacts from internal reflections within the camera lens. Evidence of this is that the lines connecting each of the first 4 streetlamps from the left to the 4 lights in the sky from the right intersect exactly at the same point. The other streetlamps do not produce lens flare artifacts as they are farther from the optical center.
