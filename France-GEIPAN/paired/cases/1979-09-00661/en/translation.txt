Description: On September 23, 1979, around 6:00 a.m., several people observed in the sky a bright point moving silently in a northeast direction. This point emitted green and red colors. A few moments later, the witnesses' car was flooded with light that covered the entire road. Suddenly, the light went out and one of the witnesses saw a shape rising at a 45° angle in the sky, without any sound but leaving behind a red flame. The on-site investigation revealed no clues, and no other information was gathered about this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
