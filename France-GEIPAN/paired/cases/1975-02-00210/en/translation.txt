Summary: Sequential observations of a sparkling phenomenon: astronomical misperception.
--- seg-5d279ad8c75792bd5e25a729 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Two witnesses observed, over several consecutive nights and in a clear sky, a sparkling circular phenomenon. Some observations were made with a telescope. An astronomical verification revealed that starting on February 17, 1975, a Jupiter-Venus conjunction could be observed. These stars were gradually moving apart. The scintillation produced the rest of the effect observed with the telescope: apparent motion, color changes, etc... These witnesses made an astronomical misperception: the case is classified A.
