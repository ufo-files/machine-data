Summary: Observations of the movement of a luminous phenomenon.
--- seg-e7295cab244385007ff9bd15 [needs-review] ---
Description: Three individuals from the same area observed a luminous phenomenon on the night of July 23-24, 1976, around midnight. A bright white and blue light moved silently at low altitude. It disappeared towards the southeast. A gendarmerie investigation, including with a farmer witness, failed to find any trace or evidence suggesting the passage at low altitude of any device or object. The vegetation in the area of passage indicated by the main witness remained intact, and no anomalies from a heat source or air movement were observed. The descriptions were insufficient, and this phenomenon was classified C due to a lack of information.
