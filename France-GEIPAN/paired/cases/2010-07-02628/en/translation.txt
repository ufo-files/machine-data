Description: A witness reports their observations on the nights of July 15, 16, and 17, 2010, of a luminous phenomenon appearing around 10 PM and disappearing around 11:10 PM. Other present individuals did not provide testimony. The primary witness took photographs of the phenomenon. Reconstructing the direction of observation reveals a cone centered towards the West. The characteristics described by the witness (a very bright point of light in the sky, observed for several consecutive days in the same location for long minutes (up to 60 minutes), descending towards the horizon) leave no ambiguity regarding the observation of a celestial object. The planet Venus, with a strong magnitude of -4.1, was located in the portion of sky observed. The case is classified as A, indicating observation of the planet Venus.
