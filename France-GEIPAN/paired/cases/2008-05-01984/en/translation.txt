Description: On May 8, 2008 at 4:30 AM, two people observed in the sky the passage from West to East of a very bright, white oval ball. That day, the ISS could be observed with a rise (at 225) at 4:27 AM in the South West and a set at 70 (East) at 4:37 AM (source: Winstars). The observation of a bright point for approximately 2 minutes from West to East at 4:30 AM corresponds well to the passage of the ISS at its apogee and very bright since the addition of additional solar panels (magnitude -4).
