Description: A questionnaire testimony reports the observation of a very bright fixed light point to the East on December 31, 2009, around 9 PM (local time). The initially described apparent very fast and high altitude movement by the witness actually corresponds to the movement of clouds. The observation duration (10 minutes) of a very bright fixed point changing color suggests the observation of the brightest star in our sky: Sirius. The astronomical configuration of the sky indeed shows the presence of Sirius in the observed portion of the sky (East) at the time of the observation. The case is classified A as the observation of the star Sirius.
