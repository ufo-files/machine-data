Summary: Daytime observations by two witnesses of the silent passage of a dark PAN at low altitude: observation of a light, rigid debris made of expanded black PVC carried by the wind.
--- seg-75c80f027fb739823e2ccb3f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Tuesday, October 19, 2021, at 1:20 PM, two friends were having lunch on a terrace on the top floor when they were intrigued by the straight, low-altitude passage of a dark, "flat below" and "bulged on top" object. No unusual noises were heard. The PAN made a slight turn and ascended into the sky. The PAN disappeared from their view; no photos were taken during the observation that lasted about ten minutes, but each witness drew a sketch of the PAN and reconstructed its trajectory. The witnesses put forth various hypotheses but remain uncertain about what they saw. No other testimonies were collected. We were able to show through analysis that the witnesses likely observed a light, rigid debris, possibly made of black expanded PVC (maybe a construction site debris...) carried by a non-turbulent wind gust (as detailed in the investigation report). This conclusion is based on the following elements: - the shape remained regular; the object, both light and rigid, did not deform during its transport by the wind gust, - the black color is possible for this type of object, - the overall straight and regular movement in the direction of the dominant wind, towards the north. Meteorological data indicates an average wind with strong gusts towards the north, in the direction of the PAN's movement. The GEIPAN classified the case as "A": observation of a light, rigid debris made of black expanded PVC carried by the wind.
