Description: On June 6, 1976, around 11:35 PM, a motorist observed for a few seconds the steady descent and explosion of a luminous phenomenon. No noise was heard. Believing it to be an aircraft accident, the witness informed the gendarmerie. The investigation will show that no aircraft has been reported missing. No other testimony about this phenomenon will be collected. The motorist likely witnessed an atmospheric reentry.
