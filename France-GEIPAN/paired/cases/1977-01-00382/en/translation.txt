Summary: Observation of the movements of a luminous object.
--- seg-1e26d6c73aaa76cb7103fc28 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 14, 1977, at approximately 6:40 PM, two individuals observed a luminous, spherical object that intrigued them. The object first moved in a south-north direction, then east-west. The gendarmes observed a slowly moving, circular, luminous object with a diameter greater than that of a star in the western direction. It was visible until 9:30 PM. No other testimonies about this phenomenon were collected, and we lack information about it.
