Description: On September 4, 1978, around 10 PM, a group of people observed a bright object in the sky that intrigued them. This object, spherical in shape and white in color, moved at the speed of an airplane on a North-South trajectory between the peaks of the sector. After several minutes of observation, as an airplane flying in the opposite direction approached, the object rose vertically and changed color before disappearing at extremely high speed into the sky. The gendarmerie investigation was unable to gather any additional information. The noise heard and the evasive maneuver as the second airplane approached with its standard identification lights on suggest that these witnesses likely observed the maneuvers of a military jet aircraft.
