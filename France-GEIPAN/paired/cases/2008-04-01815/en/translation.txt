Description: On April 19, 2008, around 9:20 PM, three individuals observed in the sky the slow movement of three large, yellow-orange glowing points. No particular noise was heard during the observation. The points suddenly disappeared. One of the witnesses reflexively took a photograph; unfortunately, the low quality of this image, along with the absence of references, only allows for the illustration of the testimony. This testimony has all the characteristics of flying lanterns. However, the witness does not provide any terrestrial or celestial references that would allow for the precise direction, which they estimate to be from Southeast to Northeast. This imprecision prevents a definitive conclusion as the reported easterly - northeasterly wind direction does not align with the described direction. This case is classified as B, likely observation of flying lanterns.
