Summary: Observation of the silent movement of a very bright blue ball in the sky: likely observation of the passage of the International Space Station (ISS).
--- seg-f4ad15f0efd49f9ff3824dd8 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 13, 2013, at 12:30 AM, a cyclist observes a bright light that he initially mistakes for Saturn or Jupiter. Upon arriving home, the witness is surprised to see that the blue light passes overhead without any noise. The trajectory is straight but the ball seems to make zigzags at times. It eventually fades away in the distance. No other testimonies were collected. Given the objective elements gathered, namely that the object moved in a straight line with small zigzags, and had a luminosity and trajectory consistent with the passage of the ISS (International Space Station), the strangeness of the phenomenon can be considered low with two possible explanations, one very probable: airplane or ISS. (see the investigation report). The testimony has average to low consistency: an internal contradiction regarding the direction of movement is noted, and there is a lack of schema or photo. In light of these elements, this case is classified as "B" as a very probable observation of the ISS, the witness having likely been the victim of the autokinetic illusion.
