Description: On Thursday, July 16, around 11 PM, a witness in their garden spots a bright light in the night sky. The witness describes an orange fireball moving silently, skimming the tops of nearby trees. Its trajectory is linear, then it slowly disappears into the horizon. Prior to this, their partner was able to take a photograph, which will be attached and scanned to the gendarmerie's police report. Only one testimony will be collected. The description mirrors typical characteristics of a Thai lantern (color, trajectory), and its movement aligns with the wind direction measured at a few kilometers east to west (refer to the weather on Infoclimat). The witness states that it does not resemble what they once saw with a lantern. However, lanterns can vary in appearance depending on their color, altitude, and flight phase. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B: Thai lantern.
