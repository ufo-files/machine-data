Description: On the night of April 17 to 18, 2008, at 12:43 a.m., a resident of RODEZ (12) observed from his home two lights above the city in a SSE direction. He viewed them through a telescope and noted that the lights "vibrated." He then took five hand-held photographs: the first three were spaced 15 to 20 seconds apart, and the other two about seven minutes later. Only one statement was collected. Several hypotheses were explored: aeronautical, astronomical, or festive (see investigation notes). Due to its strange nature and low consistency (single witness, unusable photographs), this case exhibits several characteristics that could suggest multiple explanatory hypotheses. Unfortunately, the long delay between the observation and its analysis, the imprecision of the statement, and the witness's lack of response to requests by GEIPAN prevent any formal identification of PAN. This case is classified as C due to a lack of details.
