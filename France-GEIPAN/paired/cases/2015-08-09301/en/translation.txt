Description: On August 29, 2015, between 9:45 PM and 11:00 PM, a family observed successive movements of orange or white glowing points in the sky. Some dimmed gradually, while others disappeared from view, moving in various directions. No sounds were heard during the observations. Only one statement was collected. The observation of orange or white glowing points in the sky on a weekend night strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The successive disappearance of these lights supports this hypothesis as it results from the end of the burners' burning in the lanterns. The very brief statement does not allow for correlating the observation with wind. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable Thai lanterns.
