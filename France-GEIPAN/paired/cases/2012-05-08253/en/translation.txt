Summary: Observations of the passage of over one hundred lights associated with frightening shapes in the sky: release of Thai lanterns on the occasion of a wedding.
--- seg-149c53a3d8aa05b92a05306e [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 19, 2012, at 23:20, several family members witnessed hundreds of lights in the sky, accompanied by shapes that frightened them. No noise was heard during the passage of PAN overhead. The Gendarmerie conducted a local investigation and identified a release of 100 Chinese lanterns at a wedding, a few hundred meters from the witnesses. The GEIPAN verified the compatibility of the phenomenon with local wind conditions. The GEIPAN confirmed the conclusions of the Gendarmerie's investigation, classifying this case as "A", perfectly identified, as a release of Thai lanterns. The witnesses' interpretation of this large-scale phenomenon was likely heavily influenced by surprise and fear.
