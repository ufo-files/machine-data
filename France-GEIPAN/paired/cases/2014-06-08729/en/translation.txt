Description: On June 26, 2014, at 5:48 PM, a witness is intrigued by the presence of a black dot in the sky of Saint-Mandé (94). He believes he sees a parachutist (see sketch on the questionnaire), and films with his phone. In order to protect himself from the sun, the witness moves but loses sight of the PAN. It is a posteriori on his video that the witness thinks the object seems to follow a trajectory. Only one testimony is collected. This observation of an object seemingly floating in the Parisian sky is rather unusual. The witness thinks he can distinguish a parachutist, which seems highly unlikely in Paris. Upon inquiry, it turns out that the prefecture may authorize parachute jumps over Paris for specific missions. The GEIPAN (French UFO Investigation Group) did not find any parachute clubs that had made this type of jump, which is probably reserved for professional activities. It could also be a peculiar-shaped child's helium-filled balloon that was accidentally released. The weak, very unstable wind recorded at Paris-Montsouris is consistent with either of these hypotheses (see the meteociel website). Due to the lack of high-quality images or the identification of the potential parachutist, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of information or cross-references. This observation case is not strange, as it can be easily explained by several relatively mundane causes.
