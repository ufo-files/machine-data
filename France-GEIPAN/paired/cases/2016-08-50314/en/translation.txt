Summary: Observation of the silent east-west passage of an incandescent orange sphere in the night sky: very probable observation of a Thai lantern.
--- seg-16e8b2bf8607e535b7373ac8 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 23, 2016, between 9:15 PM and 9:30 PM, a witness and his son on the outdoor terrace of their home were intrigued by the silent movement in the sky of an orange, incandescent sphere. The witness distinguishes flames on the object and its surface. This "fireball" described thus by the child moves from East to West without changing direction until the witness loses sight of it. No noise or trail was noticed. The observation lasts 30 seconds. Only one testimony is collected. The entire thing is perfectly consistent with a lantern release observation (see the investigation report):

- The movement is consistent with the direction of the wind;
- The appearance, color, and brightness of the PAN are consistent;
- It was a summer night conducive to a festive event. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as A: very probable observation of a Thai lantern.
