Description: On Saturday, April 21, 2007, a person left their friends around 12:15 a.m. to go home. While crossing the garden, they spotted three bright lights forming a triangle directly above them. Calling their friends, all went to see this triangle moving at a high speed without noise. During the observation, which lasted less than 10 seconds, they could see low-intensity orange lights at each end of the triangle. The craft was moving from West to East. No further information was collected on this phenomenon for which we lack data.
