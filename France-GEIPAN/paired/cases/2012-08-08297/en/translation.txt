Summary: Observation of the slow movement of a luminous object: probable observation of a party balloon.
--- seg-cfef54a59f36f1b0bde92047 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 14, 2012, at 12:00, a driver notices a bright object in the sky that intrigues him. According to the witness, the object appears to have articulated wings and blinking lights. He films the phenomenon and calls his wife, who also confirms the presence of the object in the sky. A third witness also observes the PAN. The testimony of T1 is very precise and of good quality. The observation duration is approximately 3 minutes. The three witnesses do not describe the exact same thing, which is quite normal for witnesses T2 and T3 who see the PAN at a considerable distance and at different times. Therefore, there is no doubt about the reality of this phenomenon, which has all the characteristics of a helium-filled party balloon that may have escaped its owner. The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'B', a probable observation of a helium-filled party balloon.
