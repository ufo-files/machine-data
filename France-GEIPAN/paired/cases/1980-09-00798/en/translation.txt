Summary: Observation of a strange craft at 150 m above ground: Lack of information
--- seg-6e7948aa198312369758aeed [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of September 5, a young girl gets up to close the open window of her room, which had been blown open by a gust of wind. She spots a luminous object in the sky about 100 meters from her home. This object is half-rounded, emitting a very bright yellow-orange light. It is stationary at an altitude of approximately 150 meters. Several times, the craft will turn off, leaving a light on at each end. The observation lasts about a half-hour without the object touching the ground, then it goes dark permanently. The child makes no further observations, and no other witnesses are found for this phenomenon, for which we lack data.
