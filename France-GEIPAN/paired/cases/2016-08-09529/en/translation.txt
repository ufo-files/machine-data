Summary: Observation of a very bright object in the sky with a change in direction: probable observation of an object carried by the wind (and in particular a child's balloon).
--- seg-a7a5551d91addd0740c18919 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 23, 2016, around 10 a.m., a witness on their roof was approached by a roofer who pointed out a very bright object in the clear sky. This object moved towards the witnesses as it changed direction. No noise was heard. The object disappeared at the horizon. Only one testimony was collected. The witness described the object as being carried by the wind, "a bit like a balloon in the wind and at an irregular speed." The oddness of the direction change can be explained by the very changeable wind that day, especially around 10 a.m., as indicated by these Infoclimat Vinon and Infoclimat La Marche weather reports. The witness's perception of the object's high speed is necessarily subjective since they did not recognize it and could not estimate its distance of movement. The object should likely have a round shape; otherwise, its tumbling in the wind would have caused light flashes. It could be a child's balloon. In conclusion, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable observation of an object carried by the wind (and specifically, a child's balloon).
