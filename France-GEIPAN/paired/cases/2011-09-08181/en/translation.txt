Summary: Observation of the silent movement of a dozen flying objects: probable Thai lanterns
--- seg-28beab4a921677f4c5b73bcb [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 3, 2011, around 10:50 PM, the witness observed from his balcony overlooking the Seine a formation of a dozen balls arranged in a pyramid. This formation moved silently in a trajectory opposite to usual planes descending towards ORLY. As the witness was reaching for his camcorder, part of the objects disappeared, leaving only two that quickly ascended towards a large cloud. This observation is quite typical of Thai lantern releases. The red color of the envelope, the group movement, the ascending movement and/or with the wind (light east wind that evening), the disappearance (extinction) after a few minutes, the time and date (evening of a Saturday) of the observation, are all indications supporting this hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
