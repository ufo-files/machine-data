Description: Recent (2014) testimony of an old observation (summer 1998 - the exact date is not given; we arbitrarily date it to 01). In July or August 1998, a young boy was playing with another child on a property in the middle of the countryside near CHATEL-MONTAGNE (Allier). Their attention was drawn to a elongated object, gray in color, with (indeterminate number of) portholes that allowed white light from inside to pass through. The object appeared to the right of a chalet directly in front of them and moved from their right to their left in the clear sky. The main witness described the object as a "cylinder" that he estimated to be approximately 50 meters long, with a "hard" appearance, and a dark "copper" color (Pantone # ON. 00.36 or ON.00.45). The object, moving silently, seemed to graze the tops of nearby trees before disappearing a little further away, behind the trees on a hill. The observation lasted between 30 seconds and one minute. The children ran to warn the adults who were dining outside, turning their backs to the phenomenon and its trajectory. No adult was able to confirm their observation. Only one testimony was collected. The strangeness experienced by the witness resulted from a "cylindrical object with portholes that allowed white lights to pass through." This is a recent (2014) testimony of an old (1998) observation. The witness was 12 years old at the time. He is not able today to determine the precise date and month of the observation (he indicates August on the questionnaire but will indicate July as possible to the investigator). Under these conditions, despite an encounter with the witness, the investigation field remains very limited. The hypothesis of a solar balloon (type Piff gadget) is possible due to the shape, silent movement, and it answers to the main strangeness. However, it is impossible to verify the consistency of the direction of movement with the wind. (see the investigation report). In conclusion, the case allows for a possible explanation, which, given the lack of information, cannot be denied or confirmed. Consequently, the GEIPAN is classified as C: lack of reliable information.
