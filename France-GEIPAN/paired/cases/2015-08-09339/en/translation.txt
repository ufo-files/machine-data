Description: On August 6, 2015, at 10:20 PM, a witness observed in the sky the quick passage of a translucent triangular shape that intrigued them. No noise, no flashing, no trail is observed. Only one testimony is collected. The witness observes the phenomenon moving from southwest to northeast over several dozen degrees in one second. A preliminary astronautic context check via the Calsky site (see attached log), indicates a passage of the International Space Station (ISS) from 10:20 PM to 10:25 PM, from West to East, at a low altitude, simultaneous with the observation but quite far from the direction of observation of the witness, who is lying down and looking towards the zenith. The speed of the phenomenon, and the coincidence with the observation period of the Perseid meteor shower (shooting stars), points towards a hypothesis of 3 simultaneous shooting stars (possibly from the same body). This hypothesis, of course, does not explain the hollow triangular shape observed by the witness. This shape could be a perception illusion (see the link Illusion of shape or outline .) But this hypothesis cannot be confirmed. The witness, interviewed by phone, specified that the glow of the bright points did not resemble that of a shooting star. The very short observation, by a single witness, does not allow for a fine characterization of the phenomenon. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of information and cross-references.
