Description: During the nights of July 14th and 15th, 1983, several witnesses observed in the sky an object emitting a strong white light and moving slowly from west to east. Despite prolonged observation, the precise shape and size of the object could not be described. The characteristics of the various observations, as well as the astronomical analysis of the sky during those evenings, support the hypothesis of an astronomical observation: the witnesses likely observed Venus, which was particularly visible in that sector at that time.
