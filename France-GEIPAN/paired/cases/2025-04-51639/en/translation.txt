Description: On April 29, 2025, around 11 PM, a witness observed for 3 minutes from the garden of their home located at POINTEL (61), a dark lenticular shape with 2 green lights at the ends flying at a constant speed, at low altitude, without any perceptible noise. The witness perceives a stop and a change in trajectory. The witness is intrigued by this unusual dark shape, the fluorescent green lights, and the stop during the journey. The reactions of familiar animals increase the strangeness (cat, goats, and cows). The consistency* of the testimony is considered good despite only one witness and the absence of exploitable photos/videos of the PAN, the witness having provided clear and precise information about the observation conditions and the characteristics of the PAN (see the investigation report). *According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. It has been established through radar scans by the Air Planning and Command Center for Air Operations and Air Defense - CAPCODA - of the French Air and Space Force that this observation is consistent with the passage of two military aircraft flying in formation, equipped with slime lights (fluorescent green lights). Explanations for the elements of strangeness noted by the witness:

- The illustration of the PAN provided by the witness is due to the lenticular and dark shape of these massive military planes observed at night and in profile at low altitude.

- The green lights come from two possible sources: the green fluorescent "slime lights" used during formation flights, confirmed by military sources, and/or the green signaling on the right wing, which is constantly facing the witness.

- The absence of perceptible noise from the witness's position, located no closer than 3 km from the planes: these planes are designed to be quiet. Moreover, a light southwest wind contributed to its auditory range's displacement. Finally, external noises might have been dampened by the helmet the witness was wearing while talking on the phone with her friend. - The animals' reaction can be explained by the fact that the witness was on the phone for the entire first part of the observation, probably raising her voice and moving quickly to keep up with the phenomenon, which disappears repeatedly, hidden by the foliage and the house. Animals not accustomed to these nocturnal movements may have been frightened by the witness's excitement. - The perception of an "excessively brutal acceleration" can be explained when an aircraft moving away to the northeast suddenly changes course and approaches from the southeast, as indicated by the trajectory on the military map. - The perception of immobility: when an aircraft changes course, the viewing angle and distance create an effect of immobility or momentary stop, especially if the aircraft is approaching the observer. This bias is known in aeronautics. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: observation of military aircraft.
