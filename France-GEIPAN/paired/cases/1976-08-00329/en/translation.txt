Summary: Observation of an advertising balloon.
--- seg-33ccf3dadd4e9154b6ca94fa [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 3, 1976, around 11:45 a.m., a farmer noticed in the sky the slow movement of a cylindrical object with a constant, bright aluminum color. The observation lasted 30 minutes before the object disappeared over the horizon. During the neighborhood investigation, several people identified the object's description as a promotional balloon for a tire manufacturer, seen several times that same day.
