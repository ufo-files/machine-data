Description: On January 14, 2012, around 9:35 PM local time in Saint-Quentin (02), a witness on their fifth-floor balcony observed three small, white, glowing points forming a dark, triangular shape. No noise was heard during the object's very fast, south-to-north movement. The observation resembles an airplane with its landing lights on. Radar data (primary) from aircraft overflying the area at that time indicates only a high-altitude commercial jet at 9:48 PM (7 minutes before the estimated observation time, located a few kilometers west of Saint-Quentin's vertical axis), but this radar track is north-to-south, while the observation is the opposite. Therefore, the observation does not match the radar-detected aircraft. Given the uniqueness of the testimony, this case is estimated to have low consistency; due to the regular, linear movement of the three glowing points, the case is considered unremarkable; it is classified as "C" due to a lack of information. Further investigation would likely yield no additional information.
