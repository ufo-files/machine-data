Summary: A driver briefly observed two oval, white, glowing orbs moving slowly in the sky; likely observation of two military aircraft.
--- seg-829867f5972d335f3f4f2302 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Monday, November 1, 2021, around 6:15 PM, a driver on the A86 highway heading towards Paris and at the exit #27, was intrigued by the presence of two very bright white oval-shaped orbs, side by side, at the same height, and moving slowly in the sky. His observation is brief, as the motorist enters a tunnel and does not see them again afterward. Only one testimony will be collected. We studied a first hypothesis of observation of lights from 2 commercial aircraft approaching Runway 3 at Orly Airport (see the investigation report). The planes TO3451/TVF34JK of Transavia coming from Porto, and U24066/EJU641M of EasyJet coming from Nice are good candidates, but their altitude does not match. The elevation of the aircraft was estimated at 2.5° for a 45° reported by the witness. A second hypothesis was studied: the hypothesis of observing landing lights of two military aircraft, briefly observed by the witness, as they headed in his direction. This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- White color consistent with landing lights.
- The shape, described by the witness as oval, corresponds to the overall group of lights visually confused because observed at a certain distance, the two lights most distant from the main ones are located on the wings. The other elements of the aircraft (other navigation and collision lights and fuselage) cannot be observed, as they are "overwhelmed" by the intense brightness of the landing lights. The observation distance, unknown but potentially significant, may also play a role in the absence of perception of the other lights and the fuselage of the craft. - The perceived absence of noise is not surprising, as the witness was in their car, with closed windows, in a traffic flow on the A86. The significant distance separating the witness from the planes (up to several kilometers) also contributes to the lack of perception of the noise emitted by these aircraft. - **Location**: The observation took place near the Orly and Villacoublay airports. - A slow movement was observed, but it does not correspond to the actual speed of the planes, as they were not observed from a side angle, and the observation was very brief, with the witness entering a tunnel shortly afterward. Furthermore, it happens that November 1, 2021, was the first day of the climate conference "COP26" bringing together many heads of state and government in Glasgow, Scotland. Two military Falcon 7X aircraft carrying the President of the Republic and his delegation took off from Villacoublay base in the morning, bound for Glasgow, and returned in the late evening. It is possible that the aircraft observed by the witness were nearby to secure the airspace around Paris in anticipation of the return of the two Falcon 7X to Villacoublay. No tangible data allowing to confirm this point could be established. No trace of these craft could also be found, but it is possible that, within the operational framework of securing the airspace for this particular mission, the transponders of these craft may have been switched off. Classification "B": probable observation of two military aircraft.
