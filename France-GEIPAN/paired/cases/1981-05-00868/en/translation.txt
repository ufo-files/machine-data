Description: On May 10, 1981, at approximately 8:30 PM, witnesses observed a peculiar luminous phenomenon. A red ball high in the sky appeared to descend gently and land. Suddenly, an intense light burst forth from a triangle, illuminating the entire area, followed by white smoke seen descending towards the ground by the witnesses. This phenomenon repeated itself four times in succession, with roughly 10 minutes between each occurrence. The investigation suggests that the witnesses were likely deceived by illumination rockets launched that evening to mark the results of the second round of presidential elections.
