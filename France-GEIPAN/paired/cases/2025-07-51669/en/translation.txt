Summary: Multiple witnesses in different municipalities observed a bright rectangular PAN in the night sky, which was later identified as a train of Starlink® satellites.
--- seg-7eff71974c89bb8af6c8a214 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 31, 2025, at 11:17 PM, two witnesses (T1 and T2), residing in Mandres-les-Roses (Val-de-Marne, 94), observed from their garden an PAN in the eastern sector, northeast of the sky. The phenomenon appears as a long, bright light bar, colored from light blue to pure white, with a strong and constant brightness. It moves according to a straight trajectory from south to north, at a moderate apparent speed. T1 was able to take two short videos in which the PAN is clearly visible intermittently through the clouds. The observation lasts approximately two minutes, after which the PAN is lost from view, obscured first by clouds, then by a tree. The witness initially mentions the hypothesis of Starlink®-type satellites, but this is ruled out in light of the observed characteristics. The consistency of this case, in terms of GEIPAN* criteria, is good, although only one of the two witnesses reported their observation. This GEIPAN report is supported by two videos taken by T1, on which the PAN is clearly visible. The strangeness of the PAN lies, for T1, in the very close appearance of the elements that compose it. *According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The hypothesis of observing Starlink® satellites visible shortly after their launch was studied. In fact, on that same day, 19 Starlink® satellites (including 2 Starshield) were launched from Vanderberg Base (US) according to a highly inclined orbit that brings the satellites, once deployed, over France on a south-north trajectory (Annex 1). Many other witness reports have been posted online, with some observers even taking photos that attest to the similarity with the PAN filmed by T1 (annex 2). Note that another observation of these satellites made on the same day at the same time from Beaumont-les-Valence (27) was also the subject of a testimony in GEIPAN. The GEIPAN classifies the case as 'A': observation of a Starlink® satellite train.
