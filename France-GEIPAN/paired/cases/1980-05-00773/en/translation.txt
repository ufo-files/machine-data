Description: On May 15, 1980, at 12:30 AM, a witness observed a luminous, oval-shaped object crossing the sky rapidly. Of a red-orange color, it was followed by a tail. The object's brightness was significantly greater than that of a star. The object disappeared while heading west. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected. This person likely witnessed an atmospheric reentry.
