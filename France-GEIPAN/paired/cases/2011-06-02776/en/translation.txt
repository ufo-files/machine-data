Summary: Successive observations of the passage of the International Space Station (ISS).
--- seg-ba24881610fd5643676d184f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Several witnesses observed the regular passage on the same trajectory of a bright point of light. Considering the times given by the witness, the observation corresponds to the passage of the ISS station (to which the ATV2 cargo was docked). The witness was observing towards the south a light moving on a southwest-to-east axis, consistent with the ISS trajectory, whose magnitude of -3.0 made it a readily observable luminous object with the naked eye. Posteriorly retrieved passage times on the Heavens Above site are slightly offset due to the station's trajectory correction (weekly). The case is classified A as an observation of the International Space Station (ISS).
