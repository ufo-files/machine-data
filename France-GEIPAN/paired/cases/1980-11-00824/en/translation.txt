Description: On September 5, 1980, at 6:15 a.m., a witness observed at a distance of 200 meters in the sky an object with a circular shape, one meter in diameter. Purple in color, a disk was emitting light beams. The observation lasted 5 minutes before the witness left. No military maneuvers were conducted in this area on that day. No other testimonies about this phenomenon were collected, and we lack information about it.
