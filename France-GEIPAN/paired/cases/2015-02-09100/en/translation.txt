Summary: Numerous observations of a bright phenomenon passing through the sky: bolide observations, caused by the re-entry into the atmosphere of a small astronomical body.
--- seg-8d9bb6cebb700e1c9fcbe186 [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 10, 2015, numerous witnesses across southern France, from the Atlantic Pyrenees to Hérault, observed a very bright, luminous ball traverse a portion of the sky. This phenomenon was also observed in Spain, from the Basque Country to Catalonia. The automatic camera of CNES in Toulouse successfully recorded the phenomenon at 7:22 PM. GEIPAN received three formal testimonies (see summary table), La Dépêche du Midi and the Ciel des Hommes website received over 80 testimonies. A 3-dimensional reconstruction of the trajectory is underway thanks to other cameras (Pic du Midi, Spain) that recorded the phenomenon. This luminous phenomenon, called a bolide, is caused by the atmospheric entry of a "large" meteorite ("rock" weighing over 100g, i.e., from a few centimeters to 1 meter in size). It occurs at altitudes between 100 to 40 kilometers due to the heating and ionization of the upper atmosphere. The color can vary (green, white, yellow) depending on the incidence angle of the meteorite; debris can reach the ground, then called meteorites. The high altitude of the phenomenon explains why it could be observed within a radius of over 200 kilometers. Its intense brightness often leads to a mistaken belief that the phenomenon is very close. It often creates strong illusions of proximity, while the phenomenon actually occurs at high altitude (beginning around 100 kilometers and ending around 20 kilometers). A notable variability in the observation time of the phenomenon (from 7:15 PM to 7:40 PM) is common in this type of phenomenon observed by multiple witnesses. The trajectory and speed of the phenomenon are characteristic of the atmospheric entry of a natural meteorite. GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of a bolide, caused by the atmospheric entry of a small astronomical body.
