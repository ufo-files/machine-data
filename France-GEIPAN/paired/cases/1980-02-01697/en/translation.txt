Summary: Observation of several bright points
--- seg-653654618a7c4932f33aae8b [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 29, 1980, between 8:30 PM and 9:40 PM, two witnesses observed a peculiar lighting phenomenon multiple times. An object composed of several spheres moved very high in the sky. The object resembled "a brooch" with several blue and pink spheres. The overall observed shape was oval, and on the right side, a brighter, flickering light was noticed. No noise was heard during the observations. As the object moved, it took the form of a glowing bar and suddenly disappeared. No further information was received about this phenomenon, for which we lack data.
