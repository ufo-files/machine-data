Summary: Sequential observations in September of the passage of aircraft preceded by luminous phenomena: probable observations of military aircraft.
--- seg-810c3629748f09db229bc292 [needs-review] ---
Description: The witness heard the sounds of aircraft engines and observed several glowing balls moving very quickly in multiple directions. Two planes are maneuvering near these balls. The witness observed a similar phenomenon the previous week (on September 14, 2015) and considers this new observation to be a logical continuation. The witness provided two very similar statements on September 14 and 23, 2015, from the same location and at the same time (around 10:30 PM). Each time, there is a perfectly observed environment by the witness of aerial military operations with the witness's descriptions in terms of planes with their sounds and trails. Furthermore, the witness states that this made him think of "very recent exercises (early September) of Rafale planes along the coast (border between Roscoff and St-Pol-de-Leon) flying at low altitude (exercises?) for hours, they flew over our heads at low altitude!" And he specifies for September 14th that "For me, it is almost certain that on Monday, 14/9, it was military planes." In this aerial operation environment, what surprises the witness on September 14:

"- 'Two very loud and distinct sounds (like a 'missile' or a huge firework without a bang) made me look out the window.'" - a PAN that appears different from the planes flying in the sky because it is a spherical ball and at one point it released a glowing curtain towards the ground. And what surprised the witness on September 23: "- 'Several (4 or 5) 'light balls' (much brighter than the few pale stars that were visible) moving very quickly in different directions (could be mistaken for a 'ballet') appearing and disappearing alternately at high speed. - 'Two planes (identifiable by the white trails they formed) near these light balls (I only saw 2, there may have been more, given the noise).'" The phenomenon lasted approximately 1 to 2 minutes, the "light balls" disappeared quickly, only the planes remained visible and audible. The PAN area, as reported by the witness in relation to his/her location (SO or SE according to day 14 or 23), and also as plotted on the map by the witness, corresponds to a low-altitude flight zone for the military (see 2015 RTBA map). The witness therefore witnessed two aerial exercises. In such a context, what seemed strange to the witness is not necessarily strange a priori. These exercises can for example result in tests of infrared bright-light decoys that can generate strange lights for those who would have difficulty associating them with the context of maneuvers and exercises (see the article: Illuminating ammunition and military decoys (military flares) as OVNI generators). No other witness came forward with the GEIPAN to report oddities of these events that nevertheless attracted attention in the sky at least by their noise (case of this testimony). As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the cases of September 14 and 23 as B: aircraft.
