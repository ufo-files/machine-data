Description: On August 9, 1988, around 10:45 PM, a witness observed a very bright green glow moving very quickly in an elliptical pattern. A second green glow suddenly appeared and eventually merged with the first. No noise was heard, and the phenomenon was observed for approximately a quarter of an hour. No elements confirm this formally, but the phenomenon strongly resembles the effects of a laser projector.
