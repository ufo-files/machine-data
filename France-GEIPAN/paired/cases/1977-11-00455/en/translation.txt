Description: On November 15, 1977, around 6:45 a.m., a witness on a motorcycle suddenly spotted in the sky a elongated object moving rapidly towards the northwest. A few moments later, the witness observed the object fragmenting into a spray of sparks falling back to the ground, revealing a ball of several colors that continued in the same direction before completely disappearing. No particular noise was heard during the observation. Another witness at the same time saw a luminous phenomenon followed by a long trail of several colors. The gendarmerie found no trace on the ground in the affected area. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. These witnesses likely observed an atmospheric reentry.
