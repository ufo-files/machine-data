--- seg-f8449b4d048aaad5b6cc4cba [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 22, 2009, at 4:45 a.m., an amateur astronomer was intrigued by a fixed object in the sky with strong color variations (green, red, blue) and a variable magnitude (significant increase followed by decrease). The event lasted 15 seconds according to the witness. This observation strongly suggests a temporary reflection on a satellite: Iridium satellite flash or reflection on a rotating satellite. Despite his requests, the GEIPAN did not receive further details and cannot verify this hypothesis too long after the facts. This peculiar case is classified "C" due to lack of information.
