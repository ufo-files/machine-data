--- seg-22b256678dc4eade4e2d669a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 10, 2012, at 10:25 PM, a witness quickly observed a very bright, white-orange phenomenon in the sky. This intense light suddenly disappeared. The witness did not notice any noise. Only one testimony was collected regarding this phenomenon. Based on the objective elements defined in the investigation report (see document), namely the observation duration compatible with that of satellite flashes (approximately 20 seconds), the aspect of a very bright flash, the elongated shape moving slowly horizontally, and the sky position (azimuth and altitude) compatible with that of the registered and visible satellite "ISIS 1" at the observation date and time, we can conclude that this case likely involves the observation of a flash from satellite "ISIS 1" during its emergence from the Earth's shadow cone. This case should be classified as "B," a probable observation of a flash from satellite "ISIS 1." There remains uncertainty regarding the identity of the satellite source of the phenomenon.
