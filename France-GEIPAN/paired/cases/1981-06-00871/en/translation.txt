Summary: Observation of a glowing object above the sea: astronomical observation of the Moon.
--- seg-51f49c1be45e6bd0d1cf556e [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archives on its public website www.geipan.fr. Among its publications are old cases classified at the time as Type "C" cases, which are now being reassessed with the sole purpose of being more relevant in their conclusions. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over all these years, this reassessment sometimes leads to new observations or even an explicit change in classification in an investigation note. This observation case is part of cases classified as "C" in 1981 by the GEPAN. On June 7, 1981, around 11 PM, two witnesses observed in the direction of the sea a glowing object. Above this object was a star in the shape of a half-moon that gradually transformed and enlarged. The object itself did not move and disappeared after about eight minutes. The described phenomenon has many common characteristics (observation duration, shape, size, color) with a perfectly known astronomical object: the Moon at sunset. Moreover, the Moon was clearly present in the observed area of the sky, while the witnesses do not mention it. In the current classification of the GEIPAN, this case of weak strangeness is classified as PAN A, an observation related to a misperception with the Moon.
