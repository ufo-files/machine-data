Summary: Sequential observations of the movement of glowing balls descending down a hill towards a stream: probable observations of geomagnetic light phenomena.
--- seg-46a5e8eca842253adc830eda [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reports several observations of light phenomena seen from their home (they indicate in their statement the date of February 22, 2008, around 10 PM or 11 PM, but during the interview with the investigator, they refine the date to February 29, 2008, at 9:30 PM). Therefore, the witness has seen three times in two years and in the evening "fireballs" of white color. These descend from the hill down to a stream. No other testimony was collected. No traces of fire on the ground were found. After verification with an expert on atmospheric transient luminous phenomena (lightning, plasmas), it appears that all parameters are consistent with geomagnetic light phenomena ("Earth balls" or "earth lights") related to geological faults: presence of an electric line and a water course oriented in the direction of the flow of the spheres. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", very probable observations of geomagnetic light phenomena.
