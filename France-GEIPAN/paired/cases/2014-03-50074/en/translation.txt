Description: On March 19, 2014, at 10:15 PM, a person closing their shutters observed several cylindrical and glowing objects in the sky (see the investigation report). The witness made other similar observations. A neighbor also saw very bright lights in the sky, but identical to the stars. Only one testimony was collected. Following the on-site investigation, it appears highly likely that the strange shapes observed by the witness are the result of vision problems (cataract and presbyopia). As the observations repeat, they should cease after the witness' cataract surgery scheduled for August 2014. Confirmation or denial should also be provided by the receipt of the questionnaire from witness #2 (the neighbor). Pending either of these pieces of information, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable star observations by a witness with impaired vision.
