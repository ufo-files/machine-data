--- seg-be68e93fb9dba3e13219d625 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 13, 2006, at 10:00 a.m., a witness, along with others, observed a stationary luminous phenomenon above the Iroise Bridge for 3 to 4 minutes. This phenomenon was circular. It disappeared in an upward trajectory into the sky. The reported testimony is very brief. Given the response from the maritime region, there is a strong probability that the witness saw a small plane (a white single-engine civil aircraft was indeed in this area at the indicated time).
