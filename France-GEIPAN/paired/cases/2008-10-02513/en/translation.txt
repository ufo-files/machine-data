Description: Testimonies collected in November 2009 regarding an old observation made in October 2008 (the witness cannot recall the exact date but indicates the end of the month). Around 6 a.m., two witnesses, traveling in their utility vehicle, begin their route. They initially notice a very bright phenomenon resembling a star, then later, above the houses of the village they are driving through, they observe a huge black triangle with a red light in the center. This observation includes two phenomena that may or may not be related:

- The bright white light, which is too bright to be Saturn, which is located in a nearby direction.
- The black triangle with a red light: this could be an airplane, but the witness would have reported one of the position lights and, more importantly, the flashing of the collision lights. It is noted that the movement of this object would be consistent with that of a conventional aircraft: it is the shape and the lighting that make it unusual. The late date of the testimony did not allow for verification with aviation authorities. This observation case is of medium to high strangeness, and the investigation conducted has significantly strengthened its consistency. None of the usual hypotheses can adequately explain the observation of the triangular object described by the witnesses. This case is classified as "D1" (unexplained).
