Description: On December 21, 2018, between 6:55 PM and 7:00 PM, a driver was surprised to see 5 or 6 glowing objects moving silently in the sky. The witness draws one of the PAN she quickly saw in the sky and completes her report with an image and a newspaper article about the phenomenon. Only one testimony is collected. The vast majority of the elements provided by the witness remain consistent with the hypothesis of an observation of a group of celestial lanterns, specifically: - The reddish color with the presence around each element of a halo of the same color - The ball-shaped form of each element - The number: 5 or 6, the lanterns being released in groups - The absence of perceived noise - The observation duration of 10 minutes, compatible with the life span of the lanterns, which can last up to approximately 20 minutes - The movement, from west to east, either in the direction of the dominant wind at ground level or nearby (see wind map). We also know, via a local press article, that such a release was conducted at the time of the observation in tribute to one of the Villeneuve-sur-Lot yellow vest spokespersons who had passed away the previous day (see article: "Insolite: qui a vu les ovnis au-dessus de Villeneuve-sur-Lot?" [Strange: Who Saw the UFOs Over Villeneuve-sur-Lot?]). The only strangeness is that of a "tube" or "neon" connecting the balls to each other, two by two. It may be a LED light rope. Case classification B: Very probable observation of sky lanterns.
