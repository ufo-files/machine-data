Summary: Observations of the movement of glowing balls.
--- seg-1ec7b05ba96948c0c95d30e2 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness reports that as a child during the summer of 1994 (the witness cannot recall the exact month and day; they are given here arbitrarily), they observed the movements of a glowing ball. This one, of a reddish-orange color, was the size of a ball bearing and very close to the witness. It moved silently. The witness was and remains deeply impressed. In 2006, the same witness observed the furtive and more distant movements of glowing balls on two occasions. No other testimony was collected by the gendarmerie regarding similar observations in the area. Despite an IPN investigation with the witness, this recent testimony of old observations lacks data.
