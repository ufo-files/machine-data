Description: On March 3, 2004, around 4 PM, several witnesses observed the fall of an object. This object caused a ground impact felt by the witnesses and recorded by the Institute of Global Physics in Strasbourg. Aerial maneuvers in the area with the presence of several helicopters were taking place at the time of the observation. The observation was classified as B, which could be explained by the fall of a meteorite or a ground impact related to the aerial maneuvers.
