Description: On February 24, 1980, around 3:15 AM, a group of young people spotted an orange glow through the pines towards the beach. Moving to gain better visibility, they then saw a very large object heading towards the island of Yeu, which was three-quarters spherical, orange in color, and not shiny. This craft moved vertically and at a very slow and constant speed. The observation, which lasted about ten minutes, ended when the object slowly descended into the ocean, revealing a pale glow. No noise was heard. Around 3:05 AM, a gendarme on patrol also spotted a half-spherical object, larger than a quarter moon, red-orange in color, and not shiny. This object moved slowly and descended vertically without changing shape or color. All these individuals had been making an astronomical observation of the moon setting (exactly at 3:12 AM) which explains the object's size and orange color observed. Furthermore, the moon was in its first quarter, giving it the appearance of a half-disk facing down.
