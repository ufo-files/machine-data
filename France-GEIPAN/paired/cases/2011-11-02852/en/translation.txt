Description: On November 26, 2011, at 4:53 a.m., a witness observed the passage of a bright light with a green trail. The witness's description closely matches a meteor: intense light with a green trail, straight trajectory, lasting for a few seconds. A "bolide" refers to the phenomenon caused by the atmospheric entry of a "large" meteorite (over 100 grams, or a few centimeters to 1 meter in size). Since this observation was also recorded on the REFORME network (meteor observers) at the same time (within a minute) by several observers, there is no doubt about the meteoritic origin of the phenomenon. REFORME records the magnitude as -10, making it an exceptional object. To view the video online: (November 26, 2011 bolide).
