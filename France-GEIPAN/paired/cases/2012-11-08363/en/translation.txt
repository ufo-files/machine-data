Summary: Observations of two red-orange glowing balls successively appearing in the sky and moving towards the sea: likely observations of the passage of two Thai lanterns.
--- seg-fd6e4898cf692336de5a11a4 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 2, 2012, around 11 PM, three individuals were intrigued by the passage in the sky, at five-minute intervals, of two red light orbs. These objects moved slowly towards the sea and disappeared on an ascending trajectory. This observation exhibits all characteristics of Thai lanterns: orange-red lights moving silently at low altitude according to the wind (see Meteociel report: light northwest wind), on a weekend evening. The hazy appearance above the lanterns could correspond to steam emitted by the humid and heated envelope, or to smoke produced by the burner. Although the witness does not validate this hypothesis, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of two Thai lanterns.
