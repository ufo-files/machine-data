Summary: Observation of the passage of three consecutive bright points: observation of Yaogan 20A, B, and C satellite triplet.
--- seg-cc273a50cd43918634227649 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 1, 2015, between midnight and 12:15 a.m., a witness observed in the clear sky a bright point moving across the sky. Two seconds later, the witness again observed the passage of two other bright points. The three points followed each other at the same speed, moving strictly in the same direction. No flickering or particular noises were noticed. The points disappeared due to the brightness of the Moon. The observation lasted between 15 and 20 seconds. No other testimonies were collected. Due to its strangeness and average consistency (single witness, precise description), this case turns out to be a misidentification with a particular triplet of satellites: the Chinese military satellites Yaogan 20A, B, and C. The PAN indeed presents all typical characteristics of such a triplet: luminosity, trajectory, and timing perfectly consistent. This case is classified as A, observation of Yaogan 20A, B, and C satellite triplet.
