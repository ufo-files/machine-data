Summary: Long observation of a very bright and stationary light: mistaken for the planet Venus.
--- seg-d88bf3a87bd8034549d7a6ba [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 5, 2009, between 8:45 PM and 9:00 PM, a driver observes a bright light in the sky. Intrigued, he parks and takes pictures of the PAN with his camera. The observation lasts between 10 and 12 minutes during which the PAN appears stationary. The observation of the PAN will be featured in news articles in the newspapers L'Est Eclair and Libération Champagne. With a low strangeness rating but sufficient consistency (many coherent and precise details that validate the case, photos), this case turns out to be a classic misidentification with a perfectly known astronomical object: the planet Venus. Furthermore, Venus was indeed present in the observed area of the sky, something the witness does not mention, and it was particularly bright. The issue is not the witness's visual perception but the interpretation the witness makes of his observation through his feelings (surprise, driving at night). This case is classified A, misidentification with Venus.
