Description: On January 28, 1983 at 12:10 AM, a motorist spotted a spinning object to his right. The craft was 2 or 3 meters above the ground and emitted low-intensity red and green lights. The object zigzagged before rising in very high spirals into the air and disappearing. No further information could be gathered about this phenomenon.
