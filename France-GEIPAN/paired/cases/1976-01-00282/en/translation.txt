Summary: Observations of the slow passage of a red object at high altitude.
--- seg-099a1d49df82e51cb8aaa7b6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 13, 1976, around 11 PM, a witness observed with binoculars the movement of an elongated object that was red in color. No noise was heard, and the object gradually disappeared. This witness made the same observation on January 15 at the same time. The gendarmerie made similar observations on January 17 and 18 between 10:50 PM and 11:30 PM according to a SSE-NNO trajectory. The descriptions collected resemble descriptions of satellite passes, but we lack data to validate this hypothesis.
