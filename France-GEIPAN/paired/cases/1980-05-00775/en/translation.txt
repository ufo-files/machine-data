Description: On May 26, 1980, around 7 PM, two hikers observed an unusual object in the clear sky. A navigator mechanic officer, one of the witnesses, described an object appearing metallic, with a round base and a dome-shaped upper part, silver in color. The object was stationary at 200 to 300 meters above the ground, seemingly oscillating on its axis. Its lower part was darker and exhibited intermittent red luminescence. Over the 6 minutes of observation, no noise or condensation was noticed. The object disappeared into the clouds, marking successive levels. At Basel/Mulhouse Airport, a radar echo was recorded but was drowned in private and general aviation traffic, making it of little use. No other witnesses or information were gathered in France or in the German border area. This phenomenon remains unexplained.
