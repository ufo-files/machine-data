Description: On December 27, 2015, at 6:35 PM, a witness observed two circular shapes flying slightly offset from one another in a clear sky. The trajectory was south-north (or northeast). The witness noticed a detail on the second shape: a red glowing point faded out after two seconds. The observation lasted less than 10 seconds in total. No other testimonies were collected. The observation of several "white circular shapes flying side by side" strongly suggests Thai lanterns. The movement was observed from south to north, which is consistent with the wind direction reported in Lille and Cambrai (10 km apart) (see the attached weather document). The angular velocity appears rapid (50° in 10 seconds) but compatible with the wind speeds recorded (20 km/h), considering a possible error or uncertainty of a factor of 2 in the actual wind speed on the spot, the lantern's altitude (50 or 100 meters), or the observation duration (10 or 20 seconds). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
