Description: On August 19, 2012, between 01:10 (qt) and 01:21 (mail), a witness observed in the sky a luminous phenomenon of color that intrigued them. No other testimony was collected. Of average strangeness but weak consistency (single witness, concise testimony, contradictions, short observation, excessive interpretation), this case proves too incomplete to be identified. Thus, it is unclear whether the PAN was observed near the horizon or high in the sky. The duration of the observation phases is also questionable, as they are described as lasting several minutes in some accounts and only a few seconds in others (see the investigation report). Therefore, the description of the observation is too incomplete to be properly analyzed. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to lack of information and cross-references.
