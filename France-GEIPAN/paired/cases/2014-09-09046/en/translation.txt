Description: On September 12, 2014, at 7:50 PM, a witness is intrigued by a sky phenomenon he cannot identify. The weather is clear, the sun is setting, the witness is looking west, observing what appears to be a "rocket" almost stationary at the beginning of the observation, with a "V"-shaped smoke trail. Only one testimony was collected. This type of observation clearly resembles an airplane contrail seen at sunset. Specific information about airplane contrails, published on June 4, 2013, can be found on the GEIPAN website, in the News tab: "Strange Airplane Contrails."
