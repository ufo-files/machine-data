Description: On Sunday, August 26, 2012 at 10:07 PM, a witness was intrigued by the quick and silent passage of a white glowing phenomenon across the sky. Only one questionnaire is received, but other witnesses in the South-West are emailing the GEIPAN. This very short observation of a glowing ball crossing a portion of the sky in 1 second suggests an atmospheric reentry of a natural meteoroid, an event called a bolide. The GEIPAN received 3 other simultaneous reports in southwestern France: in Agen (47), Toulouse (31), and Martel (46). The Futura-Sciences website also documented several sightings of the meteor: Futura-sciences. Let us remember that these phenomena occur at very high altitudes (from 80 km to 30 km) and are therefore visible from the ground over an area of approximately 500 km in diameter. They are always perceived as close by the witnesses (see A Meteor? ). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of a bolide: atmospheric reentry of a natural meteorite.
