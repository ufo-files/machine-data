Description: On Thursday, August 19, 2010, around 2:30 AM, a witness and their companions were intrigued by the presence in the sky of bright, silent, stationary or moving objects (designated 1 and 2 in the testimony). The witness made another observation on August 21, 2010, between 6:00 and 6:30 AM (objects designated 3 and 4). Objects 1 and 3 are very likely Jupiter, and object 4 is undoubtedly the International Space Station (ISS). Object 2, also stationary and located to the East on August 19, could be the star Capella (az. 47 and 24° elevation and magnitude 0.05). A typical orientation error margin of around 45° is commonly found in the testimonies. Of low strangeness and average consistency, this observation case is the result of several astronomical and astronautic misperceptions.
