Description: On July 30, 2010, around 9 PM, on the beach of Golfe-Juan, several people observed the rapid and silent passage in the sky of a bright white opaque light phenomenon. Only one testimony was collected. The object's characteristics given in the testimony (a very bright sphere moving at extremely high speed for one and a half seconds before disappearing) perfectly match the appearance of a meteor and, in this case, a "large" shooting star. This case is classified as B probable observation of a meteor.
