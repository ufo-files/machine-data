Summary: Observation of the rapid movement of a bright circular object in the sky: Lack of information.
--- seg-cf94d9cdf088c9f576f0f1e0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 1, 2011, at 8:33 PM, a witness observed a silent, 30-second passage of a bright, circular object in the sky. The direction of the observed movement, from East-Southeast to West-Northwest, is consistent with wind direction. However, the description of the PAN, a perfect disk-shaped object of brown color with a slightly lighter or brighter circle in its center, does not match any of the commonly observed aerostats (Thai lanterns or balloons). Finally, the relative speed of the movement (observed for 30 seconds before the PAN was obscured by buildings) leads us to believe that the object was moving faster than the wind (in similar conditions, Thai lantern observations last at least one minute). Given the originality of the observed object and its relatively good consistency, this observation case is provisionally classified as C due to a lack of corroboration. This type of observation leaves few leads to investigate, and an on-site investigation would yield little information.
