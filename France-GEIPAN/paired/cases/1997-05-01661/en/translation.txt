Summary: Observation of the rapid passage of a round light shape
--- seg-1f7977d656f0a61d8d2195c1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 3, 1997, around 01:30, a couple returning from work observed a brighter point in the sky than the stars. This point approached at high speed, and the witnesses were able to observe a large, rather round, luminous mass that passed just overhead of the housing development and disappeared to the south. An air-displacement noise was perceived during the passage of the phenomenon. The total observation duration was around 5 minutes. No explanation could be found for this phenomenon.
