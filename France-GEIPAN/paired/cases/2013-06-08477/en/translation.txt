Description: A witness observed on June 9, 2013, at approximately 0:45 a.m., the presence and subsequent north-south movement of a very bright, orange-red object. The object disappeared behind a hill. No unusual noises were heard during the 4-5 minute observation. This observation of a strong red-orange light in the evening of the weekend, moving slowly in the sky, is likely a Thai lantern released from Saint-Jean-de-Verges following a celebration. Weather reports from Saint-Girons (see weather document) indicate a light, very unstable wind during the night; however, the wind at Crampagna cannot be precisely determined, especially given that the area is in a small mountain range. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
