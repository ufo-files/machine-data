Description: The two witnesses were driving on D654 between Terraube (32) and Cologne (32) on 11/23/2023 between 10:30 and 11:00 a.m. when they observed a black, generally round object in the sky moving erratically. The consistency* is good, although only one testimony was collected (T1), the associated video, despite its brief duration, was able to be used in the analysis. *According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information collected for a testimony. The aeronautical situation analysis highlighted, along the line of sight and at a compatible time, the presence of a dark gray military transport plane C-17 (see the investigation report). It is highly likely that this aircraft was briefly spotted by the witness, visually breaking away either against the blue sky background or against the present white cloud formations, before disappearing from view, allowing the main witness (T1) to grab his smartphone to photograph and film the PAN. The detailed analysis of the provided video, and in particular the initial frames, has revealed: - A motion effect of the PAN occurring twice, in identical directions and amplitudes to that of internal vehicle elements (for example, parts of the dashboard or rear-view mirrors), which cannot be coincidental, suggesting it is likely a reflection or optical artifact caused by light and the vehicle's glass surface, rather than a real object in the sky. (See the .gif animation). - A defocusing effect, causing a significant reduction in depth of field on certain images while maintaining sharpness for both the PAN and internal vehicle elements, suggesting the observed object was very small. This focusing phenomenon, which primarily affects objects close to the camera while maintaining a clear image of the vehicle's internal elements, strongly indicates that the PAN was located just behind the vehicle's windshield. The following images are unusable, due to both excessive camera shake during the vehicle's movement and the PAN exiting the video field of view. The main witness naturally assimilated the two stimuli into one due to their similar appearance and spatial and temporal conjunction. The case is classified "A": observation of a military C17 plane and a very small and very close object located immediately next to or on the car's windshield.
