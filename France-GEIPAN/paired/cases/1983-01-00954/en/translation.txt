--- seg-148b64ae8820c3844843c3fd [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 12, 1983, at 6:30 PM, several witnesses will spot a yellow sphere silently moving at very low altitude. This sphere is followed by a cluster of small blue flames. Suddenly, the whole thing disintegrates, leading the witnesses to believe that something fell nearby. As soon as the authorities are notified, several searches in the area are conducted but without result. It is likely that the witnesses observed an atmospheric reentry.
