Description: On December 11, 2014, at 3:35 AM, a witness closes their window when they observe three equally spaced bright points. It is this exact point that intrigues the witness and makes them think of a particular observation. Afraid, they do not look at the sky again until around 3:50 AM, but the points have disappeared. No other testimonies are recorded. Due to its strangeness and low consistency, this case appears to be a possible misidentification with the three stars that form the asterism known as Orion's Belt. Unfortunately, the lack of data (precise direction of observation, angular spacing between the points of PAN) and the witness's state of fear during the observation prevent any useful exploitation of the case. This case is classified C, an unidentified case due to a lack of data, although a misidentification with the stars of Orion's Belt is possible.
