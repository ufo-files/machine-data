Summary: Observation of a cylindrical-shaped craft flying over a college, 5 to 6 meters in diameter, and silent.
--- seg-5da82239489fc8c657e06707 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 4, 1990, around 12:40 PM, approximately ten children observed a black, oval (or cylindrical, according to testimonies) object with 'legs' and 'windows' in the courtyard of a college. The object silently passed approximately 150 meters above the college. Alerted teachers reported nothing. On January 16, in a neighboring commune, a weather balloon launched from Bordeaux was found. Reconstruction of the balloon's shape from debris identified the observation as this balloon, the 'legs' being in fact the lower part of the radar reflector.
