Description: On February 26, 2023, at 1 a.m., the witness observed from their home a fiery flying object with orange flames, moving at the same speed and altitude as a tourist plane. The PAN appeared to be coming from the commune of Silly-le-Long and heading towards Roissy Charles de Gaulle Airport, moving from the northwest to the southeast. The case consistency is low, with only a gendarmerie report as documentation, no technical questionnaire filled out, nor any photos or videos. Nevertheless, the witness's description in the report (fiery red color, orange flames) points towards the hypothesis of observing a sky lantern. A lantern is a passive object carried by the wind. The "orange flames" aspect aligns perfectly. Meteorological data from a station 18 kilometers from the witness reports a 18 km/h wind from the northwest to the southeast, which seems consistent with the PAN's movement direction (see the attached weather extract). Additionally, the observation occurred during the night from Saturday to Sunday: weekends are generally common and conducive to lantern releases. The case is classified as A: highly probable observation of a sky lantern.
