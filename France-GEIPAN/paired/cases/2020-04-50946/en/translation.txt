Summary: Brief observation of the rapid and straight passage of a white object in the sky: probable observation of an aircraft.
--- seg-099386c15d18740db09d0c9c [machine-unreviewed] ---
**Report:** On April 5, 2020, at 12:55, a witness in their garden observed a white object passing quickly across the sky on a straight trajectory from east to west. No noise was heard, and no trail was observed. Only one testimony was collected. This testimony is of average consistency: one witness, no photos, it was a very short observation, but the provided information remains clear and relevant (see the investigation report). After analysis, the observation is not particularly strange because this type of observation reported to GEIPAN is fairly common. The observation parameters are as follows:

- PAN moving in a straight line,
- Round, white object, quite bright,
- The sun was behind the witness,
- Moderate elevation of PAN: 20°.

The hypothesis of observing an airplane, specifically the airplane's fuselage brightly lit by the sun, seems probable. The strong illumination, coupled with a low elevation, prevents distinguishing the airplane's wings. The absence of condensation trail is explained by the fact that the airplane was probably not at a sufficient altitude for one to form under adequate temperature, pressure, and humidity conditions. The distance between the witness and the airplane is likely relatively modest, giving the impression of high speed, which is, however, only apparent. This reduced distance did not allow the witness to hear the airplane since the wind was not carrying the sound. Finally, the very short observation did not allow the witness to observe in detail all parameters and identify the airplane.

Our study leads GEIPAN to conclude that it is a probable observation of an airplane, an airplane observed under conditions of sun reflection, absence of condensation trail, absence of noise perception, and apparent high speed. This case is classified as "B," probable airplane observation.
