Description: On October 9, 2016 at 12:13 AM, two witnesses on a terrace were intrigued by the presence several kilometers away of two luminous phenomena, one heading towards Corsica and the other towards Nice. Climbing to an upper floor on another terrace, the witness took photographs of these two identical and stationary forms. The two PANs (presumed to be ships) disappeared abruptly. Only one testimony was collected. The hypothesis of two cruise ships sailing at sea on maritime routes seems the most plausible explanation for the observed phenomena (see the investigation report). This hypothesis is supported by: • the determination, through comparison with a day photograph, of the horizon line on which the PAN 1 ship was located, and by correlation for the second PAN; • measurements and calculations of distances, lengths, and speeds are all consistent with the ships and their positions on the maritime route passing offshore; • the sudden disappearance of the PANs, unnoted by the witnesses, can be explained by the sudden extinguishing of the ships' lights as they moved away from the coast near San Remo; • finally, the witness's difficulty in discerning the horizon is explained by the uniformity of the colors of the sky and sea at night. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": probable observation of two cruise ships sailing at sea on maritime routes.
