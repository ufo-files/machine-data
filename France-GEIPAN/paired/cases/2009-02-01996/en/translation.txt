Description: On Sunday, February 15, 2009, around 8:00 PM, two people in a car observed in the sky a bright orange ball-shaped phenomenon moving quite quickly. The observation lasted a few seconds before the phenomenon disappeared. This case could have been linked to Hautefort on the same day, almost at the same time (30 minutes earlier), but the witness did not complete a questionnaire and we have no details to draw any conclusions. The witness mentions a probable meteoroid return (meteorite fall) but we do not have the direction of the trajectory followed by the phenomenon. This case is classified "C" due to a lack of information.
