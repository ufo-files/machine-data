Description: On April 14, 2013, around 1:30 AM, a driver observes for a few seconds the straight passage of a black sphere that disappears behind trees. No other witnesses have been found. This observation of a black ball cannot be explained by classic misperceptions. However, the relative distance and the linear movement of the phenomenon make it a relatively unremarkable observation. The fact that the witness was alone and only saw it for a few seconds after a very long day makes this testimony unreliable: an illusion, a misperception with a nocturnal bird, or a vision problem due to fatigue cannot be ruled out. The relatively low strangeness of the case does not justify a thorough investigation. To date, the gendarmerie protocol has not been received by the GEIPAN. For now, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to lack of information and cross-references.
