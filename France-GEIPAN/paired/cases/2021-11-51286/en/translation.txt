**Description:** On November 27, 2021, around 1:30 PM, a resident (T1) of TOULON (83) spotted a bright point of light while looking from her balcony. The observation lasted a maximum of 2 minutes, but she managed to take two photos. Then, the PAN (presumed Unidentified Flying Object) disappeared behind a hill. T1's companion (T2) also observed the PAN but did not provide a statement. Despite low strangeness and good consistency (based on a single witness report, with two witnesses and photos of the PAN), this case is determined to be the observation of a festive mylar (aluminized) balloon in the shape of an 8 (as detailed in the investigation report). According to GEIPAN criteria, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered from a witness report. The visual aspect of the PAN in the first photograph strongly resembles an 8-shaped mylar balloon. Several elements support this hypothesis: the observed gray hue contrasting with the light reflection on the surface facing the sun, a characteristic of reflective mylar material; as well as the trajectory and apparent speed of the PAN, generally consistent with the direction and force of the wind at the time of observation. Minor variations in trajectory can be explained by local aerological conditions, such as wind shear or the presence of hilly terrain. It is possible that this balloon was released during a birthday party (observation made on a Saturday). The case is classified as "A," observation of a festive mylar balloon.
