Summary: Observation of the silent low-altitude movement of several color-varied forms quickly flashing: probable LED balloons.
--- seg-eb473ba66c1fa4263701b9a1 [failed-protected-token-check] ---
Description: On Sunday, August 5, 2012, shortly after 10:00 PM, a young couple returning from NICE to their home, took the D2564 and were surprised to see, while driving and then stopped at the side of the road, the silent movement of 5 to 6 colored and rapidly blinking shapes. These shapes moved westward on a descending trajectory and were quickly hidden by a hill. The observation lasted less than a minute. Only one statement was collected. The witnesses' lack of knowledge about glowing balloons is undoubtedly the cause of the strangeness and the reason for providing this statement. After study (see the investigation report), the observed phenomenon would consist of several glowing balloons with bright and changing colors attributable to LEDs inside. These balloons, inflated with helium, would thus be carried by the east wind blowing that night: the speed and trajectory would be consistent for a distance of approximately 300 meters or less; this would also explain the absence of noise. The presence of 2 dead-end roads with very low traffic above the observation location could reasonably have been the setting for this "balloon party." Finally, it was a summer evening and a weekend, conducive to such festive events. The GEIPAN classifies this case as B (probably identified phenomenon): LED balloons; observation of glowing balloons whose release author could not be found.
