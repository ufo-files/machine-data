Summary: Observation of the silent low-altitude movement of several color-varied forms quickly flashing: probable LED balloons.
--- seg-eb473ba66c1fa4263701b9a1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Sunday, August 5, 2012, shortly after 22:00, a young couple returning home from NICE take the D2564 and are surprised to see, first while driving and then while stopped at the roadside, 5 to 6 shapes of various colours moving silently and flashing rapidly. These shapes move westwards on a descending path and are very quickly hidden by a hill. The observation lasts less than a minute. Only one witness account is collected. The witnesses' lack of knowledge of the existence of luminous balloons is without any doubt the cause of the perceived strangeness and the reason for submitting this account. After study (see the investigation report), the observed phenomenon would consist of various luminous balloons whose bright, changing colours would be attributable to LEDs inside them. These helium-filled balloons would thus be carried by the easterly wind blowing that evening: the speed and path would be consistent with a PAN/witness distance of approximately 300 at most; this would also explain the absence of noise. The presence of 2 roads without an exit and with very little traffic above the observation location could reasonably have provided the setting for this "balloon party". Finally, it is a summer weekend evening, suitable for festive events of this kind. GEIPAN classifies this case as B (phenomenon probably identified): LED balloons; observation of luminous balloons whose releaser could not be found.
