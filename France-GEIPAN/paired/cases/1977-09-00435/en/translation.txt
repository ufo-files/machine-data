--- seg-36a8285c615806457596c8c5 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: In the evening of September 30, 1977, numerous people observed in the sky the presence of objects evolving very slowly at high altitude and emitting flashing lights of various colors. The events occurred above the city of Sens and in the region of Brienon-sur-Armancon in the Yonne department. It is likely that the observed phenomenon was an important traffic of general aerial circulation, unusual in the region, due to the activation of the Sens hold circuit.
