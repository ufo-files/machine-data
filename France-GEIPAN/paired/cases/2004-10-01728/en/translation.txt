Summary: Cylindrical object taking off from a property.
--- seg-38eca5c381cfa3ec067bd3e7 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 6, 2004, between 11:00 PM and 11:30 PM, two witnesses observed a luminous phenomenon 150 meters from their residence. The object was cylindrical in shape, approximately 1 meter thick and 1.5 meters in circumference; bright, white and red lights could also be seen. The object slowly rose into the air, leaving a neighboring property with no trace detected. Following an oblique trajectory, the object disappeared into the clouds. No further information was gathered about this phenomenon.
