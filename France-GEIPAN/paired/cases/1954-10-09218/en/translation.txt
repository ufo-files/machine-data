Description: On October 14, 1954, around 5 PM, four witnesses observed in the south-east direction and at an altitude of 130 feet (40 meters) a spherical object moving silently but at a very high speed. The object had colors similar to a rainbow. A brief intelligence report was filed for the GEIPAN without witness statements. The description of a spherical, high-speed, multi-colored object suggests a meteor. This too-brief case is classified C due to lack of information.
