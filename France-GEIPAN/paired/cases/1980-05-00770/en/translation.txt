Description: On May 13, 1980, around 4:45 a.m., a witness, walking to work, was surprised by the appearance in the sky of a very bright green ball. It moved on a south-north trajectory at very low altitude. A spray of yellow sparks followed the ball. The observation lasted less than 5 seconds and no noise was noticed. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. This person likely witnessed an atmospheric reentry.
