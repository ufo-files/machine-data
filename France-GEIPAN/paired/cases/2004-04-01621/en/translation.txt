Summary: 2004. Observations of the slow movement of a very bright point amidst a cluster of stars: observation of a rare configuration (every eight years) of the planet Venus amidst the Pleiades star cluster.
--- seg-58230fe1bd27cf65f27467ff [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of April 2, 2004, several witnesses observed a very bright light to the West among the stars. At the scene, gendarmes determined that the light was at approximately 30° to the horizon and appeared to be moving very slowly. A gendarmerie investigation will show that the observed point was Venus seen among a cluster of stars, the Pleiades, often mistaken for the Little Bear. This phenomenon is rare and occurs every eight years. The case is classified A: Venus observations.
