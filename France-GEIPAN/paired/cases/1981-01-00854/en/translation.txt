Description: On January 26, 1981, around 11:15 PM, a skywatcher in his car noticed, in a clear sky without clouds, a flash of light lasting 3 seconds. Intrigued, he leaned out his window and saw a bright white point of light descending rapidly. The observation lasted 6 to 7 seconds. The light emitted then became intense and abruptly cut out. The limited information provided in this description suggests a possible atmospheric reentry.
