Summary: Observations by numerous witnesses of a probable large-scale atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-f3ab18ac9204266029d83e28 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 10, 1991, around 9:30 PM, numerous witnesses across all departments in southwestern France observed for a few seconds a very bright white light moving very quickly with a significant trail in a generally indicated north-south direction. Some witnesses also heard a loud explosion accompanying the phenomenon. A local newspaper article allowed many witnesses to come forward. The characteristics of the observation favor the hypothesis of a large-scale atmospheric reentry, probably natural given the observed direction.
