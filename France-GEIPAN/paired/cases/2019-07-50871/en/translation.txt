Description: On July 14, 2019, the witness is at a friend's house. At 8:35 PM, he notices an object, of anthracite color, matte but shiny in the sun, appearing to approach him at a fairly rapid speed. He says nothing to his friends who will later see the PAN on the witness's camera. The witness loses sight of the PAN as it moves away in a SSE direction. The total observation duration is 25 seconds. Only one testimony about this phenomenon will be collected. The case is consistent: one testimony, one photo, and additional information provided by the witness later (see the investigation report). The investigation, in particular the photo analysis using the IPACO software and the wind analysis, concluded that it is likely a lost helium-filled balloon, 0.80 cm in size, in the shape of a fish, or at least very probably an elongated shape. The strangeness perceived by the witness results from the following elements: shape type, absence of noise, speed of movement, and is also explained by the rarity of such an observation. A rarity emphasized by the witness, who cannot explain what he observed. The GEIPAN is classified as B: probable observation of a festive balloon carried away by the wind.
