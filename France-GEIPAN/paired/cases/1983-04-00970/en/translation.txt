Summary: Probable observation of the planet Venus
--- seg-cff1e58321ee442d43bf5fb2 [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 13, 1983, between 10:45 PM and 11:05 PM, two motorists observed in the sky at a very high altitude a very bright sphere. This sphere, about the size of a handball, was yellowish-green in color with variable brightness. When observed through binoculars, the sphere appeared to make rapid vertical movements but never moved more than 40 to 50 cm from its initial position, returning to that point. Due to the unbearable brightness, the witnesses stopped their observation. An examination of the sky map for the day of the observation reveals that Venus was quite low on the horizon (less than 5°) at a bearing of 300°, corresponding to the angle of observation. Therefore, the witnesses were likely impressed by Venus' intense brightness.
