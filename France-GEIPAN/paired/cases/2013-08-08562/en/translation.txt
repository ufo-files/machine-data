Summary: Observation of the silent and straight-line movement of light in the sky: observation of the passage of the International Space Station (ISS).
--- seg-ecbad134da5f751e08c4fb1e [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 17, 2013, around 11 PM, two people were intrigued by the presence of a very bright light moving silently in the sky on a straight trajectory. The description of the phenomenon corresponds exactly to the observation of the International Space Station (ISS). The passage logs of this station (source Calsky.com) indicate that it was well visible that evening from Pontarlier at 11:15 PM, which corresponds to the time indicated by the witness (approximate time, according to the witness). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the ISS.
