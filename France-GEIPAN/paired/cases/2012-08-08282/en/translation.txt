Description: On August 8, 2012, at 11:45, a witness and those around them observed for 15 seconds the straight and constant speed passage of a gray-white point in the sky. No trail was noticed. Only one testimony was collected. The witness, looking south, saw an object pass from his left to his right, that is, from east to west. He observed that the apparent size of this object diminished during its passage. This testimony suggests the passage of a balloon, of an undetermined shape, probably at very low altitude as its apparent size quickly decreases. The weather report for Limoges indicates an east-northwesterly wind at that time, which is consistent with this hypothesis. The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a toy balloon, thermal balloon, or helium-filled balloon.
