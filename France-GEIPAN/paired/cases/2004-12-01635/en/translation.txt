Summary: Discovery of a hole in a residential secondary parking lot with no PAN observation: lack of information.
--- seg-5f192ac213e9ff1131405477 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 25, 2004, the gendarmerie received a phone call reporting the discovery of a hole in the ground at a secondary residence parking lot. The owner of the residence believed it could be a hole caused by a meteorite. The gendarmes immediately went to the scene, and were joined by a fire department team for soil testing. Observations indicated a hole approximately 15 cm in diameter and 20 to 25 cm deep.

In accordance with the agreement with the gendarmerie, the incident report was forwarded to GEIPAN. However, the owner made no observation of PAN, and no object (such as a meteorite) was found in the hole.

Previously classified as B: probable meteorite fall, this case is outside the GEIPAN perimeter due to the absence of observation of PAN and the absence of an object on the ground.

This case is now classified as C: lack of information.
