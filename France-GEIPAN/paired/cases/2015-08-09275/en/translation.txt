Description: On August 7, 2015, at 3:00 a.m., a witness observed through the foliage four fixed, bright silver lights with strong sparkling. The lights are side by side, two by two on the same horizontal line. The witness observed the presence of these lights until 4:30 a.m. and then decided to go to bed. No other testimonies were collected. Fixed lights seen through low-hanging trees (from the first floor) could have various potential ground origins a priori. Here, contact with the witness established that there is a stadium behind these trees. There could have been special work done that night, or the usual lights may have remained on exceptionally all night. Consequently, the GEIPAN is classified B: probable ground lighting.
