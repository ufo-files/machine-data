Description: On Friday, April 24, 2015, between 10:30 PM and 11:00 PM, a father and his children were lying on a terrace to admire the starry sky. Suddenly, the father's gaze is drawn to a moving shadow in the sky. The witness perceives "a dark delta-wing-like shape flying at an extremely high speed towards the south" on a straight trajectory. No particular noise is heard during the 2-3 second observation. The witness loses sight of the object far away, obscured by light pollution. No other testimonies are collected. This very brief observation of a dark object could be consistent with the observation of a nocturnal raptor (owl, great horned owl) flying over the observer. The witness lives on the edge of a forest, making this hypothesis plausible. However, the GEIPAN has no elements to support this hypothesis. The witness thinks of a stealth aircraft: this type of aircraft is radar-stealthy but not particularly quiet, and usually flies at high altitude. Due to the lack of corroboration and the brevity of the testimony, the GEIPAN classifies this case as category "C".
