Description: On October 5, 1985, around 7:20 PM, a motorist and their passengers will witness a significant luminescent phenomenon. During the movement of this phenomenon, they will see a triangular shape with flashing green and red lights. No noise was heard, and the estimated speed was 80 to 100 km/h. During the investigation, it will be established that 25-minute training flights over the same sector took place with a CESSNA 150-type aircraft. The pilot, who had several times illuminated the cockpit and turned on the landing light for their students, observed nothing unusual during these flights. It is likely that the witnesses saw the aircraft in question during one of its passes over the sector.
