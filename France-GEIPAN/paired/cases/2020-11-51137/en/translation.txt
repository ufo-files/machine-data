Description: The witness returns from a walk with his dog at 8:14 PM. While wiping the dog's paws, the animal barks in an unusual and repeated manner. Raising his eyes, the witness observes a dimly glowing spherical object crossing the sky. This sphere is yellow-orange in color and moves silently on a straight trajectory, apparently parallel to the ground. It quickly disappears from view, hidden by a nearby house. The observation is brief (1-3 seconds), within a narrow field of vision. Only one testimony is collected for this phenomenon. The elements provided by the witness, such as the spherical shape and yellow-orange color, suggest the observation of a luminescent object carried by the wind, like a sky lantern or an LED balloon (see the investigation report). The direction and sense of movement of the PAN from East to West are consistent with meteorological data from the Lège-Cap-Ferret and La Teste-de-Buch stations, reinforcing this hypothesis. The testimony is moderately consistent: only one witness, no photos or videos of the PAN. The sense of proximity of the PAN, the light it emitted, the absence of noise, and the dog's reaction made the observation unusual for the witness, and these factors are fully explainable by the accepted hypothesis. Consequently, the GEIPAN concludes that the observation was of a sky lantern or an LED balloon. As remote investigation did not allow for identifying the source or determining between a sky lantern and an LED balloon, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B.
