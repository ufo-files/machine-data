Description: On September 5 and 6, 1981, the Lourdes brigade was called upon to take statements from individuals who had spotted bright objects in the sky. These objects moved at high speed and without noise at low altitude. The very short observation times (5 seconds) did not allow for enough information to make any hypotheses about the observed phenomena.
