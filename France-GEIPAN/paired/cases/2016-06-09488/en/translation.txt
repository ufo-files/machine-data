Description: On Monday, June 27, 2016, around 9 PM, a witness is intrigued by a light coming from an object in ascending movement in the sky. The object spins on itself without a regular trajectory. The witness is taking photographs. The object eventually disappeared into the clouds. Only one testimony is collected. The testimony is consistent, with enough data provided and exploitable. There are, in addition, seven photographs that could be partially utilized for the analysis of the case (see the investigation report). A thorough analysis combining meteorological data (wind, clouds) and image processing of the witness's photos (photo analysis with IPACO software) established that the observation very likely involved a decorative Mylar balloon moving with the wind. The witness was unable to recognize it, having probably never observed anything like it before, or not in similar conditions. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as 'A', a very probable observation of a 1m to 2m long Mylar fantasy balloon carried by the wind.
