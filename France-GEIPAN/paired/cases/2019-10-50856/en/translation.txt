Description: On October 5, 2019, during the performance of the show "LE BAL DES OISEAUX FANTÔMES" at the "Le Puy du Fou" park, the witness, before the show started, looked around, especially up into the sky, to check for any potential birds or raptors. That's when they spotted an unusual shape moving and changing form. They filmed it. Only one testimony was collected. The witness undoubtedly observed a smoke ring produced by pyrotechnic devices used in a previous show (see Puy-du-Fou's correspondence). After contacting the security service at Puy du Fou, we confirmed that the show "Les Vikings" did take place in the early afternoon. It started 15 minutes before "Le Bal des Oiseaux Fantômes" on October 5. They indeed specified that this show uses pyrotechnics to create a "tri-flame" that regularly generates black smoke rings in the sky. This phenomenon is likely the result of an interaction between the combustion of pyrotechnic products and ambient humidity. But, thanks to favorable weather conditions, the smoke can remain stationary for several minutes. This case is comparable to that of LAGNIEU (01) on April 14, 2016. The strangeness of this case is due to the unusual appearance of the PAN, which the witness could not relate to a known phenomenon, probably having never observed anything like it before. The testimony is consistent, with enough exploitable data. This case should be classified as "A", indicating a certain observation of a smoke ring phenomenon created by a pyrotechnic show at "Le Puy du Fou" prior to the witness's show.
