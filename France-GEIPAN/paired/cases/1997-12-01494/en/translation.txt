Description: On December 27, 1997, around 7:45 a.m., a driver observed the high-speed fall of a flaming object. The phenomenon was silent and very brief, approximately 3 seconds. It was blue-yellow in color. Another witness also reported the same observation. The description given by the witnesses leads to the hypothesis of an atmospheric reentry.
