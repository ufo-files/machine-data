Summary: Observation of an object in the shape of a "clamshell"
--- seg-7dcb905341f92ba620d97d8e [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 17, 1980, at 11:15 PM, several witnesses saw two powerful headlights arrive at an altitude of approximately 200 meters. When the headlights turned left, the witnesses observed a dark object "in the shape of a scallop shell" with two fixed red lights on the sides. The object emitted a deep noise. It is very likely that the witnesses saw an unidentified aircraft that could be a dirigible balloon.
