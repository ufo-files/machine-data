Description: On Sunday, January 31, 2016, around 6:45 PM, a witness observed in the sky a set of white lights resembling halogen lights forming an immobile bar. No noise was heard. The phenomenon quickly disappeared behind a cloud. No other testimonies were collected for that day. The witness, while conducting research, discovered that a similar object had been seen in Vichy in 2012. Several aspects of this testimony are unusual: the shape (a 5° wide white light bar), the high elevation (70°), the angular immobility, and the clouds that could pass in front. Stars, planes, balloons, and lanterns seem ruled out, leaving possibly a construction helicopter carrying a beam or ramp lit up? The gendarmerie reported surveillance of EDF lines by helicopter in the preceding days or weeks but never on Sunday. Since the witness did not go to testify at the gendarmerie as requested by the GEIPAN and did not follow up on the two requests to meet with the investigator GEIPAN, it is not possible to better understand this testimony and process it. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as C: no witness response, lack of reliable information.
