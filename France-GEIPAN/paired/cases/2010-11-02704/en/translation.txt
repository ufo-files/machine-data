Summary: Unknown objects in photographs without direct observation during the taking of the pictures: probably reflections on the vehicle's window.
--- seg-5e706131c9ad14f973b0c2d9 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 17, 2010 at 9:38 a.m., a driver took photographs with his mobile phone through the window of his vehicle while in motion and noticed three months later unknown bright objects in his photos. At the time of the photos, the sky was clear with an intense early morning sun (refer to one of the witness photos). Without any police reports or detailed testimonies, these photos very likely show reflections on the vehicle window of one or more objects inside the vehicle. The gendarmerie police report arrived at GEIPAN on March 8, 2011. The witness confirms his previous observations. The gendarmerie investigation was unable to provide any additional testimony.
