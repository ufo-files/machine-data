Summary: Observation in the sky of a "vertical smoke column" from which emerges a dark point, which in turn generates a finer smoke trail: observation of the condensation trail of a commercial aircraft.
--- seg-651415b90ad7c8e8737f0a09 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 19, 2022, a group of five individuals (two women and three men) were talking in one of the streets of VIVIERS-LES-OFFROICOURT (88), when one of the people observed an atypical smoke column in the sky. The group notes that this thick column rises in an astonishingly vertical manner. While examining the phenomenon, a dark point suddenly emerges from the column and slowly moves away, leaving behind a trail of thinner, less dense smoke. The PAN then moves progressively towards the southwest before disappearing as it moves away. No noise was heard, leaving the group perplexed. The initial witness attempts to photograph the phenomenon with his phone, but the images are blurry. He then seeks a more powerful camera, without success. Intrigued, he confided in a journalist friend, who published an article in the local press. Subsequently, the witness decides to provide a statement to the gendarmerie, which directs him to GEIPAN. The photographs, after image processing, led the investigation towards an aeronautical context, revealing elements that could correspond to an aircraft flying at high altitude. Thanks to the specialized tools commonly used by GEIPAN investigators, notably data extracted from radar maps from the National Center for Air Operations (CNOA), the craft was identified as an Airbus A319-111 belonging to Air France, operating on the Paris-Florence route (see the investigation report). The initial trajectory of the aircraft was obscured by the smoke column and became visible to witnesses only after a change in course resulting in a visual separation. The object, flying at an altitude between 11,000 and 12,000 meters and initially at a distance of 58 km, then closing to 25 km from the ground, it is not surprising that no noise was heard. As for the smoke column observed by witnesses concurrently with PAN, after eliminating the track of chimneys of two large companies located in the direction of observation, investigators managed to locate the remains of a burn pile, also located in the direction of observation. The light wind allowed the smoke to rise almost vertically. The humidity and heterogeneity of the straw generated thick smoke that, in backlighting, appeared very dark to the witnesses' eyes. This fire, whether authorized or not, intentional or accidental, was located just 470 meters from the witnesses. Unfortunately for them, a bank hid it from their view, and that distance, combined with a very light crosswind, did not allow them to perceive the sound of combustion or that of potential interveners. The perceived strangeness is therefore perfectly reduced by a totally demonstrated explanation. The case is classified as "A": observation of the condensation trail of a commercial aircraft.
