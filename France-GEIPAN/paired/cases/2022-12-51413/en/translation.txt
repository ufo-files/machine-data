Description: On Wednesday, December 14, 2022, at 07:36, a witness stepped onto their balcony to smoke a cigarette when they spotted "light balls" appearing and disappearing to their left, moving chaotically, remaining in the same area for several minutes. They took photos and a video. The witness's spouse also observed the same phenomenon but did not provide a statement. The testimony is precise, dated, timed, and accompanied by photos and a video (see the investigation report). The exact location of the witnesses (their home address) and the orientation of the photos and video confirm the direction from which the phenomenon was observed. Thus, although only one witness reported their observation to GEIPAN, the consistency is good. Examining the footage reveals a perceptible movement of the "balls" consistent with wingbeats. The behavior leaves no doubt as to the nature of these "light balls." They are very likely a group of approximately thirty gulls (called gabians in Marseille) visible in the direction of the Velodrome stadium (its distinctive roof is clearly illuminated in the photos and video). The birds appear luminous due to urban lighting reflecting off their bright chests. During the investigation, the witness was also able to view their video on a tablet with a larger screen and concluded that the movements and context are entirely compatible with a formation of birds flying together, illuminated by urban lights. The peculiarity of their observation lay in their brightness, which they could not explain, and their distance, which did not allow them to identify them with the naked eye, nor could they recognize them later on their phone screen. The absence of other independent witnesses or unusual reports can be explained by the natural and identifiable nature of the phenomenon viewed from other angles or in proximity. Discussions with the witness and analysis of the photos and video lead to the conclusion that they observed a group of birds, likely gulls, illuminated by urban lights. The presence of these birds is common in this neighborhood, confirmed by the sound on the video footage where one can hear birds of this type. The case is classified as A: Observation of a group of birds illuminated by urban lights. They are probably gulls.
