Description: On December 12, 1980, around 5 a.m., a driver notices an orange light in her rearview mirror. Approximately 300 meters into the forest, an oval-shaped object emits flashing lights that change from green to red and then to blue. The witness stops to observe. The object is about 5 meters wide and 2 meters tall. Impressed, the driver continues on her way and sees the object disappear at an intersection. No further information or testimonies were collected regarding this phenomenon, for which we have limited data.
