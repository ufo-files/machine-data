Description: On March 8, 2015, around 6:45 PM, a witness (T1) out walking with his daughter observed in the sky an oval-shaped red object with black "legs" resembling feet, which landed in a field, rolled to the field's end, then took off into the air. The witness observes this shape landing in front of the garage of his residence. The witness's wife (T2), who was in her kitchen at the time, was surprised to see a red oval shape hopping around the barn and stopping in front of a tractor. This second witness approaches, but frightened, runs immediately to find her neighbor: the two women will see the object make successive jumps in a wheat field, take off, and disappear in the direction of Castelnaudary. Two witnesses give a statement at the gendarmerie. The third witness (the neighbor) will be met: she states that she only observed a distant orange shape in the sky, but provides no further details. No trace was found on the ground. We have, based on the Gendarmerie report, two witness statements: one from the husband (T1) and one from the wife (T2). What is described in the report is compatible with a landing, followed by the takeoff of a paramotor (paraglider with a motor on the pilot's back). The attached videos show paramotor takeoffs that can be performed without wind and thus in any direction relative to a light wind (see the no-wind takeoff technique). The sail of a paramotor can certainly have a red oval appearance as described. A paramotor can walk, run, take off, then rest and finally take off as described by T2. It can come from one direction and leave in another. The paramotor may present an aspect of "more than two legs" (three or four as described by T2) as it can be biplace (instructor plus student). A paramotor is making noise. But, during the closest phase for T2, the PAN is on the ground and "walking", the engine can be turned off. When the PAN is in the air, the distance is not specified by T2 while T1 sees the PAN at its closest at 300m. Note that on March 10 (the day after the observation), a video of a paraglider flying over the village of Souilhanels (11), a few kilometers from the observation location, was "posted" on a website. Unfortunately, this video is no longer available today. This, however, does allow for the possibility of take-off and landing from the village of Airoux (11), whether by the same amateur or another. An on-site investigation conducted by GEIPAN, two years after the observation, did not allow this hypothesis to be validated. The new testimony record and reconstruction introduce significant differences compared to the statements in the police report, one day after the observation (see the document "Reconstruction and Differences with the Police Report"): - T1 and T2 describe positions that are too close together to not have distinguished or heard a paramotor. The ground phase for T2 includes a portion in front of the kitchen at a few meters, while the closest point for T1 is 100m and corresponds to a flight phase (above the pole); - the explicit notion of walking and legs has disappeared; - the duration. These new elements, taken alone, contradict the hypothesis. The witnesses themselves refute it. In terms of consistency, the differences between the two information-gathering sessions are too significant to not affect the reliability perception of the testimonies, even if the time taken by the GEIPAN to conduct the on-site investigation may have introduced variations. Contact with the daughter of the witnesses, present during the observation (walking with T1), or with the neighbor of T2 might have allowed these differences to be clarified. But, one did not follow up, and the other moved away and is no longer contactable. In conclusion, no hypothesis with a sufficient level of probability is found, but on the other hand, the level of consistency is not sufficient to support an unexplained or inexplicable character. The case is classified "C" as unexploitable due to a lack of reliable information.
