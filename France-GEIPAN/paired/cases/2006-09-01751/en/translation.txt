Summary: Observation of the passage of 2 military aircraft in maneuver
--- seg-064702724688a347d8aed971 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 26, 2006, at approximately 9:30 PM in LANDRECIES, a witness, watching television with their children, suddenly heard a loud noise that shook the house and saw a light outside. They went outside and saw two headlights approaching the house with a deep, rumbling sound. Two orange orbs hovered above the house for a few seconds before moving away. Further away, the orbs separated before disappearing in the distance. Throughout the observation, the deep rumbling noise continued, intensifying at the moment of separation. The passage of the phenomenon was accompanied by an irritating gas affecting the eyes and throat. The witness's spouse, absent during the phenomenon, returned about ten minutes later and noticed the persistent irritating odor. Within a twenty-kilometer radius, other witnesses heard and/or saw part of the phenomenon. The next day, witnesses discovered a white substance on the ground at several locations under the presumed path of the phenomenon. This substance disappeared in the sun. Analysis of samples of this substance by the Scientific Police Institute did not detect any traces of kerosene, and nothing links these deposits (probably a natural phenomenon caused by humidity) to the observed phenomenon. The investigation's conclusion shows that the witnesses observed the passage of 2 mirages in approach maneuvers over Cambrai in a configuration that required them to fly very low and slowly.
