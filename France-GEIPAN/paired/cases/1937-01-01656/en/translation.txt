Description: Recent testimony (11/09/1979) of an old observation made in 1937 in the eastern part of Martinique (the witness cannot remember the date and month; January 1st is an arbitrarily chosen date.). The witness reports seeing at 6:00 PM the west-east passage of a well-lit globe by the setting sun, moving slowly. The witness describes the phenomenon as having a diameter greater than the Moon. The phenomenon gradually disappears on the horizon. The observation lasted nearly a minute. No other testimony was collected. Case GEIPAN classification: C: Lack of precision.
