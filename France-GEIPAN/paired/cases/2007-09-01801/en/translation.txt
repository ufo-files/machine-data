Description: On September 13, 2007, between 5:08 and 5:13 AM, a witness observed outside their home the north-south passage of two bright lights (white in color with red reflections) separated by approximately two degrees. No noise was heard during the observation. The described observation largely corresponds to a satellite passage: straight trajectory, gradual disappearance (extinction). At this time and during this season, many satellites pass on this trajectory. The two objects could be the satellite and its launcher: it is noted that on September 11, 2007, the launcher COSMOS 2429 launched the satellite SL-8 R/B into a near polar orbit (the time of passage in visibility could not be verified). However, the high brightness is surprising: these objects should not be that bright (magnitude 4). The perception of immobility for 5 seconds at the beginning of the observation is not compatible with this hypothesis, but could be a perception error. Despite these uncertainties, this case is classified "B": probable observation of satellites, due to the low strangeness of the phenomenon.
