Description: On September 27, 2011, at 10:10 PM, a witness observed from his terrace a fixed bright point in the direction of the East. Ten minutes later, he was intrigued by a light that emerged at the top of the trees and rose into the sky on an oblique trajectory at a steady speed. Of low strangeness and average to low consistency, this observation case does not find a certain explanation among usual hypotheses, although some of these hypotheses are compatible. This case is classified C due to the lack of information to consolidate the considered hypotheses.
