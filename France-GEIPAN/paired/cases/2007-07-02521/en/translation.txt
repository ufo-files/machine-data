--- seg-ac20f56ed559d1ed6b364f5e [needs-review] ---
Description: While reviewing his video taken during a rural car ride on July 27, 2007 at 9 PM, the witness observes the very quick and silent passage of a dark gray, oval or round phenomenon across the landscape. The witness, who saw nothing during the filming, later sends a questionnaire, three images, and a video link to GEIPAN. The requested original video is never provided. Only one testimony about the phenomenon is collected. PAN appears at minute 1:01 in the video and disappears at the same time (see the investigation report). The witness believes it to be an object passing at extremely high speed, even making a 90-degree turn. The image that briefly appears is very similar to that produced by an insect or a speck of dust passing through the area where the camera's focus is not sharp (defocused observation), then it disappears quickly as it is no longer resolvable by the camera. GEIPAN classifies the case as A: video artifact.
