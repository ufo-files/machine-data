Description: On the night of June 17 to 18, 2025, in Fréjus (83), a witness observed from their home a PAN that was stationary and surrounded by blue lights. The object, elongated in shape and emitting a bright light, performs multidirectional movements alternating between ascents, descents, phases of high speed, and stoppages. At the end of the observation, the lights turned white and blue before the phenomenon disappeared high in the sky after several minutes. No noise was perceived. The case consistency is judged medium: although three individuals observed the PAN, only one testimony was collected, without photographic or video evidence, and with limited temporal precision. *According to the GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The strangeness reported by the witness mainly stems from the multiplicity of the PAN's movements, particularly the erratic direction changes and the phases of instantaneous speed, without any apparent acceleration. The investigation focused on the hypothesis of observing a recreational drone, supported by the following elements: - The behavior of PAN, consisting of numerous movements at high speed, phases of immobility, and takeoffs without acceleration, entirely typical of drone capabilities (see the investigation report). The lack of perceived noise can be explained by an observation distance that may be sufficiently great without the witness being able to accurately assess it, in the dark without references, with a light wind. Finally, note that the user of this drone could not be identified. He is using this device in violation of two prohibitions. (Night flight over an unauthorized flight zone). The GEIPAN classifies this observation case as 'B': probable observation of a drone.
