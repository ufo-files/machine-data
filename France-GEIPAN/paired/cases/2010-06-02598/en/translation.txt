Description: A witness observed, on June 7, 2010 at approximately 12:30 AM, a stationary, circular, glowing object in the airspace. The observation lasted about 15 seconds before the object quickly disappeared without any sound. Following additional information received from SNA-Ouest on 12/07/2010, this observation is likely the approach/landing phase of an aircraft at Nantes Airport (a commercial flight landed at 0:37 local time). The landing light was described as a "brilliant white ball" "surrounded by flashes" (flashing wing lights).
