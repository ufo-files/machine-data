Description: The witness reports observing, from their place of work, on Monday, August 26, 2024 at 11:40 AM, a black object in a stationary position and rotating regularly for approximately 5 minutes. The phenomenon reflected sunlight, creating intermittent flashes, before disappearing from the witness's view. The case consistency is average with two witnesses, but only one testimony and no photos or videos. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The two hypotheses considered are those of a helium-filled mylar balloon and a drone (see the investigation report). However, the mylar balloon hypothesis receives a greater number of arguments in its favor. Indeed, the rotation on itself, the high altitude, the reflection of sunlight, as well as the final movement in the direction of the wind that leads the phenomenon to disappear, are all elements that favor this hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": probable observation of a mylar envelope balloon carried by the wind.
