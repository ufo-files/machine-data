Summary: Observation of the slow and silent movement of clusters of yellow glowing points in the night sky: observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-26e8d86292820a239077a5f9 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: During the night of September 13 to 14, 2014, between midnight and 12:30 AM, several round, yellow glowing points were observed in the sky in the Val de Marne (94) region between the communes of Champigny-sur-Marne (94) and Créteil (94). These objects, coming from the northeast, moved linearly at a slow pace and in successive waves without any abrupt changes. Approximately forty points were estimated. Two observation testimonies were collected. This observation case is notable for several reasons: - On the one hand, since the witnesses reported the case to the Gendarmerie des transports aériens, which is particularly vigilant at this time regarding small flying objects at low altitude (drones in particular), this observation case was directly handled by this organization with great care. - The investigation successfully established the origin of the phenomenon: 35 Thai lanterns released to celebrate a PACS (civil union). - The release of these lanterns had been the subject of an authorization request to the prefecture, which was granted. Therefore, based on the gendarmerie investigation, the GEIPAN classifies this perfectly identified case in category A: observation of Thai lanterns.
