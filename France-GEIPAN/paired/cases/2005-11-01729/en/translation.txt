Summary: Sequential observations of luminous phenomena in the night sky: likely astronomical observations.
--- seg-25f76b90f22c8ed6e0d2f8f1 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 1, 2005, between 6:25 and 6:45 PM, witnesses observed in the sky a particular luminous phenomenon in the form of a very bright white ball with two red points on each side. This phenomenon moved very slowly and silently to the southwest without changing direction. The same observation was made two weeks later by the same person. The gendarmerie brigade on the scene noted the presence of a star. The witness, during his first observation, may have also made an astronomical observation. An examination of the sky map at the time of the observation shows a conjunction between Venus, Mercury, Uranus, and Antares, with Venus particularly bright (magnitude -3). It is therefore very likely that the witness observed this set of planets and stars that were beginning to shine in the setting sun. The GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable astronomical observations.
