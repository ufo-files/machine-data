Description: On the morning of November 29, 2021, a resident of Morlaas (64) was looking out the door window of their home facing their garden. She discovers circular clouds in the sky and observes the phenomenon, for approximately 5 minutes during which the clouds gently fade away and eventually disappear. This case is of very good consistency: one unique witness but several photographs of the PAN (see the investigation report). The strangeness to the witness came from the spectacular and unexplained nature of the phenomenon. The witness conducted research to understand their observation. Some phenomena were completely circular, which did not correspond to the explanations given by the press of horseshoe clouds. "I sincerely thought it was a weather phenomenon (which is why I was looking for similar clouds) but the explanation given later in the press (horseshoe clouds) does not fully satisfy me due to the very clear and complete circle, which does not correspond to a horseshoe cloud according to what I have seen/read so far. "I have not given up on understanding, which is why I filled out the questionnaire for you. Perhaps with your knowledge and resources, you will find other examples of complete circular clouds, which will allow me to validate the meteorological explanation." (Excerpts from Questions E3 E7 of the questionnaire). The hypothesis of the presence of horseshoe clouds was refuted as the atmospheric conditions (sky very overcast, atmospheric stability or very slow movement) and the fact that the shapes are horizontal and not vertical are incompatible with the formation of horseshoe clouds. Furthermore, the witness experiences a "strange sensation, which he can only describe as a vibration, a feeling of tension or electricity in the air." Such a vibration environment can be explained by the presence of several helicopters in operation (see Fig. 15 in the investigation report, showing the red, blue, and orange tracks near the witness, with very close points (positions) due to the slow speed of the helicopters). The investigation revealed that the witness observed condensation trails from aerial maneuvers of a military patrol located approximately thirty kilometers north of the witness. The uniform cloud ceiling visible in the photos is indicative of very slow cloud movement, consistent with the persistence for an extended period, here of the order of 45 minutes of condensation trails. The case is classified A: condensation trail observation.
