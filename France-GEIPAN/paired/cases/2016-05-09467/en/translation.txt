Summary: Observation of the slow south-north movement of six orange glowing orbs in the night sky; sudden extinction of 5 orbs: observation of the passage of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-31ef924b077da45580d631cb [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 21, 2016 at 23:45, a couple in a car observed, first while driving and then while parked, six glowing orange orbs slowly moving above a nearby castle. Two balls move independently, one in front and the other behind the group of the other four remaining balls, forming a parallelogram. All the orbs move according to a south-north trajectory. The first ball gradually disappears behind a cloud, and the other five balls disappear suddenly. No noise is heard. No further testimony will be collected. The witness's observation is characteristic of a release of Thai lanterns, both in terms of the colors, shapes, type of trajectory, including the extinction at the end of life of the burner. The occurrence one night during the weekend, favorable for festive events, strengthens this hypothesis. The witness describes a movement from South to North, which is consistent with the wind direction measured at multiple stations 20 km away (see the Infoclimat record.). As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: Thai lanterns.
