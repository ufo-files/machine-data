Description: Around midnight, the witness sees two bright orange objects in the sky, moving slowly and silently. She then wakes up her companion. The objects disappear and are replaced by three more that pass in front of the Moon. The observation of a succession of bright orange to yellow circular objects crossing the sky in a few minutes, on the eve of a holiday, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement is observed towards the East, consistent with the reported light wind from the South-west (see météociel). The GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
