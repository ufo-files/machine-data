Summary: Observation of a dark and mobile PAN with a peculiar trajectory in the Paris sky: observation of one or more balloons carried by the wind.
--- seg-7439fd7a52ae4dd849b5a84d [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the evening of June 22, 2025, a resident (T1) of PARIS (75) observed the sky from the terrace of his home. His attention was drawn to a dark object, of intense black, located approximately at a bearing of 265°, at an estimated altitude of around 3,000 feet. The object, moving slowly towards the East, exhibited an unusual flight dynamic: its shape seemed unstable, alternating between a solid structure and what appeared to be two distinct spherical volumes. Initially, T1 considered a tarp carried by the wind. However, the morphological evolution of the object, suggesting a continuous transformation or fragmentation into two connected entities, led him to hypothesize two large balloons linked together. He photographed and filmed the object. The observation's end was also witnessed by a second witness (T2), the spouse of the primary observer, present at the time of the event. With low strangeness and good consistency* (2 witnesses, but a single testimony, photos and video of PAN), the case turned out to be an observation of one or more balloons attached to each other (see the investigation report). Indeed, the visual aspect and T1's description of PAN support this type of observation. Furthermore, it appears that the apparent trajectory of PAN is entirely consistent with the wind direction at the time of the observation. The exact origin of PAN (released or balloon loss) could not, however, be determined during the investigation. GEIPAN classified the case as "A", observation of one or more balloons carried by the wind. CONSISTENCY: according to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.
