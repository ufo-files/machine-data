Summary: Brief observation of the passage of a luminous phenomenon with trail: probable observation of a meteor.
--- seg-92abcaf97da76bd49753cc2e [needs-review] ---
Description: On the 2nd of August 2012 at midnight eight, two individuals were intrigued by the swift and silent passage across the sky of a luminous phenomenon followed by a trail. This testimony possesses all the characteristics of an observation of a bolide (meteorite fall). The BOAM database records numerous bolide observations on that night, but not this one (note that southern France is relatively lacking in cameras). The webastro forum indicates other simultaneous witnesses of the same phenomenon, previously identified as such by an amateur astronomer. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of an atmospheric re-entry of a meteoroid.
