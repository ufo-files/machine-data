The characteristics reported by the witness leave little doubt as to the nature of the phenomenon, consistent with the witness's second intuition, namely the observation of a bolide, likely due to the fall of a meteoroid or space debris. This case is classified as B, indicating a probable observation of a bolide (fall of a meteoroid or space debris).
