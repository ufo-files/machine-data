Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archive on its public website www.geipan.fr. Among its publications are old cases classified at the time (A, B, C, or D) that are now being re-examined with the sole purpose of being more relevant in their conclusions. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over the past many years, this re-examination sometimes leads to new observations or a change in classification. The observation case previously classified as D and named TOUSSUS LE NOBLE (78) 07.09.2001 is part of a recent set of cases that have been re-examined. On September 7, 2001, at the football field in Villiers-Le-bacle (91), the representative of the prefect discovered three circles of charred grass, approximately 30 to 40 cm in diameter, forming an isosceles triangle of about 120 meters. A gendarmerie investigation revealed wood charcoal spilled into the nearby ditch and numerous vehicle tracks due to the recent occupation of the adjacent land by travelers. The GEIPAN (at the time known as SEPRA) had classified the case as unexplained. The GEIPAN no longer handles observation cases that do not include an aerospace phase, i.e., an observation perceived by the witness as having been (at least during a phase of the observation) in the sky or immediately adjacent to the sky and the ground. It is in this aerospace domain that the GEIPAN has expertise and tools to seek explanations (of Aerospace Phenomena). Ground tracks are of course exploited and valuable when they are associated or can be associated with an aerospace observation. This is absolutely not the case here; there is no testimony from PAN. Consequently, the GEIPAN should declare the testimony exploitable: classify the case as C.
