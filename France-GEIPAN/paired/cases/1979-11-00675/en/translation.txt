Description: On November 1st, 1979, around 6:15 PM, several witnesses observed a red glowing phenomenon in the sky. According to the witnesses, it consisted of one to two red balls moving across the sky without making any noise. The object had a southwest to northeast trajectory. One witness reported a trail of fire behind the object. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon for which we lack data.
