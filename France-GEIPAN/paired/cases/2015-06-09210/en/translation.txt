Description: On June 8, 2015, at 2:30 AM, a witness observed through a roof window approximately 10 red-orange glowing points moving silently in a straight line across the night sky. The points gradually disappeared. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of red-orange glowing points crossing the sky in less than a minute, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The arrival and gradual disappearance of the lights are quite characteristic of these chain-launched objects, with a lifespan of a few minutes (depending on the burner). The movement was observed from East to South, which is consistent with the North West to North wind reported in Neuilly-sur-Marne (see the Infoclimat record at Roissy, 20km away: a light North West wind is noted). The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of Thai lanterns.
