Description: On July 17, 2009, around midnight, a couple was intrigued by the rapid passage overhead of a brightly white phenomenon. One witness reported their observation, which lasted 30 seconds. The phenomenon moved at a steady speed along a straight trajectory before disappearing. No unusual noises were noticed during the observation. This observation, though slightly odd, was described precisely and matches every detail of an observation of the International Space Station (ISS) passing by. This case is classified as A.
