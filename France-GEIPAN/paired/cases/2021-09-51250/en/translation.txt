Summary: Long observation of the movement of a multitude of bright points in the sky: observation of LED balloons released for a festival.
--- seg-0092ba925b22a10185c32e3d [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of September 4 to 5, 2021, between approximately 11:50 PM and 12:40 AM, in a garden, two individuals observed in the sky the movement of a bright point followed by about a hundred others. Their trajectory was always identical: a straight line to the zenith of the witnesses, then a turn. Only one testimony was collected. According to Geipan criteria, of low strangeness and good consistency (two witnesses, but a single testimony, photos of PAN), this case turns out to be a misperception involving LED balloons released for a wedding (see the investigation report). The visual aspect of PAN on the photos strongly resembles a group of luminous balloons flying together. The explanatory hypothesis is all the more plausible because the observation took place on a Saturday night around midnight, traditional time for releasing balloons or lanterns at weddings, and the trajectory of PAN is consistent with that of the wind. Notably, the direction of PAN corresponds to that of a domain used for wedding celebrations, located only a few hundred meters from the observation site. Contacted, the domain manager confirmed that a release of LED balloons had indeed taken place at the time of the observation. GEIPAN classifies the case as "A", observation of LED balloons.
