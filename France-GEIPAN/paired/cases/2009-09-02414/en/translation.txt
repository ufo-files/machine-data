Description: On September 27, 2009, at approximately 23:05, a witness observed for 2 minutes in the sky three slightly orange glowing points forming a triangle. One of the glowing points split into two, after which the witness observed two phenomena. The entire scene disappeared silently towards the forest of Saint Germain en Laye. The testimony is too brief to warrant an investigation: the witness, contacted 18 months after the observation, does not wish to provide any additional information. This case is classified in category C: Lack of Information.
