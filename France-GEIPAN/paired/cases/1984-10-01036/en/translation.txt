Summary: Helicopter lighthouse beam
--- seg-c1c8efc93b0cd38b169592e9 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 22, 1984, at approximately 6:50 PM, two motorists observed near a railroad track a bright light. It had the shape of a white ball with a distinct white beam emanating from it. As the windows of the cars were closed, no sound was heard. The investigation will show that it was the 600-watt searchlight of a Puma helicopter in the approach phase for landing at the nearby military base.
