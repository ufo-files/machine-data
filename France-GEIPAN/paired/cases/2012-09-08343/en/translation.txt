Description: On September 16, 2012, at 20:24, a witness on their balcony had their attention drawn by the silent movement of a white glowing point. The trajectory is E-W and the phenomenon disappears behind the Jura mountains. No noise was heard during the 4-second observation. No other testimonies about this phenomenon were collected. Of average strangeness and good consistency (single witness, but with a very precise testimony), this case appears to be a possible misperception with a natural atmospheric re-entry (bolide, also called a meteor). Although the description of the PAN is quite characteristic of a bolide, no other witnesses could be found to formally explain the case. It is not the witness's visual perception that is in question, but the interpretation the witness makes of their observation through their feeling (short-duration observation in a twilight sky). This case is classified B, probable observation of a bolide.
