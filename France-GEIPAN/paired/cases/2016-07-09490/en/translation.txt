Summary: Observation of the successive appearance of red-orange and yellow glowing points and their movement in the night sky: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-91f56118c19cdfb11b543be3 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 3, 2016, around 1 a.m., a witness on a parking lot observed the successive appearance and silent movement of several bright red, yellow-orange, irregularly shaped oval points in a clear, starry sky. The witness was frightened by these points which seemed to be approaching. The points disappeared, hidden by the landscape. Only one testimony was collected. The witness likely observed Thai lanterns, which he was unable to recognize due to a lack of experience with this type of observation. The testimony is weakly consistent, with few data points noted and exploitable. In particular, there is a lack of details on the movement of the PANs, trajectory, and final orientation. However, video and photo analysis support this hypothesis (see the investigation report). This case is classified as "B" for a probable observation of a group of three Thai lanterns moving with the wind and launched during a local festival.
