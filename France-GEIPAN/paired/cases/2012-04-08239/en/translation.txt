Description: On April 30, 2012, around 9:30 PM, a witness observed in the sky the movement of an orange glowing point followed by three others. These points moved on a continuous upward trajectory before gradually disappearing into the sky. The witness took videos. Of low strangeness and good consistency, thanks to the videos, despite certain direction imprecisions, which are somewhat common, this observation case has all the characteristics of a misperception due to a group of Thai lanterns released during festivities on the eve of May 1st. The local wind direction cannot be affirmed with certainty, despite a general trend compatible before the storm around the observation location, the verification of the local wind's compliance with the movement of the objects is insufficient. It is also noted that rain does not suit the release of lanterns. The GEIPAN did not search for the authors of the release. In light of this, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B, as a probable observation of Thai lanterns.
