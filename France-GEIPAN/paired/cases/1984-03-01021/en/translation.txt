Summary: Red ball emerging in front of a motorist
--- seg-637ffff9d01bb0a574dfa5d9 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: After a night of work, a motorist sees a red ball appear above his car. A beam of light forces him to veer off the main road. The witness gets out of his vehicle and walks towards the red ball until it disappears. Terrified, he approaches a farmer in the field bordering the road. The farmer had not seen the phenomenon but, during the investigation, he stated that at the location of the observation, one can see the rising sun in the form of a red ball. The investigation concludes that several factors, such as fatigue and blinding by the rising sun, are responsible for the optical illusion, the fear, and the driver's change in direction.
