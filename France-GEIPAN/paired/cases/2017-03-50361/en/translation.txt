Description: On Monday, March 27, 2017, a witness was discussing with a friend on the beach of Le Havre (76). It is between 21:30 and 23:00 when he catches a glimpse, for a few seconds, of five dimly glowing points flying in a V formation heading inland, towards the East, and the city lights quickly obscure them. The witness addresses the other person present, but the latter does not have time to see the phenomenon, especially since their field of vision is limited by the beach huts. Only one testimony will be collected. The possibility of a group of isolated birds, five, flying in V formation inland towards the East is plausible according to the development indicated in the detailed report of the analysis of this case (see the investigation report). The testimony is weakly consistent, with little data. The case analysis does not highlight any strong strangeness, other than the witness's initial perception that led to the submission of the completed questionnaire to GEIPAN. This case is classified as A, as a highly probable observation of birds that cannot be formally identified as volatiles but whose ventral plumage reflects urban lights. The small size of the volatiles, as well as the distance and the fleeting nature of the observation, did not allow the witness to distinguish these volatiles better. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: extremely probable birds.
