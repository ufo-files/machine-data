Summary: Quick observations of the movement of a ball with a trail in the sky: probable observations of an atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-70ff243bc16ec431088c4e56 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 26, 1983, between 6:55 and 7:08 a.m., several witnesses in Bouches-du-Rhône observed a white glowing ball moving horizontally. This ball was followed by a colored trail. Observations lasting between 4 and 45 seconds allowed witnesses to note the complete absence of noise and a very high speed. The phenomenon then disappeared from view. No further information could be gathered on this phenomenon, which appears to resemble an atmospheric reentry.
