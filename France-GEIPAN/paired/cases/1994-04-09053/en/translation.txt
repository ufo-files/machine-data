Description: Recent (12/06/2014) testimony of an old observation without a precise date. In April 1994, around 10 PM, a witness and his friend initially observed a white, lens-shaped glow in the sky. One of the witnesses believed it to be an object, present for over an hour and a half, and estimated its height at 300 meters. The object seemed to move back and forth, then disappeared suddenly as if sucked into the sky. This long (1.5 hours) observation of a white glow low on the horizon simply suggests the Moon, possibly partially obscured by clouds, and then disappearing behind a cloud. This type of misidentification is common. The lack of a precise date does not allow for either confirming or denying this hypothesis. This case is classified "C" due to the lack of precise information about the event's date.
