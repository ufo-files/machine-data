Summary: Observation of silent movement across the clear, night sky of 27 orange-colored lights: likely Thai lanterns.
--- seg-f50ac62fe995853ce6544726 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 22, 2015, at 3:03 AM, a witness noticed through a window a movement of an orange glow in the clear sky. The witness eventually counted 27 identical lights coming from the NNE and disappearing towards the East "in a blinding manner." No noise was heard during the 34-minute observation. No photographs were taken. No other testimony was collected. One hypothesis was retained (see investigation notes): observation of the movement of Thai lanterns. The consistent aspects (color, brightness, duration), the number, the day and time, and the identical trajectory for all objects are elements favoring this hypothesis. However, the authors of the release were not identified. The phase of disappearance of the phenomena "at blinding speed towards the East" corresponds to the extinction phase. As often for this type of case, the witness would have interpreted the rapid dimming of the lantern as a very rapid distance. Based on the elements analyzed during the investigation, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns released after a private party.
