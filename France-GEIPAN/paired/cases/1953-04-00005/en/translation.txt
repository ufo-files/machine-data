--- seg-11d169e2c825095bd80425ee [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 11, 1953 at 21:10, three witnesses at an air force warehouse observed the silent east-to-west movement of a large red-orange point. The captain described a phenomenon that made an out-and-back trajectory across the celestial vault at 45 degrees in 6 minutes according to a broken irregular line forming 15-degree angles along the direction of movement. The 180-degree turn was also accomplished through successive changes in course. Changes in course occurred every second. The other two witnesses tracked the phenomenon for two to three minutes. Eventually, the phenomenon was lost from view. Only one testimony was collected. This phenomenon does not evoke any known misidentifications. The GEIPAN classifies this case as D: Strange to Very Strange/Medium to High Consistency.
