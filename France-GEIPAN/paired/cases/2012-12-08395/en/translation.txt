Summary: Long car observation of 8-10 white light phenomena photographed: observation of sun reflections through the windshield.
--- seg-305fb38af19590060933ec71 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 29, 2012, at 1:05 PM, two people in a car were intrigued by the presence of 8 to 10 white light phenomena seen through the windshield in a clear sky. No noise was heard during the entire observation. Only one testimony was collected, and the witness provided GEIPAN with a set of photographs taken with their smartphone. The photos show various more or less luminous shapes: the witness did not specify, among all these artifacts, which ones he saw with the naked eye. Indeed, the commented photo shows various reflections on the photo lens or on the windshield; with the naked eye, the witness must have noticed the reflections on the windshield, but he probably discovered later even more surprising effects in the photos. Given the observation conditions, in a car facing south, at the zenith hour, close to the winter solstice, the conditions are ideal for observing multiple sun reflections on the car windows or phone optics. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": observation of sun reflections through the windshield.
