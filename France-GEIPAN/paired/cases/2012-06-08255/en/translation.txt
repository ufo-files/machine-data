--- seg-e5d3819c08f253a9156ab096 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Sunday, June 24, 2012, at 12:20 a.m., a witness and her husband observed in the clear sky about a dozen yellow-white glowing points. The witness noticed that these points, which appeared static, then moved, gained altitude, and sped off towards the horizon. No noise was heard during the 5-minute observation. Only one testimony was collected, but a photograph was taken. The observation of orange orbs in the sky on a weekend night strongly resembles Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The description and photo match many lantern observations. The Gendarmerie reaches the same conclusion. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable lantern.
