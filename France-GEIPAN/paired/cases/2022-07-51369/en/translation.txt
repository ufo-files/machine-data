Description: On July 19, 2022, a resident of COLPO (56) was about to take his dog out. He is on his porch when he spots an oval-shaped light pointing towards the Moon. This light remains stationary for at least 5 minutes. The witness then stopped observing the phenomenon. Only one testimony was collected. Analysis of the photograph revealed that the time indicated by the witness was incorrect, the observation having taken place at 3:07 AM, not 11:00 PM, which the witness confirmed a posteriori (see the investigation report). The consistency of this case is average: one photo, a concise testimony (according to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony). The investigation concluded that the witness had observed the planet Jupiter, whose characteristics and location exactly match the reported observation. It should be noted that the PAN in the photograph does not present a point-like appearance, but rather a slightly elongated shape, compared by the witness to that of a "banana." This characteristic is consistent with a slight motion blur, given that the photograph was taken hand-held. The case is classified as A, observation of the planet Jupiter.
