--- seg-0029920a86470c5ec4c70cff [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of August 13 to 14, 2021, around midnight, the witness left their residence to enjoy the air on their terrace. While observing the sky, they noticed a group of several dim white lights moving in a V-formation. These lights followed a straight trajectory from northwest to southeast at a relatively fast speed. The formation appeared to be very close to the witness and disappeared behind nearby trees. The observation lasted approximately 8 to 10 seconds. Only one testimony was collected (see the investigation report). The consistency of this case is average: one witness, no photos or videos (Consistency refers to the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony). The investigation concludes that it is very likely that a group of birds was observed moving in a V-formation (probably wild ducks of the Colvert type), either during their migration between their feeding grounds and their roosting sites, or as part of a partial migration. The elements supporting this conclusion are primarily: - the description of the PAN as a V-formation and the dimness of the small lights - the presence of urban lighting - the absence of noise - the witness's proximity - the speed of movement. The GEIPAN is classified as an "A" rating for the observation of birds moving in flight formation in a V.
