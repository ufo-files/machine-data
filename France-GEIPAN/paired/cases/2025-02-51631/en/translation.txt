Description: On February 1, 2025, around 4:05 PM (confirmed after investigation), the witness is in their garden in TOURS (37) when they spot, upon lifting their head, a spherical object appearing to rotate on itself. The object moves in a straight line, without noise, trail, or other associated visual or auditory effects. The phenomenon is described as a sphere or ball alternately changing between light and dark tones in a regular pattern while spinning. It follows a straight trajectory before disappearing behind a large tree. The oddity of the observation lies in the perception of a sphere appearing to alternate between white and black in a regular pattern while rotating. The case consistency is considered quite good, with only one witness providing a video that could be analyzed to extract the PAN's movement characteristics. According to the GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The contextual elements gathered during the investigation (see the investigation report), along with the video analysis, point towards the hypothesis of observing a balloon. Indeed, the regular motion, alternating light reflection, absence of noise, and consistent trajectory with the wind direction and speed suggest a Mylar-type balloon, periodically reflecting sunlight and carried by the winds. February 1st was a Saturday, a day conducive to festive activities or demonstrations. However, the origin of the balloon (released or lost) was not identified. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": probable observation of a Mylar balloon carried by the wind.
