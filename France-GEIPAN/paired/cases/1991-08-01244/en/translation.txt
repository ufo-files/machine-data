Description: On August 20, 1991, around 5:30 AM, two gendarmes on patrol observed for a brief moment a very bright, blue sphere moving at an extremely high speed and leaving a significant trail of sparks. No other witnesses could be found, but the described phenomenon resembles a natural or artificial re-entry into the atmosphere.
