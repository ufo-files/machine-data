Summary: Observation of the rapid passage of a very bright white light: probable observation of a meteoroid reentry.
--- seg-61347426f0567a1dd401ed12 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 15, 2009, around 7:30 PM, a witness observed the rapid passage of a white and bright phenomenon at low altitude. The phenomenon disappeared, hidden by the landscape. No particular noise was heard during the observation, which lasted less than five seconds. We interpret the witness's description of a tadpole-shaped light as a bright object followed by a trail. The very short observation duration, combined with a linear trajectory, suggests a very probable observation of a bolide atmospheric reentry. This case of low strangeness and low consistency is classified "B": probable misperception with a meteoroid reentry.
