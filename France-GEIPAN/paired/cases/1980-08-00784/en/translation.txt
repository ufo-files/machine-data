Description: On August 7 at 9:40 PM and then at 10:45 PM, several witnesses observed a yellow-orange light phenomenon pass over a radio station at the F.N.S. base in Taverny. Each time, the object moved at extremely high speed and gained altitude. No noise was noticed. No traces on the vegetation were found at the scene. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
