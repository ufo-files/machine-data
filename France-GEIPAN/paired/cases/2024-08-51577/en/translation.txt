Description: On Tuesday, August 20, 2024, while on vacation in Biscarrosse (40), the witness was with his family on Maguide beach, by the Cazaux lake. Laid down and watching the sky towards Sanguinet (40), on the opposite shore, he spotted around 1:30 PM a flying object on the horizon. He describes it as a spherical, metallic-looking object moving from right to left at a constant, relatively high speed. The object remained visible for approximately 20 seconds before disappearing into the sky above Sanguinet. The consistency* of this case is average, with only one witness who was unable to film the PAN. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. An aeronautical verification with CAPCODA* did not indicate any aircraft flight matching the PAN's trajectory described by the witness towards Sanguinet.*

*CAPCODA: Air Center for Planning and Conducting Operations and Air Defense (Air and Space Force).

The explored hypothesis, that the object was carried by the wind, like a Mylar balloon, is supported by the following elements:

- Spherical shape and metallic color, typical of such a balloon.
- Observation in summer, not far from beaches from which a balloon could have been blown away.
- Straight horizontal movement in the direction of the dominant wind.

The balloon's trajectory appears quite oblique relative to the witness's observation axis, which explains that he quickly lost sight of it to his left.

The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of a Mylar balloon carried by the wind.
