Description: Recent (2008) testimony of an old observation. On Saturday, November 2, 1974, around 8:30 PM, a motorist and his passenger were surprised by the slow and silent movement of an object in the shape of a tube, black with three very dark red areas: two were at the ends and one in the middle. The witness got out of his vehicle and saw the craft rise vertically before moving very slowly over the road and then over the trees before disappearing. The observation lasted several minutes before the frightened witnesses resumed their journey. Only one testimony was collected on this phenomenon. This 1974 observation report was not filed with GEIPAN until 2008 and therefore treated with little priority. This nocturnal observation of a "velvet black" tube limited by three glowing disks should first be analyzed using the tools of perception psychology (so-called contour or shape illusion): it is very common for an observer of a set of moving lights to mentally reconstruct a volume limited by these glowing points. In this case, while the presence of the three glowing disks (called windows by the witness) is undeniable, the existence of the tube should not be considered certain. Consequently, the phenomenon becomes a set of three glowing disks moving slowly together, which could correspond to wind-carried luminous objects, such as Thai lanterns, which are very common today (2016) but not widely used in France at that time. It is noted that the observation took place on a Saturday evening at 8:30 PM, a time conducive to festive events. It is also the Day of the Dead; some people symbolically honor their deceased by releasing Thai lanterns. Although this hypothesis is plausible, GEIPAN cannot retain it, on the one hand, due to the absence of a weather report correlating the movement of lights with local wind, and on the other hand, because Thai lanterns were only used at that time by Asians or tourists returning from Thailand. Without being able to perform these checks or find other corroborations, GEIPAN classifies this case as category C due to lack of information.
