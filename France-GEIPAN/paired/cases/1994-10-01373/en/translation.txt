Summary: Discovery of stone circles on the ground
--- seg-57afae38e8e4edf6ea744cdb [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 18, 1994, around 2:00 PM, a gendarme on leave, while collecting mushrooms, discovered on the ground a circle composed of rock fragments resembling slag. The leaves in contact with the rocks had taken on a sulfurous green hue. As night fell, he decided to return the next day but was unable to locate the site again.
