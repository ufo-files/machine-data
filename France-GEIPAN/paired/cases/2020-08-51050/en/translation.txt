Summary: Observation on a Saturday evening of the close and silent passage of a glowing object undulating on a straight trajectory: misidentification with a cluster of festive LED balloons.
--- seg-824ecba63b5dcfbd176d1261 [needs-review] ---
Description: Several witnesses on August 8, 2020, around 10:00 PM, were surprised to see the silent passage of an object "in the shape of a jellyfish" with blue glowing halos that only illuminated the object itself. The PAN at low altitude above a cherry tree, moved on a linear trajectory, undulating. The observation lasted approximately 30 seconds, then the PAN was lost from view, gradually becoming a bright point on the southern horizon. The witness's observation and the reconstruction drawing suggest a flyover of LED balloons. The witness perfectly described a north/south trajectory and a slow movement at an altitude of around 100 meters. The reconstruction drawing shows several round shapes. The investigation confirmed the following points, which are entirely compatible with a festive balloon release:

- On August 10, 2020, the witness submitted a complete questionnaire about the observation, including a photo montage reconstruction from August 8, 2020, and GEIPAN asked other witnesses to also fill out questionnaires.
- No aircraft was overhead (confirmed on flightradar24 at that time; see annex).
- The orientation of the witness's house (confirmed on Google Maps but not included in the file) with its terrace facing south confirms that they could not have seen the PAN coming from the north until after it flew over their house, and they were able to follow it with their eyes to the south until it disappeared.
- The Info Climat website confirms that at 10:00 PM, there was a light north/south wind compatible with objects carried by the wind (see annex).
- LED balloons have shapes consistent with the description (see photo of a typical LED balloon release in the annex).
- The village hall is located a few streets north of the observation position.
- August 8, 2020, was a Saturday night conducive to celebrations.
- A call to the village mayor confirmed that a wedding had taken place that evening.
- Additionally, the mayor confirmed that the newlyweds had released festive white balloons tied in bouquets, each with an LED light inside.
- A photo of the balloon release preparations and the type of LED was sent to GEIPAN:
- The balloon release took place early in the night, which is consistent with the observation time of 10:00 PM in August.
- On August 13, 2020, GEIPAN informed the witness that it was not necessary to transmit the other questionnaires as there was no longer any doubt about the observed phenomenon. GEIPAN classified the case as A: Certain misperception with a cluster of LED festive balloons.
