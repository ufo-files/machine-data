Description: A witness reports their observation made on Sunday, August 29, 2010, around midnight twenty. They observe, from the first floor of their residence, approximately thirty "shuttles" flying beneath the cloud layer. All are moving slowly from the horizon and will reach the zenith before disappearing, hidden by the urban landscape. A video is taken with a mobile phone. Unfortunately, the video disappoints the witness, not accurately depicting their observation. No particular noise was heard during the observation, except for a faint rumble in the distance at times. Only one testimony is collected by the gendarmerie (consolidating the 703 Procedural Report with the questionnaire and the 1073 Procedural Report with a complementary file from the witness) and by the GEIPAN. The video sent by the witness is very similar to numerous similar videos received at the GEIPAN and illustrating cases of observation of Thai lanterns. The characteristics of the observation correspond as well: duration, apparent speed, direction of movement consistent with local wind. The drawing made by the witness is quite surprising regarding this hypothesis; it's a very free interpretation of a spectacle consisting only of a set of glowing points. A private investigator directly called by the witness conducted an on-site investigation and concludes: "This is without any possible contestation a release of Thai lanterns performed during a wedding celebration that was organized in the Tenteling town hall. The newlyweds told me they had released 60 lanterns. Everything confirms the reality of this release." The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of Thai lanterns released on occasion of a wedding.
