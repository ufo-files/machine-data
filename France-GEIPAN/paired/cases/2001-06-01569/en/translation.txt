Description: On June 24, 2001, around 7 PM, a witness observed a vertical cylindrical object moving slowly. The witness filmed the phenomenon by following it on a motorcycle to get a better view of its movements. The estimated altitude of the object was 600 meters, and it appeared to be 15 meters tall with a diameter of 2.5 meters. It was colored aluminum with bright points like headlights. The movement was slow, estimated at approximately 10 km/h. The investigation was unable to identify this observation.
