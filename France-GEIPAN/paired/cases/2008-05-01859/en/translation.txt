Description: On May 10th around 11 PM, a witness observed from their garden a red object in a clear, starry sky. The object moved quickly from the South to the West. The witness returned home to get their camera. By the time they returned, the object was gone, but the witness still managed to take a photograph that they could see the next day, showing a white object near a star. The information provided by the sole witness seems contradictory and lacking in detail (no statement to the gendarmerie and no observation questionnaire completed), making the investigation difficult and conclusions impossible to draw.
