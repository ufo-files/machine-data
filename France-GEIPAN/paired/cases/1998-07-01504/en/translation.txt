Summary: Observation of distress rocket fires
--- seg-8136ad8236694078709de517 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the 19th of July, 1998, around 11 PM, several witnesses successively observed three red lights ascending into the sky before gently descending. One of these phenomena reportedly caused a fire. The investigation would quickly reveal that they were the result of launches of expired emergency rockets by a private individual.
