--- seg-5293eb5fe50aa816175d0b4a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 8, 2012, a witness lying on a terrace observing the starry sky noticed a movement of an object they did not recognize for 5 seconds. This object had the shape of a "weaver's shuttle" in brown-green color, with diffused edges. A dark round shape was seen at the center of the object. The whole scene appeared very blurry but was still identifiable against the background of the sky it occluded. No sound was heard during the object's straight-line movement from north-east to south-west. The object moved with its main axis oriented perpendicularly to the direction of its motion. Only one testimony was collected. Based on the objective elements gathered during the investigation (see the investigation report), namely: - Shape and appearance of the PAN. - Speed and trajectory not consistent with a passive object pushed by the wind present at the time and place of observation. - Speed and/or altitude and/or size estimates not consistent with those of a motorized manufactured craft (no sound perceived), we can conclude that the PAN observed by the witness does not resemble any known object or phenomenon. This case should be classified as "D1" - observation of an unidentified phenomenon. This testimony is of good consistency: precise and detailed, but observed from a distance by a single witness without a photograph. The observation is strange because it involves a PAN with an uncommon appearance that is difficult to compare to anything known.
