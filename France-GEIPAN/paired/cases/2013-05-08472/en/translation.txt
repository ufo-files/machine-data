Description: On Saturday, May 11, 2013 at 11:45 PM, a motorist and his son observed in the sky approximately forty red balls silently moving through the air. The witness's account resembles that of Thai lanterns, at a typical date and time for these objects. The estimated distance and size of the objects, as is often the case, are highly inaccurate. Following the publication of the event in the newspaper, a person contacted the gendarmerie, claiming to have released Thai lanterns into the sky of Domancy that evening, during a party for her wedding. The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": perfectly identified observation, Thai lanterns.
