Summary: Observations of light formations in the night sky: confirmed observations of 8 Alpha Jet aircraft from the French Air Force Patrol (PAF).
--- seg-a2c578c7407f3b0fc6abbb5b [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 1, 2015, starting at 10:30 PM, several people in the department of Alpes-de-Haute-Provence observed the movement of luminous phenomena in the sky. Some witnesses describe geometric shapes or some kind of craft formed by bright points of light. Local press also reports the observations (see last witness). The observation of lights in the sky, arranged in regular geometric patterns, suggests an aerial patrol in training or demonstration. A check with the Patrouille de France, based in Salon de Provence, indicates that the patrol exceptionally flew at night in this region on July 1st and 3rd: on July 1st to repeat the demonstration that took place on July 3rd for the baptism of the outgoing promotions. The silence accompanying the observation is quite normal, as the noise of an airplane seen from the front, at more than a few kilometers, is very low. The 8 Alpha Jets of the French Patrol are equipped with powerful headlights visible more than 10 km along the flight axis. When the planes turned, the lighthouse beam changed direction, and the observers located along the initial flight path saw it disappear suddenly: these planes, seen from the side, are only visible by their red or green position lights, which are not powerful enough to be seen from a great distance. Seen from the front, the approach is not perceptible; the planes appear stationary, only lateral movement is perceived, and it may be slight. The GEIPAN received reports of several observations from the same patrol: from Valensole (04) and Sainte-Tulle (04) on July 1st, and from Villeneuve (04), 10km away, on July 3rd. During the early days of July, the GEIPAN receives annual reports of similar observations in Provence; in particular, one was reported in Villeneuve (04) on July 5, 2013. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": certain observations of the 8 Alpha Jets from the French Patrol. The witness video from Sainte-Tulle will be uploaded to the site later due to a temporary technical issue. To see a video of the French Air Force Patrol (Patrouille de France) flying at night over Paris.
