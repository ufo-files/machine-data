Description: On Sunday, June 26, 2011, around 11:10 PM, a witness is intrigued by the observation of the movement of a bright light source of high intensity. As it passes over the witness's residence, the witness distinguishes three white/orange glowing points arranged in a triangle. No noise is heard during the slow passage and observation that lasted four minutes. Another witness present observed the phenomenon but did not report it. Due to the lack of validated hypotheses (see the investigation report), this case is classified as "C" due to insufficient information.
