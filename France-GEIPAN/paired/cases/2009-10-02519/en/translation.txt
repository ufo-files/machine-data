Description: On October 3, 2009, around 9:30 PM, a family (witnesses 1 and 2) observed in the sky the passage, in groups, of bright and flying objects (UFO_PROTECTED_000__). These round objects moved horizontally and quickly. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted between 6 and 7 minutes. The glow of these objects faded before completely disappearing. The gendarmerie investigation determined that they were Chinese lanterns released for a birthday party. The person responsible for the release (witness 3) provided some photographs taken on that occasion. The description of the observation by witnesses 1 and 2 around 9:30 PM corresponds to the probable observation of Thai lanterns. The release of flying lanterns reported to the gendarmerie (around 11:00 PM) by witness 3 does not match the observation time. However, the data in the photos sent by witness 3 confirm this release time of around 11:00 PM.
