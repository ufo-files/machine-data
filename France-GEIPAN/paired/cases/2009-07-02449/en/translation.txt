Description: On the 19th of July, 2009, around 10:40 PM, several individuals witnessed a silent, bright red-orange object in the sky. Despite some perceptible oscillations, the object remained stationary. Their observation lasted approximately 15 minutes: the object was then obscured by clouds. Only one report was made. A luminous captive balloon, or illuminated by projectors from the ground (advertising or festive balloon), could have produced the same impression. An altitude weather balloon, still illuminated by the sun an hour after its setting, could also give this appearance. It is quite difficult to verify either of these hypotheses three years after the observation. This observation case owes its low strangeness only to the static position of the observed phenomenon, and the unique testimony, despite a precise description, does not allow for its consistency to be noted. Although the balloon hypotheses are plausible, this case is classified as C due to the lack of independent testimonies and overlaps to validate an hypothesis.
