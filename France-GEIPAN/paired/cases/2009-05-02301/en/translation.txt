Summary: Observation of the slow, silent movement of orange glowing balls: Lack of information.
--- seg-c30cd5bb15a1f80a857f1e7e [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 17, 2009, around 1 a.m., two individuals observed approximately a dozen orange-colored lights in the sky. They appeared to emanate from a single point and silently moved in the direction of Grange-sur-Lot, disappearing over the horizon. The witness provided several photographs of the reconstruction and positioning. Unfortunately, the video recording was not sent to GEIPAN. No other testimonies were collected. Although the strangeness was relatively low, the detailed testimony gave the observation case all the characteristics of a misidentification with a Thai lantern release. The lack of precise meteorological data at the observation location and in the region prevent a definitive conclusion. This case is classified as C: lack of precise meteorological information and cross-references to validate the Thai lantern hypothesis.
