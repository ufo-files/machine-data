Description: On July 20, 2015, at approximately 10:57 PM, a family observed the straight and silent passage of a bright red object in the night sky before disappearing behind a building. Only one testimony is collected. A video is made with such a phone but cannot be sent. The witness's description of the phenomenon's movement creates confusion regarding the end of the trajectory (but the witness does not seem familiar with the area). This one is reportedly from the northeast but ends in the southwest rather than the southeast. Indeed, the mention "heading towards the centre of Nérac" indicates an end of trajectory located in the southwest of the observation location, not in the southeast. Similarly, the movement indications "across the entire visible horizon from the left to the right balcony" and "disappearance of the object behind the right-side annex building" confirm the end of a southwest observation. The weather situation in Agen-La-Garenne at 23:00, located 20 km northeast, shows a light wind (6 km/h) from the north and visibility of 45 km (see the Météociel website). The trajectory towards the southwest is compatible with a north wind direction measured at 20 kilometers away. These elements support the observation of a Thai lantern: high brightness, red color, moving in the direction of the wind, straight and uniform trajectory, no noise. This hypothesis is reinforced by the summer period and the location in a vacation village. Lanterns are frequently released during this period as well as on New Year's Eve. The launch of a single lantern is frequently observed. Consequently, GEPAN is classified as B: observation of the passage of a Thai lantern.
