--- seg-0b7a5f0a6bfe88bf2cb63ee3 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 3, 2013, around 11:30 PM, a witness and his wife observed fixed lights forming a triangle and thought it was an airplane, but the lights approached and the witness distinguished between them a dark, flat shape. Only one statement was collected. This case was deemed strange enough by the GEIPAN to warrant an investigator for a field inquiry. This observation case proves to be very consistent, with two non-independent witnesses and a low-quality video, leaving no doubt about the reality of the phenomenon, and with a good estimate of its apparent trajectory. The strangeness of the case turns out to be low as it is consistent with the behavior of various well-known flying objects. Despite a few unexplained points (see the investigation report), the observed aspect is consistent with that of a group of helicopters.
