Description: On March 27, 1976, between 6:30 and 6:55 a.m., a military personnel experiencing a sudden vehicle malfunction. All light indicators were off. His attention was then drawn to a grayish fog patch with a metallic reflection. It was located near the river, 15 to 20 meters from the vehicle. Peering out the window, he observed the phenomenon as it rose and disappeared at high speed above the buildings in a south-southwest direction. Extremely surprised by what he had just seen, he checked his dashboard and easily restarted the engine to continue his journey. No other witness statements were collected regarding this phenomenon, for which we lack information.
