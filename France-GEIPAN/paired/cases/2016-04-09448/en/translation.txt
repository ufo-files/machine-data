--- seg-024f6da6f18a05dbd42ef3bb [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 19, 2016 at 3:56 AM, a witness observed through a window a light moving from southeast to northwest in a clear sky. As it passed directly overhead, the witness described a white-orange sphere with an orange halo around its edges. Dimly lit, it moved silently without leaving a trail. The observation lasted 5 seconds before the witness lost sight of it. No other witnesses were found regarding this phenomenon. The witness's description was very precise, especially with the drawing (not provided to GEIPAN but posted on a website) that recreates the trajectory on the observation window. The description matches all aspects of a Thai lantern flight (color, shape, trajectory according to the local east wind, as detailed in the investigation report). Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of a Thai lantern.
