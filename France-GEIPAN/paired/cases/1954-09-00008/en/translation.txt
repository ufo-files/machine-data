Summary: Observations of a circular object on the ground with an oscillation motion. Silent takeoff in a slant then vertical with smoke; no trace on the ground: Strange to very strange phenomenon of medium to strong consistency.
--- seg-f6bafdc2cfd59351ad450d94 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 7, 1954, around 7:30 a.m., two witnesses are biking on communal road 47 between Harponville (80) and Contay (80) on their way to work. The weather is clear and without fog. They observe on the ground in a field 200m from the road, a circular shape affected by an oscillatory motion. Both witnesses describe a dark-colored cylinder with a slightly rounded upper surface and an opening around its side. Intrigued, the witnesses approached within one hundred meters and observed the object rise silently at an oblique angle for approximately fifteen meters before going vertical. During the ascent, witnesses noticed gray-blue smoke escaping from a pipe. The observation lasted between 4 and 5 minutes before the phenomenon disappeared into the sky. The frightened witnesses immediately leave for the gendarmerie. On the same day, the investigation found no trace on the ground in the field. One of the witnesses having already seen helicopters, he claims it is not such a craft. No other testimony is collected. The credibility of the witnesses is not in question. The GEIPAN classifies this case as D: Strange phenomenon / Very strange, with a consistency ranging from medium to strong.
