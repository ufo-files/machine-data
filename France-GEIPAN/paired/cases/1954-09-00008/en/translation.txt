Summary: Observations of a circular object on the ground with an oscillation motion. Silent takeoff in an oblique then vertical direction with smoke; no trace on the ground: Strange to very strange phenomenon of medium to strong consistency.
--- seg-f6bafdc2cfd59351ad450d94 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 7, 1954, around 7:30 a.m., two witnesses are biking on communal road 47 between Harponville (80) and Contay (80). The weather is clear, without fog. They observe on the ground, in a field 200 meters from the road, a circular shape with an oscillatory motion. The two witnesses describe a dark cylinder with a slightly rounded top surface and an opening around it. Intrigued, the witnesses approach within 100 meters and see the object rise silently at an oblique angle for about 15 meters, then vertically. During the ascent, the witnesses notice blue-gray smoke escaping from a pipe. The observation lasted between 4 and 5 minutes before the phenomenon disappeared into the sky. Frightened, the witnesses immediately went to the gendarmerie. On the same day, the investigation found no traces on the ground in the field. One of the witnesses, having already seen helicopters, asserts it was not one of them. No other testimonies were collected. The credibility of the witnesses is not in question. The GEIPAN classifies this case as D: Strange phenomenon / Very strange, with average to strong consistency.
