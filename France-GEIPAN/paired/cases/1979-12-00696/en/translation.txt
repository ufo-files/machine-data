--- seg-4c76e5eb24d84e1afa256111 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 8, 1979, at 9:52 AM, two witnesses from their home spotted a blue-marble and white object in the shape of a crescent with no shine. It was approximately 4 to 5 meters tall. No particular noise or light was noticed. The object, at ground level, swayed near a small wood. The observation lasted one minute and thirty seconds at a distance of 200 meters from the object. It disappeared as it quickly flew north. Neither the witnesses nor the gendarmes found any traces on the ground.
