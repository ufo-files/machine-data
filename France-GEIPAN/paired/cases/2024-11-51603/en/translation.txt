Description: On November 29, 2024, between 8:30 PM and 9:00 PM, a witness and their friend observed from their home two aligned, rectangular, glowing shapes separated by a gap. When the witness moved for a clear visual, the phenomenon suddenly went dark. The observation, conducted under a completely clear sky and absolute silence, lasted about ten seconds at an estimated relatively low altitude. A few days later, on December 3, 2024 at 7:38 PM, the witness alone from the same location witnessed a similar event and managed to photograph it with their smartphone. Of average strangeness and consistency, this case presents a marked residual strangeness, reinforced by the witness's connection between the two appearances. However, there are still questions. Some stem from the photographic expertise of the confirmed "original authentic" image of the December 3, 2024 observation by our image authentication system, and others from the statements reported on the QT for the November 29, 2024 observation, which were validated by the remote and on-site investigation conducted by the GEIPAN. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C", an unidentified phenomenon due to a lack of objective information. *CONSISTENCY: According to GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.
