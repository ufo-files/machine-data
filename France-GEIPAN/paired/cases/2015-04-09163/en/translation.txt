Description: On April 21, 2015, at 9:30 PM, a witness observed in the sky and for 5 minutes a "flashing" light of all colors. The object disappeared after a few minutes, allowing the witness to film the phenomenon. After verification with the witness, he was looking above Mont-Saxonnex (74), that is, southwest. The witness observed the phenomenon again two days after his first observation. The observation corresponds perfectly to the observation of the star Sirius at its setting, with a strong scintillation phenomenon (interaction of light with atmospheric particles), frequent with this star, the brightest in the sky. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the star Sirius.
