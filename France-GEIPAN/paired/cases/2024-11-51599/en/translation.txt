Summary: Observation of a cloudy sky of the evolutions of several "green circles": probable observation of light shows produced by a projector called sky-tracer.
--- seg-6b1cf452f73948baa5d27a36 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 25, 2024, at 04:40, from his home in Mitry-Mory (Seine-et-Marne, 77), the witness observed green, circular light phenomena in a cloudy sky. These lights perform back-and-forth movements before gradually dispersing and then disappearing. The total duration of the phenomenon is estimated at approximately 30 seconds, of which 11 seconds were filmed. The video, transmitted to GEIPAN with the witness questionnaire (QT), was able to be analyzed (annex 1). The case consistency is good (one witness but exploitable video). The perceived strangeness by the witness is rather dictated by curiosity to understand their observation. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of information considered reliable and objective, gathered for a testimony. The hypothesis retained is that of the observation of a sky-tracer (light animation projector) pointed towards the sky. It relies on: - The round shape, typical of an impact spot on a cloud surface from a ground-based projector. - The light spot trajectory in all directions but typical of the automated movement of a sky-tracer. - The confirmed presence of a cloud ceiling (8/8 octa nebulosity recorded on MétéoCiel data - annex 2). - The green color of the spots, which is diffuse in the video. However, as no public data has allowed us to locate a sky-tracer along the line of observation, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a sky-tracer.
