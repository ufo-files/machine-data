--- seg-56ce5a60ea0bfd04bb7274d6 [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 12, 2008, at 2:48 a.m., a witness is intrigued by the presence of a peculiar orange-red light in the sky. The observation lasts for two to three minutes before the phenomenon disappears over the horizon. Due to its strangeness and weak consistency (single witness, description of the PAN resembling the Moon), this case turns out to be a certain misidentification with the setting Moon, which was directly in the observation direction of the PAN. This case is classified A, misidentification with the setting Moon.
