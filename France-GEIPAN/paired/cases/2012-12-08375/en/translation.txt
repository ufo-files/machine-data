--- seg-d3b4b420db2297a9cbc75ec2 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Around 7 AM on December 12, 2012, a witness was intrigued by a deep noise then observed a luminous phenomenon followed by the quick passage of a ball of blue or yellow color. No trail was observed. The observation report clearly describes an aspect of a large meteor: a powerful light phenomenon lasting a few seconds, accompanied by a noise. The date of the event is very close to the peak of the Geminid meteor shower. The BOAM (http://www.boam.fr)) reports the recording of numerous events of this type at that time (cameras are installed in northern France and probably did not capture that meteor, located south of Toulouse). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": observation of a bolide, likely from the Geminid swarm.
