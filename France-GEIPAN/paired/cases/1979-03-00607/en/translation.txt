Summary: Observations of a powerful light phenomenon and power outage: observations and consequences on animals of an electric arc on the EDF line.
--- seg-a7a48ae0c05878cd5f3f5c18 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of March 11, 1979, around 0:45 a.m., several individuals will witness a peculiar luminous phenomenon. They will be dazzled by a very bright, blue-green-purple light that illuminates the surrounding landscape for approximately one minute. A power outage affects the entire town. A motorist states that he and his wife observed a slightly oval, motionless mass at 15 to 20 meters above the ground. Suddenly, this mass went dark, revealing a red-orange fire on each side.

A pisciculturist, awakened on March 11 by crackling noises and intense light, will find on March 13, 1800 kilograms of dead fish in one of his breeding ponds. A 10,000-volt power line runs above the pond, and on the night of March 14-15, one of these wires failed: it was damaged over a 4-5 meter length. The EDF (Electricité de France) sector manager was unable to record the incident on the control tape due to the brief power outage (one minute) in the town.

These individuals witnessed an electric arc on the EDF line, which likely contributed to the fish deaths, although analyses revealed a viral infection. We have no information on the precise nature of this infection or its potential impact on fish mortality.
