Summary: Observation of the passage of a bright cylindrical object in the sky with a red halo on one side; rapid disappearance: observation of the passage of a sunlit aircraft.
--- seg-65192b12e030f659d2d81fa3 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 28, 2012, around 10 a.m., a witness observed a luminous phenomenon in a clear sky. From their balcony facing west, the witness tracked with their eyes the slow movement of a cylindrical phenomenon that was bright and silver in color, with a small red halo on its left. The witness went inside their apartment to retrieve their camera, but when they returned after 15 seconds, everything had disappeared. No noise or smoke was noted. Only one testimony was collected. The persistent red light (red halo) to the left of the "cigar" and the sun behind the witness is consistent with a sunlit aircraft. According to the witness's drawing, the viewing angle of a fuselage (seen end-on) could explain the visible absence of wings. The duration of the observation (3 minutes) seems exaggerated. The witness's viewpoint (approximately 35° portion) seems restricted for such a long duration. The sky above the witness is crossed by numerous air lines in all directions (Google Street View shows condensation trails at this location). What makes the phenomenon strange for the witness is the absence of wings and the rapid disappearance. What reduces the strangeness is the lack of other witnesses and the interruption of the observation. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: Aircraft.
