Summary: Observation of luminous halos
--- seg-04c1348904807c7afb7791d4 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 23, 1993, around 00:20, a witness observed from his window glowing halos in the cloudy sky. There were five of them, with an apparent size of 1.5m, rotating among themselves while maintaining the same distance from each other. The witness's mother was also able to observe the phenomenon. After some time, one of the halos suddenly departed at high speed, while the others continued to rotate. The phenomenon remained silent and was observed for approximately 15 minutes. The hypothesis of distant projectors was prioritized.
