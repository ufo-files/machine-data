Summary: Nighttime observation and video of a glowing ball: aeronautical misperception with flight AF7684.
--- seg-5990bce6d68a24763fc2ed7c [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 18, 2020, at 10:10 PM, a witness from their home bedroom, intrigued by the presence of a stationary, never-before-seen white glowing sphere in the sky, never seen at this location before. Given that the witness's bedroom window offers a particularly clear view, they regularly photograph the panorama. The witness then observes that the PAN moves, revealing "luminary emissions" behind it. The witness has time to take a video and photos with their smartphone before the phenomenon disappears from their field of vision. Only one testimony was collected. With very low strangeness and very good consistency (single witness, but quite precise testimony, video and photos of the PAN), the case turns out to be a misidentification with Air France flight AF7684 from Paris landing in Montpellier (see the investigation report and the witness's video and photographic documents). The visual aspect and the description of the PAN prove to be entirely characteristic of a commercial airliner approaching head-on. An Air France A320 (flight AF7684) from Paris landing in Montpellier followed a trajectory perfectly matching that of the PAN, at the exact time indicated by the witness. The witness describes a silent PAN, which remains consistent, given that the plane passed 2 km from the observation location. The case is classified A, misidentification with flight AF7684.
