Summary: Sequential observations of luminous phenomena in the sky: astronomical observations of Venus and the passage of a bolide.
--- seg-62188ec5c99bffccf49470b6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness reports successive observations of silent, glowing orbs in the sky between September 22 and 28, 2013, in the evening. This testimony of observations of a very bright orb close to the horizon, visible for a long time and for several days in a row, and appearing to follow the observer, is a classic misperception, sometimes called a "following orb." Its origin is obviously astronomical: in this case, it is the planet Venus, which was particularly bright that autumn in the early evening (Magnitude = -4.7). The direction indicated by the witness on his drawing corresponds to an approximate azimuth of 241°, while Venus at the same time has an azimuth of 235°. The photos are entirely consistent with this hypothesis, and the witness mentions another orange orb, less bright, slightly above and to the right, which is the planet Saturn (see astronomical chart). The witness reports another, very different phenomenon on September 23, 2013: a brief observation of a passing orange orb. This much rarer phenomenon, called a bolide, very likely corresponds to the atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid (meteorite). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of Venus and a bolide.
