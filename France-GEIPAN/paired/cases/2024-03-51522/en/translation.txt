Description: On March 3, 2024, around noon, the witness, at their home in Plabennec (29), observes through a closed roof window a blue-green circular object reflecting sunlight. The object moves in a straight, stable line from west to east, without notable changes in speed or direction. After 30 to 60 seconds, the witness loses sight of it due to its distance. The consistency of this observation case is average with one witness and no photos or videos of the PAN. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The investigation established that the object observed by the witness was very likely a Mylar-type fantasy balloon, carried by the wind (see investigation report). This conclusion is based on the following elements: - Round shape, quite typical for such a balloon. - Reflective blue-green color, typical of a Mylar-type balloon. - Straight and regular movement with the wind. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of a Mylar balloon.
