Description: Observation made in November 1963 by three witnesses and reported to GEIPAN in April 2008. On November 11, 1963, around 3:00 or 4:00 PM, a father and his two sons (aged 18 and 13) were walking on a rural path and noticed a puzzling light phenomenon. A stationary, orange-colored light ball was below dark clouds for over a minute. After rotating, it oscillated from right to left for a few seconds before disappearing at extremely high speed into the clouds along a curved, upward trajectory. No noise or trail was observed. No other witness accounts regarding this phenomenon were reported. The facts were gradually revealed between 2003 and 2007 by one of the witnesses (the 18-year-old student). The hypothesis of observing a ball lightning (plasma) movement was ruled out, as the witness does not confirm the presence of a storm during the family walk, and there are differences in the characteristics between the observed phenomenon on November 11, 1963, and known ball lightning. GEIPAN classifies this case as D1: Unexplained, moderately consistent phenomenon (single, old, and distant witness) but with a strong strangeness character.
