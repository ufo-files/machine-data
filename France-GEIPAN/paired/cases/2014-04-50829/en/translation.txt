Summary: Observations of the silent movements of a bright orange PAN: unexplained phenomenon of medium strangeness.
--- seg-4a1cd0f8695493c7283b3f91 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the evening of April 24, 2014, a retired couple was watching television in their veranda. At 10:26 PM, Mr. (T1) had his attention drawn by an orange-colored light source appearing through one of the veranda's light wells. He described an incandescent orange ball with an orange flame-like edge without a trail. The light was moving across the overcast sky from the east. This witness thought it was a burning plane about to crash, so he asked his wife (T2) to take a picture of the phenomenon in case of a Civil Aviation investigation. T1 was puzzled by the absence of noise and the odd flight. He noticed a triangular shape before the PAN disappeared behind a curtain of trees at the edge of the cloud cover. T2 had time to take several photos with a mobile phone. Only one questionnaire was completed. No other testimony was collected. No rational explanation seemed possible (see investigation report). - The drone hypothesis is the least bad, but largely relies on the fact that in this area, the field of possibilities is broader than the field of the truly known. While all shapes and lights are possible, we do not encounter them in reality; especially in 2014. It is also unrealistic and dangerous to fly at night over urban areas (beyond the simple fact that it is prohibited); - Other hypotheses are even more fragile. The consistency of the testimonies proved good: - T1 had ample time to observe, given that observation is one of his primary qualities due to his excellent visual acuity and his specific professional practice; - T2's testimony was unfortunately less rich due to the dispersion caused by her attempts to capture a photo of the PAN. The only exploitable image does not allow for a true confirmation of the presence of the PAN, even though the light does not find an explanation from the ambient elements. The confusion introduced by T2 is likely due to unexpected difficulties in mastering the shooting mode (flash activation and glass presence, in addition to the need to act quickly in relative darkness). However, T2's testimony reinforces T1's: - The triangular structure described by T1 is not without reminding other testimonies, even if they remain controversial and a "stereotype" exists at this level, which may potentially influence testimonies. Both witnesses remained measured in their reactions; - An on-site investigation and a cognitive interview were conducted within a short time frame. Elements collected on-site could be more precise and sometimes different from those from the questionnaire. Overall, no element was found against the reliability of the testimonies. The level of consistency allows for validating an unexplained character at this stage. Consequently: the GEIPAN classifies the case as D1: unexplained phenomenon of average strangeness.
