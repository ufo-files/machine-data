Summary: Observation of a cigar-shaped object spinning on itself.
--- seg-f38ff0aa4a9a614232651bd1 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 23, 1979, at approximately 9:40 PM, a driver and her passengers observed a peculiar object for several minutes that intrigued them. This bright orange object, shaped like a cigar, was two meters long and moved quickly from southeast to northwest. Flying at 200 meters above ground level, it appeared to spin on its own axis. It quickly disappeared into the horizon. No other witnesses were interviewed regarding this phenomenon, for which we lack data.
