Summary: Observation of the movement of a luminous phenomenon: observation of the passage of the International Space Station (ISS)
--- seg-54b2c10b71c0c911e8b911de [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness observed on April 25, 2009 at 5:10 AM the silent SO-NE passage of a very bright sphere in the sky. The trajectory was straight and the speed fast and regular. Unfortunately, the photographs taken are unusable. After verification with the space station orbit bulletins (Space-track), this observation case undoubtedly corresponds to a passage of the International Space Station (ISS).
