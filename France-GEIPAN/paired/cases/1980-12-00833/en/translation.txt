--- seg-4683e4a707ab7a2de0212673 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 3, 1980, around 11 PM, a motorist spotted a large circular object approximately 150 meters ahead of him. It was a fluorescent green color and stationary at 10 meters above the road surface. After stopping to observe the object, it moved silently and slowly approached the witness's vehicle. Frightened, the motorist continued on his way, passing under the craft which followed him for about a kilometer. At an intersection, the motorist noticed that the craft sped off in the opposite direction and quickly disappeared. His observation lasted 3 to 4 minutes. During the investigation, another witness stated that he saw a blue glow that evening, but his testimony was very brief. Despite an appeal for witnesses and an investigation at the scene, no further information was gathered about this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
