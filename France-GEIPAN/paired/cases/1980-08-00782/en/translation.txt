Summary: Observation of a glowing ball.
--- seg-2854ac14e9635a377891b380 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 24, 1980, two witnesses returning home around 4:30 a.m. spotted a very bright white ball in the sky. It emitted intense light illuminating the countryside. The ball rose and took a northern direction after performing a right-angle maneuver. It then became stationary, allowing the witnesses to observe the phenomenon for an hour despite the intense brightness that hurt their eyes. They went to bed but heard crackling sounds. Photographs taken by one of the witnesses could not be developed: the film was declared "burnt" by the photographer who refused to process it. One of the witnesses reported seeing the very bright white ball again later that same day. No other witnesses in the area came forward. No additional information was gathered on this phenomenon for which we lack details.
