With low strangeness and good consistency given the precision of the testimony, this observation case cannot be concluded without additional information. The hypothesis of an aircraft with particular lighting could fit, but could not be confirmed (see the investigation report). This case is classified C due to lack of independent testimonies and cross-checks.
