Description: On July 14, 2009, around 11:45 PM, a witness observed in the sky red lights moving at a constant speed 150 meters (492 feet) above ground. No noise was heard during the 10 seconds of observation. These lights, forming a V, disappeared into a cloud. Many similar observations have been reported in the local press. This witness likely observed flying lanterns, a phenomenon currently very popular, especially during festive events.
