--- seg-3e972f5fbef10c28368e5363 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 6th at 0:24 a.m., a witness observed from the terrace of his home the passage of 12 glowing yellow-orange orbs in the sky. The movement was silent and on an ascending trajectory. The light of these orbs gradually dimmed. One of the photographs (DSC 03903) provided by the witness allows us to position the Moon relative to the orbs, then to recalibrate the observation direction indicated by the witness, which is found to be incorrect. The orbs would have been floating in a southeast direction, which is consistent with wind direction. As the phenomenon lasted no more than 5 minutes with the gradual extinction of the lights, the slow trajectory, and the variation in relative positions between the orbs, the time of observation (in the night from Saturday to Sunday), and the yellow-orange color. All of this confirms the hypothesis of Thai lanterns. Without having found the origin of these lanterns, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
