Description: On December 10, 1976, at 10:10 PM, a witness observed the passage E-N of a bright point moving silently at a speed equivalent to that of a shooting star. In the shape of an oval and silver in color, the phenomenon left no glowing trail. Visible for a few seconds, it disappeared, hidden by the landscape. On December 11, at 1:00 AM, a military personnel observed for 3 to 4 minutes the passage N-S of an oval object at high altitude, leaving a glowing trail. It is highly probable that these witnesses observed atmospheric reentries.
