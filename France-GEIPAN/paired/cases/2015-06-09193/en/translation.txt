Description: On Saturday, June 6, 2015, at 10:10 AM, a witness near a theme park was intrigued by something spinning in the sky. The PAN made small circles, reminiscent of a bird circling a prey or a drone. However, the object suddenly changed course and headed northwest at a very steady speed without any noise. The object was then obscured by vegetation. Only one testimony was collected. This testimony expresses little perceived strangeness as the witness had one or several possible explanations for their observation, such as a Common Shelduck (a type of bird) or a drone. Indeed, it could be either, although the (recreational) drone hypothesis is more compatible with all phases of the observation. We were near a theme park (Radome Park) during the daytime around 10 AM on a Saturday. There were, a priori, many potential observers (the witness mentioned others present) of the phenomenon, both outside and inside the park. Nevertheless, we have no other testimonies. Therefore, the most likely scenario is that the witness witnessed something relatively ordinary, and that this ordinary phenomenon was a recreational drone (the witness's video shows nothing, but the audio indicates a discussion where one voice opposes the drone hypothesis with the argument that the drone pilot is not visible, an argument contested by another voice). Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable drone observation.
