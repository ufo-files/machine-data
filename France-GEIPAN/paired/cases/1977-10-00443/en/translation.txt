--- seg-a22031d1ac08179b96a30ae1 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 18, 1977, at 12:24 AM, a witness observed for 4 minutes a bright light in the sky that approached, made a wide curve while losing altitude, and seemed to hover above a hill. The witness reported seeing the dark mass of a craft with a circular porthole emitting bright blue and orange lights. After 10 seconds, it vanished instantly. Despite a gendarmerie investigation, no other testimonies were collected.
