Summary: Observation of the O-E passage of a luminous phenomenon: probable observation of an atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-4976f86b1f073ac148b502ef [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: The witness is at the summit of the Col de l'Escrinet in Ardèche when they spot in the sky a fiery light phenomenon with a wide, yellow trail that crosses the sky from West to East on a horizontal trajectory. This case is to be associated with the simultaneous observation case Aero ARA 102 (001) 07.01.2013 made by an airline pilot. Other testimonies will appear in the press. Given the testimonies of the witnesses, the observed phenomenon is very likely the re-entry into the atmosphere of a satellite debris, possibly from the Iridium 33 satellite. This case is classified as "B".
