Summary: Observation of the low-altitude passage of two green lights in the sky, preceded by an unusual noise: observation of a military aircraft of type A400M.
--- seg-a274a34bf2a39034e2e9dfec [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 26, 2023, at approximately 9:40 PM, in the commune of Kervignac (56), a witness (T1) was observing the sky when they heard an atypical noise emanating from an unidentified aerial phenomenon (PAN) that they were then able to visualize. This phenomenon was described as consisting of two intense, non-flashing green lights, moving at a relatively low altitude (between 20 and 30 degrees) and following a trajectory from north to east-southeast. The observation lasted several minutes. Simultaneously, a colleague (T2), residing nearby the observation location, corroborated having heard this peculiar noise, although they did not observe the phenomenon as they remained inside their home. The case's consistency is good, as there are two witnesses (one only auditory). The absence of a photograph of the PAN is offset by a relatively complete and precise testimony. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony. The investigation revealed that the PAN corresponded to a military aircraft of type A400M, which had recently taken off from the Lann-Bihoué Naval Air Base and was operating at low altitude. This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- Proven presence on radar traces provided by the National Center for Air Operations of an A400M evolving along a trajectory similar to that of the PAN, at the same time, and at low altitude according to the testimony.
- Both witnesses heard a noise that T1 described as a hum, which is perfectly compatible with the sound emitted by the turboprop engines of the A400M.
- Observation at the level of the treetops, therefore at a low elevation angle, calculated around 4 degrees; Note an overestimation of the elevation by the witness (20 to 30 degrees), which is quite common for night observations.
- The witness suspected it might be a military aircraft, but the nature of the observed lights and the noise formed for him the essence of the strangeness. The absence of anti-collision lights (red or white) is entirely possible for a military aircraft such as the A400M, which can turn them off at will depending on flight needs (exercise, stealth...). Stealth is indeed reinforced on this aircraft by its gray color, which makes it more difficult to see at night, explaining why the witness could only see lights. The only observed lights were green, corresponding to the aircraft's position lights, observed from its right side by the witness. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A", observation of a military aircraft of type A400M.
