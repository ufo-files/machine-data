Description: Recent testimony (23/08/2008) of an old observation: on August 12, 1994, at 11:45 PM, a group of people was leaving a seaside village. The person in the lead suddenly had his attention drawn to a stationary black mass above him. For 6 seconds, he saw a huge triangular shape bordered on two sides by small, irregular lights in terms of intensity and alignment. Two children also observed the triangle, which, in complete silence, spun around itself and moved away towards the southeast, disappearing over the horizon. The witness noted all of this in a sketchbook and attached an illustration of his observation to his testimony. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, making any investigation today impossible.
