Description: On June 1, 1952, at 2:40 a.m., two individuals from the crew of a ship at anchor off Port-Gentil (Gabon) observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky. Initially, the second captain clearly saw an object coming from the interior of the land that stopped, turned to the right, and resumed its initial course—twice (the second turn occurring vertically above the ship). He called the commander, who then observed with binoculars and saw, for three minutes, an extremely bright and phosphorescent orange circular glow. This phenomenon moved at a very high speed along a substantially straight trajectory. The commander lost sight of the phenomenon. He reported the observation. A sketch was made (author unknown). No other testimonies were collected. The hypotheses of a bolide (meteor) or missile launch were suggested but not confirmed. The GEIPAN classified this case as D: Strange phenomenon, very strange/of medium to strong consistency.
