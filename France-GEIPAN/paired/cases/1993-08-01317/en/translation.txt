--- seg-cbdf2264a7038f0ef0fdfbd5 [needs-review] ---
Description: During the night of July 24 to 25, 1993, between 2:45 and 3:30 a.m., three witnesses observed a white glowing phenomenon that moved by spinning with many bright points. The investigation quickly revealed that it was a multi-faceted projector from a nearby nightclub, whose light reflecting off the low overcast ceiling could be seen from a great distance. The nightclub manager, also interviewed, confirmed this hypothesis.
