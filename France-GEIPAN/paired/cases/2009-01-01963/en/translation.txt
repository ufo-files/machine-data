Description: A witness reports successive observations (between January 23 and February 2, 2009) of bright points. The witness provided several dozen photos that underpin their testimony. These night-time photos are taken with a flash. None of them show anything unusual, but orbs (dust illuminated by the flash) are visible on a large majority of them. The witness likely exaggerated the description and interpretation of their observations. See an example of orbs and an explanation on http://www.geipan.fr/index.php?id=374. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": perfectly identified phenomenon.
