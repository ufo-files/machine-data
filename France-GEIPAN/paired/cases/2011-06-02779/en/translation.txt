Summary: Observations of the movements of a luminous object and a circular trace of laid-down grass: lack of information.
--- seg-611dd8d7309c1d0be31ff5f3 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 14, 2011, around 10:30 PM, two individuals were intrigued by the silent and variable speed movements of a bright object in the sky. The observation lasted between 5 and 10 minutes. On June 16, when these two people returned to the site, they discovered a circular area of 1.5m with grass laid down in the middle of tall grass. The description of the phenomenon is dual. Initially, it is mentioned a powerful white light, moving two to three times faster than a plane, accompanied by a red glow when the object crosses a cloud: this clearly corresponds to the observation of the International Space Station (ISS). Subsequently, the second phenomenon, independent of the first, rapidly changing trajectory and speed, resembles a helicopter, but the GEIPAN was unable to verify this hypothesis, and the testimony is not precise enough to justify this explanation. Of average strangeness, this observation case would be classified "A" for the first part, "C" for the second, and without object for the traces in the grass. This case is overall classified as "C," due to lack of detailed information about the second part of the observation.
