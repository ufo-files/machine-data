Summary: Observation of the very high-speed movement of a bright point along a straight trajectory: lack of information.
--- seg-bef2f58cc7fd57d5d1a155de [needs-review] ---
Description: During the night of Saturday, June 5th, to Sunday, June 6th, 2010, several witnesses were intrigued by the rapid passage of a white light. The trajectory was low and from East to South. The observation lasted between 5 to 10 seconds. A large satellite could have given this appearance (besides the visible shape, which might be some kind of optical illusion). Some satellites are on slightly retrograde orbits (inclination of around 100°) that could follow an East to South trajectory. When GEIPAN investigated this (in 2012), simulations were no longer possible for a 2010 date. We are unable to conclude firmly this observation case, with its relatively low strangeness (a glowing point moving in a straight line) and good consistency (precise testimony). This case is classified C due to lack of independent testimonies and cross-checks.
