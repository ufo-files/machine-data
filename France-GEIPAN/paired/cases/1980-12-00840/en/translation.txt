--- seg-88edf9600aafbb16ef8496cd [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 16, 1980, around 11 a.m., a witness spotted in the clear sky a round object they could not identify. This object was topped with a pyramid-shaped antenna; it was metallic in color and momentarily blinded the witness. A light yellow halo surrounded it. It was heading east, performing ascending and descending movements. The observation lasted 4 minutes before the object progressively and completely disappeared towards the south. The gendarmerie did not gather any other testimonies about this phenomenon, for which we lack information.
