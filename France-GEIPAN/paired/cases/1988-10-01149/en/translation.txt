Summary: Observation of laser projector from a hypermarket
--- seg-f2bf74e1d936c4b64fcaae0e [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 6, 1988, around 9:30 PM, several witnesses observed above region 3 three blue lights moving slowly in the sky. The gendarmes called to the scene confirmed the phenomenon, which definitively disappeared around 12:30 AM. The investigation, which unsuccessfully explored the possibility of a helicopter theft, ultimately led to a hypermarket conducting laser projector tests for an advertising operation.
