Summary: A pilot observed several moving bright points with variable luminosity in a limited area of the sky: observation of Starlink satellite flashes in operational orbit.
--- seg-a7d950750f79fa70472c3ff5 [needs-review] ---
Description: The witness, a commercial pilot (T1), reports the observation on January 31, 2023, during a TVF flight from Amman (Jordan) to Paris-Orly Airport, of several bright points moving for 5 to 10 seconds, with their brightness varying significantly before disappearing. Two recurring axes are followed by these bright points. The observation is also carried out by his colleague (T2), who will not testify. The observation begins at 20:10 (UTC) as the aircraft flies over the south of the city of Milan (Italy) and ends over Lake Geneva. It lasts about thirty minutes. During this event, T1 takes a video. Of low strangeness and good consistency (multiple witnesses, video of PAN), this case turns out to be an observation of Starlink satellite flashes (see the investigation report). The description and visual aspect of the PAN are very consistent with this type of observation: a long observation duration located in a limited sector of the sky, several mobile bright points varying in brightness before disappearing. A check in Stellarium, after identifying the visible stars in the PAN video, confirms that the latter is at 45° vertically from the apparent position of the Sun, while the Sun is approximately 38° below the horizon. These conditions are entirely consistent with those enabling the phenomenon of sunlight reflection off structural elements of Starlink satellites, also known as "cluster flares." A satellite pass verification confirms that numerous Starlink satellites passed near the star Markab, according to two main axes corresponding to those observed in the video of PAN. The case is classified A, observation of Starlink satellite flashes in operational orbit. This observation presents very great similarities with other cases investigated by the GEIPAN and published on the GEIPAN website. [AERO AFR] from ABIDJAN (CI.AB) to PARIS (95) on 04/08/2023. [AERO TVF] from EREVAN (AM.ER) to ORLY (94) on 12/13/2023. [AERO AFR] from KINSHASA (CD.KN) to PARIS (75) on 04/10/2023. These four cases support an investigation technical note and the publication of a news item.
