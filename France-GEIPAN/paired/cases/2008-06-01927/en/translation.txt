Summary: Observations of a black spherical object with gray tubes below; initially stationary, then slow movement towards the West on a straight trajectory at the same altitude in the sky: mistaken for a public promotional festive balloon.
--- seg-22ebf8a021c492d00275932c [needs-review] ---
Description: On Monday, June 23, 2008, at approximately 8:45 a.m., a witness (T1) is on a supermarket parking lot and is talking to a person who informs him of the presence in the sky of a stationary object. T1 observes the object moving silently and straight towards the West. The witness decides to run to his girlfriend's (T2) apartment, which is on the sixth floor, thinking he will have better visibility. Upon arrival, T1 and his girlfriend (T2) go to the balcony: the PAN is now just a point above the bus station. No smoke or lights are seen coming from the circular PAN with smooth walls. T1 describes below the black sphere gray tubes. A drawing in the report illustrates his statements. Two testimonies will be collected (T1 and T2); the third witness present on the parking lot never made a statement. The observed object moved in the direction of the prevailing wind (see attached: weather; source: archives: Météociel): West to East (12 km/h max). Its shape (sphere with 4 tubes, smooth appearance, rounded ends of tubes) is consistent with advertising balloons and festive balloons (polymorphic, animal, logo, etc.). June 23 and 24 are special days in this department: descent of the flame of Canigou, fireworks, rides, etc. Balloon sellers are numerous near exhibition parks. The shape and behavior are consistent with a escaped balloon, flying in the direction of the wind, at a slow to moderate speed. The object appeared 400 meters west of the Palace of Exhibitions, which hosts numerous fairs and salons, with advertising objects at the entrances and inside to mark the stands. What seemed strange to the witness were the apparent tubes attached to the sphere and the regular and linear movement. However, the humanoid or animal shape strengthens the hypothesis of a festive balloon (the tubes would be the four legs of a spherical body). The linear movement is consistent with the prevailing wind between 8 a.m. and 9 a.m. The homogeneous and smooth appearance described by the witness corresponds to the description of a polymorphic balloon carried by the wind. It is rather the linear and regular movement towards the West that seemed strange to him than the shape. The consistency of the testimony is good (two witnesses but no photos), the strangeness is low. For the witness, the strangeness comes from the linear movement towards the West. The witness, in an urban environment, did not notice the East wind (12 km/h) in progress. The description and movement of the object correspond to a festive balloon with an animal or fanciful (or polymorphic) shape carried by the wind: "spherical body equipped with 4 tubes with rounded ends, overall homogeneous appearance, smooth appearance." June 23 is a regional holiday in Perpignan. The phenomenon is classified A: strong probability of a misperception with a festive advertising balloon.
