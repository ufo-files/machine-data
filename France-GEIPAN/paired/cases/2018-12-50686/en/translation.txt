Description: On Monday, December 10, 2018, between 07:20 and 07:40, a witness driving down the alleyway in front of their home was intrigued by an unusual phenomenon to the west, above a field at a low altitude. The witness stops and describes the PAN as a "electric" yellow bar with a large green fluorescent beacon in its center. While in the car with the window open, the witness heard no noise during the PAN's movements. The object first moves slowly and regularly, then turns directly south towards the witness and remains stationary. The witness arrives at the end of the alley, turns around, and sees this bar depart full East and slowly disappear into the fog. The discontinuous observation (witness's movement and stop while in a car) lasted 1 minute 30 seconds. The witness reports a problem with the radio in their vehicle following this observation. He also states in the questionnaire: "I justify the strangeness by the absolutely silent and regular movement as well as the bright appearance." No further testimony will be collected. Several combined factors (the three-phase trajectory with a middle stop, the perceived large size, the slow moving speed, and the very clear outline appearance) contradict, individually or in combination, the various hypotheses considered (see the investigation report): a drone (size, appearance), reflections from agricultural vehicle lights or sky-tracer laser projectors onto the cloud layer (speed, appearance), an object carried by the wind, similar to Thai lanterns (trajectory, appearance). - The observation took place at night (early morning), and French regulations do not permit drone flights at night. In the hypothetical scenario of a rule-breaking owner still using their device, the appearance of the PAN is unusual and contributes to the witness's perceived strangeness, in addition to the observed changes in trajectory. - The investigation revealed that no agricultural device (light projection hypothesis) was present in the fields or where the PAN was observed. - Finally, for the last hypothesis, the three-phase trajectory with a stop facing the witness and a subsequent change in direction is a strong argument against the hypothesis of an object carried by the wind. In the end, the drone hypothesis is the strongest, but its probability is assessed as well below 50%, so it cannot be retained. These hypotheses do not allow for an explanation of the strangeness of the observation perceived by the witness. This is a unique testimony, but it is entirely possible (rural area, low population density, early morning hour) that the phenomenon was not seen by other people. The witness is precise, the consistency (level of information) of the testimony is good despite the lack of photos or video of the phenomenon itself. But the observation was clarified by photos of situations and reconstructions (analysis of dimensions, distances, trajectories of PAN) during a field investigation supported by a cognitive interview of the witness (very cooperative). Thus, the strangeness of the observation remains great, the consistency of the testimony is good. The analysis did not allow for the identification of a probable compatible hypothesis at more than 50%. This indicates according to the GEIPAN methodology the unexplained character of the observation after investigation for this strange phenomenon of average or strong consistency. As a result, this case is classified by the GEIPAN as an unidentified phenomenon "D1".
