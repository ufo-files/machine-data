Summary: Observation of the movement of luminous objects.
--- seg-a653a1a482f472c3743e6d62 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 23, 1977, around 1:45 a.m., a witness observed a distant orange light that seemed to be approaching them. Awakening other people, these witnesses saw very close to each other glowing balls, about the size of a soccer ball. One object stood out from the others: it was rectangular in shape and narrower at the back with lit openings. These objects moved without making any noise. The hypothesis of observing the movement of a transport plane (the presence of a military airport in Châteauroux) could not be confirmed due to a lack of information.
