Description: On November 14, 1979, at approximately 5:42 PM, a witness, an air force officer on leave, observed peculiar luminous phenomena in a clear sky. While driving, the witness and his passenger suddenly saw two point lights of red-orange color. Quite close together, they stabilized at an altitude of about 100 meters above the woods and went out one after the other. No noise was noticed. Thirty seconds later, a double "bang" occurred almost simultaneously. The witness thought it was probably the breaking of the sound barrier by a high-altitude patrol. One minute later, two lights appeared at the same altitude and distance but further north than the first ones. Five seconds later, these lights went out one after the other. Local press also reported that several people in the area witnessed this phenomenon. The gendarmerie collected no further testimonies or details about this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
