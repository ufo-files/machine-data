Description: On January 20, 2010, around 10:30 PM, an individual observed from their doorstep the rapid movement of a light. No particular noise was heard during the East-West movement of the phenomenon. The information provided in the questionnaire is too brief to allow for an investigation. Despite reminders from GEIPAN encouraging the witness to testify at the gendarmerie in order to initiate a preliminary inquiry, no additional information allows progress on this case which does not present a degree of strangeness (distant sparkling light?). The consistency of the case (1 sole witness and brief questionnaire) is low. This case is classified C due to lack of information and an impossible investigation (notably the verification of air traffic).
