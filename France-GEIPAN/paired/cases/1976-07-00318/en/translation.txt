Summary: Probable observation of a sun rise reflection phenomenon.
--- seg-17d5429e76c1d6ef6f95fc66 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 13, 1976, at 5:45 a.m., two witnesses, finishing their night shift at a quarry, suddenly observed a stationary white sphere in the sky that gradually increased in size. The observation lasted 4 to 5 seconds, after which the phenomenon disappeared. No sound was heard. According to the gendarmerie investigation, the observers were in the highest point of the region and had a very wide field of vision. The weather was sunny and there were no clouds. The phenomenon was located vertically above the coast of Vaucouleurs, at a distance of approximately 8 km. At this date and time, it is likely that these individuals witnessed a phenomenon of reflection of the sun just risen.
