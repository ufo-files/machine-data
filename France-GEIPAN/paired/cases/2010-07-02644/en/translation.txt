Description: On July 13, 2010, at 11:27 PM, a witness observed in the sky at approximately 150 meters distance three orange glowing points arranged in a triangle, delineating a dark mass. Initially stationary, the entire formation then moved slowly in a linear motion without any sound. No other testimonies were collected regarding this phenomenon. The characteristics of the observation resemble those of a known and frequently observed phenomenon associated with the passage of Thai lanterns (festive period for observation in July, night of July 13-14, observation of three orange glowing points, absence of sound, objects moving linearly in the same direction, wind direction roughly consistent with the observation given that it was made near the sea with locally changing winds...). The dark triangular shape between the three glowing points described by the witness is a 'phenomenon' suggested by the human brain. The observation presents no peculiarities. The case consistency is low due to the unique testimony (despite the large population that should have been outdoors at that time) and the limited information contained in the gendarmerie hearing report. The plausible hypothesis of observing the passage of Thai lanterns is retained, and this case of low strangeness level is classified B as very probably explained.
