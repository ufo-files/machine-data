Description: On December 21, 2014, at 21:09, a witness, positioned on their balcony facing the ocean, was intrigued by a luminous phenomenon floating above the ocean. They observed this glow through binoculars and distinguished a dark object with a lower section glowing an orange light. The witness took photographs, but they do not capture what was seen through the binoculars. At 22:15, the craft silently glided in a southerly direction. This testimony is consistent: precise and accompanied by two original photographs, but from a single witness. Given the collected elements (see the investigation report), namely: - PAN observed near the horizon. - Visual appearance similar to that of a ship. - Observation of the green navigation light on the starboard side. - Observation of the ship's reflections, possibly bridges, on the water. - Observation axis directed towards a frequented maritime area. The observation is not unusual because the PAN is ultimately mundane, both in its appearance and behavior. The GEIPAN in this case is classified as 'B', indicating a very probable observation of a ship sailing off the island of Réunion.
