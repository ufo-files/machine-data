Summary: Long observation in the early morning sky of a white, elongated, bright object: observation of an airplane and its condensation trail.
--- seg-2f21e93878a4c01a8ca8164d [machine-unreviewed] ---
**Description:** On May 13, 2015, at 8:40 a.m., a driver on the Paris ring road was intrigued by the presence in the sky of a long, bright, white object. Through his windshield, he took three photographs. The PAN (presumed Aircraft with Non-identifiable Properties) seemed stationary then moved barely at all. The witness lost sight of it as he exited the ring road. Only one statement was collected. The strangeness of this case is low; it is mainly due to the perception of the PAN's immobility. The statement is quite consistent, with enough data provided by the witness and exploitable. Several photographs of the PAN were taken and could be used for analysis (see the investigation report). The appearance of the PAN and its slow, or even "stationary" movement, as noted by the witness, are typical of commercial aircraft and their condensation trails observed at high altitude when they are already illuminated by the rising or setting sun, not yet visible from the ground. The three photographs were taken by the witness at 08:42:37, 08:42:48, and 08:42:54, that is, at the exact moment of sunrise. Depending on local weather conditions at the altitude of the considered aircraft, a condensation trail may or may not form. If present, it may also not be distinguished as such by the witness, due to a combined effect of distance and perspective. The witness therefore equated the trail with a single object, with the aircraft itself possibly not being visible. This condensation trail can dissipate quickly behind the aircraft in some places and take a long time in others along the same trajectory, which explains here the duration of the observation over more than 10 minutes (which was not that of the aircraft, but that of a more persistent trail segment). This case is to be classified as "A" as an observation of an aircraft and its condensation trail.
