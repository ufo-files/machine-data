They then see, for 5 seconds, two non-flashing white spheres moving side by side in the sky. Their partner also catches sight of them, but will not testify. Only one statement is collected. This case has average consistency: only one witness reported their observation, with clear information but no photos and a very short observation (see the investigation report). The investigation concluded that the observation of two LED balloons is very probable, given the objective elements defined in the previous chapters, namely: - PAN moving in a generally straight line with the wind, - Spherical shapes of bright white and fixed color, - No noise, - Balloons are often released in small groups. Observing two individuals is quite plausible, - Observation on a Wednesday evening: balloons could have been released for a child's birthday. Classification as "A": Very probable observation of two LED balloons.
