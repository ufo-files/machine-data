Description: On July 26, 2012, at 10:20 PM, a witness on their balcony was intrigued by the quick and silent passage of a dim, hazy ball in the clear sky. During the object's S-N movement, the witness noticed bright points below the object as well as several small jet trails at the rear. The observation lasted 6 seconds before the object was hidden by the house roof. No other witnesses have come forward. We are dealing with a case of a straight-line moving phenomenon, thus of average strangeness a priori, but the completely unusual aspect of the phenomenon makes it stranger. A relatively large number of hypotheses need to be considered, as the phenomenon resembles various causes (see investigation report), but none fit well. The case consistency is good, with a high-quality, very precise, well-documented testimony written by the witness immediately after the observation. However, this remains a unique testimony, without photos or videos. The GEIPAN, unable to confirm any of the considered hypotheses, classifies this case as "D1" as an unexplained phenomenon.
