Summary: OBSERVATION OF AN OBJECT CHANGING SHAPE. SUDDEN DISAPPEARANCE.
--- seg-422cdec33f396b69b0ae13b6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 29, 1980, around 8:50 PM, two witnesses on their terrace observed a peculiar phenomenon in the sky. From the West, a black sphere, seen in silhouette, passed at a high speed directly overhead. The witnesses then saw its shape change, and they caught a glimpse of a dozen square portholes as if illuminated from within. An intense light suddenly appeared, and this entire assembly disappeared towards the East. The total observation lasted 20 to 30 seconds, during which no noise was heard. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, and we lack information about it.
