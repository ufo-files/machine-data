Summary: Observations of the movement of a dozen orange glowing orbs in the sky: observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-e3f73f92f7dd735ee8114604 [needs-review] ---
Description: From September 20th to 21st, 2008, a witness observed about a dozen orange balls in the night sky moving between the communes of Gouvieux and Precy-sur-Oise. These glowing orbs, "lit up from the inside as well as on all sides," disappeared at the level of the commune of Precy-sur-Oise. The silent movement of the objects was in a line at low altitude. Only one testimony was collected at GEIPAN. The gendarmerie received two more calls that night regarding lights in the sky. The witness's observation is characteristic of an observation of Thai lanterns, both in terms of colors, shapes, and the type of trajectory. The occurrence on a weekend night, favorable for festive events, strengthens this hypothesis. The general east-west direction of the movement is consistent with the direction of the wind measured. Furthermore, the local gendarmerie's report mentions reports at the same time that are also characteristic of Thai lanterns. Consequently, GEIPAN classifies the case as B: observation of Thai lanterns.
