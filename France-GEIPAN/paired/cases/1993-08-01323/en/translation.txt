Summary: Observation of white light from an airplane in landing phase
--- seg-bfdfc9ec3f9ec22ad3f6ffdb [needs-review] ---
Description: On the 17th of August 1993, around 11 PM, two witnesses observed two white lights and flashing red lights moving slowly without any audible noise. The investigation quickly revealed that that evening, a military DC-8 aircraft from England was searching for the landing strip and made wide turns in the relevant area. The observed lights and signals correspond to the aircraft's lighting.
