The testimony aligns with the characteristics of Thai lanterns: groups of bright lights moving in the direction of the wind (from southwest) and lasting for a few minutes. The time and day (Saturday evening) are fairly common for this type of festive event. However, it is worth noting that the witness mentions white lights: even white lanterns typically emit a yellow-orange light. This case is classified as "B": probable Thai lanterns.
