Summary: Multiple observations of luminous phenomena in the Ardennes sky: lack of information.
--- seg-e0589ae3c9657685f458f8e1 [needs-review] ---
Description: Between October 30, 1957, at 11:30 PM and October 31, 1957, at 1:30 AM, two distinct phenomena were observed by several groups of witnesses in the Ardennes (08). The first group observed from Louvergny at 11:40 PM in a western direction a stationary red disk of great diameter that seemed to split into two parts at 11:50 PM before disappearing. Astronomical verification indicates the Moon at an azimuth of 245° and an elevation of 30°, appearing as a half-disk tilted. The descriptions provided do not match the Moon (elevation and shape). On the same day, a second group on RN46 between Pauvres and Mazagran observed a small object with green reflections moving rapidly from approximately southeast to northwest. The observation lasted a few dozen seconds. The witnesses' description suggests the passage of a bolide (meteorite fall). In the same evening, a third group located 100 meters from the Novy-Chevrières level crossing observed at 11:50 PM in the direction of this town the passage through the overcast sky of a bright red object (see map). No noise was heard during the observation. Despite uncertainties in observation directions, it is possible that these witnesses mistook the Moon located exactly in that direction (Moon in front of which clouds could have moved). However, this hypothesis is not consistent with the object's movement as reported by the witnesses. Still on October 30th, at 11:50 PM in Ambly-Fleury, a fourth group observed the passage of a red glow in the sky in the same observation direction as the third group. It could also be a temporary observation of the Moon through a gap in the clouds. The GEIPAN classifies these cases as C due to lack of information and cross-checking: it could be a mistake with the Moon and the passage of a bolide, but the elements are not precise enough to confirm these hypotheses.
