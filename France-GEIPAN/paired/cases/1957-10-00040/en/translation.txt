Summary: Multiple observations of luminous phenomena in the Ardennes sky: lack of information.
--- seg-e0589ae3c9657685f458f8e1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Between October 30, 1957, at 11:30 PM, and October 31, 1957, at 1:30 AM, two distinct phenomena were observed by several groups of witnesses in the Ardennes (08). The first group observed from Louvergny at 23:40 a large stationary red disk facing west, which seemed to split into two parts at 23:50 before disappearing. Astronomical verification gives the Moon an azimuth of 245° and an elevation of 30°, appearing as a half-disk tilted. The given descriptions do not correspond well with the Moon (elevation and shape). On the same day, a second group observed on RN46 between Pauvres and Mazagran a small object with green reflections moving rapidly from approximately southeast to northwest. The observation lasted a few dozen seconds. The witness description suggests the passage of a meteoroid (meteorite fall). During the same evening, a third group located 100m from the Novy-Chevrières level crossing observed at 23:50, in the direction of this small town, the passage through the cloudy sky of a red glowing object in the overcast sky (see map). No noise was heard during the observation. Despite uncertainties about observation directions, it is possible that these witnesses mistook the Moon located exactly in that direction (Moon in front of which clouds could have moved). However, this hypothesis is not compatible with the object's movement as reported by the witnesses. On October 30 at 23:50 in Ambly-Fleury, a fourth group observed the passage of a red light in the sky in the same observation direction as the third group. It could also be a temporary observation of the Moon through a gap in the clouds. The GEIPAN classifies these cases as C due to lack of information and cross-checking: it could be a misperception of the Moon and passage of a meteoroid, but the elements are not precise enough to confirm these hypotheses.
