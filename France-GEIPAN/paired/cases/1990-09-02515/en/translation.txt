Description: Recent testimony (31/07/2009) of an old observation (September 1990 or 1991, the witness cannot remember the exact date). A witness reports observing at dusk a slowly moving circular object of large dimensions (between 20 and 40 meters) emitting a regular rumble. For 3 to 4 minutes, this person saw blinking lights of various colors around the object. The object had a straight trajectory from northeast to south at an altitude between 100 and 200 meters. The object disappeared without changing its trajectory, blending into the sky. The witness will file a formal complaint with the local police. No other testimony about this phenomenon has been collected. The age of the observation makes any investigation difficult, if not impossible. Following a new letter from the witness (dated 14 September 2010), he specifies the observation date as September 1991 (no precision on the day) and attaches documents about the object's trajectory as well as a schematic representation he made of the observed object (see witness letter).
