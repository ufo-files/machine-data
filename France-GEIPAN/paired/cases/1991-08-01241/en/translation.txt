Description: On August 5, 1991, around 2:20 AM, two gendarmes on patrol observed a red sphere approximately 80 cm in diameter moving very quickly and silently at about 15 meters above the ground. The phenomenon lasted for a very brief period. The statements of the two gendarmes are consistent, and the phenomenon could not be identified.
