Description: On September 28, 2015, at 5:26 AM, a witness outside observed for 5 seconds the silent passage across the sky of a red triangular shape (round red ends, the object itself being less bright than its ends). The PAN moved parallel to the horizon, in a straight line, and instantly disappeared into deep sky. Only one testimony was collected. The witness observed a red triangle bounded by 3 bright red lights; it's possible the triangular shape was a perception illusion (contour illusion), and only the 3 lights were genuinely there. A review of satellite passes on Calsky indicates two visible satellites on the North East horizon where the witness observed: USA 16/NOSS 1-7A, and Lacrosse 5 Rocket. There is indeed a third satellite on the same trajectory: NOSS 6 (D), but it passes 10 minutes later. Therefore, we cannot directly correlate the witness's observation with these 3 satellites, unless the Calsky modeling is inaccurate. The red color would be surprising, but at low elevation, it would be plausible due to the thickness of the atmosphere traversed. We can also consider Thai lanterns moving south, pushed by the light north wind (as per Météociel), but the day and time (Monday morning) seem less compatible with this hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies this unusual phenomenon as Category C due to the lack of information to confirm or refute these hypotheses.
