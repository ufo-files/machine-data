Summary: Observation of the passage of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-2654ac72faed61a0ffbc0bef [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Sunday, September 6, 2009, two witnesses observed from their home the O-E passage of several orange orbs, all equidistant from each other. No noise was heard during the observation. Local press reported in the following days that many people made similar observations that night and that photos and videos were taken. A release of one hundred Thai lanterns during a wedding celebration is the source of these observations.
