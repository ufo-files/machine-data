Summary: Observation of silent movements of 5 bright red and orange spheres in the night sky: lack of information.
--- seg-d92f816b1d0aed4d705ae675 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 1, 2015, around 1 a.m., several members of the same family were intrigued by the movements in the clear sky of 5 bright, red (for one) and orange (for the others) glowing spheres. No noise was heard during their movement. The objects change position, rising and falling before disappearing one after the other towards the East. Only one testimony will be collected. The observation of orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a night of a big celebration, clearly suggests Thai lanterns, probably released nearby after a private party. The movement is observed from southwest to east, which does not correspond well with the light south wind recorded in Auch, 30 km away (see the Météociel record for the day of the observation in Auch. We also note a quite atypical behavior of the phenomenon with a very variable altitude, perhaps related to the great cold of this night (-4°C). Due to uncertainty about local winds and the absence of photos or other witnesses, GEIPAN classifies this case as C, lacking meteorological information and corroboration.
