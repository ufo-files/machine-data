Description: On July 20, 2015, two people at their home were intrigued by the slow movement in the sky of an object with several lights of different colors. A rumbling engine sound was heard. The PAN moved straight from North to South. The observation lasted between 4 and 7 minutes. Only one testimony was reported to GEIPAN. Given the elements collected (see the investigation report), namely:

- Observation of lights with colors identical to those of aircraft navigation lights.
- Correct positioning of these lights in relation to the direction of movement of PAN and the position of the witnesses.
- Observation of a searchlight beam comparable to that equipped on civil security and gendarmerie helicopters.
- Perception of a motor noise identical to that of an airplane.

GEIPAN classified this case as "B" as a probable observation of a helicopter. Due to the low strangeness of the observation, GEIPAN did not conduct inquiries with the national gendarmerie or air traffic control.
