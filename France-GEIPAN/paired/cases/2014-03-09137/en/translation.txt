Description: On March 21, 2014, around 9 PM, a witness observed for 2 to 3 minutes three equally spaced, yellow-orange lights moving silently on a straight trajectory and then suddenly accelerating before disappearing. The observation of equally spaced, yellow-orange lights crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from East to West, which is consistent with the South wind shifting to West reported in Marignane (see the Météociel observation report for the day of observation). The perception of acceleration and disappearance is probably the witness's interpretation of the rapid dimming before the lantern's burner went out. The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", a probable observation of Thai lanterns.
