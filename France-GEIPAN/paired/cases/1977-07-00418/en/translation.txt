Summary: Observations of three stationary and bright objects.
--- seg-c0865738a824ce4dcc3c9afe [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 27, 1977, at 3:55 AM, members of the same family observed three objects hovering beneath a cloudy ceiling for one to two minutes. They described the objects as having a pyramid-like shape with a rounded top. They emitted a white light with varying intensity. The objects disappeared silently and in a matter of seconds around 4 AM, only to reappear around 4:20 AM. They then disappeared permanently into the clouds. No investigation by the gendarmerie was conducted, and no other testimonies were collected.
