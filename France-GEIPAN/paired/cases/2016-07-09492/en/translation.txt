Summary: Observation of the rapid movement of a spherical object surrounded by a ring and reflecting the sun: misidentification with a commercial airliner.
--- seg-39df528b3d932f128c129c2d [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 26, 2016, at approximately 7:14 PM, a witness observing from their outdoor terrace noticed the rapid and silent passage of a spherical object surrounded by a ring moving from North-North-East. The object was reflecting sunlight. No trail was observed. The witness was surprised as they did not recognize any known aircraft type (plane, weather balloon...). Following on-site measurements, interviews with the witness, and investigations using Flightradar replays and weather archives, it can be concluded that the observation was a confusion with an airplane (see investigation report). Indeed, there was a simultaneous (within a minute) appearance of an airplane, unseen by the witness at the time of the observation, following a trajectory consistent with the path described by the witness for the PAN, except for a significant difference in angular height (plane at 56°, PAN estimated at 32°) but not conclusive given the lack of reference points during the observation in open sky. Airplanes do not always leave trails. The other peculiar aspect regarding the spherical shape and "ring" can be explained by a non-uniform mirror effect of sunlight along the aircraft's body, and by a partial view of the wings due to the geometric configuration (and a less favorable mirror effect on the wings). This peculiarity may also have led to an increased perception of size compared to what the witness typically observes when airplanes pass by. Consequently, the GEIPAN is classified as A: Misidentification with a commercial airliner.
