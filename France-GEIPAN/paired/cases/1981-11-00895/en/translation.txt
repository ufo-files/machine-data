Description: On November 4, 1981, roofing workers observed around 10 a.m. at high altitude a round flying object moving slowly. Visible for about ten minutes, the witnesses could see several colors or lights that seemed to blink or appear as the craft performed rotations on itself. The sky was clear, allowing for good visibility. The craft disappeared into the landscape. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon, which cannot be explained due to a lack of data.
