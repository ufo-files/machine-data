Description: On July 25, 1979, around 9:30 PM, two witnesses observed in the sky an orange-colored bright point that moved and then seemed to stop. The brightness of this small point suddenly declined significantly. It disappeared instantly, leaving only a white trail. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon, for which we lack information.
