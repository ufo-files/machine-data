Description: On December 24, 2015, at 11:48 PM, a witness observed in the sky the silent movement of a bright red point surrounded by a red halo, following a straight trajectory beneath the cloud mass. The witness took two pictures. When the object flew overhead, he estimated its approximate size to be that of a car. The phenomenon disappeared behind trees after traversing the sky in two minutes. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of an orange ball crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a festive night, strongly suggests a Thai lantern, likely released nearby for Christmas. The movement was observed from southwest to northeast, which is consistent with the southwest wind recorded in Asnières-sur-Oise (Weather records: light southwest wind consistent with the movement. Weather in Asnières-sur-Oise and a strong southwest wind recorded in Creil, consistent with the movement. WeatherMap: wind in Creil. Strong southeast wind recorded in Roissy, inconsistent with the movement. WeatherMap Roissy). The photographs are consistent with this hypothesis: characteristic "fire" color. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of a Thai lantern. The witness, informed of the Thai lantern hypothesis, does not accept it. However, the observation report fully aligns with the observation of a Thai lantern: horizontal and straight movement of a bright point with a red or orange halo, in a direction identical to the local wind, on a festive evening. As is often the case in such reports, the witness overestimates the distance and speed of the phenomenon due to the low intrinsic luminosity of lantern burners compared to airplane lights.
