Description: On September 17, 2012, at 2:20 PM, a witness noticed in the sky a black ball that seemed stationary. The witness observed this black object in the sky, appearing immobile, at a low altitude on the horizon. The witness turned around it a quarter turn, which proves the low distance and altitude of the object. The north wind of 10 to 20 km/h (see record) seems weak for a kite to fly. The spherical shape rather suggests a balloon. The overcast cloud cover seems unsuitable for a thermal balloon (heated by the sun). It is probably a festive balloon inflated with helium, held by a string (of course invisible at more than 100 meters) or flying in the weak wind of that day. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a balloon.
