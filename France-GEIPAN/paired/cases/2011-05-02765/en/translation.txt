Description: A witness reports his observation made with binoculars on May 29, 2011, between 10:41 PM and 10:45 PM. He observes the slow, random East-South movement of about one hundred white light points. Given the low movement of the whole group, without cohesion, correlating with the low wind at that time, the GEIPAN hypothesizes that these white points are balloons released for a party, dimly lit by city lights (no moon visible at that time). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of balloons.
