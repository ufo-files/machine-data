Description: On May 11, 2025 at 10:45 AM, a witness observed a PAN from their home in Saint-Genis-Pouilly (01). In a completely clear sky, to the west and at a 45° elevation, he spotted a white sphere with a hazy texture, roughly the size of Venus. The object moves slowly: it first advances towards the witness, veers north, then heads west before disappearing behind the top of the trees after approximately 4 minutes of observation. The case consistency is good: despite the presence of a single witness, who was able to film the PAN - video used for the investigation (see the investigation report). *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The analysis of this video has revealed the following:

- The presence of a lower appendage, visible in the unfocused portions of the video.
- That the elements contributing to the witness's perception of oddness are mostly artifacts induced by the filming conditions:
  - Use of the maximum digital zoom.
  - Difficulty maintaining stability without a tripod.
  - Universal defocusing that makes the PAN more or less blurry.
  - Additional enlargement performed after the fact on a computer.
- That the speed of movement of the PAN in the second part of the trajectory is consistent with that of a light wind, as indicated in the modeled AROME* data from Météo France, for dimension estimates compatible with those of a fantasy balloon or a weather balloon.

*Application of Research to Operations at Mesoscale (AROME) is a model with a very fine mesh (1.3 km mesh) for forecasting in France (METEO France). The hypothesis explored, that of the observation of a weather balloon or a fantasy balloon, is supported by the following elements:

- Round shape and presence of a "appendage" below, similar to a cable holding the instrument chain carried by the weather balloon or simply a string for a balloon.
- White color, conform.
- Movement and speed conform with the present wind. The type of balloon could not be determined. Despite intensive investigations, the origin of the release (France or Switzerland) remains undetermined. If it is a weather balloon, an amateur release is possible, as transponder signaling is not mandatory. The GEIPAN case is classified as "B": probable observation of a wind-carried balloon. *AROME: Application of Research to Operations at Mesoscale) is a model with a very fine grid (1.3 km grid) for forecasting in France (METEO France).
