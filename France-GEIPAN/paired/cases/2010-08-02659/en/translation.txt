--- seg-3b1232bde0fb3e18fa0e5303 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 28, 2010, around 10:20 PM, a driver and his passenger spotted eight orange orbs moving across the sky in a quincunx formation at an altitude of approximately 500 meters and following a north-south-east trajectory. The phenomenon lasted about 4 minutes; no sounds or smells were detected. Following the receipt of the gendarmerie report on 10/14/2010, the weather conditions established for the location, date, and time of the observation show a wind coming from the northwest, consistent with the direction of movement from north to south-east of lighter-than-air objects like balloons or flying lanterns ("8 orange orbs"). The description of "8 orange orbs" moving according to "a regular trajectory" together at low altitude (500 meters), during the day (Saturday evening, wedding, party…) around 10:30 PM local time slightly increases the credibility of observing a probable passage of Thai lanterns.
