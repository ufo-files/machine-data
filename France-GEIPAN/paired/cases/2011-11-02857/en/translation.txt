Description: On November 20, 2011, at 3:20 PM, a witness observed in the clear sky a fast-moving white spherical object that appeared to "race past a commercial airliner." The witness watched the object until it disappeared on the horizon 2 to 3 minutes later. No other witnesses came forward. Radar tracks requested from air traffic control only mentioned the commercial aircraft reported by the witness. We investigated whether the witness might have observed a bright flash from a high-intensity Iridium satellite, which can sometimes be seen during the day. However, no such phenomenon occurred on the afternoon of November 20, 2011, in Ourouer-les-Bourdelins. No other usual misconceptions seem to fit the observation. Due to the lack of information regarding the path of the PAN, this observation is of low strangeness and consistency, and it can only be concluded as "C" (uncorroborated) due to the lack of independent testimony and cross-references.
