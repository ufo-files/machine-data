--- seg-bf21406744bcea1f85ca0906 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Friday, July 26, 2013, around 11 PM, a witness observed a orange ball to the South that disappeared to the East, then made another similar observation 15 minutes later. The witness points out the Moon visible in the background of the photo: however, at 23:00, the Moon has not yet risen, moreover, it is only three-quarters full (rising in the East around 23:15). The gray circle in the photo is more likely the reflection of the camera flash on a dust particle. The photos provided to GEIPAN are not in the format of the original photos. The wind raised by Meteociel in Cambrai is very weak, oscillating around the North direction during the evening. The witness's description is consistent with the observation of two Thai lanterns released less than one kilometer south of the witness: the very light wind would have barely moved the lantern, which would have gone out after a few minutes.
