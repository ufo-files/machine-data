Description: On November 23, 2013, at 6:45 PM, an individual observed for 15 seconds the silent, straight-line passage across a cloudy sky of a very bright, round object of yellow-orange color. Traveling on a west/southwest trajectory, the phenomenon was quickly obscured by the angle of a nearby house. The observation of a yellow-orange ball moving horizontally on a Saturday evening suggests a Thai lantern released at the start of a festival. However, the witness was in a sparsely populated residential area, and there is no visible point of origin for the lantern, carried by the northwest wind. Weather records in Rodez confirm a strong northwest wind and cloudy skies. (see: weather. Given the briefness of the observation (15 seconds), it is difficult to confirm or deny this hypothesis. No other testimonies were collected. It is also possible to consider a ball lightning phenomenon, though very unlikely at this time of year. Notably, the weather on that day was highly disturbed. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of information.
