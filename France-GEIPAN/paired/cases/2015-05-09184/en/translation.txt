Description: On May 25, 2015, at 7:58 PM, a witness observed in the sky a dark phenomenon composed of 6 lights arranged in a circle. Only one testimony was collected. This observation report is particularly concise, the geographical references are imprecise, the narrative is very brief, and there are no drawings or photos. The circular shape described by the witness may not be real; it could merely be the abstract outline of the 6 observed lights. These lights could belong to an airplane or a helicopter (a heliport is very close). The GEIPAN did not process this case in time to obtain verification of air traffic at that moment. Due to a lack of specifics in the testimony and air traffic, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of information and specifics.
