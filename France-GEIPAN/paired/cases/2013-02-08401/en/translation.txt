Summary: Observation of the movement in the sky of a luminous object that takes, during a change in direction, a cylindrical shape of black color: observation during a flight from Marseille to Bastia on final approach for landing.
--- seg-fe53303b60ec4eb931afc7e1 [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 7, 2013, around 9:45 PM, a witness and his wife observed the slow movement of an object that intrigued them. The witness's description has all the characteristics of an aircraft on final approach: a slow-moving bright shape, then a black cylindrical shape. The time he mentions, 9:45 PM, coincides with the arrival of Air Corsica Flight Marseille-Bastia, which departs Marseille at 9:05 PM and is expected to land in Bastia at 9:55 PM; this could correspond to what he observed. The Bastia Poretta Airport is located 3 or 4 kilometers as the crow flies from the observation location, to the northwest. See the attached approach trajectories for the Bastia airport: in red is the path used with a southwesterly wind, as was the case that day (see weather report). What is surprising is the witness's surprise, who states the phenomenon was two times lower than usual aircraft; note that the witness was not at his home, giving him a different perspective from his usual viewpoint. It is also possible that the pilot used a lower approach altitude that day for reasons specific to him. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the Marseille-Bastia flight on final approach for landing.
