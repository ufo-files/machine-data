Description: On February 26, 1978, around 2:30 AM, several people observed a silver and bright shape in the sky. Witnesses describe a bright explosion. This phenomenon disappeared instantly, without any sound. Gendarmerie patrols observed that night lightning from distant thunderstorms. These witnesses likely observed a lighting phenomenon from this storm passage.
