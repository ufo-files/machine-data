--- seg-c1b650496f4843ed779decc7 [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 25, 2009, between 8:43 and 8:44 a.m., two hikers observed the low-altitude movement of a large white sphere that disappeared behind the mountain range of the Dent de Rez. The witness, in a letter dated April 3, 2009, specifies that what he photographed was indeed a signaling sphere, but that he saw something else: this could be Venus, which was near the eastern horizon that day. A precise measurement of the movement through alignment with fixed objects would have allowed this doubt to be resolved. However, since the observation point is not known precisely, this hypothesis cannot be confirmed.
