Description: On December 17, 1957, four military personnel at the Cognac Air Base observed a radar echo at 15:01 (UTC) from an object moving from 40 km away and disappearing at 16 km in the fixed echoes. The total observation time on the radar is 30 seconds, which corresponds to a speed of 2,880 km/h. On the same day but at 15:45 (TU), another radar echo is noticed at 37 km and disappears at 25 km. The total observation time lasts 15 seconds, which corresponds to a speed of 2,880 km/h as well. An illustrated report with sketches is drafted by two witnesses. THE GEIPAN classifies this case as C due to a lack of information (no visual observation).
