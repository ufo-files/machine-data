--- seg-4ee6f981429f9e0361d9c675 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 17, 1957, four military personnel at the Cognac Air Base observed a radar echo at 15:01 (UTC) from an object moving at 40 kilometers per hour, disappearing at 16 kilometers in the fixed echoes. The total radar observation time was 30 seconds, corresponding to a speed of 2,880 kilometers per hour. Later that same day, at 15:45 (UTC), another radar echo was noticed at 37 kilometers, disappearing at 25 kilometers. The total observation time was 15 seconds, also corresponding to a speed of 2,880 kilometers per hour. An illustrated report with sketches was drafted by two witnesses. The GEIPAN classified this case as C due to lack of information (no visual observation).
