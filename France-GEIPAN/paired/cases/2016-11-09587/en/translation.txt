Summary: Long observations of a green streak in the cloudy night sky: laser observation.
--- seg-8b68d60deb32d544b1737f0a [needs-review] ---
Description: On Thursday, November 17, 2016, around 10 PM, two people were intrigued by the presence in the cloudy sky of a green trail. One of the witnesses takes several photographs. Two testimonies are collected. The green streak in the sky results from laser spots from the Toulouse event carried out by international artist Yvette Mattern. The giant laser show installed on the roof of the Quai des Savoirs, along the Jules-Guesde alleys, illuminated from November 18 to 20, 2016, but testing began much earlier. The actu.cotetoulouse site reports witnesses surprised on the evening of November 16 with descriptions and geographical distances to the event similar to those of the witness on November 17. It was verified with the organizer that the tests were also present as of November 15. This laser was also observed by a witness who testified at GEIPAN (case LAPEYROUSE-FOSSAT (31) 15.11.2016). The present testimony and attached photos show an identical aspect of the phenomenon, in particular with this green light trait. The phenomenon's azimuth in the sky (134°) as reported by witnesses is consistent with a laser shot from the Quai des savoirs in Toulouse (located 25km at 155°) considering the imprecision of a smartphone compass and the fact that laser impact on clouds can be more than 10km from the shooting location. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as A: laser.
