Description: On Wednesday, February 17, 2016, around 6:20 PM, a witness at their home saw through the window the northward movement of a bright point on a descending trajectory. The observation lasted 3 to 5 seconds. Only one testimony was collected. This was a meteor seen by numerous witnesses at the same time and in southern France, and listed by the International Meteor Organization (IMO). See the IMO site which lists over 120 testimonies from various regions in France. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A: Meteor Observation.
