Description: On May 17, 2010, around 9:45 PM, a couple observed in the sky the slow fall of a burning triangular object. The main witness thought it was the fall of a burning ULM (Ultra Light Motorized Aircraft). From this object, another detached forming a torch. No other testimony was collected. Given the extent of the gendarmerie's investigations, the hypothesis of ULM fall can be ruled out. One can then think of a misidentification with a piece of space debris (the aspect and duration of the observation might correspond). A reported debris re-entered the atmosphere that day (29569 DELTA 4 DEB), but its orbit inclination of 98° does not match the observation (towards E-NE); this hypothesis cannot be confirmed. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C": lack of information to conclude.
