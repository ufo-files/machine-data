Description: A witness reports sightings of white balls that he claims to have seen on three occasions since the beginning of 1980. Each time, these balls seemed to follow a vehicle driving at night. They could leave a white trail upon disappearing. In the presence of a friend, they both observed a ball near a residential house. The witness also describes his encounter, while in his vehicle, with an entity that allegedly contacted him three times and warned him of the invasion of Earth. He describes a tall and handsome man with blue eyes and long hair. The witness mentions a half-hour memory loss and arrived at his parents' home in a state of shock. Taken back to his home, his condition was noted the following day by a doctor. No other witnesses were found, and no additional information was gathered about these phenomena that would support this testimony.
