Description: On June 6, 2015, at 12:14 AM, two witnesses were intrigued by the presence in the sky of an orange-colored mass above the city and an orange geometric shape, but lighter inside the mass. The phenomenon moved away very quickly on a straight trajectory. No noise was heard. The witnesses themselves mentioned the possibility of a cloud. The objections they raised against this hypothesis could be related to the orange color, the final geometric shape, or even the final rapid movement. As for the color, it is not unusual for T2, and we can add the following: a low cloud over a city acts as a projection screen for the city lights: it thus reflects the orange of the streetlights, which can be very powerful if, for example, there is a large stadium or a major intersection very well lit. If searchlights illuminate the sky, the cloud will project the image of their beams. That evening, there was a cloudy sky in Lyon-Bron, and a south to southwest wind according to Meteociel: the observed movement of the cloud is consistent with the direction of the wind. That evening, there was an Ethnotek event at the Gerland Park, in a direction of observation quite close to the phenomenon: the light effects of the shows may have illuminated the sky in a particular way. As for the final shape, it is common to observe rapid shape changes in clouds. Any type of shape is possible, and of course, some are more evocative than others, particularly geometric shapes. The final rapid movement is only reported by T1, without explicitly adding an additional objection to the cloud hypothesis. Without objective indications of speed perception, nothing seems incompatible with the hypothesis of a moving cloud, especially in the presence of ground wind and which can be even stronger at altitude. In conclusion, the GEIPAN concludes that the observation of a cloud is probable and classifies the case as "B": cloud.
