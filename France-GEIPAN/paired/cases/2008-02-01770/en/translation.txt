Description: On February 1, 2008, around 6:30 PM, a witness observed in the clear sky a white streak at approximately 30° above the horizon in the south-southeast direction. A few seconds later, the witness lost sight of the white streak but noticed smoke that gradually dissipated. Local press reported similar observations on that day. This person likely observed an atmospheric reentry.
