Description: Between July 26th and 29th, 1976, at different times, several witnesses observed luminous and electrical phenomena that intrigued them. One witness reported the rapid passage of a flickering green ball in the sky, leaving a red trail. TV reception disruptions and an electric meter fire were noted during these days. A gendarmerie investigation reported multiple, differing witness testimonies. This case is classified C due to a lack of consistency and information in the testimonies.
