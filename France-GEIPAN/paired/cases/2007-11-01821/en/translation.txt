Summary: Yellow light falling vertically from the sky: probable observation of a bolide.
--- seg-0c87aa78990e0b24a0753667 [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reports an unusual observation in March 2007 (between November 22 and 28, the exact date is unclear), while driving on a departmental road between 8:00 PM and 10:00 PM, along with his passenger. They suddenly observed a bright phenomenon in the middle of the sky that appeared to "dive towards the ground." The observation lasted 1 to 2 seconds, during which they could distinguish a yellow light in the shape of a drop moving silently. The object quickly disappeared over the horizon. Based on the description of the phenomenon (trajectory and duration), these individuals witnessed the re-entry of a meteoroid into the atmosphere. The "water drop" shape is likely due to the presence of a wake trailing behind the object.
