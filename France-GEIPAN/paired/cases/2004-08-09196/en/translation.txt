Description: Recent testimony (May 2015) of an old observation. The witness reports an observation made in July or August 2004 around 1:40 PM on D6009 after Prat-de-Cest (11) in the municipality of Peyriac-de-Mer. While driving, the witness sees a circular, yellow object approach the left corner of their windshield at low altitude. The driver stops and observes a yellow disk crossing D6009 and moving towards A9, less than one meter above the green vines without disturbing them. Crossing the highway, the object ascends vertically before resuming a horizontal trajectory to pass over the cars and then overfly a bank. The witness loses sight of the object as it is hidden by the highway bank. The object appears to be coming from the Doule ponds to the east and heading west. No unusual noises were noted during the observation. No other testimonies were collected. This observation is of medium to high strangeness, as it describes a phenomenon close to a known event and exhibits atypical behavior (see investigation notes). The consistency of this case is not high: it involves a single witness, old memories, and the precise date is unknown, making it impossible to verify meteorological conditions, for example. The only considered hypothesis, a ball lightning, is not sufficiently supported to be retained. Eventually, this case is classified as C due to the lack of date information and cross-references.
