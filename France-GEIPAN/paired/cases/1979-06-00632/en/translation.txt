Description: On June 10, 1979, between 3:30 and 4:00 a.m. and after a thunderstorm, a witness sees a strange light through a window that intrigues them. They observe a powerful light in the southwest direction above the horizon. This light moves slowly horizontally with occasional vertical up-and-down motions. Depending on the light's position, the witness sees glowing circles. A second flickering light from the north appears, passing over the first. It quickly disappears in a southerly direction. At 4:00 a.m., with the light still present, the witness went to bed. No sounds were heard, and no other witnesses were found for this phenomenon, about which we have limited information.
