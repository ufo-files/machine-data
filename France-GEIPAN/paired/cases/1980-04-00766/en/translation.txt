Description: On April 21, 1980, at 11:55 AM, a witness heard a whistle and was frightened by a fireball and the fall a few meters from him of an object coming from the northwest. He heard no impact noise and saw no smoke. This gardener described the object as being the size of a bucket, with a diameter of 20 cm, and the colors of the rainbow. The gendarmes called to the scene found ground traces: on a circle of 100 meters in diameter, the ground was covered with a white powdery substance and the grass was burned. No impact crater-like marks were present on the ground. The next day, a metallic piece was found. No record of analysis results was found in the GEIPAN archives, leading to a classification C while waiting to recover these pieces.
