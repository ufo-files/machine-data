Description: On July 1, 2017 at 10:14 PM, several witnesses observed an object leaving orange streaks in the clear sky. The witness films his observation. He is then surprised to see other objects leaving orange trails behind them. The trajectories are different. No noise was heard during the observation. The witness thinks of space debris entering the atmosphere. Only one testimony is collected. The video unequivocally shows an airplane trail. What surprises the witness the most is the short, orange trail. It is orange because it is illuminated by the setting sun (which still illuminates at the altitude of the plane), the length and persistence depend on many factors that can also cause the trail to not exist at all. To a lesser degree, he is amazed by the speed, five times faster than a commercial jet. A witness can only speak of angular velocity, and this varies greatly: it is stronger vertically than horizontally, at low flight altitude than at high altitude. The video does not give the impression of exceptional speed. It is verified that many commercial airplanes are flying in this sky at this time. The GEIPAN will not lead to further investigation since the object appears to be a plane. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: airplane.
