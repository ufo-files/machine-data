Summary: Observation of a saucer-shaped craft.
--- seg-bbc2145bcc70d861a204dcc5 [needs-review] ---
Description: A person testifies by letter that on the morning of July 20, 1977, around 1 a.m., while driving with their daughter, they observed for a few seconds an object in the shape of a saucer. Surrounded by a slightly yellow halo, the object moved slowly in the sky, spinning on itself, and appeared to be descending towards the car. However, as it gained altitude, the object disappeared towards Bordeaux. Several people will be interviewed, but no other testimony will be collected to confirm or deny the statements of this witness.
