Description: On October 11, 1992, between 9:30 PM and 10:30 PM, two independent witnesses observed a white, oval-shaped object moving slowly over Marseille. Both witnesses took the precaution of filming their observation. The investigations by the two brigades quickly revealed that the witnesses had observed from a distance a dirigible based in Marignagne and conducting promotional night flights. The flight data are perfectly consistent with the witnesses' description.
