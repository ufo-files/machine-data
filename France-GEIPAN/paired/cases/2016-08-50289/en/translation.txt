Summary: Nighttime observation of the silent passage of a bright white PAN in the sky: probable misidentification with a Thai lantern.
--- seg-4a216aea6000854d4ad89dbe [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 8, 2016, the witness, with three friends, decided to observe the stars from a fallow field. The witness, turning their head, discovered in the sky the presence of a bright white light object silently following a straight trajectory. The brightness quickly faded and the PAN disappeared. The observation lasted about ten seconds. Only one testimony was collected. Of moderate strangeness and weak consistency (multiple witnesses, but only one left a testimony, no photos), the case appears to be a probable misidentification with a Thai lantern (see the investigation report). The description of the PAN indeed presents many characteristics of this type of misidentification: silent movement of a very bright, white light point in the direction of the wind, and presence of a weak red light at the end of the observation compatible with the extinction of a Thai lantern. It should be noted that the witness considers this hypothesis unconvincing, given the absence of a wind sensation at ground level. However, the absence of wind at ground level does not mean the absence of wind at altitude, which was confirmed by the weather investigation. It was not possible to formally identify the launch site. The case is classified B: probable misidentification with a Thai lantern.
