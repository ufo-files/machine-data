Summary: Observation of glowing spheres spinning in the sky: probable laser effects.
--- seg-dc540d569e652d61101cc2d0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 27, 1997, around 11:00 PM, a witness observed six glowing orbs spinning in the sky. After some time, five of the orbs disappeared, leaving a large milky sphere (three times the size of the sunset) which the witness watched for approximately half an hour. The witness called his mother, who only observed the large white sphere. Although the investigation did not prove it, the described observation resembles the effects produced on clouds by advertising laser projectors.
