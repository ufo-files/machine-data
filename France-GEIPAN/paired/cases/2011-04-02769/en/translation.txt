Summary: Observation of a straight-line passage across the sky of a very bright blue ball that changed color before disappearing: likely observation of a meteoroid atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-15bc3447dfad0215d3a8d457 [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 5, 2011, at 5:21 a.m., a driver had his attention drawn by a light approaching directly from the sky. He described a very bright, blue ball that quickly crossed the sky on a straight trajectory. The phenomenon then changed color to orange and seemed to stop before disappearing. The front passenger made the same observation. Only one testimony was collected. This case of weak strangeness and low to moderate consistency appears to have all the characteristics of an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid. Classification A is, however, excluded due to the lack of independent sources. This case is classified B as probable bolide: atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
