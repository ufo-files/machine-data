Description: Recent testimony (10/14/2015) of an old observation. On Sunday, April 7, 2013, at 5:10 a.m., a witness observed through his window several orange balls silently moving away from his building towards the sea. The lights seem incandescent, about the size of a basketball. They do not flicker. He takes a video and then sees five new lights following the same trajectory. Eight orange balls will be observed. No other testimony will be collected. The observation of pink orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, probably released nearby after a private party. The movement is observed from East to West, which is consistent with the 7-11 km/h East wind reported in Le Havre (see the Météociel record for the day of observation). The witness's video confirms this hypothesis without any doubt (especially around the 3rd minute). The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of Thai lanterns.
