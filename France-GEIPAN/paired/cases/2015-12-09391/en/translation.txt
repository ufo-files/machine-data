--- seg-d3b8e268681508df29b3e1a5 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 24, 2015, at 6:10 PM, three people observed for 45 seconds from an apartment on the 10th floor a silent passing of a luminous object with changing colors (green, blue, orange). The object disappeared behind the building, passing over the witnesses. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of a multicolored flying object at low altitude on a holiday evening suggests a "lighter than air" object carried by the wind. The Orly Météociel report indicates a southwest wind, consistent with the object's movement (see the Météociel report). The multiple and changing colors point to a LED-lit balloon of a model similar to those described here. This type of festive balloon is becoming increasingly popular. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," a probable observation of a LED festive balloon.
