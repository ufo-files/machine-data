Description: On August 29, 2015 at 23:02, a couple observed the silent passage of a red ball in the night sky for 5 minutes. (Unidentified Flying Object [UFO] observed La photographie prise intrigue les témoins (voir image2.jpg). Only one testimony was collected. The witness first saw a PAN in the sky. The description (in particular the color, the occurrence on a weekend night, and the wind direction with a strong "towards Switzerland" component) are highly compatible elements of a Thai lantern. The witness wanted to photograph the PAN. But the motivation for his testimony comes from what he believes he detects in the photo of the figures inside the PAN ("It's clearly visible that there are figures inside," from the questionnaire). The analyse de la photographie (voir Reconstitution PAN 2 publication.pdf) indicates, in addition to an obvious movement structure, that the photo was not taken towards the sky, but rather towards a square. The origin of this visual deviation remains unknown. One can recognize, by comparing with the day photo of the square and despite the movements: - the lights of two streetlamps, one of which inspired the witness with a PAN - the playground structure illuminated by these streetlamps - and perhaps even the balcony railing where the photo was taken. A geometric reconstruction shows that from the probable location of the sighting (based on the exact point on the balcony), the respective alignment of street lights and playground structures is observed. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as A: misinterpretation of a photograph by the witness.
