Summary: Observations by two individuals of the silent passage of an orange-colored flying object: likely observation of a Thai lantern.
--- seg-b72463d4b6b8fd67149ebee5 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Two people in their kitchen observed, during two minutes around 2:40 AM on February 28, 2010, the silent passage of a bright orange shimmering sphere without flickering. The object, at low altitude, moved on a straight trajectory before disappearing vertically. This observation case raises no doubt about the authenticity of the testimonies, although they were initially different during the investigation. The witnesses' account strongly suggests the passage of a Thai lantern. The impression of disappearing upwards is quite common when the lantern's flame goes out. The estimated direction of the object (NNE) is not entirely consistent with the wind direction (towards NNO), but on the one hand, the wind at ground level in Rivesaltes is not the same as at 200 or 300 meters altitude in Bages, and on the other hand, the witnesses' direction estimation is necessarily imprecise. The day and time (Saturday night to Sunday morning) are quite common for lantern releases, which are fairly widespread in the region. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
