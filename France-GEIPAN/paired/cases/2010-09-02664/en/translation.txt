--- seg-484bc466ff1f9196755d3429 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 19, 2010, at 7:45 PM, a witness observed from their home the presence and silent O-E (east-west) movement of a round, black flying object. The witness took photographs of the object. No other testimonies were collected. The photographs of the object undeniably resemble a helium-filled party balloon adorned with ribbons, or a somewhat complex shape. Although made from reflective materials such as Mylar, the dark color is explained by the fact that the pictures were taken into the setting sun, facing west-southwest. The object's slow and straight movement is consistent with the strong wind and is very likely from the multiple sales points scattered around Paris's tourist sites. With low strangeness and average consistency, this observation case very likely resulted from a misidentification of a helium-filled party balloon that had escaped its owner's control. This case is classified as B.
