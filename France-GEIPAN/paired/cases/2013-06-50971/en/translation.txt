Description: On Sunday, June 2, 2013 at 10:59 PM, a witness is intrigued by the silent passage across the sky of an orange ball. He takes a photograph. The description of an orange ball moving in a straight line at the end of the weekend evening evokes a Thai lantern. The witness's photos are perfectly consistent with this hypothesis. Weather conditions indicate a light north wind (meteo). The witness reports the object's movement from northeast to southwest: this change in direction can be explained by local geographic conditions. The witness, contacted by phone, has seen Thai lanterns on July 14th and recognized the same type of object. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the passage of a Thai lantern.
