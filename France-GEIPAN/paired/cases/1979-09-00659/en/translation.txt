Description: On September 19, 1979, at 10:00 PM, during their jog, two witnesses observed a red, ball-shaped glow in the sky that twinkled like a star. Suddenly, this glow moved at a dizzying speed to approach the witnesses. No noise, air movement, or heat was noticed. After describing a curve, the glow stopped for 3 to 4 seconds at about 200 meters from the witnesses, above the edge of the forest. A dark mass appeared inside the glow. The phenomenon disappeared abruptly. No other testimonies or evidence were collected on this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
