--- seg-7a0fb0e4774d8ffdedc0d0d5 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Saturday, September 17, 2011, around 11 PM, a witness from their balcony was intrigued by the passage of an object they initially mistook for a burning plane heading towards Bordeaux-Mérignac Airport (from south to north) at an elevation of approximately 40°. The observation lasted about 1 minute and 15 seconds. This observation of red lights moving silently resembles several known phenomena, but certain characteristics do not fit: an atmospheric reentry of a satellite or space debris does not align with the momentary stop and change in trajectory observed; astronomical or atmospheric hypotheses also do not seem to fit; though the described colors (red, orange, yellow) evoke flames and the time and day are compatible with a festive event, the hypothesis of a Thai lantern (flying lantern) does not fit well: unsuitable shape, change in direction, change in appearance. The hypothesis of a motorized airship or dirigible illuminated for festive or commercial purposes seems compatible with the observation and cannot be entirely ruled out given that the observation occurred on a Saturday night. However, we have not found any record of such an event. This weak case (one witness only), and moderately strange case is classified C due to lack of independent testimonies and cross-references.
