Description: On June 28, 2009, at approximately 4:30 a.m., campers were intrigued by the movements of light phenomena in the sky. Only one report was sent to GEIPAN. The movements described by the objects are difficult to reconcile with typical sources of misperception, except for nightclub projectors, but these are mostly noticed on overcast nights rather than clear skies. In any case, it is rare for their operation to be limited to just a few dozen seconds. This observation case presents a high level of strangeness and would merit in-depth investigation to affirm its consistency. GEIPAN actively attempted to re-establish contact with the witness without success. This strange case remains inconsistent due to the uniqueness of the testimony and the absence of an investigation. This case is provisionally classified as C, awaiting hypothetical additional information.
