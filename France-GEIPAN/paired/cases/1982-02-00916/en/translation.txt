Description: On February 15, 1982, around 7 a.m., witnesses observed a peculiar glowing phenomenon in the sky approaching. One witness noted that flashing white lights emanated from each edge of a dark, hexagonal-shaped mass. No noise was heard during the object's movement. No other information was gathered on the observed phenomenon.
