Description: On the night of September 28, 1983, several witnesses, including police officers, observed a luminous object in the sky. Located between 2000 and 2500 meters altitude and drifting south, the object occasionally assumed a round or triangular shape. No pilot or radar trace was reported for this object, which was, however, photographed by the witnesses. No further information could be gathered about this observation.
