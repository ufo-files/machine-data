Description: Around July 26, 2012, at approximately 10 PM, two witnesses were intrigued by the observation of three lights (two white and one blue at the tip of a gray triangle passing over them). This observation of three bright points crossing the sky in a straight line is somewhat strange, as it could be attributed to the simultaneous passage of three satellites; however, the witnesses insist that they perceived a dark triangle between these points, making the phenomenon even stranger. The GEIPAN was unable to clearly correlate the observation with known satellites, despite considering two potential satellite candidates. The testimony is somewhat consistent since the two witnesses are not independent, and there is no photo or video evidence. The date of the observation remains slightly uncertain. Due to these uncertainties, this case is classified as 'C' for lack of information.
