Summary: Periodic observation for 1.5 hours of a spinning white light. Likely laser light from a disco.
--- seg-60c68957b0edcbb14b6bc2f4 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 10, 1993, around 0100 hours, a witness observed an amorphous light rotating in a clockwise direction with a faint humming sound. Intrigued, the witness went to find his friend, who was also observing the phenomenon, and then woke up his mother, who could also see it. Two other witnesses were located by the gendarmes but did not wish to give statements. The phenomenon recurred approximately every 20 minutes until around 0230 hours, at which time the witnesses decided to go to sleep. The phenomenon was not formally identified, but it is likely that it was the observation of a laser beam from a distant nightclub, approximately 10 kilometers away, given the cloud layer present that day and the line of sight.
