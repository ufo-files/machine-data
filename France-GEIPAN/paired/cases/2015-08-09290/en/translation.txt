Description: On August 11, 2015, around 10:05 PM, a witness on their terrace was intrigued by two lights of different colors at low altitude that they saw in the clear sky. The lights moved silently in a straight line, then accelerated, zigzagged, and resumed a straight trajectory before disappearing. The lights maintained the same spacing between them during their movement. The witness added to their report their GPS position, the exact azimuth of observation, and a annotated photograph depicting their observation. Only one report was received by GEIPAN.
