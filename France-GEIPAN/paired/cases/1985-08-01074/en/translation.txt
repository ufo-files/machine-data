Description: On August 1, 1985, around 11:15 PM, a witness observed in the sky a fixed, glowing, slightly reddish point, like a star. This point then moved vertically above the village, changing in color and light intensity, taking the shape of a "delta" and moving horizontally at increasing speed. The witness noted that a few seconds after its vertical movement, a deep noise was heard, and as the object moved away, it returned to a glowing point. No other observations of this phenomenon could be gathered.
