Description: On April 8, 2015, around 7:15 PM, a witness seated on a couch beneath a skylight observed the passage of a bright white flying object with no visible logo. The object, with V-shaped wings, made a curve and then ascended vertically at great speed, transforming into a white streak in the clear sky. It then disappeared from the witness's view. The observation lasted approximately one minute, allowing the witness to sketch a diagram of the observed object. The witness's description, the shape, and the behavior all suggest a radio-controlled model aircraft, of the flying wing type (see a video of a model of this type, with a shape matching the witness's description, showcasing impressive flight capabilities). The witness resides less than 150 meters from a sports area, which is conducive to model aircraft flying and within radio range, and with good visibility. Wednesday afternoons are generally favorable times. Unable to verify this information, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a radio-controlled model aircraft.
