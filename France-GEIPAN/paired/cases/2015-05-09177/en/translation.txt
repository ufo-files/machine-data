Description: On the night of Saturday to Sunday, May 17, 2015, at midnight forty-five, a witness closing a window was surprised to observe 5 or 6 balls of light of color silently passing through the clear sky. These lights seem to delineate a dark craft. The observation lasted for a very short time and the lights suddenly went out. The witness describes an "gray and metallic" craft equipped with "6 large balls of light in colors." If one admits that the witness may have been a victim of the classic shape illusion (see the article on the psychology of shape), it is possible that the only real lights balls are, but not the gray craft. The observation of glowing balls crossing the sky in a straight line on a weekend night suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The disappearance of the orbs could correspond to their extinction, or their passage behind a building. Since the observation is very brief, and the witness does not describe the direction of movement, it is not possible to verify if the movement is consistent with the wind. It would be surprising if this were a drone, very difficult to pilot at night without risk in an urban environment; moreover, drones generally have non-linear trajectories. The GEIPAN did not receive any other testimony that could correspond to the same phenomenon. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of precise information and corroboration.
