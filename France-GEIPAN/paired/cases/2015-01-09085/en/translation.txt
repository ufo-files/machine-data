Summary: Long observation of white lights in the sky: observation of Venus and Mercury at their setting.
--- seg-436193d134f84678758d9511 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 14, 2015, at 6:25 PM, a witness observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky, composed of several white lights, without trail or halo. No sound accompanied the phenomenon. Only one testimony was collected. This observation of several minutes of two low lights on the horizon resembles stars, close to their setting: the sky map shows that in the West-South-West direction, that is, the direction of the dune du Pilat, south of Arcachon, the planets Venus and Mercury, rarely so close and so bright, were well visible at 8° above the horizon (see the sky map under Stellarium). The photo shows a shot taken at approximately 45° south from the axis of the allee du plaçot, which is oriented west. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": observation of Venus and Mercury at their setting. Since the direction of observation indicated by the witness is not very precise, this hypothesis is not confirmed.
