--- seg-63e5388a850c222a87203127 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 08, 2009, at 21:14, a witness observed in the sky a bright, unblinking light moving at a high speed from West to East on a straight trajectory. This testimony is very unremarkable and the brief description inadequate for serious study. It is very likely that this was the passage of the International Space Station (ISS) moving from West to East above the southern horizon from 21:07 to 21:14 (see Calsky log). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": observation of the International Space Station (ISS).
