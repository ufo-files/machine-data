Description: On Saturday, June 19, 2010, around 11:30 PM, a witness near a village party was intrigued by a varying intensity red light and a white-lit object that the witness did not recognize. The observation lasted between 5 to 10 minutes. The witness initially thought it was one or more helicopters, as the observed lights remained static throughout, but the absence of noise and the slow, atypical blinking of the lights do not align with this hypothesis. A tethered balloon with lights could also explain the observation, especially if it was part of the fireworks display also observed. Unfortunately, we were unable to verify this second hypothesis. Some balloons at altitude, illuminated by the last rays of a sun setting over an hour ago, could have presented a similar spectacle. With an average strangeness due to the static nature and light pulsing rhythm of PAN, and good consistency thanks to a precise description from the witness, this observation case unfortunately cannot be firmly concluded without additional investigations. This case is classified C due to lack of independent testimonies and cross-references.
