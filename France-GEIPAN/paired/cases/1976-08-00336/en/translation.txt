Description: On August 27, 1976, at approximately 9:16 PM, an amateur astronomer witness again observes an object in the cloudy sky that intrigues him. He has another person witness the passage of a circular craft emitting a substantially orange light at the rear. During the investigation, the witness provides a precise description, a diagram of the observed object, and the trajectory. No other testimony is collected. It is likely that this witness made successive observations of an aircraft.
