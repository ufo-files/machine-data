Summary: Observation of light movements in the cloudy night sky: very probable observation of a multi-beam light display.
--- seg-4cbafea7a60d72043544075b [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of June 8-9, 2013, around 1 a.m., a driver observes for several minutes the silent movements of bright, clear lights in the cloudy night sky. Only one testimony was collected. The description of the phenomenon clearly evokes a light display (laser?) frequently used by nightclubs to signal their presence. This phenomenon was also observed at the same location on the night of June 22-23 by another witness. Saturday and Sunday nights are, of course, favored for these displays. The cloudy weather (see the Météociel report) is conducive to a very strong effect of this type of projector. The witness's description suggests the effect produced by a multi-beam projector similar to that in this Nottingham Wedding searchlight hire video. The GEIPAN did not conduct identification investigations of the source of this display that night, but the nightclub "Le Coyote" in Saint-Elix-Le-Château often uses this device: it is very likely that it is the source. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": very probable observation of a light display of multi-beam projector type.
