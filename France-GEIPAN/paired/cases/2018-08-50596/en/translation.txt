Description: On August 23, 2018, early evening, a couple on their terrace observed a black object flying from the northwest towards the Défense area in the southeast. The PAN had a round shape and appeared to be at a considerable height, with witnesses unable to gauge its distance. It moved swiftly, taking just over a minute to reach the Défense area. The PAN flew silently, with a straight trajectory, occasionally appearing to spin without altering its path. Having recently been flown over by a drone twice, the witnesses compared the object to a PAN, but they believed it was different. The case consistency was good: two witnesses, but no photos of the PAN. The investigation concluded that it was very likely a balloon, as reported by the witnesses (see the investigation report). The description of the PAN closely resembles a balloon, with its elongated rectangular or round shape. The color of the PAN suggests it was black or dark, an impression heightened by the fact that the observation occurred at sunset, in backlighting. The brief glimpse of red light could correspond to a temporary reflection of the sunset's glow on the balloon's envelope. Additionally, the PAN's trajectory aligns well with the wind direction. The case is classified as A, very likely observation of a balloon.
