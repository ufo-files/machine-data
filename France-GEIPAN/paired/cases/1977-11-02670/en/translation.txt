Description: On November 19, 1977, at approximately 01:05, a motorist and his passenger observed a red-orange sphere in motion and approaching them. The driver stopped, and the phenomenon remained stationary in a field 1.5 meters above the ground and about 30 meters from the car. No sounds were heard. The frightened driver decided to continue on his way and change his route, but encountered the phenomenon several times during his journey. The phenomenon was observed for a quarter of an hour and over nine kilometers by other motorists before disappearing. No traces or clues were found at the various locations where the phenomenon was observed. No unusual events were reported by the observer at the Floirac Observatory. No military maneuvers were conducted that night. There was no intervention of any kind on the high-voltage line or transformers located near the roads taken by the driver. Nevertheless, it is likely that the observed manifestation could be attributed to an electrical phenomenon, as it was seen moving always in the vicinity of electrical conducting and transforming equipment.
