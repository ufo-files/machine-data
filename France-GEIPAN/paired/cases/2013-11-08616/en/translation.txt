--- seg-d28245565376bc3bf0c9f30f [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 28, 2013, at 12:10, a driver heard a blast and immediately observed in the sky above a field a large yellow fireball and, all around, black smoke. The phenomenon quickly disappeared. Another person, on a road adjacent to the one where the witness was, seemed to have looked in the same direction at that time. No other testimony was collected. The description of the observation, in particular the sequence (blast followed by a bright yellow flash accompanied by a black cloud), all within a short duration (<1 minute), is consistent with the launch of a detonating and/or illuminating rocket (see the video of a detonating rocket for a blank pistol). The origin of the launch cannot be determined, but it is possible that the driver who was on the parallel road to where the witness was (and who was also looking up) was responsible. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B: illuminating rocket.
