Summary: Successive observations of the passage of a satellite.
--- seg-f5d26765c8ec3a4950213275 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: For several consecutive evenings around 9 PM, multiple witnesses observe a bright object passing at an extremely high altitude and at a constant speed. It moves in a northeasterly to southwesterly direction. The regularity of the trajectory, the duration of visibility, and the repetition over two days confirm the hypothesis of observing the passage of an artificial satellite.
