Summary: Observation of the movement of unknown aircraft.
--- seg-c9aaa163d8771b75c248e110 [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reports seeing, in July of the years 1970 to 1973 (his memories regarding the observation date are imprecise, so we date it to 01/07/1970) around 21:00, an aircraft shaped like a saucer, white or aluminum in color, arriving at an altitude of 300 to 400 meters. It was between 6 to 8 meters in diameter; it moved very slowly in a zigzag line from right to left. A beam of light came from two powerful projectors placed at the front. Calling family members, they were able to watch the aircraft disappear at extremely high speed, without noise, and in an ascending movement of 45°. A second aircraft then appeared: shaped like a white or aluminum cigar, about 30 meters long, and arriving silently, it was at the same altitude as the first. About thirty portholes allowed a soft, dim light to be seen inside. It moved very slowly in a straight line and disappeared, hidden by the top of the trees. Both aircraft came from the N-NW and were heading towards the S-SE. The witness mentions a press article about lights appearing at Bordeaux airport on the evening in question. This very old testimony provides surprising details, however, we lack information and it is too old to conduct an investigation.
