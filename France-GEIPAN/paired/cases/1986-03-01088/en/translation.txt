Description: On March 1, 1986, around 7:30 PM, a witness heard a peculiar clapping noise outside. He found his car's hood damaged by an object that was melting. The witness placed the object in his refrigerator before calling the gendarmerie. This crystalline mass, blue in color, had a detergent-like smell. Due to the impact, fragments of this object scattered in the yard, leaving blue traces on the leaves of affected plants. Analyses revealed it was aircraft deicing fluid.
