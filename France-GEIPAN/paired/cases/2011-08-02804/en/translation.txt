**Report:** A witness reports an observation made on August 20, 2011, at 8:15 PM from the terrace of their 4th-floor apartment. Looking towards Montjuzet (63), they saw a dark gray, oval, flat object in motion at cloud level. No unusual noises were heard. The witness took a photograph with a camera. The UFO disappeared very quickly, with the observation lasting only 10 seconds. No other testimonies were collected. The witness's photograph is the key piece of evidence (see investigation report). The analysis of this photo reveals unusual elements suggesting a very probable hoax, at the very least involving the photo, and most likely the entire observation. 

Three hypotheses can be considered:

- **The photo is genuine:** The unusual effects are defects of the camera. The observed defects, particularly a 2° shift of the grid relative to the vertical, are not feasible defects for this type of camera, especially since these defects do not persist in subsequent photos. The shadow under the object is inconsistent with the declared time of observation. The technical parameters (ISO) of the UFO_PROTECTED_001 photo are significantly different from other photos taken with the same camera showing a sky view. This hypothesis is rejected.

- **The photo is genuine:** The unusual effects are due to the camera's exposure to an unknown phenomenon. This hypothesis suggests that electromagnetic perturbations could add this grid effect to the photo without significantly disturbing the image itself. However, the type of radiation capable of producing this effect is unimaginable. The shadow under the object is inconsistent with the declared time of observation. The technical parameters (ISO) of the UFO_PROTECTED_001 photo are significantly different from other photos taken with the same camera showing a sky view. This hypothesis is rejected.

- **The witness fabricated the observation and the photo to make their testimony more credible:** In this case, the witness would have needed to react quickly to find a photo resembling their observation, set up the shooting procedure, and provide the photo to the press promptly. While this hypothesis remains possible, it significantly weakens the reliability of the visual observation, especially as the witness does not recognize the hoax when presented with these arguments. It's a hoax; the witness fabricated the entire observation scenario from an image extracted from a video documentary. The observed defects are inconsistent with an authentic photo of a cloudy sky. The grid effect is easily explained if the witness photographed a document with this grid or a video screen. The shadow under the object is inconsistent with the declared time of observation. The technical parameters (ISO) of the UFO_PROTECTED_001 photo are significantly different from other photos taken with the same camera showing a sky view. The witness was unable to provide photos preceding and following the reference photo. Hoaxes often involve multiple attempts to select the "best" result. The witness's failure to provide these photos, as requested by UFO_PROTECTED_000, supports the hoax hypothesis, although this point is less decisive than the others.

These elements strongly indicate the hoax hypothesis, leading to the conclusion: UFO_PROTECTED_000 concludes with a: very high probability of hoax. The photo is an image of a photo or screen depicting UFO_PROTECTED_001.

The late publication of this report is due to the fact that the responsible party for UFO_PROTECTED_000 (in 2011) informed the witness by phone of their conclusions indicating a probable hoax. The witness claimed their photo was authentic. It was agreed at the time between the witness and UFO_PROTECTED_000 not to publish on the UFO_PROTECTED_000 website the conclusions (unmodified by the exchange) of UFO_PROTECTED_000 out of respect for the witness. In return, the witness was to refrain from publicly stating this observation, mentioning the absence of a conclusion or a different conclusion from UFO_PROTECTED_000, which would harm the work of UFO_PROTECTED_000. Nevertheless, in 2012, the witness did so on a forum, stating that UFO_PROTECTED_000 found no explanation but did not publish this non-explanation conclusion. The witness's behavior has led UFO_PROTECTED_000 to publish its conclusions today.
