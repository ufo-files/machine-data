Description: Recent testimony (February 19, 2010) of an old observation: on December 26 or 27, 2000, between 9:00 PM and 10:30 PM, several family members observed a very bright disk-like object in the sky and noted power outages and interference on television. No unusual noises were heard during the observation of the object, which moved and disappeared instantly. This old case, without precise date or time, and without a gendarmerie report at the time, makes any investigation impossible due to a lack of information.
