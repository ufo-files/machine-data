**Description:** On the morning of January 22, 2022, two individuals outdoors in a building courtyard noticed two black PANs (a type of flying object) moving together in the sky, staying close to each other while silently gliding. Only one statement was collected (see the investigation report). A first hypothesis, that of observing a paraglider flight, was explored. Indeed, the witness initially thought of this hypothesis, and the observation direction points towards a site frequently used by paragliders. The local paragliding club confirmed that, though rare, paraglider flights at the time of the observation were possible. Unfortunately, they could neither confirm nor deny the presence of paragliders at the time of the observation. The second hypothesis explored proved more plausible as it better matched the appearance and behavior of the PAN:

- The rounded shape of the two components of the PAN aligns well with the hypothesis of two balloons.
- The color, described as "black," is possible for such balloons.
- The observation took place on a Saturday, allowing for the possibility of lost balloons from a child.
- Their behavior relative to each other (spinning and passing under each other) is typical of two balloons attached together, likely by a string allowing them to be held in hand, but invisible at such an observation distance.
- Finally, the wind, at a moderate altitude (less than 200 meters), was variable over 360° and estimated as zero or very low, explaining their overall movement, successively from left to right and then inversely.

According to GEIPAN criteria, with average strangeness and good consistency (two witnesses, but a unique statement, photos, and video of the PAN), this case appears as a probable observation of two fantasy balloons attached and swayed by the very low and variable wind. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: observation of two fantasy balloons linked together.
