Summary: Observation of the passage of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-9b24c31b64ff5208de0b5f2d [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 22, 2010, at 2:40 a.m., several people observed from their homes the silent passage of orange-colored glowing balls forming a triangle, all moving in the same direction (S-NE) on a straight trajectory. These balls eventually disappeared after turning a brighter red. One of the witnesses filmed part of the observation. Following a gendarmerie deposition from a person who read an article on this subject in the press and in accordance with the information provided in the gendarmerie report, this is a confirmed case of lantern releases during a private party in the neighboring commune.
