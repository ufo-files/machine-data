Summary: Observation of the rapid passage of a white glowing ball in the sky followed by a glowing trail of the same color: observation of a probable return of a meteoroid.
--- seg-57dba10f04e99836e9cdd319 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 20, 1990, between 6:30 and 6:45 PM, a witness and his wife were walking on the Roman Path in Pennautier (11) when a bright white light ball appeared in the sky. This ball was followed by a glowing trail of the same color. The whole thing followed a curved trajectory from south-east to north-east and quickly disappeared behind the houses bordering the path. No noise was heard. The total duration of the observation is not indicated. Only one testimony was collected. The description of the PAN resembles that of a meteor. With average strangeness and consistency (single testimony), this case appears to be a probable misperception with a perfectly known astronomical phenomenon: the atmospheric reentry of a meteorite.
