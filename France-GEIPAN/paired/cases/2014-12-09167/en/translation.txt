Description: On December 26th and 29th, 2014, gendarmes were contacted by two witnesses residing in a hamlet within the commune of Nizerolles (03) who were intrigued by the presence of various lights in the night sky (see the investigation report). Based on the evidence collected, including: - The existence of regulated nighttime lighting tests on wind turbines located approximately 12 kilometers from the witnesses, partially visible in the observation axis, confirmed by on-site gendarmes. These lights are the same red color as two of the observed lights. - The presence of the planet Jupiter, observed in the same axis, moving and of the same color as one of the lights. - Public lighting of a nearby hamlet, witness #1 confusion noted by the gendarmes. - Christmas light decorations, witness #1 confusion also noted by the gendarmes. - The existence, for one witness, of a physiological phenomenon of micro-ocular mobility ("micro-nystagmus"). We can conclude that the lights observed by the witnesses are very likely a combination of various natural or man-made phenomena and objects (Jupiter, wind turbine red lights, public lighting, Christmas lights...) accompanied, for most, by the micro-nystagmus phenomenon. This case is classified as "A" for a certain observation of nighttime lighting of wind turbines, the planet Jupiter, and public lighting.
