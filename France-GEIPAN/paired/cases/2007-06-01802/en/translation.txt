--- seg-05440e5655d388aee6e5d462 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A person reports their observation made on June 19, 2007 at 10:10 PM. From their balcony, the witness had their attention drawn by the silent north-south passage of a very bright phenomenon, followed closely by a second, much larger in volume. The entire ensemble moved at a constant speed and disappeared behind a hill on a descending trajectory. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. A passage of the space station ISS, accompanied by the shuttle that had just separated from the station, occurred that day within visibility of Nyons. The case is classified as A, indicating the observation of an ISS and shuttle passage.
