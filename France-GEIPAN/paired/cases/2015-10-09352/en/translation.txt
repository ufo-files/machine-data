Summary: Successive and filmed observations of fixed light phenomena in the sky: certain misidentifications with the following astronomical objects: Arcturus, Vega, Capella, Aldébaran, Sirius, Jupiter, and Regulus.
--- seg-631a4fe2cdbdfef516f7f96a [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 7, 2015, around 8:20 PM, a resident of EVRON (53) sees through his window a blue-reflected light in the sky. He sees the PAN again 5 minutes later, as he is about to go to bed. He then calls his son, and both of them observe the PAN for 2 to 3 minutes. He then calls his brother, who resides in a hamlet of the neighboring commune of SAINTE-GEMMES-LE-ROBERT (53), to report the observation, and retrieves his camcorder to film the PAN. At the end of this first observation, the witness leaves his home with his son around 9:10 PM and arrives at his brother's house around 9:15 PM. He was waiting for it with a neighbor. Upon exiting his vehicle, the main witness sees the PAN again. The four people see a new PAN above a local relief. The brother and the neighbor return home. The main witness and his son then see 5 new light phenomena above the relief. The witness attempts to film them, but his camcorder refuses to work. The observation stops at 21:25. The witness reports his observation to the gendarmerie the following day. On October 9th around 6:00 AM, he sees two new PAN in the sky, which he films. These videos were recorded using a Sony DSR PD150 camcorder. The witness provided two videos of the observations: the first, lasting 1m51s, is a recap of the 7 October observation and was uploaded to Youtube on 10 October. The second, lasting 12m09s, shows all the witness's views taken on the evening of October 7 and the morning of October 9. Of average strangeness, but with sufficient consistency (see the investigation report), this case turns out to be a certain misperception with various astronomical objects: Arcturus, Vega, Capella, Aldébaran, Sirius, Jupiter, and Regulus. The quality of the videos taken by the witness allows for a good positioning of the various PAN in relation to terrestrial references, and the zooms show different less bright stars nearby. With these elements, a check in Stellarium allows certain identification of each of the PAN. This case is classified A, mistaking certain astronomical objects: Arcturus, Vega, Capella, Aldébaran, Sirius, Jupiter, and Regulus.
