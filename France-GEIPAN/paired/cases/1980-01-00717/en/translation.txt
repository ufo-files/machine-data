Description: On January 16, 1980, at 7 PM, witnesses observed a peculiar luminescent phenomenon in the sky. It was a very bright white ball located in the S/O of the sky. During the observation, the phenomenon changed colors and light intensity, growing or shrinking; strings seemed to be descending from the object. No noise was heard during the entire observation by all witnesses. The phenomenon quickly moved away and was lost from view around 9 PM. One of the witnesses took photos that showed nothing, the film being black. According to the gendarmerie investigation with the meteorological station, no French weather balloon was launched or reported in the area. However, it should be noted that a few days earlier, a foreign weather balloon was discovered in another commune. According to the sky map, Venus was particularly visible at that time (-3.84) and low on the horizon in the S/O. It also disappeared around 9 PM. These people likely made an astronomical observation of Venus.
