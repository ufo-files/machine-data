Summary: Observation of a blue-tinged conical light form surrounded by a halo: gas cloud resulting from the passivation of the upper cryogenic stage (ESC-A) of the Ariane VA225 launch vehicle.
--- seg-eb0d0ce5cb074a0d6d9381b3 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 21, 2015, at 2:00 AM on the island of Réunion, a witness photographed a phenomenon in the sky that intrigued them. The witness reported seeing a blue-hued, cone-shaped, luminous form surrounded by a halo. Only one testimony was collected. This observation of a large, translucent, luminous phenomenon moving slowly in the sky is quite exceptional, and the quality of the photos taken by the witness allows for its perfect characterization. Due to its similarity to other similar phenomena (see the article on a luminous halo in the Indian Ocean sky on September 29, 2013), the investigation quickly focused on recent satellite launches. Indeed, after investigation, the object's position in the sky is consistent with the trajectory of the cryogenic upper stage of the Ariane VA225 launcher, launched from Kourou on August 20 at 8:16 PM UTC; this stage, listed as N40876, began its passivation (tank emptying) at 9:12 PM; it passed vertically over Indonesia at an altitude of over 8,000 km (see attached map). Around 9:30 PM UTC (the time of the photograph), the object was clearly visible from the island of Réunion. As this is a geostationary satellite launch on a highly elliptical transfer orbit (approximately 300 km - 36,000 km), the launcher is already at a very high altitude at that time, making it visible from a very large area. The cloud's own trajectory is close to that of the launcher but disturbed by the gas ejection speed, which is difficult to model. The gas cloud released during passivation may have persisted until it was diluted in space. The halo observed is the cloud of unused gas ejected by the launcher after the propulsion phase; it is illuminated by the sun and becomes visible to ground observers who are in the night. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A" for a perfectly identified phenomenon: a gas cloud resulting from the passivation of the cryogenic upper stage (ESC-A) of the Ariane VA225 launcher.
