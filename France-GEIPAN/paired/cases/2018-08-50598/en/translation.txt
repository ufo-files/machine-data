Description: On the evening of August 13, 2018, a resident of SAINT-THURIAU (56) was enjoying a clear sky to stargaze, when along a S/SO to N/NE axis, her gaze was drawn to a movement. She sees three bright points forming a triangle, at equal distance from each other, advancing at the same speed. Their brightness is that of the surrounding stars. The observation lasted about thirty seconds before he lost their trace in a blend of stars. Of weak strangeness and average consistency (single witness, but precise testimony), this case turns out to be the observation of a triplet of Yaogan 31 satellites (see the investigation report). 
According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The witness's description of the PAN is perfectly characteristic of a triplet of satellites of the "design" NOSS (Naval Ocean Surveillance System) adopted by China (China's NOSS-Like Triplets) orbiting in formation: 3 faintly bright points moving together, isosceles triangle formation, apparent size of 10 cm, sky visible through the PAN, and apparent speed of approximately 0.5°/s (see NOSS Double and Triple Satellite Formations). During the time slot indicated by the witness, the Yaogan 31 triplet satellite passed on a trajectory corresponding to that of the PAN. The case is classified "A", observation of the Yaogan 31 satellite trio.
