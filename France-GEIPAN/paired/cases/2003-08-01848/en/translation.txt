Summary: Observation of white flashes.
--- seg-c383d058a340da88ee9d096c [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Direct testimony: During the course of August 2003 (the witness cannot recall the exact date) around midnight, five individuals gathered in the village square to stargaze witnessed a peculiar lighting phenomenon. While observing the night sky and shooting stars, they were "flashed" for half a second by two rows of three lights directly above them. The flash was white in color, with intense brightness akin to daytime. The lights gradually dimmed over one second. The entire event transpired without any noise. No other testimonies regarding this phenomenon have been collected, and we lack information about it.
