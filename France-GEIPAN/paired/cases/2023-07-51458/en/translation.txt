Description: On July 14, 2023, at 20:40, a family is dining outdoors when a witness is intrigued by the silent passage across the sky of an object they cannot recognize. Only one testimony was collected. The witness observed a Boeing 738 en route from Bristol to Barcelona. It passed at 20:40 local time (18:40 UTC) to the east of the witness with a general NS orientation (see FR24 document witness-aircraft position). At this time, the aircraft was at an altitude of 37,000 feet (11.28 km), resulting in an elevation of 62°, which is compatible with the witness's estimate of 50° to 70° given the difficulty in accurately estimating elevation without a fixed reference point. The witness's perceived oddity is due to the absence of a condensation trail. Condensation trails are not always present, forming based on pressure, temperature, and humidity conditions, particularly in cold air conditions. See a very detailed example with the KANFEN case study (57) 12.07.2020. Case classification A: observation of a commercial aircraft.
