Summary: Probable observation of a weather balloon.
--- seg-7c3aec432d96270b3a61a7af [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 4, 1978, between 8:00 PM and 10:00 PM, numerous people observed the passage of a spherical and very bright object. This object advanced while oscillating from East to West. A darker mass sometimes distinguished itself beneath the object. All these people were likely witnesses to the passage of a brightly sunlit weather balloon, as well as a weather probe placed beneath the balloon. The weather centers in Rennes or Guipavas frequently launch this type of object in the region.
