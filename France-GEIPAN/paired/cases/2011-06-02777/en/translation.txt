Description: A witness regularly observes a bright light moving in the sky from their home. The light expands and then diminishes before disappearing on the horizon. The witness is almost directly in line with one of the runways at Orly Airport. With a north wind, planes take off facing the witness. The planes' front lights are very directional and powerful, making it impossible for the witness to distinguish the dimmer red flashing lights. When the plane deviates from the line of sight, the light received by the witness significantly decreases. The repeatability of the observation supports this hypothesis; the witness could confirm that this occurs with a north wind. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of planes taking off from Orly.
