Description: On February 7, 2008, around 7:10 PM, a witness observed in the sky what he described as a spacecraft with red and white lights. At the same time, a massive fireball fell to the right of this first PAN, in a descending trajectory. The observation lasted a total of approximately 12 seconds. No other testimonies were collected. Of average strangeness but low consistency (single witness, brief testimony, contradictions, short observation), this case proves too incomplete to be identified. The observation consists of a probable meteor shower observation simultaneous with a possible aircraft observation. The latter cannot unfortunately be verified due to a too long transmission delay to GEIPAN. This case is classified C, for lack of data.
