Description: On June 18, 2024, around 10:30 PM, while returning home to Rennes (35) from Fougères (35), the witness observed an unidentified aerial object in Cesson-Sévigné (35). Initially mistaken for an airplane or hot air balloon due to its round silhouette, the witness quickly realized it didn't match either of these. Without any audible noise, the object appeared as a sphere with pointed appendages and a flame directed downwards. The witness filmed the PAN in motion before it disappeared behind the clouds. The case exhibits good consistency* with a duly completed questionnaire, clear explanations provided by the witness, and the availability of exploitable video footage. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The investigation concludes that the witness filmed a hot air balloon with an unusual shape, a sphere surrounded by points, "with a flame directed downwards" (see the investigation report). The witness's sense of strangeness arises from the inability to identify the PAN. The presence of flames below resembles Thai (or Chinese) lanterns. The investigation reduced the strangeness of the observation by highlighting similar balloons designed by the association "Semeurs des étoiles," visible on their website. However, analysis demonstrates that the object observed on June 18, 2024, did not directly come from this source but is likely the result of an individual reproducing this model. The GEIPAN classifies it as: festive paper hot air balloon.
