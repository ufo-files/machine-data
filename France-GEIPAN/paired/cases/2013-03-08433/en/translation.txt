Summary: Observation of the silent movement of an orange and very bright object: probable Thai lantern.
--- seg-09fa4ea4476af9d3e94aab51 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 31, 2013, around 9:20 PM, in Marseille, three individuals observed a silent, orange and intensely bright object moving across the sky. The object came from the West, passed over the witnesses' home, then turned right and disappeared towards the Southeast. Only one of the three witnesses provided a statement. It was noted that the direction of travel indicated by the witness (from Salon-de-Provence to Aubagne-Cassis, that is, Northwest to Southeast) matches the wind direction recorded at Marignane that evening. This observation shares characteristics with a Thai lantern (flying lantern): linear movement for a few minutes, silent, following the wind direction, orange in color, on a holiday evening. Due to the low strangeness of this case, the GEIPAN did not conduct any investigations to identify the person who released the lantern. The GEIPAN classified this case as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
