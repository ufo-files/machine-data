Description: Around 8 PM, a witness observed from their 5th-floor balcony for three minutes a phenomenon that intrigued them: above a circular cloud, they noticed in the sky two groups of objects, one composed of white silver spheres and the other of two red spheres. The north-south movement of these groups was silent. The witness took photographs. The observation's characteristics suggest a balloon flight. The photos sent to GEIPAN were of poor quality and only allowed the distinction of the two red spheres described by the witness (photo zoom file IMGP0422). Notably, at 9 PM, a rugby match between USAP and ASM was held at the Aimé Guiral Stadium in Perpignan, during which balloons (red being one of the colors of "sang et or") could have been released. The observation duration of 3 minutes is consistent with the distance between the stadium and the observation location (approximately 2 km) and the speed (33 km/h with gusts up to 57 km/h), as well as the wind direction (north-west) given in the day's weather bulletin. The case is classified as B, indicating a probable observation of released balloons approximately 2 km from the observation location.
