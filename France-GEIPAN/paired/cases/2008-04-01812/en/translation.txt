Description: A witness reports, upon reviewing photos taken along a beach on April 23, 2008, at approximately 4:15 PM, they noticed unexplained spots they cannot account for. It remains challenging to analyze photos featuring unexplained spots without visual observation to confirm them. However, in this case, it's likely birds that briefly passed through the camera's field of view. This is particularly evident on the first photo, where the body and wings are clearly distinguishable, less evident on the second, but by analogy, it's highly probable the same phenomenon is at play. Following the publication of this observation on the site and a message from a new witness, the object in the second photo is clearly identified as a frisbee (photo2_testimony_retouched_100826) also visible in the hands of a thrower on the updated photo 1 (photo1_testimony_retouched_100826). This case thus involves the observation of two distinct objects (probable observation of a bird on photo 1 and a frisbee on photo 2): the classification as Case B remains unchanged in relation to the probable observation of a bird on photo 1.
