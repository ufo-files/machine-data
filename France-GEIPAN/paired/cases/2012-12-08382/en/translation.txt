Summary: Observation for three seconds of a luminous phenomenon illuminating the sky: probable atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
--- seg-a8a74e336a18412f5e10baa8 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 17, 2012, at 11:30 PM, a witness suddenly saw the sky light up with a deep blue color for 3 seconds from their home. This testimony is very brief; the witness only saw a powerful indirect glow. The color and duration of the phenomenon are consistent with a bolide (atmospheric entry of a meteoroid, or meteorite) that is likely related to the particularly notable Geminid meteor shower this year (see http://clubregulus.free.fr/geminides.html). One could also think of an electrical flash (electric welding, major short circuit on an electrical line) however, the witness does not report the usual flickering of electrical lightning but a complete illumination of the sky (which was overcast), which is well suited to a high-altitude phenomenon. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of a bolide.
