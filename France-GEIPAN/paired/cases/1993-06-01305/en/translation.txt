Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archive on its public website www.geipan.fr. Among its publications are old cases classified at the time (A, B, C, or D) that are now being re-examined with the sole purpose of being more relevant in their conclusions. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over the past many years, this re-examination sometimes leads to new observations or even a change in classification. This observation case previously classified as D and named CREIL (92) 17.06.1993 is part of a recent set of cases that have been re-examined. On June 17, 1993, around 4 a.m., a witness observed, at the bottom of their building and then in a car, the rapid movements of two white lights in the sky and on the ground. The PAN describes circular figures moving at very high speed. No particular noise was heard during the observation, which lasted over 15 minutes. No other testimony was collected. The description might have seemed stranger during the initial analysis of the case in 1993. With the experience gained by the GEIPAN, there is no doubt today. The description is consistent with laser reflections in the sky with one or more spots moving back and forth. The witness mentions the presence of clouds that enhance a screen effect. The laser beams rising are visible here (not always the case in the GEIPAN experience, only spots may be visible while in other cases only the rising beams are visible without a final spot in the sky) and are interpreted by the witness as descending beams emitted by the PAN towards the ground. In 1993, laser uses were much less advanced than today. However, laser trials have been identified (see gendarmerie protocol) in proximity and on the night in question. This element, added to the well-mastered characterization of sky effects today, allows us to conclude without hesitation. The testimony is typical of the emotion that an unknown phenomenon can generate in a witness, especially when the illusion of intelligence in the phenomenon that would follow the witness and only appear when the witness is unaccompanied, etc., takes hold. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: laser.
