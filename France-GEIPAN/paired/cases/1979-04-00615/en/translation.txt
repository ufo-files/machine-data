--- seg-45c1dbe195b1ef241cb84bf3 [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 2, 1979, at approximately 8:40 PM, the gendarmerie received telephone calls reporting the overflight of the commune by a craft emitting a bright light. On the scene, the gendarmes observed an orange luminescent phenomenon moving across the clear sky at low altitude towards the northeast. Red blinking lights and a fixed green light emanated from the craft. The investigation demonstrated that it was the passage of a craft of type DC.9 conducting flight training in the region between 8:30 PM and 9:15 PM. The case is classified A: observation of an aircraft.
