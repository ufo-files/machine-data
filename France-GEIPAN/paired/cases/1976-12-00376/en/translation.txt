Description: On December 26, 1976, at approximately 8:15 PM, two witnesses observed pale red and yellow lights from the west for several minutes. The lights described intricate patterns as they slowly moved towards the east. This phenomenon occurred over Mont Fevrier, not far from the radio station. The witnesses also saw a red ball rise from the high-tension line to meet the luminous formation and then disappear. No other testimonies were collected, and the radio station agent in Cuisseaux reported nothing. This case is classified C due to a lack of information.
