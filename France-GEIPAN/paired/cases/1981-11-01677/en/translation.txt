Summary: Observation of the probable re-entry of an object into the atmosphere
--- seg-fba3e825ab3198ab052f5f8b [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 20, 1981, around 6:00 PM, several witnesses observed in the sky a glowing ball followed by a multicolored trail. This ball moved at a very high speed, crossing the sky in a south-north direction. The observation lasted 15 to 30 seconds in a clear sky. One of the witnesses heard a whistle. These individuals may have witnessed an atmospheric reentry.
