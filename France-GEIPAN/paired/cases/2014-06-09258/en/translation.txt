Summary: Observation of the silent movement of a dark spherical object in the cloudy sky: lack of information.
--- seg-c9cdce523544c5ade159a627 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 14, 2014, at 8:00 PM, a witness observed a dark spherical object emerging from a residual storm cloud. It moved slowly and silently from North to South. The witness made a video, but it only shows the object for 2 seconds, viewed from a distance: it is indeed possible to discern a roughly spherical, dark object reflecting the sun. The object disappeared into another cloud. The simplest hypothesis would be a balloon, either a hot air balloon or one inflated with helium (a small party balloon or a larger one made of plastic film). Its linear, relatively slow movement is consistent with this hypothesis. Let's check if the wind direction matches the observed movement: regional weather reports in Toulon show: West wind 11-30 km/h, shifting to South, weakening (weather report in Cannes: light South wind, shifting to North (weather report). In Le Luc (20 km away) after an afternoon thunderstorm, at 8:00 PM, a light West wind blows, shifting to North at 9:00 PM, very unstable, changing direction every hour (weather report). We thus observe a disrupted weather due to storm foci, making it impossible to determine the local wind at this precise moment, at low altitude, even more so as the region's relief is accidented. This balloon hypothesis, although compatible with the observation, cannot be confirmed by the weather report. The GEIPAN classifies this case as category "C" due to lack of verification and cross-checking. The witness refutes the hypothesis of a balloon.
