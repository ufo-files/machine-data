Description: The witness takes to the air at his window at 6:30 PM when he sees and films a black dot with two orange trails moving across the sky. The phenomenon disappears over the horizon. The witness posts his video online. The observation of orange trails at dusk on very clear weather suggests high-altitude airplane condensation trails, at a distance of 20 to 100 km. The video leaves no doubt about this phenomenon, easily observable on clear days to the West. See also: strange airplane trails. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A: certain observation of condensation trails.
