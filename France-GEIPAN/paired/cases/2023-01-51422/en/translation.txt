**Description:** On January 19, 2023, at approximately 7:50 PM, a witness from their home in the commune of Plouhinec (56), observed a PAN in the form of an isosceles triangle to the southeast. The entire lower surface of the PAN was illuminated with uniform yellow lights. This PAN was observed to move slightly to the left and was visually below power lines passing in front of the house. The witness attempted to photograph the PAN without success. Indeed, on the viewing screen, instead of the image of the PAN, there was a light blue color, never observed before with this device. At that moment, the PAN was no longer visible to the witness, completely obscured by the camera. When they lowered the camera to observe the PAN with the naked eye, they noticed it was no longer present, and the viewing screen of the camera returned to normal. The witness reported their observation to GEIPAN two months after the event, enabling a field investigation by two experts on May 13, 2023. The lead investigator then conducted numerous exchanges with the witness to deepen the data collection, resulting in the loan of the original camera. A technical test campaign was conducted during the week of July 31, 2023. The results of these tests are included in Annex 1 (see the investigation report). Analysis of the CNOA* map and data from the specialized website "ADS-B.NL" reveals that no aeronautical traffic corresponding to the PAN was present at the time of the observation in the azimuth of the phenomenon.

The analysis of all collected data concludes:

- The witness accurately described the appearance and behavior of the PAN without contradicting themselves between the initial report, the field investigation, and subsequent exchanges.
- Although brief, the observation describes a phenomenon with characteristics that are difficult to align with known phenomena. We did, however, explore the hypothesis of a drone, potentially of military origin. But the appearance and location of the PAN are incompatible with this hypothesis. Furthermore, the possible dimensions calculated for the PAN, considering the witness's apparent size overestimation, imply that this drone would have been of a certain span and in close proximity to the witness - approximately 15-20 meters for a drone around 50 cm to 1 meter. Such proximity would introduce other constraints (prohibited overflight, noise perception) not validated by the investigation.
- Various epiphenomena noted by the witness could, for the most part, be logically explained and/or unrelated to the PAN. Examples include:
    - The existence of a shift in the internal clock of the camera, for which we demonstrated was already present at least for almost 18 months.
    - The presence of an artifact found on the ground by the witness, consisting of twigs arranged in a triangle. Although the witness seems to associate this element with their observation indirectly (considering the reconstruction of a third party, a child, or other), no objective evidence from the investigation confirms this link.
- A thorough analysis of the camera used by the witness, which unexpectedly displayed a blue screen during the attempt to photograph the PAN, concludes: "Although we were able to recreate a very similar blue screen during our tests, its generation during the observation requires a sequence of false manipulations that seem unimaginable in the context and/or the existence of at least one blue-tinted image in memory, which, though theoretically possible, proved impossible to reproduce during the observation of the PAN." The appearance of this blue screen, never observed before on this device in the witnesses' possession for approximately a decade, remains unexplained for now.

**Conclusion:** The residual strangeness after the investigation remains significant compared to known phenomena. The drone hypothesis, extensively explored, could not be confirmed. The investigation file was presented to the expert committee of GEIPAN (most recently on December 3, 2025), which noted that according to GEIPAN's criteria, the consistency of the testimony remains average despite a thorough investigation and the witness's full collaboration. In particular, several elements reported, considered annexes to the observation, seem to give rise to interpretations that could not be linked to the PAN. Consequently, GEIPAN classifies the case as 'C', an unidentified phenomenon due to a lack of objective data.

**CONSISTENCY:** According to GEIPAN's criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.
