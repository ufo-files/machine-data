Description: On Friday, December 5, 2014, between 7:45 and 7:50 PM, a walker was intrigued by the silent movement across the night sky of 5 very bright red-orange lights. No other witnesses were collected. The observation of orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on the eve of a holiday (St. Nicholas), strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from west to east, which is consistent with the light southwest to west wind recorded in Pontoise (see the Météociel report). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of Thai lanterns.
