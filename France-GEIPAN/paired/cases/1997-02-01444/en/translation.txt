Description: On February 4, 1997, around 6:20 PM, approximately ten witnesses across several departments in southwestern France observed, for a few seconds, the very fast passage of a bright sphere with a significant trail descending in trajectory. Some witnesses observed an explosion into several pieces of this sphere. This is likely an atmospheric reentry, probably that of a meteorite.
