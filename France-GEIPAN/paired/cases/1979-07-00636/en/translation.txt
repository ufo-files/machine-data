Description: On June 30, 1979, around 11:30 PM, motorists on a departmental road witnessed a bright light in the sky. Initially stationary, the light moved progressively closer to the witnesses, with red and green lights on each side of the point flashing alternately. A whooshing sound was heard as the object flew overhead, and a triangular shape was observed. The craft quickly moved away to the south. This information corresponds to descriptions of night flights of aircraft (noise, geometry, and color of lights). These witnesses likely observed an airplane passing by.
