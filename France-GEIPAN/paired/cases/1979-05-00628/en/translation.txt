Description: On May 30, 1979, two individuals observed in the sky for 20 minutes a peculiar phenomenon. An object shaped like a flattened rugby ball crossed the sky without following a Southeast-Northwest direction. The witnesses noticed a bright orange trail, of variable length, following the object, which was approximately 500 meters in altitude. One of the two witnesses, a student pilot, did not recognize it as an aircraft. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected, and we lack information such as the observation time.
