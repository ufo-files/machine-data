Description: On July 21, 2009, around midnight forty, a witness observed from his home the north-south passage of four red lights in the sky. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted about two minutes. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon. The information provided suggests a probable passage of Thai lanterns. No photos or videos, but the description once again relates orange-red lights, silent movement, low altitude, sudden extinction, and the multiplication of observations of this type of phenomenon, leading to a case classification as probably identified.
