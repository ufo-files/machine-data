Description: On January 18, 2012, at approximately 10:45 PM and on January 19 at 12:15 AM, a couple observed the silent, constant-speed passes of two sets of orange fireballs. The description of orange balls flying silently at a constant speed immediately suggests Thai lanterns. This type of observation is more common on weekends, but it should be noted that the Chinese New Year on January 23 may have led to multiple celebrations during this period. The weather in Bordeaux indicates a south wind, while the witnesses report a movement towards Bordeaux, suggesting rather a southwest wind. The lanterns could have been released from one of the many villas or restaurants southwest of La Teste. The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
