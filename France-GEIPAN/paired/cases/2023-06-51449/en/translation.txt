Summary: Long observations in the sky of a zigzag trail: observations of a trail from a hypersonic probe rocket.
--- seg-558513d074804ba15e14f041 [needs-review] ---
Description: The GEIPAN has received multiple witness reports of the same phenomenon described as "a sort of smoke trail or condensation" and "forming zigzags." The observations are related to a probe rocket test conducted by the General Directorate of Armaments, from Biscarrosse. This is a test shot of the hypersonic glider technology demonstrator VMaX (See on the Ministry of Defense website). As part of its experiments, the DGA launched a three-stage rocket on June 26, 2023. - The rocket generated the observed trails. - The zigzag shapes are due to the separation of the different stages during the firing and the systematic relighting of the upper stages. The high-altitude winds subsequently did the rest, drawing the zigzag patterns observed. These tracks, like those left behind by commercial airplanes, can indeed be seen over much of France, especially since the flight altitude was very high. They can subsequently remain visible for several hours, depending on their dispersion, which depends on the high-altitude wind as well as the temperature prevailing there. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: observation of a trail from a hypersonic probe rocket.
