Summary: Observation of two aerial phenomena
--- seg-3166217b08f52610cdb05a63 [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 16, 1999, around 8:00 PM, a witness observed the very fast passage of two aerial phenomena consisting of a small orange ball and a ball of flames. The trajectory of the two balls was straight, but one of them seemed to dive into the valley before rising again in altitude. The phenomenon remained perfectly silent and lasted only a few seconds. The trajectory of one of the two phenomena a priori rules out the hypothesis of an atmospheric reentry. The proximity of military installations also did not allow for the identification of the phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
