Description: On August 20, 2013 at 10:40 PM, three people observed the silent passage in the sky of an orange-colored light phenomenon. The observation of an orange ball moving silently in the sky resembles a Thai lantern. Weather reports for Grenoble and Lyon indicate a strong north wind. This wind is consistent with the observed movement from northwest to southeast. The video is entirely consistent with this hypothesis. These lanterns are more often launched on weekends, but summer holidays during the week are not uncommon (Tuesday in this case). This very unstrange observation is classified "B" as a probable observation of a Thai lantern.
