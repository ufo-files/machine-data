Summary: Observation in the sky of the movement of an object with a tubular shape, aluminum-colored and dark: observation of a festive mylar balloon.
--- seg-1be446ca8fcfcae48a808171 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 12, 2022, the witness, accompanied by 2 people, was walking outside the town of PONT-A-MOUSSON (54), when they spotted in the sky, to the east, an object of aluminum/black color with a tubular shape. The witnesses observed this object, moving in a straight line and seemingly turning on itself at moments. The observation lasted five minutes before it was lost from sight. The case's consistency is good: only one of the three witnesses reported their observation, but the two videos and the photo usefully complete the questionnaire. The investigation concluded that the witness observed an object carried by the wind, very likely a mylar party balloon (see the investigation report). The strangeness perceived by the witness was mainly due to the shape of the PAN "tubular object", but the investigation confirms that mylar balloons can be of varied shapes, some of which can give the impression of a tubular shape. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: mylar party balloon.
