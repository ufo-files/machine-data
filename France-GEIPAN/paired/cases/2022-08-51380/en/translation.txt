**Case Description:**

On the night of August 7th to 8th, 2022, at 12:14 AM, a witness, unable to sleep, decided to observe the starry sky from his closed window. He noticed a unique light emitting alternating colors. He then took out his phone and recorded the scene. Subsequently, two distinct lights were observed across a unique phenomenon. He made four videos, pausing for 5 to 10 minutes between each to review his footage. The fourth video showed lights arranged on a diagonal line. Tired and slightly anxious, after several minutes, he stopped observing, believing he had seen the phenomenon again in the morning moving northward at high speed. The case is well-documented through the provision of videos. The witness's availability and answers to additional questions contribute to the overall consistency of the report, according to the criteria of the GEIPAN (consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony).

The peculiarity of the case mainly stems from the fourth video. The investigation into the case allowed for the elimination of numerous hypotheses (see the investigation report). After several explanatory attempts, verifications answered all the questions raised by the case. On August 7th, in the afternoon, a forest fire broke out at Le Plessis, in the commune of Sainte-Maure-de-Touraine (37), ravaging half a hectare. The presence of fire vehicles for surveillance purposes extended into the early morning. These factors adequately explain the witness's statements regarding the perceived multiple colors, the duration of observations, and the line of lights at 12:17 on August 8th, which were ultimately just the various lights on the vehicles leaving the scene.

The findings from analyzing videos and images, along with the circumstances of the recordings, highlighted the proximity of the lights, not in the sky but relatively close to the ground. Information provided by the gendarmerie confirms a fire in a direction very close to that reported by the witness. The peculiarity of the case itself arises from the conditions under which the videos were recorded. The analysis of the case file reduces the strangeness of the observation by revealing the presence of firefighters at the site during the observation.

The case is classified as "A": observation of lights on the ground indicating human activity following a forest fire.
