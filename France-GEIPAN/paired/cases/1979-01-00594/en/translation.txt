Description: On January 19, 1979, around 6:30 PM, two motorists observed in the sky two intense white lights, as well as a red and orange glow from an object they could not define. A movement towards the southeast was noted, but no sound was heard. No other witnesses could be found. During the investigation, it was established that training flights of a bimotor aircraft had taken place on January 19 between 5:30 PM and 7:00 PM. This aircraft is equipped with two powerful forward lights, and turns were made over the affected area.
