--- seg-1c74f4bc02313af5634d23cc [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 3, 2015, at 10:29 PM, several people were intrigued by the presence in the night sky of a particular luminous phenomenon. One witness reports observing for 3 to 4 minutes two rows of round, white, horizontal, parallel lights without a halo. These lights form a triangle progressively, without apparent noise, and begin a curve before disappearing to the West. Repeated observations of lights in the sky, arranged in regular geometric patterns, suggest an aerial patrol or demonstration exercise. A check with the Patrouille de France, based in Salon de Provence, indicates that the patrol exceptionally flew at night in this region on July 1st and 3rd: on July 1st to repeat the demonstration that took place on the 3rd for the baptism of the outgoing promotions. The silence accompanying the observation is quite normal, as the noise of an airplane seen from the front, at more than a few kilometers, is very low. The 8 Alpha Jets of the French Patrol are equipped with powerful headlights visible more than 10 km along the flight axis. When the planes turned, the lighthouse beam changed direction, and the observers located along the initial flight path saw it suddenly disappear. These planes, seen from the side, are only visible by their red or green position lights, which are not powerful enough to be seen from a great distance. Seen from the front, the approach is not perceptible; the planes appear stationary, only lateral movement is perceived, and it may be slight. The GEIPAN also received reports of an observation of the same patrol seen on July 1st, from Valensole, Lurs, Sainte-Tulle, about a dozen kilometers away. During the early days of July, the GEIPAN receives similar observation reports annually in Provence, one of which was reported in Villeneuve on July 5, 2013. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": certain observation of the 8 Alpha Jets from the French patrol. To see a video of the French Air Force Patrol (Patrouille de France) flying at night over Paris.
