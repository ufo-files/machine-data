--- seg-7c000aec4783f89d2fa8f0ca [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 25, 2009, around 7 PM, several people observed from their homes a luminous phenomenon in the sky that intrigued them. One of the witnesses filmed the phenomenon, which appears as a glowing ball with a blue halo and describes circular movements when zoomed in. The main witness indicated that the phenomenon repeated itself at the beginning of the night over the following days. The observed phenomenon (over several days) and filmed (for several minutes) was none other than the Planet Venus, a conclusion noted in the gendarmerie report and acknowledged by the main witness. The very bright Venus (magnitude -4.5) was indeed in the sector of the sky observed and filmed by the witness.
