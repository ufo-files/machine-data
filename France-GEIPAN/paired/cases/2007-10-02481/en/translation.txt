Description: On October 27, 2007, at 23:00, a motorist observed in the sky the rapid passage to the northeast of a luminous phenomenon followed by a persistent smoke trail. The weather was clear with some clouds at altitude. The observation lasted between 1 and 2 seconds. He deduced that it could be a meteorite. No other testimony was collected. The witness's impression of a meteorite is probably correct... but the description is too vague to confirm it. The description is nothing very strange. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies this case as C: lack of reliable information.
