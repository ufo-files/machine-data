Summary: Long observation of a luminous phenomenon appearing next to the setting sun: observation of a parhelion (optical phenomenon related to the solar halo).
--- seg-6a49ebf6db54eec4e634b8b5 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 15, 2021, early in the evening, a couple of motorists are traveling to Switzerland. Due to a significant traffic jam on highway A9/E27, they are stopped, on a large bridge, at the exit of a tunnel near Fort de Chillon. The witness then has the impression of seeing a second sun, at an angle of approximately 15° north of the "true" sun, at about the same height and roughly the same distance from it as the sun. However, this second sun appears to have less light intensity, as if its power were only a quarter of the real sun's. This luminous mass seems fixed in the sky, and the witness does not initially perceive any movement. The couple spends almost 20 minutes on this bridge, observing this phenomenon. Over time, the witness feels that this luminous phenomenon is descending, similar to the sun setting. Only one testimony will be collected. This case is of very good consistency: one witness with a fairly complete testimony and clear photographs that allow the positioning of the PAN in its environment (see the investigation report). The witness's description of the PAN and, in particular, its visual aspect in the photographs clearly show that the PAN is a parhelion, or false sun, an optical phenomenon related to the solar halo, appearing at 22° to the right or left of the Sun, at its angular height. The landscape elements visible in the photos allow the position of the PAN to be determined and confirm that it is located where a parhelion would be expected. The case is classified as A: observation of a parhelion.
