Description: On March 15, 2013, at 7:38 PM, a witness and his son were intrigued by the silent, zigzagging movement of a golden, round object in the sky. The witness's description (slowly advancing golden ball) suggests a Thai lantern. The wind direction, south (see Meteociel report), is consistent with the beginning of the observation. Note that the witness is located north of Disneyland Park (see map), which could be the source of the release. The zigzag trajectory may be due to turbulence caused by the urban environment. The impression of a rapid ascent at the beginning is due to the gradual extinction of the burner: the dimming of light is interpreted as a receding. This case is classified "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
