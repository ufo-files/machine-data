Description: A witness and his wife, who live in a rural house, have observed for several years sporadic or recurring phenomena near their residence: bright balls that move, sometimes far, sometimes close. Following the assessments of experts sent to the witnesses, it is clear that the bright balls they observe every night are stars or planets. The brightest one is Jupiter, which, at the end of October 2011, rises in the East around 7:30 PM. As for the other supposed observations above their field, no element has allowed to refute or confirm the witness's statements. The GEIPAN classifies the recurring observations (Jupiter and stars) as A and the others as C. The file is therefore generally classified as 'C'.
