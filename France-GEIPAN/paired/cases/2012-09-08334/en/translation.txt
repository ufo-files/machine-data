Description: On Saturday, September 15, 2012, around 11 PM, several people observed in the sky the silent movement of several yellow-orange lights that gradually faded out. Only one person testified. The witness's description (yellow points fading gradually into a black shape, slow horizontal movement) is quite characteristic of a Thai lantern observation. The coincidence with a fireworks display on a Saturday night is also a favorable factor. The very light wind, rather from the southwest, is not incompatible with this hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," a probable observation of Thai lanterns (flying lanterns).
