Description: On December 16, 1988, in the region of Cambrai, at sunset, a student driving her car observed a silent, round object stabilized at approximately 20 meters above a field with 4 or 6 "large lights" on its lower part. Additionally, light was seen in a neighboring house that was supposedly abandoned. The observation lasted about 1 minute as the witness did not dare to stop. After investigation, the light in the house could be explained as the farm was actually used by the military for maneuvers, but no other data could be gathered regarding the observed shape, which could not be identified.
