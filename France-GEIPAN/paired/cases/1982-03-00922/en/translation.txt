Description: On February 1, 1982, between 11:20 PM and 11:30 PM, several amateur astronomers observed with their binoculars approximately fifteen objects moving in a south-southeast direction without making any noise. Of slightly elongated shape and colored, they were very bright. No change in shape or color was noticed during the observation. The available information does not allow for an analysis of the phenomenon.
