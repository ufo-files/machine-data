--- seg-fc8075c0e6a5f27e6f1a0539 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 6, 1980, around 11 PM, three individuals observed a peculiar luminous phenomenon in the sky. For two to five minutes, they watched two groups of three red lights, one of which was blinking. These two groups followed each other closely. A motor noise accompanied the entire event, which moved at low altitude and at a slow speed. No other testimonies were collected regarding this phenomenon. The investigation will show that at the time of the observation, many planes were using this air corridor. However, the phenomenon remains unexplained due to a lack of information.
