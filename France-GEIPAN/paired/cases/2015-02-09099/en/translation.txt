Description: On February 1, 2015, at 1:30 AM, two witnesses observed for an extended period a fixed blue light pointing south, above the clouds. Only one statement was collected. The observation of a fixed light for over an hour suggests an astronomical phenomenon. The moon was particularly visible that night, almost full. The blue color mentioned by the witness suggests moonlight. The weather was unstable, with frequent cloud passages, potentially creating holes or dense patches in random patterns. The hypothesis that the moon produced this luminous phenomenon is quite probable, but it is very difficult to confirm this hypothesis without a photo or additional testimony. Despite the possibility of this hypothesis, the GEIPAN classified this case as "C" due to a lack of information.
