Summary: Observation of the silent movement of a red-orange ball: probable observation of the Thai lantern.
--- seg-22f63fe97ff720622b3656e7 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 13, 2012 at 12:09 AM, a witness observed from their window a silent passage across the sky of a bright, orange-red ball that disappeared behind clouds after a peculiar trajectory. As the witness specifies their observation direction very precisely, the cartographic reconstruction is accurate (see map). The SO observation direction is, roughly, the direction of the light easterly wind: the witness therefore had the wind at their back. An object in flight, carried by the wind, then appears to move very little laterally; this hypothesis was retained. The color of the described object resembles a Thai lantern, which could have been released at the end of a small private party, common during these summer evenings. However, the observation duration, estimated at 20 or 30 seconds, seems short. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", a probable observation of the passage of a Thai lantern.
