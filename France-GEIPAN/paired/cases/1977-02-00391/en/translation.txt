Description: On February 2, 1977, around 8:45 PM, a witness observed a luminous phenomenon through binoculars for approximately three minutes. He described a circular shape in two parts: the upper part was bright and yellow, and the lower part had a vivid red flame. The phenomenon then seemed to rotate on itself, taking an oval shape without the colors changing in intensity. The witness resumed his activities before the phenomenon disappeared. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, and we lack information about it.
