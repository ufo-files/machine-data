Description: On February 17, 2012, around 8:20 a.m., a driver and her daughter were intrigued by the presence of a bright, silver-white object in the sky. The intense light made it difficult for the witness to accurately describe the shape (rather rectangular and quite flat) and the large size of the object. The observation of the PAN (UFO), which was stationary and silent, lasted approximately 20 seconds. To maneuver, the witness momentarily took her eyes off the UFO for 3 seconds. Upon looking back, the witness could not find the object in the sky. No other testimonies about the phenomenon were received at the GEIPAN. The GEIPAN dispatched an investigator to look into the case: dozens of people were interviewed, but none confirmed the events. Nevertheless, at 8:30 a.m. that morning, there were people taking children to school. The cognitive interview yielded no additional information beyond the witness's initial report. This brief observation from a car, without any possible cross-referencing, is found to be insufficiently consistent. Given that the witness was facing the sun, the observation could be a reflection of a bright object in the car's windshield or a reflection of the sun on a flying helicopter, but this hypothesis could not be confirmed. Due to the lack of other testimonies or additional elements, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" (unclassified) due to insufficient information and cross-references.
