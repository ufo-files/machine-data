Description: On Saturday, August 20, 2022, at 9:40 PM, a witness and his partner observed the silent movement of a luminous phenomenon in the sky. The witness described a very long "form that resembled a mesh bracelet." Only one testimony was collected. The witness observed the Starlink satellite train, resulting from the August 19, 2022 launch of the Falcon-9 rocket by SpaceX. For more information, refer to the GEIPAN site: a succession of glowing points (click on the Starlink icon) as well as on the Sky Misinterpretations site, via the Starlink Satellites article. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A.
