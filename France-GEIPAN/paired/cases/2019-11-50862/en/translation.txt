--- seg-f6b75f9d0e9923f562fc4383 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 21, 2019, at 05:10, a witness, upon opening their shutters, observed in the sky of Guadeloupe a bright white light. Then, they noticed several other lights, possibly several dozen, at high altitude. All of them moved in the same direction at a constant speed, without any noise. The lights followed each other at regular intervals. Only one testimony was collected. The witness had observed the satellites of the Starlink constellation launched on November 11, 2019, by the American rocket SpaceX. They were observed that evening in Guadeloupe by several witnesses. The local press reported the event, see the RCI website. See also the passage of the satellites on the Heaven-Above website. And some information on the Starlink constellation. The GEIPAN case is classified as A (unidentified) observation of a satellite constellation.
