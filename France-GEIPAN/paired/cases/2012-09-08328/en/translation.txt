--- seg-49f057183b1e99b06cecb697 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 17, 2012, from 6:50 to 7:20 AM, a witness was intrigued by the presence of two luminous phenomena in the sky. The witness took two photographs: one towards the East and the second towards the South. The witness undoubtedly observed towards the South the planet Jupiter, of magnitude -2.5 (i.e., much brighter than the stars of the Big Dipper, of magnitude 1.7), and he saw towards the East the planet Venus, of magnitude -4.7 (i.e., even brighter), which became invisible at sunrise. (See the corresponding sky maps.) The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of Venus and Jupiter.
