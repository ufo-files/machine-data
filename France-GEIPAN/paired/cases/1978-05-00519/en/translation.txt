Description: On May 22, 1978, around 0:15 a.m., a motorist and his passengers observed a very bright, white-silver ball in the sky. The witnesses reported that a beam of light emanating from this ball struck their vehicle on the left rear. No traces were found on the vehicle. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
