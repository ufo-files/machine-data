Summary: Observation of a flaming object followed by a trail on an oblique trajectory towards the ground: probable atmospheric re-entry.
--- seg-9dc6f53b5a09da7c0b31d54c [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 12, 1989, around 7:20 PM, a woman observed the passage towards the ground of a flaming object followed by a trail that changed color from yellow to red before fading out. She heard a deep noise at the presumed moment of impact. At the supposed location of the fall, the gendarmes found nothing. It is likely the atmospheric reentry of a meteorite.
