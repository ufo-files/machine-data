Summary: Observation of the Saturn-Jupiter conjunction.
--- seg-216d576b7bf7f76437eb20b4 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Between February 12 and March 11, 1981, several witnesses observed at night and at the same times a light in the sky that seemed unusual. This bright point moved very slowly in the same direction, from east to southeast, and appeared to flicker. A check using an astronomy software allows us to affirm that the witnesses likely saw the conjunction of Saturn and Jupiter, which was particularly visible at the beginning of the year.
