--- seg-8639481cb6306c2f1904686c [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 3, 1957 at 20:22 (UTC) three witnesses in flight south of Marengo (now Hadjout, Algeria) observed for 15 seconds a glowing object with a green then red trail crossing the sky on a regular descending trajectory from south to north east. Only one testimony was collected. The GEIPAN classified this case B: probable passage of a meteoroid.
