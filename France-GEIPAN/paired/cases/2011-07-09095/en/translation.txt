Description: During a hike in mid-mountain on July 18, 2011 at 6:05 PM, two individuals were intrigued by the silent movement across the sky of eight silver V-shaped objects. The objects appeared to fly in various formations. One witness took photographs. Only one testimony was collected. The analysis of the testimonies, and especially the photographs (see the investigation report), leads to the conclusion that all conditions were present for the witnesses to observe and photograph simple white birds flying at a relatively high altitude. They could be leucophae gulls in post-nuptial migration. The range of possible flying speeds reconstructed from the photos supports this hypothesis. The possibility of these birds flying at that time and place is verified through ornithological data. The aspect (formation, color, number) is consistent. The bird's flight is usually flapping, but it can glide like a raptor. The witnesses' lack of perception of wingbeats can also be explained by the observation distance. At approximately 600 meters, it seems difficult (with white birds contrasting less with the blue sky background than if they had been black) to clearly perceive wingbeats. The individual V-shaped aspect reversed compared to the flight direction is not consistent, but for the same reasons, it is difficult to imagine that the witnesses could have clearly distinguished the individual "V" shape of each bird, despite the witness mentioning that the PANS ("Pans" likely refers to "flying objects" or "unidentified flying objects") were "clearly visible." Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: birds.
