Description: On September 24, 1990, around 10:15 PM, five witnesses were intrigued by three glowing white-yellow orbs with blurred outlines, as if seen through the clouds. A sixth witness, residing in a nearby town, stated that the lights were the reflection of three large spotlights placed on the ground onto the clouds.
