Summary: Observation of an object moving slowly at a constant speed
--- seg-81acc573394c560cb9939381 [needs-review] ---
Description: Around 6 a.m. on April 12, 1982, several witnesses claim to have seen a luminous phenomenon in the sky. In an oval shape and orange in color, an object flies at an extremely high altitude but very slowly. Throughout the observation, which lasted approximately 10 minutes, the object did not change shape or color. Its speed remains constant. No other information was gathered on this phenomenon.
