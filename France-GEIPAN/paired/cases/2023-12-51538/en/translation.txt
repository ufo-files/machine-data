Description: On December 3, 2023, the witness, outside their home in the commune of Maubec (38), observed for approximately 20 seconds a PAN of pale yellow color, of low intensity and without trail. It moved from east to west on a nearly horizontal straight trajectory. The consistency* of this observation case is average, due to a single witness testifying five months after the observation, without photo or video support of the PAN. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The investigation concluded (see the investigation report) that the witness likely observed a "slow" meteor, a rare phenomenon but one that has been characterized before. This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- The elongated shape characteristic of a meteor entering the atmosphere, accompanied by a short trail integrated into the main body.
- The pale yellow diffuse color and low brightness, perfectly consistent with those observed for a small meteor.
- The straight-line movement following a nearly horizontal and flat trajectory, with an observation duration of approximately twenty seconds. Regarding this last point, although this trajectory and observation duration (potentially overestimated due to the late testimony) are not the most common, a similar case has already been documented in France on October 7, 2021, and was recorded by several cameras of the FRIPON network (www.fripon.org).

The GEIPAN classifies this observation case as "B": probable observation of a meteor.
