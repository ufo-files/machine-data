--- seg-14ca81dff21c27fec464bbbf [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 12, 2013, at 12:08 a.m., an entire family observed the silent movement of three very bright orbs: the first two followed a linear trajectory and then ascended into the clear sky, while the third moved above the houses, changing color. The three phenomena disappeared at the same time. This testimony of orange or colored, slow-moving orbs in the sky clearly corresponds to the observation of Thai lanterns. The weather report for the day indicates a light and variable wind, oriented more southwest at the time of observation, which corresponds to the overall movement observed towards the east. The irregular movement is not surprising given the light wind in an urban coastal area, due to the complexity of various urban air currents of thermal or marine origin. A video posted on YouTube at the time fully supported this hypothesis. The tourist location, the summer period, and the time of day are all quite conducive to the release of lanterns during celebrations. The witness is not convinced by this hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of Thai lanterns.
