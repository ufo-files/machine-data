Summary: Observation of a Boeing during landing phase
--- seg-5412dfa3f2cd60486ce06803 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Thursday, November 25, 2004, a witness driving a car saw an object of triangular shape estimated to be about ten meters in size. The object was stationary above the trees and had 3 fixed white, red, and blue lights. The object then moved, causing the witness to flee, who did not otherwise hear any noise. The phenomenon was identified as a commercial airliner at low altitude in the landing phase that was forced to make a turn before landing. The triangle observed by the witness is most likely the Boeing 738 seen from 3/4 rear.
