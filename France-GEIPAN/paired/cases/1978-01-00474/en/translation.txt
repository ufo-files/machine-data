Description: On January 7, 1978, around 4:50 a.m., a witness riding a moped observed three very bright lights near the ground. One of these very powerful lights separated from the group and approached the witness, following them until the entrance to the neighboring village, before disappearing. No noise was heard. No helicopter flights were conducted in this area that night. This phenomenon remains unidentified.
