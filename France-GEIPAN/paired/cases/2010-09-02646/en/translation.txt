Summary: Observation of a captive balloon in the shape of a dirigible.
--- seg-f464c03d8a88a6a390a128ce [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 24, 2010, motorists traveling on the A11 (direction Le Mans-Paris) noticed a stationary object in the sky that intrigued them. The witnesses, upon arriving at the rest area of Sargé-lés-Le-Mans, photographed a structure resembling a type of airship, dark gray in color, without any mooring point, appearing immobile throughout the observation period. As they resumed their journey, they lost sight of the balloon. A statement from another witness revealed that it was a captive balloon (connected by a long cable) located above the Auvours military camp, situated in Champagné (72).
