--- seg-9f8556df6a70a9eb531d1861 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Saturday, September 3, 2011, around 11:15 PM or 11:30 PM, a witness and his wife observed several orange glowing lights silently moving across the sky in the south-eastern horizon. During the approximately 2-minute observation, several more orange glowing lights appeared with the same flight characteristics. Of low strangeness and very good consistency due to a particularly detailed questionnaire, this observation case very likely corresponds to an observation of multiple Thai lanterns in the process of extinguishing. The witness, informed of this hypothesis, viewed photos and videos of lanterns and perfectly recognized the object. This case is classified A: Observation of Thai lanterns.
