Summary: Multiple observations of the rapid passage of a multicolored and luminous phenomenon in the sky: probable observation of the atmospheric reentry of a natural meteoroid.
--- seg-3677781d87b5bd7fc4801975 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 31, 2012, around 10 PM, several witnesses observed the rapid passage of a luminous and colored phenomenon in the sky. Witnesses in Calmont (31) and Castelnaudary (11) provided more detailed reports. The testimony of the Calmont witness is under particular investigation. This phenomenon does not correspond to any listed space object (debris) that could have entered the atmosphere. The REFORME network (meteor observers) recorded this meteor, which is very likely a natural meteoroid: indeed, the observation duration, less than 10 seconds, suggests a speed well exceeding 7 km/s, which is the re-entry speed of debris from rockets or satellites. This case is classified as "B": probable atmospheric re-entry of a natural meteoroid.
