Description: On October 1, 1987, around 2 a.m., a driver and his son observed several distant lights several meters away in the countryside. This same observation was repeated during three successive stops. The investigation will demonstrate that these are the headlights and two 40-watt spotlights of a tank truck specifically equipped in the rear for milk collection.
