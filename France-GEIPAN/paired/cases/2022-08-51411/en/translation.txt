Description: On August 21, 2022, the witness was on a family outing at the beach in CARRY-LE-ROUET (13) in the afternoon. He is piloting a recreational drone equipped with a camera. He observes a posteriori the presence of the PAN in the video recorded at 16:07. Through the use of IPACO software, an authentication and photo analysis tool specifically adapted to the needs of GEIPAN, the investigation was able to demonstrate that the PAN observed was a cluster of dandelion seeds carried by the wind (see the investigation report). This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- Shape consistent with that of a cluster
- White color
- Rectilinear movement in the direction of the wind, from northwest
- The period of dispersion of these seeds is consistent with the date of the observation. These seeds may likely come from one of the hills visible in the background of the video, heavily vegetated. Classification A: Observation of a cluster of dandelion seeds.
