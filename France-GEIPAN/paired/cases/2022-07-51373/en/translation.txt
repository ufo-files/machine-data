Description: On the 22nd of July, 2022, around 11 PM, several people were in a garden. A witness noticed a light in the sky and alerted the others. The witnesses then observed a black mass moving silently along a straight upward trajectory. The phenomenon disappeared in the distance. Only one testimony was collected. This case has poor consistency: only one testimony lacking essential information (see the investigation report): 
- The exact address of the observation location. The witness only provides "Monks' Quarter in Cornebarrieu."
- Azimuthal coordinates of the appearance and disappearance of PAN (the witness states for the appearance: "appeared on my right when I am facing the row of trees at the back of the garden" and for the disappearance: "the object disappeared on the left, (landmark: Cornebarrieu village church)," information that cannot be utilized without knowing the witness's position.
- Details about the red/orange lights "under" PAN. The GEIPAN classifies this case as C due to the lack of information.
