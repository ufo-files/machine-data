Description: On August 22, 1979, at approximately 10:45 PM, two witnesses observed a bright point in the sky that intrigued them. Initially fixed at a distance of 100 to 150 meters directly below the golf course, it then moved towards the West while dimming. The witness subsequently observed the point with binoculars and stated they saw two yellow parallelograms. No other testimonies regarding this phenomenon were collected. The various observed shapes could be due to aberrations of the binoculars, common when dealing with low-quality binoculars, and observing a bright point. It is likely that the observed phenomenon was a sunlit airplane.
