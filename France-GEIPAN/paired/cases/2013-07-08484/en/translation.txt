Description: A witness reports their observation made on July 11, 2013 at 10:30 PM: in the sky, they witness the appearance of three bright orange lights that move silently from northeast to south-west, then disappear one by one after changing direction towards the west. Unfortunately, the videos taken barely capture the bright points. However, the witness attaches a screenshot photograph to their questionnaire. No other testimonies were collected. This very unusual observation of several bright orange lights on similar trajectories inevitably evokes Thai lanterns. However, the observed movement (northeast to south-west, then west) does not exactly match the north wind recorded in Dijon (50 km) or Mâcon (60 km), but rather aligns with that recorded in Paray le Monial (70 km) (northeast wind). The change in direction could be explained by local relief effects, or by a slight imprecision or illusion in the witness's perception of the lights' direction. Due to the lack of very local wind data, the GEIPAN classifies this unusual case as "C".
