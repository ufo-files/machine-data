Description: On October 12, 2017, at 06:20, a witness observed from the window of their kitchen for 1 to 2 seconds a very bright phenomenon in the sky consisting of about a dozen blue objects in a descending trajectory. The PAN disappeared, hidden by trees. Only one testimony was collected on this phenomenon. The description of the PAN could suggest a meteor or an emergency rocket (see the investigation report). The first hypothesis can be eliminated as no detection was made by dedicated networks and there were clouds in the direction of the PAN. Of moderate strangeness and low consistency (single witness, no photos), this case could also be a possible misidentification with an emergency rocket. The hypothesis of a privately launched emergency rocket is more coherent, but cannot be formally validated due to the single witness. In any case, it cannot be a rocket launched from the fort of Nogent, near the observation location, as the use of fireworks is prohibited there. The case is classified "C", non-identifiable case due to lack of reliable data, although the hypothesis of an emergency rocket is favored.
