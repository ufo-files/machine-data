Summary: Observation of a nightclub laser projector
--- seg-9a99da29e6a9140fd4516821 [needs-review] ---
Description: During the night of August 12 to 13, 1994, between 1:00 and 1:30 a.m., four witnesses reported observing a spinning white ball along the road for approximately 14 km. The investigation quickly demonstrated that it was the observation of a "skyrose" laser projector being tested by a nightclub located about 6 km from the observation site.
