Summary: Observation of a pyramid-shaped object at five meters from the ground. High-pitched voices and incomprehensible words heard; severe headaches: lack of information and cross-references.
--- seg-a299ae756df5547e54f80c48 [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 1st, 2008, at 10:15 PM, a young adolescent outside his home heard "sharp" voices and words he didn't recognize before seeing a "non-identified object suspended in the air five meters from the ground." The witness described the "dark-colored" object as having a "pyramidal" shape, great size, and a large beam of light on top. Different colors were also seen. The object remained static. The witness believes he stood in front of this vision for perhaps 15 or 20 minutes. He was then blinded by a "big flash," and upon reopening his eyes, the object had disappeared. The witness then experienced severe headaches that dissipated upon his brother's arrival. Another witness reported to the gendarmerie that on the same evening, in the same area, between 9:00 PM and 10:00 PM, his dog had barked abnormally, unlike its usual behavior in the presence of visitors. This observation is of a high degree of strangeness as it involves a close observation of an absolutely unknown phenomenon, coupled with the hearing of sounds entirely unknown. The witness and his close relatives gave a deposition at the gendarmerie. The gendarmes conducted a very serious investigation and found no traces on the ground or confirmations of aircraft flights in the area at that time. The GEIPAN contacted the witness by phone in 2011 to inquire if he had any new information about his experience, or if it had happened again; but there was nothing new on these subjects. This observation report has none of the characteristics of a hoax. The GEIPAN searched local press to see if there had been other similar reports in the region at that time. The GEIPAN consulted a doctor who noted that, "in ophthalmic migraine, visual phenomena (sparkling points, geometric shapes...) precede intense headaches, which seems to be the case for this young man. The crisis occurs abruptly, without initiators. It can be impressive and very anxiety-inducing." Age and sex are consistent with this diagnosis. This hypothesis cannot be confirmed but must be considered. As in all cases of very strange observations by a single witness, it is very difficult to confirm the reality of the observed phenomenon, even if the witness is considered sincere. Given that the account contains certain elements characteristic of a transient hallucination, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of concrete elements and cross-references.
