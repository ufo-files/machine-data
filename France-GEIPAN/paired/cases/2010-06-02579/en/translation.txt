Description: On June 5, 2010, around 11:55 PM, numerous witnesses observed the movement of a column of orange balls. The review of the single questionnaire and the characteristics of the observation described led the investigation towards the observation of Thai lanterns. Contacted by GEIPAN, the Communication Service of the Town Hall of Tournan-en-Brie confirms this conviction by specifying that a wedding took place in their municipality with a release of Thai lanterns at midnight on June 5.
