Description: On September 23, 2022, between 01:20 and 01:30 AM, a witness, staying at Bagnères-de-Bigorre (65), reported observing a particularly bright PAN from their residence window. Initially thinking it was the Moon, they approached the window after five minutes and saw a pale yellow light ball, surrounded by a thin brown and yellow outline, moving horizontally to the left. The object appeared to float in the sky, at eye level, above the street. The object disappeared after a brief moment. This enigmatic observation lasted approximately 5 seconds. Only one testimony was collected. Since the GEIPAN was unable to contact the witness or obtain additional information through the technical questionnaire, the investigation relies solely on the Gendarmerie's incident report as the information source. Consequently, due to the absence of critical data such as azimuths, elevations of the PAN, its trajectory, brightness, apparent size, noise, etc., this case is classified in category "C", indicating an unidentified phenomenon due to insufficient data.
