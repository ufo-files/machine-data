Description: On February 8, 2015, between midnight and 12:20 a.m., a witness briefly spotted through their window a flickering red light moving silently from right to left across their field of view. The object suddenly changed direction and disappeared very quickly. The observation of a red light less than 30 meters away, moving at a constant altitude, in less than a minute on a weekend night, strongly suggests a Thai lantern carried by the wind, possibly released nearby after a private party. The witness described the object's movement as "from southwest to southeast." Upon verification with the witness, however, it appears they were looking towards west-southwest (see photo), and the movement from right to left was actually from north-northwest (N-NO) to south-southeast (S-SE), which is consistent with a north wind recorded at Roissy. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," a probable observation of a Thai lantern or possibly a glowing balloon. The weather report for the day of the observation: Meteociel at Roissy.
