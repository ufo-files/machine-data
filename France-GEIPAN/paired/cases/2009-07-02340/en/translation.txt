Summary: Observation of luminous phenomena moving and changing direction: Lack of information
--- seg-008ab7cbb835a2839e058fc5 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of July 4, 2009, between approximately 12:30 AM and 12:43 AM, a witness observing from a parking lot saw a luminous phenomenon above a neighboring building for 2 to 3 minutes. The sky was clear, and the witness described white-colored fireballs. The phenomenon moved silently along a trajectory from S to N, then stopped and changed direction before disappearing rapidly to the East. The witness filmed the scene on his mobile phone. Unfortunately, the videos from this case are unusable, and the two photographs extracted by the witness from the video are also unusable. No other testimony regarding the observation of this phenomenon was collected at GEIPAN. GEIPAN classified this case as "C" due to lack of information.
