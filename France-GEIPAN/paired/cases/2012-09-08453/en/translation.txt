Summary: Observation of the movement in the sky of a white ball resembling a cloud: lack of information.
--- seg-06ac5752a8247af54e6ba92b [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 16, 2012, at 4:40 PM, a walker observed a white ball that looked like a cloud moving towards him, while remaining high in the sky. As the object approached, it was estimated to be one centimeter in size, but it enlarged to about three times its volume. The witness took a photograph. The GEIPAN received its testimony in May 2013. The GEIPAN analyzed the hypothesis of a wind-carried object. Unfortunately, there is no nearby weather station in Gondrecourt. Weather reports at Saint-Dizier (52) 45 km away, and Nancy Essey (55 km away) show extremely variable wind conditions, both in direction (NE, SO, S, SE, etc.) and speed (4 to 25 km/h). It is therefore impossible to assert that this object was carried by the wind, although it is highly probable. The change in brightness of the object from the zenith, where it appears white in the photo, to the horizon where it appears gray, points more towards the hypothesis of a fairly low object, rather than the hypothesis of a cloud. Furthermore, the witness clearly sees the object grow in size throughout the observation, which supports the hypothesis of an object at very low altitude. If the object was at 100m altitude, a 12km/h wind would have taken it across half the sky (90°) in one minute, covering 200m. The described shape could be compatible with a down ball, perhaps carried away from a nest, or an insect cloud. These are only hypotheses that cannot be confirmed. Due to the lack of local wind data and the inability to estimate the altitude of the object, GEIPAN classifies this case as "C": insufficient precise information to provide a consolidated opinion.
