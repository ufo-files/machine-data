Summary: Observation of silent presence in the sky of luminous phenomena with color alternation then movement: probable observation of LED balloons.
--- seg-5abf5eed754fb4a89d536de1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 3, 2015, around 10:15 PM, a witness closing their shutters observed three teardrop-shaped objects above their neighbor's trees. The alternating colors of red, blue, and green are different for each object. At first, they remain stationary for several minutes, then they move away towards the South. The witness films his observation. His son and his wife are driving along the coastline to continue their observation with binoculars. No noise was heard during the 10 to 15 minutes of observation. Only one testimony is collected. According to the attached investigation report, an aeronautical phenomenon is possible but unlikely, given the blue-green-red color alternation of the PAN, which does not correspond to any type of aeronautical position lights. The observation of a Thai lantern is also unlikely due to the 3 colors observed in alternation. The observation of 3 distinct PAN described by the witness cannot be confirmed by the video analysis, where the distinction of 3 __UFO_PROTECTED_000__s is not evident. This video shows that the PAN did indeed move away, and corresponds to the witness's description providing a phase where the PAN appears to move very little then slowly moves towards the South. The data regarding apparent size and brightness is not confirmed by the video. Indeed, the streetlamps visible in the video, as well as in the images extracted from the video, allow for a comparison-based assessment of brightness. However, the video was not taken at the beginning of the observation, so it is possible that the brightness observed at the beginning was greater. This dimming would also confirm a southward movement of the PAN. Following the analysis of these data and the visit to the witness, the most probable hypothesis is the LED balloon or a group of 3 LED balloons. Indeed, the alternation of the 3 colors at regular intervals, the slow movement with the wind, as well as the absence of sound, correspond to this type of object. Additionally, the observation was made on a Saturday night between 10:00 PM and 11:00 PM, by the seaside. This increases the probability that a festive event took place that evening. The comparison with images extracted from the witness's video, and several examples of LED balloon photographs also support this (see the investigation report). As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: LED balloons.
