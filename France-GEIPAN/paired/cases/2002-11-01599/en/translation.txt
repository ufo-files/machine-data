Summary: Probable observation of a public laser projector
--- seg-2524c9d9a0b692fcf9a72f6a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 8, 2002, from 23:45 to 00:15, a couple of witnesses observed a luminous phenomenon consisting of a dozen glowing points arranged in an oval shape and rotating counterclockwise. At the center of the phenomenon, there were half a dozen fixed glowing points. No noise was heard. Based on their description, it is likely, although the investigation could not confirm it, that the witnesses saw from a great distance the effects of a public laser projector.
