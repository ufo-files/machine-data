Description: On September 14, 1985, at 5:00 PM, a witness observed for one hour an object in the shape of a dome with lights inside. Much later in the evening, using binoculars, the object appeared to be followed by an incandescence. The observation was also made with binoculars by gendarmes and the director of an observatory: unfortunately, fog prevented any telescope observation. No other information was gathered about this phenomenon.
