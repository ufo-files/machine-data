Description: The witness is on a boat on the Oise when, on August 16, 2014 at 9:55 PM for 10 to 20 seconds, they observe a black, rectangular flying object with rounded corners. The object disappears behind trees. The object moves silently in a straight line, from Cergy to Pontoise, that is, from West to East. It is the beginning of night (sunset at 9:03 PM). The Meteociel report indicates a light West wind of 6 to 18 km/h (see weather . This observation can be explained by a child's balloon, inflated with helium, carried by the wind. These balloons have very diverse shapes, and some may appear rectangular from certain perspectives. GEIPAN cannot confirm this hypothesis; the date of the observation (end of summer weekend) is compatible with this hypothesis. GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": probable observation of a festive balloon.
