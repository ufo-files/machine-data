Description: On May 7, 2008 at 1:30 AM, two witnesses observed a round, white object at an altitude and distance they could not gauge. This object moved quickly from east to west on a slightly descending trajectory. No noise was heard. Only one of the witnesses provided a statement. These individuals likely observed an atmospheric reentry of a meteorite.
