Description: On December 10, 2025, at approximately 03:05, a witness observed from their home in Huriel (Allier) a luminous phenomenon for about twenty seconds. The observation took place at night, in a rural environment without light pollution, under a fully overcast sky with intermittent precipitation. The witness, who had previously noticed several low-altitude military aircraft passes, described a very intense white light and a red light moving slowly, silently, with a sparkling appearance. The object was perceived as close, with an apparent size comparable to that of a small vehicle or a supermarket cart. The case has average consistency, the investigation relying on a single, undocumented testimony. *CONSISTENCY: According to the GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The investigation quickly established that the observation coincided with the national military exercise "Poker," mobilizing numerous aircraft from the Air and Space Force, such as Rafale fighters, Mirage 2000s, and A330 MRTT refueling planes. Aerospace tracking data and CAPCODA feedback confirm the passage of military aircraft in the area at the exact time of the incident (see the investigation report). The witness's descriptions perfectly match the nighttime lighting signal of a distant military aircraft. The absence of visibility of the fuselage in the darkness isolates the white landing lights and red anti-collision lights. Moreover, the humid and cloudy weather conditions generated optical turbulence and refraction, explaining the irregular sparkling and the fluctuating halo effect perceived as a flame or an air distortion. The impression of proximity and the difficulties in estimating size and speed are due to the intense light of the aircraft's headlights in complete darkness, a classic phenomenon leading to perceptual biases. Additionally, the low angular elevation of the object (estimated at around 5°) placed it in the visual reference frame of terrestrial objects, like trees, enhancing the illusion of its relative position. All technical, aeronautical, and meteorological elements thus converge towards the formal identification of the phenomenon. The case is classified in category B: probable observation of a military aircraft, very likely an A330 MRTT refueling plane operating within the ongoing exercise. Based on these information, the case is classified as "B": probable observation of a military aircraft.
