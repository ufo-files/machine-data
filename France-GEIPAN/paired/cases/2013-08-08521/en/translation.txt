Description: On Friday, August 2, 2013, around midnight, a family in a car was intrigued by the silent and rapid movement of a bright orange ball in the clear sky. The object disappeared, hidden by the landscape. The observation of an orange ball moving linearly on a weekend evening suggests a Thai lantern. At that time, a south wind of 4 to 19 km/h was noted in Mâcon: weather. The witness was surprised by the speed of the phenomenon: if a lantern really flies at 19 km/h in a straight line, it may be difficult to follow it in a car. The case is classified as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
