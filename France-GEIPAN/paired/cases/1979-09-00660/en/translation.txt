--- seg-2483a8addcc68a0ef92a6a86 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 20, 1979, around 11 PM, several people observed a red glowing phenomenon moving rapidly across the sky from West to East. A circle, approximately 4 meters in diameter, was crossed by two red diagonals. It flickered and made a loud noise. The object, which had turned white, disappeared behind the hills. No other testimonies about this phenomenon were collected, and we lack information about it.
