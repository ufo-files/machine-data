Description: On August 29, 2013, at 7:45 PM, a driver was surprised to witness two luminous phenomena not far from the setting sun. He took the time to stop and photograph the phenomenon. This testimony is of very good consistency: precise and with 5 usable photos (see the investigation report). Based on the investigation elements, namely: - appearance, color of the UFOs. - the sun low on the horizon and UFOs at the same height. - presence of clouds conducive to the formation of parhelia, we can conclude that the UFO observed by the witnesses is very likely a parhelion phenomenon. The observation is ultimately not very strange as the objects are easily explicable and their presence demonstrable. This case is to be classified as "A" as a certain observation of a parhelion.
