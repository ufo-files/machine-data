Description: On April 26, 1981, around 10 PM, several witnesses observed a strange luminous phenomenon in the sky. A red glowing ball accompanied by a few flickers moved from East to West. The phenomenon lasted for a few seconds before changing shape to resemble a beam of light and suddenly disappearing. These individuals likely witnessed an atmospheric reentry.
