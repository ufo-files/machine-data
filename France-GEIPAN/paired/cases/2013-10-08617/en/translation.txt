Description: On October 28, 2013, at 10:50 PM, a witness driving on D342 in the commune of Taluyers observed a red light in the clear sky through their rearview mirror. They made a second observation at 11:03 PM on D63 at the Rosseon sign (commune of Mornant): an orange, spherical object with a tail quickly crossed their field of vision through the windshield of their car. Other people were on the road, but no other testimonies were collected. With average strangeness and low consistency (single witness, poor quality testimony, date uncertainty), this case is not precise enough from each observation to be formally identified. It is not the visual perception of the witness that is in question, but the interpretation the witness makes of their observation through their feelings (surprise, stress, fatigue, driving at night). This case is classified C, due to lack of data.
