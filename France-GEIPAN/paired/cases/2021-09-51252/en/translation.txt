**Description:** On September 11, 2021 at 12:45 AM, a witness, from their 3rd-floor apartment in Nice (06), observed a very bright and stationary PAN (Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon) at the top of trees, heading towards the Castellet hill in Villefranche-sur-Mer. The witness conducted a visual observation and took photographs of the PAN, then ended their observation at 1:45 AM. Another person present that night reported observing the PAN still present at 4:00 AM, but chose not to testify. According to GEIPAN criteria, the consistency of this testimony is excellent. The witness took photographs of the PAN and conducted their own investigation, which precisely established the observation axis and the PAN's position above a rental villa. Throughout the investigation (as detailed in the investigation report), we demonstrated that the primary witness likely observed powerful spotlights mounted on a pole to illuminate the area. This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- Similar appearance to one or more construction site spotlights mounted on a telescopic or hydraulic pole.
- Very high brightness (similar to construction site spotlights).
- Phenomenon observed near the ground; measurements and calculations determine it was approximately 10/15 meters above the ground.
- The PAN did not change appearance and remained stationary during a long observation period.
- Precise measurements taken by the witness and confirmed by the investigation demonstrate that the PAN is located above a villa, usually rented by the owners. In the garden, a clear, flat space provides ideal conditions for installing a telescopic pole positioned directly in the observation axis.
- The observation was made on a Saturday night to Sunday morning, a propitious time for a festive event. However, we found no evidence of a festive event at this property on the date of the observation. No other testimonies were reported, as the neighborhood may be familiar with these occasional festive events.

GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable observation of lighting from spotlights mounted on a pole.
