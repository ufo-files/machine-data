Description: The witness observes a large, bright light in the shape of a disk, emitting very bright white light. The very fast movement follows a straight trajectory from north to south (NO-SE) at a constant altitude. The very bright disk crossing the sky in 8 to 10 seconds clearly suggests re-entry into the atmosphere of a satellite debris or, more likely, a meteoroid (meteorite). This phenomenon, called a bolide, is very spectacular. The BOAM network did not record this specific event, but reports numerous similar occurrences on the same night; this is the period of several meteor showers, in particular the Eta Aquarids, which is the most intense of this time of year. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a bolide, caused by atmospheric re-entry of a natural object.
