Summary: Long observation of a luminous, opaque, white ovoid object at the zenith: movement towards the East: observation of a technological stratospheric balloon.
--- seg-742a73fdd939a4c6401d182b [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 12, 2017, at 5:15 p.m. (local time), a witness at their residence observed a very bright, opaque, white, oval-shaped object at the zenith. The witness observed the PAN with binoculars in a clear sky: they initially thought it was a weather balloon but were surprised as the object was nearly motionless. No unusual noises were heard. The witness and a patrol from the gendarmerie unit observed the phenomenon until 6:15 p.m., when it moved towards the East. The witness took a photograph (original not transmitted to GEIPAN). Only one testimony was collected. For both the witness and the gendarmes, this observation resembled the passage of a "weather balloon". This hypothesis was verified with the Air Transport Brigade in Lamentin (972). After consulting the "Flight Radar" site, it was determined to be a balloon deployed in Puerto Rico that was pushed towards Martinique by high-altitude winds. These balloons are part of the Loon project, which aims to provide internet access to disaster-stricken and/or difficult-to-reach areas using high-altitude balloons. Following the passage of Hurricane Maria in September 2017 over Puerto Rico, it was decided to use a fleet of balloons to allow Puerto Ricans without cellular connectivity, due to the destruction of antennas, to use their phones' basic functions. The GEIPAN classified this case as 'A', observing a stratospheric balloon from Puerto Rico pushed towards Martinique by high-altitude winds.
