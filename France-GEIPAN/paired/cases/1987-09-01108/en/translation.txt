Summary: Multiple observations of the silent passage across the sky of a luminous phenomenon with a trail: mistaken for an artificial atmospheric reentry of space debris, specifically that of the Cosmos 1873 rocket.
--- seg-037295fb72794cc45bc968db [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 1, 1987, numerous witnesses in a vast geographical area between Guyana, Martinique, and Guadeloupe observed in the sky the movement of a particularly bright phenomenon, usually followed by a trail. The described phenomenon shares many common characteristics (observation duration, shape, size, movement) with a known astronautic phenomenon: an artificial re-entry into the atmosphere. This hypothesis had indeed been raised by American authorities and the GEPAN, but it could not be confirmed (see the investigation report). Additionally, a large-mass rocket stage, Cosmos 1873, was then in re-entry phase. Its trajectory aligns perfectly with the testimonies collected in the Antilles that day. It is not the visual perception of the witnesses that is in question, but the interpretation they make of their observations through their feelings (observation of a rare phenomenon, dating error for witnesses of LAMENTIN). The case is classified as A: misperception with an artificial re-entry of space debris, specifically that of the Cosmos 1873 rocket.
