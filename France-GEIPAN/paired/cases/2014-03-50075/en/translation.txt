--- seg-a2bc0a3110e5a01efb3c0f45 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 2, 2014, between 6:30 PM and 7:00 PM, a witness, while closing their shutters, observed a white glowing phenomenon moving slowly above the roofs of houses in the housing estate. The witness took photographs before the phenomenon was hidden by trees. The observation lasted approximately thirty minutes. No other testimonies were collected. The witness likely saw a light outside that surprised them that evening to the point of taking their camera. However, the strangeness underlying the testimony (and which is very likely its origin) lies in the rectangular and overlapping nature of the lights the witness saw in the photos taken a posteriori. The witness's photos showing these lights highlight a framing through a glass door and light rectangles formed by reflections of the rectangular flash of the Minolta camera used. The light responsible for the observation is practically not described in the testimony, which focuses on the rectangular strangeness resulting from a photo artifact. Therefore, the GEIPAN only concludes based on what is described and makes the strangeness experienced by the witness, and classifies as A: photographic reflection.
