Summary: Observation in the sky of the movement of a white bright point from West to East with progressive decrease in intensity: observation of the International Space Station (ISS).
--- seg-8319d40d6dea20a225c3d958 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Sunday, July 23, 2017 at 11:27 PM, a witness and his partner observed for less than a minute a slow-moving bright white light point on a west-east trajectory in a clear sky. The intensity of the point dimmed as it moved. Only one testimony was collected. The witness's observation is extremely precise in terms of date and sky position! It corresponds exactly to the passage of the International Space Station (ISS) recorded on Calsky. The ISS is indeed at its brightest when first spotted by the witness in southeast at a 70° altitude, then sets towards the east while fading in intensity. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Observation of the International Space Station (ISS).
