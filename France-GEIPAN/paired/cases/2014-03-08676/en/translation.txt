Description: On Saturday, March 15, 2014, at 11:43 PM, a witness observed the successive and silent passage of two large, identical objects flying over the roof of a supermarket. The witness describes blue balls, the size of a soccer ball, spaced 3 or 4 meters apart at the base of these flying objects. The first object has a straight trajectory, and the second object has an ascending trajectory with a change in direction. They disappear in different directions in a few seconds. The observation lasted 3 minutes. Only one testimony is collected. Given the information gathered in the attached investigation (see the investigation report), including:

- Unspecified number of UFOs (PAN) in each formation.
- Unspecified relative positions of UFOs (PAN) within the formations.
- Lack of angular measurements, both for the dimensions of the formations and for individual UFOs (PAN).
- Unclear attribution of UFO (PAN) colors.
- Lack of specifics regarding the "large Boeing"-shaped UFO compared to the blue balls.
- In the context of the disco laser hypothesis, no data on the potential use by the accused establishment of such equipment during the evening of 03/15/2014. The lack of information does not allow for a definitive conclusion in favor of any hypothesis. This case is classified as "C" for the observation of an unidentified phenomenon at this time, due to a lack of factual elements and cross-references. This GEIPAN testimony is of low consistency: vague though accompanied by a diagram depicting the observation, and coming from a single witness without a photo. The observation is not very strange as it could be entirely mundane objects.
