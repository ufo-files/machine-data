Description: The witness observed an airplane making a U-turn in mid-air, closely followed by an object positioned between its left wing and tail. Air traffic control was consulted on this case: the witness indeed saw an airplane, but a peculiar one, as it was an Airbus A350 from Airbus Industrie, on a test or demonstration flight, following an atypical trajectory: two loops over the Pyrenees before landing in Toulouse at 12:50. Note that Airbus is preparing to deliver its first A350 in the coming month. The point observed by the witness was a smaller airplane, a Corvette, conducting aerial photography (information obtained from Airbus Industrie). Note that the escort's position, south of the Airbus, is well-placed relative to the sun for photo and video shots. At the time of observation, the airplane was flying at approximately 10,000 meters altitude; the accompanying device appeared very close to the Airbus to the witness, but it is likely significantly higher than the airplane, allowing it to be seen from above. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A: certain observation of a large carrier airplane in a test or demonstration flight, accompanied by a light airplane conducting aerial photography.
