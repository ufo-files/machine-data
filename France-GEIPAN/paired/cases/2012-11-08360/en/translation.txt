--- seg-a85c76051fcaf66d17d5ac9b [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 5, 2012, around 7:30 PM, two witnesses observed in the sky for 1 minute a red glowing object with two flickering white lights in a hexagonal shape. Considering the objective elements presented in the investigation report, namely the observed lighting signal and the noise, which are consistent with those of an airplane, the trajectory along the axis of two nearby airports in the context of a landing or takeoff of a nearby tourist airplane, and the existence of an airport housing an aerobatic club with approved tourist airplanes for night flying, we can conclude that the PAN observed by the witnesses is probably a tourist airplane. This case is classified as "B" as a probable observation of a tourist airplane shortly after its takeoff or just before its landing from or to an airport located nearby.
