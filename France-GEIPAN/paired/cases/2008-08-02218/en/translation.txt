Description: On August 10, 2008, a GEIPAN was contacted by phone by a witness who recounted an observation made in the presence of others, including gendarmes. Between 11:00 PM and 2:17 AM on the night of August 10 to 11, 2008, two bright points of light, resembling stars, were observed with the naked eye and then with binoculars. The phenomenon was stationary before slowly moving. A similar observation in the same direction but slightly lower on the horizon occurred on August 22 at 10:35 PM. The investigation will demonstrate that the witnesses made an astronomical observation of Arcturus and Capella, particularly visible during this time of year.
