Description: On September 26, 2015, at 3:00 PM, a witness observed for approximately 5 minutes, in a clear blue sky, 5 white points slowly approaching without any noise. The formation continued to fly, then entered a cloud and disappeared from the witness's view. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of white points flying together in a blue sky, moving in the same direction as the wind, clearly suggests helium balloons, possibly released for a celebration. The objects appeared to come from Remoncourt, northeast of the observer. The wind direction recorded in Nancy is indeed a northeast wind (refer to the Météociel record). The date and time (Saturday at 3:00 PM) could correspond to a wedding (there was indeed a wedding in Remoncourt on September 26). The GEIPAN investigation was not pursued to verify this hypothesis. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of helium balloons.
