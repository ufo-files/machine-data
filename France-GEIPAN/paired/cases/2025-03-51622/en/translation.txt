Description: On Saturday, March 1, 2025, the witness, residing in ESPIRA-DE-L'AGLY (66), took their dogs out at 9:20 PM and observed for several minutes an aerial display of glowing rays in the sky, as well as two white discs. He specifies that everything is happening in the same location. The case consistency is good with only one witness, but numerous photos and videos of the event found on the web (see the investigation report and the video annex). *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The investigation identifies that at a distance of 8km from the observation direction, the Aimé Giral Stadium is located, where a rugby match was taking place that evening. During this event, a particularly impressive sound and light show is organized. The PAN is produced by the reflection of light beams from multiple skytracers (robotic projectors) onto clouds and fog. On the other hand, the distance between the witness and the spectacle did not allow him to hear the sounds. The description of the phenomenon corresponds to a ballet of several beams of light coming from the stadium. The smoke and fumes from fireworks created a screen as they ascended into the air, sometimes making parts of the beams visible. The presence of scattered clouds contributed to enhancing the brightness of the end-of-path beams, making white disks appear against the clouds (as evidenced by photos from another witness). The fan-like and then parallel arrangement of the beams, as described by the witness, was part of the spectacle. A video excerpt from that night's match, filmed by a spectator, shows very fast, fan-shaped light effects from inside the stadium. Thus, the witness precisely explains what corresponds to the light show performed by the company "Dimensions Events ®" (Light shows and fireworks, robotic projectors Lyres Led, beam and waterproof). The witness's home location relative to the Aimé Giral stadium should allow him to observe the phenomenon of the lights during the upcoming night rugby matches, if they are covered by the "Dimension Events" company, provided the sky is foggy, cloudy, or the smoke creates a favorable screen. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A": observation of projector reflections on clouds.
