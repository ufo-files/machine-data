Description: On April 10, 2014, around 6:00 PM, a witness observes a stationary bright object in the sky. The observation lasts for approximately thirty seconds before the object disappears instantly. Only one testimony was collected. A bright object observed in the west during the late afternoon suggests a reflection of the sun on an airplane or balloon. If it is an airplane, it could be a distant aircraft (more than 30 miles away) flying in a direction close to that of the witness. The sudden disappearance is likely explained by a change in the object's orientation relative to the sun: the reflection would then have disappeared, but not the object. This peculiar case is classified as "B," a probable observation of the setting sun's reflection on a distant high-altitude airplane (likely EZY434P according to Gasperflights).
