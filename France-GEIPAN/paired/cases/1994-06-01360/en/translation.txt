Testimonies: USSY-SUR-MARNE (77) 24.06.1994
--- seg-556282bb60e6a7eb92f870d2 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Summary: Observation of a stationary triangular shape
--- seg-e7485ccc56a41c02cff58be5 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 24, 1994, around 2:40 a.m., during a night patrol, the attention of three gendarmes was drawn to lights above the highway. As they approached, they realized these lights were coming from a stationary triangular object at an unspecified distance from the ground. After stopping their vehicle, the object gently approached and hovered above the gendarmes' car. When they started driving again, the object suddenly accelerated, becoming just a point in the sky. Without being able to pinpoint the exact moment, the gendarmes also noticed their communication equipment on board the vehicle had stopped working. The observation lasted approximately 10 minutes; no noise or smoke was observed. No explanation could be given for this phenomenon.
