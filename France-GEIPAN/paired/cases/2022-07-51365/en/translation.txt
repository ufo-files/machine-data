Description: On July 16, 2022, around 11:30 PM, while observing the sky, the witness and his friend were intrigued by the straight, low-altitude passage of a bright white round object. The PAN disappeared into the clouds. Only one testimony was collected. The consistency was average: a well-detailed testimony, but the two photos provided by the witness remain unusable (too blurry). The investigation concluded that the observation was very likely the passage of the ISS by the witness and his friend (see the investigation report). This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- The appearance was entirely consistent with that of the ISS (brightness, color, straight movement), which the witness also noted himself.
- The ISS was known to be present in the sky (23:44-23:51) about 15 minutes after the reported observation time by the witness. However, the witness gave three different mentions of the time (23:00-23:30 and around midnight). He confirmed the 23:30 timing by phone but three months after the observation. A 15-minute estimation error is not disqualifying.

The oddity mainly resides in the apparent size mentioned by the witness: that of the Moon. However, it is very likely that the witness refers to the appearance of the PAN in the photos, defocused and appearing circular, rather than an unaided visual estimate.

The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A", very probable observation of the ISS passage.
