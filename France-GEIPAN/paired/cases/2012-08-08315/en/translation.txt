Description: Two people at a regular fishing spot were intrigued by the presence in the clear sky of bright, stationary or rapidly moving objects, followed by trails. One person filmed and photographed the phenomena; they later reported their observation. The photos and videos clearly suggest airplane trails. Note that this morning, with likely very dry air and in the light of dawn, these trails appeared very bright and gave a particular impression of speed. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of airplane condensation trails.
