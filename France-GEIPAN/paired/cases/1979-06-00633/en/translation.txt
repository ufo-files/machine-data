--- seg-cf0d02a30d74a66ec22fb7c1 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 15, 1979, around 11 PM, several individuals will spot on the horizon an orange glow that intrigues them. This glow in the northwest appears to be composed of several bright points. The observation will last about ten minutes before everything goes dark. That evening, a ground-to-air firing training exercise took place at the nearby military camp located northwest of the witnesses' position between 11 PM and 11:30 PM. These people witnessed the glows resulting from these shots.
