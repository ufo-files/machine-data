--- seg-c8a30d361b4f6388c43c3c2a [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 1, 2009, around 0:40 a.m., a witness observed a moving light phenomenon through the window of his room. The witness reported that a red light point crossed the cloud layer, then descended slowly in a curve, followed a horizontal trajectory before stopping. It then dimmed, seemed to climb back into the cloud cover, and disappeared. No other testimonies were reported. The GEIPAN suggested that this observation could correspond to the launch of one or two distress rockets fired to celebrate the New Year. This hypothesis is plausible and fairly consistent with the observation and the observation date, but it is risky to confirm it without additional photos, videos, or testimonies. This case is classified as C due to a lack of information.
