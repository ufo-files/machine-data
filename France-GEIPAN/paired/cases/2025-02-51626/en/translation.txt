Description: On February 20, 2025, at 7:26 PM, while entering his home north of Valras-Plage (34), the witness observed for 2 to 3 seconds a luminous phenomenon. It consisted of two reddish-orange lights located at the front of a translucent, pink triangular shape, suddenly appearing and disappearing in the sky. The credibility of the testimony is average: while there is only one witness and no photos/videos, the investigation highlights the clarity of the information provided by the witness, as well as the confirmation of the observation date and time by a receipt provided. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, credibility refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. Several hypotheses were considered (see the investigation report): atmospheric reentry, aircraft, Starlink satellites, LED balloons or lanterns, and birds. None of the considered hypotheses could explain the observation. The atmospheric reentry hypothesis seems the most probable based on the characteristics provided by the witness, but it could not be confirmed by a recording or other testimony. The very limited observation duration and the absence of other witnesses did not allow for consolidated information on the trajectory and distance of PAN, and thus, the GEIPAN classifies the case as "C," an unidentified phenomenon due to a lack of data.
