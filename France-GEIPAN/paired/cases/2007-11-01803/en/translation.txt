Description: On November 26, 2007, between 6:20 PM and 6:35 PM, a witness, driving with her son, observed three yellow glowing points in a triangle formation with a red light at the center. The altitude appeared low, and the phenomenon was stationary. The witnesses perceived a rumbling sound. The driver slowed down to observe the phenomenon, and her son opened the window and leaned his head outside to get a better view. He then experienced intense headaches and a nervous breakdown, causing great fear in his mother. The investigation precisely established the witnesses' vehicle position at 46°46'39N and 1°13'48O, as well as the observation direction towards the NNE. Since the observation took place at an unspecified time between 6:20 PM and 6:35 PM local time, all aircraft movements in Nantes and La Roche-sur-Yon between 6:00 PM and 7:00 PM were reconstructed with the effective help of the Nantes DGAC services. Seven aircraft were identified at a sufficiently low level to be clearly distinguished from the ground (below FL020). The GEIPAN investigation conclusively establishes that the witnesses' observation was of the LXPRG flight at 6:27'16" (local time) approaching La Roche-sur-Yon Airport. Medical analysis did not provide any answers, so it is likely that the effects are related to the stress of an unexplained observation. The headache could have been triggered by the child's incorrect position when leaning his head out the moving car window, with fear completing the picture and stress communicated by the already anxious mother who was worried about her other younger son being home alone.
