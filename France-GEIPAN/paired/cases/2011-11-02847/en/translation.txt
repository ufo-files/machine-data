Description: A witness reports their observation made on November 19, 2011 at 22:08 above their residence. Several bright, yellow-orange lights move in a straight line from SE to NO. No noise was heard during the observation. The points disappeared as they gained altitude and moved at a very high speed. The description given by the witness immediately suggests Thai lanterns. The wind direction indicated by the weather (SE->NO) is consistent with the observation. The time and day (a Saturday evening) are also typical indicators of lantern releases. No search was conducted for a possible release point (wedding, party, etc.).
