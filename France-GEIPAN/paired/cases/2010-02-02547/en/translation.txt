Description: On February 27, 2010, at 1:58 PM, a witness observed a strange light phenomenon in the south-southwest direction. For 2 minutes, they saw a round, rainbow-colored glow and a white glow. No sounds were heard. The witness took photographs. The observation described in the report and the 2 photographs taken by the witness do not show anything unusual. They depict separate cirrus clouds, a well-known type to meteorologists, appearing as filaments mostly white in color. These clouds have a fibrous texture and a silky sheen. The 'flying objects' described and photographed by the witness correspond to the south direction of the sun through the cloud layer, which explains the rainbow light of the 'second object' corresponding to the visible spectrum of sunlight.
