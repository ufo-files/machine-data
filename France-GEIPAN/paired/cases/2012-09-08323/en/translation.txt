Summary: Observation of the slow and silent passage of an object in the sky: probable low-altitude helicopter.
--- seg-eb1cb6664c2735914c018f70 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 7, 2012, at 9 PM, a group of 4 individuals observed a bright point slowly and silently moving towards them in the dark sky. When the object changed trajectory, the witnesses noticed a dark, rounded shape. Only one statement was collected. This observation of a low-flying bright point suggests an aircraft at low altitude. An investigation was requested from military air traffic control: the radar traces (included) indicate low-altitude air traffic between 9:23 PM and 9:25 PM (Universal Time) in the direction of the witness's observation between Morangis and Brugny, coming from the West, in an area without an airport. The low altitude and the winding path suggest it was a helicopter operating in that area. However, the witness observed between 9:00 PM and 9:10 PM, and the observed object was moving towards the West, while the radar traces indicate a path coming from the West. Therefore, these traces do not correspond to the observation but confirm aerial activity in that zone. It can be assumed that a similar activity occurred 20 minutes earlier (rotation) but at too low an altitude to be detected by radars. The radar traces begin at 9:00 PM. If the observation took place just before 9:00 PM, the aerial activity would not have been recorded at that time. The silence observed during the observation is not surprising for a helicopter observed at 10 km (6.2 miles). Given these information and uncertainties, the GEIPAN classifies this case as unlikely in category B as a probable observation of a helicopter, due to the low strangeness of the phenomenon.
