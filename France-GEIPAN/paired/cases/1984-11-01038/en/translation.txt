Summary: Probable observation of an atmospheric reentry
--- seg-96d25cf1dc615e90bf70baa5 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 12, 1984, around 7 PM, several witnesses saw a white ball, about the size of a soccer ball, with a bright red tail approximately 2 meters long, moving slowly, silently, and at low altitude along a straight trajectory above the coastline for 5 to 6 seconds. This phenomenon exhibits characteristics consistent with an atmospheric reentry.
