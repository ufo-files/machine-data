Description: On November 18, 1995, around 7 PM, a witness observed large, circular lights rotating in the sky. Following an investigation, it was found that a nearby Opel garage had used a promotional laser light projector during a sales campaign. A test, conducted at the request of the investigators, confirmed the observation reported by the witness.
