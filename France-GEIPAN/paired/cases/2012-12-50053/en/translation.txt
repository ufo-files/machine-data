Summary: Paris sky observation of a stationary flight followed by a slow straight-line movement of a gray triangular object with flashing red lights: probable observation of a giant fantasy balloon equipped with LEDs.
--- seg-0ab169dd9ac45afe62d119a7 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 1, 2012, at 5:00 PM, a witness observed a stationary gray object in the Paris sky. The witness described the object as both triangular and like a spinning top with "a pointed peak on the nose and a cross shape in the center." No noise or smoke was noticed. The object then moved slowly, as if carried by the wind, at the same altitude. The witness then saw several red flashing lights aligned in the center of the craft. During his observation, he took a photograph. No other testimony was collected. Based on the objective elements gathered (see the investigation report), namely: - The object moving in the direction of the wind. - The shape and color compatible with existing models of fantasy balloons. - Estimates of size and distance compatible. We can conclude that the PAN observed and photographed by the witness may be a model of a fantasy balloon in the shape of a fighter jet, moving in the wind. This case should be classified as "B" as a probable observation of a fantasy balloon equipped with LEDs.
