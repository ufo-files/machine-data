Description: On March 6, 2014, at 8:41 a.m., a witness observed in the sky the movement of a dark object in the shape of a horse that appeared to spin on itself. He took photos and a video. The witness saw the shape at a distance of 300 meters from his observation point. The analysis of the photos and the object's behavior are fully consistent with a child's helium-filled balloon, an airborne horse-shaped balloon, a type easily found: here or there. This model is particularly common during the "Year of the Horse" on the Chinese calendar. The shape is very clear in the photo, and even the string that normally holds it to the ground can be seen. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A" (identified): child's balloon.
