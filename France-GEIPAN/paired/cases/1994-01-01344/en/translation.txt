Summary: Likely observation of a laser projector
--- seg-bab4ccbc28d6452ced2630e6 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 10, 1994, around 5:00 AM, a couple observed from their home a bright oval shape moving up and down in altitude without ever touching the ground. A hissing sound was also heard. The investigation will reveal that it is likely a laser advertising projector being tested at a nearby nightclub. The phenomenon was actually observed in several neighboring communes, and witnesses had been warned about the projector's tests.
