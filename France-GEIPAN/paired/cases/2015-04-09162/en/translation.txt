Description: On April 22, 2015, around 1:20/1:30 AM, two individuals observed the very quick passage in a clear sky of an object (triangular or round in shape) with three white LEDs and a green light in its center. No particular noise was heard. No trail or halo was seen. The object moved on a descending curve. Only one statement was collected. This observation of a triangle formed by 3 bright points is atypical, but very brief: less than 3 seconds. The GEIPAN would have liked to conduct an on-site investigation for this case, but, as the witness is a minor, parental consent is required; the GEIPAN was unable to obtain it. This very brief observation, moreover through a window, proves to be quite poor in reliable information. The GEIPAN has put forward the hypotheses of a drone flight, or a reflection in the window glass of a light located in the room, carried by one of the witness's guests. Without an on-site investigation, the validity of these hypotheses could not be verified. The GEIPAN classifies this case as C due to lack of reliable information and cross-checking.
