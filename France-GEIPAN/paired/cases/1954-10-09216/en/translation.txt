Summary: Quick observation of the SO-NE movement of a very bright elliptical object in the sky: Lack of information
--- seg-446f895538e26ea31a7b72b2 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 2, 1954, a witness was preparing to take a photograph of a dolmen around 4 pm on a sunny day when he spotted a light-colored, elliptical object in motion from south-east to north-east. He takes two photos and sends the negatives to the "Radar" newspaper. The gendarmerie learns of the event through the press and does not meet the witness until October 15. The drawings were only included in the first expedition of the report (no photos attached to the available report copies at GEIPAN). A brief inquiry into this case was conducted by the GEPAN in the late 1970s. She concludes that the photos would not have been published in the magazine "Radar" and that the search for the witness (approximately 58 years old at the time) remained fruitless. The observation lasts only a few seconds, the time to take two photos back-to-back. The phenomenon disappears before the witness has time to adjust the setting of his camera. The too-brief description and lack of photos prevent analysis of this observation; the GEIPAN classifies this case as C.
