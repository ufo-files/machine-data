--- seg-446f895538e26ea31a7b72b2 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 2, 1954, a witness was preparing to photograph a dolmen around 4 p.m. on a sunny day when he spotted a light-colored, elliptical object in motion from south-east to north-east. He took two photos and sent the negatives to the newspaper "Radar." The gendarmerie learned of the event through the press and did not meet with the witness until October 15. The prints were only added to the first shipment of the report (no photos attached to the available copies of the report at GEIPAN). A brief investigation into this case was conducted by GEPAN in the late 1970s. It concluded that the photos would not have been published in the magazine "Radar" and that efforts to locate the witness (who was approximately 58 years old at the time) were unsuccessful. The observation lasted only a few seconds, the time it took to take two consecutive photos. The phenomenon disappeared before the witness had a chance to adjust his camera settings. The overly brief description and lack of photos prevent analysis of this observation; GEIPAN classifies this case as C.
