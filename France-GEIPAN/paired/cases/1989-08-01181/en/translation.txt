Description: On August 19, 1989, around 11 PM, a gendarme (French police officer) returning from duty and his wife observed above the buildings of the brigade a large orange glow (four times larger than a star). Suddenly, the phenomenon abruptly took off at high speed until it was out of sight. No further information could be gathered about this observation.
