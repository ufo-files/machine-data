Description: On March 9, 1996, at approximately 9:20 PM, a private pilot observed the movement for 40 minutes of one or two white lights, as well as a green and red light. These lights were followed by a glowing trail. The witness immediately alerted the gendarmes, but the phenomenon had disappeared when they arrived around 10:15 PM. No further information could be gathered.
