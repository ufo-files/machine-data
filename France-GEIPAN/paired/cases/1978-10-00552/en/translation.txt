Summary: Observation of a luminous phenomenon related to the Moon.
--- seg-e589594303fe9e13a613d5cc [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 6, 1978, two witnesses observed a unexplained luminous phenomenon for 15 minutes. They described an abnormal pink glow moving slowly at about 7 to 8 meters above the ground. No sound was heard during the observation. When the gendarmes quickly arrived at the scene, they were unable to witness the phenomenon as it had disappeared. No further reports of this phenomenon were made. The transition between forest and cultivated field is conducive to atmospheric turbulences of a thermal nature, causing distortion of the observed object. At the time and in the direction indicated that evening, these individuals likely observed this type of phenomenon related to the moon's first quarter phase, which was very low in the sky.
