Description: On July 12, 2014, around 11:50 PM, a driver and his passenger observed the silent passage of 7 to 8 glowing objects in the sky, colored yellow-orange. The witness has time to take photographs. The observation of yellow-orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, clearly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The photos taken by the witness are entirely consistent with this hypothesis. The movement is observed from North to South. The Geneva weather report indicates a weak, unstable wind (from North at 21:00, West at 22:00, South at 23:00, South-West at 00:00). Furthermore, in the mountainous area, very local winds are complex at the end of the day: unfortunately, it is not possible to precisely determine the local wind a posteriori to confirm or not that these flying objects are carried by the wind. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns. See the Meteociel report for the day of the observation.
