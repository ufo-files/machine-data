Description: From his home in Vinneuf (89), on September 6, 2025, around 05:00 AM, a witness observed a peculiar phenomenon in the southwest direction. The horizon glowed like a sunrise or sunset. Intrigued, the witness took out his smartphone, took several photos, then stopped observing. This case, of good consistency* and low strangeness, turns out to be an observation of the Moon (see the investigation report). *According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. Analyzing the photographs allows for a direct comparison with the sky situation at the time of the observation. Through the Stellarium® simulation tool, the investigation demonstrates that the Moon is precisely in the witness's line of sight. It is then at an elevation of approximately 1°, or just at the horizon. The strangeness perceived by the witness comes from the light shape projected onto the clouds, which he identified as a PAN because he couldn't see the actual source. Indeed, although the Moon was very bright on the eve of a full moon, it was invisible to the witness because it was too low on the horizon and hidden by vegetation and buildings. The GEIPAN classifies this case as an "A" observation, that of the Moon setting.
