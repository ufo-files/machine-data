Description: On the evening of July 18, 2024, three members of the same family observed an Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon (PAN) from a parking area for recreational vehicles located near the tip of the Bay at Saint-Cast-le-Guildo (22). They noticed the presence of an oval-shaped, white object hovering motionless over the Bay of Arguenon. The witness reported the observation to GEIPAN by completing a detailed questionnaire, along with two original photographs of PAN. This case, of low strangeness and good consistency (multiple witnesses, one precise testimony with 2 photos of PAN), appears to be the observation of a tourist airplane (as confirmed by the investigation report). The photographs of PAN allow for its precise location in the witnesses' environment (azimuth and angular height). A radar analysis provided by the CNOA (National Center for Air Operations) and a reconstruction on Flightradar24 reveal that a tourist airplane (Jodel D-20) was at a position corresponding to that of PAN at the time of the images, with a temporal offset of approximately 2 minutes. This offset could be explained by a possible divergence in the internal clock of the phone used to take the photos. The white color of PAN is consistent with the visual characteristics of such an airplane. The distance of the object from the witness, between 19 and 39 km, explains the inability to clearly identify the shape of the airplane, notably due to the absence of visibility of the wings at this distance. The case is classified as "A", observation of a tourist airplane. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.
