Description: On July 14, 1983, at approximately 11:55 PM, a group of people observed the peculiar movement of a bright point of light for 45 seconds. It moved on a north-south trajectory in jerks with a light intensity comparable to that of a satellite. After remaining stationary for several seconds during which the light intensity increased, it reportedly took off quickly and disappeared over the horizon. No other information could be obtained regarding this phenomenon.
