Description: On Tuesday, August 31, 2010, around 9 PM, a witness, seated at the dinner table, noticed a very bright light through the open window of his dining room. Intrigued by what he called "a little sun," he filmed his observation. He made other similar observations. Of low strangeness but good consistency given the videos, we can unambiguously classify this observation case as A, misidentification with Venus.
