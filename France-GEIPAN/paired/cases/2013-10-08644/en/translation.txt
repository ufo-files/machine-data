Description: The witness, awakened by the rain, went outside into their courtyard and observed a silent, dark triangle with a glowing perimeter. The object gained altitude and blended into the clouds. The described observation does not correspond to any known phenomenon. Since this observation does not evoke any of the usual misperceptions, the GEIPAN dispatched an investigator to interview the witness on site, but the witness did not follow through with this investigation proposal. Due to the observation's brevity and the fact that it occurred at night, upon the witness's awakening, a possible explanation could be an "hypnopompic hallucination" (which occurs upon waking); see the end of the article page: Hallucination. However, the witness indicates that before getting up, he was already fully awake. One could also consider a phenomenon of retinal persistence if the witness turned on the light (neon tubes?) between their room and the courtyard. The investigation could have confirmed or not either of these hypotheses, or opened up other possibilities. Pending additional information or cross-checking, this case remains classified as "C".
