Summary: Observations of the movements of a red-hot glowing object in the night sky: probable observation of a Thai lantern.
--- seg-c6261a09a9efbd1009928dfa [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 5, 2014, around 10:30 PM, three individuals observed a bright red light object in the sky above the hamlet of Boissezon. The object initially remained stationary before moving upwards and heading towards Roquebrun. It was then lost from view towards the southeast. Two witnesses reported their observation in a gendarmerie protocol. No other testimonies were reported to the gendarmerie or the municipality. Based on the objective elements gathered in the investigation report (see attached document), namely: - movement according to the wind, - color, - brightness, - shape, - duration of observation, we can conclude that the PAN observed by the witnesses is likely only a sky lantern, also known as a "Thai lantern." This case should be classified as "B" for a probable observation of a Thai lantern.
