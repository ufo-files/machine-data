Description: On May 12, 2014, at 10:26 PM, a mother and her daughter were amazed to see a bright, orange-fire light mass moving in the sky. They then think of a burning airplane or hot air balloon. But the phenomenon moves quietly towards the NE without losing altitude and then disappears, hidden by trees. The observation lasted one minute. Only one testimony is collected. The observed light mass comes from Corbeil/Mennecy (South of Evry) and is heading towards Etioles/Tigery (East), so it appears to be following a northeast direction. The Meteorology report indicates a light west wind (at 22:00) shifting to a south-west wind (at 23:00), and significant cloud cover (4 to 7/8): therefore, the object's movement is consistent with the local wind. The color, duration, and mode of movement are consistent with a Thai lantern, likely released by a private individual, after a festival or for a test. However, the shape described (with orange rays) and the day (Monday) are unusual for a lantern. Another possible hypothesis would be a plane approaching Orly, preparing to land on the western runways (a face-on plane appears as a large fixed light point, the navigation lights are not powerful enough to be visible in these conditions). The fact that the photograph taken by the witness did not produce a result is not surprising: the Nintendo DS console's optical sensor is not sensitive enough for nighttime photography. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
