Description: On Sunday, June 22, 2014 at 12:55 AM, a witness, exiting their residence, observed the movement of two powerful, orange lights in the night sky. The witness followed the silent movements of the phenomena until they were out of sight. No other testimony was collected. The observation of orange light crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests a Thai lantern, likely released nearby after a private party, possibly on Music Day. The described shape resembles 2 connected lanterns, possibly attached together. However, it is noted that the witness's drawing (2 orange circles) does not accurately represent a Thai lantern viewed from below: a very bright point should be observed in the center. The movement is observed from Northeast to West. A very light west wind (approximately 2 km) is observed in Neuilly-sur-Marne at 2:00 a.m., shifting to the southeast (see the Météociel record). The raised wind at Roissy (20 km north of Noisy-le-Grand) is from the northeast to the north and weak (see the Météociel record). The proximity of the Marne, at 600m, and the urban environment can explain the change in direction, a consequence of variable wind flows. The GEIPAN classifies this case as a "B" observation, likely Thai lanterns.
