Description: On August 24, 2012, at 11:45 PM, a witness observed an oval glowing point circling in the cloudy sky for at least 30 minutes. This observation, on a Saturday night, resembles a projector used for nightclub animations. However, a nightclub in Bourgoin-Jallieu has stated they do not use this type of device. One might also question the use of such systems only 20 km from Lyon Satolas Airport. Despite these reservations, knowing that there are mobile projectors of this type (available for rental) that can be seen from more than 10 km away, this hypothesis remains the most likely. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a light animation.
