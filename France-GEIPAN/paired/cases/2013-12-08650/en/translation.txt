--- seg-3811f4de1e624663bb9638cf [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 9, 2013, at 5:15 PM, a witness observed in a clear sky, the passage of two compact, bright red orbs, followed by two more. He took several photos. The orbs behaved like a squadron. They disappeared as they gained altitude. Analysis of successive images shows bright orbs approaching from the left of the witness's field of view, and moving away from the witness. As the witness was looking southwest and the wind was blowing from the southwest (see report), the hypothesis of objects carried by the wind seems appropriate: it is normal that all objects follow the same trajectory. These wind-carried bright orbs do indeed evoke Thai lanterns. However, it is surprising to see this type of phenomenon on a weekday in the late afternoon: one might think of an early Christmas or Saint Nicholas party with children. The GEIPAN did not investigate potential perpetrators of this release. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of Thai lanterns.
