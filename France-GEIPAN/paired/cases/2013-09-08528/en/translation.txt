--- seg-69e78213b41f3b2189f958db [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 4, 2013, starting at 11:45 PM, two witnesses observing the sky were intrigued by round, multicolored light phenomena they could not explain. A total of 2 to 30 phenomena were observed. Only one testimony was collected. This complex testimony lacked precision given the lengthy observation sequence (approximately 1 hour). The witness, contacted by phone on February 24, 2014, announced that the other witness would also send in an observation report, but as of November 2014, the GEIPAN had not received it. This case is classified "C" due to lack of information, awaiting additional testimony.
