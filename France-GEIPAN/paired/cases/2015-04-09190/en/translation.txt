Description: On April 18, 2015, around 11:30 PM, a driver is alerted by his backseat passenger about a red light in the sky. When he looks in his rearview mirror, he sees six or seven bright, reddish-orange orbs moving in formation and seemingly following the vehicle. The witness stops his vehicle and observes the phenomena's progress until they completely disappear, except for one orb. He manages to take a few pictures. The observation of orange orbs crossing the sky in just a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement is observed from northeast to northwest, which is consistent with the northeast wind reported in Le Mans on Météociel, indicating a steady northeast wind. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
