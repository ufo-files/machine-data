Summary: Observation of the silent movement of three yellow glowing orbs: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-0b0258626fa43aa7cf820426 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 27, 2012, around 11 PM - 11:30 PM, a witness observed the rapid east-north-east movements of three yellow glowing orbs in the sky. The objects quickly disappear into the horizon. The observation of three yellow balls flying for one minute in formation with the wind (20 km/h south wind: weather) one weekend evening suggests Thai lanterns. The witness describes "a triangular halo" which is a priori not compatible with classic Thai lanterns; however, the drawing made by the witness (ovni5) corresponds fairly well to a lantern photo (See GEIPAN photo). The witness reports a change in trajectory that is not consistent with movement due to the wind (a priori linear movement): it could be a local gust, or perhaps an illusion in estimating the distance. The apparent speed observed by the witness is consistent with objects flying at 20 km/h at an altitude of one hundred meters. Although the witness refutes this hypothesis, the GEIPAN classifies this case as a "B" probable observation of Thai lanterns, given the strong similarity with these objects, the observation date and time compatible with the end of a private party.
