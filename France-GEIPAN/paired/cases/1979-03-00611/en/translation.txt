Summary: Observation of a glowing object that appears to follow a motorist for 10 kilometers: astronomical observation of the Moon.
--- seg-fcee8f73773738b7c76893be [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 21, 1979, around 2 a.m., a driver traveling on D16 between Ayguesvives and Gardouch (31), spots a luminous phenomenon that changes shapes and colors throughout the journey and appears to follow the witness. Over the 10 kilometers driven, the PAN sometimes seems to stop and then accelerates rapidly. Upon arriving home, the witness wakes up his wife, who also observes the phenomenon. The witness takes photos before going to bed around 3 a.m. No unusual noises are heard, and no other testimonies are collected by the gendarmerie. The GEPAN investigation concludes that the witness likely made an astronomical observation of a moonrise on a foggy night (see the GEPAN investigation report). A check done in 2008 on the astronomical configuration at the time of the observation also shows a conjunction between the Moon, Neptune, and Antares, confirming the 1979 GEPAN investigation's conclusion. This case is classified as A: Astronomical observation of the Moon.
