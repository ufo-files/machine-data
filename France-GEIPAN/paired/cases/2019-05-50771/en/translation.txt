Description: On May 18, 2019, at 10:25 PM, two people in a vehicle, while in transit, were surprised to see in the sky the low-altitude maneuvers of a PAN with 3 lights, one of which was flashing. The witnesses stop at the side of the road. One of the witnesses films the phenomenon, then together they notice that the PAN are circling them. Scared, these witnesses quickly leave. We have studied two hypotheses: the confusion with a drone and the confusion with a commercial aircraft approaching Dole-Jura Airport (see the investigation report). Only the last hypothesis will be retained for the conclusion, with the following arguments: - The colors of the lights observed are in every way consistent with those used in aeronautics (white and red). - The lights visible on the video can be identified as: - The aircraft's landing light, - Two of the three navigation lights (the red located on the left wing and the white located on the stabilizer, the green located on the right wing not being visible, probably obscured by the aircraft's body), - Two white anti-collision lights, blinking at the same frequency as a A319/A320/A321, one located on the tail of the aircraft, the other on the left wing. - The apparent trajectory of the PAN could be reconstructed using the testimonies; it resembles that of a commercial aircraft practically facing the witnesses at the beginning of the observation, before approaching them and passing to their left, then making a loop behind them, heading towards the Dole-Jura airport. - The audible noise on the video soundtrack resembles that of the aircraft engines, although the witnesses only describe a "sifflement" (whistle). The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A": observation of a commercial aircraft, type A319/A320/A321, approaching the Dole-Jura airport, located approximately 20 km from the witnesses.
