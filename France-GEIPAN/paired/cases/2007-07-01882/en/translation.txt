Description: In May 2008, a witness reported that on July 29, 2007, around 8:00 PM, he and several others observed a dark ball with irregular, clear spots scattered across it moving across the sky in a straight line on a west-southeast trajectory. During the observation, which lasted approximately thirty seconds, no noise was heard and no smoke was noticed. These individuals likely saw a balloon, but no hypothesis can be confirmed given the age of the observation.
