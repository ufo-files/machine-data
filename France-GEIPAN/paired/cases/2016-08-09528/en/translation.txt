Description: On August 21, 2016, around 11:30 PM, a witness observes a first phenomenon entering the atmosphere with a white combustion but with phases of green and orange. A few minutes later, he notices below his first observation a set of approximately twenty flickering objects at a constant rate, all grouped in ascension in the sky. The phenomenon crosses the sky without sound. A neighbor also saw the phenomenon, but only one testimony was collected. The witness saw two phenomena. The first is likely a small meteor shower. What makes the witness' experience strange and the origin of the testimony is the second phenomenon, apparently totally different from the first. It is probably a release of LED balloons from a boat at sea, rising into the sky with wind pushing them towards land (the east wind that evening allowed this as shown by the weather report). This causes a significant passage across the sky. LED balloons can take any color, flicker, change color, all in a random or pre-defined manner, or even synchronized, depending on the price. See the article on reusable (lifetime) LED balloons. The summer period and the location are conducive to such festive events. As the witness is not a resident of the location, he was able to observe this for the first time. In conclusion, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: LED balloons.
