Description: On September 23, 1980, at 4:15 a.m., two people returning from work spotted a flying saucer taking off behind the village church. As it ascended rapidly, it emitted bright light rays that dazzled the witnesses. One of the two people saw the craft again above a forest. No other witnesses or traces were found behind the church regarding this phenomenon, for which we have insufficient information.
