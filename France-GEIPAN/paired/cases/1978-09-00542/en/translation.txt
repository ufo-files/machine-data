Description: On September 8, 1978, around 7:15 PM, several people heard a whistling sound from the sky followed by a loud noise. A farmer later discovered that his barn roof had been damaged and found pieces of white ice, varying in size. The sky that day was cloudy. The diameter and total weight of the ice could not be determined as it broke into several pieces. Some pieces were collected by witnesses, while others melted on the ground without leaving any particular traces. No other similar incident occurred in the region that day. This rare phenomenon is, however, known in meteorology.
