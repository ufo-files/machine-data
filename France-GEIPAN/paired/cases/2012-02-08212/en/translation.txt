--- seg-0726ced04b1198babb4fcc10 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Saturday, February 18, 2012, around 8:00 PM, a witness observed in the sky a luminous phenomenon that intrigued them: a white, oblong glow appeared stationary above the farm buildings. It suddenly disappeared but reappeared a few seconds later at the same location, performing a geometric figure (a half Z) at high speed before quickly disappearing. No other testimonies were collected. Of low strangeness and low consistency due to a lack of precise orientation, this observation case seems to correspond to a misperception with the movements of a beam of light from a SkyTracer-type projector. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a light show.
