--- seg-729d9802ea60f06e7fa48fcf [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 22, 2013, after 10:30 PM, two people were surprised by the silent and straight passage of a bright light phenomenon in the sky. The description of this unusual phenomenon (a very bright point slowly crossing the sky) clearly suggests the International Space Station (ISS). The Calsky site indicates an ISS passage from 10:45 PM to 10:52 PM from West to South (see the attached Calsky log, and the witness's point of view simulation). However, there is an imperfect match between the witness's stated time: "22h30 could be more" and the simulated time, and the witness's maximum elevation indicated (90°) is not the same as that shown in the simulation (17.5°): an imprecision in the simulation parameters could be considered. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of the ISS passage with some uncertainty.
