Description: Two witnesses report their observation of silently moving lights above the ocean on Sunday, April 17, 2011, between 21:50 and 22:10. The description of the phenomenon closely resembles that of Thai lanterns. The wind direction (East, NE) is consistent with the observed movement. It is simply noted that the observation occurred on a Sunday, which is not the usual day for releasing lanterns; however, it is the school holiday period.
