Summary: Probable observation of the passage of a Thai lantern.
--- seg-7d352fe7c69fc161b1659e58 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 4, 2010, at 11:12 PM, a witness films the passage in the sky of an orange light. A second witness, who took photographs but cannot provide them to investigators, describes the phenomenon as "some sort of balloon containing a kind of lantern inside." The main witness is interviewed by the gendarmerie four months after the observation. The video attached to the gendarmerie's police report clearly shows a flight of a Thai-style lantern. The origin and launch site have not been determined.
