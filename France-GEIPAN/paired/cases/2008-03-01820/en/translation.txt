Description: On March 29, 2008, around 9:30 PM, several witnesses observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky for 3 seconds. A very bright, yellow sphere moved at high speed from southwest to northeast. The witnesses noted a non-straight trajectory and no sound. The luminous phenomenon suddenly disappeared. According to the description of the phenomenon, these people witnessed a probable atmospheric reentry.
