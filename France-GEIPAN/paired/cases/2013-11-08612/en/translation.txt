Summary: Discontinuous observation of a luminous phenomenon in a clear sky: observation of Venus and aircraft.
--- seg-d3f2bbb0980b9afaa07896da [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 27, 2013, at 6:00 PM, two individuals observed an intermittent light phenomenon in the sky. The observation lasted 1.5 hours and was divided into three phases:

- A stationary bright light to the northeast (two large yellow headlights with a red light in the middle) that then disappeared behind a building, reminiscent of a plane or helicopter seen face-on with its lights on.

- The son of the witness observed a bright point of light passing over the house that "wobbled." At 6:02 PM and 6:05 PM, two aircraft flew over the area at high altitude (refer to Flightradar records and the military radar trace on the Geoportail map in local time). There was also activity from a helicopter at that time, between 6:12 PM and 6:30 PM, flying over the high-voltage line between Quinssaines and Nouhant, 7 km southwest of the observation location. The witness's description is not precise enough to determine what aircraft was actually observed.

- For the end of the observation, a very bright point to the southwest, almost fixed for over an hour: this is undoubtedly the planet Venus, which is particularly bright at this time (Magnitude -4.7). The variety of the witness's photos can be explained by inconsistent focus settings and camera movement.

The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of Venus for the end of the observation, and probable observation of planes and/or helicopters for the beginning of the observation.
