Description: A pilot reports his observation made in flight: on December 13, 2012 at 4:48 pm (UTC), he observed (in the 030° Toulouse) the east-west passage of a green meteorite at an estimated altitude between 45,000 and 60,000 feet. The concise description suggests a meteoroid re-entry into the atmosphere (meteorite fall). This time of year corresponds to the peak of the Geminid meteor shower (originating from the northeast horizon at this time); numerous observations were made on December 12 and 13, by automatic cameras or observers. However, the GEIPAN did not receive any other simultaneous testimony. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of a meteorite fall.
