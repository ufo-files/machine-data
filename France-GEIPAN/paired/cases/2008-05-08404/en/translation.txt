Summary: Observation of the passage of a luminous phenomenon: Lack of information.
--- seg-1a4f95ab746987fbc43c0bdb [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness briefly reports their observation on May 21, 2008, around 11 PM. From their garden, they observed a linear path from south to north of a "small sun" at approximately 30° above the horizon. The speed was constant. The phenomenon then headed towards the east before quickly disappearing. This unusual testimony resembles a Thai lantern (a kite-like flying lantern carried by the wind), but it is too brief to allow for precise analysis against typical misidentification hypotheses. Weather stations surrounding the observation location provide contradictory wind directions, which can be explained by the passage of a visible front visible in weather satellite photos. In any case, the precise weather conditions at the location are not known (closest station: Caen, 50 km northeast). This case is classified C due to a lack of information.
