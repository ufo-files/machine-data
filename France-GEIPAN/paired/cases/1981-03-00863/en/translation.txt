Description: On February 23, 1981, a witness, emerging from their home, spotted in the sky two large, luminous balls. For a few seconds, these emit a very intense, blinding light. Very surprised by the phenomenon, the witness sees these balls relight a few seconds later at the same location before disappearing completely. The witness claims that since this observation, they have been experiencing health issues (constant state of fatigue, malaise). No other testimony regarding this phenomenon has been collected, for which we lack information.
