Summary: Nighttime observation of a yellow triangular light towards a bridge: moon observation.

Description: On October 22, 2020, around 11:20 PM, a witness observed, first with the naked eye and then with binoculars, a PAN in the shape of a triangle. This PAN was bright but not blinding, intense yellow in color bordered with horizontal and vertical black lines at the center. It was located above and to the right of the left pillar of the Joseph le Brix bridge on the D101 axis, above the Auray river, to the west of the witness's position, in the commune of Bono (56). Although there is only one witness, the consistency* of the case is considered good, with a complete and detailed testimony. The absence of a photo of the PAN is compensated by a perfectly understandable sketch. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony. Following an investigation, it was determined that the witness had actually observed the Moon (see the investigation report). The position of the PAN reported by the witness perfectly coincides with that of the Moon near sunset. The observed yellow color is also consistent with that of the Moon near the horizon. Furthermore, since the Moon was in a First Quarter phase, its shape matches that of the PAN if its lower part was hidden by the landscape. The black lines seen through binoculars are compatible with branches, antennas, or even the bridge's handrail, which overlapped with the Moon's image. The rapid departure of the PAN towards the rear can be explained by the Moon's disappearance from the horizon after a few minutes of observation. This hypothesis explains the strangeness described by the witness, notably the "rapid and regular" rate of distance, as well as the triangular shape and color. The case is classified as A, observation of the Moon.
