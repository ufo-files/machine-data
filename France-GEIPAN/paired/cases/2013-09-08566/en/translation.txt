Summary: Sequential observations of white light balls dancing in the sky: observation of a light display of a "sky tracer" type.
--- seg-0a98d8d2cff71a291ad2880c [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Saturday, September 14, 2013, between 11:45 PM and midnight, two witnesses were intrigued by successive ballets of white glowing orbs in the sky. No noise was heard during the observations. The appearance of the phenomenon clearly suggested a "sky tracer," a light projection system used for festive events and nightclubs. A nightclub in Pézenas confirmed using one of these systems exceptionally that night for a special event. Note that the witnesses observed the phenomenon to the South, and the weather report for Montpellier for that evening indicated a cloudy sky, conducive to the beautiful effects of this system. This case is classified "A": certain observation of a light display of the "sky tracer" type.
