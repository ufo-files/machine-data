Description: On December 31, 1977, after 7 PM, several people observed an intriguing orange-colored light phenomenon in the sky. A person who learned of these events from their neighborhood, later testifying at the gendarmerie, claimed to have fired a fireworks display consisting of two illuminating rockets and four Chinese rockets between 7 PM and 7:30 PM to celebrate the end of the year. Another testimony was collected regarding an observation on December 31, 1977, around 5 PM. A driver saw an immobile object in the sky that quickly disappeared from view. This testimony is brief, and no other information could be added regarding this phenomenon.
