Description: On the RN12 between Jouars-Ponchartrain (78) and Garches (92), on October 26, 2011 around 7 PM, a driver and his son were intrigued by stationary or moving bright points in the sky. A triangular craft was then spotted. It appears that the starting point of the observation (fixed and aligned bright points as the witness drives east on the RN12) can easily be explained by a misidentification of planes due to the heavy air traffic in the Paris region. The rest is more complicated, especially since the description remains vague: a "flashing triangular craft in red and white," then a bright point with "only vertical white flashes" crossing a portion of sky at 80° in two seconds, do not evoke any usual explanations and are the only truly strange elements of this observation case, for which we would have appreciated more details. We also received no other similar testimonies for this October 26, 2011 in the Paris region. With low to moderate strangeness, this observation case is too inconsistent to be firmly concluded. This case is classified C due to lack of independent testimonies and cross-corroboration.
