Description: The witnesses are on the street in front of their home in Mulhouse (68) to observe a fireworks display when, around 10:45 PM, they notice the presence in the sky of a PAN consisting of two fixed red lights framing a flickering green light. At first immobile above the roof of a neighboring house, this PAN suddenly gains speed before quickly disappearing, masked by the roof of that house. The observation lasted for approximately 5 minutes in total. T1 was able to take six photographs before this disappearance, during the immobility phase. Of average strangeness and good consistency* (2 witnesses, but unique testimony, photos of PAN), this case appears to be a probable observation of a drone (see the investigation report). *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of information considered reliable and objective, gathered for a testimony. The description of PAN by T1 and its appearance in the photographs resemble a drone, as its colors are perfectly typical of those used in aeronautics and by drones. Additionally, the PAN's immobility as well as its final rapid movement are very consistent with the flight capabilities of a drone. The observation was made just a few moments before the closing fireworks display at the Mulhouse (68) fairground kermesse, which provides an excellent opportunity to fly a drone. However, the municipality of Mulhouse (68) was unable to confirm the explanatory hypothesis, as no request for the use of a drone had been made. She does not, however, rule out the use of a drone by a private individual. The case is classified B, probable observation of a drone.
