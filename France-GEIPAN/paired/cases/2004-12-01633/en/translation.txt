Description: On December 1, 2004, around 7:30 PM, a witness observed in the sky a very bright white ball descending at a very high speed. Before reaching the ground, this ball transformed into a small red point and disappeared. Another witness, a military person off duty, also observed this phenomenon, which made him think of a flashbang grenade. The witnesses likely observed an atmospheric reentry.
