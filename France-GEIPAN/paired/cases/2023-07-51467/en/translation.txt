**Description:** On the evening of July 13, 2023, a resident of Toulouse (31) was standing outside in front of his building, in the midst of a phone conversation, when he spotted a PAN in the sky. He took two photographs and a video of the observation on this occasion. The case presents good consistency according to the criteria of GEIPAN (single witness, photos and video of the PAN that could be used for analysis). The PAN observed turned out, after investigation, to be a commercial airliner (as detailed in the investigation report). This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- The elongated shape typical of an airplane fuselage.
- The white/gray color is also consistent with that of the aircraft fuselage illuminated by the setting sun's rays.
- The straight-line motion at a constant speed is also a very consistent element supporting this hypothesis.

We were able to explain some peculiarities:

- The absence of noise can be explained by the distance of the object (over 30 km) as well as by the ambient noise of traffic around the witness during the observation.
- The invisibility of the airplane's wings can be attributed to a combination of factors, including the observation distance, which generates a very reduced field of view, an observation angle of 35°, atmospheric diffusion effects, and the aircraft's geometry, favoring reflections on the largest circular surfaces, such as the fuselage.

The absence of a condensation trail is entirely possible, provided the temperature and humidity conditions prevailing at the altitude of the aircraft are favorable; however, these could not be determined. A reconstruction of air traffic reveals that two commercial airliners crossed the observation axis at the time of the observation. The analysis carried out on Google Earth, coupled with the flight data of these aircraft, allows the unidentified aerial phenomenon (PAN) to be identified as corresponding to the flight EXS4LX. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A," observation of a commercial airliner.

*According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.*
