Description: Early in the morning of December 10, 2014, a witness observed several green light beams, followed by a massive object in the center of the Moon, which appeared quite large that morning. The phenomenon was oval and elongated. The frightened witness stopped observing and cannot say how long the phenomenon lasted. Only one testimony was collected. The oddness of the observation is due to the beams and the oval described in front of the Moon during the different phases of the observation (see the investigation report). They strongly resemble both a peak of a meteor shower and the presence of a lingering meteor trail in front of the Moon. Note that the position of the radiant of the Geminids, a particularly active swarm close to its peak activity, was near the Moon, which is entirely consistent with the explanatory hypothesis. The elongated oval also resembles a rare and controversial astronomical phenomenon, known as a Transient Lunar Phenomenon (TLP). However, the hypothesis of a lingering trail in front of the Moon is the most probable. The analysis shows that the entire observation relates to a probable brief peak in the activity of the Geminids. Due to its low strangeness and consistency (single witness, no photo, lack of details), this case appears to be a probable misperception with phenomena related to a meteor shower. The case is classified as B, probable misperception with phenomena related to a meteor shower (meteors, lingering trail).
