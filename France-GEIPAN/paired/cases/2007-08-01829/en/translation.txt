Description: On Thursday, August 30, 2007, around 7 PM, a witness, looking out the window, noticed a bright light in the clear sky. They used their camcorder to record it. The light dimmed, and the witness saw a small dark object that became a dark patch in motion. Intrigued, the witness moved to another room to follow the object, zoomed in with their camcorder, and saw four black squares or points surrounding the bright light. The observation lasted less than 25 seconds. Only one testimony was collected. The phenomenon observed by the witness was a very bright light point, very likely a reflection of the sun on a flying object. The video of the phenomenon shows four black rectangles surrounding the white point; these elements are clearly artifacts, that is, parasitic effects of the video sensor and compression. Therefore, the oddity of this case is reduced to the passage of a flying object reflecting the sun. That day, a strong wind (Mistral) was blowing from the north to northwest (see Météociel). Since the object's movement was from northeast to southwest, it is highly unlikely that it was carried by the wind. However, an airplane or helicopter could have played the role of a mirror in relation to the sun, which was low on the horizon (due west at 8°). The strong wind would have masked the noise of the craft. The investigation was carried out too late to confirm this hypothesis. The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of information and cross-references.
