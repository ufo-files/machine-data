Description: On April 9, 2023, in the late afternoon, in a garden located in Valence-d'Agen (82), a couple (T1 and T2) was walking when T1 observed, for approximately two minutes, two __UFO_PROTECTED_000__s. Twenty minutes later, T1 spotted another PAN with similar characteristics and behavior to the first two. During this second observation, T1 managed to film the PAN. Only one testimony was collected. Of average strangeness and good consistency* (two witnesses, but only one reported their observation and provided a video of the PAN), the case appears to be a probable observation of birds flying at a relatively high altitude. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. This conclusion is drawn after exploring two hypotheses: balloons or birds (see the investigation report). The balloon hypothesis presents characteristics compatible with those of the PAN (white color, brightness...). However, uncertainties exist regarding their circular trajectory due to the nature of the winds, which should be whirling, while the Meteofrance "AROME" data does not confirm this. The bird hypothesis is more plausible for the following reasons: - White color: compatible with the ventral white plumage of some birds. - Trajectory shape, composed of circles ("circling") that birds use to take advantage of upward currents and regular straight lines. An ornithological expert consulted on this matter indicates that the circling seems smaller than usual. However, the movements of the PAN in the video are difficult to assess due to the camera's own movement. - Wing beats also seem perceptible in the video, although this is not certain. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": probable bird observation.
