Description: On August 17, 2014, around 9:10 PM, a witness observed, through an open window, the rapid passage of a large fireball with a short, yellow-white trail, very bright and very luminous. After a horizontal movement, the phenomenon disappeared. The observation lasted only a few seconds. The GEIPAN, located in CNES Toulouse, approximately 12 km from the witness, has a camera constantly ready to record this type of phenomenon. This camera recorded the same phenomenon at 9:11 PM (See video: the phenomenon is visible in the upper left corner of the circle, corresponding to the southeast direction). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": certain observation of a bolide, also recorded on the GEIPAN camera.
