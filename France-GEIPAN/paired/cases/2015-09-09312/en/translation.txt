Description: On the night of September 4 to 5, 2015, two witnesses observed silent, floating lights in the night sky of Roquebrune-sur-Argens. T1 made his observation at 11:40 PM on September 4, and T2 at 12:15 AM. No other testimonies were collected. The observation of milky, moving lights in low clouds, during a weekend night for several dozen minutes, clearly suggests a light show produced by a nightclub or other public entertainment. The tourist zone where the observation took place, of course, is very conducive to this type of event. The two witnesses observed a similar phenomenon with a 45-minute interval, probably from the same source but possibly in different directions: these laser projectors are clearly visible more than 10 kilometers away. One of the witnesses took a series of photos of the phenomenon, which are entirely consistent with this explanation. The GEIPAN did not conduct searches to locate the precise source of this animation and classifies this case as B: probable observation of a light show.
