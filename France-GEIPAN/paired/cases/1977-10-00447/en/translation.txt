Description: On October 27, 1977, around 6:00 a.m., two individuals observed a red ball of fire in the sky for a few seconds, followed by a long trail of red and orange. This phenomenon moved from east to west at an extremely high speed and suddenly disappeared into the sky. No noise was heard. These witnesses likely observed an atmospheric reentry.
