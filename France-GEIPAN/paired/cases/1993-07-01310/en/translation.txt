Description: On the night of July 13 to 14, 1993, around 1:30 AM, a witness observed for approximately fifteen minutes a white circular shape spinning while splitting in two. No other witnesses were found, but given the observation date and the many festivities organized for the occasion, the observation of a disco light or laser beam is likely.
