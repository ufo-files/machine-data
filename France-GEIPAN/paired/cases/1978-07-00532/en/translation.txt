Description: On July 27, 1978, around 11:30 PM, several witnesses observed a very bright red ball emitting a very intense glow. It appeared to fall from the sky near their homes. One witness stated that at midnight and forty-five seconds, the ball would rise again and depart. No trace of an object's fall was found by the witnesses or the gendarmerie in the area. No further information about this phenomenon was gathered.
