Description: On June 02, 2010, around 20:30-20:45, a witness briefly observed the passage of an oval-shaped object with a pale fluorescent green color. This luminous object came from the south and headed north-northeast on a straight trajectory at a high speed. No noise was heard, and no trail was seen following this object. The observation described a very brief (1 to 2 seconds) and fast luminous phenomenon, shaped like a comet (suggesting an atmospheric reentry), with a pale fluorescent green color (also characteristic of an atmospheric reentry). This case is classified B as a probable atmospheric reentry of a meteor.
