Summary: Observations of the slow and silent passage of a large glowing ball: ISS passage.
--- seg-00810ea71856c610d1dfcc70 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 22, 2012, at 10:18 PM, an entire family was looking at the starry sky. These people were intrigued by the slow and silent passage of a glowing ball whose intensity dimmed before disappearing. The witnesses' description is in every way consistent with the observation of the International Space Station (ISS) (see Calsky document - ISS passage). In the second document (Calsky - sky map), the white balls represent the ISS's successive positions against the backdrop of stars; the balls are larger to the extent that the ISS appears bright. It is noted that at the beginning of the passage, the apparent movement of the ISS is minimal, and as it moves towards the East, the station entered the Earth's shadow. It is no longer illuminated by the sun and becomes invisible to the observers.

This case is classified as "A": observation of the passage of the International Space Station (ISS).
