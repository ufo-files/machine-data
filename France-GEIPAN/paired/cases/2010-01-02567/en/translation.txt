Summary: Observation of the slow movement of flashing white lights: probable observation of a meteorology balloon train.
--- seg-a313b3b5dab7c0e8e4d7ef79 [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reports an observation made on January 21st between 7:42 and 7:45 AM: between the peaks of Grosse Tête and Grand Miceau, a group of white, blinking lights moves slowly. The entire group disappears, hidden by the landscape. The investigation revealed that a radiosonde attached to a balloon was released near St Exupéry Airport 1 hour and 40 minutes before the observation. The hypothesis that these illuminated balloons were observed as the sun was rising remains the most plausible. This case is classified as B: probable observation of a meteorology balloon train.
