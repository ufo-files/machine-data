--- seg-c34056de208baccda0a75bc0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 5th, around 11 PM, four individuals observed a peculiar luminous phenomenon in a pine forest near their residence. In the shape of a trapezoid, this intensely bright light was observed for approximately 10 minutes before abruptly disappearing. No sounds were heard. At 11:45 PM, the brigade that arrived at the scene found no trace of light. The on-site investigation the following day discovered a circle of 20 to 30 cm in diameter, made of ferns, all lying in the same direction. The gendarmerie brigade present at the same location the following evening determined that the phenomenon was caused by moonlight filtering through the pines. It was later established that the ground traces were due to a forestry vehicle.
