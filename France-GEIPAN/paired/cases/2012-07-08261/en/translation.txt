Summary: Observation of a bright point moving in a straight line and then in a zigzag pattern across the sky: probable observation of a satellite.
--- seg-cea66259b80994691855b3d5 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 8, 2012, around 11:30 PM, a witness observed with the naked eye, from their garden, a bright point in a clear and unobstructed sky. Initially straight, the point's trajectory continued in a zigzag pattern oriented North-South for several minutes. No other testimonies were collected regarding this phenomenon. The witness's description, for the straight portion of the trajectory, strongly resembles a satellite, many of which pass at this time (see Calsky list). The zigzag at the end of the trajectory is not uncommon, but could be the result of the physiological phenomenon of autocinematic (see pages 26 and 27 of the document "Aids to Identification of Flying Objects" available on the GEIPAN website, currently accessible via news). Several satellites are candidates according to the Calsky site, IGS 1A appears to be the best match (see satellite passage in the document). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable satellite observation, perceived on a portion of its trajectory with an autocinematic phenomenon.
