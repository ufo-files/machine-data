Description: On November 30, 1977, at approximately 5:35 PM, a driver reported seeing a luminous phenomenon multiple times during their commute. The phenomenon consisted of two very bright white orbs, with numerous lights seemingly delineating a craft between them. The craft moved at varying altitudes and changed direction without any particular noise being heard. No flickering was observed on the lights. No radio or vehicle interference was noted by the witness, who then lost sight of the phenomenon. No other testimonies were collected.
