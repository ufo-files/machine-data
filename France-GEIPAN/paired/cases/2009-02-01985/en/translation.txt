Description: A witness reports two observations made from their 9th-floor kitchen looking northwest. On February 4, 2009 at 2:45 AM, the witness observes unusual movements of colored shapes that quickly disappear into the horizon. On February 8, 2009 at 6:45 AM, the witness and their son observe a red-orange sphere moving upward and then downward. The first PAN observed, red-orange in color, crosses the witness's field of vision from southeast to northwest, aligning with wind direction: a festive lantern (known as a "Thai lantern") could potentially explain this observation. The second PAN observed appears exactly in the direction of the Moon, which was not mentioned by the witness. The red and changing appearance may have confused the witness at this late hour of the night. For the main observation (PAN #2), the GEIPAN classifies the case as "A": observation of the Moon at sunset, partially obscured by clouds. For the other observations, classifications would be "C" due to lack of information.
