--- seg-7889b16e1ae49ea9ab187076 [needs-review] ---
Description: Between 2:00 and 2:30 AM on September 11, 1983, a witness observed above his house and the surrounding houses a peculiar luminous phenomenon. Cylindrical beams one meter in diameter brightly illuminated the houses. These 8 fixed bands of slightly yellow color were spaced 3 to 4 meters apart and gradually disappeared into the clouds. No further information was received about this phenomenon.
