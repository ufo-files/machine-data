Description: On the night of Saturday to Sunday, July 12, 2015 at 12:45 a.m., several people at a friend's gathering were intrigued by the silent passage of more than a dozen orange orbs in the clear sky. The trajectory was straight. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of orange orbs crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from SSE to East, which is consistent with the West wind reported in Le Havre-Octeville (see the Météociel weather report for the observation date). The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", a probable observation of Thai lanterns.
