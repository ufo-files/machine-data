Description: On May 8, 2008, the GEIPAN received by email a brief description of two observations made in the evening of April 29, 2008, by a driver (T1) and his passenger (T2). At the entrance to the hamlet of La Chalousière, commune of Vallet (44), they noticed the presence at low altitude of a luminous phenomenon composed of two lights. Just after dropping off his colleague, T1 quickly made a second observation of the slow movement at very low altitude of a lit shape with colored lights. No particular noise was heard during the two observations. T1 reported his two observations in a questionnaire on January 8, 2009. No other testimony was collected. Based on the objective elements analyzed, namely: - An apparently immobile object, similar to an airplane approaching the witnesses on an axis close to or identical to their movement axis, - Presence of two or three powerful lights, similar to those of airplane landing lights, - Dark night and layered cloud cover that did not clearly distinguish the contours of the plane on one hand and sometimes obscured it on the other (according to the Nantes bulletin from METAR SCT012 and BKN055, presence of scattered clouds (3/8 to 4/8) at a ceiling of 1200 feet (350m) and a very cloudy sky (5/8 to 7/8) at 5500 feet (1700m), we can conclude that this observation likely concerns one or two airplanes in the approach phase to Nantes Atlantique Airport with landing lights on. This case is to be classified as "C" due to the lack of precise information on the exact time (around 21h or 22h), the precise observation locations (especially for the second observation) and the observation directions of the witnesses.
