Description: Recent testimony (August 17, 2009) of an old observation made at the beginning of July 1965 (the exact date is not specified by the witness; an arbitrary date is given as July 1, 1965; the witness also does not specify the exact location of the observation). A concise email from a witness reports an observation made from a vehicle on an undetermined road between Moustiers-Sainte-Marie (04) and Manosque (04): around 7 PM or 7:30 PM, while traveling through the Verdon Gorges and crossing a lavender field, the witness, then aged 14, and his 4-year-old brother, saw from the back of the car "a large plate turned over in a field" and "children coming out of that plate." No further information is collected, and no other testimony will be reported. This at first sight strange case is too poorly documented for an investigation (no precise location or date). The additional elements requested by GEIPAN were not provided. This case is classified as "C" - Impossible investigation. However, it is noted that the position of the Moon at its rise in the southeast on July 11 could be a cause of confusion. This obviously does not explain the small beings (children?) observed.
