Description: Recent testimony (August 17, 2009) of an old observation made at the beginning of July 1965 (the exact date is not specified by the witness; an arbitrary date is given as July 1, 1965; the witness also does not specify the exact location of the observation). By a concise email, a witness reports an observation made from a vehicle on an undetermined road between Moustiers-Sainte-Marie (04) and Manosque (04): around 7 PM or 7:30 PM, while traveling through the Verdon Gorges and crossing a lavender field, the witness, then aged 14, and his 4-year-old brother, saw from the back of the car "a large plate turned over in a field" and "children coming out of the plate." No other information was gathered, and no other testimony was reported. This seemingly strange case is too poorly documented for an investigation (no precise location or date). The additional information requested by GEIPAN was not provided. This case is classified as "C," impossible to investigate. However, we note that the position of the Moon at its rising in the southeast on July 11 could be a cause of misperception. This, of course, does not explain the small beings (children?) observed.
