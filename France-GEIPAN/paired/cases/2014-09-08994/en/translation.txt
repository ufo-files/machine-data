Summary: Observations of the rapid passage of a luminous phenomenon with a trail in the sky: observation of a large fireball, consequence of the atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
--- seg-1491036ea7bc14ae944e0680 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: This phenomenon was reported to GEIPAN by two witnesses located in Toulouse and one in Gleize (69). It was recorded by the camera of GEIPAN in Toulouse for more than 10 seconds. 7 other testimonies reached GEIPAN in the days that followed. (see attached: "summary of observations"). Seen from Toulouse, it was observed crossing the southern horizon from west to east at a relatively low altitude (from 10° to 0° according to the GEIPAN camera). It was noticed by numerous witnesses in the Perpignan region and reported by local press: New asteroid in the Catalan sky. This phenomenon has all the characteristics of an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid (small rocky body orbiting the Sun). The phenomenon occurred at an altitude between 60 and 25 kilometers, and it ended its course over the Mediterranean Sea. This meteoroid lasted relatively long, due to its trajectory being quite shallow in relation to the Earth's surface; the meteoroid of September 7, on the contrary, had a much steeper trajectory. This is not a reentry of space debris (no known reentry, and too high a speed for a debris in Earth orbit). This observation case is very consistent due to multiple testimonies and camera recordings. This case is very little strange after analysis. The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'A': observation of a large bolide, consequence of the atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
