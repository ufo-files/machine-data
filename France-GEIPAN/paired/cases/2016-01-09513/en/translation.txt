Summary: Observations of rapid movements of a green ball that splits into several green glowing balls: likely observations of effects of a playful laser.
--- seg-5cbaa32033e3cc313d6f8d65 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Sunday, January 24, 2016, around 00:40, a witness on their terrace saw a green glowing ball pass by and stop in the middle of the trees. The witness returns to look for his companion, and in less than two minutes they observe the silent movements of the phenomenon. The ball moves above a soccer field then makes a very quick round trip before splitting into four smaller balls. They disappear behind a nearby hedge to reappear in front. The 4 green balls depart very quickly towards the NNO. Intrigued witnesses come down into the street, see no other witnesses, and will not see the phenomenon again. One of the witnesses provides a statement to the gendarmerie. No further testimony will be collected. The gendarmerie states that a nightclub located 9km from the commune is equipped with a laser projector: the latter is visible within a radius of approximately 20km. In addition, a wind farm is installed in the area. The witnesses are reliable and do their best to accurately recount what they saw and experienced. Following an on-site investigation, checks are made (see the investigation report). The phenomenon is not very strange as it can resemble the effects (not always known and thus surprising) that a small green laser emitter equipped with a few functions (emitting a wide beam, or several small beams) manipulated by a neighbor could produce. The collected elements do not allow for the identification of the origin of the laser (without a neighborhood investigation), but the laser remains the probable cause of the phenomenon. The GEIPAN classifies the case as B: playful laser.
