Description: Case previously published under the name Pont-De-Salars (12). On September 12, 1996, around 8:00 PM, a mother and her daughter, while driving out of Flavin (12), observed an object they could not identify. The craft consisted of an oval skirt and a cylinder topped with a dome (with three bright red lights) and a fluorescent green light spinning like a beacon. The object, silent, was suspended about 4 meters above the ground, approximately 10 meters from the road. Its size was compared to that of two cars. After a few minutes, the object moved away in a northern or northwest direction. That same evening, after the observation, each witness drew a picture and took notes. They reported their observations to the gendarmerie the following day. The observation was estimated to have lasted about two minutes. A computer-aided drawing was made by a gendarme based on the witnesses' information. An investigation at the scene failed to uncover any traces or clues. On the evening of the observation, the sky was clear and starry, and there was no wind. Air traffic and radar showed no abnormal activity. Surveillance patrols conducted in the following days revealed nothing. Searches for additional witnesses nearby were negative. No similar cases were reported in the press or on television. Despite the gendarmerie's investigation and the witnesses' descriptions, no coherent explanation could be given for the phenomenon. The phenomenon remains unexplained.
