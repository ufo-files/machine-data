Description: On March 19, 1979, between 11:10 PM and 11:15 PM, two witnesses observed from their villa a luminous phenomenon that intrigued them. Looking out a window, they saw a large, stationary sphere of bluish-white color about fifty meters from the ground. This phenomenon was observed for approximately thirty seconds before gradually fading away. That night, a severe orange (presumably lightning) struck Nice, and lightning crisscrossed the sky. The witness visited the site on March 21 and learned that a lightning bolt had struck a power pole at about 11:10 PM on March 19, causing a huge ball of light. This person likely witnessed an electrical phenomenon due to the storm.
