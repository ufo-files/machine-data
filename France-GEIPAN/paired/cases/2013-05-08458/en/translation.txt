Description: On Saturday, May 25, 2013 at 11:05 PM, a witness observed in the sky a slowly moving, flickering light with a red halo. No particular noise was heard during the 2-minute observation. The light ascended into the sky and disappeared into a cloud. The witness described a flickering light that alternated between white, red, and yellow, moving slowly from the West and ascending into the sky until it disappeared. The video shows a yellow light surrounded by red. It is noted that the wind was facing the witness (see the weather report). The observation is entirely consistent with that of a Thai lantern released in the line of sight of the witness, at one or two kilometers away. The day and time of observation (Saturday evening) is typical for the release of such lanterns during private parties. The perceived flickering likely corresponds to an imperfect combustion of the burner. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
