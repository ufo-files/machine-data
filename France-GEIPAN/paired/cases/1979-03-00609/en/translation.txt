Summary: Observation of a rocket-shaped object
--- seg-a861681ea9a3d631fe9f6980 [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 18, 1979, at approximately 9:15 PM, a driver and her passenger observed a dark, fluorescent gray object in the shape of a rocket three meters above the expressway. This silent object moved slowly towards the North. The car quickly passed underneath it. On the return trip at around 9:45 PM, the same driver saw, almost at the same location, a flat wing of about 3 square meters above the expressway. She described the object as consisting of a gray metallic grid a few centimeters thick, all appearing to have an undulating motion. The object, flying over the car, stopped 3 or 4 meters in front of her vehicle. Impressed, the driver accelerated to quickly pass underneath without hearing any particular noise or seeing lights. No heat source seemed to emanate from the object. These observations lasted only a few seconds under poor weather conditions (continuous rain at night) and near the Brest-Guipavas airport. The gendarmerie found no traces, and no other information was gathered about this phenomenon, which remains unidentified.
