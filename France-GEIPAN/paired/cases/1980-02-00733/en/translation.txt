Description: On February 13, 1980, around 5:40 a.m., a PTT (public telecommunications) employee on his way to work noticed a strange light phenomenon in a clear sky. Above the Lyon mountains, he observed for 8 minutes a glow in the shape of a half-moon. This glow was yellow-orange in color and moved slowly on a north-south trajectory. The glow gradually faded away. Only another witness who accompanied the individual was able to see the phenomenon; however, he did not make a statement to the gendarmerie. Examining the sky map at that time shows the moon very faint (new moon on the 16th) and very low on the horizon, but in the direction of the observation. The witness specifies that there was no moon; in fact, he likely observed the glow of the moon without recognizing it (magnitude -8.9).
