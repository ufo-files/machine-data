--- seg-79bf6faf47048740d0ce4479 [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reports in April 2008 an observation that occurred in the summer of 1992. As the witness cannot recall the date and month, the case is arbitrarily dated to July 1st, 1992. Around midnight, at the Mirador location in Cap-Ferret, two individuals observe the slow, silent, and low-altitude passage of two superimposed triangles illuminated at their ends by a white light. The phenomenon abruptly ends. This testimony of a strange observation does not mention a precise observation date. As a result, an investigation is impossible. This case is classified "C" due to the lack of a precise date.
