Description: On January 15, 1989, just before 9 PM, after watching a television weather show that mentioned possible unusual planet observations, a woman went outside with her children to observe the sky using high-powered binoculars. She observed a bright glowing point that she described as a shimmering oval shape moving in a "caterpillar" pattern. The gendarmes called to the scene saw a shimmering bright point but could not determine a precise shape. It is likely that the witness observed a very bright planet or star and that the reported effects were due to the motion of the high-powered binoculars.
