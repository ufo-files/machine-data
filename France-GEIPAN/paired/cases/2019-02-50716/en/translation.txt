--- seg-bc835f9014acfc4f0a951022 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 13, 2019, around 9 PM, several witnesses at their homes were surprised by an intense humming and observed the low-altitude passage of a dark mass in the shape of an "inverted V" with lights. The observations were very brief (less than a minute). The PAN moved slowly on a straight trajectory before disappearing from the witnesses' view. The noise persisted for a few more moments before fading away. An on-site investigation allowed for the clarification and confirmation of several pieces of information (location, azimuth, observation time, etc.) provided by the witnesses in their questionnaires (see the investigation report). The analysis of the radar map received from the CNOA shows without ambiguity, on that day and at the time of the observation, military traffic at low altitude passing near the witnesses at 8:07 PM UTC (9:07 PM local time). Since the Transall C-160 planes have been replaced by the A400M, the witnesses likely heard the passage of this much larger aircraft. The GEIPAN classified the case as A: Misidentification with a military plane passing at low altitude.
