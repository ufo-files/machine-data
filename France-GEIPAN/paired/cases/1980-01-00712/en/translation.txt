Summary: Observation of the planet Venus
--- seg-6116d27257b1c197e636ad29 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 5, 1980, starting at 6:30 PM, several witnesses observed a luminous phenomenon that intrigued them. A sphere emitting intense light was stationary, then moved slowly and silently through the clear sky. Colors and volume varied according to the witnesses. Some saw it appear to be rotating on its own axis. Based on the witnesses' positions and observation directions, the phenomenon was located in the southwest. The phenomenon disappeared around 7:30 PM. Examining the sky map for this date and time reveals that the witnesses simply observed Venus, which was particularly visible and bright at that time of year (magnitude -3.8). Venus' sunset time was indeed 7:30 PM, as the witnesses indicated.
