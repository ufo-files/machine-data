Summary: Observation of rotating light rings likely originating from a public laser projector.
--- seg-e4f372c6136ab3bc0613ff70 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of August 29 to 30, 1997, around midnight, several witnesses were alerted by the abnormal behavior of farm animals. Upon arriving at the scene, they observed a white, rounded glow with an outer ring and an inner section adorned with small, glowing circles, projecting beams of light towards the ground. The entire object was moving by spinning. At 2 a.m., as the witnesses went to sleep, the phenomenon was still present. Although no evidence from the investigation supports it, it appears that this observation was a reflection on clouds of a promotional laser projector capable of operating within a radius of 8 to 10 km.
