Description: A witness reports via email repeated observations of blue, more or less round light shapes in the cloudy sky over the Paris region. The witness sends photographs. No other testimony about this phenomenon will be collected. The photograph strongly suggests a reflection on the clouds of lighting coming from the ground, which is not surprising: although the precise location of the witness and the direction of observation are unknown, the Paris region is filled with lighting on monuments, town halls... and of course, night clubs whose powerful lights get lost in the cloud background. This is a very likely case of misidentification with lighting reflections on the cloud background. The very low strangeness of this case does not justify a more in-depth investigation. This case is classified B, probable observation of nighttime lighting on clouds.
