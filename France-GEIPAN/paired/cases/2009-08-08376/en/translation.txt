Description: This is a 2009 observation reported three years later. The witness states, "A great majority of people I told about these facts laughed at me. Nevertheless, after careful consideration, I today believe that your official body should take into account my observations and photos." During a festive terrace evening at 23:00, a reddish-orange glowing point was observed, followed by a dozen other points, moving across the starry sky. The entire group stopped for 30 seconds, then each point disappeared one by one. No sounds were heard. Fifteen people witnessed the phenomenon. Only the witness provided a testimony and photographs. The observation shares many characteristics with Thai lanterns, particularly the colors, formation movement, and sequential extinction of lights. The provided photos also support this hypothesis. The movement and stop are weather-compatible, indicating a wind with slight gusts (Infoclimat). The sudden extinction of the flame is often interpreted as a departure at a dizzying speed. This testimony vividly illustrates the emotion that such an interpretation can evoke, with the witness stating, "It's simply an OVNI, because current technology on Earth does not allow for such acceleration speed into space." In 2008, Thai lanterns were already on sale. It was a summer weekend evening conducive to festive events. The GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
