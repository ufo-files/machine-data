Description: On October 5, 1981, between 9 a.m. and 10 a.m., a driver and her passenger were traveling on a national road. They suddenly spotted a black object resembling a pointed stick crossing the road slowly from left to right, just in front of their car. The observation lasted two seconds before the object disappeared completely. No other information was gathered on this phenomenon for which we lack data.
