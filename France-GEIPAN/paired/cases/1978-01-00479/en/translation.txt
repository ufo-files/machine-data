Description: On January 25, 1978, around 6:30 PM, seven children playing in the street observed the passage of an orange, oval-shaped craft. Red and white lights blinked alternately at the base of the craft. Approaching from the south, it followed a curve to head west at the same altitude as tourist planes. The children's accounts differ regarding the noise heard during the craft's passage. No other testimony regarding this probable aircraft's passage was collected.
