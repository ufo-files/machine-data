Summary: Multiple observations of a red-orange phenomenon in the sky and disappearance to the West: mistaken for the Moon at sunset.
--- seg-0bfe58a1c1a050bf94e47d00 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 18, 1977, around 1:20 a.m., approximately ten people in a hamlet of the commune of Sainte-Cérotte (72) observed a red-orange phenomenon in the sky. The witnesses were unable to gauge the distance of the phenomenon and did not agree on its shape. However, all noticed the movements (descending and ascending) of the object and its gradual disappearance towards the West. No unusual noises were heard during the observations of this phenomenon, and no other testimonies were collected by the gendarmerie. This observation case is part of cases classified "C" by the GEPAN under the name of SAINT-CALAIS (72) 18.12.1977. It was recently reviewed (see the document Notes d'enquête). This case is consistent (approximately ten witnesses), and the description of the PAN observed is fairly precise. The sincerity and credibility of the witnesses have never been questioned. The described phenomenon shares many common characteristics (observation duration, shape, size, color) with a perfectly known astronomical object: the Moon at sunset. Moreover, the Moon was clearly present in the observed area of the sky, while the witnesses made no mention of it. It is not the visual perception of the witnesses that is in question, but the interpretation they make of their observation through their feelings (fatigue, night observation, poor interpretation of the Moon's position). In the current classification of GEIPAN, this case of average strangeness is classified as "A," an observation related to a misperception with the Moon.
