Summary: A long observation of a bright light point in the night sky, initially stationary and then in motion: observation of a gendarmerie helicopter deployed during an intervention following an urban rodeo.
--- seg-c7856989245fe43038cc5746 [needs-review] ---
Description: During the night of July 21 to 22, 2023, a resident of L’UNION (31) noticed, from their home, a very bright red light, stationary, emitting red pulsating lights. Initially thought to be an airplane, it does not warrant much attention and goes inside. About fifteen minutes later, upon returning to his balcony, he again observes this red light (with a slight green glow also visible) that remains stationary. Suddenly, the red light begins to move to the left, stops for a few minutes, then resumes its course to the right. During a period of approximately twenty minutes, the light performs back-and-forth movements, from left to right, with occasional stationary pauses. The witness continues to observe the phenomenon uninterrupted for approximately twenty minutes until 2:00 a.m., at which point the light disappears towards the northwest at approximately 2:05 a.m. The witness observed the phenomenon continuously for approximately twenty minutes until 2:00 a.m., and then around 2:05 a.m. the light disappeared in a northwest direction. No further testimony will be collected. The case consistency is very good, although there is only one witness. Indeed, the testimony is complete and precise, the PAN was able to be filmed and photographed. The investigation revealed that the observation corresponds to that of a gendarmerie helicopter (see the investigation report). The description of the unidentified aero-spatial phenomenon (PAN) provided by the witness presents striking similarities to an aeronautical observation: presence of red and green lights, as well as a flickering bright point. The apparent trajectory of PAN, initially stationary before performing back-and-forth movements, strongly supports the possibility of a helicopter. A complete analysis of air traffic by the CNOA confirms that the PAN does indeed correspond to a gendarmerie helicopter. Further investigation into the rounds made by this helicopter reveals it was a gendarmerie intervention following an urban rodeo. The witness mentioned that they did not identify any aircraft using the Flightradar24 application, which is normal as Flightradar24 does not track all military aircraft. The case is classified A, observation of a gendarmerie helicopter.
