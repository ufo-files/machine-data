Description: On Saturday, March 31, 2018 at 7:37 PM, a witness observing from their window noticed the movement in a clear sky of a white, elongated object. The last one moves quickly in a straight line towards southeast. The witness films the scene, then after 50 seconds, returns to his activities. Only one witness statement is collected with the video. The witness observed an airplane. The video shows the PAN passing transversely into the view from the SE-oriented house. There is indeed (see reconstruction) an airplane passing at that moment in a flight orientation from S0 to NE and thus parallel to the horizon line as indicated by the witness according to an elevation of the order of 45°, consistent with the video's appearance. The witness does not recognize the plane even though there is an airplane noise in the video. He is intrigued by: - an altitude lower than an airplane. Indeed, this one is at an altitude of 9000 meters, lower than a cruise flight; - the shape, indeed, we see like a cigar. This is classic. When the sun is very low and illuminates the aircraft strongly transversely (full sun East), the fuselage reflects strongly and the only visible wing is lit according to its section and therefore little or not reflective; - the absence of contrail, contrails are far from being systematic and depend on atmospheric conditions and/or altitude. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as A: airplane.
