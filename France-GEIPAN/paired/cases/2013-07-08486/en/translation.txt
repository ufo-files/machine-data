Description: On July 13, 2013, between 11:30 PM and 11:40 PM, a witness was intrigued by the successive and silent passage of several orange glowing orbs in the sky. The witness's account and photos exhibit all the characteristics of a report of an observation of Thai lanterns: orange orbs moving with the wind (northeasterly wind reported at Cap Ferret). The time and date of the year are also very conducive to this type of event. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
