Summary: Observation of a white ball in the sky that moves, stops twice, and then departs at high speed: Insufficient information.
--- seg-77782fbf61cba8077bd6e2ea [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 19, 2022, at 3:50 PM, two witnesses were intrigued by the very fast passage of a white ball in the sky. It "stopped twice above them... for a few seconds, then resumed at great speed." No noise was heard. Despite follow-ups by GEIPAN, no questionnaires were collected. Only a Gendarmerie Protocol (P.V.) reports the observation. The observation of a white ball moving from northeast to southwest, i.e. with the wind, with two brief stops above the witnesses, suggests the hypothesis of an object carried by the wind. However, the lack of elements does not allow for a deeper analysis of this hypothesis (see the investigation report). The description in the Gendarmerie P.V. is incomplete, not allowing for proper handling of this case: no elevation data at the beginning and end of the observation, no precise description of the trajectory, no estimates of the apparent size of PAN, no information on brightness. Moreover, it seems that only one of the witnesses was interviewed by the gendarmerie, very briefly (a few descriptive lines) and only by phone. Despite two phone follow-ups by the investigator on July 5 and September 27, 2022, no response to the request for additional testimony could be obtained. The case is classified C due to lack of information.
