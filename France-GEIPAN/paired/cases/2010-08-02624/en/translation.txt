Description: The witness observes, over several consecutive days, a brighter phenomenon than a star. This object appears at the same location each morning in the sky facing south. A classic case of astronomical misperception, this observation case exhibits all the characteristics of a Jupiter observation, particularly the repeated nature from one day to the next, and the brightness typical of nearby planets. This case is classified A, perfectly identified as an observation of Jupiter.
