Description: On July 24, 2013, at midnight, a witness observed more than 10 flying objects over the course of 30 minutes, with varying shapes and behaviors. No other testimonies were collected. This atypical testimony reports numerous apparently spectacular phenomena and varied behaviors. Given the magnitude of the phenomenon, it is surprising that only one witness came forward, unless the objects were very faint lights: in that case, they could be satellites or shooting stars, which are numerous and visible at this time of night during this time of year (see Calsky log). We also note two flashes from Iridium satellites, which were quite spectacular. On the same day, the GEIPAN received a report of a fireball observation (BACHANT (59) 24.07.2013) and many witnesses reported numerous large shooting stars. This time of year is a peak for various meteor showers. Due to a lack of precise observation data from the witness, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of precise information on the position in the sky and the direction of movement, or photos. The observation is likely a combination of phenomena of different origins.
