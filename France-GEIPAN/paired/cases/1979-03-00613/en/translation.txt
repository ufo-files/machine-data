Description: Between 21:30 and 22:30 on March 26, 1979, motorists observed in the sky an object illuminated by three lights. One of the lights was white, and the other two were flashing green and red. The object moved quickly, changing direction. One of the witnesses stated that he made this observation several times between February and March. These individuals likely observed an aircraft of helicopter type.
