Summary: Firework explosion creating smoke and ground traces: hoax.
--- seg-b091fca507da4d4cd295b178 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 8, 1976, the gendarmerie was alerted by the press that on April 5, 1976, around 9 PM, a person observed a OVNI near a sports field. At the exact location of the observation, several burnt herb traces were noted, and the charred trunk of a tree was found. During his deposition, the witness stated he saw a light alternating between white and red after a smoke blast. He provided the dimensions of an aluminum-colored object that overhung the stream. The witness heard a faint hiss. The observation lasted 5 minutes. The gendarmerie investigation determined that the traces were caused by fireworks ignited by several individuals. The witness admitted he had never seen a craft and that journalists had told him the dimensions of the object he thought he had seen. The case was classified as A: Hoax.
