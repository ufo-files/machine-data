Description: On October 16, 2021, at 10:00 PM, the witness randomly saw an oval object that he believed to be a lost Mylar balloon moving at a constant speed in the clear sky. He called out to his father, who also saw it. The PAN continued its progression without any apparent change in speed until the witness lost sight of it. The observation lasted approximately 30 seconds. Only one testimony was collected. The consistency was average, with two witnesses, but only one provided a statement and did not take any photos or videos of the PAN (see the investigation report). The investigation revealed that the witness very likely observed a Mylar balloon, dimly lit by the city lights, moving with the wind. This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- Oval shape, common for this type of balloon
- Color: described by the witness as brown-salmon, reflecting city lights on its surface
- Brightness: none, the balloon is only actually visible due to the reflection of city lights on its surface
- Noise: absence
- Movement: generally in the direction of the dominant wind
- Location of the PAN: above a large city on a Saturday night, a propitious time for local festivals using such balloons.

The GEIPAN classified the case as "A": observation of a Mylar balloon.
