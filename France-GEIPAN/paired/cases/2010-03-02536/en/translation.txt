Summary: observation of the movement of orange balls.
--- seg-c2f0fc07944b35fba509a4a3 [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 6, 2010, around 11:05 PM, a motorist and their passengers observed the silent movement of approximately fifteen red-orange orbs. A video taken on a cell phone during the observation, which lasted over five minutes, shows the gradual disappearance of the glowing orbs. The detailed description provided by the motorist closely resembles that of Thai lanterns released during celebrations. Following the publication of an article in a local newspaper about this observation, a person responsible for releasing 13 Thai lanterns came forward. A gendarmerie investigation will also conclude that Thai lanterns were released at a private party located 2.5 km from the observation location of the witnesses.
