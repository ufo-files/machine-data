--- seg-bbf7a0baa0988a82cdda7080 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 29, 2021, at 11:07 PM, a resident of DOLE (39) was preparing to go to bed when, upon opening his window to close his blinds, he was surprised by the appearance of a dazzling light. The witness describes a white line moving silently from the forest of LA SERRE towards the southern part of the forest of CHAUX. The PAN had an oval shape, moving at a high speed and at an apparently constant altitude. The witness observed this phenomenon for approximately 6 to 7 seconds. No other testimonies were collected. The case consistency is low: a single, concise testimony (one report and no quotes), with no sketches, photos, or videos. The description of the PAN is quite typical of a meteor observation: very bright, moving in a straight line at an extremely high speed, for just a few seconds. Research in databases listing meteors allowed the identification of an atmospheric reentry corresponding to the PAN mentioned in this testimony. This meteor was observed from northern Italy, with a schedule and trajectory perfectly consistent with the observation described (see investigation report for more details). The case is classified as A, observation of a meteor. *According to the methodology of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony.
