Summary: Sky observation of an object that appears to be on fire and falling: observation of condensation trail from a large commercial aircraft.
--- seg-dcdac6fd140c92d31bbfad5c [needs-review] ---
On April 11, 2023, an agent of CNES received an email report concerning an observation of an unexplained aerospace phenomenon. This email was forwarded to GEIPAN. Because this phenomenon was unique, GEIPAN exceptionally decided to investigate it despite the absence of a technical questionnaire. It was processed during a participatory meeting, during which investigators share their ongoing inquiries. A simplified report was drafted for this inquiry (see attached document). The witness's perception was observing "an object falling that seemed to be in fusion." Image analysis suggests sunlight scattering on a condensation trail of an airplane. The time of 07:04 corresponds to sunrise, explaining the orange, "in fusion" appearance of the observation. The investigation shows that an airplane was passing in the direction of the observation at approximately 07:04:45, exactly as indicated by the photo 1 provided by the witness (see witness photos). It was British Airways flight BA54 from Johannesburg to London. The airplane is an Airbus A380-841, flying at over 12,000 meters and over 800 km/h, according to the 349° heading. The strangeness of the observation is created by a perspective effect that can give the perception of a falling object, as well as by the scattering of the orange sunlight of sunrise, giving the impression of a burning or fusing object. The significant size of the airplane, an Airbus A380-841 with a 80-meter wingspan and 4 Rolls Royce engines, created a very wide condensation trail, unusually wide. This contributed to the non-identification and perceived strangeness. GEIPAN concludes that it was an observation of a condensation trail of a large commercial airplane. The scattering of the sunrise sunlight created colors giving the appearance of "fusion." The receding trajectory gave the impression of an object falling in perspective. GEIPAN classification conclusion: observation of a condensation trail of a large commercial airplane.
