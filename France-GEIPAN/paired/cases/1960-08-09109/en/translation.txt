Description: Recent testimony (02/16/2015) of an undated observation (the 1st is given arbitrarily). The witness reports three observations in the same document; this translation addresses the third observation. In August 1960, just before noon, the witness, then aged 10, observed lightning during a severe storm when they saw a very bright, pale yellow sphere appear above them, moving slowly along a straight trajectory; it stopped above the roof of a nearby house, returned to its starting point, and disappeared, hidden by trees. The observation report of this phenomenon was submitted to the Lightning Laboratory, which identified it as a typical case of ball lightning, as the witness had suspected. The ending is not so strange; ball lightning often follows the ascending vortices of cumulonimbus clouds, and the description of the overall atmosphere is indicative of the passage of a storm accompanied by an Arcus (it is almost nighttime, with a very low sky). It is common to see "chimneys" rising towards the anvil, and often the sky and even some rays of sunlight can be seen during the storm. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of ball lightning. The lack of a precise date does not allow for a formal verification of this hypothesis.
