Summary: Long observation of the silent presence of a glowing ball in the sky: probable observation of a weather balloon.
--- seg-fa832e4fd926d17e7f71f945 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Wednesday, August 21, 2013, at 9:46 a.m., a walker was intrigued by a bright light in the clear sky above the sea. The brightness gradually faded before disappearing. The observation lasted between 20 and 25 minutes. No other witnesses were reported for this bright and silent phenomenon. Astronomical conditions: Sun: azimuth: 104°, elevation: 32°. Atmospheric conditions: Steady north-east wind: 19-28 km/h, and clear sky. The observation, at the beginning or end of the day, for 20 minutes, of a nearly immobile bright point in the sky, clearly suggests a high-altitude balloon. The GEIPAN could not verify this hypothesis, which nevertheless well fits the observation. There is no regular release of weather balloons from Nice or its surroundings, but several scientific institutes are likely to release them in the South-East region or in Italy. Modeling the movement of a high-altitude balloon is extremely complex because high-altitude winds, often very powerful, are different from ground winds. The GEIPAN classifies this peculiar observation case "B" as probably a weather balloon.
