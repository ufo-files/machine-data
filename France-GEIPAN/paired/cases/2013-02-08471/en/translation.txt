Summary: Observations of several red lights in the sky: probable observations of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-2a523dd6fdfa9d392a103564 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Sunday, February 17, 2013, around 7 PM, two witnesses observed from their vehicle red lights aligned that appeared to be stationary in the sky. The description provided by the witnesses is consistent with the observation of Thai lanterns: several red lights in the sky, apparently at low altitude, on an evening during the weekend. Since the wind was blowing slightly from the East (see Meteociel report), these lanterns could have been released from Curgies, likely after a party. Given the light wind and easterly direction, the witnesses, who actually saw them slightly receding, were unable to clearly perceive their movement. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
