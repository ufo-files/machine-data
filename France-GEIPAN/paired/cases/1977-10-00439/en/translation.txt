Description: On October 9, 1977, starting at 8:30 PM and lasting until 11:30 PM, several people, including gendarmes, observed the movements of a luminous phenomenon with varied colors that was not conform to the stars. Another luminous phenomenon was observed on the same day around 9 PM by a witness. This witness reported a ground observation of two intensely white and symmetrical light jets. A third observation on October 12, 1977, at 7:45 PM, was reported: a witness observed for two seconds a phenomenon that illuminated his entire surroundings, consisting of light jets with colors resembling a fireworks sun. The gendarmerie investigation did not draw any connections between the observations, for which we lack information.
