Summary: Observation of a triangular flying object with a span of 50 meters, no noise, variable speed.
--- seg-0db018e97381ad09a27bfb25 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 27, 1980, around 10 PM, a motorist and his passenger observed in the sky a dark, triangular-shaped mass. Red and white flashing lights at each end gave the object's span, which the witness estimated at 50 meters. The craft, initially stationary, then moved, tilting slowly while remaining both 50 meters from the ground and the car. After regaining altitude, the craft tilted to the right and disappeared into the sky at a high speed. During the 4 to 5 minutes of observation, with the windows closed, no unusual noises were heard. No other information was gathered on this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
