Summary: Observation of silent movements of an object with changing shape in a clear sky: probable observation of a cluster of "Mylar" balloons.
--- seg-c98e7aac2d93f872cd438803 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Tuesday, June 16, 2015, around 9:25 a.m., two people on a beach were intrigued by the presence in the clear sky of a shape described as first triangular then round, composed of balls, one out of two of which is bright and blinking, and the other white or light gray, non-blinking. The PAN then formed a straight line of 6 distinct objects that eventually disappeared at 10:10 while gaining altitude. No noise was heard during the maneuvers in the sky. Only one testimony was collected. Given the elements gathered (see the investigation report), namely: - the appearance, color, brightness, and behavior of the PAN. - the favorable weather conditions: sun present and transition wind, variable and very weak, following the land breeze. - the summer period favorable. We can conclude that the PAN observed by the witnesses is certainly a cluster of Mylar balloons, linked together, with high reflective power and swayed by the changing wind. This testimony is of good consistency: precise, but from a single witness without a photo. The observation is not strange because the PAN is ultimately easily characterizable, both in its appearance and behavior. This case is to be classified as "B" as a very probable observation of a cluster of Mylar balloons.
