Summary: Observation of the Moon under unusual conditions
--- seg-fdcaa1873c04cc83ba493eff [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 7, 1997, around 2 a.m., a couple watching a fireworks display observed a stationary light phenomenon to the right of the fireworks. Simultaneously, in another commune, another couple made the same observation. One of the witnesses filmed the phenomenon, which appeared to be very close, about ten meters from the ground. The joint investigation by the gendarmerie and the SEPRA will unequivocally show that the observed phenomenon was the moon under particular conditions (cloudy sky and beginning eclipse).
