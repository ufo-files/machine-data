Description: On September 2, 2022, at 6:35 AM, during a strong storm, a resident of Frontignan stepped onto their terrace. The sky was very overcast and low, and it was still dark; they then spotted a very bright, flickering green light in the sky, visible above the roof of a neighboring house. Intrigued by its immobility, the witness thought it might not be very high due to its position beneath dark, thick clouds. The flickering was rapid, approximately 1 flash per second. The witness took a photo with their smartphone. Limited by their phone's zoom, they decided to get their binoculars and, upon returning, noticed the light had moved towards them. They left the terrace again to retrieve a camera with a zoom lens, but when they returned, the PAN had disappeared. The perceived oddity stemmed from the "very bright, flickering green light in the sky" that was initially stationary and then moved towards the witness, which the witness was unable to identify. The witness's description strongly suggests the observation of a drone in flight. Several drone flight training centers or drone photography companies operate in the vicinity of Frontignan. One of these companies, DTS Drone Thau Services located in Sète, contacted by the GEIPAN, confirmed that the drone hypothesis is plausible, noting that the green color and flickering lights are very indicative. For example, the attached general information document regarding Mavic drone light codes states that flashing green lights correspond to a "safe flight" mode. Furthermore, DTS Drone Thau specified that there has been an increase in drone flights in the region in recent times. The residual oddity is low in light of these elements. The GEIPAN classified the observation as A: Drone.
