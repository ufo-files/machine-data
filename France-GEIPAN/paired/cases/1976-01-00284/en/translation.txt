Summary: Observation of the movement of a luminous circular object.
--- seg-6e8aa6405843fff2c2ba5aef [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 19, 1976, around 9:30 PM, a witness exiting their home observed for 3 minutes an orange-phosphorescent disk-shaped craft approximately 2.50 meters in diameter. It appeared to be resting on a paved path along a ridge line. The witness saw the object move at a low altitude down the valley before disappearing behind farm buildings. No unusual noises were heard during the object's movement. No distinctive tracks were noted by the witness and his son at the presumed location of the object. Two other individuals testified to seeing a peculiar light that evening on the road, but their accounts remain very brief. The case is classified C due to lack of information.
