Summary: Observation of rapidly moving light phenomena.
--- seg-30deb8e4d33f68a7ce07adaf [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 25, 1979, around 0:45 a.m., a witness in their garden observed an unusual light phenomenon in the sky. They saw two pale green light trails moving parallel in a north-northwest to south-southeast direction. These trails were preceded by objects that moved very quickly without making any noise. Despite the presence of many people due to a local party, no other witnesses or information were gathered about this phenomenon, for which we have limited data.
