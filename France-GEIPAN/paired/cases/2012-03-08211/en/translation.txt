Summary: Successive observations of two bright points crossing the Toulouse night sky: lack of exploitable data.
--- seg-43fb4d801f2f4381101b9348 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 19 at 23:45, three friends, leaving a restaurant, observed from the Place du Capitole in Toulouse the successive movements of two luminous phenomena: first, a bright point crossing the sky in a straight line in less than 10 seconds, then another point that described some zig-zags before disappearing. This case proves to be of very good consistency with 3 quality witnesses, who have drafted very precise reports but obviously not independent ones (see the investigation report). The strangeness of the case is fairly low given the relatively great distance from the phenomenon. The linear movement of the first phenomenon is not strange, the chaotic movement of the second one is more so. The GEIPAN considered multiple hypotheses, some could not be totally excluded, but none could be confirmed. The bird hypothesis comes top, though it remains insufficiently confirmed. It could not be validated by an ornithologist expert. Pending elements to consolidate one or the other of these hypotheses, the GEIPAN classifies this case as C due to a lack of corroboration.
