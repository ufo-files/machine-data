Description: A witness makes successive observations of the movement of a luminous phenomenon in the sky. He films his observations. The entire video documentation corresponds to a misidentification with the planet Venus. The slow perceived movement relative to the trees is due to the strong magnification used (the zoom is likely at maximum) which reveals the Earth's rotation, imperceptible to the naked eye. Of low strangeness and good consistency thanks to multiple video clips, this observation case corresponds to multiple misidentifications with the planet Venus for daytime observations at sunset, and with the planet Jupiter or the ISS for nighttime observations. This case is classified as A.
