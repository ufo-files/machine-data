Description: On June 24, 2011, at 10:30 PM, a witness is intrigued by the presence of a red-purple glowing phenomenon in their neighborhood. They observe this silent phenomenon for approximately fifteen seconds from a window of their apartment. The light moves slowly before disappearing instantly. No other testimonies were collected. The result of the witness's interrogation by investigators reveals a fragile narrative, significantly impacting the consistency of the testimony. The factual information gathered on the scene neither advanced knowledge of the event nor solidified the testimony. The uniqueness of the testimony in an urban area for such a strange phenomenon is remarkable. With high strangeness but insufficient consistency, this observation case is classified "C" due to a lack of reliable information.
