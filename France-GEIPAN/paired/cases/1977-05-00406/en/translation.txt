Description: On May 11, 1977, around 11:20 PM, an individual noticed the presence of two enormous glowing balls located to the East of his home. At 50 meters above ground, they were stationary and brightly illuminated the ground. After one minute, and before the phenomenon completely disappeared, the witness heard a sound like helicopter blades and saw a very intense yellow light illuminate the area. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
