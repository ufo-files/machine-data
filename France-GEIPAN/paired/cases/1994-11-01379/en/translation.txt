Description: On the night of November 6 to 7, 1994, a young girl observed in the sky a round, glowing object with red and white lights. This phenomenon was accompanied by loud, repeated sounds occurring regularly. The grandmother was also a witness to the phenomenon. The description of the phenomenon suggests it was likely a nightclub advertising beacon, the loud sound probably due to the propagation of low-frequency sound waves.
