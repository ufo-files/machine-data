Description: On September 15, 2024, at approximately 8:42 PM, a driver traveling on N145 observed, through her vehicle's window, a stationary gray point in the sky, located above a tree. Intrigued, she took a picture of the object before it suddenly disappeared after a few minutes of observation. On December 10, 2024, the witness submitted a Technical Questionnaire (QT) to the GEIPAN, accompanied by a photo of the PAN. This case has an average consistency, with a single witness but the presence of a photo of the PAN. According to the GEIPAN's criteria, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The investigation reveals that the observation concerned a gas balloon from the LTU-1 team, participating in the 67th Gordon Bennett Cup. During this event, 24 balloons took off from Germany towards southern Portugal, crossing part of France. Thanks to the photo, the witness's location can be precisely determined, and the direction of her observation established. The balloons' trajectories were being tracked in real-time via a "tracking" website, confirming that the LTU-1 team's balloon was indeed present in the direction of the observation at that time (see annex). This type of balloon, more rounded than a hot air balloon, can appear without a gondola when observed from a distance. The witness's sense of strangeness, related to the object's apparent immobility, is due to an optical effect resulting from the witness's own movement and the distance separating her from the balloon, which was flying at approximately 46 km/h. (see similar case: GIGNY (89), 14.09.2019). Furthermore, as the observation took place against the light, the object's sudden disappearance can be explained by a change in the witness's position relative to the balloon, altering lighting conditions and making the latter invisible. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of a gas balloon from the 2024 Gordon Bennett Cup.
