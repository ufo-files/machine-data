Summary: Observation of the passage of a stratospheric balloon, CNES.
--- seg-e0cf7f45f171e629b95b93ce [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 17, 1976, between 8:15 PM and 8:30 PM, several people observed the silent movement of a spherical, luminous phenomenon. Some witnesses noted changes in colors. A gendarmerie investigation quickly determined that it was the passage of a stratospheric balloon from the National Center for Space Studies, released at Air-Sur-Adour (40), to perform high-altitude measurements. The changes in colors were caused by the diffraction of sunlight as it set, while atmospheric movements produced optical distortions of the object's contours.
