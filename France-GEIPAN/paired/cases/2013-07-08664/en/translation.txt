Description: On Thursday, July 11, 2013, around 11:45 PM, a witness is intrigued by the silent passage of several luminous phenomena across the horizon. The rectangular objects with rounded edges move from North to South on a straight trajectory. Due to the linear movement of the objects, the GEIPAN considered them to be wind-carried and temporarily obscured by trees (see the animation produced by the witness). The Météociel report indicates a northeast wind that roughly corresponds to the observed movement (the witness may not perceive the movement from east to west, which is a slight approach from their perspective). Jaunay-Clan is adjacent to the Futuroscope park, where many nighttime animations take place in the summer, but this park is not within the witness's field of vision. Note that on this evening (July 11), the summer evenings in Jaunay-Clan were opening, which could have been the occasion for festivities. However, the original direction of the lights suggests a release near the Clain river, north of Jaunay-Clan. The description of the phenomenon evokes the observation of Thai lanterns, white or red. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
