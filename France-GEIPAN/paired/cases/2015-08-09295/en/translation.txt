Summary: Observation of the movement of a particularly bright point in the night sky: probable observation of the International Space Station.
--- seg-c2b784bfcaa77deed5c17a8f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 12, 2015, between 10:00 PM and 11:00 PM, a witness and his grandson, on a walk, observed a particularly bright point in the sky that, after a few minutes, began to move, crossing the sky in a straight line and disappearing behind a forest. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of a very dim point moving in a straight line can have several origins:

- Satellite: The initial impression of immobility could have been an illusion due to the witnesses walking. There was a passage of the ISS, very bright, on the northeast to east trajectory around 9:50 PM, but slightly before the time slot defined by the witness. Several satellites passed on this northeast to east trajectory during the time slot: Cosmos 2082, Rocket, Cosmos 1833 Rocket, USA 29/DMSP 5D-2/F9, but they are a priori not bright enough (magnitude 3 at best) to have given that impression. However, it cannot be ruled out that one or the other was in a position that day that made it very bright (negative magnitude).

- Plane head-on: This explanation is possible but unlikely: for such an appearance, a plane must be seen quite low on the horizon (less than 30 degrees), and when it changes direction, its flashing lights become visible. Verification is impossible (too late).

- Helicopter: This hypothesis can also fit the description; the perceived silence does not fit this hypothesis, and flashing lights should have been seen.

In summary, due to the strong similarity of the described phenomenon with the appearance of the International Space Station, despite a slight deviation from the time slot defined by the witness, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": observation of the ISS.
