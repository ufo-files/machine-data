Summary: Nighttime observation of a white streak moving in a straight line from west to east in a clear sky and gradual disappearance: observation of the orbital maneuver performed by a Chinese Long March 8 rocket (visualization of the gases ejected by the launcher stage).
--- seg-7e597d780b1e224d3b794c5c [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the evening of August 25, 2025, a resident of Gueux, Marne, observed from her terrace in the countryside a slow-moving white streak on a straight trajectory from west to east before gradually disappearing. The observation was shared by two other witnesses and documented with photographs. Due to its low strangeness and good consistency, verified by the multitude of witnesses and the pictures taken, this case was formally identified as the observation of an orbital maneuver performed by the Chinese rocket Long March 8 (CZ-8 R/B). This launcher was then placing satellites in orbit for the Guowang mega internet constellation (see the investigation report). The visual phenomenon corresponds to the dispersion of gases ejected by the launcher stage, following a motor reignition or fuel degassing, illuminated by the Sun as it was already below the horizon for the ground observer. This type of space event sparked numerous consistent reports along the trajectory in France, Belgium, and Germany, and is listed on platforms. Many sites report this influx of observations, such as the VigieCiel site. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A", observation of the orbital maneuver performed by a Chinese Long March 8 rocket. *CONSISTENCY: according to the GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.
