Summary: Observation of a circle of laid-down grass in the garden of a pavilion.
--- seg-2a5954de7839e92c4af7b07a [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 27, 1993, the owner of a cottage noticed a regular circle approximately 8 meters in diameter on their property, with grass regularly matted outward along a 30 cm width around its perimeter. The center of the circle was occupied by an automatic sprinkler head. It should be noted that a violent storm had flooded the property in the days prior, and electrical disruptions occurred during this storm, specifically affecting the fuse that powered the automatic sprinkler system. A quick analysis of the SEPRA suggests the hypothesis of the sudden opening of the sprinkler head, leading to a period of low-pressure watering, which created the observed phenomenon.
