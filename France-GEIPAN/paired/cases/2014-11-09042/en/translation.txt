--- seg-5ba161fe93fb7d3c4698a0a5 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 11, 2014, around 6 a.m., a witness, from their caravan by the sea, observed a bright white round object moving slowly at first, then accelerating and making zig-zags along the coast. The observation lasted 20 minutes. The phenomenon disappeared over the horizon. Only one testimony was collected. After analyzing various hypotheses (see the investigation report), including: - a ship moving in the Mont-Saint-Michel bay. - a helicopter flying low along the Granville coast. - an airplane approaching the Granville airport with landing lights on. - an airplane flying over the same area with lights also on. the preferred hypothesis is that of the helicopter (civil security or coast guard) equipped with a spotlight. Therefore, the GEIPAN classifies this case as 'B': probable observation of a helicopter (civil security or coast guard) equipped with a spotlight flying along the Granville coast.
