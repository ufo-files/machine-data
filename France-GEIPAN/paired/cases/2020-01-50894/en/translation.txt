Description: On January 21, 2020, around 4:45/5:00 PM, the witness sees through his window a dark object. Below this object, much lower, he observes four horizontal bands of different colors (red, green, blue, and possibly yellow for the fourth, with uncertainty). The witness takes a photo of the phenomenon as the object continues to move in the sky, performing turning movements around a vertical axis, until nightfall. The case is consistent: presence of a photo and very good quality of the testimony (see the investigation report). In trying to understand what he saw, the witness will find the explanation: a kite present at the Pépinière (a district of Belfort) and equipped with a Go Pro camera. The GEIPAN is classified as A: observation of a kite.
