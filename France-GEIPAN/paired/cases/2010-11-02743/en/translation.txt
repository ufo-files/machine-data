--- seg-d0abde322317fe966ba8afb1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 26, 2010, between 11:30 PM and midnight, two witnesses observed in the sky five to six bright lights of the same size and yellow-orange color that moved, without noise, making chaotic and rapid curves around another fixed bright point. The lights suddenly went out, and the witnesses noticed that the fixed point itself dimmed gradually. The GEIPAN considered several hypotheses for this observation: initially, Thai lanterns, but one witness claims the lights' movements were much too fast and focused on a fixed point. It could be a pyrotechnic or electrical device set up for a party, but there doesn't seem to have been a major party in Mirande that day. A "natural" cause is ruled out, given the complexity of the observed movements. Since the events are too old to seek out additional witnesses and launch an investigation, this case is classified in category "C" for lack of information.
