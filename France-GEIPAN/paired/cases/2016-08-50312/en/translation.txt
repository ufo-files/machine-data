Summary: Observation of successive and silent passages of orange lights in the night sky: observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-0257d91e85f85a54582798cb [needs-review] ---
Description: The witness was with approximately thirty guests during a dinner when, at around 00:02 on Sunday, August 28, 2016, they saw an orange glow moving from south to north in absolute silence. After a few minutes, a second glow appeared and followed the same path as the first. The same phenomenon repeated itself between 7 and 10 times, always with the same appearance, speed, and altitude. A photograph was taken. Only one testimony was collected. The entire event is consistent with a lantern release (see the investigation report: - the movement aligns with wind direction; - the PAN's appearance, trajectory, and repetition are consistent; - chromatic analysis using the IPACO software indicates compatibility with lantern origin; - it was a weekend night conducive to a festive event. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as A: very probable observation of Thai lanterns.
