Description: On January 5, 2015, at 7:41 PM, a witness observed the passage of a unique, white, blue, green, very bright phenomenon with a trail. As the witness suspected, the observation of a white-blue-green glowing point, followed by a trail, quickly traversing a portion of the sky in a few seconds, has all the characteristics of a bolide, that is, a phenomenon produced in the upper atmosphere by the fall of a meteorite. The REFORME network confirms this hypothesis (see attached document). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
