Summary: Observation of an advertising projector in test.
--- seg-132a4a64e78e0aeca2120135 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of July 21, 1993, around 12:30 a.m., six witnesses observed numerous glowing orbs spinning in the sky and scanning the horizon. After investigation, it was determined that the source of the observed phenomenon was a spotlight being tested on the parking lot of a nearby nightclub. This type of spotlight projects 30 beams that rotate and scan the horizon.
