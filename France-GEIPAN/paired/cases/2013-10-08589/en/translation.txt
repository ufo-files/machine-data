Description: On October 22, 2013, at 10:40 PM, a witness looking at the clear sky was intrigued by the S-N passage of a dark, half-cupola shaped object with hanging protrusions. The very short observation (less than 10 seconds) did not allow the witness to take a photograph. The silent object disappeared into the darkness of the night sky. Based on the information collected through the questionnaire and interview with the witness, the GEIPAN classified this case as 'B', likely a helium-filled party balloon of 'ladybug' type that had escaped its owner.
