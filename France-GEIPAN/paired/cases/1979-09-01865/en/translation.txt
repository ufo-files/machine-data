Description: On September 17, 1979, around 7:45 a.m., an individual noticed a fixed bright point in the sky. Of white color and oval shape, this single point then quickly moved horizontally across an area where three planes were flying. The object disappeared towards the East. Following an appeal for witnesses in the local press, several people contacted the authorities. They reported the observation on the same day but within a larger time frame, describing the passage in the sky of a peculiar object. However, the varied descriptions did not allow for a precise identification of the object.
