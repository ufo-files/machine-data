--- seg-11909ddacc79564c78390cdc [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 3, 2009, around 8:30 PM, four people in a car on a rural road were intrigued by the slow movement of three flashing and colored lights in the sky delineating a triangular object. The object at low altitude disappeared behind a hill; no unusual noises were heard during the observation. Only one testimony was reported. Due to its linear movement and its appearance partly resembling an airplane (flashing lights and red light), the phenomenon is considered not very strange. It is possible that the triangle shape mentioned by the witness is only the minimum shape that encompasses the three bright points. Since the testimony arrived over a month after the events, the radar trace archives of civil or military aviation are no longer accessible. The impreciseness of the testimony regarding the orientation of the trajectory and the size of the phenomenon compared to known references does not allow one to conclude about its exact nature. For these various reasons, this case is classified C for lack of information.
