Summary: Bright spherical object making a U-turn
--- seg-32d14b76d6d465cf99dd2103 [needs-review] ---
Description: In September 1979, around 6:30 PM, a witness, alone outside their property, observes a bright object appearing on the horizon. This object moves very slowly in a north-south direction. Thinking it was a commercial airliner, the witness does not look away from the object. It comes to a stop above the witness, who then observes a very bright, metallic gray spherical object. After approximately one minute of being stationary, the object reverses direction, reaching the horizon in a few seconds and disappearing completely. No unusual noises were heard during the observation. This very late (October 2007) and poorly documented testimony does not allow for a serious investigation that could validate or refute certain hypotheses, such as an airliner making a turn, a balloon subject to wind shear, etc.
