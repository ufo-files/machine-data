Summary: Observation of luminous phenomena spinning in the sky
--- seg-f1de9b5d56c257f8d0ffc09c [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 12, 1995, around 06:30, several independent witnesses observed a series of white lights rotating in the sky, sometimes appearing to follow the witnesses' vehicles. The phenomenon was silent and lasted for about ten minutes. Although all efforts to investigate have been fruitless, the witnesses' descriptions correspond to those generally given during observations of public laser light shows. It is possible that tests may have been conducted in the area at that time.
