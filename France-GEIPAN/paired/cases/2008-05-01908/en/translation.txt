Summary: Observation of a dance of glowing orbs in the cloudy sky: likely light projections (type sky rose)
--- seg-77bc9db14bcd92776f8d78fa [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 17, 2008, between 10:35 PM and 11:15 PM, two individuals observed, at low altitude in the cloudy sky, the dance of three glowing orbs with fusion and multiplication. This observation is very likely a confusion with the light projected onto clouds from a "Sky Rose" lighting effect, similar to a very similar observation made in the same direction on the D101 road from Servon sur Vilaine to Châteaugiron the previous night (see case Châteaugiron (35) 05/17/2008). This case is classified B, probable observation of light projections (commercial animation).
