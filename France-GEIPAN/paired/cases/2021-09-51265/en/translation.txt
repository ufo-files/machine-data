Summary: Observation of a glowing phenomenon moving back and forth in the cloudy sky:
--- seg-ad5ba901b0fdbdfea706f3a5 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 4, 2021, at 1:13 a.m., an inexplicable observation in the sky was reported. The witness took photos and a video. The image captured at that exact moment shows the object stationary. The video clearly shows an object moving up and down in the sky, disappearing and reappearing intermittently. According to the criteria for GEIPAN, the consistency of this case is good (consistency refers to the amount of reliable information gathered from a testimony). Although the witness is unique, a photo and video of PAN taken by the witness could be analyzed (see the investigation report). The investigation concluded that the witness very likely observed a light spot generated by the impact of a ground-based projector (skyrose) onto a relatively low layer of clouds. This hypothesis is supported by the following: - The circular shape of PAN, which is characteristic of such an impact light spot; - The white color, commonly associated with this type of projector; - The absence of noise and the movements of PAN consisting of regular up-and-down (scanning) motions. Most of these observations occur when the sky is covered by a uniform, low to relatively low cloud layer, typically composed of stratocumulus or nimbostratus. These types of clouds provide sufficient density to reflect the light emitted by the ground projector, making it visible from the witness's position. In this case, an intermittent cloud layer was located at an altitude of approximately 1700/1900 meters (according to weather data). The good to very good horizontal visibility did not favor the formation of a light beam between the ground and the clouds, explaining why the witness did not observe this light formation. The observation took place during the night from Friday to Saturday, which corresponds to the possible use of a skyrose for a local party or event, or even by a nightclub. However, since these establishments were not yet authorized to reopen due to health restrictions related to the COVID-19 pandemic, it is more likely that it was a private party or similar activity. Although the actual presence of such an event could not be confirmed, this hypothesis remains plausible. The peculiar strangeness noted by the witness, namely the difference in shape of PAN between the photo and video, is related to an optical artifact caused by the automatic technical parameters of the smartphone during the shot, in low-light conditions. Indeed, the prolonged exposure time, combined with the movement of PAN, artificially stretches its shape, which is actually circular. GEIPAN classifies this case as A: very probable observation of a light spot caused by the impact of a ground-based projector (skyrose) onto a relatively low cloud layer.
