Description: On April 14, 2013, around 8:00 PM, a witness observed the movement of approximately fifteen very bright points in the sky. They moved in the same direction. No particular noise was heard during the twenty-second observation. Four minutes before sunset, the witness observed towards the East approximately fifteen chaotic bright points moving towards Southeast and seeming to "flash." This observation could correspond to metallic balloons (Mylar) moving in the wind: the ground wind is South, but surveys done in Trappes indicate that the wind blows from Northwest at altitude, which is consistent with the observation. This type of balloon, inflated with helium, can reach great heights and last for several hours. Their origin is very difficult to determine. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of helium-filled party balloons.
