Description: On March 9, 2013 at 23:15, a witness on their terrace was intrigued by the silent passage across the sky of several yellow glowing orbs. This observation report possesses all the characteristics of a Thai lantern observation: luminous, yellow (more often described as yellow-orange) objects moving in more or less organized groups, moving according to local wind direction (southwest to west: see Infoclimat record), crossing the sky in a few minutes, on an evening in an urban area. Given the low strangeness of this case, and considering the density of housing in the neighborhood, it was not deemed reasonable to investigate the author of the release. Although the witness did not confirm this hypothesis, the GEIPAN sees no element in the testimony contrary to this hypothesis, and classifies this case as a "B" - probable observation of Thai lanterns.
