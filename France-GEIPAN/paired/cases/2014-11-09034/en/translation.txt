Description: Observation of a silent dance of several white lights that seemed to form a single, large object in the sky. The witness made a video. This completely exceptional observation was found to be caused by a very spectacular light show set up for the inauguration of the Schuman Bridge over the Saône in Lyon on November 5, 2014 (see Schuman Bridge Inaugurated). The GEIPAN inquired with the production company of this spectacle, who indicated that on the evening of November 3, two days before the inauguration, a rehearsal of the show had taken place. The very particular effects observed by the witnesses were the result of these projections onto low clouds pushed by the strong south wind. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A" due to explanation by a light show.
