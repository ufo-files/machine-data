Description: On January 13, 1980, around 7:10 a.m., a driver passing through the Charente-Maritime region spotted a saucer-shaped craft. Stationary, the object had a diameter of approximately 5 to 6 meters and blinking lights at its ends. Getting out of his vehicle, the witness, a customs agent, noticed that the craft was slowly moving away at about 30 to 40 km/h. What surprised the witness was that during the 5 minutes of observation, he heard no noise coming from the craft. Due to time constraints, the witness provided his statement upon arrival in Haute-Garonne. No other witnesses came forward in the observation area, and no further information was gathered on this unexplained phenomenon.
