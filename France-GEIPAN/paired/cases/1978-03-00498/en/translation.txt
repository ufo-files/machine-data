Description: On March 14, 1978, around 7:30 PM, several people observed in the sky a luminous object with a peculiar shape. Two white, glowing orbs were connected by a red-orange bar. The stationary object moved for a moment, then suddenly changed direction. The lights remained fixed and no change in intensity was noted. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
