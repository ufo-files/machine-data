--- seg-a12f41fb6e33243db66ea386 [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 6, 1989, around 11:20 PM, approximately ten independent witnesses across several departments in southwestern France observed an intense light that turned blue with a trail and disappeared in about ten seconds. Several investigations were conducted by brigades, who found nothing at the presumed locations of an object's fall. This widely observed phenomenon was seen from several relatively distant locations in the departments of Aveyron, Tarn et Garonne, Haute Garonne, Lot et Garonne, and Puy de Dôme. It is likely a major atmospheric re-entry event.
