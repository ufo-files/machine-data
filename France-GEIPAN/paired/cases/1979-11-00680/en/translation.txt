Summary: Observation of the movement of a luminous triangular object.
--- seg-ea3c3d087cc55b4c461b1d4d [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 15, 1979, around 7:30 a.m., a witness and his wife observed from their home a white, triangular object. This luminous object was initially stationary, then moved very slowly towards the southwest while ascending. It gradually disappeared into the air. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, and we have limited information about it.
