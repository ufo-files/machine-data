Summary: Observation of a large dark mass projecting intense light.
--- seg-8541feef14b6a063f34a2948 [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 7, 1979, around 6:15 a.m., a motorist and their passenger were surprised to see an extremely intense, fluorescent white light in a field; it illuminated the ground within a radius of several meters. This light came from a dark, rounded mass that was larger than a car. This observation lasted for a few seconds before the light completely faded away. The field is located on a crest and is bordered by a communal path. No ground traces were found. No other testimonies about this phenomenon were collected, as we lack information about it.
