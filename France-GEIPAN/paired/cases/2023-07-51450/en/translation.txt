Description: On July 4, 2023, around 5:00 AM, while the witness was preparing to leave their residence in Champigny-sur-Marne (94), they observed an unidentified aerial phenomenon (PAN) from their home for approximately 10 minutes. They described this PAN as resembling a "slow explosion" in the sky, amber-yellow in color, with increasing light intensity. They also noted that the "mark" representing the PAN changed shape without moving. The witness took three photographs. Only one testimony was recorded. The consistency* of the testimony was deemed good. The exploitation of the photographs was difficult due to their poor quality, but one image aided in studying the case to specify the azimuth and altitude of the observation. *According to GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. We were able to demonstrate (see the investigation report) that the witness observed the Moon under particular conditions (full, at perigee, and partially obscured by clouds). This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- Amber-yellow color and strong brightness, frequently reported in such observations.
- Perfect correspondence between the Moon's azimuth reconstructed using the "Stellarium" software and that of the PAN, determined through analysis of one of the photographs.
- Observation for approximately 10 minutes with no visible movement to the naked eye, quite characteristic of an astronomical observation.
- Round shape similar to that of the Moon with passages of clouds partially obscuring it.

The witness's perceived strangeness is explained by the unusual appearance of the celestial body (color, strong brightness, size, shape partially obscured by clouds). The case is classified as "A", observation of the Moon.
