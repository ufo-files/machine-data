Description: On January 16, 1977, at 20:15, a motorist and his passenger observed the movements of a glowing orange ball with a diameter of approximately 15 cm. It was located at an altitude of about 2000 meters. No trail or noise was visible during the observation. The object dimmed gradually three times before reigniting quickly. The object disappeared very rapidly.
