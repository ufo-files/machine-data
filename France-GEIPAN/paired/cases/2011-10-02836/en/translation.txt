Description: On October 26, 2011, around 8:30 PM, a witness observed in the sky (region of the constellation of Aquarius) an extremely bright star. The witness was surprised to note that the light intensity gradually diminished to end up as a very small point in the sky. The observation suggests an airplane seen face-on, with its lights on, at a distance of over 10 kilometers (which makes the green and red lights invisible) and that may have changed direction (see map). This unverified hypothesis was presented to the witness, who did not respond. This case is classified as "B" by the GEIPAN: probable observation of an airplane seen face-on with its lights on.
