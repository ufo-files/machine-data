Description: The questionnaire testimony describes the observation of several bright (binocular then bare-eyed) elongated points of dark color. The characteristics of the observation, as well as the date (a Sunday morning in July), suggest a formation of illuminated balloons. The direction taken by the first object (to the left of the witness who is observing south, therefore an easterly direction) and then by the other objects is consistent with the direction of the wind at the time of observation. The witness's description of a "gray trail" is likely due to visual persistence. The case is classified B as a probable observation of balloons.
