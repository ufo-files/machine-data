Summary: White light ball seemingly following and passing an airplane.
--- seg-f5c8de8f823ca3b82a588908 [needs-review] ---
Description: Around 10 PM from the terrace of their home, two individuals observed in the sky the arrival of a Caravelle coming from Guadeloupe. Their attention was drawn to a white glowing phenomenon that initially appeared to follow the plane. At an extremely high speed, this ball of light surpassed the plane on a straight trajectory and disappeared to the West. No particular noise was heard during the observation, which was of short duration (3 to 5 seconds). This event was reported several years later, and the witness specified that their observation could have taken place between the second half of 1969 and before July 1970 (we therefore date it to January 1st, 1970). No other testimony of this type in Martinique or during this period has been collected regarding this phenomenon, for which we lack information.
