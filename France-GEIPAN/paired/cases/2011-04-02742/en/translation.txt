Description: On April 9, 2011, around 10:45 PM, two witnesses in a car very quickly spotted white lights that seemed to be emitted by a dark object moving very close to the roof of a nearby house. The group of lights appeared close and moved very slowly, without any noticeable noise from the vehicle's cabin. The lights disappeared behind the roof. The witnesses, having exited the car, attempted to locate the object, but were unsuccessful. Numerous hypotheses are compatible with this observation: planes, helicopters, Thai lanterns (although they are more commonly known as yellow, orange, or red)... only the atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid or space debris, as well as a satellite pass, can be ruled out. Due to its low consistency and weak strangeness, this observation case cannot be clearly concluded. The hypothesis of Thai lanterns could be a good fit if the witness had noted yellow or orange lights. This case is classified as C due to the impreciseness of the testimony.
