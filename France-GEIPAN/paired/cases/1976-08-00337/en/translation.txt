Description: On August 28, 1976, around 4:30 AM, a witness observed a bright light phenomenon in a cloudy sky. A bright red ball passed quickly and silently overhead and disappeared behind the landscape. The witness's dog became frightened. Later, the witness saw a white glow in the shape of a half-circle, motionless behind a curtain of trees, in the direction of the phenomenon's disappearance. No traces were found at the site, and no other witnesses came forward regarding this phenomenon, about which we have limited information.
