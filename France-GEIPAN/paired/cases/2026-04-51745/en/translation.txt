Description: On April 7, 2026, at 10:20 PM, outside his property in the commune of BURGNAC (87), a witness observed for 20 to 30 seconds a phenomenon of elongated and segmented shape, similar to a segmented cylinder. The object moves on a North-South axis before disappearing towards the South-East. Of low strangeness and medium consistency (2 witnesses, but unique testimony, no photo of PAN), this case turns out to be the observation of all or part of the 18 satellites "Qianfan SpaceSail 109 - 116" launched less than seven hours earlier by a Chinese rocket "Long March 8" according to a quasi-polar orbit (see the investigation report). *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. These satellites become visible due to their direct illumination by the Sun, which is in the observer's sunset position, reflecting off an element of the structure of these satellites located, at the time of observation, on an intermediate orbit before reaching their position in the network of the megaconstellation "Quaifan SpaceSail" (satellite internet >> competitor of Starlink®). It appears that numerous testimonies, including one at GEIPAN (VERNOIL-LE-FOURRIER (49) 07.04.2026) but also in Europe, corroborate the witness's account, confirming the overall appearance of PAN. The orbital parameters recovered during the investigation confirm that this group of satellites was in the witness's visibility according to the described trajectory. The observation case is classified "A", observation of satellite flares in intermediate orbit before joining their position in the megaconstellation "Qianfan SpaceSail".
