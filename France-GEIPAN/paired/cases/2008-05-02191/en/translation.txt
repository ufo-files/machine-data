Description: Following a television report about lights in the sky, a witness states that on May 31, 2008, between 10:30 PM and 11:00 PM, along with other people, they saw a large white light followed by a smaller red light moving one behind the other, without noise, before disappearing. This testimony is very unremarkable, and the description too vague for serious study. The witness did not respond to our request for a questionnaire or to provide a statement to the gendarmerie. It could simply be satellite passes and/or flying lanterns (Thai), but the lack of detail in the testimony does not allow us to verify either of these hypotheses. Without precise data, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of information.
