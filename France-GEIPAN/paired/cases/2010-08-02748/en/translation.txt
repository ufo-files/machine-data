--- seg-73ff4993dbd853084e386c72 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 29, 2010, around 4:20 a.m., several people observed the slow and silent passage of two orange-colored lights in the sky. The media reported similar incidents on the same day at 9:50 p.m. The description of the phenomenon (red-orange spheres moving silently at low altitude) aligns with those described in cases of Thai lantern observations. The Sauchay Manor frequently hosts wedding ceremonies, and the observation time (Saturday night to Sunday) contributes to the high likelihood of lantern passage.
