Description: On Saturday, September 7, 2013, (between 1:10 p.m. and 1:40 p.m.), a driver traveling towards northeast toward Poissy (78) observed a long white cylinder at a 15° angle above the horizon. He saw the cylinder move away on a straight trajectory and then disappear towards Poissy (78). No other witnesses were reported. The Météociel report indicates a southwest wind at Toussus le Noble (southeast of Poissy) and a west wind at Pontoise. A south to west wind can be estimated at Poissy. The observation is consistent with a cylindrical balloon carried by the wind: the cylindrical shape suggests a thermal balloon (solar balloon) released from Orgeval or Feucherolles, or even further away, since these balloons can fly for hours. (Note: a forum of associations was held in Orgeval and Feucherolles on that day). These balloons are often dark gray or transparent; the white color may be the result of a reflection of the sun, almost at the zenith, on the object. The GEIPAN classifies this observation as "B," ultimately not strange, likely that of a balloon. The low strangeness of the case does not justify a field investigation, which would be too random in an urban area for this type of phenomenon.
