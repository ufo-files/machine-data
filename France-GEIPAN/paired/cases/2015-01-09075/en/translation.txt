Description: On January 8, 2015, around 7:35 PM, a witness, while in their vehicle, observed for one minute, through a gap in the clouds, five bright points in the sky: three points perfectly aligned, with the other two completing the formation of a rhombus. The witness themselves later found the explanation for their observation, observing the same spectacle in the same direction under clear skies: the three aligned bright points are "the belt" of the Orion constellation (see astronomical map). It was the sight of these isolated stars amidst the clouds, without their stellar context, that temporarily gave them their strange character. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": certain observation of the Orion constellation, between clouds.
