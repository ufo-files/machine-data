Summary: Observation of a bright point in photographs: photographic artifact.
--- seg-582159d3f7f1a69924f165fb [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 02, 2022, the witness, driving between Gan (64) and Sévignacq-Meyracq (64), decided to stop to take some photographs of the sky and clouds, without seeing any PAN. The Sun is present in the field of view of each of the photographs. Upon returning home, she notices a bright point appearing at various locations in these photos. The witness sends us his photographs, a questionnaire, and later his publication authorization. After studying the photographs, the GEIPAN concludes that this observation is a fairly common phenomenon of observing a parasitic light effect internal to the camera. The signal coming from the sun is so strong that the image is saturated at the level of the sun. All optical systems transmit the signal, but despite anti-glare treatments, there is always a portion of the signal (typically less than 1% of the signal) that is reflected. The phenomenon appears symmetrically with respect to the center of the images (or optical axis of the camera) by appearing opposite the position of the Sun with respect to that center. When the position of the Sun changes on the photos, the position of the phenomenon also changes, while remaining symmetrical with respect to the center of the image. This phenomenon can be highlighted by drawing 2 diagonals on each image to determine its center, identifying the optical axis of the system, and then, by connecting the phenomenon to the Sun, the line will pass through this center. The signal from the sun is so significant that a small part, a few percentage fractions, is enough to create this signal on a darker background through parasitic reflection inside the camera. The phenomenon appears, symmetrical to the sun relative to the optical center of the camera. In optics, this phenomenon is called a "ghost" or "phantom image." It is a "parasitic image" created by parasitic reflection in the optical system. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: photographic artifact.
