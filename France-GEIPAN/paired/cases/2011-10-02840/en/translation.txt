Description: On October 26, 2011, between 9:30 PM and 11:00 PM, a witness spotted several lights of blue, pink, and yellow colors in a single thick cloud about 200 meters away from their house and in the clear sky. These lights would light up and then turn off. The witness woke up their friend who also witnessed the phenomenon but quickly went back to sleep. The witness observed the phenomenon multiple times for over an hour from various windows of the house. No particular noise was heard. Eventually, the frightened witness went to bed. No photographs were taken, and no ground traces were seen by the witness and their husband in the following days in the observation area. No other testimonies were collected. None of the considered hypotheses (aircraft, laser, air ionization phenomenon) were deemed convincing enough to be retained (see the investigation report). Therefore, the GEIPAN classifies this case as 'D1' due to its strangeness, the uniqueness of the testimony, and the absence of recording or trace.
