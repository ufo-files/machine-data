Description: On Friday, January 15, 2016, around 6:30 PM, a witness, standing in front of their garage, observed a flickering, orange-white object among the stars. They ruled out the possibility of an Iridium flash due to the flickering, as well as a passing of the ISS. The object was quickly hidden by the house. Only one testimony was collected. The garage entrance is located at the southeast corner of the house, facing east. At the end of the observation, the PAN disappeared behind the house, obscured by the building ("the object passed over the house and I could no longer see it"), confirming the direction of its disappearance, that is, northwest. With low strangeness and average consistency (single witness, no photos or videos), this case appears to be a probable misidentification with a rapidly spinning satellite, possibly adrift (see the investigation report). The observation indeed exhibits characteristics of such a misidentification (see the investigation report). This type of object, relatively unknown to the general public, can be a significant source of non-identification. It is noted that several failed satellites or rocket stages, potentially spinning rapidly, passed through the sky, on the trajectory described by the witness, at the time of the observation. However, the lack of specifics in the testimony and doubt about the observation time prevent the formal identification of this satellite. This case is classified B: probable misidentification with a satellite.
