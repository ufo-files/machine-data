Description: On September 23, 2012, at 5:05 PM, a motorist and his companion observed several round, glowing objects appearing and disappearing in the clouds. No other testimonies were collected. The witnesses were traveling in a south-southwest direction (see map), and the weather (see Meteociel report) in Clermont-Ferrand indicated a strong south wind and a fairly overcast sky. The glowing objects seen by the witnesses could be metallic balloons reflecting the sun. As the wind was blowing in the opposite direction of the witnesses, the objects seemed almost stationary. The time and date of the observation are consistent with a release following a party or a sports event. Although this hypothesis aligns with the witnesses' statements, the GEIPAN was unable to confirm it through other cross-references. This case is classified as "C" due to a lack of information to validate the balloon hypothesis.
