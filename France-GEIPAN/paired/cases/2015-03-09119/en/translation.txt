Description: On March 3, 2015, at 9:30 AM, a witness observed through a roof window a transparent sphere and a white, bright triangular object passing through a clear sky. Both objects disappeared towards the North on a straight trajectory. No particular noise was heard. The observation lasted less than 10 seconds with a restricted field of view. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. This testimony is very brief, observed through a narrow window, not allowing to assess the trajectory of the phenomenon. The transparent sphere might have been a balloon carried by a South wind (see the Meteociel record for Melun). The triangle emitting flashes suggests an airplane. Flightradar24 indicates a possible candidate (see attached) in the witness's visibility, but this airplane flies high, and would not have appeared as a triangle, plus it flies towards Southeast, while the witness saw the objects depart towards the North. A drone equipped with flashing LEDs could be considered, but verification is impossible without further evidence. The observation's extreme brevity and the narrow field of view do not allow for a consolidated opinion on this case of average oddity and low consistency. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to lack of corroboration.
