Summary: Observation on photograph of a round, brown object: probable misinterpretation of photograph or video.
--- seg-028b9bd886ffa9603e45c156 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 21, 2018, at 11:55, a passenger in a moving car took two consecutive photos. He observes that on the first photo, a round, brown object appears in the sky. Nothing appears on the second photograph. Only one testimony is collected. The elements of the testimony indicate that the most probable is that the witness did not see the PAN with his own eyes, other than through the lens of his device. In fact, to the question "Have other witnesses seen the same phenomenon as you? If yes, how many?, the witness replies "Only myself with my camera lens." Under the most likely conditions, as derived from GEIPAN experience, it is most probable that the object is a camera artifact, here likely an insect or bird that the human eye cannot capture but that a digital camera can record even at (1/400) second shutter speed. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable error in the interpretation of a photograph or video. For several years, the GEIPAN no longer processes testimonies that do not emphasize that the strangeness was observed by the eyes without optical instruments. Optical instrument traces are only valued when they come in addition.
