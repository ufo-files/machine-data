--- seg-cdedc6258a40bb910580b4ac [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 18, 2011, around 9:15 PM, a witness is intrigued by the presence above a hill in the Tanneron massif of several orange glowing orbs and white light flashes. The witness quickly notices the disappearance of some orbs followed by the sudden appearance of others. No noise is heard during the observation. From his home, the witness takes numerous photographs. Of low strangeness and good consistency, this observation, in its photographic portion, is undoubtedly a misidentification with aircraft circling around Nice airport, unusually configured that evening due to the strong west wind. The other elements cited by the witness also evoke civilian aircraft, although the imprecision of the testimony does not allow this to be affirmed. This case is classified B: probable observation of aircraft near landing.
