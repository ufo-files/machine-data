Description: On April 23, 2014, at 1:28 PM, a witness observing the clouds was intrigued by the passage of a white spherical shape followed by several others. The witness's account of small spherical objects moving beneath the cloud cover resembles a release of rubber balloons. The Meteociel Pau weather report, located near the observation site, indicates that day a change in wind direction, from Southeast at 10:00 AM to Northwest at 2:00 PM, with an increasing cloud cover. The wind at 1:30 PM is quite uncertain, more or less coming from the N to NW sector. Therefore, the wind was very variable at that time, but rather oriented towards the North-Northwest at the time of observation, that is, in the opposite direction of the observed movement. No aircraft traffic is indicated in this area on Flightradar24. The balloon hypothesis is not confirmed, unless one considers a more frequent wind variation than the hourly report. In doubt, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to the lack of simultaneous local wind information.
