Description: During a festive outdoor evening, approximately twenty people observed the slow movement of white light spots in the night sky. No noise was heard. One witness reported his observation that he was able to film. The observation of glowing orbs crossing the sky in a matter of minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from North to South, which is consistent with the North wind recorded in Lyon - Bron (see the Météociel link). The high-quality video absolutely confirms this hypothesis: the glowing orbs are clearly seen moving together, but not connected; some of them are observed to fade out. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of Thai lanterns.
