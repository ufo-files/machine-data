Description: During a patrol on June 15, 2009 at 10:40 PM, two military personnel witnessed the silent passage of a green ball on a descending trajectory. The phenomenon suddenly disappeared into the horizon. This observation of a very bright green ball moving in a straight line for 5 to 6 seconds corresponds to the classic testimony of a meteor. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of the return of a meteoroid (meteorite) into the atmosphere.
