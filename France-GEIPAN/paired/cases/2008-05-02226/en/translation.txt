Description: Testimony of successive observations of luminous phenomena in the sky, particularly on May 29, 2008, observation of the silent movement of a luminous ball with a yellow-white color. The witness reports two phenomena that can be clearly identified. Stars that change color are simply stars that twinkle due to altitude winds; this effect depends on the weather conditions of the day and can be more or less pronounced. The PAN observed as a bright luminous ball was very likely the International Space Station (ISS), passing through the Breton sky at the time indicated by the witness (see the "ISS Passage Calsky" document). The testimony was sent on May 30th, and the witness mentions "last night", so it indeed occurred during the night of May 29-30, 2008. Given the lack of precision in the testimony, while this hypothesis is very probable, it cannot be definitively confirmed. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of the International Space Station (ISS).
