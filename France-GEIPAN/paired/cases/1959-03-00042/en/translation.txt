Description: On March 20, 1959, at approximately 8:15 PM, two witnesses in Tombouctou (Mali) observed a stationary, orange glowing point low on the horizon. The stationary point appears to move quite slowly from northeast to south, without a trail. The point recedes and diminishes in size and intensity before completely disappearing. Only one testimony was collected. An astronomical verification found no star in the indicated direction. Furthermore, the object is in apparent motion. The phenomenon as described could correspond to a distant airplane seen from the front with its headlights on. The GEIPAN classifies this case as C due to lack of information and corroboration.
