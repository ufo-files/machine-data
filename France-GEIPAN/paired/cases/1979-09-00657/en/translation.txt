--- seg-269e22206b03c851a9d0f4fd [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 8, 1979, around 4:30 a.m., two witnesses observed from their room a circular object passing through the sky. It moved at a high speed while losing altitude. An orange-colored light halo surrounded the object. When one of the witnesses attempted to open the window, he received an electric shock. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, and the EDF power station reported no anomalies on its lines. These individuals likely witnessed an atmospheric reentry. The episode of the electric shock received by one of the witnesses, even though it occurred during the observation, cannot be directly associated with the type of observed phenomenon.
