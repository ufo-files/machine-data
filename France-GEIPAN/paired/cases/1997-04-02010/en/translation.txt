Description: Recent testimony (05/28/2009) of an old observation (04/25/1997). A witness observes from his office the movement of a large, irised, yellow light point. The observation lasts 20 to 25 minutes. No other testimony about this phenomenon has been collected. The description of this light point moving slowly in a straight line from South to North suggests a high-altitude balloon. Météo-France reports for that day "Light wind from the South West," which is not entirely compatible with the observation. If it is a scientific balloon, it is not clear where it could have come from (from Italy, at best?). Although the observation is very similar to that of a balloon, the GEIPAN lacks elements to support or refute this hypothesis. Given the age of the case and its low strangeness, this case is classified "C": lack of information.
