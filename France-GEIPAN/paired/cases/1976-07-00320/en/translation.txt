Description: On the night of July 16, 1976, at 4:30 a.m., a witness observed from a window of his home a luminous phenomenon without clearly seeing any form or hearing any particular noise. He described his observation as first a glowing point approaching, then becoming a ramp of lights, and finally, from the rear, he noticed two glowing points of green and red. No other testimony was collected regarding this phenomenon, which is likely an observation of an aircraft.
