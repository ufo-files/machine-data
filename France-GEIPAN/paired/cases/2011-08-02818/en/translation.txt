--- seg-fb1b88360a631a868a2e5986 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 11, 2011, around 11 PM, a witness observed from his home the slow, straight movement of a bright point of light. He filmed the phenomenon before it disappeared behind trees. This witness had already witnessed the same phenomenon in June 2011. Of low strangeness and good consistency, this observation case resembles in every way a misidentification with an airplane in landing phase. The relative distance of the observation location from the main airports of Île-de-France, whose approach is very likely from the east that evening, however, prevents us from classifying it as A. This case is classified B.
