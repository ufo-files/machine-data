--- seg-b70a707348c4ce86f340bc19 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 10, 2022, around 10 a.m., the witness observed from his window a bright point, very high in the sky, moving slowly and without a trail. He was engaged in household tasks in his children's room. His attention was drawn to an object appearing as a seemingly fixed point in the sky, resembling a "small bright point." The object seemed stationary for almost a minute, then moved faster and made abrupt changes in direction. Again, the object seemed to stop and tilt. It appeared in the shape of a "T" with a bar on each side, a point at the center of one side. The observation lasted over five minutes. The object disappeared in the distance after a very rapid acceleration. The perceived oddity was created by the object's trajectory and shape at the end of the observation. The witness also considers the object's altitude to be higher than typical airplane observations he makes. Finally, the absence of a trail, which initially led him to think of a glider. The consistency of this observation is of good quality due to the information provided by the witness, as well as their coherence with all the data collected during our study. The examination of the file, the analysis of the CNOA map, and an exchange with the Azelot airfield allowed the observed object to be identified. It was a Pilatus aircraft departing from the Azelot airport, dropping parachutists (Nancy Parachute School). GEIPAN classifies the case as A: observation of an aircraft.
