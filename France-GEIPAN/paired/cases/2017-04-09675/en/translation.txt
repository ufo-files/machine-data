Description: On April 22, 2017 at 23:41, a witness observed in the sky 3 lights aligned horizontally, with the arrival a few minutes later of 2 additional lights in a vertical position, forming a "L" shape. No movement, displacement, or change in light intensity was noticed during the entire 45-minute observation. The witness photographed the PAN. The witness made a somewhat similar observation on April 18, 2017. He has attached two photographs taken during this observation, which may aid in understanding the case of April 22. No other testimony was collected. The description of the PAN presents characteristics of several prosaic explanatory hypotheses, which are a priori to be eliminated based on certain details of the observation (see the investigation report). However, unfortunately, it was not possible to verify or clarify some key points, such as the exact duration of the observation (did it really last 45 minutes, when we only have photos at intervals of a few seconds), the open or closed state of the window depending on the phases of the observation. Despite several follow-ups, the witness did not respond to the questions asked. Furthermore, the second phase of the observation, with the arrival of 2 additional bright points, lacks precision, despite its strange character. The case bears a certain strangeness. But the unique testimony, and above all the imprecision on key points, coupled with the witness's failure to respond to requests for clarification, confer a low level of consistency on the testimony. This is not sufficient to validate the strange nature of the observation. The case is classified C, non-exploitable testimony due to lack of data.
