**Description:** On May 16, 2022, at 11:26 PM, three individuals in a vehicle driving on D2 towards Mourenx (64) in Arbus (64) observed four bright white lights in the sky that appeared aligned and stationary. The phenomenon disappeared suddenly after two minutes of observation. Only one statement was collected. The case presents an average strangeness and low consistency. Despite three witnesses being present, only one statement was collected, which lacked detail in the technical questionnaire (QT). No photos or videos were provided. The absence of data and details made the investigation complex. (According to GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a statement).

However, hypotheses were proposed:

- The description of PAN could have suggested observing lights near the ground (stadium lights, high-tension line construction, agricultural vehicles, etc.). The alignment and possible immobility of the lights, combined with the witnesses' movement around the phenomenon, led to this hypothesis. However, the hypothesis that the stadium lights of Siros (64) could be associated with or the source of this PAN was ruled out; it was confirmed they were off at the time of observation.

- The hypothesis of nighttime construction on the high-tension line in the observation axis, which would require the use of lighting devices, proved inconclusive as the line manager (RTE) told GEIPAN there was no construction at the time of observation.

- The hypothesis of observing an artificial re-entry into the atmosphere was suggested despite angular location data imprecisions. The CNES Operational Surveillance Center, contacted about this, indicated that none of their database elements correspond to the characteristics of PAN. No precision (azimuth, elevation, size, etc.) was obtained from the (one) witness(es) despite follow-ups, so the case is classified "C", unidentifiable due to a lack of data.
