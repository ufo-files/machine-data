Summary: Red spots on photograph.
--- seg-8d583f69fc2b246861b89a1a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness sends us a photograph on which red spots appear. After analyzing the photograph, it turns out that the red points are reflections of street lamps inside the camera lens. If we connect each lamp to a red point, the 3 segments intersect at a point that is the "mirror point" of the phenomenon, which mirror point is located exactly in the center of the photo, corresponding to the focus point of the lens. Each red point has an intensity proportional to the distance of the lamps on the photo, so their light intensity varies. This phenomenon is fairly common, but this photo is truly a "textbook case," probably due to the fact that the 3 lamps are grouped around the optical center of the lens.
