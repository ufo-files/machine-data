Description: On the night of October 20, 2013, between midnight and 1 a.m., two people walking together were intrigued by a group of equally spaced lights in the sky forming a triangle. Only one statement was collected. Of average strangeness and consistency, this case turns out to be a certain misperception with the Hyades star cluster. Furthermore, the Hyades were perfectly visible along the axis indicated by the witness, and the position of the lights forming the PAN perfectly matches that of the cluster's stars. The PAN exhibits only a single orange light, much like the nearby star Aldébaran, a neighbor of the Hyades. The issue is not the witness's visual perception but the interpretation the witness makes of their observation through their feelings (surprise, fatigue, walking at night, clouds that could give an illusion of movement to the PAN). This case is classified A, certain misperception with the Hyades cluster.
