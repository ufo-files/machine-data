Description: On Saturday, May 30, 2009, at approximately 2:50 a.m., a witness observing from their terrace saw the quick and silent passage of an object composed of three orange glowing points forming an equilateral triangle. The witness distinguished between these points an orange halo, but of lower intensity. No other testimonies were collected. This case is slightly strange given its very short duration and straight trajectory, but the testimony is very consistent: the witness is an experienced observer, very precise when possible (direction, wind speed), and abstains when not. The testimony has several characteristics of meteor observations, but not enough to affirm it was one. No confirmation within the BOAM base was provided. This case is classified C due to the lack of independent testimonies and cross-checks.
