Description: On January 25, 2020, between 4:30 am and 5:00 am, a resident of Les Sauvages (69) unable to sleep went outside with her dog. During her walk, she observed a large white sphere traversing the sky (low and veiled), making round trips above Route D8, with graceful turns at her level. No noise was heard. Only one testimony was collected. Of low strangeness and good consistency (unique witness but fairly precise testimony), the case appears to be a very probable misidentification with a skytracer (see the investigation report). The description of the PAN exhibits all characteristics of a misidentification with a skytracer: round shape, round-trip movements on an oval trajectory, long observation time, presence of an overcast sky, and white color. The high brightness of the PAN and the relatively low height of the clouds imply a misidentification with a nearby skytracer. It turns out that a nightclub located only 9 km from the observation site has a skytracer, which was active at the time of the observation. Its location to the North is consistent with the observation direction of the PAN. The case is classified as A: Misidentification with a skytracer.
