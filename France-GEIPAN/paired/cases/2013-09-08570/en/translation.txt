--- seg-7cee160b11e77f4af97db7ed [needs-review] ---
Description: On Friday, September 27, 2013, between 10:15 PM and 10:30 PM, a witness from their home noticed successive passages in the cloudy sky of an orange ball. The object always moves silently from East to West. The description of the observation has all the characteristics of Thai lanterns: successive orange balls on the same horizontal trajectory, according to the local wind (an established east wind of approximately 15 km/h according to www.meteociel.fr), on a weekend evening. The photographs, of fairly poor quality, provide little information. The GEIPAN classifies this unusual case in category "B": observation of two successive Thai lanterns.
