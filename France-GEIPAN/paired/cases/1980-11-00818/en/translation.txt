Summary: probable observation of an aircraft towing a glowing advertisement
--- seg-02ded60d19cb9a766a7c0ed4 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 5, 1980, between 7:25 PM and 8:00 PM, several witnesses observed the low-altitude movement of an object that intrigued them. Shaped either circular or rectangular, according to the witnesses, it emitted lights. It moved very slowly and without any noise, making back-and-forth movements. Witnesses observed this object for over a quarter of an hour in the sky. It disappeared heading towards Belgium. During the investigation, it was noted that that evening, a Belgian plane, illuminated in a peculiar way on its wings and fuselage, was dragging a light advertisement.
