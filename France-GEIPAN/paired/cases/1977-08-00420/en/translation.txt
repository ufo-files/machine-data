Summary: Observation of an orange and triangular light phenomenon: Lack of information
--- seg-9bc64569677563519682b06f [needs-review] ---
Description: The gendarmerie was alerted of an observation reported in the local press. On August 7, 1977, around 1:30 a.m., several people observed a bright light at the top of a hill, and then they saw a large orange triangle gradually appear. When seen in full, the object had the shape of a cone and seemed to be moving towards them. Overcome with fear, the witnesses left in their car. The phenomenon disappeared, hidden by a hedge of trees. When they returned to the location a little later with other people, they observed nothing further. At the location indicated by the witnesses, the gendarmerie found no ground traces, and no other testimony was collected from the local population.
