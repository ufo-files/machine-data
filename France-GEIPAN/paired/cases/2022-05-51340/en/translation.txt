Description: On May 31, 2022, around 11:57 PM, a resident of PAIMPONT (35) is at their home and hears a progressively louder, deafening noise approaching their house. He first thinks it's a truck, then an airplane; except that the noise is strangely loud like jet engines and keeps getting louder. The intrigued witness goes to the window and sees an object with 3 bright points pass overhead, flying above his house. These 3 white points do not flash. The overflight is at low altitude. The distance between each glowing point is equivalent to that of a large cargo plane. It moves in a southeast direction. Only one testimony is collected. What catches the witness's attention is the position of the three bright points: in an inverted triangle relative to the object's direction of movement. The central point is at the rear in relation to the two lateral points. These 3 points move in a linear manner as if they were part of a single object. Even after losing visual contact with the flying object, the witness continues to hear the muffled noise accompanying the flight for at least a dozen seconds. The consistency of this case is good: one witness but a video. The investigation revealed that the witness observed an A400M (military aircraft) at low altitude (see the investigation report). The passage of this aircraft at the time, location, and according to the trajectory mentioned by the witness has been confirmed by the CNOA (National Center for Air Operations, Air and Space Force). The perceived strangeness by the witness, namely the unusually loud noise and the respective positions of the lights (two in front, one behind), is entirely explained by the hypothesis under consideration. In fact, the A400M is a four-bladed aircraft and it is moving at low altitude here, so it does indeed make a loud humming noise. Additionally, the landing lights have the configuration described by the witness. The case is classified A, observation of a military aircraft A400M.
