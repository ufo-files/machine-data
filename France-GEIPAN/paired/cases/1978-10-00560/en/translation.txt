Description: On October 23, 1978, around 8:00 PM, a witness, accompanied by his frightened dog, observed for several minutes the movements of a glowing object emitting an orange light. The round, sparkling object changed speed and direction without any particular noise being heard by the witness. A fireball was seen emerging from the object. The object suddenly disappeared. No other testimony was collected by the gendarmerie to corroborate all the information given by the sole witness.
