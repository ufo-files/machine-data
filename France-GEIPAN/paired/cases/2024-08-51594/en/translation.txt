Description: On September 7, 2024, at 03:30, from his garden in Perpignan (Pyrénées-Orientales, 66), the witness observed the passage of an unidentified aerospacial phenomenon (PAN) composed of five white glowing points. This group followed a straight trajectory oriented from northeast to southwest. The witness lost sight of the PAN behind a tree after approximately five seconds of observation. This case presents an average consistency due to a single witness and the absence of photos or videos of the PAN (see the investigation report). 

*According to the GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. For the witness, the strangeness of the phenomenon stems from the regularity of the formation and the identical appearance of the five glowing elements. This uniformity gives the impression of a single large object rather than five separate objects. The investigation determined that this observation is very likely a group of storks illuminated by urban lights. Indeed, between August 24 and early September, the city of Perpignan and the department of Pyrénées-Orientales hosted an unusual number of white storks, piquing the curiosity of residents. Several local media reported the event. The witness's home, located on the Perpignan-Villeneuve-de-la-Raho axis, corresponds to the trajectory of the five observed lights. It appears the witness considered the stork flight hypothesis as he specifies in the questionnaire: "I did not want to be told I had observed a stork flight."

The GEIPAN classifies this observation case as "B": probable observation of a group of birds.
