Description: On October 9, 1954, around 8:00 PM, three farmers observed in the sky for 45 minutes a orange disk shaped like an upside-down plate. It disappeared towards the northwest. The observation was briefly reported in a national gendarmerie intelligence message. The duration of the observation and the described shape suggest some misperceptions with the Moon. The Moon was indeed present in the sky but in the southeast at almost 30 degrees of elevation, which absolutely does not correspond to the context of the observation. The GEIPAN classified this case as C due to lack of information.
