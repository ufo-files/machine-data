--- seg-dd7a1893d16a9c804aeb008a [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 1, 2009, at approximately 10:23 PM, several witnesses observed the straight-line movement of two bright red spherical objects. No noise was heard during the observation. The objects gradually faded into the distance. The witnesses described multiple bright points moving on the same trajectory, following the direction of the wind, at a low altitude and at a moderate speed (20 km/h)... which favors the hypothesis of flying light balloons, similar to Thai lanterns. However, without a report to the gendarmerie and thus no police report, a thorough investigation is not possible. These observations are therefore classified as C due to a lack of sufficient information.
