Description: On September 17, 2025, at Marlenheim (67), around 2:25 PM, the witness, outside their home, observes a quad-engine aircraft heading northwest. Three circular objects accompany the aircraft, moving at the same speed for approximately 10 seconds before making a left turn and accelerating to disappear behind a cloud.Around 2:50 PM, the witness observes a second unidentified aerial phenomenon (PAN). They hear the sound of an aircraft again and see a bi-engine aircraft, followed by two similar circular objects moving parallel to the aircraft. This appearance lasts for about 8 seconds. The radar return from the Air Planning and Conduct of Air Defense Operations Center (CAPCODA) shows, during the first observation, an A330MRTT (blue trace) in mid-air refueling of several fighter jets (red traces) (see Annex 1). The refueling occurs around 12:28 UTC (2:28 PM local time), almost directly overhead the witness. The refueler's trajectory is nearly linear from southeast to northwest, with the fighter jets making a left turn after refueling. Upon questioning, CAPCODA mentions that during the refueling phase, the transponders of the fighter jets are turned off, which explains the absence of red traces during this phase. In response to the question: "Is it possible that 3 fighter jets approach the refueler at the same time, even though only one has its transponder on?", CAPCODA answers: "Absolutely, this kinematics is part of standard procedures."The Airbus A330MRTT is a military transport and refueling aircraft. It is white, like the PAN. The circular objects are fighter jets, with dimensions much smaller than the refueler (4 to 5 times smaller in length and wingspan) and whose details (wings, etc.) cannot be seen from the observation distance (they were flying at 7,000 meters altitude). The time and trajectories match the observation: - time: 2:28 PM for around 2:25 PM, - linear trajectory from southeast to northwest with a left turn for the fighter jets.For the second observation, we find the presence around 2:48 PM UTC (4:48 PM local time) of the refueler (almost directly overhead the observer) at the end of the in-flight refueling phase (blue trace) with fighter jets continuing the same trajectory as the refueler (red traces), from southeast to northwest. See Annex 2. These are therefore the same types of aircraft (refueler and fighter jets) for the two observations. The fact that the transporter was identified as a quad-engine aircraft during the first observation is likely due to a lack of visibility caused by the distance and the number of aircraft flying in the same sector.The case consistency is average with only one witness and the absence of photos/videos. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A", observation of military aircraft.
