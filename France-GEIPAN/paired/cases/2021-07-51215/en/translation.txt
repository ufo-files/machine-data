Description: On July 23, 2021, at 10:14 a.m., a mother and her daughter at the beach were intrigued by the silent passage, in a clear sky, of a dark PAN of "slightly oval" shape with "legs." The slow movement was initially horizontal, then the PAN seemed to hover before ascending and disappearing into the sky. One of the two witnesses managed to record a video with their smartphone. Only one testimony was collected. The witness's video suggests a fantasy or mylar-type balloon (see the attached video document). The data provided by the witness regarding the horizontal direction of the PAN is not precise enough to compare it with wind conditions. However, based on the video, it does not seem necessary to conduct a specific weather request using the AROME model (AROME is the high-resolution numerical weather prediction model used operationally by Météo-France). Video analysis points towards a misidentification with a helium or mylar-type festive balloon (see the provided links). The witness describes a black PAN of slightly oval shape, with "legs" or resembling a spider with folded legs. The low horizontal movement and the strong vertical movement are consistent with the behavior of this type of object. The low residual strangeness degree leads to no further analysis of wind conditions. The GEIPAN case is classified as "B": probable misidentification with a helium or mylar-type festive balloon.
