Description: On April 23, 2009, the mayor of a small commune submitted to the gendarmerie a CD and a letter addressed to the town hall of his village. The CD contains two photographs taken on July 27, 2008 by a vacationer passing through the region. A black spot in the landscape is noted on one of the photos. No resident of the commune reported any unusual observation on that day. The author of the photographs presented himself at the gendarmerie on August 11, 2009 to relate an observation made during his vacation on July 27, 2008 at 10:45 AM and the discovery of a sphere and a disk on photographs taken on that date. The witness specifies that he noticed nothing unusual while taking his photos that day. Without direct visual during the taking of the photos and without any other observation or information, any investigation into this phenomenon is difficult, if not impossible.
