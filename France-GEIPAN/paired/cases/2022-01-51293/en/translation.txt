Description: On the morning of January 11, 2022, a resident of SAINTE-HELENE (33) was walking his dog and looked up at the sky, which was clear with ground fog approximately 100 meters high. Suddenly, he saw a bright, yellowish glow descending in a northwest-to-southeast axis. He stopped to observe the phenomenon, which lasted about 2 seconds before disappearing abruptly. Initially, the witness thought it might be a meteorite, but there was no trail, unlike those already observed. The PAN's consistency was average (single witness, no photo). The description of the PAN strongly resembles that of a bolide (shape, duration, apparent speed). It turns out that a bolide was detected from Dax (40) by the FRIPON network at the time of the observation, in a direction corresponding to the PAN (see the investigation report). The perceived oddity stems from the absence of a visible light trail. However, some bolides without trails can sometimes be observed, depending on observation conditions. Since the observation took place in fog, it may also have obscured a faint light trail. The case is classified as A, observation of a bolide.
