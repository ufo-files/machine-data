Description: On Friday, December 7, 2018, around 5 PM, a witness decides to take pictures of clouds while waiting for his child to return from school. On his first photo, he is intrigued by three bright points forming a triangle. These points are moving towards the East. The witness takes several photographs. His son arrives, and the witness stops his observation. He sends his photos to GEIPAN. Only one testimony is collected. The witness is intrigued by three white points near a cloud. It is noted that he only notices these points in the photo of a cloud, and only after zooming in. It is reasonable to think that it is only the triangular configuration of the round shapes that makes the witness find it strange, as the edges of the cloud in the witness's photos show other small nebulosities of varying shapes. In one of the other photos, the witness points out to GEIPAN some nebulosities that no longer have a very round shape and had initially caught his attention. It is indeed in the nature of nebulosities to rapidly change in form and position, whether alone or near clouds. Consequently, GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Cloud observation.
