Summary: Long observation with an onboard camera of a very bright PAN with twinkling: definite confusion with the star Antares.
--- seg-a50a3f072497f52ed124bc9d [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 15, 2014, around 11 PM, the gendarmerie was patrolling above Lyon with a helicopter. An onboard camera films a bright PAN with strong flickering for more than 10 minutes. The bright fixed object is visible to the naked eye for 2.5 hours. Many parameters lead to a conclusion of confusion with the star Antares (see the investigation report):

- Long observation, over two and a half hours.
- Fixed position in the sky.
- Presence of strong, visible scintillation on the video, even when the light point appears as an artifact ("truncated crown"). At this low altitude, star twinkling is often very pronounced. - Angular proximity of the Moon and the PAN, the latter being located in the video. The strangeness of this case is low: it is mainly caused by the unusual aspect of PAN, with its strong scintillation, as perceived by witnesses who had never used the camera before to observe the sky. The testimony is inconsistent, with relatively few details provided by the witness, but a video of the phenomenon was made and could be analyzed. This case is to be classified as "A" for certain confusion with the star Antares.
