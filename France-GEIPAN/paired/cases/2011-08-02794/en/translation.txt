Description: On August 2, 2011, around 8:15 PM, a driver and her son observed a bright, stationary, white light in the sky above the rooftops of the downtown area of Langon. Continuing on their way, the witnesses lost sight of the phenomenon. Only the driver's testimony has been reported. Due to the geometry of the observation (opposite the sun, just before sunset), several potential sources compatible with the characteristics of this observation can be considered: a reflection of the sun on the fuselage of a distant plane, a reflection on a weather balloon released from Bordeaux Mérignac, a reflection on a child's metallic balloon released from Langon, or a temporary reflection on a satellite. In all cases, it was not possible to definitively identify the source of this possible misperception. Moderately strange and of average consistency, this case cannot be concluded as is, but is very likely a reflection of the sun on a mundane flying object. This case is classified C due to the lack of independent testimony and cross-references.
