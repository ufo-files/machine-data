Summary: Multiple observations of a probable atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
--- seg-c63b02a437a6b9627fe69a4d [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 17, 1983, between 6:30 PM and 7:00 PM, witnesses in three departments (Haute-Saône, Moselle, and Meurthe et Moselle) observed a luminous phenomenon crossing the sky. A round and very colorful mass moved horizontally at extremely high speed and without any noise. The estimated altitude of the object was 1000 to 2000 feet. During the few seconds of observation, witnesses did not notice any change in direction, but some saw the main mass explode into several pieces of unequal sizes and reddish-orange color. A yellow-orange trail was also visible behind the object. These individuals were likely witnesses to the re-entry of a meteoroid into the atmosphere.
