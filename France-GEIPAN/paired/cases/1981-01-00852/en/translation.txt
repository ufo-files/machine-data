Description: On January 19, 1981, around 3 a.m., witnesses heard a rumbling and felt strong vibrations. Some also saw pink lights in the sky above a factory. After investigation, the phenomenon was identified as stemming from the commissioning of a new structure at a refinery in the specified area. It was explained, in particular, that the lights on the clouds were from a new flare lit that night.
