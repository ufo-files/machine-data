Summary: Observation of a dark object in the shape of a "dragon" or "embryo" passing through the sky: observation of a festive balloon carried by the wind.

**Report:** On June 5, 2021, early evening, a resident (T1) of Creil (60) is at home, indoors. His daughter (T2), alone in the garden, is playing with a ball. Around 7:30 PM, she enters the house and announces that a dragon is flying in the sky. An object does indeed cross the sky at a regular speed from east to west. After attempting to identify it with the naked eye, the witness films it twice until the object disappears behind the roof of the neighbor's house. It is a dark shape resembling a "embryo" (curved shape like a thick comma with three outward-pointing spikes of different sizes). The shape spins around itself. Face-on, it looks like a bean. No sound. The witness estimates the distance at one kilometer, so it is flying over the center of Creil. A few minutes later, a helicopter follows the same route. The consistency* of this case is good: one testimony but very complete with two exploitable videos. *According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The visual aspect of the PAN and its apparent movement on the videos strongly resemble a festive balloon (see the investigation report). The shape of the PAN closely resembles a balloon model used during festive events, which were organized on a Saturday. The explanatory hypothesis is strengthened by the fact that the PAN was at a low altitude (under the clouds) and that its trajectory is fully consistent with the wind direction. The witness had initially thought of a balloon but dismissed the hypothesis due to the shape of the PAN and its regular, straight flight. The investigation revealed that "dinosaur"-shaped festive balloons exist, and the PAN was moving in the direction of a moderately strong wind. Therefore, the witnesses very likely observed a festive balloon carried by the wind. The case is classified as "A," observation of a festive balloon carried by the wind.
