--- seg-d9cf4ec7fdac4ba9f792206f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 5, 2015, between 10 PM and 10:30 PM, two individuals in the same commune observed the silent passage of several orange orbs that shimmered in the clear sky. The intensity of these orbs gradually diminished until they faded away. The observation of orange orbs crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a summer night, strongly resembles Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed coming from the southeast. The Infoclimat record in Epinal (approximately 10 km away) reports a weak, very variable wind during the evening (a south wind at 9 PM, a north wind at 10 PM, according to the Infoclimat record of the observation day). It is not possible to correlate this wind with the observed movement. The second witness indicated that they identified a shimmer similar to a candle flame. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," a probable observation of Thai lanterns.
