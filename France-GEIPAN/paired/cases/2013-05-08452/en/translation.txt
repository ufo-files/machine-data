--- seg-8833721932ff94da2582798c [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 10, 2013, in Nemours, three people observed at 10:30 PM from their terrace a moving light of dark orange fluorescent color surrounded by red. One of the witnesses photographs and films the phenomenon with a mobile phone for forty seconds as the object moves away. Seven to eight minutes later, the witnesses spot a second light coming from the same direction and disappearing at exactly the same location as the first. The witness also films this second observation for approximately one minute. The only testimony collected describes in detail this observation of a "dark orange fluorescent light surrounded by red, with no trail" and clearly refers to Thai lanterns (flying lights) that were likely released during a festival. The witness' videos confirm this hypothesis (see Youtube links in the witness' questionnaire). The observed movement from southwest to northeast is consistent with the south-west to south wind recorded in Melun (see weather report). The lanterns have a dimension between 50 cm and 1m, and fly between 50 and 300 m in altitude. This case is classified B: probable observation of Thai lantern flights.
