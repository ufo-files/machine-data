Description: On July 5, 1982, around 7:30 PM, several witnesses observed a peculiar luminous phenomenon. An orange-colored oval mass twinkles while a variable intensity point seems to irregularly rotate around it. This mass is much larger than a star. The phenomenon disappeared into a cloudy formation. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon.
