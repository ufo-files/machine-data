Summary: Observation of the silent movement of five red lights in the sky: observation of Thai lanterns released during a private party.
--- seg-bc719e0e27eea23b4c3c9b88 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Sunday, September 8, 2013 at 4:19 a.m., two individuals were intrigued by the successive arrival in the sky of several very bright red lights that moved silently in the same direction. One witness filmed his observation and posted it on an internet site dedicated to such events. This observation was investigated on the ground by investigators mandated by the GEIPAN. The timing, the video, the wind direction, the testimonies, the measurements, all point to the conclusion that these were sky lanterns. The release location was found, not without difficulty (see the investigation report). The witnesses were well-intentioned but misinterpreted the event as they initially dismissed the lantern explanation based on their internet research. The GEIPAN classified this case as "A", perfectly explained by the observation of Thai sky lanterns released during a private party.
