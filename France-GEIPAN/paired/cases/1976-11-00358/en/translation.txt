Description: On November 4, 1976, around 9:45 PM, several witnesses observed the slow and silent passage of a triangular shape in the direction of Marseille - La Ciotat. The object was slightly larger than a commercial airliner and emitted a red light at the front and a white glow at the rear. During several night shifts, the gendarmerie noted, between 9:30 PM and approximately 10:00 PM, the passage of a heavy commercial airliner following the same route as the observed object. The witnesses likely observed an aircraft.
