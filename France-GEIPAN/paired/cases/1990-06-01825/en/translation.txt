--- seg-f968b434f9f48581065cb72b [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness reports that in 1990 or 1991, while walking down the street, they were blinded by an object passing in front of the sun. This object was composed of three intense lights that outlined an equilateral triangle. The object moved slowly and made no noise. It appeared metallic, gray in color, and had geometric designs. It suddenly disappeared at an "incredible" speed. This late testimony (April 2008) and poorly documented account does not allow for a serious investigation that could validate or refute certain hypotheses, including the passage of an aircraft.
