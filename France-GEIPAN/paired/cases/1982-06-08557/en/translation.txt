Description: Recent (08/09/2013) testimony of an old observation without a certain date provided by the witness. The date of June 25th is arbitrarily assigned (considering some indications from the witness). In late June 1982, several people gathered in the evening to watch the night sky for the passage of a manned flight. Around 3 a.m., they are surprised to observe the passage of two other unknown bright objects. Only one testimony was collected. The French First Manned Flight mission took place from June 25th to July 2nd, 1982. The observation could have occurred on June 25th or 26th. The scenario described by the witness may be due to the coincidence of apparent tracks from other satellites at different altitudes. The arrival of the third object could simply be its emergence from the Earth's shadow, which allows it to be suddenly illuminated by the sun. Orbital data for the multiple satellites in orbit at that time is not available. The GEIPAN does not have the means to verify this hypothesis. This case is classified C due to a lack of information.
