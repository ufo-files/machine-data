--- seg-5803ff43f8dbcae1a83f110e [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 3, 1992, around 0:30 a.m., two 16-year-olds spotted a red object in the shape of a rugby ball surrounded by flames. After remaining stationary for a few seconds, the object dived very quickly towards the ground. The next day, the young people went to the presumed crash site and observed flattened grass with ground traces. The investigation was able to explain the flattened grass and traces by severe weather (several fields were in the same condition) but did not uncover any further information about the phenomenon.
