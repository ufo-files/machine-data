Description: On March 4, 2010, around 8:30 PM, a driver noticed "suspicious" lights in the sky that she couldn't explain. She saw them several times afterward, always in the same location. It's likely these lights were projectors illuminating the numerous castles located on the heights of the mountains in the extreme east, in the Black Forest, on the other side of the Franco-German border. The observation period coincided with a festive time in both France and Germany, where very colorful (carnival) celebrations take place at this time of year (Kappensitzung). Germans particularly enjoy these festivities.
