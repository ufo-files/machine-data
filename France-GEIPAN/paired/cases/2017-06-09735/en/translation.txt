Summary: During an astrophotography session, a bright streak and then a cone of light were observed in space: the observation of the passage and then the reentry of the last stage of the Falcon 9 rocket.
--- seg-1d0912907a56d8522731e135 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: During an astrophotography session at Piton Maido on the island of Réunion, a witness observes a peculiar luminous phenomenon during their imaging. The witness describes the trajectory of a shooting star that transforms into a bright white cone of light before ending as a spherical nebula whose trace takes several minutes to fade. The witness provides us with their photographs. Only one testimony was collected. The witness observed the passage and subsequent deorbiting of the final stage of the Falcon 9 rocket launched on June 23, 2017 (see the video regarding this launch here). The launch took place at 7:10 PM (UTC) in Florida, which is 11:10 PM local time on Réunion. The rocket is heading east along the equator, and an hour later, at the time of observation on Réunion, it is already at 8,000 to 10,000 km altitude, illuminated by the sun, and thus visible from a large area on the ground, particularly in the eastern part of Réunion. The witness's photo recording, reconstructed using the stars photographed (see the reconstruction map), shows an eastward-oriented, linear, and descending trajectory towards the horizon as the rocket is about to pass behind the Earth (as seen from Réunion). The cone of light on the photo corresponds to an ejection of deorbiting gas from the rocket (tank venting) according to an increasing volume of gas (represented as a cone on the photo due to the 40-second exposure time). The disappearance of the rocket's reflection is likely due to its rotation (either natural or induced at the same time as deorbiting) changing the mirror effect of the sun. The phenomenon is known and has been the subject of several testimonies, including one from Réunion with a similar geometry (it was an Ariane launch from Kourou: see the case ENTRE-DEUX (974) 21.08.2015 on the GEIPAN site). See also the Ciel des Hommes article: Luminescent Halo in the Indian Ocean Sky on September 29, 2013. Based on these elements and previous cases, we classify this case as an observation of "Rocket" without requiring further investigation resources GEIPAN (in particular for the fine reconstruction of Falcon 9's position in the sky as seen by the witness). The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Rocket.
