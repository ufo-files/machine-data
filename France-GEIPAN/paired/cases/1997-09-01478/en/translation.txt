Summary: Observation of a bright point next to the Moon: mistaken for the planet Jupiter.
--- seg-0a2f1f77865d9ef11eb80eaa [needs-review] ---
Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archive on its public website www.geipan.fr. Among its publications are old cases classified at the time as Type 'C' cases, which are now being reassessed with the sole purpose of being more relevant in their conclusions. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over all these years, this reassessment sometimes leads to new observations or even an explicit change in classification in an investigation note. This observation case is part of cases classified as 'C' in 1997 by the SEPRA. On September 13, 1997 at 10 PM, a witness observed, from their balcony, a very bright light point next to the moon. The witness films their observation and notices that the PAN is moving very fast and seems to be circling the Moon. The witness will also notice the presence of this phenomenon at 10:30 PM, still at the same location. No copy of the video recording was transmitted to the GEIPAN. This case is consistent (one witness, but the PAN was filmed), and the description of the PAN observed is very precise. The sincerity and credibility of the witness have never been questioned. The phenomenon described has many common characteristics (observation duration, shape, size, color, position) with a perfectly known astronomical object: the planet Jupiter. Moreover, Jupiter was well present in the observed sky area, to the left of the Moon, while the witness makes no mention of it. The alleged movements of the PAN can be perfectly explained by the camera's movements. It is not the witness's visual perception that is in question, but the interpretation the witness makes of their observation through their feeling (surprise, night observation, interpretation of a video band). In the current classification of the GEIPAN, this case of weak strangeness is classified as PAN A, an observation related to a misperception with the planet Jupiter.
