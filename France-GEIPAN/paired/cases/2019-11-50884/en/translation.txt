Summary: Long observation of many moving lights in the sky: misidentification with a training flight of drones.
--- seg-83842700e9532e0793a63c71 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the evening of November 15, 2019, after leaving work, a driver, looking to his left through his vehicle's window, spots numerous lights in the sky. He stops on the side of the road and observes, for 10 minutes, a phenomenon that amazes him. The phenomenon forms a circle of several moving, powerful, blinding lights, similar to Venus, with a purple-mauve color at its center. The phenomenon then disperses. The witness, who took photos, goes home. No other testimony was collected for this phenomenon. Of low strangeness and good consistency (single witness, but quite complete testimony and photos of PAN), the case turns out to be a misidentification with drones in training for a show (see the investigation report). The description of PAN and the photos taken by the witness show that PAN draws a stable geometric figure in the air, before descending chaotically towards the ground. This type of behavior strongly suggests a drone ballet, landing just before sunset. Investigations show that the observation location serves as a training ground for a company specializing in drone shows. Among the colors used, we find, in particular, the violet color described by the witness. Therefore, a drone training flight perfectly explains the case. The case is classified A, misidentification with drones.
