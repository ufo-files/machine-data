Description: On July 15, 2009, from 12:26 AM to 12:28 AM, a witness observed and photographed a very bright light phenomenon in the cloudy sky from a fourth-floor balcony. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. The investigator noted an inconsistency in the witness's statement about the event time: the witness wrote "from 12:26 AM to 12:28 AM as noted and as indicated on the photos," while the photos mention 11:28 PM; thus, the observation may have occurred on July 14, 2009. If the photo-taking time is 11:28 PM, it corresponds exactly to the grand fireworks display from the Eiffel Tower to celebrate its 120th anniversary. The 2-minute observation duration could correspond to the finale (refer to the link provided in the investigation report). If it is indeed 12:26 AM, it could be another light show, but it remains undetermined. This case is classified as B: probable observation of the reflection on clouds of the July 14, 2009 light shows. The uncertainty about the observation time does not allow us to affirm if it was the fireworks or another light show.
