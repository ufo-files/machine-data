--- seg-5cb2e433d87308847edd7ecd [needs-review] ---
Description: On Friday, August 26, 2016, at 4:54 a.m., a witness on their terrace observed three bright, opaque white lights in a clear sky, moving in a north-south direction in a triangular formation. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted 4 to 5 seconds. Only one testimony was collected. The description is consistent with a lantern release (appearance, color). The trajectory matched the wind direction that night, heading south (see the Infoclimat record). It was a night in August, a time conducive to such festive events. Indeed, the gendarmerie investigation reported observing a similar light phenomenon on the night of August 27-28, 2016, at 1:30 a.m. For more cases involving lanterns, visit the website www.GEIPAN.fr (search cases using the keyword: lantern). Therefore, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
