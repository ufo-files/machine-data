Description: On June 3, 1979, around 11:30 PM, a driver observed in her rearview mirror a bright orange ball that seemed to follow her until an urban area where, frightened, she honked her horn to alert the residents. A person would exit their home but refused to approach the ball. Continuing on her way, she saw the object until she reached her home. The witness experienced physical effects and the dashboard lights went out. The object made no noise and was at a low altitude in the north-northwest. She encountered no other vehicles and no other witnesses were reported on this phenomenon. Despite the lack of information, the sky map for that evening indicates the presence of Jupiter, particularly visible (magnitude -1.47), Pollux (magnitude 0.98), and Capella. The hypothesis of an astronomical observation is probable but cannot be confirmed. The witness experienced a severe fright that likely explains her behavior and the effects she felt.
