Summary: Observations of the movement of two oval-shaped, gray metallic objects: Insufficient information.
--- seg-16d1356b82418d4c985b80d0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Saturday, July 17, 2010, around 8:45 PM, several family members observed in the sky to the southeast two circular objects spinning around each other. One witness, equipped with binoculars, described two oval-shaped objects with a metallic gray color and a series of blue lights arranged in a circle below each object. Gaining altitude, the two objects then headed southeast and quickly disappeared. This observation is quite strange and atypical, and it cannot be definitively concluded due to its very weak consistency, which mainly relies on a brief report to the National Gendarmerie. The latter was unable to further investigate. A field investigation would yield little new evidence, more than two years after the event. This case is classified as C.
