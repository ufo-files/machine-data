Summary: Observation of an oval object moving in an up-and-down sawtooth pattern.
--- seg-14ad3c543e6748d5fe22e4ad [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 9, 1982, several witnesses observed around 6:40 a.m. a luminous phenomenon. With an oval shape, it emitted a red-orange light. During the 47 minutes of observation, the object moved, performing up-and-down reciprocating motions. One witness claims to have already observed this phenomenon during the months of January and February at the same time. The witnesses' descriptions and the repetition of the observation on several days at the same time suggest an astronomical observation, though this hypothesis cannot be confirmed due to a lack of evidence.
