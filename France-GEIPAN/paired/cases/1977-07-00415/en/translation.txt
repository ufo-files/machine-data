Summary: Observation of a ball and glowing discs.
--- seg-21dd25ef37a12de76b662f87 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 19, 1977, around 11 PM, a hunter in the forest observed a bright ball of light moving through the sky, with several colors. As he continued towards the edge, he noticed, at the same location as the glowing ball that had disappeared, pale discs about the size of a plate. The disoriented hunter went to a cabin to find someone else who could confirm what he was seeing. Another witness confirmed to the gendarmes that he had seen through binoculars three stationary glowing discs in the sky. Around midnight, no objects were visible in the sky. No other testimony providing additional information was collected regarding this phenomenon.
