Summary: Observation of the silent and slow movement of an orange ball: lack of information and cross-checking.
--- seg-c0ddc59ad9ba43d189c153a7 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 19, 2010, at 10:05 PM, a couple observed for approximately 30 seconds a large, orange sphere moving slowly and silently in the sky. Only one statement was collected. This observation of an orange sphere moving horizontally in the sky resembles a Thai lantern. However, since the witness did not provide the video footage he took, nor did he indicate the direction of the movement, the GEIPAN was unable to verify this hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to lack of information and corroboration.
