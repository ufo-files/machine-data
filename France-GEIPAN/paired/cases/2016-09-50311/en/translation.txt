Summary: Successive observations of a bright point in the same western sector of the night sky: multiple misidentifications with Venus.
--- seg-c73cc2d28d91fc32c95e199b [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 6th, around 8:45 PM, the witness, along with his friend, spotted a particularly bright light in the sky while standing on their terrace. Intrigued, he recorded a 9-minute video capturing a significant portion of the event. The previous evening, they had also observed the same light in the same part of the sky, approximately to the west. They witnessed the phenomenon two more times at slightly different times: on September 7th at 8:30 PM and on September 8th at 8:15 PM. The observations were made in the evening, after sunset, and for each day and time, the planetarium software Stellarium indicates Venus in the witnesses' field of view. The recurring nature of the phenomenon, its movement, and the viewing of the video suggest a misidentification with a celestial body, specifically a planet: Venus. The video clearly confirms the presence of Venus, which was particularly bright on the evening of September 6th. This is attributed to the clarity of the sky that night and Venus's magnitude. Therefore, any residual oddity is minimal. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Multiple misidentifications with Venus.
