Description: On January 3, 2014, around 3:10 PM, a driver and his passenger were intrigued by the slow passage, through a gap in thick clouds, of two cigar-shaped objects of anthracite gray color. Only one statement was collected. Based on the evidence gathered during the investigation (see investigation document), it appears that the altitude, height, azimuths, and distance from the witnesses of the UFO sightings are consistent with those of airplanes suspected to be behind the observation. We can conclude that the UFO sightings reported by the witness were likely just two commercial airplanes observed under unusual conditions. These two airplanes were those of flights AFR1263 and AFR1471. This testimony is quite consistent: fairly precise, but without photographs and from a single witness. A summary diagram made by the witness was helpful in this case. The observation is somewhat strange as the objects appeared to behave in an unordinary manner. However, due to uncertainty about the time of passage of the airplanes and the angular size of the objects reported by the witnesses, this case is classified as "B," indicating a very probable observation of two commercial airplanes.
