--- seg-e9c9c164ce12bb8fc83d3507 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 4, 1981, between 8:00 PM and 11:00 PM, numerous witnesses, including military personnel, observed orange-colored light phenomena in the sky heading towards Switzerland. During the investigation, it was established that military maneuvers had indeed taken place that evening near the Swiss border and that the phenomena were illuminating rockets.
