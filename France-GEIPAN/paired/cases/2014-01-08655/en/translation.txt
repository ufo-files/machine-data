Summary: Quick observation between two clouds of a bright white object: observation of the sun's reflection on an aircraft.
--- seg-eeb59c2e348c08a30987c88a [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 9, 2014 at 9:35 a.m., a witness, driving a car, spotted for 3/4 of a second a bright, white, sparkling object in the sky, among the clouds. The witness, on the A6 highway heading from the province to Paris, was looking straight ahead. The Casperflights flight record indicates at 9:37 a.m. in that direction the flight AF620 JL (Airbus A321) climbing between 2500 and 3000 meters after taking off from Roissy. This plane has the ideal orientation for its fuselage to reflect the rising sun towards the observer. The wings, on a horizontal plane, do not reflect the sun and are therefore barely visible. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the reflection of the sun on an airplane.
