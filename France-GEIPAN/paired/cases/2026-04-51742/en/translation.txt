Description: On April 7, 2026, at 10:14 PM, from his home in Vernoil-le-Fourrier (49), the witness observed a faint light passing through a clear sky. The object moved in a straight line at a constant speed, without flickering, crossing the celestial vault from the constellation of Hercules to that of the Lion, appearing as an elongated segment. Only one testimony was collected. Presenting a moderate strangeness and very good consistency (the file including a complete testimony as well as exploitable photographs and videos), this case is identified as the observation of satellite flares of the "Qianfan SpaceSail 109 - 126" (see the investigation report). According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information collected for a testimony. These objects, launched less than seven hours earlier by a Chinese rocket "Long March 8" on a near-polar orbit, are visible due to the reflection of sunlight on their structure while the observer is in darkness. They are on an intermediate orbit, prior to their final positioning within the satellite internet megaconstellation "Qianfan SpaceSail". It turns out that numerous testimonies in Europe overlap with that of the witness, including one at the GEIPAN (the case BURGNAC (87) 07.04.2026) with confirmation of the overall aspect of the PAN. The orbital parameters recovered during the investigation confirm that this group of satellites was in the witness's visibility according to the described trajectory. The observation case is classified "A", observation of satellite flares on an intermediate orbit before joining their position in the megaconstellation "Qianfan SpaceSail".
