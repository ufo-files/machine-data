Description: On March 17, 1980, at 20:40, three individuals observed a very bright object moving slowly in a north-south direction. This object was circular in shape. This observation was not confirmed by gendarmes who repeatedly looked up in that direction. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon, for which we have limited data.
