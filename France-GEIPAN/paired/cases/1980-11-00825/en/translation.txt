Description: On November 15, 1980, a motorist spotted behind trees on a hill a peculiar phenomenon. An object in the shape of a disk rose zigzagging into the sky. This dazzling white object moved at high speed. Two beams of light suddenly illuminated the witness's vehicle, causing him to be afraid. The object quickly disappeared, leaving only a point. No traces on the vehicle were found. No evidence on the presumed takeoff site was noted on the ground. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
