Summary: Observation of the slow movement of a luminous sphere over the landscape: mistaken for the full moon at sunset.
--- seg-466728291ba6a7a910525cfa [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 23, 2013, early in the morning, the witness observed a bright sphere moving slowly and silently above the crest of a nearby mountain. Astronomical verification and several email exchanges providing additional information led to the conclusion that it was a misidentification with the Moon. The next day, about an hour later, the witness observed almost the same spectacle. This type of misidentification is fairly common when the sky is slightly cloudy. A light near the horizon is often interpreted as an object overflying the landscape, rather than being associated with a star. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of a full Moon at its setting.
