--- seg-37bb90bd524e2c07420c1d24 [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness observed, on Thursday, October 3, 2013 at 7:40 PM, two fixed bright points advancing silently in a symmetrical and uniform manner. The observation of two similar bright points, like stars, continuously crossing the sky in a straight line, clearly suggests a passage of two satellites. A check with the Calsky.com site shows the passage, at the same time on a trajectory corresponding to the observation, of the Chinese observation satellite triplet Yaogan17, launched on September 1, 2013. These satellites are known to reflect the sun only slightly (magnitude 5 to 6), but they could probably be oriented in such a way that they become brighter. A video of other Yaogan satellite series satellites can be seen here. This could explain why the witness sees 2 and not 3. The GEIPAN classifies this case as a "B" observation, very probable satellites Yaogan.
