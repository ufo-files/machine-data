Summary: Observation of an unusual bright point in a photograph.
--- seg-af598ce28f2920fd16b76257 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: During the course of October 1981, between 11:00 PM and 1:00 AM, an individual finishes a photographic roll by taking the final shots from his parents' apartment. He photographs the city lights. Once the slides are developed by a private laboratory, he notices the presence of a peculiar bright point on one of them. Having a 13X18 enlargement printed, he then spots an object foreign to the natural setting and resembling a flying saucer. He decides to file a report with the gendarmerie and leaves a sketch of the photograph. No further information was gathered regarding this observation.
