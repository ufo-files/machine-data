Summary: Observation of the silent movement of orange light phenomena
--- seg-0dec802f0dc7060ef93cc0f0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 28, 2011, around 0:35 a.m., a driver and his wife observed in the sky the silent movement from north-northwest to east of orange points. The witnesses then distinguished 7 triangles that seemed equilateral and all had a rounded shape inside one of the vertices. The observation lasted about a minute before the phenomenon disappeared rapidly towards the east. The description of the phenomenon (number of objects, color, speed) perfectly matches Thai lanterns. The date and time (Saturday night to Sunday), and the west wind direction strengthen this hypothesis.
