Description: On July 4, 1980, shortly before 10:30 PM, a witness observed in the sky a very bright, orange-colored disk. This disk was accompanied on its right by a smoke trail. The whole scene moved silently. After less than a minute of observation, the disk disappeared, replaced by a yellow light. No other information was collected on this phenomenon, for which we lack details.
