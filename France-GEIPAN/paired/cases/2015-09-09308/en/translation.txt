Description: On Sunday, September 6, 2015, around 11:30 PM, a witness hears the peculiar sound of an aircraft flying overhead at night, something that has been happening in their neighborhood for a few months. At midnight, the noise starts again, and the witness turns off the radio and lights, opens their 4th-floor apartment window, and observes three fixed silver lights and a flickering red light. The object's trajectory is straight. It passes over the building across the street and disappears behind the urban landscape. The observation repeats at 12:11 AM and 12:35 AM. No other testimonies were collected. This observation does not present any particularly strange elements:

- The witness themselves states that the noise does not surprise them, "I recognize the sound of this aircraft flying overhead and irregularly at night in our neighborhood for several months, I don't see the need to get up from my chair."

- The peculiar aspect of the evening is that the witness finally looked at the object causing the unusual noise and does not recognize it as an airplane or helicopter, though they specify they can only discern the lights, not the shape, of PAN. This object passes over the building across the street multiple times, and it is reasonable to assume it is a helicopter, as helicopters use varying night lighting depending on their type or mission. Furthermore, a noisy and particularly strange object flying over a Paris neighborhood around midnight during summer would have likely elicited other testimonies.

Therefore, the GEIPAN case is classified as B: probable observation of an aircraft.
