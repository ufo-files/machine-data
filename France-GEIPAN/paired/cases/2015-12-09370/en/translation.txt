Description: On December 7, 2015, at 10:45 PM, a witness observed three bright points located high in the sky forming a triangle. The points (white or red) seemed connected to each other. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of bright points crossing the sky in a few minutes strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from South-South West to North-North East, which is consistent with the South wind recorded at Orly (7 km): Météo France. Due to their low intrinsic brightness, Thai lanterns are often interpreted as being farther away than they actually are, and therefore faster. Lantern releases most often occur on weekends; these might have been released to send a message to Santa Claus! The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
