Description: On January 26, 1979, at 12:45 AM, a motorist saw approximately 150 meters in front of him a glowing sphere at ground level. As he approached, he noticed that this sphere, with a diameter of 4 to 5 meters, was composed of several panels. Antennas measuring 1 to 2 meters were visible, along with small blue and white flames. No noise was heard. The sphere disappeared as it moved away towards the horizon. No other information was collected on this phenomenon, for which we lack data.
