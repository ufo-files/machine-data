Description: On August 6, 1981, around 1 a.m., an individual was taking photographs of the starry sky from the parking lot at the Joux Pass. The weather was clear, without clouds, but in the distance, occasional heat flashes appeared. The camera was mounted on a tripod, and each photo was taken with an exposure time of one minute and thirty seconds. During development, the witness noticed a luminous phenomenon on their photographs that they had not seen during the shooting. This object was located in a halo to the left of the star Arcturus. A sky map for that day and time clearly shows Arcturus in the constellation Boötes. However, no other information allows for the identification of the photographed phenomenon.
