Description: On August 13, 2013, around 8:00 PM, a witness spotted a bright point in the sky moving along a straight trajectory. The speed was low, and the magnitude constant. The object suddenly intensified in brightness. Of average strangeness but with sufficient consistency (many coherent and precise details allowing the case to be credible), this case turned out to be a classic misidentification with a satellite reflection phenomenon. Further investigation determined the satellite most likely responsible for the misperception: the Russian military satellite COSMOS 2268 of the Strela3 type, out of control. After investigation, the witness himself validated and confirmed this hypothesis. This case is classified A: satellite reflection phenomenon, involving the former satellite Cosmos 2268.
