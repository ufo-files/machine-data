Summary: Observation and photographs of a PAN in the shape of a black streak near the Moon: very likely a misperception with the shadow of an airplane trail on clouds.
--- seg-9b4771149dc569af3bfb9bb6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 5, 2020, around 8:30 PM, a witness, observing through a window of their 3rd-floor apartment, is intrigued by a black streak just below the Moon. The witness initially thinks it is the shadow cast by a cloud, taking several photographs. As they continue observing, the witness notices a slow movement of the PAN to the East, emerging from the Moon's luminous halo. Later, when reviewing their photographs, the witness doubts their initial assumption of a shadow. Only one testimony was collected. Although there is only one witness, the case consistency is very good as the testimony is complete and fairly precise, and the PAN was photographed multiple times (see the investigation report). With a low strangeness and very good consistency (single witness but precise testimony, photos of PAN), the case turns out to be a misidentification with the shadow of the condensation trail of flight FR3919 from Palermo to London. The shape of the PAN and the fact that it moves slowly strongly suggest an airplane trail or its shadow. The star positions on the PAN photos allow for the shooting time to be determined. It turns out that at the indicated direction and at the angular height corresponding to the PAN, flight FR3919 was located. Illuminated by the Moon and well above the clouds, the shadow of this airplane's condensation trail was projected onto the clouds. The case is classified "A": very probable misidentification with the shadow of an airplane trail.
