Description: On the evening of September 6, 2021, a resident of SAINT-PIERRE-D’AURILLAC (33), during a walk with her daughter, had her attention drawn to two very bright white points moving in tandem, as if they were part of the same object. The lights are white, they do not flash. When the witness spotted them, they were high in the sky, then they described "some sort of curve, descending towards the horizon." Gradually, the lights faded, although the sky was totally clear. The observation lasted one or two minutes. She specifies that the lights in question were not heading towards the Mérignac airport, but towards the Northeast. The consistency of this case is good: one witness, no photos of the PAN, a somewhat vague observation schedule, but the trajectory of the PAN among the stars is precise. The description of the PAN strongly resembles the observation of a satellite duo (see the investigation report). It turns out that the NOSS 3-8 satellite pair passed at a time corresponding to that of the PAN, and with a trajectory perfectly consistent with that of the PAN as described by the witness. The strangeness perceived by the witness is due to her thinking she was seeing a space shuttle, but this PAN seemed too large and too low for that hypothesis. She therefore thought of an astronautic object, but it is very difficult, when one does not know what one is actually observing, to get a correct idea of the size and altitude of an object, especially at night. The case is classified A, observation of the NOSS 3-8 satellite pair.
