Description: On Saturday, December 6 at 22:18, a witness observed three red-orange glowing points silently and consistently moving across the sky on a northwest to southeast trajectory. The witness just had time to take three pictures before the phenomenon was obscured by the roof of the building. He then observed a fourth object with the same characteristics moving on the same trajectory. The observation lasted approximately fifteen minutes in total. The noted characteristics (red-orange color, slow and straight movement consistent with a wind at a few degrees) strongly suggest a misidentification with a festive lantern flight (so-called Thai or Chinese lanterns). The time and date (Saturday after 22:00, St. Nicholas Day, a very celebrated holiday in Alsace) are perfectly compatible with this hypothesis. This observation case is a classic misidentification with festive lanterns. This case is classified B as a very probable observation of festive lanterns.
