Summary: Observation of a cigar-shaped object with a change in direction.
--- seg-bc74a88542472d4296ed13db [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 28, 1981, at approximately 12:30, two individuals on their terrace observed a black point in the sky that, upon enlargement, appeared as a cigar. When observed through binoculars, this object frequently changed direction and seemed to be of great size. It was bright in the sunlight and disappeared as it ascended due West. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon, which could be mistaken for an aircraft passage but for which we have no details.
