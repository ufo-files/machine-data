Summary: Observation of a shape resembling an airship.
--- seg-2f8adebb78cb6d48a146ad88 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the last Wednesday of January 1977, a witness observed from the first floor of a residence a flying object in the sky at an altitude of 500 meters. This object, shaped like a rugby ball, was imposing and resembled a dirigible. It moved at a speed of 100 to 150 km/h against the wind. Its color was dark metallic gray. No noise was heard by the witness. This very late testimony (09/07/2007) does not allow for a serious investigation that could validate or refute certain hypotheses, such as the passage of a balloon.
