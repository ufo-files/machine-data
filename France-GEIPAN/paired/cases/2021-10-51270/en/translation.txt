Description: On Thursday, October 14, 2021, shortly before 4:00 p.m. local time, the witness observed what they identified as the beacon of a helicopter. The object followed an ascending trajectory towards the north, without any rapid movement. The witness believed they saw a cable or pole suspended from the object. Despite its consistent brightness, they hoped to discern the object's contours when the beacon changed orientation. The witness recorded two videos. Upon later reviewing them, they noticed a very bright white light, resembling a flame emerging from a nozzle, behind a darker circular shape that appeared to rotate rapidly in a clockwise direction. The consistency* of this case is good. Despite a single witness, we have a complete testimony and two videos that were used for analysis. *According to GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony. In the attached investigation report, we demonstrate that the witness observed a fantasy balloon, possibly made of Mylar (highly reflective polyester film), that was likely lost by its owner and drifting with the wind. This conclusion is based on the following elements: - the white color visible in the videos, common for balloons, - its high brightness, due to the reflection of sunlight on its surface, - movement in the direction of the dominant wind, - permanent visibility, indicating that the light reflected by PAN is omnidirectional, like a balloon, - the presence of a string or pole allowing for preemption. The GEIPAN classifies this observation as "A": observation of a fantasy balloon.
