Description: On May 4, 1981, several witnesses observed in the sky around 11 PM several objects emitting intense white lights in intermittence. The objects were at a great distance and moved from southeast to north. No noise was heard during the observation. One witness observed the objects with binoculars and was able to make a sketch. No other information was gathered on this phenomenon for which we lack details.
