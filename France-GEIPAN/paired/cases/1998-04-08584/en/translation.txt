Description: Recent testimony (10/18/2013) of an old observation. In April 1998 (the witness cannot remember the exact date; arbitrary date chosen) around 2:00 PM, a witness in front of their vacation home is surprised by a persistent shadow above them. Raising their head, they observe just above the house a round, black craft moving slowly and silently. The craft stabilizes in front of the witness at 3 or 4 meters above ground, and the witness sees in a cockpit two men, "relatively young, one behind the other, wearing simple white helmets without visors." The craft departs above the trees heading west. No other testimony was collected. This testimony of a close, thus strange observation, was not reported to GEIPAN for over 15 years after the event, which suggests a significant risk of false memories, at least partial; moreover, the precise date of the observation is not known, preventing any comparison with similar cases.
