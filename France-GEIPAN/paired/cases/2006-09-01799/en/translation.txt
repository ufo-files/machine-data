Summary: Observation of the rapid movement of two glowing orbs.
--- seg-4de8d5a2aa567b6cb64657df [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 18, 2006, at 10:33 PM (phone time of one of the witnesses), two fishermen observing the shore noticed a peculiar phenomenon. Two glowing balls of yellowish-orange color and slightly translucent moved at high speed. These balls disappeared into the fog as they headed north. No other testimonies about this phenomenon were collected. The investigation, launched late in 2008, did not yield any conclusive evidence (such as radar traces, only archived for two months). Initial investigations were inconclusive.
