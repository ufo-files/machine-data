Summary: Observation of Thai lantern passage
--- seg-9072a9e6ea79b4d3fd9d65bc [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of May 3, 2008, starting at 10 PM, numerous witnesses along the Atlantic coast and on the island of Yeu observed several glowing orbs in the sky. No unusual noises were heard during the various observations. These observations were reported by local and national press. One person contacted the local gendarmerie on the island of Yeu to explain that he was likely the cause of these light phenomena. During a private party that evening, he had indeed launched, around 10 PM at the tip of Cantin to the northwest of the island of Yeu, Thai flying lanterns, a type of hot air balloon that drifts with the winds and currents before eventually extinguishing. These cases of observations were the subject of specific news on the GEIPAN website.
