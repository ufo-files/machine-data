Description: A witness looks at the sky, specifically the constellation of Orion, on August 29, 2012 at 6:00 AM. He observes for approximately 5 seconds a bright light crossing the upper part of Orion, continuing on a straight trajectory before gradually disappearing towards the northeast. A check of satellite passes (see Calsky log) shows at that time the passage of the Chinese space station Tiangong1 moving from west to east, very bright, and just above Orion (see sky map). The witness reporting a trajectory from southwest to northeast, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of the Chinese space station Tiangong1.
