Description: On Saturday, March 2, 2013 at 7 PM, two people were greatly intrigued by the passage over their home of round, slightly flattened, orange glowing objects. The objects appeared in two stages, with a 5-minute interval, and the observation lasted a total of 10 minutes. These objects moved silently from southeast to northeast before disappearing into the altitude. No photographs were taken by the witnesses during their observation. The witness's account: round, flattened, glowing, orange shape, moving alone then in groups of 3, at low altitude (overcast sky), on a Saturday evening, clearly recalls Thai lanterns. The witness's impression of great size is due to the short distance of the objects. The Meteociel report for Lille indicates a variable wind from the northeast to the east, not exactly in line with the objects' movement: the slight relief (Halluin Mountain, 30 meters above the plain) could possibly explain the deviation of the flying objects. These indicators are considered sufficient to classify this case as "B": probable observation of the passage of Thai lanterns.
