Description: A witness observes on April 7th and 9th, 2010, between 21:30 and 22:30, a glowing point in the sky. He films this point, whose color varies. The astronomical configuration of the sky at the date and time of the observation shows the planet Venus (of magnitude -3.9, easily observable with the naked eye) in the observed sky sector (at 280° at 21:30 local time). The observation was made over several consecutive days, and the witness recalls having observed the same phenomenon at the same time the previous year. The color changes described by the witness are due to optical effects (luminosity variations) during video recording with the witness's camera.
