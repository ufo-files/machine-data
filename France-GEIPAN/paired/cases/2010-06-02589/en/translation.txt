--- seg-55f15718ab0fe1359812e700 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 25, 2010, at 11:40 PM, a witness observed in the sky the passage of a bright spherical object that intrigued them. The object moved from West to East, passing over their residence. The witness undoubtedly observed the International Space Station (ISS) as it crossed the sky from West-Southwest to East, passing virtually directly overhead at the observation location between 11:34 PM and 11:38 PM. This case is classified as A: Observation of the ISS.
