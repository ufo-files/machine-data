Description: Recent testimony of an old observation. On July 23, 2015, a witness reports an observation made on October 31, 2012. That day, at 7:50 PM, the witness is intrigued by the silent, north-to-south passage of an orange ball in the sky. The movement is quite slow and straight. The witness films his observation. No other testimony will be collected. The crossing of the sky by an orange ball in a few minutes strongly suggests a Thai lantern, especially the eve of a holiday and Halloween. The video, once made public on YouTube, is consistent with this scenario. The GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable Thai lantern.
