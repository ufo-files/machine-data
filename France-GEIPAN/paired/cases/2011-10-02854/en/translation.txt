Summary: Observation across France of the passage of a luminous phenomenon: probable atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid or space debris.
--- seg-80777fdfdda3258028c06a27 [needs-review] ---
Description: Numerous people report observing a bright light phenomenon in the sky on October 21, 2011, around 8 PM. Witnesses are located in various departments of France. The descriptions closely match a meteor: intense green light with a trail, straight trajectory, lasting for a few seconds. A "bolide" refers to the phenomenon caused by the atmospheric entry of a "large" meteorite (over 100 grams, or a few centimeters to 1 meter in size). Since this observation was also recorded on the REFORME network (meteor observers) at the same time (within two minutes) and by several observers in northern France, the reality of the phenomenon is undeniable. REFORME records the magnitude at -6, making it a medium-sized object. It could also be the atmospheric reentry of an unlisted space debris. This case is classified as "B": probable reentry of a meteoroid or space debris.
