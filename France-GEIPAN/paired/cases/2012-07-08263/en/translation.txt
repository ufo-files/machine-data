Description: On the 6th of July, 2012, starting at 21:50, several people were intrigued by the observation of luminous phenomena in the sky, appearing numerous times (13 times) during the evening. One witness reported their observation to the GEIPAN and the gendarmerie. This observation by multiple witnesses describes a strange phenomenon that is difficult to identify without prior knowledge. On the first Friday of July, the new patrol of the Patrouille de France performed a nighttime demonstration shortly after sunset, in the region of Salon de Provence. The Alphajets are equipped with a powerful headlight on the nose of the aircraft, visible from several dozen kilometers away, only if the observer is in the plane's axis. During the acrobatic flight maneuvers, the distant observer sees a set of bright points appear and disappear. (See the case VAUCLUSE (84) 02.07.2008 and APT (84) 02.07.2010 http://www.patrouilledefrance.fr/calendrier/2012-07-05-220000/d-fil-et-pr-sentation-salon-de-provence). This case is classified as "A": observation of the Patrouille de France.
