Description: On August 3, 1992, around 1 a.m., a motorist spotted, above a bank bordering a field, a white, lens-shaped mist. Located 10 meters from the vehicle and 3 meters high, the mist faded, revealing an oval-shaped, dark gray object with a transparent top that showed a human figure in a pilot's position, wearing a silver suit and a helmet. The object made no noise. Waking up his wife in the back seat, she confirmed the sighting. Suddenly, purple light beams appeared in front of and above the vehicle, frightening the witnesses. The driver decided to accelerate, and his wife was the last to see a cylindrical-shaped object disappear at extremely high speed. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon, for which we have limited data.
