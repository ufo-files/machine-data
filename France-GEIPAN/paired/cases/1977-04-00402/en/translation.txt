Description: On April 2, 1977, between 7:15 and 7:30 PM, several people in a vehicle noticed a glowing cloud in the sky that intrigued them. The witnesses then saw a elongated glow separate slowly from the cloud on a straight trajectory. No particular noise was heard by the witnesses who had stopped on the side of the road. The glow disappeared into a cloudy mass. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
