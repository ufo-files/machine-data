Description: In the evening of December 2, 1985, around 10 PM, several witnesses heard a loud, deep noise. Stepping outside, they observed a series of lights on a flying object: red lights at the front and back of the object, as well as bright lights in the middle. The object passed slowly over the housing development, and the lights and craft gradually disappeared. The investigation suggests the possibility of an "Transal" type aircraft flying over the commune that evening at that time, as per its flight route.
