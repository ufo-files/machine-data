Description: On Sunday, July 20, 2008, at 10:12 PM, a driver and his passenger observed for one minute the movement of a shape that intrigued them. A elongated object of matte light gray color with two large fins made a loop and disappeared towards the West. Two photographs were taken with the witness's cell phone. No other testimony was collected, and no unusual aerial phenomena were reported in the area. The gendarmerie's investigation conclusion is credible: a commercial airliner making a sharp turn to align with the runways of Paris Charles de Gaulle International Airport.
