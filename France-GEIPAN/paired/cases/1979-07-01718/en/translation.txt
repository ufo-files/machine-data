Summary: Observation of an object changing direction and speed
--- seg-5af7bfcf260807e2bcab7573 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 16, 1979, around 9:40 PM, two individuals observed a luminous object in the sky. This object had a trapezoidal shape, was dark in color, and featured four lights in the front and a red light in the back. Initially stationary above the Anse du Savoyard, the object slowly moved towards the East. After a sudden acceleration, it rose vertically into the sky before disappearing. No noise was heard. No aircraft took off or landed on the island after 8:00 PM, and no other witnesses were found regarding this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
