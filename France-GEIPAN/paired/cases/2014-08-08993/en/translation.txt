--- seg-2234ebfbabee25dc88ec52ef [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 30, 2014, around 11:40 PM, several people observed numerous (approximately 70) bright and sparkling objects silently moving across the night sky. All the sparkling lights followed the same direction to disappear at the zenith. Only one testimony was collected. The witness attempted to take a photograph with their smartphone but without success. This testimony is typical of a lantern release observation: "It was a bit like if a large number of stars had decided to travel in a group across the sky." "Their trajectory, heading north, seemed to be guided by leading objects. The other objects followed, all forming a coherent ensemble that really looked like a fleet." Indeed, the entire ensemble follows the wind current that in the mountains can be winding. The general trajectory is compatible with a relatively weak north wind measured nearby (see weather on Météociel). It is summer, a weekend night in a vacation spot: all of this is conducive to festive displays... The lantern lights flicker and can also suggest "Morse code signals." The GEIPAN classifies the case as B: observation of Thai lanterns.
