Description: Recent testimony (06/05/2014) of an old observation. On December 14, 1990, at 10:30 PM, a witness observed four white glowing points in a line: three of them formed a triangle while the fourth circled around, then the points reset in a line and flew quickly towards the East. The phenomenon repeated itself in March 2000. The observation of glowing points forming and quickly crossing the sky in a straight line suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The GEIPAN was unable to easily access the weather record for that day (too old for web sites), this hypothesis could not be verified. Without this information, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C".
