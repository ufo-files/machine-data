--- seg-198559bdf1ae394112d5204a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 16, 2013, at 6:00 PM, a witness on a scooter returning to Vienne-en-Arthies (95) stopped and observed above the trees an oval-shaped object, first static then moving slowly. The witness had time to take photographs before the object suddenly disappeared. Given the certainty that the reference photo for this testimony is not authentic (see detailed report), the credibility and consistency of this case are very, very low, despite an apparently sincere observation report. Following this conclusion regarding the photograph, the GEIPAN did not find it useful to continue investigating this case. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable hoax: the observation may be real, but the photo is certainly not authentic.
