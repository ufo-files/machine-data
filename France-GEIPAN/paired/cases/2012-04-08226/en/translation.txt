Description: A witness reports their observation made on April 12, 2012, around 10:30 PM. From their balcony, they observed for 2 minutes the south-north movement of a white light source. No trail or unusual noise was heard during the observation. No other testimony was collected by the gendarmerie. No known flight was reported by air traffic control over the Dinard airspace at the time of the incident. The witness's description matches exactly the observation of the passage of the International Space Station (ISS), which was over Brittany that evening: see the trajectory of the ISS's movement against the night sky (the time indicated on the trajectory is accurate to within a few minutes). This case is classified as "A": observation of the International Space Station (ISS).
