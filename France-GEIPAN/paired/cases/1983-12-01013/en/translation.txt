Description: On December 1, 1983, at approximately 10:00 PM, two witnesses observed a very bright phenomenon for a few seconds. In circular shape and fluorescent color, the object left behind it trails of red and yellow colors. It moved quickly without changing its shape or direction, but seemed to be losing altitude. It disappeared into the landscape. No other information could be gathered about this phenomenon.
