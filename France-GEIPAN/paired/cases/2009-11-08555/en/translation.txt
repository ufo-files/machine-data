Description: On November 19, a witness observed, from their home for 10 minutes, a luminous phenomenon they cannot explain. The light, of cylindrical shape, is very bright yellow, and the PAN is stationary before moving slowly along a straight trajectory near the horizon. With low strangeness and average consistency (single testimony, uncertain date, but very precise provided documents), this case is unfortunately not identifiable due to the too long interval between the observation and the publication of the testimony, making any field investigation impossible. The hypothesis of a misperception with a lighthouse or a projector mounted on any vehicle (a priori terrestrial) cannot unfortunately be formally validated (see the investigation report). This case is classified C due to the too long interval between the observation date and the sending of the testimony (almost 4 years). Furthermore, the observation date is uncertain.
