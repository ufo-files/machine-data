--- seg-1959259a650e3c9e76c5329d [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 2, 2012, at 4:00 a.m., several people observed in the cloudy sky a circular shape, estimated to be between 50 and 100 meters in diameter, silently moving at low altitude and containing approximately fifteen fixed lights. The object spins on itself. The lights suddenly go out and the shape disappears. Only one testimony was collected. This report of circular lights spinning (see witness drawing) clearly recalls nightclub light shows. A nightclub in Cazères (31) or Martres (31) often uses this kind of device. What surprised the witness was the absence of a rising beam, but this is only visible on humid weather: the weather for that day (in Toulouse-Blagnac) records relatively dry air (50% humidity) and a total overcast ceiling (8/8), providing ideal conditions for the best effect of this type of projector. The end of the observation, on Sunday at around 4:20 a.m., corresponds well to the closing time of nightclubs. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", a probable observation of a Skyrose-type light animation.
