Description: On January 1, 2013, between 4:00 p.m. and 4:30 p.m., a witness observed the passage from west to east of a first group of lights, followed by another group, always following the same trajectory. The objects moved without making any noise and disappeared on the horizon. The witness's description (multiple bright points in two groups), the date of the observation (a holiday), and the wind direction (light west wind) are entirely consistent with an observation of Thai lanterns. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": observation of Thai lanterns.
