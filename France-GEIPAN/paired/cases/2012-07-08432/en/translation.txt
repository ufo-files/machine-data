Description: On July 14, 2012, around midnight, a driver was intrigued by the silent movement across the sky of three white lights that were quickly hidden by the landscape. The witness reported this observation in 2013, as well as another observation made seven years prior. The witness details two observations on the same report form. The first, too vague to consider, is excluded. For the second observation, the fact that it occurred on July 14 in the evening suggests three festive objects carried by the wind: glowing balloons or Thai lanterns. The very short duration of the observation does not allow for deducing the direction of the objects' movement, nor correlating it with the wind (strong northwesterly wind: tramontane). The lights' variation suggests more a lantern, but they are generally perceived as yellow or orange. This case does not exhibit all characteristics of Thai lanterns, but is certainly linked to three wind-carried luminous objects. The GEIPAN classified case as "B": observation of glowing balloons or Thai lanterns.
