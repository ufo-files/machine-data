Description: Around 7 PM in the evening of June 3rd or 4th, 2009 (the witness cannot recall the exact date), a photography enthusiast took a picture of the sky, where the Moon and a white streak from an airplane can be seen. Two days later, while reviewing the photo, he noticed something unusual that he had not seen during the shoot. This person photographed a commercial airplane moving, creating the impression of "parallel bars" described by the witness. In comparison, jet trails appear stationary.
