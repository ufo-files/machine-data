Description: During the month of April 2020, five observations in the Île-de-France region are reported to the GEIPAN. These observations are made in different communes on different dates. All reports describe the unusual presence near Venus of a bright light phenomenon. The testimonies are recorded under the following names: BLANC-MESNIL (LE) (93) 04/15/2020, GONESSE (95) 04/15/2020, CERGY (95) 04/15/2020, GRISY-SUISNES (77) 04/19/2020, SAINT-PIERRE-DU-PERRAY (91) 04/23/2020. The attached investigation report focuses particularly on the testimony from SAINT-PIERRE-DU-PERRAY (91) on 04/23/2020, which was analyzed first. On the evening of April 23, 2020, a resident of SAINT-PIERRE-DU-PERRAY (91) was smoking a cigarette on his terrace and looking at the sky. He saw Venus, and then, an instant later, he saw a second bright point almost as bright as Venus. Due to its very low residual strangeness and good consistency (single witness, but quite precise testimony, measurement of the PAN's position relative to a star), the SAINT-PIERRE-DU-PERRAY (91) testimony turns out to be a misperception with a weather balloon. In fact, it appears that a radiosonde launch from the Trappes (78) station was in progress at the time of the observation, and the direction of observation, the angular height, and the burst time of the balloon used perfectly match the observation. The confirmation by the Météo France services of a doubling of radiosonde launches from Trappes during the very specific period of the April health confinement also explains the similar observations made in the Paris region in April 2020 (namely, those of BLANC-MESNIL (LE) (93) at 20:30 on 04/15/2020, GONESSE (95) at 20:30 on 04/15/2020, CERGY (95) at 20:50 on 04/15/2020, and GRISY-SUISNES (77) at 21:10 on 04/19/2020). The provided radiosonde data show excellent correspondence in both observation directions and the disappearance times of the __UFO_PROTECTED_001__s. All the above-mentioned testimonies are grouped into a single observation case classified as "A": weather balloon.
