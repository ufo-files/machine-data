Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archive on its public website www.geipan.fr. Among its publications are old cases classified at the time (A, B, C, or D) that are now being reexamined with the sole purpose of being more relevant in the conclusions. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over all these years, this reexamination sometimes leads to new observations or even a change in classification. The observation case previously classified as D and named METZ (57) 30.03.1981 is part of a recent set of cases that have been reexamined. It concerns the observation on March 30, 1981, between 11 a.m. and 12 p.m., by six independent witnesses of a PAN flying over certain neighborhoods of the city of METZ (57). The gendarmes, notified through a newspaper article in the "Républicain Lorrain," conducted an on-site investigation the day after the incident. The exploitable testimonial data seriously suggest a confusion with a multicolored festive balloon (see the investigation report). In particular, it is noted that out of six witnesses, four refer to a balloon ("sort of balloon"), and the other two to a sphere. However, the contradictions and inaccuracies in the testimonies do not allow for a sufficiently solid opinion. One may also regret the absence of a more in-depth investigation, conducted on-site and at the Gendarmerie, particularly the collection of angular data from the witnesses (angular dimensions of the PAN, elevation above the horizon...), precise positioning of the witnesses, precise observation durations, a better-established chronology, etc... Without these data, nothing allows us to deny or confirm the hypothesis of a festive balloon. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "C," the lack of information not allowing for sufficient depth in an otherwise plausible hypothesis.
