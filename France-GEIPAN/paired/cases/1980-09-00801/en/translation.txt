Description: On September 12, 1980, at 4:28 a.m., patroling gendarmes observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky. For 1 hour and 30 minutes, a luminous ball located to the N-E moved slowly. This sphere was yellow in color and exhibited variations in intensity. The weather was very clear, visibility was excellent, and there was no Moon. These individuals made an astronomical observation of Venus, which was particularly visible (magnitude 4.15) at this time of year.
