Summary: Observation of the movement of three bright points in a clear sky: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-021ba85491ed2977067bdb30 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 25, 2013, at 3:41 a.m., a worker on break outside a building was intrigued by the presence in the clear sky of three lights moving at a constant speed and appearing to form a dark triangle. A building obstructing his view, the witness moved, made two round trips between two viewing points, but the glowing points had disappeared. The observation lasted approximately two minutes. There are several possible explanations for the observation of a triplet of glowing points in the sky. What makes this case strange is essentially the low probability of the alignment between the witness, the points where the light sources went out, and the moon disk. After a field investigation, it appears that this observation of three lights moving in concert according to the direction of the wind is consistent with the observation of Thai lanterns. The GEIPAN was unable to identify the perpetrators of the release. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
