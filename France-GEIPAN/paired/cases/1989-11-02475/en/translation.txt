Description: Recent testimony (2 December 2009) of an old observation (November-December 1989). A witness reports a less than 5-second observation of an object with a triangular shape, rounded edges, and gray-black color. The witness had time to see three glowing points on each side. The object disappeared very quickly. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon. Any investigation concerning this old observation and for which we lack information is difficult, if not impossible.
