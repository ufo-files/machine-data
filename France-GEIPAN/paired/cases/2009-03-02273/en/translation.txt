Description: On March 15, 2009, around 10:15 PM, a witness is intrigued by the sight of a very bright, stationary, ionized circle above the ridge of his house's roof. This object disappears after a rapid diminution. No noise is heard during this observation of approximately 4 seconds. No other witness accounts or community activities have been reported. Note that the planet Saturn is very close to the direction indicated, and it was particularly bright at that time (magnitude 0.64, meaning slightly brighter than the stars of the Big Dipper). However, the witness describes a disk, while Saturn appears as a single point to the naked eye for a witness with very good vision. The disappearance is also puzzling, but the photo of the observation location shows there is a tree between the observer and the sky, a movement by the witness or a branch could have created an occultation of the planet. These two questions lead to this case being classified as C.
