Summary: Observation of two green-red glowing balls
--- seg-170d43d37f220ae613c91c76 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 25, around 9:20 PM, two witnesses observed from their home two red-green balls moving in the sky. One of the balls occasionally spun around the other. The phenomenon was silent, with an estimated diameter of these balls of about 4 or 5 meters. The investigation managed to find another witness who testified late. No other information could be gathered.
