Summary: Observation of a red ball with smoke
--- seg-e445a776efa3dc810c7b5834 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Friday, June 8, 1979, between 1:00 p.m. and 1:05 p.m., several people witnessed a peculiar phenomenon above the bay. They first observed a vertical smoke column that gradually dispersed. A few minutes later, a red-hot, spherical object traversed the sky on a straight trajectory towards the southwest with a descending curve. Moving at a relatively slow pace, the object was accompanied by white or gray smoke. The observations lasted for a few seconds without any noise being heard. A gendarmerie investigation with the navy revealed that distress and signaling rockets had been fired. However, the information provided is too imprecise to validate this hypothesis.
