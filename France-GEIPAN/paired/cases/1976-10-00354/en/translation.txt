Summary: Observation of the movement of two light phenomena.
--- seg-276b5d90263764eec598b8bd [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 21, 1976, between 6:30 PM and 6:45 PM, three people at two different locations observed the slow movement from southeast to northwest of a bright, intense light ball with a red-orange color. The observation lasted 45 seconds before the phenomenon suddenly disappeared. A second, identical phenomenon to the first one and appearing to follow the same trajectory was seen a few minutes later. This phenomenon is classified as C due to a lack of information.
