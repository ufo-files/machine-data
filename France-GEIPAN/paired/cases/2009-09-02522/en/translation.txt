Summary: Observations of a dark mass with two flashing lights; light buzzing and slow movement of the object: probable observation of a small airship, possibly remotely controlled, likely electrically powered.
--- seg-2ad618f66558e462a0d69b2c [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 13, 2009, between 9:30 PM and 10:00 PM, two gendarmes on patrol observed a stationary, dark, immobile mass above the town hall of Chahaignes. In an elongated shape with two types of wings and two red lights flashing in an asynchronous manner, the object then moved slowly. A faint humming sound was heard coming from the object. As the patrol left the village, they observed the mass again, which was at very low altitude above the fields and seemed to follow the terrain. The patrol continued its rounds, leaving behind the object which disappeared when they returned to the area. No other witnesses were found regarding this phenomenon. This observation has very good consistency, with two credible witnesses providing a very detailed report to the gendarmerie. The strangeness is rated as average because the observed object has many characteristics of a small civilian airship: size, shape, lighting, flight dynamics. What makes this case strange is that it was nighttime, the craft flew very low, taking significant risks and violating regulations, and no trace of it was found in the region on sites where such devices are known to be. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a small airship, possibly remotely controlled, likely electrically powered.
