Description: On September 07, 2015, around 21:43, a witness observed two brief flashes separated by a few seconds in a clear sky. Only one testimony was collected. Of average strangeness but sufficient consistency, this case turns out to be a misperception with a satellite reflection phenomenon. Further research (see the investigation report) determined the satellite that is very likely the source of this misperception, as the trajectory and timing are perfectly consistent with the witness's description. This case is classified A, misperception with a satellite reflection phenomenon, involving the Globalstar 56 satellite.
