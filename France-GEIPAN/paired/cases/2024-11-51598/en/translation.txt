Description: On November 25, 2024, at 7:01 PM, from his home in Villefranche-sur-Mer (Alpes-Maritimes), a witness spotted an unusual luminous phenomenon heading northeast. He observed 5 to 10 white circular structures moving in circles before disappearing after five minutes. The origin of the phenomenon remains to be determined. The consistency* of this observation case is good, with a single witness able to record a video of the PAN. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. Analysis of the video and testimony indicates that the observed phenomenon is very likely a set of ground-based searchlights directed towards the sky, whose beams interact with a layer of low-level clouds. This layer, composed of stratocumulus at approximately 2000 meters altitude, is clearly visible on the recording, a observation corroborated by the witness and cloud data from Météo-France (annex 1). These searchlights, commonly known as "skytrackers," are frequently used for animations or promotional events. The investigation was able to establish that at the exact time of the observation and in the direction indicated by the witness, about 9.5 km away, a show titled "Stand up Monaco" was beginning at the Grimaldi Forum in Monaco (annexes 2 and 3). Although no information could be obtained on the possible use of outdoor searchlights, this hypothesis remains plausible and consistent with the appearance and position of the observed phenomenon. The GEIPAN classifies this observation case as B: probable observation of skytrackers.
