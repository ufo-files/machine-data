Summary: Observation of a luminous phenomenon in motion across the night sky: observation of effects related to the ignition intended for the deorbitation of the Falcon 9 rocket stage that served in the launch of the Starlink® satellite train n°28.

Description: On May 26, 2021, around 11:00 PM, the main witness (T1) visits a friend (T2) in the commune of Malrevers (43). Together, they observe a phenomenon that manifests as five bright lights, accompanied by two halos, silently moving along a straight trajectory from northwest to southeast. After approximately 30 seconds of observation, the PAN becomes indistinguishable due to the brightness of the full moon, disappearing in a southeast direction. The case consistency is average: while there were two witnesses during the observation, only the main witness submitted a questionnaire to the GEIPAN, and there is no visual evidence (photo or video) related to the PAN. According to the GEIPAN's criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The investigation revealed that this case corresponds to the observation of effects generated by the ignition of the final stage of the Falcon 9 rocket during its deorbitation after the launch of Starlink® satellite batch #28 (see the investigation report). The description of the PAN components (halos, bright glowing light - the elongated shape could correspond to the non-dispersed Starlink® satellites) provided by the main witness (T1) shows similarities with other observations made on May 4, 2021, for which an GEIPAN investigation note was published. This note explains that these observations are due to the effects of the ignition of the final stage of the Falcon 9 rocket during its deorbitation, after the launch of Starlink® satellite batch #25. It's important to note that another Starlink® satellite launch took place on the day of the observation, at a very similar time and trajectory to those observed on May 4. On May 26, 2021, a gas cloud related to the ignition of the final stage of the Falcon 9 rocket for its deorbitation was observed, with a trajectory passing over southwest France around 10:55 PM. Witnesses in France and Spain filmed this gas cloud, whose visual appearance is perfectly consistent with the description of the PAN. Furthermore, the trajectory followed by the rocket stage is also entirely compatible with that of the PAN observed from the observation location. The case is classified as "A", observation of effects related to the ignition of the Falcon 9 rocket stage used for the launch of Starlink® satellite batch #28 for its deorbitation. The description of the PAN is very similar to that of another case studied and published by the GEIPAN, and which occurred on the same day at the same time: see case NOE (31) 26.05.2021.
