Summary: observation of an atmospheric reentry
--- seg-b19b39722d6216581b46fbfa [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 1, 2007, while closing his shutters at 20:34, a witness spotted in the sky a luminous phenomenon that intrigued him. For 2 to 3 seconds, he observed a phenomenon resembling a shooting star or a white fireworks rocket moving at high speed in the sky at low altitude in a NE-NW direction. No other information was collected. This witness made an observation of an atmospheric reentry of a meteorite or space debris, but it was not possible to identify it with certainty.
