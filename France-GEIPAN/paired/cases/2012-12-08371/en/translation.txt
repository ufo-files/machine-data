--- seg-eae2d67c9c06f597050cec9a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of Saturday to Sunday, December 2, at 12:03 AM, a couple observed from their home a dozen red glowing objects following each other at regular intervals and disappearing one after the other into the cloudy sky. The witness's description corresponds to the appearance of Thai lanterns (note, however, the description of blue reflections, rare on lanterns, unless they are blue). The local wind, as recorded in Verneuil, is weak and unstable (see recordings) from a west sector, compatible with the movement of these objects. They may have been released along the Seine, at the end of a festive evening. This case is classified "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
