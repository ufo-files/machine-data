Summary: Observation of unusual lights.
--- seg-d57ee5c3cb8ccd01fcc01684 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 6, 1979, between 9:15 and 9:30 PM, several people observed in the sky luminous phenomena that intrigued them. A white glowing mass seemed to move from West to East, then 5 orange luminous sources of different shapes according to the witnesses (streaks or oval shapes) appeared. They would gradually disappear towards the East, dimming progressively. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
