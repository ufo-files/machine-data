Description: On June 30, 2012, at 11:25 PM, two witnesses observed from their terrace the successive passage of three red-orange lights. All moved silently in the same direction. The described behavior of the phenomenon, the photographs and video, the time, the silence, the date (a Saturday night) undoubtedly evoke Thai lanterns. The West wind present that day (see record) is consistent with the movement towards the East of the objects. This case is classified as "A".
