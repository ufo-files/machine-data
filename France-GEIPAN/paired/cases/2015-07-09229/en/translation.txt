Summary: Observations of a bright ball passing through the sky: observations of an atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-ef1d7387a221054e63530c44 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 9, 2015, around 7 PM, two witnesses in the department of Seine-et-Marne (77) observed the rapid passage of a luminous phenomenon in the sky. The phenomenon is described as a glowing ball with a tail. This observation, lasting a few seconds, of a glowing ball followed by a tail is typical of a meteoroid atmospheric reentry phenomenon. See the article "Is it a Meteor?" The GEIPAN received another simultaneous observation report and the specialized site IMO (International Meteor Organization) received 14. (see on the IMO site). Therefore, the GEIPAN classifies this case as A: certain observations of a meteoroid atmospheric reentry that caused a meteor.
