Summary: Observation of the passage of a luminous phenomenon in the sky: probable observation of the passage of a meteoroid.
--- seg-7999aa9be4da11155947927d [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 19, 1955, around 10:10 PM, three individuals observed the passage of a luminous phenomenon from the northeast (NE) to the south-west (SO). They described a white, luminous disk followed by a long, orange trail. No noise was heard during the 5 seconds of observation. The gendarmerie investigation was unable to find other witnesses to the phenomenon, except for one person who claimed to have seen something but could not provide any specific details about the date. The GEIPAN classified this case as B: probable observation of a bolide, meaning the atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
