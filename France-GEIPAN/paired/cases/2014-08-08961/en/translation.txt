--- seg-5bd217d7ffd56e4af49b84bc [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 3, 2014, around 10:30 PM, a witness observed in the sky a bright white light moving silently from SO-NE to NE at approximately the speed of an airplane. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of a very bright light crossing the sky towards the East in less than two hours after sunset clearly suggests a pass by the International Space Station (ISS). The Calsky site confirms this hypothesis: the ISS "overflew" the witness's area from 10:36 PM to 10:45 PM, from Southwest to Northeast. Since the witness did not report the ISS in addition to his observation, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the International Space Station.
