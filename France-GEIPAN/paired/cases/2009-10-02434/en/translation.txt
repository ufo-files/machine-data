Description: On October 31, 2009, around 3:30 AM, a witness observed in the sky a dance of several glowing orbs intersecting multiple times. He eventually woke up his partner who confirmed the same observation. This lasted for several hours. The witness finally returned to bed around 5 AM while the phenomenon was still ongoing. The phenomenon observed on the night of October 31, 2009, in Raincy (93), is likely due to the rotation of two sky trackers (see investigation report). The location of the animation could not be found and confirmed. This low-strangeness observation case is classified as B: probable observation of a laser beam light show.
