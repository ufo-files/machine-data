Description: The GEIPAN was contacted by the witness via email on Sunday, February 28, 2010, following the broadcast of a show on TF1 the previous day. The observation dates back to Saturday, May 23, 2009. While on vacation, the witness and their family saw a black rectangular object move within their field of vision before disappearing with an increase in altitude. The witness submitted the Terrestrial Questionnaire on May 12, 2009, almost a year after the incident, along with a location plan including the object's trajectory and a drawing of its shape. The characteristics of this observation case are not particularly strange or consistent (only one observation report, no photos). The observed phenomenon is likely a balloon, but of a very unusual shape. Therefore, and since the absence of reliable meteorological data prevents verifying that the balloon's movement is indeed consistent with the wind, the GEIPAN classifies this case as 'C': Lack of Information.
