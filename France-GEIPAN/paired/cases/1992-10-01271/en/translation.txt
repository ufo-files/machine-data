Summary: Possible observation of two night helicopters
--- seg-591c658b58ea133fba17c7d5 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 18th around 10 PM, a couple in a car observed one, then two, flickering lights at a low altitude that were stationary. The lights were orange with a white light and spun in a counterclockwise direction. A dark shape was located below the lights. After about ten minutes, the lights eventually left, moving very slowly. Although the observation was silent, it is very likely that the witnesses saw the lights of two helicopters in stationary flight that then moved away.
