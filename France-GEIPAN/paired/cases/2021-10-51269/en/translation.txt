Description: On October 5, 2021, at 10:47 PM, the witness observed in the municipality of SAVIGNY-LE-TEMPLE (77) moving bright points. Only one testimony was collected. Despite the existence of a video of the PAN made by the witness, this testimony is unusable due to a significant lack of data, particularly: - Initial and final azimuth - Initial and final elevation - Possible movement of the PAN and, if so, the nature of their trajectory (see the investigation report). Other data are imprecise: - T1's mention of an observation that is both "continuous and discontinuous" without further details - Unclear definition of the end of the observation. The video suggests the observation of a starlight or planet twinkle effect, but we do not have the elements (observation site and azimuth) to confirm it. The consistency, low, is not compensated by the level of strangeness, also low, given the ultimately banal nature of the observed and filmed objects: white bright points that could be entirely known and identifiable phenomena, but without further details in the testimony, we cannot better qualify them. Since the witness did not respond to the investigator's requests for clarification, this case is classified as "C": significant lack of data not allowing to conclude.
