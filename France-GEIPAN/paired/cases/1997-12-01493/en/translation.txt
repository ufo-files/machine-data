Summary: Observation of bright flash causing brief power outages
--- seg-33dd7563cc60574bcffe0e10 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 11, 1997, around 11 PM, military personnel observed silent light flashes. Simultaneously, brief power outages were observed. These outages were confirmed by EDF due to incidents on the 33 kV line serving the area. Given the simultaneous nature of the events, it is likely that the observed flashes occurred on the 33 kV line during the incidents reported on that line.
