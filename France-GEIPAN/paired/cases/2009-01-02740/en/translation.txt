--- seg-a24ed81197d3947e70e57274 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 17, 2009 at 21:05, a witness observed from their home the slow and silent movement of a bright red light above Grasse. The luminous phenomenon then made a rapid ascent before disappearing into the sky. This observation, of low strangeness but very thoroughly described by the witness, exhibits all the characteristics of a Thai lantern: red color, halo, movement according to the wind, duration of a few minutes, on a Saturday (near the Chinese New Year). This case is classified as a "B" observation, probable Thai lantern.
