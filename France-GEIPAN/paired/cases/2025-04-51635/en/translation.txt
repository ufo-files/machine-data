Summary: Brief observation of the passage on a descending trajectory of a very bright white object: bolide observation.
--- seg-e74508d4b64a5903446f2adf [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: The two witnesses were in a car on Sunday, April 13, 2025, at approximately 9:10 PM local time on the Toulouse (31) beltway when they observed a bright white object in the sky for 4 seconds that moved according to a descending parabolic trajectory. No trail or halo was visible. The object, which resembled "a helicopter," appeared at an azimuth of 315° and an elevation of 30° and disappeared after 4 seconds of observation to the east, at the horizon. The consistency* of the case is average, with only one of the two witnesses completing a Technical Questionnaire (QT), and no photos or videos of the PAN available. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The only hypothesis explored, that of observing a meteor, is supported by the following elements:

- Oblong shape, similar to the cabin of a helicopter
- White color
- High brightness
- Rapid movement according to a descending parabolic trajectory
- Verified passage at the time of observation of a meteor over France, observed by 56 witnesses from Toulouse to the south and as far northwest as Chartres, as well as throughout the entire west (see the IMO (International Météo Organization) website for the event report 20252094-2025).

- The appearance of this meteor, described by numerous witnesses, is consistent with that described by T1
- The trajectory is generally consistent (from west to east)
- The observation duration was very short (4 seconds), consistent with the observation of a meteor (in the vast majority of cases, 3.5 seconds)
- The appearance location of the PAN (azimuth 315°, elevation 30°) is consistent with the observation of a meteor by a witness in the Toulouse region (Fonsorbes): azimuth 330°, elevation 30°.

The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of a meteor.
