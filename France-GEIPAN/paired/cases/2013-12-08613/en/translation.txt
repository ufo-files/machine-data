Summary: Observations of a bright light in the sky: observations of an airplane trail.
--- seg-014a1a16414fc60f5096fe20 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 1, 2013, around 5:00 PM, people walking on the customs path in Bandol were intrigued by a bright light in the sky to the West of the sun. The glow persists after sunset and allows for the sight of two fixed, yellow-orange lights visible to the naked eye. They fade away after 15 minutes. Only one testimony is collected. Red-orange streaks at sunset clearly evoke airplane contrails made brilliant by backlighting. See: strange aircraft trails. The attached "flightradar24" site log indicates the aircraft in visibility on that day. Aircraft trails are visible up to 120 km in these excellent visibility conditions. Due to the distance and the direction of the plane receding, the trail appears at a very low viewing angle, giving a distorted perspective of the phenomenon. For the same reasons, the angular velocity of movement of the phenomenon appears to be low. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A, perfectly identified.
