--- seg-a060506e738343eb7fe6bddc [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 11, 2013, around 7 a.m., on the A62 highway between Agen and Toulouse, a driver and his passenger observed for approximately twenty minutes a white glowing point that seemed static, followed by two white trails forming an equilateral triangle to the northeast in the sky. The car eventually passed the phenomenon, and the witnesses lost sight of the PAN. Only one statement was collected. This statement is of good consistency: precise but without photographs or videos. The observation is not particularly strange; it involves an object with a behavior and appearance ultimately mundane. Given the elements defined in the investigation report (see document), namely the favorable meteorology for the formation or absence of high-altitude condensation trails, as well as an appearance consistent with similar photographed cases occurring under the same conditions, this case is classified as "A." It is a certain observation of a commercial jet with short condensation trails.
