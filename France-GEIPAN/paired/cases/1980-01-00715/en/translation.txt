--- seg-f74767aa5032baebd203c267 [needs-review] ---
Description: Between January 10 and January 16, 1980, numerous people, including gendarmes, will spot in the sky a bright object. This circular object is glowing and twinkling. It moves slowly from East to West. Photographs of the object will be taken. Gradually, the object loses its brightness, turning orange, and then disappears. All these people have made an astronomical observation of Venus, particularly visible during this time of year (magnitude -3.83 on January 12, 1980, at 7:04 PM, and disappearing into the ocean at 8:30 PM).
