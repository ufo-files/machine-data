Description: On July 7, 2013, a witness participated in a night sky watching vigil. At the end of the vigil at 03:10, the witness observed in the starry sky above Montchevret a bright white light moving across the sky. The trajectory of this light intrigued the witness as a PAN directly above his position suddenly turned 90 degrees in less than 3 seconds, heading towards the hamlet of Romandie. The light was lost from view due to terrain. No trail or noise was noticed. No other testimonies were collected. The witness's account appears somewhat strange, as it describes a sudden change in trajectory of a bright light in the sky... and it wasn't an airplane. In reality, it's possible that a light trajectory from a satellite stops when it exits the sunlit area, right near where the light trajectory of another satellite entering the sunlit zone begins. Since these two trajectories are not continuous, the observer might mistake them for a single object changing its trajectory. It was not possible to delve further into this type of analysis as the witness was uncooperative with information requests (incomplete or no responses to questions, despite follow-ups). The observation was made during a night sky watching vigil organized by a UFO group. This context, for some participants, may bias the objectivity of the observation (emulation among participants, quest for strangeness), even unbeknownst to the witness. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as C: lack of reliable information.
