Description: On April 15, 1976, between 9:00 and 9:30 PM, a witness observed an orange-colored light phenomenon for approximately two minutes. It remained stationary above the bell tower of a church located two kilometers away. No unusual noises were heard. It suddenly disappeared. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
