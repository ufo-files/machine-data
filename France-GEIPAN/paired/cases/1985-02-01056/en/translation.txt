Description: On February 13th and 18th, 1985, multiple witnesses observed a silent, luminous phenomenon in the sky at 9:30 PM: a ball much larger than a star moving slowly at a low altitude. With a circular shape and very bright white color, this ball quickly disappeared from the witnesses' field of vision, who nonetheless took the time to film the phenomenon. The hypothesis regarding the observation of a planet or star could not be validated due to the lack of collected information.
