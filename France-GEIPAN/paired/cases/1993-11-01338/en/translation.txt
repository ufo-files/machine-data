Summary: Observation of a stationary yellow ball
--- seg-e8341d7b6a2c4748840a37c9 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 26th between 7:15 and 8:15, a witness observed for nearly an hour a yellow ball that seemed to be burning up. After an hour, the ball quickly departed, leaving behind a black trail. The investigation was unable to identify the ball as a civilian or military aircraft, or a balloon. The phenomenon remains unexplained.
