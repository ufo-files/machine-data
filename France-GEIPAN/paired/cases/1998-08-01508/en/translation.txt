Summary: Observations of the passage of an aircraft.
--- seg-81f44c8fe8b78205daee8627 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 10, 1998, around midnight, numerous witnesses, including a train driver, observed a triangular shape in the sky with two red lights and one very bright white light. One witness, referring to newspaper articles, stated that the observed phenomenon was a ULM (Unidentified Flying Object) that also passed near his home during the same time frame. The passage of a Transall aircraft at low altitude along the axis of the two observation locations was also reported. It is therefore almost certain that the observed phenomenon was the passage of one of the two aircraft.
