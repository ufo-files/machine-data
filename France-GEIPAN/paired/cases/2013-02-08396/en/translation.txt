Summary: Observations of several stationary tracks in the cloudy sky: observation of aircraft condensation trails.
--- seg-5567fbf6d94f044160b71b26 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Witnesses observed several tracks in the cloudy sky that eventually disappeared one after the other. A child took photographs with his smartphone. The questionnaire and the gendarmerie protocol will report the testimonies. The photograph of this phenomenon (taken at 1/125s, very clear), clearly depicts aircraft condensation trails. Due to the north wind, dry (65% humidity, source: Météociel), these trails are short; because of the observation conditions (sunset at 18:01 in Le Mans), they are still in direct sunlight, while the blue of the sky begins to darken: the contrast is then very strong, the trails look like flames. When the Earth's shadow reaches these trails, they are no longer illuminated and thus are no longer visible as they are much less bright than the blue of the sky. This case is classified "A": observation of aircraft trails.
