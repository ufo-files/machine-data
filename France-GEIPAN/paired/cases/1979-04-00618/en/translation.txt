Description: During several nights in April 1979, between 11:00 PM and 12:00 AM, two individuals observed three flashing white and red lights arranged in a triangle in the sky. On April 17th, between 11:00 PM and 11:30 PM, while returning home along a rural path, these people saw one of these lights quickly descending towards the ground, appearing to come from a flattened circular object approximately two meters in diameter. Despite their observation lasting several minutes, the witnesses could not determine if the object, then at 200 meters away, was on the ground or stationary above it, as it emitted an extremely intense light. Frightened, they returned home experiencing tingling in their eyes. The gendarmerie found no traces on the ground or in the environment. No other testimonies were collected regarding this phenomenon, for which we lack information.
