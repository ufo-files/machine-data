Summary: Ancient observation of the passage of three oval objects in the cloudy sky: lack of information.
--- seg-e5e8336ef50184d68733480f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 3, 2009, a witness contacted the GEIPAN regarding a previous observation made on June 3, 2008 at 9 PM. From the third-floor balcony, the witness observed the successive presence of three objects with a shape comparable to a rugby ball and a faded brown and yellow color. The objects moved along a straight trajectory, with two moving in the same direction. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted about ten seconds: the phenomenon left the field of view as it was obscured by clouds. Overall, this observation of oval objects appears to be of low to moderate strangeness, with a fairly probable hypothesis that the witness observed planes with a particular shape and brightness due to the time being near sunset (14 minutes later). Since the investigation, which was too late, did not support this hypothesis, this case is classified as "C" due to a lack of information on aircraft flights at that time.
