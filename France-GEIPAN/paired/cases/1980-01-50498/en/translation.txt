Summary: Fake abduction of a witness by extraterrestrials staged for advertising purposes.
--- seg-58a68f782b2c07df851d4c1c [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 22, 1980, while reading the weekly magazine "SPECIAL DERNIERE" No. 572 from February 15 to 21, 1980, gendarmes learned that the husband of a restaurant owner had allegedly been abducted by extraterrestrials. The incident is said to have occurred on January 21, 1980, around 9 a.m. in the Vierzon forest. Very quickly, the gendarmerie investigation led to a carefully constructed hoax, admitted by the witness.
