--- seg-89ea2499502e76b1e0898234 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 31, 2015, at 12:15 AM, a witness observed a bright point moving from south to north in the sky. Very quickly, the witness saw more than a dozen white and red lights appear, moving silently. No other testimonies were collected. The observation of series of red or white glowing shapes crossing the sky in just a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The observed movement from south to north is consistent with the reported south-to-north wind in Evreux (refer to the Evreux Meteorology report on the observation day). The triangular shape described by the witness could be the shape of the brightest part of the lantern: see the attached photo GEIPAN which shows the glowing part as approximately triangular. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of Thai lanterns.
