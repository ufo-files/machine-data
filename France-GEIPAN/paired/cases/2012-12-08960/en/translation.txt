Description: Old case (2012) reported in August 2014. An investigator will contact the witness by phone and then meet with them in December 2014 to complete their testimony. On December 14, 2012, around 5 PM, a witness is intrigued by a shadow obscuring the living room window. Going to their terrace, the person notices a black object in motion above their residence. The witness describes a triangular object with superimposed side panels and a circular shape in the center. Steam is escaping from the object. During the 2-4 minute observation, humming and "clapping" noises are heard. The witness makes a gesture towards the object, and it disappears quickly towards the northwest. No other testimony is collected. After investigation, this very strange case of a close encounter with an unknown craft, reported by a single witness only 20 months after the facts, is found to be insufficiently consistent to provide a consolidated scientific opinion (see the investigation report). The case is classified in category C due to the lack of sufficient information related to the strangeness of the case.
