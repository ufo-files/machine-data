Description: On July 17, 2014, around 11 PM, a witness and his son observed a series of flashes coming from the sky. The __UFO_PROTECTED_000__ remained stationary in the sky throughout the observation, which lasted 40 minutes. The intensity of the flashes was comparable in shape and brightness to stars. Using the planetarium in Aix-en-Provence, the witness identified the constellation of Scorpio. The following day, the witness, with his children, still looked at the same place in the sky, and at 11:10 PM, the family saw the flash of least intensity three times. Only one testimony was collected. With an average strangeness and consistency (multiple witnesses, but only one testimony), this case appears to be the probable observation of a rapidly rotating geostationary satellite. Several geostationary satellites are present in the area indicated by the witness, and at least one of them is known to rotate rapidly on itself. This case is classified as B, probable observation of a rapidly rotating geostationary satellite.
