Description: Recent testimony (03/10/2012) of an old observation whose date remains approximate according to the witness. On September 15, 2003, around 9:30 PM, an individual observes the successive presence in the sky of two dark, triangular objects equipped with several lights. Initially stationary, they then move rapidly with changes in direction and disappear towards the setting sun. No noise is heard during the observation. The witness illustrates his account with a photo of the Petit-Rechain case from 1990 in Belgium, specifying that there was more light during his observation. Due to the variable lighting in the middle of the triangle, it could be a misidentification with an airplane or helicopter, observed from far enough away to not be heard. The impreciseness regarding the date and the age of the observation makes a serious investigation impossible. This case is classified "C" due to lack of information.
