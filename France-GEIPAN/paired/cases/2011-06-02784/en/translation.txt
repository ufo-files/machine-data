Description: On June 20, 2011 at 23h, in a field, two people observed a silent, yellow-orange glowing sphere above them. After making a turn over the top of the trees, a triangular shape appeared to them. Very frightened, they left the area, but one of the witnesses had filmed the observation. The witnesses likely observed the passage of an aircraft, as the video shows an object resembling an airplane with its standard nighttime lighting and runway lights on. No investigation was conducted to identify which aircraft was overflying the site.
