--- seg-66df893bae66744984a6e042 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 5, 2013, at 4:29 a.m., a witness, taking a break at their workplace, sat outside a building. They were then surprised by a succession of five powerful, white flashes that they observed heading towards a nearby GSM relay antenna. No unusual noises were heard. No other testimonies were collected. After a field investigation with the witness, the GEIPAN investigator carefully analyzed several hypotheses: the illumination of the cloud layer by flashes produced by the pantographs of a passing train on the curved railroad track located 2500 meters in the line of sight of the observer appears to account for nearly all the characteristics described by the witness. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of flashes from an electric locomotive.
