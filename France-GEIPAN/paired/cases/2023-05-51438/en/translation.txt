**Description:** On May 11, 2023, in the late afternoon, a witness, a passenger on a bus traveling on the N77 from Neuvy-Sautour (89) to Saint-Florentin (89) and passing through the hamlet of Courcelles (89), observed a curtain of water under a very dark cloud: a PAN (Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon) seemed to emerge from this cloud, leaving behind a persistent gray trail. The observation was very brief (2 to 3 seconds for the PAN and several minutes for the trail) but obstructed by numerous visual obstacles (trees and buildings). Only one testimony was collected. With an average strangeness and consistency (single witness, no photo of the PAN), the investigation suggests the possibility of two distinct phenomena (a bird and the upper edge of a distant cloud), but the lack of precision and fleeting nature of one of the observations does not allow for a conclusion (see the investigation report). There are no elements to formally confirm that the PAN and its trail are directly related, as they were not observed simultaneously. They could therefore be independent of each other. The temporal conjunction of the two successive observations, as well as the similar shape of the PAN's trajectory and the subsequent trail, may have led the witness to believe that the second was a consequence of the first. Although the investigation explores hypotheses, the low strangeness and lack of precision lead the GEIPAN to classify the case as "C," Unidentified Phenomenon due to a lack of information. *According to GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.
