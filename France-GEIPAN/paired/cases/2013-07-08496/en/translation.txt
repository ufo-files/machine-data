Summary: Observation of the rapid passage in the sky of a white incandescent object without trail: probable observation of a bolide.
--- seg-149aaf94f671a22b04356409 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 17, 2013, at 11:58 PM, a witness observed from their window a blazing object with a white light, without a trail, moving rapidly in a descending trajectory. The description of an "incandescent object with a white light, without a trail, very fast" perfectly matches a bolide (atmospheric entry of a meteoroid). There was no automatic camera recording, but another similar testimony was received at GEIPAN, with a reported time very close: at Saint-Germain-Sur-Ille (35) at 11:45 PM, observed to the East, moving from North to South. It is reminded that these bolide phenomena, often perceived by witnesses as very close, occur at altitudes of 100 to 50 km, and are thus visible over an area of approximately 200 km in diameter. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable bolide observation.
