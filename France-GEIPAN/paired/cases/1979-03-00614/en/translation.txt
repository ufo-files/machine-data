Description: On March 31, 1979, the gendarmerie was contacted to verify the presence of a piece of ice that had fallen onto the train station parking lot. There were no victims or damages, despite ice fragments scattered over approximately thirty meters. Within a piece of ice, the presence of beige and red substances appeared. Samples of this material will be analyzed to conclude the presence of fat of plant or animal origin from a block of ice that likely detached from an aircraft. The village is indeed located below a heavily trafficked air corridor, and particular climatic conditions can cause such incidents.
