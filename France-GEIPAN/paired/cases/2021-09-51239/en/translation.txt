--- seg-6e8a16214eb4bcfc202efa7d [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 1, 2021, at 05:13 in the morning, the witness saw through the window of their ground-floor apartment an extremely bright, white, and oval-shaped PAN. The PAN crossed their field of vision in 3 or 4 seconds and appeared to be heading towards "Les Grands Moulins de Paris" in Nancy as if it were going to land. The investigation, using FRIPON data, concluded that a meteor crossed the sky at exactly the same time as the observation (see the investigation elements attached). The recalculated azimuths from the witness's photos are consistent with the meteor's trajectory. The angular height of approximately 19° is consistent with the field of vision allowed by the window. The magnitude of -4.7 corresponds to an extremely bright object, consistent with the observation. The case is consistent and precise. The strangeness perceived by the witness, describing: "The light intensity, size, and especially the speed/motion surprised me," is compatible with observing a meteor. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A: observation of a meteor.
