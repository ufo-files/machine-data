Description: On June 26, 1985, a witness observed for 5 minutes an object with a circular shape, 1.50 meters in diameter and approximately 1.80 meters in height. According to the witness, the object appeared to be composed of an unstable, gaseous-like material. It was stationary at 5 meters above the ground and made no noise. An incandescing radiation emanated from the object, which then suddenly dematerialized. The witness had previously made a similar observation in 1976. No further information could be gathered about this phenomenon.
