Description: On February 9, 1981, around 2:45 PM, a witness observed in the sky a fireball of orange color followed by a trail of gray smoke. This sphere descended slowly and silently. It disappeared into the landscape. Another ball of the same type then emerged. During the investigation, it was found that on February 9 at approximately 2:30 PM, the airport firefighters nearby had launched distress rockets with parachutes. These rockets rise vertically to an altitude of about 100 to 200 meters, stay alight for a minute before gently falling and turning off. The witness likely saw these distress rockets.
