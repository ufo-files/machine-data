--- seg-bc01cbedd48a4801637ed8d9 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 15, 2013, at 23:00, a witness observed the clear sky from his terrace. He saw a stationary, colored light phenomenon. The observation lasted several hours with the naked eye and binoculars. No particular noise was heard. Only one testimony was collected. This observation of a fixed bright point in the sky corresponds to the planet Jupiter, which is brilliant in the evening sky to the East. The color changes observed by the witness with binoculars are due in part to atmospheric turbulence (twinkling) and in part to the optics of the binoculars (a white bright point generally appears irized). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A" for observation of the planet Jupiter.
