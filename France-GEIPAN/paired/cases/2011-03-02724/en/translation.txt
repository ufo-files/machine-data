Summary: Pilot evasive maneuver after observing an object with a faceted spherical shape.
--- seg-4cea84077e3a24e0cca83965 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Sunday, March 20, 2011, at 3:30 PM, a witness flying his plane, located slightly vertically above the Dreux airport, performed an evasive maneuver to avoid an object shaped like a polygon with numerous black and some green facets. This object, estimated to be 2 to 3 meters in diameter, appeared static or moving very slowly. By the time the pilot turned around, the object was gone. The encountered object is likely a thermal balloon (or solar balloon), launched by amateurs. The black color, the apparent absence of a nacelle, and the size are consistent with this hypothesis. We have not found any other examples of a thermal balloon of this shape. Strictly speaking, the balloon's disappearance can be explained by the plane's turbulence, which would have destabilized it and caused it to descend rapidly. The case is classified as B in the associated investigation report.
