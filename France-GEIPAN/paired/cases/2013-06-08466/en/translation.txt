Description: On June 1, 2013, at 12:02 AM, several people observed the silent passage of a dozen glowing yellow-orange orbs that appeared successively in the sky. The witness's description (very bright yellow-orange orbs moving slowly in the sky) perfectly matches Thai lanterns. After viewing videos and photos of Thai lanterns, the witness recognized the phenomenon, which he had never seen before. These lanterns could have been released during a wedding held 600 meters away. The Meteociel wind report for the local area indicates a light west-northwesterly wind that is not consistent with the observed movement (rather towards the east). However, a north-easterly wind is reported in Boulogne; coastal winds are often complex and vary in direction with altitude. Given all these elements, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns. The low strangeness of the case does not require a field investigation.
