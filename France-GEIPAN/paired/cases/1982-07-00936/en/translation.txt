Summary: Observation of a disk-shaped object moving very fast
--- seg-99067adce00102b76edb151a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 3, 1982, a witness saw from the window of his kitchen a 20cm-diameter orange ball that was twinkling. The weather was very stormy, and the ball was in front of a cloud formation approaching the area. During the observation, which lasted over a minute, the ball maintained the same trajectory and moved quickly. The phenomenon disappeared abruptly. No other information was gathered about this event.
