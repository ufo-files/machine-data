Description: On October 1, 2012, at 7:35 PM, several witnesses observed in the sky the rapid passage of a ball with a white trail that seemed to be descending towards the ocean. A total power outage occurred very quickly after the ball's passage. Only one testimony was collected. The observation characteristics are identical in all respects to those of a meteor. The witness experienced a strangeness by associating the observation and the power outage, which was only a coincidence (power outages are not uncommon on the island, and in fact, EDF had predicted them for that week). The meteor could not be confirmed in the records (which are never exhaustive, especially outside the continent), but that is the most likely hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies the case as B: Meteor
