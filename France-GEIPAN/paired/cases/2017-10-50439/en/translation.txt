Description: On October 11, 2017, around 2:30 PM, a witness lying on a chaise lounge was looking at the sky to the South. He suddenly became intrigued by the rapid passage to the East of a white, round object, about the size of a "handball," in the sky. The observation lasted one minute, and suddenly the witness saw the PAN "move backward." The witness got up to retrieve his smartphone to take a picture. Upon his return, he had difficulty locating the PAN, but eventually managed to photograph it: he then noticed a bright white spot on his device. Intrigued, the witness picked up his phone again and searched the sky for the PAN: a smaller object was found, but it disappeared from his view. The witness sat back down on his chaise lounge and observed floating filaments in the sky. His daughter also noticed the presence of these threads. The witness took several pictures. He later found some of these threads attached to his cherry tree. Only one testimony was collected. The testimony's consistency is good, and the witness is precise. He provided the investigator with all the elements allowing for a good analysis of the case. The photos are a valuable addition. The investigations and analyses (see the investigation report) led to the conclusion of two identified phenomena: - simultaneous presence of two planes crossing at the same time and at the same location (visually), creating the illusion of a single object moving backward; - the photographed threads show "Virgin's threads," commonly known filaments. These silk threads are the vehicles for tiny young spiders that are thus carried by the wind. This phenomenon is called ballooning. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies these two cases as: Case CHALLES-LES-EAUX (73) 11.10.2017 T1: Misidentification with commercial airplanes. Case CHALLES-LES-EAUX (73) 11.10.2017 T2: Observation of ballooning.
