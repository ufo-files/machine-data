Description: On the night of August 24 to 25, 1992, around midnight, two young people on motorcycles observed a glow "following" them up to the home of one of them, above the trees. This glow was white and oval in shape, and silent. The weather was pleasant and the sky clear. The two young people were very frightened, which motivated their statement, but no other information could be gathered.
