Description: In April 2008, a witness reported that during a vacation in Camargue in 1993, he observed with another person a particular luminous phenomenon. This phenomenon arrived very quickly, resembling a shooting star, then slowed and stopped, making several zigzags before disappearing vertically at very high speed. Our requests for additional information went unanswered. This very old observation lacks precise indications of location or date (January 1st was given arbitrarily). No investigation is possible on an observation lacking significant oddities that suggest the observation of an atmospheric reentry.
