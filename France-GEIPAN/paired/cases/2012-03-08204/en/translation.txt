Summary: Successive short observations in the same evening of red-brown objects passing through a clear sky: lack of information and cross-references.
--- seg-326a2ba69dd199f2fc362747 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 12, 2012, at 8:00 PM, a witness steps outside onto their terrace and looks up at the sky when they spot 3 red-brown objects in the form of a cross crossing the clear sky from West to North. The observation lasts only a few seconds. At 10:50 PM, the witness steps outside again with their companion, and they both see 5 objects in the sky moving from North to East. They are quickly obscured by the landscape (trees and houses in the neighborhood). Only one testimony is collected. The observation of red flying objects moving in a group in the sky evokes Thai lanterns released for a festival. The Meteociel report for Strasbourg, 40 km away, indicates a light variable wind from the North-East, North, and North-West. In this case, the described movement towards North-East does not correspond well to the local wind; however, this wind was light and variable, and we do not have a very close weather report. We cannot formally rule out this hypothesis. The day of the week (Monday) is rarely a day for festivals, which also does not support this hypothesis, nor does the cross shape described by the witness. Air traffic reports no aircraft that could correspond to the observation. The GEIPAN received no other reports of phenomena in the region on that day. Due to a lack of precise weather information and cross-references, the GEIPAN classifies this case as Category C.
