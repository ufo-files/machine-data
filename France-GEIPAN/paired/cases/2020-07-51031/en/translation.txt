Summary: Long observation in the early morning sky of a dark PAN emitting stroboscopic white light as well as intermittent incandescent light from the bottom of the PAN up: observation of a hot air balloon.
--- seg-f4afe1fecdb3f27e3995fc55 [machine-unreviewed] ---
**Description:** On the morning of July 29, 2020, two residents of SAINT-OUEN-LES-VIGNES (37) distinctly observed a white, strobe-light emitting object. Every 5 to 6 sequences, a bright red light ascends from below the object, illuminating the entire object. The PAN (presumed to mean "unidentified flying object" or UFO) hovers in place for a quarter hour, then begins moving very slowly horizontally from south-west (SO) to south-east (SE), stopping regularly. One of the witnesses recorded short videos of the PAN with his smartphone. Due to its low strangeness and very good consistency (two witnesses, PAN videos), this case is classified as a hot air balloon observation (see investigation report). The visual aspect of the PAN in the videos and the description provided by the witnesses could suggest a drone or a hot air balloon seen from a distance. The drone hypothesis can be ruled out as the shape of the PAN is inconsistent. In contrast, the hot air balloon hypothesis aligns well with several elements described in the testimonies: - rounded shape, - light present beneath the PAN, - glowing red light emanating from below and spreading upwards, reminiscent of a burner flame, - powerful white strobe light, consistent with the light used by hot air balloons during night flights. The observation direction corresponds to the Loire Valley, specifically the areas around the castles of Amboise (37) and Chenonceau (37), where several hot air balloon flights occurred on the morning of July 29, 2020, at times consistent with the observation. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: observation of a hot air balloon.
