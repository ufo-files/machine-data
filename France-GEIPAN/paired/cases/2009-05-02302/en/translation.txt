No additional documentation will be provided despite a request for a Terrestrial Questionnaire and submission of the testimony to the Gendarmerie. It is very difficult to draw conclusions based on such a limited amount of information. The strangeness of the case is relatively low, as is its consistency (one witness, no detailed questionnaire, no photos or videos). This case is classified C due to a lack of information.
