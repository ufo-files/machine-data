Description: On September 30, 2018, while driving home to Manthelan (37) after a night out in Reignac-sur-Indre (37), witness (T1) and her husband (T2) observed lights above the fields, visible through the trees. They then drive on D58. This refers to 3 stationary, orange lights forming a "triangle." The observation took place around 1:45 a.m. and lasted for a few minutes before the lights disappeared. The consistency* of the case is judged average with the presence of 2 witness accounts but the absence of photo or video documentation. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. In view of the observation, only one hypothesis is retained, that of three Thai lanterns likely released from a hamlet or village in the area for a private celebration (see the investigation report). Indeed, the characteristics of the PAN (color, shape, brightness, number) do correspond well to this type of object. Furthermore, the impression of immobility can be explained by the light wind at the time of observation and the movement of the car. It is also worth noting that the night from Saturday to Sunday is conducive to festive activities during which this type of object may be released. Finally, the lifespan of these lanterns generally does not exceed 15 minutes, which is consistent with the observation duration of a few minutes. The origin of the release could not be identified and the consistency was average, so the case is classified as "B": probable observation of a group of three Thai lanterns.
