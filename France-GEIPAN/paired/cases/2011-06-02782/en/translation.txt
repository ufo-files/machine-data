Summary: Observation of the rapid and silent movement of lights.
--- seg-181f4322fac81b60dc1c40c4 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness reports two observations, one on June 26, 2010, around midnight. On that date, they observed a bright, white light moving in a straight line from the northwest and quickly disappearing to the southeast above their home. As the light passed overhead, the witness noticed three white lights, two in front and one behind, forming a triangular shape. No noise was heard during the observation. Another report of this phenomenon will be collected. This observation has some characteristic elements of low-altitude aircraft, but some elements do not correspond (e.g., silence, signaling). The GEIPAN searched for radar traces of aircraft in flight with the civil aviation and Air Force (see radar trace document): only high-altitude flights are reported. It should be noted that radars do not cover low-altitude flights, and this area is known to the Air Force as it is one of their low-altitude exercise zones. Due to this lack of precise information, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C."
