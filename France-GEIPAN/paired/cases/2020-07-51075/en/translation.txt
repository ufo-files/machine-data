Description: On the night of July 18 to 19, 2020, the main witness, accompanied by 4 other people, was in the fields on the heights of the commune of Saint-Germain-Des-Près (45) to observe the comet Neowise. Between midnight and 1 a.m., they observe a very bright white flash, followed by a straight line streaking south, then a second flash much less bright before the line dissipates. The observation lasted less than a dozen seconds. The consistency* is judged good. Although only one direct witness reported the observation, the questionnaire was meticulously completed, and a 2D video reconstruction could be made by the main witness (T1), providing highly exploitable elements for analysis. Two hypotheses were explored: the observation of a double satellite flash and the atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid (see the investigation report). The latter hypothesis was prioritized for the following reasons: - The actual presence, at the date and time of the observation, of a meteor shower, observed from various locations across the French territory, and reported on the IMO/AMS* website. - A double flash corresponding to the fragmentation of the meteor into two parts during its entry into the dense layers of the atmosphere. The persistent streak for several seconds is the trace left by the plasma resulting from the vaporization of the elements composing this meteor. - The disappearance of this persistent streak "from its ends to its center" is explained by the higher concentration in the middle of this plasma vapor trail, which takes longer to dissipate than at the ends. - The white color is common and consistent with what is observable during such entries. - Short observation duration, consistent. - Observation direction consistent with what was observed by meteor witnesses (on IMO/AMS). The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A": observation of the atmospheric reentry of a bolide. *CONSISTENCY: According to the GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. *IMO: International Meteor Organization.https://www.imo.net/
