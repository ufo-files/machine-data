--- seg-c7e24562e3c47657f7292f91 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 14, 2011, around 9:45 PM, a witness observed for several minutes the rapid movements of a light. The light described "eights" in the cloudy sky. The witness's description closely matches the appearance of laser projectors used for festive animations (nightclubs, fairs, private events, etc.). The presence of low clouds made the phenomenon very visible. Note that on that day there was a fair in Reims, which is likely the origin of the phenomenon.
