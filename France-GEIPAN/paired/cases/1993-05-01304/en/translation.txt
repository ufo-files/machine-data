Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archive on its public website www.geipan.fr. Among its publications are old cases classified at the time (A, B, C, or D) that are now being reassessed with the sole purpose of being more relevant in their conclusions. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over the past many years, this reassessment sometimes leads to new observations or a change in classification. This observation case previously classified as D and named HERSERANGE (54) 1993 is part of a recent set of cases that have been reassessed. In the night of May 30 to 31, 1993, around 2 a.m., a family returns home by car from a family party. The mother and her son observe for approximately 5 minutes 6 to 8 oval, glowing shapes in the overcast sky. These shapes move in jumps and circles over a small space or from right to left in a zigzag pattern through the clouds. Two testimonies are collected, as the other family members were not witnesses to the phenomenon. The elements of the testimonies from the Gendarmerie protocol allow us to conclude to a confusion with a set of "Skytracker" or "SkyRose" lasers (see the investigation report). The number, shape, visibility, and behavior of the PAN support this hypothesis. Furthermore, the observation day, at the end of the week, is more conducive to the use of such a Skytracker, either by a nightclub or at a private festive party. The laser shows, now frequent and easily recognizable in the testimonies received by the GEIPAN, were rarer in 1993. It is not possible today to investigate the origin of this show at the site or in its vicinity. However, we have an observation from that year where the investigation allowed the show to be found and highlights that the equipment producing such effects was sold to nightclubs in France (see Gendarmerie protocol for the observation case ILE BOUCHARD (37) 11.12.1993). The GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable (rather than certain, due to the inability to identify the installation or verify the compatibility of weather conditions) observation of a laser or "Sky Rose".
