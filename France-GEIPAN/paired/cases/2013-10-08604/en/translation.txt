Summary: Successive observations on the same evening of red light phenomena in the sky: probable observations of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-c2af89c921cbd88e3d90aa80 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 30, 2013, between 6:50 PM and 8:30 PM, three witnesses observed a red glowing phenomenon in the sky, which appeared and then seemed to hover above a nearby forest. The phenomenon disappeared only to reappear later, still stationary in the sky. Eventually, it faded out like a halogen lamp. Two people testified. The observation of orange spheres in the sky for several minutes resembles Thai lanterns, but this case is notable in several ways:

- It was not a Saturday night; it was Halloween, which could have been the occasion for a release.
- The objects seemed stationary: the witness, interviewed on the phone, was looking towards the northwest (see map), meaning they were facing the light southeast wind (see weather report for Île d'Oléron). They observed an object carried by the wind slowly moving away from them and appeared immobile.
- A second object appeared at the same location 15 minutes later: it can be assumed, as often in such cases, that there were multiple releases of lanterns that followed strictly the same trajectory.

The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
