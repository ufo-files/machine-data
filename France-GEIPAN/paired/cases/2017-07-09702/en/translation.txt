Summary: Observation of the movement of several orange glowing discs in the night sky on the same trajectory: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-cc5797e9612e86dcc90598b8 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 8, 2017, around 11:45 PM, a witness and his spouse were intrigued by the movement on a single trajectory of two orange disks in the clear sky. Several other disks of the same color then appear. All seem to be coming from the East (Gondecourt) and heading towards the Southwest (Ostricourt). No noise was heard during the observation. The witness took five photos, which were not transmitted. Only one statement was collected. The description (colors and shapes) is consistent with a lantern release from Thailand. At low altitude and vertical, lanterns can appear as disks. According to Infoclimat, the wind that evening was from the NNE to SSW. Therefore, it could have caused this trajectory observed from the ground (described as coming from the North to go South-West). It was a weekend, and summer as well: so it was a propitious moment for festive events and releases of Thai lanterns into the night sky. The GEIPAN classifies this case as B: Thai lanterns.
