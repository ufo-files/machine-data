Description: On Wednesday, August 8, 2012, around 9:30 PM, a witness is intrigued by the presence of an orange-colored light source in the sky. It suddenly disappears, and the witness then observes a dark circular object that abruptly changes course. The object disappears into the urban landscape. The observation in the sky of an orange glowing object ten minutes after sunset (21:17) clearly suggests a reflection of the sun on an airplane (see attached example GEIPAN). Many airplanes fly over Paris at this time. The extinction of the light corresponds to the airplane passing into shadow. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of the sun's reflection on an airplane.
