Description: On September 24, 2013, a witness having lunch with their family observed a very bright ball silently passing above a curtain of trees on a straight trajectory, then seemingly zigzagging very quickly and changing direction. Based on the elements defined in the investigation report, namely a very bright, glowing ball in a straight motion at the beginning and end of its trajectory, consistent with an object carried by the wind and with an object's change of direction compatible with variable winds in the area, but also the possibility of storm activity that could produce ball lightning phenomena, we have two plausible hypotheses, though not very likely. The information provided by the witnesses neither confirms nor rules out these hypotheses. This case is classified as "C" due to a lack of information and cross-references.
