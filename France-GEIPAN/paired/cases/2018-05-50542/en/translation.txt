Summary: Observation of the movement of a black ball with bright reflections: observation of the flight of a bird (raptor from the Courzieux zoo).
--- seg-711ab0dc01a0953d4ab594b7 [needs-review] ---
Description: The witness observed on May 22, 2018 at 14:09 for 1 to 1'30'', from the terrace of his apartment on the 3rd and top floor of his building, a dark black sphere with bright reflections, resembling a "bowling or bocce ball." This object was moving linearly from north to south at a high speed. The witness has time to take a photograph (voir DSC_2061.jpg). No other testimony was collected. On June 09, 2018, the witness, who had continued to investigate his observation, sent to the GEIPAN his own explanation (see witness mail document). After ruling out the drone hypothesis (following contacts with the Lyon drone service and the Lyon Saint Exupéry Air Transport Gendarmerie), the witness focused on the "raptor" hypothesis. The witness made numerous observations between May 22 and June 9, 2018. He noticed very large raptors coming from the Courzieux zoo (200 raptors reside there). He studied the viewing angles of these raptors by considering the same observation time and weather conditions. Based on the numerous views taken, the witness was able to note that under a specific angle, the raptors formed a ball (voir documents screenshot_144449 et 144320.png). The observation of May 22, 2018, therefore very likely corresponds to that of a large black raptor in slow passive flight, gliding in a perfectly horizontal current with a constant heading. The sun's position (above) is very likely the cause of the reflections and spherical perception. The witness was able to recreate his observation and photography of May 22, and on June 9, he definitively confirmed the hypothesis of a raptor. The observation is therefore that of a raptor from the Courzieux zoo, flying slowly, without changing course, with a spherical shape and perceived reflections due to an optical illusion related to sunlight reflection. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: observation of a bird.
