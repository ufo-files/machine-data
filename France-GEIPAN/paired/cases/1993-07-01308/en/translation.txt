Description: On July 02, 1993, around 10:45 PM, two witnesses observed white glowing balls forming two V-shapes. These shapes remained stationary for a few seconds before disappearing at very high speed. These observations are confirmed by the witnesses' spouses. No noise was heard, the weather was clear, and the observation lasted about ten seconds. No further information could be gathered about this phenomenon. A case previously classified as D was re-examined: similar observations were investigated and classified as A in August 2009, like the formation flight of the French Patrol: http://www.cnes-geipan.fr/geipan/actualites/13082009-Des-PAN-en-formation-dans-le-Vaucluse-en-Juillet-2008.html This case is classified as A, like the observation of the French Patrol's flyby.
