--- seg-70ea2281ee9c8261bf1d59ac [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 14, 2019, in Gigny (89), five people observed for 1.5 hours the movement of a nearly motionless black point in the sky that appeared very far away and very high up. At the end of the observation, as night falls, lights appear. Only one person testifies to the GEIPAN. This case is well-consistent: although only one witness testified, five people observed the phenomenon, which is very well described, and a photograph allows for calculations of azimuth and angular height (see the investigation report). This is an observation of a gas balloon, the USA-1 balloon from the 2019 Gordon Bennett Cup. All its characteristics are known and correspond in every respect to the testimony collected. The witnesses initially thought it was a hot air balloon, but they were perplexed by its apparent immobility and the appearance of lights. Or this strangeness can be explained as follows:

- Immobility: very high distance and low wind speed,
- Appearance of lights: the competition regulations require balloons to have night lighting, an aeronautical strobe light, and a landing spotlight. Their nighttime lighting is therefore mandatory, as soon as it gets dark. This case is classified A: observation of a gas balloon: the USA-1 balloon from the 2019 Gordon Bennett Cup.
