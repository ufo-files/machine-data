Summary: Observations by children of a small person and a "flying box": lack of information and cross-checking.
--- seg-c905002a191c16d39cd860b9 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 27, 1954, around 8:40 a.m., three children (aged 10, 8.5, and 5) were walking to school on RN 13 east of Ceint-D'eau (Figeac, 26) when they encountered a small person who fired a shot, frightening the children. The older two had previously noticed a kind of box in the field bordering the road. Both children stated that the box quickly took off: the eldest reported it at noon, and the younger around 8:40 a.m. The gendarmerie was notified by the teacher. The children were questioned by the gendarmes the next morning. No traces on the ground were found by the gendarmerie. No other testimonies were collected. The GEIPAN classified this case as C due to lack of information, contradictory statements, and lack of corroboration.
