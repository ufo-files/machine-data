--- seg-c8a05f90e6a13ec28beaa648 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 17, 2011, at 12:05 AM, a witness had their attention drawn to two bright red points slightly offset in the sky, moving at a rapid and identical speed and following an upward trajectory. No other testimonies were collected. The speed of the phenomena and the upward aspect of the glowing points point towards a probable observation of distress rockets. The lack of received information does not allow this hypothesis to be corroborated, and does not permit further investigations.
