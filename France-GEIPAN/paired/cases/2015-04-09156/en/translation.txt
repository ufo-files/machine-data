Description: On April 14, 2015, around 9:50 PM, a witness observed for one minute the passage to the south-east of two flashing points moving at a considerable speed. In the final observation direction (West), the civil flight "Flighradar" log shows, at the time indicated by the witness, 3 planes located approximately 80 km away, with two very close but at different altitudes, which can give the impression of being superimposed. At this distance, they appear to the witness at an elevation of approximately 10° (20 times the lunar diameter). At this distance, one can still clearly see the condensation trails of planes (at sunset time), but not necessarily the flash lights (strobe). But the witness describes a movement from the south towards the west, at a speed that appears to be faster than high-altitude commercial jets (10 to 12 km). It is more likely that he saw two military planes, at a distance of around 4 to 8 km. The area is a frequent maneuver zone, Tuesday is a fairly favorable day. The MétéoCiel report for Nîmes indicates very clear weather, and a light wind from the south to south-west (see the weather report). The perceived silence by the witness is not incompatible with an airplane more than 4 km away; the perceived noise depends on the type of airplane, engine speed, relative wind, and the sound environment in which the witness is located. The video made by the witness at the end of the observation clearly shows 2 flickering light sources blinking once per second, typical of "strobe" lights (see the observation video). The green or red position lights were not seen by the witness: on one hand, they are not necessarily lit during military operations, and on the other hand, beyond a certain distance, these weak lights are hard to discern alongside the powerful strobes. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of two aircraft.
