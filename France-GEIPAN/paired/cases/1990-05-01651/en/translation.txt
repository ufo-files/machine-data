Summary: Discovery of several dried grass circles in two fields
--- seg-5c9a6295d1b0edaeb03f94fe [needs-review] ---
Description: A farmer noticed in one of his pastures a circle of yellowed grass with a width of approximately 15 to 25 cm. The circle has a diameter of nearly 8 meters. This phenomenon was observed in two different properties at intervals of a few weeks. An investigation by SEPRA at the site, along with soil analyses conducted by the Rennes Higher Agricultural School, revealed that it was a fungal attack causing the well-known "witches' ring" phenomenon, which, in this case, was of a large size. The perfectly identified phenomenon was classified as A.
