Description: On July 5, 2015, at 10:30 PM, a witness observed through his glass bay a yellow glowing point coming towards him. He then went outside onto his terrace and saw the object split, flickering rapidly, before disappearing at high speed. The witness filmed his observation: in the video, distant streetlights and the moving glowing point can be seen. No other testimonies were collected. The powerful yellow light (see photo), with its flickering (see video), moving slowly low on the horizon, resembles an airplane preparing for landing. The apparent disappearance of the phenomenon corresponds to the airplane changing course, no longer facing the witness. In this configuration, an airplane is visible from more than 20 kilometers away. The witness is located 20 kilometers from Basel Mulhouse airport, with heavy traffic, and 10 kilometers from Mulhouse-Habsheim recreational airfield. The GEIPAN did not process the case quickly enough to determine which airplane might have been involved in the observation. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": probable observation of an airplane preparing to land. Southwest to west wind recorded in Mulhouse: see Météociel.
