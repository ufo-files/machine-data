Summary: New observation during a commercial flight of bright points moving in the sky: observation of brief sun glints from certain satellites of the Starlink® constellation at their assigned and operational orbits according to a very specific configuration.
--- seg-b4ef1175cfc3c41a691e2ebe [needs-review] ---
Description: The witness reports that during their flight on November 18, 2025, they again observe, as they did the previous day (see the [AERO] case of AMSTERDAM (NL.1118) to NANTES (44) on November 17, 2025), up to 9 or 10 mobile and varying intensity points in the sky. Only one testimony is collected. Of low strangeness and very good consistency* (at least 4 witnesses, PAN videos), this case turns out to be an observation of Starlink® satellite flares. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The description of the PAN is entirely consistent with this type of observation: straight and short paths always in the same area of the sky, strong light variations, numerous occurrences. The PAN's position in the sky corresponds to the vertical of the Sun, which is setting below the horizon at -40°. It is in every respect consistent with the analysis of the phenomenon published under this news item GEIPAN a new phenomenon explained: the illumination of Starlink® satellites by the Sun: "satellite flaring". The case is classified "A", observation of Starlink® satellite flares.
