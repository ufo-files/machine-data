Summary: Observation of OVNI in the Polynesian sky: probable observations of an atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-9d0e78bde96686f1e4687c32 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 26, 1983, at approximately 6:50 PM, a large number of witnesses scattered across the entire territory of French Polynesia were able to observe a particular luminous phenomenon. A group of 6 to 8 points of varying sizes and extremely bright moved at extremely high speed from west to east, leaving behind them glowing trails. The altitude of this group was estimated at 10,000 meters. No noise associated with the movement of this group was perceived during the observations. These witnesses likely witnessed an atmospheric re-entry.
