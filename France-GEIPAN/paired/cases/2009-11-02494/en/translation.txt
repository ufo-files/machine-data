Summary: Observation of movements of several bright white points: lack of information.
--- seg-604d99044ee0923e3e6fa1df [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 30, 2009, between 8:30 a.m. and 10:00 a.m., three witnesses observed in a clear and open sky three white points moving. The GEIPAN only received one testimony reporting a very long, discontinuous observation of the movements of several bright points in the sky. The observation reported a month later to the GEIPAN and the witness's request for a police report (PV) not followed up did not allow for an investigation. The characteristics of the observation (very long = 1 hour and 30 minutes, slow movements) could correspond to balloons, gulls (present in the witness's field of vision) as suggested by the witness, or any other imaginable hypothesis, without these plausible hypotheses being verifiable. The level of strangeness is low and the testimony unique. This case is classified in category C for lack of information.
