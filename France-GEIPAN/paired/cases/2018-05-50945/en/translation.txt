Description: The witness is driving home to Toulon (83) on May 29, 2018 at 8:48 PM when they observe in the sky a PAN with a spherical shape and black color, moving slowly along a horizontal linear trajectory at low altitude and low speed. The witness observes the PAN until reaching their home, where they record a video. The PAN flies over residences before disappearing in the distance after approximately 6 minutes of observation. The witness's testimony through the Technical Questionnaire (QT) of GEIPAN is received in May 2020. With low strangeness and good consistency (single witness, fairly precise testimony, and video of PAN), the case turns out to be the observation of a balloon (see the investigation report). The characteristics provided by the witness and the visual aspect of the PAN on the video strongly suggest a balloon, with a spherical or elongated shape. The dark color suggests a festive balloon rather than a weather balloon, which is usually white. Since the PAN is below the cloud layer, its altitude cannot exceed a few hundred meters, which is consistent with the explanatory hypothesis. Additionally, the trajectory of the PAN is perfectly consistent with that of a lightly blowing wind, explaining its slow movement. Therefore, the PAN exhibits all characteristics of an object carried by the wind. The case is classified as "A," observation of a festive balloon.
