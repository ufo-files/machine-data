Summary: Multiple observations of several PAN moving silently and with flashing lights: probable aircraft observations.
--- seg-b005c5e942616a64b534c975 [needs-review] ---
Description: During the night of July 17 to 18, 1990, numerous witnesses, including a patrol of gendarmes, observed up to 13 objects moving silently with flashing lights. One witness mentioned a kite-like shape. It is very likely a case of confusion with aircraft. This observation should be considered in conjunction with the departure of the Young Pilots' Air Tour de France on the same day.
