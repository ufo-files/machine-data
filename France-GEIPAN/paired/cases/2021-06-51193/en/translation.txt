Description: On June 13, 2021, between 11:35 PM and 11:40 PM, the witness, in their garden in Carcassonne, observed, in the darkness, the moon through their telescope. He then notices a glowing point moving in a linear manner, with a slightly curved trajectory. The PAN emits a steady light, first red, then white. The witness states: "It emitted lights, in the manner of a lighthouse: soft (no flickering), bright and progressive." "At first red towards the west, then white, almost blue... Maybe the spots were square or rectangular." Only one testimony will be collected. The radar map transmitted by the National Center for Air Operations allows the identification of three aircraft following the trajectory mentioned by the witness at the indicated time. These three aircraft are located between 25 and 50 km from the witness. The description and behavior of the PAN correspond to the observation of navigation lights of an aircraft (see the investigation report). The investigation concludes that the witness observed the closest aircraft to him. It is a CESSNA 525. The perceived strangeness by the witness is due to two elements: - The change in colors emitted: red to the North and white, almost blue to the South. These lights correspond to the aircraft's navigation lights. The witness first perceived the red light (lateral signaling) as the aircraft approached and then the white light (rear signaling) as the aircraft moved away. - "Perhaps the lights were square or rectangular": The perception of the fixed red light at the end of the left wing (facing the witness) could be affected by the four aligned rectangular windows located on the aircraft's side. The witness does not seem certain when he states "maybe." This point is difficult to explain but does not present a strong strangeness. The UFO protected GEIPAN classifies this case as A: observation of an aircraft, a CESSNA 525.
