Description: On October 20, 2010, the GEIPAN receives a testimony from an individual regarding an old observation made in late October 1996 at 21:00 (the witness does not specify the exact date, so October 30th is given arbitrarily). At the age of 11, the witness, accompanied by his mother and younger brother, was initially intrigued by the sight of three stationary lights above the trees in a square in Angers. The witness then describes the phenomenon as a triangle with a black circle in the center that moves and disappears at a blinding speed. No noise was heard during the 20-second observation. The second witness (the mother) remembers an observation made in the fall of 1996 around 20:00 of an unusual light and a moving craft that she does not recognize. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon. The description of this case is too imprecise (incomplete date) and too old for the GEIPAN to investigate, let alone make a determination, as it is classified "C" due to a lack of information.
