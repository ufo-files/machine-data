Description: On Sunday, June 12, 2011, at approximately 1:26 a.m., two witnesses were surprised to see a brightly glowing, yellow-red ball rapidly traverse the sky. No unusual noises were heard during the object's passage on a straight trajectory. The observation lasted about three minutes. Of low strangeness but good consistency despite the absence of elevation data (likely due to confusion regarding the question "height in the sky"), this observation case exhibits all characteristics of a misidentification due to a Thai lantern. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
