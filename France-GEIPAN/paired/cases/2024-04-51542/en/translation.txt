Summary: Observation of very rapid movements in the sky of a black object: observation of a fighter plane in a phase of rapid ascent for military control.
--- seg-43edfa5f500f5743e83d4dac [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Thursday, April 25, while driving on the A432 highway back from L'Isle-d'Abeau to Paris at approximately 110 km/h, a driver observed an unusual phenomenon near Lyon-Saint-Exupéry Airport. He first discerns an object of deep black that he initially associates with a jet aircraft. This device moves rapidly from right to left, about one kilometer ahead of its vehicle, and at an estimated altitude of 200 meters. Less than five seconds later, after initiating a slight left turn, the object begins a spectacular ascent, quickly gaining altitude. The strangeness felt by the witness mainly resides in the speed of the object's movement and the rapidity of its ascent, all of this without any perceived noise. The case consistency is judged average according to GEIPAN criteria with one witness, a complete testimony, no photo of PAN. The hypothesis of observing a fighter plane is supported by the radar trace report provided by the Air and Space Army (CAPCODA), whose data corresponds to the trajectory described at the time of the event (see the attached CAPCODA and Earthnull documents). This aircraft was flying in visual (uncontrolled) flight (IFF code 0300) before initiating a turn to military control (IFF code 3677), which corresponds in every way to the rapid ascent reported by the witness. The lack of noise perception could be due to the combination of the environment resulting from driving at 110km/h and the light wind that was not carrying any load on that day. The absence of a sonic boom perception can mean that the aircraft had already reached that speed or was about to do so after climbing in altitude. The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'A': observation of a fighter plane in a rapid ascent phase for military control. *CONSISTENCY: According to the GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.
