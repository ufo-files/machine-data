--- seg-3a67a674acc1889b70a4482b [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 12, 2009, around 11:10 PM, a witness in their garden observed the south-to-north passage of two oval-shaped objects in succession. Orange in color, they were located quite high in the sky and moved in a straight trajectory. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted 30 seconds. The sole witness, who spontaneously testified at the gendarmerie, seemed to be familiar with the visual characteristics of meteors they intended to observe, as well as those of aircraft from traffic near Lyon-St Exupéry Airport. These two hypotheses can be ruled out. The characteristics of the 30-second observation described by the witness (two not too bright oval lights of pale orange color following each other, not flying very high, following a straight trajectory, one of the lights oscillating, and no noise) lead to considering the hypothesis of Thai-style glowing lanterns. The absence of steady wind is not in contradiction with a probable release of lanterns from the south of the observation location (stadium area of the commune). The sole testimony gives little consistency to this observation. The level of strangeness is low, and a high proportion of the described characteristics correspond to those of a flight of glowing lanterns. This case is classified as B, indicating a probable observation of glowing lanterns.
