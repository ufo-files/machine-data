--- seg-16bcac4f71a3f1c080505bcc [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A person took photographs of a rainbow and noticed, the following day, three gray spherical spots on a photo that they could not explain. Without direct observation during the photography, it is very difficult to explain the nature of the spots that appear in the photos. The small black dots cannot be explained by a flying insect near the lens: the spherical shape and the season (winter) do not match. The luminance of the main "object" is comparable to that of an electric wire, which indicates that the distance between the object and the lens was probably of the order of the distance between electric wires and the lens of the camera. The explanation that comes to mind is the presence of balloons: small helium-filled balloons that may have moved or left the clouds before the next photo. Due to the absence of direct observation, no research was conducted on the origin of these balloons.
