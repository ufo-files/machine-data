Summary: Observations of the silent, straight passage of a dozen orange objects forming a glowing triangle in the sky: observations of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-abc1204da32dd9d958bc80be [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 18, 2013, around 2 a.m., three witnesses were intrigued by the straight and silent passage of a luminous phenomenon across the sky. Up to 11 orange-yellow disks were counted forming a moving triangular shape. The phenomenon moved away and disappeared into the landscape. We are certainly dealing with a lantern observation, all characteristics are present: color, absence of noise, characteristic formation of releases in clusters. Furthermore, the hall where a wedding was held is approximately 400 meters from the observation location, so the lanterns were likely still at a low altitude and very close to each other when the witnesses saw them, reinforcing the impression of a single structure. The near-total absence of wind allowed these lanterns to move slowly above the witnesses in a straight line. Additionally, confirmations from the municipality, and then from the person who rented the wedding hall, leave no doubt about the release of lanterns at the observation time. This case is classified "A": certain observations of Thai lanterns.
