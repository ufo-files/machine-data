--- seg-dce420f7f46d0359414e9fa4 [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reported on November 15, 2011, an observation made on April 10, 2010 at 6:23 a.m. of a bright white/yellow sphere passing over a neighboring residence. After 5 seconds, the witness noticed the sphere moving slowly and straight towards another residence before quickly disappearing while rising silently into the sky. The witness's description resembles a plane seen head-on with its runway lights on, but the observed object was silent, and the witness, who is accustomed to watching the sky at this time of day, did not recognize it as an airplane. This case was initially classified as C due to a lack of information. Following a report from an internet user who proposed the hypothesis of an ISS pass, the GEIPAN confirmed this hypothesis: the international space station (ISS) was passing exactly along that trajectory at that time (see the investigation elements, Calsky screen copy). The file is now classified as "A": observation of the ISS.
