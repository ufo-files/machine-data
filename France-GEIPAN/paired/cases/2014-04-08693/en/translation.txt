Summary: Observation of the silent movement of fluorescent green light points in the night sky: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-657187f09b9cf672c29afc5d [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 8, 2014, between 10:00 PM and 11:30 PM, a witness observed through a partially open glass panel the silent, straight movement of a set of bright green fluorescent lights connected by lines and extending over approximately 30 feet. The phenomenon was eventually obscured by nearby buildings. Only one testimony was collected. The witness felt as if objects were being carried by the wind ("the impression of 'floating' in the air."). The wind measured in Avignon was blowing from North to South, which is consistent with a perceived movement mainly from North to South as described (North-Northwest to Southeast). It is likely that these were green Thai lanterns (lanterns can vary in color and shape (see the Thai lanterns site), and may be attached to each other (giving the appearance of lines connecting the glowing points). Generally, such festive events occur on weekends, but they can also happen on weekdays (for example, a birthday party). Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B: Thai lanterns.
