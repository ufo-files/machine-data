Description: On the evening of August 14, 2016, a vacationer at the edge of a beach in the Gorges de la Cèze (30) gazes at the starry sky. He is surprised by the very quick passage of a bright point "like a shooting star." The trajectory of the PAN is straight, then a zigzag is observed by the witness before the PAN disappears, hidden by a rock. Only one testimony was collected. Of moderate strangeness and good consistency (single witness, no photo, but quite precise testimony), this case appears to be a possible misidentification with a meteor (see the investigation report). The description of the PAN strongly resembles a meteor, although the zigzags observed at the end of the PAN's trajectory are rather rare for this type of misidentification. However, the witness himself validated this hypothesis after conducting research. Meteor observation reporting sites did not confirm it in a certain manner. The case is classified B, probable misidentification with a meteor.
