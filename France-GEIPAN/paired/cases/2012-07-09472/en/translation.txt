--- seg-ae86e5486ad38ce52fd2053f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Recent report (05/12/2014) of an old observation. On July 28, 2012 at 23:00, a witness observed from his window a glowing point of colors (red/yellow/green) that intrigued him. This point was stationary. In photographs taken after a few seconds of observation, he noticed the presence of 3 points forming a triangle. The object disappeared, then 4 points reappeared. The observation lasted only a few minutes in the clear sky. No other testimony was collected. The witness observed and photographed a point of light twice at short intervals. In the direction indicated by the witness, Arcturus, Saturn, and Mars are visible. By carefully examining photograph #2 and comparing it to the environment of the star Arcturus, there is no doubt that this star was photographed (see the investigation report). The appearance and disappearance of the star can be explained by the presence of clouds that evening. The three visible points (instead of a single light for Arcturus) on photograph #1 can be explained by the instability of the handheld device used. The color variation can be explained by atmospheric turbulence. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Observation of the star Arcturus.
