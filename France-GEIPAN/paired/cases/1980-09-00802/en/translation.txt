Description: On September 14, 1980, at 12:45, an air traffic controller on duty observed a peculiar phenomenon. Directly vertical to him, a black, cylindrical object was stationary. The object, located approximately 300 meters above the ground, then suddenly accelerated and disappeared heading East. No information was gathered to allow for a serious study of this observation.
