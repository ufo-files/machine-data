--- seg-ffb08eceba084d41414198d7 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Two witnesses observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky heading west-northwest on several consecutive nights. This fixed light slowly sets. They took photographs. This observation case is strange not because of the observed phenomenon, but due to the interpretation the witnesses made of it, given the line shape seen by the witnesses and the photo taken from the bottom of the building. Indeed, the object observed in the sky has all the characteristics of the planet Venus. The photograph taken from the ground is indeed strange at first glance; but the witness photographed a streetlamp, not Venus: once down on the ground floor, Venus was hidden by the wooded hill, and only the public lighting light penetrates through the foliage. This case is classified A: repeated observations of the planet Venus, and photographic confusion with a streetlamp through foliage.
