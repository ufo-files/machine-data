Summary: Observation of a bright ball splitting into two: probable atmospheric reentry of space debris.
--- seg-a167d4cf1fc341fbeb48a5e3 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Three people observed on July 11, 2012 at 9:30 PM (21h30) the slow, east-to-west (O-E) passage of a bright, yellow ball that split in half before disappearing. No sound was heard during the passage. After ruling out the possibility of Thai lanterns (inconsistent eastward movement with northwest wind, splitting into two objects), the hypothesis of an atmospheric reentry of a space debris is favored: the witness's description, fragmentation, duration, and eastward movement are consistent. The Calsky atmospheric reentry catalog lists two reentries on that day, but they do not appear to match this event (nearly polar orbits). The BOAM (meteor observers) catalog makes no mention at that time. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of atmospheric reentry of space debris.
