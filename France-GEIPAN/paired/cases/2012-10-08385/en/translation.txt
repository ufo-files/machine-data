--- seg-def8b9b74e2d3bf55aeaf41f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Tuesday, October 9, 2012, around 10:40 PM, a witness observed from their garden two bright yellow points in the sky. These points appeared stationary before quickly moving in a southwest direction. No unusual noises were heard during the three-minute observation. The witness lost sight of the glowing points. Following an on-site investigation with the witness and inquiries with military authorities, it was found that the observation perfectly coincides with major military maneuvers. However, the authorities did not specify the type of aircraft involved (helicopter, plane, lighting balloon). Furthermore, we do not have another observation of the same phenomenon to confirm that it occurred over an airbase. In conclusion, we have a serious and supported hypothesis, but no formal proof. The GEIPAN classifies this case as Category B, very probable observation of two aircraft during military maneuvers.
