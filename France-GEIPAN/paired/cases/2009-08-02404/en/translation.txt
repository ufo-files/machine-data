Description: On August 16, 2009, around 10:30 PM, two walkers observed in the sky for 4 minutes two particularly bright stationary objects. These objects then moved silently as their light intensity decreased. The objects eventually disappeared, hidden by fog. The description provided by one of the witnesses and the photographs taken are explicit regarding a probable observation of celestial lanterns.
