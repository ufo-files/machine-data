Description: The witness, a pilot of a CESSNA150, flying to the south-east of Metz (flight from Colmar to Liège), observed a small, oval-shaped object of yellow color moving at high speed to the northwest at the same altitude, from north to south. The duration of the observation is not specified. As the distance to an unknown object cannot be estimated, the altitude estimated by the observer is uncertain: it could be a very bright and very distant object, at a higher altitude: this would then be an atmospheric re-entry of a meteoroid (meteorite) or space debris. The space debris re-entry catalogs (Space-Track) do not mention any re-entry on that day. As the observation took place during the day, we can also consider a reflection of the sun on another aircraft. Without information to strengthen one of these hypotheses, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C".
