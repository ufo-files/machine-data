Description: On the night of August 5, 2009, around 2 a.m., two individuals observed from their home a particularly bright circular object. While filming the phenomenon, the main witness noticed a very slow horizontal movement of the object. The astronomical configuration and a call for witnesses confirm that the observation was made on a full moon night. The "phenomenon" was to the left of the full moon. The astronomical configuration indicates a very probable observation of the planet Jupiter, which is particularly visible during this time of year (apparent magnitude -2.8 with its maximum angular diameter of 48 arc seconds).
