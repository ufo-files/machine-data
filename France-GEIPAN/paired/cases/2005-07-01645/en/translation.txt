Summary: Observation of the passage of a boomerang-shaped object.
--- seg-8010d781368dc417dfab92b6 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of June 27 to 28, 2005, around 4:15 a.m., a witness observed the relatively slow passage of a dark shape resembling a boomerang with no visible lights or apparent illumination and completely silent. The observation lasted approximately 8 to 10 seconds. About 5 to 10 minutes after this observation, the witness specified that fog banks and a fairly strong breeze appeared. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. Although a confusion with an aircraft is not to be excluded, no other hypothesis could be validated due to a lack of information.
