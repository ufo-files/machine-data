Description: On October 18, 2014, around 10:25 PM, a witness (T1) and his wife (T2) were surprised to see a silent, elongated, orange object pass overhead in less than 4 seconds on a straight trajectory. The object obscured the stars that the witnesses were looking at. One single questionnaire is collected (T1). The profile drawn by the witness is exactly the profile of an orange paraglider wing, so this is the first hypothesis considered. An electric paramotor (the only one that allows for instant reserve in case of trouble, engine stopped), helps explain the problem of launching a paraglider, flying distances greater than 1km, and the silent passage (the engine can be restarted just before landing). However, landing at night without the moon, without any lighting, without releasing the throttle in final, in a field that is almost always (potentially) filled with horses (animals often terrified by flying wings), is considered simply suicidal by experienced paragliders. Even though this scenario describes an extremely dangerous, even suicidal situation, no solid argument formally eliminates it, and many arguments support it. We cannot speak of certainty because it is very surprising in this scenario that neither the witnesses nor the horses saw or heard the paraglider land, perhaps less than 100/200 meters away. The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'B': probable observation of the landing of a paraglider, possibly motorized.
