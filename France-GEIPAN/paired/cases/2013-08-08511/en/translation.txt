Summary: Two observations of the passage of a dark, rectangular shape with red and white lights: very likely observation of a military cargo plane.
--- seg-6ca9d14e0e5b88b6e0e9d78f [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 8, 2013, around 11:10-15 PM, two individuals in an apartment heard a fairly intense and constant noise and observed the low-altitude passage of a dark, rectangular object they did not recognize. Red and white lights were seen. Given the collected elements, namely the lights, distance, and size compatible with a military cargo plane, the typical noise of such an aircraft, the gray color, the indefinable shape against the night sky, the corresponding flight path, and a plane having already used it, we can conclude that the PAN observed by the witnesses is very likely a military cargo plane, an A400M, approaching the Vélizy-Villacoublay airport (see the investigation report). This case is of very good consistency: two witnesses made two statements to the Gendarmerie and the GEIPAN, each with detailed and illustrated sketches; however, no photographic or video documents exist. The observation is ultimately not strange, as it involves a ultimately mundane object, but observed under unusual conditions, as it is not frequent. This case should be classified as "B," indicating a very probable observation of a military cargo plane.
