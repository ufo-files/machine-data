Summary: Probable observation of an atmospheric reentry
--- seg-3eca6b4fc673d377c79e2a51 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 27, 1997, around 4:45 a.m., several witnesses across the Lorraine departments observed the very quick passage of a green ball crossing the sky with a yellow-orange trail. The silent phenomenon lasted 2 to 3 seconds. The characteristics of these testimonies are consistent with the probable observation of an atmospheric reentry.
