Summary: Possible observations of Thai lantern flights.
--- seg-c5285e2e79eb1ed91bae41d1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 6, 2011, around 10:15 PM, two witnesses observed in the sky the straight passage of several glowing objects of red-orange color appearing in intervals. No noise was heard during the observation, which was filmed. Photographs were also taken. The description of the phenomenon suggests a passage of Thai lanterns. The videos sent by the witness show the passage of a red-orange object very similar to other videos reported to the GEIPAN and clearly showing a passage of Chinese lanterns. This possibility was not ruled out by the witness himself (letter dated March 25, 2011), and after examining all the elements, the hypothesis retained by the GEIPAN is that of a probable observation of the passage of Thai lanterns.
