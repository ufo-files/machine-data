--- seg-1ae4214f48687b28a8bb419f [needs-review] ---
Description: On Sunday, February 21, 2021, around 12:30 local time, while driving to the Robertsau district in Strasbourg, the witness observed a bright white point in the sky to the northeast, sometimes stationary and sometimes moving slowly. They initially thought it was an aircraft lighthouse but the presence of other nearby points led them to doubt this. They continued observing as their vehicle approached the Phario Bridge. Upon reaching their destination, they noticed these bright white points again, this time to the southwest, appearing distant. They recorded a short video using their smartphone at 12:39. The video shows two faint bright points in the sky. The witness did not witness any further movements of these PAN and returned home. Extremely surprised, they decided to contact GEIPAN and complete a questionnaire. Only one testimony was collected. This case is well-consistently reported: one witness with a video that could be analyzed. The investigation revealed that the witness likely observed some festive balloons. This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- Appearance: point
- Color: white
- Consistent wind data movements: stationary, minor movements, appearance and disappearance (effects of rotation due to gusts).

The witness's perceived strangeness is primarily due to their initial belief that they were observing probe balloons and being informed that these balloons are launched individually, making this hypothesis unacceptable. They did not consider any other type of balloon.

GEIPAN classification: A: Observation of some festive balloons.
