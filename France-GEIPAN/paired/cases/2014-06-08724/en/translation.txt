Description: On Sunday, June 8, 2014, between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM, a witness observes and photographs a gigantic gray-blue circle around the sun. This observation case is typical of a halo phenomenon, which occurs under particular atmospheric conditions. See: Solar Halo. The photo also shows a small bright point in the top left corner, inside the halo, which is a parasitic reflection of the sun caused by the camera's optics. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A: Solar Halo.
