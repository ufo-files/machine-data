Description: On February 3, 2019, in the late morning, a witness walking through a field observed a gray mass resembling a small cloud under the cloud cover. This "small cloud" moved very quickly despite the light wind. What caught the witness's attention was that suddenly the PAN changed altitude until it disappeared into the clouds, then performed maneuvers before disappearing on a descending trajectory. To the naked eye, this left a trace resembling distant falling rain for about 3 seconds before disappearing. The witness had time to film with their smartphone. Only one testimony was collected. The observation presents several apparent oddities: final shape change, non-linear trajectory, entering the clouds, plunge towards the ground, silence. However, the shape of the PAN, its dark appearance, its trajectory, and its final plunge towards the ground strongly suggest the explanatory hypothesis of a flock of birds in flight, which confirms all aspects of the observation (see the investigation report). The brief passage of the PAN through the clouds is not exceptional, given that the low cloud ceiling was only about 500 meters above the ground. It is therefore very likely that a flock of birds (particularly starlings) passed just under the clouds, before plunging towards the ground upon seeing a source of food or rest. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A," indicating a highly probable observation of a flock of birds in flight.
