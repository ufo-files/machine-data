Description: On July 7, 2005 at 14:14 (UTC), the captain of flight AF 7705, approaching Charles-De-Gaulle Airport, reported crossing with an object of gray color, seemingly stationary and shaped like an hourglass, according to the pilot's drawing. The object, which appears to be metallic, does not resemble a weather balloon and did not seem to be falling freely. This encounter (reported as an Airprox) between a commercial aircraft and a flying object is unusual. The observed shape does not correspond to a balloon, whether scientific, recreational, or festive. No radar echo was reported. Local ground analysis by aviation authorities did not provide an explanation. It is not specified if other pilots observed this object. The hypothesis of a balloon escaped from EuroDisney does not seem to match the shape or color: balloons are almost always convex in shape and one would imagine EuroDisney balloons to be bright in color. As it is no longer possible to advance in this investigation (searching for other observations, inquiry at EuroDisney), this case is classified as 'C' due to lack of information.
