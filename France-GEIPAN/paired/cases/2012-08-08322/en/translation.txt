Summary: Observations of several waves of glowing objects in the sky moving silently: probable observations of Thai lantern flights.
--- seg-c20536a19a55bc7bc0165fb0 [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reports an observation made by several people on August 26, 2012 at 9:30 PM: 12 objects silently move towards the West. These objects appear in waves of several objects. They are described as having an oval shape with a red border, an orange interior, and three well-visible yellow balls inside the objects. The witness submitted their testimony to the gendarmerie, which will conduct an investigation. Local press will also be contacted. The objects' color, their slow movement according to the light easterly wind (see map and weather report), and their appearance in series, are characteristics of floating lanterns releases. A thorough photographic analysis showed that the light emitted by the objects was very similar to that emitted by Thai lanterns (see photographic analysis). However, these objects are not circular or elliptical like regular lanterns, but composed of 3 yellow points in a red-orange envelope. Three Thai lanterns attached together would have the same appearance. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns, attached together by three.
