Summary: Observation of the passage of a bright point following a straight trajectory then with jerky movements: observation of the passage of the ISS with autokinetic illusion.
--- seg-2eb6c5f4d25b86b962c96f9a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness is intrigued by an observation made on August 22, 2012 at 10:19 PM. He observed the passage of a bright point that, after an initial straight trajectory, makes jerky movements from right to left before disappearing. The witness's description (appearance and direction of movement) corresponds, within a minute, to the observation of the passage of the International Space Station (ISS). The temporary extinction corresponds to variations in the sun's reflections on the station during its rotation. The jerky right-to-left movements are a perceptual illusion known as "autokinetic illusion" (see pages 26 and 30 in http://www.cnes-geipan.fr/typo3conf/ext/dam_frontend/pushfile.php?docID=9932; this illusion occurs when the bright point being followed is no longer surrounded by fixed references, for example, when it crosses a starless area of the sky. This case is classified as "A": observation of the International Space Station (ISS) with autokinetic illusion.
