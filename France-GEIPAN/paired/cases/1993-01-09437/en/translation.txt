Description: Recent (2015) testimony of an old (1993) observation. On January 6, 1993, at 7:10 PM, three people (one adult and two children) observed above the street lamps, the silent passage of a triangular-shaped craft. Dark in color, it was illuminated by a red light in the center and several white lights grouped at each end. It suddenly took off at very high speed and disappeared behind a mountain in the direction of Annecy. Only one testimony was reported. Given the elements analyzed in the attached investigation report, we can conclude that the lack of information and the testimonial data in chapter 3.0 (in particular the antiquity of the case) do not allow us to decide in favor of any hypothesis. This testimony is of low consistency: detailed but very old. Single eyewitness and no photo or video documents. This case is classified as "C" as an observation of an unidentified phenomenon at this stage, the significant lack of elements not allowing to establish a solid opinion.
