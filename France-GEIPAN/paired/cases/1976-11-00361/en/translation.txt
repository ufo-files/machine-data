Description: On November 20, 1976, around 7:30 PM, several people observed a round, glowing object in the sky that intrigued them. The object appeared to spin on its own axis and a flickering light was visible. It emitted bright, yellow-orange rays. The observation lasted about a good quarter of an hour before the object disappeared in a descending trajectory. No other more detailed testimony was collected on this phenomenon, for which we lack information.
