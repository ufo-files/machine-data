Summary: Observation of the progressive increase and decrease of a bright point in the night sky: observation of an iridium flare.
--- seg-0f25d4754e74f40a806fa55b [needs-review] ---
Description: On Saturday, August 29, 2015 at 21:26, a witness on their terrace was observing the stars and an airplane, and was surprised to see in the northwest a luminous phenomenon increase and then decrease in intensity until disappearing. Only one testimony was collected. The witness reported the following characteristics of PAN: - A stationary bright point 
- Observation duration of 10 seconds
- Brightness similar to that of a star

These data are typical of a flare or flash Iridium. Furthermore, the data regarding the time and direction of the observation (exact time noted as 21:26 and observation direction towards the northwest) align with those concerning the Iridium flare of the Iridium 54 object, which had a maximum magnitude of -6.6 (see the investigation note). 

Therefore, GEIPAN classifies the case as A: observation of an Iridium flare.
