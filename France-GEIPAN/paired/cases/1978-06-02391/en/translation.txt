Description: On June 7, 1978, at approximately 8:55 PM, a motorist and his passenger observed a distant (about 5 km) glowing point that intrigued them. At 9:15 PM, a glowing orange disk began its slow vertical ascent and disappeared into the clouds, leaving only a colored trail visible. Following the publication of this observation in the press, a witness reported seeing a yellow-orange circular light phenomenon in the sky on the same date, between 9:30 PM and 9:45 PM. These observations remain unexplained due to a lack of details.
