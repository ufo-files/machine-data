Summary: Observation of the rapid east-to-west movement of a green light in the sky of the Antilles: probable atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
--- seg-bff644e3ee1ff6d0e18ee8b1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: An air traffic controller at the Martinique Aimé Césaire International Airport control tower reported an observation made by several people on February 11, 2012, around 11:10 PM. A green glowing phenomenon moved quickly across the Caribbean sky from East to West. Controllers in Guadeloupe, ground gendarmes, and pilots on AFR 3570 (Paris-Martinique) also saw it. The phenomenon of a very bright green sphere crossing the sky in just a few seconds immediately suggests a meteoroid (meteorite) atmospheric reentry or space debris. Space debris enters the atmosphere at approximately 7 km/s and crosses the sky for 20 seconds to a few minutes. Given the observed appearance and duration of the phenomenon, the hypothesis of a meteoroid (meteorite) atmospheric reentry was chosen for this case, classified as "B."
