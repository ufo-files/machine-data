Description: On November 15, 2022, around 9:30 PM, in the village of Orphin, a witness, there to retrieve his vehicle, observed a PAN in the shape of a "Y," very bright and stationary. He pointed it out to his present father, who didn't pay attention. The witness then observed several other identical PANs, "more or less white" and stationary, less bright than the first. He also noticed a "very large and bright shape" moving in a straight line at high speed in the sky. The object resembled two Yagi antennas stuck together, and it flickered everywhere. He stated that it seemed like all objects were heading in the same direction. After retrieving his car, he attempted to record a video of the PANs but "there were no longer the large objects moving very quickly." No other testimonies were collected. The only source of information for this observation of PANs is a Gendarmerie protocol, as the witness did not respond to our request for further details. The consistency is low: only one witness reported his observation to the two people present, and many essential data are missing from the only available information source, the Gendarmerie protocol. The recorded video is of little use. It lasts only a few seconds, against a very dark sky, with a barely visible PAN, out of focus, and with significant camera shake during recording. Essential data for analyzing the case are missing, namely (see the investigation report): - Azimuths and elevation of the PANs - Number of PANs - Apparent size - Duration of observation. There also appear to be contradictory data, particularly regarding: - Shape: in the shape of a "Y" or bright points - Color: white, then described as red, orange, and green flickering lights. Therefore, this case is classified as C: lack of data for analysis.
