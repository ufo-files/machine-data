Description: On July 1, 2014, at 10:00 PM, a witness, awakened by a noise, observed through their window a triangular craft with three white lights at each end and a red light in the center. A humming sound is heard by the witness. The object appears to graze the roof of the neighboring house. The witness goes to get a pair of binoculars and a camera but the PAN disappears before the witness can take a picture of it. Only one testimony is collected. The witness's initial thought was that it was a drone. The humming perceived during the observation is compatible with this hypothesis, as well as the sensation of proximity and possibly the unregulated light signaling for civilian drones. It should be noted, however, that the overflight of cities by drones is prohibited, especially at night, so it is beyond doubt that no advertisement of such an overflight would have been made in this case. Moreover, a search on the Internet for the possible use of a drone in the area around Troyes at the date and time of the observation yielded nothing. Moreover, confusion with an airplane or helicopter seems difficult to imagine, as the lights do not correspond to the navigation lights of an aircraft. In order to proceed further with the analysis, it would have been necessary to have the following information, which was not provided in the questionnaire: - the height of the PAN at the time of the first sighting. - the angular size measurement of the PAN. - was the triangular shape perceived or deduced from the lights? - were the lights stationary or flashing? - photographs of the environment, during the day, and in particular from the witness's window position. A request for clarification for each of these points was emailed to the witness with no response. The hypothesis of a drone remains possible, but due to the missing information, the GEIPAN classifies the case as C: lack of reliable information.
