Summary: Probable observations of an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
--- seg-9703b9c0fde815c0904f1a75 [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 4, 2012, around 23:15, individuals located in Yvelines and Aube witnessed in the sky the silent movement of a luminous phenomenon with blue-green and orange colors. The trajectory was straight. The testimonies closely match the characteristics of an atmospheric reentry observation. The direction towards West-Southwest and the duration of only a few seconds rule out the hypothesis of a reentry of space debris. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid (meteorite fall).
