Summary: Observation of a luminous mass with a change in direction
--- seg-0e8df920e35c28fd71fdcde6 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 4, 1982, at approximately 2 a.m., witnesses observed a luminous mass stationary above a factory. They described the mass as some kind of triangle with red and gold lights whose intensity varied. The mass then moved slowly and silently before moving away towards the sea. No further information was gathered about this phenomenon.
