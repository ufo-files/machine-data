Description: On February 7, 2002, around 8:00 AM, several independent witnesses observed the very rapid fall of an orange fireball. The observation was very brief (1 to 2 seconds): the ball had a trail and quickly disappeared. Other witnesses in a neighboring department also reported observing this phenomenon, which is likely an atmospheric reentry.
