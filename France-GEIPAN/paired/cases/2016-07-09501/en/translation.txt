--- seg-d4321200eed3e3be2592169a [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 30, 2016, at 4:15 a.m., a witness closing their shutters observed a bright orange point approximately 20 to 25° above the horizon in the cloudy sky. Initially, the witness thought it might be a fire in the forest or an atmospheric reentry. The observation lasted 2 to 3 minutes before the phenomenon gradually disappeared, spreading out in length in the form of an inverted "L". Only one testimony was collected. The witness was surprised by the Moon, which occurs when the Moon is low and partially visible through the clouds, and the observed light shape was then that of the gap between the clouds, here an inverted "L". Reconstruction from the witness's photo drawing and the sky map leave no doubt. GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Misidentification with the Moon.
