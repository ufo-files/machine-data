Description: On November 19, 2017 at 20:35, two people, driving in a car (T1 driver) and (T2 front passenger), were intrigued by colored light shapes seen through the windshield. The witnesses are located on the D39 at the exit of Fouchy (67) heading towards Urbeis (67). The witnesses disagree on the description of the phenomenon. However, they agree to say that the phenomenon was silent and stationary. They are taking photographs with their respective smartphones. The two witnesses describe the PAN in very different ways, particularly T2 attributes his differing perception compared to T1 (See investigation notes). By observing the photos provided by the witnesses, it is also noted that the PAN observed by passenger T2 is clear, while that seen by driver T1 is blurry: this suggests the possibility of two distinct observations of two PAN. Two separate observation cases are open (see investigation notes). Passenger T2 describes the PAN as 4 bright points in a cross shape, with one blinking in the middle. This corresponds well to the observation of an airplane that he compares to a drone equipped with white LEDs at its ends. In fact, from a distance and through the windows of a car, it is possible to not distinguish the colors of the navigation lights. The photo clearly shows the double headlights of a Boeing 717 during its approach to land in Strasbourg. The converging path can give the impression of stillness or minimal movement. The two testimonies are consistent with each other in terms of their individual observations, and we have photos (perfectly usable) for both PAN as well as an aircraft trace for the PAN observed by T2. As a result, the GEIPAN concludes that the observation of T2 corresponds to the observation of a commercial airliner. The GEIPAN categorizes case A: commercial airliner. See the case [CD39] of FOUCHY (67) to URBEIS (67) on 19.11.2017 T1 to know what phenomenon was observed by T1 (driver) and what classification was given to the case.
