Summary: Long filmed observation of a spherical, bright, and stationary object, then realization of the presence of objects on the film without direct observation during the taking of the shot.
--- seg-673ca73ff79eed2a0214e8fb [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 9, 2011, at 6:50 AM, two individuals observed and filmed the presence of a bright, stationary spherical object vertical to an airport. No unusual noises were heard during the observation. Upon reviewing the footage, other objects were noticed, but there was no direct observation during the filming. Two phenomena are described in this testimony (see the investigation report): first, a bright metallic object with a spherical shape, whose description suggests a scientific balloon (not a Meteo-France balloon). And dark objects crossing the camera's field of vision at high speed: these are likely flying insects about one meter from the camera.
