Summary: Observation of a silent round/oval object with 3 powerful white searchlights
--- seg-4fcd00193f4554d1ae8e5c08 [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 27, 1990, a couple of young people driving a car observed an object shaped like a rugby ball with three powerful white lights. This object, approximately 2.5 to 3 meters in diameter, flew slowly at an extremely low altitude and in silence. After following the object for about ten minutes, it disappeared at a very high speed. The investigation failed to identify the object as a known aircraft or any other phenomenon.
