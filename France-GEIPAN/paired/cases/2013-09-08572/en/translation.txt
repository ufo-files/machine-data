Summary: Observation of a silver spherical object becoming white, bright, and elongated, apparently stationary, then duplicating and disappearing instantly: observation of a sun reflection on an airplane.
--- seg-5fa036cde4d05ea25fa67cf5 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Sunday, September 22, 2013, at 6:45 PM, several people in cars were intrigued by a sight in the sky they couldn't explain. The witness, in a car, described a silver spherical shape transforming into a bright white elongated shape, apparently stationary, then duplicating and disappearing. The radar plot (see diagram) clearly shows a high-altitude plane (black trace) as described by the witness, and also, at low altitude (thus without a trail), a plane moving away from the witness (red trace). The plane, moving away from the witness almost in the line of sight, appeared stationary. When the plane changed direction at 6:50 PM (see top right of the diagram, left turn from 6:48 PM to 51), the reflection changed and disappeared, suggesting the object itself had vanished. (Note: the times indicated along the plane traces are universal time). The witness's first intuition: a reflection of the sun on an airplane ultimately proved to be the correct explanation. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": perfectly explained by the reflection of the sun on an airplane.
