Summary: Observation of a luminous phenomenon and sensation of intense heat during stormy weather. Sudden disappearance. Probable observation of a globular lightning phenomenon.
--- seg-efd3896a24e62d6735f86f71 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 12, 1976, around 10:50 PM, a family observed a luminous phenomenon near their home. At the doorstep, one of the witnesses felt intense heat around their home and their attention was drawn to an elliptical shape of incandescent white with a glowing halo. This phenomenon, behind some trees, lasted about two minutes and descended towards the ground, disappearing suddenly. A few moments earlier, a disturbance had been noted on the television screen and an animal had been startled. The next day, the gendarmerie found no traces on the ground in the observation area. A neighbor testified hearing around 11 PM what sounded like a thunderclap and noticing a power outage for a few minutes but saw nothing of the phenomenon. This observation case was long classified C due to lack of information. It was recently submitted to an expert on lightning phenomena. The atmospheric conditions at the time of the observation, the description of the phenomenon's behavior, are consistent with the hypothesis of an atmospheric electrical manifestation called ball lightning or globular lightning. This case is classified B: probable observation of a globular lightning phenomenon.
