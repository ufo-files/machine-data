Description: On May 20, 1955, at 2:30 a.m., two gendarmes observed the southeast to northwest passage of a luminous phenomenon that intrigued them and disappeared in a few seconds. This phenomenon had the shape of a shell and was followed by a multicolored light trail. A gendarmerie report was filed. The GEIPAN classified this case as B: probable observation of a bolide, that is, the atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
