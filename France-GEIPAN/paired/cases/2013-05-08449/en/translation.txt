Summary: Observation of the movement of orange light points in the Paris sky: probable observation of the passage of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-2c43bee65f0e4017c3973a49 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the evening of May 7, 2013, witnesses observed multiple bright points flying in pairs across the sky. The objects were red/orange in color and left no trail. The observation describes 6 to 8 bright points moving back and forth on either side of the Montparnasse Tower in an approximate southwest to northeast direction (see map). Weather conditions in Paris Montsouris (very close to the witnesses), according to Meteociel, indicate a light southwest wind at 7 and 9 PM but a northeast and southeast wind at 8 PM and 10 PM. The weather at Orly is more stable and does not vary from southwest during this period. Witnesses do not provide a precise observation time, other than "apero-dinner." Assuming a southwest wind, the bright objects moved with the wind; their appearance and movement are entirely consistent with Thai lanterns. The release of Thai lanterns is common on festive nights, even in cities, although strongly discouraged in urban environments. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," a probable observation of Thai lanterns.
