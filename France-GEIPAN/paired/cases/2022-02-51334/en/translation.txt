Summary: Brief observation by a motorist of a PAN at very low altitude: observation of a military aircraft.
--- seg-af0ba229299832b2aae589d7 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 22, 2022, at noon, a driver was traveling on Departmental Road 16 between Magescq and Dax (40), with his daughter. On a long straight stretch, they observed from the left what the driver described as an airplane wing accompanied by a portion of its fuselage. The observer stated that the craft passed in front of them at a relatively slow speed. The phenomenon appeared masked by pines, crossed the road at their level, and disappeared again behind them, to the right. The driver initially thought it was a plane about to crash because it was flying so low.

Of low strangeness and fairly consistent (two witnesses, unique but fairly complete testimony, no photo of the PAN), this case turns out to be the observation of a military plane (as confirmed by the investigation report). The description of the phenomenon suggests a partially masked plane by trees, observed under unfavorable conditions (reduced duration of 5 to 10 seconds and restricted field of vision). The large apparent size of the craft suggests a close proximity to the observation location. A traffic air flow report from CAPCODA confirms the passage of a military plane in visual flight at low altitude (approximately 300 meters). The trajectory and timing perfectly match those of the PAN. It was also found that the craft was making a series of turns in the direction of observation of the witnesses. This perfectly explains the peculiar shape perceived, with a vertical wing overhanging the fuselage. Finally, the angular height of this plane corresponds in every way to that recorded in the testimony. The case is classified as "A", observation of a military plane.

*CONSISTENCY: According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.

*CAPCODA: Air Center for Planning and Conducting Operations and Air Defense (Air and Space Force).
