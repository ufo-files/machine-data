Description: On October 16, 1994, around 10:30 PM, a witness spotted a bright yellow light approximately 200 meters away. Then, a geometric shape (hexagonal or octagonal) red-orange rose above the woods from which the light originated. No noise was heard during this observation. No other testimony was collected. No particular tracks were observed in the observation area indicated by the witness. No other information could be gathered about this phenomenon.
