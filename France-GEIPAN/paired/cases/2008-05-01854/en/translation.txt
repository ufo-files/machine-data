Summary: Probable observation of Thai lanterns
--- seg-7b88794adfc97278b35cb3b9 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Sunday, May 4, 2008, between 10:45 PM and 10:50 PM, two individuals observed for fifteen seconds from their terrace the movement of three sources of yellow-orange light. No noise was heard. These lights moved slowly from west to north before disappearing suddenly. This observation follows numerous others during the same period in the western region. Many of these observations are explained by releases of Thai lanterns. The witnesses' description, the behavior of the light points, their yellow-orange color, and the observation day (a Sunday evening) suggest we are also dealing with the flight of three Thai lanterns, likely released during a family gathering.
