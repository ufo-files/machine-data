Summary: Observation of movements in the sky of a dark, elongated object that occasionally reflects sunlight: very likely a paraglider's wing.
--- seg-c598a6f227b25799a1d7c02e [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 22, 2016, in broad daylight, a technician on an outdoor mission in Plouha (22) observed in the sky, heading north, a dark object that occasionally reflected the sunlight. The shape of the object made him think of a paraglider's sail, but nothing hung beneath it. The witness tracked the object's movements in the sky and decided to take a photo and follow it in his car. The object moved slowly towards the northeast, making small rotations. The witness then lost sight of it. No other testimonies were reported. The witness provided the investigator with the photo taken during the observation: unfortunately, this photo does not show the PAN (too far away), but it confirms the observation time (3:28 PM). The observation was somewhat unusual, but it still prompted the witness to testify to understand what he had seen. The witness is reliable. The investigation shows that it is very likely a paraglider from the PLOUEZAILES club, as indicated by its president. The case is consistent (0.7) and not very strange (0.25). It is classified as A: probable paraglider in flight.
