Description: On December 15, 1979, around 2 a.m., a witness observed from the third floor of his apartment an orange, phosphorescent object in the sky. It had the shape of a cigar in vertical position and moved silently from north to south. The object disappeared into the urban landscape. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
