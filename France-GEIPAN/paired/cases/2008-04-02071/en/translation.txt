Summary: Observation of the movement of a bright white point in the sky with an apparent irregular trajectory: observation of the passage of the International Space Station.
--- seg-eb162d5e6b6254595a3e9207 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 4, 2008, at 10:28 PM, a motorist on the rest area of Mas d'Agenais was intrigued by the peculiar movement of a white object in the starry sky. The object moved according to an irregular trajectory. The observation lasted approximately 50 seconds before the object gradually disappeared. Given the objective elements collected, namely the presence in the sky of the International Space Station at the exact time of the observation, then the witness's position and the strong resemblance of the object to a satellite, according to the witness himself, we can conclude that the object observed by the witness is very likely the International Space Station, and that the "stop" impression near commercial airplanes mentioned in the testimony is a natural physiological eye phenomenon (autostasis/micronystagmus). This case is to be classified as "A".
