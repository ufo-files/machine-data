Description: On February 13, 1979, around 9:45 PM, a witness with his children observed a large white sphere moving across the sky at a low altitude in an east-west direction. This round, very bright object had numerous lights flashing from within. Three lights (yellow, green, and red) blinked beneath the object. Despite observing it for two to three minutes, the witness could not identify the object. During the investigation, other testimonies regarding similar observations were gathered: since December 1978, a white sphere moving in the same direction has been sighted. Witnesses also reported regular power drops in the area. The origin remained undetermined. Another witness stated that on December 4, 1978, around 6:00 PM, she saw an aircraft with a trapezoidal and rectangular shape at its base. This object hovered at a low altitude and was illuminated by red lights. The noise it made resembled that of a helicopter. The hypothesis of a tourist plane flying in the valley was considered, but the air traffic control center did not detect any craft on their surveillance and control radars at the specified time. The presence of airports in the region and the repetitiveness of the observations suggest that some of them correspond to aircraft. However, the available information does not confirm this hypothesis.
