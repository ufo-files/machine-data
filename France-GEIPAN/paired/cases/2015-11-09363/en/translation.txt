Summary: Observation of the passage of a fragmented red light phenomenon over the Pyrenees: observation of the atmospheric reentry of a Centaur stage.
--- seg-5f930cf1011b514fa96000e0 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 3, 2015 at 7:06 a.m., a driver near Blajan (31) observed in the sky the O-E passage of a phenomenon composed of several red-tinted light fragments. The phenomenon split into two, then four or five pieces, and quickly faded out over the Pyrenees. No unusual sounds were heard. This bolide (very fast glowing ball) observed for 15 to 20 seconds, during which it fragmented into 4 or 5 pieces, clearly exhibits characteristics of a large space debris re-entry. Many witnesses also saw it in Spain. Another driver on the A64 highway near Montréjeau (31) also filled out the questionnaire to provide testimony. However, this space debris does not appear on the list of objects published by the American service SpaceTrack. A remarkable investigation was conducted by Ted Molczan, who identified this large debris as the third stage of the Centaur launcher that was used to launch an American military satellite in 2002: see his investigation: Re-entry Sightings and Debris Recovery of 2008-010B Spain-2015 Nevember 03 UTC. Spherical fuel tanks survived the re-entry and were found in Spain: see Three mysterious metallic balls from the sky crashed in Spain. This observation case is remarkable because it combines eye witness accounts and debris found on the ground. The GEIPAN classifies these observations as perfectly identified: Centaur stage re-entry.
