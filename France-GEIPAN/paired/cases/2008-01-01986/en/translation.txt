Description: On January 24, 2008, at 1:15 a.m., a witness observed at high altitude a white light that drew two circles in the clear sky before disappearing. During the observation, a loud rumbling noise was heard. The witness's description clearly suggests an airplane. However, the movement of the light point is surprising. The witness was, however, on the edge of a military air training zone (see attached map, taken from the "Very Low Altitude Defense Network Map" RTBA): a military airplane could very well exhibit this behavior during a maneuver. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of a military airplane in maneuver.
