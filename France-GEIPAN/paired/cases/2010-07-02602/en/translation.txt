Description: On the night of July 4 to 5, 2010, around 12:15 a.m., a witness observed from the window of their room a straight-line east-to-west movement of a very bright light. The object disappeared, masked by the roof. Although the International Space Station (ISS) was only visible from the observation location starting at 12:23 a.m. (legally), we consider an error margin of 7 minutes acceptable compared to the time provided by the witness, who himself indicates "around 12:15 a.m." This case is classified as B, very probable observation of the International Space Station.
