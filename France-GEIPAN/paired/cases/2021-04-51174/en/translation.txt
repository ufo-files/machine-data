Description: On Wednesday, April 7, 2021, between 8:35 PM and 8:40 PM, a witness, accompanied by his wife, observed a luminous phenomenon moving across the sky without making any noise. A detailed analysis, transcribed in the attached investigation report, reveals that the witnesses observed a stage of SpaceX's Falcon 9 rocket, following the deployment of its Starlink satellite constellation, in the process of deorbiting before making a controlled reentry into the ocean. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, the consistency of this case is good (consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony): one testimony complemented by exploitable video. This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- The diffuse, point-like, white shape corresponds to what is generally observed during the ejection of residual gases from the rocket stage, illuminated by the sun at high altitude (approximately 270 km). However, it is possible that the actual shape of the unidentified object (PAN) is that of a butterfly, although this shape is not clearly discernible to the naked eye or in the video due to the poor definition of the smartphone camera used for the recording.

- Regular and straight-line movement

- The presence of the rocket stage, confirmed by the Operational Orbitography Center of CNES, at the initial and final observed azimuths

The visibility of these gases is made possible by several factors:

- The controlled reentry of the second stage with relight of the second-stage engine and passivation of the tanks

- The fact that this relight occurs near France

- Nightfall has already occurred at ground level, but the stage is still illuminated by the sun, allowing the gases (potentially the propellants) ejected from the propulsive stage to be lit up, as if sunset had occurred approximately one hour before the observation.

GEIPAN classifies the case as "A": observation of a Falcon 9 rocket stage in deorbiting phase.
