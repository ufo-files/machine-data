--- seg-746f350c8d852ad0e6e869bd [needs-review] ---
Description: The GEIPAN was contacted by email on July 7, 2010, by a witness who briefly describes an old observation case that occurred on February 3, 2009, between 7:00 and 7:15 PM. The witness, walking down a street, was intrigued by the passage of several orange oval lights that moved silently in a triangle formation. The witness lost sight of the phenomenon as it gradually moved away. Initially appearing quite strange (one immediately thinks of a release of Thai lanterns during celebrations following the Chinese New Year that began on January 29), this observation case proves to be more complex as the observed lights moved against the wind. However, the consistency of the testimony remains weak due to the multiple inaccuracies in the testimony and the significant time gap between the submission of this report and the events (17 months), which precludes any request to air traffic control. This case is classified as C due to the lack of additional information.
