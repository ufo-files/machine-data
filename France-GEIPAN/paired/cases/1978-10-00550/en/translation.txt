--- seg-c9aa972257f524727ca24ce9 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 3, 1978, around 2 a.m., a person on a moped was surprised by a bright light above a forest. The witness then saw an object in the shape of a cigar, red-orange in color, surrounded by a bright green ring. This object, stationary at 100 meters in the sky, dazzled the witness. The moped stopped working. The object suddenly disappeared with a faint humming sound. The moped started working again, allowing the frightened witness to continue their journey. Another person testified to the recurrence of such a craft passing through the area, without providing further details.
