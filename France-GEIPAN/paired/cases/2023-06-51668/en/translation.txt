Summary: Observation in the sky of a dark object with a component connected by a string: probable observation of a type paraglider artificial object in bivouac flight carried by the wind.
--- seg-cd2d3fe43010b4520149d3d6 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 19, 2023, between 18:22 and 18:30, a witness driving on the "Route des Espagnols" connecting Les Arcs 1800 to Peisey-Vallandry (Savoie) observed a dark isolated object in the sky. He stops, gets out of the vehicle, and observes the phenomenon with the naked eye for several minutes before filming and photographing it twice with his phone. Two hikers, unknown to the witness, are also seen observing the phenomenon. The object disappears after eight minutes, when the witness resumes his route and a turn obscures its visibility. The witness describes the object as a hollow, cube-shaped structure, with a dark gray, metallic appearance, carrying underneath it an element resembling a board connected by a thread. The whole is perceived as silent, immobile or drifting very slowly, carried by the wind, without flickering, halo or light emission. The case consistency is judged good, despite the late (July 2025) testimony of the only witness who reported the observation to GEIPAN. The duration of the observation allows for a detailed and consistent description, complemented by a video and two exploitable photographs. The strangeness felt by the witness is based on the perceived shape, long visibility, and absence of perceptible noise or movement, characteristics that differ from his references for known aerial objects. This impression is reinforced by the presence of an element suspended below the object, giving the whole an unexpected appearance. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of information considered reliable and objective, gathered for a testimony. Photographic analysis, however, nuances certain elements of the description. In the images, the object does not present the geometric characteristics of a rigid cube: no sharp edges or angular structure is visible. Its shape appears rather rectangular with rounded edges, evoking more of a flexible surface or a textile envelope than metallic walls. The suspended object is clearly visible and confirms a main object with payload configuration, classic in aerostatic devices such as, for example, a paraglider. A geometric estimation based on camera characteristics and the apparent size of the object in the image indicates an angular dimension approximately twice that of the Moon (~1.2°). By considering a range of possible distances, including the witness' estimate (between 100m and ~500m) and the witness' reported angular elevation of approximately 15°, we obtain an altitude between roughly 26 and 140 meters above the observation point, and a probable actual size between approximately 2 and 10 meters, this value being consistent with the dimensions of a paraglider sail (see Annex 1: IPACO* tool analysis). The behavior of the phenomenon is determining: remaining almost motionless for eight minutes, without noise or fall, necessarily implies an object carried by the air. At this altitude, a massive object would fall rapidly; a motorized device would produce a perceptible noise; a drone would have visible supporting structures. The absence of significant movement indicates a very low horizontal speed, estimated to be less than one meter per second, compatible with a simple drift in light wind or local thermal ascents, which are frequent in mountainous areas. Weather data from the two closest stations confirm a light variable wind (4 to 10 km/h) in general direction. The following hypotheses have been examined and ruled out: conventional aircraft, drone, official weather balloon, astronomical phenomenon, particular atmospheric phenomenon, space or military craft. All are incompatible with the observed shape, the absence of noise, the very low apparent speed, or the day observation conditions. The hypothesis retained is that of a light artificial object carried by the air: a homemade balloon, a textile sail, or an improvised device assimilable, equipped with a stabilizing ballast (the suspended element visible under the main form). It could also be a bivouac flight, combining the use of a paraglider and the transport of the necessary equipment for a bivouac. The shape of the suspended device is indeed very similar to that of the bag in which the paraglider slips, and the upper shape resembles that of a paraglider, seen from the side and almost from below, giving a false impression of cubic shape. The impression of rigidity and mass described by the witness can then be interpreted as a perception bias related to distance, the absence of depth cues, and the significant visual contrast between the dark hue of PAN and the very clear background sky. Textile envelopes inflated with air, like a paraglider, can visually give a misleading impression of hardness when observed from altitude. Investigations were conducted to determine if there was any organized activity, public entertainment, school project, or artistic installation involving a release of a flying object in this area and on this date. No trace was found in local press, public announcements, or institutional communications. We also attempted to determine if a bivouac flight in a hang glider could have taken place in the observation area, which is part of an official hang glider takeoff sector, without success. Note that the significant delay in the witness reporting his observation complicated the investigation. In conclusion, the observed phenomenon very likely corresponds to a light artificial object carried by local air currents, possibly a paraglider equipped for bivouac flying, but its precise origin has not been found. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": probable observation of a type paraglider artificial object in flight, bivouac carried by the wind.
