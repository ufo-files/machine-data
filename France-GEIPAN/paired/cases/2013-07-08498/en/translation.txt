Summary: Observation of the silent movement of two colored lights in the sky: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-2ae54893a1d0c11ae477c58f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of July 20, 2013, around 11:30 PM to 11:45 PM, two witnesses observed two intense lights in the sky. The first light was orange, and the second was red-purple. No sound accompanied their movements or disappearance in different directions. Only one statement was collected. This case of observation of glowing orbs clearly recalls Thai lanterns, with some originalities: first, the relatively rare red-purple color, and most importantly, the different movements of the two lights. The Meteociel report for Nevers indicates an unstable wind at that time, which could potentially explain why the two objects did not follow the same trajectory. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": observation of Thai lanterns.
