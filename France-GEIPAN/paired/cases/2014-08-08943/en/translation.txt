Description: At 23:10, the witness observed a light that burned out, leaving a white trail in the sky for several hundred meters, lasting for 5 seconds. No other witnesses were reported. The witness speculated about an atmospheric reentry. A light that burns out, leaving a white trail in the sky for several hundred meters, suggests a flare (see a flare video). The persistent trail (smoke) is quite characteristic of this type of device; the smoke would have remained visible as it was lit by city lights. The witness, located in the city of Marseille, was observing towards the sea, in the west direction: in this direction, there is the old port, the MUCEM, and the Frioul Islands (Château d'If). One could imagine multiple origins for such rocket launches: summer festivals, or distress rocket launches (which are red a priori) from boats. For information, atmospheric reentries of space debris do not leave persistent trails at low altitude. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a flare rocket.
