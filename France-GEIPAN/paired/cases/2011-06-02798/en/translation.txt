Description: During a walk in the Corbières on June 2, 2011 at 6:15 PM, a witness observed three white metallic objects spinning rapidly above the Albas valley (11). After one minute, the three objects disappeared into the clouds. Of average strangeness but low consistency due to the witness's imprecise position and the uncertain direction of the observed phenomenon's movement, this observation case cannot be firmly concluded. This case is classified C due to the lack of independent testimonies and cross-references.
