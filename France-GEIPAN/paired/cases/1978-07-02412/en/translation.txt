Description: On July 22, 1978, around 11:45 PM, several people observed in the sky a very bright, yellow-orange luminescent phenomenon emitting rays. For 15 minutes, one witness observed the phenomenon with binoculars before it disappeared. Due to the direction of observation and the position of Venus, particularly visible at this time of year, we can say that the witnesses made an astronomical observation.
