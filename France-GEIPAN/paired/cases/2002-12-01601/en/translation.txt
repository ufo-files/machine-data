--- seg-d0a4b9e24d5638a8bbb61280 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 3, 2002, at 6:00 PM, two witnesses observed in the sky two elongated bright points moving in a straight line without any noise. The observation lasted approximately 5 minutes. The two bright points observed by the witness correspond to the International Space Station (ISS, object 25544), preceded shortly by the space shuttle STS113 which had undocked from the station the previous day, before returning to Earth on December 7. (See in the annex the traces of these objects reported on the ground between 5:55 PM and 6:05 PM). This case is classified "A" as a perfectly identified phenomenon.
