Description: On January 18, 2025, around 6:00 PM, a witness standing in the parking lot of a supermarket observed a strange phenomenon in the very cloudy sky. They described a glowing bar composed of "about a dozen yellow rectangles like windows. It wasn't a solid structure, just rectangles of lights..." At first stationary, the phenomenon moved slowly and silently for approximately 30 seconds before disappearing behind the cloud layer. The case's consistency is deemed low: The file relies on a single statement taken down in a police report, with no photos or videos to support it. Due to the witness not returning to the investigator's requests, the necessary details for a deeper analysis could not be obtained.

According to the GEIPAN's criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. Various hypotheses were explored (see the investigation report) but without consolidated information, none could be weighted, therefore not retained. The GEIPAN classifies the case as 'C', unidentified phenomenon due to lack of information.
