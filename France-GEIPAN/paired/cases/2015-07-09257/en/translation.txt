--- seg-38a57a33ed57769c118fe640 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Tuesday, July 21, 2015 at 23:00, a witness observed for 10 to 15 seconds a flying object moving quickly and silently in a straight line across the sky. Only one testimony was collected. Based on the evidence gathered (see the investigation report), namely: - the orange color identical to that of a lantern - the rounded shape, like a lantern observed at a distance - the movement in the direction of the dominant wind - the short observation duration (10 to 15 seconds) compatible with the lifespan of lanterns - the absence of noise, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B" for a probable observation of a Thai lantern.
