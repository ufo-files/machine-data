Summary: Observation of a glowing sphere
--- seg-84168cb27c5a81620edc66ff [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 22, 194, a witness reported seeing from his vehicle a glowing sphere descending to the ground, then taking off again while splitting into three. The following day, the gendarmes on the scene found military personnel using a cannon positioned at the site of the observation. Although we have no other information, these military maneuvers taking place in the region are likely to explain the phenomenon observed.
