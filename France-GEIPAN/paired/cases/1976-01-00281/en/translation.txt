Description: On January 10, 1976, between 7:50 and 8:50 a.m., two individuals observed a very bright spherical object above the horizon. The witnesses noted that the object remained stationary. It gradually became invisible as the sun rose. The Gendarmerie, contacted promptly, found no object in the sky that morning or the next morning. No other testimonies were collected. Based on the collected descriptions, the witnesses likely observed a weather balloon.
