Summary: Multiple observations of a fast-moving fireball on a descending trajectory in the sky: likely observations of an atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-3e6ba64893ce4b26e7fe629e [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: The GEIPAN has received multiple reports of a bright phenomenon moving quickly across the sky. Observations were made in Peypin (13), along the RN 568 between Arles and Fos-sur-Mer (13), and from an helicopter flying over a station at Pyrénées 2000 (the indications 91 and 93 in the case name refer to the region code). The descriptions share characteristics (shape, trajectory, rapid angular motion) of an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid or space debris. Furthermore, the alignment, given the aspect, the general direction from North to South, and especially the time, with other reports (outside of the GEIPAN) made in quite distant locations, allows for the hypothesis of an atmospheric reentry visible to distant witnesses because it occurred at a high altitude (50 to 150 km) to be posited with a good probability. See the article on canalblog. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable atmospheric reentry.
