Summary: Observation of silent movements of a glowing sphere above the sea: light display related to an advertising campaign.
--- seg-563e637b62b91176758f383a [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 11, 2009, between 4:45 AM and 5:20 AM, two individuals observed above the sea a white glowing point with a verdant edge. No noise was heard during the object's variable speed maneuvers and its high-speed departure towards the open sea. The witnesses noted that two pleasure boats were in the vicinity near the phenomenon's movements. The observation in Canet-en-Roussillon took place during an advertising campaign conducted by the agency Buzzman for the SyFy channel at night on the night of August 10 to 11, 2009, between Canet and Argeles (see the investigation report). The "Close Encounter of the Third Kind" theme and the special effects used for this campaign align with the unusual observation of the two witnesses. The very early morning hour of this display (4:45 AM) corresponds to the end of an animation that began in the evening in the neighboring town of Argeles. This case is fully explained and therefore classified as "A," a light display for an advertising campaign.
