Description: According to a letter received by the Gendarmerie, a witness on vacation reported seeing on September 1, 1977 at 20:15 the movements of three objects shaped like slightly flattened eggs. They were bright in color and emitted rays of light. No other testimony was collected in the observation sector, which is also regularly overflown by planes from two nearby airports. The witness likely observed an aircraft.
