Summary: Long observation of two bright points moving very slowly one above the other: conjunction of Venus and Jupiter.
--- seg-2cef095680555a1a5afa7a81 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 30, 2015, between 11:30 PM and midnight, a witness observed for an extended period two unusual lights in the sky, colored yellow-orange, positioned one above the other. He took several photos. The observation of slowly moving bright points immediately suggests an astronomical origin. A check on the star map reveals that on that evening, Venus and Jupiter were visible at their closest, just above the horizon O/NO: this rare phenomenon of apparent proximity is called a conjunction. The attached star maps show the appearance of the phenomenon at 11:33 PM, and again at 11:55 PM when the planets were about to disappear below the horizon. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A," perfectly explained: conjunction of Venus and Jupiter.
