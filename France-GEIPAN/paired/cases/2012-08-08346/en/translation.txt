Summary: Observations of unusual and silent movements of a bright round object in the sky: probable observation of a passing luminous balloon.
--- seg-e4ad7e34d1937f9ca687b31d [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 20, 2012, around 10:30 PM, two witnesses observed the peculiar and silent movements of a round, glowing object in the sky. It disappeared quickly. The weather report for Béziers Cap d'Agde (very close to Mèze) for that evening shows a light, very unstable breeze from the West, then shifting to the North; the movements of the object observed by the witnesses are clearly the movements of an object carried by the wind. Due to the fireworks that evening, one might think it was an object like a Thai lantern or another glowing balloon launched for the festivities. The witness noted a white light, while witnesses of Thai lanterns most often describe lights in orange-red. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," a probable observation of a glowing balloon (LED balloon).
