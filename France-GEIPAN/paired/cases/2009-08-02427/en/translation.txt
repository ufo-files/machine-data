Summary: Observation of the presence and then the movement in the sky of an oval phenomenon with lights: lack of information.
--- seg-48262f0f3b751eab694aade0 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 21, 2009, at 9:49 PM, a witness observed for three minutes in the sky an oval-shaped object equipped with a powerful white light beacon and small lights. Initially stationary, the object then flew in a straight line without any noise being heard. The witness's wife also observed the phenomenon and took photographs. This observation case is accompanied by several photos provided by the witness. These photos, on the one hand, do not correspond to the observation and are clearly the result of graphic processing. In view of this constatation, the photos are considered unusable. The observation of the phenomenon remains, which appears moderately strange due to its straight-line movement, which can be explained by the observation of a low-flying plane. Given the conclusions on the analysis of the photo, the GEIPAN classifies this case as 'C' due to the lack of reliable information.
