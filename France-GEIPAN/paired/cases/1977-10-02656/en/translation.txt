Description: On October 8, 1977, between 12:30 AM and 1 AM, a person on a motorcycle observed the passage of an unrecognized craft. It had a flat base topped with a rounded shape; the craft was entirely lit at its base by yellow lights surrounding a larger red light in the center. The frightened witness reported being followed for three kilometers until his home. The craft then stopped above his house before departing at very high speed. The light description suggests this witness likely observed the passage of a helicopter-type aircraft.
