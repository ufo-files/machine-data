Description: On March 5, 2015 at 7:13 a.m., a witness at Longueville train station was intrigued by the shape of an airplane trail. As well observed by the witness, this trail was quite exceptional. The GEIPAN submitted the photograph to experts at Météo-France: "It is indeed a condensation trail that, after its formation, was distorted by the turbulence prevailing at the location where it was created. Very turbulent zones can indeed exist in clear skies (known as 'clear-air turbulence'). These zones are feared by pilots as they are hard to predict, not visible on radar, and are the seat of violent phenomena that force crews to change flight levels to preserve passenger comfort. A photo with such pronounced meanders is however a first for us. This rare case is classified as "A" observation of a condensation trail of the Airbus A319 on flight AF7614 from Marseille to Lille (see Flight Radar map at 7:13 local time), heavily disturbed by atmospheric turbulence. PJ: Flightradar log, Météociel ground log, at Melun.
