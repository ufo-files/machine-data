Description: A witness reports their observation made on May 21, 2011 at 1:55 AM: in the sky, a group of 7 red-orange glowing orbs move slowly in a north-south direction. No noise was heard during the observation before these lights disappeared behind the clouds. The description matches the classic testimony of Thai lanterns perfectly: color, brightness, number, movement according to the wind. The witness mentions that Lille was celebrating its football double that evening, which could be an opportunity for a lantern release. This case is classified as "B", probable observation of Thai lanterns.
