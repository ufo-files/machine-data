--- seg-b8a4902ab91e1d151ca30dff [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 18, 2007, a driver was intrigued by the passage of an object in the sky. She called her husband, who was also able to see the rapid movement of a large white oval patch in the sky. According to the Gendarmerie investigation, no object fell to the ground in the indicated area. This observation is not particularly strange: the shape of the object, its movement according to the wind (Mistral: N to N/O), are the characteristics of a balloon, very bright at the end of the day. One could think of a scientific balloon released from the Observatory of Haute Provence (verification not done). This case is classified "B": probable observation of a scientific balloon.
