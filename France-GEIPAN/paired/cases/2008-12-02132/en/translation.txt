Summary: Two witnesses in a car observed a large black mass at very low altitude with lights arranged in a triangle: certain misidentification with a C160 TRANSALL on a night mission.
--- seg-e584507c1229219de35d20d9 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 2, 2008, at 10:15 PM, two witnesses in a car were surprised by the presence in the sky of a large black mass with green, red/orange, and pale lights arranged in a triangle. The PAN was at very low altitude and appeared static. It was suddenly hidden by trees. The driver continued on their way and turned around a few hundred meters further. Upon returning to the observation site, the PAN had disappeared. The Gendarmerie's Procedural Report (PV) includes consistent statements from 3 witnesses regarding the observation. The gendarmerie investigation, after inquiries with various authorities, indicates that the witnesses could have definitely seen a C160 TRANSALL conducting a planned low-altitude mission (see attached documents on PV Nos. 5, 6, 7, and 9, radar returns). The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A": observation of an airplane.
