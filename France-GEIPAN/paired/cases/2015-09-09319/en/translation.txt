Description: On Sunday, September 20, 2015, around 8:00-8:15 PM, a witness on their terrace observed in the clear sky "a vertical fall" of an object at high speed that disappeared into the landscape. The witness thought it was the "fall of a supersonic aircraft." A few seconds later, the witness saw another object ascending very quickly towards the zenith. A part of the object seemed to detach and fall back at "horizontal" to the landscape. The witness believed it was the same object for both observations. No noise was heard during the observation of these orange or gray phenomena. Given the information gathered (see the investigation report), including:

- Lack of precise angular data.
- Absence of azimuthal data for the appearance and disappearance of the UAPs for each observation phase.
- Incomplete information regarding the presence of condensation trails.
- Unknown observation durations for each phase.
- Lack of precision on the nature and amplitude of the movements reported by the witness as being in an "obtuse angle."

Although the only plausible hypothesis considered is a confusion with condensation trails of planes observed at sunset, we can conclude that the lack of information does not allow a definitive conclusion in favor of this hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C," an observation of an unidentified phenomenon at this stage, with the lack of elements not allowing a solid opinion.
