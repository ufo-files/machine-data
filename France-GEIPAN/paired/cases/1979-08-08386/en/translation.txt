Description: Recent testimony (10/20/2012) of an old observation. A witness reports observing on August 24, 1979, around midnight, a stationary but silently rotating silver-colored craft above urban electrical cables. No other testimony has been collected on this phenomenon. A fine analysis of the testimony by a university psychologist reveals several inconsistencies and does not allow for the consideration that the reported events were entirely experienced, but rather that it is more likely a mix of real facts and imagined elements or from other narratives. Memory could have confused these elements after more than 30 years. It is also important to know that adolescence is also an age conducive to the creation of "false memories." An investigation 30 years later would not bring back any certain information. This case is classified "C" for lack of reliable information, cross-references, or others.
