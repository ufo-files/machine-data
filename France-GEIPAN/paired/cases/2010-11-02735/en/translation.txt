Description: A person testifies late (PV of April 14, 2011) about an observation made between 2:40 p.m. and 3:10 p.m. at the beginning of November 2010 (on November 2, 8, 9, or 10, 2010). While walking his dog on a rural road, a witness was fascinated for about a dozen seconds by an object he could not identify. Flying approximately 2.5 meters above the ground, a blue object moves at a steady speed along a straight trajectory. The witness describes a cubic shape with two white circles on each side connected by beams of light. The object crosses the road in front of the witness and suddenly disappears near a tree. No trace of the object in the tree branches is found by the witness. No other testimony about this phenomenon is collected, and no particular traces are found at the scene by the gendarmerie, who were informed late. The hypothesis considered in the IPN investigation of a remotely piloted flying object or a quadcopter drone could correspond to the description. These devices are multiplying, available for sale everywhere, at all prices, in all sizes and colors (some are controllable with an iPhone). The characteristics of 'blur' around the body ('rounded cube') described by the witness could correspond to the associated wing rotation, the witness's visual acuity not allowing him to distinguish the details of an object or accurately judge distances. This observation has a low degree of strangeness, without a precise observation date. The majority of the described characteristics correspond to known objects (remote-controlled object, drone, etc.). This case is classified B as the probable observation of a remotely piloted object (toy, drone, etc.).
