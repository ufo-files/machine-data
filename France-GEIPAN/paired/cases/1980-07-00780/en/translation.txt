Description: This observation case is part of cases classified "C" by the GEPAN, under the name SAINT-CYR L’ECOLE (78) 21.07.1980. It was recently re-examined (see the investigation notes document). On July 21, 1980, at 6:25 a.m., a witness, from their 5th-floor apartment, spotted an orange glow in the clear sky. Moving onto their balcony, they distinguished a half-sphere of very bright orange color. The object remained stationary for a minute before moving straight and disappearing. No other information was gathered by the gendarmerie. This case is weak (single witness, brief observation), and the description of the PAN observed is fairly accurate. The witness's sincerity and credibility have never been questioned. The described phenomenon shares many common characteristics (shape, size, color) with a perfectly known astronomical object: the Sun. Moreover, the Sun was clearly present in the observed area of the sky. The issue is not the witness's visual perception but the interpretation they make of their observation through their feelings (observing the Sun through clouds). In the current GEIPAN classification, this case of low strangeness is classified "A," an observation related to a misperception with the Sun.
