Description: On December 31, 2014, around 7:30 PM, a witness observed in the sky two equidistant, stationary, and silent lights. After a few minutes, the lights slowly and silently passed over the roofs of houses, out of the witness's view. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of glowing balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a festive night, suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The very low movement observed towards the west could be consistent with the weak and unstable wind, temporarily southwest, reported in Lille by Météociel. The directions of movement indicated by the witness are not well consistent across different parts of the testimony. As the witness reports white lights, but Thai lanterns are most often described as yellow-orange, and the wind is uncertain, this hypothesis of lanterns, though probable, lacks confirming elements. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to the lack of precise information about the wind, awaiting further related testimonies.
