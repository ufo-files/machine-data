Description: On June 24, 2015, around 9:45 PM (the questionnaire incorrectly mentions June 25), an individual watching TV had their attention drawn outdoors by the movement of about a dozen bright, colorful lights flying over a hill and the tops of trees. No noise was heard during the fifteen-minute observation. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of flying lights crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests illuminated balloons (LED balloons or Thai lanterns), likely released nearby after a private party (St. John's Day celebration?). The witness indicates in their account a movement towards the West, but the drawing shows a movement from NNE to SSO (see the cartographic reconstruction). The movement was generally observed from North to South, which is consistent with the Northwesterly wind at Mulhouse-Bâle (at 9 PM: light North wind of 7-11 km/h shifting to West at 11 PM: Météociel). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," a probable observation of LED balloons or Thai lanterns. The multiple colors and the long duration of the phenomenon point more towards LED balloons, as Thai lanterns most often have a dominant yellow-orange hue due to the flame that provides the light.
