Summary: Probable observation of an atmospheric reentry
--- seg-b26c96ee475dc37f84af0b64 [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 20, 2004, around 00:30, a witness and his spouse observed from their terrace a silent, extremely fast-moving shape that made no noise. A powerful beam of light was located at the rear of the shape, the front being green. The shape slowed down, the beam went out, leaving only the green light before completely disappearing. The observation lasted 3 to 4 minutes. Following a press article, a second witness, an amateur astronomer, came forward. He observed the phenomenon for a few dozen seconds and interpreted it as an atmospheric reentry. The phenomenon was classified B: the hypothesis of an atmospheric reentry was retained.
