Summary: Sequential long observations of the developments of a silent light phenomenon in the sky: probable observations of light displays.
--- seg-d329b8d1fba19aab50ae5ec6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Tuesday, September 24, 2013, between 10:00 PM and 11:00 PM, two individuals observed in the sky the rapid movements of a luminous phenomenon. Subsequent observations of a similar nature were made from the hamlet. No unusual noises were heard, and each observation lasted an hour or more. The witness's description of the luminous phenomenon and the confirmation by the gendarmes clearly point to the hypothesis of a beam from a ground-based projector. Although there is no nightclub in the direction of the observation, the gendarmes inform that the mountainous and sparsely populated area is often the site of informal parties (raves). The photo taken by the witness, which contains a clear circle, actually shows nothing of the observed phenomenon: the clear circle at the top of the image is an "orbe" caused by the camera flash on dust very close to the lens. See: Circular or orbe clear spots. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a light show. The GEIPAN did not conduct research to find the source of the projector, an extremely difficult task due to these installations being temporary and often clandestine.
