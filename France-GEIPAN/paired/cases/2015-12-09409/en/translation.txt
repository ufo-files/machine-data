Description: The witness is on the first floor of their home in Ampuis (69), on December 29, 2015, at approximately 4:58 PM, when they observe three elongated, glowing, white objects in the sky to the south and southwest. The objects appear stationary, then move in a straight line, without any sound. The observation is made with a friend and a relative, lasting around 12 minutes. The case consistency is good: while only one of the three witnesses reported their observation to GEIPAN, three photographs of PAN were taken and clearly identify the phenomenon: three short, sun-lit contrails from airplanes. The shape, color, and movement are consistent with the hypothesis. Numerous photographs of such contrails exist in the GEIPAN database or on the Méprises-du-ciel.fr website, in the condensation trails page, showing exactly the same appearance as the __UFO_PROTECTED_001__s photographed by the witness. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A: observation of condensation trails produced by commercial airplanes.

CONSISTANCE: According to GEIPAN criteria, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.
