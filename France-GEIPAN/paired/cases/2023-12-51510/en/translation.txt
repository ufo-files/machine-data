Description: From their home garden in La Charité-sur-Loire (58), the main witness (T1) and occasionally his wife (T2) regularly observed in the sky several __UFO_PROTECTED_000__s, appearing as brief, punctual, and stationary white light flashes. Only the two observations on 12/02/2023 were analyzed here, as these are the only ones with a precise date and time (see the investigation report). The case consistency is average, with 2 witnesses, but only 1 detailed testimony without illustration of the PAN. Furthermore, while T1 made numerous observations of the __UFO_PROTECTED_000__s, only those on 12/02/2023 are well-documented. *According to GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The description of the __UFO_PROTECTED_000__s is characteristic of satellite flash observations. This type of observation (localized flashes), recurrent for T1, was confirmed through detailed reconstruction of satellite passes, demonstrating that one of them could correspond to the position of the first PAN, well-identified thanks to the reference point formed by nearby planet Jupiter. Regarding the second PAN, the position indicated by T1 lacks precision, preventing formal identification among the numerous satellites present that night at the zenith. The case is classified as "B," probable observation of satellite flashes.
