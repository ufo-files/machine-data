Summary: Observation of silent, straight, and ascending movement of reddish-orange light phenomena; instantaneou extinction of some: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-63b93a9efbc62024d32f1730 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 24, 2012, at approximately 9:45 PM, a family observed for 5 minutes the silent movements of 6 to 8 glowing points of red to orange-yellow color. They appear successively at the same frequency and seem to follow each other at equal distances in the sky. All have an upward trajectory. Some disappear instantly. One witness made a video. The observation by the witnesses is characteristic of an observation of Thai lantern releases, both in terms of colors, shapes, and the type of trajectory, including in-flight extinction (disappearance due to the end of the burner's combustion). The rapid movement from south to north corresponds to the wind measured in Biarritz (strong south wind recorded on infoclimat). The first two PANs differ in their direction of movement (east to west), but these directional indications are reported by the witness and not by the witnesses themselves, and do not benefit from the added attention or precision associated with filling out a questionnaire or a testimony report. Moreover, near an ocean, the wind can be unstable. Finally, the date and time on an evening of December 24 support the hypothesis of Thai lanterns as they are conducive to a festive event of balloon releases. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: Thai lanterns.
