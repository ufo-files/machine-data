Summary: Observation of a glowing disk crossing the sky.
--- seg-307551df1a8579f853826aba [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 14, 1979, around 10:45 PM, a witness in the middle of the crowd watching the fireworks noticed in the sky (at 45°) the passage of a light that intrigued them. They observed for 8 to 12 seconds this brightness crossing the sky in a straight line from south to north at a constant speed. The object had the shape of a glowing disk with a small, fainter tail. The witness noted color variations but no noise coming from the object was perceptible. It quickly disappeared. Despite the crowd present at that time, no other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
