Description: A witness reports an observation that took place on August 15, 2009 at 1:15 PM from the garden of their home. They saw pass through the sky, along a straight trajectory, an oval-shaped object of dark brown color. The phenomenon moved from East to West towards Cognac. No noise was heard during the observation. No other witnesses were reported on this phenomenon. The hypothesis of an airplane or helicopter passing was ruled out by the witness and could not be verified with air traffic control due to the late receipt at GEIPAN of the gendarmerie report. Several other hypotheses were considered for this atypical case, even the hypothesis of objects very close: rugby ball, trap ball, frisbee, model aviation, but none of these correspond to the witness's testimony (the phenomenon clearly passing over the roofs). The hypothesis of an atmospheric reentry of a meteorite was also considered (period of intense meteorite activity, peak of the Perseids) but impossible to verify without additional testimony collected. Without a consolidated hypothesis, this case is classified as D: Strange phenomenon / Very strange to average to strong consistency.
