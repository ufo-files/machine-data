Description: On July 27, 1981, around 11:30 PM, two witnesses spotted two bright lights in the sky. They moved parallel to each other, then changed their trajectories before returning to their starting point. The observation, which lasted about 10 minutes, allowed several passes to be witnessed. The objects suddenly turned quickly towards the West and disappeared. No noise was heard during the observation. No other testimony was received on this phenomenon for which we lack information.
