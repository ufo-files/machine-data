Description: On October 3, 2013, around 10:05 PM, a witness was intrigued by the presence at low altitude in the sky of some kind of bright orange half-circle. The observation lasted 5 minutes. Over a year after the event, and after studying various possibilities of misperception, the GEIPAN proposed to the on-site investigation witness; the latter responded that he had himself found the origin of the phenomenon: a distant streetlamp. The GEIPAN classified this case as "A": observation of a streetlamp.
