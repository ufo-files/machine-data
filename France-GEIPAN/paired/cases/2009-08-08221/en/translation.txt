Summary: Observations of the silent passage of an orange ball in the sky: probable passage of a Thai lantern.
--- seg-e51057dc75650afa64ab7f46 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Several people observed on August 22, 2009, around 9:30 PM, the passage of an orange glowing ball. It moved on a straight trajectory from West to East: no noise was heard during the observation. The ball stopped before disappearing at very high speed towards the south. The GEIPAN will later receive email testimonies from two other witnesses. The observation has the characteristics of Thai lanterns: orange color, movement consistent with the wind (light variable wind, predominantly from the West), local festival nearby (Maubourguet Encounters). The apparent temporary immobility of the object can be explained by its movement facing the observers, and the object's disappearance corresponds to the extinguishing of the flame. The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'B': probable observation of a Thai lantern.
