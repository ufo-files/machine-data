Summary: Nighttime observations in the Paris sky of a red glow: probable observations of a bright spot coming from a powerful red laser.
--- seg-f78a258ee1d844cc70917d9f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of August 23 to 24, 2015, witnesses were intrigued by a bright red glow in the Paris sky. They independently reported their observations. The first witness began observing at 11:25 PM on August 23, 2015, noting a very bright red light in the Paris sky heading southeast. This light appeared permanent (with changes in intensity) and moved slightly in the sky before returning to its original position. Intrigued, the witness took photographs with his smartphone and observed the phenomenon until 1:00 AM before going to sleep (see investigation report). A second witness on August 24, 2015 at 12:58 AM observed for 10 minutes a red-hot ball located northwest above Paris buildings. The witness had time to take three photographs with a mobile phone. Upon returning to the window to wake up his wife, the object was gone (see investigation report). The testimonies are consistent, with enough data provided by the witnesses and exploitable. Several photographs of the PAN were taken and used for analysis leading to fruitful overlaps. In conclusion, the physical and dynamic characteristics of the PAN observed and photographed by the witnesses resemble those of a powerful red laser used by a private individual in Paris, fired from a building's rooftop, and whose light spot produced by its impact on the low cloud/fog layer resembles the PAN. The ambient lighting conditions surrounding the witnesses during their observation and photo-taking prevent the laser beam from being visible, both to the naked eye and through the photographic sensor, except after enhancing one of the photographs. The strangeness of this case is ultimately quite low and is mainly caused by the light intensity of the PAN and its persistence for over an hour and a half. The two testimonies are subject to independent analysis thanks to the photographs and consistent testimonies (see investigation reports). This case is to be classified as 'B' as a probable observation of the light spot on the cloud or fog cover, projected by a powerful red laser fired from a building in the 11th arrondissement of Paris.
