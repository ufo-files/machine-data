Description: On October 7, 1954, at 9:00 AM, two individuals observed a very bright, oval-shaped object made of aluminum-colored material directly above the church bell tower. The object moved in a southeast to northwest direction. The observation lasted for one minute. The National Gendarmerie created a concise intelligence report on this observation. This type of observation can have various explanations (sunlight reflection on an airplane, balloon, meteor, etc.). The GEIPAN classified this case as C due to a lack of information.
