Summary: Observation of the slow and silent movement of two intense red light points: probable observation of Thai lanterns
--- seg-e74b9acf684c95f46be1f550 [needs-review] ---
Description: Two people observed on September 8, 2012 at 23:00, the slow and silent passage of two round, red glowing objects. One of the two objects changed direction from the first and suddenly disappeared. The description of the phenomenon and the photo strongly suggest Thai lanterns: orange-red balls moving slowly for a few minutes. The movement from South to North or Northwest is not contradictory with the very light, unstable wind from the South sector. Saturday evenings are the privileged moments for lantern releases. The disappearance of the objects simply corresponds to the flame's extinction due to the paraffin's depletion. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
