Summary: Observation of red glowing points accompanied by noise
--- seg-53b5b634dcaa3853873ac520 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of July 8, 1993, around midnight, a taxi driver and his passenger, returning from the train station, observed through the windshield red glowing points. After stopping the car, the driver got out, but the points had disappeared; however, a loud, piercing noise moved away to the south. Two people in the vicinity reported hearing a piercing noise move quickly by and causing dogs to bark without apparent reason. No other information could be gathered.
