Summary: Observations of the passage of four white lights forming the four corners of a rectangle: likely observations of light reflections from car headlights on railway catenary.
--- seg-6155a8fca500868d23d0cd99 [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 4, 2012, around 8:00 PM, a driver and her passenger made an observation that intrigued them. While stopped at a stop sign, they witnessed four white lights passing from right to left, forming the four corners of a rectangle several meters above the ground. These lights disappeared at the height of the treetops. No unusual noises were heard. The GEIPAN initially considered a wind-carried light object, but the wind direction (West) did not correspond to the object's movement (South -> North). An investigation with military air control, seeking any aircraft, yielded a negative result. A GEIPAN investigator visited the site at night and observed, then recorded on video, a lighting phenomenon that could easily explain the observation: in fact, the headlights of cars approaching the observer reflected off the overhead power lines; the reflections moved from right to left, then disappeared, as reported by the witnesses. It is highly likely that this was the same phenomenon witnessed by the observers. The witness did not wish to meet with the investigator to refine the investigation. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of car headlight reflections on the railroad power lines.
