Description: On April 7, 2011 at 23:06, a witness living on the 7th floor of an apartment building noticed in the sky a luminous phenomenon that intrigued them. The witness described as a set of two parts, rather oval and very close, transitioning from white to orange, then to red, and disappearing without any particular noise. The observed phenomenon resembles two Thai lanterns receding from the observer. The color transition from white to orange then to red, and the movement according to the wind in a direction close to the line of sight, support this hypothesis. One could think that these lanterns were launched from the banks of the Seine not far from the observer, and then followed the wind, roughly from North to South. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
