Summary: Observation of a glowing phenomenon changing from white to orange then to red: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-b2d92cb7d456a9891603aa0a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 7, 2011, at 23:06, a witness living on the 7th floor of an apartment building observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky that intrigued them. The witness describes it as a set of two parts, rather oval and very close to each other, turning from white to orange, then to red, and disappearing without any particular noise. The observed phenomenon resembles two Thai lanterns moving away from the observer. The color changing from white to orange then red, and the movement according to the wind in a direction close to the direction of sight, supports this hypothesis. It can be thought that these lanterns were launched from the banks of the Seine not far from the observer, and then followed the wind, roughly from North to South. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
