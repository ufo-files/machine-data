Summary: Observation of 3 balls of different sizes.
--- seg-5bc433574cbb356905227c39 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of August 5, 1979, between 11:00 PM and 11:30 PM, a witness and gendarmes observed peculiar luminous phenomena in the sky. Balls of varying sizes and colors shone to the left of the Moon. Some moved in quick jerks from left to right and sometimes at very high speeds. They would disappear into the clouds. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we have few details.
