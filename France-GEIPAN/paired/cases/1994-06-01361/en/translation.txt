Description: On June 27, 1994, around 11:00 a.m., a witness driving a car observed at a distance estimated at 8 km (5 miles) two dark, pyramid-shaped objects, without any lights or indicators, remaining stationary. As the witness turned a corner, he lost sight of the two phenomena. A short distance later, he encountered a third black triangular object flying above his car at the same speed, approximately 80 km/h (50 mph). Within moments, the object accelerated and disappeared with a deep rumble and a high-pitched squeal, leaving the witness with an unexplained phenomenon.
