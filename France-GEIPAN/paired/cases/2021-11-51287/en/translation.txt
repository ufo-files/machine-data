Summary: Observation of several dark "smokes" in the sky: observation of condensation trails.
--- seg-1eb5f3a7924066456ff91860 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Saturday, November 20, 2021, between 5:10 PM and 5:30 PM, several people observed in the sky the presence of dark "smoke." Intrigued, they took photographs. Only one testimony was collected. The testimony is consistent: multiple witnesses (even though only one person testified) and numerous photos of PAN. The visual aspect of PAN is perfectly consistent with condensation trails from commercial airplanes: hazy ("smoke") appearance, elongated shapes in motion, dark color with an orange tint oriented towards the setting sun. The observation presents all typical aspects of this category of observation, as it occurred between sunset and the end of twilight. It turns out that the trajectory of the "smoke" observed corresponds to an air corridor frequently used by commercial airplanes. GEIPAN requested a radar return of aircraft passages at the time of the observation from the CNOA (National Center for Air Operations of the Air and Space Force). The provided radar map covers the time range 4:00 PM - 4:30 PM UTC (or 5:00 PM - 5:30 PM local time). It shows that traffic is heavy in this air corridor (see attached map). The perceived strangeness by the witness is related to the dark color and the impression that the trails "rise" vertically. The dark color is explained by the time of day when the observation was made (sunset). Regarding the perceived direction of motion, the PAN gave the impression of rising vertically, while in fact they were approaching the witness. Illustrations of this type of observation are referenced on the Misperceptions-in-the-Sky website in the article condensation trail. The case is classified as A, observation of condensation trails.
