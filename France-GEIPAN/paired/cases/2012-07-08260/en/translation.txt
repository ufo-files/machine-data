Description: On July 4, 2012 at 7:20 AM, a driver on the A75 highway near the commune of Authezat (63) was intrigued by the presence in the sky of a dark, round to oval-shaped object. It is stationary in the direction of Sancy. Six minutes later, while driving on D2013 between Saint-Amand-Tallende and Aydat at the height of Saint-Saturnin, the driver sees the object again and notices 5 other shapes moving at a reduced speed and low altitude. The witness thinks of fighter planes. As the driver heads towards Chadrat, he loses sight of the various objects. The observation lasted 8 minutes. No further testimony will be collected. This GEIPAN report translates to:

This testimony has average consistency as it is imprecisely reported, coming from a single witness without any photographs. The observation is not very strange as it could be a perfectly ordinary object. The hypotheses considered (see attached report) are those of an aerostat, a captive balloon, or a free-flying craft (ULM, paraglider, etc.). However, given the evidence gathered in the investigation, namely: - The analysis of three hypotheses that do not fit well with the observation - The absence of information (angular measurements) that would allow for better verification of the estimates of the size, distance, and altitude of the PAN In this case, the report is classified as C due to a lack of information.
