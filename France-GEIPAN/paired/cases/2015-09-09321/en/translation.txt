Summary: Observation of the silent movement of 5 yellow-orange light points in the night sky: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-898212c14f0f9bf351894bdf [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 13, 2015, at 8:30 PM, two people on a balcony were intrigued by the silent movement on an ascending trajectory in the sky of five bright, yellow-orange lights. Three of them formed a triangle. Only one testimony was collected. Based on the objective elements defined in the investigation report (attached document), namely:

- The orange or yellow-orange color identical to that of lanterns.
- The number corresponding to a classic group release of lanterns.
- The simultaneous movement of the lanterns, carried by the wind and creating an illusion of psychological shape.
- The movement in the direction of the dominant wind.
- The behavior identical to that of objects subject to no or low wind: low speed.
- The short observation duration (1 to 2 minutes) compatible with the lifespan of lanterns.
- The date: a Sunday evening, compatible with those favorable to the organization of lantern releases on celebrations.
- The disappearance caused by the extinction of lanterns reaching the end of their life.
- The absence of noise.
- The presence nearby of open-air installations conducive to lantern releases, located in the direction of observation.

The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B" for a probable observation of Thai lanterns.
