Description: A witness reports observing streaks followed by several red-orange discs silently moving across the sky on July 11, 2010, at approximately 02:20. The witness will take photographs during their observation, lasting around 15 to 20 minutes. The "red discs" photographed by the witness are due to the photo zoom used during the capture. The described characteristics (multiple waves of red-orange lights moving in the same direction) largely correspond to known phenomena, such as flying lanterns. This hypothesis is supported by the discovery by the village mayor of partially burnt lanterns on the town hall parking lot. This case is classified as A, indicating an observation of Thai lanterns.
