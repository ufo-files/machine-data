Summary: Observation of an orange glow crossing the sky
--- seg-68bfee7fe36ad0cb4afe8e6b [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 15, 1979, at approximately 7:25 a.m., five individuals working on the renovation of a railroad track observed a luminous phenomenon that intrigued them. An orange glow, roughly the size of a soccer ball, moved slowly across the sky from east to west on a descending trajectory. The observation, lasting about 30 seconds, revealed a trail of white smoke. No sound was heard. The object disappeared, hidden by the landscape. These individuals may have witnessed an atmospheric reentry, which, given the observation time, could be a space debris.
