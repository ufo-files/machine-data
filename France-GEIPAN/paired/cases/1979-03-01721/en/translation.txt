Description: On March 25, 1979, between 21:55 and 22:10, a witness observed from his factory surveillance post a sky phenomenon that intrigued him. He first saw a milky white glow in the shape of an arc, followed by a green ball with a red dot in the middle performing various movements. No noise was heard. This witness and two other firefighters had already observed a luminous phenomenon at the beginning of the year. No other testimony could be collected on the March 25 phenomenon, for which we lack information.
