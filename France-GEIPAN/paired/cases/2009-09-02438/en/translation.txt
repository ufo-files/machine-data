Description: A witness observed between midnight and 1:15 a.m. from the windows of his home, the silent and straight movement of three orange-light orbs. After a change in direction, they disappeared as they ascended into the sky. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon. The witness's description corresponds perfectly to Thai lanterns.
