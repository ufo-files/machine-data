Summary: Laser beam used in the context of meteorological research by the Faculty of Sciences of Saint-Denis.
--- seg-5a8c4de9ec123981cf8c8943 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Two witnesses report seeing, on several evenings between 10:00 PM and midnight, a fluorescent green light rising vertically in the sky. A gendarme confirms also having seen this brightness in the sky at the same times. The gendarmerie investigation will demonstrate that the atmospheric and cyclone study laboratory at the Faculty of Sciences in Saint-Denis regularly uses a "laser" to study meteorological phenomena at night. The description of the fluorescent green, fixed light perfectly matches the accounts of the witnesses.
