Summary: Sequential observations in September 2015 of aircraft passages preceded by light phenomena: probable observations of aircraft in military operations.
--- seg-547a36cad2f7ccd069b438f2 [needs-review] ---
Description: The witness provided two very similar statements on September 14 and 23, 2015, from the same location and at the same time (around 10:30 PM). Each time, there is a perfectly observed environment by the witness of aerial military operations with descriptions in terms of planes with their sounds and trails. Furthermore, the witness states that this made him think of "very recent exercises (early September) of Rafale planes along the coast (border between Roscoff and St-Pol-de-Leon) flying at low altitude (exercises?) for hours, they flew over our heads at low altitude!" And the witness specifies on September 14th that "For me, it's almost certain that on Monday, September 14th, it was military planes." In this aerial operation environment, what surprises the witness on September 14:

"- 'Two very loud and distinct sounds (like a 'missile' or a huge firework without a bang) made me look out the window.'" - a PAN that appears different from the planes flying in the sky because the PAN is in the form of a spherical ball that at one point released a glowing curtain towards the ground. And what surprised the witness on September 23: "- 'Several (4 or 5) 'light balls' (much brighter than the few pale stars that were visible) moving very quickly in different directions (could be mistaken for a 'ballet') appearing and disappearing alternately at high speed. - 'Two planes (identifiable by the white trails they formed) near these light balls (I only saw 2, there may have been more, given the noise.)." The phenomenon lasted approximately 1 to 2 minutes, the "light balls" quickly disappeared, only the planes remained visible and audible. The PANS area, as reported by the witness according to his location (SO or SE on day 14 or 23), and also as plotted on the map by the witness, corresponds to a low-altitude flight zone for the military (see 2015 RTBA map). The witness therefore witnessed two aerial exercises. In such a context, what seemed strange to the witness is not necessarily strange a priori. These exercises can for example result in tests of infrared light decoys that can generate strange lights for anyone who would have difficulty associating them with the context of military maneuvers and exercises (see the article "Military Flares and Decoys as OVNI Generators"). No other witness came close to the GEIPAN to report oddities of these events that, nevertheless, attracted attention in the sky, at least by their noise (case of this testimony). As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the cases of September 14 and 23, 2015, as B: aircraft.
