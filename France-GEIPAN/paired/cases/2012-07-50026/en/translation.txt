Summary: Successive observations of different phenomena in the sky: Lack of information
--- seg-818ccb72e8a87eb78821305b [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 23, 2012, at 10:45 PM, two witnesses on a beach successively made observations of phenomena that intrigued them. Three distinct phenomena were briefly reported by both witnesses. No other testimony was collected. This case presents a fairly high degree of strangeness, but it has rather weak consistency: indeed, there are indeed two witnesses, but they are not independent, and it is surprising, at an hour when there are still many walkers on the beach, that no other witness has come forward. It is also noted that the observation was very short. The investigation did not find similarities with classic misperceptions. Due to a lack of corroboration, this case is classified as "C."
