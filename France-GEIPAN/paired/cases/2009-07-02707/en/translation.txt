Description: A witness reports that during an evening (around 11 PM) in July 2009, while on their terrace, they heard a engine noise and saw a dark square shape pass overhead with four glowing points at its ends. Frightened, they went inside their home but continued to watch the slow southern movement of the object until it disappeared, hidden by the neighboring house. The recent questionnaire (from January 19, 2011) about this old observation provides few details and makes any investigation impossible. No other similar testimony in this area will be collected on this phenomenon for which we lack information (no specific date for the day of the observation, which is arbitrarily dated to the first day of the month).
