Description: On September 18, 1998, several witnesses observed a bright, oval object that appeared to follow a precise trajectory. Flashes of light regularly illuminated the craft. One of the witnesses was able to film the observation before the craft moved away and disappeared. The investigation will demonstrate that it was a night flight of a Zeppelin-shaped airship used for advertising purposes.
