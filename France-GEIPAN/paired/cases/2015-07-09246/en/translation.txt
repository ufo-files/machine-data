Description: On Sunday, July 19, 2015, around 12:15 a.m., a witness observed for 30 seconds a bright, orange-red sphere moving rapidly from west to east before disappearing into the clouds. He later noticed light irregularities in the photos he took. Five other people witnessed the phenomenon, but only one well-documented questionnaire was collected. Based on the objective elements outlined in the investigation report (see attached document), namely: - Orange-red color identical to that of lanterns. - Short observation duration (approximately 30 seconds) consistent with the lifespan of lanterns. - Movement in the direction of the dominant wind. - Day: a Saturday night, a day consistent with those conducive to the launch of lanterns for celebrations. - Illusion of disappearance caused by the lantern's extinction or its entry into the cloud layer. - Absence of noise. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," indicating a probable observation of a Thai lantern.
