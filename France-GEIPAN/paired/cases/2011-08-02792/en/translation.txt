Description: On August 2, 2011, while filming the landscape, a person was perplexed to see a phenomenon pass very quickly in front of their lens, which they were unable to identify. No noise was heard. They submitted their photos to the gendarmerie. The video clearly shows, in the last two seconds, an insect crossing the field of view. The very fleeting phenomenon that appears at the 5th second is very likely also a yellow insect passing even closer to the lens than the one at the end of the video.
