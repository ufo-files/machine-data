Description: The witness looks out their window early in the morning and sees a very bright orange light, relatively static, between two trees. The phenomenon disappears after two minutes. This is the observation of the star Capella, which that night is at an altitude and direction (15°N N) consistent with the description of a bright point ("star-like") that "does not move or moves very little." In particular, the low altitude of 15° is conducive to changing colors (flicker). The witness reports the presence of clouds, which explains the disappearance of PAN after 2 minutes. The witness indicates a North direction, but the layout of the streets and buildings is conducive to a "VELUX" oriented N NE, and the only large trees between which a PAN could have been seen are located in the N NE direction. Consequently, GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Astronomical observation of Capella.
