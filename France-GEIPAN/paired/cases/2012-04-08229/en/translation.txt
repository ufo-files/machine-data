Description: A witness reports their observations of the sky on April 8, 2012, first around 10 PM then around 11:30 PM. The witness films their final observation. The direction indicated by the witness (the Jura Mountains) at an azimuth of approximately 290° coincides perfectly with the position of the planet Venus, which the witness does not mention. The planet Venus, particularly visible at this time, surprises many witnesses when it is low on the horizon: it is then interpreted as an object flying over the Earth. The same celestial body, observed by the witness 1.5 hours earlier, was then high in the sky; it was not interpreted by the witness as a strange object. Regarding the video: a bright point on a black background filmed by a video camera with the zoom all the way in produces numerous optical and electronic artifacts: the diamond-shaped form is the result of the camera's diaphragm shape, which appears when focus is incorrect (at night, many cameras in autofocus mode unsuccessfully try to focus, and alternate between blurry and clear images); the colors are more due to electronic noise and parasites and optical aberrations (even with a very good quality camera) than to the image of the filmed object. Therefore, the video does not provide us with much information about the observed object. This case is classified as "A": observation of the planet Venus.
