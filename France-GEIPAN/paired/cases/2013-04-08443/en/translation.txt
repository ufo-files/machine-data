Summary: Observation of the movement of a bright point in the sky: ISS passage.
--- seg-ebf7dd3783bab0bd803b101c [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 23, 2013 at 21:42, a witness observed a bright point located near the Moon moving vertically at a constant speed until it disappeared approximately 300 meters above the ground. This testimony clearly suggests a pass by the International Space Station (ISS). A check with specialized sites (Calsky, Heavens-above) confirms the station's pass, but 5 minutes earlier. When questioned about this discrepancy, the witness confirmed by email that his clock was 5 minutes fast. This case is therefore classified as "A": observation of the International Space Station (ISS). See the attached Calsky predictions for that evening, and the simulation of the station's pass in the sky, from the witness's perspective.
