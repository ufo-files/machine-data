Description: On August 5, 2007, a significant gathering of hot air balloons took place at the former military base of Chambley-Bussière (54), attracting thousands of spectators and resulting in thousands of photographs. Following the event, one participant discovered a suspicious spot on one of his approximately 190 photos, which did not appear to be an aircraft, bird, or artifact from the camera. He contacted private investigator Mr. Christian Comtesse, and the GEIPAN was only notified through media coverage of the event, known as the "Chambley OVNI." A case of observation consisting of a single photograph with an anomaly, without visual observation at the time of capture, makes in-depth investigation difficult: the lack of precise information leads to numerous hypotheses but without the possibility of verifying them, and if there are not at least two independent sources of information, the case of observation presents little interest a priori. Locally, Mr. Christian Comtesse, who had received only one photo, sought to learn more. He collected numerous other photos, and the same spot appears on another photograph. Specifically, one of the photos collected during Christian Comtesse's investigation is relatively rich in information (presence of many balloons at various distances). It served as the basis for a photo analysis procedure study entrusted to François Louange, a specialist in the field. The complete analysis of the photo is presented in an annex document (Louange Photo Analysis Report), and the result is that it is an object of a size between 75 cm and 1 meter placed approximately 300 meters from the photographer. A plausible hypothesis is that of a runaway child's balloon. The tenacity of C. Comtesse will be rewarded, as he will eventually, in March 2008, have the testimony of a pilot of one of the hot air balloons, a visual witness of the object. This witness confirms that the object is a child's balloon in the shape of a fish, approximately 80 cm in size. The report from Christian Comtesse submitted to the GEIPAN is attached (Chambley OVNI Investigation Report). This case, ultimately classified as A, is exemplary in several respects: - it demonstrates the importance of collaboration between the GEIPAN and local investigators, a collaboration now established by the GEIPAN with First Level Intervenors (FLI): indeed, without the collaboration of Christian Comtesse, this case of a photograph without visual observation would not have been thoroughly investigated by the GEIPAN and may have remained unexplained (and likely classified as C). - it validates a posteriori the method of analyzing photographs developed by François Louange for the GEIPAN.
