--- seg-f43e37fc4122d54b22aa2bd0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 11, 1976, at approximately 10:05 PM, two individuals witnessed a stationary, circular, bright red light phenomenon. Due to the distant observation (around 2 kilometers), no details could be discerned. While one of the witnesses was looking for their binoculars, the phenomenon moved towards the West and then disappeared. No other testimonies were collected. The gendarmerie investigation that visited the site with the witnesses determined the presence of a 50-meter dirt path that connects to the West with an unclassified, unknown rural path to the witnesses. The hypothesis of a vehicle passing is considered. However, given the observation distance and the lack of detailed elements, no information allows for the identification of the phenomenon.
