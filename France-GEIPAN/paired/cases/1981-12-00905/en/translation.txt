Summary: observation of a luminous phenomenon at the edge of a forest.
--- seg-4aa4a7bf7037be0d88567683 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 21, 1981, around 8:00 PM, two individuals observed a peculiar lighting phenomenon in the forest facing their home, which intrigued them. This rectangular-shaped object was orange-red in color and remained stationary. During the observation that lasted nearly an hour, the witnesses could see a green halo gradually surround the rectangle, followed by two very powerful lights emitting yellow light. The object slowly ascended into the air at a 45-degree angle before disappearing into the sky. No sounds were heard during the observation. The following day, one of the witnesses visited the site but found no trace. Although the gendarmes were notified 25 days later, no traces on the ground or in the surroundings could be found, and no other witnesses came forward to provide additional information about the phenomenon.
