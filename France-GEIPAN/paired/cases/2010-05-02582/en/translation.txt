Description: On Sunday, May 23, 2010, at approximately 10:35 PM, a witness on their balcony on the 6th floor is intrigued by an unusual object in the Paris sky. The object "spins" silently and without lights, and is seen again at 10:41 PM. No other testimonies were collected. The spinning shape described by the witness is unlikely to be a balloon; a visual defect (phosphene, floating body) remains possible but impossible to prove. Of low to moderate strangeness, but lacking sufficient consistency due to inconsistent weather conditions, this observation case cannot be concluded without additional data. This case is classified "C" due to the lack of independent testimonies or cross-references.
