Description: On December 17, 1977, around midnight, several members of the same family observed for a few seconds, at their home's front door, the passage of a luminous phenomenon. A ball of yellow-orange color moving from N-E to South at extremely high speed. It left behind a brightness that illuminated the night. These people were most likely witnesses to an atmospheric reentry.
