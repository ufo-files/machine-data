Summary: Observation of a PAN consisting of a juxtaposition of green lights arranged in a rose pattern and a mobile green light beam in the overcast night sky: probable observation of a recreational drone.
--- seg-892fe34613637f05c1e2036e [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 28, 2018, at 02:45, the witness is awakened to tend to her sick husband. His attention is drawn to a very intense glow seen almost directly north through the living room window. The witness sees an arrangement of green lights in a rose pattern and a green glowing ray that moves in all directions, scanning the surroundings. The PAN lights up and completely turns off and moves several times. No noise was heard during the movements of the PAN, but the witness never opened the living room window. The PAN then heads towards the witness, who, frightened, crouches against the wall of the living room. When the witness looks out the window again, the PAN is gone. The observation lasted between 30 and 45 seconds. No further testimony will be collected. The description translated to an unfamiliar strangeness to the GEIPAN (see the investigation report). A plausible hypothesis could be established in view of the appearance and behavior of PAN, namely the use of a drone equipped with LEDs and a laser beam. Internet examples show toy drones (available at this address) with a laser beam in motion at night, with characteristics that closely match the observation: - the beam's "movement in all directions," - the green color, - the beam's thickness; the witness's observation of a 30 cm thickness corresponds well to a laser beam, while the observation of small particles within the beam is characteristic of light diffraction in the presence of aerosols or microscopic fine particles. These examples of these toy drones do not present an aspect of a "green light rosace" surrounding the green beam. But, the addition of green LEDs (the modular possibilities are multiple) on various ends of the drone can by far create such an effect. Angular and distance estimates made using the testimony and photographs taken during the cognitive interview suggest a sufficiently large drone (1 m) such as those used professionally, or even militarily. The presence of this drone in a residential area at 2 AM can be understood as a playful use as long as such activity is prohibited and is intended to meet the fewest witnesses possible, and the sought-after sensation effect is stronger in areas where the beam can "play" with obstacles and facades. It understands much less for a professional or military drone. One may question the reason for such a whirling beam for one and the hypothesis of particular training operations for the other, which clashes with the fact that the authorities of the nearby training camp (Camp de Frileuse in Beynes) indicated an absence of operations of any kind that night. The presence of a toy drone is more likely, while the angular size suggests a professional drone. However, the overestimation of size (factor 2) by the witness is not excluded despite the reliability of the references used (space between two trees), as it is also possible that a professional drone could have been used for a playful purpose. The consistency of the testimony is very good, both in terms of information and reliability. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of a drone used for recreational purposes.
