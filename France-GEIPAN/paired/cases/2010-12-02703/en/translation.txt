Description: On December 31, 2010, around 11:30 PM, a witness observed the movement of an intense red spot. No particular noise was heard by the witness as the spot approached, then suddenly changed direction, and quickly moved away on an ascending trajectory. After viewing the video and photos on the GEIPAN website, the witness recognized that they had witnessed the passage of a Thai lantern.
