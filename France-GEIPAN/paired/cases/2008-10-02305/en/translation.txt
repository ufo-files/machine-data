Description: Following the acquisition of a new camcorder, a private individual conducted tests by filming a helicopter. Upon viewing, he noticed the presence of a gray sphere throughout the footage. Unfortunately, the witness did not wish for his video to be broadcast, although this document is an excellent example of the genre. This video, almost certainly, shows a very small object passing very close to the lens, without necessarily being noticed by the operator, very likely an insect or a bird. This type of document (photo or video) is quite common since the advent of digital cameras, and in particular compact cameras, whose very short focal length ensures that such small objects are not completely blurred and therefore "diluted" in the landscape, as they would be in analog photography or classic video.
