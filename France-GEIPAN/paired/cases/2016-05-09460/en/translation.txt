Description: On May 15, 2016, around 12:30 AM, while out for a walk with his dog, a witness observed at low altitude in the sky three orange lights arranged in a triangle, stationary, emitting a flash every second. The first light disappeared, the second and third rose very quickly and also disappeared. Only one testimony was collected. The passage of three orange-red balls on a weekend evening suggests the release of sky lanterns (often referred to as Chinese or Thai lanterns) following a wedding or private party (as detailed in the investigation report). The witness was looking predominantly south, and the objects moved from his left to his right, that is, from northeast to southwest; the wind was blowing at 233° (south), thus consistent with the direction taken by these lights (as per the meteorological report). One of the lights rose before disappearing. The shape described is consistent with a Chinese lantern, as is the color (orange then red). The witness's precision, "flash every second," accurately describes the flickering of flames. The fact that one of the "balls" rose towards the clouds is also compatible, as rising currents or calm conditions between buildings can give the illusion of inconsistent movement relative to the dominant wind, as evidenced by numerous videos online. The subsequent path taken by another ball confirms the phenomenon of wind currents. The GEIPAN (French UFO investigation group) concludes: observation of Thai lanterns.
