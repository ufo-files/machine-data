Description: On August 17, 2016, the GEIPAN received a questionnaire regarding an observation from 2012. On June 4, 2012, around 3:00 AM, a witness observed through the glass door of their living room an orange-colored, column-shaped luminescent phenomenon directed towards the Fourches hill in the commune of Langres. After fifteen seconds, the phenomenon abruptly disappeared, but the witness then saw two yellow, glowing orbs in motion around the location of the initial observation. These two glowing orbs also abruptly went out. The following day, the witness visited the presumed observation site but found no signs of burnt grass or trampling. Only one testimony was collected. This observation of a ground phenomenon at 3:00 AM on a hill 2 kilometers away is not unusual, as the witness themselves described it as a possible campfire or flame, and mentioned that the nearby chapel can accommodate scouts. It could also be an agricultural vehicle surrounded by people with flashlights. The fact that the witness did not notice any signs of burnt grass or trampling on the presumed site the following day does not rule out any of the previous hypotheses. The same witness made a similar observation in 2016 (moving ground lights around a central glow) on the same hill (a few dozen meters apart), an observation also of low strangeness and likely the same origin. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: ground lights of vehicle or campfire type, with people using flashlight torches.
