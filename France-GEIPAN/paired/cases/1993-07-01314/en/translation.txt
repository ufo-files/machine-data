Summary: Observation of crop circles
--- seg-10415fb5b2875475c246427b [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Around July 3, 1993, the owner of a wheat field noticed suspicious traces. The wheat was laid down in large circles connected by straight lines. After investigation, a witness, an architect landscape designer, explained that these were drawings he himself made and provided a step-by-step explanation of the process and method. He was not the author of these circles but the technique used was the same.
