Description: On Wednesday, November 9, 2011, at 21:05, several people in a car were intrigued by a quick glimpse of a blue phenomenon (described as a flash, a lightning bolt, or a half-circle). Arriving at their home and exiting the vehicle, one of the passengers observed again, for 5 to 7 seconds, a peculiar phenomenon in the sky: the witness described a large, stationary, orange wheel with a significant diameter. It disappeared instantly. None of the other witnesses saw this second phenomenon at that time. The hypothesis of a temporarily lit Sky Tracker (possibly accidentally: the observation lasted only 5 to 10 seconds), made particularly spectacular by the fog, seems plausible; but this hypothesis could not be confirmed by the nightclub manager and was completely refuted by the witness. Given these contradictions and the brevity of the observation, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C": lack of reliable information.
