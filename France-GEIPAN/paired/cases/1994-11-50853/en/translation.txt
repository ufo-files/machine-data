Description: A person testifies in 2010 about an observation made when they were a child on November 10, 1994, after 8:00 PM. While riding in the back of a vehicle on a rural road, they looked east and through the tops of trees in a forest and saw a very intense green light followed by a bright point of light right next to it. This object suddenly changed trajectory after a significant acceleration and disappeared heading north. No unusual noises were heard during the observation. No other witnesses were found for this phenomenon. Initially, the observation seemed to be a meteoroid or space debris re-entry (bolide) due to the green color and speed, which are consistent with such events. However, the change in direction was quite surprising: one might imagine a second bolide or shooting star that started randomly at the end of the previous phenomenon. Given the type of phenomenon and the age of the case, a posteriori investigation would not provide new elements. Since the GEIPAN lacks factual elements to confirm this speculative hypothesis, this case is classified as "C": Unidentified Phenomenon: The GEIPAN does not have enough elements to form a solid opinion.
