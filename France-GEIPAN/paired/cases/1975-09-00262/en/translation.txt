--- seg-5f8f1511eef539365bdcfb3f [needs-review] ---
Description: Following a press article, the gendarmerie investigated on October 1, 1975, an observation by several witnesses of a large orange phenomenon seen in the distance in the sky of the communes of Elesnes-Assevent-Boussois and Vieux-Reng on Friday, September 26, 1975. The gendarmerie investigation identified two individuals with contradictory testimonies. Photographs taken by one of the witnesses and sent to the press were found to be negative. These individuals likely observed a phenomenon related to the metallurgical region of Charleroi in Belgium, which is on the trajectory indicated by the witnesses. This case is classified as B.
