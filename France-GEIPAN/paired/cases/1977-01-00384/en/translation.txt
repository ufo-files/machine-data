Description: A motorist observed and photographed a peculiar meteorological phenomenon on January 17, 1977, around 5:00 PM. An oval-shaped cloud moved imperceptibly without a North-South orientation. Two other witnesses made the same observation. According to the photo attached to the police report, these individuals saw a lenticular cloud.
