Summary: Observation of the passage of a luminous phenomenon.
--- seg-f1284e0286bea58821115135 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 7, 1977, around 6:45 PM, several people observed the passage of a luminous phenomenon in the sky. It moved from west to east without any particular noise being heard. The phenomenon appeared as an elongated shape emitting white light. Then, along its trajectory, this shape gave way to a red glowing point that gradually faded and disappeared. During the investigation, the gendarmerie found no trace on the ground of the phenomenon's passage. A witness also reported seeing, on December 4 at 10:15 PM, a ball of orange color passing through the sky. If these testimonies resemble descriptions of atmospheric reentries, the information about these phenomena remains too imprecise to validate this hypothesis.
