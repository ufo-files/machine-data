Description: On April 3, 2015, at 4:15 PM, a motorist and her two passengers were intrigued by the slow, silent, and low-altitude flight of an aircraft they did not recognize. The GEIPAN consulted military air traffic control regarding this observation; it turns out that on that day at 4:00 PM, a military aircraft was flying at an extremely low altitude (2,100 feet, or approximately 700 meters) over Lapeyrouse, on a north/northwestern heading. Despite discrepancies between the witnesses' statements and the information provided by air traffic control regarding the event's timing (15 minutes) and altitude estimates, the exceptional presence of this aircraft fully explains the witnesses' observation. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A," indicating a certain observation of a military aircraft.
