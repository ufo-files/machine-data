Description: On June 13, 2015, at 11:20 PM, several people on a terrace observed in the sky an orange-colored shape moving silently on a straight trajectory. At 12:05 AM, two witnesses again saw in the sky the silent movement of an orange shape. Intrigued, they called the gendarmerie, which dispatched a patrol. Only one testimony about this phenomenon was collected. The observation of orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, probably released nearby after a private party. The movement was first observed from Northwest to Southwest, then from East to West, which is consistent with the light North wind at 11:20 PM shifting to Northeast at 12:00 AM, as recorded at Orly (see the Météociel records for the observation time and the one for 1 AM on June 14th, Météociel). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of Thai lanterns.
