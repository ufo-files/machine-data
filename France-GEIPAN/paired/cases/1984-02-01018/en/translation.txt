Description: On February 12, 1984, a motorist and his passenger will catch sight of a cylindrical shape emitting a sparkling light for 3 seconds. This object, which according to witnesses resembles a javelin, crosses the sky at a very high speed and will be lost from view before reaching the horizon. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon, which based on witness descriptions appears to be a re-entry atmosphere event.
