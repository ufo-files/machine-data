Description: On June 21, 2013, a witness observed and photographed a stationary object with several changing and flashing colors in the night sky heading west. The photographs were taken between 04:23 and 04:30; the witness also kindly sent us two day photographs taken from the same location and in the same direction. Based on the elements outlined in the investigation report (see document), particularly the color of the PAN, the very pronounced twinkling effect, and the height and azimuth position, this case is classified as 'A' for a certain observation of the star Arcturus, low on the horizon and subject to twinkling effects.
