--- seg-edac6be6d5a96ec473e8be94 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 16, 2008, between 9:45 PM and 11:00 PM, a witness was intrigued by seeing, between two house roofs in the S-SW, a bright (flashing) yellow-orange light that appeared every two or three minutes in a restricted area. With low strangeness and average consistency (single witness, but with a well-detailed and precise report), this case turns out to be a certain misidentification with vehicle headlight reflections bouncing off a nearby house antenna. This explanation was indeed provided by the witness personally, following a later similar observation. This case is classified A, misidentification with automobile headlight reflections.
