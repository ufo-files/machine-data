Description: On July 28, 1986, at approximately 10:00 PM, two individuals driving towards Cazals observed in a clear sky for about ten seconds three stationary and silent balls or discs, bright and glowing yellow. Deciding to turn around, the witnesses saw nothing more in the sky. Only one person provided a brief testimony. This 1986 case was reported to the GEIPAN in 2012. No straightforward explanation for the phenomenon was found. Investigating cases of this nature after over 25 years is, of course, difficult to envisage. The GEIPAN cannot make a determination: this case is classified "C" due to lack of information.
