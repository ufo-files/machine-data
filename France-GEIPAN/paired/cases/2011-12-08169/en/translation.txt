Summary: Observations of the atmospheric reentry of the 4th stage of the Soyuz-FG launch vehicle.
--- seg-c01e920a6aeae06b963affc5 [needs-review] ---
Description: The GEIPAN received by email several testimonies of spectacular light trails in the sky on December 24, 2011, in the late afternoon. Additionally, four observations by airplane pilots are reported to the GEIPAN: all describe a strong light phenomenon observed at very high altitude above the Strasbourg - Black Forest region. The official U.S. website for information on space debris reentries, reentrynews.aero, indicates that object numbered 2011 078B entered the atmosphere at 4:26 PM UTC, thus at the same time as the observations. The last orbit indicated barely crosses the observation locations. The debris in question is identified as the fourth stage of the Soyuz-FG launcher launched on December 21, 2011, at 1:16 PM UTC from the Baikonur cosmodrome, for the mission TMA-03M: sending 3 cosmonauts to the ISS. All witnesses clearly observed the reentry of the fourth stage of the Soyuz-FG launcher launched on December 21, 2011.
