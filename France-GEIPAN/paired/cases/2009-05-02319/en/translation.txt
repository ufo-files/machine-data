Summary: Observation of the silent passage of a triangular flying object: lack of information.
--- seg-82b3ac563b43c54ff750e8aa [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 3, 2009, around 10:15 PM, a witness is intrigued by the silent passage overhead of an object above their home. Flashing white lights at each corner define a dark triangular shape for the witness, and a fixed red light is also seen. The witness calls their spouse, who only sees two stationary white lights and a red light receding into the sky. The first witness watches the object for approximately 4-5 minutes. No other testimonies are collected. No civilian aircraft flights are reported in the air space of the communes cited by the witness. Following investigation, based on the gendarmerie report, gathered elements, field visits, witness testimony, and conducted research, this case appears complex, with three observation phases that are not necessarily linked, and also inconsistent as the sole witness provides a narrative that is not always coherent (the lengthy investigation period is a significant factor in this issue). However, the spouse's testimony is noted, but only regarding the end of the observation, the least unusual part. It is disturbing that the witness does not mention the moon, which was exactly within their field of observation; it is nearly certain that this celestial body played a part in the phenomenon, but it cannot be clearly stated how. But this cannot explain the fixed red light observed by both witnesses. A complex scenario involving fireworks, followed by a misidentification with the moon through clouds coinciding with an aircraft passage, could be considered, but several aspects do not align. Given these various uncertainties, and without being able to even establish that the phenomenon is truly strange, the GEIPAN classifies this case as 'C' due to a lack of reliable information.
