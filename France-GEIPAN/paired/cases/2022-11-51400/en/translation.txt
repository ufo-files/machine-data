Description: On November 8, 2022, at 07:45, during a trip on D944, near the town of Les Petites-Loges (51), a witness observed a very bright point moving quickly towards the west, without a trail, and then disappearing. The phenomenon lasts for a short time, approximately 1 to 2 seconds. Only one testimony will be collected. The consistency is average: only one witness, no photo or video of the PAN (due to the brevity of the phenomenon). The investigation was able to demonstrate through analysis (see the investigation report) that the witness observed the entry into the atmosphere of a small meteor, similar to a shooting star. This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- The appearance of the PAN (color, brightness, etc.) is consistent with that of a meteor
- The observation duration was very short
- The trajectory was straight

When it comes to meteors of modest size and characteristics that more closely resemble a simple shooting star, as is likely the case here, fewer observers tend to report their sightings, which often remain less spectacular. It is therefore logical that no report was filed with the IMO (International Meteor Organization). Additionally, the trigger threshold of the automated cameras likely plays a role in the absence of a recording at the time of the observation. The main strangeness for the witness, who thought it was a meteor, is the absence of a trail behind the PAN. Or the generation of a trail is not systematic. Some meteors, depending on their size, the entry angle into the atmosphere, and the potential perspective influenced by their position and the witness's, do not produce or produce indistinguishable trails with the naked eye. The UFO protected GEIPAN classifies this case as A: observation of a meteor.
