--- seg-201cf3328013e533e39f99d9 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: During a fair on October 27, 2013, a witness observed and photographed a black object moving very slowly under the clouds. Several people then observed the phenomenon, but only one testimony was collected. The witness described the object as: a black to gray, oval object that changes shape, resembling a complexly shaped balloon turning with the wind. Due to it being observed against a clear sky, it appears extremely dark, regardless of its actual color. The wind at Luc-Cannet des Maures (Source: Meteociel) was variable, light, changing from East in the morning, North at noon, and West in late afternoon; this is not entirely consistent with the observed movement, but it is extremely difficult to estimate wind in a mountainous area with light winds. Despite this uncertainty, given the similarity of the object to a balloon, likely related to a local festival, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "unremarkable" category "B": probable observation of an helium-filled festive balloon.
