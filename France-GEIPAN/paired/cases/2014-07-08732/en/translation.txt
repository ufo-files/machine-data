Description: On July 8, 2014, at 9:33 PM, a witness is intrigued by the straight-line passage of a very bright rectangular shape. During the 6 to 8 seconds of observation, the fast object moves silently. No other witnesses were reported. The witness observes a yellow rectangle advancing above the horizon. The sun has set for 8 minutes. The witness looks towards the southeast and sees the object pass from left to right (refer to his drawings). A plane, illuminated from the side by the setting sun, appears as a very bright rectangle (the wings do not receive the sun's rays directly, the drift, level, reflects the sun in a different direction than the cylindrical fuselage). The gray halo around the object may be an atmospheric optical phenomenon, quite complex at low elevation, or a local blinding phenomenon at the observer's eye level. The Flightradar record indicates an airplane that could be the subject of this observation: the AF Orly-Marseille flight in approach to Marignane. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of an airplane at sunset.
