Description: On July 18, 2009, around 10:40 PM, a witness observed above the horizon approximately twenty orange glowing orbs that moved horizontally together. No noise was heard. The orbs disappeared from the witness's view due to the landscape. The characteristics of the described observation, twenty non-blinding glowing orbs moving slowly and together, strongly validate the observation as a formation of flying lanterns.
