Summary: Observation of the noisy passage of 3 orange lights delineating a dark triangle: observation of military aircraft in exercise.
--- seg-f60263ef88d10816c7da2b0b [needs-review] ---
Description: The main witness (T1) observed, from the garden of his house located in Loctudy (29), on May 13, 2025 around 23:00, a fixed orange light passing over him, followed by two blinking orange lights, all delineating a very dark, fairly large triangle, moving at low altitude quite slowly from north to south. The PAN emits a fairly loud rumble, similar to a distant thunderstorm, that remains audible after the lights pass. The observation (visual + auditory) lasted a total of 9/10 minutes, according to T1. The neighbor of T1, and her companion also observed the PAN and heard the rumble, however they did not report their observations to GEIPAN. The witness consulted specialized aeronautical tracking sites, but identified no aircraft overflying his position at the time of the observation. Of average consistency: only one witness completed the questionnaire and no photos or videos of PAN are available. This GEIPAN case concerns the observation of aircraft engaged in the military exercise "Poker" (see the investigation report). This conclusion is based on the following elements: - Verified passage by the study of the radar return map provided by CAPCODA* of a set of several aircraft over the witnesses shortly before 23:00. Five of them are present in an identical time slot and three of them have speeds and altitudes close to each other. - A slight shift in altitude and position of these three aircraft can, from the ground, give the impression that they form a triangle, creating the illusion for T1 of a single, larger, closer object, of triangular shape and dark color. - The lights observed (one steady and two flashing) likely correspond to position lights and anti-collision lights. The perceived orange hue could actually be a "white-orange" color. - The rumble heard is consistent with the passage of such a formation of aircraft and may seem unusual from the ground. The sensation of slowness is due to a moderate speed for fighters (approximately 700 to 750 km/h) at a relatively high altitude (6 to 7 km). The dark triangular shape mentioned by T1 is very likely due to a shape perception illusion that occurs at night when a witness observes multiple identical point light phenomena. These military aircraft are usually not visible on public trajectory sites, which explains why T1 did not spot them during his subsequent searches. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A": observation of military aircraft. CONSISTENCY: According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. CAPCODA: Air Defense and Operations Planning and Control Center (Air and Space Army).
