--- seg-2b9111c9d4e68c735c26685f [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 28, 1989, around 8:30 PM, the Gendarmerie Group of Guéret requested that all its brigades look up at the sky towards the East for a particular phenomenon. Very bright, multicolored points were observed. These observations were made over several consecutive evenings. The phenomenon was caused by starlight scintillation due to favorable weather conditions.
