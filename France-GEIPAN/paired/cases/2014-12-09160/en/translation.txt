Summary: Observation and photography of an orange-colored oval light phenomenon in the sky: Hoax.
--- seg-26c07f618fd8a003ff0faae9 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 16, 2014, around 2 a.m. to 2:15 a.m., a witness had their attention drawn by a bright light outside. Moving to their terrace, they observed a yellow-gold, oval object slowly and silently advancing over the tops of trees across from their home. A photograph was taken during the observation, which lasted between 5 and 10 minutes. The object disappeared at high speed, departing vertically. The image presented as the witness's photograph was found to be a cropped version of a 1981 photograph taken by a Mexican photographer named Carloz Diaz (see the complete photograph on 1981: The Beginning of Contact). The witness was evasive in response to inquiries, providing no specific details or clear answers to the questions asked (see investigation notes). Given the evidence and the lack of precise answers to the questions posed to the witness, we consider this to be a hoax. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Hoax.
