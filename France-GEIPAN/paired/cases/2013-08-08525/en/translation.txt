Description: On the night of August 9, 2013, midnight, several people were stargazing and were surprised to see a light, gray triangular object moving northeast to southwest at a low altitude without making any noise. Only one brief testimony was collected. This testimony is of very low consistency: it is imprecise and lacks detail, and it also lacks essential data needed for further study. The eyewitness is unique and there are no photos or videos to corroborate the observation. The observation could be somewhat strange, but too many inaccuracies and gaps in the testimony prevent a truly valid judgment of its oddity. Based on the information presented in the attached investigation report, the GEIPAN classifies this case as C (unidentified) due to a lack of information.
