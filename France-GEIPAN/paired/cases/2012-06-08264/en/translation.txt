Summary: Observation of a bright point moving across the sky: observation of the passage of the International Space Station.
--- seg-4dc430a03f1e6288533d3636 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 20, 2012, around 10:50 PM, a witness is intrigued by the silent passage across the sky of a bright point of light. He films his observation. After considering and ruling out the hypotheses of a plane seen head-on preparing to land at Roissy, and the passage of a Thai lantern (wind is against it), the GEIPAN ultimately chose the hypothesis of the ISS: clear weather, and the passage of the International Space Station, very well visible from Paris (see Calsky log), rising at 10:46 PM, precisely in the direction indicated by the witness, at its highest point at 10:51 PM, the time indicated by the witness. This case is classified "A": Perfectly identified observation of the ISS.
