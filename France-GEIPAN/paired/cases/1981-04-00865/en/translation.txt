Description: On April 7, 1981, around 8:00 PM, witnesses observed a fireball at an altitude of approximately 4,000 meters. It was followed by a colored trail and then seemed to descend into the area. No other testimony was collected. According to the descriptions they give of the phenomenon, these people were likely witnesses to an atmospheric reentry.
