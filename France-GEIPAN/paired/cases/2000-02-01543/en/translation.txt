Description: On the night of February 4 to 5, 2000, around 2 a.m., a witness driving a car observed a luminous phenomenon slowly moving and then stopping above some woods. The phenomenon was silent, and, according to the witness, was approximately 200 meters away and 150 meters in altitude. The observation lasted for about 30 to 40 seconds. No other information could be gathered.
