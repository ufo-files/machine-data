Description: On October 16, 1977, around 3:30 AM, a motorist and his passenger observed the O-E passage of a circular object of color. The movement was at high altitude and at high speed. The trajectory was curved, and the object disappeared behind vegetation. No signs of interference were noticed in the vehicle. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon.
