Description: On February 23, 2014, at 10:10 PM, a couple observed through a glass bay the silent and straight passage of a very bright light. Suddenly, the phenomenon made several upward and downward movements, and the light turned red before disappearing towards the West. The observation lasted between 2 and 3 minutes in the clear sky. The observation of a glowing light with red reflections, moving horizontally in the sky for several minutes, with small upward or downward movements, suggests a Thai lantern carried by the wind, likely released nearby after a private party. Meteociel indicates for Tours a high cloud cover (7/8) and an east wind of 17 to 28 km/h (see the weather . The wind direction is consistent with this hypothesis. However, it is noted that lantern releases are more frequent on Saturday nights than on Sundays. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
