--- seg-6ffcb5865756c9ea37d34c93 [needs-review] ---
Description: During the night of Friday, June 25, to Saturday, June 26, 2010, around 1 a.m., eight individuals observed in the Paris sky the silent, low-altitude, constant-speed movement of a very bright orange ball that suddenly plummeted in altitude and light intensity. Only one report was made to GEIPAN. Of low strangeness and low consistency (given the absence of independent testimony), this observation case cannot be concluded, although a strong presumption of a flying lantern remains in mind. This case is classified C due to the lack of independent testimony and cross-references to validate or refute the flying lantern hypothesis.
