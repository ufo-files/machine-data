Summary: Observation of three red lights in silent movement across the sky: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-bcc303070c91948fa6eebb90 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Wednesday, July 15, 2015, at 10:37 PM, a witness on a parking lot observed three very bright red lights forming a triangle and flying at low altitude. The object emerged from behind a forest, headed towards the witness's building, then made a 45-degree turn and disappeared from their field of vision. Only one testimony was collected. Given the elements gathered (see the investigation report), namely:

- The identical red color to that of lanterns.
- The number corresponding to a classic group release of lanterns.
- The simultaneous movement of the lanterns, carried by the wind and creating an illusion of psychological shape (movement in the direction of the dominant wind).
- Behavior identical to that of objects subject to no or low wind: moderate speed.
- Absence of noise.
- Short observation duration (10 to 15 seconds), compatible with the lifespan of lanterns.
- Proximity of installations conducive to lantern releases, located in the direction of observation and not visible from the witness's position.

The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," indicating a probable observation of Thai lanterns.
