Description: On July 19, 2015, around midnight, two people observed through binoculars a line of several orange lights in the sky. The objects shimmered, and the whole scene resembled a formation flight moving from West to East. One witness noticed a last light quickly joining the others. Only one testimony was collected. Given the elements gathered in the investigation report (see attached), namely: - the identical orange color to that of lanterns. - the behavior identical to that of objects subject to no or weak wind (moderate speed). - the short observation duration (around 1 minute), compatible with the lifespan of lanterns. - the date: a Sunday evening, compatible with those propitious for the organization of lantern releases on celebrations. - the number compatible with that of lanterns launched in quantity. - the illusion of acceleration and disappearance caused by the extinction of lanterns or entry into the cloud layer. - the absence of noise. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B" as a very probable observation of Thai lanterns.
