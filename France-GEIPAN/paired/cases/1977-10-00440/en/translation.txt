Description: On October 12, 1977, at 5:45 a.m., a motorist observed in the sky an object surrounded by an orange halo that was vibrating or oscillating. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected by the gendarmerie or the Floirac Observatory (33). Although the testimony is very brief, this person likely observed the rising sun through a cloud (which rose that day at 5:20 a.m.).
