Description: A witness reports in March 2015 an observation made between August 20th and 25th, 2004, while they were a front-seat passenger in a vehicle. At the exit for Saint-Maximin-la-Sainte-Baume (83), the witness observed through the windshield the passage of nine white lights that seemed to delineate a large dark mass. The trajectory was straight. Only one testimony was collected. Given the position and aspect of the Moon described by the witness (full moon, to the right of the highway, high in the sky), the observation likely took place on August 26th, 27th, 28th, or 29th (see sky maps). The appearance and behavior of the phenomenon described by the witness resemble a low-altitude plane, with all lights on, preparing for a landing. We note that the witness observed very close to the aeronautical reference point OGREN (see map), which serves as a reference for the approach trajectory to the Marignane airport, located 50 km away. Therefore, planes at medium or low altitude, with all lights on, pass through this area very frequently. The witness, focused on the landing lights, may not have noticed the blinking lights, which are less powerful. However, it is surprising that the witness could make such a mistake, having worked in the aviation field; but one does not have the same references and reflexes outside of a professional context. This hypothesis of a plane cannot be verified due to the uncertainty about the date and, more importantly, the delay of over 10 years between the events and the testimony. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of information.
