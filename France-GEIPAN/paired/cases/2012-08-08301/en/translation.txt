Description: A witness is intrigued by the slow movements of a very bright phenomenon in the sky. He makes these observations on the evenings of August 8th and 9th, 2012. The witness's description and the passage times are entirely compatible with the observation of International Space Station (ISS) passages on the evenings of August 8th and 9th, 2012 (see Calsky ISS passage records on August 8th from 21:31 to 21:40 and then from 23:06 to 23:15, and on August 9th from 22:13 to 22:24 and then from 23:50 to 23:57), to be compared with the observation dates and times (around 22:30 and 23:30). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation over two consecutive evenings, of the two successive passages of the International Space Station (ISS).
