Summary: Likely observation by numerous witnesses of an entry into the atmosphere.
--- seg-734c8fc4c0a2742f0bded3f1 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of September 4 to 5, 1988, around 1:15 a.m., numerous witnesses across several departments in central France, primarily in Burgundy, observed a bright, yellow or white light with a large, reddish trail that quickly crossed the sky for a few seconds. The phenomenon was particularly intense in Yonne, where many witnesses were able to observe it. Depending on their geographical position, witnesses heard the sound of 4 or 5 explosions, ranging from loud to faint, at the moment the phenomenon disappeared. This explosion was particularly well-perceived in Nièvre and Yonne, but the phenomenon was also observed as far as Essonne. The phenomenon is likely a major atmospheric re-entry.
