--- seg-73b9e156f2a9b38d31ef553d [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 16, 2014, at 21:15, a witness took two photographs of a white, spherical, luminous phenomenon. The phenomenon's appearances and disappearances intrigued the witness. The observation lasted 5 minutes. The witness sent us two photographs, but we did not receive the video also taken during the observation. No other testimonies were reported. Based on the evidence gathered during the investigation (see the investigation report), specifically the azimuth measurement of the PAN aligning with the approach trajectory of planes to Nice Airport, the corresponding altitude matching that of planes approaching, and the observation distance only allowing the distinction of one or more landing lights, the case is classified as "A," certain observation of a commercial airliner approaching Nice Airport.
