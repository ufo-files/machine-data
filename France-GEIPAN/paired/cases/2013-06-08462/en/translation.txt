Description: On June 6, 2013, at 8:50 a.m., while walking in the street, a witness noticed the presence in a sunny sky of an oval, beige and gray object spinning on itself. No unusual noises were heard during the observation of the object, which remained stationary. Another person also made the observation. Only one testimony was collected. The witness observed a stationary but spinning shape in the sky for 6 minutes. This description clearly evokes a helium-filled captive balloon, probably a novelty balloon or an advertising balloon. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a captive novelty balloon.
