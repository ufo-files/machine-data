Description: On the 13th of August 2012, at 5 a.m., a witness leaving their home was intrigued by the sight of a white light phenomenon moving within the cloud layer. A large circle, formed by smaller circles, appeared cyclically and spun around, changing direction. No sound was heard. The observation lasted 20 minutes at the witness's location before they got into their vehicle and continued to observe the phenomenon while driving. The phenomenon witnessed by the witness resembles a light show of this nature: a laser animation (the "ascending" beam is visible here as the observer is close to the projector; a distant observer would only see the glowing discs on the clouds). This animation is not unexpected in this area during peak tourist season, even at this early morning hour when nightclubs are still open. The presence of the nightclub "Asiana" in the direction of the witness's observation (West) is noted. The weather conditions (overcast 8/8, see record) are entirely favorable for the use of such devices. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a light animation.
