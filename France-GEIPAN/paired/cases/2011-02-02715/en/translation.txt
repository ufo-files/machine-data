Description: On February 26, 2011, at 7:45 PM, a witness is intrigued by the presence in the sky of a very bright white object. This object moves on an east-west axis but makes ascending and descending movements. The observation lasts for three minutes before the object, descending, merges into the night. No other testimony about this phenomenon is collected. The observed phenomenon presents a certain degree of strangeness due to the complex movements of the observed point. The hypothesis of a helicopter or a military plane can be considered, but the GEIPAN handled this case too late to verify it. Due to lack of information and the uniqueness of this testimony, this case is classified as "C".
