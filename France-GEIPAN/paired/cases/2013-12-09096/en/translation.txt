Description: Recent (02/08/2015) testimony of an old observation. On December 24, 2014, at 8:30 PM, a witness observed the slow and silent passage of at least 5 orange glowing orbs. The observation of orange orbs crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a festive night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby for Christmas. The observed movement was towards Southeast, while a strong southwest wind was noted in Geneva (see weather). In a mountainous area, it is very difficult to model low-altitude wind. The observed southeast movement of objects carried by the wind is not incompatible with a strong southwest wind at ground level in Geneva. Furthermore, the witness states that he is observing straight south facing Mont Blanc, while Mont Blanc is southeast (Az 126°). An uncertainty of around 45° is probable. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of Thai lanterns.
