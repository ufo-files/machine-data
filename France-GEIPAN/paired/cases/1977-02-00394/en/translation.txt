--- seg-f9503c2b8e6327734cccb7e4 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 9, 1977, around 6:50 PM, several people observed an unusual light in the sky heading southeast, almost at the level of the mountain's ridge line. At first stationary, this spherical-shaped light gradually ascended, changing color, and then disappeared suddenly. When contacted by phone, the gendarmes at their station reported no abnormal light or noise associated with the phenomenon, for which we have limited information.
