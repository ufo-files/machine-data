Summary: Observation of the steady speed movement of a white, luminous airship-shaped object: probable misidentification with an airplane.
--- seg-8bd06df60fea639407db6e0b [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 17, 2015, at 7:04 a.m., a witness observed for one minute a very bright white object resembling a dirigible moving at a constant speed at high altitude. It gradually disappeared towards the South. The observation lasted one minute. The witness filmed the phenomenon with his smartphone and remains intrigued. The video (see the investigation note) clearly shows a commercial airliner moving at cruising altitude in a stable atmosphere, which prevents the formation of a condensation trail and also contributes to the apparent disappearance (and confusion with the surrounding blue sky) of areas in shadow under the plane or less well lit by the sun than the fuselage. Here, the wings disappear, giving this "dirigible" aspect. It was not possible (too late analysis) to access flight records to confirm the presence of the plane and its identity. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable misidentification with an airplane.
