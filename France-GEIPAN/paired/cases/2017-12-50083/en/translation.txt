Summary: Observations of three very bright points moving from west to east in a clear sky: observations of elements of the Dragon resupply spacecraft for the International Space Station (ISS).
--- seg-62b666dfc971e2b21348e5d5 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 15, 2017, at 6:33 PM, two witnesses were intrigued by the movement of three very bright points in the sky. The three points were perfectly aligned and moving from West to East. The observation lasted 30 seconds. The two witnesses observed three objects on the same orbit, namely the Dragon resupply spacecraft for the International Space Station (ISS), accompanied by the two protection elements of its solar generators. The launch of this mission (CRS 13) took place from Florida at 4:36 PM (local time in France) and the observation occurred approximately two hours later, explaining why the three objects were still very close. These three objects are listed by the US Space Command under numbers 43060 to 43062 (international designation 2017-080 A to C). Images including these three same objects plus the upper stage of the launcher had indeed been published on the Web during the launch of the CRS 11 mission (see the video Dragon resupply Mission CRS 11). In this case, as the upper stage had already performed its re-entry burn, only three objects remained visible. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: elements of the Dragon resupply spacecraft for the International Space Station (ISS).
