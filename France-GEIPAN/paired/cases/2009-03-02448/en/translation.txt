Description: On March 30, 2009, at 17:10, a family in a car was intrigued by the rapid and silent passage of a luminous phenomenon that followed a straight trajectory in the sky. With an oval shape, light green in color, and very bright, the phenomenon was followed by a silvery trail. This observation case has the characteristics of a bolide (entry of a meteoroid into the atmosphere). Unfortunately, the absence of other witnesses does not allow this hypothesis to be confirmed. This case is classified as B: observation of a bolide, probably due to the entry of a meteoroid (meteorite).
