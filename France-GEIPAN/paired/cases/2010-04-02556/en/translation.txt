Description: A witness reports observing a slow-moving red glowing point on April 14, 2010, between 21:50 and 21:56. The characteristics of the observation, including a slowly ascending red glowing point, becoming stationary, and then disappearing, suggest the release (now well-known) of a Thai-style flying lantern. Unfortunately, follow-up information requested by the GEIPAN from the primary witness (a gendarmerie protocol that would have allowed for a preliminary investigation) was not pursued. The case is classified C due to a lack of information.
