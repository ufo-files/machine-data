Description: A witness reports various observations of PANs (presumed Unidentified Flying Objects) in the sky, including one on March 5, 2017, over the sea. On that day, the witness observes a yellow-orange cloud for 30 minutes, within which a black sphere appears; the entire formation changes shape during the observation. Only one testimony was collected. This testimony is unusable for several reasons:

- The information provided is imprecise and relates without clear distinction to various observations.
- Photos/videos of the sky, supposedly showing the PANs and "invaders" in the sky, correspond to the movements of small, lit clouds at sunset (though the unlit, shadowed black clouds have similar shapes) or they correspond to airplanes (which is intriguing here because they have wings and a black tail...).
- The sense of strangeness experienced by the witness seems to greatly exceed what the sky is actually showing, and thus the capabilities of GEIPAN. For example, the witness insisted on providing us with somewhat unintelligible videos, but enough to understand that it was an exorcism.

Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as C: lack of reliable information.
