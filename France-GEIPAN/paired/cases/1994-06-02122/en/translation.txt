Summary: Observation of lights at night.
--- seg-3c2b83e2179e29a09fdbf08e [needs-review] ---
Description: Recent testimony (on June 3, 2009) of an older observation (on June 23, 1994 around 11:23 PM to 11:30 PM). A witness reports seeing for 5 to 10 minutes from a rooftop window, a grouping of 6 lights above the forest. These lights were first stationary then began rotating on an axis before disappearing. No noise was heard during the observation. The witness states that an EDF transformer on the electrical line that crosses this part of the forest had to be quickly replaced that night. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon has been collected, for which we lack information.
