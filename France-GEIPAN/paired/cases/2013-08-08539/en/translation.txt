Description: This testimony reports fixed bright points in the sky every evening, without further details. Also, a brief light phenomenon on August 13, 2013; only the latter phenomenon was considered, due to a lack of precise information for the initial phenomena, very likely related to stars. A strong light moving at high speed in the sky for 2 seconds clearly suggests a bolide, that is, the atmospheric entry of a fragment of an asteroid (meteoroid) or a satellite debris. An automatic camera from the BOAM meteor observers network is located in May-sur-Orne, approximately 65 km from the observation site. It recorded numerous bolides during that night, during the full Perseid meteor shower period. Three bolides corresponding roughly to the observation are found in a 5-minute slice around the reported observation time (2:35 local time). The GEIPAN classifies this observation as "A": bolide, meaning the atmospheric entry of a fragment of an asteroid (meteoroid) or a satellite debris.
