Description: On Sunday, April 21 at 10:10 PM, a couple was driving on D31 near Villeron (84). The front passenger was surprised to see multiple round, red-orange lights in the slightly cloudy sky heading east at low altitude. With the window open, the witness used her smartphone to record two videos of the observation while her husband continued driving. No unusual noises were heard. Suddenly, the lights disappeared. Her husband also observed the same thing, but less clearly since he was driving. Only one report of this phenomenon was collected. With a low strangeness and high consistency (two witnesses, two videos, fairly detailed testimony), this case appears to be a very probable misidentification with Thai lanterns (see the investigation report). The appearance of the PAN in the videos and its description in the testimony strongly resemble Thai lanterns: round, red-orange lights, very bright, observed during the weekend. The witness described the PAN as stationary, but it turns out that the lights had a slight apparent movement, which is entirely consistent with the absence of wind at the time of observation. Only the short duration of the observation and the disappearance of the PAN seem surprising, but the change in the road axis traveled by the witnesses explains this apparent strangeness. The case is classified B, very probable misidentification with Thai lanterns.
