Summary: Observation of an orange crescent in the sky: observing the Moon at sunset.
--- seg-76e694f927c1147c0eef8c46 [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 7, 2014 between 0:25 and 0:40, a person and his young daughter observed a yellow-orange object in the sky, in the shape of a crescent. The witness had the reflex to take pictures. The photograph presented by the witness perfectly matches the shape the Moon had that day; first quarter at 34%. The Moon was indeed setting, in the West-North-West (astronomical sunset at 0:40). It is surprising that the witness found this spectacle abnormal, and claimed that the Moon rises and sets on the other side of his house! (NB: The Moon, like the Sun, always rises close to the East and sets close to the West). Perhaps it was the fear expressed by his young daughter at such a late hour of the night that moved the witness. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the Moon at sunset.
