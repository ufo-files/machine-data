Description: A witness contacted the GEIPAN on March 31, 2009 regarding an observation of a yellow sphere seen on Sunday, March 22, 2009, and a similar observation on October 13, 2009. These two observations were analyzed together at the GEIPAN but processed in two separate files (see investigation report). The GEIPAN attempted to re-establish contact with the witness in 2012, but the phone number and email provided in 2009 were found to be unusable in 2012. Other than changes in direction, the testimony closely resembles the observation of an atmospheric reentry of satellite debris or a meteorite, or a festive lantern. We have here an observation of average strangeness, but the consistency of the whole does not allow for a D1 classification. This case is classified as C due to the lack of independent testimony and cross-referencing.
