--- seg-1262b5ea7f2a870e69515138 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A farmer observed, from a great distance, the passage of an object roughly the size of a semi-truck with numerous lights. No noise was heard during the observation. The witness was not wearing his glasses at the time. Nevertheless, his first impression made him think of a combine harvester rolling along with its work lights on (very likely in an agricultural field area). His unaided vision did not allow him to see the craft in detail as it traveled on the road. The estimated speed of 80 km/h seems obviously too high for a combine harvester, but not abnormal for a large, exceptional convoy carrying such a craft. The absence of noise is explicable by the wind direction (see weather situation) which carried the noise away from the witness. This case is classified B as a very likely observation of a large, terrestrial vehicle traveling on the road.
