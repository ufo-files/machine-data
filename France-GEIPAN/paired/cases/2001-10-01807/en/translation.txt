Description: A witness reports in 2008 an observation made on October 5, 2001: While hunting, the witness observes a long, transparent, undulating tube in the sky for several minutes. Initially thinking it an optical effect, the intrigued witness sees the object suddenly disappear. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon is collected. The described object resembles a thermal (solar) tubular balloon. However, due to lack of precise information, this case is classified as "C".
