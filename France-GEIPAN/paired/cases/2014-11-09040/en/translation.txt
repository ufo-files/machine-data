Description: The witness is driving on N13 from Chef-du-Pont to Carentan, heading southeast. He observes "like a comet" of white-yellow color. The witness states that the weather is clear, but the weather reports from Meteociel indicate rain in Cherbourg, and showers fading in Caen. The sun rose at 7:45 am E-SE (114°), that is, a little to the left of the N13 axis (approximately 135°). At 8:00, the witness therefore has the rising sun almost directly in front of him, possibly obscured by morning fog. The Flightradar24 site (see attached) at this time shows several large commercial aircraft flying at high altitude, coming from the West towards the East: an Airbus A330 with tail number DLH425, and a Boeing 777. The trails of these aircraft are visible up to about 120 km from the observer, where they appear with an elevation of approximately 6° from the horizon. At this distance, the apparent motion is very low, the trail appears very short due to perspective, and against the rising sun it appears yellow, orange, or even red in some cases. See: strange aircraft trails. These are the comet-shaped plane trails that the witness saw near the horizon. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": airplane trail observation.
