Description: On September 28, 2014, between 9:02 and 9:08 PM, a driver observed from his car the slow and silent movement of a dozen sparkling lights in the night sky. The lights moved in the same direction, at low altitude, and at the same speed. As they moved away, the witness described them as glowing red halos. Other drivers were present and parked on the road. No other testimonies were collected. The observation of orange or red balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from west to northeast (see the map), which is consistent with the south wind reported at Roissy (see the Météociel weather report for the observation date). Due to the relatively low brightness of these lanterns, witnesses generally estimate them as being farther away and larger than they actually are. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," a probable observation of Thai lanterns.
