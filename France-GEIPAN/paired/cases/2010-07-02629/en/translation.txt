Description: On the evening of July 16, 2010, at 11:30 PM, two individuals witnessed the passage of two luminous phenomena in the sky. One witness reported seeing a very bright white glow similar to a large star moving rapidly towards the northwest. After one minute, it faded and eventually disappeared. Intrigued, the witness decided to retrieve a pair of binoculars and upon his return, he observed the movement of another circular luminous phenomenon. Accelerations and oscillations were noted as the object moved away and disappeared. Relatively strange, this observation case exhibits many characteristics of satellite passage. However, due to a lack of precise information (exact time, archives of satellite passage bulletins for 2010) to validate this hypothesis, this case is classified as C.
