Summary: Observation of a sudden and intense light phenomenon in the sky: observation of the flash of an Iridium satellite.
--- seg-9fc9fc2ad0017bf33481cd80 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 19, 2013, around 11 PM, a witness observed a star that initially seemed to enter the atmosphere, then stopped, lit up the sky, and departed in the opposite direction of its initial trajectory. Based on the objective evidence gathered (see the investigation report), namely: - the exceptional brightness, 
- the identical color to that of a satellite,
- the visibility from the witness's position,
- the visibility timeline matching that of the presence of satellite Iridium 49, we can conclude that the witness's observation was of the Iridium flash caused by satellite "Iridium 49." This phenomenon is caused by the sun reflecting off the satellite's large antenna. The glare is so bright that it is even visible in broad daylight. This case is to be classified as "A," an observation of an Iridium satellite flash.
