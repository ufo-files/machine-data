--- seg-032a1901628924e2a1ac3ac7 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 25, 2014, at 12:45 AM, a witness watching through windows saw twice in succession, two red lights in motion from West to East. No particular noise was heard. The witness followed the phenomenon until it was lost in sight towards the Black Forest. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. The observation of red balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a festive night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, probably released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from West to East, which is consistent with the West wind, from 11 to 24 km/h recorded in Basel-Mulhouse at 1 AM on December 25 (see the Météociel record). However, it is noted that the wind was very unstable during the night, changing direction several times. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of Thai lanterns.
