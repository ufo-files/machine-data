Description: On January 28, 2017, between 5:07 and 5:10 a.m., a driver was intrigued by a luminous phenomenon in the clear sky. It appears 4 aligned white lights and 2 blinking lights of different colors. This observation is made through his windshield. Although driving at 110 km/h, the witness films what intrigues him with his mobile phone. The testimony first translates to a feeling of a sense of wonder or strangeness perceived by the witness. It is additionally (and perhaps related) very poorly documented. The video does not show any PAN, but appears to indicate that what intrigues the witness is generally facing him and therefore on his axis of travel. The witness, moreover, qualifies the movement of the PAN as its direction of movement. The PAN could be Capella exactly in that direction, the row of light and color scintillation could be caused by the windshield optics. The witness does not specify the angular width of the row of lights, nor whether the perception of the PAN's movement is due to an angular change or the idea that its trajectory is parallel to theirs (the movement attributed to a star when it is not recognized as such is that it moves with you!). The description is too vague to delve deeper: a row of lights seen through a windshield can have various explanations, including the burst light of a star or plane, reflection of an object in the vehicle... As a result, the GEIPAN concludes that the case is classified as C: the observation is not very strange and, above all, is unusable due to a lack of information.
