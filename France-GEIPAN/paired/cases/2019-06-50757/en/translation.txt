Summary: Observation of the silent movement of approximately thirty white lights in the night sky: probable misidentification with Thai lanterns.
--- seg-0716ab85ddfe3513549ae481 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Saturday, June 1, 2019, around 11:30 PM, a driver observed in the sky the movement of approximately thirty white lights in a clear sky. The witness stops to observe the phenomenon and hears no noise when he gets out of his vehicle. The entire light formation that appeared to the North eventually heads towards the West, and the lights disappear on the horizon. The motorist testifies at the GEIPAN and then, with the Gendarmerie. The information is complete even though there is no video or photo, the witness has made representative drawings. Only one testimony is collected. Of low strangeness and good consistency (single witness, but testimony quite precise), this case appears to be a very probable misperception with a flock of Thai lanterns (see the investigation report). The description of the PAN is perfectly classic of a formation flight of Thai lanterns, likely released for a festival or wedding, as it's a dozen or so glowing points flying in formation on a Saturday night, with the wind. This hypothesis had been rejected by the witness because he had seen on the internet that the trajectory did not match the wind trajectory, but it turns out that he likely made a mistake in this check, as the wind was blowing from the East-Northeast or Northeast. The case is classified B, probable misidentification with Thai lanterns.
