Description: On the night of December 31, 2009, to January 1, 2010, at midnight thirty, a witness is intrigued by the slow O-N movement across the sky of a red ball with a yellow outline. The phenomenon is observed for approximately three minutes before making a sudden "departure." Slightly strange and very inconsistent, this observation case is likely a misidentification with a Thai lantern. This case is classified B: probable observation of a Thai lantern.
