Description: On January 1, 2008, a driver took photos of Mount Dôme from inside his car using his mobile phone. A month later, when he reviewed the photos, he noticed a strange light phenomenon he couldn't explain, and he reported it to GEIPAN along with his photographs. In fact, it was a reflection of interior light from the vehicle (such as the ceiling light).
