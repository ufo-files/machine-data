Summary: Observations of the movement of orange light phenomena: probable passage of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-15aeabe74116b1595c7a62b1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Witnesses reported their observations on May 6, 2012, between 11:00 PM and 11:30 PM of the silent movement of several orange glowing points. The phenomenon appears to fade out and disappears after several minutes of observation. The appearance of the lights (orange balls), their movement according to the east wind, the duration of a few minutes, and the slow and silent movement, strongly suggest Thai lanterns, probably released by some happy voter celebrating the presidential election outcome. This case is classified "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
