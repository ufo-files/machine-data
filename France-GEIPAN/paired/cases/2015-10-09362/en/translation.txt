Description: On October 14, 2015, at 23:31, a witness observed several successive flashes in the sky, in a very identifiable area. He checked the Heavens Above site but identified no Iridium satellite flashes or satellite passes that could correspond. Of average strangeness but sufficient consistency, this case appears to be a probable misperception with a satellite out of control, rotating relatively quickly on itself at a very high altitude. A reconstruction shows that in the area of the sky described by the witness, there are 5 or 6 satellites that could explain the observation. Monitoring of these satellites' passes will allow determining which one is responsible for the observed flashes. This case is classified B, misperception with a satellite reflection phenomenon, involving a satellite at a very high altitude.
