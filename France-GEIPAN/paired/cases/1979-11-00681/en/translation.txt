Description: On November 18, 1979, at 3:20 AM, a witness observed from their home a glowing circular object. Accompanied by two fins shaped like tails in a gray-beige color, the object moved at a low altitude and speed in a straight line in a north-south direction. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted only a few seconds. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, as we lack information about it.
