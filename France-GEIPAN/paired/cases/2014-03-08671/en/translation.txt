Description: On March 11, 2014, at 3:05 AM, a witness observing from a window facing south noticed a very bright point to his left (east) slowly ascending in the sky for 40 minutes. The schema developed by the witness places the phenomenon at an azimuth of 109°. The sky map shows the planet Venus, brilliant in the morning sky at an azimuth of 107° at its rise (see the 2 maps produced by the Winstars software at 03:05 and 03:40, in the direction indicated by the witness (109°)). The UFO GEIPAN classifies this case as 'A': observation of the planet Venus. The witness did not recognize Venus, perhaps because a star at the horizon is often not recognized as a star, but as an object flying across the landscape, but also because there were particular meteorological conditions that night: Infoclimat indicates only 66% humidity, thus exceptional clarity of the sky, rare in this region (see weather report from March 10-11). The other reports for the week indicate at least 76% humidity. If it had been another phenomenon than Venus, given that the witness was looking directly at that planet, the witness should have seen both the phenomenon AND Venus in the sky.
