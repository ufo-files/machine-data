Summary: Observation of a ULM (Unidentified Flying Object) intended for cinema.
--- seg-0ca815ceb3f8eaa784782a33 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 11, 2000, a witness observed a disk-shaped craft with three legs. This craft flew quickly at low altitude without making a sound. A similar observation was made by a gendarme who reported a motor noise. The investigation conducted by the gendarmerie will show that it was the test flight of a ULM (Ultra Light Motorized Aircraft) prototype intended for filming birds and for the filming of the movie "Le Peuple migrateur" (The Migrating People).
