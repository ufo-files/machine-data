Description: On June 3, 2010, at 7:28 a.m., a witness observed for approximately ten seconds a white sphere passing through the sky. Small in size and flying above buildings, this object quickly disappeared, hidden by the urban landscape. No noise was heard during the observation. No other testimonies about this phenomenon were collected. The witness did not wish to file a report with the gendarmerie. The detailed description of the observation, provided in the questionnaire sent to GEIPAN by the main witness, clearly suggests the observation of an object lighter than air (weather balloon or otherwise...). The weather conditions at Roissy at the time of the observation indicate a light north wind (between 360° and 10°) consistent with the 'north-south' trajectory described by the witness.
