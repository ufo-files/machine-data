Summary: Observation of the silent movement of an orange light: lack of information.
--- seg-35d99c6339ca796cc0dbc59f [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 14, 2012, between 11:30 PM and 11:35 PM, a witness observed an orange light in the sky, with a rounded shape and a vague triangular form. They noted a silent movement: the trajectory was perfectly straight, constant, and horizontal. Three other witnesses saw the phenomenon but did not provide a statement. The observation lasted approximately 2 minutes. Given the witness's description and the fact that it was a traditional day for festivals, the hypothesis of a Thai lantern was prioritized. However, the weather report for Avignon indicates a light south wind, which is apparently incompatible with a northeast flow in the Methamis valley. This hypothesis was therefore not retained. The hypothesis of an atmospheric reentry of satellite debris is excluded due to the westward movement. The apparent speed of the object is considered too slow for a meteoroid (meteorite reentry) described as dim. The described appearance is also inconsistent with that of an aircraft. Due to these various uncertainties, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C": lack of information.
