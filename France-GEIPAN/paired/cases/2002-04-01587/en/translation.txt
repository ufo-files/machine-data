Description: On April 6, 2002, at 10:45 PM, several independent witnesses across multiple departments observed the very rapid fall of a fireball that made no noise. All witnesses indicate that the observation was in the eastern direction. This phenomenon was also reported by witnesses in Bavaria. The hypothesis under consideration is that of an atmospheric reentry.
