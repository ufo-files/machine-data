Summary: Observation of the silent and joint passage of three gray light spots on a straight trajectory: probable observation of the passage of birds illuminated by a bright area from the ground.
--- seg-d4f4fd7d0f2f8ba33ede7a20 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 19, 2015, a witness observing the stars, and in particular the Little Bear constellation, was surprised to notice the straight-line movement of 3 fixed gray lights forming an isosceles triangle in the sky. No trail, no halo, no noise was observed during the simultaneous passage of the three spots. These last ones gradually fade out in a second. The observation lasted a total of 8 seconds. Immediately after this observation, the witness follows the passage of an airplane crossing the trajectory of PAN. No further testimony will be collected. The witness cannot provide the exact time of observation, but does give an indication (French rugby trial against the Italians): the investigator was able to determine the time of observation, which is 10:31 PM (see the investigation report). Aeronautical (plane passage) and aerospace (satellites or rocket launch) hypotheses could therefore be studied. Of average strangeness, but with sufficient consistency, this case could be a possible misperception with a flight of birds illuminated by a ground-based light source. The non-punctual aspect of the glowing spots composing the PAN as well as its rapid angular velocity are compatible with this hypothesis. Additionally, there are two potential sources that could illuminate birds from below in the direction indicated by the witness. This GEIPAN case is classified as B, possible misidentification with a bird flight illuminated by a nocturnal light source.
