Description: On April 5, 2015, between 9:30 PM and 11:00 PM, a driver observes in the sky a bright, fixed, elongated light. He stops several times on his route to observe the luminous phenomenon in the clear sky. This lengthy observation of a very bright point in the sky, which appears to follow the observer but remains consistently positioned to the West at the same altitude, is typical of observing a celestial body. The planet Venus is particularly bright at that time, and exactly in the direction indicated by the witness. After being called by the GEIPAN, the witness verified a few days later that Venus was indeed present in that part of the sky. The elongated shape, described by the witness in his initial observation, was not observed again when checking for Venus a few days later: this shape could be due to the witness' visual fatigue or a particular atmospheric effect that distorted the planet's point image. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the planet Venus.
