Summary: Long observation of the evolutions of luminous phenomena of colors in the night cloudy sky: observation of luminous animations on clouds caused by sky-tracers during a night show ("Disney Tales of Magic" of Disneyland Paris)
--- seg-189eaf9cf513e14b4acc27b9 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 26, 2025, at 21:17, from his home in Neuilly-sur-Marne (93), a main witness (T1), joined by his wife and then by a neighbor, observed for nearly two hours several luminous phenomena heading east. He first describes two glowing halos forming circles at the base of the clouds, then the sudden appearance of a faintly glowing, bluish spherical shape, a beam resembling an inverted cone briefly lighting up vertically, and finally several localized sky ignitions, first blue then reddish. The whole thing is perceived as silent, localized in a restricted area of the sky, and has resulted in several videos as well as a police call. The consistency* of the case is good, without being maximum. The file relies on a detailed technical questionnaire and exploitable videos, but only one witness formalised their testimony, although two other witnesses were mentioned. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The perceived oddity by T1 is based on the autonomous aspect of the lights, their circular movements across the cloud cover, the absence of a visible beam connecting the ground to the light sources, the silence of the scene, as well as the succession of varied lighting effects throughout the evening. These elements led the witness to dismiss the hypothesis of simple projectors or ordinary drones, interpreting the phenomenon as having non-conventional characteristics. The hypothesis retained is that of an indirect observation of the nighttime show "Disney Tales of Magic" at Disneyland Paris (see the investigation report). This hypothesis is supported by several strong correspondences: 1. The spectacle was well presented at Disneyland Paris during this period; 2. It takes place around the Sleeping Beauty Castle and Main Street; 3. It utilizes powerful lighting effects, projections/lasers, pyrotechnics, and at that time, drones; 4. Finally, the direction chosen by the investigation, between 75° and 95°, with a center around 85°, precisely corresponds to the direction of the site from the observation location. The halos observed on the cloud cover result from the scattering of the show's projectors by low or medium-level clouds. The structured effects correspond to focused light sequences, such as the concentration of beams on the castle producing the reported conical shape. The blue and reddish illuminations are compatible with final pyrotechnic effects. The residual strangeness becomes weak once this explanation is taken into account. It is explained by the observation conditions: very partial viewing of the phenomenon from a distance, between houses, under a very cloudy sky, with patchy perception of the light effects projected onto the cloud base rather than their actual source on the ground. In this context, a highly staged spectacle can create an impression of autonomous movements, stillness, or even "emerging from the clouds" without invoking an unknown aerial phenomenon. Analysis of the gathered elements allows this observation to be attributed to the nighttime show at Disneyland Paris, atmospheric conditions having favored the projection of light onto the cloud layer. The GEIPAN classifies this case as category « A »: observation of luminous movements on clouds produced by projectors (sky-tracers).
