Summary: Observation of the passage of a luminous ball. Glow on the ground.
--- seg-fa799db62f1568a48bab3cb9 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 2, 1977, late in the day, a farmer was briefly overflown by a luminous spherical object in a field. Other people observed an unusual glow at the end of the field where the farmer was located. The gendarmerie, notified three days later, found no traces on the ground in the area indicated by the witnesses and no other testimony was collected that could provide further details.
