Description: On April 13, 2015, at 9:55 PM, a witness, who regularly observes and knows the starry sky, first observes a bright, brief white flash, followed by three static red orbs forming a triangle. The phenomenon is silent. Initially intense, its brightness diminishes until the phenomenon disappears. The observation lasts less than 10 seconds. Only one testimony is collected. Of average strangeness and consistency (single witness, precise description), this case turns out to be a misidentification with a particular triplet of satellites: the Chinese military satellites Yaogan 16A, B, and C (see the investigation report). The PAN indeed exhibits all the typical characteristics of such a triplet: luminosity, trajectory, and timing perfectly consistent. This case is classified A, misidentification with the Yaogan 16A, B, and C satellite triplet.
