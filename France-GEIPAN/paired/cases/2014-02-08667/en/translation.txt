Description: On January 2, 2014, at 6:30 PM, two individuals observed the slow, silent passage of several orange lights in the sky. Only one report was made. Orange balls moving slowly in the sky, with the wind (a south wind of 11 to 19 km/h recorded in Lille) on a weekend evening clearly suggest Thai lanterns (flying lamps). See weather report. The witness refutes this hypothesis. Given that the observation report is entirely consistent with this hypothesis, and that releases of lanterns are very common, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns. Note that February 2 is the festival day of Chandeleur, traditionally celebrated with torches or candles. The GEIPAN, after a quick search on the Internet, did not find the authors of the release.
