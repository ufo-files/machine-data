--- seg-c6bca0ed1a4786953df7f69b [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 17, 2011, around 8:30 PM, a witness heard a deep turbine-like noise coming from the sky and saw in the air an object traveling at high speed, passing very close to the roof of his home on a straight trajectory. The object described is cylindrical in shape, 80 cm long and 20 to 30 cm wide. Its color is black, and no smell or light was noticed. The object moved away in the direction of the village. An investigation by the IPN (French National Police Institute) did not find a coherent hypothesis, although the object resembles a drone, of an unknown model. The witness described the event to the Gendarmerie and the IPN several times, without distorting the narrative, which could be considered a PAN. This case is classified as D1: Unidentified. However, it should be noted that the case file is relatively weak: only one witness, a very brief observation, no photos or videos, and the object's performance is not significantly different from known vehicles.
