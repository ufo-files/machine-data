Summary: Observation of the movement of two flashing light points: probable observation of the passage of two military satellites.
--- seg-1878486a2aabccc7768f5eb7 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 8, 2013, a witness at a summer camp made astronomical observations with a friend. He reported one of his observations around 10:30 PM where he saw two bright points in the sky moving on a straight trajectory in a flickering manner. This well-documented observation report clearly suggests the observation of satellites. A consultation of the Calsky site allows for two hypotheses:

- The passage of the ISS at 10:27 PM: However, the International Space Station is much brighter than stars, and this hardly explains the two bright points. The ISS passed south of the North Star, while the witness described the objects to the north.

- The passage of the two military satellites NOSS 2-1B and 1C at 10:37 PM, less bright, but capable of irregularly reflecting sunlight and producing the effect described by the witness. Their trajectory closely matches the witness's observation. This hypothesis is the one retained by GEIPAN, despite a 7-minute deviation from the testimony.
