Description: On November 2, 2014, at 6:25 PM, a witness observed the silent movement of an orange glowing point that advanced towards them, then changed course before ascending into a clear sky. The observation of a slowly moving orange point in the sky resembles a Thai lantern carried by the wind. In this case, the non-linear movement is not consistent with this hypothesis, but not impossible due to the steep terrain in the valley; it is common in valleys to have a very low altitude wind that is very different from the wind higher up. However, it is nearly impossible to model and thus predict this wind pattern according to the considered altitude. Furthermore, weather stations are far from the observation location: Lyon, Macon, Geneva, and they indicate different winds (a strong south wind in Lyon, weaker in Macon, a south-west wind in Geneva) so they do not allow for an estimate of the wind at Matafelon. The photograph shows a pink and blue object, but the witness describes an orange object: due to the shooting conditions (night, movement), the shape appearing in the photo is more the result of various imperfections in the shot than a faithful image of the observed object. Due to these various uncertainties, the GEIPAN is not able to provide a justified explanation for the phenomenon, this case is classified as "C".
