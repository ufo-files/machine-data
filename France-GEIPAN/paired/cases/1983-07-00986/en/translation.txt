Summary: Observation of an object moving at very high speed
--- seg-0cdde3575eef7c31d2154900 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 15, 1983, at approximately 9:30 PM, a witness observed from their terrace an rounded object moving at high speed across the sky. This green and blue-colored shape left behind a very long, sparkling white trail. The observation, lasting about 4 seconds, revealed a regular and straight trajectory. The object disappeared at the horizon. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon, which exhibits characteristics of an atmospheric reentry.
