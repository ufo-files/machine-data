--- seg-fb593e2f124c23ab60030d8e [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 06, 1980, three officials on patrol observed at 2 a.m. a curious object crossing the sky from West to East on a straight trajectory and at high speed. The object had a rounded front and a pointed rear and glowed like a neon light. The observation lasted 4 seconds before the object disappeared on the horizon. No sound of movement or fall was heard. No other information was received about this phenomenon, for which we lack details.
