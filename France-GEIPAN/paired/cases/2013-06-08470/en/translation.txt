Description: On June 12, 2013, at 10:59 PM, a witness observed the silent movement of an orange-colored light disk in the sky. The object disappeared into the clouds. The appearance and movement of the light point clearly evoke a Thai lantern. The witness states that he has seen them in numbers on other occasions, forming a constellation. It is rare to observe Thai lanterns on weekdays. However, according to a person renting a reception hall in the area, lantern releases are very common at weddings, and test releases on weekdays are conducted. This unique lantern may be a test. The south wind (recorded by Meteociel in Rouen) is not entirely consistent with the witness's observed movement towards the northwest, but the particular relief (the La Risle river flows along a steep coast, see map) can cause local winds at low altitude. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
