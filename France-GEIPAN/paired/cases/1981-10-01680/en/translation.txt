Description: On October 9, 1981, around 5 a.m., an individual observed from their garden a cylindrical flying object. It was approximately 100 meters away, and the witness described it as having a diameter of 1.50 meters and a height of 30 meters. During the observation, which lasted 6 minutes, the witness noticed that the object was orange in the center, then turned yellow and white on the edges. No other witnesses or traces were found during the investigation. This phenomenon remains unexplained due to a lack of data.
