Description: On December 19, 2009, between 8:30 PM and 9:30 PM, several witnesses observed intense red lights moving above the ocean. The main witness reported 5 observation sequences during which he saw the same light. The witness did not report to the local gendarmerie and no other testimonies were collected regarding this phenomenon. By letter dated June 9, 2010, the main witness informed the GEIPAN that his observation was of a Thai lantern.
