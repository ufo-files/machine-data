--- seg-e5278d97ee9142aa08a0748b [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 22, 2013 at 1:30 AM, a witness observed from their doorstep the silent, south-to-north passage of two very bright, vivid orange orbs in a straight line. A third identical orb then passed over their house and quickly ascended. The witness went to the heights of their village to observe the phenomenon, which they would not see again. The observation of bright orange orbs crossing the sky with the wind (from south to north; see weather report for the night from Saturday to Sunday) clearly evokes Thai lanterns, probably released after a party in the southern district of Esperaza. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
