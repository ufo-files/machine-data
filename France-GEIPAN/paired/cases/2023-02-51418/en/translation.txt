Description: On February 12, 2023, the witness filed a report of his observation with the gendarmerie. On February 10, 2023, between 23:16 and 23:21, he observed several PANs (unidentified flying objects) much larger than stars, very bright and luminous, and orange in color. Then the phenomenon became less intense. The movements were a bit chaotic: slightly from right to left, at times it seemed to him from top to bottom, quite gently. The lights were approaching and sometimes moving apart from each other. Then they started to flicker "as if they were going to turn off" and gradually all turned off. The witness was able to make a video of his observation. The witness's description and video indicate the following: - orange color - very bright - flashing and fading - movement of lights in a "formation"; very slow geometric progression. These elements point very strongly towards an observation of celestial lanterns. By the way, the witness states, "I looked at the GEIPAN site and thought it might be Chinese lanterns," and the gendarme conducting the interview notes, "Very likely that the observed phenomenon is of the type of flying Chinese lanterns with a candle." The witness states that the UFOs (PANs) were moving from his right to his left, according to his direction of travel: on the Keravel road coming from Morlaix, the UFOs could move in the following quadrants: northwest to southeast to southwest to northeast. Wind speeds at 23:00, at an altitude of approximately 100 meters, at the observation location, from 235°, or west-southwest, are consistent with the direction of movement of the PANs. Since the wind force is very weak: 2km/h, this could explain the erratic movements of UFOs (PANs) subject to the slightest local change in orientation (see the Earthnull document). The GEIPAN classifies this observation as A: observation of celestial lanterns. This conclusion aligns with the analysis formulated by the gendarmerie.
