Summary: Observation of the movement of advertising balloons in the sky.
--- seg-4dd4a6625743579266d0106d [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 11, 2010, at 5:30 PM, several people observed in the sky the slow movement of a dozen bright white points. No noise was heard during the observation which lasted 10 minutes. The photograph taken by the witness immediately suggested a possible balloon flight. An internet search revealed that a fashion show had taken place in the southwest of the observation location (information reported in a local newspaper article). This show was concluded with a release of balloons (information confirmed by the organizer contacted by GEIPAN) around 5:30 PM. Weather conditions, with a light south-southwest wind, caused these balloons to hover over the beach at the observation location. This case is classified A as a balloon observation.
