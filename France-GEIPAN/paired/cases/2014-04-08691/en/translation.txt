Summary: Observation of the north-south passage of two luminous phenomena in the sky (spherical shape for one and cigar-shaped for the other): observation of the passage of a satellite and a commercial aircraft.
--- seg-64eba2da963d55fe84ce2a25 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 24, 2014, around 10:40 PM, a witness observed from their balcony the movement of a bright white sphere followed by a second one, shaped like a elongated cigar with green and red lights and marked along its length by white lights. No noise was heard, which convinces the witness that it cannot be an airplane. No other testimony is collected. Due to its strong strangeness and sufficient consistency, this case turns out to be a misidentification with an artificial satellite (particularly NOSS 3-6 Rocket) and a commercial airliner such as a B737 or A330. The reconstruction of the trajectories of these two objects shows indeed that they perfectly correspond to those of PAN. Furthermore, the description of the PAN perfectly matches these two artificial objects. It is normal that the witness did not recognize an airplane during his observation, as any possible noise emanating from that airplane could not have reached the witness due to the distance between him and the airplane, as well as because of the wind blowing in the opposite direction of PAN / witness. Similarly, the witness ruled out the possibility of an airplane, as he is convinced that no airplane flies on the trajectory indicated, which is incorrect. The rapid and unusual succession (same trajectory, same angular height) of a satellite pass and that of a commercial airliner can perfectly create confusion, and thus the non-identification of the two objects by the witness. This GEIPAN case is classified as A: Misidentification with a satellite and a commercial aircraft.
