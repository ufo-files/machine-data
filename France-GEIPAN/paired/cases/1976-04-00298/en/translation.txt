Summary: Successive observations of luminous phenomena.
--- seg-c75eaaa37c42b6758efedf00 [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reports, on June 19, 1976, his three observations of light phenomena between April 1976 and June 1976. On April 7, 1976, around 11:30 PM, he notices a luminous object in the sky shaped like a cigar: it lands on the path leading to the witness's home and emits a strong light directed in a single direction. During May 1976, at the same time and practically at the same location, he observes two very bright craft, one of which appears to be parked on a nearby path near the home. He immediately wakes his brother and parents, who observe these luminous phenomena for approximately 1.5 hours without being able to determine a precise shape. No noise is heard. Everything disappears suddenly. One of the witnesses will find traces on the asphalt at the presumed landing site. On June 16, still at the same time, the witness notices a strong brightness on a field near his home. Following all these observations, he decides to testify. During the gendarmerie investigation, no findings could be made regarding the first two observations due to the witness's late declaration. For the third observation, the landowner will claim that he lit a wood fire in his field that evening to grill.
