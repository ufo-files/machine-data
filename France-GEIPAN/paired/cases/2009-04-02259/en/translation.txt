Description: On April 4, 2009, at 11:00 AM, two pedestrians observed in a cloudy sky a large, gray, oval object that was swaying on its axis. The observation lasted 5 minutes, after which the witnesses saw the object move without any sound or trail. The object disappeared gradually towards the East. On April 10, the GEIPAN received an email from a journalist who had an anonymous photo of a PAN taken on April 4th around 12:00 PM in Roissy, and he was seeking to decipher it. Analysis of the photograph revealed that it was indeed taken on April 4, 2009, at 9:22 AM. However, further investigation determined that the photo was not taken in Roissy but near the taxiways W2, F3, or V2 at Le Bourget. Enlarging the photograph showed the logo of a company that owns a hangar at Le Bourget. Finally, the gendarmerie investigation established that on April 4, 2009, at 11:00 AM, the control tower at Le Bourget Airport reported the presence of a balloon in the shape of a flying saucer to the north of the airport. The device, neutralized and taken into custody by the territorial national police, was a 20-meter diameter balloon intended for an animation during a sports event at the Stade de France.
