Summary: Probable observation of an atmospheric reentry
--- seg-52a27ad7e7c797e79d240327 [needs-review] ---
Description: Around 5 a.m. on February 5, 1984, several people observed in the pitch black night a rectangular object emitting a bright light of pale blue color, allowing them to see as if it were daytime. This object moved silently from north to south at a very high speed. The observation lasted between 5 and 8 seconds: the phenomenon disappeared at the horizon. The descriptions of the witnesses suggest a probable observation of an atmospheric reentry.
