Description: On November 1st, 1981, several people in a vehicle observed at a distance of 100 meters a stationary craft about 30 meters above a field. As they drove by at a slow pace, the witnesses noticed that the object had a hemispherical shape and a fluorescent color. In the lower right corner, a spotlight directed towards the ground emitted an intermittent white light. Some sort of heat haze made the object's contours blurry. Upon passing along the field again after dropping off a person at their home, they noted the disappearance of the craft. The investigation was unable to find any traces or other witnesses of this phenomenon for which we lack data.
