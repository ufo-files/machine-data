Description: On July 3, 2010, at 3:13 AM, a witness observed the presence between two clouds of an object with a flame-red color. The object, initially stationary, moved silently in a southerly direction before disappearing. The observation's characteristics resemble those of a known phenomenon often observed: Thai lanterns (July's festive period, Saturday night to Sunday, proximity to a festive location, such as a castle hosting weddings and guest rooms, orange flame...). According to weather documents (Auxerre weather records), wind was light and unstable. The witness's description of a helical shape can be explained by observing a flying lantern from below (see comparison image). The observation does not present any unusual features. The case's consistency is low due to the single testimony and limited information in the gendarmerie's hearing report. The plausible hypothesis of observing a Thai-style lantern is accepted, and this case, with a low strangeness level, is classified as B, very probably explained.
