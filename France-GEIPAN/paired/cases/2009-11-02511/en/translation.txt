--- seg-21f90684dc5a8218b904da43 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 26, 2009, at 6:35 PM, a witness observed in the sky the passage of two large bright points that intrigued them. For 6 to 7 minutes, the witness noticed a particular intensity of light and the absence of flashing lights and any noise associated with this passage. The points followed each other at a slow and constant speed. No other similar observation testimony was collected. After verification, it is very likely that the observation made by the witness was that of the International Space Station (ISS) followed by the Space Shuttle Atlantis during mission STS-129.
