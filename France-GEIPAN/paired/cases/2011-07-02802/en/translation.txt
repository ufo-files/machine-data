Description: A witness reports an observation made on July 31, 2011, around 7:30 PM of a very fast-moving phenomenon in the sky. Oval in shape and white in color, the phenomenon moved without any sound. It quickly disappeared towards the horizon. The object's speed and brightness suggest an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid or space debris. The observation date corresponds to a period of numerous meteor showers, and the trajectory's direction is consistent with a Perseid. The file was forwarded for information to scientists specializing in meteorites (IMCCE and National Museum of Natural History).
