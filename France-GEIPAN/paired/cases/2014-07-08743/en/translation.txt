Description: The witness is sitting on his terrace when he spots three slow-moving orange circles in the sky. The phenomenon photo, published in the press the next day for a case reported in Gratentour, is identical to his observation. The observation of orange circles crossing the sky in a few minutes, according to the same trajectory one weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement is observed coming from the southeast (Gratentour), which is consistent with the southeast wind recorded at Toulouse-Blagnac (south wind to Montauban). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", a probable observation of a Thai lantern.
