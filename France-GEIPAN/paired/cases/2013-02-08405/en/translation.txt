Summary: Recurrent observations of the movements of an object with yellow lights: probable observations of aircraft
--- seg-73b8bb5ee6854f063d31f1a4 [needs-review] ---
Description: The witness regularly observes yellow lights in the southeast that appear, move slightly, and disappear. The witness's observation direction corresponds to the area where, by West or North-West winds, planes coming from the South align on Orly airport's runway (see map). The witness only sees the landing lights of these planes, which are very directional: thus, they are only visible when the plane is facing them. The plane is too far away (20km) to distinguish its red-green position lights or even its flashing lights. The witness observed the phenomenon numerous times in January and early February, when winds were West or North-West; they were not seen on the 12th and 13th because, with winds blowing from Northeast or South, Orly's approach then comes from the West. The witness can identify the planes they observe in real-time by consulting: air traffic. This case is classified "B": probable observations of commercial jets before landing.
