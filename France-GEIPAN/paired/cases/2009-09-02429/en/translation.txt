Description: On September 29, 2009, at 8:27 a.m., a witness observed in their garden for 30 to 50 seconds the silent N-NE passage of a white, luminous, waterdrop-shaped phenomenon radiating a slightly blue light. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. The low strangeness of the observed phenomenon and the inconsistency of the testimony do not allow for a certain conclusion about a meteor, although the drawing made by the witness is typical of a meteor. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of a meteor.
