Description: On December 5, 2010, around 11:45 PM, a driver and their passenger observed in the sky an object they could not explain. A cylindrical object, illuminated in front and behind, crossed the sky. As indicated by the witness themselves in the questionnaire, this testimony corresponds to the probable observation of the 2 approach lights of an aircraft passing through a foggy and polluted sky of the Paris metropolitan area. The questionnaire is not very detailed and does not allow for further details in this interpretation.
