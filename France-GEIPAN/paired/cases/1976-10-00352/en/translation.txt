Description: A witness succinctly reports observing on October 15, 1976, at approximately 17:55, the movement of a luminous object in the sky following a S-N trajectory. It subsequently appeared in the shape of a large cigar and continued to move silently. The object disappeared in the direction of NE. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
