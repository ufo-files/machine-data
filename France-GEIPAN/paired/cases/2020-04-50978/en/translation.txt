**Description:** On April 13, 2020, around 16:20, two witnesses on their balcony were intrigued by the slow and silent passage in the sky of a white object shaped like a "large jellyfish." The object sometimes seemed to spin on itself and moved on a straight trajectory then ascended before disappearing. According to the witness, the PAN (Unidentified Flying Object) did not resemble any motorized aircraft or weather balloon. A photo was taken. Only one testimony was collected on this phenomenon. We have shown through analysis that all parameters of the testimony are compatible with the observation of a light, small debris carried by the wind (see the investigation report). This conclusion is based on the following elements: - general aspect: irregularly shaped, semi-transparent object, - the PAN's movement in the direction of the wind, - compatibility with a modest-sized object, even at a distance of 500 meters. The appearance on the photo provided by the witness suggests a neutral supermarket bag (such as a plastic bag for fruits and vegetables) or a light debris from insulation (bubble wrap or packaging foam). GEIPAN classifies it as A: observation of a light debris carried by the wind.
