Description: On December 14, 2008, around 9 PM, several witnesses observed the silent passage of a spherical orange object for 20 seconds. The object moved from East to Northwest on a straight trajectory without any trail. After a wavering of its brightness, the object disappeared. One witness claims similar balls were seen the previous week. The Makes Contact Observatory, contacted about this, refers to "wishing lanterns" of the same type as Thai lanterns. This hypothesis will be confirmed by the main witness who will observe them again on Christmas Eve.
