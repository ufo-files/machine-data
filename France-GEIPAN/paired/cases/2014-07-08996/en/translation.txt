Summary: Successive observations the same evening of the silent movement of bright white points forming a triangle in the night sky: observations likely of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-cd8ea21c021a0e5940c38869 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 26, 2014, at 11:10 PM, a couple observed from their garden the silent passage of 3 white glowing points. These three points form a triangle. Three minutes later, the couple sees the same thing under the same circumstances but farther away from their position. The movement is always towards the S-SW. Only one testimony is collected. The witness very likely saw Thai lanterns. Indeed, the shape, color, configuration, and trajectory in the sky indicate a release of lanterns, especially since the observed direction of movement (towards southwest) is consistent with the wind recorded that evening in the region (see the weather). The date during the weekend and/or in the summer is common for festive releases of events. Finally, a similar observation at the same time and in support of a Thai lantern hypothesis was made nearby (see UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECT 66). As a result, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable Thai lanterns.
