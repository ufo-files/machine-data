Description: On September 11, 1997, in the evening, and the following day, witnesses reported a sparkling bright point in the sky. The phenomenon was silent, moving very slowly in the sky and about the size of a star. It produced a red halo and flickered in several colors. The observation lasted approximately one and a half hours. This observation is likely that of a star's trajectory.
