Description: On May 21, 2012 (the exact date was confirmed during the investigation) at 10:12 PM, a driver notices, in the overcast sky, the north-south passage of a luminous phenomenon that intrigues him. While stopped at a red light, he observes a cylindrical shape "resembling an icon with its tip pointing towards the ground." Getting out of his vehicle, he notices the object is stationary and a powerful light is emanating from small portholes. Around the object, a glowing orange sphere is observed. The object then takes an ascending trajectory and changes shape (inverted V) and color (white) before disappearing into the cloud layer. The observation lasted a total of 15 seconds. Only one testimony was collected. Based on the objective elements defined in the investigation report, namely: - An object with an unusual shape and equipped with "portholes" that emit light. - Pausing above the road facing the witness. - Taking off after a few seconds while changing shape. We can conclude that the PAN observed by the witnesses is not explicable and exhibits numerous characteristics that are difficult to reconcile with the only plausible hypothesis considered, that of a festive balloon. This case should be classified as "D1" - observation of an object of unknown nature. This testimony is of good consistency: very precise, but from a single witness without a photo. One might wonder why, in the middle of the city, no one else noticed the phenomenon. The observation is strange as the object's physical and dynamic characteristics are uncommon and do not strictly conform to any known flying object.
