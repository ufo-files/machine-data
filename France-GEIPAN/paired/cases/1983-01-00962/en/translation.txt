Description: On January 29, 1983, two farmers will observe a peculiar blue-tinged light phenomenon. One of the witnesses will see a fireball the size of a balloon arrive: it moves horizontally, emits a hiss, then explodes, startling the livestock. Despite ground trace searches, no further information will be gathered on this phenomenon, whose description suggests a lightning manifestation.
