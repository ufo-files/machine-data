**Description:** A witness reports several phenomena observed from their home in SAN-MARTINO-DI-LOTA (20), particularly on August 15, 2017, between 9:10 PM and 9:20 PM. That evening, the witness describes the observation of instantaneous and simultaneous flashes of light to the northeast at 25° azimuth, which, after disappearing, would reappear in the same manner to the east at 90° azimuth, instantly vanishing and reappearing four times in different parts of the sky. The observation duration is estimated at approximately 10 minutes. Only one testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected. The witness compiled a video documenting various observed light phenomena. An investigator met with the witness, who mixed up observations from August 15, 2017, with those from October 9 and 14, 2017. He recounted his multiple sightings, himself analyzing his observations. The witness confirmed that focus should be solely on the August 15 observation, suggesting that other sightings could be helicopters or drones, based on his analysis.

On August 15, 2017, the witness observed two simultaneous flashes of white light to the left of their terrace (northeast, azimuth 25°, horizon height 20°). They then noticed four flashes in the sky at different locations. According to the witness, the weather was clear, with a heat haze partially obscuring the Italian islands of Capraia and Elba. The weather bulletin confirmed fair weather, clear skies, and a temperature of 21°C around 9:00 PM, with a visibility of 40 kilometers. Contact was made with the Air Transport Gendarmerie at Bastia-Poretta Airport. No unusual reports or testimonies were recorded during August 2017, or afterward. Checks were conducted with two local gendarmes, who reported no incidents. Contact was also made with the Air Navigation Service of the Bastia-Poretta Airport control tower to verify flight schedules and radar data on August 15, but no confirmation was obtained.

**The witness's impression:** The witness is a former scientist with a keen interest in everything happening in the sky. They were particularly aware and interested in anything that could be mistaken for "UFOs" in the Corsican sky and had been expecting such observations.

**Conclusion:** Several Corsican communes often fire fireworks on August 15 to celebrate Napoleon Bonaparte's birth and a religious festival (Virgin Mary's Day). Private individuals, especially restaurants and nightclubs, do the same due to the high tourist influx during this period, potentially using powerful lasers (though we found no confirmation regarding this on the specific evening in question). However, a fireworks display was set off in BASTIA at 10:00 PM and in the commune of SAN-MARTINO-DI-LOTA (20) at 9:30 PM on the Miomo beach. At 9:15 PM, a test firing was conducted on Miomo with some rockets to assess atmospheric conditions. It can be assumed that tests were also conducted in BASTIA before 10:00 PM, but we received no response from the local municipality. Searches for iridium flashes and the passage of the International Space Station (ISS) yielded negative results. There were numerous boats in the sea on the evening of August 15, confirmed by the Port of BASTIA Captaincy. People were waiting for the fireworks. Yet, we have only one witness to the phenomenon. Nothing was reported to local gendarmes, and no articles were published in the press. The observation site is conducive to seeing many lights in the sky; indeed, Italy is directly across, Elba Island has an airport, and numerous helicopters shuttle between Elba and Capraia islands. Many air corridors pass through this area with French, Italian, and international traffic. We were unable to obtain information on air traffic. While the witness undoubtedly saw lights, we lack sufficient information to confirm any of the above hypotheses, even though the probability of seeing fireworks on August 15 is real.

The GEIPAN case is classified as "C" due to a lack of exploitable information.
