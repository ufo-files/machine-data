Description: The observation of orange-glowing objects crossing the sky on the same trajectories within minutes on a weekend night strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The observed trail could be smoke from the burner. Meteociel indicates a very light north to northeast wind: Weather. The observation reports two types of movement, only one is compatible with this northeast wind. The other movement could be explained by a slightly different wind pattern at altitude, a possible scheme for a very light wind, but hypothetical. Although this case has many characteristics of Thai lantern observations, the complex movements leave doubt about the nature of the phenomenon. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to lack of information and cross-references.
