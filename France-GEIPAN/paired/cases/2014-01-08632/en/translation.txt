--- seg-92b2b761c50a69dc30011588 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 1, 2014, around 00:08, a witness observing from their balcony saw in the sky the silent movement of numerous red glowing objects that progressed in a straight line through the cloudy sky. This observation of 23 red glowing points flying linearly on the same trajectory has all the characteristics of Thai lanterns, which are very commonly launched after festivals. The New Year's occasion strengthens this hypothesis. The weather report notes a light south wind (southwest at 23:00, south at midnight, southeast at 01:00), see: weather. Although the witness denies the possibility that it could be flying lanterns, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns. The low strangeness of this type of observation on January 1st at midnight does not justify a field investigation.
