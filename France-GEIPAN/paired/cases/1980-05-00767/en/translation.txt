--- seg-db180417d9dbd777cd63a72f [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 27, 1980, a witness observed a peculiar phenomenon as he was preparing to go home around 12:30 AM. A red-orange ball, about the size of a soccer ball, approached him and stopped at 15 feet (5 meters) from the house. This object shone brightly with a metallic gleam. Waking other people at his residence, they too observed this glowing ball. Frightened, they decided to go back to bed. The following day, no suspicious traces were noticed on the front door. No other testimonies about this phenomenon were collected, and we lack information about it.
