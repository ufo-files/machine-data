Description: On November 24, 1996, around 4:00 PM, a witness observed bright points and objects moving in the sky. A second witness, contacted by the first, observed the phenomenon and clearly identified it as the frequent passage of aircraft in the area. This is therefore a case of aircraft misidentification.
