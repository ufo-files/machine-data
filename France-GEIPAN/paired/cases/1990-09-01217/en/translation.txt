--- seg-a78746d215c3bd30ab55cf13 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 26, 1990, at 5:00 PM, a woman was watching television when her attention was drawn through her window by a glowing ball heading towards her house. She became frightened and called her two neighbors who witnessed the phenomenon. After ascending, the ball descended and parked above a tree. At that moment, it became extremely bright, illuminating the entire area and blinding the witnesses. The entire observation lasted 5 to 6 minutes. No explanation could be given for the phenomenon.
