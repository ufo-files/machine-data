Description: Following a local press article indicating that a witness had reportedly seen an unidentified flying object in the sky, the gendarmerie is seeking additional potential witnesses. Two witnesses located confirm having observed on August 11, 1977, around 10:30 PM, a very bright and stationary object in the sky. Observed through binoculars, the object had spheres emitting a yellow light at its base and a kind of red strobe light that flickered. It then moved slowly without any noise being heard before disappearing. These witnesses likely observed the passage of a helicopter-type aircraft.
