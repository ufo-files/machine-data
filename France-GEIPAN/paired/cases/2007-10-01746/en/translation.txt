Summary: Black circles several meters in diameter of myxomycetes.
--- seg-a1589fda411ee8c34ce4a26b [needs-review] ---
Description: On the mornings of October 2, 3, and 5, 2007, people found black residue rings in their lawns. Appearing during the night, their diameters ranged from 5.80 meters to 10 meters, with a width of approximately 30 cm. During investigations conducted by the gendarmerie and the GEIPAN, samples were taken. Analyses of these samples, entrusted to the mycology department of the Toulouse Faculty of Pharmacy, will enable the identification of an exceptional formation of Myxomycetes of the Badhamia genus, organisms akin to fungi, intermediate between the animal and plant kingdoms.
