Description: On March 12, 1982, around 8:30 PM, a witness observed in the sky a very bright ball approximately 80 cm in diameter. Silver in color, it was followed by a light orange trail. Throughout the observation period, the object did not change color or direction. This person may have been a witness to an atmospheric reentry.
