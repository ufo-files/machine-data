--- seg-d72c823f870ff2c9fa90abe6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 12, 2013, between approximately 11:15 PM and 11:33 PM, a witness observed a bright, flickering light slowly and erratically moving along a mountain slope. It was located near an area of very difficult access, which surprised the witness. The phenomenon dimmed and then disappeared. Based on the investigation file, which indicates that the observation area is a zone of human activity (access to the Valmenier chairlifts) and that the color and appearance of the PAN are typical of a flashlight use, we can conclude that the PAN observed by the witness is very likely a powerful flashlight used by one or more individuals present on the site, near the chairlift facilities. This case is classified as "B" for a very probable observation of a powerful flashlight.
