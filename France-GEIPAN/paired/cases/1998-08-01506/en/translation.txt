Description: On August 6, 1998, around 10:40 PM, a man in a car was listening to music and looking at the sky. He then observed a bright light moving rapidly north to south. The observation lasted 15 to 20 seconds. An investigation with aviation authorities revealed the passage of a commercial airliner, as well as an unidentified radar track at the time of the observation. The end time of this radar track corresponds to the witness's observation time. There is no formal evidence, but it is likely that the witness's observation was an aircraft.
