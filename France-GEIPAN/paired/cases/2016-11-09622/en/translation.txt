Summary: Long night observation of a luminous phenomenon through a window: observation of a reflection of an external light.
--- seg-9c9ba10ec460b054d5eca1aa [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 29, 2016, around midnight, a person gets up and is intrigued by what they see through the window of their bathroom. The witness spots a flickering light that grows and shrinks, as well as a hexagonal object with varied colors flickering positioned above the neighbor's field. The observation lasts for about twenty minutes during which several photos and videos are taken. The frightened witness wakes up her husband then goes back to sleep. Processing the video, by allowing the lights to be located on the bathroom's glass bricks, undeniably shows that the lights observed by the witness were due to the distortion of a light source by wavy glass bricks (see the investigation report). The light source was not identified. But it's not necessary since the strangeness is explained and does not come from the light itself. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A: observation of a reflection of an external light.
