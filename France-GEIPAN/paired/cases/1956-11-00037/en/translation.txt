Description: On November 15, 1956, at 7:05 PM in Paris, a pilot observed from the ground a bright red ball moving from north-west to south-east in the clear sky. A second ball separated from the first and followed a parabolic trajectory downward for one second. No trail was noticed. Both balls suddenly and simultaneously disappeared. Only one witness report was collected at the time. The GEIPAN classified this case as B: probable observation of the passage of a meteor caused by the atmospheric reentry of a natural meteorite that fragments.
