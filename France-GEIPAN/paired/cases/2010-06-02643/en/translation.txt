Summary: Observation of the passage of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-0f13bf8002449acb425cd450 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 20, 2010, around 12:30 AM, witnesses observed three bright, orange-red spheres in the sky. They moved in a north-northwest direction. The observation characteristics reported by three witnesses (Saturday night into Sunday around midnight, three orange spheres moving in the same direction, without noise, and at a low altitude) correspond to a flight of Thai lanterns. This hypothesis is indeed considered by one of the witnesses. The north-northwest wind direction also aligns with the movement direction described in the observation.
