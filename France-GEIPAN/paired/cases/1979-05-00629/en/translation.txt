Description: On May 31, 1979, at 12:40 a.m., two individuals observed a luminous phenomenon in the direct West in the sky. For several minutes, a large object in the shape of a crescent and orange in color appeared. Another object also appeared for a few moments. The sky map for that morning indicates in the direct West the presence of Jupiter (magnitude -1.49), Pollux (magnitude 0.98), and a moonset (magnitude -9.51). These individuals made an astronomical observation of this ensemble.
