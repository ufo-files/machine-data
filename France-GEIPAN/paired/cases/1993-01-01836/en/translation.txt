--- seg-f8feeda6bfa5695026ed65fc [needs-review] ---
Description: A person reports on April 24, 2008 an observation made during the summer between 1993 and 1997 (the witness cannot recall the exact date and the given date is arbitrary). The witness, along with five others, observed between 9:00 PM and 10:00 PM a peculiar glowing phenomenon in a clear sky. Points forming two isosceles triangles seemed to rotate around the central axis of each triangle. Suddenly, these points ceased their movement and the triangles took opposite directions: the first, heading towards Mont Gros, disappeared hidden by the landscape, while the second initially remained stationary over the sea before resuming its rotation and suddenly vanishing. It is very difficult, if not impossible, to investigate a late testimony like this one (testimony on 04/24/2008). We frequently receive reports of flying triangles, some of which remain unexplained, but many others are also misidentifications of planes seen from a particular angle. In this case, we have too few details to verify today the hypothesis of planes.
