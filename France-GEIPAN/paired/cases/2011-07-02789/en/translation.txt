Summary: Observation of the silent movement of three glowing orbs on a straight trajectory: lack of information.
--- seg-70048fb85cf826cc844ab4df [needs-review] ---
Description: On Sunday, July 3, 2011, around 8:30 a.m., the witness, occupied with their personal computer, decided to go smoke a cigarette on the terrace of their residence, which faces south-southwest. They then spotted three white glowing orbs in the sky heading in the same direction. Two of them drew closer together, and all three objects began moving in a straight line from south-southwest to northwest, without the noise or condensation typically associated with high-altitude commercial aircraft, and at an apparent speed greater than those aircraft. The witness paused their observation to retrieve their camcorder, but then changed their mind and used their mobile phone instead. The phenomenon disappeared when the witness returned to the terrace a few moments later, and they were unable to locate it through their west-facing windows. The observation lasted approximately 60 seconds. This case of three glowing orbs crossing a portion of the sky is of average strangeness, as they appear similar to balloons. Since the measured wind speed of 7 km/h is contrary to the observed movement, this hypothesis cannot be either confirmed or formally ruled out. This observation case lacks sufficient consistency: it is an isolated testimony with no photos, and there is uncertainty regarding the wind speed. Without additional evidence to validate or rule out these hypotheses, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C."
