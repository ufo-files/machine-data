Description: On October 21, 2011 at 21:05, a driver on A10 was intrigued by the passage of a luminous phenomenon in the sky, followed by the presence of glowing circles and a dust cloud in a field. After observing a bolide (meteorite fall) while driving, the witness observed near the road, above a field, white lights and flashing lights, as well as a dust cloud. The description strongly suggests a combine harvester, likely harvesting corn. Agricultural machinery is necessarily equipped with an orange rotating light, which could have been left on even though the machine was not on the road. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a combine harvester (see examples of combines seen at night in photos).
