Description: A motorist is intrigued by three successive observations made on September 23rd at approximately 8:30 PM. At the exit of highway A20, the witness describes a large triangular shape with three lights (red, yellow, and blue) at each end. A little further, she observes a similar shape farther away. And finally, around a bend, she spots a "potato-shaped" mass of light flying low and slowly, with small triangles around it. No other witnesses were collected. This case relies on 3 observations by an isolated witness during a car trip of about ten kilometers. The witness's stress is clearly evident during her account. The 2nd and 3rd observations may therefore have been influenced by the observation of the first PAN which strongly impressed the witness. All three observations can be subject to plausible explanation hypotheses, but none has been proven (see the investigation report). The strangeness of this case can thus be estimated at 0.5. The consistency of the account is low: the observer is isolated at the time of her account, even though she encounters other vehicles that do not seem surprised by the PAN, which is actually surprising to the witness. She was very moved by these observations during the interview, which may have affected the accuracy of her account. Furthermore, certain inconsistencies in the account (presence of the moon) weaken the consistency of the PAN, evaluated at 0.5. In conclusion, based on these elements, the GEIPAN classifies this case in category C, due to lack of verifiable information and cross-references.
