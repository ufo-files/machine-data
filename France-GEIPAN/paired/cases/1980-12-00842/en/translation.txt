Description: On December 24, 1980, around 0:15 a.m., a witness, from their home, spotted a green-colored light phenomenon for one to two seconds. This phenomenon appeared to descend from the sky and disappeared into the mountain. Despite an investigation with an active runway service team that night and ground searches, no additional information was gathered about this phenomenon, for which we lack data.
