Summary: Observations of the movement of a luminous object in the sky.
--- seg-dfc18523b0e7dd7a49c14e16 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 2, 1978, between 6:45 PM and 7:30 PM, several people in the departments of Loire and Haute-Loire observed the movements of a luminous object composed of several lights of different colors. Despite discrepancies in testimonies, all witnesses were intrigued by the absence of noise from this object. Despite a gendarmerie investigation, too few information were gathered to solidify any hypothesis.
