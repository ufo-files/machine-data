Summary: Observation of silent north-south movement of lights delineating a "V-shape": Lack of information
--- seg-a24b2cb913e495244dc22b88 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Saturday, March 14, 2009, between 10:00 PM and 10:05 PM, an individual on their terrace was intrigued by the regular S-N movement of a group of lights forming a "V" shape. The observation was very brief, and the witness did not notice any particular noise. The most plausible hypothesis (a low-flying plane or a formation of military aircraft) does not fit (appearance, silence); the GEIPAN was unable to request verification from air traffic control in time. A field investigation conducted several years later would yield very little reliable information. Without these verifications, this case is classified as C due to a lack of information.
