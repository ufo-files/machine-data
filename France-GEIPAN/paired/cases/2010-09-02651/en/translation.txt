Description: A witness in their garden, observed on September 1, 2010 at 21:58 a dark red object passing very quickly overhead. The object's N-S movement was observed for three seconds. No noise was heard. The level of strangeness of the observation is low. The consistency of the observation is also low (one unique witness expecting "to try to observe this type of phenomenon," a very brief observation of a very furtive phenomenon that the witness has difficulty describing in terms of shape... ). Since this testimony is not corroborated by a gendarmerie report (requested by GEIPAN), any investigation is impossible due to a lack of information. This case is classified as C.
