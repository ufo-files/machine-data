--- seg-077cd914f53521809017a81c [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 12, 2009, a witness noticed that a room in their home was illuminated and the source seemed to come from an oval-shaped object floating in the air in line with power poles. The observation lasted 20 seconds and no noise was heard. No other testimonies were reported. The additional investigations conducted by the mandated IPN (National Institute for Preventive and Radiological Nuclear Safety) mention work done in November 2009 on the EDF (Electricity Distribution Company) pole in the direction of the observation, and the presence nearby of a military balloon with powerful lighting. These elements allow for a rational explanation of the observation, concluding the investigation, and classifying the case. A case that initially seemed to present a certain level of strangeness is likely explained by the observation of a military balloon, which was "powerfully" lit at the time of the observation.
