Description: On December 13, 2009, around 11 PM, two individuals observed from their second-floor kitchen for an extended period, the dance of several blue-hued light rings in an overcast sky. The witnesses clearly observed the beams of a multiple-beam "Sky Tracer" projector, whose elegant and unusual choreography deceived their vigilance. This case is classified B as a probable observation of a multi-beam projector.
