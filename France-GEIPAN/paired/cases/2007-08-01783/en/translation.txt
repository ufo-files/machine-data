Description: On the night of August 15, 2007, between 11:25 PM and 11:45 PM, a witness observed a peculiar phenomenon in the clear sky. A dark gray metallic object surrounded by an orange halo and trails behind it crossed the sky. During the 6-second observation, the object moved from the East towards the Northwest. The object abruptly disappeared. No noise was heard. The description and duration are consistent with what one might see during an atmospheric reentry.
