--- seg-a63a8ad3aad5381dd1093f46 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Saturday, June 5, 2010, around midnight, a witness observed for 5 to 6 seconds a silent, yellow glowing ball surrounded by an orange halo. The trajectory was S-N. The described phenomenon resembles very strongly a Thai lantern, which can be found commercially in various colors. The firework observed by the witness also suggests that this night was the occasion for various festivities. The linear trajectory is characteristic of this type of observation; however, the wind was blowing from the North at midnight (recorded at the airport), which does not allow us to confirm the lantern hypothesis. Nevertheless, in mountainous areas, with weak wind, local breezes can be oriented differently from one valley to another. Of low strangeness but good consistency thanks to a good recounting of the facts, this observation case has many characteristics of a Thai lantern, but the absence of a record of the local wind does not allow us to confirm this hypothesis. This case is classified C due to the lack of very precise meteorological records, independent testimonies, and cross-checks.
