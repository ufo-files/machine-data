Description: Recent testimony (uncertain date) of an old observation. On December 28, 1978, between 9:00 and 10:00 PM local time, three people observed for 20 to 30 minutes a bright point of light moving irregularly and angularly across the sky. Changes in direction were noted and appeared random. The witnesses left the terrace while the PAN was still in motion. Only one testimony was collected. The description could correspond to a civilian or non-civilian aircraft actually following an unusual trajectory, but this cannot be ruled out (we do not have the means to investigate such old aeronautical traffic), or to a satellite. Indeed, the GEIPAN has experienced oddities caused by satellites whose trajectories are not necessarily straight. The strangeness is either due to perceptual effects (as described in the autocinematic effect article) for the zigzagging, or to the coincidence of a trajectory ending in solar illumination with another trajectory beginning in solar illumination, giving the impression of a single satellite with a significant change in trajectory. The oldness of the observation, the lack of tools and databases from that era, prevent a conclusion in this case. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies as C: lack of information.
