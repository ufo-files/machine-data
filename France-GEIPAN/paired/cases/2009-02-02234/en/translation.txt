Description: On February 11, 2009, around 8:20 PM, a witness observed a particularly bright star directly to the west. Viewed through binoculars and then with the zoom function of a camcorder, the object intrigued the witness. A few days later and at a different location, the witness made a similar observation. The witness observed Venus, particularly visible at that time of year, both with the naked eye and through their camcorder.
