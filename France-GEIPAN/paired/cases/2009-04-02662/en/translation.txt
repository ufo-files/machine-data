--- seg-05ea82c56de64706a6f0afcc [needs-review] ---
Description: A person reports in October 2010 their observation made on April 1, 2009. At 6:45 a.m., a witness, from an open window, observed through a gap in the overcast and dark sky, the passage O-E at three-second intervals of three identical white, circular phenomena in size, speed, and trajectory. A dark red shape then appeared, giving the witness the impression of seeing fire. The sky then brightened very quickly. No particular noise was heard during the continuous observation of about twenty seconds. Only one testimony was collected. The strangeness of this case is difficult to assess given the particular description of the observed phenomenon. Its consistency remains weak without any cross-references or independent testimonies. However, the observation zone being located in a highly frequented military air corridor, the hypothesis of military aircraft is probable, but some elements remain difficult to explain (complete absence of noise, thin red line behind the phenomenon). Unfortunately, the lack of available data after three years regarding any potential activity does not allow for a certain conclusion on this lead. This case is classified C due to the lack of independent testimonies and cross-references.
