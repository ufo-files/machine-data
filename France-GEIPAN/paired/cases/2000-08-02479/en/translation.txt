Summary: Observation of the passage of light points.
--- seg-d76d675d5847b164c909ed1a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Recent testimony (10/23/2009) of an old observation. Three people observed during the summer of 2008 (but the year remains uncertain) around 11 PM the passage in a line and without noise of several bright points in the sky at high altitude. Of variable brightness, these points very quickly crossed the sky in a probable direction from south to north and with a slightly elliptical trajectory. The phenomenon faded at the horizon. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected. Due to the lack of precise information, especially regarding the date, and given the age of the observation, any investigation is difficult, if not impossible.
