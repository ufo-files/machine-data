--- seg-282ba0fe8053d167884b5616 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 25, 2021, around 4:30 a.m., while driving his vehicle with a work colleague as a passenger, taking the A20 motorway ramp towards VIERZON (18), the witness suddenly noticed a significant bright phenomenon, colored yellow and orange in the center. The visual impression is that the PAN appears to be low - at the height of wind turbines located a few hundred meters from the witness - or behind them. It visually moves from left to right at a slow speed before heading towards NNE at a lightning speed and disappearing. Only the driver testifies to GEIPAN, but the precision of the testimony and its consistency with data recorded by 6 cameras in the FRIPON network allow us to conclude that a meteor was observed (see the investigation report). The strangeness for the witness is due to the observation of a phenomenon he does not recognize because of the perception of a large object, changing speed and trajectory, and without a trail. After analysis, the strangeness is low. On the day of the observation, in the observation area and at the indicated time, a meteor was present. It was observed and recorded by several cameras in the FRIPON network. The case offers good consistency. The testimony is precise. The location of the wind turbines was a good reference point to reconstruct the configuration. GEIPAN classifies as A: observation of a meteor.
