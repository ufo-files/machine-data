Description: On May 4, 2015 (the witness confirms the date) around 4 a.m., a witness, upon waking, was intrigued by the passage in the sky of a triangular shape induced by lights on the corners. The Pan passes at a right angle in front of his terrace, at the height of a plane passing at low altitude. The witness hears the noise of an aircraft flying at low altitude. No other testimony is collected. This observation case describes a phenomenon with many characteristics of an aircraft: fixed white-yellow lights, and flashing red and white lights, apparent size, and noise. But the witness, who does not distinguish a fuselage or wings, gives it a triangular shape, which makes him find this device strange. When an observer sees a set of glowing points, it is common for them to interpret this set of points as an object simply delimited by these glowing points. This illusion, known as shape or contour illusion, occurs with independent lights (such as Thai lanterns) and also with airplanes when the wings are poorly visible: the witness then reconstructs a black volume limited by the visible lights. If the witness did not see the wings or fuselage of the aircraft, it may be because his line of sight did not allow it (seeing the wings in profile), or more likely because the aircraft itself was very dark in color, as are, for example, large military transport planes. The axis of one of the runways at Bourget Airport does indeed pass through Épinay-sur-Seine (7.5km). The Meteorology report notes a southwest wind at Roissy: therefore, planes take off towards the west in the direction of Épinay (see the Meteorology report). The GEIPAN classifies this case as category B: probable observation of an aircraft. Unfortunately, the GEIPAN did not handle this observation case quickly enough to verify with air traffic control if there had indeed been a flight at that time.
