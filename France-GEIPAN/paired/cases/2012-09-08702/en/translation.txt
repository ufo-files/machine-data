Description: Recent testimony of an old observation. In May 2015, the GEIPAN receives a testimony of an observation made on September 16, 2012. That evening, around 11:30 PM, a witness observes the silent passage of several white glowing points in the sky. The witness notices this three times, with the glowing points always following the same trajectory. No other testimony is collected. The observation of glowing balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby during a private party. The witness's video also shows successive disappearances of lanterns, which is also typical (end of the burner's life). Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable Thai lanterns.
