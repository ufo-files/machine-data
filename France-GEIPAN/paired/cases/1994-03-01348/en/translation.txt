Description: On March 5, 1994, four witnesses went to the Col de Vence to attempt to catch sight of unidentified aerial phenomena. They did indeed observe three bright points forming a triangle that seemed to approach them. The phenomenon remained silent and was observed for approximately 1 minute and 30 seconds. No other information could be gathered.
