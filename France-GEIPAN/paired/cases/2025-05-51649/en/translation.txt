Summary: Successive observations of a red ball in motion in the night sky: probable observation of celestial lanterns.
--- seg-6f2ce42ccaf7d5832117a731 [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 14, 2025, at 10:19 PM, from their terrace in the commune of Nice, two witnesses observed an atypical luminous phenomenon. A red spherical source, apparently sized comparable to the solar disk, moves according to an irregular and non-straight trajectory, marked by notable speed variations. The observation lasts over a minute without any noise being heard, then the light gradually fades away. A identical phenomenon, concerning a single light source, is reported about ten minutes earlier under the same conditions. The case consistency is good, with one witness but two witnesses and a video of the PAN. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The hypothesis of celestial lanterns, developed in the investigation report, is corroborated by the description and visual aspect of the phenomenon, as well as by a trajectory perfectly consistent with wind direction. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B", probable observation of celestial lanterns.
