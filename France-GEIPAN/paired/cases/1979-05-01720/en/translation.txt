--- seg-ba4509572d64c6fe7d5d84ec [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 15, 1979, between 2:15 p.m. and 2:45 p.m., several people in fields observed an object on the ground that then slowly took flight horizontally. It had a spherical shape and a metallic color, moving while oscillating and following the contours of the terrain, gaining speed as it moved vertically. Some witnesses were able to see it for approximately 30 minutes before it gradually disappeared. No information was gathered regarding a possible weather balloon release in the area, but based on the descriptions provided, these people likely saw a balloon.
