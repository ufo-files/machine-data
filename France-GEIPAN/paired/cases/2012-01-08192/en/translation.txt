Summary: Observations of the atmospheric reentry of a bolide.
--- seg-3903f5dbab18b86220930681 [needs-review] ---
Description: The GEIPAN received numerous witness reports of a bright phenomenon passing through the sky on January 24, 2012, around 7:50 a.m., over the Lorraine region. All of these witness reports clearly correspond to the observation of a meteor: the entry into the Earth's atmosphere of a naturally occurring meteoroid (small asteroid). The reform-meteor network also identifies this event as a naturally occurring meteor. This case is classified "A."
