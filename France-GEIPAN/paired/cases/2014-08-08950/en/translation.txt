Description: On Saturday, August 9, 2014, at 10:40 PM, a witness in their garden was intrigued by the movement below the clouds of three large, round, white headlights arranged in a V-shape, and a red light. The PAN silently flies over his house, then accelerates and disappears. No other testimony is collected. The weather conditions for this observation case are as follows: overcast sky, north wind, and intense storm activity without rain (dry lightning): see the attached lightning impact weather map. For information, at the same time, the International Space Station (ISS) followed by the European cargo ATV5 were potentially visible in the North and Northeast at a low altitude on the horizon (see Calsky log), but the witness was not looking in that direction and the sky was overcast. He was unable to see this rather exceptional event. The witness's description does not at all correspond to this type of phenomenon. The observation of glowing balls close by during a thunderstorm suggests a globular lightning (ball lightning) phenomenon. The trajectory and behavior of the phenomenon are consistent with this hypothesis, but there are still some elements of the testimony that do not fit well: the red point and the relative position of the orbs that seem stable, which may have been due to a perception illusion. The reality of the triangle with the 3 lights is not certain: it is a well-known perceptual illusion to perceive an object encompassing the outline of bright points (called the "Gestalt" illusion). The GEIPAN does not have the means to confirm or deny this last hypothesis (other witnesses, photos), and as a result, this case is classified as "C" due to a lack of information and cross-references.
