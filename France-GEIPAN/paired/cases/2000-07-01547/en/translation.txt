Summary: Probable observation of aerial maneuvers
--- seg-cf8a30a53d847a89c578e810 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 27, 2000, around 11 PM, several witnesses observed the passage of objects with lights of various colors flying over houses. Trajectories varied depending on the point of observation. Although witnesses described them as flying saucers, it is likely these observations were of Mirage 2000s performing maneuvers in the area until 2 AM.
