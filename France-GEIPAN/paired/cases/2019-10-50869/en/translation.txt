Summary: Long observation of the movements of a luminous object of color above the sea: observation of a fantasy balloon of "Mylar" type.
--- seg-f342847fd3fc2141d237f2b8 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 25, 2019, at 11 a.m., a witness, from his terrace, observed, in the presence of his daughter and his wife, a luminous object, stationary over the sea, which he believed exhibited unusual behavior. Intrigued, the witness took his binoculars and then his camera. For 12 minutes, the witness observed the movements of the PAN (stoppages, rotation on itself, straight trajectory...). The object eventually took an upward trajectory and disappeared into the cloud mass. The witness attached photographs to his questionnaire and as attachments. Through analysis (see investigation report), we were able to show that the PAN observed by the three witnesses was very likely a Mylar fantasy balloon drifting with the light breeze present at the time and place of observation, possibly having escaped its owner from one of the beaches located to the southeast of the witnesses' position. This conclusion is based on the following elements: - whimsical shape, - "electric blue" color and high brightness, the metallic surface of the balloon reflecting sunlight, - presence of a "stem" used to hold the balloon, - movement in the direction of the dominant wind, negligible to very low, resulting in periods of immobility and rotation around a vertical axis of the whole, - calculations and measurements of speeds, distances, and dimensions compatible with the hypothesis. We finally noted that the balloon could come from one of the beaches west of Fort-de-France and that the school vacation period and mild temperatures were conducive to balloon sellers walking the beaches to sell them to children.

Classification: "A": Observation of a Mylar fantasy balloon.
