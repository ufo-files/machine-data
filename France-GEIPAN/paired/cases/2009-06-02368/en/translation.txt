Description: On June 3, 2009, a witness observed the fall of an object that struck a fir tree and rolled on the ground. The object measured 4.5 x 7.5 centimeters and weighed approximately 300 grams. He assumed it was a piece of meteorite. Analysis by the mineralogist attached to the Laboratory for the Study of Transfer Mechanisms in Geology at OMP Toulouse concluded in the certain identification of hematite, more commonly known as iron ore, and in no way a celestial body. No impact trace was found at the indicated location of the object's fall, and there were no other witnesses to this observation.
