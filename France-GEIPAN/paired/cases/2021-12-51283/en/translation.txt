Description: The GEIPAN was reported via an AIR SAFETY REPORT (incident report), provided by the crew of RYANAIR flight RYR2BB, flight FR4603 from LONDON to NANTES. The pilot reported encountering conflicting traffic at 6,000 feet while approaching NANTES Airport (NANTES ATLANTIQUE Airport, LFRS-NTE). The pilot was forced to perform an evasive maneuver. They accelerated their descent to avoid a collision. When the aircraft passed under the PAN, it was estimated to be 300 to 400 feet above the aircraft. The PAN did not appear on the TCAS (a cockpit instrument that prevents in-flight collisions between aircraft) or the airport radar. No other traffic was encountered during landing. The pilot mentioned the possibility of a drone. The BGTA NANTES-ATLANTIQUE (Air Transport Gendarmerie) investigated, considering the drone hypothesis likely. The file lacks information. No element confirms the presence of a drone. The GEIPAN, based on the gendarmerie investigation, did not conduct a specific inquiry. The GEIPAN classifies this case in category "C": Lack of Information.
