Summary: Observation of the rapid passage of a dark, flat lens-shaped object at low altitude: probable observation of a small aircraft in visual flight at low altitude, seen against the light.
--- seg-f844e3084d7bc07aef05bd18 [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 12, 2015, between 1:30 PM and 2:15 PM, a witness on a terrace observed planes high in the sky when they saw a dark, flat lens-shaped object moving very quickly at low altitude on a straight trajectory. The object disappeared from the witness's view, obscured by the landscape. Only one testimony was collected. An air traffic radar check was requested from the military control tower. The resulting tracks were overlaid on a Google Earth map (with an accuracy of around 500 meters): there is indeed quite a bit of air traffic from small aircraft flying visually (VFR), including a particular trajectory that seems to fit the observation quite well (see the radar map document and in particular the red track that moves from east to west through Plaisir from 12:10:48 UTC (2:10 PM local time) to 12:12:18. This aircraft slowly rises from east to west by 400 meters (Z12) to 500 meters (Z16) and flies at around 160 km/h (89-75 knots). The passage in front of the witness lasted less than a minute, compared to the 30 seconds of observation indicated by the witness. This device, probably small, appeared to the witness as farther away and therefore faster than it actually was. Since the witness was looking south, near the zenith hour, the device in contrail necessarily appeared black to them. Due to the low angular elevation, the wings, seen in profile, may be invisible. The GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable observation of a small aircraft in visual flight at low altitude, seen in contrail. Due to uncertainty about the observation time, this case is not classified as A.
