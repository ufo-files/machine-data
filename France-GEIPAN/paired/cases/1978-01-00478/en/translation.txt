Description: On January 16, 1978, at approximately 6:10 PM, two witnesses observed a circular, phosphorescent, white-yellow light phenomenon for one minute. It moved at a very high speed from North to South along a straight trajectory. During its movement, they noticed the object had a cylindrical shape with a red light, and then a bright green color was visible at the front of the object. No noise was heard during its movement, but the phenomenon left a glowing trail. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
