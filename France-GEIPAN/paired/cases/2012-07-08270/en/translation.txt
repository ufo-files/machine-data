Summary: Observation of the silent movement of six orange glowing orbs: probable observation of Thai lanterns
--- seg-2cb4f7b9eda021145733581a [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 1, 2012, around 11 PM, a witness observed the silent passage of several orange lights moving on a slightly curved trajectory towards the East. These lights suddenly changed direction to head North before quickly disappearing. The witness's description matches all aspects of an observation of Thai lanterns: orange color, smooth and silent movement according to the wind (from South West to North East, variable and weak, see weather report), and disappearance after a few minutes. The GEIPAN did not investigate the possible location of the release. (Perhaps the Priory Park, at the end of the Bô Festival?) This case is classified as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
