Summary: Probable observations of Thai lantern flights.
--- seg-4786f8f79c920562f5386b22 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 12, 2010, starting at 11:30 PM, several Calais residents observed from various locations a dozen orange and white glowing orbs in the sky. They appeared to come from the same place and moved slowly and silently along an ascending trajectory before disappearing. A local newspaper article from August 17, 2010, reports a witness who claimed to have seen a release of lanterns in front of the Detroit High School, but the gendarmerie collected no testimonies. These individuals likely observed the passage of Thai lanterns.
