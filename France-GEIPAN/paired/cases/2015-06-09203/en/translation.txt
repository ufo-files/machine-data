Description: On June 14, 2015, between 03:28 and 03:45, a witness observed in the sky a peculiar movement of an elongated oval object of a large size, with a yellow-orange color. The object moved silently, making successive stops before disappearing. Joining a second person for a ride-share, the two witnesses again observed the phenomenon and saw it in another commune before it was lost from sight. The photos and video taken by the witness were not usable. Only one testimony was collected. The phenomenon observed by the witnesses is consistent with the hypothesis of confusion with a nightclub laser ("sky tracker") used one weekend night (see the investigation report). Such an establishment is located in Jouy-sur-Morin, within the theoretical visibility zone of such a sky tracker. Although the investigator was unable to demonstrate the use of such a laser from that nightclub on the night of the observation, the explanatory hypothesis remains probable in light of other testimonial elements. The GEIPAN supports the hypothesis formulated by the witness. The objection he raised was the absence of beams rising from the ground. In fact, the projection beam of a laser can be or not be visible, depending on local weather conditions, with pollution also playing a very certain role, as well as the light power of this beam. The presence of fine particles suspended in the atmosphere is a primordial and indispensable factor for the formation of a light beam. These particles can be water droplets, microparticles of pollutants, etc. At the time of the observation, visibility was good (over 20 km) and no precipitation was recorded. The conditions were therefore rather favorable for a laser light beam to be invisible. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": probable laser animation of Sky Tracer or Sky Rose.
