Summary: Nighttime observations of the silent, straight passage of two white lights across the landscape: observation of an A400M aircraft.
--- seg-c3cbf06ca8c57d23150afacb [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of August 12 to 13, 2021, around 00:12, a couple on a walk along a path in VICQ-SUR-GARTEMPE (86) observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky. Two distinct and well-defined white light sources move at low altitude along a straight, perfectly horizontal trajectory from right to left at a constant, relatively high speed. These lights appear and disappear intermittently due to the interposition of a first grove of trees, before reappearing on the same trajectory. Finally, they disappear definitively behind a denser wooded area on the left. Of low strangeness and good consistency*, this case turns out to be the observation of an A400M French military aircraft (see the investigation report). *According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The analysis of the description of the PAN, correlated with its low angular height of observation, led to consider the hypotheses of a light source emitted by a terrestrial vehicle, a ship, or an aircraft. The analysis of the site configuration, in particular its topography, ruled out hypotheses involving a terrestrial vehicle or a ship. Additionally, a reconstruction using ADS-B Exchange data indicates the passage of an A400M French military aircraft at low altitude along the observation axis of PAN at the time reported by witnesses. The distance of the aircraft from the observation point justifies the absence of auditory perception. The case is classified "A", observation of an A400M aircraft.
