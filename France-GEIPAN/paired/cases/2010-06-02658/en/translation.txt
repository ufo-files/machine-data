Description: A witness observed from their balcony an unusual phenomenon on the night of June 5th to 6th, 2010, at 12:37 AM. A white light in the shape of a rectangle, coming from the mountains above Ajaccio, crossed the bay at an extremely high speed, without making any noise, and along a straight trajectory. The observation characteristics (night of Saturday/Sunday just after midnight, non-blinding white light, no noise, trajectory consistent with wind direction) suggest a luminous balloon of Thai lantern type.
