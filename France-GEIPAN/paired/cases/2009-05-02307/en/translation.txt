Description: On May 10, 2009 at 2:58 AM, two people in a vehicle observed the rapid passage of a white sphere surrounded by a slight halo. The phenomenon moved along a straight trajectory from south-east to north-east before completely disappearing. The observation lasted for a few seconds. Of low strangeness and very good consistency, this observation case has all the characteristics of a meteoroid re-entry. However, the absence of other testimonies prevents a firm conclusion. This case is classified B: probable bolide observation (atmospheric re-entry of a meteoroid).
