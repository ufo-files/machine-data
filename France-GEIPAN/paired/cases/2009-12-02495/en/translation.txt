Description: On December 27, 2009, at 1 a.m., a witness observed from his window the rapid S-NE passage of an intense, continuous green light. No noise was noticed during the observation, lasting several seconds. This observation exhibits all characteristics of an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid (meteorite), also known as a bolide: a straight-line traverse of the sky in a few seconds, green in color. This event was not noted by the REFORME observer network. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid (meteorite fall).
