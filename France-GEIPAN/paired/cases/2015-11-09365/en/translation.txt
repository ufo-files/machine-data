Description: On November 27, 2015, between 7:32 and 7:37 a.m., a driver on the A87 highway was intrigued by what she saw in the sky. She reported the presence of mobile glowing beams in the sky. These beams moved quickly and reminded her of a circular craft with beams in a swirling circle rotating at high speed. Only one testimony was collected. This report of circular lights spinning suggests clearly the light shows of a nightclub or other festive venue reflecting off cloud layers and/or fine rain (both were present that morning). Ascending beams are not visible in this report. However, depending on the air layer conditions, the patterns created (on the sky screen) or just the beams alone, without patterns, or both, can be seen, somewhat like in a movie theater, imagining at times cigarette smoke (visible beams) in conjunction or not with the presence of a screen (patterns present or not). According to atmospheric conditions, the laser effect distance varies greatly, reaching dozens of kilometers, which can make the phenomenon fleeting or exceptional at great distances from the emission source. The GEIPAN did not identify the beam emitter. The hypothesis of a technical test from the Puy-du-Fou installations was ruled out after investigation. Since some nightclubs stay open late into the night from Thursday to Friday, the origin of the phenomenon from a nightclub is possible even at 7:30 a.m. Nearby nightclubs could not be reached or refused to provide a precise response. Other similar cases are on the GEIPAN site (use keywords SKYROSE or LASER). Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B: SKYROSE or LIGHT SPOTS.
