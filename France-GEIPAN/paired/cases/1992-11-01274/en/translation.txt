Description: On November 16, 1992, around 4:30 a.m., a witness was awakened by a loud noise resembling a jet engine with two flashes illuminating their room. They then observed a large, very bright white glowing ball. After about ten seconds, a nearby villa was lit in red, and with another noise, the white light disappeared. The unoccupied villa at the time of the incident did not sustain any damage. After the white light disappeared, a red point persisted above a tree, and a light mist remained at the location of the white ball. Despite the described noise, no other testimony could be found. However, a neighbor, though they saw nothing, noted around the same time that their dogs were very frightened. The observed phenomenon could not be identified.
