Description: On May 21, 2008, between 11:30 PM and 12:20 AM, a witness observed the movement of a luminous phenomenon in the sky. Intrigued, he observed the phenomenon with binoculars and then filmed his observation. Given that the video leaves little doubt about the observed object: an airplane approaching, seen from the front, with all lights on, and that this area is likely a training zone for tactical transport aircraft, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of military aircraft in training. However, it should be noted that the 50-minute observation duration is not consistent with the sequence described by the witness: either the witness saw more than two passes of aircraft, which remains consistent with the proposed hypothesis, or he misjudged the total observation time.
