Description: On March 28, 1981, between 3:00 p.m. and 5:00 p.m., two individuals observed an object they initially mistook for a ball of fire. According to a northwest to northeast trajectory, the craft's speed was very high. Circular in shape, the object's circumference was approximately 3 to 4 meters. No further information was gathered.
