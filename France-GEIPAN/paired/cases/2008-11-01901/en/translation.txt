Description: On November 12, 2008, at approximately 10:40 PM, a driver observed a green light passing through a descending curve, followed by a cone-shaped trail. The observation lasted only a few seconds. The testimony is consistent and the phenomenon is corroborated by two other independent witnesses (found on a forum) and very distant, or even by a radio recording (see the investigation report). This observation, as the witness suspected after some research, corresponds well to the re-entry into the atmosphere of a meteor. In reality, it comes from the Taurid "North" meteor shower, which produces beautiful bolides that are relatively slow. The date also corresponds to the maximum activity of this shower, which, observed otherwise, was active at that time. Two testimonies from the Bordeaux region, consistent in time, description, and trajectory, confirm, given the distance between Bordeaux and Carspach, that the PAN was necessarily at a high altitude. This further strengthens the hypothesis. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Meteoroid Re-entry.
