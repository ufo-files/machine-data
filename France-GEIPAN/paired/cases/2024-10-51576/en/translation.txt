Description: On the evening of October 12, 2024, witness (T1) with his wife (T2) and their son (T3), residing in COLLEVILLE (76), observed blue flashes in the sky. These flashes, regular and localized to the same area, appeared at a constant angle. The observation took place from the driveway in front of their home, with the flashes visible on the horizon, above the neighbors' rooftops. It lasted approximately 3 minutes. Despite being a unique testimony (3 witnesses and a video of the PAN), the case is considered a probable observation of sky-tracer effects due to its low strangeness and good consistency. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The description and visual aspect of the PAN are very consistent with a sky-tracer (straight flashes, regular appearances, alternating every second), especially given that the observation was made on a Saturday night, under overcast skies. However, the investigation did not reveal the exact source of the PAN (see the investigation report). GEIPAN classifies the case as "B", probable observation of a sky-tracer.
