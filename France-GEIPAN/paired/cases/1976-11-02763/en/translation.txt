--- seg-58c4496cb6957b26f4d2f37a [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 25, 1976, around 07:10, a farmer hunting observed a distant red glow for a few seconds, mistaking it for a chimney fire. Dominating the landscape, he then saw a sparkling light approaching him on an E-W trajectory. When it reached ten or fifteen meters from the hunter, the phenomenon changed direction, and the witness realized he was facing an elongated object about two meters long. The cylindrical object was motionless at ten meters above ground, and a red intermittent glow spread around it. It then moved silently towards the South, stopped, started again, and disappeared, hidden by the landscape. Following a gendarmerie investigation, no other testimonies were collected, and no traces were found in the area indicated by the witness. It's difficult to conduct a retrospective investigation on such an old case (see investigation notes). The hypothesis of observing an aircraft is not retained. This unexplained case, with a single witness and low consistency, is classified as PAN D1 in the current GEIPAN classification due to its high degree of strangeness (close but brief observation, 8-10 seconds).
