Description: On March 23, 1980, at 10:20 PM, several people alert the Gendarmerie: they observe unexplained glowing phenomena moving at the base of the television relay tower on "Mont Caume." After immediate dispatch to the scene and checks conducted in the following nights, it was determined that the glowing phenomena originated from newly installed traffic signal projectors at the tower's base. Furthermore, the gendarmes will note that other observed light effects from this vantage point are merely public streetlights from nearby villages and vehicle headlights from moving cars. These luminous signals were at that time regularly obscured and disrupted by fog and unfavorable weather conditions.
