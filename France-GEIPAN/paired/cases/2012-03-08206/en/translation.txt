Description: On March 13, 2012, around 8:30 PM, a witness observed from their terrace the silent passage of a triangular formation composed of several green-orange lights moving at high speed and at low altitude. Another testimony will be collected regarding a very similar phenomenon that also occurred that evening. Following an investigation entrusted to a PNI and in light of the received information, the GEIPAN classifies this observation as B, probable observation of an atmospheric reentry of meteoroids (see investigation report).
