Description: On Saturday, August 24, 2013, at 10 PM, while taking photographs, witnesses were intrigued by the presence of a sphere to the left of the Moon. The witness took a photo that later revealed a second sphere. The witness's photo clearly shows the Moon: a small, slightly curved white disk (see Stellarium map). The two large bright disks to the left are photographic artifacts, sometimes called "orbs," created by dust or water droplets very close to the lens, and illuminated by the flash. This does not explain the second large bright ball observed by the witness to the left of the Moon: lunar halo? Stadium lamppost (see Google photo)? Another phenomenon? Due to this doubt and the lack of evidence provided by the photo, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of information.
