Summary: Observation of the silent movement and disappearance of a luminous object.
--- seg-8ed81476f51eccad6a92830e [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 28, 1978, between 7:30 PM and 7:40 PM, a motorist and her son observed a very bright orange object moving rapidly across the sky from north to south. The observation lasted three minutes during which no unusual noises were heard. The object stabilized and then slowly moved south before disappearing instantly. No traces were found at the site. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected, leaving it unexplained.
