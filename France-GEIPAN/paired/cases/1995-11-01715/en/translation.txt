Description: On November 28, 1995, around 5 a.m., a witness heading to work observed eight orange glowing spheres arranged in a circle. These spheres moved silently and were noticed by another witness despite the thick fog surrounding them. The two witnesses were able to observe the phenomenon for approximately 10 minutes before leaving the area to go to work. No further information could be gathered about this event.
