Description: Alarmed by newspaper articles in the local press, the gendarmerie is investigating a peculiar phenomenon observed by several individuals. On April 24, 1976, around 4:00 p.m., a couple in the countryside noticed an unusual noise, followed by a strong air movement and the appearance of two green glowing discs at 300 meters from them. These objects suddenly dove towards the ground at a very high speed before disappearing. A violent gust of wind was followed by a snowfall. The same phenomenon occurred twice more. In the vicinity, other people reported television interference at the same time. Local brigade intelligence suggests it is likely an electromagnetic phenomenon caused by a local meteorological event.
