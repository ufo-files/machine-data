Description: On August 9, 1980, at 10:18 PM, two witnesses observed from their home a glow in the sky. It moved from north to south, then changed direction to head southwest, making a curve. It had a circular shape with a very bright core. It also changed color, going from white-yellow to red-violet. No noise was heard during the observation. The glow suddenly disappeared. No other testimony was collected regarding what appears to be a probable atmospheric reentry.
