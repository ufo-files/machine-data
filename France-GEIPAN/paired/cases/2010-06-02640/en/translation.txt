Description: On June 10, 2010, between 10:30 PM and 11:00 PM, a witness observed from the terrace of his home a bright point that intrigued him; it was stationary in the SE for approximately 15 minutes before moving towards the NE and disappearing behind the landscape. Following the gendarmerie report, investigations conducted by the Gendarmerie were unable to recontact the main witness or the other designated witnesses. No rapid communication channels (phone, email) allowed for contact with the witness. The time elapsed since the observation and the information contained in the report do not permit further investigation or the conduct of an inquiry. The duration (unverifiable) and the concise description by a single witness of the phenomenon leave open all possibilities (waiting aircraft?, balloon with its payload below ('circular shape with a plume below?'), something else…?). The case is classified C due to lack of information.
