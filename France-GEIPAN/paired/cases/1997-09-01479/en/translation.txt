Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archives on its public website www.geipan.fr. In its publications, it includes older cases classified at the time (A, B, C, or D) that are now being re-examined, with the sole purpose of being more relevant in the conclusions. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over all these past years, this re-examination sometimes leads to new observations or even a change in classification. This previously classified D case named PARIS (75) 1997 is part of a set of cases recently reviewed. On September 7, 1997 at 01:43, a witness observed from their balcony a large triangular shape with two blue and one green light moving slowly along a straight trajectory. The observation lasts 12 seconds. No noise was perceived, but the witness felt vibrations in his body, and when he tried to take a picture, his camera froze. Only one testimony was collected. The testimony describes numerous oddities that, taken at face value, could have led the GEIPAN of the time (SEPRA) to classify the case as unexplained. This new examination does not allow for confirming this classification. Indeed, the testimony lacks sufficient consistency, especially in terms of reliability, to validate its strange character and thus becomes unusable. The elements of strangeness (unexplained) are strong and numerous, but they present strong differences and inconsistencies according to their source: the police report of the gendarmerie interview the day after the observation, and the witness's publication made 3 months later and handed over to the gendarmerie by that witness. The witness is the editor-in-chief of a magazine "Strangeness and Mysteries" and publishes his observation in his own magazine. It can be noted that it is therefore in his interest to have, for his review, testimonies leading to mystery. But we first look at the inconsistencies between the two descriptions of the observation: - initiation of the observation. In one case (PV), the trigger is visual; the witness first sees the triangle, observes it with binoculars, then with a camera, and feels vibrations in their body and in the glass panels. In the other (publication), the strange starts with vibrations, not from the stained-glass windows, but from numerous plants on the balcony with details on the synchronism with vibrations in his body, then he sees the triangle in the sky. It is surprising that the witness does not know exactly what started the strange, as it is surprising that what a priori is the strangest (vibrations around in the absence of noise) affects such different elements (windows, plants). It is noted that there is a temporal inconsistency already present between the two interviews with the gendarmes (at the gendarmerie and at home). - duration: 12 seconds in one case and over 17 seconds in the other where it is mentioned that it was measured with a stopwatch. Why is the stopwatch mentioned three months later to provide a different information? - Angular size: the PAN is covered by 3 fingers in one case, 4 in another. - Camera: in one case, the witness reports the impossibility for the camera to capture the image and simply states (without conveying any strangeness) that he took no pictures. But three months later, it's a real oddity: the device refuses to take a photo when he presses the shutter, even after switching to manual focus. These are enough elements to question the reliability of a single human testimony without trace of photo or others, as such a level of strangeness reported (methodology GEIPAN) demands a very high level of reliability to characterize the observation as unexplained. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as C: Unusable testimony. The fact that the witness provides his testimony in his own magazine dedicated to mysteries and the strange, where he states his conviction regarding the reality of the strange and the conspiracy to hide this reality, is another factor in questioning the reliability (although the inconsistencies noted are already sufficient to conclude on reliability). Indeed, the witness's testimony serves their activity in their magazine and the positions and convictions held by them or the magazine.
