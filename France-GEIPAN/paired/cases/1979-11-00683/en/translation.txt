Summary: Observation of a diamond-shaped craft. Abnormal behavior on sheep.
--- seg-d7718b135fb1a65b86df08a8 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 21, 1979, a witness saw approximately 100 meters away an object equipped with several lights, including three red and one orange, forming a vertical diamond shape. The orange light alternately replaced the red lights by rotating in a clockwise direction. Stationary and making no noise, the object hovered in the air. No unusual odor emanated from the craft, which then disappeared towards the North, leaving a white smoke. Sheep penned 600 meters away showed great agitation. A second testimony was collected, describing the observation of red lights forming a diamond. No ground traces or other information were collected on this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
