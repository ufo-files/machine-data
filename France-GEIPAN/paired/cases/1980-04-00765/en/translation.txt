Description: On the evening of April 13, 1980, two individuals observed from their residence glowing points in the sky. These points moved rapidly at high altitude in a north-south direction. They emitted a blinking white light. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information. It is, however, likely that it was a high-altitude aircraft passage.
