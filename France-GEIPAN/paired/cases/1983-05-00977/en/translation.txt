--- seg-4c301747bd0a980eb0847ec8 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of May 5, 1983, between 2:00 and 2:15 a.m., a driver noticed a glow on the right front window of his vehicle: this glow moving up and down and in all directions intrigued the driver, who continued his journey. Very quickly, the witness saw the glow again, forming a circular arc before moving directly towards his vehicle. Overcome with fear and panic, he stalled and pulled over to the right side of the road. The witness noticed that the car's radio was static and heard a loud crack. The light then rose to the right and disappeared in the direction of Pau. The witness described a blue-tinged, oval light phenomenon 4 to 5 meters long, moving quickly but without noise or trail. Although the driver crossed paths with a trucker who likely also saw the phenomenon, only one testimony was collected. The witness described a clear night with a well-clear sky and normally starry, but also noted it was muggy with a light south wind. Initially classified as D, this observation case was re-examined, and the testimony was sent to the Lightning Research Laboratory in Cantal. The information provided by the witness now leads to the following hypothesis: the driver likely encountered a globular lightning phenomenon. The loud crack heard by the witness is a characteristic of globular lightning or a tracking igniter. Moreover, even if the dimensions given by the witness seem exaggerated at first glance, diameters of 10 to 15 meters have been observed for this type of phenomenon. This case is classified as B: probable observation of a globular lightning phenomenon.
