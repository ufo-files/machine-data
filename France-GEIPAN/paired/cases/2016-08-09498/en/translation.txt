Description: The GEIPAN has finalized the review of certain cases under investigation that had been pending classification. He publishes this synthesis today with the available elements from both the testimony and investigation gathered at the time. On August 02, 2016, a witness observed for 5 seconds in the clear night sky the silent and linear passage of 5 dark objects in triangle shape with very low intensity red points at the ends. The V-shaped formation moves away quite quickly and then it disappears from view. Only one witness statement is collected on this phenomenon. Testimony extracted from the free text of the questionnaire: "I am walking to join the studio where I sleep because the house has two floors." Around 23:05, before reaching the studio, I glance at the sky to observe the stars, then I see shadows in the sky that seem to be moving. I look closely and I see objects moving linearly in the shape of a triangle, with a red glowing point at each end (less bright than a star) that remains illuminated (no interruption). These shadows moved quite quickly (my observation lasted approximately 5 seconds) and formed a V shape, like birds in flight, at first I thought it was a flock of birds but when I saw these bright points at the ends, I realized it wasn't birds. I also thought about planes, which seems logical, but there was no noise in the sky because where I live is rural. "Then these shadows continued on their way, out of sight." The strangeness perceived by the witness stems from the logical deduction of what he observes, as there is an element that doesn't fit between what he knows and what he sees. At first, he thought he saw birds, but it is not normal for them to have red lights, even of low intensity. He logically deduces that they are planes, but it is mainly the absence of noise, in addition to the shape and the various red lights, that strikes him as strange. A field investigation accompanied by a cognitive interview was conducted on 06/26/2017 at the witness's home. Clarifications reported by the witness allow for some points to be specified: silent passage north 360° (+ or - 5°) towards northeast 65° (+ or - 5°) at 25° (+ or - 3°) elevation, most likely hour between 23:00 and 23:20, observation duration between 5 and 9 seconds. Following this investigation and having explored several weakly consistent hypotheses (Thai lanterns, formation flying aircraft, migratory birds, laser), the investigator proposed a classification B, drone: "There then remains only the hypothesis of a possible drone: the free construction of such a device can very well correspond to the description given by the witness. But several elements oppose this hypothesis: absence of noise (but it might be hidden by the noise of cicadas), ban on night flying and overflight of a populated area, and significant risk of crash. The conclusion of the various analyses conducted in 2019 led to the hypothesis of bird migration. However, the presence of even low-intensity red lights remains a discriminating factor. Despite the good collaboration of the witness, the consistency of the case (one witness, no photo of PAN) and the limited data available do not allow for further identification of the lights and shapes explaining the phenomenon. The consistency is average. The strangeness is also average. The case is classified as "C" according to the GEIPAN methodology.
