--- seg-523957dccbabcf1e1f254c22 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness makes two distinct observations at two different dates: Around January 25, 1979, at approximately 10 PM, this witness, accompanied by another person, notices that their vehicle is illuminated by a headlight above their car. Taking the time to stop, they see a triangular object emitting different colored lights, predominantly orange-red, accompanied by two headlights aimed at the ground. The object moves slowly while emitting a whistling sound. Although very close to the witness (5 meters), they cannot assess the size of the object as it disappears behind a hill. The second observation occurred around February 10, 1979: around 8 PM, from their apartment, the witness claims to have heard a rumbling sound from outside. They then observe an object composed of two parts, emitting white and red lights, moving in a north-south direction. Upon reaching the urban area, the object is joined by a series of bright objects in motion, also emitting lights for about a half-hour. Despite the significant duration of this lighting phenomenon, no other testimony was collected regarding this event, for which we lack information.
