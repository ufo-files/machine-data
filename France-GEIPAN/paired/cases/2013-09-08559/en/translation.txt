Description: On Sunday, September 22, 2013, at 7:45 a.m., a witness walking to his workplace was intrigued by the presence in the sky of a very thin and long cylinder-like object moving faster than any previously seen plane. The object disappeared into the sky towards the southwest. Given the elements defined in the report, namely the altitude and azimuth of the PAN consistent with those of planes present in the area at the time of observation, and its appearance resembling that of a sunlit aircraft, we can conclude that the PAN observed by the witness is likely a commercial airliner flying at a medium altitude and illuminated by the rising sun. This testimony is quite consistent: fairly precise but from a single witness without a photo. The observation is not particularly strange; the PAN was simply observed under special conditions easily explicable. This case is classified as "B" for observation of a commercial airliner at sunrise: the testimony is not precise enough to identify the exact plane observed among the multitude of planes at that time.
