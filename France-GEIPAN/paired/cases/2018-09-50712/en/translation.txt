Description: On September 20, 2018, around 9:00 PM, a witness driving their vehicle observed a rectangular or oval PAN with flashing red and yellow lights. The object appears to be hovering over the Cinqueux forest (60). Continuing its route, it returns to the site 45 minutes later but the PAN has disappeared. The witness provided a concise statement to the gendarmerie, which was later supplemented by a questionnaire partially completed by the gendarmerie. The witness was contacted to complete the questionnaire but no response was received. Only one witness statement is collected on this phenomenon. The Gendarmerie investigation indicated that the lights reported by the witness could resemble the beacons of an aircraft, so this lead was pursued. The Creil Air Base indicated that they had no flights during this period. A drone overflight was scheduled in Verneuil-en-Halatte, but the pilot reported that this flight had been canceled. The ABC helicopter company, which was authorized to conduct low-altitude flights in the sector, indicated that no flights had been conducted on the date mentioned by the witness. So the hypotheses of military aircraft, drone, helicopter are not validated. On the other hand, three civilian aircraft passed through this sector at the mentioned times: - AF1565 F-GKXS Birmingham-Roissy - BA2625 G-DBCH Nice/London - IBK2906 EI-FJP London/Copenhagen. It is therefore very likely that the witness made a mistake with an aircraft. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable misidentification with an aircraft.
