Description: Around 7:02 a.m., the witness observed, with the naked eye and from the hotel balcony where she was staying, a unique phenomenon with a nearly point-like appearance, constant brightness, and white color. The phenomenon appeared stationary and very high in the sky. The witness took 3 pictures of the phenomenon, which was also observed by her husband. After that, the witness left the balcony. The case consistency is good with two witnesses, one very precise testimony, and 3 photographs of the PAN. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony. This observation perfectly matches the classic description of a planet (or a first-magnitude star), which the main witness had suggested (see the investigation note). Research conducted using Stellarium and Google Earth reveals that planet Jupiter was in a position very close to the PAN that evening: the same azimuth (within 0.2°) and the same angular height (within 3°) as the PAN. The deviations can be explained by uncertainties related to the accuracy of calculations, impreciseness of plots, and distortions in the photograph. The description of the PAN (shape, color, fixity, position), corroborated by the photographs taken, supports the hypothesis of a celestial body, with a very high level of confidence, especially since the investigations show the presence of planet Jupiter in a position very close to that of the PAN. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: observation of Jupiter.
