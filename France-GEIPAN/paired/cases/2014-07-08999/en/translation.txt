Description: A witness observes from his apartment a phenomenon in the shape of a flattened rugby ball of bright orange color moving across the night sky (23:45). He repeats the observation three times in succession. The observation of red-orange balls silently crossing the sky in a few minutes on the same trajectory strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement is observed from North to South, which is consistent with the light North wind reported at Roissy (see météociel). The observed stop during the trajectory could correspond to flying over a geographical peculiarity: it might be a forest or the hill of Fosses Maloré. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns. The original photos provided support this hypothesis.
