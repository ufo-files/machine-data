Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archive on its public website www.geipan.fr. Among its publications are old cases classified at the time (A, B, C, or D) that are now being re-examined with the sole purpose of being more relevant in their conclusions. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over the past many years, this re-examination sometimes leads to new observations or a change in classification. A previously classified case D and published under the name BOURG-MADAME (66) 1981 is part of a recent set of cases re-examined. This case involved observations by three witnesses in three different communes in the department of Pyrénées-Orientales (66) during the months of September and October 1981. These three PAN observations of different nature had been mistakenly considered part of the same phenomenon during the initial analysis. The failure to detect this led to a perception of strong strangeness (initial D classification). The new analysis (see the investigation report) highlights that these are indeed three observations of PAN of different nature. - SAINTE-LEOCADIE (66) 19.09.1981 - CORNEILLA-DE-CONFLENT (66) 27.09.1981 - VERNET-LES-BAINS (66) 03.10.1981

Regarding CORNEILLA-DE-CONFLENT (66) 27.09.1981: Around 3 a.m., a witness observes in the direction of the West a PAN of red color surrounded by an aureole of the size of a star moving slowly south on a straight trajectory below the highest ridge line and above the closest hills. The PAN is at the level of the Fuilla valley when the witness loses sight of it. A second witness is present but will not give a statement to the Gendarmerie. It turns out that the observation of the PAN is entirely compatible with a phenomenon at ground level with a calculated movement speed, based on the azimuths of the observation axis, compatible with a terrestrial vehicle or even pedestrian movement. Therefore, the search for an explanation goes beyond the sole competence of GEIPAN since the observation is compatible with a phenomenon entirely located on the ground. GEIPAN classifies the case as C: Uninvestigable as it exceeds the sole competence of GEIPAN.
