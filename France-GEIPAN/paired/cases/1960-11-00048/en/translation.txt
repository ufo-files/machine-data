Summary: Observation of the landing and then the silent takeoff of a circular craft on a disused railway track: likely a hoax.
--- seg-5f2fe06a37b04833d4706914 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Saturday, November 5, 1960, around 8:30 PM, two individuals observed green-orange-red lights appearing to emanate from a slowly moving craft in the sky. A few days later, on November 13 at 2:45 AM, one of the witnesses was intrigued by a green glow in his room. After waking his companion, she confirmed the blinking of green-orange-red lights, and then went back to sleep. The witness then went to his door and noticed a circular craft, several meters in diameter, landing 150 meters away on a disused railroad track near his home. The silent craft, resting on a tripod, emitted orange and green lights. The observation lasted approximately twenty minutes before the craft silently lifted off and disappeared towards the south-west, alternating green lights. Intrigued, the witness reported his observation to a regional newspaper reporter. No traces were found at the scene during a gendarmerie investigation. Investigators interviewed several residents, including two SNCF employees on duty that night at the level crossings in the affected area; nothing unusual was noted by these railway workers that evening. During the investigation, the credibility of the primary witness, well-known to the authorities, was strongly challenged by all those questioned and local authorities' representatives. No other testimony regarding this observation corroborated the witness's account. The GEIPAN classified this case as "B," likely a hoax.
