Description: On February 10, 2015, at 6:00 PM, a driver noticed in the sky a white, striped chemtrail with a descending spiral trajectory and another object to the right of the trail. The GEIPAN requested from military air traffic control information about air traffic at that time in that area. It appears that a military aircraft was flying precisely in that area between 6:00 PM and 6:05 PM. However, it was not possible to verify if the spiral condensation trails described by the witness correspond exactly to the trajectory of the military aircraft. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of condensation trails from a military aircraft in maneuver.
