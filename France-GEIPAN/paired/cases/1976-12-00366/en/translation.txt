Description: On December 7, 1976, around 5:45 PM, a bus driver and several schoolchildren observed a luminous spherical phenomenon moving in the sky twice. The very bright object, above the sea, moved slowly towards the earth before disappearing. One of the hypotheses considered is the observation of an astronomical object. However, no further precise information was gathered about this phenomenon, for which we lack details.
