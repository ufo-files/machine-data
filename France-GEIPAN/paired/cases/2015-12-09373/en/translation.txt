--- seg-2103bbfa4287c94e1edc74d4 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 13, 2015, around 11:30 PM, a witness observed in the night sky a stationary bright point: it flickered in several colors (red, yellow, green, blue). This point, at a very high altitude, seemed too bright to be a satellite. He filmed the phenomenon. His wife also observed the PAN. The witness decided to stop observing. Only one testimony was collected on this phenomenon. The witness observed and filmed the star Sirius, under exceptional conditions conducive to generating strong scintillation (see the investigation synthesis). This analysis was carried out using the specialized software of the CNES/GEIPAN : IPACO. The GEIPAN classified this observation as "A": astronomical misperception.
