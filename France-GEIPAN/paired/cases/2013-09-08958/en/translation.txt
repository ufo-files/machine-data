Description: Recent testimony of an old observation. The witness observed several orange balls moving at low altitude in the sky and fading one after the other. The observation of orange balls slowly moving in the sky for several minutes during a Saturday to Sunday night clearly evokes Thai lanterns, probably released after a festival. The GEIPAN was unable to verify that these balls were indeed moving with the wind, as the testimony was too vague. The GEIPAN classifies this testimony as somewhat strange in category "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
