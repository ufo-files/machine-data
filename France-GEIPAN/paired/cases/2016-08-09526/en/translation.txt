Summary: Two observations of orange-colored light phenomena at ground level in the same rural area: probable ground lights from a vehicle or human activities.
--- seg-1510dbe6919b3dc687c11aa5 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 17, 2016 at 5:45 AM, a witness on their terrace was intrigued by an orange-colored light phenomenon directly West in a field at ground level. He observes, with the naked eye and then with binoculars, a large, stationary, orange-colored light form accompanied by three small, aligned, glowing balls that appear to rotate around the larger light. The witness estimates the phenomenon to be at a distance of 2km. The observation lasts 2 to 3 minutes: the witness goes to get his camera and upon his return notices the disappearance of the phenomenon. Only one testimony is collected. This observation from Peigney (52) of a ground phenomenon at 5:45 AM, on a hill 2 km away (in the commune of Langres (52)), is not very strange as it could be an agricultural vehicle, or even just a simple vehicle (since it is on or near a path) with people moving around it with flashlights (the witness perceived a rotation of lights effect). On the police report, the witness describes a similar observation made on June 4, 2012, around 3 a.m.: the sighting of lights on the ground moving around a central light initially described as a flame, on the same hill (dozens of meters apart), with the additional detail that there is a church there that can accommodate scouts. This therefore constitutes another hypothesis for the observation of August 17, 2016. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable ground lights from a vehicle or campfire, and people with flashlights or torches.
