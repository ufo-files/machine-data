Description: A witness reports to GEIPAN on March 5, 2009, a brief observation on March 13, 1994. Between 5:00 and 5:20 PM on a road leading to Ploubalay, the witness observed an object moving very quickly towards the North on a slightly inclined trajectory. The object disappeared into the clouds. No precise information was gathered on the shape and size of the high-altitude object. The age of the observation and lack of information make any investigation difficult, if not impossible.
