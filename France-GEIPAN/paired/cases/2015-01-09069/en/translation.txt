Description: On January 2, 2015, at 1:30 AM, a witness was observing the stars from his window. He suddenly saw a chaotic group of about a dozen glowing orbs silently flying over the city. The observation lasted 15 seconds before the objects were hidden by rooftops and the horizon. Only one testimony was collected. The witness, contacted by phone, eventually indicated that his window faces southwest. The astronomical map confirms that Orion is indeed in the southwest (not west as the witness stated), and the Moon is in the southwest/south; therefore, the cardinal points need to be adjusted: the witness saw the objects heading towards southeast. The Meteociel report for Troyes indicates a light southeast wind measured at ground level, which is apparently incompatible with the objects moving towards southeast, assuming they were carried by the wind. The observation of yellow orbs crossing the sky in one minute, on a weekend night during celebrations, strongly resembles Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party; the witness's description of these objects matches these items frequently launched on various occasions, but the behavior and movement direction of these objects do not correspond to the wind reported in Troyes. A weather expert from Météo-France indicated that the wind at altitude was from the southwest sector, perpendicular to the ground wind; this could explain the turbulent movements, but not the southeast direction. The hypothesis of Thai lanterns released for celebrations cannot be confirmed. Due to the lack of supporting evidence such as photos, videos, or other testimonies, the GEIPAN classified this case as "C".
