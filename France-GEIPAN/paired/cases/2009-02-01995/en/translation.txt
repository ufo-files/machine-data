Description: On Friday, February 20, 2009, around 7 a.m., a driver observed a north-to-south passage in the sky of a luminous phenomenon with blue/green lighting. It was not possible to corroborate this testimony with the database of amateur meteor observers or the French meteor observation network. However, the characteristics reported by the witness are fully consistent with an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid. This case is classified as B, indicating a probable bolide (meteoroid fall).
