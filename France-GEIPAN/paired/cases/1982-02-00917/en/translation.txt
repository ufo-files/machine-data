Description: On February 19, 1982, at approximately 6:30 PM, two witnesses observed a very bright, stationary point much larger than the star of the shepherd. Near this point, a cylinder was also stationary. It appeared to emit a cold, non-glowing light. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted about 4 minutes. As the witnesses moved, the objects disappeared into the landscape. No other information was gathered.
