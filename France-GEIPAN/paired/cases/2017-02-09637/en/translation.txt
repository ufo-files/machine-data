Summary: Observations of an opaque white light ball moving through a forest and then positioning itself in front of the vehicle; instantaneous disappearance: observations of a ball lightning-type "globular lightning".
--- seg-faf60b6718093bba0b3a7c1d [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 24, 2017, around 7:25 PM, a driver and his son, the front passenger, were traveling on a small rural road surrounded by woods. Suddenly, the driver spotted a white ball-shaped object on his right that was moving parallel to their path and in the same direction. This phenomenon then positioned itself in front of the car and seemed to lose intensity. The opaque ball was observed for one minute and thirty seconds before disappearing instantly without leaving a trail. No particular noise was noted. The description of the observation made by both witnesses corresponds to a case of ball lightning of the "globular lightning" type. This hypothesis is very consistent in terms of visual appearance, apparent trajectory, and altitude. This type of phenomenon occurs during stormy weather, but without necessarily an active storm in the area. The testimonies were submitted to the Lightning Research Laboratory, which unambiguously recognizes a ball lightning phenomenon. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of ball lightning of the "globular lightning" type.
