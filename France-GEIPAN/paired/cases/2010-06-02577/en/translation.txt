Description: On June 4, 2010, at 11:15 PM, a witness observed for two to three minutes in the sky three bright points. Stationary and the same size, they all disappeared at the same time. The witness did not wish to testify at the gendarmerie and no other testimony was collected on this phenomenon. The description of the phenomenon (successive appearance of 1 then 3 bright points) suggests once again the observation of luminous lanterns appearing successively in the sky and then going out ('disappearing' to the witness). The weather at midnight at the Montpellier station indicates a light north wind compatible with the trajectory of a light 'object' described by the witness. The observed phenomenon has a low degree of strangeness. The hypothesis of observing flying lanterns is the most probable.
