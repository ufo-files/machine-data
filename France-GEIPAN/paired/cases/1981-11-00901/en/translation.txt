Summary: Very long observation of a stationary light: Sirius observation
--- seg-37b591b87d9acee88df5027b [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 28, 1981 at 12:45 AM, two gendarmes on duty observed an extremely bright light in the sky. The light was stationary and seemed to blink. Using binoculars, they were able to discern around the light, several colored light flashes. These flashes lit up and went out in a chaotic manner. As the gendarmes had a star map, they located the glowing point to the left of the Orion constellation. This observation lasted several hours. After checking with an astronomy software, these witnesses undoubtedly saw Sirius that night, which is particularly visible in the sky at this time of year.
