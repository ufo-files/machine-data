Description: On September 6, 2015, at 12:15 AM, a witness is driving between Airvault (79) and Viennay (79) when they observe the silent movement of 8 to 9 lights in the clear sky. They first form two different figures, the first four in a square shape, the last three or four irregularly aligned. The witness then stops at a parking lot and particularly distinguishes the last light above him. He sees a perfect circular object of orange to yellow color and takes a picture with his mobile phone. The observation lasts 8 to 9 minutes in total. No other testimony is collected. The observation does correspond well to a formation of Thai lanterns, considering the overall appearance, the dominant orange color, the time of night on a weekend, or the closer, detailed observation of one of them passing vertically ("light appearing as a perfect circle with orange or yellow color circulating inside"). The point regarding the speed assessment (90 km/h) cannot correspond to reality given the a priori wind speed. But the speed assessment remains difficult and subjective, and is, in fact, inconsistent here with other elements of the testimony that indicate a much lower speed. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies this observation as B: strong probability of Thai lanterns.
