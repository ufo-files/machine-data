Description: On June 20, 2006, at 11:30 PM, a witness in the Fontainebleau forest was intrigued by the passage of a white-cream glowing disk in the sky. The trajectory was straight from West to East with a steady speed. No particular noise was heard during the two to three-minute observation. This peculiar phenomenon of a glowing point passing through the sky, according to the direction of the wind, suggests a Thai lantern, likely released during a solstice celebration. The oval shape, the cream color (the observer is in complete darkness, any light seems white) are consistent with this hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
