Summary: Observation of bright points in the sky on the move: probable airplane observation.
--- seg-3eab639915d17c2f96e8eca8 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Sunday, August 19, 2012, at 8:30 PM, a witness was intrigued by the movements of two bright points in the sky. Based on the objective evidence gathered during the investigation, namely that the initial behavior of the PANs resembles that of aircraft observed moving along the witness's line of sight, that the color and brightness of the PANs resemble that of landing lights on commercial airplanes, and that the area overflown is frequently used by all types of civilian and military aircraft and is near airports, the witness likely observed an airplane. However, the final behavior of the first PAN, moving in a "right angle," is, in the context of a zenith observation, a priori incompatible with such aircraft, but it's a common perception illusion with all moving bright points that fade into the night. At the time of the investigation, GEIPAN no longer had access to aeronautical data to formally verify this hypothesis. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of an airplane.
