Description: On July 28, 2010, around 10:15 PM, several people observed the passage of luminous objects in the shape of hot air balloons. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted for 5 minutes. Only one witness reported his observation. The gendarmerie's investigations concluded that the observation was likely of Thai lanterns.
