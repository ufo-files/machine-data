Summary: Observation of the silent passage of two orange glowing orbs in the sky: observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-ba6beaa3424c5439ab3b1c6e [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 13, 2015, around 0:25 a.m., a witness at their residence spotted in the clear sky the silent passage of two glowing red-orange orbs. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of orange orbs crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from southeast to southwest, which is consistent with the southeast to north wind reported in Brive-Souillac (refer to the Météociel weather report for the observation day in Brive-Souillac). The very precise testimony and especially photo 477 allow us to confirm this hypothesis: the "madeleine" shape, the characteristic color, and the brighter center leave no doubt. Other photos show the phenomenon from a distance, and are more difficult to interpret. The witness, an amateur astronomer, was surprised by this unusual object, of a nature quite different from the stars he is well acquainted with. Due to the relatively low intrinsic brightness of Thai lanterns, they are often estimated to be farther away and therefore larger than they actually are. The GEIPAN classifies this case as an observation of Thai lanterns, category "A".
