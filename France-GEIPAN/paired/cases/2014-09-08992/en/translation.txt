Description: On September 1, 2014, between 11:30 PM and 11:40 PM, a witness observing from their 5th-floor bedroom window saw for a few seconds a triangular flying object composed of 6 non-blinding white lights, moving at a slow speed less than 50 meters above the ground. The witness saw the PAN triangular object in profile without seeing a "mass" disappear from view above a nearby building. No noise was heard during this "unanticipated" observation. No other witness statements are reported. This case involved a meeting with the witness for a reconstruction and an interview (which, among other things, allowed for refining the observation time (between 23:00 and midnight according to the questionnaire)). The description of the PAN (gliding aspect, speed, V-shaped configuration, size, and other elements) strongly suggests an observation of a flight of migratory birds (grey geese) heading towards the eastern area of Lake Geneva (presence of an ornithological reserve) or local birds (swans) disturbed by concert noises that night. In both cases, the city lights reflect on the plumage, giving the appearance as described (see the investigation report). As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable bird observation.
