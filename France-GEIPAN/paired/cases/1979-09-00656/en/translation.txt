--- seg-e7184b8d3032bc046ddc0fc4 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 7, 1979, at approximately 4:30 a.m., two witnesses observed for less than 5 seconds a green oval shape with four white lights surrounded by a black circle. The colors were very bright and phosphorescent. The object rose very quickly into the sky at a 45° angle following a south-north direction. It suddenly went out. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
