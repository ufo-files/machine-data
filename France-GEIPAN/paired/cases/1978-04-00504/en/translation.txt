Description: On April 2, 1978, around 11:35 PM, a motorist and his passenger observed a very bright, yellow-orange glow. It appeared almost at ground level on the hill. After stopping his vehicle, the witness observed a large half-sphere, with the other part hidden by trees. It moved from north to south without any sound. The observation lasted approximately 50 minutes before the object disappeared as its brightness faded. No other information was gathered about this phenomenon.
