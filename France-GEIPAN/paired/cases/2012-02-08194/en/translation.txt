--- seg-811de09c04ba9cd2a1ea24f1 [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reports their observation of a few seconds on February 19, 2012 at 9:15 AM. Upon opening a velux, the witness notices in the sky a gray aluminum object, in a spherical shape, passing over their house. It moves in a southerly direction without making any noise or leaving a trail. It disappears into the clouds. The observed object, moving with the wind, is likely a helium or hydrogen-filled balloon. It could be a weather balloon, perhaps launched from Mérignac one hour prior and carried to Arès by the winds which had significantly changed in the previous hour (see weather records for Bordeaux and Biscarrosse), or more probably a fairground balloon released from Lège Cap Ferret, or yet another pilot balloon launched to prepare hot air balloon flights. This case is classified as "B": probable balloon observation.
