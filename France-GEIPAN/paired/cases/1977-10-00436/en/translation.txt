Summary: Observation of an orange-colored light phenomenon that seemed to follow motorists: astronomical observation of the Moon.
--- seg-1b760868d0d52622bd31b33f [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 2, 1977, between 9:50 PM and 10:00 PM, motorists in two different sectors of the Sarthe department observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky that they could not explain. The object was round, slightly flattened on top, and orange in color. Initially, the SEPRA classified this case as "C" due to a lack of information, but it was re-analyzed in 2013 by an internet user who proposed an explanation for the GEIPAN, an explanation that was validated. This observation case is a typical example of a "following ball": an effect witnessed when observers see a bright point or disk that seems to accompany them as they move; in fact, this very distant object is always in the same geographic direction, and it is usually a star. The testimony is very consistent, with two independent groups of witnesses who provided their statements to the gendarmerie. The observed phenomenon is not particularly strange, as it closely resembles the moon. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the Moon.
