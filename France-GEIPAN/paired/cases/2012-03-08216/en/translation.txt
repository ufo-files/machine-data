--- seg-91549906d9be6ecccbe42e76 [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 24, 2012, around 8:20 PM, a witness observed in the sky the silent and very rapid movement of a group of four white lights. The amateur astronomer reported that the lights appeared at the height of Capella, turning off as they reached the constellation Ursa Major. No other witnesses were found for this phenomenon. It is highly likely that this was the same phenomenon observed in Valras and Brantôme: the atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid, with fragmentation, or a non-cataloged space debris (see the investigation report and the verification done on SpaceTrack). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": observation of an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid or space debris.
