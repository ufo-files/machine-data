--- seg-a1472b960a9f3f7a44e1e403 [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 29, 1978, while traveling in the Verdun region, a motorist and their passengers spotted around 2:30 a.m. a white glow in the sky that intrigued them. A large triangular luminescent phenomenon of red-orange color then appeared, stationary in the sky. Stopping their vehicle, no sound was heard during the observation. A white light then headed towards the frightened witnesses who got back into their vehicle and continued on their way. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected.
