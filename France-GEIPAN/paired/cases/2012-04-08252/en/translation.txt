Summary: Sequential observations over several evenings of the movement of a bright point: astronomical observation of Venus.
--- seg-6bb7c88e60269017ec66582c [needs-review] ---
Description: A person testifies to successive observations of a bright light in the sky in April 2012. On April 2, the witness made an unaided visual observation followed by a binocular observation. The witness states that for several consecutive days, they observed in the evening a very bright object that gently descended towards the horizon and disappeared around 0:30. This rate of movement undeniably suggests an astronomical body, similar to the setting sun. At that time, Venus, particularly bright (magnitude -5.2), was noted in the evening sky. The object's shapes described by the witness are likely the result of an over-interpretation of visual or optical defects highlighted by the nocturnal observation of a very bright point. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A", an observation of particularly bright Venus during those nights.
