Description: On September 1, 2012, a witness was intrigued by the horizontal and silent movements of a yellow glowing circle in the cloudy sky. The observation lasted for nearly half an hour. No other testimonies were collected. The description of the phenomenon (a yellow circle appearing or disappearing among the clouds) suggests a nightclub spotlight; Saturday night is an appropriate time for such an event. The gendarmerie investigation did not identify any nearby establishment capable of producing this kind of animation, but it can sometimes be seen more than 10 kilometers away. The GEIPAN classifies this unusual case in category "B": probable observation of a light show.
