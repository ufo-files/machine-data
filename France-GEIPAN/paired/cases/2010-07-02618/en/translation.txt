--- seg-28e3d1f88a6cdbcd3969e357 [needs-review] ---
Description: During an outdoor gathering on July 8, 2010, at 23:00, approximately twenty people observed a red light phenomenon. One witness reported seeing a red patch that grew larger and took on the appearance of a flickering red ball surrounded by a purple-violet ring. It moved silently and straight ahead at a constant speed. The object's sudden disappearance ended the observation. Only one testimony was collected. Of average strangeness but low consistency due to the lack of precise coordinates of the observation location and the imprecision regarding the direction of the phenomenon (initially reported as west, then from the Bauges massif towards Mont Granier, which corresponds to a south-southwest axis that barely passes vertically over Barberaz), we cannot confirm the Thai lantern hypothesis.
