Description: On August 22, 2014, at 1:30 PM, two witnesses were intrigued by the movements of several bright white points several kilometers in the sky. Only one testimony was collected. The attached Meteo-France map indicates partly cloudy conditions, with very weak and unstable wind (Weather). In the observation region (southwest of Lyon), the weather conditions at that time meant that planes only left trails between the aeronautical levels 357 and 416 (35700 and 41600 feet): the multiple planes detected by Flightradar24 flew below these levels (e.g., 24725 feet), and thus did not leave condensation trails. The somewhat peculiar situation of this accumulation of planes at high altitude (at least 5, as seen in the Flightradar record), not leaving trails, and likely temporarily reflecting the sun according to their position, would have caused this luminous dance that surprised the witness. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A: observation of reflections on commercial aircraft, without trails.
