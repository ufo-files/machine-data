Description: On August 15, 2009, at 10:00 PM, a witness observed for approximately 40 seconds the movement of a luminous phenomenon. It was a bright green in color and had a long, linear shape. No noise was heard. The witness observed an atmospheric reentry of a meteorite: the green color comes from ionization in the upper atmosphere. This meteorite was observed from Basel, Switzerland, to the extreme south of Great Britain.
