Description: On August 31, 1978, several people observed three lights in the sky around 9:15 PM. A first orange light was observed for approximately fifteen minutes. Subsequently, two additional silver lights appeared and moved rapidly before disappearing instantly. The witnesses decided to move outside of town and saw the lights reappear around 11:00 PM. They observed them for an hour using binoculars. The phenomenon completely disappeared around midnight 15. No other reports were recorded regarding this event for which we have limited information.
