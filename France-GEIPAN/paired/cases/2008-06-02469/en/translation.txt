--- seg-0ba6d8631a99963bd11de22b [needs-review] ---
Description: A person reports, in 2009, their successive observations from their home. They testify to the presence and movement of light balls seen near Charles de Gaulle Airport. The witness filmed their observations between 11 PM and midnight in the early summer days at the end of June 2008 (the date of June 21st is given arbitrarily). The videos show yellow-orange glowing points slowly descending towards the ground. As the witness claims to observe in the direction of the airspace of Charles de Gaulle Airport, these points are likely the landing lights of planes approaching that airport. These beacons are visible against the plane up to approximately 30 km: therefore, on the one hand, their color can vary from white to yellow-orange according to the distance and atmospheric turbulence, on the other hand, one can observe simultaneously several planes whose distance cannot be estimated, even relatively; with the effect of perspective, these points may seem to get closer at the apparent contact or to move away. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of planes from the front, close to landing. As the witness does not provide specific dates or precise observation directions, the GEIPAN was unable to refine the investigation.
