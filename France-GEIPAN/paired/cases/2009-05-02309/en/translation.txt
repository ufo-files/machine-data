Description: On Friday, May 22, 2009, around 11:30 PM, two witnesses were intrigued by the west-to-east passage of a white glowing phenomenon with a steady speed. One witness observed a zigzag pattern made by the phenomenon before it quickly faded away. The witnesses likely observed a pass of the International Space Station (ISS) as it entered the Earth's shadow. The brief zigzag seen by one witness during the observation could be due to autocinetic phenomenon: focused on the glowing point crossing the sky, the brain momentarily lost its bearings and interpreted the natural movements of the eyes as a displacement of the glowing point itself. This case is classified A, observation of a pass of the International Space Station.
