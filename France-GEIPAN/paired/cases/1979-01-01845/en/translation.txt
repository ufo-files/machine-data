Description: On January 11, 1979, at 4:35 PM, two witnesses observed in the sky approximately fifteen flying objects. They appeared to be between 24 and 32 inches in size. Metallic in color, they spun and emitted flashes of light as they reflected the setting sun. They moved slowly, without any noise, towards the southeast where they disappeared. A weather balloon launch had occurred earlier that day at 12:00 PM, and it is likely that the witnesses saw fragments of the balloon's nacelle or envelope.
