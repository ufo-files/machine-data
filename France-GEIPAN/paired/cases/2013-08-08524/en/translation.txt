Description: On August 5, 2013, at 11:14 PM, a couple observed the passage of a very bright point in the sky. This observation of a very bright point crossing the sky from West to East at the beginning of the night has all the characteristics of observing a large satellite: that evening, the passage of the International Space Station (ISS) was noted precisely at that time: from 11:11 PM to 11:16 PM (see the Calsky log). The fact that the witness states that the light "at times seemed to deviate from its trajectory" is likely the result of a visual perception illusion: seeing the movement of a bright point in the sky. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the passage of the ISS.
