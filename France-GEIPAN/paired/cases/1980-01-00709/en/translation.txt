Summary: Periodic observation of a glowing ball moving slowly in a straight line.
--- seg-64ef7de3303705e2ccd3f3a1 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 1, 1980, several military personnel on duty witnessed a luminous phenomenon in the sky three times. A small orange ball suddenly appeared and successively at 23:16, 23:33, and 23:46. An intense glow accompanied this object as it moved slowly and always on a horizontal trajectory. At each appearance at the same location, the base kennel dogs would bark frantically. No noise or smoke accompanied the object's movement, and the military personnel were certain it was not light emitted by an airplane or helicopter. No civilians witnessed this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
