Summary: Observation of the passage of two craft.
--- seg-c1270001019654e9a68f8d10 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 23, 1977, around midnight, a person walking their dog saw in the sky for one minute two circular-shaped objects. The underside was bright white and the top side darker. The objects moved in a northeasterly-southwestern direction without making any noise. They would initiate a turn before accelerating to a high altitude and disappearing. No unexpected echoes were recorded during the night at the control tower of the Istres Air Base. No other testimonies were collected.
