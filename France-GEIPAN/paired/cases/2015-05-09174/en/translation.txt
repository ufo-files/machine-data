Description: On May 6, 2015, around 9 PM, a witness is intrigued by streaks in the sky. He takes photographs. Only one testimony is reported. Given the collected elements, namely the appearance of the phenomenon consistent with that of numerous other examples of condensation trails created by commercial airplanes and illuminated by the setting sun in a favorable atmospheric layer and the optical effect due to perspective, we can conclude that the phenomena observed by the witness are very likely persistent condensation trails created by commercial airplanes (see the investigation report). This testimony is of very good consistency: precise and accompanied by numerous photos, but from a single witness. The observation is very unremarkable as PANs (Persistent Airstreams) are immediately recognizable. This case is classified as "A" as a certain observation of airplane condensation trails.
