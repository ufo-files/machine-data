Description: On March 26, 2013 at 10:00 PM, a driver was intrigued by the rapid east/west movement of a bright orange light framed by white points. Due to its strange and weak consistency (two witnesses, but only one report; photographs unusable), this case appears to be a probable misidentification with an airplane. Notably, the radar return provided at the request of GEIPAN led to the identification of an airplane flight whose trajectory and timing are consistent with the observation. Unfortunately, the report lacks precision and the witness leaves too much to their personal interpretation. It is not the visual perception of the witness that is in question, but the interpretation the witness makes of their observation through their feelings (surprise, fatigue, driving at night). This case is classified B, probable misidentification with an airplane observation.
