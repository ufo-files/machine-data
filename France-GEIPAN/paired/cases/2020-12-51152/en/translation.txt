Summary: Observation during a flight of three successive appearances of a fast-moving white light: lack of information.
--- seg-c7b4465912bab7ac8a19fbf9 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: During a night flight between Paris and Bogota, a pilot and his colleague observed three times the presence of a white light with an "oblong shape; like a rice grain" that moved rapidly. The light intensity made them think "of a maritime navigation beacon when the beam reaches the night observer." The observation lasted a total of 15 to 20 seconds. No trail was seen. Only one testimony was collected. The strangeness lies in the high angular velocity of the PAN, the appearance of flashes with clear and very intense illumination and extinction, which do not seem to correspond to a flash caused by the reflection of the sun on a satellite (see the investigation report). The consistency is average with two direct but dependent witnesses who are accustomed to observing atmospheric reentry phenomena, high-altitude aircraft, and satellite passages. We have no photos or videos of the phenomenon. The strangeness is not high, even though no known phenomenon can explain the observation. We were unable to obtain radar information on the observation area, which could have strengthened the hypothesis of an aircraft. Due to the lack of the second testimony and additional data, this case is classified as "C" for lack of reliable information.
