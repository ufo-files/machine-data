Description: On September 28, 2013, around 9 PM, a witness observed the passage of eight bright red objects crossing the sky from east to west on the same trajectory. The observation of several red shapes moving on the same trajectory in the same direction as the wind (southeast) on a Saturday night clearly suggests a release of Thai lanterns (flying lanterns). The GEIPAN classifies this unusual case as category "B", likely observation of Thai lanterns.
