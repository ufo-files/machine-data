Description: On October 9, 2012, between 11:00 PM and 11:30 PM, a witness in their garden observed in the clear sky a fast-moving white light with a small blue halo. Five minutes later, the witness recognized the passage of a shooting star. The observation of a white point crossing the sky in a few seconds suggests a bolide (large shooting star), meaning the atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid (meteorite). The absence of a trail is not ordinary. This event was not recorded on the BOAM (meteor observer database). The provided information is not sufficient to validate the bolide hypothesis, which remains, however, plausible (Delta Aurigids meteor shower peak on October 10). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to lack of information. On the same day, 20 minutes after a similar observation in Lieu-Saint-Amand (59).
