Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archive on its public website www.geipan.fr. Among its publications are old cases classified at the time as Type "B" cases, which are now being reassessed with the sole purpose of being more relevant in the conclusions. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over all these years, this reassessment sometimes leads to new observations or even an explicit change in classification in an investigation note. This previously classified B case, named COUCY LE CHÂTEAU (02) 1980, is part of a set of cases that have been reassessed (see the investigation note). On March 2, 1980, between 9:45 PM and 10:00 PM, four witnesses in Saint-Paul-Aux-Bois observed a luminous object in the direction of Salency (02) whose light intensity and color varied. For three witnesses (T1, T2, T3), the object was moving downward along a trajectory. For T4, the object remained stationary. The gendarmes, called at 9:55 PM, immediately arrived on the scene but found nothing unusual in the western sky. This case is quite consistent (four witnesses), the description of the PAN observed is quite precise. The sincerity and credibility of the witnesses have never been questioned. The phenomenon described has many common characteristics (observation duration, shape, size, color) with a perfectly known astronomical object: Venus setting. Moreover, Venus was setting on the horizon in the direction indicated by the witnesses. It is not the visual perception of the witnesses that is in question, but the interpretation they make of their observation through their feelings (fatigue, surprise). In the current classification of the GEIPAN, this weak strangeness case is classified as PAN A, an observation related to a misperception with Venus setting.
