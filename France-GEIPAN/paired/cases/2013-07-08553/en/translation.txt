Summary: Observation of the silent passage of groups of dark red glowing balls: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-05e4f5f8f77c2fe9510eeead [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of Saturday, July 20, 2013, at 1:30 a.m., a witness was intrigued by the silent passage across the sky of groups of dark red balls. The witness took photographs. This testimony of orange-red balls moving in a line during weekend nights clearly evokes Thai lanterns. It is difficult to correlate their movement, estimated from East to West, with the local wind, which is very weak and variable, making it uncertain (northern wind in Colmar, southern wind in Basel; see weather). This could explain the change in direction. The witness, unaware of the existence of such objects, documented himself and recognized that they could be lanterns. Despite uncertainty about the local wind, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
