Summary: Observation of unusual movements of a blue-colored light beam: probable laser animation.
--- seg-cec40f5ad56b919a8cf57105 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Sunday, November 15, 2009, around 9 PM, two people out for a walk were intrigued by the unusual movements of a blue light beam coming from the sky. The beam swept across the sky in a wavering, oscillating motion, giving the impression of following the couple. The phenomenon lasted 35 minutes, and no sound came from the beam. This case is similar to that of Jeantes (02) on November 14, 2009, which also reports an observation on November 15 at 8:55 PM, and for which the Hirson Gendarmerie Brigade identified the observed phenomenon as a nightclub projector. Given the coincidental timing of both observations and the region's multiple possible locations for such a projector, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": very probable confusion with a nightclub projector.
