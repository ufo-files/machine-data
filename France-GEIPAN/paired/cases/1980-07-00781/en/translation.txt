Summary: Observation of a stationary light shape.
--- seg-c38de73ca7b42ef1dcab28e0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of July 25, 1980, around 2:45 a.m., a witness claims to have seen, 50 meters from their home, a stationary, orange, oval-shaped light form measuring approximately 0.80 meters high by 0.40 meters wide. This luminous mass was observed by the witness for 40 minutes. The light disappeared as the witness entered their kitchen. No ground traces were found. Another person reported witnessing a glowing ball 50 meters above the ground, moving alongside their vehicle, one month prior. No further information was received about these phenomena, for which we lack details.
