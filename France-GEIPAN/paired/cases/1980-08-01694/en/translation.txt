Summary: Observation of a glowing mushroom-shaped object
--- seg-88b9208c99836e054a639df1 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 16, 1980, around 3:30 AM, while returning from a popular dance, a witness observed above a field an object shaped like a mushroom cap. With a diameter of 50 feet, it emitted a very intense red light. After approaching to within approximately 400 meters, the witness became frightened and fled. During his journey, he noticed the object flying over the village, still emitting red light and remaining completely silent. No traces on the ground or vegetation were observed. No explanation was found for this observation.
