Description: On October 30, 2013, at 11:38 PM, a witness observed three silent, high-speed, glowing objects flying at the same altitude. The objects disappeared over the rooftops. The observation of very quickly moving bright points in straight lines for a few seconds suggests meteors or bolides. This observation is notable for the presence of three objects moving in parallel. This could be a meteoroid that fragmented into three upon re-entry. The slight lateral movement of one of them may be an illusion of perception known as autokinesthesia (see Perception of the movement of a bright point in the sky). The BOAM network (see: BOAM recorded numerous bolides that night, including one precisely at 10:38 PM UTC, but they were observed to the East, from the East of France; this is therefore not the same event. The network does not have a camera close to Sablé-sur-Sarthe (72). However, the frequency of events that night only indicates a significant probability of observing bolides. Nevertheless, given the brevity of the observation, the uniqueness of the testimony, the fact that there are three objects, this hypothesis of observing a bolide is quite fragile. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" pending any potential overlaps.
