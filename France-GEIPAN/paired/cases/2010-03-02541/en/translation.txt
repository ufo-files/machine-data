Description: While driving along the Thau Lake (34), a driver observed on March 11, 2010, between 10:45 PM and 11:00 PM, two round lights passing overhead. He then distinguished a large, cigar-shaped mass moving quickly but without any sound at an altitude of 100 meters. The gendarmerie was unable to gather any other testimonies. The witness also reported that he had previously witnessed several impressive observations. The level of strangeness of the observation is low (two lights moving at a not very high speed). The investigation elements do not allow for validating or refuting a possible interpretation, and the case remains insufficiently consistent. The unique testimony could not be corroborated with other elements, and the precise location of the phenomenon was not possible due to the witness's unavailability. This case remains unexplained and inconsistently reported, lacking any strangeness, it is classified as C.
