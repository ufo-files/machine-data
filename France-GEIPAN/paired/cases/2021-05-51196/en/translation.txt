Description: During the night of May 19 to 20, 2021, a resident of LAON (02) observed the sky. He sees a flickering light for a few minutes. He tries to film with his phone what he sees best with the naked eye. He starts to film, but a few seconds later, he sees something bright appear under a small glow. What appeared happened in a non-linear fashion, changing direction abruptly and very quickly, then paused slightly before finally departing to the left at such a speed that the witness could not follow it with their mobile phone. The witness sends the GEIPAN his video. Only one witness statement will be collected on this phenomenon. Of average strangeness and good consistency (single witness, video of PAN), this case turns out to be a probable observation of a bird or nocturnal insect (see the investigation report). The fact that the PAN appears blurry while the background is relatively clear indicates that the PAN was very close to the witness. Its characteristics, with variations in trajectory consistent with the flight of a bird or a nocturnal insect, illuminated for a few seconds by a streetlamp.
