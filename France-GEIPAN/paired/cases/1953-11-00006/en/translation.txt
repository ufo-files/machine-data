Summary: Observation of a bright white sphere in the northeast sky: astronomical misidentification with the planet Jupiter.
--- seg-c5a15a60c373959b7fa4b4e3 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 3, 1953 at 7:14 pm (UTC, 8:14 pm local time) the press reports multiple witness accounts of a white, luminous sphere in the sky. This phenomenon is stationary in the northeast sky, clear of clouds. A controller recorded his observation in the tower's log on November 3, 1953. He gave a statement on November 20, 1953 stating that two other military personnel had also made the same observation. A sky map shows Jupiter in the northeast with a high magnitude (-2.22) and an elevation of 3.6°. The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as A: Astronomical misperception with the planet Jupiter.
