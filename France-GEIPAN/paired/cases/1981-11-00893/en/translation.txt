Summary: Observation of the flight of an advertising balloon.
--- seg-7cde2e8d66dae83edb1995e1 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 1, 1981, around 11 a.m., several witnesses spotted a flying object slowly heading towards the sea. This gray, cylindrical object was approximately 4 meters long. No noise was heard during the object's movement. The investigation will reveal that it was a promotional balloon launched by children from the terrace of the "Le Corbusier" building, about 300 meters from the observation location.
