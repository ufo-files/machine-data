Description: On May 18, 2012, between 5:30 PM and 6:00 PM, two individuals were intrigued by a phenomenon in the clear sky. One of the witnesses filmed the movement of an object that sometimes lit up in the sky. The poor-quality video document does not allow for any exploitation for possible measurements of the object's dimensions, distance, and speed. Given the objective elements gathered, namely the presence of a dedicated glider airport nearby, gliders covered in white paint that favor the reflection of solar light, the object's movement consistent with that of a glider in thermal flight, and favorable weather conditions for glider flying, we can conclude that the object observed by the witnesses was probably a glider coming from Avignon-Pujaut airport, performing thermal spirals, and whose wings and/or fuselage intermittently reflected solar light rays. This case is to be classified as "B" as a probable observation of a glider swooping near the Avignon-Pujaut airport, its components intermittently reflecting sunlight.
