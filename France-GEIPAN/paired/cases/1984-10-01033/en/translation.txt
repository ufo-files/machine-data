Description: On October 2, 1984, between 11:30 PM and 11:42 PM, a witness and his wife observed an abnormal luminosity through their house window. This yellow light appeared to come from a straight line of lights. During the 12 minutes of observation, a beam became more distinct and illuminated the ground. A movement of the lights was noticed without any sound being heard. The luminosity faded until the light completely went out. No further information was gathered about this phenomenon.
