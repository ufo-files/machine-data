Summary: Observation of a probable atmospheric reentry
--- seg-2969df8b93bef8f3a600bf5b [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 25, 1980, between 21:25 and 22:00, several witnesses observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky. A circular shape of color moved without any particular noise being heard. Witnesses in the city lost sight of the object, which was hidden by buildings. The observation was very brief, but the witnesses claim that the object emitted radiant light. The Pic du Midi Observatory was contacted, but no unusual activity was noted. It is likely that the witnesses observed an atmospheric reentry.
