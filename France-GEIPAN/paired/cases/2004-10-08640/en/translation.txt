Description: In 2014, a witness reported several incidents in October 2004 where, feeling watched (on the highway and later at home), they made various observations or notes. The testimony lacks consistency, as: - the observation includes many details despite being old and made under poor conditions, while driving at 130K/H and looking backward; - the hypothesis of a reflection from closed windows is not entirely ruled out, although it was mentioned and rejected by the witness. - the observation occurs in a highly emotional context, driven by the feeling of being watched, which was present even before the observation, and appears to lead to remembering details or comparisons related to an "observing eye." The witness also describes other emotional or strange situations linked to this feeling of being watched, but they are not related to a presence or observation of PAN. Consequently, GEIPAN classifies the case as C: lack of reliable information.
