Summary: Observation in the sky of a white net with a black end: observation of an airplane condensation trail distorted by wind shear and part of which is not illuminated by the rising sun.

Description: On January 20, 2012, at approximately 8:25 a.m., a witness and his daughter observed in the clear sky above the Blaux pass a spatial phenomenon that intrigued them. The witness took pictures of white smoke whose left end turned black and disappeared behind the mountain on the side of the Cairo valley (04). The witness thought it might be something falling to the ground that had crashed, but did not see any aircraft or hear any unusual noises. He reported the incident to the gendarmerie. The gendarmerie investigation found no traces on the ground, no other testimonies from residents of the valley, and no signs of burning or fire in the area. The hypothesis of a military aircraft passing over could not be confirmed. The gendarmes noted that at that time of day and in that time slot, the sunrise is low and blinding, which could explain, among other things, the change in color of the smoke. This observation was the subject of several press or internet articles. Based on the investigation, three hypotheses were considered: aircraft crash, smoke coming from the ground, or a persistent condensation trail from an aircraft (see the investigation report). The GEIPAN classified this case as "B": very probable observation of a persistent aircraft condensation trail that was distorted by wind shear since its creation. The gray part is indeed an area that has not yet been illuminated (sunrise).
