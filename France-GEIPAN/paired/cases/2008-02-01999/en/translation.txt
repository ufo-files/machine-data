--- seg-de5aca9e25e4b723a0fd7582 [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 13, 2008, around 1:25 a.m., a witness, waking up in the middle of the night, observes from their home a large orange ball moving at extremely high speed in the sky. The observation lasts for a few seconds and the PAN disappears immediately. Only one testimony is collected. Due to its strangeness and low consistency (single witness, very brief observation), this case appears to be a very probable misperception with a natural bolide. However, the absence of independent testimonies or a capture by a device such as an all-sky camera does not allow this hypothesis to be formally validated. This case is classified as B, probable misperception with a natural atmospheric reentry (bolide).
