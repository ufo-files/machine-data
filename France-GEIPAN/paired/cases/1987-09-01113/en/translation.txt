Description: On September 29, 1987, two witnesses observed a bright light phenomenon for a few seconds. A bright point with a diameter larger than that of a shooting star moved rapidly across a clear sky. After a practically straight trajectory, the white point faded away. The description suggests it was likely an atmospheric reentry.
