Summary: Observation of rapid and silent movements of a flat circular object in the sky: observation of a light display due to a nightclub laser.
--- seg-13fe8ca3bf4fb49ee0efcb2f [needs-review] ---
Description: On Sunday, April 27, 2008, at 4:17 a.m., a witness, looking out their window, was intrigued by the very fast dance of a circular and flattened light phenomenon. The object moved, in particular, by forming circles. No particular noise was heard. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. Given the object's shape similar to that produced by the projection of a laser onto a layer of low clouds, the object's movements similar to those produced by the type of laser commonly used by nightclubs, the presence of a "tail" that could be equated with the terminal part of the laser beam before it meets the base of the clouds, the observation azimuth being the same as that towards the nightclub producing the incriminated laser, the date (night from Saturday to Sunday) being consistent with that of the use of said laser, and the laser's visibility from the witness's home (verified on site by the gendarmerie), we can conclude that the object described by the witness is certainly an effect of the laser from the nightclub "Le Fashion Room", regularly used on Saturday nights from 11 p.m. to approximately 4 a.m., and reflecting off the base of low clouds or local fog, probably present that day. This case is to be classified as "A" as a light show observation.
