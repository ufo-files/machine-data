Summary: Filmed observation of several flashes during a snowy night: probable observation of several flashes coming from an electrical installation and/or power line.
--- seg-8290c089e9c69a3151c97235 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 15, 2019, around midnight, while a snowstorm was hitting the area, a witness observed and filmed several pink flashes from their home. Only one testimony was collected. The witness's strange feeling resulted from watching their video in slow motion, as they explain in their testimony: "After an initial viewing, I slowed this one down and clearly noticed that the light moves from left to right during the flashes." They indicated in the questionnaire that this made them think of "an ultra-fast object." This case has good consistency with a video showing the phenomenon twice (see the investigation report). According to the opinion of an ENEDIS technician, accustomed to working in the field, and given the weather conditions, we can hypothesize a probable observation of several flashes coming from an electrical installation or line, following an attempt to restart the electrical network. However, the lack of information regarding the location and nature of any potential repair operations does not allow us to formally validate the explanatory hypothesis. This case should be classified as B, as a probable observation of several flashes coming from an electrical installation and/or line.
