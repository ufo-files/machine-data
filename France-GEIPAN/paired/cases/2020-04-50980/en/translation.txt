Summary: Very long observation of the passage of glowing points delineating multiple shapes in the Nice night sky: lack of information.
--- seg-38101857c287b4ea92e5b412 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 17, 2020, at 9:15 PM, a witness, located in the heights of Nice, observed in the direction of Saint-Laurent-du-Var and Grasse. They saw several bright points in the sky. The witness then observed, for approximately 50 minutes, various shapes "represented by bright points" (triangles, vertical or horizontal "L", circles). These shapes moved across the sky "in a regular and constant manner." Then, the group of points headed towards Italy before disappearing. The witness took photos "that didn't work" and a video, which they believe could be useful, although "blurred." The Gendarmerie did not gather any additional information on this phenomenon. When contacted again on June 9, 2020, for more information, the witness did not respond to our questionnaire requests or the video request regarding their observation. Furthermore, as of today, no matching in the GEIPAN database with other testimonies about this phenomenon at this date has been possible. The GEIPAN classifies this case as C due to a lack of information.
