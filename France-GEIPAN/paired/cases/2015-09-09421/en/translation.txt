Description: Testimony given in February 2016 regarding an observation made during the second half of September 2015 (the current date is arbitrary). The witness is on a dock, in a boat, when she observes the movement of a bright point through her open porthole. The point suddenly stops and then starts moving very quickly, performing a level ascent. Only one testimony was collected. What is described may seem strange but is already reported in the GEIPAN and could be explained by several phenomena in conjunction (satellites entering and exiting visibility, visual autocinematic effect, etc.). Due to the lack of a precise date (the witness does not know the day of the month), it is impossible to delve deeper. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies this case as C: lack of information.
