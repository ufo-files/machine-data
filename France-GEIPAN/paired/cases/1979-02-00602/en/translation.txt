Summary: Observation of a stationary and luminous object.
--- seg-5628c8879aff7991435d25cb [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 6, 1979, at 9:20 a.m., a passerby observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky that intrigued him. For two minutes, he saw a saucer-shaped object with blue and yellow lines at the rear that disappeared. The object was then hidden by buildings, and when the witness found it again, he noticed a denser, rounded light in its center. The object was motionless within a glowing halo and formed a 20° angle with the ground. No other testimony was collected. At this time, the sun was very low on the horizon and in the observation axis: it was likely a reflection of sunlight through the clouds.
