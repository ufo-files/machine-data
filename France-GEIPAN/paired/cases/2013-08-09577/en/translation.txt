Summary: Observation of the passage of a white-light point in the night sky and questioning upon zoomed photography: observation of a satellite's passage.
--- seg-9af4c4b234f79cc266c6047f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 4, 2013, at 10:12 PM, a couple observed the passage of a white, glowing point moving at the same speed as a commercial jet, but without flickering, in a straight line, without sound. The witness takes two photos with a camera and is amazed by what he gets when zooming in from his computer (see photo3). Only one testimony is collected. The strangeness of the testimony stems from what the witness saw in the photo, not what he saw with his own eyes, which he himself admits is likely a satellite. In fact, at the indicated time, several satellites passed in a SO to NE direction (see Callsky). The GEIPAN no longer (due to the multiple photo artifacts) examines the oddities from photos that were not seen with the eyes and only makes a statement about the visual aspect here. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: observation of the passage of a satellite.
