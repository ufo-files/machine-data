Description: On December 18, 2008, between 5:58 and 6:05 a.m., a witness was intrigued by a noise and blue lights in the sky and over their garden. The observation lasted one minute. The witness believed something was slowly flying over their house. Afraid, this person warned their husband, who was on the ground floor, but by that time the lights had disappeared. No other testimony in the area was collected regarding this phenomenon, and the witness provided too few details to support any hypothesis. This case is classified as C due to a lack of information.
