Summary: Filmed observation of bright flashes and a dark mass in motion across the night sky: misidentification with military aircraft training.
--- seg-cf5f8aa2d75b7c21efaf1f1c [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 10, 2019, at 10:30 PM (not 8:30 PM as incorrectly indicated in the questionnaire), a witness was outside in their garden when they heard an increasingly loud noise, similar to a powerful ventilation system. He raises his head towards the sky and sees a flash of a fraction of a second at about 200 meters above ground on his left and then on his right. In the center, he observes a kind of ULM (ultralight aircraft) cockpit that is larger and black, connected to the two flashes by an elastic cable. The entire object moves in the direction of Lautrec (81), the witness has time to film the end of the observation with his mobile phone. The perceived strangeness originates from: • a succession of bright flashes seen in the sky; • a deep rumble heard throughout the city; • the movement of the flashes with the impression of a connection (cable) between them; • the impression of a dark mass seen as a cockpit of a craft; • the brief film of the two lights departing in the direction of Lautrec (81) above the bypass; • the memory of similar events that allegedly occurred the previous year around the same date, but the witness did not make a statement to the GEIPAN at that time. This last point initially directs the analysis towards the festival "Les Extravadanses" which takes place every year in Castres (81) from July 7th to 12th. On July 10, 2019, there was indeed an evening event at the theater square. Indeed, the witness states that he observed the same phenomena in 2018 (but only lights, without seeing the cables and cockpit) near the Pierre Fabre Stadium and the Place de la République. Except in 2018, the festival did indeed take place on the Place de la République (information confirmed by the Castres City Hall, Events Service). The festival could partially correspond to the witness's perceived strangeness: - the muffled noise over the city; - the initial orientation of the observation towards the theater square; - the visualization of flashes that could be reflections of bright sports lights; The hypothesis of a misidentification with reflections of festival lights from 2018 and 2019 is therefore considered. On the other hand, the strangeness of the closer observation (lights passing over the house) is not explained except to imagine a laser bounce off the city's skyline. However, the festival program for 2019 does not mention any light show, and particularly on July 10th: it is a dance performance at Place Jean Jaurès. Here is the translation of the provided GEIPAN text:

This is a part of the program "The Extraterrestrials" on Wednesday, July 10th (see the complete program in the annex):

- 7 p.m. - Theater courtyard: Modern'jazz.
- 8 p.m. - Jean Jaurès Place: "Through the Notes" - silk dance and piano;
- 8:45 p.m. - Jean Jaurès Place: "The Lost Little Ball" - participatory dance;

Furthermore, this hypothesis does not explain the witness' perception of a vehicle flying overhead with the shape of a cockpit, nor the visualization of cables between the lights. The GEIPAN then continued the analysis of another hypothesis. The observation was made from the witness's garden, who reports the movement of a PAN. The weather for the day (infoclimat) indicates clear skies, no precipitation, and a very light wind, consistent with the witness's statements. The movement (trajectory southeast/northwest) of the PAN observed and passing over the witness's house could not correspond to an astral light phenomenon. Air traffic over Castres (81) at this time indicates no civil flights. The GEIPAN made an air traffic request to the CNOA (National Center for Air Operations of the Air Force):

• The CNOA confirms a military training activity, specifically involving a tanker refueling aircraft, at the time of the observation;

• The radar return graphically shows traces of aircraft that correspond to a refueling mission for Air Force fighters, which could explain the visibility of "a stretched cable illuminated by flashes";

• Therefore, some aircraft could have been visible (which could correspond to the cockpit seen by the witness) and the sound of aircraft overhead could also have been heard;

• The radar traces show flight paths from southeast to northwest, which correspond to the directions indicated by the witness ("above his home and then towards Lautrec above the bypass");

• The radar return graph (attached as an annex) is crossed out regarding the indicatives of the involved aircraft for reasons of military operation confidentiality, which are nevertheless confirmed. The strangeness of the observation as seen by the witness is explicable by these aerial maneuvers. The residual strangeness is therefore very low. The consistency of the observation is very good, as the witness:

• Wrote a very detailed letter (3 pages with drawings, attached) on the same evening of the observation, providing specific information about the trajectory of the PAN relative to the city of Castres;
• Is approximately 1km from the center of Castres;
• Submitted a formal statement to the police the following day;
• Provided a complete testimony questionnaire at the request of GEIPAN, including drawings;
• Sent a photo (attached);
• Filmed the end of the observation, a video that shows the environment (trees and chimney) visible from his garden, allowing confirmation on Google of the orientation of the PAN's trajectory towards northwest. Two hypotheses were therefore considered, namely the "Les Extravadanses" festival and the overflight of planes. The analysis highlights that the observation does not correspond to the festival, but is compatible in every way with the military training flight that was confirmed by the CNOA. This testimony is of good consistency and has a weak residual strangeness. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A: misidentification with military aircraft training.
