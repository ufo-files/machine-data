Description: A witness testifies and submits to the gendarmerie two videos recorded by an automatic video recording station on March 30, 2009 at 2:38 PM. The first video, recorded at 2:34 PM (M20090330_123414_LINER), shows the trail of a commercial aircraft, and the second (M20090330_123824_PAN) shows the passage of an unknown, sparkling object. This rare case of a daytime light trail video recording (M20090330_123824_PAN) suggests a meteor, although its apparent speed (angular velocity) appears slower than a typical meteor, compared to the angular velocity of the commercial aircraft recorded with the same device. This relative slowness can be explained by the fact that it was daytime and the sky was partially overcast, allowing only the brightest part of the trajectory to be observed, which is the end of a meteor's trajectory when the object is moving the slowest through the atmosphere.

Another uncertainty regarding the meteor hypothesis: it is only acceptable if the cloud cover is considered sufficiently transparent, as the physical location of the light phenomenon associated with a meteor is beyond 10 kilometers altitude; the video of the aircraft's trail (likely at over 3000 meters) seems to support this hypothesis, but without certainty. The witness will submit another video to GEIPAN taken on May 4, 2009, showing the movement of a glowing ball along an ascending trajectory, for which GEIPAN has no explanations at this time (M20090504_162251).
