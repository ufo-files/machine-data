Description: A witness reports an observation made on December 21, 1976, between 10:30 PM and 11:45 PM: The witness observed with binoculars a luminous phenomenon of several colors. Two other people also observed the phenomenon, which appeared stationary. These individuals likely made an astronomical observation of Sirius, which is very visible during this time of year. The colors noted by the primary witness are probably the result of atmospheric perturbations causing the star to twinkle, as well as chromatic aberrations inherent to consumer binoculars (these aberrations cause irizations around very bright points).
