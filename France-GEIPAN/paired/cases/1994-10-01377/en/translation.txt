Description: On October 30, 1994, around 01:45, a couple in a car observed a glowing circle spinning on itself, with regularly arranged lights around its perimeter. The assumed altitude of the phenomenon is 5 to 6 meters, and it remains stationary above the car. The car quickly exits the city, but encounters the phenomenon again a short distance ahead: a triangle with the same type of lights around its edge has joined the circle. The triangle is equilateral, approximately 10 meters per side, and at an altitude of 10 to 15 meters. The phenomenon accompanied the vehicle as it traveled at around 80 km/h, adjusting to the speed for several kilometers. During a change in direction, the object accelerated and moved away, witnesses specifying that it had the shape of a boomerang. During the same time frame and on the same road, another couple in a different car described the phenomenon with surprisingly similar terms and reported the same movements relative to the vehicle. This phenomenon could not be explained.
