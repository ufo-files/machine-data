Description: The sole witness to this observation case is on the terrace of his home in the commune of La Montagne (44) on July 14, 2022, around 10:17 a.m., when he observes towards the east in the sky a PAN of an oblong shape and a light off-white color moving silently, according to a straight trajectory and at a rapid and regular speed, from north to south. The witness compares the PAN to an "egg with both sides of the same shape or a 'tic-tac' candy." No trail or smoke plume was visible, and the PAN was lost from view after approximately 15 seconds of observation. The case consistency is average, with only one witness, the absence of a photo of the PAN, and a testimony nearly a year after the observation. The description of the PAN could suggest two types of observations: those of an airplane or a balloon (see the investigation report). The investigation ruled out the possibility of an airplane, as no radar trace was detected that could correspond to the movement of the PAN. The description of the PAN is consistent with an oblong balloon of a light color, carried by the wind according to an apparent trajectory in its direction. Finally, calculations of the distance and speed of the PAN, assuming a typical dimension for a festive balloon (~30cm), lead to values compatible with the testimony. The perception of the speed of an object observed moving in the sky depends on the distance separating it from the observer, which is undefined here. Indeed, without any references, it will be impossible to estimate both the dimensions and the distance of an unknown object present in the sky. The witness may have the impression of a relatively large object moving quickly at a high altitude, while it could be a small object at a low altitude moving at a moderate speed. Although the origin of the balloon could not be proven, all the elements of this investigation converge towards the observation of a white balloon carried by the wind, possibly lost or released during a private party. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B," probable observation of a festive balloon.
