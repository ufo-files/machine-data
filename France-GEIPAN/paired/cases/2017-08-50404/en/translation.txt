--- seg-39502707ba4e1932ca86e1af [needs-review] ---
Description: Witnesses emerged from an evening at the theater in BUSSANG (Vosges) on Saturday, August 5, 2017, around 10:45 PM. They spotted three red-orange spheres floating in the air and moving slowly towards the east, seemingly located in the direction of "La tête des neufs Bois," the name of a mountain overlooking the area. Intrigued, they watched the scene without hearing any noise. After approximately two minutes of observation, the spheres disappeared. Only one testimony was collected. The case presents a very low strangeness factor; in fact, the witnesses were only intrigued and didn't provide further details (see the investigation report). August is a favorable time, and the town of BUSSANG (88) offers many activities during the summer season. Celestial lanterns have been implicitly part of this for several years now, and mandatory declarations are rarely respected. The general description of the spheres advancing slowly in the sky after a moment that appeared static, the number (three in this case), the fact that it was a Saturday night (a propitious day), combined with the weak force of the wind (creating an effect of immobility or very slight movement), the direction of this movement, and the description of one sphere appearing larger than the others, are all consistent with the proposed hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: observation of Thai lanterns.
