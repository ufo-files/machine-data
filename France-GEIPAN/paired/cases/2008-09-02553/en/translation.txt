--- seg-068bd6a1a133be1d13a24a45 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Recent testimony (April 19, 2010) of an old observation. On September 28, 2008, a witness observed in the sky for approximately 15 minutes two red glowing points. One of the two points moved slowly then accelerated before disappearing. The stationary light described could correspond to the planet Venus, observable with the naked eye in the West at sunset (red-orange light) with a magnitude of -4.2. The "red planet" Mars was not observable with the naked eye (apparent magnitude of +1.5, too low on the horizon). The second mobile red light could be an aircraft. However, since it is an old observation reported several months later, any verification and investigation are impossible. The case is classified C due to lack of information.
