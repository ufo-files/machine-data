Description: On February 16, 2013, at 7:32 PM, a witness observed through a window door the very fast and silent passage of an orange ball on a straight trajectory. An orange ball crossing approximately 45° of the sky from East to West in 3 seconds suggests an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid (meteorite). The absence of a trail and the spirals described by the witness are not incompatible with this hypothesis, but are a priori rare. The BOAM camera network does not mention any event at that time, but there is no camera covering the southern Alps. The GEIPAN did not receive any other observation reports that day. Despite some remarkable characteristics, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a bolide (atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid).
