Description: On June 12, 2013, at 8:40 a.m., a witness and his son, on their terrace, heard a series of detonations and saw in the sky a dark oval object whose rear became bright with each detonation. This object flew over the village in a straight line. The Pan was lost behind a farm building. Only one statement was collected. The meteorological data collected show overcast conditions, generally low ceiling, light wind blowing mostly from the south, and average visibility (see the investigation report). This testimony is of low consistency: it is vague and detailed, and lacks crucial and necessary data for further study. The only eye witness has testified, with no photos or videos. The observation could be strange, however, too many inaccuracies and gaps in the testimony prevent judging its strangeness in a truly valid way. After analyzing the hypothesis of a helicopter, suggested by the gendarmes, considering the elements collected in the remote investigation and the lack of angular data on the size of the PAN, no hypothesis can be clearly confirmed. Unfortunately, the GEIPAN did not handle this case quickly enough to verify the air traffic in time. This case is classified in category "C" due to lack of information, the significant lack of elements and overlaps does not allow to establish a solid opinion.
