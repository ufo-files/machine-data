Summary: Observation of an oval-shaped entity. Hoax
--- seg-500f18e669f57eab723ebc4e [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 14, 1997, around 5 PM, a witness, while running, reported encountering an oval-shaped being with a triangular head. This being allegedly fled upon the witness's approach, releasing a soft, reddish object that the witness picked up and claimed hardened in their pocket. The witness later presented this stone as a "fetus of an extraterrestrial." The investigation quickly revealed this to be a fabrication.
