Summary: Observations of the fall of a bright, round object with green streaks: atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid.
--- seg-bd8847bc43290e18fe214c09 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 2, 2013, around 10 PM, two teenagers observed the fall of a round, glowing object with green trails towards the commune of Rantswiller. This case was handled by the Sierentz (68) gendarmerie who meticulously collected essential elements and conducted an exemplary investigation with relevant services. In addition to these elements, we can note that the database www.boam.fr indicates a meteoroid capture observed in the southwest by a camera located in Chaligny (54) near Nancy at 21:57 that day. We also notice (see attached document) that several other large meteors were observed that night. The event was noted by several observers in the Paris region who testified on the "Ciel des hommes" meteorite Paris website. There is some dispersion in the testimonies regarding the time of the phenomenon, ranging from "around 10 PM" to 10:15 PM. The Walbach witness indicated "around 10:15 PM," but he called his godmother at 10:03 PM, immediately after the observation: this precise indication, collected pertinently by the gendarmes, allows for certain correlation of this observation with the meteoroid recorded on the Chaligny camera at 21:57. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": certain observation of a meteor caused by the atmospheric entry of a meteorite (natural meteoroid).
