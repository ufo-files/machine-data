--- seg-efab4006ef8e197e488320ee [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 27, 2016, the witness (T1) was watching television and decided to check on his son upstairs. At the top of the stairs, he spotted through the window a light moving "quite quickly" and appearing to head towards Crozon (29). The witness called his son, who also confirmed the presence of the light. He went downstairs to get his camera, then returned upstairs to photograph the phenomenon through the window. Between 11:44 PM on December 27 and 12:59 AM on December 28, the witness took 19 photographs. Only one statement was collected. There is a significant inconsistency in the account, as T1 mentions a high speed and the observation lasted over an hour. Additionally, the witnesses went to bed before the phenomenon disappeared, indicating a low level of strangeness. The witness (T1) indicated the phenomenon's position was west-southwest, yet Sirius was clearly visible that evening in the direction indicated by the witness, appearing to move westward towards Crozon. Given the photographs were taken through windows with a powerful zoom, this explains the "Z" movement visible in image #3. The various colors are due to the twinkling of the Sirius star. It therefore appears that T1 likely photographed Sirius. The GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of Sirius.
