Description: On December 31, 1979 (not January as indicated in the report under "enquête") between 11:15 PM and 12:30 AM, two witnesses observed in the sky two unusual lights. These lights sometimes seemed connected by a set of very bright, multi-colored serpentines. The observation was not continuous, and the phenomenon completely disappeared around 1 AM. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack data.
