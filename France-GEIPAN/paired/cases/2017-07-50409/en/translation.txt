Summary: Observation of the successive and silent passage of several luminous objects in the night sky: observation of celestial lanterns.
--- seg-2530f9a31a5c4b74ea2741d3 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 7, 2017, between 11 PM and midnight, the witness, from his terrace with friends, observed 5 luminous objects in the sky, moving one after the other from northeast to southwest, completely silent and on the same trajectory. Never were two objects visible at the same time; they did not flicker. They moved at a fairly steady speed and remained visible for a considerable length of time. On July 13, 2017, the witness contacted the GEIPAN to report that after researching a similar case observed in 2008 on the island of Yeu and viewing in detail the GEIPAN site, including the video about Thai lanterns, he is convinced it is the same phenomenon. His observation corresponds in every way (lights, speed, trajectory, etc.) to that of lanterns. He was surprised because he was unaware of the existence of these celestial lanterns. The case is classified as A: Observation of celestial lanterns.
