--- seg-28b46a69ae7ba9fb55216aa4 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 17, 2015, at 10:30 PM, five people were intrigued by the presence of a round, orange object near a storm cloud. The light phenomenon ends after 10 minutes. It returns and disappears again. Fighter planes are seen at that time. Photos taken with mobile phones are not usable. Only one testimony is collected. The strangeness of this case is primarily created by the simultaneous observation of the PANS with two known phenomena (storm and fighter jets) that the witness unconsciously associates, while nothing indicates that there is a link between the three phenomena other than temporal and spatial coincidence (see the investigation report). The testimony is weakly consistent, with few data points noted and exploitable. The observation conditions are also poor, at night. Although some clues suggest a confusion with two Thai lanterns, this case should be classified as "C" (unidentified phenomenon) as the absence of concrete and exploitable data does not allow for a conclusion.
