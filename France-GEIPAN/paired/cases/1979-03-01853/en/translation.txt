Description: On March 18, 1979, around 9:45 PM, a witness observed from his courtyard for 5 seconds a luminous phenomenon that intrigued him. He saw two orange spheres, 15 cm in diameter, appear and disappear in the clouds, moving at extremely high speed from west to southeast. These two spheres were approximately 50 cm apart. No noise was heard. Despite an appeal for witnesses launched by the gendarmerie, no other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, and we lack information about it.
