Description: On December 3, 2009, around 10:30 PM, a motorist observed the very quick passage of a very low-flying bright light phenomenon. The witness described two very intense light halos. No other reports of this phenomenon were made. The observation is entirely consistent with a meteor phenomenon or the atmospheric reentry of space debris: high brightness, great speed, straight trajectory. The possible double trail likely corresponds to the fragmentation of the object as it enters the atmosphere, which is a frequent phenomenon.
