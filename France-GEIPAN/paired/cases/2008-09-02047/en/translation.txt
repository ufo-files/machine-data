Description: On the night of September 5th or 6th, 2008, between 10:30 PM and 11:00 PM, several people observed in the direction of the Braus pass strange light movements in the sky. One testimony (with an uncertain date) describes the successive appearances of glowing balls of light in various colors, some of which seem to spin around each other. Two other balls move parallel to each other at a high speed. No unusual noises were heard during the observation. This observation consists of the sequence of three independent phenomena: a glowing point at the western horizon, orange in color, slowly disappearing after 30 minutes (possibly the setting star Arcturus, see astro maps at 10:30 PM and 11:00 PM); white, glowing balls moving chaotically against a backdrop of clouds (this description closely matches the appearance of a disco laser, although the observer does not see the ascending ray, which is always the case except in very humid atmospheres); and finally, the very quick, straight-line passage of two glowing points (this phenomenon clearly suggests an atmospheric re-entry of a meteoroid (fragmented meteorite) or space debris). Due to the uncertainty over the date and the lack of evidence to support these hypotheses, the GEIPAN currently classifies this unusual case as "C" (unclassified) due to a lack of information. The strangeness of the case comes mainly from the sequence of these three phenomena.
