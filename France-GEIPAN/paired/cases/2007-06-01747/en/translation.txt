Description: On June 9, 2007, around 1:30 AM, a witness observed a very bright light in the sky that intrigued him. He filmed the phenomenon for several minutes. Since the Nice East gendarmerie was very reluctant to take his statement, he contacted the GEIPAN directly. His testimony is therefore recorded on a direct questionnaire and not on a gendarmerie report. A search using the Winstars 2 software quickly showed that the witness had observed Jupiter under very favorable conditions, which were indeed recalled in the June issue of the Sciences et Avenir magazine. The witness himself, who conducted his own analysis, reached the same conclusion. The phenomenon is therefore perfectly identified.
