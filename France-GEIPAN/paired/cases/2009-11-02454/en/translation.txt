Description: The witness reports in their questionnaire an observation made at 6:55 PM on November 9, 2009, as they were leaving their home. The witness describes a glowing sphere surrounded by a glowing ring that passed very quickly overhead at an altitude of 500 meters, heading towards the Corbières, and then disappeared. The witness mentions a strong wind and a plane present to the north, near the Rivesaltes airport. They had time to take a photograph with their mobile phone. Only one testimony was collected. A photograph is attached to the questionnaire (see attached document:  temoin_image_097.jpg). In fact, the witness was not flown over vertically (confirmed by them during the investigation), and the phenomenon was over 13 kilometers away, above the Vingrau quarries to the northwest of Claira (see attached investigation document:  situation_PAN.png). The PAN flew over the Montpins domain, then Rivesaltes, and then towards the sea (east). Therefore, the witness observed the phenomenon from a distance and face-on for the entire duration of the observation. The PAN gained altitude and disappeared (as if it had turned off). On the photo sent by the witness (see attached document:  temoin_image_097.jpg), the PAN has the same characteristics as the last 10 street lamps that border the road to the right (sphere + hook). The hook is likely an anomaly of the sensor. The shape is unusual, but very similar to the defects on the street lamps at the bottom (see attached investigation document: image_097_deformation). The strangeness felt by the witness is due to the intensity of the white light emitted, and then its extinction as it moved away (not really because of the perceived half-ring). The trajectory, speed, and altitude are entirely consistent with a helicopter (Gendarmerie or Civil Security). The Dragon66 helicopter is equipped with a forward projector and also conducts night training in the early evening. However, we were unable to confirm a flight on that evening. This case presents a relatively low strangeness that was reinforced by the anomaly on the photo. The consistency of the testimony is average (one witness and one photo), but the passage of the phenomenon at 6:55 PM overhead two very active villages at that time should have been noticed. In the absence of other testimonies, we can assume that the other witnesses identified the phenomenon (as it was closer). The phenomenon is classified as B: probable helicopter equipped with a forward projector of type SX-16.
