Summary: Long observations of a bright point seemingly following the Moon: astronomical observations of Antares.
--- seg-c9ba19e7c608a875aec7d38a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 17, 2009, at 2:20 AM, a witness observes a bright point near the Moon that intrigues them. At work and with colleagues, the witness makes several more of this observation. The intensity of the bright point fades with the dawn. The position and movement of the point intrigue the witnesses. The astronomical configuration clearly shows that the witnesses observed the star ANTARES. This star "follows" the Moon and appears to orbit around it, as described by one of the witnesses. This apparent natural movement is simply due to the respective distances of the star and Moon, and the Earth's rotation.
