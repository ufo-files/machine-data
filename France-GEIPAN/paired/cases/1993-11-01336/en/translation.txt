Description: On the night of November 16 to 17, 1993, around 00:45, a witness observed a fixed bright light in the sky "like a neon tube" around which a number of glowing points were circling. This phenomenon was accompanied by a faint noise reminiscent of a small plane. The witness woke up his companion who was also able to observe the phenomenon. Despite a newspaper article, no other information could be gathered.
