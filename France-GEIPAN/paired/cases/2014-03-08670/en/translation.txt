Description: On March 9, 2014, at 7:50 PM, a witness observed from their home for a few seconds a faintly glowing phenomenon followed by a long, white streak in the sky. This observation of a glowing ball followed by a streak, crossing the sky in a straight line in 2 or 3 seconds, has all the characteristics of a meteor (see the term: meteor). The BOAM (see BOAM Network) network of automatic cameras did not record this phenomenon, perhaps not bright enough. However, there are two meteor recordings in the same evening, at 9:03 PM and 11:28 PM, from cameras located in eastern France. The GEIPAN received a direct testimony of an observation made in Saint-André-de-Cubzac (33), near Bordeaux, on the same day between 7:30 PM and 7:45 PM, which could correspond to this phenomenon: the directions of the phenomenon's movement are compatible, but the reported observation times do not coincide exactly. Without confirmations, this case is currently classified as "B": probable observation of a meteor. Overall, there have been numerous meteor observations in February and March.
