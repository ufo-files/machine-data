Description: On April 25, 1983, several witnesses observed in the sky between 8:15 and 8:45 an oval-shaped object. Its color was light, almost transparent, and sometimes metallic, appearing silver. The object moved at a very low speed from west to south-southwest. Several times, it was obscured by clouds before disappearing completely. The witnesses likely observed a weather balloon, of the type used for meteorological sonding.
