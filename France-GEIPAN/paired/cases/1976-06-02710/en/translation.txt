Description: On June 6, 1976, between 5:40 a.m. and 7:20 a.m., a witness observed the high-altitude movement of a luminous, oval object. The object appeared to be composed of two unevenly sized bright parts as if connected. It moved slowly in an east-west direction. No other testimony was collected. This person likely witnessed the passage of a stratospheric balloon.
