Summary: Observation of the passage of a PAN oval green fluorescent object with red signals; humming noise: misidentification with a military helicopter in training.
--- seg-679bef300785ec5c7eaa51b7 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 25, 2019, at 12:30 AM, a witness is awakened by a humming outside. Looking out the open window, the witness is surprised to see a PAN (unidentified flying object) of a color and awakens their companion. The two witnesses observe for one minute the passage of a bright green, oval-shaped PAN at a very low altitude with some red light signals at the rear. The PAN moves away in a southwest direction. The witness did not have time to take a photo. Only one testimony was collected. The peculiarities reported by the witnesses—the bright green oval shape and the noise (humming, air propulsion, gust…) never seen or heard before—allow them to assert that it was not a helicopter (see investigation report). The consistency is average, with two dependent witnesses but only one providing testimony; however, the description allows for the hypothesis of observing a helicopter. Several elements of the testimony correspond to this hypothesis, and the occurrence of an aircraft in the region with these characteristics allows for a good evaluation. That day, an international-scale exercise was taking place 40 kilometers from the witnesses' home. It involved several foreign special forces aircraft, including the American CV-22 Osprey helicopter with the distinctive feature of green lights at the ends of its rotors to remain visible to the crew. When the rotors spin, these lights create two "virtual green disks." People in the training area (ZRT de Dignac) reported that evening to the local newspaper the presence of helicopters and a green light. Since the witnesses' home is between the exercise location and Mont-de-Marsan (the exercise base), the helicopter's trajectory over their home is consistent with a return from training toward the military base. Their misidentification can be explained by unfamiliarity with this type of helicopter, which is very rare in France, and the fact that this kind of mission receives little media coverage. The GEIPAN confirms a classification of A for the identified phenomenon: military helicopter in exercise.
