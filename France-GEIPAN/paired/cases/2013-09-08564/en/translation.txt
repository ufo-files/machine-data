Summary: Observation of the movement of a bright point in the sky: mistaken for an airplane trail.
--- seg-5c75c984d5a96fb889f97f44 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 23, 2013, between 8:10 and 8:25 PM, a driver on D650 between Les Mezières and Saint-Germain-Sur-Ay (beach) was intrigued by two bright points in the sky: one in particular seemed to follow a straight trajectory and moved away at a constant speed towards the NW. The witness clearly identified an airplane with its condensation trail crossing their field of vision. The witness stopped at two locations to take several photographs of the bright point. The testimony and high-quality photos clearly suggest the observation of a distant airplane trail receding from the witness. Zooming in on the last photos, the bright point appears to have a flame-like shape, which is a perspective view of a brightly sunlit airplane trail (sunset at 7:54 PM). Analysis of the Flightradar24 (attached) renditions reveals one or even two potential aircraft that could have caused these trails. (Note: the witness's observation direction estimate is not precise enough to be certain these are the aircraft. Also, FlightRadar24 only indicates civilian aircraft equipped with certain equipment.) See other similar cases involving airplane trails. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," a highly probable observation of airplane condensation trails, at sunset.
