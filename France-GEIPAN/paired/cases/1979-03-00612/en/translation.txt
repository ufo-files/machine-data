Summary: Observation of a ball performing back-and-forth movements
--- seg-df8b59cdb5d1cbd76547cdf1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 22, around 11 PM, a witness observed at a high altitude an orange ball the size of a soccer ball. It moved horizontally, making rapid back-and-forth motions; it then moved vertically, emitting green reflections. The same phenomenon occurred the next day at the same place and time. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we have limited information.
