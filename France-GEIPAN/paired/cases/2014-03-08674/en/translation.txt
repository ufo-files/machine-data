Summary: Observation of the silent passage of an orange diamond-shaped object in the sky: probable observation of a Thai lantern.
--- seg-db39857444eece9595d21064 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Sunday, March 9, 2014 at 12:28 AM, a witness observed from the terrace of their apartment a silent, orange-colored diamond-shaped object moving across the sky; according to the witness, it traveled at the speed of an airplane on a trajectory from southeast to northwest. The observation of a silent, orange-colored flying object moving horizontally in the same direction as the wind strongly suggests a sky lantern (Thai lantern or Tura balloon) likely released after a celebration. The witness saw the object depart towards the northwest; the wind in Bordeaux was a light southeast breeze. The diamond shape is unusual for Thai lanterns; it could also be a Tura balloon operating on the same principle as a hot air balloon. See: Tura balloon. The GEIPAN classified this case as "B": probable observation of a sky lantern. The GEIPAN did not investigate the release author.
