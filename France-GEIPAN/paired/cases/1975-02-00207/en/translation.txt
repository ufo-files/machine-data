Description: In September 1975, two individuals reported an observation made on February 15, 1975, around 6:00 PM: a bright, circular object (one meter in diameter) moved silently at a low altitude in the sky. It traveled from East to West and disappeared behind trees. No other witnesses or details were collected regarding this phenomenon, for which we have limited information.
