Description: On August 15, 2016, around midnight thirty, a witness is astonished to see a luminous phenomenon in a clear sky: three white lights in a triangle and a red light at the center move continuously at very low altitude. The witness hears a loud humming. The observation lasts 10 seconds. The witness takes a photograph with his smartphone but it does not capture the observation. Only one testimony is collected. The witness is surprised, very likely by an airplane flying exceptionally low, possibly a military aircraft (airplane or helicopter). It is not listed on civilian aircraft tracking sites, and a request to defense authorities was made too late (archives accessible for one month). However, the description of three white lights with a red light at the center is quite typical of aircraft testimony (as these are lights that can be present in addition to the standard navigation lights), and it is common for witnesses not to recognize the standard lights (fixed green and red plus a white blinking light). See cases on the Geipan website such as CONCHES-EN-OUCHE (27) 02/23/2016 or case [D417] of MUNSTER (68) near COLMAR (68) 11/13/2015. Note here that the witness hears a humming like an airplane engine. Consequently, Geipan classifies this case as A: observation of an aircraft.
