Description: On the evening of September 14, 2024, a resident of Emancé (78) and his children were observing the moon when they noticed unusual navigation lights behind a small wood. T1 identifies a military helicopter, likely a Tigre, flying slightly above the moon. A few minutes later, a second object follows a similar trajectory towards the moon. Navigation lights are visible, and a elongated silhouette resembling a "cigar" appears in the moonlight, with a white light at the front. The passage across the moon lasts 1 to 2 seconds, without any perceptible noise. With a low strangeness and good consistency (multiple witnesses, unique but fairly complete testimony, no photo of PAN), the case turns out to be an observation of a commercial airliner (see the investigation report).

*According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The description of PAN strongly suggests an airplane, as PAN is equipped with navigation lights. A reconstruction of air traffic does not reveal the helicopter described by T1, but identifies PAN as an A321 of the Vueling airline (flight VLG43PQ), whose trajectory and passage time match those of PAN. The reconstruction of its apparent trajectory shows that it did indeed pass in front of the moon. The case is classified as "A", observation of a commercial airliner.
