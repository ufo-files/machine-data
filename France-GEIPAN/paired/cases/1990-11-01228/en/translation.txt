--- seg-cdd2d34c3ff504f463548b51 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 24, 1990, in two regions (Pays de la Loire and Brittany), several witnesses observed about a dozen glowing balls moving at high speed. The investigation will show that they were laser ramps installed by EDF to mark the 20th anniversary of the Cordemais thermal power plant (44). These lasers were visible from 40 km away.
