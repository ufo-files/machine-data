--- seg-8203673b8e0c5e9b99058935 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 26, 1980, around 10:35 a.m., an amateur pilot witness observed from his property several bright flashes in the sky, resembling the beacon of an aircraft. He then saw a very bright flame followed by black smoke. No noise was heard. Believing it to be an aircraft accident, he alerted the nearest control tower. During the gendarmerie investigation, no distressed aircraft was reported in the area, and no rocket fire was recorded nearby. No other testimony was collected regarding this report, for which we lack information.
