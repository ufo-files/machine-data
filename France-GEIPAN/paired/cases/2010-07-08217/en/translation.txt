--- seg-b54d3d7cc87ae23588d87cdc [needs-review] ---
Description: Two witnesses observed on 09/07/2010 in the sky around 10:00 PM a bright white oval ball flying at high speed on a straight trajectory. No noise was heard; the object disappeared towards the North. The person who testified took a photograph and will contact local press. Several hypotheses have been considered for this moderately strange phenomenon of a light spot moving in a straight line from South to North. A meteor would have been much faster, and would have produced a trail; the ISS station, present that evening in the French sky, was not moving in that direction, and appears as a very bright point, not a "plate." The passage of a metallic festive balloon flying with the wind (10 to 20 km/h), still exposed to the sun because it was higher (sunset at 9:20 PM), could explain this observation but its apparent speed would have been lower (unless altitude winds were much stronger). Given this uncertainty, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C," a moderately strange case of average consistency.
