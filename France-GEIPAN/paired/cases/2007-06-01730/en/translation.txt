Summary: Possible observation of a Transal-type aircraft
--- seg-3bb72ee23f5b79209ad1dfc0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 12, 2007, around 10:30 PM, two witnesses at home heard a deafening noise for 10 minutes but did not see anything through their windows. At exactly 12:08 AM, the same rumbling was heard and one witness saw for 5 seconds a gray triangular object with colored lights. The object flew at very low altitude in a specific direction. It disappeared from the witness's view and the noise gradually faded away. Several Transal flights took place during the night of June 12-13 in this sector, and the witness's description supports the hypothesis of a probable aeronautical observation.
