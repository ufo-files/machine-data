Description: Over several nights starting on April 15, 2007, in a camping area in Le Croisic (44), multiple witnesses observed a bright point in the sky. The phenomenon presented as a large glowing ball with numerous changes in color, observed with binoculars, from green to red to blue. During the investigation, gendarmes themselves witnessed the phenomenon and took several photos as well as a 13-second video sequence. Preliminary analysis was entrusted to an astronomer expert who quickly leaned towards the hypothesis of an astronomical phenomenon, as the sky was particularly clear during this period, conducive to spectacular observations. To strengthen this hypothesis, an on-the-ground investigation was conducted from June 11 to 13, 2007: it will be demonstrated that the witnesses observed Sirius, a star particularly visible during this time of year. Several reports to the GEIPAN during the same period also mentioned exceptionally bright stars. The investigation also served as a basis for filming the "Pièces à convictions" (Convincing Pieces) show on June 29 on France 3.
