--- seg-46fd7aacd5e94da86abb3ef7 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 1, 2013, around 9:30 PM, a witness observed from their home a light on the horizon. Along with their spouse, they witnessed the disappearance of the PAN and the rapid passage of three blinking lights. This passage was accompanied by a resonating noise, similar to that of an airplane. Of average strangeness and weak consistency (single witness, brief observation), this case could be a misidentification with the star Arcturus followed by an aircraft formation. However, the lack of precision in the testimony (differences in direction between the account and sketches) and information on air traffic prevent the formal identification of the PAN. It is not the witness's visual perception that is in question, but the interpretation the witness makes of their observation through their feelings (surprise, nocturnal observation). This case is classified C, due to the lack of information to verify the proposed hypotheses.
