Description: On October 26, 2014, a business jet (Cessna) pilot reported crossing very quickly a dark, rectangular patch that flew very close to his windshield, following a north/south trajectory at flight level 115, three minutes after takeoff from Nice Airport at 3:21 PM local time. His passenger sitting to his right also saw the object but did not report it. The observation took place over the commune of Le Bar-Sur-Loup (06). Given the objective elements defined in the investigation report (attached document), namely: - the plane's overflight of a protected area housing many bird species, - among these birds, some are black, large, diurnal, and capable of flying at an altitude identical to that of the plane, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B" as a probable observation of a large bird (eagle, falcon, hawk, milan, vulture...) hunting or migrating.
