Summary: Likely observation of an atmospheric reentry
--- seg-dd310ed87070e94c9198a28d [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 1, 1996, around 7:30 PM, four witnesses observed the movement of a very bright point in the sky with a white trail, resembling a "neon tube." The phenomenon was silent and lasted between one and three minutes. It is most likely the atmospheric reentry of a meteorite or space debris.
