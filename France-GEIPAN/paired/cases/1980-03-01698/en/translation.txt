Description: On March 3, 1980, between 8:30 PM and 8:45 PM, a couple driving observed an orange ball the size of the setting sun. A little later, they no longer saw the ball but noticed the presence of the moon, which was whiter. It is likely that the witnesses were surprised by the moon's unusual appearance at the beginning of the night, illuminated by the setting sun.
