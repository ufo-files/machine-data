Description: In Beynac (87), on August 15, 2023, at 10:30 PM, the witness sees a glow appear in the sky. He observes for 40 seconds an "object" that moves relatively slowly. The witness then takes photos. He distinguishes a "yellow square of light" in a low position and an "orange plume" in a high position, which then disappear, giving way to a single, much smaller light point that shrinks progressively and disappears suddenly. The consistency of this case is good, one witness but a precise description and an exploitable photo. The witness's description of shape and color, along with the observation made by zooming in on the photo, points towards the observation of a celestial lantern. The movement slowly occurred from the south-east towards the south. This is entirely consistent with the meteorological data from a weather station located 8 km from the observation site (see the weather document): wind directed towards the SE and very weak (0 km/h average and gusts up to 6.4 km/h). The sudden disappearance can be explained by the extinction of the flame. The witness very likely observed a celestial lantern. See the Sky Misperceptions website. The case is classified B: very probable observation of a celestial lantern.
