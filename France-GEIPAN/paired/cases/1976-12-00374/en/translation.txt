Description: On December 23, 1976, around 2:20 AM, a military guard on surveillance noticed a round object flying at low altitude, illuminated by several lights emitting bright red light. Another red light rotated at its top, resembling a rotating beacon. The craft moved slowly and emitted a continuous hiss of medium intensity. It disappeared, hidden by another building. The observation lasted 30 seconds. No other direct witnesses to the observation came forward. This military personnel likely observed the passage of a helicopter-type aircraft.
