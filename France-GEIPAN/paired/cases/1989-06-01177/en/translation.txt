Summary: Observation of flattened wheat in a field.
--- seg-205d49664edf5314c7963c6b [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Monday, June 12, 1989, a farmer noticed in one of his wheat fields an area approximately 25 meters by 3 meters where the wheat was yellowed, laid down, and even pulled up. At the center of the trace, there is a hole 10 cm by 20 cm deep, as well as a gash in the soil along the trace's axis. Several hypotheses have been suggested, such as lightning, or more likely, the "rough" landing of a hot air balloon, although at that time and in that location, all aerial activity was prohibited by a prefectural decree due to the Bourget Air Show. The latter hypothesis, however, is favored by the SEPRA.
