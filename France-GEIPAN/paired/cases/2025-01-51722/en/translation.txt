Description: On January 25, 2025, at 2:00 PM, in the municipality of Artigues-Près-Bordeaux (33), two witnesses observed a metallic-looking, black sphere. The object moved slowly and straight, following an east-west axis, at a constant elevation of 45°. After approximately 10 seconds of observation, the phenomenon was obscured by a building, ending direct visibility. The consistency* of this observation case is considered average: only one of the two witnesses reported their observation to GEIPAN, and there is no photo or video of PAN. *According to GEIPAN's criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. Strangeness is low, mainly consisting of the metallic appearance of PAN. The investigation prioritizes the observation of a fantasy balloon, of Mylar type (consisting of an aluminized envelope) carried by the wind (see annex Météo). This conclusion is based on the following elements: - Round shape, entirely classic for such a balloon. - Black color with a metallic appearance, typical of a Mylar-type balloon. - Straight movement in the direction of the wind, from west to east. - Slow speed, consistent with that of the wind and the distance traveled. However, there is uncertainty regarding the accuracy of the date or time reported by the witnesses. Indeed, weather records attest to total cloud cover with precipitation in Bordeaux during the indicated time slot, which weakens the consistency with the declared visual observation conditions. GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a Mylar balloon.
