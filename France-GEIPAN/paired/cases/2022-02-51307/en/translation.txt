Summary: Observation of a bright arc and then two close bright points: observation of the reflection of headlights from one or several snowmobiles on the cloud layer.
--- seg-eb36445a88798341ab1a1718 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the evening of February 14, 2022, three witnesses, positioned behind the window of their balcony apartment on the third floor of a building in Eybens, observed a PAN above the crests of the Belledonne mountain range to the east. The PAN appeared as a bright circular arc, with an apparent diameter equivalent to that of the Moon, and exhibited similar brightness and color. Initially believing they were observing the Moon, the witnesses were surprised to see, after approximately two minutes, the PAN gradually decrease in size while remaining stationary above the crests. During this decrease, an observation through binoculars allowed them to briefly distinguish two close bright points. Their certainty that it was not the Moon was reinforced when they located it in a different direction, higher in the sky. The witness reported their observation via a technical questionnaire and a Gendarmerie protocol (police report). For this case of good consistency (multiple witnesses, remote investigation followed by an on-site investigation with a cognitive interview for one witness), we were able to study several hypotheses, one of which emerged as the most likely: the observation of a reflection phenomenon (halo) on the cloud layer from the powerful projectors of one (or several) snowgroomer(s) operating in the area around Alpe d'Huez, possibly near the Blanc Pic, located approximately 30 kilometers from the witnesses (see the investigation report).

According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- The shape of the PAN is consistent with such a halo. The lower part of the halo's circle is obscured by the mountain crests in the foreground, creating an arc-shaped appearance.
- The color matches that of the snowgroomer's projectors.
- The presence of several snowgroomers in the area during the entire observation period has been confirmed (webcams).
- The azimuth corresponds to the direction of observation.
- A cloud layer at 3300-3500 meters and fragmented nebulosity, or the absence of nebulosity below 2700 meters, have been confirmed (Meteorology France + webcams).
- The light power of the snowgroomers is visible in numerous photos and videos.
- The movement of a snowgroomer (turns, ascents, descents, forward/backward motion) is consistent with the "rising/setting" impression described by the witnesses.
- No noise was reported (distance of ~30 km).

The case is classified as "A," observation of the reflection of one or more snowgroomer projectors on the cloud layer.
