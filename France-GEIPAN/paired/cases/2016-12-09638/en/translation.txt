Description: On Sunday, December 18, 2016 at 21:05, three people on a public square observed the silent movement of a very bright phenomenon in the night sky. This intense white light seemed to stop above a building before quickly disappearing towards the North, hidden by fog. The observation lasted approximately one minute. Only one testimony was collected in 2017. It is likely that the witness saw a Thai lantern: the shape indications (rounded) and light (very bright white) are compatible. The PAN arrived from the NE, consistent with the dominant wind that evening measured at 15 km per hour: see the weather report on Infoclimat. The fact that the PAN did not follow a regular trajectory (stop, change course, acceleration) is due to the variable wind currents in a valley near a major river. A weekend night (especially during Christmas holidays) is conducive to festive events like releasing lanterns. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable observation of a Thai lantern.
