Description: On the night of September 7 to 8, 2013 at 1:25 AM, a couple noticed in the sky the east-west movement of approximately thirty bright white lights. The speed was constant, no noise, trails, or flickering lights were observed. The observation of dozens of glowing points flying together in a straight line from Saturday night into Sunday is fairly common, and clearly evokes Thai lanterns, probably released after a wedding. The witness notes that they were observing from 800 meters from the Saint-Aignan castle, which hosted a wedding meal that evening. It is not possible to correlate the direction of movement with the wind in Bordeaux-Mérignac at that time because the wind, very light, changed direction at least every hour, and the proximity of the Dordogne river can cause local breezes. The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'B': probable observation of Thai lanterns.
