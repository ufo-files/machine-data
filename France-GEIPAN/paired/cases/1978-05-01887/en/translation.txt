Description: On May 5, 1978, around 7:30 PM, three individuals observed a red glow above an island. This glow slowly moved south before abruptly changing course to head northwest. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted over 4 minutes. On May 12, gendarmes observed the same phenomenon for one minute. No further information was gathered on this event, for which we lack precise details.
