Description: A witness reports their observation made during the night from Sunday to Monday, July 14, 2008 (between midnight and 00:05) in a clear and cloudless sky. They observed above their home the silent passage SO-NE of a large, dark triangular shape bordered at each end by non-flashing round lights, of great size and yellow-orange in color. The witness steps outside but the PAN has disappeared. Only one testimony was collected. Considering the following objective elements:

• Fine weather, good visibility, pitch black night.
• Light breeze, the trajectory of the PAN is consistent with the direction of the local wind.
• Short duration observation.
• The witness saw several large lights with the perception of a single object.
• Dark orange color, relatively moderate light intensity of the PAN.
• The observation was made during a 3-day weekend coinciding with a national holiday.

It is concluded that the witness very likely observed several Thai lanterns (or luminous festive balloons) carried aloft by the wind, giving the impression of a single object. These lanterns could have been launched either from Saint-Malo itself, or from Dinard or one of its beaches on a summer Sunday evening, the eve of a national holiday. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Observation of Thai lanterns.
