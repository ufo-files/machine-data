Summary: Observation of a disk-shaped object moving at very high speed
--- seg-16e7688131b48a730002ee9e [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 12, 1979, a night watchman observed in the sky an object shaped like a flying saucer, slightly rounded in the middle and tapering at the edges. The color of the object was orange. A trail of purple and orange color was visible behind the object. It suddenly disappeared at the horizon at very high speed. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
