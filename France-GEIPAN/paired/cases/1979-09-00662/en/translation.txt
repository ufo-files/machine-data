Summary: Observation of an object at high altitude
--- seg-dfe524bc5a6743f1429dc51b [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 24, 1979, at 7:42 a.m., a witness initially spotted a streak in the sky, which he assumed was from an airplane jet. Then he noticed an object that remained stationary for 10 to 15 seconds. Orange in color, it had the shape of a disk. Suddenly, the object moved at a very high speed, turning into a brilliant white before disappearing. No other testimonies about this phenomenon were collected, and we lack information about it.
