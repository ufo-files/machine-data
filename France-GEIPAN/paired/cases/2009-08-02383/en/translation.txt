Summary: Observation of the movement of an orange ball: probable observation of the passage of a Thai lantern.
--- seg-9d4eb22d2776774ab796a6c0 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness reports an observation made on August 7, 2009 at 9:20 PM in Golf-Juan, a seaside resort in the commune of Vallauris (06): his son observed in the sky the movement and change in direction of an orange object in the shape of a sphere that intrigued him. The main witness arrived on the balcony of the first floor and actually observed at high altitude the silent movement and constant speed of an orange ball. A photograph was taken. The object moved away then faded becoming gray before completely disappearing hidden by a hill. The description of the flying object corresponds to that of a Thai lantern: the movement speed is compatible with local weather conditions (little wind, variable thermal breezes), the observation duration (3 minutes) as well, and the description of the object's disappearance. The witness interprets this object as distant and very large, a frequent confusion since it is impossible to estimate the distance of an unknown object. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable passage of a Thai lantern.
