--- seg-9762343dfa02b5f767a1660d [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 23, 2009, between 6:15 and 6:30 PM, several people observed four bright points moving very high in the sky. These points formed intriguing geometric patterns. Only one witness contacted the GEIPAN. This testimony is rather unremarkable and the description too vague for serious study. They could simply be four helium-filled balloons reflecting sunlight. The geometric shapes would be merely the result of wind movements. However, the GEIPAN has no concrete evidence to support this hypothesis. Unfortunately, this case was not investigated until 2012, too late to search for scientific or amateur balloon launches. Without precise data, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to lack of information.
