Summary: Observation of a round-shaped light in the East sky: astronomical observation of the Moon.
--- seg-6d5eb7cdad04ef3a8082171f [needs-review] ---
Description: This observation case is part of cases classified "C" by the GEPAN and has been subject to a re-examination (see the investigation notes document). On January 23, 1981, around 21:00, a witness returning from work on a moped was intrigued by a large round shape to the east in the sky. This shape emitted an intense orange light. According to the witness, it changed position then disappeared suddenly. During the investigation, no other testimonies were collected. This case is inconsistent (single witness, brief observation), and the description of the PAN observed is quite precise. The witness's sincerity and credibility were never questioned. The phenomenon described shares many common characteristics (shape, size, color) with a perfectly known astronomical object: the Moon at sunrise. Furthermore, the Moon was clearly present in the observed area of the sky. The issue is not the witness's visual perception but the interpretation the witness makes of their observation through their feelings (fatigue, driving at night on frozen ground, fog presence). In the current classification of GEIPAN, this case of weak strangeness is classified "A," an observation related to a misperception with the Moon.
