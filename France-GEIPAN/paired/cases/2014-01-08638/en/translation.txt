Description: On January 1, 2014, around 1:10 AM, a witness on a street observed the successive passage of five glowing orbs of a yellow-orange color in a cloudy sky. The observation lasts for approximately twenty minutes before the orbs disappear one after the other. No other testimony was collected. This very precise and well-documented testimony describes the passage of five glowing balls with a yellow-orange light. The horizontal trajectory is straight and the objects are moving below the cloud layer. The wind at Pontoise-Cormeilles is from the southwest according to the weather report. The Infoclimat record at Saint-Ouen-L'aumône indicates SSO. The witness looks to the southeast and sees the orbs move from his right to his left while ascending. He feels like these balls are closing in, either actually closing in or due to an increase in their brightness. The videos, excessively zoomed and defocused (out of focus blur), do not show the details of the object, just its orange color, and various optical and digital artifacts. The trajectory of the lights as proposed in the report compiled by the witness is not justified. This testimony has all the characteristics of a Thai lantern observation: orange glowing balls succeeding on roughly linear trajectory according to local wind during a festive night. The objects are probably much closer than the witness estimates: likely between 1 and 2 kilometers. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns. The witness refutes this hypothesis.
