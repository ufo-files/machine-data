Description: On March 14, 2014 at 7:30 PM, a motorist and his daughter observed through the windshield the very fast movement of a luminous phenomenon in the cloudy sky. The phenomenon disappeared behind the horizon. The observation of a large, very bright point, followed by a short trail, crossing a portion of the sky in about 1 second, suggests a meteor (the very fast atmospheric entry of a meteorite, a small rocky body). This phenomenon was observed by numerous other witnesses who wrote about it on forums: Meteor of March 14, 2014. See also a video taken in Spanish Catalonia: Meteor in Spain. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A", identified as a meteor. See also: explanation: Meteor.
