Description: On the night of April 7 to 8, 2025, at 12:22 AM, a resident of LUGNY-CHAMPAGNE (18) was observing the Moon through a telescope at the entrance of his home. He noticed a large shadow passing through his field of vision. He did not see the passage of a physical object between himself and the Moon, but rather that of a shadow, giving an impression of "transparency." The observation lasted about one second. No other testimonies were collected. The case's consistency is judged low, with only one witness and a furtive indirect observation (through the telescope). Due to the dark appearance and apparent size of the PAN, the considered hypothesis is that of a flying animal in the observation field between the telescope and the Moon (bird, insect, bat, etc.). However, with a judged low consistency, this hypothesis cannot be confirmed (see the investigation report). Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as "C": Unidentified phenomenon due to lack of data.
