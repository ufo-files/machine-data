Description: On December 25, 2022, while driving on highway A20 between Brive-la-Gaillarde (19) and Limoges (87), in the area of Uzerche, the witness observes through their windshield two PAN around 21:45. The observation lasts for approximately 20 seconds. The two objects, round in shape and blue/white/gray in color, exhibit distinct behaviors: one remains stationary, while the other moves in all directions nearby. The two PAN suddenly disappear when the witness attempts to photograph them. The case consistency is average, as there is only one witness, and no photos or videos of the PAN are available. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The description of the PAN closely matches the observation of a skytracer (round shape, blue/white/gray color, immobility, and movements in all directions). The overcast sky with relatively low clouds is also a favorable factor for the hypothesis, as these clouds serve as a medium for the light projected into the sky. A nightclub was open that evening in the observation area, but it does not have a skytracer (as noted in the investigation report). Such a device, used for private parties, especially during the Christmas season, is entirely plausible with the democratization of this type of equipment. However, the absence of other witnesses and the imprecision of the witness's location do not allow for a formal confirmation or denial of the explanatory hypothesis, which remains probable given the similarity of the PAN to the characteristics of a light projection on a cloudy cover. The case is classified as "B," probable observation of a light projection that may be from a skytracer.
