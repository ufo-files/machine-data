--- seg-9598625b01a1d1d7d3dbf8c2 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 5, 1977, between 10:30 PM and 11:15 PM, three individuals observed an unusual phenomenon in the sky for several minutes. All witnesses described the movements of a bright red-orange ball that, according to them, either remained stationary or moved from right to left and up and down before taking a descending trajectory and disappearing. No ground traces were found in the area. This phenomenon appears to have followed a NE-to-W trajectory. Too few details were provided to validate a specific hypothesis. This case is classified as C due to a lack of information.
