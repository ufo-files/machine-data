--- seg-d4fd50ef20afa946f3d5a105 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 9, 2016, around 10:45 PM, a witness and his spouse observed for 3 minutes the silent movement under a cloudy ceiling of a circular, orange object that became cylindrical and black after changing direction. The object was observed with the naked eye and then with binoculars. Only one testimony was collected. The description strongly resembles a Thai lantern flight, in terms of color and appearance, the type of movement, and the consistency of that movement with the dominant wind direction (northeasterly wind reported at Sénébret, 9 km away). Additionally, the final phase of the lantern's extinction, where the bright light can give way to the lantern's dark body, is also indicative. In particular, the lantern appeared as an orange disk when it was closest, and later, in a more profile view, as a vertical cylinder (the exact shape of a lantern, here a dark cylinder as the lantern was extinguished). The uneven glow within the ball or disk, especially when observed through binoculars, is also typical of a lantern. Consequently, the GEIPAN is classified as B: probable observation of a Thai lantern.
