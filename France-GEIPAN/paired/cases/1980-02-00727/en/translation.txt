Description: On January 28, 1980, around 4:15 a.m., a witness driving a car spotted a bright object to his right. This spherical object was stationary about 40 meters above the ground and 150 meters from the witness. Orange in color, it emitted a strong light directed towards the sky. The object itself was contained within a larger, brighter circle. The witness continued on his way and noticed that the object seemed to be following him. No noise or smell was noticed by the witness, who became afraid. The object disappeared into the landscape shortly after. No other witnesses or additional information were gathered regarding this phenomenon, for which we lack details.
