--- seg-14c9b5d4860de8a85d59f185 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 14, 2025, around 7 a.m., the witness is on their southeast-facing terrace, watching the sunrise. While observing the stars during this transition between night and day, he notices a group of approximately 10 white lights aligned in a straight line. These lights appear to emerge from the roof of his house (northwest side) and head southeast (direction confirmed by the reconstruction photo that the witness sent), more precisely slightly east of the usual sunrise position. Only one testimony will be collected. The description of the phenomenon shows that the witness observed a fleet of Starlink® satellites aligned (Starlink® satellite train), a configuration they take once deployed from their launcher before taking their place in the network of SpaceX's megaconstellation of Starlink® satellites, enabling internet distribution from low Earth orbit. It is the Sun, setting for the observer, that reflects off the solar panels of satellites, making them visible (see the technical note). The track of these satellites is confirmed by COSE* (see the technical note in the annex). The launch was performed by a FALCON9 rocket from CAP CANAVERAL on 01/13/2025 at 16:47:09 UTC. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the STARLINK® satellite train from the 2025-008 launch on 01/13/2025. *COSE: Operational Space Surveillance Center of CNES.
