Description: On July 18, 2007, around 4:45 a.m., a witness observed from their terrace a luminous phenomenon that intrigued them. A light moved closer, then changed direction and disappeared. No noise was heard by the witness during the observation. The location data provided by the witness does not allow for a precise point but a zone (based on estimated distances to nearby villages by the witness). The observation of a growing light approaching the witness corresponds well to an airplane landing light approaching Figari Airport. The absence of noise is due to the wind direction, coming from the northeast, behind the witness.
