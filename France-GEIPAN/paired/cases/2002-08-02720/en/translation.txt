Description: Recent testimony (02/13/2011) of an old observation without a precise date. We arbitrarily date the observation to August 1, 2002. In the early part of August 2002, around 8:30 PM, a motorist observed in the sky the passage of a flying object moving slowly on a straight trajectory in a south-east direction. The object had a cylindrical shape, a brown color, moved without any noise, leaving a short trail behind it. He then took an analog photograph. The witness called the gendarmerie, stating that he had seen "a rocket" moving from northwest to southeast. It was only after finding the photograph years later that the witness filled out a questionnaire and sent two photographs to GEIPAN. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected. The late testimony of this observation makes any investigation impossible at this time.
