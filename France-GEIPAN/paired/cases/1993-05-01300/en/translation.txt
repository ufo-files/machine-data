--- seg-8f07b5a81350c803e259118b [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 1, 1993, around 8 PM, and the following day, three witnesses observed "balls" that were either stationary or moving faster than an airplane, with variable colors (green or orange). There were three to five of these balls, about the size of a star, and silent. One of the witnesses indicated that he had already seen the phenomenon the previous year. The observation lasted for a few minutes. No other information could be gathered.
