Description: On October 30, 2011, between 15:10 and 15:20, a person spotted in the sky a glowing point that twinkled. The object appeared stationary or moved very slowly. No noise was heard during this observation. Photos taken by the witness suggest a balloon, similar to those used by Météo-France, which conducts several daily releases of radiosonde balloons across the metropolitan territory. Météo-France launches a weather balloon from Bordeaux-Mérignac around 12:00 UTC (2:00 PM local time that day). It is highly likely that the witness observed one of these balloons. This case is classified as "B" - Probable cause: weather balloon.
