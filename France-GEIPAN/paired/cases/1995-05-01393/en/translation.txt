Summary: Observation of a large object above a house with alleged effects on witnesses, animals, and plants.
--- seg-713c95943152700e5aa8f860 [needs-review] ---
Description: Between May 25 and 28, 1995, around 3:30 a.m., a couple observed a large, dark object overhead that resembled a helicopter. The object was stationary, without any noise, smoke, or trail. After illuminating the ground with light beams several times, the object departed at a moderate speed. During this observation, the witness's dogs entered the house and hid without barking. After about a week, the dogs began vomiting and experiencing diarrhea. The witness's wife experienced health issues, while the witness felt extremely tired. The witness also noted a certain degradation of vegetation at the location illuminated by the lights. They also reported a neighbor's sudden hospitalization. Frightened, the witness contacted Civil Security to inquire if a link could be established between the observed phenomenon and the effects observed on themselves, their wife, their neighbor, their dogs, and their plants. The investigation, while unable to identify the observed object, also failed to establish any connection between the object's observation and the alleged effects. The witnesses' health conditions can be explained by the wife's medical treatment and the intense heat. The neighbor's hospitalization is not surprising given their age. Many plants located outside the assumed influence zone also exhibit similar signs of degradation. Despite this, the observed phenomenon remains unexplained.
