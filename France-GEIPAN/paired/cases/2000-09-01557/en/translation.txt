Description: On September 23, 24, and 25, 1999, around 6:00 a.m., several witnesses from two neighboring houses observed a strong, stationary white light that disappeared with dawn. The observation was also made in the evening around 10:15 p.m. The phenomenon was silent and very bright, making it difficult to observe with binoculars. No other information is available on this observation.
