Summary: Observation of the passage of glowing balls: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-278d86db1ecd40b7302bbcd9 [needs-review] ---
Description: Several people reported seeing on April 2, 2011, around 11 PM, the silent and grouped movement in the sky of numerous orange balls above the city; these disappear instantly. The witnesses' descriptions perfectly match Thai lanterns (orange lights moving with the wind, lasting less than 20 minutes). A new release occurred a week later (see the April 11, 2011, article in L'Est Républicain in the procedural record). This case is therefore classified as "B", with a very probable identification, but the authors of the release have not been found.
