--- seg-81e866eef6b9f668aa6e5a06 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness and his son observed on February 11, 2015, around 6:40 PM, a bright point in the sky moving faster than a commercial airliner. The point then passed over the top of a building and eventually faded away. This observation report is typical of a "large" satellite observation. The "Calsky" record indicates the passage of the International Space Station (ISS) at exactly this time, according to the diagram provided by the witness (note, however, an error in the initial azimuth of the observation, due to a misunderstanding in the form). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the International Space Station (ISS).
