Summary: Observation of the movements of a PAN in the sky: insufficient information for in-depth study.
--- seg-b93d2acd907f8288d037fdd8 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 15, 2017, at 4:30 PM, a witness in their garden noticed an unusual, stationary "cloud" in the sky. The witness lost sight of the PAN for a moment, then spotted it again, much larger and in motion. The witness described the top of the object as the "top of a saucer" in a color "white, black, and gray." The PAN then disappeared, moving at an extremely high speed in a "low flyby" direction towards Montflanquin and Fumel. Only one testimony was collected. The consistency is very low, with very few details. The case description had to be reconstructed from primarily graphical elements provided by the witness in the questionnaire (see the investigation report). The GEIPAN considered conducting a field investigation to meet with the witness and improve the consistency. However, it seemed that a field investigation would not provide any more reliable information for this observation. Although usable data a priori are present (altitude, size, duration estimates...), they remain vague and disparate. The GEIPAN classifies this observation as C: lack of information for in-depth study.
