--- seg-39a4bdceeb2b77d4675ab9b1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Wednesday, November 6, 2013, at 6:00 p.m., several people were intrigued by the presence in the sky of a type of fireball, followed by oblique streaks of the same color (flaming orange). The witness filmed the scene. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of bright orange streaks in the sunset sky clearly suggests distant airplane trails illuminated by the sun already below the horizon. The video confirms this hypothesis, as does the "Casperflights" record of commercial airplanes, which were very numerous in this area at that time (North European traffic / Spain-Portugal-Morocco). The first video (vertical descending streak) corresponds to an airplane moving away from the witnesses, while the other horizontal streaks correspond to airplanes traveling from North to South, about fifty kilometers west of Pau. Exceptional meteorological conditions made this spectacle particularly remarkable that evening. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of airplane trails.
