Summary: Possible observation of an astral conjunction
--- seg-fb0c088bc67bfb50e5abf78a [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 31, 1980, returning from work, an automobilist spotted three low-altitude lights in the sky. The approximate direction of his observation was N/E (to the left of the vehicle from the direction of travel). He will have the impression that these points are following him during his movements or stop at the same time as him. No noise was heard. At his home, his wife made the same observation until 7:30 a.m., the time he left for work. During the investigation, other witnesses to this same phenomenon came forward. An examination of the sky that night revealed a particularly visible conjunction of Venus, Jupiter, and Saturn in the sky to the East and very low on the horizon until dawn. The witnesses likely observed this phenomenon.
