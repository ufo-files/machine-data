Description: A concise email reports an observation made on May 10, 2008, around 11:30 PM in Avignon. A witness spots a bright light in the clear sky, without any storms. Ten seconds later, the witness observes the movement of a round, orange object coming from the north, followed by another similar object. Both objects are heading towards the southeast before disappearing. Only one testimony was collected. This case is very lightly documented: three lines of email from the witness. The witness's observation is characteristic of an observation of Thai lanterns, both in terms of colors, shapes, and the type of trajectory. The perceived direction of movement from the witness (north to southeast) is consistent with the wind measured in Avignon that day (40° east origin), considering that the PAN appears to pass to the east of the witness without passing directly overhead (he is looking out the window). Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: Thai lanterns.
