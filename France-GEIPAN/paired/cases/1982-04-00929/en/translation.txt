Summary: Observation of a triangular-shaped object moving in a zigzag pattern.
--- seg-ba7332210720ac6bf71fe080 [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 21, 1982, around 11 PM, several skywatchers scattered across a wide area began communicating about a particularly bright point of light. Through binoculars, the object appeared as a triangle emitting intense light. At an extremely high altitude, it performed zigzags and sometimes hovered in place. No trail was observed behind the object. For some skywatchers, the observation lasted until 3:30 AM before the object disappeared. No further information was gathered about this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
