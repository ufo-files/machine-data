Description: On April 13, 2012, around 9:34 PM, a witness is intrigued by the silent passage of a bright object in the sky that he cannot identify. Of low strangeness and good consistency despite an orientation error, later corrected, this observation case is undoubtedly related to the passage of the International Space Station (ISS). There is no doubt that the witness observed the ISS on the evening of April 13 between 9:34 PM and 9:37 PM. The "curved" trajectory described by the witness is due to a misinterpretation of the nearly circular orbit; only satellites passing at zenith give the impression of a linear trajectory. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A" for a certain observation of the International Space Station.
