Description: On June 10, 1982, several people, including airport control tower staff, will observe a bright point in the sky. The investigation reveals that pilots also sighted this bright object, which is likely a stratospheric balloon launched by CNES from the Gap site and drifting and evolving at 30,000 to 40,000 meters altitude, driven by southerly winds after releasing its payload.
