Summary: Observation of the movement and then rapid departure of a very bright white point in the sky: observation of the passage of the International Space Station.
--- seg-16da7194677a62776d4aeee0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the evening of August 22, 2012, witnesses from their terrace observed a very bright point moving across the sky. The object departed in an unusual manner at high speed into the sky. It came from the West and disappeared towards the East. The Calsky site indicates the passage of the International Space Station (ISS), extremely bright, visible from Brue-Auriac from 10:11 PM (22:11) West to 10:17 PM (22:17) Southeast (see attached). The appearance of the ISS is consistent with the witnesses' account. Its disappearance in broad daylight corresponds to its entering the Earth's shadow. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the ISS.
