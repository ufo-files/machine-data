--- seg-b97b21a2d1386a44fd1e3fa2 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 21, 1979, at 10:25 local time, several sailors on a French Navy vessel in the Ionian Sea (lat 38°02N and long 17°41E) observed a luminous phenomenon at a 15° angle above the horizon. A growing bright point suddenly split into three glowing parts whose intensity gradually decreased. The colors changed from white to orange before returning to white. The observed trajectory was south-east. The phenomenon, moving at high speed, was observed for one minute. These individuals were likely witnesses to an atmospheric reentry.
