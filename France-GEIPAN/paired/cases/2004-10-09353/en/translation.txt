--- seg-12d600e51e30ddba743a5f5e [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 26, 2004, around 2:15 PM, a driver on the highway suddenly felt observed and, when he turned his head, he was intrigued by the presence of gray circular shapes in a cloud. These shapes moved like fan blades and made him think of "an eye." The observation was intermittent over several seconds; the witness did not stop. Only one testimony was collected. The witness reported his observation three years later. The testimony lacks consistency, as:

- The observation includes many details despite being old and made under poor conditions, while driving at 130 km/h and looking backward;
- The hypothesis of a reflection off closed windows is not entirely ruled out, although it was mentioned and rejected by the witness.

- The observation took place in a highly emotional context, induced by the feeling of being observed, a feeling present even before the observation, and which seems to have led the witness to remember details or comparisons related to a "watching eye."

Therefore, GEIPAN classifies the case as C: lack of reliable information. Note: The witness had made a similar observation (a tracking eye on the highway) two weeks earlier. Case AIX-EN-PROVENCE (13) 05.10.2004
