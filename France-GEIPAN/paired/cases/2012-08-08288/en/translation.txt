Summary: Observation of 3 colored and flashing lights moving in the night sky: probable observation of a helicopter or a tourist plane in VFR night flight.
--- seg-a9d9bf63140469b5470af1ac [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 15, 2012, around 10 PM, a witness on a terrace was stargazing. Intrigued by the unusual movement of two, then three, flashing lights of different colors, the witness changed their observation location. After considering various known phenomenon hypotheses, none satisfied them. The observation was confirmed by two other family members who thought it might be a helicopter or a satellite. During the 15-minute observation, no sounds were heard. The PAN left the witness's field of vision. No other testimonies were collected. Based on the investigation's findings, including the analysis of six hypotheses, two of which fit the observation very well in terms of physical and dynamic characteristics (see the investigation report), the GEIPAN classifies this case as category "B," a probable observation of a helicopter or a tourist plane flying VFR at night towards Nantes Airport, Ancenis Airfield, or Saint-Nazaire's DAG base. This testimony is of average quality and consistency as, while three witnesses were present, only one completed the questionnaire, while the other two only provided very brief responses to the gendarmes. Furthermore, the testimony is not accompanied by photographs. The observation is ultimately not strange as the PAN can be easily characterized, both in appearance and behavior.
