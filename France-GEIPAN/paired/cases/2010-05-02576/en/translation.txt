Description: On May 30, 2010, at 10:50 PM, two individuals observed the passage above their residence of approximately twenty orange balls. These objects moved silently in the same direction. The witness did not respond to the Geipan's repeated requests for additional information. Without a gendarmerie report or additional information, any investigation is impossible. Although the characteristics (multiple orange balls with simultaneous transverse trajectories) suggest the known phenomenon of flying lantern formation, this case is classified as C due to a lack of information.
