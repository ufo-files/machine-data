Description: On October 24, 2012, at 7:50 PM, a driver had her attention drawn by the quick passage of a luminous phenomenon in the sky. The witness describes a bright white light that, on a descending trajectory, disappears after a few seconds. The witness then discerns a shape, and the light reappears progressively on an ascending trajectory before definitively disappearing behind the hill. No noise was heard. Only one testimony was collected. First and foremost, in an attempt to answer one of the questions posed by the witness ("I would like to know if other people have reported this phenomenon to you"), we searched for similar reports at the date and time of the observation in the Grenoble region. Unfortunately, without success. In view of the objective elements collected in the attached investigation report, namely that the physical and dynamic characteristics of the PAN do not correspond to those of an airplane, helicopter, drone, or meteor, but that a reflection in the windshield could have produced the same scene, we cannot conclude on the nature of the PAN observed, due to lack of corroboration, other testimonies, or photographs. This GEIPAN testimony is of average consistency: precise but brief (observation duration of 10-15 seconds), from a single witness in a car, without a photograph. The observation is somewhat strange because it involves an object with unusual behavior and appearance, but it can be explained by conventional phenomena. This case is classified as "C" due to a lack of information.
