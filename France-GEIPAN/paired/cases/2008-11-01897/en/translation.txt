Description: On November 7, 2008, at 6:25 PM, a witness is waiting for his companion to go to a show. He steps outside to observe the sky and spots two unusual, fixed bright points located to the East. As his friend arrives, the points slowly fade out. The observation lasted for a few minutes. High-altitude weather balloons could easily account for this appearance: the observation took place approximately one hour after sunset; at altitude, the balloons are still in sunlight; their simultaneous fading, as they enter shadow, is perfectly logical. This entirely plausible hypothesis, however, could not be confirmed by GEIPAN due to the significant time gap since the event. This case is classified B: probable observation of two weather balloons.
