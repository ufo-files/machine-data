Description: On July 27, 2015 at 8:00 pm (Tahiti time) a witness observed a long, glowing trail with parts detaching and moving East-West. The continuous observation lasted between 30 and 40 seconds. The phenomenon disappeared behind clouds. Only one testimony was collected. Given the collected elements, namely: - The reentry time is identical to that of the observation. - Existing records on the SATOBS site. - The physical and dynamic characteristics of the observation are in every way similar to those of a reentry of space debris. The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'A' as a certain observation of the disintegration upon entry into the atmosphere of a Soyuz rocket stage cataloged under the number "15035B/40745", the day after its resupply of the ISS.
