Description: A witness reports in August 2009 an observation made in July 2003. Around 2:00 PM, several people in a garden observe the silent passage of twenty to thirty orbs in the blue sky. These orbs come from the northeast and head towards the southwest. The observation lasts for approximately 10 minutes. Only one testimony is collected. It is an unusable testimony by GEIPAN as it is a report six years after the observation, which does not specify the date (July 2003...that's all). What is described is not very strange as it is compatible with a release of helium balloons. There is no way to confirm the hypothesis with wind direction without a date. GEIPAN classifies as C: Lack of information.
