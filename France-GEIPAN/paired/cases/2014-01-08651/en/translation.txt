--- seg-72d9712d884930ce01478a5e [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Through the glass bay of their living room, witnesses see a very bright, glowing ball pass in 2 seconds. This single description already evokes the observation of a bolide, that is to say, the entry into the atmosphere of a meteoroid. The glowing ball has a slight trail. The GEIPAN received no other testimonies, and the BOAM (Meteor Observers Database) site did not record this type of event at that time. This testimony is very consistent due to the two witnesses and the precision of the description of the phenomenon's trajectory. The estimated altitude (100m) made intuitively is very likely incorrect, as with all unknown phenomena: in this case, as the phenomenon was very bright, the witness interpreted it as very close, but without objective references. The very clear sky allowed the phenomenon to be very visible, and perceived even closer. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a bolide. Note: Since the witness observed the phenomenon through glass, the GEIPAN also considered the hypothesis of artificial light (motorcycle headlight, electric torch) coming from behind the witnesses, but the fields of view do not seem to correspond to the directions of observation.
