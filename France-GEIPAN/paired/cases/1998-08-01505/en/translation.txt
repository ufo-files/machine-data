Summary: Possible observation of MIR space station passes
--- seg-8ce88cc6442953fd4e276971 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 5 and 6, 1998, around 10:30 PM, a woman observed a white light moving across the sky. The investigation revealed that the MIR station was perfectly visible during those two days at the time of the observation. An American satellite-guided unmanned aerial vehicle experiment was also potentially visible on August 22. Given the observations made, the hypothesis that the woman observed the MIR station is retained.
