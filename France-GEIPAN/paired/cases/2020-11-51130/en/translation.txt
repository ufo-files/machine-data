Description: On November 25, 2020, around midday, a driver had their attention drawn to a dark, shape-shifting object in the sky. The witness stopped and took two photos and two videos. No unusual noises were heard during the passage of the PAN. No other testimonies were collected about this phenomenon. With a relatively low strangeness rating and average consistency (single witness, PAN not appearing in the photos or videos), this case appears to be a probable misidentification with a balloon (see the investigation report). The witness's description of the PAN aligns with a balloon (oblong shape, dark color), and the indicated trajectory remains consistent with the wind direction. Unfortunately, the PAN does not appear in the photos or videos taken by the witness, which could have provided useful comparisons to validate the explanatory hypothesis. It's worth noting that the observation occurred during the daytime, above a city, and no other witnesses came forward, reducing the strangeness of the case. The hypothesis of a misidentification with a balloon is strengthened by the fact that the witness later compared their observation to a photograph of a PAN that was identified as a solar balloon. The case is classified as B, probable misidentification with a balloon.
