Description: On July 13th around 4 a.m., the witness, at their home in Chaville (92), observed very organized light movements: "at first in the form of a crown with aligned lines, these shapes very quickly changed as if to form a light vortex before giving birth to a donut-shaped form." This lasted several minutes before ending in a small halo of light. Only one testimony will be collected. The photos provided by the witness point towards the observation of bright light spots, from mono-beam projectors ("skyrose") impacting a low cloud layer. Indeed, we note: - a white, blue, translucent color, - evolving shapes: "crown", line, "donut". Additionally, the witness mentions the absence of noise. The range of such projectors is generally over 10 km. They can be commanded automatically or manually and produce fixed or moving light beams (independent or not) of various shapes (see the JM Sky Tracers website). The fact that the movements stop, leaving only a small light halo, can be explained by a pause to adjust the device or the end of the animation before shutdown. After investigations, between 4 a.m. and 5 a.m. on July 13, the first cloud layer was between 1800 meters and 2300 meters in altitude with very good visibility: around 43 km (see the weather document for Villacoublay (78), approximately 4 km from Chaville (92)). These are conditions conducive to the formation of impact patches on the base of clouds and the possibility of seeing this phenomenon from a distance. It is possible that there were either early-phase festive activities in the vicinity of Chaville (92) or test operations on equipment in anticipation of July 14th. The case is classified as A: very probable observation of impact spots on the cloud layer from mono-beam projectors located on the ground.
