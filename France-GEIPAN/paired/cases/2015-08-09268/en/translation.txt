Description: On August 6, 2015, around 10 PM, a witness briefly glanced out their roof window and saw a stationary gray triangle with orange-red lights at each end. The PAN rapidly departed towards the West on a straight trajectory. No other witnesses were found. This brief observation describes a gray triangle with a light at each corner. This type of description often results from a perceptual illusion known as contour or Gestalt illusion (see the article on the psychology of form). It is highly likely that what the witness saw was simply a cluster of bright points. Similarly, the "phenomenal speed" at which the phenomenon departed may only correspond to the lights going out. The origins could be diverse: Thai lanterns, planes, satellites. The observation is too brief, and the time frame too wide, to conduct a reasonable investigation. The Meteociel report indicates a light north wind in Reims. Without precise information and cross-referencing, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C".
