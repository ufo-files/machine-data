--- seg-c3647875154495753d27836f [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reports observing, with his wife, a bright, orange light silently crossing the sky on July 14, 2011 at 23:49. The object moved at a constant speed along a straight trajectory. A video taken from a mobile phone captures the last 43 seconds of the object's visibility. (The witness uploaded the video online. The description matches the characteristics of a Thai lantern: size, light, movement, date, and time. However, the origin of the release was not investigated, and the case is classified as B. A gendarmerie report received on November 4, 2011 suggests the phenomenon could be a flying wing with its brightness explained by diodes connected to a battery.
