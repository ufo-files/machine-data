Description: On February 21, 1984, around 7:15 a.m., a witness in a car observed three red glowing points in the sky. After exiting his vehicle, he observed for one minute a triangle formed by three red lights: a green light was shining at the center of this triangle. The stationary phenomenon was approximately 200 meters in altitude. It disappeared as it slowly moved away. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon.
