Summary: Observation of a silver object descending slowly and departing vertically.
--- seg-a0f4cee6265af8e8f6b698df [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 14, 1979, around 1:00 PM, several people observed a bright object in a clear, windless sky. The object was oval-shaped and silver in color; it moved slowly, oscillating on a north-south trajectory and gradually losing altitude. As it appeared to be falling behind a hill, one of the witnesses went to look for it and saw the object quickly ascend and disappear. No noise was heard by the witnesses. These people likely saw a balloon, although this hypothesis is not formally proven.
