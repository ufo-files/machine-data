Summary: Observation of the passage of a Thai lantern.
--- seg-52b5260b78732e364c258b18 [needs-review] ---
Description: On Saturday, July 2, 2011, around 10:30 PM, two individuals observed the silent passage of an orange spherical object flying in a descending trajectory across the sky. Flames were seen at the base of the object. The sphere quickly disappeared as it ascended into the sky. The witness's description matches that of a Thai lantern, and its movement is consistent with the wind conditions that day (north wind). It is possible to assume that this lantern was released at the end of the medieval festival that took place in Bergues that day, but the GEIPAN was unable to contact the festival organizer to confirm this hypothesis. This case is classified as B: probable Thai lantern. A new witness contacted the GEIPAN in December 2012 to confirm it was a lantern (see letter and photo IMG 5081), allowing this case to be reclassified as "A".
