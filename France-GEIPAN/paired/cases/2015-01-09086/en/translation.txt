Description: On January 31, 2015, around 5:45 PM, two children in two different rooms of their house, observed through the window, the same unknown phenomenon: a gray, oval object with colored lights moving at low altitude and in close proximity to the house. Each reports their observation at intervals of a few seconds to their mother. A questionnaire is filled out for both witnesses by their father. Then, each child independently describes their observation through a drawing. No other testimony is collected. An on-site investigation is conducted (see the investigation notes document). As of now, the observation appears to correspond to a drone seen in profile (oval and flat shape), equipped with multicolored LEDs, with a probable misjudgment by witnesses regarding the distance and size of the object. The testimonies are very brief but considered reliable, and relatively independent as, though siblings, the witnesses were in separate rooms during the observation. The observation is quite strange. Following the testimony of the drone pilot (named H1) encountered during the neighborhood investigation on 04/25/2015, it is proposed to classify the case PAN B as very probable observation of an LED-lit drone with the witnesses misestimating the distance and dimensions of the object. If the owner had been able to confirm the theft of his drone on January 31, 2015, this case would have been classified as A.
