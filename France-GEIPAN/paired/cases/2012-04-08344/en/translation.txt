Summary: Observation of the slow and silent movement of a hundred pink glowing points in the night sky: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-7bfeb8aafd14d20d14c6b266 [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 7, 2012, around 11:30 PM, two individuals observed the silent movement of a hundred pink glowing points in the cloudy sky. Two successive groups, as well as other points following in a line, were distinguished at low altitude. All points appeared from the NNE and turned full East. The observation lasted 30 minutes. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of a sequence of white and pink balls, on a weekend night, strongly suggests a release of lanterns, probably from a private party. The ordered movement, including a change in trajectory, is common with lanterns, as it results from the common tracking of the same wind current, not necessarily uniform close to the ground. White and pink lanterns are very popular for wedding celebrations. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable Thai lanterns.
