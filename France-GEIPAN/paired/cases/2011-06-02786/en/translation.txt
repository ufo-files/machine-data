Description: While driving, a person and their spouse repeatedly observe a flying object in the sky that moves. The flashing lights and low altitude of the object intrigue them. No particular noise is heard during the observation. The observation described by the witness is consistent with the appearance of a helicopter in flight at night. New helicopters are infinitely less noisy than conventional helicopters. The Air Section of the Gendarmerie in Amiens, equipped with these new helicopters, confirmed being in flight in that sector on that day, but they cannot provide the exact route taken that night. This case is therefore classified as "B": probable observation of a helicopter.
