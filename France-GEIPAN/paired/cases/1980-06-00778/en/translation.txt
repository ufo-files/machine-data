Summary: Observation of a silent-moving triangular object: Lack of information.
--- seg-7ea95605adba51372380446f [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 19, 1980, at 11:45 PM, a child saw his room light up and spotted through the window a triangular-shaped craft. Fixed or flashing lights illuminated the object, which moved silently. The base of the craft was lit yellow, and yellow and red lights alternated on the sides, flashing as they neared the top. A red beacon lit up brightly when the side lights reached the summit. Frightened, the child called his mother, and together they watched the craft disappear into the landscape. The sky was cloudy during the observation. No other information was gathered on this phenomenon, for which we lack details.
