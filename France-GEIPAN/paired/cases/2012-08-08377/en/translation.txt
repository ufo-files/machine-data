Description: On August 27, 2012, at 10 PM, a motorist and their passengers were intrigued by the presence in the sky of a white and green glowing phenomenon in motion. Only one report was made. This very brief report of a very fast green phenomenon has some characteristics of a meteor (meteorite fall). However, the report is too vague, despite a request for information from GEIPAN, to confirm this hypothesis. GEIPAN classifies this case "C" due to the lack of consistency in the report.
