Summary: A motorist observed a static, relatively flat, triangular object with rounded corners for a few seconds: Unexplained phenomenon.
--- seg-feb4189f684093794b85a9be [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 15, 2011, a driver observed an object she did not recognize. She described a static object, shaped like a triangle with three rounded corners, relatively flat, and green in color (between gray and green). Continuing on her way, the observation lasted for a few seconds. No other witnesses were reported on this phenomenon. The witness, contacted by phone, regularly passes through this area and has never seen anything like it again. The witness's account is relatively precise, but it is surprising that there were no other witnesses, making this a strange but unsubstantial case, as it is a single, unrecorded testimony (no photos or videos). The described object, in stationary flight, does not resemble any known object, except perhaps a kite or paraglider, which would be incongruous in this location (see investigation elements).
