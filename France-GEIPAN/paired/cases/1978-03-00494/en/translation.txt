**Description:** The GEIPAN (French UFO investigation body) continues to publish its entire archive on its public website: www.geipan.fr. Among its publications are older cases classified at the time (A, B, C, or D) that are now being re-examined to enhance the accuracy of conclusions. Thanks to new technical means (software) and investigative experience gained over the years, this re-examination sometimes leads to new observations or a change in classification. A previous observation case classified as C due to a lack of information and named BONNIERES SUR SEINE (78) 1978 is part of a recent batch of cases re-examined.

On March 8, 1978, at 6:45 a.m., a driver noticed multicolored lights through a forest from a field. Upon reaching the field, she saw a very bright, metallic, gray-blue object shaped like a large inverted bowl. Motionless and silent, it emitted flickering lights from its base. Fearing for her safety, the witness left the area after one minute but returned at noon with work colleagues. A circular imprint was observed at the location. The next day, alerted by public rumor, the gendarmerie contacted the witness and visited the site. No specific traces were found, except for those of a tractor. According to the witness, many curious people had trampled the area.

**Preliminary note regarding cases where testimonies and/or traces relate to a phenomenon explicitly described or perceived on the ground:**

- In the absence of evidence that the phenomenon on the ground could only have arrived and/or departed by air, the search for an explanation exceeds the GEIPAN's sole competence, and a GEIPAN conclusion cannot be formulated unless proof or near-proof is established. Otherwise, the GEIPAN must conclude C: Uninvestigable as it exceeds its sole competence.
- Conversely, the absence of testimonies or traces related to phases or the phenomenon being above ground can lead the GEIPAN to be unable to conclude due to a lack of reliable information (classic C classification).

We are in the first case here, as nothing rules out the possibility that the phenomenon arrived or departed by the ground. The examination of traces in the field proves nothing in either direction.

Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies C: Uninvestigable as it exceeds the GEIPAN's sole competence.

Within the limits of its competence and experience, the GEIPAN's analysis is as follows:

- The visual description is moderately strange.
- The visual description is consistent with the presence of a vehicle with lights in a field near the road before dawn, the night observation, and the improbable nature of a vehicle at that time, which may have led to a misperception.
- The strangeness experienced in real time by the witness seems limited because, while he did stop (for one minute), he did not wait for the phenomenon's evolution. He did return to the site, perhaps also driven by the reaction of his colleagues to his soucoupe (flying saucer) hypothesis.

On the other hand, the presence of a vehicle at that time is surprising and constitutes the main element of strangeness. It could be linked to illegal activity, of course, not verifiable, such as poaching.

The observation is characterized above all by low consistency in both the information and reliability:

- The main testimony and those from secondary colleagues were not spontaneous but requested by the gendarmerie.
- The phenomenon's evolution or disappearance was not observed.
- Round or suspicious traces were not observed by the gendarmerie.
- The strangeness for the witness may have increased due to the perception of unusual traces, collective excitement (colleagues, journalists), and impacting some aspects of his subsequent deposition.
- The non-spontaneous testimony from colleagues, regarding minimal and nearly identical descriptions of traces, opens a possibility (non-verifiable) of prior coordination or influence.
