Description: On August 13, 2013, a passenger on a Nantes-Rome flight observed, on the left side of the plane at a distance of several kilometers, a vertical black shape surpassing the top of the clouds. The witness describes a black, matte airplane tail fin shape. The object appeared stationary before transitioning to a horizontal position and taking on the form of a "very thin, black saucer at its height." The UFO disappeared after approximately one minute of observation, but the witness managed to take a photo. The object was described as having a very large size ("maybe 3 times the size of a commercial jet or more"). The neighboring passenger also made the observation but does not wish to testify. No verbal exchanges were made with other passengers during the flight. Only one testimony was collected. The apparent oddity of the case lies primarily in the estimated size of the phenomenon by the witness (see the investigation report). However, the estimation of size, distance, and speed of an unknown object is strictly impossible, even by an experienced observer. The oddity of the case remains strong. The characterization of the observation relies mainly on the testimony, as the photos taken were not close enough to clearly show the UFO. This observation case is atypical and does not resemble the usual misperceptions that UFO reports often involve.

- The UFO is not a cloud: compact black mass revealing first an angular silhouette then elliptical shapes after slow rotation on its longitudinal axis;
- The UFO is not an airplane or drone: estimated size far too large given the observation distance; no wings;
- The UFO is not a balloon or airship: stationary at the same air corridors as commercial jets (altitudes between 10,000 and 12,000 meters);
- The UFO is not an asteroid or debris re-entering the atmosphere: it remained at the same altitude throughout the observation;
- The UFO cannot be confused with a star: perfectly clear conditions, "pure blue" sky, and an azimut of 0° relative to the observer's line of sight, precluding any confusion;
- The UFO does not seem to be a shadow of the plane on the clouds, as the shadow would be visible on closer clouds.

The consistency of the case rests solely on a single human testimony, but an investigator's interview with the witness confirms its reliability. The photos reveal no decisive details in favor of any flying object. The consistency remains, therefore, quite average. Given the strong oddity of the reported phenomenon, the consistency of available data does not allow the UFO_PROTECTED_001 to provide a consolidated opinion on the origin of the observation. The UFO_PROTECTED_001 classifies this case as Category C: Lack of reliable information.
