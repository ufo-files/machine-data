Summary: Observation of a small hot air balloon on fire.
--- seg-960234dac49eaff9fa2cadb6 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 19, 1980, around 9:30 PM, a group of young people observed a bright mass moving slowly and swaying in the sky. This light object had a rounded, luminous top. It was at the altitude of tourist planes and moved in a north-south direction. Suddenly, the object burst into flames and plummeted abruptly, leading witnesses to believe it crashed nearby, though they could not pinpoint the location. The gendarmerie found a portion of the object stuck to a tree. The debris confirmed that the object was a paper hot air balloon with wire, constructed by an amateur. The owner of the object was never found.
