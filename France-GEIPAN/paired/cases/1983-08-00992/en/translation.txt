Summary: Observation of a yellow-orange light ball descending upon a witness; physical effects on very shocked witness.
--- seg-809b7d860d696a68a7a25db9 [needs-review] ---
Description: Following several night-time observations of luminous phenomena on July 15 and 16, 1983, a witness decides to go on July 17 around 9:30 PM to observe with binoculars what is happening on a hill. Several hours later, his loved ones find him at home, dazed, with injuries on his body and severely traumatized. He claims to have been pulled into a yellow-orange fireball and then fallen a few kilometers away. His incoherent speech and injuries lead his family to have him hospitalized. The investigation will demonstrate that these luminous phenomena could have actually occurred: indeed, a high-tension line of EDF passes through this hill, where electrical arcs could have occurred. The witness could have been the victim of an electric shock due to the presence of the HT line (60 KV) and the very stormy weather, but the explanation of "abduction" seems unlikely.
