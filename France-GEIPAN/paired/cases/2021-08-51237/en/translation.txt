Description: On Friday, August 20, 2021, around 5:00 PM, from his home in Cognac, the witness observed a commercial airliner heading north when something suddenly crossed its path, taking a direct south course, almost in the opposite direction of the airliner. The object had a roughly rectangular shape, light gray in color ("similar to an airplane where the clouds below it make it appear almost white"). Suddenly, in the space of a second, the witness saw the object slow down rapidly until it came to a complete stop, remaining stationary for a fraction of a second (without any turning maneuver, as if it were in reverse). It then resumed movement at a 90-degree angle to the west, reaching its cruising speed almost instantaneously. Only one testimony was collected. The remote investigation data collected do not allow for any hypotheses to be made regarding this observation. The phenomenon observed, very strange, resembles nothing known. Notably, the movement of the PAN, including a stop, a brief retreat, and a 90-degree turn, constitutes the primary strangeness for the witness. The appearance of the PAN, with its roughly rectangular shape and light gray color, without lights or other identifiable structures, are also significant oddities. To better characterize the PAN regarding its trajectory and speed, we contacted the witness, who did not respond. An on-site investigation may have helped clarify uncertainties or provide new elements. Unfortunately, it could not be carried out as the witness refused all requests regarding this case. Under these conditions, the missing data seem too significant for the assessment and characterization of the case, even if the strangeness remains high a priori. Consequently, the case is classified as 'C': the significant lack of consolidated data does not allow for a conclusion at this time.
