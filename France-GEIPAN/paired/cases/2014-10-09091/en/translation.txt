Description: On October 13, 2014, around 6:10 a.m., a driver and their passenger were surprised to see a bright point in the sky moving in a comma-shaped trajectory. No trail, no sound was noticed. The phenomenon suddenly disappeared. Only one testimony was collected. Based on the elements defined in the investigation report, in particular the absence of description by the Gendarmerie services of the object observed above the Nogent power plant, and pending information in this regard, the lack of information does not allow for a decision in favor of any hypothesis. This case should be classified as "C" as an observation of an unidentified phenomenon at this stage, the significant lack of elements not allowing to establish a solid opinion.
