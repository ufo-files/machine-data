--- seg-a817533dce27515f52af3425 [needs-review] ---
Description: Two witnesses report their lengthy observations of a very bright phenomenon in the sky. On February 20 and 21, 2012, around 8:20 PM, a witness in Aveyron observed the slow movement of a particularly bright point in the western direction. The witness used a camera and a video camera to see and film the phenomenon. Another witness located in Tarn observed "a kind of very, very bright star." The bright point observed by the witnesses corresponds to the planet Venus (see sky map), particularly visible at this time and very sparkling in the sunset. This case is classified as "A."
