Description: On January 1, 2016 at 12:16 a.m., a witness observed silent movements in the sky of flame-colored light phenomena. The objects moved towards the NNE on a horizontal trajectory at low altitude. The observation lasted 5 minutes. No other reports of sightings in the area were made. The observation of orange balls or discs crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend or holiday night like New Year's Eve, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement in groups of 4 or 5 aligned horizontally is probably the result of close, sequential launches separated by a longer time gap. The movement was observed towards the Northeast, while the wind in the area (8 km) was blowing from the Northwest. However, from the observer's location, they could not accurately assess the South to North component of the movement. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable Thai lanterns.
