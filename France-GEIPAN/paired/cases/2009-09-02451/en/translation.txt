Summary: Observation of a material form at the end of a cloud: lack of information.
--- seg-1b326ecaa5e71e2cbceab385 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 16, 2009, at 4:26 a.m., a witness observed in the sky a phenomenon he could not explain. He reported to the gendarmerie that he had observed a material shape at the end of a cloud. The object was round with three or four lit windows. Below this object, he also noticed the presence of a bar itself surmounted by an oval light that moved laterally from left to right. The phenomenon was stationary, at low altitude, and was visible for more than five minutes. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon, and no additional information was provided during the gendarmerie investigation. Despite the request of GEIPAN, the witness did not provide any further information, which does not allow for any precise investigation. The case is classified C due to lack of information.
