Description: On Monday, January 12, 2015, at 7 a.m., a witness is intrigued by a diffuse light he sees through a glass door at his home. Stepping onto his terrace, he finds himself a few meters from a PAN silently approaching him. A non-blinding white light is mounted at the front of the latter. The PAN moves slowly between closely situated mobile homes. The witness walks around his house and finds himself face-to-face with the right side of the PAN, on which a yellow and red "logo" is visible. The witness "loses track of time" as the PAN continues its trajectory towards the NNE, dips, then accelerates and gains altitude before disappearing above the trees. The witness then notices the oval shape of the PAN. No other testimonies will be collected. The observation is strange because it involves a phenomenon with an appearance far from ordinary at a very early hour of the night and in a very isolated location (see the investigation report). The characteristics of the PAN in terms of speed, size, shape, do not present any "out of the ordinary" aspects; from this perspective, it could be a promotional inflatable advertisement balloon. But the trajectory, at the very least fortuitous as it narrowly avoids obstacles, is surprising. It would have had to escape its owner, and what would it be doing there, far from any promotional interest? It is not possible to validate such a hypothesis. The consistency of the testimony is good, although it comes from only one witness. The uniqueness of the testimony and the absence of a photo place the level of consistency at the bare minimum to validate the strange and unexplained nature of the observation. The case was submitted to the panel of experts of GEIPAN. GEIPAN classifies this case as D1: unexplained phenomenon of average strangeness.
