Description: On May 5, 1978, between 10:45 PM and 11:15 PM, three individuals observed the passage of a craft approximately 50 meters above them. No noise was heard despite the craft's substantial size. Different-colored lights were noticed on the craft. At the time indicated by the witnesses, a gendarmerie investigation will reveal silent passes of aircraft awaiting landing clearance at Charles de Gaulle Airport. These people were likely witnesses to similar aircraft passes.
