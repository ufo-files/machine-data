Description: On February 22 and 23, 1999, around 7:15 and 7:30 PM, several witnesses observed one or two stationary bright points in the sky. These points, three or four times larger than a star, intrigued the observers. The observed phenomenon was the conjunction of the planets Venus and Jupiter, a natural event announced in the media.
