Description: The witness, having set up an automated system for recording images of the celestial vault from the terrace of his home in Lias (32) during the night of August 19-20, 2023, he discovers after reviewing the recordings the presence of two UFOs having the same appearance and the same type of sinuous and erratic trajectory. The consistency* of this case is considered high, despite the absence of direct observation. The images recorded by the instrument, featuring the UFOs, were exhaustively analyzed for the investigation, with precise temporal alignment achieved through analysis of the metadata associated with the files (see the investigation report). *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. Several hypotheses were examined as part of the investigation. The synthesis is as follows:

- The trajectories of the UFOs could suggest chiropters. However, the data related to altitude and speed, once calculated and correlated, do not align with this hypothesis at all, as the speeds are too low and the altitudes too high.

- Similarly, the hypothesis of birds, particularly two nocturnal birds (great horned owls), one of which is a juvenile learning from an adult, does not cohere with the calculated altitude data, which are too high considering the flight speed. The observation period is also too late.

- The hypothesis of two insects near the camera lens was also considered but not retained as it implies they would have been more than 3.50 meters from the camera lens, and thus flying at a speed far too low to cover the distance in about 30 minutes.

- The hypothesis of two glowing balloons equipped with LED lights, however, aligns well with most of the data from the analysis, particularly a slow, sinusoidal movement consistent with the low wind speeds at altitude, with the proven presence of a wind shear phenomenon. These elements were confirmed by the expert from Météo France to the GEIPAN. Further details:

- A pale-white color, quite common for this type of balloon,
- General movement in the overall direction of the wind, oriented southeast,
- Erratic movement caused by the presence of a wind shear phenomenon between altitudes of 750 and 875 meters,
- Calculated speeds compatible with those of the wind estimated at these altitudes by Météo France data,
- Observation on a weekend, conducive to the use of such festive, glowing balloons.

However, the analysis of the images did not allow for an estimate of the actual shape of the UFOs, given the distortion caused by the exposure time of each photograph. The GEIPAN considers that this hypothesis reduces the strangeness of this observation, but reminds that the absence of a direct visual witness would normally have led to rejecting the case, in accordance with the criteria for eligibility of cases investigated at GEIPAN. This study was conducted due to its curious nature for us and the witness, as well as the interest in the methodology employed for the investigation. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of two LED balloons carried by the wind at altitude.
