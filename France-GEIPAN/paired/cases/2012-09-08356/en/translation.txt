Description: On September 12, 2012, around 9:30 PM, a driver was intrigued by the presence in the sky and at low altitude of three lights of color (red, blue, white) that blinked and outlined a triangular shape. No particular noise was heard during the movement of the phenomenon, which changed course and disappeared towards Saussenac. The observation lasted 2 minutes. No other testimonies were collected. Of average strangeness and low consistency (single witness, short observation duration), this case appears to be a possible misperception with an aircraft (see the investigation note). However, despite a fairly typical description, the low quality of the testimony and the long delay in processing the file prevent the formal identification of a potential plane. It is not the visual perception of the witness that is in question, but the interpretation the witness makes of his observation through his feelings (fatigue, driving at night). This case is classified B, probable observation of an aircraft.
