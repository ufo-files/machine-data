Description: On February 7, 2010, at 4:33 a.m., a driver on National Road A1 in the direction of Lamentin-Fort-de-France (Martinique) was "overtaken" over a distance of 300 meters by what he calls "a star" that was not sparkling. The phenomenon was at an estimated altitude of at least 700 meters. This "light" disappeared instantly at the end of the straight line. No particular noise was heard during the 10-second observation. Among the hypotheses considered (plasma on an electric line, reflection, meteor, visual fatigue), none could be confirmed or clearly ruled out. Due to this uncertainty, and given the low strangeness of this case (it is ultimately just a distant glowing point in a straight line), the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C": Lack of elements to conclude. It is possible that new elements will confirm or refute either of these hypotheses.
