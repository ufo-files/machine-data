Summary: Astronomical observations of Venus.
--- seg-4c9d7bf603125ac8eb674817 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 1, 1976, several witnesses observed a stationary, glowing object above the horizon. The observation lasted 15 minutes before the object disappeared. No unusual noises were heard. The gendarmerie quickly arrived on the scene but made no observations and found no particular traces on the ground that evening. The gendarmerie investigation revealed that several nights in a row, a luminous phenomenon was observed disappearing behind the horizon in the direction indicated by all the witnesses. All these people had been making astronomical observations of Venus.
