Summary: Observation of the silent movement in the sky of about twenty glowing balls: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-9ae9cc05040feb5e5939bcc1 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 1, 2015, at 4:25 AM, witnesses observed the presence in the sky of around twenty glowing balls of color moving silently through the air. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a festive night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party, as frequently happens on New Year's Eve. The movement was observed from North to South, which is consistent with the North wind reported by Météociel in Orange. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," a very probable observation of Thai lanterns. The videos taken by the witness leave no doubt about this hypothesis. The first reported observation could also correspond to a festive object carried by the wind, but of a less common model than the classic Thai lanterns.
