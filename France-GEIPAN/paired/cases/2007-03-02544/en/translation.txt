Description: Recent concise testimony (March 28, 2010) of an old observation (the date provided is arbitrary). In spring 2007 (early May according to the witness who does not provide a precise date) two individuals were frightened by a quick glimpse of a large mass in the sky. It was 11:30 PM and dense fog covered the pavilion area. On returning to their car in the street, two witnesses observed twice at a distance and altitude of approximately 500 meters, a huge metallic shape stationary and surrounded by a halo of light. No noise was heard during the very brief observations. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. Any investigation is impossible due to the lack of sufficient information.
