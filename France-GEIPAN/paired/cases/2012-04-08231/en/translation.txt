Description: On April 12, 2012, around 9:40 PM, a witness's attention was drawn to a very bright red glow suddenly appearing behind the windows of their first-floor window. The observation lasted several minutes before the light changed color and intensity, dimming and disappearing. No other testimonies were collected. Of low to moderate strangeness and consistent at the same level, this observation case remains difficult to conclude: a seemingly fixed light point, of vivid orange-red color and turning to white as it fades, suggests a plane seen face-on, but not with certainty in the absence of air traffic data. (See the investigation report). The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'C' in the absence of other objective elements.
