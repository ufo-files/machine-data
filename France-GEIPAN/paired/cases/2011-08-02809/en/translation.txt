Description: On August 19, 2011, during an evening walk (between 10:45 PM and 11:00 PM), a witness was intrigued by a "very large star": at first stationary, it quickly rose to the zenith before disappearing. No particular noise was heard during the observation, which lasted approximately 10 seconds. A very bright point in the night sky, early in the morning, that suddenly disappears, suggests a satellite entering the Earth's shadow. The ISS station was visible that evening. The lack of precision in the testimony does not allow for precisely verifying that it was indeed the ISS that was observed. The perception of the disappearance at the zenith is likely an optical illusion, frequent in the sudden disappearances of bright points. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C": lack of information for a precise investigation.
