Description: On November 9, 2009, a witness observed at a distance of approximately 800 meters and for less than a minute a glowing phenomenon passing between two mountains. Of red color and round shape, followed by flames, this object moved in a northern direction towards Tontouta. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, which resembles an atmospheric reentry but for which we lack information.
