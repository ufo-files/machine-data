Description: On September 4, 2016 at 07:45, a witness from their 7th-floor kitchen was surprised to observe the appearances and disappearances of a very bright PAN in the overcast sky. The object remained stationary for the entire 45 seconds of observation. Only one testimony was collected. Due to its oddity and low consistency (single witness, brief observation), the case appears to be a probable misidentification with the Sun (see the investigation report). Although very dense cloud cover was present at the time of observation, satellite images show gaps in the direction of observation, consistent with the appearances and gradual disappearances of the PAN. Additionally, the direction of observation and the description of the PAN closely match those of the Sun. Uncertainty regarding the height of the PAN prevents a definitive conclusion of misidentification. The case is classified B, probable misidentification with the Sun.
