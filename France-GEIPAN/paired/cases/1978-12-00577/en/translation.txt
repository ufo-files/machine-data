Description: On December 10, 1978, starting at 6:40 a.m., several witnesses, including gendarmes, observed a peculiar luminous phenomenon in the western sky. This phenomenon appeared to be moving in a pendular and ascending manner. It gradually diminished and disappeared around 9:30 a.m. These witnesses reported observing the planet Venus.
