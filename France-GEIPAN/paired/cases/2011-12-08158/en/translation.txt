--- seg-d6f6347246050695fef6e40e [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 10, 2011, at 4:10 PM, a driver on the A16 highway heading from Paris to Froissy noticed "numerous very powerful lights above the horizon" in the stormy sky. All of these lights seemed to originate from a triangular shape that quickly ascended into the black clouds, then reappeared and disappeared rapidly towards the North. According to the witness, several other vehicles slowed down during the observation. However, no other testimonies about the GEIPAN have been received. With average strangeness and low consistency (one witness, uncertain observation location and viewing directions), the considered hypotheses (reentry of space debris, Thai lanterns) are not sufficiently coherent with the observation report to be retained. As there are no other potential testimonies, this case is classified as C (lack of information).
