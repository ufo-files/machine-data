Summary: Observations from a vehicle and at several locations of a PAN consisting of two very close white bright points in slow drift at very low altitude in the sky: observation of position lights of an A400M aircraft in exercise.
--- seg-3dc9fbc78bccf6a2d0b8488e [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 26, 2023, around 8:00 PM, the witness (T1) observed, along with 3 other family members, a PAN consisting of 2 very close white bright points, evolving very slowly in the sky while driving between Rebigue (31) and Pechabou (31). The observation lasts 5 minutes, including a 3-minute interruption during which the PAN was obscured by the environment. The case is judged to have average consistency. Although four people were present during the observation, only one witness formalised their account with the GEIPAN without providing any photos or videos. *According to the GEIPAN's criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. Two hypotheses are investigated (see the investigation report):

- The observation of the conjunction between the planets Venus and Jupiter, which were close together at the time of the observation, setting below the western horizon at an angle of a little over 3°.
- The observation of an A400M in training, operating in stealth mode, with only a few position lights on. The hypothesis of the Venus/Jupiter conjunction is ruled out based on the following factual elements:

- The horizontal configuration of the two bright points contradicts the 65° angle that the planetary conjunction presented relative to the horizontal at this exact moment.
- The continuous and sustained movement of PAN, persisting even during the witnesses' stop phases, is incompatible with the kinematics of celestial bodies.
- The succession of reported azimuths (East/North, then South, East-South-East, and finally Northeast) is entirely at odds with the fixed position of the conjunction, located in the West quadrant. The hypothesis of the passage of an A400M aircraft closely matches the characteristics reported of the PAN: - Analysis of data from specialized platforms (ADSB-Exchange and ADS-B.NL) confirms the presence of an A400M in the area, flying at low altitude as part of a large-scale military exercise. The transient flight configuration - transponder off and reduced lighting signaling ("stealth mode") - explains the atypical perception of the craft. - Comparing the azimuths and elevations shows a high correlation with the initial phase of the observation as well as with the stop phase of the witnesses. The correlation is more complex to establish on the final phase due to the absence of radar data (transponder off) and the simultaneous presence of another aircraft approaching Toulouse-Blagnac Airport, which could have caused confusion. The absence of noise reported by witnesses can be correlated with the great distance of the craft, the direction of the wind (north sector, non-carrying), and the "stealth" flight configuration adopted during this exercise. At the conclusion of this investigation, the GEIPAN classifies the case as "A": observation of an aircraft type A400M.
