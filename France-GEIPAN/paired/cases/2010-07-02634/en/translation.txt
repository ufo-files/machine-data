Description: On July 30, 2010, around 10:10 PM, a driver observed for a few seconds the passage, lower than airplanes, of a triangular object with two domes on top, creating a silver and bright trail in the sky. No particular noise was heard during the observation. The object quickly disappeared from the witness's field of vision. No other testimonies were collected. The gendarmerie report does not provide any particular information, and the witness did not respond to requests for additional information that were addressed and repeated (on September 9, 2010 (email), November 17, 2010 (phone), and December 16, 2010 (phone)). The case is classified C due to lack of information.
