Description: On March 17, 2010, at approximately 8:40 PM, a driver observed an intriguing orange-colored light phenomenon just above the horizon. He stopped and took photographs towards the West just before the phenomenon, then in the shape of a crescent moon, disappeared. The ephemeris for March 17, 2010 (and March 16) shows at 6:40 PM UTC, which is 8:40 PM local time, the last phase of the new moon just above the horizon, still illuminated by the sun, and visible as a faint orange crescent (as photographed and described by the witness).
