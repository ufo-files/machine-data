Description: On May 13, 2011, around 11:30 PM, a witness observed from the courtyard of his home a bright white point in the sky that intrigued him. During the observation, which lasted less than three minutes, the witness noticed the object's movement from north to northwest without hearing any noise. No other testimonies were received about this phenomenon. The witness saw a very bright point, apparently stationary for a few seconds, which then moved and dimmed for a few seconds. A possible cause could be an airplane that, heading towards the witness, appears stationary; its front lights are then pointed in the witness's direction, who perceives a maximum glow. The airplane might have then changed course, the light beam moving away from the witness's direction, becoming fainter; from the witness's perspective, the point moves laterally until it disappears when the airplane's axis is too far from the witness-airplane direction. Given the relative lack of strangeness in the phenomenon, the GEIPAN did not conduct research to identify the airplane. As a result, this case remains classified as "C," lacking information.
