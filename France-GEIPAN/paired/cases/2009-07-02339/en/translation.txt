--- seg-c1511bd84ac9afcf3e937f9f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Saturday, July 4, 2009, around 11:40 PM, from his home, a witness observed the silent passage of a bright disk in the sky for 2 to 3 seconds. The movement was straight-line and at a very high speed along a trajectory from Bordeaux to Perpignan. No other witnesses were reported for this phenomenon. This case of observation of a very bright disk crossing the sky in a few seconds suggests an atmospheric reentry of a meteorite. However, the witness does not note a green or yellow streak, as expected in such cases. It could be assumed that the witness's night-time visual acuity is not very good due to his age. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid (meteorite).
