Description: On March 1, 2012, the GEIPAN received by email a terrestrial questionnaire (QT) from a witness of a multi-light observation on the evening of Thursday, August 25, 2011, just over 5 months after the event. These various lights would eventually form a single fixed light point before disappearing. Of average strangeness and good consistency, this observation case leaves two simultaneously possible but unlikely hypotheses: model aircraft and Thai lanterns. (See investigation report). An on-site investigation could potentially uncover additional witnesses to the phenomenon. This case is provisionally classified as C, awaiting additional simultaneous testimonies.
