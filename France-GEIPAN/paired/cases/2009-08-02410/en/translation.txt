Description: A witness reports an observation made at night on August 30th or 31st, 2009 from the terrace of their home. A pair of flickering and static lights appear above the sea. Through binoculars, the witness notices that these lights are connected by "a flat and elongated base like a line." After twenty to thirty minutes of observation, the object eventually moves away to the East. No other testimonies are collected. On an old photograph provided to us, the witness sketches their observation. The given time (approximately 2:30) will not be validated by the investigator (see investigation report). The hypotheses (starlight scintillation, stars, and even planets as possible boat lights or lights on Pianosa Island) are unlikely. This observation case is actually not strange, but the weak consistency of the testimony and the impossibility of validating the sea fire hypothesis do not allow for a clear conclusion. This case is classified C due to the noted inconsistencies.
