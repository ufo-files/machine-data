Description: On September 13, 1985, around 3:00 PM, several witnesses, including gendarmes, will catch sight of a bright white mass for a few minutes as it moves silently along the marked path leading to the volcano. This object, shaped like a boomerang, exhibits oscillatory movements: it will suddenly gain altitude and assume a spherical form before disappearing without leaving any trace. Following a press article on the subject, a person will come forward to testify to the same observation made on August 6, 1985, a few kilometers away. No additional information has confirmed any of the local gendarmerie's hypotheses.
