Summary: Observation of the slow movement of a luminous phenomenon.
--- seg-59492f27ad864d01e69ae947 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: In the evening of January 27, 1977, starting at 8:30 PM, two motorists observed the slow movement of a luminous phenomenon. A ball-shaped object, about the size of a soccer ball, emitted a bright white light and moved silently while oscillating on its axis. The primary witness was unable to provide any distance or speed of movement, as he observed the movement of a second light point. The witness left the scene at 9:45 PM after the phenomenon's complete disappearance. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon, for which we lack information.
