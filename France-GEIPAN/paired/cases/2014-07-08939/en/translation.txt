Description: This high-quality testimony describes the observation at 10:59 PM of a group of white lights (approximately fifteen) silently crossing a portion of the sky in one minute. The witness describes the gradual disappearance of the phenomenon; this testimony suggests the observation of luminous balloons or Thai lanterns carried by the wind, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement is observed from East to West (see the witness' diagram). The wind in Colmar at 11:00 PM is light, coming from the North (Northeast a little earlier). The weather is roughly compatible with this movement. The variability of the wind at low altitude and the witness' inability to judge the distance or proximity of the lights over such a short period can explain this deviation in direction. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns or luminous balloons carried by the wind.
