Summary: Observation of a bright and silent PAN in the night sky: observation of a flying lantern.
--- seg-5d509db798d37195ec0ed26f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 13, 2020, around 11:30 PM, two individuals in a parking lot were intrigued by a yellow or orange-red light phenomenon in the night sky heading northeast. No noise was heard from the PAN, which moved barely at all, and which was described as having a shape resembling an "upside-down Y." The PAN quickly disappeared. One of the witnesses was able to take photographs with his smartphone. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected. After analysis (see the investigation report), some oddness remains, particularly related to the witnesses' description of the PAN. Consistency is average: two witnesses, photographs of the PAN, but a contradiction between the testimonies, as the shape described does not match the photographs. The visual aspect of the PAN in the photos resembles a flying lantern, especially due to its orange color. The observation took place on a Saturday night around 11:30 PM, a time and day conducive to lantern releases. The apparent immobility of the PAN is consistent with the wind being zero or very low. Similarly, the perception of the PAN's disappearance reported by both witnesses is very similar to that of a lantern: "receding view by T1," "size reduction," and "extinction" by T2 are explained by the gradual decrease in light intensity and then the lantern's extinction. The case is classified as A, observation of a flying lantern.
