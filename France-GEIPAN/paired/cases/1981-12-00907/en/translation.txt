Description: On December 23rd and 24th, 1981, between 6:30 PM and 7:00 PM, several individuals witnessed a peculiar luminescent phenomenon in the cloudy sky. This circular phenomenon repeatedly changed color, shifting from white to orange. Initially stationary, it seemed to move, explaining observations of it growing or shrinking in size. Pilots and air traffic controllers also noticed this glow on December 24th. Despite a thorough investigation by the gendarmerie with various services and verification using astronomy software, we lack information to confirm that the witnesses observed Venus, which was particularly visible on that evening.
