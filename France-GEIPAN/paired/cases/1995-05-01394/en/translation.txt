--- seg-f80d5254c785519954583094 [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 29, 1995, around 1 a.m., a couple in a car observed an oval-shaped glowing object in the sky. Beams emerged from this form, and the witnesses felt they were being followed. Despite the witnesses' denials, the description of the observed phenomenon strongly suggests a disco light, with gendarmes in this brigade receiving numerous reports of similar observations. The effects produced by such devices indeed vary depending on weather conditions and the distance at which they are observed.
