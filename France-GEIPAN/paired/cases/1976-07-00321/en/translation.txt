Summary: Observation of a stationary green glowing phenomenon and disappearance towards the N-E.
--- seg-4a622531c89fee938cdb6e73 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 19, 1976, around 3 a.m., a motorist observed a stationary object of intense green light and bright color for a few seconds. The observation distance was approximately between 300 and 400 meters. The shape of the object made the witness think of a melon hat. No particular noise was heard by the witness when a reddish color appeared at the back of the object and it quickly disappeared towards the northeast. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon for which we lack information.
