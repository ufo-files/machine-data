Description: On November 7, 2016, at 12:35 AM, a witness observed from their room a very bright, colored phenomenon ("round base with a yellow flame topped by a blue, red, and green triangle, somewhat translucent") passing through the sky. The movement, with a slight ascent, followed a trajectory of small zigzags towards the East. Only one testimony was collected. The witness likely observed a passage of illuminated balloons with LED lights, inflated with helium. The movement is consistent with the general wind direction from West to East (see the Infoclimat website). The slight zigzag movement around a main axis is also consistent with an object being carried by the wind. LED balloons can be assembled into clusters that can form various shapes and color combinations, like what was observed here (round shape with a triangle). See, for example, reusable LED balloons that last a lifetime. The flame-like appearance may result from the flickering of some balloons in the cluster, while others may have fixed lighting. The partial and progressive dimming of the PAN results from the end of life of the LEDs. Additionally, it was a weekend night conducive to such a festive event. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable LED balloon.
