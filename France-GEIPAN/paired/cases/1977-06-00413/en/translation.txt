Description: On June 25, 1977, at 2:05 a.m., gendarmes were alerted to the presence of abnormal luminous phenomena in the sky. Witnesses reported the movements of two beams of light between 1:05 a.m. and 2:05 a.m. These beams moved across the sky, illuminating the area as they rotated. The light appeared every 15 to 20 seconds. When the gendarmes arrived at the scene at 2:30 a.m., they found no phenomena.
