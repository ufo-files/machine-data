Description: On Sunday, June 3, 2018, at 10:45 PM, a witness was surprised to see in the overcast sky the silent movement of a round, red-orange light. The witness used binoculars and observed that three red-orange orbs were aligned horizontally moving across the overcast sky. The witness noticed a kind of blurry halo above the 3 orbs that eventually moved away towards the North, still silently and at the same speed. Another present witness also observed the PAN but did not make the observation with binoculars and will not testify. Concluding the investigation, almost all the physical and dynamic parameters of the PAN suggest an observation of a group of lanterns carried by the wind (see the investigation report). One important point remains: the observation duration of 30 minutes is hard to reconcile with the hypothesis of classic lanterns, for which 20 minutes seems to be the maximum autonomy duration. This leads us to formulate a second hypothesis of festive light balloons with integrated LEDs. These balloons could all have been orange. A deeper examination of the witness' observation, through email exchanges with the investigator, did not shed light on this incompatibility element. On the contrary, it introduces another strange element that turns out to be quite incompatible with our hypothesis: regular rotation relative to the horizon during movement of -10/+10°, while maintaining the alignment of the three PAN. The PAN subject of this investigation is very similar to that of the MOTTE-SERVOLEX case study (LA) (73) 02.06.2018. The reported observation in LA MOTTE-SERVOLEX occurred the previous day, at roughly the same times and at a very close distance (PAN estimated at 2km from the 2 testimonies). Both testimonies report a very long observation duration. This is the most critical point for our hypothesis of classic flying lanterns. The second hypothesis of festive light balloons with integrated LEDs is more consistent. Their autonomy is several hours. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": probable observation of a group of three flying lanterns or festive light balloons with integrated LEDs.
