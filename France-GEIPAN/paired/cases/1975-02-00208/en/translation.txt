Description: On February 17, 1975, between 7:20 and 7:50 PM, several witnesses in different French departments observed in the sky the unusual presence of one or two very bright points. No noise was heard during the prolonged observations made by the witnesses. These witnesses were making an astronomical observation of the conjunction of the planets Venus and Jupiter, which was particularly visible during this time of year.
