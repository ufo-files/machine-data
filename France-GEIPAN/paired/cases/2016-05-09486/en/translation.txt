Summary: Multiple observations of silent, yellow-orange light balls passing through the night sky: likely Thai lanterns.
--- seg-91f2a1587fd88d3fa1c3e8dd [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 15, 2016, between 10:30 PM and 11:00 PM, several people in a garden observed the slow passage of several glowing yellow-orange balls or spheres. All the orbs are moving in the same direction (East-West) with a regular gap. No noise was heard throughout the observation. A video and photos are taken. Witnesses report their observations to the gendarmerie. The GEIPAN does not have the photos and video. The statements of the 4 witnesses are consistent and are very characteristic of an observation of a release of Thai lanterns, both in terms of the colors, shapes, type of trajectory, and including the mode of disappearance with the extinguishing of lanterns that can create an impression of disappearance by distance "flash" or sudden. The occurrence one night during the weekend, favorable for festive events, strengthens this hypothesis. We do not have the measurement of the local wind; the one measured at 20 Km is weak (5 Km/h) and of fluctuating orientation. The movement of the lanterns can be of the order of 100m/minutes, they actually fly much lower (at 100 or 200m) than witnesses can imagine, who cannot accurately assess the distance to an unrecognized phenomenon. Thus in 5 or 10 minutes, the movement in the sky can be very significant, as has been reported, even with a weak wind. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: Thai lanterns.
