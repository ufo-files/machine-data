Summary: Possible observations of an atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-f31bf2deb74de27209eff61a [needs-review] ---
Description: On the evening of August 1, 1996, numerous witnesses observed the very quick passage of a very bright white ball with a yellow and green trail. The luminous phenomenon lasted approximately thirty seconds. It was described in the same terms from Cantal to Alsace. The indicated hours differ slightly, but the phenomenon appears to have occurred between 9:30 PM and 9:45 PM with a movement towards the northwest. This large-scale phenomenon, seen by numerous witnesses in eastern France, is probably an atmospheric reentry.
