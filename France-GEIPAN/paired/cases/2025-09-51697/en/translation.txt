Description: On September 15, 2025, in the evening, the witness, from their home in the commune of Betton (35), observed a luminous phenomenon. The object presents two white lights with a cold tone, arranged parallel to the ground, blinking intermittently with an alternation between left and right. The light intensity varies from low to high, without complete extinction of the sources. The trajectory, initially oriented east to west, includes rapid turns to the north—a phase during which only one light remains visible—then to the south, before disappearing behind vegetation. The estimated altitude suggests a low-altitude flight, with no audible signature of a motor or thermal reactor detected. Of low strangeness and average consistency (2 witnesses, but a single testimony, no photo of PAN), this case turns out to be an observation of a TBM-700 by the Ministry of Defense in flight (see the investigation report). *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The description of the PAN is consistent with position lights of an aircraft. The CAPCODA data confirms, at the time and direction indicated, the presence of a TBM-700 from the Ministry of Defense. The device was following a holding trajectory ("hippodrome") south of Rennes, with flight parameters consistent with the observation. *Air Center for Planning and Conducting Operations and Air Defense (Air and Space Army). The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A": observation of an aircraft.
