Description: On November 18, 1983, between 6:00 and 7:00 p.m., several witnesses in Moselle observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky for a few seconds. A ball, about the size of a soccer ball and orange in color, crossed the sky without sound but leaving behind a long, colored trail. It is likely an atmospheric reentry.
