Summary: Observation and photography of three stationary black dots then forming a conical shape: extremely probable observation of a group of three isolated migratory birds.
--- seg-7862e5ce0222ed76b9318daf [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 5, 2016, at sunrise, a witness observed 3 stationary black points that drew closer to form a conical shape. After the disappearance of the last one, another elongated shape of white color appears in the sky and remains visible and immobile until 11:30. The witness takes photographs. No other testimony was collected about this phenomenon. According to the investigation report (see attached document), the hypothesis is that it was a confusion with a group of three migratory birds traveling towards the southeast, poorly perceived by the witness due to the poor quality of the optics used and the distance of the birds, which did not allow for a clear distinction of their shape. The testimony is weakly consistent, with little data provided by the witness and exploitable, the questionnaire was not completed, and the Gendarmerie protocol is very succinct. However, there are 15 photographs, but only a few of them have been provided in their original form and thus could be used for the case analysis. The analysis of the photos (in particular the multi-temporal analysis with the IPACO software) allows the hypothesis to be consolidated despite the initially poor quality of the photos. This GEIPAN case is classified as "A", indicating an extremely probable observation of a group of three migratory birds flying southeast. The poor quality of the optics, along with the distance of the birds, prevented their shape from being clearly distinguished.
