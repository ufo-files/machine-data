--- seg-30f9ca1234008d3434cbc1b5 [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 7, 2013 at 6:40 PM during an Ariane launch in French Guiana, a witness and his children saw and filmed three parallel lines clearly separating from the bottom of the horizon. The lines dissipated shortly after. Based on the investigation's findings (see attached report), namely: - the appearance of the PAN was identical to that of straight-line shadows, - other similar existing examples, - the sun's position, this case should be classified as "A" - a certain observation of shadows created by the sun striking dense areas of the Ariane rocket's condensation trail, at twilight.
