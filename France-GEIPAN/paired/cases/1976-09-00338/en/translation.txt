Description: On September 5, 1976, at approximately 7:22 a.m., an individual observed from the 15th floor of their building a luminous phenomenon in the sky that intrigued them. For about ten minutes, the witness observed in the southeast direction the presence above the Sarrouilles (65) hills of a red glowing line before it disappeared towards the east. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected. The gendarmerie investigation revealed that the observation made on September 5 was likely directly related to the passage of a weather balloon that remained airborne after releasing its payload in a field in Soues. The winds were calm on September 4 and 5, 1976, and the two communes are roughly located in the same direction, southeast of Tarbes.
