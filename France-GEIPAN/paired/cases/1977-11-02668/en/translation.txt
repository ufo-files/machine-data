Summary: Observation of a kite.
--- seg-b8b5e834b137e355d296a81e [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 10, 1977, around 7 PM, witnesses in a restaurant in Kourou observed for fifteen minutes a luminous object performing geometric figures in the sky. It disappeared while descending in a spiral. No aircraft movements or balloon or probe rocket launches were reported for that day. A witness reported to the brigade on November 11, 1977, that the previous day, at the time of the aforementioned events, he saw a kite equipped with a battery land on the parking lot of a hotel in the city. Despite the investigations conducted, the owner of the kite was not found.
