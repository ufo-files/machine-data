Description: On September 26, 2007 at 7:30 am, two individuals were intrigued by a luminous phenomenon in the cloudy sky. Heading southeast and approximately 100 meters high, an oval shape emitted a very dense light. This stationary shape slowly rose vertically starting at 9:15 am and disappeared around 11 am. On September 27, 2007, one of the witnesses again observed this shape. It will be shown that the witnesses made an astronomical observation of Venus, particularly visible during this time of year (magnitude -4.31). These astronomical references will be confirmed by the September 2007 issue of the SCIENCES ET VIE magazine.
