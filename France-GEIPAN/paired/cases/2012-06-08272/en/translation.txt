--- seg-b2abc6e91871875e8a27e34a [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 17, 2012, around 1 a.m., a couple is driving home when the passenger notices several stationary, non-flashing orange lights in the clear sky. These lights are at low altitude. Only one statement is collected. The observation of orange balls in the sky on a weekend night strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The fact that they disappeared after a few minutes is also typical of lanterns (burner operation times). The witness is surprised by the observation (as also happens with lanterns), but he does mention the presence of other observers of these phenomena, and these latter have not made statements, neither to the GEIPAN nor to the gendarmerie. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable Thai lanterns.
