Description: On October 2, 2023, at 05:17, a resident of Monès (31), while opening the door to his terrace, observed, along with his wife, a particularly bright longitudinal shape moving in the direction of Lombez (32). The consistency* of the case is assessed as average: while there were two witnesses, only one completed the witness statement questionnaire (QT) and no visual documentation (photo or video) was provided. *According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony. Analysis of the maps provided by the National Center for Space Operations (CNOA) of the Air and Space Army, as well as data from FlightRadar24, allowed us to demonstrate that the witnesses observed a commercial airliner, a Boeing 77W, flight REU975 operated by Air Austral, flying from Saint-Denis (974) to Tarbes (65) (as detailed in the investigation report). The trajectory followed by the aircraft from east to west is very close to the direction of movement of the PAN from east-southeast to west-northwest. The timing of the observation corresponds to the aircraft's passage overhead. The longitudinal shape is consistent with the appearance of an aircraft moving transversely relative to the observation point, especially considering that the Boeing 777-300 is the largest model in the B777 series. The lack of noise reported by the witness can be explained by the aircraft being in descent, thus at a reduced speed, and the wind from the east-southeast not carrying the noise towards the witnesses. The high brightness is likely due to the landing lights turning on slightly before the regulatory requirement, the T2 representation with lights around the shape very likely refers to distributed brightness and not strictly to point lighting. Finally, if the PAN observed was not this aircraft, it would be surprising that the witnesses did not mention it as part of their testimony, as it was very visible as we demonstrated. The case is classified as A, very probable observation of a commercial airliner.
