Summary: Sequential observations of bright yellow light appearances and disappearances in the night sky: mistaken for guiding missiles during a military maneuver.
--- seg-ee757f3dfb8c6ce5659c604b [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the evening of May 25, 2018, around 9 PM, a witness was watching television. Their window faced the Rupt-sur-Moselle fort, approximately 2 kilometers away from their home. They observed a bright light, similar to a moon in color, with yellow hues that seemed to appear and disappear at irregular intervals, accompanied by beams. The event lasted between 5 and 10 minutes. The witness reported the incident to the Gendarmerie. The following week, the witness witnessed the same phenomenon again and this time took photographs, after which they conducted their own investigation. It was found that soldiers from the 1st Tirailleurs Regiment in Epinal were conducting maneuvers in the area using illuminating rockets. A photo on a social media page dedicated to this regiment, published on June 13, 2018, 23 days after May 25, 2018, or 16 days after the second observation (with photographs), confirms this. Only one witness came forward about this phenomenon. The investigations conducted align with the hypothesis of military maneuvers using illuminating rockets (see the investigation report). Therefore, the GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Misidentification with illuminating rockets during a military maneuver.
