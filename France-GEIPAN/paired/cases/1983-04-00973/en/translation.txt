Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archive on its public website www.geipan.fr. Among its publications are old cases classified at the time and now being re-examined with the sole purpose of being more relevant in the conclusions. At the initiative of an GEIPAN investigator, the previously named LAPOUTROIE (68) case on 24.04.1983 was re-studied in 2013. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over the years, this re-examination sometimes leads to new observations or even an explicit change in classification in a document (see investigation notes). On April 24, 1983, at 10:20 PM sharp, two witnesses saw a bright object above a mountain range, roughly the size of a tennis ball. During naked-eye observation, the object appeared slightly oval, static with occasional slight oscillations. It was surrounded by a white light halo. The object then descended in three successive stages, leading to a color change. One of the people with binoculars observed a sphere higher than wide with very bright striations on the object. The object disappeared into the landscape during its descent. The new investigation demonstrates that the PAN is exactly at the location of Venus. Since the witnesses described the sky as starry and cloudless, it is not possible that they did not mention Venus next to the PAN if it was not Venus. The Gendarmerie investigation had dismissed the hypothesis of a possible astronomical misperception (it seems there was a wrong transmission of information regarding Venus' position at the time of observation between the Gendarmerie and the contacted astronomical observatory). The credibility of the witnesses is not in question, and moreover, they were deceived by a particular phenomenon of disruption of perception (see investigation notes). This case is classified "A": Astronomical observation of Venus.
