Description: On July 3, 2014, around 5:30 a.m., several witnesses observed a very bright light appearing to float above a nearby military base. The shape resembled a crescent. One witness filmed the phenomenon. The prolonged observation of a slowly moving light in the sky suggests a star. The video shows a bright light low on the horizon. A cartographic check of the observation direction of the Pierre-sur-Haute military base indicates an azimuth of approximately 72° (NNE). An astronomical check reveals that Venus, very bright that morning, was the precise celestial body observed. The variable shape and colors noted by the witnesses are common for such low-horizon star observations, resulting from the twinkling caused by atmospheric turbulence. The witness considered this light strange due to its position above the military base; this is, of course, a mere coincidence, a random alignment that caught the witness's attention. Such coincidences often make a phenomenon seem strange that would have been perceived as mundane in another context. The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as "A" (perfectly identified): Venus at sunrise.
