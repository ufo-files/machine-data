Summary: Probable observation of an atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-17f04afaa4436fd875a2efa9 [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 1, 1992, around 3:30 a.m., a witness and a dozen of his guests observed a luminous mass slowly moving across the sky with a trail of various colors. None of the present guests were interviewed. At the same time in the department, other witnesses in cars observed a similar phenomenon for 30 to 40 seconds. Each of the testimonies is quite concise, but the combination of all of them suggests that it was an atmospheric reentry.
