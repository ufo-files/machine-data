Summary: Observation of two very bright points.
--- seg-0a6272a62e18a66d47d17f3b [needs-review] ---
Description: An amateur astronomer reports that on November 5, 2005, between 6:41 and 6:45 p.m., he observed two very bright points located 10° east of the horizon above the horizon. These two white balls had the size and magnitude of Mars (very visible at this time of year). Their brightness gradually and quickly diminished, completely disappearing. The available data are contradictory (fixed and very quickly moving phenomena, poorly defined observation duration between 30 seconds and 5 minutes) and do not allow for a thorough investigation. The level of strangeness is low (it could be an atmospheric reentry, but the imprecise data on duration do not support this). No element on the sky map allows for the identification of this phenomenon, which is classified as C.
