Description: On February 3, 1976, around 7:30 PM, several witnesses observed in the sky a white-silver glow. After rising above the hill, the glow gradually disappeared down the hidden slope. This phenomenon was preceded by a noise similar to that of a supersonic aircraft. The gendarmerie ruled out the hypothesis of a fire or the fall of a body, no traces on the ground having been noticed in the indicated area. The case is classified C due to lack of information.
