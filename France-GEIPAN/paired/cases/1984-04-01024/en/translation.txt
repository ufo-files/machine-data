Description: On April 27, 1984, multiple witnesses reported observations above two communes in the Isère: a very bright triangular object remained stationary and could be seen for between a quarter hour and one hour and thirty-five minutes. Two witnesses observed the object's elevation and gradual disappearance after the appearance of thick smoke. No further information was collected on this phenomenon.
