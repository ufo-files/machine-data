Summary: Observation of a dark, elongated shape rising at a constant speed to disappear into the cloud layer: probable observation of a balloon.
--- seg-9ff6bcbb068ff19873f1b321 [machine-unreviewed] ---
**Report:** On August 6, 2025, at 10:25 UTC, at the Nouméa-Magenta airport, a pilot reporting by radio the presence of an unidentified object at the vertical of the runway, estimated to be between 150 and 200 feet altitude during rolling. An air traffic controller confirmed the visual observation with the naked eye, then with binoculars after removing solar protections on the watchtower. A second technical staff member also validated the observation. The phenomenon was described as a dark, elongated object maintained in a vertical orientation and slowly ascending at a constant speed. Over the 7-8 minutes of observation, the entity showed no light emissions, no acoustic signature, nor any apparent lateral movement, until its final disappearance into the cloud layer. The case consistency was judged as medium to good. The observation, conducted professionally by qualified staff, was corroborated by several control tower witnesses and a crew on approach. Due to the unavailability of exploitable photographic or video evidence, the report relies exclusively on direct testimonies from the observers.

**According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The perceived strangeness by the witnesses mainly stems from the object's unusual shape, its vertical orientation, and its regular ascending movement without apparent propulsion system. Several hypotheses were examined. The parachute hypothesis was ruled out due to the observed ascending movement. The drone hypothesis is not compatible with the described shape and no drone activity was reported on the platform or by relevant services at the time of the event. The balloon hypothesis, on the other hand, appears compatible with the observed characteristics: dark, elongated shape, slow movement primarily vertical, possible visible thread under the object, and gradual disappearance into the cloud layer. The aircraft crew also indicated that the object resembled a black, elongated balloon depicted in a photograph during the investigation. Some elements of strangeness remain, notably the witness's perception of a shape resembling a torch parachute. However, the consistent observations of other witnesses and the dynamic of the phenomenon are compatible with the behavior of a balloon carried by upward currents and local wind, possibly accidentally released near the airport area.

The Nouméa-Magenta airport is immediately adjacent to the public Magenta beach. This proximity makes plausible the accidental release of a recreational or advertising balloon that drifted towards the runway axis before ascending into the atmosphere. Given all available elements, the most probable hypothesis is that of a dark balloon drifting in the atmosphere and gradually ascending to the cloud layer. The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'B': probable observation of a balloon.
