Summary: Long observation from an airplane of a white sphere with very blurred edges: observation of an atmospheric optical phenomenon (similar to the Brocken specter)
--- seg-be9c3f59a04b334c22b282f7 [needs-review] ---
Description: A person reports the following events which they cannot explain: a passenger on a commercial airliner on May 21, 2011, the plane encounters a turbulence zone while the sky is clear blue with a cloud layer below. The witness then notices a large horizontal brown streak parallel to the plane, and below the plane's wing, they observe a white sphere the size of a penny coin held at arm's length. They then take photographs. The observation lasted approximately fifteen minutes between 7:55 PM and 8:10 PM. The gray streak is the shadow cast by the plane's streak on the cloud layer, the bright ball is an atmospheric optical phenomenon (similar to the Brocken specter): light reflected back from water droplets in the direction of the observer, who is facing the sun. This explanation posits that the vibrations felt by the witnesses are independent of this phenomenon. The disappearance of the bright ball is likely due to the interruption of the cloud layer.
