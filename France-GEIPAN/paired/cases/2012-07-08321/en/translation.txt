--- seg-20d5ed64a0b1e6f0222d2adf [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 14, 2012, at 10:50 PM, during communal festivities and before the fireworks began, a witness was intrigued by the silent passage from East to West of an orange light. The appearance of the object (orange light halo), its mode of movement according to the strong West wind (see Meteociel record), its short duration, and the observation on the national holiday are all consistent with the passage of a Thai lantern. The witness, not knowing this type of object, was unable to estimate its actual distance or size. The GEIPAN did not conduct searches to identify the perpetrators of the release. This case is classified "B": probable observation of the passage of a Thai lantern.
