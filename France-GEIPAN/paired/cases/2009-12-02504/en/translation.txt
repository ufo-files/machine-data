Summary: Probable observation of an atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-7bbb1af98535e8c663934080 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 8, 2009, between 7:20 and 7:30 AM, a witness observed for 10 to 20 seconds in the sky a glowing ball. Its movement was horizontal on a west-east trajectory. The ball disintegrated by fragmenting into two or three pieces before disappearing. The described phenomenon, moving horizontally at a high speed and splitting ("exploding") into several pieces, appears to correspond to the characteristics of an atmospheric reentry.
