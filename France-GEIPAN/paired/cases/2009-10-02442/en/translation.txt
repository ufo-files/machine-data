Summary: Numerous observations in France of the atmospheric reentry of the Centaur stage of the Atlas rocket and the release of residual fuel.
--- seg-7ff85070671c2e6a6729a88b [needs-review] ---
Description: The GEIPAN has received numerous witness reports of observations made on Sunday, October 18, 2009, around 21:15 local time, concerning the passage from south to northwest of two bright lights in the sky. These observations were made across the entire national territory. They very often describe one or two bright lights followed by a white halo (like a comet's tail). No noise was heard. These observations correspond to the phase of the fuel dumping of the Centaur stage of a US military weather satellite DSMP F18 launched by an Atlas rocket from the Vanderberg launch base in California (USA). This case is featured in a news update on the GEIPAN website with a trajectory graph and two photographs. New witness reports are regularly added to this case.
