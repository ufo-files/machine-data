Description: On August 15, 1978, between 9:00 and 9:10 PM, several witnesses located in the departments of Gard and Ardèche observed a red-tinted light phenomenon moving from south-east (SO) to north-east (NE). The witnesses described a glowing ball followed by a trail. No sound was heard during the observation. These individuals witnessed what was likely an atmospheric re-entry.
