Description: Several people from the Island of Pines (New Caledonia) reported what they observed on July 10, 2011, around 5:30 PM: a round-shaped object crossed the sky diagonally on a descending trajectory towards the sea. Several colors and a trail were noticed. No noise was heard. A white-colored flash was seen before the total disappearance of the phenomenon. The witnesses very likely observed a meteor, caused by the fall of a meteorite or space debris. Although the testimonies vary, they correspond well to this phenomenon. As there is no proof of the object's origin, this case is classified as "B": probable meteorite fall.
