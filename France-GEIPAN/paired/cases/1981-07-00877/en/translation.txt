Description: On July 16, 1981, around 11:45 PM, several witnesses on a boat observed above the island of Port-Cros a flying object, larger than a star. This object, described as having the shape of a torch, was white-green in color. During the observation, which lasted several seconds, the object moved slowly and on a horizontal trajectory. No other information was gathered about this phenomenon.
