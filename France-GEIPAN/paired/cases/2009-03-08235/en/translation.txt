Description: A witness reports their observation made on March 17, 2009 between 8:10 PM and 8:15 PM. They observe towards the North the passage of two white light points moving at a constant speed from West to East and following a straight trajectory. During the day and at the time of the observation, the space shuttle was in the approach phase to the International Station. The testimony is consistent with the observation of this "team". This case is classified "A": observation of the space shuttle and the ISS.
