Summary: An automobilist observed a white glowing ball moving and stopping in a clear, starry sky: observation of Jupiter and an illusion of the "following ball".
--- seg-b6239ac12b9f1841dc581220 [machine-unreviewed] ---
**Description:** On February 25, 2018, at 3:30 AM, a driver notices a bright white ball of light in a clear, starry sky. This ball appears to move and stops when the driver stops their vehicle. No sounds are heard. The passenger films the phenomenon. Only one testimony is collected. This case is highly illustrative of the illusion of a following ball that can be caused by misperception with a star when the witness is in motion. Here, we have a video of the witness during their movement. The strangeness for the witness begins with the inability to recognize the star's appearance. When the witness's car maintains a roughly constant heading, the star appears fixed relative to the vehicle and moves with the landscape. Since the witness does not see the star but a PAN (unidentified flying object) and considers it at a close distance, this movement along the landscape is perceived as the PAN's parallel movement to the vehicle, mimicking steps/stops. When the witness's heading changes, the PAN's position relative to the road changes. The video clearly illustrates these perceived oddities by the witness. The accompanying reconstruction unequivocally shows that it is Jupiter, very bright that night, with two points along the trajectory, once with Jupiter on the road's axis and once on the left. Consequently, GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Observation of Jupiter.
