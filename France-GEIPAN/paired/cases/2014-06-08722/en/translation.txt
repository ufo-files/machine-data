Description: This witness report of static orange lights in the sky at 10:45 PM resembles Thai lanterns seen downwind. The weather report indeed indicates a north wind of 15 to 30 km/h: Weather. The other flying object joining this group of lights also moves according to the wind. It could be another lantern, launched after the first group. However, the witness's description of this object does not match a Thai lantern; it should be noted that he observes it through his car's windshield while driving, and the observation was very brief (5 seconds). Due to these rather poor observation conditions, the absence of other witnesses despite a very populated environment, and the peculiarity of the case not being proven, its consistency is very low (single witness, very brief 5-second observation). This case is classified "C": Lack of information and cross-checking.
