Description: On September 20, 1985, at approximately 7:15 a.m., two witnesses observed in the sky a bright, circular, stationary object resembling a star. During this relatively long observation, one of the witnesses, equipped with binoculars, saw the object flatten into a cigar shape. From one end, a bright point of light seemed to detach and move at a low speed, leaving behind it bright turbulence. Two to three minutes later, the phenomenon returned to its initial form. Around 7:40 a.m., clouds definitively obscured the phenomenon. No further information could be gathered about this phenomenon.
