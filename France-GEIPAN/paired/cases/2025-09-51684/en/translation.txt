Description: On September 3, 2025, in Saint-Priest-Taurion (87), a witness installed in their garden observed a formation of buses. Around 4:07 PM, they initially mistook a white plane coming from the northeast for an object. The object then entered the clouds, disappeared from view for a few seconds, and reappeared traveling in the opposite direction without making any apparent turns. The witness captured the event by photographing the object with their smartphone. The observation lasted approximately 15 seconds, during which no noise was heard. The radar data provided by the Center for Air Planning and Conduct of Air Defense Operations (CAPCODA) (Annex 1) reveals the presence of a Piper P24 Comanche aircraft (Annex 2) that the witness observed at the time. The aircraft was at an altitude of 4,000 feet (approximately 1,200 meters). The radar shows that the plane initially followed a northwest to southeast trajectory before making a loop to head northeast to southwest at 2:07 PM. Between 2:07 and 2:09 PM, the plane's trajectory appears irregular, likely due to the disturbed weather conditions reported by the witness, including strong winds and clouds. This unusual trajectory, combined with limited visibility due to the clouds, explains the witness's sense of strangeness. The case exhibits good consistency despite only one witness report, but it is supported by photos/videos and CAPCODA's data. According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A," observation of a tourist plane.
