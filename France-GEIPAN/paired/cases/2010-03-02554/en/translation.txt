Description: On March 30, 2010, around 11:30 PM, a witness observed from their home the slow and silent northeast movement of a luminous phenomenon beneath the clouds. The witness saw three glowing orbs with a trail behind the phenomenon. No other testimonies were collected. With low strangeness and weak consistency due to the uniqueness of the testimony, this observation case remains unexplained due to a lack of information. A field investigation more than two years after the facts would yield little additional information. This case is classified C due to a lack of information.
