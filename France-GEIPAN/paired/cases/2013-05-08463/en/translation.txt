Description: On May 18, 2013, around 10 PM, a witness observed from the first floor of their home a slow-moving bright disk that suddenly distanced itself and disappeared. This downward observation of a bright disk, about the size of a half-sphere lamp, suggests a relatively small object carried by the wind. The Meteociel report mentions a south wind: the witness observes from their home on the first floor, oriented north, so it could be turbulence in the sheltered street. This long Pentecost weekend was conducive to many parties, which may have led to the release of Thai lanterns. The disk shape described by the witness corresponds to the shape of a cylinder viewed from above. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation from above of a Thai lantern.
