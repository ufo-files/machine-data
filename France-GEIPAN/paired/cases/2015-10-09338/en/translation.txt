Description: On October 14, 2015, at approximately 1:25 AM, a witness is awakened by a deafening noise outside and observes through the bathroom window a bright white spherical object passing by, describing two large circles as it gradually moves away. No other testimonies were collected. The GEIPAN requested military air traffic control to verify the air traffic present in the area at that time. The radar trace (attached) shows an aircraft making circles north of Nîmes. "The track whose trajectory is concentric to the north of Nîmes between 11:14 PM and 11:22 PM is that of a military aircraft flying at level 105 (approximately 3000 meters) and needing to perform this maneuver for operational reasons before continuing its flight." The witness, blinded by the plane's headlights, describes only a glowing ball: he was unable to distinguish the shape of the fuselage and wings, which are usually dark in color on military aircraft. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of a perfectly identified military aircraft.
