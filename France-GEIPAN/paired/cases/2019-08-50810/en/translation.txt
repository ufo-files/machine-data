Summary: Nighttime observation of silent movement of a luminous shape with rotating red light: probable observation of a drone.
--- seg-accaf5caf9e2c95af6e1e4a7 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 29, 2019, around 10 PM, the witness was observing the sky. He saw several planes, then a glowing circular shape with a red light spinning around the edge of the round shape without the shape itself spinning. It moved in complete silence at an apparent speed of 3 to 5 times that of a commercial airliner. Only one testimony was collected. The consistency of this case is average: one witness, no photos or videos of the PAN. The investigation (see the investigation report) suggested that the witness likely observed a drone, as the elements provided in the description: - having a circular "disk or potato" dark faintly luminous shape, 
- with a red light spinning around the edge,
- silent, are consistent with drone characteristics. The witness was greatly surprised by the very high speed of the PAN, its shape, and its luminescence. The shape, as demonstrated, is entirely compatible with the drone hypothesis. The same goes for the dark faintly luminous color, as indeed at night, only the lights of drones are really perceived. The mention of speed is difficult to exploit: to estimate speed, one needs an idea of the object's actual size and distance, which is practically impossible at night without a fixed reference point. The case is classified B, probable observation of a drone.
