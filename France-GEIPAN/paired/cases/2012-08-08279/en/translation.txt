Description: A witness, an amateur astronomer residing near the Istres Air Base and familiar with observing a wide variety of aircraft, observed on August 7, 2012 at 10:25 PM a powerful light maintaining a constant heading and estimated altitude between 1000 and 2000 meters. The phenomenon, coming from the southwest, moved to the northeast in approximately 5 minutes and then disappeared. The witness's description and the time of passage are entirely consistent with the observation of a pass of the International Space Station (ISS): see the Calsky log. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the International Space Station (ISS).
