Summary: Observation from an airplane of a yellow glow and debris crossing the atmosphere: bolide observation.
--- seg-43d0c469227c254220f59486 [needs-review] ---
Description: An air traffic controller reports an observation made by a commercial airline pilot en route from Rome to Casablanca. On August 16 at 20:37 UTC, the pilot, overflying Sardinia, observed a yellow glow with debris passing through the atmosphere. At the same time, the air traffic controller at Bastia Tower observed a bright white light. This was a meteor seen by numerous witnesses (104) and cataloged by the International Meteor Organization (IMO). The GEIPAN classifies this case as A: observation of a meteor.
