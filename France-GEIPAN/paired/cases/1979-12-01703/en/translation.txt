Summary: Observation of a triangular object changing speed and direction
--- seg-2cf889cf4a4a274789447eab [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 3, 1979, at approximately 4:50 PM, two witnesses observed a triangular-shaped object emitting two red smoke trails. As it passed over a radio tower, the object stopped for a few seconds before continuing on. The witnesses followed it with their eyes until it was out of sight, with the red smoke visible until the end of the observation. A third witness observed a very bright point at the same time, but it was not possible to correlate the two observations. The investigation was unable to identify any air traffic corresponding to the observations, and the case remains unexplained.
