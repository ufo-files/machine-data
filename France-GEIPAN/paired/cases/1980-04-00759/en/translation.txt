Summary: Observation of a bright ball, stationary with an air-moving sound.
--- seg-00c21670082b4dc5c65205ae [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 2, 1980, around 11:30 PM, several people observed in the sky a bright ball shaped like a rugby ball. This stationary object was observed for 20 minutes. Then it moved very slowly, and a swirling air noise, described by witnesses as a fan noise, was heard. The ball moved away towards the full west. The observation continued for almost an hour before the witnesses gave up. No other testimony about this phenomenon, for which we lack information, was collected. The hypothesis of a light helicopter's nighttime flight is consistent with the testimonies and should not be dismissed, although it has not been verified.
