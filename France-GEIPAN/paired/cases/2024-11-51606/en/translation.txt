Description: During the night of November 16 to 17, 2024, a witness residing in Sallanches (74) observed a red glowing point at the top of the Tête du Colonney. They assumed it was people lighting the summit with pyrotechnics, a traditional practice on Saint John's Day. The red point then moved along the eastern ridge before being replaced by a white glowing point that split into three distinct points. These three points continued moving towards the East. The total observation duration was estimated at five minutes, with approximately two minutes for the red light phase and three minutes for the white light phase, according to the witness. Of average strangeness and good consistency* (single witness, but quite accurate testimony, no photo of PAN), the case turned out to be an observation of 3 wingsuiters (BASE jumpers) who performed a nighttime jump from the Aiguille de Varan (see the investigation report). *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The witness's description of PAN is perfectly coherent with this rather unusual observation, as a local paragliding club confirmed that 3 wingsuiters made a nighttime flight from the Aiguille de Varan during a full moon, using pyrotechnics (red color) and lights inside their suits (white lights). The case is classified as "A," an observation of 3 parachutists (wingsuiters) who performed a nighttime jump from the Aiguille de Varan.
