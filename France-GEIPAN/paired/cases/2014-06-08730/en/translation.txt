Summary: Sequential observations of the movement of several gray "boat"-shaped objects in the sky: probable observation of kites.
--- seg-066c7fd6a6c44edf61c074d1 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 24, 2014, at 8:40 a.m., a driver taking her son to school was intrigued by two "boat"-shaped objects of gray color that were stationary side by side in the sky. She cannot explain what she sees to her son. The two objects eventually depart slowly. On her return trip, at 9:08, she spotted a third object in the same location, moving in the same direction as the others, that is, NNE. What the witness describes. "things in the shape of boats" very likely result from Chinese lanterns. The attached reconstruction positions on the satellite photo the locations of the 4 (reconstructed) observations by the witness. These observations from different angles:

- Allow the approximate area of the PANS' position to be located
- Reveal a profile view (observation 1 measured across the wind) and a front or rear view (observation 3 measured with the wind) of the PANS, making them perfectly consistent with an observation of kites in profile and front/rear views relative to the wind. Although the witness (contacted by phone two years later) refutes this hypothesis, it remains for the GEIPAN the most probable hypothesis, given the described elements and also because the GEIPAN was able to gather indications regarding kite flying in a leisure area just upstream (in relation to the wind direction, see position in reconstruction). It could have been users of the area who, exceptionally (the area was inaccessible that day?), decided to fly kites further downstream in the field (PANs) or just upstream of this field, such that the witness saw the kites (on one side of the road) without noticing the kite flyers (on the other side of the road). As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: kite observation.
