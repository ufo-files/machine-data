Description: On March 30, 2008, around 10:40 PM, a witness on their balcony was intrigued by the presence of several red glowing points on the horizon. Before moving, these points emit a strong white flash. They then gain altitude but are quickly obscured by a building. This observation case has too low consistency to allow for a conclusion. As it only involves a few distant lights observed for a few seconds, the strangeness is average but the description is too short for a proper evaluation. An investigation long after the facts would yield little information. This case is classified C due to lack of independent testimonies and cross-checks.
