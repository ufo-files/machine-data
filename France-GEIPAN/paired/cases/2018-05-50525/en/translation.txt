Description: On May 9, 2018, around 7:30 PM, a witness from a rugby field was intrigued by the passage in the sky of a very bright cylindrical object and a black sphere. The black sphere came from the northeast and headed south. No noise was heard. The observation lasted about a minute before the witness lost sight of the PAN. However, the witness had time to take three photographs. No other testimony was collected at GEIPAN. The witness was both excited and perplexed by his observation, as he really hoped for an explanation (or a lack thereof). He had made other strange observations in the past (without reporting at GEIPAN), he is documented on UFOs, and this time his hope was high as he took pictures. He might be disappointed by an explanation that does not meet his expectations. Indeed, the speed of movement and the appearance of the PAN in the photo clearly suggest a commercial airliner. In the unzoomed photo, equivalent to the naked eye, the plane is just a bright point without trail (trails are not automatic) and classic of an airplane. The strangeness for the witness must have arisen from the zoom (or a particularly sharp natural vision) that reveals the elongated shape without wings. Multiple examples processed at GEIPAN show that it is common for only the fuselage to reflect the sun, making the plane appear wingless like a cigar. This is particularly the case when the sun is transverse to the fuselage and only "sees" the thickness of the wing, as here with a sun at 282° azimuth orthogonal to the NE-SO trajectory described for this PAN. And the proof is made, as we find the plane "in question." As shown in the attached reconstruction, at 7:29 PM (photo time), we have an airplane going from northeast to south, and its movement seen from the rugby field is consistent with the testimony. In particular, the photo allows, thanks to the IPACO software, to identify the height of the PAN relative to the top of the first trees bordering the south of the rugby field. These trees are about a hundred meters away, considering the witness is in the middle of the stand. The top height is between 5° and 10° depending on whether the tree height is 5 or 15 meters (there are two trees of different heights in the photo). This places the PAN at an altitude of about 35° in the sky, when we add the measured heights on the photo. And it is indeed the order of magnitude of the altitude of the plane on the radar capture at 12 km and 10.6 km altitude, or 41° altitude in the sky. The difference is explained by the unknowns and errors on the witness's position along the stands, the knowledge of the tree heights, the synchronization between the photo and the radar capture of the plane. The latter quickly moving away while descending in the sky. The black spheres mentioned by the witness are rather lens flares on the lens, there are indeed many of them. Consequently, GEIPAN classifies the case as A: airplane.
