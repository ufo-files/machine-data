Description: On the night of June 21 to 22, 2025, a resident of Achères, Yvelines, observed from their terrace an extremely intense orange glow positioned almost vertically. It immediately attracts the attention of a second person present, whose reaction underscores the unusual nature of the scene. The light source, devoid of any clear outline and totally silent, moves quickly on a bearing estimated at 300° at a constant speed before disappearing. Of average strangeness and good consistency* (2 witnesses, but unique testimony, photo and videos of PAN), this case appears to be a probable observation of a celestial lantern. *CONSISTENCY: According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The description and visual aspect of the PAN are consistent with the observation of a celestial lantern. Furthermore, as the observation took place during the night from Saturday to Sunday, coinciding with Music Day celebrations, and the apparent trajectory of the light was perfectly in line with the local wind direction, the hypothesis is highly favored, although the exact origin of the release could not be formally identified (see the investigation report). The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B", probable observation of a celestial lantern.
