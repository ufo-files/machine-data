--- seg-9af1d37bd31a76242fe00c95 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 20, 2014, at 9:37 PM, a witness observed for 2 minutes the movement according to an S-N trajectory of three glowing, orange-yellow orbs, appearing larger than an airplane. The orbs seem incandescent and disappear, leaving a trail. The witness takes photographs. This testimony is typical of Thai lantern observations: orange orbs slowly moving in a straight line at low altitude, according to the wind, on a weekend evening. The MétéoCiel report for Melin confirms a light south wind. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": very probable observation of Thai lanterns. Given the low strangeness of this observation, the GEIPAN did not investigate the perpetrators of this release.
