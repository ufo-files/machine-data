--- seg-7112fa0d830590cad1ef11af [needs-review] ---
Description: This observation on September 25, 2008 at 21:00 of a bright, large point moving across the sky from west to east in just a few minutes at the beginning of night suggests the passage of a large satellite. A check on the Calsky site indicates the passage of the International Space Station (ISS) within a few minutes of the time given by the witness (see log). The witness specifies that he has seen this same phenomenon several times in the previous years and months; this statement is consistent with this hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the International Space Station (ISS). Note: On the same evening at 23:00, a much more spectacular phenomenon was visible in the same region: the re-entry into Earth's atmosphere of a third stage of a Russian PROTON launcher.
