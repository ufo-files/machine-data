Summary: Observation of rapid movements of a luminous object.
--- seg-8df3983e1a96deede93b4191 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 25, 1978, between 3:30 p.m. and 3:45 p.m., a witness was intrigued from his place of work by a stationary object in the cloudy sky. This rectangular-shaped object was emitting a very bright light. This witness and a colleague observed the object moving at a very high speed in opposite directions twice. The control tower at Limoges-Bellegarde Airport confirmed the flights of two aircraft in the region between 3:20 p.m. and 4:14 p.m. The available information on these distant observations does not allow for an analysis of the observed object.
