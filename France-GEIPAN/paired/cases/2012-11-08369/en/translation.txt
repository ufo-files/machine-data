Summary: Long observations of a luminous phenomenon above the sea: observation of the lighting of a ship.
--- seg-d154e2613baa82f18ecee066 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On November 25, 2012, two witnesses observed from the esplanade of the Fort de la Collégiale a bright light between the Gaou peninsula and the Embiez island. Intrigued, they watched this phenomenon for approximately 3 hours. During the investigation, the valuable help of an employee from the "Marine Traffic" service was crucial. He was able to clearly identify the presence of the "Mol Marvel," a container ship heading south of Toulon. It is this very ship that the witnesses saw on the date and at the time corresponding to its passage in the indicated direction. Its size, speed, and distance from the coast are entirely consistent with the testimonies and photos taken (see the document "enquête notes"). The GEIPAN classifies this case as A (perfectly identified phenomenon): observation of the lighting of a ship.
