Summary: Observation of a disk-shaped light mass: misidentification with a secondary radar of the DGAC.
--- seg-da2f24af6ac8922ae1293a2e [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 2, 2002, around 7:40 PM, a family in a car observed, above a forested area, an illuminated disk-shaped object. During the night, this object, topped with a dome, was gray in color with spaced-out white lights around its belt. The phenomenon remained stationary above the trees, with an estimated diameter of about 15 meters by the witnesses. No unusual noises were heard. After a change in the road axis, the witnesses lost sight of the object. At the time, the case was classified as D. Following a recent email from an internet user mentioning a water tower in the observation sector that could have led to a misidentification at the time, the case was reopened and additional investigation is sought. Based on the witnesses' position, it is likely there was a misidentification, not with a water tower but with the secondary radar of the General Delegation for Civil Aviation of Paris Sud Palaiseau, used for Orly Airport, which is indeed located along the observation axis and corresponds to the descriptions given by the witnesses. This case is reclassified as B.
