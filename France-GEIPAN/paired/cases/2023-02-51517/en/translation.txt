Description: From February 10 to 13, 2023, a driver traveling between GRAND BOURGTHEROULDE (27) and ROUEN (76) made the same observation at the same time for 4 consecutive days. On the final day, she recorded a video with her smartphone. Only the observation on February 13, with video evidence, is analyzed due to a lack of data on the observations from the previous 3 days (see the investigation report). This low-strangeness, well-consistent* observation (single witness, video of PAN) turns out to be a reflection of powerful truck headlights approaching the witness's vehicle. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The hypothesis of observing a star was considered. However, the short duration of the observation, combined with the witness's description of rapid movement, do not align with this hypothesis. Similarly, the hypothesis of observing a meteor is ruled out based on the absence of surveillance network testimony on that date and in that area. A closer examination of the video of PAN reveals that it appears simultaneously with the arrival of a truck equipped with powerful headlights facing the witness's car. Analysis of the video using IPACO software highlights the position of an undesirable optical reflection opposite the light source from these headlights, according to a central symmetry axis passing through the optical center of the image. The position of this undesirable reflection is confused with that of PAN. This analysis is supported by the movement of PAN, which moves in sync with the truck's headlights' light when viewing the witness's video. Other less visible bright spots on the video correspond to the same artifact produced by the headlights of following vehicles, which are less blinding to the camera's detector. The case is classified as 'A', camera artifacts due to vehicle headlights (ghost images).
