Description: On August 14, 2009, around 10:55 PM, a witness was intrigued by the southeast to northwest and very rapid passage across the sky of a round object of red-orange color. No trail was noticed during the 2 to 3 seconds of observation. At 11:10 PM, the witness made a second observation of one second of the southeast to northwest passage of another object. This case suggests the observation of a bolide (meteorite fall) but the absence of a trail is surprising, and the GEIPAN found no confirmation from other observations. Due to its average strangeness with a linear movement, this observation case suffers from too much imprecision and thus has too little consistency at this stage. A field investigation would yield little information more than 4 years after the facts. This case is classified C due to lack of independent testimonies and cross-checks.
