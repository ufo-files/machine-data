Summary: Observations of the straight-line movement of three bright points in a clear night sky across three different departments: certain observations of a triplet of Chinese surveillance satellites.
--- seg-07032eee9906fee0c04b7831 [needs-review] ---
**Report: A witness located in Lalinde (24) reports to GEIPAN having followed the straight-line movement of three bright points grouped in the night sky on August 11, 2018, around 11:10 p.m. local time. Two other reports are sent to GEIPAN for similar observations on the same night: thus, three independent testimonies are collected for this phenomenon:

- LALINDE (24) 08/11/2018
- ARGELES-GAZOST (65) 08/11/2018
- LUNAN (46) 08/11/2018

The investigation report provides a detailed analysis of the testimony from LALINDE (24) 08/11/2018. This main witness, by chance, observed a triplet of Chinese surveillance satellites, recently launched, in a temporary configuration favorable for naked-eye observation. This conclusion is based on the following:

- Appearance fully consistent with that of satellites (brightness, color, point-like aspect, straight-line movement), which this witness also noted;
- Proven presence in the sky at the time of observation of these satellites;
- Other identical observations on all points, on the same date and at the same time, one of which was well described as observed by a satellite specialist;
- Initial and final elevations provided by the witness perfectly match those given by the satellite specialist regarding his own observation and the reconstruction of the triplet's trajectory on Stellarium, which correspond to the position of this triplet at its culmination.

We were also able to show that the witness likely overestimated the angular movement of PAN, but also underestimated, to a lesser extent, the observation duration, for a straight-line movement at a conserved elevation of approximately 75°, which perfectly matches the characteristics of the satellite triplet.

Although the initial observation direction was roughly correctly estimated, the final direction was not. This is likely due to the presence of natural and artificial obstacles (trees, buildings...) significantly obscuring the horizon and preventing the witness from accurately estimating the azimuths. The short observation duration also likely played a role, as well as the high position of the triplet, an observation close to the zenith making more difficult the estimates of the azimuthal positions of appearance and/or disappearance.

What makes this strange for this witness, who has already observed satellites, is essentially the fact that they are three in number and move together, without visible stars. We have seen that a movement by three satellites is possible, within the framework of these particular surveillance satellites of the NOSS type, American and now Chinese. If the stars were not observed by the witness between the bright points, it is probably because, at the time of observation, they were passing through a sky area devoid of stars of sufficient magnitude to be visible.

Two other very similar reports reached GEIPAN, for observations on the same date and around 11 p.m.: ARGELES-GAZOST (65) 08/11/2018 and LUNAN (46) 08/11/2018. These also involve the observation of three bright points in movement, with the first, leading, less visible. There is little doubt that it is the same phenomenon, which could be well observed under ideal conditions, with mild temperatures, a very clear, unobstructed sky, no moon, and during a period when more people observe the sky ("Meteor Shower Night").

GEIPAN classifies the case as 'A': certain observation of a triplet of Chinese surveillance satellites Yaogan-31 A, B, and C, a few months after their launch, in a temporarily favorable configuration.
