Summary: Observations of the atmospheric reentry of a bolide.
--- seg-c26d46471786d6c1f0ca6c14 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 25, 2008, around 6:15 PM, numerous witnesses, primarily in southwestern France, observed a bright object in the sky. The investigation conducted by the GEIPAN did not validate the hypothesis of the atmospheric reentry of a space debris, and the assumed scenario is the reentry of a bolide. Over 170 testimonies were collected, allowing the establishment of a trajectory and a probable impact point to the east of Albi. Although several witnesses provided the GEIPAN with stones found after this observation, no debris from this meteorite was recovered.
