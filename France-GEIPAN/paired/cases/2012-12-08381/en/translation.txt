Description: On December 28, 2012, at 9:45 PM, a witness, driving on the A61 highway near the Toulouse-sud rest area, observed for 20 to 30 seconds a stationary luminous phenomenon very close by that intrigued them. They saw two horizontal white lights at very low altitude, followed by a central white beacon illuminating the ground. The lights went out and were replaced by 6 to 8 rectangular glowing spots that blinked successively in yellow and orange. The witness stopped at a gas station but trees blocked their view of the phenomenon. Despite the high traffic volume that night on the highway, no other testimonies were collected. The difficulty of this case lies in the uniqueness of the testimony in an otherwise very frequented environment, making it challenging to accurately characterize the phenomenon. Attempts to find other witnesses were unsuccessful, despite the phenomenon being quite spectacular (see the investigation report). The observation was very brief and occurred in a car driving at night on a heavily congested highway. The witness was sincere and so impressed by what they saw that they returned to the location the next day, went to the Muret gendarmerie, and sent a letter to GEIPAN. Their testimony is relatively precise but unique. The hypothesis of "reflections" combined with poor perception or interpretation seems the most likely explanation, but it does not undermine the witness's credibility. This case is classified as B: probable observation of headlight reflections on the car windows.
