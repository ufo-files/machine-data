--- seg-1343985b7b34ead0de21e11f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 2, 2013, between 9:10 PM and 9:30 PM, a driver was intrigued by the presence of several orange orbs in the sky. The witness's description is typical of a Thai lantern observation: slowly moving orange orbs that lasted for a few minutes. The end-of-year holiday season is conducive to lantern releases. The strong wind in Vannes was a light west wind. Inland, it is likely weaker and more southerly oriented (see the south wind in Rennes), making compatible the observed movement of the orbs from south to north (towards Neuillac, see map). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
