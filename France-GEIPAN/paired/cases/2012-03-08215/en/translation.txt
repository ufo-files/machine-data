Description: On March 31, 2012, around 9:15 PM, a witness observed in the sky towards the N-W, the silent north-south passage of several non-flashing orange lights. A second witness observed part of the phenomenon (but did not provide a statement). The observation ended with the gradual extinction of the lights. This observation exhibits all the characteristics of the passage of Thai lanterns: the movement is consistent with the relatively strong northeast wind (17 to 39 km/h), the orange color, the duration of the observation, and the number of objects. Saturday is the evening holiday, and therefore the day when releases of lanterns are common. The GEIPAN did not conduct research to identify the source of the release of these flying lanterns. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
