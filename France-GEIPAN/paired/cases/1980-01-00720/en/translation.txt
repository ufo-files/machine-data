Summary: Probable atmospheric reentries.
--- seg-e466d0763ff31aeedd24e4d2 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 23 and 24, 1980, in Martinique and Guadeloupe, peculiar light phenomena were observed over the ocean. From various locations on the islands between 6:00 and 7:00 PM, witnesses saw one or several luminous objects, followed or not by multicolored light trails. The sky was clear, and according to the witnesses, who only saw the phenomena for a few seconds, the objects quickly disappeared as they moved forward. Based on the various descriptions, the hypothesis considered is that of probable atmospheric reentries.
