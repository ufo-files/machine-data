Description: On May 1st, 2014 at 6:30 PM, a witness closes their window and notices four white circles moving very slowly and silently through the clouds towards the North, approximately 200 meters in height. The phenomenon disappears quickly. No other testimonies were collected. The witness reports at the end of the day observing white circles in a white cloud. This could simply be gaps in the top of the cloud. This case is not very strange or consistent due to the brevity and uniqueness of the testimony, without any photos. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to the lack of precise information.
