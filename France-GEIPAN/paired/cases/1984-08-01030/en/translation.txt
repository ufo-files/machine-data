Description: On August 31, 1984, between 9:00 and 9:15 PM, two witnesses observed a large, stationary white light below the moon. During the following 10 minutes of observation, this light elongated into the shape of a rugby ball, positioned itself above the moon, and took the form of two distinct white lights. According to the witnesses, these lights appeared to be connected by a compact mass. No noise or smoke trail was detected. The phenomenon abruptly disappeared. No further information was gathered about this event.
