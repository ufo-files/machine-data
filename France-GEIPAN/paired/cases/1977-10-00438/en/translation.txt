Summary: Bright object observation identified: optical effect due to two streetlamps lit up.
--- seg-3dcf8b29057bc3535aea3348 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 8, 1977, at 12:30 AM, a motorist and his passenger were surprised by a glowing object that appeared to hover over their vehicle and landed in a field next to the road. The rounded craft was equipped with two nozzles expelling flames that lit up the entire horizon. The witnesses were very shaken by their observation. Following a gendarmerie investigation, the witnesses will recognize that they were deceived by two gas lanterns lit and projecting, at times and depending on the wind direction, high flames into the sky. These lanterns are part of a gas station and are located on the crest of a field, the approximate location of the observation made by the two witnesses.
