Description: On May 27, 2022, around 11:30 PM, the witness, at their home on their terrace, observed in the sky a PAN of round shape and reddish-orange color. This PAN moved silently along a straight trajectory at a steady speed. Initially observed to the south at an elevation of approximately 30°, it was lost from view to the north, obscured by trees at around 45° elevation. The witness provided two photos of the PAN. Only one testimony was collected. The consistency of this case is good: despite the presence of only one witness, the latter was able to take, using their smartphone, two photographs of the PAN, including one zoomed in, which could be used for analysis (see investigation report). The investigation revealed that this PAN was a sky lantern, likely released for a private celebration the night before a weekend. This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- Round shape, only definable as such by the observation distance. This shape is typical of those reported by sky lantern witnesses.
- Red-orange color, also typical of a lantern.
- Straight-line movement at a steady speed without noise.
- Possible orientation of this movement in the direction of zero or very low wind, and possibly oriented locally southwest/northeast or southssouthwest/northeast, an orientation enhanced by the presence to the east of the witness of local relief.
- Calculated distance, using one of the two photographs, entirely compatible with that of a lantern evolving at low altitude.
- Time period, a Friday night in May, and calm weather, elements conducive to the release of sky lanterns. Although releases are generally done in numbers, it is possible that this lantern was released by the organizers of a private party for test purposes before a more massive release the following night (or Sunday). It is also possible that due to variable winds, one of the released lanterns became isolated and visible in the witness's sector.

The perceived strangeness was due to the witness' inability to match the characteristics of their observation with those of a sky lantern, as represented on the internet. Indeed, on the internet, a lantern is represented close up, with its shape and flame clearly visible, and in numbers, with releases of several dozen or even hundreds of elements. However, this representation is only exact in specific cases, for example, if the lantern is observed very closely and not alone, which was not the case in this observation.

The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": very probable observation of a sky lantern.
