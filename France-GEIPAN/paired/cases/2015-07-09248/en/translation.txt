Summary: Silent flight observation of a fireball and sudden disappearance: probable observation of a Thai lantern.
--- seg-a421fa78e16e78613c44a89a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Thursday, July 16, 2015, at 10:21 PM, a witness was surprised to see an object flying at low altitude that appeared to be on fire. It flew silently from west to east, then stopped and changed direction. It disappeared in less than a few seconds, vertically to the ground with a "surprising" acceleration. The witness had time to take a picture with his phone and discovered on the photo an "orange fireball." No other testimony was collected. Given the objective elements gathered (see the investigation report), namely: - the orange color identical to that of lanterns. - the behavior identical to that of an object subject to no or weak wind (moderate speed). - the movement in the direction of the dominant wind. - the short observation duration (1 minute) compatible with the lifespan of lanterns. - the illusion of disappearance caused by the lantern's extinction. - the absence of noise. - the time of year and location conducive to festivals. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B" as a probable observation of a Thai lantern.
