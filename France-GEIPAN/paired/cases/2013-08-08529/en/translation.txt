Description: On Sunday, August 25, 2013, between 7:30 PM and 7:45 PM, a witness observed from their balcony two successive luminous phenomena in the sky that they did not recognize. The observation of a large red light in the sky for 2 minutes, moving slowly, could be the observation of a Thai lantern moving in the wind towards the witness (dominant east wind on the island of Réunion). The intense white light coming from the sea could be a satellite: the Calsky predictions (attached) indicate the passage of the satellite "USA 217/STPSat-2" on a trajectory compatible with the given time; however, the brightness reported by the witness does not match the low brightness predicted by the Calsky model (magnitude between 5 and 8), but a reflection is not ruled out. It could also be another unlisted military satellite, brighter. Although these two hypotheses are plausible, they are not sufficiently solidified for the GEIPAN to adopt them as very likely. The GEIPAN classifies this observation as "C," due to a lack of information to solidify these hypotheses.
