Description: On June 26, 2010, around 12:10 AM, a witness observed the passage of two yellow-orange glowing points. They crossed the sky from East to West at a constant speed. They disappeared, hidden by the urban landscape. Two other people reportedly saw this phenomenon, but no other testimony was collected. According to the witness's description, it is likely that the witness observed the passage of Thai lanterns.
