Description: On Saturday, August 29, 2015, around 2:00 PM, a witness watching television had their attention drawn to the movement in the sky of four distinct round, yellow-orange objects with a red center, appearing to form a single phenomenon. The objects moved slowly and silently, passing over a building, then were lost from view. The witness had time to take a photograph. Only one testimony was collected. The witness likely saw objects carried by the wind. The slow movement, closing in and flying over the building (oriented NE), is consistent with a light NE wind (see weather report). Furthermore, the photos and description suggest a rather round shape like balloons. The red or orange light aspect could correspond to Thai lanterns that can vary in color and shape, but it could also be LED balloons inflated with helium. In conclusion, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable observation of balloons.
