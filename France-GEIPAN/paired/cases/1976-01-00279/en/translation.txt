--- seg-d31031607d78406af52dfde4 [needs-review] ---
Description: Following a local press article (not present in the archives GEIPAN), several people testified at the gendarmerie about their observations made during January 1976 (the date of January 5th is indicated for the first observation). Each witness observed a peculiar luminous phenomenon or object at different days, hours, and locations. One witness reported seeing a man emerge from a grounded craft and approach him. Frightened, the witness fled. He claimed to have encountered the same thing the next day. The gendarmerie found no traces or evidence on the scene to confirm or refute the brief statements of the witnesses.
