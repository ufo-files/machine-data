Description: A witness reports in March 2009 an old observation. On April 24, 2007, between 10:00 PM and midnight, a driver on a rural road observed and followed a flying object at low altitude that intrigued him. No other testimony was collected. The observed object resembles a reconnaissance helicopter: the speed and movements are consistent with those of a helicopter. The perceived noise was low, but the witness was in his car. However, the GEIPAN was unable to confirm this hypothesis due to the delay between the observation and the investigation. This case is classified as "C" due to the lack of elements to validate the hypothesis.
