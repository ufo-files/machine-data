Description: On February 19, 2022, around 12:15, a driver was traveling in SAINT-JUNIEN (87) when they observed an "unconventional" aerial phenomenon that they described as a "singularity." He sees, in the very cloudy sky, at low altitude, 4 static lights of extremely intense white that seem to twinkle, pulse. He distinguished a segment of matte gray material emerging from the clouds from which the lights originate. His vehicle being equipped with an onboard camera that films all his trips, the witness was able to record the sequence of his observation. The consistency of this case is good: one witness but an onboard camera video. The witness likely observed cloud gaps (see the investigation report). The video accounts for this observation perfectly. The flickering lights and the matte gray-silver segment observed by the witness that made the observation strange are likely the result of changes in luminosity and movements of the cloud layer. Furthermore, an examination of the onboard video shows that the PAN is visible at times well before the witness reaches the roundabout, showing no difference from the other gaps. The GEIPAN classifies this case as B: very probable observation of sun glints and breaks in the clouds.
