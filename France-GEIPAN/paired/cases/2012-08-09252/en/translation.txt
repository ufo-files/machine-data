Description: 2012 observation report to GEIPAN in 2015. On August 31, 2012, a passenger in a car traveling from Chartres (28) to Caen (14) was intrigued by the presence of a very bright, yellow-orange phenomenon low on the horizon that they could not explain. The phenomenon appeared to move forward, just like the witnesses. The witness took a photograph. Only one questionnaire was collected, but the driver also saw the bright phenomenon. The observation location or road was not precisely indicated by the witness or defined by the investigation. Given the collected elements (see the investigation report), namely: - the aspect, brightness, and color of PAN 
- the sun low on the horizon and the PAN at the same height
- the presence of clouds conducive to the formation of parhelia
- the presence of a characteristic trail of a parhelion
- the angular distance separating the sun from the PAN consistent with that of a small halo of a parhelion.

GEIPAN concludes that the PAN observed by the witnesses is very likely a parhelion phenomenon. The case is classified as "A" for a certain observation of a parhelion.
