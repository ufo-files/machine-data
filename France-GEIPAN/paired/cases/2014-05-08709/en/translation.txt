Description: The observation around 23:00 of a group of orange balls for several minutes on a Saturday night suggests a release of Thai lanterns following a festival. As the witness is looking West, into a light wind, the objects appear stationary. The shape of a cornucopia may have been perceived due to a perspective effect from the lanterns which were actually almost aligned with the wind direction. Meteo France reports a light west wind of around 10 km/h in Bordeaux: Weather. No other testimonies were collected. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
