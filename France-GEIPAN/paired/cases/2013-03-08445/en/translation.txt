Description: A witness observed for over an hour 5 bright points of light above a building across from their home. Having observed the PAN with binoculars, he describes it as a burning orange. He calls the gendarmes and has them witness the phenomenon, seeing the balls appear and disappear. The witness takes several photographs. The series of photos posted online by the witness clearly depict aircraft at landing seen from the front. Let's verify this hypothesis (refer to the Google Earth representation: DOMONT (95) 18.03.2013 triangulation.kmz): - par analyse de la photo IMG_0873.jpg and compare it with Google Earth, we find 86.28° as the direction of the distant light source with the highest angular height, leading to an azimuth of 86° + 180 = 266°. - On aeronautical charts, the approach referenced 27R is made at an azimuth of 266° (AIP France CDG document, page AD2 LFPG IAC 13, page 36 de 1306_AD-2.LFPG Charles de Gaulle.pdf). - de plus toujours dans l'image Img_0873_FNA27R.jpg), and in Google Earth, if we draw a line between the landing point on 27R and the witness, this line passes exactly in the middle between the two buildings. - Just as in image IMG0873, if we draw a line between the three light points and intersect it with the horizon, we end up exactly in the middle between the two buildings. Identification is therefore complete: the witness saw and photographed airplanes approaching Roissy. Images IMG_0546, 547, 549, show in the same way a takeoff from runway 09R/27L; images IMG_0688, 689, 690, show in the same way a takeoff from 08L/26R, at the same time as a takeoff from 09R/27L (light aircraft clearly taking off before reaching the end of the runway). The GEIPAN categorizes this case as "A" for the observation of several commercial aircraft approaching or departing from Roissy-CDG. Additional information on air traffic at PARIS CDG: To ensure the best airport capacity, the runways at PARIS CDG are operated as follows:

- Outer runways (08R/26L and 09L/27R) are primarily used for arrivals.
- Inner runways (08L/26R and 09R/27L) are primarily used for departures.
- Parallel movements on specialized runways: simultaneous movements on runways with parallel or nearly parallel instruments, where one runway is used exclusively for approaches and the other for departures.
- Runway selection for parallel operations: two parallel runways can be selected for simultaneous movements: simultaneous landings on both runways; simultaneous takeoffs on both runways; landing on one runway and simultaneous takeoff on the other. The GEIPAN categorizes this case as A: airplane observation.
