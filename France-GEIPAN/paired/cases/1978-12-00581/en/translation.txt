Description: On December 20, 1978, around 10:15 PM, a witness spotted long pink streaks in the sky, followed by two spinning objects in the shape of top. During the objects' very high-speed movement, the main witness heard no noise. Another witness, present at the scene, only saw a sky on fire. No other testimonies were collected regarding this phenomenon, for which we have limited information.
