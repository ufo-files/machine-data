--- seg-d5e466c0e09853ce68afbe46 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Saturday, February 21, 2015, at 11:30 PM, a motorist and their passenger were intrigued by several silent, orange glowing objects moving across the Lille sky. Only one statement was collected. The observation of orange balls crossing the sky in a matter of minutes, on a weekend night, strongly resembles Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. It's noted that this was the first Saturday after the Chinese New Year (February 19), a significant occasion for releasing flying lanterns. Due to the witness observing the objects while driving, and their intermittent appearance, the precise trajectory of the objects could not be reconstructed and cross-referenced with wind direction (a light easterly wind); see the Météociel report. The GEIPAN received no other reports of similar phenomena in Lille that evening. Because this verification was impossible, and despite the strong resemblance to Thai lanterns, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to lack of information. The witness refutes the hypothesis of Thai lanterns presented to them.
