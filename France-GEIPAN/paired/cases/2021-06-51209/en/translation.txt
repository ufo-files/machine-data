--- seg-f88cd7878caeb2056b46ccf0 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 22, 2021 at 17:25, two witnesses, on their terrace, were intrigued by the passage in the sky of a very bright object. One of the witnesses has time to take photographs with his camera. No noise was perceived during the object's movement, which followed an upward trajectory with a change in direction. The PAN disappears while moving quickly above the clouds. Only one testimony will be collected. The witness's photos point towards a fantasy balloon (see the following articles first on the GEIPAN site in Qu'ai-je vu? and on the Sky Misperceptions site in Fantasy Balloon). The witness indeed mentioned, "At first, I thought it was a weather balloon." The witness describes a cylindrical, very bright, silver PAN in vertical motion with a change in direction. The vertical movement is consistent with the trajectory of this type of balloon. The change in direction can be explained by changes in winds at high altitude. The low residual strangeness degree leads to not pushing further the analysis with a detailed study of winds. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": helium or mylar fantasy balloon.
