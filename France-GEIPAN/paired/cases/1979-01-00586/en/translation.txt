Description: On the night of January 1st to 2nd, 1979, two witnesses observed for less than 10 seconds a lighted object with red lights. This object, which they compared to a kite, moved quickly from north to south without any noise and at an altitude of approximately 100 meters above the urban area. No other witnesses were found for this phenomenon, for which we lack information.
