Summary: Observation of a luminous phenomenon in the Paris sky: lack of information.
--- seg-807b0457ad8da1630ef225d1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 29, 2008, the GEIPAN received an email accompanied by two photographs depicting a glowing ball, corresponding to a report of a luminous phenomenon in the sky observed on the evening of March 26, 2007. This testimony is of fairly low consistency: the account was given several months after the events, it is a bit confused, unreliable though certainly sincere; the long-exposure photo is difficult to interpret (there is doubt about the blurriness: due to the object or the camera?). The observed phenomenon is not very strange: it is merely a glowing point whose motion is poorly characterized, either by the witness or by the photo; the change in shape is somewhat strange but may be due to a change in position. Given these various inaccuracies, this case is classified as 'C' due to a lack of reliable information.
