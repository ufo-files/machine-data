Summary: Observation of a bright point moving rapidly across the horizon on a straight trajectory
--- seg-26fd9186a19fbe35e49addb1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 28, 1989, around 00:40, a witness observed from their home a bright point of light moving rapidly (faster than a plane) across the horizon on a straight path from southwest to northeast. The phenomenon was silent and eventually disappeared, leaving a halo and a trail of smoke that persisted for several minutes. Another witness, approximately fifteen kilometers further south, reported seeing a point of orange color that disappeared, leaving a small, persistent cloud. Given the notice broadcast to navigators by the Oceanographic Service of Brest regarding possible reentries, during the night of June 27-28, of missiles launched from the Test Center in Landes, the hypothesis that this observation was one of these reentries is the most likely.
