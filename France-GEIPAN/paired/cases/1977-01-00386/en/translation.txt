Description: On January 24, 1977, at 6:17 a.m., three people observed a column of steam or smoke coming from the sky. A froth spot was visible on the surface, indicating the fall of an object into the ocean about 4 kilometers from the shore and about 8 to 10 kilometers from the observation location. No other testimonies about this phenomenon were collected, and as the impact point was difficult to determine, no search was conducted.
