Summary: Observation of the slow passage of an object with sun reflections on its surface: very probable observation of an object carried by the wind, type festive balloon.
--- seg-8bd5eb953fae9afb4c982254 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 06 at 10:56, the witness spots a very high up bright point in the sky. It moves slowly from northwest to west and its movements are straight-line, despite half-turns, as if it were carried by the wind at altitude. The witness took photos of his observation and noted that the object was quite different in each photo, and that the sun was reflecting off its surface so that "it was definitely material." No other testimony was collected at GEIPAN. Testimony of medium strangeness and very good consistency (unique testimony but complete testimony with photos of PAN). The visual aspect of PAN in the photos and the description "the sun was reflecting off its surface so it was definitely material" and "It was slow and its movements were straight-line, despite its half-turns, as if it was being carried by the wind at altitude" strongly suggest an object with a metallic surface, similar to a festive balloon, carried by the wind. According to the Google Earth extract sent by the witness (not published for the sake of the witness's location confidentiality), the PAN moved from an azimuth of 348° (NNW) to an azimuth of 235° (SSE), representing a directional shift of 23°. The Lesquin weather station, located 9 km from the witness, reports a wind at 20° with a speed of 11 km/h and gusts of 18 km/h at the time of observation (see attached document). The trajectory of the PAN is therefore completely consistent with that of the wind. The U-turns can be explained by local variations in wind direction, considering the strength of the gusts at 18km/h. The GEIPAN therefore concludes to the very probable observation of a wind-carried object, type festive balloon. The case is classified A.
