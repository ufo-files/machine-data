Summary: Simultaneous observations of two luminous phenomena in the sky: probable observation of Thai lanterns, simultaneously with the observation of a bolide.
--- seg-452b0a8529ae929c887f79c6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 20, 2015, around 11 PM, a driver observes three red-orange glowing points moving silently from North to South. At the same time, a bright green light moves from East to West. This testimony describes two distinct phenomena: - Three red-orange glowing points moving slowly from North to South for several minutes. - A very bright green light moving very quickly to the West. The witness naturally connects the two phenomena, which turn out to be coincidental. Indeed, the first phenomena have all the characteristics of Thai lanterns, frequently observed on summer Saturday evenings, launched after weddings or other celebrations. Their movement towards the South is consistent with the North wind recorded in Lyon-St Exupery (see the weather report). The green phenomenon has all the characteristics of a meteor. It was observed simultaneously by several witnesses in the region: in Ain, Saône-et-Loire, Nièvre, and Rhône. Unfortunately, the BOAM camera network did not record this meteor, probably due to cloud coverage at the locations of these cameras. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns, simultaneous to a meteor.
