Description: Observation around 23:20 by a group of witnesses, of 5 to 7 lights silently flying over a forest, then gaining altitude. The objects make a loop before disappearing. This observation case is quite typical of Thai lantern releases. The red color of the envelope, the group movement, the ascending and/or wind-driven movement (north wind that evening: weather), the disappearance (extinction) after a few minutes, the time and date (evening of a Friday) of the observation, are all indications in favor of this hypothesis. The movement towards the south (Grigny, Evry) is compatible with the north wind reported at that time in Orly. The lanterns likely flew over the Seine, and experienced some turbulence at that moment. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
