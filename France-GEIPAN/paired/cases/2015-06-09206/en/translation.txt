Description: On June 10, 2015, around 00:20, a person hears a loud noise coming from outside. From their terrace, the witness observes for approximately 3 minutes a flying object emitting a sound resembling a lawnmower engine. The object is circular, dark in color, and surrounded by a fluorescent green halo. The object comes from the West and heads straight East before disappearing into low clouds on this stormy day. Only one testimony was collected. The description of a fluorescent green flying object making a lawnmower noise suggests a paramotor (motorized paraglider) in flight at night, the sail illuminated by a spotlight behind the pilot (see an example of night flight with frontal lighting). A paramotor club in Manosque was consulted to determine if this practice is common in the region; they responded that night flights in paramotors are not conducted in that area, to their knowledge. These flights are very regulated, permitted only by special dispensation (see the air regulations for free flight) on clear nights and during the nights near the full moon, conditions that were not met on that day (cloudy weather, last quarter). While this hypothesis corresponds quite well with the witness's description, it cannot be confirmed and thus appears extremely unlikely, but not impossible. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of information and cross-referencing.
