--- seg-fa30fd7be1cb37fa0eda0d44 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of July 3, 2013, between 10:00 PM and midnight, several people in Apt (84) and Aix-en-Provence (13) were intrigued by the swift and silent movements of light phenomena in the sky. The night flights of the French Patrol (Patrouille de France) regularly prompt witness reports. A very rapid gendarmerie investigation and official confirmation from the BTA allow us to classify this night flight of the French Patrol as A, perfectly identified. Another observation of this type was made on July 5, 2013 at 10:22 PM in Villeneuve, Alpes-de-Haute-Provence.
