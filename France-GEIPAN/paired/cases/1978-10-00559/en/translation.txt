--- seg-8363b65566af3bf1c7407a2a [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 15, 1978, around 6:50 PM, several people in a vehicle observed two lights and a fixed red light above trees along the roadside. Continuing their journey, they saw an object with several lights, but could not discern its precise shape. No unusual noises were heard during the observation of the object, which appeared stationary at an altitude of approximately 150 meters. By the time they parked to get a better view, the object had disappeared, leaving only a red light visible for a few moments. The object vanished without noise or smoke to the West. A passenger in another car also reported observing a flying object that intrigued them on the same day and road between 6:30 PM and 6:45 PM. No further details were obtained about this phenomenon, for which we lack information.
