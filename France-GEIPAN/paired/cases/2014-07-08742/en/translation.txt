--- seg-172001341dd786cccc06e9b1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 17, 2014, around 00:15, a witness observed in the sky a bright yellow-orange point of light approaching the color of stars, with a very peculiar trajectory (straight line, descent, half turn, ascending zigzag) before disappearing very quickly. No other testimonies were collected. Based on the objective elements described in the investigation report, namely the observation time and duration, the initial and final observation azimuth, the good visibility of the ISIS satellite and the exceptional satellite's apparent magnitude, as well as the "micro-nystagmus" effect (autokinetic illusion), we can conclude that the case concerns the probable observation of the star Arcturus, then the ISIS 1 satellite. However, an important observation point does not correspond to this hypothesis; it could be a confusion or a false memory, but we do not have any compelling evidence to adopt this hypothesis. This case should be classified as "C" due to the lack of overlap allowing to confirm the "ISIS" hypothesis, or to confirm the complex movement of the observed bright point.
