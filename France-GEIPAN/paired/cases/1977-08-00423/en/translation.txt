Description: Following a local press article, two witnesses are interviewed about their observation on August 15, 1977, at approximately 00:05 of a bright, spherical object coming from the southwest. It moves slowly at a low altitude, stops for a few seconds, and then heads northeast at a higher speed. No noise was heard during the several-minute observation. No other testimony about this phenomenon, for which we have limited information, was collected.
