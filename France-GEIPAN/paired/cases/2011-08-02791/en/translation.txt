Summary: Numerous observations in southern France of the atmospheric reentry of a bolide.
--- seg-4047a16075246f58dc3aa18d [needs-review] ---
Description: On Tuesday, August 2, 2011, between 3:27 and 3:35 a.m., several dozen witnesses observed a sudden lighting of the sky as bright as day, followed by an object, often described as green/blue, passing for 4 to 5 seconds. Some witnesses also heard one or two explosions, a few seconds after the passage of the phenomenon. A few reported fragmentation. The fifty witnesses located across a large southern region (Ariège, Aveyron, Aude, Gironde, Haute Garonne, Tarn, Tarn et Garonne) who contacted the GEIPAN provided precise details about this observation. The preliminary data collected indicate a North-South oriented trajectory with a slight shift from East to West. The bolide appears to have ended its course in a triangular area between Andorra, Carcassonne, and Perpignan (see on the GEIPAN website the news "a bolide on August 2 in the sky of southern France and the mapping in .kmz file). Scientists from the IMCCE (Institute of Celestial Mechanics and Ephemeris Calculation) have identified this phenomenon as a bolide. See the article on the REFORME website and the synthesis report of the GEIPAN.
