--- seg-f17b32016bf388dfb999a174 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 22, 2009, around 8:45 PM, two individuals were intrigued by a silent, low-flying yellow sphere in the sky. The witnesses observed the phenomenon as it began to zigzag laterally and rapidly in short bursts, turning orange before quickly disappearing. Only one testimony was collected. Other than the changes in direction, the testimony closely resembles the observation of an atmospheric reentry of satellite debris or a meteoroid, or a festive lantern. We have here an observation of average strangeness, but the consistency of the whole does not allow for a D1 classification. This case is classified as C due to the lack of independent testimonies and cross-referencing.
