--- seg-7d8456bbf211011d4eb5fc5d [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness reports their observation made on April 27, 2012 at 12:20 AM above the Grec and Pérols ponds in Carnon Plage. The yellow glowing phenomenon is in the shape of a crescent moon that is nearly static before moving back and forth. No noise was heard during the 40-minute observation. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon. The direction of the phenomenon indicated by the witness (see witness sketch) is very close to the position of the Moon in the sky (Azimuth of 290°, see astro map of the West sky). The video shows that the observed object is indeed the Moon, occasionally occulted by clouds. The apparent movements of the object towards the West are an optical illusion due to the shifting of the background cloud layer moving towards the East (see weather report). This case is classified "A": observation of the Moon with optical illusion for its movement.
