Summary: Observation of the slow movement of a dark triangle with two white lights: probable observation of an aircraft.
--- seg-cfd1572a59bba81e7fa2ade2 [needs-review] ---
Description: On February 6, 2017, at 7:16 PM, a witness in front of their garage observed a dark triangle with two white lights moving slowly above their house. The observation lasted 30 seconds, not long enough for the witness to take a photograph. Only one testimony was collected. The witness likely observed a low-flying aircraft. Indeed (see attached reconstruction), both the virtual radar (Plane Finder) and the national radar records show the movement of an aircraft at low altitude, either directly above or in the immediate vicinity of the observation location. The national radar record was unfortunately framed beside the observation location, but it shows the end of the aircraft's track heading towards the observation location. Both records note that the aircraft is making "circles" or going back and forth, which makes its observation by the witness possible according to the described heading from East to West. The GEIPAN did not find it necessary or justifiable to investigate the identification of this aircraft, which is anyway not "strange" as it is cooperating with national radars. Therefore, the characteristics of this aircraft are not available for correlation with the observation, but the latter is compatible with an aircraft observation (in many situations, green and red navigation lights are not visible from the ground, and white landing lights or ground-based targeting lights (red) can be dominant). Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of an aircraft.
