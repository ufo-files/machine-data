Description: On March 30, 2009, at 11:50, a witness observed fighter jets in the sky. Suddenly, a loud, metallic clanging noise was heard. The witness then saw a stationary metallic gray-white sphere in the sky. Its size was larger than the jets. The sphere disappeared and reappeared a few seconds later. Frightened, the witness returned home. This poorly documented 2009 observation was submitted late for analysis by the panel of experts working with the GEIPAN. The correlation between the noise described by the witness and the observation is not obvious. Air navigation data is unavailable (military maneuvers? aerial movements?...) although the observation was made along the axis of the nearby air base's runway and it is common for military planes to 'check' flying objects in their airspace. Without a thorough investigation, this case, lacking in substance and with no strange elements, is classified in category "C".
