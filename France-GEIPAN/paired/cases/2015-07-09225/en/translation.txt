--- seg-74f6f5f7a0e715160cb97ece [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: The witness observes each night (without specifying for how long) starting at 9:45 PM a bright point, soon followed by a second, less bright one. These points move slowly together and disappear hidden by the landscape. The long and repeated observation of bright points in the sky obviously suggests a mundane astronomical phenomenon: stars or planets. A check with a sky map indicates Venus and Jupiter exactly in the observation axis of the witness. It is regrettable that the witness, despite being of a high level of university study, did not independently think of an astronomical phenomenon; he was clearly too influenced by TV shows on the topic of UFOs. The GEIPAN classifies this case A: observation of Venus and Jupiter.
