Description: On February 22, 2020, around 9:00 PM, from his home in Saint-Christoly-Medoc, the witness observed a distant, very bright glow, a very sparkling point compared to the stars. To the naked eye, this point appeared fixed and sparkling. Using binoculars and the phone's zoom, a diamond-shaped object with a golden color became visible. The light intensified before fading and blending into the night. The observation lasted approximately 45 minutes. This case is consistent with photos, videos, and a reconstruction image that allow for positioning the PAN during the observation (see the investigation report). The investigation concluded that the observed object was the planet Venus. Present in the observation direction at the specified time: Venus was the brightest object in the night sky that evening. The peculiarities perceived by the witness include: 
- The particular shape observed with the binoculars and phone zoom is explained by the optical and imaging effect of a poor focus, a focusing issue.
- The disappearance of the phenomenon is likely caused by a high-altitude cloud layer progressively occulting it, giving the impression of "melting" into the night sky. 
This observation is classified as A, observation of the planet Venus.
