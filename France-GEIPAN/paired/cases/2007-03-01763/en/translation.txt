Summary: Observation of the passage of an aircraft.
--- seg-f98f42b17169e8dc06d1c34b [needs-review] ---
Description: During the night of March 30 to 31, 2007, between 8:00 PM and 2:00 AM near Dompaire (88), four young witnesses observed several white and red lights in the stormy, black sky. They formed a metallic triangle that moved back and forth and at varying speeds before passing at a distance of approximately 15 to 20 meters above their vehicle. The observation ended around 2:00 AM. The witnesses were strongly impressed by this observation. During the investigation, an analysis of air traffic in the area indicated the landing of a cargo plane in Epinal at 1:58 AM and confirmed a flight crew training for an MD 90 (DC9) of the Swiss company Hello AG during the witnesses' observation times: the plane, coming from Zurich, performed 30 "Touch and Go" maneuvers around Epinal's runway in the afternoon after detecting a technical issue, then repeated these same rotations between 7:47 PM and 9:16 PM; the takeoff for a final departure to Zurich occurred at 9:45 PM. This type of training is regular on runways with low traffic, and Hello AG is a frequent user of Epinal's runway. It is likely that, given the state of mind of the witnesses, the bright point observed at 2:00 AM was mistaken for a streetlamp or car light, as has happened several times during their travels, as they themselves explain. There is no doubt that the observation made by the group of friends was that of the Hello AG company's DC9 during training exercises: the witnesses were always under the plane's flight path in unusual conditions around the Epinal-Mirecourt airport.
