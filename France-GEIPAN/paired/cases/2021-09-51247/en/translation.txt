Summary: Nighttime observation in the sky of a very bright, static "cigar," then very slowly moving before disappearing behind a tree: observation of an airplane, flight TO4159.
--- seg-2fc51a8c99d4a76ed267c9af [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 4, 2021, at approximately 11:06 PM, like every night before bed, a resident of SEILHAC (19) observed through the window of his bathroom, the Big Bear, directly above his garage. Upon opening the window, his gaze immediately fell upon a very bright "cigar" in a horizontal position. White in color, with a slight red flash in the middle. The witness thought it was an airplane, continued observing, and quickly noticed that this "cigar" was static and fixed. It didn't move for "a good minute," then started moving very, very slowly until disappearing behind a tree. The witness estimates the duration of the phenomenon at around 1 minute and 30 seconds. Often observing stars at night, especially shooting stars, the witness claims to be able to distinguish between airplanes, satellites, and bolides...He admits being overcome with joy at having observed this "bright cigar," thinking it might have been a OVNI. The witness observed the passage of a commercial airliner: flight TO4159 from Oran to Paris (see the investigation report). The description of the PAN is typical of an airplane observation. At the time indicated by the witness, a Transavia commercial airliner is located exactly at the position reported for the PAN. The significant distance of the plane is perfectly consistent with the low angular height of the PAN. Moreover, due to perspective, an airplane at this distance and so close to the horizon appears static. This explains the perception of the immobility of the PAN for "a good minute." This is what created the strangeness. The testimony is of good consistency: unique witness, no photo of the PAN, but a fairly complete testimony, and we have radar charts of aircraft trajectories. The case is classified A, observation of an airplane, flight TO4159.
