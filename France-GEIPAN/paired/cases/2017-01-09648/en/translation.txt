Description: A witness reports successive observations on January 6th and 7th, and then on February 25th and 26th, 2017: they observe colorful phenomena in motion in the sky or near the ground. The observations are lengthy (from 10:30 PM until sunrise). The gendarmes on the scene find nothing. Only one testimony is collected (through a questionnaire and phone calls). The testimony describes great oddity: the observations occur almost every night (with or without clouds) at ground level (or even on the ground) both in front of and behind the trees. There are mainly lights, but there can also be something like a craft. Noises in the sky and tree creaking are also noted. Computer photos can also move or reveal the witness's name in the trees. The reported oddity is such that the GEIPAN wishes to have the testimony of other witnesses present at the scene. However, the witness's spouse does not want to testify. The witness's friends promise to do so but do not, whether it's the one the GEIPAN was able to reach by phone in the presence of the initial witness, or another one reached separately. These two friends describe over the phone significant elements of oddity that, while of similar nature (lights in the sky and on the ground), only partially overlap between witnesses. The gendarmes present one night notice nothing, but the witness considers the phenomenon less intense that night (but still present). The refusal to testify from three witnesses greatly weakens the consistency of this testimony. Its weakness is too great to validate the strange character of a single witness's observation. Consequently, the testimony is classified as unusable in C: lack of reliable information. The fact that the gendarmes' observation portion is at odds with the main witness's perception only exacerbates the already established lack of consistency.
