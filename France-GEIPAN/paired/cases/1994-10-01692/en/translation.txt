Summary: Observation from an airplane and on the ground of a probable atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-230d51e0803eae747fa725e0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 28, 1994, at 6:57 PM, the pilot of a DORNIER 228 aircraft reported unidentified traffic at 10,000 feet, describing it as an intense light with a significant yellow-orange trail moving at a very high speed to pass above the aircraft in an east-west direction. The co-pilot confirmed the phenomenon, although the duration estimate differed (12 seconds to 1 minute). Two other ground witnesses also reported the passage of a bright light for about ten seconds. Air traffic was perfectly known and cannot explain the phenomenon. The most likely hypothesis is that of an atmospheric reentry.
