Description: On Sunday, March 20, 2011, between 9:45 PM and 10:00 PM, several family members observed the sky and were intrigued by the presence of two particularly bright and moving stars. One witness then observed through binoculars two triangular shapes with blinking orange lights. The two objects moved at a constant speed in the sky along a straight trajectory from North to East. No noise was heard during the observation. No other testimonies about this phenomenon were reported. The gendarmerie investigation highlights the thesis of a mid-air refueling operation of military aircraft that operated in the area described by the witness.
