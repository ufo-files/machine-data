Description: According to the Gendarmerie Procedural Report, on August 10, 2020, around 11:30 PM, the witness, in their living room, observed "a very bright, large light." They went onto their balcony, and the light dimmed slowly. A short time later, they made a similar observation but positioned further to the left than the first PAN seen. According to the Technical Questionnaire, the observation of a powerful, white, fixed light occurred on August 9th around midnight. The light went out and then relit a little further away. The observation lasted 10 minutes. No other testimonies were collected. The consistency is poor for several reasons: - a major inconsistency between the two information sources regarding the observation date, which could not be resolved (August 10th in the PV and August 9th in the questionnaire) - limited exploitable information, both in the PV and the questionnaire. The hypothesis of observing the comet Neowise was examined. This comet could under no circumstances appear as the witness describes it, and even less at two different locations in such a short time frame. The description of the PAN resembles satellite flashes (a bright and brief light from the PAN of white color, repeating twice at two distinct locations in the sky). For the date of August 10, 2020, the Operational Space Surveillance Center (COSE) of the CNES identified several Starlink satellites in view from the witness's position. Nothing corresponded for the date of August 9, 2020 (see the COSE Analysis file). Due to the presence of an inconsistency regarding the observation date that could not be resolved, and the absence of certain important data, this case is classified as C: Lack of Information.
