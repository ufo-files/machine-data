Description: On September 2, 2013, at 12:45, a witness was intrigued by the silent movement of an object in the sky. He made a few turns and the object appeared round and oblong depending on the viewing angle during these turns. Given the available information in the investigation report, namely the presence of a nearby airfield offering gyrocopter flights, the absence of non-mandatory lighting signaling on gyrocopters, the compatible shape and color, and the largely compatible movements and speeds, we can conclude that the PAN observed by the witness is likely a gyrocopter in ascent phase. This testimony is of average consistency: fairly imprecise with many inaccuracies, coming from a single witness without a photo. The observation is not particularly strange as it could be a rather mundane object. This case is classified as "B" for a probable observation of a gyrocopter.
