Summary: Repeated appearances and disappearances of a luminous phenomenon composed of white orbs positioned on a bar: mistaken for a night flight of the French Patrol.
--- seg-127df868d41db7a1f38f0ae6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of Thursday, July 22, 2021, between 10:00 PM and 10:30 PM, in Lambesc (13), a witness, accompanied by a friend, spotted in the sky an unusual brightness composed of "6 white balls" arranged on a bar. The witness initially thought it was a constellation of satellites but then realized it was too large. The phenomenon, appearing close to the witness, disappeared and re-appeared twice more, this time farther away. The witness then took photos with his Samsung, but they were unusable. After a third appearance, heading east as always, the phenomenon did not re-appear. Only one testimony was collected at GEIPAN. The Salon-de-Provence base, which houses the French Patrol (Patrouille de France), as well as the radar trace map of the Air Force of the CNOA, confirm this flight on July 22 around 10:00 PM/10:30 PM, which remains a surprising spectacle for those not aware of this occasional nocturnal flight specificity. It is this night flight, which only occurs once a year, that surprised and prompted the witness to report his observation to GEIPAN (see the investigation report). The case is well-documented. The precise testimony, the elements it provides, leave no doubt. The maps obtained via Flightradar and the CNOA, the response from the French Patrol, other media testimonies, confirm that it is a nocturnal flight of the French Patrol. GEIPAN classifies the case as "A": Misidentification with a French Patrol night flight.
