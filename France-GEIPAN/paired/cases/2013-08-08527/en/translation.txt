Description: On August 5, 2013, between 11:00 PM and 11:20 PM, a witness observed in the stormy sky the movement of 5 amber-colored lights, appearing to flicker. Their movement was rapid. The lights turned to the left and disappeared from the witness's view. A few seconds later, a new light appeared and followed the same path. This testimony is of high quality, best describing the positions of the observed objects. The witness's description is entirely consistent with the observation of Thai lanterns, quite common on summer nights in cities: orange balls flying continuously. The objects, observed in South-South-East, moved towards East. However, the wind direction, Northwest, does not exactly match the observed trajectory; local perturbations due to the Moselle, very close, and the hill of Plappeville just west of the witness could be considered. Wind gusting in Metz: Northwest 16 to 30 km/h: weather. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
