Description: On April 20th or 27th, 2008, at 8:44 PM, a motorist took a photograph from his vehicle of a luminous phenomenon that intrigued him. The photograph was submitted to GEIPAN through an indirect witness. The provided photograph shows a very probable reflection of the sun in the windshield and/or camera lens, as well as water droplets on the windshield illuminated by the sun. This type of photo highlighting "flare" phenomena and various reflections does not present any strange characteristics. The testimony is too brief, and the direct witness did not express himself to describe what he saw with his own eyes. This case is classified "C" due to lack of information: no original photo, no direct testimony.
