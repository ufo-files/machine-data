Description: On September 29, 2016, at 8:15 PM, a witness in their garden was intrigued by fluffy masses spinning rapidly and silently in a cloudy sky. The witness called their partner and neighbor, who confirmed the same observation. The PAN spun in a counterclockwise direction. The phenomenon disappeared behind the clouds at 11:00 PM. It was not bright enough to be photographed. Two witness accounts of this phenomenon were collected. It is very likely that this was laser beams, emanating from a nightclub or a special event, reflecting off the cloud layer or a fine mist (both of which were present that evening). The witness themselves confirmed this origin as they occasionally saw the light beam rising from the ground towards the location in the sky where the "strange" discs had previously been. Neighbors of the witnesses reported seeing spotlights. Indeed, depending on the conditions of the air layer, one can see the patterns created (on the natural screen) or just the beams alone, without the patterns, or both, somewhat like in a movie theater, imagining cigarette smoke (visible beams) in conjunction or not with the presence of the screen (patterns present or not). According to atmospheric conditions, the distance a laser can affect varies greatly: it can reach dozens of kilometers, making the phenomenon fleeting or exceptional at great distances from the emission source. GEIPAN did not identify the source of the beam, but has several similar cases with identified effects. Consequently, GEIPAN classifies this case as B: pink sky or bright spots.
