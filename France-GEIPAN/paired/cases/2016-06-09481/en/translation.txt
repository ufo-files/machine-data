Description: On Monday, June 6, 2016, around 9:30 PM, a witness observed for more than 10 minutes a large, cloudy white point with a white trail. When viewed through binoculars, this object appeared to be composed of several dozen smaller objects. The white point eventually faded and disappeared completely. The witness's description does not inherently seem unusual and could correspond to one or more airplanes. In fact, this was the witness's initial interpretation of the phenomenon, and there was a close conjunction of airplanes heading SSE. However, the GEIPAN (presumably a GEIPAN investigator) was unable to pursue this or any other hypothesis further because the witness did not respond to requests for additional information (such as the height of the PAN in the sky) or to visit the gendarmerie to file a statement under the protocol between the gendarmerie and the GEIPAN. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as C: Lack of Information.
