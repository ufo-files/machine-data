Summary: Observation of a flying object at low altitude.
--- seg-301e5d3b0fc614de1bb252a3 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 3, 1981, around 10:55 PM, a witness observed for approximately 40 seconds, at a distance of less than 400 meters, a flying object moving very slowly and close to the ground. As it passed, a halo of light illuminated the landscape. The object had a rounded shape facing forward and emitted a bright flame of several colors. The object moved on an east-west trajectory with a slight tilt towards the ground. Fearing that it might collide with the mountain, the witness expected to hear an impact noise, but nothing happened. During the investigation, no additional information was gathered to provide an explanation for this observation.
