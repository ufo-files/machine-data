Summary: Multiple observations of the movement in formation of luminous phenomena: flight of the French Patrol.
--- seg-efa777446c3da2ae5623dc0f [needs-review] ---
Description: On the 2nd and 4th of July 2008, between 10:15 PM and 10:45 PM, several witnesses in the Apt region of Vaucluse observed unusual movements of light phenomena. Lights moved in formation, appearing and disappearing over a 20-minute period. No sounds were heard during the observations. One witness filmed the phenomenon on the 4th of July, while another took photographs. The investigation revealed that these individuals witnessed the nighttime flight of the French Patrol (Patrouille de France) practicing in the sky over Apt on the evenings of the 2nd and 4th of July between Saint-Saturnin-Les-Avignons and Fontaine-de-Vaucluse. The timing of the testimonies aligns with the flight schedules of this squadron (on the 2nd of July, for a rehearsal of a ceremony that took place on the 4th of July). The aircraft of the Patrouille de France are Alpha Jets, which are equipped with a light on the front of the aircraft, illuminated during flight displays to ensure visibility of the planes. On those evenings, the Patrouille de France conducted waiting circuits in the affected region, forming in groups of 8 aircraft prior to the display, then 4 aircraft prior to initiating their approach procedure on the Salon landing strip, confirming the reported observations. This case is featured in a special news section on the site.
