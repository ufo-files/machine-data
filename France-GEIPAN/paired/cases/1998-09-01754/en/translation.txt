Summary: Observation of the movement of a helium-filled balloon in the shape of a ladybug.
--- seg-a5a7fc856311e01560039991 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 6, 1998, around 8:00 PM, witnesses in their garden spotted a circular object above a tree on a neighboring property. According to the witnesses, the object was seen in varying sizes and colors. However, several people noticed that the object seemed to have legs. Initially stationary above the tree, the object later moved slowly across the sky. One witness was able to film it for several minutes before it completely disappeared. A few days later, a person, alerted by the press, testified to the gendarmerie about the flight of a child's helium-filled balloon shaped and colored like a ladybug with large legs. This balloon, poorly attached to his son's wrist, slowly flew away in the same sector and at the time the witnesses saw the strange object.
