Description: Recent and concise testimony (August 28, 2009) of an old observation. In the year 1965 or 1966, probably in August (the witness remains vague about the date), several people observe, on multiple mornings in a row, a stationary, metallic, ball-like object located to the East in the sky near a demolished chimney. No noise is heard during observations of this phenomenon, which eventually disappears. No other testimony about this phenomenon is collected, and we lack information about it.
