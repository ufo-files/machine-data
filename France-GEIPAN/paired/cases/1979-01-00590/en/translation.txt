--- seg-e8e091cf8dcfd4dd546b4066 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 11, 1979, around 6:45 a.m., a witness sees from a window an object that intrigues him. Above a road, an oval object moves silently. This object has six fixed lights in red and green at its bottom. In the center, a yellow light emits a bright light. The object moves from East to West at a slow speed. According to the investigation, the witness saw a twin-engine commercial airliner, FBOOM, that landed at 6:42 a.m. and took off again at 6:59 a.m. from the nearby Brive airport.
