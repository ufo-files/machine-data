--- seg-8930354832ff376f2b96188b [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 1, 2008, around 11:50 PM, several people observed a low-altitude, brief luminescent phenomenon in the sky. A circular object of color moved horizontally in an east-west direction. This sphere then took a descending trajectory and disappeared in a burst of light seen by some witnesses. No ground traces were found. These people witnessed what was likely an atmospheric reentry.
