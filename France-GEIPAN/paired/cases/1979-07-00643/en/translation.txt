--- seg-bab7afc60ad7a4a1cafabc22 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 20, 1979, around 10 a.m., several people observed in the sky for approximately twenty minutes a peculiar bright object. According to the witnesses' locations, this ovoid object measured between 80 cm and 5 meters in diameter and had a metallic or black color. It was initially stationary above a house, then rose and gradually moved away without making any noise. According to the gendarmerie, no unusual observations were reported from airports or bases in the region, and no public advertising flights were conducted during this period. The witnesses' descriptions of the object and its movement, however, remain very similar to those of a balloon.
