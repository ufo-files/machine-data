--- seg-a43f413f7cb1fd7ece4d78ec [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 14, 2011, between 3:50 PM and 4:00 PM, a witness filmed the movements of a black point in a cloudy sky: when strongly zooming in with his camcorder, a dark object appears. Watching the video strongly suggests the movement of a helium-filled balloon. The observed direction of movement is consistent with a southeasterly wind. Of low strangeness and average consistency, this observation case is certainly the result of a confusion with a festive balloon filled with helium. This case is classified as A.
