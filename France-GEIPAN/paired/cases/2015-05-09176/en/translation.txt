Description: On May 7, 2015, around 9:45 PM, two people were intrigued by a luminous phenomenon in the sky. Bright lights (white, red, and orange) seem to rotate around a craft. No noise is heard during the movements. A verification of air traffic in the direction of the witness's observation by the FlightRadar24 site indicates that a Ryanair flight on descent, at an altitude of approximately 2000m, was approaching from the North (heading Southeast) and at 9:40 PM executed a 90-degree turn to the Northeast to head towards Strasbourg Airport (see FlightRadar screenshots attached). After this change in direction, it therefore appeared "from behind" to the observers, thus almost stationary since its distance is hard to perceive. The witness will however perceive this distance at the end of the observation. This movement is absolutely consistent with the testimony: "It arrived from the N-E at a fairly high speed and suddenly went into level flight." During this flight phase, the aircraft's lights are multiple: very powerful landing lights, but directed forward, fixed position lights in green, red, and white, and especially two types of flashing lights: red anti-collision lights, and white strobe flashes. These two types of lights blink approximately once per second but are not synchronized, sometimes giving the impression of a chain of blinking lights. The silence described by the witnesses is not surprising: the aircraft was 15 or 20 km away, the engines are at low power during this descent phase and therefore not very loud; in addition, the witness's environment does not seem very quiet: the television is on and a significant background noise can be heard on the video. The UFO GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the Ryanair flight 5061 plane.
