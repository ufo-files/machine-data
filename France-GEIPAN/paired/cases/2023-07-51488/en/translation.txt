Description: On July 4, 2023, around 4:25 PM, while on vacation at Cavalaire-sur-Mer (83), witness (T1) observes a elongated shape moving slowly in the sky. The observation is shared with his spouse, son, and daughter, but only T1 completes a Technical Questionnaire. Photos of the PAN are also sent to GEIPAN. Of low strangeness and good consistency* (multiple witnesses though only one provides a testimony, multiple photos), this PAN is identified as a helium balloon, most likely in the shape of a shark (see investigation note). A quick online search reveals that this type of balloon is available for sale, with dimensions reaching up to 2m in length. Furthermore, during the summer season and near the coast, conditions are particularly favorable for using this recreational object. By zooming in on a photo, T1 confirms the resemblance to a shark. Additionally, the trajectory of the PAN is consistent with the direction of the wind present at the time of observation. The case is classified as A: Helium balloon type "Shark". *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony.
