Description: On November 25, 2016, at 6:03 PM, a driver was intrigued by the movement of a red light. He thought it was a motorized vehicle ascending a hill. But the light gained altitude above the Gergovie plateau before disappearing. No other witnesses were reported. The described elements suggest a release of a Thai lantern. The trajectory is consistent with the wind measured in Aulnat on the Infoclimat record. This wind is highly variable but is directed towards the West at 6:00 PM, thus capable of lifting the lantern towards the plateau. The weekend is often more conducive to such a playful or festive event, as long as it is already a little dark. This is the case at 6:00 PM (the witness described other lights in the falling darkness). There may have been other releases of lanterns later that the witness did not have the opportunity to see. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: observation of a Thai lantern.
