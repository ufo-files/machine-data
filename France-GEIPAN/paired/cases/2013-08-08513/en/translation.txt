Summary: Observation of a silent passage in the sky of an orange ball: lack of information.
--- seg-e9dacb3f22f66e317a1b535b [needs-review] ---
Description: On the 1st of August 2013 at 0:40, two individuals were intrigued by the presence above a tree of a flickering orange ball that then ascended into the clear sky and was hidden by a neighboring building. No unusual noises were heard. Only one statement was collected. The witness's description perfectly aligns with the observation of a Thai lantern, fairly common in summer nights in cities: an orange ball crossing the sky from West to East continuously, and dimming at the end. However, the wind direction, North, thus perpendicular to the observed trajectory, does not allow this hypothesis to be confirmed. The wind is very light (1-4 km/h), so one might consider a local breeze oriented differently. Without very local meteorological information, this case is classified "C" due to a lack of data.
