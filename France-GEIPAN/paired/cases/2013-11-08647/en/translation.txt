Description: On Tuesday, November 26, 2013, around 5:30 PM, a witness is intrigued by the presence in the sky of lights with a particular shape. The phenomenon of elongated shapes, apparently stationary in the setting sky (see witness photo), clearly resembles illuminated airplane contrails (the sun set at 5:00 PM at an azimuth of 240° SO-SE). See the Flightradar log which shows a potential candidate aircraft for this contrail. See: Strange Contrails These contrails are visible up to 80 km in clear weather: at this distance, the angular motion of the aircraft appears very slight, especially if it is moving away from or towards the witness. When the sun is too far below the horizon, the unlit contrails become invisible. The GEIPAN classifies this case as an "A" observation of airplane contrails.
