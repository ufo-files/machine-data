Summary: Observation of a red ball with sudden disappearance: probable observation of the front projector of an airplane seen face-on.
--- seg-d68b2a26468fd608a9535d78 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 23, 2011, around 11:30 PM, a driver noticed in the sky a large ball with a color leaning towards red. He stopped and took a picture of the ball just before it suddenly disappeared. The witness's description corresponds to the observation of a face-on view of an airplane coming from the East (possibly the Nice-Toulouse flight that lands at Blagnac around 9:55 PM): facing the airplane, the observer sees the airplane's front beacon as a stationary point (the flashing lights are not seen because the front beacon blinds), if the airplane slightly changes its course, for example to join the Blagnac runway axis, the light point moves laterally, then disappears because the witness is no longer in the axis of the beacons. This hypothesis is retained by the GEIPAN for this case classified as "B": probable observation of an airplane from the front.
