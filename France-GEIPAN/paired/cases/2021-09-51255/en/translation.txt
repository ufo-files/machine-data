Description: On September 3, 2021, around 6:30 PM, the witness spotted a small black dot located at an extremely high altitude in the sky. It appeared stationary, without reflections or other lights. The sky behind this black dot was clear; no clouds were present in the background at that time. The shape seemed rather round, of a "metal black" (very dark) color, and positioned to the southwest from the witness's perspective. The object seemed to appear suddenly, leaving no trace or smoke behind. The witness observed it for at least 30 seconds. Then, the object appeared to move towards the west at a very low speed. The consistency* was good, with a single witness who had thoroughly completed the questionnaire: no photos or videos of the PAN, but the witness reconstructed the scene on a photograph. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The investigation (see the investigation report) revealed that the witness very likely observed a weather balloon (which was, incidentally, one of his hypotheses). This conclusion is based on the following elements: - The shape of the PAN was round, consistent with that of a weather balloon,
- A possible dark color due to the observation being taken in contra-daylight,
- Slow movement in the direction of the wind.

We can provide some explanations for the peculiarities perceived by the witness:
- Sudden appearance, disappearance: the PAN is very small and at the limit of visual perception,
- Perceptible absence of movement: perceptual illusion related to the presence of clouds nearby. The PAN appears stationary while the clouds are moving, when in reality the opposite is happening.

Classification as "A": Observation of a weather balloon.
