Description: On September 15, 2013, at 4 a.m., a driver observed an intermittent light in the sky, followed by the passage of a dark oval mass with "small integrated glowing disks" above her vehicle. No other witnesses were collected. The witness reported her observation in May 2014. Based on the case file: - The appearance is not consistent with that of a helicopter. - The appearance is, in principle, consistent with disco laser lights (from Saturday to Sunday), but there are no elements to confirm this hypothesis. We can conclude that the PAN observed by the witness is of unknown nature and origin. This case should be classified as 'C' due to the lack of information to verify hypotheses that are fairly compatible with the observation. This testimony is fairly consistent: quite precise but from a single witness without a photo. The observation is strange because it involves a phenomenon with an uncommon appearance, although its movement itself is common.
