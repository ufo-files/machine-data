Summary: Observations of a fixed white light phenomenon over two successive nights: reflection of public lighting on an antenna.
--- seg-224de2a54ed8cd676a7b1c37 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 7, 2013 at 1 a.m., a witness is intrigued by a fixed white light phenomenon in the sky. He describes a white glowing point with a horizontal glowing trail and below it a curved pink shape. No particular noise is heard. He makes the same observation the following night. The GEIPAN dispatched a person to observe the surroundings of the village at night in order to detect a possible fixed light, without success. Eventually, it was the witness himself, with a neighbor, who meticulously observed the phenomenon and noticed it was a reflection of public lighting on an antenna. See the photos: one rather dark (6730) corresponds roughly to what the witness saw with the naked eye, and the other (6738), with a longer exposure, reveals the origin of the phenomenon. This peculiar case is therefore classified "A" as perfectly identified.
