Description: An amateur astronomer reports his observation from August 18, 2007: around 10:15 PM, while observing the stars, he notices with the naked eye a bright point moving from East to West at a constant speed. After 30 seconds of observation, he decides to record a video with his camcorder. He first films at maximum zoom (x20), then zooms out to better track the bright point. The witness is intrigued by this same phenomenon that he will observe two more times in August 2007. The witness observes a bright point that moves, he takes his camera and films. The phenomenon's movements on the witness's video (very little exploitable) correspond to the camera's movements, the diamond shape is the shape of the camera's diaphragm. It could be a plane bound for Blagnac, seen from the front, landing lights on, but the witness did not answer the additional questions posed for the investigation. After being classified "C" due to lack of information, this case was re-examined. On August 18 at 10:15 PM, there was a passage of the International Space Station (ISS) visible from Gimont. The GEIPAN now classifies the case as "A": observation of the International Space Station (ISS).
