Description: Two people are watching from their homes, the landscape and flashes of a dry storm. They are intrigued by the presence of a light gray cylindrical object between the clouds and the roofs of the houses. The stationary object suddenly disappears. The observation lasted less than a minute. This observation case is poorly documented, the observation is short, and reported late. However, two hypotheses can be considered: a cylindrical balloon that suddenly burst, or a complex atmospheric phenomenon related to the storm (plasma). The GEIPAN cannot confirm either of these hypotheses. Without information, the GEIPAN classifies the case as "C", a case not presenting a great degree of strangeness.
