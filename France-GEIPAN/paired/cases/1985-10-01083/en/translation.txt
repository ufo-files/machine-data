Description: On October 8, 1985, witnesses will observe peculiar luminous phenomena. Despite the stormy weather and lightning present at the beginning of the evening, one witness sees four times in succession two beams of blinding light: the light source appears to be located 1 km from his home, above a group of trees, and positioned on a straight line. Two other witnesses will see red, blue, and white lights appear three times in succession. No other information could be gathered about this phenomenon.
