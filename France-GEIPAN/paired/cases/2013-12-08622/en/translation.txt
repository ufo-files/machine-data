Description: On December 14, 2013 at 3:00 AM, a driver observed a very fast passing in the sky of a silver fireball that disappeared towards the N-E. No other witnesses have come forward. The observation of a very bright fireball, crossing the sky in less than a second, suggests a bolide (large meteor) phenomenon caused by the atmospheric entry of a fragment of a comet, or asteroid. December 14 is a date during the peak activity of the Geminid meteor shower: the BOAM observer network recorded dozens of them that night. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a bolide, from the Geminid shower.
