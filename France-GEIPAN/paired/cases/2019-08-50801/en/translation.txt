Description: On the night of August 7, 2019, starting at 11:28 PM and lasting over one and a half hours, two individuals (observing with the naked eye and binoculars) witnessed strange light phenomena in the night sky. Six mobile phone videos were recorded. Only one statement was collected. With average strangeness and consistency (two witnesses, videos of PAN, but the statement was vague), this case appears to be a probable misidentification with the star Arcturus, at least for the third PAN. The other two PAN cannot be identified due to a lack of data (see the investigation report). The visual aspect of the third PAN strongly resembles Arcturus, with a color and brightness that match very well. The rapid color changes of the PAN are quite typical of the twinkling of this star. The observation direction of the PAN is entirely consistent with Arcturus. Only the reported rapid movements and "medusa" shape by the witness seem inconsistent with this hypothesis, but the very long observation duration implies that the PAN could not have moved rapidly. Furthermore, the medusa shape is likely due to the poor optics of the binoculars, as it does not appear in the videos. The case is classified A for the third PAN, a very probable misidentification with the star Arcturus. The first two PAN cannot be identified or utilized due to a lack of data.
