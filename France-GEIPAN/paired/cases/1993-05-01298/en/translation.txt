Summary: Observations of a slow-moving red ball in the sky: lack of information.
--- seg-16770fdb23b0fa152d4bb4ab [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 8, 1993, around 10:30 PM, three witnesses observed in the sky a large, red, glowing ball that moved silently and slowly, emitting rays of the same color all around it. After 2 to 3 minutes, the ball quickly disappeared, becoming only a small, glowing point in the clear sky. No other testimony was collected, and no further details about the phenomenon could be gathered by the gendarmerie. The case is classified as C: lack of information.
