--- seg-58196dd7db57c61693468d67 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 14th and 15th, 2009, witnesses made successive observations of a blue light moving in the sky. The first observation on November 14th was publicized, and other individuals came forward with similar observations in northern communities at Buironfosse (02) and Beaurieux (59). Witnesses described a blue light in the sky with a rounded shape moving slowly from right to left (although they did not agree on the distance or origin of the phenomenon). A third observation on November 21st was identified by gendarmes as a spotlight from one of the region's nightclubs. This plausible hypothesis likely explains the observations of November 14th and 15th, 2009.
