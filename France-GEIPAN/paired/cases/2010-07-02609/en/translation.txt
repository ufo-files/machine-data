Summary: Observation of the movement of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-8c1428f2a9dba2731bcc21dc [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Around 1:30 AM on July 25, 2010, two individuals observed from their home the movement of numerous flying objects in the sky. These objects, which were either triangular or round (according to testimonies) and two-colored, passed over their house on a straight trajectory. No noise was heard during the observations. After checking with the village mayor's office, it was confirmed that the night of July 24-25 had been marked by celebrations in the commune, with a high point consisting of a release of Thai lanterns at the time specified by the witnesses (as well as by other individuals who contacted the mayor's office).
