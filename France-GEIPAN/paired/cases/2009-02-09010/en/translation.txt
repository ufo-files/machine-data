Description: On February 9, 2009, between 8:00 PM and 10:00 PM, several people witnessed a frightening light phenomenon. Only one statement was collected describing the observation of a large, multi-colored light stationary in the sky. No sounds were heard during this two-hour observation. A similar observation occurred on February 5, 2009, also in this same commune. Of low strangeness and average consistency (brief statement, photo of very poor quality), this case presents the characteristics of an astronomical misperception (static light object for two hours). Several consistent details suggest the possibility of a misperception with the planet Venus, but the lack of concrete information, particularly the direction of observation, prevents formal identification of the PAN. This case is classified B, probable astronomical misperception, with the privileged hypothesis being that of the planet Venus.
