Summary: Hardened brown earth circle that does not absorb water.
--- seg-4cb93a3f5f50593e2570fcec [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 7, 1986, a person comes forward to testify about the presence of a hardened earth circle in a chicken coop. This circle, with a diameter of 3.28 feet (1.10m), appeared in early June. Within a 13.12-inch (0.40m) crown, the earth does not absorb water, and vegetation is absent or nearly so. Additionally, a tingling sensation is felt when entering the circle. Two other witnesses who saw a bright white light column in June are also interviewed as part of the investigation. The phenomenon resembles what is known as "witches' circles," likely composed of mycelium preventing any vegetation. This hypothesis does not explain the tingling sensations reported by the witnesses, which are otherwise unverified.
