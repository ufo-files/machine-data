Description: On June 4, 2023, the witness was at the window of their third-floor apartment building in Sceaux. At 9:29 PM, a dark, round object appeared, moving relatively quickly and steadily but not very high. This object moved in a straight line for about a minute before disappearing behind a building. The witness observed what appeared to be "flames," possibly reflections on a metallic surface. The witness captured a video with their smartphone. This case is considered consistent: one witness with a usable video. The appearance of the PAN in the video quickly led to the hypothesis of a mylar balloon (see the investigation report and video). Analysis of the video allowed the trajectory of the PAN to be determined using easily identifiable visual landmarks. It was found that the balloon followed a generally North-South trajectory, not North/West - East as the witness described. This trajectory is consistent with the wind direction at that time (20° from NNE to SSW). The "flames" described by the witness can be explained by the setting sun's rays reflecting off the balloon's mylar, as it rotated and was carried by the wind. The balloon's "flattened round shape with a dip" justifies the irregular appearance of the rays. The witness very likely observed a mylar balloon carried by the wind, approximately 100 meters away. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: very probable observation of a mylar balloon.
