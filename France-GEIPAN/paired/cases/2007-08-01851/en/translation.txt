Description: On August 28, 2007, between 7:00 and 7:10 PM, two individuals observed a peculiar flying object for 3 or 4 minutes from their terrace. The object was round and flat with a transparent dome. It rose while swaying and moved from East to West. The underside of the object appeared dark. No noise was heard. No other testimonies about this phenomenon were collected. After investigation, it appears that the witnesses likely saw a type of circuflex kite.
