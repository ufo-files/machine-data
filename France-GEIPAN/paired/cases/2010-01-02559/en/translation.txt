Description: A witness reports their observation made on January 21, 2010, around 5:15 PM. While driving on National Road 524 towards Eauze, they spotted above a hill a spherical object appearing metallic with a dark gray color that seems to be composed of hexagons. The object appears stationary but is oscillating from side to side. It disappears instantly after 10 to 15 seconds of observation. No other testimonies were collected. This case is of medium to high strangeness due to the peculiar appearance of the object and its instant disappearance. However, given the imprecision of the exact observation location, the very short observation duration, and the uniqueness of the testimony, this case appears to have a consistency of medium to low quality. This case is classified C due to a lack of information and cross-checking.
