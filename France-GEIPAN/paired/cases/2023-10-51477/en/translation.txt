Description: On October 9, 2023, around 5:20 PM, the witness observed a rainbow-colored sphere in the sky to the right of the sun. The witness specifies, "I was in the street in a car, facing the observation, I specify that I saw the rainbow sphere even when getting out of the car (it doesn't come from the windows)." Upon exiting their vehicle, through this direct visual observation, without any propagation through the windshield, the witness had a very good reflex. They also took 2 photos. The photos sent by the witness allow for the conclusion of the observation of an iridescent cloud. Refer to the "Sky Misinterpretations" website: the atmospheric irization phenomenon is quite rare. In mid-level clouds, the propagation of sunlight can create a dispersion of colors and result in a beautiful coloration, the entire solar spectrum coloration, that is, the colors of the rainbow. Iridescent clouds form up to 30 degrees from the sun or the moon. It is also sometimes possible to observe this phenomenon in condensation trails of an airplane, or even in those of a rocket during its ascent. The case is classified as A: observation of an iridescent cloud.
