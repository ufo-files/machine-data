--- seg-f5571380522036328b6cfbfd [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 9, 1977, at approximately 7:15 PM, two military personnel on duty observed in the sky the maneuvers of an oval-shaped object moving north-south. The object rose and fell at a slow pace, leaving a glowing trail behind it. No sound was heard during the observation, which lasted between 5 and 7 minutes. A similar observation had been reported a few years earlier: at that time, it was determined to be weather balloons launched from the Istres Air Base (13).
