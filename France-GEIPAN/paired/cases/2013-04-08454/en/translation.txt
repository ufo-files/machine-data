Description: On April 1, 2013, around 9:15 PM, two individuals from their home were intrigued by the slow and silent movement of an orange-colored glowing ball that then moved away more quickly and disappeared instantly. The very detailed account of this observation, "a yellow-orange ball, with no trail," moving in the direction of the wind from the North (see Meteociel report on Vichy), clearly suggests the movement of a Thai lantern (flying lamp), probably released during a celebration for this holiday (Easter Monday). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a Thai lantern.
