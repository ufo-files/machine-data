Summary: Possible successive observations of flying lanterns.
--- seg-e6039c63ab01d46dc800df3d [needs-review] ---
Description: Between July 19 and 21, 2009, a witness observed for a few seconds from his home some luminous phenomena that intrigued him. Luminescent spheres appear at night between 10:50 PM and 11:15 PM. No sound was heard during the objects' straight-line movement, which quickly faded and disappeared. The witness will send us montage photos of his successive observations. The characteristics described (several lights, straight trajectory, etc.) are related to the probable observation of flying lanterns. Releases of flying lanterns, very popular in recent times, have been the subject of news on our site.
