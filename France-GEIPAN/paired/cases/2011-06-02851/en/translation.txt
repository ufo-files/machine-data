--- seg-6de6430f7f1288a3e48a787c [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 26, 2011, at 2:30 a.m., an individual was stargazing. He was intrigued by a bright point moving, quickly followed by several other bright points (between 5 and 7). These points moved slowly and silently, aligned in a V-formation and heading west. The description of the orange points in V-formation suggests flying lanterns. This hypothesis is refuted by the witness, and movement according to the wind could not be confirmed as the wind was very unstable that night. Because he could not see stars in the middle of the triangle, the witness interprets all the points as the perimeter of an object, but he may have been blinded by the bright points. The hypothesis of formation flight of planes heading to the Bourget Air Show could not be verified as the investigation was too late. Given these dubious or contradictory elements, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C".
