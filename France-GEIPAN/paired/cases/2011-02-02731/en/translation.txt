Description: A witness reports his observation on February 26, 2011 at exactly 23:00, regarding the movements of white light halos and an oval shape. The gendarmerie investigation corroborates this testimony with that of other individuals. All these precise observations regarding the location lead the gendarmerie to conclude that they are light beams used that evening by a local nightclub.
