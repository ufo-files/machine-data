--- seg-c5e955193cb6d23ab1c0d7be [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of May 14, 2008, between 9:30 PM and 10:30 PM, during a birthday party, three individuals observed the slow movement in an east-west direction of three glowing orbs of yellow-orange light. No sound was heard. The observation lasted less than a minute. Only one statement was collected. The witness's observation is characteristic of an observation of a release of Thai lanterns, both in terms of colors, shapes, and the type of trajectory. The general east-west direction of the movement is consistent with the direction of the wind measured in Avignon at 10:00 PM (easterly wind). Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
