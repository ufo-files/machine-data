Description: On August 13, 2007, around 11:30 PM, a family was stargazing in their garden. They observed a puzzling luminous phenomenon in the sky: a bright white ball coming from the west and growing larger as it approached them. Slowly changing its trajectory to head towards the North Star, the sphere diminished in size before completely disappearing. A sky map taken at the indicated time and location shows that these people witnessed the passage of the International Space Station.
