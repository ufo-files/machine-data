Description: On September 26, 1983, between 6:48 PM and 7:00 PM, several witnesses observed the movement of lights of varying sizes and colors. This high-altitude, fast-moving object made no sound during the observations, which lasted from one to several minutes. The lights left trails of color in their wake, according to witness descriptions, suggesting the hypothesis of a relatively significant atmospheric reentry.
