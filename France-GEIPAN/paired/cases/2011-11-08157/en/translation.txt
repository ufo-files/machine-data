Summary: Observation of the slow movement of a bright point of light with multiple colors in the sky: astronomical misperception with Sirius
--- seg-ff35c0d73c37e606d9fc2d63 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of Sunday, November 20, 2011, around 4 a.m., a young scout in a municipal hall is intrigued by a brightly glowing point of several colors that he observes through a window. He notices that the point moves slowly then stops. The child does not perceive any unusual noises during his observation that lasts about twenty minutes. An investigator was dispatched to verify on the scene the directions of gaze corresponding to the observation. The direction observed corresponds with the position of the very bright star Sirius. However, the testimony does not exactly match the appearance of a fixed star. But given the context of the scene (a 9-year-old child, in the middle of his sleep, in an unfamiliar location), this relative inconsistency is not surprising. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Sirius.
