Description: On July 14, 1992, at approximately 2 a.m., and early in September at around 11 p.m., a witness observed a very bright white light for about a minute, with its intensity slowly varying. Then, in the company of his daughter, they observed for another minute a very bright white oval phenomenon moving slowly and silently through the sky. No other information could be gathered.
