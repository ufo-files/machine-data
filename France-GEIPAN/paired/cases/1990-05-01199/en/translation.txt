Summary: Observation of the movements of a triangular phenomenon with three white bright points and a red point
--- seg-214d6b475abf92b560652e95 [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 1, 1990, around 11 PM, a couple of young people were driving their vehicle. The man noticed the presence in the sky of a stationary red point. He stopped to observe it for about ten minutes during which the point remained stationary. Then the point moved very quickly laterally to stop again. After about two minutes, the point had approached the witnesses who then noticed 3 white lights in the form of an isosceles triangle, with the red point in the center. The entire phenomenon flew over the car and left at high speed. No noise was heard during the entire observation. Despite articles in the press, no other witnesses could be found and the phenomenon could not be explained.
