--- seg-97d624de27131a776d73d63d [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 5, 2012, around 10 PM on the beach of Les Hattes near Mana in French Guiana, a witness observed strange lights that intrigued them. Several red spheres moved slowly and silently across the sky. Based on the objective evidence collected (the red color of the observed and photographed objects, their slow and simultaneous movement on an ascending trajectory, calm or very light winds, and a weeknight conducive to lantern releases, as well as the recent presence of a lantern seller in the department), we can conclude that the three objects observed and photographed by witness #1 were likely celestial lanterns, also known as "Thai lanterns." This case should be classified as "B," indicating a very probable observation of Thai lanterns.
