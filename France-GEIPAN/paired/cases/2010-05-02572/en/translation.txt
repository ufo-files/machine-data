Description: On May 22, 2010, at 1:54 PM, an individual took photographs of a child. Post-hoc, the witness noticed an unusual object in one of the photos that had not been observed during the original shoot. The witness initiated a formal police report, which was received by the Geipan (French UFO investigation body) late. A preliminary police investigation and inquiries into air traffic and the surrounding area failed to determine the nature of the object, which was only photographed by the witness. Ultimately, analysis of the photo sent by the witness convinced the Geipan that it was a very close insect to the camera lens, consistent with the shutter speed and focus on the nearest subject (the child on the motorcycle). Given the shooting conditions, for the object to appear so blurred, it must have been less than the hyperfocal distance, well under 1 meter. However, based on the camera's characteristics and settings, if the object were 1 cm, it should have been 13 cm from the lens, which is consistent with the size of a winged insect. The lighting of this "insectoid device" is also consistent with sunlight. This case falls under the category of observations made on one of the many media (photos or films) that reach the GEIPAN: while not a direct visual observation during the shoot, the observation is made during subsequent viewing on a computer screen. The case is classified as B, indicating a probable photograph of an insect very close to the camera.
