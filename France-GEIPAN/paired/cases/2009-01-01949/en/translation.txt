--- seg-b66ea4e3a1289805c40caa06 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 13, 2009, at 6:42 a.m., a driver on Highway A20 near Réaville observed through their windshield an object passing quickly at an altitude of approximately 50 meters. The object was the size of a grapefruit and had a yellow-orange color. No noise was heard. The object disappeared and no trail was noticed. Despite our request to the witness, no gendarmerie report was provided to us. This case is classified C due to a lack of information.
