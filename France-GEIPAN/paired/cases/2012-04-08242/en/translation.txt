Description: On Sunday, April 29, 2012, around 4:15 PM, a witness observed the passage of a gray spherical object below the clouds. Intrigued, the witness filmed the object as it moved in a straight line. A plume of white smoke or flames was seen by the witness beneath the object. It disappeared, hidden by a neighboring building. The object's nearly linear motion, consistent with the direction of the wind (S to SO), which was blowing that day, strongly suggests a balloon. Because it was seen in backlighting, it appeared dark. It is not spherical, so as it turned, it occasionally reflected sunlight, causing temporary flashes. The object's relative sharpness suggests it was relatively close (less than 1 kilometer) and therefore of modest size (less than one meter). The small, spinning white points could be pollen, common at this time of year during the first gentle days of spring, very bright if seen in backlighting. No other witnesses were reported for this phenomenon. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a festive balloon, likely released from Ars sur Moselle or further south.
