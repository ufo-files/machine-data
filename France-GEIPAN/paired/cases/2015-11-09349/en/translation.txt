Description: On Sunday, November 1, 2015, around 7 a.m., a witness was observing the stars and noticed a random bright point, which then appeared to split into two. Using his camcorder, he watched and zoomed in, finding the multicolored diamond shape curious. No other witnesses were reported. The bright point described by the witness as "due west at the level of the Moon" is Capella. The witness did not report anything particularly strange until viewing the video ("I stayed on a random bright point"... "With the naked eye, I thought it was a satellite"), except perhaps a star twinkle that appeared to split. All the strangeness perceived by the witness and leading to his testimony comes from what he saw in the video. Here, once again, we have a classic manifestation of artifacts generated by a camera filming and zooming in on a star. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies this case as A: Capella.
