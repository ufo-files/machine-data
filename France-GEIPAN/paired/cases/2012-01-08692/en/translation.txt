--- seg-db42c0b837fcca96d5c8e11d [needs-review] ---
Description: Recent (12/04/2014) testimony of an old observation. The witness was waiting for a local fireworks display when he saw a cylindrical shape with a red light at its center, passing overhead under low clouds. The witness loses sight of the object while retrieving his camera. His son takes over to track the object as it disappears and then reappears further away. According to the child, the object vanished, leaving a red trail behind. Only one testimony was collected. The METEOCIEL report for Blagnac indicates a very cloudy sky and a strong west wind: 20 to 30 km/h. The object described has characteristics of Thai lanterns (color, duration, speed of movement) but its movement is not initially consistent with the west wind, as it is first observed in the northeast and then in the northwest. However, it should be noted that the observation is not continuous; it could be two successive, similar objects coming from the west. The hypothesis of a wind-carried light object (Thai lantern) becomes acceptable, and even highly probable, given that these releases are very frequent during this New Year's Eve night. The aspect described by the witness is unusual for a Thai lantern, explicable by the fact that the observations are made through a cloudy veil. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
