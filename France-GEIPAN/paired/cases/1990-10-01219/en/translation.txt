Summary: Observation of a bright point followed by a glowing ball
--- seg-74e527f5ba5d126f9ec66c5b [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 8, 1989, around 10:30 PM, a couple observed through a velux a very bright point for a few moments. The next day, they noticed freshly charred herbs and logs. After investigation, it was determined that the logs had been partially burned intentionally. As for the light, observed by another witness as well, it is likely the conjunction of the planets Saturn, Jupiter, and Uranus with the moon, which could only be observed in a specific direction.
