Description: On the night of July 19, 2009, between 11:00 PM and 11:15 PM, a witness observed a bright white light moving from north to south in the sky with a very intense glow. No noise was heard during the slow movement of the phenomenon. The witness did not observe any smoke or traces on the ground. This person was also a witness to the passage of the International Space Station (ISS), which was particularly bright (magnitude -3.3).
