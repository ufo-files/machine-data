--- seg-8e2a99efac70785d61f11dea [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Saturday, August 10, 2013, around 7:30 PM, several people were intrigued by the silent passage of a dark, cigar-shaped object in the sky. It flew with the wind and moved at a considerable speed just below the clouds. The intermittent observation lasted from 15 to 20 seconds. The testimonies are very precise, and the witnesses highly reliable. The strangeness of the phenomenon is average. Indeed, while no hypothesis can explain it based on the precise elements reported by the witnesses, the phenomenon could correspond to an airplane or a drone if the observation conditions prevented the witnesses from seeing certain characteristic elements, or to a wind-carried object (balloon in the shape of an airplane). The elements collected during the investigation did not allow for a certain or approximate identification of the phenomenon, which remains moderately strange. Given the current state of available information, this case is classified in category C, due to the lack of precise data on distance estimation and wind conditions.
