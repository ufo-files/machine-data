Description: On January 7, 2000, at approximately 7:20 PM, the pilot of an A320 aircraft bound for Casablanca observed two balloons, one blue and one white, carrying a radar reflector-like payload, passing at a low distance beneath his aircraft. Following a complaint, Météo-France stated that these were not weather radiosondes. Inquiring with the military yielded no new information. While the nature of the objects (balloons) does not seem in doubt, the origin of these balloons could not be determined.
