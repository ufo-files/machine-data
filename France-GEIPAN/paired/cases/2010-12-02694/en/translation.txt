Description: On December 27, 2010, at 6:45 PM, a driver was intrigued by a phenomenon on the left side of D937 at the location known as La Boule d'Or in Geneston (44). He saw a black mass with two yellow lights below it. The witness described these lights as rotating, causing a dozen more yellow lights to appear. The observation lasted between 5 and 6 seconds. The driver pulled over but did not find the phenomenon. Continuing on his way, he saw the PAN again as it flew over him, but darkness and fog prevented him from clearly distinguishing the object's contours. The PAN made a fairly muffled and deep engine noise; a beam in front and a red rotating light were seen. The driver saw the PAN one last time at a higher altitude a little further down the road. Only one testimony was collected. It is reasonable to believe that the witness saw an aircraft (and likely a helicopter). Towards the end of his observation, the witness himself formulated this hypothesis: "I remember thinking this time about an airplane because the Nantes airport is in that direction." Moreover, the witness does not introduce any strangeness that would explicitly oppose this hypothesis of an aircraft, and the descriptive elements ("sequence of yellow lights like windows," "oval shape," "engine noise, fairly muffled and deep like a blower," "front light with a mobile beam, shaken by very rapid oblique movements but without great range, as it only lit the clouds a few meters in front of the craft," "very powerful red rotating light and on the other side an identical light but blue, of the same shade as those on emergency vehicles") are compatible with an aircraft, and more likely a helicopter, given the particular lighting of the latter (including rotating spotlights), the proximity of hospitals, and the relatively slow movement. The witness mentioned the presence of heavy road traffic and the absence of a reaction, which supports the fact that it was perceived as an aircraft without any notable strangeness. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of a helicopter.
