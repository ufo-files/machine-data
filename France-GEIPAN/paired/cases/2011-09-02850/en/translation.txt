Description: On September 13, 2011, at 11:15 PM, the gendarmerie was contacted by individuals who had spotted an unidentified flying object. The gendarmerie responded to the scene, and witnesses reported observing a spherical object with three distinct lights in the sky; initially stationary, it then moved slowly towards the North. The witness himself offered an explanation, stating that he had seen the star Mira Ceti (Whale constellation). However, this star is known to be visible in the East at that time, while the witness claimed to have observed it in the West, towards Rosans. Furthermore, the witness indicated that the star disappeared: an Eastern star rises during the night (see the "Eastern Sky Map" document). Therefore, the observed star was Arcturus, a very bright star (Magnitude 0), which indeed sets in the West around 11:30 PM, moving towards the North. As with all bright stars, its color shifts towards orange during sunset, and it twinkles particularly. (See the "Western Sky Map" document). This case is classified as A: Identified.
