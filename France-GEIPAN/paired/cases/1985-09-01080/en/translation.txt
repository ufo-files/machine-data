Description: Between September 16 and 18, 1985, several witnesses reported seeing in the sky a fixed bright point with slight red, blue, yellow, or green coloration. These observations over several days, lasting up to 2 hours, were confirmed by staff at the Observatory of the Alpes de Haute-Provence. It is likely the observation of a planet or star.
