Description: On January 11, 2014, around 4:25 PM, a witness was observing the moon in a cloudy sky when they noticed up to 8 bright white lights appearing in a silent, swirling motion, and then disappearing from view after a few minutes on a straight trajectory. The observation lasted 15 seconds. No other testimonies were collected. Given the objective elements outlined in the investigation report, namely the presence of a clear and bright sky with good visibility, the sun's position low on the horizon, and the contrast effect, the UFOs being in a favorable spatial configuration for visibility by the witness (strong reflection of sunlight), and the visible shape of the UFOs, we can conclude that all conditions were met for the witness to observe simple white birds flying at high altitude, strongly reflecting sunlight towards them. This case should be classified as "B" for a probable observation of birds flying at high altitude.
