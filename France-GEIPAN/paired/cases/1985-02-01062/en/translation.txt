Description: On February 24, 1985, around 7:30 PM, several witnesses in the same area saw a red-pink ball fall: an explosion was also heard. The investigation will show that it was the effects of a distress rocket launched without particular intent by a person, the witnesses having seen the red-pink smoke of the rocket and then the parachute drifting with the wind.
