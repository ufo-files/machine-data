Description: On October 10, 1979, at approximately 7:55 a.m., a witness observed an unusual object in the sky. In the shape of a dirigible, the object moved at a constant speed on a horizontal trajectory towards the northwest. No noise emanating from the object was heard during the entire observation. The object gradually disappeared into the sky. No other testimony regarding this object, which resembled a dirigible or the Super Guppy (a cargo aircraft replaced in 1996 by the Beluga), was collected, but we lack information about it.
