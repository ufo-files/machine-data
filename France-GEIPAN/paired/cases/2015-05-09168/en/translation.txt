Description: On May 4, 2015 at 10:05 AM, a witness observed a shimmering object moving slowly across the sky from their home. No other witnesses were reported. The shimmering object with changing reflections, in broad daylight, moving slowly across the Paris sky, has all the characteristics of a fairground balloon (metallized mylar balloon) that has escaped its owner. These balloons have a wide variety of shapes and colors (perform a Google Images search for "mylar balloon"); once released, they spin on themselves, creating very variable appearances and reflections, as seen in the witness's photos. The testimony is not detailed enough to determine the balloon's trajectory or its conformity with local wind conditions. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a child's balloon.
