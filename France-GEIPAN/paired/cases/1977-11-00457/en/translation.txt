--- seg-3b2b48a0a98d8674220b9afb [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 24, 1977, at 7:20 a.m., a witness observed the passage of an oval-shaped object, about the size of a rugby ball. It emitted a bright light and moved along an east-north trajectory before making a U-turn, retracing the same distance in the opposite direction, and disappearing behind the urban landscape. The observation lasted approximately thirty seconds, during which no unusual noises were heard. No other witnesses were found for this phenomenon, for which we have limited information.
