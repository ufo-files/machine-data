Description: Between October 20 and 26, 1976, in the evening, several people in the same geographic area observed multicolored lights that seemed to come from a craft that no one was able to identify. The witnesses were frightened by these lights and the stationary or moving craft that emitted a deep noise. This phenomenon is classified as C due to a lack of information.
