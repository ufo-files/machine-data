Description: On September 15, 2024, around 10:45 PM, witness T1, along with his spouse, observed from their house terrace a black circular shape vertical to their position moving towards the southwest. The sky is clear. The circular shape remains stationary for a few minutes and emits a flickering white light for a few seconds. The witness decides to go back inside the house, ending the observation. The duration of the observation is estimated at approximately 5 minutes. Only witness T1 submits a completed Technical Questionnaire (QT) to GEIPAN on September 16, 2024. On October 4, 2024, the GEIPAN made a request to the Air and Space Defense Air Operations Planning and Control Center (CAPCODA). Radar tracks were transmitted to us on October 11, 2024. These indicate the presence of a gas balloon flying over the witness between 10:43 PM and 10:49 PM local time (between 8:43 PM and 8:49 PM UTC), on a straight trajectory from northeast to southwest, at an altitude of 4100 feet (1200 meters) (see Annex 1). The balloon is of Roland JBG-1050 type (see Annex 2). It was part of a gas balloon race, the "Gordon Bennett cup," which flew over northeast to southwest France, specifically Haute-Vienne, on Monday, September 15, 2024 (see LA MONTAGNE article: Why So Many Balloons Crossed the Limousin Sky on Sunday . The case consistency is good with a single witness observation, but a detailed description of the PAN and its trajectory, without photos or videos. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The description of the observation, both in form and in trajectory and time of passage, corresponds perfectly to the overflight of this gas balloon over the witness's house. The case is classified "A": observation of a inhabited gas balloon.
