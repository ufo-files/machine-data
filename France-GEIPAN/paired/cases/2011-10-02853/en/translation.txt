Description: On October 22, 2011, between 9:20 and 9:30 PM, a witness from their home observed the slow, silent passage at low altitude of an orange glow they compared to a streetlight. A second glow arrived a few seconds later. The phenomena disappeared into the clouds. The witness ruled out the possibility of Thai lanterns as they had seen them up close before. No other testimonies were collected. The witness's description aligns perfectly with the hypothesis of two Thai lanterns: orange color, moving with the wind (20-30 km/h SE -> NO), no noise, Saturday night. However, the witness, who claims to have seen lanterns from a distance before, rejects this hypothesis. Without further investigation elements, the GEIPAN classifies the case as "C" (lack of data to conclude), while strongly favoring the hypothesis of flying lanterns.
