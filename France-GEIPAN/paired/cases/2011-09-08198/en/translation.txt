Description: A witness reports his observation made on September 1, 2011 at 10:35 PM: While on his terrace, he observes the rapid and silent east-west passage of three yellow lights. These lights disappear behind a cloud. The observation of these yellow points crossing the sky in the direction of the wind (see Lorraine weather) is perfectly consistent with the observation of Thai lanterns launched to the east of Bar-le-Duc (possibly near the stadium). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
