--- seg-fbb5940f090911817349ea22 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Recent report of an old observation made on July 23, 1965, around 6 PM. A metallic-looking triangular object is stationary in the sky and strongly reflects the sunlight. This observation case by multiple witnesses of a fixed bright object in the evening sky has all the characteristics of a tetrahedral stratospheric sounding balloon. The luminous and metallic appearance is characteristic, the apparent immobility is not rare, their very high altitude (up to 40km) makes movements appear very slow, and winds at this altitude are very variable. The altitude calculation made by the witness is relevant; it is well consistent with this hypothesis of a weather balloon that reflected sunlight up to 40 minutes after sunset. The presence of the planet Venus was noted that evening, which was brilliant to the west shortly after sunset. It is surprising that the witnesses do not mention it; but it was much further west and lower than the phenomenon observed. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a stratospheric balloon.
