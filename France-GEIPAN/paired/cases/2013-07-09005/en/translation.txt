Summary: Observation of silent movements of white lights in the sky: observation of the night flight of the French Patrol.
--- seg-22918d3a361fa5c6d1eaacff [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 5, 2013, between 10:40 PM and 11:05 PM, a driver and his companion were intrigued by the silent movements of several white lights in the sky. Other drivers also stopped to observe the phenomenon. Only one testimony was collected. The witness's description closely resembles the French Air Force Patrol in nighttime exercise; their testimony is very similar to other accounts of this annual event (first Friday of July). Like many witnesses, he was surprised by the silence of the phenomenon, which is fully explained by the great distance of observation and because he was facing the planes; this misjudgment of distance also made the planes' movement seem too slow. The number of distinct light points depends on the formation's geometry: some may hide another. This testimony well illustrates the misinterpretation one can make of a phenomenon simply because one misjudges the distance to it; this estimation is nearly impossible to make a priori, even for an experienced observer. The headlights of these Alpha jets are very powerful, giving the impression of closer objects than they actually are. A very clear sky can also enhance this illusion of proximity. The French Air Force Patrol has indeed confirmed that on that day, maneuvers lasted until 11:13 PM. This case is classified as "A": observation of the French Air Force Patrol.
