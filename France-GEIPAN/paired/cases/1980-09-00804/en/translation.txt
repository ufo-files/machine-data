Summary: Observation of a elongated object moving in jerks
--- seg-ca63e9bc77ea6b8f881c36c5 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of September 9 to 10, 1980, around 0:45 a.m., a witness observing from their doorstep saw a luminous phenomenon. 100 meters along the path leading to their home, a glow illuminated the traffic sign. No cars or noises were heard, and the witness then saw an elongated object moving in jerks at 1.50 meters above the ground. The object gradually ascended and disappeared into the landscape. No unusual odors were noted. No other testimonies were collected regarding this phenomenon, for which we lack information.
