Summary: Observation of the movement of a dark patch in the sky; sudden changes in direction and disappearance over the sea at the horizon: lack of information.
--- seg-afbd0b00fe3f595e66c71c37 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Sunday, September 5, 1954, at 5:15 PM, a couple and their daughter, located near the PTT radio station near Cape Spartel (Morocco), observed a dark, circular patch high in the sky, approximately the size of an airplane. One of the witnesses reported following this dark phenomenon for 10 minutes in a southerly direction, without noticing any lights, smoke, or sparks. No sounds were heard. The movements were punctuated by sudden stops. The phenomenon disappeared towards the West, diving towards the sea until it vanished on the horizon. Only one testimony with a map and diagram was collected. The hypothesis of an airplane from the Rabat-Salé air base (home to the 8th Fighter Squadron flying Vampire planes) performing aerial acrobatics is possible. The fixed points could correspond to the moment the witness sees the airplane face-on. However, the lack of information regarding the angular size of the object and its disappearance over the sea towards the horizon does not allow this hypothesis to be fully confirmed. The GEIPAN classifies this case as C due to the lack of information.
