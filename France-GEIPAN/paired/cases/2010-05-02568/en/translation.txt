Summary: Observation of a black dot in the sky: probable observation of a balloon.
--- seg-d92aa4d7a9a97b07ef30dd5f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 1, 2010, at 5:00 PM, several people observed from an apartment a black dot in the sky. Initially stationary, the dot moved without any sound. The black dot was filmed by one of the witnesses. The testimony strongly suggests a "lighter than air" object following more or less the local wind (appearing in the West, disappearing towards the South). Note that the wind pattern on that day seems disturbed and complex: North wind in Lyon, no wind in Grenoble, West wind in Geneva. The precise direction of the wind in Sallanches could not be established. A local association conducting hot air balloon flights was consulted: no flight at that time. It could be a helium balloon launched or lost by a private individual, or an experimental airship. The GEIPAN did not observe green reflections on the object in the video. This case, ultimately not strange, is classified "B": probable observation of a balloon (balloon not identified by the GEIPAN).
