Description: On February 2, 2011, at 6:15 AM, two witnesses observed from their apartment living room a dark circle in which bright lights flickered. Approximately the size of a full moon, this phenomenon remained stationary for about fifteen minutes before dimming and moving away, becoming in a matter of seconds a very bright point. The observation ended at 6:45 AM. The initial hypothesis considered was that of astronomical observation of the planet Venus, particularly visible during this time of year (magnitude -3.9, thus very bright) in the southeast (135°) at 10° above the horizon (see celestial map). However, this hypothesis does not explain the observation of a dark circle and bright lights, nor the speed at which the object disappeared. No other testimonies regarding this phenomenon were collected. Given the nature of the observed phenomenon, conducting an in-depth investigation is difficult. The case is classified as "C" due to a lack of information.
