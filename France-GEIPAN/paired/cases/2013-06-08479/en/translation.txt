Description: On June 22, 2013, at 10:30 PM, two witnesses observed a series of perfectly aligned red and orange-yellow points, which seemed to pulse or flash in the direction of the peak of Sous-Dine (Direct South, see map). The phenomenon, initially static, quickly disappears by merging into an orange mass. Only one testimony is collected, accompanied by photographs. The witness thinks he is observing to the southeast, but the Sous-Dine mountain is directly south. The witness believes he is observing objects above that mountain; it is very likely that these objects are much closer, but the alignment above the mountain's peak is deceptive. In the event of Thai lanterns, a preliminary analysis of the photos indicates that these objects would be at a distance of 5 to 10 km from the witness, that is, between La Roche-sur-Foron and the mountain, at an altitude 500m to 1000m higher than the witness (who is at 450m altitude). Weather reports (Meteociel) from nearby cities to Geneva and Annecy indicate a light, very unstable wind that evening: it is impossible to determine the wind direction at low altitude towards La Roche-sur-Foron that evening. The Tourism Office of La Roche-sur-Foron informed us that on June 22, the Music Festival and the arrival of the Bornes Rally took place, but no release of Thai lanterns was reported (not even to the Gendarmerie). Without possible verification of the wind with a precise weather log, and without confirmation from local authorities, the GEIPAN cannot confirm the hypothesis of white and red Thai lanterns (appearing yellow and red), though entirely compatible with the photos and testimony. Pending new information, this case is classified "C"
