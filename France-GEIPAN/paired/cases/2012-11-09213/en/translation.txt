--- seg-46e08e57be26bd03849fc4ed [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 30, 2015, the GEIPAN received a questionnaire regarding an observation made on November 10, 2012 at 9:30 PM by a single witness. The witness, on their terrace, observed in the sky a dark triangular phenomenon with angles and a center illuminated in a yellow-orange color. It moves at a low speed without noise, then rotates 90 degrees and heads full west at a blinding speed. Given the elements gathered in the investigation report (see attached document), namely:

- Yellow-orange color identical to that of lanterns.
- Behavior identical to that of objects subject to no or weak wind.
- Short observation duration (less than 1 minute) compatible with the lifespan of lanterns.
- Date: a Saturday night, compatible with those conducive to the organization of lantern releases for celebrations.
- Number compatible with the amount of lanterns launched.
- Illusion of acceleration and disappearance caused by the lanterns' extinction.
- 90-degree turn possibly caused by a horizontal shear phenomenon.

The GEIPAN classifies this observation case as "B," indicating a probable observation of Thai lanterns.
