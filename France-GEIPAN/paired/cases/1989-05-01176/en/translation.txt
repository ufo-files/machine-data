Summary: Observation of an object moving silently at very high speed with sudden changes in direction.
--- seg-408a886c678547b8e6a0b442 [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 30, 1989, around 11:15 PM, three gamekeepers on patrol observed three lights emitting very bright flashes at very close intervals. After approximately twenty seconds of immobility, these three lights ascended vertically at a very high speed, performing zigzags. After a few seconds, the phenomenon returned above the witnesses and stopped at its starting point. A red light then lit up in the middle of the three headlights before departing again, changing direction several times. A Jaguar fighter plane flew by shortly after, but it could not be confused with the observed phenomenon, which remained silent. Investigations conducted at the nearby air base did not confirm the testimony of the three witnesses. The observed phenomenon could not be identified.
