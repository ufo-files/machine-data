Description: On December 8, 2013, starting at 5:15 PM, several people observed in the sky to the south-southwest a unique and spherical phenomenon that intrigued them. Very bright, the phenomenon appeared stationary in the clear sky. The phenomenon was observed until around 8:00 PM. The very bright light point that the witness observed for a long time in the southwest at the beginning of the evening is undoubtedly the planet Venus, particularly bright at that time. Very good weather conditions made the spectacle surprising for the witness. The witness classified this case as "A": observation of the planet Venus.
