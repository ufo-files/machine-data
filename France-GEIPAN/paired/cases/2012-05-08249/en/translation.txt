Description: The witness reports multiple sky observations since 2011. The last observed phenomena occurred on Saturday, May 12, 2012, at 23:05, Tuesday, May 15, 2012, at 23:53, and Tuesday, June 5, 2012, at 00:19. Each time, it involves bright, star-like orbs moving across the sky in zigzag patterns, capable of accelerating, descending, and ascending very quickly and without any sound. The mentioned observations precisely correspond to light flashes on Iridium and Metop satellites (see Calsky records). Other observations likely involve other satellites: the impression of slight zig-zags according to the trajectories of observed bright points on a black background is frequent, it's an illusion, named autokinetic, due to the micro-movements the eye makes to follow the point in the sky. The impression of distancing and approaching comes from the variations in brightness of the objects: an object whose brightness fades is interpreted as moving away. This observation case is classified "A": observation of sun reflections on satellites.
