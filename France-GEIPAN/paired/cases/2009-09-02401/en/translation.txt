Description: During a street meal on September 5, 2009, around 10 PM, several people observed the silent passage of a glowing object in the sky. One witness reported seeing a slowly moving orange ball that suddenly accelerated and headed south without making any noise. The characteristics of the observation suggest a well-known and frequently reported phenomenon to the GEIPAN: the passage of a luminous flying lantern. The presence of a firework nearby and thus festive activity greatly strengthens this hypothesis. This case is classified as B, indicating a probable observation of a luminous flying lantern.
