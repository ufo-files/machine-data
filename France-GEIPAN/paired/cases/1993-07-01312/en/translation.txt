Summary: Observation of light circles, likely from projectors or laser beams
--- seg-8dd0ad45ad6a3bf3d42ac241 [needs-review] ---
Description: During the night of July 17 to 18, 1993, between 12:30 AM and 2:30 AM, a witness observed from their home a dance of white glowing circles spinning at high speed in the sky. After approximately one hour, a third glowing circle joined the other two. The witness stopped observing the phenomenon around 2:30 AM. It is highly probable, though not proven, that the observed phenomenon was a beam of searchlights or promotional laser lights or disco beams.
