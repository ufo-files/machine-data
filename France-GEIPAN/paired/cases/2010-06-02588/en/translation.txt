Description: On Friday, June 25, 2010 at 12:50 AM, a witness observed, from the terrace of their home, a luminous phenomenon that intrigued them. For one to two minutes, this person followed the silent movement of a bright, spherical, white glow. An investigation assigned to an IPN (French National Police) will establish that the witness likely observed the passage of the International Space Station.
