Description: A witness reported to the gendarmerie and the GEIPAN an observation of a luminous phenomenon from their home on Wednesday, February 3, 2010, around 4 PM. The direction and elevation, according to the first reconstruction photograph, correspond exactly to the position of the Sun. It is therefore natural that the object would be dazzling despite sunglasses, change in shape and color, and that interior lighting appears weak and pink or orange (QT p.2-4). The phenomenon also disappears with the setting of the Sun (QT p.15) behind the mountains. In this context, the second object described, whose position is given by the second photograph, could simply be a consequence of prolonged observation of the Sun, namely a phenomenon of retinal persistence. The witness cites their neighbor, whom they contacted by phone during the observation. The neighbor's statements, reproduced by the witness, are ambiguous: sometimes it's a "sputnik" or a fireball, depending on whether one consults the hearing protocol or the Terrestrial questionnaire. Very low stars on the horizon are sometimes interpreted by witnesses as much closer and at a low altitude. This case is classified as B, probable confusion with the Sun, followed by dazzling subsequent to its observation.
