Description: On June 13, 2010, at 1 a.m., a witness sees orange glowing orbs gradually appear in the sky. The witness counts between 15 and 20 orbs moving quickly and silently before fading away while ascending into the air. The witness's description matches all aspects of the observation of a Thai lantern release: orange color, smooth and silent movement according to the wind (northeasterly to southwesterly, see weather report), and disappearance after a few minutes. Furthermore, the time and date (1 a.m. on a Sunday in June) are common for such events. This case is classified "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
