Description: On April 15, 2012, at 10:31 PM, a witness observed a stationary object in a clear sky, then moving from West to East at low altitude. A deep noise was heard. The object passed over the building and the witness lost sight of it. Although brief, the observation allowed the witness to record a video with his smartphone. No other testimonies were reported. It appears, most likely, that this testimony is a misidentification of a commercial airliner approaching Roissy Airport. The most peculiar aspect of this observation is the witness's confusion: residing on the axis of Roissy's runways, he should be accustomed to the heavy air traffic bound for this international airport, although only a part of this traffic flies over his home, located 50 kilometers from the runway threshold. Photo and video documents could have helped to clarify the situation, but the witness did not submit them directly but shared them through online services. These documents are no longer accessible and could not be analyzed. Consequently, GEIPAN classifies the case as C due to lack of information, on the border of a case B (Airplane).
