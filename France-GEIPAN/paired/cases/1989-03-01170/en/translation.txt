--- seg-fd27ff6bc70c401f49709b88 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of March 2 to 3, 1989, two witnesses were driving their car when it suddenly stopped and the headlights went out. They then observed a ball of fire emitting intense red-orange light moving from west to east. After passing the car, it started moving on its own again. The witnesses were very shocked by this event and wondered if they had dreamed it. Following a local press article, a resident of Le Mans contacted the witnesses to explain that she, too, had seen an orange ball of fire on the night of March 2 to 3 around 1:30 a.m., which greatly alarmed her dog, confirming to them that they had not dreamed.
