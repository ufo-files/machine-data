Summary: A red fireball appeared and moved, causing effects on the witnesses' car. Another witness of this phenomenon specifies that his dog was frightened during the fireball's appearance.
--- seg-fd27ff6bc70c401f49709b88 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: During the night of March 2 to 3, 1989, two witnesses are travelling in their car when it stops and the headlights suddenly go out. The witnesses then observe a fireball emitting an intense red-orange light and moving from west to east. After the fireball passes the car, the car starts again by itself. The witnesses are very shaken by this experience and wonder whether they had not dreamed it. Following an article in the local press, a resident of Le Mans contacts the witnesses to explain that she also saw an orange fireball during the night of March 2 to 3, at around 01:30, which greatly alarmed her dog, and confirms to them that they had not dreamed it.
