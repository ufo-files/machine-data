Summary: Observations of the slow movement of a bright point in the sky: astronomical observations of the planet Venus.
--- seg-475a840dc7e8be435d6856a8 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 26, 1983, several people observed between 5:30 and 6:30 a very bright object in the sky that appeared, to the naked eye, like a star. This object moved slowly, and observation through binoculars revealed, according to witnesses, a yellow crown surrounding a light gray crater. An examination of the sky map at the time of the observation shows the presence of Venus at the location indicated by the witness, with a movement strictly consistent with his estimate between 5:30 and 6:30.
