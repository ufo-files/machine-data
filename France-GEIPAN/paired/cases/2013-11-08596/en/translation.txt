Description: The witness, who was flying a kite, observed a gray metallic oval-shaped object with two round wings high in the sky. Its trajectory is very straight and its speed particularly fast. The witness, due to his activity, is more or less facing into the wind, looking towards the East or North-East (wind from West to North-West). The witness saw the object pass over the kite and disappear towards the North (according to the questionnaire) or East (according to the diagram of the named witness:  stbenoit.jpg). The object behaves like a wind-carried object (wind speed sustained between 26 and 44 km/h), flying probably quite low (100 to 200 meters) so as to appear very fast to the witness. The shape drawn by the witness resembles the silhouette of a Mickey Mouse balloon (See Mickey Mouse balloon). The witness perceives the object's trajectory at a 45° angle from the wind direction: this deviation can be explained on the one hand by the uncertainty of the estimation, and on the other hand by the variability of the wind according to time (see weather report) and altitude. This observation is somewhat strange: the object moves in a straight line, and very likely with the wind. This very brief visual testimony from a single witness has an average consistency (a photo or a second testimony could have confirmed or refuted the hypothesis put forward). The GEIPAN classifies this observation as "B", indicating a probable observation of a child's balloon.
