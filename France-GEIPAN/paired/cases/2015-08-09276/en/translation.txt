Description: On August 21, 2015, at 11:13 PM, a witness by the seashore had their gaze drawn to a movement in the sky: three bright, white, star-like forms formed an isosceles triangle that moved in a straight line without sound. After turning away from the PAN to call another person, the witness never saw the phenomenon in the sky again. The observation lasted approximately 7 seconds. Only one testimony was collected. Of average strangeness but sufficient consistency, this case could be a misidentification with a flock of birds illuminated by a local light source (see investigation notes). The proximity to the sea and the presence of a lighted pole reported by the witness are consistent with this hypothesis, which remains, however, fragile due to the lack of possible verification or other witnesses. The case lacks precision, though, due to the very short duration of the observation. This case is classified B, possible misidentification with white birds illuminated by urban lighting.
