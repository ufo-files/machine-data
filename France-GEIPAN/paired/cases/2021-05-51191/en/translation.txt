Summary: Observation of a luminous phenomenon in the form of a halo in the night sky: observation of effects related to the ignition intended for the deorbitation of the Falcon 9 rocket stage that served in the launch of the Starlink® satellite train n°28.
--- seg-28eca46b1227671189aff7bf [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 26, 2021, around 11 PM, the witness, from the terrace of their home in Noé (31), observed a PAN they described as a cylindrical glowing halo with a small cloud in the center and a star above it. Inside the halo, on each side of the cloud, were ripples resembling waves. This halo was followed by a very bright rectangle. The whole scene moved in a straight line, from west to east, without emitting any sound. The investigation revealed that this case corresponds to the observation of the effects generated by the ignition of the final stage of the Falcon 9 rocket in preparation for its deorbitation, following the launch of the Starlink® satellite batch #28 (see the investigation report). The witness's description of the PAN, mentioning "a halo" and "a star," is entirely consistent with this aerospace phenomenon, observed over a large area in France and Spain, at an hour very close to the one indicated by the witness. Furthermore, the trajectory of the PAN is perfectly aligned with that of this rocket stage, following an northwest-southeast axis, with a nearly vertical passage to the northeast of the observation location. The bright rectangle observed by the witness could correspond to the Starlink® satellites, which had not yet dispersed. The apparent large size and distance of the PAN, as well as its intense brightness, contributed to the witness's perception of strangeness. The underestimation of the actual distance of the phenomenon, this operation of separation and reentry of the Falcon 9 launcher stages by SpaceX taking place at an altitude of over 100 km, leads to an overestimation of its size. Additionally, the observation duration seems also overestimated, likely being less than 15 minutes. The case is classified "A," observation of the effects related to the ignition for its deorbitation of the Falcon 9 rocket stage used to launch the Starlink® satellite batch #28. The description of the PAN precisely matches that of another case studied and documented by the GEIPAN, which occurred on the same day and at the same time: see the case in Malrevers (43), on May 26, 2021.
