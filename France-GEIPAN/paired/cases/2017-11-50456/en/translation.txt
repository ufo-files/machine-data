Summary: Observation of a moving bright point in the night sky: Lack of information.
--- seg-aeec89977b4aeb821d75c230 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Thursday, November 16, 2017, around 00:50, the witness stepped outside to smoke a cigarette. They spotted, to the North, a bright point in the sky, similar to a star but with a white hue containing red. The phenomenon seemed to move from right to left, then up and down, in a slightly circular pattern. They observed it for a quarter of an hour and then returned home. They did not see it disappear and had time to film it. Only one testimony was collected. The case consistency is quite low. There is a lack of information and video. The witness remains unreachable as of the writing of this report. The strangeness lies primarily in the fact that no notable star in the North appears to be able to cause a misperception. The described movements could be due to a so-called autokinesis effect. However, the witness states that the PAN climbed to 90° at the end of the observation. This indication also explains the high strangeness of the case. The missing elements are certainly available from the witness, but all our attempts to reach them have been unsuccessful. As the witness does not respond to our requests, we cannot conclude due to a lack of information. In the GEIPAN methodology, if the strangeness is high, the consistency must also be high for a D classification. Here, the video would have certainly been valuable to get an idea of the described phenomenon and increase the case consistency. The GEIPAN classifies the case as C: lack of information.
