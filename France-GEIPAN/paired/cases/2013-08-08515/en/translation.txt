Summary: Observation of a very bright point in the sky with intensity and movement variations: mistaken for a flash from satellite Iridium 95.
--- seg-12406a8d1c1428cd1c569837 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 31, 2013, around 11:30 PM, a witness observed from the threshold of his door a very bright white point among the stars. During the observation, the witness noted that the light intensity varied (increasing then decreasing) and that the object moved in a straight upward line before disappearing from view. Only one testimony was collected. Of low strangeness and average consistency (single witness but fairly precise testimony), this case turns out to be the observation of a rare but well-known astronautic phenomenon: a reflection of the sun on satellite Iridium 95, called a flash or flare (see the investigation note and Flash of Iridium Satellites?). This case is classified A, misperception with a flash of satellite Iridium 95.
