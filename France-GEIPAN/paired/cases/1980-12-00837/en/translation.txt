Description: For several consecutive days, a witness observes, between 6:30 and 8:30 AM, an orange glow appearing in the East. On December 12, 1980, the gendarmerie brigade decided to observe the luminous phenomenon. Indeed, a glow gradually and slowly rises in the sky. Through prolonged observation, an optical effect puts the phenomenon in motion, which changes color with the rising sun, continuing its ascent. The gendarmes think it might be a particular star. The sky map for those days indicates the gradual appearance of Venus in the East, rising and then disappearing with the dawn.
