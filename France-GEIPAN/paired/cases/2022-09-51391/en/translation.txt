Summary: Observation of a luminous phenomenon changing shape during its movement in the sky: observation of the relighting and passivation of the second stage of the Falcon 9 rocket.
--- seg-a0f59bf5cd86bd3782569246 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness reports an observation dated September 6, 2022, in the free narrative section of the questionnaire. Subsequently, in the questionnaire and other testimonial documents (email, protocol), he specifies the date as Monday, September 5: we consider this as the observation date. The witness took photographs using his mobile phone in the early morning. On September 5, 2022, around 06:05, the witness observed a moving bright point, then a second point to the left, offset downwards. He then distinguished a diffuse light between the two points. The slightly blue-tinted light became more defined, like a "rectangular hologram between the two points." He then saw a white light "aura" around the second point, resembling an iris, and finally a "helix" or "double spiral" of white light, parallel to the ground, with a size equivalent to the iris. The first bright point was no longer visible, but it was replaced by a "bar" of less intense light. The witness's description and associated photographs led to the conclusion that it was the relighting and passivation of the second stage of the Falcon 9 rocket during the launch on September 5, 2022, intended to place Starlink constellation satellites into orbit. This is now a very classic and known observation of a GEIPAN. This procedure allows for monitoring the return of this stage to Earth. This occurs during the second orbit, during the second pass over Europe, and in this specific case, over or near France. The stage is then at an altitude of approximately 320 km. The engine is relit for 1 to 2 seconds to place the stage on the correct re-entry orbit, and after engine extinction, the second stage is passivated: the gases in the tanks are ejected to reduce pressure before the end of the mission to minimize the risk of explosion during the subsequent atmospheric re-entry. This ejection is done symmetrically on both sides of the stage, giving it a "bow tie" appearance for a ground observer; if the gases are not completely symmetrically ejected, the appearance is then that of a spiral. Many witnesses have contacted the GEIPAN following this observation. You can find additional explanations in the article A Falcon rocket degasses over France. This case is classified A: a perfectly identified phenomenon.
