Summary: Observation of the S-NE movement of a white glowing object: observation of the passage of the ISS.
--- seg-f16056854f32b32502c8ab38 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 6, 2013, at 10:50 PM, several witnesses observed from their homes a silent, S-NE moving white light phenomenon in the sky. The object was then hidden by the landscape. The elements provided by the witness demonstrate, in support of Calsky records, that it was the passage of the International Space Station (ISS), visible that evening over the witness's city. The general trajectory (from South to NE, reaching a maximum of 60°) corresponds well to the Calsky record (see document). The brightness perceived by the witness, twice as bright as Venus, also corresponds to the calculated magnitude of -4.2 for the ISS under these conditions. The witness described the light point's movement as "like a worm": this is an optical illusion often occurring when following a very bright point on a black background: the perception of the movement of a light point in the sky. However, there is a deviation of about 5 minutes between the time indicated by the witness and the ISS bulletin. The GEIPAN sent the witness ISS passage predictions for June 8 and 9, which the witness confirmed he had seen. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the International Space Station (ISS).
