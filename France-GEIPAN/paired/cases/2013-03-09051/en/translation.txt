Description: Testimony in 2014 of an old observation. On March 24, 2013, at 3 or 4 a.m., a driver observed in the black sky a static small amber-yellow light. He informed his two passengers when the light changed shape and moved quickly in all directions, approaching the vehicle, which scared the driver. The observation lasted 10 to 15 minutes before the light disappeared. Only one testimony was collected. The description translates to "strange." This is the case for all testimonies sent to GEIPAN, where strangeness (to varying degrees) is the very object of a testimony at GEIPAN. Often, it is the contacts or encounters with the witness that allow GEIPAN to "lift" the strangeness, that is, to find an explanation. The peculiarity here is that the witness did not follow up on the request for a meeting, despite a reminder. Consequently, GEIPAN classifies the case as C: lack of reliable information.
