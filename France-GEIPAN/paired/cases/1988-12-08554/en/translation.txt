Description: Recent testimony (08/05/2013) of an old observation on a December Sunday in 1988 (the exact date of the day is not specified by the witness, and we date the case as the first Sunday of the month). Around 00:35 on a December Sunday morning in 1988, a driver and his passenger suddenly realize that a bright light illuminates the entire landscape. The witness turns off their headlights and observes a dark mass from which the light emanates, and hears a faint humming. The light gradually disappears then quickly moves towards the west, plunging the landscape back into complete darkness. This observation case mentions an imprecisely dated event, and the account is not detailed. The witness was very moved by the observation, as was his wife, and the witness claims she does not want to talk about it. No other testimony will be collected. The observed phenomenon (a strong lighting of the sky for several minutes) could be a light phenomenon behind clouds (e.g. atmospheric re-entry of satellite debris) or a glare due to fatigue. The lack of precision in the date makes any verification impossible. This moderately strange and very unconsistent case is classified "C" for lack of precise information.
