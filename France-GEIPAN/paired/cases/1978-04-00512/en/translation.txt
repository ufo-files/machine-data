Description: On April 27, 1978, around 10 PM, a motorist observed three blinking red lights at 100 meters above ground level. At first, the witness could not discern any specific shape or source for the lights, but noticed they were moving slowly from East to West. A faint humming sound was heard. During the observation, one of the lights descended closer to the ground and appeared to inspect it with what looked like a spotlight. The other two lights remained stationary. The witness then saw a dome-shaped object illuminated in various colors. Local press reported other testimonies regarding this phenomenon, which remains unexplained.
