Description: On September 12, 2025, between 09:44 and 09:46, from his home in the commune of Cachan (Val-de-Marne), a witness briefly observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky, between east and southeast. The observation, made from a room through a glass bay, concerns a horizontal elongated shape composed of a series of aligned lights. The overall appearance evokes aircraft windows, with a constant medium brightness, no color, or perceptible sound. The object followed a straight and slow trajectory for a sequence estimated at one or two seconds. The consistency* of the case is assessed as low to medium. The observation is based on a unique testimony, without iconographic support. The technical questionnaire presents data discrepancies, notably between the initial duration of 1 to 2 seconds and the mention of a disappearance after several minutes. The absence of additional contact did not allow these contradictions to be resolved or the distance or apparent dimensions data to be refined. *According to the GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The perceived strangeness by the witness is evaluated as low: the witness itself rated the strangeness of its observation as 1 out of 5, specifying that it was merely intrigued by the unusual appearance of aligned lights. The absence of an acoustic signature and the perception of a structure resembling windows are the main factors in the reporting. Several explanatory hypotheses can be considered, notably the fleeting observation of an aircraft seen at a particular angle, some of whose structures or openings could have produced the impression of aligned lights, or a transient light reflection observed through the glass bay. However, the available information is insufficient to rigorously test these hypotheses, in particular due to the lack of reliable data regarding the distance, apparent size, and actual trajectory of the phenomenon. In the absence of additional elements to characterize the phenomenon and despite a relented witness, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C": lack of data.
