Description: On May 14, 2024, at 11:30 PM, two witnesses were walking their dog along the Agay esplanade, located on the heights of Saint-Raphaël (06), when they spotted in the sky, heading northwest, a very bright white oval shape positioned below a cloud layer. Attached to this shape was a thick diagonal light trail of the same color. After a few seconds, the unidentified aerial phenomenon (PAN) moved rapidly at high speed towards the south, then immediately returned to its initial position where it remained stationary. No noise was heard by the first witness. The observation lasted approximately ten minutes. The witnesses then started running towards their home when they realized that the PAN was heading towards them. The consistency* of this observation case is excellent. Although only one of the two witnesses completed the GEIPAN questionnaire, the very detailed testimony was written the day after the observation. Moreover, videos from two webcams could be analyzed, clearly showing the unidentified aerial phenomenon (PAN), thus contributing to its identification (see investigation report). *According to the GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The investigation established that this PAN observed was very likely coming from a powerful skytracker, that is, an external beam projector with a single or multiple beam equipped with a 180° scanning system, probably used by a private individual from one of the villas located in a place named in the commune of Bagnols-en-Forêt. This conclusion is based on the following elements: - Oval shape and white color, typical characteristics of a skytracker. - The presence of low clouds, which allowed the light emitted from the ground to generate a bright spot visible at the base of the clouds. - Rapid movement of the light source, consistent with the dynamic capabilities of a skytracker-type projector installed on the ground. - Consultation of the video archives of the surveillance cameras of the cities of Fréjus and Puget-sur-Argens, confirming the presence of a skytracker in the observation axis and at the time corresponding to the phenomenon. - Detection of a light beam typical of a skytracker in the municipal camera images, whose visibility varies depending on atmospheric conditions such as nebulosity and humidity. - Consistency of the respective positions of the projected beam on the ground and its reflection on the cloud base, observed coherently on the three analyzed sources: direct testimonies as well as video captures from the two cameras. - Precise determination of the observation azimuts of the PAN and the location of the ground-based skytracker, which converge towards the same area located at the southern limit of the inhabited sectors of the commune of Bagnols-en-Forêt, about 20 km from the witnesses. The municipality of Bagnols-en-Forêt, contacted, was unable to determine the origin of this skytracker following investigations. The case is classified as 'A', very certain observation of a skytracker.
