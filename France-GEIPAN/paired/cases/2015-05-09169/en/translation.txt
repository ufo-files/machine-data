Summary: Observation of the silent passage of three bright orange balls of light: lack of information and cross-references.
--- seg-895c4e6248c7f14e4cfb139c [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Sunday, May 3, 2015, at 1:30 a.m., a witness observed the silent passage of three very bright, vivid orange orbs in the night sky. The witness has time to go get a camera to film his observation, but he will be disappointed with the video quality and will not provide it to GEIPAN. No further testimony will be collected. The observation of orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, one weekend night, first suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement is observed from East to West, which does not correspond well with the light South wind recorded in Caen (6 km), while the terrain cannot explain such a sharp change in direction at only 6 km. The weather report for the end of the evening in Caen indicates a sustained southeast wind with rain (see the Météociel report). The rain made the atmosphere very clear (see the horizontal visibility indicator at 55km), and thus made the lanterns appear brighter. At the time of the observation, there was a light south wind (see the Météociel record). The movement of Thai lanterns follows the local wind: it is very regular if the wind is established and if the terrain is flat, more agitated if the area is subject to various origin convections. The witness is disappointed with the video rendering: the brightness remains low, proving that the objects were not that bright; the witness, in complete darkness, under an overcast sky, perceived the lights as very bright. The video shows the balls as whiter due to the automatic white balance effect; the diamond shape corresponds to a defocused (blurred) image, the diamond is actually the shape of the camera's diaphragm. Due to the wind conditions incompatible with the observed movement, the GEIPAN cannot confirm the hypothesis of Thai lanterns. This case is classified as C due to a lack of information (video, other witnesses) and overlaps.
