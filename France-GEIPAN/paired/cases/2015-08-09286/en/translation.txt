--- seg-bd1939e2a15ea518bc9e7343 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Saturday, August 15, 2015, at 10:30 PM, two people observed the passage of 7 glowing points in a clear sky. Round in shape and orange-red in color, the points silently moved first on a straight trajectory then seemingly ascending. Only one well-documented testimony was collected. The observation of orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from Northwest to Southeast, which is consistent with the North wind recorded in Angers (see the Meteorology report for Angers on the day of the observation). The photos are entirely compatible with this type of object. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of Thai lanterns.
