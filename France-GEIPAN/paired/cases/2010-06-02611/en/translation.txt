Of average strangeness and good consistency despite a lack of orientation, this observation of a large black dot cannot be concluded at this time; further checks on various hypotheses cannot be made. Unfortunately, the one-month delay after which the questionnaire arrived does not allow for obtaining radar records. This case is classified C due to lack of independent testimonies and cross-references.
