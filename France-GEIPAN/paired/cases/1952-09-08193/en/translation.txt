Description: Recent testimony (21/11/2010) of an old observation (21/09/1952). The witness, then a child, was returning from vacation in their parents' car when they were stopped by another motorist on a road. Very close by, a circular craft of metallic gray color hovers above a field at an altitude of approximately 15m. The craft suddenly starts, then descends back to its initial position and heads back towards the clouds. No further testimony will be collected on this phenomenon. This old case of a very strange observation was submitted to Geipan in 2010. The account does not correspond to any of the classic misidentifications. An investigation would be quite difficult; as this case is part of the early 1950s UFO wave, the GEIPAN is releasing it as is without investigation, so that it may potentially be linked to other similar observations. This GEIPAN case, from a single witness reported 58 years after the event, is classified "C" due to a lack of information.
