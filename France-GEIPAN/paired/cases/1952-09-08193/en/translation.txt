Description: Recent testimony (21/11/2010) of an old observation (21/09/1952). The witness, then a child, is returning from vacation in his parents' car when they are stopped by another motorist by the side of the road. Very close by, a circular craft of metallic gray color hovers above a field at an altitude of approximately 15 meters. The craft suddenly starts, then descends back to its initial position and departs towards the clouds. No other testimony will be collected on this phenomenon. This old case of a very strange observation was submitted to Geipan in 2010. The account does not correspond to any of the classic misperceptions. An investigation would be very difficult; as this case is part of the beginning of the 1950s UFO wave, GEIPAN publishes it as is without investigation, so that it may potentially be linked to other similar observations. This case, coming from a single witness, reported 58 years after the facts, is classified "C" due to lack of information.
