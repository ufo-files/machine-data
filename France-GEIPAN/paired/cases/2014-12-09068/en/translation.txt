Summary: Observation of the movement on the same trajectory of two orange lights: probable observation of Thai lanterns
--- seg-d19041ef9108d9392f025e03 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 25, 2014, around 1 a.m., a witness observes the movement of two orange lights in the sky. The lights follow the same trajectory, with a few seconds interval. Only one testimony is collected. The observation of orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a festive night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby for Christmas. The movement is observed from Southwest to East, which is consistent with the 11-20 km/h West wind reported by Météociel at Reims-Prunay (40 km East). The object is probably flying lower than the witness estimates, around 50 meters altitude, as he already notices quite a few details. The relative blur observed is mainly due to the flickering flame, and the steam or smoke released from it. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of Thai lanterns.
