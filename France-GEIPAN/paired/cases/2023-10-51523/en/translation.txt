Summary: Nighttime observation of the silent movement of a PAN consisting of 3 faintly glowing orange points: probable observation of 3 birds illuminated by urban lighting.
--- seg-18309d60f745f380befcf32d [needs-review] ---
Description: The two witnesses are on the terrace of their home located in Quimper (29) on October 16, 2023, when they observe in the sky to the west, around 21:15, a phenomenon consisting of three faintly glowing orange points, forming a triangle for T1, moving silently, horizontally, according to a regular straight-line trajectory, and at an elevation of approximately 20°. No noise was perceived by the witnesses who observed the PAN for a few seconds before it disappeared, obscured by a neighbor's house. The consistency* is good, with two dependent witnesses each completing a Technical Questionnaire, but with no photo or video of the PAN. A field investigation was also conducted with cognitive interviews. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The high level of strangeness perceived by the witnesses is related (particularly for T1) to the high speed of the PAN. This is due to the absence of a visible reference point in the night sky, which does not allow for its positioning in terms of distance. As a result, his perception is that it is much farther and faster (500 to 1000 meters distance, with a speed of 300 to 500 meters per hour). Additionally, the triangular shape formed by the three glowing points moving together led him to assume a "craft" with atypical characteristics (shape, translucent silhouette, gliding through the air) without considering the possibility that the three points could be separated. The investigation examined several hypotheses (see the investigation report): the aircraft hypothesis was quickly eliminated due to the absence of noise, the atypical position of the entire formation, constantly inclined at 45°, and the absence of radar traces. The drone hypothesis was also not retained, due to the significant size of the whole, which would have implied a short distance, incompatible with the absence of noise, or the simultaneous presence of 3 craft. The hypothesis of observing three celestial lanterns proved to be incompatible with the direction of the wind, on the ground and in altitude. Finally, the last hypothesis, that of observing three birds with white ventral plumage, proves to be the most plausible for the following reasons: - The orange color is consistent with that of birds whose white ventral plumage is illuminated by the urban lights of the city of Quimper (in October 2023, many streetlights producing an orange (sodium vapor) color were still active in the streets). - The punctual shape of each individual suggests a certain distance and, therefore, a very small angular size, making it difficult for the witnesses to perceive the details (wings, etc.). Calculations show an angular size less than a degree for each individual of a group of three birds flying at their cruising speed at a distance between 74 and 93 meters. The observation conditions (nighttime, low contrast, and very short observation duration) did not allow the witnesses to see anything other than the bodies of the birds, reduced to simple point-like forms. Furthermore, the observation angle (20° above the horizon) does not allow for the complete visibility of the wings for a "level" flight, horizontal in relation to the ground, of the birds. We note the divergence in assessment between the witnesses, T2 describing three independent lights, while T1 perceives a triangle with three orange lights, which is indicative of a perceptual illusion caused by the identical appearance of the three lights moving simultaneously in the same direction. We finally requested an expert opinion from the association "Bretagne Vivante" on this hypothesis. This nature protection association confirms that it is possibly the observation of 3 birds of the species bull-headed heron, very common in Quimper and which move at night. The association specifies that it could be three of these birds joining late at their roost. The GEIPAN classifies this observation case as 'B': probable observation of 3 birds illuminated by urban lighting.
