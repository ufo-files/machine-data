Summary: Observation of a cigar-shaped craft and lights.
--- seg-f8360fd4d43fcdb20c485693 [needs-review] ---
Description: Between January 24th and 28th, 1979, three witnesses observed peculiar phenomena in the same area. On January 24th, between 7:00 and 7:15 AM, a witness reported seeing an orange-colored, cigar-shaped craft. It moved slowly along a north-south trajectory and suddenly lifted vertically, disappearing at an extremely high speed while changing color. No military maneuvers by the navy were reported during this period, and no other testimonies were collected regarding this phenomenon, for which we lack information. On January 28th, around 10:15 PM, two other individuals spotted light phenomena. Blue and white lights with a red dot (according to one witness) appeared at regular intervals. According to the gendarmerie investigation, these lights originated from the traffic lights located at the top of the masts of a German cargo ship at anchor in a bay. These two different observation testimonies are classified as A.
