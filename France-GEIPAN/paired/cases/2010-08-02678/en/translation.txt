--- seg-ea8307acc69eabe7c96a7afb [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 26, 2010, at approximately 9:20 PM, two individuals observed above their residence a flaming object that suddenly broke into four distinct pieces. Just before igniting, it had a straight trajectory from north to south and oscillated vertically. No noise was heard during the object's movement or disintegration. A gendarmerie report and an administrative intelligence supplement were received at GEIPAN on November 15, 2010. The additional investigation revealed that the witness likely observed a Thai lantern from a preliminary test prior to a significant release of Thai lanterns planned for a communal festival during the weekend of August 28-29, 2010.
