Description: On October 13th, around 8 PM, two witnesses in Troyes and Reims observed the passage of an elongated object. Colored lights were also seen. One of the two witnesses made a similar observation on October 18th between 7 PM and 8 PM. No abnormal phenomena were detected at the satellite detection center of Aube. The technical service of the Barberey-Saint-Sulpice airport will inform the gendarmerie that no unusual observations were reported during these days. No other testimonies were collected. These witnesses probably observed the passage of an aircraft.
