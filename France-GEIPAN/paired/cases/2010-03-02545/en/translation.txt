Summary: Observation of the movement of a dark object in the sky: lack of information and cross-checking.
--- seg-07686ac345325f6974cd3d8d [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 31, 2010, at 6:20 PM, an individual observed from their balcony a dark, indefinable shape moving in the sky. The witness had time to take photographs and called their spouse. Only one testimony was collected. This case of observing a distant object (estimated at 2 to 3 km away), with chaotic movement (see reconstruction based on photos), presents an average degree of strangeness. Due to the photographs, the consistency of this case is good. The photographed object could correspond to many possibilities of bodies moving through the air between the witness and the nuclear power plant: most likely a large bird, as the witness's wife initially perceived (see statement), a tethered balloon, a kite, a radio-controlled model... A tethered balloon could exhibit such behavior due to the wind, which was strong that day (around 25 km/h), however, the object disappearing into the clouds does not correspond, unless the clouds obscured it. One could also think of a helicopter, given the heavy traffic in the area (see statement), but the photographed shape does not match well. The dynamic behavior of the object does not present any strange characteristics: it could correspond to a bird or a kite as the witnesses initially thought, or a tethered balloon or a helicopter. Verifying these multiple hypotheses is no longer possible. This case, credible but not particularly strange, is classified "C" due to the lack of additional information and cross-references.
