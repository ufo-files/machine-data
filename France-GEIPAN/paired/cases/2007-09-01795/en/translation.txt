Description: A witness reports observing a strange shape in a photograph without direct observation during the taking of the picture. This type of case of spots detected on a photo while nothing was visually observed has become very frequent with the proliferation of digital cameras. The origin of the largest spot is probably a bird or insect that flew relatively close to the camera, hence the blur. The small gray central spot is a minor defect of the camera, likely visible on all photos. This very unstrange case is classified "B": probable passage of a bird or insect within the camera's field of view.
