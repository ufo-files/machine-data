--- seg-526d1a6d5c14b1026d4ee716 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 17, 2012, at 4:00 AM, a witness on their terrace observed for 15 seconds the silent passage of several (10/15) transparent spheres flying in the clear sky. The entire group quickly moved towards the North in the direction of a cloud formation where everything disappeared. Only one testimony was collected. Based on the objective elements defined in the investigation report, namely: - transparent, spherical objects moving silently at an altitude between 1460 meters and 2000 meters. - the absence of noise, we can conclude that the UAPs observed by the witness have characteristics, particularly physical ones, that do not resemble any known natural or manufactured object. This case is classified as "D1" as an observation of objects of unknown nature. This testimony is of good consistency: precise, but from a single witness without a photo. The observation is strange as the objects have an appearance far from ordinary, although their behavior does not present anything extraordinary.
