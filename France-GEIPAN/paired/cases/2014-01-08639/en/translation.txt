Description: On January 19, 2014, at 8:30 PM, a driver on a highway observed three orange glowing shapes in the sky that slowly moved beneath him. He then saw a sharp triangle. A very light, variable wind from the south to the east was recorded: weather. The behavior of the objects is consistent with objects carried by the wind. The orange color suggests the color of Thai lantern flames. However, the triangular shape described by the witness does not seem compatible with this hypothesis. (See photo GEIPAN DSC7031 of three Thai lanterns that could resemble what the witness saw). This testimony has an average to low consistency: the witness was driving and observing through the windshield; it is very difficult to estimate the actual movement of the objects due to the witness's own movement; GEIPAN did not receive any other testimony of this phenomenon despite the area being very populated. GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of corroboration to refute or confirm the hypothesis of Thai lanterns.
