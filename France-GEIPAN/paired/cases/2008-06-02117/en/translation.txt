Summary: Observation and photographs of a moving luminous phenomenon in the sky: lack of information.
--- seg-b080355674e7cdc96dd72b1c [needs-review] ---
Description: Following a press article, the GEIPAN decides to investigate an observation made on June 23, 2008 at 4:52 a.m. by a witness who took photographs of a luminous phenomenon in the sky. According to the investigation report, there are many inconsistencies in the testimony (shooting conditions, untraceable additional witnesses), and a strong coincidence between the diameter of the photographed object and the Moon (however, the GEIPAN was unable to replicate this hazy aspect and its colors). The elements are too fragile and disputable for this case to be considered consistent. It is classified as 'C' due to a lack of reliable information.
