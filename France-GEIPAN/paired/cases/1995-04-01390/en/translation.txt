Description: On April 30, 1995, around 3:30 AM, a witness and his wife observed a bright glowing mass with numerous bright points moving in the sky. The investigation will quickly show that it is likely a disco light projector "Sky rose" whose light is visible for several kilometers depending on cloud cover and atmospheric conditions.
