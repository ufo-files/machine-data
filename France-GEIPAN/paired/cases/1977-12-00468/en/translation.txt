Description: Periodic reports of a glowing object. These occurred on December 19th around 10 PM and December 20th at 4 AM and again around 10 PM. Witnesses observed a glowing object for extended periods, which alternated between green, orange, and red lights. The object moved with distinct stops. During the gendarmerie investigation, TV interference was noted at the times of the object's observations during the early night. The hypothesis of observing a communications satellite was suggested but not validated due to a lack of information.
