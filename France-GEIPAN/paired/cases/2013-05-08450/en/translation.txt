--- seg-16c3be93f874e7560de53282 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 3, 2013, around 4:45 PM, a witness on the school's courtyard was intrigued by light sources arranged in a triangle in the sky. They moved slowly towards the East. The witness (T1) approached another person who also observed these objects. These two individuals observed the phenomenon for a long time (30 to 40 minutes) while heading back to their respective homes. No other testimonies were collected. Based on the elements outlined in the investigation report (see attached document), notably the shape, appearance, and brightness comparable to a cluster of Mylar balloons floating with the wind at high altitude, as well as the slow movement in calm atmosphere, this case is classified as "B": probable observation of a cluster of Mylar balloons. This testimony is consistent, precise, and well-detailed. Presence of two eyewitnesses who testified, but absence of photographs (although one was taken) and videos. The observation is not particularly strange, as the objects are ultimately quite mundane, but they can appear unusual under certain conditions. This case is classified B: probable observation of a cluster of Mylar balloons.
