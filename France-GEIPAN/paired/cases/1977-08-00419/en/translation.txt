Description: On August 6, 1977, at 1:30 a.m., two individuals observed a strange light phenomenon outside their home, which intrigued them. A bright, yellow-orange glow moved slowly, stopped, and briefly displayed two small red lights before continuing its path. During the three minutes of observation, no noise was heard. On August 13, 1977, at the same time, one of the witnesses again observed an oval object resting in a field across the river from his property from his home. The glowing object was stationary on the ground, and no noise was heard during the ten minutes of observation. The field owner did not notice any traces or unusual changes at the presumed landing site. The gendarmerie found three shallow holes, but could not determine their exact origin. Soil samples were collected. Radioactivity tests were negative, and checks with nearby meteorological, engineering, and radar services were also unremarkable. No further testimonies about this phenomenon were gathered.
