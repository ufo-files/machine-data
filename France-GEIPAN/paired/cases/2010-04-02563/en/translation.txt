Summary: Observations of silent, bright red lights at low altitude: probable observation of Thai lanterns passing by.
--- seg-1f2112c037a579e95911a67f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Saturday, April 10, 2010, between 9:00 PM and 11:00 PM, several people observed the silent movement of two very bright red lights in the sky at low altitude and on a straight trajectory from North to South. These observations have all the characteristics of a Thai lantern passage: color, duration, movement according to the wind. This case is classified as B, very probable case of Thai lantern observation.
