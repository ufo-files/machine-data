Summary: Observations by two motorists of a white light followed by a black triangular shape in motion in the sky: observation of an unidentified phenomenon.
--- seg-5ccf5640ee750bc1fb4583f4 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 26, 2019, a couple was traveling in separate vehicles from Vesoul (70) towards their home in Villers-lès-Luxeuil (70). The husband (T1), following his wife (T2), observes a first luminous phenomenon shortly after 5:00 p.m., near the village of Saulx-de-Vesoul (70). This is a white light that appears to move beneath the cloud layer. After approximately 15 seconds, this light rises vertically and disappears into the clouds, at a distance estimated between 10 and 12 km from the village. Less than 10 seconds later, it reappears above the area. T1 then attempts to alert T2 by performing flashing headlights and calling her on her phone, but she does not perceive the phenomenon. Later, upon their arrival in Villers-lès-Luxeuil (70), the two witnesses successively observe (T2 first, then T1) a large black triangular structure, nearly stationary above the village. The object presents a bright white light at the front as well as a fixed red light. T1 stops his vehicle, turns off the radio, and opens the windows to better perceive any sounds. The PAN then moves slowly, gliding through the air without making any noise, at an estimated altitude of less than 100 meters. The observation lasts approximately 20 seconds, until, according to T2, the object rolls over itself before disappearing towards North-Northwest. The case consistency is good: two witnesses, who were highly cooperative. However, it should be noted that the testimonies cannot be considered entirely independent. Furthermore, no physical evidence, such as photos or videos, is available to corroborate the statements. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The degree of strangeness, as perceived by the witnesses, comes from the size of the PAN, its dark triangular shape, and the complete absence of noise. Three hypotheses were examined (see the investigation report): a civil aircraft of the Robin DR400 type, a Mirage 2000-5F fighter jet, and a military drone. - The military drone hypothesis seemed plausible due to a note (SUP AIP 299/19) mentioning the presence of a temporary regulated zone (ZRT) related to a potential transit of military drones between 11/07/2019 and 05/06/2020, a period encompassing the date of the observation. However, the permitted flight altitudes (from 14,935 m to 16,764 m) are not compatible with an unaided-eye observation from the ground, taking into account the total cloud cover (8/8 octa) and the altitude below 3,000 m. Based on this, the hypothesis was dismissed. - The hypothesis of the Robin DR400 is strengthened by the presence of this low-flying aircraft within the observation's temporal window and by the concordance of its trajectory. Indeed, it has been confirmed that the aircraft was in the process of landing at Air Base BA116. Additionally, the observed white and red lights can be attributed to the landing beacon and position light on the left wing. However, the apparent size of the aircraft (compared to the full Moon) does not correspond to the "impressive" and "dark" size of the PAN described by the witnesses. T1 specifically states that "my hand (extended) would not have been enough to cover the dark triangle." Furthermore, the dark triangular shape finds no logical explanation, except for a possible optical illusion. - The hypothesis of a Mirage 2000-5F is supported by the confirmation of a military exercise departing from the BA116 base, involving four Mirages, one of which followed a trajectory consistent with that of PAN. This hypothesis also seems to better correspond to the observed triangular shape. However, it presents several inconsistencies that should be examined in more detail: - a 10-minute deviation from the established observation time, - an elevation (14°) significantly lower than that mentioned by the witnesses (>30°), - a size that does not match the witness's statement, - the absence of noise perceived by the witnesses and their failure to identify it, despite their prolonged residence in the village and their familiarity with Mirage flights. Given the persistent strangeness of the phenomenon after investigation, and the consistency of the testimony, the GEIPAN classifies this case in category « D1 »: observation of an unidentified phenomenon.
