Description: On Friday, June 5, 2015, around 11:15 PM, a witness and his wife observed from their bathroom window facing North, the slow and silent passage of red lights in the clear sky. The lights moved towards the East. The witness reported his observation to the gendarmerie. No other testimonies were collected. The observation of orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from North to East, which could be consistent with the very light and unstable wind reported in Salon-de-Provence at about 10 km (see METEOCIEL record). Due to the lack of knowledge about the local wind at the exact moment of the observation, the GEIPAN cannot confirm the hypothesis of Thai lanterns. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to the lack of a photo and information about the local wind.
