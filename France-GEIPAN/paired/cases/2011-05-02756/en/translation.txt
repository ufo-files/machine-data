--- seg-2da544a9ca4be7c6bb675455 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of May 13, 2011, at 12:35 AM, several people observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky. One witness reported seeing the movements of 7 very bright orange orbs. The witness's description aligns with the characteristics of Thai lanterns in terms of color, number, and altitude. The circular and erratic movements may seem surprising, but in an urban environment, there are many local sources of atmospheric turbulence: automobile traffic, forced ventilation of buildings, heating, or air conditioning. Other testimonies indicate chaotic movements of the lanterns above cities. Even though releasing Thai lanterns in cities is prohibited, it appears that this prohibition is not always respected, perhaps due to ignorance of the perpetrators. This case is therefore classified as "B": probable Thai lanterns.
