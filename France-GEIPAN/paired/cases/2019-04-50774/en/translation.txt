Summary: Silent passage of an object described as "a fireball with a gray dome on top": observation of a celestial lantern (also known as a Thai lantern).
--- seg-3dcdc9b48fc7debab894b5a4 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 18, 2019, at approximately 9:17 PM, while in his garden, the witness observed a round object, bright red in color, topped with a gray dome, passing close to him and over the trees in his garden. He then took out his smartphone, attempted to take several photos and a video (very short, too short) before the phenomenon quickly disappeared towards the southwest. Only one testimony was collected regarding this phenomenon. The observation was fleeting. It lasted only one minute. This prevents immediate identification of the phenomenon. The testimony is consistent, and the photos help to better pinpoint the location, as well as the azimuths and elevations of the UFO (see the investigation report). This object exhibits characteristics of a sky lantern (also known as a Thai or Chinese lantern): trajectory (from north-west to south-east) and speed similar to wind, orange color, fiery appearance from below, and a "gray dome" on top, round shape. The strangeness is mainly attributed to the presence of the "gray dome," but as explained in the report, the GEIPAN has already handled this type of description, for example, the "lantern" case in Pontarlier (25) on 02.12.2019. In this case as well, the speed of the PAN is high. Focusing on this point, the station logs show that the average speed was indeed significant, and the presence of wind gusts can also contribute to a high rate of movement. The photographs, of fairly poor quality, prevent formal identification, but the available elements, such as wind direction and the object's trajectory, are consistent and suggest that we are dealing with a solitary lantern. The object is clearly carried by the wind. Image processing with our IPACO software reinforces the probable hypothesis of a lantern. The lantern must have been launched from one of the nearby houses. The (remote) investigation did not allow the author of this release to be identified. GEIPAN classifies this observation as A: observation of a sky lantern.
