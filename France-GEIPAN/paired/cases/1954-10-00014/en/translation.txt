Summary: Observation of a glowing disk moving very slowly: probable observation of a scientific balloon.
--- seg-b11c5f6c645d122b235da19f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 6, 1954, around 8:00 PM, several members of a family observed from their home for approximately 40 minutes a bright red-orange disk with an apparent diameter half that of a full moon, supporting some kind of nacelle. The light intensity varies, alternating between bright and dim. The object appears to be above Senlis and moves slowly to the right. The GEIPAN received a concise intelligence report from the Gendarmerie. Only one testimony was collected. At the time of the observation, the sun had been down for half an hour; it still illuminates high-altitude objects like planes or scientific balloons. The shape described by the witnesses and the observation direction to the West support this. The GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable observation of a scientific balloon.
