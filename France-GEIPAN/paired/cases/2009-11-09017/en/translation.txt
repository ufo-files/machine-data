Description: On November 26, 2009, around 10:45 PM, two individuals were surprised to witness in the clear sky the silent passage of a dozen square-shaped objects with a central white light. The observation lasted approximately one and a half minutes. Only one testimony was collected on October 13, 2014. The observation of bright objects crossing the sky in a few minutes suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The unusual shape described by the witness (square with a central bright point and two wings) could be an interpretation of the classic Thai lantern's barrel shape, or it could be the actual shape of peculiar lanterns. The movement was observed from West to East, which is consistent with the North-West wind reported by the weather in Nice. The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", a probable observation of Thai lanterns.
