--- seg-d62e0468f22423428a823c1c [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Tuesday, November 12, 2013, from 6:55 to 7:15 AM, a witness observed a multicolored light, first stationary, then moving in a straight line and then in a curve at the speed of a hot air balloon. The luminous phenomenon was perceived as a vertical stick. No particular noise was heard. No other testimony was collected. Based on the gathered elements:

- Areas suitable for the flight of small motorized craft
- Behavior similar to the movement of such vehicles
- Night flight regulations respected

But also:

- No similar luminous balising found
- Nature and behavior of a part of the PAN unknown
- No events related to the use of ultralights or helicopters reported on the day of the observation

This case is classified as "C," due to the lack of information to validate either of the hypotheses, with a possible confusion with an ultralight, helicopter, or drone under certain conditions. Another origin could be involved. This testimony is of good consistency: very precise but without photographs or videos. The observation is strange as rather unusual, because although, given its movement and geographical location, the PAN could be assimilated to a motorized craft: helicopter, ultralight, or drone, the lights observed and certain physical characteristics described in the testimony do not correspond to what is known about such vehicles; the varied colors could potentially be explained by atmospheric scintillation. Due to the only moderately strange nature of this observation case, the GEIPAN did not deem it necessary to conduct a more in-depth investigation. Elements provided later by other witnesses could potentially change this conclusion.
