--- seg-28242c1cf7aa145473b4d6eb [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the morning of January 3, 1980, at 8:05 a.m., a witness, while closing their shutters, spotted a red ball in the sky. This ball disappeared behind clouds and then reappeared in the sky. It moved erratically and at a very high speed. The given trajectory was southeast to northwest. Interrupting their neighbor, the neighbor did not see the described object. As the witness had taken photographs, the gendarmes were able to examine the negative, which showed a brown spot. To this day, the negative has not been found in the archives. No other testimony about this phenomenon has been collected. The witness likely made an astronomical observation of a full moon that, at the given time, was setting and illuminated by the rising sun, giving the celestial body its red color.
