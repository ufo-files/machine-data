Description: On March 19, 2013, at 20:30, a resident of NICE (06) observed from his home the passage of three bright points in a line across the sky. The observation lasted approximately 10 seconds before the PAN disappeared from the witness's view, obscured by a tree. Of average strangeness and consistency (a fairly complete unique testimony, albeit with poor weather coherence), this case lacks external data to be formally identified. The PAN indeed shares characteristics with several hypothesis types. While a misidentification with a satellite formation or an airplane can be a priori excluded, the observation could still be explained by the presence of a fragmented meteor or an object carried by the wind. However, the short duration of the observation does not allow either of these two hypotheses to be ruled out. It is not the witness's visual perception that is in question, but the interpretation the witness makes of his observation through his feelings (surprise, brief observation). This case is classified C, due to lack of information.
