Description: On May 25, 1979, at 1:10 a.m., two police officers on duty spotted in the sky a luminous phenomenon that was moving and intrigued them. At low altitude, four oval-shaped lights with a white-yellow hue passed slowly overhead and then changed direction. It was impossible for them to determine if the lights belonged to a single object or were autonomous phenomena. A faint humming sound was heard before the phenomenon disappeared towards the southeast, hidden by the landscape. No interference was noted on their onboard equipment. Based on the witnesses' description and the proximity of several airports (in addition to Lyon-Satolas), it is highly probable that the two police officers witnessed an aircraft in approach phase.
