Summary: Observation of the silent movement of a dark triangular shape bordered by lights.
--- seg-5fd9191bee2ffe69e0664c70 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 12, 2009, at 11:10 PM, a witness observed above them lights delineating a large black triangular mass moving on a north-south trajectory without any sound. No other witnesses were reported. The witness described alignments of lights, similar to stars, forming a black triangle. This triangle moved slowly towards the sea. It is possible that the black triangle was merely an optical illusion created by the alignment of the glowing points, which could be Thai lanterns. The movement of the points might only be due to the wind (light, from the north). The description of the lights and the movement is insufficiently precise for the GEIPAN to confirm this hypothesis. This case is classified "C": Insufficient elements to conclude.
