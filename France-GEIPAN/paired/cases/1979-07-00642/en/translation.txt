Summary: Unauthorized fireworks shots.
--- seg-b880b18a5007a1eaa485b48b [needs-review] ---
Description: During the night of July 18 to 19, 1979, several residents of the same neighborhood heard between 10:30 PM and 12:15 AM sharp, short noises. These frightened individuals then saw unusual lights in the sky. The same phenomenon occurred again on the evening of July 20. The Gendarmerie investigation revealed that these phenomena were caused by whistling rocket fires launched for fun and without authorization from the garden of a nearby pavilion.
