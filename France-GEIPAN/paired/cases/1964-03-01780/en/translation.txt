Description: A person reports that in 1964, he was a witness to a particular phenomenon. Returning to the Bourget school base around 2:30 AM on an early March Sunday, he will be surprised to regularly catch sight of a faint, short-duration glow about 200 or 300 meters away from him. As he approached the fence of the military base, he saw 5 silhouettes moving near a craft that was on the ground. The upper part of the object, illuminated by the glow, is rounded. The silhouettes are making round trips from inside the object to its immediate surroundings. Suddenly, the light goes out and in total silence, the object rises a meter or two off the ground and remains stationary for one to two seconds. The witness then describes the object as having a round or oval shape, a flat bottom, and a raised center and sides. Its dimensions are 8 to 10 m in diameter and its height is 2.50m to 3 m. This object rises vertically without transition at very high speed towards the south. No noise is heard and a brief vertical streak appears as well as two other tracks in the sky. The next day, the witness will go to the site of his observation, and although separated by a dozen meters from the exact location by the military base fence, he will notice a slight depression in the grass and some bushes. He will decide not to talk to anyone about this observation for which we lack confirmed information. These a priori very strange facts, reported very late (June 2007) by a single witness, are unverifiable and no serious analysis can be conducted.
