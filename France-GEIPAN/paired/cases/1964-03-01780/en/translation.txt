Description: A person reports witnessing a peculiar phenomenon in 1964. While returning to the Bourget school base around 2:30 AM on an early March Sunday, they noticed a regular, brief, dim glow approximately 200-300 meters away. As they approached the military base fence, they observed five silhouettes moving near a craft sitting on the ground. The upper part of the object, illuminated by the glow, was rounded. The silhouettes made round trips between the object's interior and its immediate surroundings. Suddenly, the glow faded, and in complete silence, the object rose a meter or two off the ground and remained stationary for one to two seconds. The witness describes the object as having a round or oval shape, a flat bottom, a rounded top in the center and on the sides, with dimensions of 8-10 meters in diameter and a height of 2.50-3 meters. The object rose vertically at a very high speed towards the south. No sound was heard, and a brief vertical trail, as well as two other traces in the sky, appeared. The next day, the witness visited the observation site and, although separated by a dozen meters from the exact location by the military base fence, they noticed a slight depression in the grass and some bushes. They decided not to tell anyone about this observation, for which we lack confirmed information. These seemingly very strange events, reported very late (June 2007) by a single witness, are unverifiable, and no serious analysis can be conducted.
