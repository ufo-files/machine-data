--- seg-5e22c4e63747be3c109ccb11 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 5, 1955, at 20:55, three witnesses in Cavaillon observed for 20 seconds in the direction of the west side of the Luberon mountain range a red-hot rectangular light in the sky. Initially stationary, the phenomenon then moved very quickly, changing shape (from a crescent to a ball) and heading towards the east. It shrank and then disappeared. Astronomical verification eliminated the Moon. The hypothesis of an aircraft was abandoned. The GEIPAN classified this case as C due to a lack of information and corroboration.
