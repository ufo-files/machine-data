Summary: Observations of a yellow-orange light phenomenon in the sky: flashbang grenade used during military training.
--- seg-8812b2ea72346dd8b9dd03b2 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 12, 2012 at 7:20 pm (UTC), a pilot and a controller were intrigued by the presence in the sky of a bright yellow-orange light of high magnitude. A photograph was taken from the plane. The phenomenon was also observed by a ground observer at ESPIRA in AGLY (66) on the same day. Local investigation at the nearby military base revealed that training exercises had taken place over two consecutive nights with illuminating rockets. This well-characterized case is therefore classified as "A".
