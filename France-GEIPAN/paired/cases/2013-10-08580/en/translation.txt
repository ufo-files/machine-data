--- seg-65d61c52d189bc6ddda65334 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 9, 2013, around 5:45 PM, two witnesses observed in the sky a dark flying object with an oval-shaped upper part and a cylindrical-shaped lower part. It was approximately 164 feet (50 meters) high and moving slowly, without noise. The observation of an object at low altitude moving regularly and silently with the wind (a west wind of about 15 km/h that day, according to Meteociel) strongly suggests a helium-filled balloon, possibly a child's balloon. These objects have very varied shapes, and when observed in the sky against the light, they generally appear black. The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as somewhat unusual in category "B": probable observation of a helium-filled balloon.
