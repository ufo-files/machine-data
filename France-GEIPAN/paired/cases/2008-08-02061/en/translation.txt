Description: On August 30, 2008, around 7:50 PM, a witness observed a flying object they were unable to identify. They took a series of photographs and recorded a video of the object's movement. (The video was never sent to GEIPAN). Following a neighborhood investigation, the gendarmerie inquiry concluded it was a kite. There are indeed regular kite enthusiasts who practice this activity at the stadium in the commune of ABLIS. This was confirmed for that day by several witnesses, one of whom reported a particularly high kite in the sky.
