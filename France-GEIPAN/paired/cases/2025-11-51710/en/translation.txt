Description: The witness reports, on the same Technical Questionnaire (QT), two observations made on November 17 and 18, 2025 during two commercial flights between Amsterdam (NL) and Nantes (44). Many bright points are observed for a long time and seem to move or fluctuate, their brightness oscillating from invisible to an intensity comparable to that of an airplane lighthouse. The observations end when the craft begins its descent into the lower cloud layers on approach to Nantes, making the visibility of the phenomenon impossible. Of weak strangeness and very good consistency* (at least 4 witnesses), this case turns out to be an observation of Starlink® satellite flares (see the investigation report). *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The description of the PAN is entirely consistent with this type of observation: straight and short paths always in the same area of the sky, strong light variations, numerous occurrences. The PAN's position in the sky corresponds to the vertical of the Sun, while the latter is below the horizon towards -40°. It is in every way consistent with the analysis of the phenomenon published under this news item GEIPAN a new phenomenon explained: the illumination of Starlink® satellites by the Sun: "satellite flaring". The case is classified "A", observation of Starlink® satellite flares.
