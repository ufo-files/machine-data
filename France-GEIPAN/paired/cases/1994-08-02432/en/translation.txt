Description: Recent testimony (October 13, 2009) of an old observation (August 28, 1994). During a picnic, four family members observe a particular point approaching. A huge copper-colored cylinder with portholes appears to them. It remains stationary at 10 or 15 meters above the ground, then changes direction and speed to disappear instantly. No photographs of the craft or the site were taken at the time. The antiquity of the case makes any investigation difficult, if not impossible.
