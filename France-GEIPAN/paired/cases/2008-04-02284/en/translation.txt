Summary: Observation of a light passing by and disappearing behind a hill: Lack of information
--- seg-a40dda4246e544d1bc136228 [needs-review] ---
Description: On April 25, 2008, around 8:30 PM, a witness and his spouse were intrigued by the passage of a light source identical to a lighthouse above a hill facing their terrace. The observation made by the witnesses could have several origins:

- a head-on view of an airplane. The powerful landing light of the airplane, seen in this case at approximately 10km (6.2 miles), blinds the observer and does not allow them to see the airplane. The airplane, descending, then disappears behind the hill and turns towards the West, after which the beam of its light disappears for the observer. The low traffic at the airport, especially at that time, could explain the observers' surprise.

- a flash from an Iridium satellite could have produced the same effect of a disappearing bright point behind the hill. This unusual case is classified "C" due to a lack of information to validate either of the two hypotheses.
