Description: On Saturday, December 10, 2011, around 11:45 PM, several people witnessed strange lights in the sky that intrigued them. The witnesses' reports are entirely consistent with the observation of Thai lanterns: an airborne object traveling with the wind, colored yellow-orange, with a brighter center. The time and day (near midnight, a Saturday evening) are typical for this type of event. Identifying the individual who released these lanterns would allow this case to be classified as "A." However, without that information, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
