Summary: Observation of the movement of a very bright white point in the night sky: observation of the passage of the International Space Station (ISS).
--- seg-746ccdbd584fbc62b91e3b60 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 5, 2015 at 11:10 PM, a witness is intrigued by a bright white glowing point that moves across the sky before disappearing. This witness testimony of observing a very bright point crossing a portion of the sky is very precise, both in terms of dating and the position in the sky. The trajectory of the phenomenon corresponds exactly to that of the International Space Station (ISS) which was very visible that evening. The witness only observed the end of the passage. (see Calsky log, textual and graphical format on a sky background). A detail of the observation may seem contradictory to this explanation: the witness describes several zig-zags in the trajectory of the phenomenon. This is an illusion of perception named kinetic or autokinetic illusion (see the article on the perception of a moving light point in the sky) which applies to fixed or moving bright points. This illusion is frequent when observing the ISS, particularly bright (see the CLARET observation case (04) 22.08.2012). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the International Space Station (ISS).
