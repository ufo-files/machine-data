--- seg-3dee8c7bdebb61ea8465c87e [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 21, 1954, at 7:30 PM, two gendarmes observed for a maximum of 15 seconds a very bright, glowing streak that silently crossed the sky at high speed in a north-south direction. In the dark night with a clear sky and visible stars, this streak had the shape of a cone with the point forward and the base extended by numerous sparks. Its trajectory was horizontal. Although the witnesses (incorrectly) ruled out a meteor "due to the importance of its dimensions and the horizontality of its trajectory," what they likely saw was a meteoroid entering the atmosphere and fragmenting. The GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable observation of the passage of a bolide caused by the atmospheric entry of a natural meteoroid that fragments.
