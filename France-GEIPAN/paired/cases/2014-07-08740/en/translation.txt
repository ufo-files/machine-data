Description: The witness observed the full moon at 1 a.m. and saw seven yellow-orange orbs silently appear one after the other before disappearing. The observation of successive orange orbs on the same trajectory clearly evokes Thai lanterns. The witness describes a movement from northwest to southeast, consistent with the light wind reported in Romorantin (15 km) from the west at 1 a.m. and northwest at 2 a.m. (see: weather). The time of observation, following the World Cup final and the eve of July 14th, is very conducive to all types of public or private celebrations. The witness, who was shown various photos or videos of Thai lanterns, recognized this type of object (see email), which they were previously unfamiliar with. This case is classified as "A": observation of Thai lanterns.
