Description: On June 18, 1976, around 9:30 PM, two individuals observed a luminous object in the sky that intrigued them. The object was shaped like a cigar and quite long. It flew at a low altitude and maintained a steady speed along a northeast-southwest trajectory. No sound was heard. Only one report of this phenomenon was collected, for which we have limited information.
