Description: Testimony in 2008 of an observation made in the years 1988-1990 (the witness can no longer remember the exact date, spring or summer 1988-1990). The date of 01/04/1988 is given arbitrarily. In the spring or summer of 1988-1990, three people arriving on their property are intrigued by the presence of a ground mark at the edge of a hedge. This circular mark, about 20 centimeters wide, resulted from the crushing of the grass. The thus-formed circle has an outer diameter of approximately 2 meters. This mark will remain materialized for several months due to differential growth of the affected grass compared to the rest of the garden. Only one testimony will be collected. It is a non-exploitable testimony by GEIPAN because it does not describe an PAN (aerospace phenomenon) but a ground circle without possibility of linking it to an PAN as no PAN is described. GEIPAN classifies this case as C: lack of information.
