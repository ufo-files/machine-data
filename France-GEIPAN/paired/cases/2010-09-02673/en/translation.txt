Description: A family testifies to the silent passage of 3 glowing spheres on September 5, 2010, between 1:10 and 1:30 AM. One of the witnesses takes photographs with a mobile phone. The characteristics of the observation testimonies (night from Saturday/Sunday after midnight, non-blinding yellow-orange light followed by other lights of the same type, no noise, constant speed trajectory compatible with wind direction) evoke a release of Thai lanterns. The village hall was rented for the same period (wedding, party?..). Contacts with the village mayor's office to request additional information having received no response, the case is classified B as the probable observation of Thai lanterns.
