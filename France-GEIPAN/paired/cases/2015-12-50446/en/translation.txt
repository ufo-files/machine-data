Summary: Observation of silent movements of a fluorescent fuchsia light in the night sky: probable observation of a recreational drone.
--- seg-dca4c95ef5946f830a6c9226 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Testimony in 2017 of an observation made on December 18, 2015, around 7:45 PM - 8:00 PM. The witness reports the movements (straight-line trajectory, stop, and resumption of movement) of a fluorescent fuchsia light in the sky near the downtown area of Mulhouse (68). No unusual noises were heard. No shape was distinguished. The witness loses sight of the phenomenon as it is hidden by surrounding roofs. Only one testimony was collected. The consistency is correct, with the witness providing a good description of the observation and a photograph reconstructing it. We were able to show (see the investigation report) in this analysis that the witnesses likely observed a recreational drone equipped with light signaling and used in unauthorized conditions (at night and in an urban area). This conclusion is based on the following two main elements:

- The behavior of the PAN, with a straight-line trajectory, a sudden stop, followed by an instant restart at the initial speed, is typical of drones. The observation distance and the low inertia of these devices prevent the witnesses from distinguishing possible small parasitic movements of the drone, which could have occurred during its stop and restart.

- The observed color, fuchsia, is unusual and does not correspond at all to the colors typically used in aerial navigation. This use of whimsical colors is typical of the "customization" that some owners can do to drones. Furthermore, the noise could not be heard by the witnesses, due to the distance separating them from the drone, the moderate noise emitted by the engines of this type of device, the non-carrying wind, and ambient traffic noise. Also due to the observation distance, but also because of its reduced dimensions, the shape of the drone could not be observed, only its light was visible. We can note that although the owner of this drone equipped his device with light signaling, he does not respect the prohibition of flying his drone at night or using it over an urban area. Some drone owners, in addition to not respecting the regulations, take sometimes reckless risks (possibility of material damage, even bodily injury), unaware of the danger. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": probable observation of a recreational drone.
