Description: Recent testimony (01/18/2015) of an old observation. On October 11, 2013 at 10:08 AM, a witness observed 9 dim lights forming a sort of checkerboard pattern. The points are disappearing little by little. The witness takes photos. No other testimony is collected. The witness observes towards the northwest (see his photos and Google map), and sees the points move slightly from his right to his left. The wind at Nimes-Garons is from the north, weak (see log). Bright points moving in straight lines in the sky suggest balloons reflecting the sun. Their movement is consistent with a north wind; October 10 is a holiday in Aigues-Mortes, a balloon release is not surprising; these balloons, carried by the north wind, approach the witness horizontally but move away vertically as they ascend in altitude, until they burst. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of festive balloons. Given the low strangeness of the case, and the over one-year delay between the observation and its reporting, the GEIPAN did not conduct local inquiries into the origin of the release.
