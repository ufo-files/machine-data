Summary: Observation of a luminous phenomenon in the sky: Insufficient information to validate hypotheses.
--- seg-89a052d1bab4c3fed96b9bd2 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 15, 2009, between 11:15 PM and 11:25 PM, a pedestrian on the promenade in Menton observed in the sky to the right of the Star of the Shepherd a stationary luminous phenomenon. After three minutes of observation, the light intensity decreased. The phenomenon then quickly ascended vertically and disappeared in two seconds towards Italy. This occurred over the Mediterranean Sea. Several hypotheses have been analyzed (illuminating rocket, pyrotechnic balloon, plane, satellite) and remain possible, but could not be validated. (see investigation report). This case is classified "C" due to a lack of information to fully validate or refute any of these hypotheses.
