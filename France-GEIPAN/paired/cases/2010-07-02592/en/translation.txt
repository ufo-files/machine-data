Description: On July 1, 2010, around midnight, a witness observed in the sky a particular movement in a "V" shape of a bright point of light. The peculiarity of this case lies solely in the V-shaped movement. The witness reported a frequency of approximately 2 seconds for this V-shaped movement. By drawing a parallel with a long-exposure photo of a commercial airliner (attached), where one can see an alternation of red and white lights along the axis of movement, lit every second, the GEIPAN hypothesizes that this blinking could have been interpreted by the witness as a V-shaped movement. Therefore, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of an airplane.
