Description: A witness observes and films multiple sightings of a white light in the sky (on February 9, 6, and 11, 2011). The characteristics of the observation described by the witness (phenomenon observed "on several mornings when I wake up," "of white color," disappears when it's daylight, observation duration of a stationary phenomenon for a very long period of 45 minutes...) suggest an astronomical phenomenon. Verification of the observation direction towards south-southeast (direction Lavelanet-Belesta) and the astromical configuration of the sky on those days, and particularly on March 11, 2011 around 7:00 local time, leave no doubt about the observation of the planet Venus present in the observed sky sector and well visible to the naked eye. The classic shimmering impression described by the witness is due to atmospheric perturbations (temperature difference and refraction index of the layers crossed). The case is classified as A, as an observation of the planet Venus.
