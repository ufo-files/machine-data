**Description:** On March 27, 2022, at 9:30 PM, while observing the sky from his garden in Douai (59) during his dogs' walk, the witness spots an unidentified aerial phenomenon (UFO) slightly to the right of his neighbor's roof antenna, moving along a straight trajectory. After briefly lowering his gaze to the ground to reposition himself, he pauses his observation for a moment. When he resumes, the UFO has moved a bit further to the right, between the neighbor's antenna and his own, taking the form of an equilateral triangle with sharp, difficult-to-specify edges - pink, orange, or coral. Several brighter, clearer lights are visible along the two sides adjacent to the forward tip. The UFO moves in a regular, silent manner, appearing to follow an axis of progression at the level of the witness's property's rear street, before disappearing, estimated to take between 10 and 20 seconds, behind the line of tall pines marking the neighbor's property. No changes in appearance or behavior are observed throughout the sequence. The consistency of this case is estimated as good. Despite a single witness and the absence of photos or videos of the UFO, the testimony is very detailed and was given the next day.

According to the criteria of GEIPAN (presumably a GEIPAN classification system), consistency refers to the amount of reliable, objective information gathered for a testimony. An investigation conducted over an extended period and requiring numerous verifications (as detailed in the investigation report) was initially carried out remotely, then supplemented with field investigations during 2023. These steps helped clarify several aspects of the testimony, ruled out the hypothesis of an airplane (except for a possible very low-flying, silent, transponder-less small aircraft, deemed highly unlikely), and considered the possibility of a drone following a curved trajectory. This latter hypothesis was examined based on the following:

- The movement is compatible, both in terms of the trajectory's shape and the apparent speed.
- Proximity to the detention center could suggest surveillance or reconnaissance missions.

However, several factors challenge this hypothesis:

- No known visual correspondence with a civilian drone model. It should be noted, though, that a user can modify their device by adding personalized LED lights, as has been observed before. Nevertheless, the clean triangular shape described by the witness is uncommon, even for modified drones.

On-site measurements and subsequent calculations demonstrated that only large-sized drones (equal to or greater than approximately 1 meter long) could have been observed at the minimum possible distance, around 28 meters, placing the UFO at the level of the witness's house roof and its disappearance just above the pines bordering the road. While this is possible, other inconsistencies emerge related to these findings:

- The appearance of large drones does not match the witness's description. These devices, typically multicopters with 4, 6, or 8 engines, have a visual configuration unrelated to the observed triangular shape, except for an exceptional "customized" model as mentioned above.
- Recreational drones are usually small, with their size decreasing over the years. Models over one meter are more suited for professional or military use.
- The closer and larger a drone is, the more noise it generates. At a distance of several dozen meters, such a craft should have been audible to the witness, even with a slight background noise (road traffic). Wind is not a relevant explanatory factor: it was unfavorable during the first part of the observation and favorable in the second.

Regulatory aspects: a civilian drone flight under these conditions would constitute an infraction (nighttime flying and overflight of a restricted area). While some operators might break these rules, for example, to perform nocturnal reconnaissance over the Douai detention center, this possibility remains unsubstantiated to date. The use of a drone by a public authority (penitentiary administration, armed forces) cannot be entirely ruled out. In such a case, however, the device would be identified, authorized, and equipped with standard regulatory lighting devices for nighttime flights as part of a surveillance mission. Furthermore, several companies operating drones within a wide perimeter around the observation site were contacted. Only one responded, stating they were unaware of such an operation or of a drone matching the description.

Illegal drones used near correctional facilities, including Douai, are typically small recreational quadcopters, unrelated to the PAN observed. Finally, a specialized drone instructor from the French Navy and Aeronavale was consulted regarding the lighting markings used on military drones. According to them, no known lighting configuration matches the reported one. They also state that military drones, especially those engaged in exercises, are designed to maximize both their visual and auditory discretion, reducing the plausibility of the drone hypothesis given the observed brightness.

In light of the testimony's analysis and its comparison with known phenomenon hypotheses, GEIPAN classifies the case as "D": Unidentified phenomenon after investigation.
