Description: On September 4, 1977, around 11 PM, two individuals observed an unusual phenomenon in the sky. A very bright white ball moved at high speed. It disappeared from the witnesses' view as it moved away. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted for a few seconds. The gendarmerie was unable to make any findings regarding this phenomenon, for which we have limited information.
