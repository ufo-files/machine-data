Summary: Observations of successive appearances and movements of white lights in the sky: lack of information.
--- seg-5df999f3d0752ac898ae391c [needs-review] ---
Description: On the 4th of July 2009, around 1 a.m., four individuals observed in the sky the movement of two large white lights that quickly disappeared, followed by three smaller ones that departed in different directions. One witness reported that these three lights formed a triangular shape in the sky. No noise was heard during the 15-minute observation. The witness intended to report the incident to the gendarmerie, but the GEIPAN only received one testimony via email. The observation took place during the night from Friday to Saturday, around 1 a.m. The described characteristics of two white lights followed by three smaller lights with fluctuating trajectories, moving silently, could lead to the plausible hypothesis of flying lanterns. However, the old observation date and the lack of information (despite the witness's good will) do not allow for further investigations. This case is classified in category C: Lack of Information.
