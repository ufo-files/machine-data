Description: On July 9, 2015, at 5:30 PM, a witness in their garden spotted a white spherical object in stationary flight. The sphere moves forward, then backward, shifts to the right and left, and very quickly takes a straight upward trajectory and disappears into the sky. The observation lasted 3 minutes. No sounds were heard. Only one testimony was reported. Upon reading this observation report, the GEIPAN considered the possibility of a child's balloon, initially captured and subject to the whims of the wind, or to the will of its owner, then intentionally or not released. This rather unusual hypothesis could fit the observation, but it cannot be confirmed due to the lack of photos, videos, or other testimonies. The Meteociel record indicates a strong northwest wind in Blagnac. Consequently, despite a plausible hypothesis, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to a lack of information and cross-references.
