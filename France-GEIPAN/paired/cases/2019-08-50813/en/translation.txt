Summary: Sequential observations of the passage of a luminous object in the deep sky: observation of the Telkom 3 satellite.
--- seg-b61614cca5d6ecbb86b84d78 [needs-review] ---
Description: During his vacation, a witness spends several nights making astronomical observations with five other amateur astronomers. These people are intrigued by the observation, over several consecutive evenings, of the passage of a luminous object with variable brightness. This object crosses the same constellations each time. Only one witness report is collected. The witnesses did indeed observe, as they describe, a drifting satellite. This is the Telkom 3 satellite (see Gunter's Space Page website). This Indonesian telecommunications satellite was launched in 2012 into an incorrect orbit due to a defect in the Proton launcher. It is now orbiting on an elliptical orbit with a low perigee (250km) and an inclination of 50°, instead of the intended geostationary orbit. In the absence of any stabilization, it is likely spinning on itself, which causes the variable glow described by the witnesses. We verify (see attached file) the witness's statements, which indicate that this satellite is moving from West Southwest (constellation of Boötes) to South Southeast (constellation of Aquila) at an elevation close to 50°, and on August 31, the movement occurs a bit earlier (~ -5 minutes) and at a lower altitude (~ -5°). The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: satellite observation.
