Description: On December 8, 1983, at 11:27 PM, a witness heard a whistle similar to that produced by a glider in flight. He then saw for 5 to 6 seconds a spherical object, blue-white in color and very bright, moving horizontally. This object was followed by a trail of long orange flames. It disappeared into the landscape. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon.
