Summary: Observations in the same evening of several objects passing through the sky; similar observation the next day: probable observations of planes.
--- seg-0735c82aca0fe933a54ed8c1 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 5, 1980, around 11 PM, several people observed in the sky the passage of a craft from east to west. This elongated craft emitted a central light. It suddenly changed direction, heading south then east. That same evening, other similar passages were reported by witnesses. On August 6, at approximately 11:45 PM, the flight of a craft heading northeast was spotted. No further testimonies were collected. The Basel/Mulhouse airport reported numerous aircraft flights that night. The witnesses likely observed an aircraft.
