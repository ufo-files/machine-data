Description: Between July 1st and 12th, 2010, around 11 a.m., a witness observed for a few seconds in the sky the rapid passage of a rectangular object of aluminum color. No noise was heard during the observation. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon. This observation case is very strange, but the testimony is very weak: only one witness despite a very populated area, a very short observation of a few seconds, no precise date. The witness's sincerity is not in question, but one cannot rule out a temporary vision problem (glare). It is not possible to conduct a precise investigation on this case. The GEIPAN case is classified as "C" due to the lack of reliable information.
