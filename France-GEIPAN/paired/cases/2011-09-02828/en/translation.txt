Summary: Observations of the rapid and silent passage of a luminous phenomenon in the sky: probable observation of an atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-55bb69d54798e4edbfcee32f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 09, 2011, around 10:10 PM, several people in the same neighborhood of Montauban observed a swift and silent South-to-North passage of a luminous phenomenon in the sky. We received two independent testimonies: during the brief observation (from 5 to 30 seconds), the observed phenomenon appears to have the shape of a "water drop" or a "white bean" moving quickly and illuminating nothing outside of the object itself. No noise was heard during the object's observation, which gradually shrinks in size. This phenomenon, observed by two independent witnesses, describes a flying luminous object moving from South to North for a duration of 7 to 30 seconds. The investigation was initially focused on Thai lanterns or in relation to the 400 Blows celebration taking place that day in Montauban. Both witnesses completely refuted these hypotheses. A complementary analysis brought the observation closer to an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid or satellite debris. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a bolide.
