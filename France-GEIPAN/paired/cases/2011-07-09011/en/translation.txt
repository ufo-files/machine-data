Description: Recent (01/10/2014) testimony of an old observation without a specific date and uncertainty about the month. In June or July 2011 at 6:30 PM, a witness observed a cluster of black balls in the sky, appearing very blurry. The altitude was quite low (not more than 1000 meters). The object seemed to spin on itself. The witness's description of the phenomenon remains fully compatible with a cluster of balloons, likely accidentally released. Only the blurry outline may be surprising: a hazy atmosphere, or a slight blindness due to backlighting could cause this effect. Since the exact date is unknown, the GEIPAN was unable to conduct appropriate verifications for this type of phenomenon (checking movement against local wind, searching for the perpetrators or other witnesses). Due to the lack of a precise date, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to lack of information.
