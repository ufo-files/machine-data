Summary: Observation of the presence and slow, silent movement of colored balls (red and green) in a clear sky: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-4b1ed0c0f12f22edfee67108 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 13, 2017, at 10:38 PM, a witness observing from their backyard noticed a stationary presence followed by the slow movement of a red ball and three green balls in a clear sky. The witness took photos before the sparkling phenomenon moved away and disappeared. No sound was heard. Only one testimony was collected. The witness likely observed a group of Thai lanterns of different colors. Indeed, the wind that evening, according to Infoclimat, was blowing from the southeast, in the direction of the observation. This explains the quasi-stationary effect of PAN in azimuth. The slow disappearance could be due to the slow extinction of the burners. The date (summer and the eve of the national holiday) is conducive to such festive events. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B: Thai lanterns.
