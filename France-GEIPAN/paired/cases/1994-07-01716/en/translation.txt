Summary: Observations of a half-sphere shaped, red to yellow-colored light phenomenon: mistaken for a moonrise.
--- seg-c39822c641f99fc7b29aeb08 [needs-review] ---
Description: Two witnesses report seeing, on July 31, 1994 at approximately 1:15 a.m., just above the hills east of Marseille, a red half-sphere about twice the size of the moon turning yellow as it shrunk. The witnesses witnessed the phenomenon disappear into a thin ray of light. The witnesses could not explain what they saw. This case was classified "C" due to a lack of information. In July 2012, a recreation at the same location and time will be conducted as part of a possible saros cycle (see explanations in the investigation report). Following a highly convincing recreation 18 years later, the GEIPAN reclassifies this case as "A": misidentification with the Moon at its rising.
