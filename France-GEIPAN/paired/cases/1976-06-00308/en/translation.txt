Summary: Observation of a high-altitude red glow.
--- seg-5487fbc1e9fce7cd599b627e [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 22, 1976, around 10:15 PM, several people in cars observed a slow, silent red light moving very high in the sky, intriguing them. This stationary phenomenon above the petrol terminal reportedly moved over the Herrlisheim-Drusenheim refinery and then headed towards the Rhine in Germany. One witness took several photographs, but the film was unusable. Two refinery employees reported seeing a bright object in the east-northeast direction indicated by the first witness, but none of the refinery's on-duty guards saw an object overhead. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon, for which we have limited details.
