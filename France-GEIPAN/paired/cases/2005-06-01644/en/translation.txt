Summary: Observations of several moving points in the sky: likely observations of commercial aircraft.
--- seg-b1791e6b1f6a068145513ead [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 13, 2005, around 4:35 PM, a witness, a professor with his students, observed a bright point in the sky, about the size of a star. After a few minutes, this point seemed to split in two, and in the following minutes, the witness observed several points moving slowly relative to each other, like commercial airplanes at very high altitude. The witness took photographs with his cell phone but did not provide them to the gendarmerie. The hypothesis considered is indeed that of reflections of the sun on passing commercial airplanes at high altitude.
