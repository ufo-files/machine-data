Description: On September 1, 2006, a witness took photographs during the filming of a movie in his village. Upon reviewing the photos, he noticed an unknown spot on one of the images, despite not making any visual observations. He contacted the Air Transport Gendarmerie to provide a statement. A thorough investigation by the gendarmerie ruled out the possibility of a meteorological phenomenon but revealed an unknown radar trace consisting of 3 blips recorded by Nice's primary radar during the same period as the photo was taken, indicating a sudden acceleration of the presumed object. This lead was dismissed (see report) in favor of investigating the possibility of a commercial aircraft passing, referenced as flight 7637. Additionally, the company responsible for maintaining the clock in the church tower stated that pigeons causing interference by landing on the hands disrupted the pendulum's operation. There is therefore a pigeon colony in the tower, confirmed by a photo showing numerous pigeons clearly visible on the cornice below the clock. The hypothesis of a bird passing through the field of view is thus plausible, as the blur observed could be caused by wing beats. The available evidence does not allow us to formally conclude between the two hypotheses—the passage of a pigeon, despite some uncertainties, seems to be the more probable explanation compared to the passage of commercial flight 7637. Regardless of the hypothesis chosen, a conventional explanation can be provided for this case, which should therefore be classified as 'B'.
