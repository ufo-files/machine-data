--- seg-856f2a48ad24c8a310d1f32b [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Following an Internet documentary, a report was made in November 2008 regarding an observation on June 30, 2007. A driver on Highway A410 en route to Annecy observed at 9:43 am an object suspended in the air. Dark in color and multi-faceted, the object moved very slowly while oscillating. A photograph was taken while driving and with a mobile phone; according to the witness, it did not capture the color and shape of the observed object. By correlating this observation with a film of another case (Annemasse, 74) at the same date but in the late morning), the witness formally identified the object as a tetrahedral weather balloon used by the National Weather Service or scientists.
