Description: Recent March 2015 testimony of an old observation (July 1985). On July 22, 1985, around 2:30 PM, a driver and his passengers were surprised to see the front fuselage of a craft appear at very low altitude and move very slowly, with two individuals behind a window. Stopping his vehicle, the driver leaned forward to observe further, but everything disappeared. No other testimony was collected. This very strange 1985 observation report, as it describes unknown beings, was not submitted to GEIPAN until March 2015, nearly 30 years after the events. As with all cases of this nature, GEIPAN referred the observation narrative to a university cognitive psychology lab to assess the component of false memories in the narrative. The lab's report is attached. It appears that the strangest elements of the testimony are suspected of being false memories; nevertheless, the witness undoubtedly saw an unusual flying object (large solar balloon, or low-altitude plane). Due to this testimony disrupted by false memories and a lack of corroboration, GEIPAN classifies this case "C" for lack of reliable information.
