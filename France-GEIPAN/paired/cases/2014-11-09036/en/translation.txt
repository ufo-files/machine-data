Description: On November 1, 2014, at 9:15 PM, a witness observed three round objects, orange-red in color, moving silently in the sky. Eventually, the witness lost sight of the objects. The observation of orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes, on a weekend night, suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from the north side of the witness's house, from west to east. This movement does not correspond to the light south wind reported in Melun: weather. Therefore, the hypothesis of a Thai lantern, compatible with the witness's description, cannot be confirmed, except to consider a wind gust just at the time of observation. The GEIPAN cannot draw a conclusion on this case due to the lack of precise weather records near the observation location and lack of corroboration. This low-strangeness observation case is thus classified in category "C".
