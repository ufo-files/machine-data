Description: On Thursday, March 1st, 2012, at 10:25 PM, a driver notices a puzzling glow in the sky. He observes the passage of a massive "falling star" of yellow color with a white-blue border, followed by a blue trail. The size impresses the witness. The aspect of the phenomenon (straight trajectory, very rapid movement, intense light) clearly characterizes a bolide: atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid or space debris. Since the witness indicates a duration of about a second, and there is no recorded reentry of space debris on that day, the hypothesis of a meteorite fall is favored. The REFORME network of meteor observers does not report (yet?) this phenomenon. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B".
