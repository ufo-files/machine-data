Description: On October 4, 2014, between 11:00 PM and 11:30 PM, a witness observed for 10 minutes from their balcony a column of lights forming an "S" in the sky above a nearby hill. The observation of orange orbs in the sky for several minutes on a weekend night strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from the southwest, which is consistent with the very light and unstable southwest wind reported in Lyon-Bron (weather). Photos confirm this hypothesis: the complex shapes seen in the photos are the result of camera shake due to the relatively long exposure time (1/4 second). The vertically stretched "S" shape corresponds to the ascending trajectory of the lanterns, viewed in an axis close to the wind axis (the witness's graphic reconstruction seems to misalign with the photos). The lanterns were probably released beyond the Saône (in Collonges au Mont d'Or), and are between 400 and 800 meters from the witness (as seen in the first photo). They measure 40 to 60 centimeters in diameter. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of Thai lanterns.
