Description: On January 6, 2009, between 7:35 and 7:39 a.m., a witness observed a bright light on the side of an aircraft passing at low altitude. No other testimonies were collected. Due to the lack of a precise date for the initial observations, the absence of other testimonies, the lack of accurately completed questionnaires, and the absence of telephone contact, the GEIPAN was unable to properly investigate this case. Furthermore, the late submission of the testimony (four months after the event) makes it impossible to verify the aeronautical aspect. Pending any potential additional testimonies, this case is classified as C due to a lack of information.
