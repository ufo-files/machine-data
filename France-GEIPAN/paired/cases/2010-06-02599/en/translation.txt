Summary: Observation of a ball of light: iridium flash.
--- seg-3d1963cd20e0e81dd853fd8d [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness observed in the sky on June 3, 2010 around 0:45 a ball of light that intrigued them. This fixed object lost its brightness and the witness lost sight of it. No noise was noticed during the observation of 15 to 20 seconds. Following the publication of cases in "C", an internet user reported to the GEIPAN the possibility of an iridium flash. The GEIPAN verified this hypothesis. The witness's description closely matches the observation of an iridium satellite flash, a rare and spectacular phenomenon that occurred at that time, visible at that location, and could not have escaped the observer looking in that direction. However, there is a lack of information provided by the witness about the elevation (site) of the phenomenon in the sky. The observation of this PAN is classified "A", as an iridium flash.
