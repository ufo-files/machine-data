Description: On June 15, 2015, a witness observed and filmed around 11:15 PM a bright star in the NW direction in the sky, but remained intrigued by a sphere placed in front of the star. No other testimonies were collected. This testimony is of fairly good consistency: although not very precise and coming from a single witness, it is accompanied by two exploitable videos. The only hypothesis considered is that of confusion with an optical defect on the defocused image of planet Venus, captured with a strong zoom (see the investigation report). Based on the collected elements, this case is classified as "A" as an observation of planet Venus with a camcorder equipped with a strong zoom, with a shifted focus causing an artifact in the image.
