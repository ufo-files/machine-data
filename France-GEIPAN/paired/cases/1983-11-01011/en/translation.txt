Description: On November 26, 1983, at approximately 9:10 a.m., a driver observed for one second a round object with a diameter of 20 to 40 centimeters. Bright red in color and followed by a white trail, the object was falling vertically at a high speed. No other information or traces could be gathered regarding what is likely an atmospheric reentry.
