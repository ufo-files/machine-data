Description: A witness reports by email in January 2009 three observations of PAN. Unfortunately, they no longer remember the exact date (the first two in the 1955/1960 years in Remiremont (88) and in the Nancy (54) suburbs, and the third in 1975 in Guadeloupe). The date of January 1, 1955, is arbitrarily given for the first observation of the movement of a luminous phenomenon. The witness reports seeing a circular, luminous object about 50 meters in diameter at around 5:00 PM or 6:00 PM, approximately 400 or 500 meters from the road and 50 meters above the ground. Despite our requests for details about each observation, no questionnaire was returned to us. The GEIPAN classifies these observations as C: lack of information.
