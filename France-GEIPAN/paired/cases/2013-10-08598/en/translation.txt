Description: On Tuesday, October 22, 2013, around 7:50 PM, a driver was intrigued by an object in the sky. The witness described a triangular object composed of three glowing lights: red, green, and blue. The object was stationary before slowly and silently moving. The lights turned white and it disappeared. Only one testimony was collected. Given the objective elements gathered in the attached investigation report, namely the very probable presence of a regular flight at the observed time and azimuth, the triangular shape delimited by lights identical to the navigation lights of the involved aircraft, the movement of the PAN identical to that of the observed flight, in the witness's observation axis, and the temporary, then definitive, disappearance of the PAN behind clouds, in the same manner as a high-altitude aircraft is masked by partial cloud layers, we can conclude that the case concerns the probable observation of a commercial aircraft observed under special conditions. This case is to be classified as "B," as a probable observation of a commercial aircraft on a regular Venice/London route, illuminated by its navigation lights, temporarily obscured by cloud layers. The uncertainty about the precise flight schedule and the doubt about the colors of the lights reported by the witness prevent this case from being classified as "A."
