Summary: Observation of a stationary object in the sky
--- seg-c8ccc900e618c541f770e531 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 24, 1980, at 3:30 AM, a witness observed a very bright object in the sky. The intense brightness made observation difficult, but the witness was able to see a slightly pale pink parachute detach from beneath the object. This parachute was only visible for a few seconds; however, the witness observed the glowing sphere for over 10 minutes before going to bed. Upon waking several hours later, everything had disappeared. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected, and we lack information about it.
