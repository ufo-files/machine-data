Summary: Likely observation of a child's balloon
--- seg-fa0d4e3f9b55549d3728878d [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 14, 1994, at approximately 9:20 PM, four witnesses observed, photographed, and filmed an oval object moving in the sky. Pieces of balloons with cards from a balloon launch contest were found in a nearby field and in the neighboring village. This children's contest was launched from England. Therefore, the observed phenomenon is likely one of these balloons.
