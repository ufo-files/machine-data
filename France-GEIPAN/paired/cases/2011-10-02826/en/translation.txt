--- seg-6f6439efbfff893981509e90 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 7, 2011, around 10:15 PM, a witness observed a luminous phenomenon in the sky for 15 seconds. A group of three pale white triangular luminous objects, themselves arranged in a triangle, moved N.E/S.O. Accompanying this group was a fourth object, separated from the group to the west, moving at the same speed and direction. The entire group moved silently under the clouds present that evening and eventually disappeared into the clouds. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected. The witness's description of four luminous points moving slowly in a straight line suggests Thai lanterns. However, the triangular shape, the movement perpendicular to the wind (according to measurements from Nantes and St. Nazaire provided by Météociel), do not allow this hypothesis to be confirmed; the witness does not recognize these lanterns either. The hypothesis of planes also does not fit well: the triangular shape of the lights, the silence. Given these elements, the GEIPAN classifies this case as "C": Unidentifiable due to a lack of data.
