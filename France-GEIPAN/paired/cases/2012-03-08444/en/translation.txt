Description: On March 23, 2012 at 23h, a witness is intrigued by the silent movement of two red lights above Paris buildings. They disappear, hidden by the urban landscape. This observation case has all the characteristics of Thai lanterns: silent and uniform movement of red lights, according to the wind (northeasterly wind, 6 to 15 km/h), for a few minutes, during a weekend evening or party. Releases in the middle of the city are not actually recommended, but in fact, they do occur quite often! The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
