The object disappeared very quickly behind the hill. No other witnesses were found. This well-described and located observation case, thanks to the witness's experience in astronomical observations, is consistent but unfortunately too brief to properly measure its strangeness. Hypotheses about balloons or aircraft are possible but unverifiable with the provided information. This case is classified as C due to a lack of object data, independent witnesses, and cross-checks.
