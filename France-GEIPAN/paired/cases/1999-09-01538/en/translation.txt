Summary: Observation of the movement of a brown shape with sudden changes in direction
--- seg-b3f3ca77f88523fd1aeaa335 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the night of August 7 to 8, 1999, between 11:30 PM and 1:30 AM, an amateur astronomer observing the Andromeda galaxy noticed with the naked eye an object moving at a constant speed on a North-South trajectory. After a few moments, the trajectory suddenly changed direction, shifting to North-Northwest before returning to North. The observed phenomenon was silent, not luminous, with a brown-maroon color. The sketch made by the witness depicts a barrel-shaped object. The observation lasted approximately one minute. A thorough investigation with nearby airports, as well as civil and military authorities, failed to associate this observation with any aircraft, both in terms of air traffic examination and radar traces. This phenomenon remains unexplained.
