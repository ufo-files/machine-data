Summary: Observation of a flying object following a road.
--- seg-9b09062eeffc97b4316a5740 [needs-review] ---
Description: On July 10, 1976, around 4:30 a.m., a driver noticed a low-flying object ahead of him on the road that he did not recognize. The witness described an object approximately 4 to 5 meters long and 1 meter in diameter. It was a bright red color with a black strip running the length of it. The upper part seemed to have a cabin with a blinking light. The driver stopped to observe the craft, which appeared to be moving backward as if approaching the witness. Frightened, the witness immediately resumed his journey. The gendarmerie collected no further information.
