Description: On December 5, 1979, at approximately 5:40 PM, a witness observed a ball-shaped object about the size of an orange with an orange reflection. It moved by spinning and alternately rising and descending. No noise was heard. The observation lasted for a quarter of an hour before the ball disappeared at a very high speed towards the southeast. The following day at the same time, the ball was visible again but changed size three times and disappeared towards the west. Another witness saw a similar phenomenon a few months earlier.
