Description: On April 24, 2010, around 11:30 PM, two individuals observed in the sky the passage of approximately thirty lights moving in the same direction, without any noise being heard. Their number diminished over the minutes and they eventually disappeared completely. The witness, who filmed their observation, concluded after some research that these were the passage of Thai lanterns. No other testimony regarding the passage or origin of these flying lanterns was collected. Based on the given description, these individuals likely observed the passage of Thai lanterns.
