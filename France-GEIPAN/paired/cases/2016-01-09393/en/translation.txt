Summary: Observation of the slow, silent movement of three diamond-shaped objects with red-orange coloring in the night sky on New Year's Eve: likely observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-888f3702d6370d0d9fdfabbc [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 1, 2016 at 12:15 AM, a driver and his passenger observed the slow, north-to-south passage at low altitude of a diamond-shaped object that was yellow-orange in color, making no noise, soon followed by two others. Fireworks were going off at the same time. No other testimony was collected. The observation of orange balls or discs in the sky, on a weekend or holiday night like New Year's Eve (the witness also mentions fireworks) strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The wind (Armentieres station at 3 km) at that time is light but with a strong south-to-north component compatible with the passage of PAN across the road. The witness then reports that the PAN turned to follow the road; the impression of following the road does not rule out the continuation of a trajectory with a strong transverse component to the road, especially since the witness also reports that the PAN was moving slowly (in the direction of the road) and all of this was observed while driving. Finally, the wind measurements indicate a great instability of directions. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
