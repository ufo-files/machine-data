Description: Recent testimony (on November 26, 2011) of an old observation without a precise date (August 25 or 26, 2009, as mentioned in an email of 05/26/2011). A witness reports observing unusual movements of a round, white light shape in the sky. No flickering or noise was noticed. No other testimony on this phenomenon was collected. The following hypotheses were considered: a helicopter scanning an observation area, or a construction vehicle in the nearby quarry (Carrière de la Fosse), located northwest of the observation point. The lack of a precise date, the relatively old observation date, and its low strangeness do not allow for an investigation to be launched. The case is classified "C" due to lack of information, awaiting possible additional elements.
