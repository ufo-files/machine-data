Summary: Observation of a luminous phenomenon in the sky with a curved trajectory: strong presumption of a helicopter in maneuver.
--- seg-4fca2b1bd20068612af19345 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 17, 2014, at 21:03, a witness from his home observed and filmed a multicolored light phenomenon that intrigued him. The observation lasted approximately two minutes before the PAN in motion according to a curved trajectory disappeared. No other testimonies were collected. That day, weather conditions were very disturbed: wind inversion between 8:00 and 9:00 PM. The video shows a light phenomenon that has all the characteristics of a helicopter in maneuver: we note a white strobe flash once per second, a characteristic of aircraft and helicopters in flight at night. The change in appearance is due to the diversity of faces the helicopter presents during its maneuver, successively revealing a headlight, then two, with its green position light (to the right of the craft). The GEIPAN did not make the requests in time to obtain confirmation of this hypothesis from air traffic control. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "strange" in category "B": strong presumption of a helicopter in maneuver over Bayonne.
