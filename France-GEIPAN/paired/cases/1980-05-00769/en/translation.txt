Summary: Observations of the rapid passage of a red-orange glowing object described as two upside-down saucers: probable misidentification with a short condensation trail from an airplane.
--- seg-f2b457d974f1df2cb9ea0a5a [needs-review] ---
Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archive on its public website www.geipan.fr. Among its publications are old cases classified at the time (A, B, C, or D) that are now being reassessed, with the sole purpose of being more relevant in the conclusions. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over many years, this reassessment sometimes leads to new observations or a change in classification. This observation case previously classified as D and named BUXY (71) 1980 is part of a recent set of cases that have been reassessed. On May 8, 1980, around 9:15 PM, alerted by his dog's barking, a person steps outside their yard and sees a bright object in the sky. The witness describes a large, orange-red spinning top or an object resembling "two upside-down saucers." The witness immediately calls their neighbor, who also sees a red and orange ball of fire moving across the sky. The PAN moves quickly on a straight trajectory from Buxy to La Ferté to Sennecey-le-Grand at low altitude. The witnesses specify that that evening, dog barking in the area was unusual, and the television briefly went fuzzy. The Gendarmerie found no other witnesses to the passage of the phenomenon, but other residents in the area confirmed their televisions going fuzzy. They also reported issues with their livestock. This case appears to be a probable misperception with a short condensation trail from a high-altitude airplane lit by the setting sun. This conclusion is based on the following elements (see the investigation report):

- Rounded shape or resembling "two upside-down saucers," identical to other similar examples photographed under the same conditions;
- Orange-red color, "fire" identical to the examples displayed;
- High brightness;
- Continuous movement;
- Observation time, shortly after sunset, at 3° below the horizon, which could still illuminate objects at altitude, like a commercial airliner. The presence of a commercial airliner in this direction is plausible but cannot be verified 40 years later. The observation of this condensation trail coincided with a nearby thunderstorm, which the witnesses did not describe, but whose secondary effects are reflected in the dogs' agitation in the area and television failures. These elements are confirmed by other people in the neighborhood who did not see the PAN. The decrease in milk production noted by a neighbor could have multiple causes. It is common for witnesses to associate the initial strangeness of other phenomena that might not have been noted without the climate of anxiety or alert that was triggered. The consistency is average: two witnesses, but with some lack of precision in the information (duration of observation, angular size). Consequently, the case is classified as B: probable observation of a condensation trail from an airplane.
