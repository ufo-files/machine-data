Description: On November 16, 1985, a witness observed from their 4th-floor apartment a circular dance of 5 white discs. This dance appeared to occur approximately 2 km away and within a 500-meter perimeter. The discs formed a square around a central disc. Then, the 4 outer discs merged with the central disc, causing it to grow and become brighter. The initial dance suddenly resumed and vanished abruptly. No sound was heard. Although the gendarmes did not further investigate this hypothesis, the witness's description leads us to favor the hypothesis of a laser beam dance of the "sky rose" type.
