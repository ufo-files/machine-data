Description: On Friday, May 2, 2008, around 11:15 PM, a motorist and his passengers witnessed the rapid movement of a white glowing phenomenon in the sky. An oval shape was observed with several brightly glowing points rotating inside. A new observation occurred the same night around 2 AM. No sounds were heard during the observations. The family observed the phenomenon again on Saturday, May 10, 2008, at 2:30 AM. This observation case is not very strange: the hypothesis of a nightclub projector is entirely compatible with the observation. The stated observation direction (Northeast on page 2 then North on page 3) does not allow us to conclude about the origin point of the probable lighting that was observed. This case is therefore classified B: probable observations of commercial projections, such as those used by nightclubs.
