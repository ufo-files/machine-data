Summary: Observation of unidentified objects on two occasions
--- seg-0b5d8a7042ed76a2e2b22c04 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 17, 1998, three passengers in the same car observed a "shiny carcass" with a diameter of 20 to 25 meters, featuring black stripes and several bright points or yellow and red lights. This observation was reported in a press article, but the witnesses could not be identified and interviewed. Following the initial report, a fourth witness came forward to describe a similar phenomenon on January 21, 1998, around 6:30 PM. While in her car, she observed a luminous disk, twice the size of the Moon, for approximately 20 seconds. These summary and somewhat contradictory testimonies do not allow for a serious analysis of the observed phenomena.
