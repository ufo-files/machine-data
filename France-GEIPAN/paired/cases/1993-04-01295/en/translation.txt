Description: On April 8, 1993, at 11:45 PM, a patrol of gendarmes was approached by several witnesses who reported seeing a large light above their homes. The phenomenon lasted for nearly an hour. Following an investigation, the laser cannon of a nearby nightclub was clearly identified as the source.
