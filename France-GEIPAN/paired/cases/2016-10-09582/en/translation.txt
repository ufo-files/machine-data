Summary: Observation of the straight-line movement of two egg-shaped objects reflecting sunlight: probable observation of aircraft.
--- seg-74b453ece9032fe5ce23ca18 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Monday, October 31, 2016, around 4:30 PM, several people on a veranda observed the passage in a clear sky of 2 oval objects of cream or white color. The objects gradually moved away and were then hidden from the witnesses' view. Only one testimony was collected. The witness likely saw aircraft passing through the air corridor (entering France from the west) over Gravelines, approximately 50 kilometers away. The movement of the aircraft gives the appearance (as described by the witness) of passing over Boulogne and Saint-Leonard. At 4:30 PM, the sunlight is behind the witness, and it has the peculiarity of reflecting almost perpendicularly on the fuselage of some aircraft (sun mirror effect due to their own orientation and heading). In this configuration, the aircraft appears as an oval without wings, as the wings are confused with the fuselage. The aircraft fly at 10 kilometers altitude at a distance of 50 kilometers, thus appearing at an elevation of around 10 degrees, which seems consistent with the PAN recorded on the witness's photo. Their speed varies, as does their distance, which can create the effect of two parallel horizontal lines with different speeds. The length of the aircraft ranges from 20 to 80 meters, allowing for angular openings of approximately 1/20 to 1/5 of a full moon, an opening still large enough to distinguish an oval shape. Note that the witness was not at the location and may have been surprised, while this visibility of aircraft (even fleeting due to the mirror effects with the sun) does not surprise the residents and neighbors. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable observation of aircraft.
