**Report:** On August 13, 2016, at 01:30, a father and his two sons were lying in their city garden observing celestial objects (shooting stars, satellites, planes, etc.) when a sudden, silent passage of a triangular, light-colored red and orange phenomenon without trail or halo caught their attention. The light intensity of the UFO was relatively dim. The trajectory was straight. The UFO disappeared completely after 6 to 8 seconds of observation. Only one testimony was collected. The consistency was good, with a very detailed questionnaire and a very cooperative main witness with whom the UFO (GEIPAN) could easily communicate. An on-site investigation and an interview with the main witness were conducted. All angular measurements and witness data are present and were exploited for analysis (see investigation report).

In conclusion, throughout this analysis, we demonstrated that the witnesses likely observed a barn owl flying overhead above the housing block, including their garden. This conclusion is based on the following elements:

- The described color, light red or orange, is the result of the reflection of orange street lamp lights on the white plumage of the bird.
- The shape, described as triangular but, based on drawings made by one of the children of T1, resembling more of a three-branched star, representing the tail and two wings of the bird. The fact that this shape did not change during the few seconds of observation is consistent with the preservation of this shape during the bird's gliding flight. We also verified that the barn owl could glide with its tail closed and its wings in the same position as in powered flight. However, a small uncertainty remains regarding the orientation of its wings, which may be more forward-directed in the drawing made by one of the witness's sons than in reality.
- The brightness, described by T1 as "dim, non-aggressive, very soft," is entirely consistent with what is produced by reflected light.
- The complete absence of noise. Barn owls are known for being very silent in flight.
- The straight trajectory, the short observation duration, the absence of a visible halo or trail are consistent with the hypothesis of gliding flight.
- After calculations regarding observation distance estimates, dimensions, and speed, the final results, highly sensitive to the estimates given by T1 (apparent dimensions, observation duration, final angular height), remain consistent with the hypothesis of an bird, and in particular a barn owl, crossing the sky in gliding flight at a modest distance from the witnesses, around 20 meters. The difference between the actual altitude and the one estimated by T1 is considerable, but we identified three factors that may have prevented T1 from accurately evaluating it:

- The element of surprise
- Prior familiarity with observing distant objects
- The brevity of the observation
- The barn owl is present in the area and likely nests nearby, in a loft, belfry, or the attic of a building, which confirms the plausibility of the hypothesis. After analysis, the observation ultimately proves less strange. The main strangeness of the case, the rapid and progressive disappearance of the UFO (PAN), may have been explained by the configuration of the location. Street lamps surrounding the housing block produce a reflection and certain light pollution that propagates through the air. This reflection is stopped by the roofs of the houses, creating a "oasis" of shadow at the garden level up to a certain altitude. The owl was moving approximately 20 meters from the witnesses, at an altitude initially outside this shadow zone. During gliding flight, this trajectory was probably slightly descending, then, at around 45 degrees of elevation, it entered the shadow zone, possibly intensifying its descent towards the houses, which did not allow the witness to see it again, thinking it would continue flying at its initial altitude. It is also possible that visibility at this location is limited (tree) or that the brevity of the observation did not allow the witnesses to realize that the owl was passing back into the reflection zone.

The testimony has good consistency, based solely on the observation of the main witness (no photos or videos, and the normal difficulty in estimating distances, altitudes, and dimensions in the sky). Consequently, UFO (GEIPAN) classifies this observation as "B," likely a mistake with a bird, possibly a barn owl reflecting city lights, gliding overhead above the housing block and gardens where the witnesses were located.
