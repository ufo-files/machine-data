Description: On January 13, 1979, at 6:15 a.m., a witness observed in the courtyard of his residence a luminous phenomenon that intrigued him. In the sky, an object shaped like a dumbbell was stationary. Two pale yellow spheres, surrounded by a halo, were connected by a vivid blue-violet section. After a few seconds of observation, the object departed at high speed and disappeared behind the roofs of the houses. No other witnesses to this phenomenon on that day have been found. On January 14, another person witnessed a different luminous phenomenon. We lack information on these phenomena.
