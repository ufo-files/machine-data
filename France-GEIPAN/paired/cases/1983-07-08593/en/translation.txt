Description: Between __5__ and __25__ July __1983__ (date uncertain) around __6__ a.m., two people in a room suddenly wake up feeling watched. They discover a stationary, silent, spherical ball about the size of a handball in a corner of the ceiling. The ball was transparent, glowing without being blinding. The ball quickly crosses the room and goes through the open window. This observation report, written in October __2013__, though recounting an old memory (1983), is very precise; speech analysis reveals no indication of false memory or fabrication. According to the expert opinion of the Lightning Laboratory, the reported events closely resemble a ball lightning phenomenon. The very hot and heavy weather described by the witness is consistent with this hypothesis. However, as the precise date of the phenomenon is unknown and there is only one testimony, the GEIPAN was unable to find elements confirming this hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to the lack of precise information about the date. The hypothesis of observing ball lightning remains consistent with the witness's account.
