Description: On April 20, 2007, a witness took photos of a sailing race in the Brest Bay. Upon reviewing some of the pictures later, he noticed spots he couldn't explain. Since no visual observations were made during the photography, analyzing this type of photographs is very difficult. Furthermore, the images themselves are not informative, and no other reports of similar observations on that same day in that location have been made. Given that the Brest Bay is known for having many birds, it is highly likely that this witness photographed the passing of birds that were not noticed at the time of the photography.
