Summary: Observation of red light movements in the Tahitian sky: Insufficient information.
--- seg-a48427e8ee42de1cdf974d14 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 12, 2011, at 6:36 PM, several people on the beach of Punaaiua Bay (Tahiti) observed the passage of a bright light in the sky. A very strong red light moved at high speed, then stopped and disappeared vertically into the night. Only one testimony was reported, accompanied by photographs of average strangeness and good consistency. This case suggests a Thai lantern in many ways. However, due to the abrupt stop of the phenomenon, this hypothesis is too fragile to be adopted, lacking knowledge or modeling of local wind. This case is provisionally classified as C, awaiting further testimony.
