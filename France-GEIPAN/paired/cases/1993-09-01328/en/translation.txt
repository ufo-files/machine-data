Description: On the night of September 4 to 5, 1993, around midnight, four witnesses in their vehicle observed a bright orange light, larger than a star, moving like an airplane as it approached. The observation lasted 2 to 3 minutes during which the object suddenly changed shape, becoming a very elongated diamond or a cigar-shaped object. No noise was heard. No other information could be gathered.
