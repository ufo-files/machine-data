Description: On July 8, 2008, at 9:25 PM, from the summit of Bergiès mountain, witnesses were intrigued by a bright, high-magnitude light point that appeared and disappeared several times in the sky. Witnesses located it using the finder of an astronomical telescope. Each in turn, they looked through the finder and described an oval object of a gray steel color, moving slowly (with a pendulum-like motion for one of the two witnesses). The object moved in a southerly direction before disappearing. This case has a high degree of strangeness due to the shape of the object observed through the 9x50 finder of an astronomical telescope. It is also consistent, as the witnesses are qualified and experienced in intensive day and night observation (see the investigation report). The investigator dispatched to the scene by the GEIPAN found nothing unusual and confirmed the testimony and sincerity of Mr. A (Mr. B was not present during his visit a month after the observation). This case is classified as D1, unexplained observation, moderately strange, consistent.
