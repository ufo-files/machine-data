Description: On May 5, 2014, at 6:40 PM, a witness and his friend observed a flickering white light crossing the sky from West to East in 20 seconds. Only one testimony was collected. The Calsky website indicates at 6:33 PM on that day the passage of the satellite "Sinosat 2 Rocket" (third stage that launched the Sinosat2 satellite) on an West to East trajectory consistent with the observation. A observer of the same object who posted his observation report on the web states "The photometric period, by visual estimation, is between 4 and 4.5 seconds" (see Sinosat doc) which is consistent with the flickering reported by the witness (4 seconds lit, then 2 seconds without light). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A" for perfectly identified: observation of the satellite "Sinosat 2 Rocket". However, we note that the observation duration of 20 seconds is much shorter than the satellite's complete passage which lasted 6 minutes, but the witness only observed it on the uppermost part of its trajectory (above 75°).
