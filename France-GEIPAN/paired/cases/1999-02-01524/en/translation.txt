Description: On February 1, 1999, just after 7:00 a.m., a dozen witnesses in two departments of southeastern France observed a large, luminous ball with sparks trailing behind it moving at high speed before disappearing. The phenomenon was silent and lasted only a few seconds. Based on the descriptions provided, it is likely the observation of an atmospheric reentry.
