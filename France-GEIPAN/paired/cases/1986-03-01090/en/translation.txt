Description: On March 17, 1986, at approximately 4 a.m., a witness claims to have seen a luminous phenomenon. It was a sphere emitting light. This sphere moved slowly, illuminating a 1000-square-meter area. The observation lasted about 20 seconds. No further information was gathered on this phenomenon due to the very early hour.
