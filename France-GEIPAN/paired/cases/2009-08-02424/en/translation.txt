--- seg-dba29ab3e3cac0a4d3182969 [needs-review] ---
Description: A fighter pilot observed on August 31, 2009, around 5 a.m., a cylindrical object of orange color passing through the sky. Observed with binoculars, the witness described a uniform and bright color, almost fluorescent. Initially stationary, the object began to rise in a swinging motion for about ten minutes. Then it descended in the same way before disappearing. The event took place over the English Channel several kilometers from the coast. Several hypotheses were considered (illuminating rockets, torch, boat, helicopter, airplane), but none fit. This rather strange observation would merit a field investigation; however, the GEIPAN was unable to contact the witness; moreover, more than three years after the facts, verifications would be risky. This case is classified C due to lack of information.
