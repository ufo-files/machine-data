Summary: Observation of a bright PAN at low altitude, stationary then in motion, without any noise heard: observation of an aircraft.
--- seg-c6dab6ce52828d0d2e078739 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On April 28, 2019, at 21:35, a witness at their window was intrigued by the presence in the sky of a "craft" with irregularly flashing white lights. The PAN seemed stationary, then moved at a slow speed, followed by a very fast speed at low altitude. The witness made two videos. Only one testimony was collected. The cross-referencing of the testimony, videos, and radar trace allows us to positively identify the PAN observed (see the investigation report). The strangeness felt by the witness is explained by the craft's various positions and orientations, a converging trajectory with landing lights lit obscuring the flashing lights at first giving an impression of immobility, a 90-degree left turn making the flashing lights visible, then a straight-line trajectory from east to west giving a sudden impression of acceleration. This case is classified as "A" for observation of an aircraft.
