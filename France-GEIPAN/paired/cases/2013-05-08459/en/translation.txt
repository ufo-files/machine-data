Description: On May 29, 2013 (not 30 May as indicated by the witness in their questionnaire) at 23:45, a driver observed several dozen meters above the ground a black phenomenon in the shape of a pebble with two red lights in front and behind. The phenomenon moved towards the N-W and disappeared behind a hedge. A high-pitched humming noise was heard during the minute of observation. This observation is of average strangeness as it does not correspond to classic misperceptions. The pebble shape reported by the witness is unusual in PAN reports. However, the phenomenon's movement was quite slow and linear. There is no reason to doubt the witness's sincerity, but it should be noted that they were the only one to report observing the phenomenon, and there are no photos or videos: which makes the case moderately consistent, especially as the witness's description was vague (see the investigation report). Potential additional testimonies could better characterize the case, either by explaining it or adding to its consistency. This strange, moderately consistent observation is classified as D1, an unexplained phenomenon.
