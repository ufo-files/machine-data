Description: While returning to their home on April 13, 2013 at 0:43 a.m., a couple observed three bright, orange-white lights silently passing through a clear sky. The witness had time to make two videos. No other testimonies were collected. The passage of glowing points in a straight line for several minutes during a weekend night resembles Thai lanterns. This hypothesis cannot be verified solely by comparing it with wind direction, as it was highly variable at that time. However, it can be noted that it was weak, consistent with the relatively long observation duration (3 minutes) and a relatively slow passage speed, which allowed the witness to film the phenomenon: the video has the typical appearance of this phenomenon: a very bright center and a red-orange halo. The weather report for Marignane indicates a weakening west wind at 11 p.m. on April 12: weather. On April 13 between 0 a.m. and 2 a.m.: light, very unstable wind, from the north at 1 a.m.: weather. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
