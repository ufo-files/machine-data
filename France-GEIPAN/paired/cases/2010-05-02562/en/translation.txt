Description: A witness observed, for a few seconds on May 2, 2010 at 3:20 AM, a very bright sphere moving rapidly towards the southeast before suddenly disappearing. A few seconds later, a second sphere appeared and then disappeared, executing a 180° loop downwards. The description of observing a bright sphere disappearing suddenly, followed by a second bright sphere disappearing in turn (extinction due to fuel depletion), suggests an observation of luminous flying lanterns, especially since it occurred during the night from Saturday to Sunday. Nevertheless, despite follow-ups by GEIPAN, no additional information emerged to permit a more in-depth investigation. The case is classified B as a probable observation of lanterns.
