Description: On February 23, 2008, a witness emailed a report of their observation of an unidentified flying object (UFO) on September 15, 1986, from the commune of Velizy-Villacoublay (78), France. Walking to work at 7:30 a.m., the witness was surprised to see a silent movement of ten identical green lights across the sky from east to west. Only one testimony was collected. Let's summarize the key points of the witness's description of the UFO:

• Green color
• Horizontal movement from east to west
• Absence of noise
• A dozen identical lights composed the UFO
• All lights moved in synchronized fashion

These points suggest either an atmospheric reentry or a fragmented meteor (as detailed in the investigation report). A search of existing databases and online at the observation date and time yielded no results for either hypothesis. However, expanding the time frame, we found other identical observation reports in Germany, France, and Benelux around 7:30 a.m. but on September 23, 1986, not September 15. In the Paris region, the press reported these observations the following day. For instance, the newspaper "Libération" on September 24, 1986, mentioned the presence of about fifteen bright points observed in the area. Following investigations by several experts in atmospheric reentries and astronomers, it was determined that the phenomenon was a fragmented meteor. All characteristics align with the witness's description: color, horizontal movement from east to west, composition of about a dozen fragments...

This testimony was given 22 years after the observation. There is reason to believe the witness may have mistaken the date, but the time of 7:30 a.m. was clearly remembered. This error is further supported by the witness mentioning a press release after the September 23 observation: "He told me that this very morning on his way to work by car, he had heard a journalist on the radio say he had seen something unusual in the sky over Île-de-France."

In conclusion, the witness observed a fragmented meteor entering the atmosphere. The GEIPAN (French UFO investigation group) classifies this case as A: observation of a meteor.
