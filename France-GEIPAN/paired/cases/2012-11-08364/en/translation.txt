Description: On November 12, 2012, at 2:43 a.m., a witness observed for 20 seconds a phenomenon they did not recognize passing through the sky. No other testimony was collected. The witness described a flying object as a "sort of flying car, lit up green, and equipped with red lights in the back" moving at low altitude over the city. The craft's trajectory and red and green lights evoke a helicopter, but the witness did not mention flashing lights or noise. The testimony is vague; the GEIPAN offered several times to the witness a meeting on the scene with an investigator, but the witness could not make it. Due to these insufficient information, this case is classified "C."
