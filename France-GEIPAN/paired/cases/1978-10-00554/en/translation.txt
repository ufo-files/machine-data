Summary: Observation of a stationary red glowing sphere in the sky: observation of the Moon at sunset.
--- seg-1672e8287f89092804053823 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: This observation case is part of cases classified as "C" in 2010 by the GEIPAN. It was the subject of a new investigation (see document: investigation notes). On October 8, 1978, at 10:20 PM, two witnesses observed above fields facing their residence a bright red sphere that was stationary. A partial red circle surrounded the object. The phenomenon, with variable brightness, lasted about ten minutes and then gradually disappeared into the night. No noise was heard during the observation or the disappearance of the phenomenon. No other testimony was collected on this phenomenon. The described phenomenon shares many common characteristics (observation duration, shape, size, color) with a perfectly known astronomical phenomenon: the Moon at sunset. Furthermore, the Moon was clearly present in the observed area of the sky, while the witness did not mention it. This is not a matter of the witness's visual perception but the interpretation the witness makes of his observation through his feelings. In the current classification of the GEIPAN, this case of weak strangeness is classified as PAN A, an observation related to a misperception with the sunset Moon.
