--- seg-1b251a559fa11dc699a6dbc7 [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 22, 1979, around 9:25 PM, a motorist and his passenger observed a dazzling light. For 2 to 3 minutes, these witnesses were able to observe at a distance of 100 meters an object shaped like a "slice of orange" with a flickering light on its left side and a row of lights of different colors. The object then moved quickly towards the southwest. A distant rumbling noise was heard. Photos were taken by one of the witnesses but were never provided to the gendarmerie. No other information was gathered on this phenomenon, for which we lack details.
