Summary: Observation of the silent movement of several glowing orange balls in succession in the sky: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-593ee514515e5860d7535d5a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 11, 2013, around midnight, a driver observes the silent movement of several glowing orange orbs in the sky. The witness reports a low-altitude passage beneath the clouds, following a straight trajectory towards Marcais, northeast of Le Chatelet. The witness took photographs but the originals were not provided. No other testimonies were collected. According to Meteociel, the wind at Chateauroux was blowing from O-SO in a well-established regime (a reading similar to that in Bourges). The hypothesis, proposed by the gendarmes, that these phenomena are Thai lanterns is entirely consistent with the observation and with the local wind. The evenings of long May weekends are very conducive to all types of celebrations, occasions to launch these types of flying lanterns. This case is classified as B: probable observation of the passage of Thai lanterns.
