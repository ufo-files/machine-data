Description: In the evening of December 16, 2008, between 8:45 PM and 9:00 PM, two witnesses observed the rapid movements of a luminous phenomenon. A very bright red glow moves silently and according to a random trajectory above the city of Saint-Pierre. The object is sometimes hidden by clouds. No other testimony about this phenomenon was recorded that evening. However, during the same period, many witnesses observed the movements of Thai lanterns at various locations on the island. The witnesses likely observed a Thai lantern, also known on Réunion as a "wishing lantern" or "Chinese hot air balloon."
