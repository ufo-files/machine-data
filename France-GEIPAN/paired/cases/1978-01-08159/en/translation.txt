Description: Recent (2011) testimony of an old observation made on January 15, 1978, between 6:45 PM and 7:10 PM. Witnesses in a car on D177 between Venasque and Gordes made several successive observations of silent light phenomena they cannot explain, leaving them very impressed. The first observation corresponds to the planet Jupiter, directly to the East (see astro map). The testimony then aligns with the characteristics of Thai lanterns: groups of lights moving in the direction of the wind (from southwest) for several minutes. However, it's worth noting that the witness describes white and red lights; even white lanterns emit a more yellow-orange light. (Very popular in Thailand for centuries, these lanterns have become widely used in France since the 2000s, but were rare at that time in France, used by Asians and tourists returning from Asia.) It has not been verified that the movement of the lanterns was consistent with the wind (web archives unavailable). Other observations remain strange, but could be confusions with the Moon (half-moon directly south), vehicles on the road, or a result of the emotion from the second observation. But this does not explain everything. This case is classified "C": insufficient information to conclude, but one of the observations corresponds to Thai lanterns.
