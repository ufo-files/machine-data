Description: In the evening of January 7, 2014, between 8:00 PM and 11:00 PM, an individual observes successive light phenomena in the cloudy sky above Paris, from their home. The phenomenon moves in an arc of variable length with occasional one-second stops. Only one testimony is collected and two photos are attached to the questionnaire. Based on the objective elements defined in the investigation report, namely the movement, color, shape, and diffuse aspect of the LALs (light aerial phenomena), the absence of a completely plausible and explicable beam, and the presence of installations along the observation azimuth that could use such equipment, we can conclude that the observed LALs are likely only laser impacts on the dense lower cloud layer. This case is classified as "B," indicating a probable observation of laser beam impacts used in the context of a local event on the cloud layer.
