Summary: Filmed observation of the movement of a white point in the sky of Réunion Island: observation of a weather balloon.
--- seg-afd43569e391b8fa4dca8edb [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 9, 2017, early in the afternoon, a witness had their attention drawn to a stationary white point against a blue sky. The PAN eventually moved, prompting the witness to use their smartphone to film it as it passed in front of a cloud. The PAN disappears from view, then reemerges from the cloud. The witness films the PAN again. Two original videos will be received at GEIPAN. Only one testimony will be collected. Through analysis, we have shown that the witness undoubtedly observed a weather balloon, launched daily at 3:00 p.m. local time from the Gillot station for radiosonde purposes (see investigation report). This conclusion is based on the following elements: - The round shape and white color of the PAN are identical to those of a radiosonde balloon, - The launch of a radiosonde balloon ¼ hour before the observation is confirmed, - The witness's position near (approximately 4 km) the launch site, - The movements of the PAN are consistent with those of a balloon taking altitude and little affected by light winds at the altitude at which it is observed and filmed by the witness. The PAN has all the physical and dynamic characteristics related to the hypothesis. Classification as "A": Confusion with a weather balloon.
