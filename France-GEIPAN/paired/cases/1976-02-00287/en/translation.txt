Description: On February 5, 1976, at 12:45 AM, two individuals observed a stationary flying object in a luminous formation. The witnesses placed it 25 meters from the house and 15 meters above the ground. Two silhouettes were seen inside the craft, which disappeared slowly towards the S-SE without any noise being heard. The gendarmerie found no evidence in the indicated area and no other testimonies were collected.
