Description: On February 22, 2010, at 1:55 AM, a witness observed from the garden a stationary glowing point that intrigued them. Upon closer observation, they noticed that the light emitted by this point was composed of various colors. Impressed, the person called their brother who confirmed the observation. The observation duration exceeding 20 minutes clearly suggests the observation of a celestial phenomenon. The sky configuration at the observation date shows the star Sirius in the observed sky area. This star, the brightest in our sky, exhibits variable lighting aspects (as described by the witness). The case is classified as A, indicating the observation of the Sirius star.
