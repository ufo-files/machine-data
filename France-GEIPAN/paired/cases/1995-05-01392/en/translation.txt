Description: This observation case, formerly named LORGUES (83) on May 14, 1995, is part of cases classified as 'D' by the SEPRA and currently under review (see the Investigation Notes document). On May 14, 1995, a witness, alone in a remote camping site, spotted between the trees but close to them, a PAN (bright, egg-shaped, silent, and stationary) UFO approximately 1 meter from the ground. The witness attempted to take a picture of the object but the camera jammed. The PAN then seemed to change in size and move towards the witness, who became frightened and fled to call the gendarmerie. He returned to his tent in their presence. During the investigation, no traces on the ground or in the vegetation were noticed by the gendarmes, and no other testimonies were collected. No military aircraft theft occurred in the area on the night of the observation. This case is weak (single, isolated witness), and the description of the PAN observed is fairly accurate. The witness's sincerity and credibility were never questioned. However, the impreciseness of the timing is a significant obstacle to formally identifying the PAN. The described phenomenon shares many common characteristics (observation direction, shape, size, color) with a perfectly known astronomical object: the Moon. The issue is not the witness's visual perception but the interpretation the witness makes of their observation through their feelings (panic, fatigue, night observation). In the current GEIPAN classification, this low-strangeness case is classified as 'C', an unidentified case due to a lack of data, although a misidentification with the Moon is considered.
