Description: On January 3, 1979, at 6:50 AM, two individuals observed a bright object at low altitude in the south-southeast direction. Half-spherical and accompanied by a spray of sparks, the object moved progressively and silently before disappearing towards the south. These individuals were likely making an astronomical observation of Venus, which was particularly visible in the sky at that time (magnitude 4.46) according to the sky map. Around 7:00 PM, a driver saw another flying object that day. For 3 to 4 seconds, he observed a ball moving silently at very high altitude on a north-south trajectory. This person probably saw a weather balloon, but we have insufficient information about the latter object.
