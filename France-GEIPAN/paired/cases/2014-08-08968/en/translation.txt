Summary: Long observation by a witness of the silent movement of a dark circular mass with light in its center, preceded and followed by several rows of groups of smaller dark masses: Unidentified phenomenon.
--- seg-f98041914ad0589f491152f7 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 28, 2014, at 5:00 AM, a witness observed for 10 minutes between two coppices the silent movement of a dark circular mass with light in its center, preceded and followed by several rows of groups of smaller dark masses. Following the investigation, it appears that this observation does not correspond to the most frequent misidentifications (planes, satellites, clouds, light shows), although some aspects of the testimony may suggest otherwise. The case turns out to be of certain strangeness, but the observed movements do not have an intentional character. The consistency of the testimony is good, the witness provided their testimony to the gendarmerie, and a field investigation confirmed the testimony (see the investigation report). The GEIPAN classifies this case as category D1, unexplained, of good consistency, and of medium to high strangeness.
