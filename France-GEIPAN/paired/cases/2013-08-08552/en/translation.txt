Description: On August 31, 2013, between 10:40 PM and 11:00 PM, an amateur astronomer, observing the sky with two friends from CHALMAZEL (42), noticed sudden flashes of white light in the constellation of Pegasus. Two other individuals witnessed the PAN, but could not be interviewed. The anomaly, of low strangeness and average consistency (multiple witnesses, but only one detailed report), appears to be a probable misidentification with a rapidly rotating very high altitude satellite (see investigation notes). Furthermore, at least 7 very high altitude satellites are present in the observation sector during the given time frame, according to the witness's report. The issue is not the witness's visual perception, but the interpretation the witness makes of his observation through his emotional response (surprise, observation of only 4 brief flashes). This case is classified B, probable misidentification with a rapidly rotating very high altitude satellite.
