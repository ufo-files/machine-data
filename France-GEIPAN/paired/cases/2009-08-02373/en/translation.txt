Description: On the night of August 7, 2009, around 1 a.m., several people observed two luminous phenomena consisting of two reddish-orange spheres. These objects moved silently. Only one witness out of approximately ten observers provided a statement. The description of the parallel trajectories of the two luminous phenomena at a slow speed, as if in "sustentation," with one slowly going out, suggests a probable passage of Thai lanterns.
