Summary: Observation of a "bright ball" crossing the night sky at a very high altitude and high speed: observation of an ISS (International Space Station) pass.
--- seg-8c967226891c1492a786715f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: During the night of July 16 to 17, 2022, a resident of Châtillon (92) (T1), accompanied by her son (T2), observed the sky from her home. She then spotted, above La Défense, a glowing sphere moving at a very high altitude. This sphere, of great brightness and an apparent size disproportionate for its altitude, does not exhibit any flickering. It crosses the sky at high speed and quickly disappears from the witness's field of vision. Only one testimony was collected. Of weak strangeness and very good consistency* (two witnesses, video of PAN), this case turns out to be an observation of the passage of the ISS (International Space Station). *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of information considered reliable and objective, gathered for a testimony. Indeed, the investigation established that the ISS was traversing the sky at the indicated time, according to a trajectory and angular height very consistent with those indicated by the witness (see the investigation report). The perceived strangeness by the witness, described as a "ball," actually results from the interpretation of a zoomed-in image extracted from the video. The phenomenon there appears at times in the form of two concentric circles, a characteristic configuration of a defocused image, linked to the taking-of-view conditions: night recording using a handheld phone, the device being unable to focus on the phenomenon. Some sequences of the video do show, however, that the PAN has a point-like shape, consistent with a distant light source. The case is classified "A", observation of a pass of the ISS.
