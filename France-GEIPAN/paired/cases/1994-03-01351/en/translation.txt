Description: On March 17, 1994, around 8:00 PM, a witness alerted the gendarmes that he was observing a turquoise green light with a red dot spinning at high speed above the sea. The gendarmes conducted a patrol for approximately one hour but did not observe the phenomenon. During the same evening, students at a high school were observing the sky to watch Jupiter's rise but saw nothing else. On April 3, the phenomenon occurred again and was observed by several witnesses. No aircraft was identified. The characteristics of the observation suggest reflections related to the use of a laser.
