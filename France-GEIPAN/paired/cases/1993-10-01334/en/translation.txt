Summary: Observation of the passage of a glowing halo on a security camera
--- seg-55e70c0e424352ca0d856554 [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 21, 1993, around 9 PM, a security agent for a company observed on his surveillance camera for several minutes a light halo with a very bright half-sphere. The phenomenon slowly entered the camera's field of view before disappearing behind trees. It's possible this could have been the observation of car headlights, but no evidence was collected to support this hypothesis.
