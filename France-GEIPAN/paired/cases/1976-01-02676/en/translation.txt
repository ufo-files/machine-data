Description: On January 13, 1976, at 5:50 PM, a witness marvels at what he sees in the sky. A circular, emerald-green glowing form adorned with a coppery red aura passes overhead. Its trajectory is straight from West to East. No noise is heard. It disappears very quickly, hidden by the landscape and then the horizon. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
