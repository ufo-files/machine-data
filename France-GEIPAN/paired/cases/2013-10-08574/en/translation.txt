Summary: Observations of movements in a cloudy sky of blue-white lights: probable observation of a light display from a Skyrose-type projector.
--- seg-0b78cab07a1692ee580a8ddb [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 1, 2013, between 11:15 PM and 11:25 PM, a witness observed from their home the movements of two blue-white lights that moved together in a random manner and passed through the clouds. Ten minutes later, the phenomena disappeared. Two hours later, identical lights appeared and suddenly vanished. The witness took several photos. The witness's description of blue-white lights moving together in a random manner, with light passing through the clouds, strongly resembles a light show such as those used in nightclubs. The witness's photos clearly show that the light spots are only visible on the clouds, evidence of a light projection from the ground. However, it is noted that Wednesday evening is an unusual day for this type of light show. Due to the low strangeness of this phenomenon, the GEIPAN did not conduct a thorough investigation to precisely determine the origin of the phenomenon. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a light show, type skyrose.
