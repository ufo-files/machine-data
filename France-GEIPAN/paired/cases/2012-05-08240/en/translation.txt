Summary: Observation of the passage of three orange lights: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-a8561c4981e29cc3c978ff86 [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 6, 2012, at 10:11 PM, a witness is intrigued by what he sees in the sky: three orange lights move across the sky on a north-south trajectory. Two of the lights suddenly disappear, while the third descends towards the ground and is lost from view behind buildings. The appearance of the lights (orange balls), the duration of a few minutes, the slow and silent movement, strongly suggest Thai lanterns. Two hours after the presidential election results, some may have wanted to celebrate the event with a release of lanterns. The light wind and unstable conditions can explain the delay between the arrivals of the lights in the sky (this is reinforced by a perspective effect); the brightness of the flame means that even at a short distance (100 meters), details of the object are no longer distinguishable. Furthermore, it appears that for one of the lanterns, a portion of the block of burning paraffin detached and fell to the ground. This case is classified "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
