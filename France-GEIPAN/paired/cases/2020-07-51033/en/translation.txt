Description: On July 14, 2020, at 6:55 PM, a resident of Chatte (38) observed for several dozen seconds a white, triangular object moving rapidly from northeast to southwest at a high altitude and constant speed. The object, in the shape of an equilateral triangle, had well-defined contours, and the front of the triangle corresponded to one of the angles. Only one testimony was collected. According to GEIPAN criteria, the testimony has an average consistency: it comes from a single witness and lacks photos or videos of PAN (*according to GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony). The analysis has shown that the only plausible hypothesis is of aeronautical order (see the investigation report). By consulting the FlightRadar 24 site, it was observed that an A320 aircraft overflew Chatte (38) at a time corresponding to the observation, with a trajectory similar to that described. The witness was surprised by the high altitude of the PAN and its shape described as a "equilateral triangle." The analysis shows that the aircraft was in cruise mode and passed directly overhead of the witness at a high altitude. It is likely that the perception of the triangular shape is the result of a misinterpretation of the aircraft's fuselage and wing silhouette. The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'A' for an observation of an aircraft, a Vueling A320 flying from Copenhagen to Barcelona, overhead at the time of the observation.
