Description: On November 24, 1959, at approximately 8:20 PM, a witness aboard a boat at the Dakar (Senegal) commercial port observed the movement of a luminous phenomenon composed of bright points surrounded by a halo. This phenomenon was located in the south-east at an angle of about 15 degrees. The phenomenon's speed increased, then its trajectory became descending. The halo darkened progressively and the phenomenon disappeared. The observation lasted 5 minutes. In the report established by the witness, he mentions 4 boats in different observation locations for the same phenomenon, roughly at the same time. Only one testimony was collected. The description of the phenomenon could suggest a distant airplane seen from the front with its headlights on. The GEIPAN classifies this case as C due to lack of information.
