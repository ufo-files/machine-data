Description: On November 28, 1995, two witnesses observed in the sky around 6:30 a.m. a white glowing crown rotating clockwise. The previous night, two municipal police officers made the same observation around 11:00 p.m. The phenomenon was silent. This crown is likely from a laser projector at a nightclub.
