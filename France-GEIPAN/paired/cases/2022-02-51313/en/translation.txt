Description: On February 9, 2022, around 7:45 PM, a driver traveling through the commune of Marciac (32) was intrigued by the observation of a luminous phenomenon, consisting of glowing orbs moving from west to east on a slightly descending trajectory. She then noticed a trail in the sky heading towards PAN. Of average strangeness and consistency: only one witness, no photos or videos of PAN, but a fairly complete testimony with, however, some doubt about the precise observation time (no response to our request for clarification), this case appears to be the probable observation of a meteor (see the investigation report). The description of PAN (spherical shape, green and yellow color, intense brightness, slightly descending horizontal straight trajectory, presence of remnants) suggests a re-entry into the atmosphere, whether natural (meteor) or artificial (satellite debris re-entry). While checks with the Operational Center for Space Surveillance CNES revealed that no artificial atmospheric re-entry had occurred over France at the time of the observation, the FRIPON camera at the Dax station (40) recorded the passage of a meteor at 8:05 PM (local time) in a direction and with a trajectory consistent with that of PAN. The observation duration may have been overestimated by the witness, a meteor visible for two to three minutes being exceptional. It is also possible that the observation time given by the witness, "around 7:45 PM," is closer to 8:05 PM than to 7:45 PM. The case is classified B, probable observation of a meteor.
