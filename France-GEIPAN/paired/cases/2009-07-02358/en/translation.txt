Description: On July 14, 2009, at 11:10 PM on the beach, a witness observed in the clear sky the silent O-E movement of a very bright red-orange light phenomenon. This phenomenon disappeared at the horizon. The description of the phenomenon, which was red-orange, slow-moving (observation duration of 10 minutes), and had a linear trajectory aligned with an axis compatible with the wind that night, strongly suggests the hypothesis of a festive aerial lantern (so-called "Thai" lantern) passing by. When contacted about the exact location of the observation, the witness stated: "Later, I realized that this unidentified flying object was actually a (sort of) Chinese lantern that, in the night, was not identifiable." Following the witness's confirmation, this case is classified as A, as an observation of a Thai lantern, a classic July 14th sight.
