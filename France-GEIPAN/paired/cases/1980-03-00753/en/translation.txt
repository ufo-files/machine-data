Summary: Probable observation of the planet Venus
--- seg-ce2a511e7d3bbd83bd40b910 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 25, 1980, starting at 9:50 PM, several military personnel observed a peculiar luminous phenomenon for one hour. A ball larger than a star and of yellowish color emitted intense beams of light. It was observed in the Western direction, at a position slightly above the horizon, without a precise angular measurement. Examining the sky chart reveals Venus in the Western direction at approximately 11° above the horizon with a significant magnitude of -4.22. Sirius is also very visible with a magnitude of -1.45, but the witnesses were likely confused by the observation of Venus.
