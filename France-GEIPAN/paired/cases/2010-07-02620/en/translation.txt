Summary: Sky observation with sudden change in direction of light phenomena: France Patrol flight.
--- seg-cffa6d89630e2345a2435a80 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 2, 2010, starting at 10:45 PM and for thirty minutes, three witnesses observed from a balcony the movements with rapid changes in direction of luminous phenomena in the sky passing over the urban area. A loud propulsion noise was heard during the observation. As is customary every first Friday of July, the French Patrol (Patrouille de France) performed a night flight in the region (information confirmed by the CNOA). The testimony corresponds fairly well with this event.
