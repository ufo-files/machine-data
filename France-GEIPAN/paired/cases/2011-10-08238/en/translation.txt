--- seg-b5955e1ae9a500d937a9b9b8 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 20, 2011, around 9:10 PM, an amateur astronomer observes the slow west-to-east movement of a bright light point. Of low strangeness and good consistency, this observation case describes a particularly bright light point moving from west to east that rises towards the celestial vault and disappears in a few seconds. This perfectly matches the aspect of the ISS disappearing into Earth's shadow. A slight discrepancy is noted between the disappearance time (9:03 PM) provided by the simulator and the observation time (9:10 PM), and the maximum elevation is lower than the witness's record (50° versus 60/70°): the ISS orbit bulletins change significantly from one day to the next at this time, likely due to maneuvers; it is very difficult to obtain a very precise orbit bulletin in these situations. This case is classified B: probable observation of the International Space Station (ISS).
