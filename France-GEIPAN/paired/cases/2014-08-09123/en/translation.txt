Description: On August 17, 2014, a witness observed a straight, glowing line, then several others moving in the sky. He filmed and photographed the phenomenon. The observation of straight lines at sunset near the horizon is characteristic of condensation trails from airplanes. The excellent visibility on that day allowed the witness to see planes farther than usual: due to this distance, the trails appear shorter, slower, and more or less vertically oriented if the plane has a trajectory away from or towards the witness. The appearance or disappearance of the trail may depend on local weather conditions to the plane. The photos leave no doubt about this phenomenon, and the witness is looking in a direction where air traffic is very heavy, particularly on a summer Sunday evening. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of condensation trails from commercial airplanes.
