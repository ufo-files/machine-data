Description: A witness takes a burst of a snowy landscape from his 7th-floor room on Thursday, December 17, 2009, around 3:37 PM. Upon viewing the images on his computer, he is intrigued by a spot on one of the photos. The assumed hypothesis is that of an insect close to the photographer and therefore the lens. The strangeness presented by this photo is low, as are the initial information, but they are enriched by the analyses of the IPACO software. Analyzing the photo with the IPACO software reveals parameters that lead to the conclusion that a very close insect, passing quickly, is near the lens. The dimensions and estimated speed are consistent with a typical drosophila (fly) insect. This photo is typical of cases that the GEIPAN no longer deals with or quickly classifies: a PAN not seen directly by the witness and only viewed afterward on a photo, and which, moreover, does not appear on the following photo. The GEIPAN concludes that it is a small insect, a drosophila type (also known as "rod" in ufology) and classifies it as A.
