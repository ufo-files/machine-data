Description: On March 22, 1986, at approximately 6:15 a.m., a witness, while driving, observed for 7 to 8 seconds a spherical object moving from east to west. This object was blue in color on its front part and blue-green in the part that was narrowing at the back. The witness estimated the length of this object at 100 meters. No other information was gathered about this observation, which exhibits all characteristics of an atmospheric reentry.
