Summary: Silent passage of a light phenomenon in train-like shape followed by a trail: observation related to the artificial atmospheric reentry of 31 March 1993 (Cyclone-M rocket).
--- seg-b55e1f6c6ba1f162e5a255db [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness at their post at the Sylans toll station on the A40 motorway (in the commune of Neyrolles, 01), observed for approximately twenty seconds the silent passage of a luminous phenomenon across the sky. This phenomenon, followed by a trail, moved slowly at low altitude through the mountainous landscape. A press article reporting several witness accounts encouraged the witness to give a statement, but no other witnesses could be found. This case is moderately consistent (one witness only), and the description of the PAN observed is accurate. The witness's sincerity and credibility were never questioned, although they likely made an error in the date of their observation (see document: investigation notes). The described phenomenon shares many common characteristics (observation duration, shape, size, speed of movement, trajectory) with a perfectly known astronomical phenomenon: an artificial re-entry into the atmosphere. Furthermore, this testimony can easily be integrated into the mass observation following the re-entry of the Cyclone-M rocket on March 31, 1993. The issue is not the witness's visual perception, but their interpretation of their observation through their personal feelings (nighttime observation of an unusual astronomical phenomenon, late testimony). In the current classification of GEIPAN, this case of low strangeness is classified as PAN A, an observation related to the artificial re-entry into the atmosphere on March 31, 1993.
