Description: On November 21, 2010, at 5:55 PM, a person opening the window of their kitchen on the 9th floor of their apartment was intrigued by the unusual presence of a luminous phenomenon heading east. In the cloudy sky, the witness observed the movement of three lights (one red and two blue) arranged in a triangle and quickly approaching their building. The witness then saw a darker triangular shape bounded by these lights and heard a rustling in the air. The phenomenon disappeared as it passed over their building. The observation lasted between 10 and 15 seconds. No other testimonies were collected. This testimony is of good consistency thanks to a very well-filled questionnaire and a field investigation that confirmed the observation elements. Nevertheless, it remains a unique testimony with all the resulting uncertainties (see the investigation report). This observation is of high strangeness: the phenomenon does not resemble any of the most known misidentifications. However, it is noted that, although it does not resemble any known flying object, the phenomenon's movement still follows the dynamics of current flying objects. Due to its good consistency and high strangeness, this case is classified as D1, unexplained.
