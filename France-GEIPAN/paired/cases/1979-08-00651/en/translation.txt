Description: On August 11, 1979, around 9:10 PM, several people observed a peculiar luminous phenomenon. As the sun sets, three red-orange rings are visible on the horizon beneath a cloud cover. These disks then merged, taking the form of a horizontal line that quickly disappeared towards the North within the clouds. No other testimonies were collected in the area. According to the witnesses' description, it is likely that this phenomenon was an optical effect due to the setting sun reflecting off a low cloud cover.
