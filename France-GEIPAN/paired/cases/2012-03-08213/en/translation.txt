--- seg-28187b89a837ac612c1f9368 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 1, 2012, at approximately 7:05 PM, a witness observed the passage of a bright, spherical object in the sky. The object's movement was straight-line and at a constant speed with no change in direction. The observation described is identical to that of a large satellite passing by. That evening, the International Space Station (ISS) was overhead, traveling from northwest to southeast between 7:00 PM and 7:03 PM. Despite a 2-minute difference between the witness's account and the recorded passage (likely due to the witness's watch being imprecise or the orbit model used for the recording), the GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the International Space Station.
