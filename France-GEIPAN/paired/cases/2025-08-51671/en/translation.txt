Description: On the night of August 4, 2025, at midnight, the sole witness is in her garden when she spots, heading southeast, a PAN with a spherical shape and orange color. The object exhibits fluctuating brightness at times, moves slowly in a straight line, and makes no noise. After approximately forty seconds of observation, the phenomenon disappears behind trees, ending the visibility of the PAN. The consistency* of this case is average, with a single witness and no photos or videos of the PAN. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. A careful examination of the reconstruction photograph provided by the witness, cross-referenced with a location map, enabled the determination of her position and the various observation axes of the PAN with good precision (see annexes 1 and 2). This yields an apparent trajectory visually from right to left of the witness, potentially corresponding to a movement from west to east, southwest to northeast, or northwest to southeast. Data regarding ground or nearby wind from the closest observation station (Saunay (37)) about 6km away indicate it was nearly zero, with variable direction (see annexe 3). The explored hypothesis, the observation of a sky lantern, is supported by the following elements: - spherical shape and orange color, typical of a lantern,
- fluctuating brightness, typical of the flickering flame inside an open-bottomed lantern,
- silent and slow movement. In the absence of wind, the lantern's movement is only due to local micro-aerosol currents,
- ideal time for a sky lantern launch: in summer, on a weekend (Sunday night to Monday), and under calm conditions: no wind,
- brief observation period, consistent with the lifespan of lanterns. These lanterns are generally released in small groups to celebrate private occasions (weddings, birthdays, etc.), but it can happen that only one is launched for testing purposes, for example. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A", very probable observation of a sky lantern.
