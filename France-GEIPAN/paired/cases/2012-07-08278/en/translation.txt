Description: A witness reports an observation made on July 27, 2013, around 3:30 AM: the witness observed twice the rapid and silent passage across the sky of orange lights forming a triangular shape. The first passage followed a path from West to East (O-E) and the second from East to West (E-O). No other testimonies were collected. Given the following:

- No information on the exact azimuth of appearance and disappearance.
- No information on the angular heights of appearance and disappearance.
- The number of objects in each formation is not specified.
- The relative position of objects within each formation is not specified.
- Lack of angular measurements for both the dimensions of the formations and individual objects.

This testimony has relatively low consistency: important assessment elements are missing, coming from a single witness without a photo. The observation is moderately strange: the shapes are not mundane, but the movement is linear, and the phenomenon is distant. The lack of information does not allow for a conclusion in favor of any hypothesis. This case should be classified as "C", as an observation of an unidentified phenomenon at this stage, with the lack of elements not permitting a solid opinion.
