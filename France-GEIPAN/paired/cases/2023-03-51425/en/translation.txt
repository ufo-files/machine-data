Description: On March 16, 2023, while she was in the parking lot of a large retail store in Verneuil d'Avre et d'Iton (27), the witness noticed a person observing a PAN in the sky, which she then began to observe as well. She described the object as being triangular in shape, very pointed, dark in color, and remaining stationary. The witness immediately took a photograph of the object while continuing to observe it for approximately 5 minutes, in the company of others. She ended her observation to enter the store. Upon returning, the object was no longer visible. The case consistency is good, although the witness is only one. Indeed, the PAN was photographed, allowing for its precise orientation in the environment. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The appearance of the PAN, as well as its immobility, recalls the characteristics of a kite (see the investigation report). Contacted by email, the municipal police of Verneuil d'Avre et d'Iton (27) confirmed that it was indeed a kite, whose base was located at Saint Nicolas Abbey, in the same direction as the PAN observed by the witness, and whose owner was visited by a patrol. The municipal police provided GEIPAN with a photograph of this kite, which resembled in every way the PAN photographed by the witness. We were able to reconstruct its position by crossing the observation azimuth data from both photographs, that of the witness and that of the municipal police, and verify that the PAN was indeed located near the Saint Nicolas Abbey park. The case is classified as "A", observation of a kite.
