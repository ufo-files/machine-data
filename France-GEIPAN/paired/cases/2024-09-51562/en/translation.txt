Description: On September 7, 2024, early evening, from their home in Sainghin-en-Weppes (Nord, 59), two witnesses (T1 and T2, father and son) observed a black, circular object resembling a "donut" positioned above houses across the street. T1 filmed the object with his smartphone, noting an east-west movement accompanied by rotations not seemingly related to aerodynamic conditions. Subsequently, a second object, triangular in shape (in the form of a V), appeared. T1 attempted to film it, but the first seconds of the sequence were lost due to incorrect handling. Only images of the object moving away were preserved. Upon his return, T1's wife (T4) confirmed that his brother (T3) also observed the first object passing over his home. The case consistency is good, despite only one testimony, with multiple witnesses and 2 videos of the PAN. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. This case, of low strangeness and good consistency (multiple witnesses, but a single testimony, videos of the PAN), turns out to be an observation of letter-shaped balloons (as detailed in the investigation report). The description and visual aspect of the PAN strongly evoke letter-shaped balloons, used particularly at weddings or birthdays. Notably, the observation was made on a Saturday evening. Furthermore, the apparent trajectory of the 2 objects composing the PAN is entirely consistent with wind direction. The investigation did not determine the exact origin of the balloon release. GEIPAN classifies the case as "A": observation of festive balloons.
