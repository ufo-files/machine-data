Description: On October 3, 2014, at around 22:40, a witness leaves his house and observes numerous twinkling points (about forty in total), red-orange in colour, moving silently in a straight line. The witness has time to take a few photographs before the phenomenon disappears towards the west. Given the objective evidence collected (see the investigation report), namely: - A golden colour, then yellow-orange or red-orange, identical to that of lanterns; - The point-like appearance of the PANs, comparable to that of lanterns at a high altitude; - Behaviour identical to that of objects subjected to no wind or a light wind; - A short observation duration (+/- 1 minute), consistent with the lifetime of lanterns; - The date: a Friday evening, consistent with occasions suitable for organising lantern releases during celebrations; - A number consistent with lanterns released in quantity, GEIPAN places this case in "B" as a very probable observation of Thai lanterns.
