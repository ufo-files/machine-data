Description: On October 3, 2014, around 10:40 PM, a witness exits their home and observes numerous sparkling points (approximately forty in total), of a red-orange color, moving silently in a linear fashion. The witness has time to take several photographs before the phenomenon disappears to the West. Given the objective elements gathered (see the investigation report), namely:

- Golden, then yellow-orange or red-orange color identical to that of lanterns,
- Point-like form of the PANs comparable to that of high-altitude lanterns,
- Behavior identical to that of objects subject to no or weak wind,
- Short observation duration (+/- 1 minute) compatible with the lifespan of lanterns,
- Date: a Friday evening, compatible with those conducive to the organization of lantern releases for celebrations,
- Number compatible with that of lanterns launched in quantity, making this case in "B" a very probable observation of Thai lanterns.
