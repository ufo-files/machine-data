Description: A witness observed on February 11, 2009 at 10:00 a fireball in a descending trajectory in an overcast sky. The gendarmerie, alerted, found no fire or debris in the defined impact area. No damage to property or persons was reported in the affected area, and no other similar phenomena were recorded on that day. The brief testimony describes a "fireball, without color or brightness, descending like a parachute." These characteristics correspond to the probable observation of an atmospheric reentry.
