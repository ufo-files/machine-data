Summary: Filmed observation of movements in the sky of 8 white lights forming a square; humming sound heard: observation of the France Patrol in night flight.

Description: On July 23, 2021, at 22:07, the main witness (T1) accompanied by his spouse observed from their vehicle near LOURMARIN (84) several lights of white-yellow color (numbering 8) forming a diamond shape evolving in the night sky. The PAN makes several curves in the sky before disappearing. The movement of these lights is slow and accompanied by a noise resembling a hum. The observation lasted several minutes, allowing the witnesses to stop their vehicle on the roadside and record a video with an iPhone 6. A Technical Questionnaire (QT) completed was transmitted to GEIPAN by T1 on May 15, 2025. Attached to this QT are provided: a video of the phenomenon, two photographs, and a map of the observation area. The timing of the testimony did not allow for a radar return from CAPCODA*. The affected sector is known to be the subject of similar recurring observations for several years. Indeed, very close by is the Salon-de-Provence Air Base, which houses, among other things, the Patrouille de France (French Air Force Display Team). It carries out night flights once or twice a year, usually in July. They intervene on the occasion of the Baptism of a new promotion of the Air School. A date confusion was noted in the Technical Questionnaire filled out by the witness. The observation is dated July 23rd, while another mention in the responses refers to July 21st. The QT was completed late, on May 15, 2025, which may explain a memory error. A similar observation was reported on July 22, 2021, in Lambesc (13), at the same time and in the same visibility sector. This case, LAMBESC (13) 22.07.2021, was published on the GEIPAN site on 18/11/2021. The investigation showed (with radar map from CAPCODA as evidence) that it was indeed a night flight by the Patrouille de France. Many photos and videos have been circulating for several years, documenting identical configurations in this area, in connection with night flights by the French Patrol. The elements noted in this testimony (number of lights, diamond arrangement, curved movements, flickering, turbine noise) correspond in every way to the characteristics of Alpha Jets used by the French Patrol. The fact that the witnesses were not from the municipality and were driving a car at the time of the observation explains the perceived level of strangeness. The consistency** of the case is average with doubt on the exact date but the presence of a video. In conclusion, the observed phenomenon corresponds without a doubt to the night aerial maneuvers of Alpha Jets in formation. The case was classified by the GEIPAN as "A": observation of the French Patrol in night flight. *CAPCODA: Air Center for Planning and Conducting Operations and Air Defense (Air and Space Army). **CONSISTENCY: According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.
