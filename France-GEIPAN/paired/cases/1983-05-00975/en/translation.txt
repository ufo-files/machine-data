Summary: Observation of a round shape with portholes
--- seg-70443cba604812193f9161d7 [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 8th and 9th, 1983, several witnesses reported seeing in the sky a round object emitting intense white light. These observations took place between 10:00 PM and 3:00 AM. During a telescopic observation, one witness stated they saw a round mass with portholes inside. Intermittent fog disrupted the observations, but witnesses still reported seeing the object move both horizontally and vertically. No sounds were heard. The sky map for May 8th and 9th, 1983, at the indicated times, shows Venus in the observed sector. However, we lack evidence to confirm the hypothesis of an astronomical observation.
