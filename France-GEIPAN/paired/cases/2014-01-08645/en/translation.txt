Description: A witness reports observing approximately fifteen blue or white lights in the sky while looking south from Grand-Bornand (in the direction of La Clusaz, located behind the Danay mountain) on January 7, 2014, around 10 PM. The nightclub "le bal" in La Clusaz uses a laser, which it operates every night, even on weekdays. This establishment is approximately 4 kilometers south of the observation point. The laser projects a white spot that rapidly describes circles in the sky. Since the observation stops when the sky becomes clear, this strengthens the hypothesis that the witness observed the laser projected onto the clouds. The witness, familiar with the area, believes what he saw was not a nightclub laser. The gendarmerie report notes that the witness had taken IXPRIM on that day, an analgesic containing Tramadol, known to rarely cause hallucinations (between 1/100 and 1/100). The witness may have had his perception altered by medication. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of a nightclub laser projection onto clouds.
