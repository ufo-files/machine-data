Description: On January 18, 1993, around 7 PM, two witnesses in a car observed the very quick passage (2 to 3 seconds) of a green light. At the front, the phenomenon was very red with red "jets." No other information was gathered, but the hypothesis of an atmospheric reentry is the most likely.
