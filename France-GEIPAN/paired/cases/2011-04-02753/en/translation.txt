Description: On Thursday, April 21, 2011, several people observed a silent, wave-like passage of orange-colored light phenomena in the sky. The photos submitted are similar to those in numerous other cases that resulted in observations of Chinese lanterns. The swirling winds present that evening could explain the trajectory described by the witness. While no formal testimony corroborates a lantern release, the GEIPAN accepts this hypothesis as highly probable.
