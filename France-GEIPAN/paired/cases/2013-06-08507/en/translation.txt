Description: On June 9, 2013, at 8:09 PM, several people observed the simultaneous and silent movement of two objects in the cloudy sky. These two polygonal objects, always at equal distance, rose into the sky and gradually disappeared into the clouds. The witness's house faces east-southeast on the street, and the living room window, facing the garden, is oriented northwest. The flying object was seen moving from right to left from this window, in the direction of the Senart forest to the left, southwest. Therefore, the object was flying in a north or northeast direction towards south or southeast. The witness indicates an east to west direction: if that were the case, the object would have flown over his house. The wind at Orly was blowing from the north at 8:00 PM and from northeast at 9:00 PM. This wind seems compatible with the observed movement of the objects, which can be assumed to be helium-filled balloons carried by the wind. The shapes mentioned are compatible with various models of balloons sold at fairs and kermesses, which are common on June Sundays. The low strangeness of the case does not justify a thorough investigation to find the author of the release of these probable festive balloons. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of festive balloons
