Description: On March 7, 1978, at approximately 7:15 PM, two witnesses on their terrace observed two overlapping red points heading in a south-southeast direction. The observation lasted two minutes, after which the points suddenly disappeared. After a few seconds, a point of the same size emitting a white glow appeared and moved at high speed towards the northeast. The observation lasted one minute. Another witness provided a statement regarding a bright point moving across the sky, but the time does not match the first observation. No other testimonies about this phenomenon were collected, and we lack information about it.
