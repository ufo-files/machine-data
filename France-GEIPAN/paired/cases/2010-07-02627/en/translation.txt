Summary: Observation of the movement of a dozen white and opaque circular objects in the sky: probable observation of balloons.
--- seg-7c231a83ade89251928a5ec6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On July 18, 2010, starting at 5:00 PM, a witness made several successive observations of the movement in the sky over Albi of about a dozen circular, opaque white objects. No unusual noises were heard, and no trails or halos were visible. The distance to the phenomenon was estimated to be at least 1500 meters. The observation lasted approximately 40 minutes. Two children also witnessed the phenomenon. The characteristics of the observation resemble those of a known and often observed phenomenon: the movement of festive balloons (daytime observation, festive period in July, 45-minute observation duration, successive observations of the same phenomena over several periods, opaque white spherical objects, no noise, objects moving linearly in the same direction, wind direction roughly consistent with the observation—northwest to southeast—and the object size described as 'pinhead'...). The observation did not present any unusual features. The case consistency is low due to the single testimony (despite the large population likely to be outdoors at that time). The hypothesis that the observation involved festive balloons (mylar balloons) is considered plausible, and this case of low strangeness level is classified as B, very probably explained.
