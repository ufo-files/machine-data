Description: On the morning of September 6, 2025, a witness, along with their partner, observed a unique light phenomenon moving in a straight line across the sky above a star from the terrace of their home in Saint-François (Guadeloupe - 971). The scene was filmed for just over one minute with a smartphone. The PAN was captured on video, moving in a straight line from left to right above a star, for just over one minute using a smartphone. The original video was sent to the GEIPAN. Analysis of the metadata using the IPACO* image processing tool from the GEIPAN confirmed the recording time, 9:17 UTC (or 5:17 local time). The intense brightness observed on the video corresponds to sunrise conditions (azimuth 80°, elevation -9°). Astronomical reconstruction using the Stellarium tool indicated that the visible star in the sequence is Venus, positioned at an azimuth of approximately 77° and an elevation of approximately 20°. Jupiter was also present in the sky at that time, but at a higher altitude and therefore outside the camera's field of view (see Annex 1). Analysis of air traffic, conducted using FlightRadar24 and ADSB Exchange data, revealed no aircraft passing during the observation period. One hypothesis is favored, that of an astronautical observation. The Operational Space Surveillance Center of the CNES was contacted. The report provided highlighted the presence of the International Space Station (ISS), visible from the witnesses' position at the time of the observation. Its trajectory, oriented from northeast to southeast, is consistent with the statements collected. The azimuths and elevations recorded at 9:17 UTC (50° to 112° for azimuth and 10° to 30° for elevation) also corroborate the testimony (see Annex 2). This case presents an average consistency* with 2 witnesses (but only one direct testimony) and the presence of exploitable video but little information regarding direction and context. The GEIPAN classified the case as "A": observation of an ISS (International Space Station) passage.

*IPACO: Image Analysis and Processing Software from the GEIPAN (IPACO.fr).

*Consistency: According to GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.
