Summary: Observation and photography of a bright light in the sky followed by extinction: misidentification with an airplane.
--- seg-1161caf6b61bb9c0b64c15be [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On August 21, 2018, at 9:20 PM, a witness on his terrace with his brother was intrigued by a bright light in front of him. His brother pointing out that there is no airport in front of them, the witness immediately realized that this light was not natural. He grabbed his camera and took 2 photos. On the first one, the intensity had dimmed, while on the other, it was very powerful. Then everything disappeared. No engine noise was heard, the environment was silent. Only one testimony was collected on this phenomenon. The witness recontacted the GEIPAN in November 2018 to report new information: "Hello, as the phenomenon repeated itself several times in the same direction, my brother who lives there noticed, by looking at a map, that about 25KM away was the Nantes airport and a runway was oriented in that direction... the problem is solved. From time to time, planes take off by lighting up their landing lights and turn them off 40 seconds later. So the mystery is cleared up... Best regards." Given the repetition of the phenomenon mentioned by the witness, the proximity of Nantes Atlantique Airport (30 km), and the observation direction mentioned by the witness, it is highly probable that the observation was of an airplane coming from or going to the airport. The witness would have seen either the landing light or the rear position light. As Flightradar 24 does not allow to go back so far in time, a formal confirmation of the aircraft involved that day cannot be given. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": aeronautical misperception.
