Description: The witness observes, in the vicinity of a commercial aircraft, a cigar-shaped phenomenon. He rules out the possibility of a shadow and believes it to be a plane/pan (airfoil) flight. Based on the evidence gathered in the attached investigation report, namely: - The respective positions of the witness, the aircraft, and the sun conducive to the formation of a cast shadow. - Existing similar examples. - The "concurrent" movement of the entire aircraft/airfoil assembly. - A PAN slightly larger than an aircraft. - The presence of clouds in the immediate vicinity. This case is classified as "A" for a certain observation of an aircraft shadow on a layer of non-condensable microparticles at a nearby altitude.
