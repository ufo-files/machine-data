Description: Between July 29 and August 2, 1984, several witnesses will spot in the sky bright objects resembling stars. These objects emit red and green light flashes. Observations take place at night after a day of intense heat and clear skies. Over all these nights of observation, the objects' position varies significantly. The sky map shows that the observed region of the sky is the constellation of Perseus, which at this time of year is the site of numerous light phenomena known as the Perseids. The witnesses likely observed this type of phenomenon.
