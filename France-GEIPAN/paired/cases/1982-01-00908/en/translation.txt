Description: On January 6, 1982, in the late afternoon, witnesses observed a peculiar luminous phenomenon. An object emitting red, white, and blue lights moved slowly and then disappeared over the horizon. The investigation revealed that between 6:00 p.m. and 7:00 p.m., a commercial airliner passed overhead with navigation lights of the same colors flashing.
