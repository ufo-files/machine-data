Summary: Observation of movements in the night sky of a yellow ball: probable observation of a Thai lantern.
--- seg-14ff59aba76ff5fec7ded098 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On Saturday, August 30, 2008, at 11:45 PM, a witness was stargazing with the naked eye when their attention was suddenly drawn to the presence, slightly to the left vertically, of a yellow sphere surrounded by shimmering objects of all colors. They observed it with binoculars and noticed it was a yellow sphere with lights of all colors inside it. At the beginning of their observation, the sphere was stationary. This lasted for two to three seconds. It then moved very slowly, gaining speed. It first moved horizontally and then descended diagonally until the witness lost sight of it because in front of their home is the forest of Montcy-Notre-Dame. The witness was away for ten seconds to use the restroom. Their friend, who was present, called to inform them that the sphere had returned to its original starting point when it first appeared. It then replicated the exact same trajectory and disappeared behind the forest massif. The witness's observation (yellow sphere), the movement consistent with the light dominant wind (west) noted that day on Météociel, the time and day of the week (Saturday night), and the presence of a festival nearby (Le cabaret vert) are consistent with the observation of several Thai lanterns. Indeed, the observed colors and shimmering can be explained by observing the burner (flicker) and the envelope of the lantern, which are different colors. The impression that the sphere returned to the same place after the observation was interrupted is likely due to the presence of a second lantern following the same air current. Furthermore, at that time, lanterns were less common, and 2008 marked the beginning of observations (see case LANTERNES 03.05.2008 on the GEIPAN site). In conclusion, probable observation of Thai lanterns.
