Summary: Long observation of a bright orange light in the sky: probable airplane trail observation.
--- seg-ec483fa8e7467ac112f9e577 [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness observes an intense, elongated, light-colored slightly orange light in the sky, resembling the reflection of a sunset on an airplane's fuselage. The bright point is visible for a long time. The observation of an orange-colored light shape in the full west shortly after sunset clearly suggests an object at altitude illuminated by the setting sun. The planet Venus is located further south than the direction indicated by the witness, and should appear circular and white, rather than elongated and orange. A distant airplane trail (between 40 and 80 km) from an airplane flying at 12,000m at 50 km from Paris, south to north, could have created this impression. Due to the distance, these trails appear very small and seem almost stationary. As the sun sets below the horizon, these trails become invisible. See also Strange Airplane Trails. This case is classified as "B": probable airplane trail observation.
