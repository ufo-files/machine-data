Summary: Observations of three fixed orange-yellow lights that successively go out: probable observation of Thai lanterns.
--- seg-09fa91e0a21b6018763b1177 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 4, 2015 at 7:20 PM, a witness and his wife observed for 5 minutes, from their car, three fixed bright points. The three points successively went out. The observation of orange-yellow balls in the sky for several minutes, one after the other, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby for New Year celebrations. The balls seemed stationary, which is consistent with the very light South wind (2-4 km/h) reported by the weather in Saint-Brieuc (22). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B", probable observation of Thai lanterns. The GEIPAN has not received any other related testimonies regarding this observation to date.
