Summary: Long observation of a bright, stationary PAN in the night sky: Sirius observation.
--- seg-f7eb32bce915795600a3d7c6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
**Description:** On March 3, 2022, at SAUSSINES (34), around 00:20, witnesses, from their home, observed a bright, curious "star" to the west. Intrigued by its intense brightness and glowing rays, they went outside to the terrace and continued observing it for half an hour. The PAN (presumed Unidentified Flying Object) seemed stationary, emitting blue, red, and green lights. Tired, they returned to bed after this half-hour, leaving the phenomenon still visible in the sky. They made four videos with zoom during the observation. The case exhibits good consistency due to the presence of a high-quality, well-detailed testimony and four videos that were utilized for the investigation (see *definition of consistency according to GEIPAN criteria on the GEIPAN website*). The investigation concluded that the witnesses had actually observed the star Sirius (see investigation note) based on the following elements:

- Shape of the PAN: point-like, resembling a star, visible to the naked eye. Constantly distorts on videos due to effects caused by scintillation,
- High brightness,
- Colors, only reported in the videos, multiple and changing due to a combination of factors: chromatic aberration and atmospheric diffusion of light rays,
- Presence of the PAN at an azimuth and elevation very close to those of Sirius, during an hourly slice when the star is well visible.

The strangeness perceived by the witnesses stemmed from the apparent immobility of the PAN and the observed shape distortions and color changes with zoom. We were able to establish that the immobility was only apparent: a nocturnal observation without reference points makes perceiving the slow movement of a star nearly impossible. Furthermore, the distortions and color changes observed were due to a combination of three phenomena: chromatic aberration, scintillation of the star, and atmospheric diffusion.

GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Observation of Sirius.
