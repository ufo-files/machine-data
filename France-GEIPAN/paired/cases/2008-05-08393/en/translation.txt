Summary: Observation of east-to-west movement of a glowing ball: likely misidentification with Jupiter.
--- seg-21d019ad79321a18b31091e3 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 8, 2009, the GEIPAN received a concise email regarding an observation that occurred during the night of Monday, May 5 to Tuesday, May 6, 2008, between 3 and 4 a.m. No further details will be provided. The direct testimony contains too few details for a proper analysis. Jupiter is present in the southeast (azimuth 128 and 3°48" elevation) and could be a source of confusion, especially since the witness does not mention it. The perception of motion may be due to the autokinetic illusion: see the link: Perception of movement of a bright point in the sky. This case is classified B: probable misperception with Jupiter.
