Description: On January 1, 2016, at 1:30 AM, several people observed the silent movement of 3 orange spheres in the sky. The observation lasted 5 minutes before they disappeared into the sky at high speed. Only one testimony was reported. The observation of orange balls in the sky, on a weekend or festive night like New Year's Eve, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The similarity in trajectory of the successive PAN is also typical of lanterns launched one after the other and following the same wind current. The extinction of a lantern, which can be rapid (very dimming light) without being instantaneous, is often interpreted as a sudden disappearance of the object. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable Thai lantern.
