Description: This case, formerly named PARCQ (62) 1981, was recently reviewed (see the investigation notes document). On August 11, 1981, around 1 a.m., a farmer driving a tractor in a field observed red and white glowing orbs in the sky. At first stationary, the orbs slowly moved from north to south. Panicked, the witness returned home where he continued to see the lights. A gendarmerie investigation failed to find other witnesses or evidence to explain the phenomenon. This case is inconsistent (single witness, brief testimony collected several days after the event, no field investigation), and the description of the PAN observed is imprecise. The witness's sincerity and credibility were never questioned. The described phenomenon shares some common characteristics (observation duration, shape, color) with the Moon, possibly obscured by clouds. Notably, the Moon was present in the sky at the time of observation, but it was not mentioned by the witness. However, the provided testimony lacks the necessary details to formally validate this hypothesis. The phenomenon also exhibits some aspects of light phenomena related to storm activity. Fatigue after a long day of work and the panic felt by the witness may have significantly altered the witness's perception or memories. In the current GEIPAN classification, this case of medium oddity is classified as PAN C due to a lack of reliable data and cross-correlations.
