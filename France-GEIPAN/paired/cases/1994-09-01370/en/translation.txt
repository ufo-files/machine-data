Summary: Probable observation of an atmospheric reentry
--- seg-68890bce0387488139c6fd92 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 25, 1994, around 6:10 a.m., a witness observed from his room the rapid descent of a blue ball behind a nearby quarry. No noise was heard, but blue lights rose behind the mountain after the supposed fall. The witness woke up his partner, who only observed the lights behind the quarry. It is likely an atmospheric reentry.
