Summary: Observation of the rapid passage of a formation of five medium-intensity light points: probable observation of an atmospheric reentry of meteoroids.
--- seg-233d75e83c6fc3cc68ef68ef [needs-review] ---
Description: A witness observed on March 13, 2012 at 20:35, the rapid passage of five bright points evolving in formation according to a descending trajectory. No noise was heard during the observation. Another nearly simultaneous testimony with a very similar appearance was reported to the GEIPAN, seen from Saint-Mathieu de Treviers (34) on the same evening and seen from the West by this witness. It is very likely that it is the same phenomenon. The hypothesis of an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid (meteorite) or space debris is therefore very probable. In the light of these information, the GEIPAN classifies this observation as category B, probable observation of atmospheric reentry of meteoroids (meteorite).
