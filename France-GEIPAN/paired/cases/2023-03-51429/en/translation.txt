Description: On March 27, 2023, between 05:50 and 06:08, a witness, in front of his home, was intrigued by the presence in the sky of an "abnormal line of bright lights" that were white. The points seemed to be at roughly equal distances from each other. The phenomenon moved slowly and disappeared from the witness's view towards the east. Only one testimony was collected. The witness, contacted by the GEIPAN, specified that he identified the phenomenon with his son, and confirmed observing Starlinks. The witness also indicated to the GEIPAN that he did not wish to provide a QT to answer requests for clarification. As the Starlinks move from west to east, the GEIPAN notes an inconsistency with the direction of movement indicated by the witness in the report. The GEIPAN classifies the case as C: lack of information.
