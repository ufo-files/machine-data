--- seg-ced837b115c1098dd3823937 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 7, 1988, around 0:45 a.m., several witnesses observed a luminous phenomenon in a perfectly clear sky. Of a white-orange color resembling a quarter moon, this phenomenon suddenly disappeared with a violent explosion followed by a red dot that faded away. It is likely this was an atmospheric reentry.
