Description: On May 13, 1977, around 9:30 PM, several witnesses observed the movements of several glowing points. They moved, changing colors, at a great distance that the witnesses could not assess more precisely. The sequences of movements continued until 10:20 PM, the time of the sudden disappearance of the phenomenon.
