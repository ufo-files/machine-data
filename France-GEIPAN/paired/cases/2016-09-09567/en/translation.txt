Description: On September 29, 2016, around 10:56 PM, a witness observed the cloudy sky and noticed a bright phenomenon (in the shape of a huge helicopter blade) above the Cauffry park. It appeared to rotate silently in place. The observation lasted between 50 and 55 minutes before the phenomenon disappeared abruptly. Only one testimony was collected. The observation shares many similarities with one made 2-3 kilometers away at the same time (see the RANTIGNY (60) case on 09/29/2016), leading to the same conclusions. It is very likely a laser beam, originating from a nightclub or a festive or exceptional event, reflecting off the cloud layer or a fine rain screen (both were present that evening). Depending on atmospheric conditions, the distance a laser can reach varies greatly; it can extend to dozens of kilometers, making the phenomenon fleeting or exceptional at greater distances from the emission source. The GEIPAN did not identify the laser emitter, but has several similar cases with identified effects. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of a laser animation (pink sky or bright spots).
