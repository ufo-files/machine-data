Summary: Observation of a large triangular shape with very bright headlights
--- seg-8c99100532c7f0e4abeb931c [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 22, 2001, around 2:30 AM, a witness in a car observed a large triangular shape flying slowly and silently. After stopping his vehicle, the witness got out and noticed that the phenomenon hovered for about ten seconds above him before departing. Under the object were very bright and very mobile lights, and the "front" of the object seemed to be made of plexiglass. The investigation will show that the witness's personality makes this testimony less credible.
