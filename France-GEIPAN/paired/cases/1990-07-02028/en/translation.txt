Description: The witness in 2008 recounted an observation made during the summer between 1990 and 1993 (the date of 01/06/1990 is given arbitrarily). One evening around 11 PM, while preparing to camp with a friend in his backyard, the witness, aged around ten, observed from his tent's entrance the arrival in the clear sky of three white lights forming an opaque triangular shape of great size. The phenomenon moved silently and very slowly above the town on a straight trajectory. During the brief observation, the witness had the impression that the main triangle was formed by a set of triangles due to other lights. The young boy was frightened, and his friend did not have time to see the phenomenon. Only one testimony was collected.
