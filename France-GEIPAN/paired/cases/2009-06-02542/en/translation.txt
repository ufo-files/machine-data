Description: A witness contacted the GEIPAN on January 10, 2010 regarding a photograph taken on May 12, 2009, containing 7 black dots that the witness cannot identify. Of low strangeness and inconsistent, this observation case without direct observation during the taking of the photograph, has all the characteristics of a misidentification with an insect or possibly bird flight. Therefore, the verification of civil and military air traffic does not seem necessary, it is in any case difficult more than a month after the facts. This case is classified B: insect or bird spots on photograph.
