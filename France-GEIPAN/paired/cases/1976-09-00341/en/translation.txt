Description: On September 10, 1976, at 11:15 PM, a witness observed the passage from the north-east (NO-E) of an object with a round shape and the size of a helicopter, which successively and rapidly emitted three colors: yellow, green, and red. No noise was heard during the observation, which lasted 45 seconds. The person would later testify to the gendarmerie. No other testimony was collected despite a press article on the subject. The witness likely observed the passage of an aircraft.
