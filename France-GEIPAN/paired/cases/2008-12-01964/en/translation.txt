Summary: Multiple observations of the rapid passage of a luminous phenomenon in the night sky: likely observations of an atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-65d0f185f234c8cdde27f49b [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 23, 2008, multiple witnesses across different departments in France observed a brief passage of a luminous phenomenon in the night sky. Four testimonies were collected. All four testimonies from that day (Labarthe-Inard (31), Aureil (87), Roquelaure (32), Le Vigean (86)) describe an observation of a meteor (visual aspect, brief duration of 1 to a few seconds). The available information on azimuth and elevation (with varying precision according to the testimonies) allows for the definition of an overlap zone located, a priori, in northern Spain (refer to the PowerPoint document representing the four testimonies and the bolide synthesis document). The only inconsistent aspect is in the information regarding the course (i.e., the direction of movement). The witness in Le Vigean indicates North-South while the other three indicate East-West. However, this is not uncommon in the observation of proven meteors, as there can be discrepancies in the course due to the brevity of the observation (still compatible with a good recording of the position in the sky but less precise for the course). It is also worth noting that three observers place the observation close to 10 PM, while one of them (Aureil (87)) mentions 9 PM. It is challenging, seven years later, to resolve this discrepancy through a new call to the witness. The GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable observation of a meteor.
