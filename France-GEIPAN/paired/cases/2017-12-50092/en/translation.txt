Summary: Brief observation of the silent passage of a bright round object and gradual dimming before disappearance: probable observation of a white LED balloon.
--- seg-54d832f73a7ab87b25bebff9 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On December 12, 2017, around 9 PM, a witness was walking his dog along a small road adjacent to a railway track when he noticed a silent, bright object passing through the clear sky. It was round and matte white, resembling a "ping-pong ball" and seemed to be internally illuminated. The PAN moved towards an old bridge, then changed direction. Its light intensity gradually diminished and then disappeared. The observation lasted approximately thirty seconds. The witness, contacted by phone on March 15, 2018, provided additional information (see investigation note). Only one testimony was collected.

Analysis of Testimony:
- Appearance of the PAN: Round, bright object, matte white like a "ping-pong ball" and seemed internally illuminated.
- Noise: Silent.
- Appearance location: Above the overhead wire, at approximately a 30-degree angle (see plan in investigation note).
- Size: Significantly smaller than the Moon, 23 millimeters on a rule held at arm's length. The witness specified that it appeared largest during its appearance above the overhead wire, and the object appeared to shrink as it moved away.
- Brightness: Its brightness did not decrease instantly as stated in the testimony, but dimmed with distance. Finally, the PAN flickered and disappeared. The witness believes the flickering may have been caused by the PAN passing behind trees.
- Movement: Altitude and speed were constant.
- Trajectory: According to the witness's drawing, it came from the northwest and headed southeast, which contradicts the answers in the questionnaire. The witness indicated a disappearance to the southwest. However, if he saw the PAN heading towards the bridge, that would be southeast.
- Disappearance: Behind a crane, at approximately 15 degrees, the witness doesn't know if the PAN "went out" or if it passed behind the horizon (buildings, trees).
- Observation duration: Approximately thirty seconds, less than a minute.

Additional Information: The witness's dog did not react, and the witness revealed he is colorblind, but the phenomenon was white, so there was no risk of confusion with another color. The witness likely observed a white LED balloon, and the matte white color like a "ping-pong ball" is close to this type of balloon. The integrated LED light would explain the "internally illuminated" brightness described by the witness. Additionally, the object was silent, and the movement is consistent with the wind direction from the northwest to the southeast. The turn at the end of the observation is probably due to a change in wind direction, which can vary greatly close to the ground due to terrain or potential obstacles (trees, houses, etc.). Throughout the observation, the PAN appeared larger upon its appearance and its size diminished until it disappeared, consistent with the hypothesis of a balloon passing near the witness and moving away. The flickering observed before the disappearance is likely due to the balloon passing behind trees present in the witness's field of observation. For a wind-carried balloon traveling approximately one hundred meters during the observation, the speed is consistent with the wind recorded that evening (see investigation note). However, uncertainty lies in the day (Tuesday) and season (winter), which are not conducive to balloon releases or various festivities, but it is not impossible that it came from a private party or celebration.

Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": probable observation of a white LED balloon.
