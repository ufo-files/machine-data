--- seg-548bde6a07cd2cee9ec51b81 [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 29, 2008, around 7:05 PM or 7:10 PM, a witness was intrigued by the silent passage across the sky of three dark, circular objects. The objects were quickly obscured by the urban landscape. The observation lasted approximately 30 seconds. Based on the objective evidence gathered, namely:

- The rounded shape of the objects.
- The objects' movement along an axis parallel to that of the wind: South to North.
- The estimated maximum possible size between roughly 1 meter and 3 meters.
- The maximum possible zenith altitude estimated between roughly 30 meters and 38.5 meters.
- The objects' color and appearance similar to those of a transparent balloon.
- An undefined release area.
- Three objects observed.
- A significant discrepancy between the altitude estimates given by the primary witness and the calculations made in this report.

We can conclude that the objects described by the witness are wind-carried objects: balloons whose nature and release point could not be defined. This case should be classified as "B" for a probable balloon observation of unknown origin.
