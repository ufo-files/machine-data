Description: On September 30, 2023, at 10:57 PM, the witness, present at their home in Vigneux-sur-Seine (91), observed in the sky a luminous, oval object with a red-orange hue. They described it as radiant, giving the impression of being on fire or reflecting sunlight, without leaving a visible trail. The object remained in their visual field for approximately 30 seconds, moving steadily towards the northeast horizon. Due to its peculiarity and average consistency (single witness, no photo of the PAN), this case appears to be the probable observation of a sky lantern (see the investigation report). According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency refers to the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. This hypothesis is supported by the following elements:

- The observation on a Saturday evening, a typical day for releasing sky lanterns to celebrate various events (parties, birthdays, weddings, etc.),
- The typical color of a sky lantern, red/orange, described by the witness as similar to "fire,"
- The observation lasting a few dozen seconds; thus, persistent,
- Absence of noise,
- Possible presence of a halo, corresponding to the envelope of the lantern.

The study of weather conditions, particularly wind, at the time of the observation, reveals that its direction and strength, both on the ground and at altitude, were compatible with the orientation of the movement of the PAN, suggesting it was a wind-carried object. The case is classified as "A": observation of a sky lantern.
