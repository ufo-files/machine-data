--- seg-689b6db93a83d9cecae681d4 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 14, 1959, at 5:10-15 a.m., three witnesses south of Sali (Algeria) were intrigued by the movements of a bright, orange-colored object in the sky. At 5:30 a.m., the object stopped. For 40 minutes, they observed the object slowly drifting towards the NE, frequently changing in appearance and color (red at one end). The observation was made using binoculars. No trail or noise was reported. The observation lasted approximately one hour in total. Only one statement was collected with two diagrams (the object's movement and shape changes). Another observation is noted in the report. It describes on September 12, 1959, at 7:00 p.m., the observation of an orange-colored phenomenon with an apparent diameter of a star located at a very high altitude and moving at a high speed of 180° to 360° in about 30 seconds. No particular sound was noticed. Only one statement was collected. For the first observation, due to the duration of the observation, the slow movement, and the time close to sunrise, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of a sunlit balloon. The directions of observation and movement lack precision. For the observation on September 12, also reported in the statement, the GEIPAN proposes the hypothesis of a meteoroid passing (short observation of the high-speed movement of an elongated, orange-colored light phenomenon at a very high altitude).
