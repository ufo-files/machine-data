Summary: Observation of the movement and shape changes of an orange-colored glowing object in the sky: probable observation of a balloon.
--- seg-689b6db93a83d9cecae681d4 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On September 14, 1959, at 5:10-15 a.m., three witnesses south of Sali (Algeria) were intrigued by the movements of a bright, orange-colored object in the sky. At 5:30 AM, the object comes to a stop. For 40 minutes, they observe that the body drifts slowly towards the NE, frequently changing in appearance and color (red at one end). The observation is made with binoculars. No trail or noise was observed. The observation will last approximately one hour in total. Only one testimony is collected with two schematics (object's evolution and shape change). Another observation is noted in the report. It is described for September 12, 1959, at 7 PM, the observation of an orange-colored phenomenon and an apparent diameter of a star located at a very high altitude and moving at a high speed of 180° to 360° in approximately 30 seconds. No particular sound is noticed. Only one testimony is collected. For the first observation, due to the duration of the observation, the slow movement, and the time close to sunrise, GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable observation of a sunlit balloon. The observation and movement directions lack precision. For the observation of September 12, also reported in the testimony, the GEIPAN proposes the hypothesis of the passage of a meteoroid (short observation of the high-speed movement of an elongated, orange-colored light phenomenon at very high altitude).
