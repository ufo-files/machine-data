Summary: Possible observations of an atmospheric reentry.
--- seg-08f47439b350297c52329b31 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the morning of November 8, 1978, several witnesses in different departments observed the rapid passage in the sky of a luminous phenomenon. According to the descriptions of the witnesses, this object was followed by a colored trail in the sky and moved according to a descending trajectory. The presumed locations of the object's fall were searched, but no ground traces were discovered. These witnesses likely witnessed an atmospheric reentry.
