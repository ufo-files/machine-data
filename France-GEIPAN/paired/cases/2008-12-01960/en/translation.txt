Description: On December 21, 2008, at 7:46 PM, a driver and his passenger noticed a phenomenon in the sky that intrigued them. After parking their car, the witnesses observed in silence for several minutes three yellow-orange light circles that moved. The description of the phenomenon: "Three orange balls moving for a few minutes with the wind (a light west wind that evening, as per weather report)" perfectly matches the characteristics of Thai lanterns; the witnesses' account provides details that strengthen this hypothesis: "with a ring," "yellow-orange color, like a lighter flame." The time (7:46 PM) and day (Sunday) are unusual, but it could have been a test for a larger release planned for the end-of-year holidays. Although the witness does not endorse this hypothesis, this case is classified as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
