Description: Recent (2016) testimony of an old observation. On July 17, 1978, at 11:30 PM, several people in the courtyard of a farm observed above the house a perfect, fluorescent green disk, motionless. The phenomenon disappeared at a blinding speed. Only one testimony was collected. This observation, for 3 seconds, of a green disk disappearing at blinding speed, has certain characteristics of a bolide (atmospheric entry of a meteorite). The immobility at the beginning of the observation is surprising for this hypothesis, but if the phenomenon is seen almost face-on, the witness may have the illusion of immobility. The apparent change of direction may also be the result of the relative geometry of the phenomenon in relation to the witness. Nevertheless, this observation is notably different from other bolide observations: no glowing trail was observed. Moreover, no other observation of this type seems to have been cataloged, which is surprising for a phenomenon of this type. The GEIPAN received this observation report in 2016; beyond these elementary verifications, the GEIPAN may conduct a more in-depth investigation. The GEIPAN classifies this case "C" due to lack of information and cross-checks.
