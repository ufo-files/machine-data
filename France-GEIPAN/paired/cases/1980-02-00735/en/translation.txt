Description: Around 8:35 PM on February 15, 1980, a witness observed a bright sphere in the southwest direction. It was located just above a building. Leaning against a wall to view the phenomenon with binoculars, the witness noticed the presence of two balls, one red and one white, positioned above the first. They moved without shifting. The position of the two balls reversed, and the entire scene disappeared after tracing a relatively large "Z" in the sky. The observation lasted approximately 10 minutes in a perfectly clear sky. No other witnesses to this phenomenon, for which we lack data, came forward to the gendarmerie. This witness likely made an astronomical observation of Venus (magnitude -4), very low on the horizon, disappearing into the urban landscape.
