Description: On Wednesday, July 22 at 0:45 a.m., a witness observed from his home the movement of four pale yellow lights heading towards Paris. He went to get his camera and upon his return, he only saw one light, which he photographed before it disappeared as well. The description corresponds to observations of flying lanterns (also known as Thai lanterns), and the wind direction recorded in Paris at the time of the observation supports this hypothesis. This observation case, of low strangeness and average consistency given the limited details in the questionnaire and some discrepancies between it and the information transmitted by phone, is very likely a misidentification of Thai lanterns. This case is classified as B: probable observation of Thai lanterns passing.
