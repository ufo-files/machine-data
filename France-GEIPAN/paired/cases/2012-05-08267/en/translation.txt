Description: On May 30, 2012, a witness observed a phenomenon of bent grass on a plot of wood that belongs to them. Over a diameter of approximately 4 meters, the grass is bent and slightly yellowed, with no evidence nearby suggesting that an animal or vehicle could have caused it. Based on the objective evidence gathered, namely: - A single photograph taken by the witness. - No detailed surveys or meticulous inspections of the trace and its surroundings. The hypothesis of a vortex created by local wind phenomena remains the least improbable, but could not be corroborated by relevant meteorological data or the observation of other local disturbances. We can conclude that despite numerous hypotheses, none are satisfactory in light of the quantifiable and exploitable data provided by examining the photograph and the testimony. This testimony is of rather average consistency: fairly precise, but from a single witness with only one photograph and without an examination of the site. The observation is quite strange as it involves a phenomenon that appears a priori unremarkable. This case is classified as "C" as an unidentified phenomenon in its current state, as the lack of elements does not allow for a solid opinion.
