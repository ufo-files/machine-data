Description: On October 20, 2022, around 7:00 a.m., the witness is on the terrace of her home in Parthenay (Deux-Sèvres). She observes two __UFO_PROTECTED_000__s, appearing as two intense yellow lights, stationary in the sky towards the northwest. These lights are approximately 15 cm apart when measured with a rule held at arm's length, each with an estimated apparent diameter of 2 cm. After a few moments of observation, the light on the left moves very quickly to the right, leaving behind a brief orange trail before disappearing. The witness states she cannot specify the disappearance of the second PAN, as it may have either turned off in place or disappeared without her noticing, as her attention was focused on the movement of the first. The total observation duration is estimated at around 15 seconds. No other witness statements were collected regarding this event. The case consistency is estimated as low, due to the witness's unique account, the absence of PAN photographs, and the lack of response to the investigator's requests for additional information.

According to GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. Checks with specialized services revealed no aircraft (airplane or helicopter) presence in the area during the observation period. Furthermore, analysis of databases dedicated to known astronomical phenomena did not show any meteor passage matching the temporal and directional criteria of the reported event. Finally, the hypothesis of observing two drones is considered unlikely, partly due to the orange trail observed on one of the unidentified aerial phenomena (PAN), as described by the witness and noted in the investigation report. A request for additional information was made to the witness to refine the search, but no response was received. The GEIPAN classifies the case as 'C', an unidentified phenomenon due to a lack of data.
