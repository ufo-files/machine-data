Description: Observation over several days and hours starting at 9:30 PM of oval shapes with red and green lights moving slowly or remaining stationary. This observation was made by several witnesses as well as by a patrol of the National Police. The phenomenon could not be identified, though it might be mistaken for lasers from a Lille nightclub. It could also be linked to the observation on July 17 if the hypothesis of aircraft passage is incorrect.
