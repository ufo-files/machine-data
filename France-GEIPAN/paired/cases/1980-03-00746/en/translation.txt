Summary: Long observations from a moving vehicle of a bright PAN at low altitude in the night sky: likely observation of Venus.
--- seg-c63811e972a4444f3d2816fd [needs-review] ---
Description: On March 3, 1980, starting at 8:45 PM, witnesses in a vehicle observed above the ridges a bright PAN in the shape of a plate. According to the witnesses, the object was between 100 and 200 meters from the vehicle. During a stop, the witnesses heard no noise and smelled no unusual odors. Over approximately a 12-kilometer distance, these individuals felt the object was following them as it seemed to stop or take off at the same time as their vehicle. The object suddenly disappeared and another red, conical light phenomenon appeared. Upon their arrival home, the witnesses went to the gendarmerie to make a statement. No other witnesses or traces of the phenomenon were found during the investigation. According to the direction of the route taken by the witnesses between Favalello and Corte (2B), and considering the very winding road between these two locations, it is likely that these individuals made an astronomical observation of Venus. Particularly visible at this time of year (magnitude -4.10) and very low on the horizon, this star would have appeared and disappeared along the ridge line.
