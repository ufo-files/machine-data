Summary: Nighttime observation of a luminous phenomenon described differently by two witnesses in a car: observation by the driver (T1) of a dashboard reflection on a vehicle glass element.
--- seg-ddbc5e2b7cdff3856fd9190c [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 19, 2017 at 20:35, two people, driving in a car (T1 driver) and (T2 front passenger), were intrigued by colored light shapes seen through the windshield. The witnesses are located on the D39 at the exit of Fouchy (67) heading towards Urbeis (67). The witnesses disagree on the description of the phenomenon. However, they agree to say that the phenomenon was silent and stationary. They are taking photographs with their respective smartphones. The two witnesses describe the PAN in very different ways, particularly T2 attributes his differing perception compared to T1 (See investigation notes). By observing the photos provided by the witnesses, it is also noted that the PAN observed by passenger T2 is clear, while that seen by driver T1 is blurry: this suggests the possibility of two distinct observations of two PAN. Two separate observation cases are open (see investigation notes). An examination of the car dashboard (Dacia Sandéro) of the witnesses allows us to identify the origin of the PAN seen by driver T1. By overlaying the witness photo and the dashboard image transparently, we can see that it is a reflection in the windshield of the "Media Nav" screen, with the presence of a wire or cable in front of it explaining the vision of two triangles. The red dot is the "warning" button, and the orange dot is the "door lock" button. This also explains the silence and immobility of the phenomenon. As a result, the GEIPAN concludes that the observation of T1 corresponds to a dashboard board mirror reflection on a glass element. The GEIPAN classifies the case as: reflection. See the case [CD39] of FOUCHY (67) to URBEIS (67) on 19.11.2017 T2 to understand what phenomenon was observed by the T2 (passenger) and what classification was given to the case.
