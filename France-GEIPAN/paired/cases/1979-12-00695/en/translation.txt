Summary: Motorists observed a glowing object moving towards the East: astronomical observations of the Moon.
--- seg-2175cb3d1aebc65128c01304 [needs-review] ---
Description: This observation case was recently reviewed (see the *Investigation Notes* document). On December 7, 1979, around 9:15 PM, several drivers in the same geographical area spotted a luminous phenomenon in the sky that intrigued them. It appeared to be moving at low altitude and was seen in various forms by the witnesses (ball, parachute, capital 'L'). The investigation was unable to gather any additional information, except for barking heard from a nearby farm. This case is consistent (multiple independent witnesses), and the description of the PAN observed is accurate. The sincerity and credibility of the witnesses have never been questioned. The described phenomenon shares many common characteristics (observation duration, shape, size, color) with a perfectly known astronomical object: the Moon at sunrise. Furthermore, the Moon was indeed present in the observed area of the sky. The issue is not the witnesses' visual perception, but the interpretation they make of their observation through their feelings (fatigue, driving at night). In the current classification of GEIPAN, this case of weak strangeness is classified as 'A', observations related to a misperception with the Moon.
