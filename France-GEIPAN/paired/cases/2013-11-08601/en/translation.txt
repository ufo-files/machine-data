--- seg-c65b75310d4d901d68bb45b3 [needs-review] ---
Description: On November 8, 2013, at 5:40 a.m., a witness, lying in bed, observed through the window a very slow-moving elongated shape with a flickering light in a clear sky. The PAN was heading southeast. No noise was heard during the 15 minutes of observation; the phenomenon gradually disappeared. Only one testimony was collected. This observation report is indeed brief: the witness did not describe the shape, color, or lighting of what he saw. The strangeness of the phenomenon is fairly low, as the witness initially thought he was seeing an airplane; it is only the apparent slowness of the phenomenon that makes it strange: a relative movement angle compared to the witness could potentially explain this surprising effect. This odd and inconsistent case is classified "C" due to a lack of information.
