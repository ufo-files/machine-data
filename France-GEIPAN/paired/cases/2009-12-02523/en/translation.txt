Description: On December 31, 2009, around midnight, two groups of people observed in the sky of Contamines-Montjoie (Haute-Savoie) the movement of lights that quickly disappeared. Upon returning from vacation, one of the witnesses, intrigued by his observation, contacted the GEIPAN and will make a statement of his observation to the gendarmerie. He will also detail in the questionnaire the characteristics of the objects observed by all witnesses. These resemble the characteristics of Thai lanterns released during festivities. The case is classified as B, as a probable observation of flying lanterns (see the investigation report GEIPAN).
