Summary: Observation of an oval and dark PAN in levitation over a field: lack of information.
--- seg-4a8b3d76021c31fdd024a16a [needs-review] ---
Description: On October 9, 2015, at 9:00 PM, a motorcyclist on a small road observed an oval, dark-colored PAN 50 meters away in a field. The witness stopped to observe: he heard no noise and saw people entering the PAN which was levitating above the ground. Frightened, the witness resumed his journey, stopping again a little further to see the PAN depart quickly on a straight trajectory. No other testimony was collected. This testimony is surprisingly brief given the strangeness of the long-observed phenomenon (10 minutes). Due to this strangeness, the GEIPAN asked the witness to go to the gendarmerie, which he verbally agreed to do. However, despite several reminders by email and phone from the gendarmerie and the GEIPAN, he never showed up. Therefore, the GEIPAN classifies this case as category "C" due to lack of information.
