Description: On the night of September 3 to 4, 2025, in Dommartin-Lès-Toul (54), a witness driving on an unlit path adjacent to the Toulois Equestrian Center reported observing two distinct glowing orbs in a partly clear sky. A first orb, red in color, appeared above the treetops. It moved slowly, with minor altitude variations, giving an overall impression of stability, with no correlation to a known aircraft or celestial body. A second orb, white and larger, remained stationary to the naked eye but appears on photos and videos as gradually rising, without displaying characteristics associated with an identified terrestrial or aerial light source. The GEIPAN collected one testimony. The peculiarity of this case mainly rests on the witness's (T1) perception. However, the investigation results allow for relativizing the apparent anomaly of the observation (see investigation report). The remarkably precise testimony, with the valuable contribution of photos that could be analyzed, increases the case's consistency. According to the GEIPAN's criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. Consulting the astronomy software Stellarium® and overlaying the photos on sky condition captures at the observation times reveals a confusion of the PAN with Jupiter, Betelgeuse, and Venus. The GEIPAN classifies this case as A: observation of celestial bodies (Jupiter, Betelgeuse, and Venus).
