Description: A witness reports observing two bright points moving slowly across the sky. They move in parallel, maintaining the same distance between them, and no sound is heard during the brief observation before the objects disappear. The time and date of the observation, the space shuttle was in the approach phase to the International Space Station. The testimony is consistent with the observation of this "team" (a French term for a pair of objects moving in tandem). However, the direction described by the witness (south) is inconsistent with the "Corvids' Point" direction he also mentions, which is east-southeast, matching the ISS trajectory (see map). This case is classified as "A": observation of the space shuttle + ISS.
