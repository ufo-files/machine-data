Summary: Observation of the movement of orange-colored light phenomena: probable Thai lanterns.
--- seg-becad1b832e02bc89fa0711f [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 25, 2011, between 6:35 and 6:40 PM, a motorist and his passenger observed in the sky a bright, stationary light performing swinging movements. Initially thinking it was a firework, the witness continued driving but then stopped to take photographs. Upon arriving home, they noticed three luminescent masses of color in a line, gaining altitude. The objects disappeared very quickly. No noise was heard during the several-minute observation. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected by the gendarmerie. The hypothesis of Thai lanterns remains the most probable; it is consistent with the wind and the photographs, and is highly plausible for the observation date. However, it should be noted that the irregular movement of the lanterns raises questions: one would have to assume the hypothesis of upward currents along the path. This case is classified as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
