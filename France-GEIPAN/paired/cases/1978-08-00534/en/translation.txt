Description: On August 6, 1978, two witnesses reported observing a slow-moving bright light in the sky. According to the witnesses, a very bright yellow light moved slowly and at a steady pace for three-quarters of an hour at a high altitude, following a southeast-northwest direction, before disappearing from the witnesses' view. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected. These individuals likely observed the movement of a satellite.
