Description: On June 20, 2009, at approximately 7:20 PM, a witness filmed a phenomenon that intrigued them: a bright point of light moved silently in the sky. Upon watching the video, it is clear that there was a misidentification with an airplane taking off or approaching Saint-Pierre – Pierrefonds (Réunion) Airport: green position lights, flashing anti-collision lights. This case is classified as "A": airplane observation.
