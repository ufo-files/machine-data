--- seg-12c89f248b01d54a95440ff5 [needs-review] ---
Description: On the morning of October 8, 2019, a witness in a car observed a very bright point in the sky, whose intensity was stronger than that of Venus. He immediately thought it was a large debris falling from its orbit. This description aligns with numerous testimonies (at least 50) reporting the passage of a bolide (the atmospheric entry of a meteoroid, a small rocky body) on October 8, 2019 at 05:20 UTC (or 07:20 local French time), visible in Occitanie but also in Nouvelle Aquitaine, and cataloged by the International Meteor Organization (IMO). For more information, consult our website. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A" for bolide.
