Description: On July 31, 20474, at 8:40 PM, a witness and their family observed a dark object, roughly the size of a commercial airliner, in a clear sky. It moved silently from the east to the west, moving slowly while oscillating and appearing to spin. Flashes were noticed. No trail or halo was observed. Only one statement was collected. This statement is of good consistency: precise, from three acquainted witnesses expressing independently; no photos were taken. The observation is strange as the object had an unusual appearance, with non-classical behavior. However, its characteristics in terms of speed, size, shape, color, and trajectory do not present any extraordinary aspects that cannot be explained. Based on the objective elements gathered in the investigation report, this case is classified as "B" for a probable wind-carried object observation.
