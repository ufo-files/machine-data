Summary: Observations of the silent movement of a glowing flying object: astronomical misperception with the Moon.
--- seg-f0b4e7cc53019d63f4fc66c3 [needs-review] ---
Description: On January 27, 1982, around 7:15 PM, an entire family in a car on CD 910, in the commune of Guenviller (57), observed for two minutes a peculiar object in the cloudy sky. The object emitted light and moved slowly without making a sound. It was in the shape of a half-sphere. Two of the witnesses returned to the site around 7:30 PM, but the object had disappeared. On the map (see map), the witnesses' journey is shown in black dots, and the direction of observation in blue. It is noted that the direction of observation was generally along the road axis, a little to the left or right, depending on the observation phases, which gives an azimuth between 242 and 244°. An astronomical verification (for Nancy at 7:15 PM) indicates that the Moon is at an azimuth of 244, at 4.5° elevation (see celestial map), and appears as a thin crescent inclined. Since the witnesses did not mention the Moon in their observation, although it was exactly in their field of view, it is the Moon they observed, deceived by the low elevation of the celestial body, and by some low clouds that masked a part of it, giving it the shape of a cigar or half-sphere. Other than estimates of the object's size, the witnesses' account is consistent with this hypothesis: an inclined plate shape that moves from the right to the left of the road according to the vehicle's progress and direction, and stops when the vehicle stops. The object's disappearance corresponds to the gradual setting of the Moon and/or a cloud passage. This case, initially classified "D," is now classified "A": observation of the Moon at sunset.
