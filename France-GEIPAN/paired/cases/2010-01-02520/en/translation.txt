Summary: Atmospheric reentry of a bolide.
--- seg-99642d9f12edfe65c7e9cd88 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On January 13, 2010, at 6:35 PM, a driver observed an intense glowing phenomenon for 6 to 7 seconds that intrigued him. A fireball with a trajectory from south-east to north traveled very quickly across the sky before disappearing on the horizon. Another witness of this phenomenon expressed himself on an internet site. An atmospheric reentry of a bolide or meteor was recorded by the French Network for the Observation of Meteors that day at 5:35 PM universal time, which corresponds to 6:35 PM French time. It was particularly visible in Gironde.
