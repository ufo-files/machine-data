Description: On the night of September 26 to 27 at 2:14 AM, a witness observed for 7 to 10 seconds the rapid passage of a large triangular object. This object, which appears much larger than an airplane, moves silently and very quickly, about 2 to 3 times faster than an airplane at an estimated altitude of 600 to 700 meters. The triangular shape was dark, with a phosphorescent light at each end. No explanation could be given for this phenomenon.
