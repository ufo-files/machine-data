Summary: Possible observations of a light display in the sky.
--- seg-c76492ee84b6a024489dc54d [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A motorist and his passengers observed in the sky the rapid and random movements of an oval, glowing object. Bluish in color, it moved across the cloudless sky. Only one report was sent to GEIPAN. The characteristics described (bluish light spot "on" the clouds, impression of a large electric torch), random movements in direction and altitude, observation duration of 30 minutes, observation at 2:00 AM during a long weekend and "festive" period) inevitably suggest the observation of a light show from one of the many nightclubs in the Agde-Béziers region. This type of mobile spots on cloudless ceilings are frequently a source of misidentification, and according to weather conditions, the beams may not be visible. Locating and precisely identifying the relevant nightclub would not add anything to the low degree of strangeness of an observation whose majority of characteristics are those of a known phenomenon. The case is classified as B, indicating a probable observation of a nightclub light show.
