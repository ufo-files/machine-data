Description: A witness reports his astonishment after discovering peculiar shapes in photographic images. This case, without direct observation during the taking of the pictures, exhibits all characteristics of a posteriori misidentification of a pair of insects that passed very quickly and very close to the camera lens, resulting in a blurred effect while the rest of the image is clear. This case is classified as B.
