Summary: Observation of the silent movement of yellow-red light phenomena in the night sky: lack of reliable information.
--- seg-ea5dfd3f8e1d513c3cae9880 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On June 25 at 12:13 AM and then 12:14 AM, two witnesses on their terrace observed the movements of round, glowing, yellow-red phenomena that successively appeared. No noise is noticed. They quickly disappear into the sky. Only one testimony is collected. The testimony is given to the gendarmerie by a single witness, who reports, in part, observations made solely by his wife. This witness never responded to the GEIPAN request to complete a questionnaire, in particular from his wife. Let's break down the witness statement elements from the Gendarmerie report:

- The PAN appeared to be point-like.
- Predominantly yellow and red in color.
- Followed a linear trajectory.
- Each of the two sequences was immobilized for approximately 5 seconds.
- The observation duration was 4 seconds (Note: incompatible with the noted immobilization duration above, which is longer) for the first sequence, then between 15 and 20 seconds for the second.
- The PAN was very bright.
- The observation was directed towards the southeast (Note: for which sequence?). Also note the inclusion in the protocol of a photograph showing a circular, white luminous phenomenon, without mentioning if this image has a connection to the observation. As it stands, some of these data could correspond to several distinct known phenomena (satellite, meteor, airplane, or helicopter, etc.). However, there is a lack of information to confirm or deny either of these hypotheses, in particular: - Was the trajectory consistently linear? - Although the observation azimuth is known from the overlaid information provided by the gendarmes, we do not know if this azimuth applies to one or both of the sequences. - Height data relative to the horizon is also missing. - Finally, there is doubt regarding the duration of the observation and the PAN's immobility mentioned by the witness twice in the protocol, which contradicts itself. The wife's testimony of the main witness would have also been useful to include in the file, but it was never provided despite reminders from GEIPAN. The lack of information and inconsistency (in particular due to many elements of indirect testimony and the non-response to the request for additional information GEIPAN) prevent the testimony from being exploited. The case is classified C: lack of reliable information.
