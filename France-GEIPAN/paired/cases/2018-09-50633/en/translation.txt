Summary: Observation of the slow passage in the sky of a triangular shape spinning on itself: very probable observation of a fantasy balloon carried by the wind.
--- seg-be4490cd067532345f0aae6f [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: During a family weekend, between 5:20 PM and 5:50 PM, the witness observes a strange object in the sky. He sees "a black equilateral triangle slowly crossing the sky silently while turning on itself." This "device" appears larger than a commercial airliner, its sides reflect sunlight and intensify it, and it produces small flashes from below, but this might simply be a reflection. Initially, the witness thinks it is a lost satellite, but the observation lasts too long. The object slowly crosses the sky, losing a little altitude after twenty minutes, then slowly regains altitude while changing direction slightly, ending as a mere sparkling star in broad daylight. Only one testimony will be collected. The consistency* is good, with a testimony supplemented by a video that could be analyzed. According to the criteria of the GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony. The witness likely observed a fantasy balloon (helium-filled party balloon) carried by the local light breeze. Our analysis (see the investigation report) is based on the following elements: - The shape and color of the PAN are consistent with what is possible to find in terms of fantasy balloons. - The movement of the PAN is in the direction of the dominant wind; its 'triangle' shape contributes to its turning on itself as reported and visible on the video. - Its low speed appears also compatible with that of the wind. The strangeness perceived by the witness is created by the incomprehension of the phenomenon, the non-identification of the observed object. "What is that thing?" says the witness in the video. The silence, the movement ("turning on itself"), and an exaggerated assessment of size ("this device must have been larger than a commercial airliner") also contribute to the incomprehension. After analysis, the strangeness of the case is quite low. All physical and dynamic characteristics of the PAN are consistent with the studied hypothesis. The GEIPAN classifies this case as 'A', very probable observation of a fantasy balloon carried by the wind.
