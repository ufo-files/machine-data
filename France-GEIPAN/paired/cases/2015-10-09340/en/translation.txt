Summary: Observation of the silent passage of 3 to 4 red-orange PANs (unidentified flying objects) in the night sky: observation of Thai lanterns released after funerals.
--- seg-dbdc2e74ed3b784ca5400f91 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 20, 2015, at 8:10 PM, a witness observed at their residence three to four circular, red-orange light phenomena in the sky, moving silently on a straight trajectory from North to South. The observation lasted approximately 15 minutes before the phenomena disappeared from the witness's view. The witness took several photographs. No other testimonies were collected. The observation of red-orange balls crossing the sky in a few minutes strongly resembles Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from North to South, which is consistent with the North wind recorded in Nancy (see the Météociel record indicating a North wind for the observation day; record at Nancy-Ochey, 22km away). It is rare to observe such festive phenomena on a weekday, but it is noted that this occurred during school holidays. The photos are affected by significant camera shake, which is normal for night photos taken by hand; they clearly show the yellow-orange color described by the witness, a characteristic of Thai lanterns. The change in direction to East at the end of the observation could correspond to a local wind following the valley of the Ruppes stream. The GEIPAN classified and published this case as a probable "B" (lantern) for the first time, then, after receiving additional information from the gendarmerie confirming the release of Thai lanterns following a funeral, the GEIPAN reclassified this case as A: certain observation of the passage of Thai lanterns.
