Description: On the first day, at the time of observation, the space shuttle was in approach phase to the International Space Station. The next day, the shuttle had docked with the ISS. The testimony is consistent with the observation of this "team", except that the witness describes a change in direction: as this change in direction occurred behind a mask (chimney), one might think of a misestimation of the direction of movement. Changes in brightness and appearance are normal: the reflection of the sun varies during passage, and the approach to the horizon turns all luminous objects reddish. This case is classified "B": probable observation of the space shuttle and the ISS.
