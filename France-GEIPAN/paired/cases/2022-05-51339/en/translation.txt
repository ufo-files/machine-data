Summary: Long observation of bright points in the night sky: observation of planets in conjunction, Venus and Jupiter.
--- seg-463ed61ebbfbe3fe952381c8 [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 1, 2022, starting at 05:30, two residents of BALAN (01) observed, during their entire trip, in the distance, bright points (larger points at the top and smaller points following) that did not appear to move or flicker. Upon arrival at BALAN, around 6:05 a.m., the witness takes photographs. The observation is made to the East with an elevation angle of approximately 30 to 45°. Two or three other photos taken a little later, around 06:20, show that only two points remain visible and do not appear to be arranged in the same way (but their relative position remains the same): they seem to be moving towards the South. As the day breaks, it becomes more difficult to observe them. The case consistency is good: one single witness report but a fairly complete one including 6 photographs (see the investigation report). This concerns the observation of the conjunction of Jupiter and Venus (see the investigation report). The witness's description of the PAN, the positions of the two points relative to each other, and the azimuth are perfectly consistent with those of Jupiter and Venus between 05:30 and 06:20 on that day. Those two planets were in conjunction and particularly bright. The case is classified A, planet observation, conjunction, Venus and Jupiter.
