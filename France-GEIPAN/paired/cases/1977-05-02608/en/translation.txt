Summary: Observations of a night flight of helicopters.
--- seg-7a449e348f8360c5207023f6 [needs-review] ---
Description: On May 11, 1977, between 10:05 PM and 10:25 PM, several people observed two objects in the sky moving silently and emitting a bright red light intermittently. The witnesses also noted that these objects emitted a white light towards the ground. A gendarmerie investigation determined that two "PUMA-SA/330" aircraft from the Cannet-des-Maures (83) base school conducted a night flight, performing exercises (including ground illuminations) in the residential area of the witnesses.
