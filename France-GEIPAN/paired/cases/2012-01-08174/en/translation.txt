Description: On January 1st, 2012 at 1:35 AM, two witnesses in a car observed in the sky the silent east-to-west movement of an unidentified craft. The witnesses do not agree on the shape, but they both noticed a yellow-orange glow surrounding the object. The witnesses lose sight of the object as it is hidden by the forest. The description and wind direction (see weather report) are consistent with the appearance of a Thai lantern. However, one witness describes a triangular shape, while the other describes a circular shape. There were numerous releases of lanterns on this January 1st, 2012 night. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of the movement of a Thai lantern.
