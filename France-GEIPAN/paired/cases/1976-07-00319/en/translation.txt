--- seg-8818f058f9651a3604d2b231 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of July 15, 1976, around midnight, several people observed the movements of a multi-colored light phenomenon on Mount "Malgré Tout," where a TV relay tower is located. The phenomenon's evolution was rapid, but no noise was heard during the 20 minutes of observation. Despite a gendarmerie investigation and observations made with binoculars by witnesses, we lack information and details about this observed phenomenon.
