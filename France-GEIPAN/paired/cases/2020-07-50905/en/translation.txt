Description: On January 2, 2020, at 7:50 AM, the witness observed a stationary bright point through their window, at an angular height of approximately 40° and a distance of about twenty centimeters from a nearby parasol pine. This point, located north-northwest, exhibited characteristics of twinkling and brightness, with a color shift towards red, slightly larger than a planet. The observation lasted about 5 minutes, then around 8 AM, the witness observed the point move at the same speed as an airplane, following a straight trajectory towards the east. According to the criteria of GEIPAN, the case consistency is good (consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony): one testimony, without photos or videos. Three hypotheses were analyzed that could correspond to the physical and dynamic characteristics of PAN observed: an astronomical observation of the star Capella, an aeronautical observation of an airplane, and an alternative possibility involving the observation of a helicopter. The hypothesis of a star observation is dismissed due to several inconsistencies, such as a brightness "greater than" that of Venus and an elevation 27° lower than the star Capella. Furthermore, the final reported movement by the witness of approximately 50° towards the east in a relatively short time span tends to contradict this hypothesis. The hypothesis of an aeronautical observation is of an airplane or a helicopter. The hypothesis of an aircraft seems consistent with the testimony data. The preference for a helicopter is based on the witness mentioning a red light, a characteristic of helicopters. In the studied case, an observation of an airplane moving towards the east would have been associated with a green light (from signaling on the right wing). In conclusion, the elements of the testimony align with the hypothesis of a helicopter following the N-EA air route and changing its trajectory towards the East. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "B": probable observation of a helicopter. *According to the criteria of GEIPAN, consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony.
