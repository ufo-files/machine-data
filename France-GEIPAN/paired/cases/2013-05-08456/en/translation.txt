Description: On May 19, 2013, around midnight thirty, several people observed from a first-floor balcony six to eight bright, orange-yellow light balls silently crossing the horizon from the south. The witness, facing north, saw bright objects in the northwest and watched them move away towards the south. The Meteociel report for Rouen indicates a cloudy sky, northeast wind, following a rain under a north wind. This testimony is in every way consistent with the observation of Thai lanterns: appearance, duration, movement according to the wind, time, and day of the week, all aligning with the end of a festival. The slightly rainy weather is indeed not very favorable for flying lanterns, but they still work under a few raindrops. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns.
