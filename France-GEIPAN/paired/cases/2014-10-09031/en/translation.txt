Summary: Observation of the silent movement in the night sky of a PAN in the shape of an upside-down "Y": probable observation of a flight of large migratory birds.
--- seg-27ca31eb3514a3de3b72525c [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 26, 2014, around 1:30 AM, a witness opening a residential gate was surprised by the silent S-N movement of an object in the shape of a "Y" upside down. No color or flashing was observed during the approximately 10 seconds of observation. All present family members witnessed the movement of this phenomenon, which was then lost from sight. Only one testimony was collected. Based on the objective evidence gathered in the investigation file (see the investigation report), we can conclude that the PAN observed by the witness could be mistaken for a flight of birds in formation. But the lack of information and verification cannot reasonably allow us to conclude with certainty. This testimony turns out to be of fairly average consistency since it is quite vague and lacks many details, all related by a single witness (which can indeed be corroborated by 2 other members of his family) and without a photo. The observation is actually quite unremarkable as it involves a PAN with an appearance (or shape) that is certainly unusual, but potentially explainable, at least in terms of shape, size, speed, trajectory, and luminosity. The GEIPAN classifies this case as B, likely an observation of a flight of large migratory birds.
