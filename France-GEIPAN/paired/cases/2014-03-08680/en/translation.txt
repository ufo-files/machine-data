--- seg-13cba171a6d63536d13dc234 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On March 31, 2014, around 10:33 PM, several people in different regions of France observed a bright phenomenon in the sky. Three testimonies were collected, describing observations in Strasbourg (Bas-Rhin), Peigney (Haute-Marne), and Rombas (Moselle). That day, specialized sites listed numerous observations not only in France but also in Germany. The direction indicated by the witnesses corresponds to the reconstructed trajectory of an atmospheric reentry. See the amsmeteors site. As a result, the GEIPAN classifies the case as A: Meteoroid Return.
