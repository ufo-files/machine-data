Description: On August 30, 2013, around 10:30 PM, a witness observed for about ten seconds the silent movement of a blue point, about the size of a tennis ball, descending from the sky on a S-shaped trajectory. During the descent, the colors changed from blue to red to green and then to yellow. The light suddenly went out. Given the information gathered, in particular: - No precise information on the trajectory of the PAN. - No precise information either on the light and colorimetric characteristics of the PAN. - Lack of angular measurements. We can conclude that the lack of information does not allow us to favor any hypothesis. This case is classified as "C" as an observation of an unidentified phenomenon at this stage, with the significant lack of elements not allowing a solid opinion to be formed. This testimony is of low consistency: although a sketch exists, it remains imprecise and lacks essential and necessary data for further study. Only one eyewitness testified and no photos or videos were documented. The observation could be moderately strange, however, too many imprecisions and gaps in the testimony prevent us from judging its strangeness in a truly valid way.
