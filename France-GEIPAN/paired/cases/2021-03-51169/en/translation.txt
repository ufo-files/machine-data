**Description:** On March 27, 2021, around 4:50 a.m., the witness, accompanied by his son, drives from the village of BRUGNY-VAUDANCOURT (51) towards NANCY (54) on the RN4 highway. At the village of SOUDE (51), he spots numerous lights in the sky that remind him of "an antenna" illuminated by a sequence of white lights. He estimates seeing between 50 and 100 lights. This shape is on his left (northward) and covers a large portion of the sky. He wakes up his sleeping son to witness the scene. As they drive, he observes that this "antenna" on his left seems to follow them. He thinks he can pass it but it remains at his level. Then, the shape moves towards VITRY-LE-FRANCOIS (51). It accelerates to pass him very quickly and disappears in about ten seconds. The witness estimates the total observation duration between 40 and 50 seconds. Only one other testimony corroborates this phenomenon. The visible (apparent) trajectory, according to the witness, is a zone extending from northwest to east (oblique to the vehicle's direction). The witness estimates the angular height of the formation between 15° (base) and 60° (top), and a disappearance directly east. The description resembles a train of Starlink satellites. A Falcon9 launch by SpaceX took place on March 24 at 8 a.m.: launch number 22. Sixty satellites crossed the Côte-d’Or department on March 25, around 5:05 a.m. Several people reported a similar phenomenon. Verification using the Heavens Above software confirms this formation at the date and time mentioned by the witness. The train of satellites appears from the northwest and disappears towards the east. The observation schedule is compatible with the passage of satellites. Due to the low PAN elevation (15°): - the witness thought he saw an "antenna" illuminated by a sequence of white lights, - the witness mentions an acceleration at the end of the observation, which perfectly reflects the disappearance of the lights. The sudden disappearance can be likened to an increase in speed. Precision is required regarding the distance and height of a phenomenon. Without comparison elements with a recognized object, it is impossible to estimate a distance. Here, the witness states a distance of "100 meters" due to the magnitude of the observed phenomenon, or 60 lights. Similarly, the estimated angular size between "40 and 50m" is also consistent with a large phenomenon. Note: an angular size of 40 to 50m at a distance of ~100m (testimony data) corresponds to an angular size of 22 to 28°. However, the angular size of a Starlink train is rather 7° to 8° (an accepted average). This results in an overestimation by a factor of 3 to 4, which is quite common among witnesses. The case does not present any strong oddities. It is also significantly reduced by the proposed explanation. The remaining aspect, always difficult to quantify, is the witness's feeling. The consistency of the file is interesting given the precise description of the object seen by the driver and his son, even though the latter did not testify. The study of the file concludes to a misperception with the launch number 22 of Starlink satellites. GEIPAN classifies it as A: Starlink satellites n° 22.
