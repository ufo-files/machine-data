Summary: Very long observation and from different locations of several lights in the slightly cloudy sky, then a glowing stream sweeping the horizon: probable laser light projections onto the base of the clouds.
--- seg-d1146646c490a70dbbbd361e [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 24, 2015, at 10:30 PM, the witness and her husband were on a beach when they observed 8 lights forming two lines, one with 5 points and the other with 3 points, for 1 hour. They change their observation location to view these lights from a different angle. They seem to come from the clouds, not from the ground. The phenomenon abruptly stops, replaced by an immense green light sweep across the horizon very quickly. After two hours, they stop the observation. Only one testimony is collected. The phenomenon observed by a witness (and his spouse who did not complete a questionnaire) appears above the sea and presents 2 aspects: - it is initially primarily composed of 8 large round bright points (estimated to be roughly the size of a half-moon), green in color, flickering slowly at first, then changing at a faster pace. These lights were never lit at the same time. These 8 lights were arranged in 2 lines: one with 5 points, the other with 3; - the second aspect is an "immense green fluorescent light beam sweeping the horizon at high speed." The phenomenon extended over a dozen kilometers at an altitude of approximately 10 kilometers. The witness specified without noise or trail. The witness reports "it was slightly cloudy" (confirmed by Cap Leucate weather station), "Stars were visible", "The Moon was in the southwest." The PAN is observed from 10:30 PM until around 12:30 AM, with the phenomenon continuing, but the witness went to bed. The strangeness felt by the witness is: "No light was coming from the ground and lighting up in that direction and didn't go that far." We rather had the impression that the light was behind the clouds. The 3 videos made available have an audio track that mainly consists of background noises (smartphone manipulation, wind, sea waves) and some exchanges between the 2 witnesses, but does not allow the phenomena observed to be distinguished. The photos are reconstructions and not actual shots (attached to the questionnaire). The environment is a popular tourist location on the Mediterranean shores, with an unobstructed view of the sea. The witnesses' location at a seaside resort during vacation in August leads to two reflections: 1) the seaside area is frequented during the mentioned time slot from 10:30 PM to midnight, the witness specifying the proximity of a restaurant ("in front of: La Terrasse - Chez Laundry") at dinnertime. The witness is the only one (with his spouse) to report this phenomenon, which he finds strange. He does not mention any discussions with other tourists present at the scene about the strangeness of the phenomenon; 2) at this time of evening, night-time entertainment venues make their presence known by projecting laser light shows into the sky to attract customers. The bright points and green ray of the observation could be festive lasers. The witness specifies, "We were looking at the horizon; it was slightly cloudy," which can explain the reflection of light streams at the base of these clouds, but not from the ground. The strangeness is thus considered rather weak. The consistency is average, with one witness, photos, and videos, but they do not allow for a clear visualization of the observation. The GEIPAN classifies the case as B: probable laser light projections onto the cloud base.
