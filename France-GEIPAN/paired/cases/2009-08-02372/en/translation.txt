Summary: Observation of a white-silver disk-shaped object above a wind turbine.
--- seg-daed8cfaa4fc50e2c86e9806 [needs-review] ---
Description: On August 8, 2009, around 11 PM - 11:30 PM, a driver observed for 2 or 3 seconds an object in the shape of a disk but slightly oval and silver in color above a wind turbine. Her passenger did not have time to see the phenomenon as it disappeared very quickly. This case, submitted late for analysis by the expert college working with the GEIPAN, was subject to a non-conclusive preliminary investigation by the IPN. Note the strong emotional state of the main witness. The analysis shows that the size of the PAN observed at 4° corresponds to the size of a possible reflection of the Moon on the blade of the wind turbine (a reflection mentioned by the main witness). Apart from this plausible hypothesis, the observation has a low degree of strangeness and was only observed by a single witness from inside a car. The second witness did not make a direct observation. The case is classified "C" as an unexplained, weak, and non-strange case.
