--- seg-9e63d6031e57c2266fa5ece9 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the evening of May 19, 2022, a resident of VIEILLEY (25) is at their home. All is calm, save for the usual night sounds; then they hear a powerful, deafening sound, reminiscent of a strong rumble during a storm, but much louder, coming from behind their house. The witness turns their head towards the noise. They see a giant, reddish-orange sphere (the witness compares it to a small planet). The sphere appears to be on fire. However, there are no glowing trails, as is often seen in meteorite images. The sphere is very round. The witness is surprised by its volume, its trajectory, its rapid and constant speed, and its proximity to the Earth. The PAN is heading towards Besançon. The witness observed it for approximately 7 to 10 seconds. The PAN continued its course and disappeared. The sound persisted for 30 seconds. The case consistency is average before study: one witness and no photos or videos of the PAN. The radar map of aircraft and its consistency with the observation make the consistency after study very good. The investigation revealed that the witness observed a Mirage 2000, in post-combustion and at low altitude (see the investigation report). The CNOA (National Center for Air and Space Operations of the French Air and Space Force) confirmed the presence nearby of a military patrol consisting of two Mirage 2000 at the time of the observation. One of these aircraft flew only 2 km southeast of the observation location, with a trajectory entirely consistent with that of the PAN. This hypothesis explains all the oddities mentioned by the witness: large reddish-orange sphere, sphere of fire, powerful and deafening sound, persistence of the noise after the visual loss, very high speed, proximity to the ground. The case is classified as A, observation of a Mirage 2000.
