Description: On July 12, 2020, at 9:22 PM, two witnesses in their garden observed a silent and straight passage of a white object they did not recognize in the sky. The PAN was described as round and white, with an apparent size similar to or slightly larger than a commercial airliner. It moved in a straight line at a speed greater than a commercial airliner, without leaving any condensation trail. The PAN passed behind a cloud. No sound coming from the PAN was heard. The witness filmed the PAN with their smartphone. Only one testimony was collected on this phenomenon. After low strangeness analysis and good consistency (two witnesses, unique and fairly precise testimony, PAN video), the case appears to be a misidentification with a private business jet (Cessna 750 Citation X) (see the investigation report). The PAN trajectory on the video strongly resembles that of an aircraft at high altitude. A reconstruction on Flightradar24 shows that a private aircraft (Cessna 750 Citation X) was present in the sky at the time of the observation, with a trajectory very similar to the PAN. Despite the perceived strangeness, quite legitimately by the witness due to the absence of condensation trail, it is demonstrated that the Cessna, because of the atmospheric conditions it was flying in, could not have left a trail. Furthermore, its apparent speed superior to that of a commercial airliner and its angular height remain very consistent with those of the PAN. The case is classified A: observation of a Cessna 750 Citation X type aircraft.
