Description: On Wednesday, November 21, 2018, around 8:00 PM, a driver while traveling on RN154 observed through his right window a round shape with glowing points of light above a field. The PAN "gently rose for a few seconds" and departed in a direction opposite to that of the driver on a straight trajectory. The driver lost sight of the PAN in his right rearview mirror. The sky was foggy. No other witnesses were found. This case is of average strangeness and consistency (single witness, imprecise location and date, no photo of the PAN). The description of the PAN could suggest two types of misidentifications, with an aircraft or a ground vehicle, due to the color of the flashing lights and the proximity of the PAN to the ground (see the investigation report). Furthermore, the observation took place during the peak of beet harvesting season and was presumably near the Evreux Air Base. However, the time lag between the observation and the report did not allow for a radar trace request from authorities. The case is classified as 'C' due to the lack of reliable information.
