Summary: Observation of the passage of red-orange glowing points in the sky: probable observation of Thai lanterns passing by.
--- seg-4827da3725c2652538e54aa7 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On February 17, 2013, around 7:50 PM, a witness and his wife observed 4 red-orange glowing points aligned above the village of Ham. These move slowly and silently towards the witnesses. Other identical bright points appear during the observation, which lasts about 10 minutes. The GEIPAN contacted the witness by phone to clarify the direction of the phenomenon's movement: it was approaching "13:00" in front of the witness, who was returning to HAM, heading east. The balls crossed the road almost vertically to the witness and were hidden by the factory buildings to the north of the road. The balls were therefore going from E/SE to N/W (the witness gives a different indication in the detail of the testimony). As the wind (recorded in Albert) at that time was southeast, the movement of the objects is consistent with the wind. The red-orange aspect of the balls, their clustering, and the identity of the trajectories are all indications pointing to the passage of Thai lanterns. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": observation of Thai lanterns.
