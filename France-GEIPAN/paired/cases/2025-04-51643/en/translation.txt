Description: On Sunday, April 27, 2025, in the morning, a passenger on a Transavia flight from Marseille to Tunis observed from his window, on the right side of the aircraft, an unusual aerial phenomenon. Around 10:05 a.m., he noticed a dark, oval object resembling an eye. The object seemed to emerge vertically from the cloud layer, as if it were crossing or tearing through it. Once out of the clouds, it remained stationary for a few moments just above, partially visible through the ambient lighting. According to the witness, the object then slowly tilted until it was positioned horizontally, parallel to the cloud layer, before becoming less and less visible, seemingly blending with its surroundings. The passenger was able to take several photos with his smartphone and record a short video. The images show the presence of the phenomenon, but their quality remains poor due to the distance, the plane's motion, and the poor stability of the shot. This case, of low strangeness and good consistency (single testimony, two usable photographs of PAN), corresponds to the observation of a condensation trail emitted by a commercial aircraft, specifically flight ITY672 (see the investigation report for more details). The visual appearance of the unidentified aerial phenomenon (PAN) is entirely compatible with a condensation trail. A reconstruction performed using Flightradar24 data, precisely synchronized with the observation time determined through metadata analysis of the PAN photographs using our image authentication software, shows that the trajectory of flight ITY672 perfectly matches the observed phenomenon. The direction and altitude of this flight, lower than that of flight TVF8800 but high enough (above 10,000 meters), are consistent with passing through the cloud layer, thus forming a condensation trail. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A", observation of a condensation trail. *Consistency: According to the GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable and objective information gathered for a testimony.
