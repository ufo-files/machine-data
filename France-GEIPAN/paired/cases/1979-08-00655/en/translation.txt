Description: On August 28, 1979, at 20:45, a witness observed with binoculars the passage of a commercial airliner. He then noticed a very bright and rectangular shape at the rear of the aircraft and above it. No further information was provided about this phenomenon by the neighboring gendarmerie brigades of Orly Airport or by the head of the regional control center of Orly Sud.
