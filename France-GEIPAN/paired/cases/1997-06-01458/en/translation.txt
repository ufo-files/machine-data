Summary: Observation of two glowing balls departing at high speed
--- seg-ab95a2a94090586be3ec21b0 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 6, 1997, around 11 PM, a witness in their garden observed a stationary bright ball, approximately four times smaller than the moon. After a few minutes, a second ball arrived at very high speed next to the first, and the whole scene moved at high speed towards the North. The witness called their friend who was able to observe the departure of the two balls. The gendarmerie, it seems, received other calls about this phenomenon, which was not explained.
