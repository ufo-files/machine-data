Description: On September 16, 2023, around 5:55 am, the witness, from their home in Quesnoy-le-Montant (80), observed in the sky an intense white light, brighter than the stars surrounding it. He is filming the scene through the window. Upon zooming in, he notes that the shape is circular with a second circle inside that is white or gray, which does not correspond to what he observes with the naked eye. This light oriented full East, moves very slowly. The observation lasts approximately 1.5 hours, with the dawn making the bright point less visible, causing it to appear to move away and gain altitude. The consistency* of this case is good: a detailed testimony, written on the very same day of the observation, comprising numerous videos (18). The witness actually observed the planet Venus. The astronomical analysis, performed using the Stellarium software, indeed shows that the position of Venus corresponds to the witness's indications in terms of date, time, azimuths, and initial elevation, although the witness's final elevation may have been incorrectly estimated. The high brightness of the observed object is explained by the high magnitude of Venus at this time (-4.28 for an average of -4.14). The prolonged observation and the straight-line motion described are typical characteristics of an astronomical observation. The witness's perceived strangeness is attributed to the unusual appearance of the phenomenon in videos taken with a smartphone, as well as the intense brightness. However, these anomalies can be explained by the equipment used. As this is not suitable for observing point light sources at night, it can produce a visible defocusing of a circular, blurred shape identical to that of the camera's diaphragm, often accompanied by concentric circles (diffraction spots). Furthermore, the high magnitude of Venus at the time of observation tends to justify the exceptional brightness. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: observation of the planet Venus. According to the GEIPAN criteria, the consistency of this case is good (consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony).
