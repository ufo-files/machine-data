Description: On December 11, 2023, around 7:10 PM, the witness, in the midst of a conversation on the 7th floor of a building, spotted through the glass bay five bright orbs of different colors moving in the sky. These objects form geometric figures as well as random trajectories. She then takes her phone to film the scene and goes out onto the balcony to continue observing. No noise is heard. The phenomena appear to be located above Saint Cloud Park. The sky is fairly clear and there is little wind. The observation lasted approximately 10 minutes intermittently before the objects disappeared. Of low strangeness and good consistency* (2 witnesses, but a unique testimony, PAN videos), this case turns out to be a drone observation (*according to the GEIPAN criteria, consistency is the amount of reliable information gathered for a testimony). The description and visual aspect of the observed phenomenon are very consistent with those of drones: color variations, apparently coordinated movements of bright points, forming geometric figures, simultaneous lightings and extinctions. An internet search reveals that the day after the observation, a ballet of 1000 drones took place from the Saint-Cloud Park, in the direction of observation of the PAN. Contacted by email, the event organizer, Magic Drone, confirmed, after examining the provided photos, that the observed phenomenon was related to a pre-show test for the aerial display (see Magic Drone's confirmation in the attached document). The case is classified A: drone observation.
