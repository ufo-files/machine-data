--- seg-2cff1f52fd567ca58419e260 [needs-review] ---
Description: On June 1, 2014, at 23:00, a witness observed a silent object crossing the sky at an extremely high speed, appearing as three bright points. The description of the PAN, typical of a meteor, leaves little doubt as to the misidentification. Of average strangeness and consistency (single witness), this case appears to be a probable misidentification with a perfectly known astronomical phenomenon: a meteorite. It is noted that a meteorite was recorded by an amateur network at a time close to the observation, but its trajectory is not consistent. An independent testimony published on the internet, however, presents great similarities with the observation of the PAN. This case is classified B, probable misidentification with the observation of a meteor. There is also, within a few minutes, an observation at ETRELLES (35): see this case.
