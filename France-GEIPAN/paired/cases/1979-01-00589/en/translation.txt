Description: On January 12, 1979, around 7:30 PM, an individual observed a peculiar luminous phenomenon in the sky to the North-North-East. For 20 minutes, the witness, equipped with binoculars, observed a fireball of orange color, about the size of a fist. No noise was heard. The phenomenon disappeared into the clouds. No other testimony about this phenomenon was collected, for which we lack information.
