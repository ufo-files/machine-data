Description: On September 10, 2012, around 8:20 PM, two witnesses observed the slow, very high altitude passage of an orange glowing phenomenon with a long tail. The trajectory was straight. It disappeared towards the South, hidden by a cloud. Only one witness reported his observation succinctly. This distant, odd observation case is very poorly documented and imprecise; the description suggests an airplane contrail visible through low clouds. The orange color is common for contrails located to the West during sunset (at 8:13 PM that day at that location). However, the GEIPAN does not have enough concrete elements to support this hypothesis. This case is classified "C" due to lack of information.
