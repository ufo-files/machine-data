Description: On Saturday, May 23, 2015, between 11:00 PM and 11:15 PM, two individuals observed for approximately 5 minutes the swift and silent passage of a very bright, orange object in the sky. A few minutes later, a second object with the same characteristics as the first was spotted. Only one statement was collected. The observation of orange orbs crossing the sky in a matter of minutes, on a weekend night, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released nearby after a private party. The movement was observed from West to East, which is consistent with the West wind reported in Cognac (see the Météociel weather report). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," a probable observation of Thai lanterns.
