--- seg-45efeb59f6f06e09e7c6e44b [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 25, 2010, around 11:30 PM, a witness observed from their garden the silent passage of 8 orange balls. The characteristics of the observation (several orange balls flying silently in the same direction, seen in successive bursts then disappearing, observed for 15-20 minutes) as well as the date and time (night from Saturday to Sunday around midnight) and the reconstruction/drawings made by the main witness correspond to a known phenomenon of flying lantern flight. The north-east wind (310°) corresponds to the direction of movement described by the witness ('coming from the West and heading towards the East'). The level of strangeness of the observation is low and the likely hypothesis of a flight of Thai-style lanterns is strongly probable. The case is classified B.
