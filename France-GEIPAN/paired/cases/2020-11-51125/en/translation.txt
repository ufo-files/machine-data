Summary: Multiple nighttime observations of "craft" in the sky: astronomical misperception.
--- seg-25fdab8083150587883bffd1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: Between 11/11/2020 and 20/11/2020, two witnesses observed in the sky moving luminous phenomena that intrigued them. The observations were very long (sometimes several hours) and discontinuous. One of the witnesses filmed the PAN. The astronomical context analysis revealed that the positions of Sirius, Jupiter, Saturn, and Mars coincide precisely with the positions of the PAN described and filmed by the witness (see the investigation report). This hypothesis also explains the slow movement of several hours, due to the Earth's rotation. The shape of the PAN observed with binoculars is, in turn, explicable by the exceptional phenomenon of conjunction between Jupiter and Saturn. The consistency of the case is very good. The recurring observation of this case by two witnesses, including the investigator, reinforces its consistency. The main hypothesis of a confusion with stars explains most of the characteristics of this PAN. The position of the PAN coincides precisely with the positions of Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, and Sirius. Furthermore, the planets Jupiter and Saturn were in conjunction, which may have increased the confusion. The slow movements are explained by the Earth's rotation, and the brief movements can be the consequence of optical effects related to autokinesis. Therefore, this hypothesis is considered explicative, with the ephemeris serving as proof. The GEIPAN classifies the case as "A" (Phenomenon perfectly explained): Astronomical misapprehension.
