Description: On the night of March 21 to 22, 2025, the witness is driving their car when they see, around 2 a.m., a light in the sky that they describe as "a small moving bank of fog." He quickly interrupts his observation to remain focused on his driving. A few minutes later, upon arriving at his home in Beaumont-sur-Sarthe (72), he again spots the phenomenon and attempts to film it. In the video, an object appears in the visual field around the eleventh second of the recording. The consistency of this case is assessed as average: the investigation relies on the testimony of a single person and on exploitable video, but no data could be collected regarding the first light phenomenon observed with the naked eye. According to the GEIPAN criteria, consistency corresponds to the amount of reliable and verifiable information gathered within a testimony. Indeed, the information provided by the witness in the questionnaire GEIPAN only pertains to the phenomenon captured on the video, without a detailed description of the initial phenomenon observed with the naked eye. The absence of additional information makes its identification impossible. The analysis was therefore limited to the visible phenomenon in the video sequence. Regarding the luminous phenomenon visible in the video, appearing around the eleventh second, the analysis conducted using the IPACO image authentication and processing software concludes that it is a parasitic reflection generated by the intense light of the streetlamp present in the camera's field of view. The apparent movement of the phenomenon is caused by the movement of the witness's mobile phone during the recording. (See Annex 1 - Photographic Analysis.) The witness, intrigued by a first visual observation made from his vehicle, naturally associates this experience with the subsequent observation, recorded a few moments later on video, despite the marked differences between the two phenomena ("bank of fog" for the first observation and "drone-like appearance" for the second). The GEIPAN classifies this case as A for the video: parasitic reflection in the camera lens.
