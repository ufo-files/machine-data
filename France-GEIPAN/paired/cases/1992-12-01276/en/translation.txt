--- seg-fe2ffd0780e9c95284fdd5be [needs-review] ---
Description: On Wednesday, December 9, 1992, around 9 PM, an adjoint gendarme to the brigade chief, along with his wife, observed a large-scale luminous phenomenon featuring 5 or 6 illuminated and aligned rectangles, as well as two particularly intense blue flashes at each end of the rectangle row. The observation lasted only a few seconds. The driver braked, and the vehicle following behind also slowed down, but the second vehicle could not be located. The phenomenon could not be explained.
