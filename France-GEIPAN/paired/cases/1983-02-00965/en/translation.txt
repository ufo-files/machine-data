Description: On February 6, 1983, around 1 a.m., a witness on his moped encountered cars and reported being abruptly stopped by a very bright beam of light, after which he lost consciousness. Upon waking about an hour later, he saw a very bright disk above him receding while making noise. The witness's character, likely under the influence of alcohol at the time of the incident, does not allow this testimony to be taken seriously.
