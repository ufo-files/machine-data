Description: Following an article in the newspaper 'l'Indépendant' on December 20, 1987, the Gendarmerie interviews a witness who claims to have encountered on December 12, 1987, seeking shelter from the rain in the ruins of a house, a group of 6 strange individuals with whom he exchanged a few words. These individuals reported coming from another planet, and one of them drew two symbols on the ground, which he interpreted for the witness. They then boarded vehicles without wheels resembling sleds. The witness heard a noise resembling ultrasound as the vehicles departed. The witness returned to the site with his daughter two hours after his observation and found round tracks on the ground where the figures' feet had been. During its investigation, the Gendarmerie found no ground traces and no other testimonies were collected. This very strange but unsubstantial case (single witness, absence of ground traces despite allegations) contains several details that cast doubt on the testimony: the aliens' planet of origin ("Earth") is merely a simple English translation of the word "Terre", the characters speaking approximate French, inconsistency between the description of resting athletes and their estimated height of 1m or 1.5m... In the current classification of GEIPAN, this case is classified as C due to lack of reliable information.
