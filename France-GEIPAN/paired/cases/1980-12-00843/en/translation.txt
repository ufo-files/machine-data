Description: The GEIPAN continues to publish its entire archive on its public website www.geipan.fr. Among its publications are old cases previously classified as Type "C" cases that are now being re-examined with the sole purpose of reaching more relevant conclusions. Thanks to new technical means (software) and the investigative experience gained over the years, this re-examination sometimes leads to new observations or even an explicit change in classification in an investigation note. This observation case was previously classified as "C". This case is quite consistent (three witnesses, only two of whom were interviewed), and the description of the PAN observed is quite precise. The sincerity and credibility of the witnesses have never been questioned. The phenomenon described shares many common characteristics (observation duration, shape, size, color, trajectory) with an artificial atmospheric re-entry. Furthermore, this observation case fits within a mass observation of an artificial atmospheric re-entry (namely that of the Cosmos 749 rocket), occurring during the indicated time slot and on the trajectory. It is not the visual perception of the witnesses that is in question, but the interpretation they make of their observation through their feelings (surprise). In the current classification of the GEIPAN, this weak strangeness case is classified as PAN A, an observation related to a misperception with the atmospheric re-entry of the Cosmos 749 rocket.
