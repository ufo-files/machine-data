Description: On January 4, 2010, around 7:45 PM, a witness on a walk was intrigued by the east-west movement of a large, dark triangular shape with dim, yellow-orange circles at each end. The speed was constant, and no unusual noises were heard during the 10-15 seconds of observation. No other witness observations were collected. The investigation confirmed and clarified the witness's testimony, lending it credibility. This strange observation, not resembling typical misidentifications, is also not consistent with the passage of a commercial airliner, especially since the witness is very familiar with aircraft observations. The testimony could be compatible with a small drone or model aircraft, 2.5 meters in size, flying at 150 meters altitude at approximately 35-150 km/h below the radar cone of the air traffic control (CDG); however, it seems highly unlikely that this type of craft would fly in an urban area at night on a winter evening. Although this hypothesis aligns with the observation, it was not pursued due to lack of other compelling evidence. This strange observation was reported by only one witness, without photos or videos; despite the quality of the testimony, this case remains of average consistency. The GEIPAN classifies this strange and average case as D1, an unexplained phenomenon.
