Description: On June 29, 2014, starting at 9:30 PM, a witness is intrigued by the straight-line passage across the sky of a white object they cannot identify: the witness sees no condensation trail, no wings, no lights. However, throughout the observation period, they hear a dull noise. The witness takes a photo with their phone. It is not uncommon for planes to produce no condensation trail (this depends on local atmospheric conditions: air humidity and temperature). At sunset, a high-altitude plane is illuminated from the side: the unlit underside of the wings is darker than the blue, bright background of the sky. Only the illuminated fuselage is visible from the ground. From the ground, it appears in the shape of a cigar. Additionally, the flashing (anti-collision) lights of a high-altitude plane are never visible during the day from the ground. The Flightradar record at that time indicates a flight from north to south (Airbus A320 Paris-Toulouse AF6168) which corresponds to the observation. This case is classified "A": observation of a commercial airliner at sunset.
