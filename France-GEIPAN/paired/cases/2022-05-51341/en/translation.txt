Description: On May 11, 2022, around 5 a.m., the witness, outside their home in Vernajoul (09), sees "something" heading towards the Plantauren mountain. It consists of a dozen white glowing points of equal intensity as satellites, tilted between 0° and 45° approximately, moving simultaneously from South to North, then progressively, one by one, and disappearing one after the other. The observation lasted between 30 seconds and 1 minute. No other testimonies were collected. The witness observed a train of Starlink satellites, very likely those from the Falcon-9 launch on March 19, 2022 (see the visibility path map). The GEIPAN classifies this case as an observation of a Starlink satellite train.
