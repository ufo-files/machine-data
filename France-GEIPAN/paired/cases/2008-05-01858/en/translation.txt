--- seg-8c1218978b50c8d55ba5520b [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On May 11, 2008, around 10:50 PM, two witnesses observed the high-speed passage in the sky of a dark, triangular mass with fixed and flashing white, green, and red lights. During the observation, which lasted several dozen seconds, a faint, deep noise was heard. This previous observation, in addition to the main testimony, led to several additional testimonies after the facts were published in the press. Submitted for analysis to the panel of experts working with the GEIPAN, this case, with a low level of strangeness, is classified as B, indicating a probable observation of an airplane, an hypothesis suggested by one of the spontaneous testimonies. This case is a typical example of inversion/confusion in describing the lights of an airplane (misunderstanding between flashing and fixed white, green, and red lights... ).
