Description: On July 15, 2015, at 11:45 PM, a witness observing the stars noticed the passage of a satellite, then was surprised to see a flying object in the shape of a triangle composed of three weak, orange circles at each end. The phenomenon was fleeting and disappeared very quickly to the northwest in the clear sky. The observation lasted 5 seconds. Only one testimony was collected. This testimony is of average consistency: it is imprecise and comes from a single witness without a photo. The observation is not particularly strange because the behavior and appearance of the PAN are ultimately mundane. Considering the whole and pending clarifications from the witness regarding the angular measurements of the PAN, its movement, and certain observation conditions, this case is provisionally classified as "C": observation of an unidentified phenomenon in its current state, the significant lack of elements preventing a solid opinion.
