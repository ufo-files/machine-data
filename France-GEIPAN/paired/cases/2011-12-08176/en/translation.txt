Description: A witness reports their observations made on December 10, 2011, around 20:30 from their home (see the investigation report). This phenomenon is ultimately not strange given the speed and fairly common movement of these light points. The hypothesis of foreign military aircraft overflying the area without contact and without radar response remains the most plausible, but without the GEIPAN being able to support it due to a lack of radar information. This case remains classified as "C" for lack of evidence.
