Description: On Friday, December 3, 2010, at 7:17 PM, a driver and their passenger observed a group of white, purple-reflected glowing points in the sky. The phenomenon gradually disappeared. The First-Level Investigator conducting a supplementary investigation confirmed, through the municipality of Riedisheim, that on that evening, a humanitarian association had organized a release of "light balloons." "Rods" (pyrotechnic devices, similar to Blinklanzen from Switzerland) were attached to balloons filled with helium. No photos were taken, but the confirmation from the municipality and the provided details leave no doubt about the event. The case is therefore perfectly identified.
