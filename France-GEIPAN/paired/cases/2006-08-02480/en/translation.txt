Description: Recent testimony (04/06/2008) of an old observation. In August 2006 (possibly on the 12th) during "Star Night" around 11:30 PM, a witness observed a luminous phenomenon from the Saint-Jean-de-Mont pier. Two low-intensity orbs moved above the ocean at a low altitude. The observation lasted 30 seconds before the brightness of these orbs intensified and they disappeared in a fraction of a second. No other testimony regarding this phenomenon was collected. This observation resembles that of flying lanterns, but we lack precision and information.
