Description: On October 9, 2012, at 23:32, two people observed in the starry black sky the rapid movement of a white glowing point. Only one report was made. The observation of a white point crossing the sky in a few seconds from East to West suggests a bolide (large shooting star), meaning the atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid (meteorite). The absence of a trail is not ordinary. This event was not recorded on the BOAM (meteor observer database). The provided information is not sufficient to validate the bolide hypothesis, which remains, however, plausible (Delta Aurigids meteor shower peak on October 10). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to lack of information. On the same day, 20 minutes earlier, a similar observation was reported in Limoges.
