Description: On May 8, 1989, around 10:30 PM, a man observed a very bright fireball quickly crossing the atmosphere. This observation is confirmed by a gendarme who was driving several kilometers away at the same time and observed the same phenomenon. This is likely an observation of a re-entry into the atmosphere.
