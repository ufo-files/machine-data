Description: A witness reports that in 1991, around mid-August, at approximately 10:30 PM, she observed a group of white glowing points moving chaotically in the sky. These points, very high in the sky, suddenly formed a ballet, organizing into various geometric shapes. This lasted about 5 minutes before the entire group consolidated into a point and disappeared, leaving a brief phosphorescent trail. This person was likely a witness to the short-lived operation of a nightclub laser projector, which typically forms such patterns in the sky. The witness also mentions that a nightclub in a nearby village has this type of equipment.
