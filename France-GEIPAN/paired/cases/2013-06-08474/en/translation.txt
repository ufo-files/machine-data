Description: On June 16, 2013, around 1:30 PM, a witness notices while putting on their sunglasses, they see blue and pink lights in the sky, which they immediately photograph. No other witness statements were collected regarding this phenomenon. The witness observes iridescent reflections in the sky in Paris, but only when viewing through their sunglasses; a photo also reveals the phenomenon. The appearance of the phenomenon resembles iridescent clouds, but according to a Météo-France expert, this type of phenomenon only occurs around very high clouds (Cirrus), which is not the case here (high stratocumulus or altocumulus). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": an optical phenomenon likely caused by the reflection of sunlight off the sunglasses and the camera lens.
