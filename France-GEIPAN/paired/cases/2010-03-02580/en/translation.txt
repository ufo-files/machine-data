Description: On March 27, 2010, at 10:30 PM, two individuals observed from their home the passage of around twenty glowing orbs that grouped together and followed a west-east trajectory. No noise was heard by the witnesses. The observation characteristics (around twenty yellow-orange glowing orbs moving along the same trajectory for several minutes…) and the photographs taken by a witness correspond to the (now well-known) observation of a flight of Thai-style luminous lanterns. The date (Saturday evening) is also common for this type of observation (party, wedding, end of weekend…). The case is classified as B.
