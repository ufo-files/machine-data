Description: On March 13, 2014, around 10:15 PM, a witness, while closing their shutters, observed the very quick passage in a clear sky of a PAN oval-shaped green object "resembling a turnip with a red circle all around it." During the brief observation, no noise was heard. That evening, numerous witnesses in France, as well as in Switzerland, observed the quick passage of a luminous and colored phenomenon in the night sky. A meteor was indeed reported in a large southeastern quarter of France and recorded at 21:59:47 (see the definition of a meteor and the Webastro website. The GEIPAN classifies the case as A: very probable observation of a meteor.
