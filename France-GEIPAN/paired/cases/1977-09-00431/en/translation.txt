Description: Following a press article reporting the observation by three witnesses of a "OVNI", an investigation is opened. The gendarmerie locates a witness who claims that on September 13, 1977, at approximately 9:30 PM, she observed the north-south movement of a reddish glow. No noise was heard during the observation. No trail was observed on the flight path. The second witness remains untraceable. A third witness will be interviewed but will state that on September 7, 1977, at around 7 AM in the morning, she observed a white, luminous oval object in the clouds. No other testimony regarding the September 13 phenomenon will be collected.
