Description: On Tuesday, March 18, 2014, around 8:30 PM, a couple of walkers were surprised to observe in the clear sky four constant, pale orange lights forming a dark trapezoidal shape. These lights moved silently at a constant speed on a straight trajectory. The witness turned away but the object was gone when he looked back. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of orange lights flying silently, horizontally, in the same direction as the wind, strongly suggests Thai lanterns, likely released after a festival. It is common to perceive a dark shape when observing multiple lights moving together (Gestalt effect). The witness reported seeing the object depart towards the East or Northeast: the wind at Lyon-Satolas is a light southwest wind, consistent with the observed movement: weather. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of Thai lanterns. The GEIPAN did not investigate the release author.
