Description: On July 21, 2009, two individuals witnessed a silent west-to-east movement of a bright white light in the sky that faded away at the horizon. One of the witnesses took photographs and contacted GEIPAN to report his observation by phone. He then sent his direct testimony along with the attached photos. This case is classified as "A": The witness observed the passage of the International Space Station (ISS).
