Description: On September 27, 1954, at 8:30 PM, three children (aged 12, 9, and 8) observed near their home a strange aluminum-colored, rectangular craft that was partially split down its middle vertically. The craft had two angled supports on each side at its base. It was approximately two meters tall and one meter wide. One of the children threw a stone, then an arrow from a toy bow and arrow at the craft, which slowly advanced and knocked the child over. Frightened, the child returned home and went to bed. Two sisters also saw the craft and, frightened, hid. No witness saw the craft disappear. The two sisters reported seeing a red light swaying near the ground near their home. The parents did not believe the children's accounts. Their teacher reported the observation to the gendarmerie. During the gendarmerie investigation at the time, two reports were filed. The captain did not question the credibility of the children. These children lived on an isolated farm, did not read newspapers, and did not go to the cinema. Soil tracks were found in the direction of the appearance of the red light: it was a large crown in the laid-down grass oriented in a clockwise direction. These tracks were not recognized as being from human or animal trampling. Four holes were also found in this crown. The GEIPAN was aware of the private ufologist Yves Bosson's investigation, which met with the witnesses in the 1980s and confirmed that they had pulled a prank whose consequences they had not anticipated. His report was presented at the Lyon Congress in 1992 ("Actes des sixièmes Rencontres européennes de Lyon, 1-2-3 mai 1992", SOS OVNI, Aix-en-Provence 1992, pp. 4-16) and later published in the chapter "Prémanon ou l'innocence: enquête sur un cas au-dessus de tout soupçon" in the book "OVNI, Vers une Anthropologie d'un Mythe Contemporain" by Thierry Pinvidic et al., Heimdal éditeur, France, pp 123-145, 1993. Pending verification of these statements, which appear reliable a priori, the GEIPAN classifies this case as 'C': lack of confirmed information.
