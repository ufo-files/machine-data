Description: On Sunday, July 27, 2014, around 5:30 PM, a witness observed a commercial airliner, followed by a smaller, black disk moving in the opposite direction of the plane and at a slower pace. The disk became oval and flattened out completely as it moved away. Only one testimony was collected. The observation of a black object crossing the sky in a straight line in just a few minutes initially suggested a wind-driven balloon. The Meteociel report indicates a west to southwest wind consistent with the eastward movement reported by the witness. What the witness saw must have been a motorized object: possibly a small circular drone (such as a quadcopter) at an altitude of less than 100 meters, operated by someone at the leisure base, or a helicopter at a relatively high altitude, or even a plane as large as the one observed by the witness, but four times farther away. Verifying either of these hypotheses, though compatible with the observation, is nearly impossible. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C": lack of information or cross-corroboration. Note: The witness erred in his date in his account: he writes "Sunday, July 28," while Sunday is July 27. The weather for July 27 corresponds exactly to his description (thunderstorm in the evening). After verification with the witness, the observation did indeed take place on Sunday, July 27.
