Summary: Observations of the movement of flying objects with a dome and flashing colored lights: lack of information.
--- seg-507d42daa348f7d49a1dab42 [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 1, 1979, the gendarmerie learned from the press that residents of the same locality had observed on November 11, 1979, around 6:45 PM, "craft" or "objects" moving above Mareau-Aux-Bois (45). These objects were described as having a round shape, with a dome on top, and blinking lights of various colors. Testimonies were brief but numerous (8 witnesses). The gendarmerie found a 9th witness who reported an observation made on November 25, 1979, at 9:30 PM, above Courcy-Aux-Loges (45). Due to the time gap between the events and their disclosure, the gendarmerie was unable to make any findings. No other information was gathered on this phenomenon, for which we lack data.
