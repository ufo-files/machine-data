Description: On May 17, 1993, around 12:15, a witness in their garden observed a bright white disk in the distance, whose speed could be estimated at 3 or 4 times that of a commercial airliner. No noise was heard; the witness followed the phenomenon with binoculars for approximately 2 minutes. No other information could be gathered about this observation.
