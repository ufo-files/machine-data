Description: On August 22, 2017 at 4:30 AM, a witness observed from the first floor of their residence a fixed luminous phenomenon in the sky facing East. The witness takes photos and videos (tablet and camcorder) during the half-hour observation. Only one testimony is collected. Thanks to a video taken by the witness outside at dawn showing landscape features, we can deduce that the PAN was located at approximately azimuth 84° at 6:08 AM on 08/22/2017; a comparison with Stellarium at the same time shows the presence of Venus at azimuth 84° and an apparent magnitude of -4. Venus is the third brightest natural object in the sky (after the sun and the moon). Enlarging an image of a planet or star is impossible with this type of equipment due to defocusing, that is, the focus cannot be achieved on an object that far away. Filming a bright point with a zoom lens causes optical and electronic artifacts. The observed shape that varies with movements is due to the diaphragm; a defect in it could be the cause of the shape seen in all zoomed videos (a circle with a point downward, see investigation elements). The colors are chromatic aberrations also related to defocusing. (See case on site www.geipan.fr Annemasse (74) 04/08/2012). Additionally, the witness describes the PAN as a large star "without change" in trajectory and "not much movement." We are in a case where the strangeness perceived by the witness results mainly from what he sees in the craft. He only saw it with his eyes, he might not have given a testimony. The UFO protected GEIPAN classifies this case as A for a certain observation of Venus.
