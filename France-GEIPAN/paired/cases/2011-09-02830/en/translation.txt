Summary: Observation of the horizontal movement of a green fluorescent and red light phenomenon: probable observation of a meteor's passage.
--- seg-3bb0188301e532f5aa4cd879 [needs-review] ---
Description: On September 24, 2011, at 3:35 a.m., a Parisian taxi driver observed from his vehicle the silent and horizontal movement of a green fluorescent cone closed by a red line. The object crossed his field of vision and disappeared quickly. No other witnesses were reported on this phenomenon. Of good consistency and low to medium strangeness, this observation case has the characteristics of a bolide (entry into the atmosphere of a meteoroid [meteorite]): a green luminous phenomenon with a trail, moving linearly in a few seconds. Since the phenomenon is likely recorded by only one of the BOAM (meteor observer network) cameras, while at least two should have recorded it, a firm and definitive conclusion is not strictly possible. This case is classified B: probable observation of a bolide (entry into the atmosphere of a meteoroid).
