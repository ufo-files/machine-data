--- seg-9c11e3e5c943b04fe92b68d3 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 13, 2013, four pilots (see the estimated positions of the 4 aircraft on the aeronautical questionnaire) observed a powerful light phenomenon in the east of France, a phenomenon also observed on the ground (see press article), and by the Chaligny camera (near Nancy) of the BOAM network. This phenomenon, called a bolide, was characterized as an atmospheric reentry of a meteoroid (meteorite fall). The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A".
