Description: On August 25, 2009, around 11 PM, two walkers observed two stationary red-orange orbs just above the trees. During the 5-minute observation, no noise was heard. A few minutes later, one of the witnesses, returning home, noticed the orbs had disappeared. The characteristics of the two stationary glowing orbs, red-orange in color, describe a probable observation of Thai lanterns. Another local press report mentions a similar observation in Arzon on the same evening: the given trajectories and descriptions in both cases confirm the similarity between the different observations, likely of flying lanterns.
