Description: The GEIPAN is contacted by a witness regarding a singular object observed in the sky of Thônes (74) during the Saint-Maurice agricultural fair on September 26, 2009. The object appears to be spinning on itself. The witness will attach to his email two photographs of the object, but will not complete a terrestrial questionnaire, nor respond to requests for details. He does not specify if other witnesses saw the phenomenon. This case is strange only by the shape of the object: its movement seems to be that of a balloon, therefore an "airplane-like" object. Unfortunately, the testimony is too inconsistent to allow for a solid conclusion, unless additional details about the observation are obtained. This case is classified C due to lack of information.
