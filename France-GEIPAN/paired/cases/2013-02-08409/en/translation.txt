Summary: Recurrent observations of a bright point moving across the sky: observations of satellite passes.
--- seg-cac0f2b93a2788e62cafca9a [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: A witness observes, at a fifteen-day interval and the same time, the same phenomenon in the night sky: a bright light of high intensity passes overhead and suddenly disappears. The Calsky satellite visibility report for Brest on that evening shows that the object "Cosmos 2297 Rocket" (23405 1994-077-B launched from Baikonur on November 24, 1994) enters the Earth's shadow at 8:04 PM, the exact time of the observation (and disappearance) of the observed phenomenon. Every evening at this time of year, numerous satellites, varying in brightness, are well visible (see Calsky report). However, weather conditions are not always favorable for observation. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "A": observation of the third stage of the Zenit-2 launcher of the Cosmos 2297 satellite, which is still in orbit.
