Summary: Observation of light shape movements through clouds.
--- seg-526b1825ca9548ac9c6a473c [needs-review] ---
Description: On December 11, 2007, around 8:40 PM, two witnesses observed lights in the Toulouse sky, which was overcast with clouds. One person testified to observing for 25 minutes the rapid movements of slightly oval, white, and opaque shapes that created various geometric figures through the clouds. He filmed his observation for a few minutes using his mobile phone. No particular noises were heard during the observation. The gendarmes viewed the video taken by this witness and indeed distinguish some movements (see report). Based on the descriptions given by the witness and after excluding an astronomical misperception, it is likely that these people observed a light show (significant duration of the phenomenon). This type of mobile tasks on a cloudless ceiling is frequently a source of misperception, and under certain weather conditions, the beams may not be visible. Therefore, it is difficult to easily pinpoint the position of the source, which may be close to the observation sector or much farther away depending on the range of the projectors.
