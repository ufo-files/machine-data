Description: On March 15, 2010, between 9:30 PM and 10:45 PM, a witness observed the passage in the sky of about a dozen glowing balls. The characteristics of the formation described by the witness (orange-colored glowing balls moving in a wave in a uniform manner for several minutes before disappearing) describe a known phenomenon with no level of strangeness. The case is classified as PAN B, likely flying lanterns.
