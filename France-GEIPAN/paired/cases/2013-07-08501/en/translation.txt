--- seg-6aef1eec6e3aeeec6110b5aa [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On the night of July 24, 2013, around midnight, several people observed for a few seconds the silent movement of a bright phenomenon in the sky. The observation of a very bright, long trail for a few seconds clearly suggests a bolide (the entry into Earth's atmosphere of a meteoroid, which becomes a meteorite if it reaches the ground). The GEIPAN and other organizations (IMCCE, Ciel des hommes, BOAM) received numerous similar reports the night before, that night, and the following ones, indicating that Earth passes through a relatively dense region of space filled with small bodies (meteorite swarms) at this time of year. However, this specific event was not fully characterized. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B," indicating a probable observation of a bolide.
