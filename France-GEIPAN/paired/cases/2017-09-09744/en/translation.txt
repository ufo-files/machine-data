Description: While fishing at night on the Barcarès beach, at 21:13, a witness observes the straight-line movement of a very bright orange light ball that quickly disappears into the clouds. Only one testimony is collected. The witness recovers two photos from a tourist. The witness very likely observed a Thai lantern. The appearance is consistent in terms of shape, the type of glow which can be non-uniform, and also the size which is never too large (the witness says "weaker than the Moon" in a mistake regarding the measurement which cannot be 3 cm at arm's length). Furthermore, the wind measured that evening in Perpignan is perfectly in line with the trajectory faithfully reconstructed by the witness (see Infoclimat). It is early September, in a resort area, the location and date are conducive to festive events such as lantern releases. Consequently, the GEIPAN classifies the case as B: Thai lantern.
