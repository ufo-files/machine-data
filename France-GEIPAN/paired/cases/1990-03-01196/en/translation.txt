Description: On March 22, 1990, three witnesses separately and repeatedly observed an unusual light phenomenon. One witness reported being flown over by an orange disk that made a strange whooshing sound. Frightened, he fell off his motorcycle. Another witness saw a similar craft and strange lights behind the hills twice. The investigation was unable to provide an explanation for the phenomenon.
