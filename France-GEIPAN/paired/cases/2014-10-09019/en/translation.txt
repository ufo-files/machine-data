Description: On the night of October 15, 2014, two separate witnesses (each in a different vehicle) observed stationary, non-flashing red and green lights surrounding a dark, round object about 100 meters above ground, estimated to be between 7 and 10 meters in size, moving in a straight line. The first witness made the observation around 10:35 p.m. local time near the village of Vescheim (57), while the second witness observed the same phenomenon around 11:45 p.m. local time between the villages of Vescheim (57) and Vilsberg (57). In both cases, the observation lasted about a minute. Initially, the investigation was unable to identify a plausible hypothesis. The strangeness of the phenomenon was high, although consistency was strong (detailed description by two witnesses). For these reasons, the case was classified in category D (see the 2016 investigation report). Unexplained cases have significant potential for advancing scientific knowledge, provided their unexplained nature is confirmed over time. Thanks to new technical means (software) and experience gained over the past years, a re-examination can sometimes lead to new observations or even a change in classification. A case of observation in MOSELLE (57) on October 15, 2014, previously classified in category D, was recently re-examined. New information gathered in 2020 allows for reconsideration of one of the previously evaluated hypotheses, without challenging the consistency of the case (see the additional investigation). On October 22, 2020, at 7:11 p.m. local time, one of the investigators happened to observe the passage of an aircraft "enveloped in a green halo." Internet investigations, followed by inquiries at the National Center for Aerial Operations (CNOA), confirmed that it was a NH90 combat helicopter. This type of helicopter can be equipped with green LED lights on its rotors. It now seems much more likely that our two witnesses in VESCHEIM (57) and VILSBERG (57) simply observed the passage of a military helicopter performing low-altitude maneuvers as part of a night mission. Based on the description, it would be a NH90 combat helicopter, based at the 1st HRC military camp in Phalsbourg, located nearby. This camp recently received four of these aircraft, some of which are equipped with green LED lights on their rotors. Due to the consistency of the observations and the possible presence of this type of aircraft in the area at that time, this hypothesis has a high level of probability. The case maintains strong consistency, although it decreases from 0.8 to 0.7 following subsequent re-evaluation of the scales. The GEIPAN classifies this case as B: probable observation of a NH90 combat helicopter.
