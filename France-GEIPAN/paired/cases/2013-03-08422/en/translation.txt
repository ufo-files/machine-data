Description: On March 9, 2013, around 8:20 PM, a witness observed the silent and linear passage of approximately fifteen white glowing points across the sky. They disappeared, hidden by the landscape. The behavior of this series of luminous flying objects is perfectly consistent with the local wind, from the south-west to the west, which is variable (see the Meteociel report. Nearby station reports from St Jean de Vedas and Aniane provide slightly different directions, but still with light winds). The white color mentioned by the witness suggests more glowing balloons than Thai lanterns. Glowing balloons are helium-filled balloons containing a small lamp powered by a very small battery. The GEIPAN did not investigate to find the source of these balloons or lanterns; it appears that the large residential area located to the southwest of the observer is the origin. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "B": probable observation of glowing balloons or flying lanterns.
