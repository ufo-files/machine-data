Description: On April 12, 2017 at 2:00 PM, a witness was intrigued by the slow movement of a very bright round shape in the clear sky. He filmed and took a picture of the phenomenon. Only one testimony was collected. As the witness suggests, his observation could well correspond to the passage of a weather balloon. Indeed, after verification with the Brest-Guipavas Weather Center contacted on June 27, 2017, balloon releases take place twice a day at 11:00 AM and 11:15 PM local time. It is quite probable that a weather balloon released in Brest-Guipavas at 11:00 AM local time would end up in Saint-Brieuc at 1:50 PM local time. Balloons can fly at an altitude of 20km and are not affected by ground wind. The GEIPAN GEIPAN classifies this case as B, likely the observation of a Météo-France weather balloon released from Brest-Guipavas.
