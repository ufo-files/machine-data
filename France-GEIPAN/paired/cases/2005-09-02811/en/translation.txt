Summary: Observation of the movement of a boomerang-shaped object.
--- seg-45e2a7469b98ac3054b1c25d [machine-unreviewed] ---
Description: On October 4, 2005, the GEIPAN was alerted by a gendarme at Blagnac Airport who observed a curious phenomenon moving at the end of a runway. The witness describes an object shaped like a boomerang and thinks it might be a remotely controlled object, but it disappears at a high speed, which surprises the witness. No other witnesses were found regarding this phenomenon. The report's reading leaves a strong impression of credibility: the observation is carefully described in precise and objective terms; the witness's professionalism is recognized. The events reported have an average strangeness: although this device may not have had any business here a priori, its performance remains compatible with those of a remotely controlled device (albeit high-performance). The pendular motion observed at the beginning of the observation could have been due to wind, while the rapid motion was in the direction of the wind. The description resembles certain models of drones, which were very rare at that time. However, as the testimony is old, it is not possible to verify this hypothesis. The case is classified as "C" with the "drone" hypothesis not sufficiently confirmed.
