Description: On Monday, July 22, 2013, around 6:15 PM, a witness observed the movement of a bright, streaking object in the sky, without sound and at very high speed. This person described a silver, rounded object resembling a "bucket." The approximate trajectory given is southeast to northwest across an area where planes fly from Montpellier-Fréjorgues Airport. This very brief (2 to 3 seconds) observation of a bright object in the sky by a single witness is poorly documented (no diagram). The oddness of this observation lies in the object's speed of movement. If we consider that the object covered a field of view of 60° in 3 seconds, and it was 200 meters away, this corresponds to a speed already in the order of 270 km/h. It could be a small object carried by the wind at 15 or 20 meters, but this would then be a strong illusion regarding the witness's estimate of the object's distance. The weather report indicates a south to south-west wind of about 18 km/h, weakening, which could strictly fit with this last hypothesis. A more in-depth investigation would yield little additional information. The GEIPAN classifies this case as "C" due to the lack of precise information and lack of cross-references.
