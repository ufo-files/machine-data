I was 18 years old when I went into the air force in 1964, after basic
and tech school I was stationed at Malmstrom AFB in the ADC S.A.G.E. Center.
In the winter of 65 or the spring of 64 I saw a TTY msg about a UFO incident
in Montana and they wanted our UFO investigation officer (I did not even
know that we had one) to go out and talk to the people and get a report, and
then tell them that ufo's were not real.

   That's it, nothing great but hope it will help. I have never seen a UFO but
would like to some day.

GW 8/29/95
