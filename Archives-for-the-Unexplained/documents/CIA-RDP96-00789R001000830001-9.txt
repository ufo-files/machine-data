E.     SESSION SUMMARY:

     The target is a woman with darker hair and younger than middle
age. She is somehow associated with the sense of motion, of being
suspended above a pointed object and of flying. She is associated
with water, but the feeling is more with rain or mist rather than
a body of water.    There are clouds and high altitude associated
with her. An object is silvery and smooth and feels "futuristic".
It has triangular points on it. Again, a sense of flying.

     The woman is sitting, staring at a 3-sided panel. This panel
illuminates her face. There is again the sense of suspension; of
isolation; of being alone.   There are curved features associated
with her head.

     There is a rhythmic sound; a throbbing, a spinning motion and
a sense of continual adjustment.      The time feels ca. 1930's.
Everything in the scene is in shades of gray; as if I were looking
at a B/W photograph; an old photograph.         There are people
whispering in the background. They are voicing doubt and anxiety
about the woman. The woman seems "doomed to failure".
