          EXTRA-SENSORY PERCEPTION IN SPACE.

     Captain Edgar D. Mitchell startled the world with the
first E.S.P. experiment in outer spac e during the flight
of Apollo 14 to the moon.    The Apollo 14 flight, which
lasted from January 31st to February 9, 1971, set
several space programme rec ords, and yet only a few people
on earth knew that he was in the middle of an historic
moon-earth E.S.P. experiment.    Four men (three of whom
are still anonymous) were attempting to use E.S.P. to
traverse the hundreds of thousands of miles separating
them from astronaut Mitchell as he concentrated on E.S.P.
targets.
     During the 20 0 odd hours of the Apollo 14 flight,
Captain Mitchell was able to find time for four E.S.P.
transmission sessions - two on the way to the moon, and
two on the way back.    His targets were recorded on a
sheet of paper which shows evidence of salt water from the
splashdown in the Pacific .
     Collaborating in analyzing the results were Dr. J.B.
Rhine of the Foundation for Researc h on the Nature of Man
in Durham, U.S. A., and Dr. K. Osis of the American Society
for Psychical Research· in New York.
     Captain Mitchell has predicted that such tests might
prove as important to man as space exploration itself.
He feels that Man's whole paranormal experience is very
important, because throughout our history we have based
philosophies, theolog1es ana ways of life on these
phenomena, quite often without even acknowledging them
as such.   Furthermore, he states that we are approaching

where we can bring this modern knowledge to bear on the

is convinced that they are totally natural phenomena without

just totally normal human experiences - perhaps of the
spiritual side of man - and very valid and important ones.
      As is the case with so many people today, Captain

      The actual E.S. P. experiments consisted of trying to
transmit columns of numbers, and to assign to each n umber
an E.S.P. symbol (cross, square, wavy lines, circle, star)

practise with three of the subjects to establish a rapport
and work out the procedures they were going to use.

the flight tests, as it turned out, appeared initially to

    ANSWER TO THE MYSTERY OF THE RUSSIAN ASTRONAUTS?


     In July, 1971, Mrs. S. Hart of Port Elizabeth
attended a group meeting at Edmonton, Alberta, Canada,
and the following questions were answered through the
well-known medium, Les Harwood:-
Question: What caused the death or transition of the
three Russian astronauts on re-entering the atmosphere
after their long flight in orbit?
Answer:   There is a three-fold significance to this
transition of these three individuals, and first of all
there is significance in the fact that there were three
of them, the holy number of three, the number of
completion.    These three individuals were placed into
orbit and in effect reflected the hopes, aspirations and
the dreams of the Russian people.    We speak here of the
spiritual aspect and do not concern ourselves that there
are those in Russia who have a negative approach to this,
and who would use the technology for destructive purposes.
But here we speak of the spiritual yearnings, the longings,
the hopes and the dreams of the· Russian people.   And
these three individuals, in fact and in symbol, carried
these with them up into the heavens and thus removed these
from the earthly vibrations.    From a cosmic point of
view, they were not permitted to bring these back to
earth, as these aspirations were to remain in the higher
regions.    The three astronauts not only represented the
nation of Russia, but also the upliftment which must come
to that nation.    They are a type of sacrifice, if you
wish, not in the sense of a blood sacrifice, but in the
sense that they had given themselves in order that there
might be the spiritual upliftment in that country in due
time.    They were taken up, not to be returned to the
earth where the darkness and the miasma of ignorance,
fear and persecution resides.    But they have, in effect,
taken up the Russian people into the higher regions.

      There is, in addition to this, the third aspect which
is perhaps a bit more subtle. ·These three individuals
have encircled the entire globe, and so have inscribed in
the heavens the circle, the symbol of God, on behalf of
the Russian people.   They exhibited the dreams of the
people to be lifted up above their materialisms and the
unhappiness of their present physical world. And it was
necessary that this be worked out in symbol by these three
individuals.   The Russians have not yet successfully
placed another individual upon another planet or the moon.
However, when their space capabilities have done that, it
will mark a step forward for the nation of Russia in
spiritual terms, and it will mean that t hey have finally
broken their bondage in figure and in symbol.
Question: Have you any idea how far the astronauts were
from the earth's surface when the transition period came?
Answer:  We believe that in essence this occurred at a
very high altitude, i.e. in keeping with the symbolism
which we have mentioned, the transition in effect occurred
reasonably soon after the atmosphere was re-entered. In
other words, before they were really able to enter into
the denser atmosphere to any extent whatsoever.
Question:  It would be interesting to see what reaction
came from the astronauts when they made the transition.
Answer: Actually, they were in a state of being, very
close to astral travelling, even prior to re-entry. This
was one of the difficulties they were having, and they
were subject to the same phenomena that many of the
American astronauts experienced, namely, remaining in
their physical bodies while in orbit. One of the three
astronauts, particularly, was half out of the body long
before the re-entry began. So generally, the transition
was extremely easy.
Question:  There has been some talk of a leakage which
would bring this transition about from a mechanical point
of view. Would you care to enlarge on this?

Answer:   Yes, there vJere the mechanicnl aspects to it as
well.   It is part of an overall plan;    there had to be
a physical manifestation to coincide with the spiritual
and metaphysical aspects.     The essential part was that
they made the transition, in a certain way before they
could return to earth, so there was definitely, as you
have stated, a mechanical failure.     However, this was
only incidental, in as much as I have said, the three of
them were half over already.     The astronauts would have
experienced very great difficulties on the earth plane,
as a result of the prolonged period of time spent in
orbit.    The difficulties include not only a type of
weightlessness which they would experience, symbolic of
the adjustment of their bodies to the higher reaches
and not just a higher atmospheric region, but also the
fact that they would not be too closely attached to the
physical body for a considerable period of time.     As
a result they would experience many strange phenomena,
even upon the earth plane, after a prolonged period in
orbit.    In going beyond the earth gravity, or reduced
gravity, there is that problem at the moment.     And
these t hree individuals would have been very distressed
as a result of this, had they arrived safely without
the transition.
