STRANGE HISTORY           OF THE GRAY BARKER DOCUMENTARIEE

       Hopefully most of you at least remember the name of ufologist Gray Barker, who died
back i� 19e4. He was a protegee of Ray Palmer ( founder of Fat e Magazine ) and an inspira­

died, his estate was actually in debt to the government. His books, correspondence,      and
other papers were sold to the public library in Clarksburg, West Virginia, where he had
lived a large part of his 11 fe.

a documentary on Barker. Coon, though not really a saucer fan , had apparently come across
Gray's name and fame from studying the antics of contactee George Van Tassel, well known
for his outdoor UFO conventions years ago at Giant Rock, California.

quite a few of the people who had known Barker well. He shot a lot of footage of your edi­
tor, and short segments of this are cleverly inserted throughout the film.

Coon made very little effort to sell it, and secondly because, as everyone who     saw it
agreed, it was much too long and included parts that were irrelevant. Coon realized this,
but for a very long time he resisted advice to come out with an improved version.
