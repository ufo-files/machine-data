Courier-Mail (Brisbane, Qld. : 1933 - 1954), Monday 21 June 1954, page 1



       Tweed              'saucer'
         MURWILLUMBAH              —


                                       Six
       Tweed     residents   said    they
       watched     a  ''flying   saucer'
       for 15 minutes       at    Crystal
       Creek    last night     while     it
       streaked    about the sky       be
       fore looping the loop          and
       disappearing.
