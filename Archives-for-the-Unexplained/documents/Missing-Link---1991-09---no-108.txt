                                                      WITHOUT A NAME
                                                         By Bob Anthony

              On our planet everything has a name. Our people have names, our countries, states, counties, cities,
            suburbs, parks - even our buildings have names. We are used to these names. They give us a common
            ground of reference. When we wish to talk about a person, a place, a thing, any object we wish to refer
            to we use a name in our description.
              Imagine just for a moment that names were a thing of the past, a distant past. Imagine that language is
            no longer important. Oh, the words are still in your memory, the writing still familiar. Such things are
            still a necessary part of your life, but through evolution of communication, certain aspects of language
            are no longer needed.
              For some people questions arise. What kind of evolution would have to take place in order for names
            to become obsolete? For the sake of argument we will consider communicating through telepathy. It
            has been said, more than a few times, that everyone has the ability within them. If it is possible to have
            a race of telepaths would that be enough alleviate names? Would thinking about a person, place, or
            thing, be enough to make an identification? Would thinking about a person be enough to call that
            person to facilitate a conversation? If everyone has their own telepathic signature would it be enough?
              If Earth people were a race of telepaths, it would forever change life on this planet as we know it.
            Honesty would have to prevail. It would literally be impossible for us to lie to one another. Breakdowns
            in relationships due to failure to communicate would be a thing of the past. If anything, the problem
            would be too much communicating. What kinds of problems would there be in knowing everything
            about everyone around us? Or would we know everything? Certainly there would be a way to block
            telepathic images. But by blocking them, would it make us "deaf"? If we became so engulfed in
            protecting our secrets, would we be able to hear some one calling us? There are many people on Earth
            at this time who are telepathic. Do they have the answers to these questions. Can they help us? Would
            we accept their help?
              Before we can learn the art of telepathy, a natural step in the progression would have to be of an
            empathic nature (being able to sense the emotions of others). Certainly more people can identify with
            that ability. Are those people better in touch with the world around them?
              What about names? How long will we need them? Someday we will learn to be telepathic, and the
            names will become obsolete. Imagine just for a moment what it would be like ...
