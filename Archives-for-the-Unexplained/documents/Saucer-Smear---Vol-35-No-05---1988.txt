has been exposed as a (gasp!) science fiction writer, and on the      other

que, N.M., saying: "I have just learned that the FBI has arrested William
Moore and Jaime Shandera and charged them with 'public disclosure' of 'ex­
tremely sensitive information', and that the U.S. Government has official­
ly requested Canada to extradite Stanton Friedman". It seems obvious that
Klass was merely indulging in his unpopular heavy -handed humor here. Since
Klass believes the M J -12 documents are fakes, how could he believe or ser­

traveling in Europe and has already missed the deadline. Poor Phil!
