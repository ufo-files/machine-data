The   purpose   of    STRANGE     PHBNOMBNA         INVBSTIGATIONS         as   an
organization, is defined clearly by its title: to investigate strange phenomena in
its many categories. Such categories include reported hauntings, ie apparitions,
poltergeist e\rents; UFO sightings and various forms of psychic happenings.
SPI was formed in 1979. Involvement in SPI is more than academic beyond
the appeal as a study. We are drawn to it because we share the mutual opinion
that the subject demands investigation and definite awareness on as wide a
scale as possible, which will help to establish a fmner foundation that:
"There are such things".

The aims of SPI are to collect, research and publish accounts relating to most
aspects of Strange Phenomena, and to inform the general public that there is,
indeed, a case to be answered.
SPI does not hold or express any strict viewpoint as to the nature of the events
of which we research; we keep an open mind and hope that by continued
research we may come up with some answers to account for what as present
eludes us.
