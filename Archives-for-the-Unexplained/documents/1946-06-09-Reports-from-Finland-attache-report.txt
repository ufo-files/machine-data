Property of the military attache in Helsinki
No. 69/3: 45/1946

Observation reports on Finnish light phenomena (Dated 9/6-1946)
 
Descriptions:

1. Haapajärvi: 22:17 o’clock. A light phenomenon, traveling from west to the east, leaving a puff of flame and smoke.

2. Liperi: 22:17 o’clock. An intense flame which traveled from northwest to the southeast and left traces of a smoke trail.

3. Tampere. Unknown time point. Possible shooting star, which appeared from the southwest.

4. Lammi. Unknown time point. A light phenomenon which left behind sparks and an enduring smoke trail.

5. Lahti: 22:17 o’clock. A light phenomenon, traveling from northwest to the southeast. It had a silver cylinder whose front shone like fire (illuminated by the sun?). Smoke trail.

6. Mankala. Unknown time point. Southern light phenomenon.

7. Orimattila. Unknown time point. A light phenomenon, traveling from northwest to the southeast. The object exploded, at which point sparks flew in every direction, leaving a cloud of smoke in its midst.

8. Pornainen. Unknown time point. A rocket-like burst of light, approaching the ground from northeast to the southwest. A smoke trail remained visible for 10 minutes.

9. Helsinki and its surrounding settlements:
Koivuniemi: 22.15 o’clock. A light phenomenon, traveling from northwest to the southeast.
Käpylä. Unknown time point. Ascending projectile, traveling to the southwest.
Käpylä. Unknown time point. A yellow light, following a path of vertical descent.
Mellunkylä. Unknown time point. Light phenomena, reminiscent of a fireworks display.
Degerö. Unknown time point. Two silver balls, traveling from northwest to the southeast, causing a woozing sound and leaving a fire trail behind.
Herttoniemi. Unknown time point. Descending light phenomenon, traveling northeast to the southwest.
Jollas. Unknown time point. A sparkling light trail, which burned out.
The city center. Unknown time point. Descending light phenomenon, yellow in colour. Three explosions were heard, during which the object changed its course.
The city center. Unknown time point. One red and two white lights.
The city center. Unknown time point. Explosion, puff of flame and smoke, slowly rising smoke trail, heading SSW.
The city center: 22:17 o’clock. Advancing optical phenomenon, traveling at great velocity, leaving a smoke trail behind.
The city center: 22.20 o’clock. A red light with the approximate altitude of 10 kilometers appeared from the northeast, leaving behind a smoke trail which remained visible for about 10 minutes.
Kilo: 22:05 o’clock (?). Ascending flame, heading SSW, leaving a smoke trail behind.

10. Suomusjärvi: 22:20 o’clock. A light phenomenon descended beyond the forest, followed by an explosion.

11. Meltola: 22:35 o’clock. A light phenomenon, traveling from northwest to the southeast, ultimately transforming into a rising smoke trail. A powerful explosion was heard after about 1-1 ½ minutes.
