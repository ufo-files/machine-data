SUMMARY:

        The target site has land, a structure and activity.             Colors
evident are green, red, and blue.           The main aspect of the site is
something curved and rounded.          There is also a pointed shape.

        There are various shapes at the target (see sketches).             One

shape looks to me like a statue, solid, and possibly made of

stone, that has broken and/or fallen down.             It is a structure

with an intricate design, artistic in some way.              It is brighter
towards the bottom.       It is very dimensional but mostly curved.
There is something that "slides away".            There is a very strong
smell at the site, almost metallic, that stays in your throat

when you breathe it.       Combined with the paint smell int he other

building, it made me nauseous and sneeze.             Something about the

target is purposeful and meaningful.           It is informative and
educational.
        The activity at the site seems to be movement.             I picked up

red, dangerous, jagged, and burning.           This made me think of a

fire.    AOL-Structure on fire.
