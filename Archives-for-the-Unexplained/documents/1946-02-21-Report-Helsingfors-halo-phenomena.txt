Case XXX.
A halo over Helsingfors

Time: February 21, between 16.00 and 16.30 [Finnish time]
Place: Helsingfors (Helsinki), Finland

”The...phenomenon was seen between 16 and 16.30, when the sun was in the southwest. To the left of the sun a rainbow-coloured spot was observed, first shining in bright colours but later fading. We immediately phoned the meteorological central bureau where the phenomenon had been spotted and identified. Dr. Angervo told us that the spot was part of a so called sunring which sometimes can be seen to surround the sun at about 22 ½ degrees from it...”

Status: Halo phenomenon
Source: Hufvudstadsbladet, February 22
