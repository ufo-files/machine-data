what looked like a plane at first, then it started to hover. It was very low
and it had these red and green lights pulsating all around it. The green
light was very bright, then, it swung around in a circle coming towards us
and hovered in between the houses. There was no noise or sound coming
from it.    I have never seen anything so big in the sky before and so
brilliantly lit up. It was about 100 yards away from us and well below the
housetops. We thought it was going to land.
Also above it and higher up in the clouds was this round white object
surrounded by what looked like musts which was also very large.         We
stood and looked at the saucer shaped object for all of 6 minutes then it
tipped on its side and whoosh it was gone.     I have never seen anything
move so fast you had to see it to believe it, and when that disappeared so
did the round white object that was above. Even the mist disappeared.

August 1993.     An unnamed witness was interviewed by a Hampshire
UFO Investigator who saw early one morning in a field of rape seed an
unusual craft hovering just off the ground.     He watched as the object
swayed from side to side in a pendulum fashion. He was able to observe
that the craft had portholes underneath which gave off a heat vapour
which floated upwards distorting the edges of the saucer-like craft.
Suddenly the whole thing disappeared and the witness just carried onto
work as if nothing had happened.     He had totally forgotten what he had
seen .. It was not until he was on his way home and passed the spot where
he had witnessed the strange craft did he suddenly remember it.
The Investigator thought this unusual because it was the first time he had
experienced seeing a UFO and it wasn't something you could just
"forget".
A strange thing had happened prior to the sighting which the witness then
went on to explain that a large number of birds had died within a few
hours and so far a post mortem had shown there was nothing wrong with
them. There was also a field of sheep next to where the craft landed who
had been taken ill and unusually alongside this a bore hole covered over
with grass has shown that the grass has died and there have not been any
rabbits sighted for a few weeks, co-incidence it may be but worth noting.
