  Experience at Rendelsham Forest 2008

  Brenda Butler © John Hanson 2008

  Larry Warren, of the famous UFO incident which occurred in December 1980.

  After a short sharp shower we continued on our journey, heading towards
  an area, nicknamed ‘Blair Witch’,(not by me!), a secluded location in
  the forest, (where I had the ignominies delight of being struck on the
  back by a small white stone, during a previous visit to the forest ) but
  stopped along the track to continue our observations over the field, now
  the site of a table and seats, installed by the Forestry Commission as

  was rewarded by the capture on photo of a green and blue orb but no

  As we were about to enter the area ‘Blair Witch area, we heard a very disconcerting
  noise like a thud and decided not to continue our journey there. Brenda then
  told me she could see a white figure standing about 20 yards inside the trees.
  I looked, not expecting to see anything, and with astonishment saw a slightly glowing
  figure, with no facial features, standing on the forest floor before it vanished from sight. I rubbed my eyes
  and thought… did I really see that? But I had.
  We walked back to the car park, Brenda maintaining she could see small silver figures and lights along the
  track. I never saw anything else but saw no reason to disbelieve her. I had, after all, just seen something I
  couldn’t explain rationally.

  By now it was 11.45 p.m. Time seemed to be going slow, (ironically that night the clocks went forward to
  British Summer Time at 12 pm), and made our way back to
  the car park, where we had a drink and then decided to walk up the track leading
  to East Gate.

  We stopped at the barrier leading to East Gate and began to walk slowly along the
  tarmac road, now overgrown with weeds, when Brenda told me she could hear
  something walking along the forest floor, adjacent to our position on the left. My
  first thoughts were to hope it wasn’t a big cat, as there had been several sighting of
  the animal in the locality. We slowed down and listened intently. There it was
  again! … a padding noise to our left. I brought my misgivings to Brenda’s attention.
  She dismissed the noise as being an animal.
  The temperature had dropped. I felt cold for the first time. Something then lightly touched me on the back
  and neck, which began to unsettle me, as although I had
  heard many people talk about being pulled and touched, I had always wondered if
  their imaginations had taken hold of them. After all, the forest could be eerie as well
  as breathtaking, especially at night.

  As Brenda felt uncomfortable, we retuned to the barrier at the end of the track and
  decided to monitor anything likely to happen. We both felt there was something
  around us - a most uncomfortable feeling, which I had never felt before, and tried
  to dismiss it from my mind, wondering if my imagination was playing tricks.

  We looked up the East Gate track and saw something moving from side to side.
  Brenda estimated it was 20 yards away. I would have put it at more than double that.
  Suddenly, a large beam of light projected from above the forest track, not from any
  appreciable distance in the sky but apparently just over the track, illuminating
  something resembling the gantry of a crane to the left of the track, at a height I
  estimated to be 30-40 feet in the air, dark and substantial in appearance, with a
  rounded part on the top bent over. Another less defined similar object could be
  seen on the opposite side of the track.

  Even more incredible were a number of small black indistinct figures, a few feet in height, seen running
  backwards and forwards along the track, between the larger devices.

  Brenda’s impression:

  ‘Like they were telling the little shapes what to do. It looked like they were building something. The tall
  shapes at the side then disappeared but the figures continued

  John: I saw a number of lights exploding in the air beside Brenda, who shouted,
  ‘Oh my god, look at that!’ I looked and saw a grey figure stood next to her,
  a few feet away. It then moved away into the Forest, accompanied by three or four other similar figures,
  rather than shapes, but with no discernible
  features. I felt no fear or amazement whatsoever and, while remaining puzzled
  about what I had seen, still felt a long way from concluding they were aliens from
  an Alien vessel, which will no doubt exasperate Brenda. What the purpose of all this was I have no idea,
  only that I had experienced something highly unusual. How I wished Peter McCue had been with us this
  time!
  Brenda: ‘I looked back up the East Gate track and saw a black object in the sky
  above the track. The figures were still running from side-to-side, then light shone down on them again,
  illuminating the whole area far more brightly than before.
  It was like daylight. I looked at my watch. It was 2 a.m. (old time).’

  John: I never saw this object but then, without any noise or warning, something
  quite large black in colour passed overhead, no more than 50-60 feet above us,
  travelling towards the Forest. I shouted to Brenda but, by then, it had gone. The
  next thing to happen was that we both felt something strike us on the right
  leg, accompanied by the appearance of a white s shape at the side of the track. Once again we strained
  our eyes, looking up the track, and saw a small white puffy cloud
  moving towards it, which disappeared in front of our eyes.

  I was feeling really cold and had started to shiver, so I advised Brenda I
  wanted to leave and make our way back to Brenda’s home. Brenda felt
  she wanted to stay and continue with the observations, clearly excited she
  had got some corroboration about the most peculiar events I had ever seen
  manifest in the forest.

  The glow in the middle of the track became more intense and a large black
  ‘blob’ appeared to come out of the light and head towards us. When a few feet
  away, it too disappeared from sight. By now I was freezing, although oddly
  Brenda said the left side of her face and ear were burning. It was 3.15 am.
  (old time). Brenda was ‘cock a hoop’ that her pleas to whatever deities
  occupied the forest for them to show me something had come to fruition.
  What puzzled me more than anything was why we had never walked closer
  to the scene of the incident. It wasn’t as if we were frightened. Also why
  hadn’t I taken any photographs. I found this most odd.




  The following morning, after separately writing up what we had seen, we
  returned to the forest - now busy with people walking their dogs and children
  cycling - the atmosphere completely different, and carefully examined
  the sides of the track, which appeared to have been swept clean for a distance
  of 10-15feet. but saw nothing of any note. It was almost as if it
  hadn’t happened, except with one chilling difference…. it had!

  Now I finally realised the substance of other similar reports brought to my
  attention by others who had been visiting the Forest for a number of years
  with their tales of strange figures and weird UFOS, spiralling out of the
  forest on carpets of mist. I knew I would now no longer treat such reports
  with scepticism, although I was not naive enough to know my own version
  of events which had taken place would be treated any differently.
