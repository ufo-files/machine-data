TEXT: EXTRACT.
                        THE MODEL TDN-1 FOUR WIRE CEREBRAL BIOELECTRICAL SOURCE ELECTRODE
                        CAP WAS DEVELOPED FOR RECORDING HUMAN CEREB.RAL BIOELECTRICITY UNDER
                        SOME SPECIAL CONDITIONS.    THE ELECTRODE CAP HAS RELATIVELY HIGH
                        NOISE IMMUNITY AND CAN BETTER RECORD HUMAN BIOELECTRICAL SIGNALS
                        DURING SOME MOVING STATES.      AS PROVED FOLLOWING LONG PERIODS OF
                        ACTUAL   USE, THE INSTRUMENT CAN ACCURATELY AND CLEARLY RECORD
                        VARIATIONS OF HUMAN CEREBRAL BIOELECTRICITY UNDER CONDITIONS OF LOW
                        ATMOSPHERE PRESSURE WITH OXYGEN DEFICIENCY, QIGONG (GAS ENERGY)
                        MEDITATION STATE, AND.FIELD EXPERIMENTAL CONDITIONS WITH RELATIVELY
                        HIGH INTERFERENCE.    THUS, THESE ELECTRODE CAPS HAVE GOOD COMMENTS
                        FROM USERS.    IN ADDITION, IF A SMALL MAGNETIC TAPE RECORDER OR A
                        TRANSMITTER   IS   ATTACHED  TO    THE  INSTRUMENT, HUMAN CEREBRAL
                        BIOELECTRICITY DURING FREE MOVING CONDITIONS CAN ALSO BE RECORDED.
                        THUS, THIS IS A VERY FEASIBLE INSTRUMENT FOR RECORDING CEREBRAL
                        BIOELECTRICITY FOR PROLONGED CLINICAL MONITORING OF PATIENTS AS WELL
                        AS ATHLETIC ACTIVITIES.
