Sleuths decline probe of UFO report
   LONDON   (UP!)     Scotland Yard has   too much down-to-earth crime to
probe to delve into the mysterious case of a UFO reported hovering over
London, a spokesman said Tuesday.
   Three policemen, summoned by a teenage girl who spotted a bizzare
object in the sky, reported back to their stationhouse that they too had
seen it, a police spokesman said.
   Zena Sfei, 16, noticed the saucer-shaped, brightly colored object
and viewed it through a telescope mounted on the doorstep to her family
home, then calleq police to verify the sighting.
   The Evening Standard newspaper said the officers watched the
 "gray-green, pink and electric blue object as it spun, banked and
hovered"  above London's posh Kensington neighborhood for about 30
minutes.
    "Police officers saw an unidentified object after a call from a
member of the public,"  the spokesman confirmed.  "We are unable to say
what it was."
   He said the officers entered it into their stationhouse logbook but
there would be no further investigation.
    "It's a minor matter although it's something unusual,"  he said.
 "We have enough trouble dealing with murders let alone investigating
lights   in the sky."
