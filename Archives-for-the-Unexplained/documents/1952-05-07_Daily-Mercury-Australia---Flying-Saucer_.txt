          MURWILLUMBAH.          Tues.          —




       A    silent, very   bright object
       flew over   Mt. Burrell at great
       speed   about     10 o'clock    last
       night, Mrs. Bill Grant, of Mt.
       Burrell, 20 miles south of Mur-
       willumbah. claimed to-day.
         She   said    the      sky   was   over-
       cast, which  ruled out its being
       a meteor.  and the fact that no
       sound could be heard suggested
       that it could not be an aircraft.
