Mercury (Hobart, Tas. : 1860 - 1954), Thursday 7 December 1950, page 6




          "Flying Saucer"                                In
              Queensland
         BRISBANE,   Wed.              -


                                               Residents       of
       Maryborough, about             200        miles north
       of Brisbane, tonight                    watched      a
       "flying saucer" make                a     wide   sweep
       over     the     district.

             About    8 p.m.    a   bright blue object
       was     seen      travelling    at high speed
       to the        north.
          It returned    at low   level, ap-
       proaching the river mouth.
         The   object moved  almost perpen-
       dicularly to an estimated
                                   height of
       8,000ft.
         The          Maryborough              R.S.L.    sub
       branch         president     (Mr.        S.   Bryant
       Smith) said he estimated the speed
       at 400 to 500 m.p.h.                                         &nbsp;
