Case xxx: 
Hissing black streak in the air

Time: July 13, a.m. 
Place: River Ljungan, near Fränsta (BM49)

"The manager of the Fränsta laundry Mr. Ture Andersson stated that he, while bathing in Ljungan yesterday morning suddenly heard a hissing sound in the air. He discovered a meter-long black streak in the air at a considerable altitude. The black line was moving towards a southeasterly direction."

Note: This case was never noted in the Defence files.

Status: Possibly aircraft contrail.
Source: Sundsvalls Tidning, July 14. ScanCat 1281.
