    RESEARCH      UNCOVERS         NEW       LIFE FORM

    Biologists working for the National Aeronautics and Space Administration and
    the US National Science Foundation have identified a 'new' form of life on
    Earth,   possibly datinsback to the planet's first billion years.

    The new form - previously thought to                 be a type of ordinary bacteria - is
    a METHANE-PRODUCING organism,              representing a line of evolutionary descent
    that is totally separate from the two traditi onally-recognised lines -
    animals and plants,      and bacteria.

    The organisms may be the oldest of life forms too - coming not only before
    plants and animals,      but also before bacteria.

    The finding was made by a research team headed by Dr earl Woese,                   Professor
    of Genetics and Development at the University of Illinois,                   an expert on the
    genetic code.

    Methane-producing organisms appear to be ideally suited to what scientists
    believe to have been the Earth's primitive atmosphere.                  The organisms can:

    * Get all their food and energy from very simple compounds such as ca rbon
    dioxide and hydrogen,      the main gases in a primitive atmosphere.

    * Not use any of the complex chemicals most other organisms require as food
    such as sugars and amino acids.
