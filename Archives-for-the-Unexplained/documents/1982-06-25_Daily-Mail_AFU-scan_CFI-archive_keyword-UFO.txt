YESTERDAY was the 35th anniversary of
the birth of t'hat now ubiquitous exclama­

    It is customarily delivered from the
ground with head upturned and arm extend­
ed, pointing, but back then on June 24, 1947.
an Idaho businessman was sitting at the con­
trols of his plane and cruising near Mount
Rainier in Washington State when he made
the first recorded sighting of an unidentified
ftying something.
   There were nine disc-shaped objects mov­
ing in formation.   They made undulations
in the sky that he described as 'like a saucer
skipping over water' and thus begat the
mythology of the ftying saucer.
    Since then there have been thousands of
reported sightings, and those who claim to
have seen UFO's have been generally dis­
missed as genuinely mistaken. drunk or
nutty. (Detractors have been abetted by the
likes of the woman who said she knew it was
a ftying saucer because it had 'UFO' painted
on the side.) But there has been a nagging
residue of sightings that could not be ex­
plained by known natural or man-made
objects and now on their 35th anniversary
UFO-spotters have gained a new credibility.
  Fonnerly classified files forced out of the
