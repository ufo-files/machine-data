28th of July 1946 00.52 AM centre of Perniö

The story of the witness:

It was a warm summernight. There were no clouds in the sky. We were on our way home. There were three men among us; me, Veikko and a friend. I was much younger then them and I am the only one alive today. Martha, from whom we were on our way home, was also with us and maybe some other women. Martha is still alive, she is little younger than I am.

We had been to a party that´s why we were out so late. I don´t remember that we had consumed any alchohol that night. In any case I was in good condition and my head was clear. We stopped for a while, had a conversation, on the country road next to Lampola. While we were standing there we observed something special in the sky. All of us saw a shining object which was flying on a very high altitude. I can no longer remember the exact course of the object but I think it was moving west or northwest or somewhere in between. 

It was no meteorit because it was flying horizontally and the speed was also to slow for a meteorit. The witness has estimated the speed, altitude and size. If the altidude, only estimated, was 10 km or about the same as a regular air liner, the object might have been about the same size as a regular air liner. The object seemed fairly long. The object wasn´t visible and additionally it was a bit dark at night. We only saw the light it reflected. I don´t remember it having a fireflame in the rear. In any case we believed that it was some kind of a rocket sent out by Russia or USA for testing. The object made no sound what so ever. We followed it in the sky for a couple of minutes before it slowly disappeared behind the trees and buildings.

I have been a pensioner for almost three years and  have during this period read rather a lot of books about U.F.O:s. After studying astrological litterature I am of the opinion that it also in our galaxy probably exists a number of planets which has conditions that make life possibly. Probably some of them are more developed than we are here on earth.

Regarding the light-phenomenon I saw in 1946 I am absolutely certain it was caused by an object which has been manufactured by an intelligent being whether it was a rocket, type V2, made by some foreign government, or something else.

 Early winter 1946 Räimä, Finland, PM 1700

A 12-year-old girl, together with her brother and sister, 8 and 10, had been at a neighbour   
buying a kitten. The children were walking home along a creek on a horsetrail suddenly they got dazzled by an enormous light. A bang was heard and after the light had been visible for a few seconds at the same spot it shrunk and disappeared. The small children burst out in tears and started to run home. The observer had the kitten in a basket. After stumbling around in the snow for a while she managed to get back home after her brother and sister. The children told their father and he went out together with some of the children to investigate the place. He found an area on the ground were the snow had melted and the ground had been coloured. The observer dont remember what colour.

December 1946, Laihia, Allinen AM 0720

During the time of the observation the 10-year-old boy was a guard in school and therefor he had to be in school before the other students. He had been countryskiing about a kilometer from northwest  to southeast when he suddenly saw the snow around him turning blue. When he looked up in front of him he discovered a shining lightphenomenon, which was flat and shaped as a disc. The phenomenon had a blue light in the middle. It was kind of yellow and towards the edges it was bluer.

The witness also lost his breath and heard a strong humming noice, like a fuss, while the thing flew over him. The witness looked behind himself and saw the strongly lit blue-yellow-shining thing disappear.

General account

for the work at Kölmjärv 20/7 - 10/8 1946.

A. Initial point for the work.

The place of the impact indicated by the witnesses was clearly marked by scattered reed and water-lillys. Water-lillys thick as a wrist and laying almost as a halfcircle. After two of the eye witnesses story the impact area could be decided to about 1000 which also was well related to the way the impact area looked. Though a slightly less compass is more likely. The angle of fall was indicated to about 45 degrees, possibly slightly steeper. Due to the information given etc it was probebal that some kind of impact had occured.

B. Conclussion of work done etc.

During the time 20/7-29/7 tests with earth probes were taking place according to a linear system, so that on every square meter within the searched area 2 to 3 samples were made. (compare to appendix 1 and 2). Alltogether there were about 3500 samples made. The method gave a negative result. Though one could assume that no objects of great magnitude were found in the sediments above the firm bottom and probably not on the bottom. (hard to separate from stone). Some of the tests were conducted with electronical mine detectors. The tests were not positive. The water was checked for oil stains but none were found. A couple of profiles of the bottom were made appendix 3-4.

During the time 30/7-10/8 searches and tests have been done with a number of electronical devices. To our disposal were some "projsökapparatur" handled by engineer Leveaux, KAFT didn´t function very well on these particular depths. An reconstructed ore prospecting instrument from Boliden however gave good results on tests and therefor it was used further on, when area, due to appendix 5, was searched. Some suspect places were marked, appendix 8, of which none were proved to be of metal. The component was handled by specialists, Dr. Malmqvist and a foreman of the mining company of Boliden. To view the sensitifity of the component a testserie on a generatorgas spring with a description, appendix 7, and a testserie of two of the most suspicious places, appendix 6, have been done. 

On every suspect place a measure of the electrical external resistance of the bottom on every halfmeter has been made, it reduces heavily if there are objects of metal between the electrode of meissure. The sediments of the bottom have also been thorouhly checked. The results have been put in a curve-diagram in appendix 6. No positive results were found, that could indicate objects of metal on the bottom or in layers of sediments above. A survey of the out-flow of Kölmjärv has been done to ascertain the possibillities of draining the lake. A report with a sketch is attached to appendix 9. A search of the nearest terrain, east of the lake has not indicated that a possible procetile has continued north-east after a bounce on the bottom or the water surface.  

C. Final conclusion.

In the area searched one could with certainty determine that no objects of metal with, seen from above, larger size than 0,25 m does exist and probably not smaller objects either unless they are round. The device is namely very good on objects with         . Non metallic objects could have undergone discovery.

Kölmjärv 10/8 1946 

Karl-Gösta Bartell
Löjtnant.

August 8
 
Peaceconference with sounds of armour

According to the military staff our country has probably been used as a shooting-field by a friendly foreign unknown country for testing of V rockets. This is for the time being just an assumtion though nothing has been clearly proved. The assumtion however has been made after a number of serious investigations and is very probable. The observations have indeed been vague but on the other hand to many to be imaginative. The flying objects have to be considered real. The authorities are inclined to identify them as rockets of the type the germans called V-1. Contrary to the V-2 rockets the V-1 rockets travel at low altidude and with a speed that makes them possible to detect with the eye. During the war we had  numerous occasions similiar to these events. Just like now the bombs came from south. Some landed on swedish territory, though it was unintentional. The pieces were wisely picked up and sent to the wright address, which wasn´t the senders intention. Generally it was regarded that the launching-base was located on Bornholm, an island that after the german surrender was surprisingly occupated by the russians. Later the radar-station was located to Peenemunde on Usedom, were a swedish force one time made a landing somewhat smaller than the Normandie-landing. The facilities on Bornholm should have fallen undamaged into Russian hands. So far the shooting-exercises have caused no damage in Sweden. The bombs have probably not warheads, and therefor they are part of an exercise. But they are not entirely without danger. An impact on settled ground or an airplane collision could cause severe damage and are not unthinkable though the exercises seem to be at large scale. Naturally these events have been noticed both in Sweden and abroad, especially in USA. In the USA they have been interpreted as advanced diplomacy to show us our exposed situation in case of a military conflict but that´s just speculations. Maybe there is some kind of demonstration but in that case not directly to the swedish public. The russian comments to the nuclearbomb experiments in the Pacific were that this was a unapropriate demonstration of military means with a political purpose. These things are known to happen you show your new weapons to scare off the opponents. Demonstrations of fleets are most common when you show your diplomatic wishes and intents. Atomicbombs and rocket-bombs are also used. The world will have to get used to this kind of diplomatic talk. This kind of operations should not take place to close to other countries borders though no country can indifferent watch their territory being used as a shooting-field for rockets. Internationally, visits from alien naval-forces are noticed in appearing order. Alien air-forces are noticed in the same way though they are not so common so far. To use the territory of a country for military-operations without any kind of warning is not only inpolite it is a violation of the code between countries. So far this is just a hypothetical discusion but in the same moment it is determined that the country has been used as a shooting-field by a foreign government the foreign office has to find out were the bombs come from and stop them. It is one thing if the superpowers wants to show their military strength at the piece-conference but if they violate other countries borders frequently while doing it the matter becomes very serious. We want full clarity in this matter and the military investigations should continue and if possible end satisfactory. The investigation will show if further measures are necessary.
