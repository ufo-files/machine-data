ICFUFOR Journal files No 42 January 2004


  Nesher/ 10 October 2003


  It was 18:20, when I (Abi Greiff) walked towards the east gate of the Haifa Technion Institute. I saw 2 lights in the sky,
  towards Haifa, high in the sky, 90° above the horizon. In the beginning I saw a yellow-orange light for few seconds.
  After 1 minute a white light appeared, also for few seconds. It appeared in the right of the first light. It seems to me that
  it moved a little to the right, before it turned off. The lights were lower from location of satellites, and were seen in front
  of clear sky. In the area, wasn’t any movements of airplanes.


   Hod Ha’Sharon/ 6 November 2003


  Tamar, who gave us this message, watched at 17:00 in an unusual object from her balcony, which faces Ramataim. She
  tells us: “in bright sky, towards the horizon, I saw a red-orange sphere. It flew upwards, in a dark angle (45°). I observed
  this object for almost 2 minutes. It flew towards Petach-Tikva, moved upwards, became a tiny spot and disappeared in
  the sky. It looked like a bright ball in the background of the blue sky. No sound was heard, it was totally quiet. The
  object wasn’t close, but I saw it clearly. It was something big”.


    Ma’agan Michael/ 17 November 2003


  Arik Hasidim tells: “At 19:00 I saw in the sky, above me, 2 aircrafts, which, in the beginning, thought to be airplanes.
  But there was no noise at all. Their color was bright, but not shiny. One of them flew over my head and was (from my
  point of view) 1 meter in size. They made complex they came from south and flew northward”
