                   COULD THE ALIENS BE HERE AMONG US?
There may be over fifteen different types of Aliens visiting the Earth at present, in fact some researchers
claim there are many more. The big question is, what do they look like? We have heard numerous
descriptions of 'The Greys' and references to other more unusual types of Aliens can be found in a number
of UFO reference books.
Is it possible that Aliens of human shape and form can be existing alongside us here on Planet Earth?
For a number of years we have heard of the 'Men In Black' (MIB) visiting contactees and witnesses of
UFO events. Our own group has on record, cases of such events as MIB visits (Ilkeston family 1990's), of
strange black sedan cars appearing quite suddenly at night on remote roads (Postal driver Tom Culleton
1996). We also have an incident where two young guys were hitch-hiking in southern England and were
given a lift on a lonely road, by a strange driver who apparently was covered from his neck to his ankles in
a thin rubberised skin-tight suit, beneath his 'normal' clothing. During the journey the two teenagers
mentioned a few everyday facts in their conversation to the driver, who it seems was totally unaware of
normal everyday events. They were dropped off a few miles further down the road and· were then amazed
to see ·what appeared ·to be ·the two distant red car tail lights ·rise into the s�y and disappear out of sight.

In May 1974 there was the strange case of 'Wally Chum' who was walking on his way to work in
Farnborough, Hants. He was a bit of a rough and ready guy and at 8.02am he was in a hurry to pick up his
cigarettes and newspaper from the paper shop. "I was about to cross the road, when this fellow spoke to
me, I didn't take any notice of him as I was late for work." The the stranger spoke again... "I am (sounded
like Alfreka), I am from the saucer people do you understand?" The stranger held out his hand, but Wally
was in a hurry and it was only shortly afterwards that he stopped and looked around, but the stranger had
disappeared.
Although sueh- reports eould be put down to· a vivid imagination, the same could not be said of the event
related by Sir Peter·Horsley ·in his book 'Sounds from Another Room'.
The book was published in 1997 and it is only recently that the National Press has 'discovered' it and Sir
Peter's account of his invitation from a 'Mrs Markham' to a meeting with 'Janus', a figure seated in a
dimly lit room and a person that as Sir Peter stated "had the extraordinary ability to read my thoughts." (A
detailed account of the meeting can be read in Timothy Good's book 'Alien Base' pages 210 -215).
This was no story by .a starry .eyed teenager, but an.account given by a man who at one time was the
Deputy-Commander of RAF Strike Command in 1973 and at the time of his meeting with 'Janus', was
Equerry to HRH Prince Philip.
The author Timothy Good ·has also related his own experience of a strange 'coincidental' meeting with a
person that could possibly have been an Alien, while he was on tour in the United States. The person
appeared in a crowded hotel vestibule and sat next to Tim', after he had put out a telepathic message for
any Aliens in the area to come and visit him in the hotel.
What are we to make of these strange events? Are there Aliens living among us? Many people believe that
there are ...... Have a. good.look at your neighbour next time he appears!

                     THE UFO SCENE AROUND DERBYSHIRE
The sighting of a 'saucer'- type craft took place at 1. 02am on Tuesday 9th April 02 at the junction of the
Uttoxeter new Road and Manor Road, Derby.
The witness was Ms. Julia Perry, who sighted the UFO craft over this busy Derby 'Ring-Road' junction, in
the early hours of the morning. Julia had ventured into the kitchen at lam, to fetch a drink of water and
happened to peer through the kitchen window, which overlooks the junction. "I looked out of the window
and saw this thing in the sky", commented Julia. "It was saucer shaped. It seemed to be tilted (at an angle)
as I could only see the lights clearly, on the right side."
"It was stationary for quite a while and then moved off very slowly at a speed of only 3 - 5 mph. It seemed
to me as if it was gliding sideways. I was watching it for at least ten minutes, then it disappeared very
quickly" stated Julia.
Julia described the object as being 'a saucer'. It was sharply defined and there was no sound. White lights
were visible around the craft, "as bright as a 200 watt bulb". The size of the craft was described as being
approx. 6" at arms length, when viewed by the witness.

           TWO 'DIAMOND' CRAFT SEEN OVER MELBOURNE
At 9.40pm on the night of the 13th march 2002, Mr Brian Hampson walked slowly towards his front
door, after locking up his car for the night. As he looked up into the night sky, his attention was attracted
to an aircraft passing overhead, on its way to land at the nearby East Midlands Airport. He was used to
the constant aircraft activity over Melbourne, Derbyshire and familiar with the lighting pattern showing
on night flying aircraft.
Mr Hampson then related what he saw next:      "I noticed two sets of lights approaching as I looked up.
They were about twice the height of the plane that had been approaching the EMA (2 - 3k ?). What struck
me as unusual was the direction and the lighting pattern of two identical sets of four lights in diamond
pattern and so close together. The lights were distinct but not bright, with no beam. At the same time as
the diamond lights were approaching, another aircraft flew high overhead (at approx. 30,000ft.) which
gave me a good perspective.''
"After a few seconds I called to my wife to come outside and she also watched the objects as they
approached. They were straight and level, with the shape changing so that it was obvious that the lights
that were on the bottom of the objects were of very similar intensity and were of an amber/white colour."
"Belatedly, I then went in to fetch my binoculars and I was then able to view the underside of one object
for a few seconds, I could then see that there were other, smaller lights underneath. I was able to see that
the underside of the craft was not flat but there were angular ridges showing in the low light. There
appeared to be a solid structure between the lights illuminating the diamond shape."
"My view was then obscured by a tree and I moved to a better position and watched them disappear over
Melbourne, which had low patchy cloud around at the time."
The objects had passed over in complete silence and according to Brian's wife (who had watched them
while Brian had fetched his binoculars) one of the objects had pulled alongside the other as they passed
overhead. Brian commented that they still looked 'staggered' to him as they moved away and out of
sight. The whole event had lasted only one and a half to two minutes.
According to Brian, the diamond lights, which had first attracted his attention because of their unusual
lighting configuration, approached from the East and were heading in a WSW direction. The speed, he
estimated to be only about 100 mph.

                         BLUE BEAM MYSTERY AT BONSALL MOOR

Radio enthusiast Ron Gaunt of Darley Dale had a strange experience at 3am on 7th April, when his Ford
car was engulfed in an eerie bluey light for about two minutes, while parked on Bonsall Moor.
At the time, Ron was transmitting on Long Wave to a radio friend Barbara Whyman in Sheffield, when
he noticed interference on his radio. Suddenly his car was covered by the light "Like broad daylight!"
It is understood that the experience left him disorientated for a while and he left for home shortly
afterwards. He later tested his radio equipment and found that it worked perfectly.
The PRA have attempted to contact Mr Gaunt, but have had no response to their enquiry.
This may be due to the fact that Mr Gaunt was transmitting on Long Wave, which we have been reliably
informed (by a competent Radio Amateur) is illegal. In either case, it does appear that Mr Gaunt does not
wish to be involved in any further publicity regarding this incident.
The event is reminiscent of a similar case, some years ago, when a PRA member drove up to the top of
Alport Heights (also circled on the map above), when suddenly the immediate area around his car was
illuminated by a bright white beam, "As though it was a cone of light" .The intended sky watch was
brought to a rapid conclusion, as the witness returned to Derby without delay.
