  Hi John,
  U-2s were likely to be the ones that deployed to Wethersfield in 1975. I have no record of a U-2 ever
  landing at Woodbridge. Not a lot was missed by the aviation spotters at Woodbridge and Bentwaters (night
  or day), so someone would have known about it.

  Mildenhall. I have no record of one landing at Woodbridge.

  http://www.abpic.co.uk/photo/1047222/), however the ALL version (s/n: 55-3123), did not.
