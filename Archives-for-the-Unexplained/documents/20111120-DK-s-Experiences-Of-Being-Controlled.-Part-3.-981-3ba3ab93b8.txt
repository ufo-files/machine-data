  I remember a period more then two years after the whole
  thing began, when they became a little careless and used an
  identical sound recording repetetively, so there wasn't any
  natural variation in the surveillance imitations.
  In 1992, when there was intensive surveillance going on and
  when I would be accompanied by a stream of people every
  time I left my home, they had all these people activated
  anyway, so there wouldn't be much additional trouble having
  them do a little extra door slamming above me and making
  other synchronized signs of surveilling me. In the long run, it
  would however be much more cost effective to simulate it
  through the implant.
  They produced sounds directly into my brain to give me the
  impression that my neighbors would be surveying or harassing
  me long after the real surveyors had left. There were also
  much more subtle types of influence acting on me. E. g.,
  simultaneous with the production of threatening sounds
  seeming to be emanating from a neighbor, there was a
  kind of suggestive effect at work. Such as to make me
  fear my neighbors or inducing anger directed towards
  them. It felt like something coming from without and I
  never could mistake it for a genuine feeling, but such
  things are so subtle, that you tend to repress the thought
  of it, for the usual f-ar of being suspected of suffering
  from some kind of insanity.
  The use of both the oldfashioned type of surveillance and
  harassment, is using ordinary people or p-lice officers, and

  is not only aimed at the victim, but in addition, it is also a

  action. I will not elaborate on it here.
  I also noted that there seemed to be a slight anomaly,
  regarding the vehicles they used for part of the surveillance.
  There seemed to be an anomalously high fraction that had
  the same color. I thought that they had hired the cars and
  that they had been given some bargain price if they accepted
  a limited choice of colors. Normally I would never mention
  this, since it would seem to divert the attention from more
  important matters, but I have seen some other mind-control

  trouble of deliberately arranging this sort of subtle anomaly.

  the surveillance is exposed to a lot of little anomalies like the

  of everything, as proof. This will redirect the persons attention
  and energy from concentrating on those facts that seem more

  like and maybe finding out their identities. In a sense, what
  they could hope to accomplish, by doing it would be to raise
  the noise level, to blur everything. To make the person devote
  a lot of time just to keep track of insignificant details.

  surveyors a good beating, but being basically a very
  nonviolent nature, I never really came close to doing that.
  And besides, I thought that the mind controllers would

  directly into my brain. Those are not fair conditions, and
