     Details
    133 boxes (66.5 linear ft.)

    1 carton (1 linear ft.)

    COLLECTION STORED OFF-SITE: Advance notice required for access.

    Collection consists of the investigative reports of the US Air Force's Project Blue Book into the phenomenon
    of UFO's, and includes photocopied records obtained by Mark VII Ltd. under the Freedom of Information
    Act, as well as scripts and related material from the television show "Project U.F.O." produced by Jack
    Webb.
