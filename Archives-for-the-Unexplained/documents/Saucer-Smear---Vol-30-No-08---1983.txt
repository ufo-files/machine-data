           Olde-time saucer fans may remember the name of Elliott Rockmore of -
    Brooklyn, N.Y., the man who published the first amateur magazine devoted
    to factual reporting of current UFOs and Fortean events, back in the very
    earl y 1950's. Rockmore began his publication before SAU CER NEWS (founded
    1954) or even AFRO (1952) , . though not before the formation of southern
    California's BSRA (1946).
           In any case, Rockmore literally went paranoid around 1954, and dis­
    appeared completely from the scene, refusing to communicate with anyone.
_   He later subscribed to SAUCER NEWS off & on from the Rockland county, New­
    York state mental hospital. By 1965 he was loose again; we ran into him
    accidentally at a social gathering in Brooklyn, and he seemed friendly &
    lucid at that time.                                          _


           Then we lost track of Rockmore again until just the other day, when
    he mailed us a long, � crazy handwritten letter which begins "(Dear)
    Mr. TreasGp. Queer". We got the feeling from this salutation that the let­
    ter would be unfriendly, and it is. Rockmore, though still in Brooklyn,
    has gone off the deep end once more! Why he chose to contact us after so
    many years, we do not know; but the letter is semi-threatening in nature, ·
    and we don't intend to answer it.
