Underground Bases and Tunnels: What Is The Government Trying To Hide?


  In spite of the somewhat lurid subtitle, this is apparently a well researched guide to confirmed information
  on underground government facilities, including Cheyenne Mtn., FEMA facilities, tunnelling methods, etc.
  A primer on the "how and why" of underground construction. Includes many reproduced government
  documents, patent diagrams and photos of boring machines. The only irrelevancy is a chapter at the end on
  alien abduction, which departs into speculation and doesn't seem to fit. -- Glenn Campbell
