                           Quarterly Report, Third Quarter 2001, July-September


        Office Activity
        Office activity continues negligible. A single sighting was called in from NW
        Washington, D.C. On September 5, booming sounds were heard and flashes of light
        seen, but this was also the night of a fireworks display for visiting dignitaries, and no
        object was actually seen in the sky.

        Fund Web Site
        A "Hall of Fame" of individuals who have distinguished themselves within ufology over
        five decades has been drafted as well as a brief introduction to the UFO subject. As of
        September, the new web site format is not in place.

        UFOs.

           On-going Proposals and Projects
           Dick Haines is preparing to submit his UFO photographic report.

           New Projects
           Steve Kaeser would like the Fund to make the Mario Pazzaglini document on alien
           writtings available on CD-ROM and is in the process of obtaining permission to do so.

           James Houran has asked for assistance with page costs of a paper by himself and Kevin
           Randle on the Ramey memo that has been sent to the Journal ofScientific Exploration.
           The executive board agreed to a $750 payment.

           The Center for UFO Studies would like to organize the John Timmerman interview
           transcripts, which range over several decades, into a coherent monograph. Mike Swords
           will collate the best reports from thousands of pages of documents. The cost is $3000
           and the time to completion 6 months after approval. The executive committee approved
           the proposal, and it is being sent to the national board for a vote.

           Coalition (CUFOS, FUFOR, MUFON) Activities
           Delphos Report.-Tom Deuley has agreed to help prepare this manuscript for
           publication. Mike Swords is writing a summary of the Budinger Report, a technical
           paper which will be incorporated in this manuscript.

           Coalition publication The Best Available Evidence was twentieth among UFO titles on
           Amazon. com in late August and sixtieth in overall sales. The Coalition has almost paid
           back the advance by sales recepts which covered the second half of2000.

           Coalition publication The UFO Evidence II are climbing but sales data has not been
           submitted to the Coalition (information is usually received in June of the following year).
