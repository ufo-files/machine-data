                                                 Tucson, on Oct . 7, there were 60 flying
                                                 machines sighted over Tucson. An editorial

                                                 unable to obtain any explanations from the
                                                 AF , FAA, or NORAD as to these objects. The

                                                 govt. sources . We have been in contact
