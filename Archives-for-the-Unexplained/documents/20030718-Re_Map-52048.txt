       Selamat Jarin! We arrive, dear Hearts! Around you, the vast
  alteration of your world has been continuing for more than eight
  millennia. During the past two decades, this change began to

  series of agreements and the formation of a number of critical
  alliances, created out of the formerly unified cabals that were at the
  heart of the Anunnaki's earthly minions. As we have explained in
  earlier messages, these groups are now severely divided. A plan,
  designed by our earthly allies, is under way to ensure that the last
  remaining cabal, still dedicated to its old, evil ways, is stripped of
  its vast power. This project has taken far too long for our liking.
  However, a major part of this operation involves the re-education of
  all concerned. It is important for you to realize that our primary
  agenda remains first contact. In that context, we petitioned your
  Ascended Masters and duly requested that this present scheme be
  completed as soon as divinely possible. For this reason, we expect some
  astounding events to unfold quite soon.
       This series of actions is a major part of our present first
  contact scenarios. Nonetheless, we reserve the right to alter our main
  scenario. We are determined to transform your present reality and the
  severe restrictions it puts on you every day. The untold abundance that
  is capable of achieving this grows daily in value. Now, this
  unimaginable sum is about to be disbursed. This act will end the need
  for you to be accountable to your former masters. It will usher in a
  golden new era. It will transform your core perceptions and allow a new
  and freer world to be established. Among the many extraordinary changes
  will be our formal introduction, which has compelled us to enter into
  agreements with our earthly allies and wait for the divine right time
  in which to intervene, directly, in your affairs. To us, you are a
  society that has reached a special turning point in your development.
  For you, this crucial point is either to retreat into even greater
  darkness or to move forward and fully accept the Light. Your local
  Spiritual Hierarchy and this galaxy's heavenly Administrations
  graciously assure us that for you, the divine plan is to move fully
  into the Light.
       This brings us to what we are doing to promote these matters. We
  have already given you a brief overview of our galactic human culture.
  This culture is similar to that now found in Agharta. It is the last,
  true vestige of the culture of Lemuria. Its present mission is to
  observe surface world societies and to assist in their transformation
  toward the Light. To do so, we dispatched to the surface a number of
  secret emissaries and liaisons, whose task is to inform selected
  persons, powers and organizations of the divine messages given by the
  Ascended Masters to Agharta. Recently, these messages have been helped
  along by the actual intervention of your Ascended Masters. When they
  appear, the Ascended Ones will make it crystal-clear to anyone that a
  new world of Light is soon to dawn. In the past, the peoples of Agharta
  sheltered many who, escaping the surface world, sought their
  protection. They, too, yearn to free the surface world's societies from
  their present travails. Their desire has brought them into a close
  alliance with us.
       This alliance enabled us to establish many special subterranean
  bases across your globe. These bases form the core of a continual
  worldwide observation of your planet's covert and overt leaders. Right
  now, your world is caught in the midst of a final conflict between this
  last cabal and those who wish to see its powers terminated. Our task,
  and that of Agharta, is to see that this last cabal is stripped of its
  present power positions. To this end, we outlined various 'end-game'
  scenarios. Your Ascended Masters allowed them to be drawn up and the
  operation parameters for their success to be manifested. We wish you to
  know that what now has begun will not last much longer. Means are in
