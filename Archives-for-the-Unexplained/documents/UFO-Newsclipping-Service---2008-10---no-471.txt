                                                 2008 marks the centennial of an unprecedented world-wide wave of reports of mystery airships, aeroplanes,
                                                 "lights in the sky" and balloons.

                                                 Starting in Alaska and Texas in January 1908, the wave passed over Washington State in February, with
                                                 scattered reports from England and Scandinavia that Summer and New England in October. 1909 saw significant
                                                 numbers of reports from England (March-May), New Zealand (July-August), the southwestern US during the
                                                 Summer, and New England again in December, with reports from many places in the central US continuing into
                                                 August of 191 0.

                                                 These 1908-1910 events was a major event in proto-UFOiogy (i.e. pre-1947), equaling in duration the great 1896-
                                                 1898 Canada/US/Siberia wave and exceeding that wave in geographic scope.


                                                 To recognize the importance of this event, and hopefully to stimulate research, we'll be presenting clips --many
                                                 not previously published -- from this era over the next issues.
