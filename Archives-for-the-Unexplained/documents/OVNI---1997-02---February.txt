     The picture below, was taken at Livington Pike, Lancashire. Is it a secret aircraft in the shape
     of the 'Halo' or is it the Eurofighter undergoing trials?     Interestingly the photograph does
     show a delta shape, but one that has a conventional        look to it.   It also sports the greyish
     colour of your typical modem aircraft.


                                                                               (Photo: Sunday Mirror)
     The hill shown in the photograph give us more clues, as does the tower shown on the left. It.
     gives us some idea of scale and indicates that the object was of a typical fighter size
     (Eurofighter).      The fact that no noise was reported can be accounted for by the action of the
     surrounding hills in deadening the sound.          This fact has been noted previously on many
     occasions during aircraft exercises in the area.

     The picture could also be showing the underside of an aircraft, slightly angled as it swoops up
     and away, again out of sight. The picture could also show a distortion of the aircraft shape.
     Remember that the photographer had photographed the view from a distance and was not
     aware of the aircraft at the time.     The prime purpose of the photographer was to take the
     scenic view, not a moving aircraft.

     We have now gained information from a number of sources which confirm that the
     Eurofighter aircraft was airborne at that time and flying in that area on the day in question.

                        AN UNUSUAL LINCOLNSHIRE EVENT: 10 AUGUST 1996

         A family were on holiday at Trusthorpe on the Lincolnshire coast and as it was a fine night,
         the small group decided to go for a stroll along the coast.

         Mrs P relates what happened at about midnight: "When we reached the beach, we all stopped
         and looked into the sky, we had to crane our heads right back to see, but what we witnessed
         was amazing. We found ourselves watching a meteorite storm. We watched it for about an
         hour and a half It seemed that we could see a whole galaxy of stars, planets and meteorites.
         We must have counted over 20 shooting stars."

         "Then we witnessed a thunderstorm out at sea, we watched the sky light up, very low down
         below this cluster of stars, the sea was reflecting the lightning. It must have been some way
         out, as where we stood was cool, clear and dry. We noticed that there was no moon that night
         and it was very dark. We were alone in our spot, my husband, myself, my son and the dog, in
         that order, but other people must have been watthing, as later I saw some people coming
         towards us and could only just make them out in the blackness."

         "We had understood what we had seen so far....."

         "My husband saw the phenomenon first, as it appeared in the NE He described the objects as
         'coming close to' and he described the perspective as 'tree top height.'   "I watched the objects
         as they withdrew and soared higher. The flight path seemed to snake into the horizon, re­
         emerging and finally disappearing in the galaxy of stars." "It was as though there were three
         separate entities, each one a triangle, but they were in moving unison, forming three points of
         a triangle."

         "Each separate triangle was translucent with a hazy, but very bright light radiating almost from
         behind the objects, towards us." "In terms of size, it is hard to say, whatever we had seen, I
         did not feel that this phenomenon were alien space craft."

         "Since then, it could be construed that I have had a psychic experience, I've also had a
         dream, which although I know it to be only a dream, parts of it compare to what I've heard of
         people's so called 'abduction' stories." I've also been driven to spiritual enlightenment,
         however, I am a psychotherapist and this is more likely a part of my own growth process. But
         many things have formed a series of coincidences since the sighting. I believe that a time of
         enlightenment is on the horizon and what I saw might just be a sign!" "Make of it what
         you will."

Skywatch Diary                                                       by Carole Baker


       Have you noticed the number of television shows that have been on recently concerning UFOs and
abductions. I started to make a list the other day of things that we have recorded from the television
recently and I was amazed. The good thing is, these shows have not all been the "tabloid" kind. There
have been a couple of talk shows like "Leeza" who had experiencers on recently and "Charles Grodin"
who is supposed to have Whitley Strieber in the next few days talking about his new book ''The Secret
School" which was just recently published.     On the Discovery Channel recently shows with titles such
as "UFOs:Down to Earth", and the series on that channel "What If" have been aired. The Learning
Channel ran a show on Crop Circles and A & E had a really good show on in their Ancient Mysteries
Series, narrated by Leonard Nimoy called "UFOs:The First Encounter" which chronicled evidence from
ancient civilizations and texts of UFO contact in the d!stant past. This was a particularly well produced
and interesting program. Also A & E has a new series "The Unexplained" - these shows have had titles
like "UFOs:Reason to Believe" and "UFOs vs. The Government". All very interesting. Then of course,
there are a whole slew of regular TV series such as X-Files, Dark Skies, Psi Factor:Chronicles of the
Paranormal, Sightings, Star Trek, etc. etc.   Of course, Hollywood has been churning out its share of
UFO related movies including "The Arrival", "First Contact", "Independence Day", "Mars Attacks" etc.
etc. Remember when it was a real event when something UFO related appeared on the TV or in the
movies. Now it is a case of "Oh, there's another UFO show on Thursday, let's tape itf" I wonder what, or
perhaps more appropriately who has engendered this plethora of shows and movies, and to what end?


        I have it on good authority that there will be more vendor space than expected available at the
5th Anniversary Project Awareness Gulf Breeze UFO Conference to be held at the Beachside Resort on
Pensacola Beach on March 21st, 22nd and 23rd. So, if you wanted a table and could not get one, now is
the time to call.   This Conference seems to be growing of its own accord and looks like there will be
record numbers of attendees and vendors and of course a fantastic line-up of speakers. If you have not
made a reservation, please contact Project Awareness at (904) 438-8888 so that they can get the
information out to you.


        Another quiet week of skywatching at Shoreline Park. I was expecting a couple of calls on the
hotline this week, since myself and several friends witnessed a blimp flying north from the Bay over
Pensacola on Saturday, January 18th at approximately 6:30p.m. At first glance you can easily see how
an untrained eye would think "saucer" and "flashing lights". lt is only on closer, more careful
observation that you can see the gondola and pick out the distinctive noise of the engine. I guess
skywatchers in Pensacola have become well versed though, since no calls were received!


        If you have a sighting or unusual experience to report or you would wish to contact the
Experiencer Support Group, please call the Pensacola/Gulf Breeze MUFON Hotline at 438-3261. Keep
Looking Up!
