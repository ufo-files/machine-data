      ANOTHER BECKLEY EXTRAVAGANZA IN PHOENIX:

Conspiracies Conference" in Phoenix, Arizona. A new record of approximately
50 speakers were advertised, and if things keep going like this, the day may
come when Beckley has more speakers than attendees!
      A few of the lecturers arrived late or not at all, leading to the as­
tounding fact that your editor was asked to speak twice (for the same fee,
of course! ) We were breathtaking in our performances, as always.             _

an illness in her family, and America's answer to Socialism, Bill Cooper,who

      "...At a small UFO gathering in the sixties, I had been drinking beer
and watching a ball game on TV with the hostess's husband, while my magazine
partner was lecturing. The ball game over, we ambled into the lecture ques­
tion and answer period. One woman asked: How could we tell the difference
between an alien and a human? My partner replied: That is a loaded question.
At that point, I blurted in with: That's very simple. You can spot them at
the beach if you look closely. They have no belly buttons. I expected the
audience to laugh. They didn't. I continued: They lay eggs, much like some
species of life on this planet. Hence, their oval-shaped craft. I then re­
ceived a 'serious' round of applause! No wonder; my partner had been spiel­
ing some George Adamski stuff!
      "What do the old contactees and the abductees of today have in common?
No Proof! With the demise of Gray Barker and Ray Palmer, ufology would have
died with them, were it not for James W. Moseley, who keeps it alive   with
his ego, wit, sarcasm, indignity, etc., in his 'Saucer Smear'. I cheerfully
donate $10 to the Cause! Why don't the rest of you freeloaders do so too?"
