ANDREW LITAVSKY PRESENTS ARTWORK TO SOCIETY
      Andrew Litavsky, Chicago artist, presented to
the Society his painting of an Ancient Astronaut
baseci upon a drawing in Erich von Daniken's book
"The Go!d of The Gods", which depicts what appears
to be an astronaut holding a disc which may have
been a record or computer activator . The original
is a rock paintin~ near Fergana, Uzbekistan, USSR.
      Mr. Litavsky s work was featured in a display
at the Citizens Bank & Trust Company, Park Ridge,
Ill., and is currently on display at the World
Headquarters of the Society.

MASTHEAD LOGO
     The logo on the masthead was drawn by Editor
Richard T. Crowe, and depicts a gold trinket dis-
covered in Colombia, South America, which bears an
uncanny resemblance to a modern delta-wing jet
fighter. The problem, however, is that this trin-
ket was made one thousand years before the Wright
Brothers historic flight.                             Erich von Daniken         Photo by Mary Ann Kresin
