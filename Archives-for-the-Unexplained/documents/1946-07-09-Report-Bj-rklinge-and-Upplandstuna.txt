PHONE REPORTS

9/7						14-1430

Phone report from Miss Karin Andersson, Fredriksberg, Björklinge.

On July 9, between the hours of 14:00 to 14:30 o’clock, there were observations of a bright light, almost resembling bluish-white lamplight. Its front was noted as being significantly wider than the rear. The phenomenon had arrived from the southwest and went in a northeasterly direction, slightly above the treetops. Its speed was described as unremarkable.

CLASSIFIED

Miss Ingrid Sjöberg, Upplandstuna, reports having observed as a remarkable glow traveled in a northerly direction on Tuesday, July 9 between 14.00-15.00 o’clock.
