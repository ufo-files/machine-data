   THE MEMORY OF 'FLYING TRIANGLES' STILL DISTURBING
          DERB Y WOMAN TWENT Y YEARS LATER!

The 'Derby Telegraph' of the 7th May 20 15 carried a letter written by a Mrs MD Fleetwood of
Doncaster, enquiring if anyone could offer any information on her sighting of a black triangular object,
hovering over her house during the early 1990's ...... .


During the 1980's and 90's, I worked at Rolls-Royce on the switchboard and I lived in Harcourt Street,
Derby.


One particular night (I am coming up to 82 years old and can't recall the dates) I'd arrived home at
around 10.30pm and let the cat into the back garden, where she sat rigidly staring up into the sky. I
went to see what it was she was looking at and, above my head, what I saw was a huge black triangular
shaped object just hanging there, making no sound. It stretched over mine and the neighbours garden
either side of our house.


I immediately thought of the students who lived in a house a couple of doors down as they were
always up to something.

After looking up for what seemed like ages, I picked up 'Ginny' (the cat) and started backing towards
the back door, then still silently, the shape started to move slowly towards Gerard Street and then
swiftly up Burton Road.


I thought I was going mad and I told my son, who agreed!

Then within a couple of days, the Evening Telegraph reported that a lady walking her dog in Littleover
and two policemen driving home down Burton Road had also reported something similar.


I would really like to find out if anyone remembers this incident and can tell me when it happened.

                CANNOCK CLOSE ENCOUNTER SPARKS UFO ALERT

                             (From the Sunday Mercury 29 March 20 15)
                                                   (By Mike Lockley)




       Hundreds of householders reported hearing a loud drone, then spotting the massive object move
                                        slowly over their homes!
     A strange craft spotted moving slowly and loudly across the night sky has sparked off a UFO frenzy.

     Hundreds of Cannock (Staffordshire) householders took to social media after first hearing a loud drone,
     then spotting the massive object move slowly over their homes.

     Many believe that there is a very earthly explanation for a Close Encounter that has the community
     buzzing.

     Locals in the Staffordshire town believe they saw a secret military prototype, possibly from a US aircraft
     carrier currently stationed off Portsmouth.

     The British UFO Research Association has not discounted claims that a drone might be responsible for
     the rash of reports.

     One thing is for sure, the people of Cannock saw something!

     The area has been a UFO hotbed since the early 1980's but last weeks incident is the biggest in terms of
     the sheer volume of sightings.

     Shortly after the X Files incident at around 9pm on Tuesday (24th March), Facebook sites were awash
    with details of the UFO alert.

     One individual posted: "My house was rumbling and I'm still shaking. It was slow and it was huge."

     Another wrote: "Way too slow for fighter jets. It flew directly over our house, made a sharp left turn and
     carried on."

     The craft, sporting three red lights, was so low that many feared it was a plane in trouble.

    Paranormal investigator Lee Brickley witnessed the same phenomenon and began blogging as the drama
    unfolded. He described the thunderous rumbling as like a WW2 bomber.

    "Before anything could be seen with the naked eye, there was a deep and very loud droning" he said.

    "After about three minutes, the craft came into sight. It seemed incredibly large and astonishingly low in
    the sky, with three red lights that were noticeable."

    "The UFO travelled very slowly and many people thought it was about to crash."

    Lee claimed you could still hear the growl of the engines after the mystery machine disappeared."

    Cannock journalist Hannah Hiles, who has worked for the Sunday Mercury, was also alerted by the
    thunderous noise.                                                                      (Continued .... )

     military jet, It was very curious."

    years."

     why people see things in the sky."

      STRANGE UFO CRAFT SPOTTED OVER BIRMINGHAM 11th MAY

        (Mike Lockley: SUNDAY MERCURY May 17th 2015-Abridged Report by PRA)




     What's going on? A fleet of strange spacecraft have been spotted in the sky over Birmingham - by a

     And Mike Bedward has heard it through the grapevine that extraterrestrials are drawn to spectacular
     scenes, such as dazzling sunrises and rainbows. The Stechford singer didn't know what he'd inadver­
     tently caught on camera until he studied pictures of the rainbows. It Takes Two, apparently to attract
     aliens.


     The 44 year-old was intrigued by the shrapnel-type flecks against the rainbow, snapped at 6.30pm on
     Monday ( 1 1th May). Zooming in on the images revealed the shining silvers to be cigar-shaped crafts in
     the blue sky over Alum Rock, Birmingham. He also captured a strange vapour trail.


     Mike said "I was trying to take a picture of the rainbow-it was only later that I noticed the shapes. I'm
     not saying they are UFO's, although I am a believer. I'm saying I can't explain what I've pictured and
     neither can other people."


    "The crafts are rectangular and glow like strip lights. I've seen strange things in the sky before, but this
     is the first time I've had a camera with me." He added "I believe UFO's are attracted to beautiful things
     like rainbows."


     Birmingham UFO Group believe there's a decidedly earthly, even mundane explanation for Monday's
     phenomenon. Chairman Dave Hodrien has been quick to point out that "it's very rare that we see a
    picture and think 'Wow'. It's very rare that we see something that can't be explained."


    "In this instance the probable explanation is 'lens flare', a scattering of light in the lens system. It has
    nothing to do with the rainbow. It's caused by sunlight."
