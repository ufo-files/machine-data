E. SESSION SUMMARY
     The target area is an interface between "established"
wilderness or park land and examples of "civilization"; i.e.
parking lot, administration building, cars, bikes, etc.        It
contains a    large stone structure which has an historical
affiliation; this could be considered monumental. The site itself
has a religious/military aspect associated with it.

     There is a squarish, stone-lined canal or ditch.    There are
several sets or locations of coursed stone steps. One particular
set of steps or stairs are extremely steep and narrow (depth wise) .
They are reminiscent of the stone stair cases on a classic Mayan
temple e.g. Palenque or Tikal.

     There is a row of large, cube-shaped stone blocks which appear
to be embedded in the ground.     They are separated enough for a
person to walk between them. These act as a "separator" of sorts
between a grassy area and a large flat area in which portions feel
paved or prepared with concrete or brick. This target has lots of
angles; angles which feel carved out of stone.

     There is a circular, ring-shaped object with an interior
section which forms a depression. There is also a cone-shaped tube
which resembles the aft-end of a jet aircraft.        There is an
orangish sphere set against darkness or a dark field.

     There is a blocky two story building which feels wooden; maybe
constructed out of squared-off logs. There is a particularly nice
