   Evidences by Ada Botnarro ( May 2002 )

  This is what Ada reports: - at 21 May 2002 I was operated in the English Hospital at Nazareth. A night later, I woke up
  in my bed at the hospital, where I was connected to infusion.
  The room was dark. It was between 02:00 to 03:00, next to my bed I saw a blue light, like an opening window, and
  something like a key. It disappeared and later I saw a hand coming out of the window. It had 3 very long fingers, with
  something looked like a ball at their top. They were soil color, with very thin veins with darker shade.

  Suddenly I felt a stroke, like an energy that is difficult for me to explain ( this phenomena is familiar to me from my
  birthplace in Russia). Then the blue-lighted window disappeared. I couldn't sleep at all that night.

  After the operation, I was tranferred to the surgical department, there I had a dream.
  In my dream I saw stairs and a corridor with a strong light at its end. I saw there, aliens 1.2 meters high with long big
  hads. There were 20 or 30 of them, I felt they are very happy to see me .They had blue colored eyes.They didn't allow
  me to go down the stairs.

  I want to emphasize that there is such a thing- "Man in Black". Three times I saw vehicles with MIBs, the car window
  were sealed, and made from a metal-like material.
  I saw 3 man with a very thin woman with a big had.

  The man look Scandinavian (Nordic), high,sportive. One of them was about 35 years of age, and the 2 others seem to be
  younger then 25, they spoke to me, but I don't remember what they said.

   The azure light circle September 2002

  It happened in the first week of September at Hanni Rosen's home in Menahamia. Hanni reports for several years about
  presence in her home and extraordinary events, including body marks.

  She tells: "approximately at 05:20 I woke up without any reason, but without panic and with a good feeling. I asked
  myself why?. The window was closed, because of the air-condition. In less then 1 meter from me, on the wall,where
  some decorations hang, I saw a spot of bright and beautiful light. It was a circle, 90 centimeters at diameter. At the
  beginning, it's color was bright, and then it changed into bright azure. It was totally quiet.
  My 2 dogs were calm, also my son didn't woke up.


  The light didn't come from outside, not from the window, because the wall wasn't in front it . It was on the west wall,
  and the highway is at south direction.
  I rubbed my eyes. I felt happiness and excitement, the azure light was very beautifull.
  It seems to move out of the wall then turning slowly and fusing back with the wall. The light covered the decorations on
  the wall, then the shape of it changed and became a violin-symmetrical-shape.
  It's shape changed slowly, as giving me the opportunity to digest the sight. I often closed my eyes and opened them. I
  felt it won't be taking a long time, but I didn't want to miss anything of the sight.

  The "violin" shape lasted for 2 minutes, and all the show lasted for about 10 minutes. Then the light disappeared slowly
  and gently.
  All the time I had an excellent internal feeling. I returned to bed very happy.

  In the morning I felt very tired, but everything I did that day, was done with happiness.
  What is odd in this story, is the quiteness and that the dogs didn't bark."
