                              SIGMA DELTA EPSILON GRANT-IN-AID
                                                 Available for 1967-1968
                                                  To Women in Science

  This award, to be given annually by Sigma Delta Epsilon, Graduate Women's Scientific Fraternity, is available to any
woman who (a) holds a degree from a recognized institution of higher learning and (b) has demonstrated outstanding
ability and promise in research in one of the mathematical, physical, or biological sciences. The period of the grant shall
not exceed one calendar year. The $500 stipend may be applied either directly to the research project or to course work,
provided the latter is relevant to the research program. In previous years, three grants have been made.
  Appointments will be made irrespective of race, nationality, creed, or marital status. Preference will be shown to appli-
cants 35 years of age or older but this limitation is not obsolute.
  New application forms may be obtained from . Dr. Lois M. Hutchings, a Fellow of Western Reserve University School
of Medicine. Her home address is: 3384 Scranton Road, Cleveland, Ohio 44109. Applications should be submitted be-
fore February 1, 1967. Announcement of the award will be made April 1, 1967.
