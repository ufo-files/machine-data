                 EXTRACT OF LETTER FROM PROJECT OFFICER




"(a)   On twenty- four (24) hours notice from W. R.E. that plants will be
       required , they can be made ready and packed for onward despatch.
 (b)   The New Zealand Moss Lawn has not been tried here at Woomera because.
       of doubtful reports from Adelaide . It is very doubtful if this Moss
       would survive at Maralinga - firstly because of the climatic
       conditions, and secondly and most important, water supply. It is
       believed that the water supply contains a high percentage of salt.
       It is therefore suggested that a grass known as Salt Water Paspalum
       be tried . On 21st . October 1959 the following information was
       received from Director of Adelaide Botanic Gardens:-
       "These runners should be planted immediately in much the same way
       as couch grass runners are planted and approximately the same
       distance apart . As soon as growth appears a light dressing of
