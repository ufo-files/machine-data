               ourselves.

               fill our thoughts. Love, laughter, and celebration will fill us with
               Creator's joy and we will simply "know" everything will be all right; that
               the change has occured, and the new a-e was here.
               Our memories will be cleared of past traumas, hurts, grievances, pain. We
               will simply "know" our frequencies have been upgraded and we are ready to
               change, to tune into the new that is being created - that we are helping to
               create.
               We have worked so hard to upgrade ourselves and change out of third
               dimensional vibrations of duality. At Ascension the change will permanantly
               take affect. Many new thoughts on how to live in this new level will assail
               us, but guidance will be provided from those who have gone before. We will
               not be abandoned. But many new vistas will be opened to us.
               The slate will have been wiped clean. Only positive choices will prevail.
               Many new vistas will be open to us. Planet wide these changes will occur.
               Everyone will be given the chance to live in the ascended frequencies. It is
               up to each s-ul whether to accept and integrate the new frequencies or to

               children, family members, friends......all will make their individual choice
               from the so-l level. Eventually another opportunity will open for Ascension
               for those who choose not to advantage themselves of this opportunity.
               I wish I could show you pictures of the clearness and brightness of the
               visions I was given, but I am at a loss for words and not an artist. I did
               not see much in the way of concrete structures, nor any fossil fuel vehicles
               anywhere, but assume housing and transportation would be provided. I did see
               beautiful trees and flowers, many animals and millions of people in
               attitudes of great joy. All appeared to have physical bodies, although none
               seemed to be misshapen, ill or infirm. I did not take that to mean that the
               old or infirm had not ascended, but rather they had shed any outer
               distortions and the true beauty of the form an soul had been restored.
               I was so ecstaticly happy to be in the ascended state that I did not want to
               return to 3-D even for a little while, but was told I needed to come back to
               let others know what to look forward to.
