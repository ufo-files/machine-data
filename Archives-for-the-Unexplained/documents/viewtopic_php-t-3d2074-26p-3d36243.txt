  What happened to "Project Djedi"?

    by suspicioso » Mon May 02, 2011 10:44 pm

  Last heard of in October 2010, "Project Djedi" was a collaboration between Zawi Hawass' team in Egypt and Leeds
  University to send a robot into the shafts in the Queen's Chamber of the Great Pyramid, and drill through the doors to
  see what was behind it:

  http://www.drhawass.com/blog/djedi-team-robot

  There was also a little bit of press information about the project:

  http://www.independent.co.uk/news/scien ... 46506.html

  However, Dr. Richardson's personal site and department site don't mention this project at all.

  http://www.personal.leeds.ac.uk/~menrcr/

  http://www.engineering.leeds.ac.uk/cgi- ... funit%253dIESD

  I can imagine all kinds of scenarios that might put things on hold - funding issues, problems with travel around the time
  of the popular revolt in January - but it seems a bit odd that there is absolutely no information post the press releases
  back in October 2010, and nothing at all at Leeds.

  Anyone know anything more?
