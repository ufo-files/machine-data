Cairns Post (Qld. : 1909 - 1954), Thursday 3 June 1954, page 1



          "FLYING SAUCER"
             SIGHTINGS
        FALL      OFF      IN  U.S.   SINCE
                         1952.

           WASHINGTON,                 June        1
        (A.A.P.)-Air            Force-    officials
       said to-day that reports of "fly-
       ing saucers" had              fallen      off
       swiftly since 1952
           They believed more scientific
       investigating and           better   public
       education        were     responsible.
           So    far this year, only              87
       "sightings" had           been    reported
       but    the    "flying saucer       season"
       -this coincided with the July
       August        meteor        showers-was
       just coming        in. Reports of "fly-
       ing saucers" increased                when
       meteors      were     most common.
           The    Air    Force     said   it   still
       was     unable     to explain about        10
       per     cent-    of the   sightings.
