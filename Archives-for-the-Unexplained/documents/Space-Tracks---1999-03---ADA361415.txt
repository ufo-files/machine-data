                      Teaching Core Values
            Lessons From A Cave
     Lt. Michael Scott, assistant operations officer for
    Fleet Surveillance Support Command, accompa-
   nied his son's Boy Scout troop on a recent caving
     expedition. In one of Lt. Scott's photos from the
trip, a Scout and a professional cave guide pause in
      a tunnel. Lt. Scott has been actively involved in
     scouting from the time his son joined as a Tiger
   Scout. According to Lt. Scott, it's a natural exten-
 sion of his work to volunteer with the Scouts. "The
       Navy starts you out with a good base in honor,
    courage, and commitment. The Boy Scouts start
  out by instilling core values from day one." For Lt.
  Scott, helping teach his son and other Scouts core
    values is like "being a light in a pitch-dark cave."
