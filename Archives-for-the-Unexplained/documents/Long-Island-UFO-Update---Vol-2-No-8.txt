                   In the early spring LIUFON received several reports that
             indicated that there was a sudden increase in UFO activity in
             the Long Island area after a somewhat mild period of activity
             over the winter months. It appeared that something was up. Just
             like Holmes would remark to Watson,"Something's a foot." It
             appeared that there was something being seen in the skies during
             the evening hours in the metropolitan area.
                   The first indication that something was up involved a
             sighting that was mentioned in earlier,"Updates", that occurred
             in early June in Kings Park. That involved a sighting by one
             of our member's teenage son of a oval or cigar shaped object
             that appeared on the night of June 9th. It dipped down from
             the night sky and displayed sparks and a bright red light and
             disappeared as suddenly as it had appeared.
                   On the night of June 11th, 1991 a large oval object was
             reported over Hicksville which was seen and followed by a car
             load of retired woman telephone workers around the hour of 10:30
             P.M. to 10:45 P.M. that night. It was also seen by hospital
             workers at the local Hospital. It slowly depai:'ted to the North
             West and had blinking white and red lights on it. It was also
             totally silent.
                   Then there came a report in late June near West Islip by
             a young woman of a large bright cigar shaped object that changed
             shape and illuminated the sky to the South West. She watched
             it for some twenty minutes before. it too disappeared. The object
             was never identified, although she called the Vanderbilt
             Planetarium which told her it was the Moon. By the way, the
             moon was located in the opposite direction that night.
                     Then reports came in about a large cigar shaped object
              seen in Bayside at 1:00 A.M. on the morning of July 5th over
              the water near the Bayside Marina, which was slowly moving East
              ward. It was described as large, self illuminated and silent.
              Next, on July 14th at 8:30 P.M. an incident was reported to
              us about a large cigar shaped object being seen over the Meadow
              Brook Parkway near the toll bridge to Jones Beach. The large
              silent, ill4minated cigar shaped object was visible to hundreds.
              It hovered over the area, lighting up the early evening sky
              when it lifted straight up and was gone.
                    Reported to us in August was an incident of a large c.irculor
              object seen on August 9th near Jamesport along Herricks Road.

              to check out verify that there were no involvement of any blimps

              if not all the incidents •.•The Met Life, Fugi, and Virgin Light
              Ship blimps were either on the ground, someplace else or were

              September 1st. It was reported sighted an hour after the Met
