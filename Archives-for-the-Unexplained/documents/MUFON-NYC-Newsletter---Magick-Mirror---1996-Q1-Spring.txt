                        BUGHOUSE SQUARE
Closely adjacent to the Newbury Rare Book Library on the near north
 side of Chicago, there used to be a public park where people were
   allowed to stand and give speeches on any topic they pleased
   --so long as their presentation did not offend basic community
standards of human decency. This park was called by local residents
                         " Bughouse Square".


It is important that, in a free society, there does exist a public forum
 in addition to intellectual clubs and libraries which are restricted by
                  membership fees and regulations.


    The purpose of the MAGICK MIRROR WEB SITE will be to
                 collect data on anomalous situations
    and supernatural occurrences, as well as UFO experiences.
           You will not have to be a card-carrying member
            with a special code ID in order to participate.
  Like the poetry readings here in New York's Greenwich Village,
we will run the MAGICK MIRROR WEB SITE on voluntary donations
            and subscriptions to the hard copy edition of
                    MAGICK MIRROR MAGAZINE
       which will contain data logged into the web site "park",
                 and also articles from other sources.
