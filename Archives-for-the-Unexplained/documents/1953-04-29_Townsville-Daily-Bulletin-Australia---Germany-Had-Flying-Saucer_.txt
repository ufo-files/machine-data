Townsville Daily Bulletin (Qld. : 1907 - 1954), Wednesday 29 April 1953, page 5


         GERMANY   HAD
        FLYING SAUCER?
          BERLIN,        April 28. —George
        Klein,   war-time aviation ad-
        viser, claimed Germany      ex-
       perimented with the first fly-
        ing saucer-shaped planes in
       1945.  He said the first crew-
       carrying flying    saucer   was
       tested at Prague on February
       15, 1945.  It  reached    40,300
       feet in three minutes,      and
       flew at 1375 miles    per hour.
       Protype     and      plans   were   de-
       stroyed.
