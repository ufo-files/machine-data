    Our Review                                                                                 Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  A biography of the clairvoyant by the journalist who knew him best. Cayce was a psychic known for his
  trance readings, especially on the medical conditions of people far away. Being written by a Cayce
  partisan, it is likely to be biased in his favor, but the book also appears to contain a lot of useful
  biographical information. This is probably the first book to read on Cayce and his claims. -- Glenn
  Campbell
