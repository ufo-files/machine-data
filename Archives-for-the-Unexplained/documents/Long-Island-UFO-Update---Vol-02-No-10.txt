that has a contract to. transport mail for the u.s. Post Office.

surrounded by residential and business areas. The gentleman
who wishes to remain anonymous, but whom we will call David
reported that as he turned and looked towards the direction
of the Bronx across the river he observed an unusual object.
      He reported that he saw a pulsating, blue-white object
appear on the horizon rising above the Bronx. It appeared to
be emitting a powerful blue, pulsating light, but appeared to
be a solid object. It was considerably large in size and gave
the appearance of being oval in shape. It rose at an angle on
a heading of 150 to 170 degrees magnetic on a North to South
East course. As it rose it began to accelerate to a speed that
was estimated by the witness to be supersonic in performance.
The witness watched the object during its performance and never
lost sight of it. In seconds it disappe9Ied as it past overhead
to the South East.                                            .....
      The witness, who is a licensed pilot, has stated that the
object was not any type of known aircraft that he is familiar
with. It should also have been on La Guardia's radar, since
its heading should have put it on their radar plots.
      LIUFON has checked with contacts at the FAA who have been
unable to verify any unusual air traffic that morning. The
witness has filed a formal report with us. The case has been
marked for further investigation, but has been given a high
degree of credibility.

        into a flap of high intensity since the fall of 1989. This summer

        began keeping track of an elusive object that appeared over
        the Causeway Bridge. Beginning in 1990, Ufologists, media and
        local residents reported an object that appeared as an intense
        whitelight, which has appeared over the bridge since last year
        and has been observed and reported by the local press. This
        August, it was again seen, but with a different twist. Appearing
        as an intense red light, it appeared over the bridge and was
        observed to come lower and nearer. On this occasion and over
        several nights, it was photographed and reported to appear as
        a circle of intense lights and was also photographed and observed
        to have a ring of what appeared to be port hole lights. These
        sightings appear to continue and have caused nightly expeditions
        to stake out the Causeway Bridge. The activity in Gulf Breeze
        continues. ( LIUFON will feature a special report on the activity
