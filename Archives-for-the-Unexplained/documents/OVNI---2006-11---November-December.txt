                      FURTHER THOUGHTS ON TRIANGLES & UFO'S
                                                By Bill Foster


It has been almost a year since our book: 'THE BLACK TRIANGLE ABDUCTION' was
published. For those of you \vho have not read the book, (shame on you}, a brief synopsis is necessary.

In November of 1996 tny wife, tnyself and another couple were taken inside our car, up into the belly
of a massive flying triangle. The car came to rest on a seemingly slate or granite floor. We were in a
massive structure that appeared to me like the deck of an aircraft carrier with a domed roof on it.

I saw fourteen gray aliens surrounding the car and all four of us were floated out of the vehicle and
taken in separate directions. My wife and I, at the least, were examined extensively. I subsequently
found out, through over two years of regression hypnosis, that I had been abducted at least thirteen
times, over tny lifetime. That is from age five through age sixty-two!

The book details much of this during our years of hypnosis. Many details have slowly come back to
me over the past six years. The remembrances start as a vague fog and then crystallize over time.
Here then, some of the items that were not in the book.

CURVATURE:

Every wall, deck, doorway, room, table and bench that I ever saw aboard any alien vessel was always
rounded. There was never a straight edge or a sharp or square corner.

If you think back to the "art deco�' period of the early 195 0's you will remetnber, if only from movies,
that all of our furniture, lamps, dishes, glasses and coffee tables were round or rounded. If you were
wealthy or trendy during that period of time your home was filled with lots of a new invention called
PLASTIC. Plastic was most commonly molded then into round or oblong shapes. For us it was a
modern style. For the aliens?

Imagine for a moment a veterinarians' examination room. There is a stainless steel table in the center
of the room sitting atop a pedestal, which rests on a tile or linoleum floor.

Keep that picture in mind but make a few changes. The examination table is seven feet long. The ta­
ble has all rounded edges. There are no seams, no joints, no dents and no flaws.

The table looks to be stainless steel but since it is all one piece it could be just a shiny, gray, metal
color of some other material. The table rests on a pedestal but that pedestal is also one solid piece of
the table as though cast from one mold; same color and round. The entire structure melts into the floor
flawlessly and the floor is as solid as thick glass blocks would be only appearing to the eye as granite
or slate.

The room is round, the table is oblong, and the cabinets are rounded, as is the doorway into the small
rootn. Even the ceiling is domed as if the entire room were made through one giant, contiguous piece
by injection molding. If you can picture this you now know what every room I ever saw aboard ship
looked like.

Some rooms were larger and some rootns did not have examination tables but all rooms had the same
rounded features. Incidentally, no doorway I ever saw had a door. Each doorway had an arch and
curved to a domed hallway. Ho\v can something so massive be constructed with no square, angles and
no straight lines? The walls and ceilings appeared translucent and thin as plastic sheeting would be but
with an internal light source. That brings us to lighting.

BRIGHT LIGHTS:

In the book, my description of bright lighting conditions left a lot to be desired. Words seetn to fail to
convey the intensity of the lighting throughout the ship yet the brightness did not hurt my eyes. The
lighting cast absolutely no shadows whatsoever, leading me to believe that it was coming from a
variety of sources.

In retrospect I believe the light source may have been the walls and ceilings themselves! If you
had the capability to evenly and internally light all the walls and ceiling of a room it would
cast no shadows because the light source would be coming from five sides. (Assuming a
square room.)

Of course this begs the question, how would you evenly light an entire wall, let alone the
whole room? The walls appeared so thin to my eyes that the entire panel or material itself
would have to be a solid, light source. There was another strange quality.

Have you ever seen a Chinese lantern? The light inside the lantern is self-contained. That is, it
does not cast light out like a spotlight or flashlight. The light inside the lantern appears to
glow, evenly, throughout the inside of the lantern.

The walls appeared to be lit as a glow but with the brightness pumped up much beyond our
comprehension of brightness.

Even if you stuck your face an inch from a 60-watt bulb the brightness would still hurt your
eyes. This brightness did not hurt. In the examination room itself, the aliens did bring a light
down from the ceiling that hurt my eyes very much from an even more extreme brightness.

This leads me to believe that the grays might have sight     problems since the examination is
conducted under such caustically white-bright conditions. Even though the grays eyes are
considerably larger than ours could it be that they have limited vision?

SIZE-SHIFfiNG:
One of the most amazing statements made by the grays to me was that they could change the
physical size of any part, or all, of the ship to suit their needs. The exact quote to me telepathi­
cally was: "We can make this (ship) any size we want."

After our 1996 abduction I became a voracious reader of UFO and abduction books. I
distinctly remember reading the famous 1947 Roswell, New Mexico saucer crash. When the
second crash site was discovered, (first debris at Roswell and the intact saucer, near Socorro)
one of the first military people to look inside the ship said a strange thing.

To paraphrase, he said: "The inside of this thing looks much, much bigger than it does on
the outside."

That particular saucer was thought to be about thirty feet in diameter, viewed from the out­
side. When military personnel took turns, looking inside, it seemed many times over, larger.
How can this be?

Could it be that in addition to the aliens' ability to physically change the entire size of an
object that the aliens can change internal perception from external perception? This was not
an isolated case of perception differences.

Many abductees tell of being escorted into small saucers appearing to be only fifteen to twenty
feet in diameter. Once inside the vessel these folks exclaimed that the inside was very large.
One particular man's report stated that the ship was not tall enough to accommodate his 6'2"
frame.

As he viewed it from the outside, he thought from a quick glance, it looked about five feet tall.
Once inside, this fellow said the ceiling was still a couple of feet over his head! How can this
be?

"think" they see a size difference there is a physical or dimensional difference.
Many abductees have walked through and around a ship, which appeared small while outside,
but when walking more than ten or twenty steps inside the craft, proved to these folks that the
ship just had to be physically larger inside, than was seen from the outside.

TRIANGLE DOCKING:

ever heard a sound or felt any moven1ent within the craft.

   1.   How could there have been time enough for this craft to take me somewhere in space to

One thing appears certain about all of these oddities. Our current earth technology is hundreds

I have many other remembrances that are scattered throughout the recesses of my brain but I

EARTH CHANGES- By Paul Usher (PRA)

It does look as though the Earth has natural cycles and we cannot stop these. We must, it seems, expect
extremes of climate from time to time. The worry is, will our way of living drive these extremes over
the top? Nobody knows the answer to this for sure, but what we do, does have an impact on the climate.
Green house gases might push things a little too far and some predict a runaway effect, not a very nice
scenario with temperatures predicted to be unsurviveable. Green house gases have actually been
responsible for our nice warm and comfortable climate of late, without them things would be quite cold ,
an Ice-age! A balance must be maintained, that i s what the boo-ha is all about. . . . a balance! Nobody
knows how to do this, so I guess we will be finding out shortly, what our contribution to the global
balance might be.
The sun is getting hotter naturally, so we will get warmer, we can counter that to a small extent by
curbing our emissions, meanwhile, a toasting is inevitable. Mars will warm up and release whatever
water is available and thus become more habitable, as will some of the moons in our solar system.
Europa may have an ocean waiting to thaw, there may already be liquid water and life supported by
geothermal activity, though not very likely. It seems mankind must eventually reach out and leave this
world, but not for another million years or so. Meantime we should see extremes of climate change, Ice­
ages and Poles melting, the lot !
