July 1: The US Navy in Gothenburg

On July 1 the first American naval visit to Gothenburg since 1938. Destroyers Washington and Perry, the cruiser Little Rock anchor in the Swedish port on the North Sea. The three ships continue for Malmö, Copenhagen and Stockholm. (Stockholms-Tidningen, July 2).
