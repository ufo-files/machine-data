                 COVERT FLYING TRIANGLES ARE NOT ALL UFO's
There is increasing speculation that the British Aerospace industry is undertaking a test flight
programme on one or more types of Stealth aircraft from their base at Warton in Lancashire.
We have received information from the Lancashire UFO Society, that a small black triangular
craft approx. 3Oft in length has been test flown along the Lancashire coast during the last 18
months and has been seen by a number of LUFOS members.

The excellent work .carried out by the LUFOS organisation has established to their
satisfaction, that a prototype unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) is up and flying and has been
designated 'HALO' (High Altitude Low Observable).            This aircraft, together with its 'chase'
plane, a Tornado, has been seen by numerous witnesses in recent weeks.

However, there is one claim made by LUFOS that is somewhat presumptuous and that is, their
statement that all 'Flying Triangles' seen in Northern skies are of terrestrial origin. Conversely,
as a responsible UFO study group, we do not agree with their statement that, 'Ufologists
insist that all triangular craft are of ET origin'. We always consider the possibility that a
man-made craft of delta wing design may be responsible.           However since the passing of the
Avro 707a, 707b, Boulton Paul 1 1 1, Fairey Delta 2 and various other delta wing aircraft,
including the Avro Vulcan, there is a marked absence of alternative possibilities.

During a BBC Radio Derby broadcast on the 15th of January, your Editor was invited to
respond to a number of claims made by Tim Matthews of the LUFOS group.                 Tim claimed
that the HALO' craft was probably responsible for all Flying Triangle sightings, this included:

(a) All Flying Triangle incidents that took place during the 'Belgian Wave' of 1989/90.
(b) The UFO incident filmed by Daryl Spence over Mansfield on the 23/8/94, showing two
helicopters intercepting a UFO.    Tim Matthews commented that the 'HALO' controllers had
apparently forgotten to inform military sources that they would be flying in that area!

(c) The FT encounter with a British Airways airliner on the 6th January 1995 (British
Aerospace employees informed Tim, that it was a 'HALO' craft that had temporarily gone
out of control).
(d) All Flying Triangles overDerby, including one that hovered over a traffic Island during the
1994/5 flap. (At this stage PRA member Jonathan Frank/in rang in to dispute this fact,
stating that his sighting of 1619196 could not be explained by a man-made craft).



                                                  l
Your Editor drew attention to the fact that FT craft are currently being observed in many
countries around the world, particularly in the United States and that FT's are in no way
confined to British air space. Mr Matthews responded by indicating that the Americans were
flying similar craft. During the programme he went on to comment that 'Nick Pope knew
nothing about UFO's' and that Tony Dodd (the senior Quest International investigator), had
doubts over the recent Scottish landing case!

The assumption that all FT craft can be accounted for by covert HALO' operations,
completely ignores the research that has been carried out on the subject of 'Flying Triangles',
particularly the work of Victor J. Kean.     Victor has amassed a large database on FT activity,
logging over 1,900 incidents in 1995 and well over 1,000 so far for 1996.      His plotting of FT
tracks, shows that in general, they approach our shores from the North Sea, cross the country,
taking particular interest in Nuclear Power Stations and then return back over the East coast.
(a graph showing Victor's latest findings is included in this issue).

In recent weeks there have also been rumours that media information on 'Flying Triangles' has
been restricted by means of a Government             'D Notice'.   As we understand it, this is a
censorship restriction on any news relating to 'Flying Triangle' craft.          In view of the
information received from the LUFOS group that a new secret triangular Stealth aircraft is
being developed, the 'D Notice' could have a logical explanation. In no way connected with
alien craft of a similar design.

The well-known author and researcher Timothy Good, recently told us that if a 'D Notice'
was in operation concerning FT information, then as a journalist, he would have known
about it (and he has not)!

If government 'sources' were intent on feeding dis-information to UFO researchers, with the
intention of destabilising their research, then what better than to take advantage of the current
triangular craft research.   They could then circulate a story that the FT's were not alien craft
but were really a secret British aircraft.    Therefore there would be no need for anyone to
waste their time investigating such reports, as the Flying Triangle craft could be logically
explained!

Tim Matthews has produced a most interesting publication called 'Stealth, Lies and
Videotapes'. I have not yet studied it in full, but I can vouch for the fact that the statement on
page 1, para. 4. "and it is known that Professor (my italics) John Searl ofD.I.S.C. flew saucer­
shaped objects in Britain in the late 1960's/early 1970's using similar means of propulsion, the
'Searl Effect Generator' (5)" is a wild claim to make (the Editor having attended one of John
Searl's demonstrations).

It does appear that Tim does hold extreme views about Ufologists. PRA members who
attended a Rugeley, Staffordshire UFO meeting last year, were somewhat taken aback, when
Tim Matthews announced that his house was being spied on by Ufologists, hiding in unmarked
vans!

We acknowledge the excellent work that Tim is doing in the field of British Stealth aircraft,
but his claim that they can probably account for all 'Flying Triangle' reports should be viewed
with some circumspection.

            THE ' Ff ' LANDING IN SCOTLAND: IS THERE A COVER UP?

In last month's OVNI we reported the alleged landing of a huge 'Flying Triangle' craft at
Falklands Hill, in Fife, Scotland.    We have heard from a number of sources that this incident
has been widely reported by the news media in the USA and yet there appears to have been no
mention of it by the British Press.

The story was covered in detail by the 'UFO Magazine' , produced by Quest International and
one of the best UFO publications now available.       A synopsis of the up-dated story, which
includes new information supplied by David Colman of the Scottish Unexplained Phenomena
Research organisation, is produced below.
On the night of September 23rd 1996, after two initial sightings of a huge FT at low altitude,
four witnesses returned to the scene to be met with the sight of a huge Flying Triangle which
had landed on the ground. Two women witnesses accompanied by a boy and a girl, watched
mesmerised as they saw multi coloured lights shooting from a pulsating blue glowing object.
They then realised that there were small figures moving around the object.                          They were also
able to identify a number of taller figures estimated to be seven feet tall.                  The smaller figures
were seen to be carrying small boxes and tubular canisters around in the vicinity of some
nearby trees. The taller figures appeared to be directing the smaller figures in their work. They
appeared to be working in groups and were going in and out of the woods.

Shortly afterwards the witnesses were approached by what they described as cocoons, which
contained one of the small beings. Within a few moments there were dozens of the cocoons
hovering only a few feet away from them. The witnesses panicked and drove away from the
scene quickly. When they arrived home, they realised that they had 'lost time'.

It was only later that the witnesses began to recollect what had happened to them.                                    They
remembered that the 'beings' had very big dark eyes and that their heads looked too big for
their bodies. They also remembered that the 'FT' craft was triangular in shape and had a dome
on top. It had two large white lights, which shone beams onto the ground.

Although the story has been extensively covered in the UFO Magazine (Jan/Feb. issue), one of
the witnesses who spoke to David Colman, commented "I have only given you the tip of the
iceberg"!

                       CHRISTMAS EVE TRIANGLE SIGHTING

Mr Tom   Culleton, the Post Office driver who has already experienced a number of FT
encounters, had another experience at 0115hrs on the 24th December last year.
Tom noticed that his radio was beginning to "kick up a din" as he drove off the A3 8, while on
his usual run travelling from Derby to Burton-on-Trent. Tom's radio was crackling as it had
done previously just before a Flying Triangle encounter.
After visiting the sorting office in Burton, Tom drove through the town on the next stage of
his journey which would take him along the A50 to Swadlincote. He pulled up at a set of
traffic lights near Falcon close and was set to cross the bridge and head up the hill out of the
town. He remembers that he was about 200yds from the bottom of the hill and was stationary
at the traffic lights.
It was then that Tom noticed a bright light approaching from the East (this is the same
direction as his previous sighting of 3rd May 96).' He could see the light to the right of the
castle on the hill. Tom was soon able to make out a large black shape associated with the
light, which had now become visible as three white lights on each corner of a large black FT.
Tom noted that the white lights appeared to be rotating.
As he sat there watching, the large black FT banked over and silently flew back along its
original approach course, heading towards the East until it was out of sight.

Skywatch Diary                                                        by Carole Baker

        The sighting took place at approximately 5:50 p.m. on Friday, November 22nd, 1 996. Weather
conditions were cloudy and drizzling with the temperature around the sixty degree mark. The sighting
took place over a power sub-station at the junction of Jernigan Road and JoJo Road, in north Pensacola.
Adjacent to this site is a church, and this is where the witnesses observed from.


        Two witness were driving to a local mall from their home in north-west Pensacola when one of
them was paged. She knew there was a telephone she could use at her Church, and drove directly there
to return the page. As the two witnesses drove up to the Church outbuilding where the telephone was,
one of them noticed a large bright orange light over the power sub-station. "I was joking around saying
it was a UFO! " the witness told me. The second witness the saw the light and they stopped the car. Still
watching the light, one of the witnesses tried to use, the telephone in the Church building. "lt was all
just static" the witness states "you could not get anything but static". Meanwhile, as one witness was
trying to use the telephone, the other witness, carrying the pager decided to go toward the power sub­
station to get a closer look at the light. "I just wanted to see what it was" she told me. "lt was perfectly
round and just hovering there, low above- the power thingi'. . As she walked toward the sub-station, the
pager began acting strangely, vibrating and beeping at the same time (which it is not designed to do) and
no numbers were showing up on it. The other witness saw what her friend was doing and became
hysterical, calling the second witness back. Together again in the doorway of the Church building, the
two witnesses watched at the central ball of light began to interact with the power sub-station. "There
were sparks, like from. a sparkler just raining down from this big orange ball of light to the sub­
station. Then as we watched, two smaller balls of light, the same color, kind of rolled out from the big
one and hung there next to it." One of the witnesses estimates that the large ball of light was about two
inches across at arm's length and the other, smaller lights, were about half that size. The witnesses
both went on to describe how they watched for over five minutes as the small balls of light would merge

 back with the larger ball and then separate from it again. This occurred three or four times. "When
 they went back in, the sparks started going up to the big ball instead of raining down" one witness
 stated. "You know there was something intelligent controlling the thing, because every time a car would
 come by and shine it's headlights, the whole thing would disappea�, like someone flipped a switch, then
 when the car went by, it would switch back on again" , the witness who approached the sub-station told
 me "As I got closer to it, it was just one big ball at that time, I 'could see it was very bright, but there
 was a darker ring around the outside of it. I could not see any structure." After watching this display
 for about two and one half to three minutes the two original witnesses were joined by a third. From the
 third witness' description, it would appear that by this time· the UFO was moving away, beyond the sub­
 station. "lt looked to ine to be about a mile beyond the power plant thing" the third witness states. "I
 could see lights in a triangle shape". This witness described the size at arm's length as being about one
 inch across. "lt looked to me like they were in a perfect triangle, all sides the same".    After the three
 witnesses watched amazed for another approximately two and one half minutes, all three concurr that
 "... it just went out and never came back on". Immediately after the final disappearance of the UFO, the
 pager returned to its normal functions and the telephone line became clear.


        The UFO was not more than 80 yards away from all three witnesses. The one witness who ran
 toward the power sub-station, was at one point much closer than that, but just how close is not possible
 to determine.. The witness cannot remember how close to the sub-station she got before her friend
 called her back. "We were excited. My friend was freaking out, so I ran back. I'm not sure how close I
 got to it" she told me.

ANOMALOUS OBJECT SPOTTED IN DEEP SPACE

On Thursday, November 14, 1996, at 6 p.m., Cluck Shramek, an amateur atronomer fromHouston, Texas was
observing the Comet Hale-Bopp in his backyard telescope when he spied an anomalous object seemingly near the
comet.

Shramek described the object as "S atum -shaped, self- luminous" and "four-and-a-half times larger than the Earth." He
immediately photographed that area of space and obtained a clear image ofHale-Bopp with the highly-luminous UFO
at a two o'clock position relative to the comet.

Shramek's photo can be seen at the following URL-- http://paradise.dpl.net.com/shram/comelhtm.

On Friday, November 15, Shramek appeared as a guest on the Art Bell Show and described the strange object he had
seen. Art Bell's broadcast ignited an immediate debate among scientists.

Astronomer Terence C. Platt identified the object as a "nearby star, SA0141894, which is sufficiently bright to show
diffraction images .. .Several spikes are often seen when imaging bright stars in a Newtonian reflector. SA0141894 is
listed as magnitude 8.5 but it is of spectral type M and this gives it a very bright infrared excess."

Another astronomer, David W. Knisely, said the object might be "another star at that location--HD162115," which is a
magnitude 9.9 star. "But," he added, "I think that this object may be a geostationary satellite which just happened to
get a sun flash off a solar panel."

colours, which shone out."
"I ran downstairs and grabbed a pair of binoculars and called for my family to take a look

underside of the object appeared to have coloured cells, giving it the red, orange, blue, yellow
and green lights, which dimmed and shone like a pulse of beating lights."
"I again shouted down the stairs for my family to observe it, however when I returned to the
window it had gone."

THE 'OZ' FACTOR is a word coined by Jenny Randles to cover the strange silent and
detached period experienced by witnesses while in the close proximity of a UFO. It has been
considered that this effect might be explained by some kind of physiological shock brought on
by the sight of a UFO, but a case recently reported to us has discounted this explanation.

1991. The driver parked his lorry at a weighbridge and went to see if anyone was about. "I
was about 2/3 of the way across the yard when I became aware that something was behind
me, it wasn 't a noise, but complete silence. " "I turned my head around and caught sight of a
fast moving light in the sky, moving North. Within a second it stopped and then accelerated
vertically out of sight." "At a rough guess I would say that it was about a 1/4 mile away and
the size of a small van." ..... . . . .. . .... Strange indeed
