      THE PHENOMENON BY WHICH CERTAIN PHYSICAL TRANSFORMATIONS OCCUR AS A
      RESULT OF THE DIRECT ACTIONS OF A PERSON'S THOUGHTS OR CERTAIN
      STATES OF CONSCIOUSNESS (ASC)      IS CALLED PSYCHOKINESIS (PK). SINCE
      THIS TYPE OF PHENOMENON (PARANORMAL PHENOMENON) TRANSCENDS THE
      COGNIZANCE OF THE FIVE SENSES AND FURTHERMORE SINCE THE APPEARANCE
      OF SUCH PHENOMENON IS INITIMATELY INTERDEPENDENT ON THE PHYSIOLOGY
      OF THE PERSON POSSESSING PARANORMAL ABILITIES, SUCH PHENOMENON TEND
      TO BE LIMITED IN UNIVERSALITY AND OBJECTIVITY AND AS A RESULT
      REMAINS UNACKNOWLEDGED THROUGHOUT THE WORLD BY PEOPLE IN GENERAL.
      THUS, THE FUNDAMENTAL PROBLEM ENCOUNTERED IN CONDUCTING THIS TYPE OF
      RESEARCH,    IS    THE DEVELOPMENT OF AN EXPERIMENTAL METHOD,       ITS
      SELECTION, AND THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE CONDITIONS UNDER WHICH THE
      EXPERIMENTS WOULD TAKE PLACE.       THE AUTHOR, ALONG WITH CONDUCTING
      EXPERIMENTS    ·oN METAL BENDING BY PSYCHOKINESIS UNDER UUNIQUELY
      ESTABLISHED CONDITIONS, HAS ALSO CONDUCTED VARIOUS STUDIES ON THE
      FRAMEWORK REQUIRED TO SUITABLY EXPLAIN THE RESULTS OBTAINED IN THE
      EXPERIMENTS.     IN THIS ARTICLE THE VARIOUS CHARACTERISTIC OF PSI
      ENERGY DURING PSYCHOKINETIC DEFORMATION USING ONE OF THE PROPOSED
      MODELS WILL BE CONSIDERED.
