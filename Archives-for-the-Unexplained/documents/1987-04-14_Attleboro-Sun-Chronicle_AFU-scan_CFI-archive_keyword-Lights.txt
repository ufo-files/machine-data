      Tornadoes strike Arkansas
         GREEN FOREST, Ark. (AP) - Tornadoes and high-speed winds
      ripped apart a mobile home park, injuring four people, and peeled the
      roof off an elementary school filled with children in rural northwest
      Arkansas. No students were injured.
          The storm Monday afternoon appeared to have cut a 3oo- to 400-
      yard wide swath through the Old Race Track Mobile Home Park four
      miles north here, said Carron County Judge Jack Beeson, who
      surveyed the damage.
          The storm also uprooted trees, snapped utility poles; damaged
      barns and struck another mobile home near the park, he said.
