August 7: Nuclear rockets across 
the earth

”THE US CAN REACH THE WHOLE WORLD WITH ROCKETS. New York, August 8 (TT from Reuter). Remote-controlled projectiles with nuclear warheads that have a speed of thousands of kilometres per hour was prophesized Wednesday evening by the head of the American Air Force´s department for remote-controlled projectiles, General Richardson. Speaking of the flight by two remote-controlled flying fortresses had made between Hawaii and California, Richardson said that the US should be able to direct an attack at almost any point on earth with remote-controlled projectiles. We expect to be able to construct projectiles that have a speed of several thousand kilometres per hour, and they should be possible to control at a distance of several hundred or thousand kilometres.” (Sydsvenska Dagbladet, August 9).
