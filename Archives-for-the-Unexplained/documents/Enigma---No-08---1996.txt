distinctive pattern fit of crop circles with one, and only one, historical and cultural knowledge base -
Sumerian symbols. lbis close matching of crop circles and Sumerian symbols provides the key to
deciphering the redundant patterns in crop circles. They are a communication system.

origin, who they are, some of their ancient and ongoing programs of interest (such as
genetics-reproduction) and possibly an indication of when they can be expected to return to Earth.

evidence (eyewitness testi mony, still photos, videos) which allows us to identify where UFO craft are
from and who is responsible for them.

circles and the so-call ed Monuments of Mars.
