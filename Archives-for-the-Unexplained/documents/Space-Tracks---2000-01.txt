COM) systems, usually in low-Earth
orbit (LEO), that are designed to pro-
vide voice, data or paging services via
satellite to small, handheld terminals

years, various companies have proposed
and developed systems that may provide
some or all of the above communica-

munications systems for non-tactical

communications-on-the-move, point-to-

of Defense (DoD) examines emerging
commercial SATCOM capabilities, and
may increase the use of commercial sys-
tems in the future.
