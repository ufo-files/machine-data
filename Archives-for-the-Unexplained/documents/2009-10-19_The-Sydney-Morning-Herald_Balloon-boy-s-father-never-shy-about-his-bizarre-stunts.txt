Balloon boy's father never shy about his bizarre stunts
October 19, 2009 — 11.57am

Richard Heene, the father of US "balloon boy" Falcon, provided a glimpse of the chaos he was about to spark when he described for a national TV audience in March his belief that aliens are humanity's
ancestors, his latest madcap inventions and his unconventional approach to raising a family.
Appearing on the ABC reality TV show Wife Swap, he told of once passing out in a fast-food restaurant and hearing aliens speak to him. He boasted of his plans to build a flying saucer covered in aluminum foil
and send it into a tornado. He pulled his children around a hockey rink on a hovercraft-like device and took them on UFO-hunting expeditions.
