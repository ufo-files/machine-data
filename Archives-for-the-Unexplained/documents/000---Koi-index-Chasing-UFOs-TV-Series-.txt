     Wow. Most pathetic UFO show I have ever seen.


     I have a big interest in UFOs, but even I can't stand to watch this reality-type UFO show. What they do is get three people together, one
     who believes in UFOs, one who doesn't, and then one person who is in the middle. It shows them periodically interviewing them, hence
     the reality TV show style. But just like many reality shows, it comes across as very fake and unauthentic.


     They try to add suspense by pretending that they see something, but it is so terribly fake. I'm totally convinced these are actors, and
     they aren't even trying to "chase UFOs".


     They should also present more evidence.
