July 24: Colonel Ramström a skeptic of ghost bombs...

””BOMB CRASH” AT SIGTUNA EXPOSED AS METEORITE

”- I firmly believe that the many light phenomena in the sky and those ”bombs” which lately have been reported from different parts of the country, is nothing but meteorites and that conviction I intend to keep until something else has been proved, declares the head of the Rosersberg artillery school, Colonel Sven Ramström, to Stockholms-Tidningen

”Colonel Ramström has had his firm belief further strenghtened by a ”bomb crash” recently observed near Sigtuna [see case xxx, July 9], where the remains of ”the bomb”, on close inspection, showed all signs of having come from a meteorite. The find has been sent to the Air Defence department of the Defence Staff. 

”The find from Sigtuna is also interesting because ”the bomb”, in this case, appeared in about the same way as the flying objects which have been observed elsewhere. Eye witnesses describe it as a ball about the size of a cricket ball [Sw. krocketklot]. Behind it was a 2,5 - 3 meter long tail, luminous with all colours of the rainbow. 

”It was a landowner at Brantshammar who saw ”the bomb” and the crash. He reported the case to the police office in Sigtuna and, from there, expert help was ordered from the Rosersberg artillery school. The head of the test department Captain Åke Toll went to the place in the company of Major Sten Ankarkrona and Captain Bengt Ahlström, and they found ”the remains of the bomb”: a piece of iron and two lumps of slag. During the investigation nothing emerged that could prove that the finds came from anything but a meteor.

”Colonel Ramström repeats his personal opinion that ”the bombs”...are nothing but meteorites and thinks one ought to reduce ”the bomb psychosis” presently raging. - People have been upset by all these ”bombs”. It would be better if they would calm down until one really has proof that it is the question of such aerial guests. Meteorites can be annoying, too, says Colonel Ramström, but not so difficult to explain. In fact, the astronomers could, a long time ago, foretell that we right now had to expect an unusually abundant rain of meteors. This is a fact that further strengthens my opinion that ”the bombs” are not man-made.

Source: Stockholms-Tidningen, July 24; also syndicated by STB to many other newspapers.
