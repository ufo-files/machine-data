1. (S/NF) As background and IAW Chapter 3, Declassification and
Downgrading Authority, the Chief, Technology Assessment and
Support Activity is designated as the original classification
authority for approving recommendations to declassify documents
that were created under a DoD contract to support Project STAR
GATE and its project predecessors which are currently in the
possession of the Science Applications International Corporation
(SAIC} and the CSL.
2.   (U) The documents approved for declassification will be
remarked IAW instruction contained in Chapter 4, Marking
Classified Material. See enclosure one for a copy of the
instructions.
3.   (S/NF) Also, and as discussed, the Director of the CSL will
review and recommend for downgrading research issues contained in
the documents that are subject to the contract. Key caveats
would include but are not limited to all data relative to source,
target, and references to current andfor potential applications
and DoD sponsorship.
4. (U) Upon receipt of the data recommended for
declassification, a review of the recommendations and the
document will be made and an expeditious reply will be provided
