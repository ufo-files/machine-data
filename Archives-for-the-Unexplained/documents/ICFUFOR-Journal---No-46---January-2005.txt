ICFUFOR Journal files No 46 January 2005


  Ha’arava Road       23.10.2004


  Zohar from Jerusalem reports: while traveling with her husband to the Arava, at Saturday, she got bored. So, she
  photographed the landscape with her digital camera. She says: “in the east side of the road, in the sky between the
  clouds and the mountains, I saw red lights and beneath them a green light, like the shape of a sea horse. At first, I didn't
  take any significance, despite of the fact I made the photos. Back at home, I deleted all the photos, except one. Till
  today, I don't understand what I saw. Also, everyone who saw the photo couldn't give me an explanation.”

  The photo was transported to us by Hannan Sabat. We see in the photo red lights, a green light, and a light in the shape
  of a sea horse. We also see a white light in the left side of the picture. The picture will be placed in our web site.

  To my opinion (Hannan Sabat), we see in the photo, a reflection of the internal lights of the car. The lights of the cars’
  control panel reflected in the cars’ windowpane, and the photo, taken through the closed windowpane, reflected these
  lights.


  Jaffa 5.11.2004


  Eliyahoo Gilad reports: it happened on “Valentine Day”. At 16:30 I stood next to “Abulafiah Bakery”. First we saw a
  helicopter. Suddenly the helicopter took off. When we looked, we saw something like a ball in the sky, while the
  helicopter is trying to get closer. More viewers, About 7 people, said they saw 2 more balls, which’s colors changed
  from white to red. Other viewers said they also see a third ball. The balls reached, in a short time, to 3 km height.
  Despite the fact there was a wind; the balls took off in a straight line up, until they disappeared.

  It reminds me, once I observed seagulls in Jaffa, and also saw above them something like a big balloon. I had the felling
  it observed the people, and when it felt they know about it, the balloon started to move away to the sea. In the beginning
  it started to spin in the same place, and afterwards it flew to the direction of the sea, until it disappeared. I think that
  many things happen in Jaffa…

  Nesher 5.11.2004


  Abi Greiff reports: I went to the balcony of my house at 04:30. In the east, I saw in the sky 2 unusual objects. These
  were 2 lighted objects, standing almost horizontally in the sky, one next to each other. The distance between them was
  as a finger width from my point of view. The one in the right was smaller and higher. The left one was bigger and stood
  lower comparing to the other. The objects were unusual because of these reasons:


  1.    I never saw such bright stars in this area of the sky.

  2.    The objects were shinier then star.

  3.    They seem to be much closer then stars, and in the altitude of airplanes flight.


  The objects were 45? above the horizon, and were in the sky sector above Rehasim or more far-off. The smaller ones’
  color was like a star color. The bigger one was more yellowish.

  I continued the observation for the next 10 days. In the next day, 6.11.2004, I saw the pair again, but there was a change.
  The bigger seemed to be lower then the smaller one. Both were very shiny and stocked out in the background of the sky.
  They seem to be relatively close.


  It’s important to mention, that in that night, at 02:30, when I went to see them – they were not found. A day after, on the
  7.11.2004, I observed every hour, starting at midnight, until 04:30. The objects weren’t there.

  On the 13.11.2004, I saw in the same sky sector, 2 far objects, looked like little stars. It happened at 05:30. On the
  15.11.2004, I checked again at 05:30, and nothing unusual was seen in the sky. On the 16.11.2004, at 05:30, again
  nothing unusual was seen.

  Rishon Le-Zion 8.11.2004


  Tali reports, that from her balcony, which is directed southward, she and her husband, her son and daughter, see, for
  more then a week an extraordinary view. 2 objects reveals in the east, one of them is blinking all the time, and the other
  isn’t. They move in the sky for about 1.5 hours and then disappear in the west. To her claim, it starts after the sunset,
  and it’s clearly visible they are moving.

  In the day she reported this view, she claim they are moving in a circle, and like crossing one the path of the other. They
  are located very high in the sky. Through a binocular, she saw the object blinks. Sometimes its color is white and
  sometimes red. The stable object looks colored. Also her niece and her husband saw the phenomena from their homes in
  another place of the city.


  Holon 15.11.2004

  Eliyahoo Gilad reports: at 07:10 I drove at the Merkava Street, located in the industrial area of Holon. I stopped my car
  in front a traffic light, near “Universe” parking lot. Suddenly, I saw something, thinking at first it's a helicopter, in the
  direction of the meteorological station and east. It advanced to the industrial area. Its size was like a helicopter, and it
  flew in the altitude helicopter fly. The light in the traffic light changed, so I parked the car and turned the motor off.
  This object came in front of me, and I saw its orange, rusted like color. It flew, stopped and flew again. It stopped above
  the industrial area, and took off in an angle of 90?. When it reached an altitude of about 3 km it became small. No sound
  was heard. It was something amazing, dramatic. It reached to the heights very fast. It had a circular shape. It was clearly
  visible when it reached the clouds. It wasn't in any way a balloon.

  Assaf Barr , a journalist in “Ashdod Magazine”, felt at 01:33 he needs to go out and observe. His dog also began
  barking. Outside, he saw a UFO moving from the sea direction. He went back to take his glasses, and returned outside.
  He reports: “An unidentified object appeared above the neighbors’ house, in front of my house. It started to get closer,
  and my dog barked. For about 15 minutes I observed the light ball, which appeared in the dark. I remembered I have a
  camera, but wasn't sure if it's a good idea to take it out. I thought that maybe the light ball is only a ‘flashlight’ or
  something like it. I started to take pictures, and the object started to move. It stood there for long minutes, and also split
  into 3 parts”.

  Assaf took 22 pictures with his digital camera, but he was sure no one will believe him. It took him sometime to return
  to his normal routine, but nothing especial or exceptional happened.

  His observation was published in the magazine, and in conclusion he wrote “it frightens me to write about it”.

  The magazine asked the police and the army to respond. In the answer, they were told, that there wasn't any kind of
  exception activity in that area, and they don't have any idea what happened. The police claimed they don't handle cases
  like this, and the IDF Spokesman referred them to the principality of the civilian aviation.

  The magazine concluded that “every time that evidenced of this kind appear, security forces and officials, try to evade
  the matter…”


  Ma’agan Michael 8.1.2005


  Arik Hasidim reports: I woke on the seashore after the sunset. As usual, my binoculars were with me. At about 17:30 I
  saw a strange cloud in the sky, and decided to focus on it. Its shape reminded me a beehive.

  Suddenly, a dark object appeared in the left side of the cloud, entered into the cloud and created a black stripe in it. It
  crossed the cloud and stood still in the sky, in the direction of the island located about 1 km from the shore. I observed it
  is a small object, moving right and left in moderate movements. Then it seems the UFO comes closer to me, but it
  disappeared.

  When I observed the object through the binoculars, I saw bumps in its sides, like star beams. The observation took about
  5 minutes, and I was sorry there weren’t more witnesses to the observation.
