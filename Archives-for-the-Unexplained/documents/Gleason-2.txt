September 18, 1975




Dear Jackie:

Thank you so much for your most gracious letter, reminding
me of my promise to be present at your golf tournament
nex t year provided you invited me!

I anticipate that my schedule during February, 1976 will
be extremely heavy; but you may be sure that if it can
possibly be arranged I definitely want to accept your very
cordial invitation to participate in the Jackie Gleason
Golf Classic on February 25th. I thoroughly enjoyed the
opportunity las! year antt•hope and trust I can be with you
again.

I am sending your letter and a copy of this reply to my
scheduling office and asking that every effort be made to
keep it on my calendar. You should be hearing definitely
from my scheduling director as the date draws nearer.

Thank you again for your kindness and warmest personal
regards.
