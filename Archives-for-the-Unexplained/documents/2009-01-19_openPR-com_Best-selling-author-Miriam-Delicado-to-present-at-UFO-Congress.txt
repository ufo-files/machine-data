Best selling author Miriam Delicado to present at UFO
Congress
01-19-2009 12:41 PM CET | Arts & Culture
Press release from: TheHotStory PR / PR Agency: TheHotStory PR


Author & alien abductee Miriam Delicado
(Laughlin, Nevada) Miriam Delicado, author of Blue Star: Fulfilling Prophecy, and a renowned speaker will be presenting at the
2009 UFO Conference and Film Festival, organized by the International UFO Congress to be held from February 22nd – 28th. It is
the largest such conference in the world.

Delicado, whose book recounts in detail her experience as a UFO Abductee and her lifelong contact with the Tall Blond Aliens, has
travelled internationally to speak and share the messages she reports the Aliens asked her to reveal on their behalf.

Her presentation covering the connections between the beginning of man, the Extra-terrestrials and the Indigenous people and the
influence on daily human life is a culmination of her years of contact with the Aliens and the strong Hopi influences encompassed
within their messages to her.

In 2003, Delicado, who remains in contact with the Aliens, was prompted to begin sharing her experiences and the Aliens’ messages
with others.
