FÖRSVARSSTABEN LUFTFÖRSVARSAVDELNINGEN

May 23 - May 24.

PM regarding observations of fiery phenomena

I. The reports we have received on the matter have included contradictory statements regarding the performance of these fiery phenomena. Their speed have been estimated at values ranging between about 100-1000 kilometers per hour. Furthermore, elevation data have varied from approximately 0.1 to 50 kilometers.

II. By laying out the itineraries of these phenomena, the following notes were made:
1) Date: April 23rd.
Observation area: Untran. 23.45 o’clock. Northeasterly course.
Observation area: Gävle. 23.48 o’clock. Southeasterly course.

The distance between Untran and Gävle is some 30 kilometers. Had it been a single phenomenon in this case, it must have covered the distance traveling at a speed of 600 kilometers per hour. In doing so, however, its northeasterly course changed into northwesterly as it traveled over Untran. Thereafter, the phenomenon started to head in a southwesterly direction by the time it had reached Gävle.

April 24th.
Västerås. At around 00.05 o’clock. Southeasterly course.

Distance between Untran and Västerås: 100 kilometers. Distance between Gävle and Västerås: 125 kilometers. The distances have been covered during the timespan of 20 and 17 minutes, or at 300 and 450 kilometers per hour, respectively. Changes in course are bound to have been made over the aforementioned sites.

2) April 24th. The observation in Karlskrona was made at around 02.00 o’clock, while the event in Landskrona had occurred at 02.20 o’clock. Both of these airborne phenomena had followed a southerly course.

The estimated distance between Karlskrona and Landskrona is 175 kilometers. If we are dealing with a single phenomenon, which is likely in view of the relatively coincident course, its speed would have been about 525 kilometers per hour.

3) Had the exact same phenomenon been observed in both Västerås and Karlskrona, it is plausible to suggest that it would have covered the distance of 400 kilometers in 2 hours, ie, at a speed of 200 kilometers per hour.

4) The observations in Finland cannot be compiled with those with Swedish origin.

5) The observation in Gävle was made at around 08.00 o’clock, while the event in Uddevalla had occurred at 10.30 o’clock. Distance: 400 km. Speed: 170 km / h.
