                             THE MIDNIGHT BALL

We are still learning of past UFO incidents concerning people in the
Derbyshire area.   We are grateful to Ian Heafield of Burton-on-Trent for
permission to print this account of his UFO experience while driving home
late at night in 1979.  A map is printed on the following page.

On July 24th (1979) at around midnight, my wife and I were driving home
from a friend's house at Stretton. It was a clear night and the stars were
quite visible in the night sky.

We were travelling at about 45mph in the direction of Tutbury, when just
after we had gone over the top of Lodge Hill and were on the straight part
of the road, we saw a white ball of light flying down the Tutbury to Burton
road towards us. 1· estimated that it was 40' to 50' in diameter.

It rapidly approached the car and flew so 1 ow towards us,     that both my
wife and I thought that it was going to hit us and we both ducked down.
A cold shiver ran down my spine and I said to my wife "what the hell was
that!"  I quickly glanced back and saw that the whole road was ablaze with
light as the object flew away behind us and then went up into the sky.

My wife and I were discussing what had happened and we had not travelled
more than a few hundred yards,    when the ball of light flew over the car
again, this time going in the other direction.   I quickly stopped the car
and both my wife and I watched the ball of 1 ight as it flew on and then
curved straight up into the sky over in the Hatton direction, it then
vanished out of sight in the night sky.

The speed was too fast for an aircraft and I cannot say what it was!



                              A Lucky Escape?

similar incidents the car occupants have not always got off so lightly.

Readers may recollect the case that gained World publicity in January 1988.
Mrs Faye Knowles and her three sons, were overtaken by a glowing UFO while
they were driving along the road through the Nullabor Plains in Australia.

The UFO buzzed them severa 1 times and then the UFO "pounced" on the Ford
Telstar car and clung to the roof. The car was then lifted up and shaken
violently.  Mrs Knowles said "I thought we were going to die". "We were
going silly, something was going into our heads, we did not know what it
was." "I felt like we were dying, I was going hysterical.11

Faye and her sons tried to shout, but their voices became slurred as if
they were talking in slow motion. The car was then dropped back onto the
ground with such force that one of the tyres burst.     Truck driver Graham
Hewley found the family in a state of shock, commented: 11even the dogs were
cowering in the front of the car.11

                           THE ILLUSION THEORY - IOTA           , Pat Delgado March 1986

In many circumstances it seems our optical senses feed information to our
reasoning senses whereby we accept a scene which is not really what it seems,
and what we are seeing or what it would seem logical to see.�t that special
moment, could in fact be an optical illusion.

Where UFOs are concerned, some of the sightings may be through the medium of
an illusion, the nature of which is only known to the occupants.

We all accept optical illusions wherever they occur, whether they are 'fun•
pictures, specially devised for this purpose, or mirages, silhouettes,
conjuring tricks, colour clashes and combinations of size and distance.
Taking the latter, for instance, how many times do we see the illusion on
our TV and film screens where an object is first shown in close-up then shown
getting smaller and moving away to one side or corner of the screen until it
is a pin-point and then vanishes.  We readily accept that the object was close
at first then shot off out of sight, but in reality it did not go anywhere, in
fact it remained the same distance from you and merely diminished in size.

This sequence of events may apply to. ·some UFO encounters, especially those
where the description of the UFO•s departure is described as 'lifting up from
the ground and moving away at great speed, becoming a pin-point of light in a
few seconds•.  If the UFO possessed the capability of appearing to diminish in
size while moving left, right, upwards or even staying where it was it would
create the impression that it had moved away from you but in fact it had not
gone anywhere but simply diminished into a parallel world or other dimension.

This suggestion remains valid even if the UFO is optically triangulated
between two observers within certain parameters, rather like standing
somewhere in the area taken up by a cinema screen and looking back at the
projector, they could both observe the same effect.

The diminishing UFO theory, when taken to extremes, could present a condition
where the object appeared to be of a certain size and distance, and if it had
the capability of diminishing it could actually move closer to the observer
but still appear to be moving away because your mind receives the message
•it•s getting smaller so it must be increasing its distance from me•.  If this
were so, the UFO would not have gone anywhere but simply diminished into a
parallel world in another dimension.

Some UFO sighting departures are described as 'shooting at great speed
skywards'.  If it had a c tua l l y shot skywards, this could only be verified by
an observer positioned some distance from the encounter and to this person it
would appear as a light leaving the ground and shooting skywards like a tracer
bullet, only much faster and to an infinite height.  Have there been such
reports?  If not, maybe the observer at the encounter position experienced the
rising and diminishing e f fect.


If, as is widely thought. the occupants of UFOs are capable of im plantin g
certain knowledge and thoughts in our minds when they contact us, then, by the
same token, they must be capable of subjecting us to another type of illusion,
and that is, causing our eyes to operate similar to the effect binoculars      ·



have when used the wrong way round but with a diminishing magnitude, while the
UFO just faded away.  If this combination were used, an observer would swear
the UFO had moved away.  This would be providing there were no significant
objects in the foreground or background, but up in the air for instance.

Anyone who has observed a rainbow from an aircraft will know of the near

Continuing the illusion theory, it would be interesting to learn if any
readers have toyed with the idea in their minds that the principle of
holograms or some form of this subject might have some standing with regard to
some UFO sightings, after all, UFOs are seen but the conditions prevailing
which brought about the sighting are nebulous as are most aspects of them.  It
is feasible that an intelligence much higher than ours would be capable of
producing some form of hologram which enables us to see them.

Because descriptions of a UFO's departure after a visit are often described as
leaving at some enormous speed, not always straight up but at some low angle,
one would consequently think that someone in the vicinity or under the flight
path would hear the effect of a solid body breaking through the sound barrier,
especially as some pictorial diagrams of UFOs show a very un-aerodynamic
shape, in fact, a UFO having the classical shape we are familiar with, would
create an enormous noise if it were to ascend vertically at high speed.  This
noise would be caused by the large volume of air displaced by the upper half
passing over the outer rim with turbulence and vortices.  I don't think a
sonic boom has ever been connected with the passage of a UFO, so if there is
no solid body to compress the air at high speed, what was seen only appeared
to be a solid body.

So here again it would seem an illusion of some kind has been operated for our
limited perceptance and understanding as it stands at this time on the long
incline of the intelligence chart.

 the observers facing each other with the UFO between them.  This would be an
 interesting test case for the diminishing object theory because under these

 the opposite direction.

a bright light and then experiencing an 'out of body' state which continues
until they are returned to normal.  This 'out of body' state may be the
requirement that conforms to the illusion state in which the UFO is first
seen.  Has there been a case where one person has watched another person enter
a UFO and return with 'nuts and bolts' souvenirs?  If so, all would have to
