 Western Daily Press Reporter
TWIN investigations were
launched yesterday into
claims that a passenger
airliner was "buzzed" by
military jets flying at up
to 500mph.
  And as the Civil Aviation
Authority and RAF investi­
gations   began,     Yorkshire
MP Mr Bob Cryer called
for an immediate ban on
low flying by the RAF until
the results were known.
  The drama happened over
Keighley,   West     Yorkshire
on Monday evening when
two    military    jets    were
reported to have        flashed
past   an   inter-city    turbo­
prop airliner within 200 ft.


    'GOD FORBID'
  The     airline pilot  later
officially reported his air­
craft was at risk, and filed
an official "air-tniss".
  Captain    Tony    Leach,    a
chief pilot with Dan Air,
thought the fighters were
RAF Jaguars.
   They flashed past from
behind at about 5,000 feet,
one overhead and the other
at the same level.
   Captain Leach was flying
35 passengers, mostly busi­
nessmen, from the Leeds
and    Bradford    airport   to
Glasgow     in  a     48-seater
Hawker Siddeley 748.
   The passeigers didn't rea­
lise anything was wrong.
The jets were passed in the
blink of an eye, said a Dan
Air spokesman.
   An RAF spokesman said:
"We are investigating but
at the moment it is a mys­
tery.
   "We can't even find the
aircraft. All   our    Jaguars
were tucked up at the time.
 They could have been test
Jaguars, American or even
NATO aircraft. God forbid
 a foreign power."
