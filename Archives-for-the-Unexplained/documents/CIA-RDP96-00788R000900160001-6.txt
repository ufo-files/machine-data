                           SUMMARY ANALYSIS

                  REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION C 12



1.  (S)  This report provides documentation of a remote view-
ing session conducted for training purposes to enhance a remote
viewer's demonstrated ability.

2.   (S)   The viewer appeared very relaxed prior to and throughout
the session. There appears to be some target correlation in this
session.    Room noise was minimal although the air conditioner was
making a steady vibrating sound. The noise did not seem to bother
the remote viewer's concentration. The viewer never did identify
the airfield as the target; however, his impressions of the
countryside certainly demonstrated some correlation to the target
area.    Of particular interest is the way the viewer described the
"dike like road" when, in fact, there is a road built on a levee
in the nearby area. This was the first time that #19.5 and
#43.5 had done a session together and we encountered no problems.

3.  (S)  The protocol used for this session is detailed in the
document, GRILL FLAME Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing
Protocol (S), undated.

4.   (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions
during the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made
by the viewer reference his impressions of the target site. At
TAB B is a map and description of the target site.
