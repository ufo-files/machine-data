Morjärvs DEFENSE AREA
Department I a
No. 143
August 10, 1946.

Subject: projectile of unknown origin.

To the defense staff, department L.


1. Appendix to the August 7 telephone report  concerning a space projectile.
a) The observed projectile in Kalix-Nyborg traveled north-northeast.

b) We have gathered the following information regarding the projectile:
Location: 800 meters west of Penttilä village (which is located at the south-eastern tip of Kukasjärvi).
Reporter: Birger Pettersson. There were several other observers, including Mr. Per Gustafsson.
Point in time: The observation was made on August 3, at approximately 20:00 o'clock. A report on the matter was completed on August 7.
Observations: During a short period of about 10 seconds, a shining, fusiform tube-like object was observed 1 kilometer east of Penttilä village. It reflected sunlight and emanated small puffs of smoke. The phenomenon was completely soundless. Its approximate altitude had been about 400-500 meters as it traveled parallel to the ground surface. The course was almost due north (380 degrees).

2. Report from Övertorneå:
According to his own report, on August 9, as Finnish customs fiscal Mr. Pauli Pärmi stood fishing in Munio river at Kihlanki (5 mil north of Pajala) along with two friends, he came to hear a whining sound, almost reminiscent of a grenade, resulting in a water cascade 5 meters from the Swedish shore.
Mr. Pärmi means to spend his vacation during August 14 to 17, but will pinpoint the crash site at the time of his return.
This hasn’t been previously reported and the information contained herein should be handled confidentially in regards to Mr. Pärmi’s position.

3. Increasing the flow of reports:
Excerpts from Fst, Section L no 7:49 June 12, 1946 (regarding space projectiles) have been sent to all concerned parties as well as every customs post (about 60 in total).

Sven Dimander
Stabschef

Sven Dimander
Chief of Staff
