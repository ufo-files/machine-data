                                   Ufomind Mailing List
                                             Information and Standards
  The Ufomind mailing list plays a supporting role for the Ufomind/PsiPsy website. The list is actively moderated by
  webmaster Glenn Campbell and covers selected topics in UFOs, parapsychology, fringe physics, military secrets and
  any other subject included on the site. Anyone may submit postings to this list, but moderation is highly selective, and
  only a few messages are sent out to the list each day.

  See Main Page for archives and latest postings.


                                                                  How to Subscribe

           Since messages to the list are now available immediately in this archive, we expect that many people will
           prefer to view these messages on the web rather than subscribing by email. Should you chose to subscribe
           by email, here are the instructions...

           To subscribe to the "single message" version of the Ufomind Mailing List (the most popular version),
           send an email message to ufomind-request@lists.best.com with the word "subsingle" in the body of the

           To subscribe to the "digest" version of the Ufomind Mailing List, send an email message to ufomind-

  Topics Covered
  In December 1997, we renamed the "AREA 51" mailing list to "UFOMIND" to give us more flexibility in accepting
  messages not directly related to That Secret Base. The mailing list now plays a supporting role for the entire
  Ufomind/PsiSpy website, not just the Area 51 section.

  Moderation will continue to be "tight" - in that only a few messages will be allowed through each day - and back-and-
  forth debate will be limited to that which is truly productive. However, the new rules are much looser, and the
  moderator now has the discretion to accept any message he deems interesting. Instead of a strict charter, the new list
  will develop its own "personality" that will become clearer with time.

  We do not intend to cover the same ground as other mailing lists and newsgroups (especially UFO UpDates, which is
  already archived on this server at http://www.ufomind.com/ufo/updates/). Instead we intend to fill certain "niches" that
  are not covered as well elsewhere. These areas include...

          Area 51 - We will still cover all issues relating to the secret base in the Nevada desert.

          UFO and mystery claims relating to SPECIFIC government installations and organizations.

          UFO reports and claims in Nevada and surrounding areas.

          Documents reflecting on the character and credibility of specific UFO and paranormal researchers.

          Documents regarding disciplined experimental parapsychology research.

          Reviews of new books added to the Research Center catalog - to help "pay the rent". (If any readers disagree with
          our reviews, we encourage them to respond.)

          Technical notices regarding the Ufomind server and how to use its advanced editing features. (Previously
          provided by the Ufomind Techniques mailing list at http://www.ufomind.com/org/technique/)

          Samples of especially good writing in UFO or paranormal fields, as well as miscellaneous UFO reports that seem
          symbolic of a "genre."

          UFO and paranormal claims that are so wacky or bizarre that they deserve attention, at least for comic relief (i.e.
          "loon" posts).

          Issues of the Desert Rat newsletter as they become available.

  This mailing list will NOT cover....

          General UFO reports, claims and theories (unless they are specifically related to the topics above), since these are
          adequately covered in other mailing lists.

          General conspiracy claims.

          General black aircraft discussions

          Paranormal or spiritual claims without empirical data.

  We also DO NOT ACCEPT known copyrighted material, apart from brief quoted excerpts. Because all messages from
  this list are permanently archived on our server, we don't want to run into hassles for having copyrighted material on our
  site. Therefore, we will not redistribute whole articles from newspapers, magazines, original websites or other
  copyrighted sources. Instead, if you want to call attention to an article, you can submit a message with one or two
  paragraphs from it, along with a URL leading to the rest. You can also freely SUMMARIZE or RESTATE copyrighted
  material without infringement.

  We DO accept re-posted messages from other mailing lists and newsgroups, so long as they conform with our topics
  and "personality". We encourage readers to puruse other mailing lists and news sources for material that might be
  relevant to this list.

  This is a "low bandwidth" mailing list. On average, we expect no more than six messages a day to be distributed. Our
  emphasis is on quality, not quantity -- selecting intelligent, stimulating messages rather than a lot of low-interest ones.

  The reason for having a mailing list, instead of just a website, is to alert people to current news and to stimulate
  discussion and research in the most promising topics. Responses to previous messages are encouraged - but only if they
  are "substantial" and add something significant to the discussion. When you respond to a previous message, you should
  try to include the URL of the message you are responding to (from the archive at http://www.ufomind.com/misc/ ).

  The best way to get to know this mailing list is to subscribe to it or review the archive. Further advice on submissions
  will be posted to this list as its personality is defined.

  Remember that you don't need to subscribe to this list to read it or post to it. All messages are immediately archived on
  our website, and the latest are linked to our What's New page at http://www.ufomind.com/new/current/. The storage
  location for new archived messages has been changed to http://www.ufomind.com/misc/, but all messages from the
  Area 51 list remain at the old archive address: http://www.ufomind.com/area51/list/. Every message retains a permanent
  URL on our site, so you may feel free to link to any message from your own web pages and postings.

                                                                               Stats

       This list was started as the Area 51 mailing list on Sept. 1, 1996. A separate Ufomind list was started in Jan.
       1997, and the two lists were combined under the Ufomind name in Dec. 1997.

       As of 11/09/98, there were 1573 subscribers (about half digest-only).

       Digest is generated every 5 days or 12 messages, whichever comes first.

       Performance. Due to high load on the mail server, a delay of 4-12 hours is now common between message
       approval and delivery to subscribers (not including the delay before the moderator approves the message, which
       can be up to several days).
