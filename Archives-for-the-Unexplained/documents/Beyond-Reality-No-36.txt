   Editorial


                       In the past seven years we have witnessed the
                    rise of what is commonly called the New Age·.
                    During this time many important discoveries have
                    been made and many personalities have fascinat­
                    ed us with their unique psychic powers. But,
                    strangly, these personalities, along with their
                    mysterious powers, seemed to have faded away.
                       Uri Geller, for one was given more coverage in
                    the media than most important news events. He
                    appeared on many popular TV shows and was
                    written up in just about every magazine, including Beyond Reality
                    (September 1973). Where is he now? And what is he doing? A person with
                    such powers should be doing something to help the affairs of mankind. Alex
                    Tanous is another highly gifted psychic who seems to have disappeared from
                    the public eye. Here is a man who has an impressive academic background,
                    and has been tested by the U.S. Navy and a host of research organizations.
                    Why is his name not heard of lately?
                       Besides personalities, many breakthroughs such as recording discarnate
                    voices on tape, pyramid energy, Kirijan photography, etc. have not been
                    followed up by "official" science. To date, reports from psychic research
                    organizations are few and far between. We know of very few individuals who
                    are still active in research. Could it be possible that parapsychological re­
                    search is suffering because of lack of funds-although we are on the
                    threshhold of monumentous breakthroughs? Of course, the most captivating
                    topic revoles around the alleged UFO cover-up by the government. But here
                    at Beyond Reality we feel that there is another cover-up, again by the
                    government, which is keeping much research information from reaching the
                    public even though we are clamoring for the release of such information.
                       Further proof can be seen in most local bookstores and newsstands.
                    Compare the present lack of books on parapsychology aJ¥1 related subjects,
                    with the more abundant supply available just two years ago-although
                    there is more demand for this type of material. Are book publishers being
                    pressured by the government to stop the flow of this material to the public?
                    Because of our constant need for material, we have become aware of this
                    fact. Many good writers and researchers have confided in me that they have
                    been discouraged from writing and reporting the results of their theories and
                    research results.
                       Despite the evident cover-up this magazine will continue its zeal in
                    documenting the latest discoveries in the realm of the unusual, and to bring
                    you the latest news and discoveries. In order to continue our wide coverage
                    and keep our standards of excellence we urge you to subscribe. As many of
                    you have found out, our publications are not always available at your
                    newsstands. By subscribing, not only will you support us but you will be
                    assured of receiving each and every issue at home. Let's continue to explore
                    the strange and challanging world Beyond Reality together.

                                                                                      Sincerely,
