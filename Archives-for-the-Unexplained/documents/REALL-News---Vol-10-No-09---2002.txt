            Our Next Meeting
       The Evolution of Intelligent Design
        Presented by Prof. Karen Bartelt
Starting in the 1980s, the case was put forth for
"intelligent design" as an alternative to naturalistic
processes such as evolution. Since that time, the ID
proponents have focused their attention on the general
public, and to this day have not published a peer-
reviewed paper that supports intelligent design. This
presentation will address some of the main arguments
proposed by ID, and the evolution of these arguments
in the last 20 years. Among those topics will be some of
the tactics used by the "Big Four" of the ID movement,
and the responses from the scientific community.
