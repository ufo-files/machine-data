  Here's a bit of nostalgia from the Freedom Ridge days....
  On the morning of Oct. 31, 1994, I and an aviation journalist
  visited Freedom Ridge. I had my scanner tuned to the Groom Lake
  tower frequency, and the journalist was recording it with a small
  tape recorder.
  We never saw any secrect aircraft, but we did have the satisfaction
  of preventing something secret from landing.
  The following is a transcript of monitored Groom Lake Tower radio
  traffic, as close as the journalist could decipher it. We are
  mostly hearing only the tower side of the conversation, and there
  may be significant periods of silence between transmissions.
  "Watchdog" is the code word indicating that watchers are on
  Freedom Ridge. The "Red and White" is a "Janet" 737 bringing
  workers to the base -- a routine flight. "Pyramid" is presumed
  to be a visual or radio waypoint.
