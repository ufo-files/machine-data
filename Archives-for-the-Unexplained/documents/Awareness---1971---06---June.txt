    His new work has two main themes. Firstly, the idea that an underwater
civilization has existed for possibly millenia on this planet. He states, quite
rightly, that three quarters of the earth's surface is covered by water, and that
much of the oceans are two miles or more in depth. Furthermore, we know next

where many aeroplanes and ships have mysteriously disappeared. He points out
that this is not really a triangle at all, but in reality, lozenge-shaped. The author
and his associates have discovered some ten similar lozenge-shaped areas in which
strange things happen, placed geometrically around the world . This is all excell­
ent reading.
     I strongly recommend that you buy, beg, or borrow this book. lt wi 1 1 add a
lot to your thinking on our sub ject. There are three appendices, reference notes,
and the Lord be praised, an index.
