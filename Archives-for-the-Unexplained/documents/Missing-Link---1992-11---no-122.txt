    this place is just one of the many places throughout Puerto Rico where people have stated seeing these strange
     objects. Because of the many testimonies that have been reported in relation to this strange phenomenon, serious
    investigations are actually taking place.
                                           Scientific groups from all around the world have traveled here to
     observe and investigate these UFO sightings. Puerto Rico is one of the most visited places by UFOs in the world.

      There are different explanations and theories seeking answers to many questions that have arisen because of
    these sightings.   One of the many explanations given by UFOlogists is that "El Yunque" is a UFO Base of a
    Geomagnetic Radiation Center. For other investigators, such sightings occur mostly at this place because "El
    Yunque" is located at the South side from the mysterious and controversial Bermuda Triangle. And finally, for
    parapsychology and occultism investigators, "El Yunque" is an excellent location for paranormal activities to
    take place. Meanwhile, others prefer to say that this place has some certain special energy or telluric energy
    that these spaceships take advantage of for their specific "purposes".
      The experience that I am about to narrate is just one of the most amazing UFO encounter experiences. The
    same took place here, in "El Yunque".

     MORE PET PEEVES AND ROOF BRAIN CHArfERING

 I want to say a few words about a recent UFO meeting that we had right here in Waynesboro, at the TVEC
Building on Tuesday, August 11 from    6:00 p.m. until about 9:00 p.m. Aileen Garoutte of Federal Way,
Washington, visited us for a couple of days and had earlier agreed to speak to our local public on UFOs. Aileen
is the founder and director of the UFO Contact Center International (UFOCCI), and our paths first crossed by
mail back in July 1981 when Aileen wrote me on a referral from Dr. Sprinkle of Wyoming. Seems we all had (and
still have) a common interest in the abducteefcontactee syndrome, and, in fact, at that time I put out a free
networking newsletter entitled Contactee Syndrome.
 This was the first time that we had met face-to-face. Although over the years we had conducted some rather
extensive correspondence, had some long-winded long distance phone calls, and I had contributed a number of
articles to her Missing Link magazine. In addition, myself and Harv Howard (Associate Director) of Manchaca,
Texas both worked together on a revised UFO questionnaire for UFOCCI (since we had both become members)
as well as preparing what we hoped was a balanced report on the hypnotic process.
 Over the years I have belonged to big name UFO organizations like APRO and MUFON. And while they have
done commendable work in the field, and I have certainly appreciated that they were there, Aileen made
UFOCCI more of a creative challenge and a partnership. Before she came here to speak we discussed on the
phone what she'd say, and she said she'd "speak from the heart." That is, I have found, her way, and it's a very
endearing quality. She has a charming charisma, and she genuinely cares about people and truth.
 Her downfall, in the eyes of skeptics and the more cautious, is her literal acceptance and belief in the claims of
many contactees. For example, at the meeting she spoke of a cure for AIDS given one contactee recently, and
she showed us a rock given another that, when you look at it from a couple of different angles, is supposed to
show a couple different alien profiles. The concensus of the rock was that it was just an ordinary rock, and as
for the claim of an AIDS cure everybody has a skeptical wait and see attitude.
  To Aileen's credit she handles criticisms and differences very well.      We had a panel discussion following
Aileen's talk that consisted of myself, Aileen, and Dr. Greg Little and Rick Potter from over Memphis way. I had
heard that Greg told Aileen, "We agree to disagree". It was a real lively panel discussion and good audience
participation, and many different perspectives explored and described, and everybody kept a civil tongue in
their heads.   I was impressed that it was an atmosphere of genuine sharing and caring, and in spite of the
differences there were many similarities on important key issues.      For example, we are dealing with a real
mysterious phenomenon, trauma reactions result in many cases, and because of screen memories and
displacements we may not be perceiving a lot of what is really there. A point even Aileen agreed with upon my
questioning. Of course, I realize that in her mind and heart she feels that she has a pretty good idea.
 In talking with people afterwards, it seems that folks enjoyed the panel discussions most of all. They were very
impressed with Greg and Rick and the things that they had to say, and a number stated that they will want to
read Greg's new book that he's working on as soon as it comes out.
 George Holman is a counselor at the boot camp in Clifton (where I also work) and in years past organized local
New Age Seminars, attended and afterwards told me that it was a tremendous success.
 Now back to UFOCCI again. Aileen told us that her life may soon be taking another direction. She may step
down as UFOCCI's director, and let somebody else have that responsibility now.
 Well I'm certainly glad that before that happened we did finally get to met in person. Just before she came she
told me on the phone, "I feel that I know you already," and I agreed that the feeling was mutual.
 When here Aileen asked me about why in my article on support groups in Recovery Times I hadn't focused
more exclusively on UFOCCI. I told her simply that I was wanting to give an objective overview on the subject,
which was true. But I think I should confess a little more.
 I agree with UFOCCI's stance of helping and supporting UFO experiencers. It's a humanitarian gesture that
does need emphasis.
 However, I have wondered at times if I should not have formally resigned from my voluntary position as an
associate director in Tennessee. In fact, I have kept a low profile on that. While I agree in principal with the
humanitarian concept, UFOCCI seems overly absorbed in subjective belief-ridden issues of, to me, questionable

                                                                                                                                                              By Corey Wolfe

                   Epictetus said: "it's not things, but our opinions of things that matter."

             I gave that thought a good deal of my own and saw in it one of the great Truths.                                                                                                                                                                                               A mixture of viewpoints are
        after all what moves us to react to life and everything in it. "Life is what we bring to it" seems another apt
        phrase. While one type of individual would view a fatal car crash as a terrible tragedy and for weeks thereafter
        be unable to release it from his mind, another perhaps wider viewpoint would see the crash as something quite
        different. This individual might sense the soul leaving the now dead body and imagine the joy it will soon
        experience. This person might be happy for the opportunity it affords the family and friends left behind. They
        can, after all, learn a lot from this, if they will only allow themselves to. The survivors will most assuredly find
        themselves reflecting on their own mortality. This will lead them to new purpose, and more focus in their own
        lives.
            Our beliefs and prejudices are based on what? That is a question we must think deeply on. For I think most of
        us will find that they are founded on information that was received from outside of ourselves. Through TV,
        movies, friends and family we are "taught" how to react to circumstances. This is a two sided sword. It allows
        us to fit in with many different types of people, but if we look deeper we discover that we may not be reacting
        outwardly the way we feel inwardly. Whenever I attend funerals I just cannot feel grief, though I understand it in
        others who do not share my viewpoints. My loving mother brought me up with the saying, "Death is a door,
        nothing more". So because of her, my viewpoint was different that most. Thank you Mom.
            I often practice noticing my reactions to things from a point of view slightly outside of myself. I try to think
        back to see what trigger made me react a certain way. I then follow feelings that erupt from such
        attention/focus and find that they all merge somewhere in the past at the point where I originally learned how
        to react to that particular circumstance. I now am able to adjust the belief and the reaction to it. The next time
        such a circumstance arises, I catch my "old" reaction to it before it sees actuality.
            Life can be almost a fun game if you let it. Someone said, "Life is much too serious a thing to take seriously." I
        suppose it all comes down to expanding our viewpoints. I as a young boy found a game I still occasionally play.
        I called this the God game, in that I would try to imagine God's point of view.
            I would sit quietly (I guess I was meditating, but didn't know the term then) and practice expanding my
        imagination larger and larger until my mind encompassed the whole universe and all its dimensions. Then as
        quickly as possible I would zoom down down                                                                                                                  down             to the smallest teeniest sub atomic particle I could imagine.
        Back and forth I would do this, over and over. I discovered eventually that each time I did this the "Ring-pass­
        not" I encountered at both ends would stretch a little, and I would be "allowed" to see a little more. One day
        something wonderful happened, I discovered that the two ends meet each other. ! ? ! I, to this day can't grasp
        that concept with my intellect, it seems it can only be experienced.
            We must be flexible, and open to new points of view. After all, the universe itself is evolving, so what is truth
        today may very well be half truth tomorrow. We must be willing to swallow our pride and admit to ourselves
        and others that we were wrong and have discovered a new way of looking at the same old situations. Pride,
        honor and vanity are all close cousins. I sometimes find myself showing off and being quite egotistical just
        because I think my viewpoint allows me a larger grasp on people's motivations. This is a danger that cannot be
        exaggerated. With knowledge should come wisdom. It is very important to squelch the ego while helping
        others. I find people listen much more intently to a speaker that is projecting only love and understanding.
            In closing, I must mention the joy that comes from the wider field of view. The compassionate nature rises to
        the surface along with tolerance. These are the attributes of all great Teachers, and we can do no better than in
        our own humble ways, try to emulate them.

                                                                                                                                                                 Associate Director, Manchaca, TX


  A reoccurring thread woven across the UFO tableau in the last few years is the mention of black helicopters.
To this writer's knowledge, they first came to attention in livestock mutilation cases in the western part of the
US. Since then, black helicopters have been reported to "visit" witnesses of recent UFO sightings. Typically,
black craft will reportedly buzz the witness' house, hover nearby for long periods, and in some cases, follow his
or her car.
  Are these machines friend, foe, or something far less real, such as figments of over-active imaginations? If we
take a short look at these three possibilities, maybe we can have a better understanding without the tinges of
fear and terror that some witnesses seem to interject into their reports about them. As a result, perhaps we can
come up with some solutions for counteracting their intrusions into our lives.
  By labeling the black helicopters "friend," we must not take that definition too seriously! The United States
government and other governments around the world may not have an antagonistic attitude toward their
citizens in general, but certainly it has shown itself to be definitely unfriendly and unappreciative to people
involved with UFO work.
  As so called friendly craft, our first possible explanation is to suspect the 'copters as being conventional
machines, manned by conventional service personnel as part of conventional military units. If that were the
case, they would not truly be black, but would be the standard Gl issue of dark olive drab (OD) which is the
color of most if not all combat helicopters.
  So perhaps the craft are not the sinister black as reported? Practical experience shows us that regardless of
the angle of view, it is almost impossible to distinguish the dark, non-reflective OD color of these 'copters from
black when they are silhouetted against the sky. An Army National Guard helicopter pilot stated to the writer
that the 'copters also look black when viewed from above and seen against a backdrop of ground vegetation.
(It makes you wonder why they use such a dark color for "camouflage.")
  But some of the people are adamant that the craft are indeed black, sometimes shiny black. Further, a few
reports have mentioned black uniforms on the individuals seen inside or near a landed craft. These clues tend
to indicate that the black coloration of craft and crew signifies a very special-purpose craft flown by special
purpose personnel. Collaborating evidence comes from the antics and unconventional--if not sometimes
danger of flying style--of the aircrew in causing the craft to gain the attention of the witness in the first place.
These three unconventional clues tie together to strongly indicate that Uncle does indeed have special units
with which he does dirty tricks against its rather decent and normal citizenry. ... But that assessment comes as
no surprise to anyone knowledgeable about UFO history.
  Finally, as for the argument that the craft, crew and mission are all conventional: it is difficult to believe that
normal service personnel would be ordered to perform flights to harass specific civilians. For one thing, such
strange orders would cause too many questions by the aircrews involved, and they obviously would not be
trusted with the real reason for such missions.
  Could the choppers be foe? --But who would that foe be? Hardly could it be space aliens. All of them are
zipping around in highly technologically advanced UFO craft. It is not reasonable to suspect that some of them
following around their own craft by using conventional-type helicopters to further spook the citizenry. What
would be the purpose in that? So this possibility doesn't seem to rationally work. If nothing else, we maybe can
consider the helicopters as "friendly" foe? (You know, something like the term, "They were killed by friendly
fire.")
  The last possibility we will briefly examine is unthinkable to some people but at the same time the most
mundane. And that is the UFO sighting itself has made the witness unduly anxious, frightened, and aware of
things in the sky. Therefore, he or she will build too much meaning into innocent flights of helicopters which
had previously passed virtually unnoticed.
  In a normal sense, there is no discernible connection between a UFO sighting and helicopter traffic after the
event which seems to hover too long, too close and appear too black. Simple paranoic hysteria we could say.
-And some people would want to throw the UFO sighting itself into the deal. In effect, saying none of it
happened.

         We must allow this possibility of unusual 'copter activity happening after a sighting in case or two. After all, it
       could be happenstance. But when a pattern repeated develops of an excellent UFO sighting being shortly
       followed by pesky 'copters buzzing around, then the bare facts themselves point out a connection even if the
       actual processes of the connection remain obscure and vague.
         If we tally up our score sheet, we find four unconventional clues: 1) the black 'copter, 2) the infrequently
       noticed dark uniforms, 3) the unconventional actions of the chopper, and 4) the incident coming on the heels of
       a UFO sighting). These clues tie themselves together into one meaningful knot. That knot is part of a chain of
       knots. It twists its way, we can be sure, through cloaked military outfits to the inner sanctums of our own super­
       secret spy agencies and obscure levels of the Pentagon.
         So, what are we to think? Apart from this black 'copter business, we know that there are secret military units
       which deal with UFO "problems". The primary purpose of these units which were created in the 60s is to rush to
       the site of a UFO crash, landing site, or close encounter area and secure and check the area for hardware or
       entities.
         Think about it. What would these highly specialized units do during slack time? As a rapid deployment force
       waited for a hot call, they would make training runs dry runs, to the locals of recent sightings. This is standard
       practice in military, fire, police, and rescue units. It serves two extremely important purposes in such an
       organization. It is genuine training and it serves as a "make-work" function when the units have no hot calls to
       make.
         A secondary aspect of their mission which would dovetail precisely into these primary and training functions
       would be to pinpoint and harass recent UFO witnesses. (We can also imagine that they may also harass (scare
       off) UFOs if they are swift enough to catch the UFOs in the act of doing what they do.) The intimidation of
       witnesses has long been a staple tactic with military and secret agencies in their efforts to debunk UFOs, so
       there is no far-fetched imagining required to add this aspect as an operations parameter for a security and
       recovery outfit. They would be perfectly suited for the job. They would have the knowledge of the UFO
       situation (from the government's perspective), the time to do such activities, the handy cloak of secrecy over
       their entire operations, and the 'copters for carrying out such activities. All in one neat package.

         What can you do to protect yourself from any helicopter or even a mysterious car parked outside your house
       which you believe is an attempt to harass you? First, you can understand the purpose of it being there. It is an
       active attempt to interject itself into your awareness and disrupt your ability to make your own decisions. The
       message being sent is singular and clear: "Big Brother is Watching You."
         Don't venture into their simplistic little trap, don't succumb to being the fearful, worrying, unproductive shell c�
       a person they would have you become. Take reactive action!
         Unfortunately, laws are a bit archaic in regard to airspace--at least a citizen's rights immediately over his
       property--and little can be done about even a very low-flying chopper that threatens to blow your roof away.
       You absolutely cannot shoot at it or even threaten it with a gun. Probably throwing rocks at one would get you
       in trouble too even if it were that low! Remember those are our own good--but misguided--people in those
       flivvers.
         Legal action? --Good luck. The Cash-Landrum Case didn't get very far in court despite the fact the victims
       have undergone incredible mental and physical suffering. Big Brother is watching you, but don't expect him to
       take care of you!
         Our advice with black helicopters and mysterious car incidents is to simply enjoy the attention...and document
       it. Smile, wave, and slip in to get a camera.
         Use a good 35mm camera with a telephoto lens if you can. Better yet, use a video camera with its telephoto
       lens to capture the vehicle on tape while also recording your commentary about what is happening. A video
       camera is especially suited for this purpose because it can capture details in low-light situations that even the
       eye cannot see. (Don't have a camera, grab a small box-like object of any kind and pretend that it is a camera
       and you are taking pictures.)
         It is guaranteed that no black helicopter or car is going to hang around if you point (or pretend to point) a
       camera in its direction.
         To really catch them in the act, you may wish to try to video or photograph them from inside the house through
       a window or an open door rather than rush out in the open with the camera in plain sight. The more you can

  Enlist the help of a neighbor if you are repeatedly harassed. Ask the neighbor to grab his or her camera to

  After an incident, do some sleuthing. Write down every detail of the event possible. Call the local police or

are all very dark OD and come in many shapes and sizes. Some are noisy--such as the old Viet Nam era
"Hueys." But some are so quiet all you hear is a subdued turbine whine and rush of air with absolutely none of
the "chop-chop" sound.
  If you got a good view of the craft, you may want to check the local library for a book on helicopters and try to
identify the one you saw. Numbers and markings will be exceedingly important to identifying these craft. One
of the frequent observations about the "black" helicopters is that they have no markings. That is almost the case
with the OD military helicopters. The markings are usually small and black and hardly intended to be
recognized from more than a few feet away.

representative and senator too.

possibly collect on this problem. We need not put up with this kind of treatment. We are not powerless. If we

 Puerto Rico is mentioned many times in the world of UFOlogy, but many do not know anything about
its history or its inhabitants, or the extraterrestrial life forms that have established direct contact.
Puerto Rico is a small island located in the Caribbean Sea. It is 35 miles wide and 1 00 miles long. Its
population nearly reaches the three million mark. Way too many folks, compared to the expansion of
the land. Most of the inhabitants live in a concentrated manner on the north-east side of the island; the
San Juan area, the Capital of the island.
 The people of Puerto Rico nowadays are a rich mixture of different cultures.             Descendants of many
nations.   The "TAINO" Indians (the islands original residents), the Spaniards and the African are the
basic components of the current culture. Throughout different periods of our history many have been
added. Amongst the various cultures who came and colonized on the island we find Holland, Germany,
France and many more. Currently we have what many call American.
 But as strange as it may sound we seem to be mixing yet with another race; an extraterrestrial one.
Like the Taino Indian did once with the Spaniards, some people have "Deified" these alien beings.
Others who are more open minded accept them for what they really are, beings alien to our world.
Puerto Rico is basically a religious island; the Catholic religion being predominant, hence the other side
of the coin; the concept that these strange alien visitors are nothing more than demons.
 There seems to be mixed emotions regarding these alien beings. Many of us who have encountered
them start out with the opinion that the initial experience is a negative one. As time goes by we tend to
shift more towards the positive side. My own personal experience is a classic example. After getting
rid of the fear and anxiety many of us have been able to speak freely about our experience. I, for one,
have been featured in many television talk shows and several newspaper articles.             Speaking up has
helped break many barriers. Once the long silence is broken, a great many minds are opened. So has
been the case here in our small tropical island, our people have demanded more openness regarding
extraterrestrial activity among us. This reality is so much of our daily lives that our leading newspaper,
"El Vocero," carries a weekly column which keeps all of our citizens informed of most about the
extraterrestrial activity.

articles are written by Mr. Julio Victor Ramirez, a notable reporter who was once a skeptic till the day he
was confronted with this particular reality. One of the articles to be published, explains his personal

                                         MY PERSONAL EXPERIENCE

                                                       New York


             I was born and raised in New York city. I was working as a waiter in one of the major hotel chains when
           I received an alarming phone call from one of my aunts. She informed me that my grandmother was
           terribly ill. My grandmother was the woman whom I considered to be my mother. I just had to go to
           Puerto Rico where she lived to see her.

                                                      Puerto Rico 1 988

             When I arrived on the island I was a mazed by its beauty. After being there a few days my grandmother
           recuperated and I was able to make my brief visit a real vacation. Upon returning to New York City I
           found myself constantly thinking about Puerto Rico. Something was pulling me - drawing me in like a
           magnet. I phoned my aunt out in P.R. and expressed to her my feelings. I told her that I wanted to come
           out and live on the island. She reminded me that the employment situation on the island was pitiful.
             Without knowing why I lifted my roots. I either sold, or gave away, everything that I owned and headed
           towards the island. Even though it was the birth place of my parents I really didn't know P.R. I was like
           a stranger in my own land. Even its roads were unfamiliar to me.
             One of my cousins had heard over the radio about a job opening at a local night club. The waiters job
           suited me perfectly. I landed the job and bought myself an old jalopy of a car as a means of
           transportation to and from my new job.
             Everything went fine until the night before Mother's Day. A few days before this event, the same cousin
           who had helped me obtain my job; called me up to ask a favor of me. She asked me to please
           photograph one of her favorite musical groups, which was going to be appearing at the night club
           where I was working.
             The night before Mother's Day I arrived at the nightclub toting my cousin's 1 1 0 Instamatic Camera.
           The club was packed and I was barely able to snap some pictures of the musical group called "El Gran
           Combo". I managed to take a few. When the night was over I headed home. My feet were aching and
           all I could think at the time was about getting home and having something to eat.
             I took the usual route home, the one which one of my other relatives had showed me to utilize going to
           and from the night club. I was driving very slowly. It was about 4:30 a.m. I had to keep in mind what
           some of my family members had warned me about; they said that while driving along these country
           roads one had to watch out for stray cows or horses that often wandered on to the roads. What they
           really should have warned me about was something else !
               As I drove through this lonely stretch of unpopulated country road named "La Bajura" (literally
           translated means "the low part"), I came across a thick mass of fog. I didn't find this strange because I
           had encountered this cloud of fog other times on my way home from work. I slowed down even more so
           and kept my eyes on the road at all times. Suddenly I heard what I initially thought were the sounds of
           hooves on the paved road. Calves? The sound was coming from the left side, the driver's side, my side.
             I turned my head towards where the sound was emanating from and to my great surprise what I saw in
           that moment changed my whole sense of reality. Jogging alongside my old car was a ... midget? child?
           "What the __       is it?", I remember thinking to myself. My mind was going wild. I didn't know what to
           think. Demons? "Oh, my God, it's a demon," I thought. I was scared beyond description. This thing was
           about three or four feet tall. It had a huge head and black bulging eyes. The eyes are what really
           freaked me out. The being looked at me with those black pools it had for eyes and it had a strange
           effect on me. This thing had eyes that seemed to penetrate my very being. They were like those of a
           Pekingese dog except a lot larger. Its skin was white, almost like if it were wearing clown makeup. The
           nose was a pugged one and the mouth was no more than a slit. It wore what seemed to be a small one
           piece suit; the color being olive green. What in hell was this? The farthest thing from my mind was that
           this strange creature was an extraterrestrial being. At that place in time I didn't think about space
           beings or UFOs. My mind did not ponder into those realms.

  I didn't believe in UFOs so I didn't think about UFOs or their occupants. I was so frightened and
nervous that when I attempted to speed away from the horror, instead of stepping on the accelerator I
stepped on the brakes. When I was about to move on I see movement on the right hand side in front of
my car. Coming in from the fog was that same creature. How could this be? I thought. How did it get
from where it was running beside my door to where it was located now? In those few precious
moments in which I debated whether to run the thing over with my car something even more
unexpected occurred. Someone or something opened my car door. I jerked my head to the left and
there it was staring at me. There were two. Not one - but two. I gasped and held tight to the steering
wheel. My foot felt like lead on the brake pedal. Mentally I prayed to God and asked him to forgive my
many sins. I urinated in my pants and helplessly passed out.
  When I woke up I had no idea of my whereabouts. I was still seated in my car but I was no longer on
the road. I found myself in a strange place where there were other cars. These other cars were empty. I
distinctly remember looking a round for a sign that would read "Exit." There were none in this place that
looked like an underground parking lot. There were no doors that I could see; no windows either. The
colors of the walls were a non metallic light grey. As I sat there confused and gripping the steering
wheel, the thing appeared next to the drivers seat door - mine. This time I did not hear it approach my
car. I just happened to look towards my left in search of a way out and there it was. Those eyes! It
reached out to me with its skinny arm. The hand, I noticed, had five fing�rs but they were all about the
same length. With his white hand he, or it, touched me. He placed the palm of its tiny hand upon my
forehead. With this action on the creatures behalf, I faded into darkness.

  When I regained my mind I found myself seated in another room along with 14 other people. This
room was also the same color as the previous one; a light grey. There were no light fixtures visible to
the eye, yet the place was illuminated. I saw no doors or windows. The rest of the people looked as
terrified as I felt. None of us spoke. Out of what seemed nowhere, from some point of departure behind
us, walked in the two small beings and right behind them came a man.
  This man was dressed in a black shirt and black pants. His shoes were of the same color. His skin was
a dark tan complexion. Like the skin color of an Arab or a Hindu. He did not appear to be tall; maybe 5
feet 7 inches or so. He stood before us and the two small beings situated themselves - one to his right,
the other to his left. This man with long black shoulder length hair spoke to us in perfect Spanish. He
claimed to be as human as we are but from a different planet, another world which rotates around an
alien star (sun).
   In this room the extraterrestrial human displayed to us all various projections which seemed to be
totally real in appearance. At the end of these events, the small beings, (the ones that the E.T. human
had informed us were in fact made by his people) touched everyone of us on the forehead. Everything
went away and I awoke seated in my car.
  It was now daylight outside and I was in a different area of my home town. At the time I didn't have any
idea of where I was. I knew this was not the "Bajura Road". Where was I? I had woken up in a total state
of confusion and I couldn't stop crying. The Bajura was a paved road and this was a dirt road.
  The next thing I knew, I heard the roar of military jets flying above. When I looked out the window of
my car I saw it for the first time. It was hovering without making a single sound. The F-14 Tomcats were
on their way back. Like a zombie I took hold of my cousin's camera and found the disc-shaped object
through the lens. I just stood there observing it through the lens when suddenly out of nowhere I
started to hear a strange beeping sound. Automatically I would snap a photo on every second beep.
Beep-beep-snap-beep-beep-snap.
  The disc tilted itself at an odd angle and shot off straight into the sky. The jets flew into formation and
they took off frustrated in their attempts to ground the alien aircraft.
  After this experience my life was never the same. These photographs have brought me lots of grief. In
the near future I hope to be able to publish my book which I'm now completing, where my experience in
its totality will be described in depth. There are many wonderful things out there awaiting us, but let us
not forget the many wonderful things that we have right here on earth .... Amaury Rivera Toro
