    Our Review                                                                                      Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  A chance to apply the diagnositic criteria you learned in DSM-IV. This book provides a series of cases --
  short descriptions of patients and their symptoms. You have to decide what their diagnosis should be...
  borderline, bipolar, etc. Makes an amusing parlor game for those who are so inclined.
