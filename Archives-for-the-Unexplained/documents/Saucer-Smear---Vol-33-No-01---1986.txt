IN WHICH
YOUR EDITOR IS UNEXPECTEDLY SUBJECTED TO
A CLOSE ENCOUNTER OF THE FOURTH KIND!

      Recently Jerry Clark of FATE and Phil Klass of CSICOP have been argu­
ing by mail over the question of whether the UFO field engages (as it prop­
erly should) in internal self-criticism. Clark says yes; Klass says no.
We agree with Clark on this one, because he came up with a recent example
of ufological self-criticism which is so extreme as to be ludicrous.
      Dr. J. Allen Hynek of CUFOS is editor-in-chief of "The International
UFO Reporter", which is supposedly distributed to all paid-up associates.
of CUFOS (though we have never received it, for some reason! ) Jerry Clark
is currently editor of IUR, under Hynek.--These two gentlemen disagree with
each other strongly over the merits of the Westchester County, N.Y. ("Boom­
erang from Outer Space") flap, in that Clark disparages it, while Hynek in­
sists that some of the events are genuine Unknowns. This in itself is in­
teresting, since Hynek is a scientist while Clark, though an experienced
editor, is not. One might therefore expect Hynek to take the more cautious
approach rather than Clark, who has a history of a strong Will to Believe.
      In any case, this dispute over the merits of the Westchester flap has
spilled onto the pages of IUR. In the July/August 1985 issue (published
much later than its dateline), Hynek takes his own editor to task, and
seems particularly upset over an anti-Boomerang letter-to-the-editor that
Clark.wrote for the July 1st 1985 issue of "Saucer Smear". In his signed
editorial in IUR, Hynek characterizes "Smear" as a "journal of lesser re­
pute" (which it certainly is! ); "sometimes called unprintable";  and "no­
torious". All this because we printed Clark's letter, whereas in the past
Hynek has always seemed friendly & tolerent toward "Smear" and its humble
editor!
      But the final irony is that, at the same time, Clark is mad at us
because of an editorial we wrote knocking the complex Rendlesham case 1n
England. Here Clark is the pro-UFO person, and your editor is seen as be­
ing too skeptical and critical.   (Hynek's opinion on Rendlesham is   not
known to us.)
      Why is Hynek so upset about Clark's attitude on Westchester? Appar­
ently he has put a lot of time into studying this flap, and is involved in
a book about it with UFO researcher Philip Imbrogno, who has resigned from
CUFOS because of Clark's attitude!
      We are not claiming to have the last word on either of these two com­
plicated cases (Westchester and Rendlesham.) We merely insist that there
are important adverse factors in each, making both of these cases far less
than ideal. In regard to the "Boomerang", it was found that night-flying
stunt pilots may well have been behind the otherwise unexplained sightings.
Perhaps this is not really the full answer after all, in view of the fact
that many of the witnesses insist strongly that what they saw was a single
dimly-lit craft, rather than a formation of small planes.
                                                          (2)
       But if the small-plane theory is incorrect, what are the alterna­
tives? Are we to believe that stunt pilots went out again & again to de­
liberately confuse people about a real UFO? Has there ever been another
case of the same UFO persisting in flying over one area again & again over
a· period of months? Common sense would seem to�ll us that there must be
a reasonable connection between the stunt pilots and the "Boomerang ". If
not. then this flap is weird indeed and totally without precedent!
       We have always respected Hynek, and it goes without saying that we
feel terribly sorry for him in regard to the serious illness he still faces.
However, we are disappointed in his lack of scientific objectivity shown
here. As for Clark's attitude, we are not surprised.
       Hynek's editorial in IUR is reprinted on the back page of this issue.
      P.S:  In a very recent telephone interview, Jerry Clark tells us that
he and Hynek have made up, but that Hynek has resigned the editorship   of
IUR for health reasons, leaving Clark in charge for now.
