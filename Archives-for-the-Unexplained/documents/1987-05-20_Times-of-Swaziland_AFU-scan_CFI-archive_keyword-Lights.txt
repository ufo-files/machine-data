    Without exception,
diseases are caused by
diet imbalances. Every
food is hot or cold, wet
or dry. Honey is hot, for
example, and large fish,
which are cold, cause
lung diseases if eaten to
excess.
    Swahili doctors treat
diseases with dietary
changes and herbal and
mineral medicines. They
don't perform surgery,
leaving that to Western­
style physicians in Mom­
basa's modern hospitals.
