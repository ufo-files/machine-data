    Our Review                                                                       Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  This is a hand-painted sculpture of Bob Lazar doing what he does best: reluctantly explaining anti-gravity
  propulsion systems at the blackboard. He is wearing a white lab coat, quite useless of course, but it does
  make him look more like a "scientist." The Research Center commissioned an anonymous artist to produce
  this work, preserving in rock-like material the "Little Bob" we fondly remember. Forget the missing
  educational credentials and that sordid brothel case; this Bob is real and will never move from his dignified
  position. This model is Totally Unauthorized by the other Bob, so it will be available only from the
  Research Center and in limited quantities. Each model is hand-produced and hand-painted by the artist.
  This heirloom is not sold in stores at any price! -- Glenn Campbell
