  Their search system is new, and as you might guess, buggy. It takes a while
  (and a bit of educated guesswork) to find what you're looking for.
  http://www.coresw.com
  Has a searchable database of Russian (Sovinformsuptnik- HiRes), French
  (SPOT series), and American (Landsat) imagery that has *preview* images you
  can download. There are three big caveats, however: *You need to register
  to use it, though it's FREE *You need pretty precise coordinates to find
  what you're looking for. Glenn has a great set for Groom and Papoose Lakes
  on his site, but finding images of other interesting places (Holloman,
  Marana, Tonopah, Beale, Edwards, Yongbyon, etc.) takes a bit of book work.
  The Delorne atlases are great for finding coordinates though, and, again,
  the Research Center has the full set for the USA.
  *Their search engine is only a few months new and is somewhat buggy. You'll
  get a good percentage of "sludge" hit- images you don't want. Forinstance,
  when seaching Landsat for Groom, I regularly get images of my home state of
  NJ (Cape May- nice pics of the Coast Guard Acadamy and the ferry) and
  Italy. Use at your own frustration.
  I'd recommend Core Software's ImageNet site for advanced researchers right now.
  Dan Z
