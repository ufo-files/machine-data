MEMORIES OF THE 1980 NATIONAL UFO CONFERENCE:

      Well, it came off on June 20th and 21st as scheduled - the 17th an­
nual National UFO Conference (NUFOC), at the Doral Inn in Manhattan, Big
Apple. The oldest and most prestigious of the nation-wide "Middle Ufology"
groups again showed the doubters that we are in the Field to stay:    All
things considered, it was an unexpectedly successful & meaningful event.
To re-cap briefly:
      First came the Press Conference, on the morning of Friday June 20th,
attended by most of our out-of-town speakers. Due to intense effort by
our publicity team of Harold Salkin and Mike Luckman, at least thirty gen­
uine members of the Media were present (and many others came Saturday),
leading to publicity from, among others: Channels 4, 7, and 11 in New York;
WOR, WMCA, WBAI, WNEW-FM and WPIX-FM radio in New York; The New York Post;
The New York News; United Press International, etc.
      Press interest centered largely on Bob Sheaffer's anti-UFO views,which
he expressed there, and on a group of life-sized alien heads and statues
brought by a genial artist named Georg·e Rackus, to whom we are very grate­
ful. There was also interest in our group's challenge to President Carter
and the other presidential candidates, re release of UFO information to the
public. This challenge was contained in two of our several press releases.
      On Friday evening there was a dinner at the World Trade Center   (the
world's tallest or second tallest buildings) for most of the Convention
speakers and several others of-the "hard core".
      The next morning at· 10 a.m., a "closed -session" began, consisting
first of a relatively short business meeting, during which business    of
primary interest only to delegates was discussed. It was_decided there
that next �ummer's annual meeting, on a date ye·t to be determined, will be
held in Oshkosh, Wisconsin, by Ronald Spanbauer, of the International liFO­
Photo Exchange. It was also decided that one resignation from the Confer­
ence's governing board (called "The Permanent Organizing Cornmittee") would
be handled as follows:  Charles Wilhelm, director of OUFOOL of Cincinnati,
Ohio, who resigned under an ominous Cloud of incompetence (see details fur­
ther along in this issue), would be replaced by Tom Benson of New Jersey,
the man who brilliantly set up and supervised the "closed session" of the
1980 convention.
      As in previous years, the "closed session" consisted mainly of speak­
ers who had something very worthwhile to say, but who, nevertheless, were
thought by the Permanent Organizing Committee to have views or subjects
not of wide enough interest for the general public.
      These formal speakers at the "closed session", who spoke without pay
and to whom we are exceedingly grateful, include: Art Goodstone; Gary Le­
vine; Marcel Guesnon (replacing Ward Campbell, who couldn't make it); Bob ·
Jones of "Vestigia"; Leon Davidson; and Bob Warth of SITU. Dr. Davidson
went so far as to prepare his own press release regarding his presentation,
and this we have read and enjoyed.
      The "closed session" was marred only by Eugene Steinberg, . a member
of the "Permanent Organizing Committee", who arrived an hour late for the

THE WILHELM CONSPIRACY

      We mentioned that we would have more to say about Charles Wilhelm,
director of Cincinnati, Ohio's OUFOOL organization, and former member of
the National UFO Conference.
      When Mr. Wilhelm resigned from our organization earlier this year, we
could only imagine that it had something to do with the conspiciously in­
competent way that OUFOOL, under Wilhelm's insipid leadership, ran the 1979
National UFO Conference. The attendance was-dismal, due to an almost total
lack of publicity, and the speeches were almost uniformly mediocre  • .



      However, we eventually learned that Wilhelm was resigning in anger,

not in shame.    His anger was heightened by misstatements that· inadvertent­
ly crept into one of our early press releases for the 1980 Conference, and
he thereupon wrote us an absurdly arrogant registered letter demanding that
we retract the news release in question.    When we refused to do this, he
started going . to frenzied lengths to sabotage our convention - such as
writing Stan Friedman in an attempt to get him to back out as a speaker
and writing to the Post Office Dept. asking them to get out an injunction
to prevent our convention from taking place!    (How ridiculous can he get?
If every convention that includes misstatements of some kind were banned,
what chance would the Republicans and Democrats have to hold theirs??)
      In conjunction with such people as "Dr. " Mcintyre of Maryland and
Dennis Pilichis of Ohio, Wilhelm has organized an anti-NUFOC "Middle Ufol­
ogy" group called the "Coalition of Concerned Ufologists".    (Remember all
those anonymous letters awhile back, from "Concerned", "Damn Concerned",
etc? Every time we hear the word, we can't help but remember that weird
epidemic of hoax letters, some of which were extremely vicious and too lu­
rid to be printed even in our liberal zine. )
      No name is given on the "Coalition" literature we have obtained, but
the address at the bottom of the page is "Dr. " Mcintyre's. A preliminary
meeting is to be held at Kent State University, Ohio, on the weekend of
August 2Jrd.    We have no further details, and would probably suppress them
if we did!
      It is beyond us as to how Wilhelm hopes to unite "Middle Ufology"
while using the vicious tactics he has demonstrated so far.    Less vicious
- in fact, flattering - is an imitation of our zine apparently published
by "Dr. " Mcintyre.   The only issue we have seen is called "Saucer Feces",
and includes some decent cartoon work and some semi-amusing passages. True
fans of our zine should swamp the good "Doctor" with requests for this par­
ody.  His address is: Box 218, Kensington, Md. 20795.
      So, it seems that in our Ufological wanderings we have picked up some
enemies along the way.    Sad, but hardly tragic.  The National UFO Confer­
ence will go right on, and will probably still be around long after Charles
Wilhelm has faded from the scene.    And of course, the Saucer Entities are
no doubt still laughing at all of us, from their 3�-D hideout, somewhere
