         Office Activity
         A local woman reported having an audio account of a recent abduction experience. After
         an initial interview, she failed to reestablish contact in order to tum over materials for
         scrutiny as had been agreed upon.

         The Edmonton Sun (Canada) inquired about the release of UFO documents by the
         Canadian Government.

         The Fund disseminated a press release regarding the closure of the British Flying Saucer
         Bureau.

         Three radio interviews have been done this quarter, however, office activity remains low.


         Press Newsletter
         Eleven issues of The UFO Report have been distributed to the press. There has been
         virtually no feedback and nothing to indicate that reporters are using The UFO Report as
         a source of information. Unfortunately, concurrent with the time that The UFO Report
         has been issued, there has been little UFO activity. Publication of The UFO Report will
         be suspended, but may be revived on short notice if conditions should change.

              New Proposals
              Angela Thompson Smith has contacted the Fund about publishing Paula Underwood's
              writings. The late Paula Underwood was a Native American with knowledge ofNative
              American philosophies. The writings concern the mythology of early Americans'
              interactions with the so-called Star People.

              Steve Kaeser proposed the creation of a new CD containing several classic CUFOS
              reports (Kelly-Hopkinsville and Bloecher's 1947 catalog plus one Fund report). This
              would be done jointly with CUFOS and the Fund.
