which, conveniently, is not
far from Clarksburg, where

       At the urging of these

upon what we thought was a
classic photograph (shown on
this page.) Here we sup­

       UFO pioneer George Hunt Williamson, who had lived a secluded life
in Long Beach, California in recent years, died of a heart attack about
Jan. 25th, 1986. In the 1950's Williamson was the author of "Secret
Places of the Lions" and several other books on saucers and South Ameri­
can archaeology. He made several trips to Peru and founded a metaphysi­
cal colony there. To us, Williamson is best known as one of the six wit­
nesses to George Adamski's famous "Desert Contact" with a Venusian, which
became the basis of the best-selling book Adamski co-authored with Des­
mond Leslie in 1953, called "Flying Saucers Have Landed". Few knew that
Williamson's real name was Michel d'Obrenovic, and that George Hunt Wil­
liamson was just a pen name. Williamson is survived by a close friend
and co-worker named Dame Thelma Dunlap, who helped him with various new
manuscripts the author was preparing for publication at the time of hi.s
death. . ..
