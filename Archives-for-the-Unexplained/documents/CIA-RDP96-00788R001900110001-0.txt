 attempt to obtain information relevant to the activities of 8

     The designated location was an area with rail lines or a
 rail yard in a built-up area.    Source described it as "a
 marshaling yard of some kind" because of the large amount of
 equipment and material. At this location were heavy trucks,
 different types of armored vehicles, heavy tanks with heavy
 gun barrels and front armor plating, large boxes, material
 (not further identified), and people. There was also a high
 skeletal str:ucture made of metal at this location.   Source

 angular cross braces. The location also included a multilane
 road and rows of buildings, some with smoke stacks.

     On 8 April 1983, armor (not further identified) , square
 boxes (not further identified) and long thin containers (not
 further identified) were being unloaded from trains.   These
 items were then loaded onto large trucks and moved within the
 "marshaling yard" to a large "containment area."
     On 9 April 1983, more trains were ,unloaded and convoys
 left the rail yard area in a southeasterly direct ion.   The
 convoys seemed to run parallel to the rail line and then turn
 more south.    In the convoys were tanks (not further
 identified), trucks (not further identified), armored
 personnel carrier type vehicles wi tb wheels (not further
 identified), and long flat trailers which carried some armored
 vehicles and/or long narrow boxes (not further identified).
