Ball lightning knocked pedestrians 
Time: May 2, afternoon
Place: Svaneke on the Danish island of Bornholm in the Baltic

”BALL LIGHTNINGS KNOCKED DOWN PEDESTRIANS IN THE STREETS. A violent storm passed, on Thursday afternoon, through the small town Svaneke on Bornholm. Ball-lightnings burst out in the houses. Several people in the streets were hit by the lightning and thrown to the ground, some of them paralyzed and with numbness for several hours afterwards. On several instances the lightnings twined round the legs of people and continued out in the street where they exploded with large sparks thrown in all directions. Great damages were caused on the electrical wires and the telephone net. Almost all fuses in the town burnt out and many radio receivers were destroyed. The church steeple was split by a lightning-bolt.

Status: Ball lightning
Source: Sydöstra Sveriges Dagblad, May 4.
