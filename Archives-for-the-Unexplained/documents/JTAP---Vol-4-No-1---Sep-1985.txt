Research and investigation into unidentified flying object (UFO) phenomena has progressed
frorn the early days of wild speculation into an area where scientific analysis lnd-

It is realised that ufological research is subject to a great deal of speculative
conment, much of which lies on the boundaries of current scientific thought. Many existing
scientific institutions accept limited discussion of UFOs and related phenonena where it
has some bearing on their discipline. The Journal of Transient Aerial Phenomena (Journal
