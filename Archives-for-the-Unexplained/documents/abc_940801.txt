1994.08.01      GROOM LAKE, NV / SECRET AIR BASE / ENVIRONMENTAL
PROBLEMS
5:49:40-5:54:20              Monday               ABC
 
   (Studio: Forrest Sawyer)  Report introduced.
 
   (On the road to Groom Lake: Cynthia McFadden)  ABC News
investigation into worker claims of dangerous environmental practices
committed by the government at a secret base in Groom Lake, NV,
examined.  [Two disguised WORKERS - talk about their health problems
from the smoke from trenches at the base in which classified material
were burned.]  The death of base sheet metal worker Robert Frost
recalled; photos shown.  [Rutgers University professor Peter KAHN,
widow Helen FROST - talk about Frost's case.]  The government's
opposition to the workers' lawsuit which is deemed in violation of the
need for secrecy at Groom Lake noted.  [George Washington law center
professor Jonathan TURLEY - comments.]
