Case xxx.
A sighting from Finland

Time: July 28, at 00.52 hours (local time)
Place: Centre of Perniö. Finland.

The story of the witness given to a Finnish UFO researcher in the early 1990´s:

It was a warm summernight. There were no clouds in the sky. We were on our way home from a woman called Martta whome we had visited. There were three men among us; me, Veikko and a friend (a building contractor). I was much younger then them and I am the only one alive today. Martta, from whom we were on our way home, was also with us and maybe some other women. Martha is still alive, she is little younger than I am.

We had been to a party that´s why we were out so late. I don´t remember that we had consumed any alchohol that night. In any case I was in good condition and my head was clear. 

We stopped for a while, had a conversation, on the country road close to Lampola. While standing there we observed something special in the sky. All of us saw a luminous object which was flying on a very high altitude. I can no longer remember the exact course of the object but since it had already passed over us in the sky when we noticed it in the northwest, I suppose it´s direction of flight was towards the west or the northwest or somewhere in between. 

It was no meteorit because it was flying horizontally and the speed was also to slow for a meteorite. The witness has estimated the speed, altitude and size. If the altidude, only estimated, supposedly was 10 km or about the same as a regular air liner, the object might have been about the same size as a regular air liner. The object seemed fairly long. The object wasn´t visible and additionally it was a bit dark at night. We only saw the light it gave off or reflected. I don´t remember it having a fireflame in the rear. In any case we believed that it was some kind of a rocket sent out by any superpower for testing. The object made no sound what so ever. We followed it in the sky for a couple of minutes before it slowly disappeared out of our field of vision behind the trees and buildings.

I have been a pensioner for almost three years and  have during this period read rather a lot of books about U.F.O:s.... Regarding the light-phenomenon I saw in 1946 I am absolutely certain it was caused by an object which has been manufactured by an intelligent being whether it was a rocket, type V2, made by some foreign government, or something else. 

Status: Probable meteor.
