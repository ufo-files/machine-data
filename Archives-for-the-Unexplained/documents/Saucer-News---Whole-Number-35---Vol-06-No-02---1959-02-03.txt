                          - by Thomas M. Comella -

                 A great change is taking place in UFO research. Those investigators
who used to believe that the flying saucers are real, are now not so sure.
This existing attitude has brought serious research to a virtual standstill,
because everyone is asking, "'·fuat are we investigating, any1rray?11 For almost

Universe. This lesson is in two parts, one a corollary of the other, and it is
of such fundamental importance that it might provide us with the very key.to
the UFO mystery.
                  Man is a rational being. That is, his mind operates logically, or
according to the process of reasoning   •   . That which is not logical is, by defi­
nition, chaotic and not meaningful, for logic is the human being 1 s index for
intelligence. For centuries Man has searched for the secrets of the Universe,
and out of this search has come the science and technology of today. However,
all science and technology is based upon one assumption - that the Universe is
not chaotic or unmeaningful; that it is logical and can be understood by the
human mind. This is the crux of the UFO problem as well as of untold other
mysteries.
                  When Man attacks a problem, he proceeds logically and his solution
conforms vrith the facts that his mind perceives. For years this method has
yielded very practical results. Once in awhile, though, Man is confronted with
a situation that is different. The UFO problem is such a situation. For ten
years, ��n has applied logic to the UFO and he has failed miserably to resolve
the mystery. In 1947, after the initial shock had worn off, it was believed
that Man1 s proven formula would solve the UFO saga. All that was needed was
time, money, and the human mind. Man1 s inability to cope with this problem has
been neatly covered up, I believe, by the very workings of his mind. He has
rationaliz ed that because his problem-solving formula failed, there wasn1 t any
mystery to begin with. In other words, if our logical approach finds nothing,
there must be nothing there.
                  But there is a fallacy here, and a very prominent one! For the UFO
is not a logical quantity. It has been proven time and time againJ These
strange obj ects show us no pattern, no reasonable conformity to what we know
as intelligence, and no purpose. We have applied logic to an illogical quan­
tity. This is like trying to force a square peg into a round hole; It simply
will not work. But just because it will not work, there is no reason to de­
clare that the round hole is nonexistent. We must find the round peg before we
can solve the UFO mystery.
                  Consider for a moment a race of beings from some planet in the
depths of space - a race that, by our standards,is almost completely undetect­
