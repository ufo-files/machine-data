  It seems that proboards doesn't allow free forum operators to take backups of their forum database. Does anyone know
  if they offer that possibility for paying customers? If they don't, then it seems that software like Mouse Trace is the only
  way OM admins could possibly spy on their users private discussions. As the PMs are stored in the database just like
  any other message, it would be easy to read them from there (and without any possibility of getting caught).

  Sites that either use their own servers or buy a virtual server from a hosting company and who install the software by
  themselves have full access to all the server content so ... There's very little on the internet that is secure. And Proboards
  admins of course can view everything and anything that is on their servers, as can be seen confirmed here.

  Less security means ease of use, more security means more difficult (or slower) to use. Most people pick ease of use
  over security because they either have nothing to hide (as most people do) or are naïve enough to think that no one will
  snoop on them. Every time you send a message in plain text to someone else in an environment you have no full control
  of, you take the risk that the message could also be read by persons other than the recipient.
