   
   Anthropologists, Spencer and Gillen, recorded in their 1899 study that the

   Australia, had a myth which described how a group of women, the
   Kunkarunkara, were protected from the unwelcome attentions of the moon-man,
   Kula. Another variation, indicates they were being continuously pursued, and
   occassionally raped, by Jula, a man of the constellation of Orion. The women,
   legend has it, finally escaped into the sky and became the Pleiades. [12]
