SUBJECT: SRV report for week of 20 JUL - 23 JUL,    1987

1.  Early sessions brought out a scene of people and equipment
lined up on barren ground.  This did not change throughout the
sessions.

2.  Early sessions indicated military vehicles, OD green in
color, but the people were wearing 1) solid dark blue clothes
<AOL: like an Air Force uniform) and 2) dark blue trousers
w/light blue shirt <AOL: like a blue dress shirt).  The ground
was perceived as bare gravel only.

light, and very strange looking, due to the lighting.  The ground
was perceived as looking like coffee-grounds in both color and
textur.  The impression was very strong that the scene might be
under water.  The people were seen dressed then in 1) solid blue
and 2) dark/light blue wet suits.  The machines were perceived as
being light/dark green, and tubular in structure.
