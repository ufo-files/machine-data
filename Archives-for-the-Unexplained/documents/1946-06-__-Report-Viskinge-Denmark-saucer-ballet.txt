Case xxx.
A ”saucer” ballet over Denmark

Time: early June or July 1946, about 14.00 hours
Place: Viskinge, between Jyderup and Kalundborg (on Sjaelland, Danmark)

Loren Gross, the tireless chronicler of American ufology, wrote about this case: ”Unfortunately the following case was not published in a 1946 newspaper which makes it suspect and not good evidence that ”flying saucers” were seen prior to 1947”. We continue to cite from Gross (with some alterations in view of the original clipping, which is the AFU files):

”A Mr. Hans Sörensen, a citizen of Denmark, wrote a letter to the Danish newspaper Sjaellands Tidende in the 1970´s concerning an aerial phenomenon he witnessed 25 years before:

”In the summer of 1946, I was cycling [on the way] from Jyderup to Kalundborg. A couple of kilometres outside Viskinge, I suddenly noticed something odd. At first I thought I saw smoke rings from a tractor, so I stopped in a spot, where there were no trees, so as to see better. Then I saw three thingamajings fitting the descriptions of UFO´s. They flew towards me, on a   vertical upwards course. They sort of tilted in the middle, choppily [?], not very rapidly. They were like polished mirrors on top, and in profile almost like a flat and deep saucer. The underside was uneven and dull grey. That was in July, in calm weather. It was about 2.00 p.m. [14.00], and the sun was behind me. As I stood there, several people came up, and we concurred that there were no sounds, such as a normal aircraft would make. Hans Sörensen, Klövermarksvej 16, 4450 Jyderup.”

The witness was contacted by the Scandinavian UFO Information (SUFOI) of Copenhagen and filled out one of their questionnaires. From this, and a short note on the incident in SUFOI:s magazine UFO-Nyt, we can deduce the following supplementary details:

The objects first went straight up [vertically] from the ground on the right side [of the road]. The shape was like a disc, round, and they had a small superstructure in the middle. The diameter of the cupola was about ¼ of the object´s [diameter]. As they reached a certain altitude, they stopped abruptly and then continued horizontally - then to stop once again and then change direction of flight. They flew up and down, back and forth, for 20 minutes. Trees obstructed the objects when they were down close to the ground. The diameters of the object were estimated to be about 30 meters. As the objects flew away, after this ”aerial ballet” Hans Sörensen remained standing on the road with ten other passers-by, discussing the phenomenon.  

Hans Sörensen was 26 years old in 1946. In the questionnaire (and in UFO-Nyt) Sörensen has changed the time to June 1946 [with a question mark after]. It was a clear, windless summers day, about 25 degrees centrigrade. The objects were like Danish ten-öre coins at arm´s length. The other [about 10] spectators were unknown to the reporting witness. 

Status: Unidentified
Sources: Loren Gross: The mystery of the ghost rockets, second ed., 1982, pp. 12-13; Sjaellands Tidende, April 17, 1971; SUFOI questionnaire dated May 16, 1971 (case 194-017); UFO-Nyt, 1971, nr 4, page 162.
