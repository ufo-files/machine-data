(AV) of a Carrier Group exercise.

or naval-based exercise. My sense is that a new organization or
unit wlll be involved.     It will be a new operation for the
country(ies) involved. The activity is definitely associated with
water.

probably the Arabian Sea area. It will take place in an area which
is characteristically shallow and where the ocean surface is
generally smooth.

some new equipment, assets or technology.      It has to do with
launching or releasing a smaller vessel from beneath a larger one;
the pursuit of, and eventual attachment of a small object onto,

these larger vessels pass through a subsurface "beam" of sorts,
this beam in turn triggers or otherwise affects the small object
attached to the ship.

water. Pointed and backward slanting projections angling up from
its upper surface each produce a constant stream of bubbles. This
