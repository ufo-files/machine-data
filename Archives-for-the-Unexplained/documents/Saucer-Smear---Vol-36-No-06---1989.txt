GLORIOUS RE-HASH OF MUfON's LAS VEGAS CONVENTION:
       During the period from July 6th through 9th, your editor and some
500 other devotees attended the 29th annual MUFON conVention, held this
year in one of Las Vegas's ma jor casino-hotels.     I_
       In addition to the convention, which was weird enough, your editor
had two rather unusual experiences: ·The first was the fact that the air­
line lost our bag for three full days, till after the\convention was over!
This helps to explain the lack of photos herein, as our camera was in the
bag. The second experience was even more startling: �or the first time
in dozens of visits to gambling joints, we actually won a respectable
amount of money. Our explanation for this, which we_canno�,prove, is that
the Alladin Hotel was going bankrupt ( as mentioned on TV news while      we
were there) , and decided to "open up" the slot machines to attract more
business. Either that, or else we have finally perfected our previously
non-existent psychic powers.
       The convention was wild, wild, wild, just as we had anticipated! The
tone was set by Bill Lear, MUFON's eccentric state director for       Nevada.
Originally Lear was to be the convention's chairman, but when it became
obvious to the MUFON hierarchy that Lear was semi-incommunicado, Hal Starr
stepped in from neighboring Arizona as cO-chairman; and indeed it       was
Starr who did all of the MC'ing. Not all of Starr's humorous        remarks
would make it on the Johnny Carson Show, but he performed well, as        the
( very) old Pro that he most assuredly is.
       Lear even failed to appear at the press conference Friday morning,
where most of the convention speakers gave a brief summary of their views.
Most astounding was the fact that the "real" press was barely        present
among the approx. 50 people in the press conference audience. When Walt
Andrus asked for questions from the press, there were none. This boggles
the mind, as the speakers had just made allegations which, if true, would
change the destiny of the planet Earth! Obviously, no one present        was
ready to take this stuff seriously. Nor were we.
       Lear got into gear on Sunday morning, however, as lead-off among a
group of "unofficial speakers", billed in the MUFON program as an "added
featura". of the convention. Translation: Lear and his cohorts -        Bill
Cooper, Bill English, and Don Ecker - were too "far out" even for the lib­
eral official MUFON format, which was wild enough & included government
conspiracy, crashed saucers, cattle mutilation, and numerous other horrors.
       Among Lear's revelations: The Government UFO investigation is fi­
nanced by illegal drugs from Southeast Asia; The Government has 15 to 20
captured UFOs; There are 30 to 100 alien bodies at Wright-Patterson AFB
and Homestead AFB in Florida ( uncomfortably close to Key West! ) ;    Seven
alien civilizations are currently visiting us; Pro ject Grudge Report #13
( thought by many to be a hoax) was co-authored by the late Dr. Hynek and
includes photos of humans mutilated by aliens.
