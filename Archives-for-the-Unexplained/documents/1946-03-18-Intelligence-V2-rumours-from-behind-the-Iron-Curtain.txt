March 18: V-2 rumours from behind the (new) ”Iron Curtain”

A TT newswire, with the [British] Daily Mail as the original source, distributed on March 19 helped set the scene for what was to occur in the spring and summer of 1946:

”SECRET V-2 EXPERIMENTS. London, March 18 (NTB from Reuters). German scientists and technicians who work under Russian supervision will shortly release a number of V2-bombs from a secret research stations on the Baltic, reports the Daily Mirror Berlin correspondent, Edwin Tetlow. The Soviet Union follows the example of the British who fired V2-bombs from Cuxhaven in October last. 

”The bombs that are to be used for the secret experiments on the Baltic coast, were found almost completed by Russian troups in a German factory in Mecklenburg, about 95 kilometres northwest of Berlin. They have later been completed into fullgrown V2-bombs by scientists who worked on them for Hitler during the war.”

”Other messages from the east, the correspondent continues, confirm that the Soviet-Russians systematically seek to find out everything the Germans know about the nuclear bomb, V-weapons and other ultra modern war innovations. Physicists who helped by planning of the German cyclotrones , huge magnetic machines used for nuclear fission are among the experts now working for the Russians.”

Source: NTB newswire published March 18 by Aftenposten and Dagbladet. TT newswire published by Sydöstra Sveriges Dagblad, Umebladet, Norra Västerbotten and other Swedish papers on March 19.
