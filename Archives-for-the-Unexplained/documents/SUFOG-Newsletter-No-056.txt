Whilst going through my press articles for this months newsletter, I came across the
articles relating to the famous photograph taken by Gordon Faulkner on the 29th
August 1965. The articles from April last year, claimed that the photographs were in
fact of a cotton reel and a few other bits and pieces, and another sighting bit the dust
(Although most people seemed not to believe the April story). Over the years, many
photographs and video films have been proven as hoaxes or a mis-understanding of the
object captured on film i.e. airplanes,birds,stars,planets etc ... And, I feel that the
Roswell film certainly is a hoax and more and more people appear to be thinking along
the same lines. As previously published, the film has not caught the media's eye as I
assume the finder(s) thought it would, and the media has been silent and have not
been promoting it. There are many under currents in this case and in the May/June
edition of UFO Magazine, Stanton Friedman (Who has been denied access to the film
footage), knocks a few more holes in the film :


I. No military records could be found of the 82 year-old American gentleman who
   allegedly made a secret copy of the film.


2. No body at Kodak has heard or seen anything about this film (Which is contrary
   to Mr. Philip Mantle's (BUFORA) comments in the National press in March 95).


3. President Truman's diaries for the period showed he made no visits outside of
   Washington De at the time, although he is allegedly shown in the film footage.


  And a 4th thing to knock another hole in the already very shaky foundations, is
that the film shows a telephone mounted on the wall with a coiled telephone wire.
It appears that this type of cable was not used on phones in this period of time and
came out in approx. 1949/50.


  Hoaxes come and go and some hang around to haunt the UFO investigator for years
to come. I mean to say, if one admits that a photograph or video is genuine without
thorough investigation and/or research, and takes the footage on its merits, then watch
out, you will either get your fingers badly burnt, or even live to regret the day, as the
press is not as forgiving as some investigators are.

20. 5.95: Southampton Evening Echo: Stalk of the town as circle crops up:

  Summer is here - as reliable as the first swallow, the first crop circle of the year has
been spotted in Hampshire.
  The mysterious pattern cropped up in a field of oilseed rape between Winchester and
Basingstoke. Experts are keeping its exact location secret because the farmer is
worried about hordes of sightseers trampling his crop.
  The complex shape is made up of six circles and straight lines and boffins have
already examined it to determine its authenticity.
  The riddle of the circles fascinated the nation in the late 1980s and early 1990s with
theories ranging from electrified whirlwinds to little green men.
  But in 1991 it appeared the mystery had been solved when two Southampton
pensioners, Doug Bower and Dave Chorley, claimed to have been making the shapes
for years. The duo were rubbished by the experts, and although they announced their
retirement, circles continued to appear.
  Lucy Pringle, who has been researching the phenomenon for ten years, has inspected
the fonnation - being dubbed a 'pictogram' - on the ground.
  'It is a magnificent location and the circle is really very spectacular,' she said. 'There
are some broken stems but most of them are not broken'.
   Oilseed rape - the yellow crop that is now a familiar sight in the fields in the South -
has thick stems similar to 'sticks of celery'.
  crop circle experts say it is impossible to bend oilseed rape stems without snapping
them. They point to this as a reason why the circles cannot be hoaxed.

1994 Crop-Circle season having visited nearly 50 formations and many night watches
around the active areas. The 1995 season has begun late compared to last year but
already there has been more formations than this time last year, although tell-tale
damage has appeared suggesting less mystical forces are at work, so I'll leave that can
of worms alone for the time being and concentrate on Crop-circle related matters. One
of the most famous fields for Crop-circles not only in England but who could argue,
the world, has to be the large field near Alton Bames called East Field. During 90 - 92
    this field graced many of the world tabloids with it's breathtaking pictograms including
 the early double pictogram, the huge 'Dodman' or snail of 1992 and the suspicious
·
 'Eye' of last year to name a few. Is there any reason why this area is so active during
    the summer nights? Perhaps a team of shy expert hoaxers picked East Field out of a
    hat some years ago and the legend has grown around this, however, I think more of a
    clue lies in the past.
  As is well known one of England's most famous tracks- the prehistoric Ridgeway
passes near the field overlooking the village's of Alton Priors and Alton Barnes. The
whole area around Alton Barnes is littered with Barrows, Hillforts, Camps and Stone
Circles, the importance of this land to our ancestors is hard to imagine although many
signs still remain. This land was and in some ways still is for a few, a Sacred and
wondrous area, I always feel very eager to return when coming home from the area
after a day or night spent nearby. It certainly has an atmosphere. Overlooking the 2
villages stands the viewpoint of Adams Grave at the top of Walkers Hill, this peak was
called Woden's Barrow or Wodensbury, however Adams Grave seemed more
acceptable to a people who were being lured away from the old religion for the
benefits of the Christian world order.


 Woden or Odin was essentially a Norse god, who upon stormy nights would career
