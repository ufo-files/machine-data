                                   IS 'UFOLOGY' LOSING ITS WAY?

    We might well ask, where are we going with 'UFOLOGY'?                    What was once the study of the
    UFO phenomena appears to be deteriorating into a stage where individuals with little interest
    in the subject can expound their theories on various issues with scant regard to the subject
    matter itself

    With the advent of the expensive 'Glossy' magazines now flooding into the newsagents, we are
    subjected to the same old repeated 'standards' of Barney and Betty Hill, Roswell, Adamski,
    Thomas Mantell etc.       We also have a series of poorly made video's entering the market, at
    exorbitant prices, plus a range of new books inferior in quality and content.                   (A pleasant
    contrast to this, is the new book ' Left At East Gate', reviewed in this issue.)

    It seems that many 'researchers' carry out very little investigation into the basic facts of               an
    incident/report, preferring to accept second-hand information as verbatim. Over recent years
    we have seen such glaring errors as the 'Foo Fighter' photograph, showing a flight of fixed u/c
    aircraft, accompanied by a blob or two of light (depending on who prints the photograph).
    Variously described as 'Allied Aircraft Over Europe/ Over The Pacific, or 'A Flight Of
    Japanese Aircraft' photographed during WW2', (It is actually a flight of Japanese aircraft over
    Manchuria in 1936). Then we are told that during WW2, the Nazi SS had designed a 'Flying
    Saucer', (complete with crosses and an 88mm gun). . .. need I go on?

    The recent 'Fife Landing Incident' (23rd September 96) is also a case that has raised many
    questions. Why for instance, was a case of such importance treated so flippantly by a number
    of 'seasoned' investigators? Questions have been asked as to why basic procedures, such as
    taking soil samples and checking the area for radiation were not carried out. Why were so few
    photographs of the area taken? Why was the landing site not minutely inspected for signs of
    alien interaction? Surely with a hundred-plus aliens wandering around, there must have been
    broken twigs, footprints or some trace residue? The lack of professionalism shown
    investigating what could well be an incident of major importance, is tragic. It is pretty obvious
    that we need to get our act together.

    There are fortunately a number of dedicated researchers such as Timothy Good, still on the
"

l
    scene and showing how UFO research should really be carried out. Tim's eye for detail and
    inquiring mind is an excellent example on how the job should be done! We are presently being
    inundated with a wealth of mediocrity on the UFO scene and researchers should concentrate
    their minds on the task ahead. That is, to collect analyse and disseminate the information and
    not to treat witnesses flippantly and sightings as being of little consequence.

Tony James wound up his last EMUFORA meeting with a flourish, when the two American
guest speakers, Larry Warren and Peter Robbins addressed the audience and gave an account
of their new book 'LEFT AT EAST GATE'. The story of the Rendlesham Forest encounter in
December 1980, when American Air Force police surrounded a landed UFO, complete with
semi-transparent aliens.

The following account has been edited to conserve space, but is an accurate reflection of
what was said
Peter Robbins began with an account of how the book had taken 9 years to write. It all began
in 1986 when Larry Warren wanted to set the record straight, after his 'account' of the incident
had been presented in so many distorted ways in so many other books and articles.       Another
factor, was the statement by the Ministry of Defence, that the Rendlesham Forest incident was
"of no defence significance" and yet it had happened next the base holding the largest stockpile
of nuclear weapons in NATO!         'Officially'   American nuclear weapons arrived here (in the
U.K.) in 1982, yet this incident happened in 1980! Larry Warren commented that "not many
Americans would have been very happy if the RAF had secretly housed weapons of mass
destruction in their backyard."

Larry continued with an amazing revelation made by the (then) Deputy Base Commander, Lt.
Col. Charles I. Halt USAF, in one of their three 'in depth' interviews. Col. Halt stated that on
the night of December 29th/30th 1980, these UFO object came over and fired pencil thin
beams of light down to the ground into the 'hardened' earth covered weapons storage facility
and allegedly affected the nuclear ordnance in an adverse way. Larry commented "Wasn't that
of some defence significance", he added that just one of these nuclear weapons could have
wiped out most of Eastern England. He went on to say that as if the fact that these craft had
neutralised a number of nuclear weapons was not enough, it was only part of the whole story.

The purpose of the book, was not to make money, Larry stated, he had been trying tell this
story for the past fifteen years. The book was being well received commented Larry, it wasn't
the best written book in the world and yet people seemed to like it.

Larry Warren then went on to explain his participation in the incident on the night of the
29th/30th December.     The USAF was on 'high alert' at the RAF Woodbridge base, because of
the Solidarity events occurring in Poland at that time.         Larry had no idea of what had
happened during the previous two nights at the base, he was only concerned with telling his
story, other people could tell theirs.

He had decided to write a book because of the many inaccuracies that he had read about,
concerning his own involvement. He wanted to put the record straight, mainly for himself and
his family.

There was a difference between 'scepticism' and 'debunking', commented Larry, one showed
disbelief, but the other involved character assassination. He had suffered many indignities as
the result of his involvement in the Rendlesham incident. At one stage his passport was seized
by the American authorities and it took high powered action by a prominent public figure, to
retrieve it.

Larry went on to say that he and Peter had decided not to have such things as 'Flying Saucers'
or 'Alien Head' on the cover or inside their book, which seemed to be the rage these days.

                                SPINNING LIGHTS OVER DERBY                            Case 97/6

On Saturday the 20th April at 11.20pm, Mr C.P. (36), had just entered his bedroom at his
home in Littleover Derby. He walked over to the widow to close the curtains and as he did so
his attention was drawn to a "cluster of lights" shining through the clouds in a NW direction.
"The bedroom was in darkness and the sky was cloudy" commented Mr C.P.

The white lights were spinning in an anti-clockwise direction, resembling a 'mirror ball' and
they travelled in a spiralling motion across the sky. The lights were not very bright, but
extensive "about tray size" as they moved across the sky from W to N and then back again.

At this point the witness ran downstairs and out of the front door to get a better view, he was
closely followed by his wife (who thought his car was being stolen!).       Mr C . P. 's wife then
watched the phenomenon with her husband, then she ran into the house to telephone her
mother, who lived just around the corner. The mother also went into her garden and observed
the lights.

The phenomenon was observed for about 25 minutes and came to an end, when the lights
'froze' in the sky. At this point, commented the witness, "it was still cloudy, but as a break in
the clouds came over, the lights just went out."

                      AN AMAZING ABDUCTION CASE FROM ' BRETB Y' IN 1973

         How many times have we heard the name 'Bretby' pop up in connection with unusual UFO
         sightings. This small village, just a mile or so, NE of Burton-on-Trent, has been the scene of
         several 'Flying Triangle' incidents over recent years.     The following account, refers not to a
         'FT', but a 'Pyramid' shaped UFO (see previous page) encountered in the winter of 1973.

         During the period 1971-74 the witness and his wife experienced a number of UFO sightings
         and they were becoming concerned that they were 'being observed'.            Over a period of years
•        they have seen a series of white moving lights in the area and have had one 'high strangeness'
         encounter. (This is related first and is independent of the other incident. . . . or was it?)

         Mr JR relates what happened when he was out walking with his wife and his German
         Shepherd dog one winter's evening. They were in a country area with a few trees dotted
         around, when they saw "a man dressed in black clothing and with a trilby hat pulled down over
         his face". "He carried a black box under his right arm and tried repeatedly to point it at us".
         "We dodged behind some trees, our dog was bearing her teeth at him." "We fled and the man
         disappeared." "Other people were walking about at the time and seemed not to notice him."

         (There have been many instances where there has been an encounter of some kind and people
         surrounding the witness have not appeared to notice anything?)
         Tbe UFO Experience: A True Account (By Witness 'JR')

         More than twenty years ago, my home was in in the Bretby area.              It was a winter's night
         (1993/4), not too cold and the sky was heavy with dark clouds, scudding before strong winds.
         I had retired to bed as usual and fallen to sleep shortly afterwards.

         I then became aware of a clanging of metal, I slowly aroused from my slumber, (my wife was
         sleeping quietly next to me).   The sound was coming from somewhere above the roof above
         my bedroom, obviously caused by the rough, gusting wind.         I then realised that the sound was
         coming from the area of the chimney stack and I wondered what was going to happen, when
         suddenly, I realised that I was not alone!

         I sensed that there was a woman positioned near to the back of my head, I recollect that she
         appeared to be about 30'ish and quite good looking, somehow this seemed to be put into my
         mind.    I next found myself in a vertical position and rising up through the roof, with the
         woman being by my left side and a little to the back of me. I didn't feel afraid, somehow my
         mind seemed pleasantly controlled, as though to avoid being alarmed or even to question what
         was happening to me. I was now rising up by the chimney stack and could see directly ahead.
         I next realised that there was a pyramid or triangular shaped object, not large, perhaps it could
         have fitted into a 14ft. square room.      It was of very simple design, with simple metal legs
         attached to each corner. It was one of these legs that was banging against the chimney stack
         and making the clanging noise as the craft gently rocked in the wind.

         In a second or two, I was seated inside the thing, with the woman standing behind the chair,
         again I didn't see much of her as my eyes seemed only capable of looking straight ahead. The
         interior was lit by a green light, the source of which, I couldn't see. Then I saw a Chinese­
         looking man, he was wearing a top similar to that which dental surgeons wear entered.           He
         was between 5'-6" to 5'-9" in height and had a slim build.

         I know his face was pleasant and he had Chinese type eyes. He was speaking to the woman,
         but I have no memory of what they said.

     he came back out the vehicle was gone.
