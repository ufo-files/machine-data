Case xxx.
Astronomer watch meteor?

Time: Monday, July 15, at 05.30 (04.30 Swedish time)
Place: Helsingfors (Helsinki)

”METEORS OR BOMBS? At 05.30 hours, Monday morning, once again a projectile of the same kind as the light phenomena seen several times this summer, was seen over Helsinki. The astronomer at the Helsinki University´s astronomical observatory, dr. W.R. Elander [Ölander?], who sighted the light phenomenon, says that there is nothing that speaks against the possibility that it could be a meteor.”

Status: Meteor
Source: Arbeiderbladet, Oslo, Norway, July 18.
