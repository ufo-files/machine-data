                   WILL THE UFONAUTS SAVE OUR PLANET?
There have been a number of events recently that have raised the question, will the UFO occupants
intervene and prevent us from destroying the environment on our planet Earth?

For several years, those of us that have been involved with interviewing 'contactees' have often heard the
familiar message that we on Earth are 'too warlike' and need to 'live in peace' and to raise our Spiritual
consciousness to a higher level of understanding.

We have been told that there is a 'Galactic Confederation' and that the people of Earth are tmworthy to join
in this consortium of interplanetary brotherhood.

In some instances 'contactees', just ordinary people, plucked at random it seems have been shown three
dimensional films/projections demonstrating coming disasters on Earth and scenes of desolation and
starving masses.

But why show these scenes to the ordinary people, when they are powerless to make any changes in the
governments and regimes of the world? Why not pluck out the leading ministers or El Supremo's from their
government offices and show them how their policies are leading the people of the world to disaster?

In this issue of the OVNI we have published two articles on page 11 and 12 that refer to messages from the
'Galactic Confederation'.   You may read them and recognise the repeated warnings of the dire conse­
quences facing our planet. On their own, the warnings have the 'old familiar ring', but now other
information from various sources is being brought to our notice.

In an e-mail headed 'Breaking News' and sent world-wide on the 19th November by the 'Siriusufo Space
Sciences Research Center' (Turkey), we have been told that no less than six airliner crews witnessed a UFO
destroying a large meteor that was heading for the Earth on a trajectory that would have caused possible
havoc and disaster. We intend to cover the testimony of the witnesses fully in the next issue of the OVNI
together with possible photographic 'stills' taken by an amateur photographer.

If the report is genuine then it could contradict our understanding, also related by 'contactees', that the
extraterrestrials observing this planet are not permitted to intervene with events taking place on Earth!

Finally an amazing story is starting to unfold of a local 'contactee' who has experienced a number of
astounding events. Initially, the PRA received a letter saying "In the past year I have had two strange things
happen to me, which I would liked to tell you, but I couldn't write it down on paper- one really upset me and
is still difficult to talk about. If I would have told anyone other than my husband and son, I know it sounds
ridiculous, but I would have feared for my safety. I will tell you, but no-one other than you!"

I subsequently arranged to visit the home of the 'contactee' on the 16th ofNovember, where the woman, her
husband and son were interviewed at length. I can only describe the story related to me as 'mind-blowing'!

Taking care to guard the anonymity of the family involved, I will reveal the extraordinary events that took
place, in the next issue of theOVNI.

                     Huge UFO seen over Derby and Ilkeston


On the night of the 30th September at 7.45pm this year, a huge brilliantly lit craft was seen passing over
Derby in the Chaddesden area, heading Southeast in the direction of Nottingham.
The initial witness was a Mr Philip D. Carter who had been sitting indoors watching television, when the
glowing object attracted his attention and was then also witnessed by his wife and two sons.
Mr Carter described what had happened:

I was sitting in my front room watching 'Coronation Street' on TV, when there was a commercial break. It
was then that I happened to look out of the window and noticed what looked like two big crosses of, very
bright lights, very low and heading West. It seemed to have appeared from the 'Pride Park' (football
stadium) direction.
I ran outside to get a better look and as I did so, it turned around slowly and changed (its attitude) to a
horizontal position, making it harder to see.
It had many bright cream coloured lights and two red lights. The red lights were flickering near the back of
the object. There was also a strange humming sound coming from it. It then seemed to slowly glide by,
heading Southeast and disappeared behind some nearby trees and rooftops. My wife and two sons said
"What was that?"
It looked to me like two identical objects, but the way they moved together gave the impression that it was
one. (See sketch).
I observed the object for about two minutes, but I couldn't be sure of the shape because of the brightness
of the lights, which appeared rectangular in shape. It seemed strangely quiet outside as it passed over.
The size, shape and the brightness of the lights made me doubt that it was man-made.

 METEOR LIGHTS UP CENTRAL UK ON SUNDAY 6 OCTOBER 2002
A burning object seen streaking across West Midlands skies on Sunday morning (6/10/02)
may have been a meteor, astronomers have said. The streaking fireball was seen heading
south shortly before 0600 BST.

Its passage was followed by a bright flash which lit up the sky. Sightings of the object were
reported at sites including Coventry, England's south coast and Wales.

Andy Salmon from the Birmingham Astronomical Society, said it may have been a meteor
- space debris burning up in the atmosphere.                         Source: UFO News UK
Meanwhile several reports were received by the PRA including one from a witness who was convinced an
aircraft was about to crash in the vicinity.

   certain plankton that light up when disturbed. This effect flickered like low flame, and did
   not extend far behind the ship before the glow dimmed out. Like an underwater magnetic
   field, dancing here and there while we watched it.


   Then we saw a large blue glow approaching our ship underwater from the stern. As it
   caught up to us, and began passing underneath, we sat on the fantail and looked right down

                                      The Evening Sky
                                       November 2002
  The ringed planet Saturn dominates the eastern sky throughout November, rising mid­
  evening after twilight has faded. Jupiter appears above the horizon four hours later and
  so you will have to wait until after midnight to get a good view of the giant planet. Venus
  and Mars are visible in the morning sky at dawn about two hours before sunrise. Venus
  is dazzlingly bright and outshines everything in the sky. Mercury is in conjunction with the
  sun and will not be visible this month.

  The Leonid meteor shower peaks on the 18th and 19th of November and promises to be
  quite spectacular in spite of a full moon. The moon's glare will overwhelm the fainter
  meteors but many bright meteors should be visible. On the 18th and 19th the Earth
  collides head-on with two dust trails: the Leonids so named after the star constellation
  Leo the Lion from whose constellation the meteor shower radiates; and the dust trails or
  meteoroid streams left by comet Temple-Tuttle when it passed close to Earth in 1769
  and 1866. Do make an effort to look and please remember to wrap up warm. If
  possible, lie on your back or sit back in a sun lounger to get the best view and simply
  enjoy the experience. I shall be observing with a glass of red wine.

  Contrary to the popular urban myth, no man-made objects on Earth can be seen from
  the moon. The Great Wall of China or the Great Pyramid at Giza are not visible by the
  naked eye from the surface of the moon. Even from a low Earth orbit of 250 miles you
  will not be able to see the Great Wall of China. You may, with excellent vision, identify
  large square objects such as the Great pyramid at Giza.

  We are often confused by the difference between an eclipse and an occultation. An
  occultation occurs when an object hides another object from view by passing in front of it
  and the observer. An eclipse involves one object passing into the shadow of another and
  there lies the subtle difference.

  The starting point, which enabled the miracle of life on Earth to evolve, still remains a
  mystery. The fossil records show primitive life appeared on Earth around four billion
  years ago. Yet the jump from inanimate to animate remains a mystery. Organic
  molecules permeate space and are carried in comet tails and space dust, which still falls
  on our planet to this day. New theories do lean towards life on Earth getting a boost from
  space. However, creating a pre-biotic soup seems fairly easy but making the leap to life
  is a huge one and one that has not yet been replicated in our laboratories.

  Full Moon occurs on the 19th. November Moon names are Snow Moon, Dark Moon,
  Fog Moon and Mad Moon. November was the ninth month in the oldest Roman
  calendar. In the Ce�tic tradition, it was the beginning of a New Year. The Celtic year
  ended on the eve of Samhain (Halloween) and began again on the day after. They
  considered it a Moon (month) of beginnings and endings and many still do.
