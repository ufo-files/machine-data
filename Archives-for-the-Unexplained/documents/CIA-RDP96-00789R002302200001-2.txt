                      Summary of Information                f) G   •J
     The target contains three features which are of similar
shape and appear superimposed on one another.   (One is prominent
while the others occur in the background).          They have a
triangular shape and are extremely massive.   There is a sense of
great height and isolation.        There is exposed layers of
geological strata which are visible.   There is also an area that
is softer than the surrounding features and gives the appearance
of moving or movement.   There are large expanses of uninhabited
land and the "view" is to the south.
     The target feels both manmade and natural.    Natural stone
has been manipulated into large squares or blocks.   The objects
also appear brown-gray and have smooth-rough surfaces. There is
a sense of interlocking or fitting together. There is a feeling
of the presence of a hollowed-out area within one of these
objects.    The concept "Something is still in there!" is
associated with this particular object.
     Some portions andfor features of the target are partially
obscured from view by material which has been windblown.   There
is a sense of continuing decay or decomposition and wearing down
associated with the target.
     There is a yellowish glow or shaded area and large, flat
surfaces.   There is a sense of decay and crumbling associated
with at least one of the objects.
     There are rounded circle shapes and a rapid movement of
individuals. This movement is reminiscent of dancing.
