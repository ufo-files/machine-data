  Private Security Firm
  Founded in 1954 by former FBI agent George Wackenhut, Wackenhut Security
  now provides security for numerous private ventures and government facilities
  worldwide, including the Nevada Test Site. Wackenhut has been accused of
  operating perimeter security for Area 51, a charge that has not been proven and has been denied by former employees.
