Morning Bulletin (Rockhampton, Qld. : 1878 - 1954), Monday 31 May 1954, page 4



        Shapes Like People In           Flying Object




          MELBOURNE,     May                30.    —
                                                          A
       flying object reported               over        the
       Melbourne      suburb   of    East
                                        to
       Malvern    early today seemed
       have    people in it, Mr    David
       Reese,       of     Elwood,         said          to-
       night.
          "I could distinctly see  dark
       shapes, like figures, in the ob-
       ject," he         said. "If     I    had        seen

       the same    shapes in an    aero-
       plane, I would have   said they
       were  people."
          Mr  Reese   said he and     his
       friends first looked   into    the
       air when   they heard a buzzing
       sound.
          "The   object was   dark   with
       faint streaks of vertical light
       on   the  side. The   outlines   of
       what    looked like people, were
       darker than     the rest of    the
       object."
          Mr     Reese      said     that  descrip-
       tions, which         he and      his friends
       later wrote  down               about            the
       object, coincided             almost             ex-

       actly.
