(U)   PREFACE:

     (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS) The Special Activity Report is the first
in a series of reports which provides data in response to a
request from Joint Task Force Five (JTF-5) in Alameda,
California. The data provided in this report will be used by the
J22 division in their assessment effort regarding their
counterdrug mission. The information provided in this report was
generated by PAG-TA employing remote viewing (RV) methodologies.

     (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS) The data provided in this first report
concentrated on the target vessel               The results were
forwarded to JTF-5 by secure fax on           94. The JTF-5
evaluation feedback is at Appendix B.

     (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS) The methodology used in this activity is
exploratory. Results should not be considered as stand-alone;
but used or evaluated in conjunction with other proven data
sources (HUMINT, SIGINT, IMAGERY, OPEN SOURCE, and/or MASINT).
Generic data (e.g., general shapes) are more reliable then
specific data (e.g. dimensions, names).

            (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)     Source A:

            -- The target is a white medium to large vessel. Men are

       been at some point off the coast of California but is currently

       and observing to perhaps make a connection. The activity is
