  From the Book Jacket...

                                              Exploring the Parapsychology of Spirituality
                                                        Edited by Charles Tart

  The split between science and spirituality has long been a thorn in the sides of both camps. Scientists have found no
  evidence for the existence of a soul, for example, while those with a spiritual bent criticize scientists for having too
  narrow a view. Now, the author of the classic books Altered States of Consciousness and Transpersonal Psychologies
  delivers a selection of articles which provide scientific evidence for the existence of spiritual dimensions.

  Acclaimed scientist Charles Tart presents the considerations of well-known researchers and authors such as William
  Roll, Ramakrishna Rao, Michael Grosso, and Jeffrey Mishlove. With these notables, you'll examine provocative topics
  such as:

          The existence of God
          Life after death
          Channeling
          Life in other dimensions

  This groundbreaking work serves to liberate all of us, and asks that we look objectively at the evidence. We may be
  surprised at what we find.


  From the Publisher...

  Addressing the split between practitioners of science and those of spirituality, Charles Tart presents the considerations

  on such subjects as God, life after death, channeling, and other dimensions. A ground-breaking work that may surprise
  many readers.
