Ants' flood threat
CHINA is facing the threat of serious
flooding later this year because of freak
weather, heavy rainfall and the activities of
white ants drilling holes in river dykes, said
the New China News Agency. Forest fires in
the northeast last month killed 200 people,
but floods around the country this year have
already killed 230.
