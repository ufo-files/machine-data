Runcorn and Widnes Weekly News – 27.08.2009…
Jennie McCloughlin, of Widnes, says UFOs are Chinese lanterns
A SMOKER may have got to the bottom of the recent wave of UFO sightings in Widnes.
Last week a resident reported seeing a ‘big orange ball with flames dropping off it’ over Victoria Park, at 10.20pm, on Saturday, August 15.
But another witness, Jennie McCloughlin, of Larch Avenue, Widnes, believes the mysterious objects are home-made Chinese lanterns released by children, and not alien spacecraft.
Jennie thinks the improvised hot air balloons are probably being launched from Pex Hill.
Widnesian Andrew Wood also saw the ‘fireball’ and is sure it was a Chinese lantern.
However, other sightings may cast doubt on the theory.
Two witnesses – ‘Pat and Andy’ – reported sightings in Widnes at 9.35pm and 10.20pm on Saturday, August 15. The reports are posted online.
They said the orb must have had ‘mechanical propulsion to be able travel so quickly’.
‘Pat and Andy’ added that it was the third time they had seen the glowing balls during the week.
There were also three alleged UFO sightings on three consecutive nights in Widnes between July 20-22. The witness reported a ‘large white object hovering above the chimney stacks’. It moved ‘side to side at great speed’. She said it had yellow lights on one side and an orange trail shaped like ‘a tick’. The UFO stayed in the sky for between eight minutes and two hours on each occasion.
Other reports include a “convoy of white lights” heading towards the Silver Jubilee Bridge and several appearances of lights over Runcorn between May-July.
However, Jennie McCloughlin maintains a terrestrial explanation for what was seen over Victoria Park on Saturday, August 15.
She told the Weekly News: “I’m a smoker and sometimes stand on my back doorstep having a cigarette.
“I see these lights coming past all the time – the kids make them out of bin bags and candles.
“Sometimes one will move faster than another depending on how high they are and the air currents.
“Eventually they burn out. I don’t know where they set them off from but I reckon it might be Pex Hill. I always see two or three a week and sometimes they scare the kids. I do believe there is something out there but I know these are not UFOs.”
Andrew Wood added: “I also saw the orange fireball UFO which was clearly to me just a paper Chinese lantern which had most likely been let off in Victoria Park, the unnamed individual can keep their scepticism on little green men coming to our town.”
