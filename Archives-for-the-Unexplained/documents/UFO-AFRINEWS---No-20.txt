Mars on the Wind

Among a great deal of material on MARS, several items are of interest to
our readers. In the New York Times of Tuesday, Nov. 10, 1998, William
J. Broad says that Mars is a •whistling' planet It is a windy place with
clouds, dust storms and windblown sand dunes. In the summer, when the
winds are especially strong. there is sometimes enough dust to cover the
whole planet! Last year, the Pathfinder probe which landed on Mars,
recorded wind data which was turned into sound The Mars Polar Lander
set to arrive on Mars in December t 999, has on board a special two-ounce
microphone developed at the University ofCalifornia at Berkeley. This will
be able to record the sound of wind, the blowing dust and even perhaps,
electrical discharges.
