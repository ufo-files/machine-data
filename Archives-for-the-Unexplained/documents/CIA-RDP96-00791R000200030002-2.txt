           MEMO FOR:          Record

SG11       FROM:

           SUBJECT:           Complete Star Gate Transfer Chronology


           Jan:          Preliminary planning meetings by CIA senior
                         management in response to Congressionally Directed
                         Action (CDA) to tgransfer Star Gate Program.

                         D/ORD Appointed program manager at CIA/ORD

                         Contacted Office of Military Affairs to coordinate
                         meetings with DIA and US Army (in anticipation of
                         Star Gate review)

           Feb-Mar:      Informal discussions with US Army Research
                         Institute (ARI) and National Research Council (NRC)
                         about potential CIA paranormal study request

           Feb-Apr:      Archiving initiated of all DIA and CIA
                         files relating to paranormal studies going back 20
                         years

           21 Feb:       Formal memo sent from ORD to DIA requesting their
                         assistance with declassification to facilitate
                         execution of the CDA

           Feb:          Met with Center for Studies on Intelligence (CSI)
                         to discuss potential strategies for
                         declassification

           28 Feb:       CIA met with DIA principal investigator at SAIC,
                         Dr. Ed May. All-day technical exchange.

           1 Mar:        CIA and Representative from Office of Military
                         Affairs make site visit to Ft. Meade to meet all
                         program personnel and discuss transfer. All-day
                         technical exchange.

           15 Mar:       After informal discussions with NRC, panel member
                         selection begins: Jessica Utts, John Palmer, James
                         Alcock, and Ray Hyman contacted by NRC and agree to
                         act as commissioned reviewers

           27     Mar:   Submitted a "Terms of Reference" in formal response
                         to the Congressionally Directed Action stating NRC
                         would perform unclassified retrospective, CIA would
