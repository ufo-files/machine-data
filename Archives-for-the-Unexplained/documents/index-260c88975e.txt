                                                                   Explanation of Ratings
   Credibility = Low: In general, this catagory is a rating of the author's intellectual discipline and the strength of his argument (which are low), not
   the veracity of his claims (which could be true in spite of the weak argument).

  FUFOR suffered some criticism for funding this report. Stanton Friedman could hardly be called unbiased
  with regard to the alleged "MJ-12" documents. Friedman is almost alone among major ufologists in
  declaring the documents genuine. (Our own theory -- only half in jest -- is that some secret government
  agency designed and forged the documents especially for him.) In this report, Friedman starts from a
  dubious premise: that the burden of proof is on the skeptics to prove that the document is forgery, not on
  the believers to prove that it is real. Although we disagree with his philosophy, we still find Friedman's
  paper interesting. It includes a lot of data and a large appendix of correspondence and government
  documents. -- Glenn Campbell

  Comparison to Other Books

  Many of Friedman's arguments here are repeated in his later book Top Secret/Majic. Also see Kevin Randle's skeptical
  response, Conclusions on Operation Majestic Twelve.
