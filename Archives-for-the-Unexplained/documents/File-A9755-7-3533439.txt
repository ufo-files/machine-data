        UFO SIGHTING


        1.       UFO sighting reported at 2020 by Ms Grieves, 25 Delia
        Ave, Budgewoi, Phone 043 - 900502. Caller sounded rational and
        there were no background noises.   Before giving her name she
        wanted an assurance that this would be treated in the strictest
        confidence.

        2.       Ms Grieves was in her garden at 2000 when she noticed a
        bright (very bright> light in the sky to the SW.   The light was
        below the clouds, and the light intensity was omnidirectional.
        The light moved very slowly, would stop and then continue on very
        slowly.   This pattern was repeated continually.   When the light
        got closer to her it stopped and then made a rapid vertical
        ascent, before descending and continuing onwards at a 'reasonable'
        speed to the NE.

        3.       Object 'appeared' round but caller was not sure.    The
        object also had a 'soft' motor noise.

        4.        I advised caller that the details would be forwarded to
        an Intelligence Officer who may wish to question her.    She
        understood this, and agreed to write down all the details.     Phone
        call lasted 10 minutes.    Caller reiterated desire to 'do the
        right thing'.

        5.          For your consideration.

        Date :     Sunday 29th October 1990

        Weather Conditions :      Clear Moonlit night

        Whilst travelling south through the village of Federal we noticed a
        bright light in a stationary position above a farm house . We pulled
        off the road as we drew level with the house . At this point we were
        some 50 metres from the house .

        We estimate that the light was approximately 20 metres to 30 metres
        above the ground . The light was a very bright sphere , yellow to
        orange in colour and about 1 . 5 metres in diameter .

        As the car came to a standstill the light began to move due south
        in a horizonal line .

        We drove down the hill for some 300 metres with the light on the
        passenger side of the car .

        The light turned through some 90 degrees to the east , diminished
        in size to approximately a quarter of its original diameter .

        Our view was obscured by road-side trees and the road embankment .

        We stopped the car , got out and walked back along the road to our
        last vantage point .

         The light was no longer visible .

         Despite it being a clear and still night, we heard no sound that
         could be associated with aircraft of any description .

         Total viewing time 5-7 minutes .
