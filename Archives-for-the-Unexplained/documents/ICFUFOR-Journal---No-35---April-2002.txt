Journal No 35 April 2002

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------


   Nesher ( A town in north of Israel, next to Haiffa) 2/8/2002

  At 16:30 Irina EDELMAN, her daughter Svetlana and a friend named Nathalie Romanov, were at a public parc located
  on top of "Giv'at-Nesher" neighborhood .
  In the sky, they saw in the west, an object, estimated by them, big as an airplane. It's shape was round, and it cycled
  around itself moving east. It's shape was like "2 saucers connected together". The object was surrounded with a blue
  vapor and was more dark in its surround, and lighter in its center-"like aluminum color". At one of corners, the
  witnesses saw something, they describe like a "window", from which a blue light was seen like "fluorescent light". The
  UFO was observed for about 5 minutes, cycling around itself. it become smaller as it went away, until it desappeared.

  Irina, Svetlana, and her husband Vladimir had previous UFO observation back from the period they lived in Odessa.
  Once they saw a triangular UFO, and once, at 1989 or 1990, they saw from their flat in a high building, a red colored
  ball flying over a big military base next to their town. The ball rise up, became small and dark, and suddenly climbed up
  towards the sky and desappeared.

   Tel - Aviv, 19 February 2002

  This report was given by a member of our center, Mr Ronnen Bass, who the witness is his family member.

  Ommer,19 years old says: It was between 1:30 - 2:15 . I parked my mothers car in the parking lot at "Beit-Tzuri" street,
  Ramat-Aviv (north Tel-Aviv). I made 4 steps and somehow continued looking at the sky. There I saw 3 silver ball-shape
  UFO, moving closely one to each other in a strait line. They were together, like in one structure ( I'm not sure, but
  maybe something connected all of them) They flew from south to north, slowly and lower from any airplane. Suddenley
  they diappeared whithout anything blocking them. I din't heard any noise.
  The structure was at the size of a small airplane. I saw them for about 3 - 4 seconds.

   South Lebanon 5 April 2002

  A report about Israeli airforce attak in south Lebanon was in the news broadcast of the Israeli second channel (TV-2).
  The report was filmed in daylight. We noticed 2 "perl like" objects with a silver color. Afterwards one of them
  disappeared an the second object seen to fly away, leaving a dark trail. In the second part of this broadcast,shown
  minutes after the first one, there is an explosion on the gound and the 2 UFO's are clearly seen (this part took almost a
  second and a half) Afterwards, the camera focuses in the airplane for about a second.
  We see clearly the difference between the airplane's shape an dark color, and the round shape and silver color of the
  UFO's.
