    December 1987

    Dear subscribers,

    Dossier 4 is as mixed a bag as ever.     It's a pity about the quality of
    the copying of the Baxter-Davis item, but when you're working from a
    17th century original, there are limits as to how rough you can be with
    the book   !

    There seems to be no end to the material which can be found in books
    and periodicals from the past ; however, it ought to be set alongside
    material from our own time, and I do urge you all to keep your. eyes
    open for articles in current scientific journals which could have a
    relevance to our field of interest.

    A lot has happened in anomaly research this year. . An event which
    involved many Bolide contributors was BUFORA' s publication UFOs
    1947-1987, which contains important articles on UFOs as natural
    phenomena ( Chris Rutkowski) and British spooklights ( David Clarke &
    Granville Oldroyd).     These and other contributions to the book indicated
    the interdisciplinary nature of all anomaly research - and of BOLs in
    particular.    If one thing emerges above all from the varied contents of
    our dossiers, it is that BOLs are all things to all men ; not simply a
    phenomenon/range of phenomena in their own right, but also some kind of
    ea talyst for other phenomena.

    Is this because they are archetypal in the Jungian sense ? or because
    they are primordial· in their phy�ical make-up ? or both ? All thoughts
    and comments welcomed   !

    Also welcomed will be subscriptions from those who have not paid theirs
    Bolide is run on a strictly cost basis, and no further dossiers will be
    sent to those who don't reimburse me for paper, copying and postage.
    Rates are £10 I $15, or the equivalent in any currency ; please send
    banknotes rather than cheques or money orders, or the banks will
    swallow half of it...   The subscription covers three dossiers, which are
    sent out when ( 1) I can fit it in among my other activities and (2) when
    I get sufficient material from you people.

    All good wishes to you all,      and thanks for your support and your
    contributions.
