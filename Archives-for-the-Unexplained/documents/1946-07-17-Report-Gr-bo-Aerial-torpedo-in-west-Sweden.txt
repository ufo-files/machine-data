Case xxx: 
Aerial torpedo in west-Sweden

Time: July 17, 11.40 (noon)
Place: Gråbo, 25 km NE of Gothenburg (CG34)

From Göteborgs-Tidningen: "Yesterday an "aerial torpedo" was observed, according to Göteborgs-Posten, by a person from Gråbo. At 11.40 he had heard a sound like a tornado and thought that such a phenomenon was coming. He discovered, however, a torpedo-shaped object at high altitude, which flew in a northwesterly direction. It went very fast and had disappeared within 10-12 seconds. At the local defence staff one had not received any report about the incident."

Further, from Expressen: "...The local policeman states that he has received no report from any other place that anyone saw the aerial torpedo. The information is not in doubt, however, since the witness is a very reliable person."

Note: The report was not investigated by the defence, neither is the case listed in the Defence Staff tabulation of sightings. Gråbo is situated at the southern tip of lake Mjörn. If the object maintained a northwesterly direction it should have passed in a direction out over the lake.

Status: Posibble aircraft
Sources: Göteborgs-Posten, July 18. Expres-sen, July 18. TT telegram nr 33, July 18.        ScanCat 1305.
