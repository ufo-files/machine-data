When the men a few hours after the incident carne home and told of
their experiences, nobody took them seriously. The folks at home
thought that the "boys had inventend the whole story". True, Petter

The story of a "little green man" was much too incredible. The next
day Aliranta 's hand was so sore that he could not hold an axe in
the hand. Anyway, the men went to the forest as usual, because no
other symptoms were felt.     Howev eT,    the working did not proceed quite
normally as the men kept looking round more attentively as before.
The fear caused by the incident gradually disappeared but two months
later Aliranta still complained that he did not dare walk alone in     the

dark.   They did not feel other aftereffects.
The investigations carried out did not reveal any such details that
would have given cause to doubt the story of these young men. H.ow­
ever, due to the negative attitude of the villagers ( particularly their
home folks ) they did not want to speak to still other people of their
experience. Therefore, by the time the word was spread of this case
the traces of the decent were already covered under a half metre
thick snow blanket.
