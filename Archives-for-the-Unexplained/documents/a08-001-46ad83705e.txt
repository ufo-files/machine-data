  Dear fellow newsgroup members,
  Let us take a moment to reflect on the mystique of Area 51. For many years
  it has been the subject of countless documentaries, articles, news reports
  and books. One incident has elevated this military base to the status of a
  legend. (The 1947 Roswell incident.) Speak to any conspiracy theorist or
  UFO investigator and Area 51 is always at the top of the list.
  Let us face some hard facts: We will probably never know in our lifetimes
  what really occurred in Roswell New Mexico in 1947. It is one of those
  mysteries that we will always be part of the American mystique. Area 51 is
  a top secret testing ground and as such is governed by the military, and
  the modern military, since its birth in the U.S. is ruled by its own laws,
  not the laws by which we are bound to abide. As dificult as it may seem, we
  must except the fact that there are a great many things that we cannot
  know. Yes, we always here the excuse that certain things are a matter of
  national security, and I will be the first one to agree that that excuse is
  often abused, mainly for the fact that we the poeple of the U.S. would more
  than likely strongly disagree with what the military or government has done
  or is doing.
  The truth is that we have enjoyed security within our borders. The truth
  is, the military has kept us safe from outside invaders, the military has
  devoted its essence and being to upholding the constitution of the U.S. and
  ensuring that we are able to enjoy the freedoms we have now.
  The cold truth is that we, the people have elevated Area 51, Roswell New
  Mexico, the assination of Kennedy and King to the level of legends in this
  country. We have all contributed to the frenzy over conspiracies, black
  projects and secret governments. We have given authors, reporters and
  dillusional people an outlet for their voices to be heard. As loud as they
  may yell we have yet to see definitive proof of their claims. As much as
  they point their fingers, they never seem to be pointing in the right
  direction. But yet we are hungry for more. Hungry for any shred of
  evidence, wether it be manufactured (alien autopsy) or misrepresentational
  to say to the powers that be,"We knew you were covering up, we knew you
  couldn't be trusted.."
  A. Lanciano
