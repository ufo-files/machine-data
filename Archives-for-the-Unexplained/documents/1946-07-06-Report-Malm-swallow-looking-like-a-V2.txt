Case xxx: 
”Swallow” looking like a V2

Time: July 6, 14.25 (p.m.)
Place: City of Malmö, province of Skåne (CO36)

The Malmö regional defence area staff reported this case, to the Defence Staff, on July 11:

"Enclosing a report I have herewith the honour to forward the following verbal statement from draughtsman Torsten Frykmar, employee of Sydsvenska Dagbladet [newspaper], Malmö. 

"On July 6, 14.25, Frykmar observed a swallow-like object at a very great altitude. A short while later a thick, white, cloudy contrail was formed. Due to the noise from the street no sound could be heard.
Some five minutes later Frykmar sighted from a roof the remains of a cloud-like trail although now more dispersed. He asumed it was the same trail as he had seen before. Change of altitude or the course of direction could not be estimated by Frykmar. Observation points and directions are to be found on the enclosed map. 

"Frykmar stated that he had seen a V-bomb over Ystad (southern tip of Sweden) in January 1945, which, according to him, was confirmed by the press. The July 6 phenomenon had the same appearance. On order, P.R.Jahnke, chief of staff."

Note: The map mentioned was not enclosed with the report in the Defence Staff archives. The January 1945 phenomena, seen on several consecutive days over the provinces of Skåne and Blekinge, were probably due to high altitude aircraft, and definitely not German V-bombs. A separate report on these incidents is available from Archives for UFO Research.

Status: Probable high altitude aircraft.
Source: Report to Defence Staff, July 11. ScanCat 794.
