U.P.O. Sighting
#r. Prince George, B.C. - 6 APR 78
Statement of Jean ANDERSON (BD: 14 PEB 23) taken
by Cat. J.N. HOLYK, 16 APR 78.
On the 6 APR 78, I was at home. At 8.00 PM, I received a
phone call from Vicki BURNS. She told me that a light beam came
from up in the trees and knocked the horses down.
I went over immediately. When I arrived, I went immediately
to the barn (and) assisted in attempting to quiet the horses down.
We were there for approx. 3/4 hour.
We went into the house and
phoned Lenore LOPTUS. We then went outside. We looked up and saw
an object in the aky, almost directly above.
The sky was clear.
The object moved around very erratically in all directions.
The
object was off-white in colour and would brighten when it stopped
moving.
To me, it seemed to pulsate.
It appeared to be the size of
a small plane. It appeared oval in shape and when it stopped, it
appeared round. It seemed to move around the sky, around us.
We
heard the horses raising a fuss so we went to see to them.
We were
tending to them when Lenore and Wayne LOPTUS, Ray and Alice MILLER
came.
I told them, "you won't believe this but we saw an object in
the sky." I looked up and saw it again. It was moving south to
south-east. It looked like a moving star but it was again erratic
in it's motions. It was moving in the south-east sky for a minute
and the next minute, it was gone.
There was no sound or bursts,
1e: fireballs, from the object. When I firat saw the object, it
was low to the ground but was hard to determine.
I viewed the
object for approx. y hour total in time. It was approx. 1/3 the
size of the moon.
(Signed) "Jean ANDERSON"
