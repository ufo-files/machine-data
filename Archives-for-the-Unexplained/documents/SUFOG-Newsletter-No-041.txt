  If  you've    ever had   the  feeling that the world is getting
stranger,then according to a new survey,you may well be right.
1993 was    apparently 3.5% stranger   than 1992,according to the
compilers of the journal of strange phenomena 'Fortean Times'.
Although their    measurements isn't   that scientific,they  hope
that their    reports will   open our eyes  to some of  the odder
events   in   the  world.Kerry   Dyson has  compiled-this  report
starting with a typical tit-bit from the Fortean Times :
   'In  1977,there was a   shower of rain in  Poole,which turnt
into a fall of  hay and grass.According   to witness  John Kay,
the hay was  coming from a   large rain cloud  about 1,000 feet
up'.

  The kind of strange stories   that the Fortean Times has been
collecting  for years.But now,they have   decided to take their
compendium  of  fascinating   facts a  stage  further.They  are
creating their  own FT Index of the   years strange  phenomena,
which  they  hope  to  quantify and   compare  with last  years
events.According to Bob Rickard :

   'We get   1000's of news   clippings from all     over the   world
and   we've   noticed  that    certain   types    of  phenomena   and
subjects    come  and go.We know    about a lot of people who     are
interested    in this area and    want to get some kind    of handle
on   it  as   do  we.So we   had   to  decide   a   method.We   tried
measuring    column inches or    by weighing them    by the pound.In
the   end we   decided,we   would   actualy    monitor   wether   the
reportage            �
