   interesting/disturbing dream
   took a nap for a short while... got an uncontrolable need to sleep for
   a moment... and dreamt:
   I was at Lowe's [lumber supply place] with my cousin buying materials
   for some job we had...
   this went on almost endlessly... picking wood and one conversation
   with a guy I don't remember...
   more tagging along with Freddy since he's the smarts & I the helper
   then through the check out line... long line...
   then out... or almost out... Freddy was ahead of me, leading the
   attendant to the truck [a van I think, actually--- this comes into
   play soon]
   As I was coming out... to leave... I spotted a familiar figure in
   ablack nylon aviator's jacket... eye contact... and it was Fawn...
   this was early morning, before dawn
   I smiled... she looked shocked... and sort of whispered "talk to you
   later..." and zipped on into the store in her zippy way... blonde
   curls breezing back... I said "What???" not having seen her in way too
   many days...
   "Jack will hear... you know that." she whispered.
   Then she stopped looked up eye to eye with me and said: "You know how
   I feel." her stare into my eyes was close and hard to explain.
   "Later." she said and was gone into the crowded store.
   I went into the huge parking lot to find Freddy... one end to the
   other... no Freddy. [it didn't help that I couldn't remember what he
   drove, or even if it was a van or truck]
   At the other end of this huge "supercenter" was a "grocery" area to
   try to spot Freddy and of course leave Fawn be, so this was difficult,
   because I WANTED Freddy to find me, but not make Fawn think I was
   annoying her, since she "promised" that we'd talk later, and she
   always eventually keeps her promise.
   No Freddy.
   Back to the parking lot... dawn is peeking over the hill.
   back inside... a long hallway seperating the two parts of Lowe's had
   been waxed... I looked around and saw no one looking so I slipped over
   the "Wet Floor" sign/rope and SLID down the long hallway... this was a
   lot like a ballet... ***an arty action scene***
   back through and then outside... it had become early morning now...
   not as many cars... no Freddy but I could see her little brown mini
   van way over by what probably is the "warehouse loading area" quite a
   few people were around and I HAD to go that way since every other area
   had no trace of Freddy.
   Walked by and was luckily un noticed...
