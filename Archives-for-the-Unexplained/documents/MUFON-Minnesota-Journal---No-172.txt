poop and pee, just like we all do on Earth. So even

lighten their craft's load, compensating for the
lunar rocks they'd brought aboard.

Recently, some astrobiologists have actually gotten
interested in possibly looking at this poop, to see if
there are any bacteria remaining in the feces and
whether they've mutated at elevated rates due to
