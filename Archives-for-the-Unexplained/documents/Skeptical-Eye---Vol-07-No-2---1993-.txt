On June 11 of this year the long awaited film version of Michael
Crichton's 1990 best-seller,Jurassic Park, debuted,
in theaters across the country. The result was
not only a major hit for director Steven
Spielberg, as Jurassic Park became the first
movie to make over $50 million in the first
three days, but also a nationwide plunge into
dinosaur-mania. Dinosaurs graced the covers
ofperiodicals as diverse as Scientific American
andThe New Yorker, and everything in between.
And as of this writing, Jurassic Park has earned
over $324 million, becoming the third highest
grossingfilm ofall time. Now, we at the Skeptical
Eye feel it is our tu rn to jump on the bandwagon,
join in the fray, and just generally put our two
cents worth in. So, please join us as we catch a
little dino-mania ourselves, and cast our "skeptical
eye" towards Jurassic Park.
