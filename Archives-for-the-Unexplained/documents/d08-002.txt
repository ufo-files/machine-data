            Lincoln LaPaz has no known Roswell connection
     From: jan@cyberzone.net (Jan Aldrich)
     Date: Mon, 8 Dec 1997 12:44:40 -0800


  Re: A Latino/conspiracy angle on Roswell claims
  http://www.ufomind.com/misc/1997/dec/d07-001.shtml
  Well, now we find that Dr. Lincoln LaPaz was connected to Roswell.                                 I
  think not.
  He was indeed recruited to help in the Japanese Balloon Bomb attacks
  which he wrote about in 1954.
  We now also find that LaPaz was poking around Roswell. At the time of
  Roswell, Dr. Lincoln LaPaz was in Oklahoma. He was stopping off at
  places he had heard might have meteor impacts. Some local newspapers
  reported his search.
  Only rumor connects him with Roswell.                    If there are documents, let's see
  them.
  LaPaz was very interested in the "green fireball" phenomenon. He did
  look into a number of UFO reports. He generally maintained that the
  green fireballs and the "unknowns" of UFO reports represented some
  secret man-made devices. LaPaz once called UFOs, "The Fifth Horseman of
  the Apocalypse." Loren Gross adapted this phrase for the title of his
  UFO History series.
  Again, asserting that LaPaz was at Roswell does not prove anything,
  let's see the proof.
