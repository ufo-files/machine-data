AMATEUR ASTRONOMER REPORTS FLYING OBJECT- Mr. Roger Haste and Mr. W. Bailey, both of
Auckland City, New Zealand, reported the sighting of an unidentified object which
crossed the sky almost directly overhead at 10:20 p.m., on January 28th. The object
was first sighted about 10 degrees north of the Orion constellation, traveling from
west to east at high speed. Its shape was cylindrical or elongated, and it appeared
to glow with a bluish-white light along its entire length, somewhat like a dimly lit
fluorescent tube. The shape of the object ruled out the possibility of it being
either an astronomical body or an artificial satellite. Both observers watched the
transit of the object until it was lost to sight approximately 20 degrees from the
easter horizon. Neither were able to relate it to any object. Mr. Haste, an
experienced amateur astronomer, confirmed that the object observed was neither a
meteor nor another astrononical phenomenon. Source: New Zealand Scientific Space
Research Bi-Monthly Newsletter--March-April, 1963.
