                            AN EXCLUSIVE INTERVIEW WITH HOWARD MENGER

            In recent months, there has been much speculation among UFO researchers con­
cerning the current activities of saucer contactee Howard Menger.  A rumor was circulat ed
to the effect that Menger would hold a convention this Fall, similar to the one he spon­
sored on his New Jersey farm in September of 1958.
                On October 14th, your Editor received a phone call from Howard Menger, and two
days later we had a four hour mee ting in New York City.              Accompanying your Editor was
Eugene Steinberg, Advertising Manager of SAUCER NEWS.
                It developed that Mr. Menger had driven to New York all the way from an un­
disclosed location in the Pennsylvania mountains,              apparently just for this one day       and
just for the interview.         He explained that he is working on a 4-foot radio-controlled
saucer model, to be powered by a new principle he has invented.               The complex mechanism,
which he drew for us, involves "free electrons under extreme pressure, used together with
certain rare metals which have a high valence.0
                Before any public convention is held,          there is to be a private demonstration
of this machine, called the X4.           Scientists and a high-ranking military observer will be
present, according to Menger, and your Editor will also be allowed to attend.                 Then,    at
some time thereafter, - allowing enough time for a publicity build-up, - a convention
will be held.      This apparently could not be before the Spring of 1964, as the model is
not yet operating, and Menger does not know when he will be able to complete it, as he is
still overcoming various technical difficulties.               He is also short of funds, having given
up his sign painting business.
                The purpose of the convention will be to prove that athere are people like us
on other planets, far more advanced than us, scientifically, spiritually, and biological­
ly."    However, Menger no longer talks about his space contacts as related in his                well­
known book °From Outer Space to You,0 and he seems to feel that he was misinformed at
the time as to the origin of the people he was meeting.
                There is a lot more to the Menger story, and we will bring as much of it to
you as possible in the March 1964 issue of SAUCER NEWS.              Menger tells us that all new
information for public release will be sent exclusively to our magazine.
