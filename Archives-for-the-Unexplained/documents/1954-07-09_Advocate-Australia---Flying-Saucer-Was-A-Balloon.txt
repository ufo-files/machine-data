                      LONDON                      -
                                                        London                   had

                   thousands                      of        people               stop-

                   a  mysterious                         silver                 object
                   floating high                        in    the                 sun-
                   shine.

                 pear      -

                                    shaped and                      boomerang
                 shaped.
                   Switchboards                               at            the          Air
                 Ministry,                police             headquarters,

                 jammed     with   calls.

                 ied the   thing  for  over an                                      hour
                 from          the         roof        of     the           Air      Min-
                 istry building.
                    Then              came             the                official       ex-

                 planation.                       It        was             a 60-foot
                 wide          balloon            released                  by scien-
                 tists         at     Cardington                            (Bedford-
                 shire)  for cosmic        radiation     re-
                 search.  It carried       photographic
                 plates, and    was     expected     even-
                 tually to reach         a    height       of
                 100,000   feet     before     a   timing
                              would              the    sci-
       entific
                 apparatus               drop                 parachute
                 equipment       by                                        &nbsp;
                 somewhere                      in     western                   Eng-
                 land.
                     Professor     C.  F.  Powell,    of

                 was     the    biggest balloon    they
                 had     ever   launched.
