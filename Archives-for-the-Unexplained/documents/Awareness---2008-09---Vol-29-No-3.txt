         More and more spectacular cases are being reported of medical patients who have received
         transplanted    vital   organs   from   recently   deceased   donors,    suddenly   develop   different
         personalities and take up unusual pastimes they had previously taken no interest in.            Eating
         and drinking preferences may also be markedly changed.             Organ transplants, once a rare
         event, are becoming commonplace and, thus, an increasing number of interesting patient
         histories are coming to light. Awareness first published information on this subject in Vol 27
         No 1, pp 1 1 - 1 9, in an article by Malcolm Robinson.


         Unfortunately there is a tragic tale or two amongst the many who benefit from transplanted
         organs.   We hear of a man who committed suicide after receiving a transplanted heart 12
         years previously from another suicide victim.         He had in fact married his donor's former
         wife.


         Selecting from many case histories available, details of heart/lung transplant, Claire Sylvia,
         have recently been published.           The   47 year was dying from a disease called primary
         pulmonary hypertension, when in 1988 she received the organs of an 18 year old youth who
         had been killed in a motorcycle accident in Maine, USA              The identity of the donor was
         withheld at the time for ethical reasons.          This transfer of a vast amount of tissue was a
         pioneering operation at the time and the patient has survived 20 years.                Within days of
         leaving hospital, Claire experienced a craving for the taste of beer, a drink she had never
         been interested in before.       This was followed by a desire for certain foods she hadn't liked
         before the operation, such        as   Snickers bars, green peppers and Kentucky fried chicken
         takeaways.     Her personality was also changing in that she was becoming            masculine - more
         aggressive and assertive     than she used to be - and developed a lumbering walk which was
         remarked on by her daughter. Although her sexuality had not changed, she was mistaken by
         a lesbian by a woman she became friendly with at a conference.


         She then had remarkable dreams in which she was vividly aware of being with a young man
         called Tim Leighton? and woke up knowing for certain this person was in fact her donor.
         As stated the hospital would not reveal the name of her donor, but Claire set out to find out
         who he might be by checking newspaper records of any young persons who had died in a
         motorbike accident a few days before her transplant surgery.                   She found a Timothy
         Lamirande who fitted the details exactly and managed to make contact with              his parents and
         they were willing to reveal that all the unusual dietary habits and personality traits that Claire
         was picking up corresponded exactly with those of Timothy when last alive.              (Report: Daily
         Mail 9/4/08).
