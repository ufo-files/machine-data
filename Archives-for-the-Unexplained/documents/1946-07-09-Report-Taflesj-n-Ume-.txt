KUNGLIGA VÄSTERBOTTENS REGEMENTE
Regementsexpeditionen
No. I a: 140  July 11, 1946.  To the air defense department.  I have the honour of submitting a report regarding Mr. Arthur Hansson’s observation of a light phenomenon; information which was originally reported by Mr. Hansson himself earlier during this very day, the 11th of July.
  Place of observation: the northern tip of Taftlesjön (1 kilometer east of Umeå).  Point in time: July 9th, during 14:30 to 15:00 o’clock.  Duration: about 2 seconds.  Direction from the observation site: across the middle of Taflesjön.  Description of the phenomenon: a soundless bluish green trail, about 10 meters in size. It emitted a uniform light.  A. Westrin Acting regimental commander
