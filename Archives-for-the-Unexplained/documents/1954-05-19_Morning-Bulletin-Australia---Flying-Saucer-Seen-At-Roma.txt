Morning Bulletin (Rockhampton, Qld. : 1878 - 1954), Wednesday 19 May 1954, page 5



                FLYING  SAUCER
                 SEEN AT ROMA
            ROMA,        May          17.   -
                                                     A   strange
        V-shaped         object,            described          as

        a           saucer" is reported
               "flying
        by   residents   here   to   have
        swept across     Roma     at   the
        week-end.
          At   least half    a dozen    of
       Roma's   citizens, spread over    a

       distance   of up      to 40  miles,
       claim to have    seen   the object.
       They        set its time                 of       appear-
        ance      at   11.45 pm.            on       Saturday
       and    say        lt     was         travelling at
       hundreds           of      miles          an        hour,
       only 100       to 200           ft up,            towards
        the     south-west.
