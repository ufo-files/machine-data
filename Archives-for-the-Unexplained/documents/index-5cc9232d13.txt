  From the Book Cover

  Bob Larson's recent book, IN THE NAME OF SATAN, will show you how the forces of evil work and what you can do
  to defeat them.

  Bob Larson has spent more than twenty-five years ministering to victims of demonic bondage. Now, Larson dispels the
  contemporary myths surrounding Satan and his demons, equipping you with crucial biblical information on how you
  and your family can withstand the forces of evil.

  IN THE NAME OF SATAN shows you Satan's line of attack and provides startling answers to questions such as:

          Who is Satan?
          How does Satan gain access to us---and how does he plot to stay?
          What can't Satan do?
          How does Satan afflict Christians?

  You'll find case-by-case, real-life examples of demonic possession and witness spiritual warfare and deliverance tactics
  everyday Christians can use to combat and dispel Satan's influence over a person's mind and body. Plus, you'll open up a
  new area of faith as you discover a God who is victorious over Satan's evil influences.

  Here's what you can do to prevent Satan from taking control of you life - and how you can reclaim the lives of others in
  which he may have already gained a foothold.
