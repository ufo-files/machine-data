         Researchers   in parapsychology     living   in Europe confront a set of
         inter-related problens, none unique to the European situation, which may

         various problems in detail. Instead, the reasons why these factors can
         defeat the conventional communication/collaboration means will          be
         highlighted.   The efforts being made by European parapsychologists to
         combat these difficulties will be discussed, and data collected examining
