Dear Stoned,


I'm all for having a large turnout at the 1978 National UFO Conference, but I think you ought to do some­
thing to make it easier for the public to attend.


First off, for those who do not want to run up long-distance phone bills, the toll free number given for motel
reseravations at the Holiday Inn of Strongsville, Ohio is wrong! It may be valid in Ohio {I haven't checked
this!), but not anywhere else. Local phone directories should have such a number listed�s the Holiday Inn
has a nationwide computerized reservation system. This will make the matter of being assured of a place to
stay easy.


Secondly, there ought to be a public invitation for local groups to sponsor the Conference. So far, the Cleve­
land area has always been available when no one else would take a gamble on such a proiect. But there
are enough organizations around the country to surely get some kind of response to this kind of offer! How
about it Chairman Moseley? (For the record, I have done my part, having sponsored the 1974 Conference in
Valley Forge, Pennsylvania.)


On other matters, I have great respect for Jerome Eden in waging an almost single-handed battle to get
some recognition for Wilhelm Reich's UFO work. I think, however, that he would succeed better in his task
were he not so hysterical about his beliefs. For example, Eden refers to theories of a "collective ur:'­
conscious" as "balderdash" because it cannot be measured. Would he care to cite cases where anyone
{Reich included) has ever measured and obiectified the human consciousness? There are a lot of mysteries
about which science is still painfully ignorant.


I would agree with Uncle Lou: there should be a wide-ranging scientific study into UFOs, one far beyond the
scope of anything done in the past. But such a study shouldn't be limited. Reich's work should be evaluated,
as should the work of many others who have approached UFO study from differing vantage points. At this
stage, there is no reason to assume that any one explanation is correct.


It would be necessary, also, to be certain that psychic phenomena and its possible connection with UFO­
related sightings is thoroughly explored.


On other matters: I applaud the return of Gray Barker's long-missed column "Chasing the Flying Saucers" as
a part of Tim Beckley's UFO Review. I'm told that interested parties can get information on this publication
from 303 Fifth Avenue, Suite 1306, New York, N.Y. 10016. Saucer Stoned readers who followed Barker's
feud with Moseley through the years should be greatly excited by this new development.
