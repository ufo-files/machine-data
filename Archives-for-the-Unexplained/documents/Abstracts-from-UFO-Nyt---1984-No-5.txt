  Kim Møller Hansen:   The Motunau Photo, p.   5-6

  On the 27th of October 1979 a friend of June and Norman Neilson,
  Lou Blackburn, wanted to take some photos of Neilson new boat
  when leaving in the morning (Motunau, New Zealand) . On one of
  the photos a group of lights were discovered which were not
  seen in the sky when the pieture was taken. The lights were
  not revealed until January 1980 and the photo was published
  in CUFOS Associate Newsletter, June 1981.

  The Xenolog UFO Magazine, New Zealand, is of the opinion that
  the photo shows a real UFO, but Kim Møller Hansen, who is in
  charge of SUFOI's photo department, is of the opinion that the
  lights could be due to natura! causes.

  Kim Møller Hansen:   UFO Classic - Exeter, 1965, p.     8-13

  In thsi article Kim Møller Hansen describes the well known
  sighting from Exeter, 1965, worldwide known from the hook
  "Incident at Exeter" by John G. Fuller. Based on the files
  of Project Blue Book the reparts from police officers Norman
  J. Muscarello and Eugene Bertrand are quoted. Basically they
  observed S red lights which one by one were turned on in
  sequence. Other sightings were also made in the area.

  The first official statement came from Pentagon: The sightings
  were of aircraft activities and inversion phenomena. A month
  later the police officers received a letter from Project Blue
  Book which claimed that the case was not yet closed! And
  finally in 1966 another official letter was received: The
  sightings could not be explained!!

 The UFO skeptic Robert Sheaffer has discovered that some of
 the observations in faet did correlate to Jupiter - a well
 known IFO source. Kim Møller Hansen speculates that the
 sightings may not be as mysterious as supposed - though the
 police officers still are of the opinion that they saw
 something unexplained.

Torben Birkeholm:    Car stopped by UFO?, p.   3 0 -3 3

On Sunday the 21st of November 1982 at 0 3 . 3 0 a man was on his
way home in car (Hobro, Denmark) . Suddenly 4 bright blue
lights were turned on above the car. The engine of the car
stopped and the car ralled on about 10 0 m. Same moments later
the lights were turned off and nothing more was observed.

After the sighting the witness had pain in his eyes and when
he loaked at normal bulbs they had a red glow.

Four natural explanations were evaluated:
     l) Air horne natural phenomenon (aircraft etc. ) .
     2)   Electrical discharges in the atmosphere.
     3)   Electrical discharges from a nearby high-voltage line.
     4)   Combination of 2 and 3 .

None of the above mentianed explanations fits with the observed
phenomenon, but as there were no other witnesses there is no
way of evaluating the exactness of the statements from the
witness.

The case could then be filed as a UFO though a natural
explanation could not entirely be ruled out.
