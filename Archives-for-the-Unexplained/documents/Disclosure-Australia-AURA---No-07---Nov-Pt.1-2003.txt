  NEWSLETTER SEVEN Pt.1                 NOVEMBER 2003

  Federal Government document search update:

  RAAF Policy file:

  Work continues on summarising the contents of files located in the National Australian Archives. This issue presents
  file A703 554/1/30 titled “Investigation of Flying Saucers Policy” which covers the time period 1953-1967 (see
  attachment one.)

  Although this file commences in November 1953, there are some glimpses into the earlier years. Of particular interest
  is a Memo dated 16 November 1953 which forwards a new report form for the gathering of details about “flying
  saucer” sightings. The memo reveals that the original pro forma was issued on 17 January 1951. However, the relevant
  RAAF file for this era has not been sighted by any researchers to date. Elsewhere on file 554/1/30 we do find a clue to
  the identifying number of these earlier files, when a memo dated 24 October 1966 reveals that there are four files on
  the subject 569/5/461; 2/25/1; 574/3/88 and 554/1/30. Three of these files were classified. A request has been
  submitted to the National Archives for assistance in locating these earlier files.

  The file reveals that there was an extensive correspondence between civilian UFO groups and the RAAF about both
  RAAF policy, and seeking explanations from the RAAF for some of the spectacular sightings which have occurred in
  Australia. Of particular note is correspondence from Fred Stone of the Australian Flying Saucer Research Society in
  Adelaide, and Peter Norris of both the Victorian Flying Saucer Research Society and CAPIO.

  ASIO files:

  As mentioned in Newsletter 4, we found it of interest that the Commonwealth Investigation Service, the forerunner to
  ASIO, had a file on the Australian Flying Saucer Research Society, in 1956.
