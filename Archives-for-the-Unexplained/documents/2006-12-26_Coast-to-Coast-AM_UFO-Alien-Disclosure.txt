About the show

Author and filmmaker Robert Miles discussed his personal ET experience as well as his recent work, Fastwalkers, which
examines UFO & alien disclosure. A number of the people interviewed in his documentary also appeared during the
program.

Miles described being teleported to New York City where he boarded a saucer and was taken to a briefing held by
human-appearing ETs on Jupiter. The planet impressed him with its radiant energy, silver seas, and colorful fluorescence.
But he learned that Earth was facing catastrophic changes in its near future, if we don't change our ways.

Journalist Paola Harris believes we can't depend on the US govt. for disclosure, but rather it may come from international
sources. She also discussed the testimony of a Cape Canaveral "ground crew astronaut," Clark McClelland, who had
knowledge of "fastwalkers," UFOs tracked entering Earth's atmosphere.

Alfred Webre, known for his work in Canadian "exopolitics," said there has been replicable communication through
remote viewing to intelligences on planets like Mars, and that we live in a populated solar system.

Another researcher associated with exopolitics, Dr. Michael Salla, discussed the significance of the black budget, and
how the CIA siphons unaccountable money into the US Dept. of Defense. Marine scientist Elliott Maynard concurred
that planet Earth is in a state of emergency, and filmmaker Anthony Miles spoke about the creation of Fastwalkers,
which was shot in high-definition.

Skycar Update

First hour guest Paul Moller gave an update on his Skycar and commented on the possible Ford-Toyota merger. The
heyday of the US automotive industry is over, he said, but he was hopeful that his skycar, a hybrid vehicle that can travel
on both roads and in the air, would be put into production. He predicted that within 10 years we could see a significant
number of such vehicles in use. Check out some of the videos from Moller International.
