PA MUFON NEWSLETTER


March 1, 2008 Vol.1 No.3


The recent sightings in Texas and the general surge of
UFO sightings across the country have put MUFON in
the spotlight. Reports have doubled in some areas of
the country and Field Investigators are busy. MUFON
has had 100 new members sign up since the Texas
sightings story broke. PA MUFON has had 8 new
members enrolled, which brings total membership in
the state to approximately 100.

We are still in need of investigators, and those
interested in becoming a Field Investigator for MUFON
can go to www.mufon.com and click on UFO Hunters
logo for the information needed.

John Ventre, State Director for Pennsylvania will be
giving a presentation at Westmoreland County
Community College on Feb. 27, 2008 at 7:30 PM in the
Founders Hall amphitheater. Free to the public.

A UFO – Paranormal Conference will be held by PA
MUFON on Oct 25, 2008 . More on the conference as to
speakers and times in the next newsletter.

There were 7 sightings reported in the state in February
and January reports submitted amounted to 12. There
were a total of 19 sightings in PA reported to other
agencies not reported to MUFON. We are working on
that issue.

I would like to recognize the efforts of two PA MUFON
Field Investigators, Jason Donmoyer and Troy Noll who
have most diligently fulfilled any and all asked of them.
Their untiring efforts in following thru with
investigations assigned to them demonstrates
professional expertise and generates substantial
results.

We are still asking for assistance from our members to
help find a meeting location for the group. We also want
to remind everyone we have presentations and
speakers available if needed for a group in your areas
throughout the state.

Sky Watches and Mock training sessions will begin in
April. Dates and site locations, to be announced.

Dates and times for events scheduled in our area have
been posted on the Meetings and Events page at
www.pamufon.com

Note: To Investigators....If you are assigned a case and
won’t have the time advise John Ventre and it can be
reassigned that is not a problem.

“KEEP YOUR EYES TO THE SKIES”

Thank you for time and dedication:

S.Witkowski , PA Sate Section Director / Field
Investigator
