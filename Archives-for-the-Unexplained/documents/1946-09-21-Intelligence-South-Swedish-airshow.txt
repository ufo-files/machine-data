September 21: South-Swedish airshow

The 1946 autumn defence training exercise ended on Saturday, September 21, with a spectacular airshow over the Lomma bay [on the straits of Oresund] to the north of Malmö. 250 planes from almost all air force wings took part. The show was witnessed by a line-up of Swedish military big brass, including the Supreme Commander, General Helge Jung. In the audience were four air attachés: from the US, Britain, France and the Soviet Union. ”50.000 crouched with fear of the Vampire”, the Helsingborgs Dagblad headed their full-page article. 

Source: Helsingborgs Dagblad, Sept. 23.
