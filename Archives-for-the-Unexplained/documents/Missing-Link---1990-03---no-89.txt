                            By Elena Ruiz de Castilla

  I know our cosmic friends are perplexed at the fear they seem to generate in the
course of their work here on our planet, but perhaps it is more perplexing and
confusing to them when one of us develops what might be considered as
inappropriate feelings towards the contactor. Such has been my case, and I have
been struggling with it for several months now. No, I cannot say that I have been the
victim of cosmic seduction, although if they have been monitoring me as I believe
they have, I may have provided them with a certain degree of temptation.
  The problem began when I started to channel Yusov, and for the first time in my life I
felt that I had found someone who understood and cared about me. Due to his
efforts my life has changed completely and am still changing. I am no longer the
person I was even a month ago. I possess a strength and determination which is new
to me. He has taught me to solve my problems through prayer and meditation rather
than by drinking; and believe me, the problenlS I have would drive Jesus himself to
drink!
   So, I have been faced with the confusion that one might naturally feel concerning
channeling plus the problem of the feelings I have had for Yusov. I suppose that my
problem is that I do not wish to offend my ET friends in any way since they are of a
much higher nature, and are here for the purpose of elevating the consciousness of
those who are willing to understand their message.
   Yusov has told me that he is a physical being; that he is Plieadean, and that his
communications with me are done through telepathic projection. He often describes
the work he is doing and the areas where he prefers to work. I have met him a
number of times in dreams and astral-projection; however, I am waiting for him to
come to me on the physical level.
   In one communication he described his one earth incarnation which occurred in
Atlantis. He told me that I knew that he was Plieadian, and that I loved him for this
reason, that we shared a beautiful relationship, and that he died tragically in a
political battle, and that I committed suicide by throwing myself from some cliffs into
the sea.
  I told no one of this information because quite frankly, I thought I might be going
crazy. However, something very strange happened about three weeks later. I
received a cassette tape from a friend in which she told me that I had had an
Atlantian incarnation with Yusov, and for this reason there now exists a strong
attraction between him and myself. She went on to describe the details exactly as
Yusov had described them to me, with one exception; according to what she was
seeing, Yusov died as a result of a fight with a riwl for my love - a man whom I was
later forced to marry and whom I never loved.
  Further confirmation of the Atlantian incarnation came through my work with a Dick
Sutphen video on past life regression, in which I relived those experiences, including
my first intimacy with Yusov on an isolated beach which now lies miles beneath the
surface of the Atlantic Ocean.
  It is not unusual to me that Yusov has had an Earth incarnation. Many ETs who find
themselves between lives on the astral plane choose to come here in times of
difficulty to assist us. There are also star people who are the result of genetic
enpeavors, that is just one of several ways they are able to mingle with the Earth
population.
  As for my feelings for Yusov, it is up to him. I realize the problems of a relationship
with an ET. The first problem would be my husband. Maybe they could drop him off
in Siberia somewhere. It would be something that nobody would understand or
approve of. For the moment I know that I must concentrate on the spiritual lessons
