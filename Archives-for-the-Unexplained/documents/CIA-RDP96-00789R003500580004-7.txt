                       SUMMARY OF INFORMATION

     During this session, I could see an object that looked like

an airplane but instead of flying, it was standing straight up.

I could see that something on the tip top of the plane was

spinning very fast and the airplane started to move from side to

side conducting a sweeping motion while the ball on the top was

moving very fast.     When the plane was moving from side to side,

it could have been in an

intelligence instrument.

     Away from this site, I then saw a man.         He was bald and had

thick shoulders.    He was not near the site however, he could have

been waiting for information.

     I then could see a van or a truck driving down a hill and

across a desert away from the site.      This van or truck was dark

in color and it was a civilian truck or van.         It was not military

but somehow it was associated with the airplane.
