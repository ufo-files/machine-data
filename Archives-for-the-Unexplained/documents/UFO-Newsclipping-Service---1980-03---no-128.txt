              Teenagers Report UFO Sighting
          Two local teenagers have reported seeing a UFO headed toward the Chapel
       Hill Public Library about 10:40 Saturday night.
          The lighted object Elizabeth and Garland Thomas saw pass over their North
       Street home Saturday night didn't look like the Starship Enterprise or even a
       flying saucer, but it was a UFO as it remains unidentified.
          The Phillips Junior High students and four friends were outside in the yard
        when, according to Elizabeth, she spotted an unusual lighted object flying
       toward their house. They tried to follow it but were unable to keep up, she said.
          No, it definitely was not an airplane, said Elizabeth. She described it as wide
       at the bottom and narrowing toward the top "like a vase ." Garland said it look-
       ed like a megaphone ~                                                             .
          A flying vase or megaphone may sound bizarre, but a blackboard drawing of
       the object bore a strong resemblance to a space capsule.
          There was a big light on the bottom, a row of lights on the side and more
       lights on top, Elizabeth said . The lights were clear; they didn't blink and the
       object didn't make a sound, she recalled .
          The object was in sight " at least a minute" and it moved the whole time, ac-
       cording to Elizabeth.
          One of their companions saw an "X" shape on the bottom, made by the por-
       tion of the object not lit, Garland said.
          ··we couldn't believe we saw it; we got pretty excited," Elizabeth said when
       asked to describe how it felt to see a UFO . But some friends don't believe the
       story and the police who called to investigate the incident were laughing when
       they left, she said .
