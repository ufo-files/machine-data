That's called 'camera shake' and it's documented many times.

Trying to get a still shot of something very far away with a camera and no tripod is very
difficult because you are basically trying to balance a 'fulcrum' that is very near to your
hand.

Take a yardstick and hold on to the very end of it between your thumb and forefinger.
Then hold it out in front of you and try to make the other end perfectly still.

It's the same phenomenon. The object isn't moving, but the image sensor is.
