           Correspondence
                                            Frank Boyer Salisbury was born in Utah in 1926. He severed in the U.S. Army Air Force in 1945, and was a
           Correspondence, Heb-N            representative for the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints in German-speaking Switzerland from 1946 to
                                            1949. From 1949-1950 he was a photographer for Boyart Studio and till present has remained s part-time portrait-
           Correspondence, D-Heb            commercial photographer. He graduated from the University of Utah with a B.S. (1951) and M.A. (1952). He received
                                            his Ph.D. from the California Institute of Technology (1955). He was an assistant professor of botany at Pomona
           Correspondence, O-Sal            College from 1954 to 1955, then assistant professor and professor of plant physiology at Colorado State University
                                            from 1955 to 1966. He served as the NSF Postdoctoral Fellow in Tubingen, Germany, and Innsbruck, Austria from
           Correspondence, Sal-Z            1962 to 1963. He served as a professor and head of the Department of Plant Science at Utah State University from
                                            1966 to 1970. Between 1970 until his retirement in 1997, Salisbury worked in a number of distinguished positions
           papers
                                            including: Professor of Plant Physiology (1966 to present), Botany (1968 to present), and Distinguished Professor of
                                            Agriculture (1987, honor) at Utah State University. He was on the Board of Trustees at the Colorado State University
                                            Research Foundation from 1960-1962 and was a technical representative in Plant Physiology for the U.S. Atomic
           papers
                                            Energy Commission (now the Department of Energy) from 1973-1974. He was also guess professor at the University
                                            of Innsbruck, and Lady Davis Fellow, the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, 1983. He served as Financial Secretary for
           papers
                                            the Ohio Columbus Mission (LDS Church) from 1997-1999.
