     Santiago , Chile. July 8th. From the antarctic today came official reports

tinas' Antarctic Base saw the flying object and photographed it.

     Mario Jahn Barrera , commander o f the Chilean Base told the Defense Ministry

one of these science-fiction things". However , it was something real , an object
