MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD
SUBJECT:     CENTER LANE Interview Summary - HU-1045/8310/01 (U)



1.   ( S/CL-3/NOFORN) On 181000 October 1983, source 1101 was
interviewed concerning the activities of an unidentified
suspect between the hours of 0700 and 0830 on 24 July 1983.
The project 8310 sponsor had provided a photograph of the
suspect (see Incl 1).
2.   (S/CL-3/NOFORN) For this interview source was shown the
photograph provided by sponsor and directed to focus his
attention on the suspect during the appropriate time.      No
other identifying data was provided to source.     All other
information was held for use during subsequent interviews.
3. (U)     A transcript of the interview was prepared (see Incl
2).
4.  (U) A DA Form 341 (Agent Report) was not prepared for the
sponsor. Information related by source did not appear to be
relevant to the sponsor's area of interest.
