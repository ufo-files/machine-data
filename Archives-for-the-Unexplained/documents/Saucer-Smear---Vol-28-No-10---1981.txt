MISC. RAVINGS:
      On the night/morning of Sept. 2lst/22nd, your "Saucer Smear" editor
made an appearance on the Candy Jones radio show in New York City, for the
first time since December of last year. Apparently public apathy concern­
ing UFOs is on the increase, because this show was billed as a mixture of
saucers, monsters, and several other mysteries. The other studio guests
were Tim ("Mr. UFO") Beckley and a man with the unlikely name of Donald
Fangboner, who is connected with the Lake George (New York) Historical So­
ciety. Two other monster hunters were interviewed by telephone, and your
editor gave an update on the West Jefferson, Ohio Bigfoot case discussed
in the past two issues of this zine        •   •   •   •




      Beckley's appearance on the above-mentioned show was connected with
his then-forthcoming "National UF O & New Age Conference", held in the su"Q­
basement of a midtown N.Y.C. church. Among the UFO personalities     that
participated in this 2-day conference were Gray Barker, Dr.Frank Stranges
(all the way from California) , contactee Alex McNeill, and your humble
"Saucer Smear" editor.
      Your editor's contribution to the festivities was to preside over a
"panel of experts" consisting of himself, Tim Beckley, John Keel, public­
ist Mike Luckman, and former UFO enthusiast Dominic Lucchesi. Although
all five of us raved on past our appointed time limits, the most glaring
breach of truth and reasonable behavior was by Dom Lucchesi, who teed off
on a wild pseudo-scientific flight of fantasy, based loosely on his fal­
tering recollections about the Albert K. Bender "hush-up" in 1953.
      From our rather cynical viewpoint, the best speaker of the symposium
may well have been a gentleman named Frank Winn, who is said to be a col­
lege professor. Winn spoke first as himself, then allowed the Martian
entity "Mon-Ka" to take over his body, and continued speaking as that en­
tity. As Winn wound up, he received two rounds of applause - the first
for himself as Mon- Ka, and the second for himself as himself. What more
glorious Ego Trip can there be?
      "New Age" (as opposed to UFO) speakers included psychic Kitty Steele,
Pam Miller, Ivan Ortiz, Lynn & Anthony Volpe, Bill Cox, and Jane Allyson -
a psychicly-oriented young lady whom we always enjoy hearing.    Although
Jane says little if anything that hasn't been said many times before, she
says it well and most sincerely.
      The crowd at the symposium consisted of about 200 hard-core UF O and
psychic enthusiasts. Beckley is to be commended for having gotten     to­
gether a group even as large as that, in these lean times. Let's all say
nice things about him so he'll do it again next year:            .   •   •   .
