Floaters
It's over 40 years now that I have been reading about or investigating the UFO phenomena, am I any the wiser, I'm not
really sure. On looking back the UFOs of the 1950 era look so simple, daylight discs, glowing cigar mother-ships, what
else could they be but 'Flying Saucers' from outer space?

I suppose it was only a matter of time that we progressed to occupants from the UFO being reported, although the aliens
quite often turned out to be blonde long haired Venusians or the like. As the kidnapping turned to abductions so the
occupants became standardised as the little greys. I often wonder if the power of the media has coloured our perception
of what an alien should look like. Some areas of South America featured sightings of bellicose hairy dwarfs, maybe if
they had appeared in the good old US of A they may have via the media become the accepted norm for UFO occupants.
Whilst UFO sightings continue throughout the world the unexplained events seemed to have been swamped by a host of
abduction cases, crashed saucers and conspiracies. So much more has been added in over the years, crop circles,
poltergeist connections, animal mutilations, ancient astronauts, pyramids, Atlantes, just to name a few. Oh, for the simple
discs of the 1950s!

After investigating sightings for the Centre over many years I realise that discovering that a local event is unexplained is
not going to uncover an answer. However, having said that, the collection of data over the world has demonstrated that
an ·unexplained phenomena exists, The obvjous difficulty is uncovering the source for the phenomena. Would you chase
endless cars along highways at night time, rather like the farm sheep dog chases all the cars that come by, he never seems
to fmd out what they are. Landings that suggest that something is looking over the area, or beams of light searching the
ground for reasons unknown. Abductions that appear to be growing out of control, bedroom invasions where the
abductee is just whisked off to some-place, do the occupants even need a 'space craft' for this operation. A question that
I never seem to see asked but should be of paramount importance, where do the UFOs and their occupants go to? So
often during a flap a series of reports of similar objects occurs over several weeks, it appears at night, then seemingly
goes into thin air until it re-appears some evenings later.

So many events across such a wide range of countries and cultures. Almost always the same response from Governments
or scientific circles, Maybe because explanations as to the phenomena's source have come from dubious sources, fringe
cults, and some just out for a few dollars. One example is the theocy that the UFOnauts are from the Pleides constellation
and obviously are called Pleidians. The Pleides as seen from earth is a group of stars, but should you be able to travel
there you would fmd no Pledians. The stars are in fact separated by many millions of lights years and are not actually
grouped together, in fact with the passage of time the constellation patterns as seen from earth will change and the
Pledians disappear. It's rather like saying that the Chinese origii:J.ate in such disparate places as Hong Kong, Cairo,
Shetland Islands, and Barbados!

It seems that the real answer to the UFO phenomena may never be found. Despite numerous books and TV shows that
present UFO data as if they are proved fact no one solution has stood the test. I some times wonder what I will do if the
answer is uncovered, there will be a lot of unemployed UFO officers. Does the average man in the street care about
UFOs, probably no, only when it starts to affect the hip pocket nerve, then we may see some action.

I guess many of us like a good old mystecy, the Loch Ness monster, Abominable Snowmen, and UFOs. Its sometimes
nice to know that we do not know the answers to everything, although maybe we should tl)' and fmd out just what the
UFO phenomena is all about.
