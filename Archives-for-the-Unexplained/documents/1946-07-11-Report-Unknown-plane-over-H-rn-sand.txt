Unknown plane over Härnösand

Time: July 11, a.m.
Place: Smitingens havsbad, Härnösand (BM57)

Summary: A "ghost airplane" was reported to have circled at great altitude over the city of Härnösand. It was soon identified as an airplane from AB Norrlandsflyg making flights for the anti-aircraft defence. It was an Arado Ar 96 B, ex-German, bought by AB Norrlandsflyg in March 1946.

Status: Identified as aircraft
Sources: Sundsvalls Tidning, July 12. Dagens Nyheter, July 12. ScanCat 1262.
