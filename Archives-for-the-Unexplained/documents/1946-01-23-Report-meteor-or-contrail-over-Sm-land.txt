Meteor or contrail over Småland

Time: January 23, 15.00 hours (afternoon)
Place: Northern parts of the county of Småland

”A mighty light phenomenon was observed at about 15.00 hours, on the Wednesday, over the northern parts of [the county of] Småland, for instance at Eksjö and Mariannelund. At very great altitude a streak of light, almost like a comet, moved in an east-westerly direction. The light, which could be seen for 12 minutes, was reportedly seen, at about the same time, at Rydsnäs. Eyewitnesses guess that what was seen was a meteor. It is not impossible, however, that it was an airplane, exhaust-gases from which transformed into ice-crystals, giving the illusion of a streak of light.”

Note: Seeing a meteor for 12 minutes would not be possible but seeing the (luminous) vapour trail from a meteor for such a time would be possible.

Status: Probable meteor or contrail
Source: Smålands Dagblad, January 24.
