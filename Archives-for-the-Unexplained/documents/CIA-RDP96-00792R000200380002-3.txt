TEXT: ENTIRE.

      THE CHINESE ACADEMY OF SCIENCES RECENTLY PRESENTED MEDALS OF HONOR
      (RONG YU ZHANG) TO MEMBERS OF THE SCIENTIFIC COUNCIL OF THE ACADEMY
      WHO HAVE A HIGH REPUTATION IN SCIENCE. THE MEDALS ARE AN EPRESSION
      OF   GRATITUDE  FOR   THEIR    OUTSTANDING ACHIEVEMENTS IN CHINA'S
      SCIENTIFIC UNDERTAKINGS.    MEDALS OF HONOR WERE PRESENTED TO 323
      MEMBERS OF THE SCIENTIFIC COUNCIL, MOST OF WHOM ARE PATHBREAKERS OR
      LEADERS IN VARIOUS BRANCHES OF SCIENCE IN OUR COUNTRY, INCLUDING
      SUCH FAMOUS SCIENTISTS AND EXPERTS AS YAN JICI, SU BUQING, WANG
      GANCHANG, QIAN XUESEN, QIAN SANQIANG, QIAN WEICHANG, HUANG KUN, ZHU
      GUANGYA, XIE XIDE, JIN SHANBAO, CAI QIAO, HU FUTAG, SHEN QIZHEN, LU
      JIAXI, YAN DONGSHENG, ZHANG QINGLIAN, HOU XIANGLIN, TU GUANGZHI,
      HUANG, JIQING, CHENG YUQI, (HAO ZHICHUN), WANG DAHENG, LIN LANYING,
      SHI CHANGXU, AND SHEN HONG. THE SCIENTIFIC COUNCIL OF THE CHINESE
      ACADEMY OF SCIENCES CONSISTS OF OUTSTANDING SCIENTISTS AND TECHNICAL
      EXPERTS WHO HAVE LAID A FOUNDATION FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF SCIENCE IN
      OUR   COUNTRY.   THEY    HAVE   ALSO   PROMOTED THE DEVELOPMENT OF
      MATHEMATICS, PHYSICS, CHEMISTRY, EARTH SCIENCE, ASTRONOMY, BIOLOGY,
      AND OTHER SCIENCES, AND HAVE TRAINED GENERATIONS OF SCIENTISTS FOR
      THE COUNTRY.
