July 30: Ninth shot from White Sands

Swedish media reported on the most recent V-2 firing in New Mexico: 

”NEW ALTITUDE RECORD BY SPACE ROCKETS.
The ninth space rocket of German V2 construction was, a few days ago [July 31, according to Willy Ley: Rockets, missilies and men in space, page 506], fired from the experimental area in New Mexico. The rocket reached an altitude of 165 km, a new altitude record. After its aerial voyage the rocket landed 111 kms from the shooting point... The head of the Air Force´s research department, Calhoun, declared, after the test, that humanity is facing a planetary investigation never before seen. Next autumn a rocket with a vertical range of 280 kms will be sent to collect facts about the stratosphere at this enormous altitude. A special rocket for the transport of human beings will also be constructed, and human beings will be brought outside the earth´s atmosphere without any harm to them.” (Umebladet, August 10; Expressen, July 31).
