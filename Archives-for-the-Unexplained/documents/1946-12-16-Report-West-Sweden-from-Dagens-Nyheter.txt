December 17, 1946.  Observations of a bright fireball over Western Sweden  A brilliant fireball, assumed to have been a so-called aerial bomb, was seen traveling over Western Sweden at 17:30 o’clock on Monday evening. It was reported as having a long tail and went with fierce momentum to the southwest-northeast. No sounds were heard and the altitude of the object remains completely unknown. According to the observers, the object couldn’t possibly have been a shooting star or any other celestial phenomenon.
