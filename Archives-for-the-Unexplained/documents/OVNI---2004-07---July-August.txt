              CROP CIRCLES: IS THIS THE END OF THE LINE?
                  "Crop Circles used to be the work of amateur pranksters.

             Now flattening wheat fields is a lucrative commercial enterprise."

           A Report by Kate Burt: Independent.co.uk News               (Extract). 06 July 2004
Rod Dickinson clearly remember the night he made his first crop circle. It was the summer of 1991, just
months before the famous crop-circle hoaxing duo, Doug Bower and Dave Chorley, came out about their
nocturnal wheat flattening activities; a time when the nation was still gripped by the idea that aliens might
feasibly have been parking up in fields at night, all over the southern English countryside.

"No one had really mooted the idea these things might have been man-made," said Dickinson, an artist "as
far as people were concerned, me included, there were definitely other possibilities; at least some
unexplained natural phenomena. I �as fascinated." So when a friend challenged Dickinson to join him in
an illicit attempt to create their own crop circle, the pair found themselves on their knees in the middle of
the night, with a not very elegant, not very round mess on their hands.

Thirteen years later, Dickinson has just completed what he estimates to be his 500th foray into the art of
what's become known as "circle-making". This time things were a little different. The medium was sand,
not crops and the ambitious formation replicated a photo image that Dickinson and his circle-making
partner, John Lundberg, 35, had spent several weeks translating into a series of co-ordinates on a computer
designed programme. From this, they'd created complex numerical spreadsheets, filled with measurements,
from which his team of 1 3 assistants worked. He'd also secured an advance permission from the
landowner, there was a four strong BBC film crew to capture the work in progress; a helicopter booked so
a photographer could capture the end result and a PR. Oh yes, and at this time, he and Lundberg got paid
several thousand pounds for their efforts by the satellite channel UK TV Gold, who commissioned the
piece to launch their new comedy season.

Last year, Dickinson and Lundberg completed their most lucrative commission to date, for the American
computer-chip company AMD. It involved making two "eco-paint" designs on grass in England and three
across the States--one in sand, two in grass, all photographed by satellites. Thanks to the pair's slick,
award-winning website (circlemakers.org), the business has snowballed since it was launched 10 years ago.

They've already also been booked by clients including Weetabix, 02, Big Brother, Mitsubishi and
Thompson Holidays and have just taken on a big commission for the Japanese company, Hello Kitty. The
budget for the Big Brother campaign was rumoured to have been a quarter of a million.

                  UFO OVER NEWTHORPE, NOTTINGHAM 5th JUNE 04
We received a report from a Mrs 'LM' of Newthorpe (near Eastwood), Nottingham describing the
sighting of an object at 5.55am on the 5th June this year:

I had woken up early because an arthritic pain in my leg (I am not on any strong medication, just
glucosamine sulphate and paracetamol). I had to take some papers out to the garage and I opened the
garage door, looking across my neighbours hedge to the west as I did so.

I saw what I thought was an aeroplane flying in the sky, it looked odd, so I looked again. It looked about
the size of a holiday jet liner, but it didn't taper off towards the tail and there were no wings! Also there
was a bright light, it looked like a star, I thought at first that it was a star, but it moved along keeping
pace with the main craft. It then disappeared out of sight behind my neighbours bungalow. I went
around to the front of the bungalow and watched it from there. The 'star thing' was still there with it.

The main object was cylindrical, perhaps what some people might call 'cigar shaped', but at the centre it
was nipped in all around, like a waist.

It looked silvery in the sky like aeroplanes do and it was at a similar height to aeroplanes, but I could not
hear any noise.

While I was watching it at the front of the house, the 'star' suddenly just disappeared.

I returned indoors and my husband said "it's five to six, where have you beenT' That's how I know the
exact time. When I went in, the object was slightly to the West of me, travelling North East.

I should add that it was a beautiful sunny morning at that time. The sky was clear as a bell-a lovely
light blue. The sun rises to the left of my bungalow, so it was behind me, lighting up the sky where the
objects were.

The outline of the object was sharply defined at the time and it moved across the sky quite quickly.

A difficult problem here, with the object reflecting the sun in a similar way to an aircraft. This can cause
all kinds of reflective problems, leading to mis-identification of an aircraft.

                           UFO's -'The French Connection'
                                          By Paul Ponssot       ©
For the past three months (April-June 2004), Paul Ponssot has been investigating 'contactee' incidents
in France as part of his University Course (Paris) and also working on behalf of the Derby based
Phenomenon Research Association. He has unearthed some intriguing cases and the first three are
covered in the following article.
France is well known for its good wine and cheeses. For UFO researchers France is the country of the
'COMETA' report, where for the first time in the world, top military officers and scientists acknowledged
that the UFO phenomenon was of ET origin.
It is also we ll known for its close encounters of the 3rd kind such as Valensole . However, many people­
including Ufologists- think that France is a poor country as far as abductions and contacts are concerned,
but nothing could be further from the truth.
At a UFO conference, when one might ask if any people have experienced contact or contact-like
symptoms, there will be an average of 3 to4 people that will speak out. Although some might be del uded
most of them are normal people from various backgrounds.
My belief is that France, like the USA or Brazil is not spared from alien abductions. But because of the
aura of ridicule that surrounds alien contact and the subject of UFO's in the eyes of the general public, the
country of Descartes (France) has no network to investigate any abduction claim, therefore they remain
unknown.
In my investigation I have uncovered no less than 3 abduction cases and they are j ust the tip of the
icebe�g.
These cases are:
(1) Mr JC, who woke up as a child with black figures at the side of his bed and who seem to have guided
    his thoughts ever since.
(2) Edmonde is a life long abductee - contactee who has also experienced the psychic phenomena often
    associated with abductions. Her account seemed at first to be extremely far fetched, however, after
    having spoken with her extensively , I realized she was telling the truth.
(3) Mr D, who experienced an abduction with physical effects on his bddy that were later medically
    assessed. This gentleman's account also included multiple witnesses, making it potentially one of the
    most important cases in the world.
In the following pages I will present you with as much detail as possible on these cases. In the second part
of my article, I wil l relate what I think the abduction phenomenon means to France and the world.

Part 1     The witnesses
(1) JC is a forty year old man from the south of France: he is of French, German, Jewish and African
stock. His first encounter took place when he was j ust 3 years old.
One evening he woke up to the presence of several figures: they were shadowy-looking with extremely
piercing eyes. In their presence JC did not experience the fear that would have been the classic symptoms
of a child of his age, instead he felt a sensation of inner peace and calm. Our witness also experienced
another contact when he was five years old.
He is certain that he has since undergone further contacts although he does not remember experiencing
any missing time .
When he was a child and a teenager JC thought that the beings he encountered were benevolent
extraterrestrials, however with the passing of time he has become increasingly wary of the entities he
encountered.

Although the account of this man may seem like a spirit possession rather than a UFO contact, I have

(2) The next case I will delve into concerns a 57 year old woman called Edmonde B. She lives in Paris
and has undergone numerous contacts with extraterrestrial beings. In the aftermath of these contacts she
has developed (like many other abductees and contactees), numerous psychic abilities, clairvoyance and
healing powers.
What I am about to tell you will seem incredible to some, but having met the main witness and having
spoken to her extensively I can vouch for her sincerity. Her first encounter took place near the town of
Montebeliard in the North East of France.
One winter afternoon of 1972 Edmonde was driving home from work with two colleagues when they
encountered a landed saucer shaped object 10 meters in diameter, in a field near the road .....Suddenly it
was dark! The three were puzzled at first and then shocked as they realized that 3 hours had vanished
from their lives!
It was only when she was placed under hypnosis many years later that Edmonde was able to reveal that
during her episode of missing time she had undergone a typical abduction examination.
During the period of the abduction, Edmonde's colleagues had simply been left in the car in a comatose
type state but she had been subjected to a medical examination by beings with a greenish scaly skin.
Although they had such an unconventional appearance, she had described them in her own words as
'beautiful'.
Edmonde's next contact took place in the town of Orange in south-eastern France in 1978.
She was staying in the town with a colleague, for professional reasons. They were supposed to stay in a
small hotel in the town centre, however at the last minute Edmonde heard a voice telling her to change
her hotel and to spend the evening in the hills above Orange. She decided to take the advice of 'the
voice' and changed her hotel, then later together with her colleague, she went up into the hills.
Suddenly the pair noticed a huge saucer shaped object emitting beams of light, "it appeared from
nowhere" said Edmonde. The UFO appeared to be structured and more importantly, it seemed to be
playing with her: it was literally answering her thoughts. The scene lasted for over one hour, much to the
astonishment of her previously sceptical colleague. That same night, Edmonde learnt that a shoot out
had taken place in the hotel where she was supposed to have been staying, killing 17 people in all.
To this day, she feels the aliens saved her life!
Edmonde's next encounter is extremely spectacular and is situated between alien contact and space and
tim� shifts. It took place in Marseilles one warm July evening of 1984. She was travelling with her
personal taxi driver, a French-Tunisian. As they reached the Marseilles Sant Charles area (Central
Station), Edmonde and her driver noticed how calm the city was: not a single passer by, not one car.
They thought this strange as in July the city is always full of holiday makers (the 'Oz' factor).
Suddenly the pair had the impression that the car had lifted off the road. All of a sudden, they both
noticed in horror that a strange 3 wheel car had passed by them, its shape was almost round (nothing
like the 3 wheel car made famous in the 'Mr Bean' series in the early 90's). However, what frightened
them most was the driver of the car: a woman with totally green skin, green hair and enormous spots all
over her body. Her description can be matched with that of the reptilian beings often encountered in the
USA and Canada.

This case is currently still being investigated. There have recently been a few discoveries made that

Prior to the January lOth incident, 2 members of Mr D's family had sighted UFO's. Could they be

                                    Part 2: Analysis and Theories
These cases show us that the UFO contact phenomenon is very widespread in France and that evidence
seems to point to the fact that it is worldly. Let us try and find the meaning to all of this.

First of all we might ask where do the aliens come from? Well the conclusion that I have come to after
talking to the people that I have met, is that the UFO phenomenon has multiple origins.

Some UFO's seem to come from outer space, for they have been tracked on radar. Once here they leave
multiple marks on the contactees bodies, as was shown in Mr D's case. In many incidents the ufonauts
seem as physical and as material as we are.

However to reduce the alien contact phenomenon to one single theory is far too simplistic, as a great
part of the UFO phenomenon seems to overlap the barriers of psychic phenomenon. Let us reflect on
Edmonde and her journey into space and time. In that other reality the entity that she encountered was
almost identical to the reptilians that are so widely described in the USA or Canada.

We know that like Edmonde, many contactees develop psychic abilities that range from clairvoyance �o
mediumship. They develop second sight and are often plagued by a poltergeist-like phenomenon.

Is it possible that some aliens could originate from other dimensions in space and time and that they
somehow pop into our reality through some mysterious doorway?

There is also another point of origin that is worth discussing, it is often dismissed even by the most
liberal Ufologists but I think it is a possibility like any other. 'Some' aliens could originate from under­
ground bases/caves beneath the earth's crust, or even bases under the sea that have yet to be discovered.

This assumption is based on the fact there have been incidents where the UFO's have been seen coming
in and out of the sea. Edmonde, who has become a channeler since her contact experience, has received
messages telling her about a race of lizard-like humanoids that live in caves beneath the earth's crust
and have always had contact with surface humans. Is this piece of news genuine or is it manipulation on
the part of the aliens? At this time no one can claim to have all the answers!

What is the purpose of an alien or 'non human' presence in the skies of France and the world?

To answer this question we must look at the intentions of the aliens. It is clear that one cannot say all the
aliens are benevolent. From what we can see in the 3 French cases, some aliens seem well disposed
while others seem not.

There is certainly a difference between the manipulative entities met by JC and the lifesaving aliens en­
countered by Edmonde. Other aliens seem total indifferent to humans: neither hostile nor benevolent:
they treat us like lab. Animals. One of the contactees I met at a conference, but whose account is not
mentioned in this paper , said very emotionally that what shocked him the most with the Greys (the
aliens he encountered) was their lack of compassion, the lack of emotion they showed towards their hu­
man victims. Mr D said that the impression he received from the aliens were that they felt cold, emo­
tionless almost like robots of some sort.

The fact that some aliens seem hostile or indifferent is worrying, however even more frightening is the
fact that we could be witnessing a silent invasion: in a subtle way some aliens could be modifying the
genetic structure of the abductees for their own reasons and purposes.

When one looks at the health reports concerning abductees, one can see that their blood is different to
that of other people: Mr D is affected by polyglobulemia, a rare blood condition. About 15% of abduc­
tees posses the B-blood group that is rare in other categories of the population. Many of the population,
be it in France or some other country posses psychic powers (Edmonde who has a gift for clairvoyance
and healing is just one example). If this theory is true let us hope that humanity will be the beneficiary
of these alterations . . . .Alas only time will tell.

Ancient Egyptians

In one of these chambers, we view an enormous sarcophagus, made of basalt. Its surface is marble-smooth and on one side are
