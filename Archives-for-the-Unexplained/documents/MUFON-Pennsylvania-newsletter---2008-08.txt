PA MUFON NEWSLETTER


August 1, 2008 Vol. 1 No. 8


Pa MUFON was again #1 in CMS for July. Thanks to all FI’s
for making that possible.


Welcome to our three newest Field Investigators: Tracy
Roy, John Weaver and James Matthews. That brings our
FI staff to 10 Investigators.


A Field Investigator Training Class has been scheduled for
September 28, 2008 and FI’s will be notified by email as
to times and location.


August 11, 2008 PA MUFON presented a lecture at the
Spirit Society of Pennsylvania on the Todd Sees
Investigation. A great turnout, for an interesting, although
strange case.


The PA MUFON Paranormal UFO Conference is scheduled
for October 25, 2008 at the Westmoreland Community
College, in Youngwood PA. Please see our website for
more information under Special Announcements.
