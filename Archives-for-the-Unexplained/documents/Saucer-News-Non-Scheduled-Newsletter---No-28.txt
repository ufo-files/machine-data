         HERE IS THE INSIDE STORY as to why Kenneth Arnold will not appear at our New York
   flying saucer convention.  Negotiations with Arnold were begun by telephone and letter
   nearly a year ahead of time, and it looked very much like an agreement would be reached.
   The basic idea was that we would pay his air fare round trip from Australia, where he
   was then planning to move.  Arnold also asked for a fantastic fee of one hundred dollars
   per day, which we finally cut down to a flat $500 figure.  The negotiations came to an
   end when Arnold began insisting on a first class air ticket from Australia - increasing
   the cost of the ticket from about $1,200 to about $2,000.  In late March, Arnold, who
   obviously had no interest in coming other than monetary, wrote us a letter concluding&
   "Unless I receive exactly what I asked for, forget the whole damn thing." We decided to
   do just that.
