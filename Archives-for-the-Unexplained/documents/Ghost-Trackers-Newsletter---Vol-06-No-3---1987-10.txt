    that     the       inhabitants              regard            their hauntings as part of                           their
    heritage;       so       many of       the      stories          dating from         times          immemorial,
    with their origins lost in                        the mists of              antiquity.
            The     British             people are proud of                    their historical heritage
    and     consequently                much        money         has       been      spent        by    successive
    Governments             and     various           Voluntary               bodies     over          the years to
    maintain these ancient castles,                               mansions         and churches in                as    good
    state   of     preservation             as is possible,                   thereby    providing            visitors
    to these       shores          with a multitude                  of   interesting         sites          to      enjoy

            Most       people           are      probably             already    aware of the stories                     of
