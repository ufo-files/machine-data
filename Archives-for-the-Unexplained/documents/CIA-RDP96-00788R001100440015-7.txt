                        SUBJECT:       Phoncon MAJ Stoner and MAJ Watt, 0830, 27 Mar 79



                        l. At 0830 hours, 27 Mar 79, I called MAJ Stoner, DAMI-ISH,
                        OACSI, to find out the status of Project GRILL FLAME. MAJ
                        Stoner supplied the following guidance:
-~·-~
        i                   a. Surgeon General's Human Use Sub-Committee had met on
                        26 Mar 79 and declared that Project GRILL FLAHE (as represented
                        by protocol submitted by AMSAA) was a NO RISK, NO HUMAN USE
                        type protocol. Further, he stated that AR 70-25 did not apply
                        to Project GRILL FLAME.
                            b. A Consent Statement is not needed. Note: I informed
                        MAJ Stoner that we would get a copy of Information Statement
                        that AMSAA was going to use and apply it to our program if
                        appropriate.
                            c. MAJ Stoner stated that Sub-Committee expressed concern
                        with ethics of program - i.e., how did one assure himself that
                        individuals would not take advantage of his/her ability? Note:
                        We have recognized this as a potential problem and have already
                        discussed the issue with all our project personnel. We will
                        continue to stress the importance of individual trust and
                        responsibility and that invasion of privacy violates the very
                        foundation of our program.
                        2. MAJ Stoner stated that INSCOM could immediately restart
                        their GRILL FLAME Program and reference this phoncon as
                        authority. Written authority will be forthcoming via SSO
                        message channels.
