Air Defense Department
Stockholm


After having learned that members of the Defense Staff are seeking clarification of factual findings connected to observations of an alleged light phenomenon (dated Tuesday, July 9, at about 14:40), I hereby report the following.

At this time, my wife and I found ourselves up in an observation tower, located at an old abandoned silver mine near Guldsmedshyttan, NNW of Lindesberg in Västmanland. In former times, the tower has been in use by the Air Guard and stands roughly at p 152 on the General Staff map, 1400 meters South of Guldsmedshytte Church. We then observed a brilliant, greenish (neon-like) beam of light, which rapidly traveled in a Northwesterly direction, after which it disappeared. The phenomenon vanished in a blaze of light and I thought I noticed a strange sound. We figured that the phenomenon must have been a meteorite, though we have before seen anything of the sort during the day. It was all over in an instant.

We are unable to report exactly when this event had occurred, but we do remember it being 14:40 PM when we had climbed back down. The sky was cloudless at the time.

Guldsmedshyttan, Prästgården, July 11, 1946.

Erik A.P. Reuterswärd
Administrative Assistant
Address: Pastellvägen 17, Enskede.
