ICFUFOR Journal files No 45 October 2004


  Kiriat-Yam 20.7.2004


  Sagit Ksirer saw UFO few times. She remembers one case, with her friend at 1996. This is why she is aware to the
  phenomenon, despite of her non-aligned surrounding.

  That day, between 14:30-14:45, she observed from her window at the 8th floor and saw bright lights in the south. In this
  location (south) there are the Carmel Mountains and the city of Nesher. She videotaped the lights with her Sony 220x
  video camera (automatic zoom) for about 3 minutes. There is an interruption in the film, because the lights disappeared for
  about 40 seconds, and then appeared again.


  The video – at the beginning, we see 8-10 linear and horizontal lights, and the houses are in the background of them. One
  of the lights seems to lit strongly with 4 light beams.

  In slow projection of the movie, frame after frame, we see a dark perimeter with a central shiny white light. We can also
  see blue and orange lights inside the white lights. In the left, we can observe a circular light. Afterwards, the second light
  from the right turns shiny, and then the fourth light turns on then the third light. Without zoom, we see the lights shining,
  blinking and turning on and off. Afterwards, the lights shape changes to blinking trapezoid shapes. We can also observe
  green lights with dark edges, in the margins, and yellowish light in the center. Afterwards, the shape of the lights changes
  again. It seems like every light is formed of 4 blinking rectangles. Afterwards, 3 of the lights are on, turning off and on
  again, while changing their shape to a cross-like shape, colored white and blinking, with dark edges. Sometimes, we can
  see a yellowish light in the center. Near the end of the video, we see the left light, which is lower then the other, blinking.


  Ha’arava 9.8.2004


  It happened at 04:30. Kobbi, a Petah-Tikva resident, drove his car northward. About half a hour before Sodom, he noticed
  a yellowish light in the sky, moving up and down. He woke up his wife, her brother and his friend, who were sleeping in
  the car. They parked the car to observe the object. Then they saw the object stopping and hovering, and 3 more lights
  appeared. They could see it was a triangular object, with 3 lights in its bottom ant a yellow light on its nose (in the front).

  According to Kobbi, the object wasn't far away from them; it was a few dozen meters in the air and west to the road.

  Everything around was quiet, but it was strange because they could hear a noise like an “industrial compressor”.

   Ashdod 17-19.8.2004


  Eli Abramovits, a dental technician, his wife, a high school teacher and their 17 year old son, noticed at night at a lighted
  object, bigger then a star, which was low in the sky. It started when Eli and his son sat on the balcony at 03:30 and played
  chess. Then they observed the object the first time. They decided to videotape it. The UFO appeared 3 nights, between
  03:30 to 04:00 and was videotaped (totally 35 minutes of tape).


  The video - when magnified, we see a circular object with a reduction – a linear strip in its bottom. Afterwards, we see it
  blinking in the houses background. We see that its center is circular with a bump in the middle. The perimeter is green.
  Then the UFO changes its shape, and becomes a white shining spot with green edges. We can observe changed in its
  central part to reddish-purple, which afterwards turns totally green.

  Afterwards, we can observe the object turning again to a spot of shiny light. Then it became purple in its center and green
  in its perimeter. Then it turns reddish in the center and green in the perimeter. Afterwards, it turned its light to orange,
  with a dark purple in its perimeter. On the objects surface we can observe blinks of green and purple lights. In the 13th
  minute of the film (dated 18.8.2004), according to Eli, the camera stood on a tripod. First, the UFO seems to change its
  shape from circle to a form created of little green-white rectangles, moving and changing. Afterwards it turned circular
  again, with the central circle lower (maybe because of a turn). Afterwards it changes its light to light-orange with a green
  perimeter and strips of light surrounds the object.

  Afterwards, the central circle seemed to be more higher and in the right (maybe a turn again). Then the central circle
  moves down to the lower right corner. In the 32nd minute of the video, the object seems to be a perfect circle, with
  prominent edges, like a hoop. Afterwards it shines, but not very bright. According to Eli, he needed to move the tripod,
  because the UFO moved and got away while filming it. He estimates that it was giant and located more then 10 km away.

  A sister of the witness to this case, called me ( Abi Greiff ). She defines her sister as a “very rationally” person. She told

  alternative field.


  Kiriat-Arba 23.9.2004


  Natti called me (Abi Greif) at 23:45. He told me that he and his father see from their balcony, started at 23:00, an unusual
  view in the sky.

  To their claim “right and close to the moon there is a yellowish-white object, blinking in red”. Natti adds, that in the
  beginning, he saw a smaller object arrives, and locates itself between this object and the moon. The smaller object started
  to become smaller, and it seemed to be getting away.

  His father says that the bigger object changed to a “sausage” shape and emitted a “flame”. Natti said, the object sometimes
  increases its light and emits “bims of light”. It seems to rotate around it self.

  First they observed the object with their naked eyes, then with binoculars. They claimed the object is located very high in
  the sky.

  Natti called at 20:45 the day after, and said the UFO is there again, but this time at left of the moon. When I (Abi Greif)
  went out to observe the moon, at the same time, I didn't see anything unusual there.

  Sefad 24.9.2004


  Oshri , a resident of Haifa, was that day in Sefad. It was 17:30, the start of the Yom-Kippur fast. Together with his friends,
  they noticed in the clear sky, an elongated white lump, moving high in the sky to the west. He claims it was above Kefar-

  The UFO moved horizontally, and was seen for about 10 minutes. Oshri photographed it with his digital camera 5 times.
  The object didn't leave a trail and seemed very strange, also because airplanes do not fly at Yom-Kippur.
