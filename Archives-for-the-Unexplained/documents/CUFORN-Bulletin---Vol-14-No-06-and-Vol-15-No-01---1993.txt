                       REGRESSIVE HYPNOSIS UNCOVERS STARTLING
                   SERIES OF ABDUCTIONS INVOLVING ONTARIO MAN.

For people who have read a great deal of the classic books on UFO abductions, and have wondered
"Are some people abducted more than once?", CUFORN has the answer.


Our main focus is on a man whom we will call Jack T. He is 38 years old and works in an electronics
warehouse as a supervisor and technician. In his spare time he plays a recorder in a local rock band
in his home town of St. Catharines, Ontario, Canada. In the spring of 1982, he had purchased Budd
Hopkins book Missing Time. After reading it, he felt there were some moments in his past where he
had some unaccountable periods of time. He wrote to Hopkins, got his New York City address, and
went through a series of three regressive hypnosis sessions under the direction of Dr. Aphrodite Clamar
at her office. He felt he had gained some knowledge from the sessions after he heard the tapes played
back to him in New York City.


When Jack returned to Canada, he heard that the MUFON UFO Symposium was to be held in Toronto
on the first weekend in July. He felt he should contact a Canadian group to continue the hypnosis, in
case there might be more he could find out about his past memory losses. When he heard that David
Haisell, one of the speakers, also had a UFO organization, he tried to contact him at the Symposium.
He mistook Eric Smith for Haisell. Thus one of our membe(s at CUFORN made contact with Jack. Eric
telephoned Joe Muskat, CUFORN's President and Co-Director, three days later.


Muskat finally spoke to Jack and found that Jack had undergone various tests in New York prior to
the hypnosis there. These included the MMPI, IQ, Allport-Vernon Value test, and a general sounding
out verbally with Dr. Clamar. At that time, he was only vaguely aware of the "van incident", which
we will mention later, and a UFO encounter with his father when he was young. His test results
showed him to be normal in all respects.


The series of events began when he was two years old in 1957. The others occurred in 1959, 1961.
some time in 1963 or 1964 when he was eight or nine years old in 1969 (age 14), on October 16,
1971, and in 1976. Four of the events happened in the St Catharines area. The 1957 and 1959 events
were in the Owen Sound district near Georgian Bay in central Ontario. We do not know the location
of the 1 961 event.


                             SEVEN HOUR PRELIMINARY INTERVIEW



CUFORN's Co-Directors first met Jack T. at the apartment of Harry Tokarz in Toronto on July 17,
1982. This seven hour interview was held in the presence of Mike Lambert, a reporter from the
Toronto Sun. He was there as a privately interested party and solely on the promise that no publicity
be given to the case until the investigation was completed. No decision has been made as to whether
there will be any publicity at all other than in ufological j_ournals. Mike Lambert also attended the 1982
MUFON Symposium and wrote two excellent stories about it. He did not tape the interview and agreed
that, if an article was done, CUFORN would have to approve all his notes in advance of the final story.

So far, he believed this to have been his first encounter with UFOs.


Jack seemed intelligent, stable, subdued, honest, and a bit nervous at meeting new people. He talked

                       BIGFOOT, A BLACK CADILLAC AND AN IMPLANT

As Jack talked to us, he recalled consciously the following events: He mentioned that he had heard
the tapes of his hypnosis sessions with Dr. Clamar and briefly mentioned an incident when he was six
while driving in the country near his home, with his father. Another recollection which was very vague
was an incident with a friend who accompanied him when he was nine or ten in either 1964 or 1965.
This was at Twelve Mile Creek, a wooded, hilly conservation area outside St. Catharines. The latter
city is on the Niagara Peninsula in Southern Ontario. He recalled that his friend whom we will call Jim
Voss was with him at the time. He did not remember seeing a UFO in this case.


Later on he was to relate under hypnosis an incredible tale about two type 1 humanoids and another
individual who maneuvered the two boys into a garage near Jack's house and into a black Cadillac.
They travelled over a dirt road to a nearby marshy area where there was a landed, large, metallic, disc­
shaped object. They were taken aboard for some sort of experimentation.


The next incident was an encounter when Jack was 14. He was with his friend Ken Johnson (a
pseudonym) i n another St. Catharines conservation area, Short Hills. He had a definite conscious recall
of a large Bigfoot type creature assisting Type 1 humanoids in abducting the boys. He had memories
of seeing his friend on an operating table with blood issuing from his left ear and temple, while
surrounded by humanoids who were dressed like doctors. Jack felt certain that this was an operation
in which some sort of implant was inserted in Ken's head. He felt that he may have had an implant
given him. Subsequent infrared photography done by Joe Muskat showed no anomalies in his local area
because the infrared film was past its expiry date. This was not noticed until it was too late to take
more photos. We urged Jack to have X rays taken, but Jack is sometimes a bit lazy. He has to be
prodded and he has not bothered to go to get the X rays take, despite our continual urging. We will
make a further attempt in the near future to repeat the infrared photography. Results will be known
to the readers when available.


The next occurance was the "van" incident of which Jack had a very vivid and detailed recall. It
happened at 1 :30 am in the very early morning hours of october 16, 1971, while driving home with
the band members after an engagement at a house party in Vineland Station, not far from St.
Catharines.
