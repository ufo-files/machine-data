Question: What is STRANGE DAYS ?
Answer:   It is a small magazine/newsletter written by,             and for,   those interested
           in UFOs, primarily in the North East.
Q. What it it � function?
A. The original intention of Strange Days was to provide a means of communication
     between the members of NUFORI and to provide a place where members could get                   i
     their thoughts, theories and ideas printed.
              However,   Strange Days is open to anyone         who wishes to write in      with
     their thoughts, comments or complaints regarding the subject of UFOs or related
     "Fortean" phenomena.
Q. How often is it produced?
A.   About once every four months,          depending upon how many people contribute and
     whether or not we need to pass on information to all of the members.
Q. How much does it cost?
A.   It varies, depending upon how many pages are in each issue, and the price of
     photocopying. This is a non-profitmaking venture, so you only get charged for
     the cost of copying and postage.
Q. How can I get hold of it?
A. Send £1.00 for each future issue you want. Send money in cash,                  stamps, P.O. s
     or cheques payable to D.A. Newton to the Editorial Address.               After the   costs
     have been calculated, any excess money will be refunded. And I can't say fairer
     than that, Guv.
