UFO Report (1971)
On the 6th of November, 1971, two families had left their summer houses in Edsbro (Uppland) in order to stroll through a nearby forest. They wandered several kilometers from the settlement and would finally reach a broader panorama of fields and meadows. One man cast his gaze upon the bright blue sky and would then come to observe something strange about 30 degrees above the horizon.
"What the hell is that?" he cried to the others, whom had slacked off and got left behind. Everyone stopped in their tracks, completely amazed. In the air hung a huge cigar shaped UFO. It was coloured intense yellow and the four adults estimated its size to be about 200 meters in length. The "cigar" lied dormant in a vertical position, at about 400 meters away. Its both ends were sharply marked and had a "greenish glow". Every now and then, orange puffs of smoke came out of its top.
After approximately seven minutes had passed, this "cigar" slowly began to maneuver in a uniform circular motion. No additional puffs of smoke were observed. After a few minutes had elapsed, the "cigar" stopped moving and would, very slowly I might add, begin to adopt a horizontal mode. (I have chosen to ignore all discussions and hypotheses referred to by the observers in question. I shall only include the concrete facts.)
For a few minutes, nothing remarkable would occur while the "cigar" remained in horizontal mode. However, in the next moment the center of the "cigar" started to shine and emit a "bright, star-like light". Immediately afterward, a classic saucer came out of the "cigar" and many more would be seen within mere seconds. Yes, all in all there were five saucers, which began to circle around the "cigar" in a corkscrew movement. The flying saucers were about 30 meters in diameter. They were described as aluminium-like and with flat domes.
"Now we did realize that we saw something that none of us had previously believed in", said one of the anonymous observers.
"Crikey Moses! Oi! Those are blooming flying saucers, blokes!", one man would exclaim, as he stood in awe of the situation. He was met with the response: "that's utterly impossible, ‘tis a load of codswallop."
As the saucers had circled around the mothership during a few minutes, they suddenly assumed a "plough formulation" and flew away with tremendous speed in southerly direction. Shortly thereafter, the "cigar" assumed a vertical position. It then began to accelerate straight into the skies, soon resembling a "yellow, glistening star", until it finally disappeared from view. The UFO formation was initially seen in a northerly direction, at 10.00 AM. The sighting was over in about 20 minutes. The weather was excellent.
A good friend told me about this "sighting". These four observers are middle-aged, trustworthy teetotallers. For various reasons, they all wish to remain anonymous. One of these male observers is employed as an agricultural engineer and the other is a master builder. Both women are housewives. The social and economic status of these families is very good.
The completed report was summarized with the words: "never before have we experienced something so strange (and alien). We have always believed that flying saucers (UFOs) were pure nonsense, but after having witnessed this event, we all are totally convinced that they really do exist. Even more remarkable is the fact that observations such as ours are not subject to a thorough investigation by all possible scientific expertise."
The case is crystal clear and will of course be categorized as an UFO sighting.
