CIRCLES FROM THE SKY
edited by Terence Meaden.

A mine of information by sixteen contributors on the subject of
the mystery circles and their origins .


The opening 128 pages constitute the proceedings of the first
international   conference     on   the   circles   effect.   They   are
followed    by a further 80 pages which update the reader by
reporting     the    latest    observational ,      theoretical      and
experimental results in circles research.


Did you know that quintuplet circles have recently been made
artificially by the impact of a single vortex upon an anode?
That    ringed circles can now be          made under controlled
laboratory conditions? And that one humid evening in 1990
two people      crossing a wheatfield were caught in a vortex
which swept out a spiral circle where they stood , the spinning
air rendered visible by condensed water vapour inside it? This
and other eyewitness reports, together with a long summary
of the 1990 circles season, two articles by agronomists and
several articles by scientists, are part of the historic volume
that is Circlesfrom the Sky.
