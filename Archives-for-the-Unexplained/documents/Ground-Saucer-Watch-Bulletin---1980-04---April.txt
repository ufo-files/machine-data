As the season of spring 1980 sets upon us there are some important
items that must be clarified. Ufology is always a world of rumprs,
stories and big news scoops. In this column I hope we can set the
record straight.
One of the lingering rumors of 1979 was the situation of GSW Director
of Research, Todd Zechel. Mr. Zechel dropped out of . ufology months
ago, surrounded by rumors ranging from abduction by the CIA to
leaving the country for other lines of work. The truth is much simpler
than all of that. Todd left ufology over the matter of funding.
Mr. Zechel is one of the few individuals who eats, sleeps and literally
lives with UFO's seven days a week. This is fine for research, but
does not help with ones livelyhood. The old adage applies about;

money alone". Todd unfortunately, could not afford UF0 s1

                             UFO UPDATE - PHOTO ANALYSIS
                             By: Editorial Staff

 The decline of serious, scientific ufology is becoming more apparent
 each month. With each new contact claim, new book, and UFO photo-
 graph, all attempts at moving forward to establish credibility to
 an already abused subject, are falling short of expectations. The
 latest attempt to establish 'proof positive' comes in the form of
 a slick new UFO book, containing numerous · daylight photographs and
 the claims of the prime witness of over 100 contacts with 'space
 beings' .                                            ·
  Under the facade of scientific investigations, including computer
  enhancement, laser scanning, micro-densitometry and other equally
  fascinating terminology, the readership of UFO: Contact From The
  Pleiades are lead to believe the complete scientific authenticity
  of the story and photographs. This book had to be written, after
  all it was time. Many years have transpired since George Adamski
  successfully published UFO contact books and presented them to the
  American public. The time was right. So, do it again with a time
  proven standard - slick detailed photographs and claims of contact
· with 'space brothers'.
 UFO reports have come a long way since Kenneth Arnold's famous
 sighting back in 1947. The UFO-listening public was tired of simple
 UFO reports, now they had to be flashy. After all, who's interested
 in a series of UFOs hovering over a military base, when we can bypass
 this and have elaborate contact. It's like comparing two science
 fiction movies: The Day the Earth Stood Still (circa 1950) to the
 modern Star Wars or Star Trek heavily laden with special effects
 and visual wizardry.
 The above scenario capsulates the writing efforts of UFO: Contact
 From The Pleiades. GSW, during the past five (5) months has received
 dozens of phone calls and letters asking about the expensive (twenty-
 plus dollars) book. Our reply was to be careful and wait for UFO

 Since GSW's intent is not to start another controversy, as had been
 the case with previous cases of this type, we will only report the
 results of our analysis on ten of the photographs.
 In 1977 GSW was contacted by a Wilfried Falk of West Germany, with
 a request to evaluate the now famous Meier Photographs. The fi~st
 thing anyone interpreting the photographs will notice is the high
 volume of color, daylight photographs taken on so many different
 dates. Culling over thirty years of photographic cases, there is no
 record of multiple-exposures of authentic UFO's. Generally a UFO
 photograph is a one time experience that catches the photographer off
 guard, not a bi-weekly experience.

The second obvious item noticeable in all of the photographs is the
clarity of the images. Many of the Meier photographs contain extreme
detail, yet in the small percentile of bonafide images analyzed to
date, most are inarticulate sources of light or a relatively clear
disc-shaped object void of any markings or protrusions. Another
observation in all of the photographs evaluated by GSW was the lack
of contrast in the sky above the UO image. This appears to be more
than just a coincidence especially in light of all the photographs
taken.
The GSW photographic interpretation began over two and a half years
ago when the organization was asked to evaluate the pictorial evidence.
High resolution negatives were made from the ten 'original' photo-
graphs and distributed through our normal chain of .photographic
analyzers. Representatives from two of the photographic firms and
our own consultants "conventionally" evaluated the data. We presented
the UFO photos in a non-biased manner, whereas, there was no mention
of multi pie (continual) experiences and the claims of contact were
given to the sources. Using densitometry, sizing techniques based
on the film/camera data, sectional enlargements and other techniques,
the photographs were all returned with negative conclusions.
The opinion of our consultants, who by now have become slightly bias
with pictures of purported UFOs, were carefully weighed against that
of the outside sources. In capsule form, all of the opinions indicated
a hoax. Basic questions encountered during the analyses included:
(1) The "sharpness of the images" compared to other features in the
same photograph being extremely out-of-focus indicated, in their
opinion, the closeness of the images to the camera. (2) The suggestion
of a linear structure above one of the 11 saucers 11 could be construed
as a supportive device. (3) The spatial placement of the UO "in a tree"
in another picture was questioned.
Simulta~eously, a computer analysis was conducted using close to forty
sub-routines. The consensus from GSW that the UO images depicted a
combination of grandiose and crude attempts to hoax a series of UFO
encounters was formulated based on the following items:
1)   All of the analyzed UO (unidentified object) images are close
     to the camera/witness. In reference to three of the photographs
     the UO appears to be located between the 'close' background
     trees and the camera. The camera lens is a 42mm variety and
     with this data the UO calculates less than four (4) feet from
     the camera.
2)   In another photograph the UO image is close to the camera and
     revealed that a model of eight to ten inches in diameter was
     utilized. Pixel (picture cell) jaggedness measured for both
     density and waviness, and compared to other features at known
     distances, revealed straight edges with even densities.

SUBJECT:    Summary of observations of Aerial Phenomena in the
            New Mexico Area, December 1948 - May 1950
TO:        Brigadier General Joseph F. Carroll
           Director of Special Investigations
           Headquarters USAF
           Washington 25, D.C.
      1. IN a liaison meeting with other military and government
intelligence and investigativ.e agencies in December 1948, it was
determined that the frequency of unexplained aerial phenomena in the
New Mexico area was such that an organized plan of reporting these
observations should be undertaken. The organization and physical
location of units of this District were most suitable for collecting
these data, therefore, since December 1948, this District has assumed
the responsibility for collecting and reporting basic information
with respect to aerial phenomena occurring in this general area.
These reports have been distributed to the Air Material Command,
USAF, in accordance with Air Intelligence Requirements No. 4, and to
other interested military and government agencies.
     2. There is attached, as a part of this summary, a compilation
of aerial phenomena sightings that have occurred mostly in the New
Mexico area and have been reported by this District Office subsequent
to December 1948. This compilation of sightings is not a complete
record of all reported observations, but includes only these in which
sufficient information was available to justify their inclusion. The
observers of those phenomena include scientists, Special Agents of
the Office of Special Investigations (IC) USAF, airline pilots,
military pilots, Los Alamos Security Inspectors, military personnel,
and many other persons of various occupations whose reliability is
not questioned. This compilation sets forth the most important
characteristics with respect to each observation and evaluates each
sighting into one of three classifications,(!) green fireball phenomenon,
(2) disc or variation, and (3) probably meteoric.
     3. There is also attached an analysis of the green fireball
occurrences in this area made by Dr. Lincoln LaPaz. Dr. LaPaz is the
Director of the Institute of Meteoritics and Head of the Department
of Mathematics and Astronomy at the University of New Mexico. He was
Research Mathematician at the New Mexico Proving Grounds under an
OSRD appointment in 1913 and 1944, and Technical Director of the
Operations Analysis Section, Headquarters, Second Air Force, 1944-45.
Since 1948, Dr. LaPaz has served on a voluntary basis as consultant
for this District in connection with the green fireball investigations.
     4. On 17 February 1949 and again on 14 October 1949, conferences
were held at Los Alamos, New Mexico, for the purpose of discussing
the green fireball phenomena. Representatives of the following
organizations were present at these meetings: Fourth Army, Armed
Forces Stecial Weapons Project, University of New Mexico, Federal
Bureau of Investigation, U.S. Atomic Energy Commission, University
of California, U.S. Air Force Scientific Advisory Board, Geophysical
Research Division Air Material Command USAF, and the Office of Special
Investigations (IG) USAF. A logical explanation was not proffered
with respect to the origin of the green fireballs.  It was, however,
generally concluded that the phenomena existed and that they should
be studied scientifically until these occurrences have been satisfactoril
explained. Further, that the continued ·occurrence of unexplained
phenomena of this nature in the vicinity of sensitive installations
is cause for concern.

     5. The Geophysical Research Division, Air Material Command,
Cambridge, Massachusetts, has recently let a contract to Lend-Air,
Inc., Hdoloman AFB, Alamogordo, New Mexico, for a limited scientific
study of green fireballs. The results of this scientific approach
to the problem will undoubtedly be of great value in determining
the origin of this phenomena.

military and government installations.
