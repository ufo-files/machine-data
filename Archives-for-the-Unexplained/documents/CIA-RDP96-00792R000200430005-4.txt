      A COMPREHENSIVE DATA BASE OF TRADITIONAL CHINESE MEDICINE HAS
      PROVIDED A USEFUL AND CONVENIENT SERVICE FOR OVER 1,000 FOREIGN AND
      DOMESTIC CLIENTS SINCE IT WAS PUT INTO USE IN 1990. CREATED BY THE
      INSTITUTE OF PUBLICATION AND INFORMATION UNDER THE CHINESE ACADEMY
      OF TRADITIONAL CHINESE MEDICINE, IT IS THE MOST COMPLETE MEDICAL
      DATA BASE OF ITS KIND SO FAR AND HAS GREATLY PROMOTED THE SPREAD OF
      UNDERSTANDING   OF   THE   THEORIES AND CLINICAL APPLICATIONS OF
      TRADITIONAL CHINESE MEDICINE.
      CONTAINING NEARLY 100,000 ENTRIES, THE DATA BASE CONSISTS OF FOUR
      DOCUMENT SEARCH AND RETRIEVAL SYSTEMS FOR THE AREAS OF ACUPUNCTURE,
      TRADITIONAL CHINESE MEDICINE, TRADITIONAL CHINESE PHARMACEUTICALS
      AND QIGONG.

      DEMANDS OF CUSTOMERS AND HANDLES TECHNICAL TRAINING.
