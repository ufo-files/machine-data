    Our Review                                                                       Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  Full size reproduction of the famous front page announcing, "RAAF Captures Flying Saucer On Ranch in
  Roswell Region," along with more routine news of the day. ("Dairymen of Area Hear Lecture Series.") A
  sidebar describes local reaction: "Roswellians Have Differing Opinions On Flying Saucers." The print
  quality is roughly equivalent of what you would have seen on your doorstep that Tuesday morning in
  1947. Probably one of the most memorable front pages of all time. As a bonus, we also include the
  following day's front page: "General Ramey Empties Roswell Saucer." This page includes several
  articles responding to the previous day's story. ...Fascinating pieces of American history. -- Glenn
  Campbell
