                                      INTERESTING DEVELOPMENT
Middlesex county section director Steve Firmani is making an attempt to set up a
UFO reporting topic of UFO sightings within the Digital Equipment Corporation where
Steve is employed. Digital utilizes many types of access systems, one which is a
notes/file . Within these files are what is called conferences which in turn can be
broken down into topics. Each topic has the ability to receive replies and carry on
conversations with any active account within Digital in any part of the world. Steve's
plan is to try and set up a topic within this file for the reporting of UFO sightings.
One advantage to this is that a fellow Digita l employee would be more likely to open up
to one of hiw own peers about a sighting and others within the corporation accessing
this system will be aware of where they can report such incidents. Steve also has
access to conferences on Meteorology and Astronomy. (see attached sample}
