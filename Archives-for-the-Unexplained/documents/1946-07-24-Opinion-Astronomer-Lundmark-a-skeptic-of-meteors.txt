July 24:...and astronomer Lundmark a skeptic of meteors...

- Well, says professor Knut Lundmark, when Stockholms-Tidningen late on Tuesday evening speaks to him at the place where he spends his holidays. I really just know what the papers have written and hardly that, since I have been away in the wilderness. But after what I have read it seems unbelievable that it would be the question of meteorites. 

”It is true that sometimes one can foretell frequent meteoric visitations - and such are not uncommon in the late summer - but if I have read the papers correctly most of these ”meteors” have been sighted in daylight and it is extremely rare that a real meteor can be viewed during the bright part of the day. So, I am mostly inclined to think it is some kind of a projectile. But that, I suppose, the military will eventually find out.”

Source: Stockholms-Tidningen, July 24. Also syndicated to many other papers by STB.
