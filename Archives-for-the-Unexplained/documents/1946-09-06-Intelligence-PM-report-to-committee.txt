PM beträffande läget 6/9 1946
1. Undersökning av II. milo rörande nedslag 27/8 kl 1420 300 m norr Bräcke har ej givit något resultat. Detaljbeskrivning 50 m från observatören en skarp knall från jvg, varefter en svart rök steg up. Omedelbart efteråt en eldkula med bländande sken rakt mot observatören. Kan ej vara knalldosa.

PM regarding the situation as of 6/9 1946
1. Investigation by II. milo regarding impact 300 m north of Bräcke on 27/8 2:20 PM have not given results. Description of a sharp bang from a railroad 50 meter from observer, followed by rising black smoke. Immediately followed by a fireball with a blinding light towards observer. Could not be a detonator.
2. Observation southwest of Uddevalla 22/8 9:38 - 9:41 PM unlikely to be an airplane. The sound reported by fobef is of the opposite direction.
3. Received reports26/8 Two light phenomena27/8 at 11:40 AM, 30 km south of Östersund, southerly direction, low altitude, smaller airplane with hissing soundapprox 9 PM, explosion at impact at northern Jyllandtwo light phenomena28/8 9:45 AM Västede (60 km west southwest of Sollefteå) easterly course, 300-400 m altitude, airplane slightly longer than 1 meter with strong hissing sound.2:13-3:25 PM, Radar report F 16, investigated by FFA light phenomena29/8-31/8 and 2/9 no reports1/9 a light phenomena3/9 2:10 PM Fjärderholmarna, westerly course, height 800-1000m, 6-8m long, spool shaped object with yellow white shine, silent. According to observer not an airplane.A light phenomena4/9 no reportIn total 11 reports of which 7 light phenomenon during 10 days
4. Compilation of four reports from different parts of Jämtland 24/8 around 11 PM, could possibly give result of an object passing in a mostly westerly course at a speed of 450 m/second.
5. CFV and I. milo received orders regarding surveillance during FV practice.
6. The Helsinki Naval Attaché have on the 9/8 reported:Statistics not allowed in the finnish air force's staff, whereby the central meteorological agency currently collect data alone in Finland.The courses point towards Porkala point as starting point, which is unlikely, as the border guards would have made several observations. More likely launch sites in Balticum.Theory: Radio triggered rocket charges on projectile sides give changes in direction. Projectile course tracked against internal transmitter.
