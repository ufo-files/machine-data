"Flying Saucers" Here?

that recently lighted the skies over parts of this pro-
vince was no mere flash in the pan, it seems.
The inci-
, dent, which excited people from River John, through

shore, has not been ignored by scientists.

force after the appearance of such a heavenly object. In

seem to have been little more than hysterical, gathered

not in the way they had expected; the earth spun on
