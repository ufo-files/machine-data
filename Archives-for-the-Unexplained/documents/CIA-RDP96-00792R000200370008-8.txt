   THE COMMON PEOPLE THINK THAT SINCE "PUBLIC OFFICIALS" SET STORE

DEPARTMENTS, SPORTS DEPARTMENTS, TRADE UNIONS, AND OTHER DEPARTMENTS
ARE CARRYING OUT DIFFERENT QIGONG ACTIVITIES, HOLDING VARIOUS KINDS

ATTENDED THEM.   TICKETS FOR MASTER YANXIN'S DEMONSTRATION LECTURES

SYMPOSIUMS ON QIGONG FREQUENTLY. BECAUSE AT THAT TIME "QIGONG" WAS
NOT INVOLVED IN POLITICS. IT NEITHER ATTEMPTED TO OVERTHROW THE CPC
NOR DEMANDED IT TO SPEED UP REFORM IN ANY FORM. IN THESE
CIRCUMSTANCES, THE QIGONG CRAZE SPREAD AS NEVER BEFORE. SINCE 11 4

SUSPENSION MANAGEMENT REGULATIONS FOR QIGONG PHYSICAL TRAINING."

PLACES-HAVE ALSO ESTABLISHED "OFFICES MANAGING QIGONG FOR PHYSICAL
