Case xxx.
Finned object seen by conscript

Time: Tuesday, July 30, 15.12 hours
Place: Oscar-Fredriksborg, coast artillery base in the estuary of Stockholm

Note on a slip of paper in the Defence Staff files:

”Conscript 3864-1-45 4171, Wilhelm Jörgensen. Time: July 30, at 15.12. Place: Oscar-Fredriksborg. Direction: over the barracks [SW according to the Defence Staff tabulations]. Course: Westerly. Flying object with short wings or fins. No sound and no fire [exhaust]. Speed greater than jet airplane. Thickest 1/3 from the nose. Distance 2.000 meters, about 30° over horizon (giving an altitude of 1.000 m). The witness has seen a V1 starting as well as a V2 in Germany.” 

The sighting tabulations offer one further piece of information: ”horizontal trajectory”. There is no indication, from the archives, that the sighting was further investigated by the Staff. A radar indication had been reported at the same place (Oscar Fredricsborg) exactly 47 minutes before Jörgensens sighting (case xxx above).

In the summer of 1986 Anders Liljegren attempted to trace the witness [via Captain Lind of KA1, the Coast artillery regiment]. He is a man born June 5, 1925 [thus 21 years old at the time of the sighting]. When checked against the Swedish registry [personal number] Wilhelm Jörgensen is no longer registered, meaning he is either dead, or has emigrated. 

Status: Unidentified
Source: Slip of paper in Defence Staff archives; Defence Staff tabulation July 29-August 2. Minutes of committee meeting, August 5.
