            (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS) The Special Activity Report is the sixth
       in a series of reports which provides data in response to a
       request from Joint Task Force Five (JTF-5) in Alameda,
       California. The data provided in this report will be used by the
       J22 division in their assessment effort regarding their
       counterdrug mission. The information provided in this report was
       generated by PAG-TA employing remote viewing (RV) methodologies.

             (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS) The data provided in this report is the
       sixth  ~· a series of taskings. This project concentrated on the
SG1B

       exploratory. Results should not be considered as stand-alone;

          {S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)       Source C:
         -- The target consists of a medium vessel that is on
water; however, there is land nearby. This vessel is white on
the top and gray, brown, tan, or black on the bottom. A flapping
sound is associated with this vessel. Masts may be on this
vessel. Smaller boats surround this vessel. Some of the smaller
boats look like canoes and some look like dinghies. There are
men in the smaller boats rowing up to this larger vessel. Then
an off-loading occurs from the big vessel to the smaller vessels.
Many people are involved in this activity. Contraband is
involved.
           The vessel and water were only part of the activity.
Much work goes on here concerning the contraband on land and air.
There was either a helicopter or airplane involved with the
activity. This flying object at one point flew very close over
the vessel. I felt that I was in a warm climate and many dark
skinned people live and work here. There are some parts of the
area where poverty exists.
        -- As for location, at one point I could see a large
structure that looked like a building that was sitting in water!
Also, I had a feeling that the waters are conducive for whales or
sharks.
