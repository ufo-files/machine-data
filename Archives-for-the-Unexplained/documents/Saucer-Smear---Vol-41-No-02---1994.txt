FURTHER THOUGHTS ON THE ROSWELL, NEW MEXICO CASE:
      As expected, our comments about the Roswell case in the last "Smear"
brought forth sharp disagreement from many of our fans. Some of these points
are printed in the Letters section of this issueo
      One thing that everyone, including skeptic Christopher Allan, seems to
agree on is that the proper date for the FBI telex is July 8th, 1947, rather
than July 6th, even though it says July 6th right on the telex. Obviously
we are missing something here.
      Another thing that we feel confident everyone agrees on, is that the
Roswell UFO was not a Japanese Fugo Balloon, as famed Fortean theorist John
Keel insists it was!   Some aspects of the fragments found in the   "debris
field" sound like a Fugo; but since these Japanese balloons must have stopped
being sent across the Pacific at least by the time World War II ended in Aug­
ust, 1945, it is hard to imagine how one of them could have stayed aloft for
almost two years and then come crashing down into New Mexico:   Sorry, Johno
      One thing that makes us happy is that our timing could not have been
better, in starting a Roswell re-hash in our last issue. We now learn from
Phil Klass' SUN Newsletter (Jan., 1994) and elsewhere that an official in­
vestigation of the a�leged Roswell government cover-up has been launched by
the General Accounting Office (GAO) , at the instigation of Congressman Stev­
en Schiff of New Mexico, a member of the influential Government Operations
Committee .. This same GAO recently made headlines by revealing that the'Atom­
ic Energy Commission conducted experiments from 1948 to 1952 which secretly
and intentionally exposed UoS. citizens to radiationo   Thus it can be assum­
ed that the GAO is serious when they make an investigation, and there    may
well be hope that we will learn something new about Ro.swell quite soon,  af­
ter all these years!
      Incidentally, the letter on Page 8 is from a well-known UFO personality,
and though it is a parody of Klass' SUN Newsletter, the last two paragraphs
of this letter are literally true - according to the writero
      In closing, we would like to congratulate Thomas Carey for his series
of articles in the Intern ational UFO Reporter (IUR) in which he explains his
painstaking efforts to find the legendary archaeologists who supposedly ap­
peared at one of the two supposed Roswell "crash sites" shortly after the
crashed UFO & dead bodies were found by others. In short, there seems to
have been no such archaeologists. However, there is a second "crash site"
in which Carey and other researchers have more confidence.
      And, we would like to congratulate ourselves for having snagged the two
leading current experts (or "experts") on the Roswell case as speakers at
our forthcoming National UFO Conference in Cleveland, Ohio on the weekend of
Sept. 16th-18th. They are, in alphabetical order, Stanton Friedman and Ke­
vin Randle. To say that these two gentlemen don't see eye to eye on all as­
pects of the case is putting it mildly; but both are excellent speakers, and
both believe that an interplanetary craft was involved. Randle will have
published by that time a brand new book appropriately called "UFO Crash at
Roswell" ..
