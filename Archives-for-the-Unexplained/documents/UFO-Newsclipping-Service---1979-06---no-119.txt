      TIMES, Dunedin , FL - May 24, 1979                 A special poll of 50 area residents ranging from 18 up,
                                                      revealed that 78 percent believe in the possibility of
                                                      Unidentified Flying Objects, yet only 13 percent would
                                                 .! ever report one if they saw one.
                                                         "Anyone would be a fool to not believe in UFOs," said
                                                      Pam Farley, now attending summer school at St.
                                                      Petersburg Junior College, "anything that's in the sky
                                                      and is unidentified is a UFO."
                                                         One middle-aged dark-haired man said that he saw a
                                                      "dish shaped glowing object," while in his backyard
                                                     Saturday afternoon. " It sped laterally," he said, "and
                                                      then it stopped and disappeared into a cloudless sky."
                                                      He said it disappeared in the west.
                                                        The man never saw anything like this before and was
                                                     _u naware of other sightings in the area. The Kozak
                                                      family in Dunedin spotted a similar object, but
                                                      remaining relatively stationary, three w~ks ago.
                                                        One elderly gentleman refused to answer whether he
                                                      had seen an UFO. "I'm saying 'no comment'," said the
                                                      man, "I'm old and people'd think I was senile."
                                                        One man answered the poll in one of many
                                                     questionnaires handed out at local shopping areas. He
                                                     wrote that if his wife claimed she saw a UFO he would
                                                     "divorce her right away."

                                                    the truth.

                                                    publicity on UFO sightings and reports, she spoke to
                                                    many people about the subject after being intrigued by
                                                    Reeves' report.
