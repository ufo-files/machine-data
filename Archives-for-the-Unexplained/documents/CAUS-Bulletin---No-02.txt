BULLETIN
#2                Ed. by L. Fawcett &   B. Greenwood       December 1985




      The length of the MJ12 article effectively crowded out other news
in Just Cause so we'll briefly cover a few items here.
      There seems to be a general increase in UFO sightings worldwide.
We are pleased with this trend because people are starting to ask ques�
tions about UFOs again and CAUS has been given a few new leads on in­
cidents, one allegedly involving an Air Force pilot who took an extre�­
ely clear photograph of a domed UFO. As always we are evaluating the
photo at present and should have something to say about it by the next
issue.
      We are including in this bulletin a (crummy, unavoidably) copy of
the so -ca11ed ".Aquarius" document as a supp1ement to the Just Cause art­
icle on MJ12. Keep in mind, it is unconfirmed through the FOIA at present.



      UFO SIGHTING IN ZIMBABWE- AN UPDATE: Some U.S. papers carried a
news story about a rather sensitional UFO sighting over Zimbabwe, Africa.
The sighting involved the scramble of two BAE Hawk MK 60 fighter jets
from Thornhill AFB and radar confirmation. It took place on July 22, 1985.
We have been advised by Cynthia Hind, a well-known UFOlogist from Harare,
Zimbabwe, that the sighting has been tentatively identified as an instr­
ument- carrying,high altitude French balloon. Cynthia is still investig­
ating other sightings in the area but the main incidents in the press
stories appear to have an ordinary explanation.


      NEW SECRECY GROUP FORMED:- An item in the New Mexico DAILY LOBO in
Albuquerque for Sept. 4, 1985, indicates that a new group, patterning
itself after CAUS and, in fact calling itself "Students Against UFO
Secrecy" (SAUS), has formed. Organized by University of New Mexico stud­
ent Rodney Towers Beaton, SAUS's aim is to "educate the public, find out
what UFOs are, and to open discussion on the subject". Beaton has so far
spent $250 this year to secure government documents, mainly from the FBI.
We wish him luck (and a deep pocketbook) in his efforts to gain release
of classified UFO documents. We also sincerely hope his   organization is
not pronounced "souse"!


      FT. DIX/MCGUIRE AFB- UPDATE: So far we have been unable to confirm
the supposed official form report on this CE3 incident. We have also been
unable to confirm independently any UFO activity whatsoever in NJ on the
date of the sighting. A single "East Coast Boom" report was found in NJ on
the morning of January 18th but it appears to have no connection to
the Dix/McGuire report. Larry Fawcett, using his police contacts in
New Jersey, reports that no State Police records or logs could be
located with reference to the incident. In fact, no one recalls-the story.
