     The next stop on Kalin’s fascinating train of discovery is
the famous Michelson-Morley experiment (MM). These two
were awarded a Nobel Prize in 1907 for a failed experiment.
Their intent was to demonstrate that Earth is moving through
the aether of space by measuring the difference in the light
traveling through two channels - one longitudinal and the
other latitudinal (interferometer). They detected no difference,
which took us back to the vacuum of empty space.
