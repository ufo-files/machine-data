                                  DID THE STS-73 ASTRONAUTS SEE A UFO?
             According to the November 1995 issue ofTHE UFO ENIGMA, published by the UFO Study Group of
          Greater St. Louis, a female astronaut aboard the STS-73 flight qf the Space Shuttle Columbia said it
          happened. UFOSG member Bob Soetebier obtained the information from the computer networks.
           STS-73 had two female astronauts aboard -- Kathryn Thornton (Payload Commander) and Catherine
          Coleman (Mission Specialist 1). The amateur radio operator credited with taping the downlink feed from
          Columbia said that on October 22, at 2:06 a.m., a female astronaut said these exact words: "We have an
          Unidentified Flying Object." For 10-15 minutes nothing else was said. When something was finally said, it
          was not about the UFO. Did the Columbia crew see a UFO and switch to a secure downlink to discuss it?
          Or was this just a joke? I doubt that we will ever see photos or video tape of the event; so all we can do is
          speculate about this event as we have all the other astronaut reports in the past.


                                                       COMPELLING                   EVIDENCE
             It was reported on the computer network on October 28, 1995, that two compelling pieces of evidence
          were shown at the Dialog With the Universe Conference in Dussledorf.
            The first was a videotape of Astronaut Gordon Cooper saying that he knew for a fact that a UFO landed at
          an airbase where he was stationed, was filmed landing, and he had been the person who had sent the film
          and documents to Washington, D. C.                  If true, this is one piece of filmed evidence that the U.S.
          government could easily produce for public disclosure. Sufficient time has lapsed for automatic security
          classification downgrading.
            The second was a film shown by Mr. Bongiovanni, the Italian stigmatic, which shows a crashed disc UFO
          on a 40 foot military flatbed truck.          It also shows a big hanger, then shows something (perhaps the
          propulsion system) being hoisted by chains. It was claimed that the film had come from Wright Patterson
          AFB and shows a crashed UFO recovered in 1947. If true, this film could be a bigger bombshell than the
          Santilli films.
           This material, when considered along with the hundreds, perhaps thousands, of gun camera films of
          UFOs taken by military pilots during and since WWII, is a part of a vast and growing reservoir of UFO
          information that should be made public.
