E.   SESSION SUMMARY:

     The target site consists of a fenced-in enclosure with one
side partially obscured. There are areas which are overgrown and
uncleared of vegetation reminiscent of weeds and vines. There is
stuff piled up throughout the area.   The target feels seemingly
unkept.

     There are concentrations of rusty metal, boxes with one end
open, grating and a sharp, piercing smell.     Concepts associated
with the target are: heat; smoke; spinning water; movement like
treaded vehicles; parallel marks reminiscent of scrape marks in the
sides of a large, squarish hole in the ground; rubber and series of
uniform "humps".

      There is a low building which feels empty and unoccupied (see
sketch on page 3). It has a low, wide opening or entrance on one
side.    The walls consist of concrete or brick.      There are no
windows.     The side is a light color and its appearance is
reminiscent of white paint which has peeled. There is a high wall
which abuts along one side of the low structure and towers over my
head. Other associated features are depicted in the sketch on page
3.

                                          PROTOCOL



                              JOINT COMMUNICATIONS SERIES



            This Joint communications (JC) series will be series of four
       (targets) with feedback provided after all four projects are
       completed.
            The targets will be selected by "CW". At 1000 hours each
       day "CW" will focus on one target element as well as any key
       dynamicjfeelingjsensationjsoundjcolor associated with or implied
       by the target content. This "sender" information will be
       recorded as part of the target material.

            The sender will hold target focus for at least 30-45 minutes
       initially at a specific time during the day.

            Viewers will initiate their response at 1000 hours, or at
       any other time throughout the day. At least two sessions are
       desired per target (the latter session for refinement and for
       developing target spatial relationship or dynamic/feeling aspects
       in more detail) . Viewers also have the option of working on the
       target during the evening.
            All sessions will be summarized as soon as completed.  In
       the case of evening work, this material will be documented by
       0900 hours the following day.

            Evaluation of this series will be accomplished on a case-by-
       case basis based on a 0-5 scale comparison, and on a blind
       ranking procedure. The four targets will be arranged in random
       order and forwarded to us after all session data is completed for
       the last target.  This will facilitate the blind ranking
       procedure. Ground truth will be obtained only after the judging
       is completed.
