USK ASTRONOMY club members, shown here, have
  Invited the public to "come and have a peep" at
. Halley'a comet.
    The club normally meet every Thursday at Usk
Adult Education Centre. but have been keeping a more
regular vigil.

     On Wednesday and Thursday last week between
sixty to seventy people turned up to take up their offer.
    The offer is also open this week.

    Education centre warden Mr Vlan Roberts said the
club had recently bought a ten-inch reflector telescope,
prompted by the comet's appearance.

    "The education centre must be the only place In
Gwent where the pubHc can come and have a peep at
the comet through a good telescope," said Mr Roberts.

Friday from 6pm but Mr Roberta added: "lt's no good
coming down If Its cloudy. But if it's clear and the moon
