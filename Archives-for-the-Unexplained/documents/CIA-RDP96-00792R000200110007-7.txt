         ((TEXT)} NOW THAT THE HEALTH OF THE PREREFORM ECONOMY IS
     DISCUSSED ALOUD AND UNHYPOCRITICALLY, WE BELIEVE THE TIME HAS COME
     TO SPEAK EQUALLY FRANKLY ABOUT ITS SHADOWY SIDE, AS A SHORTCOMING
     WHICH IS A DIRECT CONTINUATION OF THE "MERITS" OF THE
     ADMINISTRATIVE DECREE STYLE OF ECONOMY: STRICT CENTRALIZATION OF
     PLANNING AND RATIONED DISTRIBUTION OF RESOURCES, PREVALENCE OF
     COERCIVE MOTIVATIONS IN THE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM, ADMINISTRATIVE
     DECISIONMAKING METHODS ORIENTED TOWARD QUANTITATIVE PARAMETERS,
     HIERARCHICAL CRITERIA FOR ASSESSING LEADERSHIP CADRES, CONSTANT
     REPRODUCTION OF LEVELING TRENDS, AND SO FORTH, THAT IS, A
     PHENOMENON GENERATED BY THE MAIN FEATURES OF THE ECONOMIC
     MANAGEMENT SYSTEM THEN IN OPERATION.
        THIS APPROACH MEANS BROADENING THE FRAMEWORK AND REGARDING AS
     THE SHADOW ECONOMY NOT ONLY OUTRIGHT ECONOMIC CRIME (THAT IS, THE
     "BLACK" ECONOMY), BUT ALL UNRECORDED, UNREGULATED METHODS OF
     ECONOMIC ACTIVITY, AS DISTINCT FROM THOSE SET FORTH IN NORMATIVE
     DOCUMENTS AND RULES. THE SHADOW ECONOMY EXISTS BOTH OUTSIDE THE
     SOCIALLY ORGANIZED SECTOR (IN THE SO-CALLED "SECOND" ECONOMY), AND

                ICEBERGS OF THE "SECOND" ECONOMY ((SUBHEAD))
                THE TERM "SECOND" ECONOMY IS NOT INFREQUENTLY USED AS A. SYNONYM
            FOR THE SHADOW ECONOMY IN ITS CRIMINAL INTERPRETATION. HOWEVER, WE
            WILL FOLLOW THE WELL KNOWN HUNGARIAN ECONOMIST J. KORNAI AND USE IT
            TO REFER TO ALL THOSE FORMS OF PRODUCTION ACTIVITY THAT WERE
            FORMERLY DEEMED INEXPEDIENT--INDIVIDUAL LABOR ACTIVITY, SMALL-SCALE
            GROUP PRODUCTION (ARTELS AND SMALL COOPERATIVES), AND OTHER "SMALL
            FORMS" (THE "FIRST" ECONOMY, CORRESPONDINGLY, IS LARGE-SCALE STATE,
            COOPERATIVE,A ND KOLKHOZ PRODUCTION, THE ONLY FORM CONSIDERED TO BE
            A GENUINELY SOCIALIST SECTOR).
               THE "SECOND" ECONOMY IS LIKE AN ICEBERG: ON THE SURFACE IS THE
            LEGALIZED, REGISTERED PART COVERED BY TAXATION; BUT THE GREATER
            PART IS CONCEALED (FROM FINANCIAL AND CONTROL ORGANS, BUT NOT FROM
            CONSUMERS) AND EXISTS WITHIN THE FRAMEWORK OF THE SHADOW ECONOMY.
               UNTIL RECENTLY THE "SECOND" ECONOMY WAS ILLEGAL OF NECESSITY,
            ALTHOUGH THERE WAS, FOR INSTANCE, NO DIRECT BAN ON INDIVIDUAL LABOR
            ACTIVITY AS SUCH (NOT ONLY IN LEGISLATIVE ACTS, BUT EVEN IN
            NORMATIVE DOCUMENTS AND PROVISIONS). MOREOVER THE RIGHT TO SUCH
            LABOR IS GUARANTEED BY THE CONSTITUTION, ALTHOUGH THE LOCAL
            AUTHORITIES, ON THE PRINCIPLE "WHAT IS NOT PERMITTED IS
            PROHIBITED," WERE EXTREMELY RELUCTANT TO REGISTER IT: IN THE
            COUNTRY AS A WHOLE, BY 1987 ONLY 100,000 INDIVIDUALS WERE
            "LICENCED." BUT THERE WERE CONSIDERABLY MORE OF THEM
            "UNDERGROUND": ACCORDING TO ROUGH ESTIMATES, SOME 20 MILLION
            PEOPLE WERE INVOLVED IN THE PROVISION OF SERVICES ALONE IN THE
            "SECOND" ECONOMY.
                "PRIVATE OPERATORS," FOR INSTANCE, LONG BEFORE INDUSTRY AND
            TRADE COULD GET THINGS MOVING, WOULD SATISFY THE SPONTANEOUS
            PREMIUM DEMAND, WHETHER AMONG THE MASSES OR AN ELITE, FOR VARIOUS
            GOODS IN SHORT SUPPLY. THEY MANUFACTURED PHONOGRAPH RECORDS FROM
            X-RAY PHOTOS AND MADE "DESIGNER" CLOTHES; THEY MET THE DEMAND FOR
            CULTURAL WORKS THAT WERE NOT PERMITTED AT THE TIME: THEY COPIED
            WESTERN VIDEOS AND XEROXED "LOLITA," "WE," "DOCTOR ZHIVAGO," AND
            "HEART OF A DOG." AND IN THE BIG CITIES THE "PRIVATE OPERATORS"
            CLIPPED POODLES AND LAPDOGS; TAUGHT YOGA AND IKEBANA; PRACTISED
            PSYCHOANALYSIS AND CLAIRVOYANCE IN THEIR OWN HOMES OR THE CLIENT'S;
            SANG AT WEDDINGS, DID PEOPLE'S WORK FOR DIPLOMAS AND COURSES, AND
            SO FORTH.
