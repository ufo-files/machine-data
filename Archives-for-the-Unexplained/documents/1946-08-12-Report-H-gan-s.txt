HELGE JOHNSSON
SHIPOWNER

August 12, 22.45
Höganäs

Air Defense Department
Stockholm

On August 12, at 22:45 o’clock, there were observations of a brightly shining object over Höganäs. Reportedly, its size and shape resembled a small croquet ball.
Course: southeasterly.
Altitude: at about 200 meters.
Additional notes: overcast skies. The object remained visible for only a few seconds, after which it disappeared into the clouds. Its speed was reminiscent of a modern fighter jet. No sound was heard and no smoke was observed.

Regarding the trustworthiness of this very report, I must stress that I have been an instructor and inspecting officer at lbevkomp. Both Captain Lennart Bunke at Air Force Headquarters and Captain Wilhelm Lothigius (formerly active at the Air Defense Staff) can attest to my credibility.

I made a point of not contacting the press in this matter, since I found no reason to provide the experimenter with any information.

Höganäs, August 12, 1946.

Helge Johnsson
