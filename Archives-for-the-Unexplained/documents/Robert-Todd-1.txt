Dear Mr. Todd

Reference your Freedom of Information Act request, dated
30 September 1978, in which you requested copies of a United States
Defense Attache Office message, dated 24210oz May 78 and a Foreign
Technology Division message, dated FTD CONF NOFORN 19183oz May 78.
I n addition, you also requested any further information pertaining
to the subjec t matter discussed therein.

We were unabl e to locate any additional information relative to
the subject matter of these messages; however, we were able to
locate both messages . Since they are classified, we do not have
disclosure authority .
Queries were made with the Foreign Technology Division (FTD) and the
Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) to ascertain whether or not they
had received a request from you for these documents. We were advised
by FTD that they were processing a request from you for FTD CONF
NOFORN 19183oz May 78. Therefore, we are not transferring your
letter to them.
However, we were advised by DIA to transfer your letter to them for
direct response.
A determination will be made in regard to whether the records you
requested will be released or withheld from disclosure under the
Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. 552, within 10 days (except
Saturdays, Sundays, and legal public holidays) after receipt of your
request by the responsible processing agency.
