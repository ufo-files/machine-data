    Our Review                                                                       Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  "Three states rolled into one, Utah has the pristine alpine country of the Rockies, the awesome canyons of
  the Colorado Plateau, and the remote mountain ranges of the Great Basin. You can explore vast
  wildernesses, take in cosmopolitan Salt Lake City, or ski Utah's "Greatest Snow On Earth." Wonder at the
  spectacular rock formations in the national parks of Zion, Bryce Canyon, Capitol Reef, Canyonlands, and
  Arches. All this and more await you in Utah. Like his best-selling Arizona Traveler's Handbook, Bill
  Weir's Utah Handbook has carefully researched facts and valuable recommendations; even Utah natives
  will wonder how they ever got along without this well-organized guide!" -- From the Publisher. -- Glenn

          Utah Handbook features:

                  Places to go--scenic drives that retrace the Oregon Trail, hiking in Canyonlands National Park,
                  fishing the Green River, snowshoeing in Bryce Canyon, and river-running the Colorado

                  Essays on the state's fascinating history--early Mormon settlement to Native American folklore--plus
                  background info on Utah's spectacular geology and wildlife

                  Lodging, camping, dining, recreation, and transportation suggestions for every taste and budget

                  Thirty detailed maps to guide your way

                  Practical tips ranging from wilderness travel and ski-slope safety to bargain-hunting and museum-
