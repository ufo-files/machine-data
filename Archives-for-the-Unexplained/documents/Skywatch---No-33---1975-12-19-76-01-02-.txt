     So, for those who will be reading about Aquarian
Estates for the first time, we will tell you about the birth
of this project, which started as a thought in the minds of
a small group of people - people who wanted to serve.

     After much searching for a situation accessible to
Durban, we finally chose this pretty farm of 235 acres
which nestles in a fertile valley between Pietermaritzburg,
the capital of Natal, and Richmond .   We did not have
sufficient funds to buy the farm outright and so carry a
fairly large bond, the repayments on which are somewhat
crippling .   It is now two years since Aquarian Estates came
into being - two years of hard work, many problems, but
much growth .

     In 1974 approximately 3 000 fruit and nut trees were
planted, the existing farmhouse was renovated and a block
of three rooms erected . The year 1975 saw still further
growth with the building of a five -roomed house, the
acquiring of a further 50 acres, preparation of large
tracts of ground for vegetable planting, together with the
making of masses of compost, and the pruning, grafting and
budding of hundreds of fruit trees.   In August our Farm
Manager and his family arrived from Belgium - he is an
expert in the field of Bio -dynamic and Biological farming,
and as it is anticipated that we will soon have continuous
supplies of vegetables, as an outlet, we are building a
farmstall; there are also a good number of other •outlet
projects• in the melting pot .

     Besides all this farming activity, a good number of
Groups have taken over the farmhouse for weekends or longer
periods and held enjoyable and successful seminars and get­
togethers . All facilities are provided and interested
Groups are invited to drop us a line.    As from 1976 part of
our activities will include a Self - Realisation Course the
last week of each month; the first Course will be from 26th
January to 1st February.  Those unable to spend a full week
with us are welcome to attend the final two days .   The
inclusive charge for the Course and full accommodation will
be R5 per day . Contact us for full details .

     As a healthy body is essential for living a full and
happy life, a Homoeopathic and Naturopathic Clinic will be
held on the first Sunday of each month.

                A GLIMPSE INTO THE LIFE OF
                     RUDOLPH STEINER


      Rudolph Steiner was born on the Austro-Hungarian
border in 1861.  From an early age he was both spiritually
and scientifically inclined, finding no fundamental con­
tradiction between the two.  At the University of Vienna
science was his principle subject, but at the same time he
was immersing himself in the great works of philosophy, as
well as taking a keen interest in all branches of the arts.

       The works of Goethe held a great attraction for him
and he eventually edited many of the great author•s
scientific works.    By this time he was writing books of
philosophy himself.    He did not believe that rational
thought and intelligence must be suspended in order to
achieve enlightenment; on the contrary, he was convinced
that man • s reason was an important tool for spiritual
advancement.    At the close of the 19th century he came in
contact with the Theosophical Society, maintaining a close
association with them for ten years, and becoming General
Secretary of the German branch of the Society .   However, it
gradually became evident that his own ideas were tending to
veer away from those of the Theosophists, so that ultimately
he broke away to form his own movement, the Anthroposophical
Society (the Greek word •anthropos • means man, while
• sophia • means wisdom).  Writing about his movement the year
before his death, Rudolf Steiner stated:    •Anthroposophy has
its roots in the perceptions - already gained - into the
spiritual world.    Yet these are no more than its roots. The
branches, leaves, blossoms and fruits of Anthroposophy grow
into all the fields of human life and action • .  And so,
indeed, it has proved.

      In the field of the arts, Rudolf Steiner wrote four
mystical plays, and at the same time he developed a com­
pletely new art which he called eurythmy - speech and music
in movement.  Courses in eurythmy are now held in centres
all over the world, as well as courses in speech and drama,
painting and other aspects of the artistic media, based on
the inter-relationship between these various means of
expression and spiritual consciousness.  As an architect,
Steiner designed the headquarters of the Society in
5.

Switzerland.
     Toward the end of his life he was lectur·ing and writing
on a formidable range of subjects including mathematics,
astronomy, science, theology, philosophy, education,
economics and many otherso  Specialists in the particular
fields were impressed at his detailed knowledge.

     During the 1920's he turned his attention very
particularly towards medicine and agriculture, applying
both scientific and spiritual knowledgeo   His curative
preparations were based entirely on natural substances,
which could counteract the imbalance which caused the
particular disease.  To meet the demand for these medica­
ments the Weleda Company was formed in Switzerland in 1923.
Shortly after, branches were introduced in many other
countries, including Britain o  At this stage, until the
outbreak of World War I I a large proportion of the products
distributed by Weleda were imported from Switzerland;
however, since that time the U. Ko company has concentrated
on making their own preparations.

     At the same time, because the skin is a vital organ of
the body which breathes and absorbs, the principles indi­
cated by Rudolph Steiner were carried over to the field of
toiletrieso  These beauty preparations, all of which use
only natural substances such as herbs, vegetable oils,
honey, seaweed and plant essences, often gathered fresh
from Weleda's own garden, have become increasingly signifi­
cant in this age of mass-produced synthetics, and the·
escalating use of animal products in commercial cosmetics.

      Also of increasing importance is the poineer work
which has been carried out in organic growing methodso     By
planning the sowing times of different seeds and concen­
trating on the basic health of the soil, the Bio-Dynamic
method produces high- quality crops without using artificial
fertilisers o  In the U. Ko and on the Continent there are
Bio- Dynamic Agricultural Associations giving advice and
practical help on farming and gardening and in the U.K.
there is a newly-formed School of Bio-Dynamic Agriculture
and Earth Science.

     Social welfare has never been neglected, and there are
Steiner -based homes, schools and village communities for

of bringing down to earth in a practical manner spiritual
ideals, has pointed the way of the New Ageo The potential

                       WHAT IS AN AGE.

      The Celestial Zodiac is a band of twelve constellations
lying close to the ecliptic, or to the path of the earth
around the Sun.  These constellations have a profound
influence on our solar system as energy is channelled from
this larger field to the lesser field of our solar system,
which is in motion within that area.

      There are three movements of the earth that bring in
Zodiacal influences.  Firstly, as it turns on its own axis
every 24 hours, secondly as it revolves around the Sun
every 365 days, and the third movement by precession of the
equinoxes which determines the Great Year of 25 800 years.

      The unequal pull of the Sun and Moon on the earth's
bulges causes the poles of the heavens - the point in the
sky to which the axis of the ear h points - to move round in
a circle.  It is the same as the slow circle described by a
spinning top, but in the case of the earth a precession
takes place in the opposite direction to its rotation.

      The North Pole of the earth's axis at present points
almost at Polaris, our Pole Star, and in the year A�D. 2 000
will point directly at Polaris.  In the year A. D. 14 000
Vega will be the Pole Star, and in about A.D. 28 000, Polaris
will be the Pole Star again.  So the axis of the earth will
have traced a circle in the heavens, and it takes approx.
25 800 years to complete one full circle.  During this
great period of time the constellations visible from the
hemispheres, change.

      This rotation of the ear h's axis, causing the pre­
cession ( retrograde motion ) of the equinoxes, affects the
evolutionary process of planetary life as the Sun appears to
pass through the various constellations .   As there are 12
Signs of the Zodiac, the Sun appears to occupy each Sign for
an average of 2 150 years, constituting an Age, also called
a Great Month of the Great Year of 25 800 years.    The Sun
is not in each Sign for the same duration of time - for
instance the Age of Aquarius is of longer duration than that
of Pisces, but for convenience an Age is referred to as a

HISTORICAL NOTE:

    Fiery chariots, pillars of fire, flying scrolls
and other strange flying objects in the skies have been
recorded throughout history.

objects began to be seen in large numbers.  Since then,
millions of people all over the world have seen UFO's.
Where do they come from?  Are they terrestrial or extra­
terrestrial?  And what does their presence mean to
mankind?  These are all vital questions to be resolved.

INTRODUCTION:

    Contact International (S A.) is committed to the
premise that the UFO phenomena and associated
manifestations are important enough to warrant in­
depth study.  It is a non-profit making, non­
political movement and is run by responsible, intelli­
gent people, variously qualified and from all walks
of life, who are prepared to give of their free time
to assist in trying to resolve one of the most
interesting enigmas the world has ever known.  Contact
International (S.A.) is neither a cult, nor a
restricted research unit and adheres to no particular
hypothesis or ideology.  It DOES approach the UFO
phenomena with open-minded interest, progressive
thought and properly-placed speculation, at the same
time helping to undertake a continous programme of
objective research, which is conducted in the U.K.

    It is, in fact, part of an international
movement of close mutual co-operation between people
committed to the investigation of this great world
mystery.

REPORT ON THE 1975 NATIONAL CONFERENCE

 The 4th National Contact Conference was held this year
'at Everglades Hotel, Lion's River, in the foothills of
 the beautiful Drakensberg mountains, over the third
 weekend of August.  This weekend was well attended by
 the Durban and Johannesburg members.  The conveners must
 thank Elizabeth Klarer and her members from Johannesburg
 who took the trouble to attend this conference and make
 it the success it was.

On Saturday afternoon, after the welcome on Friday by
the National chairman, Mr. Les Mitchell, the group met
for a meditation and discussion and then in the evening
there were talks from the new Durban secretary, Miss
Elesa Willies, Mr. Dermot Stranix and Mrs. Elizabeth
Klarer.  Elizabeth mentioned some of her experiences
within the space craft and her contact with the great
souls who came so far to make contact with some of the
earth people.  The great advantage is, of course, that
Elizabeth has had actual experience of the contact and
has been taken by her friend to his home star.  The
group then discussed the many different aspects of
contact and all it meant and the implication of contact
with these advanced souls from the other spheres&  Dermot
led this discussion and gave the conference some very
interesting pieces of information regarding contact on
the telepathic level.
Later on that evening it was thought that a few of the
members did see the physical evidence of the space people
in the form of their ships in our skies.  The atmosphere
was charged with an unusual energy and at the beginning
of the evening all the hotel lights went out and stayed
out until the following day.  This had not happened very
often before and there were no storms in the area to
cause a power cut!
If the foregoing seems too far-fetched to our new readers
we have only to remember the reaction of a completely
illiterate tribe in central Africa when they witness,
for instance, a helicopter dropping out of a clear blue
sky; a few white-skinned men jumping out of it and
switching on their communications radio - on speaking
into it, a reply is heard from the so-called 'box'.

On Sunday morning the National Committee met, with the
two branches, to discuss the next year's plans for the
organisation.
During the weekend there was plenty of free time for all
to enjoy the quiet of the beautiful country.  Those who
were energetic were introduced to the 'bell' stone, a
phenomenon of nature where, if a certain large rock is
struck, it emits a deep, metallic, bell-like sound which
reminds one of a monastery bell. To our knowledge,
there are only a handful of these in the whole of this
country and we were fortunate indeed to have this one
virtually on our doorstep.
There was a great spirit of co-operation and togetherness
during this National Conference and an experience that
all who attended it will treasure in their memories.

GREAT MINDS HAVE BELIEVED IN INTERPLANETARY
COMMUNICATIONS (With acknowledgements to 'Heralds of
the New Age') .
Nikola Tesla (who died in 1943), originally Yugoslavian,
was one of America's great minds, who declared that he
was not an inventor but a discoverer of (new) cosmic
principles which he had learned from the deep secrets of
Nature herself.  Like many great ones, he was 'before
his time', and although his scientific discoveries were
hailed and put to use, his philosophies were disregarded
or scoffed at.  Scientists were incredulous when he
announced that he was - and had been - in constant
touch with other planets!
This man, who extended the fields of communication and
light, (among others) , stated that he spent two hours
daily for 15 years, sending and receiving 'inter­
planetary messages'.
Although an American citizen for over 50 years, he died a
pauper in the land for which he had done so much to assist
in the mighty industrial revolution.  The benefits of
his works are legion and range through modern radio and
T.V., to remote control by radio, neon lighting and much
more, all this at around the turn of the century.  Perhaps
his greatest gift to our world was his discovery of the
                                                      1 1.

polyphase alternating current system, the backbone of our
electrical distribution networks without which our indus­
tries would long since have stagnated.  Even textbooks on
electrical engineering give only extremely scant credit
and recognition to the genius of these modern times.

Here was a man who suffered greatly from man's ignorance.
Surely it is a cardinal mistake to dismiss anything, prior
to right and proper investigation? Tesla's 'interplanetary
communication' was never taken seriously, despite his
being a brilliant scientist.   This, apparently, was so
'off beat' that it was relegated to the realms of the sort
of thing which springs from an eccentric�  Why should such
a brilliant man suddenly become 'eccentic' with regard to
a different type of communication, when he first estab­
lished his worth through concrete evidence in the
scientific field?

Let us hope that the day is not far off when further
honours will be poured on this man, when interplanetary
communication will become an acknowledged scientific
truth.
