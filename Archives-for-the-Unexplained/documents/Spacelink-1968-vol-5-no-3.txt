 Editorial                 THE       C REDIBI LITY              GAP

     This man-made malaise has been diagnosed in many fields of human conduct and courageous exposes have
appeared recently, to the chagrin of many government departments in the "free world."   It goes without
saying that behind the "iron curtain" there is no gap - just a gaping abyss.
    In few fields of human discourse is there a greater gap between the truth and popular belief (active­
ly propagated by the distorted explanations of official bodies) than in the field of ufology.
      Fortunately, nearly every epoch making discovery in the realm of pure science, brings forth a refres­
 hing rumble at the foundations of traditional scientific dogma. The recent spate of signals emanating
 from outer space even made the astonomical and cosmological community invoke that terribly unscientific
 bogey- L.G.M. (Little Green Men). In these rare and lofty moments of utter disbelief, when the orthodox
 savant is confronted by the unbelievable, it may well be a fruitful ex e rcise to reflectArthur Clarke's
Third Law, which says, "Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic."
     Those who are prepared to spend their free time searching for the fruit of the tree of knowledge will
 reap a rich harvest, thus bridging the credibility gap. Alas, most of us are too pre-occupied with mun­
 dane things to be able to explore abstruse fields. But there already exist a number of abstruse mathemat­
 ical theories which hint thatEinstein 1 s speed limit may one day be surmounted and incredible undreamed of
 shortcuts discovered for getting from here to there in the universe.
    In our field of interest we have two groups of "exponents of the truth," those who know, because they
were at the right place at the right time, and saw for themselves; and those who know (as a result of tbeF
researches) that what the first group saw, could be true.
     It should be our aim to bring the two groups together, and to convince those who saw, that it is sci­
 entifically possible that they saw something out of this world.    And secondly, to persuade those who
 have the knowledge to understand and interpret the sightings, to give them the credibility they deserve.
