October 12: Rocket firings in Australia

”ROCKET WEAPONS TEST FIRED IN AUSTRALIA. London the 12th, from United Press. Ten prominent weapons experts directed by General C.Evetts have been appointed to test secret British rocket weapons on the world´s longest testing range ”which stretches all across Australia and out over the Pacific”, reports the British Ministry of Supply. Among Evetts co-workers are radar expert A.P.Rowe and several prominent scientists. Australian experts will also take part in the experiments.” (Helsingborgs Dagblad, October 13).
