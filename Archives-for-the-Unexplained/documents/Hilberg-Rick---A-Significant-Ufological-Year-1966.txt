    This booklet ends our series of reports on the major UFO flaps of the 1 960s, having previously
covered 1 964 and 1 965 some years ago. Of the three flap years 1 966 probably stands out in most
people's minds simply because of the massive amount of media coverage that it generated. 1 966
could be described as the year that went from swamp gas to Condon, although that would be ignoring
the large numbers of really good cases that predated and followed those two memorable benchmarks
that year. And while the media seemed to focus in on the Michigan cases from March, many
important high strangeness incidents from other parts of the eastern and Midwestern states (as well
as in other parts of the country in less�r numbers) that year only g ot coverage in local and regional
newspapers. I ncluding these cases shows just how 1 966 was indeed a significant year for the UFO
phenomenon.
    As has been our custom , we are running these cases as they were originally printed at the time
with only minimal editing. In so doing we believe that we gain a more accurate rendering, as
subsequent retelli ngs tend to subtly alter many of the basic elements of the cases themselves. We
have also eliminated obvious I FOs as much as possible, as well as cases that could very well be
hoaxes or tall tales. We have also included stories that g ive some insight into the way the UFO
phenomenon was generally perceived in those times now so long past.
    Space limitations forced us to select those items that we felt were significant to an understanding
of just what was going on UFO-wise in 1 966. We feel, however, that this report g ives an accurate
picture of just what was indeed happening , and take full responsibility for our choices.
    Lastly, we would like to dedicate this publication to Gene Duplantier, an important UFO pioneer.
