                                First Quarter 2001, January-March


       Office Activity
       Office activity remains at record low levels. One radio interview request was received
       and accommodated.

       Press Newsletter
       The UFO Report has been published and distributed to the press for almost a year. The
       first correspondence with a news outlet was received in February 2001, when the Asahi,
       Japan, Shimbun asked to be added to the mailing list. Most of the material in the press
       newsletter has been background material because no real news or major sightings has
       occurred during this year.


       National Aviation Reporting Center on Anomalous Phenomena
       (NARCAP)
       NARCAP is contemplating promotion of the view that aircraft encounters with UFOs
       could be dangerous to aircraft passengers or crew if instruments were lost or if sudden
       maneuvers were taken during an encounter. web site: www.narcap.org

       Fund Raising
       Forms for purchasing various books will be sent with the next quarterly report.

       Lack of interest by Joe Firmage in underwriting ufological initiatives means money will
       have to..be raised the old-fashioned way-individual contributions and document sales.

       News Item
       The Canadian government has declassified and released UFO reports from the 1995-1999
       time period. In one report from the Yukon area in 1997, a high-speed triangular object
       was seen to make an instantaneous turn.

       Press reports on an alleged UFO that shut down a Russian airport have not stimulated
       widespread interest although both UPI and AFP released the story.

      New Proposals
      Ramey memo.--Robert Swiatek suggested taking a high resolution copy of the Ramey
      memo to a commercial outfit to see what resources it could apply to J!lake the document
      readable.

      Richard Hall has been in communication with a man who has collected an extensive
      number of reports from individuals manning forest fire towers. The reports span a
      number of years. This could make an important contribution to the field as these reports
      are untapped by researchers. Inquires are being made as to the possibility of publishing
      this collection.

      Publications
      The Coalition (CUFOS, MUFON, FUFOR) publication The UFO Evidence II is in
      bookstores and has received a number of very favorable reviews.

      The project to scan the NICAP monthly UFO Investigator onto CD-ROM continues to be
      delayed due to scanning and cleaning problems.

      Fund Web Site Modernization Program
      A number of new pages have been proposed by Paul Nahay. A shopping cart feature has
      been suggested as well as a page to provide details of recent grants. Scanning past issues
      of the press newsletter The UFO Report was also discussed. Also under consideration
      are: guest essays; UFO news; "Hall of :Fame" classic cases; and sighting/abduction
      report forms .
