That bible of "funny goings on", the Fortean Times, has come out with an interesting remark in
its current edition, No 77. Of all the strange phenomena reported - only crop circles and the
current bout of religious hysteria or "rolling in the aisles" can be witnessed by one and all. We
know these things are happening, but everything else is more or less subject to anecdotal
evidence or dodgy photographs. Perhaps there is a link between the religious thing and the
very large number of crop circles seen during 1994?


Having said that, what evidence can we provide to support the evidence of UFOs being some
kind of spacecraft of a physical structure with equally solid occupants? An ample opportunity
arose this year with the claimed UFO crash that took place in the north of England, possibly in
November 1993. Data Research has tried to establish some facts relating to this case, but as
usual not a jot of firm evidence has come out. A rather interesting compilation of what we can
gather is set out inside.    It has the contents of almost every UFO fantasy, with animal
mutilations, "men-in-black", time losses etc, etc. It all sounds too "cooked" to be the real thing,
but convince me otherwise.


Following on this, we have obtained full details of the USAF investigation into the Roswell
Crash of July 1947. It was forced into the open under pressure from New Mexico congressman,
Steven H. Schiff. Parts of the highly predictable report are set out for you inside - sorry its too
long to include it all. Reaction from the UFO community is equally predictable - "whitewash n I
"cover-up" etc. If anything tangible is ever to develop from Roswell, it may need a totally new
approach with entirely neutral investigators not tied to government or armed forces, and not
wishing to make a fat profit from yet another book on the subject!


Just in case there are no true facts in this magazine, I have put together some material on the V2
rocket crashes which have their rather infamous 50th anniversary, this very Autumn.            The
German weapon of mass destruction was kept top secret for 2 months - it had to be as who
could possibly tell the public that something there was no defence to might fall on their homes
at any time of day or night without any warning whatsoever!       This is the first true case of an
invasion from space, and should be compared to similar claims of government cover-ups in
more recent years.


The long awaited book on British abductions, "Without Consent" by Philip Mantle and Carl
Nagaitis has now been published.        Material from Contact lnt. files is included so do try to
obtain a copy.   What is surprising is that the British abductions total only about 40 cases
compared tu many thousands in the USA. This is certainly outside the population ratio of about
1 to 5, so does British reserve prevent more cases emerging, or is it hogwash anyway?          The
answer might lie in the fact that Americans consider psychiatric consultations as a normal part
of life, whereas the British feel embarrassed about these things. I notice that Awareness has not
included much abduction material for some time, so if any reader is holding back an interesting
experience, we need to hear about it.


Finally we have some very exciting news about energy life-forms appearing on photographs.
These are not visible to the human eye and have only been recorded at dusk on dull days over a
field in the west of England. As well as circles which have been reported before, there are now
SQUARES, HEXAGONS and OVOIDS! The latest news is reported in side.
