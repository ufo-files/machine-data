                      WHO AND WHAT ARE SAUCERITES?
                              By Orfeo Angelucci

        Saucerites are a brand new lot of people who have shown up since June 24,
1947. A saucerite, naturally enough, is one who believes that flying saucers are
objects that originate not on Earth, but from some world out in the Cosmos. Be ..
lievers and adherents include all types of people and schools of thought, to a more
or less degree, and in all walks of life, from the beggar to royalty.

        It would be difficult to standardize saucerites. Your next door neighbor
may be a saucerite, though you never suspected it. A member of a family may be
a dedicated saucerite, although the remainder of the members rejects the idea of
space visitors, as well as space travel. A minister or a school teacher may be a
secret worker for the case of saucerdom. You can know only by the beam on their
faces at the slightest mention of the subjecto

        If we cannot tell real saucerites by their behavior and there is no set cri-
terion by which we can recognize them, there must be another way we can pick
them out. First, we eliminate those who normally do things that saucerites seldom
or never do. In other words, if we can't know them by what taey do, we can know
them by what they donlt usually do. A good portion of the population is automatic-
ally removed by this process of elimination. Most people are governed by what we
call normal behavior, and as we look about us and find no more people to set aside,
what we have left over would most likely be saucerites. We are not discussing a
particular type of saucerite, but just the average .. the majority of them. Let us
see if we can find them by moving aside those who do not fall in that category.

        When you find people taking up most of a day, every week during the months
of a baseball season, arguing about who won how many games or points, rest as-
sured they are not saucerites. So we set thousands of these aside -perhaps a
couple of million.

       A great number could be filtered also from football and other professional
sports or games. The crowds we see at fights, wrestling matches, and other
sports of dubious value can be almost totally set aside.

        Take all those who work inside the terror chambers of the slaughter houses
and all those who would volunteer to be executioners in penal institutions and set
these aside also 0 There will scarcely be one sauce rite in these rankso To these
add a few thousand incorrigible criminals, thus swelling the ranks of the non-
sauceriteso
        We will not yet easily find our saucerites because we still have a large
mixed population remaining to be filtered. Although saucerites have been called
screwballs and crackpots, we rarely find them in mental institutions, for these
are filled with the truly unfortunate ones with delusions which cover nearly all the
facets of human life. Rarely, however, do they have illusions about space and

                       SPACECRAFT CONVENTION

        After a hectic twelve hours on the road from Phoenix to Giant Rock Airport,
we arrived at the 6th Annual Convention .sponsored by George Van Tassel with

As we approached our destination we were rewarded by seeing a bright object blaz-
ing a sky path in the direction of Giant Rock•.

unive1·sal energies of creation. It would appear that each of the contactees has been

May 13.,   8:05 P. Mo Going south on 16th Street at about Bethany Home Road, Jo-
                      anne Lacy saw two bright lights in the eastern sky, Thinking
                      they were airplane landing lights because they were close to--
                      gether and because she was driving and couldn~t watch too
                      closely, she paid little attention, just glancing in that direc-
                      tion several times, seeing only one light that had grown
                      fainter, and then nothingo To her surprise, upon reaching
                      home her parents had been on Camelback Road at 8:05andhad
                      seen a very bright light near Saturn, which was on the hori-
                      zon. They said it looked like it had points coming out from it
                      and moved away at a terrific speed. Kno.ving the exact time
                      in both cases, they assumed they had seen .the same space-
                      craft_ Joanne then recalled that there had been no flashing
                      lights such as on airplanes so there must have been more
                      than one craft.
May 15.    6:00P.M.    Ruth Hallas was driving north on Third Avenue on the way to

                       round silver disc traveling northwest., Because of the traffic
                       she was unable to pull off to the side to watch it more care-
                       fully, but she described it as being as 11 round as a dollar 11
                       and a beautiful sight to behold.

chipped or splintered windshields~ Even the extra thick windshields of buses were

                                 LETTERS TO THE EDITOR

    Dear Mrs. Miller:

            I have read your Spacecrafter booklets with, I must say, a great deal of in-
    teresto I have also read with great interest as many books about Flying Sauce.rs as
    I have been able to get my hands on. To say the least, the material gathered from
    these books is astoWlding, The evidence surely seems to indicate that Flying Sau-
    cers do exist and that they originate from a source other than our planet Earth,

            However, as you must be very well aware, it is extremely difficult to
    change the beliefs and opinions of most people especially when our own government
    denies the existence of these Flying Saucers and labels anyone who has anything to
    do with them crack pots,
            There is a way though to present evidence that would be almost impossible
    to disproveo Eventually our government will have succeeded in sending a rocket
    aroWld the moon and sending back a picture of the other side. J£ one of those who
    claim to have been in a Flying Saucer could produce a photograph or sketch of the
    other side of the moon and publish it before our government does, it would prove
    beyond a shadow of a doubt the existence of a craft that can only have been that
    which is called a Flying Saucer,
           At that time there would be no more use of our government denying them
    and I'm sure they will then release any information they may have on fileo
           It will also be the final proof most people will need to convince them that
    Flying Saucers are fact and not fiction.
           More people will then watch the skies and not fear reporting sightings,
    This way eventually the complete riddle will be solved.

                                                 Yours truly,
                                                   John Mannoia
