elimination of a "power base" of this third party.      The
negotiation involved a great deal of money (not further

an object, but more likely a "power" or "effectiveness" of

out person not present to Person A."

"degree of power" which was to be maintained by the
unidentified third party. Many "give and take proposals" (not

meeting ended with person A attempting to reason with Person
