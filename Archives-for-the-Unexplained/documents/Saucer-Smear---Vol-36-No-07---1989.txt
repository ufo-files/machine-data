       Every religiously-oriented movement must have its gods as well as
its "devils", and the UFO movement is no exception. For most hard-core
UFO believers, the "devils" are assembled under the banner of CSI CO P -
the Committee for the Scientific Investigation of Claims of the Paranor­
mal. Phil Klass, as head of CSI COP's (anti) UFO Subcommittee, and James
Randi, head of the (anti) Psychic Subcommittee, naturally receive more neg­
ative attention from Believers than any of the other members.
      Now, as stated in our last issue, CSI COP is a co-defendant in a
$18,000,000 lawsuit filed by a friend of Uri Geller named Dr. Eldon Byrd.
Through our own Secret Sources, "Smear" has obtained a copy of the lawsuit
papers, which were recently filed in the U.S. District Court for the Dis­
trict of Maryland, where Byrd lives.
      The background of this suit is that several years ago, many members
of the psychic community, so to speak, received by mail anonymously     a
"blackmail packet" about Randi, consisting of an audio ta·pe of Randi's
phone conversations with young men, plus photos, etc., purporting to show
that Randi is a pederast. Obviously, these were sent by one or more of
the many psychics, psychic healers, etc. that Randi has "exposed" over the
years.
       Randi eventually learned to his own satisfaction that his arch-enemy
Uri Geller, along with Dr. Eldon Byrd, were the culprits, and said so ih
an interview for "Twilight Zone" Magazine last year.   Randi's exact words,
as quoted in the lawsuit papers, were: "...Eldon Byrd was the one - along
with Geller - who launched a blackmail campaign against me and accused me
of being a child molester. Byrd is now in prison in Washington, D. C. -
for child molesting, and is going to be there for the next six years     "               .   •   .


      Later, in a public lecture in New York City, Randi stated, according
to the text of the lawsuit pa pers, that "Eldon Byrd had been sought after
by the Washington area police for ten years, and was known as the 'shop­
ping market molester'� In the same lecture Randi further indicated that
Byrd "would utilize his wife's children to attract .other small children
into his car, after which he would molest the children and free them." He
further indicated that "Byrd had plead guilty to the charge of child mo­
lestation and is presently out on probation for same."
      Byrd, in a phone interview with your editor, denies that he has ever
made any moral charges against Randi. Randi, as well as CSI COP Chairman
Paul Kurtz, have declined to comment on the lawsuit for "Smear". Phil Klass
states that "Byrd will very much regret having taken this action", imply­
ing that there is some truth in Randi's charges.
       We understand that Uri Geller has also sued Randi, for a mere $5,000,-
000, although it is not clear whether or not CSICOP is named in Geller's
suit. Stay tuned!     .   .   •   .

program will attract other MUFON-oriented speakers, and there will also be
several speakers of various stripes from the Florida area. More details
later....

Phoenix, there is another flying saucer convention taking place this Fall.
