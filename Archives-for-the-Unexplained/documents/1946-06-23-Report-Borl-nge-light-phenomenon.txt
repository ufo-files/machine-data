Case xxx.
A light phenomenon near Borlänge

Time: Sunday, June 23 (Midsummer´s Day), 08.30
Place: Borlänge, county of Dalarna

”METEOR, OR WHAT? A person thinks that he on the Midsummer´s Day morning saw a meteor or a luminous ball which at great speed crossed the sky, coming from the Tuna area and disappearing behind the Hönsarvsbergen [mountains]. Our spectator first thought it was light reflexes from his window pane, so he opened his window. But the light phenomenon was still there and any illusion or similar he did not want to believe that it was. The time was 08.30 in the morning. Now the question is: Has anyone else in this area seen the phenomenon, and in that case, at what time?”
