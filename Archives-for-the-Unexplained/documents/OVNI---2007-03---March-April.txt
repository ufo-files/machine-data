                     A DISTURBING MEMORY REVEALED
                                           By Omar Fowler
There have been numerous UFO events in the past that have been observed by surprised witnesses, but
due to the fear of ridicule often associated with the subject, many of these incidents have gone
unreported.

A UFO report in a recent issue of the OVNI was read by such a witness, she recognized an incident
that had been similar to her own past experience many years ago. This had remained with her and
puzzled her to the present day.

Mrs 'CJ' subsequently contacted us and related the following story.

This incident took place in the northern town of Warrington (Lancashire) back in 1969.

It was a July evening when Mrs 'CJ' and a colleague were walking on their way to work at a nearby
hospital in the Warrington area. Their route took them through a large council estate of houses. At that
time, Mrs 'CJ' commented, she had "no interest whatsoever in UFO's".

It was about ten past nine in the evening and dusk was approaching, when suddenly a classic 'flying
saucer' suddenly appeared close to them, low over the nearby houses. "It was like an upturned saucer
with a ridge on top and was a gunmetal colour" said Mrs 'CJ'.

"It was low down, only about 500ft up and it had a series of red and orange lights around the top, like a
bridge on a ship", she added.

"I noticed the size of it, which was about the width of two houses . We watched it for about 10/20
seconds and it made no sound , then it rose up and faded away into the distance. "

Mrs 'CJ' and her friend were astounded by what they had seen but then continued on their way to work.
The next day, they anxiously. scanned the local paper for a news report of the incident, but there was
nothing !

"Other people must have seen it" said Mrs 'CJ', "I just can't understand why it wasn't reported in the
local paper ! "

Her reaction was typical of many other cases, when the witnesses were puzzled by the lack of response
from other members of the public that must have been in the area at the time.

This brings into question the mystery of the 'Oz Factor', (the name given to this strange occurrence by
UFO Researcher, Jenny Randles), where there is complete silence during the sighting and all movement
stops.

This unusual phenomenon has been reported on numerous occasions in the area of a UFO sighting. The
scene becomes quiet, suddenly there are no passing cars or signs of movement and the scene becomes
eerie and unreal. This unusual quiet lasts during the period of the sighting, then once the UFO departs,
suddenly traffic appears and the scene returns to normality.

Could the UFO's be capable of stopping 'time'?
                          .
I have a number of cases on record, where the traffic has disappeared, no sound is heard and in one
instance, pouring rain seemed to be suspended , only to continue after the UFO craft had departed !

At various times the UFO occupants have given us numerous messages, including the fact that "time is
of our own making".

There is also the often asked question as to why a craft doesn't "land on the White House lawn?"

We have also been told that. we on Earth are "warlike" (don't we know that! ). Who can blame the
'Ufonauts' for not wanting to land and be subject to a barrage of gunfire and missiles from the
'Presidents men'!

          Sighting over Market Harborough 24/02/07 11:45 pm
                                         By Mark Stalker

My family and I were travelling back from Leicester on the A6 near to Great Glen late on 24th
February (earlier tonight) when I noticed in the distance to the south west a bright light about 5 miles
away (we thought, possibly over Bruntingthorpe) that seemed to be an unusual colour - it was a
slightly orangey white but I thought it was a plane with its landing lights on heading towards us
probably heading towards Stoughton Airfield, which is nearby. There was a sort of beam that lit up the
low clouds above it. I drew the attention of my wife and son to it.

We watched it for a while and it seemed to ascend into the clouds and disappeared. Must have been a
plane we thought. Then back at home at about 1 1:45, we were taking our son to bed when out of the
upstairs landing window we saw two shimmering fiery orange objects drifting past above north of the
house heading in a ENE direction. They were low enough to be below the clouds I guess the cloud
base was about 3,000 feet as later a plane passed . They looked like balls of flame perhaps like a hot
air balloon that had caught fire.

We watched and I opened the window there was no sound at all. They were travelling at a steady
speed and maintained the same distance apart I would estimate the speed to be about the same as the
police helicopter that flies past occasionally at a similar height. I suppose about 100 knots or so. Then
(about 15 seconds) a little further behind, another identical object followed them in the same direction.
Still no sound. We watched as they went out of sight in the direction of the Langton villages.

Then again 2 more identical objects went past in exactly the same direction on the same course. Still
no sound. rushed to get my sons educational toy telescope but couldn't get an image. Other people
definitely saw this as they were walking past on their way home and were looking up.

About 15 seconds after the last object (time now probably about 1 1 :50pm), a commercial passenger jet
plane flew past heading due north (probably to East Midlands Airport) which was just below the cloud
base too. It seemed to have been at the same altitude as the objects.

I would be very surprised if the pilots didn't notice the last two objects as in aviation terms I would
have said it was a "near miss". I then went out for a dog walk at about 1 1:55 with my camera but no
more objects were seen, unfortunately.

This is a truthful and accurate account of what I saw. I would be interested to know if there are any
other reports or explanations as to the cause of the aforementioned events.

                  Comet-bound probe enjoys close encounter with Mars
                                                BY STEPBEN CLARK
                                                 SPACEFLIGHT NOW
                                                Posted: February 25, 2007




•




      Stunning image taken by the CIVA imaging instrument on Rosetta's Philae lander just four minutes
     before closest approach at a distance of some 620 miles from Mars. A portion of the spacecraft and
      one of its solar arrays are visible in nice detail. Beneath, an area close to the Syrtis region is visible on
     the planet's disk. Credits: CIVA I Philae I ESA Rosetta

     Europe's Rosetta comet probe shot past Mars early Sunday to line up for another swing by Earth later
     this year, putting the craft on course to reach its icy target in 2014

     Launched three years ago, Rosetta zoomed just 150 miles above the Martian surface at about 22,500
     miles per hour relative to the Red Planet. The probe made its closest approach at 0215 GMT Sunday
     (9: 15 p.m. EST Saturday) as it flew behind Mars and temporarily broke offcommunications with
     Earth Officials at the ESA Space Operations Center confirmed the speedy flyby was successful at
     0257 GMT (9:57 p.m. EST Saturday) as telemetry from Rosetta began to again feed into the control
     room. The Mars flyby created a slingshot-like effect for Rosetta, putting the spacecraft on a path to
     reach Earth on Nov. 13 for a similar maneuver.

     "We actually use this flyby to slow down the spacecraft to optimize the Earth gravity assist," said
     Gerhard Schwehm, Rosetta project scientist. Martian gravity was to have naturally slowed Rosetta by
     4,900 miles per hour relative to the Sun, according to a European Space Agency statement. Rosetta
     already completed a pass by Earth in 2005, and the craft will fly past Earth two more times later this
     year and in 2009 to set up for a rendezvous with comet 67PIChuryumov-Gerasimenko in early 2014.
