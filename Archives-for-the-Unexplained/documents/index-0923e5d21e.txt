  Our Synopsis

  The premiere issue of what is intended to be a semi-annual journal featuring the work of Hispanic ufologists. It includes
  5 articles and two columns, most of them short. Published as a photocopied report with cardstock cover.

  Publisher's Synopsis

  This 35 page forum hopes to provide full-length articles on UFO, paranormal and high strangeness events in Spain and
  Latin America, covering events both past and present. Issue One includes "A History of Flying Saucers" by Manuel
  Carballal, Salvador Freixedo 's discussions on the UFO phenomenon in China and Dr.Rafael A.Lara's regular feature,
  "Mexico Watch".


  From the Book Cover

  A twice-yearly publication showcasing the research and concerns of UFO and paranormal investigators in Spain, the
  Carribean, Central and South America.
