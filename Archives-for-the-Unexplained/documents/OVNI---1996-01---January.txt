         WELCOME TO THE NEW YEAR AND TO OUR NEW FORMAT

We wish our readers good luck in the coming year and with the newsletter now being
printed with the aid of a PC, we can promise you an improved publication in the coming
months (i.e. as your Editor finds his way around the PC!)


The new cover has been designed by PRA member Garry Stapleton and is intended to
show the varied interests of our association.        We are active in a variety of subjects
although UFO' s have been our primary interest for some years.         One of our more recent
success stories was our involvement in Crop Circle research, which dates back to 1984.
Although the origin of many pictograms remain a mystery, we feel that we have the
natural Crop Circle phenomenon accounted for.

At the request of      Mr Shinichiro Namiki of J. S.P.S. (the Japanese Space Phenomena
Society) and Editor of the ''MU'' magazine,       we are arranging a tour of this years Crop
Circles for a number of visiting Japanese tourists. This is expected to take place about the
last week in June and will be undertaken with the kind help of Crop Circle photographer
and private pilot, Busty Taylor who lives in Andover, Hants.        The Japanese group have
also asked to visit a number of ancient sites, including Stonehenge.


In this issue we have contributions by a number of new members and two very interesting
photographs sent to us by Busty Taylor, who has recently returned from a lecture tour in
the USA.   The photographs were taken at the time of an incident in Brazil and they are in
brilliant colour.... as yet we are not in a position to reproduce colour in our newsletter, but
the photo's are nonetheless quite impressive, even in black & white!


We are also pleased to welcome contributions to the newsletter from Andy Emmerson
and Daryl Spence who have recently undertaken a new project "Seek To Discover'' in the
Mansfield area.   This involves the study of FT (Flying Triangle) behaviour in the area and
geometric analysis of features in local stone circles. Their work looks quite promising and
we wish them luck in their new project.


We regret that there will be no PRA meeting this month, this is due partly to the fact that
we are looking for a new venue.        If anyone knows of a local meeting place that might be
suitable, please contact the editor.

                CHRISTMAS EVE "ENCOUNTER" ON THE All (ESSEX)

    At 6.30pm on the evening of 24th December 1995, Mr Chris Lamb (35) and a friend were
    driving down the A12 in Essex when their attention was attracted by a group of             some
    fifteen to twenty lights   descending through the low cloud. At first they hovered and
    appeared to be stationary over on the left-hand side of the car, slowly turning in a
    clockwise direction.    The formation was described by Mr Lamb as being ''very big".
    Then the lights began to move in the same direction as the car ''travelling with us for
    about a minute". Then the flight formation passed over the top of the car and hovered
·   over a field on the right of the road.    Slowly it tilted downwards, exposing a slight dome­
    shaped top.     The lights then moved away and upwards into the low cloud. (see Mr
    Lamb's sketch on next page) .


    ''I had a feeling of shock and felt my skin tingle and my hair stand on end" said Mr Lamb,
    ''then I saw a large tear shaped bright light through a gap in the clouds about five miles
    away, much to my relief my passenger also witnessed this sighting". Mr Lamb also had a
    dog in the back of the car, but he was unable to confirm whether there had been any
    animal reaction at the time as his attention had been focussed on the group of lights
    overhead.


    Mr Lamb later telephoned two police stations and the local newspaper, enquiring                if
    anyone else had reported seeing any lights, but much to his disappointment there were no
    other sightings. However he did mention that two weeks previously a similar sighting had
    been reported in the ''Braintree & Witham Times".        An elderly couple had seen a similar
    group of lights from their country home and they had described it        ulike a flying gas ring
    in the sky. "

    What are we to make of these mysterious lights? UFO's come in all shapes and sizes and
    certainly a variety of light formations have been observed over many years.           We must
    consider whether the light mentioned in this article could have been some form of natural
    phenomena, but this seems unlikely as they appeared to be under some form of intelligent
    control.    The photograph taken by Brian Maguire on the South Downs showed a mass of
    pulsing light patterns in colours of red and pale yellow.      There was no sign of anything
    resembling a series of rotating lights.


    Could the witnesses have been observing not a craft, but some kind of intelligence in
    plasma form? The technology of the aliens is probably thousands of years ahead of us and
    performing feats which are beyond our comprehension.          Remember also that there is not
    just one alien intelligence visiting the Earth but more in the order of 15 + , some
    researchers have postulated as many as 50!        It is therefore possible that even in the field
    of alien intelligence, there could be the "nuts & bolts" Flying Saucers and          other alien
    craft but on the other hand there could be examples of visitations by a pure intelligence in
    plasma form.      If this is so, the likelihood of any communication interchange could be
    virtually impossible, something like us watching the behaviour of ants.       They may realise
    that we are watching them, but how could we talk to an ant?

                  ANOTHER MACKWORTB (DERBY) UFO INCIDENT

    On Monday      the 11th of December, at about 1.15pm, Mr Dennis Hardwick, the
    Mackworth correspondent for the Derby Express                      newspaper, received a telephone call
    from a local resident. The person was very excited and said that he had se�n a bright oval
    shaped object in the sky, NW of the Mackworth Estate.


    Dennis quickly located his camera and ran outside his house, looking up in the sky
    towards the NW   .    He saw two bright silvery circular objects travelling away from his
·   position and heading skywards "at a terrific speed''.                   The silvery objects, (the one on the
    right was larger than the one on the left) were heading skyward at high speed and quickly
    approaching the high thin strata cloud covering most of the sky. Then as they passed into
    and through the cloud layer, they appeared to punch a hole through the cloud, leaving two
    "doughnut" holes in the cloud layer.


    Mr Hardwick quickly raised his camera and took a photograph, it was too late to catch the
    silvery objects, but at least he managed to get a photograph of the holes in the cloud. (see
    photo: the hole effects have been darkened to show up more clearly).


    After the event, Mr Hardwick commented "I am not a person to over-react, but this event
    and the circumstances, I found hard to absorb."                      "One hesitates to discuss a subject
    regarding UFO's with your friends, they tend to think that your imagination has run wild.,
    but thanks to the camera I have proof"      Dennis continued, "one other person has rung
    me since the incident, which confirmed what I had seen, but she wished to remain
    anonymous for fear of being ridiculed."


    One of the unusual features of this incident was Dennis Harwick's comment that when the
    silvery balls disappeared they did not seem to fly away, but to disappear as though they
    were being viewed through a telescope the wrong way around!                              The fact that they
    "punched" holes through the cloud would suggest that they were objects with some
    substance and not light sources.

                  AMAZING UFO PHOTOGRAPHS FROM BRAZIL
                            (Courtesy: Busty Taylor)

The extraordinary photographs on the adjoining page, claim to show a UFO taking off
from behind a group of trees. They are believed to have been taken in Brazil in 1991. The
well known researcher Busty Taylor passed the photographs to us, he obtained them
during a recent lecture tour in the USA.


The photographs purport to show a massive orange & red UFO (sorry we can't print it in
colour), rising up from behind a group of trees alongside the highway. Apparently there
has been extensive UFO activity in the area and on this particular occasion the witness was
driving along the road when he saw the huge UFO hovering low over the ground.              He
stopped his car, grabbed his camera and jumped out of the vehicle. He took the first of
two photographs as the massive UFO was beginning to rise up from the ground (Photo 1)


The main body of the object is bright orange, with red area's showing up as dark patches
on these b/w photo's. The trees can be seen in front of the object, in silhouette, with their
reflection showing on the car bonnet (foreground). An "Annco" crash barrier situated on
the side of the road, can be seen running across the lower section of the photograph.


In the lower photograph (Photo 2) the UFO can be seen moving skyward.              Once again,
the main body is bright orange, with red glowing sections showing up as dark blotches. In
this shot, there is a dark oblong "window'' in the centre, with four small "lights" at each
corner (highlighted in white).


We know few other details about the photographs, apart from the fact that they were
taken in an area of Brazil where there has been extensive UFO activity since the 1970's.
We understand that the witness who took these two photographs, has also taken a video
film of a ''blinking" UFO and this has since been shown on the "60 minutes" TV show in
the USA.


Are the photographs genuine, who knows?           If they are and the reflections and the tree
silouhette suggests that they could be,        then they show a huge craft, something that
appears to be more like a mass of pulsating energy.       With such technology, obviously so
far more advanced than anything we can imagine,          the analysis of   the photographs and
what they represent is really beyond our comprehension.


Regrettably we have few details about the incident, but we understand that the
photographs have been subject to extensive scrutiny and are believed to be genuine.
Certainly if they are fakes, then the photographer has gone to extraordinary lengths to
produce such convincing prints.   The reflections on the car bonnet show the tree branches
and the red and yellow hue of the object, there is also the reflection reproduced along the
Armco barrier,   with the centre brilliantly illuminated in yellow fading out at each end of
the barrier.   Hopefully we will find out more about this incident, in which case a fuller
report will be printed in a future issue of the OVNI .
