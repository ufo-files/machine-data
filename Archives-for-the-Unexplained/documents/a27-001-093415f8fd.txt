                          On Contrails and a trip to the bookstore
   From: James R Graham
   Date: Thu, 27 Feb 1997 10:04:56 -0500 (EST)
   Subject: On Contrails and a trip to the bookstore
   Hi all,
   Last night (2/26), I went to the bookstore and saw a book called, "Silicon
   Embrace" by John Shirley (I could be way wrong on the author). The cover
   grabbed my attention as it was of the head of a grey, but with a model of
   the earth forming the bulbous head. Intrigued, I picked it up. The blurbs
   on the inside cover flaps mentioned Area51 and S-4, so I looked further.
   It turns out that this books is 'cyber-fiction', which I have come
   interpret as meaning very strange writing style with the characters
   accepting "way-out" technologies as commonplace. I skimmed it catching
   various terms such as desert base, military base, etc. I put it down when
   I read the sentence: "The alien bit the cigarette in half." Perhaps I did
   not give the book a chance, but at $30 it didn't deserve one.
   I then made my way over to the the magazine section and thumbed through
   some of the UFO magazines, my disgust growing with every page. What
   red-lined my disgust-o-meter was an article all about Area51 and S-4
   (among other 'secret' bases) that used as a source: "Robert Lazar, PhD."
   'Nuff said, eh?
   Onto another topic. A number of years ago I sent an email to
   Gl--,er...Psychospy concerning a possible Aurora sighting here in MA at
   Hanscom AFB. Ever since then I have been hyper-vigilant to contrails and
   lights in the sky. The night before last, I went out onto my back porch
   here in Somerville, MA (just outside Davis Sq.) and saw a very typical
   contrail directly above my house. In the 5 minutes I was out on the porch,
   the contrail had drifted due to high winds and had taken on the
   characteristic 'donuts-on-a-rope' look. I guess I only mention this 'cause
   if I had come out 5 minutes later, I would have been scrambling all over
   the house for camera and film all the while silently cursing my fiance for
   taking the video camera to her place. Also, I want this list to pick up a
   bit.
