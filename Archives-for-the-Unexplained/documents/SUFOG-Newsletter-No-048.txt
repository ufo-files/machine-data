Granny Mary Sparks UFO Alert    •   •   •   •




   Hampshire UFO watchers are on the alert following the
sighting of strange objects in the sky over Waltham Chase.
   An a UFO expert today confirmed that the mysterious objects
spotted by a Hampshire grandmother could well be UFOs.
   Mrs Mary Emmett and her friend,Wendy Collins,were doing
their early morning milk d elivery in the Waltham Chase area at
around 3am when they spotted the object.
   Said Mrs Emmett,who has six grandchildren & lives at Lower
Upham : 'It was a long object with three or four flashing lights
in the front.
   'It was moving very slowly and then seemed to stop.It then
came towards us again.You couldn't hear any noise.When we
turned off our car lights it shot off in another direction'
   Three mornings earlier the couple claim to have spotted
another object in the sky in the same area.
   'This time it was a big round object with just two lights
under a wing,' she said.
   Tony Dodd,director of investigations for UFO magazine,said :
'There has been quite a lot of activity reported from that
area for some time now.There is no doubt there is something
very strange happening there'.
