  Kiriat-Bialik 28.4.2003


  Mark Gelman reports, that at 21:00, he observed a blue object, moving from south to north-west, in a vertical course, for
  approximately 2 seconds. To his estimate, it was a big object.


  Modiin 30.4.2003


  Hanoch Nevo reports: “At 20:30 I saw an UFO flying towerds Jerusalem. In the beginning, I saw it above my house,
  and also my children saw it. It was in the size of a medium star. It flew to north-east direction in twice an airplane speed.
  Using 50x20 binoculars, it seems to be like a shiny light dot. It flew in airplane altitude. Its color was white-orange, and
  had a stable light. The observation lasted approximately 5 minutes.”


  Geha Road, Rishon-Le’Zion 16.5.2003


  It happened at the night of our last meeting (“The Israeli UFO’s research center”). Hanoch Nevo returned to his home at
  Modiin, and in the way back, he entered to a fuel station. It happened in Geha Road, in Ariel direction. “At 12:30, I saw
  3 white shining fixed lights. When I returned from Kefar-Saba direction, I couldn't see them anymore. The lights were
  located in an airplane flight height, in a linear formation and diagonal downwards”

  Yaniv, 18.5 years old from Rishon-Le’Zion reports, that starting at midnight, he observed 3 fixed linear lights in the
  sky. After 2 of them turned off, he succeeded to grab his video camera and videotape the last light, before it also turned
  off. He claims that the lights suddenly appeared in the sky. Yaniv was with 6 of his friends, who also saw the white-
  bluish lights. The right light, seemed like getting away from the others, and then returned back. The lights turned off in
  an order, beginning with the left one, then the middle one, then the one in the right. The lights were bigger then a star
  and observed for approximately 4 minutes. He filmed them, standing next to an electric pole, so that he could use it as a
  relative point.

  Ma’alot 15.6.2003


  In that morning, Shimon Rosenberg discovered new marks on his leg. He reports: “I don't know if there is a connection,
  but 8 days after the observation on Pentecost (15.6), in the morning – I went to the bathroom to take a bath. To my
  surprise, I noticed a sign on the back of my right leg, similar to the sign that appeared on my hand in October 2002 (a
  rectangular shape, but without the central dot).

  This time, I don't remember any unusual event during the night, and I didn't dream about anything.”

  In the photo taken a day after (16.6), we can see dots, creating a rectangular shape. The distance between the dots is
  approximately 1.5 cm. The base of the triangle is turned towards the fingers.

  “I didn't feel anything and it didn't bother me”, says Shimon, “The wounds were still seen on the 22.6, but it’s
  interesting, that the color of the skin inside the triangle area, seemed to become darker, comparing to the color of the
  normal skin.”.

  Kerem Maharal 26.6.2003


  Gil Vinshtein reports: I took part in a social event, took place in the west part of the Moshav (cooperative Israeli
  settlement- H.S.), with some friends. The area surrounding the place is mountainous.

  Approximately at 01:30, I stood with my friend Paniel, discussing about aliens.

  We could see a lot of vehicle lights in the mountain roads. Afterwarrds, when it became dark, we observed dots of light
  in the mountain area. Afterwards, a huge light beam appeared from the earth up to the sky. It moved from side to side
  for about half a minute. Then we noticed a red light in the sky, crossing the Moshav area, from south to north, for about
  half a minute.

  After half an hour, approximately at 02:00, I saw again a red dot of light, crossing the area, and this time really low. We
  called our friends, but until they come, it disappeared.

  Paniel, who lives In Moshav Tsruffa, reports: “We observed the mountain and saw there dots of red, blue or orange
  lights. Afterwards, a big, wide, spotlight beam came out from the mountain area, extending more and more, and moving
  from side to side. I saw also an orange dot of moving light”.

  The two witnessed added, that in the moment the light beam appeared, the chickens in the Moshav, started to crow.
  When the beam disappeared, they became quiet.

  Tel-Aviv 27.6.2003


  Bar, 13.5 years old, who his observations reported also in journal No. 35, reports: I woke up in a way I don't understand.
  I heard a voice “Hallo” strong and continuously. I want to emphasize, that I don't wake up at night even from a TV
  sound. In the beginning, I thought that my parrots are to blame, but this was different. I watched the window, and saw
  there a yellow-white light. Inside the house everything was dark. I looked again at the window, but the light
  disappeared.

  I had some previous experiences. When I was 8 years old, I walked with my father and brother at “Napoleon Mountain”
  garden in Ramat-Gan. I went next to a bush, and saw a part of a head with 2 shiny eyes. I called my father and brother,
  but they didn't respond and went on waking.

  A year and a half ago, together with my brother and a friend of my father, we saw in day light, a gray-back object,
  flying in the sky. It flew to the right and left, and had the shape of a saucer turning diagonally.

  2 months before the above observation, in March 2000, I saw something like a flying saucer. When I observed it
  through my binoculars, I felt like “I’m here and the aliens are inside this thing”

  This observation was surprising, so I didn't have my telescope with me.
  The following day, I was prepared from 20:30, with my telescope.

  (6.6).
  I must mention, that even though I was ready and “waited” to see it, I noticed it at the last minute (this time it flew from
  south-west to north-east).

  Ma’a lot 13.7.2003


  Shimon Rosenberg reports: I went out smoking at 21:00. As I do usually, I looked up to the sky. To my surprise, I saw
  an orange light, blinking, and moving slowly without noise. For a moment, I was astonished, because I never saw such a
  view above my house. Immediately, I asked to bring me my video camera. I started to film, but without any success,
  because of the automatic zoom. Afterwards, I succeed to see 3 lights, ordered in a rectangular shape, blinking together.
  All the view continued approximately 15-20 minutes. Because of my shouting, my neighbor came out. Today he is a
  pensioner, but in the fifties, he was a Russian air force man, and served on a strategic bomber. He knows about my
  interest in UFO’s and he is also is enthusiastic of my observations.
  Meanwhile, another neighbor arrived with his wife, and brought binoculars. All of us saw the object moving slowly and
  disappears quietly south-east direction.
  After 15 minutes, 2 jet planes appeared, exactly where we saw the object spinning and blinking. We could observe, for
  sure, that the jets were looking for something. They flew around and around, first in small circles and afterwards in
  bigger circles – but always at the same area.
  Here, I must mention, that before the arrival of the jets, a big orange light appeared moving fast, high in the sky, from
  south-east to north-west. I wasn't able to see it clearly in my telescope, but my neighbor with the binoculars said that it
  was something round. The jest cycled the area for about 15 minutes, and then left.
  The neighborhood became quiet and then my younger son shouted “dad, it’s behind you!”. When I looked in the other
  direction, north-west, I observed again the orange blinking light. At this moment I was able to videotape it successfully.
  In the video, we see 3 lights – white, orange, green – blinking constantly, and the whole object is turning or turning
  over. We can see in the video that occasionally, there are 2 lights, and occasionally there are 4 lights. Unfortunately, at
  22:00 the battery in my camera became empty.
  To my opinion, the object seemed to be thin and not too wide, because its rolling-over was immediate. I want to
  mention again, this object moved or flew slowly and for a short time hovered and then moved again.
  From my knowledge of aircrafts and different airplanes, there isn’t an airplane that flies so slowly without any noise. At
  these hours, the surrounding it is very quiet at my place, and any minimal sound is heard (also a humming sound) – but
  this object flew without any sound or noise at all.
  This object was, to my opinion, smaller then an airplane
