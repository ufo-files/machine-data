PA MUFON NEWSLETTER

April 2, 2008 Vol.1 No.4

PA MUFON would like to welcome all the new members. We have
had a 20% increase in our membership since January. We have
also added two new Field Investigators and one STAR Team
member. That brings our Field Investigator staff to 8. We also
have a number of members taking the course right now and we
hope to bring our FI staff to 15 by the years end.

We are still in need of Field Investigators in the western part of
the state, so if you live in that area and would like to become a
Field Investigator for MUFON email Chief Investigator S.
Witkowski at butch221@dejazzd.com or
mufon11555@dejazzd.com.


PA MUFON has investigated 21 cases since January and have 5
in progress now. As far as national standings go, we are still in
the top 10 in CMS . That standing is due to the fine group of Field
Investigators we have out there.

PA MUFON State Director John Ventre will be hosting a UFO-
Paranormal Conference, October 25 2008 at Westmoreland
Community College . See www.12-21-2012-a-prophecy.com or
email John at jventre1@comcast.net for more information.

PA MUFON and S.E.A.R.C.H will be hosting a public UFO
Awareness Day on May 17 2008 at the East Shore Library in
Harrisburg PA from 10AM to 5PM . For more information contact
C.I. S. Witkowski at butch221@dejazzd.com or
mufon11555@dejazzd.com.


PA MUFON would also like to thank Steve Groff of S.E.A.R.C.H for
his help in putting together our first public UFO Awareness Day.
Steve and his group are located in Harrisburg and cover
everything from the Paranormal to Bigfoot and UFO’s along with
many other strange happenings in our state. For information on
how to join their group contact sgroff@paonline.com


PA MUFON is still engaged in the investigations of the Kecksburg
and Carbondale crashes and anyone with information can email
mufon11528@epix.net or mufon11555@dejazzd.com
