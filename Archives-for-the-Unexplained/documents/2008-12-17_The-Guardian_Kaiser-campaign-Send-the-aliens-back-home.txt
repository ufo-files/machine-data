Kaiser campaign: Send the aliens back home!
This article is more than 16 years old
One-man angry mob Paul MacInnes campaigns to stop martians emigrating to earth


Paul MacInnes
Wed 17 Dec 2008 14.44 GMT
Share
We told music site guest editors Kaiser Chiefs that they could commission from a wide pool of Guardian writers, from Polly
Toynbee to Jonathan Steele. "Well, what about that bloke we just met?" they said. "He seemed alright." And so it came to
be that online entertainment editor and one-man angry mob Paul MacInnes was commissioned to write a spoof Daily Mail
column from 2038 on how, were aliens to land on earth tomorrow, they'd probably campaign to have them deported ...
Now, don't get me wrong, I like martians as much as the next man. Some of my best friends are little green men. But while we're
welcoming our far-flung friends through the front door, let's just be careful they're not nipping in over the garden wall and doing us
from behind.
Ever since that flying saucer first crash-landed into Noel Gallagher's Primrose Hill mansion destroying his home, possessions and
Beatles memorabilia (including his beloved Ringo wig), I've been eyeing up these visitors. Nice enough chaps, if you like your eyes
boggling and your ears shaped like traffic cones. But it's another part of their martian makeup that concerns me. Excuse my
Venusian, but have you seen what they're packing in their space pants?
Now I'm not normally one to be sizing up another fella's dangly bits (that incident in the toilets at the British press awards was an
accident, I was looking for my keyring). But even I can't help but notice that your average martian is carrying something a little
special down below. Forget Linford's lunchbox, this is the spaceman's suitcase – and it ain't stuffed full of spare socks neither.
Friends of Gary the Green Man might insist he only uses his magic hose to pull petrified pussies out of trees. But I think we all know
the saying: the bigger the green hose, the more grass it waters. So, for want of a better phrase, I think these guys should put a knot in
it. Keep it away from our lady folk. Because our lady folk don't like it (not that I've asked, but they're hardly going to want to curl up
with a six-foot tall, superbly muscular and verbally dextrous alien whose manhood is the size of East Anglia, are they?)
So here's what I want to say to Barry the Martian. Watch it. Because Big Mac is watching you (that's me, by the way) and if I catch
you anywhere near anything that smells nice and looks lovely in a dress I will, personally, put you on the first spaceship back to the
red planet, and you won't need your passport stamped because you'll have the imprint of my size seven and a halfs on the seat of
your spacesuit.
