   NEW CASTLE, N.Y.- The pub­
lic hearing was plodding along rou­
tinely at Town Hall one night last
month.
   "All of a sudden, a cop burst in
yellin g : 'The UFO's here! The
UFO's here!' " said Peter A. Bran­
denberg, 43, a real-estate develop­
er. "We went flying down the
stairs to see this thing, just staring
at it."
   On a night before that, William
A. Pollard was driving along Inter­
state 84 near Brewster.
   "My neighbors said they had
seen something," said Pollard, 29,
the manager of an automobile ser­
vice center. "I said, 'Yeah, yeah,
yeah.' I never believed in that stuff.
But off in a field I saw this gigantic
triangle with lights, about 30 feet
off the ground - hovering. Then it
turned off its lights and shot
straight up - straight up. That's
when I said, 'Whoa! Wait a minute
here.' "
   In northern Westchester County,
Dutchess and Putnam Counties, and
in western Connecticut, thousands
of residents this summer have re­
ported strange objects in the sky-
each usually in a V-shape or a
circle, about the size of a football
field, noiseless and outlined in
lights of white, red or green.
  AT NIGHT, the curious some­
times crowd the Taconic Parkway,
a prime site for viewing. A hot line
has been set up to field inquiries.
And in shopping centers and at
parties, the talk is of a secret
weapon or of close encounters.
  "If it's not a UFO," said Irene
Lunn of Mahopac, "I want to know
exactly what it is and what it's
doing around here. And I want it
stopped."
  Mrs. Lunn was among those who
reported the most recent sighting,
last Monday night. She was coming
home from the supermarket at 9
p.m. with her daughter, Erica, 5,
when, she said, "I saw it over a
pond on a nearby farm, high
enough to just clear the trees,
travelirig south.
   "It wasn't an airplane, it wasn't a
helicopter, it wasn't a hang glider,"
Mrs. Lunn went on. "There was no
sound at all, you could hear the
crickets."
   She described an object "about
three-quarters the size of my house,
with an L-shaped structure sus­
pended underneath it."
   State and local police are flooded
with calls every time the objects
are seen. Many of the sightings
have been reported by police offi­
cers on patrol.
   The state police say the "object"
is really five or six small planes
flying in tight formation as part of
a hoax.
   Some residents are not so sure.
Others say that if that really is the
case, authorities ought to put an
end to it.
