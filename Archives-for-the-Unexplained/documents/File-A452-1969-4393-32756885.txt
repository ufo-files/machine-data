                             UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECTS

                      Reports have been received from the Senior Police Officer
            at Rabaul of the sighting by three European women of an unidentified -
            flying object at approximately 5 am on 16th May, 1969.
            2.
                              -
                      Mrs. Patricia Stiller, wife of Police Sub-Inspector Stiller,
            and M.rs. Jennifer Aschoff were asleep in the latter's house in Bay
            Road, Rabaul on the 16th May. They were awakened by a child of the
            house at 5 am. They both saw, through the windows, a bright object in
            the sky.            t


            3.        The object was described as bright, six times more so than
            the evening star and of indeterminate shape. There was a strong white
            light emanating from it. The object appeared to be etationary at an
            altitude of several thousands of feet and south-easterly from Rabaul.
            During a period of half an hour the object receded slowly in a south-
            easterly direction and disappeared at about 5.30 am.
            4.        A further sighting was reported by Mrs. Tessie Keyes of
            Butuwim Hospital, Kokopo. The description given was identical, and
            the object was moving in an easterly direction, at approximately
            5 am.
            5.        Mrs. Keyes claims to have seen similar flying objects on
            two other occasions, at Christmas 1968, and on Monday 19th May, 1969.
            6.        The Meterological Officer at Rabaul states that there were
            no weather balloons released at the time in question, and can offer
            no suggestions as to the identity of the object sighted.
            7.        The Department of Civil Aviation has no record of aircraft
            movements in the area at that time.

                           Reported Sighting of Falling Object
                            •
                            Corporals J. Mcculloch and B. Blyth, R.A.E.,
                stationed at VANIMO have reported the sighting of an
                object in the sky. I quote from a written report given me
                by Cpl. McCulloch.
                2.           "The object was sighted at exactly 9.30 p.m. on
                Friday 6th December. It appeared as a bright, white light
                a little above the crest of the ranges" (to the South WestJ
                "and fell from sight behind the horizon. Although out of
                sight a bright glow remained and lasted for about 5 seconds.
                Soon after the glow disappeared a muffled impact was heard
                as though a heavy object had hit the ground at some distance."
                3.          "A compass bearing on the position••• was found to
                be 243 degrees" (Magnetic)" ••••• true within 2 degrees
                either side. The bearing was taken from the exact position
                from where the object was sighted."
