                 IN THE JOINT EXPERIMENT REPORT ON THE REALITY OF HUMAN PSYCHIC
                 FUNCTIONS THERE WERE RECORDS OF TESTS CARRIED OUT ON Z'S ABILITY TO
                 DO PSYCHIC WRITING. THE AUTHOR OF THIS ARTICLE TOOK PART IN MAKING
                 UP THE SAMPLES. THE TEST AT THAT TIHE WAS TO TAKE A BLANK SHEET OF
                 PAPER WHICH THE TESTER WOULD SIGN, ROLL IT UP INTO A TUBE AROUND A
                 BALL POINT PEN, AND FOLD BACK BOTH ENDS. THIS WOULD BE PLACED ON
                 THE TABLE IN FRONT OF THE PERSON BEING TESTED WITH THREE PEOPLE
                 WATCHING AT THE SAME TIME.      Z WAS ONLY ALLOWED TO PICK UP THE TUBE
                 AND SMELL IT.      THEN HE HAD TO PUT IT BACK ON THE DESK. HE WAS NOT
                 ALLOWED TO OPEN THE TUBE.        THEN Z WAS GIVEN A FOUNTAIN PEN AND
                 ANOTHER BLANK SHEET OF PAPER. AFTER Z HAD WRITTEN ON THE SECEOND
                 SHEET OF PAPER AND STATED THAT HE HAD WRITTEN THE SAME THING ON THE
                 PIECE OF PAPER ROLLED UP AROUND THE BALL POINT PEN, THE OBSERVERS
                 OPENED THE ROLLED PAPER.     IT WAS DISCOVERED THAT WHAT Z HAD WRITTEN
                 ON THE SECOND SHEET OF PAPER WITH A FOUNTAIN PEN WAS ALSO WRITTEN ON
                 THE SIGNED SHEET OF PAPER IN BALL POINT PEN. THE STYLE OF THE
                 CHARACTERS   WAS ALSO VERY SIHILAR.       TO DO FURTHER TESTING TO
                 DETERMINE IF Z'S PSYCHIC WRITING COULD BREAK THROUGH THE BARRIERS OF
                 SPACE THIS EXPERIMENT USED A SEALED ENVELOPE. THE RESULTS OF THIS
                 TEST ARE RECORDED.
