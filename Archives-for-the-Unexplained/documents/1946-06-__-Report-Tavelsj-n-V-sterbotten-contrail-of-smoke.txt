Case xxx.
Smokestreak over lake Tavelsjön

Time: One day in the spring of 1946
Place: Långviken, Tavelsjö (county of Västerbotten)

This is another case from the investigations in the Trehörningen/Tavelsjö area of Västerbotten:

”7. One day in the spring of 1946. ([Investigated] September 17, 1946. [by] F.Johansson). Yeoman Karl Oskar Karlsson, born on April 29, 1882, registered and living at Långviken, parish of Umeå, postal address Hissjö, telephone nr 21, Trehörningen: 

”One day in the spring of 1946 at about 12.30 hours Karlsson´s daughter-in-law, Mrs. Märta Karlsson of Långviken, came into Karlsson´s house and said that there was something ”strange” in the air. Karlsson hurried out in the company of his wife and then saw a smokestreak running over the heavens in direction SW to NE. In the NE the streak was very narrow and then it gradually grew broader towards the SW. At the time the sky was clear with sunshine and almost no clouds. The smokestreak was bright in colour and dispersed after about five minutes. Karlsson couldn´t see any object in the sky, neither did he hear any sound. On being questioned Karlsson stated that neither before nor after had he seen any similar phenomenon.”

There is no evidence that Märta Karlsson, the daughter-in-law, was interviewed.

Status: Probable aircraft contrail
