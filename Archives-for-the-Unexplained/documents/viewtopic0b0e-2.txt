  Hello everyone,

  I wasn’t certain how to begin this post, but here are the facts. I have been running this website for almost
  four years now, and I need a break. The UFO subject is certainly fascinating, but at the same time mentally
  taxing. Furthermore, I simply don’t have the time to maintain the website.

  Secondly, I think it’s time to stop searching for something which may have never been there in the first
  place. The truth is there is no ‘right’ answer, no ‘correct’ explanation – we simply don’t know. The
  Rendlesham forest incident will most likely never be solved; and I’m sure that some people would rather it
  remained that way.

  Thank you to all our forum members and contributors for your time and effort. I am grateful to have had
  correspondence with so many pleasant and interesting people.

  I plan to downsize the main website, leaving only necessary information, such as pictures, documents and
  audio recordings. My comments - unless absolutely necessary - will be removed. There’s no way I will let a
