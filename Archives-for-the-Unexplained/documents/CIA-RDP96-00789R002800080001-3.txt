     (U)   DATA SUMMARIES:
     (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS) Following are the perceived highlights
from each source. Tasking was: Access and describe target
vessel. Provide name, type, and location.
     - (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)      Source A:
       -- The target vessel is medium sized and light in color.
It is located south or south east of the United States. The
activity is narcotics smuggling and four to five men are involved
in this scenario. Two of these men are of interest in particular
and are suspected of illegal drug activity. This vessel will
travel in a northern direction in the future in an attempt to
make a connection.
     - (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)      Source   B:
       -- The target vessel is a multi-hulled ship i.e. catamaran
or trimaran. It is white and blue. It has a bizarre or uncommon
motor/engine/energy source.
       -- It is somehow related or otherwise associated with
either a produce grower or tree nursery owner west of Miami,
Florida.
          The concept of sheets of plywood is somehow associated
with this target.
          The phonetics LAZY GIRL and/or GIRL'S ---- are
associated with the target. It's location is associated with the
term STINGRAY i.e. Stingray Cove or Cay or Bay or something like
that.
