                 "Boomerang" Sighting Near Delamar in 1983
     From: capman@cyberis.net
     Date: Thu, 10 Jul 1997 13:11:41 -0800
     Subject: "Boomerang" Sighting Near Delamar in 1983


  A friend of mine and I were driving down to the Ghost Town of Delamar in
  1983. You get there by going through Tonopah, Rachel and those places
  before it was called the Extraterrestrial Highway.
  Anyhow it was about 4:30 in the after noon when this huge shadow flew
  over us at a remarkably slow speed.It was about in my estimation no more
  than 100 feet above.It was shaped like a Boomarang, no markings, painted
  olive Green (almost black) and very large. We were about 20 miles on the
  west side of Crystal Springs when this occurred. We proceeded to Crystal
  Springs to gas up the rig where after we changed our underwear we asked
  the gas station attendant about this. He laughed as he saw that we were
  scared sh**less. He told us that what we saw was the Stealth Prototype
  aircraft practicing. I said yeah Right! Scared the living Crap out of us.
