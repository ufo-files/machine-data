Diary, compiling the activites at Vasara Swamp in the region of Gällivare, between August 12 - August 19, 1946.

Monday (August 12)
A workforce consisting of 12 men was set into motion.
Six men had arrived by train, another six by car. The cars were delayed due to three instances of flat tires. The road had to be repaired in order for the required materials to be delivered at the crash site. Scouting underway.

Tuesday (August 13)
A workforce consisting of 12 men was set into motion.
These men remained working during 07:45 to 18:30 o’clock.
A raft, 12 meters in size, was constructed.
The apparatus was assembled.
A thorough search was underway.

Wednesday (August 14)
A workforce consisting of 12 men was set into motion.
Operations were being performed  during 07:45 to 11:00 o’clock.
A search was conducted using prospecting equipment. One transmitter proved to be unusable due to its malfunctioning DDD11 tube (one cathode had suffered damage). DL 11 and DC 11 proved rather frail. Since there was no way of obtaining replacements at the site in question, these items were requisitioned via express from Boden at 13.00 o’clock. All activities had to be stopped at 11.00 o’clock. At around 17:00 o’clock, yet more problems arose when we were made aware that the required tubes weren’t available in Boden. Thus, a telegraphic request was sent to at Boliden 18.00 o’clock.

Thursday (August 15)
All activities were set on hold pending the delivery of radio tubes from Boliden. Heavy rain throughout the day.

Friday (August 16)
A workforce consisting of 12 men was set into motion.
They remained working during 07:45 to 18:45 o’clock.
The road was being repaired. A collection of tools was borrowed from SJ. A search was undertaken by using an ore management unit when the requested tubes had arrived from Boliden.

Saturday (August 17)
A workforce consisting of 12 men was set into motion.
They remained working during 7:45 to 17:00 o’clock.
Searches were conducted using prospecting equipment. Mining of materials underway.

Sunday (August 18)
Workforce: A Furir + 10 underlings.
Mining of materials and raft. Cleaning, loading, seven privates traveled to Boden via train at 12.00 o’clock.

Monday (August 19)
Workforce: A Furir + 3 privates.
Cleaning, submission of materials.

Boden, August 19, 1946.

   Karl-Gösta Bartoll
   Lieutenant.
