After a short break in the proceedings, Phyllis Budinger, of Frontier
Analysis, Ltd., gave a report on her independent chemical analysis
of six samples of soil from the Delphos site, three of which were
control samples taken from outside the affected area. All of these
samples were taken by Ted Phillips two months after the event oc­
curred. Her analysis was done in 1999. She states that earlier analy­
ses on the soil provided limited information regarding the molecu­
lar composition of the release, and of course lacked the use of devel­
opments in scientific procedures that have occurred since they were
done. She found the soil to contain high quantities of fulvic acids.
Also present is a high level of calcium oxalate, which she speculates
was deposited as free oxalic acid that combined with calcium in the
ground. Both oxalic acid and calcium oxalate (to a lesser extent)
would irritate the skin. She states that oxalic acid would not have
been readily available to a Kansas farmer in 1971. She further found
the soil still retains its hydrophobic properties after all these years.
