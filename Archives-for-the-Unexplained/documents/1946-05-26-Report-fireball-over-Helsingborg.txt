Once again a fireball over Hälsingborg

Time: Sunday, May 26, at 23.00 hours
Place: Hälsingborg (county of Malmöhus)

Aftontidningen (AT) of Stockholm mentioned a sighting from Helsingborg ”Sunday, 23.00 hours in Hälsingborg. A strong, luminous ”star” moved towards the north and was eventually engulfed by the clouds. Too strong a light for being the lanterns on an airplane.”
