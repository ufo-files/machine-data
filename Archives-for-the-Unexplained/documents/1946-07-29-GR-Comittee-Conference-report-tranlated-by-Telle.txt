July 29: Meeting of the committee

CONFERENCE REPORT 

Conference at the Defence Staff on July 29, 1946

Re: Guided missiles over Sweden

Attending:From KFFÖv B Jacobsson, chairmanFld Kjellson
Fld Norén
Fli Malmberg, keeper of the minutes
From FV/FSMajor Söderlindh
From FstÖv Kempff
Maj Ahlgren
From FOAAvd ch Fehrm
Lab Skärblom
From FRAByrådir Kempe
Dr Moberg


”1. Maj Ahlgren gave a briefing on observation reports received at Fst since July 22. Two overflights have been reported from the Stockholm area, one from Karlstad and one from Västerås.

”A report was received on July 22 from Vomsjön in Skåne concerning a tin-like material falling down from the sky and burning the ground. The matter will be investigated by Fo and possible material found will be sent to Fst.

”A report has been received regarding an alleged impact taking place a fortnight ago near a chalet in Dalarna. The matter will be investigated by Falu-Mora Fo.

”2. Reports have been received from FV ER concerning indications from F16 and F17.

”F16: July 21 at 08.51 pm incoming bearing 333 and outgoing bearing 337, distance 34 km - 31 km - 35 km, altitude 3800 m, echo strength 1-2.5-1. The object has thus turned around and returned in the same direction. No Swedish plane was in the area at the time.

”F17: July 24 at 08.55 pm, bearing 131, distance 29 km, no altitude given, speed of the object 480 km/h, echo strength 2, duration 45 sec.

”By inadvertence FV ER-indications were not forwarded to Fst. This will be handled in the future by Maj Söderlindh.

”3. Maj Ahlgren reported that the Cabinet Minister has been informed verbally on July 23 and so has the Foreign Office.

”A memo concerning debris found has been forwarded to the Cabinet Minister by Fst.

”Fst has asked for and received kr 10.000:- to cover immediate expenses.

”4. Engineer Malmberg gave a briefing regarding his visit to the alleged points of impact in Upper Norrland. A separate report has been made on this subject.

”Except for three observations as per report of July 22 one additional observation has been made at Lägenön in the archipelago of Kalix.

”The only place where an impact has been observed is at nr 1 Kölmjärv. The lake is being searched through manual sounding, 2-3 soundings/m2. So far 13.000 m2 have been searched without result. The sounding is continued and on July 29 also a missile-discoverer from KAFT together with an ore prospector from Boliden will be set in.

”If these measures do not succeed the only possibility would be to empty the lake by blowing up a sill of the dam. Air commodore Dahlgren, Ing 3, would not, however, recommend this measure as it would be both expensive and time consuming.

”The observations at 2 (Rödupp) and 3 (Kamlunge) may have been caused by tornados. The one at 4 (Lägenön) may have been caused by propeller waves behind a motor boat.

”Decisions:
”a) Fst will assign Colonel Dahlgren to make a cost estimate concerning the emptying of Kölmjärv or building a demarcation of the point of impact.

”b) No further investigations will be carried out at sites 2-4.


”5. Reader Skärblom gave a briefing of the investigations presently carried out on findings of debris.

”Some slaggy pieces have been received from Glanshammar (Örebro). They were found at a farmyard where they had burnt off the grass on the ground. They must have had a very high temperature as the sand has been vitryfiend.

”Analysis has revealed that the material mostly contains iron but also minor contents of tin have been found. This material does not derive from a meteorite. Analysis will be continued.

”The yellow material has proved to contain coal, hydrogen and oxygen and the auto-ignition temperature is relatively low. Reader Skärblom is of the opinion that the material has an inadequate structure which would be the result of a failed trial or some kind of waste product. It emits very small amounts of gas and leaves large amounts of slag residue.

”Debris received from Stöds, Rosersberg and Lunds are not believed to derive from a missile.

”Decisions: 
a) Engineer Malmberg will pay a visit to Glanshammar in order to get more information.

”b) Existing industries in the Sundsvall area will be contacted in order to find out whether the yellow material could be deriving from any of these companies. This mission will be carried out by FF.

”6. Senior administrative officer Kempe reported that a number of listening controls have been carried out during the last week. The indications can be referred to impulsing. Listening controls will be continued.

”7. If a missile should be found at Kölmjärv for example it will be left on the site and FF will be informed. FF will send a representative for inspection.

”8. All documents concerning this matter are classified by the Commander in Chief. All reports, inspection reports etc from any other authority in charge are to be classified as well.

”9. Fst has been in touch with a German lady who is staying in Sweden at present. During the war she lived in the neighbourhood of Peenemünde and her son worked at Peenemünde. An appointment with this lady has been arranged for July 29 at 04.00 pm. Fld Kjjellson and fli Malmberg will also attend this meeting.

”10. Next meeting will take place on Aug 5 at 02.00 pm without notice.

Stockholm July 28, 1946
Eric Malmberg
Engineer
