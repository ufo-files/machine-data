   Credibility = High: With the except of the MJ-12 papers, the government documents given here are unquestionably authentic. However, the
   author's commentary is much less credible.
   Visual Information = Very High: Documents reproduced in full-page format exactly as they appear.
   Silliness = High: Commentary draws conclusions not supported by the documents.
   Wow Factor = Surprising: Fascinating documents.

  A fascinating collection of government documents regarding UFOs, obtained through FOIA. The size and
  weight of a telephone book, this volume offers a sampler of formerly-classifed documents from the Air
  Force, FBI, NSA and various defense agencies. They report on sightings, studies and analyses during the
  period in which the government openly studied the UFO question. Included are portions of the final report
  on Project Twinkle, Air Intelligence Division Study #203, the July 1994 Weaver report on the Roswell
  Incident, and dozens of other interesting documents. The only weakness of this collection is the
  commentary by the author, which draws conspiratorial conclusions that do not seem to be supported by the
  documents themselves. There are also a lot of FOIA responses reproduced here that do not have much
  relevance. Fortunately, these diversions occupy less than a quarter of the book. The rest is authentic
  government paperwork (with the exception of the MJ-12 documents, whose origin is debatable). Well
  worth the price. -- Glenn Campbell
