Maryborough Chronicle (Qld. : 1947 - 1954), Tuesday 19 May 1953, page 1


         "SHOOTING MOON"

                        REPORTED
         MELBOURNE,                               May             18.—           A
       "shooting moon"                            was       reported
       over             Essendon                  airport,               Mel-
       bourne, to-day by                           a     civil        avia-
       tion     officer.
          He                  said         that    he
                                                            saw
                                                                     a bright

       object the              size         of     a     full         moon

       travelling                at         terrific                  speed
       across      the          sky         at     3     a.m.              The
                                     brilliantly                lit          for
       ground           was

       two      seconds,              he   said.
          A     "meteor-like                       object" was
       also       seen          over            Bayside   sub-
       urbs     between                3    and         4   a.m.           and
       strange          lights             in     thesky                 were

       reported                from             Bendigo,                     100
       miles      north           of        Melbourne.
          Later          this          morning                  in       day-
       light        pilots             and         officials                    at
       Essendon               said         that         they         saw             a

       stationary              small              silvery            object
       over             the      aerodrome.                           They
       heard       no         sound.
          The           Civil          Aviation                   Depart
       ment     says   that  there                          were                no

       aircraft    in the area   at                         the time.
