  Aussie mike's tall grey

    by AussieMike » Thu Apr 01, 2010 5:20 am

  As is customary i'll relate my own experiance.
  Ive always been interested in UFO's i got my hands on a copy of chariots of the gods when i was 5 in the early 70's and
  the rest is as they say history, ive never seen a UFO although ive seen some odd things.
  But one night i had the most profound experience of my life.
  I awoke in the small hours of the morning, not the drowsy slowly coming awake thats typical when i awake, but wide
  awake, whole body alert and tensed.
  something was moving around in the lounge at the end of the hallway, i could hear things being moved about and my
  first thought was burglar, but as my rottweiller dogs slept under the stairs it was clearly not possible, the next thing i am
  aware of is i cant move, with the exception of my eyes, so im there in bed unable to move, and i can hear something
  moving around in the lounge, my eyes flick from one corner of the room to the doorway and back over and over, each
  time i look at the doorway its empty, but my sense of dread builds, on about the 6th or 7th glance, its not empty
  anymore.
  There in the doorway is what can only be described as a tall grey about 6 or 7 feet tall, its a blue white colour and seems
  faintly illuminated, not so strong its lighting up the doorframe or anything, but more visable than it should be in the
  dark, a man standing there would have been a featurless shadow, this i could see details on clearly.
  Its arms were writhing like snakes, think the robot from lost in space doing the warning will robinson routine, describing
  these figure 8 like patterns, and it "slides" into the room like its on a conveyor, then without changing its facing it slides
  sideways up the side of the bed, the arms still waving around , at this point my fear peaked and i literally passed out in
  fright, my last thoughts to my eternal shame were "take my wife, not me"

  I woke hours later and shot out of bed like a scalded cat, and just stood there panting.
  i had no intention of mentioning this to workmates (i was a manager at a local hospital), but as soon as i walked in the
  door , people began asking me whats wrong, they were later to tell me i appeared to be in shock.

  The fear is something i will never forget, ive never felt anything like it, and ive been in life threatening situations before.
  It was as if i had been stripped of my humanity, reduced to an animal state

  No problem.
  Its been wisely said "I dont know" are the three most useful words we have when dealing with this stuff, and i wont
  suggest that what i experienced was an "Abduction", it could have been sleep paralysis.
  At the time (before the internets) i had no one i could talk to about it, and so contacted a local UFO research group, i
  spoke with a guy called Doug Moffett here in Sydney, he offered regression hypnosis, but i declined.
  To be frank, i didnt want confirmation of an abduction scenario, not knowing gave me the safety valve of SP as a
  possibility. At the time every account i had read in books (again pre internets days) described greys as short , 3 or 4 feet
  tall, so it was easy to write off my experience as something other than the real deal, being as it was completley atypical
  in terms of its height.
  It wasnt until years later and the Internets, that i began to read accounts of taller types of grey beings.
  Thankfully i have had no repeat of the experience, its not one id care to have again, but there have been other odd
  things.
  In winter i put coats on my dogs (i live in the mountains west of Sydney and it gets cold at night in winter)
  One morning i came downstairs and found all 3 dogs divested of their jackets, but the ties were still done up.
  i have no idea how they could have come off, its just not possible unless they are untied.
  On another occasion i found a small black pebble from a collection of 3 pebbles that decorate a water feature in the back
