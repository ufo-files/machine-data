    Our Review                                                                                  Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  Participants in aircraft development projects like the Stealth fighter or military operations like Desert
  Storm always have a cloth patch to commemorate the event. The maligned workers at Groom Lake are
  denied this opportunity because their operation is supposed to be secret. As a service to them, we at the
  Area 51 Research Center have designed our own high-quality, 8-color cloth patch, the first of its kind to
  commemorate Area 51 and its crew. (A 3-inch version of the same patch is available for $5.00)
