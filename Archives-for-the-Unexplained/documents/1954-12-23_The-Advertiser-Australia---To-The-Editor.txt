        To The Editori                                                 P. X-. THOMAS.
                                                                          '



                                                                Burnside.
         Sir— Prom a news      item in
        The  Advertiser'   (20/12/54)
       I   learn   that    a    'flying
       saucer'   shot    across     the
       northern   suburbs of Mel
       bourne at 2.15 a.m. on Sun
       day (SA time), at a height
       of less than 2,500 ft., being
       observed over a track some
       five miles long.             \


            One          hour           later,       the
       same        saucer,         or     a       sister
       ship (if the               term        may     be
       allowed) was                investigating
       the sky above Adelaide, hov
       ering or moving at subsonic
       speeds.. At low speeds it
       would        emit     no         light,       and
       hence      would be invisible or
       at     least inconspicuous. As
       it departed it .accelerated
       rapidly, probably vertically,
       and in passing through the
       'sound barrier' it                        caused
       the familiar bang                         which
       is  often produced by mod
       ern   jet aircraft. This bang
       was   heard over a large area
       of the city, shaking     win
       dows        and     causing a             slight,
       but prolonged, tremor                        to be
       felt at Burnside.

            Here     areoptical illu
                             no
       sions        or    hallucina
                           mass
       tions..   These things exist
       and   if they are of terres
       trial origin the public    on
       whom          they
                     are   inflicted
       have a right  to  demand    a
       scientific     explanation.
       Lacking this, I can      only                       .'



       conclude that the 'saucers
       come  from outer space.
                         P. X-. THOMAS.
