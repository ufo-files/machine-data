At the beginning oft~ gyarter, Dr. Bruce Maccabee, Chairman of the Fund for UFO Research, appeared on the two national
TV shows which featured the Gulf Breeze UFO sightings: "Unsolved Mysteries" (NBC) on October 5, and "UFO Cover-
up? ... Live" (Seligman Productions) on October 14. The second show was produced live from a studio located one block from
the White House. Although the syndicated program was seen by an estimated 40 million viewers and elicited more than 75,000
phone calls, it went virtually unnoticed by the media, despite the astounding revelations made during the broadcast.

Our major effort during this quarter was to raise $16,000 for MJ-12 related research by Stanton Friedman. We contacted
several hundred people who have an interest in the UFO phenomenon. Although our goal had not been reached by the end
of December, we are happy to say that we raised $8,365, bringing the total amount committed to MJ -12 research to $10,310.69.
Whitley Strieber's Communion Foundation has promised us a matching grant of $5,000. Thus by the first Quarter of 1989,
we will be well on the way to completing our fundraisin8Efort. We plan to tell Stanton to begin as soon as we have raised
$14,000, under the assumption that we can raise the remainder over the several months of his investigation. (Contributors of
$50 or more will receive a copy of Stanton's report before it is released to the public. If you would like to make a contribution
to this vital area of research, please mark the appropriate space on the enclosed Reply Form.)

During this quarter, Budd Hopkins appealed for fmancial help in his abduction research. He has said that he has over a
thousand letters that await responses and that he has been getting 20 or more letters a day at times. At a bare minimum, he
needs clerical help. We approached one of our major sponsors who stated that he would contribute to this effort to help
Budd.

There are several other projects related to abduction research which have been submitted to the Fund. Dr. Richard Haines
has proposed creating an educational videotape for use by abduction researchers and clinical psychologists. Dr. Rima Laibow
has proposed an abduction "crisis center" with a hotline for people who are emotionally disturbed over their experiences.
Such a center would be staffed with specially trained people and run by a clinical psychologist or psychiatrist. Dr. Laibow
also has proposed a conference of clinical psychologists and psychiatrists to review the abduction phenomenon with an
emphasis on its connection with the Post Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) which has recently been identified as a
characteristic disorder of Vietnam veterans. These projects also require a considerable amount of funding (Dr. Haines~
project might require $20,000; a conference could cost several hundred thousand dollars). We hope to be able to raise enough
money this year to support at least some of these abduction research efforts.

The Executive Committee of the Fund is entertaining proposals for research into the areas of government secrecy and
abduction cases. If you_have a proposalfor_consider_ation, please request a grant proposal form in a letter stating your general
area of interest.

During this quarter, we initiated a project to obtain signatures from a large number of scientists who agree that UFO research
should be conducted. We hope this will be a joint project with the Center for UFO Studies and the Mutual UFO Network.
Fund Associate Howard Hoffman is coordinating the effort.

We are pleased to armounce several newly available papers by Dr. Maccabee. The Scientific Investigation of Unidentified.
Flying Objects presents the most exhaustive discussion ever published by Project Blue Book Special Report #14. Dr.
Maccabee's report was originally published in two parts by the Center for UFO Studies in the Joumal of Scientific Studies
(JUS), Vol. I and III. It is being offered by the Fund for the convenience of interested people who do not have access to the
JUSvolum s.

Another paper, The Fantastic Flight of JAL 1628, was published by CUFOS in the March/April issue of Intemational UFO
Reporter. It is the complete report on the Japan Air Lines sighting of November 17, 1986, which became public knowledge in
late December.
