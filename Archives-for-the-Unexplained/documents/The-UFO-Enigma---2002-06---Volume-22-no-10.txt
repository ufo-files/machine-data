                                            Forest Crawford's Presentation
                               UFO Study Group's Meeting- May 19, 2002


                                                by Clarence Barnes, Pres.



The thought provoking presentation Forest Crawford gave May 19, 2002 to the UFO Study Group of
Greater St. Louis was much appreciated. To set the mood Forest introduced his talk by playing an ethereal
melody on a flute he had made .. He then explained how he had become interested in UFOs after seeing
one up rather close when he was ten years old.


Right off, Mr. Crawford wanted us to know he was reared as a Catholic, but he had left its fundamental
principles when science helped to answer more of his questions than the church. Science then became
his personal religion rather than Catholism.


As Forest continued he introduced several TV clips to explain the different points that he wanted the
audience to understand. He went on to say how he now believed we all were immortal beings, that we
all have lived other Jives. (To support the foregoing, he advised the audience to read the books by
Robert Monroe)


Forest wanted us all to fully realize that most students of ufology were more interested in validating their
own personal beliefs than they were in actually searching for the truth. In supporting the foregoing and
other concepts, he directed us to read "The Holographic Universe" by Michael Talbert. He added, the
public doesn't really want to the truth about UFOs.


He wound up his speech by quoting from "The Truth Shall Set You Free" by David Icke. "Nothing or
anything can take the personal power that you and I have by being personal Souls."
