Case xxx.
Oblong, wingless object over Kroksjö

Time: at the end of May, or early June 1946, 14.00 hours
Place: Kroksjö (county of Västerbotten)

From the police investigations in the Trehörningen-Tavelsjö area in September 1946:



(Map of Trehörningen area)












Det finns en karta (bilaga 9) i Krigsarkivet med alla observationsplatser kring Trehörningen 1946 inritade samt flygriktningar. Borde tryckas av här eller i anslutning till någon av de sista observationerna under året i området.

”8. One day at the end of May or beginning of June 1946. ([Investigation] September 17, 1946 [by] F. Johansson.)  Shopkeeper Per Gustav Hubert Högberg, born May 9, 1905, registered and living at Kroksjö, parish of Umeå, postal address Kroksjö, Umeå, telephone nr 6, Kroksjöby: 

”One day at the end of May or early June 1946 at about 14.00 hours, when Högberg was in his shop in Kroksjö, he suddenly heard a ”roar”. He hurried out of the shop and then discovered a stream of smoke, extending in a straight line over the sky. He followed the smoke with his eyes and caught sight of an object, which, at very great speed, came from the west and went away towards east. The object passed almost straight over [at the zenith of] the place where Högberg stood, at an altitude estimated at 2.000 meters. It was round and oblong in shape and had no wings. According to Högbergs view the object was about as big as an ordinary airplane. The object was trailed by a dark stream of smoke, which later widened and rested in the sky for about half an hour. Over Kroksjö the streak lay completely straight, but to the east it seemed to turn to the south. Immediately after the discovery of the object Högberg called to his wife and called her attention to the object and the smokestream. The object had by then reached rather far to the east. At the time in question it was a beautiful weather with sunshine and a completely cloudless sky. Concerning the appearance of the object, see the enclosed sketch, enclosure 8, drawn by Högberg.

”On being questioned Högberg declared that he at no other time had seen a similar phenomenon. 

”Mrs. Conny Gunborg Högberg, born August 30, 1909, registered and living at Kroksjö, parish of Umeå, postal address Kroksjö, telephone nr 6, Kroksjöby. Mrs. Högberg is the wife of shop keeper Hubert Högberg in Kroksjö. She confirmed the information given by her husband. She further stated that she was indoors when her husband called to her. Before that she had heard that it was a ”roar” outside. As she came out, her husband pointed out the stream of smoke, and the object, to her. It had then gotten rather far to the east.”










”Sketch made by shop keeper Högberg of Kroksjö, parish of Umeå, and showing the shape of the object which he one day in the autumn of 1945 [sic] saw in the air over his shop in Kroksjö. Sketch was made on September 17, 1946.”

Another streak of smoke was seen over the same area on February 21, see case xxx, and on February 25 or 26, see case xxx. The note, on enclosure 8 with Mr. Höglunds sketch, that the sighting occured in the autumn of 1945 does not fit with the text of the report, above.

Status: Unidentified
