July 15: First committee meeting

”Conference proceedings nr 46-497. 
”Conference at the Air Administration on July 15, 1946.
”Re: projectiles over Sweden. 
”Attending:Air Administration
Col. B.Jacobsson
Air Director Kjellson
Air Engineer Kylberg
Defence Staff
Captain Stangenberg
Lieutenant Neckman
Defence Radio Institution
Division head Kempe
Defence Research Institute
Captain von Vegesack
Dr. H. Larsson

”1. Captain Stangenberg reported on reported find areas and materials found. Compilation of results so far not finished except for on map. Analysis will be perfomed and results sent out. 

”Projectiles mainly in two waves on July 1 and July 9 and seemingly between 14.00 and 15.00 hours. 

”Projectiles are mainly of four shapes: fireball, like a Thermos flask without wings, like a torpedo and shaped like a droplet. 

”Re. their trajectory this is either parallell to the earth´s surface or at an angle of 30-45 degrees. Explosion in the air has occured, whereafter 5 or 6 parts have been observed.

”The found parts look like slag (non-metallic materials). 

”The trajectories have been sketched on map, wherefrom it seems possible that two or three trajectories are evident, which intersect each other on [the islands of] Ösel-Dagö [Esthonian islands under Soviet control]. 

”2. The following was decided: 
1. Listening will immediately start through FRA and FOA (time 14.00-15.00). 
2. Echo search [radar finding] shall start through the Air Force. 
3. The Geiger instrument owned by FOA shall be put into order as soon as possible. 
4. The distance at which a projectile can be visible shall be determined in order to estimate the altitude on which the projectile has passed. (Air director Kjellson has in this connection contact with the Saltsjöbaden observatory.)

”3. Air Director Kjellson was appointed as the Air Force representative in this errand. 

”4. Next meeting at 14.00 hours on July 22. 

”Stockholm, July 15, 1946. Keeper of the minutes: H.Kjellson.”
