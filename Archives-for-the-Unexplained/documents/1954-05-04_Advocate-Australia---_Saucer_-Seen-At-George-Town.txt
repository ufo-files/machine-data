Advocate (Burnie, Tas. : 1890 - 1954), Tuesday 4 May 1954, page 5



            "Saucer" seen                          at
               George Town
            LAUNCESTON            -
                                       The     "flying
       saucer"   reported             over     Beauty
       Point         onFriday    was     also
       clearly seen     at George      Town,
       according    to  Mrs.  T.    Thomas,
       of     Paterson     Cres.
            Mrs.    Thomas      said         yesterday
        thc  object appeared     the   exact
       shape    of  the  "flying    saucer"
       sketched    recently by Mr. K. L.
       Littlejohn, of Perth.
            Mrs.    Thomas'      attention   was
        directed       to   the   object by      a
        loud      rumbling      noise, and    she
        saw     it clearly as it dived     away.
