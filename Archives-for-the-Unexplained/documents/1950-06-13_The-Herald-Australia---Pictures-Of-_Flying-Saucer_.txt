Herald (Melbourne, Vic. : 1861 - 1954), Tuesday 13 June 1950, page 2



           PICTURES
         OF   "FLYING
            SAUCER"
         Australian      Associated     Press
             NEW    YORK,      Monday.—
         Three     pictures purporting
         to show      a  "flying saucer"
         In action      were    published
         today by the         New'   York
         Journal-American,          which
         said that the photographs
         were    taken by a farmer      as

         a   "saucer"    zipped over his
         barn near      Dayton, Oregon.
           One   of the pictures depicted
       a    dome-like object in the sky.
       Another      gave   a  close-up    side
       view.     The    third   showed     the
       under    side of the object.
         The    pictures were     captioned:
       "Do     saucers     fly?     Farmer's
       photos answer       'Yes'."

       cers" were      real.

                           you
