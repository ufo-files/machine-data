   Subject: Railroad tracks at Jackass Flats--FYI
   In answer to someone's query about tracks at Jackass Flats, yes there
   are tracks there. They were used to move the rocket engine from the
   assembly-disassembly buildings out to the test pad. When I worked there
   in early to mid 60s, the project wasn't a ram jet, but a nuclear powered
   rocket engine using lhydrogen as the fuel. (The hydrogen was heated by
   the reactor, then run to the rocket engine.)
