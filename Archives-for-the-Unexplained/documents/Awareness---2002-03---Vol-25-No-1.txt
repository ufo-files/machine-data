                                       EDITORIAL


Rumblings of changes in government UFO policy have been emerging over the last few
months. Apparently the intelligence branch of the Ministry of Defence has been receiving
reports from the early 1 950s onwards and failed to draw any definite conclusions after 50
years of investigation. (Thi·s branch is apparently not the well known U FO desk in London
where such luminaries as Nick Pope were in charge over the years and will remain active
mainly for PR reasons). At least this revelation showed that more covert monitoring of
UFOs was actually going on at places such as Rudloe Manor, Wilts, which we exposed to
members over 1 5 years ago. The decision to scrap the intelligence activity looks to have led
to the release of some 50 year old files not previously available in the Public Record Office,
and newspapers commented on these early in the new year.

So there was a government cover-up all along! It seems fairly obvious to me that the UFO
subject would be taken seriously during the Cold War in case the Ruskies should be up to
something in the skies. (And no doubt vice-versa over in Russia.) Of course most of the
Russian threat was entirely bogus and the countless spies working for both sides must have
known all there was to know. None of the 50 years investigation seems to have revealed a
"threat to British airspace", but there is no doubt that objects of exceptional aerial
performance were seen by eye and on radar from what we see at the Public Record Office.
These UFOs "mind their own business" and clearly do not desire interaction with h umans
unless there is a connection to the "abduction" scenario. The purpose of civilian groups such
as ours is to show that the subject is still very much alive and of interest to searching minds
who are not looking for "threats" but "enlightenment". Only on December 27th last year a
classic black triangle was described by a witness just a few miles from Oxford, replicating the
description of dozens of other such objects over the past 1 0 years. As no hostile action has
ever been reported by any of these triangles, if that is what they are, then the MOD would
cease to be interested whereas CIUFOR and other groups remain extremely keen on the
nature of what this can be.

We now have a detailed report of the French COMETA study and the activities of the
"Disclosure Group" led by Dr Stephen G reer in the USA. (See p21 ), by journalist Leslie
Kean, which has been published in American, I rish and French newspapers. The French
"top brass" General putS over a convincing case for the existence of UFOs in a very
comprehensive study, but this is not much more than the flag-waving exercise similar to the
House of Lords U FO debate instigated by our founder, Lord Clancarty. A very impressive
array of names took part in the debate, but nothing more than speculation ever emerged. It
seems every 10 years or so "important" people pronounce U FOs are real which raises up the
profile of the subject and improves the status of the "Joe Public" members that report UFOs
who might otherwise be considered barmy by their fellows.

Let us imagine an incredibly hyped celebrity such as a footballer called Beckham, at the
present time, should declare his hobby was studying UFOs. As soon as he had finished
bending in his wonder free kicks for Manchester United on a Saturday afternoon he was off
to the Pennines to skywatch! The interest i n the subject would then rocket and thousands
would clamour to hear the great man should he ever become a conference speaker! But
would we be any closer to solving the UFO enigma as a result? Those minor celebrities who
have become interested in UFOs or related areas do attract a larger following than
impeccable researchers who have never been on television. For instance there is Reg Presley,
member of an ancient pop g roup called the "Troggs", and who skywatches all summer

conspiracy theories, which would probably never get printed but for his former TV persona.

To put it bluntly, from what I have learnt in my 24 year involvement with the UFO subject,

manage to find a hole into the kitchen. We will live our lives entirely separately without
ever seeing each other, or needing or wishing to interact. You could say the same applies to
the UFO enigma. What fleeting glimpses of UFOs have been recorded, demonstrate high
technology and, in military terms, a possible "threat". After the threat never materialises,
those secret governmental agencies studying the subject close down, until a rush of
heightened UFO activity causes a rethink. Although in 2002 this is now less likely, as the
USA are likely to be the only nation that has the resources and funds to produce advanced
technology that can match UFO manoeuvres and they should know what they are testing.
