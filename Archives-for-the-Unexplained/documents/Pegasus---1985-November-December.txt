                          SPANISH SCARE


Again as the result of the Bagshot UFO incident, another report
came to light. This time it was not in the local area, but took
place in Alicante, Spain in the late Spring of 1972.


Mr Alan Smith who was living in Spain at the time, recounted how
he was taking his two dogs for an evening walk down through the
Almond Groves when a UFO appeared from the West and heading N.E.
It was possible to see the brilliantly lit windows as it flew
silently by. One of his dogs pressed close to his leg as the UFO
disappeared out of sight behind a distant hill.


Mr Smith described its visual size as that of a golf ball and the
angle at first sight as 60° until it was lost from sight at 15°.
It appeared to have an elliptical shape, although the outline was
hazy and not clearly defined. The weather conditons were clear,
dry and the stars were visible.
