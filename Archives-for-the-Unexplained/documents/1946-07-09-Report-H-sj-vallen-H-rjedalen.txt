Forestry Board of Jämtland					14-1435
DISTRIKTSJÄGMÄSTAREN
Härjedalens forest management district

9/7

To the Military Staffs

Stockholm 90.

CLASSIFIED

I hereby respectfully offer additional information regarding the reported observations of a light phenomenon on Tuesday, July 9. Strange though it may seem, yet another, though remarkably similar observation was made during the same day, at Håsjövallen, Härjedalen.

The phenomenon was being observed between the hours of 2:00 p.m. to 2:30 p.m. in and can be described as a blue-white glowing sphere with slightly yellow casing, followed by a faint, blue streak, which revealed vague markings in the bright sunshine. It was moving in Northerly direction with an inclination to the horizon of about 60 degrees. In the very moment of its discovery, the object hovered just above the horizon (the visibility of the observers increased greatly due to the forest being remarkably sparse). As the unexplained object appeared to plunge in the adjacent vicinity, thorough searches were initiated, but none of these yielded any results.

Hede, July 12, 1946.
Sven Forsberg
