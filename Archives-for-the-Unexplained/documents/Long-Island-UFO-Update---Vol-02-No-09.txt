LIUFON can not state publicly that it contacted and talked to
the witnesses involved. The witnesses,have asked the paper not
to divulge their identities, therefore, LIUFON will respect
their wishes also.

Taxi company delivered her fare one Robert Tyler to his home
which is up the access road which cuts through the adjoining
Pindar Winery property and the farm in question. At that time
Mrs. cox report. seeing a formation of very bright white lights
above her taxi which bathed the vehicle in light and were also
