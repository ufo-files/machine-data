FALU-MORA
FÖRSVARSOMRÅDESSTAB

Department I c / L
No. 11
August 12, 1946.

To the air defense department.

1. At 9 o’clock today, a gardener by the name of E. H. Eriksson reported observations of a light phenomenon via phone.
Location of sighting: 2.9 kilometers southwest of Falun Railway Station.
Point in time: At 20:45, August 11, 1946.
Direction: Southwest to northeast.
Description: A fireball, which seemed to explode over agricultural land east of the Samuelsdal mansion (2.5 kilometers southwest of Falun Railway Station).
The credible observers had been traveling by car at the time. An examination of the crash site was to be carried out during the day by Eriksson himself, who's a deputy commander at Falu HVO.

2. Earlier today we received a report via phone that a forester by the name of E.O. Sundell had stood gazing upon a remarkable light phenomenon.
Location of sighting: At the rail end, 200 meters east-southeast of Stjärnsund.
Point in time: At 20:50 o'clock, August 11, 1946.
Direction: Southwest to northeast.
Description: A descending, smoke emitting fireball. Moving at great speed, it had only remained visible during a few seconds at an altitude of 300-400 meters.

3. At 09.15 o’clock today, two men, Officer Hedemora and farm owner Erik Danielsson, reported sightings of a light phenomenon via phone.
a) Location of sighting: Tinghusparken in Hedemora city (100 meters north Hedemora station).
Point in time: At 20:50 o'clock, August 11, 1946.
Direction: South-southeast to north-northwest.
Altitude: 200-300 meters.
Description: fireball with burning tail.
b) Location of sighting: Bergbacken (4.1 km north-northeast Hedemora station).
Time: At 20:50 o'clock, August 11, 1946.
Direction: South to north.
Description: A fireball, which exploded in Ufberget (6.5 kilometers north-northeast of Hedemora station) and split into four fiery "rings". The impact was estimated to have occurred about 150 meters east of the western slope of the mountain.

4. Officer Folkärna and sawmill owner Oskar Olsson has reported observations of a light phenomenon to us via phone.
Location of sighting: Approximately 500 meters north of the northernmost farm in Vestanberg (1.1 kilometers south Åsgarn).
Point in time: At around 21:00 o'clock, August 11, 1946.
Direction: South to north.
Altitude: Approximately 100 meters, steadily decreasing.
Description: Oval form. No sound.

5. Our phone conversation with Officer Säfanäs and trustee A. Hultin has resulted in information regarding an additional light phenomenon:
Location of sighting: 500 meters south of Fredriksberg.
Point in time: At 20:45 o'clock, August 11, 1946.
Direction: West to east (5.2 kilometers east of Frederiksberg station).
Altitude: 500-600 meters.
Description: A fusiform object shining bluish-white. There hadn’t been any smoke and no sound was heard.

6. Yet another report regarding a light phenomenon was communicated to us via telephone today by officer Bika and school teacher Nils Danielsson:
Location of sighting: 4 kilometers southeast of Vika church.
Point in time: At 20:47 o'clock, August 11, 1946.
Direction: West to east.
Description: Oval fireball, which shone with a bright white light during its flight.

7. At 11.30 o’clock today, Ms. Maja Lundgren supplied information on yet another light phenomenon, via phone.
Location of sighting: Bäsna, north of Borlänge.
Pont in time: At around 20:30 o'clock, August 11, 1946.
Direction: South to north.
Altitude: 50 meters.
Speed: Exceeding the capabilities of a manmade aircraft.
Other observations: Initially resembling an airplane, as it moved towards the observer it kind of looked like a strange, reddish fireball, which lacked a tail. Reportedly, it landed in the woods beyond Dalälven. We have received mentions of a slight humming sound, as well as a foul stench of sulfur (which in itself seems strange taking the strong winds into consideration). No object was found.

8. Earlier today, at 14:50 o’clock, an engineer by the name of John G. Carlsson contacted us via phone and supplied yet another report regarding a light phenomenon.
a) Location of sighting: Norsbro.
Point in time: At around 20:50 o'clock, August 11, 1946.
Direction: From southeast to northwest.
Description: A globular, brightly lit object.

b) Location of sighting: Persbo, 5.7 kilometers north of Norsbro.
Point in time: At around 20:50 o'clock, August 11, 1946.
Description: A bright object, flying at low altitude. It appeared to have crashed in the arable fields between the railway and the main road.

9. Earlier today, at 15:15 o’clock, an accountant named Herou contacted us via phone and revealed the following information regarding a supposed light phenomenon:
Location of sighting: the crossroads between the viaduct and railway in the neighborhood of Karlsvik, 3.2 kilometers west of Hinsnoret station.
Point in time: At around 20:50 o'clock, August 11, 1946.
Altitude: 40 meters.
Description: A sparkling fireball.
Additional details: It had been completely soundless.
