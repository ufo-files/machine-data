  consensus of December 26 - 28, 1980; however, there is a possibility that yet another UFO event occurred
  on December 29, 1980, referenced in the Ministry of Defence memo further below:

  MoD: “We are aware that on 27 and 29 December 1980, a number of personnel from RAF (Royal Air Force)
  Woodbridge saw strange lights in the vicinity of the base in Rendlesham forest. Lt. Col. Charles Halt USAF,
  the Deputy Base Commander, submitted a report on these events, which was passed to the Ministry of
  Defence.”
