PA MUFON NEWSLETTER
January 1 2008 Vol. 1 No. 1


Welcome to the first edition of the Pennsylvania MUFON
Newsletter.


Looking back at 2007 saw a number of changes in our group.
A new State Director, John Ventre, and a new State Section
Director, and new Field Investigators, not to mention approx 6
new investigators in training. We now have our own website
www.pamufon.com and are planning public UFO Awareness
days throughout Pennsylvania. We initiated opening the cold
cases of The Kecksburg and Carbondale Incidents. Field trips
to both sites were undertaken and they are only the first of
many to come in the future.


Some other key plans are Sky Watches and Mock training on
sightings and landing sites. We are in need of Field
Investigators in the Northeastern and Central PA areas. Anyone
interested in becoming a Field Investigator can contact
www.mufon.com or visit the website and contact a field
investigator listed.
There were 112 sightings in the skies over Pennsylvania in
2007 and 55 were investigated by PA MUFON with 11 new
cases in progress. The NUFORC Database shows Pennsylvania
with 1254 reported sightings from 6-47 to 11-07. We are one
of the top 11 states reporting the UFO phenomenon. California
being the highest reporting 5618 since 1937.


“ KEEP YOUR EYES TO THE SKIES “


NOTE: Our State Director John Ventre has published his first
book titled “ 12/21/2012 A Prophecy “ a combination of The
Day After Tomorrow and The Da Vinci Code......A must read.

John’s book is available on Amazon.com


We are looking forward to 2008 with the anticipation of new
investigators and educating the public on the importance of
reporting UFO activity in the skies over Pennsylvania. PA
MUFON members are going to be giving lectures to other PA
based groups in 2008 and will be attending as many
conferences and forums as possible.


We are also working to get a site for meetings centrally located
so attending will not involve a 2 or 3 hour drive. Maybe we can
look at a number of locations. The majority of our membership
is in the eastern and western parts of the state with few in the
central area. One idea was to have and eastern section
meeting and western section meeting....Any feedback would be
appreciated.


NOTE: To Investigators......If you are assigned a case and won’t
have the time, advise John Ventre and it can be assigned to
someone who may have the time. That is not a problem.


If any member has a group they belong to that would like a
presentation, contact State Section Director S. Witkowski at
butch221@dejazzd.com and arrangements will be made.


An upcoming must attend event for any Ufologist is the X-
Conference in Gaithersburg Maryland April 18-20. Lots of the
top researchers in the field will be there. Go to
www.paradigmresearchgroup.org


MUFON will also have a table at the Flatwoods Festival in West
Virginia in Sept. We will post dates a soon as received.
A PA MUFON Investigator will be giving a presentation at the
Spirit Society of PA, for their annual Spirit Day May 3 2008.

For info go to www.spiritsocietyofpa.com


And for those with an interest in the Paranormal another must
attend event is the 7th Annual Pennsylvania Paranormal
Conference on April 12 2008 at Bube’s Brewery in Mt Joy PA

For info go to www.paranormalpa.com


Thank you for your time and dedication:
