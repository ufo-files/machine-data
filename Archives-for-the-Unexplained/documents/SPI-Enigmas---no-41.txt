The purpose of Strange Phenomena Investigations as an organisation, is defined clearly by its
title: to investigate strange phenomena in its many categories. Such categories include reported
hauntings, i.e. apparitions, poltergeist events; UFO sightings and various forms of psychic
happenings.SPI was formed in 1979. Involvement in SPI is more than academic beyond the
appeal as a study. We are drawn to it because we share the mutual opinion that the subject
demands investigation and definite awareness on as wide a scale as possible, which will help
to establish a firmer foundation that: "There are such things".

The aims of SPI are collected, researched and published accounts relating to most aspects of
Strange Phenomena, and to inform the general public that there is, indeed, a case to be
answered.

SPI does not hold or express any strict viewpoint as to the nature of the events of which we
research; we keep an open mind and hope that by continued research we may come up with
some answers to account for what at present eludes us.
