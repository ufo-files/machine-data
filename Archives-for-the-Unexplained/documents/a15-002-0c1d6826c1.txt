  From: Katharina Wilson <kwilson@alienjigsaw.com>
  A response from Katharina Wilson concerning: campbell@ufomind.com (Glenn
  Campbell, Las Vegas) Subject: Desert Rat #38: "Project Preserve Destiny"
  <<Quotes>> have been taken directly from "THE DESERT RAT" - Issue #38.
  Nov. 7, 1997
  KW: I'd like to thank both Dan Sherman and Glenn Campbell. My comments
  below are not intended to sound overly skeptical. I am trying to ask
  logical questions about the information that was provided and compare it
  with what we already know. We need to be careful how we associate
  different types of information that appear to have much in common, but
  in the end, may not be related at all.
  <<Sherman's book, in contrast, seems quite original. It offers a new
  synthesis of popular themes, including alien abduction, genetic
  manipulation, government cover-up, alien-government collusion and the
  growth categories of remote viewing and psychokinesis. Regardless of its
  veracity, we expect this book to have a significant impact on the
  direction of the UFO movement....>>
  KW: With my knowledge of alien abductions - which is quite extensive, I
  read very little new information being offered by Sherman in that area
  of ufology. Abductees have been reporting *for years* that:
  (1) they have seen military personell working alongside the aliens
  (2) they have been shown and have been given information about genetic
  manipulation of themselves, other abductees and animals
  (3) they have seen evidence of psychokinesis; for example when the
  aliens levitate themselves with thought that produces movement; and
  placing their hands on the abductee to mask or remove their pain
  (4) they have described underground bases as well as military bases
  (5) One abductee whom I know quite well has even described being in a
  warehouse with boxes around them with the word "Langley" written on them
  [All of these have been documented in (the late) Dr. Karla Turner's book
  "Taken," and in my monograph "Project Open Mind" located on my web
  site.]

  <<He was told that he had a special "intuitive" ability that had been
  genetically engineered by aliens during an abduction of his mother in
  the early 1960s. (He expresses as much surprise as we do.)>>
  KW: Abductees (via their mothers) have been told, and have been telling
  others for years how they were altered while in the womb, taken out and
  placed back, etc. The idea of being altered and/or trained for a future
  mission - something else that abductees have been reporting for a number
  of years - is not new.
  <<He was told that this form of communication, under the name of
  "Project Preserve Destiny," was being developed because at some point in
  earth's future all electromagnetic communication would be disrupted, but
  he was given no details.>>
  KW: It is illogical that ICs are trained on computers and depend on
  those
  computers to communicate with the alleged aliens. When the power goes
  down - so will computers. Does this make sense? - No. If an IC has
  always used a computer to communicate with aliens, chances are that he
  will not be so good at it when EM comms are disrupted.
  <<Now, Sherman is giving us a reason for that manipulation: The aliens
  are genetically preparing our offspring for psychic communication later
  in life. Soon, we predict, we'll be hearing a lot more about this in
  abduction circles.>>
  KW: Again - there is nothing new here. We have ALREADY been hearing
  about this from abductees for a number of years. Abductees have reported
  time and time again that they have been in contact with some type of
  alien being since birth; that they have been commnicating with them off
  and on throughout their lives; that they are being PREPARED for a big
  event in the future - some believe it will be Worldwide earth changes,
  others
  believe it will be a World war.
  <<The UFO subculture seems ready for this kind of claim right now.>>
  KW: What exactly is a UFO "subculture"?
  How much more of a "subculture" can one in be when they are emersed in
  ufology?
  KW: Serious researchers are not bored with what we are learning. There
  is much important information coming from the people who have
  experienced this phenomenon. It may seem "old hat" to some, but nothing
  has stopped. The information is all around us and more and more people
  are coming forward with their accounts every day.
  <<This is because psychic "information exchange" has come to be widely
  known and accepted, especially remote viewing. Even hardware-oriented
  ufologists (i.e. males) are more open to psychic claims these days
  because there seems to be some experimental evidence for them.>>
  KW: Excuse me? What about female ufologists and researchers, not to
  mention the thousands of female Engineers in the World?
  <<Sherman's book adds a slightly more plausible dimension by saying that
  the aliens are far more reliable in the psychic domain than we are.
  Sherman indicates that his communication sessions were dominated
  primarily by the aliens and that he himself has shown no psychic ability
  outside of those controlled conditions.>>
  KW: That may be because Sherman had to take two pills everyday prior to
  his IC training and IC comms. He wasn't told what the pills were; his
  senior officers (Captains) said they didn't know why he had to take them
  or what they were, other than they would help him comm better... This
  should raise some red flags. Abductees don't need computers OR pills to
  communicate with aliens - aliens are telepathic. There is some faulty
  logic in this.
  <<This project seemed to treat its people like laboratory rats, which
  works only in the laboratory or in the minds of military planners.>>
  KW: Bingo - You may be on to the true nature of PPD: It may have been
  just that; an experiment on an unwitting soldier. Sherman even says in
  his book that he believed that one of the "onion" layers for this black
  project was the study of noise cancellation. Noise cancellation and the
  use of white noise and/or frequencies to mask out sounds - are used in
  **senory deprivation experiments.**
