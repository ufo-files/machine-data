Northern Miner (Charters Towers, Qld. : 1874 - 1954), Monday 12 January 1948, page 2



         PLANE                EXPLODES
        CHASING                 "SAUCER"
                    NEW        YORK,                 10
          Captain  Thomas   Mantell  was    kill-
       ed In a plane explosion near      Frank-
       lin, Kentucky,   while he was    chasing
       what was     believed to be a      flying
        saucer.
          Mantell was    one   of three    National
        Guard   officers   sent  to  investigate   a
        flying object reported by Colonel Hix,
        commander of the Fort Knox               Air
        Force base, described as "very white,
        looking like an umbrella".                          &nbsp;




          The  object was        said    to   have   been
       visible at points        in     Kentucky      and
       Tennessee.    &nbsp;
