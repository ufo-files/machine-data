     From: Katharina Wilson <kwilson@alienjigsaw.com>
     Date: Mon, 30 Jun 1997 18:04:45 -0800
     Subject: Coming this weekend: A&E Documentary on Conspiracies


  A new documentary premiered on the A&E channel Sunday, June 29, 1997. It
  will be airing again this weekend. I highly recommend that you watch it.
  It is titled "Conspiracies." The two-hour program covers the subject of
  conspiracy theories very seriously and to-the-point. There are both pro
  and con discussions. The basic message is: (1) There have been
  conspiracies within the American Government and its secret agencies, and
  (2) People who research and believe in conspiracies are not "kooks."
  The topics covered include:
  The Kennedy Assassinations; The Martin Luther King Assassination; The
  Tuskegee Syphilis Experiments carried out on hundreds of
  African-American Men; Mind Control; The CIA's Connection with Drugs,
  mainly LSD; The Suppression of Technology; The "Absorption" of the
  (Nazi) Gaehlen Society into the CIA after W.W.II; and The New World
  Order (which touches on the Freemasons, Trilateralism, and militias).
  <<A "George" magazine's readers' poll showed that 74% of Americans
  believe the United States Government is involved in conspiracies.>>
  I hope everyone will watch this new documentary. -- Katharina Wilson
