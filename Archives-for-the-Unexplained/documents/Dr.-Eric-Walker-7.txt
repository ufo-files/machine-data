                        Source advised DR. WALKER headed the United States
              Delegation to an international conference in December 1968
              held in Paris, France, on engineering education. The conference
              was organized by the United Nations Educational, Scientific
              and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) and included participants
              throughout the world. DR. WALKER gave a paper at the conference
              on the development and structure of engineering education in
              the U.S.  DR. WALKER is the President of the National Academy
              of Engineering.

                        Although DR. WALKER has been friendly and cooperative
              with this office over the years in all matters-, it is not felt
              that contact with him in the DESECO program would produce
      • I
         I    fruitful results. DR. WALKER,in fulfilling his responsibilities
              as President, PSU, is severely limited in amount of time he
              has available to be able to participate in this program with
              any degree of effectiveness. His stay at the international
              conference was of brief duration.

                        It is not being recommended that he be interviewed
              under the DESECO program for reasons cited above.

                        No further investigation being conducted by this
              office.
