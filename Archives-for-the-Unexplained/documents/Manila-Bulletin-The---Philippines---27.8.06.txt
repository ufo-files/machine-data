Manila Bulletin (The) – Philippines… 27th August 2006…
The Bible-UFO Connection - Part 2…
   What does the Holy Bible say about UFOs? As mentioned in my article last week appearing in this column - UFO is the acronym for unidentified flying objects referred to by many as extraterrestrials, extraterrestrial beings, space visitors or alien beings. Many believe that UFOs existed even during Biblical times. If we believe in UFOs, we also think that there is physical extraterrestrial life on other planets outside our solar system. Others assume further that the physical beings in this vast universe living outside our planet earth visit us now and then. Finally, there are those who strongly advocate that not only are there physical beings outside planet earth, but such physical beings outside planet earth have intelligence superior to human beings with more advanced technology at their command.
  The theme of alien super wisdom and intelligence is destroying a lot of human confidence. In worse cases, this speculation of super intelligent aliens from outer space is destroying a lot of human lives. Man's feeling of inferiority complex vis-a-vis UFOs has been heightened by claims of UFO contacts and abduction accounts. No wonder then that many UFO contactees and abductees have reported anxiety-ridden nightmares, frightening falshbacks, disturbed minds, and in worse cases, the feeling that they are controlled by the UFOs and can no longer have further life of their own without the interference of the UFOs.
   Stories of UFOs have identified these space alients with angels, the heavenly messengers mentioned in the Holy Bible. Many UFO buffs even go as far as saying that stories of angels in the Holy Bible are really stories about extraterrestrial visitations. Others hold the opposite view that these unexplainable UFO reports are the deceptive work of the devil (Satan) and his cohorts, the demons who are the fallen angels. Stories have been told and retold by people who had reported encounters with alien beings that there is a "reptilian likeness" in the appearance of the alien beings. This brings to mind the Biblical account that when Satan appeared to Eve in the garden of Eden, Satan appeared in the form of a snake/reptile.
   From Biblical account we see that there are angels and demons. God created the angels. While there is no specific verse in the Holy Bible that says that God created angels, we know that God created everything and that includes angels. Angels don't have bodies, hence do not grow old and die like human beings. They are spiritual beings. Angels are not born. They are created. The word angel means messenger. They serve God all the time. Sometimes angels may look like human beings and have appeared to Abraham lile persons.
   On the other hand, the demons are the fallen angels who once followed God but later rebelled against God with Lucifer (Satan) as their leader. As the Biblical story goes, Satan and his fallen angels were kicked out of heaven. One may ask: Can the fallen angels known as demons also take different forms if they want to, like other angels? Many believe that the fallen angels/demons may possess many of the same powers that the good angels possess. From Biblical account, the angels are very powerful spiritual beings. They can appear as bright shining beings that terrify those who see them. (See Matthew 28:2-7)
   Does Biblical logic support the thesis that UFOs were created by God? What the Holy Bible in the Book of Genesis, the first book of the Old Testament of the Bible says is that "God created man in His own image, in the image of God He created him; male and female He created them." (Genesis 1:27). From this Biblical verse, it is very clear that life on earth was created by our Omnipotent and Almighty God. Our planet Earth as we know is only a small part of the universe. While many of us believe that the Earth is the only planet where life exists - we cannot deny the fact that ongoing scientific studies and researches are being conducted by different countries to advance the theory that there might be a planet out there where there is also life like in the earth. But be it as is - to date, the divine act of creating man and woman is something that has never been duplicated in any place of the vast universe. The claim of many  that UFOs are real, is indeed an attempt to promote the thinking that the divine act of creating human life has been duplicated. If not by God, who then created the UFOs, if they really exist. This is food for thought. (To be continued)
