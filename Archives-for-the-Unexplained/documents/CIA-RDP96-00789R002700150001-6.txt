         (U)    DATA SUMMARIES:
         {S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)     Following are the perceived highlights
    from each source.
               (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)   Source A:
             -- At first there was an image of a suburban cottage
    style house around evergreens with overcast skies. The beacon
    person has a big heart and a female may be associated with this
    tasking.
               I feel the target site itself is located near a remote
    land, water, or beach area where large numbers of people visit or
    come to watch and see events in a warm sunny climate. Planes fly
    over this location.
            -- The target has the colors blue, white, and brown
    (wooden items may be around the site). The target is a dark
    structure or object on a blue background. In particular, there
    is a metal man-made object which is big, long, and narrow, but is
    white domed or has a rounded end (sketch a). Only a few people
    can be transported by this object at any one time. The object is
    active when moving in motion and has superior technology, can
    move at a fast rate and may have radar or sonar capabilities.
    Only experienced personnel, usually military related, operate it
    and white streaks are left on blue water or skies in the
    aftermath of its motion (sketch b).  (The color blue is above and
    below the target). A propeller moves around and on this object
    and a country banner may be related to the target.
            -- The object is near the water's surface or water's
    edge, is modular and costly to be active. A gentle rocking
    motion sometimes occurs and a noise can be heard while operating
    this technical craft.

         (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)   Source       B:
        -- The target is a location where there is lots of
movement; like milling around. There is a large, open area.
Light is passing through large glass walls or windows. There are
numerous pointed or cone-shaped objects present. There is the
sensation of spinning; a whirring sound; angled glass; whirling
pieces of metal; a rippling effect; and the concept of sleek and
smooth.
        -- Another swirling movement is almost a cascading motion
which repeats itself. There is a white, powdery substance
reminiscent of a limestone or chalk deposit. There is the
whirling noise of a propeller with dust flying. A series of v-
shaped trenches are cut into the earth.
        -- There are numerous squares wrapped in reflecting foil;
these are stacked vertically like books on a shelf. There is a
large steel pipe which continuously rolls on it's side.
        -- An individual has an interest in oil fields. They are
planning an exchange of money. Sounds like rapid fire gun shots
can be heard in the distance.
        -- There is the concept of long rounded objects
(tubular), some of which are suspended from the ceiling. This
place feels like a collection of "like" or "similarly" related
objects. Other concepts include: mechanical; spinning: energy;
segmented; angular shapes; exploration; space; and rockets
(sketches c, d & e).
         (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)   Source C:
        -- Site consists of many objects. One object is circular
with a ring around it and could be slightly moving. One object
was very flat and slanted and had "grooves" in it.
         -- I felt that I was outside and people were attending an
event.
           Sometimes it seemed dark at the site. Some colors
associated with the site could be dark. Blue and green were
associated with the site.
           Water may be at the site. small structures could
surround the site and there may be a large structure at the site.

 Date:   12 May 1994

 Time: 1000 - 1030 EST



 Environment:    Sitting in my car on a bright sunny day looking through the windshield at target.

 Object: Orange and white water tank located at the US Army Airfield Fort Belvoir, Virginia.

 Comments:       Sitting in my car looking through the windshield and beyond the fence at the target
 in the background. There is a 7 foot high chain link fence with strands of barbed wire along the
 top of the fence. Although, I could see buildings, parked cars and ocasional vehicle passed in
 front of my position I tried to focus my vision only on the tank. I tried to concentrate only on
 the water tank as though I were in a cell and could see only the fence outside and the water tank
 in the distance. Water tanks are normally silver in color however, water tanks near military
 airfields are orange and white. There are buildings in the area possible military aircraft hangers.
 I did not look to the right of my position to see the runway, but I could hear helicopters in the
 back ground as they took off and landed.

(S/NF/SGILIMDIS)      Source A:

        Source A's description bears little resemblance to the water tank or surrounding area in
which I concentrated on. There is no suburban cottage in the general area nor is there a female
associated with this tasking. Behind my car is a small military park where families come for a
picnic. There is a small stream of water behind the park that runs East/West past the end of the
runway. At the West end of the runway on the south side is a built-up area with shops and on
the right side there is a marsh area with a small pond and a land fill. People in the park do
watch the choppers and planes take-off and land at the airfield. The long, narrow metal man-
made objects could be the hangers or maintenance buildings.

Attachment A: The rounded white domed object could be the water tank. A military helicopter
has radar and is operated by experienced personnel and usually carries only a few people.

Attachment B: As you can see blue sky below and above the target in the picture. One can hear
the noise of the helicopter as the blade moves around or the propeller noise of a plane. The
banner may be the star on the sides of the planes and choppers that symbolizes the United States
military.

Attachment C:    Unable to correlate. Drawing "C" could be the large hanger.

(S/NF/SGILIMDIS)       Source B:

        Source B's description is vety good. The milling around and lots of movement could be
military aircrew/maintenance perosnnel outside the hangers. There is a large open area, the
runway and parking area for choppers and light planes. The doors on the hangers have large
glass windows that lets sun light shine in. The whirring sound and the whirling noise could be
the aircraft motors. The propeller of a plane or the blade of a helicopter will stir up dust debris
as the craft nears the edge of the parking pad. The long rounded (tubular) objects are pipes that
construction engineers are placing as drainage pipes.
