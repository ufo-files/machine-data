                    On the principle that the way to bring a
                  good thing into use is to employ it, the
                  Fortean Society follows the lead of the
                  Eastman Kodak Company and hundreds of
                  other commercial institutions (654 big ones
                  as of 1934, and now more than 1000), and
                  adopts the eminently sensible 13-month cal­
                  endar ... If we wait for the League of
                  Nations to do it for us, we shall all get black
                  in the face. (Haile Selassie is a case in
                  point.) Nor has the United Nations stated
                  this (or anything else) as one of its “war”

                      Accordingly, on page 16 of this issue, is
                   the Fortean Calender for the year 14 F.S.
                   (1944 old-style). There you will see that we
                   have reconciled the old-style calendar with
                   the new, day and date, according to the basic
                   principles for calendar reform advocated by
                   The International Fixed Calendar League—
                   with Fortean tpuches added. For instance,
                   the International Fixed Calendar League does
                   not realize that “1944 old-style” is the 14th
                   year of a new era of mental freedom under
                   the auspices of the Fortean Society, and so
                   has not so far changed the number of the
                   year. The League still holds for 1944, in
                   common with the dead and dying churches
                   which pretend-—in the face of overwhelming
                   evidence to the contrary—that this year ac­
                   tually is the one thousand, nine-hundred
                   and forty-fourth of the “Christian era”.
                   Any school child could tell them that
                    1944 is jour years off its base as a matter
                    of simple arithmetic, if all the ? rest of the
                    hoax is granted; and any student of history
                    will inform them that never yet hath there
                    been a single Christian year.
                      Away, then, with all this moss-grown
                   folly! If we have not now come of age, most
                   assuredly we never shall.
