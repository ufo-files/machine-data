Case xxx.
An electrical ”ghost bomb” 

Time: Wednesday, August 14
Place: Svarvartorpssjön near Anderstorp (county of Jönköping)

””GHOSTBOMB SEEN IN ANDERSTORP”. The police at Anderstorp was yesterday alarmed by some youngsters who had observed a mysterious fireball in a treetop in the woods near Svarvartorpssjön. The youngsters stated that the fireball was luminous and gave a shine that was visible far away, and that a crackling sound was heard. 

”With a certain amount of tension one went out to investigate the case more closely, and, yes, when one arrived at the three, where the phenomenon had been observed, and proceeded to touch the tree, not only came the luminousity back, but the tree was even loaded with electricity. The reason for this was that a high-voltage cable had been damaged, by the strong wind, and was hanging in the tree. Despite the wet weather, voltage decreased so much that one could stand the current and no accident thus occured. In the night the cable was repaired.” 

Status: Electrical short-circuit 
Source: Smålands Folkblad, August 15.
