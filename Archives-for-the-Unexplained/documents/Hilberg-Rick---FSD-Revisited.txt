    The publication that you now hold in your hands is one that we have wanted to do for quite some
time now. Flying Saucer Digest has been publishing since the spring of 1 967 (there were several
brief name changes over the years, but it was indeed the same basic publication) and is rapidly
approaching its 200th issue, a feat seldom seen in "ufozine" publishing . So I suppose the time is
indeed right to take a retrospective look at those 44 years and see just how the UFO field has
evolved. It hasn't been an easy job either, as picking and choosing just what to include out of all the
thousand-plus pages of material published has been quite daunting . Anyway, I sincerely hope you
like our selection of what we believe to be the essence of the publication that the late Allan Manak
launched those many years ago.
    As is our custom with this series of booklets that we have been doing since the 1 990s, we are
running the following artic1es and news stories in the order that they were published with only minimal
editing in an effort to give some flavor of those times now so long in the past.
    As many of you know, I didn't come on board FSD until 1 970 when my "original" UFO Magazine
was incorporated with FSD at the urging of AI Manak, so I wasn't around for the 1 967 founding. FSD
was simply Al's dream and his joy over the years, and rm happy that I can continue to keep the
publication that he so loved alive and healthy. AI was a very capable editor, and it was truly a joy to
work with him for so many productive years.

was also a very capable and enthusiastic editor as well as a writer, and thanks must go out to him for
his tenure with the publication. Thanks must also go out to the "secret editor" of FSD lo these many

    FSD has also been blessed with two very talented staff artists over the years, Ronald J . Pelger
and the late Benita "Bunny" Owens, whose many illustrations graced the publication's pages for more
than forty years. Kudos to them as well.
    It's only fitting that we dedicate this publication to our founder and the big brother that I never had,

1 967 - CAPTURED SAUCER NEAR GERMANY? - Ronald Pelger
     Periodically, since 1 954, I have been doing a bit of research on a fantastic claim of a "captured
 flying saucer". By using the word "captured," I do not mean that it was caught with a net or
 threatened at gunpoint. However, I am mereJy stating that scientists perplexity stumbled upon a
 "flying saucer" which may have accidentally crash landed.
     On a tiny island in the North Sea off the German coast, it is believed that a possibility of hydrogen
 bomb explosions in the Pacific Ocean could have knocked a UFO to the earth. Preliminary findings
were revealed in Oslo, Norway, by a Dr. Hans Larsen Loberg, a retired Norwegian scientist, who said
 investigators have made some startling discoveries.
     The saucer came down on Heligoland, a small island which the Germans used as a U-boat base
 during World War f. Since the island is only a speck of land in a large body of water, Dr. Loberg
 believes the disk was forced to earth when H-Bomb blasts created conditions of atmospheric
pressure that made flight impossible.
     It was not a crash-up, and the investigators found most of the saucer's instruments in good
 condition. On the ground surrounding the ship were found the bodies of seven men, all burned
beyond recognition. They may, or may not, have been passengers aboard the flying craft.
     Dr. Loberg said descriptive details of the saucer were told to him by a fellow scientist who was with
the investigating team on HeligoJand.           '
     The craft, it seems, landed under guidance of its own instruments and the investigators studied it
for two days before risking closer observation.
     The area where the saucer came down was bombarded with cosmic rays, Geiger counters, and
other protective devices before investigators began.
     The seven charred bodies found around the saucer were yet unidentified.
     Their clothing was burned away completely and there were no clues to indicate whether they were
passengers aboard the craft, or whether they were Heligoland residents who ventured too close to
the saucer too soon. All seven men were estimated to range from 25 to 30 years of age and of the
same height - about 5 feet 8 inches. They all had excellent teeth.
     A theory has been com piled :
     The seven men, who were passengers, were consumed by fire inside the descending ship. The
blaze could have been caused by sudden changes in atmospheric pressure conditions inside the
saucer's hermetically sealed cabin.
     On top of the craft, was a trap-door through which lhe seven bodies could have been thrown by
the impact of landing.
     The ship's measurements were very peculiar. It was 91 feet in diameter and the cabin was 70 feet
in height. I n fact, all the dimensions were divisible by seven.
     On the control board were a series of push buttons, which probably controlled the saucer in flight.
     The landing gear resembled a tripod of three metal spheres which could revolve in any direction.
There were no bolts, rivets, or screws on the saucer and in the construction were found two metals
which are unknown to scientists.
     The outer metal of the craft was very light and in a way resembled aluminum, but it was so hard
that even 1 5,000 degrees Fahrenheit could not melt it down. I n fact, the saucer itself was so light two
men could easily lift one side of it.
     The scientists found equipment inside which definitely resembled living quarters. On one side of
the cabin's interior, were placed wall enclosed bunks.
     A liquid resembling water but almost three times heavier than normal drinking water was found in
two small containers. On a wall bracket was a tube filled with a large number of pills. It is possible
these pills could be a form of tabulated food .
     The saucer's radio, which had no tubes, wires, nor antenna, was about as big as a package of
cigarettes.
     Pamphlets and booklets which seem to deal with navigation problems, were also found, but the
script could not be deciphered.
     Looking back to the 1 954 "captured saucer" incident, there are a few questions which still need

it? (3 ) Was the investigating team hushed up for some reason? (4) Was the astounding claim just

at all, but I have put my thoughts to one possibility. How many UFOs have actually, by accident, or

 1 968 ·CHALLENGING THE HOLLOW EARTH THEORY - Lawrence Blazey
     There has been a great deal written lately on the possibility of the Earth being hollow and a super­
 intelligent race living within. This idea seemed to originate from a misinte rj:>retation to the semantics
 attributed to the description of Admiral Byrd's flight over the North Pole in which it was stated that
 Byrd went beyond the North Pole and found evidence of an iceless domain.
     Delving back in history we find innumerable accounts of explorers who have reached the North
1Pole by sled, by plane, by dirigible, and lastly by submarine. In 1926 Amundson and Ellsworth took
 off by plane from Kings Bay, Spitzbergen, which is 600 miles from the Pole and dropped flags on the
 North Pole, then proceeded straight across to Teller, Alaska. They proved that between the North
 Pole and Alaska lies only the deep Polar Sea.
     In the Hollow Earth theory it is noted that the Earth has a hole at the North and South Pole about
  1400 miles in diameter. If this were so, any investigation of a map would show that this would take in
 the northern rim of Siberia, and part of the Scandinavian Peninsula and part of Canada. Obviously
 these countries would know whether their northern borders dipped downward into a hole. Admiral
 Byrd's flight took off from Sptizbergen in 1926 in a Fokker plane. He started at 12 :5 0 a.m. and
 reached the Pole at 9 : 00 p.m., circled it several times, then returned to Spitzbergen at 4:30 a.m. or
 after about 15 hours of flying, or 1,400 miles. Since it is about 600 miles from Spitzbergen to the
 Pole, Byrd must have gone about 1 00 miles around or beyond the Pole before turning back because
 of low gas supply. History does not relate anything unusual about his discoveries.
     Again in 1928 Wilkins flew from Pt. Barrow, Alaska over the North Pole and, after 20 hours,
 reached Spitzbergen. He stated no new land areas were sighted.
                                                           j
     In 193 7 two non-stop flights from Moscow to the United States were made over the North Pole,
 the first commercial flights made over the Pole. Surely all of these flights would have noticed some
peculiarity in the Earth at the Pole.
   Again in 193 7 the Russian ship Sedov reached within 300 miles of the Pole and was frozen in the
ice for months. They could not be frozen in a hole. The most recent demonstration of a normal North
Pole was in 1958 when the Nautilus sailed under the Pole making a sounding which showed about
1,500 ft. between the bottom of the Sea and the underside of the ice shelf. The Nautilus sailed from
Spitzbergen and made a straight line north arriving at Point Barrow in Alaska.
    Now let us look at some of the known physical facts which are beyond doubt. The claim of the
Hollow Earth proponents is that a ship traveling thru the North Pole sails over an edge which is 400
miles thick or curved, and the trip is so gradual that you do not notice the change. Anyone can plainly
see the stars at night and, if a ship descended downward, they sky would be gradually blanked out
therefore all sextant readings become impossible to establish direction. Furthermore, where the
oceans of the exterior met those of the interior, there would arise a terrific cavitation of forces.
   A plane flying over the Pole at night would be able to see a huge light area emerge like dawn and
the central sun, as is presumed exists inside the Earth, would be seen as a stationary source. Since
the inside of the Earth is supposed to be a warm climate, these warm air currents would rise thru the
opening and cause ice to melt at the rim. How can you justify the action of rain to replenish the rivers
and lakes inside the Earth even if we supposed centrifugal force of the Earth's rotation were to keep
them in their place? There would be no chance of evaporation, and rivers would pile up in one spot.

   What happens to the gravitational force which is supposed to draw all things to the center of the
Earth? This force evidently does not exist beyond the 400 mile depth of the crust, so say the
proponents, and how could a sun remain stationary or spin at the center violating all physical laws?
How could seismic waves of the Alaskan earthquake travel thru the Earth and be recorded in Russia?
At the midway point on the 4 00 mile crust a ship would see a sun behind and one ahead. Quite
confusing, it would seem, yet no one has noted this duality.
   Now the proponents maintain that the icebergs at the North Pole are formed from the flow of fresh
water from the interior rivers which end in the ocean and freeze when they reach the cold exterior.
Any high school student knows that icebergs are formed over eons of time by the constant
compacting of snowfall which the Polar Winds precipitate over the Pole. Then when the weight of
snow becomes enormous, the snow tums to a crystallized ice called Neve'. When this ice shelf
creeps toward the warm Atlantic currents, the underside is melted and huge chunks break off
because of the weight above. These chunks drift into the oceans and are called icebergs. The
physical facts, I believe outweigh the fantastic.
    One of the most important theories advanced by the Hollow Earth proponents is that the flying
saucers seen in our atmosphere originate inside the Earth and come out at the poles to see what the
Earth beings are doing with atomic power that might jeopardize the planet.
    After 39 years since Admiral Byrd flew beyond the Pole, it would seem a great stretch of anyone's
intelligence to believe that a country or individuals could keep this secret. It is normal human nature
to investigate further any discovery as earth-shaking as this regardless of national barriers. This
fantastic tale originated only recently by authors of books for entertaining reading.
    We have enough proof of extraterrestrial space ships and their origin to negate any reference to
them being Earth centered, even though many have been sighted going into or out of the Polar Seas.
These space ships can navigate under water or in the air, and their need for Earth exploration and
demand for water makes this a natural consequence.
   As a last rebuttal, the writer examined several books writte n by authors of the Hollow Earth and
has concluded that their motives are suspect in drawing attention away from the truth that is being
expounded by the forces of Light for the spiritual evolution of mankind. This includes the flying
saucer phenomena which is, in part, a spiritual manifestation repeating the visitations given to
mankind as recorded in Biblical times. Source: Spring 1968 issue.

ARE THE SAUCERS SPACE ANIMALS?             -  J. Philip Bessor
    From the time Kenneth Arnold saw his undulating, shining "saucers", in June, 1947, until Buck
Fitzgerald and Mase Gamey, two desert prospectors, claimed that they watched a flying saucer land
and a little fellow jump out and run off, on August 19, 1949, a periOd of over two years, there was no
mention of saucer crewmen landing or making conversation with Earthmen by sign language, mental
telepathy, or speech.
    But the Fitzgerald-Garney, affair, carried by news services, touched off a veritable chain reaction
of "little men" tales.  In English-speaking countries the littl e men have magically grown up into
benevolent "guardians" or "masters" seriously concerned with the cosmic effects of atomic
experimentation.    In France and Italy, the crewmen remain diminutive, elf-like, and surprisingly
enough, assume the physical characteristics and mannerisms of the countrymen.
   Even research groups have rearranged their concepts of the flying saucers. For example, a cigar­
shaped, winged object seen against the moon over San Diego during the meteor shower of 1946 was
described by a medium as a space ship piloted by benevolent people "west of the moon" (and
presumably "east of the sun," as in the song title}. Now it has magically evolved from a creaking
vehicle of balsa wood and metal alloy covering, with electric motors to propel its huge wings, into a
sleek "aeroform", piloted by "guardians" who are supposed to be warning us, by their senseless aerial
gyrations and aerial explosions, against the careless use of the atom bomb.
   The United States Air Force, through a series of admissions and denials, has left the issue
hanging in mid-air, ripe fruit to be plucked by science-fiction opportunists. Wrthin the past several
years these writers have released a number of patently fiction "flying saucer" books (but called the


                                                  6
gospel truth ) on a gullible public. I have studied each instance of alleged human contact with a space
man or space woman and have found none halfway convincing.
     I do not imply here that space travel is an impossibility or that other planets are uninhabited.
Indeed, if our Earth is not speeding around the sun approximately 1 8 miles a second, and if our
constellation is not hurtling toward Vega at about the same rate of speed - as our orthodox
astronomers assure us - our Earth might easily be visited by inhabitants of other planets and in tum
we might easily visit them, if they exist.
     We know that time is a many dimensional thing, that it both exists and does not exist, that it is
relative.    By the same token, if there is any merit to my theory of the homogeneity of opposites, the
Earth both moves and does not move in space on a four - or more dimensional system of motion and
non-motion. Research seems to indicate that this theory is applicable to various objects in haunted
dwellings.
     Many have seen "impossible" instances of locked, bolted and nailed doors bursting open, only to
be found seconds later securely locked, bolted and nailed as before. A calendar on the wall of a
haunted building in New Orleans, although firmly glued to the surface, spun like a pinwheel before the
eyes of amazed onlookers. Yet it was found, moments later, glued to the wall as before.
     There was the time when the sun "danced" in the sky over a large group of people in Portugal.
They had assembled to view a miracle the Virgin had promised the devout children at Fatima. A
photograph of the misplaced sun appeared in Life magazine.               At the time of the occurrence
astronomers in other parts of the world noticed nothing peculiar about the sun's behavior.
     I have in my possession an account of a husband and wife who had retired for the night in a
haunted house. They were awakened by the sensation of the floor collapsing. They felt themselves
falling and panicked, only to find, moments later, that the floor was as solid as before.
     Perhaps the entire cosmic plan of the universe is based on a many dimensional, paranormal
system of super physics.
     In July, 1 947, I submitted to the Air Force a theory I had evolved reading Kenneth Arnold's
description of the flight of the silvery disks. He described them as undulating, or weaving back and
forth. An undulating motion characterizes many poltergeist-propelled objects, and many of the aerial
disks zig-zagged, or "skipped" through the sky like a flat pebble thrown along the surface of a creek,
or sailed along on edge, and often exploded, or vanished, in to thin air.
     Because of this I contended that the "flying disks" (as they were first called) are a form of space
animal, or creature, of a highly attenuated (ectoplasmic? ) substance, capable of materialization and
dematerialization, whose propellant is a form of telekinetic energy.
     There is a saying that Nature abhors a vacuum. If the seas of our earth are swarming with
varieties of living things, both great and small, is it not logical to assume that the "sea" of our sky
abounds with sundry forms of living things, lifewise both great and small, of varied shapes, but
adaptable to their celestial environment? Some may be quite invisible, others translucent, others
opaque, still others capable of changing, chameleon-like, from one color to another, from one form to
another, from visibility to complete invisibility, all in a moment.
     Keeping an account of each new sighting on a large map, I noted that there appeared to be
"saucer' zones or "belts" wherein the phenomena were more apt to be observed. I found that there
was a predominance of sightings along both coasts, and that the northwestern part of the United
States seemed extremely favored. I noted a "belt" extending from British Columbia, Oregon and
Washington, through Idaho, Indiana, southern Ohio and into West Virginia.
     I noted that remarkably few sightings occurred in Pennsylvania, and that the great industrial area
of Pittsburgh was practically shunned by the sky objects, whereas such cities as Dayton, Columbus,
and Cincinnati, Ohio, were frequently visited by the aerial saucers.
    A recent survey of ghosts showed that Great Britain is one of the most haunted on the surface of
the Earth and that Sussex, in England, can boast more hauntings than any other spot in that country.
     Is there some energy, magnetic or otherwise, in the ground or in the air, which attracts or gives
vitality to, both ghosts and flying saucers? Disk-shaped objects are more likely to be observed in the
northwestern states and along the eastern coast, whereas torpedo - or cigar-shaped - objects are

    In reviewing the monumental Books of Charles Fort, I noted the prevalence of aerial phenomena
during the 187 0s, 188 0s and 189 0s, and discovered that these years saw record and near-record
breaking weather, exactly as we are experiencing today. Is the current outburst of saucer sightings
evidence that our aerial visitors are "migrating" into our atmosphere, forced, possibly, by some solar,
or cosmic, cyclic disturbance occurring in outer space, which is also the cause of our odd weather?

shower on the night of November 13, 1833, and also a "very brilliant, hook-like form" seen in the sky
at Poland, Trumball County, Ohio, at the same time. It remained visible for more than an hour.
   Possibly these leviathans of the air seek shelter in the dense atmosphere of our Earth during
meteor showers and other disturbances, as ships seek harbor during a tempest at sea. Within recent
years we have read of the migration of fishes into waters previously foreign to them, and the intrusion
of wild animals into areas in which they never before had been observed. Did the mysterious,
sustained vibration of the earth noted by seismologists in the early part of January, 1948, cause the
huge "flaming red cone, trailing a green misr to fly low over Clinton County, Ohio, on the night of
January 8, 1948?
    There are bonafide reports of "saucers" the size of tennis balls, or larger than "Constellation"
planes, and of shapes that vary from perfectly square to perfectly round, from paper-thin to very thick.
Probably the phenomena represent varied species of aerial organisms. The luminescence observed
in some may be analogous to that of the luminescent deep-sea-fish, or the mysterious glow of the
fire-fly.
    Some of the disk-shaped objects may be likened to the clam or oyster, with a semi-material "shell"
to facilitate its passage through space. The huge, bullet-shaped object, with bands around it, seen in
East Africa by the passengers of an aerial transport, might be the analogue of a striped, eel-like fish
found in the ocean.
    There are reports of tailed saucers which certainly support my space animal theory. On May 1 6,
1818, at approximately 4 p.m. a vast procession of dark brown, circular bodies, with tails "three or
four fathoms long, " sailed over Skeninge, Sweden, causing the sun to turn a deep brick red by their
passage. Occasionally one of the weird objects would fall to Earth, leaving a "soapy or jellied" film

    In September, 195 0, during the unexplained phenot;nenon of the "purple sun", a purple-glowing,
six-foot globe fell tightly onto a field in Philadelphia, scarcely bending the grass with its weight.
Touched by one of the policemen who saw the object fall, it dematerialized into a jelly-like mass which
soon disappeared.
    There were several early accounts of airplanes being "frozen" in mid-air by nearby flying saucers.
Could these mysterious objects exert a force equivalent to the potent charge of the electric eel?
Could the heavy trail of smoke-like substance seen coming from certain torpedo-shaped objects be
the equivalent of the "ink" ejected by the octopus? Could the brilliant searchlight beamed down on
Earth by some of these objects be equivalent to the glowing lantern of the deep sea lantemfish?
    In my Brown Mountain, N.C., researches I learned from witnesses that many of the unexplained
lights seen on its summit emitted a sizzling noise. How often we read of flying saucers emitting
sizzling, humming, or howling sounds! Evidence points to the Brown Mountain lights, which vary from
light green to orange-pink in color, being of paranormal origin, possibly of an electrical sort of energy
akin to that of the flying saucer. "Ball lightning" and St. Elmo's fire may be elemental relatives of the
flying saucer!
    It is impossible to discern where the meteorological ends and the supernormal begins. We read of
unknown bodies tearing through the structure of airplanes while in flight, or ripping through
signboards and continuing on their way. There is even an instance of an object supposedly making a
hole in a store window in a New York town and magically sealing it up again.
    Are mysterious objects which pass through solid objects necessarily solid themselves? I believe
not. At Clydesdale, Scotland, (near Paisley ) in the year 1295, an extremely powerful ghost haunted

    "Acceleration" apparently does not cause the change in color of a flying saucer. There are

    We shudder when we read of the many falls of flesh and blood from the sky in times past and
wonder whether a species of "saucer" is carnivorous, finding animals and humans a tasty dish. A
report from France tells of a man becoming enmeshed in a fall of "angel hair" from the sky. It is
claimed that only after a battle did he escape from being pulled up into the air. Fort often claimed we
were "fished for" by aerial intruders.
    We read of the weird disappearance of the occupants of sailing ships, of the strange
disappearance into thin air of the 25 or 3 0 inhabitants of the Eskimo village on Hudson's Bay in
November, 1930. We read of the C-46 (which Kenneth Arnold was hunting when he saw the group of
flying saucers) with 32 Marines on it which crashed in 1947. The plane was found, but there was no
trace of a single body in or about the wreckage.
    We read of Oliver Lerch, the I ndiana boy who went down to the spring to draw a bucket of water
one winter's night and who was caught up into the sky, his cries for help fading in the distance. We
read of "thunderstones· falling to Earth . They are almost always wedge-shaped, and we wonder if
they are the implements of ancient hunters who were abducted by carnivorous species of flying
saucer, having lain, perhaps for centuries, in some limbo of the sky. We wonder what effect these
flying saucers have upon the very "Gates of Heaven."
    Do they frighten or subsist upon the souls of the departed, as the ancients hinted, with their belief
in the "Devourer of Souls"? Surely the Church should be concerned about these things.
    We look to so-called "mediums" for some answer and find a wide variety of "explanations" from
their colorful "guides", ranging from "reflections of cosmic ray apparatus" to sage and esoteric "Elder
Brethren" from Venus.
    Meanwhile, the Air Force has clamped strict cerisorship on all American and most foreign
sightings. There is little doubt that the Air Force, disdaining to admit that the flying saucers border on
the preternatural, prefer to let the subject rest, knowing that science-fiction writers and amateurish
publications will finish off what Donald Menzel , the champion of the "mirage" notion, failed to
complete.
    If the intelligent and sober segment of the American public does not want to sit idly by and see the
subject of flying saucers thus buried in the grave of fantasy-fiction and the ridiculous, let each one
beg the Air Force and our American news services to remove all censorship, eliminate all
suppression, concerning the greatest phenomena of our time.
    After all, it is OUR sky, too!  Source: Summer 19 68 issue.

1 969 - DIMENSIONAL FAULT ZONES AND UFOs - Ronald Pelger
     I have always been intrigued by the many strange and mysterious disappearances of people all
over the world. There are numerous accounts of people vanishing into thin air without a single trace.
It's as though one were walking through an invisible door into another world . . . never to be seen again.
     I once read of a farmer who went into a field to work when suddenly he completely disappeared in
front of the eyes of his son . For days after, the farmer's son could, at times, hear the cries of his
father in the field begging for help to get him "out" from wherever he was.
     Then there was the mysterious disappearance of five U. S. Naval planes on the afternoon of
December 5, 1945 over the Atlantic. All five planes vanished at the same time. Strange that five

                                                    9
planes should disappear all at once.
    Many reports recall classic mysteries of puzzling disappearances on the high seas of ships and
crew. Such mysteries are nothing new. They have been happening for centuries.
    In 18 6 6, the Lotta, a Swedish ship vanished along with her crew. An American cargo ship called
Cyclops disappeared in the early 1900's near the so-called "Bermuda Triangle". The nuclear sub
Scorpian bid farewell out of sight as it was ending a journey from the Azores.
    What about UFOs? Let's insert them into the picture. Scores of sightings by reputable witnesses
reported real physical objects appearing and then suddenly "disappearing" out of sight. They just
seemed to "vanish" into the air.
    Oh yes, then too, there are the certain "appearances" of people and objects. In England, for
instance, in 1914, a man "appeared" suddenly out of nowhere in the middle of a crowded street. He
seemed to be in a state of shock and knew nothing of who he was or where he came from.
    In the book, New Lands, written by the great Charles Fort, many accounts of appearances of
representations of unknown cities were seen in the skies. Witnesses claimed that these strange
appearances would last for quite some time before they vanished.
    Back to the UFOs again. These objects, which we really know very little about except for visual
sightings and photographs, could very possibly be devices used by members of an alien world with
technical developments so incredible, they are able to penetrate the invisible screen which divides our
world from that other "plane". They come and go as they please.
    In 1943, the U. S. Navy, according to the "Allende Letters" (See the book The Case for the UFO
by Or. Morris Jessup ) was said to have successfully accomplished a secret experiment by applying
Dr. Albert Einstein's Unified Field Theory.      An entire warship and crew was supposedly made

       Both the Muroc and Roswell reports were part of a wave of sightings that introduced the term
 "flying saucer" to America.
       The first widely publicized sighting came June 24, 1 947, when an Idaho pilot and businessman
 reported nine bright metallic objects flying at tremendous speed near Mount Rainier in Washington.
       Scores followed.
       "People in widely separated places and from all walks of life began to report having seen shining,
high-speed, strangely maneuvering objects in the sky," UFO researcher Ted Bloecher wrote in 1 967.
      "On July 4, the reports rose sharply and spontaneously, and for five days there was scarcely any
part of the United States that had not been visited by these strangely elusive aerial objects," Bloecher
added.
      The number of sightings crested on July 7, and during the next few days reports began to diminish
until, about a week later, only a handful were being made from scattered sections of the country.
      The airmen at Muroc were aware of the reports.

airmen in the post exchange: "Someone will have to show me one of these disks before I will believe
it."

documents now posted by UFO enthusiasts on the I nternet.
