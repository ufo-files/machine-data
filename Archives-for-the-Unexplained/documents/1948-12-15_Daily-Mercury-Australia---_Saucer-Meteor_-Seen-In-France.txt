Daily Mercury (Mackay, Qld. : 1906 - 1954), Wednesday 15 December 1948, page 2




        "Saucer Meteor"
         Seen In France
          PARIS,      December    14.— News
       papers    reported    that  a   "flying
       saucer    meteor," which    resembled
       a round     red ball tinged with blue
       and    with a long train, was      seen
       travelling east to west over      Nice,
       in the French     Riviera.
          It appeared to come      from     the
       Italian frontier. The meteor        was
       later      reported      over   Central
        France.
