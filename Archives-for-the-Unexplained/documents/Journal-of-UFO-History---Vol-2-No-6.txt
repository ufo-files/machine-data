process of tracking a large "Skyhook" cosmic ray balloon over the North Atlantic was
startled to see three disc-shaped objects that attached themselves to the balloon's
scientific instrument package. After several minutes the objects separated from the
balloon, established a V-formation, accelerated rapidly and climbed out of sight. (See story,
Vol. II, No. 4, Sept.-Oct. 2005, p. 8). As intelligence analysts must have been aware at the
time, this was not the first such case, nor would it be the last. Scientists, pilots, and
technicians engaged in balloon tracking have contributed an intriguing body of significant
UFO reports.
