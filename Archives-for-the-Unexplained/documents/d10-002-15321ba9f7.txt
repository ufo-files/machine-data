  BLACK HELICOPTERS AT GREEN RIVER
    Date: Sun, 06 Sep 1998 18:55:14 -0500
    From: Dave Kirby <lbear@rmi.net> [posted with permission]
    To: "Glenn Campbell, Las Vegas" <campbell@ufomind.com>
    Subject: Black Helicopters In Green River?
  For whatever this is worth relative to the trickle of speculation about
  Green River, Utah...
  My wife and I were camped at Fretwater Falls, in Desolation Canyon on
  the Green River on Tuesday, Sept 1. Third day into a seven day rafting
  trip. Fretwater is located at Mile 59.5, which is 59.5 miles above the
  I-70 bridge over Green River, Utah. At approximately 5:15PM, two large
  black helicopters came around the bend from downriver and flew directly
  over our heads, following the canyon upstream. The canyon walls there
  are about 500' high, the two chopppers were about 150' or 200' below the
  top of the canyon walls. No numbers on the fuselage, though I thought I
  saw a large circle on the bottom of the lead craft. Sighting lasted no
  more than 15 - 18 seconds. They were loud and fast, and I haven't a clue
  what they could possibly be doing in some a remote area. They definitely
  did not appear to be search and rescue (almost nobody ever has to be
  rescued out of this canyon, as it is fairly mild whitewater...) Wherever
  they originated, they did not return the same way, as we did not see or
  hear them again the rest of the trip. I talked with one commercial guide
  in another party who had been camped 4-5 miles further upstream, and
  they saw the choppers too.
