good reason to have assumed that Apodaca's experiences \-Tere connected with Venusi­

respecting the alleged Mexic�n governmental investigation of the case. If the Mex­
ican authorities did investigate the affair and interview the many farm-hands ap­

relating to the case, while it is not unreasonable to suppose that at least of the
farm-hands are still alive and available for re-interviewing. It is to be hoped
that properly conducted investigations designe� expressly to elicit information
about this case from all such individuals as can still be traced will be put into
operation at the earliest possible moments and the final results published in due
course.
Adamski's reference to Apodaca retreatinG from publicity is most .interesting-­
especially as it seems to have been the reverse of his own personal tendencies    -­



and indicates that fipodaca was, as Adamski pointed out!' not interested in financial
gain through exploitation of his claims. This, while not in any way proving the re­
ality of Apodaca1s experiences, is nevertheless a point in its favour, and, to a
degre� supports the conclusion reached by Bernard Delair in his separate appraisal
of the various ingredients comprising Apodaca's narrative (1'Awareness0, Spring
1977), the conclusion being that a definite substratum of fact underlies :the case
as a whole. If demonstrably so? then the Apodaca affair could well turn out to be
the most important document in the entire field of ufology. Time will tell.
