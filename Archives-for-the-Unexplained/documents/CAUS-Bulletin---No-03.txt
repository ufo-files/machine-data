                                  EDITORIAL

      As you can see , we have changed the physical format of JUST CAUSE
a bit. This will result in a neater appearing newsletter for our readers
and easier preparation   for the editor. Until we get a word processor,we
will stay with this style for the time being.
      Despite the successes in obtaining revealing government information,
recently, we are still having great difficulty on a majority of our FOIA
requests. The main problem seems to be a lack of promptness in receiving
answers from various agencies. One request has taken nearly a year and
is still in the processing stage! There is little we can do about this
situation since the regulations regarding agency response time have been
eased. There has also been a problem in getting appropri*te.answers to
direct questions. We are led thtough .a verbal waltz that would have had
Fred Astaire tripping over his feet if it were translated into music. How­
ever, we will press on with the paper chase until we have all the facts

      On page 2 of this Bulletin we include a copy of a 1980 teletype to
the Defense Intelligence Agency on a UFO/jet chase over Peru. The document
was previously known to us in a heavily-censored form. This new copy was
released as a result of the successful FOIA request to the DIA by your
editor (see JUST CAUSE).
