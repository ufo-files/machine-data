locate the constellation of Orion. The N/Ls were travelling north northwest to south
southeast, one minute duration. Report received from the hotline March 24, 1987, 9:37p.m.
Possible overflight of a CS tanker or KC 135 aircraft. Joanne Bruno is investigating this case.
China, Maine - followup on case reported in the January newsletter. Two University of
Southern Maine sophormores - S.P. and K.S. - were ice skating. A red light was seen
and believed to be on a tower. Other red lights appeared around the initial light -
approximately 34 in all. The lights then moved in a jerky, upward motion and then turned
to what seemed to be its side where a glowing dome appeared. At this time the radio
they were listening to would not work. The object then swooped down to the far side of
the lake behind some tall pine trees. Becoming frightened, the girls then ran home to
report this to the father of K.S. At this time the father was trying to get his television
set to operate since it had stopped working also. The father then when to the lake
with the witnesses but did not see anything unusual. The girls then began to feel
dizzy and nauseous. They returned to the house whereupon the father called Loring AFB,
Brunswick Naval Air Station, the FAA in Augusta, Maine and the Maine State Police
inquiring about the sighting but with no results. A strange tw i st to the sighting is
that the girls and the father returned again to·the lake and found alongside the lake a
piece of jewelry (ring) whjch had on it an insigna which resemb led the insigna on
the Socorro object. This case is continuing under the direction of Dr. Bechtel,
Maine State Director.

     Recently the subject of Ufology has been catapulted into the
limelight in several of your big-name newspapers, i.e. Philadelphia
Inquirer, The Washington Post, LA Times and the Boston Globe to
name a few.   Some of the most knowledgeable people on the subject
were interviewed like Dr. Bruce Maccabee, Dr. David Jacobs, Budd
Hopkins , Walt Andrus and others.    Also receiving wide publicity
were two books, "Intruders:   The Incredible Visitation at Copley
Woods", by Budd Hopkins and Communion by Whitley Str ieber.    This
publicity will open the gate towards public awareness on abduction
reports.

     MUFON headquarters in Seguin, TX reports that membership has
risen to 10 percent in the last two months. Between this publicity
and the books, ongoing library exhibits and press releases in
reference to the exhibits, Mass. MUFON has also received a boost
in active membership.

      It is the concern of myself and others in the organization
that the latest publications and articles will draw members strictly
interested in abduction casework, not to mention the moderate influx
of this type of casework we have received already.       New members
corning into the subject for the abduction casework will most likely
find investigating N/L' s radar/visual, CE I and CE II antic lima tic

      One must keep in mind that abduction casework is not a new
practice .   For over 30 years scores of abduction experiences have
been well publicized in books and periodicals, the more prominent
being the 1957 Villa Boas abduction , the Hill abduction in 1961,
the Schirmer and Andreassen abductions in 1967, the Roach abduction
in 1973, and the Travis Walton abduction in 1975.     To add to this,
there are the hundreds of books and periodicals written by contactees
and their disciples which claim to have met benevolent space brothers
and to have ridden in their saucers to the lush green side of the
moon.    They have not faded into the woodwork.      They continue to
stay close to Ufology and contribute to the fringy atmosphere
surrounding the subject.   A new type of contactee, the "channeler "
claims to lend his vocal cords to a cosmic spirit.        Same circus ,
different costume!    In fact, channeling is now catching on with
the Hollywood jet setters like Linda Evans, Richard Chamberlain,
Shirley MacLaine and their fans and followers do likewise.

the same.    It is  just that an abductee report can easily be
contaminated by something that was read or publicized as far back
as the 1950's.
