        A woman and her brother from Wasilla,                                        Alaska spotted a fiery
object over a lonely country lane on Tuesday,                                              October 29,     1985.
At     first sighting the bright white light seemed to hover in                                                the
sky over the trees.                She stopped the car for a better look.
       Her      brother said,              "it's a                    UFO."        She said there's no such

       Other         reports       came in from Cantwell                              and    Soldotna.         Some

       Federal          Aviation                  Administration                      controllers        quickly
dismissed        the reports          as      meteor                     showers.      Unfortunately,          they
were wrong.
       Katherine Cormier,                  an Air Force spokesperson from the                                 Space

to     the     mystery.         The       UFO was the final booster stage                              for     the
Soviet       Cosmos 1699      satellite.                             It had circled the        earth for       five
days before reentry.
     The   large elongated fiery object entered the atmosphere  at
17,000   miles per hour at 10:26 pm over McGrath,  Alaska.   Fifty
seconds   later it was over Anchorage where witnesses saw  burning
pieces breaking off, leaving fiery trails.
       The     satellite,  Cosmos 1699,  remains in stable  elliptical
orbit,       106 to 218 miles above the earth's surface.   The  Soviet
news        agency     Tass     claims                 the purpose of                 Cosmos    1699     is    "to
continue ourter space research."
       The Soviet rocket's last pass over the Earth took 87 minutes
and 29 seconds.            It's lowest point was 85 miles and it's highest
116 miles.
