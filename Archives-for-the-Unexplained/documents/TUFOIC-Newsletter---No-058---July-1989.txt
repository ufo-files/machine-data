               Our next report came on January 25th and was from two witnesses
 on a fine summers night in Launceston. High in the north west sky they saw a
 light like a falling star, in fact that is what they took it tobe. However, the
 light suddenly came to a halt and hung in the sky for some 15seconds. Then it
 moved off horizontally towards the south, en route it stopped at least two more
 times, each stop being about 10 seconds duration. Each time the light moved it
 seemed to have a tail behind it. The light disappeared into the southern sky.
 There was no air traffic in the area at the time of the sighting.
              Two witnesses on Bruny Island watched a series of lights in the sky                  •

 on March 5th at about 10pm, the incidents occured over half an hour. Again it was
 a fine evening and our witnesses were outside their holiday caravan when they saw
 a bright light blink on in the western sky. Then this light moved off towards the
 south blinking as it departed. A second bluish light was now noticed also moving
 along the same path. Soon after a third light was seen coming from the south to
 the original position of the first light, this light went on and off with a
 regular rythm before it disappeared. Another light came on a short period in
 the numter one position before the sightings terminated.
              At the time of writing a couple of cases are under investigation, one
 from March 22nd in the Midlands were two dogs acted in anunusual manner as their
 owners watch a round orange light pass through the trees some 150mts away. The
 second report is from June 11th when several witnesses saw a large bluish light
 with a sparkling tail drop from the sky to the north west of Kingston. It �hen ,
 much to their suprise went back back up into the sky, did a ninety degree turn
 and headed off into the southern sky disappearing behind some clouds.
