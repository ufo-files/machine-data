The sunny side


Skämtteckning.... 












Irritating winged creatures!
These swarms of ghost rockets
unfortunately cannot be turned away.
Do they come from other planets?
Or are they a much closer threat?
Where the hell is the wasp´s nest?, 
asks the General Staff and so do we. 

S.

  From: Morgon Tidningen, August 13, 1946.
