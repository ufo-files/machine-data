Introduction
Mr. Roy R. Wiks of Surrey BC made reports of UPO sightings
on 28 Aug 67 and 21 Sep 67 to RCC Vancouver (Annex A).
CFHQ reques-
ted that an officer interview Mr. Wiks and 242620 F/L J Bulloch was
appointed to do so. On the evening of 18 Sep, F/L Bulloch intervièwed
Mr. Wiks and his parents at their home in Surrey.

Position of Sightings
Both sightings were made from the back door of the Wike resi-
dence which is situated 70 feet north of a busy highway which runs
approximately east and west at this location.
The sightings were
both made in an ENE direction towards the Port Mann Bridge which is
visible over the trees and is about three miles distant.
There are
fairly strong lights on a drive-in hamburger stand approximately 150
feet east of the house and on a Higher level.
Sky Condition
On both occasions the sighting was at night.
The weather was
clear with some scattered cloud. The stars were visible but no moon
was noticed.
Description of First Sighting
5-
One sharply outlined solid luminous object about the size of
the full moon but as bright as a plane's landing light was seen with
smaller fluorescent red lights. It first appeared very high in the
sky and came swiftly down to just above a house about 100 yards away
here it hovered momentarily lighting up the area.
It then moved to
the left about 100 yarda and moved away to the right, vanishing as
the light appeared to go out.
There was no sound and the object was
visible for about three minutes.
Changes of direction were instanta-
neous with tilting of the object. (See Annexes B, C and D).
