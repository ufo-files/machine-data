US TYCOON FUNDS CROP CIRCLE RESEARCH
8:20 Wednesday 19 May 1999
American billionaire Laurance Rockefeller is funding research into the cause of
crop circles in Britain, it emerged today.
Scientists will be carrying out aerial research over Wiltshire next month in a bid to
determine whether or not the mysterious pattems are caused by hoaxers.
The 88-year-old Mr Rockefeller, from the famous New York dynasty, is backing
the most extensive study in Britain so far.
His organisation has set up a large database and will be employing leading
authorities on the subject to carry out further studies.
The huge field markings have attracted a range of theories to explain their
appearance, ranging from freak weather, to aliens, to invisible lines on the earth's
surtace.
Many people are convinced the circles are hoaxes - but often the lack of tracks or
footprints leading from the patters is a perplexing factor.
