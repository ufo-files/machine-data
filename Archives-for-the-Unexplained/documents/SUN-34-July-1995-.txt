Dr. Mack/Harvard To Conduct Controlled Experiment On .. Abductees ..

        Harvard University bas accepted a (belated) proposal from Dr. John Mack, its
controversial professor of psychiatry, to conduct a controlled test to determine whether persons
who tell abduction tales are ordinary, run-of-the-mill folks as he bas repeatedly claimed. The
psychological test reportedly will involve 40 persons who report UFO- abduction experiences and
40 control subjects who do not. Although Mack has now worked with more than 90 persons who
meet his qualifications for "abductees" (or "experienccrs"), he himself has bad only four of
them undergo psychological testing, as Mack admitted during a CBS radio interview in early
1994. Mack proposed the controlled test after a Harvard faculty committee began to investigate
his research methodology [SUN #32/Mar. 1995].

other members of the Harvard Medical School will be involved to assure scientifically
meaningful test results. Some skeptics are concerned that Mack might "coach" some of the
"abductees" on how to respond to the test questions.

become a world center for UFO- abduction research. If the tests indicate that some "abductees"

universe" explanation.
