                              SUMMARY ANALYSIS

                 REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSIONS C 47 AND C        1i
       1.  (S) This report provides documentation of two of four
       remote viewing sessions (see also Sessions C54 and C55} conducted
       in compliance with a request from OACSI, DA. This office was
       requested to provide information concerning what was described

SG1A




       2.   (S)  The remote viewer was highly motivated during these
       sessions. He was able to achieve his normal state of relaxed
       concentration and was able to resolve target area impressions.
       The initial information provided the viewer for the first session
       (C47) was geographic coordinates. As the viewer "scanned" the
       area, he was immediately attracted to a circular building which
       may have been in an area later identified as being 3,300 meters
       SWW of the target.   In the second session (C53} the viewer
       seemed to be unable to focus on the target but provided some
       relevant terrain data which appeared appropriate.

       3.  (S)  The protocol used for this session is detailed in the
       document, Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing
       Protocol (S), undated.
       4.   (S) Following are transcripts of the viewer's impressions
       during the remote viewing sessions. At TAB A are drawings made
       by the viewer reference his impressions of the target site. See
       TAB B of Session Report C54 and C55 for available target data.
