  Beer-Sheba, July, 27, 2005

  Yaniv reports, that around 24:00, he saw an object, in 40?-50? above him.

  The object was round, and changed its lights to red-green, like a symmetric ring. The object was shining. It also moved
  a little. At 02:00, Yaniv reports that the object is still seen with naked eye, despite the fact he was using a telescope.


  Beer-Sheba, August, 2, 2005

  Eliyahoo Roizman reports, that at 21:30, he saw something strange in the sky. It was different from any known aircraft.
  It was a big and bright yellow light, with a very high speed blinking light, in its far end. The UFO was far away, and
  flew straight to the south. The object made a turn above the Air-Force Museum, and then disappeared. He reports that:
  “what was strange was its slow movement, without any noise. I’m familiar with RPV’s (remote-piloted vehicle), flying
  balloons and airplanes. This was something different, strange and unusual.”


  Nesher, August, 2, 2005

  I was sitting on the balcony (Abi Greiff), looking to the east. At 18:23, I noticed at my left, a flying object. It was
  combined out of 3 parts. Two dark parts in its sides and a bright part it the middle.
  The bright part sometimes glittered in the sky. The object flew against the wind, to the east. It moved steadily, and
  sometimes it seems to turn around it self. It had a flat shape (observing it from the side).

  The strange thing was, it moved against the wind, keeping speed and its flat shape. If it was a balloon or a bunch
  balloons, it should have a spherical shape, and not a flat shape. The object was no more then 100 meters above the
  ground, and was about 300 meters away from me. I estimate, its size was no more then 1 meter. The object flew away,
  until it disappeared in the Tiv’on direction.

  Yokne’am, September, 2, 2005

  Shawn Shitrit reports: Between 21:00 – 23:00 a few yellow lights were observed in the sky. In the beginning, 3 lights
  were seen, after approximately 2 minutes, they disappeared. A few seconds later, a vertical sequence of 5 lights
  appeared. These lights faded after 2 minutes.

  A few seconds later, 3 lights appeared - 2 located one above to the other, and the last one beside them. These lights
  disappeared after 1 minute.

  The 4 witnesses to this sight are about 17 years old.

  The sight was seen above Megiddo. The witnesses saw the lights from “Ya’ar Odem” neighborhood in Yokne’am-Elit.

  According to the witness (Shawn), the lights were seen also on the September the 3rd, 2005. He intends to videotape
  them.

  This sight was reported to Hannan Sabat (webmaster of our Hebrew site), and published in our web site.


  Haifa, September, 3, 2005

  Yevgeni videotaped, at 01:00, unexplained lights, above Nave-Sha’anan ridge, in the area of the Sfaradic Elderly
  Citizens’ Home, directed to the east.

  There were 3, orange-colored lights, fixed in the sky, forming a wide triangular shape, with 15? angles in the sides, and
  the upper light was in an approximately 120? angle, comparing the other lights. The lights were blinking. Once one lit
  off, then the second, then all of them.

  He claims, the lights were 30 cm height, from his point of view, above the lower-Galilee Mountains. The sight was
  videotaped with a digital camera, and we will see it in our next convention.

  The sea shore, between Atlit and Haifa, September, 9, 2005


  I returned (Abi Greiff) by bus from Eilat. I sat in the side facing the sea. After passing Zihron-Ya’akov, I noticed a
  bright orange light at the north. The light was lower from the urbane lights. I started following it.

  When we came closer to the light, I observed that it lights strongly the clouds above it.

  When we approached south Atlit, I saw that the light moves fast. Its speed was as a jet plane. The light stopped north of
  Atlit, and stood there lighting the sky with its bright orange light. After a minute, it turned off, and disappeared. Two
  minutes later, I saw it again in a northern location. It turned off after a short time, but a cloud, in the area, was still
  lightened. I could observe inside the cloud, a reddish lights spot. Above the sea, the light turned on again, and sent a
  strip of light, towards the sea. The light turned off again after a minute, and appeared again, in front of Tirat Ha’Carmel.
  I saw it for the last time, while leaving Tirat Ha’Carmel station (next to the sea). Then I saw it above the city, fixed in
  the southern sky.

  My observation took place approximately until 21:20. The object was located, all the time, no higher then a few dozen
  meters above the sea, and wasn't far from the shore.
