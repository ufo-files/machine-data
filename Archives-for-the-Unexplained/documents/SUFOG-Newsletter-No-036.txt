             Canadian Ufo Landing        •   •   •   •   •   From The Guardian          •   •   •




     At present, the 'THING' that seems to be rocking the ufo
world, is a piece of film footage of an alleged ufo landing in
Canada.The film,sent to 3 people through the post by someone
calling themselves 'THE GUARDIAN', shows a landed ufo,with what
at first I thought was burning bushes/trees close to it.A short
section of the footage was shown at the Sheffield Ufo Congress
in 1993 by Jenny Rand les. Jenny stated that it was the first
time that she had seen the film. When she said this, I was a
little surprised and smiled slightly. The film was allegedly
shot in 1991 and sent through the post in June 92. ( A small
section was shown on GMTV on the 28th of September 1993 ) .
The reason I had a little smile when Jenny said she had only
just seen it, because I have a copy of the video which I
obtained at the end of 1992.The .copy Jenny showed probably ran
for about a minute or so. The copy I have, featured on an
American news item called 'Hard Copy' and runs for about 10
minutes.
The footage shows what appears to be a landed saucer-shaped
object, with a bright light underneath it. On the top of the
object, is what appears to be a very bright light that blinked
on or off or revolved. To the left of the object, what appears
to be burning bushes or trees.It has been suggested, that it is
flares,marking a landing sight for the object.
To date,! have not seen or read any reports on the footage,and
I would appreciate any update on the footage.
Although the footage looks genuine, the American news item,
suggests in fact that it was part of a film set,being filmed
on location and that it was the film set that was being
filmed.
Who knows   maybe one day, there will be an answer to what the
             •   •   •



film footage really shows,but until that day,it will remain a
'UFO'.
