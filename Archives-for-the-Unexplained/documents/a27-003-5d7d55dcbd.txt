                Large, Slow Aircraft Sighted at Edwards AFB
  From: campbell@ufomind.com (Glenn Campbell, Las Vegas)
  Date: Wed, 26 Mar 1997 20:34:53 -0800
  Subject: Large, Slow Aircraft Sighted at Edwards AFB

  An aviation watcher with close ties to Edwards AFB reported this to me
  around Mar. 10. He is trying to follow up.
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Several workers at the B-2 test facility at Edwards South Base reported
  seeing an unusual aircraft one recent evening. In the dark, it was difficult
  to accurately determine the size and altitude. The witnesses estimated it to
  have been large "like a B-52" and flying "at about 300 or 400 feet abouve the
  ground." It passed over the munitions storage area south of South Base, and
  was flying extremely slow. One witness said he "thought an ordinary aircraft
  would stall at that speed." Another said it was so slow, he thought it was
  hovering. All agreed that it had the standard red and green lights on the
  wingtips, and that it produced no sound.
