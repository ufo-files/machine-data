  Our Synopsis

  Contends that a lost civilization in what is now Wisconsin supplied copper to Bronze Age Europe centuries before
  Columbus. Evidence for this claim is found in pyramids at the bottom of Rock Lake, near Lake Mills, Wisconsin.
