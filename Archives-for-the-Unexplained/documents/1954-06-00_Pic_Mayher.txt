PROJECT 1947 - 29 July, 1952




                                PIC Magazine June 1954




              U.S. Marine photographer's pictures of a saucer in flight

Enlarged approximately 120 times, these are two of the movie frames shown
                                 below.

        The object is traveling in a left-to-right direction at a speed
       estimated by the University of Miami at 7550 miles per hour.

        The physics department also estimated the flying object to
          be 27 feet in diameter, calculated from the movie film.
