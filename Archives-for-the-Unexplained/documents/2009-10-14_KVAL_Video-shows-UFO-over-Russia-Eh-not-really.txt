by Scott Sistek
Wed, October 14th 2009 at 12:00 AM
Updated Wed, October 14th 2009 at 5:00 PM

It sure looks freaky, and many thought it was some sort of UFO.
But I'm betting there's a more natural explanation of this. While not having the data around makes it hard to prove, this looks
suspiciously like a harmless "Hole Punch" cloud.
They are created when a jet flies through a stable layer of super-cooled clouds. While most of the photos I've found on Hole Punch
clouds look a little brighter and sunnier than the video from Russia, I'll bet there's something similar going on in the physics behind it.
