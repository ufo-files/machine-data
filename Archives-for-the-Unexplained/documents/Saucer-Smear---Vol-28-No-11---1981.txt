      Our glorious Fall 1981 UFO Party & Disorganized Get- Together will
occur on the evening of Saturday, December 5th - as usual at our palacial
Headquarters. at 121 69th St., Guttenberg, N. J. Our phone is 201-869- 8053.

      Harry Lebelson has called our attention to what is probably the most
interesting UFO story of the year - buried on Page 35 of the Oct. 26th
issue of the New York Post. In a remote section of Yugoslavia, several
young girls reported seeing "a golden- haired Madonna floating over  a
mountain meadow". ( Shades of the "Miracle of Fatima"! ) Details are not
given, but the national Communist Party expelled 11 people and warned 48
others, apparently because they visited the site of the reported appari­
tion. The government of Yugoslavia fenced off the site so that further
visits would be impossible. We j ust wish we could get the whole story on
this one!


       The 9th annual "Fortfest" ( or Fart Feast, as some might call it )
took place near Washington, D.C. on the weekend of Oct. 17th & 18th, and
once again your humble "Saucer Smear" editor was present.
       This year a new low in poor planning was reached by the alleged or­
ganizers, in that they chose to put an arts & crafts fair into the same
ballroom with the lectures - so that the audience was treated to such
things as medieval flutes in the background while attempting to listen to
the speeches. And, due to lack of publicity, less than 100 of the 500
seats werE: occupied at any time during the 2-day fiasco. Speakers who
had come long distances at their own expense were understandably annoyed.
       Nevertheless, there were some excellent presentations and only a
few poor ones ( due mainly to the fact that the microphone had to be care­
fully hand- held in order for the speaker to be properly heard! )
       The lead- off speaker on Saturday was Jerry Clark of "Fate", giving
a well-thought-out pitch for Forteans to tred the narrow path between cre­
dulity and disbelief. Non- subscriber Loren Coleman gave a run- down on
sightings of kangeroos and alligators where they don't belong ( like in
U. S. city suburbs ) , plus lake monsters and even mysteriously disappearing
clowns. Then came the legendary John Keel who, like a once-great but
aging bullfighter, gave a disappointing performance - miraculously cre­
ating instant folklore as he rambled on.
       Other speakers included Robert Stephanos, whose dubious thesis was
that the ancient city of Jerusalem was actually on the site of the present
city of Edinburgh, Scotland. Joseph Zarzynski gave an update on "Champ",
the currently- popular monster in Lake Champlain ( spelling??? ) , together
with a plea that the creature not be harmed. Non- subscriber Bob Jones
