Dungog Chronicle : Durham and Gloucester Advertiser (NSW : 1894 - 1954), Wednesday 6 December 1950, p



                   STRANGE       LIGHT

            Two          ladies,
                    Dungog        Mesdames     S.
       Hudson,       Eloiza
                       Street,   and D. J. Neil
       son,  Abelard  Street, saw     a  strange
       light in the sky last Wednesday night
       at    7.30    p.m.
            Mrs.    Hudson  and   Mrs.   Neilson
       were   talking in the former's garden
       when   they saw   a peculiar bright light
       which   shone   from    north   to south.    '



       The  light appeared for a few seconds
       between   Abelard and Lord Streets.
