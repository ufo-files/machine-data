      SITU (pronounced sit'you) is a Latin word meaning "place." SITU is also an acro nym referring
      to THE S O C IETY FOR THE I NVESTIGATION OF THE UNEXPLA I N ED.

      S ITU exists for the purpose of collecting data on unexplaineds, promoting proper investiga­
      tion of individual reports and general subjects, and reporting significant data to its members.
      The Society studies unexplained events and "things" of a tangible nature that orthodox science,
      for one reason or another, does not or will not study.
