ICFUFOR Journal files No 44 July 2004


  The Dead-Sea 3.5.2004


  Offir, a citizen of Jerusalem, an air force veteran, drove with his son, 14 years old, from south to north.

  Suddenly, they observed 3 reddish lights, flying at an enormous speed, no more then 100 meters above the sea level. To
  his estimate, they crossed the total length of the red sea in one second and a half. Offer estimates, that their speed was a
  lot higher then a jet plane. Offir says: “at least 7 Mach”. After observing this, they parked next to the road, along with
  more drivers, who also saw the object. Together, they saw a lighted object, bigger then a star, and “at least twice bigger
  then the north-star”. The lighted object turned off after 10 minutes. Afterwards, it was seen in another location in the
  sky. It was located there for 2 minutes, and then it turned off and appeared again, in the location it was seen in the first
  time.

  This cycle happened a few times – turning off and on – in different locations in the sky. When they continued driving to
  Jerusalem, the object was still visible.

  Offer and his son called the mother, while continuing their observation at the white object. She called and reported
  about the observation at 22:00. 15 minutes afterwards, I spoke with Offir (Abi Greif), who excitedly tolled me this
  report. According to the mother, the son was also very agitated from this experience.


  Tel-Aviv 9.5.2004


  Elisha Gilad reports: it happened at 20:40, while driving in south direction at a road near the sea. In front of me, in a
  higher altitude then plane flight, I observed 2 lights – like yellow fireworks, directed towards Jaffa and fixed in the sky.
  It was something shining, brilliant, dazzling and strange. It seemed to be like the 2 lights were connected together to a
  rod. Then I saw a third light turns on, between the 2 lights, in a very bright and shiny yellow color.

  I started the observation from the Sheraton Hotel direction, and observed it for 5 minutes. Finally, everything just
  disappeared. It was located approximately, in the area next to “Jerusalem coast”, towards the sea and high in the sky. It
  was something very unnatural.

  Arava road 13.6.2004


  Liat, 20 years old, from Rishon Le-Zion, traveled along with 4 people in a vehicle, to north direction. Everyone, except
  the driver, were sleeping. After midnight, the driver noticed a strange object in the sky and woke all of us. It happened
  in the road section before Arad. We saw a white object, blinking in the sky and 3 times bigger then a star. Liat says: “it
  was very prominent, different from a star. It seems that its cycling around it self”.

  When they stopped the car, they noticed the object is fixed in the sky. More cars stopped, after seeing the object. Some
  of the viewers called the police, but the police didn't come. The UFO was located east of the road.

  When they continued traveling north, they saw the object for more haf an hour. Liat says: “it was something amazing”.


  Kfar-Ha’im 19.7.2004


  The settlement is located in the north of the Sharon area. Nir, in his 30th reports, that around 21:45, for about 10
  minutes, he observed an unusual view in the sky. Nir, who is accustomed to observe the sky, noticed at first, at a reddish
  light “like planet Mars”, moving slowly from north to south.

  Through his binoculars (30x50), he saw that the object was assembled from 3 lights in a rectangular shape, colored red,
  yellow and white. It was very quiet, and no sound was heard. The lights weren't blinking, and were located very high in
  the sky. To Nirs’ estimate, the UFO flew above the coast line.

  Kfar-Saba 21.7.2004


  Sara Maizlik reports: When I went out to my garden, at 20:45, and looked up to the sky, I saw something blinking, next
  to the moon. First I thought it an airplane, but I noticed there are 2 more blinking objects. I still thought these are planes.

  It was located west to my location, and the objects flew in north direction. Later I noticed, they came closer one each
  other and created a rectangular form, very high in the sky. I saw them for about 10 minutes. Their color was white.
  Afterwards, they disappeared one after the other.
