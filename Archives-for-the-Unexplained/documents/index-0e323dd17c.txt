   Style = Stunning: Compelling literary style: fun and easy to read.

  This volume is essentially composed of two books, each of which would be well worth the price alone.
  The first is a series of fascinating UFO stories, many of which have not been reported elsewhere. The
  second is a lengthy catalog of reported UFO landings between 1868 and 1968. The "Magonia" in the title
  refers to a fairyland which is common to legends of many ancient cultures. Vallee does not dismiss UFOs
  as myth, but see a continuum between modern UFO encounters and fairy stories of the distant past. ¶ The
  second half of the book consists of a chronological catalog of reported UFO landings, with a paragraph
  description for each, along with its source. Clearly, years of work went into this catalog. -- Glenn
  Campbell

                                             VISITORS FROM ANOTHER WORLD

  First published two decades ago, Jacques Vallee's stunning exporation of the evidence of extraterrestrial contact has
  become a classic in the field. An eminent scientist, mathamatician, and thinker who has been consulted by NASA,
  Vallee synthesizes folklore, historical and modern-day eyewitness records, and astronomical research to reveal a
  startling but undeniable pattern of fact -- that throughout history and across widely diverse cultures, there is an
  absolutely consistent tradition of visitations from another world. What that world is called and how the encounters are
  interpreted have been influenced by the prevailing beliefs of each era and place. Yet, as Vallee masterfully
  demonstrates, beneath the superficial differences there is a fundamental similarity of experience that can only lead us to
  breathtaking conclusions.

  Passport to Magonia also contains Jacques Valle's comprehensive catalog detailing the circumstances of nine hundred
  encoutners spanning a period of one hundred years.
