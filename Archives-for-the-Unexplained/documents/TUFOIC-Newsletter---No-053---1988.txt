    24 June, 1947: Private pilot and businessman, Kenneth Arnold, joined in an air

    The 24th of June marks the 40th anniversary of the commencement of the modern
    era of ufology. Since 1947, we have seen governments and military forces
    establish projects to study UFO reports, the rise of civilian UFO groups, and
    assorted commentary, sometimes accurate and sober, often inaccurate and
    sensationalist, by believers, sceptics and the mass media.


    After 40 years of research,           what have we discovered about the UFO experience?
    In a field as controversial as ufology, few absolutist statements remain
    unchallenged. But I would suggest that we have discovered:


    1. Some 90 percent of UFO sighting reports are easily explicable as misidenti­
       fications of various natural and man-made phenomena plus the occasional
       hoax or delusion. Some 10 percent of reports remain in contention.


    2. The UFO phenomenon is real in the psychological and sociological senses.
       People from all walks of life have reported sightings of UFOs. The reports
       involv� the psychological processes of perception and belief,                                            and have led
       to the development of social myths and cult movements.


    3. The UFO phenomenon is real in the physical sense. A subset of UFO reports
       feature as yet unexplained physical effects on the environment or on the
       witnesses. For example,          the multiple car stops of Levelland,                                   Texas,     1957;
       the physical traces at Socorro,                         New Mexico,              1964 and Tully, Queensland,             1966;
       and the severe,          radiation-like,                physiological effects experienced near
       Huffman,       Texas,    1980.


    4. The UFO phenomenon is international in character. UFO sightings have been
       reported from all continents.


    5. The UFO. phenomenon has often been the focus for emotional and belief-based
       debate. It is almost as if the UFO functions as a Rorschach blot on which
       individuals superimpose their own, preconceived beliefs.

 9. As in any field on the margins of science and generating considerable .
    controversy, a number of myths have arisen and been subsequently knock�d

10. The UFO phenomenon, as represented by the 10 percent residue of puzzling

These,   I suggest, are some of the discoveries we have made about UFO phenomena
