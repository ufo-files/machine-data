Abduction by
Jenny Randles (Robert Hale,
$10.95)
Jenny Randles is Britain's only professional UFO
investigator. It's an unusual occupation, and this is
an unusual book. /
Abduction of humas by UFO pilots is, appar-
ently, a common occurrence. Ms Randles recounts
case histories in a (pardon the phrase) down-to-
earth manner. Thousands of people who have been
taken aboard a UFO are not aware of the
experience, due to alien brain-washing, and know
of the incident only as a time lapse in memory.
Although the back cover of the book plugs the
more ribald cases (The Brazilian farmer who was
cosmically raped"),
the book maintains an
unhysterical air which has me more than half
convinced about this phenomenon.
The aliens' reasons for taking humans for a flip
is only hinted at, as no one really knows what they
are up to, but it
usually hinges on a medical
"examination and, fairly often, copulation.
Doppelgangers for a planetary takeover, or mere
medical curiosity / lust? Disturbing, either way.—
