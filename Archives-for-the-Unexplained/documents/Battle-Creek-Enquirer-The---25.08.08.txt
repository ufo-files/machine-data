Classic UFO Cases Program At Marshall District Library…

   Marshall District Library will host Dr. Charles Lietzau as he presents "Classic UFO Cases" to adults on Tuesday, September 9 at 7 pm in the Meeting Room. Space is limited. Registration is necessary. Marshall District Library cardholders may register beginning August 26, and all others register August 29. To register, pick up a free ticket at the Information Desk.
   Of course, UFOs, Unidentified Flying Objects, exist. However, is there any credible evidence that these UFOs represent an unknown advanced technology? Join us as we tour some "classic" cases that offer support for this hypothesis. The phenomenon of apparently advanced flying craft has been multifaceted from its earliest origins. Our perception of it however, has been a step by step linear process keeping pace with advances in science. The  "classic cases" are outstanding for a number of different reasons, such as multiple credible witnesses, public notoriety, turning points in the credibility of the government, and increasing sophistication in both the detection of extraordinary characteristics and the development of our own technological advances for compassion. No one case may present conclusive evidence for the "alien technology" hypothesis. However, taken as a whole, the accumulated evidence is compelling. In addition, when certain critical pieces of evidence are taken into account, the alternatives of misidentification or outright hoaxing often come up short. Why not decide for yourself? Plan to attend this unique presentation and find out why Roswell, Washington D.C., Los Angeles, and Phoenix are more than just place names in "Ufology", how Michigan became notorious as the "Swamp Gas State", and more! Light refreshments will be provided.
   Dr. Chuck Lietzau is a Consultant with MUFON, the international Mutual UFO Network, the Michigan chapter, MIMUFOR, and a Board Member of ICCRA, the Independent Crop Circle Researchers' Association. MUFON's motto is "dedicated to the Scientific Study of UFOs for the Benefit of Humanity". ICCRA proposes that "the data is the only authority". These principles guide his investigations at all times, especially when they cross the frontiers into regions of study generally considered to be "unorthodox".
   Lietzau recently retired from the Detroit Public School system, where he was a High School Biology teacher. Over the years, has been an Adjunct Assistant Professor at several community colleges and universities. Lietzau holds a Ph.D. from Wayne State University, and lives with his wife in Waterford.
