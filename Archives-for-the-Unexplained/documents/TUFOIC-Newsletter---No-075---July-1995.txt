       The Autopsy Film
Many of our readers will be familiar
with    the     events            regarding            the
Roswell        autopsy       film,      as       it    has
received       much         attention         in       the
Press, and on TV and Radio news
bulletins here in Tasmania.

The    information          coming          to        hand
from    the     various         UFO         computer
networks (PARANET, MUFONET &
INTERNET) is very negative at the
moment with regard to the films
authenticity. It has been said that
the film is a Brazilian documentary
made in the 1960's about Roswell.
It has been called a hoax by a UK
newspaper.           It    has       created           and
maintained a raging debate in UFO
circles all over the world.                 There is
no     further        information            on         its
authenticity as at the time of writing.


               OUR POSITION
As the Film is yet to be released to
the public, and has been seen only
by very few people, and has not as
yet been analysed at any known
scientific level,          we are not in a
position       to         comment           on         the
contents        of        the        film    or         its
authenticity.
We have decided that due to the
controversial nature of the film and
its potential        significance,          that our
members         should          be    kept        totally
informed       of     the       events       as        the
transpire.
