Case xxx.
A fireball among the clouds

Time: Thursday, June 20, at 23.17 hours
Place: Stadsgården, central Stockholm

The following report was sent, on June 21, from the F8 air force wing at Barkarby to the Defence Staff:

”Staff sergeant 8163-1-41 J.E.A.Gustavsson reports. To the head of F8. Herewith I humbly report the following sighting made by me on Thursday, June 20, 1946, concerning a light phenomenon. The place of the sighting was Stadsgården vid Katarinahissen and the time was 23.17. The pheonomenon was seen in a northerly direction and was judged to have a course towards the NNE. Angle over the horizon was 20° and the distance seemed very great. The phenomenon moved rather slowly. The light was continous and lasted for about 5 seconds, was in the beginning weak and increased into an intense white light, only to decrease and disappear behind a cloud. Size was about as a star, but the light was much stronger than a star. Barkarby, June 21, 1946. /signed/ Alvar Gustavsson, staff sergeant.

Status: Possible meteor
Source: Letter in Defence Staff files.
