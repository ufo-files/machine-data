                    UFO expert to
                    speak at Goolwa
                  The        world      of   unidentified             flying
                                                                          objects
               will be opened to sceptics and                     enthusiasts on
               February 16, when       UFO   researcher                             Colin
               Norris speaks on the subject.
                  Mr         Norris     is   a     South      Australian            UFO
               researcher,            who    is    often     called      on    to     in-


               vestigate         claimed          UFO      sitings      around       the
               State.
                  Organised           by the Goolwa             National         Trust,
               the talk will offer some insight to the world
               of UFOs   and offer explanations for events
               such    the recent Nullarbor incidents, where
                        as

               a family claimed  their car was hit and lifted
               above the ground by a UFO.
