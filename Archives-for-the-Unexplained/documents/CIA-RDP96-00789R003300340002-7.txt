                         Summary of Information
     The target is located on the eastern shore section of
Maryland just north of the town of ROCK HALL near the
intersection of map grid lines 7/8 and 62/63.
     The tail section of a small airplane is located in this
location.   It feels as if this tail section has moved, or been
moved, from it's original location to that of another.
     The tail section sets in an area of dark blue/black liquid.
There is steep terrain reminiscent of cliffs above this locale.
There is a dark metal object embedded in the surrounding soil or
earth nearby the tail section.   A wooden framed object is lying
on the ground along one edge of the area.
     There is a complex of two-story houses, mostly light brown
or yellow, separated from the target area by a large rolling
field.   Between the field and the target is a series of dirt
piles with rusting metal objects occurring throughout (see page
iii for further geographic/topographic details).
      A large spread-out town or city lies south of the target
area.    There are lots of trees with open spaces between the
structures. There is a gray-blue haze in the air.
     The following words were perceived:           RIVER ... REED; RIVERTON;
RIVERSTON; and REED RIVER.
