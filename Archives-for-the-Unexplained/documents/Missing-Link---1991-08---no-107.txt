                                                              By Phyllis Duran

               A plan to introduce our race to its larger family in a more marked manner was
              presented in his/her(?) letter received by The UFO Contact Center International. It
              was signed "peace and protection" by "Tro", and postmarked in St. Petersburg, Florida
              on June 5. The small group of UFOCCI Board Members who looked the letter over
              after Aileen received it agreed on the high probability of its veracity. A number of
              factors were considered in discussion of the letter, but the word design used by Tro
              was key to the overall agreement on the letter.
               More than showing Tro's keen understanding of our language, linguistics is a
              recurring theme in a large number of contactee cases, both directly or indirectly in the
              contacts. Billy Meier was told by Semjase that we had lost meanings in our language
              and with them knowledge for evolvement; Dick Miller was told that language brings
              one to the door of another dimension; the Mitchell sisters were told that language had
              been confused to inhibit the flow of certain information; Adamski was told that as we
              develop new methods, new language will be developed as well. Language is crucial to
              consciousness by patterning perception, comprehension, and development. A net of
              consciousness is created literally through language.

                Probably the most crucial factor in "massceptancelt sought in Tro's letter is racial identity. We have
              managed to topple "Stability of your stockmarkets, banks, gov'ments, socialtutions, andcetra" all by
              ourselves without any help from "out there". The S&L bail-out now followed by an all-out bank crisis
              could be the straw that breaks the back of that stability, especially considering that the Republican
              administration teeters on prolonged cover-up. The only question regarding economic stability at this
              point is how long before the dust settles. So that's really a moot point, although it is reassuring that Tro
              considered this element of well being. Right about now definition of the problem with banks as little
              guys from far, far away, maybe Mars, would serve Bush well. Alas, the problems with "stockmarkets,
              banks, gov'ments, socialtutions, andcetra" are much closer to home and getting closer.

               "An emergency genetics program which is being conducted to save not only another species,. but is as
              well ensuring the continuation of your geneterial" moves close to the issues massceptance is likely to
              raise, implicit to the purpose of the program, "to introduce your race to its larger family". This is juicy
              stuff, lying at the heart of who we are and why, the identity and beingness that can move universes.
                 Not very long ago, efforts to ban the public teaching of Darwinian evolution as opposed to the
              spontaneous creation of Genesis were carried to the highest court of the most powerful country on the
              planet. The argument still rages, and we still don't know where we came fro� when, or how. There is
              presently a case before the high court to determine first amendment rights where an individual burned a
              cross in his yard to discourage a mixed-race couple from moving into his neighborhood. This couple of
              mixed race likely shared the same language and cultural and national backgrounds. They likely shared
              perceptions, beliefs, and desires. Yet the common ground of human relationship was subjugated to the
              colors of their bodies and handled by torching wood in a yard then battled to a high court.
                Tro wrote about another species; our race and its larger family, and geneterial. The probability of this
              letter being exactly what it claims to be is so high in my estimation, that I am going to assume at this
              point that it is precisely that. As such, then each word and statement was chosen for precision to mean
              exactly what it says. The frrst clue to any mystery is always on the frrst page. In this case, it is in the first
              sentence: "to introduce your race to its larger family". It rings of the Galactic Confederation, who has
              conducted many projects for many different purposes with many names, of which The Confederation,
              Confederation of Planets, Solar Cross and Ashtar are just a few. In the 1950s this Confederation
              claimed to be original colonizers on earth and to have initiated renewed genetic upstepping in the mid-
              1800s, in the generation of our grandparents, and great-grandparents. It shadows the dismay of The
              Watchers with Betty Andreasson Luca that they did not understand the protest of humans at the use of
              genetic material, which was, after all, its purpose.

                H this proves to be the case, my heart goes out to those who have not accepted their neighbors as
              equal in the life of the One Creation, found in awareness of spirit. That will be the only calling card of
              friend or foe in a community of species and races of a larger family.

                                                 COSMIC THOUGHTS

                                                            By Bob Anthony

               There are those who believe that with the expansion of life and experience, birth is given to knowledge
              and awareness. On the same wavelength as this thought, it becomes easy to envision how the third
              dimension burst into existence, when our expanding awareness surpassed the limitations of our second
              dimensional reality.
               The "Big Bang" theory, in all its glory, does a lot to inspire the idea of what it might have been like when
              the universe was formed. The one crucial thing it neglects to reveal is what has been going on since
              then. There have been entire galaxies born; live long flourishing lives, then dies into that dormant state
              where life, some say, does not exist. The beings that lived there long since forgotten. Of course there
              are those whose memories we still carry with us.
                Then there are those in existence today. Their numbers have been counted by a great many who care
              to count them. These figures vary from world to world, but the one thing to be sure of is the number is
              always staggering. For a great many people on Earth these places are simply unreachable (sometimes
              even a myth). There are a few of course, who do not think so. For all intensive purposes here, we are
              going to deal with the latter. There are galaxies in the universe whose worlds are united. Dynasties of
              wealth and peace. The knowledge they have attained, a hard earned victory. Sharing all they have with
              those they encounter on their explorations in hopes of making the union stronger. There are times
              when the sharing is not desired, in which case the cultures are left to do as they will . Now for our
              purposes here, we must narrow our focus down once again.
                The history of the Milky Way galaxy was in full swing when the first observations of time were being
              noted by the primitives on Earth. The Milky Way galaxy is not a united galaxy, with its systems on a
              wide range of experience levels. From those who only believe in their own existence, to worlds who
              have many diplomatic relations with other worlds, to beings who are not indigenous to this galaxy.
                Earth Third planet in orbit around a medium yellow star which many of the natives refer to as The Sun.
                     -



              It is the only planet in the system currently capable of naturally supporting indigenous life forms. Earth
              stage of development, just past the "Denial Stage". Most inhabitants understand there is life in the
              cosmos, but know very little about the identities of just who is out there. Earth is currently being visited
              by those who represent over one quarter of the galaxies indigenous population and at least two groups
              who originally started out in the Andromeda galaxy. All have a variety of reasons for making contact
              with the intelligent life forms on the planet, and are unwilling to risk harm to the delicate balance of the
              planet. Earth is a veritable garden in space. It is a small planet containing large amounts of water,
              plant, and animal life forms. Located in a sparsely populated region of the galaxy. It is a prime location
              for replenishing supplies.
                It's a shame so many of the natives don't realize the prized potential of their planet, and what it means
              to the galaxy. It is hoped that those who do understand will continue to share their light with those who
              do not. When the flame of one candle lights another it is easier to see the beauty all around.

                                                                     'Mystery Circle' Found in Tunnel
                                                                          Sets of concentric rings, similar to those found last sum­
                                                                     mer in British wheat fields, have been discovered in a Japa­

                                                                          Subway officials are keeping the site a secret to prevent
                                                                     throngs of curious people from converging on the subway.
                                                                          Many sets of concentric rings were found drawn in dust
                                                                     that accumulated on the ground and walls inside the tube.
                                                                     The metro version of the mystery circles are much smaller­
                                                                     up to 8 centimeters in diameter-than the British ones, the
                                                                     largest of which measures scores of meters.
                                                                          "Plasma generated in the air is probably the cause in
                                                                     both cases," said Yoshihiko Otsuki, professor of physics at
                                                                     Waseda University, who found the subway rings. He is a re­
                                                                     nowned researcher of fireball phenomena often called "will­
                                                                     o'-the wisp." Otsuki searched for the circles in the tunnel,
                                                                     saying subway tunnels create conditions similar to a plas­
                                                                     ma generator he uses for experiments.

few days on life-support and monitoring devices.

displays, loads of toots and whistles. I mention this because when I finally regained
consciousness, all this hi-tech stuff was the first thing I saw. At that instant, not a
soul was in sight.

taken on board a UFO!" Typical, huh? And then a nurse came into view, totally

floor.)

life. (Most NDErs are told to go back.)

later, in 1988, I divorced from not only a marriage (already marginal) but also, from my
entire life to date. And so I left New Jersey for good (which isn't hard to do if you are
a kid from the hills of Pennsylvania). I set out to travel, and have been travelling
ever since.

almost literally fell into my hands while I was browsing in a South Carolina library.
Soon, the UFO dreams were back but not those I used to have when as a kid I would
wake up glowing from having flown to wonderfully strange places in wonderfully
strange machines. Back then, I thought everybody had such dreams.

                                      WHATS GOING ON?

                                         By George Van Tassel

     You may consider this short narration factual, or fictional; just as you wish. It is based on hearsay from
    an ex-Navy man, and several "projections" into the places described, by yours truly.
      Near a far northern Air Force base, which I am not naming out of respect for government security, is a
    large cavern in a mountain side. The mountain is surrounded by barbed wire and an armed guard.
    Inside this tremendous cavern is a large amount of machinery. Some of it is the creation of another
    advanced civilization on the Earth. Others of these machines are the product of this present American
    people.
      Secrecy shrouds every movement in this area. People working inside the cavern have the finest of
    equipment, lighting, heating, and ventilation. The very latest electronic and magnetic appliances are
    gathered there.
      The cavern is segregated into areas, each having large signs warning the people not to go from their
    own area to any other, under threat of rigid penalties.
      Parts are wrapped and covered to prevent the people from seeing any assembly they are not working
    on. Is this where some of our "missing" scientists are now working?
      In one super secret closed-in area is a "flying saucer". It sets upon a retractable center stand much like
    a lamp base. The entrance to the "saucer" is up a ladder in this center stand tube. On the base is
    stenciled in red paint "Tesla Sphere - P-A".
      Did Nicola Tesla really die, or is he still alive in this cavern?
      One assembly in the cavern looked like the rotor of the power plant of the Venusian scoutship I went
    aboard in August, 1953.
      Everyone in the cavern is living in a state of terrific tension and suspicion of everyone else. Several
    had a high psychic "pickup".
      In another room sits a humanoid under Marine guard. He has an exceptionally large head and large
    golden eyes. He is very telepathic, and is not happy in his confinement. At intervals he is fed and
    permitted some exercise, always under armed guard.
       In the opposite hemisphere is an almost equal condition. In the South Polar Region is another
    entrance to the underground. Surrounding it is vegetation, a mild climate, and warm water lakes. An
    expedition of United States military and scientific men are established nearby.
     A positive polarity fog enshrouds the area. This fog causes people to apparently disappear at a short
    distance.
     Underground is an unknown race of people. They are all that is left of a great race that occupied our
    surface at one time. They have been called by some the "Rainbow" people.
     It is not generally known that most of Shaver's "Deros" are now living on the surface in human bodies,
    preparing "fire and brimstone" for the inhabitants, in the form of nuclear devices.
     The "Deros" are subjects of their master "Demos", often referred to as the Demon, or Satan.
