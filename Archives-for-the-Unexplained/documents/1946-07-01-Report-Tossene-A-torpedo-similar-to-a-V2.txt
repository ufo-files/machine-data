Case xxx: 
Torpedo similar to a V-2PRIVAT 

Time: July 1, between 10.30 and 12.00
Place: 3 km S Tossene, province of Bohuslän 
         
Report on July 5 (at 20.00 hours) to the research officer at the Defence Staff from the officer on duty at the I17, infantry regiment at Uddevalla:

"The officer on duty reports: Sergeant Sigurdsson, I17, has learned, from trustworthy people, the following: On July 1, sometime between 10.30 and 12.00, from a place about 3 kms south of the Tossene church (about 50 kms NW of Uddevalla), a torpedo-shaped object at a constant altitude of 3-4.000 m, on a constantly southwesterly course. The object was fully visible, had small wings and a tube (pipe) on its stern. Looked like a V2-bomb [the description is more like a V1]. No smoke emitted and speed was judged to be about 2-3 times that of a fast aircraft. A strong, humming noise like that of an oil engine was heard. The sound decreased gradually."

Notes: The place of this sighting is on the Swedish west coast. If going to the SW this would have brought the object over the Skagerak, the sea between Sweden, Norway and Denmark. This case is found on the Defence Staff tabulation but no further investigation (as, for example, trying to make contact with the original witness(es)) was performed. 

This case was compared, in a pencilled note by the Air Defence people, to the sighting at Gävle (case xxx), a case which the Staff had wrongly dated as having occured on July 1 at 08.00 hours. Distance Gävle - Uddevalla (Tossene) was about 400 km, thus the object (if it was the same) must have travelled at 170 km/h (provided the Tossene object was seen at 10.30). An equation with quite many uncertainties and a glaring mistake!

Status:  Unidentified.
Sources: Report to Defence Staff. Pencilled note on memo of the May 23-24 incidents.
