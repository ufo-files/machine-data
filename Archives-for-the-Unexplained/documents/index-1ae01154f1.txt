    Our Review                                                                       Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  A collection of newspaper articles for the year 1947 -- considered to be the birth of the flying saucer era. In
  addition to the Roswell and Kenneth Arnold incidents, the popular media virtually erupted with UFO
  stories in that year. The main goal of Project 1947 and this report is the comb through thousands of
  newspapers for interesting UFO stories. Most of the original articles are on blurry microfilm or crumbling
  hard copy. This document provides the retyped text of hundreds of articles. The product of hundreds, if not
  thousands of hours of labor, this document is a valuable resource for anyone interested in that era. -- Glenn
  Campbell

  Publisher's Synopsis

  This compilation sponsored by the UFO Research Coalition surveys the extensive UFO reports documented in
  newspapers and other publications worldwide during 1947. As part of an ongoing research project, nothing so
  comprehensive has been available since the groundbreaking studies by Ted Bloecher and Loren Gross -- studies which
  are now out of print and difficult to find. This study offers an extraordinary look into the UFO mystery as it dramatically
  unfolded in 1947. Spiralbound, illustrated.
