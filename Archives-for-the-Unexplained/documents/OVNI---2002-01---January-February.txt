      A BUSY NEW YEAR AND FULL OF INTERESTING EVENTS
It is often the case that when producing the OVNI, I am faced with either a feast or famine situation in the
matter of UFO reports. Last month for instance, in view of the lack of stories/reports being received, I
reverted to filling the magazine with a tale of an English couple who experienced an Alien encounter in
Australia during 1965. Since then I have been inundated with UFO reports not only from the UK, but
from countries as far away as Australia and New Zealand.
Several of the letters and phone calls that I have received in recent weeks have been as the result of my
monthly 'UFO P hone-In' broadcast on BBC Radio Derby. A number of witness reports tell of very
strange experiences and encounters. Several of these reports have been included in this issue.

  THE SAD PASSING OF A FRIEND AND INTERNATIONAL UFO RESEARCHER
It was only while compiling this months OVNI, that I received the sad news that the well known Spanish
(Catalan) UFO researcher ANTONIO RIBERA had passed away on the 24th September 2001. A letter
received from his daughter Nuria, informed me that her father had died peacefully at the age of 81.
Although I knew of Antonio Ribera, it was only when I moved to Spain in 1986 that I had the opportunity
to meet him. Although I had only been in my residence a matter of weeks, he drove from his Catalan
home to visit me, pursued by a Spanish TV crew who were anxious to interview him. They then
proceeded to carry out the interview in my lounge, as his opinions were much sought after at the time.
At the age of 16, during the Spanish Civil War (1936-39), Antonio found himself driving a small car,
which acted as an ambulance for the Republican forces. He related how distressing it was in only being
able to help the wounded with his meagre supplies of Aspirins and a few bandages, in what turned out to
be one of the bloodiest conflicts of the 20th Century. In later years he developed his interest in UFO's and
was well known as an international author. One book being based upon his underwater survey of Easter
Island. This was an undertaking that resulted in him receiving a prestigious award from the famous
French underwater explorer Jacques Cousteau.
His contribution to UFO research, especially as a consultant toFSR Magazine, will be sadly missed.

                    REFLECTIONS ON A MOTE OF DUST
As the Voyager 1 spacecraft headed out of the Solar System in 1990. it looked back and
snapped a 'family portrait' of the Sun and planets. From beyond Pluto the Sun looks like a
bright star surrounded by a few faint dots. The earth is one of those dots.
Reprinted below is an excerpt from a presentation made by Carl Sagan in 1996 about the
striking image. This eloquent speech reveals how Astronomy has given us a unique
perspective on humankind and our precious home.

                          Reflections on a Mote of Dust
                              By Carl Sagan - Astronomer
         We succeeded in taking that picture, and, if you look at it, you see a dot.
                            That's here. That's home. That's us.
  On it everyone you have ever heard of, every human being that ever lived, lived out their
lives. The aggregate of all of our joys and sufferings, thousands of confident religions, ide­
 ologies and economic doctrines, every hunter and forager, every hero and cowards, every
 creator and destroyer of civilizations, every king and peasant, every young couple in love,
every hopeful child, every mother and father, every inventor and explorer, every teacher of
morals, every corrupt politician, every superstar, every supreme leader, every saint and sin­
  ner in the history of our species, lived there on a mote of dust, suspended in a sunbeam:
 The Earth is a very small stage in a vast cosmic arena. Think of the rivers of blood spilled
  by all those generals and emperors so that in glory and in triumph they could become the
  momentary masters of a fraction of a dot. Think of the endless cruelties visited by the in­
habitants of one corner of the dot on scarcely distinguishable inhabitants of some other cor­
  ner of the dot. How frequent their misunderstandings, how eager they are to kill one an­
other, how fervent their hatreds. Our posturings, our imagined self-importance, the delusion
 that we have some privileged position in the Universe, are challenged by this point of pale
                                             light.
Our planet is a lonely speck in the great enveloping cosmic dark. In our obscurity-in all this
 vastness-there is no hint that help will come from elsewhere to save us from ourselves.
                                        It is up to us.
 It's been said that Astronomy is a humbling, and I might add, a character-building experi­
 ence. To my mind there is no better demonstration of the folly of human conceits than this
    distant image of our tiny world. To me, it underscores our responsibility to deal more
kindly and compassionately with one another and to preserve and cherish that pale blue dot,
                              the only home we've ever known.
                                 -Carl Sagan (1934-1996)

MORE STRANGE STORIES FROM THE BRAMCOTE, NOTTS.AREA
ln the July/August 2001 edition of the OVNI, we published an article and photographs, showing lights
filmed at night by a Mr John Wright in the Trowell area.
For a number of years we have been receiving isolated reports of lights in the area between Trowell and
Bramcote (Notts.) This area is situated between Derby and Nottingham (See map).




There has been some speculation that the 'Hemlock Stone' (see map) could be the reason for the unusual
activity in the area. The history of the stone is uncertain, but it has been stated that it can be traced back to
Pagan times. Whether or not rituals �ere carried out at the site of the stone is unclear.
Yet another report has come to light as the result of the BBC Radio Derby 'UFO Phone-In' programme, in
which your editor participates. The strange details related by 'John' are published below.
Regarding the lights at Bramcote Park. The lights which I have seen during daylight hours, are nothing like
the white light described by the man at Trowell. However, about five years ago, after dark, a white light
was visible at the exact spot close to the 'Hemlock Stone', south-east of Trowel!. I watched for about
thirty minutes through field glasses, went outside to find no noise of an engine. I rang Beeston Police to
ask if local residents had reported anything odd.
The officer sent a Panda Car to investigate, but they saw nothing. The Police rang me later to let me know
that nothing had been seen (2 hours after I had telephoned them). This was too late, as the object moved
off ten minutes after I had telephoned them. It was obviously not a helicopter (no noise) or a balloon, as
the wind was westerly and the object moved off against the wind.
Turning now to the other lights; I have lived here for thirty years and the lights over Bramcote Park have
been visible for the past 2 112 years. They appear at random intervals, always in daylight. I saw them last
on Tuesday morning (27-11-01) at 11.05am. I have seen them so often that I have assumed they must be
man-made, but by whom and to what purpose is unknown to me.
The lights are visible for long periods. I first saw them at 11.05am and they were still flashing and dancing
at 11. 30am. There are 6/7 at a time and they look just like sodium street lights and flash on and off as they
move horizontally and erratically up and down the park, near the Hemlock Stone end. They are low down
and can be seen against a background of trees and are very bright indeed, as they can be seen on the
brightest of days. I cannot understand why nobody has reported anything to the press or radio!
