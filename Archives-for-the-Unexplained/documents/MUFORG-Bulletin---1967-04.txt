both amateur and professional, have laboured for twenty years to find

       There seems to be no reason to believe that this group ��11

if they eventually issue a rather noncommital statement - probably
urging further investigation.

the professionals, If they do this and if their report receives wide
acceptance among scientists, then perhaps the quality of amateur
research will be improved by an influx of people who, up to now,
have been put off by the bizarre nature of the subject.
