    Reports of the mysterious 'Flying Triangles' continue to flood in, not only locally but from locations around
. the world.    Local sightings have been covered in this issue and we have also taken advantage of information
    coming in from other UFO Study Groups and the extensive news featured in the 'Internet'.

    The Internet information must be analysed carefully, as some of the sensational stories of UFO landings etc.
    have been without foundation and some of the stories may be intentional hoaxes and mis-information.

    In early December 1998, the American CAUS organisation began relaying information on its website
    (http://caus.org ) that they had received news of a possible UFO landing in central Arizona on December 7th
    (the anniversary of 'Pearl Harbour'). According to their information, even the 'Art Bell radio show became
    involved, when quoted that a Phoenix FAA (Federal Aviation Authority) warning (unconfirmed) called a
    NOTAM (notice to airmen) had been issued, imposing temporary flight restrictions over a large area of
    central Arizona from December 5 - 7. It was stated that Richard Hoagland had made an appearance on the
    programme and claimed that his 'source' had reported intense military activity in the area of Turret
    Mountain, 30 miles due west of Payson, Arizona, east of route 17. This area is in the exact dead centre of
    Arizona.

    It was stated that Hoagland believes that somewhere under north Phoenix is a time capsule or a set of ruins,
    a buried archive of some sort. The 'Feds' have been tunnelling there since January of this year "looking for
    something". The largest land seizure in U.S. history by the 'Feds' occurred at New River last January. The
    owner has been released from jail and will not get a hearing until next January. In the meantime, he can't
    even get on his own property. It is confiscated. "What are they looking for?"

·   A later message on the CAUS website stated:        As this weekend approaches, we wait and see what, if
    anything will happen. And if something happens...will we ever know about it?

    The CA US message rambled on and what you have read above, is only part of the overall transmission.
    Needless to say, December 7th came and went and as far as I am aware, there have been no further claims
    of impending landings, or military activity. Personally, I feel that it is indicative of a sensational
    'Non-event' and all such information, from UFO groups on the Internet, should be treated with the
    utmost caution and consideration.

    On the 28th January, CAUS members were advised that disturbing reports were coming in from all over the
    U. S.A. of mysterious contrails criss-crossing the sky. Shortly afterwards people and animals were taken ill.
    ''My granddaughter was taken sick, running a fever."     ''We have a lot of people here in Phoenix, sick."
    According to CAUS: The Environmental News Service (http://www.ens.lycos.com) interviewed a Mr
    Thomas Farmer, an ex-Raytheon Missile systems engineer, who has been tracking the mysterious spraying
    incidents for over a year. Farmer confirmed that the aircraft used to spray, are Air Force jets and he has
    collected 'angel hair' materials that the jets are spraying out. The contrail experiments are believed to be
    connected with a military weather weapon development.

    Ed. If these reports are true, they could be similar to the 'Atomic fall-out' tests of years ago, when it was
    claimed the American public were used as human guinea pigs for a Military experiment!

                        "How To Defend Yourself Against Alien Abduction"
                                               by Ann Druffel


                         Published by Piatkus: @ £8.99 ISBN 0 7499 1941 8
                                      Reviewed by Omar Fowler


         Alien abduction is a difficult subject to assess, as in the first instance we are asked to
         accep� that Alien visitations are real.   To members of the general public, the title of
         this book must seem bizarre in the extreme.         Even fellow Ufologists with some
         experience of the abduction phenomenon will wince at the thought of taking evasive
         action against the Alien intruders and yet according to Ann Druffel this has happened
         on numerous occasions.

         There are many questions to be asked about the abduction phenomenon and few are
         answered in this book. It is more a compilation of events that contactee's claim to
         have taken place. The primary question must be, are these "beings" extraterrestrial or
         not? Are they in fact "interdimensional" travellers?
         The Alien intruders often appear to walk straight through a closed window and the
         potential abductee suddenly realises that they are physically paralysed. From my own
         experience, I can recall an abductee telling me that when he awoke to find Aliens
         around his bed, he was unable to move and spoke to them "through clenched teeth".
         The problem of survival therefore becomes a battle of willpower, a "mental struggle"
         that is highlighted in one of the early chapters. It seems that if the abductee can make
         a strenuous effort to move even just a finger or toe, then this will break the spell of the
         Alien's mental hold.    It seems that the Aliens "feed on fear'' and that if the initial
         terror can be overcome, then. the Aliens will depart and visitations will probably
         cease.

         If Aliens are interdimensional then they probably appear before us more as ghosts or
         in hologram form, rather than in the sense of having a physical body. We are told that
         in some cases, the Aliens were seen to "materialise" before the witness's very eyes.
         This possibility makes some claims in the book difficult to accept. In one encounter,
         we are told that a potential abductee attacked a 'Grey' with the result that the Aliens
         head and neck flopped down over its back. Could this be possible, surely it would be
         like trying to attack a shadow?

         The recommended form of defence is described as both "Righteous anger" and
         ''Protective rage". It seems that rather than take things literally "lying down" the
         potential abductee should summon all their willpower to mentally and if possible, to
         physically resist their abduction.    Whether the abductee is about to be transferred to
         an altered state of reality, it is difficult to say, but in one chapter we are told how
         ''they put her back in her physical body!" This raises the question as to whether all
         abductions take place in this manner? We have previously heard stories of, how the
         human victims are taken "through the closed window" or taken up "through the roof
         of the car", only to be returned after an invasive medical procedure has taken place.
         This can only mean that the human body is dematerialised in some manner in order to
         transport it to the Alien craft.   Certainly, abductees have been returned to their beds,
         with physical signs of their abduction, either in the form of "scoop marks"in their skin
         or suddenly being aware that their clothing is in some disarray.
         The final chapters of the book venture into the realms of "spiritual personages",
         "Jinn's" and "altered states of reality", all rather heavy going.    However, overall the
         book makes interesting reading and it is up to the reader to draw their own
         conclusions. You never know, a few of the tips could come in handy one day!
