         F E AT U R E D R E V I E W                                                                  3/10

       A travesty!
       this edition of 'britain's closest encounterts' was frankly a travesty of a programme
       that attempted to pass off a famous possible ufo sighting and landing in north wales
       in January 1974 as being 'poacher's lamps'! Totally one sided in its presentation the
       documentary's makers featured numerous so called 'sceptics' but not a single
       ufologist was included to support the view that a very significant ufo incident occurred
       in north wales that night.


       If the one sided nature of this programme was anything to go by it alas does not augur
       well for the rest of the series, which is set to look at other famous examples of ufo
       phenomenon in the uk such as the rendlesham forest incident, the welsh triangle and
       warminster ufo flap.
