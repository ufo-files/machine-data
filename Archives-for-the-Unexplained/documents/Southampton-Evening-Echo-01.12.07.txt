Lights in the sky
ON Saturday October 20, my hus-
hand and I were visiting friends
who live at Hiltingbury We arrived
at 7.30pm and on getting out of the
car were immediately aware of two
very bright orange lights in the
sky We quickly called our friends,
who watched these lights with us
They were travelling north to
east direction, at a height of
approximately 1,000ft. although it
was difficult to judge accurately.
They were completely silent, and
we watched them for perhaps less
than three minutes before they
gained height and disappeared
They were travelling completely in
unison, at a distance from each
other of about four or five moon
widths.
I phoned the Daily Echo the next
day to ask if anybody else had
reported them, but they had not.
Our friends asked around and
could not find any other witnesses.
Although this happened some
weeks ago, we are still puzzled by
what we saw, and feel sure there
must be a simple explanation. Can
anybody enlighten us?
MRS S ARTHUR,
Bitterne Park, Southampton.
