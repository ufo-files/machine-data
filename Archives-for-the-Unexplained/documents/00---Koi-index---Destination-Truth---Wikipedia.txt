  Guest appearances
  Over the seasons, Gates has built a relationship with the TAPS team from SyFy's paranormal show,
  Ghost Hunters and frequently brings his own ghost hunting evidence to them for review and
  comment. Likewise, Gates has been on their programs, frequently hosting their Halloween specials
  and other live events.
