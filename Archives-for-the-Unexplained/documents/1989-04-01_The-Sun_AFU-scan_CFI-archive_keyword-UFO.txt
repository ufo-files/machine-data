                    By MARK DONOVAN and JAMIE PYATT
MADCAP millionaire Richard Branson caused a massive April Fool
panic yesterday - when he convinced thousands of terrified people
that aliens were about to invade earth.
  Motorists swerved off the M25 in Surrey when Virgin boss Branson
sailed overhead in a hot-air balloon cleverly disguised as a saucer­
shaped spaceship.
  Police stations were besieged with calls from early risers who reported seeing
a glowing UFO in the early morning mist. And two jets were diverted at
Gatwick when Branson's Virgin Galactic Airship was blown dangerously close to
the airport.
