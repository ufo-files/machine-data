The ghost rocket in Denmark

The ghost rocket a common sight
is in these days, as you know
Around the heavens they lively travel
Recently one of them in Denmark
was caught, and found, that
last night it´ was released
by a company sitting peacefully
having their supper, somewhere on Fyn

Poem published by Dagens Nyheter, Aug. 19
