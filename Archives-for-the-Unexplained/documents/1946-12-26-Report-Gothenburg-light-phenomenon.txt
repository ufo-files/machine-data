December 27, 1946.  A "ghost rocket" going for a Christmas visit over Gothenburg  GOTHENBURG, Friday (AT).  A light phenomenon, or more accurately, an object with the same characteristics as a so-called “ghost bomb” was observed shortly after 21 o’clock last night over Gothenburg. The rocket, meteor, or whatever it may have been, passed at high altitude in a westerly direction and disappeared over the urban district of Hisingen.  Reportedly, the characteristic blue-green light had been observed at 21.05 o’clock. It attracted the attention of several people, whose general perception proved very precise: the phenomenon had in all respects constituted a repeat visit by the all-too familiar "ghost bombs", which in turn were observed in droves earlier during this very year, as you know.
