Case xxx.
A white ball over Denmark

Time: First part of July, 1946, at 01.30 hours
Place: Linderupsgård, Faarevejle, Denmark

This is yet another retrospective case, claimed to have occured at the beginning of July 1946 but not reported until 1962. What we have is a SUFOI questionnaire from which the following summary can be made:

Mr. Gunnar E. Hansen was working on Klagesholm [?] out on the Lammefjorden [a bay in the Isefjord bay on Sjaelland]...when I caught sight of the object: a round white luminous ball with a white luminous trail behind. The trail did not remain long after the object had passed. It could remind you of a falling star but it was going to slow for that, and then it flew from horizon to horizon, from the west to the east. It looked similar to Echo 1 [a satellite wellknown when the report was made in 1962]. Hansen estimated the duration at about 15-20 seconds. Weather was clear. No sound was heard. The object had about the same size as a pea at arm´s length. 

Status: Possible meteor
Source: Questionnaire from the archives of Skandinavisk UFO Information (SUFOI) of Copenhagen, dated June 17, 1962.
