Case xxx: 
Oblong object over Kullavik

Time: July 11, 09.08 hours (morning)
Place: Kullavik, 20 km S of Gothenburg (CH32)

"A person who was sunbathing north of Kullavik, some 20 km south of Gothenburg on the Thursday morning, saw, according to GHT (Göteborgs Handels- & Sjöfartstidning), at 9.08 hours a long, silvery object in the sky. The object moved rather slowly towards the south and could be followed for about 15 seconds, before disappearing. No sound could be discerned."

Note: The incident was evidently not investigated by the military since it is not detailed in the Defence files, except for a note in the daily tabulation of sightings with short reference to the newspaper GHT. Without more details it is conceivable that the sighting was of an aircraft.

Status: Possibly aircraft.
Source: TT telegram nr 48 on July 11.       ScanCat 1245.
