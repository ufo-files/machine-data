                           BETTY HILL IS SERIOUSLY ILL




                                    Betty and Barney Hill
                                    (Photo by courtesy ofBetty Hill)
           The two subjects of probably the Worlds first well documented Abduction case.


We have recently learned that Betty Hill, who is now 84 years old, is suffering from Lung Cancer and
has been subjected to extensive chemotherapy for several weeks.

In an appeal published on the internet from Stacey Alien McGee (Alternative Realities Center), fellow
Ufologists were asked to write letters of support to Betty during this difficult time. The PRA duly wrote
to Betty and received the photograph, reproduced above, in reply.

For those readers not familiar with this world-famous abduction case, a brief resume is given below.

                                 The Barney and Betty Hill Case 1961

While returning from a trip to the Niagara Falls in Canada, Barney and Betty Hill were driving through
the 'White Mountains' area ofNew Hampshire when they spotted a light in the sky.
Barney thought that the light may have been a military aircraft of some kind and he stopped the car to
look at the light through his binoculars. What he saw scared him, for there was a craft with strange look­
ing beings looking out of the windows at him. Barney jumped back into his car and the couple drove off
down the road.

It was then that the mystery deepened, for the couple 'lost' two hours of their journey home and like
awakening from a dream they realised that they could not account for what had happened. (It was only
later, when under hypnosis that Barney recollected nine figures straddling the road as their car ap­
proached).

The following day, Bamey felt he had a sore neck, then he noticed that there were some blotches on the
bodywork of his car and his shoes were scuffed. Then they both began to suffer from stress and Betty
had nightmares, in which she was taken into a room and examined by strange beings.
 Page 3                                                                                               OVNI


As a result of the couples continuing trauma they eventually sought medical help and this came in the
form of Dr. Benjamin Simon a Boston psychiatrist.

During the following months they were interviewed carefully by Dr Simon. He finally drew the conclu­
sion that it might be beneficial to the couple if he could take them through their missing two hours of time
by using hypnotic regression.

Over the next few months the couple were hypnotised to 're-live' their two hours of missing time and
gradually a terrifying story was revealed.

Under hypnosis Barney and Betty began to describe how their car had been stopped by a group of strange
beings with "pear shaped heads" and "large wrap around eyes".

Barney described how he began to feel "very weak" and under hypnosis he said "I can't even think of
questioning what is happening to me. I am thinking of a picture (film) I saw years ago of a man being car­
ried to the electric chair. I think of this and I think that I am in this mans position - my feet are dragging."

(Could this have been howBarney 's shoes had become scuffed?)
Inside the craft, Barney told how a device had been used to extract semen from him and how Betty had a
needle inserted through her navel. She was told that this was in connection with a pregnancy test. This
was unheard of in the medical pro(ession at the time and this fact made Dr. Simon sceptical.

It was only in later years that a similar procedure was developed to test the amniotic fluid in pregnant
women to detect 'Downs Syndrome' in the foetus.

It was while Betty was inside the craft that she saw a three dimensional star map of some kind on the
wall. It was made up of large and small dots connected with lines. When she asked one of the 'beings'
what it was, he said that it represented trading routes and expeditions between the stars. Betty was later
able to draw the map from memory, while under hypnosis.

In 1974 in Ohio, amateur astronomer Marjorie Fish, who had been studying the 'Star Map', published the
results of her findings. She claimed that she had found a star system that matched the map, then another
astronom·er found an even better match. However, using a computer, the noted astrophysicist Carl Sagan
compared Fish's map with the original and stated that there was little similarity.

Sceptics said that the couple had misidentified a bright star or planet, triggering off a fantasy. Betty had
shared her abduction experience with Barney, who considered that the event had been real. It seems
unlikely that in view of the emotional trauma experienced by the couple, their experience could not have
been a dream and in fact was truly one of the first well documented Alien abduction cases.

An interesting piece of information came to light later and that was the nearby 'Pease Air Force Bas' had
tracked a UFO on radar that very night.

The 'Hills Case' was one of the first abduction scenarios to be scientifically investigated as is now termed
as a classic case. Yet definitive answers to this and other similar cases reported since remain as elusive as
ever.

Initially Dr Simon concluded that the couple were not lying, hallucinating or suffering from psychosis,
but he later claimed that the episode was an unconscious fantasy.

However, he admitted "There are inexplicable things. I threw many tests at them during many months of
therapy and I couldn't shake their stories and they were definitely not malingering."

When Barney died in February 1976, Betty believed that the interest in them and their abduction would
end. It did not. For years Betty travelled around the World, visiting numerous countries such as Russia
and South America etc. She spent a good deal of time lecturing and taking part in TV spots talking about
the 1961 incident. She also gave telephone interviews sometimes more than 1 20 in a single month.

Betty is a no-nonsense type of woman and even in her advanced age (84) she is quick witted, agile and
has a strong mind for dates, names and facts.    We wish her well.

 SELLAFIELD AS HEAVILY CO NTAMINATED WITH RADIOACTIVITY AS CHE R NOBYL


 9 October 1998

 Amsterdam -- The area around the Sellafield reprocessing plant (UK) is as heavily contaminated with
 radioactivity as the zone around the stricken Chernobyl reactor in Ukraine. This is the conclusion emerging
 from the analyses of soil samples from both are as commissioned by Greenpeace to the University of Bremen
 (1).

 Over the past few weeks the University of Bremen analysed the samples taken by Greenpeace in the area
 around Chernobyl. A comparison with radioactive pollution in the area around the UK reprocessing,plant at
 Sellafield leads to the alanning conclusion that some of the figures for radioactivity at Sellafield are even
 higher than those for the Chernobyl area.

 'Sellafield is a slow-motion Chernobyt, an accident played out over the last four decades', said Mike Townsley
 of Greenpeace International. "\Nhile an area of 30km radius around Chernobyl is prohibited access for people
 and any agricultural activity, th ere are no such restrictions around Sellafield".

 Greenpeace will present these results today in Hamburg, to urge the new SPD/Green coalition Government to
 act now on their long-held policies of opposing continued reprocessing at Sellafield and La Hague in France.
 The SPD and Greens will meet on Sunda y 11th October to discuss their nuclear policy, and it seems possible
 that they will cancel their second set of contracts for reprocessing. They will also examine ways to cancel the
 base-load contracts already signed for an initial period of ten years.
           )
 'The region around the plutonium factory at Sellafield is as similarly contaminated with heavy radioactivity as
 the area around the calamitous Chernobyl reactor in Ukraine" said Heinz_Laing of Greenpeace Germany. "An
 end must be put to this now. The nu clear industry and the German Government share the blame for this
 insidious disaster. Not one gram more nuclear waste should be allowed to be delivered to Sellafield or La
 Hague. Deciding to stop this is the least a 'Red-Green' coalition must do immediate ly."

 People living by the reprocessing plant in Sellafield are today filing a suit against the Federal Export Office in
 Eschborn, which authorises nuclear exports abroad in the name of the Federal Government. The accusation
 made by the complainants says rep recessing German nuclear waste at Sellafield impairs their right to life and
 freedom from bodily injury.

 On the 20th October, the English and Welsh Environment Agency will meet to consider new authorisations for
 radioactive discharges from Sellafield. Greenpeace believes that this latest revelation, combined with the
 commitments made by the UK Government at the OSPAR meeting last July to substantially reduce
 discharges from reprocessing at Sellafield, should serve as a warning to the Environment Agency that levels
 of contamination around the Sellafield plant are already so severe that any further contamin ation presents an
 unacceptable risk to both current and future generations.
