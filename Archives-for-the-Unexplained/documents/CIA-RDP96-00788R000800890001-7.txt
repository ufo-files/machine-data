         1. (S) This report documents a Remote Viewing (RV) session conducted
         in compliance with a request from SOD, J}, OJCS, Pentagon, Washington,
         D.C. The purpose of the session was to provide information relevant to
         the hostage situation in the U.S. Embassy compound in Teheran, Iran.

         raw intelligence data and as such have not been subjected to any inter-

         the information provided is the responsibility of the requestor.
         3. (5) The protocol used for this session is detailed in the document,
         Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing Protocol (S), undated.

         remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made by the remote viewer
         reference his impressions of the target site. At TAB B is target cuing
         information provided the remote viewer.
         5. (S) The remote viewer was asked to locate William B. Royer, Jr.
         The viewer was very confident about his imagery during this session.
         He located Royer, Queen, Scott and three other individuals in building
         E. He located Royer at position A, Scott at position B, unidentified
         individual at position C, Queen at position D, and another unidentified
         individual at position E (see TAB A). The viewer also stated, after the
         session, that the women were located in building F.
         6. (S) This session was cancelled before its completion due to
         excessive ambient room noise.
