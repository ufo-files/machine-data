        14 December 1975
        Sunday Express


        INVISIBLE FORCE HALTED CAR

        Motorist Mrs. Dilys Cant was unrepentant yesterday following a council
        investigation into her strange experience in trying to park her car.                She
        claims she was prevented from reversing her car into an empty space at a new
        multi-storey car park by an "invisible force field. "           But the council who run
        the car park have carried out their own checks and say they were able to park
        without difficulty.    Mrs.   Cant,   wife of an Education Officer,       tried to park
        her car in the new multi-storey car park near the city centre in Durham.                  She
        drove in through the electronically operated entrance ramp after taking
        a ticket from the machine.       But her attempts to reverse into an empty
        parking bay nearby were in vain.       Three times she tried to back her Mini into
        the space - three times she was prevented from doing so by what she thought
        was an impregnable barrier. She said,        "it was as if I had come up against a
        kerb,   but when I got out of the car to see what was stopping me,           there was
        nothing there. It was uncanny.        I have been driving for over 25 years but I
        have never had an experience like it. There was no explanation for it. "
        Mrs. Cant,   who lives in the village of Coxhoe,      Co.   Durham,    was so puzzled
        by her strange experience that she told her daughter-in-law,             Resina,   about it.
        She asked her to try parking it in the same space to see if she could break
        down the mysterious barrier.      But when Resina visited the car park another
        motorist was already having problems similar to those of Mrs. Cant.                Council
        officials could find no explanation for the phenomenon on the ground floor
        parking area.    They claim they were able to park on the spot without any
        hindrance.   A spokesman said,   "It just seems to be one of those things for
        which there is no explanation."       Mrs.   Cant accepted a chance to prove her point
        before the television cameras - she said,        "Unfortunately,      on this occasion,    I
        drove into the parking space without being stopped.         I   would have been happier
        if I had been unable to do so. "       "But I know what happened before was real
        and I   won't go back on one word of my story. It' s the truth - even though
        it does seem to make me look rather foolish. "
