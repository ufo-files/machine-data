dress......                      and an equally ludicrous amount of literature, which often takes the form of
                                 laboriously repeated accounts of the same incident, (area 5 1 , and Roswell
                                 being just two examples), we seem no nearer to identifying what the hell is
                                 going on !
                                         Do we really have to be subjected to continual re-runs of the alleged
                                 Roswell alien story ? Clearly human nature is exercising it 's well worn
    idiosyncrasy : - the desire of certain individuals to climb on the bandwagon, and come up with a more
    entertaining story than the last one. Their motive is to attract publicity, but predominately it is usually
    one of greed. After all, with such a huge marketplace, such stories of alien bodies, whether dead or
     alive, cannot fail to be best sellers.
        In the present E. T. hysteria which has almost become a new cult, Roswell provides the ideal subject
    in which to perpetuate the belief that 'we are not alone' . Any magazine, book, or film depicting the E . T .
    hypothesis is therefore an easy way o f making lots o f lovely lolly for anyone with the inclination to
    produce and market it to an all too willing public.
       Suffice to say, the Roswell incident, whether it was man-made debris, or indeed the wondrous arrival
    of E. T . intelligence (a pity they seemed to have ignored the storm conditions when attempting to
    land ! ), has been most fortuitous for the unscrupulous merchants who squeezed the last drop of
    credibility out of it, simply to line their pockets.
       Ironically, by prompting the story they have unwittingly contributed to it' s ridicule. Such is human
    nature. We see this sad practice in many forms of phenomena. The strange desire to imitate the
    phenomena itself Whether it be crop circles, mediumship, healing, ghosts. You name it human ego is
    there attempting to mislead and confuse those who are seriously trying to study and solve the mystery.
       The media and press are often equally to blame, as they sell on the basis of sensationalism and the
    more outrageous and incredible the story the better.
t      All 'genuine' UFOlogists (and there are many) would do well to be aware of these forces at work
    which only distort the truth.

    a fantasy world, and losing our direction. Sensible research is being replaced and hindered by nonsense,
