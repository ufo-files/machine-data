     In my last article, I discussed the intriguing Maury
Island affair, which occurred just before Kenneth Arnold's
classic sighting of June, 1947, and suggested that, because

been written-off as a hoax.

searcher, has stated that ufos, and their occupants, have
been consistently dropping 'earthly' substances and artifacts
(as opposed to 'out of this world ' materials) all over ou�
planet for years ( 1)

     In this and some later articles, I intend exploring some
of these cases in a little more detail. Interestingly,
various authorities have dismissed many of these cases as
hoaxes too, again because 'earthly' substances were deposited
at sites where ufos were seen and, in several instances, are
reputed to have actually landed.

     In 1964, at an alleged landing site at Glassboro, New
Jersey, U.S�A., a small quantity of potassium nitrate was
found by investigators dealing with the case. For this
reason, the U.S. Air Force investigators discredited the
entire incident, even though there were also holes. in the
ground there, identical in almost every respect, including
size and shape, to holes found at other alleged lan�ing sites,

self, the U.S. Air Force decided that the potassium nitrate ­
commonly used in exp 1 osives, by the. way - was proof of a

     The next case centres at Kuusamo, Saapunki, in northern
Finland, where, in January 197 1, a blinding light appeared

     A systematic analysis of substances left at several ufo

were of a peculiarly 'earthly' character, and by no stretch

     An unpleasant smelling, crumbling black powder was found

Federal University of Minas Gerais, where an analysis, which

showed that the substance contained iron, aluminium, magnesium
and silica. A similar material found at an alleged landing
site near the town of Villa Constitucion contained magnesium,
carbon, aluminium, iron, nickel and copper.

three entities was conducted by the Institute of Technological
Research at Sao Paulo, Brazil, and showed that it contained
6 1 % silica, 1 9% magnesium and 1 1 % alumina.

                 P IONE E R   10   R E P O RTS.

       After a trip of about 2 2 months and half a billion
miles, Pioneer 10 filed its final report on its flyby
mission of Jupiter, and scientists here on earth are still
sorting through the mountain of information sent back.

       One of the crisis points of the mission came when the
Pioneer 1 0 craft passed through the solar shock wave sur­
rounding the planet a day earlier than planned. It also
faced a radiation belt one million times as intense as the
earth•s Van Allen belt. Although Pioneer 10 survived, some
of its instruments did not.

       The craft was far from crippled, however, and N ASA
officials were ecstatic over the spacecraft•s efficiency.
If Pioneer 10 had slipped another 65 000 miles closer to
Jupiter the increase in radiation would have completely
wiped out the spacecraft.

       Among the contributions N AS A officials are already
raving about are the more than 340 close-up photos of the
planet which are superior to any taken by earth telescopes.

       They also found the surface magnetic field of Jupiter
is eight times the earth•s, much weaker than the estimate of
20 times stronger as predicted by experts. It is also the
reverse of earth•s, so that a compass on Jupiter would point
to its south pole.

        Data from Pioneer 10 has already shown that there is
helium in its atmosphere and further analysis of measurements
made of the planet may reveal other characteristics of the
atmosphere. This may be especially important in shedding
more light on the origins of Jupiter and the solar system.
Experimenters also believe that Jupiter•s moons, Io, Europa,
Callisto and Ganymede also have atmospberes, unlike the
earth •s moon.

       NASA scientists also found that there is about a 15° F.

       While scientists are sifting through the volumes of in­

                       By Bruce Cathie

     We remember the New Zealander, Bruce Cathie, a Fokker
Friendship captain, by his well-known book 11 Harmonic 3311,
published in 1 968. In this book, as we may recall, he re­
veals his discovery of the earth•s magnetic grid that governs
many extraordinary world phenomena that were formerly thought
to be unrelated.

     Although Cathie, at the time of writing 11 Harmonic 33 11,
was certain that he was the first discoverer of this power
grid, he is no longer sure of this at all . He and his co­
author Peter Temm point out in their joint new book, 11 Har­
monic 69511, that they have every reason to believe that a
number of scientists already knew of it before Bruce Cathie
did, and that these scientists knew, and know, a great deal
more. In fact, the authors believe in very strong terms
that this group is already in possession of such knowledge
of this grid system as to make them extremely powerful.

     To understand why the book is called 1 1 Harmonic 69511
the reader must brush up on his geometry. The definition
of harmonic is: producing harmony; characterized by harmony;
pertaining to harmony.

     Now in this book, as well as in the previous one, the
author discusses the fundamental harmonies of creative fre­
quencies which form the building blocks (matter) of our
immediate universe .

     Anti-matter, which modern scientists postulate to exist
in the mirror-like image of matter, is, in theory, formed
by the inversely corresponding results of the harmonics of
these frequencies.

     If the authors are concerned to determine the geometric

values, it is to arrive at its inversely corresponding values.
Once this fundamental thought has been grasped by the reader
it is easier to understand the basis of his Harmonics.

      In geometric terms the velocity of the fundamental
harmonic of light is the angular (the circular earth is
360 degrees or 2 1 600 min. ) velocity of 1 43,888 minutes of arc
per gridsecond. The reciprocal harmonic of light, therefore,
is l/ 1 43,888 or 0,000000695. The authors have found that
zeros to the left or right of whole numbers play no roleo
Thus Harmonic 695 refers to the geometric reciprocal of
light which in theory forms the basic building blocks of the
anti-universe or region of negative quality.

     The authors are vitally interested in the harmonic
values of anti-gravity, the speed of light and mass recipro­
cals as they have proved that these values form an integral
part of the U. F. O. grid system. It is from the grid system
that the authors believe the U. F. o •s draw their power.

     Bruce Cathie claims that he appears to have discovered
a harmonic equation which, when rightly applied, could pro­
duce an anti-gravity reaction. In this light it is not
surprising that the standing procedure of the authors is to
immediately forward their discoveries to scientific bodies
and other independent bodies, in order to prevent this vital
knowledge from going with them if anything should happen to
them. There have been several attempts to scare or bribe
Bruce Cathie away from his researches.

     The book also deals with sightings. in New Zealand, all
of which they have related to the existing grid pattern;
also a number of mysterious antennae located in the Auckland
area which, mystery of mysteries, are also located on grid
lines previously mapped by Cathie.

detonated by placing them on a specific geometric point
