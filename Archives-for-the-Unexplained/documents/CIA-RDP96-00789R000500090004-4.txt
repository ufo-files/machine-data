SUMMARY OF INFORMATION:

1.   Describe the target site:     The site consists of a fenced-in area next

to an open, grassy area which is surrounded by woods.     The woods are unkept,

with tall grass and young trees scattered among the older, taller ones.         The

open space is uneven, but appears to be well kept.     The fenced-in area

appears to have at least one or more very small <child-size in appearance)

vehicles.     At least one of these vehicles is bright red and has gawdy

decorations on it.    The vehicle appeared to have a curving, curling

component (see sketch, page 4).     It was shaped almost like a boat (see

sketch, page 5), but was perceived as being on concrete or ground, rather

than water.

2.   Describe the key activity at the target:    At the time of the viewing,

there was absolutely no activity at the site.     People were passing by it,

but ignoring it.     There was not even an SRI "beacon" person present.     Moving

to a time of activity, I saw a child riding in the "boat".
