                           THE RENDLESHAM FOREST ENIGMA
A few months ago the M.O.D. released a file that they had kept secret for over twenty years. The classified
dossier contained 155 pages on the event that affected thousands of people and changed some peoples lives
forever. It concerns the event that took place in Rendlesham Forest, Suffolk, in late December 1980.

The forest is situated between the twin Air Force bases of Bentwaters and Woodbridge which were active
during the period 1942 - 1993.
                                                                                           ·
This event occurred at a time when the 'Cold War' was at its height and the Woodbridge base · was in a
condition of high alert. Armed American Air Force Security Police patrolled the base and were prepared to
use 'deadly force' to protect the aircraft and underground silos which stored the biggest nuclear arsenal in
Europe. It is was with some alarm that security police at the gates, reported seeing lights moving in the forest
on the night, just before Christmas Day.

In a recent television documentary one of the American Airforce Security Police, Sgt. Jim Penniston went
into some detail describing the alarm felt when what appeared to be a crashed aircraft on fire was first seen
in the forest. Sgt. Jim Penniston sought permission to take a jeep and then, with other policemen drove into
the woods. They reached a point where the jeep could go no further and they left the jeep and walked into the
woods towards the glowing object. Sgt. Penniston commented that he could feel his hair raising as though he
was in the presence of an electrical charge. Then as they drew closer, they were able to see a glowing craft
that was so bright it lit up the surrounding trees.

It soon became obvious to Sgt. Penniston as he walked around the glowing craft that this object was not
something made on Earth. Then the glowing craft slowly lifted off the ground, hovered at tree height for a
moment, then in a flash it was gone!

The Deputy Base Commander Lt.Col. Charles Halt, read the incident report the following day, but together
with others, was convinced that there was a logical explanation for the event. Sgt.Penniston said that the
staff treated his report as a bit of light relief However on the evening of the 27th December 1980 'It was
back'. It was then that Col. Halt took a party of personnel to investigate the incident and was shocked by
what he found. (See the 'Halt Memo 'page 8)

The TV documentary later produced a number of skeptics who amazingly tried to explain away the incident
as a light shining from a distant lighthouse. This was followed by Mr Vince Thurkettle, a forestry worker
stating that there was a natural explanation for the 'landing markings' visible in the forest...they were made
by rabbits. Understandably Col. Halt made it quite clear in the television interview, that he knew what he had
seen and it wasn't rabbits or a distant lighthouse. Remember that he was after all, the Deputy Base
Commander and nobody's fool.

What was omitted from the TV programme was the evidence of another prime witness of the UFO landing
incident and that was ex-US Air Force Policeman - Larry Warren. His account of the encounter was not
mentioned in the TV documentary and just to put the record straight, we have included his account in full in
this issue of the OVNI.

                               Tbe Rendlesham Forest Incident
                                             By Omar Fowler


          Larry Warren     was a member of the 8lst United States Air Force Security Police,
  stationed at RAF Bentwaters in Suffolk, during the period 27/28/29 December 1980 when a
  series of UFO incidents took place.         These are known as either the Rendlesham Forest,
  Bentwaters or Woodbridge incidents.

          Together with fellow American UFO Researcher Peter Robbins and Tony J ames of the
 East Midlands groupEMUFORA, Larry Warren             re-visited the site in March 1994.

          Larry Warren takes up the story:-

          1 was unable to reveal a number of aspects of the UFO events years ago because of
  security.   Forget about UFO's, 'they' can snuff that out.      We can be portrayed as either liars,
  fools, or people just out to make money.

          I arrived at RAF Bentwaters on December 1st 1980, after completing extensive air base
  ground defence and nuclear weapons tactical training. We had the largest nuclear arms dum p in
  all of NATO, it was split between the two bases (Woodbridge & Bentwaters). I knew that just
  one of our nuclear weapons could have removed East Anglia with no problem! (Both RAF
  Woodbridge and USAF Bentwaters are now closed).

          On the night of the 27th of December ( 1980) a police patrol observed lights in the
  direction ofEastgate Road, in Rendlesham Forest, it was a tall pine forest at the time. The next
  night further activity took place and that's when I became involved.

          On the night of the 29th of December our security group was called out, we boarded our
  military pick-up trucks and entered Rendlesham Forest.       Colonel Halt and Lt. England were in
  my truck and we also carried a lot of lighting equipment.       We were only able to proceed so far
  before we were ordered to stop in a clearing. There was an 'armoury' vehicle parked there and
  we had to surrender our weapons (which we shouldn't have taken off the base anyway).
  However, a few people still carried side arms and several people carried radio's. We had no idea
  what was happening but we could see a lot of activity down at the end of the road. There were a
  lot of flashlights moving about and a lot of radio transmissions.

          I was in a group of five with a Lt. and we moved through the woods down a path into the
  forest. I could see flares over to my left at about a 100 yards.     There were red surveyor sticks
  and we heard over the radio "we want you guys to avoid those hot spots", to us that meant
  radiation areas. We still had no idea what was going on.

          We walked down this logging road and just at that moment we came across our flight
  chief talking to a young airman sitting on a fallen tree. He sat holding his head in his hands and
  was out of controt very upset. I guess I was in a kind of perceptive shock by this time.

          We continued on our way and came over a rise in the ground, I remember the brambles
  tearing at my legs.   Then we saw this illuminated field, (Capel Green).       The trees were lit up
  yellow and in the distance we could see a farmhouse, with a light on in an upstairs window. This
  'thing' illuminated everything. During my visit to the site recently, it all came back to me. There
  is no way that it cannot, this event changed my life forever.

           In the centre of this field there was a huge light, it was about a foot in height and about
  fifty feet across. It was self-illuminated, you could see through it at times.

           Nearby there were cameras set up, movie cameras and video's.            We were told to move
  into the field and I came to a halt within twenty feet of the object. It was not hovering and I can
  only describe it as a "solidified mist".

           We all stood there watching it, there were security police from "D Flight", there were two
  English 'bobbies' and nearby some senior personnel.          Then we saw the 'disaster preparedness"
  crew move in. They were equipped with geiger counters and they started going around this thing
  on the ground.    Then two cows came up and stood staring at the illuminated mist, we never saw
  them again, I'm not sure what happened. (The cows ran off and were later hit by a taxi).

           Then we saw this small red light moving over the top of some trees at the edge of the
  field.   At first we thought it was an aircraft because the runway is only a mile and half away.
  Then the red light moved in quickly over the trees and approached the field in a downward arc.
  Then it stopped and hovered about twenty feet off the ground. It was the size of an American
  basketball, self-illuminated, not quite red, but that's as close as I can get to it.



           Then just as we were watching it, it exploded and light showered down on this object. I
  was fairly close to this event and the brilliance of the flash affected my eyes.        Some personnel
  ran and disappeared back into the forest and a lot of us were left standing there in a state of
  shock.

           Suddenly in place of the mist, there was now a more solid looking object.          It was about
  thirty feet across at the base and the sides rose up sharply to about twenty five feet in height. It
  had delta appendages that came out of it, I don't know whether they were landing gear or not, I
  couldn't be sure as it was so close to the ground. It had no windows, no markings, no flags, no
  country of origin, nothing.

           Because of the brilliance of the light you could hardly l<;>ok at it dead on, if you looked at
  it from the side of your peripheral vision you could just make out the shape of it. There it was
  and we couldn't move. I felt as if I were in something like a 'dream state', even now I can't
  comprehend the true dimensions of it.

           At this point my sergeant, myself, a disaster preparedness man and my shift commander
  were ordered to move in on this object.       I noticed that when we were standing close to it our
  shadows were cast upon it at an angle.       Somehow this object was 'pulling' our shadows.        This
  effect was strange because we had no lights behind us.       (Once we had got our Iighting equipment
  into the forest, none ofit would work!)
           Shortly afterwards, we were ordered back to our original position.            At that point the
  whole object was surrounded by our security police at intervals of about ten feet, there were a lot
  of personnel out there.

           Then a light moved out of the side of the object and split into three.        In each light you
  could make out what appeared to be individual beings of some kind. 1 saw clearly what looked
  like eyes, facial features, clothing and some other device, but I couldn't make out the legs and the
  lower extremities , it was almost as if the light was translucent at that point

          Then my shift commander then told us to return to our vehicle. We went back over the
  rise and headed back to the trucks. When we reached the parking area, we could see strange
  lights in the sky and these strange blue things moving around. We boarded our vehicles and
  headed back to W oodbridge with our group.

         I was totally 'screwed up' over what I had seen or thought that I had seen.        In fact it
  changed the lives of everyone who was involved in these events. I know about some of events
  that took place at Rendlesham, but I still don't know the complete story.

          According to other witnesses, when I had gone, a smaller triangular object appeared in

         Other witnesses who were at the scene, have told me that a beam then shot down out of

  possible to make out what it was.

          The next morning, hours after the event, I was back at Bentwaters, having coffee with a
  number of the other guys who had been at the incident and we were made fun of by the guys just
  coming on duty on the day shift. We got it all, "little green men" etc. I noticed that my eyes
  were watering profusely and I had a metallic taste in my mouth. My buddy looked at me across
  the table and said "what the hell happened to us?" I started to talk, but our sergeant told me to
  shut up.

 base and telephoned my mother. I figured they were never going to watch that. I got through

   MINI 'ROD' PHOTOGRAPHED IN A HINCKLEY CHURCHYARD
    A report sent in by PRA member Colin Saunders from the 'Hinckley Times 19 December 2002'.

On Sunday December 1st 2002, Anthony Martin and his wife Anne were walking through St. Mary's
churchyard, Hinckley with their three year old son Christopher looking for squirrels.

"We were walking in St Mary's churchyard about 3pm when Anthony took this particular picture of a
squirrel" said Mrs Martin. "Eventually we made our way home and just thought what a nice picture it
was, thinking nothing more of it."

It wasn't until later that day when Mr Martin was downloading the film onto his computer that he saw
something unusual. Magnifying the image, he noticed a strange translucent object in the background of
the scene.

The image reminded him of something he had seen on a television programme about 'Rods', the name
given to the sci-fi phenomenon captured on film during the 1990's. The programme featured Jose
Escamilla, an American film producer whose life changed forever in March 1994, when he recorded
video footage of a strange creature flying in the sky.

'Rods' have since been video'd world-wide and there is speculation that they may be a previously
undiscovered life-form.




Mr Martin has e-mailed the photo to
Mr Escamilla, who verified it as a 'Rod'.

                    STRANGE GLOWING BALL OF LIGHT SEEN OVER
                       CHADDESDEN, DERBY 28th MARCH 2003.

Once again we have a reported sighting of an unusual object over Chaddesden, Derby at 10. 15pm on the
night of Friday the28th March this year.

For some unknown reason this area of Derby is fast becoming a hotbed of UFO activity. Over recent
years there have been a number of reports, including a report of'Flying Crosses' flying over Chaddesden
on the night of the 30th September last year.

The latest report has been sent to us by Mr D. Lever who saw the object, with his daughter ( 10) as it
approached them. Mr Lever's report is given below:

"At 10. 15pm as I was putting my daughter to bed, I was looking out of the window at the stars, when I
noticed an amber/ orange light coming from the direction of the EMA (East Midlands Airport)."

"This was rather an unusual light, as I am used to seeing the aeroplanes landing and taking off, using
their landing/taking off lights, which are a very bright white light. Then they usually turn them off after
a few minutes. ''

"This light stayed all the way and intensified as it came over towards us. As it came very close to our
house, it almost came to a complete stop! At this point we ran outside, me and my daughter ( 10) watched
from the garden, then shouted to my girlfriend and her friend to come and look as well. They can also
confirm what we all saw. "

"There was no sound at all, even at such close proximity to our house!"

"By now my daughter thought it was an aeroplane's engine on fire (her words), but there was still no
sound at all. At this point I tried to take some video, but the camera battery was flat. "

"As I ran out of the house, my daughter was running in and by the time we were both in the garden again
(only a matter of seconds) the light had totally disappeared."

Mr Lever stated that the whole observation had taken place in 7 to 8 minutes, during which time the
object passed "more or less overhead. " His impression was of a very bright amber ball of fire, travelling
fairly fast and watched by the four witnesses before it just "disappeared. " He also stated that none of the
witnesses could recognise it as a "normal flying machine. "

The weather conditions at the time were good, with some scattered cloud, but the stars were visible.

              LASER-LIKE LIGHT BEAM SEEN NEAR TWYCROSS
                                    A report from Colin Saunders
Strange beams of light seen in the sky without an identifiable source often defy a logical explanation. One
such report, witnessed by a Fire Officer Manager and his wife has reached us recently. Although the exact
date is unknown it remains a mystery.

Mr John Elliott and his wife had just left Nuneaton railway station in their car at 1815hrs, in early March
of this year. It was dusk as they pulled out off the A444 at Twycross and headed towards Snarestone on the
B4116.

Mr Elliott takes up the story:

"As we were driving along I glanced to my left and saw what I at first thought was a TV transmitter mast.
I soon realised that it was not a mast, but a thin shaft of light shining down through the low cloud cover."

"On observation, I could see two other shafts of light that were less bright. I pointed this out to my wife
Maureen, who also observed it."

"The lights were several miles av;ay, I would estimate 5 - 10 miles at the closest, across the fields towards
Appleby and the A42. I thought it might have been the Police helicopter and I continued to observe the
                                                                                                    ·



light, without stopping until I arrived home." (Swepstone, Leicestershire).

"I then got out of my car and walked in the dark and quiet to the paddock at the rear of my house. From
there I continued to observe the lights as they moved away towards Ratcliffe Power Station i.e. in a
NNE direction. I could hear no noise at all and the light remained completely vertical at all times."

"I concluded that it was not a helicopter due to the lack of noise and no movement out of the vertical. It
could have been an aircraft, but it was moving very slowly and again, no deviation from the vertical. I
am familiar with seeing lots of aircraft approaching the East Midlands Airport, but they always turn."

being rod-like, with no spreading of the beam at any point.

tubular fluorescent tube and in some instances the light has not reached the ground but appears from the
sky and is 'cut off, leaving the impression of a suspended tube in the sky.


                             MYSTERIOUS LIGHT SEEN OVER BELPER

At about 9pm on the night of the 2nd April 03, Mr John Shelton, who lives at Oakerthorpe happened to
look out of his bedroom window, which has a view overlooking fields in the general direction of Belper.

He observed a yellow light about three times the size of a high magnitude star descending at an angle
roughly 70 degrees from the horizontal, from a height of about 3,000ft in a quite rapid rate. The time it
took to descend was no more than 10 seconds. The light was very bright and transcribed an arc as it
descended.

"I looked for navigation lights but saw none. The light was lost from sight as it went behind a hill at
Heage, which is between Oakerthorpe and Belper. My window faces South and I heard no noise during
the observation. The light was almost golden in appearance (if you can imagine a cross between yellow
and gold.)"

      Jupiter sets before midnight and so early evening is the best time to view Jupiter while it
      is still high in the sky. Jupiter is truly the king of planets for 18 new moons have recently
      been discovered orbiting the planet. This brings the total number of moons orbiting
      Jupiter to 58. This is the last month to observe Saturn, the ringed planet before it
      disappears from the evening sky. The total number of mo·ons (so far discovered) orbiting
      Saturn is 30.


      Mars rises after midnight moving through the star constellation of Capricorn. Mars is    .·.




      brightening all the time as it moves ever closer to the Earth. Mars' southern polar ice cap
      is now pointing towards the Earth. Indeed, much Martian surface detail will soon be
      visible through a small telescope.


      Venus and Mercury are not really good to observe at this time, as both are lost to the
      glare of the Sun. Two spacecraft will head toward these planets soon: the Messenger
      craft leaves for Mercury in 2004 and the Venus Express leaves in 2005.


      Full Moon occurs on the 16th bringing a total lunar eclipse. The eclipse begins around 3
      am and ends around 6 am so be up at 2.30 am sharp: no excuses! The Moon is close to
      the Earth on the 16th so we will see a large Full Moon turn a lovely coppery/orange
      colour. A solar eclipse occurs on May 31st, visible only from north-east Scotland. This
      would be a total eclipse if the Moon were closer to the Earth. The Moon is at apogee,
      farthest from the Earth on May 28, (254 thousand miles distance). The lunar disc will
      therefore appear small when observed from Earth. The lunar disc is smaller than the
      solar disc and thus does not completely cover the solar disc. We call this an annular or
      ring eclipse.


      The first of May has been celebrated as a day of fertility for thousands of years. The
      ancient Celts called May 1st "Bealtaine", the beginning of summer. The 'recent' Celts
      divided the year into eight parts: quarter-days or low-days and cross-quarter days or
      high-days. May 1st is a cross-quarter I high-day. At this time people gathered mayflowers
      (Hawthorne) to decorate houses while singing the song 'Here we go gathering knots of
      may'. The old saying 'Cast not a clout till may is out' refers to the blossoming of the
      mayflower. Let us all go a-maying this year and reconnect with the Earth and the Sky!
