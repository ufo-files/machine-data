Aurora borealis & magnetic problems

Time: March 25, March 28, etc.
Places: all over Sweden

”THE NORTHERN LIGHTS CONTINUE. SUNSPOTS CAUSE SIGNAL PROBLEMS FOR THE RAILWAYS. Stockholm (TT). A exceptionally bright aurora was visible late on the late evening and night between Monday and Tuesday, and the phenomenon was visible from the greater part of the country. It peaked at between 22.00 and 23.00 hours, when a beautiful auroral corona could be seen just south of the zenith. 

”The northern lights are related to the bright sunspots now appearing, it is explained from the Saltsjöbaden observatory. At the observatory a bright sunspot has been observed during the last days and is succeded by other spots since the sun just now is heading for a sunspot maxima. Therefore it is likely that the aurora will repeat during the coming nights. 

”As a result of the atmospheric disturbances no foreign radio broadcasts could be caught even by very good receivers. On short- as well as on long-wave there was a complete silence after the Swedish program was ended. The northern lights also caused problems for the railway´s signal systems. Until midnight observation patrols were out on certain rail sections to observerve the fall of voltage. It happens that this will amount to a few volts, says engineer T. Lundberg, and that stop signals at railway crossings are temporary set out of function. The railway board was noted by the observatory at Lovön and arranged for observations between 17.00 and 19.00 hours and 23.00 to 24.00 hours. No reports had come in so far during the evening, but on previous occasions it seems like a zone accross mid-Sweden was especially affected.” (Norra Västerbotten, Mora Tidning and other newspapers [from TT], March 26)

”POWERFUL AURORA SEEN IN BLEKINGE. A powerful aurora flamed on the northern part of the sky on Monday evening. Rare at our latitude [Blekinge is just north of the 56th parallel] - SSD [newspaper Sydöstra Sveriges Dagblad] noted the last one on October 27 last year - the natural phenomenon caused great attention due to it´s powerful flames, mostly red and green. The light started at about 21.00 hours and was, on the horizon from Svängsta, so brilliant that the northwestern sky was lit like in sunlight. This continued for several hours. At Karlskrona a number of townspeople called SSD to report the light, which was also noted at Olofström and other places.” (Sydöstra Sveriges Dagblad, March 26).

”A NEW AURORA - THE MOST BEAUTIFUL FOR AGES. 

”Evening spectators in Karlskrona could, Thursday evening, enjoy a beautiful scenery in the sky and a number of people, who made contact with SSD, report that the aurora was the most beautiful seen for ages in this area. It took on a grey-green hue and is alternating between long, broad ribbons and winding strands of smoke. The ribbons now and then disappeared only to appear at another spot. According to what SSD has learned the lights have been seen at other places, like Karlshamn, Mörrum and Olofström. It is evident that the whole of Blekinge has seen the show. The magnificient aurora was seen in Stockholm, too.” (Sydöstra Sveriges Dagblad, March 29).

”THE MAGNETIC STORM THE MOST POWERFUL EVER. Wellington, New Zealand the 29th. (TT from Reuters). This weeks magnetic storms, all over the world, which has caused northern as well as southern lights, and trouble with radio and telephone connections, is probably the most powerful ever recorded, declares I.L. Thomsen, director of the Carter observatory at Wellington. Through the storm New Zealand was completely cut off from all communications with the world, and even the radio- and cable connections between the UK and Australia broke down. Over New Zealand an extremely beautiful southern light was visible during the magnetic storm.” (Sydöstra Sveriges Dagblad, March 30).
