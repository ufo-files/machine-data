From Colin Andrews.



It is with great disappointment and with many apologies that 1 have to inform you of the cancellation of
the International Crop Circle Convention to have been held at Portsmouth, New Hampshire, USA on the
8th ApriJ 1995.


There were two primary reasons we organized the convention. The farst was to give the American public
and research community an opportunity to share the research findings and for the front line researchers
themselves to present the best evidence available. The second was to raise funds for future research now
that my own funds have been devoured by the last twelve years investigation.


Unfortunately a number of recent developments beyond our control would have seriously effected the
quality of our advertised program and may have jeopardized even more the valuable funds beyond the
$4,000 loss that this cancellation has already cost.

Some of the factors which influenced my decision are as follows:

*
       A major 1994 hoaxing article written by Gcorge Wingfield and just published in the British
Cerealogist, if true has serious ramifications and could place in jeopardy some of the scientific claims
made by well known scientists.


George Wingfield falls well short of supplying conclusive evidence of his claims that numerous of the well
known 1994 formations were man made. However the story must be carefully and urgently investigated.

•
       Two important speakers have developed health problems which could effect their attendance.

•
       Two large scale conferences have been planned to coincide with the Crop Circle Convention,
including one in which George Wingfield is going to speak.


I therefor felt this was the prudent decision.

I wish to thank you very much for your support and I want to assure you that CPR will continue to reach
for the truth surrounding the phenomenon and wiU keep you abreast of developments in the Newsletter,
published quarterly (See details). We will include in the next issue, what was to have been said at the
Convention by tbe senior British Ministry of Defence official.


                                        Yours sincerely




                                        Colin Andrews
