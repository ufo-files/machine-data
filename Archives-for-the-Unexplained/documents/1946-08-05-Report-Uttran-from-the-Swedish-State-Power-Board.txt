From the Swedish State Power Board
To the Defence Staff

Re: Guided missiles   

The following observation was made on Aug 5, 1946 at 03.14 pm in Uttran.

I am 54 years old and my position is First Administrative officer at the Swedish State Power Board. At the actual moment I was in my summer home at the building site called Norrbyuret 145, together with my wife who is 51 and my son who is 13.

My son who is interested in different types of aeroplanes called our attention to an engine sound which differed from the aeroplanes we are used to hearing.

When I looked up I noticed a white narrow line, approximately in zenith, which was moving at high altitude and with high speed in the direction north-northeast to south-southeast. None of us could, with a naked eye, see anything but the sharp white line, which seemed to move horizontally.

After about one minute when the object was at 45-50 degrees angle (the object - the eye - the horizontal plane)
my son had fetched a pair of binoculars with abt 3,5 x magnification. When I looked through the binoculars I noticed that on both sides of the front part of the white line there was something that could have been wings. I could observe this during about 30 seconds.  After that the line was so distant that the "wings" could no longer be seen.

At the same time, approximately, the line changed to an easterly course and within another 30 seconds it had disappeared due to the distance.

As the line seemed to have the same length all the time we did not think it was an aeroplane leaving "smoke" behind due to athmospheric circumstances. In such cases, as far as I know, a long white stripe can be seen.
This was not  the case here. After the change of course the "line" was still straight. I would estimate the length to 15-30 times its thickness. it had sharp outlines.

The observation time was thus about 2 minutes altogether.

Uttran Aug 7, 1946
