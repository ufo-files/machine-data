    by m0r1arty » Fri Nov 26, 2010 8:34 am

  Since we are all well mastered characters that aren't easily stepped upon but are also sensitive to our reflective practises
  I thought that a thread where we can outright vent might be in order.

  I hope that within this thread we can call out character traits of other members which we find disagreeable and contain
  any potentially embarrassments to our nature here rather than in threads appropriated for specific learnings.

  This way we may approach each new puzzle with a fresh mind stripped of our personal vendettas or bias towards other
  members.

  The concept is that if we are brutally honest about our feelings here then we shouldn't need to be whilst we are studying
  the individual aspects of our research and musings.

  I've found critically constructive feedback upon my character to be most esteeming and worthwhile. It's certainly a good
  thing to hear how a like-minded individual views you across the pane of the internet and allows for ponderings that are
  otherwise inaccessible.

  Please - whilst venting and explaining do have the goodwill to keep within the terms and conditions of this site.

  This is a playground and as such is for insults, put downs and goading.

  Please give it as much attention as you would in your adult life.

  Pick your fights but know that all fights are in themselves silly.

  I hope this works.

  -m0r
