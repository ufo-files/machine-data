Karlstad
Military district 
Staff dispatch

Defence Staff,  Air defence  unit,  Stockholm

Hereby  I most respectfully  remit a copy of report from frontier guard B G Högfeldt, 
Bottner, to  frontier custom-house, Karlstad.  Abbreviated report in the same matter
have earlier  been submitted by  Headquarter First Command, West.
By telephone interview with in report mentioned Paul Edvinsson, tel.no  Strömsjön 14,
have come to light, that the object have not landed (crashed) on Swedish territory.
Inquiries will be made with the people of the borderland.
Karlstad, august 15, 1946
On command
A N C Sjunnesson
Captain
