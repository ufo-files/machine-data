Case xxx.
A crash in the waters off Kvicksund?

Time: May 28, between 16.30 and 17.00
Place: Kvicksund, a sound in the lake Mälaren

This case was found on a few pages of handwritten notes in the Defence Staff files. It was never listed on the tables of cases regularly produced by the Defence Staff, nor is there any documents of any investigation. Maybe the case was investigated and immediately explained, we don´t know. The notes seem intriguing, however. 

From the notes it seems that on May 28, between 16.30 and 17.00 an unknown witness had observed a flying, metallic object come in over Kvicksund at low altitude. The witness heard engine sounds from the object, which then crashed into the water. 

This is what can be rendered from the few words on a slip of paper. In the summer of 1987 Anders Liljegren and Stig Aggestad attempted to trace the witness via an article on the ghost rockets in Folket, a local newspaper. No result.

Kvicksund is only about 50 kilometres to the NE of Stora Mellösa, the scene of the next case, possibly the first of all cases investigated by the Defence Staff which had any quality.

Status: Insufficient information
Source: Defence Staff files; Folket, July 27, 1989.
