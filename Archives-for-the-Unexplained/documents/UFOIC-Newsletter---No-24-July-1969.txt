  UFO Spotlights Boat
  On the evening of November 22nd, 1968 the shrimp boat "Gulf Central" was searching for a
  missing vessel "Bobby Charles" lost in the dense fog �arlier in the day off Biloxi, Miss-
  issippi.     On board the "Central" were Preston Mallette, his son Kenneth and the skipper's

  SPAIN

  Triangle Evades Jet
  Hundreds of people created a chaotic traffic jam for an hour in Madrid when news of an UFO
  was flashed over radios at 7.30 p.m. on September 5th,last year.              The Spanish Air Force
  sent one of its supersonic jet fighter bombers, an American built F104 to investigate the
  report, but after chasing the object to 50,000 ft. gave up the pursuit.              Air Force radar
  screens had tracked the UFO to 90,000 ft. and astronomers at Madrid Observatory also saw
  it through a powerful telescope which photographed and revealed it to be a triangular­
  shaped object.       Despite detailed investigation and the statements of other pilots wh�
  witnessed its presence the "triangle" still remains unidentified.               (Cr. FS   Win. 63.)
