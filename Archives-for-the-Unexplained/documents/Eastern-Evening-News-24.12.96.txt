EASTERN EVENING NEWS (NORWICH)
24 DEC 96
Page S
TWO police officers were left mystified after
tracking a UFO for 10 miles over Norfolk.
And air traffic controllers at Norwich airport can
offer no explanation for the luminous, oblong
shape with red, white and blue lights that darted
across the sky.
Sgt Steve King and PC Andy Coller, based at
Fakenham police station, followed the fast-mov-
ing shape from Holt before losing sight of it as it
hovered over Cromer.
The officers' colleagues called Norwich Airport
to find an explanation for the phenomenon.
An air traffic department spokesman said: &ẠIt
certainly wasn't anything from the airport. It is
recorded in the book as a possible UFO sighting."
Sunday's experience is the latest in a line of
unexplained sightings over Norfolk and its coast.
Just two months ago a tanker crew reported two
sets of strangely coloured lights a few miles off
the East Anglian coast.
The incident was investigated by Ministry of
defence officials who said it was caused by a
combination of the 200ft tower of the Boston
Stump church and an electrical storm but UFO
sleuths scoffed at the explanation.
Colleagues at Fakenham police station said the
two officers were taking the sighting seriously.
