• Loch Ness:     I have a number on clippings on Nessie, some general and some referring

and the Clansman Hotel. Teacher Rhona Hare:     "It was about.12.15pm on Friday the
18th. We spotted perhaps four humps and had a clear view for a bit and could
still see through some trees but then we lost sight of it." Later the same day,
Ms More said, a coach party had also seen the monster Pupil Sandra MacKenzie was
among the five children to see the humps close to the shore. Two girls sitting in
front of the bus claimed to have seen the head and neck of the creature above water.
"At first I thought it was stones or something but because it was moving, I knew it
was Nessie."      On Sept 28, 92, according to the Inverness Courier of Oct. 2, Nessie
was seen again, this time at the Clansman Hotel. Brian Alexander, kitchen porter
at the Hotel who saw the thing for 20 minutes:     "It was a dark object and looked
sort of barrel shaped. I thought at first it might be a rowing boat but I soon
ruled that out."     Brian, 41, one of the other witnesses, who also saw the object
about 300 yards from the south side of the Loch:     "As it started moving slowly to

• Lake Michigan:    Well, according to the Weekly World News of Nov 17, 92, pp. 1 &
4-5,    the "U.S. NAVY CAPTURES MONSTER I:N LAKE MICHIGAN". A very unconvincing
doctored photo of two divers and a very reptilian/horror-movie-like giant snake
in the water is on the tabloid's cover.       The inside story tells the 140 monster
was caught on Oct 22 by a secret Pentagon crew. The Navy had the thing on sonar,
tracked it for 6 months, and finally caught it in "a massive steel net" in the
northern basin.     "The monster looks like a dinosaur but there are several impor­
tant    distinctions. For one thing,    dinosaurs were exceedingly stupid but this
lake monster apparently has an intelligence rivaling that of a dog or a horse.
It also has lungs and gills, which enable it to breathe in or out of water at
will.    One of the more intriguing aspects of this case is why nobody has reported
seeing the monster before", a -certainly non-existent- marine biologist Richard
Clarke has declared.      Secret documents show that the Navy once managed to track
the monster on sonar for 72 hours before it surfaced three miles west of South
Fox    Island.   Why didn't cryptozoologists never hear of these exiting discoveries?

"The Pentagon is definitely trying to keep a lid on this. But a story of this
magnitude can't be kept under wraps forever"!

• WHALES:   In Oct, about 100 pilot whales stranded in New Sealand. Circa 50 died
immediated1y� while helpers tried         to drag them back into the sea.   (Frankfurter
Allgemeine Zeitung, Oct 28, p.12d)         At the end of Nov, 26 pilot whales stranded
in the NE of Tasmania. Animal protection helpers told the press that over the
year 1992, a total of 254 whales had been killed in strandings in Tasmania.
Biologist Hans Wapstra said the school of 26 whales had stranded 24 hours before
their discovery. All 26 died, 7 had to be shot.        (Berliner Morgenpost, Nov 26,
p. 44)    At the end of Dec, some 90 pilot whales stranded in New Zealand (RTL
plus TV news, 1845 hours, Dec 28). I have noted in the past Bilks that whale
strandings seem to concentrate on Tasmania, New Zealand, Perth in Australia,
Oregon and Massachussetts. Still, no scientist has been able to discover why
these usually intelligent animals kill themselves, often even refuse to be
towed back to the sea aoain.
