  Under Nevada's public records law and the reasoning outlined below, I hereby request from you the latest copy of the

                                MEDIA LIST FOR NEVADA'S EXTRATERRESTRIAL HIGHWAY

  This is a document of at least three pages listing newspapers that have run stories on the E.T. Highway and the column
  length of each. Also listed on this document are organizations which are planning to cover the E.T. Highway. The
  document was apparently produced by your office.

  Would you kindly fax this document to me immediately at 702-795-0487. I agree to pay any reasonable expenses
  associated with this request.

  I feel justified in making this request because the state has already provided this list to my commercial competitor, the
  Little A'Le'Inn. It is my contention that the state has been promoting only this single business in connection with the
  E.T. Highway, at the expense of any others. As the owner of a retail business also located in Rachel, I believe I am
  entitled to equal access to information.
