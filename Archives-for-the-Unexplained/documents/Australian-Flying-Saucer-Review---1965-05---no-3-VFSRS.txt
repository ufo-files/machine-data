MOST RECENT SIGHTINGS - Victoria :

          On the 25th April, two men
pruning fruit trees at Gruyere, (two miles
north of Seville, Vic.) saw what appeared
to be a circular metallic object flashing
in the sunlight about two miles east over
the Yarra Valley.

          The light appeared against a
backdrop of mountains and was visible for
several seconds. Before disappearing
behind a hill, the object changed to a
hemispherical shape and became a dull
grey in color.
