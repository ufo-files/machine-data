SUMMARY OF INFORMATION:



     a.   The general site area was in darkness at the time of the session

(daylight here).    It is an area of fairly high altitude, with a barren,

rocky, somewhat mountainous appearance.

     b.   In spite of the barren, isolated aspect of the general area, there

are several structures involved in the immediate site area.     I could not

distinguish how many buildings were involved, but the number did not look

small.

     c.   The actual target is a structure located amid the others.        It has

both an above-ground and a below-ground component <See Fig. 1).        The above-

ground area was well lit from the inside, with many windows.        However, I did

not detect much activity in the upper portion, at the time of the session.

There was activity in the lower, underground portion of the structure, and I

was drawn to that location.

2. (S/NF/SK)    Just as I was moving to investigate the lower portion of the

structure, there was a power failure <in the session area, not at the target

site), and completion of the session had to be postponed until later.
