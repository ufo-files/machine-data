Mutual UFO Network to hold Ely meeting to discuss recent sightings near here

Due to recent reports of unidentified flying objects by concerned citizens of Ely and surrounding areas in White Pine County, the Mutual UFO Network (MUFON) will be coming to the Prospector Hotel this Saturday, November 14th, 2009 to give a free public presentation about MUFON and discuss the area's recent UFO sightings. 

The presentation will be given in the convention hall at the Prospector Hotel Saturday evening at 6:00 p.m., doors open for seating at 5:30 p.m.  This presentation will be given by Mark Easter, who is MUFON's Nevada State Director as well as MUFON's International Director of Public Relations. Mark is also a MUFON Certified Field Investigator and will be conducting an on-site UFO Field investigation the entire weekend in Ely and surrounding areas.  "Due to the increased amount of recent UFO sightings in White Pine County, including video evidence recorded by a credible witness, we feel it is important to get boots on the ground to do a comprehensive investigation" says Mark Easter, "and we are looking to talk with anyone who may have witnessed UFO activity in the area and has yet to report it."  Senior News Reporter Ed Pierce from KOLO TV News Channel 8 will also make the trip to Ely this weekend with a camera crew to follow Mark Easter during this MUFON presentation and UFO investigation. If citizens have witnessed UFO activity in White Pine County, you are encouraged to attend this important free MUFON seminar on Saturday night. Mark Easter will be available all weekend to talk with anyone who has witnessed UFOs in the sky and has not yet reported it.  To find out more about the Mutual UFO Network (MUFON) you can visit us on-line at  HYPERLINK "http://www.mufon.com" www.mufon.com.
