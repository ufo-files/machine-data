County Hotspot For UFO Sightings…

   Shropshire is one of the country’s UFO hotspots with more than 20 sightings in the county over the last 40-odd years, it was revealed today.
   The research, conducted by Virgin Media, saw the county achieve fourth place for the most UFO sightings behind West Yorkshire with 34 sightings, Nottinghamshire with 29, and Lancashire with 23.
   The Virgin Media Files were compiled from data going back to 1961 and highlights 22 unexplained sightings in Shropshire alone.
