                      A      Video                       Has          Puzzled                            Us
    On March        15,   2005,        the    daily       sports paper              Tokyo Supotsu              (Tokyo       Sports) carried a
    large article about a video of a luminous body that                                             apparently nearly had                    had a
    midair collision        with a         passenger          plane,       a   Boeing        777-300 of            :Japan      Airlines.
    This spectacle was captured by Mr.                              Kiyoshi         Amamiya           (1944-),         one    of the        most active
    UFOlogists      in current :Japan,                   at Sugimoto-cho,                 Tenri City,            Nara      Prefecture,            on
    October     23,    2004.
         At 17:24     of that day,            fine       weather,        he started to video the jet plane flying
    from the east to the west                   at an         altitude of           about       2000 meters.               His digital video
    camera      (Canon     FV1),       to     which he         had attached               a telephoto lens to get larger
    images,     tracked      the       plane.       Before         long a      small reddish bright light came                              into the
    view of the finder from                   the    direction        of    movement             of the plane,               and at 17:25
    (these time are expressed                  in minutes;            to   be exact,             about 30 seconds after the
    start of     his tracking) the light apparently passed                                  by,      or touched,             the left wing
    of the passenger plane.                  Mr.     Amamiya tracked                   the      light,     and zoomed in                on    it.
         This near miss may be                an     optical        illusion,        because no one               knows       the       altitude       of
    the   object.     The    movements of the                      object,       also,     is    unknown;         Mr .      Amamiya           used     no
    tripod,     so we can not distinguish                      actual      movements from                      apparent ones            (according
    to the impression he received,                        the object was almost at                         the same position).
         Whether    it changed          its    position        or not,         it    changed         its appearance one                  after
    another:     broadly     speaking,          firstly,           two joined          round        lights leaning           to the         left;
    secondly,      a round      light;        lastly,      two joined round                 lights leaning to                the right.
    This object,       if these lights              and some feeble lights                      belong to an object,                    is very
    bright,     though the photos               below         may give you no such impression.                                And dark
    structures      of this object may                   be   false images made                     by bright objects;               such
    phenomena       are common in               video images               (in      1991 one of such               false images that was                     on
    a star,     Venus,      made a :Japanese TV                     news program                think that         this      object was           a dark
    UFO reflecting          the sunlight).
         About three minutes after the start of tracking                                        the plane,         at      last the object
    was missed;       the   video camera seems to have become unable                                             to    adjust the focus
    automatically (according to Mr.                           Amamiya,           the      object got smaller).                 He kept         an eye
    on the finder         not   to     lose    track;         he   did   not        see   the       object      with    the    naked         eye.
         If one object shows only                   character that earthling-made objects                                  can have,           the
    object is not a         UFO -            this    is my         hypothesis.            But   I    have yet to decide                 about this
    video.
