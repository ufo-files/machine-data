       September 23, 2002 - Primera Hora, Puerto Rico - Chupacabras Reappears in Ponce, Puerto Rico - Has a
royal feast in Ponce- Luis Varela-- The Associated Press- "Even the dog is scared . Something weird is happening,"
said Ramon Luis Guzman, who showed some large tracks which he believes are those of the predator. - PONCE -
The story of the so-called chupacabras, the shadowy character that attacks and slays animals, emerged again in Ponce
when 19 fighting roosters, rabbits, ducks and chickens turned up dead at a residence in the La Calzada neighborhood.
       "Thi$ is weird . I think it was the Chupacabras who slew my animals. I'm scared and alarmed by what occurred," said
the animals' owner, Ramon Luis Guzman Colon, to Associated Press (AP).
       Some of the animals were found yesterday morning by Guzman Colon outside their cages and others still within
them . A rooster was dragged 30 meters from its cage and was still agonizing on Sunday afternoon. Four of the fighting
