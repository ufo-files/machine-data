Tracking the asteroids or Near-Earth Objects (NEO's) has been a significant ep.deavour for NASA and
the astronomical community. So far 10731 known Near-Earth Objects have been discovered.

NASA is in the process of developing an Asteroid Redirection Mission (ARM). ARM will be the first

     ARM plans to use the new ORlON Spacecraft and Space Launch System Rocket and high-power solar
     electric propulsion to capture an asteroid in the near future.


     In developing ARM, NASA is developing two concepts to robotically capture and redirect an asteroid
     mass into a stable orbit around the Moon.


     In the first concept NASA will capture and redirect an entire asteroid. In the other concept NASA will
     retrieve a large boulder-like mass from a larger asteroid and return it to the lunar orbit.


     In both cases, astronauts aboard an Orlon spacecraft will then study the redirected asteroid mass in the
     vicinity of the Moon and bring back samples.


     NASA discovered a new potentially hazardous asteroid named 2013 YP139 on the 29th December
     2013. This was confirmed by NASA on the 15th January 2014.


     It was fust discovered by NASA's Near-Earth Object Wide Field Infrared Survey Explorer
     (NEOWISE) . The researchers at the University of Arizona used the Spacewatch telescope at the Kitt
     Peak National Observatory southwest of Tucson to confirm the discovery.


    The 2013 Y P139 is about 43 million kilometres from Earth. On the basis of infrared brightness it is
     found to be 650 metres in diameter and extremely dark, like a piece of coal. NEOWISE's sophisticated
    software picked up the moving object from a background of stationary stars.


    The asteroid circles the sun in an elliptical orbit tilted to the plane of the solar system. It is possible for
    its orbit to bring it as close as 482803 km from Earth. A little more than the distance to the Moon, but it
    will not come that close within the next century.

                 In 2009 Richplanet produced "Silent Killers": a documentary
                 exposing the truth about animal mutilation in the UK, and
                 attempts by authorities to cover up what is happening.
                 Animal carcases including horses, sheep and cows have
                 been discovered regularly having undergone surgical
                 procedures, where blood and organs are cleanly removed.
                 The phenomenon is difficult to explain in conventional terms,
                 and has been linked for decades with UFO's, because
                 strange lights are sometimes witnessed in proximity to sites
                 where animal carcases are found. A question people ask
                 when they hear about animal mutilation is whether human
                 beings have suffered similar attacks. This film attempts to
                 answer this very controversial question. Rumours about
                 human mutilation have circulated the UFO community for
                 years with some people claiming to have had contact with a
                 military group which is used to acquire the corpses of victims
                 of human mutilation. In the film we reveal the testimony of a
                 black ops soldier, who was employed by a secret NATO "find
                 and secure" military team. Their job is to search for and then
                 protect sites where human mutilation has taken place until
                 another team arrives to remove the evidence. A staggering
                 claim revealed in the film is the level of advanced knowledge
                 NATO has of UFO activity, and technology used to intercept
                 UFOs in attempts to bring them to the ground. The "find and
                 secure" team would be sent out to locations before UFO
                 activity had taken place, indicating NATO has some sort of
                 advanced intelligence about the behaviour of UFOs. Our
                 source also claims NATO has technology with the capability
                 of immobilising UFOs which it has used to acquire them.
