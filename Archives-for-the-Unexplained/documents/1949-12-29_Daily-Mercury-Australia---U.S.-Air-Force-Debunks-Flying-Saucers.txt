Daily Mercury (Mackay, Qld. : 1906 - 1954), Thursday 29 December 1949, page 1



              U.S. Air Force
                      Debunks
                          Saucers
               WASHINGTON,          Dec.      28.—
             Flying saucers        were        Just
             jokes,      hysteria,
                      mass                or  mis
             understanding     of          natural
             phenomena,      the United     States
             Air   Force said   to-day after
             two   years' investigation.
         .
             It has ended       a   special flying
        saucer     project established to in
        vestigate the multitude            of     re
        ports about        alleged sightings of
        saucers.      The project was         set up
        in January, 1948.
           The    announcement         of its final
        report served       as   a   denial of the
        story published by 'True' maga
        zine at the week-end, which said
        the saucers       were     real and     were
        from     other planets.
           The     Air Force        announcement
        said all      the    evidence     Indicates
        that reports of unidentified flying
        saucers    are the result of first, mis
        representation of various conven
        tional objects;        second,      a    mild
        form of mass        hysteria, and, third,
        hoaxes.
