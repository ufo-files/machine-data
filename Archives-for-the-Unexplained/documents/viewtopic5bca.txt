  I`m just going to take a chance and throw this out there and see what becomes of it, if anything at all. All I
  ask, is for you to consider the hypothesis below.

  Reconstructions in certain situations can be very valuable. The video above, is a clip from a Japanese t.v
  program that was based on the R.F.I, and which featured reconstructions of various parts of the three
  nights events.
  The reasons I`m posting this are twofold. I believe its the only halfway decent reconstruction of Larry
  Warrens account, and also because something occurred to me as a result of seeing the event played out in
  Live action. Go to 6.36 seconds in the video timeline to see the begining of the reconstruction of Larrys
  event, and then read on!
  When I watched the clip for the first time, what I saw, was something close to what Larry has always
  described seeing, and what I have always imagined the scene must have looked like, to the people
  involved, based on the descriptions given in L.A.E.G and other places, however, when I watched it a second
  time, something odd happened. I saw it all unfold again as Larry had described it, but this time, I saw the
  scene play out in a slightly different context to the way I`ve always imagined it did, and indeed, to the way
  it was meant to be depicted in the video.
  We are all familiar with the details of what happened during Larry`s encounter, he has described events as
  clearly as he can and as close as possible to the way that he perceives them to have occurred, however
  I`m sure I`m not alone in saying that I`ve always thought there was something missing from the situation
  Larry describes. One thing in particular that has always bothered me about the event, is the fact that, at
  face value, there doesn`t seem to be an obvious purpose behind the UFO landing, and in fact, the entire
  encounter, including the face off with the entities, seems to leave a big question mark, as to just, what was
  the point of the exercise. It seems to have served no purpose!
  No purpose that is, unless you go back and look at what initiated the event, the yellow mist on the ground!
  Let me explain, we think of Larry`s event, as being a "UFO landing", but what if, in actual fact, it was not
  solely a landing, and the description of the basketball object hovering over the yellow mist, and then the
  explosion of light, resulting in the triangular thing, is actually a description of the initiation of a containment
  situation, instead of an actual landing. The UFO is actually a containment device, for whatever the mist
  really was, be it nuclear material or technology, be it ours, or theirs. Whatever that yellow mist was on the
  ground, it was the reason the UFO came down on top of it. With this hypothesis in mind, watch the video
  above and cast your mind back to how Larry has always described the event, from how it started, to how it
  finished.
  Although we don`t really know what the mist was, or what was emitting it, this hypothesis would suddenly
  make sense of the entire event. What kind of Nuclear material would react the way the it was described
  and give off a yellow mist, when mixed with other substances, or upon contact with the environment?
  Maybe it wasn`t our "stuff" at all!

  "Eventually, people circled the triangular craft. There was Security Option 3 that is in the event of a nuclear

  I heard that the base news agency got wind of what was going on out there in the forest and went there

  smart move on the part of whoever ordered it. However, taken in the context I`ve described earlier, the
