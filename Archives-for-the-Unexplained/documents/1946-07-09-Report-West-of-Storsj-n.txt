Phone message from Constable Lund, Hallen.			14-15

9/7

On July 9, between 2:00 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. Mr. Lund made observations of a strange airnborne object west of Storsjön in Jämtland.

The phenomenon was seen in a south-easterly-easterly direction and passed obliquely downward toward the horizon from west to the east.

It was described as a bright white light followed by a luminous streak in slightly different colour. Most likely a shooting star.

Received by telephone on July 11, at 9:25 o’clock.
S-E Sjöstedt
Officer of the day
F 4

Telephone message from Alfred Månsson, Högsta, Arvesund (western shore of Storsjön in Jämtland).
At the time of his most unusual observation, Månsson had occupied himself at his farm in Högsta, Arvesund.
Point in time: 2:00 p.m. - 3:00 p.m.

The phenomenon was observed traveling in the direction of Storsjön and seemed to plunge into the water just to the right of the southern tip of the Gåsje islet. The last part of the track was blocked, however, by shrubs.

The phenomenon consisted of a bright light, like a sharply falling star, followed by a brilliant streak. It seemed to move irregularly and remained silent during the entire incident.

Received by telephone on July 11, at 13:50.
S-E Sjöstedt
Officer of the day
