                     FIRST OFFICIAL FOG-FIGHTER RECORDS DISCOVERED

       In January 1992, CAUS launched an effort to locate documentation
 relevant to the Foe-Fighter phenomenon of World·War 2. This has led to
 the first release of unit records giving specifics on these incidents.
       For those unfamiliar with what Foe-Fighters were, we will explain.
 During the later part of World War 2 pilots on both the allied and axis
 sides reported seeing strange balls of light flying with them while they
 were engaged in bombing and air defense missions in the European and
 Pacific theaters. The balls were generally about a foot in diameter,
 bright, of varying colors and able to maneuver with ease in and about
 aircraft formations.
       Word of the phenomenon leaked to the· press in ·December 1944 and
 became widely known in America on January 2, 1945, thFough numerous
 newspaper reprintings·of a wire service story summarizing pilot des­
 criptions of what they saw. In the press coverage it was assumed that the
 balls were Nazi secret weapons. However, they did not seem to affect air­
 craft in any way, except to be a nuisance. Reports persisted until the
 end of January 1945, when the activity abated, though not completely.
 Further mention of Foe-Fighters (which in fact was a nickname given to
�the strange balls by pilots of the 415th Night Fighter Squadron, based
 upon a frequent phrase used in the "Smokey Stover" comic strip of the day)
 disappeared from the press until December 1945, when the American Legion
 Magazine carried accounts by pilots of their experiences (see ''The Foo
 Fighter Mystery'' by Jo Chamberlin, American Legion Magazine, Dec. 1945).
 From then on the Foe-Fighter reports surfaced much after the fact in various   ·


 UFO publications over the years.                                  .

 appeared in declassified Air Force Intelligence files from 1947 and 1952.

 had contacted the Air Force to advise them of their experiences with Fan­
 Fighters in the hope that this would somehow shed light on the flying sau­
 cers being si�hted. One record, an April 23, 1952 letter from Lt. Col.
 W.W. Ottinger of the Directorate of Intelligence ' s Evaluation Division,
 said that an evaluation of Foe-Fighters was done at the end of the war.
 It was concluded that there was nothing to the phenomenon at all, though
 to this day the study has not been made public.

             AQUARIUS-- A new age of understanding- or just making waves?




It was back in the late sixties when the song 'Age of Aquarius' from the musical 'Hair' frrst

hit the record shops.! must admit that it was that song that made me wonder what the Age of

aquarius really was.It wasn't untill a few years later,however,when I first became very interes­

ted in Astrology that the real message of the song began to sink in.If memory serves me

right,one of the key phrases from the song was "Harmony and understanding".

That really seemed to sum up the whole idea very well. (I must get a tape of that song to

remind me of all the words!)

You are now probably thinking "Yes,O.K., but what has a pop song from the sixties got to do

with what's happening in 1993?" Well, the first thing to explain is that the song is trying to ex­

press in musical terms the approach of a new 'Great month' in the 'Great year' of Astrological

theory.

Now the 'Great year' theory is based on a phenomenon called the 'Precession of the equi­

noxes'. This phenomenon was first discovered by an astronomer who lived in ancient Greece

beetween 190 and 120 B.C. His name was Hipparchus of Nicaea,and he found that due to a

slight wobble in the earths rotation, the star constellation lying behind the sun at the time of

the vernal equinox was     changing very slowly. And he found by his calculations that this

change would occur throughout the centuries.He further calculated that this 'Great year' was a

total of 25,868 years divided up into 12 'Great months'each of about 2,000 years.According to

this theory,the 'Great month' now drawing to a close is the Piscean age.The arguably most

important aspect being the rise and establishment of Christianity;- with the two Piscean fish

pulling the course of human history first one way then the other.War and peace,great artistic

and musical achievement contrasting with bang,crash,wallop and a pile of bricks at the Tate

gallery.From inspirational and beautiful architecture to hopelessly inadequate blocks of con­

crete monstrosities called tower blocks.

The Age of Aquarius,however,promises to be a very different kettle of fish (if you'll pardon

the pun!) Humanity and harmony are the keywords of the incoming age.This,if the theory
holds true, will affect our whole lives and the way we run our affairs from the highest to the

lowest.

From national government to local joint action commitees.Even the prospect of world govem-

meht by consensus is within our grasp. Science and technology will become more user friendly

and tailored to our actual needs instead of just proving how clever we are.The new emphasis

will be on caring for each other and being sincerely concerned for the sick,disabled,and the

elderly. I' m an eternal optimist and hope to be still around to see the start of this marvellous

transformation.

                                                                Richard Servante
