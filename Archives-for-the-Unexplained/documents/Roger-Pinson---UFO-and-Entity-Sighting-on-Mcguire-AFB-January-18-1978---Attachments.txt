Dear A1C Bartohn Hall,

Thank you for your quick response to my previous fax. Below I have included those specific points
that you stated are necessary for the FOIA request.

I am an investigator for the National Institute for Discovery Science, (NIDS}. NIDS is a privately
funded, non-commercial scientific research institute dedicated to the study of aerial phenomena and
related topics.

I recently received information of a UFO and entity sighting witnessed and reported by Security Police
that occurred at McGuire AFB in January 1978. In addition to fonnal reports of investigations by the
Security Police and the Air Force Office of Special Investigations, (AFOSI), I am requesting the daily
blotters generated and maintained in Central Security Control, (CSG). and by Law Enforcement, (LE).
I am requesting these documents under the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. 552. It is
unknown exactly when in January this occurred, so it will be necessary to study all 90 or so blotter
entries for each of the CSG and LE shifts respectively.

The alleged facts of the case are as follows:

Occurred in January 1978.
During the hours of darkness.
Between 0300 hrs and 0500 hrs.
Numerous sightings were seen over McGuire AFB and Fort Dix.
Army MP's and New Jersey State Patrol were pursuing a UFO up to and through Gate 5 on McGuire
AFB.
AFOSI and a team from Wright-Patterson AFB responded and took the body of the entity.

It is my understanding that because The National Institute for Discovery Science is a non-commercial
scientific research institute, there will be no charges. However if there are any fees, we will be
happy to pay.

Thank you for your help in this information request. Please feel free to contact me, Investigator Roger
Pinson, or Deputy Administrator Dr. Colm Kelleher with any questions.
