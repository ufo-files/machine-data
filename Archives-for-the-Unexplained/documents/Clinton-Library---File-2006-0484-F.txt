Dear President Clinton :

        My father, Dr. Jules Davids, would send you his greetings for the New Year if he
could , but he has unfortunately been ailing in a similar way as former President
Reagan . So I send you best wishes on behalf of the whole Davids family. We all
support your re-election campaign with gusto and hope for a new House and Senate
brimming over with Democrats.

       Since hearing from you as President-elect, on Dec. 9, 1992, after sending you
some of our Star Wars books, I went on to be executive producer and co-writer of a
major film about the Roswell Incident that was nominated for a Golden Globe for Best
TV movie of 1994. In fact, it may have been partially responsible for rekindling the
controversy about the Roswell Incident which sparked some official inquiries in
Washington . It has since been released around the world in many languages, and
was also widely presented by the U.S. Navy to its servicemen and servicewomen .

        Thus, when you recently made a statement about the Roswell Incident during
your trip to Ireland, I felt moved to write to you . Your statement concluded with the
words:"/f the United States Air Force did recover alien bodies, they didn't tell me about
it either, and I want to know. " I am therefore enclosing for you :

      (1) The movie, "Roswell, " , starring Kyle Maclachlan , Martin Sheen and
Dwight Yoakam , followed by a brief documentary with witness testimony (105 min .)

       (2) "yo/den Anniversary UFO Briefing for the White Sands Missile Range
Pioneers" This is the keynote banquet speech I was asked by the founders of the
U.S. rocketry and missile programs to present about Roswell and UFO's to a group of
about 200 military men and women celebrating the 50th anniversary of the founding of
White Sands. I appealed for a more open policy but defended the rationale that
national security may have compelled secrecy on this issue for decades. (1 hr)

      (3) "Down in Roswell" This is a compendium of media and news coverage on
the Roswell case through the years, which I host. It includes the original ABC radio
broadcast announcing the military's capture of a flying saucer. (1 hr)

      (4) "Reply to the Air Force Report on the Roswell Incident" This is based on a
speech I gave in the aftermath of the release of the A.F. Report in 1994. I took the
theme 'The UFO Coverup Continues ... " in reaction to the flaws and bias of the report.

       The Government Accounting Office Report revealed that all the key documents
from Roswell that could have answered our questions were destroyed about forty
years ago without proper authorization. Your recent declassifications included troop
deployments from as long ago as World War I. People wonder why the much more
recent Roswell data was destroyed , i.e., communiques from a key World War II base,
whereas insignificant documents from 1917 survived. Regardless of whether my
assumptions of long-reigning secrecy are well founded or misguided , I concluded that
the Air Force Report on the Roswell Incident contains glaring errors, ignores key facts
and testimony, debunks and attacks the motives of investigators, authors and
Hollywood film producers alike in its seemingly transparent attempt to "spin" the issue.
The result was that it was lambasted by those it was intended to satisfy.

         Polls now indicate that about 50% of the population believes that some UFO's
are extraterrestrial spacecraft, and many also believe that some agencies of
government know this to be a fact and may be withholding hard evidence that proves
it. This is not a small minority that holds these opinions, nor does a majority still
respond by ridiculing or trivializing the issue. The media has had its influence. In fact,
no one in public life seems to have fully assessed the schism beween media and
official government positions on this issue, which bears upon public trust.

        You set a bold and much-appreciated precedent in revealing , at a strategic and
appropriate time, through the Department of Energy, the facts about experimentation in
past decades involving radioactivity. You have been moving the public in the right
direction on almost all issues, and I applaud you for your bold leadership and standing
up for what is right. I would look forward to continued public developments on this
UFO issue, as Laurance Rockefeller and others of stature, including astronaut Gordon
Cooper, now publicly recommend . In your letter to me of Feb. 12, 1992, you raised the
possibility of tapping my talents in some area. i would like you to be aware of my
desire to be of service, whether as a writer or consultant or public speaker on this
issue as it relates to media and government, to support any developments toward
preliminary disclosures, assuming that there is in fact anything to be disclosed .

      Thank you , Mr. President, for taking the time to read this letter and for
considering the point of view expressed and the material in the videotapes enclosed .

Dear Ms. Ritter:

       Thank you for offering, when we spoke today, to provide the contents of this
package to Nancy Hernreich, for the personal and confidential attention of President
Clinton. As I mentioned, my father, Dr. Jules Davids, was President Clinton's professor
of American history when the President was a student at Georgetown University, and I
have received prior letters from President Clinton .

        I am sending some of the past correspondence from President Clinton in hopes
that it will help in the process of receiving Presidential attention for this communique.
I've enclosed a copy of a letter from then-Governor Clinton to my father dated April 8,
1987, then-Governor Clinton's letter to me of February 12, 1992, and then-President-
elect Clinton's letter to me of December 9, 1992.

       In addition to my enclosed letter to the President, I am enclosing four VHS
videotapes I have produced, including the movie "Roswell," starring Kyle Maclachlan,
Martin Sheen and Dwight Yoakam. All of these bear upon a public statement
President Clinton made on his recent trip to Ireland involving the famous "Roswell
Incident" of Roswell, New Mexico. Also enclosed, attached to the letter: a New York
Daily News article and an excerpt from the Sharper Image catalogue.

      The other three tapes are: "Golden Anniversary UFO Briefing for the White
Sands Missile Range Pioneers," "Down in Roswell," and "Reply to the Air Force Report
on the Roswell Incident." All the tapes are described in my letter to the President.
Please check to make sure they did not undergo any X-ray damage. I will of course
send additional copies upon request. Thank you for your assistance and
consideration.
