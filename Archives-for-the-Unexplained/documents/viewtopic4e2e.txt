  Just out of curiosity I collected John’s statements made on this forum over the years and put them together
  in order to reconstruct his view on the events of the first night. Enjoy .. (and note that John has been very
  consistent !)

  First sighting of lights, radar contact, order to investigate

  “First night went on from around 3 to about 5.”

  “And there was nobody on duty at the east gate Steffens and I were on patrol in a police car when we
  went to check the east gate and we then saw the strange lights”

  “Nothing was flying that night the runway was shut down and there was not even any maintenance people
  working it was Christmas night. The air traffic control tower was not manned there was only a weather
  person on duty had just been up in the tower area before the incident had happened i.e. that was one of
  my checks. The 67th area was shut down nobody on duty there had not been anybody coming through the
  main gate heading out to the air field all night.”
