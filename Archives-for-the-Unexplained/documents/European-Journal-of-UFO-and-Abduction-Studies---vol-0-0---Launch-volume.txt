Abstract:      Ball lightning sightings by casual eyewitnesses are often neglected by researchers. Yet any
theoretical model has to consider and account for those described features. The present difficulty is that
such sightings are rarely reported to scientific researchers. An effort to build a catalogue of alleged ball
lightning reports in Italy has recently been launched by a small group of BL researchers stimulated by the
Italian Centre for UFO Studies, which offered to open its files to interested scientists to look for pertinent
data. A first survey of more than 1 2 ,ClXlltalian reports of unusual aerial phenomena brought a sample of
40 possible ball lightning phenomena in the last 50 years. A more thorough search is presently being
conducted, that will yield an expected total of 1 00 reports. A second important source is the impressive
collection of ball lightning cases published by lgnazio Galli in the early years of this century. All such reports
are presently being filed into an "Italian Ball Lightning Archive" and a computer database is also being
implemented , containing space-time coordinates [date, time , latitude , longitude , altitude) as well as
descriptive [colour, size, shape , behaviour) and environment [meteorological , topographical) data. Similar
projects have already been attempted in Austria and Hungary, and some more national catalogue have also
been produced for countries [Russia, Japan) with very different ecology/geography/climate . A second
planned step will thus be a comparison between the Italian database and those other ones.


1 . Introduction
The study of ball lightning [BL) has been receiving a great deal of attention by a more and
more increasing community of scientists, recently. That is clear· from a number of very
good contributions on this subject given in literature [ 1 -7]. On the basis of these
investigations a very large body of both BL sightings and experimental BL manifestations
have been carefully documented and classified in scientific reports and catalogues [8-27].
These important works now constitute a sort of "model of sample" to be used to compare
new sightings of BL collected by other researchers .
A catalogue and file of ball lightning reports in Italy has been recently proposed by some
researchers and sponsored by CISU [the Italian Centre for UFO Studies), as both of financial
support and of a large database and bibliography.
In this paper, a project for a database of BL observations in Italy [BLITA) , organised with
the material of the above file, is presented and compared with the most important existing
projects on this subject. BLITA is the first project devoted to BL ever proposed in Italy, and
it is the result of both a pre-existing collection of BL sightings by some members of CISU
and a collaboration with some Italian physicists that have been stimulated in participating

                                                    Page -6-
in such investigation .
I n the next Section, the description o f the preliminary BLITA database is given i n terms of
some physical parameters, such as space-time coordinates environment data, etc. Special
attention is given to "observational parameters", such as month of BL observations,
daytime, place of observations, colours, dimensions, decay of BL, etc. Furthermore , the
description of the sources of BL sightings chosen for the database is given as well. The
project will be carried out in three main steps, with different time scales. Section 3
describes the first two steps, the first one being a check of the database internal
consistency and the second step a comparison between preliminary BLITA reports and BL
reports from different sources. In Section 4, preliminary conclusions and remarks are
given, and future perspectives, which represent the third step, are advanced. Beside
comparison with similar catalogues of other unusual aerial phenomena, it will mainly consist
of the selection, among the preliminary set of BL reports, of those that will constitute the
actual database for deeper analysis.

2. The preliminary BLITA database.
The preliminary catalogue of alleged BL reports in Italy is presently collecting 1 25 reports.

2.1 Sources and database structure
Our initial sources used to build BLITA were the following :
- a first survey of CISU case files, presently comprising more than 1 2 ,000 Italian reports
of unusual aerial phenomena brought 30% of cases;
, - a search of scientific articles, literature and newspaper stories from CISU archives took
to another 1 0% of our sample;
- the impressive collection of BL cases published by lgnazio Galli [28] in the early years of
this century [see Appendix) contributed 60% of the preliminary database.
All the above reports are presently being filed into an "Italian Ball Lightning Archive".
A computer database is also being implemented, containing space-time coordinates (date ,
time, latitude, longitude, altitude) as well as descriptive (colour, size, shape, dimension,
behaviour) and environment (meteorological , topographical) data , plus some information
about witnesses , sources and a short summary of the described event.
Fields in the database structure are similar to those used by Smirnov [26] and Smirnov and
Strizhev [27] concerning data bank of BL observations in USSR , as composed by A. Ju .
Strizhev. Such a choice on our part is meant to allow a better comparison between those
two files.
Since we are dealing with casual sightings of unexpected phenomena, descriptions of some
parameters may indeed be affected by subjectivity. But the more reports are collected, the
greater is the probability that we get a detailed and accurate image of the physical
phenomena behind the testimonies.

2. 3 Effects and damages
A special attention is to be given to quite "odd" effects reported in some Italian BL
observations. Space prevents us a more complete discussion, so that we only offer three
examples in brief.

                                         Page -10-
         1 3 October 1 878, at 1 2 , Casarano [Lecce): all the water [more than 1 5 litres) in
         a boiling pot was sucked away. as well as all salt, pepper, oil and vinegar
         disappeared from a dinner table , with no damage to containers.
         August 1 896, at 1 4 . 30 , Frascati [Roma): a ring was extracted from a woman's
         finger and was found i n another room.
         1 September 1 979, at 2 1 . 3 5 , Torre del La go [Lucca) : gardening scissors were
         extracted from a woman's pocket, hovered for a few seconds in mid-air, then fell
         to the ground.
An interesting fact, rarely reported by other authors, is also emerging from the Italian
database: damages to things or to peopl e , though usually of low intensity, are reported in
38% of cases.
l gnazio Galli ( 1 9 1 4 , p. 286) wrote the following about BL damages [28]:
"lt would be of supreme importance the question about the probability of danger that may
come from lightning globes to health and life of man and beasts. In my [international]
catalogue of more than 720 observations, most of them reporting news about the place
and effects, I find 3 5 1 i nstances of one or more globes, and other rare forms, skimming
along, touching or assaulting one or more people, one or more animals, or also exploding
amidst of or near to them. Wel l , in only 71 cases, i . e. for one fifth of the total , we are
reported immediate killings or subsequent deaths or very serious wounds or other durable
lesions (paralysis, imbecility, organic infections, blindness, deafness). The other 280 cases
were either totally innocuous or caused minimal surface damages (burnings, scratches,
darkening, hair destruction, brief fainting, wearing laceration or kidnapping). lt must be
stressed that 1 86 of such non-damaging globes, i . e. more than three our of five , took
place in interiors. "
Only six cases are known of people killed by ball lightning in Italy, the most recent took place
at Alpignano (Torino) on March 3, 1 969. At 1 1 .45' a. m . , two workers were planting wood
signs for a new road. Nine metres above them ran 6 high tension (220 kV) cables.
Surveyor Franco Ghignone was 200 metres away: "I was coming away from them when I
heard a loud explosion. I turned back at once: two fire globes seemed wobbling between
the ground and the cables. I was blinded and I closed my eyes. When I opened them again,
where there had been the workers now was only burning grass. The two men had been
launched several metres away, and they were screaming and writhing". Local hotel owner
Lorenzo Cerutti was another witness: "I was on the door when I heard two detonations and
I saw the fire globes coming down from the high tension. They were very bright, of an
intense blue colour. After touching the ground they got up back to the cables and followed
them for about ten metres, finally exploding i nto sparks." The victims were lying on the
ground, their flesh smoking , the wearing in flames. One died instantly, the other was
severely burned.

3. Preliminary data processi ng
As we complete the collection of raw data (reports), its filing , cataloguing and database
input, we are p lanning some preliminary statistical work.


                                           Page -1 1 -
3.1 . lntemal consistency.
I n order to assess possible errors from data processing (eg. authors' bias in the choice of
cases) . a first step of the project will be to apply a control of accuracy of the
above-described observational parameters, according to what has been done in Refs [26]
and [27].
Data accuracy will be checked as the correlation between parts of the total database , i . e .
we will apply Pearson correlation index to specific parameters (season , daytime , diameter,
colour, decay) in two BLITA subsets: cases from Galli [28] (77 reports) vs. other sources
( 48 reports, by now).

3.2. Comparison with other catalogues.
A second step will be a comparison between BLITA and the summarised data given by
Stakhanov [ 1 1 -1 2], Keul [ 1 7-1 8] and Bychkov-Amirov [20]. Correlation analysis will be
carried out in terms of the same parameters described above (month, daytime , places of
observation, colour and diameter, decay). This way, all the material will be ready for further
analysis.

4. Conclusions. remarks. and future perspectives.
The project is presently in the preliminary stage of collecting and cataloguing reports of
Italian BL observations, so no properly called conclusion can be drawn as yet.
Such a stage is to be continued in the next months, and we are sure that new data will be
added to the 1 25 cases collected up to now.
The most promising source is a more thorough search of the CISU case files, that will yield
an expected doubling of the total of possible BL phenomena , probably erroneously reported
as UFO sightings in Italy (at least 100 in the last 50 years).
A questionnaire is also to be distributed to meteorological observatories and amateur
astronomer associations (more than 1 70 in Italy) , in order to get yet unpublished
testimonies. In Section 3, we have described the first two steps of the project concerning
some preliminary statistical analysis to be done for "internal" and "external" comparison
purposes.
For the sake of completeness, a similar comparison will be also extended to existing
catalogues of different aerial phenomena, such as meteors , bolides, fire-balls, etc.
Additionally, a study of geographic localisation will be carried o ut for the selected reports,
in order to correlate BL sightings with location features like geological and geomagnetic
ones, or with other phenomena (for instance, Lightning strikes).
The next project step will be the selection , among the preliminary set of Italian B L reports,
of those cases that will constitute the actual database for deeper analyses: those reports
showing the greatest resemblance with the existing BL models or definitions, while at the
same time containing more descriptive data, both quantitative and qualitative, to rely upon.
A collaboration with other researchers working on similar projects is also expecte d , with
a hopefully useful information and experience exchange.
Feedback is also expected from our project by its initial sponsor, Centra ltaliano Studi
Ufologici: we are planning to try some explanations for the set of UFO reports exhibiting
some similarities with BL, also by involving scientists either working in atmospheric and

Over the past twenty years or so. my thinking on U FOs. aliens and close encounters
