Pick up line: Hey, baby, let's go back to my spaceship for a drink

It's a sci-fi geek's wet dream: A 2,000-square-foot UFO house replete with three bedrooms, two full bathrooms, a
bar and an entertainment area. It's actually kinda sweet. All of the rooms are round and there's a retractable
staircase should you ever find yourself besieged behind its sturdy concrete and steel walls (unfortunately, the
staircase is broken and remains in the down position, so you'll have to fix that).

Built in 1973 in Signal Mountain, just north of Chattanooga, it cost a whopping $250,000 to construct, the Wall
Street Journal reports. The Spaceship House has changed hands a few times over the years, and is currently
owned by a guy who runs a local furniture store. Due to his ailing health, he's liquidating his assets here and
heading to sunny Florida. Good news is, there'll be no minimum bid. Guess the guy's relying on the inherent
goodness of Trekkies.

The auction began yesterday, so if any of you PITW readers buy it, please, let me throw a party with cool sci-fi
themes. WSJ has a slide show with pics from the interior. Check it out. It's pretty cool.
