"Concerning the museum ne motel beside NSA in Fort Meade, MD. It 
was once the Colony 7 Motel and my wife worked there as a 
chambermaid for a summer or two in the 60's. She remembers very 
distinctly a man who stayed there ALL the time, had a table 
covered with radio equipment and always asked that the maids fold 
his pajamas and put them on his pillow. The other interesting 
story is that when NSA openned the Cryptologic Museum, they didn't 
tell anyone, had no signs, no opening ceremony. Apparently the NSA 
PR department (there's an oxymoron for you!) didn't know how to 
handle announcing something -- they only knew how to deny things!"

-- SH, 5/21/95
