Argus (Melbourne, Vic. : 1848 - 1957), Friday 9 July 1954, page 3


         Advice
           Mrs.        E.       F.    SEMPLE,
                       st.,     South
        Murphy                           Yarra,
        says:
           If flying saucer    inhabitants
        want   to know     about    us, why
        not use sky-writers to supply
        information     like:
           "On   earth you can       be sent
        to gaol for selling your own
        home, bought and paid for
        with your own       money.       The
        shopkeeper     who    sells   half   a,

        pound of tea after hours         is  a
        criminal."
            [When the invasion   comes
        tell    themnot   to get hurt
        near   a  chemist's   shop at
        night. He   won't   be able to
        help.]
