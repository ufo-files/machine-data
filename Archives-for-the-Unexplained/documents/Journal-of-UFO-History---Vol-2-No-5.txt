"SPACE MAN" RADIO HOAX

   During a promotional tour for his
book Flying Saucers from Outer Space,
Maj. Donald E. Keyhoe (USMC, Ret.)
was in New York City on October 7,
1953. That morning he was a guest on
the popular NBC breakfast program
hosted by Tex Mccrary and his wife,
Jinx Falkenberg, broadcast from the
