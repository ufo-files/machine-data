TELEPHONE INTERVIEW on July 2, 1985 with 
BIRGER PETTERSSON, Umeå
Tel 090/132330 (work), 121129 (home)

Re: A cigar shaped object sighted at Kukasjärvi on Aug 3, 1946 at 10.00 pm

The interview was realized through a neighbour´s telephone (Nils-Martin Arm 0923-91041) who lives opposite of Birger Pettersson´s summer house at Taipaile.

Birger is 57 years old now and he remembers very well his observation even if he has some difficulties describing what he saw. I ask him if he might be able to make a sketch of the object but he wriggles out of it and in spite of my trying very hard to persuade him he does not make a sketch.

Birger was alone at the time of observation. He was on his way home riding a bicycle when he suddenly saw a firelight moving in a straight orbit towards north.

At first he describes it as a fireball, then he says there was some kind of light at the front but he changes his mind again and says it was an oblong object.

- Anyway it was not a shooting star, he says. It moved too slowly. I thought it was strange that there was a light at the front end. I could not hear any sound.

The next day he called the Fo-staff to report his observation.

In the report to the military forces also PER GUSTAFSSON (0923-91046) is mentioned  as observer. When I called him up he says he did not see anything. This is also confirmed by Birger P. When I talked to Per on the phone on June 27, 1985 he said he is interested in Ufo´s and he promised he would check if there were any other witnesses in the village.
