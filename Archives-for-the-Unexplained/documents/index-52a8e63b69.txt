     10. Claims
              In his speeches, Lear says there are 80 different species of aliens visiting earth.
              Lear is generally pessimistic about the future, suggesting that the alien presence is sinister and that there is
              little chance that the government will reveal its UFO knowledge.
              Lear appears to have backed off from the his earlier "aliens are eating humans at Area 51" claims and now
              sticks fairly close to the Lazar gospel. However, his Lazar stories tend to include more personal
              interpretation than Lazar's own telling.
     11. Skeptic's View
              Lear endorses nearly every UFO claim as real, no matter how loony, thus his judgement is unreliable.
     12. Supporter's View
              Lear does tend to go overboard at times, but his sources of information, including Bob Lazar, are basically
              sound.
