PM on an Estonian colonel by the name of Lutz

Mr. Olof Edgren has provided some information in connection with a report on the observation of a "fiery phenomenon" (dated July 9).
His description of an alleged "fiery phenomenon" (which would have crashed into Lill-Nien (lake), 10 km northeast Arbrå in Hälsingland) was very detailed. Due to an "aversion towards foreigners", Mr. Edgren connected the phenomenon with an Estonian colonel named Lutz, who lives in the community of Bogården.
The neighboring population had been very suspicious of colonel Lutz and his wife, above all due to one of their living quarters being strictly forbidden to outsiders. The colonel had departed from the scene the day after the crash. He reportedly went to Kalmar.
Since it is conceivable that some observations could be related to numerous aircraft overflights, which in turn seem to be bound to specific time frames, I request a closer examination of the information mentioned above, given that this would be considered possible.

Defense Staff
Air Defense Division
