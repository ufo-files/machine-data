UFO PROBE
A CYLINDER which crashed on to the roof
of a country house sparked off a UFO
investigation yesterday.
Saddler Nathaniel Underwood and his
wife Elizabeth narrowly escaped when the
12in. long, red-hot metal object fell from
the sky at Wychanger Barton, Somerset.
Police who took it away said they
assumed it was part of an aircraft, but West
Drayton air traffic control said there were
no planes flying over the area at the
time.
