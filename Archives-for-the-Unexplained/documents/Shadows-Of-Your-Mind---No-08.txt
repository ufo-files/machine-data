                                    LUIS FERNANDES CASE




                        A
                                     t dawn of April 3rd, 1976,
                                     one of the most impressive
                                     cases in Brazilian ufology
                                     history occurred. On this date,
                                     several residents of the city
                        of Quixadá, Ceará, witnessed the arrival
                        of an unidentified flying object over the
                        city. For one of these witnesses, this case
                        would mean much more than a simple UFO
