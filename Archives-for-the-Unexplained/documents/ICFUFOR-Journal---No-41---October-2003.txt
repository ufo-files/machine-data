  Hod Ha’Sharon         11 September, 2003


  It was 10:30 when Assaf set at his office desk at “Amdex” company, located in Nave-Na’aman industrial area, and
  looked out of the window, as he does from time to time, resting his work with the computer.

  He saw a small object in the sky, maneuvering up and down. First he thought it's a model-airplane, because he is very

  a bird. It hadn’t wings and no wings movement was observed!

  After observing the object for almost 20 seconds, it made a very sharp turn and accelerated to the south. This sharp turn,
