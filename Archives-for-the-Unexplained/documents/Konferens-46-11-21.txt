Meeting protocol, November 21st.
Subject: projectiles over Swedish soil

Present:

From FF:
Colonel Jacobson, Chairman.Fld KjellsonChief of Bureau NorlinFld NorénFli Malmberg

From FV / FS:
Captain Werneman

From FST:
Captain StangenbergCaptain Sandmark

From KMF:
KK Oxenstierna

From FOA:
Inge Fehrm

From FRA: Head of Division Kempe

1. Fli Malmberg gave an account of his recent visit to southern Sweden in regards to certain observations made both at Sandhammaren, and at Ottenby. A special report on the matter has been made available.

The observations at the radar station at Ottenby suggest evidence of bird activity. Fld Kjellson will contact ornithologists in order to investigate whether bird migrations occur at this time of year.

2. A thorough inspection of the radar station at Ottenby should be performed as soon as possible, courtesy of by Fld Norén.

3. Decisions regarding the transfer of radar stations at Simrishamn and Hoburgen will eventually be taken when the investigation of the bird migrations have been completed.

4. Captain Stangenberg suggested that the radar station in Kalmar should be made wholly operational.

5.

Norlin is currently preoccupied with retrieving reconnaissance aircrafts from F 17 in regards to the many observations of unknown naval vessels southeast of Öland, predominantly during November 9th to November 11th. The matter is currently being investigated by Colonel Jacobson.

6. The Navy ships reconnaissance has not resulted in any report. As of right now, KK Oxenstierna is examining whether the reconnaissance can be moved towards the southern tip of Öland.

7. Ever since October 29th, 9 reports have been submitted to Försvarsstaben, predominantly involving claims of light phenomena.
 
8. Malmberg's visit to the supposed projectile impact site at Kulltorp (Värnamo) showed that the observations were very vague, so it is doubtful that any impact had occurred whatsoever. There won’t be any need for further investigations.

During the visit to Holmsund, it became known that two people had observed a luminous orb, which came with great velocity and crashed at a nearby house. The investigation has not yet revealed the origin of the remains found by the window pane.

9. The next meeting will be held after special notice.

Stockholm, November 21, 1946

Eric Malmberg
