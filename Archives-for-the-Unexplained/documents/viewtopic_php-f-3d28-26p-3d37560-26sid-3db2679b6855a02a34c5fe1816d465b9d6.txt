         Jake and I have received a lengthy email from Admin/Bren. It is mostly a composite of emails that he had
         sent to Doc, Ivo, Manuel, NY Lily, back in the first week of October.

         By etiquette, he does not include their emails, to which he is responding, so it is difficult to get a full picture
         of what was going on.

         One of the issues concerned Fore, who appeared to be intimidating at least two members who had been
         attempting to describe their experiences. Bren was unable to get the other admins to restrain him. I, too,
         have been subjected to the unwanted and, I thought, unhelpful attentions of same.

         Then there were several instances of Bren's posts being deleted and his account being temporarily blocked,
         all without explanation. This naturally raised legal issues. The legal fact is that ProBoards requires that each
         one of their forums have a single account holder who retains the ultimate responsibility and liability for
         each forum. In the case of OMF, the account holder is Bren. It seems that the irreconcilable differences
         amongst the admins, left him with no choice but to act unilaterally. At that point he did not have the option
         to only remove some of the other admins. The only remaining option, as I understand it, was to designate
         himself as the sole administrator of OMF. This is the action that he finally took, a couple days ago. And that
         is where things stand, at present.

         I have not yet been presented with any compelling reason to discontinue my posting on OMF, and, so, here
         I still am. It seems unlikely that this unfortunate matter could ever be resolved to everyone's satisfaction.
         For Bren to publish all of the emails and posts that led up to this impasse, would possibly constitute a
         breach of his legal obligations.
