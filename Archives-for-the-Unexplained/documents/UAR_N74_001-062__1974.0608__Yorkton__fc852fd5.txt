ROY MACKENZIE GRAVES FROM 19 RIVERVIEW BAY, YORKTON, SASK,
BORN 29TH MARCH 1952. EMPLOYED BY SASK POWER WAS DRIVING HIS DADS
1972 OLDSMOBILE CUTLASS AND HAD STOPPED TO TURN AROUND ONE MILE
SOUTH OF YORKTON WHEN HE SAW WHAT LOOKED LIKE AN ORANGE FIREBALL
EXPLODE. AFTER THE EXPLOSION HE SAS WHAT LOOKED LIKE A LONG HUNK
OF WIRE AND APPEARED TO BE 15 TO 20 STREAMERS BREAKING LIKE
FIREWORKS ABOUT 20 FEET ABOVE GROUND. IT LASTED FOR ABOUT 5 SECONDS
APPROXIMATELY 30 OR 40 FEET AWAY. THERE WAS AN ORANGE COLOR METAL-
LIKE SPRAY WHICH STRUCK
THE WINDSHIELD OF THE CAR AND WITHIN SECONDS
TURNED SILVER IN COLOR. THE CAR ENGINE WAS RUNNING AND WINDOWS UP
IN THE CAR AND THE EXPLOSION STILL SOUNDED LIKE A LARGE FIRECRACKER.
TWO SMALL FIRES STARTED IN THE GRASS AND WERE STAMPED OUT.
THE FIRE
WAS A BRIGHT ORANGE IN COLOR. THERE WERE TWO STREAKES OF SILVER LIKE
METAL QUITE BRITTLE ON THE WINDSHIELD, COUPLE STREAKS ON RIGHT REAR
WINDOW, AND TWO OR THREE STREAKS ON BACK WINDOW. ON RIGHT SIDE
OF BUMPER A HOLE WAS BURNT THROUGH THE PLASTIC PANEL BETWEEN THE BUMPE
AND HEADLIGHT RIM ABOUT I INCH IN DIAMETER, THERE WAS SILVER
LIKE METAL AROUND HOLE SAME AS ON THE WINDSHIELD AND SCORCHED BLACK,
ALL AROUND THE HOLE, UNQUOTE.
THIS INFORMATION WAS PASSED, TO YOUR REPRESENTATIVE MR JOHN V HODGES
'IN REGINA, SASK, BY THE R.C.M.P.
. THE R.C.M.P. ARE CONTINUING THEIR
INVESTIGATION AND HAVE TAKEN SAMPLES OF THE GRASS FIRE AND METAL LIKE
SUBSTANCES ON THE CAR WHICH WILL BE TAKEN TO THE RCMP LAB IN REGINA
ON MONDAY FOR ANALYSIS. MR JORN HODGE WILL BE PRESENT DURING THESE
EXAMINATIONS
