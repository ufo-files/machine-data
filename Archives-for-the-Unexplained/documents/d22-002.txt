                         Who is Robert Petro? (Sedona psychic)
     From: campbell@ufomind.com (Glenn Campbell, Las Vegas)
     Date: Mon, 22 Dec 1997 13:58:29 -0800


  This psychic's autobiography ought to raise some flags....
  Originally Found at: http://www.RobertPetro.com/more.htm
  Preserved at: http://www.ufomind.com/ufo/people/f/fowlerr/more-petro.html
       Robert Petro holds his BA in Philosphy and Masters Degree in
       Psychology.
       A self-made man, Robert J. Petro owns many small businesses
       and is an acclaimed motivational speaker famous for his
       prosperity seminars. He grew up in New York as an orphan,
       dropped out of grade school, and didn't learn to read until
       he was in his thirties; yet he has become wealthy, happy,
       and successful in all areas of his life.
       A popular guest on the media, Robert Petro has appeared on
       numerous radio and TV shows from coast to coast, including,
       The David Letterman Show, PM Magazine, Good Morning New York
       and many others.
       Robert Petro lives with his wife in Sedona, AZ.
  A most unusual fellow! He couldn't read until he was in his 30s,
  yet he went on to a Masters Degree in Psychology. I wonder what
  university the degree was from.
  Reading his website at http://www.RobertPetro.com/, one is
  overwhelmed by the number of accolades he has received from the
  media. I feel out of the loop that I have never heard of him
  before.
  On his main page it says, "Robert Petro was awarded the peace
  medal by the United Nations Parapsychology Society."
  Funny that I have never heard of that society. I didn't know
  the U.N. was into that sort of thing. A metacrawler search for
  "United Nations Parapsychology Society" turns up nothing.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------
  Petro is offering a psychic training seminar in March 1998 in
  Sedona, including "How to market yourself."

  I prefer to create records under a person's "real" name.
  So I did a little on-line research....
  -----------------------------------------------------------------
  The 2-day seminar, "Developing the Intuitive Self", is
  $950.00 per person. (Must be good!) See registration at...
       http://www.RobertPetro.com/semorder.htm
  The seminar covers: "Astral Projection, Astrology, Numerology,
  Healing, Tarot, Auras and Color, Pendulum and Divining Rods,
  Dreams , Channeling, Meditation and Self Hypnosis, Rings and
  Fingers, Palmistry, Marketing."
  (If he pulls this off at $950 a head, he definitely does
  know about marketing.0
  Petro gives another seminar on Shamanism at similar cost.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------
  For a man as supposedly renowned as Mr. Petro, there is
  surprisingly little on the net about him. A metacrawler search
  for "Robert Petro" turned up no relevant documents apart from
  Petro's own site. Here is his domain name registration...
       http://rs.internic.net/cgi-bin/whois?RobertPetro.com
  The only DejaNews newsgroup posting I found was a brief third-party
  reference to Petro as a psychic who had been tested by the government.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------
  However, there are a lot of Deja News references for Bob Fowler,
  who appears to be Petro's #1 supporter.
  DejaNews author profile...
  http://x9.dejanews.com/profile.xp?author=Bob%20Fowler%20%3cbobf@kachina.net%3e&ST=PS
  There seems to be a lot of synchonicity between Fowler and Petro.
  They have the same ISP and writing style. They live in the same
  area (Sedona) and both offer on-line psychic readings.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------
  Fowler also promotes a website concerning Shamanism and tarot
  reading at http://www.awake.net. This site has certain stylistic
  resemblances to Petro's site. This domain is registered to one
  "Lakshmi Narayan", also in Sedona.
        http://rs.internic.net/cgi-bin/whois?awake.net
  This name produces no useful links in Metacrawler and DejaNews
