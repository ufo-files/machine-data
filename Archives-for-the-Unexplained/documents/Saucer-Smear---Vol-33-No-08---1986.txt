 MISCELLANEOUS RAVINGS:
        We are pleased to announce that the National UFO Conference (NUFOC)
 will hold its 24th annual convention in Los Angeles, California, with
 famed author & lecturer Bill Moore as local chairman. The date will pre­
 sumably be some time in the summer of 1987. (Details will be printed in
 "Smear" as they become available.) It should be noted that 1987 is the
 glorious 40th anniversary of Kenneth Arnold's sighting, as well as the
 40th anniversary of the Roswell (New Mexico) -Incident, of which   Bill
 Moore is the leading proponent.
        Three other possible locations were considered before the decision
 was made to hold next year's convention in Los Angeles. One of     these
 three was Cleveland, Ohio, where the National UFO Conference was co-found­
 ed in 1964 by Rick Hilberg and Allen Greenfield. Greenfield, now known
 as "Spider Rainbow", has long since left the UFO movement, and interest­
 ingly, he has moved from Atlanta, Ga. to Key West, Fl. very recently.
        Hilberg still lives in Cleveland, and is one of the nine members of
 the Permanent Organizing Committee (POC), which supposedly runs     the
 NUFOC.   The other members are his wife Carol, plus Al Manak and Robert
 Easley, all of Cleveland; Ed Biebel of Arizona; Tim Beckley of New York
 City; Charles Wilhelm of western Ohio; Kal Korff of Kalifornia; and your
 humble editor, who is Permanent Chairman of the NUFOC.
        We bring all this up in order to point out that, ever since the
 convention was last held in Cleveland in 1984, there has been a terrible
 schism between the Hilbergs on the one hand, and Manak & Easley on the
 other hand - even though they all live in the same city and all remain
 on the Permanent Organizing Committee. The schism comes about because of
 petty differences that arose around the time of that convention -    mat­
 ters so terribly petty that even "Smear" won't bother to report them to
 you. But as long as this situation continues to exist, your editor has
 decided that no more of our conventions should be held in that fair city.
 This, then, is a public message to the four Cleveland POC members to get
 their act together!
