                     Night Sighting in Knoxville, TN, Nov. 1974
     From: campbell@ufomind.com (Glenn Campbell, Las Vegas)
     Date: Sun, 24 Aug 1997 08:16:39 -0800


  Date: Sat, 23 Aug 1997 20:56:15 -0700
  From: "steve whittle@esper.com" <swhittle@esper.com>
  To: campbell@ufomind.com
  Subject: ufo's
  Nov 22, 1974 in Knoxville Tennessee, 0300 hrs. I have always worked
  the night shift and stay up all night long. I walked to my back door
  and looked out at the snow on the ground and saw a shadow going across
  the ground now normaly i would not pay atention to this but this
  shadow was huge. I walked outside and all i had on was a pair of
  shorts as the house was hot. I walked in the snow to the house next
  door as my nephew lived there and he was also up. I told him to come
  outside and look at what I was looking at. He walked outside and said
  "What in the hell is that"? I told him that I didn't know. We watched
  as it moved out of sight then we got in my car and followed it up the
  road for about 3 or 4 miles. Then it shot up at a great speed I don't
  tell people about this to often and when I told family members about
  this they said I was crazy.
  My cousin also saw this as he was delivering news papers in the
  county. His email adress is dbnawell@hotmail.com
