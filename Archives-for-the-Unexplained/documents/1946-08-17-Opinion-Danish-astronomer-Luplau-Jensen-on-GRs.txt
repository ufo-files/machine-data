August 17: View of a Danish astronomer

”The wellknown [Danish] astronomer [Carl] Luplau-Jensen declared on Saturday to Aftenbladet, that the many space projectiles seen are falling stars, which, as known, are especially numerous this time of the year.”  (TT newswire from Copenhagen, Aug. 17)
