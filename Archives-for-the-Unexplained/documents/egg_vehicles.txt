EG&G Vehicle Inventory
  Below are government vehicles spotted at the EG&G terminal (Haven Ave.) or EG&G Special Projects offices (Grier
  Ave.).

          Plate: U.S. Government 691 05813. Flatbed trailer followed from Grier Rd. office on 5/9/96. (Plate is for the
          trailer not the cab.) Spotted on routine survey of back of EG&G Grier Rd. office. Departed from office at about
          9:30 am. Agent Coyote and myself followed it to US-95 north, then beyond Las Vegas, far enough to determine
          that it was going to either Mercury or Indian Springs. Flatbed carried a small modular office unit, apparently new.
          (It began to shed bits of its insulation when the truck got going on the highway.) Truck also carried a stack of
          what appeared to be drywall and several big rolls of heavy-guage electrical cable (about 1" thick).
