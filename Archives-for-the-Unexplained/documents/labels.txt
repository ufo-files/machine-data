   Labels in Rat Issues

   To have an html label added to any issue of the Desert Rat, send your request to campbell@ufomind.com, along with
   the paragraph where you wish the label to appear and your suggested name for the label. (By giving us the name, you do
   not have to wait for us before creating the link.)

   The label name should be one word, all lowercase. We generally put our labels at the beginning of paragraphs, not
   inside them.

   Before requesting a new label, check to make sure there is not already one there that will serve your purpose. All major
   sections should be already labelled.
