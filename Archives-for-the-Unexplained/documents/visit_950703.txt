       Below is a short summary of a small trek I took to the
  town of Rachael and Tikaboo Peak. I have posted it because I think some
  of
  you may have an interest to justr look it over


     On June 21 I headed out towards Rachael from Las Vegas. Once I was
  about
  7 miles out of the downtown area on 93N I was in the middle of the desert
  with small sage bursh scattered out thinly. At 3:25 PM at the 87.1 mile
  marker there was a traffic stoppage in the middle of no where a lady in a
  uniform of some sort held of a stop sign and I waited for about twenty
  minutes until a pickup truck coming from the north told her something and
  she
  backed off and signaled me to go. About two miles down the road I could
  see
  a lot of dust off to the east but no vehicles or people that could be the
  reason to why I stopped. Under the directions provided in Campbell's
  Viewer's Guide I turned off at marker 32.2 to the west to scout out the
  road
  to Tikaboo all seamed fine and I turned around once I reached the first
  and
  only cattle fence on the road.
      Later at 5:42 I reached the Black Mailbox on Hwy. 375. Already there
  was a bunch of young German men who included this place as part of their
  graduation gift; a trip through the United States. [ Their license plate
  said "DIPLOMAT" on it, can anyone give me more information on why it
  would
  say that? ] Soon after I pulled over I heard 2 sonic booms a minute later
  an
  A-10 approached over our heads heading W NW. The two have to be
  unrelated
  because the Warthog is not a mach aircraft. Much farther down the road
  was
  Rachael with it's Little A'LE'INN the place where I would stay the night.
  When I first entered the Bar/motel two folks were working away at the slot
  machines I was then greated by a friendly middle aged lady Pat Travis.
  The
  place seems to have much hospitality even though it appears to have once
  been
  another of the many mobilehomes in the town. I sat down at the bar and
  next
  to me was chuck investigating an obviously hoaxed UFO picture. He and two
  other fellows were busily investigating the photo to find what
  characteristics made it fake. The photo was of a silver frizbee with a
  model
  of a B-1 Lancer booth hung from a tree. I later asked Chuck what he

        The next morning at exactly 7am I shot down to Tikaboo. Briefly
  after I passed the gravel pit on 375 near I could see some dust rising
  from a
  vehicle that was slowly moving down Groom Lake road. I gunned my vehicle
  up
  to about 94mph which would be quite dangerous if I hit one of the cows I
  see
  every once in awhile. I reached the point along 375 where it meets Groom
  Lake road. I got out of the car and with my binoculars I could easily see
  that it was two vehicles travelling down the road. One was a white pickup
  truck, I don't know the make, that had a license plate which read

   US AIRFORCE
     87B 4449
  For Official Use Only                 -in small print

  continued to Tikaboo. Five miles on the dirt access road to Tikaboo I see

  type of rock it is? When I went back to my car after I got out to
  investigate the caves I found that I had left my car in Drive but the
  grade
  was sloped enough so that it wound move...lucky me. Once I reached the

  that a fortification has been built up here to fight som war. I even

  few .22 shells, most likely a futile attempt to shoot down a UFO. I

  around a little built and found that some gratious person left 2 chairs,

  dried camping dinners, I could live on this stuff for almost a week!
  Nearby
  in a rock cubby hole with a blue sleeping bag over it I found the shrine
  for
  Our Lady of the Black Budget with her statue and four candels. There was
  about 2 dollars there. I left a buck and some change. I fixed up all the
  fortifications walls as best as I could and then began to observe. On the
  horizon;
  To my west is Tikaboo Valley where you can easily pickout all the roads as
  well as looking at a map. Groom Lake AFB is about 30' South of West. To
  the
  East is the mountainous area where I hiked through. 20' East of South I
  see
  what appears to be a nuclear test ground....I could be incorrect though.
  I
  spent about 45 minutes looking around and then I went back down...fixing
  even
  more cairns. I reached my car at 2:30pm and then headed back to Las
  Vegas.

          Throughout the rest of my trip I checked out two other things.

  -In Boron, California I failed to see any UFOs. This would not firm up
  Jerod's claims.

  -In Yosemite 3 F-16s roared through the main valley, interesting yet not
  unusual.
