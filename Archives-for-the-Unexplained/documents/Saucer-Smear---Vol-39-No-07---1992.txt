CONVENTION:
       This year's annual MUFON bash was held in sce­
nic Albuquerque, New Mexico on the weekend of July
lOth-13th, ably hosted by N . M . State Director Teresa
Brito-Asenap . This lady is a former "Smear" non-sub­
scriber who is now resigning from her high MUFON post
but is re-instating herself on our glorious mailing
list .
       Friday's MUFON press conference was held in an obscure conference
room that even Eastern Regional Director Don Ware had difficulty finding .
Boredom there was overcome by a spirited exchange between Captain        Kevin
Randle and physicist/lecturer Stanton Friedman, who have fought in        the
pages of "Smear" regarding the authenticity of an alleged Roswell Incident
witness named Gerald Anderson . Randle presented to Friedman for the first
time a copy of an (allegedly) forged telephone bill that Anderson has sub­
mitted to Roswell investigators . Friedman, who strongly supports Anderson's
Roswell yarn, was as nearly speechless as we have ever seen him . Poor Walt
Andrus (head of MUFON) kept saying, "A press conference is not the proper
place for a debate", and glancing in a semi-annoyed manner at your humble
"Smear" editor, who happened to be sitting next to Randle!
       Later from the podium in the nearby Albuquerque Convention Center,
Walt made a passing reference to our historic 1967 NUFOC convention in New
York City, calling it a "kook convention" presumably because there were UFO
contactees involved - but forgetting that a modern MUFON convention        in­
cludes not only contactees but abductees and channelers (such as Don Ware,
for example) .
       Yet, be not misled . Our relationship with Walt Andrus is as close as
ever . Actually, we kind of like the old guy, and he seems to have a grudg­
ing respect for some of our ufological efforts .
       The height of the convention was Saturday evening, with nearly 1, 000
card-carrying MUFONites and fellow travelers attending .      (Stanton Friedman,
who gives almost the same lecture every year, was downgraded to        Sunday
afternoon . )
       On Saturday eve . the first speaker was famed scientist/author Jacques
Vallee, a close co-worker of the late Dr . Jc Allan Hynek . In an excellent
presentation, intended to be his ufological "swan song", Dr . Vallee lament­
ed the present sad state of ufology and focused on researchers such as Budd
Hopkins (though he did not name him) who manipulate hapless abductees in
their own unscientific manner and for their own unscientific motives . Val­
lee deplores the false structure that organized ufology has given to the
UFO phenomenon, i . ec, UFOs must be physical and interplanetary, and abduct­
ions must be performed by aliens who meet the stereotype of "greys"o       (OIS­
EAU looks a lot like a "grey" to us!)
       Right after Dr . Vallee's speech came a presentation by the above-men­
tioned Budd Hopkins, who is probably America's best-known current abduction
researcher . Though we have our own personal reasons to doubt Hopkins' sta­
bility, due to a ghastly conversation we had with him back in the mid-l970s,
we will struggle valiantly to be as objective as we can about the sensation­
al information he brought forth at the MUFON convention:
       It seems that about 3: 15 A . M . one November night in 1989, a   woman
named Linda was abducted by three typical "greys" in a spaceship hovering
outside her 12th floor apartment in Manhattan, New York City . This some­
what unusual event was witnessed by two members of an unnamed government
                                    (3)
agency, who were in a car about two blocks away with an unnamed "major po­
litical figure" (we know his identity) , whom they were guarding . The event
was supposedly also witnessed by a female retired telephone worker who was
driving across the nearby Brooklyn Bridge at the time . Hopkins isn't try­
ing to sell us the Bridge, but he does want us to "buy" this story . There
are supposed to be several other witnesses, unnamed; The two government
agents are unnamed, and Hopkins still has never met either of them . Even
Linda is unnamed, but she did appear with Hopkins on the podium . After he
finished recounting to the audience some of the details of the above story,
he presented her to the audience . With unconscious humor, her first remark
was: "Are there any questions?" There were .
       Information outlined privately to your "Smear" editor by certain con­
vention attendees indicates that there are more holes in Linda's story than
in a Swiss cheese, and we intend to bring you some of this information in
our next issue . It's not that we think abductions are impossible . It's just
that, when the details are known, this particular case sounds like a proper
one to be investigated by the late, great Dr . Sigmund Freud, rather than by
ufologists . In any case, you now know the topic of Budd Hopkins' next book .
       Dr . Vallee is also bringing out a new book with the same title as his
MUFON lecture, "Forbidden Science" . It is a diary of internal scientific
debate about UFOs during the 1957-1969 period, and sounds like it should be
well worth reading .
       Another worthwhile lecture was by Montague Keen, a learned Englishman
with a wonderful sense of humor, who gave an objective (imagine that! ) sum­
mary of the state of crop circle research in the United Kingdom . He told
of a contest sponsored by his group, whereby fifteen teams of hoaxers were
given an opportunity to duplicate a specific complex crop circle pattern
supplied to them by the organization . The team with the best performance
wins a prize of about $5, 000 . We note with dismay that "Smear's" own crop
circle expert Erik Beckjord is not even in the contest!
       Other speakers included our friend Antonio Hunneus, "the man of    a
thousand slides", who becomes more & more professional as time goes by; a
Russian scientist/abduction fan named Vladimir Ajaja; Dr . Richard Haines on
electromagnetic effects of UFOs; Cattle mutilation fan Linda Moulton Howe;
Photo analyist Jeffrey Sainio; Piet van Putten from Brazil; Dr . Richard
Neal on "The Missing Embryo/Fetus Syndrome" (he says there really aren't
any proven cases of missing fetuses, thank god! ); and last but not least,
Walt Andrus, as already noted .
       There were also a number of private MUFON-related meetings which of
course your humble "Smear" editor was not invited to attend . We did make
it into a meeting of Florida State Section Directors, in spite of having
been recently demoted to Assistant State Section Director in the MUFON hier­
archy . The meeting, chaired by Florida State Director Charles Flannigan,
was boring .
       Famed debunker Phil Klass attended the convention, and joined us for
a cigarette in a prohibited zone . Also present at the convention was Klass
associate Willy Smith, whose doctorate is apparently still a matter of de­
bate . Smith is still mad at us about a variety of matters related to the
Gulf Breeze UFO case .
       All in all, a Good Scene in far-off, far-out Albuquerque, New Mexico .
Next year it's Richmond, Virginia that will be subjected to a MUFONite in­
vasion, during the July 4th weekend, no less . Be there!
