                         Underground Shipping and Receiving
                                      (Belmont, off El Camino Real)
Directions: SEE MAP; Take Laurel Street south to Willow Rd.; turn left on Willow Rd.; take Highway
101 North (towards San Francisco); take the Hillsdale exit West (towards El Camino Real); you will
continue on Hillsdale Blvd. across El Camino Real; pass the parking lot in front of the stores, and pass by
the stores; turn into the back parking lot (I think it is the second drive way); there will be an underground
entrance to the Hillsdale Mall's shipping and receiving area; you can either drive through the shipping and
receiving area or park in the parking lot and walk down through it.

Target: The target is the underground shipping and receiving area of the Hillsdale Shopping Mall. Both
the entrance and exit to this underground area is in a parking lot. The underground shipping and receiv-
ing area itself is located under the Hillsdale shopping mall. The long underground drive is lined on both
sides with lighted loading dock areas. There is a single row of lights down the center of the roof above the
drive. There are metal pipes along the sides and roof of the underground shipping and receiving area.
Most of the underground area is gray cement with a few red or white painted curbs here and there. There
are some cement pillars near the loading dock areas. There are trucks and forklifts around the loading
dock areas. Despite the lights, the underground area is dark and cool compared to outside.
