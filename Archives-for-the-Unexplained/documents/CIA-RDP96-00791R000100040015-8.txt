             In response to Congressional direction that the Defense
        Intelligence Agency (DIA) transfer the STARGATE program to
        CIA, and that we perform a retrospective review of the
        program's activities, we have initiated a number of actions,
        including:
                     •   Trading data with DIA to conduct the requested
                         review and program transfer, including several
                         officially declassified documents.
                     •   Exchanging draft taskina statements with the
                         National Research Council (NRC) to establish a
                         Blue Ribbon Panel to ensure a comprehensive and
                         unbiased technical retrospective review of the
                         program.
                     •   Beainning an effort to declassify information
                         related to STARGATE. We already have held
                         informal discussions with the Agency's Center
                         for the Study of Intelligence on developing
                         strategies for declassifying information and are
                         preparina a memorandum that specifies our
                         declassification requirements for DIA.
                     •   Requesting the cooperation of the US Army
                         Research Institute, which holds a contract
                         related to behavioral sciences with the NRC. We
                         also are preparing to transfer ~esources to the
                         Institute for the cost of managing and
                         supporting the NRC during the technical review.
                     •   Contacting several organizations involved in the
                         program to review STARGATE's operations.

   Retrospective Review
        To perform the retrospective review of STARGATE's 20-
   year history and a technical analysis of the program's
   results, we will work closely with the NRC, which has ready
   access to a variety of experts in the scientific fields
   related to the program. In addition to establishing the
   Blue Ribbon Panel of parapsychological phenomena, CIA will

               •    Review research and development studies related

               •    Meet with former program officials to develop a
                    comprehensive understanding of STARGATE's
                    current direction.
