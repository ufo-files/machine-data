As evidenced by Gen. MacArthur's sweep up to the Yalu River , it was complete anni-

In the Orient, losing face is akin to military defeat; its effects lie deep-rooted in
the attitudes of key allies . As a matter of record, we have already seen Indo-China
bow to communist demands, Anti-American riots in Japan, red revolt in Indonesia,
