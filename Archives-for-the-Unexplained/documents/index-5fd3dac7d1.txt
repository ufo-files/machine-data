                                               Seeing angles from a different angle
  While much has been written about angeles throughout history, no single source examines them as thoroughly or as
  thoughtfully as Angels A to Z.

  You'll discover how ancient, contemporary and pop cultures represent angels in their mythology, folklore art, literature
  and religion. Among the 300 angel-related topics explored are:

          Biblical angels -- like Gabriel, Michael and Lucifer

          Different classifications of angels -- such as Valkyries from Norse mythology and Hafaza from Islamic tradition

          Contemporary authors who write about angels -- such as Frank Peretti, Joan Webster Anderson, Eileen Freeman
          and Terry Lynn Taylor

          Artists who depict angels -- such as Michelangelo, Raphael, Glenda Green and Karyn Martin-Kuri
