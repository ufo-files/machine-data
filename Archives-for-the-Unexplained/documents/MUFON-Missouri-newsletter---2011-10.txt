Missouri State Director Debbie
Ziegelmeyer contacted the EAA
Flying Team and found that they
flew in formation over Lee’s
Summit, the Chief’s Stadium,
then over I-70 and back to the
Lee’s Summit airport, where they
landed in pairs. The flight was
between 7:00 p.m and 8:00 p.m.
This was a demonstration for the
Chiefs for a night time fly over on
October 31.

Margie Kay contacted the Lee’s
Summit Airport, and confirmed
the flight time for the Flying
Team. The contact at the airport
stated that they had received a
number of odd reports of UFOs
the past two weeks prior to the
October 4 event. One report in-
volves a low-flying helicopter at
1:30 a.m. when no helicopters
should be flying, and another re-
port involved low-flying balls of
light according to the dispatcher.
The airport will now send unex-
plained reports to Missouri MU-
FON.
