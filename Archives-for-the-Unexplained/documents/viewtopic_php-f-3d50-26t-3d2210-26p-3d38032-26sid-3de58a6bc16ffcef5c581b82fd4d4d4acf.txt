  Comet heading for Mars!

    by m0r1arty » Thu Mar 28, 2013 10:21 pm

  Hey all,

  I've read over at Nasa that there's a chance that a comet will hit Mars in October of 2014.

  The comet named 'Comet 2013 A1' is between 1 and 3 kilometres in diameter and is travelling at around 125,000 mph.
  Should it hit Mars an expected 35 million megatons of energy would be released from impact.

  Chances are currently 1 in 2,000 of it making impact, but either way it'll be getting quite close to Mars and should make
  for a fascinating observation.

  If it does hit Mars' environment would undergo a bit of change.
