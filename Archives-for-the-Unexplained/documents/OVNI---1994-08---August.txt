                     CROP CIRCLES GALORE!

We are now at the height of the Crop Circle 'season ' and circle
seekers c an not have been disappointed at the wide abundance of
designs that have appeared mainly, (as usual) in the fields of
Wiltshire.

The John Macnish video 'Revelations ', which many members have
now seen,   can only add fuel to the fire of incredulous
arguments that some    'hardened ' Crop Circle followers have
maintained.  We are now asked to consider the possibility of an
overall intelligence in the wheat heads performing the feat of
circle and pictogram making.

The 'Revelations ' video opened our eyes to how many of the
seemingly impossible feats of     'pictogram mak1ng ' have been
carried out at night.   The disclosure that large helium filled
illuminated balloons have been released over the Wiltshire
fields at night (with controlled flashing lights) could account
for many of the strange sights reported across Wiltshire.

However the Macnish video does not give the answer to all of
the many questions still outstanding about this phenomenon. It
made no mention of the g 1 owing 'tube of 1 ight ' seen by more
than one witness over the wheatfields.  It also made no comment
about the small silver ball filmed flying low over the wheat
fields a year or so ago.    A similar small flying silver ball
was encountered by a Cessna aircraft over Bl ackbushe Airport
some years ago, but no Crop Circles appeared on the airfield as
a result!     The small silver ball may be irrelevant to the
forming of the circles and may be purely an observation vehicle
of some kind.

It was interesting to hear the fina 1 comment of more than one
academic hoaxer, that they believed there was an underlying
mystery to the appearance of Crop Circles.

We have know a·number of prominent Crop Circle researchers for
some years and our thanks go to Busty Taylor for a selection of
his excellent aerial photographs which are reproduced in this
issue, together with some superb ground shots taken , by PRA
members Tony and Claire Upton.    Finally we have an exclusive
review by Pat Delgado of the John Macnish "Revelations" video.

The introduction lead-in shots were much          the       same as we have seen
in previous documentaries and do not warrant any special comment.


I thought coverage of the crop circle competition was handled and
described fairly,   inasmuch that it did not prove anything other
than crops can be flattened into patterns at night by manual
means.   It also showed that,    with enough practice,           a determined
hoaxer could create a neat and tidy pattern which some people
demand as important criteria found in 'genuine' circles.


It is part of life that there always were             and   there always will
be hoaxers,    I have said many times,    practice makes perfect. Rupert
Sheldrake   reiterates that forgers of bank notes and other
artifacts thought to be worth copying,          can    fool all of the people
some of the time.


Dr.Robin Allen of the   Wessex Sceptics described a non deliberate
detail of their circle making was the production of a certain
amount   of braiding and layering of the        crop   stems.     He said that
Dr.Terence meaden had stated that these features could not be
made by man unless they had a doctorate and had studied these
details.    Just this one particular extract from the video
highlights the fact that   all    special features of an inexplicably
created circle can be replicated by humans fortuitously or
intentionally.    The energy that creates non human created circles
is operating in this same human manner but,            possibly,    with a
smoother result.


I am sure   that some of the inexplicably made circles have been
rougher in appearance than those made by humans, why not? If it
is the 'cause' of these manifestations by an unknown energy we
are seeking,   we should not be    looking for only perfection in crop
patterns,   we should become aware that this planet is immersed in
un-named energies which are capable of functions beyond our
present level of understanding.


The item about recording a crop circle being made at night by
humans proves nothing significant other than the depths to which
some people will    sink to gain unworthy and shoddy points.               I
wonder how many forged bank notes and coins these tricksters have
had pass through their hands without realising the truth.


Ken   Brown seems to be implying    that all crop circles have kinked
stalks on   the stems that have been flattened and the kink is,                 'just
one of those things that happen'.       Imagine asking some scientist



                                   2
to explain   some particular detail of a certain phenomenon and he
replies,   'Oh   that is just one   of those      things that happen'.


Of course Ken Brown may be closer to the truth than he realises.
Those of the crop circles which are inexplicable may very well
be 'just one of those things that          happen'.    What a wonderful world
we live in.


I was heartened by the remarks of various individuals towards the
end of the video,     reiterating what      I   have said in recent years,
that there is     a basic phenomenon of simple crop circles.              I have
also said that     it is highly likely that        these have been appearing
for thousands of years.


Further reiteration was the question,           'What is the motivating
power that manipulates people to create crop patterns from simple
circles to apparently significant symbolism'.              This must be a good
area for psychological research, for we have crop circles made by
inexplicable means and the energy and maybe intelligence thus used
may be working through humans to produce            similar patterns.


On the whole     the video was fairly      well produced for       John
Macnish's purposes even though          the material was interspersed with
familiar scenes of crop     circles from the       past.   It is interesting
to note John's courage of his convictions in this             video.      The
process required to make a complete U turn in the presentation
and substance £rom those he made in his 1 9 90-1991 documentaries
to this most recent one must be the outcome of some powerful
driving force or desire.
