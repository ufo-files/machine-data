      Young Research Library, UCLA.
      Scope and Content
      Collection consists of the investigative reports of the U.S. Air Force's Project Blue Book into the phenomenon of UFO's, and
      includes photocopied records obtained by Mark VII Ltd. under the Freedom of Information Act, as well as scripts and related
      material from the television show Project U.F.O. produced by Jack Webb.
