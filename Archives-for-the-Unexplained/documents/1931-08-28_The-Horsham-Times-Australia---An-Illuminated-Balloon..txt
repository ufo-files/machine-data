Horsham Times (Vic. : 1882 - 1954), Friday 28 August 1931, page 8



               AN       ILLUMINATED                         BALLOON.

          Two          Narracoorte residents                        were      mo

        toring         on  the  Narracoorte                        and      Robe
        road        last       week        and       just   near         Konetta
       Station           they         observed          a   strange         light
       in      the      sky.          It    was   travelling slowly
       and                            it    descended         Wood-
                   suddenly                             near
       leigh        and   burst            into flame.    They were
        informed        that               Mr.   John   Watson, of
       Konetta, had a man           on  the   station who
       had    made       a  balloon, and     Mr. Watson
       and    a   party     had  decided    to let it go.
       It was      a striking    sight, being lit inside
       by      a     methylated              wick.
