            An issue that needs to be addressed is: What will the future role be of the Fund for UFO Research?
    I can only say that it will be more important than ever, if somewhat more complicated:      (1) Fund and
    Administer. Proposals submitted to us that the Executive Committee and National Board approve and for
    which we have the funds will be funded and administen:d by us; (2) Support and Administer. Proposals
    submitted to us that are approved by the full Board, but for which we lack the funds, will be passed on to the

    Proposals submitted to us as a representative of the CFM coalition (i.e., . directed to CFM via us) will be
    screened by the Executive Committee for possible presentation to the CFM coalition and administered by us if
    approved.

            Please note a number of new publications being offered for sale by the Fund:          1) Alien
    Discussions: Proceedings of the Abduction Study Conference, edited by Andrea Pritchard et al. This
    hardcover volume includes papers by everyone who attended the abduction conference held at lVIIT in
    June 1992. 2) Project Delta: A Study of Multiple UFO by Richard Haines. Esteemed researcher and
    Fund board member Haines details puzzling UFO sightings involving two or more objects. 3) UFOs and
