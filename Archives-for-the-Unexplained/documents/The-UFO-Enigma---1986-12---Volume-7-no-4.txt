                    By Stephen            Davis


      Fractal             Geometry             is    described
as    a   branch of mathematics                          concern­
ed    with          finding          order          in apparent
randomness.                     I    have           included          a
r-eference                guide          to    associate             the
reader          with            current              scientific
offerings            on the nature                  and uses of
this       unique          branch         of physics.                 As
described            in       the    articles,             fractal
mathematics               has       been used         in       making
sense       out       of        such          systems          as the
economic            system          and        the       weather,
with       surprising                    results.               Might
this      be        a means by            which       research­
ers       in    the        field          of        ufology          and
related          phenomena               can    finally          make
sense            out          of         the         randomness
apparent             in       the        masses          of      data
which          have       been        collected                on    the
subject          since          the last century?                      I
would           also          like        to        direct           the
reader�s            attention to an                  article in
the       magazine,           Science          Digest,          April
1986,          Page       51,       about      the    structure
of     infinite            minimal             surfaces              and
the       resulting             similarities                   in the
shapes          of     such          surfaces         and       those
of     some          reported            UFOs.             I    would
also           like         to        urge          the        reader
interested             in       research             to        expand
their          reading          into      as many diverse
fields          as possible,              for the          facili­
tation         of creative            thinking.
