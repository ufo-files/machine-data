                            Discussion Groups & Agents

        Over the years it has been noticed that many important or significant
   ideas have originated from small, local, UFO discussion groups.  Because of
   their value, the IAPA has decided to sponsor several of these groups.  The
   Discussion Group will forward to headquarters the results of their activi­
   ties and discussions for possible publication.  These groups may also serve
   by inviting the press to cover their meetings.  We have started two groups to
   date, one in Columbus, Ohio, headed by Richard Shaeffer, and one in Madera,
   California, he�ded by Jeff Murray.  Any person wishing to start such a group
   should contact the Chairman.
        In order to get increased news coverage and to set up an investigational
   network, the IAPA has decided to appoint IAPA Research Agents in various areas.
   These Agents will investigate any UFO sightings or incidents in their area,
   and will be responsible for reporting them to the Agency.  These Agents will
   differ from the.usual Representatives in that they will not have to solicit
   memberships.  Potential Agents will be notified �hartly.
