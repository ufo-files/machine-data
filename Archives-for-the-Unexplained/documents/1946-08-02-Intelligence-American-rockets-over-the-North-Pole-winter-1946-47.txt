August 2: American rockets over the North Pole winter 1946-47...?

”ROCKET PROJECTILES OVER THE NORTH POLE. New York, August 2 (from the Aftenposten correspondent). This coming winter there will be a lively traffic over the North Pole and the nearby polar areas. The American defence authorities have announced that they plan test shots of radio-controlled projectiles in these areas. It is not stated what kinds of projectiles it is the question of, but one surmises that they are rocket-powered flying bombs like the German V weapons.”  (Aftenposten [Oslo], August 3.)
