Townsville Daily Bulletin (Qld. : 1907 - 1954), Friday 22 January 1954, page 2


           Charges Secrecy
                On         Saucers
         MONTREAL,              January            21.—
       Major Donald           Keyhoe, author
       of the book           "Flying Saucers
       from  Outer          Space," said yes-
       terday that           the United States
       Air      Force      had
       a     policy       of   thought control
       on      the        subject of     flying
       saucers.
             Major Keyhoe,    who    will
       address   the Chamber    of Com-
       merce  in North   Bay, Ontario,
       to-night,    said   the    United
       States Air          Force   had clamped
       down       on      all information  per-
       taining       to saucers  and   no
       reason      had been given.
         'The       Air Force   does  not
       want       the   public to realise
                                      is
       how concerned             it         over     fly
       ing      saucersuntil           it    has     all
       the   answers,"   he           said     in    an
       interview.
