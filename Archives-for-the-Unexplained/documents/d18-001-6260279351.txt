           UFO sighting at Guantanamo Bay, Cuba, 1974/75
     From: Augie Prestano <prestano@bellsouth.net>
     Date: Sun, 18 Jan 1998 10:25:33 -0800


  Comment attached to: http://www.ufomind.com/ufo/comment/
  While stationed in Cuba (74-75),about 300 sailors/marines,were
  watching the nightly movie.I was more interested in the stars in the
  skies.I saw what looked like a star moving slowly across the
  horizon.As I watched,there were 3 similar objects directly behind the
  first. By this time we shut down the movie. The first object suddenly
  stopped, the second came upon the first, and so on.After about ten
  minutes they all departed in different directions.We all reported what
  we saw to the Base Commander,not one word was ever said about it
  again.Any of you 300 recall this sighting, feel free to E-Mail me!!!
