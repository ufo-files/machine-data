QUARTERLY REPORT

                               FUND FOR UFO RESEARCH
                                         P.O. BOX 277
                                 MT. RAINIER, MD. 20712


                        4th Quarter 1999, October-December


    Fund Finances                                                                     - -


            The Fund ends 1999 in good financial shape with total assets at over $32,000.
    We thank our generous contributors.


    New Book
         The UFO Enigma by Dr. Peter Sturrock is now available in bookstores. It is an
    overview of the UFO subject and includes a balanced criticism of Condon's University
    of Colorado UFO Study.


    COMETA Report
            The English translation of the French COMETA report--a two-year study of
    UFOs by former high-ranking French administrators, military officers, engineers, and
    physical and social science researchers--was arranged by the Fund with a contribution
    from a benefactor. The translation is now complete, and a limited number of
    translations have been distributed to members of the media. Efforts are being made to
    place this report on the Fund's publication list.


    Publications
          Scarecrow Press tendered a contract to the Coalition (CUFOS, FUFOR, and
    MUFON) for the UFO Evidence II.        The contract was approved and signed by the
    author and Coalition Directors and returned to Scarecrow.


          Dell Publishing Co. is seeking complete book publishing rights for the
    Coalitions' The Best Available Evidence.


    Upcoming Publications


I       A book on entity sightings in Malaysia is completing review and will be published
    as a joint endeavor between CUFOS and the Fund.


    DNA Study Proposal
           Researchers have critiqued a proposal co-sponsored by the Fund that would
    study possible changes in the DNA of abductees. Because the constituents of "normal"
    DNA are not known to the extent needed to acertain whether "alien" DNA might be
    present, the original proposal was turned down. An alternative study involving analysis
    of family trees of abductees might be feasible.
