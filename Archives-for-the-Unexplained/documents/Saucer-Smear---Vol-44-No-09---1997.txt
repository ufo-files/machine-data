A MILD ADVENTURE INTO THE NIGHT :
---                ----




       On the evening of September 13th, your fearless editor visited the
little town of Pine Bush, New York, together with UFO/psychic researcher
Chris Burns of Bloomfield, N.J. Pine Bush is another alleged "window area"
like Gulf Breeze, Florida, except that the Pine Bush phenomena are weirder
and closer to the ground. They have been photographed many times by Ellen
Crystall, Dr. Bruce Cornet, and several other past & present members    of
the local "hard core". Chris Burns and I saw nothing, but we did mingle
with a couple of carloads of people at the local viewing site, and     the
trip was worthwhile as an introduction to the area.
       The phenomena seen are said to be: round opaque globes of varying
sizes;   unnatural fog;  strings of segmented lights which weave and blink;
and elongated lights in the shape of poles. There have, in general, been
fewer sightings in recent years than there were in the 1980s, and the phe­
nomena usually are further from the viewers than they used to be -    for
whatever reason.
       We do hope to go back to Pine Bush some day, to see more people and
hopefully more phenomena!
