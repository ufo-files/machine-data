  Hello Friends
  The 'Prophet's Conference' was created by Cody and Robin Johnson,
  several years ago, to sponsor conferences at various places in the
  world. They have "Big Name" speakers talking about the coming
  transition to a higher consciousness. They also have a newsletter that
  announces these conferences, talks about new books, and sometimes has
  articles written by upcoming speakers. I have subscribed to their
  newsletter for some time now, and often get articles for SERI through
  this source. I sometimes list the conferences on SERI's 'Upcoming
  Events' page. I believe that they have common interests.
  The Prophet's Conference has now decided to go "social", and establish
  small groups worldwide, that they will call "MeetUps" of "Cafe Cody
  Salons". This is similar to SERI's Forums. Our group in Columbia is
  the SERI 'Mother Forum', since it meets at SERI's International
  Headquarters: http://seri-worldwide.org/forums.html.
  They will list all these MeetUps on their website, so that anyone living
  nearby can know to contact them. I thought that we had nothing to lose
  and everything to gain, if we could be listed. Maybe we might pick up
  some interested folks in our neck of the woods. Since we are already
  meeting, I gave Robin Johnson our meeting date, location and the names
  and email addresses of the ten of you that most regularly attend. The
  fringe benefit is that you will be sent the notices of the meeting a
  week ahead, which contains a link to a video of a recent conference
  speaker. This could generate and spark discussion at our meetings.
  Ralph
