Meeting protocol, December 10th, 1946.
Subject: projectiles over Sweden.

Present:

From Fst:
Major Cervell, Chairman
Captain Stanberg

From FF:
Chief of Bureau Norlin
Fld Kjellson
Fld Noren
Fli Malmberg

From E3:
Lieutenant Carlsson

From KMF:
Captain Krokstedt

From FOA:
Director General Björkesson
Head of Department Fehrm
Laborator Skärblom

From the FRA:
Kempe, Head of Division

1. Due to Colonel Jacobson being preoccupied abroad, Major Cervell served as chairman.

2.

Major Cervell announced that since the meeting on November 21st a discussion had been held between representatives of the air defense department and FF, ultimately resulting in the decision that a memo would be supplied to the Supreme Commander concerning the many unexplained observations of light phenomena and actions associated with them.

3. Captain Stangenberg went through this memo and discussed the related events that have taken place since the meeting on November 21st. Ever since then, 23 reports have been submitted, peaking on November 27 and December 3rd. On December 3rd, in the vicinity of Falköping, a most unusual observation was made by Colonel Unge, along with four officers and 9 privates during nocturnal circumstances. Refer to the special report on the matter.

The radar station at Simrishamn has been moved to Kristianopel. As of now, the station on Gotland is no longer in use.

4.

After a lengthy discussion, it was decided that these operations will be pursued until further notice, excluding the eventual retraction of both radar station activity as well as airborne reconnaissance. The radar station on Mälsten will be operated according to the KMF.

5.

The secretary was instructed to prepare the memo to the Supreme Commander. Notwithstanding proposals to include contact with Western military intelligence, it should include examples of how to quickly obtain the necessary radar stations from abroad.

6.

Head of Department Fehrm announced that the experiences from FRA reconnaissance now show that a separate organization would have to be established in order to guarantee effective and fast results. According to a rough estimate, 17 qualified technicians would be required. Head of Division Kempe showed that the FRA does not currently have the necessary funds to expand their activities.
Major Cervell felt that these issues should be addressed separately by luftförsvarsavdelningen (e.g. the air defense department).

7.

In the unlikely event that any assistance in regards to the evaluation of the reconnaissance might be required, Fehrm will apply a direct request to the administrative authorities.

8.

When completed, the orientation will be circulated to all committee members for their opinion.

9. The next meeting will be held after special notice.

Stockholm, December 10, 1946

Eric Malmberg
