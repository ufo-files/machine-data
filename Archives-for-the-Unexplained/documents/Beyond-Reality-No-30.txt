         Editorial



                 For the past six years, I've been asking myself
               whether or not I am putting out a magazine which
               satisfies the reader. It's not an easy job gathering
               and sorting out information dealing with the
               subject of parapsychology and related fields. The
               main reason is that todays consumers are
               intelligent and well educated. The readers expect
               quality-the type of quality that we here at Beyond
               Reality work so hard to achieve issue after issue.
                 We respect the need of our readers and hope that we have made the right
               decisions. Judging from our mail, we thing we have.
                 Although the going has been rough, our reader response seems to indicate
               that we are on the right track- though there's still room for improvment. I
               want to thank you readers for your loyal support, as well as those few who have
               stood by when the going was rough-Lee Walsh, Bryce Bond, and Lydia
               Belil. I could never fully compensate these individuals enough for the time
               and encouragement they have given me.
                 As we all know, David Berkowitz, the alleged Son of Sam, has been
               captured. Berkowitz claimed that he was spurred to kill by some demonic
               voices .
                      Doctors who have examined him claim that he is insane. We who are
                   involved in parapsychology and psychic research are aware that ''voices''
                   have been heard, and have controlled people throughout history. Even more
                   recently, these voices have been captured on tape by scientists, and several
                   books have been written on the nature of these strange invisible voices. In this
                   issue of Beyond Reality,        Micheal Cohen attempts to analize the
                   voices of Son of Sam. While we believe that such "voices" exist, we are not
                   attempting to condone the murders of innocence dealt by the Son of Sam.
                      Just before going to press, I've received the sad news of the death of Ray
                   Palmer. Mr: Palmer is well known for his many years of involvment with the
                   flying saucer enigma. He was also co-publisher of Fate magazine, and untill
                   recently was still active in publishing journals dealing with UFOs.
                      Please feel free to use the Letters to the Editor column as a forum to
                    exchange thoughts and opinions with fellow readers. I feel that many readers
                    would like to get to know each other, and discuss their thoughts on the
                    various subjects concerning parapsychology.
                      About a year ago, two of our readers met through our Letters to the Editor
                   column, and eventually have married. If you two are still reading this
                   magazine, we here at Beyond Reality Magazine wish you a very Happy
                   Anniversery.
                                                                                        Sincerely
                                                                                       HarryBelil
