Advertiser (Adelaide, SA : 1931 - 1954), Thursday 14 January 1954, page 4

       To the Editor
        Sir— My sister Mrs.                    Cook
       saw     the       flying       saucer     be
       tween          9 pjn.    and     9.30 pjn.
       m     January       8.

         It came   in from   the sea,
       Bying towards    Birkenhead.
       -She    said it was   a large,
       bright   globe             flying       very
       quickly.
                  .
                          M.  BALDWIN.
           Dunn         street, Semaphore.
