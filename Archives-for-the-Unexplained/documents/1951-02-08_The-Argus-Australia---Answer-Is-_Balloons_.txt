Argus (Melbourne, Vic. : 1848 - 1957), Thursday 8 February 1951, page 3




        Answer   is

        "balloons"
             "Argus" phones ran   hot
          last night as   people re-
          ported "strange lights in
          the sky" over  North  Mel-
          bourne.

           They      claimed       the    lights   to   be
        meteorites,         fireballs,     or      falling
        stars.

            One     man         said,    "They     might
        even        be       illuminated           flying
        saucers."
           But     the    Weather        Bureau     said:
        "They       are     just    hydrogen        wind
        balloons.     We     attach      Chinese  lan-
        terns     to them  so  we         can  observe
        them      in the dark.
          "The       wind       tonight   blew      them
        toward      North        Melbourne."
