                       AVEBURY REVISITED

     On   July   15th,  1962,  the  Ley   Hunter's Club and the
Pendragon Society made a visit to the stone      circle  village
of  Avebury    in Wiltshire.  Tony Wedd, Philip Heselton and I
were there, and a memorable day was had, made more so by the
fact that I had the opportunity    of   speaking   about  it  on
television   shortly   afterwards.  Almost exactly twenty-four
years later, my wife Doris and I    visited   the   village,  on
August 1 1th, and we found a number of interesting things.
