Case xxx: 
"Ghost plane" was airplane model

Time: July 10, 21.00 hours (evening)
Place: Vidön, Skoghall (CA38)

Summary: Five witnesses, including labourer Karl Thellander, reported a strange object in the sky: 
first weak sound like a distant motor-boat, minitature airplane 500-1.000 meters distant, wings discernable despite some haze in the air. Plane came from NE and moved towards SSW, "stopping" now and then and performing spirals in the air. It seemed to move forward by going in left-handed circles. It was observed during 15 minutes. Thellander thought it was radio-controlled. 

On July 12 Lennart Bosenius a 17-year old kid from Karlstad queried a local newspaper about the whereabouts of his disappeared model aircraft. The plane was 1,20 between wings and could stay in the air for 5-10 minutes or somewhat longer if winds were favourable. At about 21.30 hours in the evening it was released and moved, against what was expected, towards Vidön and Skoghall. It had probably crashed into Lake Vänern. 

On Sunday, July 14, the model was found, by two fishing men, still floating on the water, six nautical miles south of Hammarön.

Status: Identified, airplane model.
Sources: Värmlands Folblad, July 12, 13 and 16. MorgonTidningen, July 12. Aftontidningen, July 13. ScanCat 1234.
