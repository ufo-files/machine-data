Argus (Melbourne, Vic. : 1848 - 1957), Monday 4 October 1948, page 1




          'Flying saucer'
              eludes
           fighter pilot
       New    York
          A pilot's story of a                  "half
         hour   encounter   with                an     il-
         luminated    disc" over                 North
        Dakota           on     Friday night            is
         featured on the front pages
         of the Sunday papers.
           The     National Guard  pilot made
         his     report to Air Force   Intelli-
         gence.
          When    flying an F51 fighter, he
         said, he encountered   the disc at
         4,500ft.
          It     outran and    outmanoeuvred
         him  until he lost it at 17,500ft.
          The   performance      was  watched
         by two     control   tower  operators
         and  the  pilot  of another plane.
