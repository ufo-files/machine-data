What Made The Circle?
Beloit crop circle attracts attention…  By Tim Unruh…

   BELOIT - Jane Wallace is careful to speculate about what created a large circle in her field of sorghum-sudangrass, but she is certain that it wasn't a UFO. "We're just saying it wasn't man-made," said Wallace, Beloit. 
   "We think was some other kind of phenomena." 
   Whatever caused the circle - 133 feet from east to west and 132 from north to south - it soon became a tourist attraction after it was spotted on Sept. 9 in a field 21/2 miles east of Beloit off Kansas Highway 9.
   "People just started coming by to see it," Wallace said. "It was all over town." And beyond town, too.
   Word reached other states, bringing in crop investigators Rich Webb, Omaha, Neb., and Ted Robertson, Bloomington, Ind.
   "We determined it to be a nonman-made crop circle," Webb said Monday.
