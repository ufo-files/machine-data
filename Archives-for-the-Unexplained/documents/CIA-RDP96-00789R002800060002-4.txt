        The maps depicted a set of islands and I think they were
navigational or shipping charts; there were all sorts of lines
with corresponding numbers, compass-like figures super imposed
upon the maps and numbersfcontour lines in a light color. (I
felt these may have been water depth, or something like that).
        -- There was a solid piece of land along the right side
of the maps; my impression was that this was the western shore
line of a large land mass i.e. North America. There was some
sort of warning associated with the area around one of the
islands. The target individual seemed particularly interested in
this area, and the island adjacent below.      -
           The room was dark as if paneled and there was only a
work table type lamp on. There were windows along the far side
of the room but the curtains were drawn. The room was small.
        -- The target was thinking about "raking somebody over
the coals". He seemed very apprehensive about the "end" of this
activity/operation. He has isolated himself completely from most
of his associates.
        -- I sense that target individual is now in transit as he
is looking over the maps. His mind is well disciplined and
difficult to penetrate. He is very intelligent, verging on
genius.
        -- At this point, he became very agitated as if he was
sensing a probe. There was a loud noise which drew me out of the
room and shifted my focus. This noise could have either been
within our building or within the hallway outside of the room in
which the target was present.
