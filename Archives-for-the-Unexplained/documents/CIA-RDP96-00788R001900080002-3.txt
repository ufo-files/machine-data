    Source stated that a blue car (not further identified) was
parked in front of a business establishment (labeled a small
restaurant in attached drawing, but not further identified).
There was one male (not further identified) in this car. A
second car, green (not further identified), arrived from an
unspecified direction and parked across the street (not
further identified) from the blue car. In this green car were
two men (not further identified). The driver of the green car
remained behind and observed as the passenger of the green car
met with the driver of the blue car.
    The driver of the blue car gave the passenger of the green
car a small brown package approximately three inches long by
two inches wide by one half inch thick. Then the passenger of
the green car gave the driver of the blue car a white envelope
(not further identified). The package given to the passenger
of the green car contained undeveloped film (not further
identified) .  Source provided no information as to the
contents of the white envelope. After this exchange, the
passenger of the green car left the blue car. The blue car
departed in a northerly direction. The green car drove to the
business establishment, picked up the original passenger of
the green car, and departed in a northerly direction.
