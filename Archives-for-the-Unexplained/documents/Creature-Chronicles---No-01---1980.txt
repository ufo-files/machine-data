                                            INTRODUCTION




          At    last,    after     much     prolongment          and   deliberation,       I    am   finally

releasing the first of several issues of this publication.                              Unfortunately,     I

don't have the funds to seek a professional printer at this time, so I will

use the basics to push the first issue.                       I am .sure that the format will be

of interest.



          I    would    also like to take this time to sincerely apologize to all my

friends         and     correspondents           for   not    keeping     a    steady     flow    of   com­

munication.



          The objectives of           "Creature Chronicles11           is to inform Ufologists and

Forteans on the Mystery Animal Syndrome.                            This will be accomplished by

presenting personally investigated cases and newsclippings by myself and

various          researchers,         investigators          and   associates.          These    affiliated

groups and correspondents will be listed periodically in this newsletter.



          This endeavor will also keep law agencies,                      media,       and the scientific

community abreast with documented activity.                            It is our desire to keep in

constant         communication with these public personnel.                       Chronicles will also

describe          other        activities   of    the    group      and       within    the     Ohio   UFO

Investigators League (OUFOIL).



          All recommendations, compliments, or criticisms are welcome, as long

as   it       pertains    to    the   subject manner.            Heresay      evidence,       second hand

testimoney,           assumptions,      or personal insu�ts towards the staff will not be

printed.

no    matter    what   your     personal    theory     is concerning the origin of these

creatures.       It may be that the answer is para-physical manifestations, or

a natural biological animal.            Remember,          the Eastern Cougar is no longer

extinct, as thought.



       EASTERN COUGAR:                          Felis concolor cougar               ( Kerr )
       Order:     Carnivora                     Family:         FEL l DAE



       The     cougar,    sometimes       growing     to    be       8   feet   long    and    up   to   200

pounds    in    weight    is,    except   for   its   size,      very much like a house-cat.

Though its mating cry is often compared with the scream of a woman,                                       it

is merely the caterwaul of a domestic cat amplified.



       Its coat is tawny gray to rust in color, distinguished by dark spots

on either side of its muzzle, · back of its ears,                        and at the tip of its tail.

Color is a genetic trait.



       The     Indians   of     the   Amazon    called     it    COUGAR or             11false deer11    be­

cause of its color.          PUMA is its Spanish-peruvian name.                                �

trappers often called it LEON, New Englanders, CATAMOUNT for 11cat of

the   mountain11,      and     southern    mountaineers,             PANTHER.           Elsewhere it is

called 11Mountain Lion11•
