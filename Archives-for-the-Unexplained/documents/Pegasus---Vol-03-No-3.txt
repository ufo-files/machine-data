                    Paranormal Ufonauts?

      Abductions, close encounters of the fourth kind, have with
increasing frequency been reported in association with the UFO
phenomenon over the past half - century. In different frames of
reference, usually those of folklore and anthropology, accounts
of analogous events have also originated from much earlier times,
accompanied   by   explanations   in   accord with   contemporary
interpretative paradigms. From Rip van Winkel onwards the
fairies, or whatever elemental spirits were held to have been
responsible, seem to have taken a mischievous delight in playing
sometimes vicious tricks on selected specimens of humanity.
Discussion has inevitably centred on how far the events described
by the victims of these attentions are purely subjective or
whether they bear any valid relationship to what we are
accustomed to describe as objective reality. In almost all such
cases the absence of impartial witnesses with no personal
involvement in the events related has precluded any definitive
answer. We have little but anecdotal evidence to work with.
     In at least two such cases, however, witnesses have been
available. Jenny Randles describes how, on 22 February 1973,
"whilst still in the driving seat of her car, and with two
astonished UFO investigators only inches away, Maureen Puddy was
describing the inside of the UFO which she claimed she was now
aboard." (Abduction/ p.129) Randles also tells how, on 3 March
1978, while Jose Alvaro believed himself to be having sex with
an extra - terrestrial female, "a passer by admitted he had seen
the young man prone on the ground at the spot where he later
claimed to recover consciousness. The traveller had walked past,
assuming the youth was drunk or asleep." (Ibid. p.140)
     Such accounts suggest that some, though certainly not all,
UFO abductions seem to have been what occultists and students of
Fortean phenomena describe as "Out of the Body Experiences", or
OBEs. Indeed, the abductees of the 1974 Aveley case, allegedly
abducted with their car, spoke of "a puzzling period where they
'saw' themselves and the car, even though they were floating
about the UFO." (Ibid. p.BO) Such an experience is not uncommon
in the ufological literature, so that there inevitably arises the
question of what it is that undergoes the OBE while the physical
body remains quiescent, very often in its own bed. What kind of
a body is the abducted consciousness then occupying?
     OBEs are not,    of course,    uniquely experienced by UFO
abductees - they have often been reported by people undergoing
surgery under anaesthesia,     especially when near to death.
Occultists, however, claim to be able to evoke the experience at
 will, transferring their consciousness intentionally into what
they call the astral body.
      According to Fred Gettings (Encyclopaedia of the Occult,
 Guild Publishing 1986) , "The astral body is seen by clairvoyants
as having an independent existence on the astral plane during
periods when the physical body is asleep or when a person
consciously indulges in astral travel. During such periods of
conscious travel and during ordinary.sleep (or in the case of an
imposed trance) it is quite natural for the astral body to leave
the physical and etheric bodies behind and to lead an independent
existence on its proper plane of being ..." He adds that this
astral plane "is contiguous in space (if not in time) with the
material realm", which might account for the five days said to
have been lost by Corporal Valdes in fifteen minutes of normal
time in Arica, .Chile on 25 April 1977, or the similar length of
time claimed as lost by Travis Walton two years previously in·


Snowflake, Arizona.
      Moreover, if the astral plane is contiguous in space with
our everyday world, we do not need to invoke the extra -
terrestrial hypothesis (ETH) of UFO or1g1ns to explain the
incursions of some of its denizens into.the frequency band we
normally inhabit. The entity claimed to have visited Cynthia
Appleton in Birmingham on several occasions in the 1950s told her
that scientists trying to reach his place of origin would fail
because they were "... pulling against the great force of gravity
by going straight up ..." (ETH?) , when they should "travel with
a sideways attitude." (A frequency shift above the ultra-violet
or below the infra-red? The alleged statement of a putative
astral entity filtered through the consciousness of a Birmingham
housewife can be expected to be somewhat gnomic!)
      There is evidence to suggest that some abductees are not
randomly   selected but require to have a certain psychic
sensitivity which is perhaps a pre-requisite for making this
postulated frequency shift to the astral. Betty Andreasson' s
account of her alleged abduction by Strieber-type entities on 25
January 1967 at South Ashburnham, Massachusetts refers to her
family being frozen into suspended animation, possibly because
they lacked the psychic constitution which alone permits
manipulation in this way.
       Budd Hopkins reports in Intruders a similar case of
familial immobilisation. After undergoing what has become
virtually the standard physical examination ( Sachs - The UFO
Encyclopaedia 1980) , Andreasson claimed to have been conducted
through a series of alien realms where she encountered strange
and sometimes symbolic creatures and events, similar in many ways
to descriptions by occultists of travels in the astral body.
Aleister Crowley (Magick, Guild Publishing, p. 258) wrote that
"this fine body perceives a universe which we do not ordinarily
perceive ..." and which "        is naturally fitted to perceive
objects of its own class ... in particular the phantoms of the
astral plane."
       He also warned against uncritical acceptance of any
statements such entities may make, for "every spirit, up to God
himself, is ready to deceive you if possible, to make himself out
more important than he is... " (Ibid. p. 260) . Perhaps this may
account for some of the incredible extra-terrestrial origins said
to have been claimed by some uninvited visitants? Crowley also
warned of the dangers of mishandling this return to the physical

descriptions of presumably the same abductors! Are the agents of
the Phenomenon then able, as is often claimed, to present
themselves and their vehicles to human eyes in any form they deem
expedient?
     In the ages of faith before the scientific revolution in
human thinking such questions naturally did not arise - anything
that could not be accomodated within contemporary intellectual
and emotional paradigms was ipso facto evil or illusory, a work
of the devil and therefore to be cast out. When medieval peasants
who had seen ufonauts descend from a space vehicle went in a body
to their bishop,    he told them that such a happening was
impossible and that they had seen no such thing. They believed
him on authority and against the evidence of their own eyes, but
maybe the bishop was not so·stupid after all. Fa�th - any sort
of faith, however misguided - is a sure shield against psychic
manipulation. We sceptics to-day are far more vulnerable - we
cannot look for spells from the local warlock in yellow pages,
or drop in on the priestess of a nearby coven with a request for
some protective wicca-power. Maybe modern victims should try
flooding the house with garlic, wearing amulets and describing
pentagrams around their beds. Such defences were once· deemed
effective - empirically, they should be worth a try!

       On or about the evening of November 30, 1989, at approximately 3:15 AM,
Linda (last name withheld) was asleep in her apartment on the 12th floor of
an apartment building in New York City, together with her husband and two
young sons. She made contact with Budd Hopkins and under hypnosis reported
 being levitated off her bed, approached by three grey aliens, and transported
 with them through her apartment wall out above the street and then up into a
floating disk. She reported that she sensed her nightgown being drawn up to
her face.
       A few weeks later, Dan (last name withheld) wrote a letter to Hopkins,
stating that he and his partner Richard (last name withheld) had witnessed the
event from a car parked on the street about a block away.        He described a
young woman and three small grey creatures emerging from the side of the
building at the 12th floor in a fetal position, arms wrapped around their
legs, head up, with one creature above the woman and the other two below and
to each side. After emerging from the side of the building, their bodies
extended and they were then lifted up and into the floating disk, which was
described as having an orange glowing topside, with yellow illumination
extending down and outward, except for a narrow bluish beam extending straight
downward up which the four figures were lifted. Dan reported that after the
figures entered the craft, it then moved over and down into the river (name
withheld by author) and never reappeared. Dan described himself and Richard
as "undercover policemen" who would not publicly come forward for fear of
damaging their careers. Dan said they were both extremely upset by the
incident, fearing for the life or safety of the young woman, and distressed
that they had been unable to do anything to help her. Dan stated that he and
Richard would attempt to visit the apartment from which Linda had been taken
to see what became of her.
       Having no way to contact Dan or Richard, Hopkins read their letter to
Linda, advising her that they might visit her, and asking her to ask them to
contact him again if they did.
       Dan and Richard then went to Linda' s apartment, and when they identified
themselves, Linda told them Hopkins had told her to expect them. Dan and
Richard were very relieved that Linda was seemingly okay, and Linda expressed
mixed feelings, saying that it might have been easier to accept being crazy
than having it be confirmed that it really happened.
       Dan and Richard did not agree to visit Hopkins, but Linda did get them
to agree to send Hopkins an audiotape giving a more detailed account of the
events of November 30, and a few days later Hopkins did receive the audiotape,
which he played for the MUFON audience. Dan reported that Richard was so
concerned about Linda that he had taken to observing her and her apartment
during off hours to make sure she was okay.
       In the meantime, Linda developed discomfort in her right nostril and
visited her physician, who took an Xray. A day or two later, before the Xray
was developed and returned, Linda woke up with blood over her face and bedding
from her right nostril. The Xray was shown to the MUFON audience. It clearly
showed a cylindrical radio-opaque object, perhaps 2-4 mm in length, with a
helical pointed extension off each end and to one side. Subsequent examination
showed no object present and under hypnosis it came out that Linda had been
visited again by aliens and the object removed.
       In the meantime, Hopkins tried to identify Dan and Richard. Then he was
contacted by a woman who reported that she had been driving on a major New
York bridge (name withheld by author) on the evening of November 30, 1989,

                               BY SUSAN GREENI



       More than 60 campers gathered on a Lincoln County mountaintop this past
weekend for a typical tent-fumbling, fire building, weenie roasting kind of
campout. Except for a few minor details.
Like the view 12 miles to the west of a secret air base that doesn 't
officially exist.    And the camouflaged security police monitoring the group
from nearby ridges with high-tech telescopes and night vision cameras. There
were also signs, just 1, 000 feet from the campsite, warning "use of deadly
force authorized" should one of the campers have accidentally wandered across
the base's border while searching for some firewood.
       Faced with such obstacles, where else but on a ridge overlooking the
military's secret Groom Lake, Area 5 1, or "Dreamland" testing facility would
aviation buffs, government watchdogs, alien abductees, conspiracy theorists,
filmmakers, reporters, marijuana-smoking slackers, card-carrying anarchists,
socialists and libertarians, a Lincoln County commissioner and a conch-blowing
religious zealot and his second cousin so peacefully come together on a craggy
peak for a weekend in the great outdoors?
       "Only on Freedom Ridge, " said Groom Lake gadfly Glenn Campbell of the
public high ground he christened two years ago that offers the best vantage
point of the secret base.
Campbell, the author of "The Area 5 1 Visitor's Guide" and publisher of "The
Groom Lake Desert Rat" newsletter, organized the weekend-long "Groomstock" as
a protest and last hurrah before the Air Force extends the base's restricted
zone by 3972 acres to prevent gawkers like himself from stealing glimpses of
its seven mile runway and haze of buildings and hangars.
      Last September, Air Force officials filed to withdraw Freedom Ridge and
nearby White Sides Mountain from public lands, citing a need to protect the
safe and secure operation of the 3. 5 million acre Nellis Air Force Range
complex. The range includes the secret base, which the federal government has
never acknowledged exists.
      A decision by Interior Secretary Bruce Babbitt on whether to lock up the
additional land is at least six months away, Bureau of Land Management
officials said Friday. During this weekend's campout, opinions about the
withdrawal varied as much as reasons that led campers to the mountain ridge
in the first place.
      A few who believe the military uses the secret base to hide alien
spacecraft or conduct genetic tests on abducted children opposed the
withdrawal on grounds that it would keep them from monitoring clandestine
activities. For most, however, viewing the base is less important than
answering questions about it. Although Campbell and his minions have resigned
themselves to the likelihood of the withdrawal, they question the government's
need for absolute secrecy in this post Cold War era.
      "If they would just act like responsible adults and fess up to what
they're doing out there, that would satisfy most of the curiosity, " said Tom
Mahood, a civil engineer from Irvine, Calif. "The military doesn't have to
treat civilians like we're the enemy, especially when it's our tax dollars
paying for it. "
      Originally built in the early 195 0's to test the Lockheed U-2, the Groom
Lake base has been used to test high-altitude, high-speed spy planes and other
military aircraft, including the F-117A Stealth fighter, according to aviation
industry sources and witnesses who have observed the installation.
      The base has sparked particular controversy this year, including two
citizen action lawsuits filed earlier this month on behalf of workers who
     allegedly suffered from illegal and unregulated open-air burning of toxic
     materials.   Also in three incidents since March, Lincoln County sheriff's
     deputies have detained news crews suspected of illegally filming the
     installation.
           In snubs to base officials, the campers directed phony cameras at the
     base, organized a stadium-style wave for the military police observing them
     from opposite ridges and posted signs along the road to Freedom Ridge where
     guards placed covert road sensors.
           Then, on Saturday evening, when the sun went down and their protests
     were over, the campers kicked back around the campfire to toast marshmallows,
     gab about "black world" spy planes and UFO abductions and gaze into the clear
     night sky, transfixed, searching for a glimpse of something, anything
     inexplicable.

           - LAS VEGAS REVIEW-JOURNAL, 28 AUGUST 1994.

          Based on other' s observations, personal experience,       conjecture,   &
    hypothesis, the following is presented.

         A. "Greys" as alien life forms exhibit:
           1. No viewable ears.
I          2. No viewable nose.
           3. Very small mouth.
           4. Very large eyes, with large & dark appearing pupils.
           5 . Very large cranium, with perspective to the rest of the body.
           6. Adult size is small, approximately 3 (three) feet in height.
           7. Off white/grey skin or outer covering.
           8. No apparent sexual organs. Reproduction is, evidently, either asexual
               or non-existent.

       B. Synopsis:
         1. With no visible ears or nose, with large eyes & cranium, it would
    appear that there is a dependency to sense through the abilities of sight,
    touch, & MENTAL POWERS (this may include the talent to communicate using a
    form of thought transference).

    having a visible nose & a VERY small mouth would seem to point out. Either
    that, or there are other breathing dynamics involved. Instead, if gases are
    required for proper functioning, they COULD be absorbed through the skin.
         3. Because of the lack of sexual organs, either:
            a. The "greys" are creations of another species.
                1. Because of the "sexless" nature of "greys", they MIGHT be the
    genetic engineering creation of a species more advanced than ever thought of
    by humankind.
                 2. The beings, whose creations are the "greys", MAY have come to
    a point in evolution where having a "physical being" is unnecessary in order
    to survive, thus creating the "greys" became a advantageous step for them
    in order to have their presence felt in the Universe.
             b. The "greys" that are "space travellers" MAY have been surgically

THE QUEST FOR 'ALIEN IDENTITIES'
by Vicki Cooper


CHILD OF THE STARS-An abductee might remember being told about having a dual
identity-one as the human person on Earth, and another as an extraterrestrial
creature from another planet. Some feel this may represent a cosmic
intermingling of species and a promise for the future.
For their part, few 'experiencers' appear to be concerned about the methods
used by people who help them recover their memories. And most will tell you:
for all their similarities, no two encounter reports are exactly alike. More
to the point, the classic tale of 'grey aliens' and their probing
medical/genetic experimentation has perhaps grown tiresome, giving away to a
panoply of variations. While stories still give everyone the willies, many
reports now deal with areas reflecting the 'consciousness' polemic of Mack and
others. Interestingly, this 'new wave' of kinder, gentler alien beings often
shows up as white, glowing - even angelic. Also readily evident in Mack's book
and elsewhere are abductees expressing the idea that their relationship with
the alleged aliens began before birth, inspiring some hypnotherapists to
regress clients into past lives. Other paranormal components have always been
there: telepathy, out-of-body experiences, visions of light, ghost-like
appearances and disappearances, disembodied voices, ecstatic states. Even
prophetic warnings and spiritual messages-in fact, numerous encounters revolve
around specific messages the aliens apparently want to emphasize, many having
to do with a cataclysmic end of the world, along with information about the
experiencer's role or mission on what's portrayed as a progressively dying
planet. Some are now being informed that they have dual human-alien
identities. Just another facet of an illusion? Or another phase of alien
intervention revealed?

     It is generally observed that all clients in therapy - and
hypnotherapy especially - are influenced by the theoretical
perspective embraced by their analyst. Thus, people in primal
scream therapy remember their birth experience,        clients of
psychoanalysts produce Freudian dreams and those in past life
therapy recall previous incarnations.
     The    hypnotic   trance  is   a  condition   of   heightened
suggestibility wherein the beliefs and assumptions of the
hypnotist cannot but influence the outcomes of the encounter. In
social work practice the alleged recovery of repressed memories
of childhood abuse which are shown to have no basis in fact has
already achieved recognition as the False Memory Syndrome (FMS) .
None of this, however, necessarily supports a view of the
investigator as anything other than totally honest and well -
intentioned; indeed, he is himself often the principal victim of
the delusion.
     In the investigation of UFO abduction cases an excessive
trust in the validity of hypnotically generated material has
already been widely stigmatised and Frank Johnson's account of
the mill ion refugees from the mythical planet Janos wandering the
universe in .search of a new home was published (1980) in all good
faith, but totally discredited. It should have served as a
salutary warning to Harvard psychiatrist Dr. John E. Mack, whose
recent (1994) work claims on its dust cover to provide details
"reported in no previous books or television programmes." This,
unfortunately, is indeed the case and confirmation from other
investigators may not be readily forthcoming.
     From hypnotic sessions with a small opportunity sample of
not very representative subjects, Dr. Mack has evolved a complex
ideational structure including reincarnation, transmigration of
alien identities into human bodies and a prognosis of impending
ecological disaster which will destroy humanity and permit the
re-population of the planet with beings half human and half
alien. This is seen as a logical and desirable development from
the past histories of both races, facilitating the evolution of
beings of more highly developed consciousness in accordance with
a pre-existing master plan.
     Lest it be thought that the subjects providing this
information were in any way psychologically abnormal, Dr. Mack
offers his personal assurance of their normality and arranged for
just one of them to undergo a series of recognised mental tests
without, however, giving the scores. Unlike Dr. David Jacobs, to
whose (1992) book he contributed a foreword, Mack does not
present in evidence transcriptions of the hypnotic sessions,
merely quoting occasional sentences out of context. We are
expected, in short, to accept his conclusions entirely on the
basis of his academic record. It is not enough.
