at lunchtime on Sunday.
   'It was black and it was oscillating,like a plate spinning
on the top of a stick. We watched it for about 30 or 40 seconds
before it disappearerl in the direction of Southampton docks'.
   Mrs Hunt watched the mystery craft through binoculars with
her son and daughter,Sarah Macdonald.
   Sarah,18,said 'It was a disc shape and it was hovering.I
really haven't got a clue what it was.'
   Mrs Hunt added : 'I was sceptical about UFOs before this but
you have to keep an open mind'.
   The sighting is the latest in a series over the city -
although most have been strange lights in the sky rather than
daytime sightings.

encounter• .
