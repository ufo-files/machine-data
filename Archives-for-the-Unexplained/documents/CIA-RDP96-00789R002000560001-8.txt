associated with the vehicle <AOL-'fireman').    Doors are present on the
vehicle on top, in a forward position, on the sides, and on the back.
The door on the side appears to be an "access panel".    Doors in the rear
open outward to move things in and out of the vehicle; little excess
room is available inside.   An object is located ihside, near the top and
toward the front of the vehicle.

other than appearances would indicate.")
