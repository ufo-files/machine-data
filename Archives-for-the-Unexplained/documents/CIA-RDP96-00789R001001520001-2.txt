                        Summary of Information

     The target is located in a remote region of extreme terrain
with steep slopes and flat-topped knolls or hills.     Structures
occur both on the surface and subsurface.    The exposed portions
of structures blend into the top of these flattened knolls/hills.
Those above ground (see page iv, diagrams a and b) appear large
and occur far above everything else [manmade] in the area. There
is a large rectangular opening in the side of a downslope which
leads into a large, man-made or -manipulated chamber.

     Diagram ii(a) feels as if it is both within, and outside, of
an area or location. Diagrams ii(b) and iii are located within a
large room or chamber. The curved surface of diagram iii is used
either to lift something up into place or to act as additional
support for another object.
     There is an image of stacks of cylinders.          The upper
surfaces of these cylinders are covered with bubbles as if the
covering is corroded or "boiling".       There is a mechanical
clunking sound reminiscent of treaded machinery nearby.
     There is the concept of the earth bubbling up (not unlike
hot mud springs) is present. There is the smell of cooking meat.
This ''takes your breath away".
     An activity on site has been sanctioned by upper echelons;
it is controlled, dictated, non-releasable and closed-in.    This
endeavor is associated with a sense of bewilderment.   There is a
slow rolling and spinning downward motion over jagged edges.
Large flying insects are somehow involved with the activity.
Chemicals are involved.

     There is a concept of "weight-to-flight" ratio and a series
of adjacent truncated shapes reminiscent of the interior of a
sound studio.

     A bottle has fallen over and an 11 outsider" opens it.  There
are feelings of displeasure because the contents are foul. There
is a combined effort to "clean up" a spill.              There is
frustration, anxiety and "physical collapse". This has produced
great anxiety.
     An activity occurs which "squeezes off" smaller entities
producing a separation from the main body or unit.   There is an
overall concept of collection/reduction at this target.
     AOL's: LTC Higgins; a cliff edge; a sci-fi movie with giant
bats; an Iraqi facility; Rabta, Libya; An Algerian attempt to
process nuclear fuel for someone else.
