  From the Book Cover

     * UFO Encounters * Ghosts, Poltergeists, and Other Apparitions * Voodoo Rituals * Mysterious Creatures * Crime-
                        Solving ESP * Unexplained Graveyard Exhumations * Occult Sacrifices

  In the line of duty, police officers are frequently placed in positions where that are likely to witness strange events.
  Often the events are explainable. Sometimes, however, no earthly explanation can account for them: a brush with the
  occult, perhaps; a mysterious, otherworldly apparition; a startling glimpse of psychic phenomena; events so baffling
  they simply can't be explained -- or controlled -- by any law of science or of man.

  Hidden Files is a collection of these astonishing encounters, as told by the police and other law enforcement officers
  who experiences them. From ghosts, poltergeists, and psychic perception to UFOs, crop circles, and sudden,
  inexplicable disappearances, each fascinating occurrence offers intriguing evidence of powers that are beyond our laws -
  - and ofter beyond our comprehension.

  Sue Kovach is an investigative journalist.
