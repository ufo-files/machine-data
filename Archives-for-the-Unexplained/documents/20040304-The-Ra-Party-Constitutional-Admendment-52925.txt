   Frank D. Whitney was sworn in as United States Attorney for the Eastern

   Reserve Military Intelligence Corps. He served on active duty as an
   Intelligence Officer until leaving to attend law and business schools at the

   with honors from law school and received his MBA. He then clerked for the
   Honorable David B. Sentelle, Circuit Judge for the United States Court of
   Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit and entered private practice
   with the Washington, D. C., office of the firm of McKenna, Connor and Cuneo.
   In April of 1990, he joined the United States Attorney's Office for the
   Western District of North Carolina as an Assistant U. S. Attorney, where he
   served in numerous positions in the Criminal and Civil Divisions until April
   2001. He then joined the firm of Kilpatrick & Stockton, LLP, as counsel in
   the firm's Charlotte office, where he practiced until his appointment as
   United States Attorney. Mr. Whitney has continued his military service in
   the Army Reserve both as an Intelligence Officer and as a Judge Advocate. He
   is a former paratrooper, received three Meritorious Service Medals, and
   recently was selected for promotion to Lieutenant Colonel.
