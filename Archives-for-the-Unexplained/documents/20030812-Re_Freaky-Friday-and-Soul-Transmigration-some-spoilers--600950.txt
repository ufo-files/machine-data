  There was a version of this story written back in Victorian times
  (don't know the author but I saw a TV adaptation when I was a kid, I
  think it was quite a famous author):
  Typical Victorian father (cold, uncaring and uninterested in his son)
  gets swapped with son; father ends up at boarding school where he is
  bullied and beaten, son ends up running father's business (badly,
  losing loads of money, but he is more popular with everyone than his
  father and has a pretty good time until the money starts running out).
  It is a neat way to get across some pretty simple messages: listen to
  others and try to see things their way, don't assume that anyone
  else's life is any easier than your own, etc.
  Obviously, if someone turned up in real life claiming to have been
  "swapped" in this way they would be seen as crazy.
