     On 12 April 1983, Source CL-1 was interviewed in an
 attempt to locate and identify a specific item of interest.
 Source was asked to describe the events and activities of
 the location known to him as the rail yard staging area
 during the period of 8 and 9 April 1983.     Source stated
 substantially as follows:
     During 8 and 9 April 1983, trains were off loaded in the
 rail yard and equipment (not further identified) was moved
 into holding areas and/or loaded on "lowboys" or trucks (not
 further identified}.    Source also described an area
 approximately 3 kilometers away which w~s near some aircraft
 (not further identified) and had objects (not further
 identified) sitting ins ide circles.  Source described some
 of the objects as white tubes, possibly 12 feet long,
 stacked in groups of 4, and banded together. The end of the
 object or stack looked like a 4 leaf clover.      Source
 describe another object as being towed, with 2 fold out
 arms, wheels which came off the ground and a long straight
 piece that raised up into the air. Source provided drawings
 (see EXHIBIT I) of these objects and others which he
 reported as being supplies brought in by rail but having
 something to do with aircraft.
     No further amplifying information concerning the events
 and activities of the rail yard on 8 and 9 April 1983, was
 provided by Source during this interview.
     Attached hereto as EXHIBIT I are drawings provided by
 Source.
