                              IN REVIEW
     If last year (1991) was the low point for many years at the Centre, then
1992 saw a dramatic change in the profile of the UFO phenomena on the local
scene. Sightings were at the low level of recent years until a run of cases in
August to October, this combined with some TV documentaries and the opening in
Hobart of the UFO Connection shop kept the phenomena in·the public eye.
     During the period September 1991 to August 1992 the centre had only one
unexplained report.   However,   following publicity on an incident in mid
September not only calls on current cases came to hand but also a string of
very interesting sightings dating back to 1968. Other sightings continued to
come to our attention in the remaining months of .1992.
    As will be noted from the list of Australian sightings elsewhere in this
issue a general increase in reports has occured nation wide. New South Wales
during 1991/92 experienced its best run of reports in many years, in fact most
                                                                     .
Autralian states at one time or another during 1992 had their turn with
localised runs of UFO sightings.
    The main focus overseas has continued to be the crop circle phenomena,
Colin Andrews visit in April was of great interest, his lecture on the crop
circles were presented in a very professional manner. One interesting section
covered the video footage taken by an English family whilst on an outing to
see the circles. The video captured a small light moving over the corn field,
entering a circle formation, then departing past a startled tractor driver.
Again in 1991 two German tourists caught a similar light on their video, again
it was moving over the crops and entering an existing circle. Once more during
1992 we hear that further film has been taken of similar lights in conjunction
with the crop circle phenomena.
    Later in the year Bud Hopkins from the United States visited Australia,
his lectures on the abduction phenomena have brought to our attention yet
another facet of the UFO story. UFO Research Australia made a major press
release in October in an effort to collect information on similar events in
Australia, they have received an encouraging response which indicates that
there may be hidden abduction cases in Australia.
    No major flap of cases seems to have accured on the world stage during the
year, although the lights at Gulf Breeze (USA) seem to have simmered on with
claims that its all due to fake balloons, excited observers, or maybe its the
real thing, at this distance we just can't sort out the answer.
    The centre has continued to record local sightings in its TASCAT computer
listing, still on 1y one of a number of UFO data bases around the world, and
more importantly it only holds sightings that have had some measure of
investigation. The centre through Treasurer Paul Jackson's computer have also
in recent months logged onto the Paranet Network, this has enabled us to
download information as it has happened both in Australia and overseas.
    About the only poor news during 1992 was that membership fees for the
Centre had to rise to cover the inevitable cost increases. Despite the first
rise in fees for a decade membership of the Centre has continued to rise.

     How many sightings go unreported to the Centre, judging from response to a
press release in September (1992) we would have to say the bulk of cases may

The disappearance of the object took just a few seconds. The Centre was able

Bridgewater. Their account was essentially the same, a flashing light was seen
briefly in the North-West, then it suddenly shot off to the East and was gone
in a few seconds.

                                  A HIGHLAND C Y L I N DER

     Just what did the two motorists see whilst travelling to Queenstown on the
Lyell Highway on the evening of the 13th? Tedd and his friend Allan were on
their way to the Hydro-Electric works on the West Coast about 9. 30pm, they
were about half way between Bronte and Derwent Bridge.
     Allan (the passenger) had been looking at the fine night sky but then he
noticed something to the West, it looked to be crossing their Northern sky.
Allan watched for a couple of minutes before bringing it to Tedd 1 s attention.
Tedd continued to drive on but found it hard to view the object so he decided
to stop the car. Both witnesses got out of the car and they were able to watch
for another 2 or 3 minutes before the object moved away into the North-East
sky.
     Tedd says the object was about the same width as the moon and was a dull
grey colour in the . bright moonlight. The shape was cylinder like with a
rounded front. To the rear there seemed to be a blue flame. The strangest
thing though were some four.or more lights travelling with the cylinder. These
orange and brown lights could be seen moving in a steady formation, but as the
cylinder was passing away they suddenly started to buzz around the main
object, almost like fireflies. The lights kept up this performance as the
whole group gained some elevation towards the North-East, then the lights all
seemed to merge with the cylinder and the whole thing just disappeared from
the sky. The whole sighting lasted some 5 to 8 minutes. Only one other motor
vehicle was seen but there was no indication that the driver had noticed
anything. Certainly Tedd and Allan did not see the burn-up which had occured
earlier that evening.
