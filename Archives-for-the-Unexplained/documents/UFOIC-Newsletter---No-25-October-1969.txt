FINLAND - SNOW LANDING
Nivala in the western part of middle Finland was the scene of an interesting UFO sighting on
1qth November, 1968. At 5 p.m. Oili Antilla ( 16) went out to get water some distance from
her home, when she saw a strange bright "star" appear in the sky. It was descending rapidly,
so Oili, curious to see what it was, stood in the snow, under tall trees. The round object,
glowing brilliant white with a pulsating red middle, emitted a low humming sound as it flew
in a straight line above electric wires, then hovered over the trees, finally landing about
350 yards away from the girl. As it rested briefly the object glowed brighter, then rose
swiftly into the sky and disappeared. A round, shallow mark was found at the landing place
and it is interesting to note that, while the UFO was in the area, lights in the houses went
out and a fuse in the village transformer was blown.  (Ref. Vimana)

A report from Tasmania comes from Mr. & Mrs. P. Berry of Hobart who were sitting on their
front verandah at 10.50 on the morning of July 17th, 1969, when their attention was caught
by a dark grey, circular object with domed top, about the size of a full moon, travelling
under low clouds in a N-easterly direction. As they watched, a second object appeared and
followed behind the other. Mr. Berry hurried inside for his camera but by the time he
photographed them, they were mere specks under the clouds. Blow ups of the photographs
showed nothing, but Mr. Berry estimated the UFOs to be 20 ft in diameter.

white to deflect heat. Radio telescopes have great sensitivity - a modest sized one can
detect power of a hand torch while a very large one has the possibility of detecting very
powerful radio sources at the outer limits of the Universe.   Their shapes also differ. The
Mills Cross near C anberra has such a narrow beam that it can accurately bring in sources of
waves, while the Radio Heliograph near Narrabri has 96 dishes 40 ft across in a ring 2 miles
in diameter.  Pulsars have been checked for intelligent signals, but to date no results have
been achieved. However, by operating with the unique wavelength of 2 1em radiation, radio
