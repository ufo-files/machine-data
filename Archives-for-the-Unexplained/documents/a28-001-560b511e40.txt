  In private email <dvoyeur@ibm.net> writes:
  >I have just been browsing through the web page and read the message
  >you placed in the Area 51 section regarding Radio shows. I know that
  >in general there are no notes or records of what is said on the
  >programs, but in the specific case that you mentioned the Art Bell
  >program, there are on the Internet virtually all the programs
  >broadcast for quite a while into the past available for listening via
  >real audio in the art bell web site audio archives at the following
  >address : http://ww2.audionet.com/artbell/artbell.html
  >
  >I would think that if the area 51 group were so inclined they could
  >quite easily skim through the programs for the info they want then
  >transcribe it.
  I would argue with the word "transcribe." No one needs to transcribe
  these broadcasts, which would be a massive task.
  All a listener needs to do is paraphrase what the guest said, e.g.
       Joe Smith appeared on Art Bell last night. Said world will come
       to end on Oct. 31. Said aliens are bloodsucking reptilians who
       want our women.
  This can be followed by a list of specific claims made by Smith.
  The report can be as long or short as you have time for, but it
  should be neutral. Don't say what you think of Smith's claims,
  just tell us what he said.
