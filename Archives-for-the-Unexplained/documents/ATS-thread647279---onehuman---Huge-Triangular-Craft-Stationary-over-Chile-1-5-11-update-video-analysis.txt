reply to post by SkepticOverlord

I have to disagree completely with you. It is actually quite difficult to bring out the level of
definition you would need in order to prove conclusively this was not one craft. Having
worked with video technology for much of my life, and having always had an interest in
how videos can be enhanced, I can tell you a few filters will simply not do the job, unless
what is being enhanced is an area that has such optical data already encoded into the
digital medium or grain of the film itself. Its entirely likely....especially with a shot like
this where you have bright light contrasting with a dark background...that there is simply
not enough raw data there which can be enhanced to show a solid craft (if it is there).
There are a few much more complex methods which MIGHT bring out a bit more detail,
such as a process by which individual frames are lined up and super imposed upon one
another, but honestly after seeing the video I seriously doubt much more could be made
out of it. Its not quite like it is in the movies, where you can enhance any video, or
photograph so much that you can actually seemingly go far past what the camera
recorded. In truth it is only possible to enhance up to the point that the resolution of the
original image, and no more. Of course I am not suggesting anything proves this is a solid
craft......this is not apparent either. There is simply no way to tell unless someone has
better footage.
