  Hi All,
  The expedition has completed, it is necessary to summarize. I. FACTS
  1. To confirm a existence of required object on the place was failed,
  which Emil Bachurin has identified as authentic. To find the apparent
  anomalies was failed, such as the trail of sliding, tracks of squeeze (it
  must remain, as a surface of the platform consists of softlimber clay),
  traces of explosion. To find a magnetic anomaly was failed - a compass,
  and devices of a helicopter acted perfectly quietly, except for a single
  jump of altimeter, which is explained by sharp differences of heights.
  2. To look on psychological state of mind of Emil Bachurin at the place of
  suppositional UFO's accident, he really witnessed something unusual,
  because under landing has seen a proper shock, not finding out the object
  on a place. All rest of time before returning it seems he was very
  depressed and contemplative.
  3. The presence of Shaitan Mazar (the name is not geographical) was
  confirmed. A village of shepherds (Shaitan Mazar) is approximately 10 kms
  from the site. Periodically a local mullah is engaged by curing of people
  there, because there is a sacred spring nearby. One shepherd, which was
  met on a way to an airdrome, had told us in detail about this place.
  4. To find out an alternative route of Svechkov's expedition was possibly,
  which without any special handicapes enables to drive directly to Shaitan
  Mazar and therefrom already to go on foot by mountains.
  5. To find the Svechkov's son was possible, which Emil Bachurin and Alexey
