                  THE MORICHES BAY CASE OF SEPTEMBER 28,1989


              The area of Moriches Bay has been for ,a long time a
         area of high activity for U.F.O. sightings. Several LIUFON
         "Skywatch Teams", have reported sightings over the Bay and
         on three separate occasions objects have been videotaped
         by LIUFON teams. The results have been good and have left
         us with tbe impression .~..Mor.iches Bay may be a window
         of some sort. for these objects. The surrounding areas of
         Center Moriches, Shirley and Mastic Beach have also been
         centers of heavy sightings.
             What occurred on the night of September 28,1989 over
         Moriches Bay far surpassed anything that has happened before
         in LIUFON history.It was a sighting of truly epic proportions
         for the Long Island area.
               The story began on the night of September 28, 1989 at the
         Peterson's residence in Center Moriches. At about 8:30 p.m. or
         8:45 p.m., the witness approximates the occurrence began. Mr.
         Paul Peterson, the husband, was watching television in the living
         room of his Union Avenue home. He noticed out the glass doors
         which led to his backyard deck that there appeared two large amber
         lights. His first reaction was to think that they were reflections of
         his ceiling track lights. When they didn't disappear in a few minutes
         he got up to examine the strange lights.
             Examining them, he realized that they were lights over the
         Bay and not reflections. Opening the glass door, he stepped out onto
         his deck. The lights were huge and some distance away.
             The lights just hung in the sky and were of an unnatural
         brillance. It was about this time that he called them to his wife's
         attention. She stepped out onto the deck also. Watching these
         strange lights, the two observed a large Sikorsky helicopter zoom
         low over the trees bordering thei.r backyard. They could hear other
         helicopters in the distance. The impression was that there were more
         than one in the area.The noise from the helicopters was deafening.
             Realizing that something was going on, he called for his son
         Robert tojoin him outside. His son came downstairs and joined his Dad
         on the deck. Mr. Peterson went inside to obtain his video camera
         to tape the lights and aircraft activity.
             Using the camera, Mr. Peterson took several minutes of tape
         of the large amber lights. Quickly, he ran inside to play back the
         video he just made. To his amazement the tape was blank but still
         had audio. The camera had been working since he had had visual
         observation gf his surroundings through the view finder. He just
         could not explain the reason for not being able to record the
         &trahge lights over the Bay.
             Christine, Paul Peterson's wife, became very alarmed at the
         spectacle that was unfolding before her eyes. She went inside and
         locked the back door. The helicopter activity and the strange
         lights continued.
             Mr. Peterson and his son Robert decided to go down to the town
