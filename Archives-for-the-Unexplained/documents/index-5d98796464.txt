    Our Review                                                                       Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  Describes the odd happenings in the San Luis Valley in south-central Colorado. You have your lights
  darting across the sky. You have your black helicopters. You have strange military maneuvers, bigfoot
  sightings, cattle mutilations, abductions, and an occasional devil sighting or two. The San Luis Valley has
  it all, and this book is your definitive guide to the area. In spite of the potentially lurid subject matter,
  O'Brien is restrained in his reporting, and avoids jumping to conclusions. Dozens of incidents and claims
  are faithfully recorded, mostly from 1989 through 1995, in a likeable book that is easy to read. (It is
  marred, however, by the lack of an index.) -- tm/gc

  From the Book Cover

  "THIS IS NOT A WORK OF FICTION...IN COLORADO AND NEW MEXICO'S SAN LUIS VALLEY,
  REALITY CAN BE STRANGER THAN FICTION."

  Like other residents of the strange communities of Crestone and the Baca, Christopher O'Brien was drawn to the sacred
  valley of Native American myth. He was soon compelled to document, in disturbing detail, the inexplicable events
  unfolding around him and the questions they raised:

          What is the truth behind the nightly light show of UFOs pulsing across the sky?
          What are the strange rumbling noises coming from underground?
          What is the orgin of a mysterious crystal skull found in the Baca?
          And most frightening of all, who is responsible for the scores of cattle left bloodless and mutilated in inhuman
          fashion?

  Including fascinating and sometimes frightening first-hand accounts by residents of the area, The Mysterious Valley
  reveals the story of one of the most bizarre regions on the face of the earth and its chilling implications for the rest of
  humanity.
