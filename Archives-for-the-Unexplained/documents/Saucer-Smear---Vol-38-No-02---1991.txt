MISCELLANEOUS RAVINGS:
      A sensational new witness, named Gerald Anderson, has come forward
very recently to state that as a five year old child, he witnessed the al­
leged UFO landing near Roswell, New Mexico, in July of 1947. He and sev­
eral other members of his family happened upon a landed spaceship with four
aliens near it - two of whom were dead, one dying, and one unhurt. The mil­
itary soon arrived and chased them away in a very rough fashion.
      Stanton Friedman, described in the newspaper account as a "noted ex­
pert", is very impressed with Anderson's story, as is a man named John Car­
penter, who has regressed the witness hypnotically.   (Incidentally, Fried­
man was already in the process of writing a book about Roswell when this
new evidence came along.)
      We would like to get enthused, but we are much bothered, frankly,
by someone remembering so much detail about something that happened at the
age of five - and telling the story publicly for the first time more than
forty years after the event!
      Our thanks to Hal Starr, Arizona MUFON Director, for sending us the
complete newspaper account of the Anderson story.   Till then we had seen
only a brief wire service version....

      CHRISTOPHER ALLAN, a leading British skeptical UFO investigator,
writes as follows:
      "Your remarks in the last 'Smear' about Bill Moore and Roswell show
up the futility of this whole case. It is indeed ironic that Moore, so
long the champion of Roswell, should now be at odds with two new Roswell
proponents (Schmitt & Randle) who have taken up the matter. It is     even
more comic (or sad, according to viewpoint) that Moore's one time partner
Stan Friedman should now turn bitter against him.
      "Personally I think Moore thoroughly deserves everything he gets. I
have not shown you his last very abusive letter to me, and won't bother now.
He is obviously severely disappointed that his beloved Roswell  (and MJ-12)
have not 'caught on' as he hoped. His small band of followers will doubt­
less stay with him for awhile, but for how long?
      "As for Roswell itself, we now have the situation where in the origi­
nal book (see for yourself) there is one photo showing Jesse Marcel with
the actu�l debris, and another showing the alleged substituted balloon
while Randle & Schmitt say there are not two photos but six, and that these
six all show merely the balloon; whereas Moore & Shandera now claim    that
the same six show the real debris. How crazy and contradictory can it all
get? It makes you wonder if any of this 'investigation' has any value at
all!
      "We also hear, from Randle & Schmitt, that a 500-foot-long trench was
formed in the desert by the crashed spacecraft. I will offer you heavy
odds that no photo of this alleged trench appears in their forthcoming book.
Take me on? They are just piling absurdity upon absurdity, taking people's
evidence at face value. Do you realize, by the way, that only about ten
(to be generous) of the so-called 'witnesses' (out of some 200) are of any
real use. The others are all 'hangers on', just friends and relatives,etc.,
who saw nothing first hand. And every one, without exception, was inter­
viewed ]2, and now even 42, years after the event. Yet this is the kind of
evidence that Jerry Clark says will (he hopes) force scientists and Con­
gress to take note of!
      "Sure, people will make money out of this, but will science benefit?
I am sure you can see the funny side, like me. CUFOS are simply     making
fools of themselves over Roswell. I would be quite happy to revise .my
views if they could turn up just one genuine official document on it�   or
better still, just one piece of real hardware they were searching for at
the site. And if a USAF Colonel says something is 'not made on earth',
then he obviously knows what he is talking about, even 32 years later,right?
Clever people, these Colonels!  This is exactly what Moore/Friedm.an and now
Randle/Schmitt are accepting without question.... "
