PA MUFON NEWSLETTER


May 4, 2008 Vol. 1 No.5


Warmer weather is here and we will be scheduling Sky
Watches for JUNE. Any member interested please contact
FI Troy Noll at mufon11528@epix.net or
mufon11555@dejazzd.com CI Butch Witkowski .


SSP ’s Spirit Day was interesting and fun for all who
attended and I highly recommend you try to attend next

investigators. Please if you plan to attend email CI Butch
Witkowski butch221@dejazzd.com or FI Troy Noll
mufon11528@epix.net. Time and date, to be announced in
June newsletter. Seating is limited.

Congratulations to FI Fred Saluga on his promotion to
