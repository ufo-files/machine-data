(4) Late January, 1913 near Plock, Russian Poland Night

Abstract:  The mayor of a small town in Russian Poland, claims to have witnessed an "Austrian air scout" land.  Upon approaching, he was tied to "the body of the aeroplane" by 2 airmen.  The craft landed 60 miles away in the town of Bar where the mayor was set free and the aeroplane sailed off.

  "Express" Correspondent.
  Vienna, Feb. 1.
  The "spy fever," which is again epidemic on both sides of the Austro-Russian frontier, is responsible for several extraordinary incidents.
  The mayor of a small town near Plock, in Russian Poland, declares that he was kidnapped by an Austrian air scout several days ago. According to his story, he heard the sound of an aeroplane late at night, and when it descended in a field near the town, he went to capture the occupants, armed only with the insignia of his office.
  He alleges that the two airmen turned the tables by binding him hand and foot and lashing him to the body of the aeroplane. They entered their seats, warned their prisoner not to struggle, and resumed their flight.
  The terrified mayor was finally brought to earth near the town of Bar, sixty miles away, and before he could give the alarm, his aerial kidnappers vanished.
  Frontier guards continually report the passage of Russian aeroplanes at night. An Austrian aeroplane which had to descend in Russia two days ago was confiscated, and the airman arrested.

Source:  London Daily Express, February 5, 1913, p. 1.

(5) Summer 1920 & subsequent Mattawa River, Ontario, Canada

     	When a teen, Albert Coe claims to have been walking in a rocky area and heard a young blonde, blue-eyed boy caught in the rocks, yelling for help.  He wore a silvery leather-like suit with broken 'dials and instruments' on his chest.  The boy said he had landed his 'plane' nearby, stopping to fish.  Helping him back to the 'plane,' Coe found a silver-like saucer (about 20 ft. in diameter) standing on 3 legs.  The pilot got Coe to vow secrecy about the incident and he pushed the being up into the vessel, which flew off.
     	Coe claims a series of contacts with the being over the years, often fishing together.  He is one of 100 volunteers sent 'at the turn of the century' to monitor Earth's technological development and encourage peace.  The entity and his companions are originally from Tau Ceti, but now live on Venus, placing an 'ionized neutralizing screen' around Earth, preventing 'any hydrogen chain reaction' which could destroy it.  They have life spans of 600 years, are vegetarians with the sole 'goal' being to help their fellow man.'

Source:  Daily News (Philadelphia, Pennsylvania), July 5, 6, 7, 1967, in articles by Jack Helsel.

(7) 1924 Holy Name Convent School Dade City, Florida USA Day

    	Mrs. Evelyn Wendt, beauty salon operator recalls as a young schoolgirl 50 years earlier, playing in the yard of the Holy Name Convent School when 'the first thing I remember is that this egg-shaped thing was on the ground, and this bright light was shining in my eyes.'  The light went out, a 'hatch' opened and little robot people emerged.  'They were smaller than I and resembled animated flowers with faces where the bud would be.  Remember, I was just a bitty thing then, and kids don't fear flowers.'
     	They carried a weapon-like device to the school's science building.  "I wanted to help them, but someone said 'stop.'  I replied they were so small, I was going to assist.  They let me try but I couldn't even budge the machine.  I was told they were going to stop the work that was being done in the science building and they said if the work continued, they would destroy the place."  Asked what the 'work' was, she said she didn't know.  'All I know is later I heard the place was a shambles.'
     	She said 'there seemed to be a man with the little people...everything looked real, even though I wasn't so sure.  The conversation wasn't real talking,' but she understood mentally what was being said.  As they were leaving, "they asked if I wanted to go.  I said, 'no,' but I could have gone."  She adds, 'they promised to come back for me in thirty five years, but that was up a long time ago and nothing happened that I know of.'  Then, the lead-appearing 'saucer' flew straight up, hovered a minute and disappeared.

Source:  Beckley, Timothy Green, Timothy Green Beckley's Book of Space Contacts.  New York:  Global Communications, 1981, pp. 26-27, quoting from the The Weekday newspaper (West Palm Beach, Florida), no date mentioned.

(10) 1932 & subsequent New Jersey USA

     	At age 10, sign painter Howard Menger claims to have encountered 'the most esquisite woman my young eyes had ever beheld.'  She had golden hair and eyes, and wore a shiny nylon-like seamless outfit similar to what a skier would wear.  'I have come a long way to see you, Howard...and to talk with you.'  She 'knew where I had come from and what my purpose would be on Earth.'  She said 'her people' had been watching him for a long time and 'we are contacting our own.'  The woman 'answered questions before I could ask them...she seemed to know all my thoughts.'  The slender woman was 'about my mother's height.' She told Howard 'of a great change to take place...wasteful wars, torture and destruction would be brought on by misunderstandings of people.'  Before leaving, she told him they would meet again, and 'they will always be around--watching out for you...guiding you.'
     	In his several subsequent contacts, he described the aliens as looking 'just like we do,' except for their dress.  They 'come from Mars, Saturn, Venus and probably Jupiter.'  Because they are 'our brothers' and 'love us,' their purpose is to create a higher understanding 'so we can help ourselves in preventing any future destruction of our planet.'  They are vegetarians, have schools, cities, factories and gardens.  During a later contact, Menger says he was flown to the Moon in a disc-like bell-shaped craft, where he saw many buildings and was able to breathe comfortably in the atmosphere.

Source:  Menger, Howard, From Outer Space.  New York:  Pyramid, May 1967.  Also published as, From Outer Space to You. Clarksburg, West Virginia:  Saucerian Press, 1959.

(13) July 23, 1947 near Bauru, State of San Paulo, Brazil

     	Jose C. Higgins hears a high-pitched whistle as he watches a large greyish-white metallic disc land, resting on 3 legs.  After his co-workers flee, he finds himself alone with three 7 ft. entities in 'transparent suits covering head and body, and inflated like rubber bags.'  They carried 'metal boxes' on their backs.  Clothing, visible through the suits, resembled brightly colored paper.  The beings, all identical, had large bald heads, huge round eyes, no eyebrows, and legs longer in proportion than ours.  Higgins found them strangely beautiful.
     	They surrounded him and apparently tried to lure him into the disc, but seeing they shunned sunlight, he managed to elude them, then hid nearby for half an hour.  Watching from thick shrubs, he watched them move with extraordinary agility, frolicking about and tossing huge stones.  They then re-entered the craft and flew off.

Source:  Creighton, Gordon, The Humanoids in Latin America.  In Bowen. C. (ed.), The Humanoids, pp. 88-89, citing Diari da Tarde (Curitiba), Brazil, August 8, 1947; O Cruzeiro (Rio de Janeiro), 1954; La Razon (Buenos Aires), Argentina, April 13, 1950; Flying Saucer Review, November-December 1961, which cites a report from the Aerial Phenomena Research Organization.

(18) Summer, 1950 & subsequent Manatoba Canada Apparently Day

     	After seeing an NBC TV documentary in January 1979 on UFO abductions, Virginia Horton (pseudonym) told UFO investigator Budd Hopkins that at age 6 on her grandfather's Manitoba farm, while going to gather eggs in a barn, she next recalled standing in her yard and her leg wet.  Inspecting it, her left leg had a painless one-half inch deep by one-inch long scalpel-like incesion but no tear in her jeans.  She requested regressive hypnosis, which was performed by N.Y. psychologist Dr. Aphrodite Clamar in 1979.  While walking to the barn, she told of suddenly lying on a 'couch' and seeing 'plenty of light' (soft greyish in tint).  She was told, apparently mentally, about her leg cut and that it wouldn't hurt, 'explaining that they need a little bitty piece of you for understanding.'  A being (unseen) said 'we're from a long way away' in the sky and that there are many inhabited worlds similar to Earth.  The being said his place of origin was 'nice' and 'He was very happy.'  He offered to transport her at a later time on a visit of other worlds, but cautioned:  'Your Mom would be upset if we went away for a while.'
     	Ten years later while on a picnic with her parents in a wooded area in France, Virginia was reminded by her mother (during a 1979 phone conversation) of emerging from the woods with blood on her blouse and no apparent recall of what happened.  Under hypnosis with Dr. Clamar, she recalled hearing her name being mentally called and she walked to a grounded UFO, was escorted inside and made to feel happy.  Tissue and blood samples from inside her nose were taken.  She is told to forget the experience, except for seeing a deer.  Upon returning to her family, she became aware of a time lapse and blood on her blouse.

Source:  Hopkins, Budd, Missing Time:  A Documented Study of UFO Abductions.  New York:  Richard Marek, 1981, pp. 128-153 & 184-215.

(22) Late July 1952 & subsequent near Mormon Mesa, Nevada about 5 AM

     	Falling asleep in a truck in the Nevada desert, Truman Bethurum awoke surrounded by 'eight to 10 small men, all about 4 ft. 8 inches to 5 ft. high.'  He said the beings were fully developed men.  They spoke English, saying 'we have no trouble with any language.'  Bethurum was invited aboard a nearby saucer-shaped 'space scow' and introduced to their Captain, 'Aura Rhanes,' whose 'smooth skin was beautiful olive and roses.'  The 32-man crew were 'Latin types...(with complexions) something like Italians.'  All were neatly dressed in uniforms similar to those 'worn by Greyhound bus drivers.'  All had coal black hair and dark eyes.  Aura Rhanes wore a black skirt and red blouse.  She was 4 and a half ft. and said they hailed from the planet 'Clarion,' coming to Earth to rekindle old values like family and home to prevent a nuclear holocaust and bring humanity closer to God.  He wasn't told how old Aura was because, like Earth women she was cautious about revealing her age, but assured him she was 'under a thousand.'  Based on conversations with Captain Rhanes, Bethurum predicted that 'there will be no atomic or hydrogen warfare, ever,' and that 'our five-year-old children of today will not marry soldiers.  We are on the threshold of a lasting peace and prosperity era.'

Source:  Truman Bethurum's personal scrapbook (original copy), courtesy of Robert C. Girard, UFO book dealer, publisher, Scotia, N.Y.

(23) October 14, 1952 & subsequent Bakersfield, California USA 9:30 AM

     	Auto repair shop owner Cecil Michael observed a 'flying saucer' with 2 human-like occupants hover near Bakersfield in mid-August.  He viewed the craft 'too closely' and 'had been spotted and tagged for a return visit.'
     	At about 9:30 a.m. on October 14, Michael was working alone in his shop when 2 'big men' (some 220 lbs. and about 6 ft.) walked in.  They had 'medium dark' complexions, were 'smooth-shaven' and 'athletic' appearing.  They spoke mentally:  'Did we scare you, Red?'  'You sure as hell did,' he replied.  The beings stayed for a couple of months, vanishing whenever customers came.  
     	One day, the beings put Michael in a 'trance' state, taking him on a mental trip.  Tied up inside a saucer, the 2 beings took him to an orange-planet that was very hot (about 100 degrees) where he met the 'devil.'  Suddenly, "In the middle of a light, Christ appeared in plain view....I turned to the old bum (the devil) and, pointing my hand to the light, said, 'If you don't let me go, he will send for me.'  The devil responded:  'Yes, He is always interfering.'"  Michael was returned to Earth after spending about 2 and a half weeks on the planet.  Upon returning, the 2 space beings tried to regain his friendship, asking him to write the story of incident with the devil for the benefit of humanity.  The beings then left, never returning.

Source:  Michael, Cecil, Round Trip to Hell in a Flying Saucer. Auckland, New Zealand:  Phoenix, 1971.  Originally published by Vantage Press, 1955.

They came from another star, asked to take them to his leader and said 'they were frightened that, by playing...with atomic explosions ...we would not only destroy the world but endanger the universe.'  They never 'fought over silly things like flags and lands' and 'had no sex problems like us humans.'  In fact, they 'did not practice sex,' claiming to be asexual, reproducing by fission.  During the encounter, one did.  C.A.V. boarded the craft, was taken on a brief trip and returned.  The beings then flew off.

(27) Between August 17 & 20, 1953 Ciudad Valleys, Mexico 6 PM

     	Working underneath his broken-down vehicle on the side of a highway, Salvador Villanueva, Mexican taxicab driver noticed 2 pairs of legs standing nearby.  He found 2 pleasant-looking men about 4 and a half ft., clad in one-piece 'seamless grey corduroy' garments from neck to toe, with wide shiny boxes on their backs.  Under their arms were held 'helmets like those worn by pilots or by American football players.'  As many Mexican Indians are short, their height didn't alarm him.
     	Only one of the men spoke (Spanish), 'stringing the words together' in a strange accent, while the other apparently understood the conversation, but did not speak.  At first they discussed trivial matters, including his car.  Then, at one point, the man said:  'We are not of this planet.  We come from one far distant, but we know much about your world.'
     	At dawn he went with them to their saucer-shaped craft, located in a clearing by the road.  Crossing a swampy area in which his legs sank deeply, the legs of both men remained clean.  'When their feet touched the muddy pools, their belts glowed, and the mud sprang away as if repelled by some invisible force.'
     	Salvador was invited aboard the craft, which stood on 3 great metal spheres, but declined and ran away.  The craft rose, began glowing intensely, then shot off at fantastic speed.

Source:  Creighton, Gordon, The Humanoids in Latin America, in Bowen, Charles (ed.), The Humanoids.  Britain:  Futura, pp. 9O-91, citing Leslie, Desmond, 'Mexican Taxi Driver Meets Saucer Crew?', in Flying Saucer Review, March/April 1959, p. 8.

(198) November 30, 1979 near La Palma, Oaxaca, Mexico 5:00 PM

     	A Mexican peasant (Manual Fidel Cruz Lopez) was weeding his father's palm grove when 8 armed men approached after exiting a hovering saucer-shaped object emitting a green light.  They looked like tall Africans, were well dressed, had green lenses, radio transmitters and touted machine guns. (199) December 4, 1979 Vastervik, Sweden 1:00 AM

     	Taking an early morning walk in the Brevik Hills recreation area, Lilli-Ann Karlsson was suddenly paralyzed.  Ahead was a luminous object hovering above the ground.  A pair of 5 ft. tall beings appeared from behind the object.  'I couldn't hear it, but in a strange way I sensed that they were discussing my person.  I felt ridiculous in their presence but suddenly heard a voice beside me saying I shouldn't be afraid.'  They approached and one outstretched its hand to her, but she remained paralyzed.  They talked among themselves for a while and returned to the craft, disappearing behind it as they had appeared.  Smoke came from the bottom, then suddenly it was not there.

Source:  UFO Journal (Cleveland, Ohio), Issue 35, July 1981, citing the Nyhetsblad Newsletter (Sweden), April/September 1980, citing an investigation by Tor Wiklund who wrote a report on the case in the publication UFO-Information, Skanninge, Sweden, Issue 4, 1980.
