     From: jan@cyberzone.net (Jan Aldrich)
     Date: Mon, 30 Jun 1997 12:55:49 -0800
     Subject: Project Sign Action Slip


   A copy of the Project "Sign" Action Slip was found on the
   Project Sign microfilm which is not the be confused with
   the Project Blue Book microfilms. There were originally
   nine microfilm rolls containing Project Sign case files
   and summary sheets. The Project Sign material was microfilmed
   in 1948-9 to be sent to scientists and other consultants.
   Some files on the Project Sign microfilms do not survive
   in the Project Blue Book files. Today only four of these
   rolls of the Project Sign microfilm are known to survive.
   The microfilm was given to Dr. Herbert Strentz by Lt.
   Col. Hector Quintaninlla. Dr. Strentz used these and
   other material in a PhD disertation on the press and UFOs at
   the University of Colorado contemporary with the Condon
   Committee Dr. Strentz finally turned the microfilms over to
   Barry Greenwood. The Action Slips themselves were used by
   Project Sign personnel to track the steps in the investigation
   of a UFO report or as a routing slip to various agencies
   inside and outside of Wright-Patterson AFB during the
   period of 1948-49.

   > I assume MCI is AMC, Intelligence.                So other symbols are branches.

   That's correct, Jan. MCI stands for Intelligence at AMC
   at Wright-Pat. MCIAO was Operations, and MCIAXO was
   Special Operations or Projects. The designator for Project
   Sign itself was MCIAXO-3. All this courtesy of Mike Swords'
   research.
