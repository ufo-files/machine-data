August 15: Letter to the editor

”THE GHOST BOMBS. Latest news is that the ghost bombs have been observed near Stockholm. These fireballs came, however, from one of the Navy´s stores, where a man was sorting out light balls or signal shots. For the sake of variety he had shot off one of these light balls in the air, and behold, more was not needed to set the fantasy going in this case too. By now the Swedish public should recognise the mysterious ghost bombs that sweep in over our country. Most of them do not, either, take these fantasy ghost bombs for real. 

”If it wasn´t a star this time, too, but instead Russian experiments with V-1 or yet other untested weapons, as some believe, why shall they then be allowed to use Sweden as a target of their experiments? By writing in the newspapers, the Russians are informed where there projectiles fall, and they only have to make their measures and calculations. But, of course, Swedes help everyone.  I.J., Södertälje”

Source: Expressen, August 15
