     Best ghost hunting show so far!
     I LOVE the show! It's a great idea to have a 72 hour lock down. So much more time to investigate. I've watched Ghost Adventures from
     the start but with every season, I've seen Zac's ego grow bigger and bigger and it's made it hard to watch anymore. I've always liked
     Nick. I think he seems very humble and the real deal. I hope the show gets great ratings and is on for a long time.The investigations
     so far have been incredibly interesting and I've been amazed at the evidence they have come up with during each investigation. I look
     forward to each show. I am not sure who sang the song for the introduction, but really great song as well! #team Nick and Katrina! :)
