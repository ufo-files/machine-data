  Hi Sam

  I have looked at your video several times and it is really hard to make out just what the light was. The light
  starts to flash at the very end of your video which looks very much like the Nav lights on an aircraft! Your
  camera movement/shake did not help either.

  Unless tou have already done this, disengage your cam corder's auto focus and just set it manually to
  infinity. This way objects at distance will always be in focus no matter what happens any nearer to you.
  Rapid zooming on some digital cam corders can exhibit focus time lag when set on auto focus, so this is
  another reason to leave it on manual focus.
