D::s-.6.
  EADER Malcolm Robinson wrote
R  that he saw a bright stationary
object in the sky. I, too, saw an object
similar to the one described - but I
saw it on holiday in Ibiza.
   We took the object for some kind
of weather balloon, but I would like
to know what it really was.-Miss J.
Ross, Kilbirnie, Ayrshire.

M
   ALCOLM ROBINSON could be
    seeing the gas burning off at
Mossmoran power station in Fife.
This gives a bright orange glow and
can be seen for miles on a clear night.
-Mrs I. Adamson, Edinburgh.

                *   *   *


I
  ALSO noticed the strange object in
  the sky. I would say that it is the
planet Jupiter and atmospheric
conditions gave it its strange shape.
- Mrs I. Suttie, Motherwell,
Lanarkshire.
