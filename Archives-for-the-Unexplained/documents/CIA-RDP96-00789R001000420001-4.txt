                      Summary of Information


     The target consists of a series of interconnected structures.
The activity present is reminiscent of a filtration process i.e.
desalinization-like operation. There are numerous perceptions of
a liquid-like substance moving rapidly through a tube and cascading
uniformly over a straight or level edge.    There is a sweet smell
with a pleasant aroma. There was the sensation of being "sucked"
into a vaccuum down a long tube or tunnel.
     There are numerous objects which were perceived (see diagrams
pages i through vi) .   Diagram ii (b) depicts a slowing turning
cylindrically-shaped object with a "flap-like" feature on one side.
This "flap" appears to flip out from the main body of the object;
it is associated with a function for the passing of something
either into, or out of, the large circular object.

     Diagram iii(a) depicts a large, flat object which moves back
and forth in a fan-like motion.     It is attached by a feature
reminiscent of a pivot.    It is dark gray, metallic and with a
grainy surface.   The "place" it is located is a hollow location
where sound stops. This area is sterile in the sense that nothing
or no one else is present of near this object.        It has the
appearance of a giant shovel lying on its side.

     There is a bright, circular light with "blasts" of something
shooting out of a circular area. There is also spinning with great
centrifugal force.   This motion gives one the sensation of being
"pinned" against something smooth, hard and cold. Elsewhere is a
stepped figure reminiscent of the front of a Mayan temple.    This
figure has a "fluttering" effect reminscent of a deck of cards
being shuffled.

     Diagram iii (b) depicts a "system" of something in which a
substance is being moved. The term "GREEN PASSAGE" was perceived
in asssociation with this. Here is where the concept of something
cascading uniformly over something else was perceived.
     At one point a large rectangular object reminiscent of a giant
metal 2 11 x 12" board hit an individual in the forehead, knocking
them back and taking their head off.

     There was a drone of inperceivable music in the background
throughout much of the target as if a radio was playing.

     Elsewhere are the perceptions of "pulsations" and "high
energy". There is a large tube-like object rolling back and forth
over something with the concept of shaping or manipulating. There
is an object reminiscent of a flower blossom which opens and closes
