 It seems likely to me that the human race is interacting with several alien species, some of whom may be
 benevolent, and some not so.

 There seems to be much evidence that one or more of those species is carrying out abduction, taking eggs
 and sperm for purposes of a genetic engineering programme.

 Some thoughts have occurred to me about the driving force behind the genetic engineering programme.

 My thoughts involve Darwin's theory of evolution by natural selection, which explains the way that life has
 spread on the earth. By extending the concept to the spread of life from planet to planet, it see ms to me that
 we can gain insight into the abduction phenomenon.

 Natural selection acts as follows. Within a population of animals, the gene pattern will vary from one
 individual to another. The individuals having genes that make them more likely to have and raise children
 (e.g. genes conferring strength, disease resistance, social behaviour more likely to lead to finding a mate) will
 on average have and raise more children than others. So in the next generation the proportion of those genes
 is increased, thus improving the average ability of the population to replicate themselves. Thus those genes
 have been selected by natural processes, hence the term 'natural selection'.

 If the environment changes (e.g. change of climate, new diseases), then natural selection ensures that the
 population as a whole adapts to cope with it. New species can arise, e.g. when a group of animals become
 isolated.

 Natural selection is best regarded as a competition between genes to survive and spread, rather than as a
 competition between individuals or between species. This idea is explained very clearly in Richard Dawkin's
 book "The Selfish Gene" (Oxford University Press).

 When a civilization develops the technology for travel to other planets and also for genetic engineering, a

 mechanism becomes possible for genes to spread from one planet to another. That is by the beings that have
 those genes travelling to another planet, then by genetic engineering techniques forming a new hybrid species
 that has some of their own genes and also genes of a species that already lives on that planet. The genes of
 the second species are needed in order that the bodies of the new species will be well adapted to the new
 planet.

 This process does require genetic engineering techniques, since sex between two individuals of different
 species will not produce offspring. Genetic engineering makes hybridization possible even between very
 different species. Already in our university and industrial laboratories it is possible to insert genes from one
 species into a very different one, e.g. human genes can be inserted into lifeforms as different as pigs or
 bacteria (I'm not sure if those particular examples have been achieved, but I remember reading of that sort of
 thing being done).

 What sort of genes would natural selection cause to be propagated from one planet to another in that way? It
 would be the genes that determine mental capabilities and motivation, specifically the genes for the
 intelligence necessary to undertake space travel and genetic engineering, and for the motivation and
 behaviour that leads to a society that would repeat the replication process on yet another planet, i.e. a society
 that invests in travel to yet another planet t� genetically engineer yet another hybrid species with the same
 mental characteristics as themselves but with bodies suited to that planet.

 If the right genes are propagated, the process repeats itself ad infinitum. The hybrid species formed on a
 planet travels to another populated planet where it genetically engineers yet another hybrid species suited to
 that planet, which then travels to yet another planet where it genetically engineers yet another hybrid species
 suited to that planet. .. ad infinitum.

 The appearances of the species on each successive planet could be very different. What would remain
 constant would be the genes for mental capabilities and motivation. Such a set of genes has become
 self-propagating and spreads from planet to planet as described above.

 Of course, one of the new hybrid species may not be given suitable genes for some reason, and so fail to
 repeat the replication process. Or there may be some other reason for failure, such as a natural catastrophe,
 or an accident of history causing the species to produce weapons that then make it extinct during warfare.

 Failure to repeat the replication process is what happens in or�inary natural selection too, when an individual
 fails to have children. It is the individuals with the right genes to repeat the process (of making and raising
 children) who are likely to do so, thus propagating those genes further - individuals without those genes do
 not have and raise children, so their genes are not propagated further - that is the how natural selection
 works.

  That same principle of natural selection would apply to the propagation of genes from one hybrid species to
  the next. The speci es with the genes that give the mental characteristics to repeat the replication process are
  likely to do so, thus propagating those genes further. Species without those genes would not replicate, and so
  their genes would not be propagated further.

 What would be observed on the planet where this process was taking place? The genes from the species that
 is doing the geneti c engineering would cause the local population to develop the intelligence necessary to
 undertake space travel and genetic engineering, and probably a society that had th e motivation to engage in
 both of those activities. We see that that has happened to the human species here on earth.

  How would the genetic engineering be done? Perhaps by abduction of individuals of the local species for egg
  and sperm removal, and perhaps by implanting them with genetically engineered embryos. From the accounts
  of abductees that· is what seems to happening to the human species here on the earth.

  Where would the genetically engineered babies grow up? Maybe there would be several types of genetically
  engineered baby being produced, but probably one of the mainstream types would be basically the same as
  the rest of the local population but with some genes altered.

  A good way for the latter type to grow up would be as a child of the individual who was implanted with the
  genetically modified embryo. There would be several advantages. For example the child would be growing up
  in the very earth environment for which its genetic makeup was being designed. It could then be tested
  throughout its life for how well it coped in that environment.

  Those children would then become abductees themselves for two reasons: firstly for the testing mentioned
  above, and secondly to be used for further breeding in the same way that they themselves were produced.
  Thus children of abductee parents become abductees themselves. The same then goes on from generation to
  generation. This is what we are seeing now here on the earth - being an abductee see ms to follow from parent      •

  to child to grandchild etc.

  So what we observe to be happening here on earth seem to fit the above pattern, with the human race playing
  the role of the local species.

 If the process has been going on for a long time then all human beings are hybrids already. At any one time in
 history, and at present, a certain proportion of the population are being used in the genetic engineering
 program, i.e. they are abductees. The rest have alien genes from ancestors who were abductees.

 intelligence, but also behaviour and motivation similar to the aliens. The abductors are often seen to be coldly
