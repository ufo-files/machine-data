It is my distinct pleasure to announce your appointment to the position of New
Jersey Director for Ground Saucer Saucer Watch. This appointment is made with
the full knowledge and concurrence of the Director of Ground Saucer Watch,                      Will­
iam H.   Spaulding,     Phoenix,      Arizona.


Among your duties as New Jersey Director are:                 (1)    Coordinating and aiding inves­
tigations in your area;         (2)    Promoting Ground Saucer Watch as an organization by
bringing our efforts to.the public's attention;                    (3)   Encouraging the support of
our organization by members and non-members,                with either their financial contrib­
utions or sales of GSW materials;              (4) Retaining your great sense of humor--which
serves as a constant reminder that all of us have a tendency to take ourselves
too seriously.


There will undoubtably be some eyebrows raised and some derogatory comments made
about this appointment. The so-called "purists" will no doubt be rankled. However,
it is our judgement that past efforts by certain organizations to exclude you from
the mainstream of Ufology have been grossly unfair--and downright silly. Who is to
say your contributions to Ufology have less merit than other's contributions? After
all,   it was through your persistence and sacrifice that George Adamski was finally
proven to be a hoaxer. I have personally reviewed much of your early work,                      and I
think I'm safe to say that you were making an honest effort to be objective and to
report UFO events as accurately as possible--rather than the way you personally
construed them.


Ground Saucer Watch understands that your current publication is satirical in nature,
and we fully expect that we will continue to be just as much parodied as in the
past. We will not attempt to impose any conditions on what you might say about us-­
especially in light of the fact that we enjoy jokes about ourselves as we do about
others.


Ground Saucer Watch anticipates a long and fruitful relationship with you. As New
Jersey Director,       you will have an opportunity to enhance and promote what we believe
