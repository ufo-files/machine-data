Dear Mr. Somchison:
Thank you for your letter of 5 October with the enclosed
sketches of the luminous phenomenon seen between 5 and 6 a,m. that
morning.
We have received a few other reports from the Ottawa area
and also from the vicinity of Halifax.
There seems little doubt now that the cloud was produced
by a release of barium in the upper atmosphere from a rocket launched
at Nallops Island off the coast of Virginia.
