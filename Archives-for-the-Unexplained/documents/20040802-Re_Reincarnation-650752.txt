  Bill Taylor wrote:

          I see there is a silly re-incarnation thread.   So let me restart it with
          a diversion, which I suppose is almost OT for a logic group.


          What is the point of re-incarnation if you can't remember your past lives?


  There is no point involved. Either reincarnation happens, or it doesn't. Expecting there to be a point is ascribing human
  motive to something that would be a natural process, if it does in fact happen.


          Or rather, what is the point of CALLING it re-incarnation? If a person is
          re-incarnated but can't remember any detail of his past life(s), what is
          the point of saying it's the same person?   It may as well be someone else
          altogether, or someone completely new.


  Well what else would you have someone call it? I wasn't aware that the term "reincarnation" implied that anyone would
  remember anything.

  If someone is reincarnated, but can't remember any detail of his past life, then how exactly are you supposed to know
  who they were to be able to say they are the same person now? All you know is that they were someone else, knowing
  who they were is a different issue.


          Can anyone unravel this conundrum for us?


  There is not conundum.
