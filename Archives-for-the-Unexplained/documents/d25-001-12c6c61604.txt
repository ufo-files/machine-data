             Part-Time Help Wanted: Ufomind Link Auditor
     From: campbell@ufomind.com (Glenn Campbell, Las Vegas)
     Date: Sat, 25 Jul 1998 16:15:04 -0800


  Help! The Ufomind website has outgrown my ability to maintain it
  singlehandedly. I am distressed by the large number of broken or
  misplaced links on our index pages. In theory, I am supposed to
  review the new links that people add to our pages, but I haven't had
  the time.
  What I need (I think) is a part-time "link auditor" who can spend
  about an hour a day reviewing link additions and other changes to our
  website. I can't afford to pay much -- probably a fixed "honorarium"
  of about $100 per month -- so this is partly a volunteer position. If
  you agree with the goals of the Ufomind site and are interested in the
  information linked to it, you may not regard it as work at all. You
  will play an important role in deciding how paranormal information is
  classified and filed.
  Here are the requirements of the job....
       -- You must already be a frequent visitor to the Ufomind website.
       You should understand our style and have a pretty good grasp of
       our goals.
       -- You should already be a subscriber to the Ufomind mailing
       list, or read it regularly on the web. (Otherwise you will never
       see this message anyway.)
       -- You must have added your own links to a Ufomind index page at
       some point in the past. (Using the "Add New Link" at the bottom
       of the page.) This shows that you are at least familiar with the
       basic system.
       -- Obviously, you must have your own computer with a modern web
       browser (not AOL), email and a reasonably fast connection to the
       internet.
       -- You must possess good judgement, high intelligence and an
       ability to remain neutral about controversial claims.
       -- Good spelling and grammar are essential!
  The following qualifications are a plus, but we can be flexible for
  a good candidate....

       -- General knowledge of HTML
       -- General knowledge of telnet and Unix -- and telnet already
       installed on your computer.
       -- Residence within North America (to allow phone calls).
       -- Have stable daytime employment (so I can rely on you for an
       extended period).
  DUTIES
  Duties require sitting down at your computer every day or so -- at any
  time of your choosing -- and reviewing information on a series of web
  screens. I only expect you to do this for about an hour at a time,
  because that's about my own limit before fatigue sets in.
  For reviewing new links added by others, you will be looking at a
  screen similar to the "Add New Link" screen. Each new link is
  displayed for you, and you will make sure it is consistent with the
  Ufomind style and relevant to the page it is linked to. (You don't
  have to look at the actual page at the new URL unless there is
  something suspicious about it.) You will be making changes as
  appropriate: editing, moving or deleting links.
  You will also be reviewing user comments, changes to people pages and
  requests for changes sent by email. I have developed a set of
  tools to allow all of this changes with a minimum of effort, and you
  will be trained in our systems as required. Most of our tools are
  fairly intuitive and easy to use.
  This position is similar to that of a municipal judge. You have to be
  fair and even-handed, and you cannot let your own opinions intrude
  into your work. When new links or comments are added to Ufomind
  pages, you need not decide whether the claims they contain are true,
  only whether the topic is relevant and the format of the link
  conforms to our rules.
  Although a knowledge of HTML is helpful, there is no direct HTML
  programming involved, and telnet is useful only after you have
  mastered all of our other tools. Initially, all of the changes you
  make will be via email and the web browser. Your communication with
  me will be via email and telephone (at my expense).
  You will NOT be expected to perform any duty connected with the
  commercial side of the website, like our catalog or advertizing. You
  are also not required to actively seek out new links to add to our
  site (although you are free to do this on your own time). You job is
  mainly to assure the integrity of new additions made by others, and to
  respond to specific requests.
  REWARDS
  As mentioned, I cannot afford to pay you much: I propose $100/month
  initially, which isn't even minimum wage. You would only want to
  apply for this position if you feel you can derive some personal
  satisfaction from it.
  Other rewards are:
       -- You will be God (or at least the Son of God) when it comes to
       deciding what belongs on the world's largest UFO and paranormal
       website and how it should be filed.
       -- You will be among the first to know about new websites.
       -- You will be given credit for your work, being listed on our
       staff page.
       -- You can purchase any books in our catalog at wholesale.
  HOW TO APPLY
  Send me an email message with this info....
       -- Name, age, mailing address and phone number.
       -- Your website URL, if you have one.
       -- The Ufomind URL and link number for the last link you added to
       our pages.
