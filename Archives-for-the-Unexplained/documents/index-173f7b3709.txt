  A quick glance through this book suggests it would be an interesting read, although it certainly doesn't
  match the hype on the book jacket. Contrary to the jacket, there is no evidence that the FBI maintained
  systematic files on UFOs. Instead, FBI files show that the agency did track some UFO claimants like
  George van Tassel and passed a few UFO cases to the Air Force. The book includes a number of FBI
  documents we haven't seen before. These are interesting mainly as history, not as evidence of alien life or
  government cover-up. -- Glenn Campbell

  From the Book Cover

  In an unparalleled exposé, Nicholas Redfern, author of the bestselling A Covert Agenda, reveals that since the late

  astonishing number of all-too-genuine X-Files.

  In The FBI Files, Nicholas Redfern takes the reader on a remarkable journey of discovery, encompassing close
  encounters between UFOs and the American military; face to face meetings with creatures from other worlds; a
  disturbing alien operation to harvest Earth-based lifeforms; the unauthorized release of highly classified U.S.
  Government documents on UFOs; crashed alien spacecraft and the bodies of alien beings held by the American
  intelligence community; UFO technology acquired by the Nazis during World War Two; classified FBI briefings on the
  UFO subject; the mystery Men in Black who terrorize UFO witnesses; and much more.

  A crucially important book, The FBI Files reveals a truth which has far-reaching implications for the humanrace --
  Planet Earth has extra-terrestrial visitors.
