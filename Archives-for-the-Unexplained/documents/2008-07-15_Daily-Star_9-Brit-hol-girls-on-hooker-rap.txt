The raunchy tourists were arrested for prostitution offences on the Greek party island of Zakynthos. Six British and six Greek men,
including two bar owners, have also been charged with encouraging obscene behaviour after police raided the notorious Laganas
beach. A spokesman said the sordid competition had been video recorded and was to be posted on the internet. Female tourists
from Britain and Sweden reached the final of the oral sex competition after getting through several knock-out rounds. But the final
never took place because police saw footage of the contest – showing five Brit couples having oral sex – on Greek TV station
Antenna TV. Following the controversy, several bars were closed down at the resort in the south of the island. Police have brought
in tough sentences for obscene behaviour in a bid to stop the resort becoming the “next Faliraki” – a popular Brit 18-30s destination
on the nearby island of Rhodes. But last night they played down the latest scandal, saying there had been some “minor” incidents
with British couples having sex in public but “nothing out of the ordinary”. Captain Dionysis Viliardos, police chief of Laganas, said:
“It is the middle of the busiest month for British tourism here and we are on the alert every night. “We have had several minor cases
of British couples having sex on the beach, and some trials are taking place, but it is nothing out of the ordinary.” He added there
was another report of 21 British men and women having a nude midnight swim after coming out of bars in the Laganas area. Last
night Foreign Office officials confirmed they have been investigating the arrests. steve.hughes@dailystar.co.uk
