SCOTTISH SIGHTINGS: A flying saucer, top, and crop-
circles, above, are among thousands of reports of alien

Strange
sights in
the skies
above
Scotland
By MARIA CROCE
S
COTLAND
has had
more than

share of
UFO sightings since

One of the first recorded
sightings was by Andrew
Cherry, who said he saw a
disc-shaped UFO with an
occupant inside hovering
over the Portobello district of
Edinburgh.
There were more than 100
sightings of UFOs within a
few months around Moffat,
Dumfriesshire, during 1978.
In 1979, forestry worker
Bob Taylor thought he saw a
spacecraft at Dechmont Law,
near Livingston, West Lothian,
which resulted in a media
frenzy and was later featured
on Arthur C. Clarke's
Mysterious World TV series.
Gary Wood and Colin
Wright claimed to have been
abducted by aliens on the A70
in West Lothian in 1992. They
said they spotted a black
shiny object in the sky which
beamed a light on to their car.
Dozens of crop circles have
appeared across Scotland after
the first publicised appearance
at Blairgowrie in 1990, while
East Kilbride is now laying
claim to being a UFO centre
alter a number of recent
sightings. Stephen Holt, of
the town's Westwood area,
spotted a bright light, which
he said contained shadowy
figures, in the night sky.
However, Bonnybridge,
near Falkirk, is Scotland's
UFO capital. It's recognised
around the world as a UFO
hotspot with 500 reported
sightings since 1992.
Local councillor Billy
Buchanan has become an
unofficial spokesman on
the area's UFOs and he was
suspected of involvement
when it was reported that an
alien called Zalus was going
to appear at Falkirk Town
Hall in August 1995.
But councillor Buchanan
denied any connection and
ufologists believe the report
was a bid to discredit UFO
sightings.
