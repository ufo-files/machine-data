State Meeting:
Our next state meeting will be at the Comfort Inn in Portsmouth. The date is Sunday June 27, 1993. All are
encouraged to attend and to bring a friend. The Comfort Inn is located at the Yokens convention and restaurant
site just south of the Portsmouth traffic circle. We will start at 1:00PM sharp in the conference room. Many
thanks to Kevin MacLeod for making this great facility available to us.

We plan to have discussions on several cases in work including missing time in North Conway, Dual discs in
Colebrook, a possible crashed disc in Vermont and photos from a Dover sighting in 1981. Also Dr. Jim CaJvin
a new NH Mufon member and Consultant in clinical psychology has agreed to answer our questions about
communications intelligence. It should be a super meeting so plan to attend.

Crop Circle Research:
New Hampshire Mufon is working with Colin Andrews this year in scientific research of the 1993 crop circle
investigation. Colin departed for England earlier than planed and is now hard at work. His early departure was
due to increased activity in his home country. He will keep us informed and will give us a briefing during our
September conference. The latest issue of UFO Encounters, Vol. 1 No. 7 has a fascinating story of a German
crop marking with the location of allegedly antique metal plates having the exact crop marking. I will have a
copy of this story at our state meeting.

The New Hampshire Mutual UFO Network cordially invites you to join us and other UFO enthusiasts at our
third annual UFO conference. Last years event was attended by over 400 people from all over the North East
enjoying the camaraderie and excitement of interfacing with others interested in the UFO phenomenon.This
year we are fortunate to have five pioneering ufologists on our program. Each of whom will openly share their
opinions and theories with you as well as answer your questions. The best opportunity to meet and interact on a
one-on-one basis with the speakers will be at the Saturday night reception. Please Register Early!

                                        MEET THE SPEAKERS

Colin Andrews is an Electrical Engineer by profession. Mr. Andrews began the scientific study of the Crop
Circle Phenomenon over ten years ago, and is recognized as one of the phenomenon's foremost researchers. He
is also a founding member of C.P.R. (Circle Phenomenon Research) and has collaborated with scientists and
engineers throughout the world in the on-going investigation of crop circles. Colin is the co-author of two
books, the best seller Circular Evidence and his most recent work Crop Circles-The l.Lltest Evidence. In
addition, Colin was instrumental in obtaining the official cooperation of the British Military in the
implementation of Operation Blackbird, the largest crop circle surveillance project of 1990. Colin also
maintains the worlds largest crop circle database and will share with us the latest crop circle activity (Summer
of 1993) as well as up to the minute research findings.

 Richard C. Hoagland at the age of nineteen became curator of the Springfield Massachusetts Museum of
 Science. He designed and produced a commemorative event that coincided with NASA's first visit to Mars.
 One year later he served as consultant for NBC during the 'Surveyor 1' moon landing. He also conducted
 research for CBS and was an advisor to Waiter Cronlcite. Mr. Hoagland's most valued contribution to science
 and history to date, is the eo-conception of mankind's first interstellar message and engraved plaque carried on
 pioneer 10. Richard's many other contributions to science and the American space program are too numerous to
 list. For the past ten years Mr. Hoagland and the Mars Mission, founded by Mr. Hoagland have been
 researching the monuments of Mars as described in his book, The Monuments of Mars: A City on the Edge of
 Forever. This was chronicled in the video tape, Hoagland's Mars Vol Il (Extended Version). Richard will
 speak to us about the Mars Mission findings and the monuments and face of Mars.

 Linda Moulton Howe         is a three time Emmy Award winning documentary producer and author. She
 established her own production company, LMH Productions in 1983. Linda's Emmy Award winning fllm A
 Strange Harvest is about the animal mutilation mystery which has haunted parts of the U.S. and Canada Since
 the 1960's. In addition to her 32 awards, Linda was the supervising producer and original concept creator for
 the paramount studio special UFO Report: Sightings, which led to the Sightings series on the Fox network. Ms.
 Howe's presentation entitled Glimpses of Other Realities (a soon to be released book) explores the 1967-1993
 animal mutilation mystery associated with strange lights and helicopters. She will also summarize eyewitness
 testimonies linking mutilations and human abductions to non-human creation; describe alien taxonomy
 emerging from the human abduction syndrome and highlight the bio-physical investigation of the crop circle
 enigma.

 David M jacobs Ph.D. associate professor of history atTemple University, is a leading academic authority on
 UFO's. Dr. Jacobs's Ph.D. dissertation has been published by Indiana University Press as The UFO Controversy
 in America (1975). Dr. Jacobs teaches courses in American political, social and cultural history. He also
 teaches what is believed to be the only regularly scheduled undergraduate course on UFO's offered at a major
 university. His most recent boo k Secret Life takes us into the private world of the abductee and reveals what it
 is like to be abducted. Secret Life is a Simon & Schuster book, and is based on over 300 true life accounts of
 abductions, including hypnosis sessions with 60 individuals. Dr. Jacobs will speak about the results of
 abduction research.

 John E. Mack M.D. is Professor of Psychiatry, Harvard Medical School atThe Cambridge Hospital. He is
 also the Founding Director of the Center for Psychological Studies in the Nuclear Age. As a child and adult
 psychoanalyst, he has applied the insights of depth psychology to the nuclear weapons competition, the global
 ecological crisis and other collective phenomena that threaten our survival. His publications include A Prince of
 Our Disorder, a Pulitzer Prize winning biography ofT. E. Lawrence (Lawrence of Arabia). In the last few
 years he has been exploring frontiers of human consciousness and the nature and meaning of the UFO abduction
 phenomenon. In the recent CBS-TV Mini Series "Intruders" actor Richard Crenna played the role of a
 psychiatrist, based on Dr. Macks experiences. Dr. Mack will address the conference on the spiritually of
 abductions.
