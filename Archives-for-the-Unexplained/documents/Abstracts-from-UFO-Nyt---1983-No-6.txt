   Kim Møller Hansen: UFO Classic - Antonio Villas Boas,    1957,
   p. 34-37.

   In the series of UFO classies Kim M. Hansen this time deals
   with the Boas case from 1957. Both his initial sighting on
   October the 5th as well as his fallewing close encounter of
   the "worst kind" on October the 15th. On this occasion he
   was seduced by an alleged space woman.

   Two recent comments on the case are referenced. Åge Skjelborg
   tries in the Danish book "På sporet af UFOnauten" ("On the
   Track of the UFOnaut", 1979) to evaluate the Boas case in a
   psycological and sociological framework. In his opinion
   Boas had a sexual fantasy caused by lack of sleep and Boas'
   situation living as a bachelor together with two mårried
   brothers.

   In 1978 Villas Boas was interviewed on a Brazilian TV-show
   (CUFOS obtained a video copy). Boas still sticks to his
   original story and by now he is a lawyer and happily married.
