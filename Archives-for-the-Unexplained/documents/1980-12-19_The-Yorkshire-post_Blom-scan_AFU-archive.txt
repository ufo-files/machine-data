POLICE who chased an Unidentified Flying Object
for more than 12 miles have been told that their
quarry was more probably the planet Venus.
This is the view of Dr. Robin Jakeways, a physics
lecturer at Leeds University, who was called in to give
a professional opinion.
The chase began early on Saturday. September
13, after several police officers, stationed at Weth-
erby, saw a bright moving light in the sky.
Four policemen gave chase - two of them ending
up at Fulford, near York. The bill for the 13j-mile
chase will now be footed by taxpayers.
The object was photographed subsequently by a
police photographer, and recorded on radar in three
separate locations.
An inquiry conducted by Contact International, a
UFO study group, claims other possibly related
sightings.
A co-ordinator with Contact International, Mr.
Graham Birdsall, said last night he had supplied Dr.
Jakeways with copies of the police photographs.
