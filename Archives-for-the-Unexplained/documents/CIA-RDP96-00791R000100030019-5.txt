                                                                   21 Feb 1995


       MEMO FOR:           Michael Drillings
SG11
       FROM:              I             I
                           Office of Research and Development

       SUBJECT:            Draft Task Statement for NRC in Anticipation of
                           Request for Military Liaison Support


       Dear Michael:

             Enclosed is a draft task statement for NRC on this
       Congressionally directed study which we discussed last week
       on the phone.  I don't know how realistic it is to expect all
       this in three months and I will do everything I can to get
       the program transfer deadline of 1 July extended.  I realize
       this could impact both you and the NRC and will do everything
       I can to mitigate that impact.  In the mean time, tell me
       what you think. Since you've been down this road before, I
       am eager to hear your advice.  I also will be very dependent
       on your cooperation. Give me a call when you've had a chance
       to review the draft.

                                                         Thanks

                                                                                    SG11
