goal had been endorsed by the Vice President
at the time of the Apollo 11 launch in July 1969). This plan was not accepted by the president and
only the Space Shuttle was approved for
development. Agnew died 17 September, 1996 of lukemia. See Roger D . Launiu , 11 ASA and
