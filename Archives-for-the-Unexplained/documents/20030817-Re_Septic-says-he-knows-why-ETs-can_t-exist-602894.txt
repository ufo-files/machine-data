  William Paley wrote a whole book giving examples from nature. But all
  of those can be answered with the evolutionary argument: complex
  organs like the eye exist precisely because they are the ones that
  'work' for the organism in question.
  As for the modern alleged evidence of design, I'm not too familiar
  with it, but I've read some evidence of certain irrational constants -
  'Phi' being the only one I remember - popping up over and over in
  nature. And there is the probability argument that the op mentioned -
  one estimate that I've read gives something like like 1:1x10^43.
  Of course, the incidents of Phi could be completely coincidental - or
  could be caused by similar physical laws, which we don't know as yet.
  And the probability argument is not convincing unless one arbitrarily
  lowers the probability of there being a god (by assuming 'necessary
  existence' or somesuch).
