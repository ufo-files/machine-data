       I t's frightening how close your humble editor came to overlooking
the marvelous new UFO book mentioned above. When author Bob Sheaffer re­
fused to arrange a free review copy for us, we said to ourselves, "To hell
with it - Who needs to read another skeptical saucer book anyhow? "
       But Sheaffer kept sending us blurbs full of favorable reviews from
"real" magazines - sprinkled with just a scattering of unfavorable reviews,
such as the one by MUFON's Walt Andrus, who called the book "An insult to
the intelligence of any knowledgeable reader". Our curiosity was aroused
(jus·t as Sheaffer had. plotted!) After all, could any book condemned by
Walt Andrus be anything less than inspiring?    We had to find out for sure:
       Then, while visiting Gray Barker recently (see next story),we found
that he had gone so far as to buy a copy, and, gentle non-subscribers, we
actually read it - or parts of it, anyhow.
       Now, you know that your humble editor is only susceptible to flat­
tery, bribery, and (sometimes) Truth. Since no one has ever bothered to
bribe us, due to our small circulation, and Truth is hard to come by, that
leaves flattery. And, quite frankly, after reading Page 153, and especi­
ally the part quoted above, we became immediately convinced of the dedica­
tion, deep perception, and amazing 3t-D insight which the author obviously
possesses. No one has ever before had the intuitive genius to compare us
favorably to Voltaire - though we wonder:    Voltaire is said by Catholics
to have died screaming for a priest. Does this perchance imply that your
editor may someday expire while screaming for Dr. Hynek? We hope· not:
       While the quality of writing on Page 153 could scarcely be maintain­
ed throughout, there are other good parts to the book also - too numerous
to elaborate on here. Sheaffer has really (seriously, folks!) done a
thorough job of researching the complex details of the goings-on in the
UFO field, regarding interpretation of sightings as well as intramural     .
feuds such as "Saucer Smear" delights in. The book flows in a witty,read­
able manner, and the author's skepticism is not thrust upon us unduly.
       In his summation at the end of the book, Sheaffer tells us, in ef­
fect, that the world has achieved its unprecedented population and tech­
nological growth through science; and if we let the "mythology" of UFOs
and similar anti-scientific backsliding take over, all these gains will be
lost. But it could as well be argued that, through mindless over-popula­
tion and the evil side of technology (atom bombs, etc.) science itself has
taken us to the very brink of world-wide disaster. If that is the case,
and even assuming as Sheaffer does that Ufology is an aspect of mythology,
aren't we nevertheless better off with 3t-D demons and gremlins than with

" Only time will tell"
