       THE BOOK DEALS WITH HOW THE GOVERNMENT TRIED TO EDUCATE MORE DOCTORS.
       AND THE BOOK LISTS THE STRENGTH AND WEAKNESS IN THE MEDICAL
       TREATMENT AREA AND MENTIONS MEDICAL INDUSTRY COMBINES THE WESTERN
       AND TRADITIONAL CHINESE MEDICINE TOGETHER TO PROVIDE MORE EFFECTIVE
       CURING METHODS.    AT LAST, THE BOOK TALKS ABOUT THE ACHIEVEMENT IN
       BIRTH CONTROL TECHNIQUES AND IMPROVEMENT IN WOMEN AND CHILDREN
       HEALTH. THERE ARE 10 CHAPTERS TO THIS VOLUME.
