NEW LIGHT ON    THE 1e97   "AIRSHIP"   MYSTERY

       Famed UFO historian Jerry Clark has come forth with a very
interesting new analysis of this well-known 1897 "flap".     The
article is called "UFOs or Mystery Airships?" and appears in
the March 200B issue of "International UFO Reporter", of which
Clark is an editor.
       There are significant differences between the "airships"
and mainstream UFO phenomena. In fact, Clark believes the air­
ships were "experience anomalies" which in some complex way are
neither objectively true �untrue. On the other hand, we as­
sume ( though we could be wrong ) that he believes mainstream UFOs
are real, 3-D, and interplanetary.
       In general the 1B97 objects were large, egg-shaped, and
with differing external light patterns. They sometimes had fins,
wings, or even sails, and they were almost always seen at night.
Their existence is known almost entirely from newspaper articles
of that era, rather than from direct testimony by witnesses. There are apparently no photo­
graphs. It can be reasonably assumed that some of the newspaper stories were made up, as a
joke or hoax, but not all' fall into that category.
       To us the fas·cinating part is that, when the ships landed and entities were seen, they
all appeared as ordinary human beings. In at least three cases the airship riders spoke in
ordinary English to the local people on the ground, and even discussed their travels, to a
limited extent.
        Thus it is tempting to·assume that these were human inventors, but this is     almost
impossible to be the case. The detailed history    of aviation makes it clear this  is not the
answer.
       Some of the stories were, indeed, blatant hoaxes - most notably the Aurora, Texas in­
cident, in which an airship was alleged to have crashed into a windmill in the tiny town.
The Martian pilot was said to have been buried in the local cemetery. In the 1960s, a well­
known UFO enthusiast named Hayden Hewes tried unsuccessfully to allow him to dig in their
cemetery , where at one time there was actually a gravestone for the Martian. ( It was later
hidden or stolen. ) So, if the alleged Martian was ever buried there, he probably still is..
       A new book on this whole subject is due out later this year, after many years of de­
lays. It is called "Secrets of Dellschau - The Sonora Aero Club and the Airships of the
1eoos". It is co-authored by researcher Dennis Crenshaw of "Hollow Earth" fame and artist
Pete Navarro. The publisher is Anomalist Books, run by ufologists Dennis Stacy Patrick
Huyghe.                                                                            &
