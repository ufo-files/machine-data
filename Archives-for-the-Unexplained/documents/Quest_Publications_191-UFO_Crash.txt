          Dear Mr Redfern

                 This is in response to your correspondence of 3 Feb 94 regarding whether or not the so-
         called "Roswell Incident" could possibly be explained by the theory that this was a test US missile
         crash that may have had rhesus monkeys as passengers (and hence, that the bodies of such
         primates were subsequently mistaken as "aliens"). Although I have seen similar speculation in the
         popular press as a possible alternative explanation of this incident, I have never seen any US
         Government documents, classified or not, to indicate that this might be so.

                  As you know, the "Roswell Incident" was a seminal event in UFO believers' history and it
         is vitally important to believers that this incident be attributed to a crashed "flying saucer" (or
         "flying disc" as they were called in those days); and that alien bodies were recovered from the
         "spacecraft" . Simply put, if that explanation is not true and there is any other explanation (such as
         the one put forth in your letter) then much of what has been published subsequently regarding
         UFO's would automatically be proven spurious (the so-called "MJ-12 papers" are an obvious
         example). Other possible explanations, such as the crashed experimental aircraft or downed
         Japanese Fuga balloon theories would have the same result. Likewise, new confirmation of the
         historically stated explanation (downed balloon) would further detract from many subsequent
         UFO claims, which have flowed from the "Roswell Incident_"

                  Coincidentally, with the recent renewal of interest in the "Roswell Incident" within UFO
         circles, there has been concurrent official US government interest generated by a Congressman
         from New Mexico. As a result, the General Accounting Office (GAO), the investigative arm of
         Congress, will be inquiring into how such reports were previously handled, with an apparent focus
         on the "Roswell Incident" . This inquiry is being initiated as this is written_ Certainly your crashed
         rocket theory seems a reasonable scenario to explore for possible records as are the other theories
         mentioned. I assume GAO will consider them all.

                    Part of the frustration in trying to explain such incidents well after the fact is the average
           citizen's lack of knowledge of informational and archival systems. Specifically, most people do
           not realize the tyP,e or volume of inform~·on available in US archives. More importantly, they do
           not realize the type or amount of data not available. Consequently, when information is not
           available it is usually attributed to malevolent motivations or sinister "cover-ups." When it is
           available but doesn't correlate with the information seeker's theory, then it is often characterized
           as having been altered or labeled as disinformation. This is truly a "no-win" situation for those of
           us in government. We are, in effect, either bunglers or conspirators.

                    Since you have taken your valuable time to write to me in my official capacity, you should
           be aware of the fact thar any response from !ne wm be \~e•ved as highly su~pect from some
           persons within the UFO community. In fact, I am routinely vilified by a number ofUS UFO
           researchers (and at least one ofyour countrymen) as one ofthe arch co-conspirators preventing
           the public from learning the "real truth" about UFO's. The actual truth is that there is no grand
           conspiracy and I would very much like to devote full time to my real job of protecting currently
           classified national defense information.

                   I thank you for your correspondence and recommend that you await the GAO report of
           this matter. Since the GAO is not part of the US military, perhaps its findings will be viewed in a
           different light.
