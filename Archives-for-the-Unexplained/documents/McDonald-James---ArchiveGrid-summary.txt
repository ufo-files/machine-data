     Details
    66 linear ft.

    Papers, 1904-1997 (bulk 1958-1971). Mostly correspondence, cases, reports, interviews, and printed
    materials relating to James E. McDonald's investigations, 1958 to 1971, into unidentified flying objects and
    similar sightings.

    Professor and associate director of The University of Arizona Institute of Atmospheric Physics.

    Inventory available in the library folder level control.
