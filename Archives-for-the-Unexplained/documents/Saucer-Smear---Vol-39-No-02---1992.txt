      The developments in the Roswell case are too diverse and too complex
for us to keep up with all of them in "Smear". In essence, as the above
article by Moore indicates, there are three wildly competing research teams

also several lesser-known Roswell researchers, including an anthropologist
named Thomas Carey, who has written a fascinating article for the Nov.-Dec.
1991 issue of "International UFO Reporter" (IUR), entitled "The Search for
the Archaeologists".
      A key part of the Roswell folklore, begun by a witness named Barney
Barnett, is that a team of archaeologists was present in early July, 1947,
when a group of 3 or 4 alien bodies was found on the Plains of San Agustin
near Magdalena, New Mexico. (This is one of the two or possibly three sites
at which various remains of the so-called Roswell crash were supposedly found}

pedition was a man named Dr. Buskirk, from the University of Pennsylvania.
