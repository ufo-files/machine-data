Cincinnati Skeptic
                 Newsletter of the Association for Rational Thought
Vol. 14 No. 1                                                                  August/September, 2004

                     September Meeting “Penn and Teller’s Bullshit”
                 The bad boys of magic, Penn and Teller, will be featured at the September 11 meeting of
        the Association for Rational Thought. The meeting will feature two shows from the first season of
        the “Bullshit” series. The series plays on the Showtime network.
                 Each episode features an exploration of one or more skeptical topics. Penn and Teller
        attack each topic in their irreverent style. They are able to get their message across using humor.
                 We hope you will be able to join us on September 11th at 10:00 AM at the Dubliner
        Restaurant, 6111 Montgomery Road, Pleasant Ridge.

                                     Needed: Newsletter Editor
                 Last year at the ART “Futures” meeting the newsletter was described as one of the most
        important activities that ART does. After many years of editing the newsletter Wolf Roder retired
        in 2004. We are currently in need of someone to edit the newsletter. If you have the time to
        devote to editing the newsletter, we can mentor you. If you have edited a newsletter before, you
        may mould the Cincinnati Skeptic to your vision. Contact Rick Davis, ART President, 513-533-
        8142 or rrdavis@fuse.net if you would like to discuss taking over this exciting challenge.

                 Roxanne Qualls to Speak to ART at October Meeting
                 Former mayor of Cincinnati Roxanne Qualls has agreed to speak to ART on “Political
        Advertising” for the October meeting. Former Mayor Qualls is currently teaching at Northern
        Kentucky University. This meeting will be co-sponsored by the student group, the UC Skeptics.
        Rather than our regular meeting, former Mayor Qualls will speak at 7:00 PM on Wednesday,
        October 6th on the University of Cincinnati campus. At press time a venue had not be set.
        Visit our website: www.CincinnatiSkeptics.org for the building and room. For those unfamiliar
        with the University of Cincinnati campus visit www.uc.edu/directions for a campus map.

                                          Upcoming Meetings
                  At the end of October ART and UC Skeptics will co-sponsor a talk by Marc Abrahms,
        publisher and editor of the “Annals of Irrelevant Research.” Marc brings his fun review of
        research that should not be repeated (and perhaps shouldn’t have been done once). This meeting
        will held on the UC campus in the evening. Presently we are working out details of his visit. This
        will take the place of our November meeting.
                  In December we will return to our Saturday morning meeting schedule at the Dubliner.
        ART members Wolf Roder and Randy Weaver will lead an interesting and wide-ranging
        discussion of Jared Diamond’s theories of economics, sociology and geography. Jared Diamond
        is the author of “Guns, Germs and Steel,” and “Why is Sex Fun?” among other books and
        articles.
                  January will be our annual “Ten-Minute” meeting where members will be able to discuss
        what is on their minds for 10 minutes. This is always a fun meeting. Start preparing now!
                  We have a number of other meetings in the planning stages. Stay tuned for future
        announcements.
                                               Dues are Due
         August is the time for dues collection for all continuing members. A change in the
bylaws makes it cheaper for retirees and students to be members. Retirees and students pay ½ of
the regular rate: $7.50. If you don’t want to have to remember to pay your dues every year you
now have the option of a lifetime membership for $180. Regular member dues are $15 per year.
You may mail in your payment to “A.R.T., P.O Box 12896, Cincinnati, OH 45212.” Or you may
present a check or money order to our Treasurer Rick Prairie at any ART meeting.

                                          Science Book Club
         The Science Book Club meets at 2:30 on the 4th Sunday of every month in the 3rd floor
meeting room of the main library downtown. The remaining schedule for 2004: Sept. 26 – The
Collapse of Complex Societies Joseph Tainter; Oct. 24 –The Blank Slate Steven Pinker; Nov. 21
(3rd Sunday) -- A Devil’s Chaplain Richard Dawkins; Dec. 19 (3rd Sunday) -- Structure of
Evolutionary Theory Stephen Jay Gould.
