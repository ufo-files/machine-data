                      WARNING CONCERNING COPYRIGHT RESTRICTIONS
The copyright law of the United States (Title 17, United States Code) governs the making ~f photocop_ies or
other reproductions of copyrighted material. The law extends statutory rights of authorship to unpublished
works that were previously protected by literary property rights under common law. Such works do not
have to be registered with the Copyright Office to receive protection under this law.

In general the law provides copyright protection for a term of the life of the author plus 50 years .
Unpublished and uncopyrighted works created before January 1, 1978, are covered as of that date by this
same provision and are protected at the minimum until December 31, 2002. Works already in the public
domain and works prepared by U.S. Government employees as part of official duties are not protected by
copyright.

Under certain conditions specified in the law, libraries and archives are authorized to furnish a photocopy
or other reproduction . One of these specified conditions is that the photocopy or reproduction is not to be
"used tor any purpose other than private study, scholarship ·or research." If a user makes a request for, or
later uses, a photocopy or reproduction for purposes in excess of "fair use," that user may be liable for
copyright infringement.

Persons wishing to publish any unpublished writings included in the papers of the Library should obtain
permission from the holder of the copyright. Permission to reproduce copyrighted materials in the Library's
still photograph, motion picture, cartoon, and oral history collections must also be obtained from the
copyright holder. This institution reserves the right to refuse to accept a copying order if, in its judgment,
fulfillment of the order would involve a violation of copyright law.


                        CITING LYNDON B. JOHNSON LIBRARY MATERIALS

Citations should identify items clearly, specify their file locations, and end with reference to the LBJ Library. Most
citations will concern items from the manuscript collections and should contain these elements: type of document;
names of sender and recipient or title of document; date; folder title; box number; collection title; and the words "LBJ
Library." "WHCF" is the common abbreviation for White House Central Files; "C.F. 11 for Confidential File; and "NSF"
for the National Security File. The following are examples of citations. Please consult the guide "Citing Historical
Materials" for more examples. It is available in the Reading Room and on the Library's web site,
www.lbjlib.utexas.edu.
