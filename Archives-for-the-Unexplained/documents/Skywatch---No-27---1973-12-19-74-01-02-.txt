                  THE   ETERNAL       SUBJECT

                By Brinsley le Poer Trench.

      This book by the International Chairman of Contact
and well-known author, puts forward the idea that Ufo's
and the Sky People were directly involved in the creation
and development of Man, and leaves us with much to ponder
over.    Certainly, Ufo's seem to have been with us through
the ages, with reports going back all through history of
strange flying discs, cigars, triangular and star-shaped
objects being seen in our skies.    And it seems that their
interest in planet Earth has not waned!

     The Argentine has been a hive of Ufo activity over the
past few years, and according to Spanish newspaper reports
the Ufo's have a base in the depths of Lake Titicaca, which
borders Peru and Bolivia.   The author gives a number of
saucer reports over South America, and wonders whether the
authorities are aware of the bases and whether this fact
has been covered up.   Nevertheless, the author feels an
informed public is a prepared public!

     This book is illustrated with some excellent drawings
of biblical Ufo events by the Yugoslavian ufologist, Kiril
Terziev, and certainly one cannot help but read the bible
with a new outlook after reading the chapter on Flying
Saucers in the Bible and seeing the illustrations.

     No Ufo book would be complete without some mention of
the paranormal aspects of Ufo's, and yet, as Brinsley le
Poer Trench states, neither Ufo's nor psychic phenomena have
                           - 4 -


actually been officially recognised by the powers that be.
So he is attempting to show a connection between two subjects
which officially do not exist�   Nonetheless, a few chapters
are devoted to teleportation, cloud Ufo's, telepathy,
levitation, materialization and the effects of Ufo's on animals,
and some interesting cases are quoted.   Through these
chapters, the author points out the similarities between the
Ufo's and the psychic world, and suggests that some energy
or radiation is the force used in all these phenomena, and that
basically the same subtle force is being used, perhaps in a
different way in all these aspects of the paranormal.

     Some very strange experiences have occurred to people
all over the world during recent years.    It is becoming
obvious that ufonauts come from many areas - possibly some
emanate from outer space; some from invisible universes
or other space-time continuums to our own.    Some of the
visitors may be benevolent, others indifferent and some
downright hostile.

     Two contact stories quoted are most bizarre.      In the
one case Mrs. Dollie Hansen of West Akron, U. S. A. , was in
touch with the occupants of a Ufo and was offered a trip in
their spacecraft.    On the night in question Mrs. Hansen
waited until 2.00 a. m. for her space friends.    When a
knock came at the door, she opened it to find a superbly
built young woman with beautiful, dark chestnut, almost
black hair, but this unusually beautiful girl had no
features where her face should have been.    However, Mrs.
Hansen felt no fear and accompanied the young woman and was
taken on her space ride, together with a crippled boy and
two more people for whom a space ride had been requested.

     Exactly two weeks later a Mrs. Vellacca of South Akron
reported a contact with a woman, marvellously built, with
beautiful long dark chestnut, almost black hair, but the
woman had no discernible features where her face would be
located!

     The author puts forward a concept which he feels has
                          -   5   -

a great bearing on "The Eternal Subject" - the continuous
visitations of people from other realms to our planet.
Briefly, it is that the Sky People made and put us here, and
they have not only continued to pay visits to our planet,
but to interbreed with us.   So perhaps we are a hybrid
race, partly descended from super-intelligent Sky People and
sub-intelligent ape men.   Quite a thought!   And could this
explain why the occupants of the Ufo's today have such a
great interest in us?

     It is most encouraging to find that Brinsley le Poer
Trench is optimistic about our young people and our future,
although he does not deny that what some prophets of doom
have predicted might come to pass!   Certainly, we have a
great task ahead of us before we can take our place in the
galactic civilization.

     We heartily recommend this book, and are sure that
having read it you will want to read it again.   It is a
book which, although so simply put forward, covers many
deeper aspects of ufology, and the explanations offered are
worth a lot of thought.

                  STARTL ING   NEW   DISCOVERY

     One of the most inspiring and thrilling of recent dis­
closures of Astronomers, is that there is a great empty space
in the North, in the nebula of the constellation of Orion, a
heavenly cavern so gigantic that the mind of man cannot
comprehend it, and so brilliantly beautiful that words cannot
adequately describe it.

      The revelations were made possible by gigantic lenses,
plus long exposures of photographic plates which in turn can
be further magnified.    This increases the vision of man so
tremendously that he is able to peer into the depths of
interstellar space and glimpse the vastness of infinity
itself.    What has been found corroborates the words of Job:
11He stretcheth out the North over the empty place".     ( Job
26:70).    In a book entitled "Early Writing .. published in
1 848, the writer says: "The Powers of Heaven will be
shaken at the Voice of God, the Sun, Moon and Stars will
be moved out of their places.    Dark heavy clouds came up
and clashed against each other.    The atmosphere parted ..
and rolled back.    Then we could look up through the open
space in Orion whence came the Voice of God: The Holy
City will come down through that space".

     Modern astronomers all agree that there is a huge
opening in Orion which is perhaps more than 16 740 000
million miles in diameter.    The diameter around the earth's
orbit is 1 86 000 000 miles, which distance in itself is
incomprehensible to man - yet, the opening into this
heavenly cavern of Orion is 90 000 times as wide.    In other
words, we could put 30 000 solar systems like our own, with
a sun in the middle of each, across the entrance of the
opening in the North, and still have room to spare.

     But surpassing the immensity of its size is its
exquisite beauty and the luminous colours that are unlike
any upon earth.   Professor Larkin of Mt. Lowe Observatory
gives us the following description of it :

"These negatives ( photographs ) reveal the opening and

interior of a cavern so stupendous that our entire solar
system would be lost therein.   I have watched it since
the days of youth in many telescopes of many powers. But
never dreamt that the central region is the mouth of a
colossal cave.   Human speech is impotent.   Pen of writer
and brush of artist alike are lifeless and inert in any
attempt to describe this interior. "

     In the depths of the Orion nebula are torn, twisted
and riven masses of shining glass.   Irregular pillars,
columns of stalactites in glittering splendour and
stalagmites from the mighty floor. The appearance is like
that of Light, shining and glowing behind Herculean walls
of Ivory and Pearl, and studded with millions of Diamond­
shining stars.

     There must be some reason why all this grandeur is
lavished on this one spot in the Heavens, why the coloured
area hue peculiar to Orion is studded around the opening so
that they appear as a pavement of starry sand.

                      IN PASSING
     Some time ago in Skywatch magazine we reviewed the
book 11 Uninvited Visitors .. by Ivan T. Sanderson.   How many
of you have read the book by now?       Or do you perhaps
remember what the essence of his book is?

     Briefly, Ivan Sanderson believes that the evidence
shows that the greater part of the Ufo phenomenon is a
biological matter, and sets out to enlarge the reader•s
concepts of organisms.   He compares the unusual aerial
forms photographed in 1958 by New Zealander, Trevor James,
with unicellular life-forms such as the amoebic ones.
Further, he proposes that not all Ufo•s are objects or
machines manufactured by intelligent beings, but could be
cosmic life-forms with their own pattern of existence.
Or maybe some are a combination of these two possibilities.
Some extra-terrestrials may have the knowhow to construct
machines along biological lines; these could have been
copied from existing life-forms which have already
adapted themselves to life in space.   He also faces the
possibility that intelligences are able to breed these
animals or life-forms, or even create them, by purely
bio-chemical means.

     Bearing this in mind, one can but wonder about a
recent report in a Dallas, Texas, newspaper.     Apparently
a space 11thing11 suddenly appeared in the back yard of
Mrs. Marie Harris.     It came from the sky, was a jelly­
like mass which pulsated, foamed and resisted all efforts
to kill it.     It grew rapidly in size but then shrivelled
up and died before researchers had time to study it fully.

     The 11thing11 started out the size of two cookies, and
grew to the size of about 16 cookies in a couple of weeks.
By the time scientists were called in, it had grown to the
size of 32 cookies.     Tornado weather struck Dallas, and
heavy rains seemed to wash the blob away overnight - but
the next morning it was back and three times as big and
in three sections.     Then it shrivelled up and died.
                         - 13 -

     Scientists confirmed that the "thing" had come from the
skies, and was a collection of spores which constantly land
on the earth•s surface from the atmosphere.   Arnold Dittman,
the researcher, stated:

     "These spores come from God knows where.   It is in
fact a bacterial mutation which nature creates whenever
conditions are conducive to its growth".
