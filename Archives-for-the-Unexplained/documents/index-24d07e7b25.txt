  Publisher's Synopsis

  Prime Evil masterfully weaves familiar niggling lore and phenomenon with existing texts and traditions to make an
  extraordinary and provocative premise seem unnervingly plausible. And though cloaked in fiction and executed in a
  fast-paced, contemporary style with twists and turns that mask the startling conclusion until the very end, the
  underpinnings of the book include current scientific inquiry, recently translated scriptures, and actual events, which
  leaves the reader wondering just where the fiction ends and the truth begins.

  This novel is the culmination of a life-long spiritual quest, a quest that has been riddled with questions, and in some
  ways is just beginning. Prime Evil addresses the first series of questions by asking why, for all our progress, are we still
  killing each other, still destroying the planet, still honoring technology over humanity, money over love? And if indeed
  Earth was meant to be a school where fledging spirits could learn and grow and one day embrace infinitude, well then,
  thank heaven for eternity for it appears it will take that long. The book also asks the question--if all the legends and lore
  are true is reality the illusion? Though a spiritual-science thriller, Prime Evil is unique in that it proposes true science-
  fiction lies in our past, not our future, and that it's not fiction at all but historical fact supported by existing documents.
  Fairies, angels, gargoyles, bigfoot, aliens, crop circles, cattle mutilations...none are flights of fancy or of extraterrestrial
  origin, but are in fact a part of our history that dates back to the age of the dinosaurs. But in the end, as all good endings
  go, Prime Evil reveals a secret, a magical truth that holds a glorious promise for mankind's future. Enjoy and dare to
  wonder!

  IF ALL THE LEGENDS AND LORE ARE TRUE IS REALITY THE ILLUSION?

  Breanna Shepard, the strong-willed Senator from Virginia, experiences a series of bizarre and shattering events--events
  that breach the veil between myth and reality, revealing a world lost in darkness, trapped in a lie spawned 300 million
  years ago! As reality begins to shift then crumble around her Breanna wonders, is she loosing her mind or finding her
  soul?
