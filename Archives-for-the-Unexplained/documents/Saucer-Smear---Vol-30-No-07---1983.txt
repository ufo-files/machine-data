                    THE S.B.I. INVADES ALBANY,        NEW YORK!

      On the weekend of August 12th-14th your editor attended the first
annual SBI (Scientific Bureau of Investigation) convention, held    at a
luxurious Ramada Inn in Albany, N.Y.   (Left photo shows the little group
that accompanied your editor to the Con: Left to right are Anna   Mont­
gomery, Patrick Montgomery, and Betty Moseley.)
      Naturally what we enjoyed most of all was the "get acquainted"
cocktail party on Friday night.   On Saturday and Sunday, supreme  host
& master of ceremonies Pete Mazzola guided us through an array of mostly
lesser-known ufological speakers, including himself.   Among those offer­
ing presentations were John Keel, Antonio Huneeus, Arthur Bray, Marcello
Galluppi, David Waters, Pat de la Franier, Peter Robins, Christopher
Centi, Dan Lowenski, and Colman VonKeviczky.   Our friend and NUFOC   co­
worker Rick Hilberg of Cleveland was listed on the program, but chicken­
ed out at the last minute for obscure business reasons, just as   he   did
this past May in regard t.o the NUFOC convention in Tucson, Arizona.
      Although only about 50 people were present, Mazzola tells us    that
many others paid but failed to show up, and that he (miraculously!) made
a profit on the event.  This fortuitous eventuality has led him to state
that he intends to hold another SBI conclave next summer - this time in
New York City.
      Among those who did show up in Albany was Mary Robinson   of   Jer­
sey City, widow of famed researcher John J. Robinson.   Accompanying her
was Sol Lipsky of Brooklyn.   Mary has been promised a spot on the speak-

       Of the speakers we managed to listen to (the nearby pool was more
 tempting! ) , by far the best lecture was given by veteran researcher John

·later rec·onfirmed his own skepticism in the course of private conversa-

 tion of his entire career. Gesturing wildly with a pointer aimed at a
 map of the world, John beguiled us with a discussion of such things as
 "atomic hand grenades"; "steel nets across the f jords of Scandinavia"
 possibly put there by the Space People; "black urine", which became a
 problem for one Swedish contactee (as it might for anyone! ) ; and the
 astounding little-known fact that "the Miracle of Lourdes took place at
 a garbage dump". Let us hope indeed that this colorful,skeptic7believer
 never retires from the ufological scene!
       In all, an entertaining weekend, and our thanks to Pete Mazzola
 for his hospitality.
