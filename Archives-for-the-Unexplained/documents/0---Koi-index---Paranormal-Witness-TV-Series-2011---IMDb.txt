      F E AT U R E D R E V I E W                                                                                                              8/10

     Far Better Than Expected
     Paranormal Witness is a documentary style, high-production testimonial show of people who have witnessed some pretty crazy. I
     wasn't expecting much, but the first episode was pretty awesome. If you like spooky stories, like I do, this is right up your alley... it's
     really well done, and the stories are narrated by alleged first hand witnesses who tell their stories. They all come off as likable,
     realistic and not annoying, whew. That was a definite concern for me going in.


     Each episode covers 2-3 stories, and this would work really well as a Halloween marathon show with friends.


     Definitely check this one out.
