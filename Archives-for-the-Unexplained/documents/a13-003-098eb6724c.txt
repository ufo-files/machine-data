   Regarding the patent search site and the URL for the drawings of Rockwell
   and L-M proposed aircraft that I sent to the list earlier, one thing to
   keep in mind:
   In the world of secrecy, few things are so well 'hidden in plain sight' as
   patent applications.
   All the author has to do is patent the crucial components, and these can
   be hidden in the design within the complexity of the application.
   This is more apparent with complex machined parts and apparatus, and in
   the case of the tobacco industry, can be done by requesting the patent in
   foreign countries and developing in another country. (it took a long time
   for the Justice department to find the evidence of patenting strains of
   plants with higher niccotine content for this reason.)
   Also, the designs in the URL I posted, although accepted for patent in
   1996, may have been under development much earlier.
   Since this is all related to secrecy and aircraft design, I hope it is
   deemed on topic.
