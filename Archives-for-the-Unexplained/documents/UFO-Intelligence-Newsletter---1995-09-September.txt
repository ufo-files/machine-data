JUST IMAGINE
   Imagine        for     a     moment        intelligent            beings           not         limited        to         the
restrictions of mortality. They                        explore           the        cosmos        and    discover           the
Earth. What would they report?
   There is a species on the third planet from the star                                             they       call        "the
sun".    They call themselves "human beings". They are heavily influenced by
the fact that for them               there is a beginning and an end --- a time                                       to     be
born and a time to die.
   This might explain their time-dependent behavior between                                               one        another
--- impatience,          intolerance,            anxiety,      stress,          frustration with                life        and
themselves        and     desire        for      a     realm        beyond          death        that     they        cannot
adequately describe.
   We find they bicker,               fight and kill one another for power,                                   riches        and
material goods so they can live their lives                                    in     what        they     perceive          as
relative peace and comfort.                   Such      behavior           occurs           between        nations          and
families and individuals.
   Parents don't have time for their children.                                      Children don't             have        time
for their parents.             Little ones are forced to grow up fast                                   just to keep up
with    all the new information thrown their way.                                    The     older       species,           the
ones who live       beyond their normal                 lifespan,          aren't          given any time at all.
   Many are caught up              with          designing          time-saving             devices,           yet     still
complain they           have    no      time.         Some     of        the        more    inquisitive              humans,
physicists,       investigate this concept and realm of time.                                     One bushy          haired
fellow named       Einstein came up with an interesting perspective,                                           but no one
completely understands his theory.                        Their          struggle          to     understand              their
brief physical          existence       expresses            itself        in       their         paintings,          music,
plays,    architecture and especially                     literature.               There is a          common        desire
to escape mortality,             physical limitations and the confines                                   of     time        and
space.
   A long time ago the humans were told that their physical existence   is
just a prelude to a far greater eternal existence. They  were  also   told
that living       beings are the centerpiece of a master                                   plan,        but    the         vast
majority aren' t aware of this information. Instead, they                                               have     designed
many purposes for life but nothing ever satisfies them.
   Created to live in the realm of                        eternity             yet         now    confined           to     the
limitations of time,            no wonder they are stifled,                          anxious and bewildered.
   After closer observation,                     we noticed something very interesting.                                    Some
humans do want to understand the deeper meaning of their existence.                                                        They
see great design and purpose in the natural world around them.                                                 They        have
decided to trust in this                God they cannot see but know,                            as we     also       know,
actually exists.
   Because they want to learn and understand, the Creator has given  them
a spirit to comprehend the purpose of physical life.  As  they  begin  to
understand the plan,            their whole concept of time changes.
   Time isn't such a great                   burden       and        constraint              to     them       any        more.
Knowing their life is but a moment in                          eternity,            they     take        the    time         to
develop lasting relationships.                       For them,       there is time to love.                       Time       to
be kind.    Time to care.            And   time to do things right.
   We find it strange that while millions of humans know of this plan,                                                       so
few are willing to believe and follow                              its    guidelines.              No     wonder           this
planet has problems. ( Source: Unknown)

EARLY      CLOSE ENCOUNTERS
      In the May/June issue of the ROUNDTOWN UFO SOCIETY Newsletter                                 it      was
laying claim to  the  first  classic  (documented)  close  encounter,  at
Circleville, Ohio, on February  1,  1948.  Pete  Hartinger  welcomed  any
rebuttal about this.
      A   quick check with the Regional Sighting Information                        Database            showed
18 cases listed as close encounters prior to                      that       sighting.           But,      this
only covers Missouri to Ohio and              Indiana        to     Tennessee,         however.            This
count also does not include anything during the                         previous       century           since
the information is very dubious and sketchy.                  The first entry is                   an      EGBA
entry (Eberhart's "Geobibliography of               Anomalies") for March 17,                      1903      at
Helmer, IN.       But the first documented case in                the    region        was        the      Rend
City,      IN, case of 1923 (Source:        SKYLOOK,      57,12).         Another       Indiana            case
investigated by Don Worley occurred in 1931 at                       Springersville,               IN.      The
first documented Ohio case listed on the                 RSID was        actually at             Cleveland,
OH, in November of 1941.
      The Circleville, OH, case may actually be                   the    first        well-documented
case, depending on the details and depth  of   the  case  report.    Besides
being interesting to the readers, another good thing came   of  reflecting
on this incident, Pete. The case listed on the RSID had   been  given    the
Hynek value of "DD" or "Daylight Disc" and was   just  upgraded   to   "C1",
Close       Encounter One.
