      Site is a large, sprawling compound-like area with at least
one large rectangular, flat-topped structure with smaller
rectangular structures on top.   There are several smaller
structures round about the area; the compound is surrounded by a
fence and is located in an arid, isolated area.   It is further
guarded and protected and isolated from the area itself.
Anything or anyone is excluded unless they have specific
authorization and reason to be there.   The main structure is low
for its width and height, but still has a few levels or floors
within it.   Inside, it is somewhat primitive, makeshift, run-
down--impression of unshielded incandescent lights casting
shadows, austere walls, somewhat old and worn.   There are
corridors and rooms, within one of which is a group of people who
seem intensely "pulled together" by what they're discussing--
seems like some sort of religious coming-together.   In this
structure is one large open area whose purpose is "housing",
"storage", and "assembly".   Within this area are several round,
long, tapered things that are shiny cream-grey in color with
markings and small details.   One is partially covered.   They are
supported by scaffolding-like bracing and supported on cross
blocks on the floor;   there is a large rack that seems to be
holding from four to six of the objects.   There are a total of
eight to ten perceived.   They suggest strong AOL of Maverick
ATGM.   Objects are so large they must be handled with heavy-duty
forklifts or small industrial cranes when being shifted around
inside storage area or are brought in from outside or removed
from inside.   More of these objects come in a few at a time from
time to time, and leave in the same manner at about the same
intervals.   There seems to be a rather constant number on hand
despite movement in and out.   Further impression is that this is
the only place in the compound these things are kept, since they
are so important or require such stringent accountability
protocols that whoever is responsible for them wants them in a
centralized location.   This place reminds me of the intermediate
location reported in previous sessions.
