                             Summary of Information

 1.   The diagram on page (i) depicts a feature which is located
 within the vicinity of where the target personality has been.
 It is a recessed rectangular area with a stagnant lime green-
 colored oval shape in the bottom of it. The recessed area is
 surrounded by light colored squares with very heavy and dark
 lines. This, in turn, feels surrounded by overhangs or openings;
 not quite a patio or an atrium, but rather something very unique
 e.g. personal taste or vernacular style. Within the vicinity is
 a structure that has a tent-like appearance on top.
      The target personality has a panoramic view of a valley from
 where he has been. The terrain is rugged with surprisingly a lot
 of green as well as lots of exposed, jagged rocks.       From his
 viewpoint, there is an area containing ancient ruins reminiscent
 of Roman or Greek. These ruins are undeveloped; this vicinity is
 not particularly famous or well known to the average tourist or
 site-seer.  It is known only to a few.   It contains a double row
 of parallel poles or columns as well as a clutter of jumbled
 stone blocks, angles and rubble. There is a single object which
 stands isolated from the rest of the site.
      Sitting on the opposite side of the valley from these ruins
 is an abandoned structure constructed of large, prominent blocks.
 It is reminiscent of a medieval or feudal fort.    It appears to
 occur on relatively the same terrace or geologic stratum above
 the valley floor. This structure is only partially visible.
      A mining or earth removal activity occurs between the "fort"
 and the target's point of view.   It consists of a structure with
 two long objects projecting out at an angle.       Each of these
 objects have a cable or line attached to the end it. The ground
 surface around this structure is really chewed up. This activity
 sits within a depressionjsunken area below the level of the fort.
 This valley is associated with the phonetics:       BAKKA, BASRA,
 BANDAR, BEIDA or BASRI.
 2. The target personality is tired of the current situation. He
 wants to act now.    There are others who are trying to talk him
 out of it, but it is only token resistance.        The target is
 concerned about the loyalty of someone towards either him or the
 activity.   The target is having his left examined by a doctor.
 It is something internal, not like a cut or scrape.    The target
 is getting -·ready to move.   The impression is from where he is
 now to GREECE. He is carrying or delivering something small. It
 is extremely valuable and central to the activity.
      The target will enter a large building; maybe a house.     He
 will walk down a long corridor with rooms coming off of both
 sides.   There is a large room at the end of the corridor into
 which he will enter.      A complex, multi-component object is
 sitting within the center of this large room (see diagram iii).
