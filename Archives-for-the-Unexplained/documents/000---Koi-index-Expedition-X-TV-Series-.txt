     Jessica Chobot is the worst paranormal investigator
     I just finished the first season and I am appalled at the claim the Jessica Chobot is an "experienced" paranormal investigator! She
     freaks out at any potential contact and then she doesn't seem to have any ability to problem solve and look for logical cause for an
     experience. She automatically goes right to "it's haunted!". As someone who watched the series Ghost Hunters I find it hard to believe
     that she has any real experience as a paranormal investigator. This show would be much better if they would replace Jessica with a
     "real" paranormal investigator.
