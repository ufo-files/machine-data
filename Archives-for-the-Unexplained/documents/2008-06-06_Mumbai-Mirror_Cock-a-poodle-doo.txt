                                                                              camel-dog

London: Believe it or not, this is a pet pooch sheered, shaved, dyed, fluffed and puffed to look like a cockerel.

It’s the “brainchild” of Sandy Hartness, top dog of the mad fad known as creative grooming. And, yes, she lives in California.


Chicken little? A poodle styled as a chicken
Sandy, 36, has also turned her poodle Cindy and a friend’s pet into a dragon, a camel and an alien in a UFO. But some say she’s done them all up like dog’s dinners. She
has been colouring and cropping pet dogs for the past seven years, and is counted among America's most dedicated dog groomers.

Cindy, Sandy's patient pooch, has won numerous prizes for appearing at dog shows in the various guises, and the pair have featured in numerous editions of US dog care
industry bible Groomer.
