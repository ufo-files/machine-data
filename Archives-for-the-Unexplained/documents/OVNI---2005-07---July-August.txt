                                       The Valensole Case, 1965
At about 5.45 a.m. on 1 July, farmer Maurice Masse was in his lavender field in France, near Valensole,
when he heard a strange whistling sound. He was working in the field behind a pile of stones and stepping
out from behind them, he saw an object shaped like a rugby football with a cupola on top, about the size of
a Renault Dauphine car. The craft was standing on six legs, with a central pivot and from where he stood,
he could see through an open doorway. He noticed that there appeared to be two seats, back to back inside
the craft.

Monsieur Masse first thought that the object was an experimental helicopter or craft of some kind, but he
was then surprised when he next noticed what he took to be two eight-year- old boys bending over his
lavender plants. He thought at first that they might be stealing his lavender as some had recently gone
missing from his field. He later described the 'boys' as being less than four feet tall and clad in fairly dark
grey-green one-piece suits. When the 'boys' noticed Msr. Masse approaching them they straightened up,
and one of them pointed some kind of a 'tube' at the farmer which then immobilized him.

Maurice Masse stood there, unable to move but with his senses still functioning. He noticed that the two
'boys' had large hairless heads, smooth white skin, high fleshy cheeks and large eyes that slanted upwards.
They had pointed chins and mouths without any apparent lips. They seemed to communicate with each
other by making strange gurgling sounds that seemed to come from deep within their throats. "They were
looking at me and must have been making fun of me," said Maurice Masse in an unofficial statement he
later related to Maitre Chautard, a local magistrate. "Nevertheless their facial expressions were not ill­
natured, but very much the reverse." Masse said that in fact he felt a great sense of peace exuding from the
beings.

Still unable to move Maurice watched as after a short time the humanoids returned to their craft and
entered it through a sliding effects door. The legs of the craft whirled and retracted, as it slowly lifted off
the ground and the machine took off It was a quarter of an hour before Maurice Masse recovered his
mobility. Four days after the incident Masse suddenly collapsed, seized with an irresistible urge to sleep He
would have slept on for twenty-four hours had not his wife and father woken him up. Masse found he
needed at ]east ten or twelve hours sleep instead of his usual six or seven and this kept up for a period of
several months.

The ground where the craft had rested was soaked with moisture, although it had it not been raining and
investigators found strange, geometrically spaced landing indentations in the soil, apparently from the legs
of the craft. The case became even more remarkable when as the months went by, no lavender plants
would grow at the landing site. (see photo taken six years later by a SIGAP member ). It was some ten
years after the event before the lavender began to recover and grow again.

                                      Google Map Mystery
                                               18-May-2005

The Google Maps satellite map of Magnolia Park, Florida shows a clear view of an unusual object
floating above 39th Street in the town.

The shadow on the object is similar to the shadows on the ground, meaning that it is in the atmosphere
relatively close to the surface, probably no higher than 20,000 feet.

There are no towers or unusual objects in the area that could account for the formation, which appears to
be in rapid vibrational movement. Because of the unusual motion visible on its surface, it is not likely
that this is a balloon.

Without knowing its distance from the ground, it is impossible to determine the size of the object, but it
would seem to be quite large, even huge.

Calls to individuals living in the area and a search of news sources for 2005 revealed no reports ofUFOs
over Palm Beach or Magnolia Park this year. The last National UFO Reporting Center report for Palm
Beach was in 1999.

This is a confirmed unknown object in the air above a populated area in the United States, recorded
sometime in 2005.

               TRIANGULAR PERPESPECTIVES By Colin Saunders

Six years ago my family and 1 had a close encounter with a Flying Triangle, since that experience l
have done much reading and research into the subject of UFO s. This document is my personal
observations and theories about the enigmatic FT, this is how it all began.

31 sr March 1999, we had been out for a bar meal to a small village called Pailton in the Warwickshire
countryside. There were four of us in the car that night, at approximately 9.30 pm we left the White
Lion public house and headed home. We turned onto the Fosse Way near the village of Monks Kirby
and immediately noticed some bright lights ahead of us. The lights were_ mile away and just off the
road to the left. The lights appeared as deep red, like a Rose red with a bit of white mingled in.

We drove down parallel to the lights which I now estimate as being only 100 feet away! We had
slowed right down by this point. I stared at the four lights which were in a row, but not level with the
ground, 1 would say they were tilted at approx. 25% to the horizon. I stared into the light on the far left,
this light was the highest side of the tilt with the far right light being the anchor point for the 25%
angle. It was at this moment I also noticed a fifth smaller spurious light off to the left hand side (Pic. l ).

 lake were silver lines running up and down the surface, they were like box sections raised off the

                            UFO NEWS FROM WEST BENGAL, INDIA

The PRA has recently been contacted by Debamalya Gupta, a colleague in West Bengal, India. A keen
UFO researcher and someone seeking new contacts worldwide.

He has related the following account of UFO sightings that he witnessed when he was a youngster:-

In my childhood back in the 80's, my parents and I had the rare opportunity to witness two UFO events
from our home in the countryside. We watched them from our home town of Santiniketan, West Bengal
(famous for its university and for the great Indian noble lauriet and economist Amartya Sen).

It was about 8-8.30 pm , I was preparing lessons with my home tutor when suddenly my mother, who
was sitting on the balcony, called for us to come out and see something unusual that was in the eastern
sky. We went out and were surprised to see a cigar shaped light, silver in colour, with bright yellowish
twinkling lights all over its outer sides. It was flying upwards into the sky, with a jet-like light
propelling it from the rear. We watched it for 5-10 minutes before it finally disappeared. It was not like
any satellite or launching rocket and we checked the morning newspaper the following day but there
was absolutely nothing mentioned.

My second experience was to see a bright star-like light, similar to a normal star, but it was bouncing up
and down all the time, like a particle in a simple harmonic motion as we read in physics. At the same
time as it was bouncing up and down, it was moving from north to south.

It was these two incidents that developed my keen interest in extraterrestrials and UFO's. ( I also became
a devoted reader of Erich Von Damien books).        Regards, Debamalya Gupta

                                           The Evening Sky
                                              July 2005
    The nights become slightly longer after the summer solstice but the increase in the hours of
    darkness only becomes perceptible towards the end of this month. The delicate sliver-blue
    noctilucent clouds are still visible over the northern horizon at this time and are well worth
    staying up to observe. You will need a clear sky and a dark observing area away from artificial
    light. Early in the month is good for observation, as there is no moon and thus no moonlight.

    The planet Venus is bright and visible until around 80 minutes after the sun has set. Mercury
    joins Venus early in July. The best time to spot Mercury is around the 9th. You will need an
    unobstructed view of the horizon, as Mercury is low in the north-eastern sky at evening twilight.
    Mercury, Venus and a two days old crescent moon form a mystical grouping early on the
    evening of the 8th.

    Jupiter is less favourable for viewing this month, setting by 11pm. A waxing crescent moon will
    be a couple of degrees south-east of Jupiter on the 13th. Saturn is lost from view but Mars is
    becoming more favourable and by mid-month, rises around midnight. The Earth is farthest from
    the Sun (aphelion) on the morning of July 5th. Full Moon occurs on the 24th.

    After the Sun sets, when does twilight end and night begin? Night never arrives at all at high
    northern latitudes, above seventy-five degrees, in July. The northern polar region is bathed in
    perpetual daylight. However, further south, twilight depends not only on your latitude but also on
    which kind of twilight you mean. 'Civil' twilight ends when the Sun is six degrees below the true
    horizon. Under a clear sky this is when most outdoor activities need artificial lighting. Nautical
    twilight ends when the Sun descends to twelve degrees below the horizon. At this point, a sea
    horizon ceases to be visible enough for a sailor to use a sextant to take navigational sightings.
    Astronomical twilight ends when the Sun descends to eighteen degrees below the horizon. This
    is when the last trace of the Sun's afterglow disappears. In early July the Sun's afterglow is
    present throughout the night. ,

    The stars on a dark summer night convey a sense of timelessness and we are caught up,
    feeling summer is endless, the peak of the year when little changes for weeks on end.
    Astronomically time moves slowly, our solar system is situated within the outer regions of a
    spiral arm about twenty light years above the galactic equatorial plane and 28 000 light years
    from the galactic centre. Viewed from Earth the luminous band of the centre of the Milky Way
    (the galactic equator) spans the constellation of Sagittarius and touches both of its neighbour
    constellations Scorpius and Ophiuchus.
