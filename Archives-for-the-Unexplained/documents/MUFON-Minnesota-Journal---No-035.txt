Editorial
Scientists are increasingly recognizing the reality and the importance of the UFO phenomenon. For
 example, there is a group of psychologists and psychiatrists formed primarily to study the UFO
abduction phenomenon, known as "TREAT" (Treatment and Research on Experienced Anomalous
 Trauma). Now we have what seems to me to be an important breakthrough - a Harvard
psychiatrist, Dr. John E. Mack, has recognized the seriousness of the so-called UFO abduction
phenomenon. I have therefore chosen to reproduce below the foreword he wrote for David Jacobs'
 book, "Secret Life". I hope it will inspire some of you to buy or at least to read the book.

 A second breakthrough may weD be the case in New York City that Budd Hopkins described at the
 1992 MUFON Symposium, in which the abduction of a woman by aliens was witnessed by several
 other people, one apparently, a high diplomatic officiaL A brief account of that case is also given
 below. If there is a group inside our government that is trying to keep a lid on the UFO issue (and
 there are indications that there is), they should be and must be getting worried.
