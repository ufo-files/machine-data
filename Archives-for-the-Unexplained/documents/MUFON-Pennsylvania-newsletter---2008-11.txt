October 25, 2008 saw PA MUFONS first Paranormal /
UFO Conference held at the Westmoreland Community
College. A day filled with interesting speakers and
topics ranging from After Death Experiences to UFO’s.

It was the last of PA MUFON programs for 2008 and our
Field Investigators and speakers met some great folks
and helped promote some understanding of the UFO
Phenomena.


The first scheduled UFO Conference for 2009 will be
held at the Bucks County Community College, January
24, 2009 from 12pm to 5pm. Speakers will include John
Ventre, PA MUFON State Director, William Birnes, from
UFO Magazine and the hit show UFO Hunters. Dr. David
M. Jacobs and Bucks County UFO personal eyewitness
testimony.


Please watch the Discovery Channel, November 24,
2008, at 9pm to see the show “UFOs over Earth” It
features the Bucks County, UFO Flap that took place
this past summer in Pennsylvania.

give us at least 30 days notice if you are planning a
program.
