            NA86-001     CE2   2 9 Jan 8 6     Orange NSW   2330 hrs

            Robert Wren (22), Sharon Kelly (18), Joanne (17) and Jeffrey Eyeles (17)
            observed an oval-shaped object, with a flat bottom and domed top, over a period
            of 15 minutes. Initially, Wren had gone outside to check on the horses which
            were "going crazy" and making a horrific noise in a paddock across the road.
            Then the object was seen "hovering" about 50m distant and 50-60m above the
            ground. The object was about the size of a car, had a bright red, flashing or
            revolving light on the bottom, and was surrounded by a ring of about 20 fixed
            red lights. A noise like that made by an "old grader11 was heard. The object
            appeared to approach and circle, dropped about 30m and then sped off in a
            southerly direction towards the local TV tower where it appeared to land in a
            nearby paddock. The witnesses jumped into a car and gave chase for a couple of
            minutes before losing sight of the object. The four, who were by now "really
            scared'', reported their sighting to the television station and police. The next
            day, Wren went to the area where he thought the object had landed and found a
            spot where the grass had been flattened 2m wide and 4m long. Samples of the
            ground trace were obtained by a local contact and sent to UFOR ( SW).         �
            (Investigator estimates 70% probability that the object was actually an air
             ambulance aircraft; the ground trace being unrelated and spurious. A check
             with the aircraft pilot revealed he had landed at one of the two local
             airports at 11.27pm. The airport is in the line of sight of the direction in
             which the object travelled. See also Sydney "Daily Mirror" and Melbourne
             "Herald" of 30 Jan; Sydney ''Daily Telegraph", Burnie "Advocate", Brisbane
             "Courier Mail", Perth "West Australian" and "Sydney Morning Herald" of 31 Jan.)

           TB86-002      Early Oct 85      Rosebery TAS     Night

           Two witnesses travelling home saw a circular arrangement of about six orange
           and white lights. After dropping off her passenger, the driver saw the lights
           again, approximately 50m from her home. The lights were stationary except that
           they appeared to revolve as a group.

           TB86-003      15 Dec 85      Rosebery TAS   2300 hrs

           Two boys, 10 and 12 years old, viewed a cluster of lights move erratically in
           the SW, apparently over a nearby pump station. The cluster consisted of one
           large light, which went "dull" when the movement of the cluster had stopped
           and brighter when it moved, and approximately six smaller lights arranged in a
           semi-circle underneath. When the lights were lost to view, banging noises were
           heard•on the roof. Both boys ran and hid under a mattress until their parents
           returned home. The older boy got sore eyes whilst watching the lights, felt
           ill the next day and had recurrent nightmares and talked in his sleep.
           (Peter Zehmeister, SWUFOR.)
