      As we all know, a lot of crazy stuff is coming at us these days re­

sible to prove or disprove. But an article in the Sept./Oct. 1990 issue of
garding secret underground alien bases and the like. Most of it is impos­

the International UFO Reporter, called "In the Land of Dreams ", contains
one topic that is such obvious bullshit to anyone with the slightest knowl­
edge of physics, that we decided to call your attention to it. The authors
of the article are not endorsing the story, merely recounting it: It seems
that Robert Lazar, one of the current crop of far-out ufological chroniclers,
claims that certain interplanetary saucers use for propulsion a    substance
which he calls Element 115.
      Unfortunately, the highest-numbered natural element known on Earth
is Uranium (92) , and the artificially-created elements with higher numbers,
up to 10 8 or 109, have extremely short half-lives, in the range of a few
seconds. They could hardly be used for anything practical, if they exist
in nature, nor could our Government have 500 pounds of Element 115, as La­
zar claims. If we assume that nature operates basically the same way
throughout the Universe, Lazar 's story is patently absurd, as physicist Dr.
Thornton Page has confirmed to us by telephone. Yet the authors of the
article negelct to call attention to this fact....

      Well, the Gulf Breeze controversy drones on, endlessly. At the bot­
tom of Page 8 of our last "Smear ", we mentioned three "burning issues " re­
garding the case. In regard to the first of these, Ed Walters unfortunate­
ly came in 9th in a field of 9, in his race for a seat on the City Commis­
sion of Gulf Breeze. He claims that local political issues, rather than
UFOs, caused his defeat, although he admits he was also hurt by negative
UFO publicity inspired by Rex Salisberry.
      In regard to the Salisberrys, they remain co-directors for MUFON of
a section of northern Florida that includes two counties, i.e., the county
that contains Pensacola and the adjoining one that contains Gulf Breeze.
However, an organization called "Pensacola MUFON " has held an election in
which a small group gathered without even inviting the Salisberrys,     and
elected a slate of officers (President, Vice-President, etc.) that rather
deliberately excludes the Salisberrys! A release from this MUFON     sub­
group explains that "during the past six months, many MUFON members have
become concerned over the deteriation in morale and member participation in
our local chapter. As a result of recent stories in the Pensacola     News­
Journal, it has become obvious that actions by Rex and Carol Salisberry
have destroyed any hope for effective leadership from them. "
      Translation: The Salisberrys, after their reinvestigation of the Ed
Walters case, ceased to believe in it, and said so publicly, including in
the News-Journal (strangely, just before the election.) Thus they no long­
er follow the MUFON Party Line, and have become Non-People. No doubt they
will be pressured to resign as Section Directors for their two counties,
but they have not yet been fired.
      In regard to the Walters case, the Salisberrys believe they have prov­
en that Ed's Photo #19 (the famous "road shot ") is a fake. Bruce Maccabee
then reanalyzed #19, came up with new figures, and re-satisfied himself
that the photo is real. Regarding the UFO model found awhile back in Mr.
Ed's former residence, the Salisberrys apparently
accept Phil Klass's analysis, which proves at least
to his own satisfaction that the model was made by
Ed himself, whereas Ed continues to deny that he
made it.
