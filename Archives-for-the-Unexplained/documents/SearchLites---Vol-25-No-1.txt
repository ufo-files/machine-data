In Memoriam
It is with sadness that we report the passing of Patricia Marga-
ret Sterns, a longtime leader in space law. For decades she
provided guidance to the international community of scientists
engaged in the Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence (SETI),
as it contemplated the legal issues related to the detection of
life beyond Earth. Ms. Sterns is remembered by friends and
colleagues for her keen intellect and generous spirit as she
grappled with challenging issues at the intersection of space
exploration and law. For nearly forty years, she was a practic-
ing attorney in Phoenix, Arizona. For two decades during this
period, Ms. Sterns also served as judge pro tempore for the
Superior Court of Arizona, County of Maricopa. She was a
member of the International Academy of Astronautics (IAA),
as well as the IAA SETI Committee. She served on the Board
of Directors of the International Institute of Space Law, and
her many honors included the IAA's Social Science Book
Award. She was born on January 30, 1952 and passed away on
May 6, 2018. She is survived by her husband and law partner,
Leslie I. Tennen.
