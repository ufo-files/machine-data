RgSULTS:
NOTE: The session was interrupted very shortly after it began,
and there were no opportunities to re-start that evening or the

stage of operation, and should be taken as beginning impressions,
only.
1. There will be no definite acceptance of the Russian proposal,
but there will be a lot of haggling over points within it.
2. There was a perception of a small motorized craft near shore
being fired on by rifle fire. When someone from the boat returns
the rifle fire, one of the land-based troops surrenders to the
boat. His surrender begins an avalanche of ground troups
surrendering to the small craft. Several hundred stand on shore,
surrendering to the craft, and what appear to be landing craft
have to be called in to transport them all.
     There was a further impression about this event: that the
ship which accepts the troups is in very grave danger of being
attacked from land forces approximately 23 miles inland, in a
north by slightly northwesterly direction (AOL: approximately 17
degrees west of due north). If no protection is provided, the
ship could be sunk, losing all aboard as well as the Iraqi ground
troups who surrender. This impression, however, was interrupted
by a phone call and not further pursued.
