   FORTY YEARS IN UFOOLOGY!
   MOSELEY BECOMES A SELF-APPOINTED "ELDER STATESMAN"!
         Never one to be burdened by false modesty, your editor joyfully re-
   minds you that the Saucer and Unexplained Celestial Events Research Society
   (S.A.U.C.E.R.S.), of which he is president, was founded back in July     of
   1954. At that time our official publication was called "Nexus", and your
   editor then worked with co-founders August C. Roberts and the late Dominick
   Lucchesi.
         Unfortunately, S.A.U.C.E.R.S. is and always has been a somewhat myth­
   ical organization, as your editor has long been the only active member.
   Nevertheless, S.A.U.C.E.R.S. is now unquestionably the oldest UFO research
   group in the United States, anQ possibly the world. (There may also be old­
   er groups on other planets, but we have no way of knowing for sure.)
         In 1955 the name "Nexus" was changed to SAUCER NEWS, and we continued
   publishing a large quarterly zine under that title until 1968, when the mag­
   azine was sold to the late Gray Barker. For a brief time around 1967, cir­
   culation peaked out at close to 10,000!
         Barker continued SAUCER NEWS on his own only until 1970. At      that
   time SAUCER NEWS died, but S.A.U.C.E.R.S. did not. Your editor, far from
   being "hushed up", continued lecturing on UFOs to colleges all around the
   country (till pushed off the circuit by Stanton Friedman), and engaged in
   other ufological activities.
         Then, in 1976, we wanted to make a "comeback", but in our own unique
   way, which is to write mainly to please ourselves. We decided to publish
   a small newsletter that would ignore sightings almost entirely and concen­
   trate on gossip & opinions within the UFO field, letting the chips      fall
   where they may. We also decided to make the newsletter free to all, but
   available only to seriously interested parties. This was in order to avoid
   bookkeeping and for several other reasons.
         Starting out under the name SAUCER NEWS in February of 1976, the news­
   letter changed titles with almost every issue for the first few years, grad­
   ually evolving to the 8-page format it now has. In July of 1981, following
   a suggestion by Erik Beckjord, we decided on "Saucer Sme<;ir" as the permanent
   title, as it best expresses our rather sarcastic approach to the whole sub­
   ject.

       During these forty years we have had a lot of fun. We have    been
threatened with lawsuits several times, most recently by the Amazing Randi,
but we have never been sued (yet). In the early days, we engaged in a few
hoaxes with the late Gray Barker, most notably the notorious "Straith Let­
ter" sent to George Adamski in 1957. We have gone off on a few regrettable
tangents, and we have embraced a long series of different ufological theo­
ries - including the "Earth Theory", the interplanetary theory, the (relat­
ed) little-men-from-Mars theory, and finally the 4-D theory - which we face­
tiously modified in 1976 to something called the 3�-D Theory - a compromise,
you see, between 3-D and 4-D.
       Amid all the fun, we do have a serious purpose which many people miss.
It is to make people think in new ways, and to question their unproven as­
sumptions, whatever they may be. We have, in turn, attacked Believers and
Debunkers alike. In recent years we have come up with a number of "exclus­
ives", notably our story on the incarceration of Col. Wendelle Stevens
(which everyone knew about but no one else dared to print), our recent ser­
ies on James Randi, and our series awhile back on Dr. Donald Menzel - show­
ing, to our own satisfaction at least, that he could not possibly have been
a member of the supposed MJ-12 group.
       Are we unbiased? Hardly! But by continually publishing letters which
oppose our views, and which oppose each other, we have created a forum which
is not found elsewhere. We have entertained, and occasionally even enlight­
ened.
       Over the years we have seen the "middle" of ufological belief shift
ever further into left field. Stories that would have been considered too
far out for belief by ufologically conservative minds, are now accepted rou­
tinely by most of the leaders in the field. Anything goes, no matter how
absurd and unlikely. We comment on all this, sometimes with laughter, some­
times with anger, and occasionally even with sorrow.
       You may have noticed that we do not go to the supermarket tabloids
for material, as it would be a "cheap shot", i.e., few ufologists take this
stuff seriously anyhow, knowing it is largely made up or grossly exaggerat­
ed simply for the sake of entertainment. After all, space people never sue!
Another limitation is that we do not try to cover the overseas scene at all,
as there is just too much of it. We try to stick mainly to the activities
and opinions of the current crop of ufological leaders in the U.S. at any
given time.
      Just now, as this issue goes to press, we are distressed to find that
UFO guru Jerry Clark, whom we greatly admire as ufology's leading historian,
is engaged in a series of highly emotional, non-productive feuds with a num­
ber of people, including CSICOP members Phil Klass and Dr. Gary Posner, plus
Dr. Willy Smith, George Hansen, and probably others. The tirades back and
forth are contained in semi-private "pass-arounds", which are neither pub­
lications nor private letters. How professionally-trained people can spend
so much time and energy in such childish endeavors is a mystery we cannot
solve. At this point we are not stating who is right or wrong; in fact, the
issues are so complex that we fear we might bore our readers to tears by
trying to explain them in detail. We are simply stating that a number of
leaders of ufology, including some of the above-mentioned gentlemen as well
as many others, are behaving so irrationally that even "Smear" finds their
points of view on these specific issues unworthy of comment!
       This is not to say we don't appreciate being on the "pass-around"
lists. It's just that we try to keep what we print in this magazine on a
reasonably high plane. (Pause for laughter.)
       Finally, what Great Truths have we actually learned after all these
years, you might ask? The answer is: none. Our current view is that UFOs
are just one of a vast spectrum of so-called Fortean events which seem un­
solvable, but which should convince us that this Universe is a much more
complex place than most people have ever imagined. One reason ufologists
will probably never solve the UFO mystery is because it is interrelated with
