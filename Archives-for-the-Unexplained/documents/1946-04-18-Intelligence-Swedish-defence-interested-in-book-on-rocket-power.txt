April 18: Defence Staff interested in book on rocket power

On April 18, Captain Bertil Hedberg, who worked for the Swedish military attaché in Washington, sent the Defence Staff in Stockholm a copy of one of the latest American books, titled ”The Coming Age of Rocket Power”. 

Source: Letter in the files of the Defence Attachés.
