                                                UFO Hoaxes
  Documents describing UFO hoaxes and fraudulant claims. The position of this page is negative. All primary links
  should claim that a UFO report is hoaxed. Reply links can then be added responding to the criticism. Extensive
  discussions of a single case should be referred to the page for that case or the person who made the original UFO claims.

  A hoax is not an error or misperception but a deliberate attempt to deceive. To be listed on this page, there must be some
  evidence of intent.

    See UFO Skeptics for people and groups that are actively skeptical of UFO claims. See Felons & Felonies for public
    crimes involving UFO claims.
