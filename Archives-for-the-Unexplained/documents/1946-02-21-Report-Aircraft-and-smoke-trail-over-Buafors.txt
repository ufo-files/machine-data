Case XXX.
Aircraft and smoke-trail over Buafors

Time: February 21, noon (12.00-13.00)
Place: Buafors, Kroksjö and Ultervattnet, Västerbotten

”Villagers of Buafors...Thursday noon observed a strange light phenomenon in the sky. They heard engine noise like from an airplane, but there was no such in sight. On the other hand, they saw a smoke-trail, which seemed to part the clouds. This was observed for about 30 minutes and the trail among the clouds became wider and wider. The phenomen came from the southeast.” (Västerbottens-Kuriren, February 22)

”We had a phone call from Kroksjö...where on Thursday, about 13.00 hours, an airplane was sighted over the village in the east, going in a westerly direction. The plane was not discernable but the wingtips could be seen and there was a continous sound. Over the village the plane released a long, red contrail behind. This is stated in view of the information given by Friday´s Radio Echo [a regular Swedish radio news program] where not only the sightings of Thursday evening [the meteor] were mentioned, but also incidents before noon [on Thursday]. (Västerbotten, February 23)

”At Ultervattnet, Mr Albert Ekman makes it clear that it was an airplane that passed over the neighbourhood Thursday morning, nothing else. (Västerbottens-Kuriren, February 23)

”Six workers at a sawmill at Vebomark, Lövånger heard, at about 12.00 hours a sound like from an airplane, but none could be seen. Instead there was a broad streak of light across the sky.” (Västerbottens-Kuriren, and other newspapers [TT newswire], February 23) 

Status: Probable aircraft and contrail
Source: Västerbottens-Kuriren, February 22.
