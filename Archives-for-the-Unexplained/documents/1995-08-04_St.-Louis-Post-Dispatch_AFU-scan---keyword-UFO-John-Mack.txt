 Harvard Warns UFO Researcher

to censure a psychiatry professor who stud
ies people who claim space aliens abducted
them for sexual experiments.
   But Dr. John Mack received an unusual
public warning from the dean not to let his
enthusiasm for UFO research steer him
from the path of professionalism. The deci­
sion disclosed Thursday in Cambridge fol­
lowed a one-year investigation that Mack's
attorney called a challenge to academic
freedom.
   The review began after Mack appeared
on "Unsolved Mysteries" and other TV
programs to promote his 1994 book "Ab­
duction: Human Encounters With Aliens."        ·
