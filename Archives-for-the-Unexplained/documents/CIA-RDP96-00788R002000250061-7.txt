 MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD                                            21 July 1980

 SUBJECT:   Grill Flame Briefing for BG Carl Smith, Office of Secretary
            of Defense



 1. Per direction of OACSI, at 1430 hrs, 18 July 1980, I presented a 45
 minute briefing to the following:
            BG Carl Smith, Special Assistant to Sec. of Defense
            Dr. Varona, DIA, S & T
            LTC Sasai, DACSI
 2. The purpose of the briefing was to give BG Smith an in-depth understanding
 of the INSCOM Grill Flame Program. See incl 1 for outline of the briefing.
 3. BG Smith asked several questions concerning the operational methods we
 used but he always appeared satisfied with my responses.
 4. Since the briefing was intended to be informational in nature, I would
 say that the purpose of the briefing had been accomplished satisfactorily.
