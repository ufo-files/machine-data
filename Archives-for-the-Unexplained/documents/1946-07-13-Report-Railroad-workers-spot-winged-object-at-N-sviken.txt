Case xxx: 
Railroad workers spot winged object

Time: July 13, 10.30 hours
Place: 5 km from Näsviken (BP52) 
          
TT telegram: "A company of railroad workers, five km from the Näsviken railroad station, spotted a flying object at an altitude of 150 metres, at 10.30 hours on the Saturday. The object had a sound like that from an outboard motor on high speed (high number of revolutions). The object was a couple of meters long and had wings swept backwards. It travelled in a northerly direction."

Note: The case was never noted on the daily Defence Staff tabulation of sighting reports, neither is there any detail of any military investigation. Since the case was widely published and should have been noted by the Staff, this may indicate that some explanation was found.

Status: Insufficient information. 
Sources: TT telegram nr 74 of July 13. Göteborgs-Tidningen and Stockholms-Tidningen, July 14. ScanCat 1280.
