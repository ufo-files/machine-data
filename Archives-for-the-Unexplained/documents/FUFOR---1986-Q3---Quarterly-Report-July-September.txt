The FUND will be hosting the 1987 International MUFON Symposium
in Washington, DC, June 26-28, 1987.  The FUND wishes to make
this truly international by inviting speakers from about a dozen
different countries.  In September, letters of invitation went
out to ufologists in the USA, Soviet Union, England, Canada,Australia,
Spain, Africa, Sweden, Denmark, Italy, India, Japan, France,
and to a ufologist who could represent--but does not live in--
China.  The FUND had indicated that it will pay expenses during
the time that the speakers are in Washington, DC.  However, the
travel expenses are major "cost drivers" in determining whether
or not this international array of ufologists can attend.   The
FUND is therefore beginning a special "travel fund" to be u-sed
for symposium travel expenses.  We hope to raise at least $20,000
for this worthy goal.  Large contributions are certainly welcome,
but we note that if each recipient of this newsletter would­
contribute as little as $15.00, we would have enough money to
pay the travel expenses of all the speakers.

As of this writing, ufologists from England, Italy, France, Canada,
and India--and representatives of China and the u.s.--have accepted
our invitation to speak at the 1987 Symposium�  We will announce
in the next Quarterly Report the names of those who have�agreed
to appear.

Last Spring, the FUND announced the impending publication of
a book by Dr. Richard Haines.   The book contains the most complete
analysis yet done of the disappearance of pilot Frederick Valentich
off the coast of Australia.   At that time, publication was, indeed,
"impending."   However, the printer delayed and delayed for his
own reasons.   Finally, last August, the printer defaulted on
his commitment, even after going so far as to print up his own
publication announcements.   Needless to say, this has left Dr.
Haines and others--including the FUND--in the position of having
collected money for book orders.    (Recently, we were informed
that Dr. Haines has found a new printer and that the book will
be published.)   Since we cannot yet deliver the book, we offer
to those who sent us orders   the opportunity of asking for the
return of their money.   On the other hand, it will become available
eventually, and we will honor the orders when we obtain copies
of the book.   If we do not receive any nofication to the contrary,
we will assume that the money is to be retained and the book
will be shipped when it becomes available.
