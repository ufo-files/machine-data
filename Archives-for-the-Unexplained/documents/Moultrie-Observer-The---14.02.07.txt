The Moultrie Observer - Georgia, USA…  14th February 2007… 

On Egyptian Mysteries And UFOs…
Dwain Walden…

   A few days ago, there was a report of a UFO out near the county line. Now I have never seen an unidentified flying object in the conventional sense although there were a couple of times in high school when something would come sailing through the air in the lunch room that was not specifically defined other than being some form of protein.
   An unidentified flying object does not automatically imply a spaceship from another galaxy. It could be totally of this earth but not something typically seen by many people - like an honest politician on a trampoline.
   No one ever has a clear photo of a UFO. Paris Hilton can wear a short skirt with no underwear and there will be ten thousand shots of her on the web within the hour. Yet, an alleged UFO can be reported hovering along a major highway and no one has a camera. Never, ever.
   A recent Discovery channel documentary explored the mysteries of ancient Egypt. And drawn on the inside walls of some of the tombs and pyramids are designs that look like helicopters and submarines. One drawing resembles two spaceships fighting.
   The prevailing question of that documentary was: In those very early days in Egypt, was earth visited by extraterrestrials? And I suppose by extension, given reported UFOs sightings in modern times, do they come back occasionally still hoping to find intelligent life, or do they just find Paris Hiltons?
   Of course time has its way of diminishing etchings so what might appear to be a helicopter thousands of years later might actually have been the first blueprint for an egg beater.
   On the "Rants and Raves" line the other day, a fellow said he thought he had seen a UFO, but on closer inspection the humming noise was an air conditioner compressor that needed oiling and the brightly colored lights were Christmas decorations that were still up. I'm pretty sure this fellow was just expressing his cynicism.
   I have wondered about there being life out there in other galaxies. Many of my fundamentalist friends would tell me that I'm way off base because this is not mentioned in the Bible. Well, there also is no one named Larry mentioned in the Bible but you can find Larrys today in every town in the country.
   I also quote from a secular source, actor Paul Newman, who told his pool shooting student Tom Cruise, "Son, I taught you everything you know, but I didn't teach you everything I know."  (At least I think I have the right movie there.) And sometimes when I read the Bible, I think of God saying that to some of us who think we are so smart. "People, I didn't tell you everything."
   In the big picture, I can perceive that we are just one tiny dot in a magnificent display of planets, stars, meteors and black holes. With that realization, I don't bother to watch the "Miss  Universe Pageant" nor do I care who is the father of Anna Nicole Smith's baby. I do wonder what Donald Trump's hair would like if he stuck his finger in a light socket.
   I've always posed that man likes to romance the notion that there are creatures out there in the dark bigger than us, faster than us, smarter than us and scarier than us. So it's not uncommon that Bigfoots, Loch Ness Monsters, UFOs get imagined.
   I'm not saying that people aren't seeing something - things that can't be quickly explained. But there have been so many hoaxes revealed in this venue that skepticism and cynicism will be the predominant reactions from most logical thinkers. But my fear is that logical thinkers are fast becoming extinct - that we are predominated by people anxiously awaiting news on the identity of the father of Ms. Smith's baby. Surely someone will pose that it is an alien. (Dwain Walden is editor/publisher of The Moultrie Observer, 985-4545. E-mail:  HYPERLINK "mailto:dwain.walden@gaflnews.com" dwain.walden@gaflnews.com)
