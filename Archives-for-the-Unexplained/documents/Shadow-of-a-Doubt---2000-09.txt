Author Carol Diament discusses her forthcoming book, In the Counsel of the Wicked,
a true personal story about the dark side of false memory recovery therapy.
In 1989 I was unhappily married to a wildly successful contractor. I had everything money could buy but
could no longer tolerate my husband's other love affair -- drinking. Desperate to keep our family intact, I
trusted the two women therapists with whom we began treatment because they were licensed, credentialed,
and appeared to be well regarded in the community. Unwittingly, we fell into a web of deceit -- a
psychotherapy cult.

During the first year, therapy was carefully calculated to bring positive results. At the beginning of the second
year they introduced us to The Network, their corps of loyal followers. I cut off contact with my parents, three
sisters and many old friends, all of whom had been deemed toxic and not to be trusted. By the third year our
two children began treatment. During the fourth year, believing I was healed enough to resume a normal life,
I expressed a desire to terminate my therapy. Not only did the therapists respond that I was far from finished,
they began a campaign to undermine my mental health.

I was expelled from the Network for lack of commitment and simultaneously ordered to leave my home, my
husband and my children. Shattered and suicidal, I committed myself to a psychiatric hospital 2000 miles
from home. For the next two years I inched my way back to sanity with the help of legitimate mental health
practitioners. Beginning in 1996 I battled in court to liberate my children. The cult therapists had stolen them
from me -- now a bitter female judge conspired to keep them away. Ignoring Supreme Court law, she
ultimately suspended my visitation with them without any reason and railroaded the case to dead end.

Disheartened but not defeated, I hope that someday my children will seek to unlock the mystery of how our
family was torn apart. Perhaps this book will provide the key. Anyone, anywhere can be the victim of gross
manipulation if they are in the wrong place at the wrong time.
