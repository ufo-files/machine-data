DAILY MAIL
27 AUGUST 1981. K.S.
FORGET the aliens' press conference.
Those documents that surfaced purport-
ing to be evidence that the Pentagon had
recovered four bodies from a crashed
flying saucer in 1947 have now
denounced as clumsy counterfers. The
Committee For the Scientific Investi-
gation of Claims of the Paranormal says
the documents - which said that Presi-
dent Truman had set up a secret unit
called Majestic 12 to study the aliens -
represent 'one of the most deliberate acts
of deception every perpetrated.'
