        EXPLOSION ROCKS MONTREAL, POLICE SAY WAS METEORITE
           MONTREAL JUNE 13 (REUTER) - AN EXPLOSION THAT WAS LIKELY CAUSED
       BY A METEORITE SHOOK THE MONTtlEAL AREA TUESDAY NIGHT BUT APPARENTLY
       CAUSED NO INJURIES POLICE SAID.
           ''A NUMBER OP PEOPLE HAVE CALLED TO TELL US ABOUT AN EXPLOSION
       THAT WAS FELT ON THE SOUTH SHORE (ACROSS THE ST LAWRENCE RIVER FROM
       MONTREAL),'' A MONTREAL POLICE SPOKESMAN SAID. ''WE THINK IT WAS A
       METEORITE THAT IN ALL PROBABILITY LANDED IN A FIELD.''

       EXPLOSION THAT OCCURRED ABOUT 8:30 P.M. EDT {0030 GMT WEDNESDAY) MAY
       HAVE BEEN A METEORITE, BUT WAS DEFINITELY NO't AN EARTHQUAl<E, THE

       ITS EPICENTRE NEAR STE-JULIE EAST OF MONTREAL.
