                                      reetings to all my Earthling and Alien friends!

                                      April has been quite an eventful month. The weekend after
                                      Easter, team RACE embarked on a 3 day expedition into
                           the wilderness, mountainous terrain. This is an area where RACE has
                           conducted previous investigative night watches after receiving reports
                           of strange aerial phenomenon. On our 3 previous investigations we
                           have witnessed, experienced and documented (with video and photos)
                           unexplained “Goings on” (for more information go to
                           www.ufosociety.com.au ) Once again we were not disappointed, but
                           unfortunately nothing was captured on film.

                           The UFO topic and UFO-PRSA dominated the media during April
                           with the release of Top Secret Classified Military Files by the UK
                           Government. Channel 7’s Sunday Night Show aired an interview with
                           Nick Pope (UK UFO Researcher/ Author) and amongst the video
Page 6                     footage presented was our very own famous footage shot by Laszlo
                           Novak. This was repeated on The Morning Show and later in the week,
                           our last year’s interview by Sonia Kruger was also aired. We do our
                           best to use the media to get information out into the public arena.

                           Kate Murphy (Artist/Film Maker) did a great job doing this by
                           compiling a documentary featuring myself and 4 members of our
                           society, entitled “Looking Up”. It was shown at the Campbelltown Arts
                           Centre during Heritage Week and was viewed by 100 plus people. This
Page 8                     event was advertised in the local newspaper, The MacArthur
                           Chronicle, with a front page spread and caused a surprisingly large
                           response from the general public. I have been inundated by sighting
                           reports from the past and present, many with photos and video footage

                           will be the complete 18 episodes, 10 from 2008 and the new 8 part
                           series for 2009. If they continue to prove to be popular, there may be
                           future episodes.
