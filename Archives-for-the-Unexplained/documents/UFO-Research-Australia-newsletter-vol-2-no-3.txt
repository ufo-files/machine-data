     UFO Research Australia Newsletter {UFORAN} fulfils two functions, one

independent, the other eo-operational.

     Independently, the magazine presents the average reader with a broad cross­

section of UFO research activities.

     UFORAN is written by active objective researchers for serious minded people

who share a genuine curiosity in the subject.

     Ours is not a commercial magazine, there is no reason for us to sensationalise

events and very much against our principles to do so.

     UFORAN is not a closed publication,    our readers are invited to express their

views on anything relating to UFOs in our FORUM section.

     eo-operationally,   UFORAN interchanges information with the Australian Centre

for UFO studies.   The Centre has its own specialised publication - The ACUFOS Journal,

which is technical in content and written by UFO researchers specifically for UFO

researchers.

     UFORAN and the ACUFOS Journal are complimentary publications covering every

aspect of present day research.

     UFO Research Australia Newsletter and the ACUFOS Journal are objective magazines.

     Research often reveals the spectacular sighting to be misperception of a

mundane object, but surely this is better to know than not, for there are sightings,

events and evidence that cannot be explained.     This is the true mystery to explore.

Unfortunately some pieces of the puzzle lay hidden amongst the beliefs and

prejudices of those determined to see extraterrestrial visitations in every report.

These people generate sufficient confusion to further conceal the pieces, but

consistent rational research is gradually unearthing them.

     For every argument there is a counter argument and we are presenting these

to you in an unbiased manner.     There are numerous theories of what unexplained UFOs

are not   and remarkably few intelligent theories of what they could be, we are also

exploring this rather neglected approach to the subject.

     UFO investigation has come a long way in recent years.     Researchers of member

organisations of the Australian Centre for UFO Studies co-operate and unselfishly

exchange information and have the knowledge and experience to objectively assess

UFO events.

     This high level of co-operation is evident each year at the UFO Conferences

held in the various states of Australia.    It is here that researchers present and

discuss the results and conclusions of their investigations during the past year.

The general public are welcome to attend and observe Australian UFO research at work·

The 1981 UFO Conference will be held in Adelaide, South Australia, October 10-12.

Further information will be published at a later date in UFORAN-1

     The following event occurred on December 3,    1980,   but was not fully reported to
UFOR(FNQ) until March this year.

     Only one of the two witnesses involved agreed to complete a detailed report of
his experience, the other percipient who was the owner-driver of the vehicle involved,
wishes anonymity.

     Prior to the report being made,   the investigator was acquainted with the reporter
who is 18 years of age.

     lt is not UFOR(FNQ)   policy to publish   witnesses names, we shall therefore call
our reporter Gavin.

THE   I NCIDENT


     The close encounter occurred on the main road through the Murray Upper district
20km south-west of Tully.

     The two witnesses were travelling north along this road in a late model Valiant.
The surrounding area is rural and relatively isolated, mostly open paddocks upon which
horses and cattle graze.

      At approximately 7 pm the witnesses drove across the Murray River floodway. At
this point Gavin (in the front passenger seat) noticed a "strange light" through the
o pen car window.  lt was in the sky to the left of, and behind the vehicle.

     The witnesses continued to travel north,  the "light" became brighter and approached
closer to the car. Gavin began to steer (from the passenger side) so the driver could
observe the object through the open passenger window. Whilst travelling in this manner
the car's engine stopped completely, as did the radio. The car lights began to "flicker,"
after approximately six seconds the radio began to emit a combined "static and beeping"
noise. With the car completely immobilised neither Gavin nor the driver were "game
enough to get out of the car."

     The object was now "hovering" above and "the car and its contents were vibrating"
whilst the object emitted "a high pitched drumming sound." Gavin estimated that the
object was above the car for 20 minutes or more, during which time he and the other
witness "just sat there and looked at it!"

     The object then moved away (without turning) at a steady speed to the left of
the vehicle (passenger side) and stopped 750 to 800 metres away.  lt momentarily

north-east.

     Once the UFO had gone the witnesses left the car, they noticed an absence of
"insect noise" and could not see or hear any animals nearby, which they considered
unusual.  Examining the car's engine the witnesses found no appareht damage and managed
to start the car "first pop."

      The witnesses continued to their intended destination near lnnisfail.
