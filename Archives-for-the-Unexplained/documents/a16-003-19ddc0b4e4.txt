           News stories sent to mailing list must include URL
     From: campbell@ufomind.com (Glenn Campbell, Las Vegas)
     Date: Wed, 16 Jul 1997 09:43:07 -0800

  Whenever you post a copyrighted news article to the Area 51 or Ufomind
  mailing lists, be sure to include the URL of the place where you found
  it. (You can still include the text, too, but be sure the URL appears
  at the top.) This includes articles from the Associated Press, Reuters
  and any local newspaper.
  If you do not include a URL, I might not be able to post it to the
  list. However, if you cannot find a URL, feel free to send me the
  article anyway (at webmaster@ufomind.com), and I will decide what to
  do with it.
  Also remember that an article must have some kind of connection to
  Area 51 to be included in the Area 51 mailing list (consistant with
  the charter at http://www.ufomind.com/area51/list/info.shtml).
  Otherwise, it should be sent to the Ufomind Mailing List (where the
  only criteria is the webmaster's interest - See
  http://www.ufomind.com/misc/info.shtml).
  Submissions to the Area 51 list go to: area51@lists.best.com
  Archived at: www.ufomind.com/area51/list/
  Submissions to the Ufomind list go to: ufomind@lists.best.com
  Archived at: www.ufomind.com/misc/

  COPYRIGHT PHILOSOPHY....
  Copyright is bound to be a problem on the Ufomind server eventually.
  Since we preserve all mailing list postings indefinitely, it is only a
  matter of time before someone complains. A few articles shouldn't be
  a problem, but when the number runs into the 100s, we become
  vulnerable.
  It may be a copyright violation to store a news article on our server,
  but it is not a violation to link to it on another server that is
  authorized to carry it. Therefore, we want to link to the article on
  an outside server whenever possible.
  By providing the URL, you are giving the webmaster (me) more options
  for dealing with the article.
