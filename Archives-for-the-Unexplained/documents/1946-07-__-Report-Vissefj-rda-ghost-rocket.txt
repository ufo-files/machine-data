Case xxx.
A close encounter with a ”cigar”?

Time: July 1946’
Place: Muggetorp near Vissefjärda in the county of Kalmar (province of Småland)

The following, anonymous story was received by the evening newspaper Aftonbladet in 1989 after it had made a call to their readers for UFO reports: 

”Time: July 1946. Place: Muggetorp, Småland. This ”cigar” I saw at a distance of about 30 meters. At a speed of about 40 km/h it flew through an avenue [of trees]. It was completely red with whiteglistening strips along a window, which was grey-white. No wings, no propellers, no signs, nothing. A weak, sighing [Sw. susande] sound was heard all the time, and inside ”the cigar” a dark shadow was moving. Name: Unknown.”

Status: Insufficient information
Source: Reader´s letter to Aftonbladet, published March 19, 1989.
