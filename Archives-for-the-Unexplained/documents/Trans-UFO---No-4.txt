In Sodermanland by lake Yngaren, lies the little village of Bjorkvik. This is a
naturally beautiful place with about 1, 500 inhabitants belonging to the Katrineholm's
commune. I was invited to give a talk on the 11th October 1994, about ufo research,
arrangements were made by the Vuxen school study society in Bjorkvik. A total of
30 interested persons met us at Vuxen school. It turned out to be more of a hearing
about ufo observations in Bjorkvik or surrounding areas. There was much interest and
questioning and the talk resulted later in a study circle at the school.

    The 27th January 94, 2 sisters (who prefer to remain anonymous) saw a triangular

    in their car and caught sight of the object which swayed at tree-top height. One of
    the sisters clearly saw underneath the object a bright light source in the middle
    of the triangle. It travelled with the point at the back.
