and Greet” on May 22, 2010 in Shillington PA the purpose of
which was to have members meet each other in person and
discuss ongoing cases as well as new cases.

A new piece of equipment was demonstrated by, Asst.
Director, John Bainbridge. A generation 4 night vision scope.

as a new addition to his rather large collection of cryptid
footprints, etc.

          Wem Town Hall, Shropeshire England
                  Date: 11.19.95

The town hall was burning to the ground when a man across
  from the fire scene took the above photo. It showed the

   A man in England made a recent debunking attempt. He
 stated that the photo of the girl was photo enhanced from a
    1938 post card. The card was analyzed and showed the
  dress was common of the period and two other women in
  the postcard picture had the same style of dress. Also the
face on the Wem photo did not match the post card as facial
   features were not the same and the head is at a different
   angle and the hairstyle was not the same. The photo
 remains as one of the most Famous Ghost Photos ever
                           taken.

  A note…24 attempts have been made in years past to
             debunk this photo. All failed.
