        The problem I see with the psychospy material I have read so far
is that it fails to see that more common than "traditional" suspicion-
fear paranoia is the refusal to think uncomfortable thoughts. 
        
 True enough, the paranoid over-compensates by making a virtue of
thinking terrifying, disturbing thoughts.  But, the majority makes the
opposite mistake!  Both mistakes lead to a disconnection from reality! 
The more common error of the majority is the characteristic of the
majority which makes it subject to being ruled by an exploitive elite! 
        
 It seems to me that the balanced mind would try to think without
being influenced by the emotions elicited by the thoughts entertained and
realize that all thoughts about external reality are hypotheses or
theories subject to further evidence and analysis!  Certainty is not
possible to the human being.
