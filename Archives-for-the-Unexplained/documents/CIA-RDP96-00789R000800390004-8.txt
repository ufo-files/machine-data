water.  The structure is something like a warehouse.    There
is a greenish glow and a very bad smell in this area.    The
smell makes me queasy.   There are long, tall, verticals,
and many rifles.   The contraband seems to be outside of
this structure or leaning up against it.   It has a dry
taste.   I do not think it is cocaine.
