SUBJECT: Results of SRV sessions for week of 31 August - 4
September, 1987.

1.  TARGET:  The target was to describe the picture on the cover
of the 14 September 87 issue of Newsweek Magazine.

2.  SESSIONS:  Results presented here contain material gained
from all sessions.

3.  RESULTS:  There is a strong impression that the cover picture
will consist of "parts" placed together in a way that they are
not normally found.  The cover will be in color, but there is the
impres There is a definite and recurring feeling that the White
House <or some columned building which looks like it and serves
the same purpose) may be one of the integral "parts" of the
picture.  There are also recurent feelings of Arab influence, and
planes in the sky.
