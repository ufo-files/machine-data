1.   (            The target was the HAMPTON ROADS TUNNEL AND
CHESAPEAKE BAY BRIDGE, consisting of a combination of trestles and
tunnels stretching across a distance of 17.6 miles gapping the
mouth of the Chesapeake Bay.

2.                Source's descriptive included circular objects
with points, a flying object (probably an airplane), triangles
which move and expand and are controlled or related to the movement
of water, open spacious area, flat ground further described as hard
and dry, semi-circles (domes?) which emit flames and heat, other
manmade devices which can cause bodily harm and are signified by
explosive sounds and a symbolic eagle or other large bird.  Other
fleeting visuals included cloth hats worn by men and a possible
reference to a large flag or other similar waving cloth.

3.                After a review of the descriptives reported by
Source it must be concluded that the data provided does not
correlate with the known major gestalts of the target.  There was
no reference to large bodies of water, superstructures, tunnels or
roads.  The information provided was more in keeping with expected
data found in more arid regions, perhaps even a desert region.   The
one item that may have been directly related to the actual target
was the reference to the symbolic eagle or other large bird.   The
target logo is a large flying tern or gull on a blue background and
is found on road signs and guideposts leading to the target area.
