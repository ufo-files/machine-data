                                      AUSTRALIAN STATISTICS FOR 1976
Details are to hand from the Australian Co-Ordination Section (ACOS) for the Centre
for UFO Studies of Australian sighting statistics.
The 22.5% unidentified compares with the Air Force's figures of 10.26% for 1976, if it
is remembered that the Air Force mostly receives Nocturnal light cases, which have a
higher identification rate than the more representative sample reported to the groups.
The majority of reports were Nocturnal lights and came from males in the age group of
31-40 year.old, compared to 1975 when the majority came from males also, but in the 16-
20 year old bracket�
The greatest number of sightings were made between the hours of 6.00pm and midnight
(same as 1975), but on Saturday nights, compared with Friday in 1975.                 The least number
of reports were made from 6.00pm to midnight on Monday and midnight to noon on Tuesday.
Monthly the highest activity seems to have occured in October, February and September,
compared to 1975 when highest activity was in March, May and August.                 Other points of
interest, single witness casses accounted for only 34.3% of the unidentifieds, whilst
45.95% of sightings were received by groups through telephone calls.  In 15 cases the
witnesses came within 50 metres of the UFO.               Nearly 70% of all reports gave colours of
