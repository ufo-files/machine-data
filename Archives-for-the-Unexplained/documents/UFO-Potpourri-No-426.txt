                             COW MUTILATED IN 1947
From: NEW MEXICAN, Santa Fe, NM, September 18, 1994
        In 1947, the same year an unidentified flying object allegedly crashed near
Roswell, Willie C de Raca found a mutilated cow on. a ranch near La Cienega.
According to his son, Felipe Cabeza de Vaca, the cow's sexual organs and udder were
removed, its anus cored out- typical of what would later become known as the "classic"
case of cattle mutilation.
        "Everything was done real nice.       He couldn't find any tracks," Cabeza de Vaca,
who was six years old at the time, said of his father, "At that time, they thought it was
coyotes.   Coyotes kill cows, but I've never heard of a coyote sexually abusing the
corpse."

       The UFOs were tracked by radar over the cities of Ciudad Colon, Santiago de

spotted "three OVNis (Spanish acronym for UFO)" alongside his plane "reflecting the
