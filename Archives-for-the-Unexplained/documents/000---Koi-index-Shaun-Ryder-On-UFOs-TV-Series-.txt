     S1.E1 ∙ Caught on Tape
     Sun, Nov 10, 2013

     Shaun Ryrder goes to the South American UFO hotbed of Chile. He goes to an Air Force

     Sat, Nov 16, 2013

     Sun, Nov 24, 2013

     Shaun Ryder talks to Nick Pope who became convinced of the existence of UFOs after
