2.     ETI has a net peaceful, benign and probably protective motive for the
       relationship with humanity at this time.



3.     The CE-5 Initiative is proactive, bilaterally communicative and multi­
       disciplinary in nature and is not primarily motivated towards the current
       acquisition of ETI technology, except as mutually permitted by the ETI-Human
       relationship.



4.     ETI's enigmatic and elusive behaviour may be understood as human-protective
       when viewed from their perspective; a war-tom, aggressive, nuclear armed and
       disunified Earth civilisation must not receive further potentially harmful
