August 21: First Swedish rocket on show for commission members

A number of media representatives, and six members of the 1945 Swedish defence commission, were spectators as the first air-to-ground rockets were fired from Swedish-built J-22 fighters, at old tanks, at the Kråk testing field close to lake Vättern. No direct hit, ”but it will come”, said Air Force commanders. ”The projectile is perfectly new and the pilots have not had the opportunity to practice”. The rockets will, shortly, go into serial production by Bofors. (Svenska Dagbladet, Aug. 22, also TT telegram published by [among others] Katrineholms-Kuriren, Aug 21 and Bohuslänningen, Aug 22)
