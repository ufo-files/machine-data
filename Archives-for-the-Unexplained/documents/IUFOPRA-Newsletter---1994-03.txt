Welcome to the first issue of our monthly newsletter.
This form of information will keep you up to date of events

1/ NEW FACES:
  We are very proud to announce the taking on board to our
  Executive Council of IUFOPRA, the world renowned and famous
  Ufologist and Author Miss Jenny Randles, to the appointment of
  Director and Director of Investigations for IUFOPRA. We wish
  Jenny every success and many happy years with us.
  We also welcome a Computer Expert, Aongus De Barra to our
  Committee, as Assist; PRO to Karl Morris.
  And also a special welcome on board to our Committee, Tom Gardiner,
  Who will carry out the Appointment of our Astro Advisor.

       if the need arises.

   (C) The Applicant must be able to deliver a Lecture to Other
       outside Organisations if the need arises.
