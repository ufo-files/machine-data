Protocol would "tarnish" Protocol Two because of its

stated in the beginning. At its root is the fear that discussing
the hypothetical case of an alien visit or probe may offend and
scare off benevolent funding of SETI. But Carl Sagan postu-

imperative for science to help the public "distinguish between
what SETI stands for and the fraudulent claims of UFOlogy."

subordinate a genuine scientific inquiry to expediency.
Hopefully SETI via radio astronomy will hit paydirt. But
mankind should be prepared politically, legally,
communicationally and psychologically for all Contact
scenarios, not just for one.
