  From the Book Cover

  With The Alien Jigsaw, Katharina Wilson published information about the abduction phenomenon that had never before
  appeared in print.The Alien Jigsaw Researcher's Supplement completes one of the most detailed abduction cases ever
  published.

  The Researcher's Supplement is for individuals who want to know more about Katharina's case. This book contains
  hypnosis transcripts that were summarized for the book, journal entries that were omitted from The Alien Jigsaw, as
  well as a research paper correlating important physiological data with her abduction experiences. In addition, this book
  picks up where The Alien Jigsaw left off, giving the reader and overview of the year 1993 and a look at some new alien
  Beings.

  "We go on the deck. I ask him, "Are you from the Pleiades?" The Being adamantly replies, "NO." I ask him where he is
  from. We look in the sky to see the Big Dipper. He says, "See the Ursa Major?" I reply, "Yes." The Being then tells me,
  "The star cluster to the right and below. The one with the triangle to the left and the little stars in between. Well, we're
  from that one..."

The Alien Jigsaw Researcher's Supplement


  In her search for understanding and truth, Katherina designed a research table containing over one hundred queries
  relating to important questions and prevalent topic about the abduction phenomenon. She cross-referenced these with
  each of her 119 abduction experiences that she documents in her journal. This book reveals her important findings from
  her research table.

  Katherina wants other abductees to realize the important insights that can gain by keeping a journal and documenting
  their experiences. It is her hop that others will use this research supplement as a study guide for documenting and
  reporting information relating to abductions.

  About the Author

  Ketherina Wilson presently serves as a State Section Director for the Mutual UFO Network. As a referral contact for
  The Intruders Foundation and MUFON, her work with abductees involves creating a support group network in the state
  of Oregon. She served two years on the Board of Directors of The Northwest UFO Group and now devotes her time to
  speaking and writing about the alien abduction phenomenon.
