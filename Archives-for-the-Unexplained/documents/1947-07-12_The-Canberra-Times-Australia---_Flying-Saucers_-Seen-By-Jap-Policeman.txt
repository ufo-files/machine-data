                  SEEN          BY    JAP
                  POLICEMAN
                                           TOKYO,       Friday.
                                                                 appeared
                   "Flying      saucers"      have      now

                         over     Japan.      The      first flight
             was      reported by          a  police    officer at
             Kagosima,          in         Southern   Kyushu,
             where     the    Allied      invasion    was         to
             have    occurred     in   1945.
                The    police officer, Shigeru Arrifu
             ku, said that, while           on  duty shortly
             after    midnight      on     July 9, he saw
             some      brilliant    objects      flying over
             Kagosima Bay          in   a   wave-like       manner.
                                                         north-west
                     The   objects were        flying
                     at about     3,000    feet.
                 The    Kagoshima         Weather       Bureau
       declared




                          that   the   "saucers,"      seen      by
                                                              balloons
             the policeman, might have              been
                      released     by the Bureau             after
                  midnight.
