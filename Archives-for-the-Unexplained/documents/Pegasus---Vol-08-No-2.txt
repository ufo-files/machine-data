      A huge mysterious door-like structure has recently been discovered in
the Hayu Marca mountain region of Southern Peru. Hayu Marca, 35 kilometres
from the city of Puno, has long been revered by local indians as the "City
of the Gods," and has never been fully explored because of the rugged mountain
terrain. Although no actual city has ever been discovered, many of the rock
formations of the region resemble buildings and artificial structures. The
door, or "Puerta de Hayu Marca" (Gate of the gods/spirits), has been at some
time in the distant past carved out of a natural rock face and measures
exactly seven meters in height by     seven meters in width, with a smaller
alcove in the canter at the base which measures just under two meters in
height.
      Jose Luis Delgado Mamani stumbled across the structure while trekking
through the   surrounding foothills trying to familiarize himself with the
area. His job is as a guide for mountaineering tourists.
"When I saw the structure for the first time, I almost passed out," said
Mamani in an interview with the local press.       "I have dreamed of such a
construction repeatedly over the years, but in the dream the path to the door

                                            BOOK REVIEW

L E FT AT EAST GATE by La rry Warren and Peter Robbins. A first-hand account of the Bentwaters -
Woo d b ridge U FO incident, its cover- u p and i nvestigation . ( Michael O ' Mara Books, 1 997 - £ 1 5. 99)


        This is, in a sense, two books in one. There is the autobiographical account of
his life, before , during and after the incident by Larry Warren and there is also an
account by Peter Robbins of his researches over ten years, seeking confirmation of his
co-author's claims. The contribution of each is distinguished typographically, Robbins'
work being printed wholly in italic - not a happy solution, as it tires the eyes. A different
font would have served the purpose better..

         The Rendlesham incidents have sometimes and with reason been dubbed "The
B ritish Roswell", for although the human participants were all members of the armed
forces of the USA, the actual incidents all occurred in British territory or airspace,
outside the limits of the RAF bases on lease to the Americans, who were using them
to store their main supplies of tactical nuclear weapons. December 1 980 was, it will be
recalled, a time of some international stress, with Soviet forces massing on the Polish
frontier as the Solidarity trade union made its bid for power.

         U F Os pouring down energy beams into the weapons storage area, with one
ap parently malfunctioning and threshing about in Rendlesham forest; the same or
another surrounded by US forces, emitting harmful radiations as its occupants allegedly
arranged with the local commander for technical help - this _was the scenario generally
depicted. Remarkably, none of this was subsequently held by the British MoD to be of
any military significance.


         Larry Warren emerges ·from his book as a credible witness, a stubborn "whistle­
blower" whose revelations brought him involvement with the NSA and inevitable grief,
while the painstaking and well-documented researches of his co-author wrestle bravely
but inconclusively with the web of half-truths and intelligence cover-up in which the
hap penings have become enwrap ped.


         We don't even know the exact dates the main events occurred, but it is clear that
the governments concerned are determined to keep hidden from their respective
peoples for as long as possible the truth about these happenings: we can only suppose,
therefore, that this truth would, if revealed, be a cause for considerable concern.
