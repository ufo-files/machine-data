E.    SESSION SUMMARY:

     The target consists of a central object which looms over the
surrounding site area. It is metal construction/object either over
or near water. There is a sense of great height and the structure
is more open than solid. There is a long, large pipe which feels
vertical (see diagram page iv).

     The structure feels partially submerged in water. Attributes
of the target include (in clusters):      sparks, metal girders,
height, angles, sharpness (as in metallic or pointed sharp), an
electric smell, dirty, dangerous and wet.

     A second cluster of attributes consisted of: activity,
construction, manufacturing, processing, shaping and attaching.

      Lots of exposed metal is present. It is cold, desolate and
"painful working conditions". There is a movement of large metal
objects.    There are stacks/groupings of similar objects (see
diagrams page ii).

     An individual appears to be perched or suspended near the top
of the structure. A group of people nearby are having a good time;
almost festive.  It is reminiscent of a party or banquet.
