a "local resident and his wife", wrote     "On September JOth at about 1-JOpm,

black and glistening object coming slowly up the Loch on the far side and
 just below the Horseshoe, knowing that Tim was in the area I thought at first
that this must be his boat as it was in the area where he regularly cruises but
this did not seem to fit as the object appeared black and was too big for his

25-30 ft in length. The object had now ceased to move and stretched out in
front of it, or possibly asternt was a long writhing neck or tail creating
a considerable disturbance in the water. In that area of the loch it was
flat mirror calm but over on our side, the water was dappled, I am sure that
there is no doubt that this was a monster of great size and possibly feeding.
My wife and I wat~hed alternately for about ~ an hour when a pleasure launch
came down slowly and quietly on this side of the Loch, we took our eyes from
the animal momentarily and on looking back there was no sign of anything, no
wash or ripples or anything. We assume it submerged vertically. I do not
think that I could give you a more accurate description of it than the
illustration given in Tim's book, Loch Ness Monster, paper back, on page 32
except that the body was more square and much more irregular and one had the
feeling that it was an animal of an extremely primitive type and caused a
feeling of revulsion to both of us".                            ·
