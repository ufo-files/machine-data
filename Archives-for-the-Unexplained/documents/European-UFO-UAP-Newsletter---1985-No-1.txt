gives a total pjcture about the sightings in the little valley of Hessdalen
in the Mid-Norwegian mountains. The lightings have been observed from 1870,
Jan S. Krogh tells in his report. But the majority of the observations in
his report were seen by thousands of people in the 1980s in an area which
covers not only the tiny valley but an area of the size of Luxembourg.
NIVFO was the first instance which received reports from the area. The
institute started in 1981 investigations and has worked with the case until
1984 when the case was ended. In April 1984 NIVFO . in co-operation with the
Norwegian Meteorological Institute operated a meteorological station in the
area to find out if the conditions could create plasma lights. Other results
from the measurements gave valuable help to the Scandinavian meteorologists
in making weather forecasts during the 1-month long expedition.
