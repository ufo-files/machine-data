He goes on: "Ghosts are supposed to be people who haven't found their final place, right? And we just thought it would be

"And that kept popping up in my head, the idea of not being on the guest list."
But, hey kids, apparently there's also a lighter side to the album - which Chris, Jonny Buckland, Guy Berryman and Will
Champion will showcase at a free gig at Brixton Academy on Monday.
A short tour also follows but as Mr Martin told Clash magazine: "We don't want it to go on too long as some of the band start
to go mental... "
Indeed.
The clean-cut dad-of-two claims that personally he'll be relying on his porn stash to get him through.
