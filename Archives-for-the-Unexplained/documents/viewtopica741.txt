  New line of enquiry
    by Observer » Wed Feb 28, 2007 10:21 am

  Hi all

  The forum seems to be under a some stagnation at the moment and i suspect that we are struggling to
  find new leads.

  Recently i have found some convincing evidence that this incident could have been caused by the ARRS
  (Aerospace Rescue and Recovery Squadron) who were based RAF Woodbridge at that time. Admin may
  post my article on this, so i won't go further on it here.

  Failing this as a 'believable' explanation, i suggest that a new line of enquiry should be evaluating and
  comparing other incidents round the world that have had similar MO's

  For example, The Vladivostok sighting where a bright orange lit object was seen to shine light beams down
  to ground, this was also seen in Brazil. The Rendlesham forest sighting did the same thing. Are these all
  perhaps linked in some way?

  Vladivostok UFO
    by Observer » Sun Apr 01, 2007 3:52 pm

  Hi Andy

  I cannot remember much about it other than it was part of a TV Doc on the history channel.
  The Doc reported several sightings, Vladivostok, Brazil and the Rendlesham forest one and it drew
  comparisons. They were all described as having a similar shape and size (some thing on the lines of the
  Apollo capsule), they all had a red/orange glow and they all shone light beams down to the ground where
  people were standing. These comparisons were drawn from eye witness statements of each incident.

  In fact the Brazilian government took it so seriously that they put together an air force monitoring team and
  they set up camp at the last location.
  Apparently, the UFO appeared again and they filmed it but the Brazilian government would not release any
  pictures to the media.

  Some local inhabitants at the Brazilian sightings reported feeling unwell and a few suffered ultra violate
  type skin burns or so the doc reported.
