AIR FORCE REGULATION 200-2

        In 1953, as the U.S. was attempting to strengthen its air defenses against a possible
Soviet air attack, the USAF issued Regulation 200-2, which required all American and
Canadian military pilots and airline pilots to promptly report by radio if they sighted hostile
aircraft. missiles, hostile or unidentified military ships or UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECTS.
The intent was to supplement the then-meager air defense radar coverage of the U.S. and
Canada. If a pilot saw an unfamiliar object, he might refer to it as a UFO. But the inclusion
of "UFOs" in Regulation 200-2 prompted some UFOiogists to accuse the USAF of withholding
evidence that UFOs existed, according to Quintanilla.
