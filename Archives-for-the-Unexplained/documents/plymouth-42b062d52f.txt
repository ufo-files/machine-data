Hi,
I have a story that you may be interested in.
A friend of mine has just called me and told me of an event that occurred
at just 7pm this evening. The date is Sunday 16th of March 1998. Where i
can I will try and use his descriptions to the best of my memory. It was
told to me by the person involved, who is a sceptic of "unexplained
phenomena".
My friends stepfather was outside his house at 7pm, when the whole area was
lit by an incredible light. He says the whole raod was lit with light not
disimilar to lightning, but was much brighter, gave NO noise, and lasted
for far too long to actually be lightning. The duration is said to have
been about a second and a half with none of the usual flickering often
given by lightning. On looking up, he saw that the sky was relatively
clear, with a small amount of high level but quite patchy cloud. Very high
up, about the same distance from the ground to the clouds agian, there was
a large area of what he says looked like smoke. As he looked, he saw what
he describes as a sort of sparkling effect that lasted a few seconds then
stopped. On seeing this flash, then the cloud with the 'lights', he went
inside and got his partner, who joined him outside about 3 mins later. The
cloud was still there, which he describes as a 'large round cloud, sort of
pillar shaped'. On looking at this, he says that they heard what seemed
like a cross between a motorcycle engine and a helicopter. This lasted for
about 30 seconds and then stopped. This is all he said, I have no idea how
long the cloud remained after this.
        I have read so many articles in the area of the unexplained as it is an
interest of mine (which is why my friend called me). I have no  wish to
claim it was anything in particular, but the story appears odd enough to be
questioned. Weather reports and air traffic info might be useful to
determine the phenomenon, but I have no access to this info at this time.
It has been suggested to me that it might have been an aircrash, meteor or
weather effect. This is I believe quite possible, but because he says it
appeared right above his house, this seems unlikely as he would have soon
known about the debris in the case of a crash. Nonetheless I thought you
might find it interesting. The area in question is directly above 'Central
Park', Plymouth, England.

Thankyou for your time.

Cheers,
