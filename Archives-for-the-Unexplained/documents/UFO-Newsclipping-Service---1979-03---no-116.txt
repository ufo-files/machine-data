NEWS-CHIEF, Winter Haven, FL- Jan. 7, 1979

 Another UFO sighting                                                       TODAY'S POST, King of Prussia, PA- Jan. 11, 1979

 reported in Winter Haven
    Another Winter Haven resident has reported seeing a bright
  object, " larger than a star but smaller than the moon ", near
  Polk Community College this past week.
    Jeannette Bagley , 1516 Ave. D, NW , said she was on Lake
  Martha Drive , headed toward Lake Silver, at 6:45 p.m.
  Wednesd ay when she saw the bright object "that lit up the area
  by the college."
     She said it was stationary at first , but then moved off at a fast
  rate of speed, disappearing behind some bushes on Fairfax
  Avenue .
     " When I first saw it, I told my daughter, 'look at the falling
  star' ," she said . " I didn 't think about it being a UFO until I read
  in the paper about Perdue seeing an object nearTiie·college.
     "The funny thing is that I was talking to my son about UFO's
  just a couple of nights before."
     Mrs. Bagley quoted from the Bible in noting that "there shall
   be signs in the sky before.the coming of the Lord."
     " I feel we are in the last days ," she said .
     Winter Haven Police Officer Ron Perdue reported that he
   saw a round glowing object about 1,500 feet in the air near PCC
   about4 a.m. Thursday .
