 •   Australia:      The Return of theSon of The Lizardman, Part III:      While in Britain,
I was talking to everybody about my plans of doing a feature of mermaid- and
lizardman-like creatures in flying saucers. Well, they still come, and I re­
ceived a new report by someone who did not even knew I was especially looking
for them. Ok, here it comes: Austrian Hartwig Rudiger Bayerl and his pregnant
friend Susan Zack went to Australia in 1982 because Bayerl believed an atomic
war would destry the earth and lizard-like extraterrestrials would take over
earth aided by the freemasions. In Adelaide Bayerl built a nuclear shelter in
1984 which he equipped with food, protection clothing and gas masks. In 1989
Bayerl believed he was christ reborn. He met Austrian Johann Manfred              Weissen­
steiner after moving to Cairns in 1989, both worked on changing Bayerls yacht
Immanuel into a vessel that would survive atomic warfare. After a few more twists
in the story, the tree want to sail to the pacific to colonise some islands, and,
in Sept 90 Weissenstein is discovered with the yacht but without the couple,
in the South Sea. He is now charged with murder though no bodies have been
found. The newspapoer clippings i have (Darmstadter Echo and Suddeutsche Zei­
tung 12 Sept 91) are both very long and not clearly writte, but it appears all
three believed in the coming doom and the lizard men. Lizard-like ETs also
appear in the TV serial "V" and the German SF movie "Enemy mine".

                                            KRAKEN:
•   Island Beast:      While it has been very popular to follow Heuvelmans interpre­
tation of the Island Beast of the ancients as a misperceived giant squid, this
solution is obviously inadequate to explain what the ancient Mariners feared.
Moving sandbanks, and mirages, are more likely to has given rise to the idea
thatsome islands are living animals. In the Islander, a newspaper for tourists
published by the Orkadian, Kirkwall, Orkney, has, in its 1991 issue an article
on Orkney tales. One is about drifting islands off the Orkneys that are explained
as mirages. The island of Hether Blether is home of the Finn Folk and can some­
times be seen floating on the waves. During the first world war, the Island North
Ronaldsay appeared to float and approach Sanday until it lay shore to shore with
it. Thois natural phenomena rather than giant squids, which, after all, are quite
small compared to an island, could be the explanation for island beasts.
