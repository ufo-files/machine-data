USA TODAY· THURSDAY, JANUARY 6. 1983                  •    7A




THE HUMAN SIDE OF THE NEWS


12 UFOs reportedly buzzed N.C. in '82
      UNCOLNTON, N.C.       -     1982 was a good year for
unidentified flying objects in North Carolina. "There were
12 significant reports., in the state last year, UFO research­
er George Fawcett said Wednesday. Fawcett said he is a
field investigator for the Texas-based Mutual UFO Network
Inc. The sightings included one from a high school student
who complained of burning eyes, red eyes and extreme
thirst when a domed UFO hovered above him in Kerners­
ville. Ten members of a Hillsborough family reported
seeing a 50-foot object maneuver above their home.

Reported by Mark Mayfield
