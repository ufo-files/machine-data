                                        Palo Alto Airport
                                     (Palo Alto, off Embarcadero)
Directions: SEE MAP; Take Laurel south to Willow Rd.; follow Willow Rd. out to Highway 101; get on
Highway 101 South; exit the Highway at the Embarcadero off ramp; go East on Embarcadero until you get
to the Palo Alto Airport. Pull into the parking lot and park

Target: The target is the the Palo Alto Airport. Walk through the rows of planes. Walk out to the
control tower. Watch any planes that might be landing or taking off. There are also rows of metal aircraft
hangers. The top observation part of the control tower is a sort of hexagonal shape. The solid base of the
control tower is nearly completely covered over by pine trees on all sides, but one.
