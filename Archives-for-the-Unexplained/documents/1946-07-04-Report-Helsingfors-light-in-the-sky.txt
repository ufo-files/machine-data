Case xxx.
A light over Helsingfors

Time: July 4, at 21.30 hours [20.30 Swedish time]
Place: Helsingfors

The following is scribbled on a piece of paper in the Defence Staff files: 

”Report [on] August 6 by a Swede who has visited Finland. On July 4, 21.30, an object was seen at great height which died out, whereafter a new light developed. No sound. In Helsingfors there was much talk about such...[illegible]...others had since May seen phenomena with explosions in their trajectories, though to come from Porkkala [small part of Finland surrendered to the Soviet Union between 1944 and 1956]. In Helsingfors many have seen such phenomena. People close to Porkkala complain that in the nights they are woken up by explosions from the area. In Lahtis light phenomena have been seen during July, in the evenings. Hufvudstadsbladet has reported that they no longer are allowed to even write that it is [the question of] meteors.”

Status: Meteor
