MISC. RAVINGS:

       Your fearless "Saucer Smear" editor attended the June 5th - 7th AFRO
 convention in Cleveland, Ohio, where speakers included Jim Lorenzen, Bill
 Moore, Betty Hill, Dr. Leo Sprinkle, Stan Friedman, and Peter Jordan - who
 gave a fascinating talk on cattle mutilation.
       We didn't really get time to hear most of the speeches, because (due
 to a complex mix-up) your editor was in Cleveland that weekend mainly to
 give a UFO lecture for Rick Hilberg's Northern Ohio UFO Group, along with
 the semi-legendary Gray Barker of Clarksburg, W. Va.    This local ufologi­
 cal event took place in a library in nearby Parma, whereas the AFRO bash
 took place in a swank motel near the Cleveland airport.
       We had a long and interesting informal interview with Coral Lorenzen
 in the coffee shop of the AFRO convention motel, and learned among other
 things that we erred somewhat in our "Saucer Leer" (Page 2, bottom para­
 graph) account of the recent attempt to force the Lorenzens out of their
 own organization. It turns out that the problem was not related directly
 to pro and anti Pleiades feelings. In any case, the AFRO librarian,     who
 instigated the problem, has now been booted out of the club. Again we
-say - way to go, AFRO!
       Another interesting informal interview was with John Timmerman,    who
 is now chairman of the board of CUFOS, and siteth on the right hand of Dr.
 J. Allen Hynek himself. CUFOS, as many know, is in a financial hole,     and
 Mr. Timmerman has given generously of his time & money in an effort to save
 the group.  He is also an enthusiastic new non-subscriber to "Saucer Smear','
 and we welcome him a-Bored!

       On the weekend of June 26th - 27th, your editor again ventured forth
into the hitherlands, this time .to attend the 18th annual running of our
own National UFO Conference (NUFOC). This year the convention was held at
the Howard Johnson Motor Lodge in De Pere, Wisconsin, and the local host
was .Ron Spanbauer of "International UFO Photo Exchange".
       Your humble editor, as national chairman of the NUFOC, was master of
ceremonies at the delegates' session as well as the two public sessions.
Speakers included Rick Hilberg, Bill Moore (again), Timothy Greed Beckley,
Col. Wendelle Stevens of Pleiadean fame, and Spanbauer - who used the oc­
casion to deliver his swansong or exit line from the field of ufological
research. Ron feels that he has learned all he can about the subject, and
that further study would be anti-climatical or whatever.

coverage by the local radio, TV, and newspapers - and in fact the press
were in evidence around the motel all of Friday afternoon and evening,    as
well as at the delegates' session Saturday morning, and on into the public
sessions that afternoon. Their coverage was generally objective and un­
biased; and yet, in spite of all this, the crowd that came to attend the
public sessions was a grea� deal smaller than anticipated. It seems you

bus, Ohio - about which we will have . more to say ip our next issue. Way
