             The Rise & Rise of the Paranormal Celebrity!

In an era of reality TV, YouTube, and social media it seems that everyone
wants to be a paranormal celebrity and have their own reality TV show.
Once you have attained the celebrity status you immediately become, if by
magic, an authority on all things paranormal.

Of course a great paranormal reality TV show draws everyone in with the
expectation of genuine ghostly adventures but unfortunately most ghost
hunting shows can be seen as "pure theatre" and as one critic quoted in a
recent article they are as comparable to the genre of professional wrestling
with its formulaic, teasing approach.

Wearing the standard uniform with the baseball cap worn back to front,
referring to their colleagues as dudes and bros’ and trying to emulate
something creepy to thrill their audience, you almost end up with an
episode of Scooby-Doo.

Regardless of what paranormal investigation show you are watching
currently each episode features the same tired production techniques,
night vision footage and sound effects. Surely we deserve something a little
more authentic and professional!


Janine Donnellan
