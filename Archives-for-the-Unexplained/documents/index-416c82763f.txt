                                              Adventures Unlimited "Lost Cities" Series

   Adventures Unlimited Press produces a number of books on ancient mysteries and lost cities around the world, with
   emphasis on unconventional explanations of ancient mysteries. Some are original works by David Hatcher Childress
   and others, and some are reprints of earliers works. Childress is the author or editor of most of these books.

   Childress styles himself as a "rogue adventurer and maverick archaeologist." The tone is generally speculative,
   suggesting that mainstream archeology is suppressing critical facts.
