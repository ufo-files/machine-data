ARIRA (Aerospace Research Investigation Reporting Agency)
pronounced (a-rye'-ra) is a private research organization.

ARIRA is dedicated to independent research and investigation,
focused on Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP) and other
Anomalous Phenomena, within the framework of physical reality,
anomaly, the effects on society and human consciousness.

ARIRA advisory board members, investigators, researchers and
dispatchers (agents) are selected and invited to serve, based solely
on academic accomplishment, research capabilities, investigative
credentials and experience in this field.
