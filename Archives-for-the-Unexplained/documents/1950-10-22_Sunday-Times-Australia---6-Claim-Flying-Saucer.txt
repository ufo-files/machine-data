                  London, Sat:                     Six
            men       saw          what          they
            claimed           was      a     flying
           saucer           over      Ramsay,
            Isle     of Man,               yester-
           day morning.
             All engineering                     work-
          ers, the  men   were                    hav-

          break by an  open                 factory
          door when   they                 saw     the
          object.
             "It    was      a     long, narrow
          object, with             rounded    cor-

          ners,"      one        of     the       men
          said.
             "Each  end    of the ob-
         long was     dark   and solid
          looking, but     the  centre
         semed   to be almost   trans-
          parent."
              His      workmates                  con-
          firmed      the story.
