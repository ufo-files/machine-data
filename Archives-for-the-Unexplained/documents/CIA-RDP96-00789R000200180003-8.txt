1. (S/NF/SK)    Monitor Tasking:       Refer to tasking data cover sheet,
attached.

2. (S/NF/SK)  Source Tasking:  011 was informed that this session
would initiate work on a new target.  011 acquired the site
immediately (to the monitor's satisfaction), whereupon the monitor
instructed the viewer to move directly into Stage 3 and await further
instructions.  011 was then informed that the target was a composite
structure and that the task was to move inside the part represented by
the rounded part of the Stage 3 sketch.  The remainder of the session
was conducted in Stage 4.

3. (S/NF/SK)  Summary:  Other than the description of something
resembling "an afterburner" (see Viewer 003's sketches; a part of one
piece of equipment does indeed resemble this) this session was subject
to a good deal of AOL drive; this through no fault of the viewer (see
comment below).

4. (S/NF/SK)  Comments:  Jumping the gun (skipping Stage 2) and moving
011 into the structure prematurely was undoubtedly a tactical error on
the monitor's part; (the results of overconfidence).
