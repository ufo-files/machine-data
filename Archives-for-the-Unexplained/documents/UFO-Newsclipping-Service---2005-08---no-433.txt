 lengthwise, leaving behind only a small 7 centimeter chunk. These connectors, known as "puentes" (bridge.s)

 measuring approximately 5 meters in diameter burned into the grass. They also found that the soil presented a
 high degree of hardness and compactness.

 must have occurred between the electromagnetic field of the high-voltage line (132 Kv) and the energy field of
 the unidentified flying object
