PREFACE: I feel not a terribly productive session. Whatever the
target is, it made me extremely ill.       During the session, I
experienced sensations of dizziness, headaches, upset stomach,
extreme difficulty in breathing and just overall a really rotten
feeling (reminiscent of having the flu) •       I have had sinus
problems for the past two weeks, but nothing like this.
     Recurring sensation of the earth moving in waves reminiscent
of waves radiating out from a rock thrown into a pond except
these waves are long and very high.        The activity involves
intelligent people and a "countdown".   Eventually the situation
these individuals are in will change dramatically; almost like an
eruption, at least on that scale.   Soil is being shoved forward
and "thrown".
     There is the concept of "common use" and someone is
preoccupied with the thought that " 1 this 1 should be the same
price for everyone". There is also a preoccupation with "seismic
activity" and "resonance".     There are alternating signals, a
rumbling sound and a persistent up-and-down, up-and-down.
     There are scoop-like objects moving large amounts of
something resembling earth but is different from normal soil to
the touch.   The mechanical movement involves a massive shifting
in content or context. There is an alteration of material; it is
melting and being reformed. It becomes more "homogeneous".
     The area has fractures, cracks, cleavages and it is black,
deep, hollow and frightening.    One location contains a thick,
powdery and choking dust which someone is walking through.
     There is a "long rail" in a dark place. Waves are radiating
out from a central point and are moving across or through a plaza
full of people.
     There is a arched hollow which occurs within the bottom of a
very narrow and visually deceiving canyon.   It sits at the base
of a tall rugged slope.       The activity inside consists of
individuals intensely studying large rectangularly shaped objects
on the wall.
     At this point there was an overpowering image of an Arab
attack on southern Russia in order to capture "nukes".   This
situation became so overpowering and AOL producing that it
effectively ended the session.
