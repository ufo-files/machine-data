       NOW              'FLYING                       SOAPSUDS'
         LONDON,           August        18     (Special)—

                                                              'Flying saucer'
              reports    over   London        last night were   corrected later
         to  'Flying Soapsuds.'               Experts said strange globular
         objects whisking across the evening sky were               huge soap
         bubbles   lifted   by the wind from             one   ofthe Thames
         tributaries.    The   water   contained        detergents and soap
         powders    washed    in  from   kitchen        sinks and factories.
