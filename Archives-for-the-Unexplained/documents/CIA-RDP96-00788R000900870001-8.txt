                          SUMMARY ANALYSIS

                  REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION C 15



1.  (S)  This report provides documentation of a remote
viewing session conducted for training purposes to enhance
a remote viewer's demonstrated ability.

2.   (S) There appears to be no target correlation in this session.
The viewer was very anxious prior to the session. He was concerned
about a reorganization of his office. The viewer was unable to
relax and concentrate on the task at hand. Ambient room noise was
excessive and disturbed the viewer a great deal. The whole session
proved to be a frustrating experience for both the viewer and
interviewer. The viewer was presented with two targets in the
same approximate geographic area (shores of Lake Michigan). He
was unable to resolve either target.

3.  (S)  The protocol used for this session is detailed in the
document, Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing
Protocol (S) , undated.

4.   (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions
during the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made
by the viewer reference his impressions of the target site. At
TAB B is available target data.
