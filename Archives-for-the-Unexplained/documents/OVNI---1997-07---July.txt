                             1997 THE YEAR OF REVELATION?

This year, we have seen an increasing number of UFO and 'Flying Triangle' reports, not only
from Great Britain, but particularly from the United States. There has been a steady rise in FT
sightings since the early 80's and we have been expecting an increase in FT activity this year.
In fact it was Bob Dean who told us in 1996,             "There will be an increasing number of
Flying Triangle sightings in the coming months and they will start appearing in daylight "

Together with some 30 other UFO investigation groups, we have been contributing to 'Project
FT.' We have recently heard from Victor J.Kean (who is running the 'Project FT' database),
that there has been a marked increase in Flying Triangle activity this year, particularly over
Nuclear Power Stations.      In some cases the increase in sightings has been judged to be a
massive 100%. Certainly, reports have been coming in from UFO groups all over the country,
informing us of many new FT incidents. The local press in each region has been having a field
day, with each newspaper reporting that they are in the 'UFO Corridor,' not realising that they
are only part of the overall 'big picture.'

In this issue, we have departed from our preference in not reproducing newspaper cuttings.
We have recently received a whole news page from the June 18 1997 issue of 'USA Today,'
sent to us by Gene Bauer of Dover Plains NY.           Gene was one of the original witnesses of the
New York State. 'Boomerang and Flying Triangle' flap, that took place in 1983. He has told
us that "They are back!"

The 'USA Today' report has been reproduced on pages 2 & 3 of this month's newsletter.

While we are always looking for a logical explanation to the UFO reports coming to our
notice, we cannot think of any known craft that, while being the size of 'three football fields',
would travel silently over the countryside.

One incident recently caused a flurry' over Lincolnshire, when a triangular craft was seen
streaking between cloud cover and emitting a low roaring noise. We are indebted to Simon
Lewis of the North Lancashire UFO Investigation Group, for informing us that a SAAB
(Sweden) Griphon, Delta winged fighter aircraft, is currently undergoing trials from an RAF
airfield in Lincolnshire. It should be remembered that the 'Eurofighter' also has a similar wing
configuration and can often be seen, escorted by one or two Tornado aircraft. However, the
-.racket such a trio would kick up, in no way resembles a 'silent and slowly moving' FT.

We have, as usual, included a current selection of the latest 'Flying Triangle' reports, together
with other strange UFO shapes being reported by the general public.

                          A REPORT FROM BENFLEET, �SSEX.

The contributor of this report has not been able to refer to exact dates, but it is still of great
interest, considering the continuing activity in this area of the country.

The first sighting was in June 1989, I was walking with a friend in a westerly direction, along
the main road in my home town (Hadleigh, Benfleet,Essex).          It was a clear night, the stars
were out and then I noticed a group of moving lights at about the same altitude that a light
aircraft would fly at (2 I 3,000ft). The lights were above a nearby field and moving parallel to
the main road.

We were walking in the opposite direction to the way the lights were heading and within a
moment, our paths crossed. I could make out the shape clearly against the night sky and stars.
It was a perfect triangular shape, dark, with a lot of lights along the three sides, with one in the
middle. The brightness varied, there were four colours, the lights were generally not as bright
as an aircraft's navigation lights and there were no 'strobe' lights visible. It made no sound and
moved quite slowly. After a couple of minutes, we continued on our way......we were late.

The second encounter took place on a late evening in June 1990, I left a friends house on an
estate and was heading home, when I saw a cluster of lights over to my right.           I heard no
sound and then as I looked closely, I made out the same object as I had seen the previous year.
Once again, it was a warm clear night.

This time, it was moving a bit faster than before and flying in the same direction and parallel to
me. I noticed that the sides seemed to be longer than the back, but this could have been due to
the perspective. I became aware that there might be some significance in the 12 month time
gap. I began to feel uncomfortable, it is hard to explain, without making it sound more than it
was. I lost sight of it as it became obscured by some nearby buildings and when it was clearer,
I looked again, but it had gone.

The witness referred to the fact that in both instances the FT had been travelling in the same
direction i.e. East. He also considered that it was 'sinister' that he had seen the FT twice in the
same area, up to then he had been sceptical about UFO's.

            HUGE TRIANGULAR CRAFT SEEN NEAR BANBURY IN 1988

A long journey lay ahead for Mr Colin Hanison and his friend, as they set out from
Manchester in their van. The night was clear, cloudless and starry as they headed towards
Banbury (Oxfordshire). It was about 2am on the July 7 1988 and they were about three miles
from Banbury, when both occupants of the van noticed a dark shape approaching them. It had
first appeared low on the horizon, but there was something strange about it and they both
sensed that it was something unusual.

It was now only half a mile away and they could see that the object was not a plane, helicopter
or any type of aircraft that they could recognise. By now Colin had stopped the van and
switched off the lights. As the object drew closer, they could see that the craft showed no
lights and there was no sign of any wings or helicopter blades.

It was soon within about 200yds of the van and at a height of 70ft above them, moving
absolutely silently. "I knew this was a day that I wouldn't forget" remarked Colin. "By now I
could see the front of the object, it was at least 60ft across, concave and had a matt black
body, there were also three lighter coloured 'circles' on the front of it."

Colin was about to get out of the van for a better look, but his friend begged him to stay
where he was. Both witnesses sat quietly in the van as the huge craft passed silently over the
van at an estimated height of 70ft. There were no markings visible on the matt black craft, but
they did notice 5 oblong 'hatches' underneath, lighter in colour than the main body.                      "By the
time it had gone over, I realised that it must have been at least 300ft long," said Colin. "There
was still no noise or any sign of lights." Colin then moved into the back of the van to watch
the craft moving away. "It was about 600ft away and I was able to make out the shape of the
object, it was tubular, tapering to a 'beaver' shaped tail." "The craft then did an 'impossible'
manoeuvre on its own axis and shot off at great speed into the night sky", said Colin. "I was
very excited, but my friend on the other hand was very nervous and scar�, he just kept asking
me to get going." Colin's final comment: "I have never forgotten that night!"
