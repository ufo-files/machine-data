       This rambling editorial will center around three UFO conferences
 and the Giant Ufological Figures involved with each of them.

 made not by any of the speakers, but by one Phil Klass ( who, who??), who
·gave the participants a "raison d'etre" by allegedly trying to have the
 conference closed down ahead of time. Like a great dictator whom every­

 the "hard core" to close ranks ( very) temporarily in righteous indignation.

       If you think things are bad in ufology , you should hear about the
state of Bigfootology in the far northwest, as told by one Tommie Throne­

years ago, hoping to partake constructively in the search for Bigfoot
proof. All he got for his trouble was threats & harrassment from other
researchers, plus a gnawing but ever-increasing feeling that what he was
looking for might not be all that real after all! Throneberry speaks
well of ( our own!) Erik Beckjord, and seems enthusiastic about the Big­
foot documentary film Erik is now working on. Throneberry is, however,
skeptical even in regard to the famous Roger Patterson film, considered by
many· to be the most positive bit of pro-Bigfoot evidence in existence.
We don't know Throneberry personally, but he sounds like an honest but
sorely disappointed man....
