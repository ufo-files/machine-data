Recent correspondence indicates that probative activity occurred in South
Australia during April and May this year.               However since late May and June,
activity seems to have been concentrated in Queensland and particularly N.S.W.


Dr. J. Stevens has recently notified the Centre that spectroscopic analyses
indicated that the soil inside and outside the Forbes circle,                  reported in
Newsletter No. 41,       is identical,    suggesting a physical explanation.             This
finding does not rule out an unusual explanation,               and the surface thistle
damage perhaps confirms this.


Another "nest" was discovered on May 7th,            near Grafton,    N.S.W.        It had an
inside diameter of 11' and an a lmost perfect circle of dead grass,                    about 14"
wide.   The find deteriorated considerably after its initial discovery.
Although natural causes cannot be entirely ruled out,               later suggestions of
a fungal attack being the cause,          can be deemed unlikely.


Although both of the above finds are unusual,              they cannot be deemed probative
because of the lack of any direct UFO connection.
The f ollowing case,   which received some recent publicity,        can also be

has been no confirmed outstanding sighting with the new "nest" finds.




                                    INVESTIGATIONS


The last 2 months have had a very large number of UFO sightings and fortunately
a significant number of these have proved to be of quite extraordinary content.
The centre is currently attempting to investigate a large number of particularly
good accounts.     The following brief listing notes a few of the new cases,         or
old cases that have recently come to our notice.


Late 1972 near Wallacia,    N.S.W. 6 young people observed a UFO on the ground
and later encountered an 8-10' being. Their car caught fire apparently during
the experience.


February,    1973 near Grafton,    N.S.W.    2 men in a car saw a strange being on the
side of the road which raised its arm as the car passed it.


About 1973 on the South coast,       N.S.W.    Farmer observed huge aerial object land
on his property and leave behind a trace mark of extraordinary magnitude -
about \ mile long.


1.4.75 Molonglo,    near Canberra,   A.C.T.     3 men working on a construction site
observed a large bullet shaped object moving vertical�y from the ground.             It
disappeared when allegdly 3 F-Ill's arrived on the scene.


12.5.75 near Hobart,    Tasmania.     Sydney couple and a Howrah resident observed
an aerial object manoeuvring near Tranmere Point in daylight.           Coloured movie
film   (now on loan to UFOIC)     confirms that something was moving and changing
directions in a high wind.


5.6.75 near South Grafton,      N.S.W.     Mother and daughter and later family,
observed a ball of orange light which apparently flew parallel to road near

sighting.


7.7.75   Maroubra Beach,   Sydney.       Young couple observed several unusual

above the ground and 300' from them.
