U.F.O. Sighting
6 APR 78
Steen by of. J.k. BOLYS On the 13 APE% Bergman Road,
"At approx. 7:30 - 7:45 p.m. on the 6 APR 78, I
was leaving the barn located on my property, Bergman Rd.,
10 miles west of Prince George. I had finished bedding
down the animals for the night and was about to go back to
the house.
It was dark outside but there is a yard light
located near our trailer. The sky was clear.
I had just
left the barn and was standing outside when I saw a beam of
light which extended from a group of trees to the barn.
It was approx. 2" in diameter. I saw the beam, heard the
honses scream, so I immediately returned to the barn.
don't recall if the beam was still there. I attended to
the horses.
The horse nearest the window in the barn in
which the bean seemed to have gone through was down with
front feet through a plywood wall. It was conscious and
struggling to get out. He got up on his own power. When
I had left the barn both horses had been standing.
The
horse in the next stall was laying on its side, and was
motionless.
While I stood there, the horse got up and it
appeared frightened. After getting to its feet he immediately
darted forwards into the screen surrounding the stall.
I checked the third stall and checked the mare,
and attended to her.
I returned to the house and phoned Mrs, ANDERSON,
my mother. She came to my house immediately and we looked
up into the sky and saw a light which appeared to be round
and misty. It was at plane level and appeared to have no
set flight pattern and would dart from one spot to another.
There were no bursts. It was white in color no sounds, was
travelling from North to South and was approx. & the size of
the moon, when it was closest to the fround and then smaller
