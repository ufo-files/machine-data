DEFENSE STAFF
AIR DEFENSE DEPARTMENT

ADDRESS: STOCKHOLM 90
PHONE: "MILITARY staffs"

To the Firing Boss,
AB Bofors,
Bofors.

The air defense department has received a peculiar photograph, taken in Guldsmedshyttan and showing a light phenomenon that may be related to other observations made across our country. However, the photograph was taken in such a direction that it suggests that the light phenomenon may be the result of rocket firings at Bofors artillery range. In view of this, I am obligated to request that I, as soon as possible, receive your opinion on whether or not the phenomenon may be connected to rocket firings. The observation was made on July 9, at around 14:40 PM. No rocket firings were going on at this time and I would be grateful to learn what the expertise in Bofors think about the phenomenon.

The observer has been reported as saying:
"We then observed a brilliant, greenish (neon-like) beam of light, which rapidly traveled in a Northwesterly direction, after which it disappeared. The phenomenon disappeared in a blaze of light and I thought I noticed a strange sound. We figured that the phenomenon must have been a meteorite, though we have before seen anything of the sort during the day. It was all over in an instant"

This information is classified.


Respectfully, 


N. Ahlgren
Major
Head of Department
