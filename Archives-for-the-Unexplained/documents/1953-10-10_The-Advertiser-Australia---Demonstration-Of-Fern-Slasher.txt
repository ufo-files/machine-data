Advertiser (Adelaide, SA : 1931 - 1954), Saturday 10 October 1953, page 16


       Demonstration                     Of
             Fern Slasher
         A   'Flying Saucer'        bracken
       fern slasher       was   demonstra
       ted    at    Mt.     Compass
                              yes
                                K   I.

       Arthur, of Mt. Compass, to
       Mr. H. E. Orchard, of the
       weed control section of the
       Department     of Agriculture.
         The    slasher attachment
       consists of a trailing machine
       with rotating chains     driven
       from the power take-off of a
       tractor.
         It cuts a strip of bracken
       10 feet wide at 6 m.p.h. and
       follows the        contour     of   the
       ground.
          During the demonstration
       the machine effectively dealt
       with a patch of ground con
       taining Dlackberries, bracken,
       rushes and small trees.
