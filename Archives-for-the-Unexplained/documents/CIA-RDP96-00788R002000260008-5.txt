    IAGPC-G                                                  23 November 1981


    MEMORANDUM FOR ADCSOPS-HUMINT

    SUBJECT:    GRILL FLAME Committee Meeting


    1.  On 25 November 1981, from 0900-1600 hours, the semi-annual
    GRILL FLAME Committee meeting hosted by Army (ACSI) will be
    held at the Pentagon.

       a.  The morning session will be an action officer meeting
    and discussion.

       b.  The afternoon executive session is still scheduled for
    1430-1600 hours.   The new location is room 3E267 (DIA Conference
    Room).  An additional attendee will be LTG James A. Williams,
    Director DIA.   The tentative Executive Session agenda is at
    Inclosure 1.

    2.  CG's secretary was notified on friday of the new room
    number and attendance of LTG Williams.

    1 Incl
    as


                                       ROBERT J. JACHIM
                                       LTC, MI
                                       GRILL FLAME Project Manager
