Air Force Exercise 1946
Army Training Management				CLASSIFIED
No. H 3

Conditions for reports regarding observations of light phenomena have been attached to this letter (högkv: Fst dept L no. H 50:13 2/9 1945). The following guidelines shall be applied.

1. A post shall be assigned at ls during 19:30 to 04:30 o’clock.
Reinforcements have been deemed unnecessary in the potential case of actual observations.
An officer as well as an operator shall be assigned to LGE.

2. Ensure units maintain readiness levels to perform assigned mission(s) in accordance with department standard operating guidelines.

 3. Provisional elevation angle instruments shall be supplied by the company commander. The angle must be calculated at around 10 degrees.

4. All reports on observations shall be sent to the army commander’s department by phone.

On behalf of the instructor

Carl Lommel
Colonel
