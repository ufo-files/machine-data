A     WORD       BEFORE             WE       BEGIN



      While there have been many intense, and in many instances somewhat
geographically localized UFO flaps or waves since the early 1970s, the
1973 - 1974 period marked t he last instance where a large concentration
of reports received intense media coverage. And we are talking about UFO
reports here, not speculation about Roswell or alleged alien abductions
as is the case today with the many "tabloid" treatments the subject has
received in recent years.    As strange at it seems to someone who has been
involved in "things ufologica1" for more than forty years (I attended my
first organized UFO meeting in t he last few days of 195 8), many UFO
researchers today totally ignore UFO sightings.     Maybe that is why many
consider t he '73 -' 74 spate of sightings as being the last great UFO
flap, because it's quite possible that many of our current crop of UFO
" researchers" (versus maybe "investigators"?) filter out UFO sightings
and concentrate on the "modern" aspects of the UFO enigma.
      In any event, for someone who has not lived through an intense
period of UFO sighting reports that was fully covered (for a while at
least, until the media decides that the subject is becoming a little too
"stale" and moves on ·to t he next "big story") by the various segments of
the news media, it is hard to imagine the level of excitement and
confusion that such a situation causes.    I recall a period of more than a
week in 1973 when UFO reports were made the lead story by John Chancellor
of "NBC Nightly News. "      Not to be outdone, ·newspapers all over t he
country gave front page coverage to the developing flap.       By the third
week of September 1973 our clipping service began to send us multiple
packages per day literally stuffed with news clippings.     Why, on several
nights early in the flap Carol and I went to a nearby park to "skywatch"
in the hopes of spotting something.      Alas, all we did was disturb the
"neckers" in nearby parked cars!
      All of the accounts that you read on the pages that follow were
written by me from either news reports and/or personal investigation.     I
also have t o t hank the many thoughtful people who gave us reports and
leads on same.    In the days before personal computers, that was what we
would now call 11networking.11
      Lastly, besides t hanking Carol Hilberg for her never ending support
and help, I wish to dedicate this publication to my favorite raconteur
and friend, James W. Moseley.




                                            Rick Hilberg

9/1/73   THE BEGINNING
     Although there were reports from residents of Florida during the
last two weeks of August telling of seeing various-colored lights in the
sky, a photograph touched things off on t he first day of September.
     The photograph was taken by Mike Williford in the southern Georgia
town of Camilla.   It was a night shot taken with a Polaroid camera of an
object that he said had a ribbed design and "some sort of center down the
middle."   The photo itself shows what appears to be an illuminated oval
with a rectangular dark area in the center.    1
9/3/73 DRIVER "BUZZED" BY BRIGHT OBJECT
     On the night of September 3, a visibly frightened motorist rushed
into a police station in Athens, Georgia to report that a "bright -
flashing object" had swooped down towards his car.      Police said that the
unidentified man reported that the objectr with "one bright light and
four flashing lights, " came to within 15 feet of the ground before
swerving off into the sky.      The officers gave the opinion that the
motorist had really seen something.   2
9/8/73 MPs HAVE CLOSE ENCOUNTER
     Early on September 8 two Army MPs had a frightening experience while
on patrol at the deactivated Hunter Air Force Base outside of Savannah,
Georgia.    Randy Shade and Burt Burns were on patrol when they first
spotted the mysterious intruder.      According to Burns,      it was first
spotted as "a cluster of lights in the sky about two thousand feet up.
The lights tracked across the sky flashing red and orange, blue and
white."
     After disappearing behind some trees in the distance for some
minutes, the visitor returned some time around 2:45 a.m.      After hovering
over one end of the now-deserted military base runway, it began moving
toward the car containing the two startled MPs.      They then saw that the
object was saucer-shaped and apparently metallic.      As the saucer neared
the car, Shade raced away with the UFO in hot pursuit at speeds up to 1 0 0
m.p.h.   The 75 foot diameter craft seemed to follow the car only several
feet above the roof.
     Shade kept up the high speed flight from the UFO for nearly a mile
only to finally cut the wheels of the car, and slide onto the grass on
the side of the pavement.     Said Shade, · "The UFO then went back up to
tree-top level and skimmed off into the distance."     The two MPs reported
that the lighted disk made no sound during the encounter.     3
9/9/73 STRANGE MACHINE HOVERS OVER HOUSE
     When UFOs were cavorting throughout ·the South, Griffin, Georgia was
visited by a weird aerial contraption.     The report was made in the form
of a phone call to the Spaulding County Sheriff's office, reporting an
object hovering over a house.    A deputy dispatched to the scene radioed
his office that he saw "two red lights descending slowly to earth, " only
to then disappear.
     Mrs. Hugh D . Beal told local police that an "upside down cup and
saucer-shaped object" hovered over her house.      She said that the object
had gold, red and green lights on the bottom.       The woman reported that
the object, which she said made a funny noise, was too low for an
airplane and was just above tree-top level.          The strange nocturnal
visitor would change the color of its lights.    4
