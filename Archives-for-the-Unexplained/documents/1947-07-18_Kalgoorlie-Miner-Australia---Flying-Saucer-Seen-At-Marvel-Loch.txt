Kalgoorlie Miner (WA : 1895 - 1954), Friday 18 July 1947, page 4




               Flying Saucer Seen

                   at      Marvel Loch
               Marvel       Loch, July 17.— Dur-
          ing   the         height of a terrific                   ,



          windstorm           on Friday night, a
          resident        was       horrified to see       a
          vast       circular         shape   bearing
          down       on   him       in the gloom.
            The  'saucer'   landed   with an
          awful   crash   in scrub    bv the
          roadside.     The   man    did not
          wait on the order for his going,
          for a few minutes     later he was
          demanding            double
                                a
                                        brandy
          and      exclaiming,  as  glass chat
          tered      against teeth: 'I've near
          ly    been       knocked        by    a   flying
          saucer.'
             One curious soul investigated
          the locality where     the alleged
          'saucer' had landed, and found
          it to be all too true.
             The wind had lifted the coni
          cal top of a 1000-gallon tank
          and   hurled  it through the air
          to its resting place a hundred
          yards      away.
