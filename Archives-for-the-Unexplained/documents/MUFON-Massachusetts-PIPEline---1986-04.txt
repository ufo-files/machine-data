Dear Past, Present and Future Contributors to the Journal:

I think I need not stress that these are perilous times for
ufology. A majority of major UFO organizations are suffering
financial and publishing difficulties. MUFON itself has been
forced to raise its subscription rates substantially and this
will inevitably result in a loss of some subscribers.

At present the Journal is the only monthly UFO magazine in the
world, reaching well over a thousand interested researchers and
field investigators. Yet the Journal also suffers from a dearth
of hard-hitting, investigative articles that seek to grapple
with, and not skirt, both the issues and the UFO phenomenon.
Frankly, it is an embarrassment to admit that at least one
leading skeptic outstrips us all in contributions to the MUFON
Journal, second only to letters of complaint about appearance of
said articles!
Where are your submissions? We need your thoughts, findings,
theories, and reflections on the marvelously mysterious body of
widespread UFO and related phenomena. For the field itself is not
moribund. Numerous examples could be cited, in fact, to show that
ongoing events rank in excitement and importance with those of
any past period: the Cash-Landrum lawsuit, the Puget Sound case,
the recent satellite UFO picture, the installation of a compute-
rized UFO bulletin-board, and so on, ad infinitum.

Put your thoughts on paper and submit them to the Journal. Take
advantage of the only publication of its kind that can offer a
30-day, or less, turn around from manuscript to publication.
Don't keep relevant material that needs rapid dissemination
buried in your files!
The Journal seeks no less than a renaissance in contemporary UFO
writing. But to achieve that we need your help. We don't ask you
to abandon previous affiliations or commitments, only to
recognize what you can do for the MUFON Journal and what the
Journal can do for you.
The material itself can be of varied nature as regards content.
It should, however, be neatly typed, double-spaced and accom-
panied by any appropriate photographs or graphic art. We welcome
all relevant manuscripts.
