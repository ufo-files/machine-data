        Welcome to Case Briefs!       In an effort to respond to numerous requests for continued information on investigations
conducted in northern California, we will be publishing a new periodical to be known as Case Briefs.           This periodical will be
published from time to time, and will normally appear at the time of the general MUFON meetings held presently in San Jose.          As
the San Jose gatherings usually take place every other month, it is planned to publish five or six times a year. A subscription to Case
Briefs: Explorations and Review is $12.00 for six issues, or $16.00 should mailing be necessary. This should just manage to cover
most of our expenses. Checks should be sent to MUFON Case Briefs, P.O. Box 7668, Landscape Station, Berkeley, California
94707-0668.
        Response to the first two newsletters was overwhelmingly favorable, and Kathi Hennesey, Editor of the Newsletter,
established a high standard to be followed. But Kathi is overburdened with other time consuming enterprises and Laura Steiger has
                                               ·
agreed to take over the editorship. Katl\i will continue to take an active part in the creation of each issue and she will become the
Assistant Editor.   Since Laura does not have access to all of the computer potential that was generated by Kathi in the first two
newsletters, and since there is uncertainty as to the nature of future issues, it has been decided to form a totally new organ with a
different title and structure. This is what we have come up with and we would appreciate your comments. Until we procure a box in
San Francisco, material submitted to Case Briefs may be sent to the above address.     Such material should be double spaced and in
good English. It is trusted that readers will find the Case Briefs to be "a thing of beauty and a joy forever," and that no one will be
subjected to the kind of fetid ramblings generally found in the mass media.
