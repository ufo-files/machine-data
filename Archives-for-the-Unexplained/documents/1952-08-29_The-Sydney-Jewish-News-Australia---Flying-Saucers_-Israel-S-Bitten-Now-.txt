Sydney Jewish News (Sydney, N.S.W : 1939 - 1954), Friday 29 August 1952, page 3



           FLYING               SAUCERS:—
                 ISRAEL’S              BITTEN
                                           !
                                NOW

                HAIFA,            (JTA):           A res
           ident of this town                      claims
           to have  seen Israel’s                     first
           flying saucer.
             He described it as being
           “greenish,    about the size
           and         shape          of       a   saucer,
           with          a       broad,            rapidly
           revolving belt                  around       its
           middle.”
                The      witness     added that
           it    was         visible for about
           3 seconds    flying across
           the top of Mt. Carmel  in a
           north-westerly direction.
