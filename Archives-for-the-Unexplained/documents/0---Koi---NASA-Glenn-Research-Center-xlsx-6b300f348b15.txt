The ANGEL experiment demonstrated how the Airborne Systems, Inc. The integrated system will then cleanly separate and will land within the targeted impact zone. Landing loads, GPS and flight attitude and telemetry will be collected. ANGEL is an important first step towards maturing steerable paraglider technology and improving recoverability for high altitude balloon missions.
