growing up of mankin9.. Ma.n will come of age. In 1958, we announced

in the acceptance by the majority of people of the reality of Space-

that "there is something going on in the Flying Saucer business". Now,
people will come closer to what is going on. We send greetings to
friends throughout the world, viishing you great success in all your
enterprises to lift man up in your town, area and nation. Help is
waiting for all who will take the necessary steps to do constructive
work in their home area to bring man into his heritage. Intelligent
direction, guiding constructive, effort, results in progress.
