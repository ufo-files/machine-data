Conclusion : The statements made by
the three witnesses have some
significant similarities and some
differences that can be explained.
The similarities (listed below) indicate
that    something        strange    and
unexplainable happened on March 14
around 7:30 p.m. The differences
could be explained if the westbound
sighting took place before 7:32 p.m.
and the object moved northwest
toward Mundy's Gap in a few
minutes. Conventional explanations
do     not   fit    the   eyewitneses'
descriptions of their sighting. Given
that multiple independent witnesses
saw a gigantic bright light in the
Franklin Mountains on the same date
and time, and that neither they nor
the investigators could explain the
sightings away with conventional
explanations, the El Paso MUFON
Chapter must conclude that a UFO
sighting took place on March 14.

    The similarities and differences in
this investigation will be included in a
separate sheet within this newsletter.
