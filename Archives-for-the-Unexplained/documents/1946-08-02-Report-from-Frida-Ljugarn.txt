“FRIDA”

1. Time: August 2, at 0:05 o'clock. Observers: Ulriksson, Granell and Svedborg.
2. Place of observation: The middle of the community of Ljugarn.
3. Distance: About 130 meters from the observation site.
4. Elevation angle: 40 degrees.
5. The light phenomenon traveled with remarkable speed. It had been completely soundless.
6. It traveled in a southwesterly direction.
7. It was approximately 100 meters in length.
8. Light: strong, slightly violet.
9. Form: almost fusiform.
10. The phenomenon had probably disappeared behind a rain cloud.
11. Cloud cover: Heavy rain clouds (stratus nimbus).

Note: 	Due to darkness, these figures cannot be considered precise.
Ever so often we are able to notice a multitude of strange sounds whilst using FRIDA; so far these have been shown to originate from telephone printers, transmitters and the like. Should suspicions of unusal activity arise, a scout will remain during a longer period of time.

Ljugarn, August 2, 1946.

Olof Svanberg
FRA
