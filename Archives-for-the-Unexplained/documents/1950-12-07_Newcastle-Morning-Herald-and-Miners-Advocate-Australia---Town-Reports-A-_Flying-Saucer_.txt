Newcastle Morning Herald and Miners' Advocate (NSW : 1876 - 1954), Thursday 7 December 1950, page 7



        Town             Reports                  A
                    "Flying Saucer"
          MARYBOROUGIH,                      Wednesday.
       -Residents          to-night          watched      a
       "flying saucer"  make     a wide   sweep
       over the district about     8 o'clock.
          A bright blue object        was    seen
                                                It
       travelling north  at  high speed.
       returned at low    level,  approaching
       the river      mouth.
          The object moved                almost perpen
       dicularly      to an    estimated       height of
       8000 feet.
         The  President              of     Maryborough
       sub-branch    of        the        R.S.L. (Mr. S.
       Bryant   Smitht)     estimated the speed
       at 400       to 500 miles an hour.
