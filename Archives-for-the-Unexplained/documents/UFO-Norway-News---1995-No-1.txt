Norwegian sightings by Odd-Gunnar Reed
Primo July 1965, Lunde in Telemark
The witness saw an elongated, egg shaped object with distinct contours, humming like a
swarm of bees, at an altitude of some 150-200 meters. The object was estimated to be 2-3
meters long. It appeared to have a metallic reflective surface. After 30 seconds it dissolved
while level with the tree tops. The next day a local newspaper printed an article, stating
that a snowball had been sucked into the sky by a tornado about 6 kilometres east of this
sighting.

One evening we observed a peculiar light phenomena from our house in the western part
of Norway. About 350 meters away we observed 3 brightly illuminated triangles in a
pattern that formed a larger triangle. we were 3 people who observed this phenomena that
lasted over an hour.
Krokelvdalen in Troms 16,03,85 l 120pm.
On my way home late in the evening I observed an oval object above a woods on
Trom0ya. The object had bright coloured lights in the centre. It moved indirectly and
slowly towards me. At a distance of about 100 meters I was able to see that it was formed
with 8 sides and had 3 windows. About 8 lights shone from inside the object which I
estimate to have been around 6 x 15 meters.

Atland by Gyland in West-Agder.
 At a quarter past six in the evening during February 1994 they saw a low flying object
following them above their car. On stopping, the object turned left towards the west and
appeared to stop or move directly away from them. The only sound they heard was a faint

John Searl and his flying saucers.
 Condensed from article written by Anders Heerfordt.
In 1991 UFO Norway published an article about John Searl and his alleged invention of
alternative energy motors and flying saucers. His allegations have been met with a good
deal of scepticism. In 1991 John Seal gave speech at a meeting arranged by DIF0T, The
Danish society for the promotion of alternative energy. He claimed that anti gravity and
free energy effects could be produced with special magnets, and that he had constructed
flying saucers. According to him many had witnessed his flying disc. It proved difficult to
trace anyone who had actually seen this phenomena. During his speech he claimed that
Gunnar Sandberg , a scientist at the university of Sussex had witnessed his perpetual
motion device, driven by his magnetic rod and rollers. Gunnar Sandberg was unable to
confirm that he had seen any unusual effects demonstrated by this device. He could
however confirm that Searl had talked about them. It would appear that Searl has not been
completely honest about this demonstration. Mr. Louis Avilio had worked with Searl for 5
years but was unable to substantiate his claims. Searl had told him about his invention but
had never demonstrated it. Some of the alleged experiments were performed in the fifties
and sixties before they were aquainted. In all probability Searl never held any public
demonstrations, and never succeeded in achieving his goal, a flying saucer!

Can we trust our senses? by Odd-Gunnar Raed.
Every day, year in and year out we perceive what we regard as reality. But what do we
really see? Can we trust our perceptions to translate reality or is it possible that we see
things the way we expect them to be. When Captain James Cook anchored his ship off the
coast of New Zealand around 1760 the natives were unable to perceive them before the
crew were almost ashore in their longboats. Having never seen a large sailing ship they
simply had no reference to understand what they were seeing. The result being that the
ship was invisible to them. A person equipped himself with glasses that turned everything
upside down. His picture of reality was inverted for several days until his brain adjusted
and turned everything back to normal. Stories such as these set ones fantasy soaring.
Could it be that UFO phenomena are invisible to most of us simply because they are
outside our reference framework?
Our perception of reality is filtered by cultural background, our own personal way of
looking at things and of course, our mood of the moment. Is it possible the people who
have been exposed to extreme UFO experiences have brought them on by an intense
desire? How can we arrive at the truth if individuel realities are translated by our
subjective frameworks?
The Philadelphia experiment. Condensed from article by Ole Jonny Brrenne.
Our colleague has taken a look at the so called Philadelphia experiment. According to
persistent rumours the last 40 or so years, the USS Eldridge vanished from the port of
Philadephia during an attempt to make the ship radar invisible. Not only did the ship
vanish, but was teleported together with the crew to Norfolk and back. While being
teleported the crew made contact with a non human intelligent species. After the episode
some of the crew suffered from intermittent disappearing, self combustion and
dematerialisation. In 1992 Jaques Val lee came into contact with Edward Dudgen who was
onboard the USS Eldridge in 1943. He was able to tell exactly what happened. He
confirmed that top secret experiments had taken place, the object being to make the ship
invisible to German magnetic torpedoes. The ship was demagnetised and extensively
fitted with secret equipment, among other things radar for detecting submarine periscopes,
and quieter propellers.

discovered by a sheep farmer outside Roswell, New Mexico. The press announcement
was made by lieutenant Walter Haut on orders from base commander Colonel Blanchard.
The next day General Ramsey called a press conference and announced that the alleged
UFO was in fact a weather balloon.

Stephen Schiff demanded an explanation from the US Airforce and was brushed off. The
General Accounting Office was told to investigate the US Airforces handling of the
Roswell incident. The GAO ordered the USAF to write a rapport on the incident. The
report does nothing to inspire confidence in the USAF's credibility. Jesses Marcel testified
in 1978. «I could hardly believe my eyes. There was so much wreckage, I,ve been up in
all sorts of aircraft, seen what there made of, but this thing was never built on our planet.»

extraterrestrial bodies. This type of ridicule has no place in a serious report. Key people
were not conferred with and deceased witnesses testimony has been ignored. No
conclusions can be made from the USAF's report on the Roswell incident, the report
