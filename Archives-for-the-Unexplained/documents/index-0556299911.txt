                                                 Nellis Range Renewal
  Although this page may contain useful links, it is not actively maintained (after 1997) and broken links will not
  be repaired. For more recent links regarding Nellis see the new Nellis AFB page.

  Nellis Air Force Base and the Nellis Range occupies public land that has been temporarily withdrawn for military use.
  The current withdrawal expires in Nov. 2001, and it must be renewed by Congress for the Air Force to continue using
  this land. As part of the renewal application, the Air Force must complete a review of the environmental impacts of
  continued use. This process involves public hearings and culminates in an Environmental Impact Statement.

  The Nellis Renewal EIS is unrelated to the EIS for the neighboring Nevada Test Site, which is considering changes in
  its mission. Neither EIS appears to cover the block of land known as Area 51.
