                                                                              ever have been otherwise?

                                                                              explorer had reported sailing that far south, or sighting any land. The continent
                                                                              was entirely new and unexplored . And yet, there was a curious tradition among
                                                                              medieval and renaissance map makers to label the southern reaches Terra
                                                                              lncognita. Not just unknown ocean, but an unknown land. Even though no
                                                                              one had ever brought back an account of visiting this land, there see med to be a
                                                                              vague certainty that it existed. Legends about the great southern continent told
                                                                              of a temperate climate, bands of animals roaming the jungles and plains, and the

                                                                              only ice. In fact, the ice was so bad it forced him to turn back northward, for fear
                                                                              of becoming trapped. If Antarctica ever was a paradise, it was long ago.
                                                                                    Although almost all maps of the renaissance period are rather poorly
                                                                              drawn, from a geographical point of view, there exist a few examples of a

                                                                               far north or south of the equator you are. But Longitude is far more difficult. It
                                                                              requires the ability to keep track of time very precisely, to within seconds. Since
                                                                              no one in the renaissance had such technology (it was to come in 1799 with the
                                                                              invention of the marine chronometer), the maps drawn from explorers
                                                                              accounts were all hopelessly inaccurate. Just take a look at some old maps of the
                                                                               Americas from this period and you'll see what I mean. All of the continents are
