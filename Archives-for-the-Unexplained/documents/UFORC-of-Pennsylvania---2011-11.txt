           Report a UFO Sighting
To speak with one of our researchers, please send us a
confidential email and include the following information.

     Your name
     Contact phone number or email address
     Date and time of event
     Location (city / county / state)
     Brief description of your sighting
     How many witnesses were involved?
     How close were you to the object?
  Attach any photos, drawings or EVP recordings.

  Email us at butch@paufosearch.com and one of our
  researchers will contact you within 24 hours.

All contacts are strictly confidential and are shared with no
other reporting agencies.
