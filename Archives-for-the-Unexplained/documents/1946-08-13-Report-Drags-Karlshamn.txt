Folke Herin

To the head of the Ankarstierna department.

I hereby respectfully offer a statement regarding the conducted investigation (Date: August 14, 1946) for possible evidence of a so-called "ghost bomb". 

The search was performed by a small patrol consisting of 1 non-commissioned officer, 2 furirs and 10 privates.

The search started at the southeastern part of Dragsö at 13:10 o’clock and ended at 16:40 o’clock, whereupon the entirety of southern Dragsö up to Killingavik had been examined.

At about 14:20 o’clock, a rocket was found in a thicket just south of Killingaviken. It is easy to assume that the rocket had been fired on the evening of August 13 after it stopped raining. The rocket appears new and has not been exposed to water.

This assumption has been established through data which I received by two men on different occasions. Verily, on August 13 these two men had come to observe as a small group of boys were firing rockets from a mountain, located at Saltö, just south of the strait, which separates Dragsö from Saltö.

Karlskrona, August 15, 1946.

O.R. Andersson
Ast.2 gr
Patrol Commander
