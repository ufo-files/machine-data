             "UF O Puzzle for Ambulance Driver" was one of the headlines   'I
from a leading Tasmanian daily newspaper as the June 16th report made
the paper and radio news.
             The driver had taken two people from New Norfolk to Ouse
some 60kmts up the Derwent Valley. On the outward trip when 4 / 5kmts
from Ouse the ambulance lost power for a second or two, the h eadlights
going out briefly. A check under the bonnet at Ouse revealed nothing
so the witness set off alone for base at New Norfolk.
              It was about 22.05 ( 10.05pm ) as the driver reached the
same section of road, again the vehicle lost power, this time it was
more pronounced with the headlights again going out for a few seconds. ·
The motor did not stall but dropped in power then came back to life
again.
              At this point the witness noticed something to his left
on the hilltop. Initially he saw a glow on the hillside above whichwas
a well defined half semi circle shape. At the next glance he could
see two rows of bluish / white lights about four in each row. The lights
blinked twice and then went out. i
              The original dark yellow shape was twice moon sized as
the witness had time to compare it with the moon visible in the south
eastern sky.
