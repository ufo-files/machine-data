          It is somewhat ironic that the proof of remote civilization for which we have been eagerly
searching has been with us always - we simply failed to recognize it because of our own limited know-
ledge. But when scientifically trained people look at the old evidence with "today's eyes," the veil
of ignorance begins to lift and the results are startling.
          We present two reports of new, scientific interpretations of ancient cave drawings, one in
Spain and the other in the United States, both concerned with the field of biology.
