Shooting for profit
on the final frontier
A Las Vegas real-estate billionaire with an active interest in the paranormal,
Robert Bigelow might seem an unlikely candidate to transform the
commercial space industry. Yet he is making an impact. In 2006 his company,
Bigelow Aerospace, launched a small prototype inflatable space station into
orbit around the Earth, followed by a second in 2007. By 2010 he aims to have
full-sized orbiting stations available for lease to paying customers who want to
carry out research in space. He outlines his vision to David Shiga, and explains
how UFOs helped kick it all off
