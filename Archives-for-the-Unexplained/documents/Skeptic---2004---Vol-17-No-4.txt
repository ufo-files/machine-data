     WHEN THE GODS SLIP UP
     The Pope may be infallible, but the Gods themselves can make mistakes just like the rest
     of us. When the time came for the Chinese bonze Tchou Pa- Kiai to be reincarnated, some
     heavenly official had made a boob in the paperwork, and the poor fellow was compelled to
     live his next life half-man, half-pig. No wonder he was liable to periodic fits of irritation
     during which his friends feared for their skins.


     [Unnamed Chinese artist in Henri Dore's Superstitions en Chine, 1914]
