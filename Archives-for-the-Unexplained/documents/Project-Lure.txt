                                                                                MCDILL AIR FORCE BASE, FLORIDA
                                                                                In the stories of crash retrievals, i.e. the retrieval of crashed flying saucers
                                                                                and dead aliens by governments and particularly the United States govern-
                                                                                ment, it is generally held that the discs and bodies are stored at Wright
                                                                                Patterson Air Force Base. However, other locations have been given for
                                                                                the retrieval of other discs in the past and McDill Air Force Base in Florida
                                                                                is one of the more prominent of these.
                                                                                   It has been speculated that public pressure for disclosure about crash
                                                                                retrievals centred on Wright Patterson, caused the authorities to move the
                                                                                material to McDill Air Force Base where some researchers believe it is still
                                                                                held.

                                                                                MCDIVITT,ASTRONAUTJAMES
                                                                                During Gemini 4's flight, astronaut James McDivin reponed seeing the
                                                                                appearance of a cylindrical-shaped object outside the space craft.
                                                                                   Although much has been made of this repon, McDivin has stated that it
                                                                                is not believed it was anomalous, i.e. that it was an 'ordinary' object he
                                                                                simply could not identify at the time.
