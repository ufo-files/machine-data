                           SEASONS GREETINGS
                             TO ONE AND ALL

     MAY 1993 BRING US JOY AND LOTS OF UFO SIGHTINGS


Directors Message :
As hundreds of UFO enthusiasts filed into the huge conference facility at Yokens I was
reminded of how much support we received from our fellow Mufon members. State
Directors, Section Directors, and Members all helping to make our second UFO conference
a huge succes. Many said last year "I don't see how you can top this". Well we did with a
lot of support from all of you. Many thanks from New Hampshire Mufon to each one of
you. We look forward to seeing you next year on September 12th at the same place. Kevin
Mac Leod will again be our host Kevin has always been a strong supporter of NH Mufon
and we owe him and his staff a great deal of thanks.

State Meeting:
Our next state meeting will be at the ManchesterPublic Library in the auditorium. Date is
January 16, 1993. Time: 1:00PM sharp. All are encouraged to attend and bring a friend.

Research Project Completed:
Kathleen Marden has completed a research project on the use and misuse of hypnosis in
UFO investigations. NH Mufon sponsored this worthwhile project which has been
submitted to the Mufon Journal for publication. If you have an idea for a research project
please drop us a note describing your idea We may be able to sponsor you as well.

NASA plans to scramble Mars images:
Richard Hogland world renowned Mars researcher stated NASA plans to scramble data
transmitted from the new Mars probe. Time to write NASA, the White House Your
Senator and anyone else you can think of. This is our money they are spending. WHY
scramble? Could they be afraid of what we will find out, and if so, WHY ? WHl'? WHY? wHYr

Nov. 27 1992           A man living in Goffstown came home after working the night shift
                       At 2:20AM he decided to go outside on his porch. He observed two
white objects moving together heading in a South Easterly direction. He ran into the house
to waken his wife. On the way up a set of stairs he fell making a loud noise. This also
woke his son. They all went outside to watch the objects as they moved off towards
Manchester. The objects were described as egg shaped moving with the large end toward
the front This case is still under investigation and is not completed at this time.

Nov. 27 1992             At about 11:00 PM a man in Portsmouth was writing a letter in his
                         third story apartment After finishing his writing he turned off the
TV set and turned off the lights. At exactly that instant he heaid a machinery grinding
sound. He looked in the direction of the sound and saw a large set of brilliant white and red
lights moving slowly over the apartment building. At that instant the sound stopped. The
trapezoid shaped object could be clearly see n silhouetted against the lighter sky. It is
estimated at about 50 feet wide and 60 feet long. The object had a blinking or pulsating red
light in the center of body. The object was about 15 feet from the witness and about 15 feet
over the roof of the apartment. It slowly glided out of view. This man is a long time
resident of Portsmouth and very familiar with the aircraft in the area including the military
planes from Pease AFB and the presidential helicopters often seen in the area. He is
�itive what he saw was not a conventional aircrclft This case is still under investigation
and is not completed at this time. An interesting side story to this case is at about the time
of his sighting the man's Son who was staying at a residence not far from the apartment had
a strong feeling his Father was calling him.

Dec. 8 1992              A woman in Campton was called to the window of her home by her
                 15 year old daughter to see a UFO. Time was 11: 10 PM. They saw blue and
white lights in the South Eastern sky. Every once in a while they saw a red light The lights
remained at the same distance but did move up and down. Her husband also saw the lights.
She watched until 12:20AM and then went to sleep. Her husband got up at 3:00AM and the
lights were gone. The next night the same patte rn of lights were seen but further away. The
entire pattern of lights pulsated. She could make out a "V" shaped when the lights were
dimmed.
