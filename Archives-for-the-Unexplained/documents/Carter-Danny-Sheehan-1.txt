This is in response to your request of August 2, 2000 addressed to Mary Ronan. Under the
provisions of the Freedom of Inforn1ation Act (FOIA) (5 USC 552), you asked for information
concerning correspondence between Colman Von Keviczky and Jimmy Carter, and President
Carter's requests to ASA to do UFO research .

I cannot grant yo ur request under the terms of the FOIA , because the FOIA applies solely to the
records of Federal agenc ies. The term "agency" is defined in 5 USC 552(t) to include the various
departments and regul atory bodies of the executive branch of the Government.

Within the Executive Offi ce of the President, the White House Office functions primarily to advise
the President. It is therefore not considered an agency under this definition. The materials to which
you seek access fa ll within the definition of "Presidential historical materials" that is given in the
basic legislation on archi val administration that was in effect during the Carter administration.
According to this legislation, Presidential historical materials consist of "books, correspondence,
documents, papers, pamphlets, works of art, models, pictures, sound recordings, and other objects
or materials having historical or commemorative value" of a President or former President of the
United States (44 USC 2 101 ). The legislation recognized the President's right to dispose of his
materials by authori zing the Archivist of the United States to accept Presidential historical materials
for deposit in a Presidential archival depository and to make the materials available to the public
strictly in accordance with the donor's deed of gift (44 USC 2111 and 2112). It declared that the
material s are "subject to restrictions as to their availability and use stated by the donors or
depositors," and that the restri ctions are to be observed "for the period stated, or until revoked or
terminated by the donors or deposi tors or by per ons legally qualified to act on their behalf' (44 USC
2 l l 2(c)). Beginning with the Reagan Admini strati on, access to Presidential materials is governed
by the Pres identi al Record s Ac t (44 USC 2201 ).
