Victor Harbour Times (SA : 1932 - 1986), Friday 26 June 1970, page 6



               Victor Rural Youth
              At last Tuesday's meeting
        of the Victor                   Harbour          Rural
        Youth  Club, Mr. Colin                        Norris
        from   the  Australian                        Flying
        Saucer    Society   was                          guest
        speaker.
          There                 are    many   societies
        all     over            the    world, he said,
        including Japan, England, and
        America, who                    publish maga
        zines recording sightings and
        photos of flying saucers  and
        U.F.O.'s. Certain instruments
        have been devised to detect
        the presence   of flying saucers
        when  in flight, also after they
        have landed. Mr. Norris saw
        a     saucer-type object hovering
        over        an      aerodrome              in West
        ern  Australia  in 1952.
          Anyone between       the ages
        of 15 to 25 is invited to attend
        Rural   Youth    meetings. The
        next meeting, TV games,     will
        be      held       on         the   14th    July       at
       the C.W.A.    Hall   at                       8    p.m.'

        contact             Mary            Dack,        phone
