Dear Grant ,

I received your long letter, and wante d to write and thank
you for ta king so much time to talk about your experiences
with Charl ie Redstar and balls of lights. I now understand
much more what you experienced.

I didn't understand that Redstar and the BOLs wer e different
phenomena ; now I know. An object that pulses like a heart , is
estimated at 75-100' in diameter and glows in flight isn ' t
manmade aircraft. On the other hand, the balls of light pose
a different problem. I can see where Chris Rutkowski gets his
skepticism, and in several letters from him, I have detected
undue caution. All I can say is that the images of the balls
of light on a few slides he sent to me exactly match those on
slides from Yakima . As far as I'm concerned, the two are the
same phenomenon. If we posit intelligence to your balls of
light, it is quite c onceivable the phenomenon anticipates
human thought and its endless reasoning, and thus hovers
motionless, implying a LATER or optical illusion.I kn o w that
 sounds bizarre, but one can cite numerous UFO experiences
where the phenomenon vanishes as a camera is bought to bear
on it , or blends in with the stars , or bears resemblance to
manmade aircraft (the numerous sightings of disc-like o b jects
that flash red, green, and other colored lights bear witness
to such a theory) . Also, Harley Rutledge experienced UFOs
 that formed "false" constellations in the sky, or appeared to
mimic natural phenomena. I 'm not an optical physicist , but
 the orange balls of light I investigate seem intensely
 glowing and energetic , almost on fire; I don't know how
 "over- the -horizon " refraction works , beyond a general
 introduction to inversions and atmospheric refraction, but if
 the BOL s were observed during sightings of Charlie Redstar ,
 why can ' t both phenomena be associated? Chris is very brigh t
 and I respect his thoughts , but I think his short visits to
the sighting area s aren't enough ...

When you mention other areas where the BOLs have been seen ,
are these near Winnipeg? Are they the same color , same
behavior? Photos?

You asked about the challenge to Persinger regarding areas
where UFOs aren't seen. Actually, I'm the one who is about to
challenge Derr and Persinger. Mark Rodeghier of CUFOs has
shown interest in supporting this. I suppose it's better to
call it a "test," rather than a "challenge."

What I intend on doing is asking Persinger and Derr to select
two areas in the United States where earthquake activity is,
      in one area , minimal if not nonexistent for the production of
     BOLs; in the other , a sufficient level to cause electrical
     fields for ionization of at mospheric gases. A number of
     physical conditions have to be applied to the areas ,
     including basic isolation fro m outside activity in the case
     of the tectonically quiet area , as well as (ideally ) the
    presence of natural boundaries , such as rivers , mountains ,
    etc. , to isolate the " window " area . Other conditions are a
    sufficiently large population size to provide UFO witnesses ,
    as well as the presen ce of UFO investigators. The latter may
    be difficult in that , as Chris points out , less tectonically
    active areas are usually devoid of population. Nonetheless ,
   this challenge to test Persinger ' s theory is in the works . If
   his theory holds , then there should not be UFO reports in
   tectonically inactive areas ; in active areas, there should be
   UFO s, including orange BOLs--MOST IMPORTANTLY, ORANGE BOLs .
   I ' ll let you know what happens ....

  I understand by the way that Michael Persinger will appear on
  a 2 - hour " l ive" special on the UFO coverup this Friday,
  October 14 , as the resident skeptic. (I think Chris told me
  thi s.)

  Oh ye s, good to hear Chris has come up with a calculation as
  to the energy requirement for generating BOLs. The biggest
  weakness in Persinger's theory is the physics of BOL
  produc tion .

  Have you by any chance come across reports of so-called
  "underground sounds" in these BOL areas y ou're familiar with,
  as well as Bigfoot reports?

 My orange ball of light research now encompasses sifting
 through sighting reports going back to B.C. (before Christ)
 and to present. I'm going to track down all cases of orange
 BOL sightings with multiple witnesses and high strangeness. A
tedious proce ss, but necessary to establish the historical
precedence for reports of the beast. (Of course, Persinger

quakes in the area.") As you pointed out, Persinger is

Wel l , let me close for now. I hope you can squeeze in time to
