10th March 2008…

UFO Expert To Speak Thursday…

   The latest in UFO sightings will be offered by an international expert Thursday evening.
   James P. Carrion, international director of the Mutual UFO Network will be the guest speaker at a Thursday meeting a the Western Sizzlin - Steakhouse, 3033 8th Ave., Evans. Dinner will be at 6 p.m., with the program at 7 p.m. Participants may buy dinner on their own, admission to the meeting is $3.
   Carrion has been the international director of MUFON for the past two years. He has implemented new technologies to enhance the UFO investigation process for the organization. He has investigated several UFO sightings and is known as one of the  most qualified investigators in the network.
   He will talk about a recent UFO sighting near Stephenville, Texas, as well as other extraordinary UFO events.
