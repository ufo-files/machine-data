           This issue of "Smear " is a bit different from most, coming as it
     does yapping at the heels of the last issue. We decided that the only
     way to find Space for our backlog of cartoons & other such Goodies, was
     to put out two issues in a row. How else could we give you, for example,
     the charming tribute to Tim Beckley ( "Mr. UFO") on the back page, herein?
           So, instead of the usual predominance of gossip and letters, we've
     included the cream of the "fillers " that have been piling up here at Head­
     quarters.
           And, speaking of Headquarters, remember to use our new Florida mail­
     ing address above for all communications, including periodicals, etc!

           Our next issue probably won't be published until after the glorious
     forthcoming 21st annual National UFO Conference (NUFOC), scheduled for
     September 21 & 22 in Cleveland, Ohio. Speakers there will include Gray
     Barker, the Barf of Clarksburg, W. Va; Ed Biebel, speaking on recent UFO
     sightings in his home state of Arizona;   Pete Mazzola, Director of the
     Scientific Bureau of Investigation (SBI);   a contactee named   Madeline
     Teague, speaking on "The Contactee Syndrome ";  Al Manak, giving the re­
     sults of a 3-year UFOCOM (computer) study of "light source UFOs"; Charles
     Wilhelm of OUFOIL; Ron Schaffner, speaking on "Sasquatch and Alien Hom­
     inid Hoaxes ", who will debate with Larry Peters of Maryland, a pro-Bigfoot
     speaker whose topic is "The Truth About the Rome, Ohio Bigfoot Affair".
           The inimitable John Keel of New York City either will or will not
     put in an appearance at the convention (We don't know yet!); and last but
     certainly not least, your humble "Smear " editor will be present throughout
     the festivities as Master of Ceremonies.
           For motel reservations or further information of any kind, write as
     soon as possible to: Bob Easley, 3001 Colburn Ave., Cleveland, Ohio 44109.
     His telephone number is 216-741-9388.
           SEE YOU AT THE CON!

      GEORGE EARLEY leads off with:
      "How convenient of Charles Wilhelm, now that the ma jor participants
are no longer living, to 'discover' that Palmer and Arnold faked the June
24, 1947 sighting. Where were Wilhelm's investigative 'talents' when both
men were alive? "

      And, KEN BEHRENDT writes:
      "I have become aware that there is a new mentality arising in ufol­
ogy which we can use as a barometer of the decline of our field. As the
disintegration continues, it seems that there is now a tendency to re­
examine various 'classic' cases that are the foundation of the sub ject,
and cast doubts on their authenticity. There is growing distrust of any
sort of authority, as demonstrated by the growing interest in government
conspiracy theories and the resistance to unifying efforts such as those
offered by NAUFOF. Sub ject advancement seems to have given way to media
exploitation and the cult of the bizarre. These are all signs of an area
of inquiry that is in deep trouble, for internal problems are always caus­
ed by external problems.
      "The ma jor external problem we suffer from is the drop off in large
scale sightings since the '70's, and I think that even this can be at­
tributed to sinister 'silencer' type activities! Isn't it strange that
the drop off in UFO sightings seems to be related to the increase in man­
made junk now in orbit about our planet? It is no longer necessary for
alien mother ship pilots to bring their leviathan craft into our atmos­
phere to discharge scout craft to examine the surface for signs of intel­
ligent life; they can now tell at a distance in space, and probably would
not want to risk running into an orbital mine to get a closer look; and so
we ufologists have less and less case data to work with!
      "Besides cleaning up the internal problems that plague our field,
maybe our near space could use a good sweeping, too."

      Those of you with Total Recall will remember that two or three is­
sues back, we promised to eventually review a new UFO contactee-type book
called "Flying Saucers from Khabarah Khoom", published.by Gray Barker and
written by one Dominick Lucchesi of Jersey City, N.J.
      We have hesitated to keep our promise, not only because of lack of
Space but also because, quite frankly, this may well be the most confusing
UFO tome ever written! The apparent starting point for the author was in
the summer of 1978, when he supposedly made a number of trips to Pennsyl­
vania to investigate flying saucer sightings. There Lucchesi met a sur­
veyer he calls "Mr. H", who had chanced upon a grounded craft while walk_­
ing alone in a deserted area. Somehow "H" also met the pilot of the craft,
and eventually introduced Lucchesi to this entity, at a diner in Jim Thorpe
(= name of a town!), Pa. The pilot's name is "EONE", and most of the text
in Lucchesi's book is devoted to (imaginary?) renderings of long conversa­
tions he had with Eone off & on during the years 1978-1981.
      Eone, as you may have guessed by now, is a human-like Highly Evolved
Being of some sort. He comes from Khoom, which is a group of complex,
very ancient caverns somewhere under an undisclosed spot in South America.
The people of Khoom manufacture and pilot machines we would call flying
saucers, and in general they have a higher technology than we do..  Their
main aim is to be left alone - which makes it hard to understand why their
craft are �een so often!
      Then there is The Group, composed of basically evil beings who live
in one or more "Sky I slands" in orbit high above the Earth. They too have
saucers (or "Sky Craft", as called here), and they control the World in
various indirect ways. The people of Khoom have wisely decided not to
have any direct confrontation with The Group. Though these "good guys"
from Khoom would win, human society would disintegrate, for various rea­
sons.
      The book is padded with a large number of well-executed drawings,
meaningless mathematical-type gibberish (see below!), and other illustra­
tions. (No photos, however.) There are also a number of examples of Luc­
chesi's rendering of the Tarot cards. Hopefully these are not connected
with the text, as we remember seeing a couple of them in Lucchesi's apart­
ment many years ago.
