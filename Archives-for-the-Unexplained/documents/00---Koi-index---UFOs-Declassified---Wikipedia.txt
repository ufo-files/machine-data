  UFOs Declassified is a Canadian television series that
  premiered on January 9, 2015, on the History Canada                                                 UFOs Declassified
  channel.[1] Produced by Company X Studios, the program
  features declassified CIA and US Air Force documents
  pertaining to notable UFO reports.[2]


  Production
  The show was created and produced by Wayne Abbott and
  John Moores.[2] UFOlogists Nick Pope, Leslie Kean, Ben
  Hansen and Donald Schmitt appear in the first season of the
  series.[2][3]
