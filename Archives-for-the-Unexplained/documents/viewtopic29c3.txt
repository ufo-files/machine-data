  Hi. I've just joined this forum. I was born in Ipswich and used to visit Bentwaters as a child with my
  parents who has friends on the base. Thats where they got a taste for ten pin bowling. During the 70's I
  was driving lorries carting asphalt for the Wickham Market by-pass. I was told whilst there about huge
  underground complexes which were to be constructed in the area. I now live in London but as my mother
  still lives in Ipswich. I visit regularly and often make a pilgrimage to Rendlesham forest.

  Hi Deep Purple

  I was dropping asphalt onto a road surfacing plant constructing the Wickham Market by-pass. It was
  summer 1976 and as it was very hot and dry I was asked to work all the hours God sent to get it finished,
  staying on site till 11pm some days. I was told whilst there by someone who was on very close terms with
  the road laying gangs that at the end of the job a lot of them had decided to give up the caravan travelling
  around the country from site to site. They had decided to settle in the area around Wickham Market. When
  I probed a bit I was told that they had signed 3 year contracts to build a top secret underground complex
  and that they had been paid enough in advance to enable them to put down deposits on houses....
