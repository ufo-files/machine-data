 Barraba, population 1350, lies s66km north of Sydney in gently rolling countryside,
 famous for its fine grade wool. The region is a diverse, ancient landscape with complex
 geological faulting. Asbestos is no longer mined at Woods Reef 10km east of the town,
 but nearby diatomaceous earth deposits make the region Australia's 'kitty-litter capital'.
 Forty kilometres west is Mount Kaputar National Park- 37,ooo hectares of old-growth
 and regrowth forest on eroded volcanic peaks.
