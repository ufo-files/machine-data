;    SUBJECT:   DoD Program on Remote Viewing (S/ORCON)

     1.   (S/ORCON) Enclosed for your information are the minutes
     of a meeting held in Dr. Vorona's office on 24 August 1978,
     the primary intent of which was to establish a program in
     remote viewing for intelligence purposes. Current require-
     ments and funding are uncoordinated; however, a unified
     DoD/Services program would significantly improve the
     efficiency of the U.S. effort, reduce redundant research
     and make results·available equally to the DoD and Services.

     2.  (U) Copies of the minutes have been sent to the ACSI's
     of the respective Services and their comments have been
     solicited. Should you wish to comment on the minutes or
     make any suggestions regarding the program, please forward        SG1J
