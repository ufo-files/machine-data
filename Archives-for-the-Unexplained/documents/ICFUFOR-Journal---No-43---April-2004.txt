ICFUFOR Journal files No 43 April 2004


  The Chanel2 TV broadcast of the program “Foreign Guests”/29 February, 2004


  The program produced by Ilan Shushan , from “Elza” studios, and broadcasted on TV, channel 10. This broadcast was a
  breakthrough from the explanatory aspect, and from the fact that matters discussed there in a way, never saw on Israeli
  television.

  First - we could observe the video filmed by Oron Sultan from Beit-Shemesh (Journal No. 41, October 2003). It was an
  important videotape. We clearly see in it unidentified flying objects. In addition, we can hear the sound of these UFOs’.

  Second - for the first time, we hear the response of the IDF Spokesman, referring to the UFOs’ phenomena in Israel.

  Third – an international importance: the response of the former IDF Air Force commander, Ret.Gen. Avihu Ben-Noon,
  responding to the videotape.

   Ret.Gen. Avihu Ben-Noon said, while watching Oren Sultans’ videotape: “Its, no doubt, a thing I never saw something
  like it, and I’m not familiar with such a thing. We can’t see here assembly logics. I don't recognize any of these crafts.
  I’m not familiar with such an aircraft with a sound like this. The sound isn’t a helicopter sound nor an airplane sound. If
  UFO’s do exist, it might be a UFO. Anyway, it’s interesting, and I recommend investigating it. Meaning, giving the air
  force to investigate it” – end quote.

  This is the IDF spokesman response, given to the program editor: “The Israeli air force isn’t familiar with any unit,
  which investigates UFO’s. The Israeli air force does anything it can; to protect Israeli skies with its control units. There
  is a possibility, that in these areas was airplane activity, and airplanes were seen, considering the fact that these areas are
  under the air force courses. The Israeli air force doesn't recognize the existence of UFO’s.”

  Ha’chof Road, between Hadera and Natanya/ 2 February, 2004


  Yehudit from Haifa claims: “My husband, me and our two children, drove from Haifa to the south. At 16:50 or at 17:00,
  when we were in the road sector between Hadera and Natanya, the children shouted for us to look. We looked to the
  west, and we saw something orange and elongated coming down from the sky. Shining diagonally from its’ top to its
  bottom.

  It changed its’ direction and started to fly horizontally. From our point of view, it was 1 meter in size and its’ width was
  10 centimeters. It didn't fly quickly, and it looked like a bright orange trail of fire. It entered into the clouds and went out
  of them. We saw it for 2 minutes, until it disappeared inside a big cloud”.

  Kfar Hannanya/ 12 March, 2004


  The settlement is located next to Kibbutz Parud, next to the junction leading to Tsfat and Kiriat-Shmona.

  The Bloom family was the witnesses to this event: the mother Smadar , the father, the two boys, the grandfather and the
  grandmother.

  The father, who left the house around 19:30, saw something very bright in the sky, in north-west direction. He said it
  wasn't a star and was very unusual in that area. The object wasn't there before, as long as they live there (for the last half
  year). The father called all the family members, who also witnessed the phenomenon. Afterwards, he took his video
  camera, put it on a tripod and videotaped the object.

  We notice in the videotape, that the UFO seems to come closer and moves away. Around it, we see shades of red and
  purple. We can also observe some kind of a “pulse of light”. Sometimes we see around it shades of green. In its top and
  bottom, we can see something like wicks.

  When I came to the area (Abi Greif), I spoke with the witnesses and watched the videotape, filmed a day before.

  Afterwards, I looked out of the window to the spoken area, and saw in the sky a very bright object, bigger and lower
  then a common star. It had a yellow shade, different from the color of stars.

  For an hour, along with the family members, we noticed as the UFO reduced its light many times, turns to a “dot”,
  disappears and reveals again. It happened in unequal intervals of time, and happened also when the object was in clear
  sky, without any clouds. At 21:00, in my way back home, when I looked at that sky sector, from the location of Zvulun
  Valley, and afterward from the Carmel hillside, I didn't see anything at the sky.

  That night, the object was videotaped again by the family’s father. The UFO was in a direct line from Kfar Hannanya,
  above and in direction of the settlements Moran, Ramah and in direction of Nahariya.

  On the 14 of March 2004, the family told me they observed that UFO in their way from Golany Junction at 20:00. The
  UFO wasn't seen at 21:00.

  On the 17 of March 2004, from 19:30-21:00, Shimon Rosenberg observed the same sky sector, from his location in
  Ma’alot, but didn't see anything.

  The sun was located right to me, at elevated angle of about 40?, and was hidden by a tree. The flying object, which was
  very small (because of the altitude), moved steadily, in moderated speed.

  Next to its location, where some white clouds, who moved in the different direction because of the wind (from north-
  west to south-east).
