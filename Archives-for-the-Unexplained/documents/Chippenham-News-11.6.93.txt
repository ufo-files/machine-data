CHIPPENHAM NEWS
-Trowbridge-
11 JUN 1993
UFO line set up
EXTRA-terrestrials can now make contact
via a special UPO hotline - in Warminster.
Ufologist Mr Ken Rogers, who recently
returned to the town to establish a centre
for studying strange sightings, has set up
the UFO hotline in his Station Road home.
The hotline gives an update of the latest
sightings and allows spotters to record a
message about their own experiences.
Mr Rogers believes fires, floods and other
strange happenings in the Warminster area
can all be blamed on UFOs.
