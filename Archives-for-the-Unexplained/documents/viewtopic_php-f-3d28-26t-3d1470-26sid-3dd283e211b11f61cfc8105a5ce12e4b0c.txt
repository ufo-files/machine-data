  Ry:

  I Totally agree with your assessment of RU. I guess what I was getting at with the "we're all whackjobs" thing was this:

  Intended Purpose / Intended Use, (A little concept I picked up at the Culinary Institute of America) and that's where the
  rub is.

  In the case of forums, intended purpose would be the "mission statement", intended use is what really goes on. Here at
  RU purpose and use seem to closely reflect each other. There's very little "clutter" here, very little "noise"... sure, we all
  go off on tangents from time to time, but for the most part it's a tight ship. This facilitates research greatly.

  A site like ATS, on the other hand, has a very similar mission statement as RU... They claim to be seeking the truth in
  regards to alternative topics, that they deny ignorance, they are serious, grown up, etc. but the actual Use doesn't really
  reflect that very well.

  It's like... opening a Haute Cuisine restaurant and seeking a five star rating in Zaggat... you have a good location, a good
  staff, a good menu, everything is fancy and high class... but your clientèle is blue collar folks in work clothes, families
  with small children, the geriatric crowd... it's a noisy environment full of people who don't really appreciate or
  understand the setting or the cuisine. NOT the kind of place you want to take your date to after the opera, ya know?

  The owners basically have two choices:

  used to hang out at about 15 years ago: the Interactive Fantasy Network. At IF.net "mission statement" and actual use
