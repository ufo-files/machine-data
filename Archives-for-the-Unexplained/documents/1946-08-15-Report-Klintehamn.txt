16/8

Light phenomena report

On the fifteenth of August, whilst relaxing in the comfort of his home in the community of Klintehamn (2,9 mil SSW of Visby) at around 21:52 o’clock, Methodist Pastor Lindgren observed a radiant ball with a tail in its lower section. It had traveled from north to south at an altitude of about 200 meters and was allegedly considerably faster than any man-made aircraft. Additionally, yet another light phenomenon, sharing the same exact appearance, had arrived shortly afterwards.
