A Place in the Desert for New Mexico's Most Exclusive
Circles
November 27, 2005

By Richard Leiby
Secret Flying Saucer Base Found in New Mexico?
Maybe. From the state that gave us Roswell, the epicenter of UFO lore since 1947, comes a report from an Albuquerque TV station
about its discovery of strange landscape markings in the remote desert. They're etched in New Mexico's barren northern reaches,
resemble crop circles and are recognizable only from a high altitude.
