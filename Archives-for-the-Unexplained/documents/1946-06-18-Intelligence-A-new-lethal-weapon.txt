June 18: A  new lethal weapon...

Swedish newspapers distributed this gloomy American preview of the future:

”TOOTH-PASTE  TUBE -- NEW DANGEROUS WEAPON. RADIO-CONTROLLED PROJECTILE  DROWNS TARGET WITH MOLTEN METAL. Washington the 18th (TT from Reuter). Major General Everatt Hughes of the American Army has revealed some details about a terrible weapon yet in the stages of experiment but expected to be the answer to the nuclear bombs or rockets with nuclear weapons. It is the question of a rocket, fired from an airplane and then controlled by radar or electronic means toward its taget. As the projectile comes close it starts to eject molten metal almost like when you press tooth-paste out of a tube.” (Sydöstra Sveriges Dagblad, June 19, Umebladet, June 22).
