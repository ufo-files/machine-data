  edit to our posts or when a mod edited our posts at the bottom of our posts would be the line:

  (edited on [DATE] by [person who edited])

  and that line was repeated for each edit... so if you made five different edits you'd see that line five times with the
  appropriate date/time stamp for each edit.

  That "HectorRmz" persons edits did NOT have a correctly formated Date/Time stamp, the edit line formated out as a
  Link (instead of just a simple statement that an edit had been made) And the Link went nowhere.

  I had NOT noticed this "glitch" in the system Anywhere Else at that place and felt it was "suspicious", ergo I mentioned
  it in the private "Fair Skeptics" forum.

  Someone, I believe lost_shaman or maybe armap said either in the private FS forum or via PM (can't remember) that
  they Had seen a few "glitched" edit lines like that in other threads over there but not many and that it was very rare.

  No mod or site owner addressed the query.

  I was banned before I could examine the "glitch" in any detail.

  As far as the line actually high lighting as a Link, that is NOT a curio, at least, not to me, it has to do with the colon
  followed by double backslashes. Hypertext Markup Transfer Language is trying to parse the "phrase" as a URL , thus
  "linky" behavior...

  At the time my concern Was Not that the "links" went nowhere (I knew why they went nowhere) but rather that it
  seemed to me that that persons (and only that persons) date/time stamp was not accurately reflecting reality...

  I suspected hanky panky and wanted to draw a little attention to it to see what would happen *shrugs*
