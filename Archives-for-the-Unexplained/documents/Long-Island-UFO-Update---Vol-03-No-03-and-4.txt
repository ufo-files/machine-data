     On the night of February 5th, 1992 over this small
Pennsylvania city another chapter in the Hudson Valley Boomerang
phenomenon would occur which would be witnessed by at least
16 witnesses in that locale. The principle witness, who reported
the bulk of the information to the Long Island U.F.O. Network,
was a Mr. John Reitz who lives on Lenker Ave •.
     The incident began at 6:30 P.M. when Mr. Reitz reported
he heard a low rumbling noise while he was watching a taped
T.V. program on his VCR in his upstairs bedroom. As he was
watching the television, the noise became more discernible and
louder. He compared the sound to the noise that a large multi-
engined jet aircraft would make as it was approaching low over
an area. As he noticed the sound growing louder, his whole house
began to vibrate with the reverberation from the noise. At one
point, he touched the wall of his bedroom and noticed the
vibration was coming through the wall. Alarmed and curious,
he ran downstairs to the front steps of his home. When he reached
the front steps, he noticed that the front arc light over the
drive way was out ( it would later come on after the object
passed).

Natched three large lights in a triangular formation moving

to be of a unique brilliance. He could see no definitive
structure connecting the lights. Whatever it was seemed to blend
in with the night sky. The object as it passed overhead blocked
JUt the night stars and there was enough light available for
him to discern some form of a dark triangular structure now
connecting the lights. It was huge. By his own estimate he would
later that night tell LIUFON's Hotline that the object was three
football fields in width.
      He yelled at this point for his mother to leave the house
and join him. She exited the house only to ask him what it was
he wanted of her. Apparently, she did not see the object. Mr.
Reitz raced back into the house in an effort to retrieve his
binoculars and arrived outside with them to observe the object
heading over a nearby hill. The base of the hill was only some
150 feet from his back yard. The object was very low. He
estimated the altitude to be between 150 to 200 feet above
ground. The speed of the craft was so slow; the witness estimated
a jogger could have kept pace with it.
       As the object flew low over the hill, Mr. Reitz noticed
the lights' reflection on the hill's surface was so intense
that it illuminated the forest on the slopes so that individual
trees and their branches could be seen from where he was
standing. Once over the hill, the sound from the object began
to diminish and to dissipate. In another instant, it was gone
 from sight.
       Mr. Reitz's reaction was to call both friends and neighbors
 in the area in an effort to establish other witnesses to the
event. No one could claim to have seen it.
       Next, Mr. Reitz contacted the local Police in Sunbury to
determine if there had been other witnesses. The Police informed
him they had received no other reports.
       Mr. Reitz then began to call the local airport and FAA
 Officials. He obtained no verification of other sightings. He
 then remembered reading about LIUFON in, "UFO Universe". He
 telephoned Chairman John Ford of the event and a report was
 taken along with a tape recorded statement. Mr. Reitz.was very
 cooperative and allowed LIUFON to use his name with the report.
 He gave enough information to launch an investigation. Since
 LIUFON did not have any investigators stationed in Pennsylvania,
 most of the investigation was done by phone or by mail. Mr.
 Reitz proved to be extremely helpful providing a written report
 and the drawings shown here in the newsletter. It also came
 to pass there were other witnesses to the sighting. Mr. Reitz
 would provide information of the existence of some sixteen other
 witnesses. The news media would also bring the incident to public
 attention.

 the sighting reported the next day the story of area residents
 seeing low flying lights. The station also stated for the record

 One novel explanation was provided by a local College professor
 who stated the object to have been the Soyuz Space Station which
 was over the Eastern Seaboard that night. The station admitted
  that it could provide no explanation for the sighting.

                                         THE CHERRY HILL, NEW JERSEY SIGHTING:

                    LIUFON is indebted to two of its members, Peter and Stewart
               Kandel, for informing LIUFON of this sighting. The incident
               occurred approximately one half hour after the 6:30 P.M. sighting
               of the large Boomerang Object in Sunbury, Pennsylvania by John
               Reitz and sixteen other witnesses. The Kandel Brothers had a
               close friend who is a Pakistani resident alien informed them
               of this sighting and they urged him to speak to LIUFON concerning
               the case. He was rather reluctant and did not file a written
               or taped recorded report, but spoke on our Hotline the night
               after the sighting to inform LIUFON of what he saw on the
               condition of total confidentiality. His fear was that his
               resident alien status might be jeopardized with the government
               for reporting what transpired.
                    He informed LIUFON that he was traveling back to Queens
               from visiting relatives in New Jersey that day. He was on a
               major highway just outside of Cherry Hill traveling Eastbound.
               It was a very clear night with excellent visibility. In the
               car with him was his wife and two children, who were enjoying
               the ride home.
                    About five miles West of Cherry Hill which is South of
               Sunbury, Pennsylvania, he noticed at an extreme altitude which
               he could not determine at an unspecified extreme distance a
               large, illuminated crescent shape object of immense size hovering
               in the sky. He first thought it was the moon, but the object
was much brighter than the moon and had many small white lights
on its surface illuminating its structure He was startled by
what he saw and realized that it was not the moon, but something
else. He watched it for several minutes while he continued to
drive and noticed the object was much nearer than the moon.
It was too large top be an aircraft and the object made no
movements and just stayed in one position which was due East.

     He also noticed that there appeared to be several aircraft
which were passing close by the object and they appeared to
be commercial jets. The planes were much smaller than the object,
but they seemed to be circling the object as though they were
watching it. His impression at this point was that the planes
might not have been commercial planes, but military aircraft.
     As he continued to drive, he eventually lost sight of the
object and continued his journey. When he reached home he
informed his two friends of his experience and the rest is part
of the report. He could not judge the size, altitude or distance
of the object, but he did describe that it had a dull metal
gray texture which was illuminated by the bright lights.
     LIUFON checked with the FAA through Dick Ruhl and could
obtain no confirmation of anything strange reported to Aviation
Authorities in that area that night. However, the FAA sometimes
does not cooperate or it does not necessarily gets reports.
In light of the extensive activity that night in and around
Sunbury and Southern New Jersey of Boomerang objects, it is
LIUFON's opinion that the sighting is of a unknown and may have
been part of the sightings that night. That is how the object
is listed in LIUFON's report for that incident.
