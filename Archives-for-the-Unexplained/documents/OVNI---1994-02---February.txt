Mystery and not a 1 ittle confusion continues to surround the
reports of flying triangular craft over various parts of the
world.

In this issue we have included some of the latest information
to come from the USA on a new top-secret aircraft, the XR-7 spy
plane.   This aircraft may well be responsible for reports of
the mysterious triangular aircraft which is now known as the
"Aurora".   It does in fact closely resemble the sketch made by
a Scottish oil rig engineer (and ex-aircraft observer) a few
months ago. The black triangular aircraft was spotted flying
over the North Sea, in company with two F1 1 1 's whi1 e being
refuelled from a tanker aircraft.

Although the XR-7 resembles the mysterious "Flying Triangle" in
as much that it is black and triangular, it is there that all
resemblance ends.

According to the Testor Corporation (model manufacturer's), the
XR-7 is launched from the back of an SR-75, which alone denotes
that the aircraft has a high stalling speed.    If this is the
case, could the XR-7 be responsible for meandering silently
over Bakewell, Derbyshire 1 ast year, at a speed of 1 ess than
40m.p.h.?   I don't think so!

Would a top secret aircraft also fly over a built-up area and
attract attention by· carrying an assortment of high intensity
lights, so bright that they lit up the ground below!

The triangular shape of both mystery craft can only be a
coincidence.   The  Bakewell  sighting  closely resembles the
triangular craft seen over Belgiurn for severa1 months and at
one time   chased by F1 6 interceptor aircraft.         Although
subsequent   photographs  showed  a   triangular  shape   (after
computer enhancement), the craft had clipped wings/tips and the
XR-7 does not.

We are still awaiting a full "Flying Triangle" report from Ron
West of the Essex UFO Group.   Once this is to hand we wi11 be
able to compare notes on any similarities between the Essex and
Bakewell sightings.  Ran assures us that the report is nearing
completion and will be with us soon  • watch this space!

                     THE JOHN SEARL ENIGMA

We are regularly contacted by other UFO magazine editors with a
request to exchange publications.      The 11 Nexus New Times11
(published in Australia and also distributed in the USA)
recently arrived with a request for an exchange.

The Nexus is a professionally well produced magazine containing
a number of interesting articles by several well known authors
such as Zecharia Sitchen and Armen Victorian.

One name caught your editors eye and that was in the "Letters
to the Editor" page and the name was 11Professor J.R.R.Searl".

The letter referred to an article that had appeared in a
previous issue on the work of 11Prof" John R.R.Searl and the SEG
(Searl Effect Generator) that will power unmanned craft.

Could this be the same John Searl that first came to public
notice in 1 967 when he called a press conference in the village
hall at Mortimer, Berks. to show off his "Flying Saucer".

It was such an important occasion that numerous reporters from
the national press (along with your editor) turned up at the
village hall on 1 7th September 1 967.

We all sat quietly in front of the drawn curtains, impatiently
waiting for John's revelation to be made. He could explain all
of the flying saucer reports, he had claimed.    They were all
his craft (the Swallow series), travelling up to 40,000mph and
able to fly to the Moon and back in 58 hours. The disc shaped
craft were controlled from the tool shed behind his counci 1
house

As John chattered on the audience began to fidget, after all,
reporters had deadlines to meet and photographers were waiting
to rush their shots off to Fleet Street.         (no satellite
transmitters then! )  But wait, what was that, John said there
had been a hitch, some vital component parts had not arrived
from the factory and he would not be able to demonstrate the
craft today!

However all was not lost, for John was able to draw back the
curtains and show the audience a cardboard mock-up of what the
craft would look like when it was finished (see photo).

We would all know they were John's flying saucers when we saw
them.   He explained that they would all carry his personal
insignia badge on the front.

Several members of the audience groaned audibly and began to
leave.   John continued, this was the instrument consul that
would control the craft from "Swallow H.Q."

We recently published reports in the O V NI involving possible
UFO activity in the vicinity of Iceland, as yet unconfirmed.

The Winter issue of the FSR magazine (Flying Saucer Review) has
included a late 11Stop Press11    article which concerns UFO
activity in the North Polar region:-

According to a radio 11 ham11 report, Radio Moscow stated that on
August  5th   (9 3) , five    Russian  scuba  divers,  presumably
somewhere in or near the Gulf of Finland (N.E. Baltic) , came
across an enormous submarine shaped craft beneath the water.
They followed it for some time and then had been astounded to
see it shoot out of the sea and vanish silently at lightning
speed into the sky.

The FSR continues: Finally it must be noted that in December
1 992 there were secret Icelandic nava 1 reports of three UFO • s
that were tracked descending and entering the sea          near
Lengeness, on the eastern coast of Iceland.

On the following day. Icelandic trawlermen fishing off the
north-east coast saw several huge rapidly moving underwater
craft with flashing coloured lights. Also seen was an enormous
glowing airborne object, north of Scotland and travelling
towards the south.

The scared trawlermen called for protection and Icelandic
coastguard vessels were despatched to the region.     There have
continued to be other reports of UF O's entering the sea.

In February of 1993, a flotilla of U.S. Navy destroyers had
been in the Arctic Circle and actively engaged in secret
operations.   Ships outside the exclusion zone in which the
destroyers were operating are reported to have detected by
radar, numerous craft in the air above the American warships
and "yellowish lights" were observed above the ships.

Later in April there was mention in the British press and T V
media of "joint U.S. and Russian military and naval exercises"
about to take place and there was mention of plans to land U.S.
troops at Tiksi, in the Russian Arctic Circle, on the shore of
the Laptev Sea.

airliner was joined over Scotland by two huge glowing spheres
and in May (93) , two Icelandic fishing boats were reported
missing. Tubular fluorescent beams of light were seen overhead
by crews of searching vessels and from time to time radio
contact failed when the lights were overhead.

An American "Stealth"     ship  is  also   reported   as   having
mysteriously disappeared in the area.
