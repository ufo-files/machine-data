     I have word from Joe concerning his work on Lake Champlain• He devoted
the full summer to his work on lake monsters, spending one complete week
on the Vermont side of Champlain doing shore watching. He was also part of
a special schooner cruise outfitted on Lake Champlain to look for 'Champ'.
The schooner was the 'Richard Robbins'. Its owner Kempton Webb ( a professor
of Geography at Columbia University in NYC) asked Joe to co-ordinate this
cruise to specifically search for 'Champ'. The cruise· lasted from August
21st - 26th, the craft is a two masted vessel of some 80 feet in length. They
had no sightings, but it had been enjoyable time for everyone.

     To date Joe has collected 84 sightings of the Champlain creatures
36 being 'longneckers' ( reports of head and neck). He has several more which
he is trying to try to verify. There were three sightings this summer which
he is diligently trying to hunt down. Interested members may get further information
