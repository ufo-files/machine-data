The target involves a structure which is part of an organization
for the manufacture and production of materials.                     This structure

is a building with steps in front, and long and wide concrete walk

ways or run ways, extending from it in different directions.                   This

facility consists of a lot of metal and metal scraps on the inside

and is both raised above and below ground in its foundation.                    The

concept of sound is significant to this site.                       A low but loud

intermittent humming noise is present.                  Many working class workers
are currently working here in plain uniform.                      Adjacent to this
building, is a large, deep dug out land area.                     This area may be

under construction because there are unfinished structural walls

creating this space.            In other words, a high land area is at the
