                      A CONFIDENTIAL REPORT ON A RECENT "CONTACT" CLAIM


       On July 9th of this year, your SAUCER NEWS Editor made a visit to a large Eastern
city   ( which shall remain unnamed ) ,   in an effort to investigate a weird series of       "space
contacts" which allegedly had been experienced by a flying saucer research group there.
Accompanying your Editor on the trip was Richard E. Wallace,           one of our associate editors.


       The build-up to this event was fascinating.            For months we had been hearing stories
about how one member of the group,        whom we shall call Nick,     had been going into trances
during which his body was taken over by various beings from outer space.             During some of
these sessions, mysterious lights were seen - even by skeptical outsiders.               A New York area
researcher had visited the group just a week before our trip,            and had witnessed both the
trance phenomenon and the light,      and had returned home very impressed by both.          As a matter
of fact,   he had been so frightened by what he saw and heard that he cut short his investi­
gation and returned to New York rather hurriedly.


      On the afternoon of July 9th, we visited Nick's home.  Present also were a co-research­
er of his whom we will call Ed, as well as a few other members of the group.  Nick soon went
into a trance during which his body was taken over by a space being called Zayron, one of
several such entities which appear to vie for control of him during these exhibitions.                 Ed,
who had witnessed most of the previous trances, narrated for us as Nick strutted around the
room, breaking and overturning objects,         occasionally threatening those present with bodily
harm, answering questions about life on other planets - and in general acting like a poor
imitation of a bad science-fiction movie.


       We had been told that Nick exhibited strange characteristics during these trances -
that his eyes rolled back in his head in a way that no human's eyes ordinarily do;              that he
was insensible to pain;     and that he was able to take falls that no one could ordinarily
sustain without serious �nJury.       Nick's friends also claimed that he exhibited knowledge
far beyond that of an ordinary mortal.


       However, it took us only a few minutes to realize that this must be an act, and a
rather poorly-contrived one at that.          The mysterious light which other researchers had seen
failed to appear for us, although we were shown photographs of it            ( which, incidentally, had
no point of reference visible,      and therefore proved nothingl )       In addition,   we found that
all the details of Nick's actions while in trance had been grossly exaggerated.               Not only
did he fail to exhibit any unusual knowledge,          but the controlling entity actually contra­
dicted himself on several points,         and gave no information about anything which could not
be arrived at by pure guesswork, by any person who is a little bit familiar with flying
saucer literature and lore.


       Specifically, Zayron - who is from a planet either 2 million or 200 million light
years away   ( Zayron wasn't sure! ) , told us that there is no intelligent life in our solar
system.    But I later learned that Nick claims to have made trips to Mars via astral projec­
tion, and to have found a highly-advanced civilization there, with buildings "one to two
miles in height," covered by non-reflective domesl


       Our first feeling was that Nick and Ed were in on the hoax together, for the purpose
of bringing more attention to themselves and their saucer club.             However, through recent
correspondence, we have learned that Ed was apparently sincerely taken in by Nick's act,
and that he is only now beginning to see through it.             Be that as it may, we are hoping that
this whole line of nonsense, which is an insult to the intellectual capacity of any intel­
ligent person, will cease, - and that the group in question will give up these weird exper­
iments and get back to serious UF O research.
