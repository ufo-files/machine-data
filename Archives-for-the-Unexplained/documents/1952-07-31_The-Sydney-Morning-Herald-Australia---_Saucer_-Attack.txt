Sydney Morning Herald (NSW : 1842 - 1954), Thursday 31 July 1952, page 3



         "Saucer"
               Attack
                  YORK, July 30
                NEW
          (A.A.P.).-A   salesman
          told        police      at     Enid,
                               that    his
          Oklahoma,                           car

          was     almost       swept from
          the road       last night  by a
          huge    "flying              saucer"
          which swooped                low at
          high speed.
            The salesman, Mr. Sid
          Eubanks, aged 50,                  was

          trembling     when                    he
          walked   into the             police
          station.
             Mr. Eubanks said that
          the object, which he de-
          scribed as    a  "yellow
          green, then yellow-brown
          streak       about      400         feet
          long,"       dived      low        over

          the road, turned around,
          and disappeared in a few
          seconds.
            "The         tremendous
          pressure    nearly   threw
          my  car off  the road," he
          said.
