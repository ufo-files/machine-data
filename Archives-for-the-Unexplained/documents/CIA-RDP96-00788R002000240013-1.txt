            3. (S) Mr. Brandwein opened the briefing session by explaining why his
            office had stopped all involvement in psychic phenomena approximately
            4 years ago. Basically, his rationale amounted to the adverse publicity
            that CIA had been receiving at that time plus the decision that the office
            of Technical Services was not involved in R&D and that at that time all
            indicators pointed to the need for some sort of R&D effort in psychic
            behavior before anyone could take some sort of firm position. He stated
            that he tried to interest the R&D side of CIA to look into "Remote Viewing"
            as a collection tool but that nobody was interested or willing to get
            involved in something that might give them some bad publicity.
