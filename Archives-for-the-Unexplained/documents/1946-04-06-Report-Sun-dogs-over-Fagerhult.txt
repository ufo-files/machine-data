Case xxx.
Sun-dogs over Fagerhult

Time: April 6, morning
Place: Fagerhult, county of Kalmar.

”County of Kalmar. Fagerhult ([weather observer] Carlsson). On the 6th, shortly after sunrise, a big spot of light was seen to the left of the sun. It seemed like two suns had risen. Later, at 08.00-09.00 hours two rainbow-like spots were noted on each side of the sun, moving at the same pace as the sun, disappearing at about 10.00 hours. The spot of light, first noted, was, however, at almost the same place in the sky and decreased until it disappeared at about 09.00 hours.” 

Status: Halo phenomenon
Source: SMHI yearbook. Credit: Sven Schalin.
