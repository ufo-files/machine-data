  " Coast " highway ( between Netaniya to Haiffa ) 30 April 2002

  A citizen of north Israel reports: "I don't belive in such stuff", I don't want to identify myself, "so no one will hospitalize
  me". She tells us, that when she drove to the north she observed, beginning "Kibutz-Ga'ash" area, 3 shiny yellow lights.
  At first, she didn't pay any attention to them.
  Continuing driving, she saw that the 3 lights, standing vertically, continue to move northern, parallel to her. As she
  claims, they were big enough and lokked like "sreet-lights"
  When she passed "Zichron-Ya'akov" shesaw 3 vehicles whom their drivers stopped to watch the lights.

  When she arrived Haiffa, she discovered 3 more lights, who were in a distance from the first lights, moving north. She
  emphasizes that the distance between the 2 sets of lights were always equal.

  When she watched them, she noticed a shape between the lights, like the lights put their shade on a object.
  The 6 lights lit off in an order. In the beginning 3, then 2 more and at in the end, the last one.

  The observation was took between 21:00 to 22:00. To the witness estimation, the lights were lower then an airplane
  flight height.
