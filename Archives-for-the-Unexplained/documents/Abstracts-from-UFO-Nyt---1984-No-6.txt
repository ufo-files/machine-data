  Kim Møller Hansen:   GSW Stops Working, p. 9-10.

  For some time there have been rumours that Ground Saucer Watch (GSW)
  would discontinue analysing UFO photos. In personal correspondance with
  Kim Møller Hansen this is now confirmed. William Spaulding of GSW gives
  two main reasons for this step:    (l) It is too expensive and (2) the
  present results show that photos can not be used as evidence for ETI
  visitors. GSW will continue its other activities though.

  A final repart will apear from GSW at the end of 1984.

  It is true that there has been some criticism towards the work of GSW -
  also from SUFOI - but nevertheless SUFOI will miss this opportunity
  to have photos analysed. SUFOI are now intensifying the attempts to
  establish some way of digitizing and analysing photos·in Denmark by
  means of computers.

  Per Andersen: Fools ?, p. 12-13.

  For some time ret. major H.C. Petersen (Danish Get Acquainted Programme,
  IGAP) in co-operation with VonKevichzky (ICUFON) have been sending
  lots of UFO material to Danish and other Nordie statesmen. The first
  mentianed strongly believe in Adamski,     flying saucers,   ETI etc. and
  want the statesmen to look into their "evidence" for ETI visitors.

  Their "proofs" consist mainly of misinterpreted and distorted quotations
  from high level people (Schweitzer etc.),     contactees (Adamski,   Monguzzi,
  etc.) and Carter's sighting of a "UFO" (=Venus).

  SUFOI does not dissociate from IGAP believing in flying saucers nor
  making themselves a fool. But SUFOI regrets that each time IGAP
  forwards this kind of material to the statesmen it will take another
  5 years befare they will co-operate with seriously working ufologists.
