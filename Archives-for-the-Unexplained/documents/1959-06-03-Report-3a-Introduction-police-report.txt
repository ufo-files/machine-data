KUNGLIGA BOHUSLÄNS REGEMENTE
UDDEVALLA
Phone number: 140 90

Department: I 17
Date: June 9, 1959.
No. 810

To the head of the Defence Staff.

Regarding reports of an unknown robotic object which had apparently fallen into Kärnsjön, 15 kilometers north of Munkedal.

I hereby respectfully submit a report from the Landsfiskal of Munkedal.

An investigation regarding possible minesweep diving is currently underway in MKV.

III. MILITARY COMMAND STAFF
Department I e
Received on June 10, 1959.
Dispatched on June 10, 1959.
No. 810

B-E Nilsson
Acting Regimental Commander

/
P. Palmertz
