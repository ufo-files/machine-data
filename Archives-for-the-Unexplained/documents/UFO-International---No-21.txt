                                             EDITORIAL                  GABRIEL BLOWS HIS HORN

                                     By far the biggest news in the Flying Saucer Movement of the
                               past few months was the welcome "flap" of landings and sightings
                               that made headline news after Socorro,              New Mexico policeman
                               Linnie Zamora reported observing, on Friday, April 24th, a
                               landed silver-colored egg-shaped object that took off and flew away
                               from a gully near Socorro,            leaving behind as evidence,       burned
                               brush, scorched earth,             and four indentations in the ground where
                               the landing gear had apparently rested.
                                     Soon,    landings and sightings were being reported from several
                               different states.       As a result of the large amount of news coverage
   GABRIEL GREEN               given this flap, AFSCA received some very good publicity when
                               United Press International put three of our Albuquerque Saucer
                               Photos on their news wires,            which were subsequently printed in
hundreds of newspapers all over the country.                      Your editor was also ·interviewed on several
news, radio and TV shows and our saucer pictures were shown in color for three nights
in a row on KNBC-TV.              A local KABC-TV news program ran a series of serious and
unbiased five minute interviews on the saucer subject each night for over a month,                            thus
helping to gradually bring greater understanding of the subject to thousands of people
in this area.
     This great increase of activity has made many demands on your editor's time, result­
ing in extended delays in publication of this issue of our magazine. However, we now
have plans for speeding up production of forthcoming issues for the rest of this year, in
order to disseminate the huge volume of information that we are receiving from our Space
Brothers.
     Bob Renaud's series of contacts (he piloted the unique craft pictured on our cover)
continues to provide important information and evidence,                     such as the photos on our back
cover, with much better ones promised as his educational experiences with the Space
People continue.        The announcement of more flaps to come and a mass fly over within
three years (see "Special Report" on page 12) should encourage saucer movement workers
to intensify their efforts to get better organized in order to take advantage of the oppor­
tunities the Space People are helping to create.
     With the addition of 14 new AFSCA Units,                     we are developing our capabilities for
reaching more people with the Space People's message.    But we need your help to
establish an AFSCA Unit in every city and to help support already existing units and their
activities.
     If each person in our movement will sincerely do his best to be a source of Light
in an area of relative darkness,              what a bright world this will soon be,         to the lasting
benefit of all mankind.
