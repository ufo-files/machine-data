August 3: A case from Finland

Time: August 3, at 22.55 hours (Finnish local time)
Place: Brahestad, northern Finland

The following news item was published in Helsingin Sanomat on Aug 6:

”SPACE MISSILE SIGHTED IN NORTHERN FINLAND”

”Last Saturday evening at 10.55 pm some people waiting for the train at Raahe station witnessed a strange fireball moving from south towards north.

”The fireball had a yellow shine and was seen clearly for about 2-3 minutes and then it disappeared in the direction of Oulu. All of the observers were convinced that it was not the question of a meteor but it must have been some kind of missile launched by man. 

Status: Inconclusive, details lacking.
Source: Helsingin Sanomat, August 6. Finnish-Swedish translation: Heikki Virtanen. Swedish-English translation: Telle Lilja.
