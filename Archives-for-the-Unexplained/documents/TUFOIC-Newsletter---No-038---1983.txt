                    -NOCTURNAL LIGHTS IN 82.
       The most common form of sighting usually involves a
nocturnal light. A sighting which is just of a light that
may appear at low level or high in the sky, sometimes
moving about erratically but never more than a light is
seeno
       The first light case occured on March 8th near Glen­
devie. The lone witness was travelling north on the Huon
Highway about ?am. The highway comes over a hilltop near
Glendevie then winds downhill allowing views to the north.
It was at this stage that the motorist was able to observe
a huge light on or just above a hilltop· to her front. ( NW )
She stopped the car and looked at the light for 3 minutes
trying to make out what it could be. It was golden yellow
in colour and was larger than moon sized. The huge light
mass seemed to have landed although the hill is bush cov­
ered. It seemed to obscure the front of the hill but at
no·time did it move. The witness feeling a bit shaken by
the sighting decided to drive on homeo She lost sight of
the light mass as she drove downhill.
                          Rokeby.
       Another unexplained,light came to our attention on May
5th. This time the witness was lying in bed looking out at
the morning sky about 5. 15am. He could see Venus shinning
brightly in the eastern sky. Then what he took to be a
falling star, complete with tail came into view angling
down out of the north eastern sky. He was rather suprised
to see the star sized light come to halt, then after a sec­
ond or two it moved along horizontally towards the south.
Even · more puzzling was the disappearance of the tail and
it was now just a white lighto The light was on the same
level as Venus but as it neared the planet it changed dir­
ection. It climbed up above Venus passing over the planet
before dropping down the other side to the same level. It
then continued on into the southern sky, increasing speed
and fading from view. The sighting lasted 2 minuteso
                       Verona Sandso
       A similar type of light was seen by more than one wit­
ness from Verona Sands on June 24th about 1 1. 10 to 1 1. 55pmo
Mrs A says she noticed the light from her bedroom windowo
A closer look showed the large white light was gaining el­
evationo The light continued to rise until it was some 30
to 45 degrees above the horizon towards Bruny Island. The
light having come from the witnesses left now kept on work­
ing to the right as it fell away then rose up again tn
slightly higher in the sky. This pattern was repeated before
the light descended down to the water level and looked to
land on the water for 10 to 15 seconds.
       Again the light started to move upwards in the same
manner as when first noticed, going higher into the sky
and moving to the right. Then once more it descended but
seemed to disappear behind Bruny Island. It was about 1 1. 25
pm and the witness gave up looking for some 10minutes.
       Looking out again she noticed the light was back, once
more repeating its manoevres. Dropping to near sea level
then climbing back up into the sky. Eventually it moved    ·



further to the right than before and the witnesses view
was blocked by nearby trees. The sky during the night was
mostly overcast and the weather fine and still.

                     TASMAN H IGHWAYo
     The Tasman Highway snakes over the hills from Tasmania's
East Coast before a straight run brings it to the town of
Sorell some 26km east of Hobart.
     Two witnesses on july 10th ( 1982) were travelling on the
highway from the East Coast when they sighted a bright light
about 8pm. They were just starting the descent down Bust me Gall
Hill which is about 30km out of Sorello The area has bushy hills
and grazing land with scattered farms.
     Little attention was paid at the time to the bright white
light which looked like a large star some 45degrees or more up in
the NW sky. The witnesses however were suprised when they reached
the bottom of the hill to see the light was larger as if getting
closero It also had taken up a position to the right front of the
car. They had first though it was a falling star but now they
discarded that ideao
     The road now takes a fairly level course through Runnymede
towards the next series of hillso As they travelled on the light.
just stayed ahead of the car but more over to the right hand side
of the road. During the duration of the sighting no other vehicles
were encountered. Just after they lost sight of the object the
first car appeared from the opposite direction. It may sound sup­
rising to cover this distance with no other traffic but it would
not be impossible on this stretch of road in the evening.
     Little occurred at this stage as the witnesses continued on
their journey to Hobart with the light maintaining its position
in the sky. The witnesses had differing perspectives on the exact
position of the light relative to their car. The passenger felt
it was always up in the sky whilst the driver had the impression
it was lowo It seemed to be in front of the hills but beyond the
roadside trees. Another puzzling aspect was that despite the
changing direction of the road especially through the hills of
Black Charlies Opening, the light always managed to position itself
in the top right portion of the car's windscreen as viewed by the
witnesseso
     At no time did the car seem to be affected as the driver neg­
otiated the hills and straights at various speeds from 40 to 100
kpho A long straight section of road heads south into Sorell and it
was here that the object finally disappeared. It had accompanied
the car for the best part of 20 minuteso It was now its largest
in appearance as it suddenly shot across the road ahead of the car
and was lost to view over hills to the east of the highway. It
took only a second or two to move across-the highway, pause a
second, then seem to bank giving a view of what the witnesses took
to be the underside of the object before it disappeared. At this
time it looked to be almost half-moon sized. (apparent)
     The ob ject at its largest was described as dome shaped and a
whitish yellow in c0lour. When it banked there seemed to be a
string of red lights beneath. The driver thought the ob ject was
dome like with a rim below, whilst the passenger saw it as a round
shape with a flat base. When it banked both witnesses saw the red
lights beneath a dome like shape.
     Just after the mystery object vanished a car came in the opp­
osite direction, then two minutes later the witnesses noticed an
aircraft to their west heading for Hobart Airport. It had pin head
sized lights compared with those on the object. A check proved the
plane was the 8. 25 Ansett flight from Launceoton. It would not
have been visible to the witnesses when the sighting commenced
as it took off about 8pm from Launceston. Another aircraft on the
same flight path had landed before the sighting terminated.

 effect on the witnesso A visit by the Centre's representative to
 the sighting location has confirmed the witnesses claim that there
 are no traces to indicate anything out of the normal has taken
 place over the paddock. The roadside gorse bushes have died, but
 this could be due to spraying by local roadworkers or maybe the
 owner of the paddock.

Annual Report. One was near Ross in February ; the other at
the other end of the year in November near Dover. The year
was very quiet but one sighting came from north of Ross on
February 20th so that the second case that came to our notice
on February 3rd was of extra interest.
      The sighting occured near 9. 40pm as two witnesses were
travelling home together in Mrs A's car. Mrs A said they were
about a kilometer north of the town when she first noticed a
bright white light to the west. She thought it was a plane
as it descended in front of some low hills. She didn� take
much notice as they continued on towards the town centreo
However, the object changed direction and slowly approacbed
the local hotel, growing in size. It looked as if there was
going to be a crash. Mrs A did not want to be too close to
the scene of an impending accident and pulled up some 300/
400m short of the hotel. She said to her elderly passenger,
"Look at that". The passenger saw nothing at this stage as
she was looking for something on the roado There was no noise,
other traffic, or effects on the witnesses.
      Mrs A estimated the object to be moving slowly at a 30m
height off the ground, and having an apparent size of twice
the moon. It was elliptical in shape and a greenish metallic
colour with gold light beams from either side. The witness
found it hard to describe but the object presented the same
appearance despite its change of direction.
      The object now disappeared. Having reached the area of
the hotel it suddenly shot up and away to the south. It was
now noticed by Mrs A's passenger. It dwindled in size in a
couple of seconds and was lost to view in the d istance.
