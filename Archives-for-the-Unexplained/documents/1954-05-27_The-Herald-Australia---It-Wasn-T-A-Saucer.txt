Herald (Melbourne, Vic. : 1861 - 1954), Thursday 27 May 1954, page 3


            It          wasn't
             a      saucer
          People        Caulfiela and
                        in
        Glen     Iris        this
                             afternoon
        were  sure    they had seen    a
        flying saucer     glistening in

           It wasn't.  It was   only a
        weather    balloon,   used   to
        collect  information     about
        upper    air currents.
           The   Weather     Bureau   said
        it  had     been   caught by a
        northerly wind, and by the
        time    it was   over    Caulfield
        its height would        be about
        12.000 ft., and     its speed be
       tween      40 and   50 knots.
