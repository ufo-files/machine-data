                  Most of us are familiar with the many geniuses
             of our times who have been responsible for
             discoveries which have opened our minds.
                ·While having dinner with one of my many
             associates some time ago we discussed some of
             the remarkable men of our time such as Einstein ,
             Edison, Marconi and several others. But, when I
            mentioned Nikola Tesla, my companion remarked
             he did not recall the name. I have come across
            many who were not familiar with Nikola Tesla, a man who I describe as ' 'The
            Man From Another Time ' ' because it seems as though he was plucked from
            the future and inserted into our times. His mind was so creative and his
            theories so advanced that his contemporaries considered him one of the
             greatest minds ever to live.
                 Tesla made remarkable discoveries in the field of electronics, and even
             designed a light bulb before Thomas Edison did ; it also worked better than
             Edison 's bulb. Tesla was responsible for discovering alternating currents
            which we use today. He also was working on a ' ' death ray' ' which, as he
            believed, could melt rocks on the moon.
                 Few persons knew, for example , that Tesla predicted some 80 years ago
                                          man will soon travel the routes between the stars ,
                                          and that man will communicate with intelligent
                                          life in the galaxy ; at that time he was actually
                                          working on a system for intergalactic communica­
                                          tion. Unfortunately, several individuals , because
                                          of selfish motivations, prevented him from
                                          pursuing his work by cutting off his research
                                          grants. Had Tesla continued his work , I have no
                                          doubts that the world today would be decades
                                          ahead in many areas of science. I also believe that
                     Nikola Tesla         he would have also solved the energy problem as
                                          well.
                 Why then is he almost unknown? Tesla's story is a strange one, indeed,
             and one that would be hard to believe in his day. For those among you who
            know little about this fantastic man, we have presented an article in this
            issue which we feel is a good outline of the man and his many achievements.
                Do gamblers have a sixth sense? Ask any prof�ssional gambler and he will
            tell you about his ' 'hunches ' ' or ' 'feelings ' ' when placing a bet, rolling the
            dice, or playing Black Jack. Some gamblers claim they win because .they
            ' 'beliew' ' that they will win ; in other words , positive thinking. We feel that
            the article on page 32 will give some insights into the mind of a professional
            gambler.
                 Because of the many requests for more UFO material we -have , as many of
            you know, published Beyond Reality's UFO Update on an unscheduled basis
            for the past two years. Now, because of increased demand, we have decided
            to publish UFO Update on a quarterly basis. Our first issue is on the
            newsstand now.
