        The road is cut across a hill, and towards the left edge is
the bank of the hill rising up and away at a gradient of about
1:4.    To the right of the road,    beyond its edge,    the hill continues
down along a steep slope to reach a flat area with bushes, shrub
and some rocks. The next bend is roughly one hundred and fifty
yards away.




                                                       PLAN VIEW


        Reflexly, Piyasena slowed down the jeep and at the same time
turned his eyes more than the head, as drivers often do, to get
the moving object into focusu What he saw was something he had
never seen in his life before.
        It was a light, "eliya" he said. But it was not an ordinary
light like an electric bulb,       a candle flame or a kerosene lamp.
        It was a ball of light,    or as he expressed it in his own
language, "an illuminated ball". The colour was a very bright
green,    which as he put it,    "went right through the eyes" because
it was so intense. Additionally,       it had   a   yellowish-orange glow
to it. Piyasena said it was difficult to describe this mixture of
colours together - bright green and yellow-orange.
        The object did not illuminate the area as an electric light
would have done. This is why Piyasena used the expression "an
illuminated ball",    in an attempt to portray this curious feature.
Some researchers refer to this       'non-illumination effect' as B2a­
reflecting light and the theory has been put forward that it
involves an unknown type of radiation. However, Piyasena's fear
was only beginning.
        The ball was not on the ground. It was between two and three
feet above the slope of the hill.       Its size was smaller than that
of a football but larger than that of a grape-fruit,             about   8-10
inches in diametero
        By this time Piyasena had brought the jeep to        a   complete
halt but he left the lights ono The ball moved rapidly down the
hill and on to the road,following the contour of the ground and
maintaining a constant height between two and three feet.                It made
no sound and this terrified Piyasena,       who sat quietly in his seat
observing the phenomenon.       It did not affect the lights of the
jeep,    and as far as is known,    did not affect the engine which was
still idling.
