Rapportör:Herr Carl Wallenberg, Hospitalsgatan 23, NorrköpingRapportörens plats:Å landsvägen nordligaste delen av S-kurvan 200 m väster "K" i Kårtorp.Tidpunkt:11/7 1946 kl 2040-2045. Tiden kontrollerades ej med klocka, men han tror med säkerhet att det rörde sig mellan dessa tidpunktenRiktning:Söder - norr med beräknade bana över "t" i Kårtorp. Vid ingång över skogen 1200 m öster "p" i Karstorp antingen exploderade den ellerändrade den riktning mot nordost.Höjdvinkel:Beräknad till ca 20-25 grader. Höjd över marken bedömdes till ca 200 m.Beskrivning:Form: spolformig avrundad framtill, slutade med en spets.Storlek: som ett vampireplan eller något mindre, hastigheten dock större än dylikt plan.Färg: pjäsen ljus ultraviolettFör observatören föreföll det som om explosioner utgick från spetsen på pjäsen. Inga ljud hördes (2 bilar passerade dock platsen, vilka möjligen kunde förta ev ljud). Ingen rök eller s k svan syntes.Tid c:a 6 sekunder.

Tiden kontrollerades ej med klocka,men fröken D håller bestämt på, att det rörde sig mellan dessa klockslag.Riktning:Syd - nordlig, öster om och ej långt från vägenHöjdvinkel:Kunde ej bedömas, höjden låg, beräknades till ca 25m.Beskrivning:From: klotformig, färg som naturlig ljuslåga, "svans" med jämn, ej stötvis rörelse. Hastigheten ganska långsam, bedömdes långsammare än vanligt flygplan. 1)
Reporter:Mr. Carl Wallenberg, Hospitalsgatan 23, NorrköpingReporter's location:On the road on the northmost part of the S-curve 200 m west of "K" in Kårtorp.Time:11/7 1946 at 20:40-20:45. The time was not controlled against a watch, but he was very certain that it was around these times.Direction:South - north, with a path over "t" in Kårtorp. While crossing the forest 1200 m east of "p" in Karstorp it either exploded or changed directiontowards northeast.Elevation angle:Estimated to between 20 and 25 degrees. Height above ground estimated to about 200 m.Description:Form: spoolshaped, with rounded front, ended with a tip at its rear.Size: As a vampire plane or slightly smaller, however its velocity was higher than such a plane.Colour: The piece was light ultraviolet.It appeared to the observer that explosions occurred at the tip of the piece. No sounds were heard (however 2 cars passed by, which could have masked possible sounds). No smoke or so called tail was seen.Time about 6 seconds
2)
Reporter:Miss Elsa Davisson, Ringstad Gård, NorrköpingReporter's location:On the road 200 m south of the crossroads at "K" in Klubben,600m south of V Husby churchTime:11/8 1946 between 20:45-21:00. The time was not controlled against a watch but Miss D is firmly determined that it occurred between these two timesDirection:South - north, east of and not for from the roadElevation angle:Could not be determined. Altitude estimated to about 25 m.Description:Shape: orb shaped, color as a natural light flame, "tail" with even, notintermittent, movement. The velocity fairly low, estimated to be less than that of a regular airplane. The object exploded somewhere above the creek at "u" in Klubben and had a similar appearance to a firework during the explosion.Duration of observation: A few seconds.
