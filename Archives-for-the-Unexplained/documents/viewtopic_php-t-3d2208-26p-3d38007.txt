  Contacted by a Reporter

    by Tim Hebert » Tue Jan 08, 2013 2:52 am

  Thought this might interest some on the forum. I recently was contacted via email by a reporter in New York doing
  research on those who served on missile crews back in the 1980s. He had come across my blog and wanted to talk with
  me. Earlier this morning, I had a long chat with this fellow, and it seemed that he was more interested in the haunted
  missile site stories. We had briefly talked about the UFO angle and how I came to be interested in the subject. This
  naturally led to a discussion about Robert Hastings. After briefly explaining the Malmstrom story, the reporter decided
  that he wasn't interested in the UFO component. I went on talk about the Titan II fiascoes at Searcy and Damascus,
  Arkansas. This seemed to interest him more and I provided contact information concerning how to get in touch with
  former Titan crew members. All said, it was a good conversation.
