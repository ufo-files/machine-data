  Remember Ed Cabansag's description of the craft he saw?

  Bruni p193: "It was cone-shaped - egg-shaped, with lights running around its belt from left to right. They
  were blue, white and red lights, flashing, sometimes rapid, sometimes slow."

  Imagine the craft from the first sketch, with its left and right part obscured by trees. This would indeed look
  egg/cone shaped, and if the line drawn around its belt indicates lights, the similarity with Cabansag's
  description becomes remarkable.
