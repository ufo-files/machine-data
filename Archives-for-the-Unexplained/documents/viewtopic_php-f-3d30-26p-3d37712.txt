  invaluable feedback. The other is a heavily researched and footnoted 58-page essay by E.P. Heidner which “ties

  Burks, on whose website the essay appeared. Following is the script’s premise:

  FRANK lost his 26-year-old daughter LISA. ANNA lost her husband ARTURO, a cook anticipating promotion to sous-

  gain new compassion for the victims, and new perspective on the events, of 9/11.
