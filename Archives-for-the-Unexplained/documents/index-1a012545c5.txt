  Publisher's Synopsis

  In his bestselling Intruders, Hopkins chronicles alien encounters. Though Intruders greatly expanded readers' knowledge
  of the UFO abduction phenomenon, Hopkins subsequently learned of an extraordinary witnessed abduction--an
  abduction sighted by independent third party witnesses. This book reveals this unprecedented and amazingly complex
  case for the first time in its entirety.


  From the Book Cover

                         A CASE TOO COMPELLING TO IGNORE -- FROM AMERICA'S UFO EXPERT

  Budd Hopkins' extraordinary New York Times bestseller, Intruders, was hailed as "a convincing case... that the invasion
  from outer space is not fiction but fact" (Houston Chronicle). Now, this renowned UFO authority discloses
  breakthrough evidence of a shattering, unprecedented phenomenon -- a witnessed abduction.

  New York City, November 30, 1989, 3 a.m.: Linda Cortile, a married mother of two, emerges from an apartment
  window high above Manhattan's FDR Drive. Suspended in a blue beam of light, and accompanied by three alien figures,
  she is lifted into a large reddish-orange glowing UFO, which then moves off toward the Brooklyn Bridge.

  As recalled under hypnosis, the pattern of Linda Cortile's abduction seemed no different from hundreds of prior cases
  Budd Hopkins had studied. But his investigation took a dramatic turn when several witnesses -- including a major
  political leader and two police officers -- contacted him with vivid descriptions, all independently corroborated, of the
  shocking events near the Brooklyn Bridge that night. Through their letters, audiotapes, and sketches, Budd Hopkins
  uncovered keys to remarkable details of the encounter -- including an inexplicable matallic object implanted in Linda's
  body. WITNESSED is the most powerful and authoritative case yet supporting the truth about alien abductions.
