             2.     <S-SS>    The impressions were:

                  a.  Beginning impressions were of several "peopled places".
             All people in these beginning places were dark-skinned.   There
             were an abundance of people here with large, distended stomachs
             <AOL: like pregnant women or children with malnutrition).

                  b.  There was a second set of impressions of a roll~ng-hills
             type of land near a mountainous area, high enough for the
             mountains to reach the clouds.

                  c.  A third approach to the site included a "leaning"
             structure which appeared to be some kind of machine <AOL: rocket
             launcher or gun).

                  d.  A fourth approach to the site led to a structure.  The
             very large area inside the structure was hi-leveled with'white
             partitions.  There was also a small room which overlooked the
             larger area.

                   e. Tasking to perceive the area from above produced the
             diagram shown in Drawing #1.   It is an orderly complex of
             structures.  The structures are long, length approximately 3
             times width.  Some of the structures appear to be very dark
             colored, as seen from above, while others are lighter.   ~here is
             a smaller, square structure nearby, with an open area belween
             which appears to be paved. 0 Near the small square struct re there
             seems to be a strong variance in the level of the land.
