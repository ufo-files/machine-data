South of Vanern between Vanersberg and Trollhattan, lay the plateaus HALLE and
HUNNEBERG. The twin mountains are a good 150 metres high, they are seperated·by a
500 metre wide split - fracture and their unmistakable silhouettes are seen for miles
around over the surrounding 4lat land. The mountains are famous not least ffor their
great numbers of elks which yearly draw thousands of visitors and tourists.


1988 also placed the mountains into the focus of ufological circles. The 3rd August
this year (1968) found an unexplained dead and mutilated elk in L�ngedalen at -
Hunneberg. There began a flow of stories about mysterious flying objects over the
mountains with ufo hysteria quickly starting up.


The landscape around Langedalen in the middle of Hunneberg is of great natural beauty
and it was here that a walker found a dead young male elk in August of 1988.     The

elk lay metres from the road (track) out onto a little mossy swamp. All four legs
were for some unfathomable reason broken at the hip-joint and lay straight out from
the body with no visible injury on the rest of the body.

Both Halle and Hunneberg are Crown parks and all hunting is forbidden except for the
Royal hunt (What an honour for wildlife to be hunted and killed by 'Royals'- not cruel
and bloodthirsty when they do it!;E�) each autumn. Uno Gunnarsson (hunt master) heard
about the dead elk and went out to investigate, thinking it would be the work of poachers.
But as soon as he caught sight of the body he realised the situation was mysterious.

The dead elk lay in such a way that it looked as if it had fallen from a height. It was
not injured:��� no marks suggesting it had been run-over were found. Disease could defi­
nately be ruled out. The whole thing was a complete mystery and Gunnarsson said later
that he had never seen anything like it in his many years of daily visits to the. forest
and grounds.

The police in Vanersborg were called in and poli�eman Lennart Karlsson followed the case
up to try and find some leads. The skin around the broken legs was scarred(?) and injuries
showed to be identical on all four hip-joints.
"It really seemed as if the elk had been dropped from the air" said Uno Gunnarsson, when
interviewed by the_ media. In his police report, Lennart Karlsson wrote: "The:circumstances
surrounding the elk's death are strange. It lay on its stomach with all 4 legs stretched
out. The animal had not been shot and was not the victim of a traffic accident. The only
'ordinary' thought or explanation left, was that it had died as the result of a storm".

When no reasonable explanation was found, the bones were sent to the veterinary institue
of medicine in Uppsala, where the remains of the elk were analysed by Margaret Steen,
a scientist who specialises in injuries/death cases amongst elks. Sometimes, x-rays show
metal fragments from bullets in the bone but no such trace was found here. A traffic
accident could·also be ruled out. The road through Langdalen is shut off to traffic and
the nearest used road is a good 3kl from where the elk was found. The animal's injuries
were such that it could not have walked with them.

Lightening injury was the only natural expla�ation left but both Steen and the rest of
the experts at the institute were unanimous in their verdict, that the injuries were not
caused by lightning. According to SMHI there had been no bad weather in the area at that

In July 93, one dead and 15 injured sheep were found in Rossmala. Noteable was that
all of the injured animals had identical marks high up on the neck.
In October 94, the remains of a dead ewe were found with the genitals taken out, this
was outside of Ronneby and in June othis year (95) , a mutilated cow was found with

No clear connection is found in these cases to the ufo phenomenon but many people still
mistakenly make such a connection. It is of course remarkable that nobody has ever

In spite of the popular interpretation about ufo involvement in these incidents the
material is so thin that one cannot draw any concrete conclusion about them.

A mysterious object which swayed over a field in Dalarna and a near observation
from Hakesta in 1971, are two cases which Agneta Hallden writes about here. In
both cases the observers were very scared by the incidents.

Margit Bergkvist, 79, in Ovre Vanbo, last December witnessed something really
remarkable outside her home.

By Agneta Hallden.


It was just before nine in the evening when Margit went into the bedroom to switch
off the radiator. Through the window, she saw at a distance, a sphere of light
which slowly neared above the edge of the forest. She was paralysed and could not
take her eyes from the light.

 Outside-the grounds of Margit's house, lies a field and a little village road.
 When the light neared the field a couple of hundred metres from the house, it
 suddenly cast a dark "rope-cord" out. At the same time, a lorry was travelling on
 the road. Two light beams shone onto the roof (!) . "The vehicle" was all over the
 road, it had difficulty holding it's course. The sphere which was at a low height,
 continued coming nearer and was almost intensive. Just at the side of it was some­
 thing which Margit thought was like a large "Cloth", changed to a round "cushion"
 surrounded by a coarse "Ironrailing" (?) . On the left side were 4 dark spots with
1'spikes". "The cushion" was lit with a natural light colour and looked to have a
 rough linnen structure. It was in fact like the curtains Margit had in her kitchen
 window.

The "vehicle" had disappeared. Everything was so strange, the worst I have experi­
enced in my whole life. The thick long cushion was fixed skywards. I wondered how
long this would all be. At the same moment as the "cushion" disappeared, there
appeared a large silver coloured area at the side.
                   - I had never seen this colour before, said Margit. It was
heavenly. I was fascinated. The surface kind of flowed and took the form of a flat
diamond. From the top point was a long thin antennae with a rectangular shape at
the top. From there were stacked 4 others directed upwards, stuck out. They vibrated.


LIGHT & DARK GLASS FRAGMENTS.

From the left and right spikes stuck out, glvlng out light and vibrating.
In the middle of the object sat 2 very oblong vertical windows. The upper part of
these windows moved. It was like a mosaic of light and dark pieces of glass. Like
a Kaleidescope. Each prism falling from one position to another before settling
in one place. Between the windows was something round, perhaps an opening or an
additional window. On the right underside a stack of 4 parts stuck out like
"cotton-reels". On them were vibrating blue-grey "leaves". They moved as if they
billowed or surged back and forth like waves on the sea.

As Margit observed them it was as if some kind of rope was on the upper part of the
round window. When she looked back again at this it began to get light.
She thought she could make out some kind of presence behind the window and felt they
were observing, she began to feel this was (or 'it' was) weird.

                        - Now I'd had enough, said Margit, I drew the curtains and
went to phone my son.
