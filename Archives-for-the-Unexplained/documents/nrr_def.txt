Nellis Range Land Description



                                     Nellis Range Land Description
  Below is an exact township/range description of lands currently included in the Nellis Range and that will be
  presumably be included in Nellis' request to Congress for Range Renewal. These lands do not include Area 51,
  indicating that this 6-by-10 mile block of land is treated separately and may not be controlled by Nellis at all. (The Air
  Force Flight Test Center at Edwards AFB is a more likely landlord there.)

  This list was scanned directly from a two-page sheet given to me on 6/20/96 by Col. Andrew Bourland, Nellis Director
  of Public Affairs. The list was prepared by Major Jeff Shea, a real estate specialist at Nellis. The list was given to me at
  a public scoping meeting for the Nellis Range Renewal, in response to my request for the maps accompanying Public
  Laws 99-606 and 98-485. Col. Bourland said they could not find the original maps described in the public laws.
  However, he indicated that this description is accurate, and he provided a map drawn from this description, clearly
  excluding Area 51. Col. Lorrie Bourland told me that the Nellis Range Renewal will include only these lands and no
  others.

  The lands are specified in township/range coordinates. Townships and ranges appear on most USGS maps. Each
  township/range pair describes a 6-by-6 mile block, which is further divided into square-mile sections numbered like
  this....

                                                      6 5 4 3 2 1
                                                      7 8 9 10 11 12
                                                      18 17 16 15 14 13
                                                      19 20 21 22 23 24
                                                      30 29 28 27 26 25
                                                      31 32 33 34 35 36

  There has been a suggestion that two parcels of land now controlled by Nellis might be returned to the public domain in
  the course of the renewal. One is a half-mile wide strip of land extending west from the Indian Springs airfield. This was
  a runway approach zone, apparently to allow low-level approaches by the Thunderbirds and other aircraft. The other
  parcel is the former small arms range in the Vegas Valley northwest of Nellis Air Force Base.

  Note: Every effort has been made to reproduce the list below exactly as it was given to me on the two-page sheet.
  However, it is possible some errors may have occurred in the scanning process.
