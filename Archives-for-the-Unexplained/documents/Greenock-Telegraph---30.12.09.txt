Greenock Telegraph – 30th December 2009…

UFO mystery was poignant family tribute…

Lights above Inverclyde which were mistaken for UFOs on Christmas Day were a touching tribute to family members who have passed away.
John McConnachie, right, and his family lit lanterns at Gourock Cemetery on Christmas Day in memory of their loved ones.
John's wife Angela Thompson died in April from a heart attack aged just 37, while his nephew Ryan died last October from a rare condition just three days after he was born.
Ryan's dad Declan Patterson and mum Mary McConnachie, John's sister, lit a lantern in memory of their son along with around 20 members of the close-knit family.
