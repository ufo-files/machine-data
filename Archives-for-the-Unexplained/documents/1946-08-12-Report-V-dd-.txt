VÄDDÖ DISTRICT
HALLSTAVIK

PM

On the night of Sunday, August 12, Baron Bertil Leijonhufvud suddenly caught sight of a bright object whilst driving from Östhammar to Edebo. Maintaining great altitude, the object traveled with "tremendous" speed across the sky toward the north-northeast. While the prevailing clear weather should had allowed a clear view of the airborne object, Mr. Leijonhufvud proved unable to describe its shape and appearance, though he did state that it had traveled in a straight line path with a brilliant tail that followed behind it. Mr. Leijonhufvud considered it unlikely to have been a meteor . The object was completely soundless and had remained in his sight for about 4-5 seconds.

Hallstavik, August 15, 1946.

Hilding Norén
