News items
       A French report has been released claiming UFOs are real and are possibly· a
security risk and need to be studied. The report was authored by former French
defense officials and others, and appeared in the July 17, 1999, issue of a French .
magazine known as VSD. An English translation does not yet exist.

       A former NASA employee, Clark McClelland, ·reports that the space agency is
more knowledgeable about UFOs than it has admitted. McClelland has gone public
with a web site: www.stargate-chronicles.com.  There is also a book in the works.


MUFON Symposium
       The 1999 MUFON symposium was held in Arlington, VA on July 2-4. The Friday
press conference was covered by the Washington Post and the Washington Times.
Attendance was approximately 550, and the Fund's Sunday brunch attracted 116
guests. Australian Kelly Cahill was a fascinating speaker at both the brunch and the
conference. The Cahill book, published in Australia, was given to brunch participants
as part of the brunch package and is available from the Fund.
