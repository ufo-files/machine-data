Case xxx.
Projectile crash at Njutånger

Time: Saturday, July 20, at about 15.00 hours
Place: Långviken, Njutånger (county of Gävleborg).

”PROJECTILE CRASH ALSO IN NJUTÅNGER. On Saturday evening, a mysterious object, which with great noise came singing through the air, frightened several persons in Långviken - Njutånger. 

”When several people at about 15.00 hours on Saturday were by the road near the dwellings in Långviken, they suddenly caught sight of a smaller object with a white, glistening tail of about 4 decimeters behind, which at 20 meters altitude and with great speed crossed over the road and over the dwellings, peeled [Sw. skalade] off the leaves on the tops of a few birches and hit the lake, about 15 meters from shore. On the lake, which was completely calm, big waves arose as from a motor-boat, in an area of about 10 meters. A person who, immediately after the fall, went to the place by boat, only found a lot of leaves and papers floating.”

This case, published only by local media, was never noted by the Defence Staff.
