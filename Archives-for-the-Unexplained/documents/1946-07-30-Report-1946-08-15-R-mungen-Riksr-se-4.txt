I

The bureau of customs reports on an alleged observation of an unidentified flying object.
On the 30th of July, 16:54 AM, two border patrolmen sighted a torpedo-like object with short wings, altitude not exceeding 500 metres. Its speed was twice that of a conventional flying machine. No engine noises were heard, with the exception a strange wishing sound. No puffs of smoke were seen. Additionally, woodland manager Edvinsson of Östervall Forest would later observe the same exact thing.

II

This letter is adressed to the Air Defense Department, Stockholm
I hereby respectfully submit a transcript of a report sent to the border customs chamber by border patrolman B.G. Högfeldt. As you will notice, an abridged report on the same subject has previously been sent to you by the military command headquarters.
Phone conversations with one Paul Edvinsson (whose name is mentioned in the report) has revealed that the object did not land (crash) on Swedish territory.
The population in the cross-border regions are subject to further interrogations.
Karlstad on August 15, 1946.
A.N.C. Sjunnesson
Captain

III

Letter adressed to the border customs chamber in Karlstad.
Content related to the sighting of a rocket plane at lake Römungen.

I hereby respectfully report the following information.
On the 30th of July, 1946, I went on a joint border patrol to lake Römungen along with extra border patrolman B.R.I. Johansson. The time was approximately 16:50 AM when we found ourselves at the West side of the lake, about 200 metres from landmark 43. All of a sudden we heard a massive roar. The sound came straight from the South and slightly West of our location. Johansson had trouble seeing as some trees obscured his view. I saw an object that was shaped lika en airplane, though substansially smaller.
I appreciated the fuselage to be between 5 and 8 metres, approximately double the span of its short and stubby wings. I seem unable to remember the shape of the tail. I didn't notice any cockpit or passenger compartment. The plane was coloured silver grey and its speed was around 800 kilometres an hour. Its course was due North. It flew at an altitude of around 500 metres. No engine noise was heard. It seemed as if the flying object had run out of fuel, for its speed had started to reduce. It disappeared from view after it had crossed the border, due to it being obscured by trees.
We proceeded to landmark 43, where we encountered Mr. Paul Edvinsson, a resident of Boviken. Interestingly, he had observed and heard the exact same thing and added that the plane suddenly had changed its course to the East. It seemed as if it had descended toward the ground.
That is all.

Bottner on August 5th, 1946.
B.G. Högfeldt
Border patrolman
