                      ON   THE   IMPORTANCE   OF   STUDYING   UFOs   WORLDWIDE




     As you will notice we are gradually increasing the overseas content of UFORAN.

This is because, as we have stated in the past, UFOs are a global event which must be

studied on a global scale.       Researchers from every country benefit by co-operating with

each other.   Therefore, the UFO Research Australia Newsletter {UFORAN} strives to present

to its readers the broad overview possible when many nations participate in this magazine.



     It has been said before, but unfortunately it must be said again, to have prejudices

against any country is obstructive and highly counter-productive.           Surely the contributions

of intelligent rational individuals and organizations from every nation are of vital

importance if one is to gain an overall view of the UFO phenomena.               It is most unfor­

tunate that communication is hampered by those who harbour blind prejudices against a

particular country.     UFORAN will endeavour to present to its readers the research

achievements of all nations - we shall not deny you the forest for any particular tree.



     Of course, in every country there are the irrationally devoted saucer followers.

However, it is most unfair, in our opinion, to point a finger at any nation and accuse

its researchers of saucer hysteria simply because there are individuals and organizations

making totally unfounded statements about the nature of UFOs within that country, and, as

we all know, no nation is free of these.



     The capabilities of researchers cannot be assessed from what a supporter or critic

may have written.     The only way to truly evaluate the worth of an individual, or organ­

ization is to read and assess personally the resea rch articles published by them.



     It is UFORAN policy to be cosmopolitan, this is because the answer to UFOs, like

the answer to any other scientific problem, lies in research, and when we share our

knowledge we take practical steps towards a possible solution.l
