two CAT scans and an MRI. Says Wilson, "...none of these tests detected anything wrong with me. Either we do not
have the scientific tools to study this new "abduction syndrome" to establish that it is caused by a psychological
abnonnality, or abductions are really occuring."
   Wlison emphasizes the importance of what she calls teaching dreams, a subject that many researchers have chosen
to ignore. She says it is not a dream as we would normally define a dream, but is rather a particular type of experience

aliens choose to have her or him experience. During these experiences, aliens and hwnans have the opportunity to learn
from each other. She told of her interacti ons with an alien she calls "The Blonde." The Blonde is not quite hwnan­
looking, is about six feet tall, and has blue eyes, but the pupils can contract to vertical slits. The Blonde, she says, has
used her to show another group of aliens how important the concepts of the soul and etemaJ life are to hwnans and The
Blonde. [Another "abducteelerperiencer" whom I know personally told me of asking the aliens about death and

happiness. In effect, he said, he was given to understand that there is no real death.]
