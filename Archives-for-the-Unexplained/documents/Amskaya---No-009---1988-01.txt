      It   appears  that some people act like magnets to UFOs and Philip
Rodgers may be one of them.    Apart  from  his  "space  recordings"  he
tells    of  a  great  variety  of  uncanny  experiences that seem to be
connected with them    and  which  have  sometimes  been  simultaneously
witnessed by others.    Since his sight, which has always been very poor
due   to an incurable ocular disease,               diminished to virtual blindness a
few years ago,      his experiences have mostly taken on an audible nature.

         These     intimations      and     impressions            of     Bryant's       resemble
clairvoyance;       perhaps    the      shock       of    his     April    encounter,       or some
emanations either from the craft or its occupants,                        gave rise      to      that
spontaneous  faculty.   As             with  clairvoyance,   only a portion of the
pronouncements are correct,            the  rest  vague,   or  even  extravagantly
wrong.


      In   the     afternoon     everybody sat round a long table where Bryant
went through his tale once more.  It had been almost six months  since
   had  last  seen him, and I was interested to see that his story had
not altered in any detail,           not as though he had learnt                 it    by     heart,
but  as  someone recounting an actual experience.                         When he came to the
end many questions were put to him.
