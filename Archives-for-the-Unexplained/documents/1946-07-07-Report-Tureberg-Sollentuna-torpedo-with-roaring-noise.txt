Case xxx: 
Torpedo with a roaring noise?

Time: July 7, 18.00 hours 
Place: Tureberg, Sollentuna, Stockholm (CA56)

The following words are scribbled on a paper found in the Defence Staff archives: "Town treasurer G.Ankert, Tureberg. Sunday night, 18.00 hours. Torpedo-shaped object, roaring noise, behind  Edsviken, oblong body". 

On the paper are also some unintelligeble sketches that indicate that the man who took down the report (probably by telephone) attempted some calculations. Edsviken, mentioned in the report, is situated to the SE of Tureberg. 

Anders Liljegren spoke with Margareta Ankert, Stockholm, who was married to the son of the witness. She stated that both of these men were dead.

Status: Insuffient information
Sources: Report (note) in Defence Staff archives.Telephone interview (M.Ankert), April 22,1987. ScanCat 795.
