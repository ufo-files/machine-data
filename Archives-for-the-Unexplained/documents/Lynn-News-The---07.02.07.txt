The Lynn News - King's Lynn, Norfolk, UK… 7th February 2007… 

UFO Lovelights In Lynn's Night Sky… 

   Mysterious orange lights spotted in the sky over Lynn sparked speculation and more than a few outlandish theories over the weekend.
   The UFOs were seen by residents across the town at around 5.30pm on Saturday, prompting a number of calls and e-mails to the Lynn News from intrigued residents.
   "They certainly were like nothing I have seen before. They all moved very slowly together and it was very strange.
   "There was no noise and after a few minutes they just faded and vanished," said Jacqueline Hunt, of Hillen Road, South Lynn
   There were theories the lights were bombs, signals from a ship at sea, helicopters and alien spaceships.
   The lights were in fact Chinese-style lanterns released at a wedding reception being held at the town's Riverside Restaurant, on King Street.
   The happy couple had wanted something a bit different on their special day and no-one at the restaurant expected the resulting fuss.
   Owner Miss Pat Isbill said: "They were called glow lanterns and worked on the same principle as a hot air balloon. I had never seen them before. They were quite large at least two foot tall and they were very effective.
   "We had police permission to set them off, so we didn't cause any full-scale emergencies!"
   Miss Diane Breedon, a member of staff at the restaurant, was surprised when she realised the lanterns were the hot topic of a Radio Norfolk phone-in on Sunday.
    "All these people had phoned and texted in to say they had seen amber lights in the sky at around 5.30pm, and I remembered that was when we set them off.
   "People were putting two and two together and making five, thinking they were bombs from Marham or alien air craft. I phoned in and put everyone straight," she explained.
Now, as they say, the truth really is out there.
