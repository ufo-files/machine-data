Case xxx.
A Norwegian fireball

Time: April 21, 21.00 hours
Place: Hedemarken, Norway

In a review of ”the heavens of May”, S. Einbu of Dombås, Norway noted: ”A fireball passed over Hedemarken on April 21 at 21.00 in a northeasterly direction.”

Status: Meteor
