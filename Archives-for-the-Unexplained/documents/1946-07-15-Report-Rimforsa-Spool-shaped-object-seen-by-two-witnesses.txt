Case xxx: 
Spool object seen by two witnesses

Time: July 15, 14.17 p.m.
Place: Rimforsa, province of Östergötland (CF47)

"Rimforsa, July 16, 1946. To the Air Defence Department, Defence Staff, Stockholm: I would like to inform you that yesterday, at 14.17, the southeastern tip of lake Järnlunden (Rimforsa) was passed by a spool-shaped bright object. It came from the SE and travelled towards the NW at great altitude and with very great speed. The sighting was made by my wife and her brother, assistant Curt Malmgren, Box 100, Rimforsa.
 
         /signed, Ivar Göthberg, colonel (reserve)/"
          
Note: Anders Liljegren attempted to contact the witnesses but wasn't able to trace them, although he talked to several (unrelated) people named Göthberg, living in the nearby area.

Status: Possibly aircraft contrail 
Source: Letter written to Defence Staff. ScanCat 1289.
