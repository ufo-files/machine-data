the amusement of himself and his inner circle of friends. Gray had ma jor­
ed in English in college, and had spent a year as an English teacher in
West Virginia - several years before he became interested in UFOs.    Thus
some of his early poems are full of references to classical mythology, etc.,
and are therefore Over the Heads of most of us.

field. The two presented below are among the best of these, in our opin­
ion, and are presented unedited. They are side by side simply to save
Space.

lished. We also remember one time only when we persuaded him to read a
few of his works in a coffee house in Greenwich Village (N.Y.C.) , during
the golden Hippie Period of the 1960's.

Works. We read from it two or three times at a monthly poetry group     1n
