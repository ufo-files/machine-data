  The disease concerned is called "progeria". The following
  from http://chs-web.neb.net/people/students/sdweber/progeria/
  toc.html:
  "Progeria was discovered in 1886 by Jonathan Hutchinson.
  It was not officialy named until 1904 when Hastings Gilford
  gave it the name Progeria. The disease is also commonly called
  Hutchinson-Gilford syndrome,after these two researchers."
  "If a person is found to have Progeria, there is nothing that
  can be done. There is not a known treatment for the disease.
  The person could live a fairly normal lifestyle for about 20
  years of their lives. The only thing that should hinder an
  affected person is the physical appearance of him or her. This
  person will not have to worry too much about this though, since
   he or she will be dead by the time he or she turns 30. "
