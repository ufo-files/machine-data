Sydney Morning Herald (NSW : 1842 - 1954), Saturday 12 May 1951, page 1



       JET-PLANES,
                NOT             SAUCERS
           LITHGOW,                      Friday.     -




       R.A.A.F.     jet-fighter   planes,
       shining  like   silver  discs and
       throwing off a vapour                 trail       at
       a   greatheight over   Lithgow
       this  week, frightened some
       housewives, who    thought the
       objects        were      flying   saucers.
           An      R.A.A.F.              spokesman
       said     to-day the           women          had
       seen     new      jets   on   manoeuvres.
