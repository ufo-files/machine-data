        This booklet represents our last look at significant U FO happenings of the modem day UFO era in
    the 20th century. We started our little quest way back in 1 997 with our look at UFO related sightings
    and incidents from the northern Ohio area. And while we published similar material over the years
    stretching back to 1 965, this later day series was an effort first proposed by our friend and mentor,
    the late Allan Manak somewhere around 1 995. It was Al's intent to scour our rather substantial files
-   and present the "best of the best," as it were, in an effort to document the UFO enigma in a series of
    regularly issued booklets at a reasonable price for the serious students of the phenomenon. I n that I
    hope we have succeeded, although Al's illness and untimely death prevented him from seeing this
    fascinating and often times exciting journey come to its conclusion.

       Has all of this effort brought us any closer to cracking our sixty-plus year old mystery? Probably
    not, but possibly in the hundreds of pages of data that we have published over the years there may
    be clues present that will allow a future researcher to come up with the answers that have been so
    elusive thus far. And wil l that future researcher represent the physical or the social sciences? That
    too remains unanswerable to U FO research in the year 2009, although I sometimes feel that it may
    indeed be the latter. That some final answer wil l indeed be found and accepted in the future, I have
    no doubt - I just hope I'm still around to see it after toiling away in the ufological vineyards since 1 962.

       As has been my custom since the beginning of this series, I have chosen the sightings and events
    that I personally felt were significant for inclusion . And I have also used the original accounts as they
    were first published to avoid any unintentional distortion, as so often happens in any retelling of
    something from the past.

       I would also like to thank my wife Carol, for all of her tremendous help with this undertaking .
    Without her there would have been no series to finally finish.
