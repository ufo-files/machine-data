SUNDSVALL JOURNAL
Founded in 1841

Sundsvall, July 12, 1946

To the air defense department.


As requested by Captain Ragnar Westlin, Lv 5 Sundsvall, I hereby transmit a fragment (film/paper strip) which was found after a closer examination of a piece which is believed to a be part of an unexplained "projectile". The projectile in question was sighted between the hours of 14 AM and 14:40 AM on Tuesday, July 9, 1946 at the Norrvikssand. Among other observers were a transport pilot named Lindén and a house wife by the name of Söderlund. Both own summer houses out where the impact is said to have occurred.
A Dr. Bäcklund at the Cellulose Company's research laboratory in Kubikenborg has presented me with a precious gift: some remnants of the “projectile”. He has studied these fragments with his microscope. It could possibly be of interest for further research in this matter.
Sundsvall Journal would be grateful to receive a separate notice regarding the observations the air defense department has been able to make in this case, as well as the conclusions you consider to be of value to the public.

Respectfully,

Editor A. Morelius
