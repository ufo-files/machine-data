           Contact Information


     Details
    223.5 linear ft.

    Collection contains correspondence, personal papers, and projects Salisbury was involved with, as well as
    books he authored and work he did in editing the Journal of plant physiology. Also included is Salisbury's
    work on UFOs.

    Photographs (P0823) and audio-visual materials (A0589) were transferred to the Multimedia Division of
    Special Collections.

    Salisbury was a Professor of Botany and Plant Physiology at Pomona College, Colorado State University, and
    Utah State University.
