                                                                      Its July and its time to watch out
                                                                for the dreaded Sky Lanterns that are often
                                                                             mistaken for UFOs!
                                                            MUFON always receives a lot of UFO reports that turn out to be
                                                            these objects. They usually float along with the breeze, and burn
                                                            out within 20-30 minutes. People report seeing a flickering, which
                                                               is due to the flame, and the color is usually red or orange.

                                                               But keep your eye to the sky because people do see a lot of
                                                                 UFOs during this month! And keep the camera handy.
