  Controversial comedian, actor, and author George Denis Patrick Carlin was
  born on May 12, 1937 in New York City.
  George was raised by his mother, Mary, who left the father when he was two
  months old. He attended parochial school and was a altar boy, to which he
  credited his avowed atheism by the the time he reached adulthood. Mary was a
  secretary and devout C-tholic, and his father, Patrick, was a national
  advertizing manager for the New York Sun.
  At fifteen he had quite enough schooling and dropped out of
  school in the ninth grade. He also developed a pattern of
  running away from home on a regualar basis, thanks to a
  contintuous relationship with his mother, and he enlisted in
  the US Airforce in 1954, at the age of seventeen, seemed like
  a good idea at the time. He was stationed at Bossier City, Louisiana,
  trained as a radar technician, earned his high-school
  equivalency, and, moonligthted as disc jockey on KJOE radio
  in nearby Shreveport, on the downside, he also received three
  court-martials and several disiplary punishments, was declared an
  "uproductive airman," and was discharged in 1957.
  While working at KXOL Rado in Forth Worth, Texas, George
  met co-worker Jack Burns,and the two of them formed a comedy team, refining
  that act at a coffehouse called the
  Cellar before moving to Los Angeles in February 1960.
