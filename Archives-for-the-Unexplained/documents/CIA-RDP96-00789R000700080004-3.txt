SUMMARY OF INFORMATION:




      a.    Probing the underground area outside the structure located an air-

vent a little way from the structure (see prob map, page 3A).          The air vent

goes down a good way into the ground.     I could hear "people" sounds from

below, and another sound not unlike the "rush" of subway tr·ains.        I

postponed following the air vent to its bottom until completing the probe of

the area around the building.     Once a stairway down was found, I did not

come back to the air vent.

      b.    There is a stairwell which leads out of the next-to-the-bottom

floor of the basement of the structure.       I could not determine how f~r down

the stairwell went Cin terms of feet>, but followed it to the bottom.

      c.    <See FIG. 1 for a graphic sketch of the following) At the bottom

of the stairwell a small corridor (approximately 15'-20' in length> goes off

to the immediate left of the last step.       As you go down this short corridor,

there is a door on your right which leads into a very large "tunnel".         This

door is locked and is not used.     I had the impression of a sign on the door

which said, "VVOD NELZHYA" ("Entry not permitted" in Russian).

      d.    At the other end of the short corridor, there is a turn to the

left, as the corridor continues for approximately 10 feet.         At the end of

this is a heavy metal door which stands open.

     e.      On the other side of the door is a single flight of steps down,

ending in a landing which has a doorway to the right.     This doorway is

topped with an "archway".     The door goes through a very thick cement and

metal wall.     The door of this doorway is in the middle of this wall, and

closes by sliding down from the top (see FIG. 21.

     f.      Beyond this door is a room which is approximately 15' X 30'.     It

has a small area which appears to be dedicated for medical attention, but

the rest of the room is lined with what appeared to be racks of electronic

equipment.    The equipment is very plain-fronted (not many dials, lights,

knobs, etc.).    The room appears to be for a group of people to work in, but

definitely in a "no-frills" working environment.

     g.      There was a person in this room at the time of viewing.   The

person had a clipboard with what appeared to be a "checklist." on it.
