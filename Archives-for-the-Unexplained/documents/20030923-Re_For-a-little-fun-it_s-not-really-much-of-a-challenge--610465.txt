   Carnac wrote:


            I think most people who observe phenomenon like this, such as spontaneous
            bouts of insight into future events ;), for example, are simply normal
            people who have no need to find the logic behind the experience. Whereas we
            as scientists tend to pull things apart and want to know the inner workings
            of the things we study, I think most normal people who experience paranormal
            events are content to believe that 'it just occurs', and don't need to know
            why.


   The only difference between the stories of extraordinary and spooky occurrences
   that come from true believers and those from skeptics is that where the
   narratives of the former end with a "wow!", the narratives of the latter
   continue from that point with a, "...and then I investigated this and checked
   out that, and shortly a reasonable explanation presented itself". It's the
   only difference between a skeptic and everyone else.
