               THE EXPECTATION OF ALIEN CONTACT IN THE NEW MILLENNIUM

                                                   By Omar Fowler


          Will our entry into the "New Millennium" offer us the increased prospect of Alien
          contact throughout the world?

          First of all we should clarify our understanding of time and the calculation of our present-day
          calendar. It is generally understood the Christian ''New Millennium" does not commence until
                   st
          January 1 2001. Taking into account other religions of the world, the year varies from 1420
          to 4635. In other words, as many Alien contactees' claim to have been told, ''Time is of your
          own making".
                   th
          As the 20 Century draws to a close, we can reflect on the changes that have taken place in
          the general public's acceptance of the UFO phenomenon.          At the beginning of the 1900's,
          there was little reference to the possibility of Alien visitation to our planet Earth, although the
          science-fiction thriller 'War of the Worlds" by H. G. Wells, had promoted great interest in the
          possibility of Alien life existing on the planet Mars.

          In the 1930's a number of science-fiction magazines appeared on the news-stands. The
          "Astounding Magazine" arrived with spectacular covers and sensational stories from a new
          brand of science-fiction writer. This was followed by a succession of cinema film serials, such
          as ''Flash Gordon" and '13uck Rogers" which gained great popularity at the time.

          A few years ago, I interviewed an old retired Major and WWl flyer, who had seen a glowing
          UFO plasma ball over the South Downs in Hampshire in 1938. He thought that it was a secret
          weapon being developed at F amborough, a few miles from the incident. However it was WW2
          that really changed matters, when things began to get deadly serious!

          Survivors of WW2 related a series of bizarre incidents, for instance a British soldier who was
          at the '13attle of Tobruk'' (1942) told me how the British troops and the surrounding Germans
          were fighting each other with great ferocity.     It was then that both sides realised that silver
          coloured globes high above the battlefield were observing them. The British thought that the
          globes were German observation craft of some kind and the Germans thought that they were
          British! The result was that both sides commenced firing on the silver balls, which then
          zoomed skyward and out of sight. However it was in the air war that things came to a head,
          when British night bombers and American night fighter aircraft reported seeing balls of light
          following their planes.    I recently received a letter from an ex-Lancaster bomber pilot, now
          living in South Africa. He related how his aircraft had been followed by a number of lights,
          while on a bombing run over the Ruhr in Germany.          The rear gunner of the Lancaster has
          started firing at the balls of light, but without any effect and he eventually gave up and decided
          to save his ammunition.

         It was when WW2 finally ended and peace came at last, that the UFO phenomenon really
                                                                           th
         began to materialise in earnest. The now famous sighting on the 24 June 1947 by American
         pilot Kenneth Arnold, really started the ball rolling.    He saw a number of scimitar-shaped
         objects flying at high speed near Mount Rainier U. S.A. and described then as behaving like a
         saucer skimming across water and so the word ''Flying Saucer" was introduced into our
         modern vocabulary.

         Although in the 1950-60's we were in the midst of a " Cold War" with the Eastern Bloc, the
         public were completely unaware that both the NAT O and Russian forces were becoming
         increasingly concerned by their defence radar units tracking high-speed, disc shaped craft,
         flying at high altitude across the "Iron Curtain". In one instance the radar tracking of
         unidentified UFO's nearly starting WW3!     It was not until many years later that a courageous
         American ex-serviceman called Bob Dean blew the lid off the world-shattering events that had
         taken place at that time.

         Bob Dean had been an American Army; Master Sergeant based in SHAPE headquarters in
         Europe and had the highest " Cosmic Security" clearance. It was during 1963 that he
         discovered the UFO incursions were becoming such a major problem that a secret study of the
         UFO's had been initiated by British Air Marshall, Sir Thomas Pike.         The study took three
         years to complete and was carried out by some of the world's top scientists.          Their final
         conclusion was that "The Planet earth is and has been, the subject of extensive, massive and
         very thorough detailed survey by several extraterrestrial civilisations. These civilisations
         have demonstrated an extremely advanced technology, possibly hundreds or even thousands
         ofyears beyond ours!"
         Bob Dean was not the only American ex-serviceman to reveal UFO events that had previously
         been kept under wraps. It was another American ex-serviceman, Larry Warren who disclosed
         the astounding events that had taken place in woods near the American Air Force base of
         Bentwaters in Suffolk in December 1980.

         Larry stated that he base at Bentwaters held the largest Nuclear arsenal in Europe and he was
         one of the armed American Air Force police stationed around the base to protect the Nuclear
                                                       th
         arsenal from intruders. On the night of the 29 December 1980, an American forces police
         patrol observed lights in the direction of the nearby Rendlesham F crest and Larry Warren, in
         company with other security forces, boarded a military truck and drove off to investigate.

         Larry recounted the incident: ''We came over a rise in the ground, I remember we saw this
         illuminated field and in the centre of this field was a huge light. There was a flash and in place
         of what had been an illuminated mist stood a more solid looking object. It was about thirty
         feet across and about twenty five feet in height." ''The object was surrounded by our security
         police at intervals of about ten feet. Then a light moved out of the side of the object and split
         into what appeared to be three individual beings of some kind. I saw clearly what looked like
         eyes, facial features, clothing and some other device, but I couldn't make out the legs and the
         lower extremities, it was almost as if the light was translucent at that point."       Then one
         "being'' moved towards the front and was approached by the senior base commander. There
         was no sign language, no handshakes and a meeting that could only be described as a "face
         off'.

         (Full details of the incident are revealed in Larry Warren's book "Left At East Gate.'')

         These have been just a small selection of well-documented UFO encounters that have
         occurred over the years, but what of the future?

         One of the more intriguing type of UFO craft that has appeared more recently has been the
         arrival of the ''Flying Triangle" craft which have been reported in increasing numbers World­
         wide.

         They were initially seen in Missouri and Kansas in 1980, when hundreds of witnesses,
         including policemen, watched a huge craft, displaying a triangular pattern of lights, move
         slowly over the countryside for a period of four hours. The whole event was tracked on Radar
         during the period and must rate as one of the most awesome sightings to date

         The American incidents were followed by the ''Belgian Flap," when huge triangular craft
         visited this small country during the period 1989/90.                                                              The Belgian UFO group ' S OBEP S'
         were inundated with hundreds of reports during this period. Such was the efficiency of their
         organisation, that at one stage the Belgian Air Force asked for their co-operation and
         assistance! This resulted in two F16's intercepting the ''Flying Triangles," although they were
         completely unable to match the speed and manoeuvrability of theFT's, as subsequent cockpit
         radar film footage showed.

         On the night of the 26th September 1993, the residents of Bakewell, in Derbyshire, were
         treated to the sight of a huge black silent craft flying slowly over their town.                                                                                      With a bright
         white light on each corner of its triangular shape and numerous smaller lights running along
         the edges of the craft, it flew very low and at a speed of only 3 Omph. (A witness driving his
         vehicle at 40mph overtook it!)                                    The FT craft then performed several slow manoeuvres over
         the town before returning to its original course and flying slowly out of sight. Just over three
         months later a British Airways passenger aircraft was 'buzzed' by a glowing triangular craft as
         it approached Manchester Airport.

         The 'Flying Triangles' then began to arrive here in force, with 52 FT incidents being recorded
         in and around Derby from December 1994 to May 1995. This included FT craft hovering at
         night and shining down white beams of light onto the countryside. They have been observed
         close to the Rolls-Royce engine works at Sinfin and one was reported hovering over the
         A5111 roundabout "Spider Island" at Allenton.                                                             Numerous witnesses have also reported
         seeing them over and around the Derbyshire countryside late at night.

         While nobody knows what the future may hold, it appears quite likely that Derby could well be
         in the forefront of Alien visitations in the New Millennium!

                       AMAZING REPORTS FROM THE PAST
 The continuing revelations made by witnesses of hitherto unknown events is one of the intriguing aspects of
 UFO research. Many of the reports have been discovered as the result of Radio/media coverage given to
 your editor in recent weeks. A selection of reports have been included in the following pages.

 HOVERING 'BOW TIE' OVER LONDON IN 1996

 On the 9th of November 1996, Mrs Woolley in the company of her son and her two grandsons left their
 home in Derby and made their way to London to see the 'Lord Mayor's Show'. They later moved on to
 Waterloo Bridge, over the River Thames, where they watched the firework display, which commenced at
 5pm and lasted about half an hour.

 As they were making their way towards the southern end of the bridge, they noticed that the people around
 them were pointing to something in the sky. They looked up and saw a 'butterfly' shaped object, with very
 bright lights, moving around the area above the water.

 "It was spinning around very fast, whilst moving slowly towards us. It seemed to come right down to within
 a few feet of the water, then at lightning speed, it went out of sight, making everyone gasp. A lot of people

 moved away, saying that it was part of the firework display, but we stayed there and within a few minutes, it
 came back again. It came right down to the water again and I remember saying how strange it was that we
 could see no reflection or shadow. It defied logic and I felt that we were witnessing something most unusual.
 I hovered slowly around over the water and then again at lightning speed it moved away., but without
 disappearing. It did this several times. It was eerie and the children were afraid, because we as adults didn't
 . know what it was. We observed it for about half an hour , then again at great speed it disappeared, first down
 river, then very high above the South Bank complex, until it had gone out of sight. We waited another ten
 minutes or so but it did not return."

 "It was not like a spaceship that you see on TV, more like some sort of apparatus, housing equipment. It
 ·appeared quite flat, no depth to it. It also seemed to move randomly and appeared light (not heavy). It was
 as if s0mething had come to have a close look at what was going on."

 "I was concerned and bought the paper the following day, but there was no mention of it."

 A similar incident took place in 1998

                    On the 23rd May at 8am Nfr & Mrs. MJ were about to set off to work from their home
 in Somercotes, Alfreton, Derbyshire. It was then that Mr MJ noticed a strange object travelling into view, a
 matter of only 200/300yds. away. The object was dark brown in colour and shaped like two pyramids
 facing point to point, with vertical stripes showing on their surface. The movement of the object was side to
 side and also an up and down motion (very subtle movements).
 The object remained in view for about 1-2 minutes and then slowly moved out of sight behind trees. After a
 short period of time, I observed it flying slowly away southwards, at the same height. I had time to look at
 the object through binoculars and noticed that it flew with the same movement previously described.
  According to Mrs. MJ, the stripes were more like ridges and it flew at an angle (as shown). It remained
 almost static for about 2 minutes, before it flew out of sight behind the trees. It moved slowly and was
  'bow-tie shaped'.   Mrs. MJ commented "It was definitely not natural, but a machine of some sort."

                                     A Saucer Sighting in 1986
 It was about 4.30pm on Sunday the 7th of September 1986 and Mr 'DR'. was sitting, facing due East, in
 his back garden at his home in Cumberland Avenue, Derby. It was a bright warm day, with a clear sky.

  Suddenly his attention was drawn to a large saucer shaped object that appeared over the rooftops of a
 house just a few doors away. "It was at double house height, about the size of a car and a burnished silver
 colour, I could see it sharp and clear" commented Mr 'DR'. "There was total silence and I noticed that all
 the birds that are usually in the garden had gone!" ( The "OZ Effect" ref Jenny Randles).

 "I clearly saw the underside, which was egg-shaped and there were brilliant lights of such strange colours
 that I couldn't describe them." " It's hard to describe them, as they were brilliant and yet stayed within its
 form and did not flash beyond its edge." "Then it turned on its side and I saw what I thought were dark
 portholes, there were at least a dozen of them."(See sketch).

 The observation took place over a period of two to three minutes and the 'saucer' moved away slowly in a
 straight line and then suddenly rose vertically and was soon out of sight. One of the strange effects
 experienced by the witness, was that he 'hyper-ventilated' and had a desire to communicate with 'them'.

 Mr 'DR'. later telephoned the East Midlands Airport, who confirmed that they had no trace of an aircraft in
 that vicinity. " I did not believe them, because soon after, about 30 mins. maximum, the sky was full of
 military aircraft vapour trails."

 " I hereby declare that what I have described is clear and the total truth." (Mr 'DR.' 12- 1-2000)

     A 'FLYING TRIANGLE' REPORT FROM THE FILES OF TIMOTHY GOOD

  We are indebted to well known author and researcher Timothy Good for the following 'FT' report.

  ''Whatever anyone will say, both my son and I will never forget the 19th November 1987" wrote Mrs Barbara
  Farrest, in a letter to Timothy Good, describing a sighting at Brierley Hill, West Midlands. On that evening
  (7pm-7.15pm) "The craft came so very low, to enable us to have a very close look. It was massive, wonderful
  and frightening."

  "There was some kind of mesh visible when it was directly overhead and black serrated edges, which looked
  as though they were grills. The inner part of the craft was a grey or silvery colour. There were stripes
  underneath. The lights were like fluorescent, very white.and bright. I also think the whole craft was able to
  light up." "As it went 'floating' silently away, one could no longer see the individual lights, or the shape of
  the craft, just a very bright light. When it was going out of sight it looked just like a bright star."

  Timothy Good wrote: "I was impressed by the FT sighting ofMr Saunders (PRA), particularly because the
  model resembles, to an extent, the drawing by Barbara Forrest, which she sent me."

                                     A SPANISH NIGHT VISITOR
         Our thanks to PRA member Colin Saunders, for bringing this unusual incident to our attention.
 On the night of the 6th July 1999, two friends Mrs D. Walker and Mrs E.Wilkes from the Leicester area were
 staying in a villa in a small Spanish village near Jolon on the Costa Blanca. The time was about 9.45pm when
 their attention was attracted by a large star-like light in the sky.

 ''We were sitting on the steps of the villa, overlooking the pool, it was just getting dusk. I looked up and said
 'that's a big star', we looked around but there were no other stars in the sky. It was a very clear, hot night
 and it was very still."

 ''We kept on looking up at this star, it was a bright silvery white. We thought that maybe it could have been
 a plane, but it didn't move. We then thought, could it have been a helicopter with a bright light at the front
 (like the Police have), but even that would have been flickering and this was stationary."

 "After about half an hour, the colour started to change to a cream colour and the shape started to change, it
 became oblong, longer and thinner. By now all the other stars were out and it looked nothing like them and
 it remained in the same spot. "

 "After about another ten minutes, you could see a red haze or mist around it. Everything was so quiet, it was
 weird. Even the Village dogs which are normally barking non-stop, were silent. We could hear some German
 neighbours shouting to each other as they watched it as well. It then started to get dimmer, as though it was
 going up and in within seconds it was gone completely. Shortly afterwards, the village dogs started barking
 again."

 ''I would say we had watched it for about 3/4 of an hour. It was clearly defined and gave us such a weird
 sensation. We didn't experience any time loss, but it was so strange."
