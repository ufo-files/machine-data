       The Psychic, newly religious, and writers of the sensational are all cashing in on
  the well established and now respected UFO groups all over the world.      In fact the
  "fringe•• element seems to have a passion to consume the UFO phenomena.
       UFO researchers have long struggled to become established in the community as
  responsible working groups.    Presenting factual accounts of sightings, correlating
  data, and making commonsense investigations without resorting to theorising on the
  source of the phenomena.
       The ••fringe•• element have clung unwanted jepordizing the credibi 1 ity of the
  researcher. Voicing their parrot 1 ike patter, and exciting romantic notions which
  many people at the present want to hear.
       The individuals getting on the UFO bandwagon    are almost a phenomena in themselves.
  Writing articles, lecturing, or spreading their views with confidence. Are they really
� interested in genuine UFO research.    I doubt it, all the hard work is provided by the
  investigators who do not seek the 1 imel ight. The 11fringe'' tend to point out a well
  known sighting or photograph (investigated by someone else), then turn to their
  favourite angle of theorising to convince the 1 istner of what is usually unfounded
  speculation.
       Here and there the following builds up, more speculation and less fact, let
  beware that scientifically minded groups are not swept along with the tide.
       Everybody is free to do what turns them on, a religious experience, seance,
  psychic,or UFO study, but the interests should be kept seperate. One may impinge
  upon the other but there is no reason to lump all the phenomena together in a confusing
  jumble of information.
       Let us give the UFO terms of reference, this is a seperate study. "The UFO, why
  is it here, where is it from".
       Scientists have now joined the ranks of researchers all over the world and are
  working together as never before. We must patiently continue to gather data as there
