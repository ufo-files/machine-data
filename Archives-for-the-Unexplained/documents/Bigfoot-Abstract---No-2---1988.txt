    Recently I had a couple of articles published which may be
of interest to Maryland Bigfoot followers.   First, the April
1988 edition of The GATE includes a piece by myself titled
"Maryland Bigfoot Captured on Film?" which is an attempt at
taking an objective look at Dennis Volkman's September 2 , 1986
videotape which he shot in Patapsco Valley State Park.
Reactions to this article have been mixed, but I feel that if
anyone is going to review a videotape of an alleged Bigfoot taken
here in Maryland, it should be me.   I tried to give it full
and fair consideration.   Secondly, the BIGFOOT CO-OP for April
1988 included my "Encounters With Maryland's Bigfoot" in one of
their "On The Trail - Journal of a Researcher" columns.   This
deals with the unknown animal tracks which I discovered on
1-17-88 in Laurel, Maryland and includes   some area Bigfoot
sightings.   If anyone is interested in obtaining pictures of
these tracks, drop me a line and we'll work out some type of
arrangement.   I shot one roll of color and one roll of black
and white and they both turned out well.   Is Bigfoot really
wandering P.G. County these days?   I kinda doubt it, but I
certainly can't explain these tracks and I do have great faith
in several eyewitness accounts from the 1970's.   I also wouldn't
rule out that Bigfoot could be travelling by sewer (akin to those
Buena Park, California reports of 1982), which could explain
his ventures so close to the big city.
