Jan. 1, 1988
Do aliens need
human genes?
Chicago Tribane

apes and humans coupled with ad-
vances in test-tube fertilization, em-

being spliced by scientists trying to

ing premise of Budd Hopkins' book,
"Intruders: The Incredible Visita-
tions at Copley Woods," which is
being marketed and receiving re-
spectable reviews as a work of popu-
lar science, not fiction.
Hopkins presents the story of an

DNA, Hopkins speculates.

fragile-looking bodies. Oddly, they're
unaware of what a modern molecu-
