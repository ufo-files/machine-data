                             CLONES, ILLUSIONS, DECEPTIONS


Well blow me down!        Our old UFO chum Rael -he who created the "Raelian Movement"­
shocked us over the Christmas/New Year break by announcing his scientific/ medical wing,
"Clonaid" has successfully cloned a human baby, "Eve" on 26th December.               His exotic
representative, Dr Brigitte Boisselier, was headline news for a week detailing this truly
momentous event (If true, it surely would be!)        Unfortunately, since then, all attempts to
establish the whereabouts of "Eve" or release a photograph have come to nothing. "Clonaid"
have stated that the full identity of "Eve" will be protected until she reaches maturity.
Apparently another 50 such clones could be on their way according to "Clonaid".


Cloning     might be considered a natural development of Rael's original messages received in
1973, when as humble Claude Vorilhon, a sports jou rnalist in France, he is purported to
have received contact with the "Elohim", an extraterrestrial race responsible for life on planet
Earth.     Doing cloning of their own they eventually created humans.        Rael/Vorilhon was
selected by the Elohim to create an embassy on their behalf and await their arrival in 2035.
The physically attractive and charismatic, Rael, got very busy mainly in Canada, prancing
around in his all white gown like some angelic deity; advocated free love, and the "Raelian
Church" now has 32,000 members!         Of course nobody can prove any of this but why spoil
the fun!      Likewise, on past experience, any access to scientific/medical testing of Raelian
clones will be hard to come by.      A typical cop-out is invented - "sorry you can't have access
to the cloned baby as the baby and parent(s) must be protected against media and scientific
intrusion".    Hence nobody knows if we have a real event or a Raelian publicity stunt.


This modus operandi seems to follow a familiar pattern to the REAL Iraq crisis currently
upon us.      The decision to have a "regime change" there was taken last year.        Make an
enormous scene about "weapons of mass destruction" which Saddam may or may not still
have.    Weapons inspectors find hardly anything even though full access is allowed.          Every
confession by Iraq is greeted with "not enough - where's the nerve gas, the anthrax" etc.
Clearly the politicians bent on this enterprise wish to create an illusion of going along a
peaceful path, but their body language suggests otherwise.


Our final visit to the world of illusions concerns images presented by a group called
"Euroseti", at the National Space Centre, Leicester, at the end of January.     These purported
UFOs are blown up images        taken from the Solar Observatory satellite, (SOHO) as it orbits
about a million miles from Earth.       The mouth watering advertisement in UFO magazine
headlines     "UFOS----THE     TRUTH----    THE PROOF'.         CIUFOR      representatives    have
examined this footage both at the Space Centre and on a CD-ROM presented to all
delegates attending.    Yes, there are things floating about in the vicinity of the Sun which are
unknowns.      The question is whether these "blobs" could be spacecraft containing intelligent
beings or are just wishful thinking on behalf of Eu roseti.  The objects are 80-100 million
miles away and must rate as the furthest objects out in space ever claimed as UFOs in most
peoples' definition of the word.      It is quite obvious that the SOHO images will remain
"unidentified", but this seems to be another example of hi-jacking NASA footages to
promote unscientific theories - such as the "Martian Face" in the past.     A detailed report is
set out on page 12.
