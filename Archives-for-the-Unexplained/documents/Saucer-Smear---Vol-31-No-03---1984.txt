     DOCTOR HAINES "MUZZLES" NAUFOF BOARD MEMBERS!

           Long-time "Smear" fans are familiar, we
     hope, with the North American UFO Federation
     (NAUFOF ) , formed in 1982 and headed by one Dr.
     Richard Haines of California. Other members of
     the NAUFOF Board include Pete Mazzola of the SBI,
     who is Deputy Director; Walt Andrus and Ann Druf­
     fel of MUFON; Dr. J. Allen Hynek of CUFOS; Rick
     Hilberg of the NUFOC and other groups; Charles
     Wilhelm of OUFOIL; John Musgrave of the Edmonton
     (Canada ) UFO Society; and, last & certainly least,
     Richard Hall.
           Since we of "Smear" are friends with most
     of the Board members, and since we approve    the
     goal of unity in Ufology, we have sincerely tri­
     ed to take a positive attitude toward this organization (knowing as we
     do of the failure of many previous such efforts. ) Unfortunately, how­
     ever, Dr. Haines has taken a suspicious and almost paranoid attitude to­
     wards us, as evidenced by his letter in the Jan. 20th "Smear" and    in
     other ways thereafter. A mild-mannered, truth-seeking phone call we re­

     apparently sent by him to all members of the Board, for their eyes only.

      In the wee morning hours of April 15th, your humble editor partici­
pated in part of a six-hour radio tribute to Long John Nebel, who from
1956 till his death in 1978 was the most popular all-night talk show
host in New York City. John got his start on WOR radio by promoting the
Howard Menger UFO contact story endlessly, and went on to do hundreds of
flying saucer shows over the years - many of them including your editor.
Other participants in the tribute included Sanford Teller, Candy Jones
(LJ's widow ) , James Randi, Lester Del Ray, and David Field. Host of the
event was WABC's Alan Calmes (though John was never on WABC, and Calmes
never met Long John in his life. ) The show was well done and in good
taste....
