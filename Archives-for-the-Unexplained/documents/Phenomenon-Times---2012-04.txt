Q-Station: This issue will look at some results the group obtained in their
investigation at Q-Station. Q-Station is the name now given to the old
quarantine station located at North Head near Manly. This was the first stop
for many ships arriving in Australia for over 140 years. Q-Station is
renowned for its many ghosts, and so the team thought it would be a good
place for an investigation.
During this investigation they looked at many of the most haunted buildings
in the complex. Although they experienced a lot of activity neither Kellie or
guest medium Sharon Hood picked up much information in these places. It
was not until they began the spirit board that some useful information was
obtained.
Using the spirit board they made contact with a spirit that identified himself
as Paddy. Unfortunately no surname was given, and Paddy is a nickname.
The spirit did give his time of arrival as October 1887, and the name of the
ship as the ‘Diamond’. He also gave a year of death as 1917.
My research has shown that there were two ships with the name ‘Diamond’
operating in Australian waters at that time. One was a cargo ship that was
carrying timber, and newspaper reports have the ship in Brisbane in early
October, preparing to head to Sydney. These same newspaper reports also
mention an outbreak of malaria on board. I could find no list of names of
those on board the ship beside the captain.
