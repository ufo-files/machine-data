       The UFO Movement: In "philosophical disarray."

            Budd Hopkins, chief guru of the UFO-abducLion Movement, and
       his close associate, psychiatrist Dr. Rima Laibow, have had a
       bitter split since they appeared together on the Oct. 14, 1988 TV

       principal deputy Dr. David Jacobs, for "alerting and sensitizing
       the therapeutic community" to the problem, but she argues that
       trained psychotherapists    are better    equipped to help the
       subjects.
