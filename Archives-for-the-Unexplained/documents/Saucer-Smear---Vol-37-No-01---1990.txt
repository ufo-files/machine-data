PLAINTIFF AT LAST!
       So says embattled Bigfootologist Erik Beckjord, who
claims that as of Jan. 5th, Judge Douglas Robinson of the
Whitman County District Court in Washington State dismiss­
ed the criminal "death threat " complaint against him for
lack of jurisdiction. This ruling now frees Beckjord to
file criminal charges of his own against Fred Twigger, al­
ias Mark Francis, for five counts of forgery and    three
counts of perjury.
       All of this comes about because of Twigger's alleg-
ed attempt to frame Beckjord in regard to several "death threat " letters,
which Twigger apparently wrote to himself.

wind " alone, or whether he will name two Bigfooters from the Pacific North­
west who were allegedly co-conspirators in the "death threat " scheme.

weird aviary of secret agents with bird names. Beckjord weighs in as Gos­
hawk, though he is known to us as GASHAWK. Your editor, of course,     is
known merely as Buzzard.
