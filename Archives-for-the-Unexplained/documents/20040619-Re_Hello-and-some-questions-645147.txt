  Intriguing. I don't know what the OP's position is, but IMO that
  definitely qualifies as at least a partial form of Lucid Dreaming.
  The hypnosis is rather interesting, as well.
  I'd suspect that someone's already done so but it would be interesting
  to see what conclusions can be drawn about brain activity while
  hypnotized, and to compare that activity to the brain activity
  while awake and while dreaming (and possibly while Lucid Dreaming,
  if that's possible).
  A Google search coughed up
  http://webhome.idirect.com/~kehamilt/psydef4.html
  which might be useful for clarifying sleep states, and shows that
  someone out there's thought about this quite a bit already (not
  too surprising).
  http://chiron.valdosta.edu/dbriihl/intro4anotes.html
  apparently is a series of notes for a class, outlining a lecture,
  which may be somewhat informative.
  http://serendip.brynmawr.edu/bb/neuro/neuro01/web3/Hoegler.html
  is a student's paper relating a personal experience (apparently
  Melissa wasn't susceptible to hypnosis) but also some facts.
