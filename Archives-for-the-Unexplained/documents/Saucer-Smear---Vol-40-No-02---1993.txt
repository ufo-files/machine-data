  our forty year history, and he may be right.

      The January issue of the MUFON UFO Journal (now called the Mutual UFO
Network UFO Journal) contains an article entitled, "The Last Word on Ed
Walters?" The question mark is appropriate, as there may never be a last
word on this case. In brief, MUFON's reinvestigation of the case concludes
that the UFO model found in Ed's former home was not made by him, and that
Tommy Smith's testimony against Ed is inaccurate. We agree about the model,
but MUFON gives no reason why Smith would lie, and they ignore the testi­
mony of other former teenagers whom Ed knew. Our conclusion is, as it has
always been, that the case is a hoax, but we still like Ed and wish him
well. Incidentally, rumor has it that Ed and his wife Frances are (again)
about to be divorced, and that Ed is working on a second book....
