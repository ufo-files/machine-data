Alfred Burtoo had been a keen fisherman since his childhood days, now at the age of 77 he enjoyed
night fishing along the bank of the Basingstoke Canal (Nr.Aldershot, Hampshire).
When he arrived on the canal bank, Alfred positioned himself at his favourite spot opposite the fresh
water outlet on the other bank. It was here that the canal water was clear and there was no chance that
his line would snag on any weed. Alfred was sitting about a hundred yards from the nearby roadway,
which at that point, passed over the canal on a small bridge.
Everything seemed fine, it was such a quiet and still night. He poked the base of his umbrella into the
soft earth and tied his dog's lead around the base. Next he laid an old overcoat down on the ground, the
dog could lie on that if he wanted to.
As he unpacked his tackle box, he heard the clock at the nearby Buller Barracks strike 1am, it was over
half a mile away, but the night was so still the sound carried easily.
He was now ready and all his tackle was set, but he preferred to wait until the dawn when the water was
calm and still and the water temperature was just about right. He sat there watching the water, waiting
for a sign that the fish were feeding.
Although it was only just quarter past one in the morning, it was possible to see details clearly on the
surface of the water. There was nothing to do at the moment he thought, a nice cup of tea would go
down well.
Alfred reached into his tackle box and brought out his thermos flask, he slowly poured himself a cup of
tea, then he paused, carefully setting the cup down on top of his tackle box. Something had caught his
eye, he noticed a light moving across the night sky...
Now the light was becoming brighter and it was
coming in his direction, approaching from the South.
It seemed fairly low and was travelling quite fast.

Alfred noticed a row of illuminated "windows" around the circumference of the craft and looking up, he
