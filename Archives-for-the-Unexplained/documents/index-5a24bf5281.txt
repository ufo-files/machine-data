  This could be dangerous and damaging book. A psychologist is telling her patients that they need to look
  into their "past lives" for solutions to their current personal problems. While this reviewer is willing to
  entertain the possibility of reincarnation, Fiore is behaving as though it were proven fact and is basing a
  method of therapy upon it. She gives people an easy escape from responsibilty: Instead of facing our own
  problems in the current life, we are encouraged to blame them on events that happened, say, in Cleopatra's
  day. While this approach may have a certain placebo effect and indeed help some patients, others are going
  to be lured away from the proven, present-day treatment they need. This is another in a long line of
  dubious books in which hynosis is used to "retrieve" deeply hidden memories. Unfortunately, such
  memories can be just as easily confabulated, and unless you have an outside reality to check them against
  there is no way to be sure which is which. Our advice to astral travellers: Solve your current problems in
  your current life. -- Glenn Campbell
