         5 men                                see
                'saucer'
              Five   men        —

                                         four Security
        patrolmen and a milkman —
        claimed    yesterday that they
        had   seen   a  strange bright
        object streaking across     the
        sky in  the  early hours of the
        morning.
           Each of the men    said they
        had always scoffed at "flying
        saucer" stories.
          "But  we're convinced    now
        that there's     something in
        them,"        said          two watchmen
        who        watched           the phenome-
        non     together.
           Mr.       J.    B.    Hehir,        a     Com-
        monwealth               Security           patrol-
        man,  said:
          "It had   a silver,                      yellow
        and  reddish colored                       tail.It
        streaked across                   the sky          at
        great speed.
          "It seemed   to come from
        the  direction of Sunshine,
        and disappeared towards the
        Dandenongs                  at    a   height of
        about         1,000ft."
