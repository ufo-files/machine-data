In 197S, in the high mountains of Arizona. forest worker Travis Walton, in full view of 6 eo-workers, including best friend and crew
boss Michael Rogers. was blasted by a bolt of mysterious energy. whisked away in an ominous UFO. and was eventually returned 5
days and 6 hours later. A totally documented and very famous case. Travis wrote his first book, The Walton Experience. In 1978
the movie, Fire in the Sky, was later based on this book. His new book, Fire in the Sky- The Walton Experience, tells the real
story behind the movie and goes on to give first-time revelations of startling new developments. Walton is an ardent family man
who still works in the lumber industry as a purchaser for an Arizona Corp., when he isn't traveling on the lecture circuit with
Michael Rogers. Rogers worked as a construction contractor for a period of time after the incident and in 1989 he went back to
work in the woods on the north rim of the Grand Canyon as a logger and tree planting contractor. Rogers did all the artwork,
cover and interior illustrations for the new book. Together they have spent much of the last 3 years on an international tour for
Paramount Pictures and now for the new book. Presentation: Rogers will detail the encounter andWalton his abduction. They
will speak of documented government intervention and newly discovered physical evidence in their ongoing experience. The
presentation includes a video and slides.
