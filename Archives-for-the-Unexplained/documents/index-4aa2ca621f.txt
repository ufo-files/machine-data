    Our Review                                                                                   Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  This is one of the classic conspiracy books, reprinted as a collector's volume. Written during the Cold War,
  it claims that Communism is a scheme secretly supported by the world' wealthiest capitalists -- namely a
  cabal of bankers and the Council on Foreign Relations (CFR). Includes a list of CFR members. May be
  useful for historical reference, tracing the evolution of conspiracy claims. What we found interesting is the
  long list of former government officials who contributed supportive blurbs for the book, including
  Congressman John Schmitz, who wrote the introduction. -- Glenn Campbell
