        (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)   Source C:
        -- The activity consists of a very large vessel that can
be considered a freighter. This freighter is dark in color.
This vessel is carrying a very large amount of contraband in the
form of illegal drugs which it has received from the Far East.
This vessel is currently sailing north (sketch a).
        -- This vessel will soon off-load the contraband into a
smaller vessel or smaller vessels that are a little bit bigger
than canoes but smaller than a speed boat. The smaller vessel or
vessels are white and blue in color. The smaller vessel or
vessels will bring the contraband to shore. This off-loading
activity will occur at night around midnight. The man driving
the smaller boat is small and dark in color.
        -- The contraband is wrapped in plas·tic bundles. The
contraband will then be put into a very large convoy or truck
that will drive south down a highway. The convoy has big white
doors in back. There will be a helicopter that is part of the
scenario. A helicopter will be flying to the left of the convoy
monitoring it. (It is possible that the helicopter could be
carrying law enforcement agents who are monitoring the
situation).
        -- The area where this activity is taking place is very
warm and could be a resort area. It is very pretty. The area
could be an island.
        -- Also, the number "069" was seen on a side of a light
tan wall structure.
        -- Geographical coordinates associated with the overall
activity or off-loading activity are 12 degrees and 72 to 82
degrees. However, it could not be determined if 12 degrees meant
north or south and if 72 to 82 degrees meant east or west. If
customer knows approximately where activity is occurring, these
coordinates may be of help.
