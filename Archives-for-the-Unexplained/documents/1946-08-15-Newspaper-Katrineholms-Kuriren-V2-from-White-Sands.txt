August 15: Failing New Mexico rocket

”300 METER COLUMN OF FIRE WHEN V2 CRASHED. New York, Friday (TT from Reuter). A German V2 rocket which on Thursday was fired from the American experimental station at White Sands, New Mexico, misfired and changed directions several times during its flight. At 6.500 m altitude it lost its fins. Ground crew attempted to get the projectile to land and finally it crashed about 2 kms from the firing point. On impact a column of burning oil was thrown 300 meters up in the air. As far as is known, no human being was hurt.” 

(Katrineholms-Kuriren, August 16). [Willy Ley, in his book lists the August 15 crash as the tenth V2 fired from White Sands]
