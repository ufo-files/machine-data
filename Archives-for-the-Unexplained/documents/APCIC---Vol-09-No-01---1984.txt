    better defined. There were four red lights at the
    back of a white nose cone and the shape passed
    silently over the car, which had its engine turned
    off, and disappeared over the town leaving just
    a whispering of sound after it. The family,
    mother,, father and three. children were able to
    watch the lights for about five minutes.

   theory about UFOs and their sightings.

   more are due this month. In terms of timing the
   two stories are quite separate. But how strange
   it is that two parties, who clearly do not know
   each other, should be so linked. Whether or not
   you believe in UFOs it is enough to stand up the
