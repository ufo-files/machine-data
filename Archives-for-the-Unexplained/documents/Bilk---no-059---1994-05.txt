the most recent issue of the INFO Journal. Nr. 70,                   out in January,            has on pp.
7-13,    an excellent article by M1chel Raynal detailing sightings of whales with
two dorsal fins. None are recognised by zoology,                    but Raynal makes a good case

New England-sea serpent story was transformed into the giant turtle by be New
Zealand editor.
