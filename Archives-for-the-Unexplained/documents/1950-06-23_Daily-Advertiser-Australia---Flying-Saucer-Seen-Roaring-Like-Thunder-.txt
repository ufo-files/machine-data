Daily Advertiser (Wagga Wagga, NSW : 1911 - 1954), Friday 23 June 1950, page 3


       Flying Saucer
       Seen, 'Roaring
       Like Thunder'
         OAKLAND     (California) Thurs:
       A  'flying saucer', 'roaring like
       thunder at orver 1000 m.p.h.' was
       reported here early yesterday.
         Three   control-tower     officers
       [who followed   Its  course     with
       binoculars said that a disc-shaped
       object   dived  five   times  over
       Hamilton    Air Force base.
           It
            approached               at   altitudes     be
       tween 2000 and            5000         feet, shoot
       ing blue flame.

           Corporal          Roger        Pryor com
       mented:         'At    first       I thought       it

       was      a    falling star. But           It   didn't
       fall;    It    kept going.'
