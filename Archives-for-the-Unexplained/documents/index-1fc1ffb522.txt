  From the Book Cover

  Widely recognized for his pioneering work in UFO research, Dr. Vallee reveals what he has uncovered as a young
  scientist when he became involved in the mystery of unidentified flying objects at the height of the U.S. Air Force
  investigations in the mid-sixties.

  From the extraordinary Socorro landing of 1964 to the notorious "Marsh Gas case," Dr. Vallee details behind-the-scenes
  conversations and heated debates among researchers. He describes the hopes and fears he felt every day as new
  sightings were reported and as he and his colleagues struggled to develop research methodology for this new
  phenomenon.
