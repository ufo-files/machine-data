Pseudoscience and the
 U.S. Patent Process
  Mark Banash, Ph.D.
The topic of this talk is the patent process in the US. Did you know that an invention doesn't have to be de-
scribed in a scientifically accurate way to be granted a U.S. Patent? Learn why respected companies will invest
millions in a startup that cannot explain its processes and products rationally. And hear how companies can sell
such products without understanding how they work.

Mark Banash has a Ph.D. in Chemistry from Princeton and a B. A. with a major in Chemistry and minors in Math and
English from the University of PA. He is currently employed as a research scientist at Millennium Inorganic Chemi-
cals in Baltimore as well as at Howard Community College as adjunct faculty. He also writes science fiction and
serves as the producer of the audio series "The Funnt File" ( http://members.aol.com/tharsispro/series1.html )
which often deals with skeptical themes.

                                     THE TENTH EUROPEAN CONGRESS IN PRAGUE
On September 7-9 the 10th European skeptical congress will be held in the city of Prague, the capital of the Czech
Republic. All skeptics are invited to attend. There will be an interesting scientific program, and in addition, one or two
excursions will be organized through this ancient city, in the very hearth of Europe. Attending the congress will offer an
excellent opportunity to meet skeptics from the eastern and central parts of Europe. The venue of the meeting is in the
distinguished building of the Czech Academy of Sciences. Contact: icaris@bohem-net.cz Web:

                                           BE PREPARED FOR FRIDAY THE 13th
This year there are two Friday the 13ths, but to make this a regular annual event CSICOP will choose only one date per
year to act as the National Superstition Bash Day.
         This year's event will take place on July 13th and will include a major press campaign to all national media outlets.
CSICOP hopes to farm media inquiries to local groups throughout the country that are holding a Bash of their own. (See
http://www.csicop.org/superstition).
         Groups and individuals can participate, share their ideas and receive further information by contacting: Program
Director Amanda Chesworth at a.human@mindspring.com
                                                  SKEPTICS IN THE MEDIA
-        FOX Family's EXPLORING THE UNKNOWN returns: (hosted by Michael Shermer and X-Files star Mitch Pileggi)
starting Thursday, April 5 at 10pm, following Major League Baseball, which will hopefully provide a good lead-in audience
for the show. More details as we get closer, but if the ratings are good they may order more shows to be produced.
-        Beginning with the April issue, Scientific American now has a "SKEPTIC" column written by Michael Shermer.
The first column is entitled "Colorful Pebbles and Darwin's Dictum," based on a line from a letter Darwin wrote in which he
noted that "all observation must be for or against some view if it is to be of any service." And special acknowledgment
to Editor-in-Chief John Rennie for his support of skepticism and critical thinking in science.
