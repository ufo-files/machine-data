    E.     SESSION SUMMARY


    The target is a mountainous structure in size and is steep to
    climb.      It is rugged and rough in texture and consists of earth
    tones.     It is also similar to a tower and is wider than it is long.
    Even though the facade of the structure is steep it has elongated
    openings which are semi-pointed carved out of its surface.        It can
    appear quite ominous in its stature on land and water can be found
    in the vicinity.      The structure has an old appeal and is symbolic
    of a source of wisdom to some people in its cultural significance.
