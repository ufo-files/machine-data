    Our Review                                                                       Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  This book is mainly concerned with how to get "out of body." It offers eight different techniques, from
  dietary control to yoga. Appears to be well documented, with a list of bibliographic references for each
  chapter. (We can't say whether these techniques actually work, however.)

  Comparison to Other Books

  Although Rogo was a prolific writer during his lifetime, this is the only Rogo book we know of currently in print.

  Astral projection, the ability to "leave the body" at will, is a I phenomenon that has long fascinated both the scientific
