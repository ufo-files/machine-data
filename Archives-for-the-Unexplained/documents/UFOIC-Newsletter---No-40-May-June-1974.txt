SUMMARY


We have given details of some of the most interesting sightings.
Over a period from May              2 9th through to           September        26th,     there
were a total     of     74 sightings.               Most of them were similar or almost
the same as the reports we have given you.    This whole group
of sightings was investiga t ed by Mr. Bill Chalker a UFO IC and
APRO   representative,          assisted by            UFOIC's      research team over a
peripd of several months.                 At the conclusion of this exhaustive
investigation, Bill Chalker submitted a very detailed and
lengthy report which is available for purchase at a cost of
$2.00 per copy.             Orders for copies can be sent to the Research
                                                        ·
Director,     P.O. Box 6,        Lane Cove.  2066.  N.s.w.


A number of investigations in and around Boney Mountain were
made by the research team.  Nothing was found on the top of
Boney Mountain and it was felt that these things were coming
down well behind the actual mountain.                         This     area    however is
virtually inaccessible and it would necessitate a fully equipped
team with 5 or 6 days to spare to check the area out.


In conclusion,        we have been able to explain a small number of
these sightings as meteorites,                     stars,   planets etc. however
there still remains a massive proportion of sightings that
are totally     unexplainable and                  therefore can be classified as
UFO's.
