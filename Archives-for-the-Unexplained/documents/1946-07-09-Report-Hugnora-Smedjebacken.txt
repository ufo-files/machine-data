SMEDJEBACKEN DISTRICT

FALU-MORA DEFENSE FIELD & STAFF 
Department I e / L

Defense Headquarters

Falun

CLASSIFIED

9/7

14-15

A teacher by the name of Rune Karlsson, resident of Hugnore, Söderbärke, has supplied a report on his most unusual observation. On July 9, between 14-15.00 o’clock, he claims to have seen as a drip shaped object, bluish green in colour, fell into Södra barken (lake) between the villages Hugnora and Sörbo. The object had arrived from the northeast.

Smedjebacken, July 11, 1946.  Georg Hansson
Landsfiskal    [a police officer with increased authority – translator’s note]
