                             SHOW- ME UFO/lAC CONFERENCE

The UFO Study Group of Greater St. Louis, Inc. and the mutual UFO Network of Missouri proudly present the
1992 Show-Me Conference. This year's conference will be held at the Harley St. Louis Hotel in Earth city,
Missouri on Saturday, October 24. The Harley St. Louis Hotel is located just a little north oflnterstate 70 on the
Earth City Expressway. The Earth City Expressway is the next exit west of Interstate 270.


Once again, this will be a one-day marathon conference running from 9:00 a. m. all the way into the night, and
featuring world class speakers, five presentation on timely subjects and two panel discussions.


Featured Speakers:

David H Childress:
Author of many books about lost cities and ancient mysteies, including ancient aircraft and power sources. He
has also written books on anti-gravity and the unified field. His books include: ''Lost Cities of North and Central
America", "Anti-Gravity and the Unified Field", "Anti-Gravity and the World Grid", and "The Anti-Gravity
Handbook".


VirginiaM Tilly:
Ms. Tilly is MUFON's Director of Public Education and a cliuical hypnotherapist. She has worked with more
than 70 abduction cases and has contributed several articles regarding abductions to UFO publications including
"Resisting Resisting" in the August '92 MUFON Journal and "The Aftermath of Abduction" in a 1990
publication.


Dr. Thomas E. (Ecidie) Bullard:
Eddie Bullard received a doctorate in Folklore from Indiana University. His dissertation was written about UFOs
and folklore, and since then, he has carried out a comparative study of abduction reports. He has contributed
articles on abductions, hypnosis, and folklore to UFO literature, including the two volume landmark survey of
abduction literature, "UFO Abductions: The Measure of a Mystery".


Dr. John C. Kasher and Harry Jordan:
Dr. Kasher is a professor of physics and astronomy at the University of Nebraska. He is also a consultant at the
Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, where he specializes in electromagnetic theory. Dr. Kasher is a
consultant, state section director, and assistant state director for MUFON.


Harry Jordan is an M.F.A. graduate of Cranbrook International Academy of Art & Architecture. He is a lay
faculty member at Creighton Preparatory Jesuit School of Omaha. Since 1984, he produced numerous drawings
and scaled models of regions on Mars photographed by the Viking orbitors. His work is credited in the book
"The Monuments of Mars", by Hoagland. He is a special field investigator for MUFON.


Dr. Kasher and Mr. Jordan have collaborated on several Iowa and Nebraska investigations, including apparent
 landing sites.


Dr. DavidM Jacobs:
Dr. Jacobs is Associate Professor of History at Temple University. He is a leading academic authority on UFOs
 and abductions. He is the author of "The UFO Controversy in America" and "Secret Life, Firsthand Accounts of
UFO Abductions".
