The ex-"UFO EXPERT" who has held down a desk at the Ministry
of Defence  for three years and holds the equivalent rank of an
Army Major attracted a queue outside of Dillons Southampton
bookshop by the time I arrived at 6.30 on the evening of June
10th, the Daily Mail excerpts from his book,"Open Skies, Closed
Minds"(£14.99 from Dillons and countrywide outlets)and the UFO
grapevine etc saw to that!

Nearly 200 people packed the shop foyer by the time Nick was
introduced to us br, Dillons store manager, and what a gripping
talk our very own 'Fox Mulder," as the media have labelled him,
gave to us.

A quietish but forceful man, Nick Pope outlined his years at
The Ministry, and, although ensuring that he told us the views
he had were personal and not "official", ( did we note that
tongue in the cheek?), the anecdotes, followed by some
interesting slides, had the majority of us enthralled  even  •   •



yours truly, involved for some fifty years in "our subject",
learnt new ones!

Massive queues formed afterwards, to purchase the book, and to
chat with Nick. He gave his time generously to the mainly
intelligent questions asked, too, and I was lucky enough,
having missed my later 'bus and having an hour to spare,to be
introduced to Nick by his p ublishers representative, once I'd
mentioned the Southampton UFO group and a couple of my own
experiences.! learnt of Nicks next book, concerning the
paranormal aspects of contactees, percipients, and abductees,a
field I'm very conversant with!

As for "Open Skies, Closed Minds", I sat up late that night and
the next one,concluding that Nick Pope, obviously writing with
"authority" due to his MOD post, comes down firmly on the
"alien" answers, and has achieved a step forward into the next
really BIG step, which many of us feel is "just around the
corner", the "lid" being gradually lifted into whatever
scenario we are going to find ourselves in, hope fully more
prepared than in the past!Nick Pope is right at the cutting
edge of this still enigmatic mystery.The book covers much that
is already known, and much that reveals the "inside story"
behind it,and he is to be conflratulated for not only inspiring
those who have been "involved for many years   but for opening
                                             "
further, the minds of those, like the young 'student"types
present at the meeting, who are aware   of the subject and eager
to know more.

We are all aware of the peril our planet is in, and somehow,
somewhere, we are also aware of the concern felt from, "out
there"?Nick Popes book can only be a positive step towards a
positive solution.
