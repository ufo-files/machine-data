HERALD, Tamworth, England
Aug. 11, 2005
Did anyone else
remember this
UFO 'visit'?
ABOUT eight to ten years ago,
about a week or two before
Christmas, on a cold, frosty,
clear night, I was returning to
Coventry from a night out in
Wolston at about 10-10.30pm
when my partner of the time and
I witnessed something that has
intrigued and fascinated me
since!
An object about the size, shape
and colour of the moon passed
across the sky slowly, but in
what seemed to be a controlled
way and then disappeared over
the horizon.
It
only
lasted
several
seconds.
When we got back to Coventry
I reported it to the police.
About 5am the next morning
the police came to the house and
took a statement off me - and
told me there had been another
reported sighting from the
Tamworth area!
I have never heard anything
else off the authorities.
I would love to have a chat to
the other person or people
involved.
Are you one of those who saw
this?
Frank Holloran, 82 Dewis House,
Riley Square, Coventry, CV2 1LW.
