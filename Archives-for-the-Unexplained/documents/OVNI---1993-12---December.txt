                               CROP CIRCLE FORUM
                            (Reviewed by - Claire Upton)

The East Midlands branch of the CCCS hosted its first Crop Circle Forum on Saturday the
4th December at the Metro Cinema Derby.


The forum was presided over by Richard Smith who started things rolling by reading out
apologies from those who were unable to attend.      The venue itc;elf was excellent and the
audience were seated in comfort.


Michael Green the chainrum of the CCCS and Barbara Davis Secretary were guests and
the forum opened with a talk given by Karen Douglas. She spoke of the esoteric side of
the circles and the synchronicities connected with them and how they worked on the
unconscious to bring about changes of a metaphysical and psychic nature. She also asked
the audience to close their eyes and to remember where they saw their first crop circle, be it
real or a photograph in a book, and to remember what it meant to them at the time.


Following this was a coffee break and then Mark Haywood took the stage.             He spoke
about the formation which the East Midlands group have adopted as their own "Charley
Knoll and how it occurred to him, being an amateur astronomer that it represented the
constellation of Cygnus the swan.      He also told of how members of the study group
meditated and then drew formation shapes and how these shapes appeared in the actual
formation at Loughborough.


Alison Tredwell spoke next, she had the audience attempting to feel their subtle energy
fields, She spoke of the "Circlemakers" and what the circles mean to her.


Lunch followed and everyone ate well and swapped stories and photographs of the past
seasons' formations.


The first speaker after lunch was Andy Thomas of the Sussex Branch of the CCCS who
gave a highly entertaining talk with slides on the circles in his area of Sussex.     He also
spoke of their attempt to communicate with the "Circlemakers" and to catch a circle in the
making on video.       He showed    a video of a friend of theirs who is a channeller,
channelling a message from a person who calls himself Joeb, this was truly amazing even
if you do not believe in the phenomena of channelling it certainly makes you think.


Next to speak was John Hohnan Convenor of the Yorks and Humberside Branch who
spoke on how the circles seemed to have a pull on him and also about his "Campaign for
Alien Acknowl edgement. "


Last but not least a guest from the Yorks Branch Jim Lyons was persuaded to speak and
he gave an interesting talk on Quantum Physics, Euclidean Geometry and the Chaos
Theory.   He then lightened the talk with a demonstration of dousing for subtle energies
which was highly informative.


The Forum was then closed by Richard Smith and all in all an interesting day was had by
all.
