Mary Rodwell


Mary Rodwell
Mary, born in Litchfield, is the founder of ACERN - the Australian Close Encounter Resource Network -
an organisation specialising in helping alien abduction victims deal with their traumas.
For more than 15 years, she has studied the UFO and abduction phenomena and has travelled the world
giving lectures on the highly controversial subject.
Hundreds of people from around the globe - who claim to be alien abductees - have contacted Mary and
she says her hypnosis sessions have allowed them to discover the truth about their experiences.
A grey alien


A grey alien
Her book 'Awakening' forms part of her hypothesis that the human race is the product of alien genetic
engineering and that our DNA has been tampered with in attempts to accelerate the evolution of mankind.
Mary's most recent work suggests that alien intervention in the genetic makeup of children has greatly
advanced their social and intellectual development.
Mary's abduction scenario


Mary's abduction scenario
She has dubbed them 'Star Children' and claims that evidence of their existence can be found around the
world in the form of child prodigies - children with special powers and abilities way beyond conventional
understanding.

Furthermore, Mary says she has proof of her claims.
In this exclusive interview Mary Rodwell elaborates on her theories and discusses the UFO phenomenon at
large.
