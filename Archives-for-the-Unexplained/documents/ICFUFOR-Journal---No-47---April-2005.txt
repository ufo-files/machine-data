ICFUFOR Journal files No 47 April 2005


  Nahariya 28.2.2005


  Gil , 34 years old, reports: At 23:00 in west direction and close to the sea, his wife observed 3 lighted objects that
  between the northern 2 object, was a “flame like” connection. The view lasted for about 5 seconds, and afterwards, the
  “flame” split and the lights disappear. He added: “then I turned back, and saw a big light turning off and disappears in a
  second. It was big as the sun in daylight. Its’ color was a very bright yellow, and was seen for a split of a second. It was
  in west direction, with a slight turn to the south. It was about 700-800 meters height. To observe it, I was forced to raise
  my head. My wife estimates the objects moved. It a thing I can’t explain. It’s unusual and not understood. Its not like
  the light bombs, which I’m familiar with.”


  Beer-Sheba 20.3.2005

  18 minutes. It had the shape of 3 yellow lights, blinking at the same place. Sometimes it moved a little to the right and to
  the left. In the end, it took a jump in tremendous speed to the south. It had a triangle shape. It wasn't high, about 300-400
  meters.”

  Tel-Aviv      22.3.2005


  Luiz Cann , 24 years old, the soloist of the “Three Poison” band, says she saw a UFO. It happened about 23:00, when
  she was at Rothschild – Nahlat-Beniamin area.

  She saw in the sky a round spherical object, colored white that moved towards the earth. She claims that she recognizes
  meteors, but what she saw was different. The UFO hadn’t a trail, it was relatively big, and seems to be that it flies
  relatively low. It just disappeared in the air, as it turned off. The observation took about 3 seconds.

  Tel-Aviv 23.3.2005


  Shimon Shazian reports: “I saw a cigar shape object, moving from the sea direction towards Shalom-Tower, northern

  Ashdod      31.3.2005


  Assaf Barr , who’s UFO photos were published in our previous journal, reports that the UFO was seen again.

  It happened at about 17:00. According to Assaf, he had a previous feeling that it will appear again. He reports: “It
  happened when I went to look outside the window, as I usually do when I meditate. It happened in daylight, the exact
  thing I saw before. It was an amazing view. After I went out to observe it, it disappeared. I called my sister to bring me
  my camera, but it turned out that there was no film in the camera.”
