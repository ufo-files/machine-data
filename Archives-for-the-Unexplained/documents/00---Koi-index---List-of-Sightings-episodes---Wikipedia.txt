    Sightings: Heartland Ghost                                                                October 27, 2002

    In this creepy ghost story, a Kansas couple's claim that their Victorian house is haunted prompts a visit from the
    crew of "Sightings," a TV show exploring paranormal events. Nobody believes the couple's story - especially cynical
    producer Beau Bridges--but show psychic Miguel Ferrer begins feeling the presence of several entities, including a
    little girl.
