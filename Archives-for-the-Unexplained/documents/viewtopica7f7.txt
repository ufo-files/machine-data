  Radiation readings and MOD files
    by Observer » Thu May 10, 2007 2:59 pm

  Hi Admin

  If what you suspect is true, un released MOD files on the radiation readings could be interesting, especially
  for me as i was trained to take readings when i was in the Royal Observer Corps.

  I will try and put some sort of perspective on these alleged higher than normal readings.

  The Radiac survey meters [Geiger counters] issued to the USAF, ROC and many other military bodies during
  the cold war decades were designed to take readings of nuclear weapons fall out. They measured the dose
  rate in Whole units of Roentgens per hour.

  The hand held meters had a range from 0 - 300 RPH. The reason they only went up to about 300 RPH was
  because you could still take readings out side as long as you had protective clothing on and for short
  periods.

  There were not calibrated for taking Milli Roentgens, and if you tried they were very inaccurate. The hand
  held meters that the USAF had were only calibrated for the reasons i have explained. To get accurate milli
  Roentgen readings you would need a medical or geological meter. What they used was neither.

  So i take no notice of so called "significantly" higher than back ground readings perported by some people
  as i know the type of meter used and i know its capabilities.
