       1.   <SINF/SK>      MISSION:     Determine the present location of LTC Higgins.

       2. (S/NF/SK>   VIEWER TASKING:  003 began the session with an overhead,
       cutaway sketch of the building in which Higgins is being held.   Next,
       per prior request by 003, a clear acetate overlay, keyed to a Lebanon
       map sheet by reference marks, (placed on the overlay by the monitor in
       the absence of the viewer), was provided 003.   The orientation of the
       overlay with respect to the cardinal directions was unknown to 003
       {also by request).   003 proceeded to dowse this clear overlay with a
       long ruler.  An intersection of two lines, drawn on the map by the
       viewer using the ruler and a marker, indicated the approximate
       location of the target.   At this time, the monitor did not plot the
       position on the Lebanon map sheet, but provided 003 with another
       overlay (similarly referenced) keyed to a Beirut city map sheet.
       Again, the orientation of the overlay was unknown to the viewer.
       After carefully searching the overlay three times, 003 announced that
       there was no indication that the target was in this area.   003 then
       requested that the monitor plot the position of the first dowsing
       exercise (i.e. 1 the Lebanon map sheet).  This was done and the results
       shown to the viewer.

       3. CS/NF/SK/  COMMENTS:  003 places the target's location off the
       coast of Tyre, Lebanon (see enclosure), center of mass [35 degrees 07
       minutes East/33 degrees 21 minutes North].
