Case xxx. 
Falling barn blamed on ghost rockets

Time: August 11, afternoon
Place: somewhere in Norrland

”GHOST ROCKET CRASHED A BARN IN A NORRLAND VILLAGE.  On Sunday afternoon, in a place in Norrland, a recently built two-storied barn fell to the ground without visible reason. The farm people heard a sharp report and as they looked out through the window they saw the big building falling like a house of cards and turn into a heap of boards. Since one had, during the day, observed ghost rockets in the district and no other explanation for the fall was possible to get, the incident is connected to the appearance of the ghost rockets.” (Göteborgs-Tidningen, August 12). 

”A TORNADO DEMOLISHED THE BARN. Sundsvall, August 12. At the police inquiry on Monday concerning the on Sunday demolished big barn in a place in mid-Norrland, nothing emerged to indicate that a ghost rocket could have any part in the catastrophe. On the other hand, everything points to [the theory] that the building happened to be in the way of a tornado which Sunday afternoon crossed the district.” (Stockholms-Tidningen, August 13).
