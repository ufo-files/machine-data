  Publisher's Synopsis

  The story begins as a curious investigation into a machine that rests in the California desert. The investigation unfolds
  into the study of ancient monuments, legendary folklore, and the discovery of their true origin and purpose. The
  conclusions involve a history of conspiracies and the evolution of domination and fear in the Americas.

  Overlords explores the study of two very complex societies. We live in a human, worldly society beneath the elite. We
  also live in an unworldly arena, accessible to a predatorial species of non-human, electrical entities. Both groups hunger
  for the continued control and exploitation of the Earth's subordinates and underclass.

  The Earth's agnostic elite control with fear, uncertainty and promise of a natural existence while they betray us. They
  conspire to limit our knowledge and spoon-feed our experience through visual and subliminal channels.

  The unworldly are elusive, electrical units that prey on human energy for their source of nutrition. They feed on our
  human generated life force, the Aura that surrounds every living thing. They are not extra-terrestrial. They live within
  the unseen fringes of our society.


  From the Book Cover

  This is a true story about a dim-uncertain time in American history -- post World War II. It was the birth of the Atomic
  Age. Many lived in fear of communism, invasion and tomic annihilation. People lost faith. Leaders could not be trusted.
  Soon they began to look to other worlds for safety.

  Atomic energy gave mankind the power to self-destruct. An age of carelessness awakened the alien hierarchy. They are
  the ogres of the unworldly. Illusive, electrical units and predatorial. Human generated energy is their source of nutrition.
  We are their addiction.

  George Van Tassel began to build his time/energy machine in 1958. It was designed to recharge and rejuvenate cell life.
  He organized the first U.F.O. Space Convention at his small airport in the Mojave Desert in Southern California. He
  called his machine - The Integratron.
