I        (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)      Following are the perceived highlights
    from each source.

            (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)       Source A:

            -- The target site has a big, tall, and thin almost
    pyramid type shaped structure. It is off-white in color and
    situated on open grass land. Lights are present. Black asphalt
    and concrete can be found around the base as walkways for people
    (sketch 3a)
             -- There is a small body of water nearby where there are
    swans or ducks swimming (sketch 3b). This site is recreational
    for people and yet is a historical landmark. The target is
    located in the cultural seat of the city, which has fine
    architecture and well kept vegetation. This site is entertaining
    for people in that they come to see, visit, and tour a historical ·
    heritage and the things erected in people's honor. Food and
    entertainment are available in the area. Colored fountains or
    fireworks are at times displayed around the site. The target
    site draws large numbers of people and tourists.

            (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)       Source B:
            -- The target site consists of a fenced-in enclosure with
    one side partially obscured. There are areas which are overgrown
    and uncleared of vegetation reminiscent of weeds and vines.
    There is stuff piled up throughout the area. The target feels
    seemingly unkept.

        {S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)      Source C:

         -- I sensed that when the beacon person left his
destination, he made a sharp turn right (sketches 3f & g). At
first I could see a large white decorative object (sketch 3h). I
had to look up to see this and I don't think that this object was
sitting on the ground. It may be sitting on a base or foundation
of some type. When I kept looking at this object, it seemed that
the bottom was similar to the top. This object may be reflecting
itself, maybe from water (sketch 3i). This object was outside.
I also saw a plane flying in the air and then later on during the
session I had the feeling of a lot of planes (sketch 3j).
(Beacon person could have been near an airport).

        -- The site was a large flat area or square that was man-
made. This flat square was white or beige in color and it was
outside (sketch 3k). This area reminded me of an historical site
and it may have been set in a park type setting. There was a
large object sitting in this area that looked like a statue of a
man riding a horse. This statue was dark in color. To the side
of this flat monument was a very large square structure that was
quite colorful.

        -- The large square structure reminded me of a Greek
building (sketch 31). This structure could have been built based
on the Greek history.

        -- I then felt that I was inside a very large building
and the theme was history and art. It was very colorful inside
and I could see a lot of gold. It was very pleasant and one can
come here to enjoy themselves.  {The art and history looked like
Egyptian).

        (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)      Control Person:

        -- The target is a house or condo. There seems to be a
room, a bedroom with pretty flowered wallpaper in pinks and rose
color. The ceiling has a ceiling fan or ornate fixture. There
are shranks or wardrobes in different rooms and hallways. The
stair case goes downward and splits. People tour this building;
however, it is still a home. There are a lot of trees around as
you approach the target (sketch 3m).
