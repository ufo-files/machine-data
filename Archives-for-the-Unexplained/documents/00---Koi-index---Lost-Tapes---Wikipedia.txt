  Lost Tapes has received mixed reviews from critics. In a review of the season one DVD, TV Squad
  writer John Scott Lewinski gave the series a mostly unfavorable review, stating that in the case of
  this series, "Animal Planet could be accused of repacking a horror/sci-fi show as an animal
  documentary" and that much of the program is "outright bollocks." He did, however, state that the
  show holds some appeal for audiences looking for a scare, or "incredibly gullible people."[4] Emily
  Ashby of Common Sense Media gave the series three out of five stars, noting that while the acting
  was "subpar", the show overall was "simultaneously scintillating and bone-chilling."[5]
