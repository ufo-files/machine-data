    Our Review                                                                                          Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  Joseph Heller's classic black comedy of military incompetence. It introduced the term "Catch-22" into the
  American language, meaning a bureaucratic snafu that leaves no logical escape. Stationed in the

  The military will let you go home if you are crazy, so Yossarian tries to be. The catch is, if you want to go
  home, then you can't be crazy, so you have to stay. That's the catch -- Catch-22. This 1960s bestseller must
  have inspired Dr. Strangelove and the original M*A*S*H movie. An hilarious exercise in twisted logic
