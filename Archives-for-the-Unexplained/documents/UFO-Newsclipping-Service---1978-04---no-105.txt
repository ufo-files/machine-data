Palma d~ Majorca, Balearic Islands. (AP) -The Majorca newspaper
attributes to a group of technicians the expression that everything

of Puigmajor in this Mediterranean island, after several UFOs were
detected. The paper said radio sets suffered serious interferences

alarm in the station was great and observations increased.

which were flying at supersonic speed.
The paper stated the station technicians eliminated the possibility
that the objects were meteorites, since the radar only detects known
metals. The station's range includes a large Mediterranean zone, even
Corsica and Sardinia.
