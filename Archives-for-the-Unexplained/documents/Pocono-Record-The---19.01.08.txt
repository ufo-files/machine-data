19th January 2008…

UFO Sightings Not Alien To Poconos…

   The Poconos have seen their fair share of unidentified flying objects. A look through Pocono Record archives shows a long history of close encounters:

Sept. 19, 1957 - A flying saucer with a sphere-shaped tail coming from the east flies through the night sky. After being seen by two residents of Delaware Water Gap, the object quietly  escapes to the north.
March 1, 1973 - Shirly Pfeiffer of Summit Hill, state Trooper Jeffrey Hontz and 10 other West End residents report seeing 40 unknown circular objects flying from 500 to 2,000 feet above Saylors Lake.
March 21, 1973 - The same UFOs from Saylors Lake are seen in Northampton County and parts of New Jersey.
Oct. 13, 1986 - More than 100 UFO sightings are called in to the Monroe County Control Center when hot air balloons land in an East Stroudsburg University field and the Kmart plaza parking lot in East Stroudsburg for a balloon race.
March 3, 1991 - A fireball and a "plane" crash are reported in a wooded section of Normal Street in East Stroudsburg. Residents report seeing flames, smelling smoke and hearing a crash. Police, firefighters and ambulance crews search the area from  the ground and air for an hour and find nothing.
March 7, 1991 - Monroe County residents and others in the Northeast report seeing another fireball with a greenish tail moving through the sky from the northwest to southeast. This event was also witnessed by airplane pilots and observers from  Maine to West Virginia.

   The National UFO reporting center stopped counting calls after 100. Robert Gribble, director of the center, reported that it wasn't re-entry of space debris and there was no evidence of it  being a meteor.
