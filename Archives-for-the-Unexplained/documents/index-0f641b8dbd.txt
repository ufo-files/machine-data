  Publisher's Synopsis

  From a renowned expert on the paranormal comes an extraordinary work which explores such topics as the psychic
  powers of the saints and modern-day prophets, meditation, mental healing, super-human performance in sports, ghosts,
  poltergeists, and hauntings. Includes a "do-it-yourself" instruction chapter. Photos.

  Comments from the Author

  My book approaches the idea of Mind over Matter, long looked at strangely as only "paranormal," from the body-up
  perspective that all human physical (and mental) activity has some degree of Mind over Matter relationship. One's act of
  typing on a keyboard is, in effect, Mind over Matter, though not of the variety psychics talk of. The book works from
  this approach and discusses how the power of the Mind: Allows for peak performance in Athletics... Moves the chi, the
  life energy of our bodies, to allow Martial Arists to do the seeming impossible... Allows us to heal --- or harm --- our
  bodies, and possibly affect the health and well-being of others... Affects objects and events in our immediate
  environment, including computers, with PsychoKinetic ability... Causes Poltergeist outbreaks... And more... Including a
  chapter on psychic fraud, from the author's combined background as both a parapsychologist and a professional
  mentalist & magician. In addition, the chapter on how to move objects with your own mind is contributed by famed
  science and science fiction author Martin Caidin, author of CYBORG (The Six Million Dollar Man) and co-author of
  '95's hit science book A JOURNEY THROUGH TIME. Remember, you, too, have the power of Mind over Matter...
