Warwick Daily News (Qld. : 1919 -1954), Tuesday 29 April 1952, page 1




        Report of Flying
        Saucer Over                                    New
        Zealand
            WELLINGTON:                            A     report
          of a flying saucer                      over    New
          Zealand                 came            yesterday
          from         a         North            Auckland
          farmer, Mr.              J. Erceg, of Ko
          kopu,   13               miles   west    of
          Whangarei,                     who      claims   to
          have         seen       an           oval    boat
          shaped                 obiect          travelling
          across           the
                         sky              at     10.35 p.m.
          on     Saturday.
                Mr.    Erceg said the object
          was         bright blue,        and  seem
          ed     about           10ft. to 12ft. long.
          It          travelled            across             the
          night sky                 "slower            than        a
          meteor,                but faster            than        a
          plane."
            The saucer                   travelled from
          north  to south.
            Erceg claims to have seen
          it while  he   was returning
          with  his family   from                             an
            evening at the pictures.
