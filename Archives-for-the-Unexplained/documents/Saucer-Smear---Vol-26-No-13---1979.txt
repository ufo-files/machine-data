           The saucer convention called "UFO '79" was held as scheduled in sun­
    ny (but cold:) San Diego, California, on the weekend of November 17th, and
    your fearless "Saucer Yule" editor was in attendance.
           Speakers included Walt Andrus of MUFON, Dr. James Harder, non-sub­
    scriber Stanton Friedman, Dr. Leo Sprinkle, Jim Lorenzen of AFRO, and Cleve
    Backster, an expert on lie detectors and the secret emotions of plants.
    The convention host was a UFO authority from Phoenix, Arizona, with the
   improbable name of Hal Starr. ( * ) .
           Generally, the quality of the presentations was excellent.   Perhaps
    the most noteworthy talk was given by a NASA astrophysicist named Allen                                                                                        ·



    Holt, who might just lead Earthlings one step beyond Einstein, to an abil­
    ity to .. tunnel through" time and space, and reach distant stars in a matter
    of weeks rather than years.    This brilliant young man, currently working
    on his Ph. D. , apparently believes that the saucers are doing just this, and
    that we may be able to do likewise by the early 21st Century.
           A number of speakers failed to show up, for various reasons.   George
    Hunt Williamson, a notorious contactee from the 19 50's, was "scratched" due
    to pressure from AFRO;    Dr. Hynek had a conflicting commitment elsewhere;
    and the star (with a small "s") of the show, Major Donald Keyhoe, Long Re­
    tired, could not make it, allegedly due to the illness of his wife.      Ac­
    cording to Hal Starr (with a capital "s"), Keyhoe would have told us about
    some-thing called the Cosmic Watergate, whereby the U.S. Government    is
    threatening to put Keyhoe back on active duty (at age 80! ??) if he persists
    in his present ufological activities.    Somehow, we don't quite believe
    this:
           An ironic note was sounded by non-subscriber Yonah Fortner, who
    pointed out informally that the oldest existing UFO group is Borderland
    Science Research Associates (BSRA), founded in 1946 by the late Dr.    Meade
    Layne of San Diego. Whereas "UFO '79" formally honored Coral Lorenzen for
    having founded AFRO in 19 52, Layne's name was never once mentioned from
    the podium:
           A good time was had by all, and your "Saucer Yule" editor was pleased
    to note a more tolerent "vib" than in the past - regarding the speakers'
   ·attitude toward each other, and also toward our own humble efforts in this
    zine.   Might we modestly suggest that our theme - don't take yourself too
    damn seriously - is finally starting to sink in.    If so, our efforts have
    not been in vain.
