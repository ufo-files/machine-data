          Non-subscriber Charles Wilhelm of OUFOIL is "Coalition Whip" for the
   recently-formed Ohio Coalition of Ufologists. In an effort to chastise
   those Ohio Ufologists who have not cooperated with the Coalition,      he re­
   cently sent out a letter to many of them, in which he, in effect, asked
   them which of the following seven categories they fall into: " (1) Sorcer­
   er's Apprentices - the vocal "gee-whi zers" who spend a lot of time perform­
   ing their UFO magic show to astound their audiences;      (2) James Bond Types
   - those who are completely caught up in the conspiracy angle..     (3) Celebri­
   ty Types - those who are only interested in giving lectures, doing radio
   and TV talk shows, etc., to promote their Egos;      (4) Armed Guards - those
   who guard their files with such care that sharing doesn't exist in their
   world;      (5) Ass Sitters - those who just sit back and let others do the
   work;     (6) Prickassologists - any combination of the above; and (7) Rare
   Breeds - the very small percentage of bonafide individuals who work hard
   in trying to solve the UFO phenomenon without a care of making a name for
   themselves. " - Which group do we fall into, you might ask? We are The
   Rare Prickassologist Breed, i. e., a combination of all seven types. For­
   tunately, however, we don't live in Ohio, so none of this analysis applies
   to us • • • • •
