                                                                      23 April 1980



  MEMORANDUM FOR:   LTC R. W. HAWES, XO, ADCSOPS-HUMINT
  SUBJECT:   BG Flynn Update Re: GRILL FLAME (U)



  1. (S/NOFORN) Per your request, following information is submitted for
  your consideration for updating BG Flynn:
       a. IRAN SITUATION: Special Operations Division (SOD), JCS requested we
  intensify our efforts and that we attempt to set up a situation wherein the
  possibilities for aborting the mission would be sharply reduced. This request
  from JCS was relayed to INSCOM via Mr. Jackie Keith who took request to CG INSCOM
  and then to COL Stern. Arrangements are now being worked out to provide a
  suitable area.
                                                            SG11D
       b. LTC Watt will be meeting with CIA representative                    Friday,
  25 April, to work out arrangements for joint INSCOM/CIA effort using our assets.
  This effort was suggested by the DDO and agreed upon by the ACSI. At this time,
  we do nnt know what their requirements will be, but we will be sure not to overload
  our resources.
  2. (S) I do not have all the details but understand that MG Rolya and
  Mr. Speakman will be visiting SRI in the near future. Although their visit has
  nothing to do with our project, it might be beneficial if MG Rolya could arrange
  to visit with our SRI contacts (Dr. Harold Puthoff and Russell Targ). Suggest
  that BG Flynn discuss this with Mr. Speakman. (NOTE: SRJontacts are aware of
  the upcoming visit.)
