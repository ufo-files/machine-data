UFO Newsletter #2       24 March 1956          worth Jersey U. F. 0. Group



     The March 10 issue of the SATURDAY EVENING POST contains an article
by John Kobler, about James Rigberg, who publishes FLYING SAUCER NEWS
and operates an occultist bookstore in New York City*  In his article,
entitled "He Runs Flying Saucer Headquarters," Kobler says of Rigberg,
"Nobody has striven harder than Rigborg to foster belief in the reality
of space men and space ships," The article was very poorly written,
and contains many discrepencies and completely false facts and allusions.
It was calculated by its cynical author to slur the many serious re
searchers in the UFO field, and it pours on the ridicule to such an ex
tent that it has even prompted some in the occultist field, with whom
we seldom see eye-to-eye, to flood SATEVEPOST with letters taking ex
ception. We feel that we should all take action to protest such an
article. Public interest in flying saucers is perhaps at its lowest
since PROJECT GRUDGE, and this article will help to drive it lower. We
urgently ask ALL of you to first read the article to notice the various
false remarks and other poor aspects, and then write to Ben Hibbs, Ed
itor of the POST, to give him your opinion.  In this way we may help
overcome the bad effects, and at least influence POST editors not to
again run such a flagrant abuse of the duties and rights of good journ
alism.
