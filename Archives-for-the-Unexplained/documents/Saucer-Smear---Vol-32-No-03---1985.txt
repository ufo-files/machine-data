IN WHICH
YOUR HUMBLE EDITOR RESUMES HIS LAGGING RADIO CAREER AND
INADVERTENTLY SOLVES THE MYSTERY OF FLORIDA'S CORAL CASTLE

      For two hours on the evening of Feb. 9th, your "Smear" editor raved
about UFOs, Ancient Astronauts, and South American antiquities,   on a Mi­
ami radio show hosted by one Mike Spandell. (Radio station WGBS.)     The
secret purpose behind our appearance was publicity for our Key West pre­
Columbian art gallery, where sales have not been quite as brisk as anti­
cipated.
      In the course of our ravings, we touched upon the mystery of Flor­
ida's Coral Castle.   For those who do not know, Coral Castle is currently
a tourist attraction on Route One a few miles south of Miami. It consists
of huge rocks weighing many tons each, transported and carved by a Latvian
immigrant named Ed Leedskalnin. Ed died in 1951 without ever revealing
the secret of how he, a man weighing little more than 100 pounds, was able
to perform such an astounding feat single-handedly.   Many people, includ­
ing your editor, have speculated that he (just as he claimed) had re-dis­
covered the secret of the Pyramids, or even that he had extraterrestrial
help in his task. · These concepts are not discouraged by the current own­
ers of the Castle, who quite obviously are anxious to make a Buck or two
out of all this.
      Your editor "discovered" the Castle in the late 1950's,   and wrote
an article about it for the Sept. 1963 issue of SAUCER NEWS. Our article
is paraphrased from a $1.00 pamphlet that used to be on sale at the sou­
venir stand there, but which has since been suppressed, apparently,   as it
obviates the need to take a tour of the place - which now costs $5.50 or
more.  Interestingly, our Coral Castle article was chosen by the late Gray
Barker to be included in a 1967 anthology he published, called "Jim Mose-
                                    (2)
ley's Book of SAUCER NEWS".    Then, the late Otto Binder used the anthology
as a reference in a discussion of Coral Castle in one of his  - books;    and,
thereafter, the former owners of the Castle framed Binderes reference as
one of the displays on the wall of the Castle's souvenir shop. Thus, the
circle was completed, and your editor becp.me an "instant expert", essen­
tially just by having read and /rewritten a brochure for sale in the very
same shop!   (Sic transit UFO research! )
       Your editor, just like a consensus of other
 "experts", felt that the Castle was at least somewhat
mysterious.   But at no time did he or any of    these
other writers make an attempt to locate people     who
 might have known Ed while he was still alive. Such
 thoughts were discouraged by the tour guides' em­
.phasis on the fact that Ed always worked alone,   and
 would stop work if anyone were watching him.   (In
those early days, relatively few people traveled along
 that isolated stretch of Route One.) It was thus as­
 sumed by all that such a background check on Ed would
 be useless and irrelevent.
       NOW, getting back to the WGBS radio program: Dur-
 ing the phone- in portion, a man called to say that a still- living friend
 of his used to watch Ed work, back in the late 1920's.   At that time the
 construction was located about three miles south of its present location.
 Yes, gentle reader, Ed decided to move, about 1935, and took with him, by
 truck, the huge rocks he had carved up to that time. Then he re- position­
 ed them at the present site, and added many more.
       A few days after the radio show, we·located and interviewed Earl
 Lee, the man mentioned by the radio caller. As a boy, he and      several
 friends from the area had spent many hours talking with Ed and watching
 him work. (This fits in with the well-known fact that Ed liked kids and
 didn't mind having them around.) Earl Lee, a man now almost 70 years old,
 told us how, in essence, Ed built his castle with simple levers, counter­
 weights, and hard work. These facts, which are never mentioned in the
 Coral Castle tour, would take up too much space to give in detail here.
 Suffice is to say that no rational person could hear Mr. Lee's sincere ac­
 count without reaching the conclusion that the mystery is solved.
       And yet as recently as 1983, someone named Ray Stoner (no pun in­
 tended) wrote a horribly- organized mimeographed booklet (on sale at the
 Castle for $11.95!) in which he compares Coral Castle favorably with the
 Great Pyramid of Egypt, Stonehenge in England, and the Pyramid of the Sun
 in Mexico - all of which he finds mysterious.   Stoner concludes that ei­
 ther Ed used esotaric knowledge; or he was a time traveler; or he was an
 interdimensional being; or he was actually an extraterrestrial! (See P.
 28 of "The Enigma of Coral Castle" by Ray Stoner. ) Stoner's only attempt
 to solve the matter is through pseudo- mathematical mumbo- jumbo,    which
 takes up most of the booklet.
       Perhaps the worst thing we can say about Coral Castle is     another
 fact we learned from Earl Lee: It is not built of coral at all, but of
 a different type of stone called oolite-! Ed called it "Rock Gate".      The
 current name was conjured up after his death by the money- grubbers.
       And yet, let us not sell Ed short.   A feat can be impressive even if
 it is not mysterious! Probably no man in American history ever moved and
 positioned so many tons of stones single-handedly, and carved them      into
 such interesting shapes to please the eye of the beholder. So, a tip of
 the "Saucer Smear" sombrero to the late Ed Leedskalnin, even though he was
 not an extraterrestrial!
