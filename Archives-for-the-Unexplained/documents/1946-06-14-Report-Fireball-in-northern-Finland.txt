Case xxx.
Fireball in northern Finland

Time: June 14, 1946, about 22.00 hours
Place: Sodankylä region of northern Finland

This sighting was reported to the Defence Staff, on September 25, 1948, by S.Tilly, the Swedish military attaché in Helsingfors. The meteorological station at Sodankylä had, ”in strictest confidence”, given Tilly notes from their observations diary. Four different sightings were reported, three in 1946, one in 1947. 
The June 14 sighting was of a fireball in the NW with a lingering light for about 2-3 seconds.

Status: Meteor
Source: Letter from S.Tilly to the Defence Staff [Defence Staff files]
