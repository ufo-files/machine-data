           Christmas Message, 1968
                            by Tom Napier

                  We see the Earth, a blue-white ball
                  Surrounded by an empty night,
                 Whose distances can dwindle all
                Our greatest feats to points of light.

              Until this time the Earth had seemed
               An ample stage to speak our lines.
            Our play looks slighter than we'd dreamed
             Seen from beyond the Earth's confines.

                 Yet venturing out we find no place,
                   No other home for fragile man.
                  We only have this cage in space,
                Our masque will end where it began.

                  Unless we force our prison bars
                And take our place among the stars.
