Viral Video Chart: Mad Men meets Sesame Street
This article is more than 15 years old
Watch kids' TV show Sesame Street spoof hit advertising drama Mad Men, see a UFO fall from the sky and catch up with
YouTube's greatest hits
Mercedes Bunz
Fri 2 Oct 2009 07.00 BST
Share
2
2


Two of the MediaGuardian.co.uk team's favourite TV shows team up for this week's Viral Video Chart: Mad Men and Sesame
Street. This episode of the kids' show – part of the 40th anniversary season of Sesame Street – aims to teach children to understand
feelings. They can learn the differences between a mad man, a sad man and a happy man – and what honey has to do with it.
Don Draper's part is performed by the legendary Frank Oz, who is back as a Sesame Street puppeteer for the first time in 15 years.
Most of us know him because he played Miss Piggy or Fozzie Bear in The Muppets and Cookie Monster, Grover and Bert in
Sesame Street. The clip is gaining online momentum after featuring on the New York Times and Huffington Post sites in the last few
days.
We also have something for fans of conspiracy theories this week: a UFO video from Spain filmed in Blair Witch style. You see
fighter planes, something that looks like an UFO that splashes in the water and then a helicopter tells the Spanish fishermen to leave
the area. Fake or real? Judge for yourself.

Remind yourself of top YouTube moments such as Boxxy, the sneezing panda, the incredible cat stunts, the iPhone in the mixer, the
precocious children, and people flying through the air.
2 Alan Grayson on the GOP Health Care Plan: "Don't get sick! And if you do get sick, die quickly!"
The US health war is still going on. In this video Democratic representative Alan Grayson tears apart the Republicans' healthcare
policy.
3 A Nightmare On Elm Street - Bande-Annonce/Trailer HD [VO]
Preview of the remake, which will hit the streets next April. It is the first movie from director Samuel Bayer, who is known for his
rock videos for Nirvana, Green Day and the Strokes.
4 Google Wave Overview
Cheerful Google Steph and Google Greg introduce you to one of the most important Google products of this year in only 8 minutes.
If you are a real geek, watch the original 80-minute version here.
5 Carl Sagan - 'A Glorious Dawn' feat. Stephen Hawking (Cosmos Remixed)
Carl Sagan and Stephen Hawking are remixed in truly cosmic style by electronic act Colorpulse.
6 Sesame Street: Mad Men
Watch Don Draper's team handle the Happy Honey Bear account.
7 Augmenting Aerial Earth Maps with Dynamic Information (augearth)
Using crowd-casted videos, they generate a dynamic alive city in augmented virtual earth map. You know what came to my mind?
Splendid for surveillance, don't you think so, too?
8 PSA - Listen to Overpaid Celebrities
The Republicans fight back against celebrities who support Obama's health care plan.
9 LIPDUB - I Gotta Feeling (Comm-UQAM 2009)
This video from Quebec features 172 communication students who party their way through their building.
10 COMBO a collaborative animation by Blu and David Ellis (2 times loop)
Don't miss this marvellous street-art video. Painting their way up and down a house at the Italian Fame festival, Blu and David Ellis
create an amazingly creative short film out of trash and colour.
