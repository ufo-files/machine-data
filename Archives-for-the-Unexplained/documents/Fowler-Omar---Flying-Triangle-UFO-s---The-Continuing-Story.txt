Unfortunately we have no means of confirming the very early reports of 'Flying Triangle'
craft, but a selection of incidents are given below. (Source: Fooplanes by W.H. Watson B./. S.)

29 August 1871: France. Astronomer Trouvelet reported formations of highly complex
objects in the sky. Some were triangular, some round, others many-sided. A few of them
hovered and then moved off

3 July1882: Lebanoo,Connecticut,USA. Two luminous triangles were observed on the
moon's upper limb. Three minutes later two dark triangles appeared on the lower limb. The
two sets approached each other, met and disappeared.

3 August 1886: Both M.Poey and M. Trouvelet observed numerous round and triangular
objects crossing, the face of the Sun.

2 3 November 1887: A huge illuminated equilateral triangle was observed on the Moon in the
Crater Plato. Tiny points of light were seen all over the Moon, originating in different craters,
from Aristarchus, Kepler and from the Lunar Alps. They converged on Plato, traversed its
high walls and were seen to unite themselves in the huge floodlit triangle.

THE FIRST FLYING TRIANGLE 'FLAPS'.
18 November 1980 Missouri & Kansas, U.S.A. were subjected to a visit by a huge craft
displaying a triangular shaped series of lights. The FT flew over northern Missouri and Kansas
for a period of more than four hours, while hundreds of people, including policemen, looked
on in awe. The object was observed by people in at least 22 towns and described as being
triangular shaped with one· red and two very bright white lights "like headlights." The craft
was described as being as big, of spectacular size. "It was at least as big as a football field"
said 42 year-old welder Don Leslie of Milan Mo.            Another witness, Roger Bennett of
Huntsville, Mo. said "It was so big, it would make a B52 bomber look like a Piper Cub."

There was an unusual event, as the huge craft was seen to be dropping 'satellites'. "They were
round and had a bluish glow" said witness Randy Hayes, a local truck driver.        At one stage,
the craft banked over and witnesses described seeing a row of lights shining out of windows,
to a cockpit. The UFO craft flew criss-cross slowly between the two states and was tracked on
radar during a four-hour period.    (Source: Enquirer Newspaper, 3 February 1981)
October 1980-January 1981 Clifton-Morenci, Arizona, U.S.A. received a visit from a
boomerang-shaped UFO that local witnesses claimed was bigger than several football fields!
The UFO had been spotted at least three times above the giant Phelps-Dodge Smelter Plant
chimneys.   A witness described how the UFO was huge and 'V' shaped, with lights all around
it and what appeared to be a 'searchlight'. The UFO was seen to stop over the smelter plant
and shine the light down into the chimneys. One witness said "We were amazed and happy, it
took off at a tremendous rate of speed and then came back. It acted kind of friendly, I wasn't
scared."   (Source: The Phoenix Gazette 13 March 1981)
March 1983 New York State & Connecticut experienced a wave of huge 'Boomerang'
shaped craft flying over the countryside at night. The craft, were often described as being as
large as a 'football field' and in one instance 'two football fields. The craft flew over populated
areas and continued to do so for over the two following years.        It was described as a huge
'wing', 'V' or 'boomerang' shape, carrying an array of lights which switched on and off, as
though to attract attention. (see sketches).

            HUGE TRIANGULAR CRAFT SEEN NEAR BANBURY IN 1988
A long j ourney lay ahead for Mr Colin Harrison and his friend, as they set out from
Manchester in their van. The night was clear, cloudless and starry as they headed towards
Banbury (Oxfordshire). It was about 2am on the July 7 1 988 and they were about three miles
from Banbury, when both occupants of the van noticed a dark shape approaching them. It had
first appeared low on the horizon, but there was something strange about it and they both
sensed that it was something unusual.
It was now only half a mile away and they could see that the object was not a plane, helicopter
or any type of aircraft that they could recognise. By now Colin had stopped the van and
switched off the lights. As the object drew closer, they could see that the craft showed no
lights and there was no sign of any wings or helicopter blades.
It was soon within about 200yds of the van and at a height of 70ft above them, moving
absolutely silently. "I knew this was a day that I wouldn't forget" remarked Colin. "By now I
could see the front of the object, it was at least 60ft across, concave and had a matt black
body, there were also three lighter coloured 'circles' on the front of it. "
Colin was about to get out of the van for a better look, but his friend begged him t o stay
where he was. Both witnesses sat quietly in the van as the huge craft passed silently over the
van at an estimated height of 70ft. There were no markings visible on the matt black craft, but
they did notice 5 oblong 'hatches' underneath, lighter in colour than the main body. "By the
time it had gone over, I realised that it must have been at least 3 00ft long," said Colin. "There
was still no noise or any sign of lights." Colin then moved into the back of the van to watch
the craft moving away. "It was about 600ft away and I was able to make out the shape of the
object, it was tubular, tapering to a 'beaver' shaped tail." "The craft then did an 'impossible'
manoeuvre on its own axis and shot off at great speed into the night sky", said Colin. "I was
very excited, but my friend on the other hand was very nervous and scared, he just kept asking
me to get going." Colin's final comment: "I have never forgotten that night!"
(Source: Skywatch UK)

Let us now reflect on the increase in our knowledge on the behaviour and observed
characteristics of the 'Flying Triangle' craft. In my booklet 'The Flying Triangle Mystery' (May
1 996), I gave a list of observed characteristics of the FT craft. These have now been updated.
  FIRST INDICATION: The appearance of an FT may be preceded by a 'flash' in the sky.
  We have now had incidents where an FT has been seen to move rapidly across the sky, in
  association with producing a pulse of brilliant light "similar to sheet lightning, but brighter" .
  (Llanbadan Fawr, Aberystwyth 2 1/1 1 /96 see also page 1 3). It is believed that this FT moved
  across the U.K and was later seen at Eye, Cambs at 0245hrs on the 2211 1196)
  LIGHTING ARRANGEMENT: The lights on the FT craft may vary tremendously, from
  travelling in complete darkness, to being "lit up like a Christmas tree". We now have further
  information on the 'searchlight' beam, produced from the underside of the craft. It seems
  l_llghly unlikely that it is a means of illumination for 'Aliens looking through portholes
  underneath the FT'. The beam has been described more as looking like a fluorescent tube and
  has been seen to 'end' in mid-air.
  SIZE: We now have several reports showing that apart from the 'huge' FT', there is a smaller
  type (van size), which carries three/four occupants. This type has been observed with a
  'cockpit-type' canopy on top. (Derby, 1 9/4/1 956: Crayke, N. Yorks. 24/2/79: Brentwood 9/96:
  Lancaster 22.00hrs. 1 9/1 2/96)
  SOUND: Details - Silent/hum/drone/roar. MUFON (USA) have recently recorded a sound
  made by a FT craft and identified it as 1 5 . 805kHz.

  CONCLUSION
  It appears highly unlikely that the huge 'Flying triangle' craft that have been observed over
  several years, are of a terrestrial origin. Their performance and behaviour characteristics
