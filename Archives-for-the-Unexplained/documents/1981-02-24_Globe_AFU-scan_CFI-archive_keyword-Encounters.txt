THE PHOTOCOPY (above), and the artist's sketch (left)
 based 6n it, allegedly show an alien in the custody of
two U.S. military policemen. The photocopy came to
light in 1950, when an unnamed informant gave it to
New Orleans-based FBI agent John Quinn, claiming it
 showed "a man from Mars in the United States." The
original photo is said to have surfaced in Wiesbaden,
Germany, in the possession of a U.S. soldier stationed
there. How he came into possession of the photo, who
the soldiers pictured in it are, where it was taken, and
the exact nature of the respiratory apparatus the alien is
wearing are not known. The photo did receive consider­
able publicity in Germany in the late 1940s, but its rela-
    tion to the Roswell Incident remains a mystery.
