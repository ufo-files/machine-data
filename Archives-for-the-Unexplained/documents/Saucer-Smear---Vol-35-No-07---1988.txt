  RE-HASH OF
  THE 1988 NATIONAL UFO CONFERENCE' S 25TH ANNUAL CONVENTION:
         As noted in a previous issue of "Smear ", there was a serious schism
  between two groups of Cleveland, Ohio ufologists, following the 1984 con­
  vention of the National UFO Conference ( NUFOC) , which was held in    t hat
  city, This schism was eventually healed, and t hus it came about that in
  1988 Clevel.and ( once again!) saved the NUFOC from extinction by agreeing
  to sponsor our "Silver Anniversary " convention.
         The above is a highly condensed version of a long and complex story.
         This year we attracted an unusually large & diverse group of speak­
  ers, including: John Timmerman of CUFOS; Richard Dell'Aquila; Antonio ( "The
  Man of a Thousand Slides ") Huneeus; Dr. Willy Smith ( about whom more fur­
  ther on) ; and the following members of the NUFOC Governing Board: Ed Bie­
  bel; Timothy Green Beckley; Tom Benson; Ron Schaffner; Charles Wilhelm; &
  yours truly Jim Moseley, who presided as M.C. in both t he afternoon      and
  evening sessions.
         Three members of our Governing Board ( ca+led the Permanent Organiz­
  ing Committee) were unable to attend t his year. They were Bill Moore of
  M J-12 fame; Hal Starr; and Ken Behrendt of AURA. Behrendt, whom we thank
  for long & nobel service to our Cause, was replaced on t he Board by      Jim
  Speiser of ParaNet, who has agreed to hold next year's convention in Phoe­
  nix, Arizona, some time in September. Next year's speakers are expected
  to include Tracy Torme; Brad Steiger; Bill Moore; Marge Christensen of MU­
  FON; Vicki Cooper of "UFO Magazine "; and of course t he inevitable Jim Mos­
  eley.   (More info on all this in future issues.)
         Getting back to this year's Conclave - the most significant lecture,
  in our opinion at least, was that given by Dr. Willy Smith, who is locked
  in scientific combat with the MUFON hierarchy regarding t he authenticity
  of the famed Gulf Breeze, Florida, UFO p hotographs. These were taken dur­
  ing a recent period of several months by a Gulf Breeze businessman known
  to the world only as "Mr. Ed ".
         Everyone in our vast convention audience heard Dr. Smith reveal Ed's
  real last name publicly for the first time; but certain High MUFON Offic­
  ials nevertheless feel strongly that publishing the name in "Smear " would
  be harmful to Ed. After a personal plea by telephone from Mr. Ed himself
   (whom we had never spoken to before) , we of "Smear " have decided to Sup­
  press Ed's true identity for now. ( Children., children!! ) Ed seems like a
  very pleasant, inoffensive person, and we look forward to meeting him in
  the near future.
         The truth about Mr.·Ed's several strange UFO sightings, contacts,
  etc., obviously rests on the authenticity of the approximately 40 photo­
  graphs he has taken, and many of these are subject to serious doubt accord­
  ing to the scientific analysis of Dr. Smith. Dr. Bruce Maccabee, on t he
  other hand, together with most of the MUFON hierarchy, now endorse these
  photos wholeheartedly, while the CUFO S hierarchy leans the other way, to­
  gether with many rank & file MUFONites and other researchers in general.
         Smith's strongest point seems to be that Maccabee refuses to send
  him originals of Ed's photographs, so that he can have the necessary rna-

      Although your humble "Smear" editor has cleverly avoided demotion
from his MUFON post by agreeing to withhold Mr. Ed's real last name, Willy
Smith has not been so fortunate. Like Bob Boyd, formerly Alabama     State
Director for MUFON, Willy is now merely a ordinary subscriber to the MUFON
Journal (for which he will presumably pay $25 per year, as we all do.)
      Ray Fowler and Marge Christensen, former Big Wheels in MUFON,   have
resigned 9ver this mess, although Marge was persuaded to change her resigna­
tion to a "leave of absence". Likewise, Barry Greenwood of CAUS has re­
cently resigned, as has Jim Speiser of ParaNet in Arizona and Jim Melesciuc,
formerly Massachusetts State Director. No doubt there have been many other
defections from the ranks that have failed to come to our attention!
      In the August issue of the MUFON Journal, International Czar Walt An­
drus seems to endorse the Gulf Breeze case by saying "The overwhelming evi­
dence is in. Gulf Breeze is indeed one of the most incredible cases in
modern UFO history". However, in the September issue he seems to     back­
track, possibly under pressure, and states: "MUFON has opted not to take a
premature position on this important case."
      A wild article in Issue #24 (October, 1988) of the CUFOS publication
qalled "C.I.Q.", has a screaming headline: "Hoax UFO Cover-Up Exposed".
Further along in the article, Dr. Robert Nathan of the Jet Propulsion Lab­
oratory is quoted as saying, "I would have liked to have said differently,
but I'm afraid the Gulf Breeze photographs just don't check out." In an
insert by Bob Boyd ·are listed "persons and organizations that have active­
ly participated in the promotion and/or sensationalism of the Gulf Breeze
UFO Hoax". They are Walt Andrus;     Duane Cook of the Gulf Breeze Sentin­
el;  Budd Hopkins;   Dr. Bruce Maccabee;  Donald Ware (Florida State Direct­
or of MUFON), and several others.
      But most amazing of all is a form letter to "MUFON State and Provin­
cial Directors" from Dan Wright, MUFON's high-handed Deputy Director for
Investigations. This letter is a 2-page personal attack on Dr. Willy Smith,
accusing Smith of having made personal attacks on various investigators and
witnesses involved with Gulf Breeze, including of course Mr. Ed!
      In a form letter dated Nov. lst, Smith replies in part: "The time has
come to set Gulf Breeze aside as a bad experience, which nevertheless has
taught us a lesson: that UFO investigations are done perfunctorily by un­
trained persons more interested in fame and fortune than in finding    the
truth. The lack of ethics prevailing all along during the sightings is
most deplorable, involving such things as confessed suppression of negative
evidence and public fraternization of the investigators with those    they
were supposed to investigate - no doubt a first in ufological lore."
      Incidentally, Mr. Ed is said to be writing a book, with an introduct­
ion by abduction researcher Budd Hopkins and technical material supplied by
none other than Dr. Bruce Maccabee!
      Maybe, however, your "Smear" editor will change his bias on all this
after his forthcoming meeting with Ed. As George Adamski used to say, TIME
WILL TELL!
