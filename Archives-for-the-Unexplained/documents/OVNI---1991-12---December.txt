                         CCCS AMERICAN LECTURE TOUR

We heard of news further afield than the borders of the Midlands, when
"Busty" Taylor recently arrived locally for a two day visit to the area.

Busty had just completed a 12,000 miles lecture tour of the USA, together
with fellow CCCS (Centre for Crop Circle Studies) member Richard Andrews.
The tour which took place from the 26th September - 8th November, had been
arranged some weeks previously.    It had been planned to enlighten the
scientific fraternity and the general public about the true nature of the
crop circle phenomenon, ·after the adverse publicity caused by the two
hoaxers "Doug" and "Dave".

The audiences ranged from 20 to 250, apart from which there were numerous
radio and TV interviews about the subject.     Richard Andrews, a renowned
"dowser", has the ability to sense Ley Lines and other mystical powers.  He
is able to show off    his talent by holding a single bent "dowsing" wire,
which will spin in a spectacular way at high speed when he nears a
"source".   The tour took in twelve major cities and ranged from Seattle to
Phoenix, San Francisco and Washington DC.

The damage done by "Doug & Dave" to the subject was widespread.         Many
peop1 e commented that they thought    a 11 of the cire1 es were hoaxes, but
Busty Taylor and Richard Andrews soon convinced them otherwise.   As we have
mentioned before, if you deduct the total claimed by the two hoaxers, you
are left with a total of unexplained circles, in excess of 1,600!

Omar Fowler had given Busty' details of our latest Microwave theory before
he set out on his travels and this was to have a surprising result. It was
while on his lecture tour in Minnesota, that Busty met an American
researcher John Servicki.   John took Busty to see  a field of maize, where
a circle had appeared.    The circle which was not complete, was more of a
"U" shape and the crop had been separated in an angled "V".   It seems that
the top and bottom of the crop stems were still lying around, but the
middle 18" had disappeared!      Busty Taylor was given a piece of maize stem
to examine.     Whi 1 e doing so, he slid the outer cover away from the maize
stem and there revealed before      a watching audience, were several examples
of "burnt" nodes (see OVN I Nov. picture 4 - burnt Barley node).           The
American researchers had not detected this phenomenon and in a way, it's a
sma 11 'feather in our cap' for being ahead of the fie1 d!

                                 "SCENE AROUND"

Witnesses of past events have continued to come forward and relate their
experiences.  Not all of them wish to be identified, but we are indebted to
PRA member Alan Hitchcock for following up the sighting detailed below and
completing the investigation.

The incident occurred at 2am during a day in October 1989 (the witness, an
ex-employee, cannot recollect the exact day).  Mr X was on shift duty at a
water works situated in the local area and belonging to the Severn Trent
Water Co.  As the witness set off on his hourly inspection of the premises,
his attention was attracted by a brightly lit object hovering near the
works (point "A" on sketch).

The light appeared to be very close to the works and Mr X described it as
being a bright white, round shape.    Although the 1 ight seemed to have a
sharp outline, it was not possible to see any other details.   Mr X heard no
sound and described the brightness of the object as about the same as a
fluorescent 1 ight tube.  According to the sketch, Mr X was within 75yards
of the object, which he described as being 34" at arms length!

The weather conditions at the time were ideal,     there was a clear starlit
sky, no breeze and the night was cool.

The witness watched the light for several minutes, before it began to move
off slowly towards the West.  Mr X ran along the road to point "8" on the
sketch, where he observed the object gathering speed and moving in a
curving path.  It was finally seen disappearing behind some houses in the
distance.

It was then that the strangest incident took place.   The water works has a
security network of infra-red detectors surrounding key area's.    These are
designed to sound an alarm if the infra-red beam is broken by an intruder.

As Mr X ran along the road to point "8", he was suddenly aware that the
intruder alarms were sounding.    Whatever it was, the UFO had produced a
source of power that had "bent" the infra-red beams, interrupting the
circuit and setting off the intruder alarms.

The witness Mr     X commented that there were reports on the local radio the
fo 11 owing day,   about unusual lights seen North of Derby in the Heanor­
Ripley area.

Editor • s Note: I am not an expert on infra-red beams, but I have had
personal experience of setting up an infra-red security system.         They
appear to be satisfactory for an indoor environment, or for use outside
during the hours of darkness.      During daylight however, the beams are
bent/warped by rays from the sun and the alarm system will be triggered.
