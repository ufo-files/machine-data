Vpl constable / 884-4-43
Söderström; 5. batt, LV 3
 
Regarding a supposed "ghost rocket".

Appendix.

To the Head of the Air Defense Department.

CLASSIFIED

I hereby respectfully report the observation of a "ghost rocket".
Point in time: The object was discovered on the 12th of August, 1946, at 22:51 o’clock, according to the local church clock. The smoke trail thinned out after approximately 3 seconds.
Place of observation: Lv 3 Registry rooftops (adjacent to the flagpole).
Direction and elevation: refer to the attached illustration.
Course: about 2000 (from the northwest to the southeast). Refer to the attached sketch.
Additional notes: No psychical object, light or sound could been noted. There had only been observations of (grey) smoke, comparable to the liquefaction capabilities of airplanes traveling at high altitude. The smoke trails, which gradually spread out, remained for about 15 minutes, after which I stopped observing. A sketch of the observation has been attached to this letter.

Stockholm, August 13, 1946.

Bengt Söderström
Vpl constable.
