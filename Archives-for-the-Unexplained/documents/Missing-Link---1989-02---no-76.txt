          We have had an active autumn in western Colorado,
  three meetings held since October. Because this is so11nevvhah
  of a rural area, we have fewer people in our center than other
  centers, and so meetings occur when the opportunity arises.

          On Friday, Oct. 21 we had the honor of visiting with Bonnie Easterly-Huber of Tacoma, Wash., who attended our
  meeting in Paonia at the home of Associate Director Jackie Blue. Bonnie once lived in our area and appeared happy to
  be back in Colorado, if only for a short time. Bonnie showed a video she brought with her, titled E.T. Earth Mission,
  which featured two walk-in space people by the names of Savizar and Silarra. These ordinary- looking people described
  what they were doing here, talked about the importance of the New Age, and sang in whale song. It vvas a pretty awesome
      display of communication.
          Bonnie related several of her extraordinary experiences. Presently she works as an advertising salesperson for an
  imponant new metaphysical newspaper in Washington, The Light ofOlymp;a, which has a circulation of over 10,000
  and reaches people mostly in the Northwest and British Columbia. The monthly paper expects to spread even farther,
  as it isn't even a year old. Bonnie also works with AIDS patients.
          Each of her five children, now grown, has had tremendous experiences, and one of them even told her things about
  the time she was carrying him that only Bonnie knew. She told about the time when her daughter was sitting in the
      dining room next to a sliding door with sheer drapes. The daughter was talking on the telephone when she thought the
      drapes caught on fire, due to a bright orange/red light coming from the window. Her daughter's friend's father from
  Montana was sitting in the darkened living room and he was in awe at the reddish, pink, and orange colors that were
      accompanied by a pulsating hum. Bonnie explained that her daughter was going through an emotional crisis at the time
      of this strange occurrence, but after this experience the crisis was resolved, as if the colors suggested a loving, protec­
      tive energy.
          Another time, Bonnie's son and his wife watched a bright orange orb outside the window in the afternoon, and
      suddenly the orb shot straight up. Her son thought little of it, and yet her daughter-in-law was in a state of disbelief.
      Last October, while Bonnie was visiting a friend in Grand Junction, Colo., she stayed in a guest room with three walls
      of glass. On Oct. 18 there was an occurrence in which Bonnie awoke very alert and aware. She felt as though she were
      a feather, floating onto her bed. Out the window she could see two stars, and one shot off real fast. This happened at
      exactly 5 a.m., she said. On Oct. 19, she awakened again very alert, and looked out and saw a brilliant light that vanished.
          Bonnie related many more experiences which I will not get into now due to space limitations. If interested, you
      may want to read next month's issue of The Star Beacon•, which will run a fuller account of Bonnie Huber's experien­
      ces. The December issue of The Beacon contains an interesting experience which Bonnie shared with her ex-husband,
      Julian Joyce, regarding ''1be Pillar of Light.''

  proof. She has received word that her time is now to reveal all that she knows. It is interesting how we all seem to be
      coming together as we wotk toward our/their goals.
          Saturday, Nov. 19 was the date of the crystal workshop with Laurel Williams of Glenwood Springs. Laurel is a
      massage therapist/healer who works at the Glenwood Springs Vapor Caves. She has given crystal workshops all over
      the nation, and her story is printed in the April1988 issue of The Star Beacon. She was also mentioned in the nation­
      wide magazine, UFO Universe, las t summer. She uses crystals in many different ways - one of which is to balance
      the chakras, and she demonstrated this on one of the members of our group. It was fascinating, to say the least We had
      eight new people in attendance. One woman came to the class because she wanted to know more about crystals after
  buying one and using it to stop smoking (she was successful). Most the people there seemed to get a lot out of the class.
      It was a fascinating evening which left everyone hoping that Laurel will come to Delta again.
