July 25: A question of psychology?

The following editorial was published by Stockholms-Tidningen on July 25: (and was subsequently quoted by Jönköpings-Posten (July 26))

”METEOR OR GHOST BOMB? Meteor or ghostbomb - that is the question. Colonel Ramström, who undeniably is a sensible man, can, in at least one case, prove that the ”bomb psychosis” has had no basis in fact - a crash near Sigtuna of a fireball with luminous tail proved on expert investigation to be from a meteor. Now it seems undeniably strange if it should suddenly start raining stones from the sky in such numbers as the reports attest to in the last days, in this sense professor Lundmark is also a skeptic, and he should know. But the bomb theory does not seem persuasive, neither. One has difficulty seeing the finesse of, in this way, targeting our area with luminous balls, large as apples. 

”It is also talked about V 1-bombs. So far there are, in any case, no proof of such [bombs] really passing over our country. Such an obsolete weapon is rather meaningless to disturb the neighbourliness with. They already belong among those placed in the Technical Museum. Well, what is then behind all the recent reports? If the Defence Staff can give an answer to that, they have indeed performed a good deed. In any case it is too early, at this stage, to point to any international culprit. It might even happen in this matter that the right expertise is not the military but the psychological.
