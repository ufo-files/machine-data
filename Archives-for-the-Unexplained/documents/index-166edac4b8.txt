  From the Book Cover

  For decades the United States and Russian have been considered the worlds two major super powers. For the United

  surrounding this event, and how the metallic eivdence of an Extraterrestrial space craft gathered by the soviet military
  from the sight [sic], subsequently disappeared.

  Don't miss this revealing account of the events at Tunguska, and decide for yourself the cause of the "Russian Roswell"
