 scrutiny. Less fashionable still is the notion that our religions, our cultures, even our species may be the
 product of someone else's meddling.
     Unfashionable yes, even outrageous- but evidence does suggest that "alien" visitors have already
 reached our door, perhaps even in deepest antiquity.
     If so, who are these others? Extraterrestrials from another planet? Travelers through time? Denizens
 of other dimensions? Can we even tell the difference between a "god," an "angel" and an "alien?"
    Perhaps our best .experts have never been able to tell. Perhaps they feel baffled and powerless before
 the greatest mystery of our time. Perhaps this is why the best evidence of human-alien contact remains
 above top secret.
     Such notions seriously challenge our sense of reality. Denial and disbelief are bound to persist. Even
 so, huge changes may be upon us. "Coming of age in the cosmic neighborhood" may well mean opening
 ourselves to a reality much bigger, much stranger and much more inhabited than we ever expected!
