                                                                                               UFOs are reported
                                                                                               by two in Lanesboro
                                                                                                                             By Ellen G. Lahr
                                                                                                   LANESBORO - A Cheshire main claims he spotted an unidentified
                                                                                                flying object as he was driving through Lanesboro on Cheshire Road
                                                                                                last night at about 7:40p.m.
                                                                                                   Michael Rinaldi, 37, of 7 Richmond St., Cheshire, said he was driving
                                                                                                north on Cheshire Road with his mother when he saw a low-flying dia-
                                                                                                mond-shaped object direcUy above the road ahead of his car. He said
                                                                                                there were two white lights on the object that were so bright they l1t up
                                                                                                the leaves on the nearby trees. It appeared to be about 1,500 feet in the
                                                                                                air and about "a few thousand feet" away.
                                                                                                   Rinaldi said he is taking courses to obtain a private pilot's license,
                                                                                                and that -the lights be saw did not resemble any aviation lights he is fa-
                                                                                                miliar with. He also said the object bore no resemblance to any type of
                                                                                                airplane.
                                                                                                  Second witness
                                                                                                   Another witness, a Lanesboro resident who requested that his name
                                                                                                be withheld, also reported seeing a "white flash" flying at a low alti-
                                                                                                tude across Pontoosuc Lake as he was traveling north on Route 7 at
                                                                                                about 10 last night. He said, however, that the flash was so quick he
                                                                                                could not get a good look at the object.
                                                                                                . Rinaldi said when he spotted the flying object he pulled his car over
                                                                                                to the side of the road. Two other cars traveling behind him also
                                                                                                stopped, he said, and the drivers got out to watch the object. Then the
                                                                                                bright lights on the flying object went out, Rinaldi said, and he saw two
                                                                                                smaller pink lights on the bottom of the object. At that point, he said,
                                                                                                the drivers in the two other cars drove away.
                                                                                                   The flying object then made a sharp turn in an easterly direction
                                                                                                over a field, then another sharp turn toward his car, Rinaldi said. He
                                                                                                turned off his car headlights, and the flying object's two bright white
                                                                                                lights came back on, he said, one after the other. Then the object
                                                                                                veered off northwest toward Mount Greytock, he said. When he got in
                                                                                                his car and tried to follow it, he lost sight of it, he said.
                                                                                                   A spokesman for the UFO Reporting Center, a private scientific
                                                                                                agency based in Seattle, Wash., said his organization has had other re-
                                                                                                ports of diamond-shaped objects, but that he had no more specific in-
                                                                                                formation about exactly what the object could have been. He said his
                                                                                                organization receives about four reports each day of apparent UFO
                                                                                                sightings.
                                                                                                   Rinaldi said he is not "a science fiction buff."
