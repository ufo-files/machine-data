Case xxx.
The fireball that cut a telephone wire

Time: Sunday, August 18, 18.30
Place: Unknown (mid-Sweden)

”FIREBALL CUT TELEPHONE WIRE. A person in a place in middle Sweden observed at 18.30 on Sunday a luminous ball in the sky. The luminous ball seemed to be the size of a croquet ball [Sw. krocketklot] and seemingly collided with a telephone wire that snapped. Parts of the wire have been found, but from the projectile, which seemingly continued, no trace has been found.”

This case was never noted by the Defence Staff, although it was distributed by the newswire agency TT and published by Svenska Dagbladet, which was read at the Staff. The exact place of the incident is unknown but might be recovered if more local Swedish newspapers are combed for data. 

Status: Insufficient information
Source: Svenska Dagbladet and TT newswire nr 92, August 18.
