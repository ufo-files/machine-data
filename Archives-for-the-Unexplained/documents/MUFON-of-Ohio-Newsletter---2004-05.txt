                                          Strangely Lighted Field
                                             by Donnie Blessing
                                  MUFON State Section Director for Ohio


I received a report from a 72 -year old male from Columbus, Ohio, regarding a very peculiar event he
experienced on Sunday morning, December 02, 2003, at 06:40 am while deer hunting. He had just arrived
at his deer stand for bow hunting, somewhere east of Columbus, when a nearby 20 or so-acre of field lit up
like it was daylight! He stood and watched the light for several minutes, and then he noted two other lights
"roaming" around the field!! He assumed that the land owner, a friend of the witness, had illuminated the
field with a spot light, but a subsequent query to the landowner by a sheriff deputy ruled that out. Also, the
witness checked the perimeter of the field on foot, and then on a 4-wheel vehicle, and he found no traces of
footprints or tire tracks.


This gentleman said that as he was in his deer stand suddenly a light shined down on this large field of 20
plus acres of com stalks. He said the light had a pinkish tone to it. Along with this light were 2 orbs of light
the size of baseballs that he first thought were lights from quad runners. However, he was no more than 50
yards away and he soon realized it was not any type of vehicle. There was no sound and nothing attached to
theses lights. The lights roamed up and down along the perimeter of the field about three feet from the
ground and acted as if they were looking for something. All this time the one main bright light still shined
on the field. The farmer was not in the light. The event went on for approximately 10 minutes. The orbs
moved on out of view and the main light shut off. The farmer said he was not frightened by all this but now
he wishes he had looked up at the sky to see where the main light source was coming from.
