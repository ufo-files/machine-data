       Some time ago, the State Director heard Ted Oliphant, III, present a MUFON audience with what
Mr. Oliphant terms "the rules of engagement."      The rules seem so immeasurably important, that Ruben
Uriarte was asked to contact Mr. Oliphant to learn if this material might be presented in the Case Briefs. As
with many of us, Ted Oliphant has been concerned over the frequent so-called professional approach to
experiencers of various anomalous activities. In a letter to Mr. Uriarte, Mr. Oliphant writes of his concerns:
       "A lot of damage is being done these days by the research and media types, hypnotherapists and
psychologists. Automatically they treat the person who's had the experience as a victim. That means that
they've already decided the 'things' that people are encountering are attackers - some aggressive/offensive
thing. So the so-called professionals have made the decision that the phenomenon is evil.          Often when
people get involved in something that's personal and scary, they go back to their base instincts. So if you're
going to improve things, you've got to help people realize they do have control over what takes place with
them. Often the witnesses themselves are causing a lot of the problems they are having simply because they
overlook these shots that they've taken. More damage is done by the investigative and media types. They
take the persons who have had the experience and make some kind of victim out of them. The individuals go
home and watch themselves on TV and then become even more convinced that they're a victim, and before
you know it you have a snowball effect."

RE: Sighting on July 16, 1993, LOG# 950204S

Dear Virgil:

I have reviewed your report with interest. It contains several pieces of data that pr eswnably could
be used to establish the object's trajectory in relation to stars, and I have made some studies using
your data. I am reluctant to consider a sighting an "unidentified anomaly," unless the factors of
the case do not point to a more mundane conclusion, so I would like your help by answering a
couple of questions and clarifying a few points. First, I need to know if the directional
measurements you gave were corrected to true north, or if they were compass bearings.


Did you see Jupiter or Arcturus while you were watching this object? At 10: 15 pm on July 16,
1993, Jupiter was at an Azimuth (from true north) of 252° and an elevation of 19° from the true
horizon. Arcturus was at an Azimuth of 250° and an elevation of 52°. You indicate the object
was at 236°. If this was the compass bearing, then the bearing from true north would have been
about 252° -the same as Jupiter. Based on your discussion, I gather the object would have been
at an elevation of between 60° and 70° at that time, above Jupiter but close to Arcturus. At
midnight that day, you indicate a bearing of 256° at 20° elevation. Adding correction leads to
about 272° from true north. Arcturus was at 272° and an elevation of 32°.

Based on this analysis, assuming your measurements were compass bearings, I would conclude that
you must have been looking at Arcturus. Your statements for the 17th, however, do not agree.
You claim the bearing at 10:30 was 266° and did not change noticably during the next hour.
Arcturus was at 255° at 48° elevation. Jupiter was also at 255° but at an elevation of 15°. Both
changed appreciably during the following hour.


Please clarify the measurements for me and, if possible, provide more data on the observed
elevations. I will hold this report until March 15 awaiting your response.
