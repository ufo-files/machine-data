     IAGPC-G                                          02 February 1982
     MEMORANDUM FOR LTC JACHIM

     SUBJECT:   ABC News, Channel 13, Evening Edition, 01 February 1982.

   CPYRGHT
         "Finally ABC News has learned that the U.S. Defense Department
     used Clairvoyance in an effort to find Doizer.   This is the first
     known time that the Defense Department has used what is known as
     parapsychology in an ongoing military investigation, and the first
     known time these methods have been used against terrorists.   The

    American embassy in Rome received a number of messages from the Pent-
    agon with parapsychological information on the alledged location of
    Doizer, which was turned over to the Italian Police for checking.
    For example; a Defense Department Medium had visualized a quiet farm
    house outside Verona with a river running near it, and the medium had

    even conjured up a vision of the room in which Doizer was being held.
    Italian Police raided a number of farm houses that fit the mediums

    vision at gun point, and in one found an Italian businessman spending
    a quiet weekend with his lady friend.   Other raids were carried out
    by Italian police based on Parapsychological information provided by the
    pentagon but with no results.   It was not mediums but hard police work
    which finally turned up the hide out where General Doizer was held for
    42 days."                (Pierre Salinger - ABC News - Rome, Italy)
