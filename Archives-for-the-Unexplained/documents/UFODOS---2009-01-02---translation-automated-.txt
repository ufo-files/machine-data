     UKRAINIAN SCIENCE IN THE YEAR
   Many of us remember the rampant passion for "flying saucers" - the late 80's - early 90's of last century. Then the UFO boom was provoked by the lifting of censorship on
  such publications in the former Soviet Union. Meanwhile, "plates" roamed the sky of Ukraine since the Middle Ages (see, for example, Lyudmila Zaglada "A wonderful miracle
   appeared in Polotsk - at night there was a stampede, something moaned in the street, demons roamed like people…", "Facts and comments rii ", March 17, 2004), and so to
   this day have remained" unidentified flying objects. " Over time, only the public interest in such "flying objects" has changed (the political and economic situation in the country
   plays a decisive role here), and the very fact of the reality of the "UFO phenomenon" is currently virtually undisputed.


  Ufology, as a complex scientific field for the study of UFOs and related issues, was formed in the late 1940s in the United States. At present, there are state, military and
   civilian UFO research organizations in almost every country in the world. The productivity of ufological research itself is hampered by the following factors: secrecy of
  research at the state and military levels; economic factor, including the lack of funds for the construction of search, observation and laboratory bases (manifestations of the
   phenomenon are unpredictable, and we do not have money for "phantoms" and "ghosts", of course); and the superfactor: the unpredictable mimicry of the "UFO
  phenomenon" itself (its explanation requires a revision of many existing ideas about Life, Man, the Universe).


  Public interest in the "UFO phenomenon", as mentioned above, is dictated by the political and economic situation in the country. This conclusion fully illustrates the
   experience of Ukraine. At the same time, research interest does not depend on "weather", as UFO registrations continue, and their activity does not depend on the change
   of political leaders, inflation, rising gas and oil prices…

  Yes, research on the "UFO phenomenon" in Ukraine continues. The very structure of research has undergone radical changes since the UFO boom of the late twentieth
   century. We have not made any significant discoveries and achievements in the field of recognizing the phenomenon, but the evolution of the attitude of both scientists and
   ordinary people to this issue is noticeable. In general, this period was characterized by both ups and downs. In other words, there is
   the news is good and bad.


  A significant event in this period was the decline of unhealthy interest in UFOs and "green men". In the official press, the flow of reports about UFO sightings and "contact
   insights" fell sharply, provoked by the "thirst for a miracle" of the reading fraternity. People stopped reading Mukhortov's prayers at night, attaching photos of aliens to sick
   organs, and wearing amulets recommended by "brothers in mind." Schizophrenics again became Napoleons, not guests from other solar systems. In public transport and in
   the workplace, the topic of "UFOs" has receded into the background (but it was the other way around!). Along with the "plate" excitement, psychics, accompanied by telepaths,
  mystics, occultists, and other supernatural gangs, have disappeared. A natural process, the outcome of which was seen by John Baker (US diplomat): "In your country, as a
  result of certain processes, these phenomena have become the center of public attention. It will pass ... And to study such things, of course, is necessary. to be engaged in
   specialists "(S. Kurguzov," Guests from the Mirror, or the return of aliens ", -


   Kyiv, "Youth", 1992, -217 p.).

   In addition to the fact that the nation has "recovered", there are a number of other positive aspects. For example, the quality (ie truthfulness) of UFO messages has become higher
   and cleaner. Writing stories about encounters with humanoids and flights to other planets became unfashionable and "the delusion of the gray mare…" in the archives of ufologists
  began to decrease markedly (the author does not say about the complete eradication and absence of this negative phenomenon).

   But the medal has two sides. With the agony of the UFO boom, the public organizations of ufologists, which existed on the territory of Ukraine in practically all regional
  centers, began to disappear. Their existence depended (and depends) on the number and activity of members. And as soon as the Uphomanians receded into the
   background, the whole system, which was formed during the Soviet era, collapsed (however, as well as "Great, Mighty…"). At present, however, there are a number of
  informal organizations studying the topic of "UFOs", and, as a rule, these are groups of 3-5 people that exist in major cities of Ukraine. There are also virtual clubs uniting
  dozens of ufologists (like the Transcarpathian "UFODOS"), "multiple" public organizations (like the Sumy "Contact") and attempts to create an all-Ukrainian ufology center
  (like the Kiev "Probe").

   The problems with the ufology research network are not as terrible as they are portrayed. First, virtually all archives accumulated by "dead" organizations are transferred "from
  hand to hand." Secondly, almost all the accumulated ufological experience, as well as new observations of UFOs, developments and developments in this area, can be "dug
   up" on the Internet. And third, there are a lot of serious researchers of the "UFO phenomenon" for whom "temporary difficulties" do not affect enthusiasm. In general, "in recent
   years, despite the fragmentation, Ukrainian specialists in mysterious phenomena and artifacts have continued research in areas of interest to them" (Minutes of the meeting
  of the Ukrainian Center for the Study of Mysterious Phenomena and Artifacts, Kyiv, October 21, 2004).


  A special positive moment for the development of the ufological movement in Ukraine in recent years was the development of Internet technologies, which allowed UFO
   enthusiasts to fill gaps with a lack of information, as well as quickly communicate and accumulate facts and events for further analysis. Previously, ufological facts and
  opinions looked like a broken mosaic! Thanks to the Internet, they are accumulating into a huge pyramid, access to which is now unproblematic. And although the Internet
   offers great prospects for collecting and storing information, such resources in the Ukrainian sector are developing rather poorly. Worth noting: the forum "UFOs in
   Ukraine" (http: //
   ufoinukraine.iatp.org.ua/forum), Sergei Khvostov's site "UFO in Ukraine" (http://ufoinukraine.iatp.org.ua), Maxim Kozyrev's site "UFO in Ukraine is not" (http: //
   ufology.da .ru), Victor Romanchenko's site "UFO and secrecy" (http://ufosecrecy.narod.ru), site "UFODOS-Transcarpathia" (http: //
   ufoinv.pochta.ru) ...
