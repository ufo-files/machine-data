UFOs Visi~ Northern Ohio in Force           ( 10/17/73)
   Our office phone literally rang off the hook on the evening of
the 17th, with friends telling us that the CFOs were back again for
a concentrated visit to the north of Ohio.
   First reports came in at about 7sl5 P.M., when an off-duty Akron
patrolman, Henry Bertolini, told of an object hovering over his west
Akron home. He said it had red and blue flashing lights and was as
big as a car.
   At about the same time, Akron Police Sgt. A. w. Fields said that
patrolmen in Car 10 reported seeing a hovering oblong object that
suddenly sped away.
   Patrolman Lee Frasher and Joseph Gause, in Car 57, reported by
radio of seeing a cigar-shaped object "with two red glowing lights"
moving through the night sky. "I was a helicopter crew chief in the
Army," Frasher later told reporters. 11 It wasn't a chopper."
   At the time of these Akron sightings,five families in Willoughby
reported a bright light over their homes, followed by a loud explosion.
One of the witnesses, Wallace Zawacki, said that his 18-year-old
daughter, Laura, saw the yellowish light through the front window.
   Zawacki and some of his neighbors checked the area and found no
evidence of an explosion. Officials at nearby Lost Nation Airport
said that there were several takeoffs and landings at the time of
the reported incident, but had no reports of engine trouble or ex-
plosions.

UFOs Visit Toledo, Ohio Area    (10/18/73)
   A scattered number of reports came in from the northwestern area
of the state. Williams County Sheriff's deputies said they received
several calls around 3a00 A.M. They came from the Montpelier area,
telling of a very bright cigar-shaped UFO traveling very slowly south-
west of that town. Toledo airport radar noted nothing unusual on their
scopes, however.
   A Lakeside woman reported to Ottowa County Sheriff's deputies that
she saw a bright object over Lake Erie at 8a33 P.M. She said it started
sparking and added she heard a loud blast. After the blast the strange
object was gone, she stated. Two other residents reported that they
also heard the blast. A man witnessed a white light hovering over the
Catawba Island ferry dock a short time later.

Pascagoula UFO Goes Underwater ?                (11/6/73)
    Near the spot where the two shipyard workers had the strange
encounter with weird creatures and a strange UFO,Raymond Ryan was
in his boat fishing. Suddenly, claims Ryan, an underwater light became
visible and began following his boat on the Pascagoula River. Becoming
frightened, Ryan repeatedly tried to beat the light away with an oar.
However, all this splashing only made the light grow dimmer, as if
submerging to avoid damage.
   At first, Ryan decided not to tell anyone about the strange un-
derwater light, lest he find himself being called a liar or publicity
hound. But finally Ryan summoned his twin brother, Rayme, and the two
of them went back to the brackish waters and poked at the light with
oars again.
   After no further developments the two decided to report the in-
cident to the Coast Guard.
   "At 9a40 P.M. the Coast Guard at Pascagoula dispatched a 16-foot
boat to investigate the object," an official related. "The object was
located in six feet of water, moving at 4-6 knots." Officers on the
boat described it as "an amber beam, 4-6 inches in diameter, attached
to a bright metal object." Coast Guard officials said they tried to
snare the object, but "it would appear to go out and move away and then
reappear."
