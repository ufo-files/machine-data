  These researchers set up a video camera almost anywhere, point it at the sky and let it run. They then
  review the tape frame by frame, and find amazing objects zipping across the sky, making sudden turns and
  "defying the laws of physics." These elongated, blurry objects have been dubbed "rods". They are either
  distant objects moving at tremendous speed, or close objects flitting about only inches from the lens.
  Skeptics would call them insects, just happening to flying in front of the camera, but these researchers
  insist that there is something more amazing at work. An interesting challenge is to try to figure out why the
  objects seem to have structure, something like a segmented worm. -- Glenn Campbell
