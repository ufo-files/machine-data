This issue gives readers an overall look at the ufology scene in Bulgaria from 1989 to
1993. This is ·_a "first" for CUFORN. The entire issue is devoted to a summary of
scientists speeches given at a UFO conference in Sofia, the capital of Bulgaria on
November 28, 1993.


As you read it, be aware that these are the most important parts of the speeches, not the
entire speeches. The translation is literal from Bulgarian to English. Thus, the English
retains the original flavour, which in turn, means some sentences may be a bit too long
and formal for some readers.

thanks her and Lee Smith, for their kind assistance for sharing with us all details and
