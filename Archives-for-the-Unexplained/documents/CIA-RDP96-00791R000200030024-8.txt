       MEMO FOR:        Barbara E. Camph

SG11   FROM:
                        CIA Ott1ce of Research and Development
       SUBJECT:         SRI Remote Viewing Documents Declassification



            Thanks for your letter confirming that SRI has no
       objection to CIA declassifying the original association it
       had with SRI during the early 1970s when SRI was pursuing
       remote viewing investigations for ORD.  In order to apprise
       you of the full range of material which we have given to the
       American Institutes of Research (AIR) for review, I have
       enclosed a detailed bibliography of the documents.  If your
       request relates to a desire to review further the material
       itself, we can arrange to have an extra copy generated at
       some point during the study. Let me know soon if this is
       necessary since it will entail a lot of photocopying.

             The already unclassified material listed in the
       bibliography was given to AIR on 14 June. We are still in
       the process of reviewing and declassifying the remaining
       material, and this will be given to AIR in order of priority
       should they desire it. They may not even request it if once
       they have examined it they feel that it is not necessary to
       the review.

            Thanks again Barbara for rour gracious cooperation. My
