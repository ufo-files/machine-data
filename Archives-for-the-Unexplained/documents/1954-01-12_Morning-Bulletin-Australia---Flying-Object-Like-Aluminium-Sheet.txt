Morning Bulletin (Rockhampton, Qld. : 1878 - 1954), Tuesday 12 January 1954, page 4



        Flying Object like

          Aluminium Sheet
             MELBOURNE,               January 11.—
       A    Melbourne   astronomer  has
        reported seeing an "unidentifi-
        able object, like a rectangular
        sheet of aluminium"   flying over
        the southern   suburbs.
          He  is Mr  John  W. Boyle, 41
        year-old vice-president of the
        British Astronomical Associa-
        tion's Victorian branch.
          Mr   Boyle, who   is a cartog-
        rapher with the Lands Depart-
        ment, has been interested in
        astronomy for about 26 years,
        but he said tonight: "I've never
        seen       anything in daylight that
        has been        as      puzzling to me.
          "I have           always kept an          open
        mind on         the things that people
        claim       to have   seen         in the  sky.
        Now,       I am  convinced          that there
        is
             something peculiar going on."
             Mr
              Boyle sighted the object
        from St Kilda at 11 a.m.    on
        Sunday, January 3, as he was
        taking his four-year-old son,
        Ian, to the beach.  He saw   it
        for       15 to 20      seconds.
             My     first    impression      was    that
        it     looked   like      a   piece of paper
        being blown along by the wind
