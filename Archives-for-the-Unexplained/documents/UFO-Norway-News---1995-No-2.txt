Editorial by Mentz D Kaarb0.
Recently, news from England suggests that that a film, recording events from the alleged
UFO crash at Roswell has come to light. We have published material pertaining to this
event several times before. Allegedly this film will verify much of the information
gathered during the past 15 years. Last year, a report was issued by the US Airforce
stating that the wreckage gathered at Roswell was a crashed balloon and part of a secret
project called Project Mogul. This report was welcomed by many sceptics and a number
of ufologists who proclaimed that at last, the rumours of downed UFO's at Roswell could
now finally be laid to rest. We feel that the official report of the Roswell incident is
unsatisfactory and fails to explain many of the documented details and statements.
Discussion will doubtless continue for many years to come. There is a high probability that
the Roswell film is a fake, in which case it will be a masterpiece of misinformation. We
await, with some excitement for the film to be released.
Our colleague Ole Jonny Brrenne will be looking at some of the many fantastic claims of
Rauni-Leena Luukanen. Unfortunately there was not room for our astrology and
astronomy columns this time.
Norwegian sightings by Odd-Gunnar Reed
After the UFO program shown on TV2 the 20th of April where UFO Norway received a
great deal of publicity our contact telephones have been ringing nonstop. Sightings from as
far back as the 1940' s in addition to more recent ones have been reported. We bring you a
number of the most interesting ones.
Kvinesdal in West-Agder October 1953.
4 young people were on a camping trip in the county ofKvinesdal. It was dark outside and
they were playing cards in the light from a paraffin lamp and some candles. The hut where
they were staying was in a valley by a lake. Suddenly a bright illumination outside the
window, lighting up the surroundings and the interior of the hut appeared. Due east of the
hut a yellow/white sphere, larger than the moon was observed (there was no moon that
night). After about 4 minutes the sphere disappeared
Kabelvag in Nordland August 1958
This observation is unusual because the object was estimated to have been about 1
kilometre long. The phenomena was first seen through a window. It was strongly
illuminated as if the sun was being reflected from its surface. Its shape resembled a
quarter moon lying on its back. From outside the phenomena gave the same impressions
except it was apparent that the object was moving. Our observer sighted along a gate post
and perceived that the object was approaching fast. It changed direction towards the right,
moved up and rolled over until its short side was towards them and accelerated away at a
speed difficult to comprehend.
Bergen in Hordaland Autumn 1983/84
It was close to 8 o'clock in the evening. Low clouds were illuminated from the city lights.
The observer was walking home after work when he noticed an enormous cigar shaped
object flying slowly towards Mount Ulriken. The object was dark brown and from its
interior, many small lights appeared to emerge. The object which was estimated to be
about 20 to 25 meters long, disappeared after a few minutes up into the clouds.
By Hessdalen in South Trnnderlag August 1987
We bring you an interesting report from a senior citizen from Oslo. A strange phenomena
was observed from a farm situated in a valley not far from Hessdalen. In August 1987, 4
people were gathered at the farm to enjoy the country surroundings. One of the 4 came
rushing in and told the others to come outside because he had seen an UFO. All four saw
a bright object moving slowly and silently towards the north. There's no road or electricity
in the valley and from the farm they were looking down on the object. It was visible for
about 10 minutes.
Gravdal in Nordland August 1990.
A 7 year old boy was playing together with an older friend in a playground. His mother
who was together with his younger brother was told a most curious story when she came
to the playground. The child gave a detailed description of a strange phenomena he had
seen. His older playmate had not been present during the sighting but had heard a
humming noise. The mother came across a similar event seen by some tourists, in a local
newspaper at about the same time. The newspaper wrote that the tourists had seen a
pointed formation of round shiny metallic objects rising into the sky. The objects
description was similar to what her son had seen. She contacted the local newspaper and
informed them of her sons similar experience. The newspaper was reluctant to print this
because they felt that the publicity might have a negative influence on the boy. After the
incident the child had told his mother that when he saw the object he «was not there» but
was unable to be specific. Later, on an autumn evening the boys sat at the kitchen table
eating supper when they saw something strange through the window. It frightened them
so much that the visiting friend would not go home alone. A while after the incident they
drew what they had seen. A large intense yellow sphere moving from west to east 2
meters off the ground. The object was moving in the exact opposite direction to the first
one. The mother talked to people at the Northern Lights Observatory in Troms0, the
meteorological office and the university. She was concerned that the phenomena could be
ball lightning and that a repetition could be dangerous. Many UFO sightings occurred
after this in Lofoten.
Koppgang in Hedmark, September 1994.
A 16 year old boy and his family were spending the weekend at their cottage. A little
before 1Opm they noticed a colourful light in the sky towards the west . It was red, green,
blue and white, and moved in a strange inexplicable manner. After 10 minutes it moved
with tremendous speed down towards the ground where it remained motionless for about
 40 minutes before it gradually disappeared. The boy and his aunt drove towards the spot
 where the light had disappeared and after 2 kilometres saw the light again, this time
towards the north-west. Signalling to the phenomena by blinking with the car headlights
produced no response so they turned the car around and started to drive back to the
 cottage. After a while they decided to try again, stopped the car, blinked with the car
headlights and got out of the car taking their binoculars with them. Through the binoculars
they observed the phenomena moving towards them, when it was about 50 meters away
they jumped in the car and drove away. They had been subjected to a fascinating but
frightening experience.
The mystery ofMandals archipelago, by Odd-Gunnar Reed.
What is the mysterious light that keeps appearing in the archipelago close to Manda!?
 Out investigative team has visited one of the observers who has seen the phenomena
several times. Our observer was sitting together with an acquaintance looking out over the
sea when a «boat» appeared not far from the cottage where they were sitting. It appeared
to hover slightly above the surface and the underside was illuminated with a bright blue
light that shone down into the water. The «boat» looked like an oval necklace of pearls
with the windows around the circumference resembling the pearls. While the two ladies
sat looking a light descended and landed on the upper right hand side of the object, and a
moment later another light landed. After a while the lights ascended and disappeared after
 10 to 15 minutes. During the observation the lights- were extinguished several times and
reappeared after about 10 minutes. This first observation lasted over 2 hours and through
the binoculars we had with us we were able to observe closely. The next time our
observer witnessed the phenomena was in august 1993, and as before she was together
with an acquaintance. It was a warm late summer moonlit evening and as before they saw
a «boat» moving about, on or close to the water. The «boat» came to rest part way up a
mountain on an island called Frero, and shone with an intense white light. We move
forward to the middle of November. This time our observer was staying in her
summerhouse together with several friends. An enormous brightly illuminated «boat»
appeared and shortly after moved out of view behind an island illuminating the sky with a
reddish glow. Suddenly as if two enormous floodlights were switched on the surrounding
countryside was illuminated, turning night into day. Several times during the night they
were awakened as the «floodlights» swept around the countryside from the «boat» that
was still visible on the water. The same phenomena was observed in February, March,
August and October in 1994. During one of these episodes the transistor radio stopped
working, it was later discovered that the chargeable batteries were malfunctioning and had
to be replaced. Our observer was also subjected to a mysterious experience in 1990 that
had paranormal overtones. Our observer, together with a friend was returning to her
summerhouse after a fishing trip late one evening when they passed two men on the road.
The men started to shout and scream, and the two ladies ran as fast as they could back to
summerhouse where they barricaded the door and prepared to defend themselves with a
shotgun and a knife. There had been two cases of rape in the area. Our observer was
wearing 5 pieces of jewellery, 2 rings, an earring, a necklace and a bracelet. All her
jewellery fell to the floor when they reached the summerhouse. The jewellery showed
signs of melting, the clasp on the bracelet had melted apart. The necklace had melted in
nine different places, and the rings fractured in no less than seven places. Our observer
suffered no bums and is unable to explain this occurrence.
Authentic Roswell film from 1947 ? By Philip Mantle.
Much has been written and spoken about the alleged UFO crash at Roswell in 194 7.
Recently there has been written a great deal about the newly discovered Roswell film,
owned by Mr. Ray Santilli, director of the Merlin group in London. I will use this
opportunity to clarify my engagement with Ray Santilli and the film. RS approached me
about 2 years ago to discuss the possibility of producing an UFO documentary video. RS
informed me that while doing research on a documentary about Elvis Presley he was
contacted by an elderly cameraman who sold him an original film from the Roswell
incident. During the next year I had several appointments with RS to see this original film.
There were always plausible reasons why the screening had to be postponed. In March
 1995 I was however given a 7 minutes long copy of a sequence that RS called «Onsite
examinatiom>. I have studied this sequence many times The sequence was filmed in a tent
or a hut of some kind. The camera was fixed in a comer of the room and lighting was by
gas or paraffin lamps, The film depicts two men in white taking tissue samples from a
humanoid figure lying on a table and partially covered by a sheet. The film was of very
poor quality making it difficult to see fine detail, however the humanoids large black eyes
could de discerned without difficulty. Eager to see more of this film I met RS in London
on the 28th of April and this time was presented with film of good quality depicting an
autopsy of a humanoid. In April I put forward a 2 page proposal to RS regarding an
analyses of the films. Two companies have agreed to do the analysis, Kodak and Hassan
Shah Films. RS has agreed to this proposal but so far, has not followed up the agreement.
Just prior to this magazine being printed Odd-Gunnar R0ed had several telephone
conversations with Philip Mantle. According to preliminary, and so far unsubstantiated
reports, more original footage showing a crashed flying saucer with detailed recording of
the wreckage, both at the crash site and inside a tent has come to light. These sequences
have only been viewed by RS.

Mysteries of the Moon. Part 4 by Neil Nixon.
Is science on the wrong track when knowledge from other celestial bodies is used to solve
questions related to our satellite? In previous articles of this series we have seen how
scientists disagree about fundamental questions regarding the moon. There is however one
theory that can explain all the mysteries. Michael Yasin and Alexander Scherbakov first
presented this theory in the Soviet space exploration journal Sputnik in July 1970 were it
was met with ridicule and scepticism. The essence of their argument was that the moon is
an artificial Earth satellite placed in orbit around the Earth by unknown intelligent beings.
In short, they postulated that the moon was a spaceship.
