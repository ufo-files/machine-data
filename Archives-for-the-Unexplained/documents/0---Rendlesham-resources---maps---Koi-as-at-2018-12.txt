Page 67.   Forestry Commission planting map:

                  When Ian Ridpath posted this map (credited to Adrian Frearson) on the
                  old Rendlesham incident forum in March 2010 (in a thread entitled
                  "Rendlesham forest area on Google Street View"), Ian posted "Vince
                  instructed me how to read it. The big figures are the plot numbers (with
                  subdivisions marked a, b, c etc) and the numbers below are the sizes in
                  acres. Hence we can see that the area where B, P and C had to stop
                  their vehicle and walk was Plot 150e. The labelling CP 75 tells us it
                  was planted with Corsican pine in 1975, i.e. they would still have been
                  only 5-6 years old at the time of the UFO event. The area to the east,
                  Plot 145, would have contained fully grown trees back in 1980 -- it
                  was felled not long after the UFO event and replanted in 1984 and
                  1991, if I read the map aright" ... "But on seeing it everything finally
                  fell into place regarding B, P and C's expedition into the forest, and we
                  can see how surprisingly accurate Penniston's contemporary sketch
                  was".
