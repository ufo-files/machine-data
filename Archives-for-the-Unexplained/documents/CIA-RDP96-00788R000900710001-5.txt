                       SUMMARY ANALYSIS

             REMOTE VIEWING (RV} SESSION XLIII



1.  (S}  This report provides documentation of a remote
viewing session conducted for training purposes to enhance
a remote viewer's demonstrated ability.

2.   (S} The remote viewer's impressions appear to match
the "beacon's" location at the originally designated target
time. The viewer was disturbed by ambient room noise and
was unable to relax and concentrate as well as he has done
in the past. The remote viewer stated that he was confident
that he had "hit" the target and was generally enthusiastic
about the session. The "beacon" was enroute from his office
to the Mall Entrance of the Pentagon to his car at the
originally designated on-target time which was set forward
thirty minutes at the last minute.

3.  (S}  The protocol used for this session is detailed in
the document, Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote
Viewing Protocol (S}, undated.

4.   (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions
during the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings
made by the viewer reference his impressions of the target
site.
