One case I found was an elderly gentleman in his
early 70’s that had a visit during the night from his
cousin. He clearly saw him at the foot of his bed,
yet he lived miles away. His cousin told him that
he’d died, and then he disappeared. In shock, the
gentleman looked at his bedside clock which
showed 11pm. The next morning, his sister
phoned him to say that their cousin had died of
heart failure at 11pm the previous night.

There are literally hundreds of stories across the
internet with similar details. All claim that a
clock, or in some cases all clocks, had stopped at
the exact same time as the person dying. Some go
on to tell that on the anniversaries of these deaths,
the clocks stop to the exact same time again.
Once case repeated this happening for 9 years
straight.


Reading these cases sure hasn’t answered my
question as to why I looked at the time during my
near-death experience, but hopefully one day I
may get the answer I’m looking for.
