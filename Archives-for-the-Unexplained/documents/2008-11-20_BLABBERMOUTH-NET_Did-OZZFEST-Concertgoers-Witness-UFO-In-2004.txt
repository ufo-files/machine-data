Did OZZFEST Concertgoers Witness UFO In 2004?

Pollstar.com reports that The History Channel debuted an episode of "UFO Hunters" October
29 that featured one of the best-documented UFO "sightings" to date. It took place August 21,
2004, when fans were leaving an Ozzfest concert that took place First Midwest Bank
Amphitheatre in in Tinley Park, Illinois.

As the fans were caught up in a traffic jam, three lights in a triangular pattern hovered above
them for nearly 20 minutes. According to a Chicago UFO expert, it may be the most-seen
incident recorded.

Much of the episode was filmed beneath the roof of the venue, where the show's hosts and
guests reported their information. It is not known if any of the staff of the once-Tweeter
Center saw the UFO in question.
