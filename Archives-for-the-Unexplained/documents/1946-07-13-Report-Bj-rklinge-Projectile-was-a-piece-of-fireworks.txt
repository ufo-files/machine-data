Case xxx:
”Projectile” was a piece of fireworks

Time: Saturday, July 13, 21.30 hours
Place: Björklinge, 20 km N of Uppsala (county of Uppsala)

””RADIOBOMB” EXPLODED. Uppsala, Monday, the Sv.D. correspondent.  For the first time since the different ”radio bombs” were first seen, a mysterious projectile has been observed even as it exploded.  

”The sighting reported from Björklinge, 20 kilometres North of Uppsala by farmer Carl Nilsson from the Nynäs farm near Långsjön. As Mr. Nilsson, and some other persons, at about 21.30 on Saturday, were in the woods close to the farm they heard, in the air, a dull buzzing from a projectile going on 10 to 20 meters altitude and in a flat trajectory. The projectile came from the south and went towards the northwest. It came at high speed and exploded about 400 meters from the place where it was first observed. The explosion sounded like three shots in rapid succession, and an area of 4 to 5 square meters was wrapped in smoke. The incident has now been reported to police and military authorities, who have started an investigation.”

””SPACE PROJECTILE WAS BOY´S FIREWORKS. Uppsala, Saturday (MT). The mysterious projectile which a couple of days ago was sighted in Björklinge...and whose trajectory ended with a dull explosion, has now been the object of a police and military investigation. ”The projectile” has turned out to be  a fireworks article sent by a couple of boys.”

The case was never entered in the Defence Staff tabulations. It was, however, reported, in an Intelligence Report, to the [US] Central Intelligence Group, on April 9, 1947, with reference to a Danish radio broadcast on July 16 -- no mention made of the fireworks solution.

Status: Fireworks
Sources: Svenska Dagbladet, July 16, TT newswire of July 15, Morgon Tidningen, July 21 (also in Bullard: The Airship File).
