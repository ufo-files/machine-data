              "Meteor Showers" At Lonpeach?
                    A    RAAF       team   who
                                          investigated reports of flying objects in the
              Longreach        (Queensland) last m onth believe that the sightings may
                            area

              have been meteor   "showers".

                          The         Minister      for
                    Mr   Howson, said that the
                    RAAF     received a consid
                    erable number     of reports
                    of unidentified objects, and
                    these   reports   were    in
                    vestigated.
                           On  many   occasions, they
                        had turned out to be weather
                        balloons, high-flying aircraft,
                        stars or       meteors.   Over the
                        last few       years, only three or
                        four       per cent of these sight
                        ings had not been explained
                        on  the basis of some natural
                        phenomena.
                      The sightings were   made    on

                    July 22 by Mr C. Lanham       and
                    a  party from   an   airstrip   at
                    Longreach.               Mr   Lanham,      an

                    experienced   commercial   char
                    ter pilot, and his party report
                    ed that they observed from   six
                    to  10 high-flying objects, some
                    what    resembling jet aircraft,
                    flying overhead    in the vicinity
                    of Longreach.
