  UFO EXPERT WARNS EARTHLINGS: SPACE ALIENS WILL INVADE IF WE
  LAY ONE FINGER ON 'EM!
  Weekly World News, October 3, 1995
  By Scotty Paul
  The U.S. and all of planet Earth will be invaded in a matter of
  minutes by space aliens throughout the universe if we advance our
  nuclear capabilities or space exploration too far, says a noted UFO
  authority.
  "And there isn't a thing we could do about it," says Stanton
  Friedman, a nuclear scientist and UFO expert. "They are thousands of
  millions of years ahead of us in intelligence."
  In an exclusive interview with Weekly World News, Dr. Friedman made
  these startling revelations about our neighbors in outer space:
  *They are gray and tan in color with oversized heads,                           skinny bodies,
  huge eyes and holes for noses.
  *They visit the United States frequently to study our way of life and
  to make sure we are not becoming a threat to them.
  *Thousands of unsuspecting earthlings have been abducted for short
  periods of time by aliens.
  Dr. Friedman isn't saying we are going to be invaded immediately, but
  warns they are watching us and circulate among us every day.
  "You must understand the stars are billions of years old, while Earth
  is considered middle-aged. They've had far more years to advance in
  every aspect," he said.
  "They've watched earthlings kill each other for years instead of using
  our minds to settle differences."

Stanton Friedman in Weekly World News [archive]

  Dr. Friedman said aliens from outer space have extremely different
  physical features from us, but are capable of changing their
  appearance when they visit us.
  He is one of many UFO experts who believe the U.S. military captured
  an alien spacecraft "and crew members" in New Mexico in 1947.
  "What we have in this case is a cosmic Watergate among a small group
  in the government," he said. "They haven't been able to find out how
  the aliens got here<and they probably never will."
  Dr. Friedman, who has two degrees from the University of Chicago and
  has worked for many of America's leading companies in space
  exploration, says earthlings are kidnapped every day by space aliens.
  "I have letters from many of the thousands who have been abducted," he
  said. "They were studied, X-rayed and released.
  "But their memory of what happened was wiped away, much like what a
  hypnotist does when he snaps someone out of a trance."
