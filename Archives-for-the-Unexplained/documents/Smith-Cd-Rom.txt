only to the most astute researchers. <G> The Beck recording is
directly from Maj . Keyhoe. Back in the late 50s and early 60s,
researchers used recorded letters , rather than written ones.
These audio letters are exceedingly rare and I probably have the
largest collection of personal audio correspondence between
Ufologists in existence . Many times the researcher would listen
to their letter and then record over it to send back their reply .
This, of course , negated hearing both letters , but the reply gave
many good insights into what the original audio letter was about.
The unique ones I have are when the original audio letter and the
reply was on a separate tape . There are only a couple of those
that survived . Anyway , that ' s why the Beck recording is so
exceptionally important ... not just the information it contains ,
but that it survived. Now, if I could only find Keyhoe's repl y!
<G>

I have a lot of Herbert Clark ' s audio letters. He was an early
and quite astute Canadian researcher.
