Presentation
Is there any genomic evidence for interplanetary panspermio (e.g.,
Mars to Earth) at this early time?
Transfer of cellular material between the planets of the inner solar
system via impact ejecta is a statistical near-certainty over long
periods of geological time, and would be even more likely early in
Earth's history when impact rates were far higher. Therefore, the
possibility that extant life on Earth originated on Mars cannot be
reasonably excluded. It is possible that adaptations and selections
consistent with an exoplanetary origin may be preserved and
detectable within reconstructions of the LUCA or pre-LUCA genome.
