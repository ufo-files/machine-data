Tuesday, January 22, 2008
Gallery Of New Google Zurich Office
The Google employees in Zürich, Switzerland, moved to a new building, and Flickr user Tiptoe between April 2007 and January
2008 shot what seem to be a couple of photos from within. These rooms have some familiar Google office design elements, though
some of them look a bit more old-fashioned than the ones of other offices, while others look even more futuristic/ odd/ cultish than
usual (I think some new age sect waiting for UFO landings would instantly feel at home).


Graffiti on the wall.


If I read the pic right, both the bookshelf and the fireplace in this library room are fake.


This may be a meeting room (with banana on top).


The kitchen.


An egg on astroturf.


The label to the left identifies this as an Igloo Satellite Cabin, though the photographer calls it a “bathysphere.”


The “therapy zone” offers free stress tests and audits to help you advance to the level of Operating Thetan massages.


The chill out zone.
