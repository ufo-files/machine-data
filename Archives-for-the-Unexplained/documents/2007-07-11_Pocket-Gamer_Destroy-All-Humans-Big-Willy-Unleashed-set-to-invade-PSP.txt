Destroy All Humans! Big Willy Unleashed set to invade PSP
Stop sniggering or Crypto will get you
By Joao Diniz Sanches
| Jul 11, 2007
PSP
| Destroy All Humans! Big Willy Unleashed
THQ has just announced two new additions to its popular Destroy All Humans! franchise, one of which is coming to Sony's handheld (and PlayStation 2, as well as Nintendo's Wii console).
Big Willy Unleashed will serve as a prequel to the Xbox 360/PS3 release, Path of the Furon, and sees you controlling Crypto, a Furon alien travelling the world in the 1970s. Whether on foot, on board your
UFO, or in Big Willy – a prototype alien mech masquerading as a fast food restaurant mascot – your task is generally to unleash massive destruction wherever you go.
Very little else is known at this stage (THQ appears to be focusing its attention on communicating the advantages of the Wii version due to the console's unique interface), but expect the series' trademark irreverent
humour to feature heavily when the game is released early next year.

Atomic Champions brings competitive block-breaking puzzles to the palm of your hand
Brick your friends
By Iwan Morris
| Jan 3
iOS + Android
| Atomic Champions
      Atomic Champions is a newly released competitive brick-breaking puzzler
      Take turns breaking as many blocks as possible and outdo your opponent
      Utilise distinctive booster cards to get an edge and come out on top
When it comes to competitive puzzlers, I feel like there's a lot we've already explored. You've got your classic board games, your unusual takes such as PvP tower defence, and even the occasional competitive
match-three! So it's surprising that I haven't seen many (if any) competitive brick breakers, until now.
Gameplay in Atomic Champions is simple, and not too complicated. You take turns blasting bricks as you would in any other entry in this genre, and aim to get a higher score than your opponent. But, a quick twist
lets you use booster cards to alter your tactics, giving you a better chance at victory if you play it smart.
