Daily Mirror. London.
23 APR 88
Invasion of the carrots
UFO investigators yesterday warned people to look
out for huge flying carrots, as big as hot air balloons,
after two were spotted over Derbyshire. Onlookers
said the carrots, with flames spouting from them,
looped the loop and one crashed into a pond in Kirk
Hallam.
