                MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD                                                       SG1A
                SUBJECT:


SG1A            1. (U) The following information was provided:
                ~.  a. (S) Source reported an excavation type of ditch, with a gravel
                flooring approximately 8-10 feet in depth and 20 feet in width in the
                vicinity of the above location.
                    b. (S) The wall of the excavation was described as having wood fram-
                ing along the side. (Sources description does not indicate if this ex-
                cavation is recent construction or a permanent feature at the location.)
                     c. (S) Source reported a tunnel like opening at the end of the ex-
                .cavation, with lights hanging from the ceiling. This tunnel opening
                 appeared to go very far back from the opening as the lights were approx-
                 imately 100 feet apart, and the grade appeared to be about 10% approach-
                 ing the rear of the tunneled area.
                    d. (S) Source does not report any people other than an interior tunnel
                maintenance crew. He did state that he could hear a generator (large KW)
                start up in the back ground. Stated that it sounded very much like it was
                already running and had a load switched to it.
                    e. (5) Source did not state the origin of his information, but said
                that he felt there would be a demolition or deliberate destruction of the
                tunnel with explosives on or abouT lOOO hrs, 24 June 1980.
                2. (5) When specifically questioned about the possible nature of event or
                level of activity which might or might not be planned for 1000 hours on the
                24th of June 1980, He responded that he did not know of any event or activity
                which was supposed to occur other than what should be normal for that date
                and time.
                       Request the above information be evaluated and comments be provided
                       undersigned in writing no later than 19 June 1980, this will permit
                       evaluation of the source of information for both accuracy and intell-
                       value. Information is pertinent to 1000 hours, 24 June 1980.
