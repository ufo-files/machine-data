                       Stuart Miller – editor UFO Review


I, for one, certainly found the month of May 2004 a very exciting one. The
Mexican sighting, as yet still untarnished by any successful debunking effort or
for that matter, even a serious debunking effort, stands supreme above the
UFO community, smiling wistfully down upon us as we below chase around like
headless chickens in our exhilarated state. While as yet there have only been
quiet mutterings of comparison between this incident and the Phoenix Lights,
and certainly no scientific connection established, spiritually at least it comes
in the same package. Nearly. The Phoenix Lights was a banner carrying parade,
loudly proclaiming “Here We Are”. This latest lot were invisible to the Human
eye and only showed up on infrared cameras.

We have compiled a special article on Mexico containing the best of the
published research so far plus other comments and opinion, and just for good
measure, some of the more extreme points of view expressed on the incident
as well. As befits a subject that draws among its aficionados the mentally
challenged, it is amusing to see how one or two have gone off on a tangent.
And yet, in fairness, who’s to say they’re wrong? They could have been
Pleiadean ships on their way back from a soccer tournament on Zeta but don’t
offer opinions like that anywhere near a “serious” List. The Lazy Man’s Guide To
The Mexico Sighting starts on page 69 and just runs and runs!

It was hard to sense the reaction to the first edition. There was one negative
comment from a rival publication (not Gary and Russ) and a smattering of
positive ones, but beyond that, not a lot else. The number of downloads though
were good. My thanks in particular go to Joe McGonagle of Ufology UK for a
very kind review.

But I would appreciate your comments and feedback. I know the presentation
at the moment is poor, and that is in hand, but what articles would you like to
see? What topics do you want covered? Let me know at stuart@uforeview.net

We are very pleased to welcome back to the fold Andrew Pike who was with me
at UFO magazine. Andrew brings immense experience and insight to the
subject of Space and not a little “behind the door” information as well. His
article in this issue poses more questions than answers! Enjoy

                         Doctor Lynne Kitei


There are some people in life that you have to stand back and admire. They
seem to have so much energy and positive drive that it’s breadth taking. Lynne
Kitei is one such person. This lady just loves life and people, and enjoys helping
them. This isn’t some angelic do-gooder, placed on Earth to spread love and
goodwill. She is a practical, humane individual who freely admits that her “fix”
is helping others, and it is pleasing and comforting that the impression you
form of her from reading the book translates into reality when you actually
speak to her. She’s a dynamo.

An early strong impression from the book was her reluctance to come forward,
and the more cynical among us might interpret that as heroic modesty. But
when you hold a high profile position within the general medical and social
community of the city you live in, it takes a bit of courage to step forward and
tell everyone that you’ve filmed these lights in the sky and they might be from
another dimension, even if a few thousand others saw the same lights as well.
As we all know, the scientific community is not always welcoming towards
colleagues who suddenly “flip” and start talking gibberish.

But as you will read below, Lynne felt called upon, almost picked to come
forward. There were just too many coincidences and minor incidents to ignore,
that all seemed to be saying the same thing: “Get off your backside, get out
there and tell the world.” These incidents may have been imagined or they may
have been real, but regardless, they were real to Lynne and she responded. And
she found, when she peaked above the covers, a world she had no idea existed
– our world. A world of strange sightings, stories, reluctant governments, high
secrecy, and the general weird and wonderful. “Thrown in at the deep end” is a
phrase that comes to mind.

It is odd that no one beat her to it with a book on what happened and is still
happening in Phoenix, but my, what an impact she has made. In part, this is
down to her. Because of her bright personality and comfort in front of a
microphone or camera, the media have been drawn to her like bees to honey
and the result has been that the book is already on its second print run.
Inevitably, there are also controversy and counter arguments to deal with, but
this is like griss to the mill. In a game of table tennis debate, Lynne’s forehand
is unstoppable which is hardly surprising. She didn’t read about the Phoenix
Lights after the event. She was there, she experienced them and the strong
emotional impact of such an encounter acts like a suit of armour, forging her
on and deflecting the arrows of the sceptics and naysayer.

This interview was conducted on the 24th March 2004 at 11:00pm GMT.

SM = Stuart Miller
LK = Lynne Kitei

went well and gosh, just the response…………. People have been so kind and so
generous in their thoughts, really receptive to what I’ve tried to accomplish.
You can’t ask for more than that.

SM: All I’m aware of really, in terms of reaction to the book is a universal
positive reaction. Have there been any negative reactions?

KT: Not really. I received one E-mail from a military fellow who was adamant
that these must have been flares. I suggested to him that he actually look at
the book and the data, which speaks for itself. He wrote me a very kindly email
back saying, “You know what? There may be more to this”.

I’m not trying to persuade anyone, everyone in their own time. Certainly,
different people have different reactions. Some are frightened and some don’t
want to deal with it at all. I have to say that most everyone I come across, once
they know the value of what I’ve compiled, look at it again with fresh eyes. It
took me time for it to sink in but no one else in history has photographic
evidence of these unexplained phenomena that are happening world wide,
before, during and after an historic mass sighting, from the same vantage
point. They seem to be happening in the same area as well, which adds to the
curiosity of it all.

Experts have gone to great lengths to try to find a logical explanation for what
I’ve both observed and photographed and have yet to find one. If anything, I

have uncovered a whole new world that I had no idea existed.

SM: How have you found that world?

LK: Well, actually, before ’95, both myself and my husband, who is also a

phenomena. And I mean at all. The only movies that I saw were “Close
Encounters” and “ET”. We have two boys, one’s an attorney and the other is
graduating medical school - he’s going to be a neurologist. They grew up
watching “Star Trek”, as we all did as a family. It had wonderful messages and
it was very entertaining and you know, that was about it!

But as far as getting into the subject matter, I just never really had time.

SM: Or the inclination?

LK: Yes, or the desire. I just never did. I was so busy doing community
education, practicing medicine and bringing up the kids. I was just too busy to
even think about it.

The ’95 close sighting, very close to our property, really woke me up to
something incredible, the possibilities. It’s hard to describe that to people, but
when our friends or relatives come over and actually see where these amber
orbs in a pyramid formation were, hovering over a private desert area, they’re
just amazed.

SM: Wasn’t it about a 100 metres away?

LK: Just about, give or take, it’s hard to judge. To my eye, and I really tried to
take everything in, it was just so starling and unusual.

I have had my own company called Health Education Learning Programs since
‘85 that produces videos and work curricula internationally. It all comes out of
my own pocket and time because I saw the need and did what I could to help
the community. It’s been very successful as far as getting prevention education
out there, with acclaim and awards and all that, which is very rewarding.

My main purpose is to get the reality of health issues out to the community and
that’s why, when all this fell into my lap, I felt compelled to do something
productive with this issue as well. But I wondered, “Why do I have this? I don’t
know anything about this topic”.

When I asked my friends what they thought, they would turn to me and say,
“Lynne, if there’s anybody who can do something credible and professional with
all this, you can. After all, that’s what you do.” I have to admit, I felt obligated
to do something with what I had. I knew I could bring a lot to the table if I really
put my mind to it and certainly, I’ve dedicated my life’s work to education and
healing. Even before medical school, my undergraduate work was in science
education and I’ve always been committed to educating the community to vital
health issues. But now there was this vital issue that I didn’t know anything
about.

The close sighting was an extraordinary experience, it really was. One wall of
our bedroom is a window, so we have enjoyed looking out at the panoramic
view of the city skyline from our mountainside home over many years. We know
what planes look like and helicopters and streetlights and car lights etc.

My husband was the first one to see the orbs and called me out of the bath. The
reason I remember the date was because it was the eve of my birthday and I
thought it was so cool to have this experience on my birthday. I stood there
dripping wet with a towel round me that I grabbed from our Roman bath. When
he called me to the window, there were these close orbs in formation. I call
them orbs because the light did not extend outside the edge. It was startling.
My first thought was to get my video camera, but it was downstairs and I didn’t
want to miss anything, I didn’t know how long it was going to last. Something
just told me to take everything in that I could, that it was important. I noted the
size, the shape, and the colour. I tried to determine the distance.

The size was about 3 to 6 feet each, depending on how close or far they were.
They seemed to be about a 100 yards from our property and there were three
distinct objects. They were oval shaped. The colour within each orb was very
different than any colour or light I’ve ever seen. I noticed immediately that
every other light out there glares, but these did not. The light also did not
reflect anything at all. The colour was uniform throughout, not like a light bulb
that has a hotspot. Very soothing, mesmerising.

So as I’m staring at these things that just seemed to be hovering in a rigid
triangle formation, one on top and two closely aligned underneath, I thought,
“If I don’t get a picture of this, nobody’s going to believe it.” As I was running to
the closet to grab my inexpensive 35mm camera, my husband calls me back
saying, “Get over here quick. One of them is disappearing”. As we stood there
watching, the top orb seemed to be on a dimmer switch. That’s the only way I
can describe it. It didn’t budge at all. Going back to “Star Trek”, which was the
first thing I thought of (laughing), a Klingon Cloaking device came to mind! I
can’t deny it, it was the first thing I thought of! You know how it wavers before
it disappears? Well this didn’t do that. It absolutely didn’t do that. It didn’t
budge. It didn’t take off, it didn’t move, it didn’t waver, nothing. It just was
dimming as if it was going into another dimension. Like it was still there but we
didn’t see it anymore. It went somewhere.

Anyway, I stepped out onto the balcony and took a shot of the two bottom orbs.
I immediately noticed an eerie silence, as if time had stopped. It was very
bizarre. I didn’t go into this in the book because it was right at the beginning
and the publisher thought it was a little too out there, but the truth of it is that
as I stood watching these two bottom orbs, I felt they were watching back. I
felt as if there was an intelligent presence, watching. The thoughts were
actually going through my brain, “Who are you? What are you? Do you know
that I’m here? I’d love to meet you.” It was very exciting for me.

LK: It wasn’t that often, but I did come across witnesses, especially some

some people think it must be a bad thing. However, it is intriguing that after
the object passed over them during the mass sighting, witnesses suddenly felt

comfortable coming forward because their flares were so blatantly different

By the time they had set off the flares in March of 2000, I had done extensive

between all unexplained phenomena. Not only was my own near death
experience reawakened but other witnesses shared that their NDEs were also
reawakened by the Phoenix Lights. This sent me on a new road of inquiry. By
the way, after discussing this curious finding on radio programs I received
many e-mails from people either studying this at university level or people just
lauding the fact that someone of credence was coming forward to address the
possibilities. It is indeed comforting for them to know that they are not alone. I
ended up coining the phrase “An UP” for an Unexplained Phenomenon, which is
really taking off here in Arizona. People are actually excited to admit that
they’ve had an UP!

After the flare drop, I was more convinced than ever that it was important to at
least consider coming forward with what I had. I was still very apprehensive,
but have to say that in good conscience I couldn’t just stick what I had
compiled in a drawer. It seemed too compelling to dismiss and even more
important to share. Mr. Miller, my husband and I work very hard to live a
comfortable, private life. I don’t need to do this. But for some reason, I need to
do this.

Even so, I was waiting for that one more little nudge. My older son, Brett, had
been very close with my mom seven years before she passed on from colon
cancer. She had metastasis colon cancer that was already seeded in her liver.
After her surgery in Philadelphia in ’83 we brought her out to Phoenix. I had her
visualize Pacman eating up the cancer cells. We had positive thinking
discussions and sessions. She ultimately went through remission for seven
years, which was a miracle in itself. During those seven years she got very
close with Brett. She passed in 1990.

Anyway, he wanted to get a Siamese kitten for his then fiancée for Valentines
Day in 2000 but couldn’t find one in LA where they lived at the time. So he said,
“Mom, if you can find an ad in the weekend paper I’ll fly in on a Friday night
and we’ll go on Saturday morning to see the litter.”

Trying to find someone who would wait wasn’t easy. Weeks passed and finally I
see an ad. I call up. She says, “I’m not going to be here tonight anyway. Why

don’t you have your son fly in and come tomorrow morning? You’ll be the first
ones.” I said, “That’s fabulous.”

Well, it happened to be the tenth anniversary of my mother’s death, which is
tomorrow actually, March 25th. It was very endearing I have to tell you. He
ended up getting the pick of the litter. She was the cutest little kitty with bright
blue eyes. During the day we would tease him that the kitten was a gift from
his “mom-mom”, that she would be with him, just kidding him endearingly. I
have to add that he was the biggest sceptic of the family, but when he returned
to LA he called me in a tizzy. He said that he had purchased an Eye Zone
Polaroid camera, those very small ones, to take pictures of the kitten while she
was still little. In the middle of the role, he sees this one strip, number five
actually, that has an arm with a hand in front of the kitten’s face. You can
actually see through the fingers, it was translucent. He said, “I think it’s
grandma’s hand.” She had arthritis; she had a very distinctive hand. I was
sceptical and said, “Brett, you got your hand in the picture.” He replied, “Mom,
I was all alone taking the picture with my right hand. There’s just no way.” I
said, “Well, you graduate in May from Law school. Why don’t you put it away till
then? Don’t send it through the mail. I’ll take a look at it then.” I actually forgot
about it.

When he handed me the strip I freaked. It was amazing. And when I enlarged it,
not only is there an arm with a hand that’s translucent in front of the kitten’s
face, but also there is another arm cupping the kitten from behind. You can
even see through the arm to Brett’s backpack that was on the floor behind, you
can see the logo on it. Through all my research to that point, whether it was a
near death or UFO experience, the Beings on the other side were described as
wearing white robes. Well, the forearm in the photo is wearing a white robe and
it really did look like my mother’s hand. That did it for me,

We took it to Brett’s wedding in Baltimore that September, and when I showed
it to relatives there, as soon as they saw it they said, “Oh my God, that’s Ruth’s
hand.” Plus, I had it analyzed by optical physicists. They suggested that I find a
picture of my mother and see if it’s similar. She was head nurse of the
premature nursery at Einstein Hospital back east. Through all the hundreds of
photos I found one of her holding a little premy baby, the tiniest little thing,
almost exactly the same way. It is so eerie when you look at this. I thought,
“This is real. It seems that she’s coming through from the “other side” to let
Brett know she’s with him. What’s more, it also felt as if it was a strong
message to me to do what I have to do already.

                   To the Moon and Beyond?
                 By Andrew Pike, BSc, MSc, PGDip, FRAS


President Bush has unveiled a bold new space plan for NASA, a return to the
Moon, and a manned mission to Mars. But what is really behind this sudden
interest in sending astronauts to our nearest cosmic neighbours?

I can remember sitting up at about 4.00 am on 21 July 1969 looking at grainy
images of Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walking on the Moon. I can also
remember when NASA decided that the Moon shots should end with Apollo 17,
effectively scrapping the last few missions. The reason? Budget cuts. However,
as any astronomer will tell you the vast bulk of the money had already been
spent on these missions, to scrap them made no financial sense.
                                                                                66


Indeed, whilst attending the 21 st. Leeds International UFO Conference in
2002, I had a conversation about this very point with UFO investigator, Stanton
Friedman. We both agreed it made no financial sense to scrap the remaining
moon missions.

It was always a dream of Bush senior, whilst President, to return to the Moon,
so is it just a case of Bush junior fulfilling his father's dream or something else?

In a nutshell, President Bush has ordered NASA to complete the space station
by 2010 and begin development of a new spacecraft to carry astronauts back to
the Moon by the middle of the next decade.

First, it must be said that the International Space Station and its drain on
funding has become an embarrassment for all concerned, it is unlikely to fulfil
its goals and has become something of a white elephant. Best to scrap it now
say many astronomers. Moreover, the shuttle could have been so much better if
it were not for pressure by the military to go ahead with the bad design in use
today, a design which has cost several astronauts their lives and grounded the
craft for a second time. So, where do all these problems originate?

Make no mistake, NASA is not a civilian organisation, never has been, and is
unlikely to ever be. It dances to the tune of the military, plain and simple. True
NASA are allowed to give the impression of being a civilian organisation by
sending nice little probes to the planets, but at the end of the day NASA is a
military machine.

Take the Hubble Space Telescope for example. There are many 'Hubbles' up in
Earth orbit, all but one point down to the Earth's surface as spying machines.
When used in this way they are fine if they have a basic mirror. But for
astronomical work the Hubble Space Telescope needed a far more accurate
mirror. Unfortunately it was the military who ground that mirror to their own
standards, refused technicians the change to test it before launch and gave
Hubble blurred vision! Is it surprising that some astronomers automatically
smell a rat when President Bush announces a rush back to the Moon and
beyond?

We are all familiar with the fiasco surrounding weapons of mass destruction in
Iraq and their presence as a justification for war. We all heard of Ronald
Reagan's Star Wars project, a project which was doomed to failure from the
word go. It was nothing but an empty threat to the Soviets. I remember walking
through the campus of a cash strapped UK university at the time talking to a
staff member from the engineering department who told me " Star Wars will
never work but we need the yanks and their multi- million dollar funding, so we
will pretend." In effect papers and results were tailored so as to tell the
Americans what they wanted to hear not what the science was telling us.

Could domination over other nations be the true motive for returning to the
Moon? It is interesting that the announcement by President Bush came after a
