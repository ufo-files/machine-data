   Editors: The stories about
UFOs are one of the reasons I
like your newspaper. I clip
and save every story that you
print about them because I
know they exist.
   The skeptics should get the
city lights out of their eyes
and go out in the country now
and then and just maybe they
too would know UFOs exist.
All they would have to do is
look up once in a while and
they would see them!
   I live in the country and I
have seen a few of these "noc­
turnal lights." I have seen five
white lights and one reddish­
orange light since 1965. They
made no sound. On Oct. 31,
1985, I saw an emerald green
fireball pass by. It made a siz­
zling sound. - A.N W ellston,
