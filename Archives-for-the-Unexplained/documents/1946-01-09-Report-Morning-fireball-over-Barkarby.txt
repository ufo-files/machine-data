Case xxx.
Morning fireball over Barkarby

Time: January 9, 08.15 in the morning
Place: Fransborg, Barkarby, northwest of Stockholm

Letter found in Defence Staff archives:
”[To] the Military Staff, Stockholm 90:  
”Wednesday, January 9, at 8.15, before noon, the undersigned spotted a fireball large as a two-crowns coin in the sky, whirling and shining as hard as the sun. Came from a southerly direction and disappeared in the northwest. 
”Sincerely,  Alice Ahlsén, Fransborg, Barkarby, July 11, 1946.”

The letter was written in response to the Defence Staff call for reports on July 10. This case is wrongly listed in the Defence Staff tabulations under the date July 9.

Status: Insufficient information
Source: Letter in Defence Staff archives.
