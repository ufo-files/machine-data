1. Attached is a very general outline of what I believe represents the best approach to next years work.
I would like to see the program accomplish two things: First, it should support an in-house operational
group. This is covered in part II. In part III, you will have an opportunity to demonstrate a capability to
do operational remote viewing. The remainder of topics can be viewed as marketing tools. You will be
able to show a potential client a summary of what you have learned about remote viewing over the past
years. This combined with the scientific credibility and an active research effort should also serve to
impress a client.

2.    During FY 1989 we should stay in close contact to insure that the hoped for results are obtained.
