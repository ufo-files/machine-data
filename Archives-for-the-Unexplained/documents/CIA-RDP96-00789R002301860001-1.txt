                                     Summary of Information

              'rhe main emphasis of this target is three prominent
         objects, two of which are reminiscent of mountain peaks and are
         substantially taller than the third. These taller features occur
         on either side of the shorter object.   It is cold and it is hard
         to breath. The altitude causes dizziness. There is a separated
         flat area that is smooth, cold and hard.    There are large areas
         of stone that has been manipulated into blocks, roadways and
         walls.   There are wooden beams which have been cut and shaped by
         primitive hand saws.
              A shiny and yellowish hard substance reminiscent gold, brass
         or bronze has been manipulated into elaborate objects from its
         natural state.   Several of these objects are very old and their
         age is as important as their symbolic meaning.
              Sections.within the third, smaller object (see para 1) are
         old, musty, isolated and austere. These areas have "one purpose"
         and are spiritually oriented.   This place is held "close to the
         heart" and is associated with a feeling of reverence.    Its age
         and spiritual value are related.   It has an "Asian" feel to it.
         Someone is thinking about "kundalini" energy here.
              A wooden doorway leads into a large, open room.       An
         amorphously-shaped object (see diagram) sits atop a type of
         support or "holder" in the center of the room. This object is
         old and feels "antiquarian".
              Waves of something appear to be moving by, or rather, the
         perception is of shadows of waves of something moving by me.
         There is an area that is shimmering and reflecting and appears
         like a million diamonds.
