  From the Book Cover

  This is the true story of a CPA, wife and mother, whose middle-class, Southern-Baptist world was shattered when and
  innocent conversation triggered obsessive memories of abductions by "aliens." When Leah Haley sought professional
  help, she discovered, to her horror, that here memories were not manifestations of mental illness (and, therefore, she
  could not be "cured"). When she began to investigate, agencies of the U.S. Government and/or military became so
  vitally interested in her contact with other intelligences that they were willing to risk charges of burglary, kidnapping
  and torture to learn what she knew and to silence her. And then she learned that the alien craft that had crashed while
  she was aboard may have been shot down by the U.S. Air Force! The most credible of all "abductees," Ms. Haley has
  been featured in Omni magazine, has appeared on The Joan Rivers Show, Encounteres, and others, and has delivered
  speeches from coast to coast.
