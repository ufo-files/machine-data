  Professor Stephen Hawking Warns

Aliens almost certainly exist but humans should avoid
making contact, Professor Stephen Hawking has warned.
The renowned astrophysicist said it was "perfectly rational"
to assume intelligent life exists elsewhere.

But he warned that aliens might simply raid Earth for
resources, then move on.

"If aliens visit us, the outcome would be much as when
Columbus landed in America, which didn't turn out well for
the Native Americans," he said.

Prof Hawking thinks that, rather than actively trying to
communicate with extra-terrestrials, humans should do
everything possible to avoid contact.

He explained: "We only have to look at ourselves to see how
intelligent life might develop into something we wouldn't want
to meet."

In the past, probes have been sent into space with
engravings of human beings on board and diagrams
showing the location of our planet.

Radio beams have been fired into space in the hope of
reaching alien civilizations.

                    “LIGHTNING”
    DO NOT ALLOW THIS ATMOSPHEREIC
 PHENOMONA CONFUSE YOUR INVESTIGATION
             OF A SIGHTING


LIGHTNING is an atmospheric discharge of electricity
usually accompanied by thunder, which typically occurs
during thunderstorms, and sometimes during volcanic
eruptions or dust storms. In the atmospheric electrical
discharge, a leader of a bolt of lightning can travel at speeds
of 60,000 m/s and can reach temperatures approaching
54,000 degrees F. There are some 16 million lightning
storms in the world every year. How lightning initially forms
is still a matter of debate. Scientists have studied root
causes ranging from atmospheric perturbations (wind,
humidity, friction, and atmospheric pressure) to the impact of
solar wind and accumulation of charged solar particles. Ice
inside a cloud is thought to be a key element in lightning
development, and may cause a forcible separation of
positive and negative charges within the cloud, thus
assisting in the formation of lightning.

                  “TYPES OF LIGHTNING”

CLOUD TO GROUND LIGHTNING: The better-known and
second most common type of lightning. Of all the different
types of lightning, it poses the greatest threat to life and
property. Cloud to ground lightning is a lightning discharge
between a cumulonimbus cloud and the ground.


                                                               15
                                                              16


BEAD LIGHTNING: This lightning is a type of cloud to
ground lightning which appears to break up into a string of
short, bright sections, which last longer than the usual
discharge channel. It is relatively rare.

STACCATO LIGHTNING: This is lightning from cloud to
ground and is a short duration stroke that appears as a
single very bright flash and often has considerable
branching.


                                                              16
                                                               17


FORK LIGHTNING: Another name for cloud to ground
lightning. It normally refers to multiple branching of the bolt.

GROUND TO CLOUD LIGHTNING: Ground to cloud
lightning is a lightning discharge between the ground and a
cumulonimbus cloud, initiated by an upward moving leader
stroke. This type of lightning is rare.

CLOUD-TO-CLOUD LIGHTNING: Lightning discharges may
occur between areas of cloud without contacting the ground.
When it occurs between two separate clouds it is known as
inter-cloud lightning. This phenomenon is the most frequent
occurring type of lightning.

HEAT LIGHTNING: Cloud-to-Cloud lightning that occurs too
far away for the thunder to be heard. This occurs because
the lightning occurs very far away and the sound waves
dissipate before they reach the observer.

BALL LIGHTNING: Ball lightning may be an atmospheric
electrical phenomenon, the physical nature of which is still
controversial. The term refers to reports of luminous, usually
spherical objects can vary from pea sized to several meters
in diameter. Ball lightning has been reported to last many
seconds.

NOTE: THE PRESUMPTION OF ITS EXISTENCE IS
BASED ON REPORTED PUBLIC SIGHTINGS, AND HAS
THEREFORE PRODUCED SOMEWHAT INCONSISTENT
FINDINGS.

SPRITES: Sprites are large-scale electrical discharges,
which occur high above a thunderstorm cloud, or
cumulonimbus, giving rise to a quite varied range of visual
shapes. They normally are colored reddish orange or
greenish blue, with hanging tendrils below, and arcing
branches above their location and can also produce a
reddish halo.

LIGHTNING INDUCED MAGNETISM: The movement of
electrical charges produces a magnetic field
(electromagnetism). The intense currents of lightning
discharge create a fleeting but very strong magnetic field.

Where the lightning current path passes through rock, soil,
or metal and most man-made product, the materials can
become permanently magnetized. This effect is known as
lightning-induced remnant magnetism.

weapon.

In Norse mythology, Thor is the god of thunder and the
sound of thunder comes from the chariot he rides across the
sky and the lightning from his hammer.
