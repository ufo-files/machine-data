  Hi everyone

  Just a bit more on this documentary.
  As i said, Vince Thirkettle said on camera that he didn't think the lighthouse light could have penetrated the
  forest where they were standing.

  The documentary saw Lt Col Halt, Jim Penniston, the American intereviewer, a British scientist and later
  Vince Thirkettle standing at the alleged spot of the first sighting.

  The forest has changed a lot since the big hurricane and fire breaks etc are now in different places to pre
  hurricane. The first landing sight spot found by Messrs Halt & Penniston during the interview was on their
  part probably a guess.
  So to be fair to Vince Thirkettle, this spot may be much more dense(right spot or not) than 20 plus years
  ago which may have been why he said the light could not have penetrated the forest. He was basing this
  statement of retraction on what he saw now not 20 years ago.

  People have remarked that the TV company may have been instrumental in this change of mind so the
  mystery remained a mystery.

  Its about time that we all including the debunkers kicked the lighthouse theory in to touch. For all the
  debunkers, try coming up with something a little more plausable?

  For those hell bent on the lighthouse theory and won't let go. my question to them is, if it was/is the
  lighthouse why has this aeriel light phenomena not been seen more often rather than just 3 times 20 years
  ago?
