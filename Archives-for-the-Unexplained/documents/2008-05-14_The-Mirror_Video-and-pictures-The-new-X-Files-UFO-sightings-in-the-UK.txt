fever pitch in the late 1970s.

4) 1923: RNAS Culdrose: Two UFOs tracked by military radar and seen to cover 10 nautical miles in about 12 seconds - meaning
they would be travelling at a speed of 3.5 thousand miles per hour
5) 1952: Letter from an ex-RAF serviceman documenting how he and a number of other witnesses at a base in the Far East
watched the airfield get ‘buzzed’ by a UFO, the object was tracked on radar also.
6) Man who claimed to have been in physical and psychic contact with an extraterrestrial being called Algar who was tragically
then killed in a battle with a group of other space beings
7) An old man who claims he was taken on board a spaceship, which had landed somewhere near Aldershot, and told he was
too old and infirm for a flight.
8) 1924: Sighting by a pilot of an object passing 1.5 nautical miles from the portside of his aircraft. Documents it as either a) a
meteor b) space debris c) a missile
9) April 1984: A couple call police to their house after a UFO sighting. Police arrive and see it too so sketch it themselves.
Mr Pope, who worked for the MoD for 21 years and was responsible for investigating the sightings, said: "While there's no
evidence of little green men in these files, they should be of immense interest to sceptics and believers.
"Most here are probably misidentifications of aircraft lights and meteors, but some are more difficult to explain."
