Värnamo Nyheter
No. 9
November 11, 1972

"I watched as a mysterious aircraft released three shining orbs"

Mrs. Margit Johansson and her daughter Mia talks about the incident.


On early Friday morning (November 10, 1972), as Mrs. Margit Johansson was getting ready for breakfast, she looked out the window and contemplated the relevance of intuition. The weather was horrid; cold wins blew and rain streamed down from the sullen skies.

She looked to the heavens and noticed a luminous object to the Northwest. Initially assuming it to be the moon, she wondered vaguely whether the moon could indeed shine through the clouds, as well as why it wasn't illuminated from the East or the West in its own longitude and latitude system. This object was more than twice as wide as the moon. Also, it was shaped as a huge cigar.

One of her two dogs was acting very upset. The dog - a golden retriever named Katja - is normally very calm, but on this occasion she ran around anxiously in the kitchen, howling and appearing scared.

Margit Johansson, too, felt uneasy whilst viewing the "cigar", which shone with a intense yellowish light. The object was surrounded by a corona, which, given the rain seems perfectly normal.

The "cigar" constantly rocked back and forth sideways.

Nothing really happened between 05.30 PM and 06.00 AM, but then the "cigar" suddenly released a red, ball-shaped object, which took off in Northeast direction.

Margit Johansson decided to wake her 13-year-old daughter Mia, so she wouldn't be alone in witnessing "the unknown".

After about a quarter of an hour, the "cigar" released yet another red orb in the same direction, albeit slightly lower than before. Another fifteen minutes later, a third light left the "cigar", but this time it was yellowish white in colour and slightly oval. Its course was different (a tad awry, one might say).

It was now 6:15 AM. The two women noticed that the "cigar" had started emitting rays which shone down towards the ground. However, they could not really see where these light beams hit the ground, which means that this doesn't provide any further guidance on distance assessment. These light beams were apparently not unlike those you can see when the sun shines between the clouds.

Some time after the last light had ben spotted, the "cigar" began to slowly move, only to eventually disappear from view. It was then about 6:45 AM.

Mia wanted to get in the car in order to follow and go into the "light", but Margit Johansson felt a little scared and thus they both stayed at home.

Additional information:
Margit Johansson has estimated her distance to the object to approximately 200 meters. It should be mentioned that she received training at the Air Force as an air watcher.

Two dogs had been in the house at the time, but only Katja reacted to the phenomenon. The other dog had remained sleeping.

The "cigar" was sometimes partially obscured by clouds.

The third light that sped away from the "cigar" was surrounded by smaller, glowing puffs.

Weather forecast, according to the meteorologist at Axamo airfield in Jönköping:
Completely overcast. Sprinkling rain and cloud height at 300-400 meters. In other words; very bad weather.

Comments:
A very interesting case. The witness gave an honest and forthright impression, as documented by several people.

Early on, Boris Ljungkvist conversed with Margit Johansson on the phone. Sven-Olof Fredriksson made an "on-site survey" in the middle of December.

The results of the two surveys are almost identical.

According to Margit Johansson, there were several other observers. Unfortunately, these persons wish to remain anonymous.

It has not been possible to find any reasonable natural explanation for the phenomenon. Hence, we consider it to be a UFO.
