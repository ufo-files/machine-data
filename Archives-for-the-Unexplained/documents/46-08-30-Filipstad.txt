To the General Staff, Stockholm

I wish to make a report concerning a strange object passing through Filipstad on Aug 30 at 01.22 pm. I have not told the newspapers about it as I think it would be inappropriate in case the object would be one of the foreign missiles. The newspapers would not be able to report about it anyway or they would have to give false information. As regards the following I refer to the ordnance map.

I was in my home which is situated on the crossroads of Finshyttan-Hagfors/Storbron-Bosjön. Through a window I saw two objects approaching from the north over the mud- lake Storbrohyttan, heading south.

The objects looked like a cross of a grave. At the front there seemed to be a cross but without straight lines. The objekt seemed oval, then there was the cross and a long rear end. I did not see the objects separately at any time as they were flying abreast of each other. The thicker parts of the objects were clearly visible, though. Then the objects were passing side by side merging into one picture.

The objects looked dark but as I saw them towards white clouds I dare not say what colour they were.

It is also difficult for me to tell the altitude as I have never made estimates of this kind but I would guess that the altitude was abt 400-500 m.

If it would have crashed at the point where I last sighted it it would have ended up abt 500 m south of Storbrohyttan, close to the Chiller river.

I could not hear a sound. I ran out onto the balcony but it had already disappeared behind a black thundercloud coming at a low altitude from southwest. I still did not hear a sound. My son also came out at the same time and he did not hear anything either.

An ordinary aeroplane would most certainly have been heard by us as the distance must have been 1000 m at the maximum.

The speed was very low and I saw them very clearly with the white clouds as a background.

If there had not been a thunderstorm going on I would immediately have phoned the military forces in Karlstad and Kristinehamn to alert them, but the weather being as bad as it was I considered it useless.

The period of time during which I observed these objects was about 10-15 sec.

If you should already be aware of the above I ask you kindly to disregard my letter.

Filipstad Aug 7, 1946
Yours sincerely,


T Eriksson
