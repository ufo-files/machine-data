              AN ALL UNION CENTER FOR PSYCHOTHERAPY AND EXTRASENSORY METHODS
              RECEIVED ITS FIRST PATI2NTS YESTERDAY IN MOSCOW. DOCTOR EDUARD
              MIKHAYLOVICH LOZHKIN, HEAD OF THIS CENTER, RELATED THE FOLLOWING
              ABOUT ITS OBJECTIVES AND TASKS: OUR CENTER WAS CREATED AS A JOINT
              ENTERPRISE OF THE RENOWNED SCIENTIFIC PRODUCTION ASSOCIATION ROTOR
              IN CHERKASSY AND THE USSR ACADEMY OF SCIENCES' SCIENTIFIC TECHNICAL
              PRODUCTION ASSOCIATION SIMVOL. THE STATE COMMITTEE FOR SCIENCE AND
              TECHNOLOGY SUPPORTED THE IDEA OF CREATING THE ALL UNION CENTER. A
              SITE IN MOSCOW WAS SELECTED FOR ITS CHIEF ORGANIZATION, AND WE ARE
              RENTING SPACE FOR THIS BASE FACILITY.        OUR SPONSOR, THE ROTOR
              ASSOCIATION, CREATED THE CENTER'S INITIAL ASSETS, AND WE INTEND TO
              EXIST ON A SELF SUPPORTING BASIS IN THE FUTURE.

              WHAT WILL THE ROS CENTER BE ENGAGED IN?            IT IS A DIAGNOSTIC,
              THERAPEUTIC, PREVENTIVE MEDICAL, SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH, TRAINING AND
              SOCIOLOGICAL CENTER.     WE WILL THUS TREAT PRIMARILY ALCOHOLICS AND
              NARCOTICS ADDICTS, EMPLOYING METHODS OF HYPNOSIS AND PSYCHOTHERAPY
              WHICH WE HAVE DEVELOPED.        THESE METHODS CAN TENTATIVELY BE CALLED
              CODING, BY ANALOGY WITH TECHNIQUES AND PROCEDURES OF A. DOVZHENKO,
              ALTHOUGH OUR METHOD TAKES NOT SEVERAL HOURS BUT A FEW MINUTES, AND
              ITS EFFECTIVENESS IS, TO THE BEST OF MY KNOWLEDGE, UNPRECEDENTED;
              DURING THE LAST TWO AND A HALF YEARS, 640 OF A THOUSAND ALCOHOLICS
              WHO WERE TREATED GAVE UP DRINKING FOR GOOD. AMONG THE-METHODS OF
              PRODUCING    EFFECTS   WHICH WILL BE EMPLOYED AT OUR CENTER ARE
              PSYCHOTHERAPY,     EXTRASENSORY     METHODS,    HYPNOSIS,   THERAPEUTIC
              NUTRITION, MANY METHODS OF FOLK MEDICINE, AND ALSO WHAT MIGHT BE
              CALLED    CULTURAL   THERAPY,    THOUGHT THERAPY AND SOCIOPREVENTIVE
              MEDICINE.
