            Governments would like us to believe that UFOs are just some sort of
     hallucination, though they know well enough that the craft and occupants that landed
     at Roswell, at San Agustin and at Rendlesham are every bit as real as President
     Clinton or Prime Minister Major. For many years we could only guess what they were
     up to. Some people thought they were making a scientific survey of our world, and
     the Greys obligingly produced the sort of behaviour that would support the idea.
     They went in for what I call "prospecting", collecting samples of animals, plants and
     even soil to give the impression that they were strangers from distant galaxies
     visiting new worlds.
            Ufologists call this view of them the ETH - Extra-Terrestrial Hypothesis - but
     all that most people ever saw of the visitors were LITS - Lights In The Sky, and it
     was quite a long time before anyone began to suspect that the UFO Phenomenon,
     as we call the intelligences directing the operations of these alien entities, was not
     here just to satisfy its scientific curiosity. For a long time we were into position to
     realise what a carefully devised operational plan the Phenomenon was working out
     in the open skies of our vulnerable world.
            lt was not until the ufonauts stopped pretending to be interested in taking
     plant and soil samples and started taking people instead, . that we began to get
     reports from some of those unfortunate enough to have been abducted. There were
     and still are many who never tell what is happening to them, afraid to speak in case
     they are disbelieved and ridiculed. In the end, however, thanks to the work of
     researchers such as Budd Hopkins and. Professor David Jacobs , the whole
     incredible story has begun to emerge.
            The Phenomenon, as we shall· see, was very skilful in its exploitation of
     humanity and was able to make sure that for many years no one really knew what
     was going on. Fear of ridicule was cynically used to prevent victims finding help, or
     speaking out even anonymously about the nasty things that were being done to
     them and the kind of beings which were doing them.

     were able to manipulate people and even governments in such a way as to prevent
     them finding out what they were doing, or guessing what they would do next. The
     Phenomenon      knew that its activities could not be for ever hidden from humanity,

                                   ANTIPOPE           ...   by Kevin McClure

Nick Pope is the chap who answered the phone to UFO spotters at the Ministry of Defence. While Nick may possibly
know all sorts of fascinating things about substantial and unexplained reports of unidentified aerial phenomena, he
clearly has little understanding of the potential hoax content of, in particular, the Roswell Autopsy and crop circles.
I guess these misconceptions may derive from some of the people he's been mixing with. Anyway, you may recall
that on the cover of Open Skies, Closed Minds, he compares his standing in the MOD with that of a ranking army
Major. An earlier version of this claim was made in The Cropwatcher, in which Pope stated that, "I held the rank
of Executive Officer when in Sec(AS)2a; this civil service rank equates to that of an Army Captain. I am now a
Higher Executive Officer, which equates to the rank of Major."


As an Executive Officer (EO) myself, in the HQ of another department, I found this an intriguing proposition,
because after more than twenty years in the EO grade, on the maximum of the ordinary pay scale, and with some
additions for good performance, I earn around £16,000 a year. When I joined the civil service, the entry
qualifications for the EO grade were two 'A' levels of any description, and I don't think that has changed much since.
If you get fed up at your local social security office, or Jobcentre, and demand to see the supervisor, that will be an
EO. It's a job where you need to be honest, accurate and technically sound, but it's nothing special in the great
scheme of things. Higher Executive Officer is the next step up, and is a standard civil service 'middle management'
grade.


The comparison with Army ranks suggested by Mr. Pope did not ring true to me. I had this impression that a Captain
could well, in combat, be responsible for the lives and deaths of a substantial number of men. A Major even more
so. Using the straightforward investigative technique of finding out the facts, I compared the pay scales of the two
civil service jobs with their supposed Army counterparts. This was enlightening.


         Executive Officer            between£11,433 and£16,286
         Army Captain                 between£23,668 and£27,521


          Higher Executive Officer between£15,463 and£21,491
         Army Major                   between£30,054 and£36,010


In addition, Army officers receive subsidies for food and accommodation, and various allowances. Civil servants
seldom receive any addition except London Weighting. The differences in income are actually greater than the
figures suggest. The differences in respon·sibility are as great.


Continuing my investigation- actually having a chat with the Sergeant in the local Forces Information Office - I
found that probably the only way in which civil service grades equate with Army ranks as Mr Pope has suggested
is in the privileges given to civil servants if they visit an Army base. Where they eat - the Offcers' Mess - and where
they sleep. Otherwise, I suspect that they do not equate at all, and that Mr Pope's comments might possibly be
regarded as misleading.


If the Government has entrusted responsibility for the conduct of its information-gathering, assessment and public
relations regarding UFOs to a mere EO, then you can be pretty sure of one of two things. Either it has secrets to
protect, and has placed in the job someone who has no idea what they are, and whose ignorance is useful in
protecting those secrets. Or - and this is far more likely - the Government has long since decided that UFOs have
no defence or other significance, and decided to fill its 'UFO liaison' job as cheaply, and at as low a grade, as would
be consistent with the rudiments of providing a service to its customers. Either way, Mr Pope seems to have been
less than fulsome in describing the level of his status and authority, and it is easy to be reminded of prominent figures
in the UFO field who claimed to have authority to make great revelations, but have recently been shown to be prone
to exaggeration verging on deceit. Perhaps Mr Pope's reticence in conveying his real status should be considered
in assessing the significance of anything else he has to say.

        When colonists arrived in North America they fel l first upon their knees and then upon

was eventually all but destroyed, though efforts to preserve the knowledge handed down by word
of mouth from time immemorial were not without success .


        Robert Morning Sky was told by his grandfather that it was now time for the ancient lore.
of his people to be proclaimed abroad, and promised to do so . S ince then he has travelled the
world, telling his story wherever people will listen - on radio, television and in lecture halls. His
book Terra Papers: Hidden History of Planet Earth has also been widely read.


        There is, however, opposition to his activities, for his office has frequently been broken
into to steal files and computer equipment. His travel trailer has been vandalised and even his life
has been threatened. What is he saying to provoke such reactions?


        He claims that our galaxy is inhabited by billions of Star Beings, humanoid races for the
most part, who have been in perpetual conflict for millions of years . Earth, a coloni al outpost of
one such empire, had been virtually destroyed in the struggle, but eventually the Sirians decided
to rebuild the ravaged world, which they called Eridu, to tap once again its valued resources.


        King An sent his son Ea and his daughter Ninhursag, both genetic scientists, who restored
the atmosphere, refilled the seas with life, re-created plants, trees and flowers and also produced
many hybrid creatures. Readers familiar with the translations from the cuneiform writings of the
S umerians and Akkadians brought to light by the scho larship of Zecharia S itchin will not fai l to
note the detailed correspondences between the legends of two cultures so widely separated in
time and space. Both state that the race of Mankind was also created, mingling the DNA of the
Star Beings with that of a hominid ape to produce intelligent and adaptable creatures to serve as
slaves, though capable and deserving of freedom.


        The American account goes on to describe how a lizard race of genetically engineered
workers dwelling underground revolted and seized power, driving away the Star Beings and
using mind control techniques to control and alter the memories of star-born Mankind, thus
rendering them ignorant and docile.


        Robert Morning Sky believes us to be still under the malign rule of this reptilian species,
who use electronic means as well as drugs to maintain the hegemony they established so long
ago . He calls upon humanity to develop the spiritual powers which alone can break the cultural
dominance of the repti lian manipulators, and believes that crop circles are exhortations from
extraterrestrial all ies to Star-born humanity to resist the imposition of a new world order and
claim instead its rightful heritage of freedom.
