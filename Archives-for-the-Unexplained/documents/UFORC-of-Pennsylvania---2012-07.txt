YOUR WITNESS SHOWS YOU A PICTURE OF
              A “UFO”

Did you know that from the beginning of the first ever sighting of a
“UFO”, people around the world have been trying to fraudulently
reproduce a photograph of a “UFO”? Thanks to recent technological
breakthroughs most of these photographs are exposed for what they
are “Fake”, and still today we find people out there continuing this
venture for many different reasons. The main reason a person would
take up this as a hobby usually revolves around “Self Importance” or
“Notoriety” and Fortune and Glory. It becomes evident over time
there may be physiological or sociological problems with the
individual; however, they usually take things to the extreme for
“Notoriety” and if they think they have achieved their goal, they will
continue to attempt to deceive “you” the researcher.

NOTE: WHEN WE RECIEVE A REPORT, THE VAST MAJORITY OF
THE TIME THE PHOTO HAS BEEN REVIEWED FOR ITS
CONTENT OR AUTHENTISITY. WE HAVE AT OUR DISPOSAL
PROGRAMS SUCH AS PHOTO EXPLOSION DELUXE, ADOBE
PHOTOSHOP CSH EXTENDED PROFESSIONAL EDDITION,
MICROSOFT PICTURE IT, PASSPORT AND OTHER
PHOTOGRAPH ENHANCEMENT SOFTWARE THAT WE
TYPICALLY USE. SHOULD YOU ENCOUNTER A PHOTOGRAPH
DURING YOUR RESERACH AND YOU NEED ASSISTANCE TO
SCRUTINIZE IT’S AUTHENTICITY, DO NOT HESITATE TO EMAIL
US.

Most UFO pictures that are seen around the web are fraudulent.
Today we live in a world where people who are empowered by the
personal computer can easily create fake UFO photographs at will.
Many UFO pictures are faked simply by knowing one’s way around a
graphic art program, and the more skilled a graphic artist is, the
harder it is to determine if the UFO picture is authentic or fake.
With a little time on one’s hands and some patience, a person can
create a compelling argument for the existence of UFOs in less than
one hour.

The UFO craze created a myriad of hubcap throwers, who looked to
capture just the right aerial image to show their friends. Some UFO
picture creators even tossed trash can lid’s, cereal bowls, and hats
and their own UFO models against scenery that would make it
difficult to judge the object’s size. The sky afforded the best UFO
picture background, as no object for size or comparison would be
photographed.

Some UFO pictures were faked by painting an image (or pasting an
image) onto a photograph of scenery and then re-photographing
close-up the entire compilation again. The effect seemed to blend
the image of the UFO into the picture nicely.

In the modern age, UFOs can be created easily as simple light
anomalies within digital photos, or even crafted into glorious ships by
the same photo editing or art program. If one wants to fake a UFO
picture that is even more convincing, then building a realistic looking
UFO model and photographing it against a green screen background
works best. The UFO model can easily be cropped out of the photo
and pasted onto a photo of scenery quite easily, all through a
computer program.

Fake UFO photos can be easy to detect as most creators forget to
account for the direction of sunlight in the background photo, in
comparison to the shadows cast upon the fake UFO itself. Also look
for fake UFO pictures that appear “constructed” and not a naturally
created photograph. Misaligned pixels are another hint. Just imagine
the shocked, amateur photographer trying to photograph an elusive
UFO that is moving and possibly not trying to be seen. That would
lend one to think that the UFO should commonly be blurred to some
degree.

In this digital age we live in it is well to remember, as the software
programs become more sophisticated and user friendly, more people
will attempt to fool the researcher. You should also remember what is
good for the goose is also good for the gander. Those same software
photo programs can also be used to detect a fraudulently contrived
UFO photograph that is meant to fool you.

PARANORMAL TIPS

Always plan you investigation in advance and research the
background of the site you wish to investigate before hand.
Keep a log of equipment used at the site, as it will also come
in handy to make sure you didn’t overlooked a piece of
equipment and left the site without it.

Remove batteries from equipment you don’t use often as an
old and leaking battery can destroy a valuable piece of
equipment. Test and replace bad batteries monthly. Note:
Re-chargeable batteries are not the best for longevity in the
field.

On June 24, 1947, while flying near Mt. Rainer, Arnold claimed to have seen nine
unusual objects in the skies.He also claimed to have seen UFOs on several other
occasions afterwards, as well.
Arnold originally described the objects' shape as "flat like a pie pan", "shaped like
a pie plate", "half-moon shaped, oval in front and convex in the rear", "something
like a pie plate that was cut in half with a sort of a convex triangle in the rear", or
simply "saucer-like" or "like a big flat disk", and also described their erratic motion
being "like a fish flipping in the sun" or a saucer skipped across water. From
these, the press quickly coined the new terms "flying saucer” and "flying disc" to
describe such objects, many of which were reported within days after Arnold's
sighting.
Later Arnold would add that one of the objects actually resembled a crescent or
flying wing (see image above).The U.S. Air Force formally listed the Arnold case
as a mirage; this is one of many explanations that have been disputed by critics,
and researchers. There has never been an entirely persuasive conventional
explanation of the Arnold sighting.
