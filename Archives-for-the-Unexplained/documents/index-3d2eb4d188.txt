Out-of-Body Adventures: 30 Days to the Most Exciting Experience of your Life

                                                                                                               Co-Creator of the Seth Books

  As amazing as it sounds, you can learn to leave your body!

  This simple, foolproof program will show you how to walk through walls, fly around your neighborhood, travel through
  time, and experience the wonder of being in other dimentions. With Out-of-Body Adventures, just about anybody can
  have an out-of-body experience within 30 days.

  Rick Stack, a teacher of metaphysics who has helped hundred of students achieve their first out-of-body experiences,
  begins with a fresh look at the metaphysics involved and walks you through the attitude adjustment and dream work that
  promote successful astral travel. He the presents proven, step-by-step techniques for "getting out" that even beginners
  have used with ease. Descriptions of the out-of-body phenomenon from both scientific and personal perspectives help
  you to overcome fear and to know what to expect from your voyage. Chapters on what to do once you're out -- including
  meeting friends, contacting reincarnational selves, having astral sex, and communicating with guides and teachers --
  open the door to the fun and value of this ancient human art form.

  With Out-of-Body Adventures, you can experience the exhileration of watching yourself float right out of your body in
  your own bedroom, something few people can do at will -- until now. With these remarkable techniques, any traveler
  willing to take the journey can begin to explore the true nature of our complex universe.

  Rick Stack, author and educator, teaches astral travel and metaphysics in the United States and abroad. He was friend
  and student of the late Jane Roberts, author of the Seth books.
