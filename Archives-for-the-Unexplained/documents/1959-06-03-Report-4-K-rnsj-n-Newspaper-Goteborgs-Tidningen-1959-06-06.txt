GT 6/6 – 1959

Sputnik casing fell down into lake, causing high water cascades

UDDEVALLA (GT)
- Though most certainly not a flying saucer, the object did look like a sputnik casing or a military robot, said four fully credible people who acted as witnesses to a remarkable event at Fisketorp, which is located about 1 mil north of Munkedal. A space ship of some kind came roaring through the air and fell into Kärnsjön [lake – Translator’s note], where the impact induced high water cascades.

Several policemen have conducted investigations at the site and the military has also been introduced to the case.

The object very clearly had the shape of a flying saucer in miniature. Data on its size varies slightly. Some believe that the object was 1.5-2 meters long, while the man who witnessed the impact at the lake estimated its length to approximately 1 meter.

- Five people, including four adults, had a clear view of the space object, says a tenant by the name of Mats Nilsson, resident of Fisketorp. I was standing in a field right next to the lake along with my 5 year old son Jan-Åke. At the time, three road construction workers (29 -year-old excavator operator Finn Högborg , my own brother Bengt Nilsson and Lennart Torstensson) were eating breakfast some 100 meters from our location.

The object fell into Kärnsjön on Wednesday, specifically during 9:15 to 9:20 o’clock.

- We solemnly swear we saw a moving object, but we can not decisively determine what it was, the eyewitnesses claim. The answer to this riddle lies on the bottom of the lake and a group of military experts should be able to locate the object with ease. Verily, we are all perfectly able to define exactly where the object had fallen.

The water depth is about 5 meters and there is, in other words, a good chance of finding this “robotic” object. The lake bottom consists of rock and clay.

- I was out in a field in order to arrange the fence, says an eye-witness by the name of Mats Nilsson.
I heard a roaring sound in the air and at first I expected to see one of those jets come sweeping by. Some trees obscured the view in the direction where the sound had come from, but suddenly I saw an object of indeterminate colour come rushing down towards the lake. The object came from the south, ie from Uddevalla or Gothenburg.

- I think it was a military object, the tenant states.
