GHT 8/8-46  (fp)  (UD)

August 8
 
Peaceconference with sounds of armour

According to the military staff our country has probably been used as a shooting-field by a friendly foreign unknown country for testing of V rockets. This is for the time being just an assumtion though nothing has been clearly proved. The assumtion however has been made after a number of serious investigations and is very probable. The observations have indeed been vague but on the other hand to many to be imaginative. The flying objects have to be considered real. The authorities are inclined to identify them as rockets of the type the germans called V-1. Contrary to the V-2 rockets the V-1 rockets travel at low altidude and with a speed that makes them possible to detect with the eye. During the war we had  numerous occasions similiar to these events. Just like now the bombs came from south. Some landed on swedish territory, though it was unintentional. The pieces were wisely picked up and sent to the wright address, which wasn´t the senders intention. Generally it was regarded that the launching-base was located on Bornholm, an island that after the german surrender was surprisingly occupated by the russians. Later the radar-station was located to Peenemunde on Usedom, were a swedish force one time made a landing somewhat smaller than the Normandie-landing. The facilities on Bornholm should have fallen undamaged into Russian hands. So far the shooting-exercises have caused no damage in Sweden. The bombs have probably not warheads, and therefor they are part of an exercise. But they are not entirely without danger. An impact on settled ground or an airplane collision could cause severe damage and are not unthinkable though the exercises seem to be at large scale. Naturally these events have been noticed both in Sweden and abroad, especially in USA. In the USA they have been interpreted as advanced diplomacy to show us our exposed situation in case of a military conflict but that´s just speculations. Maybe there is some kind of demonstration but in that case not directly to the swedish public. The russian comments to the nuclearbomb experiments in the Pacific were that this was a unapropriate demonstration of military means with a political purpose. These things are known to happen you show your new weapons to scare off the opponents. Demonstrations of fleets are most common when you show your diplomatic wishes and intents. Atomicbombs and rocket-bombs are also used. The world will have to get used to this kind of diplomatic talk. This kind of operations should not take place to close to other countries borders though no country can indifferent watch their territory being used as a shooting-field for rockets. Internationally, visits from alien naval-forces are noticed in appearing order. Alien air-forces are noticed in the same way though they are not so common so far. To use the territory of a country for military-operations without any kind of warning is not only inpolite it is a violation of the code between countries. So far this is just a hypothetical discusion but in the same moment it is determined that the country has been used as a shooting-field by a foreign government the foreign office has to find out were the bombs come from and stop them. It is one thing if the superpowers wants to show their military strength at the piece-conference but if they violate other countries borders frequently while doing it the matter becomes very serious. We want full clarity in this matter and the military investigations should continue and if possible end satisfactory. The investigation will show if further measures are necessary.
