      The Big Darby Creek is perhaps 75 yards from the nearest houses. Most
of the Bigfoot sightings have been made either from the private road or
from the extended lawn ( see crude map) , into the wopds beyond the creek.
Apparently Bigfoot doesn't dare come up to the houses except under cover
of night. However, without the use of binoculars, it is hard to make out
what one is seeing in the dense woods, even in the daytime; and there is
little doubt that many of the· sightings have been aided by a fertile im­
agination and a driving "will to Believe".
      Such a "will to believe" would probably account for a number of Big­
foot sightings made right in front of your editor on the evening of July
17th, at a Full Moon Bigfoot fish-fry & lawn party. Your not-too-keenly
sighted editor stood by helplessly while various ladies in the crowd en­
deavored to point out Bigfoot to him, at various places in the nearby
woods. They could see him; he could not. But when he insisted on going
into the woods to take a closer look ( and still saw nothing) , the ladies
chose to stay behind, on the lawn.
      However, many of the weird ev�n�s occuring around the community can­

 of peculiar sounds in the night.

 old son Grover Jr. were on the porch of their home when they saw a row

 nearby woods, less than 100 yards away.     The lights were at tree- top lev­

 could still be seen through the trees, but after about five minutes,      the
 lights went out.    Unfortunately Betty was unwilling or unable to approach
 the object, so a golden opportunity was lost!     And, even more unfortunate­
 ly, your editor had left the Powell home just a couple of days earlier,
 though he returned that same night.

       Ezra Benz, one of our top-secret staff correspondents, has sent us a
 synopsis of the 12th annual MUFON UFO symposium, which he attended. There
 were over 400 paying fans present, at $25 to $45 per head, and over    240
 copies of last year's MUFON Proceedings were sold at $10 per copy. No fi­
 nancial loss here!
       At the press conference, MUFON czar Walt Andrus pointed out that 15
 years ago M.I. T. declined a U.S. Air Force offer to conduct a study   on
 UFOs, but now, significantly, they allow a UFO symposium to take place on
 their premises; so, some progress has been made:
       Dr. Barry Downing, one of the dozen or more speakers, discussed re­
 ligion and UFOs. He called attention to.the millions of Christians who be­
 lieve in dead Bible witnesses but refuse to believe the thousands of live
 UFO witnesses. He pointed out that conservative Christians believe in an­
 gels that would sit down and eat with them, but not those chased by U. S.
 Air Force jets.
       Benz felt that the most important lecture was given by non-subscriber
 Dr. J. Allen Hynek, who made a plea for Ufology to become a profession with
·accepted standards of action and behavior. Hynek stated: "Ufology is to­
 day what chemistry was when it was alchemy - a grand assortment of super­
 stitions, . beliefs, wishful thinking, etc. But eventually the science - the
 profession - of chemistry evolved out of the alchemical mess."
       Hynek went on to say: "Ufology must become a profession,   and   the
 start should be made soon; for unless this happens, we face another several
 decades of buffoonery, ridicule, and idle prattle about sightings and use­
 less bickering and unproductive competition among the non-professional dab­
 blers in the field.   • " Right On, Doc:
