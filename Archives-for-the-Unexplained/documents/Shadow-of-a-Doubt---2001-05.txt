   Understanding Belief
    How we know what isn't so
         May 19 and 20 - Winchester, VA
         Why do people believe and perceive what they do? How does
         science "know" what is or isn't? What perpetuates beliefs that
         have no basis in fact? What can and should you do?
Join NCAS in scenic Winchester, Virginia on May 19th and 20th as we explore these issues
and more in our annual workshop. From 8:30 am on the 19th until noon on the 20th we'll
examine how people arrive at their belief systems. An optional dinner and social event will be
planned for Saturday evening.

                                     Happy Birthday CSICOP
On May 1, 2001 CSICOP turned 25. As part of this celebration, Prometheus Books is
publishing Skeptical Odysseys: Personal accounts by the world's leading paranormal
inquirers. It contains personal ventures into the world of skepticism by many of the leading
skeptics today. It will be available August 1, 2001 for $27(ISBN: 1-57392-884-4).
