    12 cubic feet.

    Access limited to Alaska and Polar Regions Collections research room hours.

    The bulk of the Franklin E. Roach Papers consists of his notes, drafts, charts, correspondence, photographs,
    and publications generated from his research in upper-atmospheric physics. The materials also include
    Roach's lecture notes, his correspondence files from his work on unidentified flying objects, and some
    biographical papers.

    Permission for commercial use must be obtained from the Archivist.

    Franklin Evans Roach contributed greatly to the field of upper-atmosphere physics, conducting and
    publishing many photometric studies of airglow, zodiacal light, and diffuse galactic light.

    Finding aid available in research room; box level control.
