cle which we had overlooked till now.  Entitled "Alias Carlos Allende",    it
finally gets to the bottom of the controversy surrounding the mysterious man
who allegedly witnessed the "Philadelphia Experiment" in 194J.   The gist   of
it all is that Carl Allen, alias Carlos Allende, is an eccentric wanderer
and bullshit artist  whose family still live in New Kensington, Pa.   The au­
thor of the article, named Robert Goerman, interviewed Allende's father, who
is still alive, and obtained a mountain of documents showing that Carl    not
only "annotated" Morris K. Jessup's book "The Case for the UFO" (using three
names and three supposedly different handwritings) , but also has the habit
of annotating just about anything he can get his hands on!
      Of course, long-time readers of our newsletter know that the original
expose of Allende appeared in the Sept. lOth, 1977 issue of "Saucer   Stews",
which was published shortly after your editor met the mysterious wanderer in
Clarksburg, West Virginia, together with former UFO personality Gray Barker.
Our impressions of Allende as published in 1977, though not backed up by the
documents and family interviews that Goerman obtained, nevertheless   are al­
most identical.  For example, Goerman states that Allende claims that   his
confusing annotations  make him co-author of "The Case for the UFO", which
is the � claim he made in a Clarksburg newspaper interview - which we re­
produced in the above-mentioned issue of "Saucer Stews".
