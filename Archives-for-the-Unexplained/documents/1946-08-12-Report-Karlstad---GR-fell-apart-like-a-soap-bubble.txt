Ghost rocket fell apart 
like a soap-bubble 

Time: August 12, about 15.00
Place: Karlstad

”GHOST BOMB THEORY FELL APART LIKE A SOAP-BUBBLE. No less than eight agitated voices, on the telephone at the VF editorial office, declared  at about 15.00 hours yesterday afternoon, that several trustworthy persons had seen ghost bombs moving in a northerly direction over Karlstad at an altitude of a few hundred meters. De were relatively small and clearly luminous. After a detailed investigation VF can report that the ghost projectiles were soap bubbles made by a little boy who sat on a sofa on Kungsgatan with a newly bought bubble apparatus and a can of soap-water.” (Värmlands Folkblad, August 13). 

Status: Soap bubbles
Source: Värmlands Folkblad, Aug. 13; Expressen, Aug. 13; Morgon Tidningen, Aug. 17; Östergötlands Folkblad, Aug. 23.
