                                                                  Explanation of Ratings

  Although it doesn't say so in the title, this is essentially an update on the Roswell crash. About a third of
  the book is a direct defense of Randle's Roswell position. The rest is a review of official UFO
  investigations since 1947 -- as seen in light of the Roswell Crash. Although a lot of this material has been
  written about before, Randle adds some new information that only he has access to, like early interviews
  with General Arthur Exon which seems to verify the existence of secret UFO investigations. There seems
  to be a lot of good material here, worth the price at least. Investigative reporter Jack Anderson wrote the
  foreword, saying the Air Force has not been honest about its UFO investigations. -- Glenn Campbell
