July 7: Another Vampire airshow

8.000 pair of eyes scanned the skies at the Orebro airshow, not for ghost rockets, but for more wellknown things, such as the new air force Vampire jet. ”The audience kept their breaths” as it soared by. ”The Vampire was nothing but a sensation. One have heard about it as something really special but it was only when the speaker announced its arrival, and, moments later, it swept by and disappeared in the clouds, that one understood that the talk of it this plane initiated a new era in Swedish aviation is an understatement.”

Sources: Nerikes Allehanda, July 5 & 8.
