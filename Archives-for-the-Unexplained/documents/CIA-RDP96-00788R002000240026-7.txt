         (S/ORCON) To establish a program using psychoenergics for
         intelligence applications. Specifically, utilizing that
         field ·of psychoener'gics referred to as REMOTE VIEWING. The
         program encompasses the following:

              - Establishing a training program in REMOTE VIEWING
                utilizing selected INSCOM personnel.

              - Establishing procedures for intelligence collection
                techniques utilizing REMOTE VIEWING.

              - Establishing a mechanism for responding to intelligence
                collection· requirements (tasking) using REMOTE~YIEWING.
