 THE ALBANY,   NEW YORK VIDEO
      Brandon Mowry, a news photographer for Albany's Fox TV affiliate,
 was at the Albany Airport taking routine background footage for the
 evening news late in the afternoon on October 20, 2002 when the image
 shown above showed up on his video footage.
      The "rod" or missle-like image was only visible for less than a
 second, and was moving toward the left of the frame just as a landing
 airliner was moving toward the right side of the frame.  The airliner can
 be seen in the upper right side corner of the still enhanced above.
      Thinking at first that the image may have been a missile fired at
 the plane,   the footage received much coverage over the entire Fox
 network.  However, it became clear that it indeed wasn't a missile, and
 soon people were speculating that it might be a UFO that Mowry captured
 on video that day.
      Rick and I both saw the footage several times on our local Fox
 affiliate, and Rick believes that the image is actually a distorted lens
 refraction of the passing airliner since it was moving in the opposite
 direction. However, it would be nice if someone experienced in video and
 photo analysis could examine the footage.    Maybe then we will actually
 know if it's a "UFO" or an "IFO. "
