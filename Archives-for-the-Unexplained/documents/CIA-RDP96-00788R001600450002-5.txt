    SUBJECT:        CENTER LANE FY 86 - 90 GDIP Submission (U)



    1.    ( S/CL-2/NOFORN)            BACKGROUND.
         Since the beginning of USAINSCOM's exploitation of
    psychoenergetics (PE) technology with Project GRILL FLAME in
    1977 through the present effort under CENTER LANE, the project
    has been conducted on an ad hoc basis, using resources taken
    "out of hide."    Despite the provisional approach, project
    results have been very impressive.      State-of-the-art in
    psychoenergetics has advanced so far, however, that the current
    organizational approach is no longer adequate to derive the                                           })
    maximum benefit and intelligence potential from available
    technology. In recognition of this, directives received in the
    General Defense Intelligence Program ( GDIP) Program Manager's
    Guidance Memorandum, Fisc~l Years 1986-1990, Substantive
    Guidance document require the provision of "unranked packages to
    fund the operational use of CENTER LANE capabilities" (para 5i,
    TAB A).

    2.    (S/CL-2/NOFORN)      DISCUSSION.

         Attached at TAB B is a revised, proposed CENTER LANE
    organization that has an end-of-cycle personnel strength of 20.
    The organization is designed to continue current PE training,
    development, and collection efforts, and establish a base for
    INSCOM PE activities beyond FY 90.

    3.    ( U)   RECOMMENDATION.

        a.   ( S/CL-3/NOFORN) Recommend that CG, USAINSCOM approve
    the proposed CENTER LANE organization and GDIP New Initiative at
    TAB C.

        b.   ( S I CL-3/ NOFORN)   The compartmented nature of the
    information included in the justification document requires the
    initiative to be submitted separately from INSCOM's standard
    GDIP submission.       Upon approval of the concept, the proposed
    CENTER LANE GDIP packet will be transmitted directly to
    DAMI-ISH, ACSI DA for action.

        c.   (S/CL-3/NOFORN)  Submission of the CENTER LANE
    initiative by HQ, USAINSCOM in the Planning, Programming, and
    Budget process implies an agreement by the command that if the
    initiative is approved at the DA and DoD level, USAINSCOM is
    prepared to implement the program at the beginning of FY 86.
    This means that there will be a requirement to implement a
    CENTER LANE organization with 12 persons on 1 October 1985.
    Analysis of current and projected CENTER LANE resources through
    FY 85 indicates that the project will have a minimum of 10
    personnel assigned by the end of FY 85; therefore, the
    activation of an INSCOM PE activity at the beginning of FY 86
    should not create an unmanageable manpower requirement for
    INSCOM.
