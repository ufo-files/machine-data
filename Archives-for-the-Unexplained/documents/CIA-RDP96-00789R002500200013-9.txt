          (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)     Following are the perceived highlights
     from each source.

             (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)    Source A:
             -- The target site has a big, tall, and thin almost
     pyramid type shaped structure. It is off-white in color and
     situated on open grass land. Lights are present. Black asphalt
     and concrete can be found around the base as walkways for people
     (enclosure Ja)

                There is a small body of water nearby where there are
     swans or ducks swimming (enclosure Jb). This site is
     recreational for people and yet is a historical landmark. The
     target is located in the cultural seat of the city, which has
     fine architecture and well kept vegetation. This site is
     entertaining for people in that they come to see, visit, and tour
     a historical heritage and the things erected in people's honor.
     Food and entertainment are available in the area. Colored
     fountains or fireworks are at times displayed around the site.
     The target site draws large numbers of people and tourists.

             (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)    Source B:

             -- The target site consists of a fenced-in enclosure with
     one side partially obscured. There are areas which are overgrown
     and uncleared of vegetation reminiscent of weeds and vines.
     There is stuff piled up throughout the area. The target feels
     seemingly unkept.

             -- There are concentrations of rusty metal, boxes with
     one end open, grating and a sharp, piercing smell. Concepts
     associated with the target are: heat; smoke; spinning water;
     movement like treaded vehicles; parallel marks reminiscent of
     scrape marks in the sides of a large, squarish hole in the
     ground; rubber and series of uniform "humps" (enclosure Jc).

        (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)   Source C:

         -- I sensed that when the beacon person left his
destination, he made a sharp turn right (enclosures 3f and g).
At first I could see a large white decorative object (enclosure
3h).  I had to look up to see this and I don't think that this
object was sitting on the ground.   It may be sitting on a base or
foundation of some type. When I kept looking at this object, it
seemed that the bottom was similar to the top. This object may
be reflecting itself, maybe from water (enclosure 3i). This
object was outside.   I also saw a plane flying in the air and
then later on during the session I had the feeling of a lot of
planes (enclosure 3j).   (Beacon person could have been near an
airport) .

        -- The site was a large flat area or square that was man-
made. This flat square was white or beige in color and it was
outside (enclosure 3k). This area reminded me of an historical
site and it may have been set in a park type setting. There was
a large object sitting in this area that looked like a statue of
a man riding a horse. This statue was dark in color. To the
side of this flat monument was a very large square structure that
was quite colorful.

        -- The large square structure reminded me of a Gr~ek
building (enclosure 31). This structure could have been built
based on the Greek history.

        -- I then felt that I was inside a very large building
and the theme was history and art.  It was very colorful inside
and I could see a.lot of gold.  It was very pleasant and one can
come here to enjoy themselves.  (The art and history looked like
Egyptian) .

        (S/NF/SG/LIMDIS)   Control Person:

        -- The target is a house or condo. there seems to be a
room, a bedroom with pretty flowered wallpaper in pinks and rose
color. The ceiling has a ceiling fan or ornate fixture.   There
are shranks or wardrobes in different rooms and hallways. The
stair case goes downward and splits. People tour this building;
however, it is still a home. There are a lot of trees around as
you approach the target (enclosure 3m).
