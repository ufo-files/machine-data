                                                                         22 October 1979


       MEMORANDUM FOR MEMBERS OF GRILL FLAME SCIENTIFIC
                                 EVALUATION COMMITTEE

       SUBJECT: Scheduled Activities




       1. We have finally succeeded in obtaining access to information that we
       have previously sought. and I have made arrangements to hold discussions
       in place. We also are planning for some members to visit with Dr. Hawk
       at Lawrence-Livermore Laboratories, and I think it is essential to have
       one concluding executive session in Washington prior to wrapping up the
       final report.

       2.   The future scheduled activities are as follows:

            a. Visit to Wright-Patterson AFB near Dayton, Ohio early am 1 Nov
       to approximately 5:00 pm. (We are trying to arrange military aircraft
       for those in the Washington area. )

           b. Proceed by commercial air on the evening of 1 Nov from Dayton
       to Orlando, Florida; approximately 10:00 pm arrival. (Will transmit flight
       schedule and suggested overnight accommodations at a later time.)
SG1A


       3. A visit has been scheduled with Dr. Hawk of Lawrence-Livermore
       Laboratories for 6 November, for those interested to attend.

       4. There will be an executive session at the Pentagon, Room 3E385, 9 Nov
       79, 9:00 am to 2:00 pm, in order to reach a Committee consensus on the
       findings and recommendations for the final report. It is important to have
       this meeting and I would appreciate your attendance.

       5. I invite as many of you as possible to participate in the above activities
       and especially in the meeting on 9 Nov. Jeannie will be in touch with you
       to ascertain your plans and to help you with any arrangements which you may
       need.
