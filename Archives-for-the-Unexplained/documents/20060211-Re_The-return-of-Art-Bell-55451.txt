   I think you are confused or have a stereotype of some Hollywood
   movie. In the real world, the typical kind of people that go into
   science are those who have a basic irrestiable desire to figure out
   how things work. Those who are stuck in the mud or refuse to learn
   new things quickly become left behind. Technology moves forward.


           One negative of such idealization is that it stymies fresh ideas and
           the reevaluation of old ones. It leads to bullying of the bright and
           imaginative. If carried to an extreme it can turn science into
           oppression and stagnation. Old thread bare theories can, moreover, be
           used by the intellectually insufficient as clubs to beat newcomers into
           submission and intellectual death.


   I think you are talking about religion. In science and technology,
   stagnation is not happening. We see new stuff every day. Just look
   at how far computer chips have come in the past few decades. Just
   look at where they have gone in just the past 18 months with multi-core
   chips going from concept to being common. It is religion that is still
   stuck in the 8th century arguing whether of not the earth is flat.


           Yes, science is rigorous, but must include creative thought, open
           minds, and new approaches. Or else we might as well be done with it
           and begin all over again. To say, for example, that other dimensions
           don't exist because I never saw one, is not rigorous at all. Current
           physics theories and psychic evidence both point to 'other worlds'
           beyond the one we see.


   As I have argued above, science is creativity. That is where you
   find the open minds and new approaches. If that were not true, we
   would still be searching for the light bulb and wondering if N-rays
   were real or not.
   I don't think very many people in science are opposed to legitimate
   research into multiple dimensions. The string theory pretty much
   demands that other dimensions exist. What I am opposed to are people
   making up stories about other dimensions that have no basis in reality.
   Some folks have made up entire alternate universes around dimensions
   complete with being from other dimensions and gateways where these
   beings move around. All that is bunk. There is no data to suggest
   that any of it is true or is even possible. What these people are
   doing is building a religion. If they want to worship something that
   is not true, then at least put it in those terms. Don't confuse
   this bunk with legitimate science.
