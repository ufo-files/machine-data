Report on UFO searches in Kamlunge (mire) on July 25-26, 1946

Location: 200 metres South of Bergnäs (village)

Staff: Lieutenant Kestner, plus a diver named Pettersson, along with his attendants.

Elapsed time: 	         13:00 AM - 18:00 PM (July 25).
         8:00 AM - 16:00 AM (July 26).

Eyewitnesses pointed to an area approximately 200 x 300 metres in size and the site was searched thoroughly by Pettersson. Bottom conditions: ½ - 1 foot quite hard clay. Clear water with good visibility. Maximum water depth: 15 metres. The diver found no trace of any foreign objects. Bottom surface smooth and even, every small individual object could be seen.
On the 23rd of July, Captain Dimander (fo 67) went along with Lieutenant Kestner and several others to inspect the site and interview a small number of eyewitnesses, who stated the following:
Ms. Astrid Lindbäck had heard a whistling sound and seen a water cascade, 3-4 metres in size. Though, she had not seen any objects fall into the water.
At the same time, Mrs. Anna Rönnbäck sat in a boat along with three other people, who all came to observe a peculiar water column, a few metres in size. She and Ms. Lindbäck are able to agree on what they have seen.
Additional conversations with local residents resulted in contact with Ms. Helga Vesterlund, who claims to have heard a sharp whizzling sound as well as having observed a strange whirlwind whilst bringing home the cows. The timing of this event coincides with statements left by other witnesses.
The ferryman at the ferry-place in Kamlunge reported that the above-mentioned whirlwinds weren't at all unusual. They were, in fact, perfectly normal due to the hot weather. He recalled seeing at least three very similar whirlwinds in the year of 1945.
The searching was aborted in the afternoon of July 26, on the orders of "ch Fo 67".

Boden on July 29, 1946.
Lennart Kestner
Lieutenant
