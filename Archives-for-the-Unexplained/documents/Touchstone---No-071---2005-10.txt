Hills in Somerset. The Blackdowns straddle the Somerset/Devon border south of Taunton
but are not generally well-known - most of the routes bypass them and even in Laurence
Main's Walks in Mysterious Somerset they are only represented by a void on the map
between the better-known areas of the Levels, Athelney and Exmoor.

it does not have a church now - although it once had a chapel of ease, this is now a barn,

Churchstanton, a mile away to the north-east.
