RECENT ITALIAN SIGHTINGS
A motorist on the road from Azzida and Purgessimo (Udine) suddenly saw
five connected green balls in the sky spreading away in just a few seconds,
at 5.15 a.m. on September 2nd [Messaggero veneto, 4 September].
A few days later, at 21.30, a council clerk driving his daughter and
two of her friends near Tromello (Pavia) met a giant miriad of lights, like a
flying merry-go-round hovering 150 meters above the ground, then moving away
silently toward the Remondò Air Force radar station, which later denied any
detection [Provincia pavese, 10 September; investigation by Claudio Cavalli-
IDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECTS
The ball of fire that frightened several campers in Marina di Massa
(Massa Carrara) on August 22nd was a small hoax-balloon sent aloft by some
foreign tourists. The beach owner revealed he saw the balloon taking off
until the nylon rope was cut and the object flew away like a fiery ball [ANSA
news agency, August 26].
A similar explanation was put forward by CISU investigators for the red
light seen and video-recorded at another camping, in Baia Domizia (Caserta)
on August 19: it was unofficially confirmed that it was a military balloon
fallen down into the sea [Il Mattino, 12 September; investigation by Gior-
gio Russolillo and Giovanni Ascionel.
Once again U.S. Air Force F-117 stealth aircrafts are rumoured to be
the real cause for reports coming from several places in the Treviso province
on the evening of August 21: all witnesses described a dark triangular shape
with some lights on it, moving fast and silently over them. Military sources,
as always, deny any F-117 was flying in that region [La Tribuna di Treviso,
26 August].
