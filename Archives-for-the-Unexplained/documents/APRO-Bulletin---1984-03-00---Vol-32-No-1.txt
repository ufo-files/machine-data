   The staff of APRO - Mr.and Mrs. Lorenzen, Mr. Marsland, Mrs. McCoy and Betty Scott, would like to

 thank the many members who sent Holiday Greetings. We have not made a policy of relaying holiday

 messages via the Bulletin because the APRO membership is so diverse that the "Holidays" being mainly

 religious in nature, do not mean much to members who are not of the Christian faith or American by

 nationality.

   However, the many additional messages appended to the Holiday greetings, such as "keep up the good

 work", mean a lot to all of us. Some of us have met some of you and many of you are considered to be friends,

 and therefore the messages are all the more important and dear.

   So - we would all like to wish all of you happiness, health and success, with warm regards.
