  Testor's plastic model kit of one of nine alien craft allegedly housed at Papoose Lake and examined by
  Bob Lazar. Details adhere strictly to Lazar's claims. Clear shell displays interior of craft, complete with
  "gravity amplification system" and seating for 3. An accompanying 15-page booklet describes the Lazar

  Lazar story, and nice color images of Papoose Lake taken by a Russian spy satellite. Designed by John
  Andrews in consultation with Bob Lazar.
