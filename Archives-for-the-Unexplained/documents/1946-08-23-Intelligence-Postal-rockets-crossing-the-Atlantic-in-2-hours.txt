August 23: Postal rockets crossing the Atlantic in two hours...

A TT-Ritzau´s Bureau telegram from Copenhagen reported that a Director Jones of the Hawker Sidderley Aircraft Co. arrived in the city on Wednesday (Aug.21). He speculated that ”in a few decades” postal rockets would be sent from Copenhagen to land in New York just two hours later. The rockets would be controlled by radar and when close to America, wings and landing gear would be unfolded and the rocket would slowly land. (Norra Västerbotten, August 23).
