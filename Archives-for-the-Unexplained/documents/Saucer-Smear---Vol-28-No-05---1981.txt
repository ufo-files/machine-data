CREATURE UPDATE:

      Your insipid "Saucer Leer" editor has been continuing his in-depth
investigation of "Fish-Tank Man" - the alleged space creature immortalized
a few years ago by Fass Publications.  Jeff Goodman, former editor   of

magazines, and we have sent prints to Len Stringfield and others,  in an
effort to determine for once & for all whether Stringfield's creature is
really the same or not.

one in "Official UFO" but by no means identical.  Stringfield's are taken

shown is more decomposed.  There are also other differences.  But the main
reason Stringfield is not showing his photos any more, in our opinion, is
that they received a skeptical reception at the CUP meeting in Cleveland
