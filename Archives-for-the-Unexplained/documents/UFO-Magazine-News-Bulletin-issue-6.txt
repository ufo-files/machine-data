   Mrs. Madeline McKoane was driving her son· and his friend home from
a hockey meeting at Langendorf Park, when they spotted a strange object
in the sky as they approached Baker's Lake. It was early evening and
the bright object in the distance startled them.
   "We saw these glaring lights," Mrs. McKoane explained. "It was a
large spinning object lest: than 1,000 feet off the ground. It was going
straight across from Easter~ Avenue over Baker's Lake and just hovered
there.
