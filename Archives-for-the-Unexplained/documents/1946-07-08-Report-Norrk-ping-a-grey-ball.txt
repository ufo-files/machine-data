Case xxx.
A grey ball over Norrköping

Time: Monday, July 8, 12.20 hours
Place: Norrköping (Östergötland)

”As early as on Monday, Mr. J.B. Strand from Norrköping says he saw a ball-shaped object moving eastwards at great speed. Mr. Strand was sunbathing in his yard when he observed an airplane and at the same time an object, which at great speed passed from west to east. The object was ball-shaped and measured about half-a-meter in diameter. The colour was grey. No noise or other sound was heard.” 

This newspaper story was obviously investigated by someone in the military. A small slip of notes
in the archives bear witness to this. From the notes it is apparent that the sighting took place at 12.20 hours. The grey ball had a trajectory opposite to the airplane. ”400 m” is noted, distance? Object disappeared in the direction of Arkösund (to the east). Object may have crashed. The witness has experience from the Air Force.  

Status: Insufficient information
Sources: Östergötlands Dagblad, July 11; TT newswire, July 11; Defence Staff archives.
