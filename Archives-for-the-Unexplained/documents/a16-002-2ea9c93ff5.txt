                                      Nellis Air Show Press Release
  From: campbell@ufomind.com (Glenn Campbell, Las Vegas)
  Date: Sat, 15 Mar 1997 08:11:34 -0800
  Subject: Nellis Air Show Press Release
  From: Steve1957@aol.com
  Date: Fri, 14 Mar 1997 18:41:35 -0500 (EST)
  Subject: Interceptes Network News: Nellis
  WASHINGTON (AFNS) -- More than 80 air power leaders from
  around the world will meet in Las Vegas April 23-24 as part of the
  50th anniversary celebration of our nation's Air Force. This Global
  Air Chiefs Conference is to further cooperation and support among
  world air power leaders in the spirit of Global Engagement. Global
  Engagement is the new vision for the Air Force of the 21st century.
  The gathering of air chiefs represents the largest single meeting of
  air and space leaders in history. Culminating the meeting will be a
  two-day Golden Air Tattoo international air show at Nellis Air
  Force Base, Nev., April 25-26.
  The air show, which is open to the public, is scheduled to feature
  four foreign demonstration teams. There will be static displays and
  flyovers depicting examples of every aircraft in the Air Force
  inventory, as well as many vintage aircraft. The Air Force
  Thunderbirds aerial demonstration squadron will perform on both
  days.
  Additionally, the Air Force Association is sponsoring an
  International Air Power Symposium and Exposition at the Las
  Vegas Convention Center during that week.
  These events are part of worldwide recognition of the "Golden
  Legacy and Boundless Future" of the U. S. Air Force as it
  celebrates 50 years of service to the nation as an independent
  branch of the military. The Air Force was established Sept. 18,
  1947, by President Harry S. Truman with the signing of the
  National Security Act.
