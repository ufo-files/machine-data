Case xxx. 
FRA radio reconnaissance staff 
spot luminous phenomenon

Time: August 2, 00.05 (five minutes past midnight)
Place: Ljugarn (island of Gotland, in the Baltic)

The top secret Defence Radio Institute (FRA) has for decades had listening stations on the Baltic island of Gotland, closer to the Soviet Union than the Swedish mainland. Staff from a listening post in Ljugarn, station ”Frida”, spotted a luminous phenomenon and sent the following report to the Defence Staff: 

”Report ”Frida”
1. Time: August 2, at 00.05. Spectators: telegraphists Ulriksson, Granell and Svedberg. 
2. Place of observation: about in the middle of the Ljugarn village. 
3. Direction of observation: about 130 degrees [to the southeast] from the place of observation. 
4. Degree of altitude: about 45 degrees. 
5. Great speed, 1000 [km/hour], maybe more. No sound was heard. 
6. Direction of flight of the projectile about southwesterly. 
7. Length of the light phenomenon, about 100 meters, maybe more.
8. Light: powerful, somewhat violet. 
9. Shape: almost spool-shaped.
10. Seemed to diminish (probably hidden behind a cloud of rain).
11. Cloud cover: Heavy rain clouds (stratos-nimbus). 

”Remark. This time of the day it is usually rather dark, therefore this must be appromixate data. As concerns FRIDA we often catch strange sounds [on radio], but this has been found to be teleprinters, Hell writers, transmitters (of about 2000 [letters]/minute). On the long wave, a lot of carrier waves are always caught. In suspicious cases the observer [telegraphist] stays with the signal for a longer time. 

”Ljugarn, August 2, 1946. Olof Svedberg, head of detachement (FRA).”

Göran Jansson, with contacts within FRA, writes in his Internet book on the 1946 phenomena: 

”FRIDA was an abbreviation that FRA used as a code for a radio reconnaissance project. FRIDA stands for ”Foreign Robot [Swedish word for missile] activities In the Dagö area”. Dagö is an island north of [the island] Ösel - both of them off the Esthonian coast. One [FRA] gave the mission this name since one suspected that the ghost rockets were directed from Dagö. This was not a common knowledge within the ghost projectile committee, but FRA used the code name after having caught suspicious signals from Dagö.”
