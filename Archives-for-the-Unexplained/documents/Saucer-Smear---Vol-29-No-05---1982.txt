                     RE-HASH OF THE 1982 NATIONAL UFO CONFEREN CE
       The 19th (count 'em!) annual National UFO Conference (NUFOC) was held
on Saturday May 29th at the Parma Memorial Auditorium, near Cleveland, Ohio.
Delegates, such as your humble "Smear" editor, stayed at a motel nearby. To­
tal attendance at the public sessions exceeded JOO, and local media atten­
tion was above average, due to strenuous efforts by the local group, headed
by Ted Sauppe. In addition, a small New York documentary film group called
Bermuda Triangle Films was supposed to cover the convention this year, but
as we later learned, the two young men missed their plane to Cleveland and
thus did not make it.
       Speeches this year included a brief wrap-up of the West Jefferson Big­
foot case & West Virginia contactee case, by your humble editor; a UFO sta­
tistical report by .Clevelander Al Manak; "The Spiritual Aspects of UFOs"
by Larry Blazey; a brief German report read (in English) by Bob Easley;
and "Animal Mutilations & UFOs" presented by Bob Goerman of Pennsylvania.
(See also our review of Goerman's current booklet about Carlos Allende
elsewhere in this issue. )
       The most erudite job was done by Tom Benson, who spoke at the after­
noon session on UFO understructures (beautifully illustrated on slides),
and at the evening session on general Ufology - also accompanied by slides.
The most novel presentation was by Bigfootologist Jon Beck jord, who came
all the way from Seattle, Wash. to astound us with pictures purporting to
show that our favorite humanoid has 4-D characteristics. Both Benson and
Beck jord are fine speakers, whose remarks seemed to be well received by the
audience.
       But as to our favorite presentation of the day, this year we'd have
to give the unofficial "Smear" award to none other than Gray Barker, who de­
parted from his usual "Three Men in Black" routine and spoke on "The UFO
Abduction Syndrome". We especially liked the closing words of this lec­
ture, rumored to be Barker's last public appearance: "I hope you have seen
a UFO, but if you have not, look for them not in outer space, nor in an­
other dimension, nor in the oceans, or even in the Hollow Earth. And if
on a starry night you fail to see a UFO, look deep within yourselves, and I
guarantee that there you will find them. "
       Another high point of the evening came at the conclusion of Jon Beck­
jord's weird presentation. He had been showing slides in which a mysteri­
ous chipmunk-like Jt-D head was allegedly visible in the background; and,
after the last slide went off, there was the same chipmunk-like head pro­
jected somehow, by. itself, near the upper right-hand corner of the   dark
screen. This could quite easily have been a hoax, a reasonable mistake of
some sort, or even an after-image. But your editor saw it quite clearly,
and the strange thing was that only a handful of the many other people in
the hall saw it! Are we, therefore, more tuned into the Higher Realms
than was the average attendee? In all probability, only Beckjord knows for
sure just what happened.
       As usual, a short "closed session" of the convention was held (late
Friday evening), at which a few business matters were decided, including:
Next year's Con. will be held in beautiful, sunny Tucson, Arizona - hosted
                                     (2)
 by former Clevelander Ed Biebel. It was also decided to remove from    our
·governing board (called the "Permanent Organizing Committee") the names of
 Allen Greenfield of Atlanta and Timothy Green Beckley of New York, and to
 put back the name of Tom Benson, who had been removed at the time of his re­
 cent brief resignation from ufology. Thus the board now consists of Tom
 Benson, Bob Easley, Gray Barker, Ed Biebel, Al Manak, Rick & Carol Hilberg,
 and your "Smear" editor, who is Chairman.
       In view of our Chairmanship of the NUFOC, it is somewhat embarrass­
 ing for us to report that by a unanimous vote at the above-mentioned "clos­
 ed session", your editor was given the "Ufologist of the Year" award for
 the second time - the only person ever to receive this award more than
 once. Your editor modestly abstained, in the voting.
       There's a possibility that we'll be bringing the NUFOC back to New
 York City in (shudder: ) 1984, though if we do, we'll try to learn from
 past mistakes. The idea is to somehow find a way to keep the overhead low
 and the attendance high. Ideas from our non-subscribers would be most wel­
 come.

            (BIG)FOOTNOTE TO THE ABOVE & REVIEW OF WEIRD BOOK


       Rome, Ohio is a tiny community about forty miles from Cleveland,
where strange Bigfoot-type happenings have allegedly been going on for
about a year. The principal investigator, Dennis Pilichis, wrote a book
about this recently, called "Night Siege", but seems unwilling to let any­
one have it whom he considers an enemy, in the mixed-up world of Bigfoot/
UFO politics. Until the Cleveland Con. , your editor didn't even know the
book was out. We obtained a copy from Larry Blazey, the illustrator, but
kindly Jon Beck jord walked off with our copy before we could read it, as
he had been unable to buy a copy from Pilichis� We then obtained another
copy from Tim Beckley, who sells it through the mail, and once having read
it, we phoned Pilichis for.more details. Upon hearing the name "James Mos­
eley", Pilichis immediately hung up. (Why? Because Pilichis is a friend
of the notorious "Dr. " Mcintyre, who helped him investigate the case. )
       If all this is too confusing to follow - never mind, let's get on
to the book itself: It recounts a series of fantastic night incidents,
in which unnamed residents of a Rome farm, in some cases together with Pil­
ichis & friends, experience huge shadowy Bigfeet with red glowing eyes,
cavorting about in the woods, leaving 3 and 5-toed footprints. Many, many
shots are fired, but there are no bodies and no blood:
       As in the tamer West Jefferson (Ohio) scene which your editor in­
vestigated, most of this must either be utter hysteria (in our humble opin­
ion) or else something wilder than our wildest 3t-D thinking. Perhaps a
clue is this quote from the book: "I (Pilichis) must admit that I could
not at first see many of the forms moving on the woodline. I believe this
was due to my night vision abilities, and the fact that various family
members were expert hunters, and most probably could see better at night
than I. " Maybe; but we're reminded of several incidents at West Jefferson,
where ladies who were not expert hunters were able to see shadowy Bigfeet
in the nearby woods when we could not see anything at all!
       Beck jord, who has feuded with "Dr. " Mcintyre, as we have, went to
Rome right after the Cleveland convention, and immediately reported back to
us by phone that "Pilichis and Mcintyre are right", and that he could even
hear UFOs reving up their engines in the nearby swamps as he spoke: For var­
ious reasons we declined his invitation to join him. Probably just as well,
as we later learned that Pilichis called the police on Beckjord for tres­
passing on his case, so to speak - and tried hard to have him arrested.
       What is really going on in Rome, Ohio? We may never know for sure;
but if you are interested in this unique Bigfoot book, it's available for
$5. 50 from Tim Beckley, 303 Fifth Ave. , Suite 1306, New York, N. Y.

us who would like to see a greater public acceptance of Fortean phenomena.
