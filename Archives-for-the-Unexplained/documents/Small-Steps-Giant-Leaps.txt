       The Last Priest

In a cold black hall, in an old black hall,
Far beneath the ground
An old God sits, and never moves,
And never makes a sound.


Down the corridor comes a man,
Bent, and old, and frail.
An old old God, and an old old man,
Both beyond the pale.


The temple's closed, the way is lost,
No one comes any more;
Time has taken their people away,
And no one cares any more.


Who knows what God, who knows what Priest,
What church or Temple or shrine
Their time is come and gone, my friend,
And no one cares any more.
