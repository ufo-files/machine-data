Case xxx.
A mysterious light over Stockholm

Time: May 3, evening
Place: Stockholm

”A mysterious white shining light over the Stockholm horizon occasioned a lively inquiry on Friday evening in the newspaper´s telephone exchange as well as with the fire brigade. Some observers had also heard reports, but they probably arose from some blasting work. Concerning the light it is thought at the Saltsjöbaden observatory that it was some kind of aurora [northern lights]. The light had been seen from Saltsjöbaden in a northwesterly direction. It is possible that a changeover to clearer weather, which is now now signalled, has some connection with this celestial phenomenon.”

Status: Aurora borealis
Source: Morgon-Tidningen, May 4 [from Thomas Bullard: The Airship file]; Göteborgs-Tidninge, M
