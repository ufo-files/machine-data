                                                                                  Freedom of Information Program
       DIAR 12-39, 24 March 1992, is changed in order to implement the Attorney
       General's new Freedom of Information Act policy memorandum of October 4, 1993.
       That memorandum rescinded the Department of Justice's 1981 guidelines under
       which the Department of Justice would defend federal agencies being sued
       because they withheld information requested under the Freedom of Information
       Act. The Department of Justice will no longer defend an agency's decision to
       withhold information merely because there is a ''substantial legal basis" for
       doing so. Instead, the Department of Justice will apply a presumption of
       disclosure when determining whether or not to defend the agency's nondisclosure
       decision. Agencies must now set out in detail for the Department of Justice,
       the
       anticipated harm that will be caused if the requested information is released,
       and demonstrate that the anticipated harm is reasonably expected to occur.
