  Prophets event is truly all-encompassing in nature. As popular radio talk
  show host Art Bell appropriately stated on his program, "Phoenix, Phoenix,
  Phoenix ......What is it about Phoenix?. I feel that something BIG is going
  to happen in Phoenix!" Well Art, a great many people feel exactly the same

  occurring in Phoenix on October 10-12!
  For those participating in this truly unique and momentous occasion, the
  gates open at the Corona Ranch at 4:00pm on Friday, October 10 to a large
  and colorful exhibition; to the "Talking Stick Stage" filled with a carnival
  of profound ideas with passionate speakers; to the gala opening celebration
  and concert with the multiple Grammy winning Paul Winter Consort and Native
  American ceremony. This special afternoon and evening includes entry into
  the conference grounds and exhibition area, the "Talking Stick Stage", and
  the concert. Advance Tickets for Friday are $20.50 ($25 at the door) and
  are available at all Dillard's Outlets in the Phoenix area. Order by phone
  1-800-366-3606, or Arizona locally at 602-736-2121. Friday is also included
  in the 3-Day Conference Pass.
  Saturday morning begins at 8:00am with ceremony and music, followed by
  deeply insightful panels beginning at 9:00am in the 1,200 seat "Great Hall".
  Panels explore "Contact", "Beyond Contact", "Today's Native Voices",
  "Shamanic Visions", "Dreaming The Future", "What It Is And What It Isn't",
  and "Wisdom Of The Elders." Individual presenter workshops are mainly
  scheduled for the afternoon, and will be held in large, colorful and
  comfortable tents spread around the 12 acres of beautiful lawn and tree
  lined walkways. Delicious food for all tastes will be served. Saturday
  evening brings the UFOs, as Dr. Steven Greer and his crack team from CSETI
  use their successful protocols to effect Contact.
  Meanwhile in other Corona Ranch locations , Richard Dannelley will teach his
  advanced group-consciousness techniques and ceremony - "Calling In The Ships
  Of Light", to be followed by a Saturday night dance with mind-music
  body-music by the highly acclaimed trance/dance band UFAUX, featuring Jim
  Dilettoso, Susan Gordon, Jerry Wills, and a cosmic light show.
  Sunday, October 12 is another special day, being both Columbus Day and the
  anniversary of the fall of Babylon in 539 B.C. This is most appropriately
  the day for Zacharia Sitchen's latest news on the return of the planet
  Nibiru, as well as for the Gathering of the Native Elders, bringing their
  tribal prophetic messages, held close through the centuries for this
  foretold time of coming together. Workshops, panels, music and exhibition
  continue to 10:00pm Sunday, when the gatherers return home with new
  knowledge, friendships, motivations and perspectives.
