  code deal, which even as a 'believer' I had no faith in that whatsoever. I also think, that there are some
  very strong and relatively civil posts being made from the 'military incident" camp at the moment, which
  must actually come as a disappointment to the majority here (hey, even I don't like reading 'mundane'
  solutions -that's not why I started to look into the incident!), so it is understandable, though not excusable
  that there's a bit of friction at the moment. I liked the forum, pre anniversary better as well, but I've done
