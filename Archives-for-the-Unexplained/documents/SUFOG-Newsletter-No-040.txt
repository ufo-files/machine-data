Southampton Evening Echo : 11 January 94 :


CARPENTER SHOT MYSTERY GIANT CAT TERRORISING ISLE OF WIGHT :


   Experts are hailing pictures of a leopard-type animal shot
on the Isle Of Wight as proof that fearsome big cats are
roaming the British countryside.
   The three-and-a-half ton long spotted creature shot by
Stuart Skinner and his friend Jason Ward is either an Ocelot
or a Serval.
   The creature was shot by carpenter Stuart,21,with his 20
bore rifle,while.farm worker Jason,also 21,held a dim torch.
Stuart and Jason shot the creature some time ago but kept quiet
until now,fearing they had done something wrong.
   The pair had set a fox trap after 20 chickens and ducks had
been taken from a farm near Sandown - where Jason worked.
   "We were siting inside when we heard this strange howling,
growling noise coming from the trap.
   "It was making a hell of a noise.Jason and I rushed outside
with our guns and a torch," said Stuart from St Helens.
   "We thought we had finally caught the fox that had been
nicking the chickens and ducks       so I blasted it with my 20

                                   Deat h Of Ken Rogers   •   •   •


                 It is a sad day for Ufology with the death of Ken Rogers
              1950 - 1994,on the 15th of January 1994,at the Bath Royal
              Hospital,following a long illness.

                 Ken was only a 15 year old school boy in 1965 when the
              Warminster sightings made national headlines.He was the
              founder member of the Warminster Ufo Society which-became the
              British Ufo Society.

                 He started organising Skywatches on Cradle Hill,Warminster
              in 1973,after correlating local sightings of t he Warminster
              'THING'. From his research,he found that the 27th August every
              other year was t he most likely time to encounter visitors from
              beyond the stars.

                 He claimed he saw the Warminster 'THING' and in a television
              said that the crop circles were the landing marks,of the ufo's.
              Over the years,he organised many ufo exhibitions on the subject
              in Warminster,and produced a video called 'The Warminster
              Thing'.

                 Although many people may not have agreed with some of Mr
              Rogers views,one must still feel a touch of sadness at the
              loss of one of the early leading lights of ufology.
