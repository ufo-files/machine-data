INTERVIEW WITH ELOF KARLSSON, ASPNÄS AND
VERNER KARLSSON, VAUKAGÅRDEN, ARJEPLOG -
VISIT TO ARJEPLOG ON JUNE 7, 1987

Re: The crater at Aspnäs, discovered on Aug 12, 1946 by Verner Karsson

Elof told me that he believed the crater was caused by some  youngsters having played with dynamite. But he did not know whether this was the case.

Verner, who was staying for the time being at the Nomadskolan, recalls:

"I was working alone in the fields at Valvottjärn making hay as I discovered the crater down on the shore. It was filled with water almost up to the rim. A few metres away there was a dead bird.

The pit looked like something caused by an explosion or an impact of some kind. But nobody in the village had noticed any crash."

Verner called the police. Probably from his own phone at home.

"The police came immediately as far as I recall. They dug in the  the bottom of the pit but it was even and smooth and there was no trace of any objects down in the mud.

One theory was that some youngsters in the village would have blown up the som sticks of dynamite but I am doubtful about that theory. There was something strange about the crater."

At this time of year (in the spring) the pit is filled with water.
