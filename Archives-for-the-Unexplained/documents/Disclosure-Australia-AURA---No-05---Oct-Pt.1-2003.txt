  Federal Government document search update:

  DSTO files:

  Members of the Secretariat have now examined the large amount of paper lists of DSTO file titles of possible
  relevance to our Project, held in the Adelaide office of the National Australia Archives (NAA). Requests have been
  made for a number of files, in order that we may physically look at them. It is anticipated that processing of all our
  requests will take several weeks, if not months.

  “Fire Across the Desert”:

  The Secretariat has contacted Peter Morton, the author of “Fire Across the Desert,” a history of Woomera, reported on
  in the last Newsletter. His advice concerning the UFO files he had seen at Woomera, was that they were no longer
  there, but in Canberra. He also said that a UFO researcher from WA had talked with him, and as far as he (Morton)
  knew, this researcher has already accessed these files through the Department of Defence. Do any readers know of
  this researcher?

  Documenting the volume of material:

  With the release of more old files containing UFO reports, which were made to various sections of the Australian
  Government, eg. through local Police stations, through the RAAF, the Department of Civil Aviation, and to the
  CSIRO; the Secretariat has been discussing how best to process this volume of data. An examination of these reports
  indicates, as you would expect, that many do indeed have mundane explanations. However, there are others where such
  an explanation is not so readily apparent. There are also a few “good” cases which would add to our knowledge of the
  core UFO phenomenon. We have therefore started to make a summary listing of all these UFO reports, which we will
  ultimately make available, both on the Disclosure web site and in hard copy format. For a sample, taken from RAAF
  file 580/1/1 Part 1, please see attachment two to this Newsletter.
