       Jerry Clark is indeed a strange and difficult fellow. His " Dear
 Friends and Colleagues" letter, quoted in part above, was pointedly not
 sent to your " Smear" editor, even though Clark had been saying nice things
 about us in private letters just a few weeks ago.
       But were these really private letters? With Clark and a few others,
 one never knows! Favorable letters, to and fro, are passed around       to
 one's friends and admirers, whereas unfavorable ones are not. This is how
 Clark can piously claim that he has never published a word about the Linda
 Napolitano abduction case, whereas in reality he has apparently written
 a lot about it to many people.
       In our Jan. lOth " Smear" , we accused Clark of being " engaged in a
 series of highly emotional, non-productive feuds with a number of people."
 In Clark's Feb. 23rd open letter, he changes our accusation to read " irra­
 tional feuds". Thus it is Clark who is characterizing his feuds as " irra­
 tional". We'll stick with " highly emotional" ; and everything Clark has
 written recently about our relatively mild criticism, convinces us more
 than ever that we are on the right track!
       We criticized Clark because we are concerned at the excessive way
 he has taken off recently on George Hansen, and because of the fact that
 Clark simply refuses to discuss any of the important criticisms that have
 been made by many people concerning Budd Hopkins, re his handling of ab­
 duction cases in general and the Napolitano case in particular. These mat­
 ters need to be pursued - but hopefully not in an emotional ---or irrational
 way!
       Message to Jerry Clark: We're not really mad at you at all, just dis­
 appointed. But Jerry, if you can't stand the heat, get out of the oven!

"SMEAR" EDITOR DEMOTED AGAIN IN MUFON!
      Just when we thought things couldn't get any worse, we received a
curt letter from Florida State MUFON Director Charles Flannigan, which reads
in its entirety: "Toward the interest of increasing management effective­
ness, I am recommending that Walt (Andrus) reassign you to (JS) Journal
Subscriber."
      "J.S." is, of course, as low as one can go in the MUFON hierarchy.
When we were finally allowed to join this esteemed organization in 1986,
we were appointed Section Director for Monroe County, Florida, where    we
live. Then, about a year ago, we were demoted to Assistant Section Direc­
tor for Monroe County, on the recommendation of this same Charles Flanni­
gan, even though no one was appointed to our former title! (Now, finally,
there is a new Section Director for the two-county area of Monroe and Dade,
where Miami is located.)
      We will proudly write "J.S." after our name, and, as we have inform­
ed Walt Andrus, we will continue to do just as much for MUFON in the future
as we did before our unfortunate demotions.
      At least we are still better off than we were with NICAP in the old
days. Even though copies of their zine were often given out free to sym­
pathetic members of Congress as well as to many other people, your editor
was not only ejected from NICAP but not even allowed to subscribe to the
zine! We did subscribe secretly, of course, under a different name     and
address. Wheeeee!...
