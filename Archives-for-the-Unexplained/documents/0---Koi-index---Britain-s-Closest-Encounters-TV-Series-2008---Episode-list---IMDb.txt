     On the evening of 23 January 1974, the quiet Welsh village of Llandrillo was rocked by a
     loud explosion and impact. The police were inundated with 999 calls, and it was at first
     assumed that a plane had crashed on the nearby Berwyn Mountains. Witnesses came
     forward and described a bright, spherical object arcing across the sky. A local nurse
     gave her account of seeing a large glowing ball of light on the mountainside. The
     military became involved, and rumours began to circulate that this was a UFO, not a
     plane. At the same time, geologists theorised that this was an earth tremor - the impact
     had been measured at 3.5 on the Richter scale, and bright flashes of light often
     accompany these events. Sadly, no tangible evidence has been produced, and so the
     case remains a mystery to this day.

     S1.E3 ∙ UFO Alley
     Wed, Jul 23, 2008

     UK police officers tell their stories here, notably Todmorden PC Alan Godfrey, who
     sensationally claims to have been abducted by a landed UFO. Hypnotic regression was
     used to obtain full details, and no explanation has ever been found.
