                FIFTY YEARS OF UFO's - WHAT HAVE WE LEARNT?
Let us clarify one point before we start discussing the appearance of UFO's over the last fifty
years. That is, the Kenneth Arnold sighting in 194 7 did not herald the sudden arrival of UFO's
around the world*, they have been visiting our planet for hundreds, if not thousands of years.
What the Kenneth Amold event represented, was the introduction of a wave of UFO sightings
throughout the world, something that had never been experienced before.
* The U.S. Air Force explanation was ''After weighing up all the facts, the Air Force has
concluded that Mr. Amo/d had witnessed a mirage created by uncommonly stable weather
conditions. "
1254. At St. Albans Abbey, England. "An elegantly shaped, well equipped ship of marvellous
colour, appeared in the sky. "
1874. A farmer in Texas reported seeing a "dark flying object in the shape of a disk, cruising in
the sky at a wonderful speed."
I have on record, a UFO sighting made in 1937, by a Colonel Tumer-Bridger, an ex-WWI
aviator. The Colonel was with his wife driving along the South Downs (West Sussex), when
they turned into a valley and saw a pulsating ball of coloured light hovering close to a nearby
hill top. He stopped the car to observe the phenomenon, but then suddenly the ball of light
shot away at high speed and out of sight. The Colonel was aware that Farnborough (R. A.E.)
Airfield was not far away and that the war clouds were gathering in Europe. He thought that
the ball of light may have been a 'secret weapon' development from Farnborough and therefore
he took no action over his sighting. It was some thirty-five years later that he contacted me
and told me his story! (ED.)

                                 THE CONTINUING STORY
There were numerous sightings of LITS (Lights In The Sky) during the sixties and seventies,
together with the appearance of balls of light and a sighting of an occasional flying disk.

One of the earliest encounter cases was that of Mr Burtoo.         He was night fishing on the
Basingstoke Canal in the early hours of       12th August 1983, when a bright glowing craft
descended from the sky and landed on the canal bank about half a mile away.

Mr Burtoo's dog began to growl shortly afterwards as two small figures clad in 'space suits'
appeared and beckoned Mr Burtoo to follow them to the saucer craft and to go on board.

                                                2
This he did, later commenting "I thought what have I got to lose at my age ( 77), let's go and
see what it's all about."    He duly walked of his own accord, into the 'saucer', where after a
short wait, he was scanned by a beam of yellow light. Shortly afterwards, he was told to go.
"You are too old and infirmjor our purpose". Alfred left the craft and returned to his fishing!
"The sky could have fallen in, I'd have still carried on fishing'� commented Alf ....

The Burtoo incident remains one of the most important early encounter cases, partly because
Alf was able to recollect just about everything that happened, without being subjected to
'hypnotic regression'. A good example of a witness recollection, without being 'influenced' by
a hypnotist.

During the 60's to 80's there have been hundreds of incidents of 'Flying Saucers', hovering
over housing estates, football matches, town centres etc. and although there were a variety of
different craft shapes and sizes reported, the new innovation of 'Flying Triangles' arrived in
force in the early 80's.

One of the first sightings was over Clifton-Morenci, Arizona U.S.A. during the period October
1980 to January 1981, when a boomerang shaped craft, bigger than several football fields, was
observed flying over the countryside.

A similar huge 'V' shaped UFO appeared once again, quite recently on the 13th March 1997.

Flying Triangle sightings from the early 80's to the this year are included in Omar Fowler's
latest booklet "Flying Triangle UFO's: Tbe Continuing Story."              The booklet is currently
going into print and will be available the lst September, £3.50 plus SOp postage. It contains
articles from a number of contributors and has two pages of colour prints, plus statistical
charts of FT speeds and frequency diagrams.

The current 'Flying Triangle' news is becoming more astounding, almost by the day. All of our
information to date points to the likelihood that we are dealing with two types of 'Flying
Triangle' vehicles. One is the huge extra-terrestrial/Alien controlled craft, observed travelling
slowly over the countryside at night, often carrying a variety of lights and occasionally shining
down a beam of light.

The other type of Flying Triangle, is a smaller black craft, also observed at night, but carrying
no lights of any kind.     By all accounts,   we ( the U.K.) are developing a small 'Stealth' craft,
which is also flying over the countryside at night. However, we have only the barest of details
at present and can say no more than that!

What will the next fifty years bring?   The information that we are receiving continually, points
to the fact that the 'FT' sightings are increasing at a tremendous rate, in some instances by at
least aI00% over the last two years! It seems unlikely that we will have to wait another fifty
years to see a further increase in UFO activity world-wide.             The way that things are
developing at present, the next five years could produce some astounding revelations.

We know that the FT's have been seen diving into and emerging from the sea off the East
Coast of England. Could the craft have an underwater base near the coast?          There have been
some startling claims made by Larry Warren in his new book "Left At East Gate". He speaks
of underground facilities beneath the Bentwaters air base and he hints that these are extensive
and could in fact be holding aliens and possibly alien craft.

Slowly pieces of the jigsaw are coming together and the picture that is being revealed is
disquieting for us all!                                                                    0. F.

 Skywatch Diary                                                           By Carole Baker


      Well, another quiet week at Shoreline Park. We had some visitors though, inGiuding some from Ft.
 Lauderdale and a doctor from the Naples, FL area who was very interesting to talk with. He told us that his
 interest in UFOs had really been lifelong and started as a young man when someone gave him a newspaper
 containing his great-grandparents wedding announcement from back in the 1880's in Virginia. There was
 also a story in that newspaper of someone having had a UFO sighting! Speaking of sig�tings though, there
. are none   ·.
                   to report from Shoreline Park. We have enjoyed the cooler, less humid evenings this week
 courtesy of two cold fronts that made their way down to the Gulf Coast. Very unusual for this time of the
 year, but a most welcome relief from the heat and high humidity that we expect in August. Some reports of
 sightings in the local area have filtered through to us, however.   One set of reports from the Gulf Breeze
 area and the other from Perdido Key, west of ·Pensacola.


      The weekend that Hurricane Danny paid us a visit, July 18th, 19th and 20th, 1997, there were
 three sightings on Perdido Key involving a cylindrical shaped object. One of these sightings was witnessed
 by five men from the pool of a Gulf front condominium. lt was approximately 11:30 p.m. when the men
 noticed a cylindrical shaped object descending from high above about a half mile offshore and then following
 the shoreline from west to east toward them. lt stopped and hovered about five hundred yards out into the       ·




 Gulf right in front of the witnesses. All the time the object was hovering, it alternated colors between light
 lime green and white. lt hovered for over a minute and then shot off due south, rising and reaching the
 horizon in about a second and a half. One of the witnesses states that it "took off at incredible speed". There
 are some details missing from this report which we hope to be able to track down and thus provide a more
 detailed account at a later date.


      The Gulf Breeze reports all occured on the evening of Wednesday, July 30th, 1997. Two of our
 regular skywatch group, Ray and Elise Pollock saw three amber lights in the sky as they were driving to
 Gulf Breeze at around 8:45 p.m. The lights were to their south-east, toward the Navarre Bridge. They
 first spotted them while driving on the Bayfront Parkway in Pensacola and kept them in sight as they drove
 over the three mile bridge. The lights were stationary in the sky and maintained their elevation. No drop
 of fade was noted as would be the case with flares. As they travelled over the bridge, one of the lights went
 out and then the other two went out together. The lights were visible for well over five minutes.


       Another witness called the Gulf Breeze police department to report seeing amber lights in the sky at
 around 8:40 p.m. They appeared at an approximate forty-five degree elevation and were going off and
 coming on in sequence. We believe there is some video of this event, although details right now are sketchy
 and we hope to have more information at a later date.


       A third witness called, Art Hufford, president of Pensacola/Gulf 8reeze MUFON to report that at 8:1 5
 p.m. they had seen bright amber lights in the sky near the Navarre Bridge from their vantage point just
 east of The Zoo in Gulf Breeze. The sighting lasted thirty minutes with the individual lights staying lit for
 approx\mate\y two to three m\nutes at a t\me. \he \\ghts were com\ng on and go\ng off \n a sequence from
 left to right. As they went out on the left side of the configuration, more would come on to the right. This is
 a pattern that we have seen many times in the Gulf Breeze area and is not dissimilar to the effect given by
 the recent Phoenix, Arizona sightings.

                A DAY AT THE BROADMARSH SHOPPING CENTRE
      (In aid of the 'Smiles .Better Appeal' for the Nottingham Children's Hospital)


As a result of a request by the 'Smiles Better Appeal', the PRA arranged a small exhibition of
UFO effects for the interest of the general public. The exhibition was widely publicised and a
number of UFO and other groups attended, in an effort to raise money for the Nottingham
Children's Hospital appeal.
The UFO groups were represented by the PRA (Derby), Les Truman's UFO group from
Mansfield, Matthew Wtlliams from the 'Truth Seekers' group,(S.Wales) and a variety of fringe
groups, including Star Trekker's and others.
The exhibition took place on Saturday 2nd August, a busy shopping afternoon, in spite of the
holiday period. There was great interest shown by the shopping audience and there were a
number of interesting questions raised and also several local sighting reports that were
previously unknown. A number of these are being followed up at present, including a possible
abduction incident.
During the busy afternoon, crowds of onlookers in the vicinity of the UFO exhibition. They
were addressed by several speakers during the afternoon, including Matthew Williams, Les
Truman and Omar Fowler. A video presentation was shown continuously during the show
and one unique item presented by Matthew Williams, caused a great deal of interest.
Matthew announced that he was about to show for the first time in England, a piece of video,
purported to have been smuggled out of 'Area 51', showing an 'Alien' being interrogated.
Matthew has stated that the section of video came from Switzerland and sections have already
been published in 'Alien Encounters' magazine.
The video shots included, claimed TV shots of an Alien in a room, where an interrogator
stands behind the Alien, with his left hand under the unfortunate prisoners chin. The
interrogators right hand held a torch that was shining in the Aliens eyes. The Alien went into
some sort of spasm and looked extremely unwell!
The piece of video was shown after the shopping audience had been coaxed into giving more
money to the Children's Hospital Appeal. Then everyone waited for the sensational show!
There were a few seconds of blurred figures moving around (why was the interrogators faced
'hatched' out), a strange looking face with two large black eyes and then it was all over.
Matthew Williams ran the video through a second time, in case anyone missed the first
showing. The shoppers were duly non-plussed and wondered quite what it was all about. I
guess a high percentage had never heard of 'Area 5 1' or even Ray Santilli.....
Stills from the video have been printed on the back of Matthews 'Truth Seekers Review'
June/July 97 issue. Price £1. 70.
The 'Smiles Better' appeal had an original idea in producing a UFO exhibition for the shoppers.
It promoted a great deal of interest an organiser Belinda Thompson appeared to be suitably
impressed.
The UFO groups gave their services free and entered into the spirit of the afternoon. There
was local press coverage, with the photographer doing his utmost to coerce the exhibitors into
forming up for some light-hearted photographs (we drew the line at any inference to 'little
green men'!)
