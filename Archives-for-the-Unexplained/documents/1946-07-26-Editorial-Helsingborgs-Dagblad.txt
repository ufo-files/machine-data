July 26: Editorial

Another south-Swedish paper, Helsingborgs Dagblad, wrote on July 26:

”SWEDEN USED AS A SHOOTING RANGE” 

”Sweden is not directly affected by the sensational atomic experiments but we are troubled by "flying bombs" on Swedish territory.

”Experts at the Defence Staff have now declared that there is no doubt that at least a part of the flying objects are guided missiles. The Staff considers it important that all details regarding these missiles be compiled. It goes without saying that everything is done to reveal the origin of the missiles.

”Others, also claiming to be experts, have dissenting opinions about the flying objects. Among the sceptics is brigadier Sven Ramström who is well-informed concerning the investigation of a point of impact.

"I am totally convinced that the luminuous phenomena and the bombs that have been sighted lately in different areas of Sweden are nothing but meteorites and I am not going to change my conviction until something else has been proved".

”The astronomer and professor Knut Lundmark doubts that all of the objects are meteorites. It is true that meteorites sometimes appear in large numbers at certain times but normally they cannot be seen in daytime which is the case with those objects that are now appearing.

”Some of the "bombs" were probably meteorites and many others were figments caused by a "bomb psychosis" among the public. Also other flying objects than meteorites can be interpreted as "ghost bombs".

”A year or two ago we had many reports on "ghost flyers" on our territory. After careful investigations most of these observations had to be rejected but there were still many left, unexplained. That is probably the case also now. Some of the observations are of the nature that they cannot be explained as anything else than flying bombs. These were, as we remember, frequently observed here during the last phase of the war, launched by the Germans.

”It is a fact that Swedish air territory is being the target of a downright bombardment performed by a foreign nation. Sweden actually is used as a shooting range and as a guinea pig at trials with new weapons.

”If this is consistent or not with human rights and with correct foreign policy is obviously something that does not bother the sender of the flying bombs.
It has to be considered extremely rude by somebody to send a veritable swarm of missiles on some other nation.

”It cannot be the matter of a mistake or that the missiles are moving at a random course. However, they are probably not that reliable that the risk can be eliminated that they will crash, causing severe damage. That possibility does not seem to bother the sender, either.

”Swedish experts maintain that the missiles are radio controlled which reveals something about the actual level of technique. If there was a war going on the missiles could be sent off to any destination.
We ought to take the opportunity to try out methods of forcing these missiles down or affecting their trajectory. That is the only way we could benefit from this phenomenon, but perhaps we have not reached that far yet, technically.

”There is only one nation who is likely to have launched these bombs. England has probably also flying bombs but why should they launch them over Scandinavia? There are so many other areas that are more suitable, both on shore and at sea.

”It is not probable that England would start an activity like this in peace time without informing the foreign nation. However, it is common knowledge that German scientists are employed by Russia and that experiments are carried out at Peenemünde. Perhaps the bombs are originating from there, as during the German time.

”In any case this shows how little consideration is taken to small nations. We are treated with negligence. A firm standpoint on our side calls for a technical equipment which we probably do not have yet.

”Hopefully it won´t be long until we manage to catch one of the flying objects in which case many details will be clarified. At the moment 12 men are busy digging in the mud of a shallow lake for a bomb that has crashed there.

”One would assume that the Russians have already launched enough bombs at us and that they could now send them in a different direction.

”We now know that they possess such bombs and we assume that is what they wanted to show us.
