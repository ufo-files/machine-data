                              UFO PHOTOGRAPH EVALUATION
          In 19.54 a woman took a picture of a B-29 aircraft at her home near Kirtland
    AFB, New }:exico. When the film was developed, interesting patterns showed on the
    print. Aside from the airplane, the photograph contained a five sided ''object"
    and streaming light which partially hides the airplane ..
          In my analysis of the photograph, it is concluded that the pentagon shaped
    "object'' is a lens flare caused by the sun reflecting the diaphram through the
    camera lens. This is a common occurence when a camera is aimed too close to the
    sun. To further sustain this reasoning, there is a light area in the upper right
    hand corner of the picture, indicating that the sun was just barely out of view
    of the camera. With a little checking, I have found that the camera (Kodak Tourist
    Model) does have this type of diaphram and is five sided.
          The light which seems to be streaming from the five sided lens flare is not so
    easily explainable, and, therefore, more interesting. This light seems to originate
    from the lens flare and then diverge towards the lower left hand corner passing
    through the airplane. The light does not converge to a point on the "object" and
    does not emit entirely from within the object, but is shifted off center by about
    14~ of the object's width. It was,stated that this 14% shift is caused by the spaed
    of the object. I would have to disagree with this theory because if the object were
    traveling at a high enough velocity to cause this effect, the object would not have
    been captured by this camera. Also the edges of the object ara well defined, indi-
    cating very little or no speed at all. It is noted that the sides of the streaming
    light converges to a point just outside of the photograph at the point where the sun
    is believed to positioned. This is strong indication that the streaming light is
    also an optical effect caused by the sunlight passing through the lenso At the point
    where the light streaming is the narrowest, it is most clearly defined as opposed to
    distortions where it is the widest in the lower corner. The type of distortion is much
    like that of fluids at high velocity.
          Besides the foregoing, there are a couple of interesting aspects of this photo.
    A close observation reveals several dark spots within the "object".. I have no idea what
    these are. When the picture was taken, the photographer claims that the only thing
    she saw in the sky was the B-29 aircraft.
