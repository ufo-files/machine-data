Sammanställning av rapporter ang rymdprojektiler 22/9 - 29/10
Intill 25/10 föreligga 16 st rapporter. 13 iakttagelser ha gjorts under den mörka delen av dygnet. Av dessa äro 11 rapporter om enbart ljud- och ljudfenomen och 2 tro sig även ha sett projektiler.
3 st observationer har gjorts under dagen. Samtliga dessa rapportera iakttagelser av en projektil.
Nedslag har förmodats i två fall; dels vid Holmsund 4/10 och dels i sjö vid Kulltorp 12/10. Fragment från Holmsund har undersökts vid FOA, men icke givit positivt resultat. Fli Malmberg handhar undersökningarna vid Kulltorp, som pågå.
Kurserna ha varierat mellan sydlig - västlig - nordlig. En observation med nordöstlig kurs har gjorts vid Göteborg.
Samtliga observationer med kurserna utdragna mot den förmodade startplatsen äro inlagda på bifogade karta.
Höjderna 25-300 m. Farten hög (vid två observationer låg fart) Banan horisontell.
Vid mörkerobservationerna har ljusets färg beskrivits vanligen som vitt eller blåvitt, i ett par fall som gult eller rödgult. I sex fall har fenomenet följts av svans, i två fall utströmmade gasen stötvis, i tre fall exploderade fenomenet.
Vid de tre dagerobservationerna beskrevs projektilerna som aluminiumfärgade, cigarrformade, i ett fall med stjärtvingar, ½ m höga, inga ljusfenomen, i ett fall "töffande" ljud.

Compilation of reports regarding space projectiles 22/9-29/10
As of 25/10 there are 16 reports. 13 observations occurred during the dark part of the day. Of these 11 reports only regard light and sound phenomena, and 2 also believe to have seen projectiles.
3 observations have occured during the day. All of them report projectile observations.
In two cases impact has been assumed, at Holmsund 4/10 and in a lake at Kulltorp 12/10. Fragments from Holmsund has been investigated by FOA, but without positive results. Flight engineer Malmberg handle the investigations at Kulltorp.
The courses vary between south - west - north. An observation with northeasterly course have been made in Gothenburg.
All observations with courses drawn out towards the believed starting point are included on the attached map.
Heights 25-300m. High velocity (with two observations at low velocity) Horizontal course.
At observations after dark the color of light have been described as either white or blue white, in some cases yellow or red yellow. In six cases the phenomena has been followed by a tail, in two cases gas was emitted periodically, in three cases the phenomena exploded.
The three daytime observations described the projectiles as aluminum colored, cigar shaped, in one case with rear wings, ½ meter tall, no light phenomena, in one case "chugging" noise.
Of the included reports at least one could be discarded as implausible (Visby church, red and green light) while others could very possible be real.
Any connection between observations of where projectiles were constructed have not been seen.
