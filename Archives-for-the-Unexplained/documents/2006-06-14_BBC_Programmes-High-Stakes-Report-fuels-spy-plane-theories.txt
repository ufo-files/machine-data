Imagination
The blanked out sections might well contain a reference to Aurora but that
does not mean the plane definitely exists. Sweetman says the blanked out
sections "could be speculation but then why would they need to withhold
it?"
Elsewhere in the document in a section on exotic technologies is another
intriguing line. The DIS say "The projected (USAF) priority plan is to produce
unpiloted air-breathing aircraft with a Mach 8-12 capability and
transatmospheric vehicles." but it then continues "as well as highly
supersonic vehicles at Mach 4 to 6".
The MoD report will be seen by Aurora chasers as another clue to put with
unexplained sightings and mystery sonic booms but the Pentagon still insists
that Aurora is a figment of their imaginations.
