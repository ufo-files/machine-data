                         GENERAL DISCUSSION


    In 1977 and 1978 many UFO researchers quiried the u.s.
    Government for what ever information they might have on a
    Project Moon Dust.  I also asked for this information.

    Most of the documentation that was released under the terms
    of the Freedom of Information Act dealt with alleged fallen
    space debri.  However, one document released by the Office
    of Air Force Intelligence raised more questions than that
    agency was willing to answer. To the best of my knowledge
    this has been the only document released under FOIA making
    any reference to Operation Blue Fly.

    This document was once classified Secret (portions of this
    document are still classified). The document in question
    is dated November 13, 1961 and the subject of the letter
    was ''AFCIN Intelligence Team Personnel.'' The document was
    authored by a Colonel Betz of Air Force Intelligence.

    According to this once classified document, Project Moon
    Dust was a specialized aspect of the u.s. Air Force's
    over-all material exploitation program to locate, recover
    and deliver descended foreign space vehicles.   It is
    important to note at this point in this report that no
    reference is made to include UFO's as foreign space
    vehicles as part of Project Moon Dust. Therefore, one
    could conclude that we are talking about Soviet space craft
    and not UFO ' s. We shall see later in this report that this
    was not always the case.

    Operation Blue Fly, according to this document, was
    established to facilitate expeditious delivery to FTD
    (Foreign Technology Division) of Moon Dust and other items
    of great technical intelligence interest. This did include
    items such as debri of UFO ' s . Air Force Continental
    Intelligence Command (AFCIN) Standard Operating Procedures
    (SOP) dated February 12, 1961 for Blue Fly Operations makes
    it clear that objects or debri believed to be from objects
    of unknown orgin (UFO's) were to be included in Blue Fly
    operations as these items were considered to be of great
    technical intelligence interest to the U.S. Air Force
    Intelligence Command. This SOP is still classified.
    However, the u.s. Air Force can prove me wrong about the
    UFO issue. All the Air Force has to do is release this SOP
    uncensored.  Right?

I decided to appeal the Air Force's denial of the release

I forwarded my appeal request to the Air Force on May 18 ,
1990. I based my appeal on the basis of the only known
document to surface making any reference to Blue Fly was 29
years old and sure l y information that old could not have
any intelligence value worthy of classification. That is ,
of course , unless the u.s. Government was trying to hide
something . I bel ieve this to be the case and I shall

                         Conclusion



Project Moon Dust and Operation Blue Fly were programs set
up by the United States Air Force for the recovery, field
exploitation and delivery of Soviet/Bloc space vehicles and
objects of unknown origin.

If the Air Force recovered Soviet/Bloc space craft or space
craft of any other nation they must under International
agreement return them, the craft and/or debri , to the
country from which the craft originated . There cou ld e xist
a real need for the clas sifi cation of information dealing
with this area . However , I seriously doubt that security
could or would be damaged by the release of in f ormation
that is 20 to 30 years old or older.

However, if the the Air Force was to have recovered an
Alien space craft from another solar system 20 , 30 or even
40 years ago, then for reasons known only to those having
e x cess to that information , there may be cause for
classifying this information and keeping it from the
public .   I believe this to be the case and I further
believe that the U. S. Government is trying to conceal what
it knows about the Alien space crafts our government have
recovered.    I feel this is the reason for the
classification of information concerning Operation Blue Fly .

We may never known the truth. The information the u.s.
Government maintains o n Operation Blue Fly remains, still
classified in the interest of national security .
