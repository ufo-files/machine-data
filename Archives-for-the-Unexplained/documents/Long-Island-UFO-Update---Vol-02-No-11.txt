                GULF BREEZE, FLORIDA SIGHTINGS CONTINUE.

GULF BREEZE FLORIDA, SEPTEMBER~ 1991: Since January 1991,
over the Pensacola Bay Bridge, witnesses have reported observing
a large red light which has appeared in the early evening hours
and has performed unusual aerial maneuvers. U.F.O. Researchers
from the Mutual U.F.O. Network have been investigating the
incidents and have also been able to obtain photographs of the
nightly visitor. On the night of September 6th, 1991 some 20
witnesses gathered at the end of the Bay Bridge and were treated
to a spectacular sighting of a large U.F.O. at 8:20 P.M. that
night. The object appeared on a South to North course heading
at a 60 degre~ elevation. The witnesses reported seeing the
object and a large ring of porthole lights surrounding the
object. The object was brightly illuminated and was so large
that when it stopped it blocked out the night stars. At one
point during the four minute sighting the object came to a stop
and performed an incredible barrel roll while it hovered. It
then proceeded to the North, turned vertically and accelerated
up out of sight. Two nights later on September 8th, 1991, another
crowd of witnesses reported observing another large U.F.O. with ·
porthole lights blazing again over the Bay Bridge. The object
was photographed and video taped on this occasion.
 (The Sentinel, Gulf Breeze Florida, Sept. 12, 1991)

GULF BREEZE, FLORIDA, SEPTEMBER~ 1991: Witnesses reported
the presence of a large U.F.O. and a smaller one on the night
of September 10, 1991 in the vicinity of the Bay Bridge again.
Beginning at 8:20 P.M. that night witnesses reported observing
a large red light appearing in the sky which was accompanied

horizon and was located over Old Navy Cove in Gulf Breeze. The
witnesses consisted mostly of MUFON Investigators who reported
that photographs were taken and were to be analyzed for
scientific study.
 ( The Sentinel, Gulf Breeze Florida, September 12, 1991

               BOOK REVIEW: U.F.O. CRASH AT ROSWELL
              BY KEVIN RANDLE AND DONALD R. SCHMITT
                     AVON BOOKS, 1991, $4.95

      This past spring saw the publication in paperback of one
of the most important books to have published in the year of
1991. That book is "U.F.O. CRASH AT ROSWELL" by the co-authors
of Kevin Randle and Donald Schmit~which is one of the most
authoritative works published on the incident to date. It
eclipses the work of the "Roswell Incident" which appeared in
1979 under the pen of Charles Berlitz and William Moore, which
brought the incident back into public attention.
      It was first reported by ill-fated author Robert Scully
in his pioneering work on U.F.O.'s in the 1950's along with
the Aztec Crash. Duped by deliberate hoaxers, Scully was publicly
ruined and ridiculed by his association with the Aztec Hoax
which also threw the Roswell Incident into disrepute. In the
mid seventies, Stanton Friedman reactivated interest into the
incident by meeting with witnesses who told a different factual
tale. Researchers took it from there.
      To date this book brings together more information and
witnesses than the original effort into the incident had done.
It does go over the information a lot of the original sources
gave, but adds more information from new sources.
      The book reveals a lot more, like this:
1) There was definitely a cover up of something other than a
weather balloon crash at Roswell. The government file remains
closed while the government refuses still to comment on the
story.
2) There were definitely two objects. One which crashed on the
Brazel ranch which may have been a robot craft which had rammed
the second piloted craft causing that one to crash on the plains
of st. Augustine in the New Mexican Desert. These two craft
were retrieved by the military under top secret conditions.
 3) There was an immediate attempt to cover up the facts of the
 incident by the Air Force and .the Government even to retrieving
all copies of the original Army Air Foroe press release and
 the closing down of the news wire services printing of the
 report.
 4) Numerous civilian and military witnesses reported to
 researchers that there was something taken from the Brazel ranch
which was not a Weather Balloon, but which was something other
 than what was reported. The metal fragments were of something
 not seen before on this Earth as substantiated by Col. Jesse
 Marcel and his adult son.
 5) The Center for u.F.O. Studies financed further research into
 the case and also financed an unsuccessful archaeological dig
 into the field where the first object crashed on the Brazel
 Ranch. This was a first in U.F.O. History in which a major
 research organization sponsored a scientifi~ expedition to
 unearth U.F.O. artifacts.
       The book reveals a lot more information which makes this
 short review incapable of discussing. This is a must for the
 serious researcher and makes excellent reading. Put it on your
 must read book list for 1992 or a Christmas present list for
 someone who will appreciate it.
