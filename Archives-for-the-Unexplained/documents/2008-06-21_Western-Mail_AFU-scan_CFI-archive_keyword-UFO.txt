UFO sightings in Wales
• A 100ft high green and blue triangle was seen over
Pant Robert, Powys, in May, plus "pink pulsating
lights".
• Two clusters of amber, orange and white lights in
triangular formations, were spotted near Port Talbot.
• Last year one holidaymaker in Cardiff claimed to
have seen "spaceships" and that little green men had
"abducted his dog, car and tent."
• A silent bright orange globe-shaped light was seen
by a Bridgend woman in February 2007.
• A pensioner reported seeing cigar-shaped lights in
the sky, in March 2006, moving down Garth Mountain.
