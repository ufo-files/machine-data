August 13: Flying fortresses in successful remote-control flight

Östersunds-Posten reported, August 13: 

PILOTLESS FLIGHT HAWAII-CALIFORNIA. Two American radio-controlled flying fortresses recently completed a more than 4000 km long flight from Hawaii to California. It was the longest pilotless ever performed by bombers. Above [picture with article] we can see three remote-controlled flying fortresses tested in flight, here controlled from a mothership which operate at a distance of more than 80 kilometres. These airplanes have taken part in the photographing of the nuclear bomb at Bikini.”
