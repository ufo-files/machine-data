     The target was busily preoccupied with pouring over a set of
maps. He was setting on a stool-like chair and the maps appears to
rest upon a table similar to a large drafting table with a tilting
top. He wore black rimmed reading glasses and was smoking a very
short and fat cigarette.       He was wearing western clothing
consisting of a light, short sleeve dress shirt with the collar
opened and grayish, well pressed pants. The pants looked expensive
and had a sharp crease.

     The maps depicted a set of islands and I think they were
navigational or shipping charts; there were all sorts of lines with
corresponding numbers, compass-like figures super imposed upon the
maps and numbers/contour lines in a light color (I felt these may
have been water depth, or something like that) .
     There was a solid piece of land along the right side of the
maps; my impression was that this was the western shore line of a
large land mass i.e. North America. There was some sort of warning
associated with the area around one of the islands.     The target
individual seemed particularly interested in this area, and the
