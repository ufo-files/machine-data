EXPOSE OF THE MONTH:

      Crashed disc devotee William Steinman has recently hit a new low in
ufological ethics - no doubt taking a cue from his former mentor, Todd
Zechel. Some time ago we sent Steinman the original typed notes from our
1953 personal interview with the late Frank Scully (author of the 1950
classic "Behind the Flying Saucers") . It was clearly understood that
the notes were for Steinman's information only, to aid him in his alleged
research. The notes contained, among other things·, our personal apprais­
al of Scully, as being a hypocritical Catholic and a possible ufological
hoaxer. Now, Steinman has admitted to us that he has forwarded these pri­
vate notes of ours to Frank Scully's elderly widow, with whom Steinman
is in correspondence. Thus he has deliberately caused needless annoyance
to her and embarrassment to us. What possible "research" purpose is serv­
ed by this, we ask! ? All we can say is that we will never again be so free
in sharing our private material with people of Steinman's ilk!
