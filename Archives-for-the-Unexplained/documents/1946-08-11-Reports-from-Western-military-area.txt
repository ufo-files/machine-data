Western Military Area
Military Officers Staff
Department I e
August 12, 1946.


To the Head of Division at the air defense department.


The following reports include information on observations of light and sound phenomena (all made on the eleventh of August) and have been added to our archives as of August 12.
 
1. Reporter: the Arvika sheriff.
Observers: Hunting party of four people.
Locality: 1.5 km north-northwest of St. Salungens northern tip (20 km north-northeast of Arvika).
Duration: 10 seconds.
Point in time: 15:00 o’clock till 15:30 o’clock.

Three audio phenomena, sounding reminiscent of an internal combustion engine. Low to middle cloud base; nothing could be seen. The objects seemed to travel in relatively close proximity to each other and wouldn’t have stayed unseen had it been a case of ordinary airplanes.

2. Reporter: the Arvika sheriff.
Observers: The railway clerk, among others.
Locality: Krokebol (at the southwestern part of lake Ränken).
Point in time: shortly after 21:00 o'clock.
Direction: to the east of Ränken’s southern end.
Northwesterly course.

Flaming red puff of smoke, surrounded by a black ring. Great speed. No sound.

3. Reporter: The Grums sheriff.
Observer: Mr. Brolin.
Locality: a few kilometers west of Varpnäs (15 kilometers west of Karlstad).
Point in time: about 20:30 o'clock.
Direction: east, straight across Varpnäs.
Northerly course.

Fireball with tail.

4. Observer/reporter: Styckjunkare Pedersén, Sörmon. Refer to the attached report.

5. Reporters and observers: warehouse assistant G. Fernquist and cement worker J. Fernquist, Skåre.
Locality: Skåre station (6 kilometers northwest of Karlstad).
Point in time: around 21:00 o'clock.
Duration: about 1 second.
Direction: east.
Northerly course.

Strong whitish light, crackling like a sparkler at an altitude of 400-500 meters. Not quite as fast as a shooting star. Horizontal path.

6. Reporters and observers: Foreman S. Andersson, Västerås. Agriculture Student V. Hamsen, Norway.
Observation site: the viaduct, 2 kilometers west of Karlstad.
Point in time: at around 20:46 o’clock.
Duration: 30-40 seconds.
Direction: east.
Northwesterly course.

Dark object with a tail giving off sparks and smoke. Descending trajectory. No sound.

7. Reporters and observers: Office Assistant S. G. Käll, laboratory assistant A. Blomquist and errand boy Y.E. Olsen, Karlstad.
Locality: directly north of the I 2 barracks, Karlstad.
Point in time: at around 20:46 o'clock.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Northwesterly course.

Conical object with a blue and yellowish white tail. Descending trajectory. No sound.

8. Observer/reporter: Fanjunkare Edvinsson, I 2, Karlstad.
Locality: Directly north of the I 2 barracks.
Point in time: at around 20:47 o'clock.
Duration: 15 to 20 seconds.
Northwesterly course.

A suddenly emerging bright red, rectangular glow, which after a few seconds became whiter, more intense and slightly enlarged, after which it died out, as if it had been incinerated and completely annihilated. There were no observations of fog or smoke. Altitude at about 400 meters, slightly descending trajectory. Interceptor-like speed.

9. Observer/reporter: Merchant M. Broman, Karlstad.
Locality: Karlstad.
Point in time: between 20:30 and 21:00 o'clock.
Duration: 2-3 seconds.
Direction: east.
Northerly course.

Strong, clear, almost white light, crackling like sparklers, hastily traveling at an altitude of about 500 meters whilst emanating puffs of smoke. Descending trajectory.

10. Observer/reporter: Farmer T. Jansson, Toverud.
Locality: Toverud on Hammarön, 8.5 km south-southeast of Karlstad.
Point in time: at around 21:00 o'clock.
Direction: to the east along Hammaröns eastside.
Northerly course.

Reddish, shiny, big and round object. Exploded and eventually disappeared. Traveled along a horizontal path at low velocity.

11. Observer/reporter: Policeman Löved, Olme.
Locality: Sikterud (12 kilometers northwest Kristinehamn).
Point in time: at around 20:47 o'clock.
Duration: 10 to 12 seconds.
Direction: east.
Northwesterly course.

A small glowing orb giving off sparks, or flakes.

12. Reporter: Nyed’s civil defense chief.
Observers: a larger number of persons.
Localities: Blombacka mill (4 kilometers east of Molkom) and Lake Lilla Byeholm (8 kilometers northeast of Molkom).
Point in time: about 20:30 o'clock.
Direction: east.
Northerly course.

Soundless, yellow-green object flying at low altitude.

13. Reporter: the Filipstad sheriff.
Observer: a constable belonging to the community of Brattfors.
Locality: Brattfors church.
Point in time: at 20:48 o'clock.
Direction: east.
Northerly course.

Bright object, similar to a water drop in shape. Its tail had given off a shower of sparks. No smoke. No sound.
