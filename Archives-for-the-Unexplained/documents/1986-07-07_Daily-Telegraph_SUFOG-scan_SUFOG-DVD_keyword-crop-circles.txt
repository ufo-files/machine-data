PUZZLE OF
MYSTERY
CORN CIRCLES
In the quiet of a Hampshire
field, the phenomenon of pre-

cle's outer edge.

England and overseas, have

from space, freak weather con-
ditions, druid ceremonies or
electro-magnetic fields.
