  Chort, you and I agree on most everything. Lear and company can really send those of us with common sense, reason
  and logic over the edge at times. We demand proof, evidence, something more tangible than this subject can often
  supply. So we're stuck in the realm of debating ideas and claims we know full well could never be proven beyond the
  shadow of a doubt. What I miss, is the shear delight of discussing those things with Zorg (not so much John). Those
  threads were very fun, I delighted in checking those on a daily basis.

  I think sometimes you need to throw enough s^~t at the wall to see what sticks. Not so much s^~t as ATS or OM, but
  enough s^~t to give us something to sift through. Call us Ufology Scatalogists if you will.

  As for ATS selling products, is anyone surprised? On one hand I love the amount of attention ATS has the ability to
  bring to certain subjects both now and in the future, however I am saddened that most idle curiosity from folks on such
  subjects will lead them to thread with 8 pages of people talking about the Grey they have in their closest or some new
  age sleeper-type alien dribble that is obviously BUNK. Once the normal folks see stuff like that, their preconceived
  notions about Ufology "nutjobs" are verified, and they go back to what they were doing, not bothering to pursue the
  subject any further.

  It's this perpetuation of a stereotype that bothers us all, isn't it? I think it would be great if ATS tempered the thread
  titles, so they are not as sensational, and demanded actual, intelligent debate, so the average person stopping in would
  see the community policing itself with common sense, reason and logic. Then, they might stick around long enough to
  join the ranks of the informed and curious, necessitating the eventual in-depth coverage by the mainstream. Who knows.

  I do know one thing, long after the last woo woo posts his or her bunk, it will take years for those of us with common
  sense, reason and logic to wipe the stigma of idiocy from Ufology and the like. Until then, we fight the good fight, sift
  through the poo, and sometimes come up with a diamond in the rough that gives us reason to continue doing what we
  do.
