      Saucer contactee Howard Menger, author of "From Outer Space to You," nas changed
his ideas considerably since writing the book in 1959.   The first public knowledge of
this came when Menger appeared  on one of Long John Nebel' s television shows a few years
ago, and shocked everyone ·by saying he was no longer sure about his own contacts.
      Now, in an open letter to Colonel Jordan, City Clerk of Sebastian, Florida, where
Menger now lives, - Menger sounds very different indeed from the old days.  He insists
that h�s book henceforth be labeled "fact/fiction" rather than be accepted as factual.
      Long-time readers of SAUCER NEWS will recall that Dr. Leon Davidson has propounded
the idea that UFOs are a hoax perpetrated by the Central Intelligence Agency.  The fol•
lowing key passages from Menger's letter to Colonel Jordan sound like he is expressing a
view very similar to Dr. Davidson's:
      " I wrote my book based on an actual experience, which I accidentally stumbled upon
in an isolated field to the rear of my property in Highbridge, N.J. These photographs I
sent to a department in the Pentagon.    It wasn't long afterwards that I was contacted and
asked 1f I would co-operate in an experiment based on what I had inadvertently seen, and
project into this experience an expanded futuristic view of 'things to come,• with their
help and information.   ( I suppose they must have figured the only way to obtain my silence
and co-operation was to put me on the 'team.' )   In other words, the 'Highbridge Incident.•
      "The Highbridge Incident was used as a gauge to indicate the people's reaction to
alien contact.   You will think this is not too strange when you consider that our astro­
nauts today are being thoroughly conditioned and trained to meet any and every strange
condition or situation they may face upon landing on an alien worldl    •• • •



       "Bearing this in mind, let us suppose they superimpose a hypothetical condition
( based on truth ) - like a spacecraft landing in an obscure country town, and some alien
creatures disembarking, giving messages of good will by a local yokel acting as 'contactee1
• •••This would be one way of getting an index of human reaction.   Of course, I could go on
at length, but I assume by now you have the point established  ••••



      ., I agreed to co-operate in the above experiment, and when the press picked up the
story of flying saucers over Highbridge ( and they were there and the people did see them;
don't ask me how or why ) , they really had a b all.  They worked it up into a real sensation.
However, all the publicity did not help me, my work, or my family, as I had little or no
privacy left.    But this was all part of it.   I had to meet and talk to people.  At first I
was a little shy and more or less tongue-tied, but after many lectures I gained in confi­
dence, and with the material and suggestions handed to me, I was able to carry on  ••••


      "After the 'Highbridge I ncident' had been widelY publicized and the experiment was
considered finished, I completely withdrew from all activities in saucer research. I re­
fused to give lectures, or show movies, or speak to anyone about it.     I had completed my
mission. I wanted peace."
      Then, in a recent letter to Gray Barker, Menger had this to say:
      ''At present I'm working on another book, that will drop a few 'bombs' in certain
places. No one can deny the documented evidence presented in this hard-hitting book.    I
might be threatened, or bribed by people in high places, but 11m going through with it,
come what may!   I must have the courage of my own convictions.  I'm sure you and possibly
Jim Moseley and Leon Davidson suspect some hidden big story lurks in the Government shadows,
and it's true• •• •



      "Here is an excerpt from my new book, which is called 'What Happened at 1-iighbridge':
'A specialized element of Government intelligence, located in a well-guarded room of the
Pentagon, has for several years spent 24 hours a day compiling documented, indisputable
facts on the known existence of extraterrestrial beings coming and going at will, from
outer space to our planet Earth.   These beings are not, as yet, known to be hostile to
the people of Earth.  They far exceed Earth-born people in scientific, spiritual, and
social development.'"
      Menger goes on to say that he is ready and willing to come up to New York at any
time, to publicize his new book as well as his old book, provided that the latter is clear­
ly understood to be "fact/fiction."
      All we can add is this:   It takes courage for a contactee or anyone else to change
his story.  Although the editors of SAUCER NEWS tend to disbelieve all saucer contact
stories, we must admit that Menger is apparently sincere.   We eagerly await further de­
velopments in this strange case.
