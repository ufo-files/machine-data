            The only thing I signed was for Colonel Halt. I didn’t type anything out. Maybe someone else did it and asked me to sign it. (…) I don’t even remember what I signed. I was so nervous, I just

            with lights running around its belt from left to right. They were blue, white and red lights, flashing, sometimes rapid, sometimes slow. Then we saw flakes of metal coming from it.

            We got debriefed for hours also! And I know we got injections… I think now more than ever we were not supposed to remember certain things…. [Cavanasac mentions the name of an
