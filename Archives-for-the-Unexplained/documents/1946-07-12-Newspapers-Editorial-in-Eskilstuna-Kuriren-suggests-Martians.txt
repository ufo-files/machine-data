July 12: Visitors from Mars?

Eskilstuna-Kuriren (liberal) published the following editorial comment on July 12:

”UNWELCOME  RAIN OF STARS. Imaginative citizens have come up with the idea that the fireballs with tails, now seen all over the country in great numbers, emanate from the red planet Mars, which thus, after millions of years should have been caught by a sudden interest in us. If this was the case, and if the planet of the War God had peaceful intentions, it must be said that the citizens of the welfare country [Sweden] are partly pleased that the Martians are courting us, partly impressed over their accuracy in firing. It is a minimum of five million [Swedish] miles from the Earth to Mars and Sweden is a small aiming point at such a distance. 

”More down-to-earth thinkers have been attracted to [the fact] that the mysterious heavenly bodies for the main part seem to follow the same trajectory, and that this, drawn towards the origin, clearly point to a coast, wherefrom German V-bombs were already sent out during the war. Some lines go towards Stolp and Kolberg, ex-German, now Polish towns, other towards Hela, the anxiously restricted, by Russians still occupied tongue of land, which screens off the Danziger Bucht, and yet others towards Kaliningrad, the old Königsberg.

”Therefrom they draw the conslusion that the luminous objects are of an earthly origin. And then it is not that funny any more. We have tolerated much, but to serve as an artillery range and target board for V-weapons of a mysterious construction does not please us at all. It must be viewed as a decidedly unfriendly action, or at least as a criminal ignorance, to treat our oblong, peaceful nation in this way. When we now shall receive goods from the east [the trade agreement with Russia], we would rather see that they are brought here in more ortodox ways and arrive in working order. We have no use for exploding fireballs. 

”The Swedish government should, in other words, tell it as it is, that if it is peace then it is peace, and that target shooting of this kind does not excite peaceful feelings. We are not easily irritable but we have, on the other hand, not had the time to accomodate to the new forms of social intercourse in Europe, and have no disposition to accept them. There must be a stop very soon for this rain of stars!”
