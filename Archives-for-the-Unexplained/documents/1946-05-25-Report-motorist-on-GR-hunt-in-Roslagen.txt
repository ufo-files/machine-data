Case xxx.
Motorist on ”rocket hunt” in Roslagen

Time: May 25, about 18.30 hours
Place: Road between Uppsala and Rasbo, county of Uppsala 

”GHOST ROCKET  HUNTED  BY CAR THROUGH ROSLAGEN”. The wing- and noiseless ghost flier continues to bewilder [people] here and there in the country. This morning a painter told Aftonbladet that he, Saturday evening, hunted a mysterious fireball for five kilometres in his car. The fireball was faster and disappeared - but during seven minutes the painter, and his company could view the phenomenon. 

”-- Mysterious it was, indeed, he says. We came from Uppsala and were going to Rasbo [about 15-20 km to the NE of Uppsala] and it was almost 18.30 when someone in the car suddenly pointed to the sky and said: - Look at those flashes from the airplane! We looked in that direction. A few hundred meters to the right of the road, and at an altitude of only one hundred meters, a strange thing flew which could neither be meteor nor airplane. The closest thing  would be to describe it as a drop-shaped sphere made of some kind of polished metal. No wings were visible. 

”I drove at a speed of 70 km/h and since the rocket, or whatever it was, had the same direction [as us] we could follow it for five kilometers or about seven minutes. The object´s speed wasn´t great, we estimated it at about a hundred km/h. Finally it disappeared behind the horizon. Only yesterday [Monday] evening did I read in the newspapers that people had seen fireballs here and there as early as on Thursday night. Thus, before I met the strange sight myself I had no knowledge of the phenomenon, ends the painter.”

This case is noted in the Defence Staff´s report tabulations, with Aftonbladet (AB) as the source. There is no evidence, from military files, of any attempt to make a follow-up investigation. The name of the painter is thus unknown. 

Status: Insufficient information, possibly airplane
