    Our Review                                                                                        Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  A quick glance through this book suggests it would be an interesting read, although it certainly doesn't
  match the hype on the book jacket. Contrary to the jacket, there is no evidence that the FBI maintained
  systematic files on UFOs. Instead, FBI files show that the agency did track some UFO claimants like
  George van Tassel and passed a few UFO cases to the Air Force. The book includes a number of FBI
  documents we haven't seen before. These are interesting mainly as history, not as evidence of alien life or
  government cover-up. -- Glenn Campbell
