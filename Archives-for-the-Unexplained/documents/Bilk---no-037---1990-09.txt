LEVIATHAN: • facific Ocean: According to a report in the American tabloid Weekly
World News, reprinted in Fortean Times 54, p.34, an Indonesian trawler has
caught the dorsal fin                    (73 feet long!) of a 300 ft long icthyosaur. The Surabaya
was trawling over the Marianas trench, when they caught the "section" of the

USO: The excellent new book by Paul Devereux,          Earth Lights Revelatio�has been
published as paperback by         Blandford (Villiers House,      41147 Strand,    London
WC2N 5JE,    Britain). The book coveres all kinds of ghost lights,           including many
examples of lights over the sea and over lakes. A very interesting chapter deals
with Scottish "earth lights",          many of them were observed at Loch Leven,        which
is also a monster haunt. As I have speculated before,             lights and monsters are
two expressions of the elemantal forces of bodies of water throughout the
world. Today,      the dragon is interpreted as living animal,        while the ghost light
is turned into a flying saucer. Now I generally assume that at the bottom of
these myths lie misinterpretations and legends,          but Paul Devereux makes a very
convincing case for the existance of a genuine unknown phenomenon,                 the "earth
light". The book sells for 7.95 Pounds,          and I recommend it to any fortean and
cryptozoologist enthusiast who tries to understand the phenomena we deal with
in a broader context.
