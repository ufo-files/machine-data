The Shanghai Daily - Shanghai, China…
12 April, 2007…
Expert: V-shaped 'UFO' Is A Kite…

   An unidentified flying object seen by many people last night over Shanghai and reported by local media was a night-light kite, eastday.com reported today, citing an expert from Sheshan  Observatory.
   The 'UFO' appeared in the night sky over the boundary of Hongkou and Yangpu districts around 9pm. The color of the V-shaped object changed regularly from red to blue to green and then yellow. Many people were convinced it was a UFO and kept watching it.
   Many witnesses claimed it was flying higher than an airplane.
   Tang Haiming, an expert with Sheshan Observatory, said the object could be seen in a small area, which means its altitude was not as high as some witnesses claimed.
   "If it is an aircraft, it should have a flying route rather than floating in the sky," Tang said.
   Tang said there were two kinds of night-light kites, one carrying materials that can reflect light and another equipped with light-emitting diodes. Some of the kites can fly as high as  1,000 meters.
   An official with China's aviation authority urged residents not to put lights on kites and to keep the kites below 1,000-meter high, or the sight of pilots will be affected.
