                    John Lennon, Uri Geller abducted by aliens
     From: "Stig Agermose" <wanderer@post8.tele.dk>
     Date: Tue, 13 Oct 1998 18:32:20 -0800


  In a Nov 2, 1996 story in the Weekly News (UK), psychic Uri Geller
  claims that John Lennon told him he had been abducted by
  aliens from his Manhattan apartment...
  The story is found on Geller's site at:
  http://www.tcom.co.uk/hpnet/megg.htm
  Very much to the point Geller attributes his psychic powers
  to his own abduction when he was four. The story is told in
  the Jewish Telegraph 12th June 1998, URL:
  http://www.tcom.co.uk/hpnet/jt1.htm
  Excerpt:
  *******
  Aged four I saw the light
  (snipped)
  "When I was four years old, an inexplicable urge led me
  into a deserted Arabic garden in Tel Aviv, and something
  happened to me there which might be explained by the words,
  'Alien Abduction'. I saw a light, a shining disc in the
  sky. The light touched me. A figure stood before me, and it
  was not a human figure. Then I lost consciousness. When I
  came to, many hours had passed. I ran home to tell my
  mother and, of course, she did not believe me. But I can
  date the emergence of my powers from that day."
