                                            NEWSLETTERS
        Newsletters are distributed monthly at meetings . These contain important points derived
        from the National Newsletter as well as news and items pertaining directly to Mass .
        MUFON . A sample copy of the Newsletter is enclosed at the back of this report.


                                            MEETINGS
        Meetings are held on the second Saturday eveni ng of every month at the Beverly Golf
        and Tennis Club , Beverly , MA. These are dinner meetings , held in a special function
        room, where a dinner is served at 6: 15p.m. and the meeting follows the dinner at
        7: 45 p.m. , eeting agendas have included such items as case reports , guest speakers ,
        videotapes , slide-presentations, discussions, working meetings, updates on newly
        released documents , and investigator- training sessions .
.-· ~
                                             HOTLINE
        We have maintained a telephone hotline for severa l years now, and it has been highly
        successful, not only in gathering reports from witnesses, or in providing the public
        with a place to call for information on the UFO subject , but also for providing
        our own Section Directors with a method for leaving one another messages during the
        week . Three or four persons check the Hotline throughout the day and evening every
        day , so that they are able to get any messages left for them by other Section Directors
        or by the State Director or Assistant State Director. The hotline is located at
        Mass. MUFON headquarters, 8 Maple Street , Reading , MA 01867 .

                                       POLICIES OF MASS. MUFON

            MEMBERSHIP APPLICATIONS:

                · Persons submitting applications
                                     ,   I
                                                  for membership in MUFON shall be required
            to submit in writing a resume , with references, as well as a letter detailing
            reasons for desiring to join MUFON. Their applications will be forwarded to
            their respective State Section Directors, who will interview the applicants,
            and then make their recommendation to the State Director, who will then notify
            MUFON Hdqtrs. in Seguin, Texas of our recommendation regarding the particular
            applications.


            ATTENDANCE AT MUFON MEETINGS

                 Attendance at Mass. MUFON meetings is restricted to members and their
            guests only. Guests must be accompanied by members.


            FIElD INVESTIGATORS:

                  Field investigators will be pr9perly trained by State Section Directors,
            and will be accompanied on investigations by experienced investigators until
            they gain a reasonable amount of experience. Investigators are required to
            adhere to the guidelines in the Field Investigator's Manual, and to exhibit
            the highest degree of professionalism at all times. Field investigators are
            encouraged to draw upon the various backgrounds · and variety of areas of
            expertise of the other members of the group for assistance and advice. In
.,.,        all cases in which thorough investiga t ion results in labeling an object as an
            "unknown" or in more complex cases, such as abduction cases or trace cases,
            the investigator or investigative team is required to submit a report on the
            case, which adheres to the format stated in the manual. Simply completing
            forms or tapes of interviews is not acceptable.                           ~.
                  In the distribution of cases, it is expected that qualified field
            investigators will accept cases assigned to them unless there are extenuating
            circumstances which prevent them from doing so. Persons who consistently refuse
             to accept new cases will be notified that they will be dropped from the ranks . of
            field investigators.


            CASE DISCUSSIONS AT MEETINGS:

            in order to offer a learning experience · for all of us. Case discussions at meet ings
            shall preserve the confidentiality of all witnesses, however, and shall not divulge
            names or addresses or telephone numbers of witnesses. · Locations and~ates of
            sightings may be used for identification purposes .

NEWSPAPER COVERAGE
As a resu lt of the 1985 Mass. MUFON Forum, a number of Northshore newspapers covered
the events of the forum (copies of clippings enclosed). The Boxboro , MA sightings
were covered by the Middlesex News , Boston Herald, and others in the Middlesex and
Worcester Counties . These sightings also made the National wires (see clippings) .
The Field Investigator Seminar was covered by the Patriot Ledger (a South Shore Newspaper)
and the Berkshire Eagle (see clippings). The 1986 Forum press release and press release
for UFO Information Week were sent to newspapers in every county in Massachusetts.

          The Mutual UFO Network, Inc . is an international scientific organization,
     comprised of people seriously interested in researching the UFO phenomenon by
     combining their mutual talents, areas of expertise, and investigative efforts.
     MUFON's administrative staff is assisted by ita International Coordinator and
     Foreign Liaison Representatives as well as its vast network of State Directors,
     Provincial Directors, State Section Directors, Consultants, Staff, and Field
     Investigators. MUFON has volunteered its support and cooperation to the
     Center for UFO Studies, as a field investigating team. The Center for UFO
     Studies, under the direction of Dr. J. Allen Hynek, former Consultant to the
     United States Air Force (on UFO's), is a free association of scientists,
     engineers, and other professionals.

          MUFON's Board of Consultants, most of whom are PhD's or MD's, representing
     45 areas of science, technology, medicine, psychology, psychiatry, engineering,
     communications, political science, photo analysis, etc., are readily available
     as an advisory group to apply their expertise to UFO cases under study and to
     conduct advanced research in their respective fields.

          MUFON annually sponsors an International UFO Symposium, where
     internationally known scientists, engineers, and authors lecture on their
     particular specialization or contributions to resolving this perplexing
     scientific dilemma. In 1981, Massachusetts hosted the annual MUFON UFO
·~   Symposium, which was held at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.

          MUFON Field Investigators investigate sighting reports from police
     agencies, as well as those received by the Center for UFO Studies hotline,
     and from the National UFO Reporting Center in Seattle, Washington. Some
     states have state hotlines as well. In Massachusetts, the MUFON hotline
     telephone number is 944-0686. Since MUFON is the · largest and most
     extensive network of UFO field investigators, this tooperative effort has
     been mutually beneficial to all involved.
                                                                              •
          In order that only qualified, competent~ .. and sincere people may become
     involved, membership in MUFON is by invitation only. Field investigators
     are trained, and high standards of professionalism are required by MUFON.
     As objective, scientific researchers, MUFON field investigators uphold the
     confidentiality of witnesses and case information. MUFON investigators are
     volunteers who conduct research at their own expense, and attempt to document
     sighting information in a thorough, unbiased manner. Access to large data
     banks in computers aids MUFON investigators in analyzing data derived from
     cases.

          In most sighting investigations, the actual investigative proeess
     involves a telephone interview with the witness, followed by an in-depth
     personal interview during which time forms will be completed, sketches of
     the object sighted will be requested, and compass readings, or photographs
     of the location of the sighting may be taken. The investigator will then
     attempt to document the details of the sighting, and to attempt to identify
     the object sighted. In approximately 85% of the cases, the object is able
     to be identified. In the remainder of the cases, the sighting will be listed
     as an unidentified flying object, and further research will be involved.

                In order to build and maintain the credibility of the Mutual UFO Network , and
           of the UFO subject, the following guidelines must be stringently adhered to:

           I.    PROVIDING PUBLIC INFORMATION AS AN OFFICIAL REPRESENTATIVE OF MUFON

                 A.   Since MUFON is a UFO research organization, any lectures, presentations, or
                      media appearances must be limited to the UFO subject. While the paranormal
                      or other borderline subject matter may bave a relationship with the UFO
                      phenomenon, it is not wise to present such a vast amount of varied informa-
                      tion to an uninformed public in a limited amount of time such as a one-hour
                      presentation or radio or television interview. Such a situation merely
                      serves to cause confusion for the audience and to erode our credibility.

                 B.   Providing inaccurate, undocumented, incomplete, or exaggerated information
                      to the public or to the media, printed or electronic, must be avoided.

                 C.   Speakers must be prepared with the facts and supporting data on UFO cases
                      and on the various aspects of the UFO subject. The bottom line is: DO
                      YOUR HOMEWORK before you discuss the UFO subject publicly, and, in particular,
                      before making any radio or television appearances or giving a newspaper
                      interview. Do not be afraid to admit that you do not know an answer. Never
                      fabricate one. Avoid letting tv or radio talk - show hosts intimidate you or
                      cause you to be on the defensive. Be ready to counteract their charges and
                      their misinformation.

                 D.   When speaking before school or college groups or teaching UFO classes, the
       )              speaker must exercise care to present the facts objectively, both pro and
                      con sides, and not try to push his/her own personal viewpoint or theories.
