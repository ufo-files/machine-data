The AFU Shop
The new AFU shop had its first order on
December 16, 2013 (actually placed by long-time
AFU sponsor Bruno Mancusi from Switzerland).
The first year for afushop.hemsida24.se was a
small success with more than one hundred orders
bringing in a net sum of about 55.000 SEK (USD
6.900, after shipping & web site costs) to the
foundation’s budget. This could, for instance, pay
for much of our annual costs for acquiring &
shipping new collections.
The AFU Shop already has a worldwide clientele
of customers. For simplicity, we only sell in US
funds, and with Paypal payment. After testing the
American Shopify software for a while we finally
found a similar but even better Swedish alternative
to provide for the web presentation & smooth
daily handling of our shop. The shop now has more
than 5.000 items for sale on line.
It is important to underline that the shop only sells
books and magazines which are surplus to the
archive’s own long-term needs for preservation.

shelves; generates a good extra income for running
