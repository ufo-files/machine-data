                               SUMMARY ANALYSIS

                           REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION CCC15
1. (S) This report documents a remote viewing session conducted in
compliance with a request for information on a target area of interest.

2. (S) This report represents this viewer's fourth attempt to access
this target (see CC74, CCB7, and CCC13). In this session the viewer
was unable to attain his usual state of relaxed concentration and focus
on the target. He was disturbed by ambient room noise which was loud.
He also complained prior to the session of being physically tired and
feeling miserable because of the flu. The viewer had difficulty through-
out the session and had little confidence in his imagery.
3. (S) The protocol used for this session is detailed in the document,
Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote Viewing Protocol (S), undated.
4. (S) Following is a transcript of the viewer's impressions during
the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings made by the viewer
reference his impressions of the target site. At TAB B is target
cuing information provided the remote viewer.
