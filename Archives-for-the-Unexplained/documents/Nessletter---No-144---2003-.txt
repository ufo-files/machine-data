       The 29th September '02 was the fiftieth anniversary of the death of John Cobb, killed as he attempted
to set a new water speed record on Loch Ness. His boat, 'Crusader', mono-hull with twin sponsons,

of the measured mile, which was from below Achnahannet along towards Urquhart Bay. This had been
