  It's interesting...might only be odd coincidence, as people often write like this without knowing it, but the email from
  "Anonymous" is written in an SEO format focusing on particular search engine keywords. I've noticed the writer makes
  an amateur mistake of trying to emphasize those keywords with caps. A lot of writers first trying to use SEO keyword
  sometimes slip up and capitalize the first letter of the words in their attempt to add emphasis to those keywords (even
  though search engines don't care about caps). This particular writer put those keywords in all caps each time.

  Keep in mind that it's recommended for SEO that the primary keyword should be 2 to 5% of the article. Secondary
  keywords should be used sparsely throughout. Ideal article length is typically between 700 to 900 words. Every
  keyword phrase using the primary keyword "ALIEN" is in CAPS in this email. In only one case, where "alien
  civilizations" is used is it not in caps - I believe that was unintentional.

  Primary Keyword:

  ALIEN (used 19 times throughout the email)

  Total article length: 877 words
  % Primary Keyword Density: about 2.2%

  Secondary Keywords:

  ALIEN CRAFT (used 7 times throughout the article)
  ALIEN BODIES - twice
  ALIEN BEINGS - twice
  ALIEN SPACECRAFT - once
  ALIEN CIVILIZATIONS - twice
  ALIEN PROPULSION SYSTEM - once
  ALIEN HOME WORLD - once
  ALIEN STAR CHART - once
  ALIEN TECHNOLOGY - once
