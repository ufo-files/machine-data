                                  SUBTLE MOVES AFOOT?

         We appear to be in the throes of an unprecedented programme of indoctrination by the
media to educate the general public into accepting the fact, that not only do aliens exist, but
that they are visiting our planet Earth.

         The television and radio media are giving an increasing amount of 'air time' to such
programmes as 'The X-Files', 'Star Trek', 'Sightings' and 'Encounters'.        The fact that they
are gaining in popularity and the subject becoming more acceptable, can be seen in the way
that consumer products are now being produced with typical programme logos. (This
apparently entitles the retailer to market these items at three times the normal sale price.)

         What does it all mean, is there an astute force at work preparing the general populace
for the news that the aliens are here!     Are we in the midst of a subtle conditioning process,
gently educating the public to accept the fact that we are not alone in the universe and that
'the truth is out there somewhere!'

         The tone of programmes handling the subject of UFO's has changed in recent years.
There is barely a mention of 'little green men' these days. The implication is that, if they are
here, where do they come from? We have even had a 'man from the ministry' Mr Nick Pope
revealing what he knows about the UFO subject. With three years experience, he is evidently
now the expert on UFO incidents. (Does he have an explanation for the strange craft that flew
over Havant in January 1979, or was he still at school at the time?)

         With the advent of the home video camera, the evidence is increasing of not only lights
in the sky, but of unidentified craft in our airspace!   The recent filming of a Flying Triangle
over the Morecambe area is evidence of these unidentified craft flying (?) over our countryside
at night. Who would dispute the official film taken by the Belgian Air Force of a radar contact
with a Flying Triangle and the subsequent futile chase by two Fl6's. The cockpit instrument
film has been shown on television quite recently and the FT cannot be explained away as
having the behaviour characteristics of any known aircraft.

         The PRA UFO report files have recently been transferred onto a PC.          As this work
progressed, it showed that what were thought to have been isolated incidents, were UFO
events taking place in the same area, throughout the same day. Flying Triangles have been
 seen over a local Severn Trent Water treatment works on different occasions. There were also
 a number of Flying Triangle incidents over the A52 road north of Derby, I might add, much to
 the consternation of the drivers concerned.

         There are now so many UFO 'events' taking place, it would not be surprising to
 discover that official sources are behind a plan of gradual indoctrination of the population,
 preparing them for the revelations to come!
