A mysterious plane over Stöllet

Time: July 12, afternoon
Place: Stöllet, province of Värmland (BV38)

From Värmlands Folkblad, July 15: "On Friday afternoon a few people in the Stöllet parish watched a mysterious airplane coming from the south in over the village. Yeoman Sigge Värman states, at the inquiry of VF, that he and several other persons saw the mysterious plane coming from the south, (it's shape) comparable to a hawk or an eagle... It made sudden turnabouts, to and fro', some ten circles, before disappearing into a cloud. No usual engine sound was heard, but a noise similar to the rattle of a lightweight motorcycle." 

Anders Liljegren contacted Sigge Värman in October 1986. He stated: "The day of the incident I was in a field when I heard a noise from the air, like a very small two-stroke engine. As I looked up I saw a small aircraft coming towards the site where I stood. The plane came from the SW or SSW at an altitude of 100-200 meters. The plane changed course before reaching the place where I stood, and disappeared in the same direction from where it came. The plane reminded me very much a model aircraft and the size I would estimate at between 40-60 between wingtips."

Further data: Duration - about two minutes; no changes to the character of the engine sound during the sighting; no changes of altitude or speed; weather was clear, dry with no wind. 

Status:  Possibly aircraft model (compare Skoghall case on July 10)
