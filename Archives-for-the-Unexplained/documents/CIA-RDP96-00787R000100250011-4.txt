                                     PROGRESS REPORT:   CONTRACT 77F 104420

           Background
                This Progress Report covers Task 1:               Background Literature Review.

           The task began 30 September 1977 and terminated on 28 February 1978.                   The

           statement of work for this task is as follows.

                  "Generally review all background literature, largely summary papers

           and books, to generate an overall appreciation of the history of research,

           claims, criticisms, and results of investigations in paranormal psychology.

           Because the investigators bring a strength of experimental objectivity to

           this effort rather than a theoretical or historical paranormal psychology

           bias, this task is necessary and desirable. 11

           Progress to Date
                In compliance with this statement of work, Systemetrics personnel

           conducted a search of all public literature on general paranormal psychology,

           with particular emphasis on remote viewing experiments and phenomena.

           Representative works were selected· for detailed review and were obtained

           from local libraries or purchased from publishers.                  In addition, reprints

           of articles and reports provided by the Sponsor were read and incorporated

           into the body of pertinent literature.              Finally, classified reports of

           pertinent research were reviewed at the_Sponsor's facility.

                  The unclassified reports and books reviewed thus far are listed in

           Attachment 1, not only to document work performed to date, but to serve

           as a permanent listing of a portion of the pertinent literature.                   These

           references are generally grouped by topical coverage, and are listed under

           two or more topics when appropriate.

Plans for Task 2
     Activities for Task 2 will follow the contract statement of work, as

scheduled.    Each remote viewing document or source will be critically

reviewed, based upon available information.        This work will be completed,

documented, and reported by 15 July 1978, as specified in the contract.

Plans for Tasks 3 and 4
     The Systemetrics proposal for this effort included visits to appropriate

U.S. investigators and critics (Task 3) and an evaluation report (Task 4).

As of the present time, the previously proposed tasks and·statement of work

appear valid,    It ntay be possible, however, to compress somewhat the work

proposed in Tasks 2, 3, and 4.       If such turns out to be feasible, a letter

request will be submitted by 30 May 1978 indicating a revised statement

of work and cost proposal for Tasks 3 and 4.

     As stipulated in the Systemetrics proposal of 25 April 1977 and modified

slightly in the 19 May 1977 revision, it is believed that the work to be

performed through Task 4 constitutes a meaningful activity, and that only

at the completion of this work can a continuation decision be made.        If,

on the other hand, some of the Tasks 3 and 4 activity can be conducted

under Task 2, every effort will be made to do so, with appropriate

concurrence from the Contracting Officer.
