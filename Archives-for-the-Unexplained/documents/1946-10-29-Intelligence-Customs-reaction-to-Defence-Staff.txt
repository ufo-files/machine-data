KUNGLIGA
 GENERALTULLSTYRELSEN

Confidential.

FÖRSVARSSTABEN
Air Defence Division
Added: November 12, 1946.
 No. L3: 51.


To the chief of the Defense Staff.



On October 28, 1946, you applied for the Customs Officer’s involvement in the investigations of so-called space projectiles.
Hence, we have decided that the heads of customs, coastal and border districts shall receive issue circular letters (of which a copy is attached) regarding the issue.

 Stockholm, October 29, 1946.

KUNGLIGA GENERALTULLSTYRELSEN

Tuwe Jansson



/ Sixten Friberg

Regarding reports of observations concerning space projectiles.
D. No. H 65.
