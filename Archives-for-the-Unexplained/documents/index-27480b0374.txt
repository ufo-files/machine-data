  From the Book Cover

  Tape 1: A Strange Harvest

  Emmy Award-winning science reporter, Linda Moulton Howe, investigate the world-wide animal multilation mystery.
  Worldwide, from animal to animal, the same body parts are excised: an ear, circular piece of flesh around the eye,
  tongue, teeth, jaw flesh, genitals and rectums. Typically, there is no blood on the animals and no tracks around the
  bodies, even on snow or wet sand. Pathology examinations of tissue in many animals have confirmed that excision lines
  have been cut with high heat.

  Tape 2: Strange Harvests 1993

  In early 1993, residents in northeastern Alabama reported seeing moving lights, unidentified helicopters, and large disk-
  shaped objects in pastures. POlice detectives were puzzled by strange deaths of more than 30 cows and goats. A
  pathologist confirmed that odd serated cuts on a calf had been made with something hot enough to cook the
  hemoglobin. Throughout 1993, dozens of strange animal deaths were reported in the United States, Canada and Europe,
  and continue to date.
