Crop Circles Found In Galva Field…  By Lisa Hammer… The Star Courier…

   CAMBRIDGE - A report of crop circles in a bean field near Geneseo won't lead to an intensive investigation by the Henry County Sheriff's Department.
   A farmer west of Geneseo called the sheriff's office over the weekend to report finding five circles in the field. There was no track of trampled soybeans leading to the circles, the farmer said.
   Sheriff Gib Cady issued a news release that concluded, "If anyone happens to see a UFO with attached soybeans, please contact Henry County Crime Stoppers or the Henry County Sheriff's Office." *
   Sheriff's department investigator Lt. Kerry Loncka said today he won't be investing time investigating the circles.
   It is possible to walk out into the middle of a soybean field without leaving any tracks, isn't it?
   "Yeah. Yeah," Loncka said. "I asked Gib about it this morning and he said it was a slow news day and that was something (the media) looked at. Actually I don't even want to be associated with it."
   On his way to Springfield today, Sheriff Cady insisted deputies looked all over for tracks into the field and there were none.
   "They checked all that out. That isn't true" (that anyone walked out there)," he said. "We had all kinds of people out looking."
   He recalled another instance of a "cookie-cutter" near-perfect wide and deep depression in a field south of Orion years ago.
   "We couldn't explain that either," he said.
   Cady said they've considered hot-air balloons, but it was raining on the day the circles appeared so no one would have been flying.
   They've considered underground methane gas or mold, but that wouldn't explain the perfect shapes.
   "I don't know what to tell you. No, there's no explanation. This is going nowhere, so you just have to laugh it off because you're not going to explain it," the sheriff said.
