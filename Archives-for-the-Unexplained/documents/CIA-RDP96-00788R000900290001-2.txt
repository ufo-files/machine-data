                        SUMMARY ANALYSIS

             REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION XXXIII



   1.  (S) This report provides documentation of a remote
   viewing session conducted for the purpose of orienting
   a remote viewer to an applied remote viewing protocol.

   2.  (S) The remote viewer's impressions show no definitive
   correlation with the target.  The remote viewer himself felt
   that this was a poor session.  He was not confident at all
   that his images were valid. The remote viewer was unable to
   relax and concentrate. He was continually distracted by
   ambient room noise and the mechanical sounds of the tape
   recorder. The target in this case was a dental office and
   the "beacon" was having his wisdom tooth pulled at the time
   of the session.

   3.  (S) The protocol used for this session is detailed in
   the document, Grill Flame Protocol, AMSAA Applied Remote
   Viewing Protocol (S), undated.

   4,  (S) Following is a transcript of the remote viewer's
   impressions of the target.  At TAB A are drawings made by
   the remote viewer reference his target impressions.  At
   TAB B is a post-session interview. No photographs of the
   target are provided.
