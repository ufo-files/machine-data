Case xxx.
A mirage at Gagnef

Time: January 8, about 15.00 hours
Place: Gagnef, county of Dalarna

”A remarkable light phenomenon was seen last Tuesday from the northern parts of Gagnef. The phenomenon appeared in the western sky at about 15.00 hours. In the sky appeared a small landscape with a lake and a hill in the background, and several buildings in front, a small cottage being the most prominent. It is supposed that this phenomenon has some connection to the mysterious light in Skåne on the same day [a mistake here: the Skåne lights were published on January 8, but had actually occured on January 4].”

Status: Mirage.
Source: Mora Tidning, January 14. Credit: John-Erik Pettersson.
