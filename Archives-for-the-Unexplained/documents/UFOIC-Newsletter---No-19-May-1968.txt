        THE MYSTERY Of THE DEAD HORSE
        Although the case of the dead horse from Alamosa, Colorado has generally filt ered verbally
        through meetings of people' interested in flying saucers, it is probable that a number of
        UfOIC members are not aware of the actual details, since the report only appeared in one
        Sydney newspaper, the Daily Telegraph, last September .
        Top American pathologists have been astounded by the death of a three year old horse, Snippy,
        whose owner believes he was killed by ''the inhabitants of a flying saucer".       An autopsy re-
        vealed that it's abdomina~ brain and spinal cavities were empty, and in addition to this,
        the pathologist who performed the autopsy said there were no signs of entrance into the
        horse's body.    One prominent Denver pathologist and blood specialist said that the absence
        of organs i n the abdominal cavity was completely inexplicable .      When the pathologist cut
        into the horse's brain cavity, he found it completely empty.       ''There definately s hould have
        been a good bit of fluid in the brain cavity", he said.      Most puzzling was the absence of
        any material in the centre of the spinal column.      The pathologist said he was not surprised
        by the absence of all of the abdominal organs and brain because he had read of similar
        incidents in other countries.      "This horse was definately n.21 hit by lightning," the path-
        ologist said.    That was the official conclusion of Alamosa County authorities.
        The controversy over Snippy began when the horse did not return to the Harry King ranch for
        hie usual evening drink.    Two days later, mr . King went looking for Snippy and found him
        dead ·about a quarter of a mil e from the ranch house.    The ranch is 20 miles south-east of
        Alamosa in desolate mountai n country.     All the flesh had been stripped from the horse's
        neck and head and only bare bones remained.      The bones were completely clean and mr. King
        said the cut where the neck meat had been peeled up from the withers, was so perfect it
        could. not have been made with a knife .    mr. King called the owners of the horse, Mr. & Mre.
        Burl Lewis, and together they investigated the area wher e the horse had been killed.         They
        found areas wher e the brush had been squashed to within 10 i nches of the ground.       What
        appeared to be 15 circular exhause marks were found 100 yards from the horse.        Another area,
        where an unknown craft was believed to have landed, was punched with six identical holes,
        each two inches square and four inches deep.      mrs. Lewis cajoled a forestry official into
        checking the area with a geiger counter .     High readings were found all around the area, she
        said, including where the unidentified flying objects were believed to have landed.         An
        investigating committee measured the markings on the ground and found the largest to be a
        circle 75ft. i n diameter.
        The committee returned to Denver with several samples taken from the horse and an object
        presumed to be a tool, found by mrs. Lewis .  mrs. Lewis found the object on her s econd
        visit to the site on September 16th.   It was covered with horse hair, and she said when she
        tried to wipe the hair off, her hand turned red and began to burn.   The burning persisted
        until she washed her hands.
        Additional data was obtained from a radio interview soon afterwards with one of tne invast-
    t   igators who had been on the scene.    One thing new which was brought out, was the fact that
        the carcass was not unduly decomposed , nor was i t particularly "smelly", also the vultures
        or buzzards did not attack or try to eat the carcass.     Again, these points highlight the
        mystery of the abnormal situation.    A plastic type of bag has also been found on the site.
        It contained a greenish fluid but no mor e details have yet come to light.    An ominously
        similar case to the one above has recently come to notice in Canada.
