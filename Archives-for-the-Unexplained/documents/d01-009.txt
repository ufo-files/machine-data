  TOTAL-5.UFO
       Editor's Note: I was *disturbed* by what I saw on "Strange
  Universe" last night. Or, rather, by what was referred to: by
  Bob Dean and Whitley Strieber (who were allowed to see a longer
  version than was broadcast on tv). At present, I'm not convinced
  that the video is a hoax...
       Art Bell's website <http://www.artbell.com/aliens.html>
  gives a little background: "This photo is a video grab from a
  6-minute piece of footage smuggled out of Area 51 by a mysterious
  man known only as Victor.' Victor claims that he had occasion
  to be there' and took this while the entire contents of a massive
  series of interviews [Editor's Note: "Strange Universe" came to
  call it an "interrogation"] with a number of alien creatures was
  being downloaded from video to analog.
       "This video purports to be of an alien creature, being
  filmed through a one-way mirror, and being questioned by a U.S.
  general and a telepathic aide. The ET has a seizure at the end
  of the scene and is attended to by two doctors." <...picture is
  the property of Rocket Home Video...[which intends to market
  their piece on home video]>
       However, CNI News (Global News on Contact with Non-human
  Intelligence)'s website
  <http://www.cninews.com/CNI_New.html#Latest> has this
  information: "...Segment producers have shown the footage to
  several experiencers' in advance to get their reactions,
  including author Whitley Strieber and special effects artist
  Steve Neill. Neill has told CNI News that he thinks the alien is
  a puppet. I could make that film in my garage in a weekend,' he
  said. Strieber told CNI News that he was impressed with some
  details in the footage but considers it probably fraudulent."
       Like the "Alien Autopsy," we'll probably see *lots* of
  discussion about this video--which, ultimately, has a "seeding"
  (paradigm widening) effect on mass consciousness/popular
  culture...
