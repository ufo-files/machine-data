2016 CANADIAN UFO SURVEY
  The results of the 2016 Canadian UFO Survey have been released, and despite an apparent quiescence in
media, UFO sightlngs are still being reported at a near-record level.        In 2016, 1,131 UFO reports were
officially filed in Canada, the fifth year in a row above 1,000 cases.
  Since the annual Canadian UFO Survey was initiated in 1989, 18,038 Canadian UFO reports have been
catalogued during the past 28 years. This is much more than the files of the United States Air Force's
infamous Project Blue Book, which investigated and recorded 12,618 UFO reports from around the world

between 1947 and 1969.
  This data clearly contradicts comments by those who would assert that UFOs are a "passing fad" or that
UFO sightings are decreasing in number.
  In 2016, most UFO reports were recorded during the summer with June, July and August having a
combined 37.7 % of all cases. Most UFOs were simple lights in the sky, known as Nocturnal Lights 54.9 %,
and Close Encounters comprised less than one per cent of the total.
  The provinces with the largest populations also tended to have the most UFO reports: Ontario (26.3 %)           ,



BC (17.2 %), but Quebec had a significantly unusual overabundance of UFO reports in 2016 (38.5%).
  The most popular shape of UFQ reported in 2016 was a simple point source of sight (57 %). Other shapes
reported were Triangle (4 %), Ball (9 %), Cigar (3%) and Fireball (5 °h). The classic "flying saucer" was
only reported 48 times (5 %) in 2016.
  The percentage of UFO cases considered unexplained in 2016 has dropped to 4 %, the lowest in 28 years
of study. This is likely a result of more careful scrutiny of raw report information available.
  It should be emphasized the classification of unknown does not imply alien visitation. Each case may
still have an explanation following further investigation. And of those that remain unexplained, they may
remain unexplained, but still are not incontrovertible proof of extraterrestrial intervention or some
mysterious natural phenomenon.
  The Canadian UFO Survey is produced by Ufology Research, based in Manitoba, with the cooperation of
investigators and researchers across the country. Source: "Ufology Research" March 30, 2017.

SHIP REPORTS UFO IN GULF OF MEXICO
  An oval-shaped object about 1,200 feet long "rose up out of the water" to about 40 feet and then
disappeared at an incredible rate of speed into the sky, according to reports from a chief engineer aboard
an Offshore Supply Vessel March 21, 2017, 80 miles southeast of New Orleans in the Gulf of Mexico, who
filed testimony with the National U FO Reporting Center.
  The sighting was reported to NUFORC on March 23, 2017. NUFOR<; Director Peter Davenport posted the
sighting to his website and confirmed the sighting Friday evening with Mutual UFO Network Director of
Communications Roger Marsh.
  "I am a Chief Engineer on an Offshore Supply Vessel, and the events I am going to put forward are
accurate, to the best of my knowledge," the witness stated in his report. Close to 7 p.m. on March 21st, just
before dusk, myself and four of the crew members aboard our vessel saw a craft that appeared to be five
times our 240-foot vessel in length. My line of sight was about one-quarter mile from our vessel. There
was a rig behind the craft about a half-mile. I used this to help gauge the size of the craft. Sighting was
approximately 80 miles southeast of New Orleans, Louisiana.
  "The scene lasted about 40 seconds. The craft rose up out the water (Gulf of Mexico) about 40 feet, with
no water dripping from the craft. Within a split second the craft disappeared at a 30-degree angle into the
sky. Speed appeared to be faster than the speed of a light turning on in a room. Within seconds it had
disappeared completely. I can say for sure that the craft was dark colored, oval in shape and made no sound
whatsoever." Davenport then followed up with the witness by telephone. Source: Roger Marsh, MUFON
March, 25, 2017.


THE TYPICAL FORTEAN DILEMMA            -   by Martin Piechota
  As the Fortean man grows older he begins to realize that he is no longer afraid of making a fool of himself.
He is likely fully aware of history and he has discovered that it is little more than the register of the crimes,
follies and misfortunes of mankind. As he matured further, he also realized that the grand theories put
forth by experts can easily be replaced by what he actually sees.
  As he ages �e discovers_ that if out-of-da�� beliefs are to be called myths, superstiti.ons and old wives'
tales, then these beliefs can be produced by the same sorts of reasons that now lead to scientific knowledge.
If the myths are included in the histories of science, then it stands to reason that science has included beliefs
that are incompatible with the present so-called knowledge.
  If one traces the scientific knowledge of any scientific group of related phenomena backward in time, he
is likely to encounter some variant of a pattern used to describe the phenomenon.
  One phenomena that is encountered in Fortean studies is the falling of toads in precipitation throughout
history. The conventional explanation of meteorologists is that the falls of toads occur when whirlwinds
over relatively shallow water develop into waterspouts and suck up the creatures, which can then be
carried long distances by buffeting clouds even when the waterspouts disperse. Any meteorologist who
can cite chapter and verse to refute this explanation also remains unknown.
  What's more, one would not expect whirlwinds to be so selective, not only is there no associated debris
dumped in the fall, but all the toads are of one size, and in some cases deposited in very small well-defined
areas. Many falls have occurred without a hint of accompanying wind, and the toads have sometimes fallen
on the same spot for such extended periods of time (up to ten minutes) that constantly moving whirlwinds
would seem to be ruled out

                                                        3
  While the whirlwind explanation seems valid, when the particulars of the falls are examined the
whirlwind would have to be operating like a vacuum cleaner that will only pick up sugar and leave salt and
pepper behind.
  Note that to this point only toads are mentioned. The history of toads tells us that in the North Temperate
Zone the toad is hatched from an egg, usually in the month of April. By the first of June it has grown four
legs and has shed its tail. It is then ready to leave its watery home and live out its life on land. When the
weather is warm, it hibernates under logs, leaves or grass in some damp, cool place. It finds a moist spot
somewhere in the shade, using itself as a corkscrew, drills four or five inches down into the ground, out of
sight of prowling enemies and whirlwinds.
  So, how could a whirlwind scoop up and carry many toads at a time? As the toad matures and increases
its weight, it should be unnatural for a group of toads to be seen that are reported to be dime or quarter­
sized after mid-June. Yet tiny toads are reported to have fallen in showers In late summer and early fall
when they should be at least as large as a tennis ball. These reports should be considered anomalous or
the species identified that has a different lifestyle than the typical toad.
  Can a waterspout spin off tiny but powerful whirlwinds that can drill down and expose the toads and
bring them to the surface to be picked up and carried away? There would have to be as many of these
powerful entities to gather up a large total of toads and strategically position them in the larger whirlwind
to be dropped all at once.
  When a report of falling toads appears, be aware of the date of the fall. Question the expert if the fall's
date varies from the expected life cycle of the toad.

  A Nevada witness near Austin reported a close encounter with a low flying and hovering triangle-shaped
object about 300 feet long moving overhead under 500 feet, according to testimony from the MUFON
witness reporting database.
  The reporting witness is a communications engineer who was leaving work at a remote radio site on
Mount Callahan on February 15, 2017.
  'We were driving down from Mount Callahan at 8 p.m." the witness stated. 'We entered a clearing and
saw a bright light in the sky just out in front of us about 1,000 yards. It was just sitting there about 30
degrees up in the air." The witness stopped his truck and got out to watch the object
  "In an instant, it became a very large triangle with two lights in each corner. The object appeared to
rotate slowly for about a minute. The body blotted out the stars. Then it suddenly became a massive
rectangle with about 30 lights around the perimeter. It did not move or make any sound for about a minute.
  "Then it just was gone as if turning off a light. About five seconds later we could hear a very loud rushing
air sound just above us, but we could not see anything above us, just stars. The sound got louder, then
slowly as if flying away, .got softer and softer until silence. This :lasted about 20 seconds. This was an
extremely remote communications site. Our trucks were the only lights for 20 square miles. I felt that it
had been watching us and then just appeared directly in front of us, did its little show and left."
  Nevada MUFON Assistant State Director Sandra Countiss closed this case as an Unknown. Source: Roger
Marsh, "Open Minds" March 16, 2017.
