    UFO base in Lake Ontario? according to Malcolm Williams, researcher for
Northeastern UFO Organization the answer is yes. In the book "l!nvisible

been seen and photographed diving in and out of Lake Ontario just off the
Oakville - Burlington shoreline.
  At the University of Toronto map library, Malcolm says proof of his thesis
is in-the form of infra.red {heat-sensing) satellite photographs of the Great
Lakes.
  Malcolm, a former member of the Royal Astronomical Association with a long-
standing interest in unusual aerial phenomena and his experiments with infrared
photography have proved successful in his photos of UFO tracks over the lake.

feels, is in the numerous sightings - many of which he and others have recorded
on film - of the strange lights over the lake between Oakville and Toronto.
  Malcolm, gets support on that point from Harry Picken, an aeronautical
engineer, pilot and president of Gen.air (1961) Ltd. a St. Catharines aircraft
research firm.
  From Harry Picken•s Niagara-on-the-Lake home, which overlooks the lake,
strange light formations which he has been watching for years and do not resem-
ble aircraft lights. _
  Both Harry and Malcolm say the lights are usually orange, a color foreign to
aircraft lights. "The orange color indicates a high sodium content in the light
source." According to Mr. Picken sodium is never used in aircraft lights.
