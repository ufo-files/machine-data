'Silly' group label out Of· tune
THE meeting, on U.F.O.'s -
The Facts, by The Aetherius
Society turned out to be a
tremendous success. We had a
capacity audience of more
than 100 people.
  Judging by the lively question
and     answer     session     which
followed I feel I should point out
to you that the attitude expressed
in your headline about this meet­
ing is not only irresponsible but it
shows that Bristol Journal         is
grossly   out of    tune    with  its
readership and out of step with
the times.
   No matter what you may think.
we believe that Flying Saucers,
U.F.O.'s, are here to stay.
  They are real.
  They are friendly.
  They have come to help bring
peace to this trouble stricken
world. And, according to our opi­
nion poll, one in seven of Bris­
tolians have seen them!
      Or Edward Prempeh,
      Organiser - Bristol
      Group,
      Lodge Causeway,
      Fishponds.


Barmy fanatics
WELL done to the Journal for
bringing to light yet another group
of nut-cases. Bristol's barmy UFO
fanatics The Aetherius Society are
the latest.
   We all know that we are the
only planet in this universe that is
inhabited.
   Even the stars were put there as
an after thought (Genesis 16 "he
made the stars also").
   These people must join the long
list of other nut-cases such as
those    who thought the earth
revolved around the sun.
   Maybe the Aetherius Society in
their quest to find their UFO will
fall off the edge of the Earth!
       C.J. Tuttiett,
       Barrow Hill Road,
       Shirehampton.


Poor service
I WAS disgusted to see my picture,
with   my    colleagues   in   the
Aetherius Society in last week's
edition of your paper. The caption
was "The Silly Society"!
  Fortunately some members of
the public were unimpressed by
your ridiculous article, as our
meeting was crowded with people
who treated the subject of flying
saucers with the seriousness it
deserves.
  You are giving a poor service to
your readers with such misleading
nonsense.
