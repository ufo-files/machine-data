Case xxx.
Fireball near Härnösand & Sundsvall

Time: April 25, 22.05 hours
Place: Härnösand and Sundsvall area, county of Västernorrland
 
The following two reports may have a connection: 

xxx-1. A pilot officer of the Air Force reserve [F7 wing], named Winter, reported [reporting date unknown, but early in the 1946 wave] a phenomenon to the Defence Staff. The report was noted in writing on a slip of paper: At 22.05 hours on April 25 Winter was flying from Kallax to the F7 airbase [at Såtenäs]. While 10 kilometres north of Härnösand and flying at 1.000 meters, he noticed a continous white light at bearing 215 (the SW), that divided into six parts, descended and went out. The whole phenomenon lasted no more than 3 seconds. The phenomenon had a westerly course (about 270 degrees). [This is the first report noted on the Defence Staff summary tables of 1946 cases.]   

xxx-2. FIREBALL FLEW OVER THE TOWN YESTERDAY. Inhabitants of Sundsvall who took a walk yesterday night at about 23.00 hours experienced a fireball illuminating the sky. The ball, according to our source, was equipped with a mightly tail and went unusually low and slow, exploding right over the town and thus ending the heavenly fireworks. 

Status: Meteor
Sources: War Archives and Sundsvalls Tidning, April 25.
