 AN INTERVIEW WITH GLORIA DIXON (part 2)
Gloria Heather Dixon became interested in the UFO subject in 1 970 whilst living in the United
States. Her father resided in the States as an aeronautical engineer working as a parachute
specialist in the Space programme during the sixties. Her parents were divorced and a
holiday to visit him resulted in a move to the States where she lived in Wilmington, Delaware
for twelve years.


She read Project Blue Book, the United States Air Force official investigations into UFOs, and
during the first part of the seventies heard a couple of unusual stories, which reinforced her
interest further. She worked as a PA for an American businessman, who was politically active
within the Republican party during the Nixon era and who went on to become a Congressman
for Delaware in the seventies.


Her stepfather was an officer in the RAF and they travelled to many parts of the world and
Gloria developed an interest in aviation.


She joined the British UFO Research Association (BUFORA) and the Independent UFO
Network ( I UN) in 1 990 and began to attend UFO Conferences, where she admits to "hearing
some of the strangest conversations I had ever heard ...           "




She is the now the national investigations co-ordinator for BUFORA, a valued member of the
UFO Investigation Network, and also finds the time to produce a bi-monthly magazine entitled
Strange Daze.
