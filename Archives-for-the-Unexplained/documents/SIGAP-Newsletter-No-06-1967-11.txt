                       The October "Flap",Surrey's Contribution!..
The sighting by two Devonshire Police Constables of a              ' ~lying   Cross',started off one
of the biggest flaps for years,before very long we were assailed by flying 'Crosses',
'Stars','Green Balls'and ' Fireballs' .The newspapers and television reporters,plus
'steam radio',were soon on the scene and during the final days of October,UFO's were
the topic of conversation •••••• Now that the dust has settled,we can pause and assess
the reports that came to light during the 'flap' period (23/10 to 6/11).
       Since the publication of our last newsletter (Oct.6th),we have received a total
of 19 sighting reports of which 9 were sent in after the 23rd Oct.We can no doubt
attribute several of these to the publicity given to the SIGAP group in the local
press,as well as to the general publicity of the subject 'jn the radio                etc 7 (o~ Hon.Sec.

Dick Beet gave an interview on South East News).Several of the reports sent in to us
concerned sightings going back as far as a year ago,it not surprising therefore that
t      nth of October resulted in only eight reports which could have been UFO activity.
While this is quite good,it is no more than were reported in August of this year,but
the press had done their job and for a brief moment the general public were on the
point of realising exactly how much UFO activity there is,over the British Isles.
     However the Ministry of Defence soon put everyones mind at rest with the startling
information that there had been numerous sightings of Venus,Aircraft and satellite
reflections,in other words UFO's did not exist.             It is with interest that we await
the M.O.D .'s comments on the recent Russian statement that a scientific mission will
investigate all Russian UFO reports,as they have been tracing them on their Radar sets
for the past 20 years.Maybe the Russians haven't heard of Venus yet?
