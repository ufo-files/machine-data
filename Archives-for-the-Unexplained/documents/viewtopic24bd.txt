  Hello - I was at Bentwaters when Rendlesham occurred.
    by ppulatie » Mon Jan 14, 2008 6:38 am

  Just wanted to introduce myself. I was stationed at RAF Bentwaters/Woodbridge from Jun 79 to Jun 81,
  when the Rendlesham incident occurred. I was in the 81st Security Police Sq, Operations Office at the time.
  I knew all of the players, including Penniston, Burroughs, Warren, Col Halt, Lt Englund. etc.

  I have communicated over the years with Nick Pope, former MOD, who has investigated the incident. The
  last time was two years ago, when Burroughs made a new claim that Pope asked about. I was able to relay
  information from the claim that was useful for detemining some credibility to the claim.

  Every few years, the story takes on a life again. It is fascinating to me to read the new takes, etc.,
  especially since I have played a peripheral role.

  I will be posting observations, facts, rebuttals and other information about what happened at RAF
  Bentwaters as time permits from my currently very busy schedule. I think that I can shed much needed
  light on the participants in the event, the facts of the case, and how the base and the Security Police
  Squardon operated. All are relevant to understanding what happened and coming to a conclusion as to
