mum of 25 on the east coast are basking in the ·warmth of swamps of Georgia and Flordia.

  Chance believes that there is a resident family living in Harford County consisting of a mature male, a st �� aller
female and a younger male. It is believed that there is a similar family group living in southern York County
Pennsylvania, and these are the ones that raid chicken houses in Sykesville and wander literally into the back
door of Montgomery and Prince Georges Counties.
   Most persons who have seen Bigfoot and are convinced of his · existance have an overwhelming fea r of
another encounter with him. Toeing the line with a 1 0foot tall 8 00pound creature might even silence the
mouth of Muhammid Ali, but there is no hard evidence of anyone every being harmed by Bigfoot. The only
known case of any active agression by Bigfoot is an often reported occurrence in an Oregon mining camp in
the early 1 9 00s. Two miners had pursued and shot at two Bigfoots, perhaps even wounding one. When the
miners returned to their cabin for the night, their quarry returned with friends. The creatures, as the reports
go, laid seige to the cabin for two days, preventing the miners from shooting at them with a constant bar­
rage of large boulders. Near the end of the second night, the beasts pushed the cabin over onto its side and
left leaving the shaken but unhurt miners to have second thoughts about what they shot at in the future.

  In Maryland, Bigfoot is spotted while travelling, observed feeding at landfills and garbage dumps, caught
in the act of raiding chicken houses or rabbit hutches or seen peering in the windows of houses where
children are playing noisily or a TV set is on. As far as is known, no acts of belligerance toward humans have
ever been reported in Maryland. As far as other animals go, Bigfoot appears to have no hesitation in killing
poultry or other small animals for food, and he has no qualms about destroying, by a number of means,
dogs in pursuit. The killings and mutilation of cattle and horses have been frequently blamed on Bigfoot,
and the experts at American University were in disagreement about whether Bigfoot is capable of the seem­
ingly senseless killings or whether he may have felt threatened by his victims. In any event, when the mem­
bers of the panel were questioned what they would do if ever confronting a Bigfoot face to face, one re­
sponse was :"Make certain the movie camera pointing over my shoulder is running."

  An area of considerable discussion by the panel was the purpose of the peculiarly noxious odor emitted
by Bigfoot at times and seemingly turned on and off at will. Suggestions were made ranging from a simple
defense as in skunks, a sexual attraction (in which case to each his own), individual identification within the
family, to a form of nerve gas to render any potential enemy docile.

 This writer did not endear himself to the panel by suggesting that perhaps the most obvious source of the

    Hereford, Texas, June 28, 1 978 A steer that had been in excellent health was found dead in the north­
    western part of the county according to Deaf County SHeriff Travis McPherson who believes a religious cult
    is at work. The steer's tongue had been removed as had its genitals and a neat round hole replaced the ani­
    mal's anus. All of the incisions were definitely made with surgical instruments, McPherson said. The body
    had been completely drained of blood through a pencil-sized hole in the animal's jugular vein. Another steer
    was found the previous week with exactly the same mutilations Sheriff McPherson said. The animal had died
    of blackleg and the incisions were made after death and the blood had not been drained from the carcass.
    Quite a number of the town's leading citizens have reported UFO sightings at about the same time the muti­
    lations took place, McPherson said. He indicated there have been so many reports he doesn't bother filing
    them anymore because they just clog up things and he can't do anything about them anyway. It was also
    learned that several drivers in the area had nightime UFO encounters where their car engines were stalled.

    Elsberry, Missouri, August 7, 1 978 A 3 50pound six to seven month old calf was discovered in a field about
    a mile from a roadway by Angelo DiBartolo, the seventh animal mutilation discovered in the area since June 24.
    The calf was laying on its left side and the hide and meat of the upper right side had been stripped exposing
    the throat and ribcage extending from the right front shoulder to the middle of the right side and from the
    backbone to the underside. The right front leg was missing and there was no tear where the leg had been
    attached. There was no blood visible anywhere. There was a small cut on the right rear hip but no other                    ·



    visible surgery. In the six other mutilation cases reported in the area, the eyes, tongue and sex organs had all
    been removed from the butchered animals. A rash of UFO sightings have occurred, beginning just before
    the first mutilation, in the general areas of the mutilations.

    Pea Ridge, Arkansas, August 21 , 1 978 Utah Smith reported to the local sheriff that he found his missing
    two-month-old mule colt badly mutilated. The animal's eyes, tongue, and sexual organs had been removed,
    apparently surgically. Benton County Sheriff's Deputy John Greene said that a week before the eyes, tongue
    and sexual organs had been removed from a calf found near Cave Springs and the day before only the ton­
    gue had been removed from a cow found west of Centerton. No evidence of any threat to the safety of hu­
    mans has been discovered so it is unlikely enough investigation will be done to come up with any answers
    about who or why.

    New Hope, Missouri, August 7, 1 978 Eiliene and Robert Taylor called Sheriff Cliston Hilton very concerned
    when they found a second dead calf from their cattle herd under unusual circumstances. The calf was found
    with an eye and an ear and its tail missing and no blood in the carcass or around it. The thing that bothered
    them most was that although the carcass laid in place three days or longer, there were never .any buzzards.
    You can always find a cow down by following the buzzards, Taylor said. Earlier in the month the Taylors
    found a dead cow with a perfect round hole cut where its rectum had been. A veterinarian said the animal
    had been struck by lightning and another animal had been eating on it but he could not explain the pre­
    cision of the incision around the rectum.
