The Truro Daily News - Truro, Nova Scotia, Canada…
12th October 2007…

We Are At The Hands And Feet Of God, Waiting For Us To Grow Up…

   Shag Harbour, Nova Scotia, is right up there with Roswell, New Mexico, as a celebrated UFO crash site.
   Recent publicity on the 40th anniversary celebrations has reminded me of this strange happening. According to well-attested reports, on Oct. 5, 1967, at about 11:20 p.m, an object  with flashing lights was witnessed flying along the coast. Suddenly it tipped to a 45 degree angle and descended toward the water. It was seen in the water by the RCMP and others, but then it disappeared and no trace has ever been found. It left a brown foam where it had moved through the water, but when attempts were made to gather some of it the foam simply evaporated.
   This strange happening was witnessed by many people and thoroughly investigated by police and the military. Dark Object: The World's Only Government Documented UFO Crash, is the book by Don Ledger and Chris Styles detailing the event. There can be no serious doubt that an object was seen flying and then descending or crashing into the water. What it was or what became of it remains a mystery.
   It has not gained the notoriety of the Roswell incident, which happened 20 years before in early July 1947. Perhaps Canadians are not as good as Americans at fantasying about and  mythologizing events involving Unidentified Flying Objects and Extra Terrestrial beings.
   The many reports of sightings and actual encounters with E.T.'s, often involving detailed and gruesome bodily examinations, are too numerous to be written off as the figment of peoples' imagination. Their experience is real; whether it is actual, psychic, fantasy or dream remains the unanswered question.
   Certainly there is a large contingent of people who firmly believe that we have real visitors from outer space. Why we have these visitors is a matter of discussion and argument. Some  believe that they, obviously being of superior intelligence and wisdom, have come to guide and enlighten us. In other words, they will be our saviours. Others believe that they have all  sorts of malevolent intent and that we must be ready to destroy them. (Sometimes, when sleep eludes me, I listen to "From Coast to Coast," a nightly radio program where these arguments rage.)
   Then there are those, no doubt most of us, who simply write off the whole UFO business as the creation of deluded minds. We have to conclude, with Bertrand Russell, that "the most savage controversies are those about matters as to which there is no good evidence either way."
   I lean toward the psychic theory. Perhaps I am merely echoing Carl Jung, whose delvings into the working of the mind and the collective unconscious I take very seriously. He believed that UFO sightings - I'm not sure there were many reports of actual encounters in his day - were real but psychic happenings. He believed that we have a collective, but largely unconscious, yearning for a saviour to appear from some external source to rescue us from the self-destruction toward which there is a general feeling that we are heading. It might be likened to the yearning for a messiah that was abroad in Jesus's time.
   He wondered whether the power generated in the psyche could actually produce material objects and events. (That's a hard concept for me to get my head around, but who knows.) He believed that there needed to be more investigation done into the relation between psychic happenings and actual objects and events.
   What all this means for me is that our salvation is not going to come from a literal invasion from outer space, or a supernatural happening.
   Rather, as in Jesus's time, it appears in human form in the people among us who lead us out of our immature belief in violence toward a global vision of justice and harmony. Just as the psychic, or spiritual, energy of the universal yearning of the Hebrew people produced Jesus, our present day yearning is producing all manner of people who are working for justice, peace and a harmonious world.
   Are we not the hands and feet, the minds and hearts, of a universe and God, yearning for us to grow up?
