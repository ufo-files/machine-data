                                                                 Explanation of Ratings

  Most graphic software, in our opinion, is a temporary fad destined for obsolescence. Art, animation and
  music cannot compete with the written word for conveying information. This package, however, has
  earned a lot of high-powered endorsements from respected psi researchers, enough to convince us to take a
  look at it. We now stamp it with our qualified approval. Yes, this software may be obsolete in a few years,
  but it is entertaining enough for the time being. The presentation is slick and spooky and has the look and
  feel of the popular Myst and Riven games. You start in a central room with a series of doorways to
  explore. Most of the "content" you eventually come across consists of video programs, totalling over an
  hour and providing a documentary overview of psychic research and its history. There are also audio clips,
  text sections, a hypnotic "relaxation room" and a couple of classic psi experiments to play with. You don't
  really need this software, but it will keep you amused for a few hours, and then you can pass it along to
  someone else. -- Glenn Campbell
