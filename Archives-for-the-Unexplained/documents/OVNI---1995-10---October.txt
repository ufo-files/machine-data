It was a glorious day in Kerry, Ireland, the usual heavy-laden
air,   intoxicating  in   itself,  as  we   set  out   towards

around a bend in the track, we saw a lady with a small child.

were becoming a mite worried, then I opened the window on the

about.   We did not follow up the subject with them, we had a
pretty good idea that we had strayed into the unknown!
