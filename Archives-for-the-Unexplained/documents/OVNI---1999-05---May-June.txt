           REPORTS GALORE, AS THE '99' FLAP CONTINUES!
                           A review by your Editor Omar Fowler
Just what is going on, is all this UFO activity due to the crisis in the Balkans? That is just one of the many
questions that has been directed at me lately. That is a difficult question to answer, who knows the reason for
this recent upsurge of UFO incidents.
I have recently had the opportunity to broadcast on a variety of BBC Midlands programmes, as well as
several Television channels. This has not only given me the opportunity to 'spread the word', but also to
collect a number of reports that might otherwise have gone unreported. I have been a little surprised by
some of the flattering comments that have come my way and also the surprise expressed by some listeners
e.g. "I couldn't believe what I was hearing... brilliant! ". It seems that the general public are rapidly becoming
accustomed to the fact that not only do UFO's exist, but that there are dedicated researchers who take the
subject of UFO investigation seriously.
It is becoming increasingly apparent that we are now receiving excellent reports from high calibre witnesses.
We have always had the odd report coming in from little old ladies who spend half their night wandering
around the house in the early hours of the morning. Now we are receiving an increasing number of reports
from such people as engineers in a variety of fields e.g. aircraft manufacture, aero-engine project design and
structure design. When they witness a UFO event, they are well aware that. it is something not only out of
the ordinary, but a craft manufactured using advanced technology, unlikely to originate from our planet
Earth!
It is pleasing to see that there are an increasing number of high quality magazine now being produced not
only in the professional field, such as the Quest International 'UFO Magazine' and 'Flying Saucer Review' ,
but by other dedicated groups up and down the country. The majority of poorly produced newsletters, with
appalling layout and spelling mistakes seem to have fallen by the wayside. It is difficult to imagine that any
scientifically minded person would ever have taken them seriously.
Whenever I have an enquiry from the 'pre�s' or news media, I go to great lengths to ensure that they get their
story right. We have thankfully seen the passing of the "little green men" approach, most journalist are now
aware of the implications of 'Area 51', 'Roswell' and such subjects as cattle mutilations and human
abductions. 'The Greys' are now an accepted part of the consumer toy market and gradually, either by
design or natural progression, the indoctrination of the public is taking place.
The high literary and technical quality of several recent books has done a great deal to improve the
presentation of the UFO subject. A few years ago, who would have thought that a UFO book would have
been in the 'Top Ten' best seller's list for several weeks! I believe that the message is, we should persevere
to become more professional and intense in our investigation of the UFO subject. Although World news is
presently being overshadowed by the terrible events in Kosova, in the overall world-view there may be far
graver implications for mankind in the increase in world-wide UFO activity.
Prof John E.Mack once said "You have more to fear from your fellow-man than the Aliens", but have we?

         GIGANTIC UFO SEEN OVER 'IKEA' AT EASTWOOD, NOTTS.

It was a cold clear night the 8th February this year, when Mrs Campbell left her home in Mayflower Road,
Newthorpe (adjacent to Eastwood), to attend her weekly evening class. The time was about 6.40pm as she turned
down the road.    Just then, she happened to glance back towards her house and was shocked to see what appeared
to be a large 'Flying Saucer' hovering over the industrial estate situated about half a mile away. ''I saw the lights
and the disc shape, I couldn't believe it at first" commented Mrs Campbell. "I then ran back indoors and told my
husband."

Mr Campbell took a quick look at the strange disc shaped object in the distance and immediately searched for his -
binoculars. Then having retrieved them he moved to the back bedroom, where he had a clear field of view. His wife
had meanwhile left to continue her journey to night school.                                                                ·   I
Mr Campbell watched the craft for some fifteen minutes as it hovered absolutely still, over the IKEA furniture store
complex, which is situated about a quarter of a mile away. The craft was hovering so low that it was illuminated by
three bright floodlights which are situated on the industrial estate.

''I've got some pretty powerful binoculars (20x) and I was able to see it clearly. It appeared to be so close, it filled
the field of view and I had to scan with the binoculars in order to study it", said Mr Campbell.

"I estimate that it was no more than half a mile away, if that. It appeared to be white or a silvery colour and it was
so clear, I managed to see some markings on the craft, which I took note of' said Mr Campbell.       "At first I thought
it read 'Max' but then when I looked more closely I made out the figures that I have noted down."(see below) ''The
markings were on the upper right-hand side, immediately above the green illuminated strip." "The craft was was
oval shaped, not self- illuminating, but appeared to have a strip of green light which went around the centre of it.
Apart from that, there was one small light on the top of it."

It was then, after some twenty minutes, that the craft slowly started to move away, travelling from right to left. "It
didn't go off at any great speed, but just gradually moved away silently and finally disappeared as a dot on the
horizon. Initially I thought it might have been man-made, but then the sheer size of it ruled that out of my mind"
said Mr Campbell.

Editors Note: In order to rule out a hovering advertising balloon etc., a subsequent enquiry was made with the
IKEA store. Their spokesperson confirmed that they had never used a balloon for publicity or any other purpose.

          AN 'FT' ENCOUNTER ON AN OLD ROMAN ROAD 31-3-99


 We have received yet another well documented report concerning an FT . The encounter took place on 'Foss
 Way' an old Roman road and involved four witnesses in a car.          One of the witnesses in the car, Mr C.J.
 Saunders, gave an excellent description of the encounter and also supplied several computer generated
 graphics, one of which has been reproduced with this article.

 It transpired that Mr Colin Saunders is a design draughtsman and has previously been employed on aircraft
 design for Airbus Industries and SAAB .      He has also worked for Courtolds PLC and Chevron Petroleum
 (U.K.) Ltd. The other witnesses were mainly family members and their stories have been compiled to
 produce the following report:

 We were travelling along the old Foss Way (Roman Road) in a motorcar, all four of us saw some red
 pulsating lights hovering above a field close to the road in front of us. As we approached the lights which I
 estimated as being 1 00/200ft high in the air, we all mentioned how strange they looked and how close.

 The lights were obviously stationary, as we drove towards them, you could see we were getting close. We
 then drove alongside and then slightly in front, which is how we could tell it was stationary and were able to
 judge the height. (The witnesses had now established that they were on a craft of some kind)

 As we drove level with the lights I could see the shape of the object silhouetted against the sky. The outline
 at first was slightly like a diamond, but then seemed to tilt, giving the impression of a 3D pyramid or piece of
 'Toberlorone' chocolate shape (Triangular), the object was at an angle of20 or 30 degrees. We stopped the
 car slightly past the object, where a tall hedge blocked my view. We reversed the car into a gateway, where
 we could see the object had left, but was still visible but disappearing on the horizon at a distance of some20
 or 30 miles away in just a couple of seconds! The object still had the strange red lights visible, but seemed to
 me, to have grown its wings to an immense width.

 The object had been in view for some 15-20 seconds during the observation, was clearly defined and made
 no sound as far as we could ascertain. The object was last seen heading at high speed, in a Westerly direction
 towards Coventry.

 In answer to the question, what was unusual about the sighting etc., Mr Colin Saunders replied: ttThe type
 of lights, its shape, its location, its ability to hover silently, its fast disappearance and its apparent ability
 to change shape. "

 Victoria Saunders ( 12) stated, "We were driving along Foss Way after going out for a meal, when somebody
 said, that aeroplane looks a bit low and somebody else said that's got too many lights on to be an aeroplane.
 At that point the craft was stationary. We stopped the car and reversed into a gateway, but by the time we
 had got out of the car, all we could see was some red lights in the distance and we had heard no noise."

 ''When we got home, we all drew what we had seen (see sketch below). The next day Dad phoned up
 Coventry Airport, but they said there had been no other sightings and no large aircraft had landed or had
 been due to land.    For a few days afterwards, I was a bit scared of going outside!"
