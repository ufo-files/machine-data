    Our Review                                                                                                    Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  This book represents one of the handful of competing viewpoints on the Roswell incident. Friedman
  proposes that two crashes took place on the same night in 1947 -- one at Corona and one on the Plains of
  San Augustin. The latter claim is based on the testimony of one Gerald Anderson, a pivotal character in
  this book who has since been widely discredited. Friedman is also a believer in the veracity of the MJ-12
  documents, which make their way into this book. Compared to Randle & Schmitt's books or those of any
  of the skeptics, this one is relatively weak. -- Glenn Campbell
