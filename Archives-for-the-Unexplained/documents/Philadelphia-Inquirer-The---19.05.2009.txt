Tonight: That's No UFO…

   If you see a bright light low in the southern sky tonight, forget thoughts of close encounters.
   Like the giant NASA research balloon that baffled folks in Arizona and New Mexico yesterday, this will be an IFO -identified flying object.
   If, as expected, the skies stay clear tonight, an Air Force Minotaur 1 rocket will be launched from Wallops Island, Va.
   South of Delaware on the Delmarva Peninsula, the launch location is a little west of due south from Philadelphia, although the rocket's trajectory will carry it southeastward.
   Binoculars might prove handy to those hoping to spot it.
