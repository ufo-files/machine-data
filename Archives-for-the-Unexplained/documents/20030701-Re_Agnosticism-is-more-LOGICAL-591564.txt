         (ACP)

         10. If 'There may be an X' is true when there is no known of X, then:
         'There may be a god' is true when there is no known god.
         11. 'There may be a proof' is true, only if 'there may be a god' is
         true' when there is no known god. (9-10 HS)
         12. 'There may be a god' is true when there is no known god. (8,11 MP)
         13. There is no known god.
         14. There may be a god.     (12,13 MP)
         15. If 'there may be a proof' is true, then there may be a god. (8-14
         CP)
         16. Either: there may be a god, or: there may be a god. (2,7,15 CD)
