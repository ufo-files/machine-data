                          HUON MYSTERY ·LIGHTS

In  the  period 1973-75  four  investigations were  made  by
TUFOIC members into reports from the Huon Valley area.   The
reports involved lights, twice in thundery conditions but on
two occasions during tli.e fine we�ther.    The events  have
languished   in    the   files   with              the               explanation   of   possible
ball lightning,  or to quote the "Mercury" of January 8th
1974.   "It was a type of aerial phenomena but certainly a
most unusual one".  In two of the cases the main percipients
suffered eye troubles and loss of weight.    Also there may
have been associated traces in three of the cases.

                              Ball Lightning

Firstly  let's    have  a  look at    some information     on  ball
lightning.    s. Singer in "Ball Lightning" (1977) says.       "The
glowing   spheres    are   usually   associated     with  ordin�ry
lightning   in   severe  thunderstorms.     In  contrast    to  the
conunon flashes of lightning 1 however, these globes remain

through   the     air    in   extended                     paths."          "The   globes      are
approximately spherical in all but 10 to 20% of the cases".
"A blue halo or corona extending from the central mass is
observed  occasionally  and sometimes sparks and rays    are
emitted."  "The balls average 25cm in diameter."   "Most are
between 10 - 100cm, sizes from �em to several metres have

Dr. N. Charman (New Scientist 1972) notes the balls tend to

others appear in buildings passing through doors and windows
with curious ease.  The disappearance is generally rapid and
may be silent or explosive.     Hissing or crackling noises
have been noted, like an electrical discharge in the air.

been visible for a few minutes.

The first mystery report came from Dover about 3.15a.m. on
Wednesday March 2 8th (1973) . There had been some rain and
thunder in the area earlier and the clouds were still thick
and low when Mrs. A. Marks was awakened by a sound, it was
like a whirr or scream of an electric motor.     Mrs.  Marks
woke her husband and a  friend of the family holidaying with
them.  All three saw a light to the north and watched it for
20 minutes.

The dense looking object gave off a reddish light and was
below the cloud at low elevation. As the noise increased 2
smaller lights apeared to the left of the initial light.
The main light expanded when the noise was loudest then
later diminished in size. It was estimated to be two thirds
moon size at arms length.

The light just remained stationary until finally with the
noise at its loudest it expanded somewhat then all three
lights just went �ut and the noise stopped.    In a later TV
interview not everyone apparently noticed all three lights.

                                Investigation.

The sighting appeared in the "Mercury" of Saturday March
31st and the witnesses were interviewed by ABC TV.     Ken
Bennette (Sightings Investigator 1969-75) investigated the
sighting and learnt of another incident that night.

Some � km from the witnesses' location the local store fire
and burglar alarm was somehow activated.  A neighbour had to
contact the owner to come and turn it off as it was running
down a bit when turned off after going for quite some time.
The neighbour and shop owner noticed an orange glow coming
from behind trees in the direction of the Mark's light.

Did the noise of the shop alarm wake up the witness, was it
