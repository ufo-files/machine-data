 Mariner IV Report (The Quest For Life On Mars) - - Monday, November I6, 1964, was
 - for the man on the street - the same as any other Monday. The week-end was
 over and it was back to work. Back to, earning the income that is so direly- nee-
 ded to support loved ones. Back to the grueling business of making ends meet.
 That, after all, is our way of life.
 But ••• in Washington, D.C., a group of people were thinking of other wa::,s of life!
 ••• Other wa::,s of life that quite possibly bear no resemblance to our own •••••
 This group of men, comprised of a scientific panel, had their sights set on the
 mysterious red planet ••• Mars.
 At the request of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, the Space
 Science Board of the National Academy of Sciences - National Research Council -
 called for a goal that could "seize the imagination of men everywhere." The
 science panel recommended that the United States aim at Mars as the prime target
 in the search , for life in space. and proposed rocketing human explorers to the
 Red Planet by 1985.
 It picked Mars because of its relative nearness, its interesting qualities and
 said: tt Of even greater significance and excitement, to mankind, it affords the
 most likely prospect of bearing life. 11               ( * I)


 Yes indeed ••• Mars ••• with its white caps at the North and South poles, similar to
 those of Earth ••• the vast areas that grow darker in the spring, as though some
 vegetation was being fed by water from melting polar ice. Mars ••• with its net-
 work of lines that some term as "canals", intersecting at large areas that are
 called "oases". Mars ••• with its two. tiny moons that act so much like artificial
 satellites. Mars ••• the planet that may harbor living creatures ••• brothers from
 another world?
 Obviously, the recommendations of the scientific panel were considered valid and
 of the utmost importance; for, on November 28, 1964, the headlines leaped out at
 the public ••• "Mariner 4 Up In Space; Will Photograph Mars!" The main objectives
 ••• as released by NASA, were to to take 22 photos, study atmospheric density,
 radiation, magnetic fields, cosmic dust and other space properties.
 Strangely enough, after the scientific panel e~phasized the possibility ~f life
 on Mars, it was announced that Mariner 4 was not designed to detect life! The
 seven and one-half month journey will place the date of rendezvous with Mars at
 approximately July I4, 1965.                     (* 2)                       (over)
