    Our Review                                                                       Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  The most detailed topographic map available for the area around Tikaboo peak, the last remaining
  viewpoint into Area 51. A useful supplement to the Area 51 Viewer's Guide if you plan to hike this
  mountain. This map is know officially at the Badger Spring (NV) USGS 7-1/2 minute topographic
  quadrangle. The scale is 1:24000 (1 mile is about 2.5"). The features shown include Tikaboo Peak, Badger
  Mountain, Badger Spring, Badger Valley and portions of the Pahranagat and East Pahranagat Ranges.
  Shipped rolled in a tube. -- Glenn Campbell

  Reservations

  These recent features are not shown: (1) the extension of the Badger Valley road about 1/4 mile beyond Badger Spring;
  (2) the hiking trail up Tikaboo Peak; and (3) the extension of the East Paranagat Range road to meet the Badger Valley
  road.

  Comparison to Other Books

  You will still need Area 51 Viewer's Guide for Tikaboo Peak hiking instructions.
