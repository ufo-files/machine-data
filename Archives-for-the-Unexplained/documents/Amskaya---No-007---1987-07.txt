New Me Pioneer
     In the current issue of "The Ley Hunter", Paul Devereux  reviewed
Philip Heselton's biography of Tony Wedd, and spoke of the debt we owe
Tony  for  revitalising the ley ideas of Watkins, and appreciating his
catalytic influence on events in the UFO and earth                       mysteries   fields.
However,      in    his final paragraph he has this to say:                "One only wishes
he were alive now, for were he to bring  his  original  mind  to  bear
against  the  much increased background of knowledge and understanding
we now possess, he might have been able to punch  us  through  to  yet
further  undreamt-of areas of thinking and investigation. Of one thing
I am certain;       he would not be thinking the same thoughts about UFOs                  a
quarter of a century on. "  In fact Tony stuck to his principles to the
end  of  his  life in 1980;  just over a year before his death he wrote
this in his magazine "The Crow":               "As our horizons         inexorably   expand,
the planets that once seemed so remote. . .have suddenly become our near
neighbours.        How long before they become our dear neighbours as well?"
 Yes,     I   too    wish    he       was   alive now - I am sure he would have very
abrasive comments to make about the current vogue for discrediting our
space    friends.
