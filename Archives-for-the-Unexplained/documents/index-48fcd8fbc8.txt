    Technical comments

    I'm working in a chip manufacturing company, and have among others, a good know-how in electronic design. After quickly reading through the
    technical details of the tcl logic, it appears to me to be very suspect. All the pictures look "borrowed". For instance, using the picture of a slotted
    pentium to put an ACC logo with a drawing software seems odd for its prototype TCL. Showing a standard wafer doesn't fit the description of a
    unified field for data storage, etc.. Now even strange, pretending an XOR gate as the simplest gate is plain wrong: an XOR gate in its simplest
    configuration is made of 4 NAND gates... Bye bye TCL logic.
