  This is a single-color map of the entire state showing hundreds of former towns, town sites, mining camps
  and possible roads leading to them. Most of these sites consist only of some mine workings & stone
  foundations, while others are more recognizable as "towns", with a few houses and mine buildings still
  standing. This map does not distiguish the type of town or camp, but it does provide a starting point for
  exploration. Can be used as a supplement to the Delorme Nevada Atlas and the Ghost Town Atlas. Town
  sites and roads are drawn on a Nevada Bureau of Mines topgraphic base map. -- Glenn Campbell
