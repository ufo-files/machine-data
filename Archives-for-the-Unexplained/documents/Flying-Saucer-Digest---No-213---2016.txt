pebbles from the sky,- or somewhere.
   The pelting has been going on for months. Nobody could find out where the pebbles were coming from
or why. It was spooky for the Lowes. On Halloween night they gave up the ghost and moved out.
   "The rocks were falling from all directions," they said. About four times a week- at all hours of the day            ·



and night - rocks up to four inches long would rain on the house and an area of about two city blocks.
   Lowe called the sheriff, who sent investigators, who figured somebody had it in for the Lowes. But 1f it's

the hood of a patrol car and another whiStled past a deputy's car.

   Lowe's theory: "I think it might be the wind blowing off that rocky ridge a tenth of a mile away - the
closest hill. I've seen pine needles streaming paSt at treetop level when rocks fell."

continue to fall. Source: Cleveland Plain Dealer November 10, 1962.
