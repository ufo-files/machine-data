          Boom for the merchants of death, Bust for us
          by Dick Eastman

          Did I ever tell you that I taught microeconomics, macroeconomics,
          money and banking, labor economics, and current issues at a four-year
          college in washington state in the 90's? During those years I made a
          basic discovery that it is once again time to put out on the internet.
          It is wrong to say that GDP (the retail value of everything made and
          sold or added to inventory that year and services (repairing,
          brokering, cutting hair etc.) is a measure in any sense of the well
          being or standard of life of the nation.
          I would disprove the value of GDP as an indicator of social well being
          with this reducto ad absurdum thought demonstration.
          Suppose, on the first day of the year everyone but Congress and the
          President could died of pneumonia, and suppose Congress then
          authorized and the President wrote the check or a company in Mexico to
          come in, and bury all the bodies in the Grand Canyon, and say that the
