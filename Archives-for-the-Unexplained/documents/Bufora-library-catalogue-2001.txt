                                     BUFORA LIBRARY CATALOGUE

Welcome to this the first BUFORA Library catalogue. The library has just been fully re-catalogued to make its unique
contents more easily available to all members of BUFORA.
         You can use the postal service to access those books & videos in the lending library section of this catalogue. All
books and other publications in the sections marked as not available for loan are not available this way; these books may
only be viewed in person at the library or another pre-arranged place, these books are either too old, rare or too valuable
to be taken away from the library except in special circumstances.
         The library itself is stored in the ASSAP (Association for the Scientific Study of Anomalous Phenomena) Library
which is housed in the University of London Library. Access to the library is possible if you contact me in advance. I aim to
spend a day in the library once a month and it is on this day that I will retrieve any books required by members and will
make access available to anyone who needs to view old, rare or valuable books not available by post. Access to the
ASSAP library is only possible if you are accompanied by myself. Access is not available via the normal library network.

                                       THE POSTAL SERVICE WORKS LIKE THIS:

1)      Send me a list of the books you require (maximum of 3) from the library and a stamped self addressed
        envelope.

2)      I will send to you a Pro-forma invoice detailing whether the books are available and how much the
        postage and packaging will cost you.


3)       You send me a cheque for the required amount.

4)      I send you your books.

5)      You read your books and then return them to me within 3 months.

        It's as simple as that.

                                        THE RULES OF THE BUFORA LIBRARY:

1)      Please treat the books with great care, do not write in the margins, highlight text or bend the corners.
        A charge will be made to replace damaged or lost books.

2)      Books must be returned within 3 months unless an extended period is arranged by the librarian.

3)      A maximum of 3 books may be loaned to each member at a time.

Remember the BUFORA library is YOUR library, if you have any comments or suggestions please let me know by writing
to me at the above address, or e-mailing me at ghosthunter@dial.pipex.com. If you would like a postal reply please don't
forget to enclose a stamped self addressed envelope.
         If you have any books, magazines or any other publications that you wish to donate to the library please contact
me. Any donations are gratefully received.

Adam S Bailey
BUFORA Librarian
