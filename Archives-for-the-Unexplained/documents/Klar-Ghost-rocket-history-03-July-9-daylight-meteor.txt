The July 9th meteor

The July 9th daytime meteor

Introduction

Noone reported having seen two phenomena (one after the other).

Weather map from SMHI

Sightings by county

A total of more than 300 sightings from 17 counties have been noted in our ScanCat computer file. Direction from the observation place (bearing), an important factor in this case, has been noted, in following the report texts, as underlined italics.

The mass of sightings has been divided by county and a rough directional analysis has been performed for each county.


>> Västerbotten (AC) <<

5 sightings reported from the northernmost county in the observation area. From Västerbotten the bearing to the phenomenon was generally southerly, with one exception (Burträsk). All observers saw a blue or green flame. We present the sightings from north to south:

Lycksele: ”Teacher Staffan Åberg, who on Tuesday afternoon was on his veranda...saw, in a southeasterly direction, something that looked to him like a falling star with a clearly discernable bluish flame. It was right in the middle of the day, at 14.35... He had not thought further of the matter until he heard on the radio about sightings made at other places.” (Västerbottens-Kuriren, July 12).

Burträsk: 14.15-14.30, Mrs. A. Wikström, fire phenomenon with tail, bluish shine, great speed, northeasterly (?) direction. (Defence Staff table of sightings, sheet 4, position 18, sighting nr 58).

Långsele: 14.30, Mrs. Linnea Strandlund, silvery ball. luminous tail in the southwest. (There are several places called Långsele so the geographical position is uncertain). (Defence Staff, sheet 8, position 19, sighting nr 194).

Örträsk: Johan Dahlgren, chairman of the local government board, and a couple of other persons: ”...a powerful fireball with a bright, greenish light and a long, sparkling tail behind. Seemed to pass so close that one could almost believe that it came to ground in the southeastern part of the [lake] Örträsket, something which could not reasonably have been the case. The light from the object was of such a strength that if the incident had occured in darkness, the whole neighbourhood would have been lit up. The incident occured at about 14.30 hours on Tuesday.” (Västerbottens-Kuriren, July 13).

Taflesjön, 1 km E of Umeå: ”In reference to Högkv:Fst avd L nr 7:49, June 12, 1946, I have herewith the honour of submitting report concerning light phenomenon, which on July 11 was reported to this unit by repairman Arthur Hansson, Häradshöv-dinggatan 2, Umeå. Place of sighting: from the northern tip of [lake] Taflesjön (1 km E of Umeå). Time: July 9, 14.30-15.00. Duration: about 2 seconds. Angle of elevation: about 10°. Course: from West to East. Direction from the place of observation: over the mid-part of the Taflesjön. Description of the phenomenon: an approximately 10 m long blue-green streak with relatively even light, no sound or smoke. /signed/ A. Westrin, acting as regimental commander, /signed/ Erik Persson. [Comment: In the Defence Staff table ”southerly” has been entered under ”bearing”, for this sighting] (Report from the I20 regiment of Umeå, Defence Staff table, sheet 4, position 27, sighting nr 66).
