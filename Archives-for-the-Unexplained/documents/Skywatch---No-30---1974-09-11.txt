        "Contact" fills a vacuum and has a great contribution to make
    but this is only possible provided that we all conduct our­
    selves, our statements and our investigations with circumspection
    and with an emphasis upon the evidence of our own eyes and that
    which we know to be fact. If we wish to obtain the co-operation
    of legitimate scientists, we have to beware of airy-fairy atti­
    tudes, and that which I call the "Fourth Dimensional Syndrome ...
    In other words, .. If you can•t explain it, relegate it to the
    fourth dimension... In so doing we are not only abandoning the
    prime position of true research , which is to be ready to admit
    that our theories are but theories, and that we really do not
    know anything for certain, but by so doing, we are denying our­
    selves the right of being considered as anything other than de­
    luded cranks and crackpots.

         In Skywatch No. 29 a 11 Scientist11 explains a message from
    outer space as meaning that the people who sent it live in the
•
    11fourth dimension", because the man in the piece was pointing at
    a square. This prompts me to ask, was he a scientist, or one
    of the crackpots?

        In the first place, the existence of a fourth dimension pre­
    supposes a universe parallel to our own, invisible to our instru­
    ments in its entirety, which means that all the frequencies of
    its spectrum, including both radio and television frequencies
    along with all the others, would be out of coincidence with our
    three dimensional universe, and therefore, to all intents and
    purposes, would not exist. How then, would we pick up a fourth
    dimensional signal on our receivers? All mumbo- jumbo aside!

      Secondly, in mathematics the square of the number rep­
resents a number multiplied by itself O N CE. It is simplicity
itself, as is all mathematics - nothing but pure logic.

      Surely it is obvious that if a race of people on a
distant planet, our earth, had developed sufficiently to rec�
eive a televised message, their mathematical knowledge would
be on a par with their technology, and able to receive and
understand more complicated symbols than the square, which,
lets face it, is only two dimensional! No, I must look upon
this interpretation as one more manifestation of the fourth
dimensional syndrome.

      My interpretarion - assuming that the alleged decoding
was the correct one, and not misplaced enthusiasm over an un­
usual pattern of static - would be like this: 11We are dupli­
cates of yourselves as you can see from the pattern, and, we
emphasise this by showing you the square which indicates the
first step of an ever repeated pattern - ad infinitum ...

      It is reasonable to assume that if there was in fact a
message, it came, by the very nature of the transmission, from
somewhere very close to us, in our own galaxy, and in our own
three dimensional universe, from people like us who, at the
time at which the message was transmitted had no knowledge of
rapid, i. e. long distance, space travel, but who at the same
time, had assumed, and it appears quite rightly so, that who­
soever received the message would be basically a humanoid type,
a type which this, if true, gives us reason to believe is fair­
ly universal.
       It also indicates that ( a ) they have received similar
transmissions themse·lves; ( b ) · that they have had direct con­
tact with others like themselves; ( c ) that they are within
1 5 light years of ourselves and through some freak of trans­
mission have picked up one of our own early TV signals. By the
very nature of TV transmission they must have a highly accurate
idea of just where we are, to be able to do this, but at the
same time it would place them in the range of say Vega or Alpha
Centauri - as I do not believe that our Solar system is inhabit­
ed by any beings other than ourselves.

      If there is a fourth dimension of the sort evisaged by our
                            - 9 -
run of the mill enthusiast - and I do not deny the possib­
ility - I would imagine that they would have sufficient
problems of their own within their own· 11 Infinite Universe ..
without courting even more trouble in ours.

      I feel that the answer to the materialisation-dematerial­
isation or fourth dimensional aspect of Ufo sightings is that
these very obviously advanced persons from our own, and the
only known, for certain, universe have mastered to a high
degree the Unified Field - the fourth dimension of the
mathematician - which would enable them to travel really vast
distances at incredible speeds, covering intergalactic space
in the twinkling of an eye. For the Unified Field implies
that all points in time and space at any given moment are one,
and that time and space therefore do not technically exist, or,
are for the moment in a state of suspension, and therefore
in an entirely different context to the traveller. Under these
circumstances travel from one part of the universe to the other
would be virtually instantaneous.

      Of course, there would be a danger of arr1v1ng at the end

but since the preponderance of space over solid objeds in our
universe is so vast, the chances of this taking place would be
so minimal as to be absolutely remote. None the less, it would
require a technology that was many thousands of years in advance
of our own, and planetary resources beyond our wildest dreams.
Who knows, it is but a theory!

      O ut of some 48 sightings I have had since 1936 I am pre­
pared to concede that 12 were of extraterrestrial origin, and
of these 10 sightings were made in broad daylight where I was
able to bring binoculars to bear upon the object and, with the
exception of the 1936 sighting, my wife, or some other person
was present on each occasion.

      Let us endeavour to evaluate any information in a truly
unbiased and scientific manner, dealing with facts as facts
and theories as theories, and not by believing in something
because we would like it to be true. Let us not mix oil
with water!

      CONTACT   S.A.   CONFERENCE -   AUGUST,     1974.

      The third National Contact Conference was held this year
at the same venue as on the two previous occasions - Everglades
Hotel, Lions River, in the foothills of the Drakensberg - over
the weekend of the 16th/17th/18th August� 1974.   It proved to
be a most enjoyable weekend with members making the most of the
fresh mountain air, long hikes (and for those who felt fit -
climbs) and congenial atmosphere.
      Unfortunately, due to the petrol restrictions, only three
members from Johannesburg were able to attend ( including the
Chairman of the Johann-sburg branch, Mrs. Ee Klarer) and a
special vote of thanks is extended to them for the effort made.
The rest of the party of 22 was made up of Durban members.

      We realise that distance and the petrol restrictions do
create a problem, but we are hopeful that in future members
from all over South Africa will be able to attend the National
Conferences . This is an ideal way of promoting fellow-feeling
and giving an extra spurt of enthusiasm to members of Contact
S.A., and certainly each and everyone of us has something to
give - and in the giving, receives.
