                          Free Newsletter Supports New Age Writers

        Writer's Resources announces the creation of The New Age Writer , a free
        newsletter dedicated to writers interested in new age and alternative markets.
         "Through this newsletter we hope to bring together those of us who express our
        creative selves through the written word, and to provide information and sup­
        port to writers interested specifically in the new age and alternative markets,"
         says Publisher Elizabeth Gould. Adds Editor Barbara Cauper, "We see a distinct
        lack of resources for the new age writer, and we want to change that. We feel it is
        important that new age writers have a place to share experiences and have ready
        access to marketing information and freelance possibilities . "


        As publishers of the new age writer's market guide, New Choices for Writers,
         Gould and Cauper are on familiar terms with the editors and publishers of this
        rapidly growing writer's market. "We have had an excellent response from both
        magazine and book editors. They are as enthusiastic as we are about getting
         organized and networking. Many editors are actively searching for articles,
         stories and book manuscripts, but, until now, they have not had a place to let
        writers L11terested in these fields knu\Al about it," says Gould.


         In addition to marketing advice and updates, writer's tips, and advice from the
         editors, the New Age Writer is a place for like-minded writers to share their
         successes and challenges. "We hope that writers will send in their writing-related
         experiences. It can be very inspiring to hear about the success of another," says
         Cauper.


         Writers interested in receiving a free subscription to The New Age Writer should
         send their name and address to: Writer's Resources, NAW, 15 Margaret's Way,
         Nantucket, MA 02554.
