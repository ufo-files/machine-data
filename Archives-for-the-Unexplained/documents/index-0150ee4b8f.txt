    Our Review                                                                                            Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  This book that takes a sociological look at the entire UFO picture. Keith Thompson takes a step back, and
  looks over the whole strange field of "Ufology". Are UFOs real? That's not really important to Thompson.
  What is important are the magnificient stories of the "investigation" of the phenomenom, which strangely
  parallel some of our most deep rooted mythology. This book takes a decidedly new and refreshing look at
  the whole wacky subject, and will give both believers and disbelievers something to think about.
