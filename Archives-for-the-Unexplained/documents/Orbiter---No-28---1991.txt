                      Watch Out for the Swirls!
                           By: Jim Melesciuc

It was only a matter of time for the "English Swirls" to make their
debut in the U.S.  Recently, the electronic media and the press
provided ample coverage to the British circle phenomena, with extreme
detail.  Now these circles have shown up in the states of Indiana,
Kansas, South Dakota, naturally Gulf Breeze, FL., and probably
elsewhere.

These swirls, unlike their counterparts called "Saucer Nests" or
"Landing Traces", back in the 1950s, 60s, and 70s,   are unique in that
the neatly flattened areas do not exhibit the kind of rough damage
caused by harsh weather, animals, or humans.  Interestingly, when one
studies the reported landing  effects UFOs have had in the past, prior
to the hyped "swirl" publicity, much of this activity was described as
"scorched, denuded circles" and "landing marks".   (1)

In 1969 - 70 New Zealand was inundated with scorched mystery circles,
ranging from the areas of Taupo, Kaharoa, Whitford, and Te Awamutu. (2)
(3) UFO researcher Ted Philips has collected hundreds of UFO - ring
reports worlwide and feels that these traces are significant because
they remain available for study in a laboratory, unlike the elusive UFO
itself.

However, what part of these mystery circles can be attributed to the
UFO phenomenon and used as significant data in research?  After
studying the information I had accumulated on the British circles, to
be truthful, I was a bit disillussioned.  Simply, because scores of
circles were being formed and suddenly. after an explaination was given
to the nature of their  cause. by Physicist Dr. Meaden. their
configurations transfigured into bizarre patterns. to say the least.
It appeared that whoever or whatever was responsible in creating some
of the circles wanted to offset Dr. Meaden's explaination and went well
out of their in doing so.

Comparing the new redesigned configurations to the patterns seen in
weather maps suggests that someone could be making jest of Dr. Meaden's
meteorlogical vortex theory.  Could the weather map figures have
triggered an idea for someone to poke "meteorlogical -fun" at Dr.
Meaden?  Or is it just coincidental?  Notice the figures indicating
wind speed and compare it to the 'redesigned' swirl pattern illustrated
on p.13.

In my opinion, a considerable amount of the British "Mystery Swirls"
has satisfactory been explained by Dr. Meaden (editor of the Journal of
Meteorology). His theory is: (5) The circles are formed by the impact
of a body of fast - spinning air that has been partially ionized. A
columnar atmospheric vortex, with a vertical or inclined axis, provides
the channel for the formation of a plasma (ionised gas) vortex and for
its conduction towards the ground.    The ionisation of the air ought to
be sufficient to make the vortex luminous at night and the fast spin
may make the vortex appear ball -shaped.

An International team of Scientists taking part in Operation Blackbird,
keeping night vigil over a field,  excidedly announced they had
captured on film the making of circles amid a barrage of flashing
lights in a field. After going public with their findings they then
decided to have a closer look at the field.  The experts found inside
the flattened circles ouija boards - commonly used in occult rituals,
and crosses. (�)  Somebody had a good laugh.

So far, no reported landings and little or no nocturnal - lights have yet
been mentioned in any of the US "mystery swirls".     The only mention of
UFOs involvement was based on speculation by a MUFON representative,
Grant Callison, MUFON State Director, regarding the Milan, IL. swirl he
stated, · " I examined it. and since there are no human footprints. and
neither the wind nor a helicopter could have done it. the only thing
you can't rule out is a UFO". (6)
                                     -   2-
During the Indiana case investigation a young teen admitted to creating
the circles and informed the MUFON investigators at the site, of his
hoax.  Furthermore, the teen's father went as far as appearing on T.V.
to confess his sons involvement in making the circles.  The teen
acquired the idea by watching a segment  of the British Corn Circles,
aired on NBC's Unsolved Mysteries. (7)


In Gulf Breeze, FL. it was noted that the mystery circles discovered by
Ed Walters and Duane Cook, on Nov. 1989 and the other on 28 Feb. 1990,
could have possibly been created by the use· of C0/2.....  A local
investigator in the area discussed this with agricultural experts and
they tend to support this theory. The freezing of the grass by C0/2
causes the stem to be limp when they thaw, which permits easier
manipulation into patterns. When the grass recovers from the freeze it
should be in a semi - permanent pattern. (8)

So what can we' say· is significant of these U.S. Swirls being splashed
about in the press?    I for one enjoy a little mystery and intrigue  but
the evidence strongly suggests that the current U.S. swirls were
created either by a natural phenomena, hoaxes, and possibly other
mechanisms.  Also, partially at fault are investigators, knowingly or
unknowingly, guilty of subjectivity and emotional involvement during
the course of investigations.

Interestingly, the swirls of today have no similarity to the so - called
scorched denuded landing traces of yesteryear. However, there are some
reports of swirls of grass - saucer nests - if you will. Hard packed
imprints, scorched grass, with a burning odor associated with the
swirls.  Some of these swirls have been reported during "flap
activitiy" and during "close encounter observations".

So, another question must be raised about these current U.S. swirls.
Where are the UFOs that suppose to be creating them?  Or are these
swirls the latest fad from England soon to be embeded into our culture
- like the Fab-Four of the sixties!
