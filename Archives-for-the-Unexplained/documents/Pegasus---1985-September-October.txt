S1013.             THE "F IVE CIRCLE" MYSTERY.            Event Date 3/8/85



We were introduced into this strange phenomena, when on 7 th July
this year, Omar Fowler and P�ul Whitehead were requested to investigate
a report of circles in a wheatfield at Alresford in Hampshire.


The field was duly visited and a number of photographs taken of the
site. However, owing to the opposition encountered from the farm hands
and local gamekeeper, the stay at the site had to be curtailed.


 At this stage we were left with a far from satisfactory situation,
whereby we were unable to make much headway with all of the questions
that this event had raised.      We had learnt from one farmhand that
circles of this nature had appeared in a nearby field "The P unchbowl"
during 1982 ( 3 rings ) and 1983 (5 rings ) , all about the same time
of the year ( July ) .   We later contacted Mr Pat Dalgado, a design
engineer, who had studied the mystery over previous years and were
subsequently introduced to Chris Wood, a Daily Express photographer
who has made an extensive study of this phenomena and has accurate
measurement records of all known circles that have been found.


The mystery is indeed an astounding one and at this stage we will
not endeavour to itemize every known fact about the rings discovered
up to that time. We will however proceed onto the next event that
took place and that was the discovery of a further set of five rings
in a wheatfield near Andover, Hants. on 3rd August 1985.       This was
subsequently investigated by us in great detail and this is the subject
of the main report covered in this issue.


The Andover Case S.1013.


The rings were first seen by a pilot Mr'Busty'Taylor when returning
to Thruxton Airfield ( Hants. ) on the evening of Saturday 3rd August.
While unable to divert and study the circles at that time, Mr Taylor
flew over the area several times on Sunday 4th, whenever a break in the
weather enabled him to do so. A series of excellent aerial photographs
were taken from both a fixed wing aircraft and a helicopter from the
Thruxton Airfield ( See Cover P hoto ) .   Further photographs were taken
on Monday both from the air and later during the day from ground
level, after the site had been located. The photo's were in colour
and black and white ( We have copies of these now on file ) •

                     A CLOSE ENCOUNTER   ON   BAGSHOT   HEATH.         15/9/85
S. 1008


 On the night of Sunday 15th September 1985 at 9pm, the Me Murray family
 were driving home after visiting friends in Guildford, Surrey.           They
 were travelling along the busy A32 2 towards Bagshot and then their car
 turned off the main road at the 11Gordon Boys School" roundabout, heading
for Farnborough down Red Road (8311)- the time was three minutes past
 nine.    In the car was Mr Oavid Me Murray, his wife Sue and two small
 children.


 They had travelled only about a quarter of a mile down the road, when
 as they turned a slight bend in the road, they were met with an amazing
 sight.   A n object was hovering over the road (see sketch) .. only fifty to
 sixty yards ahead of them, the light was so intense that for a moment
 David Me Murray turned his head away.        Then as they continued to approach
 it, another similar object came up from behind the trees on the left
 of the road and travelled along side them just behind the trees skirting
 the road. They noticed briefly that another car following them had come
 to an abrupt stop and pulled to the side of the road.           Meanwhile the
 first object that had been hovering over the road had shot off skyward
 and disappeared.


This incident had occurred along a lonely stretch off road in heathland
 adjoining the Bagshot/Lightwater area.       There is a Tumuli within a hundred
yards of the initial sighting and a national grid power line crosses
 the road at the same point.


 Mr Me Murray made a quick decision to continue down the road as fast as
he could for about a mile, where there is a new property development and
 the chance to run to a nearby house.     As he turned the bend with.the
 houses in sight, he drew over to the opposite side of the road, stopped
and got out of the car. The children were asleep in the back of the car
and Mrs Me Murray locked her door and refused to get out.


 A s their car stopped, so did the UFO and it hovered over the top of the
 trees just across the other side of the road, at a height of about fifty
 feet.    Mr Me Murray stood watching "it" and he got the impression.that
"it" was watching him. He heard a buzzing noise "just as though someone
 had started up an electric sewing machine". He noticed that underneath
 the object was a brilliant red light on one side and a green light under
 the other. A round the body of the object were a number of dazzling
 white lights, but no sign o f any movement or rotating lights.
                                                                                 8
 This strange scene of the silent but brilliantly lit UFO hovering over
the trees and the occupants of the car just as silently watching the
UFO continued for about a minute and a half and then the object quite
suddenly accelerated and was gone, visible only as a star like object
in the distance.   Mr Me Murray jumped back into his car and drove back
up the slight hill from where he had come, in order to get a better
view, but there was no sign off the object and it had completely gone
from sight.   The car that had pulled over to the side of the road was
still there. A white Valva, with the occupant sitting inside with his
interior light on. Mr Me Murray did not stop and talk to the driver,
but on reflection, wishes that he had done so.


In a later interview on the following night Mrs Me Murray also gave
her impression of the incident. The object was "saucer" shaped and
appeared to be flat underneath, with large "spotlights" on the rim,
with a bright red light underneath on one side and a bright bluey green
light on the other.


Mr Me Murray and his family then continued their journey home, they
reported the incident to the local P olice Station on the way, where
his experience was duly noted. He also telephone Heathrow ( Control:Tower )
and then reported the incident to West Drayton Air T ra f fic Centre.


A fter Effects:-

T he following morning the whole family, including the children, one of
five months and one of sixteen_ months, all overslept.    Normally, the
children are awake at a quarter to six, but on this particular morning
they had to be woken up when Mr and Mrs Me Murray awoke at nine thirty
a. m.


Mr Me Murray then went to start his car, but there was no sound from
the engine and he discovered that his battery was completely flat.
He then received assistance from a nearby garage ahd the car started.


Within a few hours of the following day ( Monday 16th ) , Mr Me Mur�ay
developed a severe headache on the crown of his head, so severe· that
he sought medical advice from his doctor and was immediately sent for
a check up at the local hospital , E.C.G. and Blood P ressure were found
to be O.K.    He then developed symptoms of a "hollow" feeling in his
stomach and ttshaky11 legs. These symptoms persisted for some eight to nine
days after the "event" and by then, his wife had also suffered the effect
of "shaky" legs and a 11hollow11 stomach for the final three days.

CONFIRMA TION OF UFO A CTIVITY 15th September.

The same night at B.45pm Mr & Mrs Webb were driving along the M4           in
the direction of the Wokingham turning (approx 12 miles from Bagshot
Heath), when they saw a bright red glowing object flying across the
sky from the direction of Bagshot. Mrs Webb commented      n   I am sure it
was not an aircraft. It made me think it could have been a so called UFO".


Later that night at 1145pm Lyn Brookes and her boy friend saw a strange
object with flashing lights (red    &   white), after about five minutes,
it dipped down behind some trees.       The observation was made from the
Wokingham area. Miss Lyn Brookes commented " I was really frightened,
it was if it was watching me". Her Great Dane dog was "disturbed & whining".


FOLLOW UP INVESTIG ATION.

The area .adjoining the road where the Me Murray incident took place
was later inspected for possible landing marks/features without anything
being found.   The occupants of the new housing estate were questioned
about any strange happenings in the area. There were reports that about
a year previous, flashes had been seen coming from the nearby power
cables at night and this had caused dimming of the lights in the houses.


Humming noises at night have been reported from the Lightwater area
over the past few years. They appear to have been coming from the
common land normally used by the army for exercises, but it was not
thought that the noises heard (3 am) were anything to do with the army
excercises.
