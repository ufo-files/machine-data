                  :IUFOPRA UFO CONFERENCE                                        -   1997 :

Earlier this year we contacted Mr Graham Birdsall, of QUEST INTERNATIONAL and Editor of that
most prestigious and world famous publication, UFO MAGAZINE, with a view to arranging Ireland's
first ever one day UFO Conference. As this year marks the 50th Anniversary ofUfology and Roswell,
we wanted to do something very special to celebrate these historical events, and it was with this in mind
that we contacted Graham.However, an event like this just doesn't happen, it has to be planned, and so
began our 'Quest' for suitable venues in the Dublin Area which involved writing to several Hotels and
the RDS Concert Hall in Ballsbridge. On acquiring this information, we quickly sent it off to Graham
for his approval. But the best was yet to come when we received a very enthusiastic reply from Graham
indicating that he would be willing to come to Dublin to meet us, check out the venues and discuss the
pros and cons of sponsoring such a conference. Originally, the date set for Graham and his colleague
Russell Callaghan, to visit Dublin was Saturday May 24th, however, due to the British Bank Holiday
and the non-availability of flights to Dublin, it had to be postponed much to our disappointment.
In due course, another date, Saturday, June 7th was duly set, and as we, Pat and myself, headed for
Dublin Airport that morning, we had more than post-election fever on our minds. As we approached the
Arrivals Hall, the tension was mounting, the andrenalin flowing and the exictement buzzing as we
watched the crowd of holiday makers arriving and we were both on a "High". Their flight from Leeds
was in to Dublin at 11.15 am but as we anxiously looked at the monitor it told us that the flight was
delayed until 11.30 am. But we didn't care - we were too happy and excited to worry about that and we
kept our eyes peeled on the monitor until we saw the magic word "LANDED" flashed across the screen.
At long last, the moment we had been waiting for finally arrived and at approximately 11.50 am on a hot
Saturday morning Graham and Russell finally appeared and made their way towards us much to our
delight. After brief introductions and small talk we went to the car park and eventually found our car,
where we then took them to the Skylon Hotel in Drumcondra where they would be staying overnight.
At the Skylon we were met by two of our Committee Members, Dermot Butler (A.I.) and Carl Nally,
our Director of Membership and PRO. After Graham and Russell checked in they joined us in the main
foyer where we had a brief discussion on the pros and cons of organising a Conference. This brought us
up to lunchtime and by 1.15 pm 6 very hungry people headed for the dining room. Lunch was most
relaxing and pleasant with the conversation mainly focused on our favourite topic - Ufology. For us, the
most enjoyable part was just listening to Graham and Russell recounting the most remarkable and
fascinating accounts of the current spate of Triangular UFO Sightings over England. It was such a
pleasure to listen to them. If we didn.'t have venues to look at that afternoon there is no doubt that we
would have stayed all day listening to them speak. So at 2.15 pm that afternoon we managed to drag
ourselves away and head for our first venue. Carl and Dermot left at this time also to attend to other
matters and promised to return later in the evening for the "Night Shift". (More on this later).

We arrived at the RDS at 2.55 pm approximately where we were met by a young lady called Carol,
who is the Commercial Officer for the RDS, and who had agreed to meet us there as the venue is
normally closed to the public at weekends. Having thoroughly checked out the area, Acoustics,
Lighting etc, Graham asked for a price, which turned out to be way beyond what would nromally
be expected for a venue of this size. Besides, there were many hidden extras which we were not told
about at the onset. We left the RDS after 20 mins.and the decision had already been made that
the Conference would not be held at the RDS. We then headed down the road to Jury's Hotel and
Towers where we waited to meet the Banqueting Manager. While we were waiting we decided to have
a drink in the Lounge. However, just before our drinks arrived, a young lady approached us and took
Graham and Russell away to look at the Conference Room. That left Pat and Anne to mind the drinks!
and when Graham and Russell returned 15 minutes later they were looking very happy and pleased with
themselves. They were very impressed with what they saw and decided that this was ideal for a one-day
Conference and ·it suited their requirements perfectly. By 5 pm that evening, a date had been set for
Ireland's first ever one-day UFO Conference. After leaving Jury's four very tired, but extremely happy
people arrived back at the Skylon Hotel, where we-reluctantly parted company with Graham and
Russell. As we said our goodbyes we thanked both of them for their help and invaluable assistence, in
the planning of this Conference. We are very happy to announce that the Conference will take place on
Saturday 8th November, 1997 at Jury's Hotel and Towers in Ballsbridge, Dublin. A Timetable and
ticket prices will be announced as soon as possible as these items are awaiting discussion and approval
by our Committee.The Conference will be advertised both nationally and Internationally and we can tell
you that the tickets will be very reasonably priced and we've no doubt that as many of our Members as
possible, their families (yes, you can even bring your Granny along) their friends, will support us in this,
our first major and important venture.If this is successful, then we would hope to make this a major
annual event, with Dublin being among the many great UFO capitals of the world. So what happened at
the Skylon after we left? Well, our hotel spies, reliably informed us that Carl, Dermot, and Brendan
(another Committee Member who arrived later) met Graham and Russell in the Foyer at 6.30 pm
that evening where they talked the night away (in between drinking- our crowd that is!) mainly UFOs
and surprise, surprise, Football (Yes, Graham is a Leeds supporter, untill2 midnight when our crowd
decided that to finally let them go to bed, as it was an early start the next morning to catch a flight back
to Leeds. The final outcome was that everyone agreed that meeting Graham and Russell was a
magnificent experience and a truly enjoyable and rewarding day.Again, on behalf of all ofus, we would
very much like to express our deep gratitude and appreciation to both of them, for their willingness and
help, in sponsoring, what we hope will be a wonderful and momentous occassion and a landmark in
Irish Ufology.It was indeed a great pleasure for us to have them here.

                         AIRLINE PILOTS SEE UFO:
An Aer Lingus passenger jet had a close encounter with a mystery flying   object over England. The
pilots of a Stansted-bound BAe 146 aircraft reported seeing a red, white and blue striped aircraft fly by
them at high speed at 9,000 feet over Hertfordshire on a flight from Dublin. But British investigators
have now said that after extensive inquiries, an aircraft proximity team were unable to find any trace of
another plane being in the area. The report by the airprox working group said it felt that what had been
seen "was not an aeroplane".

Source: Evening Herald, Thursday June 12th, 1997.

about, you won't be disappointed.
