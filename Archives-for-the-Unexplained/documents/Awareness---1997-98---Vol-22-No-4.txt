      THE LONELINESS OF THE LONG DISTANCE UFOLOGIST


The present time marks the 20th year of my participation in Ufology and the adaption of the
well known 1960s play by Alan Sillitoe might well setve as my epitath.


In those 20 years I have reported not more than 5 possible UFO events to the group and all
of these were indeterminate "lights" rather than structured craft. At no time have I seen an
archetypal "saucer" or "cigar" or "triangle" or anything remotely resembling the alien forms
described by others. That does not necessarily mean I think what other people have seen is
hogwash, but I have not been so lucky, or more possibly, my psyche is such that I cannot
witness a UFO. What little I have seen has occurred when not looking out for UFOs rather
than at skywatches.     Conscious attempts to tune in fail - only those with relaxed minds and
no known interest in the subject seem to have the big sightings, apart from the occasional
"serial" spotter that sometimes emerges. That seems to prove something.


Many times individual witnesses report seeing UFOs in crowded areas when people all
around them see nothing.       Even photographs of purported large craft taken by the witness
turn out as pinheads when developed. This points to the witness( s) being directly effected in
mind by some controlling force which is focused on them alone, and a sort of hologram is
projected which is not a physical object.    What the possible reason or purpose behind what
this could be remains unanswered.       In the same way "abduction" has all the hallmarks of
stolen mind, and the few abductees I have investigated personally never showed what I
would consider the marks of physical abuse or having probes stuck inside them.
None-the-less the genuine fear and replicated timetables of many abductees indicates a
phenomenon at work which we do not begin to understand.              However I cannot see any
evidence that the abductors are real flesh-and-blood, but are more likely the product of an
induced mind state.


It was not until 1992, after 14 years of investigation, when I believe I made a breakthrough
into some form of communication with whatever is out there.         This was not exactly "seeing
the light" as those who aspire to religious faith call it, but as the result of a physical
experiment which thereafter made communication with "alien forces" a regular possibility.
Six years on I have maintained communication and not been locked up yet, so maybe I am
still sane!   What we are dealing with are invisible forms of controlled energy or "spirit" which
is the basis of all life. There can be no scientific proof of this but take it from me-------


Sad to say my 20th anniversary has been marred by a remarkable downturn in UFO interest
in the past year after the big rise around 1995, when "X-Files" and the purported "alien
body" story were all over the media. Unfortunately it seems that "marketing" and "hype" rule
the roost these days, and fashions come and go in months rather than years.           Most of the
new, glossy UFO magazines on the newstands look to have gone within 2 years, and the
bigger UFO research groups including ourselves are running into financial difficulties, but of
course there are always new ones coming along to take their place. "Recycling" is something
that always seems to work in this business!


As mentioned, our finances are becoming rather depleted, and there is unlikely to be any
conference in 1998.      The loss on the 1 997 conference was around £200, and the audience
was only half that of 1996 so risking another big show at the present time is not an option.

 be good grounding for the uninitiated, but for the hardened ufologist its a bit old hat, but
 useful to have on the bookshelf as an instant reference.

 Part 4,   The Photographs   is a small section only because the book rightly says: "There are
 hundreds of descriptions of UFO occupantS but only a handful of photographs!" Fakes are
 so easy to perform and even good photos are nearly always doubted if any should be
 published, but the majority are of such poor quality with practically no corroboration with
 them that for this reason they end up very thin on the ground.      The section is cleverly put
 together leaving the reader, in the main, to decide for himself which could be genuine or
 fake, but nonetheless this is very interesting from a photographic point of view, as I cannot
 recall seeing so many of the old pictures being contained in one section, to be able to make
 comparisons before.


 Part 5,     The Database     covering the wave of 1954 with detailed sections of sightings,
 witnesses etc, and the 1973 Occupant database, was compiled to show a certain pattern in
 these waves.   The 1973 wave namely being that the aliens were on a mission to Earth, and
 instucted to complete it as quickly as possible and leave.


 An interesting theory and one I certainly wouldn't argue with.
