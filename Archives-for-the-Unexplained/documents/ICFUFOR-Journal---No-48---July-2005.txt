  Eliyahoo Roizman reports: “I followed the helicopters who were training at 20:00. Suddenly I saw 2 yellow lights arriving,

  very good binoculars.”

  Havat Ha’Shikmim, 19/5/2005

  A high alertness was declared in the ranch (home place of Israel’s Prime Minister, Arik Sharon), after a report of Sderot
  citizens, about an “unidentified flying object” above Sderot’s industrial area.

  Cobra helicopters and F15 jet were launched. All the entrances and exits to the area were closed. “After an hour, a report
  came that nothing was found, no object or suspected person. The army claimed that after midnight nothing unusual was
  detected in the area.”

  This message was published in “Yediot-Ahronot” newspaper, at the 20/5/2005.

  Ramla- Tel Aviv road, 18/6/2005


  Benny Dorsky, age 70, reports: “we returned from an event in Nir-Zvi, which is not far from Kfar-Habad. We turned west
  on Ramla-Tel Aviv road, and drove to the south. I was sitting behind in the car. Through the window, I saw a landing
  airplane. Suddenly, a giant star-like object, bigger then a star crossed its way. The object was built of some star-colored
  objects. It had a long light line with a beginning and an end. It was approximately located at the height of 300 meters.

  I asked the driver to stop after Kfar-Habad. I, my wife and the driver saw a star-like object, bigger then planet Venus.
  Above the object were 2 close light spots. A long light line came out of the bigger spot, directed to the right – west. The
  object stood still. It didn't move. I observed tiny light spots in the left side of the big light spot. The drivers’ wife said: “first,
  let’s get out of here.” Later on, I continued looking at the object, while we were driving, till we got to Geha road, then we
  didn't see it anymore.

  We came home at midnight. I forgot the whole matter. After an hour, I decided to cal the police. The policewoman didn't
  make her best to help me, so I asked for the officer. The officer only opened a file (event No. 31, in Ramla police station). I
  asked the officer, to whom they are reporting the event. He answered that “we don't tell to whom I report.”

  I’m convinced it was a spaceship. I’m not decrying. Before this event, if someone would have told me such a story, I would
  have made a joke of him.”
