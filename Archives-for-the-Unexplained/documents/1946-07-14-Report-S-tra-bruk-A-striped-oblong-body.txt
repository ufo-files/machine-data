Case xxx: 
A striped, oblong body 

Time: July 14, 10.00 (a.m.)
Place: Sätra bruk, province of Västergötland,
probably Sättra 20 km NW of Karlsborg.

"Report concering light phenomenon (phoned in to the wing aide of F6 on July 15). 
"Reporter: Mr. Möll, Sätra Bruk, Västergötland.
"Time: 10.00 on July 14.
"Direction: northerly.
"Altitude: 600 - 800 meters.
"Course: towards northeast.
"Speed: exceeding 1.000 km/hour.
"Appearance: oblong body, striped crosswise with alternating dark and light bands. Weak buzzing 
sound. 
            Karlsborg July 15, 1946

            /signed C.G.Göransson, wing aide/

Note: No details of further investigations, but case listed in Defence Staff tabulation. Sättra is situated at the northern tip of lake Viken.

Status: Unidentified.
Source: Report to the Defence Staff's Air Defence Department from the F6 (Air Force Wing) at Karlsborg. ScanCat 1254.
