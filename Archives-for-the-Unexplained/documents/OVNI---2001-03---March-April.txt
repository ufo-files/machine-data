                                      THE NEVER ENDING STORY

     seemingly never ending mystery of the UFO phenomenon. Although we have come a long way in the past

     behaving strangely. Then gradually a picture began to emerge, the 'lights' appeared to be intelligently
     controlled and were reported to be following vehicles and 'landing' in remote. areas.
      There were already claims that actual 'craft' had been seen! One of the first 'contactees' had already come
    . forward, his name was George Adamski and in 1953 he published his story in 'Flying Saucers Have
      Landed'. George described how he had met a tall blonde Alien out in the desert. A type later to be
      classified as a 'Nordic'. But George Adamski's claims were hotly disputed,. Were not his photographs all
      fakes? The typical saucer craft (later to be known as an 'Adamski type'), showed three balls beneath the
      craft and it was claimed that the shape closely resembled a lampshade and also a type of cooling machine.

     years to verify that they had witnessed a similar event. This included a British Airways pilot that your

                   Extract from 'Our Ancestors Came From Outer Space'
                                 By Maurice Chatelain ( Pan Books 1980)


In 1928 European radio operators in France, Germany, Norway and Holland noticed a strange phe­
nomenon. When they transmitted in all directions, a series of signals, they received two series of echoes
instead of one. At an interval varying from 3 to 15 seconds. It was as if they had been bounced from some
object located at a distance from Earth of 4 50;000 to 2,250,000km, but always a little further from the
Moon.
A few years ago, a young Scottish astronomer by the name of of Duncan Lunan had a bright idea. He
thought that these signals could very well come from an Alien spaceship orbiting the Earth, at about the
same distance as the Moon.
The variable intervals between the transmission of signals and reception of echoes,. might represent an
intelligent coded message representing geometric figures or even the map of a constellation, as Bracewell
had already suggested in 1968.
Working on the television system of so many dots per line and so many lines per frame, Lunan transferred
the various intervals on a chart as he would have done on a television screen, He then successively obtained
several different drawings of the same constellation, with different orientations, but with the same star
always at the cent.re.
As Lunan states in his book 'Man and the Stars', as an astronomer, it did not take him long to recognise the
constellation as that of Bootis and the star as Epsilon Bootis, which our ancestors called Izar and which is
located 103 light years or 975 million, million km. from the Earth.
