  Clear Video of a Ghost - British Schoolboy

    by ryguy on Fri Feb 13, 2009 2:40 pm

  Just this week, a british schoolboy captured a pretty impressive video on his camera phone of a ghost walking toward
  the banisters at his family's home. The sister had mentioned a previous night that someone had mysteriously finished her
  jigsaw puzzle overnight.

  Video footage of ghost

  News story from the SUN

  We've seen recently, thanks to Steve's great writeup, how the Sun tends to do a poor job at times verifying (or not
  verifying rather) these stories. But I guess the footage speaks for itself. I haven't looked at it closely yet, just a few
  images - so can't speak to it's authenticity. As far as I can tell the witnesses seem credible, but again, I haven't learned
  much about them. The video itself is pretty interesting.
