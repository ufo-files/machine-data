        £11,000 flying

        saucer reward
             NEW           YORK,    Thursday.
       —
             A       reward        of       £11,000
       was    offered   today for
       proof of the existence of
       flying saucers.                  -




          Milton   Reynolds, mil
       lionaire    ballpoint  pen
       manufacturer,     said   he
       would    give that amount
                      .




       to "anyone who   can  prove
       scientificially the exist
       ence  of flying saucers."
             He           meant    by        flying
        saucer            an   interplanetary
        flying            object guided by
        living            beings    from       an
        other   planet.
           He   said he would   give
        an    additional £44,600 to
        anyone    who   could obtain
        a  ride for him- in a fly-
        ing       saucer.—
