                     SUMMARY ANALYSIS
            REMOTE VIEWING (RV) SESSION   #848

1. (S/NOFORN) This report provides documentation of a remote viewing
session conducted for training purposes only.

2. (S/NOFORN) Post analysis on the target viewing indicated no corr-
elation with the actual target. The viewer did not express much con-
fidence in his viewing immediately following the session, and stated
that he felt that there would be very little correlation following his
drawing.
3. (S/NOFORN) The protocol used for this session is detailed in the
document Standard Remote-Viewing Protocol (Remote Target) by Harold
E. Putthoff and Russel Targ, November 1978.
4. (S/NOFORN) Following is a transcript of the viewer impressions during
the remote viewing session. At TAB A are drawings with narrative made
by the remote viewer reference his target. At TAB 8 is the actual
map utilized for targeting with accompanying targeting data.
