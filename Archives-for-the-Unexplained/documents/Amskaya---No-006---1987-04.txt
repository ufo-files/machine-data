     Frank Stranges claimed that in 1959 he  met  a  Venusian  at  the
Pentagon  who  told him that there were 77 Venusians living undetected
on earth.  In England, journalist and author Arthur Shuttlewood claims
to have met beings from outer space who claim they have been living on
our planet for a long time.

     Back in America, Betty Andreasson claimed     she  was  abducted   by
aliens   who  are  living   on Earth in secret areas.   Patrolman Herbert
Schirmer claims to have been abducted by     extraterrestrials   who  have
bases   under  the  ocean,   off   the  coast of Argentina.   Carol! Watts
claims that in 1967 she    was  contacted,   and  that  there   are  alien
stations   all  over the world.    In Flying Saucer Review vol. 29, No. 1
the details of Luili Oswald's abduction were given.     She   claims  that
her   contacts  have  a   base   in  the  Antarctic, as well as a complex
oceanic tunnel system.

     American tabloid newspapers are full of reports of UFO bases, and
Arthur Shuttlewood believes a homing device is buried  on  Cley  Hill,
Warminster.   In  1984 UFO bases were mentioned in connection with the
Rendelsham Forest incident in 1980.

      For over two years, my  colleagues  and  I  have  investigated  a
 psychic  contactee.  We sat in on his contacts, similar to the contact
.methods of Philip Rodgers and Dan Fry.  We have been told they have  a
 base on the moon.

      Of  course it could be argued that all these people are mad.   But
 for most of them to have come up with   similar  information  would  be
 even  madder,   would  it  not?  To  quote   John Keel: "Why don't they
.contact us?   It might be better to   ask  why  didn't  they  leave  us
 alone?"
