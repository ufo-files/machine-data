theme informs the literary efforts of Truman Betherum (Aboard A Flying
Saucer 1954), Orfeo Angelucci (The Secret Of The Saucers 1955), Howard
Menger (From Outer Space To You 1959) et al. despite their sheer
implausibility, such accounts nevertheless speak of contemporary human
needs, a condition and a state of mind.
