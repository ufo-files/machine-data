MISSION STATEMENT:

TUFOIC is a serious minded civilian organisation that attempts to investigate all UFO reports, in a scientific,
objective and unbiased manner, and make reliable information available on the UFO subject.

As a organisation, the Centre draws no conclusions from its investigations, other than the fact that an
unexplained phenomenon exists as a fact, and requires extensive research and investigation.

TUFOIC is based in Hobart, with representatives I investigators in various regions around the state.

Contact is maintained with fellow organisations and researchers around Australia, and Overseas. Publications
are exchanged in an attempt to gain a world-wide picture of tbe UFO phenomena.

UFO reports that are considered authentic, after thorough investigation, are included in the computerised Database
file, TASCAT, and are made available to fellow researches and the public via our publications.

The Principal Aims of TUFOIC are:
•     To conduct investigations into Tasmanian UFO reports.
•     To collect and examine information on the UFO phenomena in general.
•     To distribute that which we consider authentic to our members and to fellow UFO organisations in Australia,
      and overseas via the Tasmanian UFO report, Newsletters, the Internet and other special publications, and to the
      general public via publications, the Internet and the media.


MEMBERSHIP OF THE ORGANISATION:

interest in the UFO phenomena.
Further information and a sample Newsletter may be obtained upon request by writing to or Emailing the
Membership secretary, or by attending meetings.
