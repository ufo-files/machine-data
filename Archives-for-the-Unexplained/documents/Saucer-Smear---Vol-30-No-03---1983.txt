       ALL RIGHT, TIMOTHY GOOD, HERE'S YOUR BOOK REVIEW!
       We've mentioned a couple of times lately about a new UFO book from
England, co-authored by Timothy Good, called "George Adamski: The Untold
Story". The other co-author is one Lou Zinsstag (elderly female) , who is
described on the flyleaf as having "become George Adamski's Swiss repre­
sentative. But after promoting his interests loyally for seven years, dis­
agreement and uncertainty forced her to stop working with him in 1964."
We do wonder what wondrous untold tale lies untold here!
       We really wanted to avoid reviewing this book, because Tim Good has
phoned us two or three times all the way from England during the     last
s tages of its preparation, and was always courteous & kind by phone    as
well as by letter. We sensed somehow that the book would turn out to be
violently pro-Adamski, and we didn't want to spoil an incipien t friendship
by fussing about it in print.
       BUT - now that we've received our free review copy, we ARE going to
bite the hand that fed us (to butcher a metaphor! ) and tell you that, in
spite of obvious painstaking research, this book is way off base - to put
it very mildly! Look, friends: We published an expose of George Adamski
in 1921 that would convince any reasonably rational & unbiased person that
ole George was gilding the lilly quite a bit, if not totally. Your hum­
ble editor traveled hundreds of miles and interviewed dozens of     people
first-hand. Our "Special Adamski Expose Issue" of SAUCER NEWS (long out
of prin t) ran about 25 pages of concise factual information. Yet in their
book Good & Zinsstag refer to our monumental work only twice, very brief­
ly, and in both cases re ject our conclusion on the specific poin t in ques­
tion.
       Good's "Will to Believe" is overwhelming! A case in point, though
minor, illustrates our thesis perfectly: On page 156, the authors are
discussing evidence tending to prove that Adamski's alleged trips into
outer space were genuine. Adamski is said (in "Inside the Spaceships") to
have traveled into Space with two unnamed scientists. In a footnote on
