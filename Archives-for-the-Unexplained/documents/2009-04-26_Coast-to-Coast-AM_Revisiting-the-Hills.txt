Long considered one of Ufology's premier abduction cases, the story of Betty
and Barney Hill has found new life back where it all began. An exhibit at the
University of New Hampshire is showcasing a plethora of key items associated
with the case, including Betty's dress, believed to have been touched by the
ETs during the abduction. More at the Boston Globe, including a video
featuring compelling excerpts from the Hills' hypnosis tapes.
