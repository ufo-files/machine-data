On Tuesday the 28th day of July 1992 at approximately 9:40 p.m. I was out to
Baddeck dump with my wife. As we were leaving and coming up to the area where
they put the scrap metal, my wife and I both noticed at the same time a light
amber light off in the distance to my left. It appeared suddenly just as if
someone had turned a switch on. On the top of it there was a zig zag glow from
it and from the tree where I was looking out over it was not that high above the
trees. It did not seem to be moving but there seemed to be a flicker from it.
I once saw an insulator burn off a power pole and it was similar to that but
much bigger. It was roundish shape with a tail on top of it and fairly big.
We saw it for 30 seconds as we drove out the road to get a better view of it
but it disappeared. We turned around to go back and look from the same area but
it was gone. It was approximately a mile and a half away at that time. Weather
foggy- warm - dark.
