                        FIRST WORLD UFO CONGRESS

  UFO PHOTO ARCHIVES of Tucson, Arizona, has been asked to stage the largest
and most informative international UFO congress this world has ever seen. It is
planned to exceed all other great UFO con gresses, including the ones held at
Acapulco, Mexico in 1972 and the one in Frankfort, Germany in 1989. It will offer the
most, the greatest and best UFO authorities in the world ever presented in one place,

presented before; including 30 world class figures in the UFO field, special exhibits, a
gallery of UFO art and photographs, UFO movies, audios and videos, UFO journals,
magazines and books in many languages, and over 50 merchandising tables. Six
platform presentations in nine hours of stage time will be presented each day for five
consecutive days, from 3 through 7 May 1991. Each stage presentation will be
translated into at least one other language, and sometimes two, mostly English and
Spanish.
  Closed circuit television of all events on stage will be cabled to each full delegate
room in the conference hotel. For those delegates we will provide a double
occupancy room with private bathtub and shower, air conditioning, and a spectacular
view through picture windows of Tucson and the mountains that ring the city. A
hearty full American style breakfast, or breakfast buffet, will be provided every room
occupant for the full five days. UFO movies and videos will be cabled to each room in
the delegate hotels after the stage presentations for the day are over.
   A published text of the lecture presentations on stage at this congress will be
available at the auditorium before each program begins.
  The full delegate charge for this remarkable and unusual vacation event, staged at
the best seasonable climate for Tucson, will be $590.00 per person, room included,
not much more than the going rate of $105.00 per day per room ($52.50 per person)
for the rooms provided in the most luxurious tourist hotels in Tucson. A great variety
of good dining facilities surround each of the delegate hotels, all near each other in
Tucson. A reduced charge of only $200.00 will admit a spouse or relative to all of the
events as a full delegate, and there is no additional charge for the second person
sharing the delegates room.                           ,
  Alternative, more economical plans are available for those who have no desire to
stay in the same hotels as the guest speakers and full dele�ates to the congress. The
charge for only attending the con gress presentations will be a nominal $10.00 per
lecture, $50.00 for all day, and $250.00 for all sessions of the congress. There are
other excellent hotels in the area which have rooms with bath for as little as $25.00 to
$35.00 per person per day. City bus transportation from these hotels to the other
congress hotels runs on regular schedule.
  Tucson, Arizona was selected for this congress in recognition of the fact that for 37
years it was the home of one of the earliest and longest lived international UFO
research organizations in continuous existence, without break, and sometimes the
largest in the world. Officers of the Aerial Phenomena Research Organization (APRO)
still live in Tucson.
