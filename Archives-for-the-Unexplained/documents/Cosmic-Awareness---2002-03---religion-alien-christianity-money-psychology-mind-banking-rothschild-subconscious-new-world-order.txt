THE SEASON OF NURTURING
Dear CAC family,
We have Easter and spring coming soon. It is the time of rebirth,
reemergence of fragile new life springing up and new babies, needing soft
caresses and tender care. Those of you who watched the Winter Olympic
Games saw the theme of the Children of Light carried in the opening and
closing ceremonies. It seems to be the time of the child. The Internet is
humming with messages of light and encouragement from the Children of
Oz, or Indigo children, which reminds one of the biblical reminders that "a
child will lead them" and to enter heaven, one must be like a child.
But that doesn't mean we children with x-number of years' experience are
left out of this promise. When you live in the moment of now, no one is
older than anyone else. All we need do is give our stern parent self some
time off, and enjoy our central, joyous self. If doing this full time doesn't
work for you, then give yourself a recess time when it's OK to be amazed
and amused, enthusiastic and spontaneous, delighted, silly and giggly, to
believe in invisible friends, angels, miracles-yourself. It's not just OK; is a
good thing!
When I was about 4 or 5, I was alone in a garden on a sunny day, exploring
the flowers and creatures, soil and sun. There was a bumblebee on a flower
and I held my hand up to him and he walked onto my palm and sat calmly
while I stroked his back. Like the story that the bumblebee flies because he
doesn't know that according to science he can't, we didn't know that the
moment we were sharing wasn't supposed to be possible.
If you let yourself forget about the restrictions you've accepted as the way
things have to be, all things are possible. The base for everything is love;
the more you live and express this, the better for everyone. And the starting
point is home. To make the world a better place, you start where you are
and pass it on outward. So it's not a selfish notion, it's for the good of all to
include yourself when you extend love and light.
Be kind to yourself. It's time.
With love,
Your Faithful Correspondence Secretary,
