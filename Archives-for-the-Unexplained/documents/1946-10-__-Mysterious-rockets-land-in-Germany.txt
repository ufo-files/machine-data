October: Rocket lands in Germany?

Jan Aldrich, of Project 1947, mailed us the following
report from "Boston Globe" on February 23, 1949.

”4 SECRET ROCKETS LAND IN 
U.S. ZONE OF GERMANY

”FRANKFURT, Germany, Feb 22 (UP) -- Four mysterious missiles - apparently rocket or jet-propelled - have landed in the United States zone of Germany since October 1946, American authorities disclosed today. 

”Technical experts said they still are studying fragments from the last, which shot over the northeastern corner of the state of Bavaria last Jan.
31, but they admit they are mystified. All four have landed in the same area, just east of Schweinfurt, about 35 miles from the Russian zone, these
sources said. 

”A German policeman, who witnessed the January missile´s flaming streak through the early morning sky and saw it plumet to ground, said it travelled from northeast to southwest, starting from the direction of
Russian-occupied territory. 

”The first rocket smashed into the ground in October 1946, according to German police records quoted by the Americans. Two others followed in May and July, 1947, and the fourth between 4 a.m. and 5 a.m., Jan. 31. 

”The last rocket tore a hole in the ground seven feet wide and ten inches deep, American officers said. It reportedly had a flaming tail 20 feet long. Officials were mystified by the missiles because they pointed out that if the rockets were fired from the Russian zone, they possess almost uncanny accuracy since all four landed in so small an area.”

Apparently there were no German newspapers published in 1946 due to political and economic reasons after the war. It has not been possible to check the background of the above story. Was it falling meteorites...?
