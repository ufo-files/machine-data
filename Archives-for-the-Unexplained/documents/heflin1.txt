                              UFOs spotted in many states. Orange County, California Highway

                              summer day as Heflin was out surveying the territory in Tustin near
                              Dyer Road, he spotted an object in the air.

                              UFO researcher and author Ann Druffel. Recent university analysis
                              to determine authenticity of the photos and the possibility of
                              manipulation will likely be consistent with previous findings. The
                              photos have never been proven to be anything but authentic.

                              Several individuals who have studied the photos and can provide in
                              depth analysis include Jeff Sainio, Robert M. Wood and Bruce
                              Maccabbee. Robert M. Wood is co-researching the photos with Ann
                              Druffel and the team could release a peer reviewed article some
                              time.
