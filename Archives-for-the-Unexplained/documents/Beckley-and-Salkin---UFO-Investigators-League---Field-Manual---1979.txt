                           INTRODUCTION

        All around the world today, people are seeing strange things
in the sky -- moving lights, glowing objects, discs that sometimes
touch down to earth and leave marks in the soil----- all a part of
the great modern-age mystery: what~ these Unidentified Flying
Objects -- or UFOs -- or Flying Saucers?
        As a person of intelligence and scientific curiosity, you are
now about to join the ranks of the UFO Investigators. Ahead of you
lies challenge, hard work, and adventure. You will meet interesting
people, go to intriguing places, and perhaps even come up with some
new evidence that will help solve the ever-expanding mystery of the
skies.
        Study this Manual very carefully -- apply it wherever poss-
ible, and let us hear the results. Good luck -- and "good hunting"!

               Hai TO BE ALERTED Fat. UFO REP<lt.TS

        If you live in a large city and read a daily newspaper, the
chances are you haven't seen anything about UFOs lately. But that
is only because the metropolitan dailies do not print UFO stories,
in general. Smaller papers in the actual area where an event takes
place, will print it. However, your best media source of UFO news
will probably be your radio and TV -- listen to the news there,
always with a sharp ear.
        To become a "live wire" in this field, alert your friends
and neighbors that you are now an official UFO Investigator. This
establishes your own private 'network" of information sources.
When anyone in your area hears of a UFO report, he •ill call you
first, as the local expert.
        Next, contact your local newspapers and radio/TV stations.
List your name and phone number with them as a UFO referral number.
Do the same with your local police headquarters, and with any mili-
tary bases that may be in your area. In most cases, they will be
glad to have someone to refer UFO calls over to.

                 INTERVIEWING THE UFO WITNESS

        When entering a case, it is important that you identify

yourself properly.    Use an approach such as this:
        ''Hello, my name is ______        T---~-~·    I'm a member
of the UFO Investigators League and would like to talk with you
about your experience."
        Your tone of voice should express sincerity, no matter how
strange the person's experience might seem to be. You might also
point out that you have been interested in UFOs X number of years,
and have a good background in this field. Your aim is to make the
witness confident that you are a credible investigator who will not
scoff at the case.
        If similarities exist between this case and others that you
are familiar with, say, ''You know, Mr. Brown, other people have ex-
perienced similar phenomena," and go on to relate the highlights in
order to put him at ease.
        Once you have opened the channels of communication, get the
witness to "start at the beginning" by describing all the circum-
stances surrounding the event. This data must be recorded as accur-
ately as possible. It is best to take notes, as well as to tape
record the inteview. Thus you will have a two-way ''back-up system".

                     SOME OPENING QUESTIONS TO ASK

        Start out with this line of questioning:
        _ _ _What were you doing just before the sighting
             (or contact) took place?

        ---What was the weather like?
        _ _ _Who else was present, or were you alone?
        _ _ _Did you feel any different from your normal
             self -- either uneasy or overly tranquil?
        _ _ _When you first sighted the UFO, how large was
             it compared to a dime held at arm's length?
        _ _ _Was it as large as the full iooon?
        ____If a light was seen, was it as bright as the
            brightest star?
         Allow the UFO witness to tell his story in its entirety and
then go back and have him elaborate on each phase. Be a sharp 11cross
examiner", but always in a friendly, polite manner, never overbearing
or rude.

        Ask about any previous involvement the witness may have had
with UFOs (sighting, contact, etc.) or that anyone related to them
may have had. Also, find out if they have any ESP ability -- ask
about telepathic experiences, or perhaps they had seen ghosts in
their childhood,etc. Psychic individuals tend to make extremely
good UFO witnesses. Check into any strange dreams they might have
had recently, or any "out-of-the-body" trips.
        Some contactees will even admit, "off the record", that a
voice inside their head told them to go to a particular area, where
they experienced a UFO encounter.
        To be properly equipped, you chould always carry a camera
on an investigation. Take photographs of the witness or witnesses,
and if possible go to the actual locale and get pictures of the
area, with the viewer pointing in the direction he saw the craft.

                         OTHER WITNESSES

        Try to uncover additional witnesses, by asking throughout the
neighborhood to see if others may have experienced anything related
to the incident. Even if no one actually saw the UFO, there may be
other evidence, such as radio or TV interference, the barking and
howling of dogs, power failures, strange sounds, etc.
         If you locate other witnesses, have them give their testimony
apart from the first witness, so as to get an independent version of
what hapfiened. Thus there is no chance of one person's story being
"colored ' by another's.
        Aside from the direct questioning of UFO witnesses, we must
get background material on them. We want to know whether they are
respected members of their community, whether they have a reputation
for honesty, sincerity, a balanced outlook -- in short, are they
reliable witnesses?
        This can be done in a discreet way, by holding friendly con-
versations with people in the area who know the witness. While dis-
cussing the UFO event, you can indirectly get character appraisals
by asking the friend, ''What do you think? Would Mr. Brown be the
kind of person you could trust to tell it exactly as it happened?"
        If any photos have been taken of the UFO, obtain prints of
each one. These can later be analyzed and tested for authenticity
whether double exposures, or a small model of a UFO has been used to
make a hoax photo.

                      OTHER STRANGE REACTIONS

        Be on the alert for any unusual physical or mental reactions
the UFO witness may have had during the episode. Were there any burns
on the body, or skin rashes; any impaired vision (even temporarily);
any rapid pulse beats; headaches, dizziness, hallucina t ing, loss of
consciousness?
          In a number of UFO cases, there was an apparent "loss of time"
on the part of the observers. There have been gaps of up to several
hours , t otally unaccounted for. Later, under hypnos is, strange stories

come out, detailing events caused by the UFOs.   In your questioning,
be sure to bring out this aspect.
        One other sensual item -- were there any strange odors during
the time of the event? (Many have been reported in past cases.)

                      IF THE UFO HAS LANDED

        In the event that a landing has taken place, procedure would
call for a Geiger counter to be brought to the scene to see if any
radiation can be detected. The sooner you can reach the site, the
better your chances of picking up a reading.
        If there is evidence of a landing, such as pressed-down reeds,
sharp imprints in the soil, etc., secure the area. Do not allow any-
one to disturb the ground around the landing site.
        Soil samples should be taken, as follows: Scoop up the top-
soil, place it in a jar and fasten the lid tightly, so that no air
can penetrate the container. Samples should include vegetation, and
even insects, since previous studies have shown that strange reactions
(such as phenomenal growth in insects and vegetables) frequently take
place right after a UFO landing has disturbed the tranquility of an
area.
        A tape measure should also be employed if any markings have
been made in the ground, seemingly by the landing gear of a UFO.
The distances between these indentations, as well as the depth and
dimentions of the imprints, should be measured inmediately, before
the soil has a chance to settle or be disturbed. Care should be
taken to look around for any device that might have been capable of
making the impressions, in the case of a hoax.
        If rocks have been struck by the UFO in touching down, get
scrapings of the affected portions, so that they can be tested in
the laboratory for metallic particles.
        Oceasionally, trees will be involved. If so, get samples of
burnt or broken branches, tree bark, leaves or other damaged foliage.
Preserve them carefully, as with soil samples.
        If anything has apfieared to have dropped from the UFO, such
as liquids, burnt slag or 'angel hair" (fine-spun filaments like
spider webbing), get whatever residue is available.
        For laboratory testing of any such physical evidence listed
above, locate a reliable laboratory in your area and bring the mater-
ial to these experts. Be sure to get everything back at the conclu-
sion of the testing.

                      RELATED OCCURRENCES

        As a follow-up, you will definitely want to find out if any
UFO-related occurrences have taken place, such as animal mutilations,
often reported in areas of UFO activity. Over the past several years,
the number of such cases throughout the U.S. has risen dramatically,
to a total of over 8000 reports.
        The fact that the situation is taken seriously by our govern-
ment was proven recently when the Federal Bureau of Investigation
officially entered the picture. At a specially called conference
in Albuquerque, N.M., the local FBI director declared that his agency
intends to probe into all cases of mutilations, using the most up-to-
date methods available, including computer analysis.
           If you live in the area stretching from Texas to Minnesota,
  and as far west as Colorado and Wyoming, you may be close to one of
  the "action zones". So be prepared. At the first warning of such
  an event, get to the scene as quickly as possible. You can expect
  a gory sight -- definitely not for the squeamish. Mutilations all
· bear the same trademark: blood is drained from the animal; organs,
  such as the tongue, eyes, heart and reproductive organs are removed
  with surgical precision. In fact, the skill shown is often beyond
  our earthly technique, because the internal organs may be removed
  without the skin being noticeably cut, proof that some alien force is
  at work.
        What you can do at the scene is to interview all the experts
present, such as the veterinarian or State Police investigators who
may be on hand. Get your photos, as in other UFO cases. Then follow
up with the persons who do the scientific analysis, and compile as
complete a dossier as possible. Also, do research on other similar
mutilation cases, and keep them in one file for future comparisons.
        As for the tie-in wi th UFOs, use the previously stated tech-
niques in tracking ·down eye-witnesses to sightings in the general
vicinity of the mutilations. Get all testimony, photos, tape record-
ings~ etc., and add them to your Mutilations file. A recent case in
the western area tells of the bodies of such animals being found on
snow-covered ground, with no sign of footprints or auto tire-marks in
the vicinity; and the bones of the animals have been broken as if
they had been dropped from a great height. This is merely one more
link in the chain of evidence loopi'ng together the mutilation mystery
with the UFO conundrum.

                                BIGFOOT

         You are doubtless familiar with tales of the hairy monsters
                                 - 6 -
known in various parts of the world as "Sasquatch" or ''Yeti" or (i"
this country) simply as "Bigfoot". The name, of course, comes from
the footprints that have been found -- measuring more th~n twice the
size of human prints, and deeply impressed in the soil, as if the
creature above the feet weighed in at some inordinate amount. Reports
tell of creatures measuring 8 to 10 feet in height, whose powerful
bodies are covered with hair similar to an ape's, but who walk totally
upright with a human-like stride and in fact seem to have human bone
structure around the head and neck.
        From the Himalaya Mountains of Asia to the west coast of Can-
ada, all the way to the Florida Everglades, .they have been seen, pho-
tographed and even had their voices tape-recorded. The evidence is
generally limited to footprints, but there are also tales of a hunter
who was ·"captured" by a group of creatures -- including some younger
ones, evidently offspring of the senior members of the tribe -- and
held in a cave for four days before being"allowed" to leave. He later
described them as being "friendly, curious, and coop. erative among them-
selves" -- not in the least like the typical Hollywood teeth-gnashing
beast.
        If you are lucky enough to be in the area when a Bigfoot is
sighted, take all your normal investigative gear, plus a supply of
plaster of Paris to get exact plaster casts of the indentations left
by the Big Feet in the soil. Also take photos of the prints, the sur-
rounding ground, and any broken bushes or tree branch~that may have
been smashed when the IOOnster came crashing through the underbrush.
        There is a great deal of evidence now building to the effect
that UFOs are seen about the same time and in the same locations as
the Bigfoot creatures are spotted. Add to this the fact that no Big-
foot has ever been captured, and you may reach the conclusion that
these humanoid characters are somehow tied in with the spacecraft;
could it be that they are "scouts" who are placed in our environment
by superior beings on board the ships, just as we first sent up dogs
and monkeys in our early space rockets to see how they would survive
in strange environments? According to this line of reasoning, when
the furry emissaries. get into any trouble on earth, they could be
rescued from above -- plucked out of danger from above, and escorted
in safety back to the Mother Ship in the skies.
        But returning to you, as the fearless investigator on the
trail, you are actually looking for two things: Bigfoot evidence and
UFO evidence. For the latter, you may have to scout over a wide area
for eye-witnesses, as the territory will generally be sparsely settled
(such as the Pacific Northwest, where much of the activity has been
taking place). Go to the Forest Ranger stations; park in the tourist
camps, have a drink at a local tavern -- anywhere people congregate to
chae about the latest happenings.
        And, if you can return to civilization with a big, glossy
phobo of a strange Saucerian •~eastie" alighting from curved vehicles
suspended six feet off the ground, you will really have it made!

                     THE MYSTERIOUS ''MIBS"


        And don't forget the mysterious ''Men In Black" -- those human-
like characters that have been known to appear after an encounter with
a UFO, dressed in black and out to cause trouble. These ''MIBs", as
they are known in the field, will often try to prevent the witnesses
from telling their experiences. Or they may come to the door, intro-
duce themselves as being secret government operatives, and demand to
have all the film or other evidence from a recent sighting. Once
they came dressed as Air Force officers; upon checking, it was found
that their credentials were totally faked.
         If possible, get a photo of these men -- or get their license
plate number, for checking out. Then go to your local police head-
quarters and see if they are known criminals, by going through the
''Wanted" files. Above all, be extremely careful when dealing with
MIBs, as they may be armed and thus potentially very dangerous.
        These MIB cases are not unconuoon, and can be very significant
in the final analysis.
