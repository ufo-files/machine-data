Case xxx: 
Spool-shaped projectile

Time: July 10-12, 22.30 hours
Place: Stackmora, near Orsa (BS43)

"Since I believe that my accidental sighting is of general interest I would like to report that I saw a spool-shaped projectile travelling through the air, horisontally, and without any noise at the speed of a common aircraft - altitude about 100 meters going on a straight south-to-north course. I was sitting by an open window at 10.30 in the evening at Stackmora north of Orsa, in July, maybe it was the 10-12th of July."

Status: Unidentified, but lacking of information.
Source: Letter to Defence Staff from Mrs. Margarete Lundgren, July 28, 1946. SCANCAT 1232.
