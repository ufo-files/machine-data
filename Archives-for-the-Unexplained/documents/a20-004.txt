          Archeological Reconnaissance of the Groom Range
  From: campbell@ufomind.com (Glenn Campbell, Las Vegas)
  Date: Mon, 20 Jan 1997 13:46:08 -0800
  Subject: Archeological Reconnaissance of the Groom Range
  A visitor recently showed me a government document that I wasn't
  aware of but that may be of interest to hard-core Groom Lake
  historians.
  It is a report prepared as part of the Environmental Impact
  Statement in connection with the 1980s withdrawal of the Groom
  Mountain Range. It is about 100-200 pages and reports on 265
  archeological sites in the withdrawal area.
  I haven't examined the document in detail and have yet tracked
  down a copy locally. This document may be available from NTIS
  (http://www.ntis.gov/text/ntishome.html) or by FOIA to Langley
  AFB.
  Here is all of the information that appears on the title page:
  And Archeological Reconnaissance of the Groom Range
  Lincoln County, Nevada
  By Ronald L. Reno And Lonnie C. Pippin
  With a contribution by Stephen R. Durand
  Principal Investigator: Lonnie Pippin
  Submitted to
  U.S. Air Force
  Headquarters, Tactical Air Command/DEEV
  Langley Air Force Base, Virginia
  August 1986
  Desert Research Institute
  Social Sciences Center
  Technical Report No. 46
