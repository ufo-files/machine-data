Styckjunkare O.M Pedersén
reporting light phenomena.

To the duty officer at I 2.

I hereby respectfully report that I, whilst residing in my home in Sörmon on the eleventh of August, 1946, at 20:47 o’clock,  came to observe an unidentified flying object, which traveled hastily in northwesterly  direction, at treetop height.
The object shone yellow and had a fiery tail - the length of which I am unable to state, since I only got to keep it under observance during a mere 3-4 seconds, due to the adjacent forest.

Karlstad, August 11, 1946.

O.M. Pedersén
Styckjunkare
Lv 1 mobc Karlstad.
