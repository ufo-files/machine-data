Direct questions
Having briefly considered visionary qualities, it is also
important to consider the role of direct questioning in
psychic readings. Fry claims possession of clairsentient
abilities: a form of ESP which permits psychic insight
primarily through personal ' feeling' . Given that
clairsentience acts through emotional means rather
than the relaying of directly verbalised messages, the
