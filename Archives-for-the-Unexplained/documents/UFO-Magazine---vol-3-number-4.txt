                    PICNICKING YOUTHS TELL WEIRD SAUCER TALE !!

April 7,1966- Six Denver,Colorado teenagers were picnicking at a campsite
in rugged terrain south of Denver when they·saw some weird and terrifying
sights.

   Donald Otis,17,said he and five others saw "four or five" objects rising
out of a gully directly in front of a cooking area shelter where they had
dinner. Patricia Retherford said she and three of the other picnickers saw
what appeared to be the figures of a "really,really tallman" for a few sec­
onds near the shelter.

   Otis said that about 7:45 p.m. (MST),"we heard what sounded like foot­
steps-it was something weird-and Allen Scrivner and I went out to check.
"Then we heard a weird buzzing sound-it was something else." Otis said they
walked about 100 yards in the darkness to a hill where they saw what looked
like "a huge car,with red and white lights that looked like backup lights
going on and off."

   The boys then returned to the shelter where they were told about the dark
figure. Kaye Hurley,another of the picnickers,said,"I didn't get a good
look-! was afraid to-but he was much taller than Allan,who is 6-feet-1."

   Otis said they then started to drive away when they turned around and
noticed a "pure white light-oval like an egg-rising straight up out of the
gully in front of us."

   "Then Mary screamed," he said," and we all turned around and saw tw o oth­
ers hovering near the first one. Then we saw another one with a weird red
light. Its rays went straight up. Then it looked like it was starting right
toward us and we took off."
