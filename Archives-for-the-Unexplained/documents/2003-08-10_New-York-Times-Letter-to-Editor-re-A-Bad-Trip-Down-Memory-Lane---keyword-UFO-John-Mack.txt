       A Bad Trip Down Memory Lane
       AUG. 10, 2003

       The failure of Susan Clancy and her Harvard colleagues to understand the politics
       of her research agenda is evidence of extraordinary naïveté, arrogance or both
       (Bruce Grierson, July 27). And surely the rank deception involved in her feigned
       neutrality or reported displays of apparent support for the accounts of those ''alien
       abductees,'' whom she sought out and seemingly befriended, raises serious ethical
       and methodological concerns. As she relocates her research agenda to Nicaragua,
       let us hope that this time she will make the effort necessary to understand the
       impact of her academic ambitions upon the lives of those she recruits for her
       experiments.

            James M. Statman

            Rhinebeck, N.Y.
