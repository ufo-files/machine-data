A "UFO photographed over Bridgwater, Somerset on Christmas Day
PEOPLE across the south west have been going UFO crazy all week as images of strange floating objects were published.
Pictures were taken of Unidentified Flying Objects in the skies over Bridgwater in Somerset.
The sightings come as the Ministry of Defence release figures for UFO's spotted across Cornwall last year.
A woman from Polruan reported spotted a UFO hovering over Fowey at 5.45pm on Christmas Day. Yoga teacher and crystal
healer Tessa Skola said she and her mother saw lights the size of a large house move across the Esplanade.
Other sightings reported to the MoD were at Helston on February 13, September 3 and 29 and another at Redruth on July 8. But
