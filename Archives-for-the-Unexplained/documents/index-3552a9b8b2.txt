  Publisher's Synopsis

  A controversial look at our growing fascination with extra-terrestrials.

  Ever since the first sightings of UFOs after the Second World War, thousands of people have experienced extra-
  terrestrial visitations. Some have even claimed to be abducted. And despite powerful evidence our government
  continues to deny it.

  What are these UFOs? Who or what is in them? Where do they come from? These are the key questions discussed in the
  fascinating study of extra- terrestrial phenomena. The Alien Files is an intelligent, insightful and controversial
  presentation of the most up-to-date extra-terrestrial research. Gregory van Dyk reveals information from Top Secret
  government files, including the official government procedures for "recovery and disposal" of extra-terrestrial entities.
  He explores the field of channeling, which is the well-established practice by which messages are received from "the
  beyond." This book forces the reader to stop thinking of reality as simply the physical and material. In order to
  understand extra-terrestrial phenomena, we need to examine the evidence from a spiritual perspective rather than via the
  constraints of the physical sciences. The Alien Files is a must for anyone who dares ask the question, "What is reality
  and what do I believe?"

  Gregory van Dyk has traveled widely in the United States investigating evidence, interviewing witnesses and meeting
  key people in the field. He lives in Colorado.
