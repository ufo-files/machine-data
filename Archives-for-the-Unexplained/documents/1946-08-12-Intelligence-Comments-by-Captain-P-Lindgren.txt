Comments on ghost projectiles.

1) Detective Superintendent Westlin, Gothenburg, stressed the inappropriateness of forwarding information regarding these ghost projectiles to the press.

2) A police officer, who wishes to remain anonymous, has offered the following theory on the ghost projectiles:
They are being launched from a foreign country and as they reach Swedish soil, a subversive group is deployed with the task of using some sort of remote control devices in order to take charge of the projectiles, directing them against predetermined targets. Granted, this theory sounds extraordinary, but it could serve as an explanation as to why the projectiles change course when crossing the border.
Stockholm, August 12, 1946.

P. Lindgren
Captain
