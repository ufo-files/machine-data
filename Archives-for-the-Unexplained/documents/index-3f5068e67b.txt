    Our Review                                                                                                  Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  One of several books that attempt to weave a conspiracy around the now-abandonned Montauk Air Force
  Station at Montauk Point, Long Island (New York). This one is more interesting than most in that it does
  not rely wholly on channelling and other psychic information. The author and his friends have actually
  visited the site and recorded their observations. This book contains plenty of black-and-white photos of
  abandonned military buildings, gun emplacements and "underground facilities." Long Island residents and
  visitors may get a kick out of this book and the conspiracies attributed to this base. -- Glenn Campbell
