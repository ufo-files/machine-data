to realize that there is a very pronounced theological aspect to it.
There are many biblical references to !'angelic" beings and airborne
vehicles called "fiery chariots".
   It is a relatively well-known hypothesis that the biblical angels
may have been extraterrestrial beings visiting the Earth and acting
as "agents of God". It appears that there was also another group of
adversary angels which became associated with the symbol of the
dragon. The Bible speaks of a war which took place between the angels
of light and the angels of darkness. Following this theoretical train
of thought, I managed to uncover some evidence which may be of
significant value in verifying these claims.

   In the park-cum-museum of Villa Hermosa, Tabasco, Mexico, stands a
neatly carved monolith in which a large dragon is depicted. Inside the
animal sits a man with back bent and raised extended legs. His feet are
working what appear to be pedals, his left hand rests on a gear-type
lever, and his right hand is holding a small box. He wears a close
fitting helmet covering his brow, ears and chin, leaving his face
exposed. In front of his lips is an object which greatly resembles a
microphone. The helmet and clothing fit tightly together. This monolith
provides a valuable piece of evidence to suggest that the dragon spoken
of in the Zeus/Typhon account may well have been a spacecraft which was
piloted by a man.
   In southern Yugoslavia, at a place called Dessani, there exists a
temple containing some frescoes which picture two oval-shaped spacecraft,
one in pursuit of the other with pilots aboard each craft, their hands
working levers. The people on the ground are shown covering their faces
and running in a panic.
   Why would the ancients paint such a scene on the walls of a lloly
temple unless there was a subtle theological connection. It is interesting
to note that these accounts all fall into the same area. Some stories
even indicate that the dragon fled beneath Nount Etna in Sicily and
near Egypt, Greece, Bulgaria, Yugoslavia and the Breek island of Patmos.
   What relationship does all this have to our times? Let's take a look
at the   Herbert Schirmer case.

                     Herbert     Schirmer-Dragon meeting

   On    December   J,   1967,   at about 2.30   a.m.,   Herbert   Schirmer was on
patrol as part of his duties as a police officer in Ashland, Nebraska.
He saw ahead of him an object with a row of flickering lisFTts. He thought
it was a truck until it took off into the sky. 1'111en he returned to the
police station, he entered in his logbook, "Saw a flying saucer at the
junction of highways 6 and 63, believe it or not." Later, the Condon
Committee discovered about 20 minutes missing in his police report and
had him put through time regression hypnosis. It was then that they
discovered that he had been taken aboard the UFO and met some strange
men wearing close-fitting, silvery-gray uniforms, boots and gloves. On
the right-hand part of the chest, the beings wore an insignia of a
serpent with wings--a dragon. They informed Hr. Schirmer that th�y had
bases on the Earth which are located underground.
   In retrospect, the Zeus/Typhon battle also states that the dragons
fled_ underground. Could the beings that Herbert Schirmer encountered
perhaps be descendants of the dragons that battled against Zeus and
Michael in Biblical times? If so, this particular race could be
extremely dangerous. Keep in mind that it was a dragon or serpent which
caused the do1tmf all of man back in the Gar.den of Eden.
