                        SUMMARY OF INFORMATION


        The target is located in a flat, open area, with a few

structures.     The main activity in this area seems to be movement.

There are people talking about moving certain objects to a new

location.     Either the objects or the location is "hidden".

        There are vehicles in this area.     There is one vehicle that

looks like a truck of some kind, moving equipment.        It seems to

be an elaborate production, planned in some way, moving these

objects or equipment "down".     There is something dark in the

middle of something wide and bright.        There are men who are

"down-loading" the objects or equipment into the dark area.         It

almost seems to be underground.

        There are many objects of various shapes all around the

area.     Most of the shapes are rounded in some way.     Others are

long, straight shapes, which are stacked.        There is a very large,

bright, almost glowing red shape.     It is circular, but it also

seems to open up in the middle.     This red shape has waves of

energy or light associated with it.        There is something cracked,

and abrasive that looks like two diagonal lines coming together

at a point.     There is a green odd-shaped object.
        There is a huge, machine-like object that is pointed on one

end.     The sides of this object are curved.     There are many

different little shapes (rectangles, squares, numerals) on this

object which make it "go", but it is impossible to draw it.
