Hurricane David came through the \.Jashington,      D.C. area dumping more than a foot of wind
driven rain.     Being below ground level,     The MARCEN offices were particularly vulnerable
when the massive weight of the \�ater outside pushed through the windows and filled the
offices with up to two feet of water.        All drains quickly ceased to function from the
overburden and a lack of electricity overnight prevented pumping the water away.
        By morning the water was gone a�d several weeks of drying was begun. Alternate heating
and cooling cycles were initiated       24 hours a day for 10 days as hot air sucked up moisture
from everyt�ing and then air conditioning dried out the air and the cycle was started over
again.
        Next came weeks of air drying books,     magazines and files.    A large number of the
personal files the director had built for nearly        20 years were so badly damaged that they
could not be salvaged, most other files were dried out and saved,          with the exception of
hand written letters and reports,       which nearly dissolved.
        Computer equipment and photographic equipment sufferred particularly heavily as did
a large quantity of laboratory apparatus which was broken.          All paperback books were dried
successfully and are nearly as good as new-if a bit fatter,         but many hardbound books came
completely apart as the binding disintegrated.
        The printing press used for the Journal was at a separate site and also heavily
damaged but now back in operation.
        After all of the damage was totalled up it appears that MARCEN's loss was about      $15,000.
Of most serious consequence was the total loss of more than         90 rolls of microfilm containing
all of the U.S. Air Force Project Bluebook files.         Also discouraging was the total destruct­
ion of the bokes of Xeroxes of all of the CIA and FBI           UFO Freedom of Information docum­
ents.     Plans are currently underway to replace these documents but the replacement cost of
the Bluebook microfilm alone will run almost        $1,500.
        No one who has not been through a serious flood can probably fully understand the
destructive force of water which as it rises floats desks and then tips them over dump­
everything on top and inside into the water.
        Despite the hardships and problems of this flood,       MARCEN has made every effort to
rise Phoenix-like out of the mud if not the ashes and now everything except one computer
punched card reader is back in full operation. Some photographic laboratory equipment was
not salvageable but arrangements have been made to utilize other equipment to make up for
the lost functions. Both computer terminals are back in operation and a third larger term·
inal is on order and expected about January.
        Since a number of out of town members have expressed interest about MARCEN headquar­
ters,    we have included the layout on the next page to illustrate somewhat the activity in
our   1,200 square foot office space.      The drawing is not to scale but is approximate.
        As you can probably tell,    all space is subject to maximum function utilization and
this made it particularly vulnerable to the water damage. Steps have been taken to prevent
a similar recurrance before MARCEN moves. New reinforced windows have been installed and
have already withstood two other tropical storms without .a single leak.          In case alf else
should fail,    all files and books are now elevated at least two feet from the floor on
steel frames.

earch and experimentation in-house.        We have a small but fairly well equipped laboratory

                                   UFO DISINFORMATION
     There is probaby more UFO folklore and mythology in existence today than anything
similar since the demise of the ancient Greeks and Romans.        Most serious Ufologists
acknowledge the presence of the folklore but most are at a total loss to explain its
origin.
     A prime case in example currently is the repetitive stories of crashed UFOs and their
supposed retreivel and study by the Air Force.         MARCEN finds it extremely difficult to
accept or believe any of these tales yet the same stories keep popping up often virtually
unchanged over a twenty -year or more period.        Officers of MARCEN have now personally
talked to several hundred persons claiming to have witnessed UFO retreivals,        or at least
some phase of the retreival operation.
     The credibility of the "witnesses" interviewed is high enough in most cases and the
details of the stories consistent enough that if we were in court dealing with a capital
murder case,    someone would likely hang.
     Still,    MARCEN cannot unconditionally accept any of these stories without ourselves
seeing more tangible proof and we do not think this is because of ouf innate skepticism.
     The unresolved questions that bother us most are:       how did all of the stories get so
consistnet;    how are all of the details so fresh in the minds of persons who supposedly
experienced the events 30 to 35 years ago;       and why are so many former military persons inv�
olved?
     One can easily jump to the quick conclusion that these witnesses have been program­
med to give out the manufactured stories in measured doses periodically.        But who did the
programming and why?                                                                           _


     There are many ufologists who feel that some clandestine bureau of the government
somewhere periodically manufactures fictionialized UFO information so absurd that when
it is disseminated, the public becomes quickly convinced that there is nothing factual about
the UFO phenomenon at all,    or at least this is Agency X's goal.
     This may sound good in theory but after pouring over thousands of pages of FBI and
CIA UFO related documents released through Freedom of Information actions,       MARCEN is un­
able to find any evidence of any government generated confusion or coverup as has been
charged.     There is much evidence of considerable government interest in UFOs that it did
not previously admit,    but we can find nothing to indicate any action taken to suppress
data from the public on any scale or that the government agencies involved ever did
anything with the data they collected other than read it with interest and then file it.
Of course the proponents of government conspiracies argue that none of the "really good"
material has been released or ever will be released.
     In February 1958,    a hoax was perpetrated on ufology when a letter typed on the
official letterhead of the U.S. Secretary of State was sent to the late George Adamski
claiming government knowledge of UFOs and signed by a supposed State Department official
named R.E. Straith.     More letters followed to other ufologists and it ended with the
FBI investigating a number of prominent ufologists of the time.        No results of the
investigations were ever made public and no one was ever known to have been prosecuted.
