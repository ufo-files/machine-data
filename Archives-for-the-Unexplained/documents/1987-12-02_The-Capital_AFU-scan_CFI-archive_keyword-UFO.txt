    BALTIMORE - A bright streak of light that briefly illuminat­
ed midnight skies along the East Coat from New York to Virginia
was probably a large meteor, according to astronomers.
    Weather conditions likely enhanced the meteor's light as it
whizzed at 50-60 miles per minute down the coast and then
exploded somewhere over Maryland or Virginia, scientists said.
     "We're pretty sure it was a large, extremely bright meteor,
about 12:35 or 12:40 a.m. Tuesday morning," s·aid James O'Leary,
director of the Maryland Science Center's Davis Planetarium.
"Most meteors are no bigger than grains of sand or pebbles, but
this was maybe baseball size."
    Numerous sightings were made in the mid-Atlantic area, with
some reported as far north as New York. Sightings were variously
described as a white fireball, a blue light and a yellow light.
    The high clouds prevalent in the area may have had "a prism
effect," on the meteor's light show, said astronomer Kenneth
Seidelmann said.
