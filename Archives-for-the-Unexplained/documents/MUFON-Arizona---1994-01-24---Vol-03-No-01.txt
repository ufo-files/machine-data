Dear Citizen:

We need your help!

As you may know, the Air Force is trying to seize the viewpoints into the
unacknowledged Groom Lake weapons testing facility.   At present, you can
hike about an hour from a county road to hilltops on public land to see
for yourself the base that doesn't exist.     The AF is not happy about
these visits, and has applied to occupy this land to end them.        The
withdrawal process is marching ahead, and the time for public hearings is
approaching.

The BLM officer processing the application has chosen to hold a hearing
in Caliente, Nevada, on Mon., Jan. 31 at 7pm in the VFW Hall.   This is
bad for opponents, since Caliente is hours from anywhere and very hard
from most people--public and press--to get to.   Therefore, we want the
BLM to hold an additional public hearing in Las Vegas, closer to more
