KUNGLIGA LIVREGEMENTETS GRENADJÄRA

Regiment Office
Department. L
No. 644

To the air defense department.

CLASSIFIED

I am hereby submitting a compilation of reports regarding observations of luminous phenomena, and the like. These phenomena had been observed on July 9, during 14.00-15.00 o’clock (Headquarters Defence Staff Section L, no. 7:49, June 12, 1946).

The observers locations.

2 kilometers north of Vedevåg (7 km south-southeast of Lindesberg), at around 15.00 o'clock.
Mariedamm station (15 kilometers east-southeast of Ankersund), at 14.30 o'clock.
2 kilometers southwest of Lännäs (24 kilometers southeast of Örebro) between 14.00-15.00 o'clock.

Course, etc.

The light phenomenon at Vedevåg went in a southerly direction, while the other two had traveled easterly. The light phenomenon at Vedevåg had allegedly been preceded by a fast moving aircraft, also traveling in a southerly direction.

Every single one of these phenomena had been traveling at a "great altitude", as well as following a straight course. The latter two cases involved mention of objects leaning at an angle of 45 degrees.

Description.

Apparently, these phenomena appeared quite similar, for every report on the matter involves mention of a globe-shaped, shiny object, traveling with great haste, followed by a”comet tail", which emanated a bluish-white light. A “shooting star” of sorts had been observed at Lännäs shortly after the discovery of the phenomenon in question.
No sounds had been heard.

There has been no way of determining whether the reporters had in fact observed the same exact phenomenon, or if a great deal of unidentified objects had been involved.

Örebro, July 11, 1946.

H. Ling-Vannerus
Acting Executive Officer
/ T. Droms
