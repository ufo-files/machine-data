STRÄNGNÄS AREA OF DEFENSE
Expedition staff
Department I e
No. 7:42:2

July 11, 1946.

To the head of the Air Defense Division.

Subject: aerial bomb.

Mr. Thomasson, resident of Hällebybrunn, Eskilstuna, has reported his most unusual observation to our area of defense headquearters via phone, informing that he, on July 9th, at around 14.35 o’clock, came to observe as "a big star, almost resembling a rocket with angular sides had emanated a strong firelight, which broke through the clouds and fell vertically down to the ground in the vicinity of Kungsör ". The direction from whence the rocket had originally arrived remains unknown.

Gustaf Friths
Home Guard Officer
