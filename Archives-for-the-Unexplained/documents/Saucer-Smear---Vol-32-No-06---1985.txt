       ·Our involvement is with Chapter Two, which was reprinted with our
  permission from "Jim Moseley's Book of SAUCER NEWS" (1967), edited by
  Gray Barker. The material for Chapter Two, now called "The Whi-te Man's
  Legends of Mt. Shasta", originally appeared in a very early issue of SAU­
  CER NEWS (circa 1955), under the pseudonym Richard Cohen. (Cohen was a
  co-operating friend in that era, and your editor didn't want it to look
  like he himself was doing all the writing.)
        Unfortunately, as we have pointed out to Walton, neither Cohen nor
 Moseley actually wrote the SAUCER NEWS piece. It was "borrowed" from an
  old book about Mt. Shasta, which we happened to come upon at that time.
  We don't even remember the name of the book!  Thus, this tale of legends
  is almost legendary in its own origin!
        There are 25 chapters and over 130 pages in "Mount Shasta, Home of
  the Ancients".  Undoubtedly a lot of it is bullshit, but we concur with
  the quotation on the forepiece of the book: "All of these stories may
  not agree together, yet in every one of them lay hid something of the
  truth, a grain of gold in the ore of fable that might be found by him
  who had the skill and strength to.look. "
