SUBJECT:      CENTER LANE Briefing to IC HUMINT Staff (U)



1.   (S/CL-2/NOFORN) On 10 January 1985 DT-DIA will present a
briefing to the Intelligence Community (IC) Human Resources
Intelligence (HUMINT) Staff on the DoD psychoenergetic
intelligence program. Dr. Varona has requested CENTER LANE (CL)
participation in the briefing to present information on remote
viewing as an intelligence collection technique and to represent
the collection division of the new DIA psychoenergetic program
at the briefing.    The CL portion of the briefing will be
approximately 30 minutes and will include a short history of the
development of the INSCOM program and a review of six collection
projects.
2.   (S/CL-2/NOFORN)  In response to a request by BG Hyman,
DAMI-IS, OACSI, for an update on CENTER LANE activities in
preparation for the IC HUMINT Staff Meeting, MAJ Rawley,
DAMI-ISH requested and received a status report on the
attachment of CL to DIA.    On 2 January 1985, LTC Buzby, CL
Project Manager, informed MAJ Rawley of the CL participation in
the 10 January 1985 briefing. Since the briefing is in support
of the transfer of CL to DIA, authorization to participate in
the briefing is contained in the 10 September 1984 letter from
ACSI to CG, INSCOM, that permits direct coordination with DIA to
affect the transfer (Incl 1).
