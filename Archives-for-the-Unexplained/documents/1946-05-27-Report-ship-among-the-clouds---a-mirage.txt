Ship among the clouds

Time: May 27, afternoon
Place: Visby on [the island of] Gotland

”SHIP AMONG THE CLOUDS. Residents of Visby could, Monday afternoon, see a strange sight in the sky. About ten degrees over the horizon a cargo ship steamed forward up among the clouds. It was going southwards and one could see it for about an hour. The picture was a mirage, which maybe isn´t so uncommon, but the mirages usually seen from Visby are rather uniform in their motif - it is practically always the light-house at Ölands norra udde [northern tip of the island of Öland] which now and then appears. 

Status:  Mirage
Source: Sydöstra Sveriges Dagblad, May 27.
