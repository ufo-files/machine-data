Barrier Miner (Broken Hill, NSW : 1888 - 1954), Saturday 2 December 1950, page 3




            Made  Flying
               Saucer




           STUNT         HAT        with    a   model   plane         thrust    through      the   crown

           is   worn      by    miniature       plane   maker         Arthur     Sanger,     of    South-
           ampton,       as    he   testshis latest      creation—a            flying saucer.          The
           saucer      took   him   three months          to    build,      is powered      by   a   Jetex
           rocket      motor,   and   flies for 30      seconds        up    to heights of     100    feet.
