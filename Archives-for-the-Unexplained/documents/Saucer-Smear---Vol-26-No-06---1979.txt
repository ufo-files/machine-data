   MISC. RAVINGS

          The 16th annual National UFO Conference (NUFOC) will be held in Cin­
   cinnati, Ohio on July 14th (Bastille Day) this year - as stated in previous
   issues of this rag.   Sponsored by OUFOIL, the event will take place in the
   Cincinnati (Norwood) Quality Inn Central.   For reservations, phone OUFOIL
   at 513-631-6288, or call the·motel directly at 513-351-6000. We are told
   that there are very few rooms left, and thus you would be well advised to
   get on the stick & make your reservation immediately, if you seriously in-
   tend to go.               .
          Public lectures are scheduled from 2 to 5: 30 on the afternoon of July
   14th, and from 7 to 11 in the evening. Speakers will include several mem­
   bers of OUFOIL, speaking on such topics as: "The Kentucky Abduction"; "Exo­
   biology and Alien Life Forms"; "The Nature of Ball Lightning"; "Strange En­
   counters from the Ohio Valley"; "Black Holes: Il).terstellar Subways?";  and
   " The Relationship of UFOs to Parapsychology".
          Your humble editor is Permanent Chairman of the NUFOC, and will pre­
   side over the annual "closed session" (organizational meeting) earlier in
   the day. He may also give a very brief but inspirational talk at the above­
   mentioned "open sessions", if called upon to do so • • • • •

          On two successive recent weekends, your editor made social visits to
   several of our more notable non-subscribers - Gray Barker in Clarksburg, W�
   Va. , and a few members of the Washington, D. C. ufological hard core - Dr.
   Willard Mcintyre, Phil Klass, Bob Sheaffer, and Dr. Bruce Macabee.
          Barker has very recently c'ome out with an issue of his newsletter -
   for the first time in well over a year. And, as amazing as it may seem, a
   minor-league movie company is seriously considering making a film based on
   Barker's classic UFO book, "They Knew Too Much About Flying Saucers". Pro­
   duction is scheduled to start in the Fall. We are told there will even be
   a part in it for your "Saucer Wit Rides Again" editor, i.e., some lucky ac­
   tor will be given the opportunity to play the part of your editor. We told
   Barker that we feel Robert Redford would be ideal for the part, but it
   seems much more likely that all the actors in the movie will be total un­
   knowns. Disappointing; but that's show biz: • • • • •

         The mysterious Todd Zechel is rumored to have withdrawn from the Wash­
   ington, D. C. scene, to finally write a book about his UFO research. We hope
   he doesn't forget that your humble "Saucer Wit Rides Again" editor has a
   five-year contract as his agent in regard to any & all ufological writings.

 an illogical but obnoxious skeptic. Most likely, Schroth was one of those
 pampered children so insulated from the harsh realities of the world that
 even as a teenager he continued to believe Santa Claus brought his Christmas
 toys and the Easter Bunny hopped by to leave him colored eggs. This immer­
 sion in fantasy led him to become a kiddie Ufologist, and his enthusiasm for
 kindly gift-leavers prompted him to view UFOs as the Santa-Claus-sleigh-like
 vehicles of friendly little Ufonauts who would leave him presents, as a re­
 ward for his devout faith.

 adulthood, now that he was over 21. However it happened, it's apparent

 absolute anger, like the long-term victim of a cruel hoax. Really, he was
 angry at himself for being so foolish, but his fury turned outward, mani­
 festing itself in attacks on fatherly Dr. Hynek, who perhaps represents
·Schroth's real father - the man Schroth holds responsible for duping him.
