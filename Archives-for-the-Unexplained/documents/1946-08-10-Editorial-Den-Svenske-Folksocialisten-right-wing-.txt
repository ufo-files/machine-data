August 10: A right-wing extremist view

”Den Svenske Folksocialisten” (The Swedish People Socialist) was a nazi-oriented extreme right wing newspaper [published 1939-1950]. We found the following article from their August 10 issue at the archives of the Foreign Office [Utrikesdepartementet] at the National Archives in Stockholm:

”THE PURPOSE OF THE GHOST ROCKETS? Since now photographs have been taken of the ghost rockets [there was only one photograph, see case xxx] and they have been sighted by officers of the Air Defence [see case xxx] and other persons who are not conditioned for hallucinations or mass suggestions, this should finally have silenced those Soviet lickers, who have tried to impress the world with [the theory] that a great part of the Swedish people had been caught by mass psychosis and were watching non-existing objects in the air.  

”Thursday´s revelation about the photo [case xxx] was especially fatal for the two Russian opportunists Bo Enander of Expressen and dr Eek of Aftonbladet. The forementioned has, on the same day as the rockets were photographed [actually the photo was exposed on July 9], flatly denied their existence, and dr. Eek has declared that they were meteors ”from southeast in space”. There is no southeast in space since it does not have any west or east, these cardinal points only refer to our small rotating earth  and there they create complications.

”Bo Enander is, by the way, the same person who, for several years, tried to portray the ghost fliers over Norrbotten 1934-38 as sheer hallucinations. 1939-40 it was revealed that it had, in the highest degree, been real. Now he has made another faux pas. It is this undiscriminating and unscrupulous licker of a foreign power of violence  that the Swedish Radio for a long time has allowed to comment on foreign news! 

”That the ghost rockets originate from Soviet is obvious. That they are sent deliberately is just as obvious. Otherwise it would just as often happen that they appeared over the British zone, which should be just as close to the shooting points. Why is Sweden the target of these shooting exercises? It can only be because Soviet thinks Sweden is more easily influenced than other countries. And because Sweden therefore shall be the object of a ”Baltification”-attempt according to all the rules of the art. 

”It is not strange if one thinks Sweden is ready. It is the result of the Swedish compliancy, after the ongoing Russian pressure attempts with aerial weapons -- the use of land weapons is hindered by the Baltic, and the Russian Baltic fleet is yet to weak, before the Swedish government has the time to give it [the Navy] the possibility to expand by [way of] free billion deliveries according to the coming ”credit”-deal between us and Soviet.  

”The shooting of the ghost rockets is directly caused by the release of Sidorenko after the Russian bombardment of Stockholm, and that Russian political figures were released into the Balt camps after the sinking of Hansa, and that Sweden fell for the demand for a release of the soldier internees [to Soviet], 

”Now, close at hand, is the enforcement of the Russian credit´s billion tribute to Soviet. In some time, when the world situation has changed further to the advantage of Soviet, it will concern [Swedish] domestic policy changes and bases in Sweden. Step by step the Swedish democracy is forced into the Russian sphere of dependence. 

”Only a hastily growing resistance movement  outside
democracy can break this chain of events before it is too late, and a liberation of the country from the suffocating embrace of the Russian bear will cost us streams of blood.”
