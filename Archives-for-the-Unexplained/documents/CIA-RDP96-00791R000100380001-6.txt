this pick-up point, present plans are for it to proceed SW in an arc which will take it back
to the mainland at a place SW of its present position. As it arcs around, it will engage
in fishing activities. There is an indication that this ship has diving capabilities which are
sometimes used for retrieving submerged or dropped drugs from the sea floor.

OOF: (201 045 JUL 90) The target vessel is currently involved with legitimate fishing
operations and is traveling in a northerly to northeasterly direction. The target is not
presently not carrying contraband but has done so in the past. There was an illegal
operation which was to occur but it did not gell due to a concern over the success of the
operation. The captain/crew/owner are thinking about the legitimacy of this particular
voyage and are feeling good about the overly successful [fishing] catch and that they can
make some money without smuggling illegal material. The target has been associated
in the past with a large sailing vessel with the term/name CAPE _ _ somewhere on it.
No transfer will occur. Additional information: names/terms/words associated with this
target: Alonzo, Fredrico, Chaco, Paco, Arturo, Sympatico, Escuche'.
