Case xxx.
Another Finnish fireball

Time: Thursday, June 20, at 22.20 (21.20 Swedish time)
Place: southern Finland

”FLASHING RED METEOR IN SOUTHERN FINLAND. At 22:20 o´clock on Thursday evening was observed again a strange luminous phenomenon in the sky. It was also obvious that a meteor was concerned. The luminosity, which was plainly perceptible against the light summer evening, moved slowly forward in a direction from south to north, and flamed red. According to the observers´ opinion the meteor ought to have fallen down somewhere in the Pellinge area of [the] Nylands island.”  

Status: Insufficient information
