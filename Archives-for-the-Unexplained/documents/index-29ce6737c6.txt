                                                                Explanation of Ratings
   Credibility = High: While the existence of each creature is debatable and some are clearly imaginary, the test sticks close to the available
   references.
   Style = Awkward: Drawing are relatively crude.

   Based on popular UFO literature, here is a alien "field guide", with a drawing of each creature and an
   account of how it was sighted. The author's make no representation that any of these aliens are real, but
   they do provida a "reliability index" for each. You can find Travis Walton's aliens here, as well as Ashtar
   Command, the "Roswell Autopsy" alien and the movie-prop alien that Penthouse was duped into buying
   photos of. The drawing themselves could be better, but the descriptions seem complete and balanced. As
   included are reviews of several supposed alien photos and a chronological list of UFO occupant sightings.
   -- Glenn Campbell

   Comparison to Other Books

   Similar to A Field Guide to Extraterrestrials. Field Guide offers better illustrations, while Faces of the Visitors offers
   more complete descriptions and documentation.

   Publisher's Synopsis

   It's here--the first illustrated, comprehensive, and authoritative album of alien visitors ever produced--complete with
   fascinating, detailed drawings, breathtaking, you-are-there-accounts, and experts' evaluations of each encounter. 80
   illustrations.


   From the Book Cover

   In every corner of the world, over the past century, people have been reporting encounters with a huge array of alien
   beings -- some tall and human-looking, some hairy and monstrous, some reptilian-looking, and even some that defy
   conventional description. In Faces of the Visitors,UFO expert Kevin Randle and documentary producer and artist Russ
   Estes have teamed up to produce an essential reference guide to the most important close encounters, alien contactee
   cases, abductions, and reported photos of aliens. Each of the over fifty entries is based on actual eyewitness accounts
   and includes:

           A stunning description of each alien and the name of its home world, if known

           A unique reliability rating, on a scale from 0 to 10

           A gripping you-were-there narration of the encounter, along with meticulous documentation of source material

   >Faces of the Visitors also offers a complete chronological database on the often overlooked but extremely important
   Euro-South American visitation wave of 1954 and the North American wave of 1973, as well as a comprehensive
   bibliography of important books, magazine articles, and journals. It is a fascinating reference for anyone curious about
   alien visitation and an unequaled source for those who want to learn more about their own experiences.

   KEVIN RANDLE, a sought-after expert on UFOs, is the author of several books on alien phenomena, including The
   UFO Casebook and UFO Crash at Roswell. He lives in Cedar Rapids, Iowa. Russ Estes has worked in many aspects of
   the entertainment industry. He most recently produced the documentaries Roswell Remembered and The Magic and
   Mystery of Giant Rock. He lives in Southern California.
