        This brief report updates the report of July 4,1989 wherein
   a white powder was supplied to the author by Mr. H. Tokarz of
   Toronto for compositional analysis.
        In that report it was suggested that apparent measurement
   artifacts in the X-Ray Fluorescence (XRF) method produced spurious
   results. Additional XRF measurements reported herein have largely
   cleared u p the initial confusion and added several elements to the
   original list.

        The enclosed plots Fig. C-1 and C-2 are taken from a Tracor
   XRF detector and a PGT detector respectively. They show no elements
   lower than Chlorine as discussed in the July 4 report. However,
   they confirm initial measurements and show trace quantities of the
   following elements:

                 Fe      Iron                                                  Cu   Copper
                 Zn      Zinc                                                  As   Arsenic
                 Nb      Niobium                                               Mo   Molybdenum
                 In      Indium                                                Sb   Antimony


         Possible additional elements are Kr (Krypton), Rb (Rubidium),
   Tc (Technetium), and Ag (Silver).
        The additional notations K 0( and Kf3     represent the energy
   lines   associated  with the  particular  element  and the numbers
   represent their energies in KeV.

        The plot Fig. C-3 is from a Jeol electron microscope at an
   independent lab using a Tracor detector and shows the Iron, Copper,
   Niobium and Molybdenum trace                         quantities clearly. What is still
   puzzling is why these do not                         show up in the U. of Toronto plot
   inthe July 4 report.

        Of great significance is the presence of Technetium if it can
   be confirmed. This element is not present in the crust of the
