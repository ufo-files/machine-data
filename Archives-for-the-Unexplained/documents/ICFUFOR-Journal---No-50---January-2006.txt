ICFUFOR Journal files No 50 January 2006


  Bat-Yam, October, 6, 2005

  Dudi Cohen, age 29, who lives in Hashmonaim St., makes routine searches and observations after UFO’s. He reports
  about a sight he saw between 20:12-20:33. He saw a shiny yellow orb. The object was surrounded with a red halo. He
  observed the halo, while videotaping the object.


  Bat-Yam, October, 10, 2005

  Dudi Cohen reports, that from the same location he saw and videotaped the last UFO, he sees an elliptic object, with an
  inner red-white light triangle. The object moved slowly from north to south. It disappeared in 2 minutes inside a cloud. It
  was very high and far in the sky.


  Natanya, October, 27, 2005

  A resident of the city, who lives in the upper flat in a multi-story building, reports he sees something like a very shining
  star, located in south-west. The witness sounded disturbed, and asked if we know what this object is. He claimed the
  object wasn't seen there in other days.


  Na’aleh, October, 31, 2005

  Hanoch Nebo reports, that at 17:50 he observed high in the sky, an unusual object, moving from south-east to north-east.
  Through binoculars he saw its color changed from white to orange.

  Ramat Ha’Saron, November, 1, 2005

  Michal reports, that at 17:45 she saw a very shining white object. She saw it first above an electric pole, while 4 beams

  She drove to the north, and felt the UFO follows her. She speeded up, and could see how the UFO follows parallel to her.

  Comparing to the stars, it was shiny, big and with 4 light beams. The observation lasted a few minutes.
  She also reported that twice she felt vibrations in her house, around 2:00 o’clock.


  Beer-Sheba, November, 7, 2005

  Chen, age 22, saw at 20:00 a UFO while walking with her dog. She said the object was shining and was fixed for about
  15 minutes, and changed its shape. She didn't hear any sound. She succeeded returning to her home, and to photograph
  the object with her digital camera. Then she sent us the pictures, and they were published in our Hebrew site. In the end,
  the object changed to a small spot and disappeared.
  In the photos we see a white object, with an unidentified shape, that changes its shape. Sometimes we see only a stripe of
  light, and in the other picture we see something like a half circle or saucer, that half of it is in the shadow.


  Hadera, November, 9, 2005

  I (Abi Greiff) received the report while driving to the south on the Shore Road, not far from Hadera. Doron, age 20,
  called and told me that he is frightened from what he sees. “It’s unnatural” he said. He called me a few times that
  evening. Doron saw a strange light above his house that changed its location. Late in the evening he told me the light
  moved away slowly


  Tel-Aviv, November, 9, 2005

  Eliyahoo Gilad reports’ he saw an orange light above the sea, about 40? above the horizon, and about 300 meters above
  the water. He observed civilian airplanes fly above it, and the object was between them and the sea level. He estimated
  the UFO was a few kilometers from the shore, right from Jaffa location. He observed the shining light for about 25
  minutes, while the light was fixed in its location. While observing it, the light lit off and turned on 3 times. In the end, it
  vanished and disappeared.

  Hadera, November, 13, 2005

  This event was reported to our Internet site. The witness, a 15 y.o. teen, claims that when he came back from Tel-Aviv in

  5 minutes. The witness says the observation “was really scary”.


  Haifa, November, 14, 2005

  Yehudit and Eyal Ram-On, who live on Ovadia St. on Carmel Mt., report about continuously observation, started at
  18:10. A reddish object was seen, not high, in the sky. It moved in a round course west from them. Yehudit says that a
  short time after seeing the object in the first time, a lot of military jets and helicopters appeared in the area, lying above
  the object and beneath it, when the UFO lowered. They easily heard the jets and could differ between them and the UFO.

  Eyal, observed through binoculars, and saw the object was constructed from 3 lights – 2 beneath and 1 above. At 18:30,
  Yehudit says that jets are surrounding the object and she hears also helicopters. At 19:10, she said that the object
  changed its course, and started to approach. Helicopters are always there, but they keep distance from the object.

  At 20:25, the object seemed to move away, while blinking its lights, and located higher. Yehudit says that jets and
  helicopters are always there. She saw a jet beneath the object that approached it and circled it from distance. The UFO
  blinked avery few seconds, moving slowly from one side to another. When a helicopter approached it, the UFO seemed
  to make a “jump” and changed its location.

  Another UFO appeared at 21:40. The first one seemed to be fixed, and the other circled it. They both disappeared in a
  few minutes. At 22:10 a loud sound of jets and helicopters was heard, and one of the UFO’s appeared flying to the north
  over the sea.

  5 minutes later, Yehidit saw a helicopter flying from north towards the sea. Then everything returned to be quiet. They
  didn't see nor the UFO, nor the jets.

  I (Abi Greif) arrived in the following day to the witnesses’ house. It turned out that the observation took place from the
  kitchen’s window, not bigger then ? meter in length, and faces the sea. In front of the window, I saw the near apartment
  roof and water tank. I assume that the UFO moved only in a small sector in the sky (because of the small observation
  window). According to the witnesses’, the UFO was big as an airplane. The couple reported us in the past about a UFO,
  that seem to fly diagonally and then straight north, along the sea shore.

  Moshav Petahya, November, 28, 2005

  Ariel, a bakery employee, reported: At 01:30, the witness’s daughter went to take a shower. She heard noises coming
  from the yard, and she thought that there is a possibility that robbers broke into the house, or someone is trying to steel
  the vehicle. She turned the lights in the yard on, but she didn't see anything unusual.

  The witness’s mother-in-law told me in the morning, that she heard the dogs bark, between 02:00-3:00, but she didn't pay
  any attention to the dogs.

  After 07:00 in the morning, the witness’s wife went out of their home, and discovered elliptic signs of imprinting in the
  house yard. The yard is assembled from hard pressed coarse sand and small stones. The woman called her husband, and
  he called the police. The police send a team of scouts, and then the police called Mr. Abi Greiff (chairman of ICFUFOR).

  At first, the dogs didn't want to approach the imprinting, and avoided entering the area. One of the dogs made its needs
  near the imprinting. Only after a few hours, the dogs entered freely to the imprinting.

  The police scouts, checked the house and its surroundings, but didn't find clues. When they weren’t able to get into any
  conclusions, they indicated in their report, that it is a case of a UFO. The scouts decided that an object came from above
  and probably left in the same direction. They couldn't decide what this object was. Any signs of an object brought there
  by someone, or signed of a dune buggy or any other vehicles, were rejected by the police. According to the police scouts,
  a tire sign, seen in the area of the imprinting, was made there a few days ago. The witness videotaped the police arrival.

  According to the witness, a helicopter hovered above his house, shortly afterwards (probably to scan the area).
  Hannan Sabat, the ICFUFOR investigator, arrived to the area (thanks to the witness’s invitation), about 11:00. Beside the
  witness’s family, or the police, no one was allowed to get there. With the witness’s help, he was allowed to check the
  area and photograph the imprinting (using a T70 CANON camera, and an AGFA color film, ASA-200).

  Signs in the area and the sings of the imprinting:

  The central imprinting is about 1.5 – 2 meters in diameter. It had a spiral shape, and reminds an oval elliptic shape.
  Extensions, turning outside, where found in the surrounding of the central spiral shape. The imprinting was shallow,
  about 1-2 centimeters in depth. The imprinting had a different color (brighter) then the soil around it.
