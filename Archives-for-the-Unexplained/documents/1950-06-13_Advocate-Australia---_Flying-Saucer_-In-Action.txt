Advocate (Burnie, Tas. : 1890 - 1954), Tuesday 13 June 1950, page 2




              "Flying                     Saucer"
                         in         Action
             HOBART,    Monday.                   -
                                                        About
           40 models, including                  a     "flying
            saucer"        and      jets, were        seen     in
           action        at     a   model       Aero         Club
           display at Hobart                 to-day.
               It was    staged by the West
           Hobart Model        Aero    Club.   The
           "flying saucer"        was    about     a
           foot in diameter.
               Outstanding model was a jet
           built by David         Reynolds. In
           two flights it reached            speeds
           of 90 and        95 miles     an   hour,                      &nbsp;




                              first
                                                                         &nbsp;




           and was      the          jet model   to
           be flown       in    Tasmania.        It
           was    copied from an American
           model     which     holds    the world
           speed record              of   159    m.p.h.
             The   models             were       controlled
           by wires from              the    ground.
