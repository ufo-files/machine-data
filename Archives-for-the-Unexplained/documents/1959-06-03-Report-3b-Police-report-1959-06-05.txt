Munkedal Precinct

REPORT
268.

THE LANDSFISKAL
 [a sheriff acting with greater authority – Translator’s note]
 in the district of Munkedal
June 6, 1959


Friday, June 5, 1959


Concerning reports of  how an unknown robotic object had fallen into Kärnsjön on June 3, 1959.

On Thursday, June 4, 1959 I received a report containing information on a robot-like object, said to have fallen into Kärnsjön within the municipality of Sörbygden the day before.

In connection with the resulting examination of this issue, these persons stated the following information.

Dig engineer Finn Högberg, resident of Dals Råstock and employed at Bohuslän Dozers, Hällevadsholm, was interviewed on June 4:

On Wednesday, June 3,  during 9:15 - 9:20 o’clock, whilst eating breakfast on a hill adjacent to the community of Vejebol  (about 500 meters from Kärnsjön), Mr. Högberg came to hear a whistling sound and as he looked up to the sky he was made aware of an approaching flying object, moving with tremendous speed. He estimated the length of the object to be about 1 and ½ meter and its wings had resembled those of a specific type of man-made aircraft, a “Drake”. The object, which traveled at an altitude of about 150 meters, went south by north.

A leaseholder by the name of Mats Elver Nilsson, born in 1927 and living in Fisketorp, was heard on the matter on June 4 and stated the following:

On Wednesday, June 3, at about 9:15 o’clock, Mr. Nilsson had occupied himself just north of a farm and the adjacent Kärnsjön. He then heard a whistling sound and expected the cause to be a jetplane, but instead came to realize that a flying object was moving directly above his own person. The object moved swiftly and Mr. Nilsson estimated its size to about 1 meter. Its wings were strongly curved. Only managing to keep the object under observance for about half a second, Mr. Nilsson quickly had his view obscured by the bushes. Subsequently, the object fell into Kärnsjön. According to Mr. Nilsson’s own words, the resulting water cascade was quite noticeable, being over one meter high. Also, the object seemed to flounce around just as it disappeared.

Refer to the attached map. Mr. Nilsson has identified the approximate location where the object had fallen into Kärnsjön. The water depth was estimated by Nilsson to between 3 and 5 meters.

Attachments:

A map of the site on a scale of 1:50000.
Map point 1: Approximate location where Nilsson was standing at the time.
Map point 2: Approximate location of the impact.

Unreadable signature
Policeman

PS:
Refers to the markings on the attached map concerning Nilsson's location, the course of the object and the impact site.
 
Munkedal, police office, June 5, 1959.

Gunnar Ottosson
Police assistant
