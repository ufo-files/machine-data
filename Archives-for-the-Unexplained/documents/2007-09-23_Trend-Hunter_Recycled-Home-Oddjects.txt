                                               Alien Furniture
                                   Bianca — September 23, 2007 — Art & Design
                                            References: bornrich.org


     If you're an sci-fi fan or if you really just like to showcase bizarre and odd items, then these
     oddjects could be for you! The Alien-inspired furniture collection features tables and chairs with
     the disturbing metal sculptures inspired by the classic horror/sci-fi flicks. Just like the movies,
     the horror doesn't so much come from the gore, but from the suspense -- it would be hard to sit
     in front of an object like this and not feel creeped out. On the upside, they are made from
     recycled materials, which was ultimately their saving grace and the reason they made it onto our
     site.

     1. Recycled Home Decor - Opportunity for furniture companies to tap into eco-conscious
     consumers by offering unique, recycled home decor options.

     2. Sci-fi Inspired Furniture - Opportunity for furniture companies to offer themed collections,
     catering to pop culture enthusiasts.

     3. Creepy Home Decor - Opportunity for furniture companies to create unique, unsettling designs
     that appeal to those who want to make a statement with their decor.


     1. Furniture - Furniture companies can explore new market segments by offering unique and
     unconventional home decor options that cater to a variety of niche markets.

     2. Eco-friendly - Opportunity for eco-friendly furniture companies to differentiate themselves by
     offering upcycled products that appeal to a wider audience.

     3. Pop Culture - Opportunity for pop culture merchandise companies to expand their offerings
     beyond traditional products, such as t-shirts or collectibles, and into home decor.
