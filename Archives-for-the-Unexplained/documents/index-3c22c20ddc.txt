    Our Review                                                                                 Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  The second episode of the cult TV series, takes Mulder and Scully to "Ellens Air Force Base," a
  fictionalized version of Groom Lake. Air Force pilots in a top secret aircraft program are disappearing or
  going crazy, and the wife of one of them calls the FBI. In a small town near the base, the agents visit a

  restaurant called "The Flying Saucer," where a surly woman behind the counter gives them instructions on
  where to look for the UFOs. The parallels with Rachel and the folklore of Area 51 are many. Naturally, the
  government is up to cosmic no good, aliens lurk in the wings and Scully will remain unconvinced at the
  end of the show. Nonetheless, this is one of the best episodes, and it hooked us on the series immediately.
  Also on this tape is the series pilot, a forgettable episode featuring alien abductions. -- Glenn Campbell

    Information from the Publisher                                                                           Always supportive

  It is not clear if the writers ever visited Rachel, but they certainly studied the UFO literature about the town. A warning
  sign that appeared on the cover of MUFON Journal - "There is a Restricted Military Installation to the West..." - also
  appears in the show, modified only slightly.

  This episode introduces "Deep Throat," a mysterious government source who warns Mulder off yet may also be his best
  ally. On the tarmac at Ellens Air Force base, Mulder finds what we've all suspected, but he can't take it home with him,
  and the truth remains out there.




  Promotional Material from the Publisher

          A TEST PILOT WHO MAY HAVE BEEN FLYING AIRCRAFT MADE WITH ALIEN
          TECHNOLOGY DISAPPEARS, AND MULDER AND SCULLY MUST DEFY THEIR OWN
          GOVERNMENT TO FIND THE TRUTH.

          Six top notch Air Force test pilots have disappeared after flying ultra secret experimental aircraft at Ellens
          AFB. When the wife of the latest victim files a missing persons report with the FBI, Mulder jumps at the
          chance to confirm his theory that the government is building aircraft with captured UFO technology. A
          mysterious informant, known only as "Deep Throat", appears and warns Mulder not to get involved in the
          case, but Mulder plunges in anyway. He and Scully witness an aerial display that defies knowledge of
          current technology, and find two unlikely allies in a pair of zoned-out teens who show Mulder a way onto
          the base...
