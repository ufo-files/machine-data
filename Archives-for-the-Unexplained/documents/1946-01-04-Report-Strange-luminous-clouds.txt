Strange luminous clouds in the morning and evening skies of January the 4th started this, the most ”phenomenal” year in Swedish UFO history. The snow-covered landscapes turned red and green. A bright fire-ball passed over the Swedish county of Dalarna on January 17, and there were other strange phenomena in the skies.

Swedish defence completed their technical analysis of the wreckage of a V-2 bomb that had crashed in south Sweden on June 13, 1944. 


Case 1.
Luminous morning clouds frightened horses and dogs

Time: January 4, in the morning
Place: Many places in South Sweden

A newswire from TT (Tidningarnas Telegrambyrå), the Swedish Newspaper Agency. published by many Swedish newspapers on January 8:

”On January 4, at 7.50 and 8.15 hours [in the morning] a number of fully reliable witnesses in Lund and surrounding areas, sighted, according to what professor Knut Lundmark [a wellknown Swedish astronomer, 1889-1958] has told [the newspaper] Arbetet, a light phenomenon in the sky which is difficult to explain. It seemed like a luminous zone from the south to the north and it had the strongest luminousity at the zenith. At Revingehed [a military training field] the army watch-dogs crawled into their kennels when the peculiar luminousity began. One horse, hitched to a wagon, lowered his head towards his legs and remained in that position as long as the light-phenomenon lasted.”

Weather observer Andersson in Hörby noted in the log book: 

”On the 4th between 7.50 and 8.10 a luminous phenomenon was seen over almost the whole sky. It was almost dark-red in colour and strongest at the zenith. The sky was clouded and the clouds were at a low altitude. The animals shied at the sight of the phenomenon, the dogs even crawled into their kennels. All electric lights indoors took on a greenish hue.”

Status: Luminous clouds.
Source: Several Swedish newspapers (such as Sundsvalls Tidning and Falu-Kuriren), January 8, 1946. Notes from the SMHI (Swedish weather bureau) yearbook copied by ufologist Sven Schalin.
