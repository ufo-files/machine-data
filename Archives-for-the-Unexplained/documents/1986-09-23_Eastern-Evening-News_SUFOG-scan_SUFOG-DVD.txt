Eastern Evening News. Norwich
2 3 SEP 1986
UFO mystery 2314
POLICE, air traffic controllers and
commuters were mystified by a flying
object spotted over Luxembourg today.
Police said the object, which was
also seen over Belgium, looked like
five or six bright green lights travelling
at high speed around 600 feet above
the ground.
Local radio stations said their
switchboards were jammed by com-
muters ringing in to report that they
had seen the unidentified flying object.
