GULF BREEZE·UPDATE:
       One thing we have definitely learned since starting a serious inves­
tigation of the Gulf Breeze case, is that the answer is complicated. Per­
haps not as much so as the MJ-12 mess; but nevertheless, people who simply
say "I believe it" or "I don't believe it" are expressing opinions     that
are probably based on insufficient or biased evidence. One friend actually
told us recently, "I don't care if Gulf Breeze is true or not.    I'm not in­
terested in it, because it is too complicated". Strangely, it is the com­
plexity and intrigue that attracts us to the case!
       Take, for example, the group of six UFO pictures and their negatives,
which Tom Smith Jr. recently turned in to the town elders of Gulf Breeze,
claiming they are hoax photos that Mr. Ed took with Smith's camera. We all
know that Ed's first group of five pictures was taken on Nov. 11th,    1987,
and first appeared in the local paper a week or so later. Were Smith's pic­
tures taken before or after Ed's first pictures? Smith would say      after,
since they were supposedly a part of Ed's continuing series of photographic
hoaxes. But a local former teenager known to us only as " Rob" says     that
Smith showed him his photos before anything was in the newspapers, i.e., pre­
sumably awhile before Nov. 11th, 1987. Rob also says that he was      "white
with fear" when his friend Smith showed him the pictures, and that Smith's
parents didn't give him (Smith Jr.) any support in his understandable un­
easiness after having snapped genuine pictures of a supposedly alien craft.
In other words, Rob backs up Ed's version of the matter.
       On the other hand, Mr. Smith Sr. tells us that other former teenagers
are ready to step forward and support his son's version of events. Needless
to say, Ed says the opposite. And the front page of the July 5th       Gulf
Breeze Sentinel states: "The Sentinel staff has contacted several young peo­
ple that were said to be friends of the Walters, and were told by all     of
them that they had no knowledge of fake photos or a hoax". Also Hank Bo­
land has now come out in print strongly backing up Ed.
       Bob Oschsler, a pro-Ed technician and photo expert, has examined Smith
Jr.'s pictures, and declared them to be genuine.    Another source tells us,
and we agree: "Smith's pictures are as genuine as Ed's." In other words,
if they are real, so are Ed's - and vice versa.
       If you aren't thoroughly confused yet, try this:   A reliable source
informs us that Smith's six UFO pictures are not from the same roll of film
as three landscape shots that were turned in �the Gulf Breeze authorities
at the same time and therefore presumed to be from the same roll.    The de­
tails are complex, but the gist is that this fact tends to support Smith's
version of the matter, which is that Ed shot the six UFO photos alone,     but
with Smith's camera.
       Now let's get back to the infamous " Ghost Demon" picture, which has
become a central theme with debunkers Overall and Willy Smith.    Just as we
were re-establishing contact with Nick Mock's mother (named Lina Chepult),
there appeared on the scene Zan Overall's book, entitled "Gulf Breeze Dou­
ble Exposed" .  This 50-page booklet is published by CUFOS, and oddly enough
was on sale in the vendors' room at the MUFON convention.

ed objective.   In it, Overall proves to his own satisfaction, through    a
series of convoluted assumptions, that the Ghost Demon picture is a double
exposure.   We would comment:  Even if this is true, it does nothing    to
