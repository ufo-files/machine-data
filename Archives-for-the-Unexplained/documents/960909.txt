        Well this would correlate with the seemingly dubious "MJ12 Special
  Ops Manual" published in Stan Friedman's new book "TOP SECRET / MAJIC."
  Paragraph 17 of the manual, the "Extraterrestrial Technology
  Classification Table," lists the recieving facility for numerous bits o'
  discs as "Area 51 S-4." The document is dated April 7, 1954, so I
  figured the hoaxer didn't know their base history really well. Even if
  the base was around in '54 for the good ol' U-2, sending crashed saucers
  there for R & D seems to indicate that the base had been around for quite
  a while before --i.e., long enough to establish/construct a very
  advanced facility. So if the base (or parts thereof, e.g., the runway
  and a couple buildings) were around during the early '40s then that would
  allow plenty of time for the base to have been fully operational by the
  '50s. Although, even if the base was around so long, the inclusion of
  "S-4" in the MJ12 Spec Ops Manual doesn't lend much credence to its
  authenticity. Well, unless you're pro-Lazar of course!
   So Glenn, hurry up and figure out the base's true history! :)
