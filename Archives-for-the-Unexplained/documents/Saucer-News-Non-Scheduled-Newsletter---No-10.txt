               To those familiar with saucer lore, this incident will bring to mind the night
of January 22, 1958, when Major Donald Keyhoe was cut off the Armstrong Circle Theatre due
to Air Force censorship.      In regard to the Long John hush-up, our opinion is that a more
prosaic explanation may be in ordera      It is known to your Editor that Gray Barker was one of
the very few who knew the studio's confidential phone number.      Isn't it possible that, to
increase sales of his book about the Bender hush-up, Barker arranged for a confederate to
make a hoax phone call to the studioT      The fact that Barker will stoop to almost anything
for publicity and profit has already been pretty well established by Lonzo Dove.       Only time
will tell whether this solution is the correct one, and whether the Long John program ever
dares to discuss flying saucers again.
