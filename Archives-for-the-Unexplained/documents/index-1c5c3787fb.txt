  Publisher's Synopsis

  Is planet Earth being regularly visited by extraterrestrial spacecrafts? Was there ever a crash of an alien ship, with the
  recovery of bodies of the strange creatures? Have human beings been abducted by aliens, put through bizarre
  ceremonies and tests, and lived to tell about it?

  The UFO Invasion: The Roswell Incident, Alien Abductions, and Government Coverups, a new special collection of
  Skeptical Inquirer articles just released from Prometheus Books, answers these and other questions with unique,
  insightful, in-depth investigations into UFO-related claims that span the years 1947 to today. This volume, edited by
  Skeptical Inquirer editor Kendrick Frazier, CSICOP executive director Barry Karr, and CSICOP senior research fellow
  Joe Nickell, details the first "sighting" of flying saucers in Washington State, the "discovery" of mysterious debris in
  New Mexico, and how the two incidents were tied together to incite national interest and later hysteria through
  unsubstantiated and eventually disproved media reports. Since those early days, the UFO phenomenon has escalated to
  incorporate claims of abductions, torturous testing on animals and humans by aliens, crop circles, an "alien autopsy,"
  and so much more. UFO stories from fifty years ago are now being resurrected for television, inspiring a whole new
  generation of misled believers.

  The title of The UFO Invasion, as editor Kendrick Frazier explains in the introduction, does not mean alien crafts are
  landing en masse across the United States. Rather, it means we are literally being invaded by thousands of media reports
  of everything from abductions to government coverups of UFO "evidence" -- irresponsible reporting that needs
