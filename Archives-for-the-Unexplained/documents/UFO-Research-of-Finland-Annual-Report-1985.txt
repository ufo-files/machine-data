COLLECTION            AND   TREATMENT       OF       FIELD   DATA


     In order to gather first-hand data the UFO Research of Finland has set up a
reseaecher network which covers the whole country. The membership of about 70
is the basis of the collection network. In addition we have several independent
contributors.
     Our address has often been mentioned in some periodicals, e.g. Ultra (which
covers extra-paradigm areas and alternative life views) and Tahdet ja                        Avaruus
(Stars and Space). In addition, our activities have been described in a few books
with   instructions for     data   collecting.   We get      some   50   reports   a year,    on an
average.
   From the reports we receive we will decide on further measures in each case.
The case reports are hereby classed into three groups:


1.         Unexplained observations ( UFOs as defined). These cases contain detailed
           information and precise descriptions, so that all natural causes can prac­
           tically be excluded. Such cases will be studied as thoroughly as possible,
           sparing no energy and resources.


2.         The information     remains insufficient       despite   all attempts to get more
           details.    Thus, the cause of the observation cannot be decided. This is
           the largest category of cases, unfortunately. In these cases we continue
           investigations, if possible.


3.         Explained observations: the information allows us to pinpoint a natural
           source for the observation,       e.g. astronomical, meteorological and tech­
           nological phenomena. Sometimes, too, a hoax is disclosed.

1985:     AN     INTRESTING            YEAR


    During 1985 the              UFO    Research of Finland studied six new sighting reports.
Two     cases    were       explained.     One   was   an   airplane     and the other a shower of
meteors.      Three      cases    had     insufficient information       and       could therefore      not    be
given any other classification. One case, a column of light, remains unexplained.
In the Finnish press we found about 220                     UFO related articles, an increase of
37 % from the previous year. The chairman of the organization, llkka Serra, was
interviewed on national radio on March 16th and on national television on March
17 th.
   We also uncovered a number of intresting old cases, the oldest from the year
1898 and the others from 1917 and 1943.


STRANGE          LIGHT       PHENOMENON             OBSERVED        AT   SAARIJARVI


Date: Monday 29 July 1985
Time: 12.00         pm
Place: Saarijarvi in the vicinity of the town Mahlu, central part of Finland
Witnesses: Mr. Olavi Schroderus and Mr. Erkki Nieminen
Nature of sighting: Strange sparkling light spot with a light column


   Here is the story told by the observer:
   "It was Monday 29 July 1985. I Olavi Schroderus, and Erkki Nieminen were in
Erkki's summer cottage during that evening. The weather was calm, very cloudy
and dark. We were sitting inside the cottage, watching TV and eating sausages.
The time was around 10.30 pm when I went outside in order to move my car
from    the    road.     The     road was going through the yard of the cottage.                        In   case
people should want to drive to the other cottages nearby, I had to move it. I
finally parked the car to a place some 40 meters from Erkki's cottage. At that
time, nothing unusual could be observed.
   At     Erkki's      cottage      the    forest   road    branches     off       to    the   shore   of    Lake
Kalajarvi and goes on to the other cottages. I placed my car on the road that
leads directly to the shore. Because the electric current to the movable TV-sets
was becoming inadequate,                we had to take the battery from Erkki's car. To be
able to put the lights on also in the cottage I had to move my car again to the
grass in the yard of the cottage. I went to move my car at about 12.00 pm.
    When I was walking towards the car, I noticed on the left hand side of the
forest road, some 16 meters away, a reddish, strangely sparkling ligl)t spot on the
ground.       It was about 0. 5 meter in diameter. I stopped at once and tried to look
for the origin of the light. However, I couldn't see or hear anything. I thought
that    perhaps the light might be coming from the Lake Kalajarvi.                               After having

Investigations
      Mr. Lasse Ahonen from the UFO Research of Finland visited the observation
place together with Olavi Schroderus 10 August 1985. The place where the light
spot had been, is the left hand side of the forest road, seen from the cottage.
Nothing could be seen on the ground where the light spot had been. There were
several ant paths on the place, and the small insects seemed to move as usual.
   The first observation was made from a distance of 16 meters, when only a
clearly contrasted reddish light spot was seen on the ground. The diameter was

then    50 em and the thickness 2-3 em.                                When the observer had approached the
light spot to a distance of some 2 meters away, some kind of light pillar ca. 3
meters high, could be seen. Where the light was coming from is unknown, because
nothing particular could be seen above the light pillar. The light simply ended at
a height of 3 meters. It seemed to come down in bandlike wedges. The light on
the ground was so dense or                     so    bright that the ground couldn't be seen behind it.
On the other hand, the trees behind the light pillar could be observed. Even if
the light on the ground was bright, it was not dazzling, however.
    The branches of a nearby spruce were very close, but not inside the light
pillar. The branches of the trees or the heather close to the light spot on the
ground were not lit up.
    When the light grabbed the hands of the observer, it didn't illuminate the
arms at all. The light was only on the bare skin. The shoes weren't lit up either,
despite    being just        beside           of    the light spot.           He didn't get any aftermath; like
eczema, illness or else. The light spot was stationary during the entire time of
observation. The time totalled 1-2 minutes.
    During       the   observation             the    weather          was    cloudy,     very       dark and completely
without winds. No thunder could be detected; the temperature was about l0°C.
    What caused this phenomenon is still unexplained. It can hardly be an electric
phenomenon         caused        by      some        manmade           electric     device,       bacause          there       is    no
electricity or power lines in the vicinity. No reflecting objects of any kind were
visible, either.


H UMANOIDS         OR        BALLOONISTS                AT L UMIVAARA                  IN    1898


    Lumivaara          is    a     municipality             near       Lake      Ladoga      in      eastern        Finland.         A
10-year-old girl named Riitta Nukarinen was walking in the forest when she saw
an object that looked like a balloon just above the treetops. There was a rope of
some sort going from the balloon to one treetop and there were people inside the
balloon.      Later    she       told    the        story    to    her      children      who     then      told    it    to        UFO
researchers.
   A group of engineering students from Petersburg made pioneering balloon trips
in the Lake Ladoga area in 1913, but no explanation has been found to the 1898
case. Perhaps the case was related to the 1890's airship wave in California.

A     UFO    LANDING    AT    KURKIJOKI       IN   1943


During the war years two girls from Kurkijoki had been picking lingon berries in
the forrest and were returning home. They pushed their bicycles on a remote dirt
road. When they came to a better road they suddenly saw a 1-meter-high and
1. 5-meter-long object in the middle of the road. It moved very slowly forward at
a distance of about 50 meters from the girls. The object was grey and had a
lumpy surface. The girls were amazed and just stood there.
      After 10 minutes of slow movement the object disappeared in the bushes by
the roadside. One of the girls suggested that they take a shortcut to where the
road was leading in order to find out more about the object. But the other girl
said it would not be safe since enemy soldiers had recently been seen in the
area. So the girls went home and the object remains a mystery.


GHOST       ROCKET     ANNIVERSARY


      1986 is the 40th anniversary of the ghost rocket sightings in Finland and Swe­
den. Despite extensive studies by both civilian and military authorities, the ghost
rocket cases were never solved. In Sweden alone there were 997 recorded cases,
of which 22 5 were daylight sightings. Military documents of the time show that
the    ghost rockets   were   not   Russian   test rockets.   This year would be a most
appropriate time to re-examine the documents.
