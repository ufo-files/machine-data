            If these "flying saucers" are

         In the case    of a mirage, a
       layer of very hot air a few feet
       off the ground    acts as  a kind
       of mirror which    sends a reflec-
       tion of the thing being reflected
       along the length of that layer,
       perhaps at a distance of 50
       miles, where   it  is  seen by a
       bystander who     thinks he sees
       the thing being reflected stand-
       ing or lying only a couple of
       hundred yards away  from him.
       This is a           "horizontal"         kind    of
       reflection.
