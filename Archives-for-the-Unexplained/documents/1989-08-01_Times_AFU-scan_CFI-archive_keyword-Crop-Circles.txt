Circles puzzle solved
Scientists investigating 98 mystery circles on two heather-
clad Welsh hilltops have discovered that they were mowed
by a local farmer to encourage more grouse to settle. The
circles, at Llanthony, near Abergavenny, Gwent, were
thought to be linked to scores of circles discovered across the
country since 1976. Undeterred, the scientists have moved
back to Wiltshire to study a 32-metre circle, the biggest so
far, found close to Silbury Hill, near Marlborough.
