4th April 2006.
The Daily Mail.
Ant And Dec's Alien Adventure.

   Ant and Dec thrilled fans at the premiere of their first big screen movie. 
   The kings of TV entertainment star in Alien Autopsy, a comedy based on a true story.
   They play best friends Ray Santilli and Gary Shoefield, UFO enthusiasts who 10 years ago convinced the world that they had film of a real life UFO autopsy.
   The I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here! stars spent an hour and a half signing autographs and chatting to fans outside the Odeon, in London's Leicester Square, and added that they did believe in UFOs.
   Dec, 30, said: "I do think there's something else out there. It's naive to think that we're the only intelligent life source out there."
   Ant, also 30, added: "Obviously there's some kind of life elsewhere. I don't know whether it would look like anything we recognise but I do believe in aliens."
   The pair said they had enjoyed the green carpet experience with Dec jokingly adding: "We even signed autographs for people that didn't want them, like tourists just having coffee.
   "We've seen Tom Cruise do it so thought we should too. 
   "Apparently we were out there for an hour and a half but it felt like 10 minutes.
   "I think Robbie Williams has just sent a text congratulating us. I was sat vibrating backstage if you know what I mean, but I haven't read it yet."
   The pair joked that they enjoyed being airbrushed for the film's promotion material with Dec adding: "That's the most exciting thing. We've obviously been on holiday and got suntanned, it's brilliant."
   Ant said of making the film: "Obviously we were really nervous about doing it but thought that if we're going to take risks and challenge ourselves then this is the film to do it because we really believe in it." 
   Dec added last night: "We've been really looking forward to tonight. This might be the only movie we ever make, the only premiere we ever have so we just decided to enjoy it.
   TV projects "We're signed to ITV to 2007 so certainly till the end of then we'll still be working on our TV projects. "We wanted to see how this went and if viewers can think of us as these characters.
   "If things come in then we'll look at them but we're certainly not going to launch an assault on Tinseltown."
   Other stars who attended on the green carpet last night included Hollywood star Minnie Driver, Tess Daley and Vernon Kay, Jimmy Carr, Donna Air, comic David Baddiel, Phillip Schofield and Caron Keating's widower Russ Lindsay and Sally Meen.
   Liberty X star Michelle Heaton was also at the event but wired up to a device to check her heart rate.
   She joked: "It looks glamorous doesn't it? I went to the doctor because my heart felt like it was going to explode but I'm carrying on as normal and apparently it's quite common.     "They asked me whether I do drugs as apparently this is what happens but I told them that I didn't and to do tests on me if they wanted."
    Girls Aloud star Cheryl Tweedy and Kimberley Walsh were also at the premiere with Cheryl's fiance footballer Ashley Cole. Cheryl said of her impending nuptials: "If you ever get married run away and do it somewhere else. It's very stressful. I'm panicking that no-one will turn up and thinking about running away.
   "I've got a wedding planner but they ask you everything anyway."
