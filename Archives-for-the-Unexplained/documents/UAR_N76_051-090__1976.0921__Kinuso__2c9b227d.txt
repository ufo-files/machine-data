Wayne ERICKSON ---- Sighting of U.F.O.
Kinuso, Alberta -=- 21 SEP 76
CONTINUATION-SUITE
Appendix #1
Statement of_Wayne_ERICKSON of_Fairview, Alberta_ (D.O.B:_16 Oct 50)
on the 2lst of September, 1976, at approximately 5:15 P.M.,
I was in my room at the Kodiak Lodge Hotel, Kinuso, Alta. I was
looking out the window towards the south when I noticed an object
flying in a Westerly direction. This object appeared flat and round
with a hump in the centre, much like a fried egg. The object did not
appear to have any wings. It was flying at about 6000 feet and at a
speed of approximately 150-200 miles per hour. This object changed
directions abruptly, more of a fluttering motion. It did this very
fast and always maintained a Westerly direction.
It appeared silver coloured at first and then as it proceeded
West, it changed to a white, It was travelling towards the direction
of the sun. This vehicle may have geen going in a more south-westerly
direction than west. I only saw the one object: There was no-one else
present with me and no-one else saw it that I know of. There was no
noise and it appeared close enough that if it was an airplane there
would have been noise. I have a commercial pilots license #XDC 9286
and am familiar with most types of airplanes. I judged the size speed
and altitude with reference to a CESNA 402. That is, it appeared to be
about the same size as a CESNA 402 would be at 6000 feet. The object
appeared to be about two miles away when I first seen it. I maintained
a visual contact with it for approximately one minute.
I had not been
drinking prior to this sighting. I am part-owner of the Hotel and 1
was preparing to paint.
Wit:
22-9-76
10:24 P.M.
Signed: Wayne ERICKSON
