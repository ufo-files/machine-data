ROLAND’S SUBJECT: ‘A Paranormal History of Nessie’

The popular view of the Loch Ness Monster as a relict plesiosaur has dominated the thinking of Nessie hunters since the 1930s.

Industrial Revolution only to wane before it found new life in the thinking of the 60s generation.

this most esoteric of Nessie subjects. We will also look at how this view of the monster dovetails with other paranormal
