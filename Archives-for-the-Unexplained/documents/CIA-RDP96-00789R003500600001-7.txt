                           SUMMARY OF INFORMATION

      The target site is situated in a natural setting.                 There is

a small body of water nearby.

      There are panels throughout the tunnels.               In one area, I

could see a small television screen and there were panels around

the screen.     It reminded me of a relay command and control

station. At one point, I had the impression of satellites. There

were not any people here.

      I could see vans in the tunnels.             Everything inside the

tunnels looked new and bright.           I could see a bright ring inside

the tunnels.

      At one point, I could see several men sitting around a round

table.     Several men wore military uniforms.            The men were older

and I had the feeling they were of higher rank.                They had

equipment plugged into their ears and they were sitting very

close together.      They were monitoring or listening to something

outside.     This was very sensitive information.             This may have

been a bugging.

      The men and women that work here do not come here on a

regular basis.      It seems that people come here only when required

and at a special time.
