Montevideo, 6 (ANSA) - A new mystery chapter about the existence of UFOs

and his hair was burned after seeing a strange object that emitted a
strong beam of greenish light.

works when he suddenly heard a strange noise and simultaneously the
area was intensely illuminated 300 meters all around him.

sky a small object that gave off a powerful light beam. At that moment
he fainted and shortly thereafter, upon recovering~ he noticed his hair
was burnt.
Laco Ozano was ~ken to the hospital in Rocha where it was verified how-
ever, that his scalp had no lesions.
At the hospital he was submitted to a psychiatric examination by special-
ist Clemente Sazbon, who in principle concluded that the protagonist of
the terrifying event is a normal person, without any background of mental
disorders.
He added that in spite of the witness not being a culturooman, his tes-
timony in principle should not be doubted, and he estimates that Laco
Ozano was unconscious for approximately five minutes after hearing
what he termed "the explosion of a gas container".
