                  THE INFERNAL TRIANGLE IS GETTING BOLDER

Although the 'Flying Triangle' (or any other UFO) failed to appear during our May 1st
skywatch at Bretby, their numbers have been increasing. The PRA is currently receiving FT
information from a wide number of UFO groups, including the 'Cornwall UFO Research
Group,' which has recently sent us a number of detailed and well presented investigations.

Triangles' sightings have increased over the Nuclear Power Stations around our coast. Victor
has informed us that there has been a 53% increase in sightings over Bradwell NPS (1995 -
58 reports & 1996 - 89 reports) and a massive 132o/o increase in FT sightings over Sizewell
NPS (1995- 65 reports & 1996- 151 reports).

England, I thought they only appeared over the USA?"

28th August 1995.

fighting among ourselves, so I'm very interested in addressing that."

I believe that we should all remember these words and their implications.       Now let us get
back to the business of tracking theFTs and learning as much about them as we can.           We
may even find a clue as to what they are up to!

In the early hours of the 1st April 1997 a Flying Triangle craft passed over a motor works on
the Coventry Road, at Hinckley, Leicestershire.

building. He had paused to look up at the comet Hale-Bopp, with its long tail sweeping across
the sky. His attention was suddenly attracted to a pulsating red light in the sky, over in the
direction of the nearby Ashby canal. The red glowing light was approaching from the NW and
heading on a SW course and was coming closer by the second. It appeared to be low, maybe
only 70ft. up.

He became excited and then a little panicky as he realised that this was something highly
unusual.   The dawn chorus of birdsong suddenly went silent as the red light approached. He
heard a low droning sound as it passed over. He also noticed that his guard dog's eyes

DUNKIRK

     On  the  3rd  of  April  (1997), Mr Glyn Hickling was
walking his dog at  about  21:15 in the Dunkirk area of
Nottingham. Mr Hickling was watching the comet Hale-Bop when
a red light caught his eye. As it approached and decended on
one  of  the  busiest parts of Nottingham's road systems, Mr
Hickling saw that the red light was followed by three   other
identical  lights. They  were  equally spaced and glowed the
same deep red colour. They levelled off at about 250 feet at
which point they were passing over  Mr  Sickling, he   waited
for  the sound of the engines and became confused when there
was none. The distant traffic would not have drowned a light
aircrafts engine from the location the witness was at.

     All four craft appeared as balls of red   light, but   at
their  closest  point  they were' disc shaped. As the objects
passed silently overhead, they started to   gain  height   and
move  over  to  their right. Mr Hickling noticed on the back
disc slightly above the red body shape, a tiny   white   light
which he said was part of the craft itself.

     Mr  Hickling  stated  that the most amazing thing about
the sighting was the total lack of noise.



BESTWOOD PARK

observable. The airliners seemed to be travelling across the

hours and the last glow of daylight could  be  seen   on  the
horizon. This light travelled from East to West.

     Before   the   light   disappeared, an  identical   light
appeared from the South   and  travelled  North. Again  there
were  no  flashing light on this light and it appeared to be
about the same height as the other. We assume that the light
was self illuminating because the only reason we   could   see
the airliners, was because of their flashing lights.

April I 7th 1993

After retiring to bed, I soon fell into a deep sleep, it was some time later that my vision began.

I opened my eyes and I was inside what appeared to be a control room of some sort, in an
enormous flying machine. I knew we were in flight because I could feel the movement of the
craft beneath my feet. There was a loud humming sound coming from somewhere far below
me.   The room was ablaze with light, a blue fluorescent haze pressed into every corner, it
pulsed with a strange hypnotic rhythm like a heartbeat, I felt it inside my head, beating in time
with my own heart.

The light grew brighter and brighter with every second and bathed me in its radiance. I felt
that I was suspended in a blue void, floating aimlessly on a gentle tranquil sea. My conscious
self was lost in the _unimaginable beauty of this unexplored domain.               Very gradually, I
experienced a complete inner peace, the like of which I had never known before. I felt myself
sinking slowly down and down into what looked like a huge whirlpool of colours.                They
flickered and danced before my eyes in a never ending display of hues and changing patterns.
I had no feeling of fear or apprehension, only a feeling of utter contentment. Just how long I
was in that state, I could not tell, but after what seemed an eternity, the blue haze suddenly
and without warning disappeared.

With a terribly jerk, I was suddenly catapulted back onto the deck of the ship, a little confused
and very unsteady. Fortunately the effect did not last long and I was soon able to reorientate
myself and at last see clearly for the first time.

The room was, as far as I could determine, oval shaped and blue throughout. There was a
large control console standing in the centre of the cabin. It was about ten feet long and four
feet high. It had a mass of weird coloured lights on its top and some were flashing in some sort
of sequence. There were three thickly padded seats positioned in front of the console and on
the left of this was what I could only describe as an illuminated cubicle about six feet in height.
On the right was a hatch or doorway and about fifteen feet in front of me I could see three
human figures. They were standing by a large viewing monitor, watching a transmission of
some description. They were totally unaware of my presence, but I was strangely drawn
towards them, as though there was a special bond between us, but I didn't know what it was.
The crew members were busily pointing out items of interest on the screen, then eventually the
transmission came to an end.      They spoke to each other in what sounded like English, but I
was not close enough to hear what they were saying. I walked slowly and hesitatingly
towards, a little nervous and unsure of myself

If I had expected to see beautiful, graceful creatures with the wisdom and intellect of countless
ages, then I would have been disappointed. Instead, there were three ordinary, but colourful
individuals standing before me. The leader was a little taller than his two companions, he was
about six feet tall and very slender. His head was normal in size to my own and his hair was
shoulder length and reddish brown in colour.              Around his forehead he wore a broad silver
band, with two small static lights on each side. His eyes were piercing blue in colour and of
