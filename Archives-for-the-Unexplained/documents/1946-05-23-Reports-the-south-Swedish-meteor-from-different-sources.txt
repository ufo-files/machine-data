Case xxx.
The mid-Swedish midnight meteor

Time: Thursday, May 23, shortly before midnight
Place: Västerås, Stockholm, Gävle and Sundsvall areas
   
The sightings that started ”the ghost rocket wave”, at least in the media, and with the Defence Staff, were the two phenomena reported after the night between May 23 and May 24.  

The first of these phenomena was, by all rational judgement, a brilliant meteor. Sighting times differed between 23.45 on May 23 and 00.05 on May 24. Much of the media attention concentrated on ”the Landskrona cigarr” (see next case), which explains the word ”also” in the following press accounts:

xxx-1: FIREBALL ALSO SEEN IN GÄVLE. Gävle, the 26th (TT). A phenomenon like the one observed in Landskrona [see next case] the night on Friday, is now also reported by a trustworthy person from Gävle. He says he observed, at 23.48 on Thursday evening, a fireball with tail, leaving sparks, moving in a southeasterly direction parallel to the horizon and at an altitude of about 35 degrees over this [the horizon]. The fireball was in sight for about three seconds. (Hälsingborgs Dagblad [TT newswire], May 27).

xxx-2. ”FIREBALL ALSO SEEN OVER MEDELPAD. Sundsvall. Even Medelpad has a phenomenon similar to the one observed  in Landskrona. The fireball was observed in Sundsvall and in Borgsjö-Ede in the western part of Medelpad. A night-watch in the harbour of Sundsvall says he saw a fireball, at 23.48 hours on Thursday, moving in a southeasterly direction, throwing sparks and easily viewable. In Borgsjö-Ede, a trustworthy person made a similar sighting at about the same time. Even here the fireball moved in a southeasterly direction.” (Sundsvalls Tidning and TT newswire, May 28). 

xxx-3 ”...a couple of persons in Stockholm has told Dagens Nyheter that they have seen a fireball shortly before midnight the night on Friday. It was a large fireball with a tail. One of the witnesses thinks it had an easterly course and another that it moved towards the SE... At the Stockholm observatory no observation of a similar phenomenon has been made at the time in question, but they don´t deny that it could have been a meteor, since the time estimates are so close to each other.” (Dagens Nyheter, May 28)  [Comment: this sighting was never noted by the Defence Staff. Reports from Dagens Nyheter are generally absent in the Staff files.]

xxx-4. ”...many persons in the Stockholm area stated to the MT that they saw the same phenomenon in Huddinge and Hagalund. No one knows what is going on. One guess is of experiments with secret weapons, but aviation experts that in such a case it is curious that no sound is reported in connection with the observations - long-range bombing practice gives off noise.” (Morgon-Tidningen, May 28 [from Thomas Bullard: The Airship File]). 

”...a driver from Huddinge reports that at midnight, the night on Saturday he saw a luminous projectile which he considers not to be neither an airplane nor a meteor.” (Dagens Nyheter, May 26).

xxx-5. Another report of this meteor, noted by the Defence Staff, seems to have been reported from Untran, a place 30 kilometres to the SSE of Gävle. Here a fireball with tail was observed in the northeast by the town planning director of Gävle. This report may have gone directly to the Staff, although there is no note or memo on the case in the files, only an entry in the table of  

xxx-6. A private person from Västerås reported another sighting which might have been the same phenomenon. It seems this report, too, was made directly to defence authorities, because it was never mentioned in the newspapers. The sighting occured at five minutes past midnight (00.05). (In our experience a time discrepancy of a quarter of an hour is not unusual for high-altitude sightings of meteors reported from many places).  According to the Defence Staff´s report tabulation the fireball with tail, large as a full-moon, fell at ”a maximum of 500 meters from the observer”. A whining or whistling sound was heard but no bang or splash [presumably the sighting occurred close to a lake]. As with the sightings from Gävle the phenomenon was seen in the southeast.
