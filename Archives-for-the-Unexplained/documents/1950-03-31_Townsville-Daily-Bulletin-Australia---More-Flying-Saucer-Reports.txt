Townsville Daily Bulletin (Qld. : 1907 - 1954), Friday 31 March 1950, page 3


            MORE          FLYING
         SAUCER            REPORTS
          LONDON,       March    30.— Reuter's
       Rome     office says    flying saucers
       have been sighted over         Italy five
       times   In the last 24 hours, accord-
       ing to reports from different parts
       of the country.
          At Salo, on Lake Garda, several
       people reported seeing a disc as
       large as a full moon           streaking
       across   the sky.
          Other reports came      from Carrara
        (four   saucers); Catanzaro         (one
       saucer   heading west "like a moon
       with a wake of fire") Cagliari (one
       saucer,    that hung     over   the clty
       for 20 seconds); Cal D'Aosta         (one
       saucer).
          Similar reports have        also been
       received      from     Addis      Ababa,
        Cyprus, Santiago (Chile), Bogota
        (Colombia),       and      Barranqullla
        (Northern     Caribbean).
