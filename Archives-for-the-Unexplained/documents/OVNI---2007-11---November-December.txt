                               THE ALIEN AUTOPSY FILM




                         A frame from the controversial Ray Santilli film 'Alien
                         Autopsy' allegedly taken in 1947 of a 'Roswell' Alien.




As a matter of interest, just several days before the 'fake' disclosure at the Roswell 60 Conference, the
'Alien Autopsy' film was shown to new PRA member and former Royal Army Medical Corps service­
man Mr Derek Pickering of Derby.

Mr Pickering had served in the RAMC for many years and was on active service during the Korean
War, when he was involved in a number of autopsies and he has also attended many others since then.
Derek was asked to give his opinion of the film shortly after he had viewed it and he made the following
comments:

"Everything was wrong, it just wasn't real, it was so silly!"

"There was no headrest supplied under the head, the brain was accessed by cutting through the skull the
wrong way, a half-round cut and over the top, instead of completely circular cut around the skull."

"The person performing the cutting of the skull behaved as if he was sawing through a wooden branch
instead of carefully cutting around the skull."

"The film showed only the front of the body and not the back, I just cannot believe that it was a genuine
autopsy!"

Mr Pickering also added that in view of the fact the body was alien, one should have used a biological
protection suit completely isolating the wearer, with an independently supplied air system, instead of
breathing through a mask.

It was disclosed at the conference, that the film was a fake and that Mr Ray Santilli had considerable
help in making the film. One of the persons involved was artist and sculptor Mr John Humphreys who
made the alien body and then willingly acted the part of one of the 'pathologists' in the film.

We understand that certain parties have instigated legal action for fraud and consumer fraud, plus other
charges against Ray Santilli over his faking of the 'Alien Autopsy' film.

                      Mystery aircraft baffle observer
                   From the 'Northumberland Gazette Published Date: 11 October 2007

                                             Location: Northumberland

                                               ByROBERTBROOKS

Eyes are on the skies over north Northumberland, after claims that a pair of strange black triangular air­
craft were spotted. The observer, who has a lengthy Armed Forces background but has asked not to be
identified, says he first noticed the planes as they passed over nearby hills.

He then watched from his car as they performed manoeuvres in close formation over Alnwick Moor and
the Vale ofWhittingham area at dusk last Wednesday. But he has been left baffled by the sighting,
because he couldn't identify the aircraft as anything currently being flown by either British, European or
US air forces.


He said: "I could hear the engine noise of jets, which isn't uncommon here in rural Northumberland, but I
always look up anyway. "Instead of the usual RAF Tornados or Hawk trainer jets, I saw two black
triangle-shaped objects flying low and fast towards the west."

"They were performing manoeuvres in tight formation as they
went, but their shape didn't conform to any military aircraft I'm
familiar with. They were very flat in profile and didn't appear to
have any tail as such, just what seemed to be small angular fins
towards the rear of each 'wing'."


"I've checked with various sources, but can't find anything that
even remotely matches what I saw, not even the USAF's F- 1 17
Stealth Fighter. It was all very strange."


A spokesman for the Ministry of Defence at the Otterburn
Training Area said: "We're not aware of any foreign forces
exercising in the area, we just have our normal guys up here at
the minute." Inquiries to RAF Boulmer also drew a blank.

The sighting came just days after RAF top brass admitted that
one of their Tornado jets, on a routine training flight from
Lossiemouth in Scotland, had lost a dummy bomb somewhere
over Northumberland.

                                UFO AND ALIEN PROGRESS

                                               By Bill Foster

Much is said, more is written and little is achieved or completed in the way of significant progress in
explaining or revealing a cohesive understanding of the UFO and alien situation.
   We have many brilliant, scientific, professional and studious minds all very interested in the subject
matter, yet little definitive progress has been made toward comprehension of the true depth or breadth of
the situation.
   For a moment let's assume that there are only 100 brilliant minds of males and females, in the entire
world, that are ardently focused on the UFO and alien situation. How are these mindful efforts presently
divided?

   Some folks are still trying to prove the existence of the Roswell crash, complete with alien grays.
Some investigators are trying to understand crop circles. Some people are investigating cattle mutila­
tions.   Some researchers are trying to source how many heads of state and U. S. Presidents may have
known about UFO's and aliens.       Some analysts are attempting to prove that world/national govern­
ments are "covering up" the facts and artifacts.

   Some genealogists are working on the theory that the whole topic may be a "cultural'� phenomenon
or aberration. The point here is that all these one hundred singular efforts, though diligently applied, are
randomly scattered and worse, very isolated. There is no unity of purpose behind any one investigation,
no sharing of facts, no central clearing house of information. Not even one centralized location for you
or I to ask the question: "Has anybody tried this approach?"

   Fantasize with me for a moment on a unified front. That is, we assemble one hundred folks who will
stipulate that

Roswell did happen, it is over and done

People truly see triangles, cigar shapes, discs and dozens of other aerial phenomena daily, around the
world

Rendlesham Forest happened, it is over and done

Betty and Barney Hill were really abducted by grays

There are thousands of abductions going on presently all over the world

The various governments of the world may have more or less knowledge than is publicly known, but
it does not matter to the effort at hand

There are multiple forms of alien craft in use around the earth

There are many different life forms visiting the earth

Crop circles, cattle mutilations, psychic visions and paranormal phenomenon may or may not be re­
lated to UFO's and aliens

This then, would be a starting point To acknowledge that all this information exists and quickly put it

behind the study group would allow the group to concentrate on the more foundational questions that

need answering, such as:


How do we systematically obtain credible evidence of flying craft? I. e. sightings of triangles, discs,

etc?


How can we best obtain authentic material evidence ofUFO's?

It is difficult to fathom how we can hope to understand any given sector of the UFO and alien scene

Sadly, too many encounters are received third-hand after being contaminated by the repetition of infor­

Unless private parties can unite, organize and turn this study ofUFOlogy into a scientific and logical

          Ex-defence minister joins search for aliens
 A new group is pushing for full disclosure about extra-terrestrials, they've enlisted a
 former Canadian cabinet minister to help make their case. JOANNASMITH-sTAFFREPORTER27


Victor Viggiani has one of the toughest jobs in the universe. The retired elementary school principal
spends his time lobbying reporters to blow a massive government cover-up wide open and reveal that
extra-terrestrials have been visiting our planet for years.


"I have no intention of convincing anybody of anything," said Viggiani, 59, director of media relations
for Exopolitics Toronto, a non-profit educational group pushing for full disclosure of the truth about off­
                                                          11
world beings. "What I do is point them to the evidence.


Exopolitics is a field of study that has moved far beyond the question of whether we are alone in the
universe. Its supporters believe there is enough evidence out there that they can state as fact that a) intel­
ligent, sentient, ethical extra-terrestrials exist; b) they have made contact; and c) they probably have
light years of lessons to teach us about sustainable energy sources and countless other matters of global
importance.


"This has nothing to do with lights in the sky," Viggiani said. 11   • • •   We are attempting to put together a
civilian diplomacy movement that will verse people in these kinds of things so that we can develop a
                                                                                                                   11
relationship with these extra-terrestrials and become part of that community. But we are not ready yet,
said Viggiani, who lives with his wife in Mississauga. "As a species, we are not ready yet."


A large part of getting ready to deal with diplomacy on such a high level involves lifting what exopoliti­
cians often refer to as the "truth embargo" on government information about the subject.


Viggiani does his part by collecting reams of documents, such as thick files he obtained from the Cana­
dian Department of National Defence under the Access to Information Act on what pilots talk about
when they are scrambled to track "non-correlated targets," also known as UFOs.


But in order to get to the point where a team of investigative reporters would be willing to dive into
what Viggiani calls a "cosmic Watergate," he faces what is arguably an even greater hurdle: "the ridi­
cule factor." Ridicule is a potent weapon that ensures newspapers actually willing to mention extra­
terrestrials in their pages usually add throwaway lines about little green men.


Viggiani believes this is part of a deliberate government plan to keep this stuff under wraps because it
inhibits potential witnesses from coming forward. In May, Exopolitics Toronto, on behalf of the Cana­
dian Exopolitics Initiative, sent a letter to the governor general of Canada that outlined much of the
documented material and requested she meet with credible experts to discuss disclosure and diplomacy.


Her secretary's office responded two weeks later, suggesting their "concerns would be best addressed by
the Canadian Space Agency and the Canadian Security Intelligence Service."


In a press kit he hands out to reporters and other media people, Viggiani called the response a "major
breakthrough in Canada's disclosure initiatives."

                      EMERGENCY SPACE WALK THRILLER

    NASA astronauts have successfully repaired a damaged solar wing at the International Space Station.
    The operation was completed Saturday, after being postponed twice so engineers could further study
    how to accomplish the risky mission, which posed the danger of electrocuting the astronaut doing the
    repairs. From Washington, VOA's Margaret Besheer has more.

    Astronaut Scott Parazynski, an experienced spacewalker, performed the repairs on the tom solar wing
    outside the International Space Station. He was assisted by Astronaut Doug Wheelock who \vas
    positioned at the base of the solar wing and offered guidance. NASA said repairing the wing was a top
    priority because the solar panel is necessary for powering equipment for future space missions.
