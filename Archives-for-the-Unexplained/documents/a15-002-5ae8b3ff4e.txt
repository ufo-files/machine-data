  Subject: Arizona UFO Sightings
  The local news (KNXV, Channel 15) in Phoenix reported a massive wave of UFO
  sightings last night (3/13/1997), with sightings of a large, black, triagular object
  with white lights at the corners. There was some pretty good videotape showing
  the lights (couldn't see the object). The sightings started in Prescott, AZ, and then
  were sequentially reported in Glendale, Phoenix, and Tucson over the course of
  40 minutes. This is about a 200 mile distance, giving an average speed of 300 MPH.
  The lights on the videotape shown appeared fairly low, and all the observers
  that spoke on the news reported it as low and moving; the tape didn't show movement
  that fast, but it could have been moving away.
  At least one commercial airline pilot reported the object enroute to landing, telling
  the ATC that it was directly above him. Phoenix radar showed nothing, and
  local FAA officials had no explaination, and the news reported that the local air
  force base (Luke AFB) didn't return calls.
  The news said local emergency officials usually get one or two UFO reports a day,
  but they logged "thousands" of calls last night.
