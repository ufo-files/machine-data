4.    Sole Source Justification:

    a.   SSPD has examined the available contractor-expertise for
evaluating the potential abilities of individuals in locating,
collecting, assessing and protecting data meeting the peculiar
mission and operational requirements of this organization.

     b.  McDannel Douglas Astronautics Company (MDAC)-Huntington
Beach has a long history of involvement with the intelligence
community involving data collection, processing and assessment, and
was selected as sole-source contractor for several reasons.  First,
MDAC has been one of the few firms seriously involved in developing
the unique technology with which this project deals, and has
managed to put together and synthesize into a cohesive package the
variety of disciplines and tools needed for this particular
evaluation project.   In addition,   several of the evaluation
instruments are proprietary with MDAC, having been developed by or
in coordination with MDAC personnel and therefore not available to
other potential contractors.  MDAC is the only corporation capable
of preparing and delivering a model evaluation package that falls
within the parameters required by this project within the necessary
time constraints.

    c.   This project continually and exhaustively monitors the
state-of-the-art in the field, as well as related disciplines.  No
other entity with the requisite resources and expertise for
satisfactory contract completion has been identified.

    d.  The fact that MDAC already has in place the necessary
security provisions for interfacing with a special access program
(SAP) would greatly expedite execution of the contract.
