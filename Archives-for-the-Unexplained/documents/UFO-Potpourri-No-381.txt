                                      STARING: A NEW HYPOTHESIS

    Aductees have consistently reported that their abductors have been right down in
  their face, staring intently with their big, dark eyes. Dr. David M. Jacobs carefully
  reports on the staring process in his book Secret Life. On page 96 he describes the
  staring procedures as follows:
                Throughout the abduction, both as a way of communicating with the
                abductee and , presumably, of examining and altering her mental and
                emotional state, the Beings stare deeply into the abductee's eyes. For
                instance, during the first moments of an abduction, often before the
                abductee has been transported to the UFO, an alien inexplicably stares
                deeply into an abductee' s eyes. When the abductee is very excited or
                frightened during the abduction, an alien stares into her eyes, calming her.
                Staring can also aleviate an abductee's pain. However, the most profoundly
                affecting of the staring procedure is Mindscan.
   On page 97, Dr. Jacobs describes Mindscan:
                Mindscan entails deep, penetrating staring into the abductee's eyes .....
                During Mindscan, the abductee lies on the table and the Taller Being stands
                next to her. He bends over and comes very close to the abductee, who may
                feel extremely frightened and threatened.
   On page 98, he describes how the abductee is overwhelmed by the staring
  techniques of the abductors:
                The Taller Being then gazes deeply into the abductee's eyes.        His black eyes,
                which lack pupils, occupy a huge amount of space on his face. They are
                spellbinding, riveting. He locks eyes with the abductee. Even though she
                tries, she cannot close her eyes or take her eyes off his. Most abductees feel
                overwhelmed by the procedure. They feel as if they are "falling into them (the
                eyes)" and achieving some sort of connection with the Taller Being. He may
                order her to "look at me!" to ensure proper contact.
   Statements about the staring process by abductees make it clear that the Taller
  Beings are in full control of the situation:
                I'm looking into those eyes. I can't believe that I'm looking into eyes that
                big........ Once you look into those eyes, you're gone. You're just gone.


                I can't think of anything but those eyes. It's like the eyes overwhelm me.
                How do they do that? It goes inside you, their eyes go inside you. You are
                just held. You can't stop looking. If you wanted to, you couldn't look away.
                You are drawn into them, and they sort of come into you....


                My eyes are open, but my mind is sort of gone. I have no will. I have no will.
                I am absorbed and I'm not fighting it.
   On page 99, Dr. Jacobs tells how the staring relates to bonding with the abductors:
        ·



                During Mindscan, the Taller Being can elicit specific emotions in the abductee,
                such as fear and terror. Often he will create an instant rush of pleasurable
                emotions in the abductee that •"bonds" her to him. As he stares deeply into
                her eyes, she may feel that the Taller Being is really a "good" individual.. ...
                Bonding can be a totally overwhelming experience.

 Our own manmade virtual reality devices are so good that the entertainment industry
is exploding with new machines and programs.                  In addition, government and industry
together are using virtual reality in training devices for situations were training on the
actual equipment is expensive or too hazardous. With virtual reality, the trainee
believes he or she is in a "real" situation, not a simulated one.


 Recognizing that some abductees seem to be having a virtual reality type of
experience rather than an actual one, I have puzzled over what kind of visual-display
technology is being used by the abductors. Throughout the whole abduction
experience, there is a noticable lack of visual display devices as we know them. The
only possible piece of "equipment" is the abductor's huge, almond-shaped dark eyes.
However, these are not reported to be "display" devices.


 Finally, however, through a report of federal research available to private industry,
printed in the August 1994 issue of Photonics Spectra, we may have the answer.
       At the University of Washington (UW), a new and different approach is aimed at developing
       a lightweight, high-resolution device that abandons the concept of projection surfaces
       and LCD screens altogether. Instead, scientists at UW's Human Interface Technology
       Laboratory want to use miniature scanners to project laser imagery directly onto the
       surface of the human retina.


       In its final embodiment, Virtual retinal display (VAO) inventors..... see the technology as the
       ultimate virtual-reality display - a device about the size of a pair of sunglasses, yet able to
       project an image that spans 120 degrees of the eye's field of view. A pair of microscanners
       would be used to project a raster-scanned video image onto the eye's retina using red, blue
       and green laser diodes. Because the exit pupil of the VRD would be coplaner with the
       entrance of the human eye, projection surfaces would not be needed and true 3-0 images
       could be created by providing each eye with a different image.
 Is this technology already being used by the abductors? Do their huge dark eyes
function as VRDs? Is that how they project the images into the abductee's head? Do
we now have some clues as to how this alien technology works? It is worth some
further consideration!l
