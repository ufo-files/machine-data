          UFO Research Australia Newsletter's {UFORAN} aim is to present

thoroughly researched material and intelligent objective theories to its many

and varied readers.



          We shall always be scientific, but shall give theories that are

logically presented, but with no proveable scientific basis, a voice - for

we recognize that imagination can be a skillful tool if constructively applied.



          Discoveries begin in the reasoning mind.    The aim of all research is

to uncover and understand, therefore, we shall on occassion speculate.



          UFORAN will always present to its readers a balanced overview of the

UFO phenomenon, as seen by those objective researchers who contribute their

considerable experience and knowledge to this magazine.



          The Newsletter must avoid the lunatic fringe where anything goes.

Unfortunately wild unfounded beliefs still abound, and whilst they doubtless

provide excitement for their followers, they are irrational, and shall definitely

never appear in UFORAN.



          We approach the illusive, often frustrating subject of UFOs with an

open and questioning mind.    We trust that we will enliven your thoughts, and

increase your knowledge of the subject when we ask and attempt to answer the

provocative question:     "What are UFOs ?"

                                     KURANDA

                       MOTORIST   ENCOUNTERS   BRIGHT   OBJECT



      It was almost one month after the following incident occurred that UFOR {fNQ}
was informed via a neighbour of the witness. When contacted, the witness, Mr K,
could not recall the exact date of the event, although he believed it had occurred
between 9 and 20 November, 1980.

      It was on a clear morning, at approximately 03.55 am that Mr K departed from
his home on the outskirts of Kuranda to drive to Cairns.

      After passing through Kuranda the road to Cairns {Kennedy Hwy. } traverses
the Kuranda Range, a winding section of some 12 km. Approximately 4 km from the top
of the Range there is a lengthy section of road widened to 3 lanes for passing.

      At about 04.15 am Mr K approached this section from the northern end and as
he came around the almost 180 degree corner onto the straight, he immediately became
aware of an extremely bright light source ahead.

      Mr K described this as "like a very bright floodlight" shining "straight down
at me."

      He was travelling at about 60 km/h at this stage and continued on down along
the road. In doing so he passed beneath and slightly to the left of the UO's
apparent position, after which his view of the "light" was ohstructed by the roof
of his vehicle.

      Curious as to what the light was Mr K was deciding to stop when another car
appeared very close behind him. According to Mr K, this other vehicle was travelling
so fast and so close that he decided not to stop because of the possibility of an
accident occurring. Before approaching this section of the road, the other car had
been following at a distance of some 200 metres, Mr K feels it is obvious that the
other driver had also seen the light and became frightened.

      Due to the speed Mr K was travelling, and the UO's apparent position, his observation
of it was probably limited to about 8 seconds. When he first saw the light it was at
an elevation of 40 degrees and at last sight almost 80 degrees above.

      Describing the UO, Mr K stated "it was just a very bright, round light, yellow­
white in colour." He estimated its size as approximately 30 cm at arms length. He
had the impression that it was emanating from beneath something, although the effects
of the light and darkness did not allow him to see anything. Strangely, although the
light seemed directed downwards Mr K could not recall it lighting up the road or the
surrounding scrub. He heard no sound nor were there any effects on his vehicle.

      An attempt to trace the other vehicle involved was not possible as Mr K
could not supply a description.

      UFO {fNQ} could find no explanation to account for Mr K's sighting, there is
no artificial lighting on this section of the road. The witness thought the UO was
below 500 ft above the roadway. There were no helicopter flights in this area at the
time of the event and other aircraft flying over the range are required to fly
at an altitude of some 6000 ft.l

eyes of both the public and the media.

      TUFOIC attempts to investigate all sighting reports to the best of our
ability, in an objective and unbias·ed manner.


      As a group,   TUFOI C draws no conclusions from its investigations , other
than the fact that an unexplained phenomena exists and requires further research
and rigorous investigation.


      The Centre is based in Hobart, with represent-3tives in various areas around

and many countries overseas, and publications are exchanged in an effort to gain a
world wide picture of the phenomena.

MURDUNNA


      Two boys aged 1 3 and 1 2 had a frightening experience whilst out shooting
during the June school holidays.  They were on a farm at Murdunna and the sighting
was reported to the Centre by their grandmother.

       The boys were in the habit of going about after dark checking their traps in a
nearby paddock and doing a bit of shooting if they spotted any animals.  It was a fine
clear evening on June 7 with the stars visible in the clear sky.  A couple of
hundred metres from the house they reached a paddock sloping gently uphill from the
bottom of the small valley.

       The boys slowly made their way up the paddock to see what could be found in the
bush at the top.  At this stage they noticed a white starlike light much bigger than
Venus (it was not Venus).  The light was at 20 degrees elevation to the west.  It did
not move.   They tried to rationalize what it might be, plane, reflected light etc. ,
but entering the bush once more the boys soon lost interest in the light.

were squarish in shape and 30 cm or more across·. Both fi·gures ai·mless'ly circled each
other across the paddock.


       The boys now noticed a silver 3/4 moon-sized ball 30 metres away and approximately

       One boy hid behind a bush, the other waited for him to shine the torch so he
would not stand on any of the traps.  A few seconds later the ball object grew in
size as if moving closer, the boys decided to make a run for it.


       Duration of the sighting is estimated at one minute.
