BASINGSTOKE FLAP CONTINUES
UFOs continue to be seen in and around Basingstoke. Interest in the area
is unusually high and this has resulted in some interesting multi-witness
reports.
Reasons for a flap are always difficult to determine. A common school of
thought is that UFO sightings increase proportionally to the amount of
publicity given to the subject. In this case, both Sigap and UFOs in general,
have received more than their usual share of attention. Chairman Omar Fowler
has _ already given two Thames Valley Radio interviews, as well as a spot
on Southern News (BBC) television based at Southampton. These interviews alone
have generated a number of reports - add to these, the series o£ UFO articles
in the new 'Star' newspaper, based in the Camberley/F~rnborough/Aldershot area,
the number of reports increases still further.
But whatever the reasons, one fact remains. UFOs continue to be observed,
by all sorts o£ people, in all sorts of circumstances. Publicity about UFOs,
whether good or bad, nearly always unearthes a hitherto unknown report. And the
more reports we have to go on, the nearer we shall surely be to establishing
the nature of the phenomenon.
