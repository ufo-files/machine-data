                              2nd Quarter 2000, April-June


Donations                                                                        . _


      The Fund continues to receive fairly regular donations, both large and small,
from a wide variety of individuals.   Two special purpose large donations have
been received--one to help establish a Fund office and the other for the production
of a monthly press newsletter.


Press Newsletter
       A newsletter for the press, The UFO Report is designed to provide the world's
press current, dependable, and newsworthy information and background on the UFO
subject. It will be issued monthly free of charge to legitimate news organizations and
reporters. At least two newspapers in each state will receive he newsletter along with
major media syndicates, NASA centers and Air Force Bases. About 650 copies of the
first issue (May) were mailed and presented a summary of the COMET A Report
wherein French generals and scientists support the reality of the UFO phenomena. A
second issue (June) was mailed at the end of June and presented some historical
perspectives and the Fund's position on the alien spacecraft hypothesis.


Potential Future Funding
      Members of the Coalition (C UFOS, F UFOR, and M UFO N) will travel to Denver
in June to meet with Larry Lemke, who represents Joe Firmage's interests in UFO
matters. The purpose of the meeting is to discuss possible UFO research projects
that Firmage might be interested in funding.

News Items
      Dewey Fournet, an Air Force Major in the Pentagon who served as liaison with
the official UFO project at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base and who later served on the
NICAP board of Directors, died May 3, 2000.        His UFO material will go to Tom Tulien.

newsletter, in order to take a new job involving a media project.
