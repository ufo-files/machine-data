HOTLINE CASES

The Gribble NUFORC Collection consists of dozens of reel to reel tapes, each containing 12
hours of recordings, hundreds of cassettes and thousands of pages of documentation.
However, Bob would record calls and not enter them into the Call Log, or Case Files, which
is normal across the human spectrum. These witness reports he referred to as his Hotline
cases. Unfortunately, some of the recordings have no date given and in some cases, not
even the year.
