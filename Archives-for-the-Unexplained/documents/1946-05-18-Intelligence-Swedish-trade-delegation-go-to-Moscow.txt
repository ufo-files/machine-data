May 18: Swedish trade agreement delegation to Moscow

The Swedish government appointed a seven-headed delegation, chaired by ambassador Rolf Sohlman, to negotiate with the Soviet Union on a trade agreement. On July 24, the delegation flew to Moscow to start negotiations. 

Source: Helsingborgs Dagblad, May 19. Ingemar Hägglöf: Berätta för Joen.
