June 21: Speculation from Britain

”FLYING BOMBS OVER SWEDEN? London, June 21 (NTB [Norwegian news agency] from Reuters). People in Sweden think they have seen radio-controlled flying bombs passing regularly over their country the last few weeks, reports the Daily Mail special correspondent in Stockholm. Just one explosion has been reported [the unsubstantiated Newsweek report on June 17..?] but one has not been able to find any traces after the bomb. People are convinced that the bombs are Russian and that they are shot from the Baltic coast. A strange rocket bomb has also been seen over Finland. [The June 9 meteor]  The Finns say it is powered by a series of explosions and have a long ”fire-tail” after.” 

This Reuters message, published by Norwegian papers, was sent by the Swedish attaché in Oslo to his colleagues at the Defence Staff in Stockholm. It is dated as having arrived on ”26/4” (April 26) which probably should be ”26/6” (June 26).

Sources: Norwegian newspapers such as Vårt Land, Buskerud Blad and Fremtiden, June 21. Credit: Defence Staff archives and Ole Jonny Braenne.
