    EDITORIAL

                   Many readers have written us to suggest that we
                   do a column on predictions. We have debated this
                   among ourselves, and have come to the conclusion
                   that predictions, as it is in most cases, are
                   misleading.
                     Now, before we start getting mail telling us how
                   wrong we are, or telling us that psychics have
                   been making predictions for years, I would like to
                   add that we have not arrived at our conclusion
                   without research.
                      The report comes from a very reliable young lady, Lee Walsh, Our southwest
                   editor, researcher, and reporter who is very involved in all areas of
                   parapsychology. Lee has been doing some checking on psychics and their
                   revelations, and has come up with the following predictions for 1976 which did
                   not happen; all made by so-called "leading psychics"
                      All of the notables who were to marry in 1976, Dinah Shore, Lady Bird
                   Johnson, and Burt Reynolds, to mention a few, did not. H the space probes to
                   Mars revealed any evidence of ancient alien civilizations and UFO landings, its
                   being kept quiet. Many accidents of various nature were forcast but...no Air
                   Force jet crashed over Arizona in October due to UFOs; no nuclear submarine
                   vanished beneath the Artie ice caps; a 747 did not crash in October killing
                   several politicians and movie stars, and, Prince Charles was not lost at sea.
                         H you are still with me, here are more predictions which did not happen.
                   Charles Manson was to escape from prison; a message to earth from outer
                   space aliens was to come during this period, and as to various finesses,
                   retirements and assasination attempts, none came to pass.
                      Predictions and more predictions which did not happen; Jacques Cousteau
                   did not discover Atlantis and, a massive earthquake, that was to tear
                   California's mountain ranges, never happened.
                      Of the many hundreds of predictions that never came about, one did: Jimmy
                   Carter was elected president, as many person who are not psychic could have
                   guessed.
                      Jean Dixon who is billed as "the world's most phenomenal seer" saw Ford as
                   our next president.
                      How did the psychic shape up in their predictions? 98% wrong.
                      It is the nature of man to desire a view of the future, but until some true
                   psychics approach us, we will leave the predictions to the sensationalistic
                   tabloids.
                      It seem that the federal government as well as Beyond Reality, is looking for
                   true psychics. According to a report from Associated Press, the government is
                   spending 10 million dollars in 1977 in order to see whether scientists can predict
                   earthquakes. A portion of this money has been set aside for research of
                   psychics and housewifes who claim that they have the ability to see the future.
                      Roger Hunter, the geophysicist in charge of the program is keeping a file on
                   persons with psychic ability. He stores the data on a computer, and checks the
                   claims against actual earthquakes. One close hit came from a woman who
                   predicted an explosion in Asia. On the day of the prediction, China detonated a
                   nuclear test weapon. So, it seems that the girl next door can make more
                   accurate predictions than some of the super-psychics.
                      We will be happy to check your premonitions and predictions, just send it to
                   us with a self-addressed, stamped envelope.

                                                                                         Sincerely
                                                                                       Harry Belil
