                  SUBJECT:                         Attendance at Scientific Seminar in Support of GONDOLA WISH (U)


                  1. (S) On 29 Jul 78, the undersigned attended an educational seminar
                  entitled Parapsychological Research on Out-of-the-Body Experiences and
                  Strengthening ESP Abilities conducted by Charles T. Tart, Ph.D. Dr. Tart
                  is an internationally respected authority in the field of parapsychology.
                  He has recently served as President of the Parapsychological Association,
                  the international professional organization of scientific investigators·
                  of psi. He is a professor of psychology at the Davis campus of the
                  University of California. He is currently on a one year leave of absence
                  from Davis and is serving as a consultant to Stanford Research Institute,
                  Menlo Park, CA, in the field of parapsychology.
                  2. (S) The seminar was an oral review of Dr. Tarts latest book, PSI
                  Scientific Studies of the Psychic Realm, E. P. Dutton, New York,-r977.
                  The undersigned purchased several copies of this book {for resale to
                  interested parties) as it is not yet available in nook sto~es on the East
                  coast. The seminar was conducted from 1000 hours to 1600 hours with a
                  90 minute lunch break from 1200 hours to 1330 hours. The morning
                  session involved an introduction into the scientific examination of ESP.
                  Dr. Tart followed exactly chapter one of his book. During the morning
                  session Dr. Tart also spent a short period of time explaining the
                  extinction principle which is common in routine ESP testing. Dr. Tart
                  has invented a machine for testing and teaching conventional ESP guessing.
                  The particulars of this device are well documented in psi literature.
                  Basically, the machine provides immediate reinforcement to the subject
                  as to the accuracy of his ESP guess, thereby defeating the extinction
                  affect. In the long run, subjects demonstrate a learning curve (they
                  improve in there ESP guessing ability). The afternoon hours of the
                  seminar were dedicated exclusively to the discussion of Out-of-the-Body
                  Experiences (OOBE). The subject matter covered was exactly as stated
                  in chapter nine of his book. The entire afternoon session was recorded
                  by the undersigned. The tape recording is available for review upon re-
                  quest and lasts approximately four hours. It is of interest to note
                  here that Dr. Tart•s experiments in OOBE were conducted 12-13 years ago
                  and therefore do not reflect the current state-of-the-art technology.
                  His contributions are however scientifically valu~ble.
                  3. (S) Of particular interest were the questions posed by the seminar

attendees. The attendees expressed there concern over what they believed
to be a threat from Soviet efforts in parapsychology. They asked Dr. Tart
to comment on this matter. Dr. Tart expressed the opinion that the Soviets
were far ahead of us in parapsychology research and that funding for
their efforts came from the Soviet military. Dr. Tart also stated that

4. (S) After the seminar the undersigned had the opportunity to talk with
Dr. Tart privately. The undersigned casually questioned Dr. Tart as to
how common it was for his audiences to ask him about the Soviet para-
psychology effort. Dr. Tart stated that the question always comes up.
The undersigned also asked Dr. Tart if he had ever been contacted by the
Soviets. His response was very evasive (he was and is not aware of the
official capacity of the undersignedJ but he did state that he had
received requests for copies of his scientific studies in parapsychology
from the Soviets. (no further information available)
5. (S) No classified or sensitive information was disclosed during the
seminar. Dr. Tart stated that he did not have access to classified
information when the seminar attendees asked him what was real~ly going
