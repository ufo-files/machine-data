•   abducted by mermen: In Bilk   # 45, I listed two mermen related abductions
and pregnancies and compared them to the modern UFO-abduction reports,. Fortean
Times 60, p.8, has a story on abductions of ladies by mermen in Swaziland
which closely agree with the old European stories (for more African mermaid
sightings, see Bilk 33). To complete this merman/mermaid/human/seducion/
pregnancy/abduction motif, here are the relevant stories from Brasil:           The
fishermen in the Amazon believe dolphins can transform into humans. As women,
they are called chicas preciosas, beautiful girls, as men, they hid their two
spouting holes at the forehead under a hat. They like to go to a dance and
the   dolphin males, especially, to     sleep with human women.        One woman at
Bria Grande, Rio Negro,    was pregnant from a dolphin. They child she gave
birth to had a dolphins head sans beak, and two spouting holes. An informant
og Jacques Cousteau, who collected these story (told on German TV, Hessen 3,
July 26, 1985) said as belief in these transformations is dying out, dolphins
no longer turn into girls and young guys.        At Pucallpa, Peru,Cousteau met an
old fisherman, who, while cooking, was visited by a dolphin lady turned into
young girl. She was very beautiful, the man said, and wore only slip and bra.
She looked the fisherman into the eyes, bethe withstood her seducion, although
he would have liked to sleep with her. He was tooafraid she might be a witch.
The next time she came, he told Custeau, he would go with her. He also confirmed
that girld unexpectedly pregnant believed they had been raped by a dolphin.
Indeed, his mother suffered, during one pregnancy, from strong pains aNd went
to the tribal doctor who gave her herbs.     This aborted the fetus which resembled
a fish - a grey dolphin. The informant added that in former times it was usual to
note in birthcertificates that the daddy had been a dolphin. A functionalist
analysis of these stories might stress that the embryo resembles a fish anyway
and that this might have started the legends. However, all kinds of marine
creatures in many areas of the world (dolphins in Sth America/mermaids in Af­
rica and Europe/seals and selkies in Scotland/sharks in the South Seas) have
in common that they can transform into beautiful girls and men, either lure
men into their wet death or rape girls and abduct them for sexual purposes.
This is a complkex area of neo-cryptozoology (which deals not with biological
but mythical creatures) and is nearly identical with UFO abduction stories.
As a last, but not final, piece to this jigsaw puzzle, I recall the story in
Bilk ea.    2 years ago about the Phillipine woman who gave birth to a fish (this
was also in Fortean Times).
