                                                          Used Assortment
  One of the best travel series

  15 titles below ($4.00 to $19.95) including (in alphabetical order): Arizona Handbook * Baja Handbook * Idaho Handbook * Micronesia
  Handbook * Montana Handbook * Nepal Handbook * Nevada Handbook * New Mexico Handbook * Southeast Asia Handbook * Utah Handbook
  * Utah Handbook * Vietnam, Cambodia and Laos Handbook

  Moon Publications offers one of the best series of practical travel guides for intelligent travellers. Their style is very
  similar to Lonely Planet guides. Each guide includes extensive sections on flora, fauna, geography, and culture, as well
  as all the practical information needed for economical travel. Each guide includes color photo inserts and many maps, as
  well as a lot of dense text for the money.

  We offer this selection of used copies, usually one edition back from the current edition. New copies normally retain for
  between $14 and $23, but we are offering these for $4-6. Since all have been updated within the last ten years and the
  descriptions tend to be general, these copies are certain current enought for general planning.

  Many of these copies are former library books (flb). Also see our main Moon page for new copies we carry for our areas
  of interest (Southwestern U.S., Hawaii, etc.). Those new copies are also listed below.
