STRANGE BUT TRUE   !

Recently, I began reading a book written by an American Ufologist about her
experiences with aliens at her home in New York City. I was very surprised to
discover that when she sought the help of OMNI Magazine for a chapter in her
book, she was told by a former employee of the magazine that the Management DO
NOT BELIEVE IN UFOs in spite of the fact that they give superb saturation
coverage to the UFO Phenomenon. Apparently, they publish UFO material for
commercial reasons, just like any other magazine, except that to them it is
strictly business decision, having little whatsoever to do with journalism an
scientific research. What a let down! especially as OMNI is the only American
Science magazine so far that continues to publish serious articles on UFOs and
other related phenomema. However, in spite of this revelation, it should not
put any one off buying it as we in IUFOPRA, still maintain that it is the best
American Science magazine on the market, which gives in-depth coverage to the
UFO phenomenon. Still, you can't help but wonder what Omni's Management are
really thinking when they read and publish all those wonderful articles on
Ufology.

TOP SECRET FILM:


IUFOPRA has learned that a top secret film allegedly showing Dead aliens will
be screened for the first time anywhere in the world, this Summer, and in
England at a UFO Conference at Sheffield Hallam University in August.
The 16mm film will also form the basis of a 9 1 minute documentary which will
be shown by the BBC in August of this year. This film has been classified
as"TOP SECRET"for almost fifty years. It was allegedly shot by U.S.Government
Officials in the New Mexico Desert where a flying saucer was supposed to have
crashed in 1947. Air Force Officials immediately sealed off the crash site,
and the film, which was shot by Military Camera Personnel was immediately
classified. Many Ufologists claim that the truth has been covered up ever
since.

This silent black and white film shot on 14 reels, was copied by a former
Military Camerman, now 82 years old, and still living in America. He has
always believed, and even tn this day, that the cover-up was wrong and that
the evidence should be publicly examined.

The camera man in turn, passed this film on to a British TV Documentary film
maker Ray Santilli, who was in America at the time to do a documentary on
Elvis Presley. This film is alleged to show Scientists examining a creature
laid out on a slab inside a tent at the Crash Site, gaurded by Military
Police. Scientists are seen to carry out an autopsy on the Alien body and
examining tissue samples. It also shows part of the Space Ship wreckage -
allegedly made from material that could not be burned, dented or cut. Experts
believe the coverup was intended to give  Scientists the opportunity to try
and copy the engineering.
This film has been checked by the Kodak Film Company and they have confirmed
that it ia fifty years old. IUFOPRA hopes to receive further information on
this new development and we will keep you informed when we do.

  SERIOUSLY.
  =======================================================================


  (1) Do not approach the landea UFO within 10 feet and try to remain
  under cover if possible.


   (2)   Never touch the UFO.   This could be very dangerous.


  (3) If you are alone, look for another possible witness, to verify
  your sighting, but do not spend too much time on fruitless searching
  for other people.


  (4) Try not to interfere with the activities of a UFO occupant. It m ay
  be hostile or indifferent to your fragility. If it shows signs of wishing to
  communicate, comply as best you can. Try not to allow your natural panic to
  prevent you from doing so     .


  (5) Try to memorize as much as you can about the UFO and what it does

  (6) Try to be aware of the time throughout the incident.         This is most
  important.


  (7) If you have a camera with you, try to take as many photographs as
  you can. Also, try to photograph landmarks, preferably in the same frame as
  the UFO for comparison  purposes. Photograph the occupant if they do not

  it was there and make special note of them so that they can be found again.
