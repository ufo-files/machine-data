       Things are rather quiet again in Tassie after a good
run of sightings mid 1988 to 89. So far we have only two cases
that remain unidentified, in fact we have to go back nine
months to our last unexplained case in 1989. Australia wide
the story at time of writing doesn't seem any different, a
number of cases with lights but not much else. A video film
of an early morning light taken in Can berra on February 17th,
turned out to probably be Venus rising in the east.
       Overseas in the past nine months has not been so dull,
landings and alien beings were reported in the media from
Russia. What the media didn't tell us was that there had been
a run of sightings in the USSR, but such is the power of the
media. Sightings also came from Hungary and recently out of
Belgium. In the Belgian event a triangular set of lights have
been reported at low level, we even got to see a video of these
lights in a news broadcast. I suppose the most frustrating part
of the UF O phenomena is that despite embarrasing masses of wel�
investigated and unexplained events science is just not aroused
into looking into the phenomena.
                          r..iagra Sighting.
       We received a call on February 14th from a Magra witness,
she had just seen something crossing her northern sky and moving
away towards the north north east. Immediate checks were possible
with the Airport and Weather Bureau. No aircraft were in the
area at the time of the event at 10.45 am. The weather was fine
and calm with a brilliant blue sky, the overlying wind pattern
was from the north east to south east, nearly opposite to the
direction of travel of the object seen.
       Mrs M had been outside hosing down the windows of her rural
home, meanwhile her three year old son was playing on the lawn
nearby when he started calling her to look at that thing in the
sky. Sh� did so and saw a brilliant white.round shape against the
blue sky. The object was moving across her northern sky and as
it did so it seemed to change shape or wobble to present a disc
like appearance. It may have also zig zagged in its flight path
to the north east. At one point it stopped for a few seconds,
then gained elevation and was lost to sight in the far distance.
The sighting was mentioned in the local paper but no other
witnesses came forward to report the object.
