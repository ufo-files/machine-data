Rochester, Minn. Visited by "Truck Size" UFO   ( 2/19/74 )
   A UFO "bigger than· a wrecker truck" and spewing flames buzzed
northwest Rochester, Minn. before disappearing, of all places, near
the old city dumping area. Among those persons reporting the strange
object was a Rochester policeman and a service station operator, UFO
MAGAZINE has learned.
   Patrolman James Preiss, driving a squad car, and James Kuhlman,
who runs a Clark service station and who was driving his tow truck,
pursued the UFO along Elton Hills streets until losing sight of it
as it neared the old city dump.
   "I was really terrified when I first saw it," Kuhlman said. He
said that it was about lla25 P.M. when he was driving past the Hill-
crest Shopping Center when he saw the object in the sky, "flying at
about 100 feet, towards me." Kuhlman said that he then pulled his car
into the shopping center and parked. "It scared the hell out of me," he
said.
   Kuhlman described the UFO as bigger than a wrecker truck with a
"kind of white dome shell on top." He said it seemed to be powered
by what looked like several small afterburners flaming toward the
ground from the bottom of the object.
   "It cruised slowly right over my head in a northeasterly direction,"
said Kuhlman, who gave chase in his truck. He was joined in the pursuit
by patrolman Preiss! who spotted it as he drove north on u.s. 52. He
turned east at the 9th Street ftW exit to follow the object into Elton
Kills.
   "Whatever it was, it was seen to have flames coming from it,"Preiss
reported.
   Kuhlman, who has a citizens' band radio in his truck, said he heard
chatter on his radio from Elton Hills residents who were talking about
the strange object. Kuhlman stated that he and Preiss stopped to com-
pare notes about the UFO to make sure they had seen the same thing.
A second police car was sent to help search for the UFO, but it had
vanished.
   The control tower at Rochester Municipal Airport said nothing strange
was seen in the sky at the time, and that no radar contact was made.

      Terror on a Nevada Desert Road    ( 2/14/74 )
         As we were in the process of preparing this issue for the press,
      we were surprised to read the following account which was sent to us
      by a correspondent in California. The letter was published in the Santa
      Anna Register of March 27, 1974. Commentary is by Robert Klinn •••
         "1 read an account of a UFO sighting in the Registe; on l-Iarch 13,
      1974. It was similar to a sighting that my brother and 1 had in the
      Nevada desert on February 14, 1974. We left Buhl, Idaho with a load
      of furniture in a 12-foot U-Haul truck.
        "Later on in the desert' outside of McGill, Nevada we were driving
      when my brother gestured and told me to look. 1 looked out of the left
      side of the truck and observed a round, orange object that appeared
      tu be 10 feet off the desert floor. 1 couldn't tell how big it was or
      how far away it was.
          "I then looked out the right side of the cab and saw three more
      smaller objects that were higher up and were blue. One of them was
      flickering off and on1 the other two remained constant.
         "My brother said the orange object had been on the right side of
      the truck and had passed us and crossed over the road in front of us
      and had come up to the left side of the cab.
         "We were moving a t about 55 miles-per-hour, and at that moment
      it felt like we had be en hit by a blast of wind or force field. The
      lights on the truck flickered on and off and the engine started to
      miss.
         "Then he lost control of the truck and couldn't steer it. 1 told
      him to stop, and then before we could stop, the transmission· selector

      middle of the road. Up to that point 1 wasn 1 t very scared yet.
