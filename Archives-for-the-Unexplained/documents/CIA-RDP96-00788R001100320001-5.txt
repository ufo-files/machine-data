   MEMORANDUM FOR ADCSOPS-HUMINT
   SUBJECT: Information Briefing for Senator Clairborne Pell



   1. (FOUO) On 29 September 1982, from 1700-1800 hours, a GRILL FLAME information
   briefing was presented to The Honorable Clairborne Pell, Senator, Rhode Island
   in his office at US Capitol. Briefing was arranged by Dr. Jack Varona,, Assistant
   Vice Director for Scientific & Technical Intelligence, and concurred in by
   CG, INSCOM and ACSI, DA. Although Senator Pell had invited 8 members of the
   Senate Select Committee on Intelligence (SSCI) to attend the briefing none were
   present. Attendees were:
