Rocket-like object over Dalarna

Time: May 21, between 11.00 and 12.00 hours
Place: Between Dala-Floda and Mockfjärd in the county of Dalarna

In August 1979, Sweden´s largest newspaper Expressen ran a four-page article on the UFO mystery. The newspaper reprinted UFO-Sweden´s questionnaire and invited people to send their written reports to the organizations report centre. One of the sightings received is, if correct, one of the earliest cases of a typical missile-type ”ghost rocket”. The case was never reported to the military in 1946, however. [In any case, it is not to be found in the Defence Staff files.] 

The questionnaire was filled out by Oskar Karlsson, a 58-year old [in 1979] construction worker living at Snöåbyn near Dala-Järna. The questionnaire is accompanied by a short letter that describes the incident in this way: 

”I was riding my cycle between Dala-Järna and Falun on the day in question, May 21, 1946. I had a errand to Falun. Instead of taking the bus I went by bike, which was my vehicle in those years... As I started off, equipped with a few sandwiches, I rested between Dala-Floda and Mockfjärd, 36 kilometres from my home. I paused at a small clearing near riks-71 [national road 71]. I had been sitting there for a while when I heard a hissing sound above me. I looked around but not a needle or a leaf was moving [there was no wind]. I then looked up [into the sky] and I caught sight of this object. For a very short time, two seconds, did I note the object. What confuses me is that I heard the noise before I could see the object. Maybe something passed over me before I looked up and could see the object?

”Before arriving in Falun I met a friend whom I told about what I had seen. I had thoughts of going to the Falu-Kuriren editorial office and tell them about what I had seen, but this I never did. I was in a hurry to go home, since the next day I was to start a [new] work for the railway company. There I also told [my new work-mates] about what I had seen, but noone said anything. Maybe they thought they had gotten a strange new mate. On the next Saturday I got married. This is what the object looked like. 





Oskar Karlssons teckning.





”On my honour.”

From the questionnaire, signed by Oskar Karlsson on August 9, 1979, the following data can be extracted: Mr. Karlsson was alone to see the phenomenon. He saw the object at about 11.00-12.00 hours on Tuesday, May 21, 1946. It was ”not a cloud in sky, sunshine, no wind at all”. The object was darker than the sky. The witness heard a hissing, like an artíllery....[word unreadable]. The object crossed the sky going from the north to the south, very quickly. To the question, ”What is your personal opinion about the phenomenon?”, Karlsson wrote: ”That it was some experiment from Soviet or the US, like a V2-rocket.”  

In view of the time that has passed between sighting and report [33 years] no effort was made, in 1979, to acquire more data. 

Status: Unidentified
Source: Questionnaire and letter, August 9, 1979.
