                                      Santa Ana Lecturer

     An astronomy instructor at Santa Ana College, Calif., and director of Tessman
Planetarium, Ronald Smith, gave a lecture at the college entitled "Communicating With
UFOs," stated the July 23, 1972, edition of the Garden Grove, Calif., Evening News.
     Smith said he believes UFOs from outer space have been observing earth for some
time, but "the major problem has been friendly contact and communication with them"  ·


due to the great distances in space.
     "You could send a message to outer space," he stated, "and you might not get an
answer for 200,000 years."
     Space travel and communication at the speed of light in the future is a possi­
bility, however, he added. On the basis of this, Smith observed, "the thousands of
stories reporting flying saucers cannot be discounted as fiction."
