 LAWSUIT DEPARTMENT:
        FLASH! As if it wasn't bad enough having Bill Knell (an obscure Long
 Island researcher) on our backs, we have now received another threatening
 letter from a Kansas City lawyer who purports to represent James Randi. This
 same lawyer wrote us on Oct. 3 0th and Nov. 8th, 1991, demanding that we send
 $10, 000 within about ten days in order to avoid being sued for our series of
 "Smear" articles about the Randi/Geller lawsuits. Now, in this April lOth,
 199 2 letter, the same lawyer demands that the sum of $2 0, 000 be sent within
 10 days. We will not submit to legal blackmail!
        Although we continue to send "Smear" to Randi, he and his lawyer ap­
 pear to be unaware that in the Dec. lst, 1991 issue of our zine, we printed
 as much as we dared of Randi's statement in which he attempted to respond
 to remarks we had made in the two previous issues. We did not print all of
 Randi's statement, because there were allegations therein which appeared to
 be libelous. Unlike Randi, we are very cautious in regard to possible li­
 bel and slander.
        The Kansas City lawyer insists that his letters to us are copyrighted
 and cannot be reproduced. Because of this, our lawyer Henry Morgenstern
 replied to Randi's lawyer in part as follows:
        "Even if any of the things in your recent letter needed to be promul­
 gated, which we do not admit, we would be haunted by your admonition that
 we are forbidden to repeat any of them. You seem to both demand that these
 things be told, and then order that they be kept secret, in the same letter.
        "Perhaps your client should simply allow the press to be free. His own
 protestations seem to be bringing more attention to his situation than any­
 thing else."
 YET ANOTHER GULF BREEZE UPDATE:
       Although the Gulf Breeze (Florida) sightings by Ed Walters ("Mr. Ed")
 ended in May of 1988, the controversy about his photographs goes on and on.
 Amazingly, a hard core of debunkers on the one side, and Believers on the
 other side, continue to thrash out the details of the case after all   this
 time, with no final solution in sight!
       Rex Salisberry, once a rising star in Florida MUFON, was chosen by In­
 ternational Director Walt Andrus to re-evaluate certain aspects of the Wal­
 ters case. This was about two years ago, when a model resembling Walters'
 craft was found in the house that Walters had been living in at the time of
 his UFO sightings. Shortly after that, a (now former) teenager named Tommy
                                   (2)
Smith, who was a friend of Ed and Ed's son, gave a damning statement by
telephone to a press conference consisting of reporters and Gulf Breeze town
officials. In this statement Smith gave details about how some of the photo­
graphs had allegedly been faked.
      According to MUFON, Salisberry's role was intended to be simply a re­
investigation of the above two aspects of the Walters case. However, Salis­
berry went further, and worst of all, he lost his Faith in the Walters sto­
ry. Now Salisberry has joined the debunkers, and the MUFON hard core     is
furious at him.
      As a separate matter, one William Hyzer was contracted by MUFON to do
a photographic re-analysis of the Walters pictures - to add to those already
done by physicist Bruce Maccabee and otherso Unfortunately, Hyzer came to
cautious but basically negative conclusions. Walt Andrus dismisses Hyzer's
findings as "his own personal opinion", and Walt sounds (in a phone inter­
view) as if the MUFON UFO Journal is not likely to print much if anything of
Hyzer's work. Nor, for that matter, does he sound likely to print much if
anything of Salisberry's work.
      Says Salisberry of Hyzer's analysis: "We find his work a definitive
and compelling argument that the (Walters) photos are hoaxed, although Mr.
Hyzer only offers his conclusion in regard to Photo 19". Photo 19 is the
famous "road shot", which has generated more controversy than any of   the
others. # 19 is even on a MUFON T-shirt these days!
      While all these "negative" things are going on, the Gulf Breeze/Pensa­
cola MUFON hard core are more active than ever. For many months they have
been going out almost every evening to watch for UFOs. Their efforts     are
rewarded once or twice a week on the average, and over a period of time, the
sightings seem to be getting closer and more detailed. Endless still photos
& video tapes, both amateur and professional, have been shot.  There   are
even rumors of successful experiments at communication with the craft. There
have been, however, no contacts or abductions as yet.
      There has also been a pro-Walters re-evaluation of the UFO model and
Tommy Smith problems, done by Gary Watson, who is MUFON's Chief Investigator
for the Pensacola area. This report has recently been submitted to Andrus
and apparently will be printed, at least in part, in the MUFON UFO Journal.
      There are many people who believe that the continuing sightings at
Gulf Breeze add credence to Ed Walters' photographs, especially as the ob­
jects being seen now are very similar (though not necessarily identical) .
Although it is tempting for a UFO Believer to feel this way, the fact is
that Walters was alone or with his family members when he took his pictures,
whereas these more recent ones are taken publicly.
      We at "Smear" remain skeptical of Walters' photos and at the   same
time intrigued by what is going on at Gulf Breeze currently. We remind
our readers that "Smear" has strongly defended Ed Walters on specific points
against unfair attacks by debunkers Willy Smith and Phil Klass. Smith even
threatened to sue us at one time. Phil Klass and your (former) editor dis­
agree on whether or not he too once threatened to sue us.
      In a few days we expect to make our fourth trip to Gulf Breeze and we
look forward to joining the hard core for a night or two of ufological mer­
riment. As to what is being seen, Salisberry believes it may well be a
flare, whereas the other side insists that this possibility has been elimin­
ated.
      To us, the most damaging argument against the authenticity of the Wal­
ters photos is the above-mentioned testimony of Tommy Smith at the press con­
ference. Smith, according to Salisberry, has passed two lie detector (i.e.,
voice stress) tests on that testimony. To us, there is no obvious reason
why Smith would be lying. But we have never met or interviewed him, and can
make no final conclusion about his reliability.
      Our next issue will contain a report on our forthcoming expedition to
Gulf Breeze. Stay tuned!
