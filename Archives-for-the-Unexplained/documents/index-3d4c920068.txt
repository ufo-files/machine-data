    Our Review                                                                                  Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

   The veracity of the Lazar claims aside, this is still a neat poster. It shows three schematic views of Lazar's
   "Sport Model" flying saucer found at the legendary "Area S-4." Includes cutaways and explanations of
   saucer components and functioning. In the background is a Russian satellite image of the Papoose Lake
   area -- an added bonus. Interestingly, the image fails to show any features that support Lazar's story --
   except for an ambiguous "bubble" hovering over Papoose Lake! (It could be a flying saucer, or an artifact
   of the imaging. You might have to locate a Russian satellite imagery expert to find out.) It is an attractive
   poster by the Lazar/Huff conglomerate that really ought to be on Agent Mulder's wall. -- Glenn Campbell
