    two years, the local FT activity has shown a prominent increase towards the end of April and

    Bretby village being a particular point of focus.

    May I st. (Location details are given in this issue.)

                                   OPEN SKIES:OPEN MINDS

                                       By Melanie Cunningham

"Truth is stranger than fiction" and "there are more things in Heaven and Earth...."

These are familiar words, which could not be more poignant, since we prepare ourselves to
live in the twenty-first century as more enlightened men and women.

Enlightened is said with optimism, in the hope that we can become more evolved spiritually to
an elevated awareness, which makes us open-minded about ourselves and our place in the
Universe(s).

Science, as we know it, could be described as a type of religion, in that it holds some truths
within its measurable principles, but also some distortions, manufactured by limited perception
and dogma. A grand paradox of the disciples of the science-mindset, is their own philosophy
purporting to work according to rational thought and provable formulas, conveniently
available for 'sums' and statistics.

The same scientists quickly forget that they live mainly as emotionaVspiritual beings, who over
centuries, change their so-called theories like the weather. Theories are discarded, replaced or
modified.      Scientific papers/articles written by 'rational' disciples are often worded with
phrases like 'Scientists think ....' , Scientists theorise ......'.   This language, clothed in all its
subjectivity, contradicts the logical fact-based bedrock of science.          Belief is not necessarily
fact.

To modern science, words like 'God' or 'paranormal' are bordering on blasphemy and grounds
for excommunication!       Yet, these same disciples, who respond with chronic disapproval at
such ideas, willingly, if not blindly, exist permanently in a world of stupendous unseen love
and intelligence. They do not realise that they spend most or all their lives taking part in love
relationships of one kind or another. This love is as real as the telescope that picks out the
majesty of the night sky, or the microscope that reveals the beauty of normally invisible life­
forms in a drop of sea water.

Has no one told them that love is supernatural?            It can never be detected with the
inappropriate tools of science. Can steam be measured with a ruler? Love exists at all times
outside our five senses. Science chooses to ignore this, along with anything else breaking the
'known' laws of physics and so on.

This is why science has a mega problem with UFO's -- these objects often defy 'their' laws of
motion and gravity etc.   Anything that cannot be imprisoned within their compact science­
Pandora's Bo� assumed by them, is rejected without consideration of any alternative reality.

Science itself is man-made, quite literally. It has developed to be the false idol prostituting our
spiritual, personal lives. What we perceive in terms of matter around us, is only the tip of the
iceberg of a much larger reality. The evidence of our diverse human experiences, inform
humankind that there exists other beings in different or higher dimensions.

UFO's must be part of this larger reality, stereotypes stifle truth, regardless of the subject.
Ufology is no exception. The most common stereotype of a Ufologist, is perceived as a lone
crackpot, stating that UFO's are 'flying saucers' from Mars or similar. If he is not saying that,
he is preoccupied with making models on string, to fool the public.

Fraud is committed in every area of life.. .look at the media and politicians! Fake money can be
produced . The real thing also exists, the same with gold, therapists and documents etc.

Ufology actually embraces all the sciences. Several theories are being put forward in a serious
attempt to explain these unidentified objects seen in our skies. Our human minds, which
cannot know everything, are trying to make sense of the mystery. At present, that is what
UFO's are: unidentified, according to our limited knowledge.

IT must be remembered that it is only recently that we have become students of Space and
DNA. We are so ignorant, not to mention arrogant, about life, that we do not even
understand our creation in our mother's womb. Biologists do not know how the details of
differentiation, concerning the individual cells of the tiny embryo.

Science is uninformed about the mystery of life, death and sleep.

Perhaps we are barking up the wrong tree in trying to categorise UFO's, i.e., who they are
and where they originate. It might be healthier for us to look out of our materialistic, self­
imposed bubble and recognise there is 'other-ness' out there, everywhere.



                            THE NOTTINGHAM 'BOGGART'

                                  (Encountered by Frank Earp)

In 1966, when I was fifteen, I was out with two friends UFO spotting near a disused canal at
Wollaton, Nottingham. It was beginning to get dusk, when I noticed the mist over the can was
rising a few inches above the canal. This was not unusual, except that a cloud about the size of
a fairground 'dodgem' car was detaching itself from the rest and was solidifying into the shape
of a fat doughnut, without the central hole. The object glowed inside with a strange
luminescence and as we walked, it seemed to be the same distance away, although we did not
see it move.




                                                5
    We were fascinated, but soon became afraid, as the quicker we walked and then ran, the closer
    it became. As we approached the edge of the village, we thought the object would follow us
    no further, but it showed no signs of turning back.

    As we were nearly home and feeling braver, I was nominated as spokesman and was to ask it
    if it was 'friend or foe'. Before I got the chance to speak, I felt a tapping on my shoulder, it
    was my friend, who said "when I say run, RUN! "

    I was about to ask him why, when I looked up and saw, just feet in front of me, and
   silhouetted in front of the glowing doughnut,              a six foot high furry creature with claw like
    hands. It was holding two red rods and I noticed that its feet faded into nothingness!

    I didn't need to be told twice and we ran the short distance to my friends home. Once we had
    arrived there, we said nothing but sat down and drew and wrote down what we had seen. The
    reports we made, were identical for two of us, but the third boy, who was only inches away
    from the creature, didn't see it at all, although he did see the illuminated doughnut shaped
    object.

On the 2nd March 97, Jonathan Franklin of Littleover, Derby, noticed a bright 'spotlamp' in
the sky. The time was 9.1 Opm when he and his friend were driving along Warwick Avenue.
J onathan stopped the car and the two witnesses observed that the light was hovering over
Normanton Park. There were three other white lights at the 'front end' and two red lamps at
the rear. The object then slowly moved forward then turned and shone the 'spotlamp' onto the
ground for about ten seconds. The craft then turned and moved away out of sight. The was
some speculation as to whether this could have been a helicopter, but as it was only I 50/200ft
away and no sound was heard, this appears unlikely.

On the 11th April 97, at 7 .50pm. A Derby student, Mr Philip Dore, saw an off white, ovoid
object moving slowly across the sky. It was not illuminated, but was lighter than the sky
background. It was estimated to be about a third of the size of aircraft flying overhead. Four
other witnesses were nearby and all were puzzled by the appearance of the object, which made
a slow steady progress across the sky (approx. 30mph). It made no sound and left no trail of
any kind. It maintained the same height and did not appear to be emitting lights of any kind.
The sighting lasted about a minute and was seen from the Kedlestone Road area of Derby.

Skywatch Diary                                                       by Carole Baker


        Well, Project Awareness' Fifth Annual Gulf Breeze Conference is over. lt was a great success
with around seven hundred people attending the three day event. And there were some unexpected but
hoped for guests! Sightings were reported on Thursday night, March 20th and Friday night, March
21st! So, Bubba showed up to the Conference!!!


        On Thursday night, one of the speakers at the Conference, Larren Warren who is co-author with

Peter Robbins of the book Left at East Gate concerning the Rendlesham Forest event had a sighting. He
and a friend were looking out over the Gulf of Mexico around 11 :00 p.m. on Thursday evening, March
20th when they observed what Larry described to me as "a row of amber lights, three of them, with a
red light off to the right side of them and a blue light next to that. The amber lights were not steady
though, they were moving up and down. There seemed to be a beam of blue light come out of the blue
light itself once or twice". They watched this "light show" which appeared at around ten degrees
elevation, due south from Pensacola Beach for about five minutes. "Then when we looked back, it was
not there". I asked Larry if he saw any planes in the area of the lights and he did not. Later that night,
or rather early on Friday morning around three a. m. a witness reported seeing similar lights further
to the east out over the Gulf of Mexico.


        On Friday evening, after the Conference events were concluded, a group gathered on the beach
near the Beachside resort to skywatch. About twenty people assembled there and at 10: 1 5 p.m. a
sighting occurred in the east south east over the Gulf. Two red lights appeared in very close proximity
to each other, arranged horizontally and only about three degrees above the horizon. They glowed up
very brightly, then faded down to almost nothing. Then they glowed up again, this time more amber
colored than red. These lights faded down and then glowed up brightly once more. Several people had
the lights sighted in their binoculars, including myself. I was able to observe the lights until the one
on the right hand side rotated up and around the left hand light until it was almost at a forty-five degree

angle and then both lights blinked out. The duration of this sighting was right at three minutes. This
sighting is very much in line with the type of sightings that are often reported from Shoreline Park and
Pensacola Beach area. No aircraft were observed in the sky at that time. A few minutes later a small
commercial plane was seeing coming from the Pensacola Airport on a south-westerly _course and then
about twenty minutes after that a high flying jet passed over the beach in a south-easterly direction.
Everyone who witnessed this sighting was very excited and it was great that so many visitors were able
to witness what our local skywatch group has become so familiar with seeing. Of course, due to the low
elevation of this sighting, it would not have been visible had we been skywatching as usual at Shoreline
Park.


        There was talk of another sighting having occurred during the Conference, but I was unable to
gain any firm information about it, or track down those who had witnessed it.
