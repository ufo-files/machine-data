    MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD
    SUBJECT:                   INSCOM GRILL FLAME Briefing for Congressman Rose,
                               16 January 1980 (U)


    1. (U) PURPOSE: At 1400 hrs, 16 Jan 80, I briefed Congressman ~
    Charlie Rose (Chairman, Evaluation Subcommittee of The House Permanent
    Committee on Intelligence) on the INSCOM GRILL FLAME Project. The
    briefing had been requested by Congressman Rose via Dr. Jack Varona,
    DDST, DIA and had been approved by the ACSI.
    2.         (U)             The following personnel attended the briefing:
                                            Congressman Rose
                                            MG Thompson, ACSI
                                            Jake Stewart, NSC Staff Member
                                            Dr. Jack Varona, DDST, DIA
                                            Dick Giza, Office of Congressman Rose
                                            LTC Watt, INSCOM
                                            Recorder (Identity unknown)
    3. (U) Congressman Rose stated that he wanted the briefing taped so
    that other members of the House Subcommittee would have the opportunity
    to review the proceedings. MG Thompson replied that he had no objections
    to taping the briefing.
    4. (U) Inclosure 1 shows the outline of my presentation. Page 3 of Incl 1
    was not provided to the audience at the direction of MG Thompson.
     5. (S) Congressman Rose was very receptive to the briefing (which lasted
     a little over an hour). He stated that we had made more progress than he
     had realized and encouraged us to continue with our program. He expressed
