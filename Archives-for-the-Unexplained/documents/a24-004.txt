  INTERNET MESSAGE CITING NORAD AT DEFCON 4 A HOAX
       from Rob McConnell - misterx@x-chronicles.com
  Internet users in Canada and the United States receieved the following
  e-mail message:
  "It is almost 1:30 am and I am listening to ART BELL and several of
  his guests...if you're not listening, it is possible that history is
  being made tonight. For those of you not listening NORAD at Cheyenne
  Mountain, Colorado, is on a full alert and is at DEFCON 4. Also there
  has been a full military alert tonight...."
  "...There is also the possibility that we are entering into a very
  serious deadly situation of a military nature. National Security.
  Whatever the reason for this alert it is serious fro DEFCON 4 to be
  issued for any reason."
  Within minutes of this e-mail arriving at THE 'X' CHRONICLES Newsroom,
  we received a number of telephone calls from within the Province of
  Ontario - from within the Greater Toronto Area, Golden Horseshoe -
  from Windsor to Ottawa.
  Captain Dubuc, Media Liaison Officer for Department of National
  Defence Headquarters in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada stated that the
  Defence Condition at NORAD was definitely not at DEFCON 4 - which is
  one step down from nuclear war. According to Dubuc there has been no
  unidentified traffic over the skies of Canada and this was echoed by
  the Media Liaison Officer at Canadian Armed Forces - Air and Sea
  Search and Rescue Operation headquarters at CFB Trenton, Ontario.
  The Media Liaison officer at the Pentagon was unavailable for comment.
  A report issued earlier today by the National UFO Reporting Center in
  the United States claims a federal employee contacted them stating a
  huge anomalous object was photographed by the GOES-9 weather satellite
  at 15:30 hours Zulu (8:30 am Pacific) and it was allegely reported on
  CNN that NORAD had heightened it's alert status. Jim Hedges of CNN
  News Center in Atlantoa told THE 'X' CHRONICLES that he was not aware
  of this story. Hedges said that he had just finished speaking to their
  Pentagon Liaison contact within the hour and there was no report of
  any strange, unusual or military problem.
  The US Weather Bureau and contacts at NASA said that this was the
  first that they had heard any of this.
