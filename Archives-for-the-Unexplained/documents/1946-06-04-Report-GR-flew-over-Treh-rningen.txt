Case xxx.
Another Trehörningen overflight

Time: Tuesday, June 4, 16.05 hours
Place: Flurkmark, near [lake] Trehörningen, county of Västerbotten

This incident was first reported to the local newspaper Västerbottens-Kuriren: 

”MYSTERIOUS OBJECT CROSSED THROUGH THE SKY OVER FLURKMARK. Mr Sigfrid Forsgren, Västervik, Trehörningen, reports that he and two other persons were at work on the foundations for a cement factory, at about 16.05 on Tuesday, when they sighted an object passing at 200 to 300 meters altitude in a westerly direction. It looked like a giant sea-bird with immobile wings, swept backwards, and with a speed estimated to be about three times as great as that of an airplane. It passed right over the the workers who were attracted to the it through the sound, which almost could be likened to a fizzing roar [Sw. brusande dån], thus no engine sound. One of the workers looked up and said: What is that?, and then the others, too, caught sight of the object, which apparently passed on the same altitude all the time. It left no smoke and the observers got the impression that it was a radio-guided bomb or possibly a rocket-driven projectile. 

”The place where it was observed is between Flurkmark and Tavelsjö, and ”the bomb” came from the Flurkmark area and went towards Långviksvallen.”

This sighting was, like many other sightings in 1946, published by a small local newspaper only, with no nation-wide publicity. In the autumn the Defence Staff heard about this, and other incidents in the Trehörningen area, and ordered a police investigation: 

”On order of the superintendent of the state police department the undersigned, criminal constable Folke Johansson, has done the ordered investigation, when the following has been noted. On investigation it has become apparent that space projectiles have been sighted at the following times by the following persons: 

”1. One day at the end of May 1946. [Some 3 ½ months after the incident it is only natural that it is not correctly placed in time] ([Investigation] September 16, 1946, [by] F.Johansson.)  Brick-works owner Hans Sigfrid Forsberg, born on February 14, 1914, registered in the town of Umeå, but now living at Västervik, parish of Umeå, postal address Västervik, Flurkmark, telephone nr 32, Trehörningen: 

”One day at the end of May Forsberg was at work with Holger Lundström and Tycko Andersson, both from Trehörningen, Tavelsjö, in a gravel-pit about 100 meters east of the eastern shore of lake Trehörningen. During the work in the gravel-pit Forsberg heard, at about 15.45 hours, a powerful whining sound, which was like the sound from an airplane passing by at low altitude with its engine cut off. At first Forsberg thought it was a tornado, closing in, but as he looked up in the sky, he caught sight of an object come flying from the east just over the treetops on a wooded hill, which is close by and to the south of the gravel-pit. The object went away in a westerly direction over lake Trehörningen. Forsberg could not, however, follow it with his eyes, since he was blinded by the sun, which was in the direction where the object went away. Whether the object landed in lake Trehörningen he wouldn´t with certainty say anything about but the thought this was possible, since it seemed to lower itself. At the time there was a beautful weather with sunshine and a sky without clouds.

”The object passed Forsberg at a distance of about 100 meters, therefore he rather well noted its appearance. It mostly looked like a large bird and was dark in colour. The object had a length of about one meter and a breadth, between wingtips, of about one meter. It didn´t glisten, and no emission, or similar, was seen to come from the object. Concerning the object´s shape see the enclosed sketch, enclosure 1, drawn by Forsberg. On being questioned Forsberg declared that he neither before nor after had seen any similar phenomenon.”













”Sketch drawn by brick-works owner Sigfrid Forsberg of Västervik, parish of Umeå, and showing the shape of the object which he, one day at the end of May 1946, sighted near lake Trehörningen. The sketch was made on September 16, 1946,”  

”Worker Johan Martin Holger Lundström, born March 15, 1927, registered and living at Trehörningen, parish of Umeå, postal adress Tavelsjö, telephone nr 29, Trehörningen: 

”Lundström corroborated Forsbergs information and stated that he during work in the gravel-pit suddenly heard a ”murmur” [Sw. blåsljud], which was rather strong. He looked around and then discovered an object which came through the air from the east and over the treetops on wooded hill just to the south of the gravel-pit. The object continued at very high speed over lake Trehörningen, where it, according to what Lundström could observed, changed its course somewhat to the south. The change of direction was, however, very small. Where the object disappeared he didn´t see, since it was covered by trees. It also flew very fast, and the sun was also annoying. Lundström believed, however, that the object landed in lake Trehörningen, since it lowered itself while in flight. Concerning the shape of the object, see the accomanying sketch, enclosure 2, which has been drawn by Lundström. On being questioned, Lundström declared that he had neither before nor after seen any similar object.”
















”Sketch made by worker Holger Lundström of Trehörningen, parish of Umeå, and showing the shape of the object which he, one day at the end of May 1946, saw near lake Trehörningen. The sketch was drawn on September 16, 1946.”

”Worker Anders Tycho Andersson, born on December 23, 1901, registered and living at Trehörningen, parish of Umeå, postal address Tavelsjö, telephone nr 14, Trehörningen: 

”Andersson corroborated, where applicable, what was stated by Forsberg and Lundström. During their work in the gravel-pit he heard, about 16.00 hours, a ”roaring noise” from the east. He looked around and then he caught sight of and object that came flying from the east at a rather low altitude over the treetops and very high speed. The object mostly looked like a large sea-bird and had wings stretching a bit from and along the body. The wings were not moving and any exhaust, or similar, could not be seen coming from the object. In Anderssons view the sound from the object was most powerful when he first sighted it, whereafter it decreased in strength. The object went away in a westerly direction over lake Trehörningen, and, according to what Andersson could see ”it wobbled” when it got some distance over the lake, changing direction somewhat to the south. Where the object went, Andersson couldn´t see, since it was blocked by woods and the sun was annoying. Whether the object fell into lake Trehörningen Andersson did not want to say anything definite about. He was, however, of the opinion that it lowered itself since it had passed the spot where he was standing. Concerning the shape of the object, see the enclosed sketch, enclosure 3, drawn by Andersson. On special question Andersson stated that he neither before nor since had seen any similar phenomenon.”











”Sketch drawn by worker Tycho Andersson at Trehörningen, parish of Umeå, to show the object which he, one day at the end of May 1946, saw near lake Trehörningen. Sketch drawn on September 16, 1946.” 

Status: Unidentified
