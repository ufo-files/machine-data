Sydney Morning Herald (NSW : 1842 - 1954), Monday 22 April 1929, page 12


          MYSTERY        LIGHT.
       MURWILLUMBAH,              Saturday.

         Residents      of    the Numinbah         and     Hopkins
       Creek    district    have     been   distinctly     alarmed
       by the strange          movement      of  a   very     bright
       light which      recently appeared        about     an   hour
       after  nightfall,     and    disappeared     an    hour   be-
       fore daybreak.          Local    residents believe the
       light was   carried by human         hands.     The women
       residents    are    very    much    concerned,      and   are

       anxious to see the mystery solved.
