      We have received from veteran UFO buff Laura Mundo a free copy of
her long-awaited book "The Mundo UFO Report", published by Vantage Press
- presumably at her own expense. The book can be obtained from Vantage
(516 W. 34th St., New York, N.Y.) for the price of $9.95.
      The front inside flap of the book informs us: "'The Mundo UFO Re­
port' is an interesting document. It chronicles a series of events that
only become intelligible when one enters the personal world of its author�
This is no doubt true. Laura has bee n involved in the flying saucer move­
ment since 1953� and in the 1950's sponsored Detroit lectures by George
Adamski, Desmond Leslie, and even Major Keyhoe. In recent years her over­
riding concern has been the high level of sunspots, which she believes are
having a very bad effect on this planet and its people. This assumption
apparently relates to complicated atomic theories that Laura believes in,
presumably from information supplied by the Space People. For Laura is a
contactee of sorts, and has devoted her life to propounding contactee­
oriented theories together with a genuinely humanistic feeling for Man­
kind. She is also a stout defender of the late George Adamski and his
theories. Though we have never quite understood what she is talking about,
we have always felt that Laura means well and deserves a hearing.   This
book may well be her last attempt to put forth her views, as Laura has
been seriously ill in recent years.
      A (to us) amusing note is found in the foreword, regarding an earl­
ier book by Laura Mundo, called "Flying Saucers and the Father9s Plan".
Says she: "It was published by the Saucerian Publishers of West Virginia
and Gray Barker, but unfortunately not properly proofread by Barker, .but
which Barker is still selling and gets the total profits and has raised
the price several times." In truth, Barker found the book unintelligible
and was unable to proofread it; but he should have shared the profits, if
any. Perhaps his lack of generosity was influenced by the fact that Laura
professes not to believe in the Money System. Make no mistake about it -
Barker does:•   •   •   •
