  This is probably the bestselling UFO book of all time -- 25 weeks on the New York Times bestseller list.
  We don't understand why. There is no significant data here, just one man's claims, told in the form of a
  novel. Is it coincidence that this abductee is already a novelist, whose vocation is to dream up story lines?
  The facts, if any, are so buried in the storytelling process, that it is hard to find much useful information --
  even if his claims are true. To us, the main significance of this book is how many people have read it and
  its corresponding social impact. -- Glenn Campbell

  Reservations

  Apart from this book itself, we are annoyed by Strieber's seeming penchant for endorsing every popular alien fad and
  his remarkable ability to "recall" new alien experiences whenever a new book contract requiries it.
