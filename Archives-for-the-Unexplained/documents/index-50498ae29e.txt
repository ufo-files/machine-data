  From the Book Cover

  CE-5 is the ultimate reference of real data on the hottest topic in UFOs. This book documents, explains and questions
  nearly 250 incidents from around the world where people have deliberately tried to have contact with UFO phenomena
  or alien beings and succeeded. Sceintists and paranormal experts refer to these situations as Close Encounters of the
  Fifth Kind -- or CE-5.

  Exhaustively researched and thoroughly referenced, CE-5 was compiled from technical data and research and personal
  interviews with witnesses. Dealing with both 'traditional" aerial UFO encounters and encounters with alien humanoids,
  CE-5 unwraps the mysteries of the paranormal and gives you the complete reference information you keed to answer
  questions such as:

          What characterizes the visitors' behavior?
          How do UFOs react to friendly human contact?
          How do they react to hostile human contact?
          Who is the real aggressor?
