 THE STRANGE CASE OF "DR." WILLIARD MciNTYRE

         It is more in sadness than in anger that we bring you a "Saucer Drool"
 exclusive, regarding the MARCEN organization, and in particular, its person­
 able director, "Dr." Mcintyre.
         In the most recent issue of the MARCEN Journal (Vol. 2, No. 3), it is
 stated that the organization's membership stood at 7,010 at the time the is­
 sue went to press. This, we might note, is an astonishingly large member­
 ship, considering the short period of time MARCEN has been in existence.
 Even more amazing are the results of a supposed computer study of the mem­
 bership, in which it was found that 2,684 members hold a total of 2,796
 doctorates. There were also 4,014 Masters degrees!
          In regard to participating (as opposed to sustaining or honorary)
 members, the magazine states:     "We can say that the average member is a 42
 year old male with an average of 3 academic degrees above a bachelor's de­
 gree. (Emphasis ours.) He is probably a college professor teaching in the
 physical sciences, has a military background as a commissioned officer, and
 probably has current hobbies of photography, flying, and scuba diving in
 addition to his interests in unconventional phenomena."
         To us, it is utterly amazing that the MARCEN.membership should be so
 unusually literate; but at first glance the above description of a "typical"
 participating member does seem, at least, to apply quite well indeed to the
 director, "Dr." Mcintyre. He apparently has a background as a Naval offi­
 cer, and seems to work currently as a photographic consultant to various
 firms. In the past he has, by his own admission, done consulting work for

admits sounds like a diploma mill. No one has yet checked further into any
of .this.
