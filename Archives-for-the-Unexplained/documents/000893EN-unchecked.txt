To Frontier Custom House, Karlstad
Regarding observation of a rocketplane at lake Römungen.
I the undersigned may respectfully inform of the following.
At the 30th of july, 1946, was a patrol undertaken, together  with frontier guard (temp.)
B R I Johansson to lake  Römungen. When we at 1654 were at the western side of the lake
approximately  200 meter from  border cairn ((riksrös)) 43  did we hear a roar in the air, like
an airplane with its engine  shut off. The sound was coming from due south and somewhat
west of our position. Johanssons  view  was blocked by some trees, I saw an object  which was shaped
like an airplane rather smaller than a such. The fuselages length I estimated to about between
5 and 8 meters. The wings were placed on approx. 1/3d of the fuselages length from the nose
and were short and stubby. The  wingspan about half of the bodylength. How   the tail was shaped
I can´t remember. Any cockpit or place for passengers was not seen. The plane was silvergrey 
in colour, velocity about 800 k.p.h. The heading   was straight northerly and the altitude about 
500  meters.  No   engine or motor noise could be heard. It seemed as if it had no fuel left and the
speed   diminished successively. When the plane had passed the frontier I could not see it because
there  were some trees obscuring the view.
We continued  to border cairn ((riksrös)) 43 where we came across Paul Edvinsson from Kroken,
Boviken. He had seen and heard the same as us, furthermore that when the plane  reached 
about  300 meters into Norwegian territory it changed its course by an abrupt yaw to the east
and  it seemed as if the plane dived towards the ground.
There is nothing more to report.
Bottner the 5th of august, 1946
B G Högfeldt
Frontier  guard
Vidim. in service: Anna-Britta Tydén
