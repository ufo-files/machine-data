        The target vessel is carrying contraband in the form of
illegal drugs stored in medium-sized crates associated with
"wares," possibly dental equipment. Two personnel involved in "the
steering" (direction?) of the vessel are aware of the location of
the crates used to store the contraband. The suspect crates are
located near the "inner walls" of the vessel.     In about three to
seven days, the vessel will unload its cargo to include the illegal
drugs, in Corpus Christi, Texas. While in Corpus Christi, the
cargo will be temporarily stored in a warehouse.      The narcotics
will then be transported by small closed trucks driven by Hispanic
immigrants to Potter, Texas.     Potter is located in the Texas
Panhandle.   The narcotics will then be flown from Potter to its
ultimate destination identified as Lake Tahoe, Nevada.

related to the "machinery company" involved.    Moreover, the name
"SealingjSealling" surfaced as being associated with a person
involved in the trafficking. The target vessel may be sailing to
