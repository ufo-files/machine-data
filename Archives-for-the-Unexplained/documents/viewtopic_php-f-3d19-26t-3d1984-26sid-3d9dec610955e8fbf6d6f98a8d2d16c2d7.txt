  Upon first viewing the framing of the other man with the mobile phone camera blasted the idea of hoax right to the front
  of my mind.

  If possible footage of a UFO could be seen we all know multiple camera angles are key to trying to explain what it is.
  Having a man, who you know via claims in the comments section, be centred and taped holding another recording
  device and aiming it at the UFO also in your shot then you ultimately set up the question "Where's the other footage?".

  Now it's quite possible that a genuine scenario would appear and someone with enough clout to produce real evidence
  would record this - however it's also likely that a premeditated hoax would try and use the same technique and in all
  probability would do so before 'real footage' ever showed up.

  Additional footage, or even eye witness testimony to try and assist with the current footage would be useful at this point.
  If we could get the footage prior to YouTube compression we could try and focus on where the UFO 'lands' and give us
  an indication of speed and distance using Google maps and the time it takes for the object to move upwards.

  A better view of the blanket lighting (although this is probably an effect in this case) would allow us to determine an
  exact location of the UFO if indeed it was emitted by it.

  My thinking it's a hoax does not render it as such until I can compile more evidence to substantiate that thinking. But
  between us here (and possibly a few folk elsewhere) I hope we can either validate it as genuine (That'd be great) or
  prove it as a hoax (Much more likely in my opinion).
