   A 14-year-old Pennsylvania boy has been allowed to
starve to death as a result of his father's belief that their
$3,600 bank account "belonged to God", according to a
police affidavit. The father, an unemployed former
minister of the Seventh-day Adventist Church, believed
that "God would provide", according to a former boss. The
5' 10" boy weighed 69 pounds at autopsy. His parents and
sister required treatment for malnutrition. Homicide and
reckless endangerment charges have been filed against
