                                               Palo Alto Dump
                                              (Palo Alto, off Embarcadero)
        Directions: SEE MAP; Take Laurel Street south to Willow Rd.; tum left on Willow; turn right on
        Middlefield Rd.; follow Middlefield out to Embarcadero Rd.; take Embarcadero Rd. left (East); you will
        pass Highway 101; you will pass the sewage treatment plant on the right; tum right at the end of
        Embarkadero and drive down to the entrance gate of the Palo Alto Municipal Refuse Disposal Area; the
        Guard my not let you in unless you are a Palo Alto resident and have something to dump; if you do not
        qualify, all you can do is park near the entrance.

        Target: The target is the Palo Alto Municipal Refuse Disposal Area (i.e., the Dump). The dump is
        located near the bay. The dump is an open dirt area frequented by scavenging Seagulls. As trucks bring
        rubbish into the dump, large earth moving machines work to cover the piles over with fresh dirt. The
        earth moving machines leave behind tracks and earthen mounds.
