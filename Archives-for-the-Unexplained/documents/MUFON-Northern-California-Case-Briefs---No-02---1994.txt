           It is my pleasure to announce that our premier issue of Case Briefs was a big hit at MUFON's 25th Annual
  Symposium in Austin, Texas last July. Northern California's attempt at a bi-monthly publication for local MUFON
  members started off with a bang thanks to Kathi Hennesey's two excellent issues of No. California MUFON
  Newsletters. Case Briefs will continue the good work with additional emphasis on local investigations. While our main
  focus is on northern California cases, we have started a newsletter exchange program with several MUFON chapters
  and other UFO groups. We anticipate being able to bring our readers fascinating articles, good interviews and
  information from around the globe. State Section Directors who have already presented cases to the State Director are
. invited to submit the most interesting of those cases to the Case Briefs Editor for possible publication in future issues.
  Please feel free call me at (415) 775-4579 (or more information.
