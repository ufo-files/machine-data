Case xxx.
The find of an airplane antenna

Time: Friday, August 2
Place: Buatorp, Nättraby (county of Blekinge)

”GHOST PHEMONENON HIDDEN IN A MOSS IN A BLEKINGE WOOD. Parts which are believed to come from a ghost bomb was found Friday evening in a wood in the middle of [the county of] Blekinge. The find was made by an elderly man who immediately reported the thing to the police. 

”On Saturday the object was digged out. It was bored rather deep into the ground. What came into daylight was very much like an oil-can, which in one end had a 50 meter long copper wire attached. The very can weighed about one kilo. The acting policeman didn´t dare any further investigation, since he feared that the metallic box could contain some explosive. Military authorities at Karlskrona have been summoned and promised to investigate the find. There is no doubt that the object has crashed from the sky. Evidently it has fallen from a very great height, judging from the power with thich it has hit ground. An answer from the military experts is expected on Monday.” (Dagens Nyheter, August 4)

According to the TT newswire [nr 72, August 5] the object had bored some 25-30 cm into the ground. The can was about 10 cm long. 

Result of the investigation came the following day:

””THE MYSTERIOUS FIND WAS AN AIRPLANE ANTENNA. The ”mysterious” object found in Blekinge has, according to a report from the Air Defence department of the Defence Staff, been identified as a lost Swedish airplane antenna. The find was made on the grounds owned by farmer Hjalmar Olofsson, Buatorp, Nättraby. The antenna found probably originate from a B3 airplane [Swedish designation for a Junkers Ju 86, almost 90 aircraft owned by the Swedish Air Force between 1936 and 1958].” (Sydöstra Sveriges Dagblad, August 6).
