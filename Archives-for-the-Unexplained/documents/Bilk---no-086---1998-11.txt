panther (although panther is often used as a synonym for puma) . Zoologically
speaking,    the onza (Spanish)       or Portuguese onca ist a (melanistic)                  jaguar,
scientifically: Panthera onca. In Mexico and Brasil onza/onca is often used
synonymous with black puma. Marcel Hornet,                 the explorer in search of lost ruins
in Brasil,       in his 1950s books often refers to the onca,                which,   he says,    is the
Portuguese word for panther.
