TEXT: EXTRACT.     DOCUMENT CONTAINS 1 BIOGRAPHY.

      QIAN XUESEN WAS APPOINTED AS DEPUTY MINISTER OF THE SEVENTH MACHINE
      BUILDING ON 15 FEBRUARY 1965; HE WAS APPOINTED THE FIRST PRESIDENT
      OF CHINA SPACE RESEARCH ACADEMY (A CONCURRENT POST FOR QIAN) IN 1968.
      ON 12 JUNE 1970, HE WAS APPOINTED DEPUTY DIRECTOR OF THE SCIENCE AND
      TECHNOLOGY COMMISSION.   IN 1982, HE WAS APPOINTED DEPUTY DIRECTOR OF
      THE   SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY COMMITTEE OF THE NATIONAL DEFENSE
      COMMISSION OF SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND INDUSTRY.      IN JULY 1987, HE
      WAS APPOINTED SENIOR ADVISER OF THE NATIONAL DEFENSE COMMISSION.
      OWING TO·CONTRIBUTIONS TO CHINA'S STRATEGIC MISSILE TECHNOLOGY, QIAN
      WAS CHOSEN AS THE FIRST PLACE WINNER OF THE ALL CHINA SPECIAL GRADE
      PRIZE FOR PROGRESS IN SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY; THE FOLLOWING PERSONS
     .WERE COWINNERS: TU SHOUE, YAO TONGBIN, HAO FUJIAN, LIANG SILI,
      ZHUANG FENGGAN AND LI XUE.

      ON 29 JUNE 1989, AT THE 1989 INTERNATIONAL TECHNIQUE AND TECHNICAL
      CIRCULATION CONFERENCE CONVENED AT NEW YORK QIAN WAS AWARDED A W. F.
      WEILADE AND ROCKWELL, JR. MEDAL; IN ADDITION, HE WAS HONORED AT THE
      SAME OCCASION AS A WORLD CLASS SCIENTIST AND ENGINEER, AND AN
      HONORARY MEMBER OF THE INTERNATIONAL RESEARCH INSTITUTE OF SCIENCE
      AND ENGINEERING IN HONORING HIS MAJOR PIONEERING CONTRIBUTIONS TO
      ROCKET AND GUIDED MISSILE TECHNOLOGY, AS WELL AS SYSTEMS ENGINEERING
      THEORY.
