Portsmouth News…
6th August 2007
Ministry releases its UFO files
 
UFOs are a common sight reported in the skies above Hampshire, according to top secret files.

   Records made public for the first time reveal there have been 27 UFO sightings in eight years over the county between 1998 and 2006. The files, which have been released by the MoD under the Freedom of Information Act, reveal all the sightings reported to the ministry and its responses. Among them are successions of images of objects in the sky above Portsmouth harbour last July.
   And in a letter last May, a concerned citizen warned the MoD that she and her husband were being menaced by invisible craft, the grey alien inhabitants of which have already abducted her in the past to 'extract her DNA'. To support her case, she enclosed a photograph of an object (possibly a Frisbee or a satellite dish) 'hovering' over a church.
   In September 2001, in Gosport, a witness reported 'a twinkling object in the air, in close proximity to a plane'. Two UFO sightings were recorded in the Winchester area and two on the Isle of Wight in January 2000. However, the MoD says there is no evidence to say UK airspace has been compromised by hostile or unauthorised military activity.
