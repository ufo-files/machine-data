  Publisher's Synopsis

  Stroll through time and space; reason with the spirituality and creation of man. Understand the love of family;
  experience joy, sorrow, laughter, faith, and hope, those qualities which may be unique to the "human equation."
  Question, reason, and perhaps agree with the theories on cloning a man, UFO investigations, alien contact, and
  abduction experiences, however fictional in nature.

        This well-written thought-provoking book will introduce you to an exciting new genre of written communication
  in which Dr. Michael Wolf addresses the topics of art, science, and religion. His three decades of patriotic duty to man
  and country, his vast knowledge and education, and his gentle manner of communicating his ideas encourage an
  understanding of the connection between time and space; the continuing creation of the universe; the impact of
  environmental hazards on this biosphere; and, most importantly, his work with and for the government.

        As a memorial to his son, Dr. Wolf is creating The Daniel Wolf Memorial Foundation for Children, Inc., a
  nonprofit organization designed to help ensure a viable future for children, the hope and future of the world. He will
  donate all royalties to this institution.

          "The Catchers of Heaven is more than the breathtaking story of a government scientist who encountered
          extraterrestrial beings and, through these encounters, learned to LOVE--it is an extremely beautiful,
          consciousness-expanding book, a piece of tomorrows world literature, which opens gateways into a new
          reality."

                                                                                                            --Michael Hesemann
