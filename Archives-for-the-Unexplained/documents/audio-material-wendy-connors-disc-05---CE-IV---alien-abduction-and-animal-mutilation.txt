Annex Disc 5 : CE IV : An Audio History of Alien Abduction and Animal Mutilation 1957 -
1976

56 Tracks
Total Running Time - 25+ Hours


SYNOPSIS

Alien abduction of human beings began slowly in historical Ufology

The phenomena began slowly too, but both alien abduction of humans and animal
mutilations, remain one of the most elusive and puzzling phenomena within the UFO genre
of research

Included are other types of alien abduction, termed Mutes, or animal mutilation

This disc contains 56 tracks containing almost 25 hours of recordings from the above
mentioned period

Some of these recordings will be familiar to researchers but others are presented for the first
time

This then is the audio history of Alien abduction - 1957 to 1977
