                          Mt. Aconcapua
                          (a-k8ng-ka-gwa)


                              The highest mountain in the
Western Hemisphere, is in the Argentina near the Chilean
Border.  A tooth-like eminence, it rises from its' precipitous
ridges to an elevation of 22,834 ft.  It is often visible from
yalparaiso, on the shore of the Pacific, 90 miles away.
Although it is located in the heart of the Andes, where much
volcanic action has occured, Aconcagua is not considered a
volcano.
