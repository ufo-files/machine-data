                   Magonia report on Raelian press conference
     From: campbell@ufomind.com (Glenn Campbell, Las Vegas)
     Date: Thu, 11 Dec 1997 10:56:42 -0800


  A press conference by the Raelians is reported in a British
  article...
  From Magonia 60, Summer 1997.
  Off the Tracks with British Rael
  by Mark Pilkington
  Excerpt:
  "The Raelians had picked a bad day for their first British meeting
  in about twenty years, it being less than a week since the
  Heaven's Gate suicides had hit the headlines, giving UFO fanatics
  everywhere a bad name. But this didn't stop the crowd packing
  its way into the small but glitzy function room. Small papier
  mache planets and flying saucers hung on wires from the low
  ceiling, bobbing around in the air conditioned breeze like
  Pleidian beamships surfing on gravity waves. There were no
  comets. In the end people had to be turned away, and even a
  contingent from The Nation of Islam had to stand stoically at the
  back of the room, looking, with their suits, shades and tiny red
  bow-ties, like a cross between CIA agents and Pee Wee Herman."
