       A double hoax?
       The "Daily Express" states that a profes­
       sional photographer (name not published)
       walked into the office last Friday with the
       claim that he was the author of one of the
       greatest hoaxes ever perpetrated on scien­
       tists and astronomers in this country. He
       claimed that he alone was responsible for
       the    "meteoric"        displays   confidentl�·
       thought to have been seen all over England

       highly technical wrangles between famous
       scientists.
         ·"The lights which were seen flashing
