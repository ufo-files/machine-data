Book Reviews
UFO REVELATION - THE SECRET
TECHNOLOGY EXPOSED
BY TIM MATTHEWS
After reading the first two chapters of this
book, it soon became obvious that this book
is not about Extraterrestrial technology. It is
about military black projects from all over
the world, starting from the World War II to
the present day.
Matthews goes on to reveal that the technol-
ogy is there to build and also fly 'flying sau-
cers' and 'flying triangles'. He explains that
at the end of WWII, both the Americans and
Russians 'invited' the German rocket scien-
tists to carry on their research in their new
countries. The book is very well researched,
with input from various aviation research-
ers, such as Rose, Sweetman and Goodall,
to name a few. Matthews also ties in a good
many UFO sightings involving flying trian-
gles and flying saucers, as being nothing
more than military black projects...
Aviation enthusiasts will find much of inter-
est in this book, as well as UFO researchers.
Personally, I would have liked to see Mat-
thews try and explain the Rendlesham For-
est incident - after all that involved a trian-
gular craft. I'm glad that I read the book,
but think that Matthews could have been
kinder in his comments to established UFO
researchers.
Reviewed by James A Hill
ALIEN INVESTIGATOR
BY ANTHONY DODD
I have to say from the outset that this book
really is good.. It has a bit of everything -
from a crash retrieval in South Africa (The
Kalahari incident) to an alien base beneath
the North Sea..
A lot of the book is based on Dodd's own
investigations over the years, plus some of
his own personal experiences with abduc-
tees. Some of his descriptions rang very
true.
Dodd describes clandestine meetings at a lo-
cation in London, plus a car chase. He also
describes animal mutilations in this country,
which are on a far larger scale than many of
us think.
There are also possible human
mutilations, which make you think twice
about going out late at night, even on sky-
watches.
There is also a section that covers an elite
security force, set up especially for alien
crash retrievals. It muts be said that for a
few, what Dodd has written may seem fan-
ciful, but I have seen this man lecture at
conferences.
He comes across as a very
'down to earth' and honest person, and I can
quite believe most of what he has written.
I started reading this book at 2.00pm on a
Sunday, and didn't put it down until I'd fin-
ished it at midnight - it was that captivating,
and I can thoroughly recommend it.

these photos were seen by the naked eye! It is recognised that the human eye is rather lim-
ited in terms of the range of light it can see - some animals have much better vision. In re-
cent years, an increasing number of people, from various avenues of research, have been re-
cording more and more odd lights in nocturnal photos - equally dark objects are captured
on film in daylight.

HANDY TIPS FOR SKYWATCHERS
For any would-be skywatchers reading
this, James A Hill would like to offer some
advice, so you can prepare for all eventu-
alities...
1) Do listen to the weather forecast before
setting out for the skywatch site.
2) Do take a map and directions to the sky-
watch site.
3) Do take waterproof clothing, and ther-
mal underwear if it is cold.
4) Do take cameras, with spare films and
batteries.
5) Do take something hot to drink for those
long cold nights...
6) Do try to stay awake - you might miss a
UFO flying over.
7) Don't turn up at the wrong location, and
sit there wondering where everyone else
is.
8) Don't, if in a group, stand around wav-
ing torches about - another group a few
miles away may report it as a sighting.
9) Do be careful of where you walk or
stand while out in the dark countryside -
animal 'do-do' can be slippery, and sud-
denly lying on your back can be painful,
and you will smell for the rest of the
night.
10) Don't let one of the group wander off
alone. They may find some lengths of
plastic pipe, which they fit across the
end of their torch to make it look like a
lightsaber. They might then creep up
behind someone else's car, severely
frightening the occupants!
11) Do take spare thermal underwear (See
item 10)

recent skywatches, and have all happened.

tect the guilty...
