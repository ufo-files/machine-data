EASTBOURNE HER'LL
CHRONICLE
- Sussex -
21 KAY 1938
Tongue in cheek
ABOUT 20 yeårs ago I read a book which I believe was
largely responsible for propagating the UFO ideas. The
evidence was Impressive, but Ive since realised it was
very selective too, and I've even heard It was originally
written as a joke.
To invoke a UFO force-field, whatever that is, to explain
the exodus, is to add a science-fictional complication,
when surely the Bible's own explanation of the forces in-
volved is far simpler and more credible; namely that a
strong wind drove the water back.
Either Mr Chamberlaine-Brothers risked his credibility
somewhat, or as I suspect, he wrote about this tongue in
cheek. But whichever, may he keep writing, for, after all
If one's ideas or beliefs can't stand questioning perhaps It's
because they need questioning! - M. Ball, 5 Carden
Road, Eastbourne.
