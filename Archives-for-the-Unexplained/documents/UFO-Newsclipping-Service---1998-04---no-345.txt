    UFOs a lifetime passion
    for local·investigator
                           By BILL HEWITT
                               LTN StaffWriter
      If a flying saucer was to beam down little green men, folks might
  wonder who to call- the swat team, a priest, Welcome Wagon? But
  in .Lincoln County there is no need to wonder; George D. Fawcett ·is
  the man.
       His fascination with UFOs began when he was a boy scout. He
  kept a scrapbook filled with clippings from WWII. One particular
  entry captured his imagination:                      ·
       Paris, Dec. 13, 1944. The Associated Press. "As Allied armies
  ground out new gains on the western front today, the Germans were
  disclosed to have thrown a new "device" into the war- mysterious
  silvery balls wbich float in the air."
     · But the Germans never used this mystery weapon. Fawcett won-
1 dered why. That is until he saw something during his senior year at

I Lynchburg College.
  ' It was 10: 15 a.m. Not a cloud in the sky and Fawcett was walk-
   ing all alone in front of the administration building on campus. "It
