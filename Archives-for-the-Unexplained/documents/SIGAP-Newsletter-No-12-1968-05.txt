         April was an exciting month from a ufological point of
    view. For no less than five mysterious star-like objects,
    which changed colour and appeared to be rotating, were seen over
    the village of Lightwater, near Bagshot.
     On the evening of Sunday, April 14, two schoolboys,
David Wye (15), of 127 Ambleside Road, Lightwater and
Stephen Clue (14), of 151 Ambleside Road, were playing about on
heathland at High Cur1ey, Lightwatero At about 8.15 p.m.
David saw what at first he thought was a rather bright star,
between 50° and 6oo above the south-west horizon. He pointed
it out to Stephen and both boys stood watching it.
         Said Stephen~ "It changed colour from white to red to
    turquoise-blue to red to yellow to green and then to white again.
    It would remain in one position for a few minutes repeating
    this sequence several times, then suddenly move sharply to a
    new position and change colour again".
         Then the boys noticed that a second object had appeared
    in the sky - in the east. This too was between 50° and 60°
    above the horizon and changed colour like the first object.
    Both "stars" had fuzzy edges and changed in brightness as well.
    They seemed to be about the size of a pea held at arm's length.
    Stephen thought they were very high up, possibly 150 miles•
         Unfortunately, ·the boys did not see the manner in which
    the objects disappeared, for they had to return home as it was
    getting late. They watched them however, for about lt hours,
    from 8.15 p.m. to 9.30 pom. When David arrived home he found
    that his brother, and his sister and her boyfriend, had also
    seen the objects.

          David and Stephen returned to High Curley the following
    evening (April 15) - this time accompanied by one of their
    friends, 15-year old Andrew Osborne, of 'Woodpeckers", Ambleside
    Road.

At about 8ol5 p.m. they saw both objects again, or two very
similar ones, in approximately the same positions and at the
same elevation.
     Stephen said: "They both changed colour again and moved
from one place to another. They appeared to be revolving and
signalling to one another. The only difference was that they
were shooting what looked to us like sparks. At first I
thought they were satellites, but then I realised that they
don't change colour and hovero The objects made no sound and
were not aircraft or Army flares either. Neither -·did they
emit any smoke or vapour" •
     He added~ "The objects were brighter than the sky
background" o According to David there was no cloud present
on Sunday and only occasional cloud bn Monday. The temperature
was moderate on both evenings and there was a light breeze.
     The boys reported both sightings to Camberley police,
who the·n passed the de tails on to Bag shot police, for further
investigation. Stephen also mentioned that his elder brother
Alan had s6en one of the objects on Sunday evening from their
house.
     At 9 p.m. on Wednesday, April 17, Stephen and David saw
yet another star-like object changing colour. This one was
in the south-west and the boys saw it from Macdonald Road,
which adjoins Ambleside Road. They did not report this sighting
to the police.
     Do the boys believe in "flying-saucers"? David remarked:
"I didn't before, but now these objects have certainly made
me wonder". Stephen added~ "I thought flying-saucers were a
load of rubbish, just tin cans which people threw up in the air
and photographed. But now I've changed my mindrr.
     Investigator's report: When I first heard about the
sightings I was sceptical, mainly because the witnesses were
schoolboys, a class of people prone to exaggeration. But when
I met Stephen and David on Tuesday, April 16~ I was convinced
that they had seen something unusual in the sky.
     There are minor discrepancies in their reports, but this
is only to be expected. If the whole thing was a hoax their
statements would be identical. Also, the boys reported what
they had seen to the police. Personally, I doubt if they would
have bothered to do this if they had not been telling the truth.
Then of course there were other members of the boys' families
who saw the objects. It is most unlikely that they would all
lie - just to get a bit of publicity!
     High Curley is a shrub-covered hilly area, about 4oo ft.
above sea-level. It is quite surprising the distance that one
can see from the top. An excellent UFO obsGrvation post.
Stephen Clue and I went up there on Friday, April 19. I asked
him to show me where the objects had been seen. I then took
compass be arings and estimated the angle of elevation. My
figures tallied closely with Stephen's. I went up there again
that evening hoping to see the objects. Unfortunately cloud
covered the larger part of the sky.                     s.92

               + BALL OF FIRE OVER SURBITON +
               +                                   +
               ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++



     Twenty-four year old Kevin Woodgate and his wife,
who live at 30 Glenbuck Court, Glenbuck Road, Surbiton,
saw a glowing ball of fire in the sky from their bedroom window,
shortly after midnight on April 4.
     Mr. Woodgate gives the following account of the sightingg
"On the night of April 4, at around 12.25 a.m., my wife and I
had just got into bed. As I turned round to lie down my eyes
caught sight of an extremely bright object high up in the sky.
Thinking it was a shooting-star, I called my wife to look. But
instead of moving down and burning itself out, it remained in
the same spot. We both wa tched it while it flashed on and off
like a police light. It was a bright reddy-orange colour. The
object remained in the same position for around two minutes,
and then began to move down and away towards London very slowly.
The light remained flashing for most of the time, but at one
point stopped, perhaps for 20 seconds. The object was seen for
around six to eight minutes altogether. No noise was heard.
The approximate size of the object when flashing was around six
times as large as an aircraft landing light. As to the shape
of the object, nothing was seen except the light, which seemed
to start as a circle and then project outwards horizontally.
