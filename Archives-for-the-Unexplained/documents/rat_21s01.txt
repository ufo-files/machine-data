Subj: cellular intercept isn't hard
  Date: Thu, Jun 29, 1995 8:25 PM PDT
  To: psychospy@aol.com

  The green camo guys may have broken the law when they jumped in
  on your friend's cell phone conversation, but the tech needed to
  do that is pretty garden variety stuff, just expensive. See the
  magazine 2600 (search netcom's internet search for the number) and
  download the index. There are several issues devoted to cell hacking
  and interception, most aimed at "spoofing" the ID codes which make
  your phone yours and not a general reciever for all cell calls.
  What they did was use a phone which can be instantly programmed
  to act as if it were the original. Terrestrial technology, pure and
  simple. It cuts both ways, by the way. If they are dumb enough
  to use it (the FBI does).
