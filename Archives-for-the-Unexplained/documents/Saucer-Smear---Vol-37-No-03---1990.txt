far as we know, only Antonio Huneeus managed to hit all three.)

very good (but expensive) , the speakers were okay, but the size of the
crowd was small; so our friend Tim Beckley lost money. Your editor gave a
short talk comparing the Gulf Breeze case to the adventures of    contactee

good.

audience that the ufonauts want control of our souls; that they have been
