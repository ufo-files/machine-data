The Krill papers claim that exchanges between aliens and U.S. scientists were taking place in an
underground base in New Mexico, when a security guard violated an agreement with the aliens by carrying
a weapon into a meeting room where aliens were present. This allegedly started a firefight during which 44
scientists were killed. I find it hard to believe that a member of an elite security guard would be so lax in
discipline, or that aliens would take so much offense at such a breach but both things may be true. I would
like to know: 1.) Who is John Grace; and 2.) What is the nature of his source(s) and how reliable are they?


But of equal interest to me are Bob Lazar's statements about alien craft at Groom/Papoose Lakes and
artificial gravity propulsion systems. Nothing I know about science allows me to find any serious fauh with
any of the statements he has made about these systems. The slowing of time with gravity is a part of
Einstein's Theory of Relativity. As :fur as Instantaneous Propagation, scientists recently carried out an
experiment in which an electron pair was split up, one being forced to go in the opposite direction from the
other. These electron pairs always have opposite spins. A detector was set up on one side of the room to
detect one of the electrons. This detection process changed the electron's spin. Meanwhile, the other
electron was detected at the same time on the other side ofthe room, and it was discovered that its spin
had been changed also. This meant that information had passed instantaneously from the one electron to             ·


the other.Most scientists had previously believed that to be impossible. To me, this indicates the existence
of a field that can change instantaneously, and makes it possible to believe that a gravity field can do the
 same. The increasing stability of elements up to element 115 has been theorized by scientists but not
proven, since no element 115 has been available (except possibly at Groom Lake). If element 115 is ever
found- in a meteorite perhaps- maybe a byproduct of a super-nova- and is found to be stable, this
will be another feather in Bob Lazar's cap. In additi on, claims made by physicist Tom Bearden about
 producing scalar potentials and scalar waves from electromagnetic waves, and about these scalar waves
 being identical with gravitational waves, seem to agree with statements by Lazar that gravity is related to
 electromagnetism.


 In regard to the Timothy GoodiLear dispute, Good's reliability has been questioned by others. It appears
 that Good seriously misinterpretedLear' s statements.

Regarding Paul Bennewi tz, there has been a lot of confusion. Dr. Bennewitz lived adjacent to Kirtland
AFB, from which location he observed a variety of UFO activity. He brought his observations to the
attention of the Air Force, who seem not to have appreciated his interest! They later claimed to have sent
transmissions to his come computer comprising images of alien beings, and convinced Dr. Bennewitz that
he was communicating with these alleged aliens! As a result of all this, Dr. Bennewitz suffered a nervous
breakdown and spent some time recuperating in a sanitariurn. It is not clear to me whether the story of the
murder of the 44 scientists was disinformation sent to Dr. Bennewitz or whether he got that from some
other source. However, if Bob Lazar says he later read this in a Naval Intelligence briefing, this gives it
more credibility. Lazar impresses me as being very intelligent, honest and taking great care to be as
accurate as possible.


Then there is the MJ-12/General James Doolittle connection. For those not familiar with it, MJ-12 was
supposed to be a super-secret government group formed to investigate UFOs in response to the crash of a
disc near Roswell in 1947. At the 1989 Las Vegas MUFON Symposium, I overheard a conversation
between Dr. John Altschuler, an MD. who has been researching livestock mutilations, and physicist
Stanton Friedman. While I do not recall any of the rest of the conversation, one of them, I believe Dr.
Altschuler, told the other that Gen. Jimmy Doolittle was a close friend of the William Lear fiunily (ofLear­
Jet fame), and that Mrs. Lear (John Lear's mother) visited Gen. Doolittle and asked him, "Was there an
MJ-12?" Gen. Doolittle replied, "Well, that was a long time ago, but yes, there was anMJ-12. "At the
time, I felt that I was overhearing what was probably intended to be a confidential conversation, and I don't
believe I have told anyone else about hearing this. In this connection, it is of interest that, according to
Friedman and Berliner's book Crash at Corona, Jimmy Doolittle had made a study of the "foo-fi ghters,"
small UFOs seen during WWII, and had been involved with the Swedish "ghost rocker" sightings of 1946.
