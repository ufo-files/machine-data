KUNGLIGA VÄSTERNORRLANDS REGIS REGEMENTSEXPEDITION

Department I

To regiment office I 21

Sollefteå

9/7

CLASSIFIED

I am hereby pleased to announce that I, on Tuesday, July 9th, shortly after 14.30 pm, observed a luminous phenomenon at a distance of about 200 meters. It appeared like a fireball with a tail and had arrived from the south. I watched it for about 15 seconds, after which it burned out and ultimately disappeared.

YOURS SINCERELY,

Karin Håkansson

Västby Kvarnån.

Tel. 42 Guxås
