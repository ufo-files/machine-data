                                    GONE BUT NOT FORGOTTEN


    Our last edition went out just before the tragic news reached us that Graham Birdsall, the
    Editor of UFO Magazine and International Conference speaker, had died on September
    19th after a sudden brain hemorrhage on the 11th.      In spite of vain attempts to revive him
    including a 7 hour operation, Graham died of heart failure at the age of 49.


    Readers may not be aware that Graham started out as a member and investigator for
    Contact lnt. (UK) in the late 1970s.   I have set out a review of his contributions to Ufology
    overleaf.


    We also lost Gordon Creighton, best known as the former editor of Flying Saucer Review, in
    August.     A former diplomat with extensive knowledge of China, Gordon spoke several
    languages and edited FSR for many years.       Gordon had in fact reached the great age of 95
    and had only recently passed on the FSR mantle, so he must have still been editing into his
    90s!


    Two great men gone- who can replace them?


    Recently articles have arrived here and over the Internet mentioning names and feuds over
    UFO issues.    As CIUFOR has no direct knowledge of the arguments involved, we won't
    publish details unless it affects our group.   Every UFO organisation has periodic outbreaks
    of bickering usually over differences in emphasis and style.     Where you have a range of
    persons getting together, all with searching minds and differing approaches to the subject
    and no "Holy Bible" which tells you what is right and what is wrong - disagreements are
    inevitable. Occasionally they get nasty!


    We are fortunate to live at a time when free speech in Britain is universal.        Over many
    periods of history utterings about "flying saucers" and "extraterrestrials" would be regarded as
    "heresies" and the proponents of them, "heretics".     The poor unfortunates would be lead
    away, subjected to torture and probably execution in the most vile form.


    Our UFO feuds are probably direct descendents of the religious persecutions of yesteryear.
    "Our way is right", (the only one), "God is on our side" type of nonsense which tended to
    result in the removal of the opposing parties.       Whereas religion was all powerful and
    controlled the fabric of society in Britain until about 150 years ago, nowadays most areas of
    expression can be tolerated.    The arrival of the Internet has progressed this concept above
    and beyond anything previously experienced to a point where unwholesome activities such as
    paedophilia have been able to manifest unchecked until recently.     Yes of course, mountains
    of UFO material abound on the "Net" showing many wish to express themselves on the
    subject, but not be constrained by "rules" of being inside a group.         Unfortunately now
    picking out the wheat from the chaff is now almost impossible.


    Make the most of these times folks - they may only be a brief period in our history before
    darkness descends again -----
