                                                  Our Focus
  A topic is appropriate for Ufomind if it involves a specific claim about the physical universe which defies the laws of
  conventional science, economics or politics. Within this broad definition lie all manner of fiercely held human beliefs,
  from UFOs to psychic powers to alternative health and get-rich-quick schemes.

            UFOs -- Are they real, imagined or both?
            Parapsychology -- Remote viewing, telepathy and other unexplained exchanges of information
            Spirits and General Paranormal -- Ghosts, religious miracles and other otherworldly claims that are more
            spiritual and emotional than informational
            Conspiracy -- Hidden control
            Alternative Health -- Healing methods not recognized by medical science
            Weird Physics -- Free energy, perpetual motion, alchemy and theories of matter not recognized by conventional
            physics
            Religion -- Paranormal claims made by established religions
            Forteana -- Bigfoot, Nessie and other unproven creatures and unexplained physical events
            Ancient Mysteries and Fringe Archaeology -- Unconventional beliefs about man's past
            Places -- Haunted places, UFO claims, local history, travel guides and other information connected to a specific
            location
            People -- Prominent personalities in any of the above fields
            Our Bookstore offers new and used books in all of the topics above and more

  On this website, we do not try to distinguish between "true" and "false" beliefs; we only try to record and categorize
  them and give nature an opportunity to take its course. Ufomind promotes neither belief nor disbelief: This website is
  simply a forum where beliefs can be expressed and the facts investigated.

  We do not try to record "unprovable" beliefs or those that are purely spiritual. For example, most established religions,
  although indeed "paranormal," are inherently unprovable and difficult to categorize, so Ufomind is not a useful forum
  for theological debate. However, if members of any religion make a specific, modern paranormal claim -- like faith
  healing, exorcism or coming Armageddon -- then that aspect of the faith does belong on Ufomind. We also have a
  special interest in recently founded religious movements such as Mormonism and Scientology where the original
  miraculous events are still open to investigation.
