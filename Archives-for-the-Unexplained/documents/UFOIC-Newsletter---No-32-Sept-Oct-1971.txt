Excerpts from letter from Mr.Paul Litchfield, Flat 3, 2 Belmore St., Burwood, NSW, 2134. (6.6.71.)
       I would like to tell you about a sighting of two objects which I believe were UFO's. I was walking down·Aibert Street,
Strathfield, NSW, towards Strathfield Station at about 6 p.m. on 22nd May, 1969, when I noticed a long cigar-shaped silver
coloured object on my left about 1000 ft. high. The length was about the size of a 10c. piece. The object was moving towards
the station. Just before reaching it the object made a sharp turn towards the left, where it made a half-circle until it was
parallel to Albert Street but going in the opposite direction. The object stopped just above Parramatta Road- I believe, I
can't be sure, opposite the station. There it was joined by another object which was golden in colour, disc-shaped and approx.
one-third the size of the silver cigar.' This golden disc came from the direction of Homebush Station, until it reached the cigar.
The two UFO's remained stationary at the same height, approx. 1000 ft., for three minutes. Then both craft banked to the
right and disappeared into the sky at a tremendous speed. During all this time there was no apparent noise from these objects
- though probably if there was any noise it would have been drowned out by the traffic and trains.
       This was not the first sighting of a strange object in the sky. The first was on 22.9.68, when my mother and I made the
sighting. It was of a very bright white light with a blue halo round it. This object was clearly visible for about two hours on
this date and for the following two months. (Presume he means same DATE on following two months? - Ed.) After a period
of two hours the object just rose into the sky and disappeared. The object always appeared about 7.30 - 9.30. My mother
left a record of this sighting in a note-book which we still have.

      I interviewed many people in Cairns, mostly as a direct result of several paragraphs in the local Cairns Post newspaper.
Sightings are still going on, in the higher ground, and on the more remote beaches and I got more than one account of an
"Abominable Snowman" like creature seen in the Kamerunga area, a few miles out of Cairns. I formed the opinion that a
prolonged stay in this general area would well prove to be a bonanza and towards this end I have formed plans to set out
in November of this year and extend the investigation to an "around Australia" project. This will commence from Sydney
and follow mainly Highway One, passing through the most interesting country as far as UFO's are concerned. Plans are
made to spend many days at such places as Mt. Gambier, S.A., around Perth and right through the heart of the Kimberley
Range in the far north of W.A., then cutting down to Alice Springs and across Queensland to Cairns. I will be accompanied
by an assistant and we will be travelling in a four wheel drive vehicle, carrying some elaborate camera equipment and other
detection devices.

       The object of this particular phase of the investigation will be to collect as much data, new and not so new, and try
to obtain some clear close up pictures of the UFO phenomena. We will be recording on tape some of the more interesting
interviews w.ith people who have had recent sightings. The investigation has been given the name of UFO-Shadow and we
will be displaying an emblem on both sides of the vehicle with a pertinent design. This we think will be of benefit to us as
it will encourage some people to make contact with us, that perhaps we would otherwise miss out. Needless to say, UFOIC
will be kept informed of movements and progress.

      We are endeavouring to obtain sponsors and already we have several interested parties. However, the major finance
I am supplying myself. With lots of luck, a little skill, plus the valuable help of all the many interested people in the various
areas we may get some valuable photographic evidence. At worst we will perhaps have been instrumental in fostering added
interest in the intensely absorbing subject of Ufology. This can only be good to the many UFO groups around Australia
and in particular the UFO IC-Sydney.
