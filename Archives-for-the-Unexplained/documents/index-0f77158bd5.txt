    Our Review                                                                                    Opinion of the webmaster, subject to debate

  This is a collection of 1,000 irreverent quotations, anecdotes, and interviews about romance, sex and love's
  myriad delusions. If you're deliriously in love, if you're under the influence of the powerful drug of
  romantic passion, if your heart fills with joy at the prospect of spending the rest of your natural life with
  the same person... this book ain't for you. If you are currently "out of love", then you'll find this book
  refreshing. Maybe love ain't all its supposed to be -- and you're among the lucky ones. -- tm
