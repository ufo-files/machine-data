Dear Aileen :
 Keith Powell's artistic impression, featured on the March, 1992 cover issue of TML, is definitely worthy
of praise. He portrays his character, a wise old man, rejoicing in the birth of an alien child and enjoying
an audience with two other neighborly alien humanoids. Observe how blessed they all look sitting
Buddha-style with wide open, outstretched arms with palms turned upward facing the night sky,
accepting the beam of light bestowed upon them; and observe, too, our little alien friends, one quietly
caught up in the rapture of the magical moment, and the other with his index finger pointed straight up
toward the mystical sky. A revealing gesture signifying the arrival of another newborn alien child,
something for all of us to be blessed about. Mr. Powell clearly demonstrates through his artistic
impressions, that our Love and Light (a treasured saying of yours, Aileen) is inseparable from theirs,
because, after all, isn't it really all "One Love" and "One Light" under "One Universal Family"? For
that, we thank him. A true visionary artist.
  Speaking of TML covers, and giving praise, someone sure thinks highly of our alien comrades, so much
so that s/he picture one amongst our statue presidents on the cover of TML May, 1992 issue. July's
cover is definitely making a statement also. This alien is busting through the White House with half­
moonlit eyes, shattering its governmental age-old conspiracy cover-up of the UFO-ET phenomenon. On
both covers, the artistic impressions state a clear common message- no matter who sits at the helm, or
what political bodies control the power, no man or authority can keep the reality of their existence
hidden from the masses or from themselves, for there exists no armor capable of shielding the truth.
Compliments to the designers of these covers, and to all the contributors not mentioned here, for their
manifestations are equally significant.
   What! about that article, "Deliberate Deceptions: The Big Sur UFO Filming," contributed by Bob
Jacobs, PhD, in the April, 1 992 issue of TML? He does an excellent job recounting his participation in
an "official U.S. Government-ordered UFO cover-up" He says he was an officer in the United States Air
Force (USAF) at the time in question ( 1964). He premises his story by reminding us how vicious and
devastating the so-called "Press" is in reporting and misreporting information concerning UFO activity.
That's a given by now. A lengthy discussion is then had describing the various devices used (with the
Boston University telescope being the most important piece of machinery worthy of mention here);
informing us that in September, 1 964 he was responsible for "establishing a long range tracking site at
Big Sur, California", providing Aerospace Division engineers and other personnel missile launch
photograph to their needs, while also enlightening us of the following: 1) that there was "a total of 1 1
launches" from the Vandenberg Air Force Base in California "during a 30-day test run in September of
1/1964"; 2) that he believes one of those 9/64 launches was of an Atlas-F missile, equipped with an
"American dummy atomic warhead," which was attacked and shot down with laser beam by an UFO, an
attack which forms the basis of the USAF "governmental-ordered UFO cover-up;" and 3) that this whole
program was some type of "Nike-Zeus (?)" test to "develop an anti-missile missile" for an underlying
mission to establish an "enemy radar-defeating system."
  Lt. Bob Jacobs says that this, almost third decade cover-up, transpired the following day subsequent
to the filming when he was ordered to view the Kinescope recording at the Vandenberg base by Major
Mansmann. When asked, "...what the Hell was that?", Lt. Jacobs said "It looks to me like we got a UFO."
Major Mansmann, according to the article, then said, "Well, let's just say it never happened... this
footage... never took place." Here lies the challenge folks- who can come up with that footage...?
  Bob Jacobs also drew six (6) conclusions, which you will find on page 10. His first four (4) are fair
statements in light of his article, but check out numbers 5 and 6. Aren't they interesting statements?
There's no evidence to support his conclusions that the attack alluded to was "prearranged" by
"someone in our government;" or that extraterrestrials gave us such a "demonstration.. for our benefit
for some reason." According to the article, the event just happened. There is nothing to suggest that it
was prearranged by anyone. Nor within three decades has there been any evidence shown to even
suggest that communication from an alien has been received to confirm the conclusion that the event
was a benefit demonstration. Yet, however, circumstantial evidence does suggest that conclusion
number 6 may, in fact, have a scintilla of credibility to it since there exists record and testimonial
evidence showing that such launches were, indeed, made during that September 1 964 period. The
actual footage will dispel doubts and further lend support to the multiple accounts attesting to the firm

           Dear Aileen :
            I thought you might be interested in glancing at the enclosed article from Science magazine, because
           of its implication that if our science were to advance for another few thousand years or so, we would be
           able to fully understand how it is possible for a person to he whisked out of her bedroom right through
           a wall or closed window and into a hovering UFO. Physicists have known for quite some time that this
           sort of phenomenon is possible in the laboratory on the smallest of scales for the smallest particles. It's
           called quantum-mechanical "tunneling." Now, for the first time, some are learning that it is possible for
           this to occur within the macroscopic world (large scale, human-sized objects). As you see from the
           article, they call it macroscopic quantum tunneling (MOT). In order to be at all possible, the object
           being tunneled has to somehow be decoupled from its environment. We're just at the earliest stages of
           this -- learning that it should be theoretically possible without yet knowing how to do it outside of
           magnetic crystals no larger than about 2 millionths of a centimeter in diameter and at very low
           temperatures. No doubt a breakthrough in psychic understanding and abilities is also required before
           we'll make much more progress.
              It's interesting that spokesmen for science will debunk anything that 20th-Century science can't
           understand the workings of, even though they should know that if alien intelligence and evolution are
           involved, what they could do should seem like magic to us. So, whenever science comes up with
           something "new" like this, they ought to take some notice in regards to the abduction phenomenon,
           even though it may only mean that instead of being 3,000 years behind certain alien science, we are
           only 2,995 years behind.
             Best regards, Jim Deardorff

        The star of Belen was the star that guided the Three Magician Kings in their journey to Belen. Upon
      posing itself at the place where the child Jesus was, this star preceded the Three Magician Kings.
        Those who seek an explanation for this phenomenon, partially natural, would think that the same is a
      triple conjunction of the planets of Jupiter and Saturn which occurred on October 1 , Year 7 B.C. Others
      think that this incident occurred because of the comet Halley, which was last seen at the end of the
      month of August, twelve years before Jesus Christ was born. Others prefer to talk about a star that
      shined exclusively in that period of time to only disappear afterwards and, finally, others prefer to say
      that such phenomenon occurred for the benefit of the Three Magician Kings and could only be seen
      exclusively by them.
        The Bible states that these Three Magician Kings were experts in these matters. Also it has to be
      mentioned that the old world was totally fascinated with the idea of the expectation of the "Messiah"
      and that a "light" shining way up in the sky would be the signal of His arrival to the earth to save all
      people from sin and destruction. But, there is an important detail stated in the Bible. The same says:
      "And the star posed in the place where the child Jesus was". Could it be possible that such star was a
      flying object commanded by superior intelligence? The search for a theory that can explain the journey
      of the "star" constitutes the most important challenging for all of us... for all of those who are thinking
      souls. Which possibly could have been the size of that "star" that moved with its own intelligence and
      that also knew before hand where exactly to pose?
        All these questions and theories go towards one direction: the "star'' was a flying object guided by
      superior race to indicate the Three Magician Kings that a much more higher "extraterrestrial" would
      soon be born. This extraterrestrial is Christ whom came to earth to give us light and knowledge!!
        Willie Durand Urbina, Puerto Rican Research Group, Carolina PR. 00987

            After we met I remembered that I had been keeping a journal at the time of the sighting. It was mostly
          for the model airplanes that I was rather prolific in designing and building but other stuff found its way
          in too.
            Going back to March 3, 1972 I found an entry. There is much more than I remembered of the incident.
          Here it is. I put in comments to clarify some of the original statements and did some editing to make
          things clearer to someone besides myself. I tried not to change any content.

            I was originally notified of the object at about 7:20 p.m. by John S., who had seen it the previous night.
          I gathered up the telescope (200 x 3 in reflector), camera and binoculars (8x) and went to the B.'s front
          yard with Mr. S. (and the rest of my family). (The B.s lived about a quarter mile away to the north. As I
          remember there were several neighbors there already.) At about 7:30 the first exposure was started.
          Several of us observed the light through binoculars and the telescope. · The object appeared through
          the telescope to be two large oblong greenish lights. The one on the right oriented vertically, the one
          on the left horizontally. It was impossible to focus them clearly, although nearby stars were used as a
          focusing guide. I would guess that the magnitude was about -5 to -3. The intensity was great enough
          to produce light flairs in the telescope. The only other object to do that has been the moon. It was far
          brighter than any other object in the sky. Mr. B. said that he had seen a similar light on a few occasions
          going as far back as fall of the previous year.
            At 8:05 pm the first exposure was ended and we and Mr. S. went to our respective homes. I set up the
          camera in the front yard and started the second exposure at 8 : 15 pm and we continued to observe the
          light both with the telescope (now set to 60x) and binoculars. At around 8:20 the S.'s and the G.'s came
          over to look though the telescope and binoculars. The second exposure was ended at around 8:50 pm
          and the third started three minutes later. The last exposure finished when the light set at 9:33 p.m.
            During this time Mr. S. called Andrews A.F.B. to see if any of their operations would account for the
          light. They said that there was a NASA blimp operating in the area (!).
             The next night (March 4, 1972) we kept an eye out to see if the light would return. This night was
          partially overcast, which made observation difficult. Between 6:45 and 7:15 pm a light similar to the
          one of the previous night did appear through the clouds. Observation through the telescope showed
          that this one lacked the "tail" that the light of the night before had. Thickening clouds obscured the sky
          after about 8:22 pm, the light was still there at that time.
            This about covers the journal entries. I believe Mr. S. also called the FAA Air Route Traffic Control
          Center where he worked, to see if there was anything happening on the radar, and apparently there was
          not. In his position I would imagine that he would get straight answers (he, after all, worked there).
            As I said when we last met, we lived in a fairly rural area. Most of the families knew and kept in touch
          with each other. When something interesting was going on (on any subject) the word got out
          surprisingly fast. Sitting outside in the evenings was popular with just about everyone. There were no
          street lights so the night sky was easy to see in all its splendor, although it is often hazy. It was normal
          to get together and watch for satellites or meteors when the weather was nice. (Charlotte and I still go
          over to Mom's house to sit on the deck and watch stars.)
            The people involved are all normal folks and some have an aviation background. John S. was at the
          time an air traffic controller, and my Dad and I were both pilots (I still am). Tom G. was (and still is) a
          cinematographer for the National Park Service and Mr. B. was a small businessman (I remember little
          about him). Not the sort of folks that would misidentify a blimp, or most anything else in the air (or for
          that matter be fooled by the explanation of a blimp).
