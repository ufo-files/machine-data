                            The Foreign Operations and Government Information Subcommittee today

  voted to recommend that S. 1160, to establish a Federal Public Records Law,

  be reported favorably to the House of Representatives by the Committee on

  Government Operations.                     The bill will now be referred to the Full Committee

   for further consideration.
                             S. ll6o passed by the Senate on October 13, 1965, was approved by the

   Subcommittee in place of H.R. 5012 (Moss) and 22 similar House bills.                            Hearings

   were held on the House bills last year.                        The Senate bill contains the same

   public records provisions as the House bills but adds requirements for publishing

   official records under the Administr ative Procedure Act of 1946.

                             "This legislation is the end product of a 10 year study of government

   information , " Congre ssman John E. Moss, Subcommittee Chairman, said.                         "When

   this measure is enacted i nto law it will, for the first time in our nation's

   history, guarantee the people 's right to know the facts of government."

                             S.    1160 was introduced by Senator Edward V. Long of Missouri and

   co-sponsored by 21 other Senators .
                             The House Committee on Government Operations is under the Chairmanship

   of Congressman William L. Dawson .
