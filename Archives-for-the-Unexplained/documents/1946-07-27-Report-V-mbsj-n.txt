Omma, July 27, 1946.

To TT, Stockholm.


I would like to inform you of a strange occurrence which I believe might awaken your curiosity.  I recently observed a tube-looking object, which traveled westward at great speed and, presumably, fell into the depths of Wåmbsjön, though some of its solder-like fragments caused a fire by the adjacent road. The incident occurred between 5 and 6 'o clock.

Yours faithfully,

Jöns Andersson

PS. My neighbour, Mr. Ola Jönsson, acted as co-observer.
