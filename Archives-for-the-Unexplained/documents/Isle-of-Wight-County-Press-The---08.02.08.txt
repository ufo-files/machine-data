UFO ANSWER COULD LIE IN SPACE

By Clare Wall - Friday, February 8, 2008  AN EXPLANATION could be on the horizon in the continuing saga of UFO sightings. According to the website Heavens Above, the silent yellow glowing light travelling from West to East could be the International Space Station (ISS). On a clear night the satellite can look as bright as the brightest star in the sky, Sirius. Since the County Press reported a sighting by Frank Webb a fortnight ago, other Islanders have come forward to report their own UFO experiences. And Marion Hicks’ description of her sighting does appear to fit in with the ISS movements. Mrs Hicks, of St Mary’s Close, Ryde, noticed a cigar-shaped light in the sky, moving West to East, over the area of Whitefield Woods. But a more unusual experience was reported to us last week by a man who wished to remain anonymous. The man told the County Press he has seen a triangular UFO on a regular basis for around 20 years, after first seeing it coming out of the sea off the coast of Ventnor. He said he first saw the craft while fishing with a friend, and claims it has flown over his house, a matter of metres from his roof. “I have seen it quite a few times. It shines a white light, mainly, but there are red and blue lights when it slows down,” he said. “There is a groove around the edge of the triangle but you cannot see any other markings. The lights are suspended below it and it looks like glass, but it isn’t. “If you look at it through glass, you can see a white vapour trail.” The sightings coincide with information released by the Ministry of Defence, showing the number of UFO sightings rocketing from 97 in 2006 to 135 last year.
