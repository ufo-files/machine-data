This is not a joke!

Several months ago while I was having a heated discussion on UpDates regarding the Alien Autopsy, I
received an e-mail from a person I didn't know. It was simple and to the point. He asked me to keep
defending the AA and that Santilli's creatures, depicted in the Fox TV show, were not hoaxed nor faked.
He knew, because he had seen the creatures himself He had been at Roswell when the crash occurred!
Since that time I've kept in contact with him and we've exchanged information, but he never told me the
complete story until several days ago. Here is his version of events, Roswell,July 3rd,1947.

An MP's Story

this. Here's some information for you to start with. The crash I saw was at what they call the Roswell
location. It was not the Brazel location, but on the Plains (of San Agustin).

I was stationed at the base and was taken to the site with several other men to secure the area. I was an MP
(Military Policeman). All of the men on guard with me that morning were MP s at the base. I have my

From the Sunday Mail 5th March 2000

A patch of water in the Gulf of Carpentaria is becoming known as ·Australia's Bermuda Triangle. Nine people
have gone missing in the area in the past ten years and their bodies have never been found. Authorities
stumbled on the coincidence while searching for the latest missing person. A diver was last seen three weeks
ago when he descended into the water between Bentinck and Sweers Islands.

On Wednesday, November 24th 1999 at about 9.15am, a plane disappeared when it left Mornington Island.
Searchers found debris in the waters around Bentinck Island, but the six people on board were never found.

On Wednesday, February 16th about 9.20am, a diver disappeared after descending to attach a crane head to
an anchor in a channel between Bentinck and Sweers islands.                Twenty minutes after descending, his
workmates pulled a nylon rope into the boat that had been tied onto his arm.

In another case two Department of Primary Industries workers went out on patrol in a boat about ten years
ago and never came back. Their floating dingy was found a short time later. Their bodies were never found.

State Emergency Service volunteer Tim Trad said "The area was plagued by unlucky events. There have been
quite a few planes that have crashed in the Gulf The last crash where six people disappeared was highly
unusual. The diver disappeared in the exact same spot". Mr Trad also said that despite an extensive search,
it was bizarre that the main body of the plane could not be-found. He said the area was dangerous for pilots
as the horizon seemed to disappear. "The pilots that come through place often speak of the fact that the sea
is so calm and when you get overcast weather, you can't see the horizon� it just disappears."

The Gulf is home to large sharks and fishermen regularly report seeing saltwater crocodiles 1Okm out to sea!
