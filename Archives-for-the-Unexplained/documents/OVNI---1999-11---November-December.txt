              OVNI TO CONTINUE INTO THE NEXT MILLENNIUM

      Over the past few months we have seen a· number of UFO groups falling by the wayside either through
      financial difficulties or through a decline � membership.     The UFO subject appears to be a tantalising
      mixture of vague sightings, half truths and dis-information. We believe that in spite of the difficulties in
      trying to sort the 'wheat from the chaff' in the many reports that we receive, there is continuing evidence
      of Alien intrusion into our environment.

      Reports ofUFO's of all shapes and sizes, not only the FT's, continue to be received by our group. Reports
      forwarded by group members have been particularly helpful and this has enabled us to keep our eye on the
      wider UFO picture, not just the local Derbyshire scene. We have only just received a brief report of an
      incident over the Rockies in Canada and this is being followed up at the present time (see back page).

      The month of October, was the end of our financial year and also decision time for the continuation of the
      PRA and the OVNijoumal. It was gratifying to see that the hard work put into the group throughout the
      past year has been appreciated by a vast amount of the membership. "Thank you for the excellent job you
      do for the group", ''Thanks and best wishes" are typical of the comments that accompanied th� many
      membership renewals that came in through the post. Thank YOU members!

      The past year has also seen a proliferation of 'in fighting' within the UFO community. It must be to the
      delight of various unnamed sources, who probably now find it unnecessary to feed us with mis­
     information. They need only sit back and watch with relief as UFO groups and their thinking become
     fragmented as they argue amongst themselves. As a group the PRA have refused to be baited and involved
      in personal vilification. The intemet has been flooded with pages of vitriolic antagonism in the past few
     months, an example of our difficulty as a human species in living peacefully together. There is little sign of
     the awakening of the human identity to become more spiritual in our understanding of the Universe.

     We have just heard that Diane and Peter Shepherd are giving up the 'Northants UFO Forum' group,
     because of ill-health. It seems unlikely to continue as there is nobody willing to take over the reins and
     work involved in running the group. Diane and Peter recently gave our members a talk on their contactee
     experiences which resulted in them looking anew at the meaning of life on Earth. As Dr. John E.Mack
     (Harvard University U.S.A.) once said       ''We need to change our notions of reality to understand this
     (UFO) subject."

     There is no doubt that to witnesses who experience a UFO encounter, the experience is very real. Our
     current thinking has undergone a change in how to analyse witness reports. It is apparently obvious that in
     some situations a witness may have observed something in another plane/dimension. A phenomenon which
     has been called 'differential perception'. Some witnesses will see something and others may not, some will
     see Qolours and other not. Our idea has always been that for something to be 'real' everyone must see it in
     the same way, but it is now apparent that there are realities that are differentially perceived

     People often ask the question ''Why are you so interested in the subject of UFO's?" The answer is surely,
     "If we are being visited by some other species, even if this is only a possibility, why are you not interested
     in this yourself?"

                            UFO HOVERS OVER AZERBAIJAN

         A respected Doctor of Sciences in geology and mineralogy El'chin Hallilov, has used
         an advanced Sony camcorder to capture 40 minutes of video footage of a huge
         glowing UFO hovering over the Capitol of Azerbaijan.
                A   conference   was    being   held   for   astronomers       at . the   Shemahinskoj
         Astrophysical Observatory in Azerbaijan during the sighting. The 40-minute video
         was taken to the meeting and viewed by astronomical experts who were amazed by
         the high quality digital video. The clarity and length of the tape that seems to show a
         huge hovering craft shocked them. The tape is considered sensational because it
         shows a large flashing brightly lit blue craft hanging over the city for several hours,
         starting at 21:45 on September 24 1999. The astronomers could not explain it away as
         natural phenomena such as plasma or a comet. The film then shows the craft
         magnified 300 times showing a surprising technical complex device. It consists of
         three main parts. The front is in the shape of a sphere; the rear of the craft rotates
         around an axis passing all along the object. It also seems to radiate high power heat
         and radiation.

                The object turns and it is possible to see its full length, and then it is a full ·
         sphere with a· truncated bottom. There are flashing lights and portions seem to rotate
         showing two plate like objects. At one time, it appears as an isosceles triangle and
         then forms into an eclipse and disappears.
                Th s is a rough translation of the Russian article in the Moscow newspaper
         'Komsomolskaya Pravda'. The astronomers agreed that natural objects do not take on

                 Farmers Remarks Back Up PRA Crop Circle Theory
                                 By Omar Fowler
 On the 5th of October, I gave a talk to the Derbyshire 'Ecclesbourne Farmers Discussion Society. The
 meeting took place in the pleasant surroundings of the 'Cross Keys' public house at Turnditch. A talk had
 been requested on the subject of 'UFO's, but I thought it an ideal opportunity to include a few photographic
 slides of 'Corn Circles', a subject more likely to create an interest with the audience.


 I first began my investigation into 'natural' Corn Circles in 1985, in company with Busty Taylor, Pat Delgado
 and Colin Andrews. I became fascinated with the fact that there were isolated standing stems of wheat (or
 barley) in an otherwise flattened Crop Circle. This was years before the advent of the complicated
 'Pictograms' (see pages 12 & 13). It had been established that the single Crop Circles had been appearing in
 farmers fields for well over a hundred years.                 I moved abroad in 1986 and it was not until 1990, when I
 returned the U.K. to a home in Derbyshire, that I was able to continue with my study of the Crop Circle
 mystery.   The forming of the PRA proved to be a watershed in the advancement of the theory that the
 'natural' circles were being formed by a form of High Frequency (microwave) vortex spiralling down from
 the Ionosphere when the conditions were right. Members                             of the PRA contributed to this theory and a result
 of this, I published a short illustrated paper in September 199 1, on the probability of H/F microwave activity
 being influential in the formation of Crop Circles.                       I was pleased to receive approving responses from a
 number of eminent researchers, including Prof Y.H. Ohtsuki ofWaseda University, Tokyo (Japan), Michael
