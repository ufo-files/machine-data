)'   content. Because the news clippings are deteriorating very fast, the history that they represent will soon
     become unavailable unless someone has the patience to find them again by going through the microfilm
     files of the various newspapers represented here. That is unlikely. So, I am placing them here on the chance
     that an interested ufologist of the late 21 11 or early 22nd Centuries will find this issue in some library or
     collection. Given that we publish less than 200 copies (a small subscription base that I would like to
     enlarge), I suppose that is unlikely, but I am going to try. In the meantime I hope that you, the reader, find
     these articles as interesting as I did.
