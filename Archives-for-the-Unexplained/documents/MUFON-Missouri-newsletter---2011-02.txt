         Kathleen Marden's books "Captured - the Betty and Barney Hill UFO
         Experience (with Stanton Friedman," and "Science Was Wrong" (with
         Stanton Friedman) have rave reviews. Kathleen spoke at the will be speaking
         at the 2009 MOU Conference. Kathleen has been heavily involved in MU-
         FON and served on the board of directors and director of field investigator training for 10
         years. Available at www.questig.com.

         Stanton Friedman, speaker at our 2008 conference and world-renowned UFO researcher,
         has a number of books in print, including Flying Saucers and Science; Science was Wrong
         (with Kathleen Marden), Crash at Corona, and Top Secret/ Majic. His books and DVD's
         are available at www.questig.org and www.stantonfriedman.com.

         Jason Offutt's "What Lurks Beyond: The Paranormal in Your Backyard " discusses
         ghosts, aliens, a time traveler, and other paranormal events. You may also like
         "Darkness Walks: The Shadow People Among Us," and Haunted Missouri: A Ghostly
         Guild to the Show-Me-State's Most Spirited Spots are getting great reviews. Jason was a
         speaker at last year's MOU Conference and is a member of MUFON. Available at
         www.jasonoffutt.com.

         Heidi Hollis' book "The Secret War: The Heavens Speak of the Battle," is about
         Shadow People and their agenda. Heidi is a speaker at the 2009 Conference. She has been on
         Coast-to-Coast, Jeff Rense, and numerous other T.V. and radio shows, and has worked with
         Stephen Spielburg on "Taken." Available at www.jesusisnojoke.com.

         Margie Kay's book "The Ghost Hunter's Field Guide" is now available at
         www.lulu.com. Kay describes what ghosts are, what it is like in the spirit realm, ghost hunt-
         ing tools and techniques, how to protect yourself from negative energies, how to build a ghost
         telephone, haunted locations to visit, and more. Also "Haunted Independence," which is a
         book about haunted sites in and near Independence, Missouri Both books are available at
         lulu.com. Visit www.margiekay.com for more information.

         Stan Romanek's book "Messages," out in May, 2009, is already on the best seller lists. He
         was on Larry King Live, Coast-to-Coast, and interviewed for 20/20. Stan will be speaking for
         at least three hours at the MOU Conference, and will show video and photos of aliens and
         UFO's. Stan's is the most documented UFO story ever told. Jack Kasher, who will also be
         speaking at the conference, is one of the scientists who studied his case. Available at
         www.stanromanek.com

         Bill Hamilton, K.C. resident, long-time UFO researcher and field investigator for MUFON,
         has written several good books and publications including- Space, Time, and Gravity; Cen-
         ter of the Vortex; Telos, the Cosmic Computer; Geometry of the Grid; Close Encounter
         Report; Alien Magic; The Pheonix Lights; Project Aquarius: Time Travel Now!; and
         Cosmic Top Secret. Please contact Bill directly to find out how to purchase these books.
