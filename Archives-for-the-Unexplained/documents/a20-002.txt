  Let me introduce myself.   My name is Michael Larsen, and I own the domain

  possible opportunity at AREA51.NET Please read on.
  A few months ago, I was surfing the web for info about UFO's, Area51,
  Roswell, the Philadelphia Experiment, and other such curiosities, and
  stumbled upon www.area51.com. To my dismay, the site was not developed,
  and had only one page with a poorly drawn logo on it. So, I tried checking
  out www.area51.org. At least the site had something to do with area51,
  however there was very little information available, and no real point for
  it's existence outside of a plug for the ufo watchers organization. After
  doing multiple searches using Yahoo, InfoSeek, and other search engines, I
  found plenty of content, but at sites with domains that really do no
  justice to the content.
  This is where AREA51.net comes in. After doing some domain searches, I
  found that Area51.net was not taken, so I purchased this domain with the
  hope of gathering a large amount of information about government
  conspiracies, ufo's, and other paranormal subject matter. I am currently
  looking for a partner or group of partners to help mold a structure, and
  create a Mega-site where web surfers can find the earlier mentioned
  information and resources without having to search all over the web. I
  believe that this site has a big potential to draw hits and advertisers.
  Good domain names are hard to come by these days, and I believe that AREA51
  is a great name to build on. I'm not writing this note asking for
  financial partnerships, rather, to open up a dialog with good content
  providers, such as yourself. I do not want to create a site of links only,
  neither do I want to steal content or create all new content. I believe
  there is a great deal of content out there, sites where individuals have
  already spent many hours researching their subjects, and developing their
  site. There are all kinds of possibilities for AREA51.NET. If you would
  like to discuss some of these ideas, have ideas of your own, or simply have
  questions or comments about this project, please contact me via email.
  Thank you for your time,
