Case xxx.
A glistening object without wings

Time: Sunday, June 16, 16.45 hours
Place: Svenshögen, Ödsmål, in the province of Bohuslän (county: Göteborgs och Bohuslän)

An immediate result of the June 12 memo was the following letter: 

”The Western Airbase area, Gothenburg. Nr 201. To the Air Defence department at the Defence Staff: 

”Before the arrival of memo concerning [routines for] reporting of certain light phenomena, the following report had been phoned in to this Staff. An engineer Berglund, employee of the State Railways [Statens Järnvägar], had, during a walk in the woods by lake St. Hällungen (Bohuslän near Svenshögen. Ödsmål) heard an unusual sound from the air, as from a two-stroke engine. He had stopped and looked up in the direction of the sound and thereby seen an object glistening in the sun at great height, without wings, which hastily moved in direction SSE-NNW leaving a long trail of smoke. Sky was clear with no clouds, the phenomenon lasted 30 seconds, at the most, and was sighted at 16.45 hours.  Göteborg, June 17, 1946. /signed/ S.H.Liljhagen, chief of staff.

Status: Possible high altitude aircraft
Source: Letter in Defence Staff files
