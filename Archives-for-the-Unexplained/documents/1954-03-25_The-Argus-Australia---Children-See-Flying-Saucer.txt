Argus (Melbourne, Vic. : 1848 - 1957), Thursday 25 March 1954, page 6



         Children                         see
        flying                  saucer
                 Morwell, Wednesday
          Excited     Commercial    Road
        State School      children play-
        ing  in   the   school   yard re-
        ported today that they had
        seen    a   "flying
                         saucer" flash
        over  Morwell.
           Three   of  the children
        Ian Lockwood, Wayne      Snell,
        and    Ken   Hamilton       said  -




        that about    12.45 p.m., with
        the other children, they saw
        a   "silver object" travelling
        at high speed south    of Mor-
        well.
           The object descended    sud-
        denly, and     as  its altitude
        increased       again    at   a       terrific
        rate, flames       and   sparks were
        seen.
          The    three boys' story was
        verified   by   a  number    of
        other children.
