DATE: 03.20.10, 1700 HRS EST
LETTER OF INTRODUCTION

With this writing, it is a privilege and pleasure to announce the grand
opening of the UFO RESEARCH CENTER OF PENNSYLVANIA as
of March 20, 2010.

A group of seasoned investigators in the fields of Ufology,
Paranormal, Crypto-zoology and Abduction has formed to research
the unknowns in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania and elsewhere.

UFORCOP will not only be conducting investigations into past un-
resolved cases, but the organization is also accepting new cases
obtained over our new website, email and US Mail.

UFORCOP is based in Pennsylvania but has Consultants and
Researchers throughout the United States. The organization is
independent of MUFON.

UFORCOP is also proud to exclaim the diversity and professional
backgrounds of the existing membership, which allows us to use a
multi-disciplined approach to cases presented to us.

The active consulting and researcher base membership thus far
includes the following academia……

UFORCOP will hold quarterly meetings at a designated location to be
announced, for the purpose of accepting new cases, and existing
case file management and training.

UFORCOP is also fielding two “Mobile Research Units” fully
equipped to handle any type of investigation.

If you feel that your professional qualifications, training or experience
might in some way provide UFORCOP with additional quality
membership and diversity, please contact us via email, with your
name, contact information and professional resume or background
and experience.

                              OUR GOAL

Is to take a professional and scientific look at cases in an attempt to
bring forth factual, credible, scientific evidence that will document
these unknowns and allow interested parties to better understand and
draw their own conclusions of the evidence we provide.
