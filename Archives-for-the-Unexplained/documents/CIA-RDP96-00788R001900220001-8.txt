     On 16 June 1983, Source CL-08 was interviewed concerning
the location of an individual on 14 June 1983. A photograph
of the individual was shown to Source. The individual was
otherwise unknown to Source. Source stated substantially as
follows:

    On 14 June 1983, the individual in the photograph was in
a room with a red carpet., a very large window, and a
decorative rope which was used to cordon the area. This was
a waiting room and the individual in the photograph was
waiting for a uniformed person (not further identified) who
was to take him some place (not further identified). While
in the waiting room, the individual in the photograph had
with him an important black leather case with two handles
(not further identified).

    The incH vidual in the photograph was met by a second
person, an official representative (not further identified).
The individual in the photograph then went upstairs to a room
with large wooden dark shiny doors (not further identified)
where he met with a "rather short, bald headed man" (not
further identified).
    Source was unable to report meaningful information
concerning this meeting, except to say that it represented an
agreement and an adceptance (not further identified}. Source
provided no further amplifying information as to the
whereabouts _of the person in the photograph on 14 June 1983.
