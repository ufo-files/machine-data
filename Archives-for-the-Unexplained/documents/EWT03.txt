Traces (ie imprints, marks and residues, on the ground and vegetation) ought to provi-

for traces imply that the phenomenon is not something perceived subjectively (belon-

This physical "proof" has been expedited by the "extraterrestrialists" as support for

the phenomenon in terms of a hypotheses without taking account of the physical
