WE   MUST   "LEAD"   THE   SCEPTICS.




     It has been said by one Australian researcher that we must tread a careful

line between the professional scientist and the lunatic fringe in our attempts to

unravel the mystery of the UFO phenomena.




     We must be the ones to quickly remove from public record any case which when

investigated reveals itself to be a hoax, or to have a mundane conventional

explanation.     For by so doing we increase our credibility in the eyes of the

professional scientist and remove any association with the lunatic fringe which

may still linger in people's minds.




     To do this we must be responsive to our needs and our needs only.     If a case

doesn't hold up we should say so.      Take for example the affair of the so-called

"alien honeycomb" last year.     To have such a mundane event occupy so much of our

time was wasteful.




     We must concentrate our endeavours on those cases which do hold up after

investigation and publish the results of these for all interested researchers to

be aware of.   In this regard the full account of the September 30, 1980, Rosedale

Victoria event, carried in our last NEWSLETTER, broke the fine details to the global

UFO community.    We understand an article of the same event will shortly appear in

the internationally renown Flying Saucer Review, but it was published here first.




     There can be no substitute for the self-critical approach, and the NEWSLETTER

intends to bring you as much first hand, well investigated information as possible. l

                                 A   TEN   YEAR   OLD   MYSTERY




An extraordinary   aerial   object was observed in broad daylight at a very low altitude
flying along a street in the western Sydney suburb of Whalan ,       some ten years ago.
Only now are investigations revealing what took place.




      Only because of irritation with a caller to an open line radio forum on the
Sydney station, 2GB, did a woman decide to describe her fascinating experience, some
ten years past.  Bill Chalker, Director of UFO Research (NSW ) was in the studio taking
calls and he encouraged her to contact the group directly.  Fortunately she did and
as a result the sighting is now documented after all this time.

      Bill Chalker and David Reneke undertook the investigation.  Several more possible
confirmatory and independent accounts have since come forward and are being studied.
What seemed at first to have been a single witness close encounter now appears likely to
be an event witnessed by a number of independent observers over a wide region in the
Penrith - St Marys - Mt Druitt area.

      The account given by the woman, was of such a vivid nature, there appears little
chance of a conventional stimulus being the cause.  So far no explanation has become
apparent.

      This year to date UFO Research {SA} has received 25 reports.  Most of these
have been explainable in terms of satellites, meteors, aircraft, birds and the
planets Jupiter and Saturn.


         Two interesting identified cases are of note, both of which occurred in
March-

      A lady rang at 2245 hrs on March 5 and said that she was watching a red glow
in the sky.  A moments questioning revealed that the glow was in the Southern sky
near the horizon.  However the cause was quickly understood because the glow turned out
to be a fine example of the southern aurora. Auroras are quite rare at our latitude
  °
35  south, and this one was apparently even more spectacular further south according to
one observer at Mount Gambier who we talked to.

      Later on March 24 at 1940 hrs, the group received three calls about a very
spectacular ball of light which travelled from West to East across the northern sky.
Two of the callers were in Adelaide but a third was at Williamstown in the Barossa
Valley to the north of Adelaide.  All reported observing a yellowish ball with an
orange-red-yellow tail, moving soundlessly on a very flat horizontal trajectory.
Duration of this observation varied from 3 seconds to an estimated 30 seconds.   The
almost certain cause of this was a satellite re-entry or bright meteor.  Because of
its brightness and course it is possible that the object would have been visible over a
wide area, possibly even into Victoria.

     One of the most challeng ing questions which baffles the UFO investi gator is :
how do they materialize over all parts of the world suddenly ; without warning with their
