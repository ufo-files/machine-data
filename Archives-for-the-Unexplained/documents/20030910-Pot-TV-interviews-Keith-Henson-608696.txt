  in juxtaposition against one of the more notoriously criminal cults that
  operate in the United States and Europe. The question of which is the
  more destructive -- Cannabis Sativa -- or Scientology is easily answered.

  notion by Pot TV that the putative "war on drugs" itself constitutes a
  cult. The best thing about the interview was Professor Henson's refusal
  to bullshit his way through issues he knew nothing about: when he was
  asked about his studies of Cannabis he forthrightly indicated that his
  efforts were never directed into that area. It was an honest and open
  interview of considerable interest.

  should find the interview worth checking out.
