          FLYING TRIANGLE CAUGHT ON FILM OVER BELPER,
                                  DERBYSHIRE!                 (Exclusive)
  For many months PRA member Melanie Cunningham has claimed to have been seeing UFO's of various
  types over her home in Belper, Derbyshire.    Melanie has dutifully made out report forms on a number of
  occasions in which she described 'Flying Triangles', balls of light and in one instance, what appeared to be
  a landed craft on a nearby hill.   The latter report was investigated in detail and the area surveyed for a
  possible landing site. However, what had been mis-identified as a possible landed craft turned out to be a
  large illuminated bungalow conservatory situated on a hill a few miles away.

  The reports from Melanie continued and in June of this year it was decided to supply her with an 8mm
  movie camera, fitted with an electrically operated zoom lens. This is a piece of low cost photographic
  equipment that had been supplied to previous UFO witnesses in the past. Melanie was asked to film any
  unusual objects that passed overhead in her vicinity.

  Over recent weeks Melanie has attempted to film a number of the strange objects that she has reported
  passing near her home. Melanie recently used up the complete 8mm film magazine and returned it to us in
  order to have the film processed. The film was duly sent off for processing and it was returned to us just
  a few days ago.

  The film could have been quickly run through a 'Super 8' projector, but your editor decided to study each
  individual film frame with a high powered magnifier. The reason being, that as soon as the film has been
  run through a projector, it is likely to pick up minute scratches on the emulsion surface and it was
  considered important to keep the film in pristine condition.

  Although 95% of the film showed nothing but complete blackness, suddenly there were several frames
  visible, showing the moon and a yellowy triangle at the bottom left of the 8mm frame. This section of the
  film was ultimately sent to 'Mallard Imaging', the specialist film processing company at Kimberley, Notts.

  'Mallard Imaging' produced an 'inter-negative' and then a first class quality print which has been
  reproduced over the page.    This confirmed that two objects were visible, one being the moon in its first
  qu�er and the second object showing up as a blunt cornered triangular shape, glowing yellow.

  We are not claiming anything other than this is a photograph produced by the process previously explained
  and is absolutely genuine. It is intended to have further analysis carried out on the original 8mm film in the
  near future. It is believed that the film could have been taken about the 19th June, unfortunately Melanie
  Cunningham did not keep a record of her film sequences.

  Apart from the yellow triangle section, a further section of the film showed a luminous ball of light bobbing
  about in the sk:y. (This will be shown to members at some future date.)
  Readers may recollect that Hailey Williamson, a Derby schoolgirl, reported seeing a yellow FT passing
  over Derby at 7.25pm on March 12th this year. Belper is situated some 8 miles to the North of Derby and
  has been the scene of several UFO reports in recent months.
