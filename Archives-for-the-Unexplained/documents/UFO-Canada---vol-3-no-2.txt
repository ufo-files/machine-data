SUMMARY

This was UFO CANADA's first attempt at publishing an Annual report with statistics
related to Canadian UFO/IFO reports. We do not wish to present it as anything else
but a modest effort. However, as our resources are improving we are confident that
future reports will be improved. In any case, we are certain this report will be
an aid to researchers in Canada and in other parts of the world. It has no conclu­
sions but rather presents a study of information whieh was available. It is, and
always has been our intention to be a •service • to UFO researches and not to im­
itate or try to out - do anyone else (a c01110n motive of many individuals). With
time we expect to increase publication of study papers on various scientific and
technical subjects which are of importance to UFO study in order to carry out a
more detailed and objective investigation.
