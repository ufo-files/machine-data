     Directors Message:


     Annual NH. MUFON picnic.

     At our last state meeting the date of Sunday Sept. 9, 1990 was chosen for our
     picnic. Time is noon till 5:00 P.M. I will have a gas grill, some chairs, picnic table
     etc. Bring your goodies & friends and enjoy a fun day.
                                                                               •


     25th Anniversary of Incident at Exeter.


     The Exeter public library is planning a UFO program commemorating the 25th
     anniversary of this famous New Hampshire sighting. I have been asked to develop
     a one and a half hour program. The date is Sept. 11, 1990. Time is 7:30 P.M. The
     place is the Exeter public library meeting room (lower level). Mr John G. Fuller
     renowned author of " Incident at Exeter " has agreed to participate. I expect media
     coverage. This is a great chance for NH MUFON to again show it's stuff. If you
     would like to help, Jet me know.
