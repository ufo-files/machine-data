   Kiriyat-Hayim ( near Haiffa ) 3 November 2002

  Joella reports, that her son woke up Saturday morning, and felt a scratching feeling in his lower belly,beneath the hips
  line. At his suprise, he found there alongated scraches, as made with a sharp object. He didn't remember how this
  happened, because he slept a deep sleep.
  Some of the scratches were visible also in the end of December. The area of the scratches is 10X5 sentimeters. In a
  photo, made 4 days after the case, we can see dots, like signs of stabs, in the scratched area.
  The mother turned to Mrs Miri Est (an alien contactee),who claimed that the signs were made by aliens, arrived not far
  ago. Miri Est claims,-the aliens injected her son a radioactive material in purpose to create changes in his DNA. It was
  performed because her ( Joella's ) son has a role.

  A few days after the first case, more signs were discovered, now in a shape of 2 parallel stripes with one stripe above the
  two.
  These signs disappeared after few days.

   Jerusalem 6 November 2002

  station ) in the report was claimed, that a lot of Jerusalem citizens noticed yesterday, high in the sky, at a strange flying

   In the way to Modi'in 6 November 2002

  Hanoch Nebo reports that at 21:00, while driving from Tel -Aviv to Modi'in, he saw 4 orange stable lights from north to
  east, almost above sky sector of Kfar-Saba.
  At 21:40, he noticed from his home, in Modi'in city, located high with a good obsevation, at the 4 lights and then the
  fifth light appeared.
  At 21:45 only one light was seen. The lights appeared in the sky and moved downwards.
  He reports, that he saw something like a meteor or falling star in the area. At 21:50, 3 more lights appeared, one ater the
  other.

  One of them seem to move fast donwards, and then stopped and floating, while its illuimination increases. Afterwards, 4
  lights were seen in the sky.

   Netaniya city 6 November 2002

  Neri Pinkwasser observed, at 21:00, from her home window referring east at a greenish light in the sky. After 10
  minutes, while driving with her mother towards south, she continued seeing it, and was impressed that its spinning in
  the sky. When she returned from the drive,she videotaped the object. In the film, lasting almost 2 minutes, we see at first
  the lights of the houses around, and then for a few seconds, we see in the sky a light moving from the left to right
  between two houses.

  Afterwards, the lens focuses at 2 moving lights, moving leftwards above roofs and sun heated-boilers. The light located
  higher, is more diagonal comparing the lower light.
  The upper light a yellowish shade and the lower has a bluish shade.

  The lights obviously are moving, comparing to the sun-heated boilers on the roofs and a star in the sky.

  No sound of an airplane was heard.

   Ben-Gurion intl.airport / Yokne'am 12 November 2002

  Hanoch Nebo reports at 20:15, when he was on his way to Jerusalem , he saw 2 yellow lights, one higher then the other.

  Before this event, he observed more lights in the sky. At that time, airplanes were at the sky in that area. Later, from his
  home, he saw in Modi'in area, an orange object flying towards the sea.

  At 20:30, I saw from Yokne'am ( Abi Greiff ) 2 orange orbs floating slowly south. They flew at a low altitude, untill
  they were hidden by a house and disappeared. No wind blew.

  The observation lasted almost 3 minutes.

   Kir'on 17 November 2002

  Albert Kristal reports, that at 22:30 he saw in the sky, towards Savion ( center Israel ) a red lighted object. Its shape was
  similar to a airplane with wings spread backwards. This object flew relatively slowly. It made two spins at the same sky
  sector and in the end, turned south-west and disappeard.

  Its flight was stable and no sounds was heard at all. It was a big flying object without any blinking lights ( like an
  airplane ).

  No changes in its illumination was observed. When the object passed infront of a planet (probably Mars ) it hid it.

   Nahshon-Hulda-Latrun junction 27 December 2002

  Moshe drove his car at 22:30, the weather was rainy,but the visibility was reasonable.
  From a very far distance and at a very high altitude, he noticed 4 stable yellow lights.

  After 5 seconds 3 more lights were added and a triangular formation was formed.


  His girlfriend, a soldier in a combat unit, who was in the car and also observed the view, denied any possibility that it
  was lighting bombs, because the lights were big and located at a very high altitude.
