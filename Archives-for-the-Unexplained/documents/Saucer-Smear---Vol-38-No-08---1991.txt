THE LAWSUITS AGAIN ST JAMES RANDI (Part One)
      Herein we take up one of the most delicate matters we have ever tack­
led. First let us explain that although the following information really
has nothing at all to do with UFOs, it does have something to do with the
notorious CSICOP organization, which is dedicated to debunking parapsychol­
ogy as well as UFOs and other allegedly unscientific beliefs. When    James
Randi ran afoul of alleged psychic Uri Geller and others, he was a leading
member of CSICOP, even though they claim he was not a spokesman for them.
(He has since resigned, under pressure.) CSICOP perceives itself as    the
ultimate arbitrator of Truth; yet we find that leading members of CSICOP,
such as Al Seckel on the one hand and James Randi on the other, have appar­
ently been less than wholesome in their activities and statements .  CSICOP
deliberately chooses to ignore these situations, which is the height of hyp­
ocrisy!
      Secondly, we consider Randi to be a friend whom we have known for over
25 years, and we certainly bear no malice whatever toward him. However,
"Smear" is dedicated to the Truth, within our humble abilities.
