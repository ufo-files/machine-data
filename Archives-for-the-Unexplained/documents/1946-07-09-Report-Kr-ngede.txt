9/7						14

Telephone message from Sven Matsson, Katstrupforsen, Åskott.

Mr. Matsson had spent the entirety of Tuesday, July 9, 1946 in Krångede. At around 14 o’clock during this very date, his hostess, mrs. Halvardsson, had observed a luminous phenomenon and described it as follows:
At the time of her sighting she had occupied herself in close proximity to the power plant in Krångede. She figured that the optical phenomenon had traveled in the direction of NW-SE, as it descended toward Böle, Cammalänge.
The phenomenon consisted of a dark body with a bright tail, which in turn slightly resembled an electric incandescent light bulb in terms of colour.

Received by phone, July 11, at 10.08 o’clock
S-E Sjöstedt
Officer of the day
F 4
