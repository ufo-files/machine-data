June 6: Sweden in great danger in case of war

At the Day of the Swedish flag festival in Mora (Dalarna) the Swedish Supreme Commander, general Helge Jung spoke about the dangers Sweden was in for, in case of a new war. ”A British reviewer, only a few days ago, pointed to the fact that the flight tracks between Russian and American bases go close by, and over, our country. On Scandinavian soil air and naval forces can be grouped with good possibilities of operating towards the west or the east. We must, therefore, count on that a super power is willing to occupy the whole, or parts of, our country before or after such a big conflict.” The general stated that ”...Sweden, if attacked by a super power, cannot stand alone in the long run.” (Sydöstra Sveriges Dagblad (from TT), June 7).
