DR. DONALD MENZEL'S MARTIAN FANTASIES
                    or
THE MJ-12 DOCUMENTS HE-EXPOSED, ONCE AGAIN!

       We are convinced that a key issue regarding the infamous MJ-12 docu­
ments, if not the key issue, is the question of whether or not the late
Dr. Donald Menzel, former Director of Harvard Observatory, could possibly
have been a member of this esteemed group of top U.S. dignitaries.
       Stanton Friedman persists in the belief that Dr. Menzel did indeed
live a "double life". Our thesis is just the opposite. We believe that
the.most obvious common-sense proof that the documents are fake, is the
fact that Dr. Menzel is included therein.
       Let us recall the key paragraph in this regard, from the   alleged
"Operation Majestic-12 Preliminary Briefing (paper) for President-Elect
Eisenhower", dated Nov. 18th, 1952: "Since it is virtually certain that
these craft do not originate in any country on earth, considerable specula­
tion has been centered around what their point of origin might be and how
they get here. Mars was and remains a possibility, although some scien­
tists, most notably Dr. Menzel, consider it more likely that we are deal­
ing with beings from another solar system entirely."
       Thus we are supposed to believe that Dr. Menzel, plus the other 11
MJ-12 people, plus many others, had knowledge of the fact that four dead
aliens allegedly crashed in a spaceship near Roswell, New Mexico, in early
July, 1947. Thus, a top-level Cover-Up has supposedly been going on ever
since then!
       Dr. Menzel's anti-UFO books, and his strong anti-UFO views as ex­
pressed publicly and privately (including to us) ever so many times, are
supposed to be part of the Cover-Up. But now, in this "Smear" exclusive,
we present a few of Menzel's strange paintings of mythical Martian scenes.
 (See Page J.) Were these part of the Cover-� too?? To what extraordin­
ary lengths was the poor man expected to go, to disguise his guilty know­
ledge about these four dead aliens and much more?
       The paintings are, to put it kindly, primitive. One might also call
them childish, crude, humorous, whimsical, or whatever. His widow, Flor­
ence Menzel, tells us that he began painting them for his grandchildren.
He became bored while attending the.Seattle World's Fair (date unknown to
us), and began this type of diversion. Over a period of time, he got bet­
ter at it. He eventually sold some of his works; he exhibited some; and
his widow still has quite a few. Three were sold, sometime during     the
1960's, to your esteemed "Smear" editor, for about $50 each. These are
the ones displayed on Page Three.
       It is rather obvious that the interest in Menzel's art was related
to his prestigious position in the scientific field, rather than his abil­
ity as an artist. Even his close friend Dr. Ernest Taves admits, "It's
not Picasso".
       Mrs. Menzel tells us that her late husband also painted "Moon Peo­
ple" and other similarly whimsical subjects. However, our only experience
is with the Martian fantasies.

      Florence Menzel seems to find the whole concept of life on other
planets to be humorous, just as her husband presumably did. But we warn
our readers that there is a . 001% possibility that our esteemed colleague
Stanton Friedman is right on this subject after all. When we pointed out
to Mrs. Menzel on the telephone that people with security clearances are
not even supposed to discuss classified matters with their spouses, she
replied, "Yes, he (Donald Menzel) was very good about that." In     other
words, it is possible that maybe he was holding back from her something
really important!
      So, all you MJ-12 die-hards may rest easy, on the assumption that
Menzel not only hid the dreadful reality of interplanetary invasion from
his own wife for about thirty years; not only produced ridiculous paint­
ings to bolster the non-reality of life on other planets; but lived     a
lie on this whole subject, in every respect, until the day of his death.
If that is really the case, may we suggest for Dr. Menzel a Special Post­
humous Academy Award, for Cover-Up Activity Far Above & Beyond the Call
of Duty!
      Incidentally, our sincere thanks to Florence Menzel for her co-op­
eration. She has mailed us one of her late husband's black & white draw­
ings as an aid to our studies.
