  Okay, we'll take the bait on this one. The request under FOIA for copies of
  these reports will be drafted and mailed tomorrow.
  As a follow-up to one of my previous posts, we received a response from the
  Department of Air Force regarding our request for a copy of Air Force
  Instruction 31-101 (Use of lethal force). In an August 13th letter from
  Latricia D. Grace, Freedom of Information Manager, they simply stated:
  "Publications are no longer considered records under FOIA. They are
  available through distribution channels. You may write to the National
  Technical Information Service (NTIS), 5285 Port Royal Road, Springfield VA
  22161 for a copy of Air Force Instruction 31-101."
  I found this interesting only because now I'm going to ask the NTIS for an
  index of the air force instructions since they seem to treat them as
  publications and sell them as though they were operating a book store. I'm
  just glad she didn't tell me to write to that overused address in Pueblo,
  Colorado <g>!
  Our request to the DoD for their policy on use of lethal force has not been
  answered. Neither has our request for records based on a cammo dude license
  plate number. All we've received thus far are confirmation post cards
  bearing the log numbers assigned to our requests.
  In any case, we'll keep the list posted as to our progress.
