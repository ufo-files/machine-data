  Reality Uncovered is Now Hiring Writers

    by ryguy » Thu Jun 11, 2009 1:52 pm

  This is a follow-up post to let everyone know about our latest news and updates - posted to the blog yesterday.

  Bottom line is that we are now looking to hire several staff writers to assist with the many threads of research we have
  going on, and publish articles each month (or multiple times a month, depending on what you'd like to do) to our
  website. The writing positions involve two parts - research and writing. We have a number of ongoing investigations
  that you can be assigned to and can write about, or you can suggest your own topics - we're flexible.

  Unlike just about every paranormal/conspiracy website out there, we will pay you for your efforts. If you are the type
  who became obsessed with paranormal/conspiracy topics and find yourself devoting many hours each day researching
  and writing about these topics - only to write up a post in some forum for nothing more than recognition alone, what
  we're offering you is not only recognition but a little bit of compensation for your efforts, and the promise of a lot more
  compensation as the site grows.

  What RealityUncovered can offer you:

  - Your own bio page listing your experience, past work and all of your contributed writing at RU. You can use the bio
  page to show other potential online employers your experience, or just to show friends and family that you're getting
  paid for all the time you waste online.
  - Compensation for every article that you publish on RU under your writer account. Compensation depends on the
  research required and length of the article. More details available when you apply.
  - Access to RU's library of existing research as well as our technical resources, existing contacts and sources.

  Requirements and Qualifications

  - A personal dedication and attention to detail where you get your articles in by deadline.
  - A dedication to conducting evidence-based research using thorough critical-thinking skills.
  - A preference for every claim, especially extraordinary claims, to be supported by verified and valid evidence.
  - A dislike for con artists and scammers.
  - A desire to elevate the field of the paranormal and ufology out of its current state of disarray and unprofessionalism so
  prevalent at other sites, forums and with contemporary researchers.
  - You agree that the public has a right to know the truth about issues that affect society and humanity.
  - You respect and are willing to protect the best interests of the general public with your writing
  - You dislike and are willing to fight the agenda of those who's activities harm the interest of the public
  - A passion for the truth.

  Submit Your Interest Today

  If you feel that the characteristics above describe you well, and you believe that you have what it takes to join the RU
