                  UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECTS:
               THE MEDICAL AND SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE

      Late in 1978, prominent UFO researchers approached the
 United Nations with the suggestion to set up some type of
 international clearing house whereby investigators and
 researchers would be able to coordinate on an international level
 scientific research into  unidentified flying objects, and to
 inform the Secretary-General of the observations, research, and
 evaluation of such activities.  The UN General Assembly responded
 favorably by adopting Decision 33/426.  Unfortunately, nothing
 was done to bring the decision to fruition and it lies dormant to
 this very day.

      On the other hand, UFO activity is anything but dormant.
 Sightings continue unabated throughout the world.   And as one
 would expect, private non-governmental UFO organizations have
 grown to fill the void.  Some examples are: the Mutual UFO
 Network, the J. Allen Hynek Center for UFO Studies, the
 Australian Centre for UFO Studies, the Joint USA- CIS Aerial
 Anomaly Federation, the British UFO Research Association, and a
 host of other fine organizations.  While these groups are
 functioning very well and are respected for their work, the UN
 agency is sorely missed.


      The UFO problem touches the lives of so many people and
  engenders puzzlement, fear, and at times, harm among them.
  Therefore, it is of significant scientific and technological
  interest and carries with it many implications of the existence
 .of intelligences other than our own.

      The evidence is both intriguing and frightening.  Reports of
 UFO activity describe incidents where the UFOs fly circles around
 high performance jet aircraft, shut down sophisticated electronic
 systems, black-out electrical power systems, stop automobiles,
 upset animals, damage plants, and cause medical injuries to
 humans.  All the while the skeptics are saying that nothing is
 going on and try to stop free inquiry on the subject.  They say
 it is all a case of mistaken observations, a view not shared by
 the victims of these encounters.

      MUFON has an Advisory Board of 173 Consultants, holding
 doctorates in their educational fields of expertise, and 154
 Research Specialists with Masters degrees in fields applicable to
 UFO research, that disagree with the skeptics and have evidence
 to prove it.  Other private organizations have similar boards and
 are equally convinced of the importance of the evidence.

     Father of the German V-2 rocket, Professor Hermann Oberth
was quoted as saying: "I am most satisfied by the hypothesis that
we are dealing with flying machines.  So far, other plausible
explanations have not been found.  If we accept this as a working.
hypothesis we also have to assume that these flying machines are
not built by human beings.  We cannot produce machines that fly
as UFOs do - for example, at speeds of more than 42,000 miles per
hour as measured by radar."

     In his continual pursuit for the application of the
scientific method to the UFO problem, the world's foremost UFO
expert Dr. J. Allen Hynek enabled us all to expand our scientific
horizons when he said: " I have begun to feel that there is a
tendency in the 20th-century science to forget that there will be
a 21st-century science, and indeed, a 30th-century science, from
which vantage points our knowledge of the universe may appear
quite different.  We s uffer, perhaps, from temporal
provincialism, a form of arrogance that has always irritated
posterity."  He made that statement in 1966 and it is as
important today than it was then.
