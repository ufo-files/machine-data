Case xxx.
A crash in Dalecarlia?

Time: Before June 17, 1946
Place: Province of Dalecarlia (Dalarna)

This case, reported in the American Newsweek, is unknown from all our Swedish sources (defence files, newspapers, etc). If it wasn´t for the name of the source, the report would be regarded as highly dubious: 

”MYSTERY V-BOMBS. During the war the Nazis fired several test V-rockets over Sweden from the Danish island of Bornholm [this is not true!]. Now they´re coming again. It´s impossible to tell who, where or why, but someone in Europe is firing long-range secret rockets patterned on Nazi vengeance weapons. The facts: In the last few weeks several have been observed passing over Sweden. Last week one burst into three parts over the Swedish province of Dalecarlia near Kopparberg. Swedish military patrols are looking for wreckage, but it´s a tough job in the thick forests of the area and so far none has been found. Swedish observers say the winged projectiles traveled at a fantastic speed horizontally. The direction of flight suggested they came from the southeast or south.”

Status: Insufficient information (unknown in Sweden)
Source: Newsweek, June 17, 1946, ”The Periscope”.
