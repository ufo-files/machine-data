Sunday Times (Perth, WA : 1902 - 1954), Sunday 7 October 1951, page 4




          FLASH WAS
          NO FLYING
            SAUCER
           Melbourne,  Sat:                 While
         Bruce  Burnett, of               Break-
         water, near     Geelong, was
         fishing    in   the    Barwon
         River   with his young      son
         last night, a fireball flash-
         ed    overhead      with        a

         screaming      noise      and
         plunged        into     the    river.

            Burnett         said today that
         steam       rose     25ft. from the
         water        and       waves      passed
         along the    river.   He     said
         the   object    looked    like   a
         big ball, 18 inches      across,
         with a long bluish tail. He
         said  he   believed    the ob-
         ject was   a   meteorite, but
         is certain     it   wasn't       a
         flying     saucer.
