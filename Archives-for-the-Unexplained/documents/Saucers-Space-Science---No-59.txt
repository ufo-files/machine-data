STRANGE

By Soren Andersson
(as told to him by
Mr. G. Johansson.)


The incident occurred at the market town of Mariannelund, Smaland in 1959. Mr. Gideon Jo­
                                                                                              /
hansson was employed as chief engineer at the factory-of AB Brusafors-Hallefors, and was
responsible for the operation at the factory's interlocking plant.
On one of the last days in October, 1959, just before Sp.m., there was a power blackout at
Mariannelund and its environs. As Mr. Johansson was responsible for the operation and was
on duty, he was immediately ready to carry out his service work.   When he came out from
his house, he met his son, Rolf, (25 years old), who was on his way in when it suddenly was
totally dark at t he market town. Rolf then saw a shining light and pointed it out to his
father. Over the three-storey building of Folkets Hue (House of the Public), they could
see an extraordinarily strong light appear.
They first thought it was an airplane with an engine not functioning and was going to crash,
as it seemed aimed at their house, Mr. Johansson called his wife to run out of the house.
She didn't hear him. At the same time he called, the object stopped, and Rolf and his fath­
er got the impression it could be a helicopter. Since no sound was heard, they had to re­
consider even that explanation.   After the object had been motionless for a minute, it
started moving again, and seemed as if it had the intention to land in the garden.
The object swayed three times, moved to the right and struck a maple tree, damaging it at
the top.   Thereafter a scraping sound was heard and the object descended straight at a
street, but stopped approximately one meter above the level of the street. All the while it
rocked like a boat on the waves of an ocean.
Mr. Johansson walked to his door and vas only 3 meters away from the object.  Now he saw
that it was a space vehicle. The white light emanated from something resembling a window,
like the wind screen of a larger car. It was a more compact and whiter light than he had
ever seen before. In this light, Mr. Johansson saw t wo humanoid beings with a friendly ap­
pearance. They had extremely high foreheads, and very large beautiful eyes radiating intel­
