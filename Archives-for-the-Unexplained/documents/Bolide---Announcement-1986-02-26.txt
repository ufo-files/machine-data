ball-of-light international data exchange


BOLIDE

                                       HILARY EVANS
                                     1 TRANQUIL VALE
co-ordinator                         LONDON SE3 OBU


  26 February 1986


  Dear Colleague,


  Ma�y researchers in different parts of the world are currently interesting

  themselves in anomalous light phenomena,                 and their relevance to - inter alia -

  geophysical and atmospheric phenomena on the one hand,                 UFOs and 'psychic'

  experiences on the other.                   Reports suggest that even on the level of physical

  reality,     some such lights are capable of behaviour unmatched elsewhere in

  nature   ;   further,    that some give the impression of intelligent control

  and further still,        that in some cases interaction with the witness on a mental

  level may occur.         Whether or not these reports are trustworthy,            the fact that

  they are made at all,          and in such numbers,        merits our study.

       Some of those investigating these phenomena are working in universities

  and other institutions             ;   others are engaged in anomaly research on a more or

  less amateur basis,        ranging from editors of Fortean journals to armchair

  theorists who make up for their lack of formal qualifications by a wide and

  cross-disciplinary knowledge of a literature which comprises medieval

  chronicles and The Gentleman's Magazine in addition to Perceptual & Motor

  skills and the Journal of the Society for Psychical Research.

       Probably most of these people are aware of some of the others'

  activities,        and a certain amount of information-sharing already takes place.

  However, it is evident that we could all benefit from a wider exchange of
                                 -


  information so that - for instance - spook light researchers in the United

  States can keep up to date with Norway's Project Hessdalen,                    and Italian

  ufologists investigating Adriatic fishermen's sightings can assess the

  relevance,     if any,    of earthlight research in Wales or tectonic stress theories

  from Canada.         And so forth       •   •   .


       Hence     -    BOLIDE !

                                                                                      (continues)
Bolide is neither a society nor a journal             :   it is simply a means of

exchanging information.        Correspondents will send the co-ordinator anything

you find interesting - reports on your own work,              newspaper clippings,

articles in esoteric academic journals,            reports from the past,      or simply

thoughts that came to you in the shower this morning.                And every so often

a packet of photocopies of these will be sent to you in return.                  Each item

will be on A4 paper,     decently photocopied so far as the original will allow,

and coded with a view to eventual indexing and reference.                   A list of the

correspondents, with their addresses,            will be circulated,    so that direct

communication may occur, and also so that the network can acquire whatever

further degree of cohesion its members wish.

     If you have any suggestions for improving these arrangements,

particularly ones that do not add appreciably to the burden of labour,                  I

shall be glad to have them.

     Obviously I shall want to be reimbursed for the material costs of

photocopying and mailing.           I propose an initial subscription of £10/$15/120F,

and we'll see how far that takes us.

     This initial letter is going out to about 50 people I can name off the

top of my head,     in Britain,      France,   Norway,    Australia, the United States,

Spain,   Canada and Sweden      ;   no doubt I shall think of more,         and I shall be

glad to have further nominations,          for though large numbers per se are

unimportant,    the wider the network the more truly representative it will be.

If the thing takes off,       of course,       we can look forward to international

seminars and multi-disciplinary workshops             ;   for the moment,    we'll do well

to keep it simple.

     Ideally,    you will each now reply -

1 - saying   'Yes   !'

2 - enclosing your cheque for £10/$15/120F or the equivalent (I am prepared to

    barter or trade      !)

3 - with some helpful suggestions and further names

4 - at least one item - a report,          a news-clipping or whatever - to start the

    ball rolling.


To conclude,    this is not a proposal,          it is an announcement.       In a sense,

BOLIDE already exists,        in the form of the material I already exchange with

colleagues round the world.           All that is happening now is that this informal

arrangement is being put on a more regular and wide-ranging basis.

     I look forward to hearing from you all.
