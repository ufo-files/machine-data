             1. (C) Request that a briefing be presented to the commander and
             selected staff officers of this organization at 0900 hours 6 June
             1978 in the INSCOM SSO conference room, Ft Meade, MD, concerning
             Soviet parapsychology research and the intelligence applications
             of parapsychology which the Soviets may exploit.

             2. (U) A representative of                    Group has previously
             discussed these subjects with                     of your organization                                                      SG1J
             and Mr. Dale Graff of Foreign                     sion (FTD), USAF.

             3. (C) The 902d MI Group is tasked to provide Operations Security
             (OPSEC) support in CONUS to echelons above corps. In the accom-
             plishment of this mission it is necessary for us to assess all
             hostile intelligence collection threats targeted against the US.
             Your assistance in providing information concerning the intelligence
             applications of Soviet parapsychology is necessary to insure our
             comprehensive assessment of the hostile intelligence collection
             threat.

             4. (U) A letter has been sent to FTD requesting the assistance of
             Mr. Dale Graff in presenting this briefing.
