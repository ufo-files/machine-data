          MEMORANDUM FOR THE DEPUTY SECRETARY OF DEFENSE
          SUBJECT:    Request for renewal of human use approval - ACTION MEMORANDUM               (U)
          (S/NF/SS-2) This request is in response to your desire for annual review of
          the DIA psychoenergetics activity (enclosure 1).
          (S/NF/SS-2) During    1985,  DIA continued to pursue development of
          psychoenergetics applications for the Intelligence Community by acceptin~
          operational control of the USAINSCOM CENTER LANE unit. In FY 1986, 12 DIA
          billets were authorized by Congress for this activity, and the CENTER LANE
          unit was transferred to DIA to form the nucleus of an operational
          psychoenergetics development effort. We are in the process of completing
          staffing action, and should have the remaining authorized billets filled
          shortly.   This effort is now an approved DoD spec i a 1 access program (SUN
          STREAK). Additional background material can be found in enclosure 2.
          (S/NF/SS-2) In 1985 and into early 1986, the primary focus of SUN STREAK was
          to improve psychoenergetic skills using proven training techniques. Three
          individuals progressed from mid-levels of training into operational training
'-""'     phases.    It is anticipated that select operational tasks will be pursued
          during 1986, and that limited unit operational readiness can be achieved by
          mid-1987.
          (S/NF/SS-2) Initial    applications will    be   limited to tasks where
          psychoenergetics data complement information from other intelligence
          collection sources (e.g., help narrow down search possibilities, define
          general characteristics of concealed weapon systems). Psychoenergetics data
          may also have potential in a tip-off role (e.g., predict new terrorist
          activity). An Intelligence Community Task Coordinating Group has been
          established to identify potential tasks for consideration in future SUN
          STREAK operations. Members of this group are senior executives from DIA,
          CIA, AlA, AF/IN, DNI, C3I and NSA.
          (S/NF/SS-2) Activity of the SUN STREAK project is monitored and reviewed by
          an executive-level oversight panel. This panel is chaired by Mr. D. Latham,
          ASDC3r. Members include Lt Gen Perroots, DR/DIA, with equivalent membership
          from CIA and other intelligence agencies anticipated in the future.
