  I – The events (1-2)

  May 1917 the 13th, three children, in charge of having a look on the sheeps in Fatima village, suddenly saw
  a lightning in the sky. At the same time, a "lady" appeared, as hanginfg in the air, above a oak.
  Then, this lady made the following declaration : in the next 6 following months, she’ll come back every
  month, at the same date and in the same place.
  She planned her last visit on the October 1917, the 13th. On that day, she would reveal to the children who
  she really was and what she was expecting.
  Up to the october 13th, the "lady" appeared once a month to the three children.
  Information began to spread over all the country and one rumour began to be exagerated : "one miracle
  was planned to take place in Fatima". This led a crowd of 50000 people to come to the place where
  "apparition" should take place, 1917 october the 13th. It was the sixth and last apparition.
  People must note that all appritions happened with a parallel phenomena : "the dance of the sun"
