Dear Chris:

Some time has passed since I last wrote. I am finally free of the Yakima manuscript and can
write. As you may remember, I wrote a lengthy history and analysis of the Yakima UFO
sightings. About a year ago (time flies!) I sent the manuscript to Mark Rodeghier. This
spring he provided a critique of the mss. from himself and another reviewer. The upshot
was CUFOS would be interested in publishing it, if changes were made. I have made many
of those changes, and shipped the new mss. to Mark (actually a not so new mss., but
con iderably streamlined).

So .. .now I can relax, until I restoke the fires soon for the continuing push on BOL
research.

While working on the mss., I actually broke down and called Persinger--no, not because I
"bought" his TST explanation of UFOs , but I wanted a copy of all TST and related papers
since I needed a complete, accurate bibliography of his work since I do test the TST in the
mss.

Well, this galvanized Persinger to heights of excitement. He actually called me the
following night and spoke for 1 hour and 40 minutes. I learned that he does not have a
UFO library, and he is still working full bore on the TST with Derr. The latest is that Derr
believes that underground water put under pressure somehow elicits the electromagnetic
radiation required for the "luminosities." Derr is following this line of inquiry based on
what he feels is strong evidence of the theory due to the activities of the Department of
Energy in the 1960s when it pumped radioactive waste water into the earth near
Denver...millions of gallons per month. He has worked with Persinger to correlate the
appearance of "UFOs" in ever-diminishing radii as the pressure built and was released until
it caused a quake that finally shook the area (if I understand the explanation).

  ow Persinger is all hot to show that UFOs appeared in some region around Attica, New
York (?) years ago because a similar scenario ensued there. He mentioned seeing color
prints of lights leaving Lake Ontario, but he can't track down the witness. I did come
across something on this, and would like to dig through my files to find the name for you--
if you remember something similar. This is not to necessarily steer Persinger to the
witness, but more for my own interest. All I have is that an aerodynamic engineer living
near Lake Ontario saw and photographed the lights. They apparently were ENTERING and
LEAVING the lake. Are you interested in trying to find him? I tried through the Cambridge
(?) UFO Investigating Group (a female heads it), but she failed to find a phone number.

Anyway, Persinger is now trying to further test Derr's mechanical model using some data
from Attica, New York. Sorry I can't be more definitive; I was more amazed during my
conversation with Persinger about his near total belief in this theory than in all the
complexities of his research. I did chastize him a few times about the tendency of his theory
to encompass all UFO phenomena. I don't believe Persinger has actually read any
important cases, but if you read his papers you will note th at the contents of the reports
aren't important; only the space and time data points are.

He spoke glowingly about his ongoing experiments of "zapping" normal people with
electromagnetic fields in his lab and the reported results: the sense of the "presence" of
people in the room by the subjects; sounds; disorientation; etc. He never mentioned any
result akin to an abduction, however.

Persinger strikes me as a guy who has a personal dislike for accounts that grate against his
view of reality . I do not understand why he is so relentless in proving the TST, except that
when I told him that if he proved that many or most NLs were natural in origin, he would
have accomplished something significant, he beamed (I "heard" his beaming over the
phone!). I reminded him, however, that the TST couldn't explain "spacecraft," but he had
nothing much to say on that, if I recall. Now, this is interesting ... he compared himself and
me to Kepler and Tycho Brahe (if I got it right).Seems Brahe labored for years, making
meticuluous measurements, which Kepler later used to prove what?--the orbits of planets--
or did Kepler set the groundwork for fact that the sun is at the center of our solar system?

Well, it is clear that Persinger cannot "prove" the TST without data, but I am not
enthusiastic about providing him such data. First, I would get no thanks, and second, I
would get no credit, and third, I am still having difficulties with the TST when the orange
BOLs always seem to go hand in hand with "exotic" cases, Bigfoot, even mutilations in the
same area. Also, his real lack of interest in the UFO literature is appalling. He is a strange
person, me-thinks; I am puzzled by his--as I said--single-minded drive to explain away all
UFOs.

Oh, yes, he mentioned your name during our "chat" ... something to the effect that "my
good frien d Chris Rutkowski can't seem to understand that" .... by "that" he meant strain
hundreds of miles from a UFO display can still cause UFOs; as you know, quakes almost
800 miles from Winnipeg somehow were symptoms of regional strain, and thus strain was
the cause of the mid 1970 flap in your area. Oh well...

Anyhow, it was an enlightening conversation as to who Persinger is, but my impression is
that Persinger is an island mostly unto himself who realizes he needs good UFO data, but
must rely upon "nice" people like me. Well, I have no intention of getting terribly close to
Persinger; I will keep an open mind on his work, but I will not join his "team." One bright
spot: He did invite me to provide any technical criticisms of the TST model. Well, if
CUFOS publishes the Yakima study, he will have criticisms a-plenty.

Oh yes, he also said he and his co-workers are meticulously finding latitude and longitudes
of all UFOs in his UFOCA T listing. Since Derr believes that UFOs diminish in numbers as
they "radiate" out from the epicenter, Persinger wants to look at that aspect, so spatial
relationships are critical. And if you do not know, he has explained all of Harley
Rutledge's observations as caused by tectonic strain and the "stranger" cases as essentially
the result of Rutledge "seeing things."

I could go on, but you get the idea.

How are things on your end? Have you come across any orange BOL cases or photos? As
