  US airmen's statements
    by Observer » Fri Feb 03, 2006 4:43 pm

  Most Policmen will tell you that witnesses can tell different stories when questioned about the same
  incident. The Police then have to correlate all the evidence to see if there is a general trend in the
  statements. If so they can then procede with a line of enquiry. So all the statements given by the US
  Airmen of the Rendlesham UFO incident are basically saying the same thing, give or take a few minor
  differences. The trend by all witnesses is the same. My argument is, they cannot be all wrong because
  there were too many of them and no statement that i have read is that contradictory.
