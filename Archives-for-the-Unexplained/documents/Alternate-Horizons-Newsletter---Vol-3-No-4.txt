                        "STORM PHOTO" NARRATIVE

   The photograph was taken at approximately 11:10 a. m. (CST) during mid­
  April, 1969.   The exact date was not noted, as no particular importance
  was attached to the photos at the time.
   As I returned home on this April morning, a thunderstorm was approaching
  from the West. Noting a downward projection from the storm cloud, I de­
  cided to snap a couple of photos, as I considered the projection to be
  rather unusual.   I knew full well that it was not a .tornado, but it did
  bear some resemblance to a tornado funnel.
   Taking a Kodak "Brownie Chiquita" 1 2 7 camera, I snapped two photos of the
  approaching cloud.   At this time, I was standing on my front porch, facing
  West.  My home is located 1.6 miles North of Plumerville, Arkansas.
   Upon having the roll of film developed some 4-5 weeks later, I discovered
  the anomalous "phenomena" on one of the storm cloud photos.    I should em­
  phasize that none of this--the "beam", the '"doughnut"- shaped object or the
, "glare" on the left side.of the photo--was visible to the naked eye at the
  time the photos were taken.   My sole purpose in photographing the cloud
  was to secure photos of the cloud projection, thinking to myself, "Hope­
  fully, that's as close as I'll ever come to seeing a tornado funnel."
   Within 10-15 minutes after I had taken the photos, the cloud moved'Qver
  my location.   There was nothing unusual about its actions--it was merely
  a typical spring thundershower, with rain, some wind, thunder and lightning.
   The photos (or negatives) have not been submitted to any professional
  photographers for analysis.   Two of my correspondents who are/amateur
  photographers i(with considerably more photographic knowledge th�n myself)
  hav� looked at the original prints and have not found any ready explanation
  for the "phenomena."
   I emphasize that I do not maintain that the''�Qughnutm in the photo is a
  UFO, nor do I insist that these effects have an other-than-normal explan­
  ation.  I merely state that I have not yet heard a satisfactory explanation.

 In our article, "Tenets of the Alternate Reality Theory" (UFO Sighter,
issue for Fall-Winter, 1968-69), we present a list of theoretical points
related to the Alternate Reality or AR Theory.  In that article, we did
not go into detail on the kind of background material which might tend
to support this general line of hypothetical enquiry.  The present work
provides references which interested researchers might find useful in the
pursuit of investigations in this general area.

 We do not claim to have thoroughly researched all works listed, and some
may yield little of interest in this direction, though we regard this as
somewhat unlikely.   Most or all works listed have been given at least some
attention by us.   However, the field seems open for much greater attention
than has been given to our knowledge to date.
