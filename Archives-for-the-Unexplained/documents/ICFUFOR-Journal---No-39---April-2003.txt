  Zichron-Yaakov (middle of January 2003) Mariana Tsafrir Kissing

  I am sending you this photos, taken by my friend Yael Meibloom, who is a professional photographer. The photos were
  taken from the balcony of her house towards the area above Zichron-Yaakovs winery.

  At the time she took this photos, she didn't notice anything wrong, after she developed the pictures, she noticed a
  circular "orb" UFO appearing on 2 of the photos ( 3 were taken ).

  In the first photo we see the moon, and in the second photo we see a reddish round object in front of a wooded hill,
  between a chimney and a big tree

  In the third photo, we see the same object in white color in the right side of the moon, when the sky is in its background.

  The road between Kiriyt-Ata and Zvulun junction 1 February 2003

  It happened a Saturday evening when I (Abi Greif) drove with my wife and son, to direction of Zvulun junction, while
  returning from Kiriyat-Ata. In front of us, in the south, we noticed 2 yollow-orange lights (who are familiar to us from
  other sightings) between the hills of Kfar-Hassidim.

  The lights weren't so high in the sky, for my estimation, they were almost at 300 meters high, comparing to the hills, but
  very far behind them.

  When we moved closer to the junction, the lights were hidden by those hills.

  Between Kadima and Zoran, 2 March 2003 (by David Ronen)

  Here is the testimony of Mrs Revital Ronnenm citizen of Zoran who is personaly familiar to me as a reliable person.
  The event occurred on Thursday, at 22:10.

  I drove on the road from Kadima to Zoran. I drove at a speed of 80 km/h and decrease speed to 40 km/h and then
  stopped because of what I saw . Suddenly, appeared in the dark cloudy sky in front of me this object colored mint.


  The size of it, from my point of view, was approximately as half of a private car, and I assumed that it was big enough if
  seen closer.

  The object was glowing, in a blue-mint color, it moved in an enormous speed, sharply from right to left, then in small
  circles, in an anormous speed.

  First I thought it was a jeep in the orchard and someone is playing, with a big projector. At first I was afraid to stop,
  because (not as in usual cases) I was alone on the road. But at last, my curiosity won, and I stopped in the side of the
  road. The maneuvers of the object were amazingly fast.

  To my understanding, and according to what I saw, it is impossible that a human being performed this amazing trick,
  my eyes saw.

  Maccabim / Modi'in 27 March 2003

  Liran, 10 years old boy, claims he and his family members see in the sky, starting at 19:40, a structure of lights moving
  in a circular orbit.

  At the beginning, 3 lights were visible in a rectangular shape, and afterwards a new light was seen. The lights are
  blinking and in a specific part they increase their stregth. In the opposite part, the lights was dimmer.

  He told that, the police was called, and the policeman saw the lights, but didn't have any explanation.

  Mrs Batia Lander, from Modi'in, reports: " from 19:00 till 20:40 we noticed something like a rectangular set of lights in
  the sky, with one side blinking steadily. It flew above Modi'in, a little in the east.
  The lights were yellow and in the other side was a red light. Sometimes the structure of lights seem like it "turned to
  another side" like it was pulled and sometimes stopped. Police was called, but they didn't know what it is"

  She also claimed, that a friend of her, from Petach-Tikva, also saw the view. She adds: " It seem to be located very high
  in the sky". The witness served in the I.A.F and she knew it wasn't an airplane.

  She continued and reported, that at 21:20 it appeared again in a south location and then the lights weren't blinking, but
  their color was identical.
