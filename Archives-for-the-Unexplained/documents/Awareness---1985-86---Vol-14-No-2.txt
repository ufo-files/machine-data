Luminous UFOs

UFOs are flying machines whose shapes dispay symmetrical revolution aFound a
vertical axis i.e. spheres, sp�eroids, lenses, discs. Along their periphery
are noted a series of glowing or luminous openings. (Fig.1 ) . Often the edge
is the upper basis to a belt of luminous mass/ or belt of "solid" light/ in the
form of a cylinder or truncated cone with different heights. Sometimes it can
be seen that the luminous cylinder or truncated cone consists of luminous
strings (Fig.2). In the bottom of UFOs or on the upper surface there is one
central opening (Figs 1A and 2C) or several openings (Figs. 1B and 2D) arranged
around the centre. These openings are reported to be luminous or, sometimes
columns or strings of "solid" light appear to emanate from them as if touching
the central parts in the bottom (Fig. 2 A, B ) or upper structure. The columns
often have the form of cones with very great heights and small bases. The
strings, if they are long, frequently are reported to be curved (see Fig. 3).
Often, instead of "solid" lights or a mass of light, "flames" are reported.

How UFOs Produce Light

The luminous mass ("solid lights, "flames") most often is aerial plasma
produced by UFOs. We shall show how it could be created. Namely, the
propulsion system (the UFO engines) provoke a flow of air from the .atmosphere
so the aerial particles enter the UFO through the opening(s) A. (Fig.3) in
the bottom or upper structure/entry opening(s), and after being accelerated,
theYescape/discharge/ always through the openings D on the circular periphery/
exit openings/ giving lift-force to the UFO. If the speeds of the aerial
particles in these flows are great enough/hypersonic, over 10-15mach,/since
they collide with each other, they will be excited or/and ionised. During
the de-excitation and recombination processes the result is luminosity of the
aerial propulsion along those parts of the airstreams where speeds are hyper­
sonic.

It is interesting that the luminous parts of UFOs sometimes have sharp
boundaries (Fig.2) and this feature gives an impression of "solid" lights.
Further, these boundaries could be changeable, that is the heights of the
luminous cylinders, cones, column& strings are increasing or decreasing.
This pattemcould be explained in such a way that the speeds of the aerial
particles in the flows are increasing or decreasing/when the UFO IS FLYING
DECELERATED or accelerated on a vertical line/, the longitude of the parts
whose flows are moving at hypersonic speeds is changing.

The curvity of the "solid" lights could be explaine d by electrical attraction
and repulsion between the flows if they consist of aerions in motion. Namely
we shall show that the flows could represent motions of positive aerions
which create magnetic fields. In such a case the flows from the same
directions will be attracting, and two flows in opposite directions will be
repelling.
