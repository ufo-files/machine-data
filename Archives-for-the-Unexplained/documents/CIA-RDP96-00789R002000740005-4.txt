SUBJECT: Summary of SRV session for week of 12 Jan 87.

1.   (U)   Summary of site:

     Site is a land/structure interface.  There are multiple
structures at the site in an area which is dry and dusty.  The
actual place of interest is an open (not densly populated) area,
but appears to be near a town, possibly on the outskirts of a
city or town.

    At the site there is a large, gangly, moving object <AOL:
ugly machine).   This object is massive in size and weight.  It
has a very hard feel, and gives the shape impression of a stack
of irregularly shaped blobs, smallest on top, largest on bottom.
It has a rounded and ridged top covering what looks like very
junky sides.   The bottom of the object is long and straight in
one direction, but curved under right above the "ground", and
there is a perception of rounded, junky things <AOL: wheels)
under and behind the "curved under" part.

    There is a definite feeling of movement to this object.  It
has an impressive amount of force capability, and gives the
impression that it pushes (rather than pulls) other large things.

2.   (U)   RVer's critique:

    Impressions of the site seemed very concrete and unambigous.
There seemed to be very little tendency toward AOL Drive, and
breaks were taken the few times when AOL seemed to be a problem.
I feel that the information given is accurate in its content, but
am personally disturbed by the fact that I immediately seemed to
"home in on" only a few details of the site (rather than getting
lofs of garbage to wade through at first).  That is what I would
love be to be able to do in every session, but it is not my
natural tendency, so the results must remain suspect until
feedback confirms or allieviates the suspicion.  I will get
feedback, won't I?
