A slag-like substance from Stöde

Time: mid-May, 1946
Place: Ramåsen, Stöde, county of Västernorrland

During 1946, the chemical laboratory of Försvarets forsknings-anstalt [The Research Institute of National Defence], FOA 1, analyzed more than 30 finds of materials believed to have a connection with the phenomena in the sky. All those cases were reported after the July 9 meteor and the subsequent request for reports from the general public. Although reported in mid-July the following case is reported, however, to have occurred in mid-May:

”Test nr 8. Specimen recovered July 1946 in Stöde. Specimen arrived at FOA 1 on July 16, 1946. Accompanied by the following letter from Captain Westlin of the Royal Sundsvall air defence corps:

”In addition to previous reports another report has come from Stöde, that, in mid-May, the owner of Ranåsen nr 1 had discovered, in a field, a slag-like substance, around which the gras was burnt in a one metre radius. The place is situated in an open field 100 meters from the road and 200 meters from the dwelling-house. This glowing mass had probably been thrown through the air. Drops from this [the mass] was found on several spots in the vicinity. Parts of this mass is sent separately by post.”

”The specimen is made up of several pieces of a light-grey mass, which, in it, includes withered blades of grass. The gras is tightly packed into the mass. On being broken the mass is internally of a somewhat darker grey colour. The mass is not porous, but has a compact structure. It is brittle and can easily be broken. The mass is soluble in hydrochloric acid [Sw: saltsyra] with heavy emission of gas (carbonic acid). The mass is probably lime-nitrogen [Sw: kalk-kväve], which a while ago came to the spot, where the find was made, in the form of a powdercake. It is known that if lime-nitrogen is not distributed well, it will destroy vegetation close by. The blades of gras in the specimen do not have any sign of a burning process but seem to have withered. It is therefore probable that the gras, at the claimed burnt area, was destroyed by the influence of lime-nitrogen.”

Status: No UFO report
