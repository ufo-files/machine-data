Case xxx.
Projectile crash at Njutånger

Time: Saturday, July 20, at about 15.00 hours
Place: Långviken, Njutånger (county of Gävleborg).

”PROJECTILE CRASH ALSO IN NJUTÅNGER. On Saturday evening, a mysterious object, which with great noise came singing through the air, frightened several persons in Långviken - Njutånger. 

”When several people at about 15.00 hours on Saturday were by the road near the dwellings in Långviken, they suddenly caught sight of a smaller object with a white, glistening tail of about 4 decimeters behind, which at 20 meters altitude and with great speed crossed over the road and over the dwellings, peeled [Sw. skalade] off the leaves on the tops of a few birches and hit the lake, about 15 meters from shore. On the lake, which was completely calm, big waves arose as from a motor-boat, in an area of about 10 meters. A person who, immediately after the fall, went to the place by boat, only found a lot of leaves and papers floating.”

This case, published only by local media, was never noted by the Defence Staff.

Status: Meteor?

July 21: Editorial

Editorial from the Stockholms-Tidningen (St.T.) on July 21:

”THERE´S TOO MUCH FLYING AROUND IN THE SKY”

”Things are really getting too lively in the Swedish air territory. 

”We are not referring to the increasing air service which is something that nobody would object to. What we hint at is the mysterious traffic of more or less silvery objects that are reported to move about leaving a shower of sparks behind, and which are said to have crashed on several occasions on Swedish territory.

”These sightings cannot be blamed to be pure halluciantions - even if it is hot at the moment. Nor can these phenomena - due to certain circumstances - be dismissed as some kind of "ghost rockets" similar to those that were observed at the most impossible sites in Sweden shortly before the outbreak of the war.

”The most plausible fact - not very pleasant, however - is that somebody is using our air territory as a target shooting range for experiments with a type of missile similar to the German V1 which also paid us a number of calls during the experimental stage.

”Of course the origin of these objects is unknown. They have been noticed, however, to have a westerly course when passing Swedish territory.

”It would seem advisable that the Swedish government sent a polite inquiry to certain foreign governments, to ask them if they lack some stray missiles that have ended up on Swedish territory by mistake, and if so - we would appreciate if they could see to it that the dangerous missiles be equipped with suitable tether arrangements. It cannot be denied that there is a certain risk in having such objects flying over our heads.

”We do not actually know whether they are explosive or not but in those cases where they have been traced after a crash there has not been much  left of them which would indicate that they are programmed for self-destruction, at least at the moment of impact.

”But what would happen if they hit a house for example? In any case it is contrary to the people´s rights to do something like this without having asked permission. 

”The country - whoever it may be - who is experimenting with a secret weapon should learn from the following true story from the Republic of San Marino. Many years ago they decided to modernize their field artillery and so they ordered a modern battery from France.

”In order to be able to carry out trials with the new equipment, however, it was necessary for San Marino to use a couple of kilometres of the Italian territory as the territory of San Marino was not large enough. San Marino had to ask the government of  Italy for permission to borrow a piece of Italian territory for a few hours, and this was done through their diplomatic representation in Rome. The matter was settled in good neighbourship.

”Seriously speking - wouldn´t it be possible to contact those countries that could be supposed to be owners of the stray missiles and ask them for an investigation or even forward a protest to them?

”It seems one of the missiles has previously crashed into water somewhere in Norrland. Perhaps it can be retrieved and identified. Maybe the result will be as interesting as when one of the first V-bombers happened to fall down in Småland - and was sent to England for examination. But that is hardly probable - those who carry on with experiments of this kind in peacetime are most certainly careful enough not to expose anything but the principle of propulsion - which probably will be jet propulsion.

”There is also a chance that letters or figures will be found on the object which would clarify the mystery of origin. In that case one would assume that our government would consider it justified to demand an explanation from the foreign country in question.

”Pending the outcome of the investigation in Norrbotten - provided  there is something left to examine - one would wish that our government would make it clear that we do not wish to be the target of missile experiments.

”Those who are launching the missiles are probably keen to find out how far these things can be launched. Usually we are kind enough to give them this information by publishing details from various areas. Perhaps we should abstain from publishing details in order not to encourage further experiments.

”If the consignor does not gain any information from us he may find it more profitable to aim at some other direction, less populated but still suitable for studying the course of missiles. The open sea would, in our opinion, be a suitable area for such experiments, provided there are ships and aeroplanes available.

”International respect is worth maintaining even if it would be achieved at the expense of increased costs and some trouble. 

”Unless the government of country X wishes the experiments to cause as much publicity as possible in order to impress either country Y or country Z?

July 22: Oslo (Norway) editorial

Aftenposten (Oslo) wrote on July 22:

”THE ECHO OF THE DAY

”It seems that the authorities are quite perplexed at the objects flying over our territory in this beautiful summer. The reason could be that they wish to have one of the objects for examination and documentation, before diplomatic action is taken.

”People have seen and heard the missiles and some people have been forced to throw themselves on the ground in order not to get hit by them. One would consider that veritable enough. But to get hold of one of them is more difficult - they do not move at the speed of a fjord boat. They are moving as if they "had a rocket in the back" if this expression may be used about rockets.

”As regards the missiles that recently crashed in Mjösa they are probably impossible to recover as they are lying at a depth of 3-400 metres. Perhaps a diving bell could be used in this case.

”It seems, though, that nothing much is done at the moment except that constables are taking notes of observations in areas where exceptionally numerous sightings have been made. All over the country people are also recommended to keep an eye on the sky and to report their observations to the Defence staff. Many things of interest to an Einbu or a Störmer may come up.

”There will probably be new reports on observations which will not tell us more than we already know. And what if that would happen - what the authorities are obviously hoping for - that a piece of missile would be found! What then? Maybe we are expecting to find an engravery on the object informing us that the thing is "Made in Germany"?
It could also be of Russian, American or English origin even if that is more doubtful.

”In any case it could not be used as proof of origin. It is known that, during the last war, certain countries were using the trademarks of other countries. Anyway it is understandable that the Defence Staff
would like to have a look at one.

”To a great extent this should be a matter for the Foreign Office.  When, where and to whom has permission been granted to carry out experiments with VX on Norwegian territory? It is close at hand to suspect one or two of our allied countries. But is it fair to play blind man´s buff with our defence?

”What if we asked for an honest explanation before accidents will happen? Shouldn´t there be a mutual understanding between the nations after the war, instead of devilry?

”Is it really that unlikely that somebody would confess to these experiments, that it is not even worth while to ask? 

”As things are now there is a great risk that one of the foreign missiles may hit someone anytime, or an animal, a fisherboat, a parked pram etc. We cannot be sure that they will always crash deep down into a lake or into an inaccessable mountain.
 
”Even if the missiles would turn out to be without blasting charges it won´t be considered harmless to get hit by one. Maybe the Foreign Office would like to send a diplomatic note and an official protest or possibly a compensation claim to whom it may concern so that the State be spared compensation costs to the widow.

”But to whom should the note be addressed? Nobody knows. All we know is that some foreign power is using Norwegian territory in an unpleasant and unworthy way.

”If the Norwegian Parliament had not been on vacation this question would probably already have been debated. But we do have a foreign minister who is soon going to a peace conference. What if he asked some of the nice gentlemen that he is going to confer with if any of them would know, incidentally, who is battering Norway these days?

”And furthermore - are we not on speaking terms with the big guys through United Nations? Why cannot the head of the department use one of the little telephones in his office and make a phone call to whom it may concern? It would not even have to be during office time - as the matter cannot be considered as deadly serious yet - but he could perhaps make the call sometime in the evening when he could find the time?

”It is not only unpleasant to have these missiles coming in through your windows. Also, they are reminding us very strongly of the fact that these things can be used to wipe out our towns whenever the anonymous consignor finds it convenient to do so.

”They are making our lives unnecessarily unsafe now that we are even beginning to get a better range of products at our Wine Monopoly.

”There are different opinions regarding the origin of the missiles. Some people have implied that the missiles were launched in the Soviet Union or some Russian occupied territory. However, it has now been established that those two which ended up in Mjösa were coming from west, for example from The Shetland Islands, Greenland, USA, Canada etc. 

”Ever since remote control was invented the missiles may have been launched from any country, irrespective of what course they are keeping the moment they are sighted in Norway.

”We all consider it important to find out, as soon as possible, who is violating us so that a protest may be forwarded to the nation concerned, perhaps through United Nations. That is a mission for our Foreign Office and it has to be done before a missile is crashing in our lap.

”Nobody is now trying to explain these phenomena as a result of hysteria, ghost stories, meteors or other natural or supernatural phenomena. Not only do we have the reports from Mjösa and Usken. Similar sightings have been made also in Finland and in the Alps. There have been reports of missile observations all over Scandinavia. 

”It is disturbing to think that while the Norwegian Parliament is on vacation and we are strolling around in Oslo eating ice-cream in peace and quiet, there are metallic cigars falling down all over the country. Before we have even licked our lips these objects may have polluted our country with more radioactivity than we are able to imagine.

”Or should we first start a discussion about the reforms of spelling?”

July 22: Sighting places not publicised

Copy

Defence Staff, Press Dept				Bulletin no 40/1946

TT

		To the Editorial office

				Not to be published

The origin of the light phenomenons which have been sighted in our country since the beginning of this year is still uncertain. It is however likely that they originate from trials with long distance weapons carried out by a foreign power. It is of great importance to the foreign power that they receive as detailed information as possible as far as trajectories, possible impacts etc are concerned. This information can most easily be obtained through reports published in the newspapers.

From Swedish point of view it is undesirable that the foreign power carrying out these experiments be provided with detailed information. As regards the light phenomenons the commander of the Defence Staff therefore requests that, when mentioning them in the newspaper, the area where they have been observed be restricted to one of the alternatives "northern Sweden", "central Sweden" or "southern Sweden".  The trajectory of the phenomenons should not be published at all. For special reasons it should not be mentioned whether the impact took place on shore or in the water.

The details which are not to be published should however be sent either to the Anti aircraft defence department at the Defence Staff, or to TT who have taken on the task to forward them to the departments in question.    

Except for these special details it is undesirable that the publicity regarding the light phenomenons be restricted. If there should be any doubts please ask the Press Dept at the Defence Staff.
				Stockholm the 22nd of July 1946
	TIDNINGARNAS TELEGRAMBYRÅ
	Gustaf Reuterswärd

The board of the Publicist club agree with this appeal.

	Ivar Andersson
	Chairman

July 23: Blasting experiments in British zone of Germany

/Stationary of/ The Swedish School Teachers´ Union
To: The Defence Staff, Air Defence Dept, Stockholm

Referring to a TT-message in the newspapers today, reporting  that the guided missiles sighted lately have entered our country from the south, I wish to give you the following information and hope it will be of interest to you.

On Tuesday July 23 I was travelling with a Swiss coach through northern Germany on my way to Basel. A couple of hours south (south-southeast?) of Hamburg on the southern part of the Lüneburger moor (?), in the English zone, we passed an area perhaps 20-30 km of length, where large and relatively new signs warned, in both English and German, of blasting experiments being carried out within the area. Access to the area, which started on the left side of the road, was severely forbidden for all civilians.

I also remember having seen other signs warning the local population for especially powerful blasting trials taking place on July 21 and/or 22.

Due to dense vegetation, brush-wood and bushes it was not possible for me to see anything from the bus as far as military activities within the area was concerned.

Yours faithfully,
Bruno Gustafson
Headmaster

July 23: Memo for the Secretary of Defence

To be commented... /AL

VPM

REGARDING LIGHT PHENOMENA ON SWEDISH TERRITORY

1. The Defence Staff was made aware of strange light phenomena on Swedish territory on May 23 both by reports published in the newspapers, and also by reports received from civilian observers. During the period of May 23 - July 8 about 30 reports were received. The Defence Staff has gone through the material but due to the limited number of reports, many of them being incomplete, no evaluation of significance has been made.
Later on a report on light phenomena observed on April 25 has been received.

2. The observatories in Stockholm (Saltsjöbaden) and Uppsala were contacted in order to get the astronomers' opinion on the occurring celestial phenomena. The observatory in Stockholm reported that the large amount of sightings would indicate that, in several cases, the phenomena cannot be defined as meteors.

3. At the beginning of June the Finnish newspapers reported that sightings had been made also in Finland. The military attaché was then commissioned to contact the Finnish authorities in order to get information about these sightings. Later on the military attachés in Copenhagen and Oslo were also contacted. Some information was received from Finland. Nothing was heard, however, from Denmark or Norway. 

4. After that the Finnish newspapers had published reports on numerous sightings during June 9-10  all military units got a head quarter order on June 12 to report all sightings from both military and civilian observers according to a fixed sequence to the Air Defence department at the Defence Staff.

5.On July 9 a large numer of light phenomena were observed in Sweden. In a special bulletin in the newspapers and on the radio the Chief of the Defence Staff requested  the public to report their sightings. For the period July 9-10 about 250 reports were received and thereafter, up to July 21 about 50 reports. 

6. The trajectories observed as from July 9 have been marked on the enclosed chart. As there is no air surveillance organised in the areas where overflights over certain lines at certain hours have been sighted, a reliable estimation of the trajectories is not feasible.

It would seem, however, that observations have been made in certain throughfares, two smaller half-circles heading mainly to the northeast and a larger half-circle heading mainly to the north. In each case the object has passed the east coast at an easterly course.

The sightings on July 9 mainly occurred in central Sweden.
Later a shifting towards the north has been observed.

When comparing the information with Finnish reports it would seem that the missiles were sent off in the Baltic countries heading Scancinavia in a halv-circle.

After June 9-10 the Finnish newspapers have not published any reports on light phenomena, except for a short insignificant notice.

7. The observations can be characterized as fire phenomena,
either "intensely luminuous fireballs with a tail" in blue-white or blue-green colour or "shiny silvery elliptical objects of torpedo shape".
Several reports are describing the object as a thermos bottle.

It is remarkable that the reports on "fireball with a tail" are mainly concentrated to July 9-10 while the torpedo shape is prevailing both before and after this date.

8. The phenomena have been sighted at different altitudes.
With regard to velocity the observations also diverge. Considering the difficulty of estimating these factors no certain conclusions cannot be drawn but it could be the question of rocket missiles with a velocity exceeding the sound velocity.

9. Explosions in the trajectory, split into 5-6 shooting stars, have been sighted in some cases.

10. Several cases of impact have been observed. So far no fragments of metal, as in the cases of V1 and V2, have been retrieved. However, findings of a slaggy material have been retrieved from some ten sites of alleged impact. Analysis of the material is being carried out  at the Defence Research Centre. So far the analysis has proven that part of the findings are identical with known material, without any connection to phenomena of this kind. 

Investigations carried out at alleged sites of impact in the upper Norrland have given no result so far. With consideration taken to local circumstances the prospects of retrieving debris for the reconstruction of the projectiles are not too good.

11. After July 9 matters referring to these problems are handled by a delegation lead by colonel Jacobsson FF as chairman, together with representatives from the Defence Staff, the Air Force, the Defence Research Centre and the Defence Radio Centre.

12. As no impact has been proven, enabling the determination of type of weapon efforts are made to try to ascertain the trajectory of the objects over Swedish territory and also to try to establish whether the missiles are equipped for some kind of impulse excitation. For this purpose the echo receivers of the Army and the Air Force along with FRA, will be made use of. The resources are, however, limited both with regard to quality as for quantity.

Milbef V1 milo have been instructed to take every possible action in trying to retrieve debris from alleged impacts.

13. As it is of utmost importance that possible foreign experimentors do not receive detailed information concerning observations made, the press has been ordered not to publish detailed reports. This appeal has been made through TT, with the recommendation of the Publicist Club.

14. In order to be able to carry out necessary reconnaissance work certain additional means are required, e g for civilian experts, divers and allowances for expenses. The needs cannot be estimated at this moment. The lack of means must not, however, prevent the search. To start with, a sum of say kr 10.000:- should be placed at the disposal of the Chief of the Defence Staff. 

Translation credit: Telle Lilja


July 24: Colonel Ramström a skeptic of ghost bombs...

””BOMB CRASH” AT SIGTUNA EXPOSED AS METEORITE

”- I firmly believe that the many light phenomena in the sky and those ”bombs” which lately have been reported from different parts of the country, is nothing but meteorites and that conviction I intend to keep until something else has been proved, declares the head of the Rosersberg artillery school, Colonel Sven Ramström, to Stockholms-Tidningen

”Colonel Ramström has had his firm belief further strenghtened by a ”bomb crash” recently observed near Sigtuna [see case xxx, July 9], where the remains of ”the bomb”, on close inspection, showed all signs of having come from a meteorite. The find has been sent to the Air Defence department of the Defence Staff. 

”The find from Sigtuna is also interesting because ”the bomb”, in this case, appeared in about the same way as the flying objects which have been observed elsewhere. Eye witnesses describe it as a ball about the size of a cricket ball [Sw. krocketklot]. Behind it was a 2,5 - 3 meter long tail, luminous with all colours of the rainbow. 

”It was a landowner at Brantshammar who saw ”the bomb” and the crash. He reported the case to the police office in Sigtuna and, from there, expert help was ordered from the Rosersberg artillery school. The head of the test department Captain Åke Toll went to the place in the company of Major Sten Ankarkrona and Captain Bengt Ahlström, and they found ”the remains of the bomb”: a piece of iron and two lumps of slag. During the investigation nothing emerged that could prove that the finds came from anything but a meteor.

”Colonel Ramström repeats his personal opinion that ”the bombs”...are nothing but meteorites and thinks one ought to reduce ”the bomb psychosis” presently raging. - People have been upset by all these ”bombs”. It would be better if they would calm down until one really has proof that it is the question of such aerial guests. Meteorites can be annoying, too, says Colonel Ramström, but not so difficult to explain. In fact, the astronomers could, a long time ago, foretell that we right now had to expect an unusually abundant rain of meteors. This is a fact that further strengthens my opinion that ”the bombs” are not man-made.

Source: Stockholms-Tidningen, July 24; also syndicated by STB to many other newspapers.

...and astronomer Lundmark a skeptic of meteors...

- Well, says professor Knut Lundmark, when Stockholms-Tidningen late on Tuesday evening speaks to him at the place where he spends his holidays. I really just know what the papers have written and hardly that, since I have been away in the wilderness. But after what I have read it seems unbelievable that it would be the question of meteorites. 

”It is true that sometimes one can foretell frequent meteoric visitations - and such are not uncommon in the late summer - but if I have read the papers correctly most of these ”meteors” have been sighted in daylight and it is extremely rare that a real meteor can be viewed during the bright part of the day. So, I am mostly inclined to think it is some kind of a projectile. But that, I suppose, the military will eventually find out.”

Source: Stockholms-Tidningen, July 24. Also syndicated to many other papers by STB.

July 24: French rocket heading for Mars in 1960?

Bulls-INS reported from Paris, July 24, in an article by Lowell Bennett, that French scientists were at work on a nuclear rocketship which ”should be able to travel to Mars in fifteen days”. Professor Alexander Ananoff declared that ”we should be ready for the first flight by March 1960”. Ananoff was reported as being ”Director of the Sorbonne observatory”. In a secret underground factory close to Paris the rocket ship was being built with its first test flight scheduled for the end of August [1946], when the ship will reach an altitude of 120 kms and be brought back to earth by a parachute. No living beings will follow the ship on its inaugural flight. ”The ship is constructed along the same principles as the V-2 and is expected to reach a speed of more than 160.000 km/hour.”  (Östersunds-Posten, July 24).  


July 25: Editorial 

Smålands Folkblad, a local south Swedish paper, published a syndicated editorial article on July 25: (also published by Skånska Social-Demokraten and Piteå Tidning on July 27)

"Ghost bombs or meteorites?" 

”USA is doing experiments with atomic bombs in the Bikini lagoon which yesterday was turned into a boiling maelstrom as the underwater bomb detonated. At the same time experiments with new weapons are being carried out also in other places. 
These experiments are carried out secretly and are not used for propaganda purposes.

”During the last month strange luminuous phenomena, fireballs and silvery beams have been sighted practically all over Sweden. Also impacts have occurred although search in those lakes where "ghost bombs" have been seen to crash, has so far been without success.

”One of the first impacts observed resulted in findings that would imply that the mystical objects could be meteorites instead of missiles.

”The head of the Rosersberg shooting school, Brigadier Sven Ramström, declared in a newspaper article last Wednesday that he was convinced that all "bombs" that have recently been reported from different sites all over the country, are nothing but meteorites. He said he will keep this point of view until something else can be proved.

”At the Defence Staff these celestial phenomena are getting much attention and all observations regarding the strange objects are compiled. The Defence Staff is not 100 percent convinced that these objects are fragments of celestial bodies.

”Their theory is that the objects that are crossing Sweden in both westerly and easterly direction are making a roundtrip over Sweden and that they will finally return to their original point of departure.

”The Defence Staff is convinced that there is no doubt that Sweden is serving as a shooting range for somebody experimenting with missiles. 

”This statement must be considered of great interest. The Defence Staff has been taking things seriously lately which is proved by the fact that published reports on observations do not contain any details concerning the point of observation. The
observations have been made in "Northern Sweden" or "Central Sweden". The same method is thus being used as during the time when Germans were using Sweden as shooting range for their experiments with V-bombs.

”Where do they come from, these new mysterious bombs and what can we do to stop the unpleasant traffic on our territory? The guided missiles we have seen so far have presumably not been equipped with explosives but even so we cannot consider them harmless. We have to put an end to it as we do not want Sweden to be used as a shooting range.

”Aviation reporters of the English newspaper Daily Graphics declared on Wednesday that British and American aviation experts are following the development within the Russian air force with the greatest interest.

”It is reported that jet-propelled aircrafts of German construction are now being built with the cooperation of German scientists in the Russian zone of Germany, and that Russian and German scientists are working together in Peenemünde with the construction of jet-propelled planes, rockets and guided missiles.

”In fact it was in Peenemünde that the German experiments with V-weapons were carried out and therefore it is easy to make parallells with actual observations which would explain the presence of "ghost rockets" in Sweden.

”Let us hope that the Defence Staff will soon finish their investigations on the mysterious missiles so that measures can be taken to put an end to the resumed "ghost traffic" on Swedish territory.

Case xxx.
Aerial torpedoes in the archipelago

Time: Thursday, July 25, at 13.30-14.00 hours
Place: Loskär near Grisslehamn 

On August 7, Gustaf Winberg read an interview with Major Nils Ahlgren and sat down to type the following letter about a sighting he made almost two weeks before: 

”Gnesta, August 7, 1946. To the head of the Defence Staff´s Air Defence department, Major Nils Ahlgren. Stockholm.  On account of todays articles in several morning papers concerning the appearing phenomena, I will, for my own part, like to report that on Thursday, July 25, at 13.30-14.00 hours, while on holidays in Grisslehamn, and at that time rowing out to Loskär in the company of my wife and daughter, I sighted at about 150 to 200 meters distance two aerial torpedoes passing between Grisslehamnslandet and the last-mentioned islet [Loskär] with a northeasterly trajectory. Altitude over the water surface was about 10 meters. Speed possibly 100 km/h. A powerful humming noise similar to the engine of a small aircraft. No smoke exhaust could be seen. Colour light-grey. Both of them went parallel to each other, therefore it is possible that they were linked to each other. Unfortunately we were still in the bay so they disappeared behind the point of land. 



(Sketch by Gustaf Winberg)




”About 2 meters long, about 0,4 m diameter”


”Trying to sketch from memory what we had seen, without confering with each other, we arrived at the same result concerning the basic appearance of the body. We are not clear, however, on the appearance and placing of the wings. I enclose a very simple sketch. 

”This has not been reported to any newspaper or authority before. Grateful if I could have a small note to confirm that this has arrived.  Sincerely, /signed/ Gustaf Winberg.”

Someone at the Staff penned notes about this case into the typewritten tabulation of sightings for July 24-26. The bearing to the object was noted as easterly. The stated speed (100 km/h) does not compare to any of the WWII V-weapons. It seems, no further investigation was made. 

On Sept. 6 Olof Kempe of the Defence Radio Institution (FRA), Swedish equivalent of the American NSA, sent a report to the Air Defence department that between 13.11 and 13.50 a Swedish FRA radio reconnaissance plane had caught radio traffic that ”could have a connection with the radio direction of a remote-controlled plane”. Someone at the staff penned, by hand, ”Grisslehamn 13.30 - 14.00, to be reported to FRA”, on the document. 

Göran Jansson, a Swede who has worked for the supersecret FRA, and who wrote a book (in Swedish) about the ghost rockets (available on the Internet only), notes, without stating his source, that an ”airplane over the Baltic [caught] what one calls ”special radio signals” at the USW (ultra-short wave) band - between 45 and 50 Mhz. FRA believes that this could be the guidance signals for the projectiles at Loskär - something that will never be confirmed.”   

Status: Unidentified.
Source: Letter to the Defence Staff. FRA report of Sept 6.


July 25: A question of psychology?

The following editorial was published by Stockholms-Tidningen on July 25: (and was subsequently quoted by Jönköpings-Posten (July 26))

”METEOR OR GHOST BOMB? Meteor or ghostbomb - that is the question. Colonel Ramström, who undeniably is a sensible man, can, in at least one case, prove that the ”bomb psychosis” has had no basis in fact - a crash near Sigtuna of a fireball with luminous tail proved on expert investigation to be from a meteor. Now it seems undeniably strange if it should suddenly start raining stones from the sky in such numbers as the reports attest to in the last days, in this sense professor Lundmark is also a skeptic, and he should know. But the bomb theory does not seem persuasive, neither. One has difficulty seeing the finesse of, in this way, targeting our area with luminous balls, large as apples. 

”It is also talked about V 1-bombs. So far there are, in any case, no proof of such [bombs] really passing over our country. July 29: Flying wings and pancakes...

In July, media published pictures and texts on several new American aircraft designs [...seemingly without this having any effect on what was visually reported as occuring in the Swedish skies...]. By mid-July a picture of the V-173, ”flying pancake”, was published by a south-Swedish newspaper [Sydöstra Sveriges Dagblad, July 15]. 

Details of the four-engined prop-driven Northrop XB-35 ”flying wing” was presented with a large picture [Göteborgs Handels- och Sjöfartstidning, July 29] and by way of a syndicated text out of Hawthorne, California [Sundsvalls Tidning, July 31]. The first flight of the 52 meter-spanned ”wing” had taken place on June 25.  

Expressen, the new evening newspaper in Stockholm, published, August 20, some futuristic aircraft designs, including one flying wing, very much like the crescent-shaped object pilot Kenneth Arnold reported in June 1947. 

Swedish aircraft designers presented, in September 1946, ideas for their own flying wing, the 1001. This eventually evolved into the SAAB J-35 Draken supersonic jet fighter. [MorgonTidningen, Sept. 6].

Two cross-shaped objects

Place: Filipstad area, county of Värmland, west Sweden. 

A letter in the Defence Staff files is the only available documentation on this case:

I have not told the newspapers about it as I think it would be inappropriate in case the object would be one of the foreign missiles. The newspapers would not be able to report about it anyway or they would have to give false information. As regards the following I refer to the ordnance map.

”I was in my home which is situated on the crossroads of Finshyttan towards Hagfors and the road  from Storbron to Bosjön. Through a window I saw two objects approaching from the north over the mud- lake Storbrohyttan, heading south.

”The objects looked like a cross of a grave. At the front there seemed to be a cross but without straight lines. The objekt seemed oval, then there was the cross and a long rear end. I did not see the objects separately at any time as they were flying abreast of each other. The thicker parts of the objects were clearly visible, though. Then the objects were passing side by side merging into one picture.

”The objects looked dark but as I saw them towards white clouds I dare not say what colour they were.

”It is also difficult for me to tell the altitude as I have never made estimates of this kind but I would guess that the altitude was abt 400-500 m.

”If it would have crashed at the point where I last sighted it it would have ended up abt 500 m south of Storbrohyttan, close to the Chiller river.

”I could not hear a sound. I ran out onto the balcony but it had already disappeared behind a black thundercloud coming at a low altitude from southwest. I still did not hear a sound. My son also came out at the same time and he did not hear anything either.

”An ordinary aeroplane would most certainly have been heard by us as the distance must have been 1000 m at the maximum.

”The speed was very low and I saw them very clearly with the white clouds as a background.

”If there had not been a thunderstorm going on I would immediately have phoned the military forces in Karlstad and Kristinehamn to alert them, but the weather being as bad as it was I considered it useless.

”The period of time during which I observed these objects was about 10-15 sec.

Case xxx. 
FRA radio reconnaissance staff 
spot luminous phenomenon

Time: August 2, 00.05 (five minutes past midnight)
Place: Ljugarn (island of Gotland, in the Baltic)

The top secret Defence Radio Institute (FRA) has for decades had listening stations on the Baltic island of Gotland, closer to the Soviet Union than the Swedish mainland. Staff from a listening post in Ljugarn, station ”Frida”, spotted a luminous phenomenon and sent the following report to the Defence Staff: 

”Report ”Frida”
1. Time: August 2, at 00.05. Spectators: telegraphists Ulriksson, Granell and Svedberg. 
2. Place of observation: about in the middle of the Ljugarn village. 
3. Direction of observation: about 130 degrees [to the southeast] from the place of observation. 
4. Degree of altitude: about 45 degrees. 
5. Great speed, 1000 [km/hour], maybe more. No sound was heard. 
6. Direction of flight of the projectile about southwesterly. 
7. Length of the light phenomenon, about 100 meters, maybe more.
8. Light: powerful, somewhat violet. 
9. Shape: almost spool-shaped.
10. Seemed to diminish (probably hidden behind a cloud of rain).
11. Cloud cover: Heavy rain clouds (stratos-nimbus). 

”Remark. This time of the day it is usually rather dark, therefore this must be appromixate data. As concerns FRIDA we often catch strange sounds [on radio], but this has been found to be teleprinters, Hell writers, transmitters (of about 2000 [letters]/minute). On the long wave, a lot of carrier waves are always caught. In suspicious cases the observer [telegraphist] stays with the signal for a longer time. 

”Ljugarn, August 2, 1946. Olof Svedberg, head of detachement (FRA).”

Göran Jansson, with contacts within FRA, writes in his Internet book on the 1946 phenomena: 

”FRIDA was an abbreviation that FRA used as a code for a radio reconnaissance project. FRIDA stands for ”Foreign Robot [Swedish word for missile] activities In the Dagö area”. Dagö is an island north of [the island] Ösel - both of them off the Esthonian coast. One [FRA] gave the mission this name since one suspected that the ghost rockets were directed from Dagö. This was not a common knowledge within the ghost projectile committee, but FRA used the code name after having caught suspicious signals from Dagö.”

INVESTIGATION REPORT
Tuesday August 6, 1946

Re: A missile-shaped object sighted on August 2, 1946 in the area of the village Nuoksujärvi, parish of Junosuando, Police District of Pajala.

On Friday August 2, 1946 at 5.40 pm the country constable Axel Karttu in Junosuando made the following report by telephone to undersigned, district attorney of Pajala.

Roadworker apprentice Runo Mattias Lantto, Puoltikasvaara, Gällivare - staying temporarily in Junosuando - together with roadworkers Hjalmar Katajamaa, Junosuando and Sigurd Larsson, Junosuando were working the road Junosuando-Nuoksujärvi.

The time was about 12.35. They report to have seen a spool-shaped object 2-3 m of length coming from southwest, passing above them at an altitude of 400-500 m and at a high speed, continuing towards northeast over the lake Nuoksujärvi and finally disappearing in the woods near the north shore of the lake. A sketch of the area is enclosed.

The observers have simultaneously heard a "buzzing rumble" coming from the object.

After I had spoken to Lantto on the phone and he had confirmed the correctness of Kerttu´s report I immediately left phone messages on the subject to the County Administrative Board of Norrbotten and the Area Defence Staff in Kiruna. Then I left for Junosuando and Nuoksujärvi in person to make a thorough investigation with regard to the observation. 

On the same day in Junosuando

Roadworker apprentice Runo Mattias Lantto, born on Febr 24, 1925, registered and living in Puoltikasvaara, parish of Gällivare,
but temporarily staying in Junosuando, recalls:

He had been employed for four years as volunteer at Ing 3 and after that he passed corporal and sergeant educations. The employment ceased in the autumn of 1945. 

It was August 2, 1946 and the time was about 12.35. Lantto was working the road Junosuando-Nuoksujärvi (see enclosed sketch)
together with roadworkers Hjalmar Katajamaa, Junosuando and Sigurd Larsson, Kangos.

Larsson, who was busy planning the road, was working at the site marked 1 on the sketch. Katajamaa was on the road about 40 m from Larsson, towards Puornuvaara, and Lantto himself was on the northeast road embankment about 5 m from Larsson, towards the crossroads to Nuoksujärvi.

While working in these positions Larsson suddenly called out that an aeroplane was approaching. He also pointed to the sky but Lantto did not notice anything in the sky at once. He, however, heard a "rumbling noise". Guided by the noise he had soon caught sight of an object which moved from southwest towards northeast at a high speed.

When Lantto first saw the object it was moving at a distance of about 1 km from him, at an altitude of abt 500 m. He first saw the object from behind and it was moving over the lake Nuoksujärvi or perhaps on the north side of the lake. Then it changed its course,  made a turn to the left and at the same time it started to descend.

For a moment the object contrasted with the mountain Puornuvaara and it seemed to Lantto as if it disappeared into the mountain. At the moment when Lantto last saw the object it was about 50 m above the ground. After it had changed its course Lantto saw the object from the side. It was cigar-shaped was dark in colour.
It moved with the pointed end forward.

Lantto estimated the length of the object to be 2-3 m. The distance from the site where the object changed its course to where it disappeared out of sight was estimated by Lantto to 300-400 m. 

He heard no sound of a crash in spite of all efforts. He did not see any smoke either, or anything else from a possible crash. The sound of the object disappeared at the same time as Lantto lost sight of it. The period of time of the observation was 20-30 sec totally, out of which Lantto could actually see the object during 10 sec.

Immediately when he lost the object out of sight Lantto clocked the seconds and asked Larsson and Katajamaa to listen for sounds from a possible impact.

Lantto has very carefully located the point of his observation and on the site he would be able to give exact details of the course of the object. 

As to the weather it was clear with sunshine and a soft southeasterly breeze.

The story of roadworker Hjalmar Oskar Katajamaa, born on June 5, 1908, registered and living in Junosuando:

He confirmed the correctness of the information left by Lantto with regard to time and place of the observation and in addition to this he told us the following.

At that special occasion Larsson called his attention by calling out, that an aeroplane was approaching. At the same time Katajamaa heard a buzzing sound similar to the "buzzing from a wasp´s nest".
The sound was softer than the sound of an aeroplane. 

Almost immediately he discovered an elliptical object moving at a high speed from southwest towards northeast. He had noticed the object already before it reached its position above them. It was cigar-shaped and he estimated the length to 2-3 m. The colour was dark.

When the object was above them the approximate altitude was 500 m. He watched it as it moved away over the north side of lake Nuoksujärvi. It made a left turn and a sharp descent. It seemed to Katajamaa that the object went down at the southeast end of the mountain Puornuvaara. He did not see or hear any crash.

August 3, 1946 in Kangos

/Stationary of/ The Swedish State Power Board

The following observation was made on Aug 5, 1946 at 03.14 pm in Uttran.

I am 54 years old and my position is First Administrative officer at the Swedish State Power Board. At the actual moment I was in my summer home at the building site called Norrbyuret 145, together with my wife who is 51 and my son who is 13.

My son who is interested in different types of aeroplanes called our attention to an engine sound which differed from the aeroplanes we are used to hearing.

When I looked up I noticed a white narrow line, approximately in zenith, which was moving at high altitude and with high speed in the direction north-northeast to south-southeast. None of us could, with a naked eye, see anything but the sharp white line, which seemed to move horizontally.

After about one minute when the object was at 45-50 degrees angle (the object - the eye - the horizontal plane) my son had fetched a pair of binoculars with abt 3,5 x magnification. When I looked through the binoculars I noticed that on both sides of the front part of the white line there was something that could have been wings. I could observe this during about 30 seconds.  After that the line was so distant that the "wings" could no longer be seen.

At the same time, approximately, the line changed to an easterly course and within another 30 seconds it had disappeared due to the distance.

As the line seemed to have the same length all the time we did not think it was an aeroplane leaving "smoke" behind due to athmospheric circumstances. In such cases, as far as I know, a long white stripe can be seen. This was not  the case here. After the change of course the "line" was still straight. I would estimate the length to 15-30 times its thickness. it had sharp outlines.

The observation time was thus about 2 minutes altogether.

Uttran Aug 7, 1946

TO THE AIR DEFENCE , STOCKHOLM

I have the honour of sending you a report concerning an investigation performed with regard to an alleged impact of a rocket missile into a lake. This incident has also been reported over the telephone to the Air Defence on August 12. 

On August 5 at about 5.30 pm two woodsmen, J H Näsberg and S Andersson, were sitting in a rowing boat in a lake called V Skinnsjön. They heard a "hissing" sound. At first they thought the sound came from a car travelling on the road, about 1 km from the site.

Then immediately something fell down into the water 75 metres from where the two men were sitting in their rowing boat. They were seated with their faces towards the place of impact and consequently they could watch the whole course of events.

The phenomenon looked like a water cascade coming from a stone of the size of about 0.5 m of diameter. The area of water, whipped up from the impact, seemed to have a diameter of 2-3 metres.

The sound was not like an ordinary splash but seemed more like an "explosion". The phenomenon reminded of an explosion of dynamite under the surface of water. The column of water was spiral shaped, wide at the surface of water and narrowing upwards, the height being about 2 metres. Otherwise the rest of the lake was completely calm. The phenomenon abated after some seconds and
there were just circles of water visible, without any formation of smoke or steam.

The woodsmen continued their way to the other side of the lake but marked the site with an oar. They returned at about 8.00 pm. No sediment in the lake could be seen. The water in the lake is clear down to a depth of 1,5 metres after which at least 3 metres of soft mud will follow.

The woodsmen initially believed the phenomenon to be a tornado but as it did not move and as it disappeared within a moment they assumed it must be something else. Consequently they reported the incident at the end of the week when paying a visit to Fränsta.

The site of the incident was searched on August 13 by  undersigned with the assistance of Captain Ljungdahl. In spite of drags, water binoculars and a home-made diving equipment no trace of a missile or impact could be found. 

Retrieval of metal parts in this way is not possible due to the nature of the bottom. Electrical search equipment would be required.

However, according to my opinion the phenomenon is merely a minor tornado.

6/8 - Military bulletin

Tuesday, Stockholm (TT), nr 46

The investigation made by the military authorities concerning phenomena sighted in the sky over Sweden during the summer, has not lead to any conclusive results, says Major Nils Ahlgren, Chief of the Swedish Air Defence,  to TT.

In cooperation with the Air Administration and the Defence Research Centre along with other central government authorities, the Defence Staff has made every effort trying to solve the mistery, says Major Ahlgren. The public has been a great help and also the press who have contributed with valuable reports. During the period July 9-12 about 300 reports were received. The number of reports has decreased gradually since then but daily reports are still being received.

Checkups and close investigations, where applicable, carried out on the site,  show that many of the reports must be incorrect. The observers have seen something unusual and have immediately referred the sightings to mystical rockets. In this way a tornado over a lake, for example, has been interpreted as an impact. An aeroplane in the dusk has become a rocket. Investigations concerning some observations reported as impacts have lead to entirely natural explanations. Everyone should very carefully reflect upon their observations in order to avoid a rocket psychosis.

A large number of unanimous and, to all appearances, reliable observations show, however that, in all probability some of the phenomena would originate from some kind of rocket/s.

Having knowledge of the constructions available at the end of the war in this field, especially those of German origin, the more or less fantastic shapes observed should not be inconceivable. So far, however, no parts or debris have been found that  proved to originate from possible rockets. Until this has happended no conclusive statements with regard to the real character of the phenomenon cannot be made.

The findings of slaggy material from serveral sites have proved to be ordinary slag och have thus given no clue. Also they do not originate from meteorites. Even if the theory about meteors cannot be completely refuted it is highly unlikely, according to experts, except for occasional cases.

The large amount of sightings and the usually low altitude would, among other things indicate that the phenomena are not of celestial origin.

One luminous object, photographed by coincidence, could be a meteor, but the analysis has not yet been concluded.

The most probable impact took place in Kölmjärv. Efforts are made to retrieve debris. This work is, however, very hard and could take a long time as the bottom of the lake is very muddy. Every possible action is taken in order to make progress. No decision has yet been made to empty the lake and this will probably not be necessary after the thorough search which is at present being carried out, using ore prospecting instruments etc.

Also other alleged points of impact in Norrbotten are being searched at present. Details on findings from these sites which have been published in the newspapers are, so far, entirely based on vague rumours. 

Reports of observations indicate that the objects would move in half-circles across the country. They enter Swedish territory from the south of Sweden, making a more or less wide half-circle into the country and leave in an easterly direction through Central Sweden or Norrland. It has not been determined whether they disappear into the sea or whether they are continuing in other directions. They could be gyro or radio operated rocket missiles which in some way or other have ended up in Sweden.

Case xxx. 
Slag in a yard near Orebro

From the FOA lab analysis report: 

Sample no 17

Sample taken on Aug 8, 1946 at Rynninge, Örebro

Received at FOA on Aug 10, 1946, together with the following letter.

"With reference to telephone order from Fst/L on Aug 8, 1946 at 01.25 pm we wish to give you the following information regarding a finding at Rynninge, Örebro . The info was sent to TT  through Örebro-Kuriren.

The finding was made on Aug 8, 1946 at about 08.30 am by Mrs Hilma Wallin, Stadsvägen 21, Rynninge, Örebro. On a visit to Mr and Mrs Wallin the following information was received.

Mr and Mrs Wallin have a house of their own, surrounded by a large garden. When Mrs Wallin opened her verandah door in the morning of Aug 8 at about 08.30 she noticed a number of pieces of coke and slag lying around on the gravel yard. She called her husband, a retired workshop worker named Elis Wallin who picked up the pieces. He could see similar pieces lying around also on his neighbour´s gravel yard.

The Wallins have no children and children usually do not play on their yard. The yard was newly raked and possible traces after visitors, walking or driving, would have been visible. Mrs Wallin could not therefore explain how the pieces had ended up on the yards. In any case they had not been there the previous evening when Mr Wallin was raking the yard.

Mr Wallin confirmed Mrs Wallin´s report. Neither of them had seen or heard anything that could be connected to the findings.

The report was given during the morning by Mr Wallin in person to the editor in chief at Örebro-Kuriren, who informed TT.

An article concerning the finding published on Aug 8, 1946 in Örebro-Kuriren is enclosed.

The pieces found on the yard are being sent separately.

All newspapers in the Örebro area have been asked on Aug 8 1946 to report all possible observations or findings immediately to the Fo-staff."

The samples consist of about ten small coke-like pieces, three small pieces of slag plus a redbrown piece obviously consisting of some organic material. The slag and the partly burnt coke look exactly like the ashes that are being raked out of ordinary heating boilers.

All samples are impregnated on the surface with a grey-brown layer of powder (ashes or a fine dust from the gravel yard).

As to the pieces of coke we have examined the surface of fracture and it has the typical appearance of ordinary coke. Pieces of this kind are frequently found in gravel yards in wintertime when ashes are often used for sanding.

The redbrown piece of organic material emits, when heated, a typical odour of burnt fat. A fresh surface of section emits a smell similar to linseed oil. Also an ether extract of the sample emits this smell.

A qualitative analysis of the ashes with regard to calcium gave a positive result. The ashes dissolve in acid generating carbon acid which would indicate presence of chalk. The examinations indicate that the sample consists of a piece of dried cement or a dried lump of paint.

Source: FOA analysis report. Translation by Telle Lilja.

August 9: A theory about ghost rockets dropping caterpillars

In mid-July the southern parts of Sweden (provinces of Skåne, Blekinge and Öland) were invaded by caterpillars of the metal fly (or gamma fly). The caterpillars destroyed crops such as rape, flax and sugar-beets. The layer of caterpillars was so dense that cars skidded on the asphalt where the caterpillars had landed. By July 20 the fly had even reached the northern part of Sweden [Norrbotten]. Reports of large attacks of caterpillars came from other provinces such as Halland and Värmland. The Defence Staff received the following theory concerning a connection between the wave of caterpillars and the ghost bombs:

”To the Air Defence department of the Defence Staff, Stockholm. Herewith I would like to state my view that the ravages of the metal fly has a connection to the ”the ghost bombs”. If you order reports from Svalöv [a plant research institute] and pinpoint them with dates on a map of Sweden and compare them to your compiled reports of the aerial routes of the bombs, not their crash points, you will find that the trajectories coincide with the ravaged areas and that the time from the dropping [from ghost rockets?] of the eggs   
to the development of the caterpillars is constant for all places concerned. Further, rather large wooded areas were attacked from above. Report of falling ”leaflets” from a ”ghost bomb” has also come in. Stockholm, August 9, 1946. /signed/ Jan Flinta.

Sources: Letter from Jan Flinta; Värmlands Folkblad, July 19; Sundsvalls Tidning, July 20; Nya Wermlands-Tidningen, August 1.


Case xxx.
Sighting by a FOA employee

Time: August 10, at about 21.57 hours
Place: Lidingö, a suburb of Stockholm.

The following handwritten four-page report is on file with the Defence Staff archives. The witness, engineer Rolf Moore, employed by FOA, the Research Institute of National Defence, later, during the 1950s, became one of the FOA people called upon for investigations of UFO cases. Here is Moore´s report: 

REPORT  CONCERNING A LIGHT PHENOMENON WHICH MAY HAVE BEEN A "GHOST ROCKET"

Observer: Graduate engineer Rolf Moore and his wife, phone no 276532 (work, Försvarets Forskningsanstalt), 205105 (home)

Point of observation: A flat rock in an open location about 50 m north of my grounds at Strömmigen 2, Sticklinge Udde, Lidingö. The site can also be described as abt 400 m east of the northwest corner Taheiti (?) of Lidingö.

Time of observation:  09.57 pm +- 4 min, Saturday Aug 10, 1946

Direction of observation: When the phenomenon was sighted, by pure coincidence, it was seen in north-northeast (approx i the direction towards Rydboholm) at about 60 degrees angle of elevation. It moved towards southeast, that is with about constant elevation.

When it was straight in easterly position it very quickly reduced speed and disappeared behind the horizon in direction towards east. It disappeared suddenly at about 20 degrees over the horizon which was almost free.

Speed: From the moment the phenomenon was observed until it disappeared it made a trajectory of abt 50 degrees over the sky. The whole course of events was about 2 seconds.

The speed at the beginning of the trajectory was about 5-10 degrees/sec, in the middle about 20 degrees/sec and at the end it was very high, maybe 50 degrees/sec.

Appearance: Something like the masthead light of a ship at a distance of abt 200 m and with a diffuse light due to the haze in the air. It also looked like a spot of a searchlight against the clouds, though it was much smaller, less than 1/10 degrees but much more powerful.

Nothing else except for the illuminuous spot could be seen but I had a feeling all the time that it consisted of a navigation light or a small window in the front part of a moving object. At the time I did not yet associate the light with "ghost rockets", I just made the reflection that this illuminuous object could not be a shooting star. When the spot disappeared it seemed to be hidden by the unvisible object.

Colour: Pale, diffuse white, like a spot of a searchlight against clouds or like a masthead light in haze.

Level of brightness: In relation to the stars that were barely visible in the fog the spot of light was very powerful, it was not dazzling though.

Due to the fog the light was softened and we would not, most certainly, have noticed it if we had looked in a direction 45 degrees away from it.

Towards the middle of the trajectory the spot of light was, however, considerably more powerful and would perhaps have caught our attention.

I estimate the level of brightness to be twice the one of Venus at its brightest stage, seen through the same fog.

Altitude: Thanks to exceptional weather conditions prevailing at the time I will try to estimate the altitude of the object.

A couple of hours earlier I had returned from a boat trip on Stora Värtan at Rydboholm. At 07.00-07.30 pm I had noticed a dense cloud bank approaching from southwest over the almost cloudfree sky. The bank looked like typical fog at sea but it was more diffuse and considerably more powerful. In comparison with som ordinary clouds I estimated the bank to be 1500-2000 m of thickness. 

It seemed to be completely homogene from the ground and upwards which was proved later as it gradually obscured the setting sun (which became pale yellow instead of red).

Through this fog we could barely see land at a distance of 5 km towards the light evening sky. When I noticed the phenomenon I had just been watching fireworks at a distance of 300 m. De biggest rockets reached an altitude of abt 100 m and they were not the least diffuse. Other rockets at a distance of 500 m were very diffuse.

As the light phenomenon had the same degree of diffuse light (or was even more diffuse) than the stars in the sky one could presume that it was moving on an altitude of at least 1500-2000 m. It was also probably somewhat diffuse itself (window or something like that).

Trajectory in relation to the stars in the sky 

As the phenomenon may have been a shooting star (not according to my opinion, however, as it was too bright and of too long duration) I decided to try to identify the stars that were faintly visible through the fog. The sky cleared up already after about 20 minutes enabling me to identify some stars. Subject to a marginal of error of 10 degrees I can describe the trajectory as follows.

I discovered the phenomenon in the neighbourhood of Cassiopeija and Perseus and it was slowly moving towards the connecting line between them. Then it moved on at higher speed towards ... of Pegasus (the upper star to the right in the square of  Pegasus) and then turned at high speed towards .... of Pegasus (the lower to the right in the square) where it disappeared. The trajectory was parabolic with "vertex" just behind the point of disappearance, between ... and the horizon. The trajectory was straight at the beginning.

Special observations: When I first noticed the phenomenon the speed varied from high to low in a hesitant way. The reason could be a predominating vertical course with small "guiding impulses" on the sides.

If I would make a guess regarding point of impact I would guess at Torsbyfjärd.

Stockholm Aug 12, 1946
Rolf Moore
