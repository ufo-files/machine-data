          I don't understand why Ford has this problem. He either fails or
          pretends not to understand that natural selection has been observed
          and is well understood: it is almost tautologically simple, and its
          prediction of machanisms for heredity and mutation led to the
          investigation and discovery of DNA and genes which are both well
          understood. The action of mutagens on genetic material is also well
          understood. Natural selection is a filter by which the mutated genetic
          material is passed on to surviving offspring.


          The mutagens are mostly environmental: natural radiation, natural
          chemicals etc. But as we have become more industrialised/etc can also
          be artificial that we have released into the environment without
          realising the consequences.
          We use our understanding of this in biotech, genetic modification etc.
          Which we would not even be able to do if Ford and the other
          creationists were correct.
          The same mechanisms of garbled copying of genetic material caused by
          mutagens are behind all known variations of evolution. If only
          creationists and other religiously ignorant people would realise this.
          Evolution happens faster in smaller populations. Even Darwin
          understood this. It leads to what palaeontologists call punctuated
          equilibrium which is _not_ an alternative to natural selection. It
          just means that it happened in a smaller gene pool. The resulting
          population expands to fill its ecological niche - and having expanded
          provides a much larger gene pool which evolves more slowly.
          Genetic drift is what happens when there is a large gene pool and
          evolution happens more slowly. Natural selection still plays a part in
          both of these. Because lethal mutations don't survive. Others don't
          reproduce. And those that produce more offspring that survive to
          reproduce, enentually "take over".
          Ford's problem is that he doesn't know what evolution is, how it works
          and what causes it.
          So he looks at things like natural selection, punctuated equilibrium
          and genetic drift in total isolation from each other and complete
          ignorance of what they are, just as phrases that carry no real meaning
          to him. He imagines that these are all completely different and
          incompatible instead of overlapping regions on a spectrum.
