  the end of 1993. It was the only F-111 base in Oxfordshire when it was
  open. I spent many a night doing my washing at the sit and spin on
  nearby RAF Croughton and can assure you that it is nothing but an
  antennae farm (ie, communications base). Having jogged down (what was)
  the runway, it was obvious that the last time it was in condition for
  jet aircraft was almost as long ago as when the WWII fighter pits off to
  the side still housed Spitfires. I have driven by Croughton in the last
  8 months and it is still the same.
  But then, maybe things are not what they appear to be.....I am not
  considering the underground 12,000ft runway that pops up in the middle
  of the night to receive globe trotting Aurora jets. I once saw Sean
  Connery in Oxford so it must be true! (cue the Bond theme)
