  I have been interested in the Rendlesham Incident since hearing about it in the 90s. It is one of those
  cases that presents really strong evidence through credible eyewitness testimony that something
  extraordinary happened, and rather recently compared to the Roswell Incident. This wasn't a case of just
  one or two people experiencing a UFO, but many in a professional capacity who got an up close view of a
  stationary craft. Like many on this forum I come here with an open mind and curiosity about UFOs,
  something I have been interested in since early childhood. I have read several accounts about the
  Rendlesham Incident including "Left At Eastgate" by Warren & Robbins. Thank you to the those who were
  actually a part of that extraordinary event for taking the time to participate in this forum.
