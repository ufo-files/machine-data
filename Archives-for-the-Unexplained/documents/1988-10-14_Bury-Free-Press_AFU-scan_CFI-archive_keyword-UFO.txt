Where are the little
  green Martians!
SIR, - Re the story of the mysterious event in Fornham
All Saints' sugar beet field ( BFP September 30).
  It certainly would appear that something very odd oc­
curred and I am wondering when an official investigation
will take place- if only to set our minds at rest and satis­
fy our insatiable curiosities.
  As far as I know, only wideloads, fire engines, police
vehicles, air liners and ambulances carry flashing
beacons at night, but none of these could produce the 80
foot diameter burn mark found in the field.
  I now look forward to reports of little green Martians
with eyes on stalks and antennaed heads roving around
the Suffolk countryside, sampling the delights of roasted
earthly sugar beet.
  What pity tht a camera wasn't available to record this
remarkable event �nd congratulations to the observant
young man at Tut Hill.
                                        V. L. PITCHFORD
 Cavendish Close,
 Thurston,
 Bury St Edmunds.
