  Georgina Bruni makes reference to a witness called Gary Collins who had several friends from the joint
  bases.

  He recalls that the friends were paged to return to their base on a particular night while they were all in a
  public house in Alderton.

  Gary explains on his drive back home to Capel St Andrew, he sighted a craft in the air on turning into Lions
  Corner at Capel Green.

  Having looked on the map of the area his journey from Alderton would have taken him to Capel St Andrew
  firstly. This has puzzled me so does anyone know whether Lions Corner is at Capel Green or Capel St
  Andrew

  May be its a simple typing error on behalf of Georgina Bruni or Gary decided to do around trip to see what
  was happening at the base.
