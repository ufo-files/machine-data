Fränsta Hotels

Fränsta, July 11, 1946.

To the air defense department, Stockholm.

I hereby report the following information regarding observations of an airborne projectile over Medelpad:
It had been observed at around 14:30-15:00 o’clock by two people on the slope of Slättberget near the village of Kälen, south of Ljungan. Lake Roggsjön was in clear sight. The projectile was first observed south-east - south-southeast, at about 45 degrees above the horizon. It descended consistenly and seemed to disappear in the direction east - east-southeast, about 15 degrees above the horizon. The phenomenon has, more or less, been accurately described in the newspapers. One observer argues that it had a flat shape, while another claimed it had exploded and eventually disappeared. The whole incident lasted for maybe three seconds.

Yours faithfully,

Nils Lencker
