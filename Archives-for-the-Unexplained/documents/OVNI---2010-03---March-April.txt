Occurrence Reports" (MORs) and air-miss incidents, reported by aircrews.
They have no specific responsibility for "UFOs" per se, but if UFO incidents are reported as MORs or
Air-misses - as in the recent case involving a police helicopter over Birmingham - these will be assessed
in the usual way by a joint CAA-MoD working party.
I'm assuming this situation will continue after the closure of the MoD's UFO desk.
So I would suggest that any incidents reported by civilian air traffic controllers or aircrew should be
reported to the CAA as MORs.
Other than CAA the only other official agency to whom the public could report UFO incidents would be
the police. But the police (responsible to the Home Office) have no responsibility to investigate, log or
analyse UFO reports made to them - unlike their French counterparts.
So unless you are aware that a particular incident has been reported to them, it's unlikely you will make
any progress as police UFO reports tend to be destroyed quickly. Of course in the past, police had
standard instructions to pass their reports to the MoD. But that policy has now been rescinded.
Not a very satisfactory situation, but I think this was inevitable. I hope this is of some use,
