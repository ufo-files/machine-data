  From the Moon series of highly detailed travel guides, for adventurers, not tourists. - "Arizona Traveler's
  Handbook takes readers on a journey through this state's wild history, rugged geography, and varied
  cultures. Authors Bill Weir and Robert Blake guide travelers down the raging waters of the Colorado
  River, up the heights of Humphrey's Peak, through the ancient pueblos, and into the political power center
  of Phoenix." -- From the publisher. Includes 54 maps. Sixth Edition--September 1996. -- tmp
