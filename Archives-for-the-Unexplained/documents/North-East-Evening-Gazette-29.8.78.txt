CHRYSIS - the Cleveland UFO watch-

operations into Tyne and Wear and may
even go as far as the Scottish border.

people outside the organisation it can draw on for
information and help.
The group has already held merger talks with
Sapro, the Sunderland Aerial Phenomena Re-
search Organisation, and plans to make contact
with the Borders Research group.
Chrysis spokesman Mr. Brian Straight said
today: "The Idea is to make the coverage of a larger
area more possible. If we have an Integrated unit
covering the area, it is easier to deal with
Information."
Mr. Straight, a 23-year-old research student from
Redear, said the Sunderland group has between
ten and 15 members.
"As far as I can gather, he said, "they will be
merging with us and taking our name."
The group already covers North Yorkshire as
far south as Searborough and has been kept busy
over the past fortnight by a wave of UFO sightings
in the area.
