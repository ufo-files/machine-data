MISCELLANEOUS RAVINGS:

      ITEM: On August 4th (coincidentally our 54th birthday), your edi­
tor attended a Conclave of Highly Evolved Beings in New York City,brought
on by the fact that veteran contactee Wayne Aho (pronounced AHHH-ho) &
wife Lee were visiting the City from their home in Tacoma, Washington.
Also present were such spiritual leaders as John Keel, Harold Salkin, and
Barry Hoffman (son of the late Harry H; see elsewhere in this issue.) -
Rounding out the group were two young ladies named Mary Gallante and Beth
Lovitt.
      Wayne was reminiscing about the many years he has spent pushing for­
ward, as it were, in the UFO & related fields, while your editor was try­
ing to ascertain just why nothing concrete.has come of any of this. The
answer was - and is - that there are no answers.
      ITEM: There's no connection at all with UFOs, as far as we know,
but we just have to tell you about the good fortune _that occurred about
July 20th to our good friend Mel Fisher of Key West. Your. editor has been
interested in treasure hunting (on land) ever since the late 1950's, when
we dug up quite a bit of gold & other artifacts in Peru. In recent years
the master of under-water treasure hunting has been Fisher, who has found
millions in old Spanish gold & silver off the coast of southern Florida,
though it cost him millions to do so.
      Now, finally, Fisher has located the Atocha, a Spanish galleon which
sank in 1622 with JOO to 400 million dollars worth of loot aboard! It took
Fisher about 15 years to find it, and it cost the lives of one son (a div­
er) and daughter-in-law, plus two other people.
      Fisher, who operates a treasure museum a few blocks from us in Key
West, has tremendous overhead expenses from salvage ships ahd personnel.
He also took on the Florida state and federal government, re taxes, and
he won! By the time he struck it rich, he had signed away all but about
5% of the proceeds, just to keep going; but he's made history, and has
