Scunthorpe Wave of UFO Sightings. Nigel H. Watson.
In this article I should like to cover a veritable wave of UFO sight­
ings made in and around the Scunthorpe region between Monday 25.10.71
and Friday 29.10.71. Much of the information has been obtained from
one of the main local papers; the Scunthorpe Evening Telegraph, and
from the witnesses themselves. For instance, my own grandmother saw
one of the UFOs, although personally I saw nothing of the phenomena.
Also since more information is needed on a lot of the sightings, in
this particular article I am merely going to give a basic listing of
the reports and deal with each separate case in individual articles
etc.
Scunthorpe to my mind has been well endowed with UFO sightings, for
instance see the SLUFOSG Newsletter June, July, August issue. Besides
these I have recently been informed of further Ufo activity in past
years after having a letter of mine published in the Scunthorpe Star.
These I should like to relate in later articles, and I would like to
thank the persons who replied to this letter.
Now for the listing:
1 ) Monday 25.10.71, some time during the night.
Whilst in High Lees playing field Mr. David Harrison saw a red oval
object, which appeared to be over Messingham, he did not bbther to
watch it go away. Source, Mr. Harrison.
2) Wednesday 27.10.71, 7- 7.30pm.
Whilst walking up Mary Street which is in the town centre, my grand­
mother saw a brilliant red light which looked like n balloon, go up
and then go down changing to orange as it did so. Source, my grand­
mother Mrs. Towl, and the Scunthorpe Evening Telegraph 2.11.71.
3 ) Wednesday 27.10.71 , around 8pm.
Paul Richardson of Dewsbury Avenue, nged 10 years, saw a "bright red
light that seemed to come down, changed to an orange light and moved
off terribly fast." Two other separate sightings were made by two
brothers, Richard and David Langton, and Linda Robinson. Source, Scun­
thorpe Evening Telegraph, 28.10.71.
4) Wednesday 27.10.71.
A person whose name I don't know saw a red pulsating light to the
west whilst walking his dog. I hope to gain further information later.
5) Thursday 28.10.71, teatime.
Mrs. Whybrow and her mother Mrs. Shelton, saw an object like two sauc­
ers, which was high in the sky and moving slowly. Source, Scunthorpe
Evening Telegraph 29.10.71.
6) Friday 29.10.71,8.45am.
Mr. Hills was driving towards Crowle Crossraads, and saw a hovering
port-holed craft, which seemed to be tubular in shape. Source, Scun­
thorpe Evening Telegraph 2.11.71.
Supplementary List, these have no specified date reported.
1. Wednesday and Thursday.
Five boys saw a UFO with green, red and blue lights around it. Also
they saw something which buzzed like a refrigerator and was red and
flashing. Source, Scunthorpe EveningTelegraph 30.10.71.
2. Two girls both aged 11 , saw a red thing which went from Lincoln
Gardens across to the Redbourn sports ground. Source, Scunthorpe Eve­
ning Telegraph 30.10.71.
3. Mrs. Marsh of the Riddings estate, saw a UFO which was going slowly
across the sky. It was oblong with two long exhaust pipes. Source,
Scunthorpe Evening Telegraph 2.11.71.
4. Four boys saw a UFO chasing two geese. It split into six and flew
away from the geese. The UFOs were silver with black patches on them.
Source, Scunthorpe Evening Telegraph 5.11.71.
Obviously more information is needed especially on the sightings in
the supplementary list, whilst the other reports are fairly well
covered.
I should like to follow some of these reports up and as m have said
they will be covered in this Newsletter.
It is my hope to compile a comprehensive list of Ufos throughout
Lincolnshire, this will be a difficult task and I would appreciate
any help. The reports will appear in this publication and Flying
Saucer Review.
