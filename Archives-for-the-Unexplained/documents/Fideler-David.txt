                                An Introduction to
                         MICHIGAN ANOMALY RESEARCH


  Michigan Anomaly Research, founded in 1974, is a private federation of individuals investigating
unusual phenomena. It is our goal to investigate and map out unusual phenomena within the state of
Michigan, and, working with researchers around the globe, to better understand the nature of strange
phenomena.

   Strange things are constantly happening. UFOs are reported and unusual creatures are said to be
seen, and these anomalies warrant serious consideration. Whether resulting from physical or psycho-
logical mechanisms, or both, they shou ld be seriously investigated by qualified individuals. As re-
searchers of unusual phenomena, we greatly admire the work of the late Charles Fort, who collected
reports of unusual events that couldn't be pigeonholed in the present-day paradigm of established sci-
ence . We feel that al l phenomena, whether generally accepted or not, deserve scientific investigation;
Michigan Anomaly Research is especially interested in these types of "Fortean" phenomena :


                          •UFOs
                          •Bigfoot and other Mystery Animals
                          •Hauntings and poltergeists
                          •All types of "paranormal" phenomena



   Michigan Anomaly Research is not a 'hobby' organization - there are no dues or members, al-
though individuals w ith demonstrable interest and qualifications may become Research Associates.
Because we believe in taking a serious approach and working with serious individuals, Research Asso-
ciate status is available by invitation only . Interested persons may apply for further information .


  Michigan Anomaly Research believes that heavy-handedness should be discouraged when ap-
proaching any problem; -therefore it is our aim to coordinate research and not direct it. We are seeking
an informal and dynamic network of communicatioh, while protecting the integrity of our Research
Associates.

   Michigan Anomaly Research tries to investigate all reports of unusual phenomena originating from
the state. Research Associates conduct local on-the-spot investigations whenever possible, and they
are notified of recent reports from their area . Time is an important element in work of this type, so
individuals and law enforcement a_gencies are encouraged to refer reports as quickly as possible . Mi -
chigan Anomaly Research has great interest in older incidents also - and reports of a historical na-
ture-which often prove to be extremely valuable . Witness names are protected in all instances. Any-
one with information on strange phenomena is cordially invited to contact Michigan Anomaly Re-
search.
                                             RESEARCH
   Data, as data, is useless unless something is done with it. Michigan Anomaly Research, while col-
lecting and preserving information, is also involved with several long-range research projects . An
openended catalog of the state's unusual phenomena is the result of a continuing search for patterns
in the geographical distribution of Fortean events in Michigan . Other studies involve setting up spe-
cialized recording instruments in areas prone to Fortean phenomena . Our findings are shared with re-
searchers around the world, and detailed information about on-going studies is available upon re-
quest .



                                              SERVICES
   Certain services are available to Research Associates and law enforcement agencies .


  Research Associates have unique access to a special catalog of the state's unusual phenomena re-
ports. The catalog contains 300 entries, and it is now possible for every serious investigator in Michi-
gan to obtain a printout of all reports from his or her local area. All Michigan reports are crossindexed
by city and county, and Data Request Forms may be obtained by writing .


   Law enforcement agencies are encouraged to seek assistance from Michigan Anomaly Research in
the event of emergency situations resulting from the occurrence- either real or reported- of unusual
 phenomena . In such a case professionals in psychology, sociology, and other fields are available for
 immediate consultation . In the event of repeated activity within a localized area (such as UFO sight-
