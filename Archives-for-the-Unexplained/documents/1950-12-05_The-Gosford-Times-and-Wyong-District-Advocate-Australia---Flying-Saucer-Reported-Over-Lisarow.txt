         'Flying Saucer'

                   Reported
             Over Lisarow
             'Last            Monday               evening
         about   6  o'clock       saw      a I
         strange round   silvery   object
         moving slowly in     the     sky,':
         writes Mrs  H. Mackey, of Hill
         View. Lisarow.
                 'It seemed            miles          away
         and     was     going        in      a     south
         easterly direction.
                '1 pointed it out  to my
         son    and a  friend who seemed
         to think       it was        a    plane with
         the sun                             it.
                        shining       on
             'But        we      could            hear     no
         sound.
                'It did        not    seem     like   a

         plane to me           and        hearing that
         a     flying   saucer        had         been    re
         ported in         Brisbane,               I     now
         believe it      was      a   flying saucer
         I saw.'
