                            UFOLOGY AT THE CROSSROADS?
There are signs of increasing frustration among the ranks of prominent UFO researchers. There appear to be
more UFO reports coming in daily than ever before and we have heard from various parts of the country that
there is a 'flap' going on in their area. There is now a slow realization among UFO researchers, that the 'flap'
is not only in 'their' area, but is countrywide (and judging by the latest reports from Central America, world­
wide!).
There seems little media interest in the fact that what appears to be widespread 'Alien' activity has now been
reported by the general public and in many instances been recorded on video. The general attitude appears
to be that of giving UFO activity a ten minute slot between the end of the weather forecast and the results of
the 2.30 at Chepstow. However, all may not be as it seems, could there be a subtle sidetracking of UFO
information going on? A 'source' has recently informed us, that yes, there is a 'D' notice on 'Flying Triangle'
activity. This was rumored some months ago, but it could explain some aspects of the media 'indifference'
being experienced at present.
The popular press is presently intent in a cut-throat circulation battle and headlines are given over to such
important news as a woman super-gluing her hand to her husbands willy' (I kid you not). No doubt it sells
papers, rather than news of a UFO following aircraft over the Midlands, or a FT' s hovering over motorways
and Nuclear Power Stations.
However, there is a glimmer of hope that the 'message' is getting through. We are now receiving an increas­
ing number of calls and letters from people, moving in what appear to be intelligence area 's. They are
voicing some concern and curiosity as to what is really going on. If only a small percentage of the stories that
we hear are true, then we should all be very much concerned with the way certain events are taking over the
running of our lives. Should we believe that there are underground bases, situated around the countryside?
Are there really armed personnel in unmarked uniforms prepared to challenge you if you wander off the
beaten track?
According to another, seemingly well-informed 'source', there is an underground tunnel system under the old
American airbase at Woodbridge ( as claimed by Larry Warren in his book 'Left A t East Gate '). The tunnel
system not only exists, but links up with the nearby Bentwaters Air Base. A story recently published in 'UFO
Magazine' (Quest International Publications), stated that there could be a tunnel system from Rudloe Manor
through to Whitehall in London. A 'source' has confirmed to us that there is a widespread underground
tunnel system throughout England, linking up a number of strategic locations! It is by all accounts gigan­
tic, with one of the minor locations apparently having some 1 60 miles of tunnels in its immediate area. This
extensive tunnel system may have been installed during the recent 'Cold War' years and have nothing to do
with FT activity. But what of the reports coming to light of FT's being seen apparently landing in remote
area's and more than one pursuing and curious witness being suddenly confronted by a roadblock and fully
armed militia, barring their way. These are not isolated stories, but are more like individual pieces of a jigsaw
coming together and making up the overall picture. Could these events be the result of secret Stealth triangu­
lar craft having bases in the centre of the remote countryside? We believe that triangular Stealth aircraft exist,
but they are in no way connected to the huge craft moving slowly and silently across our countryside at night !

                                    INVISffiLE 'FLYING TRIANGLES'?
                                           A Report By David Cayton
 I have made out a list of FT reports, not all the witnesses returned their sighting forms. Some were happy to
 talk to me, while others were reluctant to go into print, however, most were O.K . regarding publicity.
 Significantly some of the FT reports were from the same motorway, adjacent locations, but different dates.
 We now have 2 more pilots who saw the 'FT' delta shaped shadow (on the ground) flying past them on a
 bright sunny afternoon:
                                  Second Pilots Sighting Of The FT Shadow

 Brothers john and Paul Angus were flying their Microlight aircraft and were returning to Houghton, near
 Preston after spending an 'Aviator's Week-end' at Cranfield Eardrum on Sunday 6th July 1 997.
 At approx. 4. 1 5pm on a bright clear sunny afternoon, they were flying in the 'Cheshire Low-Level Corridor'
 at 1 200ft altitude, when they both saw a huge delta-shaped shadow pass below them, travelling in the opposite
 direction (heading South).
 Their airspeed was approx. 50mph and they estimated the ' shadow's' speed at about 200mph. Like the
 previous pilot's report (see UFO Magazine Jan/Feb), they saw no solid structured craft to create the shadow
 on the ground. John and Paul estimated their position to be near Newton-le-willows, just over the Cheshire
 border into Lancashire.
                                   M56 FT Sighting on 26-11-97 at 9.30pm

 Stuart Nelson and his girlfriend Alison, were travelling up the A49 and were just joining the M56 at Junction
 1 0, near Stretton, when their attention was drawn to a light in the sky. They were just turning on the
 roundabout when they saw the lighted object, which they identified as a triangular craft hovering over the
 fields to their left. As they drove on towards Junction 9, they saw the FT suddenly move from N to S and
 hover directly over the motorway. (This location is only two and a half miles due west of the See/ families
 encounter on June 1st at Junction 9) . As he glanced in his mirror, to change lanes, Stuart took his eyes off
 the FT for a moment and when he looked back, the FT had disappeared.
 The FT was described as being at an angle of 30/40 degrees, there was a white light in the centre, with other
 colours, red and orange also visible.
                          M56 Again! (Bank Holiday Monday 26 -5-97 at 1145pm

 The time of this sighting ( 1 1 .45pm) was the same time as the Seele's sighting 6 days later on Sunday June 1 st.,
 but 1 0 miles west towards Chester, approaching Junction 1 2,near Frodsham.
 Sharon and John Bates were driving along the M56, heading East, returning from Anglesey, to their home in
 Ramsbottom. Their two young children were asleep on the back seat of the car. Shortly after they passed
 Helsey point (on their right), they noticed some strange lights in the sky, further ahead. Two minutes or so
 later, they realised that the red lights they had seen, were on a vertical triangular object motionless in the
 sky, above the hill near Frodsham. The dark shaped FT was silhouetted against the night sky (it was a fine dry
 night). They noted that it had a series of flashing/chasing red lights down the right-hand side of the FT and
 also along the base of the triangle.
 As they approached closer, the FT made an 'odd sideways jerky motion' for short distances, first to the left
 and then to the right. The FT appeared to be standing upright, with its 'nose' at the top. As they drove on
 and neared Junction 1 2, the FT started to move sideways and in their direction, due East. When they reached
 the Sutton Weaver concrete rail bridge over the motorway at Junction 1 2, the FT just suddenly disappeared!
 They had watched the FT for 5/6 minutes and estimated it to be about half a mile from the motorway.

                                      Manchester Ship Canal Sighting

 John, a retired Special-Constable with the rank of sergeant, was walking his dog alongside the Manchester
 Ship Canal, at Latchford on the 28th November 1 997 at about 8 .45pm. When he saw some strange lights
 moving towards him, ''I had never seen anything like in my life before", he commented later.
 The source of his amazement was a 'V' shaped row of red lights (4 on the left and three on the right) moving
 slowly overhead and past his position on the canal. The 'pulsing' red lights were about the size of 4" at arms
 length and they 'opened and closed' like a slow wing beat. John estimated the lights to be at a height of about
 1 ,000ft. He observed them for about six minutes and when last seen, they were moving in line with the ship
 canal and heading in the direction of Runcorn. towards the 'Fiddlers Ferry Power Station' .
                                  Triangular Formation Seen Over Holland

 A telephone report from a Mr Gordon Coles, currently living in the southern Limburg region of Holland , in
 Tegelan, told of his observation while walking his two dogs along the bank of the river Maas at about
 1 1 . 30pm on Sunday 6th July 1 997.
 It was a clear night, with good visibility, when he first observed three reddish/orange lights in triangular
 formation moving down towards him. When the lights were about 250 metres away and 250 metres up, he
 realised that the three lights were on the apex of a giant matt black triangle shaped craft, about the size of a
 Boeing 747 aircraft.
 It was when the craft came to an abrupt halt for one or two seconds, that he noticed a 1 0 metre diameter cone
 shaped protrusion from the lower centre of the object. He could hear no sound at the time and then after a
 couple of seconds or so of hovering, the craft 'flipped over' in its back, (inverted), executed a rapid 'U' turn
 and was lost to sight !
 Two minutes later, three pairs of F 1 6 fighter aircraft, roared overhead, following in the direction of the FT.
 These aircraft were shortly followed by a Boeing 707 AWAC aircraft, climbing on full power and heading in
 the same direction!
 He noted that his two dogs did not appear to have been affected by this event.
 The following day, he enquired about the aircraft movements at the nearby airbase of Brunssum, but he got
 short shrift from the American who answered his call..... He later contacted the local Police, but was again
 'fobbed off'.
