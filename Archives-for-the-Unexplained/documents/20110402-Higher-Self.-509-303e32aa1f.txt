   Understanding the I AM Self for Our Spir-tual Growth
   Much has been written about the I AM Self Presence that we are told we
   have, albeit much of it is contradictory and somewhat confusing to
   understand. The viewpoints concerning the I AM Self or I AM Presence
   vary as much as different c-urch denominational beliefs concerning the
   tenants of C-ristianity.
   These varying perspectives include the belief that I AM Self is a part of
   us that is d-vine and connected to G-d, or specifically a part of us that
   is Go-. Others see it as part of the Higher Self, C-rist Self, True Self or
   Inner Self.
   While none of these examples would be consistent with how our etheric
   teachers define the I AM Self, they are useful in pointing to the importance
   of accessing the consciousness, perspective, outlook, attitude and
   behavior of a higher version of ourselves.
   A perfect example for us to emulate
   I AM Self is actually a facsimile (or example) of the highest version of
   ourselves that we can become, and the best example of how we should
   function on a day-to-day basis. This is even true concerning the appearance
   of the I AM Self; as it looks like us, but it's the best version of how we
   could ever look.
   I recall on one occasion that my teachers showed me my I AM Self while
   I was in meditation. I was taken aback at how 'perfect' it looked in
   appearance. It was about 25 years old, in perfect condition and dressed
   very stylishly in casual clothes.
