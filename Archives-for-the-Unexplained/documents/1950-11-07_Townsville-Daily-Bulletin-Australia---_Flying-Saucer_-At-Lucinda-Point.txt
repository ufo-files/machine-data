Townsville Daily Bulletin (Qld. : 1907 - 1954), Tuesday 7 November 1950, page 2


       'Flying Saucer' At
          Lucinda Point
           According        to  reports    from
       Lucinda     Point,        object which
                                 an
       may     lisive been   a 'flying saucer'
       was    seen    over the wharf   there at
       a   recent    date.
           The          object, said to   have
       been  semi-circular    in shape,    was
       spotted by waterside workers          us
       they were    loading a ship one   day.
       A man    in the hold of the ship saw
       the   object overhead, high in the
       sky. In order to obtain a better
       view   he went    on deck.
          Shortly afterwards it began       to
       wobble and     moved   out to sea  at a
       very   fast rate, soon  to be   lost to
       sight.
         'It seemed    to travel faster than
       a   bullet' said the waterslder.
