               Critics of the theory that flying saucer reports are due to U. S.
  Government activities (starting about 1947) have often pointed to pre-1947
  sightings as evidence against this explanation. The present article will at­
  tempt to show that, in at least one widely publicized pre-1947 sighting, there
  is strong evidence of an ordinary earthly origin.
               The sighting in question was quoted (with an error in the date) on

  appears on page 361 of his book "Altai-Himalaya" (F. A. Stokes Co.,N.Y., 1929).
  The following quotation is from that book:
               "Seven of us began to watch this unusual bird. At the same moment
