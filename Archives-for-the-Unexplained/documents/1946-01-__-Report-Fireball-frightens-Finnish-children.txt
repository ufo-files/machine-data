Case xxx.
Fireball frightens Finnish children 

Time: Early winter 1946 (exact date unknown), at 17.00 hours (local time).
Place: Räimä, Finland.

”A 12 year-old girl, together with her brother and sister, 8 and 10, had been to a neighbour fetching a kitten. The children were walking home along a creek on a horsetrail. Suddenly they got dazzled by an enormous light. A bang was heard and after the light had been visible for a few seconds at the same spot it shrunk and disappeared. The small children burst out in tears and started to run home. The observer had the kitten in a basket. After stumbling around in the snow for a while she managed to get back home after her brother and sister. The children told their father and he went out together with some of the children to investigate the place. He found an area on the ground were the snow had melted and the ground had been coloured. The observer don’t remember what colour.”

Status: Probable meteor. 
Source: Lasse Ahonen (editor): Vuosiraportti 92 (Finnish ufological yearbook 1992), pages 49-50. Translation by Telle Lilja and Tomas Ericsson.
