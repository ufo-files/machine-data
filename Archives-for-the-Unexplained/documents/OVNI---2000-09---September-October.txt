      MYSTERIOUS CRAFT CONTINUE TO PASS OVER BRADFORD

Strange craft were observed flying over Bradford, West Yorkshire, some months ago and what started off
as something unusual has developed into a real mystery. The local investigator and video photographer,
Russell Kellett has been filming strange lights passing over Bradford and more recently a falling object re­
sembling a structure similar to a hang glider was caught by him on video. ''I was gobsmacked" commented
Russell.
The main events began about last June, when Russell's attention was attracted by an unusual craft flying
overhead. It sounded similar to an aircraft but was not showing any standard navigation lights (Red-Port,            •



Green - Starboard) or anti-collision strobe lights. Instead it had a triangular set of white lights with a red
light in the centre (see page 3) and was flying ''broad side" forward. This is an identical lighting sequence
on unidentified craft seen during the Belgian ''Flying Triangle" flap 1989/90.
We checked with one of our contacts, a regular airline pilot, who confirmed that such lights did not con­
form to CAA (Civil Aviation Authority) requirements and were strictly illegal.
Russell Kellett has taken extensive footage of similar lights both in a triangular and diamond pattern. He
has also filmed balls of light zig-zagging over Bradford in the early hours of the morning. He has tak�n
these over a period of several months and up to the present day. ·He has also gone to some lengths to try
and establish what the lights are precisely. To this end, he has obtained the services ofProfessor Green of
the Electronic Imaging Department at Coventry University. An enhancement of a three light pattern
video'd over Bradford on 16th June 2000 is shown on the opposite page.
While nobody is in a position to positively identify what these craft are, it raises three distinct possibilities.
(A) An Alien ''Flying Triangle" (They do not always travel 'point' first).
(B) A conventional aircraft carrying lights identical to an 'FT' in order to confuse observers.
(Disinformation?). This is a probability, as two white lights could be positioned, one on each wing and one
under the tail. The red central light would present no problem. There was also a deep droning noise
recorded, similar to an aircraft, but also similar to FT's video'd over Belgium 1989/90.
(C) A secret aircraft development (In that case why draw attention to the craft by sporting large white
lights?)
According to Russell, he initially reported the lights to various authorities including Civil Aviation and
Military (West Drayton included). From that time on, he noted that the lights/craft passed over Bradford a
few degrees off their usual course, as though trying to confuse any observers.
(Russell was so engrossed with the strange lights, that he has purchased a new Sony ''Handicam" video
camera, with night shot facility including an infra-red illuminator).
Even stranger was the object seen falling down from the sky at 8pm on Sunday 27th August. Russell luck­
ily had his video camera with him at the time and snatched a few brief seconds of tape before the object
disappeared, out of sight at ground level. The camera was set on auto-focus and the object went out of
shot, as the camera focussed on a wire fence in the direct line of sight. However, if Russell had paused to
adjust the camera setting, he would have failed to obtain any video of the object at all.
An opinion has been expressed that the object was a hang glider, actually it is more in the shape of an air­
craft ejector seat. However this appears unlikely as the object fell slowly to the ground, with a slight rota­
tion. Subsequent enhancement has shown that it was definitely not a hang glider. It was noted that the
grass in the foreground moved in the wind from left to right, but the object bariked to the left as it fell.
According to Russell, a number of helicopters were seen hovering around the Queensbury/Bradford area
on the following day. So the mystery continues.. ... .

            FLYING TRIANGLE SEEN NEAR SWADLINCOTE
 At 9.50pm on the night of the 23rd August this year, Mr George Brealey was standing on the path outside
his home at Midway, Swadlincote, Derbyshire, when he looked up and saw something strange.
Passing overhead "at speed", Mr Brealey saw a large black triangular shaped craft, travelling from the
Southeast to the Northwest. He believes that he watched for about two minutes, but that is quite a long time
and it could have been less.
The triangular craft, which was completely black apart from a central white light on the underside, was
silhouetted against the light night sky. Mr Brealey estimated the speed to be about 8 00mph. This is in sharp
contrast to the much slower speed of about 30mph that is often reported.
The Swadlincote area, is not far away from Bretby, which was the scene of extensive 'FT' activity a few
years ago.
Mr Brealey stated that he was able to check the time of the event, from his wristwatch. The night sky was
starry and the 'FT' was sharply defined against the starry night sky background. The craft disappeared out
of sight behind some nearby terraced houses, opposite to Mr Brearley's home.
Shortly after the incident, Mr Brealey phoned Omar Fowler from a nearby BTP ayphone and reported what
he had ·seen. He made the point that by living in the vicinity of the East Midlands Airport, he was well aware
of what normal aircraft looked like.

           WAS THERE A UFO CRASH COVER-UP AT CANNOCK CHASE?
           With thanks to Irene Bott, Nick Redfern, Anthony James and 'The Post'10th Aug.2000.

Our investigation of this rumour of a UFO crash in the Cannock Chase area took us back in time to the
1960's and plunged us into a bizarre mystery involving the retrieval by the military of a strange craft that had
crashed on the Chase.

There were stories of the silencing of a key witness, rumours of Alien bodies found in the wreckage of the
vehicle and the deep involvement of the Ministry of Defence.

It began, when the late Leonard Stringfield, a US Air Force officer at the time, revealed that in 1964 a UFO
piloted by three Alien creatures crashed on the edge of Cannock Chase and was recovered by elite forces
from NATO.

Despite the incredible nature of this account, we located a source that was intimately involved. He had been
driving through the Chase on that fateful day in 1964 and had come across a cordon manned by military
personnel. He was ordered to turn his vehicle around and drove several hundred yards up the road and then
pulled over. Luckily he had a camera with him in the car and taking this, he stealthily walked through the
trees to see what all the fuss was about.....He soon found out!

He peered through the trees and was able to make out the shape of a small triangular-shaped object sitting on
a military trailer. "It looked like nothing on Earth." Unfortunately at this stage, the witness was spotted" and
apprehended. His camera was confiscated and he was questioned about his reasons for being in the area.
After he had made his explanation, it was made quite clear to him that it would not be in his best interest to
speak out publically on what he had seen.

To bring the situation up to-date, the day we went to interview him, he told us that he had received a strange
telephone call from the MOD that had unnerved him considerably. We wondered if he was a hoaxer and if the
story was true. We asked if we could dial1471 (trace the caller), to see if he was being honest. Sure enough,
the number was available and it was the MoD guards service at Whittington Army Barracks. From there
however, the only response was a deafening silence.

To this day, the origin of the mysterious object found on the- Chase in 1964 remains unknown. Was it a
crashed UFO or was it perhaps a prototype aircraft that the military and the MoD wanted to keep 'under
wraps?'
