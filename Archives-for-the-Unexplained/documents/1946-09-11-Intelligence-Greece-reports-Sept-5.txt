Till chefen för försvarsstabens utrikesavdelning.
Den grekiske tjänsteförrättande utrikesministern Stephanoposdos har 5/9 meddelat att raketprojektiler observerats över norra Grekland.
Projektiler ha, enligt S., iakttagits såväl av grekiska divisionschefer som brittiska officerare. Projektilernas flyghöjd uppskattas till 5000-10000 yards.
Brittiska ambassaden i Aten meddelar 6/9 att inga engelska officerare rapporterat att de sett några raketer. Från ambassadens sida uttalas att raketerna kunde ha varit en "type of flare, as used in a Very pistol, a number of which have been reported in the same area".
Washington den 11. September 1946
Arvid Eriksson
Överste

To the chief of Defense Staff's Foreign Affairs Department.
The acting Greek foreign affairs minister Stephanoposdos have on 5/9 reported that rocket projectiles have been spotted above northern Greece.
Projectiles have, according to S., been observed by both Greek division heads and British officers. The projectiles altitude have been estimated to 5000-10000 yards.
The British embassy in Athens report on 6/9 that no english officers have reported to have seen any rockets. The embassy explains that the rockets could have been a "type of flare, as used in a Very pistol, a number of which have been reported in the same area".
Washington, 11 September 1946
Arvid Eriksson
Colonel
