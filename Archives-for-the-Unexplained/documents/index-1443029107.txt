                                            Groom Lake Security Force
                                                  (DET 3 SP)

                                                            Who is Clorne Filkins?


                        Cammo Dude Gallery | Names & Addresses of Dudes | Security Manual


  The "Cammo Dudes" are members of the anonymous, camouflage-clad security force that patrols
  the outer boundary of the Groom Lake complex and adjoining public lands. They are a mixture of
  Air Force personnel and employees of a private contractor, probably EG&G. They are protected by
  the Lincoln County Sheriff's Department, the only local police force in the U.S. that acts in proxy for
  a secret Federal entity. Cammo Dudes detain tourists who wander across the poorly marked military
  border near the "E.T. Highway," State Route 375. Regardless of the circumstances, these tourists are
  turned over to the Sheriff's Department for the typical $600 local fine. By dealing only with the local
  authorities instead of federal ones, the Cammo Dudes avoid having to identify themselves or appear
  in court.

                                                         The Mission

     It is the position of the Research Center that any federal employee or contractor who patrols public lands or
     interacts with a public police force must be identified. This page is an attempt to assemble a roster of Cammo
     Dudes and sufficient information about each of them to assure that civil rights will be respected.

     I work on the Cammo Dude investigation only when I am motivated to do so. Motivation usually takes the form of
     another innocent tourist arrested at the border. I have no objection to the arrest of those who truly intend to violate
     the border, but the security guards who detain the prisoner must identify themselves at the scene and be prepared
     appear in court to testify. I do object to the needless arrest of those who have only innocently crossed the border
     because of insufficient marking or the state's "E.T. Highway" promotion. As long as needless arrests continue and
     Cammo Dudes do not identify themselves at the time of arrest, I will continue my effort to identify all of these
     security guards from secondary sources.

     Unfortunately, this could also result in unintended side effects: Because all other avenues are blocked, I am forced
     to identify these people in a more public forum than would normally be required. My investigation may also result
     in the unintentional identification of other base personnel not affiliated with the Cammo Dudes.

                                                                                                          -- Glenn Campbell




  Names of Dudes
  Below is a list of Cammo Dudes and their home addresses obtained from public records. (See DR#35 article for
  explanation.) On 7/25/96, two California tourists, drawn by the E.T. Highway publicity, drove beyond a restricted area
  sign at night. They were detained by unidentified guards, arrested by a Sheriff's Deputy and given $1200 in fines. In
  addition, a relative of the Undersheriff towed their vehicle for an additional $175. In the following weeks, in response to
  this needless arrest, I conducted additional research to obtain the addresses, telephone numbers, social security numbers,
  dates and places of birth, real estate assessments and other public information for the security members who had been
  deputized by the Lincoln County Sheriff's Dept. Below I have published only home addresses.

  Since each of these people once took an oath as public officer of Lincoln County, they have forfeited their right to
  anonymity. In the list below, I have chosen not to public phone numbers and social security numbers, as I do not think
  this would be productive at present, but as long as anonymous arrests continue at the border, without the opportunity for
  the accused to face their accusers, I will continue to collect information on these characters. Anonymity is the antithesis
  of civil rights, and as long as these people are not identified, they will continue to abuse visitors. Every new anonymous
  arrest I hear about will result in additional information published here.

  Notes on the list below:

      1. The part of the name in bold is as it appeared on the official duputization of that person in the Lincoln County
         Sheriff's Department (recorded in the Oath of Office book in the Lincoln County recorders office). Additional
         letters, usually for the middle name, were filled in from other public records.Unlike other deputies in Lincoln
         County, these ones were sworn in by notary publics in Clark County working for the Air Force Flight Test Center.
         [See original list.]

      2. In most of the cases below, there was only one exact match in public records of Clark or Lincoln counties for each
         of these people. The only multiple candidates were for "Robert R. Armstrong" and "Kenneth Learned," both of
         whom had both a "Junior" and "Senior" on record. Robert R. Armstrong, Sr., was born in 1918 and died in 1993.
         The other, Jr., was born in 1941, making him the likely candidate. Although his age is right, I am less than
         completely certain it is the right one, since "Robert R. Armstrong" is a common name, and Boulder City is a
         longer commute to the Janet terminal than most of the others. Kenneth Learned, Jr., was born in 1964, making
         him younger than most other Dudes, so the father seems a more likely candidate, born in 1940.

      3. There was no "Lars N. Larson", with an "o", but I did find a "Lars N. Larsen", with an "e". I assume this was a
         clerical error at the time of duputization. Lars Larson gave a written warning to a tourist at the border in 1993,
         including his signature (one of only two times I am aware of when a Dude identified himself). Although "o" or
         "e" is not clear in the signature, it does match Lars Larson's signature or real estate records in the Clark County
         Recorder's Office.

      4. Most of these duputies were hastily de-deputized by the Lincoln County Sheriff on August 24, 1994. In my
         opinion, this does not restore their anonymity, since they remain publically accountable for their past actions as
         deputies. Only two Cammo Dudes sponsored by the Air Force Flight Test Center have not been de-deputized:
         Robert R. Armstrong and Donald Ray Domkowski. Presumeably, they continue to represent the Lincoln
         County Sheriff's Department at Area 51, but this cannot be confirmed.

      5. I have been unable to find a match for only a single Cammo Dude Deputy: Hubert E. Sides. I encourage
         investigation by others to help find this person. Presumably he lived in Lincoln, Clark or Nye County in the early
         1990s. I have not yet checked Nye County or all records in Lincoln County. I also did not check Clark County
         Recorder's documents prior to computerization (before 1988). I consulted previous editions of the Las Vegas
         phone book dating back to the mid 1980s and could not find him.

      6. This list was current as of the early 1990s, but there is no guarantee that these people still work for the Cammo
         Dude Force, and some current members do not appear on this list. Nonetheless, the CDF appears to be stable, and
         I would expect the majority of these people to still be patrolling the border.

      7. From public records, I have the social security numbers, dates of birth, state of birth, party affiliations and phone
         numbers (even when "unlisted") for at least 80% of these people. By driving by their houses, one can record their
