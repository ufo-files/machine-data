                             GHOST    HAUNTS   W ITCH MUSEUM


                                               By


                                        Bob Barraco


        In the last issue of Ghost Trackers Newsletter                               I   received
there      was    enclosed a few cut outs of stories on some haunts in
my area.    There was one that caught my attention because it was
in my home town of Salem, Mass.     So I decided to check it out.
     The   article    was from The Sun, only a couple of years old.
In the   article   it   explains  how  one  visitor  had  the  most
terrifying   experiences    he's ever had.   It goes on to say that
the visitor        heard an eerie         howling      and       someone       making       noises
behind       him and things flying through                  the air.          The    writer also
injected     the      theory that the spirits were that of those who were
burned at the stake.
     After some investigation I can now safely say that  90%  of
this article is nothing more than writers embellishment.   It is
true that the Witch Museum is haunted, but that's the only truth
to the article.
     The building was first built as                        a    church       in    1844.      The
impressive         structure of brownstone and brick built on the Salem
common housed the First Church of Salem.                             The building is quite
a    powerful         site     with     it's    massive arched             windows and towers
built in         Gothic       Revival     style.        Serious        fires        damaged    the
building         in    1902    and      1969    <when       it       housed       the Salem Auto
Museum> ,    but since 1972,            with   it's      insides           entirely      rebuilt,     e
it's housed the Salem              Witch Museum.
        In   the museum there is a dramatisation of the famous witch
trials which occurred in the 1600's.                            There are         several     stage
areas where there are mannequins used for the presentation.                                     All
of    this       is in the main area of t he church                    <where mass would be
performed> .           There is another room to the back of the                          building
which  served  as              a   library.          This       is    the room the haunting
primarily occurs.
        I interviewed a couple of former employees where they                                  told
me    of     sightings of a black cloaked apparition moving about the
room late at night well after all visitors had gone.   Both  the
people  describe  the  specter exactly the same.   The figure is
draped in a flowing black cloak                     like that a monk would wear.                 It
makes no sound whatsoever,                not even the sounds of walking as                      it
moves about.          The ghost has been seen on many occasions entering
the     room      that       was   once the library,             disappearing through the
door.   The figure has also been seen lurking about the mannequin
figures used in the museum presentations.
      No one has ever heard any howling or eerie noise <at  least
spectoral> .           I'd    also like to include that all those killed as
witches during           the infamous trials - not                   one    was     burned.      In
fact       directly       behind the museum is the site of where a man was
crushed to death by having large stones put on top of him one by
one until he either confessed to being a witch or died.

astral body has been said to be the true self,                                    with the physical
shell as its home for now.                     The two bodies are united by a thin
silver        cord    that       is endless.            In death the cord is no longer
united;       thus setting the astral body free                            to   go        back       to    the
spiritual realm.
        Astral       projection          may    or     may not be a conscious effort.
When there is a sudden death,                    the person may not                     have       realized
they     are no longer in a physical body.                              Also;       during a trauma,
such as an operation,              there are people                   reported          to     have       seen
their physical bodies as they hovered above.

careful.        Although you do not have to worry                               about        the     silver

PRIEST PRAYS TO         CALM    OXFORD "GHOSTS" - February 20th, 1987
      Oxford,     England       <Associated          Press)           A    clergyman         gave       a
blessing   at  a  college  where   reports  of ghostly doings have
accompanied   an  archaelogical    dig.    Magdalen,   an   Oxford
University   college  founded   in 1458, has a reputation of being
haunted.    But many strange experiences have been reported since
the  dig   began  six  months ago, in the foundations of the 13th
Century Hospital of St.                John the Baptist beneath                      the    college.
The hospital was built               :J.n 1231 for "poore scholars and miserable
persons", according to a history of the college.
      "There is some fear that                the excavations may have unleashed
spirits    which        are     returning           to    haunt       us and it's been much
talked about", said Rev. Jeffrey John, Anglican Dean of Divinity
at Magdalen.   He blessed the room of language student Cartriona
Oliphant,       26,     who     said     she        ran out screaming after seeing a
group of figures.              "1 went there and said prayers for the peace
of  lost  souls," John said.   "These things happen from time to
time it's important to calm the prescences down.   You  have  to
stand     up     to    whatever        they     are, spiritually and physically."
Students have reported seeing figures, feeling ghastly presences
and hearing footsteps and singing from empty  rooms.   Oliphant,
who  lives  under  the  eaves at the top of what is known as the                                            1
chaplain's staircase in the oldest part of                                the    building,      said
she felt unwell and stayed in bed the previous Friday.                                      " I heard
someone        come    through       the      door        and I thought it was my scout
<college servant) coming in to clean,"                         she        said.        "I    felt       a
strong     physical       presence leaning over me.                         I wanted to scream
but   I couldn't.        Then    I sensed other figures whispering and then
everything stopped for  a  minute  before  starting  again.    It
happened  three  times  before · I felt  able  to  run  out   and
scream."Edward Shaw, 20, and  Mark  Aldwinkle,  21,  have   rooms
below     Oliphant       and     said they sensed             something          strange minutes
earlier.        They     also        reported        seeing       a       darkly      clad    figure
crawling       across     the        carpet     in        their   rooms at night.               Paul
Sinclair, 20, said he has                  heard         singing,         loud    footsteps         and
furniture       being     moved        in the room above him when its occupant
was asleep.
      Ian Burrow, director of the Oxford Archeological Unit,  was
given   a chance to dig before a new kitchen is built at Magdalen
in June 1987.    "These ghost reports came as a great  surprise",
he  said.    "We  haven't  found any burials, although there must
have been some in the hospital complex.   We have found the usual
things like bits of pottery, belt                         buckles         and    a    piece    of       a
dagger."

     When  I  first  saw this book advertised, I thought "Great!

always look forward to hearing about the final dispostion of the

agree with and several that            just plainly upset me and                I will now
point those out.
        First of all, one thing         that really began to bug me and                  I'm
sure    you'll       agree   when     you     read      the   book, is the constant
repetition of the full name             Mr.      F.R.     "Nick"    Nocerino.           Miss
Edwards     states      near the beginning of the book that he likes to
be called     Dick but she      insists on spelling out this entire                     name
each    and    every     time      she mentions anything about him.                    A bit
boring at times.
     Miss Edwards also mentions the fact that she believes there
are no negative spirits.             This 1    don't agree with and since               I've
been actively        investigating ghosts for over, 15 years                myself,        I
feel    somewhatqualified  to  make  that statement.   I've been
involved with research where very negative  or  Demonic  spirits
have been felt by clairvoyants.                  Even though these are probably                '

        She also mentions using the.Ouija board                    and    participating
in uncontrolled seances which are highly dangerous.                        I've spoken
before     that      I am against any use of the Ouija because you have
no way of determining what you are communicating                         with    and     all
seances       must    be controlled by a professional medium or psychic
of some kind.
        She further goes on to mention that she has told spirits to
"go to the light" which I feel is also wrong and very   confusing
to  spirits.   First  of  all, to them, we also appear as lights
because we give off our own illumination due to our   aura.   So,
if you tell them to "go to the light", you might be telling them
in reality to stay right here.   .
     The  book  itself it well written and                     I have a lot of faith
in Mr.  Nocerino's work and ability but I felt it  necessary  to
point  out these shortcomings.   I would rate this book a 5 on a
scale of 1-10.
