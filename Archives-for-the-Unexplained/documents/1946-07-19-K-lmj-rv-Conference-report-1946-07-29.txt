THE DEFENCE STAFF 
Air Defence Dept


Re: Meeting at the Air Staff on July 29, 1946 

On the basis of Air engineer Malmberg´s report from a visit to Kölmjärv it has been decided that investigations in Norrland will be restricted to Kölmjärv (report to Chief of Staff VI milo by telephone on the same date).

Colonel Dahlgren has proposed a drainage of Kölmjärv. He will be asked to perform a detailed scheme (Chief of Staff informed on the same day).

As soon as any fragments or a whole missile is found FF will send a specialist to the site. Engineer Malmberg will go to Glanshammar in order to make an inspection of a finding.

--------------------------

At a telephone conversation with Widell, head of division at the Ministry of Defence, he promised that the kr 10.000 needed for missile investigations, which will be placed at Fst´s disposal according to decision at a cabinet meeting on July 26, will be provided by extraordinary allowance. Possible adjustments with regular allowances will be made at a later date.

Chief of Staff VI milo was informed by telephone that the expenses are to be paid by Fst. Invoices are to be sent to VI milo who will forward them to Fst.
