  Loyd --
  There has been a CIA operation at Marana Airport, just north of
  Tucson. The name "Evergreen" does ring a bell in connection with this
  base, but I am not certain. I have visited this airport (from the
  outside). It is a commercial freight and maintenance airport whose
  main activities certainly have nothing to do with the CIA. For
  example, A lot of mothballed commercial jets are stored there. I
  think the National Guard has some sort of presence there, but that is
  common at airports. The airport is not open to the public, but
  security doesn't seem terribly tight either. It isn't a "secret base"
  by any means, but secret flights could have been staged from there.
  Supposely, Marana was (or is) a supply base for CIA operations in
  Latin America, but I have no solid confirmation.
  That part of the story seems true.                  I haven't a clue about the rest.
