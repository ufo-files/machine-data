Lights remain a mystery
By The Associated Press
    The mysterious light that was seen in the sky in the eastern third of
North America coincided with a meteor shower but some astronomers
say it probably was made by a manmade object such as a satellite.
    The light, variously described as a pinpoint or a spiral or a glowing
cloud, was seen from Louisiana to Canada about 10 p.m. EDT Tuesday.
    A Canadian scientist said the light was sparked by debris from an
unidentified satellite.
    ''The satellite was actually seen in the telescope here and we had a
report from an amateur astronomer (who) saw it and saw the release
of material from it," said Tom Bolton of the David Dunlap Observatory
north of Toronto.
    "But we're not sure which satellite it was and we're not sure what
the material was that was released,'' he said.
