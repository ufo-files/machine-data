November 21: Earth filmed from a V-2

On November 29, 1946, Associated Press reported that a V-2 rocket from White Sands had managed to film the Earth from an altitude of 100 kilometres. At top level the camera and film was catapulted from the rocket and recovered. (Norra Västerbotten, Nov. 29). [According to Willy Ley this shot occured on Nov. 21].
