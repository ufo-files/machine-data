ÖD 7/8



A WORD OF WARNING AGAINST ROCKET PSYCHOSIS
THOUGH ROCKETS NOT ELIMINATED

Investigations carried out by the Army have not lead to conclusive results

Investigations carried out by the Army concerning phenomena observed in the sky over Sweden during last summer have not lead to any conclusive results so far, says Major Nils Ahlgren, Chief of the Swedish Air Defence, to TT.

In cooperation with the Air Administration and the Defence Research Centre along with further authorities, the Defence Staff has taken all possible measures to solve the mystery, says Major
Ahlgren.

The public has been a great help and also the press who has provided valuable reports. During the period July 9-12 about 300 reports of observations were received. The number of reports has decreased gradually since then.

Checkups and in some cases close investigations on the site indicate that many of the observations are incorrect. The observers have seen something unusual and have immediately referred their observations to the mystical rockets. Everybody should very carefully reflect upon their observations in order to avoid a rocket psychosis.
