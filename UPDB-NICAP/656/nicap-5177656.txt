F9F Encounters "Shooting Star"Dan Wilson:
May 13, 1952; El Centro Naval Air Station, California (BB)
At 3:15 a.m. PST, an F9F pilot taking off from El Centro NAS saw what
appeared to be a shooting star diving at an angle of 60 degrees. At
around the same time there was a report from the El Centro Sheriff's
Office of five "Flying Saucers" as large as B-36's with a light
underneath. They disappeared to the southwest at terrific speed. All
told there were four separate reports of unidentified aerial object in
the El Centro area on this date. (Blue Book docs, Dan Wilson)
