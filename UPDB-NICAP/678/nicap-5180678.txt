3:00 p.m. Two young men, P. Aliranta and E J Sneck, were working in the woodswhen Aliranta noticed a 15 foot metallic object, shaped like two saucers puttogether, and with four thin legs, descending in a clearing 50 feet away. As itlanded, a little entity just under 3-foot tall glided to the ground from an opening inthe underside; dressed in green one-piece "diver's suit," it wore a helmet with acircular faceplate. (Humcat 1971-6, Tapani Kuningas, FSR Vol. 17 # 5)
