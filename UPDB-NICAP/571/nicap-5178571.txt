C-124 Encounters "Jack O'Lantern" At 1,000 KnotsBrad
Sparks:
Feb. 11, 1956; S of Japan (BBU)
7:15 p.m. MATS C-124 air crew sighting of a yellow or
amber object at 1,000 knots (1,150 mph). (Project 1947)

Dan Wilson:
Feb. 11, 1956; 28.53N 131.30E [S. of Japan] (BBU)
At 7:15 p.m. local time, a MATS C-124 crew observed an
object the shape of a Jack O'lantern, yellow or amber in
color, heading 130 degrees, at about 25,000 feet
altitude, at an estimated speed of 1000 knots.
