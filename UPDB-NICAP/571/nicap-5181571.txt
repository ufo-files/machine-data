Motorists observed flashing lights in the woods, stopped to investigate and saw abright red, revolving hemispherical object about 10 meters above the ground. Itwas approximately 10-12 meters in diameter. Next morning physical tracesincluding a deep imprint were found at the site and separate witnesses werelocated.
