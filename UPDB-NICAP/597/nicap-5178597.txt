Uncorrelated Target Tracked At 2400 MPHDan Wilson:
July 17, 1956; Westover AFB, Massachusetts (BB)

9:30 EST, (18/0230Z). A regular but larger than
ordinary radar target was picked up on a CPN 18-A
radar set. The target had fast acceleration of speed
as high as 2400 mph and was able to make a 180 degree
turn in less than 6 seconds. The target increased and
decreased speed in a matter of seconds. The length of
observation was 4 minutes. (BB files)

Fran Ridge:

fighter pilot saw a white­-yellow circular object
that reversed course. (McDonald list; Weinstein; FUFOR
