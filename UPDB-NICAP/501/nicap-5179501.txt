(McDonald list) GCA Radar Tracks Flat DiscBrad
Sparks:
Aug. 20, 1965; Plattsburgh, New York (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
Aug. 20, 1965; Plattsburgh, New York
At approximately 5:26 p.m. local time, a round, flat,
disc-shaped object of 15-20 feet in diameter was observed in the
sky. The
object was circling back and forth in 1/2 mile radius circles with
high
speed turns. The object was bright metallic colored and was
approximately
2-3 miles away from the observer who watched the object with artillery
binoculars.
The object showed a high rate of acceleration and disappeared to the
east
after approximately 3 minutes. A sighting was made on the GCA Radar at
Plattsburgh
AFB at about 5:30 p.m. A phenomena of isolated, high intensity
jamming was noted. (Dan Wilson)
