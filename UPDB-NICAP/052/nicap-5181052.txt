10:30 p.m.  And continuing until 1:30 a.m. next morning, residents  saw an odd redorange light, then a luminous sphere that rose from behind a hill. A motoristblinked his headlights a couple of times at the object, and it immediately dartedtoward him and stopped overhead emitting a humming sound, its light illuminatingthe terrain. (Ouranos No. 14, 2nd Quarter 1975. Investigated by M. Grazzioli andM. De Cordova.)
