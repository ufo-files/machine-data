UFO Paces B-25Brad Sparks:
June 18, 1952; 100 miles E of March AFB, Calif. (BBU)
A UFO paced a B-25 bomber for 30 minutes. No
explanation could be found for the object and it was classified as an
unknown.(Ruppelt p. 146; etc.)

Dan Wilson:
June 18, 1952, 100 miles east of March AFB,
Calif. (BBU)
1700-1730 hours PST. Capt. Erie P. Ashton, USAF, and 2nd Lt.
Clifton Ashley, USAF, sighted a silver object at approximately 11,000
feet in altitude off their left wing. The object was longer than it was
wide and was in sight for approximately 1/2 hour.
