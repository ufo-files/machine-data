Sparks:
Jan. 22, 1962; Kirksville, Missouri (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
Jan. 22, 1962; Kirksville, Missouri
At 12:45 p.m. local time, one object appeared on a FPS-6A
height finder radar at 72,000 feet in altitude at a range of
65 miles. The
object ascended to 80,000 feet then started a steady descent at 1500
feet per minute on a
heading of 55 degrees at a speed of approx. 100 knots. When the object
reached
9,000  feet the radar would no longer paint it. Length of
observation was 65
minutes. (Dan Wilson)
