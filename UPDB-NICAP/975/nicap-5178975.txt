Feb. 24, 1959; Williamsport, Pennsylvania (BBU)
8:20 p.m. A scheduled American Airlines flight from
Newark to Detroit
encountered three lights over Pennsylvania. The initial
detection took
place when the aircraft was 13 miles west of
Williamsport. The lights
remained at about the 9 o'clock position for 40 minutes,
providing an
opportunity for many of the 35 passenger to observe
them. They were
also observed by the crews of two other planes flying
much farther to
the south, as well as by the tower operators in
Pittsburgh. PA.
