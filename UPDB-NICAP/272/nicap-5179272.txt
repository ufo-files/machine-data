Dan Wilson:
March 6, 1963; Louisiana, Arkansas, Texas
At around 10:37 a.m. local time, a bright flash was observed
by numerous witnesses from Louisiana to Texas. One report said a large
bright flash was seen 20 miles north of Barksdale AFB, seen by two
light aircraft and a SAC bomber. Another report stated that there
was on observation of a bright flash and metallic falling object 40
miles NNW of Chennault AFB at 10:55 a.m. (1655Z). A request was made to
keep NORAD informed of significant events. Type of observations:
Ground-Visual, Air-Visual, and Ground-Radar. (Dan Wilson)
