10:30 p.m. EST. While watching television, a woman saw her set experiencesevere interference, then black out completely (EM effects). She glanced out thewindow and saw a huge red light that appeared to be rotating behind a glassysurface of some kind. Above the light was a very large object surrounded by a haloof white light. (U.F.O. Investigator, Vol. HI, No. 11, Jan.-Feb. 1967, p. 3)
