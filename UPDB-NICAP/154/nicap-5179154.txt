(McDonald list) Object Tracked Prior to Launch of Polaris MissileBrad
Sparks:

April 11, 1961; Cape Canaveral, Florida. (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:

At 9:57 a.m. local time, an object was picked up on
ground radar at speeds from from 150 knots to 600 knots. The
object orbited 6 or 7 times along an azimuth of 126 degrees and
between 10 and 55 miles from the Cape. The length of the observation
was 30 minutes. The UFO was picked up prior
to launch of test number 1352, a Polaris Missile. (39 documents found)
