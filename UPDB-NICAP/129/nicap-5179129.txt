T-33 Crew Observed Bright LightDan
Wilson:
Dec. 29, 1960; Miho, Japan (BB)
9:37 p.m. local. Capt. Leon C. Seale Jr., and Col.
Royal Francisr flying in a T-33 jet aircraft at
26,000 feet on a heading of 62 degrees observed a
bright light at 10 degrees elevation bearing 330
degrees from Miho and disappeared at 10 degrees
elevation on a bearing of 270 degrees from Miho.
The bright light changing from intense white to
cherry red to green to blue to white approximate
was the apparent size of basketball held at arm's
length.The length of observation was 3 minutes
beginning at 9:37 p.m. local time.
