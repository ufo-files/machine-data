AN/FPS-3 Tracks Five Or More TargetsDan
Wilson:
May 23, 1953; Lackland AFB, Texas
8:43 a.m. CST. Five or more radar targets were observed from
a radar site at San Antonio, Texas. The radar targets appeared to be
orbiting
over Monclova, Mexico. When first detected the targets were at
approximately 35,000
feet at approximately 240 knots flying in a northerly direction for 40
to 50 miles
and then began to orbit. The formation faded off the radar screen in
the same locale.
The observation lasted for one hour and five minutes. The radar in use
was the
AN/FPS-3.
