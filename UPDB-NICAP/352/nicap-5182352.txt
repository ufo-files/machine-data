8:00 PM. A large disc-shaped UFO followed a Piper airplane from Cordoba toResistensia, Argentina. It reportedly maneuvered at great speed, and changed colorfrom silver to orange. The plane's compass oscillated between 0.5 and 270 degrees,and there were several other variations noted in other onboard instruments.
