Wingshaped Object Hovers Then Passes P-51Brad Sparks:
Nov. 24, 1951; Mankato, Minn. (BBU)
3:53 p.m. One of two USAF pilots flying P-51 fighters flying
W at 25,000 ft, 210 knots IAS, Capt. William Fairbrother, saw a white 8
ft flying-wing­shaped object hovering then pass 100 ft over and 100
ft to the left of his fighter, he immediately turned 180° to follow
but could not find object. (Project 1947; NARCAP; FUFOR Index)
