1:00 a.m. CST. Three men driving to Saxon saw a rapidly spinning sphere with awhite light on top and on the bottom, and red and green lights around the equator(body lights). The object approached at low altitude, hovered over a nearby field.(U.F.O. Investigator, Vol. IV, No. 4, Jan.-Feb. 1968, p. 6.)
