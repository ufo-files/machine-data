Two Objects From Aircraft Radar Tracked (McDonald list)Brad
Sparks:
July 27, 1954; Pepperrell AFB, Newfoundland, Canada (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
July 27, 1954; Pepperrell AFB, Newfoundland, Canada (BBU)
3:58 to 4:02 am local time (0658 GMT to 0702 GMT). Two
unidentified objects were
observed from an aircraft at 81 degrees 35 minutes North, 29 degrees
West, tracked on radar on a course 210 degrees at 350 knots. The
altitude of
the objects was estimated at very low. The two objects were 2-3 miles
apart. According to the map coordinates given in the report this
sighting took place over Northeastern Greenland.
