Navy Destroyer Observes Two Discs / Tracks On RadarDan Wilson:
July 10, 1952: Off Korea
At night, 40 aboard the Canadian destroyer Crusader saw
two "saucers" and recorded them on radar. The radar fix
placed the objects at two miles high and seven miles
away. The report said the objects disappeared before
dawn.
