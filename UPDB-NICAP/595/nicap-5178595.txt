(McDonald list; FUFOR Index) Object Tracked At 59,000'Brad
Sparks:
June 29, 1956; Los Angeles to San Diego, Calif. (BBU)
5:10 p.m. (PST). (McDonald list; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:
June 29, 1956; Los Angeles to San Diego, Calif. (BBU)
At 5:10 p.m. PST, a round, sometimes irregular shaped object
was observed high in the sky to the east of the observers. The object
was
silver-white to rosy in color. Types of observations listed in the
report are: Ground-Visual,
Air-Visual, Ground-Radar, and Air-Intercept Radar. F3D aircraft tried
to intercept the
object with no success due to the extreme altitude of the object. The
total length of the observation was approximately 2 hours. The radar
site at Mt. Luguna (P-76) detected the object at 59,000 feet with no
speed.
