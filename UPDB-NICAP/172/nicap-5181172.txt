2:55 a.m. Two police officers, Francis J. Gross and C. Ragazzo were on routinepatrol when officer Ragazzo received a radio call from Gross informing him offive "strange looking" lights in the south-west sky. Both officers were able toobserve the objects for a period of five minutes. (Reference: UFOINVESTIGATOR. December 1975, page 1)
