(McDonald list) Two Blips At 4800 KnotsBrad
Sparks:
June 25, 1961; Pacific Ocean (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:

At 2200 hours local time, at 39 degrees 45'N - 127
degrees 33' W, approximately 250 miles NW of San Francisco, Calif., a
Lockheed RC-121D
aircraft of the 552nd AEW&C Squadron (Airborne Early Warning
and
Control) reported a radar sighting of two unidentified blips of
unknown size on a heading
generally N-S at an estimated speed of 4800 knots. These were tracked
for approximately 3
minutes. (Dan Wilson)
