900 MPH Target Pursued By F-86Brad Sparks:
March 27, 1953; Mount Taylor, New Mexico (BBU 2524)
7:25 p.m. (MST). Pilot of USAF F-86 jet fighter at 600 knots (700 mph)
saw and chased a bright orange circle flying at 800 knots (900 mph),
and executing three fast rolls. (Berliner; FUFOR Index)
