Gnd Radar Tracks Object Chased By Fighter & Heli PilotsBrad
Sparks:
May 20, 1961; Tyndall AFB, Florida (BBU)
Morning. USAF fighter and helicopter pilots with 4756th Air Police Sq.
saw a maneuvering white-orange round object, disappearing when the
fighter approached. Ground radar tracking. (Project 1947; BB files??)
Air Police observed unidentified light diving and climbing. [NICAP
UFOE, III]

Dan Wilson:
May 20, 1961; Tyndall AFB, Florida
At 2:50 a.m. local time, a round rusty-orange colored object
was observed near the Drone Launching Area. The object moved
up and
down and would gain altitude rapidly. The object seemed to float.
Four Air Policemen saw the
object. RAPCON was painting the target that the Air Police at the
launching site
had called in. An F-102 fighter was scrambled to run on the
object. The object was traveling at 2-4 mph and then up to 45 mph.
The fighter was getting a sporadic paint on radar. A
helicopter was scrambled due to the slowness of the object. An AF
GCI radar also
tracked the object between 2,000 and 10,000 feet. Total time of
observation was
approximately 1 hour and 40 minutes. (85 pages of documents by Dan
Wilson)
