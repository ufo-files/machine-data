Dan Wilson:

August  7, 1950; Santa Fe, New Mexico (BBU)

4:50 a.m. MST. Lt. Frazier of the 93rd Fighter Squadron
while flying at 30,000 feet observed a black unidentified object at
approximately 20,000 feet. Lt. Frazier descended toward the object at
620 mph but was unable to follow the object which disappeared in the
distance.
