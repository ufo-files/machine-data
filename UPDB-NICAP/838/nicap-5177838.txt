===============================================

July 23, 1952; South Bend, Indiana (BBU 1578)

At 11:35 P.M. CDT, USAF Capt. Harold W. Kloth, Regional
Air Provost Marshal, and a rated pilot, observed two
objects giving off a blue-white light. The objects were moving south
and were observed for approximately 9 minutes. Before contact was lost
the rear-most object appeared to swerve to the west. No sound was heard
from the objects. A pair of 6x35 binoculars were used to view the
objects for the last 4 minutes of the sighting.
