9:15 p.m. EST. A sales engineer and a county assessor saw an egg-shaped objectfor 10 minutes, apparently on or near a hill at a reservoir. The object rose slowly toaround 50 feet, hovered, then moved away after performing several radicalmaneuvers. (Johnstown Tribune-Democrat, Pa., 3/11/67, copy in NICAP files.)
