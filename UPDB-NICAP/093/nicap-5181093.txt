11:30 p.m. As Dale & Sheryl Ricard were driving home, a brightly lit globeappeared in the sky in front of them; then it approached & paced the car at adistance of 24 ft. About the size of a car, it had a flange bearing 2 red & 2 whitelights surrounding a transparent, brightly lit dome, inside of which 3 dark shadowymoving figures were visible. (Humcat 1975-3 Source: New Zealand Spaceview,Auckland # 66  Type: A)
