Dan Wilson:
March 9, 1951; About 20 miles
SE of Tsushima Island, Japan
(BBU)

2:25 p.m. This description was extracted from the Air Intelligence
Information Report IR-29-52, Page 16 of 27.  A high-speed air
target was tracked by a Mariner SA4
aircraft. The target was initially observed bearing 320
degrees true for
approximately 60 seconds. During this time the target tracked
55 miles. The calculated speed was approximately 3,350 mph.
