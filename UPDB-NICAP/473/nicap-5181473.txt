12:10 AM An on duty police officer PC IAN MACKENZIE observed two verybright horizontal lights in the vicinity of Langdale Pike that moved towards himand grew brighter. He ruled out any type of aircraft. At the same time PS JOHNMcMULLEN also spotted the UFO from his location whilst walking throughKeswick with three other officers.
