July 17, 1952; Rapid City, South Dakota (BBU 1479)

At 2200 hours at Rapid City AFB, M/Sgt Gordon S.
Anderson observed 12 to 14 orange colored discs that glowed, in flight
at an altitude of between 12,000 and 15,000 feet, traveling in an
easterly direction. The objects were in two "V" formations and were
seen for 5 to six seconds.
