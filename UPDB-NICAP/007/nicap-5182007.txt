Late night, Jane Murphy had gone to bed late when she suddenly woke up standingin a field feeling very cold and wet. She then noticed a huge metallic objecthovering over the field, suddenly a group of figures, as many as 10 approachedher. When she woke up she was on a bed in a strange room. Several figures werearound her communicating via telepathy telling her to look at them. One of theentities was seven-foot tall, human looking with totally black eyes.
