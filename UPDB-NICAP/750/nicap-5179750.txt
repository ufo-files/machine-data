2:00 a.m. LT. A couple saw an intensely bright glowing disc approach and hoverabout 1 50 yards from their house above an old tree. Some apparent damage tofoliage, circular holes in the ground, and a large depression with flattened grasswere found beneath the area where the object had hovered. (Flying Saucer Review,Vol. 12, No. 6, Nov.-Dec. 1966, pp. 4-5.)
