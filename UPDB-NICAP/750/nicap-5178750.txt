Meteor Jet Almost Collides With ObjectBrad
Sparks:
Oct. 21, 1957; RAF Gaydon [North Luffenham?],
Warwickshire, England (BBU)
9:18 p.m. RAF pilot flying Meteor fighter had near
collision with object near atomic base, 6 lights emerged
when fighter approached, object disappeared suddenly,
ground radar tracking confirmed sighting. (Project 1947;
FUFOR Index; Mary Castner/CUFOS)

Dan Wilson:
Oct. 21, 1957; RAF Gaydon, Warwickshire, England (BBU)
9:18 p.m. local time. RAF Flying Officer D. W. Sweeney
flying a Meteor jet fighter at 28,000 feet west from RAF
Station at North Luffenham almost collided with an
object which he said was moving slowly and showing six
lights. After taking violent evasive action Sweeney
approached the object from starboard side. As he closed
in on the object the lights went out and the object
vanished. Sweeney estimated that his aircraft was
directly over Gaydon where atom-bomber crews train in
Valiants. A radar sighting at RAF Station Langtoft
confirmed that the object was at approximately 28,000
feet over Gaydon.
