1:10 AM. A silver colored, heel-shaped or bullet-shaped object hovered betweenfifty and five hundred feet above some trees near the Sacramento River for threeminutes. It made a sound like an electric motor as it moved very slowly toward thetwo witnesses, Ruben and Carlos Genera. Many animals reacted to its presence.
