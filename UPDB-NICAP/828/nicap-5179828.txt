Pair Of Luminous Objects Hover Near DC-8Fran Ridge:
Dec. 30, 1966; Coast of Peru
3:00 a.m. LT. A Canadian Pacific DC-8
airliner traveling on a northwest course at 35,000 feet observed a pair
of pulsating, luminous objects hovering to their left. The objects
descended at a slight angle and took up position off their left wing,
pacing the plane
for 1-2 minutes. Some members of the crew thought there was a
connecting structure between the two lights. The lights were separated
by about 3-4 degrees. A V-shaped pair of thin light beams emanated from
the objects, first pointing upward, then downward. Finally, the objects
accelerated, pulled away, and climbed out over
the Pacific
Ocean to the west, disappearing in the distance.
(Volunteer Flight Officers Network report via
Herb Roth; Gilmor, Colorado
Project Case 11, pp. 280-82; McDonald, 1968b, reprinted in Vaughan,
1995, pp. 7-8; Hall, 2001, pp. 129-130.)
