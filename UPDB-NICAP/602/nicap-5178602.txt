Huge Cigar Observed & Tracked On RadarFran
Ridge:
Originally this incident was listed as occurring around
the 25th or 27th of July, 1956 and location unknown. Dan Wilson located
the ship's logs and not only found the exact date and location, but the
listing mentioned the UFO!: "560726 UFO sighting - Rio de
Janeiro".

Carl
Feindt:
July 26, 1956; nr. Rio de Janeiro, aboard Aircraft
Carrier FDR

A a cigar-shaped
object was seen and tracked on radar. And the CIC radars said it went
off the scope in two or three sweeps. That was witnessed by John C.
Hau, Chief Warrant Officer."
