- Category: 01; Distant Encounters

Subject:   AFOSI Case 7: Another Object in Verical Descent Explodes Near Colonel

Date:       November 23, 1948

Location: Vaughn, NM

Francis Ridge:

This one is not listed in the original or the upgraded version of the Blue Book Unknowns by Brad Sparks. The
information that we have accumulated comes from the Blue Book Microfilms. The following comes from the
documents that also include the November 3 or 4th incident:

"On 23 November 1948 at approximately 2130 hours, I was driving west on Highway 60, about 10 miles west of
Vaughn, New Mexico. I observed a ball of light, descending vertically, which burst 100-200 feet from the
ground. The conditions and appearance were the same as occurred on 3-4 November.''

The document continues: "Colonel Hayes stated that he did not report the incidents as he thought they might
have been connected with some Air Force exercises. He further stated that he heard no sound or saw no
aircraft during either incident, which is understandable as he was travelling in a closed automobile.
Weather conditions were clear."
