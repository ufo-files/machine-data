From: Joan Woodward, Animal Reaction Specialist
Subject: Owego
NY, October 19, 1973

Cat:

To: NICAP

Animal Reaction Feature:

At 5:45 AM two farmers, LH and SO, 25 and 16, were at work,
SO in the barn and the LH outdoors.1 LH
was in the process of bringing cows in for milking. He was 20 feet from the
barn when a red/blue domed object appeared over the barn, passing over his head
and then over a 250 x 450 yard pasture containing five cows.1 The object was apparently not traveling
fast.1 As the object passed over the
five cows, they ran in all directions, two of them breaking through a barbed
wire fence to the S and one breaking through the fence to the N, sustaining
injuries.

It took until 11 AM to get these cows into the barn for
milking, and their milk production for that day was less than normal. A Deputy
Sheriff White was called at 6:15 AM, and reported observing that the cows were
upset when he arrived.11 Because these
animals remained spooky, they were left in the barn for a few days, before
being turned out into the field again.

[It would have been interesting to know if any cows were
inside the barn with SO and if so, if they reacted as the object moved the barn
roof.1jw.]

The Sighting:

The weather conditions were calm, clear, with stars
visible.1 Temperature was 50 degrees.
[The moon was a waning crescent with 42% illumination, and its altitude was
55-56 degrees in the SE sky.1 The
sighting took place an hour before first pre-dawn light that would have
occurred 6:54 AM (USNO).1jw]

LH was rounding up the cattle. He was 20 from the barn and
facing it (looking west) when he saw an object coming over the barn and moving
east. 1The object had a fire engine red
body and a blue dome on top [suggesting the object must have been glowing as it
was fully dark1jw].1 LH estimated the
object was 200 feet above the 50-60 foot top of the barn.1 When the object moved over his head, LH
could see no color or lights. He heard no sound.

LH called SO to come outside as the object, still at an
estimated altitude of 250 feet, moved over the pasture with the cows (described
above), and was now estimated to be 200 yards away.1 Both witnesses observed the object, and both though it was
spinning.1 The blue dome and red body at
no time pulsated, changed shape, or gave off rays.1 They watched the object, moving west to east, until it
disappeared over a rise at the eastern end of the pasture.

A radio was playing inside the barn during the event.11 Neither witness remembers if there was any
interference.

The duration of the sighting was given as 5 minutes [which
may be an overestimate but suggests the object was moving slowly1jw]

A Deputy verified the witnesses and cows were upset, and
thought something unusual had occurred.1
Sheriff1s Department contacted local airports about air traffic.1 The report was negative.

No sound, no physiological effects, no EM effects were
reported.

Close Encounter In New York, by Douglas Dains

1111 On October19, 1973
at 10:00 a.m. EDT, I received a report of a possible landing of a UFO on the
farm of Stanley Wishinsky in Owego, New York.1
I dispatched Mr. Hand Addison, who assists me on such investigations, to
obtain a preliminary report.1 Mr.
Addison1s report contained enough information to warrant a further
investigation, although no landing had occurred as the initial report stated.

1111 On October 22,
1973, my wife and I arrived on the farm of Mr. Wishinsky and asked to talk to
the two principal witnesses, Mr. Lincoln (Link) Howe, 25 years old, of Rt.#96,
Owego, N.Y., and Stanley Olkowski, 16 years old, who resides at the residence
of Stanley Wishinsky.

OBSERVATION:

1111 Mr. Howe
reported that while he was in the process of rounding up the cattle for morning
milking (0545 a.m. EDT), he noticed a blue and red object coming over the1 barn heading east.1 Mr. Howe was approximately 20 feet from the barn facing west and
estimates the object to have been about 200 feet above the barn.1 The barn is approximately 50 to 60 feet high
at its highest point.1 The object had a
fire engine red body with a dark blue dome on top and Mr. Howe estimates the
size of the object as that of a plate held at arm1s length.1 After watching the object for approximately
5 minutes as it passed over his head (no color or lights could be seen under
the object), Mr. Howe called Stanley Olkowski, who was in the barn at the time
doing chores, to come out and see the object.1
At this point, it was approximately 200 yds. from the witnesses and at
the same height of 250 to 260 feet.1 Mr.
Olkowski estimates that when he saw the object, it was about the size of a
silver dollar at arm1s length.1 Both men
said that the object game them the impression it was spinning but the object1s
blue dome and red body at no time pulsated, gave off rays, changed shape,
etc.1 There was no noise from the
object.1 As the object passed over, five
cows, which were still in the pasture, began to run in all directions.1 Two of them jumped a four-foot barbed wire
fence on the south end of the pasture tearing it down as they did.1 Another ran through the north fence,
sustaining a cut to the right side of her udder and a large cut to the left
frontal portion of her head.1 Note: the
pasture is approximately 250 yds. by 450 yds.1
It took both men until 11:00 a.m. to get the cows into the barn for
milking.1 The object itself was lost to
sight as it went over the rise on the east end of the pasture.1 The witnesses claim they have never had any
trouble before getting the cows in for milking.1 All cows gave milk that day but not as much as they normally
would.1 Since the sighting, the men have
had a hard time getting these five cows in the barn.1 As a result, they planned to leave them inside for a few days
before letting them out again in hopes that they will come in more easily, as
was the case prior to the sighting.1 Mr.
Howe and Mr. Olkowski were reluctant at first to report the sighting but
decided they had better call the Tioga County sheriff to investigate.1 These cows are worth about $800 each and
they didn1t want a recurrence of this incident.

SHERIFF1S REPORT:

1111 The Tioga county
sheriff received the initial report at 6:15 a.m., October 19, 1973.1 Deputy Edward R. White was dispatched to the
Wishinsky farm to investigate.1 I interviewed
Deputy White on October 24, 1973.1 He
stated that when he arrived at the farm of Mr. Wishinsky, the two witnesses
related their story to him of the UFO.1
Deputy White noted that the cows were quite upset as were the
witnesses.1 He drove down Gary Hunt Road
and stopped his car at the bend in the road.1
He said that he thought he heard something so he rolled down his window
and listened.1 What he heard were two
dogs at a nearby farm house which is east of the pasture where the sighting
took place.1 Both dogs, one hound and
one German shepherd, were barking and howling.1
Deputy White thought that it was unusual that the hound dog wasn1t
chasing anything such as a rabbit.1 1Usually
when a hound dog is as upset as this dog was, he would be chasing something,
but both dogs just stood there barking and carrying on and both were facing
east.11 This is the direction which the
object was reported to have departed.1
There were no lights on at the farm where the dogs were.1 The Deputy presumed the occupants of this
farm were not up yet.1 Further
investigation revealed that the residents of this farm did hear the dogs and
thought it was unusual but they didn1t bother to investigate.

1111 Deputy White
told me that he believes something unusual did occur on the farm of Mr.
Wishinsky that morning but just what it was he couldn1t say.1 He did state, however, that he believes the
witnesses did see something unusual as both men were quite upset.

1111 There were no
physical effects to the witnesses other than the normal trauma in such

and very cooperative.1 Both witnesses
gave their permission to use their names in relation to this report.1 The Tioga County Sheriff1s Department
personnel were very cooperative, helping to obtain pertinent information.

1111 As for the
height and size of the object, I believe this was much closer to witnesses than
reported and possibly the object was tilted slightly when first observed.1 I also don1t believe the witnesses fully
understood the arm1s length concept of measurement.1
