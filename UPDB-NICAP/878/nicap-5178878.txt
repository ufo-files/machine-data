Dan Wilson:
Feb. 13, 1958; W of Wake Island (BBU)
At 2114 hours local time, a bright, flare-like explosion was
sighted by the crew of an USAF C-54 approximately 2 hours flying time
west of Wake Island. During the hour within which the sighting
occurred, one visual and two radar contacts were made. The actual
"fireball" was observed directly by only the
flight engineer. The position of the C-54 was 21degrees 19' N 161
degrees 30' E.
