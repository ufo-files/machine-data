(McDonald list; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:
July 11, 1968; Eielson AFB, AK (BBU)
At 3:00 a.m. local time, Eielson AFB tower personnel
observed an orange round object at a low altitude about 18 miles south
of the base.
The Fairbanks Radar Approach Control (RAPCON) was contacted by the
tower
personnel and they reported radar returns south of of Eielson AFB. The
object
was observed visually for 25 minutes before it faded from view. (Dan
Wilson)
