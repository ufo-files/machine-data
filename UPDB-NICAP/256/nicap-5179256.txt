Fast Moving Target on Gnd RadarDan
Wilson:
November 10, 1962; North Star Bay, Greenland
At 2:15 a.m. local time, a fast moving target was
observed on ground radar on a magnetic bearing 280 inbound. A
CIRVIS REPORT was
made. Airway notified Thule ACC. Target lost in ground clutter. Two
F-102 aircraft
and an L-20 aircraft searched the area without results.  (Dan
Wilson)
