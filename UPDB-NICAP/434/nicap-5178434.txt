DC-6 Crew Encounters Blue ObjectBrad
Sparks:
Nov. 19 [16?], 1954; 130 miles ESE of New Orleans,
Louisiana (BBU)
9:04 p.m. Crew of National Airlines Flight 918 at
17,500 ft en route from New Orleans to Tampa, Flor.,
heading about 105°, saw a light flashing blue and
white moving up and down directly in front at an
unknown distance for 3-5 mins when it moved to the NE
out of sight. (NARCAP; McDonald list; Mary
Castner/CUFOS)
