Top-Like Object Heats Up Piper CubRichard Hall:
May 5, 1958; Pan De Azucar, Uruguay
3:40 p.m. An experienced pilot was flying his Piper Cub in the vicinity
of Capitan Curbelo Naval Air Base when a brilliant top-like object
(symmetrical above and below) suddenly approached his plane head-on.
The UFO about 15-20 meters in diameter, stopped about 2,000 meters away
and "... rocked twice, in a balancing motion." Rodriguez felt strong
heat, so he removed his jacket and opened the aircraft windows. The UFO
took off abruptly toward the sea "... at a fantastic speed," leaving a
thin rapor trail. (Source: The UFO Evidence (1964) page 120)
