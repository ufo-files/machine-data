2:30 a.m. The constabulary (police) station received a phone call at 2:30 a.m.  thatan unidentified object was hovering over a garage. Constable Leslie G. Leek andConstable Ian Barnes responded, arriving in time to see the object. They took 12photographs of the object (bright luminous, shape undefined) as it slowlyascended.  (Staffordshire County and Stoke-on-Trent Constabulary Report, Aug.19, 1971, filed by Constable Leslie G. Leek.)
