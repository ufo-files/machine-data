Dog oddly silent as domed disc passed over houseForm

Animal Reaction
Feature:

A dog who normally 1barks at everything1 made no sound1 as a large, silent domed disc passed over
the house.

The sighting:

At 6 PM On a clear night in a suburban setting, a woman and
her daughters, 12 and 18, noticed colored lights as they left a store and drove
home, where they apparently observed the object pass over their home.1 They described a reflective silver domed
disc with the center of the disc domed downward.1 Red and red-orange lights were flashing on or near the rim.1 It1s size was estimated as larger than their
house, its lowest altitude at 500 feet or less, and its nearest distance
between 100-500 feet away [but I would assume at the point the object passed
over the house, it was then overhead relative to the dog1jw].1 The object disappeared when they went inside
to get their father, who would not wake up.

Case was investigated by telephone.1 A newspaper article suggested an
experimental aircraft had been seen.

No sound, no EM effects, and no physiological effects were
reported.
