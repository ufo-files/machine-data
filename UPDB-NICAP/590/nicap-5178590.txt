(McDonald list) RV Draws Scramble of F-86Brad
Sparks:
May 8, 1956;
Aliquippa, Penna. (BBU)
9:48 a.m. (EST)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:

At 9:48 a.m. (EST), a flight of seven objects, silver in color,
appearing the size of a baseball held at arm's length were observed
moving south at an altitude of 5,000 to 15,000 feet at an estimated
speed of 36 mph. An F-86D fighter was scrambled but negative sightings.
The sighting was visual and electronic by TPS 1D radar. Length of
observation was 10 minutes. (Dan Wilson)
