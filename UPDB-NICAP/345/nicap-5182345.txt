9:05 PM. Carol Cloukey and Robert White had been on their way to Pittsfield,Maine for dinner. After traveling about a mile they rounded a bend and Carol, whowas driving, was the first to see a bright yellowish-orange light. Suddenly the lightmade a perfect vertical drop in mid-air. They sped up to get ahead of the object,but the light dropped lower again, and was now dead center above the carwindshield.
