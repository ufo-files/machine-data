Dan Wilson:
July 30, 1951; Selfridge AFB, Michigan
At 7:14 a.m. EDT, an unidentified object was observed on the
scopes of an AN/CPS-6B radar at the 752nd AC&W Squadron at Empire,
Michigan. The speed of the blip varied from 3,000 mph to 28,000 mph.
The length of the observation
was 18 minutes.
