AFOSI Case 9; Commercial Pilot Reports Green FlareBrad Sparks:
Dec. 5, 1948. NW of Las Vegas near Montezuma Mission, New
Mexico
9:35 p.m. Pioneer Airlines Flight 63 pilot Ernest Van Lloyd and copilot
James Smith saw a pale green (later said to be white or whitish-orange)
fireball with pale green trail at 9:35 p.m. coming headon, while flying
W on 272° heading at 9,000 ft in a C-47, attempted evasive action
but object dropped close to ground level. (FOIA)
