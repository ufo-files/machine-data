11:45 p.m. MST.  A soldier and his wife saw an pval object with red and greenbody lights approach their car at low altitude late on a cloudy night.  The groundwas illuminated (environmental illumination) by a moving light beam emittedfrom its bottom center. (Letter from witness, 9/14/67, NICAP files;   U.F.O.Investigator, Vol. IV, No. 3, Nov.- Dec.. 1967, p. 3; Keyhoe and Lore, 1969a,p.44).
