Dogs vocalizing while domed disc was nearbyGroup /Category 4

Animal Effect Cases

Case Directory
Dallas, Oregon
October 16, 1990

Animal Reaction Feature:
Dogs alerted the witness, who then saw a domed disc approach at low altitude
(500 feet or less).  Several dogs continued to 1bark-howl1 when
the object was near. The object passed low over the house and continued
off into the distance. It made a sound described as a combination of
a washing machine and air conditioner.

Joan Woodward, Animal Reaction Specialist:
This incident occurred at 8:30 pm on a clear night. The location was
a farm. A woman, 45, and her father, 69, saw a domed disc as indicated
above. When overhead, the objected glowed white (bottom center) and
then dark, and it had a green cross-pattern at the lower perimeter (not sure
if lower perimeter refers to bottom or side of object1jw). There was
a steady yellow light on top and steady red lights around the side.
The object was viewed for an estimated 2 minutes, and the witnesses noted
that the house lights dimmed as it passed over.

Sound and EM effects were reported. No physiological effects were reported.
