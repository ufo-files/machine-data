Oct. 21, 1967; Blytheville AFB, Arkansas (BBU)
6:16 a.m. Two control tower operators and an observer at the south end
of the runway saw two dark oblong table platter-shaped objects
with 7 ft long exhaust at about 1,200-1,500 ft height fly E to W,
tracked by RAPCON radar at a distance of two miles, make a turn to the
SW when they disappeared. (Hynek UFO Exp ch. 6, case DD-3)
