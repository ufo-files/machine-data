10:15 p.m. EST A pilot and his three passengers saw a round, glowing whiteobject the size of a large apartment building, with a ring of red lights around it thatblinked off and on. The object was above the plane at about 2,000 feet. (Letter toAPRO dated April 2, 1967, posted on NICAP web site.)
