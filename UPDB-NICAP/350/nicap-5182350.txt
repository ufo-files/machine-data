9:05 PM. Pulsating orange object sighted by  MUFON SSD Ridge's 8-year-olddaughter. Object was in the north heading east at low level for approximately 10minutes. This NL was logged as an OBOL (Orange Ball Of Light) and witnessedby the SSD and his son, Brian (15), who observed the object from the roof of theirhome north of town.
