Very Large Object Tracked, No VisualDan
Wilson:

July 13, 1952; Fordland,
Missouri
At the 797th AC&W Squadron at
Fordland, Missouri, one
object was observed on radar, very large at times, varied in size up to
3 degrees in
width and at an estimated speed of 180 mph to 600 mph The
object appeared to stop maneuvering as long as two minutes. The unknown
object was carried from west to east and back west towards Kansas City
and faded.
