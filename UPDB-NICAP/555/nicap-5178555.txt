(McDonald list) Radar Ops Tracked Target For 13 Minutes / Up To 3000 KnotsBrad
Sparks:
Dec. 17, 1955; Laguna [Mt. Laguna ?], Calif. (BBU) 9:45 a.m. (PST)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
December 17, 1955; Mt. Laguna, Calif.
At 9:45 p.m. PST, three USAF radar operators tracked one
target for 13 minutes at speeds estimated as fast as 3000 knots on an
AN/FPS-3
radar. The target was inbound from Prescott Radio toward Thermal Radio,
seemed to
check 20 NM north of Thermal, then turned outbound toward
Telescopepeak.
According to the report, the object appeared to be using radio aids at
Blythe, Thermal, Dagget and Inyokern.  Maintenance said
there was no internal interference on the
radar equipment and the target did not resemble any previously know
interference.
The track passed out of radar limits. (Dan Wilson)
