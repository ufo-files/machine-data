Swept Wing Object Over TokyoFran Ridge:
Not sure if this is a UFO but a UFO report was
filed by an experienced pilot because he couldn't
identify it. Apparently no aircraft features other than
the swept wing configuration and puffs of smoke were
noted. No vertical stabilizer or canopy, etc.

Dan Wilson:
May 20, 1955; Tokyo, Japan (BB)
4:45 p.m. (20/0745Z) . A military pilot made an
air-visual sighting of a swept wing object at
approximately 60,000 feet between Tokyo Radio and Daigo
Homer. It was observed for approximately 14 minutes then
the object disappeared leaving puffs of smoke. The
observer pilot was very experienced.
