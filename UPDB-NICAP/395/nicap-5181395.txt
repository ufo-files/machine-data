1:30 AM. A 52-year-old woman was watching jackrabbits by moonlight on theVeterans Administration Hospital grounds when she heard a whirring noise andlooked up to see a luminous white UFO coming down for a landing. Then a humanlike figure, well over six-foot tall with very broad shoulders, walked toward herfrom the UFO.
