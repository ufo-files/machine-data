Night. Several independent reports overnight. A 21-year-old man reported that anunidentified object glided parallel to his car about 300 yards away. The object thenabruptly headed for the car (vehicle encounter), swerved away, and left at terrificspeed. (Memphis Press-Scimitar, 3/4/67, copy in NICAP files.)
