Driver Avoids Collision With 30' ObjectBrad
Sparks:
Jan. 12, 1965: Blaine AFB, Wash. (BBU)
1:00 a.m. Member of a federal agency, (Mr. Flickinger,
a U.S. Border Patrol officer) who was driving toward
the base, saw a low-flying object, 30 ft in diameter,
which avoided collision at the last moment. He got out
of the car and saw it hovering for 1 min, then fly off
at high speed. Object tracked on radar. Same night, a
round, glowing object with a dome on top landed on a
nearby farm, melting snow in a 30 ft diameter circle.
(UFOE II, Section V; Vallée Magonia 630; NICAP
March 1965; BB files??)

Fran Ridge:
This close encounter is multiply listed as a
radar case on the NICAP dBase. However, Ted Phillips
finds this difficult to believe and we're not sure
where the documentation is to confirm that aspect of
the case.
