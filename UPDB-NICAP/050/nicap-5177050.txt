DC-3 Crew Observes DiscDan Wilson:
July 28, 1947; Bt. Mountain Home & Boise, Idaho
8:34 p.m. Crew of a Seattle-bound United Airlines Mainliner DC-3
reported disc-like object in the sky at estimated 9,010 feet as
twin-engine liner was flying at 7,500 feet. CAA said no other planes in
the area where pilot Givian said he saw the disc.
