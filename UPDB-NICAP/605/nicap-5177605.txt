April 9, 1952; Lackland AFB], Texas (BBU)

At 2240 hours, S/Sgt Victor H. Berthene, USAF, while watching a
C-97 take off from Kelly AFB, observed a white sphere-shaped object
with a short white trail traveling at a high rate of speed from SE to
NW. The observer was in Barracks building 2162 at Lackland AFB, Texas,
during the time of the sighting.
