3:30 PM. Two young girls walking under a highway overpass by a canal in werefollowing a path up a small hill when they saw a UFO hovering above somenearby brush. From out of some bushes beneath where the object had beenhovering, a thin, human-like figure emerged. It had long brown hair and waswearing a blue top and dark pants, but it also wore strange looking boots. (Source:Albert S. Rosales, Humanoid Contact Database 1977, case # 1810, citing VirgilioSanchez Ocejo, Destino, Vol. 27 # 3, quoting APRO).
