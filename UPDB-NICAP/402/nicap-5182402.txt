Forest animals freeze as cylinder passes overheadGroup
/Category 4
Animal Effect Cases

Case Directory
Glen Allan, Mississippi
January 18, 1989

Animal Reaction Feature:
A deer hunter waiting in a tree stand at 7:30 am on a clear morning
noted that birds and squirrels became silent and still. He looked
up and saw a silent cylinder pass directly overhead at an estimated
altitude of 1500 feet. After it moved away, the sounds and
motions of the birds and squirrels resumed.

Joan Woodward, Animal Reaction Specialist:
An insurance salesman, 52, a navy pilot in WWII, looked up when the
birds and squirrels froze. A silver, reflective object in the
general shape of an airplane fuselage (possibly with a stabilizer
fin—witness unsure on this point) passed directly overhead. No
windows, no lights, and no sound were detected. Its size was
estimated to be about the same as a Boeing 747 [~230 feet long—jw], its
altitude estimated at 1500 feet, and its speed, at 300 mph. The
cylinder was in view for about a minute.

The witness talked to an air traffic controller at Greenville and was
told there was no air traffic in the area at the time of the sighting.

No sound, no EM effects, and no physiological effects were reported.
