Hissing Object With SparksDan Wilson:
Jan. 10, 1965; South Boston, Virginia (BB)
7:15 p.m. EST. The observer Strickland heard a hissing
noise and looked up to see a large object with a long
trail of sparks moving across the sky from north to
south. After a few minutes the witness heard several
planes overhead.

Fran Ridge:
The only thing this case has going for it is its
location and temporal position in the chronological
file. Sounds like leftover fireworks from a New Year's
celebration.
