12:05 p.m. Radar tracking of unidentified target.

Dan Wilson:
August 21, 1951; Northern Sea of Japan
At 12:05 a.m. local time a USN P2V-3W patrol plane at the
position of 41 degrees 55' N - 133degrees 52' E, a target appeared of
the plane's radar, an AN/FPS-20. The squadron electronic officer viewed
the scope and identified the target as authentic and distinct from sea
return. The target caused a strong presentation on the scope throughout
the 45 minute contact. (Dan Wilson)
