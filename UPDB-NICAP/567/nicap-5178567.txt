1:52 p.m. (GMT) (McDonald list; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:

Jan. 24, 1956; Wheelus AFB, Tripoli, Libya (BBU)
At 2:52 p.m. local time an unidentified object was picked
up on radar at 99 degrees azimuth at 75 miles range. The object was
tracking at 330 degrees at 75 knots at 1000 feet altitude. The target
was tracked for 12 minutes at which time the object faded from the
radar.
