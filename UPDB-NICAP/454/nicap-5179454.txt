Dan Wilson:
July 6, 1965; Arlington, Texas
At 10:30 p.m. CST, two civilians saw a light in the sky to the
north that started moving up and down and to the side in very swift
movements. An object was painted on Fort Worth radar in the same
general area and an F4C aircraft flew to the area but nothing was
observed. The length of observation was 10-15 minutes.
