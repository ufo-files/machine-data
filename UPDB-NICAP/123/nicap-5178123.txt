C-47 Encounters Light Making Volent ManeuversDan Wilson:
Jan. 28, 1953; Stuttgart, Germany (BB)
At 1952 hours (7:52 p.m. local time), Lt. Col. Max O. Mitchell was
flying north in a C-47 aircraft on top an overcast at 7,500 feet,
sighted a very bright light passing in front of the aircraft at a
slight elevation above him from left to right. Mitchell called the
pilot, Major David W. Pearsall's attention to the light which was
moving
at an extremely high rate of speed. In a few seconds the light turned
to the north and then turned again in front of the C-47. The
light
made several violent maneuvers from just above the horizon to 15
degrees above the horizon. The light was observed for approximately
1-1/2 minutes.
