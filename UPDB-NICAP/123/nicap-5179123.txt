Indiana Unit No. 1, the newspapers in southern Indiana had a small
article about an

Indiana/western

but the Nike site executive officer denied the tracking in a letter to
me written on December 1st. The site map below is partial view of
a U.S. Army map from 1968 showing
the southeastern Indiana Nike CD-63 Site protecting Cincinnati and
Dayton.
