September 28, 1960, crash reported to Sheppard
AFB, Wichita Falls, Texas. Crash at a point eight
miles west of Wichita Falls (spelled Witchita Falls in
document). Command notification has been made to ATIC for a
team to come to the scene, based upon personal evaluation of
the Assistant Provost Marshal, Sheppard AFB, who has had
experience with "Moon Dust".

Sheppard Air Force Base Provost Marshal has custody
of debris.
