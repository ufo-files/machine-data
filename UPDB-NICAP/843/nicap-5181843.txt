The yacht Rainbow II was heading for Corsica,. On board was the owner of theboat, his wife and children and a crew of 11 people. About three miles ahead of theprow of the boat they saw a black cylindrical shape. This object was coming outfrom the surface of the sea like a tower and rose 30 meters into the air. Althoughthe compass showed the yacht as headed in the right direction, the yacht insteaddrifted about 12 miles south and ended up in Bastia. Another strange occurrencewas that the dog on board started barking and appeared scared and terrified.
