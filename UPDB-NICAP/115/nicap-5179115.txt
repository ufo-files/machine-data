Sept. 28, 1960; Kirksville, Missouri
At 3:16 a.m.
CDT, a normal aircraft return
appeared on an AN/FPS-6A Height Finder radar scope at 90
degrees at 70,000 feet and disappeared at 90 degrees at
100,000 feet. The length of observation was 45
seconds.
