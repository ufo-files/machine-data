Occupants Observed In Glass-Like ObjectDan

Wilson:
Jan. 20, 1965; Long Beach, Mississippi (BB)
9:40 a.m. CST. A silver like object was seen moving
west, traveling slow and high and passed
directly above Burke St. observer. The object was
transparent as if made of glass or plastic. It
seemed to have two people aboard, one in front and
the other seemed to be sitting facing the back of'
the capsule holding something large like a camera.
The length of observation was not reported.

Fran Ridge:
The document quality is really bad, except for a
letter from Quintanilla hoping (and groping?) to
find a helicopter explanation. Although this is
possible, the lack of sound with what is indicated
as a encounter close enough to distinguish occupants
would indicate this object was probably not a
helicopter.
