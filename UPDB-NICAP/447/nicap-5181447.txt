1:00 AM. A chicken farmer was walking to town to catch a bus. A bright greenishblue light appeared and swooped down from the sky to chase him. It circled backand came to hover above some nearby woods, and he could now make out a V-shaped craft. At one point he awoke briefly to see several men staring at him. Theyexamined him in silence. (Source: Bob Pratt, UFO Danger Zone: Terror and Deathin Brazil--Where Next? and in Timothy Good (ed.), The UFO Report 1991).
