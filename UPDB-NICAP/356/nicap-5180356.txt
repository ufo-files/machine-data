Lights Observed / Tracked On RadarFran
Ridge:
Aug. 23, 1967; Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada
9:45 p.m. EDT (8:45 p.m. EDT). A commercial pilot saw a series of white
lights that flashed on and off at 1-2 second intervals. Ground radar
showed a target at the approximate location of the lights, and said
that the object was in the sky and lower than 50,000 feet. The pilot
lost sight of the lights after about a minute, but Halifax radar
tracked the objects as they moved from 36 to 16 miles from the aircraft
and the blips were still on radar after the aircraft continued on its
way. The object also was reported by a separate ground witness. (Letter
from witness, 8/31/67, NICAP files; U.F.O. Investigator, Vol. IV, No.
2, Oct. 1967, p. 1; Weinstein, 1999, p. 36, from Flammonde, 1976.)
