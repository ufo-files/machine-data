12:00 p.m. EST. On the north-northeast side of the Boston metroplex, witnessesreported an oval object with red, green, f and white  body lights, oscillating up anddown when  in  motion. The object appeared to land in a field. Later two witnessesapproached the field and saw unidentified lights rise and fly away. (Fowler, 1974,p. 339.)
