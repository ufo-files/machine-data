Sparks:
Nov. 29, 1960; S of Kyushu, Japan (BBU 7134)
6:38 p.m. USAF Lt. Col. R. L. Blwlin [sp?] and Maj.
F. B. Brown, flying a T-33 jet trainer, saw a white
light slow and parallel the course of the T-33.
(Berliner)

may be more here than meets the eye. Moved against
