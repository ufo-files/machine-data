CPS6 ADC Radar Tracks Brilliant White LightBrad
Sparks:
July 25, 1957; Niagara Falls, New York (BBU)
12:25 a.m. (EST). USAF pilot 1st Lt. Robert S. Hipkins and alert center
operator S/Sgt Raymond C. Henry, both 47th Fighter Interceptor Sq on
the ground, saw a circular brilliant white object with smaller 6 pale
green lights on its perimeter move slowly at constant altitude at 8
mins first then make fast pivoting turns, maneuvering radically SE to
NE (from azimuth 150° elevation 45° to azimuth 75°
elevation 65°), disappearing in a rapid steep climb. Radar tracked
for 3 mins by CPS­6 ground ADC site. (McDonald list; Jan Aldrich)
