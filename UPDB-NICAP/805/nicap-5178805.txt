Whiteman AFB, Missouri, G , (McDonald list)Dan
Wilson:
Nov. 6, 1957; Whiteman AFB, Missouri (BBU)
Beginning at around 4:39 p.m. CST, a number of radar tracks
at different times were observed on search radar (RAPCON) at distances
of 38 to 52 miles north and west of the base. The speed of the
objects varied from very slow
to 350 knots. On the radar scope the UFOBs were
easy to recognize
and they gave a good clear return that was as large and as bright as a
B-47. The sightings
lasted from 1 to 18 minutes. One object stopped for two sweeps of
the radar.
At one point a B-47 and an UFOB were on the radar scope at the
same time. The returns from
the UFOBs were round while the returns from the B-47 were
elongated. On the
precision radar, the UFOBs gave a return that appeared to the same as
an
aircraft. (Dan Wilson)
