A Montana Fish and Game Department employee saw a light flying directlybehind a B-52 bomber. Using his rifle scope to get a better look, he noted that thestrange object seemed to be pacing the aircraft. The object then briefly attacheditself to the B-52, detached itself, and climbed out of sight. The sighting wasreported to Sheriff Pete Howard of Choteau County. Howard conducted follow-upinterviews with military personnel and learned that as the object attached itself tothe B-52, the plane's radar equipment went out. (Reference: Lawrence Fawcett andBarry J. Greenwood, Clear Intent, page 35)
