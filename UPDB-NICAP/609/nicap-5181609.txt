A woman had been taking a nap when she woke up to see a large craft hoveringoutside the upstairs window, over a nearby wood shack. She fainted and then hearda voice telling her to open her eyes. When she did she was confronted by a tallcreature resembling an insect, thin and white in color, wearing a dark outfit. Itcommunicated with her by using telepathy.
