C-45 Encounters Three ObjectsDan Wilson
March 27, 1957; Roswell, New Mexico (BBU)
8:35 p.m. USAF pilot Lt. Sontheimer flying C-45
transport saw to the left 3 bright white circular
objects in tight formation on collision course. He
immediately flashed his taxi lights, one object shot
straight up above him the other 2 continued on passing
in front. When he flashed his taxi lights (again?) the
objects instantly blinked out and disappeared.
(Weinstein; NARCAP; FUFOR Index) 6 secs
