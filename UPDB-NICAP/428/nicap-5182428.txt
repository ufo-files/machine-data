Animal Reaction
Feature:

A dog barked and howled during the time a disc estimated to
be at treetop level and less than 500 feet away was observed.1 Reportedly the dog lost its appetite for two
days and was afraid to approach the area where the disc had been seen.

The sighting:

The sighting occurred on a farm at 5:30 pm. The sky was
partly cloudy.11 A 17-year-old girl saw
a silent disc with a reflective silver surface and ringed with unwavering white
lights.1 The object generated wind that
swayed trees.1

No EM effects or physiological effects were reported.1 No sound was reported1 [a wind sound implied1jw]
