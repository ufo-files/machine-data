MATS Pilot Tracks Unidentifed Target (BBU)Brad
Sparks:

Dec. 6, 1955; Marianna, Florida (BBU)
6 [1?] a.m. USAF pilot flying MATS transport radar tracked
unidentified target. (Project 1947; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:
At 1 a.m. (EST), one object picked up on MATS radar. Seemed
to climb from 15,000 feet to 30,000 feet. First seen over Marianna,
Florida. Elapsed time of object from Marianna, Fla. to Montgomery,
Ala., five
minutes. Approx. distance 125 miles Marianna to Montgomery. Last seen
over Maxwell
AFB, Alabama. (Dan Wilson)
