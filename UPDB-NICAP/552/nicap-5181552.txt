Mr. & Mrs. Morello were sitting in their car listening to their car's stereo whenthey began getting heavy interference on the radio. Mr. Morello got out of the carto investigate and found himself engulfed in a warm, violet cloud of light. He sawfour humanoid "shapes" and other lights around him.
