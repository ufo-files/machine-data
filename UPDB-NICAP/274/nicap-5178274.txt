(McDonald list) One to three blips on PPI Scope with speeds of 100 to 6,000 mphin 30 mile area.Brad
Sparks:

Jan. 14, 1954; Kelly AFB, Texas (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:

At 10:10 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., one to three blips were
seen on PPI scope of radar system, with speeds from 100 to
6,000 mph over one hour and 50 minute time span. The blips appeared as
solid objects.
Aircraft scrambled with no contact. Three objects joined together
for approximately three minutes then separated with a definite
heading and disappeared when local aircraft was in area. The
objects returned to area after aircraft departed.(Dan Wilson)
