Three Airliners See Silver ObjectBrad
Sparks:
Jan. 4-5, 1959; Taft, Calif.(BBU)
11:30 p.m. (PST). Pilots of TWA C-54 and TWA
Constellation saw a silver oval object with silver trail
flying at 20,000 ft and 10 miles from aircraft [?].
(Project 1947)

Dan Wilson:
Jan. 4, 1959; Taft, California
11:30 p.m. PST. Pilots of three airliners, TWA 52, TWA
87, and Resort 910, observed an oval object the size of
a basketball at 50 to 100 yards that appeared as a ball
of fire with a sliver tail and a longer trail coming
from underneath. The object was tracking SW to NE and
made a sharp 90 degree turn to the right and faded from
view. The length of observation was 4-5 seconds.
