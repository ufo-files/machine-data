July 19, 1956; Hutchinson, Kansas
Naval Air Station reported tracking "a moving unidentified
object" on radar, observed visually by state police as "teardrop
shaped" light source. (UFOE, VIII) Noticeable maneuvers of UFO
"vertically and horizontally over a wide area of the sky" mapped by
Wichita Eagle. This is of national interest, called 4602nd. Two of the
strange objects seen over Arkansas City for 5 hours. A State Patrolman
reported seeing objects moving east at a high rate of speed near
Hutchinson, Kansas.
