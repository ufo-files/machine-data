(McDonald list)  Object Rises Almost Vertically, Blip On Radar (BBU)Brad
Sparks:

March 29, 1955; Sonoma and Soledad, Calif. (BBU)
7:35/7:38 p.m. (PST) McGrath (GOC observer) and Brown.
(McDonald list)

Date: Tue, 29 Sep 2009 12:12:21
+0000 (GMT)

Dan Wilson:
A round silvery white object with red and green flashing lights
was seen to rise vertically at a 45 degree angle . The 666th
AC&W Squadron reported 8:15 p.m. (30/0415Z)  that they
had a slow
moving blip in that area, but for only a few sweeps of their radar. The
area was searched
thoroughly by two F-51 aircraft but without results. (Dan Wilson)
