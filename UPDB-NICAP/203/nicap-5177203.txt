March 6, 1949; Killeen Base, Camp Hood, Texas (BBU)
8:20 p.m. (AFOSI 29 & 30) Army Sgt. Hubert Vickery and PFC
John Ransom on patrol at the AFSWP (Armed Forces Special Weapons
Project) nuclear weapons storage site saw a blue-white oblong object
about 2 ft x 1 ft in size travel S from 286° to 279° azimuth
elevation 5°45'. Other sightings by Army patrols from 8:30 p.m. to
2 a.m. (FOIA)

Report stated that several sightings of unusual "light" phenomena in
the
atmosphere above Site Baker (Q Area) had been made by security
personnel.
