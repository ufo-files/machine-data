Man in C-118 Reports Silver ObjectBrad
Sparks:
Nov. 20, 1958; W of Calif. coast (BBU)
6:15 a.m. (PST). Military pilot saw a round silver
object traveling at high speed and high altitude.
(Project 1947) 20/1415Z

Dan Wilson:
Nov. 20, 1958; West of California (BBU)
At 6:15 a.m. PST, a man in the right seat of a C-118
transport flying at 11,000 feet on a heading of 60
degrees observed a round silver object traveling
straight at high speed at high altitude heading toward
the sun. The object was observed for 15 seconds. The
C-118 was at approximately 840 miles west of Los Angeles
(3447 N 133.07 W).
