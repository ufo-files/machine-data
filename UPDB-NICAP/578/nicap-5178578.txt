Object Evades Crew of AirlinerBrad
Sparks:
Feb, 19, 1956; Houston, Texas (BBU 3977)
6:07 a.m. (CST). Crew of Eastern Airlines Super
Constellation saw intense white light, moving 4-5
times the speed of the airplane, evaded by the pilot.
(Berliner; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:
Feb. 19, 1956; Houston, Texas (BBU 3977)
At 6:07 a.m. CST, an Eastern Airlines Super
Constellation airliner broke out on top of cloud cover
at 1,500 feet and the crew observed an intense white
light approximately 1000 feet above the airplane. The
pilot veered the plane to the left as the object
continued on to the right at approx. 285 degrees. The
object moved away rapidly at 4-5 times the plane's
speed of 250 mph. The object was at about 4,000 feet
at this time.
