Animal Effect Cases

Case Directory
White Pine, Michigan
September 1967

Animal Reaction Feature:
On an overcast night in the town of White Pine, a 16-year-old, accompanied
by his dog, saw a disc-shaped object that hovered over them at an estimated
altitude of 500 feet or less. No sound was heard. The dog did
not appear to react to the object1s presence.

Joan Woodward, Animal Behavior Specialist:
This sighting apparently was reported years after it occurred. The object
was described as disc-shaped or slightly elliptical. Square pulsating
lights of multiple colors were located around the edge of the bottom (witness
could not remember the colors). The surface of the object was dark.

The object approached from the east, hovered over the witness, then moved
southeast out of the town area and then to the northeast and out of sight.

No EM effects or physiological effects were reported.
