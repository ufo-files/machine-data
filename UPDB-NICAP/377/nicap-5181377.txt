11:00 PM. Lonnie Stites and his wife, Deborah, were driving their pickup truckaround a bend in the road when "the whole area lit up like a football field light wasturned on, and a very bright light was shined on our windshield." He saw a man ofnormal height or slightly smaller stature standing alongside the road. He washuman looking, but dressed in green coveralls with square glasses and a tight greencap over his ears. He was "waving us down." (Source: David F. Webb & TedBloecher, HUMCAT: Catalogue of Humanoid Reports, case 1977-11, citingMonte Blue Skelton & Bob Pratt).
