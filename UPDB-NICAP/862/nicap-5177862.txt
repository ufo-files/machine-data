July 27, 1952, near Wilmington, Delaware
FLYOBRPT

At 2300Z Mr. James R. Thomas observed one cylindrical object with
a domed top and bottom moving northwest to southeast. The object
appeared to move in an upright
position. The object disappeared suddenly. Sighting lasted for
approximately 90 seconds.
