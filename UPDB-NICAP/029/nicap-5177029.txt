30-50' Circular Object Paces B-25, AcceleratesBrad
Sparks:
July 6, 1947; Clay Center, Kansas (BBU)
1:45 p.m. AAF pilot Major A. B. Browning and crew flying
B-25 E to Kansas City saw a silvery circular object
30-50 ft diameter pacing the aircraft at a little lower
altitude then shot off at high speed heading E at 11,000
ft at 210 mph. (Project 1947; FOIA; Bloecher 1967)
