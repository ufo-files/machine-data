NEPA Employees Report Light In Control AreaBrad Sparks:
Dec. 18, 1950; Oak Ridge, Tennessee
Between 0820 and 0830 hours seven officials of the
NEPA Division while traveling in two cars observed an
unidentified flying object. At 0839 hours to 0845
hours a small "paint" appeared on the scopes of the
663rd AC&W Squadron AF radar station, Knoxville
Airport, near the southeast corner of the Oak Ridge
Controlled Area. The target was on a magnetic bearing
of 190 degrees at a speed of 60 mph at an estimated
altitude of 2500 feet. Fighter interception was
attempted with negative results. (Blue Book files,
McDonald list; FUFOR Index) Note: Lots of nuclear
activity going on at Oak Ridge at the time: Uranium
enrichment, NEPA Project, Spent Fuel rod reprocessing.
