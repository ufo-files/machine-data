Dan Wilson:
Dec.
10, 1952; Pope AFB, South Carolina
From 9:20 a.m. to 5:15 p.m. EST, a bright circular object
was observed on a PPI scope of a CPS/5D radar. It was
also observed on height
finding radars. The blip was detected at 155
degrees azimuth at 8,000 feet altitude.The
blip was also observed on search radar
and
height finding radar
at the 728th
AC&W Squadron, Fort Bragg, N.C. The object showed a slight
circular movement. An F-51 aircraft was directed to investigate.
The pilot saw nothing unusual.
