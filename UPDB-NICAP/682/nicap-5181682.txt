Two teenage girls from London Mills reported a tubular aircraft approached theircar as they drove on Illinois Rt.11 near Rapatee. It came within about 10 feet oftheir vehicle, circled them once, and left "toward the east at a fantastic speed, butthere wasn't any noise at all."
