Dan Wilson:
June 10, 1949; 20 miles SW of Boston, Mass (BBU)
8:45 a.m. EST. 1st Lt. Kirschbaum, USAF pilot of
the 58th Fighter Interceptor Squadron while flying a T-6 aircraft
at 4000 feet altitude observed a white tubular-shaped object
flying at an estimated speed
of 100 mph. The object was at approximately 30,000 feet altitude
and was about 100 feet long. Kirschbaum chased the object but lost it
in the overcast. (Dan Wilson)
