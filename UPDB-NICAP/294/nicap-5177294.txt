2 USAF pilots flying military aircraft saw an oval object climb at tremendousspeed to the S. (Project 1947)Dan Wilson:
Sept. 5, 1949; Near Lebec, California (BB)

At around 12:00 p.m. PST, two Air Force pilots while flying
in a private aircraft observed an oval-shaped metallic-looking object
approximately 35 feet in diameter while flying in the vicinity of
Lebec. The object was approximately 3000 feet below the plane when
first sighted. The object rose to approximately 7000 feet in altitude.
The rate of climb was terrific. The object then seemed to hover for a
time and then it turned in a sharp left bank and headed in a southerly
direction climbing gradually. The object appeared to be made of a
metallic substance and had a canopy on top. The total time the object
was in sight was about 45 seconds.
