Battleship-Sized Object Tracked By 3 AirlinesJean Waskiewicz:
Three pilot crews from 3 different airlines observed and
confirmed seeing an unidentified flying object as big as a battle ship
after being alerted by Lisbon Air Traffic Control Center. The sighting,
described by experts as the most important in the past decade, was also
made by the crew of a British Tristar below the Trident and the pilot
of a Portuguese airliner. All the stories tallied. Report and You Tube
video linked below.
