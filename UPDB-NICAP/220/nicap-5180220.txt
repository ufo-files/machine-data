5:00 p.m. MDT. A school-bus driver saw a circular, dull aluminum object that hada superstructure with eight circular marks or portholes around its midsection.Above that was a glass-like dome. The object paced the bus for 2 miles. (Clausenreport, NICAP files; U.F.O Investigator, school bus Vol. IV, No. 1, May-June, p.3.)
