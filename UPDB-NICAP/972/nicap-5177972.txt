Brad Sparks:
Aug. 29, 1952. WNW of Thule, Greenland (at 771 N.,
75115' W). 10:50 a.m. (EST). US Navy
Atlantic Fleet Patrol Squadron VP-23, Thule, aircraft
P4Y-2 at 10,000 ft heading 0001 True at 150 knots (173
mph) in a half standard rate left turn (4-min 3601
turn), copilot acting as pilot Lt JG William A.
O'Flaherty, and navigator LtJG R. S. Moore in the
aircraft astrodome, were following an 85 ft diameter
Skyhook balloon launched from an icebreaker US Coast
Guard Cutter Eastwind when upon release of the parachute
instrument package from the balloon they sighted 3 white
discs or globes, about 1 to almost the full apparent
size of the balloon, in triangle formation slightly W of
N [NNW?], clustered to the right of the Skyhook
instrument package at 74,000 ft for some 2-3
minutes. [Discrepancy in 2004 account claiming
that pilot Lt. John C. Callahan and aircraft commander
Merchant (navigator Moore?) also observed UFO1s, though
denied by the copilot report of O1Flaherty in 1952.]
O1Flaherty lost the objects in the sun [in the SSE] but
Moore then [flying very fast in triangular formation
with a vertical banking] climbed to disappearance in 5
secs [3? secs] with no trail or exhaust or visible means
of propulsion. (Sparks; Naval History
magazine Oct 2004, web version; Jan Aldrich; NICAP Dan
Wilson; Berliner) 2-3 mins

2 witnesses [4?][1/10 ? Full Moon] (Note: The
