A/C Crew Saw Lights / Radar Targets ConfirmedBrad
Sparks:
March 13, 1959. Duluth, Minn. (BBU)
6:20 p.m. (CST).
Military aircraft crews, 2 F-941s, 2 B-521s, and 2
F-1021s,
saw an object with orange, red, white, green lights, the
red
lights rotating on the bottom. B-52 pilot 1certain1 the
visual was a planet probably Venus [which was low on
horizon from 31,000 ft]. Ground radar tracking by 707th
AC&W Sq, Grand Rapids AFS, Minn., and 756th
ACWRON Sq, with scope photos taken for RAND project.
F-94C and F-102A detected ECM radar transmissions.
(Sparks; NICAP
website; Project 1947; McDonald list)
