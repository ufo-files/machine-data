July 17, 1952, near Lockbourne AFB, Ohio (Unidentified)

Between 0100 and 0400 hours William W. Stevenson and
William O. Havens, observed a circular object with an orange and green
glow at an estimated 5000 feet in altitude 10 miles north of Lockbourne
AFB, Ohio. The object would move from side to side and remain
stationary for long periods of time. Both Stevenson and Havens were
recently discharged from the USAF.
