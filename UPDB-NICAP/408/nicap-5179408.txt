Object With Rings ObservedDan Wilson:
Jan.18, 1965; Buchanan, Virginia (BB)
6:00 p.m. EST. Three children observed a round object
with 3 rings like donuts stacked one on the other moving
directly overhead. The object seemed to be high when
first sighted to the north. The object paused
momentarily and appeared to descend slightly, then
ascend and continued on course to the south at a fast
rate of speed. It was observed for 2 to 5 minutes. A
check with the Washington Air Defense Sector revealed
that there were no aircraft under their operational
control at the time of the sighting. The area was
scanned by a AN/FPS-6 height finder radar 45 minutes
after the sighting.
