3:50 AM. Eight soldiers on a military patrol suddenly saw a bright light nearby.The soldiers' dog and horses remained still while the lights hovered nearby. Theleader of the group, Corporal Armando Valdes, ordered the other soldiers to putout their campfire. The two large lights were about 800 meters away, and hoveringclose to the ground. Cpl.  Valdes suddenly reappeared. He had a strange look onhis face and he emitted a sinister laugh, asking several times where his motherwas. (Source: Albert S. Rosales, Humanoid Contact Database 1977, case # 3062,citing Diego Contreras & Raul Nunez).
