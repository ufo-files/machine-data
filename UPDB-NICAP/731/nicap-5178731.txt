Two Aircraft Observe Object / Excellent Blip On RadarNARCAP
Report:
Aug. 30, 1957; Chesapeake Bay, nr. Norfolk, VA
A Capital Airlines pilot with 17 years & 3,000,000 miles
logged was flying a Viscount at 12,000' approaching Norfolk, Va., with
a Northeast Airlines DC-6 "directly above" on the same heading at
20,000. The Viscount pilot saw a "brilliant" object which "flew fast
and then abruptly halted 20 mi. in front of us at 60,000 ft. altitude."
The Northeast pilot tried to acquire the object on radar: with the
antenna at 0 degrees elevation nothing was detected, but with the
antenna elevated to 15 degrees he acquired "an excellent blip right
where I told him to look for the object." According to the Viscount
pilot, the object "dissolved right in front of my eyes, and the crew
above lost it from the scope at the same time. They said it just faded
away." The entire incident lasted "several minutes". (NARCAP Report
item # 14)
