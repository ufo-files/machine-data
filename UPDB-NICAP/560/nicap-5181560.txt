Three boys saw two lights. Then they could see four lights; two in front and two inthe back. Then a blue light appeared between the two lights in the rear, and flashedabout 7 or 8 times. Each flash was accompanied by a beep, then the blue lightstayed on. The object was about as big as a jet and approximately 200-300' highand about 70 yards from the witnesses.
