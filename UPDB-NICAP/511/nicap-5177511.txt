Dan Wilson:
Sept.16, 1951; 3 miles NE of Marion, Ohio (BBU)
12:17 p.m. Mr. Crane Grover, President Federal
Bank of Cleveland, was flying his Cessna aircraft when
he observed an object with a high tail, 45 degree
sweptback wing, long pointed nose, and painted black,
coming towards him at a 50 degree reciprocal heading. He
barley escaped colliding with the object. The Cessna was
at an altitude of 2,800 feet on a heading of 230
degrees.
