Dan Wilson:
May 4, 1957; Approx. 240 miles West of Los Angeles,
California (33 degrees 52' N 123 degrees 25' W; 32
degrees N 122 degrees 55' W)
2:25 a.m. PST. A meteor-like object was seen to pass in
front of an RC-121 aircraft flying at 9,000 feet going
east to west. The yellowish-red colored object was
observed by 2nd Lt Leland M. Martin, the co-pilot. The
object was estimated to be at 10,000 feet altitude. A
second sighting of a similar object took place at 2:43
a.m., and this one passed below the aircraft at
approximately 8,000 feet. The objects appeared to be as
large as a baseball held at arm's length. The length of
observation was 5-8 seconds. (Dan Wilson)
