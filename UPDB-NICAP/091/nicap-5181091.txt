2:30 p.m. 15-year old David Mahon was behind his house photographing his dogwhen he heard a loud drone and saw a black disc-shaped object passing over. Hetook 5 photographs; then he felt himself drawn upward, & lost consciousness.(Humcat 1975-2. Source: Eileen Sperber & David Shert, Type: G HighStrangeness Index: 8 Reliability of Source: 7) [Comments: This case appears tohave been an early abduction that maybe was not thoroughly investigated.Hypnotic regression might have revealed more of what occurred to Mahon.]
