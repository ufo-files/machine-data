A single engine Sundowner flying from Evansville, Indiana to Cincinnati, Ohio,encountered a UFO flying a collision course approximately 22 miles north ofLouisville, Kentucky. The pilot accompanied by his instructor and girlfriend,related that he was flying at 5,500' when he spotted two bright lights at eye level atsome unknown distance ahead.
