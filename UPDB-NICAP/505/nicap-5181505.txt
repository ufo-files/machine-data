The crew of the fishing trawler Vasily Kiselev also observed something quiteextraordinary. Rising vertically from under the water was a doughnut-shapedobject. Its diameter was between 300 and 500 meters. It hovered at the altitude offour to five kilometers. The trawler's radar station was immediately renderedinoperative.
