At 2:45 a.m. a very large glowing disc-shaped UFO headed a formation of threeother craft that flew from north to south at an estimated 1000 meters altitude. Theyreportedly lit up the sky like day. Each object was white in color with a green halo,and they left behind a bluish trail. A truck driver said his trucks radio stoppedworking at the time.
