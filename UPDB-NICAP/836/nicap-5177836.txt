"Possible GCI Sighting" of Circular LightsDan
Wilson:
July 23, 1952; Santa Cruz, California (BB)
4:10 a.m. Three or more circular lights were
seen high in the sky changing colors from blue to red to
purple. Two of the objects circled
overhead and then sped away at high speed then returned to hover
overhead. The lights
were observed from 4:10 to 4:54 a.m. Page two of the teletype message
states: (3) "Visual with possible GCI radar sighting PD".
