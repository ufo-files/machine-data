Light Stops & StartsDan Wilson:
Jan. 5, 1965; Lynchburg, Virginia (BB)
On this date, (exact time not given) witnesses observed
a round circle of light high in the sky moving
southward. The object would pause then continue on
course. This was reported to the Weather Bureau at the
Lynchburg Airport by telephone. The area was scanned for
30 minutes by a AN/FPS-6 height finder radar. The length
of the visual observation was 5 minutes. (Dan Wilson)
