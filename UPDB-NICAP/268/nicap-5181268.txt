4:00 a.m. A truck driver driving saw a bright object ahead and to his left.. It waslow to the ground. The light had maintained its position relative to his truck forsome time, so the driver woke up his companion. The driver estimated the objectwas about one kilometer away and 300 meters in altitude. (Source: MarkRodeghier, UFO Reports Involving Vehicle Interference, case 392, citing ACOSBulletin, June 1976)
