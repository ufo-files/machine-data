2:00 a.m. MST. A newspaper editor and two policemen saw a torpedo-shapedobject with multi-colored flashing body lights that flew irregularly at high speed.The object had bright light beams at each end, and a made a sound like a hugevacuum cleaner. (Wichita Eagle, Kansas, 3/9/67, copy in NICAP files; McDonald,1967, Case 6, reprinted in Vaughan, 1995, p. 205.)
