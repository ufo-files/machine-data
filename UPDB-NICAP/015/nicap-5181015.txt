5:30 a.m.  The witness was riding his motorcycle to work, when suddenly he foundhimself face to face with two humanoid beings and having no memory of havingstopped the motorcycle. It was still dark at this hour. In an adjacent field he saw adark circular object. Physical traces were found at the landing site.
