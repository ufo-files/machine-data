A 31-year-old paramedic was driving to pick up her husband (a policeman) fromwork when the 3-year-old son drew her attention to a "moving star" nearlyoverhead. At destination a few seconds later she noted a silver disc-shaped objectwhich reversed direction, moved forward again and moved off behind building towhich she was parked.
