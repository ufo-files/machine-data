4:00 a.m. LT. A farm woman and farm hands saw a large disc-shaped object withbright green, red, yellow and blue body lights descend behind some woods about500 meters away. A few minutes later two cylindrical objects also with body lightsappeared, and the disc reappeared, taking up position between the two cylinders,which closed in on the disc, turned, and all three as a unit shot up at terrific speedand disappeared in less than a second.
