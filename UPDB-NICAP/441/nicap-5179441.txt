Object Splits In Two, Then ThreeBrad
Sparks:
May 7, 1965; Oxford, Mich. (BBU 9389)
7:30 p.m. M. E. Marshall saw a light, like a satellite,
split into 2 parts, one of which was copperish color,
then 2 more joined up. One object may have been
tumbling. (Berliner)

May 7, 1965; Oxford Michigan (BBU 9389)
7:30 p.m. EST. Mr. Marlin Marshall, his 12 year old son
and his 9 year old daughter, observed a shiny object
directly overhead. The object appeared to be at a great
height. Then the bright object appeared to split and
became 2 objects. Shortly afterward the shiny object
gave off three other shiny but smaller objects. Another
object was seen which appeared to be much higher making
6 objects in all. All the objects eventually faded from
view. The objects were viewed through 6 X 30 binoculars.
