3:15 AM. CST. On Interstate 80 three people driving in a van outside Cozad saw a100-foot diameter disc hover 80 feet over the highway. It was blazingly brilliantand had more than 15 colored circular lights around the rim. They heard anintermittent beeping on the van's CB radio.
