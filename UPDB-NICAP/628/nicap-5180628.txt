GCA Has Two Uncorrelated TargetsDan
Wilson:
November 28, 1968; Nakhon Phanom (NKP), Thailand
At 9:56 a.m. local time, Takhli Royal Thai Air Force
Base (RTAFB) Ground Control Approach (GCA) reported two low and
slow moving objects approximately 13 NM south of NKP. Contact was lost
when objects were 9 NM west of base. Sightings were confirmed by Invert
radar. NKP Command Post was informed that the sightings "definitely
were not ghosts." (Dan Wilson)
