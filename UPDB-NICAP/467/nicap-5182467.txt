Dog barked at hovering oval dome; also fearfulGroup /Category 4

Animal Effect Cases

Case Directory
Piketon, Ohio
June 14, 1993

Animal Reaction Feature:
At 11 am, a boy was mowing the lawn when his 3-year-old dog began barking
at a domed disc hovering over a nearby field. The dog stood and barked
furiously, hair raised up and moved only when the boy moved.

Joan Woodward, Animal Reaction Specialist:
On a clear morning in an area described as a farm and lake, the 12-year-old
witness was alerted by his dog and saw the object hovering as described above.
The object quickly moved off, executed a loop, and disappeared in the distance
to the NE. It was visible for only 5-10 seconds.

The object had a black, flattened oval bottom with a gray-white oval 1bump1
on top of that (2/3 of the bottom1s width) and a gray-white high central dome
on top of that (1/3 the bottom1s width). At least part of the surface
was reflective.

Distances were not estimated (other than 1nearby field.1). The elevation
was estimated to be 45 degrees.

The boy reported shaking nervously during and after the sighting.

The sighting was referred to MUFON by the Ohio Air National Guard and was
investigated on site.

No sound was heard. No EM effects or physiological effects were reported.
