10:30 AM. Tom Dawson, 63, a retired car salesman, was taking a walk down tohis favorite fishing pond, when suddenly a disc-shaped craft silently came intoview, hovering just a few feet off the ground between nearby trees. Dawsondescribed the object as being about 50 feet in diameter and 15 feet high, encircledby what appeared to be portholes, with a dome on top. As he watched helpless, aramp was extended down to the ground and seven hairless, snow-white entitiesappeared. (Source: The Encyclopedia of Extraterrestrial Encounters, edited byRonald D. Story. New American Library, a division of Penguin Putnam Inc., 375Hudson Street, New York, N.Y. 10014. 2001. Feature submitted by researcherBilly J. Rachels. (mysterious-america.net)
