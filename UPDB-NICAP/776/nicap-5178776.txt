Nov. 5, 1957; Eglin AFB, Florida (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:

Nov. 5, 1957; Eglin AFB, Florida (BBU)
At 2:37 p.m., local time, an object was
tracked by
an AN/FPS-16 radar set at high altitude. Later another
object was
detected at 45,000 feet while the the first object was
now at 39,000
feet. Flight paths were
not on a straight line at any time. After 1 hour and 33
minutes the
objects faded from radar at an altitude of 204,800 feet
heading west.
(Dan Wilson)
