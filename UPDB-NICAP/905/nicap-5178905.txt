Object Flies Low Over Base, Tracked On RadarFran
Ridge:
May (middle) 1958; Malmstrom AFB, Montana
Nighttime. UFO approached from the north and
hovered over the alert hanger, at about 1,000 feet,
appeared as a round metallic looking object (called a flying
saucer by the guard), no estimate of size is recalled, nor any other
details on the object itself. The base radar 1-1/2
miles distant picked up the object as did the FAA radar about five
miles
away across the city of Great Falls. Object was apparently hovering
over the alert hanger and atomic missile and bomb storage
building right nearby. Object then slowly moved down the length of the
runway, then moved across town (about 3 miles) to the Municipal
Airport at Great Falls and hovered over the National Guard (F-89)
parking ramp, then flew off. (The lead for this case came from Jan
Aldrich in the form of a letter
from the Bay Area Subcommitte of NICAP, which is an undated memo. See
the report
below which was quoted directly from that memo).

Dan Wilson:
I did a search and found nothing during May 1958 for Malmstrom. If this

4, 1958 which we
already have. I searched the Newspaper archive and found a C-119 plane
crash at Malmstrom during the middle of May. The plane had lost power
and crashed near the
base.
