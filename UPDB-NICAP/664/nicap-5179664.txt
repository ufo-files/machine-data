9:45 p.m. EST. About 25 miles southeast of I Peabody, witnesses reported an ovalobject with flashing red lights around its-circumference,  and two smaller whitebody lights. The object reacted to searchlights (light reaction) shone on it by zig-zagging, accelerating straight up and disappearing. (Fowler 1974, p. 339.)
