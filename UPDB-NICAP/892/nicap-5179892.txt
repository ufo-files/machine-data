Noon MST. A woman saw two green cigar-shaped objects with a row of windows.Two small "propellers" were on top of each end. One object hovered over thehighway, then paced the car and was joined by the second object that rose up fromthe ground. (Lubbock Avalanche-Journal, Texas, 8/10/67; NICAP report form.)
