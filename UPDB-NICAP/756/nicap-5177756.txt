Light Maneuvers Near C-60 AirlinerBrad Sparks:
July 10, 1952; Near Quantico, Virginia. (BBU)
8:18 p.m. Pilot of National Airlines Flight 42, a C-60
aircraft, saw a very bright amber glow, stationary then climbing slowly
till disappearance. (Project 1947)

Dan Wilson:
July 10, 1952; Near Quantico, Virginia.
8:18 p.m. EST. Capt. J. E. Lundy and Copilot Leon Blanks
flying a C-60 airliner for National Airlines observed one object
resembling an aircraft landing light. The object became brighter as it
approached the airliner from the north. The object then performed a
climbing turn away from the aircraft and the appearance changed,
resembling a light emitted by a red flare. The object was reportedly
too fast to be an aircraft of known type.
