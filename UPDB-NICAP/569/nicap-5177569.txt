MATS C-47 Crew Encounter ObjectBrad Sparks:
Feb. 12, 1952; Bet. Friendship Airfield and Baltimore, Maryland (BBU)
9:30 p.m. USAF MATS C-47 pilot and copilot saw a bright white object
move slowly then speed away. Then at 10 p.m. they saw 10 miles S of
Baltimore a similar object. (GRUDGE/BB Rpt; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:
Feb. 12, 1952; Bet. Friendship Airfield and Baltimore,
Maryland (BBU)
9:30 p.m. Two MATS pilots flying a C-47 on a local flight
observed a bright white object at an estimated distance of 8 to 10
miles. The object was observed twice. Description: Bright white object
about 1/6 the size of the moon. Speed: Slow at first the it
accelerated very rapidly Time: First sighting at 2030 hours Second
sighting at about 2100 hours Observers: Both are experienced pilots. In
both instances the object was observed for two or three
minutes.
