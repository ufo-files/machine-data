Radar Tracks Objects In Three InstancesBrad Sparks:
Feb. 2, 9, 21, 1956; Camp Irwin,
Calif. (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
Feb. 2, 1956; At Camp Irwin, California (BBU)
PFC Richard L. Walker of Company "C", 723rd Tank
Battalion saw a flying object moving from North to South
at an estimated speed of thousands of miles per hour at
an altitude of over 15,000 feet . During the dates 2
February - 21 February 1956, sightings of unidentified
flying objects were made at Camp Irwin, the Armored
Combat Training Center. Sightings were visual and in
three instances by radar oscilloscope. One object seen
on February 9, 1956, by SFC Kenneth D. Marrs and PFC
Ricard moved from North to South to a location at grid
square 3114. It stayed at this location for one minute.
It then moved with a tremendous burst of speed from grid
square 3114 to grid square 4502. The object was observed
in this position for four minutes. The object was
described as oval in shape, brilliant luminosity, and no
sound. The sightings were reported to the George Air
Force Base Intelligence Officer.
