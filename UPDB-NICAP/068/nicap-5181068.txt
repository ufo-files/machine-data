Evening. Two 7th graders and one of their moms reported something quite largewith lights all around it. Described as round and black, going around in a circle,bigger than an airplane.  A beeping sound was heard. The mother reported theobject as shaped like a cigar "and it had little square lights that went on and off."(Newsclipping from NCS)
