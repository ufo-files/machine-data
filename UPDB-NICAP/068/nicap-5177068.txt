September 8, 1947, Logan Utah, (BBU)

Mr. and Mrs. Richard Anderson, Mr. and Mrs. Norman
Hall and some unidentified people observed five groups of 35-60
small objects yellow-white in color flying several thousand feet in the
air at a high rate of speed. Time of observation was 10:30 P.M.
