July 23, 1952; Altoona, Penna (BBU 1567)

Two 2 man crews of F-94D fighter aircraft
observed 3 unidentified objects at approximately 1250 EDT. The objects
were cylindrical or rectangular in shape, silver in color, in a
stack formation, one directly above the other at 50,000 to 80,000
feet in altitude. The objects were under observation for approximately
20 minutes.
