Submarine...Takes
Aboard...Farmer
Charleston, MO
April 5, 1941

Dan Wilson:
The article states that the Negro man went aboard the submarine
and found one Negro man and five little brown men who escorted him
through the ship. The whole affair lasted a half hour or perhaps
longer. (This sounds very much like some alien abduction
stories of more recent times). The man was questioned by the State
Highway patrol and later it was found out that the FBI had taken the
Negro farmer to St. Louis for additional questioning.

This article was given to me by UFO researcher Kenny Young a
few years ago. Kenny sent FOIA requests to the FBI to obtain more
information on the whole affair--no luck. Kenny is no longer with
us--sad.

Charleston,
MO, Enterprise Courier, April 9, 1941 (Dan Wilson)
