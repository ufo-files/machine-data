May 7, 1948; Memphis, Tenn. (BBU)

Between 1500 to 1700 hours three witnesses, Miss Jean
Bray, Mr. F. J. Kaiser, and Mrs. F. J. Kaiser, observed 50 to 60 small
shiny objects at high altitude moving faster than any known
aircraft. Some of the objects seemed to zig-zag slightly. Some of the
objects had a silvery tail. This was known as the "Memphis Incident."
