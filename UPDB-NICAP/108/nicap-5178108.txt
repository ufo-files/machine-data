Dan Wilson:
Jan. 9, 1953; Santa Ana, Calif. (BB)
At 7:27 p.m. PST, two observers watched a V-formation of
blue-white lights approach their B-29 air refueling aircraft at a rapid
rate of closure. The lights appeared to hold the same position
momentarily and then made a slight rise and disappear. The observers
were 1st Lt. Lowell D. Brandt, USAF, Aircraft Commander, and 1st Lt.
Charles C. Loveless, USAF, co-pilot. The length of the observation was
approximately 5 seconds.
