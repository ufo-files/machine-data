Animal Effect Cases

Case Directory
Fishing Creek Valley, Pennsylvania
April 1, 1967
Animal Reaction Feature:
Two dogs barked wildly as an orange object approached with 50 feet above the
ground and roughly 150 feet away. The dogs continued barking for the
duration of the 5 minute event, and then became quiet when the object disappeared.

Joan Woodward, Animal Reaction Specialist:
Three children, ages 12, 11, and 7, were playing in a field at 6:30 p.m.
A round orange object with a halo of light alternating between yellow and
blue and antennae on either side approached as described above. For two minutes,
the object emitted a ray of light that hit the ground 10 feet from the children
before it disappeared. A vertical trail was seen by an adult neighbor
after the object disappeared.

No sound was reported. No EM or physiological effects were reported
Source:
