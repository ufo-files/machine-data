Dogs and cats non reactive to quad search lights (IFO).Form
97-AR
Date: Saturday, 18 March 2006

From:
Joan Woodward, Animal Reaction Specialist
Subject: Shreve OH,
November 23, 1987

Cat: 4
To: NICAP

Animal Reaction Feature:

In a suburban area, a man went to call his pets and saw 4
soft lights moving in a pattern.1 He and
his wife watched the lights for 25 minutes.1
They noted that several dogs and cats in the area did not react to the
lights.

The sighting (IFO):

The lights were described as four soft lights moving in a
cloverleaf pattern, always meeting in the middle. The witness estimated their
altitude as 500 feet or less and their distance greater than 500 feet to 1 mile
away.1

No sound was heard.

Jennie Zeidman, MUFON, identified these as a
quad-searchlight display.
