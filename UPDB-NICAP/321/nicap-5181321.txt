9:00 a.m. Two brothers, 8-year-old Andy and 6 year-old Joel Rygh were playingwhen they heard "strange noises followed by a grunting sound and then a whistle."When they looked in the direction of the noises they saw a seven-foot tall manstanding behind a bush.
