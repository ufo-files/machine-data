May 14, 1952; Mayaquez, Puerto Rico (BBU 1213)

Between 1900 and 1930 hours, Mr. R. B. Stipes, ex-Air Force pilot,
Mr. Miguel Garcia-Mendez, lawyer, Mrs Stipes and Mrs. Garcia-Mendez,
saw 2 shining orange spheres: one was stationary, while the other
darted away and back for 30 mins. No sound was heard and
no exhaust was detected.
