Red Egg-Shaped Object Also Tracked On Radar / 5 Photos TakenBrad Sparks:
Dec. 19, 1957; Pepperrell AFB, Newfoundland, Canada
(BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
Dec. 19, 1957; Pepperrell AFB, Newfoundland, Canada
7:02 p.m. local. One red egg-shaped object the size of a
pea held at arm's length was seen visually and picked up
on radar. The object was reportedly stationary but it
did move. There were also 5 photos taken of the object
but were undeveloped for possible instructions. The
length of the observation was 8 minutes.

Fran Ridge:
The Project Record Card says "film taken but no object
appeared on film." The actual report says film was
"undeveloped for possible instructions" and later it was
requested the film be submitted for evaluation. All five
photos were of different exposures. As Dan reported, the
object was said to have been stationary, but the report
says object moved 1-1/2 miles NE at 2310Z and visual
observer contacted radar.
