Naval Research A/c Encounters 6 Objects Nr Cosmic Ray BalloonBrad Sparks:

Feb. 1, 1954; 35 miles SW of Tuscaloosa, Alabama (BBU)
3:15 [2?] p.m. Crew of USN Office of Naval Research aircraft heading E
towards a cosmic-ray balloon at 90,000-100,000 ft and 15-30 miles away
saw 6 objects fly over and around the balloon, hover then vertically
ascend out of sight. (Hynek-CUFOS­ Willy Smith files)

Dan Wilson:
2:00 p.m. or 3:15 p.m. CST. According to the PROJECT 10073
RECORD CARD, photos were taken of these objects. See 6779839 &
6779877. The pilot of the plane indicates the time was 3:15 p.m. CST
and the passenger indicates the time was 2:00 p.m. CST.
