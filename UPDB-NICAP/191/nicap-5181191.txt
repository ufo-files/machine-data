Oct. 31, 1975; 4 miles NW of Loring AFB, ME

According to the documents, at 11:17 p.m. EST, a visual sighting of an
unidentified object
was reported 4 nautical miles NW of Loring AFB, Me. The alert helo was
launched to identify the object but was unable to make contact. The
alert helo was launched again at 0146 hours EST, November 1, 1975, in
response to a slow moving target picked up by RAPCON (Radar Approach
Control). (See NMCC assortment #1,
Frames 16-17 of 40 below).
