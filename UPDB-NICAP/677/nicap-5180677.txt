Pre-dawn. Stewart and Lyle Leppke, doing their early morning farm chores,observed a lighted object in a cattle lot. When they flashed their light on it, theysaw a diminutive figure, less than 2-feet tall, moving about near the object, whichhad small windows on it. (Humcat 1971-3; Source: Humcat, quoting newspapersource).
