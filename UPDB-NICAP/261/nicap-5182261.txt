A motorist stopped at a scenic overlook saw a bright pulsating light. As it nearedhe could see that its light was pulsating every two seconds. He could now see thatthe light was on a disc-shaped object with shiny domed top that was revolving anddark underside. It passed out of sight in about 2-3 minutes.
