(McDonald list) Large Blip on AN/TPS Radar At 1320 MPHBrad
Sparks:
Dec. 15, 1952; Honshu, Japan (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
Dec. 15, 1952; Honshu, Japan (BBU)
At approximately 2054 (8:54 p.m. local time) an
unidentified track appeared on the Early Warning Ground Radar Site #24.
It was a large blip
and estimated to be more than one target. The estimated speed of the
target was
1320 mph. The radar being used was the AN/TPS-1C. The target was seen
on the
first, second and fourth sweeps of the antenna. The antenna speed was 2
rpm. (Dan
Wilson)
