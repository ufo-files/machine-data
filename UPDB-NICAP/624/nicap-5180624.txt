Pilots, FAA, radar confirm UFOsFran
Ridge:
Nov. 26, 1968; Bismarck, ND

Pilots saw two luminous objects join up, hover about 10
seconds, then speed out of sight in seconds. Confirmed by Air Force
radar (Section III, Vol. II, The UFO Evidence).

Dan Wilson:
At 1740 hours local time two round objects
were seen in the sky. The objects traveled
considerably faster than aircraft. One object traveled north and
the other object traveled south. The object traveling south reversed
course and moved to be what appeared to be a short distance from the
other object. The two objects then hovered close together for a few
seconds and then in a split second they disappeared in a northeasterly
direction. Air Force radar at Great Falls, Montana, picked up "foreign
objects" at the time 85 miles NE of Bismarck, according to a newspaper
report. The objects were observed by three control tower personnel at
the Bismarck Airport. These were Jack Wilhelm, Jack Reeves, and John
Fishcer. The objects were also seen by Robert Watts who was flying a
Cessna 150 in the area. Watts had 30 years of experience as a
pilot. I was able to locate 21 BB documents. (See below)
