(McDonald list; FUFOR Index) FPS-6 Blip Travels 100 Miles in 20 SecondsBrad
Sparks:
Nov. 30, 1957; Minot, North Dakota (BBU)
(McDonald list; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:
Nov. 30, 1957; Minot, North Dakota
At 4:20 a.m. local time, a one inch blip on
an FPS-6 radar scope climbed from 5,000 feet to 25,000 feet in 20
seconds. The blip traveled
100 miles in 20 seconds when it faded from the radar. (Dan Wilson)
