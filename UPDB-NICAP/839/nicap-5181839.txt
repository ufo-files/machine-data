A man and a woman saw a silvery object with a dome on top emitting a blue beamof light into a nearby forest. The woman went to investigate, and saw the object,which was about one meter in diameter, at close range. Inside the dome were twovery small creatures with large eyes and frog-like skin. When she started to touchthe object, she was temporarily blinded by a bright light and the craft sped away.
