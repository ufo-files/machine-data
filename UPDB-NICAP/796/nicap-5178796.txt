(McDonald list) GCA Picks Up More Than Seven TargetsBrad
Sparks:
Nov. 6, 1957; Laredo AFB, Texas (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
November 6, 1957; Laredo AFB, Texas (BBU)
Starting at 3:05 p.m. CST, and over a period of 41 minutes,
seven or more objects were observed on a GCA Surveillance scope.
The
objects appeared as large as a T-33 to a C-47 and their speed was
estimated at 3600 mph. The
location of the objects from the observer was 170 to 350 degrees.
