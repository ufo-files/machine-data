Noon.  Prolonged sighting (more than 5 hours) by many witnesses of numerousdiscs, fall of so-called "angel's hair," which was sticky and reportedly causedphysiological effects on some who touched it (burning sensation, itching, nausea).According to reports, dogs and cows in the neighborhood "acted odd." (TennesseeNICAP Subcommittee report; U.F.O. Investigator, Vol. Ill, No. 10, Oct.-Nov.1966, pp. 5-6.)
