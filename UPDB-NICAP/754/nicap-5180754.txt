6:45 p.m. A lady was on her way to Patoka from her home on RR 2, 2 miles SW ofthe city, when she saw a sudden flash in the sky. First expected to be a "fallingstar" she then saw it was moving rapidly in a zig-zag fashion from W-E and sheslowed her car to watch. (SL-62, Page 11)
