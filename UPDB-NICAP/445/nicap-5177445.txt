Radar Targets Over AEC PlantFran
Ridge:
Dec. 14, 1950; Knoxville, Tennessee
4:05 p.m. A group of targets blanketed the radar
scopes in the area directly over the government Atomic Energy
Commission projects at Oak Ridge. These objects could not be
identified from the radar image and a perfect fighter interception met
with negative results.The FBI document found below, and the
transcript by Larry Fawcett and Barry Greenwood found in Clear
Intent (page 173), show that they were not identified and scope
photos were taken. However,
these radar tracks were not listed among the Blue Book Unknowns.
