May 5, 1949; Ft. Bliss, TX (BBU 376)
11:40 a.m. Army officers Maj. Day [May?], Maj. Olhausen, Capt. Vaughn
saw 2 oblong white discs, flying at about 200-250 mph, make a shallow
turn. Three-inch object passed through field of fire on Waco #4 firing
range. (Berliner)
==============================================================================

Three Army officers:
Major Charles D. May,
Major Jones Olhausen,
Capt. Molloy C. Vaughn,
Observed an oblong white object 1000 feet above the
ground flying west to east. Apparent size of object was that of a
three inch disc held at arm's length.
Time in sight: 30 to 50 seconds
Speed: 200-250 MPH
Luminosity: Highly reflective
