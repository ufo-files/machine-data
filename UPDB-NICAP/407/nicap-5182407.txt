Dogs are unaffected by fly over of three amber lights in formationGroup
/Category 4
Animal Effect Cases

Case Directory
Baton Rouge, Louisiana
April 8, 1989

Animal Reaction Feature:
A woman, approximately 40, advertising technician and private pilot
with an interest in psychic events, took her dog and cat outdoors at
5:30 am where she saw 3 amber lights fly by, passing above 1600 foot
clouds. The witness estimated they were more than a mile
away. The dog and cat appeared unaffected.

Joan Woodward, Animal Reaction Specialist:
The witness took her pets outdoors before leaving for work on a partly
cloudy morning in a suburban setting with woods in the area.
Coming from the WNW, the amber lights (yellow orange) were moving
abreast, with the lead light higher than the others. They passed
above1600 foot clouds and maintained the same formation as they flew a
110-degree arc in about a minute until disappearing behind trees to the
south. At their nearest, she estimated their elevation at 25
degrees and their altitude unknown.

The witness seemed to have an impression that she was seeing 3 separate
glowing amber objects maintaining an unchanging formation as opposed to
3 lights on one object.

No sound, no EM effects, and no physiological effects were reported.
