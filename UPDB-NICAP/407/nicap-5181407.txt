Mark Marston, age 11, was looking for bird's nests in a hedge when "a red glowappeared in the sky 50 yards away." A few moments later a figure appeared anddrifted through a closed gate at the other end of the field. It was dressed in a silversuit, like a diver, with a large helmet and a square, featureless face.
