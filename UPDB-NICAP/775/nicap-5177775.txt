Object "Rolling" Between Two FightersBrad Sparks:
July 15 [16?], 1952; 20 miles S of McChord AFB, Wash (BBU)
12:50 [7:50?] a.m. (PDT). USAF pilots of 2 fighters from 318th FI Sq
saw a strange object with red and green lights slowly rolling between
the aircraft. (Project 1947)

Dan Wilson:
July 14, 1952; 20 miles S of McChord AFB, Washington (BBU)
Time of sighting is given as 150750Z (July 14 at 11:50 p.m. PST). Two
pilots in F-94's observed an object that might have been silver-colored
flying at very high speed between the two aircraft. There is no
record of other aircraft in the area at the same time.
