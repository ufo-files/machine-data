Dan Wilson:
Feb. 22, 1953; Erie, Pennsylvania
At 3:30 p.m.EST, Claire Range on duty station with GOC at Lake
Erie reported to the Buffalo Filter Center the observation of a
silvery chrome object saucer like in shape 100 to 150 feet in
diameter about 1 and 1/4 miles out over Lake Erie going west. No
propulsion system observed and no sound was observed. The object
was reflecting light brightly. Claire Range was a former Navy gunner.
