12:30 a.m. A man was training his German shepherd dog in a pine grove when theanimal suddenly became agitated and sat next to him. He then saw hovering 10meters from the ground a dark metallic domed object that was emitting a beepingsound. The witness suffered from severe headaches soon after the incident, and hisdog died with no obvious cause of death in August of the same year.
