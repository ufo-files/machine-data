T-33 Crew Encounters LightBrad Sparks:
July 26, 1952; Plainview, Texas (BBU)
7:17 p.m. USAF pilot and copilot of T-33 saw a stationary object move
in a slight descent changing color from white to blue. (Project 1947)

Dan Wilson:
July 26, 1952; Plainview, Texas
7:17 p.m. The observers in a T-33 were Maj. Eldon A. Klapal and 1st Lt.
Jack Chadurgian on a flight from Kelly AFB to Denver, Colorado. The
object illuminated a brilliant white light while hovering and when
moving flickered orange and/or bluish color.
