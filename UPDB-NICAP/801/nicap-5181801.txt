Ernaldo Camusoni, age 49, was clearing an overgrown field at the entrance to afarm in Serodino late in the evening when everything became illuminated behindhim, turning the darkness into day. When he turned around he saw an approachingobject land 40 meters away from him. As he approached the object, he saw astrange luminous figure emerge from the rear of the craft. The whole encounterlasted five minutes. Ground traces were found the next day.
