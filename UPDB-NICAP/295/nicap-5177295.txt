Dan Wilson:
Sept. 9, 1949; Goose Bay, Labrador, Canada
At 9:56 p.m. (AST) The pilot of an NBC aircraft observed a
large egg-shaped object traveling an estimated 400 mph at an altitude
of approximately 9000 feet. The object was yellow and red in
color and in a slightly curved descent. The object was about
the size of a railroad oil tank car.
