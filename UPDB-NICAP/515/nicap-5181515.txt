A domed disc illuminated the area of Saint Ciers du Taillon like day, then flewaway. It came back again the same site 20 minutes later. That same night ablindingly bright light came up close to a French Navy Nord-262 transport planewith three crew members aboard and followed it, circling it for five minutes.
