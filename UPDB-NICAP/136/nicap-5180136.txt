9:30-10:00 p.m. PST. A woman and her daughter, age 16, saw a large amber-colored light approaching their car at an altitude of 60-80 feet. As it moved closer,the object was seen to be round with windows on top, and light coming from abottom bulge.  (Ricks letter in NICAP files; U.F.O. Investigator, Vol. IV, No. 1,May-June 1967, p. 1.)
