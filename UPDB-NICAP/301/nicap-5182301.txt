1:00 AM. Mr. Jonsson was driving home when he saw a disc-shaped object 80meters across and some 5-10 meters above the road ahead of him. He got to hishome, got his camera, and went back to the site of his encounter. His car stalled,and he recalled seeing several beings who attempted to abduct him. He had nearlytwo hours of missing time.
