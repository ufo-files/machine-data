10:30 PM. 5-1/2 mins duration. Girl Scout supervisor at camp headquarterssuddenly hears his dog barking in a warning manner. The witness moves to anunobstructed view of the pool and notices the pool lights are not on. Instead,directly above the pool, and higher than the regular lights, is a brilliant light.Suddenly the lights go out. He hears a whirring noise that rises in pitch as itapparently rises into the sky. When he turns on the lights he noticed the pump isnot running and the water level is down three feet. (IUR-3,6)
