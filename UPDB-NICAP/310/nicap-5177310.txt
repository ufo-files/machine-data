by the 17th District Office of Special Investigations, Kirtland AFB,
New Mexico.

January 7, 1950, Corona, NM. OSI Case 161
BB 206. S of Corona, New Mexico. 10:15 p.m. Holloman AFB
Asst. Maintenance Officer Risley while driving saw a yellowish-white
ball of light at about 45° elevation descending at a 60° angle,
changing color to orange with trailing flame, to just above a mountain
range where it leveled off becoming bright blue-green traveling 10°
E [?] until it dropped behind the mountain. (FOIA; FUFOR Index)
