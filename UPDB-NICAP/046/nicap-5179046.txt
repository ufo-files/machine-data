Radar Target Illuminated By Rotating BeaconBrad
Sparks:

Sept. 15, 1959; Kadena AFB, Okinawa (BBU)
7 a.m.-1 p.m. 3 USAF personnel radar tracked multiple green
objects, and sighted 4-5 mins a silvery object tilted downwards visible
only when lit by a rotating beacon light and which moved slowly on a
straight path in the direction of the winds. (CUFOS re-eval.; Jan
Aldrich)
