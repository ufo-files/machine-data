Harmond Field PhotoBrad Sparks:
July 10, 1947; Harmon Field, Newfoundland (BBU)
Bet.
3 and 5 p.m. [or 5:30 p.m. (ADT)].

Three ground crewmen, A. R. Leidy,
J. N. Mehrman, and J. E. Woodruff,
of Pan American Airways, briefly saw a translucent
disc or silvery wheel-shaped object the size of a C-54
transport fly very fast at 10,000 ft, leaving a dark
bluish-black trail, then ascend and cut a path through
the clouds. Photo. (Berliner; FOIA)

Fran Ridge:
Leidy and Mehrman saw the trail which remained in the
sky for about an hour and was photographed by the other
PAA employee, Woodruff. The resulting photographs
support Mr. Woodruff's observation as far as the sky
cleavage is concerned.
