In the early morning hours a married couple in Bissoe Truro, Cornwall County,England were awakened to find their bedroom lit up brightly. One of the witnesseslooked out the window and was amazed to see a bright hovering oval-shaped craft.Inside could be seen two human-looking men with long wavy blond hair.
