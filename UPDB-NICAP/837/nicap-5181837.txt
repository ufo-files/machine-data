Sr. A. Ferreira was returning home from his job as a security guard when ametallic oval-shaped craft landed on three legs near his home. Three humanoidscame out of the craft and paralyzed him with red beams of light, from boxes thatthey carried on their chests. These humanoid beings also carried boxes on theirbacks and had an insignia on their chests. He was floated onboard the craftalongside and once inside he believed he passed out.
