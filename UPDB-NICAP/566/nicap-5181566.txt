The witness was walking home alone when he observed a very bright object in thesky that began to descend at a high speed. The object came low over a nearbyfenced field, and he could now see that it was a shiny circular metallic craft withina fiery ball of flames. The area around the witness seemed to darken, and he feltcompelled to walk towards the fence. He was quickly grabbed by two shorthumanoids who wore shiny white tight fitting suits.
