(McDonald list) F-94 Radar/Visual of Rotating UFOBrad Sparks:
Jan. 9,
1953; Misawa AFB, Japan
6:50 p.m. F-94 flying 220 knots (~250 mph) at 35,000
ft heading 3601 Mag (about N) tracked object at 24,000
yards (~14 mi) 201 to the left, 101 below aircraft
horizon [thus UFO at about 22,500 ft].  Pilot
sighted object visually (after about 1 min) [at about
3351 Mag], seen as light changing color from red to
white to green, with no apparent size, shape,
aerodynamic features, means of propulsion, exhaust or
trail, or apparent motion.  Pilot turned F-94 to
head straight towards object at 3351 Mag maintaining
35,000 ft at all times, but object continued to remain
visual position 201 left of F-94 heading [thus ~3151
Mag and impossible to be a motionless star; as well as
appearing below earth horizon].  F-94 radar
lockon at 5,000 yards (3 mi).  F-94 overtook and
flew over object in 2 mins [indicating UFO traveling
about 150 mph towards F-94 on intersecting path] with
UFO radar tracked at range 200 yards (600 ft) at 201
left and 401 below horizon [thus UFO at about 34,600
ft, having climbed about 6,000 ft/min or 6x faster
than standard weather balloon, contrary to BB1s
1possible1 balloon explanation]. The F-94 reported
radar interference with radar otherwise functioning
normally.  (Sparks; BB files;  NICAP
website;  McDonald list) 2 mins
