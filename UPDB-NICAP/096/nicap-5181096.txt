5:00 a.m. Awakened by a brilliant red light shining in her bedroom window,Marjorie Nighbert scurried  to  the  window thinking one of her neighbor's houseswas on fire. It wasn't a fire, but a large red blinking ball of light, larger than a"washtub", moving slowly from the north in a southeasterly direction. Shedescribed the object as tadpole in shape.
