Auto-Sized Cylinder Tries To Intercept B-29?Fran Ridge:
This Blue Book case (with fine-tuned corrections)
illustrates the need to research and log other very brief, meteorlike
incidents as we have been doing, since this UFO might have normally
been written off.

Brad Sparks:
Feb. 23 [24?], Sinuiju [Antung?], North Korea (BBU 1061)
10:15 [11:15?] p.m. USAF 345th Bomber Sq Captain/B-29
navigator saw a bluish cylinder, 3x long as wide, with a tail and rapid
pulsations, come in high and fast, make several turns and level out
under B-29 which was evading mild antiaircraft fire. (Berliner; FUFOR
Index)

Dan Wilson:
Feb. 24, 1952; Antung, Korea,
(BBU 1061)
11:15 p.m. local. After a leaflet mission the navigator of a B-29
aircraft
observed an object approaching the aircraft at high velocity. The
object appeared to round it shape at first. As it came closer the shape
appeared to be cylindrical and the size of an automobile. The object
continued to close in and turned to left as though making a correction
to intercept the B-29. The object appeared to come within 3000 feet of
the aircraft and then headed down below the B-29. The B-29 was flying
at 22,000 feet at a speed of 200 knots.
