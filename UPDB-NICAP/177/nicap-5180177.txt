7:00 p.m. LT. Seven lenticular (disc-shaped) black objects, aligned diagonally,each with a diameter estimated as 50 feet or more, were seen above a grove oftrees about 2 km away. Each had a "smoky halo" around it. The objects suddenlyvanished, then reappeared and vanished again, leaving traces in the forest.Truncated light beams ("solid light") were emitted from three of the objects, onewhite and two greenish. (Haines, 1994, pp. 91-92, from UNICAT catalogue #403.)
