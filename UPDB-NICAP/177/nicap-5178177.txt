F-94 Encounters Maneuvering LightBrad Sparks:
April 8, 1953; Fukuoka, Japan (BBU 2535)
7:55 p.m. 1st Lt. D. J. Pichon, pilot of USAF F-94B jet interceptor,
saw a bright blue light descend, accelerate, fly parallel to the F-94,
increase its speed and blink out. (Berliner; FUFOR Index)
