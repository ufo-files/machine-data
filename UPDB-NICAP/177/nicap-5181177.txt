4:30 p.m. A couple living on a ranch near Priest Butte, Montana, reported that theyhad seen a UFO about half a mile from their home, on the east slope of PriestButte. Using binoculars, they described it as being egg-shaped, with the large endof the craft on the ground. (Reference: Lawrence Fawcett and Barry J. Greenwood,Clear Intent, page 34)
