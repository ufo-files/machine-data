Animal Reaction Feature:

Around midnight, a woman and her son (16) were at home on their
farm.1 Their dog started barking and a
horse in a corral began to whinny and move around its enclosure in an agitated
manner.1 The witnesses thought a stray
animal had come into the area.

The sighting:

The 16-year-old looked out his window and saw 4 bright
lights in a row to the NW.1 The lights
slowly increased in brilliance, and then began to wane until attaining an
orange-red hue before extinguishing. They were estimated to be 90 meters (295
feet) away to the NW and on or near the ground.

Alerted by her son, the mother looked out the front door and
saw the paddock across the road was brightly illuminated by a monochromatic
white light source.1 She went outdoors
and saw a bright rectangular object that appeared to have four square windows
along its side.1 The structure appeared
to move slowly over and around the crest of a hill.1 The event ended with both mother and son seeing a glow that
eventually faded.

Both witnesses reported the lights were so bright that they
had after-image effects.1 There is a
reference to 1noise1 without further explanation [I assume this means sound
associated with the object--jw].1 No EM
effects were reported.1 Possible traces
were found on the ground.

Noise and animal reactions were reported from neighboring
properties and independent sightings by factory workers were reported, but no
details are given.
