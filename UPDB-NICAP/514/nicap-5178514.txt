Dan Wilson:
July 26, 1955; Kansas City, Kansas (BBU)
At 3:34 p.m. CST, a round, white object leaving no
contrails or exhaust was seen by the pilot of a B-47 jet
bomber. The object was estimated to be at an altitude of
approximately 66,000 feet heading SW at the an estimated
speed of 1,300 knots.
