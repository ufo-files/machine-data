Fran Ridge:
Sept. 1944; Oak Ridge, Tennessee
On the banks of the Clinch River, a huge, black,
windowless building was being constructed. In the
middle of Sept 1944, this structure, a gaseous
diffusion plant, began operation. This unusual
factory was designed to prepare quantities of
fissionable material for the US' most important
secret weapon, the A-bomb. Shortly after the plant
began operations, the area had a very odd visitor. A
strange, metallic looking, tube-like object was
spotted hovering over the road near the Oak Ridge
plant. The object moved away as a crowd started to
gather. The sighting was reported to the FBI (20).
