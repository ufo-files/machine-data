feet from the barn, and then moved closer to the ground hovering over a
disinfecting bath used for the cattle.1

Tonna and Topo walked a few feet toward the object, which
was now glowing bright orange.1 It

ground.1 It was shining with a bright

Sound was reported but not described and its duration is

EM effects were reported (generator).1 The generator was reported to restart after

Physiological effects were reported: Tonna1s feeling of

eyes.

Topo reportedly did not eat or drink after the incident, and

He was found dead 2 or 3 days later in the spot where he had sat looking
up at the object.
