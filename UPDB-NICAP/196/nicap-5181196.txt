40' Object Causes Van to Hit DitchDon Ledger:
Nov. 5, 1975; Redwater, Alta, Canada
12:30 AM. Large black object appeared to be revolving slowly
and the observer had to drive his van into a ditch to avoid hitting it.
When observer got out of his van he heard a rushing sound and saw the
object disappear. Described as approximately 40 feet long and in the
shape of a circle and was revolving in a circular motion. 3 minute
duration. (RCMP)
