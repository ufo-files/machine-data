(McDonald list) AN/MPS-7 Tracks Object at 100,000'Brad
Sparks:
May 29, 1957; Houma, Louisiana (BBU)
3:03 p.m. (CST). (McDonald list) 14 mins

Dan Wilson:
May 29, 1957; Houma, Louisiana (BBU)
At 3:03 p.m. CST, a normal looking radar return was picked up
on the AN/MPS-7 radar scope. The track was averaging 2250 knots and
occasionally 3530(?) knots at an estimated altitude of 100,000 feet.
The description of
the flight path was, east southeast, and then southeast, and then east,
and then
northeast. The length of observation was 14 minutes before fading from
the
radar.
