Dan Wilson:

March 13, 1951; McClellan AFB, Sacramento, Calif.
(BBU 907)

1520 hours (3:20 p.m.) PST. 1st Lt. Buel J.
Hastie and
Mrs. Rafferty while driving in a car sighted an
object at an estimated
2000 foot in altitude hovering near Sacramento. The
object was
approximately 200 feet in diameter, 90 feet
deep, cylindrical in
shape with twin tails attached. The object turned in
a northerly
direction and appeared to move at incredible speed
and disappeared.
Duration of the sighting was approximately 2
minutes.
