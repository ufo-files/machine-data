July 22, 1952. MacDill AFB, Florida (BBU)
10:30 p.m. (EST). MacDill AFB air traffic
control tower operator sighted for 1 hour a
red-greenish-blue object to the WSW at about 451
elevation with 2 other [similar?] objects to the N
of it, smaller and/or farther away and lower in
elevation angle (?). Tower operator sighted
another object to the SSE about 301 elevation at
11:30 p.m. MacDill AFB Detachment 21, 3944th Radar
Bomb-scoring Sq (RBS), radar tracked object at 12:03
a.m. (July 23) at azimuth 1601 range 65,000 yards
(37 mi) at 41,200 ft altitude on a heading of 3101
True [almost directly towards MacDill AFB] speed 462
knots [532 mph]. Tampa Radar Bomb-scoring Sq
also tracked object at 1601 azimuth [about SSE]
altitude 41,000 ft [reportedly, in 1998 account,
Navy and CAA radars also tracked object]. At
12:08 a.m. USAF pilot and copilot of B-29 bomber
with 364th Bomb Sq on landing approach were vectored
by MacDill tower operator to investigate, saw high
speed object at 40,000 ft heading towards MacDill
AFB on a heading of 3081 traveling faster than the
B-29. 4 airmen at MacDill AFB radar site
sighted object as it passed close [nearly
overhead?]. (According to 1998 report of B-29 pilot,
an AF Lt Col, they were flying at 20,000 ft; the
B-29 fire control radar locked on to object at
40,000 ft and prepared to fire when UFO changed
course and disappeared at 4,000 knots speed.)
MacDill RBS lost object on radar at range 145,000
yards [82 mi] azimuth 3101 (about NW) [at about
12:15 a.m.]. Civilians near base sighted
object(s) visually. (Sparks; Robert
Klinn; Project 1947; McDonald list; BB files;
NICAP; NUFORC) 1-3/4 hrs duration, 10+
witnesses, RV case.
