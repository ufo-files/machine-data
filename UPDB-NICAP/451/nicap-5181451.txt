Date:1 April 11, 2006

From:1 Joan Woodward,
Animal Reaction Specialist

Subject:1 Emden MO,
July 13, 1973

Category: 4

To:1 Francis Ridge,
NICAP Site Coordinator

Animal Reaction Feature:

A dog that was normally outdoors at night and that normally
barked at and chased any animal that came near the house, began barking at 3:30
AM.1 This barking was unusual, described
as a 1scared1 bark, causing the witness to get up and see what was happening.

The dog was barking at a glowing object and would not leave
the porch.1 Normally in that situation,
the dog would take off after whatever had attracted its attention.1 The witness stated that this night the dog
was clearly afraid and continued barking from the porch and would not leave the
porch.

The witness also noted he heard cattle bellowing on a hill,
and when the light moved off, he heard neighbor1s dogs start barking.1 [No information was given on the location of
the cattle or of the neighbor1s house relative to witness and the unknown
light.1jw]

The Sighting:

The object was described as egg shaped or oblong, about the
size of an average car.1 It had a
3-dimensional appearance, 1..like you could look
through it, or right into it.11 The
outside was orange and toward the center it was brighter and a slightly different
color.1 The colors were described as
similar to setting sun but not dark enough to be considered red.

The night was very quiet with no wind.1 The witness made an effort to hear any sound
from the object but heard nothing.1 He
noted that even when the object started moving, he still could hear
nothing.1

The object, judging by a tree in front of it, was estimated
to be 20 feet or less from the ground and was in the area of a clearing.1 It remained still and in view for about 10
minutes.1 Then1 the witness1s wife, watching from
indoors while he watched from the porch, turned on house lights.1 At that point, the object flickered slightly
and started moving away slowly and steadily, maintaining its low altitude.1 It speed was estimated as 5 mph and it was moving
in a northwest direction..11 The object was lost to sight after after 20 seconds as it became obscured by trees.1 The witness heard the neighbor1s dogs start
barking about 3 minutes later.

[The only direction indicated was that the glowing object disappeared
moving to the NW.1 Using YourSky (fourmilab.ch/yoursky/)
to see if any bright objects were near the horizon at the time of the sighting,
I found Mars was roughly 351 in the SE sky, unlikely a factor.1 However, the 97% illuminated moon was due to set1 4:05 AM (verified
with USNO) and would have been very near the horizon SW horizon. I cannot say
the setting moon was or was not involved in this sighting without directions
and knowledge of the topography.1 The
witness reports the dog was reacting in the direction of the glowing object,
suggesting something was happening other than the moon
setting.1 However, it is always possible
that the dog was reacting to something other than what the witness was
watching.1jw]

SKYLOOK, No. 69, August 1973, page 13:

Missouri Farmer
and Wife See Large UFO1 -1 A UFO was reported by Terry Stice on his farm east of Emden, Mo.,
at 3:30 a.m. Friday, July 13.1 Stice said he and his wife, Linda, were awakened by the
barking of their dog.1 When Stice went outside to see what was wrong, he saw an oblong
object about the size of a car, moving in a northwest direction about 100 feet
from the ground.1 He said it gave off a
reddish-orange glow and stayed in the sky about ten minutes before disappearing
in the west.1 He then heard the
neighbors1 dogs barking down the road.1
He reported the incident to FAA officials at Baldwin Field near Quincy.

1111 After reading the
report in the Quincy 1Herald-Whig1, Joe Brill of
the SKYLOOK staff went to the Emden
home to interview the couple and learned they left Friday for California.1
He will see them on their return, and we hope to have more details on
this sighting in a later issue of SKYLOOK.

SKYLOOK, No. 70, September 1973, page 14-15:

More on Emden,
Mo. Sighting of July 13 1 We had a brief
account of a UFO sighting at Emden,
Mo. July 13 (See page 13 of August SKYLOOK)
and stated that Joseph Brill, of our staff, would interview the witnesses on
their return from California.1 We now have Mr. Bills report.

1111 The sighting took
place at 3:30 AM and lasted about ten minutes.1
We quote Mr. Stice when he was asked how he
happened to observe the object.1 1Well,
we were sleeping at the time.1 Our dog
started barking.1 Of course, he1ll bark
every night if an animal comes up close to the house.1 But he had a strange bark, just a scared
bark.1 That1s the main reason I got up to
see what was going on.1 He wouldn1t leave
the porch area, and he kept barking at that light.1 He just stood there and barked.1 Normally, if there1s something out there,
he1ll just take off after it.1 But he was
scared and you could tell it.1 The dog is
always left outside of the house at night.1
I didn1t go any farther than the porch and my wife stuck her head out of
the door just once.1 Other than that she
watched from behind the door.1

1111 Asked to describe
what they had seen, Mr. Stice said: 1After the dog
woke us up, we went to the front door and opened it.1 We looked out and didn1t see anything at the
time.1 I went back to bed myself.1 My wife saw the light over there.1 Then I got up and as soon as I saw it, I just
knew what it was.1 There wasn1t any doubt
in my mind.1 There wasn1t any noise, and
there wasn1t any wind, just peaceful, quiet.1
It was in view for a long time.1
I1d say around ten minutes.1 It
was about the size of the average car.1
It wasn1t round and it was really oval.1
It was kind of egg shaped or kind of oblong in shape.1 When it left, it moved in a northwest
direction and approximately three minutes or so later, our neighbor1s dogs
started barking.1 I immediately
telephoned Baldwin Airport in Quincy,
but they didn1t seem as if they were in the least interested.1 They suggested that I call Scott Air Force
Base.

1111 1As for the

that was wrong.1 I estimated this because
it was like 20 feet or less above the ground.1
I estimated this because it was about half the height of a tree from the
ground.1 It was located behind a tree and
this made it good to judge how close it was to the ground.1 There is a clearing where it was setting.

1111 1The color of
this thing was hard to describe.1 It
wasn1t quite the color of the sun as it goes down and yet it wasn1t dark enough
to be red.1 It seemed to have a different
color toward the center.1 It seemed to be
more of an orange around the outside of it.1
It had a three dimensional look to it.1
It was brighter on the inside than it was on the outside.1

1111 Asked how the
object appeared as it left the area, Stice said:
1That was really strange.1 When it left,
it looked like it just went straight without raising or without lowering.1 It just seemed to go straight, well, just a

through the trees to the northwest.1
It flew at the same level or near to it as it had been all the time I
watched it.1 It didn1t move faster than
five miles per hour.

1111 1It left when my
wife began turning on the lights inside the house.1 I was standing on the porch and she was

did seem to flicker or maybe flutter a little when it began to move off.1 Then, real slow it started moving out.1 I kept listening close for any sound, but
there wasn1t any sound at all.1 It moved

is idling and then you step on the gas.1

1111 He also added
that 1the cows were bellowing on the hill1 along with the dog barking.1 The night was calm with clear skies and a
