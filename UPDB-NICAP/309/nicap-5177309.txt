Dan Wilson:
Jan. 6, 1950; Nr. Howard, Kansas (BBU)
2:10 p.m. CST Three USAF officers (two pilots), Capt. Robert E.
Zimmerman, Capt. Frederick E. Howard, and 1st Lt. Harold G.
Simpson, flying in a C-47
aircraft at 5500 feet observed a bright silver football-shaped object
30 to 60 foot long, flying on a course of 90-100 degrees magnetic on a
straight and even flight at approximately 2000 feet altitude. The speed
of the object was estimated at 300-500 mph. The length of the
observation was less than two minutes.
