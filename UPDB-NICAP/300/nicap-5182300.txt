9:00 PM. Mine men working on an oil platform observed a bright orange objectdescending from the sky at a high rate of speed. When at an estimated altitude of10,000 feet and about five miles distant, the object came to an instant stop. Thebright orange glow faded and the crew observed an object with a bright chromedome-shaped top and dozens of flashing lights on the underside.
