Camillo. (McDonald list; FUFOR Index) Object Tracked At 54,000 FeetBrad
Sparks:
Aug.
20, 1956; North Bend, Oregon (BBU)
10:15 p.m. (PST). Camillo. (McDonald list; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:
August.
20, 1956; North Bend, Oregon (BBU)
10:15 p.m. PST. An
object was initially
sighted on height finder radar at 320 degrees
azimuth at 54,000 feet
altitude at 60 miles range. The object
descended to 50,000 feet and moved
to 32 degrees azimuth. The radar station contacted the radar station at
Nasselle, Washington and requested that
they try and get a height measurement on their radar. They were able to pick up
the object and verify the original radar reading. After two hours the target
faded from the radar scope.
