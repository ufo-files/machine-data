(McDonald list) FPS-10 Picks Up Target Large As Three B-47'sBrad
Sparks:
Dec. 11, 1957; Lake City AFS, Tenn. (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
Dec. 11, 1957; Lake City AFS, Tennessee
At 7:07 p.m. EST, an AN/FPS-10 radar set picked up object
about the size of three B-47 a/c in close formation. The speed of the
object
was estimated at 90 knots. When first detected the object was at
225 degrees
at 13,000 feet in altitude. When last detected the object had descended
to
11,000 feet at 205 degrees. The length of the observation was 41
minutes.
