2:00 AM. A woman was awakened by a loud engine type noise coming fromoutside, she ran out and watched a round object hovering nearby and emittingmulti-colored beams of light. Three human like beings cold be clearly seen inside,one them glanced briefly at the witness then the object rose and disappeared. Thenext day two dead bloodless horses were found on the field where the object hadhovered.
