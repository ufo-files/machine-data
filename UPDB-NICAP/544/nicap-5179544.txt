11-12:30 a.m. An object with a row of bright body lights was observed to hover,and to move up and down, while emitting a blinding light and making a sound likebees humming. After a long period of time, the lights brightened and the objectsuddenly sped away (luminosity/speed correlation). (Flying Saucer Review, May-June 1966, p. 32; Newcastle Morning Herald, Jan. 24, 1966.)
