10:45 PM. Mrs. Fern Frey (name changed) was lying in bed with her 11-year-olddaughter who was asleep. She cautiously peeked through the curtain and watchedin bewilderment as the green light retreated, "just like a liquid being drawn upthrough a straw." In a matter of seconds the light was "siphoned" up into a lowhovering object that was shaped like an inverted saucer a couple of hundred feetaway. She guessed it was about 30' in diameter and was metallic. (MUJ-122,9)
