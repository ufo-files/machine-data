7:30-7:45 p.m. EST. Fifteen minutes later 5 miles south of Danvers, a series ofsightings was reported by independent witnesses. An oval object with dome ontop, and red, green, blue, and white flashing body lights, was seen flying incircular and zig-zag patterns. The object suddenly darted away at high speed anddisappeared. One UFO hovered over a school, then moved away rapidly with upand down oscillatory motion. (Fowler 1974, p. 338.)
