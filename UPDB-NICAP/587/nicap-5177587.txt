F-94 Crew Made Two Sightings / Gnd Radar TrackBrad Sparks:
March 22, 1952; 20 miles S. of Yakima, Washington
(BBU 1076)
6:05 p.m.local time. USAF pilot and radar operator
of F-94 jet interceptor made 2 sightings of a
stationary red fireball that increased in brightness
then faded over 45 secs. Note: Project Blue Book
Status Report #7 (May 31, 1952) says target was also
tracked by ground radar at 78 knots (90 mph) at 22,500
ft and 25,000 ft altitude. (Berliner)

Dan Wilson:
According to the PROJECT 10073 RECORD CARD the
sighting began at 6:05 p.m. local time.
