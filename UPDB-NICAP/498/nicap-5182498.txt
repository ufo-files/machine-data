Dogs hid as boomerang passedForm
97-AR
Date: Sat, 01 Oct 2005

From: Joan Woodward, Animal Reaction Specialist
Subject: Akron,
Ohio, July 25, 1996

Cat:
4
To: NICAP

Animal Reaction
Feature:

Two dogs were observed to be fearful and to have hidden and
remained silent as a large boomerang passed over the house at low altitude with
a humming sound.

The sighting:

This sighting occurred in a city environment at 5 a.m. with
the sky clear.11 A man, 59, HS education
and employment not indicated, his wife, 53, homemaker, and 3 children saw lights
out their window.111 They saw an
airplane heading toward an airport and they also saw a second object that was a
huge, glowing silver boomerang-shaped object.1
It was estimated as 300 feet wide with about 6 1windows1 on each
side.1 The report indicates a white
light and the emission of a beam without further description.1

The boomerang passed over the house at an estimated altitude
of 500 feet or less,1 moving very
slowly.1 Duration of the sighting
estimated at 17 minutes as the object moved west to east.11 At one point, one of the witnesses saw a
small object that merged with the large one.

The source of this report is apparently an audio tape,
drawing and report sent to MUFON.1 No
field investigator report was included.

No EM effects or physiological effects were reported.
