Blue-White Flame Approaches C-47Dan Wilson:
March 23, 1950; 85 miles NW of Jacksonville, Florida (BB)
12:30 a.m. EST. A USAF pilot, Lt. Hahn, was on a flight from
Jacksonville, Florida, to Robins AFB in a C-47 aircraft. He saw a blue
white flame about 50 to 75 feet long
coming at him from 11 o'clock at very high speed. T/Sgt. Young, a
passenger on the aircraft, also saw the blue white flame approach the
C-47. (Probable meteor)
