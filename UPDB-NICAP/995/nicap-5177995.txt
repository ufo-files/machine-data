RV: Navy Patrol Plane Crew / Five UFOs Picked Up On RadarUpdated: April 15, 2010
Dan Wilson:
6:22 PM. The crew of a U.S. Navy P2V Neptune patrol plane, flying
near Portland, Maine sighted a group of five unidentified lights and
picked up a long thin blip on radar. Investigators considered whether
the crew had sighted USAF KC-97 aircraft involved in a refueling
operation, but decided that this was unlikely. The sighting lasted 20
minutes. (Source: Don Berliner, Project Bluebook UFO Unknowns)
