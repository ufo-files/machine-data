May 29, 1952, 1900 to 1914 hours CST, San Antonio, Texas

USAF Major D.W. Feuerstein, pilot at Randolph Field,
Texas, standing on his front porch observed a small flat intensely
bright object in the sky 25 to 30 degrees above the earth's surface.

Object traveled from left to right. Object turned to a vertical
position for eight minutes and then slowly tilted to the horizontal
position.
