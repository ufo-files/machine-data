T-6G Crew Encounters Hovering Metallic-Looking ObjectBrad Sparks:

Dan Wilson:
May 14, 1952; George AFB, California
At 1:05 p.m. PST, a round silver metallic looking object
was
observed by a pilot and an observer in a T-6G aircraft flying 150 mph
at 10,000 feet. The object was at an estimated altitude of 40,000 feet
and was motionless at approximately 20 miles east of Edwards AFB . An
F-86 was sent aloft to investigate the object. The length of the
observation was 25 minutes.
