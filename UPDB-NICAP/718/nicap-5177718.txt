B-29 Encounters Flat Object With Pointed FrontBrad
Sparks:
June 21, 1952; Kelly AFB, Texas (BBU 1319)
12:30 p.m. T/Sgt. Howard Davis, flight engineer of B-29 bomber at 8,000
ft altitude, saw a flat object with a sharply pointed front and rounded
rear, white with a dark blue center and red rim, trailing sparks as it
dove past the B-29 at a distance of 500 ft, in 1 sec. (Berliner)
