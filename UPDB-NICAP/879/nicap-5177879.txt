Clusters of Small Targets & One Large TargetBrad
Sparks:
July
29, 1952; Osceola, Wisc. (BBU 1731)
1:30 a.m. Radar operators on ground and pilot of F-5l Mustang in
flight. Several clusters of up to 10 small radar targets and one large
target. Small targets moved from SW to E at 50-60 knots (60-70 mph),
following each other. Large target moved at 600 knots (700 mph). Pilot
confirmed one target. 1 hr. Summary says photos were taken. (Berliner)
