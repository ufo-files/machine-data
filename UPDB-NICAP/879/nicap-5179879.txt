This is the only surviving photo of the funnel-shaped flying object that paced theFawcett Airlines DC-4 passenger liner in flight from Piura to Lima on February 2,1967, at 06:30 P.M. One passenger, who insisted on remaining anonymous,succeeded in saving his film from which this single photograph was made.
