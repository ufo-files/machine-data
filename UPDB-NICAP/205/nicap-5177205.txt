AFOSI Case 36: March 8, 1949; Los Alamos, NM.
Los Alamos Guard Station # 106 reports luminous object
slower that meteor, March 8, 1949.

Intense white light, eliptical, pointed at ends, similar
to fuselage of ME 109.
Flew south to north, descending at 45 degree angle, slower
that twin engine plane.
Central part seemed to be a solid silver colored fuselage,
with a flame or glow surrounding it.

Observer: Inspector W.E. Patterson, Atomic Security
Service (AESS), Los Alamos, New Mexico.
