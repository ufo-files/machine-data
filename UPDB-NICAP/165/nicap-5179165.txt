Oval Tracked By AN/FPS-6ADan
Wilson:
May 23, 1961; Bronson and Cross City, Florida
At 5:05 a.m. EDT, a round or oval-shaped object with a green
halo was observed moving ESE. The speed conformed to light aircraft.
Object
picked up at Cross City on AN/FPS-6A height finder at 6:00 a.m.
EDT (1000Z)
from then until dawn. Radar picked up object at 160 degrees at 45 miles
& 8400 feet.
Altitude varied from 6 to 10 thousand feet while being tracked. When
target locked on, antenna would hunt violently. When moved a few
degrees
in either direction the hunting would stop. When hunting occurred the
scope would be cluttered. Tracked for two hours. (Dan Wilson)
