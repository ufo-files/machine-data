Japan, A,V 6 interceptions of objects tracked on airborne radarRichard Hall:
Features: Aerial encounter, radar-visual,
cat-and-mouse
pursuit. An F-61 "Black Widow" night fighter
crew on
patrol over
Japan picked up a UFO target on radar moving at about 200 m.p.h. The
crew
made six attempts to close in on the object, and each time it rapidly
accelerated
to about 1,200 m.p.h. leaving the interceptor behind. Then it would

down again until the interceptor began closing in once more. On one

apparently 20-30 feet long.
