(McDonald list; FUFOR Index) FPS-3 Sights Unusual TargetBrad
Sparks:
Feb. 25, 1953; Charleston, West Virginia (BBU)
3:30 p.m. (EST). (McDonald list; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:
Feb. 25, 1953; Charleston, West Virginia
At 3:30 p.m. EST, an unusual target (oblong, 2 miles wide and 7 miles
long) was sighted on an
FPS-3 radar of the 783rd AC&W Squadron. Fade-out occurred in
like appearance to
the gaining or losing altitude. The length of observation was 15
minutes.
