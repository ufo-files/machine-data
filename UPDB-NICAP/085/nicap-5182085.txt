7:10 PM. Two groups of witnesses about twenty miles east of Hartford, CT, sawstrange lights or objects travel at a rather slow speed from east to west along a pathnorth of the witnesses. Known as The Case of the Flying Christmas Tree.(Complete report by Bruce Maccabee). (On Dec. 22, 1989 there was a similarevent at Vernon, IN)
