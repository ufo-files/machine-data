Oscar Flight / Malmstrom AFB Missile IncidentBrad Sparks:
March 24/25, 1967; Roy (about 30 miles NE of
Lewiston), Montana (BBU)
O-Flight of Malmstrom AFB, USAF 341st Strategic
Missile Wing, reportedly had unexplained
deactivation of 6-8 Minuteman nuclear ICBM's within
several secs of each other during UFO close
encounters involving a red saucer1 shaped object in
early morning hours. USAF security guard allegedly
injured and medivacked out. There is no
documentation for this missile shutdown on about
March 24, 1967, whereas the March 16 shutdown is
extensively documented in official records and unit
histories.
