Animal Reaction Feature:

At 4:15 AM (CDT), cows remained undisturbed as a farmer
observed a silent, red, circular shape with a fuzzy neon aspect to it. The
farmer described light beams that came from the object, one of which focused on
him near the end of the 2-3 minute sighting.

The Sighting:

The farmer had turned on yard lights and was going to put
out feed for the cows when he saw a circular light beam, red, fuzzy with a fog
around it.1 Thinking it the moon, he
then remembered there was no moon that night [crescent moon had set at 8:30 PM
on Oct. 4, and moonrise was at 12:18 PM on Oct. 5 (USNO)1jw].1 He described an object with a light beam
going up into the air and another moving beam from the base of the object to
the ground, eventually shining on him.1
He estimated the object to be over treetops located near a pond 200
yards to the east of the barn.1 The
elevation of the object was estimated to be 40 degrees.

Another sighting occurred at the same time (4:15 AM) in
Warrensburg MO.1 This witness looking
east saw a yellow orange object like the full moon, silent, and estimated 15-20
degrees above the eastern horizon.1 She
saw it expand, a haze develops on the southern 1/3 of the disc, and at one
point an antenna-like object came out of it.1
In this case, the investigator, Ted Phillips, was suspicious that this
object was a NASA plasma experiment [Javelin ARGO-D4 rocket, took off at 5:05
EDT, plasma mission to apogee 900 km (NASA)1jw].

Object Over
Illinois Farm

1111 Arthur J.
Epstein and Joseph Gurney were the Field Investigators in the following
incident which took place at 4:15 a.m. on October 5, 1970 on a dairy farm one
mile east of Walsh, Illinois.1 The
witness does not wish to have his name published.

1111 At the time and
date mentioned above, Mr. S. had turned on the lights which illuminate the yard
where the cows stand before going into the milking shed.1 The yard is a cement affair.1 He walked over to put some feed in a feed
bin about fifty feet from a sixty-five-foot high silo.1 He glanced up to see a light beam in a
circular shape which had a red, fuzzy 1neon1 look.1 It appeared to have fog around it.1 At first he thought it was the moon but then remembered that
there was no moon that night.1 There was
a beam of light like a searchlight which appeared as one line going from top
right to bottom left, right through the center of the red circular light.1 The whole sighting lasted about two to three
minutes.1 Toward the end of it, a third
beam of light, which was focused directed on Mr. S. for about three seconds,
seemed to come from the center bottom of the UFO.1 He said he was standing in a circle of light about four feet in
diameter.1 He could see the sides of the
beam were perfectly straight.1 There
were no other colored lights or moving lights.1
No sound accompanied the sighting and the cattle were not disturbed.

1111 The UFO
disappeared from sight by fading out.1
It had been about 25-30 feet above a tree which measured about 75 feet
high.1 Mr. S. compared the object or
light to the size of a volley-ball held at arm1s length.

1111 It was noted
that five cows were on the other side of a three-strand electric fence, one
strand of which was broken.1 No one was
able to ascertain how the cows had gotten to that side of the fence.
