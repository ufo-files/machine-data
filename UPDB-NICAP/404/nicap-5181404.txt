2:00 AM. The radio of hotel owner Rosa Gremville suddenly went dead. Momentslater she heard a loud humming noise outside and looking out she was dazzled by a"huge moon lighting up the area, moving about like a seesaw." It descended andlanded in a field 100 yards behind the hotel. "It was round, with flames comingfrom the top of a dome. (Sources: David F. Webb and Ted Bloecher, HUMCAT:Catalogue of Humanoid Reports, case 1977-18; Randall Jones Pugh, FSR, August1977, p. 6 for BUFORA).
