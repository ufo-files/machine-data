Dogs barked constantly as 2 small discs landedGroup /Category 4

Animal Effect Cases

Case Directory
Gulf Breeze, Florida
February 8, 1989

Animal Reaction Feature:
A man was awakened by dogs barking excitedly. He saw a double disc object
descending and then landing in a vacant lot about 80 feet away. The
object had many flashing body lights and a sizzling or static sound was reported.
Dogs, belonging to the witness and to his neighbors barked constantly until
the event ended.

Joan Woodward, Animal Behavior Specialist:
At 3:40 am, in a suburban area of Gulf Breeze, on a partly cloudy, windy night,
a 31 year old laborer was awakened by dogs barking. He saw an object
descend and land or hover 3-4 inches above the ground in a vacant lot about
80 feet away. He woke his wife, 24, and son, 12. He went
outside and approached to within 40 feet of the object and shown a flashlight
on it. The object flashed back and disappeared.

The overall size of the object was estimated as no more than 3 feet.
The object was described as two discs connected by a vertical rod on the lower
3 feet (not exactly clear what that means1jw). The upper 1 foot was
a clear dome and light. The bottom disc had 15-20 red-orange lights
that were rapidly flashing. The top disc had 5-6 green and white lights
that were also flashing.

A static-like or sizzling sound was reported. No EM effects or physiological
effects were reported. Vegetation in the area appeared unaffected.
