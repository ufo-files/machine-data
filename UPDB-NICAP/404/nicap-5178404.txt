Gloster "Meteor" Encounters Disc / Salandin CaseRichard Hall:
October 4,
1954; Essex, England
4:15 p.m. +. An RAF pilot encountered a UFO over Essex. Flight
Lt. J.R.
Salandin of the 604th Fighter Squadron, flying a Meteor jet out of
North Weald, Essex, nearly collided head-on with a huge, metallic
appearing object. The UFO was shaped like two saucers pressed together,
one inverted on top of the other. At the last second, it flipped to one
side and streaked past at tremendous speed. Two round UFOs had been
sighted speeding between two other Meteor jets in the vicinity just
before Lt. Salandin's sighting. (NICAP UFO EVIDENCE, 122;
London Illustrated News, December 2, 1954)

Victor J. Kean:
Project FT: Timothy Good in "Above Top Secret" (Page 35) gives
the date as October 14th, 1954 with a detailed account in which he
corrected some inaccuracies which had previously appeared in FSR. Vol.1
