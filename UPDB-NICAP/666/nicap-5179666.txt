11:55 p.m. A motorist almost struck a> blindingly bright object which had landedon the highway. It had brilliant white lights and intense red body lights. When heturned back to investigate, the UFO flew off through a corn field, at one pointilluminating the terrain. Then the lights blinked off and the obiect disappeared.Next day his eyes were sore and bloodshot (physiological effects). (U.F.O.Investigator, Vol. Ill, No. 8, May-June 1966, p. 7.)
