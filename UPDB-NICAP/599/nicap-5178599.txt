Brief Sighting By AF PilotDan Wilson:
July 17, 1956; Mastic Airport, Long Island, NY
8:55 p.m. EST. An Air Force pilot made an air-visual
sighting of one green tear shaped object the size of a
10 by 5 foot door seen for 1-2 seconds. It appeared to
flash off and on. Five minutes later the pilot saw three
more green flashes.
