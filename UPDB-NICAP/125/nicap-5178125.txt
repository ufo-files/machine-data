Four B-36-Sized Discs Observed By 3-Man CrewDan Wilson:
Jan. 29, 1953; Malibu Beach, Calif. (BB)
At 2:20 p.m. PST, four metallic disc-shaped objects the size of a B-36
flying in squadron formation in a vertical plane at approximately
20,000 feet at an
estimated speed of 1200 mph were observed by a three man crew of a
Northrup Aviation Corp. plane. These men were Rex Hardy Jr., a Northrup
aircraft test pilot, Chester Mathews, another test pilot and Jim
Wilkinson, a Northrup photographer. The objects were observed for
approximately 5 minutes.
