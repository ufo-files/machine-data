Ms. Adele Holzer was in her car driving to work when she saw a hemisphericalwhite disc. As the UFO hovered over some nearby trees, six beams of green lightshot from it and she felt slightly paralyzed by one of them. Her watch had stoppedat 08:01 a.m. and her car's ignition key, that she believed had been in her hand theentire time, was bent.
