DC-7 Encounters Object Over AtlanticFran
Ridge:
This confidential report of an interrogation by
OSI was submitted to ATIC. The object appeared dead
ahead of the aircraft at an unestimated distance and was
pulsating at 2-3 times per second. Numbered 4216 on the
sighting listing, Blue Book has it explained as "Astro
(meteor)".

Dan Wilson:
July 26, 1956; Atlantic 43.12N 49.30W / 500 miles SE of
Gander, Newfoundland (BB)
10:12 p.m. local time. A civilian airline pilot flying a
Pan American Airways DC-7 at approximately15,000 feet
observed a circular blue green pulsating light the size
of a golf ball with a speed greater than any aircraft.
