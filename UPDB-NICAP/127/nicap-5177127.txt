July 24, 1948, 0250E,  Warner Robins AFB,
Georgia

Walter C. Massey, a member of civilian alert, observed a
squash-shaped object with flaming exhaust headed south at terrific
speed.

Seven other witnesses from Augusta, Georgia, also observed the
object.
