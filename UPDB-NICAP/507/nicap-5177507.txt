Sept. 9, 1951; About 50 miles off coast of North Korea (BBU)
6:50 p.m. Radar tracking of multiple unidentified targets at
900 mph. (Jan Aldrich)

Dan Wilson:
Sept. 9, 1951; Off North Korean coast
At 1850 hours local time a high speed target was observed on
radar on the USS Cony DDE 508, operating with Task Force 77. The object
was tracked on a course of 225 degrees true, with a speed of about 900
knots for approximately one minute. The operator's interpretation was
"Many Bogies" on a line of bearing perpendicular to the course of the
target. The AN/SPS-6B air search radar was in use and was functioning
normally before and after the incident.
