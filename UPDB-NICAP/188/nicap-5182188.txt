Dogs bark and lights dim as disc follows power lineForm
97-AR
Date: Saturday, 18 Feb 2006

From:
Joan Woodward, Animal Reaction Specialist
Subject: Auberry CA, September 6,
1982

Cat: 4
To: NICAP

Animal Reaction Feature:

The witness went outdoors on a clear night at 10 PM to turn off
sprinklers when his two dogs began barking as if a car were approaching.1 Seeing no car, the witness looked up and saw
a disc-shaped light moving from E to W.1
The light appeared to be following the path of power lines leading to a
house 100 yards away.1 The dogs
continued barking throughout the estimated 2-3 minutes of the sighting, and the
outdoor lights were noted to dim.1

The Sighting:

The witness was 12 years old at the time of the
sighting.1 He initially reported the
sighting to CUFOS and filled out a sighting form*.1 In 2004, about to go on active duty with the U.S. Navy, he
reported the sighting to Brian Vike at HBCC Research (posted Oct. 1, 2004) and
the information here was taken from that report.11 He described himself as a military aircraft buff since the age of
7 and as familiar with aircraft in that he lived in the traffic pattern for
Fresno-Yosemite IAP.**

The witness described the night of September 6, 1982 as
clear, temperatures in the mid 901s, and no wind.1 [Fresno Airport, 27 miles away, reported a high T of 981 and
a low of 641
F for Sept. 6, and clear calm weather (NOAA).1jw].11

The sighting began as described above in the AR
section.1 The disc shaped light,
apparently following the power lines, changed color as follows:1 red-blue-orange-yellow-white-red.1 It was reported to be the size of a dime at
arms length.1 The porch light and yard
light both dimmed, and a TV being watched by the witness1s mother was reported
to have interference.

No sound or physiological effects were reported.1 EM effects were observed.

*Thanks to Mark Rodeghier for a search of the CUFOS
files.1 The report was not located.
