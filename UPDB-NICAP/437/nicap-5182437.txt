Case Directory
Petit-Rechain, Belgium
Photo
Apr. 4 or 7, 1990

Mark Cashman:
"Mr. P. M. is a young worker (turner). The photo was allegedly made at the
beginning of April 1990 (4 or 7 probably). The photographer's girl friend
saw the object and gave him notice. He had a roll nearly finished in his apparatus
and made the two last (numbers 35 and 36). Then he sent the roll to a lab
which was offering cheap prices. When the roll came back, only 35 was good,
there was practically nothing on 36. P. M. showed the 2 photos to several
people working with him (they have been found back and have confirmed), and
then threw away number 36. One of his colleagues, Mr. L. R., spoke to a press
photograph, who bought the copyright (Mr. L. R. and other colleagues have
confirmed this part of the story). Then the photo appeared in the press (a
couple of months after it had been made). See photos and footnote.
