Two men leaving a beachfront cafe saw a bright bluish light flying low over theocean descend at a steep angle at high speed. The light then stopped, anddescended more slowly into a wooded area next to a nearby campground. Bothmen ran into the woods, which were now illuminated by a bluish glow. Uponentering a clearing, they saw a 2.1 meter tall human-like figure, standing eightmeters away from them.
