Object Flies In Front Of DC-6, AcceleratesDan

Wilson:
Dec. 21, 1965; Putnam, Connecticut (BB)
7:33 p.m. Northeast Airlines pilot LeBewohl sighted a
round unknown object which was described as pink, then
orange, then white in color and the apparent size of a
quarter held at about arm's length. The object passed
directly in front of the plane and after 7 minutes the
object picked up speed and disappeared. The pilot was
flying a DC-6 aircraft at 10,000 feet on a heading of
260 degrees. The total length of the observation was 11
minutes.
