July 21, 1952; Randolph AFB - Converse, Texas (BBU 1522)

At 1630 hours CST, Mrs. Lovicy E. Neal, wife of Capt.
James B. Neal, Randolph AFB, observed an object like an elongated
fuselage with a pointed nose and no wings. The object had a box-like
empennage in the rear. The object flew in a straight line just above
some B-29's that were landing at Randolph AFB. The object then made an
abrupt turn upwards and climbed out of sight. Length of observation was
from 3 to 5 seconds.
