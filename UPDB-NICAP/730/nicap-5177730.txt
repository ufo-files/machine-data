June 25, 1952, Chicago, Illinois (BBU 1344)

Two witnesses saw one bright yellowish to white
egg-shaped object with a red tail light high in the sky moving
slowly in a large continuous circle. Object made 7 circles.

Observer A - Mrs. Daniel Norbury saw the object from 2030 hours to
2200 hours CDST

Observer B - Lawrence Matheis saw the object from 2120 hours
to 2130 hours CDST
