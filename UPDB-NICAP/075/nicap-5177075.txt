[AAF reserve Capt. Moore saw an object traveling at 700 mph leave

(McDonald list; FOIA; FUFOR Index)
==================================================

Oct. 8/9, 1947; Las Vegas, Nevada (BBU)

Daytime, Las Vegas, Nevada, an Air Force Reserve
pilot reported observing a trail high in the sky. The object
producing the trail was not visible. Object was traveling at an
estimated speed of 400-1000 mph.
