Sept.16, 1947; MEW Radar
Station, Fukuoka, Japan (BB)
A target was picked up at a range
of 89 miles on a bearing of 040 degrees (Point C).
Target was tracked
on an incoming course to a range of 19 miles and a
bearing of 045
degrees at which point it faded out. The nearest known
operational
U.S.S.R. air bases are on the east coast of
Korea at 391 10'
North Latitude and 127 degrees 29' East Longitude and
Haeju on west
coast of Korea at 381 02' North Latitude and 125
degrees East
Longtude. The range to these oases from Fukuoka
is 360 nautical
miles. Genzan is classified as having permanent
runways of 4500-6000
foot length, and was highly operational by the
Japanese. Haeju
has one runway of approximtely 4000 foot length,
probably surfaced. It
is therefore reasonable, to assume that the U.S.S.R.
is utilizing the
facilities at these bases to the utmost, since
the location is
strategically between Vladivostok to the north and the
U.S. occupied
areas of Korea to the south. A recent report
from the U.S. XXIV
Corps, evaluated F-3, states "a new type aircraft on
Haeju airstrip
North Korea. "'This aircraft emitted black smoke on
take-off, but was
not identified as jet propelled." Speed:
840 - 900
mph (730-780 knots); altitude: 10,000 - 20,000 feet.
