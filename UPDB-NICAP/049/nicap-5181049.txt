USO Apparently Tracked By Radar At Some PointFran
Ridge:
Sept. or Oct., 1974; off Guam
2:00 a.m. (??). The USS Reclaimer ARS-42 while
off Guam, had an underwater event that apparently was tracked by radar
on Guam at some point. The starboard lookout report a contact. What
they then saw was a single red flashing light which changed course a
little and then parallelled them. Guam control acknowledged that they
too were tracking something and were sending a wild weasel F-14 to
investigate. This thing, now a USO, accelerated and the F-14 went to
full afterburner right off the deck. The USO pulled easily ahead of the
jet, crossed the horizon in seconds while underwater with no visible
wake.
