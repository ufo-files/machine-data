April 6-7, 1949. Memphis, Tenn. (BBU)
12:01 [12:30?], 2, 3:30, 4 a.m. Housewife Mrs. Mike Love Stewart
and Dorthy [Dorothy?] Hall (and Helen Howell?), a husband and son, saw
6-9 climbing, diving, whirling yellow or silvery oval objects which
avoided 3-4 airplanes, traveling from SW to SE about 45° elevation
about 1-2 miles away, 1/4 moon angular size. (FOIA) 2-4 hrs
================================

April 7, 1949. Memphis, Tenn. (BBU)

Time: 0100 - 0400
Observers: Mrs. Dorothy J. Hall

Mr. and Mrs M. L. Stewart, and son, Tommie Stewart

Information according to Mrs. Dorothy J. Hall:
Number of objects: 4-7
Distance from observer: 2 miles
Size: 1/4 size of moon
Color: Silver like
Shape: Oval
Altitude: 45 degrees above horizon
Speed: 50 to 75 MPH
Time in sight: 3 hrs.
Maneuvers: Clockwise, whirling, diving, and climbing
suddenly
Additional information: At approach of aircraft object
moved out of flight
