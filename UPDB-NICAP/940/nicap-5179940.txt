About 6:55 p.m. An airline salesman driving on route 93 slowed his car as he sawan object hovering silently just over the road. Uncomfortably, he drove underneathit and saw other people in cars looking at it. (Massachusetts NICAP SubcommitteeReport, NICAP files; Project Blue Book files, National Archives; U.F.O.Investigator, Vol.. Ill, No. 12, March-April 1967, p. 6; Fowler, 1974, p. 344.)
