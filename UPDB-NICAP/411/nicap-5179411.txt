Light Hovers Over Radio TowerDan Wilson:
Jan. 21, 1965; Richmond, Virginia (BB)
7:10 p.m. EST. A bright light, the size compared with a
half dollar held at arm's length was observed hovering
over the tower of radio station WTVR. After hovering for
a few seconds the object appeared to move rapidly to the
north and disappear over the horizon. The light was seen
for approximately 45 seconds. (Dan Wilson)
