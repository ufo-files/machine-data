6:00 p.m. LT (2:00 p.m.EDT). A truck driver on the M6 Motorway watched a darkcircular craft moving parallel to his path for 5-1/2 miles as he drove at 35-40m.p.h. He stopped and got out to better observe the object, which was disc-shapedwith a rim around the center and had illuminated portholes. (Stanway and Pace,1968, pp. 17, 44-46.)
