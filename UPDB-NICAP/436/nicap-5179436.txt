April 10-14, 1965; Misawa AFB, Japan (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
April 10-14, 1965; Misawa AFB, Japan (BBU)
On April 10, 11, 12 and 14 April, unidentified radar targets
were observed. All sightings occurred at night. The targets direction
of travel varied. The targets appeared the size of 2 century type
aircraft and targets were observed on PAR
and ASR at the same points. The targets were seen on eight occasions on
April 14, four times on both search and precision radar. In one
instance four targets were visible simultaneously. The radars involved
were CPN-18 (S-band) and FPN-16 (X-band) GCA
radars. Maintenance checked the radar, it appeared
normal. The Chief, Target Branch, 1st Lt. David W Vaughan
stated; "In the past 12 years I have observed all types of weather,
wind, temperature inversion, false returns, birds, and numerous
other objects on radar, but never a target this size, moving with the
speed and directions of these."
