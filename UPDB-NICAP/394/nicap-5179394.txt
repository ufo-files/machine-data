Brad Sparks:

Dec. 19, 1964; Patuxent River
NAS, Maryland (BBU)
3:30 a.m. USN control tower operator Bernard Sujka and 2
other CTO's tracked 2 large target 10 miles apart
heading directly toward the radar station at about 7,000
mph, swerving off at 15 miles range, then approaching
again to 10 miles, then one target returned to 8 miles
range and made a high speed 160° turn. (NICAP)

Fran Ridge:
Investigated by Richard Hall and testimony by controller
Bernard Sujka, testimony witnessed by both Hall and Don
Berliner. Case appears to be interference [Shough].
Currently rated as "0" (see preliminary rating code
above). A former field investigator with our Indiana
NICAP Subcommittee was stationed at Naval Air Station
(NAS) Patuxent River, MD at the time of the incident,
and sent word of the incident to us in January.
(Note: This case was not included in the UFO
EVIDENCE.)

Dr. James E. McDonald:
Interference not clearly enough ruled out. Radials
suggest it, as does lack of corresponding return on
long-range set and absence of similar returns on other
stations.

Jan Aldrich:
Patuxent has interest in an historic context. The Navy
allowed the radar operator to fill out a report for
NICAP. It came at a time when NICAP could exploit it in
the media. Hall left a number of such cases which NICAP
had featured in other publications out of the UFO
Evidence. He used many cases he thought supported UFOs
being of interest for an independent investigation or
leads and incomplete cases which meeded follow up.
