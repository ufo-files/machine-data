Radar Tracks Zigzagging Object (McDonald list; FUFOR Index)Brad
Sparks:
Feb. 25, 1958; Gander AFB, Newfoundland (BBU)
2:30 a.m. (AST). Miller. (McDonald list; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:
Feb. 25, 1958; Gander AFB, Newfoundland (BBU)
2:30 a.m. AST. There was a radar sighting of an object that
moved SE to NW on a very erratic zigzagging course at a range of 12 to
15 miles at an azimuth of 150 to 300 degrees. Aircraft and balloon were
ruled out as the source of radar target. Later there was a ground
visual sighting in the area.
