10:00 p.m. Noticing a bright light shining in her bedroom, the 82-year-old witnesslooked outside and saw a "boat-shaped," or oblong, object hovering over aneighboring house.  The beings, who ignored the witness, were visible only fromthe waist up. The object remained over the church nearly a half hour, rising andfalling slightly, then moved horizontally a short distance and shot upward at highspeed. (SYMPAP 1975,64)
