Dog did not respond to boomerangGroup /Category 4

Animal Effect Cases

Case Directory
Ossining, New York
March 24, 1983

Animal Reaction Feature:
David Scarpino, accountant, was walking his Golden Retriever around the Ossining
Reservoir at 8:15 pm. He saw bright lights in a boomerang formation
hovering over a large tree a few feet away from him. His dog did not
appear to notice the object.

Joan Woodward, Animal Reaction Specialist:
Scarpino noticed the lights over his shoulder and turned to see 12 bright
lights as described above. He thought the lights were on one solid object
and compared its size to that of a 747. He could hear no sound.
As he watched, the lights slowly turned 180 degrees 1as if on a wheel1 and
started moving south at walking speed. The object again hovered. All
but one blue and one green light went out, and then those lights blinked out.
Nothing more was seen.

No EM effects and no physiological effects were reported.

This was one of hundreds of boomerang sightings between 7:30 pm and 9:30 pm
in Westchester and Putnam Counties.

Source:
Hynek, J. Allen, Philip J. Imbrogno, and Bob Pratt, 1998, Night Siege, The
Hudson Valley UFO Sightings, Llewellyn Publications, St. Paul MN, p. 37-38.
