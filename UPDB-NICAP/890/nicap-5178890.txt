Uncorrelated Target Doing 1300 KnotsDan
Wilson:
March 31, 1958; Walnut Ridge AFS, Arkansas
At 12:37 p.m. local time, an object was picked up on an
FPS-6 radar at 64,000 feet traveling at 1300 knots. The object
was seen on radar for 6 minutes at a bearing from 141 degrees
to 132 degrees. The unit picking up this target was the 725th
AC&W Squadron.
