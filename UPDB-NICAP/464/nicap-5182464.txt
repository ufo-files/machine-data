Cattle ran from area of blue oval ringGroup /Category 4

Animal Effect Cases

Case Directory
Slagle, Missouri

January 9, 1993

Animal Reaction Feature:
At 8 am on an overcast day with fog and rain, two men saw a herd of cattle
running flat-out toward them and away from the area of pasture over which
the men had just seen a silent, brilliant blue ring about 200-300 feet high
in the air and more than 500 feet from them.

Joan Woodward, Animal Reaction Specialist:
Two ranchers, 65 and 20+, in their truck saw a ring in the air over a field
for a few seconds before it disappeared. They saw cattle running from
the area and the power wires were swaying radically up and down in a 6 foot
arc. The ring was an unwavering brilliant blue, oval shaped, estimated
30 feet in diameter and 2-3 feet thick. The ring1s elevation was about 25
degrees.

Three days later a dead calf was found and investigators speculated this was
a possible cattle mutilation attempt. Investigators did a 2 hour site
visit.

No sound, no EM effects and no physiological effects were reported.
