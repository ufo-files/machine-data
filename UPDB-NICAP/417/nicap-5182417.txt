Animal Effect Cases

Case Directory
Kalamazoo, Michigan
September 11, 1989

Animal Reaction Feature:
Neighborhood dogs, including one that seldom barked, were barking
excitedly as two men, 23 and 25, arrived home. The witnesses saw
a fuselage-shaped object that rose over trees and hovered overhead at
treetop level before moving west. A low hum was heard.

Joan Woodward, Animal Reaction Specialist:
It was a clear night in an area with farms and woods. The two men,
their occupations described as laborers, arrived home at 9:15 pm from a
fishing expedition. Their attention was attracted by the barking
dogs. Looking, they saw a slow moving object rise up and
over the trees to their east. It hovered over two houses, and
then came toward them. One witness moved into the street, and the
object hovered about 40 seconds directly overhead until a car
approached and the object moved away.

The object was described as an estimated 200-300 feet long with stubby
wings near the front. The long fuselage had many read lights and
there were three white lights on the front. Tubes and pipes could
be seen underneath. The surface was a dull gray, and the object
made a low hum.

The car followed the object and the witness followed the car. The
object came toward them [witness and car?], but when the lights [car’s
lights I assume—jw] were blinked, the object slowed, turned, and
departed at speed.

No EM effects were reported. Physiological effects were
questionable. The next morning both men awoke with sore throats,
but they did not know if there was any relation to the object seen the
previous night.
