July 17, 1952, 0107CST, Batesville, Mississippi

State Highway Patrolmen observed two or
three round blue flourescent colored objects approximately 100 feet in
diamter. One object was at approximately 11,000 feet in altitude, and
the other object was at 3000 to 5000 feet in altitude. The objects
seemed to stand still and then they made a gradual back and
forth movement north to south. The objects were observed for
approximately 30 minutes.
