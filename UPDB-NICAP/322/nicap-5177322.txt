Feb. 8, 1950; Tampa, Florida (BBU)

Dan Wilson:
February. 8, 1950; Tampa, Florida
The sighting was reported to Tampa Approach Control by the pilot of
Eastern Airlines Flight 509, the pilot of Eastern Airlines Flight 225,
and a USAF B-29. Each reported the object proceeding on a straight
course in a westerly direction.
