A college student observed two brilliant lights descending in zig-zag fashion. TheUFOs hovered stationary for about 5 seconds, then "suddenly shot off at very greatspeed, at first in level flight across my front, then climbed up into the sky atapproximately 45 degrees to disappear." [Witness interviewed by NICAP AdviserHarold H. Fulton.]
