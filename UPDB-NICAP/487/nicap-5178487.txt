F-86 Pilot Encounters 10' SphereDan
Wilson:
May 18, 1955: Niagara Falls AFB, New York (BB)
8:40 p.m. One spherical, white object,
approximately 10 feet in diameter was observed by the
pilot of an F-86D jet aircraft while flying over
Niagara Falls AFB. The object was observed for about
2-3 seconds.
