11:30 p.m. Two young men were making a bulk newspaper delivery betweenBoulogne and Lille by motorbike when they were followed briefly by an unknown"vehicle" in the vicinity of St. Omer, Pas-de-Calais, France. A short distancefurther they saw a light in a field a few hundred meters from the road. (Source:David F. Webb & Ted Bloecher, HUMCAT: Catalogue of Humanoid Reports,case 1975-40, citing Joel Mesnard & Jean Marie Bigorne).
