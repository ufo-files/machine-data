11:45 p.m. EST. A number of independent sightings were reported of a reddish,glowing cigar- or football-shaped object either hovering or moving slowlyoverhead. At about 1:45 a.m. the following morning, in Milford (eastern suburb ofCincinnati), a railroad employee and companion were driving when they noticed awhite glowing light in the woods. (McDonald, 1967, Case 8, reprinted in Vaughan,1995, p. 206; Keyhoe and Lore, 1969a, p. 21, and 1969b, p. 54; Ridge, 1994, p.21.)
