3:00 AM. Traveling on a road, 3 women, 34-year old Sandra, Viviane and MarieFrance see a horizontal orange cigar-shaped object, hovering on the right side ofthe road. As they drove to the top of the hill the cigar vanishes. Soon a whitesphere follows their vehicle. Later under hypnosis Sandra remembered seeinghumanoids inside the object, described as of normal size, elongated eyes, thinbodies and wearing white coveralls from head to toes.
