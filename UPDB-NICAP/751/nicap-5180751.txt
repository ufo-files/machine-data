10:00 a.m. Several area residents reported sighting a silvery, blimp-shaped objecthigh in the sky over Knox County at midmorning today, but Vincennes and KnoxCounty police said they had no clues to its identity. Meanwhile, city policereceived a telephone can from a woman who "followed the UFO from Bicknell toVincennes." No figures were available on apparent size, speed or height of theobject, but all observers gave similar descriptions of its shape.
