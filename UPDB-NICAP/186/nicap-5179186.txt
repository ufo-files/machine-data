Radar Tracks Object Before & After Hill AbductionUpdated: 23 Feb 2010

May 1974: Air Command
& Staff College Research Report No. 0450-74:
"The time that the Hills spent aboard the
spacecraft could very well explain the two hours of
their trip which were previously unaccounted for.
Assuming the examinations did take two hours, and the
UFO left shortly after releasing the Hills, an eerie
time correlation can be drawn between the Hills'
reported midnight sighting and the UFO tracked by
Pease AFB radar at 2:14 a.m. "

Dan Wilson:
September 19, 1961; North Concord AFS, Vermont
At 5:22 local time, a return was picked up on an
AN/MPS-14 height finder radar at 62,000 feet at 196
degrees at 84 miles. The target was the size of a large
aircraft and appeared as a normal target. Contact was
lost at 199 degrees at 80 miles going NW then south.
Length of observation was 18 minutes. The target moved
at a low speed on an erratic course. The location of
observers was at 5 miles east of Claremont, New
Hampshire. (Dan Wilson)

Fran Ridge:

the actual sighting took place. Personnel at the N.
Concord AFS, Vermont sent their TWX on 22 September
