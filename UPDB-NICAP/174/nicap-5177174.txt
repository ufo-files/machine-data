AFOSI Case 8; AF C-47 Pilot Observes UFO Similar To Green FlareBrad Sparks:
Dec. 5, 1948. W of Las Vegas, New Mexico
9:05 p.m. (MST). USAF pilot Capt. William Goede, copilot Major Roger
Carter, and S/Sgt. flight engineer, flying a C-47 from Lowry AFB,
Denver, to Williams AFB, Chandler, Ariz., at 18,000 ft saw a green
fireball. 2nd green fireball sighting E of Sandia Mtns. (10 miles E of
Albuquerque) at 9:27 p.m. shot up from the ground to 500 ft height.
(Sparks; FOIA)
