Ridge:
Feb. 10, 1953; Misawa AFB, Japan (BBU)
6:45 PM. Unidentified radar targets spotted on GCA scope. Objects
seen on both 2 and 10 mile azimuth scopes. Targets were solid and
definite. The objects were reported flying at 50
knots.  An F-94 was
scrambled. The objects were not plotted or seem visually by
the F-94. The F-94 reported radar interference.(Dan Wilson,
McDonald list; FUFOR Index)
