8:00 p.m. EST. Two couples in a car at the Barkhamsted Reservoir saw a darksphere with randomly blinking red, green and white lights (body lights) around theedge. The object approached the witnesses, then hovered over the reservoir.(NICAP report form and report from Ted Thorben, NICAP investigator, 10/20/67.)
