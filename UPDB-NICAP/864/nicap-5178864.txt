Military aircraft pilot saw a bright orange light streaking across the skyBrad
Sparks:
Jan. 4, 1958; SW Libya (BBU)
12:08 a.m. (GMT). Military aircraft pilot and navigator saw
a bright orange light streaking across the sky on a NE heading, with
airborne radar tracking. (Project 1947; McDonald list; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:
The aircraft navigator observed jamming on his radar set approximately
10 minutes before the sighting, During the sighting a bright
flash occurred and simultaneously very high acceleration was noted.
