Lady Observes Saucer / Small Entity Gets OutDan Wilson:
April

8, 1954; Chicago, Illinois (BBU 2962)

4:30-5

p.m. Lelah H. Stoker saw a brilliant white
round-topped disc, parachute-shaped, with a
humanoid suspended beneath it, skim back and forth
over the water. Stoker called the Coast Guard, a
CG cutter appeared after 10 mins, then the UFO
approached shore. Stoker saw a short human-like
occupant in a green tight one-piece suit and
suspended below the object who got out in
undergrowth along the shore then walked around.
When CG cutter gave up searching the occupant
returned to the object which moved back over the
lake then took off at high speed to the E.
(Berliner; Davis-Bloecher 1978) Project
Blue Book Summary states: Lady observed a saucer.
Saucer landed and little green man got out, walked
around and climbed back into his saucer and took
off. UNIDENTIFIED.
