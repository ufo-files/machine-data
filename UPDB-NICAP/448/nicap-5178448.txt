Dec. 23, 1954; NE of Nogales, Ariz. (BBU)
Pilot flying F-86 fighter chased a light emitting red and green
flashes. (Weinstein)

Dan Wilson:
Dec. 23, 1954; Nogales, Arizona
At 10:19 p.m. MST, an F-86D pilot had both radar and visual
contact with an object described as a hazy glow of red and green
flashes. The object remained 5,000 to 10,000 feet above the F-86 at all
times. The length of the
observation was approx. 40 minutes. Momentary interference was noted on
radar scopes
both in the F-86D
and at the 684th AC&W Squadron at Davis-Monthan AFB.
(Dan Wilson)
