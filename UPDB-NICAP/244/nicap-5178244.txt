Daytime Bright Object In Vertical Climb Near C-45Dan Wilson:
Oct. 30, 1953; Norton AFB, California (BB)
1:55 p.m. PST A pilot, Major Joseph P. Tracy, co-pilot Capt. John H.
Moore, and observer 1st Lt. Raymond J. Gorman observed an object that
was bright red on the leading portion and bright white on the trailing
portion. The object passed directly below their C-45 aircraft and
directly in the flight path of the aircraft and continued almost
vertical climb until lost from sight. The C-45 was at an altitude of
7500 feet. The object appeared as large as a grapefruit held at arm's
length and was in sight for 7 minutes. (Dan Wilson, BB files)
