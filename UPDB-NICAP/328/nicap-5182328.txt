Time not given. A 13-year-old boy working at a kennel in Williston, Florida hearda humming noise, and noticed that the dogs were attempting to hide in their pensand whining. As he went out to investigate the area turned red with illumination,and a bright red disc-shaped object hovered only 20 feet over his head.
