Oval Object Zig Zags Over Naval Ship YardDan Wilson:
July 23, 1952; Long Beach, California (BB)
1:50 p.m. local. The witness Collinson located at the
Naval Ship Yard observed a orange red oval elliptical
shaped object overhead moving at a high rate of speed.
The object at an estimated altitude of 4,000 feet did a
zigzag maneuver and was in view for about 16 seconds.
The object appeared as large as a nickel held at arm's
length.
