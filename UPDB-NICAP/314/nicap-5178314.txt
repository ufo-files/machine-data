Object Signals In Reponse To A/C Blink Lights (LBR)Brad
Sparks:
May 13, 1954; Pacific bet. Hawaii and Calif. (BBU)
1:45 a.m. (AHDT). USAF pilot Capt. D. B. Mautner, 32nd
Air Transport Sq, McChord AFB, Wash., and crew of C-124
transport en route from Hickam AFB, Hawaii, to Travis
AFB, Calif., saw bright light moving parallel with them
moving to the left, object signaled back in response to
aircraft blinking lights, departed to NE at very high
speed climbing. (Jan Aldrich)

Dan Wilson:
May 13, 1954; Co-ordinates 26.5N 146.12 W (BBU)
Intelligence Report IR-117-54 states: As far as can be
determined by the preparing officer, no other friendly
aircraft were in the area at the time of the
sighting.
