T-6 Crews Encounter Object At 15,000'Brad Sparks:
Jan. 18, 1950; Denver, Colo. (BBU)
6:19 p.m. USAF pilots of T-6 saw a round reddish-white object tapered
aft flying at 15,000 ft. (Project 1947)

Dan Wilson:
January 18, 1950; Denver, Colorado
At around 1859 hours (6:59 p.m. CST) Lt. A. P. Webb and six other
pilots in four aircraft saw a similar flame or light moving at a high
rate of speed at times varying slightly. All the observers described
the flame or light in similar terms and gave its course generally as
southwesterly.
