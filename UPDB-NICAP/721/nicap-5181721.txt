A college coed saw an ovoid UFO surrounded by a white mist. It descended ontothe surface of the sea and became a very bright flourescent white light thatreflected off the surface of the water. She next noticed two short figures standingin the garden at the adjacent apartment. They were very thin with large squarishheads and large feet. Both were wearing dark gray tight-fitting uniforms.
