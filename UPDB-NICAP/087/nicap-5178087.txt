Dan Wilson:
Dec. 27, 1952; Borger, Texas & Albuquerque, New Mexico
The sighting at Borger, Texas, was at 2209 hours MST and around
six minutes later is when the object at Albuquerque was first seen at
2215 MST. This
may have been the same object. This is what the Air
Intelligence Information Report says. At 2309 hours p.m. CST
(10:09 p.m. MST) a round white
object with a white trail was sighted from DC-3 aircraft over Borger,
Texas, by an airline pilot from Central Airlines. The object appeared
to be at the same altitude as the observer (around 500 feet) and was
traveling east to west. When the object was first seen it was moving
very slowly. The sighting at
Albuquerque was a ground visual, three observers. Two CAA
radio operators at Kirtland AFB, Albuquerque at
around 2215 hours MST (10:15 p.m. MST) observed a milky-white
object round in shape, traveling east to west across the sky. The
object was also observed by an AEC Sandia Corp. employee from the
residential section of Albuquerque.(There are two different dates given
for this case. The
PROJECT 10073 RECORD CARD lists the date as December 28, 1952 at 2216
hours MST. The AIR INTELLIGENCE INFORMATION REPORT IR-23-52W states the
date was December 27, 1952. The USAF TECHNICAL INFORMATION SHEET also
says the date was December 27, 1952).
