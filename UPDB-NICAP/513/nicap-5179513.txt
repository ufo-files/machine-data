Two Orange Discs Fly In Straight LineDan Wilson:
Sept. 22, 1965; Landis, North
Carolina (BB)
8:05 p.m. EST. Roger Bost and his sister sighted two
very bright orange objects. They were flat on top and
bottom and darker around the middle like two pie pans
rim to rim, and sharply outlined. The objects were
flying on a straight path to the north. The objects were
seen for approximately 45 seconds.
