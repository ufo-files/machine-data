An orange-red, disc-shaped object descended over a paddock. It then emitted threesmaller objects that operated independently. One of these zigzagged, abruptly shotup to higher elevation, then descended again. The UFOs appeared to signal backand forth. Soil and grass samples collected from a circle of burnt grass found onthe ground reveal the burn marks were caused by an electrical discharge.
