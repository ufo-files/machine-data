2:00 AM. The radio of hotel owner Rosa Gremville suddenly went dead. Momentslater she heard a loud humming noise outside and looking out she was dazzled by a"huge moon lighting up the area, moving about like a seesaw." It descended andlanded in a field 100 yards behind the hotel. Two creatures 8 or 9-feet tall emergedfrom the dome through the flames. (Sources: David F. Webb and Ted Bloecher,HUMCAT: Catalogue of Humanoid Reports, case 1977-18; Randall Jones Pugh,FSR, August 1977, p. 6 for BUFORA).
