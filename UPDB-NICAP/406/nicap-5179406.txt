Cigar Larger Than B-52Dan Wilson:
Jan. 14, 1965; Thaxton, Virginia (BB)
4:30 p.m. EST. Witness by the name of McManaway while
watching a B-52 high in the sky saw a cigar-shaped
object larger than the B-52. The object was heading east
at an estimated 2,000 mph.
