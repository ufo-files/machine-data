July 15, 1952, 2055Z, Pendleton, Oregon, UFO report

Many civilians in 5 cars and an Oregon State
Trooper observed a spherical-shaped object, 35-100 feet in diameter,
silver in color, flying very fast at an estimated altitude of 4,000
feet. Object was moving eastbound and appeared to be dipping in flight.
Object upon last visual contact at 2100Z, was heading due
north.

At 2155Z a strike force of six B-36 aircraft were
over Pendlton, Oregon, heading north.
