(McDonald list) F-94C Tracks UFO at 200 KnotsBrad
Sparks:
Sept. 28, 1953; Palmdale,
Calif.. (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
At 7:10 p.m., PST, an unidentified object appeared on an F-94C
radar scope for a period of 15 seconds. The object was traveling on a
60 degree course at an estimated speed
of 2000 knots. Distance of object was 4 miles when first observed. The
object compared with a C-47 in size on the radar scope. The F-94 was on
a
heading of 230 degrees at
300 knots at 21,000 feet. The same or another object
was observed visually
from another F-94C for six seconds at 7:00 p.m.
The object was described as being round, orange in color and traveling
on
the same course as the as
the first object.
