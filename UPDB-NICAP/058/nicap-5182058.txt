Midnight, the witness was driving alone outside of Winnipeg when he saw a hugeUFO gliding over the roadway. Dimly outlined from the glow inside the"windows" of the central mass were two or three figures, whose features were notdiscernible, but whose heads and shoulders indicated they were humanoid inshape.
