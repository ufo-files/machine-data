A Civil Air Patrol cadet along with several other witnesses observed a dullmetallic, circular object maneuvering near Pecos, New Mexico. The cadet gotcloser to the object after it landed in a nearby clearing and took some photographs.He then observed a human looking figure dressed in a metallic suit emerge fromthe object and walk a few feet away from it.
