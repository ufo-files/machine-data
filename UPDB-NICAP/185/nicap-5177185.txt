Cigar-shaped object cross the sky in front of his plane  (Project 1947; McDonaldlist; FUFOR Index)Brad Sparks:
Jan. 1, 1949; Jackson, Mississippi (BBU)
5 p.m. Pilot Rush flying private plane saw a cigar-shaped
object cross the sky in front of the plane. (Project 1947; McDonald
list; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:
Jan. 1, 1949; Jackson, Mississippi (BBU)
5 p.m. CST. Pilot Tom Rush and his wife were in a private plane and saw
an object described as cigar-shaped, 60' long and 10' wideross their
path at 1500-1600' altitude. This estimate also placed the strange
object at 500' which is close encounter range. The speed was
approximately 200 m.p.h., accelerating to approx 400-500 m.p.h. Object
made a 50-degree turn and was visible for 10-12 seconds.
