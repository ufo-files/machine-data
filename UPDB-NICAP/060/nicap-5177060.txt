Fran Ridge:
Aug. 14, 1947; Friuli, Italy

Time not given. Professor R. L. Johannis was on a
rock-hunting expedition in the mountains in the
extreme NW part of Italy. He emerged from a stand
of trees & saw, on a rocky riverbank, a large
red object that seemed to be shaped like a lens.
The witness & the creatures approached one
another, but when they were a few paces apart,
they stopped. Witness felt paralyzed. Two
creatures, about 3 feet tall, heads larger than
normal human. No hair. Tight-fitting brown caps.
Noses straight & long. Eyes large,
protuberant, & round; yellow-green in color
with a vertical pupil like a cat. Hands had 8
fingers & seemed to be opposed. Randle/Estes
reliability rating: 6
