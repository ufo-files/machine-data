Silver Egg Observed By Navy UnitDan
Wilson:
Nov. 20, 1953; 36.55N 76.00W (Atlantic) (BB)
At 4:30 p.m. EST, a Navy unit made an air visual
observation of a small silver egg-shaped object moving
on a northerly course at an estimated 55,000 feet at an
estimated speed of 1000( mph? knots?) The length of
observation was 15 minutes.(Dan Wilson)
