(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
Sept.
28, 1952; Goose AFB, Labrador, Canada
An object resembling an aircraft traveling north to south at
approximately 120 mph, was seen on GCA radar as it passed 1 1/2 miles
east of runway 27. The object had to be at an altitude of less that
4000 feet due to the limitations of the GCA unit. The signal on the
scope was strong and clear.
