10:00 p.m. Michael D, 15, was on his way home after camping out with two otherboys; while crossing a grassy field, alone, he saw a bright object slowlyapproaching at low altitude, passing over some high-tension wires that crossed thefield. A door 'came down" out of which emerged a "huge" figure, who washunched over, had long arms, and wore shiny, light reflecting clothing. (Humcat1971-17; John Carlson, Bruce Maccabee for NICAP)
