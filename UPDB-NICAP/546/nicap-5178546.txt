Fast Object With Light On Rear Observed By AirlinerBrad
Sparks:
Nov. 14, 1955; Deming, New Mexico (BBU)
1 a.m. Commercial airline pilot in flight saw a fast
moving object, with a light on the rear, come from the
SW. (Project 1947)

Dan Wilson:
Nov. 14, 1955; Deming, New Mexico (BBU)
6:00 p.m. MST. Commercial airline pilot in flight saw an
object coming from the southwest corner of Albuquerque,
with a light on the rear, Object was estimated to be at
about 25,000' and going "very fast". This conflicts with
report because the duration was listed as 15 minutes.
Project Record Card states this was a CIRVIS Report and
came in the form of a letter. No Case on file. (Project
1947)

Fran Ridge:
