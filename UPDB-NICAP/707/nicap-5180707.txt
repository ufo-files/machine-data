About midnight a rancher and his wife observed a brilliant orange triangular-shaped object with a blinking red light on top. Two more identical objects thenappeared alongside the first. (Los Angeles NICAP Subcommittee report filed byMrs. Idabel Epperson, who interviewed the witnesses by telephone.)
