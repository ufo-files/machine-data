Glowing Disc On Legs Freaks DogsFran Ridge:
July 19, 1965; Vaucluse, Australia
5:30 p.m. Denis Crowe, a former diplomat and aircraft
artist, and a group of dogs, encountered a glowing disc
resting on legs on a beach. It was estimated to be 20
feet in diameter and about 9 feet high. The top
and bottom were silver gray and the rim in between was
glowing greenish-blue. A hollow area at the very
top may have been a glass dome. There were no
windows or antennae. The dogs all barked at it.
When the witness was 50-60 feet from the object, it took
off with a sound like air forced from a balloon. Crowe
watched it for about 10 seconds until it disappeared
into clouds. After the object took off, the dogs were
strangely silent. No EM or physiological effects were
reported. (Keyhoe, Donald E., and Gordon I. R. Lore,
Jr., 1969, Strange Effects of UFOs, Washington, D.C.,
NICAP, p. 33)
