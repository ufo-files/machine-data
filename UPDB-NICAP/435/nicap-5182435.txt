Three Lights in Triangle Are Also Picked Up On RadarFran Ridge:
March 30, 1990; nr. Wavre, Belgium
11:00 p.m. local. A call from a gendarme is received by
the Glons CRC (Control Reporting Center). Three unusual
lights are under observation, constantly changing color,
in the shape of an equilateral triangle. Ten minutes
later a second formation moves toward the first. Glons
CRC has an unidentified radar contact at 5 km N of
Beuvechain Airport, speed 25 knots. At 11:45 p.m., after
a series of erratic movements, the second formation
forms a triangle similar to the first. Glons CRC confirm
unidentified radar contacts at the correct location.
Semmerzake TCC / RP (Traffic Control Center / Reporting
Post) confirms radar contact. At 11:56 p.m. Glons CRC
scrambles one J Wing. Incidents also on the next
evening. (Source: Mark Cashman)
