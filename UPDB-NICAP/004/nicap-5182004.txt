Jose Fermin Albornoz, an illiterate shephard, awoke at 4:00 a.m. to the sounds ofhis animals causing a commotion. Looking out from his hut he saw a "beautiful"blue light hovering over a field about 20 meters away. His animals were effectedand he recalled an abduction experience.
