Blue-White Object Too Fast For AircraftDan
Wilson:
April 27, 1965; McClellan AFB, California (BB)
7:37 p.m. PST. Capt. Garth W. Skouson, Pilot, and SSGT
James H. Perkins, Flight Engineer, while flying a
EC-121D aircraft at 15,000 feet on a course of 132
degrees at 170 knots at approximately 240 miles WSW of
Los Angeles (33 degrees 2 min. N 122 degrees 30 min. W)
observed a blue-white round object the same size as a
star move across the horizon in a straight line too
swiftly for any known aircraft. The object was first
seen at 30 degrees above the horizon and 30 degrees left
of nose. The object was last seen at 15 degrees above
the horizon 60 degrees left of nose. It suddenly
disappeared after brightening slightly. The length of
observation was 4 minutes. (Blue Book files)
