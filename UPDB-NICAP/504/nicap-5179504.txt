White Ball Hits Road Flies AwayBrad Sparks:
Aug. 30, 1965; Urbana, Ohio (BBU 9864)
10:30 p.m. M. A. Lilly, N. Smith and T. Nastoff, saw a
white ball, 5-8 ft in diameter and trailed by a 2-3 ft
light, hit the road 100 ft in front of the witnesses'
car, then bounce and fly away. (Berliner)

Fran Ridge:
If this wasn't ball lightning it was a close encounter
with an unusual object to say the least, and even Blue
Book didn't try to explain it.
