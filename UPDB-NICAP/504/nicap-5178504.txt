Green Object With Trail TrackedBrad
Sparks:
July 8, 1955: Augusta, Maine (BBU)
At 9:00 p.m. EDT, one spherical object with a
green hue and a trail 9 -10 miles long was seen. A loud humming sound
was also noted. The object
was observed air-visually, ground-visually and on GCI radar. The length observation
was approximately one minute. (Dan Wilson)
