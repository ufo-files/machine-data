MATS Crew Sees Light In Descending TurnBrad Sparks:
April 8, 1953; Goose Bay, Labrador & Sondrestrom, Greenland (BBU)
7 p.m. (AST). USAF MATS transport pilot Swenson and copilot saw a white
light at 15,000 ft on a steady course in a shallow descending turn.
