6:00 p.m. A young woman with the surname of Dira had bathed in a lagoon in SaoGondolo do Amarante on the river and was washing clothes when she felt a heatwave and saw an oscillating blue light in the sky nearby. She managed to escapeinside her house, but she had a high body temp, bloodshot eyes, and her skinlooked like it had a sunburn. (Source: Claude Bourtembourg, SOBEPS News,March 1976, p. 18; Charles Bowen, Saga UFO Report, December 1976, p. 80;both sources cite J. Gualberto.)
