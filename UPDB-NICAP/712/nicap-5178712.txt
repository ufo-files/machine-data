July 18, 1957; Mt. Lemmon, Ariz. (BBU)
10:46-11:20 p.m. (MST). USAF ADC radar station 684th ACWRON, Mt. Lemmon
AFS, Senior Director Capt. Claiborne F. Bickham and crew using both
MPS-7 L-band search and MPS-14 S-band height-finder radars tracked a
stationary target at 42,000 ft to the NW at 308° azimuth 82 miles
range (S of Chandler, Ariz.). Target responded to encrypted military
IFF Mode 3 transponder signals, transmitted encrypted responses
resulting in "normal Mode 3 paint" on radar scopes, and "a very slight
strobe came from object appearing like ECM jamming." (Jan Aldrich) 34
