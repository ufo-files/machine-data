8:30 PM. Mrs. Joan Le Clair and four other local people saw a UFO hovering oversome nearby trees. Mrs. Le Clair looked through binoculars and could see anelongated object, green on top and bottom, with a red band around the center."There appeared to be another compartment on the bottom of this disc, and I couldsee windows in this lower part. There appeared to be a figure inside the object."(Sources: Sturgeon Bay Advocate, March 17, 1977; David F. Webb & TedBloecher, HUMCAT: Catalogue of Humanoid Reports, case 1977-14 (A1715),citing Thomas Heiman).
