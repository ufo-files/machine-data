3:00 PM. Six boys were racing dirt bikes near a lake when one of them fell and hithis head. Two of the other boys went to assist him, when suddenly a bright greenlight was seen descending over the surface of the lake. Inside the light appeared tobe a gray metallic oval shaped craft that descended on telescopic like legs near thewitnesses. A door became visible on the craft and a tall human like figure thenappeared, his arms seemed to be extended up into the air. It wore a loose fittingsilvery outfit, its arms were very long, and its feet resembled oval appendages.
