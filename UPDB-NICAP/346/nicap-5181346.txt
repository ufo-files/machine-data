2:30 p.m. Several witnesses observed an elongated object (estimated 45 meterslong) with round openings along the fuselage.  A blue-green light shone from theopenings, a red light at the front, and a diffuse orange glow from the underside.The object hovered above a military base built into the mountainside at anelevation of about 50 meters.   (Bertil Soderquist, MUFON representative inSweden.)
