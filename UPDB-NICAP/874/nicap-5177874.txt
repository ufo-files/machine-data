GCA Radar/Visual Of Blip ClusterBrad
Sparks:
July
28, 1952; McGuire AFB, New Jersey (BBU 1707)
6
a.m. GCA radar operator M/Sgt. W. F. Dees, and persons in the base
control tower. Radar tracked a large cluster of very distinct blips.
Visual observation was of oblong objects having neither wings nor tail,
which made a very fast turn, at one time in echelon formation. 55-secs.
(Berliner)
