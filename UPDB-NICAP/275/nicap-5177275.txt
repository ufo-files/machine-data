======================================

June 24, 1949; Mesa, Arizona (AFOSI Case 75)

Time: 1545 - 1630
Observers: Leo G. Crisman, Aircraft
gunnery instructor in WWII

Samuel Hood, Supervisor of Water Association, Mesa

Also two children of Leo G. Crisman, age 7 and 9
Number of objects: Four at first and
then a fifth object
Shape: Disc
Color: Steel gray
Size: N/S
Altitude: Extremely high
Speed: Varied, maximum of 400 MPH
Direction of travel: One moved SE,
second moved E, third moved NE,

fourth object went upward, fifth disc appeared in the

west moved eastward
Maneuvers: Same as above
Time in sight: Four objects 30
minutes, fifth object about 25 seconds
Apparent construction: Metallic
Comments: Seven power binoculars
were used to view the objects for part of

the time.
