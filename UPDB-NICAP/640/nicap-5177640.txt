The Case of the Missing ReportFran
Ridge:
In Feb. of 2006, while searching the Archive
for other case items, I came across a letter by Dr. James E. McDonald
to Maj. Quintanilla (Project Blue Book's Director) where he
mentioned their second unsuccessful attempt to locate any record of
"the
B-36 incident". Dan Wilson informed
me that he had spoken with Pestalozzi's wife (her husband had passed
away) who said her
husband
had written a small book about (guess what?) - contact with alien
beings! Dr. Hynek had said that a major report was missing, and this
was probably
the one.

Brad Sparks:
May 1, 1952; Davis-Monthan AFB, Tucson, Arizona (BBU)
9:10 a.m. Base Intelligence Officer Major Rudolph
Pestalozzi, Butonne, and several others saw 2 shiny round 20-25-foot
objects rapidly overtake then pace a B-36 in E-W flight at 20,000 ft at
about 50° ±10° elevation, then depart at high speed, one
object stopping to hover briefly, before disappearing, no sound, no
trail. B-36 crew also saw objects and interrogated on landing. (Case
file missing.) (Hynek UFO Rpt pp. 109-112; FUFOR Index)

Dr. J. Allen Hynek:
This case is a classic. The late Dr. James McDonald made a valiant
attempt in get details from original witnesses after discovering that a
major report, submitted to Blue Book by the “UFO Officer” (who was one
of the witnesses!) at Davis-Monthan, was missing. A small part of
this was apparently recovered and now appears in the Blue Book
microfilms.

Dan Wilson:

1952--mentioned in the George AFB, report.
