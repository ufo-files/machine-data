Transport Crew Encounters 100' LightBrad Sparks:
Oct. 19, 1952; (Pacific) 500 miles S of Hawaii (BBU 2175)
6:58 p.m. Crew of USAF C-50 transport plane saw a 100 ft
diameter round yellow light, with a red glowing edge, fly at 300-400
knots (350-450 mph). 20 secs. (Berliner)

Dan Wilson:
The documents say that the plane (C-50?) was from the 48th
Air Transport Squadron out of Hickman AFB, Hawaii and flying to
Travis AFB, Calif.  The 48th flew the C-54 and the C-97
aircraft, so the plane was probably a C-54 or C-97. The teletype goes
on to say the object was observed for approximately 5 minutes.
