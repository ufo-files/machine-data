Mantell Case (M)Latest update 25 Jan 2020
Fran Ridge:
Air National Guard pilot killed in crash of F-51 during
UFO pursuit. Also sighting at Lockburne AFB, Ohio, later
same afternoon, UFO maneuvering erratically up and down.
This case was re-investigated in 2006 by the NICAP
A-Team and there were major new discoveries. The full
report in book form and full documentation is produced
below. In 2018 Kevin Randle conducted a re-evaluation
and concluded the original analysis was correct and the
Mantell case was explained. However, Brad Sparks was
able to establish the opposite and presented evidence on
the oxygen cover-up.

Brad Sparks:

Jan. 7, 1948. Kentucky-Ohio (BBU)
1-7 p.m. (CST). Mantell case. Kentucky ANG pilot Capt
Thomas F. Mantell, Jr., was killed in an F-51D fighter
crash at about 3:18 p.m. about 4 miles SSW of
Franklin, Ky., about 92 miles S from Godman Control
Tower, in an attempt to intercept an unidentified
object described in one of Mantell1s last radio
reports to Godman AAF tower, Ft. Knox, Ky., about 725
ft elevation as 1metallic and tremendous in size.1
USAF 1st Lt. Orner, Commander, 733-5 Detachment, AACS,
tracked unidentified white light with red coloration
by weather tracking theodolite from Godman Field
hangar at 2401 azimuth 81 elevation at 5:35 p.m.,
which disappeared over the horizon at 2501 azimuth 01
elevation. See later sightings at Lockbourne AFB and
Clinton County AFB, Ohio (below). Expanded & greatly updated
version, including new SKYHOOK map
