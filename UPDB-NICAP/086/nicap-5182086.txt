An enormous UFO shaped like an orange ball, and estimated to be 750 feet inlength, made a number of turns in the sky south of Reserve, New Mexic and thenexecuted a ninety-degree turn near Luna Mountain. The five witnesses said theysaw a jet aircraft chase the UFO out of sight.
