Early a.m. CST. Two reoorters saw three discs with domes and flat bottoms(domed discs) that were later joined by a fourth. White light was coming fromwindows positioned around the circumference of the domes. The discs hoveredover a highway, then slowly moved away. (Joplin Globe, Missouri, 3/26/67, copyin NICAP files; APRO Bulletin, May-June 1967, p. 1.)
