Object 100' Above Sea Observed By Ship MV MarcalaBrad Sparks:
Aug. 4, 1950. Approx. 100 miles SE of New York City,
New York (BBU773)
10 a.m. (EDT). Master Nils Lewring, Chief Mate Jacob
Koelwyn, and the Third Mate, of M/V Marcala saw a 10
ft elliptical half-egg or cylindrical object, with
shiny aluminum or metallic white surface sparkling in
sunlight, approach from the SW heading NE at 50-100 ft
altitude, approaching to within 1,000 ft or 10 miles
(depending on witness) at 25 to 500 mph (depending on
witness), flying with a churning or rotary motion,
accelerating at end of sighting. (Sparks; Berliner;
Tony Rullan; NICAP website)
Fran Ridge:
This report was filed in accordance with official Navy
regulations and listed as an unknown, case #773.
