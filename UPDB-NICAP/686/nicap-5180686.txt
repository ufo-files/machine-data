1:10 a.m. A man was returning home when as he was rounding a bend in a ruralroad he noticed an unusual object hovering about 20 feet above the ground. Thecraft was saucer shaped with a dome on top and an antenna like protrusion. Aftertwo minutes the witness saw a figure walk back and forth in the opening of theobject. The figure was dressed in a gray white metallic suit with a hood and wasabout six-foot tall. (Stan Gordon, Mufon Journal # 189).
