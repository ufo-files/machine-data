May 2, 1957. Edwards AFB, Calif. (BBU 4715)
6:55-7:20 a.m. (PDT). James D. Bittick and John R.
Gettys, Jr., civilian phototheodolite operators, were
driving by truck to Askania Site #4 when they sighted
an object above them about 500 yards away. They
radioed a report to their supervisor Frank Baker who
told them to set up the camera and try to film the
object, which they did after about 10 mins. They
photographed what they described as a golden luminous
domed-saucer shaped object with holes or ports around
the dome about 100 ft in diameter about 1 mile in the
distance to the N headed E (photos show multiple?
objects). Available phototheodolite frames 614, 620
and 651 (609 too blurred, cutoff) show azimuth 10128'
elevation 2124' shifting E to azimuth 40130' elevation
110'. Disappeared at about 5 miles. Possible jet
fighter interception. [Possible weather balloon with
slow leak, explanation denied by Edwards AFB Colonel
Klein (sp?) analyzing actual tracking of balloon and
wind direction.] (Tom Tulien; Jan Aldrich; McDonald
1968 pp. 75, 85; Loren Gross Mar-May 1957 pp. 67-68;
IUR Oct 2005) 25 mins 2 witnesses 8 Full Moons?
