B-17 Photographic Mission CaseDan Wilson:
May 24, 1954; Richmond, Indiana (BBU)

12:25 EST. While flying on a photographic mapping
mission in a B-17, Major Leo Brubaker USAF, observed
and photographed an object below the plane for 45
seconds. The aircraft was flying at an altitude of
18,500 feet at 220 knots. The speed of the object was
estimated at 440 knots. The object traveled a distance
of six miles over the ground at a speed twice that of
the aircraft. The photos were taken with a F11 mapping
camera.
