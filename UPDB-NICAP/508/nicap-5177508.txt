The Fort Monmouth Radar IncidentUpdates: Drafted 22 Nov 2000, Update 10
Aug
2006, RADCAT 30 April 2010
Dan Wilson:

September 10, 1951; Fort Monmouth, New Jersey
11:10 a.m. .Several unidentified objects were sighted by
radar
at Fort Monmouth, N.J. The objects varied in speed from
0 to 700
mph and were sighted at varying altitudes. The objects
were observed on
an AN/MPG-1 radar set. At 3:15 p.m. an
object was observed on the SCR-584 radar set. The same
day an
unconventional object was sighted by crew of T-33 over
Sandy Hook, N.J.
The object disappeared out to sea near Asbury Park, New
Jersey at an
estimated speed
of 1000 mph.

Fran Ridge:

sighting by the

seen a balloon." Brad Sparks analysis
proves that the T-33 was
NOT chasing a balloon. (See below).
