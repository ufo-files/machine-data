Animal Reaction Feature:

This event took place at 2 a.m. on a farm.1 A number of different types of animals
reacted in a variety of ways.1

Barking and howling of dogs on the farm awoke the two
witnesses.1 A German Shepherd was noted
to be barking in a series of short barks.1

Four cats were climbing up a screen door to the house,
yowling and fighting.

At this point, the witnesses went out and saw a blue-white
object moving beyond the barn.1 When the
object disappeared, the animals quieted.

The next morning, it was found that horses in the barn had
gotten out of their tie-stalls and knocked harnesses off the walls.1 A neighbor reported that their horses had
also done similar damage inside the barn.

humming noise and the object went 1out like a light.1

There was no estimate of the object1s altitude or
distance.1 No EM or physiological
effects were reported.
