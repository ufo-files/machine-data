Two boys playing in a snow fort behind their house heard a humming sound thensaw a lighted disc shaped craft with red lights shining on its top and multicoloredlights on the bottom fly overhead and disappear from sight. They saw a four-foottall human like figure float over a nearby fence and come towards them.
