Governor's plane paced by globesFran Ridge:
April 25, 1966; Ocala, FL
8:52 p.m. EDT. Governor's plane paced by two objects. The crew and
passengers of a Convair aircraft carrying Florida Governor Haydon Burns
sighted two apparently connected bright yellow or yellow-orange
globe-shaped objects that paced the plane for about 40 miles. Witnesses
included newsmen and state officials. When Burns ordered his
pilot ,
Capt. Nathan Sharron, to
turn toward the UFO(s), after getting clearance from the Miami control
tower, the objects climbed steeply and disappeared (aircraft
avoidance). (U.F.O. Investigator, Vol. Ill, No. 8, May-June 1966. p. 3.)
