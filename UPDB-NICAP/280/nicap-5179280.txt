1400 MPH Airborne Radar TracksDan
Wilson:
May 31, 1963; 44N 49W (Atlantic) (BB)

2:03 p.m. local time. Three radar tracks were detected at a
speed of 1400 mph by an airborne radar. There was no visual sighting
although the targets were indicated at ranges of 2, 3 1/2 and 5 miles.
Two of the targets were following identical paths. The length of the
observation was 3 minutes.
