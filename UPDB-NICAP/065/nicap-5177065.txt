August 19, 1947, Twin Falls, Idaho (BBU)

Mr. H. H. Hedstrom, Executive Director Twin Falls Housing
Authority, his wife and a neighbor, Detective Richard A. Frazier, H. E.
Roundtree, and Richard Scott, of the Twin Falls Police Department,
observed formations of glowing objects flying overhead.

Date: August 19, 1947
Time: 9:30 P.M.
Number of objects: Groups of 3, 5, 7, and 12 in
triangular formation, at approximately 5-minute intervals.
Speed: Terrific
Direction of flight: NE and came back toward the SW

<>Objects appeared to be lighted from the inside.
