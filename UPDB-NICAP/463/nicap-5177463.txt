F-82 Encounters Maneuvering Amber LightBrad Sparks:
Feb. 1, 1951; Johnson AFB, Japan (BBU 886)
1710 MT Pilot, Lt. David H. Trexler, and radar operator, Lt.
Victor Helfenbein, of an F-82 night fighter saw an amber light make 3-4
360° turns to the right, reverse towards the F-82 then climb out of
sight.
