Winged Unidentified Executes Verical Climb Near JetsFran Ridge:
Feb. 7, 1955; Knights Landing, California (BB)
4:30 p.m. local. During observations of conventional jet
aircraft the witness observed the antics of a winged UFO
that reportedly executed a vertical climb and later, a
steep dive. This incident was discovered in an OSI
document to the FBI, found in the Blue Book file
collection. If this actually occurred it would be an
interesting, but distant encounter, worth posting in the
Cat. 1 file.
