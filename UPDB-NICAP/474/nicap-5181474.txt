Two witnesses suddenly noticed a disc-shaped craft flying above a nearby palmgrove. The craft stopped to hover. It had a large white light on the bottom and agreen and a blue flashing light, plus several brightly lit windows could be seen onits side. The object then shut off its lights and vanished. Later that night a womanhad gone out in his yard to empty some cooking waste when she saw four littlemen wearing silvery outfits standing by a water faucet.
