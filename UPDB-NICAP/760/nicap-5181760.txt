Mr. Filiberto Cardenas, age 45, was driving on Okeechobee Road just north ofMiami, Florida with three family friends when the car engine suddenly failed. Heand another man got out of the car to check under the hood when a large luminousobject emitting a humming sound descended over the vehicle. The witnessvanished in plain sight of the others, apparently taken up into the object by a beamof light.
