Observers In T-6 Watch Round ObjectDan Wilson:
May 13, 1952; George AFB, California (BB)
At 2:25 p.m. PDST, a pilot and an observer in an T-6G aircraft flying
at 10,500 feet altitude observed a white or silver round object at an
estimated altitude of 45,000 feet. The passenger stated that he was
looking at the object almost directly overhead when it disappeared very
fast, almost like a light being turned off. The object was observed
for approximately 30 minutes.
