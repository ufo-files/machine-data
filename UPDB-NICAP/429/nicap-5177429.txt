(McDonald list) Uncorrelated Targets Comparable to B-29Dan
Wilson:
November 10, 1950; South Ruislip, England
8:59 a.m. Not listed as an unknown in BB files, four radar contacts
were made
with an airborne
unidentified object over England at altitudes of 1,000 feet or
less to 30,000
feet or more and giving returns comparable to those of a B-29 aircraft,
between
8:59 a.m.and 10:26 a.m.. The speed of the object varied from 30
m.p.h. to
1000
m.p.h. statute. One contact at 9:46 a.m.lasting until 9:47
a.m.,
the
object was lost due to accelerations and ascent in excess of capability
of radar
automatic tracking equipment. The object was observed through surface
contact
by AN/MPQ-2.. Observation was made from a radar van at Heston
Airdrome (51deg. 29' N -- 00 deg. 23' W).
