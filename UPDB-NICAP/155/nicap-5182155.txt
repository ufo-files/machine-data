Several Argentine soldiers riding in a UNIMOG (Army) vehicle spotted severalshort man-like figures with large heads running towards a dark hovering discshaped object. The craft hovered only a few cm from the ground. As the vehicleapproached the object its engine began to sputter without any apparent cause.
