Dan Wilson)
April 14, 1961; Far East, NW of the Mariana
Islands (1834N 14257E)
At 2:36 p.m. local time four objects the size of a pinhead
were picked up on air intercept radar. The radar blips were
small and well defined.The length of the observation was approximately
10-15 minutes.
