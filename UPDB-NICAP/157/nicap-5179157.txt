Object at 97,000' Tracked on FPS-6A (McDonald list)Brad
Sparks:
April 20, 1961; Hanna City AFS, Illinois (BBU) (McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
April 20, 1961; Hanna City AFS, Illinois (BBU)
6:20 p.m. CST. An object at an estimated altitude of
97,000 feet was picked up on a FPS-6A radar of the 791 Radar Squadron
(SAGE)
at a bearing of 96 degrees. The object disappeared after 1 hour and 4
minutes
at 10,000 feet at a 95 degrees bearing. The object had been on a
perpendicular
descent with a 10 NM variation.
