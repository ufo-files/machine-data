(45°26' N, 141°43' E). (McDonald list) Target Circles Radar SiteFran
Ridge:
According to NICAP sources, Air Force radar men in Japan tracked
two oddly maneuvering UFOs for over an hour. One radar scope apparently
showed what looked like two airplanes dogfighting, but there were no
conventional
aircraft in the area. Type "G", ground radar, no visual.

Dan Wilson:
November 6, 1948; Wakkanai, Japan
At 11:57 a.m. local time, a radar operator at a ground radar
station observed a blip on the radar screen. The target first appeared
as one object
but at times appeared as 2 targets. The speed of the object(s) was
estimated
varying from 160 mph to 240 mph. The target circled the radar site
continuously for one hour and five minutes. No interception
action was taken due to
adverse weather. (McDonald list, Dan Wilson)
