Dan Wilson:
August 30, 1958; Gray AFB, Killeen, Texas
At 1:45 p.m. CDT, one target was observed on radar at 170
degrees at 30 miles distance. After two minutes the target
disappeared on
a heading of 175 degrees at a range of 140 miles. The estimated speed
of the object
was 3,300 knots. The witnesses stated that the target was not
like any
interference ever observed and did not differ from other IFF targets in
any respect
except speed. (Dan Wilson)
