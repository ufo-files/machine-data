Primary Target Tracked 90 MinutesDan
Wilson:
Nov. 11, 1967; North Olmsted, Ohio
At 9:05 p.m. EST, the Olmsted FAA (Cleveland) Air
Traffic Control Center tracked an object on ARSR radar for 1 1/2 hours.
The object was first detected at a point 20 to 25 miles north of Erie,
Pa. The object traveled at about 50 mph in a straight line towards
Buffalo, New, York. The object was a definite, primary target and was
in a place they didn't ordinarily pick up targets. The object
vanished
approximately 10 miles from Buffalo. Radar at Buffalo also tracked the
object. (Dan Wilson)
