Dan Wilson:
Aug. 10-11, 1956; Duncanville AFS near Dallas, Texas (BBU)
10:00 p.m. The first report of an unknown object came from the White
Rock
Lake area at 0400Z. That object disappeared. At
approximately 12:05 a.m. (0605Z) an unknown object was reported to the
GOC filter center in Dallas. This object was reportedly over the North
Dallas area. At approximately the same time 745th ACWRON sighted an
unknown aircraft on their CPS-6B radar. According to the Air
Intelligence Information Report, the track that was sighted on radar
was in the same vicinity that the civilian personnel reported seeing
their unknown object.
