Airborne Radar Tracks 3,000 MPH ObjectBrad
Sparks:
March 24, 1952, 60 miles W of Pt. Conception, Calif.
(BBU 1077)
8:45 a.m. [p.m.?] B-29 navigator and radar operator tracked
unidentified target on airborne radar at about 3,000 mph. (Berliner;
Shough)

Dan Wilson:
March 24, 1952, 60 miles west of Point Conception, Calif.
At 8:45 a.m. PST, Lt. Hancock, Radar Observer, and Lt. Cone,
Navigator, aboard a B-29 observed on the radar scope a clear and
distinct radar return of an object with a calculated speed of 3000 MPH.
The radar return was observed for 20-30 seconds. The altitude of the
object was of less than 25,000 feet. Both observers were regarded
as highly reliable officers.
