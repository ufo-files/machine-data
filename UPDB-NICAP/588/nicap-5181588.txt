A motorist saw a sharp white gleam of light some 300 meters ahead on the road.As he came to the curve, he saw that the gleam came from a mass approximately 5meters in height. He can not observe the base of the object, which is below the sideof the road. The top was rounded in the form of a dome, surmounted by a whitelight. As he drove by he saw through a transparent wall in the UFO numeroushuman like figures wearing helmets and tight-fitting suits. Ground traces werefound at the site.
