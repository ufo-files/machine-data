On this night three teenagers named Jones, Symms, and Dencie were driving byLake Erie in St. Thomas, Ontario when a blue light came and hovered over theircar, and then followed it along road. They made a sharp turn in their car, and theUFO made an even sharper turn. The car then stalled, and the radio went off as thelight drew closer. (Source: London [Ontario] Free Press, January 10, 1975).
