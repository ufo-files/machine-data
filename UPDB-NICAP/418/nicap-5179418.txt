(McDonald list) Tear Drop Shaped Object Tracked on RadarBrad
Sparks:
Jan. 24-28, 1965; Alaska (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
Jan. 28, 1965; Alaska (BBU)
3:30 a.m. (Over a period of 5 days there were
object sightings
reported throughout Alaska. Sightings were reported from
Anchorage,
Fairbanks, Cape
Lisburne, Northeast Cape, Cape Newenham, Cape Romanzoff,
Unalakleet,
Indian
Mountain, and Fort Yukon).  On January 28, at 3:30
a.m. local
time, an
object was tracked on radar at the same time and
direction as visual
sightings of
tear drop shaped objects. Object tracked on FPS-6 and
FPS-20A radars.
(Dan
Wilson)
