Dogs hid while diamond shaped object passed overGroup /Category 4

Animal Effect Cases

Case Directory
Cantonment, Florida
September 14, 1989

Animal Behavior Feature:
This is the second sighting in less than a month by this witness (see Cantonment
Florida, August 19, 1989).   As on August 19, the three large
vociferous dogs owned by the witness were silent. In this case they
hid under the house as a diamond-shaped object hovered below treetops and
just over houses across the street. A humming sound was reported.

Joan Woodward, Animal Behavior Specialist:
At 8 PM on an evening with scattered clouds at 10,000 feet, breeze from the
SW at 5 knots, the 31 year old woman saw the diamond-shaped object 1like two
Hershey1s Kisses attached together at the bases1 over houses and beneath treetops
across the street from her. The object was rotating in a clockwise direction
and had 40 or more colored lights that were green, blue, and white.
Her 12-year son also saw the object and was also interviewed by the investigator.

During the 3 minute sighting, a long-nosed airplane approached and the object
dropped downward out of sight.

No EM effects were reported. The female witnesses reported anxiety and
nausea.

This witness also reported that she began having psychic abilities after an
earlier sighting when she was 18, married to a military man stationed at an
army base in Germany. In that 1976 sighting, the witness reported EM effects
on her car and missing time.
