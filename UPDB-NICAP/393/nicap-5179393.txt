Sparks:
Dec. 4, 1964; Baker, Oregon (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
Dec. 4, 1964; Baker, Oregon
At 4:50 a.m. local time, a glowing round object that had the
apparent size ranging from a baseball to a basketball at arm's
length and was seen by at least 4 observers, Harold H. Eves
TSGT Radar Operations Crew Chief , a city
policeman, Donald H. Stinett 821st Radar Squadron, and Vernon W.
Meador 821st Radar Squadron AC&W Operator. Observations were
made with binoculars and a surveyors scope, and there were also radar
returns observed on an AN/FPS-35 Search Radar. Length of
observation was approximately 2 hours. Some observers saw
from 3 to 4 objects. (Dan Wilson)
