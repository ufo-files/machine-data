5:40 AM. Snowy morning. Blue lights were ahead which lady thought was a snowplough. One blinking light of unknown shape flew across in; front of her thensuddenly 6 to 12 blue lights took up position 2 to 3 feet in front of her slowlytraveling car. Next day at spot in road her car radio went dead. (Worley files)
