(McDonald list) Three Radars Track Slow ObjectDan
Wilson:
Nov. 13, 1958; Troy Peak and Tonopah, Nevada (BBU)
8:29 a.m.PST. Radars picked up a fairly stationary object at 279
degrees
azimuth, range of 26 miles, angle of elevation was 40 degrees, moving
quite slowly to the ENE. This occurred four times during
an eight hour period. These objects were all in the same area at the
same altitude. FPS-20,
MPS-7, and MPS-14 radars were involved in the observation. (McDonald
list)
