The witness observed several golden lights on a nearby field. He approached thelights but these suddenly rose up, circled, and landed again, this time closer to hishome. At the same time his dogs were going berserk. Later he heard footsteps andhis dog attracted him to a "figure" standing by the fence. The figure was a meterand a half tall, square in shape and metallic almost robotic in nature; it lacked ahead, arms or feet. A scorched area was found on the ground by governmentauthorities.
