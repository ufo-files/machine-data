Dogs barking at hum of boomerangGroup
/Category 4
Animal Effect Cases

Case Directory
Gulf Breeze, Florida
October 9, 1989

Animal Reaction Feature:
Area dogs barked as a slow-moving boomerang flew over the neighborhood
at tree top altitude.  The witness thought the dogs were
barking at the humming sound made by the object.

Joan Woodward, Animal Reaction Specialist:
The witness was a female, approximately 30, college degree, who
appeared well adjusted and was married to a cruise ship captain.
At 9:30 pm on a clear night, the witness was outdoors when neighbors
pointed out the boomerang. She described the object’s surface as
glowing and the color as white. No external lights were noted. No
apparent or actual size estimates were given.

The object was moving east to west very slowly at an estimated 1
mph. After 10-15 minutes, it turned and “blinked out.” The
witness got in her car and relocated the object and watched it until it
disappeared.

No EM effects and no physiological effects were reported. A
humming sound was reported.
