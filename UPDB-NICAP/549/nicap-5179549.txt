Radar Tracks Maneuvering Object Seen Over CityBrad Sparks:
Feb. 11, 1966; Showhegan, ME (BBU)
11:55 p.m. EST. A glowing orange object with domed top,
hovered, maneuvered over the area, seen by police and
citizens. The object was tracked on Air Force and FAA
radar "making tight turns at a low altitude." (NICAP
report; U.F.O. Investigator, Vol. Ill, No. 6, Jan. -Feb.
1966, pp. 1-2.) (BBU) [This case is not listed in the PBB Master Index
- Dan Wilson].
