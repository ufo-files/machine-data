At 8:05 p.m. About 40 miles north of Point Pleasant, a Ph.D. professor at OhioUniversity, who is also a private pilot, and four others saw an unidentified lightsource that alternated between yellow and pink as it moved over a wide arc of skyat moderate speed. It was observed for 10 minutes. (NICAP report form.)
