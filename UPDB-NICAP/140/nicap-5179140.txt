(McDonald list) High Speed Object Tracked on APS-95Brad
Sparks:
Feb. 16, 1961; Atlantic N of Bermuda (BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:

At 10:45 p.m. (EST), 0345Z, an abnormally large blip was
observed for 1-3 minutes on radar on a magnetic heading of 270
degrees. The speed of the object was reported to be 4,500 knots. The
object disappeared on the same heading of 270 degrees. The object was
observed on an APS-95 radar aboard a
RC-121D based at Otis AFB, Mass.
