Francis

The following report was submitted to
NICAP in 1961. The witness, a technician for the Atomic Energy
Commission at Los Alamos, one morning had noticed a group of men
outside the lab he was working at. Joining them he looked skyward, and
at an altitude of approximately 9,000 feet observed 13 saucer
shaped objects in a perfect straight line formation. During this
observation
period of about forty five minutes, several of the objects would
suddenly
leave this formation and with a tremendous burst of speed climb to what
he thought was about 20,000 ft. There were hundreds of witnesses. See
attached.
