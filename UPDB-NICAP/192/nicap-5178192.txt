May 23, 1953; Union of S. Africa
South African headquarters announced in November that on May 23, radar
had tracked an unidentified object near the Cape at over 1000 mph.
[Prescott Evening Courier, May 22; UFOE, VIII]. "Radar operators
picked up an unknown object which passed over the Cape six times at a
speed definitely exceeding 1,250 miles an hour. Each time it passed it
was within radar range, for sixteen seconds at distances varying from
35,000 to 50,000 feet, and altitudes between 5,000 and 17,000 feet."
