Sparks:

Nov. 11, 1956; El Toro Marine Corps Air Station, Calif. (BBU)
9:30 p.m. (PST). USMC
pilot flying helicopter and ground witnesses saw a flashing red light
with ground radar tracking. (Weinstein; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:

Beginning at 9:30 p.m. (PST) and continuing for up to 8
hours many targets were tracked on radar at speeds of 50 knots to
15,000 mph. At times lights were seen in the area where the radar
targets were being detected.  At times smaller objects
appeared to join larger object and merge with it to form a larger blip.
A few seconds later the
smaller objects would depart and dash all over the screen leaving
the larger blip in its original size. (Dan Wilson)
