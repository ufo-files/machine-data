Egg-Shaped Object Goes Up Then DivesDan Wilson:
Jan. 22 (4th?), 1965; Baltimore, Maryland (BB)
5:25 p.m. EST. A 10 year-old boy and 4 adults observed
an egg-shaped object that went way up in the sky. The
object suddenly dove down and disappeared far beyond the
observers. [Two different dates are given for this
sighting. Form 164 gave January 4th while the letter
gave January 22nd]

Fran Ridge:
The readable and unredacted version of the Blue Book

the 22nd.
