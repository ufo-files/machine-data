1:45 a.m. EST. Seven members of a band saw an object that first looked like aboomerang, then appeared like a teardrop.  It was a pale greenish-blue color with acluster of white lights (body lights). The object also was seen by people in anothercar as it moved northwest. (Ridge, 1994, p. 20.)
