The Rhodes Photo CaseDan Wilson:
July 7, 1947; Phoenix, Arizona (BBU)
4:00 p.m. local time. Mr. William Albert Rhodes
observed an elliptical-shaped object that spiraled
down from 5000 feet to 2000 feet and then ascended at
45 degree angle. Two photos were taken of the object.
Speed of the object was estimated at 400-600 mph.
Size: 20-30 feet with visible canopy. Color: grey
Sound: Noise like a jet on approach - no sound heard
while in view. Analysis based on camera data
indicated a diagonal size of about 40-50 feet.
(Project SIGN "Initial Report," chart, Incident No.
40)
