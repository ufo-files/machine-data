(McDonald list) Uncorrelated Target With Diffused EchoesBrad
Sparks:
Feb. 27, 1961; Yuma, Ariz.(BBU)
(McDonald list)

Dan Wilson:
At 1041 local time A2/C Paul E. Reuter and Capt. Morris
Royster of the 864th AC&W Squadron picked up an unusual return
about the
size of a single aircraft moving slowly at about 42,000
feet on a MPS-14
radar. The object was moving at only 20 knots and it left a trail of
diffused echoes
extending behind and slanting downward. The rate of descent was
approximately 300 feet per
minute. Total time of observation was 1 hour and 48 minutes.
