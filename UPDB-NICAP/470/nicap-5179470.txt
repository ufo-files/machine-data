707 Picks Up 12-15 TargetsDate:
Mon, 14 Sep 2009 06:31:15 -0700 (PDT)
Dan Wilson:
August 1, 1965; West of Topeka, Kansas
During the night a TWA Aircraft (707) in flight picked up
12-15 targets on Air-Intercept Radar flying
toward their aircraft in formation at high speed on 50 mile scope. They
changed to 20 mile scope and observed the
objects approaching in formation. The pilot, co-pilot, and engineer all
witnessed this cleary on the scope. Aircraft
passed the objects but did not see them visually. All parties concerned
are very reputable. They asked that no names be
used and that no publicity be given this sighting. Case includes 2
films. (Dan Wilson)
