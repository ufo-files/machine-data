On Collision Course & Definitely Not AircraftBrad Sparks:
Feb. 20, 1952; Mt. Diablo, Calif. (BBU)
11:30 p.m. USAF pilot Montgomery and copilot of B-25 bomber
saw bright yellow light on collision course climb and accelerate.
(Project 1947; FUFOR Index)

Dan Wilson:
Feb. 20, 1952, Mt. Diablo, Calif. (BBU)
11:30 p,m. Captain John Watkins (pilot) and Captain Richard
Montgomery (co-pilot) of B-25 #8820, observed a bright yellow light
between Stockton and
Sacramento, Calif., while flying the aircraft at 8,000 feet. As
the object approached the plane and passed in front of it the
pilot stated that the object was definitely not another aircraft. The
object first came into view on an apparent collision course with the
B-25. No other aircraft in the area & no radar returns.
