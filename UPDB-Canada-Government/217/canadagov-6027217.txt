1 stationary, delta-shaped object with a metallic gleam & reflective surface

City: Chibougameau
Shape: delta
Size: several hundred feet
Special comments: Identified by the investigator: "The direction of your observation, 30o North West, passed by Roberval, Quebec, for many years, American scientists there do experiments using big balloons, “SKYNOOK,” to raise instruments in the high atmosphere. Roberval is what we describe “a conjunction of points.” It has the same magnetic coordinates in the northern hemisphere than an American Antarctic observatory in the southern hemisphere. 
These balloons are big of about 250-300 feet and can reach high altitudes. They appear bright in the rays of the setting sun, and one can see them long after the sunset."...
