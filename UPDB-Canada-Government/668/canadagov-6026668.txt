1 bluish-grey, bell shaped UFO with 2 flashing red lights and a white beam of light in the middle, hovered low then moved slowly 

City: Rimbey
Shape: bell; other witnesses could not offer any description of the shape stating it was too dark to tell
Special comments: IFO as two aircraft flying in formation - their ground speed was reduced to almost nil because of strong head winds. Because in formation, the side lights were extinquished leaving only the flashing red tail lights. The apparent movement and tipping was a result of the aircraft trying to find a less resistant slip-stream to fly in. Also two helicopters were in the area at that time and date.
