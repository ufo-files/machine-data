1 very bright, bluish white object moving rapidly toward the horizon at a sharp angle

City: Middle Stewiacke
Shape: Unspecified
Size: 1/4 the size of the moon
Special comments: Numerous fireball sightings reported to Search & Rescue Hqtrs during the late evening of 2-May-80. Three such sightings had witnesses describe it as bright, bluish green and "sparkling" and extremely bright. The three sightings were made by persons who are believed to be reliable and it should be noted the object was bright enough to attract the attention while driving.
