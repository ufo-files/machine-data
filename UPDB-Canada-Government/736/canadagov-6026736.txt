3 events - 1) an object flew overhead so fast that they could not get an accurate description. 2) 45 mins later, 1 object with a green light on the top and red light on the bottom side, was stationary in the air then moved slowly to the left and then to the right. 3) 1 hour later, 1 beamed light came through a vent in the top of the camper and moved around to the front of the truck, moving faster than if held by hand and "came through even where there were no windows".

City: Gustafson Lake
Special comments: The 3rd event occurred while the witnesses were in the camper. The said the camper shook and they felt like their bodies were rising in the air. At 10:30 a.m they heard a loud noise like a jet taking off. All the men were so scared they did not look out.
