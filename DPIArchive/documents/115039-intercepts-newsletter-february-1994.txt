Yes, its that time of the year again. Snow is on the ground and the sky is grey. It will still be awhile till the swallows and robins
return but you still can do a bit of bird watching in the middle of winter. The kind of birds I am talking about don't have feathers. Their
songs aren't melodic but they are music to one's ears. Instead they sprout aluminum wings and their calls are interlaced with radio static.
They are of course, the birds belonging to the U.S. military.
As any good ornithologist knows, a good ear is a must in bird watching. Identify the bird call and you identify the bird. The same
goes for military monitoring. Having an up-to-date call sign list makes all the difference, So without further delay here is our exclusive
list of military identifiers.
Please note: Our list has expanded from over a couple of hundred to over a thousand. Because the listings have grown so large,
they won't fit in one issue, therefore next month we will continue the listings along with our regular features.
As you read this list, keep in mind the hard work that has gone in behind it. A very special thanks goes to Gary Moncur who compiled
the list. Thanks Gary from all of us !
