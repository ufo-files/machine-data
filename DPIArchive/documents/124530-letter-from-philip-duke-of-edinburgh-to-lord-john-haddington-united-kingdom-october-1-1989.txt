                                                       BALMORAL CASTLE


                                                       1 October 1989


              I am relpying to your letter to the Queen about the
          Corn Circles Phenomenon since I have been taking a
          rather particular interest in the subject. We were both
          fascinated by your letter and tape and by the account of
          the "Encounter" at Cheesefoet Head.

              You and your colleagues have discovered quite a lot
          about the circles, but they remain a mystery. I confess
          that I am delighted that they have not been explained. I
          think it is good for our scientific and pragmatic souls
          to discover that there are some things that are beyond
          our comprehension. I think we all need a bit of humbling
          in this 'know-all' age.

              I think the great thing about these circles is that,
          unlike ghosts or flying saucers, which are only seen and
          reported by the fortunate few, these Phenomena are THERE
          to be photographed and to be seen by whoever feels like
          going to have a look. They cannot be argued away by
          sceptical scientists 11

              By all means go on studying them, but I dearly hope
          that the explanation will never be found II
