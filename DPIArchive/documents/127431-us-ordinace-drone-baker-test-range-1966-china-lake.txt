On January 18, 1966 (4:55 PM) two civil service surveyors were involved in a UFO sighting
which took place at the United States Naval Ordnance Test Station (Mojave Desert California).
As the surveyors were taking measurements, a strange "hum" was heard, and an unusual object
was seen approaching from the north. The UFO was no more than 150 feet off of the desert
surface. One of the surveyors got a good look at it by using his 40X sighting telescope. The
strange craft eventually passed overhead, travelling at approximately 150 mph. It was described
as being dull black in color, with no noticeable lights or control surfaces. Its exterior
configuration resembled that of a modern skate-board. The craft measured twelve feet long, and
eight feet wide. It had three unusual raised surfaces running along the top surface. No heat or
noise was identified by the two primary eyewitnesses. A strange "wake" was observed
immediately behind the craft which resembled the "shock waves" seen during high speed wind
tunnel tests. Electromagnetic effects of the craft may have included a seriously damaged starter
motor in a nearby truck. A report was sent via radio to the range control office with no success.
