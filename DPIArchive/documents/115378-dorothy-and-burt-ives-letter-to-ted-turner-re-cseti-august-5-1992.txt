August 5, 1992
Dear
Please excuse the familiar term of addressing you but since the advent of CNN
and the many interviews of you with Larry King we feel as though we know you.
Indeed, through our many years of concert tours throughout the world one of the
requests of our agents, along with apple juice and fruit in the dressing room and
the three-bedroom suite, was that the hotel had CNN cable.
Over the years we've cheered your pioncer spirit providing the world with news
and, for Americans, that wonderful touch of home.
Now the time for inter-galactical communication has come - and the reason for
this letter.
The enclosed SETI program is for your study. We recommend most highly,
young Dr. Steven Greer. We find him remarkable and knowledgeable (much like
yourself and would really appreciate it if you would arrange a meeting with him.
We firmly believe CNN should be the launching pad for this worthwhile and
news-worthy project.
Yours sincerely,
Enclosure
