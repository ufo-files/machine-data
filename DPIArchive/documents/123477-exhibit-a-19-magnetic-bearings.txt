Magnetic Bearing Technologies, Inc. ("MBT") has
developed an entirely new class of magnetic bearings
that are inherently stable. MBT's Autoactive™™ bearings
generate their own power, greatly reducing the risk
of rotor delevitations and eliminating the need for a
UPS system. Because the position sensing, power
generation and force transducer functions of MBT's
Autoactive bearings are performed by a few simple
components within the bearing envelope itself, there
