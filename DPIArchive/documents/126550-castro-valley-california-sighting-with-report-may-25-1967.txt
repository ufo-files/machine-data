UFO sighting over Castro Valley CA.
On the night of May 25, 1967 (at approximately 10:30 PM), Mr.
and his wife
witnessed a strange UFO from their porch in Castro Valley California. The duration of the
sighting was approximately four minutes. Noticing a bright flickering red light which was
moving closer, Mr.
grabbed a pair of 8X30 binoculars. The craft measured
approximately 50-60 feet in diameter, with a three foot hole located in the bottom center. A
bright red flame which appeared to be 15-20 feet long was shooting down from the opening. The
strange object did not exhibit any signs of noise, and hovered at an altitude of about 5,000 feet
(only the bottom was visible). The primary witness stated that the flickering light from the flame
illuminated the bottom surface of the UFO which appeared to be constructed of "heavy armor
plated steel", and made use of multiple rivets (see original sketch). Source: NICAP/CUFOS
archives
