January 27, 1994
Dear Steven,
Your letter of January 11 has been received by Mr.
Laurance S. Rockefeller and Mr. Henry I. Diamond.
They have
asked me to thank you for writing and to respond on their
behalf.
Mr. Rockefeller appreciates your keeping him informed on
the work of CSETI and for inviting him and Mr.
Henry Diamond
to attend a private briefing in Los Angeles on February 12th.
Unfortunately, both Mr. Rockefeller and Mr. Diamond are
unable to
attend
special meeting next
month due to
conflicts in schedules.
However, they remain interested in
the activities of
CSETI and look forward to being kept
informed.
Please be assured of our best wishes.
Sincerely,
Steven M. Greer, M.D.
International Director
CSETI
