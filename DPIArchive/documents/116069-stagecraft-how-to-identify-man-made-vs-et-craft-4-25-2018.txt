"STAGECRAFT"
(Exposing the Imposters)
A technical scientific white paper report written by Michael Schratt 4/25/18
The following criteria/bullet items can be used to identify man-man (terrestrial based) UAP
(Unidentified Arial Phenomena):
1) Test flights occurring on Thursdays. (ref Hudson Valley Boomerang 3/24/83, "Aurora
air-quakes" over Los Angeles 10/31/91, 4/16/92, 1/30/92)
2) Mechanical looking understructure of craft consisting of "tubes pipes and cylinders" also
"cross-beams and girders" similar in appearance to a "truss-bridge" or "erector set".
(Hudson Valley Boomerang 1982-1989, also reference Belgium Triangle 11/7/89 &
3/30/90).
3) Bottom of craft resembling "stacked building blocks" or "Legos". (ref Southern Illinois
Flying Triangle 1/5/00)
4) Bottom of craft composed of what appears to be HVAC "vents" or "grill-work".
(reference Monique O'Driscoll sighting Kent NY 2/26/83)
5) Transparent or translucent panels on lower surface. (Taconic State Parkway sighting
3/24/83)
6) Multi-colored flashing lights. (Monique O'Driscoll sighting 2/26/83)
7) Lights that go off in sequence: red, blue, green, yellow, white (ref Monique O'Driscoll
sighting 2/6/83)
8) Low frequency "humming" noise similar to an electrical transformer or "sewing
machine". (reference Hudson Valley Boomerang 1982-1989, Belgium Triangle 11/7/89,
Southern Illinois Flying Triangle 1/5/00)
9) Static on radios as craft enters local area which then disappears as craft departs aka
electromagnetic disturbances. (reference Ed Burns sighting Millwood NY 3/24/83, and
Monique O'Driscoll sighting 2/6/83)
