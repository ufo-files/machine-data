the normal combustion cycle.
The advantages of the rotary are small
size and weight and extremely smooth
running. (Many drivers have been
known to turn the starter switch when

There are two disadvantages. First,

urrent rotary engines deliver fuel econ-

ractically unbreakable in the bargain,

• Electric-Electric cars have been with
us a long time. Unfortunately, most of
them are little more than golf carts or
retirement-community three-wheelers.

are absolutely quiet, they use no fuel
(directly), and they cause no air pollu-

are range and performance. So far, there
isn't even an experimental model that
will go fifty miles per hour for fifty miles.

that just doesn't make it for most driv-

On the credit side; the gas turbine
engine is incredibly smooth, has good
possibilities for emission control and
will last almost forever. On the debit
side, it doesn't have particularly good
fuel economy, and it requires even rarer
machinery for its manufacture than the
rotary. It also tends not to have the great-

There have been a lot of advancements
