         MEMORANDUM

         TO:

         FROM:


         DATE:           January 25, 1994

         RE:            Some thoughts for your meeting


         The fi rst decision is to determine what Is the goal of the effort. The range of
         alternatives Is quite immense, e.g.:

            • Impact U.S. Government decision making on such issues as confidentiality of
              previously acquired information.
            .     influence Governmental leaders worldwide to a given course of action In
                  response to any communication received.

               • Persuade members of the human race either selectively, e.g. Americans,
                 newspaper editors, Tibetan Monks, etc. or universally, to accept the
                 communication as in fact being from some extraterrestrial source.

         As is abundantly obvious, each of these goals would lead you down a quite different
         path as regards the planning process and, given the reality that probably each of
         these three and numerous other possible alternatives as well, are at least objectives If
         not the ultimate goal, it underscores the complexity of the process that will be
         needed.

         Whatever the goal though quite possibly the major focus at least initially needs to be
         on source credibility, i.e. from whence is this information coming and that entity
         believable. We suggest this concern only in the context of the source and not In the
         context of the information itself. Obviously, only with survey research can we know
         for sure, but as is evident in the Roswell book, there seemed to be an acceptance
         that 'flying saucers' from other planets could exist and most remarkable, at least
         based on that book, they seemed to generate very little fright within the populace.

         Therefore, if the basic message itself is not by and of itself unbelievable, then the
         key to its believability is 'who, or what organization, Is saying it?'

              We believe this is the most important challenge/decision the group is facing.

              We would not pretend to know who/what this source should be but we do strongly
              believe that it would be vastly preferred if its not government driven, at least not the
              U.S. Government, or any domestic brand politicians. That would be even more
              true In our minds of any one connected with the United Nations.

              Having said that, a real search listing all possible possibilities and then aggressively
              analyzing the pluses and minuses, strengths and weaknesses, of each in a variety of
              contexts will clearly be necessary.

              Beyond this of course comes the tedium of planning as to tactics and timing.
              However, until these initial two questions are resolved, I.e.

                     What is the primary goal so to know the primary audience?

                     Who/what will be the source that reveals this phenomenon?

              and the resources available to work with are known, it would seem premature to go
              to that level of detail.

              We.hope you find these very initial thoughts of some value and please give us a call
              when you return.
