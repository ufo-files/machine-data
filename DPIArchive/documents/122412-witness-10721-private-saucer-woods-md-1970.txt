Date: Fall, 1970 or 1971
Time: 8:30-9PM
Name: J
Occupation: Fire Fighter, Hazardous Material Specialist & Ex-Paramedic
Age: 50 years old (was 19 years of age when the incident occurred)
Location: Industrial area near the Patapsco River in Baltimore, Maryland, USA
Weather: Cloudy and overcast with light rain. No wind
Number of objects observed: One

         I first saw the object over the Patapsco River at a distance and at eye level. I was
standing on the side of a hill in a wooded area. It was just starting to rain. At a distance, I
first thought it was a plane with engine trouble. It appeared to be slowing down and
looked as if it would crash on the hillside right where I stood.
         As it approached more and more slowly, I could see a green light on either side
and I could hear a strange noise unlike a jet, helicopter or airplane. The noise was a hum
that went up and down and every minute or two, there was a sound like a muffled
backfire. I kept rubbing my eyes with one hand as I had my umbrella in the other.
         As it got closer, I could see its shape but didn't want to accept what my eyes were
seeing. I kept thinking, "It's got to be a helicopter or a plane." As it passed over my
head, a red light in the center of the saucer began to pulse once every few seconds and it
came to a dead stop. Its distance now was only 150 ft and I was at a 60 to 50 degree
angle to it. It was close enough that I could see it really well, and tried as I could, I
couldn't grow wings or a tail rotor on it.
         At this point I was way beyond scared. I was terrified. It started to wobble slightly
and rotate around slowly, kind of like a gyroscope when it slows. It turned slowly until a
window, shaped like a trapezoid, faced my direction. In the window I could clearly see a
silhouette of a humanoid form from the chest up, though the head appeared larger. The
light glowing from inside was a dull greenish yellow. I was looking at it, and it was
looking back at me. I tried to run by couldn't. My knees were like butter. The red pulsing
light on the bottom felt like it penetrated my body. Maybe that was fear, but I had the
impression the occupant wanted me to move from the wooded area to the open field. I
wished I was someplace else, anyplace else. At this point I put the black umbrella
between me and the saucer. I repeated over and over to myself, "There's no such thing,
there's no such thing" but the whole time I was saying this, looking at the ground, I could
still hear the humming noise with the muffled backfire along with the light rain hitting
my umbrella. I could also still see the pulsing red light reflecting off of the leaves and
hear the rain tapping on them. After a few minutes of saying that there no such thing, I
pulled the umbrella back. It was still there; still looking at me.
         The color of the saucer was a silver gray on the upper half. The bottom half had a
strange look to it. The center of the bottom looked like liquid mercury. It was very
reflective, like it was reflecting distorted images of the trees and field underneath. The
outer edge, even just past the edge had a fuzzy kind of distortion. It still,had a slight
wobble while hovering. I didn't see any markings on the saucer or projections. Just a
green, non-pulsing, light on each side, a red pulsing light on the bottom, and a trapezoidal
window on the front with the occupant looking out at me. After what seemed like 5 to 10
minutes, it stopped the slow wobble and muffled backfire sound. I could not swear to it,
but I think the red pulsing light stopped as it moved off slowly at first, then accelerated to
a speed I have never seen anything move at before. It was moving west and made a right
angle turn to the north at a super high speed. There was a trail following it. Not a jet trail,
but the trail of the light from the saucer as it disappeared.
        After it left, my legs worked again. I ran home and told my mom everything. She
said, "Ok. What kind of drugs are you on?" After all these 30 years she believes me. I
have told this story many times to friends, co-workers, and family. People still look at
me like I have two heads. When I was 17 years old, I had 20/20 vision. My first job I ever
had was a fish painter for a taxidermist. I had to duplicate what a fish looks like when it
is alive. I was an artist. In high school, I received a full scholarship to an art college. All
of this experience helped hone my observation skills so that I can recall details.
         The psychological effects after the event were sleepless nights, body tremors, and
nightmares. For 10 years after this happened, whenever I told anyone the story, my legs
and hands would tremble. It was like I was reliving it all over. It felt like how I would
imagine post traumatic stress syndrome to be like. It is funny, as you age, you tend to be
forgetful, except for those events that deeply affected your life. This did affect me deeply,
and that has kept me interested in extraterrestrial life, UFO's, crop-circles, abductions,
and what forms of propulsion systems could propel these craft across such large distances
at such great speeds without the effects of gravity on the occupants. How could they
cross such large distances without refueling? At what speed do they travel? Sub light?
Faster than light? Can they travel forward and backward through time, or travel across
dimensions, as easily as we jump in a car and go to the store? Are they keeping an eye on
us so we do not annihilate ourselves or the beautiful planet we live on in our infancy?
         After the event, Tasked the five guys I was with that night if anyone of them had
seen anything. They all said they had not. It has taken me thirty years to write this story.
