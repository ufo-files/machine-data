UFO CASE FILES REVEALED PART 11
Virginia Beach VA. November 6, 1976 - Mrs. Barbara Nielsen spotted a bell shaped
UFO which hovered approximately two feet off the ground in her backyard. The craft had a light
on top that blinked on and off slowly from red to white. It had rectangular windows near the
bottom which emitted a phosphorescent light. The object rotated in a counter-clockwise
direction, while it tipped up along its vertical axis. The UFO measured five feet high, and five
feet wide. The primary eyewitness noticed a faint humming sound emanating from the craft. The
bottom of the object was comprised of multiple yellow colored lights. There were black symbols
running across the center section (similar to the "newsflash" text seen in Times Square).
Source - Wendelle Stevens collection
