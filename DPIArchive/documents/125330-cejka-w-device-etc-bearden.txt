ABSTRACT
A high power semiconductor amplifier which may be
used as a power amplifier for low frequency apparatus
such as radio transmitters and/or welding apparatus. A
plurality of transistor driven amplifier sections are
commonly driven by a square wave source and are
coupled in patallel to a common load through loosely
forming the power combiner circuit. The loose cou-
pling provides a desired low distributive capacitance
and high leakage inductive reactance. Each amplifier
driver section moreover is coupled to the transformer
by a DC blocking capacitor having a selected capaci-
center of the operating frequency band. Furthermore.
the combiner circuit is coupled to the load by means
of a harmonic filter having a series inductance input
which acts to present a high reactive impedance to
harmonics in the square wave voltage applied thereto
