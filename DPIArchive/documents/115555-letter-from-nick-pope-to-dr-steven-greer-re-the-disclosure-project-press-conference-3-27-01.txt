March 27, 2001
Dear Steven,
Thank you for your letter dated March 13, concerning the forthcoming
Disclosure Project events. I apologise for the delay in replying.
I am not sure at this stage if I will be able to attend any of the events in
Washington, because of uncertainties over whether I will be able to take
leave from work during the period concerned. I should know the position
within the next couple of weeks. In the event that I am not able to attend,
I shall send you a written statement concerning my official research and
investigations into UFOs, and my belief that this is a real phenomenon
that raises serious defence and national security issues.
My funds are limited too, so I would need The Disclosure Project to meet
my travel and accommodation expenses; and because of pressures on my
time, I would ask that one of your team make the bookings, consulting me
over the details beforehand. I am sorry if this all sounds a little vague,
but perhaps you or Linda (to whom I am copying this fax) could contact
me in a week or so, to discuss all this.
I hope preparatious for the various events are going well, and would be
interested to know details of the various witnesses, VIPs and media who
have already agreed to attend.
I wish you,
all the very best.
and everyone else at The Disclosure Project
Yours sincerely,
