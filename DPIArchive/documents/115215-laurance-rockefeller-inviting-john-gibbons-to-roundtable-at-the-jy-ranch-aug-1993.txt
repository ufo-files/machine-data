Augusty4, 1993
Dear
This letter is to invite you (or a representative) to
participate in an informal Roundtable discussion, which I am
hosting, to be held September 13-15, 1993, at our JY Ranch near
Jackson, Wyoming in the Grand Teton National Park, to discuss
various aspects of unidentified flying objects (UFOs) and
extraterrestrial intelligence (ETI) phenomena.
Mary and I would
be pleased to have you as our guest at the Ranch.
The purpose of the meeting is to assess the current state of
knowledge about UFO and ETI phenomena generally. and in
particular, the extent of information which may or may not be in
the hands of the federal government. One of the purposes of the
assessment is to develop productive ways to encourage the
Administration to include information of UFO and ETI phenomena in
its current review of previously classified information, and the
need to classify information so extensively.
The format will be informal but focused.
Six students of
UFO phenomena have been invited to make presentations.
invited presenters are Steven M. Greer, Linda Moulton Howe, Bruce
Maccabee, R. Leo Sprinkle, Jill Tarter (NASA Ames Research
Center), and Keith Thompson.
Each presenter will be requested to
take 12
to 15 minutes to summarize cogently what he or she
considers to be the most important points of their research.
others invited to attend are Dr. John Mack, Dr. Peter Sturrock, a
representative of the Science Advisor to the President. and
several of my asscciates.
After the initial presentations, the
meeting will be open for discussion of the data presented in
relationship to four general themes:
U.S. government attitudes and policies toward the
specific research areas, and strategies for influencing government
policy in che direction of openness on the issues.
2. International attitudes toward specific research areas
and strategies for influencing open international cooperation on
the issues.
Assessment of alleged psychological, economic and other
barriers to a government policy of openness on the subject.
