Colusa CA

September 10, 1976

12:45 AM

           39 year old mechanic was watching a late night TV program

Wife and two small children (ages 8 and 10) were asleep

At approximately 12:45 AM the TV went dead as well as the air conditioner

Thinking that a fuse was blown he went outside to check the circuit breakers

As he approached the corner of his mobile home, he became aware of the static electrical effects
on his body

The hair on his chest arms and head began to stand up

As he looked up, he saw a large 140 diameter craft hovering 50 feet above him

In a total state of shock, he got a good look at the intricate details of the craft

The craft had no rivets, bolt, screws or seams

The dome on top had vertical ribs (similar to a lemon juice squeezer)

There were six dangling objects hanging down, which looked like heavy flexible conduit with
frayed ends

These were hanging down approximately 6-8 feet from the edge of the craft

The conduit measured 2-3 inches in diameter

As the craft moved towards the edge of his property these appendages immediately retracted into
the bottom of the craft

Two hook-like arms also retracted upward, but not completely into the hull of the craft

At the same time as the conduit retracted, two small doors opened up on each side of the craft

From these doors came a single curved piece of tubing with a light at the end which was pointing
downward

A powerful white spotlight was then seen beaming to the ground

Then the craft moved towards a nearby home
He could now see two smaller UFOs that were approximately half the size of the first hovering
directly above the 500,000 volt power lines

Each craft appeared to be resting on the power lines

Each craft was projected a powerful column of white light on either side to the top of the power
poles

The power lines were glowing red hot

The primary eyewitness ran into the house and woke up his wife

Rushed to the window facing the back field and observed the craft brilliantly lighting up a nearby
home

Then the large craft shot off at an incredible speed towards the foothills area

Foothills are 20 miles away, and the UFO covered this distance away about 2 or three seconds

Then he saw the two smaller UFOs near the power lines rapidly break away at the same time

Fearing for his life and the safety of his family, the   decided to leave the area in case the large
craft returned

In a frantic case to escape, he pulled on a pair of old jeans, and grabbed his wife and children,
and headed for the door

At this point, the TV and air conditioner came back on

Pilling his whole family into his truck, he backed out of the driveway and purposely left his
headlights off so that he didn’t attract the attention of the large craft

As he raced down the blacktop road travelling up to 90 mph, he noticed the large craft following
his truck

The large UFO was then seen on one side of the truck, then pass to the other side

Reaching his friend’s house, he slammed on the brakes, and began pounding on the door of Les
