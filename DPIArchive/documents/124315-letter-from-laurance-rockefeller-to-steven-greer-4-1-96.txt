                                              April 1st, 1996


Dear Steven,

     It was disturbing to receive your letter of March 13
outlining your concerns about Bootsie Galbraith's role in our
UFO efforts. As you know, we have supported a number of
people in this field, including      John Mack, and Bootsie's
work through the BSW Foundation.

     It has been my hope that we could engender a spirit of
cooperation among the various groups interested, not only in
UFOs, but in extraterrestrial intelligence generally, and
those working in programs to expand the human spirit. One of
our primary efforts has been to bring about more coordination
and cooperation among these groups and I am sorry that we
have not been successful in this instance.

  :' Although we do not necessarily concur with all the
conclusions of the briefing--document, we do feel it is a
useful undertaking. , I hope that any future editions might
give full credit to any of those that contributed to it in a
meaningful way. Although I supported this project
financially, I have not been involved in-the management or
direction of it, and it does not seem useful that I become
involved in any disagreements that may ensue from its
publication.

     I know you wrote out of concern for our shared efforts
and I hope we may have occasion to continue to work
cooperatively. With appreciation for your work,

                         Since/fly,


Steven M: Greer, M.D.
International Director, CSETI       ;,:
