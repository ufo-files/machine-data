 Yes, I was a military police oﬃcer in 2010 when my Alabama National Guard military police unit the 152nd
 Military Police Company was sent to Eglin Air Force Base located in Fort Walton, Florida. My unit basically had 2
 missions when we were sent there. First, it was for marijuana eradication in and around the base because Eglin
 is a huge base. Secondly, we were to work jointly with the Air Force Security Forces patrolling the garrison
 because our unit was getting ready to deploy overseas and our command wanted us to brush up on our skills
 before we deployed. Our first day there when we were briefed by the head of the Air Force Security Forces
 during guard mount we were told that if we were assigned to the marine patrol for whatever days we worked
 those areas in the waters surrounding the base that occasionally we would have to retrieve “space junk”. Most of

 surrounding Eglin. The fact that it happens enough that we were even told about it was incredible to me because
 why would anything from space be falling in and around a military base in Florida? One day, I was patrolling with
 my Air Force counterpart to an area on Eglin called Site C6. You can actually Google Site C6 to learn about some
 of it’s capabilities but you have to have a secret security clearance to visit the site itself. All military police have to
 have a secret security clearance. It’s located about 15 miles deep inside the base. When I walked into the SCIF
 (Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility) there was a sentence written in gold letters I’ll never forget, it
 read, “We monitor all space activity terrestrial and extraterrestrial from planet earth”. It was then I learned that we
 are not alone and military even monitors their activities. I could not take one more step inside the facility because
 I was so shocked at what I had just read literally on the wall. The radar at Site C6 is one of the most powerful
 radars on the Earth and can detect objects in space the size of a basketball as far out as 20,000 miles. The
 facility has now been turned over to the U.S. Space Force. At night, on two occasions I can remember, when my
 unit was back at camp located just a mile away from the U.S. Army Ranger Camp located on Eglin, our cadre
 made us all go inside because they did not want us to see what was flying around in the sky above the base. On
 numerous occasions I observed unmarked jet aircraft taking oﬀ from the base and when I asked the security
 forces oﬃcer I was with about the unmarked planes, he said all the security forces referred to them as “secret
squirrel” planes. I served active duty in the Marine Corps from 1994-1998 and Army from 2005 to 2006 on active
duty orders serving overseas for Operation Enduring Freedom. I was in the Alabama National Guard from 2003-
2006 and again from 2009-2012. I am also a disabled veteran. My two weeks I spent on Eglin Air Force Base
