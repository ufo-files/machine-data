GULF BREEZE UFO
On Wednesday, November 11, 1987, Ed Walters, a Gulf Breeze, Florida
building contractor, was alone at home working at his office desk. Through a
large window just above his desk he noticed an odd glowing object in the sky
approaching his house from the west. He could see the UFO over a house
across the street. He then went to the front door of his house to get a better
look at the UFO. He looked out the door and decided to take some
photographs of the UFO. He went back into his house to get his camera and
then went out on his front porch to photograph the UFO. He took four
photographs of the UFO as it moved slowly across the sky. The UFO
appeared to be about 200 feet away over a house across the street. He ran out
of film and went back in his house to reload his camera.
When Mr. Walters stepped back out on his front porch to take more
photographs the
UFO appeared to be moving closer to his location and he
went out onto the road in front of his house to get a better photograph of it as
it passed overhead. As he raised his camera to take a photograph a blue beam
from the UFO enveloped him and he was unable to move. He was aware of a
humming noise in his head and he was seeing pictures of dogs in some type of
book or catalog with something like a word written below each picture. As he
struggled to free himself from the blue beam he started to rise above the road
and heard a voice in his head telling him to stop struggling. His next conscious
memory was of falling to the ground from a height of several feet. It appeared
that who or whatever was in the UFO had attempted and failed to abduct him.
This
was
the first in a long series of UFO and UFO entity encounters
experienced by Mr. Walters.

in color from gray-blue to orange brown. They made no sound. They were
about 28 feet in diameter and 15 feet high. They were circular in shape with a
4 to 5 foot wide disk-shaped midsection centered between two pie pan-shaped
structures at the top and bottom. The bottom section also incorporated a
flattened lens-shaped structure about 14 feet in diameter which was illuminated
in the center. The midsection and the bottom section of the UFO were
