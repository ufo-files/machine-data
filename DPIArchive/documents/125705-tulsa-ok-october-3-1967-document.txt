Tulsa Oklahoma UFO sighting


   On the night of October 3, 1976 (9:00 PM) five eyewitnesses observed an unusual UFO
hovering over Tulsa Oklahoma (I44 and Harvard). The object was described as being sixty feet
in diameter, with a dome that rose to a total height of eight feet. The outside rim appeared to
rotate in a counter-clockwise direction. The exterior of the craft had rectangular shaped windows
through which a bright white light could be seen. Two steady lights (one red and one white) were
located on the upper surface of the craft. After approximately ten minutes, the interior white
lights went out, and the craft was observed to disappear behind a group of buildings. Source:
