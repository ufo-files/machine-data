The Disclosure Project
Dear Dr Greer:
I would first like to thank you for your book. A very welcome addition to my library.
I would then like to apologize for not getting you the copy of the tape I promised when you where up here.
My tape recorder died and I needed to get a new one. With work and the holidays I just did not get around
to buying a new one. This tape is the debate between LtCol Whedon and Maj Kehoe on the Armstrong
Circle Theater back in the 50's. Hope you will enjoy.
While stationed at Wright - Patterson AFB, Ohio I rented a room from Christine Whedon, the widow of Lt
Col Whedon. She was in her 70's and needed help around her 5 acre plot, so I helped with many chore:
like cutting grass, and chopping wood. Being from England, she loved to have dinner parties and as her
"adopted" son I was usually invited. Many of the people that came were civilians that had retired from
Wright - Patterson years ago. Many I am sure worked closely. with and knew Lt Col Whedon. One in
particular was Dr Adolf Raum who, I am convinced, had personal knowedge of the Roswell affair. He is
now deceased. I do not recall what his doctorate was in. He was at the first atomic bomb test and was
personal friends with Dr Oppenheimer, the father of the A-bomb. One night I raised the Roswell issue to
him as I asked about the little green men that were supposedly on ice at Wright - Patterson. I vividly recall
his face turning white. It was extremely evident to me that he was very uncomfortable about me raising the
topic. He did not provide many details, but he did say that the objects discovered were absolutely not
weather balloons. He finished that particular conversation by saying in a very terse tone that I will not
forget "We will not talk about this again Arnie. Understand?". I never again raised the topic.
The following I think you might find interesting also. As a youngster, maybe 3 or 4 years old, I had a
dream in which I pictured myself in a silver disc of some sort above the Earth. Looking down, I could see
the clouds and oceans. All in vivid color. I told my folks about this and their reply was that I had a
nightmare. Fine, but where did I get the scenes in my head? Back in the early 40's, we did not have TV and
books where seldom seen in our house. I lived with my grandparents and were of the old Norwegian
school. Hardworking and they had little or no time for books. I rarely told others about this dream and then
only close family members. I can still recall this dream to this day.Fast forward to the 70's. I was on an
inspection trip to Davis Monthan AFB, AZ. Onenight I was having dinner at the Officers Club and was by
myself. Suddenly, a young captain approaches me and asks "Colonel, might I join you?". The place was
packed and I had a spare chair so I of course said yes. After eating, we were enjoying a drink when out of
the blue he says "You have a vision that bothers you? " Of course I said no because I had no "vision" that
bothered me. He then said " Let me rephrase that. You have a dream that bothers you?". He then went on to
describe my dream that I told about above. He then added that in a previous life I was a physicist with an
advanced party coming to earth and we crashed landed on Earth. By the way, I was a physics/math major in
college. He ended up by saying that some day I would remember more details about the inside of the craft
and the uniform I was wearing. I think I told him that I thought he was weirder than I was.
Again, thanks for the book and I hope you enjoy the tape.
Sincaral
