CHAPTER FIV

                   The Foundations of Inter-planetary Unity


The challenge of establishing a framework and creating the foundation for

relations between human and extra-terrestrial life forms is one of the greatest

challenges that our people on this planet have faced. Because over the

course of human history we have witnessed how extremely difficult it is for

peace and cooperation and unity to be created amongst the peoples of the

world. The culture of tribalism, of inter-Nicene warfare, of racism, of religious

hatred and animosity, of nationalism, of ideological conflicts such as those

between capitalism and communism, and all the other forces which have

dominated world history throughout recorded time indicates that there is a

fundamental challenge to the human spirit in establishing peaceful relations

amongst diverse beings..
