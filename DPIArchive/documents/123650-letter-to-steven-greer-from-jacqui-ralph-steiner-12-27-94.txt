■      Pacific Rim Productions ■


Stephen Greer
CSETI
1


December 27th, 1994


Dear Stephen:


Further to our telephone conversation this morning, there are three areas we would like
to focus our attention on during our meeting in San Francisco.

•   To realistically assess how we might enable astronauts and other knowledgeable
    personnel to come forward. And, how best to disseminate this information, given the
    complexity of the situation.

•   How we can assist CSETI, given our respective media backgrounds, in advancing
    CSETI's agenda of educating the public at large, nationally and internationally.

•   To discuss my current TV project, and how you may care to participate.


Saturday night, January 7, has been set aside for a dinner and discussion on these
topics. I suggest we get together around 6:00 PM at my home and going for as along
as we need to that evening. We have invited the Deans, but are unsure, at this juncture,
as to whether they can attend. I        call Joe Burkes today and see what his availability is
like. In addition, Ralph would like td tape a radio interview with you while you're on your
whistle stop tour.


    ■
I know you are vegetarian. So are We, for the most part, but cook a little fish or chicken
from time to time. Please let me know your preferences. Also, we're available to assist
you in any way you may need to make your stay more comfortable and productive.

We are all very excited to hear your up-to-date news and explore these exciting
opportunities.

Wishing both you and Emily a joyous, healthy and prosperous New Yearl


Sincerely,
