BUSH SAYS HE WILL TELL THE TRUTH ABOUT UFOs IF ELECTED
By Peter Robbins
For Campaign Watch 2000
A breakthrough has occurred in the presidential front-runner's silence on
the UFOs. In an online news conference transcript posted by CNN
correspondent Jonathan Kari, Bush replied, "Sure / will, " when a citizen
questioner asked if he would tell the public "what the hell is going on"
with UFOs. Bush indicated that vice-presidential candidate Dick Chaney
would use his experience as Secretary of Defense to address the issue. One
of the Intemet links to this story as already been broken, but as of this
writing, a remaining link is still online.
Watch 2000 has written repeatedly to all declared presidential candidates
to leam how they would deal with the official, excessive secrecy, which
has surrounded the subject of UFOs for more than fifty years now.
So far, only two have detailed their position on the issue. Early on, a
spokesman for Libertarian Party candidate Harry Browne let us know in no
uncertain ters that if elected, Mr. Browne would release whatever
information the govemment has on this enigma. Dark horse Democratic Party
candidate Dr. Heather Anne Harder told us by phone that she would do the
same and had already included a plank in her platfor to that effect. Bill
Bradley's Issues Director thanked us for our survey and (or) questionnaire
(we sent neither), but regretted that they were too busy to complete the
one wed sent them.
As for the two front runners, Mr. Gore has been the only one to respond to
any of our half a dozen letters, though given the non-content of his brief
email, he neeant have bothered. The note read, "Thanks for taking the
time to e-mail a question to my Interactive Town Hall. Your participation
means a great deal to me, and I will be personally responding to questions
like yours on a regular basis. Please visit the site again soon. Best, Al Gore"
The Bush camp have met all of our queries with silence, but as of yesterday
