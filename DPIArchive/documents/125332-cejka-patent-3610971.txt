ABSTRACT: This invention relates to an all-electric genera-
tor yielding a motional electric field in the space surrounding
the device, but requiring no mechanical movements of its
parts in generating this field. The Theory underlying the
production of such a field postulates that each moving elec-
tron constituting the current in a linear conductor carries with
it a loop of magnetic field energy about it.
