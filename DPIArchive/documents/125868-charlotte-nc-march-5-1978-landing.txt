On the night of March 5, 1978 (8:45 PM) multiple eyewitnesses reported seeing a strange
shaped UFO land 3.2 miles north-east of Douglas airport (within the city limits of Charlotte NC).
The unusual craft (having three tri-pod legs) appears to have been a probe measuring
approximately twelve feet across which landed, and then collected soil samples by way of a
small scoop. Then, the craft turned on various multi-colored lights (red, green yellow, blue)
which drew the attention of eyewitnesses. Next, the UFO maneuvered between trees, and moved
away rapidly at tree-top level in the direction of the airport. No noise was heard in connection
with the craft. Total duration of the sighting was approximately twenty-one seconds. Imprints
made by the craft were warm to the touch when examined by the eyewitnesses. Analysis of the
broken branches near the vicinity of the landing tested negative for radiation. Two separate UFO
sightings were reported on the same night. This CE II case is unique due to the fact that the FAA
fielded multiple calls, and that plaster casts were made of the imprints left by the craft.
