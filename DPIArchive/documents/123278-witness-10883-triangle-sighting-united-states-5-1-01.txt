Event occurred late 1972 to early 1973.
I was stationed at NAS Leeward, Guantanamo Bay, Cuba (GTMO). GTMO is a
semitropical desert environment. It has a two-three week rainy season in
October, and is visited occasionally by hurricanes, but is otherwise hot and
dry. Movies are viewed at night in outdoor theaters base personnel called
"lyceums".
I was watching a movie alone at the NAS lyceum. I have no idea what movie.
The theater at NAS had only one projector, so there was an intermission as
the projectionist changed reels. There was no concession stand and no lights
were turned on during the reel change, so unless one needed to relieve
oneself, the standard practice was to lean ones head back and gaze at the
night sky until the next reel began.
As I gazed at the sky lost in thought, one of the brighter stars streaked
across the sky and came to an abrupt halt. Startled, I continued to watch, as
it streaked off in another direction and came to another abrupt halt. It did
this again, arriving back at its starting point, describing a triangle. It
retraced the first leg of the triangle, paused briefly, then shot straight up
out of sight.
Stunned, I looked around to see if anyone else had seen this. I heard other
viewers asking each other in wonder if they had seen the event, and what
they made of it. They were clearly as bewildered as I. One of the greatest
mistakes of my life was not gathering these witnesses to report or at least
discuss the event. These men and I worked daily around the latest military
attack and fighter aircraft, and there is no way what we saw was of our
technology, then or now.
