Dear Dr. Greer,

     Your letter of February 28 has been received by Mr.
Laurance S. Rockefeller. He has asked me to thank you for
writing and to respond on his behalf during his absence from
New York.

     Mr. Rockefeller appreciates your writing and is pleased
to be of help by sending the enclosed check in the amount of
$5,000   payable   to   the   Center   for  the   Study   of
Extraterrestrial Intelligence (CSETI).

     Please acknowledge receipt of these, funds by signing and
returning the enclosed memorandum to my attention.

     While Mr. Rockefeller is pleased to contribute to your
efforts   concerning   the  existence   of   extraterrestrial
evidence, limitations on his giving program, due to current
circumstances and priorities, mandate that any future support
be at a reduced level. We trust you will be understanding of
the reasons.

     You have our best wishes.


                                      omeroy


Steven M. Greer, M.D.
International Director
CSETI
