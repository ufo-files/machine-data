Io: Steven M. Greer, M.D.
The Disclosure Project
February 11, 2001
Dear Steven,
Thank you for your letter dated December 5 (which only arrived here last
week!) and for sending me a copy of your book, which I shall read with
great interest.
I was interested to hear about progress with the Witness Archive Project,
and am pleased that things seem to be going well. I'm sure that the two
Executive Briefing Summaries will be very impressive, and I hope that
they generate interest at the various briefings that you're planning.
I don't think there's anyone else within Government or the military that
I can suggest as a potential witness. Very few people are involved in this
subject in an official capacity in the United Kingdom, and none of these
people - so far as I am aware - are currently prepared to speak out on
these issues, even using a pseudonym.
I'm not able to send you copies of any documents concerning my service,
as I'm still employed by the Ministry of Defence. Under the terms of the
Official Secrets Act such internal documents may not be passed to anyone
other than an authorised recipient.
I hope all is well with you and your family. Please pass my best wishes to
everyone involved with The Disclosure Project.
Yours sincerely,
