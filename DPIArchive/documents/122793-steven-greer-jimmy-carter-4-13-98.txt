13 April 1998
Dear President Carter,
I am a medical doctor in NC and Director of SETI (the Center for the Study of Extraterrestrial
Intelligence). For years I have admired your courage, your ethics and your commitment to world
peace and human justice.
You are invited to review the enclosed materials relating to a definitive disclosure related to the
UFO/Extraterrestrial issue - a disclosure which will have profound implications for human society,
geo-politics, technology, the environment and the sense of who we are as a people.
For over four years I have been briefing world and national leaders in advance of this disclosure,
including Director of Central Intelligence Jim Woolsey, senior White House staff, UN Secretary
General Boutros Ghali via his wife Mrs. Boutros Ghali, former British Ministry of Defense head
Lord Hill-Norton and numerous members of Congress, among others.
While there is general agreement that a disclosure related to this matter is long overdue, for the
most part these world and national leaders have been deliberately left in the dark by the covert
entities which manage the subject.
I have identified over 150 top-secret military, intelligence and aerospace workers who are willing
to come forward publicly to disclose what they know and end 50 years of secrecy on this matter.
In preparation for this, I invite you to contact me confidentially to discuss this historic
development. We very much feel that, at the time of this disclosure, the world will need men of
good will such as yourself to speak to this matter in a way which is calm, reassuring and which
avoids the pitfalls of xenophobia and militarism for which our species is so noted.
Should it be your wish, I would be honored to come to Atlanta to discuss this matter with you
confidentially.
Respectfully,
Steven M. Greer M.D.
Director of SETI
