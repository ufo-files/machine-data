        As a result of these investigations and studies, and experience gained from
     investigating UFO reports since 1948, the conclusions of Project Blue Book were:

        o No UFO reported, investigated!, and evaluated by the Air Force h& ever given any
     indication of threat to our national security.

         o There has been no evidence submitted to or discovered by the Air Force that
     sightings categorized as "unidentified" represent technological developments or principles
     beyond the range of present day scientific knowledge:

        o There has been no evidence indicating that sightings categorized as "unidentified"
     are extraterrestrial vehicles.
        With the termination of Project Slue Book, the Air Force regulation establishing and
     controlling the program for investigating and analyzing UFOs was rescinded. All
     documentation regarding the former Slue Book investigation was permanently transferred
     to the Modern Military Headquarters Branch, National Archives and Record
     Administration, Washington, D.C. 20403, and is available for public review and cnalysis.

         In 1977, President Jimmy Carter asked the National Aeronautics and Space
fl   Administration (NASA) to look into the possibility of resuming UFO. investigations. After
     studying all the facts available, NASA .decided that nothing would be gained by further
     investigation. The Air Force agrees with that decision. If, however, firm evidence is
     found justifying further investigation, cm appropriate agency will be directed to undertake
     the effort.

         There are a number of universities cnd professional scientific organizations such as the
     American Association for the Advancement of Science that have considered UFO
     phenomena during periodic meetings and seminars. In addition, a list of private
     organizations interested in aerial phenomena may be found in Gale's Encyclopedia of
     Associations (Edition 19, vol. 1, pp 546-547). Such timely review of the situation by
     private groups ensures that sound evidence will not be overlooked by the scientific
     community.
