open-minded researchers who fight for a
reputation in abduction research ignore
these stories, since they represent only a
minor fraction in their files [3]. I think
that abduction cases in which the abductee
reports that he/she was also abducted by
the military are very important for two
reasons:
1.) If the UFO community has evidence
that a covert military/intelligence task
force is involved in the UFO abduction
phenomenon, we would then know that
this phenomenon represents a matter of na-
tional secunity.
2.) The alleged military involvement in
the abduction phenomenon could be evi-
dence that they use abductees for mind
control experiments, as test-targets for mi-
crowave weapons, or that they monitor and
kidnap abductees for information-gather-
ing purposes during a UFO abduction ex-
perience.
If one of these points is correct, one can
expect to run into early resistance when
proposing congressional hearings about
UFOs. For these reasons I started Project
MILAB and present in this article my pre-
liminary findings. MILAB is the acronym
for Military Abductions of alleged UFO
abductees. I reviewed the relevant litera-
