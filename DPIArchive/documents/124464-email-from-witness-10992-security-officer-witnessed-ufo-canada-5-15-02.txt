This was in this last year, 2001 October. I was working at night as a
security officer and I often search the night sky and saw a pale
phosphorescent green object moving toward me. At first I did not think much
of it as it was coming in on the same glide path as the aircraft going to
the Calgary International airport. At any rate / watched it for several
minutes and noted that it was below the highest clouds and above the lower
ones. It moved with out a sound and then came to a dead stop, made a tight
circle and then sped off to the west at an incredible rate of speed. The
write up in the Calgary Sun the next said some thing about another witness
to this phenomena and she described much the same as what I saw. However the
news paper also said that local scientists referred to it as an asteroid, of
all the most ridicules hair brained explanations I have ever heard. An
asteriod would have been red and most certainly would not have been so low
in the sky and not hit the city or the vicinity.
