ABSTRACT
A system for determining the modulation imposed on a
curl-free magnetic vector potential field. The system
includes apparatus for detecting the curl-free magnetic
vector potential field component by means of a Joseph-
son junction. The magnetic vector potential field inter-
acts with the Josephson junction by varying the phase
of the argument of the sine function which determines
the Josephson junction current. The output signals of
the Josephson junction are coupled to apparatus that
can determine the modulation of the detected field.
Because the magnitude of the change in the detected
curl-free vector potential field causes a proportional
change in the phase of the Josephson junction current,
the modulation of the field can be established.

ABSTRACT
A system for determining the modulation imposed on a
curl-free magnetic vector potential field. The system
includes apparatus for detecting the curl-free magnetic
vector potential field component by means of a Joseph-
son junction. The magnetic vector potential field inter-
acts with the Josephson junction by varying the phase
of the argument of the sine function which determines
the Josephson junction current. The output signals of
the Josephson junction are coupled to apparatus that
can determine the modulation of the detected field.
Because the magnitude of the change in the detected
curl-free vector potential field causes a proportional
change in the phase of the Josephson junction current,
the modulation of the field can be established.

ABSTRACT
A system for determining the modulation imposed on a
curl-free magnetic vector potential field. The system
includes apparatus for detecting the curl-free magnetic
vector potential field component by means of a Joseph-
son junction. The magnetic vector potential field inter-
acts with the Josephson junction by varying the phase
of the argument of the sine function which determines
the Josephson junction current. The output signals of
the Josephson junction are coupled to apparatus that
can determine the modulation of the detected field.
Because the magnitude of the change in the detected
curl-free vector potential field causes a proportional
change in the phase of the Josephson junction current,
the modulation of the field can be established.
