TOTAL MODE
This mode is intended for the recycling of highly contaminated liquids that have to be
completely eliminated and processed into Magnegas, heat and carbonaceous precipitates.
The Total Mode consists in: priming the Recycler with an inert gas (the use only since
subsequently the recycler will be full of magnegas), filling up the recycler with the
dedicated pump connected to an outside liquid feedstock tank or pond up to the maximal
allowed level automatically set by the level tower, at which point the PlasmaArcFlow
can be initiated and the liquid is entirely eliminated by the are molecule by molecule.
The Level Tower will automatically add liquid feedstock following processing. The
operator will essentially add manually the electrodes during operations and control the
proper use, such as to make sure that hoses to the outside tank or pond do not suck air.
After 10 to 15 minutes of operation, the cooling system has to be manually activated
following temperature reading on the controls via the activation of the dedicated pump
recirculating the liquid feedstock through the radiator and related fan. In this Total Mode
the operator has to set the proper mixture of liquid feedstock and water (see below), to
identify the maximal flow of the liquid feedstock under a stable arc via the sensors on the
controls, control all operations and secure all necessary samples for local EPA approvals.
Buyer warrants full knowledge that the heat produced by the Recycler in the Total Mode
is merely dissipated by the radiator and fan and cannot be used. This is due to the fact
that the addition of a heat exchanger for the utilization of the heat will imply a cost of the
recycler considerably bigger than the promotional cost identified below. In any case, heat
exchangers constitute established technology and, therefore, they are not necessary for
the purpose of this Recycler of securing orders.
LINEAR MODE
This mode is intended for the sterilization of city, farm or ship sewage with up to a
maximum of 10% biocontaminants, the rest being usable water not to be eliminated. If
the liquid feedstock has more than 10% biocontaminants, it should be diluted with tap,
lake, well, or river fresh water. Following a single passage through the arc, all substances
in suspension are carbonized, the liquid feedstock is sterilized, sent to the main tower and
then pushed by the operating pressure of MagneGas through the sand filter for release in
the outside. In the Linear Mode the Recycler will, produce MagneGas, effluent waters
excellent as liquid fertilizer, and carbonaceous precipitates without any production of
usable heat.
In this case the operator has to identify the maximal flow under which all substance in
SUSPENSION are entirely (and not partially) carbonized and removed by the sand filter
resulting in an effluent liquid that is transparent but not potable since the substances in
SOLUTION are sterilized but not essentially changed by the Recycler. A carbon filter for
the partial removal of the substance in solution is available on an optional basis jointly
with a UV Station to increase the operating flow while assuring complete sterilization of
the effluent waters.

TOTAL-LINEAR MODE
In certain liquids, such as those with a heavy lattice structure, it is useful to use first the
recycler in the Total Mode to break up said matrix and then switch to the Linear Mode
for the filtration of the carbonized substances in suspension. This is essentially a batch
mode of operation whose success depends on the training and skill of the operator.
However, once the best combination of Total-Linear Modes is identified, its industrial
repetition is easy for routine operations.
