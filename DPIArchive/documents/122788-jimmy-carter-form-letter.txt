OFFICE OF
JIMMY CARTER


         The Carter Center is a nonprofit organization with
 established programs throughout the world. President and
 Mrs. Carter's commitment to the Center's full agenda makes
 it impossible for them to respond positively to the many
 requests they receive daily. These include invitations to
 speak at events, to take part in media opportunities, or to
 contribute to books or other writing projects; requests to
 endorse, lend their name to, provide letters or advice for, or
 participate in projects; or to assist with fundraising in any
 way.

        Though we appreciate the many worthy efforts that
 individuals wish to bring to the Carters' attention, we trust
 you will understand their inability personally to review
 each one. On their behalf, we wish you well in your good
 work but regret they cannot comply with your request.
