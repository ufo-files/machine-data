Motorcycle stalled by flying Pyramid
A commuter was on his way to work near Uttoxeter England (specific date of case not given)
when he noticed his motorcycle behaving strangely. Deciding to examine what might be the
cause of the problem, he pulled over to the side of the country road that he was travelling on.
When he finally stopped his motorcycle, he noticed that he was immediately bathed in a vivid
white light. Looking up, he saw a huge square, about thirty feet above him, filled with
"superstructure and spotlights". Then, the square dimmed slightly, and began to move away.
With a new viewing perspective of the object, he noticed that it was actually gigantic pyramid,
and he had been directly under its base. His motorcycle refused to start, and he was forced to
walk home. Subsequent investigations into this case revealed that the motorcycle involved
became highly magnetic, and the primary eyewitness suffered from various physical ailments.
Source: Burton Daily Mail March 3, 1995
