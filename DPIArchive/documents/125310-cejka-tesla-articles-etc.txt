invention either in their intent or in their construction.
As previously noted, the backwave energy is to a
large extent utilized in this invention to cause vibration
of the surface of the enclosure. Therefore the choice of 20
materials used in the construction of this invention is
determined by the same considerations as determine
those of a musical instrument, such as a cello or a guitar.
Light, unpadded resilient materials capable of function-
ng as a scunding board such as light plywood, plastic 25
aminate, sheet aluminum, eto., are the preferred materi-
als in contrast with the heavy rigid materials and pad-
ding of the prior art.
