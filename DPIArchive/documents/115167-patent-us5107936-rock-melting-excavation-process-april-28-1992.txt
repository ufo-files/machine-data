ABSTRACT
In a heat drilling process for tunnels, deep well and
exploration boreholes, only a gap defining the outer
profile of the tunnel or borehole is melted down. The
drill core surrounded by this gap initially remains and is
then extracted at intervals via the melted zone. It is
expedient for the drill core to be sheared off and ex-
tracted continuously after it has passed a cooling zones.
The height of the drill core which first remains in the
borehole is determined such that the molten rock can be
pressed into the drill core, whereby for this pressing in
the necessary pressure is maintained essentially constant
independently of the depth of the borehole concerned.
