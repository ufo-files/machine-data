ROSWELL TIMELINE
July 2, 1947 (11:59 PM): UFO Crash
July 3, 1947 (Early morning): Rancher Mack Brazel and seven-year-old Timothy
Dee Proctor arrive at the debris field and discover a large area of land covered by
strange wreckage. They also discover a gouge on the ranch measuring
approximately ten feet wide by 200 feet long.
July 4, 1947: Mack Brazel travels to Capitan New Mexico and attends the annual
4"h of July rodeo. It is here that he displays pieces of the wreckage/debris that he
found on the ranch the previous day. The Roswell debris becomes "the talk of the
rodeo"
July 5, 1947: Mack Brazel travels to Corona New Mexico and stops at Wades Bar.
It is here that he also displayed pieces of the debris which were recovered from the
ranch on July 3.
July 6, 1947: Mack Brazel drives into Roswell, and arrives at the Chaves County
Sheriff's office to report the strange wreckage. He carried with him a small box of
material recovered from the debris field
July 6, 1947: KGFL radio station announcer Frank Joyce calls the Roswell court
house looking for any late breaking news. Frank speaks to Mack Brazel, and relays
the same story he had just told Sheriff George Wilcox. Brazel also drops a
bombshell and tells Joyce "they weren't human"
', clearly indicating that he had
seen something more than just crash debris.
July 6, 1947 (late afternoon/evening): Jessie Marcel and Sheridan Cavitt drive
from RAAF (509* BG HQ) to the vicinity of the debris field, and spend the night
the Hine's house.
July 7, 1947 (morning): Jesse Marcel and Sheridan Cavitt arrive at the debris field
and examine the wreckage in person.

July 7, 1947 (late afternoon/evening): Military arrives at the impact site, and
discovers a strange "egg-shaped" craft with a hole breach on the side. Two dead
alien corpses are recovered badly decomposed, and wearing tight fitting silver
colored flight suits. One entity was discovered still alive. Special note: Two
additional dead alien corpses were discovered approximately 2-1/2 miles west of
the impact site. An ambulance truck, 2-1/2-ton 6 X 6 troop transport, two jeeps and
a large "low-boy" tractor/trailer participated in the recovery operation. Special
note: There are reports that a crane from Roswell was used to hoist the unknown
object onto the flatbed trailer. Reports from civilian witnesses who had arrived at
the impact site before the military confirmed that "powerful floodlights" were seen
indicating that the military had officially begun the retrieval operation.
July 7, 1947 (late evening): Mack Brazel arrives at the private home of radio
station KGFL owner Walter Whitmore Sr. to do a wire-recorded interview which
was later confiscated by the military.
July 8, 1947 (very early morning 2:00 AM): Jesse Marcel drives back to RAAF,
and then drives home to show his wife and son pieces of the wreckage. It is here
that young Jesse Marcel Jr, discovers strange writing on the inside wall of an I-
beam.
July 8, 1947 (morning): 15 body flight out of Roswell occurs in a C-54 piloted by
Pappy Henderson. This flight was flown in the "raw" condition, and the bodies
were exposed to the air inside the fuselage of the C-54. This flight flew from
RAAF, directly to Wright Field (Dayton Ohio).
July 8, 1947 (morning): Mack Brazel is apprehended off the streets of Roswell by
the military after breakfast, and is held against his will at RAAF. He is interrogated
and threatened for five days, and eventually is forced to undergo a full body cavity
search.
July 8, 1947 (11:00 AM) Colonel William Blanchard (RAAF) issues the famous
press release that the military had retrieved a flying disc.
July 8, 1947 (4:00 PM): Military convoy consisting of two jeeps a large "low-boy"
tractor/trailer (transporting a strange egg-shaped craft covered by a tarp), and then
two additional jeeps following from behind are seen driving down Main street in
Roswell. The entire convoy was seen by at least one paperboy, and multiple
additional eyewitnesses.

July 8, 1947 (4:15 PM): Military convoy arrives at RAAF (509* BG HQ), and
drives directly into the east side of hanger P-3/Building 84. This hanger serves as
the central command center for the entire Roswell Crash/Retrieval operation. Four
dead alien bodies were laid out on the hanger floor, with two child sized caskets
sitting nearby. A large wooden crate (packed with dry ice) was built to contain the
two caskets in a desperate effort to reduce the stench of the rapidly decomposing
bodies.
July 8, 1947, B-29 "Dave's Dream" leaves RAAF and flies to 8"h AF HQ Fort
Worth carrying Jesse Marcel, and pieces of wreckage.
July 9, 1947 (6:00 AM): Wooden crate containing at least two bodies is guarded at
atomic bomb pit #1 at RAAF. The crate is concealed by two circles of
protective/concealment screens with guards posted both inside and out.
July 9, 1947 (4:00 PM): B-29 "Straight Flush" taxis over to atomic bomb pit #1,
and begins the loading process of the wooden crate into its bomb bay.
July 9, 1947: Second body flight leaves RAAF, and flies directly to 8"h AF HQ
Fort Worth Texas. This flight stays below 10,000 feet, and is flown un-pressurized.
The cargo bay of B-29 "Straight Flush" holds the large wooden crate containing
the two child sized caskets inside. Special note: On the evening of July 8th
' the
large wooden crate was stored at Atomic Bomb pit #1 (protected by two circular
rows of perimeter screens), until B-29 "Straight Flush" could be taxied over to
accept its historic cargo.
July 9, 1947: Multiple witnesses confirm seeing Mack Brazel being escorted
through downtown Roswell. He was brought to the offices of both the Roswell
Daily Record and the Roswell morning dispatch, where he was forced to recant his
original statement. He was also taken to the offices of radio stations KGFL and
KSWS.
