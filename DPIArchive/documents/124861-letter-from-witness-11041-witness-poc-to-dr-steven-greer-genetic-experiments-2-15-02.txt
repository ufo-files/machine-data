Dear Dr. Greer,

 attended your May 9, 2001, press conference and follow-up workshop intDC.
Since then I've spent hundreds of hours in researching and following this subject
and bringing it to the attention of others.

Enclosed is a donation for $25 to help with the documentation of testimony. After
reading the replies on you web site from congressional members, I am convinced
that Congress will never hold public hearings, and if they ever do it will be years
after every person on Earth has accepted the existence and presence of aliens
as fact.

I, therefore, believe strongly that organizations such as the Disclosure Project
must'press forward with collecting, documenting, and publicizing the information.

Last November I visited a retired Air Force friend and asked him whether he
believed in the existence of aliens and alien vehicles. He laughed and said of
course he believed, and that he was probably the only person in the Air Force he
knew who had NOT seen an alien aircraft while flying. When I asked him his
thoughts about genetic engineering, he told me that if he told me certain things
that the government was doing I would go kill myself because it was so evil. He
would not discuss this further. I asked him if the research were biological or
mechanical, to which he would answer only "yes."

Keep up the good work.
