contractor, filed a secret qui tam lawsuit against Hamilton Securities,
Goldman Sachs, and Blackrock PNC. He also filed a lawsuit against HUD
and HUD officials.
Transcripts of the sealed hearings show that DoJ and HUD attorneys
were clearly the strategic force behind Ervin's phony suit against
Hamilton, running interference for Ervin in closed sessions with Judge
Sporkin.
