UFOs is to determine if they pose a threat to the security of the
United States, to determine if the phenomena has any scientific value
fox research and development purposes, and to specifically identify
the object or phenomena.

crudely dug holes and the case is officially carried in Air Force
files as a "Hoax".

officially reported to them by the witnesses or observers.
