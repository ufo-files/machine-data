USO's
(Mystery beneath the waves)
By Michael Schratt
What do we really know about what goes on under the world's oceans, and lakes? It's been
said that man has spent more time on the surface of the moon than exploring the depths of the
deepest oceans. Thousands of reports from Mariners, Commanders, Captains, and Masters of
ships dating back hundreds of years indicate that USO's (Unidentified Submerged Objects) are a
documented reality. In fact, over 50% of all UFO sightings are directly related to craft plunging
into, coming out of, or being tracked moving under bodies of water. Why have officially
sponsored technical research papers like the 1969 University of Colorado $600,000 "Condon
Report" failed to address this historically significant fact? Of the 1,517 foreign (non-USA) UFO
reports categorized by famed astronomer Jacques Vallee, 50% were at sea. Are USO's some type
of unexplained meteorological phenomenon, "living" entity, or mechanical machine? This article
will explore some of the most reliable USO cases, including two which involved the U.S. Navy.

W279
(Navy Icebreaker used in "Operation Deepfreeze 1955-1956)
On a cold and dark evening during the Navy's "Operation Deepfreeze" exercise (1955-1956),
a startling encounter took place in Admiralty Bay (Antarctic region) which was witnessed by two
crew members and a scientist aboard the icebreaker USCGC Eastwind (pictured above). At
around 9:00 PM, a "silvery colored bullet shaped craft" was seen to break through 37 feet of
solid ice, and fly off into the sky at a high rate of speed. A Brazilian scientist by the name of Dr.
Rubens J. Villela was shocked as he saw the craft tear through the ice. As it broke through, the
strange craft hurled enormous blocks of ice high into the sky (see illustration below). It left a
circular shaped hole in the ice immediately after it departed. Two additional eyewitnesses (an
officer and helmsmen) also noted that boiling water and steam was seen coming out of the
circular shaped hole where the craft had broken through. (Source: Invisible Residence by Ivan T.
Sanderson)

(USO tracked by the U.S.S. Wasp)
During a 1963 naval training exercise off the coast of Puerto Rico, an astonishing event took
place. Five naval vessels were involved including the USS Wasp which served as the "commend
ship". Two American submarines are believed to have taken part as well. Each craft was linked
by way of an advanced electronic communications system. The strange "event" occurred when a
sonar operator in one of the smaller ships reported to the bridge that one of the submarines had
"broken formation" and was in pursuit of an unknown object. The underwater object was
travelling at "over 150 knots" (172 mph), and was being tracked by multiple sensors. By
comparison, the semi-official record for submerged submarines is 45 (knots (51.8 mph) which is
held by the U.S. Navy's Skipjack class. In addition, it was reported that the USO was being
monitored for a total of four days, and that it reached depths of up to 27,000 feet.

An interesting case reported by author Charles Fort indicates that an enormous object rose out
of the sea off of Cape Race on November 12, 1887. The object rose to a height of approximately
fifty feet, and then advanced close to the British steamer S.S. Siberian. It was described as
looking like a large "ball of fire". The Captain indicated that the strange object was moving
against the wind, and that he had seen similar phenomenon in that location before.
As published in The Diary of Andrew Bloxam (1925), at about three in the morning (August
12, 1825) crew members were astonished to find that everything around them had become
brightly illuminated. Looking to the East, they spotted a large round luminous object rising up
from the sea to the clouds, and then descend again out of sight. The strange object was dull red in
color, and about the size of the sun.
(Light-wheel reported through the ages by Ocean explorers)
On a voyage up the Persian Gulf during May of 1880, a strange sight was seen from the deck
of the steamer Patna. Around 11:30 PM, crewmembers noticed a large "luminous wheel" which
appeared on both sides of the ship. The unknown objects were rotating, and included "spokes"
which seemed to push the ship along. Each wheel contained sixteen spokes which measured
approximately 900 feet in length. The wheels emitted a phosphorescent gleam which seemed to
glide along the surface of the sea.

A nearly identical case was reported by the Captain of the steamship Bintang on June 10,
1909 (published in the Nautical Meteorological Annual). At around 3:00 AM, while sailing
through the Straits of Malacca, an enormous wheel of light was spotted on the surface. The
unusual object had "long arms issuing from a center around which the whole system appeared to
rotate." Apparently, the craft was so large that only half of it be seen over the horizon at any one
time. This may indicate that the wheel could have been over five miles in diameter. The entire
sighting lasted fifteen minutes.
As reported in Jacques Vallee's publication Anatomy of a Phenomenon, on November 2, 1885
a very luminous blue/green flame was reported off the coast of Constantinople. The unknown
object was moving at a height of approximately five to six meters above the water. It proceeded
to make a series of turns around a ferryboat pier at Scutari. Its high intensity light lit up nearby
streets and homes, and after 90 seconds, it fell into the sea without a sound.
During the summer of 1945, crew members of the U.S. Army Delarof spotted a large round
object which had just emerged from the sea. The ship was on its way back to Seattle from
Alaska. The unknown object climbed straight up, and then began to circle the ship. It was
estimated to be approximately 250 feet in diameter. After several minutes, the object silently
disappeared to the south.
In late summer of 1954 crew members of the Groote Beer (a Netherlands Government ship)
reported seeing a flat moon-like object rise out of the ocean eighty miles off the coast of New
York. The USO was gray in color, and had bright lights around its edges.
On July 22, 1955 multiple eyewitnesses in Santa Marina California reported seeing a long
silvery object emerge from the sea. (Reported in UFO investigator Vol. IV, No. 5, March 1968)
On December 13, 1956 a Swedish ship contacted the harbor control at La Guaira, Venezuela,
and reported that a cone-shaped object was falling into the sea of the Venezuelan coast. The
unknown object was brilliantly lit, and emitted "strange glares". When the object hit the water, a
large explosion was heard. At the area of impact, the water became extremely colored. Then,
after the colors dissipated, the water became agitated with a "boiling motion". (Reported in the
APRO Bulletin January 1964)
On September 1, 1956 police officers at Porthcawl, Glamorgan Wales saw a dark red colored
object with a jagged black streak across its center rise from the sea. It was described as being a
"good deal larger than a full-sized harvest moon". (Source FSR Vol 3 No. 6)
In March of 1959, on the coast of Poland near Kolobrzeg, Polish soldiers saw the sea
suddenly become agitated. A triangular object measuring four meters per side came out of the
water, and started to fly in circles over the barracks, then sped away and vanished. (Source:
Anatomy of a Phenomenon by Jacques Vallee).
