ABSTRACT
Apparatus for producing and demonstrating properties of mo-
tional electric fields by means of rotating magnetic flux
produced by a plurality of magnets extending parallel with the
axis of rotation, said flux of these magnets being put into rota-
tion about a common axis by mechanical or by electro mag-
netic means.
