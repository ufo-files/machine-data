Long Description of Sighting Report
It was a large yellow glowing orb that arose out of the Monterey Bay. This was after dinner, and I was looking down on the
Bay from a property on the mountains in Aptos but with a clear view of the Monterey Bay. I saw a light under the surface
of the Bay and all of the sudden it came out of the water, with kelp sliding off of it. It rose out slowly, and I told my dinner
mates (a friend and my husband) and we all watched it. It rose from that area which LOOKED like it came out from the
north side of the bay, and then took forever, about 15-20 minutes to rest opposite of the smoke stacks of the Moss
Landing Power plant, in the air. We had a spyglass so we looked at it through that (unfortunately, no cell phones then) and
we saw a series of lights around the perimeter, that looked like this: 0)0)()() and they were green and amber and yellow.
It finally decided (I guess) to hover in front of the Moss Landing Coal Power plant (don't ask me why we have a coal
powered plant here, I have no clue). We were looking at it through the spy glass (you know, one of those brass things
people keep in their living rooms when they live on the coast), and we saw an arm from the back that came out and over,
and then it extended things that looked like long extended pinchers that came out from it and it was apparently going to
the smoke stacks. I have no idea what that was about. I don't know what it was doing. We watched it for awhile, and then
about 20 minutes later it winked out. It didn't fly off, it just winked out, like an eye closing. So I guess that was about 50
minutes in totality.
She told me: "It was huge, it was really big. I think it was about the size of a bus, or larger." After I
questioned her, she decided it must be much bigger.
She couldn't hear it, but she saw the individual kelp pieces.
I think probably about fifty feet across at least, maybe 100.
She first thought it was a light, but when they viewed it through a spyglass "telescope', it appeared
solid with portholes.
