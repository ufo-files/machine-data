On April 9, 1968 (between 10:00 and 12:30 PM) five eyewitnesses observed a UFO hovering
over Baden Pennsylvania. It was a very clear night with no clouds. The craft had a slightly white
colored transparent dome on top, with three blinking red lights along the side (see sketch
sequence). In addition, three high intensity spotlights were also observed. The color of the craft
changed depending on its speed and height. During slow flight, it exhibited a dull red color. As it
sped up, the craft changed to a bright red/white color. The bottom of the UFO had what appeared
to be rotating white lights. Attempts to signal the UFO with a flashlight and car headlights
appeared to be successful when the craft responded by blinking its three red lights. It also
appeared to "wait" for ground observers who fell behind during the two hour chase. A local
policeman who was called to the scene refused to fill out a report. Source: CUFOS archives.
