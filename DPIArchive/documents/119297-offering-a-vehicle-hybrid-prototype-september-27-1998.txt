The TRIDENT is a hand-crafted, sophisticated exotic vehicle for two.
Constructed of epoxy-bonded composite materials, the monocoque
chassis is light but strong, and the modular subsystem permits the
vehicle to be updated as E.V. technology develops.
The Trident is part of an emerging new technology know as hybrid/
electric. Hybrid vehicles use an auxiliary power unit, or A.P.U., to
provide extra energy on demand for hill climbing, rapid acceleration,
and extended range. When commuting short distances or driving in
town, pure electric operation is chosen. Battery recharging can be
accomplished either by A.P.U., or a standard 120V AC outlet.
The Trident is designed as an enclosed motorcycle, making
registration/inspection and insurance simple and economical.
Environmentally considerate, exciting to drive, and capable of an
extended service life, the Trident bridges the gap between present and
future electric vehicles.

HOW DO HYBRIDS WORK?
What's the most efficient way to use the energy in gasoline? Right
now, it's by merging an ultra-high-efficiency gasoline engine and an
energy-balancing electric drive system.
Part of the reason gasoline engines have so many pollution-control
problems is because of the constantly varying loads and RPMs they are
subject to in a conventional drivetrain.
By mating the engine directly to a generator, load and RPM become
constant, with the battery bank acting as a 'load leveling' device. Using
an electric motor to drive the wheels allows the transmission to be
eliminated. The resulting vehicle uses fuel much more economically,
and because the gasoline engine is running at a constant speed, its
emissions can be reduced / controlled more effectively.
Hybrid vehicles enjoy prolonged service life due to the fact that all
systems are operating at their ideal efficiency and load, which translates
to lower operating costs for owners.
The D.O.T. has published a study showing that the average
American driver's trip lengths are below 15 miles 80% of the time. For
such short excursions, the Trident can rely exclusively on battery power
and so provide zero-emission transport.
Until research and development efforts provide vastly more
. compacț, efficient, and cost-effective battery or fuel-cell designs, hybrid
vehicle technology presents the best alternative to conventional
transport. And because hybrid vehicles allow rapid refueling
convenience via an established infrastructure, they represent a viable
long-term consumer choice compared to pure electric vehicles.
