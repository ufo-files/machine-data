A CIVILIAN RECENTLY EMPLOYED AT THE CHINA LAKE NAVAL ORDNANCE TEST STATION, IN
CALIFORNIA, UNEARTHED (METHOD NOT RELATED) A UFO REPORT MADE (AND BURIED IN THE FILES)
SOME SIXTEEN YEARS AGO. THE REPORT ITSELF IS UNCLASSIFIED. PROBABLY IT WENT FROM DESK
DIRECTLY TO FILES AND NOTHING MORE WAS DONE ABOUT IT.
SINCE IT ORIGINATED AT A WEAPONS TEST STATION, ONE IMMEDIATELY WONDERS WHETHER
A SECRET WEAPON WAS BEING DESCRIBED, FOR IT OFTEN HAPPENS AT SUCH INSTALLATIONS THAT
THE PROVERBIAL RIGHT HAND DOESN'T KNOW WHAT THE LEFT HAND IS DOING. BUT DID WE HAVE
ANYTHING LIKE THIS IN 1966?
THE REPORT CAME TO THE ATTENTION OF CUFOS IN A CIRCUITOUS MANNER; IT FOLLOWS
HERE ALMOST IN ITS ENTIRETY BECAUSE OF ITS PROFESSIONAL NATURE:
'ON TUESDAY, 18 JAN. 1966, AT 4:55 P.M. PST, ON BAKER RANGE AT THE NAVAL
ORDNANCE STATION, TWO CIVIL SERVICE-EMPLOYED SURVEYORS WERE INVOLVED IN
SIGHTING A UFO. ONE MAN WAS STANDING AT A BENCH MARK MONUMENT, ABOUT 117 DEG.
W., 35 DEG 52 MIN N. AT 3004 FT. ABOVE SEA LEVEL, USING A WILD T-3 THEODOLITE
IN A SURVEY OF POINTS IN THE AREA. TWO OF THEIR TARGETS WERE IN LINE WITH THE
BIC FLIGHT LINE, ONE BEING A BLINKING FLASH LAMP ABOUT 5 MILES DISTANT, LOCATED
ON THE FAR SIDE OF AN ANCIENT LAVA FLOW.
