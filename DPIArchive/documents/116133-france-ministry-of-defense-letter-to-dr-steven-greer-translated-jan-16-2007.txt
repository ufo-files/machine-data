Dear Sirs,
On January 14, official imperatives did not allow the top ranking representation of the French
Ministry of Defence (MOD) to assure its presence for the tests and attempted contacts beginning on
the fourth week of this year 2007.
The signing officers of this document ask respectfully for apology on behalf of the MOD for this
temporary inconvenience.
Indeed, it is only a temporary delay and the MOD requests all participants to postpone this meeting
Due to short term political imperatives, the MOD is obliged to request, according to the wishes of
the participants concerned with the feasability of the operation, a report of the tests and the contacts
to be scheduled for the 24th or the 25th week of this year.
Please take into consideration the full determination and the active collaboration of the MOD at its
highest level regarding the interests of France and its scientific environment.
A protocol of reciprocity of acquired knowledge during the scheduled experiments of the signing
officers in close connection with SPORADE will supply all necessary means of materials, human
resources and financial support.
This protocol will be based on four categories of specs in coordination with the needs and efforts
according to your recommendations with the support and help of the MOD.
1) Identification of the source, procedure and recognition
2) Biological, physico chemical, relative fields, miscellenous language(laser, Mfp,....)
3) Quality of source / To document the quality of source, biological if possible, in relation with the
the validation of our entries and contacts makes this category crucial.
4) Availability of the source / this category regroups the fields regarding contact probabilities and
future exploitation of the above mentioned.
The collaboration of the delegation (representation) of the MOD with its full technical backing
requires the agreement from each party on the process / analysis, specification, conception,
implementation of necessary means for exploiting the scientific results.
These results must allow us to conclude on a performance evaluation system in the elaborated
framework of one or more action models regarding the objectives brought for through your
experimental proposition and the electronic and optic means you wish to use that we accept to put at
your disposal during your stay in France.
Our will to participate is not only to see the first level identification of the course but to confirm the
experimental tracking and the results of all levels and categories obtained.
