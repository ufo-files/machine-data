Mid-August 1944. After home leave he became an Instructor Pilot in B-17s and Asst.
Ops. Officer at Avon Park, Fl. He again volunteered for overseas duty and completed
training as a pilot of C-87 and C-54 transport type aircraft. He departed San Francisco,
CA on Sept. 2, 1945 as a C-54 pilot with stops at Hawaii; Johnson Island; Kwajalein;
Guam; Manila; and Kunming, China; arriving at Dacca, then Calcutta in India. After a few
flights in the China-Burma-India Theater, including two flights over the famed "Hump',
he returned to the United States by the same route in October 1945 and upon separation
on Dec. 1, 1945 was promoted to Captain in the Army Reserve.
After completing West Virginia Wesleyan College in 1949 he joined the newly formed U.
S. Air Force and received a Regular Air Force Commission. Early in his career the U. S.
Air Force sponsored a full calendar year of graduate studies at the George Washington

hours. He served with HQ. United States Air Force; HQ. U.S. Air Forces Europe; HQ.
Tactical Air Command; HQ. Royal Air Force, U.K.; and Organization Joint Chief Of Staffs.

as a Lt. Col: to accept appointment (and promotion) as a Senior Foreign Service
