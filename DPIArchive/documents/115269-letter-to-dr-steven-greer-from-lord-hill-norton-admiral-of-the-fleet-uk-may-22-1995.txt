CONTENTS STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL

NOT to be duplicated.
22 May 1995
Dear Dr Greer,
Thank you for your letter of 16 May inviting me to your meeting next month. I
sincerely regret that I shall not be able to attend on those dates.
I should have liked very much indeed to have taken part, because I have been
impressed by what you have done, and what you are doing in these important
matters.. If you have any future events of a similar nature, preferably not quite
so far away, I very much hope that you will invite me again.
If there will be any minutes or other written record of the proceedings may I
particularly request that you will send me a copy?
With my best wishes for a most successful meeting.
Yours sincerely,
Hartman.
poTE-5 star Admiral
Former
ministry of.
