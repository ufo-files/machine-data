May 30, 1997
ILLUSI*N
Dr. Steven Greer

Dear Dr. Greer,
Thank you for sharing your collection of sightings, documents, and other data
about aliens and SETI. You make some interesting assumptions, some of
which may be worthy of a documentary, given enough specifics to back up
your argument. Although I'm sure that the government is not above
covering up such discoveries, I don't see a story here. It has no ending and I
am still not entirely convinced about the existence of extraterrestrial
intelligence. In any case, I hope your organization will continue to grow and
catch the imagination of generations to come.
incerely your
Oliver Stone
ILLUSION ENTERTAINMENT GROUP
