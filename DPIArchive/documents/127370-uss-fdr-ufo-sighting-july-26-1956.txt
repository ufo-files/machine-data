TREMENDOUS SPEED.

AIR OVER ONE ANOTHER WITH SEVERAL HUNDRED FEET BETWEEN

COUNTER-ROTATING LIGHTS COULD BE SEEN THROUGH THE MIDDLE

OBJECTS COULD HAVEBEEN BETWEEN 75 AND 100 FEET IN LENGTH.
