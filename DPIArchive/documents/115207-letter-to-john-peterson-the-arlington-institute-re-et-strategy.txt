Re: ET Strategy
Dear John:My answer is, that along with the normalization process, that is
finding "normal" people" who testify to the experience of seeing UFO's, (such as
I saw at the United Nations many years ago,) we should begin a public
questioning of the possible benefits of finding other intelligent beings. We
should have discussions of how wonderful it would be to find we are not alone.
personally believe that the birth analogy is the best here. I think we should say
that when we were babies in the womb we were surrounded by life but weren't
aware of it until after we were born, and even then, we still had womb-
consciousness until our nervous system linked up, we opened our eyes, and saw
that there were "others." If there had not been others, we could never have
"grown-up." The analogy here is that we ahve been in the womb of Earth
gaining our capacities to be extraterrestrials ourselves. Now that we have
accomplished the first baby steps in this direction through both the Space
programs, SETI and UFO research, we are ready to mature, to open our
collective eyes, to want to see other life, rather than to be afraid of it. I would
have contests among school children to imagine the most positive possibilities.
would suggest that as we see them, so they will tend to be. I would see if there
could be a sitcom about normalizing UFO's. I would bring credible women and
men on talk shows to raise the issues. etc. My own view is that we, as a
planetary body have been born already. Our current crisis is a birth. We are
one body, we are struggling to coordinate as a whole, we do have to take care of
our own wastes, etc. what has not yet happened is that we have not yet opened
our coleective eyes. It would be great to plan an Earth-Space celberation of our
"birth, a planetary birth day. We have had Earth Day. What about our Birth
Day. When we recognize ourselves as universal beigns, it will be less
frightening to image there are others!
Keep me posted.
