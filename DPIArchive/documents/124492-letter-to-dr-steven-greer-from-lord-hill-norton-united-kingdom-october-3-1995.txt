Admiral of the Fleet The
SCB
Dr Steven M Greer MD
CSETI
UNITED
STATES OF AMERICA
3 October, 1995
Dear Dr Greer,
Thank you for your letter of lst September and the enclosures. It must have just
missed me, for we went to Portugal on holiday on 7th, so it was waiting for me
when we got back last week.
I thoroughly enjoyed our talk when you came here in August and I am grateful to
you for your time. I read all the material you left me and I intend to read it
all again, though I have sent some of it on to the Countess of Moray, who shares
my interest (I have an idea you may have met her some time ago).
I find your latest Brief on NSI Implications of the UFO/ETI subject, very clear
and very convincing. It is important for all of us to realise that, as you say,
times have changed.
In particular the end of the Cold War has vastly reduced
the military/political importance of denying any potential UFO information to
the other side.
There has been considerable media interest here recently, provoked by the dispute
over Mr Sheffield and his approaches to the European Parliament. I have been
on the fringe of it all, but I take the view that any publicity on this matter
must be good.
I do not, certainly at the moment, have any important or prominent witnesses who
I could recommend to you for your coalition, though the recent uproar has brought
me several most convincing sighting reports.
One was from a (former) air line
Captain and another from an ex-soldier. Neither has anything to gain, and the
former has possible loss.
Please keep me in touch.
Inus smare
