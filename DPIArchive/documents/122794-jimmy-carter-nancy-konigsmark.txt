CARTER CENTER
October 29, 2007
Dear Dr. Greer,
Thank you for your letter requesting to meet with President Carter. Although he

personal obligations, and it is not possible for him to add further commitments to

sends his best wishes.
Sincerely,
Director of Scheduling
Dr. Steven M. Greer
