UFO landing/occupant case in France
On July 1, 1965
e (local farmer of Valensole France) sighted a strange "beetle"
shaped UFO which had landed near his home. The craft had an elliptical egg shaped body, with
what appeared to be a streamlined crew compartment on the top. The outline of a door was seen

walked near to the unusual craft, he

move,

government claimed that the object was a new type of experimental helicopter undergoing test
flights. However, they never bothered to explain the alleged beings associated with the case.
