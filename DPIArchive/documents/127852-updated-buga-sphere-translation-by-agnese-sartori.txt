Buga Sphere Translation done by Agnese Sartori ancient language expert
In the great beginning, the principal one, the spirit, through the Word, brought forth the
birth of races and lineages, where everything was imbued with the purpose of spreading
and bestowing upon the Sun, the sky, the radiance, the light of the universe and the
realm of the seven worlds, the planets, and the gods. Then came the lunar phases, the
harvests, and the sowing; the land was secured, and cultures were formed, governed by
the two principal movements, each with its own direction.
It was then that abundance, teaching, and knowledge were bestowed; this was
manifested from the highest, the most sublime, and the perfect. It was then that the
vortex was created with a great roar; likewise, the wheel of time, the astronomical
wheel, was created, manifesting in the cycles of the earth.
Afterward, the Absolute and Highest draws nearer for the manifestation of good and the
generation of virtues: respect for the sacred, surrender to truth, and the expression of
spiritual strength and heroism through harmony and perfect synchronicity. Then, the
resonance of the thirty gods is established.
Having done this, Indra's great ship, commanded by the god of the atmosphere and the
sky, moved freely with a great roar, for he had charged the Great Solar Warrior with
ending evil on earth.
Finally, after the ship departed, it left a warning of danger: the shattering of worlds by
atomic disasters, solar explosions, or destruction, where everything could end due to
our decisions and our responsibility, forever destroying the great life essence, the breath
and lifeblood of creation.
