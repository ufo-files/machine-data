A semi-conductor laser comprising a crystal having a
sequence of layers forming a heterostructure diode and
which includes a laser active zone interposed between a
pair of semiconductor layers. Each of these semi-con-
ductor layers has a band gap which is greater than that
of the layers within the laser active zone. The laser
active zone includes a first semiconductor layer having
a given band gap, and at least second and third semicon-
ductor layers each having a band gap which is greater
than that of the first layer. The first layer is contiguous
with and interposed between semiconductor layers each
having a band gap which is greater than that of said first
layer and forms a pn-junction with one of those contigu-
ous layers. A strip-shaped region of a uniform conduc-
tivity type diffused from the surface of the crystal pene-
trates into at least one layer of the laser active zone but
does not penetrate into the first layer.
