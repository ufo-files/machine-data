26 February 1996
Dear
I would like to thank you on behalf of SETI and the Project Starlight Coalition for your time
and interest during our meeting last friday. We realize how very busy you are and appreciate
the amount of time you were able to devote to this issue.
I hope that you will have time to thoroughly review the briefing materials which I left with you,
and will feel free to ask any follow-up questions which may come to mind.
It is our sincere hope that you may assist in the process of seeing that the numerous witnesses
whom we have located will be protected and able to provide public testimony. Certainly, the
ultra-secret, and probably illegal, management of this matter is a direct threat to the national
security, and undermines the basic principles of our constitutional democracy.
Again, thank you for your time and interest. It was an honor to meet you, and I hope that we
may have the opportunity to explore this matter further in the near future.
Sincerely,
Steven M. Greer M.D.
Director of CSETI
PS: A sincere thank you also to your able assistant,
to have her husband call me regarding his interest in this matter. SG
