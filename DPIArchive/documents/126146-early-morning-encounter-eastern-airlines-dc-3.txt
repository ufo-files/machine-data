Early Morning Encounter
At 2:30 AM on July 23, 1948 the pilots of an Eastern Airlines DC-3 were flying at an altitude
of 5,000 feet approximately twenty miles south west of Montgomery Alabama. Suddenly,
Captain
noticed a bright light heading directly towards
their aircraft at the same altitude. Immediately,
X put the airliner into a hard left
turn. The strange UFO then swooped down off their right wing (no more than 700 feet away),
made an abrupt pull-up, and then vanished instantaneously. Both pilots reported that the unusual
object was some type of structured craft, and it emitted a red/orange colored exhaust. Captain
›described the UFO as being "torpedo shaped", and measuring approximately 100 feet
long, with two rows of brightly lit lights (similar to burning magnesium) running along the side.
It had a pointed "nose"
, with no apparent wings, or control surfaces, and a strange blue colored
glow surrounded its exterior. At least one passenger onboard the flight also witnessed the
encounter.
As an interesting historical side-note, a few days after the DC-3 was buzzed by a UFO, the
Air Force published its once TOP SECRET report known as the "Estimate of the Situation".
Apparently, high level military brass in Washington D.C. felt that the reliable UFO reports by
two highly experienced commercial airline pilots (and others) could no longer be ignored.
Sources: NICAP, Captain Edward J. Ruppelt, U.S. Air Force Project Blue Book
