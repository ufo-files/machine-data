Texas-Oklahoma Border 1966
March 23, 1966 near the Texas and Oklahoma border a 56 year old
electrical engineer on his way to work at Sheppard AFB, Texas
encountered the craft on Highway 70 at 5:30 AM. He stopped his car
about 80 feet from the landed craft. It was brilliantly illuminated with
four very bright lights on the side facing him. He stated the craft was
shaped like a fish and was about 75ft. long. There was what looked like
a plastic bubble about 3ft in diameter on top of the front of the craft, and
light could be seen coming from the inside of the craft through this
bubble.
The craft had a tail and a set of horizontal stabilizers at the rear. The
stabilizers were about 2 ½ ft long. On the side of the craft was an open
doorway and a set of letters that looked like either "TL4768" or "TL
4168", which were painted black vertically on the side of the craft.
Halfway down the side of the craft was what looked like a porthole that
was about 4 ½ ft high and 2 ½ ft wide.
The inside of the craft was illuminated in a white light.
The craft was resting on four legs.
