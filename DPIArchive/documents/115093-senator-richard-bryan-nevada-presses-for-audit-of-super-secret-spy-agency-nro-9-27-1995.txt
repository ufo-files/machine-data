             BRYAN PRESSES FOR AUDIT OF SUPER SECRET SPY AGENCY

     WASHINGTON, D.C. -- Following reports that a super secret spy
     agency has accumulated $1 billion in unspent taxpayer dollars
     without irformrig Congress, United States Senator Richard Bryan
     (D-Nevada) said today he will offer an amendment to require a
     full audit of the financial management of the National
     Reconnaissance Office (NRO). Bryan, a member of the Senate
     Intelligence Committee, said the amendment will also prohibit the
     NRO from accumulating surplus funds in the future and ensure an
     increased level of NRO budget scrutiny by the Office of
     Management and Eudget COMB).

     "The most recent revelation concerning the NRO confirms the
     American publics skepticism about the intelligence community and
     its ability to live up to the public's trust," Bryan said. "This
     is an agency that is out of control and appears to have no
     intention of correcting its ways even in the face of continued
     criticism."
     It was recently reported the NRO, a spy satellite agency jointly
     overseen by the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) and the
     Department of Defense, accumulated $1 billion in unspent
     appropriations. Bryan plans to offer his amendment to the
     Intelligence Authorization bill scheduled to be considered by the
     full Senate. Bryan informed the leaders of the Intelligence
     Committee Senators Azlen Specter (R-PA) and Bob Kerrey (D-NE) of
     his intentions earlier today.
