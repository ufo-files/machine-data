NICAP,
1536 Connecticut Ave., WW,
Washington, D.C.,
Maj. Donald E. Keyhoe,
Gordon I. Lore Jr.,
Gentlemen:
The enclosure is the result of a call I had about 4 PM
Jan, 10, 1968, from the Chief of Police of the City of Exeter, Calif.,
Elmer Morehouse, whom I called on and thanked for his prompt and
considerate action in alerting me within the hour of the sighting.
I drove to Exter and gathered the substance directly from the
principal who had the sighting, his first, found him to be an
intelligent, cooperative witness with a keen analytical mind and
good ability in expressing himself although not inclined to be
loquacious as I was required to ply him with a multitude of
questions to establish the facts as set forth on the questionair.
Chief Morehouse established certain factors of good character,
reliability and honesty concerning the witness and in my opinion
and that of another patrolman who was present when I spoke with
Chief Morehouse, the report is factual.
The local newspaper, The Exeter Sun will carry an article to be
dictated by the witness, Hubert Coffman and I will pass the information
on to our paper here in Visalia, also our broadcasting stations in
the area.
My congratulations to our new assistant director, Gordon Lore d*•,
and may all your flying problems be identifyable ones.

covering a period of three years
