Life Loves Me Back
I've been in love with life for a long time
Beautiful, dynamic, all encompassing, brilliantly faceted Life!
Now my merging selves, gliding thru Soul-travelled realms
Have brought me to yet another blissful realization -
Life loves me Back!
Loves me in total fulsomeness.
As I rejoice in the Sacredness of this renlization,
AS I extol Nature's vast generosity, her constant ever living renewa...
Life loving me renews and expands me.
As I caress the newly blossoming buds in my garden
Life's ever blossoming life sends new sprigs of color up my spine.
As summer gently enfolds my home
I am imbued once again into the summer of my years.
Every cellular participant in this body buds and blossoms
And is renewed in the power of life's love.
The trees - the magnificent trees grateful for my love
Entwine their roots in loving embrace thru-out the earth.
And life loving me back instills their stalwart quality,
Generating new life in my arms and limbs,
Feeding and nourishing my heart.
As fruit ripens upon their branches so am I ripened
And thus am enabled to nourish.
New life -
Young life-
Middle life -
Old life.. all harmoniously flow thru me,
Life loving me back quickens into a vertical shaft of light
Becoming circular in the empowering of that love.
I am in awe, sacredly, silently and reverently
As my covenant with ever living, ever loving hfe is realized and renewed.
Can I ever love enough, being so completely loved?
Can I ever uplift as 1 am uplifted?
A broader more vast expanding life has gifted me.
It is appropriate that life and my soul are aligned.
Heaven on earth is here.
June a3, igga
