Pacific Rim Productions
Stephen Greer MD
CSETI
Asheville,
December 15, 1994
Dear Stephen,
I would like to propose the weekend of January 7, 1995, for your trip to San Francisco.
This date suits our calendars, given our various commitments and it appears to be clear
on yours. In addition, it would give us all some time for some advance planning and the
holiday hoopla would be over.
Dan Drasin, Ralph Steiner and I would will attend meeting, representing, if you will, BBS's,
radio and television respectively. Ralph is contacting Bob Dean and inviting him along.
This is in the hope that his travel plans will permit him to attend. We feel just the four,
maybe five of us, will be ample given the ground we intend to cover. Is there anyone else
you would like to have along?
Our notion, at this time, is to meet for dinner at my home on Saturday evening. This will
ensure privacy. We're centrally located in the city, just a half and hour from the airport.
If you would be more comfortable, you are most welcome to stay with us. We'd be
delighted to see Emily too.
As you can imagine, the three of us are very eager to meet with you and explore the
possibilities of what we can accomplish working together, pooling our talents, resources
and moxie.
At this time, it's best to write to me at
I hope you enjoy your vacation and the holidays. 1995 is going to be a
very interesting year!
Sincerely,
