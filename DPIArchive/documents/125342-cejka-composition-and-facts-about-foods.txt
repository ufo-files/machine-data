One of the newer methods is to use water only to grow plant , this is
called Hydroponic gardening. In this type of gardening they try to balance
all elements needed by the plants for maximum growth and health. The
elements are add to water in parts per million • 1gram per 10,000grams or

a food chart this is close to the ratio of elements found in the chart.
