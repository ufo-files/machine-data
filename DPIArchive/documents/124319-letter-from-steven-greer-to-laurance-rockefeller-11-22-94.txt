              Center for the Study of Extraterrestrial Intelligence

                                                               Steven M. Greer, M.D.
                                                               International Director
                                      22 November 1994


Dear Mr.

Enclosed are three RMIT reports for the past 6 months which we would like for you to have. We
plan to send a team to Mexico and South America again within the next two months because of
extraordinary activity there. A recent series of events in Monterrey resulted in some of the best
video footage ever taken of a spacecraft in daylight. We have provided some of the Mexican
researchers with information regarding our protocols, and they are beginning to apply them in the
field with apparent success!

Thank you for your help with this effort to establish a viable and peaceful bridge between
humanity and other planetary civilizations.

Wishing you and your lovely wife a bountiful Thanksgiving and the best of all worlds.

Sincerely,


Steven M. Greer M.D.
Director of CSETI
