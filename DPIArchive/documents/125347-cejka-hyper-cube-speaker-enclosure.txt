To whom it may concern:
The HYPERCUBE Speaker Enclosure was discovered as a result of
research into the construction of four-dimensional structures.
Objects occupying four dimensions must be constructed of
four-dimensional materials; sound is four-dimensional in nature. When
hypercubes were made from sound it was noticed that the clarity and
overall sonic quality of music played through them was greatly
enhanced - and the HYPERCUBE Speaker was born. These speaker
enclosures fly in the face of conventional audio theory, but they
have been demonstrated to work very well indeed.
Licenses to manufacture the Hypercube Speaker Enclosure are now
available. This enclosure represents a major breakthrough in quality
sound reproduction. The efficiency, frequency response, and transient
response of any dynamic driver are dramatically improved when
coupled with the HYPERCUBE Speaker enclosure. These are not "paper
improvements" measurable only with sophisticated instruments; rather
they are obvious, audible improvements immediately apparent to the
consumer in the dealer's showroom.
Enclosed are copies of the patent sans claims and an engineering
report concerning the relative performance of a JBL L-40 speaker
system and the same drivers and crossover coupled with the
HYPERCURE speaker enclosure. Software helpful in the design of
HYPERCUBE speaker enclosures for any size driver is available from
Tesserax upon request. This software runs on an IBM-PC with a color
graphics card and at least 128K memory. A version of this software
that allows operation with a monochrome adapter and monitor is
presently being developed and will be available soon.
The ideal material for these enclosures is injection-molded DuPont
Rynite-545. However very good results have been obtained with 1/8"
aluminum sheet. 1/8" gum plywood works well if the driver size is 8"
or less. The material used must be both THIN and RIGID. NO ACOUSTIC
DAMPING MATERIALS OF ANY KIND ARE USED WITH THIS ENCLOSURE!

contact us at our address or the phone number below. Thank you.
