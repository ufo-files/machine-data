INTERVIEW WITH
CSETI FORT COLLINS, COLORADO WORKING GROUP COORDINATOR
18 MARCH 1995:
was in the Navy during the 1950s. In 1956 through 1958, he was on board a
ship stationed at the Dew Line in the Atlantic. At this time, there was a great conflict with
Russian trawlers.
was a radar technician. He related that time and time again he
and other crew members would observe radar blips of a solid object traveling from 3,600
o 4,000 mph at an altitude of 25,000 to 30,000 feet. The signals were very strong
reported that the blips on the radar screen would be as large as his thumb. He
stated that the radar would pick up three to four good signals, then the object would be
out of range. He stated that these signals often occurred during watch changes, around
10:00 pm and then around 2:00 am
would report these signals to the officer in
charge All activity was to be reported to NORAD; however, the officer in charge never
reported these anomalous signals. This ship had a RCA radar system.
Years later,
heard a talk by a man who mentioned he had a friend who had had
similar experiences.
Through this speaker,
was put in touch with the retired Air
Force man who was living in Virginia This Air Force man was stationed on a ship off the
coast of Alaska during the same period in the 1950s. He told
they regularly
picked up solid objects on radar traveling at tremendous speeds estimated at 3,500 to
4,000 mph The objects they tracked were located at 6,000 miles above the earth in deep
space. These highly unusual trackings were eventually put on tape. His ship had a GE
radar system and the man believes that the tapes may still be in existence somewhere
Once the Air Force became aware of the regularity of these objects being tracked by radar
on this ship, they blocked the radar so that it could only "see" up so far! The radar techs
lost the ability to track objects in deep space after the Air Force's intervention
