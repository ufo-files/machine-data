                                          September 25, 1966


Professor of Astronomy
Northwestern University
Las Cruces, New Mexico

 Dear Sir;
      Although several weeks has elapsed: I have not overlook-
 ed the questionnaire you sent me.
      I had a brother injured in an industral accident on Sept.
6, and havehad time to complete it, before now: I am sure you
 can understand my tardness and negelect.
      First, I wish to state once more, that I do not seek a re-
 ward or award for this information, and neither do I wish to be
 recognized in any manner conserving this matter. I am not at'raid
of public harrasement myself, but the effect upon the lives of
my small children is my upmost concern.
      I also hope, that this information will not be received in
any other manner than that for which it is intended.
      If this information will benfit our nation and my fellow-
man in any manner: I will then have received the only reward I
 would accept, and that alone will satisfy me; Just knowing, that
I hetped in such a small way.
      I am enclosingthree pages of drawings: I know, that you will
agree, that I am not an artist, but after many tries, it is the
best I can do. I hope that it will create some image of the object,
that I saw.
      You asked me on the phone: If there was a high voltege line
near me. I dont think I made that clear. Yes, about two and one
half blocks behind my home. It routes the entire load into our
community.
      I made notatiohon the drawings, which I hope will help de-
scribe it.
      I know, that there has been many reports of U.F.O. in the
news, and mine seems only a repition, except for the fact: I be-
lieve, that I saw a section closer than anyone else, and perhaps
 you might be able to find some of the answers you seek.
      As I viewed the object, I tried to determine its shape,
power, source and identy, because it was something that I had not
seen before and was effecting my eat the same as a telivision set
does when it is arcing. This alone convinced me, that I was view-
ing something, that was powered or operated by a highly electronic
magnetic impulse and by reversing this action it could change
directions and hover as it did.
      Perhaps this object could have been from outer space, but all
the thoughts I had at the time of sighting are possible in our
 world, material, power etc.
      Best wishes on your many investigations, and I pray, that when
the answer is found, that it will be in the best interest of our
nation and for the benifit o1 our fellowman.
                                             Respectfully,
