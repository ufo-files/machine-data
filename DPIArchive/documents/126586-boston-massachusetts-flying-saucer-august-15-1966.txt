Since it would be impossible to answer your questions
in the space provided on the form, I will add my comments on

On May 25, 1967, my wife and I were on the porch say-
ing good-bye to some friends about 10.30 at night. The sky was
clear but the moon was not in sight. I'he airplanes, jets, etc.
always fly over the house, so as a rule we ignore the lights of
the planes, but on this night, my wife noticed the bright red
light flickering and getting larger and brighter, so thinsing
it was a plane on fire, I ran into the house and got my 8x50
binoculars. I saw only the bottom of the ship which was round
and disit-like and about 50-60 feet in diameter, with a hole in
the bottom about 3-4 feet round. A bright red fame that app
peared to be about 15-20 feet long, was shooting downward out
of the opening in the center of the bottom of the craft.
There was no noise that could be heard on the ground,
and the craft seemed to be coming to a stop as it hovered ai an
estimated 5000 feet. the flame seemed to withdraw into the hole
in the underside and as it did, before I could blink my eyes,
the craft appeared to zig-zag out of sight.
The bottom of the craft, which I couldsee very clearly
looked as if it was made of heavy steel and was heavily riveted
not smooth like on a jet. Ihe body was not luminous, but the
flame was reflected on the under side of the craft making it
clearly visible.
