CO-OPERATION WITH CANADIAN FORCES
SIGHTINGS OF METEORS OR FIREBALLS
The Canadian Forces have requested our assistance in reporting
sightings of very bright meteors or fireballs, and are launching a country-
wide publicity effort to bring this to the attention of the Canadian public.
2.
A fireball is a bright meteor with a luminosity which equals or
exceeds that of the brightest planets. It is usually seen moving rapidly
across the sky and sometimes a trail of glowing particles is left behind.
The meteor may explode with a burst of light and a loud sound, that may
happen several times during a single fall.
