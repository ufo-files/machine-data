Next week, the House Oversight Committee Subcommittee on National Security, the Border, and Foreign Affairs will hold a UAP hearing. This will be the third ever Congressional hearing on UAP in modern U.S. history.
I have been asked to testify along with Cmdr. David Fravor, ASA Aircrew Leadership Council member, and David Grusch.
My goal is to share my experience, but also elevate the voices of other pilots who are seeing UAP every day and deserve answers. More than 30 commercial aircrew and military UAP witnesses have approached this organization, and our team is working to help share their reports with Congress and AARO.
It is encouraging to see Congress taking UAP witnesses seriously. For too long stigma has clouded transparency on this topic, and I am encouraged by the continued pressure from elected officials to solve this mystery.
I hope you’ll tune into the hearing next week. You can watch the livestream here: https://oversight.house.gov at 10am on Wednesday, July 26, 2023.
