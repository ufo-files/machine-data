Each July, EAA AirVenture Oshkosh draws more than 600,000 pilots, mechanics, engineers, and aviation enthusiasts to Wittman Regional Airport in Oshkosh, Wisconsin, the largest aviation event in the world and one of the most consequential gatherings of the aerospace community. This year, from July 20–26, Americans for Safe Aerospace will be on the ground at Booth 3128 in Hangar C, bringing UAP aviation safety education, reporting resources, and informed discussion directly to pilots and aviation professionals.
Visit Us at Booth 3128, Hangar C
Stop by throughout the week to connect with the ASA team. At the booth you can:
Meet Ryan Graves, Executive Director of Americans for Safe Aerospace and former U.S. Navy F/A-18F pilot
Take ASA's UAP survey and contribute your firsthand perspective to one of the most comprehensive ongoing data-collection efforts in the field
Submit a report: if you have had an encounter you have never documented, our team will walk you through the process on the spot
Learn how to report: what channels exist, why they matter, and how to use them
Explore the patterns: what ASA's database of over 1,100 documented encounters reveals about reporting trends, geography, and frequency
Follow the legislation: get up to speed on the bills ASA is actively supporting to close the gaps in airspace transparency and pilot protections
Demo ASA's new UAP Reports platform, an exclusive tool for members launching this year
Learn about ASA membership and ways to stay involved
ASA will also be conducting live podcast recordings at the booth and around the show throughout the week.
ASA Events at AirVenture 2026
Monday, July 20: 1:00 p.m. | EAA Forums, Forum Stage 2: Ryan Graves gives a talk at the EAA Forums on UAP as an aviation safety issue: covering what pilots are encountering, why the reporting system is broken, and what needs to change at the policy level.
Tuesday, July 21: 1:00 p.m. | AOPA Pavilion: Ryan Graves and the ASA team take the stage at the AOPA Pavilion to discuss the UAP safety data ASA has collected from general aviation pilots, current reporting resources available to members, and the legislative progress ASA is driving on Capitol Hill.
CANCELED: Tuesday, July 21: 2:00 p.m. | AOPA 39 Lounge: Following the AOPA Pavilion presentation, Ryan Graves and the ASA team will be available for a meet and greet at the AOPA 39 Lounge.Sturgeon Spirits Craft Distillery, 2663 Oregon Street, Oshkosh, WI
AirVenture is where the aviation community comes together to share what it knows and shape what comes next. For the pilots and professionals who have seen something they could not explain, and the many more who will, we hope to see you at Booth 3128, Hangar C.
