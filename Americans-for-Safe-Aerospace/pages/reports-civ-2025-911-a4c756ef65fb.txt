eventNarrative
A couple in Georgetown, Kentucky has been experiencing frequent encounters with multicolored/multi-shaped UAPs over their farm. The objects move in a seemingly playful manner, often flying around until three or four in the morning. The couple also reports that their farm animals, and even their neighbors cows become noticeably erratic whenever the objects appear at night.
