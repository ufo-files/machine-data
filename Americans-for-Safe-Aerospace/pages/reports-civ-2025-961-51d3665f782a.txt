eventNarrative
At 6:15 am on October 10th, 2025 a witness was out walking their dog when they observed two large triangular craft around the size of half a football field hovering nearby their house. Two two craft observed were triangular and had a singular red light and three white lights in a triangular formation.
At the initial time of the sighting, both UAP were seen hovering above a nearby energy storage facility. After observing the two craft for around a minute, one of them traveled directly towards the witness at a steady, constant speed similar to that of a helicopter. After a few minutes, the craft passed low over the witnesses house at an altitude of around 2500 to 3000 feet. During this fly-over, there was no noticeable sound associate with the craft other than a low hum that was barely audible. The witnesses dog also stopped to observe the craft. During the close pass, the witness was able to observe the skin of the craft, illuminated by the three triangular white lights. The skin appeared to be silverish and somewhat pixelated.

After the witness observed the craft travelling over their house, they returned inside to start their day. Mild physical affects were felt by the witness in the 24 hours following their observation.
