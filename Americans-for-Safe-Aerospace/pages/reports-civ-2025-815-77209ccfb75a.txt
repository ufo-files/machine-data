eventNarrative
At approximately 9:28 pm on July 12, 2025, on a perfectly clear evening in Hooksett, New Hampshire, the witness observed two red orbs hovering at tree-line height over their backyard. Each object appeared as a clear sphere, estimated at two to three feet across, described by the witness as about the size of a large beach ball , containing a smaller red plasma-like ball at its center that appeared to boil or churn, comparable to footage of the sun.
The two orbs moved silently and in synchronization, traveling toward the northwest at a steady, floating, consistent pace. The witness estimated the objects passed within 50 to 75 feet, a few hundred feet in front of them and just above the trees, and recorded video footage during the encounter. No sound, electromagnetic effects, or interaction with the surrounding environment were noted.
The witness characterized the objects as appearing intelligently controlled, citing their synchronized movement. Detection was both visual and via camera, and the report carries a high confidence interval.
