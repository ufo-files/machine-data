eventNarrative
At approximately 0117 hours on 2025-02-19, the sergeant and coworkers noticed a distant light low on the western/southwestern horizon while conducting training. After relocating to the top of a parking ramp (coordinates recorded in the case file), they observed the object through Bushnell binoculars. The object was described as spherical in appearance with rapidly changing lights over its surface, red, blue, green, and white, without a steady color, “like a tie-dye shirt in motion.”
During the first phase, the object drifted lethargically with gradual ascents and descents; the group estimated the object to be roughly ~50 miles away and between ~10,000–30,000 ft, though exact range/altitude were uncertain. After ~25 minutes, a helicopter flew south over Rogers, MN (between the observers and the object, ~12 miles from the observers). At that time the object quickly moved north and climbed, with the witness estimating a displacement of ~30–40 miles and a climb to ~40,000 ft within ~5–10 seconds.
Approximately ten minutes later, a small private airplane flew north between the observers and the object; no dramatic change was noted then. Thereafter the object’s movement again appeared slow and somewhat stagnant as it continued westward until nearly out of sight through binoculars. Before leaving, the witness scanned elsewhere and briefly saw a second object with similar lighting behavior to the north but did not remain to monitor it. The total observation time recorded is ~90 minutes.

witnessBackground
Reporting witness is a Patrol Sergeant with a suburban Minneapolis police department, serving as SWAT Team Leader, Master Firearms Instructor, and K-9 Unit Coordinator, working a rotating 12-hour schedule (1800–0600) supervising ~6 officers.
