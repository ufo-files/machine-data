1 shiny aluminum object like 2 disks welded together side by side, slowly descended. Schoof Was Driving In A Remote Mountainous Area On Lyons Valley Road Between Jamula And Barrett Junction. While He Was Watching This Ufo Is Was Slowly Descending. By The Time He Was Almost Out Of View It Appeared That It Might Be Landing. Other Than The Red Stripe Between The Two Halfs, There Were No Other Lights, Markings, Or Windows.
