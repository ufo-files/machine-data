4 yellowish-white,large round lights moving slowly. When The Witnesses Drove Nearly Beneath The Lights, The Lights Shifted To The R A Little. They Would Go In Circles And Stop, Repeat, Repeat, Then They Went In To The Middle Until They Met. The Lights Always Stayed The Same Distance Apart Until They Went To The Middle And Met. At The Instant Of Meeting, They Blinked Out And Disappeared.
