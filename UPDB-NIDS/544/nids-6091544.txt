It Was A Beautiful Day So Ed Set Up His Lawn Chair In His Backyard To Rest For Awhile And Enjoy The Weather. He Initially Set It Down So That He Would Be Facing South, Then On Impulse Turned So He Was Facing North. He Immediately Saw The Boomerang Shaped Ufo. For The First Five Minutes It Was Completely Stationary. The Point Of The Ufo Was Facing South. Then, With The Point Still Facing South, The Ufo Moved Sideways Toward The East For A Distance Then Stopped. He Then Saw An Illuminated Orb West Of The Boomerang Appear And Move At Rapid Speed Toward The Ufo. When It Reached The Ufo, The Orb Was No Longer Visible. Then The Boomerang Seemed To Rise Another 20,000' Until It Was Gone From Sight.
