He Works A Swing Shift At His Security Post (Roma Hills) And Sees A Lot Of Aircraft. He Used To Be An Aircraft Fueler At Mccarran, And Also Worked Some On Aircraft. At The Time Of This Sighting, He Saw What Looked Like A White Light, Falling At A Steep Angle Toward The Mountains W Of Here. It Fell Past Some Usual Aircraft, Down, Then "Went Into A Wormhole, Kind Of Like On Star Trek" And Disappeared Behind Mountains, Or Area 51. 

He Thinks It Could Have Been A Satellite, But It Didn'T Seem Like One. It Was Over The Whole Las Vegas Valley Area.
