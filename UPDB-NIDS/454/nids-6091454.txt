She Was Traveling In A Car, Therefore She Couldn'T Gauge The Speed Of The Object One Light Would Blink On And Move Around Very High In The Sky. Then It Would Blink Off And Two Would Blink On. They Danced Around One Another And Then One Would Blink Off And Then The Other. Then One Would Come Back, Then Another And Dance Around The Other One, Then A Third Blinked On, And They All Danced Around One Another. Then One Would Go Out, Then Another, Then The Last On Went Off, And That Was All I Saw Of Them.
