Rob Saw These Lights Streak Across The Sky In Front And Several Thousand Feet Higher Than His Jet. First He Saw The Bright Yellow Light And Then Right Behind It The Dim Yellow Light. After Just A Few Seconds They Faded From Sight. He Contacted Flight Control To Ask Is The Objects Were Detected On Radar. He Was Informed That They Were Not. It Was Too Fast For Him To Even Point Out To His Co-Pilot.
