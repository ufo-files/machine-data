At First It Was Thought That It Was A Squadron Of Helicopters And Since I Had My Daughters In The Backseat (Ages 8 And 10) I Wanted Them To See And Hear Them. Only When We Departed The Vehicle, It Immediately Hit Me That There Was No Sound. The Lights Were Triangle Shaped And White Only, With No Flashing Lights Like Most Aircraft. As I Stated Early Originally Thought It To Be A Squadron Of Helicopters In A Triangle Formation. They Flew As One Though Making Me Think Maybe It Was Just One Large Vehicle, Especially The Way It Changed Direction In An Instant From The Northwest Path, To A Norheast One. I Never Reported It Because I Was In The Military (Air Force) At The Time And Because The Location Was Very Near Many Military Installation To Include Norad Which Is Less Than 40 Miles From This Sighting. The More I Think About It, I'M Not Quite Sure Why We Dismissed What We Saw So Quickly.
