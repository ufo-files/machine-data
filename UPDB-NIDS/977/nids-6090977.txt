At A Approximately 8:00 Pm, Sunday December 26, 1999, Bethany Was Sitting On A Porch Of Relative In Holman, Alabama. She Looked In The Sky And Saw A Large White Light Moving Slowly From North To South At An Altitude Of Approximately 1/2 Mile. As The Light Was Moving, Smaller Lights Dropped Off The Major Light, Like Sparks From Fireworks. She Is Sure That It Was Not Fireworks, Airplane Or Helicopter. There Was No Sound And The Light Disappeared Behind The Trees.
