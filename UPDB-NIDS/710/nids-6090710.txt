I Was On My Back Porch Gazing At The Stars And Clouds. I Had Heard That There Was A Chance Of Snow On The Radio. Of The Few Stars That Were Visible, One Was Much Brighter Than The Rest. I Figured That It Was A Planet Until I Saw It Moving. It Moved In A Left To Right Zig-Zag Motion Very Quickly. I Watched It For About 5 Minutes And Then It Disappeared.
