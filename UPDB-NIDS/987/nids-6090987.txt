size:1/4 moon in diameter Shape:Diamond . "My Wife And I Were Driving North On Sepulveda Blvd., A Few Blocks North Of Sunset Blvd. At 7:44 Pm We Saw A Very Bright Green Diamond Shape. The Shape First Became Visible At The Top Of Our Windshield, And Was Last Seen Just Before The Mountains Obscured Further Vision. The Trajectory Was A Slight Curve, From High Straight Ahead To The Horizon, From Right To Left, Traveling The Total Distance In About 2 Seconds. We Heard No Noise; We Saw No Contrail. The Sky Was Clear. By Comparison, Aircraft Were Mere Points Of Light Moving Slowly, But This Was Very Large, Bright, And Fast."
