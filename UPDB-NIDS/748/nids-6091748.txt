The Witness Thought At First They Were Just Satellites, But When 1 Came From The S Traveling To The N, The Other Object Came From The W And Went E. They Almost Converged On Each Other But As They Closed In On Each Other, The 1 From Traveling S To N Disappeared And The Other Light Continued Going In Its Original Direction (W To E). The S To N Object Changed Direction To Further Eastward. 

He Has Looked For Them Subsequently, But Has Not Seen Them Again.
