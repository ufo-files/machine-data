3 huge globes in triangular formation moving very slowly. Was On Duty, Patrolling, Looking At The Sky, Saw Bright Light ~40K Ft Going N At A Real Clip; 20 Min Later Saw Another One, Same Type, Going Same Direction. Then Off To His L As He Faced S, He Saw 3 Globes, 1 Red, 2 Orange, Coming His Direction. Looked Thru Binocs, It Passed W/In 600-700 Ft & Could See Well W/O Binocs. Looked Again W/Binocs & Saw Panels,Square At The Top, Straight Across, Then Down In A V Like A Pyramid, Each Had A Dark Ridge.
