He Observed These Almost Straight Overhead As They Retraced Their Paths Without Turning, At About Mach 8 Speed. First There Was One Object; Then There Were Instantly 6 To 8 Objects. They Were Playing, Jumping Around In Zig-Zags, Almost Like A Show. They Were Transiently Stationary. Humans Could Not Have Handled The G-Forces Involved. There Was Intense Reflection Of Light Off These Objects. All The Objects Vanished, But One; It Left Toward The Ne At About Mach 10.

The Witness Called A Local Radio Station And Gave His Sighting Story On The Air. He Doesn'T Know If Others In The Area Also Witnessed This Display, Or If Anyone Else Called In To That Radio Station.

He Also Called The Faa And Spoke With Someone There.
