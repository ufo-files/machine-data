Orange "blob" about the size of venus appearing and disapperaing . This Witness Was 12 At The Time Of This Sighting. He Was In The Yard With Friends, Watching Stars, Noticed An Odd One, An Orange Blob About The Size Of Venus. The Witness Saw 3 Jets Flying In Formation Directly Toward The Object From The Nw; As They Neared, The Object Vanished. The Planes Began To Turn And The Object Reappeared. The Jets Went After It Again, And It Disappeared Again. This Happened 2 Or 3 More Times, Then The Planes Split Up And Went At The Object From Different Directions. The Object Disappeared Again, And Did Not Reappear. The Jets Circled Around For A Couple Of Minutes Then Left Back Toward The Direction They Had Come From. On His Way Home On His Bike, A Silent, Bright Searchlight Flew Over Him. 

The Witness Indicated In A Separate E-Mail That He Has An Implant And Has Talked With Whitley Strieber About It. A Notation In The File Reflects That This Witness And Eric Have Been Emailing Each Other For Some Time.
