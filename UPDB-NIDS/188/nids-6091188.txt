My Memory Is Vivid. We Noticed Erratic Movement Of A Craft Northeast Of Us -Over Clayton, Mo. It Glided To The Dark Eastern Sky. We Walked Further Along Our Subdivision (East To West - Parallel To Highway 40). It Glided Back Toward Us, Above The Highway, Until Almost Directly Overhead. It Then Stopped In Mid-Air. The Colors Eminating From The Craft'S "Belly" Were Odd To Me, But Seemed Distinct In My Friend'S Opinion. I Can'T Explain This One Difference In Our View Of It.
