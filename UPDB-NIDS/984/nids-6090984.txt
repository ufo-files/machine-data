15 feet x 20 feet Both round . She Had Gone Out The Back Door To Smoke And Saw The First Object Flying, Then Saw The Second One Coming Out Of (Or From Over) The Hill. She Called To Her Husband John To Come Watch, And He Did. She Thought It Was Either A Plane Or A Helicopter, But It Was Too Low And Slow-Flying To Be A Plane, And Made No Noise. John Got Cold And Went Back In, Then She Did Too.
