"I Was Out On My Deck About To Bring In The Cat (11:00 Pm) When I Heard A Low Rumbling. I Looked Toward The Airport (Pdx) Figuring A Jet Was Taking Off From The Base. The Sound Only Lasted A 5 Or 6 Seconds. That'S When I Saw The Light Rising Up. I Want To Also Point Out That Airlines Test Their Engines. The Light Was Very Bright Like A Flare But Seemed A Bit Larger In Size. When It First Rose Up It Was Extremely Bright But Then It Started To Move From The N To The S. When It Disappeared It Seemed To Rise Slowly Into The Cloud And Winked Out While In The Cloud. I Could See The Reddish Light Glowing Against The Cloud, I Could Only See Light From This Thing And Could Not Make Out Any Structure."
