It Seemed To Appear As A Ball Of Light, Then Take Shape, Hard To Tell The Size, If It Was Really Big And At 2000 Ft, Or Smaller And At 1000 Ft. Floated Without Sound Effortlessly Over Head, And Seemed To Be Moving Slow But Disappeared Over The Horizon In A Matter Of Moments. Beautiful, And Seemed To Be Just Like The Beginning Of Star Wars When The Ships With Lights On The Bottom Fly Over.
