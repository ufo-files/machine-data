Saw Article In La Times And Thought Her Sighting Might Be Of Help. The Three Witness Were Traveling Along Route 81. Dianne, Who Was In The Back Seat, Saw The Ufo'S First. After Watching Them In Silence For A While, She Brought Them To The Attention Of William And Bill. They All Watched Them Together Then For About Three Minutes. They All Describe The Objects Flying In And Out Of The Clouds Several Miles In The Distance. Each Witness Describes The Same Movements, But Give Different Descriptions Of The Shapes Of The Ufo'S. Dianne Thought They Were Triangular, William Rectangular And Bill Circular. William Gives An Interesting Slant In His Description When He Says The Objects And Their Movements Reminded Him Of Huge Chunks Of Cardboard Or Debris Flying About In A Huge, Powerful Whirlwind.
