6 black, triangles - 1 very large, 5 smaller flew slowly and low. I Was Young, Ten, Camping With My Grandparents In A Camper And They Smoked Which I Didn'T Like So When I Knew They Were Finally Asleep I Went Outside To Get Fresh Air And Saw The Craft Coming Over The Trees From The North. They Passed Overhead And I Watched Them As They Moved To The South. I Didn'T Really Know This Was A Phenomenon That Others Were Experiencing So I Passed It Off As Child'S Imagination Until I Heard About It On Coast To Coast And Figured I Would Report It.
