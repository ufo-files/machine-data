"I Was Actually Building A Deck And So, It Was About 2 A.M. Well Then It Started Moving And Blinking So I Thought It Was A Plane. But Then All Of A Sudden It Just Stopped And Then It Made Some Weird Moves, I Mean To The Point That You Didn’T Know, You Couldn’T, You Know, It Wasn’T A Helicopter Or Anything Like That, And It Made No Noise. Anyway, Long Story Short, I Went, Woke The Wife Up, Which Really Upset Her, Because She Had To Go To Work The Next Day, But You Know, I Just Wanted Her To See It And Know What, Or Try To Get An Idea Of, As To What It Was. But My Autofocus Was Having A Hard Time Because The Moon Was So Bright That Night. But, So That’S Why I Haven’T Even Taken A Chance To Look At It Yet. Well, I Was Gonna Erase The Tape So Yesterday I Decided To Look At It Before I Erased It, And I’Ll Be Damned If I Didn’T Catch A Great Shot Of It."
