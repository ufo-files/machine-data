Ewing Saw This From His Hotel Room At The Desert Inn In Las Vegas. His Friend, Owensby Saw It First And Called Ewing To The Window To See It. The Object Primarily Made Huge Circlular Flights In The Sky, Ocassionally Flying Off, Only To Return Quickly And Make More Circles. What Made It Even More Incredible To Him Was The Quickness And Agility Of Such A Huge Object.
