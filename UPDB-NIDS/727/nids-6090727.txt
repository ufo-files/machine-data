This Witness And A Group Of Others Were Watching This Object As They Made Their Report. They Were Videotaping, And Could See "Bites" Taken Out Of The Top And Bottom Of It. About 40 Minutes After Making His Report, He Called Back To Advise That It Had Become Really Bright Red, Was Getting Smaller And Going Lower In The Sky, Coming Down But Going Away.

They Live Out In The Country, And There Is Very Little Light Pollution.

He Reported He Had Seen It The Night Before Too, But It Was Only 15 Miles Away, Over The Trees By His House.
