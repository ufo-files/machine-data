No One Has Ever Believed Me About This, Or Had A Similar Sighting. I'Ve Taken Flying Lessons And I Know What Planes Look Like, Day Or Night. Lights Pulsated Along Its Perimeter, Red, Green & Blue; Not The Coordinated Pulsing Of Standard Aircraft Lights. I Was Driving Home At Night And Saw It As I Drove Along The Highway. It Struck Me As Really Odd, Because Of The Wide Shape, Lights, And Slow Speed--- Well Below The Stall Speed Of A Normal Jet Airplane Of A Comparable Size.
