1 bright blue white rod of light, pulsating brighter every second.. Brandon And His Friends Saw This Ufo Initially As A Bright Blue White Rod About 500' In The Air Over The Bottle Hollow Resevoir. They Watched It Go Straight Down Into The Water. They Did Not Detect A Splash. It Emerged Immediately As A Bluish Purple Orb Of Light About The Size Of A Basketball And Moved Sporadically Around The Water And The Beach, Never Getting Very High. It Freaked Them Out So They Left While The Ball Was Still Visible.
