1 large bell/acorn-shaped, bronze object with blue, green & red lights and window-like portals flew slowly. 5 Saw It, 3 Remember It, 1 Not Very Well. It Appears To Be Similar To The Kecksburg Case, Which Is Not Very Far From This Location.
