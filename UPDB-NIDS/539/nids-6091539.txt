Multiple witnesses observe UFO that flew like an aircraft at night. On February 7, 1990 At 2030 Hrs, Dennis Zermeno, Was With A Large Group Of People At The Paseo Stadium In Guam, Coaching Softball. At The Time, The Whole Crowd Watched A Ufo Fly Over The Stadium From 20 Degrees To 90 Degrees For Several Seconds. It'S Speed Was Constant And It Had The Alititude Of An Aircraft Coming In For A Landing. It Traveled From West To East. It Appeared As Though It Had An After Burner. No Shape Was Discernible . Dennis Said There Were Plenty Of Other Witness, But He Failed After Several Weeks To Provide Names Of Any
