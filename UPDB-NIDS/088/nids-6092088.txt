I Went Outside To Shut A Shed Door Knocking In The Wind. I Looked Up Into The Sky And Saw An Orange Star. It Looked Like Mars At First. It Took Me A Second To Realize That It Was In The Wrong Place In The Sky To Be Mars. Then It Started To Move, So I Was Sure It Wasn'T Mars. It Moved A Little Ways Rather Slowly Then Stopped. It Then Dropped A Smaller Orange Light Out From Underneath It And The Smaller Light Took Off In The Other Direction. Then The First One Started Moving Again.
