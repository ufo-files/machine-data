They Noticed The Saucer Following Them Shortly After Leaving Kingman. It Followed Them Low Level For About 22 Miles. When The Stopped At The River, The Ball Appeared In Front Of Them And Electricity, Like Static Electricity, Shot Out From It To The Saucer. They Got The Impression The Ball Was Not Pleased With The Saucer And Was Forcing It To Follow The Ball. Both Objects Then Went Straight Up In A Flash.
