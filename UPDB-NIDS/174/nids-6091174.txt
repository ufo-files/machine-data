1 black, very big triangular craft with lights on each corner and rotating in center, flying slow and low. I Saw No Details On The Craft Other Than The Lights. I Remember Determining Its Shape Mostly By The Way It Blocked Out The Stars Behind It. I Was 12 At The Time And There Was At Least One Other Kid With Me, Though I Can'T Remember His Name.
