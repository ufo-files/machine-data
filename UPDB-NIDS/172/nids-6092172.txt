My Boyfriend Spotted It First, "Oh, There Is My Ufo", He Said. "What?" "Just Watch." Here Is What I Saw: A Multi-Colored Ball Of Flashing Light Moving Rapidly Towards Us. About Six Miles From Our Car, Almost Dead Center Of The Triangulation Of Former Norton Afb, The Interchange Of The 215/10 Freeways, And Ontario National Airport The Light Flipped Over Some 45 Degrees And Turned Into A Silver-Metallic V-Shaped Vehicle (?). As It Turned Toward The City Of Colton, The Vehicle Turned Back Into A Flashing Ball. At That Point It Immediately Dropped 90 Degrees Plunging Toward The Ground. But It Did Not Crash As Half-Way Into Its Descent, The Air Sort Of Opened Up, Swallowing The Craft. We Never Observed Any Kind Of Vapor Or Chem-Trail; And We Were Too Far Away To Detect Sound. After The Object Disappeared, My Boyfriend Replied, " I Am So Glad You Saw That. I'Ve Seen It 4 Times In The Last 10 Day; I Thought I Was Crazy!"
