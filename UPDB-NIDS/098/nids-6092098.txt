2 triangular objects flying low and slow. We Lived Approximately 15-18 Air Miles Southwest Of Castle Air Force Base In Atwater, California At The Time. When We Arrived Home In The County We Noticed A Low Humm. If It Were Not For The Fact That The Stars Seemed To Disappear We Would Not Have See Them. Also At That Time Of Night We Could See The Lights Of Atwater/Merced And Almost Always There Were Planes Going In And Out Of Castle. During The Entire Time Approx 20-30 Miniutes There Were No Planes In The Area. Within 10 Minutes Of The Objects Leaving There Were A Number Of B-52 And Kc-135 Landing At Castle, It Was Almost Like A Bee Hive. Also There Was An Object Of Light That Traveled From One To The Other At 25 Or So Mph. That Was The Only Lights We Saw. These Object Had To Be At Least 1000 Ft Long And 200 + Ft Wide. They Were No More Than 500 Ft High And Blocked Our View Of The Stars For More Than 1300 Ft Or A 1/4 Mile Long And About 600 To 800 Ft Wide
