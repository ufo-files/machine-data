Ellen Was Out Walking Her Dogs When She Spotted This Ufo. She Called Her Husband Out To See It Also. The Three Red Lights Were Rotating, That Is They Were Never All On At The Same Time. After It Departed They Saw Other Flying Lights, (Red Dots), Flying In The Direction The Large Ufo Had Gone. These Flying Red Lights Flashed In A Distinct Patter: Flash, Flash, Flash, Pause. Then That Pattern Would Repeat Itself. They Live About 30 Miles From Leemore Naval Air Station. They Called The Air Station And The Navy Denied That It Was Any Of Their Craft.
