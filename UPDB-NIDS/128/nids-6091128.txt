1 Lens/Saucer Shaped, Silver/Mercury Colored object passed overhead. Witness Saw A Round Shadow On Desert; As It Went Overhead, He Could See Train Reflected On Object'S Belly In "An Optical Curve." Witness Had The Impression It Was "Hermetically Sealed." The Metal Skin Was In Perfect Mathematical Curvature As There Wasn'T The Slightest Imperfection In Its Reflection. It Did Not Have A Single Screw Or Metallic Plate. Looked Exactly Like 2 Convex Mirrors On Top Of Each Other. Round Mirrors He Sees Today Remind Of Him What He Saw Then.

Catalog Entry: N-217
Shape: "like 2 convex mirrors on top of each other"
Size (Meters): 15 meters diam.
