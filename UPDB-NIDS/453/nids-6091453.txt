2 slow flying, white triangular/arrowhead shaped objects in a line. "Leading Object Flew Steadily But Following One Was Swinging In And Out Of Front Ones Flight Path In A Manner I Can Best Describe As Playful Since It Would Seem To Have Been Unnecessary. Although Sky Was Clear I Was Struck By The Fact That These Objects Would Have Been Perfectly Invisible Against A Creamy White Cloud Background."
