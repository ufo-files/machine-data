Size:Like a VW Shape:Fireball . "We Were Just Lying The Four Of Us Looking For Shoot Stars All In Our Sleeping Bags Or Lying On Top When Suddenly I Found Myself Standing On The Perry'S Back Porch. I Was Shocked To Find Myself There But My Friends Out In The Yard Were Jumping Up & Down And Acting Like 12 Year Olds Who Had Just Seen Something Astonishing. Now There Is No Reason For Me To Be On The Perry'S Porch At 3:00 In The Morning. My House Was Much Closer And There Is A Restroom In The Back Of The House. I Yelled What Happened! They Said Didn'T You See It? I Said No Where Was I? They Said They Didn'T Know, That I Was Probably In The Bath Room. That Could Not Have Been. Boys Do Not Go Into The House To Pee. Anything Else, I Would Have Used My Own Restroom. My Friends Always Became Irritable When I Tried To Talk About It, Even Years Later."
