1 dark brown, rectangular object with a a domed front, hovering. One Night, Near The Green River, Mike Saw Lights, Like Those Spot Lights Used For Advertising Openings, Coming From An Area Near The Airport. A Couple Of Weeks Later He Was In The Area So He Got Out And Looked Around. It Was Then That He Saw This Ufo. He Also Saw A Very Low Flying Cessna, But That Was Not Unusual Since He Was Near The Airport. He Got The Impression That The Ufo Was Watching The Him And The Cessna. Mike Said He Looked Away For Just An Instant And The Object Was Gone.
