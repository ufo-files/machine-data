On An Unknown Day During July 2000, Kristy Andrew And Her Husband Were Working In Their Yard At Their Home, Located 9 Miles W Of Duchesne, Ut, Along Highway 40. An Object About An Inch In Size Fell From The Sky. This Object Is Described As Being Round And Having A Black Outer Lining And Gray Glass Center. According To Kristy Andrews, The Object Landed About 2 Feet From Where Joseph Andrews Was Standing, And Went 3 To 4 Inches Into The Ground. The Object Was Warm When Removed From The Ground And The Black Outer Substance Could Be Chipped Off. 

On 02/21/01 The Object Was Loaned To Pete Pickup To Be Sent To Nids For Further Examination - To Be Returned When Nids Is Finished With It.
