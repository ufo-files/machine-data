The Lights Were Seen West Of Dyess' Location On Hwy 28. He Was Driving When He Saw The First Two Lights, One Over The Other With About 150' To 200' Between Them. When They Dimmed Out, It Was About Another 45 Seconds Before The Third And Fourth Appeared. Off In The Distance He Saw Helicopters With Strobe Lights. Dyess Thinks It Was The Military Playing With Some, "New Toys."
