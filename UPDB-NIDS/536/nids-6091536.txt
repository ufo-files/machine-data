She Was In The Loading Area Behind An Albertson'S Grocery Store. The Object Was Moving So Slowly That She Had To Compare It With A Stationary Object To Be Sure It Was Moving. It Was Traveling West, But Then It Made A Slow U-Turn And Went East While Descending. The Last Time She Saw It, The Object Seemed To Have Settled Into The Hills About 1 To 3 Miles Away. She Could Still See It, But She Couldn'T Tell In The Darkness If It Had Landed Or Was Just Hovering Near One Of The Hills.
