I Was Looking At The Stars At About 1 Am On 10-8-03. My Parents Backyard Faces East. As I Scanned The Sky I Saw Near The Horizon A Flash Of Light. It Was Moving South To North And Moved Across My Entire Field Of View Within One Second. I Saw It Because It Flashed. It Was Bright Blue Light And Lasted About 1 Sec When It Went Off, Rendering The Object Invisible Against The Night Sky. For Full Comments See E-Mail.
