Size:Softball at arm's length Shape:Round . From The Transcript Of The Taped Interview With Investigator Pete Pickup:

"When We Were Coming From Working At The Temple, And I Believe It Was 2 Years Ago, I Believe It Was In The Fall, It Was Evening, We Had Done Stopping At Dinner [?] So I Was In, Around 7:30, Kinda When The Sun’S Going Down But It’S Not Dark, You Know? And Just Above The Hill, We Could Just See This Big Round Thing Sitting Above The Hill, Kinda Like A Basketball, Kinda Yellow-Orange Color, Wasn’T Moving, Just Sitting There. So, It Wasn’T The Sun Or The Moon, Wasn’T The Right Size For Those Things, So We Thought ‘Well Gee, That’S Odd.’ So We Were Kinda Watching It And After Watching It For A Few Minutes, All Of A Sudden It Just Went Straight Down To The Ground. And It Was Gone."

Object May Have Moved Downward Out Of Sight Behind The Bluff At The Utah Ranch.

Following This Interview, Colleen Agreed To Make A Sketch Of The Object; However, She Requested A Few Days To Go To The Location On Highway 40 Where The Object Was Noted, Before Doing So. Colleen Will Also Make An Attempt To Persuade Her Husband, Virgil, To Provide An Interview.
