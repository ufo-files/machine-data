1 fat-cigar-shaped, dull brownish, metallic object moving quickly. She Remembers Incident Vividly. She Was Coming Home From Work, On Her Way To Pick Up Her Daughter, When She Saw It Ahead Of Her, Thought It Was A Blimp. She Pulled Car Over To Get A Better View & Stepped Halfway Out Of Car, But By Then It Was Gone. She Cut Off Her Engine So She'S Sure There Was "Absolutely No Noise." The Sky In The Direction It Was Going Was Clear But She Saw No Further Sign Of It - "It Was Like It Saw Me And Hauled Off." Wishes She Had Sketched It At The Time, But Her Husband At The Time Told Her Not To Say Anything About It To Anyone.
