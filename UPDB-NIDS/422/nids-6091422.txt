2 V shaped, black objects traveling slowly back and forth. Sometime In The Spring Of 1988, Russel Baker Was At His Residence Summerville, South Carolina, At About 2145 Hrs, A Clear Night. For 40 Minutes He Watched 2 V Shaped, Black Ufos. They Were Traveling About 3 To 4 Miles Per Hour, About The Size Of A Large Jet. Each One Had A White Light Underneath It In The Center. Each One Had An Additional Blue And Green Light. The Ufo'S Were Near To Him, Traveling In A East To West Direction. For Awhile He Watched Them Fly Back And Forth In The Sky To All Points On The Compass Before Leaving In The Direction Of The Ocean. As They Traveled, One Followed The Other In Perfect Unison. Baker Sensed An Intelligence, As If The Ufo'S Knew He Knew They Were There.
