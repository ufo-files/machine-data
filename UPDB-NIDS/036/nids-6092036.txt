1 black, boomerang shaped craft flew over rootop. "We Thought The Wind Was Coming Up, And Looked Up To See The Treetops Were Still. The Sound Of Wind Kept Approaching And Then The Craft Flew Into View. It Was So Low That Keeping Our Eyes On It Required Turning About Until It Disappeared Over The Rooftop. The Sound Faded As Quicly As It Had Come. The Power Came Back On Within Several Minutes."
