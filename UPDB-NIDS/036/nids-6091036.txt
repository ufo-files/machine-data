I Have Just Read An Article By Leonard David About Black Triangles. I Am So Excited That I Can Hardly Write. This Is What I Saw 20 Years Ago. I Didn'T Report It At The Time, Because I Thought No One Would Believe Me, And I Was Going Through A Divorce At The Time And Had A Lot Of Other Stuff To Deal With. But I Still Remember It, And I Swear On My Kids' Life That I Saw It. Unlike The Description In The Article, It Was Not Open, But Has A Bubble Like Clear Cockpit, And I Could Clearly See 2 Figures Inside. It Just Came Up Over My House And Flew Right Over Me In Broad Daylight. Seeing Is Believing.
