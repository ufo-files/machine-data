I Used To Live Near An Airbase And Have Never Seen Such A Craft.I Thought At The Time It Must Be A New Spy Plane Headed On N. Korea. But The Fact That It Was So Low Really Had Me Agitated. And The Silence Of It Was A Real Shock. I Forgot All About It And Now Teach At Uni Of Japan (Same Area) And Read In Msnbc An Account Of Similar Sightings Of Such Low-Flying, And Silent Craft That I Thought I May Have Seen The Same Type Back Then.
