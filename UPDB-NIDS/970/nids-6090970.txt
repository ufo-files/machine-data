1 disk-shaped object of dull brushed aluminum with tiny horizontal grooves around it flying slow & low. Saw Huge Object Way Up In The Sky, Blocking Out Stars (1 Mile In Diameter). At High Altitude There Was Enormous Black, Perfect Circle W/6 Others Positioned Equally Apart From Each Other Around Large Center Circle. They Were Much Darker Than The Moonlit Sky & Blocked The Starts Behind Them. Size Of Large Circle Seemed ~Few Hundred Ft Across.
