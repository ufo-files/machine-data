The Forward Apex Held The Brightest Of The Three Lights I Could See, While The Two Tailing Ends Each Contained A Dimmer Light. It Was Moving Parallel To Interstate 15 In A Generally Southward Direction. I Remained Stationary So Could Not Easily Judge Its Distance From Me, But Thought It To Be Between 2 And 4 Miles. The Mountain Backdrop Behind It Helped Me To Clearly See It Shape And Movement And To Help Judge Its Size. It Was Solid And Black, Except For The 3 Lights I Described, All Somewhat Dimmer Than Commercial Aircraft. From My Angle And Distance, It Appeared To Be In The Shape Of A Huge Wishbone. I Was In My Home In Las Vegas With My Lights Out At The Time. I Watched, But Never Saw It Again. I Recalled Writing About It In My Journal And Referred To The Notes For This Report. I'Ve Never Told Anyone About This Sighting.
