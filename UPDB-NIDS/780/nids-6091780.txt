"As We Were Going On Down The Canyon We Could See An Airliner Heading West. And By Then It Was Getting Kind Of Late, And It Was Leaving A Contrail Behind It. You Could See The Sunlight Reflecting Off It. It Was Kind Of Orange. Then We Noticed Another One, Another Airliner Just Below It And Behind It. It Looked Like It Was Pretty Close To It. We Noticed It Was Leaving No Contrail. The One In Front Was, The One In Back Wasn’T. And They Were At Roughly The Same Altitude. We Thought That Was Pretty Unusual. From Our Perspective They Were Both At The Same Altitude. And As We Were Watching This Thing It Did The Same Thing As The Helicopter. It Became Translucent And Vanished. "
