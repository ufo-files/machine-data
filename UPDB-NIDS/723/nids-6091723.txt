This Witness And His Friend Were Out Driving Around The Local Reservoir Because It Was Such Nice Weather. He Saw Something In The Sky And Thought It Was Venus, But It Was Shooting Things Out Of It. Then It Shut Off, Blinked Out. His Friend Hadn'T Been Able To See It At This Point. Then, It Moved Around Very Fast, Like A Swarm Of Mosquitoes, Then Disappeared Only To Reappear Again In Another Part Of The Sky. This Repeated Several Times.

The Objects Were Not Lights; They Were Reflecting The Setting Sun And Were Brightly Lit. He Did Have A Sense Of Their Distance From Him. He Stated That He Tried Very Hard To Come Up With Other Hypotheses, But Couldn'T. Prior To This Incident, He Considered Himself A Total Nonbeliever; Now He Feels He'S "Just Seen The Face Of God."

He Is Unsure Whether The Objects Actually Touched Down, But He Doesn'T Think So.
