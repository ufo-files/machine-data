I Noticed Very Bright Lights Outside The Car And Asked My Friend If They Had Left Any Exterior Lights On. All Of A Sudden The Car Was Surrounded By Very Intense Bright White Lights. They Were Like Cylinders Coming Down About As Big Around As A Gallon Mild Jug. They Started Somewhere High Out Of My Sight As I Sat In The Car And Shone Down As A Round Beacon. As Soon As One Would Shine Down, It Would Go Out And The Next One Would Come. These Lights Went Around The Car Many Times Very Fast Then Simply Vanished. The Whole Incident Lasted Only About A Minute And During That Time I Rolled Down The Window.
