Richard And Two Other Employees Were Walking On The Station Property When Some Movement Caught His Eye. He Looked Up And Saw This Object. He Pointed It Out To The Others. One Of Them Said, "Oh, Yeah, We See Those All The Time," Implying It Belonged To The Air Force. Richard Was Only At Fergus Falls For Two Weeks, Long Enough To Set Up The Station. He Does Not Remember The Names Of The Employees.
