1 delta wing shaped object, black with very small red, white lights. The Stars Were Out That Night. While Looking At The Stars I Saw This Big Wing Flying Over . I Didn'T Hear Any Noise At First Until It Was Directly Overhead, And Then It Was Like A Very Small Engine Putting Away, Maybe Turning A Propelling Type Device. The Eastside Newsjournal Just East Of Seattle Reported About Two Weeks Later About The Craft And Had A Picture In The Paper. This Happened In 1997 Or 98 In The Summer Time.
