The Object Was Moving Too Slow To Gauge Speed. It Was More Stationary Than Mobil. After A Few Seconds It Dropped A Smaller Light That Looked The Same As The Main Ufo, Just Smaller. The Smaller Light Dropped Toward The Earth At An Incredible Rate Of Speed Before Disappearing. All During This Time The Main Ufo Was Blinking On And Off. When It Would Blink Out, The Four Lights Underneath Would Remain Visible, Until It Blinked Out The Last Time. Then All The Lights Disappeared.

Catalog Entry: N-914
City: Humboldt
Shape: Round, like a star.
Size (Meters): Like a large star or planet in night sky.
