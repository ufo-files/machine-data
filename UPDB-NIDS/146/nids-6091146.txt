Joe And Some Other Usaf Security Police Had Been At Nellis Afb For A Softball Game. They Were On Their Way Back To The Angels Peak Radar Site, (Henderson Mountain), On Us 95 When The Site Called Them And Asked If They Could See Anything Over The Site. They Saw This Described Ufo Hovering Just Feet Over The Radar Site. The Radar Site Informed Them They Had Tracked This Object From The Coast Of Either Washington Or Oregon All The Way To Their Site. Joe And The Others Saw It Until They Were Nearly At The Base Of The Mountain. Then It Vanished. Joe Cannot Provide The Names Of Any Of The Other Witnesses.
