1 round, light orange ball of light moving very slowly. The Significance Of This Sighting Is That Meyer Saw This Ufo While Investigating A Crop Circle. Meyer Said The Crop Circle Was Already 2 Or 3 Days Old By The Time He Saw It. Meyer Was Investigating The Crop Circle As Part Of His Duties With Mufon. The Light Was Pointed Out To Meyer By The Farmer Whose Land Contained The Crop Circle. The Farmer Asked Meyer If He Saw The Light Over By The Shed. Meyer Responded By Saying, "You Mean The Area Light On The Shed?" The Farmer Told Him No, That There Was No Electricity Near The Shed. That'S When Meyer Realized He Was Seeing A Ufo. Meyer Said That About A 1/2 Hour After The Disappearance Of The Light And The Departure Of The Farmer, That He And His Wife Felt A Presence, As Thought Somebody Was Watching Them.
