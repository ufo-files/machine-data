He And His Partner Moved Their Police Vehicle Several Time, Always Getting Closer To The Object To Get A Better View. Finally, He Used A Secure, Scrambled Police Fequency To Contact One Of Their Fire Stations Which Was Nearer To The Object And On The South Side Of Where It Appeared To Be Hovering.
