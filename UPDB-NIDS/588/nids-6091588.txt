There Were Very Few Clouds That Day. He Went Out To Check His Mail And Get Some Air. He Looked Up And Saw A Jet Leaving A Contrail. Then He Saw 2 Disks That Came Up Behind The Jet At 2-3X The Speed Of The Jet. One Disk Sped Up And Went Around The Jet While The Other Disk Tailed It. They Both Banked Off And Was Gone. The Jet Seemed To Disappear As Well.

The Witness States There Is No Air Force Base Nearby. Griffiths Used To Be Close By But Closed Some Years Ago.
