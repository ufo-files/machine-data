1 blue/gray saucer with rotating lights flying very slow and low; developed a great interest in science & engineering both physical & theoretical. He Saw This As A 13 Year Old While Out Snow Sledding Near His Home. At Its Closest, It Was Only 24' From Him. "It Resembled The Jupiter 2 Spacecraft On On The Old 'Lost In Space' Television Series Except That There Were No Other Visible Lights Or Winodws On The Thing Except For The Light Panels Located Just Below The Midline Of The Craft, Going Around Its Circumference. The Hull Of This Craft Resembed A Stuccoed Or Blown In Ceiling,... However, It Was Not As Bumpy, ...More Of A Skin-Like Or Corkboard Appearance. ... Approx 12' Thick Through The Center With A Curve That Met At The Centerline ... Like Two Saucers Inverted One On Top Of The Other"
