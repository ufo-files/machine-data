This Is A Goo Case. When Loren Was About 15, He And A 14-Year Old Friend Had Been Camping At His Family'S Cabin In Northern Wisconsin In June Or July Of 1971. The Boys Were Out Walking Around One Night After Dark, And Loren Noticed A Glowing Object On The Side Of A Hill By The Road. They Walked Over To It, Saw That It Was A Glowing Green Gob Of Goo, And They Took Off Running. He Doesn'T Remember Much Else, Except That He Wishes No He'D Had The Sense To Take A Littler Closer Look At It.
