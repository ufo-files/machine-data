He Saw It On His Way To Work. It Was Round, And Brightly Lit With 1 Blinking Light. He Thought At The Time That The Moon Was Pretty Bright Considering They
Were Forecasting Rain. At The Time He Didn'T Realize It Wasn'T The Moon. After He Realized It Wasn'T The Moon, His Mind Started Playing Games With Him And He Then Began Imagining It Was A Ufo. He'D Never Seen Anything Like It Before, Or Since. When He First Saw It, He Was In Tree Cover, And By The Time He Got To An Opening It Was Pretty Far Away, But He Watched It There For Awhile.

He Wonders If This Was A Real Ufo Or A Local Weather Balloon. But He Has Never Seen Anything Like It Before.
