JUSTIFICATION
--- seg-5c0df6cea20f766aa81a089e [needs-review] ---
In July 2023, the United States Congress held its first public hearing on Unidentified Flying Objects (UFOs, or UAPs). Military personnel testified about their experiences with UFO sightings and addressed the topic seriously, highlighting concerns regarding national sovereignty and the environment.

"UAPs, whatever they may be, can pose a serious threat to our military and civilian aircraft, and that needs to be understood. We should encourage more reports, not less, on UAPs. The more we understand, the safer we will be," said Democratic Representative Robert Garcia.

Ufology, the field dedicated to the study of unidentified flying objects and associated activities, has garnered interest not only for its cultural relevance but also for potential connections between the phenomenon and the environment. It is important to note that a UFO does not necessarily imply an extraterrestrial ship; the term is used to describe something that could not be identified by the observer.

On June 24, 2024, 77 years passed since intense work by ufology scholars in various areas of study began, with the goal of informing society about the well-known phenomenon of flying saucers. This began on that date in 1947 when American pilot Kenneth Arnold spotted a craft near Mount Rainier in the state of Washington (USA).
