In July 2023, the United States Congress held its first public hearing involving Unidentified Flying Objects (UFO or UAP). At the time,
military personnel provided testimonies about their experiences with UFO sightings and
approached the subject seriously, highlighting concerns about national sovereignty and
the environment.

"UAPs, whatever they may be, can pose a serious threat to our military and civilian
aircraft, and this needs to be understood. We should encourage more
reports, not less, on UAPs. "The more we understand, the more secure we will be," said Democratic Representative Robert Garcia. Ufology, the field of study dedicated to investigating unidentified flying objects and associated activities, has awakened interest not only for its cultural relevance but also for possible relationships between the said phenomenon and the environment. It is worth noting that an OVNI does not necessarily mean an extraterrestrial ship; the term is used to describe something that could not be identified by those who observed the phenomenon. On June 24, 2024, 77 years of intense work by ufology scholars in their various fields of expertise came to a close, with the goal of informing society about the well-known phenomenon of flying saucers.
