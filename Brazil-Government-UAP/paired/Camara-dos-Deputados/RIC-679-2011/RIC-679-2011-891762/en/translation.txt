On April 20, 2010, this parliamentarian received from the First Secretariat the transfer of the answers from Minister of Defense, Dr. Nelson Jobim, to the Information Request of the Chamber No. 4470 of 2009 (RIC 4470/2009), through Ofício 4225-GM/Aspar-MD, informing about the diligences carried out due to public information requests protocolled at the Presidency of the Republic's Civil House and six other Ministries, including the Defense Ministry, carried out by scholars of the UFO phenomenon. The petitioners invoked the executive power using Law 11.111/2005 at the end of 2007, however, two years later, the demand had not been fully met. Given the delay of that Ministry in applying legal dictates, the commission formed by scholars of this controversial phenomenon, even recognized by our Brazilian Air Force, requested our intervention with this Legislative House to ask for speed and full attention to their request, which was initially done through the aforementioned Request.

Based on the answers from Mr. Minister, specifically the affirmations that "all related documents to the subject (UFO) that had some degree of secrecy were declassified due to the legal time limit," that the Documentation and Historical Center of the Brazilian Air Force (CENDOC) is the overall responsible for the subject in his Ministry, and that it would close on August 31, 2010, the remittance of its entire archive to the Regional Coordination of the National Archive in the Federal District (COREG); it was noticed, after the end of this stipulated deadline, the persistence of several pending issues, as well as the emergence of new demands. And, moreover, it was detected what was already assumed, i.e., that not everything requested by CBU in 2007 was - as it was not - sent to that Authority. Thus, many of the already detected and questioned absences in RIC 4470/2009 remained absent in the declassified documents' lots. And even if Mr. Minister made the above affirmations, there are consistent historical documentary indications that contradict them. The indications and research carried out by independent UFO phenomenon researchers point to the fact that, when all information is confronted, the actions of the Armed Forces' Commands do not validate the statements of their supreme command.
