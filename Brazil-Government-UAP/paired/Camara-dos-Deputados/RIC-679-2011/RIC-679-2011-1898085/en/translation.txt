EXTRACT OF THE END-OF-COMMISSION REPORT OF THE OCEANOGRAPHIC STATION
ISLAND OF TRINIDAD - PERIOD FROM NOVEMBER 1, 1957 TO JANUARY 16, 1958
OF CC CARLOS ALBERTO FERREIRA BACELLAR
--- seg-9e20c2ad29580fc6b6dfeff6 [machine-unreviewed] ---
Incidents:
--- seg-06e9563e5cdd48b1bffc5ac1 [machine-unreviewed] ---
On December 31st, a radio cipher was sent to EMA, with information to DHN,
stating that an unidentified flying object, in the form of
--- seg-18b8a55dfdc8201cdc0594b4 [machine-unreviewed] ---
well-flattened revolution ellipsoid, stainless steel color, at an altitude of about 1600 meters,
--- seg-7659fd109cdcb0b159d3b131 [needs-review] ---
moving at a vertiginous speed in an approximate northeasterly direction, making no noise. The object was seen by the doctor, 1st Lt. Ignácio Carlos Moreira Murta, 1st CI-SI Sebastião
--- seg-3e387e01bfdd55b732a01711 [needs-review] ---
Soriano de Souza and five other workers from Cia. Moraes Rego, one of whom—the first to
spot the object—stated that he had seen it previously, on December 5, and that he had reported
--- seg-43668e3bcfd8722ece5b60e7 [machine-unreviewed] ---
this to his colleagues, it not having been taken seriously. Both times it was almost eight
in the morning.
--- seg-72c3c9d419a34034fbaa3b04 [needs-review] ---
The following day, January 1st, all attention was focused on the sky when something appeared, seen by about twenty men of the garrison, and which was said to be the same object; I was also alert and it seemed to me, however, to be a gull. The object - or gull - was projected across the sky and thus there was no sense of depth; moving in the northeast direction, at a certain point in its trajectory it shone intensely, though perhaps for less than a second. If it was a gull, its speed would be great, but within the reasonable; if it was...
--- seg-f82e59472b8f4d2ac34f6c30 [machine-unreviewed] ---
really the object, should be at a considerable distance and in this case, its speed was
--- seg-aee1a355aa3c11990457deb0 [needs-review] ---
incredible. This fact was not communicated to EMA due to the doubt that persisted.
Finally, on the 16th, around 1100, I was already on board the NE "Almirante Saldanha",
after the change of command at the post, when I was notified that an unidentified aerial object
had been seen from the ship, over the island. I was in my cabin at that moment, and immediately
went up to the deck, where I found several people a little excited about what had been seen.
--- seg-675053ab12c9ff0f028cbeac [machine-unreviewed] ---
they had seen, among these Mr. Almiro Baraúna, a professional photographer, who had accompanied various
--- seg-210e840ce82a9a0f29e83681 [needs-review] ---
scuba divers heading to Trindade Island to take underwater photographs. Mr. Baraúna was particularly excited having taken several plates of the object and being in great anticipation,
--- seg-aa6564e044b68b6d3023b615 [machine-unreviewed] ---
doubted that he had achieved a positive result. I did not distance myself from that person in order to
verify the authenticity of the negatives. I saw him, still on the loading dock, remove the film roll from
the camera and followed to the darkroom to watch the development, not having entered due to the strong
heat that was making, staying outside during the time needed for the operation, that is, 10 minutes.
Leaving the darkroom, still with the film in the tank, I saw him remove it and witnessed his disappointment
--- seg-4e6c8a636cf0268765c0ccab [machine-unreviewed] ---
when, quite nervous, he supposed he had not succeeded. I took the film, examined it more closely and, in
--- seg-e3f8a9c218e6357604e10182 [machine-unreviewed] ---
three negatives and in different positions, I noticed the presence of a strange spot that later:
--- seg-6425a5bbd94cd600c105f3a8 [machine-unreviewed] ---
it was perfectly identified. The film remained in Mr. Baraúna's possession, who, upon my insistence,
--- seg-230bd39d1135f683925f2d28 [machine-unreviewed] ---
he committed to attending the EMA if necessary. Upon arriving in Rio, I went there and handled the case with CC José Geraldo Brandão, giving a verbal statement. From then on, everything that was done was the exclusive initiative of the EMA.
--- seg-17e2e6f6c7c7ac6752ed0c58 [machine-unreviewed] ---
