Mr. President,
In accordance with the rules of procedure, I request that Your Excellency, after hearing the Desk, request from the Minister of State for Defense information on the identification, location, and concise content of all documentation containing probative, operational, functional, or personal information, generated by military or civilian personnel, under the custody of the Brazilian Army, originating and classified between the years 1996 and 1997, and in subsequent years if correlatable. Specifically, I request information on generation and collection under the orders of the Military Command of the East and in the lower instances of the 4th RM/4th DE, from which the Military Organization Sergeant's School of Arms (ESA) is subordinate.
I also request information that may have derived from orders issued by superior instances, up to the Army Command in Brasília, if they refer to any extraordinary occurrence or operation recorded in that OM in the aforementioned years. That this list of documents, to be sent to this deputy, include all classified information according to the legislation in force at the time, those reclassified and those with extended secrecy deadlines from these cited years until the entry into force of the current Access to Information Law (LAI - Law 12.527/2011), or access reservation, as provided for in the same LAI, subsequent decrees, ordinances, and instructions. I further request that such data be provided, as per the examples in the attached tables (I and II).
