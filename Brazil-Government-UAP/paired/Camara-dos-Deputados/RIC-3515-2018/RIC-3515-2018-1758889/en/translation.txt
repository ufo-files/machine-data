JUSTIFICATION
--- seg-215cb57334e55e5f477fd702 [needs-review] ---
It is the responsibility of this federal deputy to present two Information Requests from the Chamber of Deputies regarding this issue, namely, the access of the Brazilian citizen to classified information related to the registration and study of unidentified flying objects (UFOs) and their crew, a field known as ufology: RICs 44 70/2009 and 679/2011. Both answered in parts by the three Armed Forces, in representation of the Ministry of Defense to a greater extent, and within what the Forces affirm to be the reported truth.
