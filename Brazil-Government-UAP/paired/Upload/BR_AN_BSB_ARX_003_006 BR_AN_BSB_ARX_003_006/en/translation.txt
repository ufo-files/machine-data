Mexico, the largest nation among the indigenous peoples, boasts 27 million Indians within its population of 90 million. One of the few Mayan pyramids built as a mortuary chamber, the Tomb of King Pakal—also known as the Temple—invites adventurers down 77 narrow steps to a crypt containing Pakal's skeleton.
