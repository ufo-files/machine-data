The questions and requests were forwarded to the Military Commands, whose responses are attached to this file. Furthermore, since the first reports of UFOs, the Air Force Command has been concerned with organizing and preserving documents related to the subject. This initiative aimed to create a database that would allow future consultation by society, and consequently, by the press and entities studying the phenomena. Thus, as early as 1978, in an internal document, the Minister of Aeronautics instructed the Air Force General Staff to establish a "UFO Registry" where observed phenomena would be chronologically archived.
