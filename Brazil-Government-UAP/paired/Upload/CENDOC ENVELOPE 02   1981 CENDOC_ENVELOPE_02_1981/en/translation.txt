Young men who went to the lake say they are doomed
Five days after the object fell on Santa Rosa farm, three young men, guided by a local lawyer, decided to drain the reservoir to unravel the mystery — and what they considered a serious threat — worrying the people of Morrinhos.
Wilson Borges (23), Bado Danzela (25) and José Borges da Silva (30), guided by the lawyer, went to the farm and, with the owner's permission, dug a trench through the dam and drained the water. The operation began at 7h and ended only at 12h.
SEARCHING THE POND
For three hours they crawled through mud up to a meter and a half deep, probing the bottom with their feet and bamboo poles for something solid. Only after removing mud with shovels did they notice a crater at the reservoir's center. With a pole approximately 10 meters long, they reached “something large, solid and smooth” inside a hole about eight meters in diameter. Realizing they could not reach the object, they gave up because they did not have suitable equipment to hoist it out. Unable to solve the problem, they closed the breach and the reservoir refilled.
FIRST SYMPTOMS
The lawyer closely followed their work and even entered the mud. Eight days after the operation, Wilson Borges felt ill: “I was working and everything disappeared before me.” While working on a heavy machine, he felt faint. “I was becoming small. I seemed to be shrinking. Smaller and smaller until I disappeared. I felt like the smallest of creatures. Then everything seemed like a dream. People spoke to me and I understood nothing. They took me to bed and I only woke the next day, with a nervousness I had never felt.” Wilson said that when he returned to his workshop, he could not even tolerate noise. “Everything irritated me. When people spoke to me I wanted to kill them. I did not want to listen or speak. I just wanted to remain still in a corner.”
“I was finished after I went into that pond,” said the mechanic, whose eyes were bloodshot. He added: “Look at me. I was always healthy. I never felt anything wrong. Now I look old. My mouth tastes bitter, I cannot sleep anymore, I feel anguish, agitation and pains in my legs, and I keep sweating without any exertion. I have not yet gone to a doctor but I know this is not good. If it were good I would not be suffering so much, would I?”
Beside Wilson are José Borges and Bado Danzela. All feel the same symptoms. They cannot work anymore and even say they seem like mad dogs. “Everything frightens us: noise, passersby, friends greeting us. We do not know what is going to happen, no...”
What is certain, however, is that everyone who touched the pond's water after the object fell has great difficulty coordinating their thoughts and confuses reality with optical illusions. All have delusions and are afraid of them: “Sometimes we stand still and begin talking to people only we can see. Everything seems like a dream. Or a terrible nightmare.”
Wilson Borges, Bado Danzela and José Borges: “we are condemned to death”

Lawyer offers a scientific explanation of the phenomenon
A lawyer in the city for just over two years, he fears ridicule and professional complications. But he speaks about the object that fell into the lake with extensive knowledge of ufology. He too is reportedly suffering from possible radiation from the reservoir, and admits consulting several doctors without any physical or mental illness being diagnosed. He believes the object can be steered and sought that location to avoid a greater impact. “They may be repairing its mechanism remotely. Do not be startled if the craft suddenly takes off.”
The lawyer believes the object might be a “rescue mother ship.” According to him, mother ships already photographed by NASA have the same shape as the object that fell in Morrinhos, but are at least ten times larger. “It might also be a fuel reservoir serving as a filling station in space,” he comments seriously, displaying nervous tics that appeared after contact with the water.
IT CHOSE THE LANDING SITE
The lawyer goes further, adding that “the device — which he prefers to call a UFO — was being guided and possibly was not crewed.” He supports this with the farmer's account that the object maneuvered quickly so as not to strike the rocks. “They sought a place that would not cause much damage to the craft,” he says. He continues: “Right after the object fell or landed in the reservoir, I flew over the area and realized there was no possibility of something falling there without being maneuvered. The pond is tucked away. It is a funnel-shaped place where a good aerial view is difficult.”

It all started when farmer Gabriel Estevão Reis was harvesting bananas. Two months prior, an unidentified flying object (UFO) had plunged into the Santa Rosa Farm's reservoir, similar to a jet plane taking off, and disappeared.

Friends of the farmer, driven by curiosity about the object, decided to take samples from the reservoir's water to analyze for radioactivity. Upon their first contact with the water through two small containers, they experienced an incredible electric shock, as they later reported.

The two men, having lost consciousness, were taken by family members to the Morrinhos General Hospital. After initial treatment, it was determined that they were no longer in danger and they returned, without the water samples, to Brazil.

Meanwhile, the farmer and the doctors at the hospital refused to reveal the identities of the men almost electrocuted. While the farmer was concerned about his animals drinking the water, authorities in Morrinhos believed the object was a type of atomic reactor from a satellite, fearing the high radioactivity of the reservoir's water where it had landed.

To investigate further, they decided to drain the reservoir. A trench was dug in the dam at ground level to allow the water to flow out.
