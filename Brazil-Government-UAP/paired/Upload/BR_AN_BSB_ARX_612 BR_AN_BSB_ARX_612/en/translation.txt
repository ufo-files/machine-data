UFO PURSUES STATE DEPUTY IN RS

    Many cases exist where unidentified flying objects (UFOs) pursue and frighten drivers traveling Brazil's roads, as with athletes returning from a sporting event in Santa Maria, 292 km from the capital Porto Alegre in 1997 and surprised several times during the night by ufo probes. Similarly, another fantastic event occurred with a member of the Legislative Assembly of the state of Rio Grande do Sul, Deputy Paulo Azeredo.

    On a trip to Pinheiro Machado city on December 28, 2000, driving on BR 293 between Pfratini and the aforementioned city, approximately halfway between them, he spotted three lights in the distance that he couldn't identify as a source, but it didn't draw his attention as the only thing different was that they had a yellowish color, not resembling headlight lights. Continuing his journey, he no longer observed those lights, and when making a turn on the road, he could indeed observe a very intense light in the distance with a blue-white color, he compared it to the brightness of a soldering iron, which for a few moments emitted fluorescent lights, it was high up. After this first contact, the deputy became very attentive to what he had seen and at one point he could observe an object below the tree canopy at less than 15 meters height, and then see its bottom with a rounded shape. He imagines that the object was about 1m tall and 0.5m wide. He later observed it in the rearview mirror and the light continued observing him, the object seemed to be above the car at one point, which left the deputy very frightened, to the point of dialing his cell phone to a friend, but with no success, he continued his journey at a speed close to 150 km/h.

    Even with all the commotion, he called the information service, 102, to obtain the phone number of someone who could help him at that moment, the operator certainly got scared with the report or thought it was a joke and hung up. He then called again and recounted what he had seen, but was not helped again. Upon reaching the Federal Highway Police post, he reported the incident to two officers on duty who commented that several farmers in the region had been witnessing a similar phenomenon, after which he continued his journey and soon arrived in Pinheiro Machado city.

    After the experience, Deputy Paulo Azeredo made the following comment: "Moreover, if we managed to reach the moon and at every moment send astronauts to space stations, why wouldn't beings from other planets visit us?"

- Márcio Dillmann de Carvalho

UFO formation occurs in the southern region of RS
--- seg-797185f44a158458e8f9ed6f [needs-review] ---
Numerous sightings were reported in the month of November 2000 in the state of Rio Grande do Sul, which is not uncommon statistically at the end and beginning of the year in our case studies. A UFO wave hit the southern region, and the peak of this wave affected several municipalities. Witnesses from cities like Pelotas, Rio Grande, Pedro Osório, Piratini, Canguçu, and others report their experiences, with the number of sightings on the same day and with the same characteristics being frightening.

Some of the contacts even caused physical changes in people and interference in their homes. The most impressive cases are reported in the cities of Pelotas and Piratini, which are approximately 100 km apart and have around 20,000 inhabitants. Life is quite peaceful in Piratini, and the fact that sightings might be UFOs is enough to cause some commotion. Nevertheless, at an altitude of 320 meters, observations of strange objects become a daily occurrence.

Meteorite?
On November 20, the GPCU received a call around 21:45 from several friends who had seen two fireballs tearing across the sky of Pelotas. Initially, when they spotted them, they didn't imagine they were traveling at a low speed, unlike a meteorite, which caught their attention. As the object passed overhead, they were able to assess what it was and how it was, and they also heard a very loud noise during its passage.

With absolute certainty, they did not make any movements that characterized them as UFOs; they were nothing more than incandescent bolides that didn't seem to be falling and disintegrated as they passed.

The following day, we received another phone call, this time from a journalist in Pelotas who wanted to know what it was all about.

Their curiosity piqued. After a few minutes, they went to bed, and the windows shook as if the objects had passed by again; this is what Rui felt in his nearby home around 9:30 PM. The windows trembled for 20 seconds, and he, already in bed, even considered it an earthquake. "The windows did shake," Rui confirms, getting up to see what it was, but saw nothing, and five minutes later, the neighbor who was with Marciana and her daughter knocked on his door, visibly shaken by what they had seen.

Other witnesses also report the formation of several objects, similar to most of the sightings on the 20th. One of them went a bit beyond just lights; Mr. João Airton F. da Rosa reports that from a distance, he thought they were planes, but as they got closer, he noticed the circular shape of the ships and clearly saw their silver color, which stood out when they emitted "flashes." His sighting lasted long enough for him to realize they weren't planes, nor meteorites, as they made a full circle around the area where he was observing them.

Nereu A. Cardoso, a carpenter, was called by his assistant to see a formation resembling a fan where one object was in front, seemingly guiding the others. He describes them as similar to an automobile headlight, and they emitted a short trail of blue light. He observed for approximately 30 seconds, and they were at a 30° angle relative to him. The formation was over 100 meters long and had a speed much greater than a plane, appearing to accelerate as it moved away.

Same formation observed in Pelotas
In the city of Pelotas, it was no different; numerous testimonies were given, mostly with the same previous characteristics, except in some cases, such as that of Mrs. Heloísa Helena P. La Rosa, who witnessed 9-10 objects in formation, with a clear diamond shape, and the objects did not emit lights. They all describe multiple objects flying in a V-formation.
