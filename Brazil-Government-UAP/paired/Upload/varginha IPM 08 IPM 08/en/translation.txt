We are present in this military police inquiry,
   initiated by the order of the Commander of the Sergeant School of Arms
   (EsSA), with the purpose of investigating the legality of criminal statements contained
   in the book titled "INCIDENT IN VARGINHA" by author VITÓRIO PACACCIM and Mr. MAXS FORIES.

Upon reviewing the publication (fls. 118/215),
   it becomes evident that the authors aimed to inform readers of a chronological sequence of events
   regarding the appearance of an extraterrestrial being, based on the statements of three residents
   of the city of Varginha, widely disseminated by written and television media.

The book presents a sensationalist version of the supposed appearance of a creature on January 20, 1996,
   on a bald spot located in the Andereno Garden district of Varginha/MG, without any scientific basis,
   although the authors refer to themselves as "ufology researchers." They only manage to give readers
   a fanciful idea, not a factual account, of the existence of extraterrestrial creatures.

The story is so implausible that it served as a topic for the "cassettes of the planet" program.

At no point in the publication's content is any evidence of a crime, either military or civil,
   found against the authors of the book titled "INCIDENT IN VARGINHA - CREATURES FROM SPACE IN MINAS GERAIS,"
   the ufologist PACACCIM and Mr. MAXS FORIES.

Therefore, not having found any direct offense to the dignity of the military, and considering that
   the statements involving the Brazilian Army, besides being false, do not have the potential to
   harm the solid reputation of the military institution, we order the closure of these present
   proceedings, in accordance with Article 397 of the Military Penal Code.
