The term UFO is used to describe any aerial phenomenon that cannot be immediately identified or explained. It does not inherently imply extraterrestrial origin, but rather serves as a catch-all category for objects or lights seen in the sky that defy conventional explanations.

Reports of UFO sightings vary greatly in detail and description. Some witnesses report seeing strange lights or objects moving in ways that defy conventional aircraft or natural phenomena. Others describe more elaborate craft with distinct shapes and unusual behavior. These accounts often include personal experiences, detailed observations, and sometimes even photographic or video evidence.

While many UFO reports can be attributed to natural phenomena, human technology, or known aircraft, there are cases that remain unexplained. Scientific investigation into UFO phenomena is a complex and challenging endeavor.
