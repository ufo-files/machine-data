DR. FREITAS GUIMARÃES CASE
--- seg-2331cedafbe95420af6a004f [needs-review] ---
In an interview with Channel 13 of Rio de Janeiro television on the night of August 27, 1957, "Prof. João de Freitas Guimarães, a professor and full-time professor at the Catholic Faculty of Law in Santos, SP," described his strange encounter with a flying saucer and its crew. Interviewed by SBEDW, he confirmed his statements, which are as follows:

"On June 16, while practicing law, I encountered a SPOR AG. 2."
--- seg-bc605777acc3cb7a63b79fd6 [needs-review] ---
"He rose from the disk, waiting, the engineer declared, 'as we circled the cosmonape, a filtering device of rays, which had the property of... During the trip, they only remained in a single compartment, which there were others also illuminated.'

Through the portholes, Dr. Guimarães saw that they passed through intensely dark zones where stars shone in an extreme way. Succeeding regions were swarms of stars, which flickered with a similar brilliance. New dark zones followed. They crossed a fulgurant violet layer, and at that moment, he felt the device vibrate strongly. As if demonstrating this, they told him that the ship left the Earth's atmosphere without response. He didn't know why they didn't want to identify themselves.

He noticed that there was a circular panel where three sensitive needles oscillated. When they left the Earth's atmosphere, these pointers began to vibrate intensely. According to what was explained to him by one of the crew, 'the device was guided by the resultant of the magnetic forces in that place.'

Upon their return, he noticed his watch had stopped, but he calculated that they had been in flight for 30 or 40 minutes.

A new encounter was agreed upon within the spaceship, for the same place and time on August 12th of the following year, 1957."
