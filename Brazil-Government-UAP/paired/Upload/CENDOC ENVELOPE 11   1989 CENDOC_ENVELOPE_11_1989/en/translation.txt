INTRODUCTION - In the early 1970s, strange phenomena began to occur along the coast and inland of the states of Pará and Maranhão in Brazil. News of these events spread across all regions, and the testimonies were unanimous: mysterious beams of light, projected by unidentified flying objects (UFOs), mostly appearing at night, that would attack men and women. According to reports, these UFOs would drain the blood of their victims, giving rise to the expression "Chupa-chupa" (Suck-suck), by which the phenomenon became popularly known. In other regions, particularly in Maranhão, it was known as "aparelho."

Despite a long incubation period, this phenomenon manifested with force and consistency never seen before in the history of Brazilian ufology. Within a few weeks, the news spread throughout Pará and Maranhão, shocking the population and creating an atmosphere of terror and collective panic. Driven by curiosity and with the aim of unraveling the mystery of Chupa-chupa, we embarked on a research project that took us to various municipalities in Pará. However, due to our limited financial resources and time constraints, we were unable to cover the entire states of Pará and Maranhão. We hope that in the near future, someone else will complete this research.

What we present in this article is not a fabrication by rural inhabitants but a faithful account of the facts and testimonies we collected. We listened attentively to each interviewee and selected the most coherent reports, which, when combined with other testimonies and the credibility of the sources, provided us with a clear picture. The goal of this work is to provide a realistic overview and a historical account of one of the most impressive UFO manifestations we have encountered.
