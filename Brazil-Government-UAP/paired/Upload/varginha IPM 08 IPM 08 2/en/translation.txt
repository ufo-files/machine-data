A review of the records reveals that the false information propagated by the named authors was based on media reports and the aforementioned book, which claimed that the Army's Sergeant School, the Military Police, and the Fire Department of Minas Gerais in Varginha had captured extraterrestrial beings on January 20, 1996, and were concealing this information from the public.

The commotion was initiated by them after learning of a reported sighting by three young residents of Varginha of a strange creature during a storm night. From there, the authors began disseminating the names of the following individuals: Lieutenant Colonel Olimpio Wanderley dos Santos, Major Cav Edson Henrique Ramires, Second Lieutenant Marcio Luiz Passos Tibério, Sergeant Valdir Cabral Pedrosa, Corporal Renato Vassalo Fernandes, Private Cirilo Martins, and Private Ricardo Silvério de Melo, as the military personnel involved in the alleged capture.

As clarified in the investigation and this IPM, the facts attributed to the military institutions were clearly false, as no state organization, let alone the Army, was involved in such an incident. It was merely a routine day at work when some Army trucks left the barracks for their regular maintenance duties.

These present proceedings concern a Military Police Inquiry,
         established by order of the Honorable Commander of the School of Non-Commissioned Officers
         (__EsSA__), with the aim of investigating the occurrence of a criminal offense as
         alleged in the book *VARGINHA INCIDENT* by ufologist VITORIO PACACCINI and Mr. MAXS PORTES.

Upon reviewing the published material (pages 118/215), it becomes evident that the authors' intention
         was to present, in a chronological sequence, their speculations about the appearance of an
         extraterrestrial being, based on the statements of three residents of the Brazilian city of Varginha,
         widely reported by the written and televised press, who claimed to have seen the being.

The book offers a sensationalist account of the alleged appearance of a creature on January 20, 1996,
         on a vacant lot in the Andere neighborhood of Varginha, Minas Gerais. Lacking scientific merit,
         although the authors identify themselves as ufology researchers, they merely provide readers with a
         fanciful, if not fictional, portrayal of the facts surrounding the existence of extraterrestrial
         creatures.

The story is so implausible that it became a subject for the "Casseta e Planeta" program.

At no point does the naivety of the subject matter in the aforementioned publication provide evidence of
         any criminal offense, either military or civil, on the part of the authors of the book *VARGINHA
         INCIDENT - CREATURES FROM SPACE IN SOUTHERN MINAS*.

Therefore, it is not proven that there was any direct insult to the dignity of the military, as the
         statements involving the Brazilian Army, in addition to being false, did not undermine the solid
         concept of the military institution.

Given this, in full agreement with the arguments presented by the Military Public Ministry, I order the
         closure of these proceedings, in accordance with Article 397 of the Military Criminal Procedure Code.
