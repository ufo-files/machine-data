The present case concerns a Military Police Inquiry,
         established by order of H.E. Mr. Commander of the School of Arms Sergeants (EsSA), with the aim of investigating the occurrence of criminal illegality in the statements contained in the book titled INCIDENT IN VARGINHA, by UFOlogist VITORIO PACACCINI and Mr. MAXS PORTES. From a careful reading of the publication (pp. 118/215),

it becomes evident that the authors' intent was to present readers with a chronological sequence of speculations regarding the appearance of an extraterrestrial being, all based on the statements of three residents of the Brazilian city of Varginha, widely reported by both the written and televised press, who claimed to have seen the being. The work presents a sensationalist version of the alleged appearance of a creature on January 20, 1996, in an empty lot located in Jardim Andere in the municipality of Varginha, MG. Without scientific backing, although the authors call themselves ufology researchers, they have only managed to give readers a fanciful, to say the least, idea of the facts regarding the existence of extraterrestrial creatures.

The story is so implausible that it served as a theme for the "Casseta e Planeta" (Cassette and Planet) show. At no point, given the naivety of the subject matter of the aforementioned publication, has any indication of a crime been proven, whether in the military or civil sphere, in the conduct of the authors of the work titled INCIDENT IN VARGINHA - CREATURES FROM SPACE IN SOUTHERN MINAS, the ufologist VICTORIO PACACCINI and Mr. MAXS PORTES.

-® No evidence of direct offense to the dignity of the military will be established, as the statements involving the Brazilian Army, in addition to false, did not have the condition to shake the solid concept of the Military Institution.

Given this, fully agreeing with the reasons presented by the Military Public Ministry, I order the ARCHIVEMENT of these proceedings, based on article 397 of the Military Criminal Procedure Code.
