c. Analysis of the documents and statements mentioned above yields the following findings and considerations:
1) There was no unusual movement of vehicles of any type on January 22, 23 and 24, 1996, but there was on January 25 and 26, 1996, when trucks were sent to VARGINHA-MG for servicing at the Mercedes-Benz dealership AUTOMACO, as recorded in commitment note No. 96NE00033, dated January 23, 1996, from EsSA, and invoice No. 003788, dated January 29, 1996, from AUTOMACO (sheets 43 and 44), the Guard Unit's vehicle departure control forms (sheets 53, 54, 55, 56 and 57), and witness statements.
2) The team that took the trucks consisted of civilian employee ORLANDO and Corporals VASSALO and WELBER on the 25th and the morning of the 26th, according to their statements on sheets 10, 23 and 24; on the afternoon of the 26th, it consisted of civilian employee ORLANDO, Corporal DAVID and Private DE MELO, according to documents and statements on sheets 11 and 23.
3) Private CIRILO did not take part in any convoy of trucks that went to VARGINHA-MG, according to his own testimony (sheet 12) and that of civilian employee ORLANDO (sheet 23).
2. CONCLUSIONS
Analysis of the various statements and documents comprising this inquiry leads to the following conclusions:
a. The military personnel mentioned by the press did not participate in any operation to transport cargo of any kind.
b. In this investigating officer's opinion, the media are mistaken and are publicizing untrue accounts.
c. This inquiry should be closed and filed.
