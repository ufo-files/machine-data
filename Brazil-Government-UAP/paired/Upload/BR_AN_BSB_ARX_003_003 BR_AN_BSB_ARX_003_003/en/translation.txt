Introduction
--- seg-207203331fcedef45b0b9629 [needs-review] ---
On April 13, 1991, the newspapers of Brasília reported an incident involving the observation of an unidentified flying object (UFO), occurring in the Papuda region, with a high degree of sensationalism, unfortunately common in most of the press.

In an attempt to clarify the facts, the Advanced Multidisciplinary Studies Center (CEAM/UnB) established the UFO Study Group (GEU). This group collected testimonies and gathered data alongside the responsible agencies, studying the case, which became known as the "Papuda Case."

With several testimonies and important documents, and crediting their sufficiency, the evaluation of the incident begins, aiming for a comprehensive and open assessment.

The specific testimonies from April 11, 1991, were provided by Lieutenant Jorge Luiz Fidelis Damasceno and Soldier Reinaldo Sergio Oliveira. Subsequently, efforts were made to confirm the data through their colleagues. During this time, some resolved to reveal other incidents occurring in different periods, mostly in the Papuda region. Although not thoroughly investigated at the time, these incidents will be mentioned for illustrative purposes.

Considering the credibility of the witnesses (see Incident Evaluation, p. Jtf-*), the integration of the relevant testimonies regarding the April 11, 1991 incident with the documents provided by the Social Communication Center of the Ministry of Aeronautics (CECOMSAER), the Air Defense Command (NUCOMDABRA), and the Aerodrome Meteorological Center (CMA-BR) will be utilized.

A brief report on the incidents occurring in other occasions with military police from the Papuda Prison will then be compiled.

Finally, we propose an evaluation of the incident and a general analysis of the possible explanations for the phenomenon under study, aiming to conclude our research.
