JUSTIFICATION
--- seg-fb5956c3be5be10dc951954f [needs-review] ---
The appearance of these strange objects, known as "flying saucers," has been stirring worldwide interest and curiosity for over ten years. For the first time, however, the phenomenon is observed by a large number of elements of a military force, and its photographs receive official approval, in a note distributed to the press by the Office of the Minister of the Navy. Nevertheless, as this issue concerns national security, it requires further clarification, since there are contradictions in the reported news without the Navy seeking to inform the public opinion. Moreover, with the official declaration by the Office of the Minister of the Navy that a large number of elements of the crew of the Training Ship Admiral Saldanha saw the strange object photographed over Trindade Island, no testimonies from the crew members or any other measures were taken, as confirmed by the Command of the General Staff of the Navy in statements to the press.
