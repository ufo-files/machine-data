1. Report from the pilot of the Defense Alert (Cap. - Av. Leonardo)
--- seg-c7b6a8647498f564ac12271b [needs-review] ---
Around 2:45 PM, while on Alert Pilot duty, I was contacted by the Air Defense through the OPO, and proceeded to roll towards Runway 06. In this position, I received orders to return to the hangar and remain on alert in the aircraft, as contact with a possible unidentified aircraft had been lost by the COPM radars. I remained in this position for approximately 20 minutes, unsure of the reason.

After being authorized to abandon the Mirage's nacelle, I contacted Captain-Av. Navarro (OPO for the day) via phone. He informed me that a bright object was sighted on radial 165 of Anápolis, at around 25 to 35 nautical miles, and that Anápolis APP had radar contact with it. He advised me to be on the lookout, as an alert could be initiated to verify the situation.

I positioned myself closer to the aircraft, attempting to spot the object from that location, but the eucalyptus trees in the desired direction hindered my view.

Exactly at 3:50 PM, the ALERTS were activated. During the taxi to Runway 06, I received information that upon takeoff, I should turn to heading 165° and climb to 10,000 feet. After takeoff, when I reached the desired heading, I sighted a bright object 30° to my right, at that moment giving me the impression that the object was approximately 20 nautical miles from my position.

It is worth noting that this distance is quite subjective, mainly due to the difficulty in assessing it in the situation where the bright point appeared.

In the exact moment I shifted my attention to the aircraft's interior, I lost visual contact with the object, not seeing it again from that point on. I proceeded to the estimated position where it was believed to be, climbing to Fl 150 and then to Fl 200. All searches were in vain. I continued the flight until reaching the fuel limit for landing and then returned.

3. Report from Anápolis Tower Operator (3S Tadeu)
--- seg-ce770f261e4f352a019d807d [needs-review] ---
On the duty of 23/MAR/01, around 15:00 PM, we received phone calls on line 4471, with civilians from Ipameri and Catalão reporting the presence of an Unidentified Flying Object (UFO) in the area.

Around 15:20, the UFO was spotted by the operators at Anápolis Tower, directly above a cloud layer in the southern sector of Anápolis, appearing and glowing similar to a star. The object did not exhibit any visible movements, remaining stationary and alternating between the shades of silver and pink.

Contact was made with Anápolis Control, reporting the incident.

Shortly after the takeoff of the BAAN alert aircraft, JG-LEO, the cloud layer moved, obscuring our view.

Anápolis Control had contact with the object, approximately 25 (twenty-five) miles away, between radial 160° and 180°.

The UFO was observed with the naked eye by several military personnel in the vicinity of DPV.
