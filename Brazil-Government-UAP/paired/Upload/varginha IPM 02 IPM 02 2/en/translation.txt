On the seventeenth day of the month of May, one thousand nine hundred and ninety-six, at the
Army Sergeant School (EsSA), the witness, Cb Kleber dos Reis Domingos,
with ID number 1°041997954-7, son of Jose Domingos Neto and Aurilia Aparecida Borges Domingos,
a resident of this city, was questioned regarding the fact described in Portaria n° 033
of May 1996.

When asked if he knew 1st Sgt Valulio Cabral Pedro, he confirmed that he did. When inquired
if 1st Sgt Pedro was present at the office on twenty-five January, one thousand nine hundred
and ninety-six, and if he had been absent from his post during the days in question, he
responded affirmatively, stating that Sgt Pedro worked normally during those days.

When asked how long he had been in the Army (EB), he answered five years and four months.
When asked about his military qualification (QM), he stated it was QMO7-01:
Computer Operator. When asked if he had been trained in informatics before joining the EB,
he replied no, as he was a mechanic.

He affirmed that his training in informatics enabled his inclusion in successive reenlistments
up to a probable stable position in the EB. He confirmed that his probable professional
stability was due to the opportunities provided by the EB. When asked if he was married and had
children, he answered yes, he was married but had no children. When inquired about his wife's
employment, he replied no.

He confirmed owning a house on the same plot of land and possessing no personal vehicle.
When asked if he had any financial problems, he answered no.

When asked if he knew or had contact with Mr. Ubirajara Rodrigues and Vittorio Pacaccini, he
replied that Mr. Pacaccini had visited his residence with Professor Petri around 10:00 PM on
the night of APR 96. When asked the purpose of the visit, he answered that it was to obtain
information about the possible ET.

When asked for his response, he stated that he could not speak as he was not authorized to give
statements without the command of EsSA, as prescribed by the Army Disciplinary
Regulation.

When asked why these people visited his residence, he replied that he did not know, as there
had been no contact with them. When inquired if he provided the names of the military
personnel of EsSA to these people, he answered no, as Mr. Pacaccini had already
had contact with some military personnel and wanted to obtain more data about the incident
through other sources.

When asked if Mr. Pacaccini mentioned the names of the military personnel he had contacted, he
replied that he could not, due to their ranks and positions. When inquired who could have
given these names to Mr. Pacaccini, he replied that he did not know and had not inquired.

He affirmed that Major Ramires was not his platoon commander, as he only provided "service"
at the PE. When asked if he had ever worked with the 1st Company of Mount and Temporary
Troops with Major Ramires, he answered no.

When asked if he was trained to drive military vehicles, he stated that he was not, but added
that he considered Col. Vanderlei a good commander. When asked if anyone, including Mr.
Pacaccini, had tried to involve him in further discussions about this matter, he answered no.

When asked if he had anything further to declare, he answered yes: that he had informed Mr.
Pacaccini of the seriousness of this incident and the unwanted presence of these people,
suggesting that he leave, as his visit would put his professional situation with the command
of EsSA at risk; that on the following day's first hour, he sought out Capt
Carletto, S/4 of BCSv, to request guidance on how to approach the Major.
