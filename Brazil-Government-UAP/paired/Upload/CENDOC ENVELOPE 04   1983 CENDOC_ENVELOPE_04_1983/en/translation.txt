A strange mystery surrounds the fall of an object that, upon impact, did not catch fire or "toast" the plants in the garden, but instead left some wires with a bluish tint. Mauro, the merchant representative, and his wife were lying in their bedroom when they suddenly heard a loud noise resembling a firework. Upon reaching the window, they witnessed a luminous object moving rapidly towards their house.

At first, they thought it was a bomb or some type of projectile. They confirmed it wasn't either, as the object collided with the roof of their house, leaving a large impact. Fragments were launched several meters away. The object, which had been moving at around 200,000 km/h, landed in their garden while others pieces were thrown over 20 meters down the street.

"When the object fell, we felt like it would explode because of the flames reaching almost 50 centimeters high," Mauro recalled. "It was then that we realized it wasn't some kind of military material, as we later approached it."
