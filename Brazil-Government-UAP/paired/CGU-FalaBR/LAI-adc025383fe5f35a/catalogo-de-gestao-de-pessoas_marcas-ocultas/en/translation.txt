1. WORKFORCE PLANNING

Workforce Planning (WFP) is
the dimension that articulates people management with
the State's strategic planning. Its objective is
to ensure that the Public Administration has, on a
continuous and sustainable basis, servants and
collaborators with the profile, quantity, and competencies
necessary to implement public policies and achieve
institutional goals.

It is based on evidence, demographic scenarios,
capacity analysis, and inclusion and diversity policies,
ensuring a balance between efficiency, equity, and fiscal
sustainability.

(References: OCDE 2010; BID 2024; República.org 2022;
Decree No. 12.102/2024)

Workforce dimensioning defines methodologies for diagnosing and projecting personnel needs (quantitative and qualitative).

It includes workload analysis, retirement scenarios, critical competency mapping, and identification of institutional capacity gaps.

DESCRIPTION OF PROCESS/PRODUCT
The People Dimensioning System (Sisdip) is a technological tool provided by the central agency of the Civil Servants System (Sipec) to support management, register data, store information, and perform dimensioning calculations through deliveries, as well as aggregate qualitative and quantitative indicators regarding the workforce in federal public administration agencies and entities.
The system was developed by the Ministry of Management and Innovation in Public Services (MGI) in partnership with the University of Brasília (UnB), without competing with the projects and teams of the Federal Data Processing Service (Serpro), and it has a robust database that allows for increased speed in mapping the activities performed.

2. CAREERS, POSITIONS,
PROGRESSION AND PROMOTION
Dimension 2 structures the system of careers and positions
in the Federal Executive Branch, establishing principles,
guidelines, and instruments that guide functional development
and the valuation of public servants.
Its objective is to promote consistency between career designs
and state needs, ensuring mobility, equity, meritocracy, and
fiscal sustainability, aiming for professionalization and
administrative efficiency.
It seeks a rational, harmonious career system capable of
promoting integrated professional and organizational development,

3. ALLOCATION, AMBIENTATION
AND WELL-BEING
Dimension 3 comprises the set of policies and practices aimed at the allocation, integration, and maintenance of the well-being of public servants, from their entry to the consolidation of their institutional adaptation.
Its purpose is to ensure that each servant and public official is positioned appropriately according to their profile and the demands of the organization, inserted in a healthy, ethical, and inclusive environment, with access to safe working conditions, psychosocial support, and a positive organizational culture.
The dimension reflects the OECD (2022) view that well-being, psychological security, and the quality of the work environment are factors directly linked to the delivery of public value, and responds to the IDB (2024) diagnosis on the absence of systematic policies on ambientation and occupational health in the Brazilian public service.

A tool for mapping the skills, experiences, and characteristics of public servants, used to support job allocation and career development. DESCRIPTION OF PROCESS/PRODUCT
Professiography, or professional analysis, is considered a type of methodological proposal whose guiding objective is to gather the professional profile of a specific position, that is, to perform a detailed analysis of its characteristics and peculiarities.

Hosted in the Sigepe Opportunities module, the functionality uses Generative Artificial Intelligence for natural language processing (GPT-4 model on cloud infrastructure) to cross-reference the information provided by the new hires (responses to the questionnaire and curriculum information) with the information related to the position for which the new hire was approved.

From the cross-referencing of information, a Report is generated that succinctly contains the professional information, experiences, profile, and interests of the new servant, as well as an allocation suggestion.

The Federal Program for Addressing Harassment and Discrimination (PFPEAD) was established by means of Decree No. 12.122 of July 30, 2024. The program emerged from the need to combat harassment and discrimination incidents in the federal public administration and, consequently, the need to promote safe, healthy, and violence-free work environments. Established by MGI Order No. 6.719/2024, the Program consists of a federal and coordinated plan by the Ministry of Management and Innovation in Public Services (MGI), and sectoral plans developed in each agency and entity that comprises the Federal Public Administration. The MGI had its Sector Plan published in MGI Directive No. 617 of January 28, 2025. The governance of the PFPEAD is carried out by the Program Management Committee, coordinated by MGI with the support of the Program Directorate attached to the Secretariat of Labor Relations (SRT).

The National People Development Policy (PNDP) was established
by Decree No. 9,991 of August 28, 2019, which regulates devices of Law
No. 8,112 of December 11, 1990, regarding leaves and absences for
development actions. The PNDP aims to promote the development of
public servants in the competencies necessary to achieve excellence in
the performance of federal public administration, autonomous and
foundational entities. The goal of the Policy is to establish a culture of
planning development actions among federal institutions, based on
the alignment of the needs of each agency and entity, with the objective
of improving public management, considering national and international
best practices.

6. RETIREMENT, PENSIONS
AND SEPARATION
Dimension 6 – Retirement and Separation
encompasses the set of policies, processes, and
instruments aimed at managing the functional transition
of the public servant, from retirement planning to the
end of the link with the Administration.
This dimension aims to ensure sustainability in
pension systems, institutional continuity, and dignity
in the transition to inactivity, recognizing retirement
as a legitimate stage of the labor journey and an
integral part of human resources governance in the
state.
In the international context, studies indicate that
the absence of planning for separation and succession
creates risks of discontinuity and loss of critical
capacities. The reference model of the Career Cycle
reinforces that the retirement phase should be treated
as part of the public servant's life cycle, and not as a
standalone event.

DESCRIPTION OF PROCESS/PRODUCT

Federal public service retirement covers different modalities and rules established by Constitutional Amendment No. 103/2019 (Pension Reform), which include voluntary retirement, retirement by age and contribution time, and mandatory retirement at 75 years of age.

Retirement management within the SIPEC (Public Servants Pension and Social Security System) is regulated by SGP/SEDGG/ME Portaria No. 10.360/2022, which provides guidelines to SIPEC entities regarding the grant, maintenance, and payment of retirement benefits. Additionally, Instruction No. 2/2024 establishes guidelines, criteria, and general procedures to be followed by SIPEC entities concerning the Special Benefit for public servants who opted for migration to the Complementary Pension Scheme, while Instruction No. 96/2021 defines norms and flows for pension offset between the Own Pension Scheme (RPPS) and the General Pension Scheme (RGPS).
