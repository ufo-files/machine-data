1. PLANNING
OF THE WORKFORCE
--- seg-28d7b00f84643a2e1f89a349 [failed-protected-token-check] ---
The Workforce Planning (WFP) is the dimension that articulates people management to budgetary, public recruitment, and mobility policies. Its objective is to ensure that the public administration has, in a continuous and sustainable manner, servers and collaborators with the necessary profile, quantity, and competencies to execute public policies and achieve institutional goals.

The People Dimensioning System (Sisdip) is a technological tool provided by the central body of the Civil Servant System (Sipec) to support management, register data, store information, and perform dimensioning calculations through deliveries, as well as aggregate qualitative and quantitative indicators regarding the workforce in federal public institutions.

The system was developed by the Ministry of Management and Innovation in Public Services (MGI) in partnership with the University of Brasília (UnB), without competing with the projects and teams of the Federal Data Processing Service (Serpro), and it has a robust database that allows for speedier mapping of activities.

Workforce Dimensioning defines methodologies for diagnosing and projecting personnel needs (quantitative and qualitative). It includes workload analysis, retirement scenarios, critical competence mapping, and institutional capacity gaps identification.

  1. **Law No. 8.878/1994**
     Grants amnesty to federal public servants who were dismissed or terminated for violations of the legal order or political motivations between March 16, 1990, and September 30, 1992, allowing them to return to their previous position or employment.

  2. **Interministerial Decree No. 5.115 (2004)**

  3. **Decree No. 9.261 (2018)**
     Defines the competence and procedure for addressing residual issues of the former Interministerial Special Commission (Decree No. 5.115/2004), specifically for processing dossiers related to amnestied personnel under Law No. 8.878/1994.

  4. **Decree No. 6.077 (2007)**
     Regulates the return to service of personnel and employees released by Law No. 8.878/1994, outlining procedures and conditions for their reintegration, including returning to their original position, and amends Decree No. 5.115/2004 by stating that the process must respect budgetary needs and availability.

The Special Commission of Former Federal Territories (CEEXT) is a temporary commission supervised by the Ministry of Labor Relations, whose main objective is to analyze and rule on requests for inclusion of civil servants from the former territories of Rondônia, Amapá, and Roraima into the public service payroll upon the Union's dissolution.

Its main responsibilities are outlined in the following normative acts:

1. **CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENTS (EC 60/2009, 79/2014, and 98/2017):** These amendments establish the Union's obligation to absorb these civil servants.

2. **LAW No. 13.681/2018:** This legal instrument translates the Amendments and sets the salary, wage, and benefit tables applicable to civilians, military personnel, and employees on the Extinction Payroll.

3. **DECREE No. 12.261/2024 (Decree on Structure):** This administrative act organizes the CEEXT, regulates its functioning, establishes its structure (including chambers for each former territory), and reorganizes leadership positions, ensuring the Commission has the necessary means to implement the law and conclude enrollment processes.

4. **SRT/MGI MINISTRY ORDER No. 5.393 of July 4, 2025:** This order outlines criteria and procedures for the CEEXT to follow in analyzing, processing, and ruling on requests for option and enrollment in the public service payroll upon the Union's dissolution, including procedural aspects such as deadlines, workflows, document submission, among others.

# National Public Servant Development Policy (PNDP)

## Policy Overview

The National Public Servant Development Policy (PNDP) was established by Decree No. 9,991 on August 28, 2019, regulating aspects of Law No. 8,112 of December 11, 1990, regarding licenses and absences for development activities.

## Objectives

- Promote the development of public servants in the necessary competencies for excellence in their roles.
- Foster a culture of planning and development actions among federal institutions, aligned with each entity's needs.
- Improve public management by adopting best national and international practices.

## Key Components

- **Policy Alignment:** The PNDP aims to harmonize development needs across federal entities, with the ultimate goal of enhancing public service excellence.
- **Performance and Development:** It includes a Performance and Development Program (PGD) that integrates work and performance management, allowing for goal setting, result monitoring, and flexible work arrangements (including partial telework or on-site work).
- **Training and Development Manuals:** The policy provides guidelines for developing training and development manuals, ensuring a structured approach to enhancing public servant skills.
- **PDP Review Calendar:** Regular reviews of the PNDP are scheduled, ensuring its continuous improvement and adaptation to evolving needs.
- **Government Schools Portfolio:** The PNDP is closely tied to the portfolio of government schools, offering training and development opportunities for public servants.

## Access to Information and Resources

- **Portal Sipec:** The PNDP documents and resources are accessible through the Sipec Portal, providing an online platform for information sharing and policy implementation.
- **Step-by-Step Guide:** A step-by-step guide is available to assist in accessing feedback and suggesting development actions for the PNDP.
- **Flowchart for PDP Development:** A flowchart outlines the process for developing the PDP, ensuring a structured and organized approach.

## Implementation and Monitoring

- **Elaboration Flow:** The PNDP elaboration process involves various steps, from goal setting to implementation and monitoring, ensuring a comprehensive development strategy.
- **Performance and Development Individual and Collective Agreement:** Established by Decree No. 072/2022, this agreement facilitates goal setting, performance monitoring, and flexible work arrangements.
- **Best Practices:** The PNDP encourages the adoption of best practices from national and international sources to enhance public servant development.

A: The Federal Public Administration uses the Sigepe Frequency system, an integrated solution with SOUVGOV.BR, to modernize and streamline this process.

**Dimension 6 - Retirement and Disconnection**

**6.1 Retirement**

**SYSTEM MANUALS**

This dimension encompasses the set of policies, processes, and instruments aimed at managing the functional transition of a public servant, from retirement planning to the end of the employment relationship. Federal public service retirement includes different modalities and rules established by Constitutional Amendment No. 103/2019 (Reforma da Previdência), which include voluntary retirement, retirement by age and contribution time, and compulsory retirement at 75 years of age.

**Objective**

This dimension aims to ensure sustainability, institutional continuity, and dignity in the transition to inactivity, recognizing retirement as a legitimate stage of the labor cycle and an integral part of public servant governance.

**International Context**

International studies indicate that the absence of planning for disconnection and succession can lead to risks of discontinuity and loss of critical capabilities. The reference model for the Labor Cycle reinforces that the retirement phase should be treated as part of the public servant's life cycle, not as a separate event.

**System Manuals**

- **System Manual - Retirement** provides guidelines, criteria, and procedures for public servants who opt for the Complementary Pension Regime.
- **Instruction Normativa No. 96/2021** defines norms and flows for pension compensation between the Social Security Regime (RPPS) and the General Social Security Regime (RGPS).

**FAQs**

For detailed information on retirement procedures, access the **FAQ on Retirement** and **Manual of Procedures - Retirement**.
