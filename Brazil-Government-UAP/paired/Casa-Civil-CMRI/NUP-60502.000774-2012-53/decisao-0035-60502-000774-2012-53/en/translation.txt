1.3.    CGU DECISION
The appeal was  **ACCEPTED**  on the merits **DENIED** by the Most Excellent Minister of the
General Control of the Union.
In summary, the denial was based on the following: The documents related to OVNI (unidentified flying object) were forwarded to the National Archive in accordance with the Air Force Command; and the Law on Access to Information established new parameters for the management of information and documents that public bodies and entities are still adapting to.
The CGU (General Control of the Union) made a reservation regarding the need for a better understanding of the systematic organization and management of information and documents used by the Ministry of Defense (Air Force Command), to be developed at an appropriate time in accordance with Article 68 of Decree No. 7.724/2012.
