         2.  "AZERBAIJAN," THE OFFICIAL DAILY, JAN 19 PUBLISHED A
         STATEMENT BY SABIR RUSTAMXANLI, MINISTER OF PRESS AND
         INFORMATION.   THE STATEMENT REMINDED "HEADS OF LOCAL
         EXECUTIVE AUTHORITIES AND ESTABLISHERS OF TV AND RADIO
         STATIONS" THAT EXISTING LAW PERMITS STATIONS TO GO ON THE
         AIR ONLY AFTER THEY REGISTER WITH THE PRESS MINISTRY AND
         OBTAIN THE PERMISSION OF THE MINISTRY OF COMMUNICATIONS;
         RUSTAMXANLI CRITICIZED PROVINCIAL SdATIONS WHICH BROADCAST
         WITHOUT SUCH PERMISSION.   HE ADDED THAT THEIR PROGRAMS
         "ARE ON AN EXTREMELY LOW PROFESSIONAL LEVEL, ... SERVE THE
         INTERESTS OF VARIOUS GROUPS AND ARE OF NO ESTHETIC VALUE
         WHATSOEVER....   SERIOUS DISCONTENT IS CREATED AMONG
         PEOPLE, AND MORE COMPLAINTS ARE BEING SENT (TO THE
         MINISTRY).   A MOVEMENT WHICH ONCE STARTED IN THE NAME OF
         DEMOCRACY AND FREEDOM OF SPEECH IS TAKING A DANGEROUS
         DIRECTION AND BECOMING AN IDEOLOGICAL PROVOCATION WHICH
         SHATTERS OUR SPIRITUAL FOUNDATIONS."

         3.  THE STATEMENT LISTED THE TV AND RADIO COMPANIES WHICH
         HAVE THE PERMISSION TO GO ON THE AIR. IRONICALLY, INCLUDED
         WAS B.M.TI, WHOSE TV BROADCASTS WERE SUSPENDED LAST SPRING
         ON THE PRETEXT THAT IT HAD VIOLATED ITS AGREEMENT WITH THE
         MINISTRY OF TELECOMMUNICATIONS AND WAS INDEBTED TO THE
         MINISTRY.  THE ACTUAL REASON, AS EVERYONE KNEW, WAS THAT
         "GUNAY" NEWSPAPER, OWNED BY B.M.TI'S OWNER, HAD CRITICIZED
         THE SPEAKER OF THE AZERBAIJANI PARLIAMENT.

         4.  ONE OF THE COMPANIES ON THE "LEGAL" LIST, "XACMAS" TV
         IN THE NORTH-EASTERN TOWN OF AZERBAIJAN, WAS OBLIQUELY
         CRITICIZED IN THE MINISTER'S STATEMENT FOR WHAT MUST HAVE
         BEEN LEZGI NATIONALIST PROGRAMS.  XACMAS HAS A
         CONSIDERABLE (IF NOT PREDOMINANT) ETHNIC LEZGI POPULATION,
         AND THE MINISTER'S STATEMENT ACCUSED THE STATION'S
         PROGRAMS OF "NOT HELPING STRENGTHEN THE FRIENDLY INTER­
         ETHNIC RELATIONS, BUT, ON THE CONTRARY, HEATING UP
         EMOTIONS..."

         5.  IN ANOTHER OFFICIAL HAPPENING, THE STATE TV AND RADIO
         COMPANY HELD A BOARD OF DIRECTORS MEETING, AT WHICH
         PRESIDENT BABAK HUSEYNOGLU REPORTED ON THE COMPANY'S 1994
         PERFORMANCE.  ACCORDING TO HUSEYNOGLU, OBVIOUS

         SHORTAGES.

         NNS1097.   SELECTIVE REENLISTMENT BONUS LEVELS INCREASE
         BY LT KELLY WATSON, SUPERS PUBLIC AFFAIRS
               WASHINGTON (NNS) — NEW SELECTIVE REENLISTMENT BONUS (SRB)
         AWARD LEVELS TAKE EFFECT JUNE 1, 1996, UPDATING THE SRB AWARD
         LEVELS CONTAINED IN NAVADMIN 308/95 OF DEC. 18, 1995.
               NO POLICY CHANGES TO THE SRB PROGRAM WERE MADE IN THE
         LATEST MESSAGE (NAVADMIN 111/96).   THE ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES
         CONTAINED IN NAVADMIN 066/91 AND NAVADMIN 308/95 REMAIN IN
         EFFECT.   ALSO, THE SRB REENLISTMENT WINDOW, WHICH WAS EXPANDED
         LAST YEAR, STILL ENABLES SAILORS TO REENLIST ANY TIME WITHIN THE
         FISCAL YEAR OF END OF ACTIVE OBLIGATED SERVICE (EAOS) OR
         PERMANENT CHANGE OF STATION (PCS) ORDERS.
               HOWEVER, BUPERS RECOMMENDS THAT SAILORS REENLIST AS CLOSE
         TO THEIR EAOS AS POSSIBLE, BECAUSE SRB DOLLARS ARE CALCULATED
         BASED ON THE NUMBER OF MONTHS A SAILOR IS REENLISTING BEYOND HIS
         OR HER EAOS.   IN THIS WAY, SAILORS CAN GET THE MOST OUT OF THEIR
         SRB DOLLARS.
               IN THE LATEST MESSAGE, 63 SRB AWARD LEVELS WERE INCREASED.

         WITH GREATER PERSONAL AND PROFESSIONAL RESPONSIBILITY THAN EVER,
         NAVY LEADERSHIP HAS RECOGNIZED THE NEED FOR A PROGRAM TO HELP
         FIRST-TERM SAILORS SET AND ACHIEVE BOTH SHORT-TERM AND LONG-TERM

        ABOARD USS JOHN YOUNG (DD 973) BOARDED A MERCHANT SHIP IN THE

        SUPPORT OF UNITED NATIONS' SANCTIONS AGAINST IRAQ.

         NNS1104.   FIRST ENVIRONMENTAL CLEANUP COMPLETED AT NTC SAN DIEGO
         BY LEE H. SAUNDERS, SAN DIEGO NAVAL BASE PUBLIC AFFAIRS
               SAN DIEGO (NNS)— THE 1999 TURNOVER OF THE NAVAL TRAINING
         CENTER SAN DIEGO SITE TO THE CITY OF SAN DIEGO HAS MOVED ONE
         STEP CLOSER WITH THE COMPLETION OF THE CENTER'S FIRST
         ENVIRONMENTAL CLEANUP OF A CONTAMINATED SITE -- AN UNDERGROUND

              "THIS IS THE FIRST DIRTY SITE GONE CLEAN," SAID THOMAS
         MACCHIARELLA, OF THE NAVAL FACILITIES ENGINEERING COMMAND,
         SOUTHWEST DIVISION.  "NAVAL TRAINING CENTER SAN DIEGO ... HAS
         COMPLETED THE CLEANUP PROCESS TO THE SATISFACTION OF THE
         REGULATORY COMMUNITY AND IS NOW READY FOR REUSE."
              THE CLEANUP SITE CONTAINED A 60 GALLON TANK USED FOR
         STORING FUEL FOR A GENERATOR AT THE MEDICAL CLINIC.   THE TANK
         WAS REMOVED FROM THE SITE IN APRIL, 1994.  ANALYSIS OF SOIL
         SAMPLES DETERMINED PETROLEUM PRODUCTS HAD LEAKED FROM THE TANK
         INTO THE GROUND.
              THE CLEANUP BEGAN IN DECEMBER 1994 WITH THE REPLACEMENT OF
         THE CONTAMINATED SOIL WITH CLEAN SOIL.  "ONE HUNDRED TONS OF
         SOIL WAS EXCAVATED AND TREATED OFF-SITE USING THERMAL
         DESORPTION," MACCHIARELLA SAID.  "THEN [THE SOIL WAS] USED AS
         FILL AT VARIOUS CONSTRUCTION PROJECTS LOCATED IN THE SAN DIEGO
         AREA."
              THE THERMAL DESORPTION PROCESS HEATS SOIL TO A TEMPERATURE
         THAT EVAPORATES THE OIL BY-PRODUCTS AND RECAPTURES THEM.   TWELVE
         SITES ARE CURRENTLY GOING THROUGH THE INVESTIGATION AND CLEANUP

               "THIS CLEANUP WAS VERY COST-EFFICIENT BECAUSE WE
         PIGGY-BACKED ON OTHER UNDERGROUND STORAGE TANK CLEANUP PROJECTS
         TO LOWER THE COST," MACCHIARELLA SAID.   THE CLEANUP WAS
         COMPLETED FOR APPROXIMATELY $60,000.
