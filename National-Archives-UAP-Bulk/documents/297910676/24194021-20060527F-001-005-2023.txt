TRANSLATEDATTACHMENT
                       GudGL.doc
                       If Raised Press Guidance:
                       Operating Location Near Groom Lake
                       (erroneously referred to as "Area 51")


                       Question: Has the President Exempted "Area 51" from the Public
                       Disclosure
                       Requirements of the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act?

                       Answer:

                       - On September 26, the President renewed an existing exemption for
                       the United
                       States Air Force operating location near Groom Lake, Nevada, from
                       any applicable
                       requirement for the disclosure to unauthorized persons of classified
7961 Fl.FIN                                                                  Page 2 of 2


              information
              concerning the location. The exemption does not limit the
              applicability or
              enforcement of any requirement of law other than those that might
              require the
              disclosure of classified information.

              - The exemption relates to two lawsuits under the Resource
              Conservation and
              Recovery Act; beyond that I am not going to comment on a matter
              potentially
              involving classified information and that is the subject of litigation.


              FYI BACKGROUND ONLY: This is the third such Presidential
              exemption. The
              exemption was necessitated by litigation over RCRA compliance at
              the facility.
              While ruling that the information in question was appropriately
              classified, the
              Federal district court ruled that under RCRA classified information
              could only be
              protected from public disclosure pursuant to a Presidential exemption.
              That
              ruling remains under appeal by both the plaintiffs and government,
              which argues
              among other things that a Presidential exemption under RCRA is not
              required in
              order to protection classified information.

              Oral arguments are scheduled for November 5. Among other things,
              the Government
              will appeal the court's decision that a Presidential exemption is
              required to
              protect classified information. Plaintiffs will seek to over-turn the
              court's
              decisions to proceed with exhibits under seal and on attorney's fees in
              addition
              to challenging the lower court's dismissal of both cases. The 9th
              Circuit would
              likely rule six to twelve months later.
