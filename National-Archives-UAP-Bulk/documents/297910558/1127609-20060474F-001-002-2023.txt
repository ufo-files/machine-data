In accordance with the requirements of the Presidential
Records Act, 44 V.S.C. 2201, the regulations promulgated
thereunder,
36 C.F.R. 1270.46 and Executive Order 12667,
this letter is our formal notice to the incumbent President
of our intent to open selected Reagan Presidential records
on November 7, 1994.
In response to a Freedom of Information request (NIS F94-
043)
submitted by Lee M. Graham the records proposed for
opening consist
of approximately 167 pages of material
relating to UFO's and the
"Operation Majestic 12" document.
This material has been reviewed for the six PRA restrictions
that President Reagan imposed and the four remaining FOIA
restrictions.
President Reagan's representative, Cathy
Busch, is also being notified of this proposed opening.
In accordance with the requirements stated in Executive
Order 12667, a final review has been conducted for any
materials that might raise a "substantial question of
Executive privilege" if opened. The final review has been
completed and the Reagan Library staff has not identified
any items within the material proposed for opening that
might raise a
"substantial question of Executive privilege,"
as defined in Executive Order 12667.
Pursuant to section 2(b) of EO 12667, we intend to disclose
the above described Presidential records on November 7, 1994
unless we receive an objection or claim of privilege from
your office.
National Archives and Records Administration
