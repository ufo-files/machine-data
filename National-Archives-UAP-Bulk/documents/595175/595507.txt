PRESS CONFERENCE
On 29 Jul 52 a press conference was held in the Pentagon to answer the
many questions that were being directed to the Air Force by the press.
conference was held by Major General John A. Sanford, Director of Intelligence,
USAF. Others participating were Major General Roger M. Ramey, Director of

menace to the United States.
