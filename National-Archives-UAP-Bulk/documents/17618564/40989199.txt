Enclosed are all of the photographs that I have from the 1947-1949 ballooning in New Mexico.

eral Mills 72.8 foot diameter balloon in front of the North Hangar (a copy of that photograph and
of the copy negatives are enclosed).
Included in this packet are two pictures of the General Mills flights I made from White Sands in

Please return the photographs after you have finished your publications involving them.
