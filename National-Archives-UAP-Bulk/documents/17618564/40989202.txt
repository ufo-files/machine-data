Dear Jim:
It was good getting a chance to meet you via phone on the two occasions we
spoke. As promised, enclosed is a tape of an Art Bell radio program.
Fortunately I happened to save a tape of the show on which Art had Don
Schmitt, who is co-author (along with Kevin Randle, Capt, USAFR) of "The Truth
About the UFO Crash at Roswell."
The show was taped Friday, September 9, just after Col. Weaver released the
USAF report on Roswell / Project Mogul. Don was a guest on the show to
respond to the AF report. As a coincidence, Art also happened to come upon a
very interesting tape recording of a 911 call regarding UFOs which he aired prior
to introducing Don. I left that segment on the tape.
I happened, through a friend, to have met Kevin Randle and Don Schmitt during
their visit to Portland in June. Since that time, Kevin, Don and I have spoken
several times about the Roswell incident. I am an Electrical Engineer (SEE,
MSEE) and a Project Manager for Intel (the computer chip company). So, as the
skeptical engineering type, I tended to poo-poo the Roswell crash stories. But
when I looked under the hood" and had a chance to speak with some of the
witnesses personally, including Walter Haut, Frank Kaufmann, Glenn Dennis,
and Dr. Jesse Marcel, Jr., I started to feel that there could be some "signal in the
noise"
• Thus my interest.
Don Schmitt sent me a copy of his and Kevin's response to the USAF's Roswell
report. Since you said that you were still working on the project, I thought id
send you a copy.
Just for the heck of it, since I knew I'd be sending you this letter, I started to
make comments in Weaver's report based on my own knowledge on the topic.
Intel keeps me busy, so I haven't had a chance to finish my comments.
However, rather than delaying this mailing, I will send those comments to you
separately. But, here are a couple of things to consider:
I've spoken to Walter Haut. He has clearly stated that he was ordered by
Blanchard to put out the famous press release. Weaver's report indicates that
he didn't know how the release came about. Maybe you could speak to Haut
about it for his perspective.
