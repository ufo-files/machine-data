Unidentified Flying Objects Division

Washington, D. C. 20000
Gentlemen:
At approximately 10:40 P. M., Central Standard Time, my daughter
was driving one of our cars one-fourth mile ahead of me, and I was driving
my car, with my wife beside me in the front seat, over the Inter-State crossing
over the bridge ten miles north of Minden, and we both saw a green round ball
object very briefly. My daughter saw it through her rear view mirror and I
through my front window.
We continued driving south toward Minden, Nebraska, and at approximately
10:49 P. M., one and one-half miles north of Minden, we three saw what appeared
to be perhaps a plane with its landing lights on. As it got closer, we could see
it was not a plane. It was three separate objects, very bright, with a white,
hot light. They were eliptical in shape and as they came toward us, all of a
sudden they turned west.
They emitted a red exhaust resembling a comet tail
two and one-half times longer than the object. It was hard to determine the
height. Soon after they emitted the red rocket burst, they separated and
disappeared.
At no time did we feel any heat or experience any difficulty with the
car, or any of the other symptoms which we have read in other reports, but
we do feel that something unusual took place at this position and at this parti-
cular time.
We think it might be connected with the green ball seen ten minutes
earlier just over the Platte River near the Minden Inter-Change of Inter-
State 80 and Highway, Nebraska, 10.
NEBBASKA CEMTENMIAZ
This is merely to report the sighting of these objects.
