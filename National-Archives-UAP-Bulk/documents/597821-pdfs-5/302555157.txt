Conversation with Lt. Wm. B. Stoecker (29th Air Division, Duluth,
Minn.) concerning alleged UFO Sighting 23 August 66 at 0455Z.

Lt. Stoecker stated that they get limited information from the
local airport. They do not have records of aircraft flying over
the area, They only have records of aircraft that take off from
the airport. Aircraft that fly over Duluth are not on records at

This explains the reason why on the initial TWIX it was reported
that there were no aircraft in the area. Lt. Stoecker said that
he thought several of his reports were aircraft, but the airport
had no record of any taking off from the Duluth Airport at the
time of the particular sightings.
