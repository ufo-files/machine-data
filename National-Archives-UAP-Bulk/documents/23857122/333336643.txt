At eleven hundred on the 26th of July 1953, we were having a picnic
at a camp site on Deer Creek in the Spring Mountains.
My brother-in-law,
Chester Anderson was looking at the clouds when he spotted a 1ight speck
which he asked me to look at through my binoculars which are fairly powerful,
being 10 power.
It was round at times appearing to get flater at others.
It seemed to be revolving such as a disk might.
The object seemed to have
a darker surface on the top, such as a hat with a shallow crown.
It was
bright as new metal and at times I noticed a vapor, mist or some such sub-
etance on one side of the object.
The object stayed in one spot for about
an hour; the last time I looked I didn't see it.
I had two pairs of binoculars with me and observed the object thru both
pair, one being 10 power and the other 7 power.
After looking for a while
at the object, I went up to another camp, about 100 yards away and showed it
to a friend of mine, George Weingarten.
The obiect seemed to be around
75,000 feet altitude, and located west of Deer Creek Spring.
