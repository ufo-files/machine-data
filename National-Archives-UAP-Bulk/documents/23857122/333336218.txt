This report contains information of the sighting of a luminous object from
Sampson Air Force Base, New York at 1820 hours, 26 January 1953.
A/2C Salzberg, Sampson AFB, reported in letter form the visual observation
of a luminous object about 180 feet long and 12 feet wide.
The object traveled
through the sky from east to west at an altitude estimated to be between 7,000
and 10,000 feet. Both the front and the rear of the object seemed to have a hazy
appearance, but the lengthy body was clearly outlined.
The object seemed to
rapidly gain altitude then fade away. A high-pitched hum, similar to that of an
electrical motor, was heard coming from the direction of the object.
After, the
object disappeared from sight the hum was heard for a few seconds then the sound
abruptly ceased.

29 January 53
At 1920 hrs., 26 January 53, I observed a luminous object
about one hundred eighty feet long and twelve feet wide
traveling through the sky from east to west at what appeared
to be between seven and ten thousand feet. Both the front
and rear of the object seemed to have a hazy appearance, but
the lengthy body was clearly outlined. It also gave off the
sound of a high-pitched humm, similar to that of an electric
motor. After a minute, the object seemed to gain altitude
very rapidly and it slowly faded away. I could still hear
the sound for a few seconds after it was completely gone,
then the sound stopped abruptly. One witness, a man from my
barracks, heard the sound after the light was gone. This all
took place in front of Barracks C31.
HARVEY N. SALZBERG
A/2C
