Report of sighting an unidentified object submitted by Mr. Paul Cooper of
9615 Evergreen Street, Silver Springs, Maryland.
2.
Mr. Cooper reported seeing a spheroid shaped silver object, appearing
approximately 6 to 8 feet in diameter, looking something like the pictures of
"flying saucers, " he had seen in the newspaper.
3. Mr. Cooper first saw the object when coming out of his home. The object
appeared to
be gliding at about 300 feet over a house across the street, and was
gliding parallel to Colesville Road, a southwest direction, and seemed to land in
an adjacent golf course, The Indian Spring Country Club.
The object did not give
off any light, although it was very bright. He saw the object for approximately
The manner of observation was ground to air, and was visual. No aids
were used.
5. The time and date of sighting was approximately 1930 hours on 30 September
1953.
Light conditions were those of a very clear night.
6. The location of the observer was 39° north by 77° west.
7. Identifying information of the observer, Mr. Cooper, is as follows:
Mr. Paul Cooper, age 36, address--9615 Evergreen Street, Silver Springs,
Maryland, the estimate of reliability is unknown, his occupation is that
of owner of the Capital Equipment Company, business phone NO 7-4386.
8. Weather conditions at the time of the sighting were very good, ceiling
was high, visibility was clear, no cloudcover and thunderstorms in the area.
Aircraft is rather prevalent in that area.
Mr. Cooper saw a plane fly
over 20 minutes after seeing the unidentified object, and stated that this object
was definitely not a landing light.
10. It is felt by Albert N. Hutchinson, Jr., 2d It, USAF, Director of
Intelligence, Headquarters Command, USAF, the preparing officer, that Mr. Cooper
did actually
see the above described object, and that it was unlikely that it was
a light from a plane.
Investigation has found that a meteor was seen by persons in
that general vicinity at approximately the time of sighting. This is the only
plausible explanation that can be given from this source.
