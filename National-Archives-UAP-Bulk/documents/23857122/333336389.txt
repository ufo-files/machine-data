Description of Object:
The observers, Central Fire Control gunner and right
waist gunner on a B-50 type aircraft, noted a silver object in the wake of a B-52 type
aircraft while they were under mock attack by F-86 type aircraft during a routine
training mission.
Their observations differed in that one observer described the ob-
ject as a silver ball with possible flat surfaces evidenced by variations in reflect-
ion characteristios, whereas, the other observer believed it to be more like a disc
which first showed its circular side then its flat side.
The object's apparent dia-
meter was three (3) feet and its distance from the observers was estimated at twenty
(20) miles.
The ball appeared to remain stationary during the entire sighting.
object made no audible sound.
Time: Sighting began at 1100 hours, PST, 2 February 1953, and the object
remained in view for approximately three (3) minutes thereafter.
Manner of Observation: The observation was made visually from the Central
Fire Control gunner's position on top the fuselage and from the right gunner's
blister on the right side of the fuselage of a B-50 type aircraft; the only items of
equipment utilized were cameras in an attempt to obtain material evidence.
