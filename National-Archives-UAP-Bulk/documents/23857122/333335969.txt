Princeton. Examination of this object with the naked eye revealed a star or planet-
like object at approximately 250° with approximately 70° elevation. Through bi-
noculars the object appeared to change shape and brilliance; at times resembling
a first quarter moon. Experimentation with the focus of the binoculars, however,
indicated that movement and change of shape were actually the result of refracted
light in the lens. Returning to Trenton at 23002, It. Watson was able to locate
this object at approximately 2600 and elevation 700 est. This fact leads observer
(Lt. Watson) to the conclusion that the object in question was actually Venus or
some other celestial body.
