1. Description: Object was delta-winged in shape with large round opening in the
center which changed color from orange to red. The nose section was bluish-green;
wings were brilliant white; tail section was fuzzy and changed color from orange
to red to blue. The tail section was not as bright as the rest of the object. The
wing-tips and nose would make an equilateral triangle, and the tail section ex-
tended to the rear of the triangle. No trail or exhaust or system of propulsion
was noted. The speed of the object was noted as follows: the object covered
ninety
(90) degree arc in three minutes, about fifty (50) miles north of the city of
Dallas. No speed in miles per hour could be given. There was no sound
reported.
Maneuvers consisted mostly of straight and level flight. In one instance it hoovered
