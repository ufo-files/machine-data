In accordance with AFL 200-5, dated 29 April 1952, Subject:
Flying Objects Reporting Short Title:
"Unidentified
FLYOBRPI)", the following report of an
unidentified flying object is submitted.
On 13 February 1953, between 1758 and 1820, PST four observers manning a
GOC Post on top of Mount Sutro in San Francisco, California sighted an un-
identified flying object. This object was sighted over the ocean at an alti-
tude estimated at 10,000 feet.
The object was described as round with what
appeared to be two vapor trails following it.
• The speed of this object was
not estimated but it appeared considerably slower than that of an aircraft.
No sound was heard from this object except during the period when it passed
over the
GOC Post.
The object appeared to move up and
down and had a zig zag
course.
Object disappeared over the city of San Francisco.
Inclosed herewith is a copy of the story pertaining to these objects as
printed by the San Francisco Examiner dated 14 February 1953. According to the
Examiner thousands of persons in the Bay area observed the same or similar
objects to the one described in this report during the early morning hours and
early evening of 13 February 1953.

An unidentified flying object was observed by four members of a GOC Post
located on top of lit. Sutro in San Francisco, California during the evening
of 13 February 1953.
1.
Description of object. One, round, black object which appeared to
have two silver streaks following it (possibly vapor trails).
Object appeared
to be gaining and losing considerable altitude and flying a zig zag course.
No means of propulsion were observed. A throbbing sound was heard appar-
ently coming from the object as it passed overhead the GOC Post where the
observers were located.
The unidentified flying object disappeared from view
over the city of San Francisco.
Object was first observed approximately four
miles to the West and over the ocean.
Speed of this unidentified object ap-
peared to be slower than that of a conventional aircraft.
2. Time of sighting was between 1758 and 1820 PSI, 13 February 1953.
3. Manner of observation was visual without aids. No radar contacts
with this object were reported.
4. Location of Observers during sighting was on top of Mount Sutro, San
Francisco, Califorria.
The unidentified flying object was estimated by the
observers as being approximately 10,000 feet altitude.
5.
The observers of this object were:
Ray Rogoway, 782 25th Ave, San
Francisco, Calif; Roy Homer, 441 Hazel Ave, San Bernanito, Calif; Mack Morgan,
115 First Ave, San Francisco, Calif.
Ine weather was clear with a few scattered clouds. Visibility was
reported as unlimited.
7.

14 February 1953, Dr. Otto Struve,
a member of the University of California
