Information was received from OSI District office No. 23, Carswell Air Force
Base on this subject as related to them by Mr. Everard Melvin Kinch,
Consolidated-Vultee Aircraft Corporation, Department 28-3 (Production), who resides
at 4223 Jerry Lane, Haltom City, Fort Worth, Texas.
Mr. Everard M. Kinch was interviewed 17 July 1953 in accordance with AFD 200-5.
Mr. Kinch was cooperative
and conversant during the interview and offered a signed
statement which is attached herein.
Mr. Kinch related the following description:
The object was blue or purple in color, appeared more of a light then a solid object,
he was unable to estimate size or any aerodynamic features. Object apparently was
traveling at a very high rate of speed, and registered no sound.
Mr. Kinch advised
the object maneuvered very sharply once or twice then disappeared in a northwesterly
direction.
Object was sighted approximately 2115 hours 9 July 1953, and was visible to
Mr. Kinch about three or four seconds. The weather at the time was hot and clear,
and according to Mr. Kinch there was no activity or condition, meteorological or
otherwise which might account for the sighting.
It should be noted Mr. Kinch's estimate of location the Lake Worth area would be
in the immediate vicinity of Consolidated-Vultee Aircraft Corporation and Carswell
Air Force Base.
Employment records reveal Everard Kinch employee #6-9-540938, born 15 July 1918,
employed as Engineer Liaison Man-B, High School Education.
The information as developed herein did not warrant further pursuit of the matter
and other witnesses herein mentioned were not interviewed.

Unidentified Phenomena Observed in Sky, N.E. of Fort Worth,

1. Information was received on this subject from Mr. EVERARD MELVIN
KINCH, an employee of Convair, Department 28-3, who resides at 4223 Jerry
2. KINCH advised that at approximately 2115 hours, 9 July 1953, he
had
just finished a church activity at the Ridgecrest Baptist Church and
was locking the front door of the church in preparation to leaving when
he observed a blue, hazy, glowing light northeast from the front of the
church which faces in a westerly direction.
The blue, hazy light had no
definite outline and appeared to be travelling in & southwesterly direc-
tion.
It changed course and travelled in a westerly direction for what
appeared to be a short distance and again changed course to a south-
westerly direction.
The total time this blue, hazy light was visible
was approximately three (3) or four (4) seconds and KINCH was unable to
Identify a shape or form which might be attributed to the light.
further advised that he was unable to estimate the altitude of the light;
however, believed it to be at the same altitude as normal aircraft in
Flight.
KINCH could not state whether or not the blue, hazy light could
be attributed to the flying saucer phenomena. KINCH stated that three
(3) other persons were present at the time he saw the light and whom he
also believed saw it. KINCH Identified these persons as:
Mrs. ESTHER
KINCH, his wife; Mr.
PAUL HARTER, residing at 4209 N.E. 30th Street, Hal-
tom City; and, a Mr. EUGENE DRAKE, who resides in the 4300 block of Jerry
Lane, Haltom City.
3. For your information, KINCH is believed to be somewhat sensitive
about this matter, believing that he might be held up to ridicule at his
place of work should his name be used in this connection. He is Willing
to give any information desired irrespective of any other consideration.
The foregoing is submitted for your information in the event
that you desire to submit information pursuant to Air Force Letter 200-5,

four seconds.

over the Lake Worth Area, and continued to travel and disappear
in a northwestern direction. It was impossible for me to give
an accurate estimation of the altitude.
The object was at an
approximate 60 angle from the point where I was standing, but
appeared to be at
the same altitude of a normal airplane in
flight.
