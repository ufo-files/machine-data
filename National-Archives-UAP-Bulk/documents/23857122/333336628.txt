GENERAL INFORMATION:
it aprox 1205, 9 July 1953, A/2C Robert G. Peeler, AF 1838589
3750th Medical Group, Sheppard Air Force Base,
Texas, was standing
on the 5th Street hospital ramp in the hospital area during his lunch
period, when the noise aroused by two (2), B-25 type aireraft flying
Westward, caused him to look up at the sky.
Upon looking upward, Peeler saw an object appear in the sky at a
very high unknown altitude. The object on its initial appearance appear-
ed to be cireular, and was aprx one and five tenths (1.5
diameter, (as observed from the ground with the naked eye). The thickness
of the object could not be determined. The object began losing altitude
fast rate of speed, but continuing to travel in a Northwest
to Southeast direction. The height of the object when it appeared at
its lowest altitude could not be determined, but appeared very much
higher than the aircraft flying in the immediate vicinity. (NOTE: It
is estimated that the aircraft were flying at an altitude of 2000 ft.)
The maximum diameter of the object as observed by Peeler, was aprx 8
inches. It was also observed at this point that the object appeared to
be spinning horizontally. (NOTE: See enclosed exhibits). The object
when observed at its lowest altitude appeared to be elliptical in
shape, and slightly conical on the top and bottom sides.
The color was silvery-white, with what appeared to be a phosphores-
cent glow. The object was apparently made of metal, although Peeler
states that he had never seen such a glow on any metal. The object
was observed at this altitude for aprx one (1) second, then it sudd-
enly began climbing once again at a very fast unknown rate of speed
and traveling in a Southwest direction dissappeared over neighbor-
ing buildings in the hospital area.
No vapor trails, nor any signs of smoke were emmitted by the object
at any time.Although unable to Judge the speed of the object, Peeler
stated that it traveled at almost the speed of a falling star.

The following is a report of the incident as observed by the
undersigned, in his own words. The report generally agrees with
statements submitted by the two(2) airmen who were with the
undersigned at the time the incident appeared.
"On 9 July 1953, I A/2C Robert G. Peeler, was sitting
on the 5th Street
ramp of the base hospital, 3750th Medical Group, with A/20 Elmer C. Grimes,
and A/2C Richard L. Porter, also of the 3750th Medical Group, this Head-
quarters. At aprx 1205 hours, I heard a niose in the sky behind me.
upward, I noticed two (2), B-25 type aircraft flying in a West-
ward direction. After a few seconds, a strange flying object appeared
in the sky, and I followed it with my eyes during its course of travel.
It appeared to be aceircular disc when first observed, aprx one (1) inch
in diameter. The object appeared to increase its size as it came towards
me from an unknown very high altitude. The circular shape of the object
seemed to change to elliptical the closer it became. The object was travel-
ing in
a Southwest direction, at a very fast unknown rate of speed. Much
faster than I have ever seen on an aircraft in flight. After the three (3)
of us had seen the object it was reported to the Base Intelligence Office.
The object when it first appeared, seemed to be very much higher than
the aircraft that were flying in the immediate area, and it appeared to be
traveling towards us, and at the same time across the sky. The convincing
factor that it was
not an aircraft, was that the object
was spinning
horizontally and yet travelling with a forward motion.
The object at its largest point, appeared to be eight (8) inches in
diameter as observed with the naked eye.
The color of the object was
difficult to define, it was a silvery-white, but emmitting an unusual
glow, that appeared to be bright and dull at the same time. It travelled
from the Northwest to the Southeast, but forming an orbit to the South-
west. It also appeared to have made a verticle "U", before disappearing
over neighboring buildings. As it came closer, the
object seemed to
slow-down considerably, and then suddenly
it again gained altitude and
dissappeared at a very fast rate of speed."
