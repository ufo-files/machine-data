These colors were
white, yellow and red. To me it seemed to have somewhat of a mist or

S-T—A-T-F—M-E—N-T
I observed an unidentified object flying high west by northwest at
280 degrees. The object resembled a star. It flashed the colors, white,
blue-green, and red. This object moved sharply and in a swerving movement
most of the time. It moved steadly from 280 degrees west by northwest to
360 degrees. There were periods when it appeared to have stopped moving.
During this time I observed many sharp maneuvers which distinguished
it clearly from the stationary objects in the sky. When it reached 360
degrees, it was very high and at least three times farther away than when
I first observed it, then it disappeared in a northerly direction. This
object was almost constantly under my observation during this time.
Shortly after the first object, I observed a similiar object in the
sky to the northeast of the Filter Center.
This second object maneuvered in
the same abrupt movements and was similiar in appearance as the first.
The
second object remained approximately in the same position, very high to the
northeast except for many square shaped movements.
Some 30 minutes after sighting the second object, I observed a third
and fourth object coming into view in line with the second and slanting
to the horizon in an eastern direction. After the appearance of the third
and fourth object, these three began to change color rapidly in the order
of red, blue-green, and white. This color change continued for at least
10 minutes. Shortly after this a cloud bank interfered and they appeared to
be getting farther away.
When I left the Filter Center at 0900 Zebra time, I could see only the
second object clearly. The second, third, and fourth object were not under my
observation continuously, but at least one-half of the time I watched them.
