At approximately 1310 hours, 26 August 1953, Ist It Norman R. Lucia, 53d
Strategic Reconnaissance Squadron, Medium, Weather, Bermuda was driving on base
in a southerly direction.
Off to his right over the field at an altitude of
about 8-10,000 feet he noticed the sun reflecting from a silvery object, cylin-
drical in shape, something like a plate about 18 inches in diameter, which seemed
to be hovering.
He could see the edge and part of the underside of it. As he
continued to watch it (driving very slowly) it began to move in a lateral line,
at right angles to him.
As it did so its shape seemed to change to spherical
cecoming elongated. It had no wings,
tail, nor windows and no exhaust. He kept
it in view from three (3) to five (5) seconds and it suddenly disappeared, not
gradually as an airplane does but suddenly and completely in a sort of "fantastic"
manner. He stopped his car and looked around but it had completely disappeared
from view.
The total sighting was five (5) to seven (7) seconds. The day was
clear with just a few puffs of cloud in the vicinity. A light wind was blowing.
Lieutenant Lucia immediately came to my office, and told me what had happened
saying he had seen a flying saucer.
about it in a contained sort
of way. Together we scanned the sky to no avail, it being about five (5) minutes
since his sighting. Queried about preconceived opinions as to flying saucers he
said he had read all about the reports in the states and their evaluation and
although he felt their was some credence to it all because of the volume and type
of reports he would remain skeptical until he actually saw one himself. He thinks
now he has seen one.
Not a science-fiction fan and not one to jump to conclusions lieutenant
Lucia is instead a realist, of moderate speech and manners.
He is twenty-eight
(28) years of age and, with the aid of glasses which he wears always, seems to
have excellent vision. He is a non-rated officer.
However, it is my opinion that because of his quick conclusion as to what
he had seen and his general reaction to his sighting that, in effect, he was so
predisposed about the topic, that when he saw this "object" it struck an immediate
responsive chord.
There had been no weather balloon releases within two (2) hours prior to the
sighting.
There were no reports from any airplanes within the vicinity, nor did
the pesple in the tower nor anyone else on base report anything unusual.
