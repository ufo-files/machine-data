Dear sirs,
I received the enclosed letter in reply to my previous letter in
which I requested information concerning the conclusion you have come
to in regard to the UFO report I had sent you on December 15, 1957.
Unfortunately due to some misunderstanding, December 15 was not the
date of the sighting. The unusual aerial object which I saw over
Baltimore appeared on the night of November 6,1957 at 9:43-9:47 P.M.,
E.S.T.
Would you please search your records for the night of November 6th,
1957 and let me know into which of the six general catagories my report
has been placed. I would very much appreciate any details accounting
for the object's most unusual appearance and whether or not your files
indicate any other sightings in the Baltimore are on the night of Nov-
ember 6, 1957.
I am anxious to know what the unusual aerial object I saw on Nov. 6
was, because in the hundreds of hours I have spent in observing the
night sky as an amateur astronomer I have never seen the likes of it.
In closing, I wish to thank you for your trouble and consideration.
Sincerely yours,
