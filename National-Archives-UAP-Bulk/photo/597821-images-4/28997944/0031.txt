Blue Globe Over Baltimore
At 9 p.m. on 2 July 1962, Mrs. Blanch
Rattagin telephoned WFBM, Baltimore
to report watching a red flickering flame
which seemed appended to a blue-color-
ed balloon-shaped object. The blue ob-
ject was moving in a northeastern direc-
tion against the wind, and below the
clouds.
WFBM received several calls
about it, as did newspapers and Friend-
ship airport, in the area. The object went
into a cloud as an aircraft approached
from the east and was not seen again.
