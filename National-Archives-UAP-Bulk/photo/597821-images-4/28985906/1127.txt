Feb 15. 1960
Army High Cormand:
Dear Sir:
I'm writing this letter in hopes that someone with an ounce of
intelligence will listen to what I have to say. Today reb 15. 1960
at 4 o'clock in the Fast foot hills of San Jose, two of your jets
passed over head, and like the months that have passed, when your jets
have passed over head something lying up there breaks out with great
rorce and follows the jets.
I know the sound of a jet, but this is a different kind of sound.
It is as though it lies in wait between the first and loth of the month.
Its motor stops and then goes again as though it can stay in one place
It followed the two jets tonite at 4 o'clock Feb 15, 1960.
Enclose is an article in the paper about an sonic boom. I vas
in the yard and close over head I coulå hear this strange noise, & l
felt such a strange feeling like I was under some kind of great heat.
that nite my body was breaking out with strange wealts & my body felt
as thou I was coming out of my skin. I went to the hospital about
4 AM. the doctors couldn't describe it so like all doctors they called
it hives. I never had hives in my life.
In 1955 at 3 AM in the morning hanging over in the foot hills we
could see this object bright moving back & forth in the hills, swinging
like pendelums on an clock, I called my husband; then he called our
teenage son to see it.
I asked your military to come see it. but they have the same thing
to say it could be a jet.
Tonite, I have heard over the news about a jet going down in Mt
View which is not far from San Jose where we live. It went into a
house on Feb 15, 1960.
Lick Observatory is right in the area above us where this strange
object seens to be most of the time.
I ask you to look into these facts.
San Jose,
Calif
