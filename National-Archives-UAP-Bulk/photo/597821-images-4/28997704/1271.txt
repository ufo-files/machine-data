Arcata, California
July 3rd, 1962
#G 2 Section"
Hamilton Air Force Base
California
Dear sirs:
On a recent trip in Modoc County, we were camped near Lake Britton
about 9:30 on evening of Tuesday, June 12th when we saw an odd thing
happen.
We would like to know if this is some "natural thing" that
has been recorded before, or if it is something "Secret" that should
not be talked about. (We are members of e rockhound clubi and often
give talks on things we see.)
Shortly before 9:30, we noticed a "shooting star"
..then right at
9:30 another came from same direction.. which we thought sort of odd.
A few minutes later another went over...and about that time we realized
ALI had come from same place in sky...and vanished at same spot after
a trip "half way" across. I got the clock and started timing the
objects. They were quite regular in appearance in north, northwest
area, coming about every ten minutes for several trips, then seeming
to miss one interval. The next was in proper time, then three came
in a five minute space, then two during next ten minute space, then
back to ten minute intervals again. These objects had an odd fluttering
flight. Then about ten minutes to twelve, an object came from the
north...almost right angles to "flight pattern".. crossed the line of
flight of the "sters'..This object was cler red, did not flutter.
It seemed to hover, or stop in place, then seemed to fade to a feint
red glow.. then suddenly passed directly back in direction it came
from, gloved a moment in place and seemed to "go out".
