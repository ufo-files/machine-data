6 June 1961
Dear Bu
I have your letter of June lst in hand.
The Eagle Rock, Wisconsin "saucer landing was investigated
by a highly qualified team from the Aerospace Technical Intelligence
Center. This team included Dr. J. Allen Hynek, internationally
know astrophysicist.
We obtained two pieces of the so called "space cake" and had
two separate laboratories make a specific analysis of these cakes.
These analyses revealed that the cake was made from know carbohydrates
(flour), table salt, table sugar, non-hydroganated fat and milk
products.
I hope this information will be helpful.
Sincerely,
WILLIAM T. COIZMAN, JR.
Major, USAF
Public Information Division
Office of Information
Kennan, Wisconsin
