September 21, 1962
To Whom It May Concern:
Dear Sir:
Pleage forward this to the proper autnority.
You may take me for a nut. But consider this for what
it's worth.
I think I have seen a "flying saucer" or some other
unidentified flying object.
I know it wasn't a satellite
because it changes course and it wasn't a place because of
its speed and 90 degree change in course.
Here is the way I saw it. I live on a farm about 2½
miles Northeast of town (Pairfield, Iowa). I was walking
through the field et 6:45 P.M. (September 199). It was
just dark enough that I could see where I was walking. I
looked up to the Bast and saw this bright light moving from
East-South-Bast to Southwest fairly fast like a fast jet
plane. I stopped to watch and listen, but couldn't hear any
noise. I thought it was close enough to hear.
I looked at -
my watch and it was 6:45 P.N. I stood and watched and when
it got due South of me it turned 90 degrees and went straight
up on avay from me at a great rate of speed until it got so
dim I couldn't see it anymore (it was 6:49 P.M.). The
weether was clear not a cloud in the sky. There were a few
stars out.
I feel sincere about this and as a citizen it is ry duty
to report it.
