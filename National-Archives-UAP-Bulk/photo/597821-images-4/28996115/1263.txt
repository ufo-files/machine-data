MOREHEAD, AL.
JANUARI 30, 1962
PROJECT BLUE BOOK
AIR TACTICAL INTELLIGENCE CENTER
WRIGHT-PATTERSON AIR FORCE BASE
DAITON, OHIO
GENTIEMEN:
ON THE NIGHT OF JANUARY 28 AT 10:00 P.M. I RECEIVED
A TELEPHONE CALL FROM MY CLOSE PRIEND
HE TOLD ME
HE HAD OBSERVED A GROUP OF FROM 8 TO 12 OBJECTS PASS OVERHEAD,
AT APPROKIMATELY 9:55 P.M. AND HAD IMIODIATELY CALDED ME. I
WENT STRIGHT OVER TO HIS HOUSE AND QUESTIONEO HIM AS TO THE
SIGHTING. I HAVE BEEN INTERESTED IN UFOS FOR SOME TIME, SO
AFTER GATHERING THE FACTS WE DECIDED TO SEND YOU THE REPORT.
HERE IS THE STORY TOLD IN HIS ON WORDS:
"AFTER RETURNING HOME PROM CHURCH ON THE NIGHT OF
JANUARY 28 I VAS PLANNING ON TAKING MY TELESCOPE OUTSIDE
TO OBSERVE A FEY CONSTELLATIONS. I PUT OFF GOING OUT UNTIL
ABOUT 9:45 P.M. AT THAT TINE I TOOK MY TELESCOPE OUTSIDE
AND SET IT UP ON THE BACK WALK. SINCE THE WEATHER WAS VERY
COID, I DECIDED TO STAY OUT FOR ONIY A PEN MINUTES. BECAUSE
THE TELESCOPE LACKED A STAR DIAGONAD AND VAS ANKARD TO USE,
I DECIDED INSTEAD TO USE BINOCUIARS. SO I TOOK THE TELESCOPE
AFTER LOCATING THE BINOCULARS RETURNED TO THE
BACK YARD.
QUICKLY AS POSSIBLE I TRAINED THE GLASSES ON THE LIGHT. I SAW
AT ONCE ABOUT 8 TO 12 OBJECTS, EACH HAVING THE SHAPE OF A "V'
TITH A VERTEX ANGLE OF ABOUT 130°. THEY GAVE OFF A BRIGHT BUI
NOT BRILLIANT LIGHT WHICH MADE THE OUTLINE PAIRIY DISTINCT.
THERE VAS NO VISIBLE MEANS OF PROPULSION, AND THEY LEFT NO
TRAIL. THE NEIGHBORHOOD WAS QUIET AND WHEN I LISTENED FOR A
SOUND FROM THE OBJECTS I HEARD NONE. THEY DID NOT FLY IN ANY
PERFECT FORMATION, BUT IT WAS NOTICED THAT THEY WERE WINGTIP
TO WINGTIP, SOLE BEING A DITTLE ARSAD OF THE OTHERS. AS THEY
PROCEEDED VESTWARD ONE OP THE OBJECTS WHICH WAS A LITTLE BEHINT
THE OTHERS AND NEAR THE RIGET HAND SIDS, MOVED OVER TO THE
LEFT OF THE GROUP.
