LITTLE SILVER,
Mar. 17, 1960
Centlemen:
This is for the purpose of reporting the sighting of what, so far
as we were concerned, were unidentified flying objects on the evening
of
March 7, 1960, at 2017 EST in the vicinity of lat 25° 18'N long
77° L3'W.
from Nassau to Chub Point, M
Three of us were on board a 38 ft auxiliary ketch en route
the Berry Is., BWI; the owner,
my wife, and myself. Mr.
and nyself were on deck, my wife was
below at the initial sighting.
The objects appeared midway between Polaris and Cassiopeia and
appeared to be seven in number to me, arranged as followa:
All appeared to be travelling exactly the same course and speed.
The
course was generally southerly, so they were moving rougniy iron our
•ight to left, as we were on about 310° T, but at the same tire they
appeared to be climbing very rapidly. They were what I would guess to be
"rather far away, which could have been anything from 15 to 100 miles.
We heard no sound relative to this at all.
Several seconds after my initial observation, having satisfied myself
that they were not a constellation and that they were moving, I called
Mr Hatch's attention to them. He confirmed my observation, and I then
called to my wife to come up and see them. She did at about 15 sec after
my first sighting. In this interval 6 of the 7 had vanished, one after
the other, in the order G FED CA, Leaving only B, which she saw, and
which then vanished in about another 5 sec.
