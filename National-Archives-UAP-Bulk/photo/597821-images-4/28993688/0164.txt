COEY
July 27, 1961
Approx. 8:15 PM my husband and I observe an unusual object flying
above us.
It appeared to be about the same size as the passenger planes
who go over preparatory to landing at S. F. or Oakland but must have been
much larger and at a proportionately higher altitude as it seemed to be
of an irregular shape but too far up for me to distinguish just what shape.
The most unusual feature was its lack of noise.
It traveled from south
to north above us.
