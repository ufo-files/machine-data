Travel Report - Lt A. G. Flues
Mitchell AFB - 12 Dec 52
It Flues contacted Maj Geyer in Base Operations at Mitchell AFB. Major Geyer
referred it Flues to Capt Dyste also of Base Operations who handled the investiga-
tion of the Flyobrpt of 1 Dec 52. Capt Dyste had completed and forwarded through
channels an AF Form l12, concerning this incident. Through the use of a statement

the object as the Plant Jupiter. Weather conditions and descriptions were striking-
