Dover Air Force Base, Delaware
26 February 1953
Description of Incident
At 2130 EST a dull red colored light was observed low on the western horizon
by several military personnel. The light was slowly moving from west to northwest
alternating color fron yellow, green, red, and back to white, Aftar being observed
for approxinately five minutes, object disappeared below the horizon.
II. Discussion of Incident
Observers were, in the opinion of the preparing officer, reliable. At the
time of sighting, weather was clear, visibility eight miles.
F-94'a in area on
other missions noticed nothing unusual.
Due to the fact that the light was seen
moving from west to northwest low on the horizon and then to disappear over the
horizon and that the planet Venus can be seen in that direction very plainly during
this part of the year, it is quite certain that the light observed was Venue.
ATIC's contract astronomer was consulted and he concluded that the object observed
was definitaly Vera.
