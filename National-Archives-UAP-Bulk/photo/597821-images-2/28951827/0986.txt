5 December 1952
I•
Description of Incident
Mile orbiting over Lackland AFB a T-28 type aircraft sighted an unusual
blue light of about half the intensity of the normal glow endtted by a T-33
position light. The time of sighting was approxmately 20us CST undur clear
weather conditions with the wind at 6,000 feet from 15 degrees at 25 knots.
The object's maneuvers consisted of a counter-clockwise orbit over the air
base, an apparent pass on the I-28 and finally an irregular rapid ascent and
disappearance to the south. The pilot of the observing aircraft attempted an
interception but overshot. The object was not sighted after 2056 CST.
II. Discussion of Incident
Student flying in jet type aircraft was in progress at the time of sighting.
The observing pilot saw these aircraft and could not have confused them with
the unidentified object. A scheduled balloon launch from Lackland AFB was set
for approxinately 2100 CST, very close to the time of sighting. In addition,
the orbiting climb of the object as well as its general southerly heading (which
ties in with the winds aloft) indicato that the unknown was probably a balloon.
Project Blue Book has had many reports in the past of know balloons apparently
intercepting investigating aircraft.
III. Conclusion
Probably a balloon.
