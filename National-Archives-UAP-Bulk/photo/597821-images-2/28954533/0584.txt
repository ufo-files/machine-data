cluding 31 December 1952 have been transferred to IBM cards. Analysia of
data contained on IBM cards was begun on 12 May 1953. By 10 June, after
completion of the first phase of analysis of data, a preliminary report will
be submitted to Project Blue Book.

1953. Three ATIC representatives and four representatives Iron Stork took
part. Avaluation of 400 questionnaires vas completed.

case is a good example of misrepresentation of know phenomena. The observer'
was a voman who sighted an extremely bright object for a very short time arching
downward and reported seeing the same to W-P APB as an unidentified flying
object.

ATIC scientiats sighted the same object and described it as an extremely bright
