STATEMENT
At approximately 2130 on 11 November, 1956, while our crew was on duty
we received a call from GA. They reported having seen visually, Lights
in the N.E. sector. I looked through the binoculars but could see nothing
at that time. & short while later, approximately 2230, the helicoptor went
up to investigate. I kept visual contact with the helicoptor most of the
time he was airborne and at one time saw a red light roughly 1000 feet
above him and heading in the direction of Saddleback range. The light
appeared to be very bright with a rapid erratic flash. I would judge it's
speed at nearly 130-180 knots. Moments later it disappeared. Another
time after the helicoptor had landed, I sighted a similar light bearing
approxinately 330° from the tower heading Easterly. It suddenly dropped
from an estimated5 5000 or 6000 feet to very near the ground and vanished.
I watched these objects for nearly three hours. They appeared to have no
definite course or system in mansuvers.
The most objects I ever sam at one
time was two heading in the same general direction.
