LUMINOUS OBJECTS SEEN OVER ALGERIA -- Oran, Oran Republic, 16 Aug 52
In Ain Sefra, on the night of 12 August 1952, a railroad agent observed
a ball of fire suddenly appearing against a background of clouds, racing
across the sky from E to W and leaving behind a luminous pink trail. App-
arently increaing in volume, the object stopped suddenly, became bright
red, and seemed to explode, but the observer did not hear any noise.
At 2115 hours on 14 August 1952, two persons in the city of Constantine saw
a luminous object flying at high speed toward Guelma. According to the
witness, the apparatus, which had no resemblance to flying saucer, emmited
a very bright light. At the time this happeded, the local weather bureau
had not launched any weather balloons.
The same day, at 1920 hours, many people at the docks of Philipp-
ville saw in the sky an enormous red disk going from N to W, leaving be-
hind a greenish trail.
