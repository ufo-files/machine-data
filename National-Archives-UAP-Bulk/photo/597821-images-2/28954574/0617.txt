ATIC DOCUMENT 126061, Iran
1. Tehran
newspapers reported that on 18 May 53 at 1805 local time (15352) at
Abadan, 30°20'N-48016'E, an unidentified flying object was seen by most of the
population.
2. The object was circular in shape and flew at a very high altitude. Observers
stated that the altitude seemed to be equal to the moon, with the circunference
greater than that of the moon. The object, called a flying saucer by the Tehran
press appeared in the northwest, flew on a northeasterly course and disappeared
after 20 minutes. One paper reported that the object had a comet-like tail,
another paper quoted NIOC personnel as saying that it was not a rocket because
there was no smoke following it.
3. At present no reports have been received of the object from other cities in
Khzistan.
