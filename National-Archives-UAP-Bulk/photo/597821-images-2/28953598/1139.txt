ATIC Document 108383, 25 Feb 53, Flying Saucer
Hundreds of curious Borneans today cranned their racks to watch a bright star-lika
object which appeared to be rotating in the sky over the oil town of Seria,
emitting twin jets of vapor. "Hying Saucer", said the spectators for want
of a better explanation.
They estimated its height at 30,000' and said it was
visible for 40 minutes.
Similar phenomena have been reported over Borneo
several times recently.
