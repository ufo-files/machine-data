27 August 1956
Fairhaven, Massachusetts
Case 202, Fairhaven, Mass, Aug. 27, 1956-Miss
Norma Mello tells of an observation made by her aunt
and uncle. She writes: "At about 3:25 a.m., Mrs. F. W.
Bence was startled by a brilliant light coming through
the east window. The light had not been there 25 min-
utes earlier when Mr. Bence had awakened. The object,
which was motionless, appeared to be about the size of a
grapefruit and was quite low on the horizon. Mr. Bence
went outdoors to watch the object and saw a small red-
dish light to the right of it There was no sound. Mr.
and Mrs. Bence continued watching it until it faded
away about one-half hour later.
Twenty-five minutes
later, Mrs. Bence looked again and the object was back,
although not as bright, At this time it began to move
horizontally back and forth in the same area. About
5 a.m., it faded away as if into a mist."
