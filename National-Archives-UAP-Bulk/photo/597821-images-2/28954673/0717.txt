Ramore, Ontario, Canada
26 May 1953
STATEMENT
At Ohl5 ve five airmen, Dean Ichonald, Vittorio Settini, Paul
Ryen, Thomas De Stefano, and Thomas Sfinas were just making the turn
off the main highway towards Ramore at the White Rose Gas Station when
Vic Settimi mentioned he noticed two lights up on the hill. I said
there should only be one. So we stopped the car at the corner and
watched the light. Suddenly the light which seemed about the size of
a softball began to move in towards the site. At the time it was to
the left of the site at about 20 to 30 miles.
We watched it move in
towards the site then we started for the base. We reached the main
gate around O420 and at that time the light seemed to hang directly
over the 3-3 set. We let Paul Ryan off at the barracks and then started
up the hill to see if everything was all right. As we started up the
hill the light began to move to the right of the site and as we approached
nearer to the object it appeared to have a slight orange glow at the base
of it. As we drew nearer it must have moved away nearly 50 or 60 miles
to the right and it seemed to be traveling around 1500 niles an hour.
After it traveled that far it started climbing straight up and disappeared
into the clouds and leaving a small indintation where it broke the cloud.
