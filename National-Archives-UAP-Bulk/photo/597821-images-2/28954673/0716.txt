STATEMENT
At approxinately Ohl5, 25 May 1953, 4 Airmen and myself were
returning from a trip to Kirkland Iake.
When turning onto the road
near the White Rose Service Station in Ramore I saw a Orange colored
object about l mile leit of the Radar Tower. I called attention to
it and the other h airmen agreed that it was not a tover light or a star
but some unknowm object. We stopped once in town and the object started
to rise and move toward the Radar Tower. We drove up to the Base Gate
(always keeping our eyes on the object when we were not obstructed by
trees) and stopped again. By this time the object appeared to be right
on the Radar Tower. We informed the A.P. at the gate about it and then
proceeded to the Radar Tower. As we started up toward the hill the
object started to go away from the Radar Tower. When we arrived on the
hill the object disappeared into the clouds.
We checked with the personnel
