a. Double printing technique, utilizing a positive transparency in register over the
negative.
b. Double exposure technique by first photographing the basic scene then pointing
the camera at a black background and somehow randomly moving a small light source across
the black background while utilizing a chopper or a continous motion picture type shutter
in front of the lens to obtain the "pulsating" light effect.

and then placing one or several small light sources at the scene and radomly gyrating the
camera. Several extremely similar pattems exist in frame 1, strongly suggesting that this
