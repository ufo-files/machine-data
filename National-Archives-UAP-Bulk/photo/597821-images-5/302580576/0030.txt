MEMO FOR THE RECORD
14 october 1968
Subj: Alleged UFO Report, 12 or 13 October 1953
The FTD Command Post notified this office at approximately 0930 hours
that an unidentified object had created an explosion, mushroom cloud and
crater in the vicinity of lakeland, Florida. The notification came from
an AP press release #29 of the 13th. This office called MacDill AFB,
Florida, It Kline ext 3732 and requested that he find out any available
information on this. MacDill had not received any reports, however, he
would call Bartow Air Field and call us back. It Kline called the
Aerial Phenomena Office back at 1025. He said there was no problem with
the unidentified object. The explosion had been caused by dynamite and
the AP was way off base. It Kline said he had contacted the Bartow Folice
Department and the lakeland Ledger and they said the report was erroneous
and the report had originated from a dynamite explosion.
It Marano had also called Orland AFB. They are a Navy Base and do not have
a UFO investigating officer. Suggested that he call McCoy AFB. It Marano
called McCoy AFB, Florida, Major Murphy.
They did not have any reports.
