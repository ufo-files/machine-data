The ground area shown above has a number of people wondering! Willard Hill
Veblen township farmer at right, called County Agent Dick Durland, left, last week
to check an unusual situation in his wheat field. After Durland arrived he was
shown a number of areas in the wheat field like the foreground in the picture. The
round areas, varying from six to seven feet in diameter, have the wheat all twisted
in a clockwise direction. Yat the wheat stem has not been cut and the roots are still
in the ground. The ground in these circular areas has been packed so the soil is
much
harder then the ground elsewhere in the field. However, the center of the
circular arca is not as hard as the balance.
Hill said he has discovered about 27 similar areas in his field which is located
less than a quarter mile from the farm buildings. The large number was discovered
by Hill's father, Danny Hill, when he cut the field last week Wednesday. Since then
a number of people have come out to look at the circular areas but no one has been
able to give a plausable explanation of what caused them, Hill said.
Hill recalled that about a month ago Chip, his white terrier, made quite a
commotion one night. The development of the grain in the circular areas indicates
that's about how long ago they were made.
Speculation has run wild since they were discovered and thoughts have ranged
from whirlwinds to whirlpools to interplanetary visitors. An effort is being made to
contact someone who might be able to explain what caused the mystery. -Journal
