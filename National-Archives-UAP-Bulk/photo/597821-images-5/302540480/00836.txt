APRO Bulletin, Sep-Oct 65
17 Jul 65
Colonia, Uruguay
UAO On Argentine Border
River
At 10 a.m. on the 17th of July, within
hours of the spotting of the object over
Buenos Aires, a group of six people who
were walking on a beach near Colonia,
Uruguay, saw a cigar-shaped object over
the Rio Plata. The Rio Plata is a 28-mile
wide river that separates Argentina and
uruguay.
One of the six observers was Jose Al-
varez Larrauma, 17, of Colonia. He and
his companions reported the object to
police and said it was cigar-shaped, red-
dish in color and about 10 meters (32
feet) above the river and not far from
the beach. Larrauma said it moved up-
ward when he ran toward it and left a
trail of white vapor similar to a jet
trail. The other five people, two of whom
were adults, confirmed Larrauma's re-
port and deseription of the object.
