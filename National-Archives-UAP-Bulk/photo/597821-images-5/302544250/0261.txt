The Commanding General
N.O.RA.D.
February 1, 1966
In re:
Missile over Kentucky
- 2 -
What has been written here about a flying missile need not be discounted
in any way, and can be taken 100% for granted. I am highly trained in
outdoor observation, having spent in the aggregate half a dozen years of
my life ranging coastal and intercoastal waters of this country aboard my
yachts.
If a person spent six years of his life in a rowboat, he would have
to look sharp for his safety.
You can appreciate the need for careful
observation in long range cruising.
If Cape Kennedy cannot account for the missile, then I suggest most earnest
consideration of origin from Cuba.
Sincerely,
PS: A check with a compass shows that I was looking almost due northeast,
actually on a 40° sight, when the missile passed by from my right to
my left.
