MEMO FOR THE RECORD
3 September 1965
Subj:
UFO Sighting, 3 Sep, 1115 (local), Columbus?
Sgt Morey, 40th Command Post, Lockborne AFB, Ohio, ext 191 called
and spoke with SSgt Jones, TDEW. Said a man called them and said
he saw an object at 1115(1), 3 Sep 65.
Man would not give name
or address. Object observed for two minutes, silvery disc shape,
traveling from East to westerly at a 50 degree angle moving very
fast and smooth with no sound.
Sgt Jones informed Sgt Morey that ISgt Moody would call hin(Morey)
if he wanted more information.
