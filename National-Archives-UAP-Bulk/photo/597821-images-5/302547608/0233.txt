STATEMENT OF U.F.O. SIGHTING BY JOHN R. WEITLICH RM3 359-266 USCO:
WHILE STANDING ON THE BRIDGE ON QUARTERMASTER WATCH AT B125S ON THE
DORNING OF 3/2%/66, 1 HAD BEEN STARING UNTHINKINGLY OUT THROUGH THE PORT-
HOLES WHEN I NOTICED THAT THE LIGHTS IN TWO HOUSES ABOUT 10G YARDS APART
FROM EACH OTHER AND APPROX ONE MILE DIRECT FROM OUR SHIP, BLINKED ON AND
OFF VERY RAPIDLY AND AT AN INTENSITY EXTREMELY BRIGHTER THAN NORMAL, AS
IF THESE TWO HOUSES IN PARTICULAR WERE COMPLETELY FLOODED FROM WITHIN BY
FLUORESCENT LIGHTS. INMEDIATELY FOLLOWING THIS, ALL POWER WAS CUT IN THE
AREA, WHICH I NOTICED AFTER A TREMEDDOUS BLUE FLASH OF LIGHT HAD OCCURED
APPROX CENTER OF THESE TWO HOUSES. TO GET A CLEARER LOOK AT THE SITUATION,
I THEN WENT OUT TO THE PORT SIDE WING OF THE BRIDGE AND NOTICED A DISTINCT
WHITISH CLOW ABOUT 45 DEG TO THE RICHT OF WHERE I HAD NOTICED THE FLUE
FLASH. THIS OBJECT APPEARED TO BE ABOUT FROM 30Ø TO 5Ø0 FEET ABOVE CROUND,
AND EMITTED AN "EERIE' GLOW OF WHITE LIGHT THAT APPEARED TO PULSATE IN
ITS INTENSITY. I MUST SAY NOW I TRIED TO KID MYSELF INTO BELEIVING THIS WAS
AN OBJECT OF SOME EARTHLY FORM BUT RULED THIS OUT AND INFORMED MY SUPERVISOR
WHO WAS SITTING IN THE RADIO SHACK AT THIS TIME, THAT I SAW A U.F.O. AND
I THEN POINTED IT OUT TO HIM. I WAS ORDERED TO IRMEDIATELY GET A CAMERA
BUT UPON MY RETURN THE U.F.O. HAD DISAPPEARED, THUS MY STORY ENDS HERE.
JOHN R. WEITLICH RM3
