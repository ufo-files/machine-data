Major Hector Quintinilla, USAF
UTO Project
Wright-Patterson AFB, Ohio
Desr Sir:
This is to report the sighting of an unusual aerial object on
Road. The boy not observing through the telescope suddenly exclaimed
to the other to the effect that something unusual was moving in the
sky. Both boys then observed the object until it disappeared from
When the boys related this to me I separated them and asked each
one to draw the object he had seen.
While they were making the draw-
ings I called Hunter Army Air Field and reported the sighting to the
officer with whom I was connected when I asked for the UFO officer.
The boys finished the drawings within ten minutes after seeing the
object.
According to the boys' accounts, the object was moving almost
due north along a south-north line about 80 degrees above the western
horizon. It was moving "much faster" than a jet aircraft that had
passed overhead shortly before. It left no trail and no sound was
heard.
According to both boys, the object presented a clearly visible
disc and had extensions from both sides that carried small lights at
their ends. The attitude of the object was constant and the motion
was at a constant rate. It was enveloped in a bright orange glow,
described by one boy as a mist.
On the evening of the sighting there was intermittent broken
cloud cover over the Savannah area but at the time of the sighting
the sky was clear. Sighting was by visual observation only because
the movement was too fast to permit aiming the telescope. Several
helicopters from the training base at Hunter were aloft in the vici-
nity, but both boys maintain that the object could not have been a
helicopter.
Enclosed herewith are the drawings made by the boys. That labeled
"A" was made by a 15-year-old. The other boy is an unusually bright
10-pear-old.
The older boy, incidentally, has a story that might be worth
investigation. About three years ago, he was one of a group of about
ten Boy Scouts camping on Big Wylie Island near Savannah who were badly
frightened by a hugh, orange-glowing object that apparently rose from
the island near their camp.
Sincerely yours,
