Vivian that it was good that we had multiple witnesses. Sgt Moody is up at
, Willow Run checking out the tower operators. Checking both positive and

to the cause of the observations as soon as possible. Hopefully this
information will be this afternoon, 24 March 1966. Dr Hynek has been given
permission to grant a press conference at Selfridge AFB as soon as the conclusions
