Says It Was Marsh Gas
Rotting vegetation produces marsh gas which can be trapped
by ice and winter conditions. This gas may be released in some
quantity when a spring thaw occurs.
"It would seem to me," Hynek said, "that the association of

coincidence. No group of witnesses observed any craft coming
to or going away from the swamps."
