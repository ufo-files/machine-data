20 MARCH AND THE FILLSDALE, MICHIGAN SIGHTING OF 21 MARCH 1966

the crucial sightings which became the focal point of local attention (and
later of nationwide) and had associated with them a veritable barrage of other
reports with no clear cut association with the two principal events under dis-
cussion here. It is possible that many of these were the result of the general

who reported them. However, there were reports of odd sightings, particularly
in the Milan area, a few nights previous to the sightings dicussed. It would
have taken an army of investigators to have enhaustively looked into all of these.
