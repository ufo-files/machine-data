COPY.
2nd. October. 1964.
Dear Sif ank you for your letter &f of the 25th. September. 1964. There is
little to add to the newspaper report which you enclosed with your letter,
except to say that this object was seen at 4.5 am. on the 12th. September. 1964,
and was observed by a number of police officers travelling in a northerly direction
towards Essex. It was quite bright in appearance and was lost from sight after
a relatively few minutes.
Yours Faithfully.
Chief Constable of Kent.
