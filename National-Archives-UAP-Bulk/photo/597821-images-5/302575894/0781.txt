NASA TO LAUNCH EXPERIMENTS

conduct a series of seven sounding rocket experiments from
the Vega Baja launch site in Puerto Rico in mid-March.

regions of the ionosphere up to about 180 miles.

Friday, March 15 -- 3:00, 3:30, and 5:30 a.m.
2:00, 2:30, and 3:00 p.m.
Saturday, March 16 -- 3:00 a.m.
