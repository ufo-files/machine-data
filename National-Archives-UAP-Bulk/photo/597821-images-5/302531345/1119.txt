AF IN: 15687 (28 Dec 63)

PAGE 5 RUWGBH 71A UNCLAS
BASE CONTROL TOWER SAW NOTHING AT THIS TIME.
THE POLICE, STATE POLICE, AND SHERIFF'S OFFICES IN CLOVIS AND
SURROUNDING AREAS WERE CONTACTED AS TO ANY REPORTINGS IN THEIR
AREAS, ALL WERE NEGATIVE.
K. POSITION, TITLE AND COMMENTS OF PREPARING OFFICER:
ALLEN K. COLLINS, 2ND LT., ASSISTANT WING INTELLIGENCE OFFICER,
27TH IFW.
COMMENTS:
ALL THE SIGHTINGS INDICATED THAT THE OBJECT WAS TRAVELING
IN A POLAR ORBIT. THE 2 SEPERATE SIGHTINGS OCCURED AT APPROXIMATELY
90 MINUTES APART MAKING NO SOUND POSSIBLY INDICATING THAT THE OBJECT
WAS AT A GREAT DISTANCE FROM THE OBSERVERS. IT WAS ASSUMED BY SOME
TO BE ABOUT 1 MILE AWAY, THIS COULD HAVE BEEN CAUSED FROM THE FACT
IT OCCURED AT NIGHT WITH NO VISIBLE REFERENCES TO GUAGE THE DISTANCE
CORRECTLY.
THIS OBJECT COULD POSSIBLY HAVE BEEN A SATELLITE RE-ENTERING
THE ATMOSPHERE FROM A POLAR ORBIT; OR A METERORITE, NEITHER CAN
BE PROVEN BECAUSE NO PHYSICAL EVIDENCE HAS BEEN FOUND, AND NO ONE
HAS REPORTED ANYTHING FALLING.
ADVANCE COPY TO DIA, & NIN.
BT
