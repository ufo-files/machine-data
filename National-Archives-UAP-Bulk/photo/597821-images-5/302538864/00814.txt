Sirs,
On Hay 10, 1965 I reported the sighting of a U.F.O. in the

Investigations.
I was later contacted and told that my letter of 10 May 65 would
be forwarded to this office in Washington D.C.
It seems to me that nearly three months is ample time for a reply
to my inquiry. I have heard nothing further and I am getting
damn impatient.
I was told I may have been sighting a new experimental laser type
light in the tail section of an Air force jet. I would imagine that
you have received my original letter some time ago which will
give you a fully explained account of my May 9 sighting, and I
will therefore not go intó more detail here.
I will be expecting to receive a reply promptly. I hope a reasonable
one, within your limits of comment on such affairs.
