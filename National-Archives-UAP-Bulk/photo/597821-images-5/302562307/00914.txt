Reference your recent letter of May 29, 1967 concerning your
unidentified observations.
Due to the delay in reporting your observations of June 1966
and Pebruary 1967, a meaningful scientific investigation is not
possible. Normally, air activity records are destroyed after thirty
days; thus, at this late date we would be unable to check for any
air activity which may have been the cause for your sighting.
The information which you provided on your April 23 sighting
is not sufficient for a detailed investigation. Request you complete
the inclosed FTD Forn 164 in order that a scientific investigation
can be performed.
There has been no tangible evidence to indicate that our
plaret has been visited by extraterrestrial vehicles. Various
articles implying that such evidence exists is without foundation.
It is hoped that the inclosed information will be of interest
to you.
