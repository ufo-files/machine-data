At 7:10 a.m., while driving back from the Cold Socing,
New York train station, I viewed what I consider to be a
UFO. The object (described on my sketch) rose from behind
a stand of trees and hovered for a moment. I distinctly saw
a revolving motion on a bottom portion. It then moved slowly
and smoothly off in a SW direction. The amber lights remained
steady and there was no noise.
I felt terribly nervous and frightened. There was a
distinct absence of animal and bird life in the area. I was
too shaken to investigate the area at the time.
Later, Mrs.
and I investigated the area with
a geiger counter and a compass. We also carried 20X50
binoculars and a camera. We found nothing unusual.
Later, we used the geiger counter on the car and found
that certain areas on the driver's side had a background
count of O.1 while the rest of the car had no count at all.
When we placed straight pins on these areas, they stuck instead
of rolling off. Also, when we placed the compass on these
same areas, the magnetic point of the needle pulled way
off (which could happen with any car) and "stuck" to the
bottom of the compass.
