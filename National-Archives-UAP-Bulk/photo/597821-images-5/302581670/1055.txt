in the top of the Tower. However, as we drove down to the Gate

Tower and that all lights were still extingished on Tower #6.

when they approach an international airport, however, this particular
light could not have been on MAx a convention-type aircraft, unless
it was a helicoptée., because it remained stationary and did not
move in any direction. My wife and I proceeded to the shipping center
and when I returned, I made a point to look for this same light. It
was no longer anywhere in sight. The time of my second observation

station duty officer who in turned notified the Officer in Charge,
