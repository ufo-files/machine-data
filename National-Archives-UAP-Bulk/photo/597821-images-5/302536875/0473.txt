Based on results of a Navy investigation and analysis of the
information supplied to Project Blue Book by the Navy, it has been
determined that the target reported by the Patuxent Naval Air Station
as an unidentified flying object on 29 Dec 1964 was not a valid target.
Shape and illumination of the target, indicates that the target was
created by some other electronic device within the station or was
caused by intermittent abnormality within the circuitry of the radar
set 1taelr.

adjacent radar sector centers on the east coast revealed no unidentified
targets.
