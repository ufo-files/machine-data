1. PURPOSE: This report is in answer to Work Order Number 67-31 which replies to a request
submitted by Major Quintanilla TDEIR, to determine the identity of the object photographed.
2. ANALYSIS: Examination of the submitted Polariod-type print has negated retouching, and
as the object is the only item appearing in the photograph, it could not be determined whether
an attempt at double exposure was made. The photography appears genuine in so far as con-
tent is concerned, however no satisfactory explanation could be made of the unidentified
object. The object appears to be circular in planform, basically flat in cross-section with
a domed "superstructure". Source stated that the photograph was taken at night under cloud
cover; yet the object possesses a small degree of high lighting along the comed "super-
structure". The background of the photograph appears dark with the exception of the top
edge which exhibits a small degree of luminosity. The object is located within this anomaly
and the result is an apparent highlighting of the domed "superstructure". As source did not
indicate a size for the object and with the absence of foreground and/or background detail,
a scaling factor to size the object was precluded. There is no evidence in the photography
that the object is extraterrestrial..
