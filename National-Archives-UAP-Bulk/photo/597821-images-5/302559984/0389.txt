17 January 1967. Without essential data and additional information

amateur astronomer, I am sure you are well acquainted with the

rose at 5:30 P. M. EST. Thus it would have been impossible for
you to have seen the three planets at the same time, a mistake on
your part, I am sure.

sighting and in addition provide the following camera data, a

a.. Type of camera
b. Type of film
