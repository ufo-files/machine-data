SHERIFF SPAUR, RAVENNA, OHIO.
0506 when I got back from my cruiser, I was checking an
abandoned vehiele on 224, very bright light, first attracted. Came
from West in an eastern direction, passed over us to the North
side of the highway. I believe there was a partial moon due Fast

reai oblong. So bright that we couldn't look directly at it. It
