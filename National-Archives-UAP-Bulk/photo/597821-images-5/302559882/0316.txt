Mr. Marshall Cartledge, Space Systems Branch of the Aircraft and
Missile Test Division, Air Proving Ground Center. The laanch vehicle,
a two stage Nike-Iroquois, contained a vaporized barium payload

northernly direction and was visible for 30 minutes.
