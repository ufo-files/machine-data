SUBJECT: Telephone to Vendenburg AFB, California Regarding UFO Observation
of 21 October 1969

to TSg: Mansfield at SAC scheduling (ext 65313). Sgt Mansfield stated

21 October 1969. This is equivalent to 2139 hours MDT or only 6 minutes
from the reported time of sighting.
