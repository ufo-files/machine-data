MEMO FOR RECORD
30 Dec 69
SUBJECT:
Telephone to Vendenburg AFB, California Regarding UPO Observation
of 21 October 1969
On 29 December 1969, It Marano called Vandenburg AFB, California and talked
to TSg: Mansfield at SAC scheduling (ext 65313). Sgt Mansfield stated
that they had a Minuteman launch from Vandenburg at 1939 hours PST on
21 October 1969. This is equivalent to 2139 hours MDT or only 6 minutes
from the reported time of sighting.
