and 5:10 A.M. was Echo II. There are over 1,000 objects in orbit
around the earth at the moment, of which about 40 are visible to
the naked eye.
Not all these satellites have an easterly component
either.
Some are in polar orbits and some are retrograde satellites
or in other words, travel from east to west. However, the majority
of the satellites travel approximately from west to east.
2.
Not all these satellites travel at the same speed either.
For example, Echo I and Echo II are about 700 miles high and make
one revolution around the earth in about 2 hours. Pageos A 1s
about 3600 miles high and makes one revolution about every 3 hours.
The higher the satellite 1s, the longer the time for one rotation.
It is also known that the human eye does not continuously follow
an object as it moves across the sky, but rather Jumps from spot
to spot, focusing at each spot. Because of this effect, satellites
may seem to cross the sky in a sawtooth fashion.
4. Your sighting of 3 Oct 1967 was very interesting.
This 1s the

sky. We have determined that this sighting was of a Barium Cloud
release from wallops island, va.
There were two cloud releases
from this sight; one on the 3rd at about 7:25 P.M. and one on the
4th at about 7:30 P.M. When we receive more complete info on
