in effect, causes the incoming rays of light to be broken down
into the various colors of the spectrum.
In technical terms it
is said the incoming rays are refracted by the atmosphere.
The
closer the stars are to the horizon, the more they scintillate,
simply, as mentioned above, because we are then looking through
a thicker layer of air.

body to appear to erratically move about in the sky.
