Sunburg

Subj:
Uro Observation, 2 July 1968
Called
He was outdoors near
the intersection of Agusta St and Fairmont Avenue when a flash of
light similar to that of a flash bulb attracted his attention.
looked in the direction of the flash and saw a orange-red fiery
object moving above the clouds. The meteor was elongated and was not
quite the size of a full moon but was brighter than a full moon. The
object was in sight from 5 to 10 seconds and was traveling from E to W.
It passed almost directly overhead and disappeared in the cloud cover to
the west. About 4 or 5 minutes after the object disappeared
heard a boom. He said it came from a different direction than he had seen
the meteor disappear.
The meteor disappeared toward the West but
thought that the boom came from the South and did not think it
was connected to the sighting.
