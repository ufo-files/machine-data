Dear
Reference your recent completed FTD Form 164 describing
your observation of an alleged unidentified flying object.
A study of the submitted information did not reveal a positive
identification. The only explanation that I can offer is that
an aircraft was observed; this, of course, being only a possibility.
There is a high speed, low level aircraft training route slightly
west of Cairo, Ohio. Aircraft operating on the route fly toward
the southwest. Since aircraft records are normally destroyed after
30 days, it would be impossible to check the records at this time
for any aircrait acitivity on the days of your sighting. It is
extremely important for one to report his observation as soon as
possible so that a meaningful investigation can be made.
Thank you for reporting your observation to the Air Force.
Do Dafore 8 May 67
