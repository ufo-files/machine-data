WALLOPS LAUNCHES SIX
EXPERIMENTS OVERNIGHT
The National Aeronautics and Space Administration conducted
six experiments between sunset and dawn last night from its
Wallops Island, Va., Station.
Five of the payloads were chemical cloud experiments
launched at 5:39 p.m., 7:32 p.m., 9:00 p.m., 12:12 a.m. and
6:31 a.m. EST. The twilight and dawn firings were sodium vapor
experiments which generated reddish-orange clouds visible for
hundreds of miles along the East Coast. The other three payloads
consisted of trimethylaluminum (IA) vapor trails which formed
blue-green clouds high above the earth. The payloads were flown
on Nike-Apache research rockets and the vapor trails were ejected
at altitude ranges of about 30 to 130 statute miles.
Purpose of these experiments was to measure wind velocities
and directions at various altitudes in the upper atmosphere.
