F. AIRMAN STRONG OF THE 331 FIGHTER SQUADRON, WEBB AFB, TEXAS.
THIS IS ALL OF THE INFORMATION RECEIVED FROM AIRMAN STRONG:
14. A. A BRIGHT GREENISH OBJECT WHICH LIT UP THE COCKPIT FOR TEN

THAT WAS RECEIVED FROM TWO PILOTS, ONE FLYING IN THE SWEETWATER AREA
(NAVY 56542) AND THE OTHER PILOT FLYING IN AN AREA NORTH EAST OF
LUBBOCK (AERO-COMMANDER 68815). THE PILOT INFORMATION WAS
FORWARDED TO SWEETWATER FROM FAA.
OBSERVATION NUMBER TWO:
14. A. DESCRIPTION OF OBJECT: CICULAR ABOUT ONE SIXTH THE SIZE
OF A FULL MOON WHICH GAVE OFF A BRIGHT GREENISH WHITE LIGHT. THE
OBJECT HAD A TRAIL OF 3 TO 4 TIMES THE SIZE OF THE OBJECT. NO
SOUND WAS HEARD.
B. OBSERVER EVALUATED IT AS A COMET. ITS MOVEMENT AND SIZE
BROUGHT HIS ATTENTION TO IT. THE AZIMUTH FIRST NOTICED WAS 176 DEGREES AT
AN ELEVATION OF WP DEGREES AND FADED TO NOTHING AT AN AZIMUTH OF
185 DEGREES AND 18 DEGREE ELEVATION. OBJECT WAS VISIBLE FOR 2 TO 3
SECONDS NO MANEUVERS.
