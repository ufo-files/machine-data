BROWN & BIGELOW
A DIVISION OF STANDARD PACKAGING CORPORATION
PAUL 4
MINNESOTA
Remembrance Advertising
July 18, 1966
Wright Patterson
Airforce Base
Akron, Ohio
Dear Sirs:
At the request of the Office of Special Investigation of the United States
Air Force, stationed at the Minneapolis-St. Paul International Airport,
we are writing this letter.
On Saturday night, July 16, 1966 between 10:30 and 11:00 PM, central day-
light time, there were four of us (two schoolteachers, a housewife and
myself, a businessman) sitting in our backyard when we observed in the
southern section of the sky a light apparently traveling at a terrific speed.
This light traveled in a north to northwest direction.
This light ap eared
to be at an extremely high altitude, but, of course, it would be impossible
to give any estimate as to what height it was.
When it was directly overhead, it appeared to have a green light in front and
a rather yellowish light in the back.
The light disappeared behind the trees
in the northern portion of the sky, but previous to disappearing, it seemed
to swerve slightly, as though it were about to turn. However, I cannot verify
the fact that it actually turned.
As I continued to watch the sky, a few seconds
later another light appeared over the treetops in the northern sky, proceeding
southward, and I can only assume that this was the same object originally
seen proceeding in a northerly direction.
When this light was directly over-
head, it disappeared as though the light had been snapped off by a switch.
It was an absolutely clear night, without a cloud in the sky..
This event in itself, I would have passed off, but the next night at approx-
imately the same time, a similar object was observed in relatively the same
position in the sky, its course being the same as the object of the previous
night. As in the previous night, the object disappeared behind the treetops
reappearing a few seconds later on a "return trip."
This night, however, the
light continued its course into the southern area of the sky and disappeared
into a cloud flurry. The second night this sighting was seen by the original
four people, plus an additional four, all of whom are responsible individuals.
We would estimate that this unidentified object traversed a 30-15 degree
segment of the sky in less than a minute.
The speed of this object, I would compare to that of a falling meteorite.
We hope that this information may have someinterest to you.
Sincerely yours
BASI
