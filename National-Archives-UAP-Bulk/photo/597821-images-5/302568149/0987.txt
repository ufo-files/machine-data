DEPARTMENT OF THE AIR FORCE
HEADQUARTERS 375th AEROMEDICAL AIRLIFT WING (MAC)
SCOTT AIR FORCE BASE, ILLINOIS 62225
PEPLY TQ
ATTN OF:
375(0)
SUBJECT: Report of Unidentified Flying Object
3 1 JUL 1967
TO:
Foreign Technology Division
Wright-Patterson AFB, Ohio
1. Reference is made to correspondence from this headquarters,
same subject, dated 20 July 1967.
2. The Florissant Police Department reports that they apprehended
two teen-age boys who were launching plastic clothes bags with
lighted candles suspended underneath.
It has definitely been deter-
mined that these were the objects sighted by the police personnel.
3. By coincidence a similar sighting occurred on 20 July 1967,
approximately three miles northwest of Scott AFB, just outside the
town of O'Fallon. Again this was attributed to the launching of
plastic clothes bags, confirmed by the boy's mother. Balsa wood
is utilized to form a light framework under the bag, and the heat
from the candles placed thereon causes the bag to rise, and gives
off a glow from the light of the candles. This also accounts for
the falling sparks as the entire device eventually catches on fire.
4. This prank seems to be well known among the teen-age set,
and it is anticipated that similar sightings will occur in the future.
FOR THE COMMANDER
Seen E. Barber
WILLIAM E. BARBER, Colonel, USAF
Deputy Commander for Operations
