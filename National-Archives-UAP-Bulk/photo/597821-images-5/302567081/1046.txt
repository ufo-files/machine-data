June 3, 1967
Project Bluebook
Wright- Patterson Air Force Base
Dayton, Ohio
Dear Sirs:
At approximately 9:45 this evening, my younger sister and I observed
a U.F.O. in the sky while stargazing. I am 15 years old and my sister
is 11. for the past 4 months or so, I have been a serious student of
the U.F.O. mystery but beleive me, I never thought I'a see one myself.
Let me describe to you what I saw and perhaps you may be able to find
an explaination for it.
My sister, an unbeleiver in U.F.O.'s, sighted the object first and
attracted my attention to it. My first impression was that it was some
sort of a plane or satellite, but after watching its strange behavior
I realized that it was something out of the ordinary. It was a fairly
bright, consistant white light, with occasional flashings of red. It
first appeared directly overhead, moved across the sky a distance, blinked
off, blinked on, and continued to do this until it was rather low in
the north-east sky. It then completely vanished, just like a light being
turned off. That was the last we saw of it. And another strange thing,
as I have read in many U.P.O. reports, a low-flying jet came into the
sky about 10 minutes later and went off in the same direction from which
the U.F.O. disappeared. Please beleive me, I have never seen anything
like this before. I would be happy to fill out one of your U.F.O.
Sighting forms, in hopes that you may be able to explain what I saw.
Yours truly,
send 1o4!
