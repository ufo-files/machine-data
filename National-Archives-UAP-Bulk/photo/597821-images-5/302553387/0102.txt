General Summary:

group of lights disappeared behind trees. Some of the lights moved, sone
remained stationary for varying periods of time. The lights appeared blue-
white, were oval in shape, and shaded off to darkness at the edges "like a
flashlight seen in fog."
A total of twelve witnesses gave accounts of the lights; the longest

observed the longest said it was clear, with stars and moon visible. Four
others were uncertain, although their impression had been that the night was
overcast.
