DRAFT/SAF-OIPB/HelenGa1ser/75630/23Aug64
Dear Mr.
This
is in response to your recent letter to the
President concerning information on several recent
satellite observations.
The President has asked that
I reply to your letter.
You indicated a date of July 3, 1963, as the time
of your first sighting.
The Air Force does not maintain
records of satellite passage for more than one year.
However, our records for July 3, 1964 do not indicate
a passage of either Echo I or Echo II for that date
in your area, and if you meant 1964 instead of 1963, it
may be that you observed a satellite of lesser magnitude
or perhaps a high-flying aircraft on that date. since
you did not indicate the length of time of the observation,
we are unable to make a firm determination of the origin
of the light.
Both Echo I and Echo II made several passes over
your area on the succeeding evenings of August 3rd,
August 4th and August 17th, between 7:54 p.m. and
10:45 p.m.
Because of atmospheric conditions, a satellite may
give the appearance of blinking or flashing as it
passes overhead in much the same way that a star may
twinkle.
Your interest in this matter is appreciated.
