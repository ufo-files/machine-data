Q. How are daily sunspot numbers
determined?
By adding the number of visible
spots to 10 times the number of spot
groups.
This total is multiplied
by a
constant, for most observers not far from
unity, to bring the daily number into
average agreement with a standard series,
such as the Zurich sunspot numbers.
Much care and observing experience are
required to obtain consistent results.
