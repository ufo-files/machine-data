"PARKED' OBJECTS
Kent, Ohio . . . April 25, 1964, 8:30-9:00 P.M.
.. Three
objects were seen in a hovering position by Miss Glenda Gless, a
student nurse, and her mother.
The place was their back yard, the
objects were in the west, the one to the left above the trees, the other
2 were seen side by side through the willow tree.
They stood out
prominently among the stars. The highest object suddenly took off
to the S. and disappeared in the distance. Its color was a yellow-
ish-white.
The second object over, rose straight up over the willow
and moved directly E over their heads. It was about 1,000 feet
up, orange in color, and hazy of outline. Its shape was that of an
elliptic leaf.
It, too, went out of sight in the distance. The third
object started
upward
at a slight angle to the right, passed over
the tree going NE. It travelled about 200 yards, blinked a dim
red three times
and
vanished.
They
weren't positive, but they
thought the object had stopped during the blinking stage.
The objects were estimated to be 1500 feet away when first
scen.
All objects were soundless, no trail or other lights.
Duration
- 30 minutes. Speed compared to a light aircraft's (120 MPH).
