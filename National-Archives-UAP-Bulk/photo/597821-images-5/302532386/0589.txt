told his story to a local newspaper
several days later.

he was doing his routine

20 feet long and 15 feet wide.
Wilcox walked up and touched the UFO, and at
that moment, two people about four feet tall appeared from behind or under the

plete with hoods and foot coverings. They spoke good English,
according to
Wilcox, but he could not tell what part of their bodies their voices
from. They told him that they were here to gather soil samples; that they vis-

for his own good

tural problems back on Mars.
As Mr. Wilcox turned away to get a fertilizer sample for his visi-
