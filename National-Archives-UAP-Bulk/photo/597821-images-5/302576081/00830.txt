Directors of Project Bluebook
Wright-Patterson Air Force Base
Dayton, Ohio
Gentlemen:
We, the undersigned, on the night of June 20, 1968 at approx-
imately 10:50 P.M. saw an Unidentified Flying Object of uncertain dimensions.
It was flying at about the same altitude as a commercial jetliner. However
it could not have been a commercial plane due to some peculiar occurences. It
wou'd hover at intervals and then would make short spurts. When first observed,
the object was proceeding east toward Fort Benjamin Harrison(U.S. Army). It hov-
ered for a second and then proceeded to make a ninety degree left turn. It then
flew out of sight. A check at the Weir Cook Weather Station showed that the
visibility at that time was ten miles.
About two minutes after it disappeared, it reappeared coming from the south=
east. It then flew to the northwest. It did this after we observed a commercial
jetliner proceeding in the same general direction as the object. The object then
disappeared, still heading to the northwest.
The object itself was completely silent. It had two flashing lights on it,
one red, one white. There was another bright light that appeared on the top of
the object as it banked into turns. The object appeared to be disk-shaped.
Following the sighting, we immediately notified the Military Police at
Fort Benjamin Harrison. After making a full report to the Police, we were instruct-
ed to contact the Indianapolis Police Department and the Lawrence Police Depart-
ment and repott to them what we had observed.
Also we were advised by the Fort to contact you as soon as possible so you
could begin further investigation.
