ABOUT THE AUTHOR
Constance B. Brewer was born in Brookline, Massachu-

Naval Academy. They have four daughters and six grand-
daughters. Mrs. Brewer is chairman of anti-litter projects
and board member of the Massachusetts Roadside Council,

Beautiful, Inc. in 1964. For the past five years she has served
on the board of the Garden Club Federation of Massa-
chusetts as Litter Prevention, Junior Gardeners and Roadside
Beautification, Chairman. She is also a member of the May-
flower Society, the National Society of Colonial Dames,
the Junior League of Boston and the Izaak Walton League
of America. She lives in Chestnut Hill and has her summer
home in Gloucester, Mass.
