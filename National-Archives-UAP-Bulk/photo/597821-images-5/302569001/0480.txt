MEMO FOR THE RECORD
24 Aug 67
SUBJECT: UFO Observations, Maryland, 22 Aug 67
At 0915, 24 Aug 67, Major Quintanilla spoke with Captain Jensen and
It Nielson of the Public Information Office, Andrews AFB, Maryland.
It. Nielson had investigated the reports on this particular UFO.
Major asked It Nielson if he had checked with the police department
on this sighting. He said he hadn't but he had heard that the police
department had made observations of the UFO. Major Quintanilla explained
that we also felt that the observation was a balloon but confirmations
from someone (like the police department) is needed before we can
release a statement to the public that it was a balloon. It. Nielson
said he would check with the police department but he wasn't sure
that he could get a confirmation. It Nielson commented that NICAP
was on an NBC report. The NBC film report left no doubt that it was
a balloon for evidently this person had observed one go up the night
before. Major Quintanilla asked It. Nielson to call the lady in the
documentary and find out where she had gotten her confirmation. It.
Nielson didn't understand why he should get a confirmation from her
since the film left no doubt that it was a balloon. Major Quintanilla
explined that we needed a confirmation before we can release a statement
that the observation was a balloon. Captain Jenson then asked what
Major Quintanilla's job was and who he was connected with. Captain
Jenson also wanted to know who was interested in this. Major Quintanilla
informed him that it was Public at Safoi that had had the inquiries
and that we must give them a statement. Captain Jenson said they would
try to get a confirmation. It. Nielson said that the sky was overcast
to 12,000 ft and the air was very still. Their extension is 4511.
