MEMO FOR THE RECORD
21 December 1967
Called SPADATS and talked to Lt. Canitelli.
Object 2934, Cos 176 debree, decayed on the 13th at 2000 Z 1 approximately
12 hours, this satellite has a maximum of 72.9° inclination. However, the
analysists didn't think the object would break up into two pieces. They
did not have
accurate enough records to plot the trajectory to find out
if the object passed over Indiana at or near the time of the sighting.
Certain information on the debree is classified. It. Canitelli requested
that we send a copy of the case to It. Lewis when we are through.
