NEW YORK, N. Y. 10022
April 26, 1966
Department of Air Force
Pentagon
Washington, D. C.
Dear Sirs:
In light of the discussion concerning UFO's, and
with a certain amount of diffidence, I feel I should make this
report. I certainly do not have an explanation.
On Friday, April 22, 1966, I was driving north on
Route 22 N.Y. heading for Bennett College to visit my wife's
sister.
At about 7:15 P.M., I was around Wingdale and through-
out this period, I was probably driving about 35 or 40 m.p.h.
It was dusk and although a crescent moon could be seen then
or shortly after, the sky was still quite light. There is a
hill to the east and woods to the west of the road.
I saw a blue-green ball of light moving fairly rapidly,
low,horizontally in the sky from east to north or east to west.
I could tell distance and at first thought it was a ball like
those that are shot from Roman candles. I looked for another
fire work display but there was none and the ball didn't fade
out but continued to move.
I then noticed a triangle of white
light like an exhaust east of the ball and thought I must be
seeing some sort of comet with a trail. It was either quite
close or moving very rapidly now above the road ahead of me.
Then it seemed that the white light trail became suddenly
brighter and disappeared. At that point the green light started
to come down in the angle of a plane on its final descent. I
saw another light of the same color seem to move slightly ahead
of the other and then it wasbehindthe trees as though it had
landed. I never saw any flying object - only these lights.
As I drove on, I realized that I didn't know what it was.
I suppose this took only a few seconds.
I couldn't find a nearby landing field on the charts
I looked at over the weekend and also realized that had it been
a plane, the light on that wing would have been red.
Very truly yours,
JLC/ik
