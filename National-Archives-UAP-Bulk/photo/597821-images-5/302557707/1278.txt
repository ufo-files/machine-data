Major Hector Quintanilla, Jr.
Wright-Patterson Air Force Base

Dear Sir:
The following is a description of a possible UPO which I witnessed
and photographed the night of October 26, 1966. Enclosed please
find 1 Kodacolor print, 1 Kodacolor slide, 1 photocopy of a letter
written by a resident of Manlius, New York with whom I am not
acquainted, 1 sketch of the object's flight pattern and a self-
addressed, stamped envelope.
DATE OF SIGHTING: October 26, 1966
TIME OF SIGHTING: 9:30 P.M. Eastern Standard Time
I was in the back yard of my home, located at the above address,
photographing the moon using an Exa with a Wirgar f=135 mm, 1:4.5
lens set at infinity with an opening of 4.5 at 1/50 a second, and
a pair of 7 X 50 binoculars. Turning away from the moon which
had already crossed the zenith, I saw a brilliant, silvery white
light approaching slowly from the east approximately 20° above the
horizon. The brightness of the light was comparable to an auto-
mobile headlight at a distance of 75' when set on high beam. It
did not appear to stand still at any time, alter its speed, break
up or explode, leave a visible trail, change shape or pulsate. As
it approached the zenith, the light lost its silvery appearance and
became milky white but did not lose its brilliance.
