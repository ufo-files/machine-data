Dr Donald H Menzel

Dear Dr Menzel:
Reference the evaluation you made on a 13 May 1967 UPO report
from Peterson Field, Colorado, which you forwarded to us in your
letter of 8 November 1907. Since we received your letter we have
looked through the files, at various times, to see if we could

copy of the sighting.
Your letter of 8 November indicated that
