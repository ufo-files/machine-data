The object was approximately 15° west of due south, and about 15° above

frequency, but were very apparent.
We called out our neighbor, Ann Kahn, and our other
neighbor on the other side of our house, who brought out a telescope and
a set of binoculars. By this time the object was in the same portion of the
sky, but considerably breghtux smaller and no brighter than the brightest
star.
When you put the object in the field of the telescope it would sometimes
move out of the field in approximately one minute. When we would line the
object up over a tree several hundred yards away, we could then move off
in an easterly or westerly direction and line it up over another tree. This,
of course, let me to believe that the object was quite distant at the time we
were taking these particular observations.
