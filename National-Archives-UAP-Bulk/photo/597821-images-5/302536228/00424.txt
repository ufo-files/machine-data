CALLED WAKE ISLAND CONTROL TOWER AT 10/1805Z AUG AND ASKED IF ATC
PERSONNEL HAD SIGHTED AN OBJECT AT 1716Z/1718Z. TOWER REPLIED
NEGATIVE, HAD SEEN NOTHING "AT THAT TIME". SPEED OF THE OBJECT WAS
DESCRIBED AS COMPARABLE TO THAT OF A JET FIGHTER ON FINAL APPROACH,
BUT THE MOVEMENTS DESCRIBED COULD NOT HAVE BEEN PERFORMED BY AN
AIRPLANE. NO WEATHER BALLONS WERE KNOWN TO BE IN THE AREA AT THAT
TIME, AND BY VIRTUE OF THE REPORTED INTERMITTENT BLINKING LIGHT AND
MOVEMENTS OF THE OBJECT AS COMPARED TO PREVAILING WINDS IT IS
CMUSIDERED UNLIKELY THAT THE OBJECT WAS A BALLOON. BIRDS ARE RULED
OUT BECAUSE OF THE BLINKING LIGHT AND THE REPORTED SPEED OF THE
OBJECT.
NO SEARCHLIGHT WAS REPORTED IN OPERATION ANYWHERE IN THE VICINITY
AT THAT TIME. THE PREPARING OFFICER CONSIDERS THE TWO OBSERVERS TO
BE HIGHLY RELIABLE. BOTH REPORTED THAT NEVER IN THEIR EXPERIENCE
HAD THEY SEEN ANYTHING WITH WHICH TO COMPARE THIS OBJECT. NO OTHER
INFORMATION IS AVAILABLE OTHER THAN THAT CONTAINED IN THIS REPORT.
