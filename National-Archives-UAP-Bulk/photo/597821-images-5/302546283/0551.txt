ROCKET EXPERIMENT TIMED
WITH TWO SATELLITE PASSES AT WALLOPS
A rocket experiment coordinated with overpasses of two
satellites was launched by the National Aeronautics and Space
Administration at Wallops Island, Va., at 3:02 p.m. EDT today.
An ionosphere experiment prepared jointly by the Southwest
Center for Advanced Studies, Dallas, Texas, and NASA's Goddard
Space Flight Center, Greenbelt, Md., was launched on a 4-stage
Javelin rocket. The launch was coordinated with overpasses of
the Canadian satellite, Alouette II, and the U. S. satellite,
Explorer XXXI, launched "piggyback" into polar orbits by a
Thor-Agena B from the Western Test Range in California
November 29, 1965. The Javelin probe was sent up between the
two satellite passes.
