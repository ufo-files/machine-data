26 Feb 68
Subj: Observation of Meteor, 25 Feb 68
(47 83 2)
On 26 Feb 68, Chanute AFB, Illinois called to inform us of a
meteor observation by a TWerPilot, near Joliet, Illinois on
25 February 1968, 1405 hours, local time. They were quite sure
it was a meteor observation, however, if additional information
proved them wrong they would forward the report to Wright-
Patterson AFB, Ohio.
