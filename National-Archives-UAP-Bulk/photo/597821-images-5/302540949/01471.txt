MEMO FOR THE RECORD: 3 Aug 65
Maj Pacterow at Kirtland AFB, has been deluged with many many reports of
unidentified flying objects. The majority of which are playing lights.
He requested permission to forvard these reports as a unit on forms rather
than in a TW, as stated in accordance with AFR 200-2. ISgt Davi a N
Moody gave him a verbal okay.
He will forward these reports to our office. Also, he requested clarification
on release of information to the press by the Base PIO. Informed
him that UTOs as such are unclassified and that AFR200-2 provides for
release of evaluations through SAFOI. Its only for him to tell the press
that they are conducting an investigation and its okay to release
information on identified objects, such as know a/c, etc because they
are not unidentified.
