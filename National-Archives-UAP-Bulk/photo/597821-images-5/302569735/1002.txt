Results of Analysis on a "Black Ball"
SEP 2 2 1967
TDAOS
Results have become available from AFML on physical and chemical
analysis conducted on a "black ball". Subject ball was the
property of LTV Aerospace Corp. and handcarfied to Colonel
Standifer (TDE) by Mr. Brennan A. Forcht of LTV.
Analysis
by the burn test showed that the composition of the inner
portion of the ball conformed exactly with that used by
Brunswick in the manufacture of duck pin balls. Additionally
the Rockwell-C. hardness, density, core diameter and serrations,
conformed with those on Brunswick duck pin balls.
Analysis
proved so conclusively that the item has been properly identified,
no further exploitation is to be carried out. LTV was notified
of these results on 18 September by telephone message from Mr.
Van Dame, IDET.
JOHN I. A. ELY, Colonel, USAF
Chief, Aerospace Technologies Division
Directorate of Technology and Subsystems
