1. PURPOSE: This report answers Work Order 65-103 submitted by Major H. Quintanilla, Jr.,
requesting analysis of a color slide to determine the cause of a circular object visible
in the sky.
Analysis was conducted by the Photo Analysis Division (IDDPA), and the Photo.
Processing Division (TDDPP).
ANALYSIS: Examination of the color slide revealed no signature to the reported object
in the sky.
However, after close examination, the image in the slide appeared to have been
caused by a processing defect.
The defect was possibly caused by a drop of bleach hitting
the film prior to the first development.
