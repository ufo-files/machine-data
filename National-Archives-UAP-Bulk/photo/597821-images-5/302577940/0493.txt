JULY METEORS
The crescent moon in the evening sky will not hamper observing when
the Delta Aquarid meteor shower is most active. This occurs on July 29th,
when about a dozen shower members may be seen hourly, but some activity
takes place for a couple of weeks before and after the predicted
peak. At maximum, the radiant lies at right ascension 22h 36", declination
--17°; it moves eastward about 1° a day.
