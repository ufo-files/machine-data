HONEY WELL
MINNEAPOLIS -HONEYWELL REGULATOR COMPANY
July 8, 196h
Capt. R. H. Henry
Macoill Air Force Base
Tanpa, Florida
Dear Captain:
In response to your request to submit data pertaining to the
rewing of the six lights during the July hth fireworks demon-
stration, I would like to submit, my observations.
My family and I were viewing the celebration from the east end
of the Memorial Causeway in the vicinity of the Clearwater audi-
torium at approximately 8:30pm, (although I am not absolutely
sure of the time. I noticed five red lights in the air in a
west southwest direction.
These lights appeared at an angle of
elevation of about 40 to h5° and subtended an arc of approxima-
tely 20°.
The lights were stationary in relation to the earth
and to each other. Four of the five lights had a constant
brightness; the fifth light, however, appeared to pulsate.
The
article in the paper mentioned six lights, however, I only noti-
ced five.
After a period of approximately & to 10 minutes the
lights simply disappeared.
I.called my wife's attention to them
and the attention of nearby spectators. One of these spectators
had in his possession a pair of binoculars.. Both he and I viewed
the lights through them but without being able to gather any addi-
tional information. The phenomena appeared against a dark sky
with no afterglow present, The four FuCs fron Machill had long
since departed the area.
I considered and rejected the ideas that these vere (1) aircraft,
(2) firework display, (3) balloon or blimp, or (h) warning devices
on any high structure.
It was impossible to determine the dis-
tance or magnitude of brilliancy of these lights at night.
I hope that these observations will be of some assistance to you.
Sincerely
