July 7,1964
Capt. R. H. Henry

Dear Sir:
In reply of your request in the Clearwater Sun of July 6,1 will

end of the Memorial Causeway in Glearwater.At about 8:20 P.M. some-
one nent to me said that some jets wher coming.I looked to by lett

that they vere jets and waited for then to fly over, but after a few
minutes it appeared to me that the red objects wher not moving.As I
observed them more closly they seemed to hover in the air.Suddenly
one red light started to descend toward the water.I followed it with
ny eyes until it faded from sight. Meanwhile above the other red lichts
very faintly there was a small dot of white light resembling a star
moving above the other lighte.All of this lasted only a few minutes.
The lights where in the sky from where we were sitting from adout
216° to 226%. I hope this bit of informaton will help you some.
