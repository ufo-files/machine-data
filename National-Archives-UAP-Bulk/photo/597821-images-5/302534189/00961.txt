6 July 1964
Capt. R.H. Henry
Public Information Office
MacDill Air Force Base
Campa, Florida
Dear Sir:
In responsa to your request in today's Clearwater
Sun, here is a description of the red lights which were
observed over Clearwater Harbor during the fireworks
display on July 4.
The news story referred to six lights, but I
saw only fire.
They appeared to be on the same altitude
as the exploding fireworks, and were spaced evenly In a
stralght horizontal Tine running from the approximate
location of the exploding fireworks
in a northerly
direction.
The lights were there when
I arrived, and
were
there when I left at the conclusion of
the display.
assumed at first that they were part of an earlier rocket,
since I arrived late, but they did not drift away as
they
would if they had been suspended from parachutes.
They
appeared to be aircraft lights or
transmission tower warning
lights.
They neither flickered nor moved, although the
drifting smoke sometimes obscured them.
I was standing at the top of the bluff facing west
at the time, in the vacant site of the new city hall, directly
east of the point of land where the fireworks were launched.
There were several hundred people there who could have seen
the lights also. Incidentally, the paper reports similar
lights at the Dunedin fireworks display also.
I hope this might add something new to your
investigations.
Sincerely,
