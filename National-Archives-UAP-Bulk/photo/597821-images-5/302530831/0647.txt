DEPARTMENT OF GEOLOGY
PRELIMINARY REPORT ON DR. HYNEK'S OBJECT
The following remarks are based on conversations with members of the
Geology Department.
Greater detail would require removal of a portion of
the object for microscopic study.
This is a clay-ironstone concretion, with an outer rim of marcasite
(Fe52) and an interior probably of argillaceous siderite (PeCO}). This
concretion probably split along the original bedding of the host rock.
It is definitely a terrestrial object. Concretions of this sort are common
in the Pennsylvanian strata of Illinois.
