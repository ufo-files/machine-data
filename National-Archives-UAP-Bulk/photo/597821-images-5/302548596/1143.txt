1. PURPOSE: This report is written for Major H. Quintanilla, IDEW/UFO, in answer to Work

2. ANALYSIS: Analysis was conducted jointly by the Photo Analysis Division, IDDPA, and the

3. The object which appears in 4 of the sequence of 8 photographs, appears to be circular
in design and to have a domed type upper surface. A cylindrical object protrudes from the
upper surface. The image appears to be a true image.
The information forwarded by Mr. Frank
I. Wilgus, as to the camera being of a cheap type, appears to be correct in that the film
was not held tight against the back of the camera.
There is no apparent evidence of any
tampering with the negatives or multiple exposure.
Information necessary for a more detailed analysis would include focal length of the camera
and lens data. Wish the focal length available, the image could be sized using the distance

in proportion to the accuracy of the range data.
