Proposed reply to letter from
14 November 1966
Dear Mn
Reference your letter of 14 November 1966 concerning your
two observations. In the past three years as chief of Project
Blue Book, I have never ridiculed anyone to my knowledge. I
honestly believe that individuals must witness an unusual
phenomena or else they would not take the time and trouble
to report the observation. I know of no lying or withholding
of information by the Air Force in regards to the UFO problem;
this is a major misconception that has plagued the Project
for years.
I want to stress that the Air Force finds itself involved
with only subjective data. The only information on which to
make a scientific study is that of an observer's own experience
or an observer's interpretation of an experience. In no case
has there been an instance where sightings have furnished scientific
and technological data that could be channeled to Research and
Development for their study and utilization. UPOs exist in
reports only; there has been no conclusive evidence submitted
to or discovered by the Air Force that our planet is being
visited by extraterrestrial vehicles.
In regards to your observations, the lapse of time involved
before reporting the sightings to the Air Force precludes a
comprehensive evaluation. The information will most certainly
be added to the present data base.
Thank you for reporting your observations to the Air Force.
