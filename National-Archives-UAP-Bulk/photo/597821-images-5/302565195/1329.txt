Actually two separate cases by young man (16 yr. old)--one a
daylight sighting one early evening. Both are one witness cases--
which I don't like. Improbable that within a month the boy could
have witnessed two "strange" sights within a month or so.
If report is taken at face value, then evaluation of "possible aircraft"
for either case is not tenable because of the duration, trajectory, colors, etc.
It is more likely that the data presented are not accurate, and so
we can say either:
Possible aircraft
Possible unknown.
