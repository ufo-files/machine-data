Hicksville, L.I., New York
May 9, 1957
Project Blue Book
Air Technical Intelligence Center
Wright-Patterson Air Force Base
Dayton, Chio
Gentlemen:
I wish to report to you, in detail, a sighting which I consider
unusual on the night of Friday, April 14, 1957:
At epproximately 10:30 p.m., April 14th, I was driving West on the
Northern State Parkway, Nessau County, Long island, when I glanced in the
sky and saw what looked like two first magnitude stars, only brighter. It
struck me as being odd inasmuch as the sky was dark and overcast due to an
impending rain prediction. When I noticed these I'stars they were ahead
and slightly to the right of my car. I drove a short distance and suddenly
saw about 5 or 8 lights in a line strung across the sky from the South to
the North (Do not misunderstand the word "suddenly" - when I originally
glanced at the sky, the trees blocked the surrounding area of these "stars".)
I do not use this particular parkway very often and I know it may sound
strange, but i thought there was some construction across the parkway, thus
the lights, and they vere getting closer as I continued in that direction.
My curiousity got the best of me since I saw no outline around these lights
and when I got to the Post Avenue exit, approximately 5 miles from my
initial sighting, I pulled onto the grass and stopped the car. At this
point I saw the lights were moving, but rather slowiy. They were almost
directly overhead, to the right and to the left. In fact, I banged my chin
on the steering wheel in an attempt to watch them through the front window.
I got out of my car to avoid the possibility of glare or distortion from
the glass. They were in view for about 10 minutes from the time I stopped
my car until the last one disappeared from view - they seemed to be
traveling at a high altitude and moving rather slowly - I saw no outlines -
the color was yellow-orange - they did not change color or shade - they did
not twinkle as stars do - they did not fade from view as the lights of a
plane do. If I could have measured the distance from each other I would
say a ruler held at arms distance - 8 to 12 inches apart.
In retrospect, I realized I was not the only witness to these moving
lights. I bed passed many cars also pulled onto the grass but did not connect
this with anything until I, too, had stopped to watch.
The next day, I called
the local police precinct to inquire about any reports but was told by the
police officer I had spoken with that he vas not working the previous night
and therefore could tell me nothing. I then called Police Headquarters only
to be told they knew nothing either, and suggested I contact the City Desk
of the Newaday, the local Nassau County newspaper. No information from them.
