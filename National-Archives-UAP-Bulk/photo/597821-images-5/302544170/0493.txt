16 Sep 65
Valladolid, Spain
Triangular UFO Over Valladolid
By Father Antonio Felices
Our co
tor is a teacher at the Dominicos College at Valladolid, in Spain.
YESTERDAY, September 16, was for me a mest exciting
day. At 5 to
mi, GAl.T., a UFO which came from
the north paused directly over our school. A father and a
brother were out in the garden taking a stroll, when they
noticed the object.
They looked at it bewildered.
Ten
minutes elapsed before they decided to call me. At that time
I was in the church supervising the repairs being carried out.
I rushed to the garden and looked up, to see it right above the
school, the size of a grapefruit, and shining very brightly. I
have no idea as to its height, but that was all we could see
with the naked eye.
The next thing I did was to call out eight other fathers to
witness the fact. Then I went into the physics laboratory
with another father to get out the +-inch telescope which we
have. By 6.20 p.m. we had the telescope mounted in the
garden. When I focussed it-well, I just don't know how to
describe the sight-but there it was, a beautiful silver craft
rocking ever
• so slightly from side to side.
It was like a
triangle
with an oval body in the centre: I made a water
colour drawing of it as I saw it.
We all took turns to watch it. At 6 p.m. the first stars had
begun to appear. The UFO was exactly between the star bela
of the constellation CEPHEUS, and the star epsilon of the
constellation DRACO. At 7.05 p.m., the UFO began to rise
until it finally disappeared from sight.
Many of the fathers who had once laughed at me for
believing that there are these things in the skies, themselves
now firmly believe in them. What a pity it did not come down
for a contact!
