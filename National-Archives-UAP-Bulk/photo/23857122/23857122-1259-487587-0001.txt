On 6 January 1953, between the hours of 0130Z and 11302, a mysterious light
was seen in the sky northeast of Dallas, Texas. In many instances reports con-
flicted with each other. One report would give the heading of the object as
south while another would give the heading in a different direction. The
altitude of the object also varied in the many reports.
Once the area became aware of something unusual in the air, reports came
in more frequently.
The object was first sighted at Paris, Texas, and was
last seen by spotters at Dallas, Texas.
Whether or not there is any tangible explanation to these sightings, it
is not known; however it is believed that we may have identified some of
sightings through our lccal investigation, which we hope will assist in the
