9 July 1953
The following information was obtained by long distance telephone from
Mr. Farl I. Iba, Box 131, Goodwell, Oklahoma, telephone no. 3076. Mr. Iba
is temporarily residing at Cordell Hall, Oklahoma A & M Campus, Stillwater,
Oklahoma.
1. What I saw looked like two (2) search light flashes that varied
back and forth, one hitting right on top of the other. They would flash back
and forth and then center on one. It looked like the beam came from the
ground. You could see part of the beam at times and other times it would
fade completely out. It was larger than a search light beam. I saw this
two (2) or three (3) different nights. I just thought it was a search light.
There are no search lights in this part of the country that I know of; how-
ever, I haven't spent much time here for the last five or six years. There
was no sound. The color was the same as a search light beam. It looked
like a search light beam reflecting off a cloud. I couldn't tell how many
objects there were. When I saw these before, they were in the same locality.
They looked like a search light crossing one another then all at once they
would stop one on top of the other. I couldn't see any trail or exhaust.
There was no propulsion that I know of.
They moved rapidly.
They moved a
lot faster than an ordinary search light beam would have moved. They would
start on one side of the horizon and move to the other. They would start
just like a beam would and move across the sky until they reached the same
place and stop on top of one another. I didn't see them disappear.
I saw this at approximately 2300 (CST) on 6 July 1953.
I observed these with the naked eye•
I was watching through the second (2d) story of the dormitory.
There was a screen on the window and the window was open. The object was in
the direction of Southeast of the dormitory, almost to a right angle - 85°
to 90°.
I have had no experience observing aircraft. I was in the ground
forces of the service for thirty-three (33) months.
6. On this particular night it was cloudy; the overcast was more or
less in the background of the object I was watching. The cloudiness was
Southeast of me.
7. I don't know anything about meteorology.
8. I did not take any pictures.
10•
I don't remember hearing or seeing any aircraft in the air on that
particular night.
