I was on duty at the Bismarck Filter Center the night of August 6th.
When at 0515 Z 6 August 1953 we were notified that there were unidentified
objects in our area at approximately 1530 Z. S/Sgt. Wesley N. Harry

of Bismarck approximately 10 miles at about 2,000 feet and almost looked
like a bright star, except that it moved around constantly, and it changed
colors. It seemed to be red in color when hovering and greenish in
color when moving. It would move in spurts. I watched this object until
0630 Z when at that time I was off duty, and I went home.
