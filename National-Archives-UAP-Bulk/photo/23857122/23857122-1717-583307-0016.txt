STATEMENT
The first time I noticed the flying object was about 2005 MSI on
5 August 1953. It was south east of Black Hawk and glowed a bright red.
Upon starting to travel west, it turned green and got greener as it in-
creased its speed. Upon reaching a spot directly south of Black Hawk,
it stopped abruptly and returned east and to the approximate position
it first begun its flight, then disappeared behind the ridges of hills
to the South east of Black Hawk.
About twenty or thirty minutes later, I spotted perhaps the same
object directly overhead and by the time I had gotten my binoculars on
it, it had disappeared to the north.
About forty five minutes later, I again saw it north east of Black
Hawk, just hovering. Each time a jet would be near, it would disappear
to the north and then as the jets left the vicinity, it would re-appear
at the first location that we had seen it. After re-appearing twice it
failed to re-appear until about 2300. While hovering north east of Black
Hawk, it would glow brightly, a sort of flaming red color, then diminish
in intensity and then glow brightly again.
The speed of this object indicated it was traveling about three or
four times as fast as the jet interceptor.
At the time I saw it the second time, it was so high that it was
barely visible and was a livid red.
