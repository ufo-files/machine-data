HEADQUARTERS
912TH AIRCRAFT CONTROL AND WARNING SQUADRON
Ramore, ontario, Canada
18 August 1953
STATEMENT
On the 16th of August, 1953, I was on patrol duty, about 2140 I
arrived at the mess hall after bringing the guard to the main gate.
Just before stopping, Sgt Hallstrom made a motion for me to come over
to the barracks. When I got there he told me to quickly turn out the
lights on the vehicle and he pointed out a bright red object. To me
it looked like a fire as it was bright reddish orange. It stayed bright
for a few seconds then went dim, I moved about four feet in a different
direction and it became bright again. Sgt Hallstrom told me to call
operation on the hill andhave them get a fix on the object. The troops
going on shift got into the vehicle and when I arrived on the hill, the
object was gone. The airmen that did see it from the hill said it was
sort of pie-shaped.
Pall & you how sli
DONALD S. LYSAKOWSKI
