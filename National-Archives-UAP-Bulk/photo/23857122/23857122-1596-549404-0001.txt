Above named civilian called Tinker Air Force Base long distance telephone
and talked with Mr. Louis B. Lackey, Civilian, Badge No. 59063, Aircraft Dispatcher
Supervisor, Flight Operations, Base Executive's Office, Tinker Air Force Base, and
reported sighting three (3) objects which looked like stars with tails that glowed
traveling South.
Objects were sighted over Muenster, Texas, (reported in message
as Muster, Texas), geographical location approximately thirty (30) miles West of
Gainesville, Texas. One object made a wide 180° turn and headed North which was the
reason Mr. Dowborn called this station.
The other two objects continued South.
Mr. Lackey advises that Mr. Dowborn left the impression he had called or intended
to call Fort Worth, Texas.
3. Speed was estimated at 2000 MPH; altitude was estimated at 60,000 feet.
Mr. Dowborn reported that he did not hear any sound.
Flight was straight and level
except for time one made 180° turn.
Time of sighting was 2305 Central Standard Time
and was reported to TAFB 2324 Central Standard Time.
Sighting was visual from the
ground and length of time observed is unknown. Weather was clear with wind velocity
of approximately 15 MPH.
