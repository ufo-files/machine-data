Statement by A/10 larry W. Skinner
At 2139 Base Operations called us in the tower, via intercom,
and asked us if
we could see any lights east of the tower. At the time we were all watching the
landing strip. Looking to the east we did see what appeared to be seven red lights
suspended just over Denison.
They looked just like any pert lights of an aircraft.
The only difference was that these lights seemed to be stationary until we saw that
they were rising very slowly. We took pictures of them with a stereo-camera we
keep in the tower for such purposes. We took about 10 pictures of different lens
openings and shutter speeds.
The lights kept rising slowly until about 2155 when they
faded out.
Throughout their ascent,
they appeared to keep the same formation except
for one, which was to the upper right. That one light drifted farther apart from
the others. Just before fading out, each light flickered on and off.
But that
could have been the atmospheric condition.
Any indication of altitude was practically impossible,
since we did not know
the size of the lights nor the distance they were from the tower.
Lany A Skinner
Skinner, AF19494755
A/10, USAF
Perrin AFB, Texas
