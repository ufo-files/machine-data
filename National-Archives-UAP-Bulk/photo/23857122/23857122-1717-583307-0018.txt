STATEMENT
At 2005, on 6 August 1953, I sighted an object about two miles north
east of our farm.
It looked like a star except that it glowed red and
green and moved.
It moved up and down, back and forth in no apparent
pattern.
We observed it through the binoculars but couldn't distinguish
a shape.
It was in sight all the time except when the jet took after it.
As soon as we could hear the jet back over the base, the object came into
sight again.
We could tell it moved at a tremendous rate of speed as it
disappeared so fast and reappeared just as fast.
nd guin Aldren
