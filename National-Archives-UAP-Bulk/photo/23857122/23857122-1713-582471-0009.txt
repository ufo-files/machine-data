The preparing officer in company with an OSI agent and a Castle AF Base PIO
representative drove to Modesto, California on 8 September 1953 to view a photograph
showing an unidentified elliptical shaped light.
2. The reason given for taking the picture was that Mr. Charles ROGERS, Modesto
Bee newspaper photographer, and Mr. Jack CROSE, a reporter for the same paper saw a
yellowish white light at 1945PDT 3 September 1953. The light seemed to be about 10
degrees above the horizon in the direction of Modesto Airport.
They said it appeared
to remain stationary as they drove a distance of about one mile East where it vanished
from view.
They believed it was an aircraft with landing light on flying the upwind
leg at Modesto Airport. Nevertheless, ROGERS took his camera to the newspaper building
and set it up on the roof in the direction he believed he had seen the light.
He left
his camera unattended with the shutter open for about four hours.
He developed the
film on 8 September 1953. When he noticed the elliptical light and could not explain
it, he called Castle AF Base.
