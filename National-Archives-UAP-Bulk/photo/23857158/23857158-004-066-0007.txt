Inquiries were made of farmers around the area in an effort to find a
parachute.
It was learned that one had been found on 2 Oot 55 by a farmer's son.
He was located and the parachute obtained.
The boy stated he had found the chute in
nearby field (about three and a half miles NE of GOC observation point).
The
parachute was of paper and had radiosonde attached to it.
It was definitely identified
as the type being used on ordinary weather balloons.

and velocity for about 24 hours.
