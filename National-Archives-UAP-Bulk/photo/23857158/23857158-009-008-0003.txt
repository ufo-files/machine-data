MEMORANDOM FOR RECORD:
1. Long-distance telaphone conversation between It. Davenport, Lowry APS
and It. Machyowsky at this Headquarters.
2. It. Machyowsky stated that he had received information that It. Davenport
had taken intercept action on the UPOB sighted over Denver, Colorado, 13/0515Z
August 1956.
3. It. Davenprot stated that was correct and that he had identified the
objecte as two balloons.
4. It. Machyowsky questioned It. Davenport concerning further details of
the intercept action for identification.
5. It. Davenport after relating the details stated that there was no doubt
whatsoever that the objects were balloons.
JOSEPH A MACHIOWSKI, 2/Lt., USAF
