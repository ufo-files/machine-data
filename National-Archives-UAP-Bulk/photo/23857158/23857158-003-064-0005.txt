Inclosure #l, Part One, Report #1C-UFOB-10-55. Statement of
Ernest I. Dunn.
S-T-A-T-E-M-E-N-T
While looking in a Southeasterly direction, I saw an object which
appeared to be a meteor, but in the thirty-five (35) seconds of which
it was observed, it traveled on a horizontal plain.
I considered this
to be unusual.
I reported this to the Filter Center, Pasadena, California
(EJBE 5705) immediately.
Object was brilliant white and seemed to
have a slight tail which while traveling changed colors from white
to red to a hazy light blue.
It covered a four mile span in thirty
(30) seconds - approximately 4,800 miles per hour.
I lost sight of
object in the extreme Southwest.
