S-T-A-T-B-M-E-N-T
1. Departed Niagara Falls AFB at approximately 2110-29 Feb 56 after being
notified by GCI that G.O.C. had reported some unidentified lives in the Hamburg
Area of Buffalo, N.I.
After leveling out at 4000' indicated and contacting GCI,-I was
vectored into the Hamburg Area of Buffalo, N.I.
3. When I arrived over the Hamburg Area I did not notice any lites, but
I did notice a large bright lite that appeared to be directly over the dow
town portion of Buffalo, N.Y. At this tine I started to investigate. Mr
heading was 300 degrees magnetic - at first I thought it was a transport or a
bomber - but I could not decrease my range from this object.
At this time I
thought this must be a star so I reported back to the Hamburg Area - with the
intentions that this star would stay in the same relative position.
When arriving over Hamburg I look outside and there this was again -
off my left wing. At this time I was a little confused.
I then took up a
heading of north and reaching the south shore of Lake Ontario I did a 180
degree turn and flew about 15-20 south of Buffalo - still this bright object
was off my right wing - never any azimuth separation.
After passing Buffalo I
decided I would determine if this was my imagination, a star or an object. I
concluded that if this was a star by pointing my aircraft directly at this
object and climbing to the maximum altitude of the F-86D I would stall out
with the object in the same position.
I started climbing and it was always up
and ahead of me. But while I was climbing it appeared that I was getting closer
in range. The object definitely got brighter and finally at 32,000' 1 was level
vith the object-because at this point instead of looking up at it, - was looking
ut and level with it at my flight altitude. This was the first time I seemed
to be getting closer, then I notice a flashing red lite - intermittently flash-
ing from this object.
At this time I was 91 N-miles W-NW of home plate - all
of a sudden it seemed to increase the gap between me and the object, at this
time I was running low on fuel - and called it quits - after turning back for
home I kept this object in sight with the rear view mirror in the A/C - I
decided to take one last look, -I banked the A/C around and there was behind me
- then, all of a sudden it disappeared -I banked the A/C around and returned
to my Base, landing at 2230 est.
At no time did I even get a radar contact
with my radar equip. -that was operating very satisfactorily.
