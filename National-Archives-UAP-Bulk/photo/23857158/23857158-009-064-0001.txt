Declassification Authority NND 923007
18 October 1956
Naples, Morida
Dear Mr.
Sometime ago, this organization sent you an AISOP Form No. 2
(Airborne Observer's Data Sheet).
As of this date, the completed form has not been received at
this organisation.
The information asked for in this form is essential to com-
pleting our evaluation of your sighting and to our statistical
compilation of Unidentified Flying Object sightings within the
boundaries of the United States.
If you have already found out what caused your sighting,
please complete the AlSOP Torm and send it to us, so that we shall
be able to close our file on your sighting.
If the form has been lost or misplaced, please write to us,
and we will be happy to send you another form. Again our thanks
for your cooperation.
Sincerely,
CHARLES W. CODSEY
Captain,
USAF
Assistant Adjutant
