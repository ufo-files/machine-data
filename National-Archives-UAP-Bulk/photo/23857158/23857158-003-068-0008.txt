Statement of CWO Carl E. Russ
S-T-A-T-E-M-E-N-T
As the Naval Air Facility duty officer, I was called to the field control
tower to observe an orange colored light in the sky that appeared to be moving.
I first observed this object at about 2215 (PDST) without visual aids.
I then
looked at it through 8 X 50 binoculars and observed it moving in a westerly
direction.
The object then saemed to reversa course and gain altitude during
this maneuver. It traveled at high speed. It then again reversed course moving
slowly again in a westerly direction on a slight descent - disappearing in the
west.
