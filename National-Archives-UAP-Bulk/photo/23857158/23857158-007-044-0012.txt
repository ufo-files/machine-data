This object was sighted about 7:50 p.m. on Nov. 2, 1955; this date
could be in error one day as I did not note the date after reporting
the sighting and thought little more about it. I had walked out on
my front porch for a breath of fresh air and was setting on the steps
when I looked up for the big dipper; the dipper wasn't up and the moon
was low in the East. I glanced at Polaris and as I looked away, I saw
a light moving and started watching it thinking monentarily it was a
plane until the light changed from white to blue then to purple rapidly;
this change in color coupled with increased momentum from due North
horizontally to the West convinced me it was not a plane. At this point
I called my wife; I called her three times quickly:
She came immediately
and I told her to look up and pointed to the approximate position of
the object and she readily picked it up and we watched it continue
Westward until it was almost due West which was a matter of possibly
one and one-half to two minutes; during this time it continued to
change colors.
When it reached the West, it stopped momentarily
seeming to move in several different directions within a small radius
then began to accend vertically very fast. It appeared to be moving
vertically and away from us. With the object still in sight, I went
inside and called the Duty Officer at Naval Air Station Corpus Christi
with the intention of giving the location for additional witnesses;
not to report a UFO. I was answering questions for several minutes
on the phone and when I returned to the
porch, my wife contended she
could still see the object, however, I did not.
