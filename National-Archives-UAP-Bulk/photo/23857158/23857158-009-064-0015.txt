DURING A VERY NORMAL AND PLEASANT FLIGHT ALONG A ROUTE
OUITE FAMILAR TO BE (I travel this raate about ten times per week)
I SUDDENLY WAS STARTLED BY AN AIRCRAFT ABOUT TO CROSS MY
FLIGHT PATH. IT WAS ENTERING MY LINE OF VISION ON THE LEFT
AND PASSING DIRECTLY ACCROSS AND TO THE RIGHT. MY FIRST IN-
CLINATION WAS TO CHANGE COURSE TO AVOID IT, NOT HAVING HAD
A DIRECT LOOK AT IT, BUT THEN REALIZED IT WAS HIGHER THAN
MY PLANE. THIS LED TO THE REALIZATION THAT THE OBJECT WAS
MILES AWAY, LARGER THAN AN AIRCRAFT, AND TRAVELING TOO
FAST TO BE ANYTHING FAMILIAR TO ME. MY ANTICIPATION OF
ANIX NEED FOR THE POSSIBLE SPEED OF THE OBJECT CAUSED ME
TO TIME THE LAST HALF OF THE CONTINUOUS FLIGHT PATH.
IT WAS NOT UNTIL THE OBJECT WAS OUT OF SIGHT THAT I REALIZED
HOW AFRAID AND SIMPLY SCARED I WAS. I SPENTABOUT TEN
MINUTES ANNALYZING THE SITUATION BEFORE CALLING FT. MYERS
RADIO. THEN I ONLY ASK THEM TO LET ME KNOW IS THERE WERE
ANY OTHER AIRCRAFT IN THE AREA.
I WAS TRAVELING AT 6500 INDICATED, AND THE OBJECT APPEARED
TO BE AT ABOUT TEN THOUSAND FEET. THE FIRST MOMENT AFTER S
SIGHTING, I THOUGHT THE OBJECT MUST BE DANGERIOUSLY NEAR,
DUE TO ITS SPEED OF CROSSING MY LINE OF VISION. INTENSE
ATTENTION SEEMED TO PROVE ITS GREAT DISTANCE AND XNSX
UNUSUAL SPEED. MY TOTAL FLYING TIME TO DATE WAS IN ESESS
OF FOUR THOUSAND HOURS. I JOINED THE NAVAL AIR CORPS IN
1943, SERVED ABOUT TWO YEARS ACTIVE DUTY IN WW2 AS A FIGHTER
PILOT. Was member of VF5 aboard USS YORKTOWN (The Fighting Lady)
I HAVE NO DOUBTS WHATEVER ABOUT ANYTHING CONCERNED EXCEPT
THE DISTANCE, SIZE, AND SPEED OF THE OBJECT.
I HOLD A COMMERCIAL MISZENZE. LICENSE WITH CURRENT PHYSICAL
KXX I DO NOT SMOKE OR DRINK.
I WAS NEVER BEFORE SO SHAKEN UP, EVEN IN COMBAT.
