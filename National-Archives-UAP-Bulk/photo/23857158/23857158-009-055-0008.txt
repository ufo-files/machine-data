Declassification Authority NND 923007
31 May 1956
CERIIEICAIE
At about 2030, 30 May 56, I was on duty in the Wing Control Room.
I overheard a conversation from the Base Operations control dispatcher,
requesting the crash crew to investigate a light off the northeast end
of the ramp.
I looked out and saw a small fire in that direction which
appeared to be off the Base. At the same time there was a moving light
and an object of indefinite shape with a yellow or orange light on it
at about 500 feet and two miles distance. It moved as though circling,
sometimes practically hovering and at others moving quite rapidly, rising
and falling. I assumed it to be the Base helicopter which I had seen
flying about, late in the afternoon. I reported on the fire, giving the
light no great concern. I had the teletalk set to monitor Base Operat-
ions, and I kept over-hearing talk about this object.
police had been alerted, and they had seen "something" in the area,
which they were unable to identify. At a little past 2300 the Base Oos
dispatcher was returning from the dining hall, when he observed this
light flying at 500 to 1,000 feet above and parallel to our long runway,
moving from southwest to northeast. He stopped his vehicle, got out to
look, and could hear no sound from the object. Upon being informed of
this, I called Major Karol and notified him of these strange goings-on.
The Base dispatcher was directed to call a Mr. Holland, of Holley Dust-
ing Corporation to see if he had any dusters flying at that hour. He
replied that he did not and knew of no-one who had.
I have been earning either all or part of my living from flying
as pilot, engineer, observer and passenger for something over 5,000
hours in the last twenty-five years. I've seen a lot of airplanes,
blimps, helicopters and other things fly, and this didn't act quite
like any of them. I do not believe in flying saucers, but there was
"something" out there.
