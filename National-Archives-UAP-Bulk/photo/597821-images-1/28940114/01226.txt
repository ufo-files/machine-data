UM MARY
1. At 0345 hours on 11 October 1951, two enlisted men of the 5250n Fighter-
Bomber Squadron stationed at heubiburg Air Base, Neubiburg, Germany observed a strange
looking object in the sky over Neubiburg Air Base. Forwarded herewith as Inclosures #l
and #2 are the statements of Sgt. Robert L. Vice and T/Sgt. Carl E. Lucas which
describe the sighting.
From their description the object appears to be some form of a
flying "disk". Its altitude was estimated to be about 20,000 feet.
2. A check was made with the meather detachment and flight operations to deter-
mine whether there might be some explanation. There were no weather balloons known to
be in the sky.
One C-47 departed from Reubiburg Air Base at 0553 hours, but the two
airmen insisted the observation was earlier than 0553 hours and that the object was not
a C-+7.
From time to time this office receives information similar to that presented
in this report. It is requested that the Analysis Division of the Air Technical
Intelligence Center, Dayton, Ohio, advise this office whether to continue to send back
reports of this nature.
