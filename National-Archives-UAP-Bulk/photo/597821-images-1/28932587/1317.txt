NEW "FLYING DISC" RUMOR
At approximately 0230 on 31 October 1948, en unidentified object
moving rapidly through the air was sighted and watched by three enlisted
men stationed at Lagens, Terceira. The object was variously reported as
being round, yellow orange in color, larger than a star and moving upward and
forward in a northerly direction from an estimated altitude of between two
and three thousand feet. There were no attendant vapor trails or sound
effects, and the object was lost from view within five minutes.
believed by an authoritative source that the object was a weather balloon
with an attached light swinging from it.
This opinion has since been
substantiated by weather squadron records showing the release of such a balloon
at 0245 on 31 October 1948.
