TO: WHOM IT MAY CONCERN.
ON TLE EVENING OF 7, JANUARY 1948 AT APPROXIMATELY 19:25
OCLOCK THE UNDERSIGNED WITNESSED A VERY BRIGHT LIGHT IN THE SKY,
IN THE SOUTH WEST DIRECTION OF C.C.A.F. THE OPERATIONS CLERK AND
MYSELF FIRST WITNESSED IT AND CALLED THE TOWER AND HAD TLEM LOCK AT
IT THROUGL THEIR FIELD GLASSES. THEY SAID IT WAS MOVING BUT COULD
NOT MAKE OUT WHAT IT WAS. I WENT UP TO THE TOWER AND LOOKED AT IT
THROUGH
THE FIELD CLASSES AND IT APPEARED TO BE VOVING UP AND DOWN
AND FROM SIDE TO SIDE. AT ONE TIME CLOUDS CAME BETWEEN THE OBJECT
AND THE BASE BUT THE LIGHT COULD STILL BE SEEN THROUGH IT. AT
APPROXIMATELY 19:45 THE OBJECT BEGAN TO MOVE AWAY TOWARD THE SOUTH
WEST AND DISAPPEARED OVER THE HOROZON AT ABOUT 20:00
T/Sgt.
