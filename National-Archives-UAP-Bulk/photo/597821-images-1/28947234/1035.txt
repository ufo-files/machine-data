4 August 1952
At 10 AN on 1 August 1952, as I was entering the Security Service
compound with a group of airmen returning to work after a movie at the
Base theater, someone called attention of the group to something in the
sky. Upon looking up, I saw a round disc like object, white in color.
It seemed to be at a high altitude and was moving at a high rate of speed
in a southwesterly direction. It performed no manuevers of any kind,
only flew in a straight and level flight until too far away to be seen
with the naked oye. I saw nothing that could be taken for wings or tail
surfaces. The plane of the object seemed to be horizontal rather than
vertical.
It appeared to have some depth, possible one to six in compar-
ison with the diamster.
JOHN R. HOLL
