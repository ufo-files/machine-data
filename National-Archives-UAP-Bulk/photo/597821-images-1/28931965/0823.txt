INCLOSURE #1
Phoenix, Ariz.
November 3rd
Dear Dr. Fruett:
On October 24th I noticed a very strange star or
fireball in the Eastern sky. It was green and my first in-
pression was that it was a plane. It moved very slowly
South and slightly Worth as if it wasn't certain of which way
to go. It slowly changed its course to N.West.
Being very curious about the star, I watched it very
closely, without taking my eyes away once.
It took at least 75 minutes to cross over Phoenix.
The star seemed to be drawn to other stars, and when it came
close to them, it would become very bright.
avertime the star would light up and leave the other
star, it would be dimmer.
Finally, we could hardly see it.
When
I thought it had completely disappeared, it suddenly became very
bright and fell apart.
From here in Phoenix, I can point out juat where the
fireball travelled, but it is rather difficult on paper but it
did start in the east and end up N.West.
If there is anything else I can tell you that you
would like to know concering the fireball, I will be very
happy to tell you.
Yours truly,
DOD DIR 5200.10
A NEY CHAISSATOE
