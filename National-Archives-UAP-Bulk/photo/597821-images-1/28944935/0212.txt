SUBJECT: Observing of object in the North evening sky
On 21 July 1952 at approximately 2230 hours, the Hauptwachmeister
of the Land Police
and myself were on the night round Number 2.
were on the road THUENGEN-RETZBACH at the kilometer distance marker 19.5.
this moment we observed a duty vehicle of the WUERZBURG Military Police.
The
driver and the assistent driver mentioned to us an incident which we at first
could not understand, both were still seated in the car. Later they dismounted
from the vehicle and pointed in the direction of the North sky. We noticed in
this direction a collection of clouda. According to our observation it was a
cumulation, which were 4 to 6 kilometers high and were moving. Towards the
East they were parted and stretched and in width they were unsy metrical stripes
that were pulled apart. In the easterly direction of this picture it looked like
it was in the shape of a great elipse and its outer edges could only be seen
slightly. Further west it looked as it was a uncompleted S. The complete cloud
formation was colored amber. It looked as the color and cloud formation was
caused by the setting sun. Both of the policemen mentioned that this is not
caused by an aircraft. What other meaning this had they did not mention.
1. Hauptwachmeister of the Land Police
2. Ober Kommisioner of the Land Police
