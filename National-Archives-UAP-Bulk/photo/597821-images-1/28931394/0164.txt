ACTION TAKEN ON HAMEL, MINN, INCIDENT
Reference letter 17 Aug 1948 from Eq 5th Army, received MCIAXO-3 on
20 August 1948, pertaining to incident in Hamel, Minnesota, the following
action has been taken:
On 20 Aug 48 a call was placed to Hq, 5th Army, contacting It Col
William B. Allison, Executive Officer, C-2. Colonel Allison informed
this Headquarters that action would be taken to resurvey the incident
and to accomplish photographic and earth-gample investigations of
the area in which the incident occurred.
On 21 August (Saturday) a call was received by Capt Gordon from a
Major Morgen of Ha, 5th Army, inforning Capt Gerdon (MCIAXO-3) that
the indentation occasioned by the missile had been covered since the
incidence. Unfortunately, however, since the occurrence there had been
two or three heavy rainfalls and the depression in the ground previous-
ly noted was found to be 1/3 full of water. Photographic coverage was
made, however, and samples of rocks and earth in the depression were
extracted. This evidence was being forwarded to 5th Army Hq Area as
of Tuesday the 24th and would be forwarded this office (MOIAXO-3)
immediately upon receipt, and should arrive this Hq. by the end of the
week.
Benjamin Gordon
Captain, USAF
Project Engineer
