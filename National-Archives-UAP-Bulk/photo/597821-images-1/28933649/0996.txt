Forwarded herewith is Closed Report of S/A JOHN T. REILLY of this
District, dated 24 Way 1949.
Alleged flying saucers sighted over Bend, Oregon, on 24 March 1949
appear to be natural phenomena. It is generally agreed by observers that
these so-called flying saucers were large pieces of paper or other articles
of like nature raised high into the sky by whirlwinds occasioned by convec-
tion currents. As all logical leads are exhausted in this District, the
case is forwarded to yur Headquarters for necessary evaluation.
