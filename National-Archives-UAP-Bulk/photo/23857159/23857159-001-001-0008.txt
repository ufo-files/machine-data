SUMMARY: An unidentified object was reported observed over the

was observed by 3 SOURCES;
one (1) civilian and two (2) Air

approximate altitude of
3000 to 4000 feet. The object appeared
in the form of a bright light, flashing like a diamond from
white thru red, blue, yellow and green. The object appeared
to the observers to be about the size of a pea held out at
arms length. See inclosures #1, #2, #3, FORM A, Statement of
Observer.
COMMENTS OF PREPARING OFFICER: The reliability of the SOURCES can
