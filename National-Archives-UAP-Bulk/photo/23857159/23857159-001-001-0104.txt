Set forth below in accordance with the authority in Air Force Regulation
200-2, 26 August 1953, is the report of investigation of an Unidentified Flying Object
(UFOB) sighted near Olmsted Air Force Base on 22 April 1954.
On 22 April 1954 at approximately 2200 hours Mr. Paul Starr, the Aircraft
Dispatcher on duty in the Flight Control Section at Olmsted Air Force Base, Middletown
Pennsylvania, received a telephone call from an individual who identified himself as
Mr. Benjamin F. Ebersole. Mr. Ebersole reported seeing an Unidentified Flying Object
in flight overElizabethtown, Pennsylvania at about 2150 hours.
3.
At about 2230 hours on 22 April 1954, Mr. Starr called Mr. Percy Wheeler,
Airway Operations Specialist on duty in the tower at the Harrisburg State Airport.
Mr. Wheeler reported that he had made radio contact with a west bound United Airlines
plane over the Lancaster range which is only a few miles from Elizabethtown, Pennsylvania
at approximately 2150 hours, which it will be noted is about the same time Mr. Ebersole
sighted an unidentified flying object.
4•
On 23 April 1954, Mr. Ebersole was interviewed by a representative of the
Inspector General's Office in connection with his knowledge of subject unidentified
flying object. Mr. Ebersole stated that his wife was attracted by the presence of an
unknown object in the sky near his home in Elizabethtown, Pennsylvania, on the evening
of 22 April 1954 at approximately 10 o'clock.
He observed what his wife referred to as
a "meteor and was impressed with the speed with which this unknown object moved through
the sky.
It was his impression that this object traversed the sky in a southerly to
northerly direction and remained in the same plane at all times. He described this
object as about the relative size of a grapefruit and slightly oval in configuration.
As well as he can recall this air-borne phenomenon was within his view for approximately
fifteen (15) to twenty (20) seconds and at no time did it emit any sound audible to
him.
Ebersole alleges that Mr. Martin Lindemuth, a neighbor also saw subject unidenti
fied flying object.
(See attached statement of Mr. Benjamin F. Ebersole for complete
details.)
5•
Estimate of informant's reliability - Ebersole stated to the investigating
officer that he is interested in unidentified flying objects and has read many articles
in popular magazines relative to "flying saucers.
