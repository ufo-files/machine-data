REPORT OF FLYING OBJECT
At 2305 hours on 26 November 57 an individual who identified himself as
Robert Canan, 312 Princeton Street, Brentwood Park, Las Vegas, Nevada
informed me that he saw what appeared to be a flying saucer.
This object
was seen at approximately 2120 hours from his residence and was midway
between Mt. Charleston, Beacon and Gene, Nevada. It disappeared over
Spring Mountain Range. The color was yellowish orange and the size of a
jet aircraft. It appeared to be too slow for a falling star or meteor.
The estimated altitude was 15,000 feet and was viewable by the naked eye
from 5 to 7 seconds. The angle was fairly steep - approximately 30 degrees
using the earth surface as a reference point.
Both he and his wife witnessed
this and the latter noticed the sky glow shortly after the object passed out
of sight.
Mr. Canan informed me that he formerly worked at Nellis and was presently
working at Mercury as a electronic technician for the Edgerton, Germeshausen,
& Crier, Inc. He informed the Sheriff's department prior to contacting
the base and they were going to inform McCarren Field.
I asked Mr. Canan for all of the particulars on this falling object as
stated above. He appeared to be a mature unexcited individual and did
not hesitate tem while responding to my questions.
This was reported, as written, to the Airdrome Officer immediately after my
conversation with Mr. Canan.
The AO was planning on contacting him again
to see if he could pin point the exact area he saw this object.
Mr. Canan will be available for any
additional information at his residence
(phone DU 2-6062) any time after 1900 hrs.
