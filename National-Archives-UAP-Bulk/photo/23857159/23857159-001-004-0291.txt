REPORT OF THE INVESTIGATING COMMITTEE OF THE C.I.O.V.I. (INVESTIGATING CENTER OF
UNIDENTIFIED FLYING OBJECTS) ON THE CASE WHICH OCCURRED 2 OCTOBER 1958
In this case although we have two observers but only one record, the persons
are absolutely reliable.
As several investigators have stated, among the several unidentified flying ob-
jects, the saucers sometimes present characteristics similar to the one seen by
Miss Cereols.
On many instances, flying saucers have been seen travelling in the dark with a
green-blue light at the bottom.
This luminosity has no definite contours so it
makes it difficult to state the real size of the object.
It must be pointed out that this light can disappear completely and the saucer
flies on in complete darkness and silently, thus being undetected.
Another circumstance worth mentioning is that the direction of its flight was
SW - NE.
In many other cases it has been observed that the predominant direction
of these objects in this country is S - N.
The Investigation Department of the C.I.O.V.I. states that on the 2nd of October
1958, a flying saucer of OVI type flew over the Aeronautical School at 2315 hours.
