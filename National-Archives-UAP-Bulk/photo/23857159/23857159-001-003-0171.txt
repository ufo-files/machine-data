Headquarters Company, 3d Battalion, 19th Infantry
On duty at OP 211 (OP Maizie). On 6 November at about 0504, I was
outside bunker, in the open, no trees around, and sudden ilash occurrec
which appeared bluish in color lasting a few seconds - then I look
around, not up in the air - then I looked up and between 2 large clouds
I saw it I checked the time as 0505 and called it in over the phone -
As soon as I saw the flash I called my companion observer, PFC Brown,
outside and showed him the smoke. In describing the "smoke" it was dingy
whitish vapor - like a jet trail. It was larger at one end than the other
about 6 inches at arms length. I was actually looking down when the flash
occurred which lit up the area and reflected off the ground. I looked up
and saw what appeared to be just a little mist between the two big heavy
clouds.
All at once it grew larger growing into a streak moving from norti
o south. I saw it at an angle of 15
elevation. I saw it in the west
and know it to be so because I work at the OP and know which is west. It
moved from right to left or north to south. It moved an angle of a few
degrees and disappeared behind the clouds. Did the vapor trail glow - No -
It was light enough out in the early dawn beginning to get light to see 2
dark clouds over the DMZ and the streak was quite light in comparison.
The fat end of the streak was on the right. No noise accompanied the
bright light or was heard after. Bright light lasted 2 - 3 seconds -
bluish white light - used no optical equipment. Vapor lasted about a
minute but we went inside and made phone calls. I determined the coor-
dinates of the smoke to be about exactly west or 270° by map features -
just south of Kaesong a few degrees.
It seemed like a jet to me - the light was like someone was taking
a flash picture of me.
I couldn't actually say that the vapor trail was
connected with the light.
It seemed actually like an object which I
couldn't see, moving and leaving a vapor trail behind which grew larger
toward the tail of it.
How high was it? I'm a bad judge of elevation -
about 11,000 ft at least.
It was light enough to see clouds the vapor
did not glow. I have no idea of the speed. My buddy saw it before it
disappeared behind the cloud.
