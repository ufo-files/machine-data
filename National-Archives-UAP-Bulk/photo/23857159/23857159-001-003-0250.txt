TIME OF SIGHTING: Approximately 2:05 a.m. July 16 to 2:40 a.m.
PLACE OF SIGHTING: HASTINGS RACEWAY located two miles northwest of Hastings.
WEATHER VISABILITY: Slightly hazy with a three-quarter waning moon.
WITNESSES TO SIGHTING: Lewis Bacon, movie projectionist and World War II Navy
veteran; Beatrice Murphy, restaurant owner; her 16 year old nephew,
Ronald Bartholomew and Marian Kelley, newspaper reporter, all of Hastings.
WIND: Soft, gentle breeze from the west or slightly southwest.
INCIDENT: What appeared at first to be a star of the first magnitude of a
red-amber or fire color, was noticed in the heavens about five degrees above
the horizon and about ten degrees south of due west. Ursa Major hung about
15 degrees above the horizon and the nearest corner of Camelopardalis had
risen to about ten degrees above the horizontal.
While gazing at the object it became invisible but reappeared in about the
same position two or three minutes later. This time it seemed to rise straight
up from the horizon for about an additional five degrees directly towards a
slightly
small star. Then it began to bravel in an arc/to the south and towards the
east towards the moon. No sound could be detected coming from the direction
of this object; only usual ground noises could be heard.
As to the shape and color, all four persons agreed it had an oval-shaped
appearance with a shaft-like shape of a brighter, whiter color towards its
center and going upwards towards the heavens. Its relative size seemed to
remain about the same as when first seen.
As this object neared the bright halo around the moon (estimated to be
about five minutes after its/appearance in the west) all of us cupped our
hands around our keyes leaving only a slit for observation purposes in order
to keep the object in view in the bright area around the moon.
A minute or less after cupping our hands, we heard the sound of several
approaching planes coming from the west-north-west. As they came into view,
we turned and counted ten planes believed to be three or four engine type
aircraft, probably propeller driven but do not know for sure.
We watched
these planes that were traveling towards the northeast until lost from our
sight and could no longer be heard.
