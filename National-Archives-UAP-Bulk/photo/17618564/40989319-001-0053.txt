5. Test Z at Oaby Island
Test 2 was recorded from the Oahu Islands in the Hawaiian group,
about 39.2° east of the test area. Recording at the floating altitudes
lasted from 219 to 264 minutes after the 2 zero time. Ground equipment
for this test consisted of two low frequency I-21 microphones with a
Brush recorder, and the recording altimeter. No triangulation equip-
ment was used because ground reception within the frequency range of
the equipment was improbable.
The recordings obtained at Oahu Island are shown in Figures
23, 24, and 25. No signals were obtained above the general noise level
during the floating periods of the balloon. A period of increased
noise level lasting for about three minutes was received during ascent
of the balloon, corresponding to an average velocity of 11.7 degrees/
hour. Signal strengths averaged about 60 dynes/cm?. If this increase
n noise level was due to the explosion, then the disturbance clearly
recorded at Alamogordo with the same average velocity must also have
been due to the explosion. At that time, ground recorders were not in
operation.
