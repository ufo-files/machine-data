for conveniently switching or patching the various incoming outpost
or microphone lines for communication and testing purposes. The
switchboard has 1 telephone circuit and 10 other circuits as follows:
2 outpost circuits and 8 microphone circuits.
b.

made by means of 10 pairs of binding post terminals located along
the bottom of the operating panel. Each circuit has two jacks, a
telephone drop, and a key. A pair of binding posts and a socket for
Cord CD-428 are provided at the output end of each microphone
circuit. The two outpost circuits have a common output which is
