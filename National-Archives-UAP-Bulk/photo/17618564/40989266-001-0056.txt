deals with a series of UFO photographs taken by Rex Heflin, a
California highway inspector. He sighted a metallic disk in the
sky above Santa Ana Freeway and took three Polaroid photo-

have left behind when it flew away. The case received wide-
spread publicity, and he was interviewed by civilian UFO invest-

Station took a special interest in the case. Among his many callers
Heflin received a man claiming to be from the North American

subsequently denied that any of its representatives had visited
the UFO witness. The sighting was on August 3, 1965. For-
tunately Heflin had copied the original photographs, but the
mystery of his sighting and the visitor remained unsolved.
