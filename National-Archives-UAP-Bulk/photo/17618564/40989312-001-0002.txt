Because the aircraft industry was expanding rapidly, Athel, through his father's influence
and connections, became a volunteer engineer at Junkers Flugzeugwerke in Germany.
Around 1931, the world famous firm of Junkers made the best commercial planes in the

Junkers used corrugated aluminum for wing skins and fuselages, a design that gave

At this time, Germany was still constrained by the Treaty of Versailles from building

For Athel, his time at Junkers was a major learning experience, but the need for further
