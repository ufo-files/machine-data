RE: FOIA Request No. I-PL-94-00110
Dear Mr Sherburn
This is in response to your Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request of 15 October
1994, for information pertaining to the SDI and/or BMDO program, the Space Station, and
UFO or UAO records. We have assigned your FOIA request case number I-PL-94-00110.
Please refer to this number when corresponding with us regarding your FOIA request.
We are not the controlling agency or records repository for UFO or UAO records.
Accordingly, we referred that portion of your FOIA request, paragraph (4), to the National
Archives, Military Reference Branch, Washington DC 20408; and to the Secretary of the Air
Force/HOR, 170 Luke Avenue, Suite 400, Bolling AFB DC 20332-5133. They will reply
direct to you concerning that portion of your FOIA request.
We are also not the controlling agency or records repository for "Space Station"
records. We referred that portion of your FOIA request, paragraph (3), to the National
Aeronautics and Space Administration, Code PSN, Washington, DC 20546. They will reply
direct to you concerning that portion of your FOIA request.
With regard to the remainder of your FOIA request (paragraphs (1) and 2)), FOIA
directives require that requesters indicate a willingness to pay any assessable FOIA tees
required before we process the request. Your request did not contain such an agreement.
For purposes of fee assessment, we have initially determined your fee category to be
Category 3, All Others. Requesters in this category are provided the first two hours of
search time and the first 100 pages of duplication without charge. If you agree with our
initial fee category determination, your reply to this letter should clearly state your agreement
to pay all assessable FOIA fees in excess of the first two hours of search time and the first
100 pages of duplication. If you wish, you may establish a fee threshold, such as $100.00.
If it appears the assessable FOIA fees for processing your request would exceed that amount,
you would be notified in advance and provided an estimated cost.
