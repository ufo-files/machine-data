Lt. Gen. Hoyt S. Vandenberg, Deputy Commander, U.S. Army Air Forces, 1947.
General Vandenberg served as Chief of Staff, United States Air Force 1947-1953.
He is alleged to have directed the recovery of a flying saucer at Roswell Army Air
Field on July 8, 1947. A review of his official daily activities calendar revealed that
he was involved with a hoax involving a flying saucer recovery on July 7 in Texas.
Records do not support the claim that he had any similar involvement on July 8, as
charged by crashed saucer conspiracy theorists (see Atch 15). U.S. Air Force Photo.
