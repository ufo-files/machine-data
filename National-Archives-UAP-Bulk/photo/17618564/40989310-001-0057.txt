"Debris Lift"
About the same time Glenn Dennis received the first telephone call from the
Roswell AAF mortuary officer, a B-29 bomber--not a C-54 transport, as has
been reported previously-was pulled up in front of the base operations
building and readied for a flight to Fort Worth, on orders from Colonel
Blanchard. Allegedly, at least the senior members of the crew for this special
flight were hand-picked by Blanchard. These are reported to have included
lieutenant colonels Payne Jennings, Roswell AAF and 509th deputy
commander, and Robert I. Barrowclough, base executive officer.[30]
Robert R. Porter, who was the bomber's flight engineer, helped load a
number of very lightweight, paper-wrapped packages delivered to the aircraft
in a staff car. Porter has testified Captain William E. Anderson, a Roswell AAF
engineering officer, said the packages contained parts from a flying saucer.[31]
