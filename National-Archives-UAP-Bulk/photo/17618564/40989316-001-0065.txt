18 July 1947 (Cont'd)
3:20 P.M.
Mr. Symington Called Gen. Vandenberg.
Gen. Van stated that
the budget is the most distressing thing he has come up against
and the next distressing is trying to find out why we are not
getting anywhere on the Alaskan lift. Said he had talked to
General Gaffney and called Gen. Norstad and didn't get much there
apparently Craig's Headquarters is the one that has thessay and
here they put in for eight million for the Ground Force and
four million for Air Fields and that kind of business isn't
going to help a bit when we get unification. We had a conference
for about 3 hours on airplane procurement. We come in for things
that couple of colonels and majors in the back room have agreed
is Air Force policy - but we have a procedure now and will see
what comes out of it. Mr. Symington stated the fewer the models
the better. Mr. Symington stated that Don Burrows, now with
War Assets would be tops and would help General Rawlings an
awful lot, as he did not want"Ed" to drop Cost Accounting for
Budget - that he considered Cost Accounting the most important.
Gen. Van stated that he would like to come out and see Mr. S.
after we get a little further along. Mr. S. said he would be
in San Francisco and the Los Angeles area around the 10th.
3:45 P.M
General Gardner said he had been worrying lest the Navy might
• get gasoline procurement but instead they are now suggesting a
Joint Procurement System. General Gardner thinks it is a very
good idea but wanted to check with General Vandenberg before he
called General Lutes back. General Vandenberg agreed that it is
all right for the interim because the new Secretary of National
Defense will probably have ideas of his own -he might go along
4:00 P.M.
General Booth called to tell General Vandenberg that Mr.
Patterson had resigned effective the 24th and that General Royall
is being appointed Secretary of War effective the 24th. Gen.
Van stated he would give Nr. Symington a call..
4:10 P.M. Gen. Vandenberg called Mr. Royall to extend his congratulations
and best wishes both for himself and General Spaatz who was flying.
Colonel Ordway called re Rio conference, which is to be a
Working Committee composed of State, War and Navy Departments.
Colonel Ordway wanted name of the general officer the AAF is
going to send to Rio. General Vandenberg gave him General Weyland'
State Department people think the conference will be
finished in about 10 days. Colonel Ordway will keep General
Weyland informed.
General Welsh called to say that he is going to have some repair
work done Tues. afternoon, which should take about a couple of hours.
