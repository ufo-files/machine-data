PROCEEDINGS OF THE
AIR FORCE BOARD FOR THE CORRECTION OF MILITARY RECORDS
ROOM 5C-860, THE NATIONAL DEFENSE BUILDING
MASHINGION, D. C.
I certify that the hereinafter recorded proceedings of the Air force Board
for the Correction of Military Records are a true and complete record of the
proceedings of this Board in the case of Brigadier General Willian Mitchell,
0-63 (Deceased), Docket Number 56-290.
The applicant, William Mitchell, Jr., requests that the proceedings, finäings,
and sentence of the general court-martial of his father, Brigaaler General
William Mitchell, be declared null and void, the said William Mitchell having
been found guilty on December 18, 1925 of violating the 96th Article of War
and having been sentenced thereon on the 25th day of January 1926.
The Air Force Board for the Correction of Military Records met in Room Number
5C-883, The National Defense Building at 9:45 A.M., on Tuesday 14 May 1957,
pursuant to the call of the Chairman.
Those present were:
Mr. George S. Robinmon, Chairman
Mr. Joseph F. Cunningham, Member
Mr. Ralph P. Dunn, Member
Mr. John J. Mclaughlin, Member
Mr. Herbert R. Wildman, Member
Mr. Frank D. Hardin, Executive Secretary
Mr. Norman A. Compton, Examiner
The applicant, William Mitchell, Jr., appeared before the Board. Appearing
on behalf of the applicant vere Mr. Stephen F. Leo, Mr. Willian R. Sweeney,
and Mr. Edmund F. Hogan, all of the Air Force Association, said organization
having been designated by the applicant to act as counsel in his case.
