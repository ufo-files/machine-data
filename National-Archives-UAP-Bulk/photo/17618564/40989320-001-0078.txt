SOUND DATA - OCTOBER 1946
The technique of analysis of the sound propagation field described
in Sections II and III above has been applied to data recorded by the
Signal Corps during the Army-Havy Munitions Board Tests at Arco, Idaho,
October 1946. The details of analysis and the computations are too
lengthy to present in this Appendix; however, an outstaading recording
was secured at Green River, Wyoming, at a distance oi 331 KM from Arco
on 16 October 1940, which together with meteorological sounding data to a
height of 27 KM, enabled an analysis to be made of the upper atmosphere
leading to significant conclusions on long distance propagation of sound.
A brief discussion of the salient features of this analysis follows.
