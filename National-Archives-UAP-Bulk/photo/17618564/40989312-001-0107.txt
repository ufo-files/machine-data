The Move to Middleburg
Athelstan had grown weary of big city life and a heavy social schedule. The time in Palm
Beach and Washington had begun to grate on him, so he moved to the Virginia country-
side where he lives today.

one!' I said, 'Where?' 'Middleburg, Virginia, of course,' he replied. "that's where I
live.'
