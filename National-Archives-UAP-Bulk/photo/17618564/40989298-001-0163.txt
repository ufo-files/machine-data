CONCLUSION
Many of the claims surrounding the events of July 1947 could be neither proved nor
disproved. Attempts were not made to investigate every allegation, but rather to start
with what was known and work toward the unknown. To complicate the situation,
events described here took place nearly 50 years ago and were highly classified. This
Top Secret project appeared to have utilized the concept of compartmentalization very
well. Interviews with individuals and review of documents of organizations revealed
that the ultimate objective of the work, or even the name of the project, in many
instances was not known. It was unlikely, therefore, that personnel from Roswell AAF,
even though they possessed the appropriate clearances, would have known about project
MOGUL. In fact, when the NYU/ AMC group returned to Alamogordo in September, their
