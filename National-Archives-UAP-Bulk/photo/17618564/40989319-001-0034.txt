determined by the acoustic constructio of the microphone. The spun

The high frequency cutoff is determined by the hole in the acoustic

A type 955 tube is connected to two parallel lines in a modi-

small plates which are spaced .005 to .008 inches from the microphone
deaphragm so as to give a nominal capacitance of 15 umfd. measured
across the two lines. Since the motic of the diaphragm varies the
capacity across the ends of the line, frequency modulation is produced.
The antenna, approximately one-half wave length, is attached
directly to the plate lead of the vacuum tube.
