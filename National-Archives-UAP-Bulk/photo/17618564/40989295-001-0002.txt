The information contained in this volume is drawn primarily from

chapter. However, these materials were supplemented, or simply clarified
in some cases, through personal consultation with a great many individuals.

Track Test Division, headed by Lieutenant Colonel Donald H. Vicek, gave
invaluable assistance at every stage of the preparation of the manuscript.

needed and answering questions on all aspects of track-test operations;

the entire first chapter was read in draft form by several members of the
Track Test Division, and many of their suggestions are incorporated in the
final version.

supplied part of the information contained in it, is Mr. Jeremiah I. Foley,
until recently Chief of the Engineering Division, Deputy Chief of Staff/
Installations. Then, too, background information on other track-type

the Naval Weapons Laboratory (formerly Naval Proving Ground), Dahlgren,
Virginia; and Mr. Lester G. Garman and Mr. J. D. DeSanto of the Naval
Ordnance Test Station, China Lake, California.
