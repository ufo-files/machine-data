United Press Association Broadcast Wire
Transmissions, July 8, 1947
The news wires carried numerous stories and other communications about
Roswell on the afternoon and early evening of July 8, 1947. This appendix
presents facsimiles of a number of such transmissions which appeared on the
United Press broadcast wire. Among other things, they suggest it is likely
Roswell AAF did not distribute a written press release on the Brazel discovery.
