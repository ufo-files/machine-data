The first balloon launches associated with Project MOGUL were carried out at several
locations on the east coast of the United Statesạồ However, unfavorable winds, conflicts
with commercial air traffic, and the need to gather data on the V-2 flights currently

further tests from Alamogordo AAF. The NYU group would make three "field trips'

III. The majority of the balloon flights over the next four years originated from
Alamogordo AAF.
