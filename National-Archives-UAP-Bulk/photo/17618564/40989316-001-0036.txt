30 June 1947
9:00 A.M. Arrive Office.
9:05 A.M. Gen. Chauncey with mail.
9:30 A.M. Gen. Burns - on phone - advised that they have tried twice to
promote It. Col. Kern, commanding officer up in the northeast.
There is no way in which we can get him promoted unless General
Spaatz takes it up with General Eisenhower. General Anderson
tried in March and Gan. Burns has a memorandum pointing out it
was given exceptional consideration.
9:40 A.M. Major White advised Colonel Burdette that General Vandenberg
wants panels removed and room painted. Wants a globe and a
polar projection map. Move General Spaatz' picture to the wider
wall, preferably the end of the room.
10:30 A.M. To Gen. Spaatz with mail.
