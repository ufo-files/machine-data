3.1 Choice of Instrument
The type of instrument we use is the Honeywell Alpha Hygrometer (developed
under Contract AF-19(604)-8418). The latest version is shown diagramatically in
Figure 10. It operates as an automatic frost-point (or dew-point) hygrometer;
that is, the amount of frost deposited on a cooled surface is measured, and the
deviation from some preset amount of deposit is used as an error signal in a
closed servo loop. In this way, the instrument maintains the temperature of the
surface at the point where the quantity of water on the surface remains constant-
evaporation is balanced by condensation--and the surface temperature is thus at
the frost or dew point.
