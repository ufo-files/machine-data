4. SUMMARY
This report concerns two of the programs in which we are engaged that involve
balloon-borne sampling probes. For this particular work which, of necessity,
requires sampling large volumes of air within discrete strata of our stratosphere,
we have found the high-altitude balloon to be an indispensable carrier vehicle.
