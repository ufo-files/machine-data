The Nuns
Here we may have solld documentary evidence of a sighting of something the
night of July 4. According to Randle and Schmitt, Franciscan nuns at
Roswell's Saint Mary's Hospital left a record of seeing a "fiery object on July 4,
1947, sometime between 11:15 and 11:30 p.m" in their hospital log, the
entry stating the object was "arcing downward north of Roswell" (emphasis
added).[17]
According to Schmitt, these nuns were "avid astronomers" and
routinely made observations of the night sky.[18]
To date, the actual log entry has not been made public. If and when it
