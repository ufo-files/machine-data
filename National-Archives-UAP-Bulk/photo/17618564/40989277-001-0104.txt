MEMORANDUM FOR SAF/AAZ
FROM: SAF/AAZD
SUBJECT: COMPROMISE OF ROSWELL BOOK AND VIDEO
At approximately 1430 on 19 June, I received a call from a person identifying himself as
Mr Mark Brender, "the ABC News Pentagon Correspondent." Mr Brender read to me a memo
he had written which contained accurate and extremely detailed information from the new
Roswell book or video. It is my opinion that Mr Brender has in his possession a copy of the
book, video, or both. Therefore, I believe that the book and video should be considered to be
compromised in their entirety.
I did not comment in any way to Mr Brender read to me and informed SAF/PAM, Ms
Cale, immediately.
