MEMORANDUM FOR SAF/AAZ (ATTN: LTCOL PATTERSON)
SUBJECT: Status Report on Supplemental Roswell Research
The purpose of this additional project was to tie together the mythology
surrounding this event as it relates to one or more discrete Air Force activities in the
region which could be interpreted to lend credence to the Roswell story.
The issues to be resolved were:
1.) the bodies
2.) the spacecraft
3.) the presence of Air Force personnel on recovery
operations in the vicinity of the "crash".
4. an explanation of coffins and body bags
observed in connection with Air Force
activities. Additionally, explanation of an
autopsy allegedly performed at Walker AFB,
NM, and observed by a local mortician, needed
to be confirmed.
5.) establishment of a time frame for each and all of
the various explanations, all of which must be
supported by documentation (including
interviews, where obtainable).
These issues were not fully accounted for in the initial response to the GAO
inquiry, principally because they could not be linked to the July 1947 time frame. Now we
are in a position to identify various projects, most never classified, which were
misinterpreted by unsophisticated observers and became part of the UFO mythology.
Release of this information in a complete and finished format will be a public relations
benefit and provide a definitive FOIA response for future inquiries into the issue.
PROGRESS TO DATE:
1. A solid explanation for the "bodies" has been identified. The information
gathered has been written up in essentially final form. The figures, graphics and
supporting data are also essentially in final form.
2. The "craft"' may also be explained as one or more research vehicles, depending
on the time frame chosen. The most prominent candidates have been identified and their
supporting information is also in final form.
3. The various Air Force activities in the vicinity of Roswell AAF and Walker
AFB have also been researched, identified and written descriptions, along with extensive
graphic support, have been obtained and are incorporated in the final report.
