SUBJECT: Recovery Report - Flight H71-48
19 October 1971
1. Upon orders from the Control Center Coordinator the Recovery party
departed Bldg 850 for Alamogordo NM at 1250 hrs, 14 Oct 71. At 1309 hrs
recovery personnel observed termination and balloon destruct. Shortly
after termination, Control Center advised payload WouLD impact in the
Sacramento Mountains south of Cloudcroft. Aircraft advised to proceed
to High Rolls, N and proceed south on West Side Rd approximately 10 miles.
Atapproximately 1415 hours recovery personnel arrived at the payload
which had impacted approximately 1000' down a steep ridge. A cursory
evaluation indicated no major damage to the system. Due to the nature of
the terrain and distance from the road it was possible to recover only
the cameras, instrumentation packages, TM package, and parachute. The
balloon and remainder of the payload have yet to be recovered.
2. The recovery party departed the impact site at approximately 1800 hrs
and arrived at Bldg 850 at 1900 hrs.
