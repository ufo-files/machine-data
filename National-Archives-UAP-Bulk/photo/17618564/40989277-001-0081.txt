DEPARTMENT OF THE AIR FORCE
WASHINGTON DC 20330-1000
OFFICE OF THE SECRETARY
27 March 1996
MEMORANDUM FOR SAF/AAZ
FROM: SAF/AAZD
SUBJECT: Progress Report - Request from Maj Gen Minnihan
Based on the letter from Neil A. Coddington, Lt Col, USAF (Ret) the following research
was conducted.
• The organizational history of the unit identified in the orders provided by Lt Col
Coddington has been reviewed. It does not contain much useful information about the unit.
• The two other Air Force members identified in the orders personnel files have been
located and reviewed at the National Personnel Records Center in ST Louis, MO.
• The two other Air Force members are both retired from the Air Force and their current
addresses are available.
• A former AF/IN historian has been contacted and has provided useful information. He
is contacting another former historian for additional information.
Interim Findings
It appears that Lt Col Coddington was interviewed by the CIA for a position that required
someone with his AFSC (Airborne Radio Repairman), although the exact nature of the
assignment is unclear. One of the other radio repairmen listed on the orders was assigned to a
Special Activities Squadron in Las Vegas, NV one year later. Speculation is that due to the
location and AFSC this person was involved in the development of the SR-71 aircraft, which was
a CIA program at that time. Hopefully the other historian can assist in this area.
JAMES McANDREW, Capt, USAFR
Declass and Review Officer
