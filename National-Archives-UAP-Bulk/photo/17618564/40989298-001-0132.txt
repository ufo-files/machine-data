This 15-foot polyethylene balloon (upper left) and 70-foot polyethylene balloon (upper
right) are representative of the type used extensively by Project MoGUL. It is this variety of
balloon that caused many UFO sightings due to their flat, spherical appearance when viewed
from the ground.
Lying on the desert floor near Roswell, NM, in July 1948 (upper right) is a Project MoGUL
balloon. Due to the prevailing westerlies, MOGUL balloons often descended in the vicinity of
Roswell after launch from Alamogordo. The unpredictability and hazards to aircraft presented
by the balloons prompted the Civil Aviation Administration (now the Federal Aviation
Administration) to conduct a hearing regarding this issue in August 1947 (see App 13, pp.
4З-44).
A blimp hangar at Lakehurst Naval Air Station, NJ (lower left), contains this Project
MOGUL balloon during its preparation for flight.
