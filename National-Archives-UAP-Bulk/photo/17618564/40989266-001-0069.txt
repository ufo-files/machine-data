Venus viewed from the Earth is the brightest planet when it
comes to within 25 million miles. It is of similar size and mass
to our Earth but the similarity ends there. Its atmospheric
pressure at the surface is about 90 times that of Earth, and this

better than at twilight on Earth, and the Venusian surroundings
would be bathed in a deep red glow.
A human being would be crushed to death by the enormous
pressure and fried to a crisp by the temperature in this huge

are known types of organic molecules that would be at home in
the Venusian environment, so it would be foolish to dismiss the
possibility of life entirely.
