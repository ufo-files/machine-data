SUBJECT: REQUESTED INFORMATION
Attached are the SILVERBUG and Roswell reports. The SIVERBUG project became the
AvroCar, an experimental aircraft developed in about 1960 jointly by the Air Force and Army
with Avro Aircraft Limited of Canada. An experimental AvroCar is in the collection of the Air
& Space Museum and the Army Transportation Museum at Ft Ustes, VA.
Norm Boney of the 88th Communications Squadron (Wright-Pat) Records Staging Area
has the R& D records of the AvroCar project, accession # 342-75-076, boxes 67-69, 87-88, and
342-75-151, box 66. Let me know if you need anything else.
