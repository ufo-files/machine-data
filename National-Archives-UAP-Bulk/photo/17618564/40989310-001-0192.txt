Roswell Witness Affidavits
Thirty-two witness affidavits are on file with the Fund for UFO Research.
These were obtained through the efforts of Don Berliner, Stanton T. Friedman,
(BarbaraDugger, John Kromschroeder, and Phyllis McGuire) did not respond
to requests to include their statements in this report. Three statements have
to do with the alleged crash of a flying saucer on the Plains of San Agustin,
which is excluded from consideration in this report. Another statement is
being withheld from release pending review of additional documentation and
other information.
Included with Glenn Dennis's affidavit are drawings of alleged alien
bodies made by artist Walter Henn, who worked from Dennis's recollections of
now lost sketches made by his friend "Miss X.," the Army nurse who told
Dennis she helped doctors examine the bodies at the Roswell AAF hospital.
Doctor Jesse Marcel's sketch based on his recollections of one of the "I
beams" found by his father on the Foster Ranch debris field is included with
hisaffidavit.
The Jim Ragsdale affidavit is reproduced in the redacted form in which
it was provided to me by Kevin Randle and Don Schmitt. The David Wagnon
affidavit has been redacted to keep confidential information about Miss X
which may be critical to locating her or determining her fate and the
usefulness of which would be undermined by premature public disclosure.
