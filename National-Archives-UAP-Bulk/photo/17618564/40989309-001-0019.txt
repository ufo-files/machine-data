HIGHLIGHTS OF CONVERSATION WITH LOIS WALKER
AMC HISTORIAN (DSN 787-3438) 29 MAR 94
- USAF HISTORIAN SINCE 1982
- HAS SECOND HAND INFO THAT SOMETHING WAS MOVED TO WRIGHT-PAT
FROM CARSWELL IN TIME FRAME. SHE HAS BEEN TOLD BY PERSONS
UNKNOWN THAT THERE ARE RECORDS TO DOCUMENT THIS STILL IN
EXISTENCE.

LAB, AEROPROPULSION AND POWER DIRECTORATE, INFAMOUS "BLDG. 18".
- BUILDING 18, ACTUALLY MANY BUILDINGS, CONTAINS COLD LAB AND
OTHER TESTING EQUIPMENT ETC.

HAVE BEEN BROUGHT TO AEROSPACE MEDICAL RESEARCH LAB, A DIVISION
OF THE ARMSTRONG LAB.

AIR FORCE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, CLAIMS FATHER WAS ON FLIGHT
AND WITNESSED LOADING OF SEVERAL TALL METALLIC CANISTERS.
