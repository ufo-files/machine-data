Author's Sketches and Notes from May 17,
1993, Interview with Frank J. Kaufmann
On May 17, 1993, 1 interviewed Frank J. Kaufmann about his alleged role in
the Roswell Incident. During the interview, Kaufmann made a number of
sketches-of the alien beings he said he saw at the crash site, their craft and
its features, etc.--and showed me a strange envelope which contained a folded
paper representation of the alien craft. (Kaufmann's sketch of the craft and
the bit of origami in no way resemble each other.) During the interview, I
copied Kaufmann's sketches in my notebook. Afterward, I made annotated
drawings of the paper craft and envelope from memory on a facing page
Facsimiles of these "masterworks" are reproduced in this appendix.
