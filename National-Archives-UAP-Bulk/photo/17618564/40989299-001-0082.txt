AM-l Transmitter
Frequency ranges from 1.5 to 9mc can be employed with the AM-1.
The modulation of the AM-l is effected by use of a triode modulator
(2-345) tubes) connected in series with the plate supply of a class
"C" RF amplifier.
Variation of the plate supply voltage of the
RF amplifier caused by change in tube resistance gives amplitude
modulation linear with plate voltage of the amplifier. By use
of this system modulation from DC to several hundred cycles is
obtained.
