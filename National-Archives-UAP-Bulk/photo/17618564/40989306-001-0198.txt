7. INITIAL PROCEDURE.
Take care in unpacking and handling this equipment. Check the
items given in the parts list, (paragraph 39).
8. INSTALLATION.
Assemble the recording equipment as illustrated in figure 1, using
the wing nuts provided for the purpose. If the central station is a
truck or a trailer, bolt the oscillograph assembly to the floor of the
vehicle. If the central station is to be a casemate, a dugout, or a
laboratory, bolt the recording equipment assembly to a planking.
This planking either should be sufficiently wide, or should be se-
cured to the flooring, to prevent the assembly from tipping over.
Protect the recording equipment assembly from dust by Cover
BG-74 (fig. 6), when not in use.
