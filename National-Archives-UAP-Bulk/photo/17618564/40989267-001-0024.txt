3. SUMMARY
In conclusion, the results of my investigation have been: The work of the
University of Minnesota has been verified, although there was never any doubt as to
the validity of their work. The sigma tables have been extended to cover most
balloon designs. Various shapes have been studied and ways to reduce weight have
been investigated; some methods of reducing their weight have been shown here.
Certain knowledge of the stresses in the balloons has been determined. Finally, as
a result of my work in calculating the shapes, I believe that special designs should
be investigated carefully using computer analysis rather than by reliance on sigma
tables alone.
