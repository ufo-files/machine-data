The Who of It
The conventional wisdom has it that Brazel's family was not with him on the
ranch the night of the crash and when he made his surprising find the next
day. It has been reported he was accompanied on his morning ride of
discovery by a seven-year-old neighbor, William D. ("Dee) Proctor.
According to Dee's mother, Loretta Proctor, Dee often spent time with
Brazel, staying over for a day or two, riding and helping with chores. Mrs.
Proctor told me that the day Mac showed some of the debris to her husband
Floyd and her she thinks he was also bringing Dee home from one of these
visits, although she is not certain of this. She added Dee does not remember
anything about the debris or related events. To date, my repeated attempts to
interview Dee Proctor have been unavailing.(9]
However, I have interviewed Mac Brazel's daughter, Bessie Brazel
