DEPARTMENT OF THE AIR FORCE
HEADQUARTERS UNITED STATES AIR FORCE
WASHINGTON
SUBJECT: Funeral Service
1 June 1966
TO: ALL PERSONNEL, HEADQUARTERS USAF
Funeral service for the late General William H. Blanchard, FR 1445,
USAF, who died 31 May 1966, will be held on Friday, 3 June 1966, at 1000
hours, at Andrews Air Force Base Chapel.
Interment, with full military
honors, will follow in the Air Force Academy Cemetery on Friday, 3 June
1966, at 1400 hours, Mountain Daylight Time.
2. General Blanchard was born on 6 February 1916, in Boston, Massachu-
setts. He was graduated from the United States Military Academy and
commissioned a Second Lieutenant on 14 June 1938. He attended Primary and
Basic Flying School at Randolph Field, Texas, in 1938. After graduation
from Advanced Flying School in August 1939, at Kelly Field, Texas, he
served for two years in various positions with the Flying Training Command.
During World War II, he served with the 20th Air Force in the Asiatic-
Pacific Theater of Operations and participated in 20 combat missions.
After the war, and until October 1961, he served in various command and
staff positions with the Strategic Air Command. After 15 years of contin-
uous service in the Strategic Air Command, he was appointed The Inspector
General, United States Air Force, and was promoted to the grade of
Lieutenant General. In August 1963, he was appointed Deputy Chief of
Staff, Programs and Requirements, HQ USAF, and in February 1964, assumed
the duty as Deputy Chief of Staff, Plans and Operations, HQ USAF. Later
that year, he was assigned the additional duty as Senior Air Force Member,
Military Staff Committee of the United Nations. On 19 February 1965, he
was appointed Vice Chief of Staff of the United States Air Force, and
promoted to the grade of General.
He was serving in this position at the
time of his death.
3. For his military service, he was awarded the Distinguished Service
Medal, Silver Star, Legion of Merit with two OLC, Distinguished Flying
Cross with one OIC, Bronze Star Medal, Air Medal with one OIC, Commendation
Ribbon, Distinguished Unit Badge, American Defense Service Medal, American
Campaign Medal, Asiatic-Pacific Campaign Medal with six Bronze Stars,
World War II Victory Medal, National Defense Service Medal, Air Force
Longevity Service Award with one Silver and one Bronze OIC, Philippine
Liberation Medal and Philippine Independence Ribbon.
