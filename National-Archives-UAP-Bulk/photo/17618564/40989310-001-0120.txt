Whenever it was that Mac Brazel moseyed into Sheriff Wilcox's office, soon
thereafter he led Major Jesse Marcel and another Army officer, probably
Counter Intelligence Corps Captain Sheridan Cavitt, out to his ranch They
arrived too late to go directly to the debris field, so they stayed overnight in an

Certain significant elements of this part of the Roswell Incident story as
incorporated into the conventional wisdom are questionable or in direct
conilict with Marcel's first-hand account.

and Cavitt examined this material, which allegedly included the "largest chunk

radiation. (24]
