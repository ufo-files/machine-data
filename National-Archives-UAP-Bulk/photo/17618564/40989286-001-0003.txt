MONDAY, JUNE 3, 1957
Start of Record 18-mile Ascent
Back on earth after his 12-hour journey to record
heights, Capt. Joe W. Kittinger has a smile for his ground
crew. From portholes in his capsule, Kittinger said the
view was magnificent.
"I could look down and see all
the way from Minneapolis to the cast side of Lake Michi-
gan," he explained. He was at top altitude (96,000 feet)
for an hour and 50 minutes.
Purpose of the flight was to
gather scientific information.
