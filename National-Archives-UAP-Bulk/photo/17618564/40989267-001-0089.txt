make better observations than can be made on earth, it is obvious that one must

"In the early stages

when more difficult experiments are attempted, a trained human observer brings to
the supervision of these experiments the ability to deal with unforeseen difficulty

A.D. StruDIe, Jr
Sea-Space Svstems, inc
Abstract
Sea-Space Systems has developed a small balloon which can achieve altitudes
up to 150,000 ft, carrying survey instrument payloads. This balloon utilizes the

performance and cold temperature accommodation. Operational objectives of this
survey balloon include launching ease along with reasonable cost and good reliability.
The new concept in launching is directed towards one man operation in elevated
winds. Recent flight experiences with the balloon and unique launching technique
have indicated very satisfactory results.
