Returned.
Col. Garland - re letter for signature of General Spaatz on
of National Airport by army aircraft and use of Andrews
Field by civilian aircraft during instrument weather conditions.
Admiral Land of the Air Transport Association on phone re
Andrews Field in bad weather. Admiral Land suggested that
someone go over the details with General Milt Arnold. General
Vandenberg suggested that Duckworth do this.
Mr. Schott on phone. General Vandenberg requested appointment
with the Secretary of War. Advised Secretary of War had no
time this afternoon.
Colonel Gill Ervin - re National Guard matters as presented to
the Secretary of war.
General Rawlings, General Chauncey and Mr. Zuckert re additional
money for procurement.
General Vandenberg and General Rawlings went to see the
Chief of Staff and discussed the information for the President
on the purchase of aircraft if we were allowed another
$100,000,000. Then went to the Secretary of War. Mr. Royall
was also present. They concurred and we then prepared a
letter for the Secretary's signature to the President along
those lines.
General Stearley in reference to hearings on unification.
Said Congressman Dorn did a splendid job. Admiral Zacharias
antagonized most of the committee. The score stands pretty
much as before - that is 19 for and 6 against. Mr. Symington
is to be called and asked to talk with Senator Wardsworth in
attempt to get hearings over next week.
Colonel Merrick calling with reference to conference to be
held in National Guard Bureau. They are getting together
all of the Adjutant Generals of the various states on Monday
and most of them are ground force officers. He suggested that
someone take up the matter of Air Force National Guard units.
New York, Pa.,Ill., Lass. & Nev. are Air Force. The rest are
ground forces. General Vandenberg was of the opinion that
this is politics and officers should stay out of it. However,
did say he would look into it. Colonel Merrick to come
Tuesday and tell General Vandenberg how the matter stands
in the Senate.
