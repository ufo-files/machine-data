The Crate Lift
Robert Slusher has provided quite credible testimony about his participation
in an unusual B-29 flight on July 9, 1947. 'Tim," another crew member on
that flight, has given researcher Leonard Stringfield similar testimony and a
diary entry about the trip. According to Slusher and Tim, their aircraft
carried a large, unmarked crate under armed guard, and when they arrived in
Fort Worth, the party which greeted them included a mortician They also
said the crew was told the flight, which "made history," was not to be talked
about 139]
Official documentation of such a flight has not yet been located As I
noted above, Tim's story includes some doubtful allegations of intimidation
directed at him, and efforts to locate and interview others involved have yet to
bear fruit. However, I have talked with Slusher a number of times, both on the
in person and on the telephone, and find him quite credible. What I have
learned about him from others supports this impression. Thus, I believe it is
reasonabie to accept this story as a working assumption which suggests the
Army Air Force had bodies of some sort the existence and disposition of which
it wanted to keep under wraps.
