XVII Scientific Aspects of Stargazer
William C. White
U. S. Naval Ordnance Test Station
China Lake, California
Abstract
The manned Stargazer balloon flight (13 December 1962) and its experimental
payload are described. The experiments were developed on the basis of establish-
ing the feasibility of astronomical research from manned balloon-borne vehicles.
The developments in testing of the instruments to carry out the experiments are
discussed. The experience in handling the scientific equipment during the above
flight is described.
