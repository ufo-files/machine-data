Figure 43. Theodolite Mount ML-180.
Status: Standard. Stock No.: 7A1750. Refer-
ence: TM 11-423.
Theodolite Mount ML-180 is a metal, adjust-
able-height support for Theodolites ML-47-(*)
and ML-247. It is designed for permanent in-
stallation on an observation platform.
The mount
consists of two vertical pipes, one of which tele-
scopes within the other and can be raised or
lowered by means of a wheel. The bottom of the
outer pipe is screwed into a mounting base; the
top of the inner pipe carries a plate with a screw-
threaded tripod head for attaching the theodolite.
The equipment includes a metal hood which fits
over and protects the instrument, and a data
shelf with a lamp. Packed for export, Theodolite
Mount ML-180 weighs 19 pounds.
