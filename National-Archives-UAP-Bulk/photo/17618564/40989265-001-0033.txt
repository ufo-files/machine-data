Tethered Aerostat Radar System (TARS), formerly called
Seek Skyhook, being used as a platform to hoist a 1,000 lb.
radar payload aloft which provides low-altitude surveillance
at certain locations. The system is designed to hoist its pay-
load to an altitude of 10,000-12,000 feet and maintain a sta-
ble platform for extended periods of time. The 250,000
cubic-foot aerostat is about 175 feet long, 60 feet across the
hull and has a tip-to-tail dimension of around 80 feet.
Time aloft is only limited by the fuel onboard and weather.
The system uses less than one gallon of fuel an hour and is sta-
ble in all but very severe weather. TARS out-performs any
ground-based station by virtue of its performance at the higher
altitude.
