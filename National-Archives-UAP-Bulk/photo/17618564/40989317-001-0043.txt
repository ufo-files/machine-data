pretation, was undertaken by the Army Air Forces School of Applied

the 62ll Unit moved into the Pacific Theater, a photographically equipped
B-25 aircraft was assigned to it for experimental work in connection
with the procurement of oceanographic and beach data from the Japanese
coastal areas.

three reports.

"Study and Recommendations for an Army Forces Beach Intelligence

"The Procurement, Training and Organization of the 6211 Beach
