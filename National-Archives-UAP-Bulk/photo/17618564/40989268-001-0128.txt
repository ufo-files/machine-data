From the point of launch to maximum altitude, the concentration of water vapor
experienced by a balloon-borne hygrometer varies by a factor of 105. Adsorption
of water by the balloon and associated equipment at low altitudes and a subsequent
desorption in the stratosphere, particularly under the influence of solar radiation,
can introduce serious errors in the measurement of low frost points.
The effect of
such contamination can be seen (Figure 7) to be quite large at float and consider-
