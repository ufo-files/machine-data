PERSONAL FACT SHEET
A. Personal data.
Born February 6, 1916, Boston, Mass.; father - Dr. W. H.
Blanchard (deceased); mother - Mrs. Walter J. Herzfeld, Miami Shores,
2. Married in October 1950; wife - Anne Hutt Blanchard; children
- Donald H. Blanchard; children by a previous marriage - son, William H.
Blanchard II, daughter, Dale Blanchard Brown.
3. Hometown address: 160 N.E. 95th Street, Miami Shores, Fla.
B. Education.
1. Graduate Chelsea, Massachusetts High School, 1933.
2. Graduate Phillips Exeter Academy, 1934.
3. Graduate U.S. Military Academy, 1938.
4. Primary and Basic Flying School, Randolph Field, Texas, 1939.
5. Advanced Flying School, Kelly Field, Texas, 1939.
C. Service.
1. July 1934 - June 1938 Cadet, U.S. Military Academy.
2. June 1938 - Aug 1939 Primary and Basic Flying School,
Randolph Field, Tex.; and Advanced Flying School, Kelly Field, Tex.
3. Aug 1939 - Sept 1941 Flying Instructor, Echelon Commander
and Public Relations Officer, Kelly Field, Tex.
4. Sept 1941 - Apr 1942 Commander, Twin Engine Training Group,
Ellington Field, Tex.
5. Apr 1942 - Nov 1943 Chief, Advanced and Advanced Transition
Pilot Training Section, Flying Training Command, Fort Worth, Tex.
6. Nov 1943 - Mar 1944 Assistant to and Assistant Chief of
Staff A-3, Executive Officer and Deputy Wing Commander, 58th Bombardment
Wing (VH), XX Bomber Command, 2nd Air Force, Salina, Kan.
7. Mar 1944 - Aug 1944 Executive Officer and Deputy Wing
Commander, 58th Bombardment Wing, XX Bomber Command, 20th Air Force,
CBI Theater.
Aug 1944 - Feb 1945 Commander, 40th Bomb Group, XX Bomber
Command, 20th Air Force, CBI Theater.
9. Feb 1945 - Sept 1945 Assistant Chief of Staff A-3, Head-
quarters XXI Bomber Command, 20th Air Force, Guam.
10. Sept 1945 - Jan 1946 Special Assistant to Chief, Aircraft
Project Section, Wright Field, Ohio.
11. Jan 1946 - June 1948 Commander, 509th Bomb Wing, Operation
Crossroads, Kwajalein, Marshall Islands, and later, Commander, 509th
Composite Group and Commander, 509th Bomb Wing, Roswell AAF, N. Mex.
