PERSONAL JOURNAL OF ALBERT P. CRARY
Albert P Crary, Project MOGUL Field Operations Director, maintained a journal of his
activities throughout his career. The following transcription was made personally by A.P.
Crary before his death. The periods which have been transcribed are:
April 2, 1946 - May 8, 1946
December 2, 1946 - August 16, 1947
Pages 13 through 16 contain information concerning the Alamogordo I and Alamogordo
II field trips during June and July of 1947. Following the transcription is the complete
untranscribed journal from April 1946, when Crary began working for Watson
Laboratories until the end of the journal, February 1948.
