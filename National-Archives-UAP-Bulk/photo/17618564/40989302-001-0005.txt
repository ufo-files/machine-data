23 OCT 1958
MEMORANDUM FOR ADMIVISTRATIVE ASSISTANT, OSAF
SUBJECT: Request for Permission to Examine Court-Martial Records
of Late Brigadier General William L. Mitchell, USA
1.
In accordance with the telephone conversation which I had
on 22 October with Mr. Curran, I am forwarding to you the communication
from Midshipman Mitchell L. Lathrop, First Class, USN, forwarded through
Naval channels to the Chief of Staff, USAF. The communication from
Midshipman lathrop requests authority to examine the record of trial
and allied papers in the court-martial trial of the late Brigadier
General William Lendrum Mitchell, USA. Naval authorities recommend
that this permission be granted because Midshipman Lathrop wishes
to inspect these records for the purpose of preparing a term paper.
2.
It would appear that the record of trial and allied papers
actually belong to the Department of the Army. They were loaned to
the Air Force solely for consideration by the Board for Correction of
Military Records. It would appear that the most desirable procedure
here would be to return the records to the Department of the Army and
then endorse the correspondence to the Department of the Army for
appropriate action with proper notification to Midshipman lathrop.
3. This correspondence is forwarded to you because your office
and not the Office of The Judge Advocate General has temporary custody
of these records and files at the present time.
