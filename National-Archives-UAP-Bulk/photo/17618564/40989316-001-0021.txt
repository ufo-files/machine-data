June 19. 1947
8:55 A.M. Arrive office.
9:00 A.M. General Chauncey cleared the papers.
9:30 A.M. General Partridge and Colonel MacCloskey re Air Reserve.
10:50 A.M. General Lindsey
11:00 A.M. General Norstad - Colonel Swofford -- re specific planning for
the implementation of the National Defense Act, if and when the
law is passed.
11:15 A.M.
Prince Firouz (Maj.Gen.) Inspecting General of the Iranian Alr
Force. He is a guest of the War Department and is visiting Staff
Agencies.
11:25 A.M. General Chauncey
12:00
Colonel Garland in with papers re flight to Germany.
12:10 P.M.
Mr. Zuckert
12:20 P.M.
General Cannon - General Vandenberg called General Cannon with
reference to going to St. Louis to see Mr. Symington either
Friday or Monday with reference to cost reports concerning
Barksdale, Mather and Williams Fields. Mr. Symington has
concluded from various analyses of figures furnished him that
there is wide discrepancy in reports. (General Cannon called
back. Colonel Swofford took the message that General Cannon
will see Mr. Symington at 1430 Monday June 23rd. General
Sims was advised.)
12:40 P.M. Lunch
2:00 P.M. General Sam Anderson on phone re briefing for General Eisenhower
on "Spearhead" after which General Vandenberg to be briefed on
Inter-American Military Cooperation Bill.
2:10 P.M.
Departed for city.
3:55 P.M. Return
4:00 P.M.
General Anderson on phone -- to be in General Vandenberg's office
at 9:00 A.M. Monday for briefing mentioned above.
4:10 P.M.
General Stearley on phone asking General Vandenberg to see Congressman
Dorn for about thirty minutes.
