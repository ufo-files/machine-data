UFOS & PROJECT BLUE BOOK

mination of Project Blue Book, the Air Force program for the investigation of

Blue Book. Of these, 701 remained "unidentified."
The project was headquartered at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, whose
personnel no longer receive, document or investigate UFO reports.
The decision to discontinue UFO investigations was based on an evaluation
of a report prepared by the University of Colorado entitled,
"Scientific
Study of Unidentified Flying Objects"; a review of the University of Colora-
do's report by the National Academy of Sciences; past UFO studies and Air
Force experience investigating UFO reports during the '40s, '508, and '608.

from investigating UFO reports since 1948, the conclusions of Project Blue
Book are: (1) no UFO reported, investigated, and evaluated by the Air Force
