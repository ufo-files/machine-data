commercial aircraft that are on authorized flight plans. Not too

identify its type, markings, and number. To date, all unknowns

safety, including ground safety, flight safety, and missile safety.

well trained by the Air Force and in civilian universities, is head-
quartered at SAGE.

the units with the hope of spotting a potential hazard and eliminating
