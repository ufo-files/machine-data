SIALEMENE
A large fiery white ball apparently 6 - 8 feet in diameter with a
trailing tail travelling from southwest to northeast appeared in tha
sky low enough to be clearly visible from the driver's seat of my
automoulle, which 1 was driving on Shaw AFB, at approximataly 0145 -
0200 on 9 March 57.
I watched it for possibly 5 - 6 secools, during which it travelled at
a rapid speed on an almost leval course then disintegrated suddenly
and as it did so the white light diaappeared from the fragments almost
immediately and nothing more was seen.

stated it appeared to be 6 - 8 feet in diameter. The night was clear.
1 March 1957
