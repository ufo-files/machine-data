On the celestial program for October
there is an eclipse of the sun, but almost the
only people to see it will be the members

making observations in connection with the
current international Geophysical Year.
An eclipse of the sun occurs when the
moon
passes between sun and earth, so
that the lunar shadow falls on our planet.
This shadow has two parts: the umbra,
or inner shadow, where the moon com-
letely hides the sun, and the outer penum
ora, from which the lunar disc would only
partially cover the sun's face.
Where the
umbra reaches, an eclipse is total; from the
penumbra only a partial eclipse may b
observed.
During the night of Oct. 22, by U. S

Madagascar and New Zealand, as well as
a large portion of the Indian Ocean, will be

these regions.
