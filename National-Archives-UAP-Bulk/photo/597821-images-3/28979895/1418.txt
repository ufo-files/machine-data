Autumn Arrives
Another important event on the astro-
nomical program for September will occur
at 8:10 a.m., E.S.T., on the 23rd. This is
the equinox, the time when the sun, which
has been moving southward since last June,
reaches the halfway point. It will then be
standing directly over the equator, above a
point a thousand miles south of Dakar, in
French West Africa. In the Northern Hemi-
sphere this will be the official beginning of
autumn, while spring will then commence in
countries of the Southern Hemisphere.

for a number of nights in succession is sup-

harvest.
