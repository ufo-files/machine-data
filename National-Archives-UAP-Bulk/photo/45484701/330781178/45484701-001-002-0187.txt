STATEMENT
At approximately 5:45 PM. on 28 April 1949 I sighted an object in the
air No or Tucson, Arizona, just over the rim oi the Catalina Nountains.
This
object was very bright and appeared to be reflecting the sun shine. From
my point of observation, this object was first sighted at approximately a
30 degree angle of elevation and I would judge it to be at that time about
300 miles NE of Tucson. This object was in sight for approximately 12 minutes
after which time it disappeared to the SW, just gradually fading from view
in the distance. It appeered to be traveling in a curved trajectory, but
I believe this object was traveling in a straight line and from my point of
observation, the illusion was curved, I would judge this object to be traval-
ing in excess of 500 miles an hour at an altitude of from 5 to 10 miles up.
It appeared to be sausage like in shape, with no fins, wings or protrubances
of any sort. It seemed to be revolving similar to a slow roll of an airplane.
It was most visiable when it reached a position where it reflected the sun's
rays but it also could be seen during the times when no reflections were
visiable,
From the height and size of the object in the air, and if it was
placed on the ground, I would judge it to be the size of a city block, I did
not hear any noise nor was there any exhaust or vapor trails.
I was in the company of Mr, Hubert and lex Keahey and the three of us
were standing near the corner of Campbell Ave and East Hedrick Drive while
this object was in sight. There were no openings visiable, windows or anj
other identifiable marks.
The only way I can describe it is that it was
sausage like in shape. I am sure that this was not a conventional aircraft
and to the best of my knowiedge and belief I can give no reasonable explanation
as to exactly what the object was that I saw.
W. Howard Hamm
