1. SYNOPSIS: JOHN GORMAN, Evansville, Indiana, reported sight-
ing what appeared to be a rocket near Newburg, Indiana, at approximately
2100 hours, 4 March 1950. Rocket appeared to be 10 or
12 inches in
diameter travelling at rate of speed of an aircraft approximately 15 feet
above and parallel to the ground. No flames observed, no noise heard.
GORMAN driving automobile 50 miles per hour drove into smoke emitted from
the object.
(Telephonic report FBI, Indianapolis, Ind.--(F-6)
2. DETAILS:
KIRBY SCHERER, Identification Officer, of the Police Department,
At approximately 2200 hours, L March 195, Evans-
Indiana, advised that at approximately 2100 hours, 4 March 1950, he was
driving east approximately two miles east of the Junction of Indiana
State Highway 66 and 261 north of Newburg, Indiana, when he observed an
object which he believed to be a rocket at a distance of approximately
50 or 75 yeards in front of his automobile. This object was flying
across the highway in a northerly direction. GORMAN did not see any
flames but he did see smoke being emitted from the object. This object
appeared to be a cylinder 10 or 12 inches in diameter and appeared to be
travelling at a rate of speed of an aircraft, approximately 15 feet above
the ground flying parallel to the ground. He saw no flames and heard no
noise. GORMAN was travelling approximately 50 miles per hour and drove
into the smoke emitted from said object. GORMAN'S wife who was also in
the automobile saw the smoke from the object.
3. ACTION: Any further reports concerning this object will be
furnished immediately. Any written report from the FBI confirming this
report will be submitted.
