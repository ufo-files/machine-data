SUBJECT:
UNCONVENTIONAL AIRCRAFT SIGHTED BETWEEN /ROCK SPRINGS

TO :
Director of Special Investigations
Headquarters, USAF
Washington 25, D. C.
1.
SYNOPSIS:
JACK AARON, civilian, of Fort Worth, Texas reported
to the Corporation Judge of the City of Del Rio that he had observed a
disc-shaped plane flying between Rock Springs and Mountain Home,
Texas
on 2 February 1950. AARON allegedly stated that he heard a loud hissing
sound and observed an odd looking plane about 700 feet away coming from
an easterly direction and flying low; that the weather was misty with
scattered clear spots; that the object was traveling at a high rate of
speed; that steam or vapor was streaming from the rear to a distance of
about 10 feet; that the craft appeared to be about 35 - 40 feet in diame-
ter and 15 - 18 feet thick in the center; that all surfaces appeared to
be smooth and no fins observed; that no part of the plane appeared to
revolve or have a spinning motion.
(Evaluation: F-6)
