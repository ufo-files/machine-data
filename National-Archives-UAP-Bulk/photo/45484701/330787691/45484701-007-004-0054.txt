Information has been received from the FBI to the effect that a Mr. Fred
Blumenthal, in the office of Drew Pearson, had contacted the Bureau, advising that
Pearson had been in contact with Dr. W. A. Rhodes concerning photographs which
Rhodes allegedly made of a Flying Disc in the vicinity of Phoenix, Arizona, in June
of 1947.
2. Dr. Rhodes allegedly informed Drew Pearson that the FBI borrowed his
negatives on the Flying Disc and that he had asked for their return. The FBI in-
formed Rhodes that the negatives were not available.
3. The FBI informed Mr. Blumenthal that the FBI did not have in its possession
the negatives referred to by Mr. Rhodes, and in fact Rhodes knew full well that
the FBI turned them over to Air Force Intelligence representatives, Hamilton Field,
on 30 August 1947, with the understanding that he might never have them returned.
Blumenthal was informed by the Bureau that the FBI did not investigate
this matter and did not investigate Rhodes.
Mr. Blumenthal stated he would contact
OSI for what assistance they might be able to give him.
5. OSI has not received any inquiry from Mr. Pearson or any of his representa-
tives.
