Visitor Describes
Flash As Meteor
The flash seen in Anchorage
skies last night was definitely
termed a meteor by Fritz Awe,

the episode last night.

white light," he said. 'It had

plane. It went low over Flat

It knocked down some trees and
burned for more than a day.
Natives reported where it landed
and pilots flew over it and re-
ported what they saw."

Friday, December 1, 1950.
