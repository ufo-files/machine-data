5. Mr. OSCAR KINDGREN, a trapper, who at the time was in a tent

6. Sgt JOHN D. BRADSHAW, Baage #14, Alaska Highway Patrol, was in-
terviewed on the Glenn Highway and stated that as he passed thru Slana
on 30 March 1951, he had heard COBEN'S, DUFFY'S and KINDGREN'S versions
of the incident and they were substantially as reported above. He stated
that he had not heard or seen anything himself.
7. Mr. DON DUFFY Sr, Operator of the roadhouse at Slana, stated
that he did not see the object pass over but did hear a muffled roar.
COBEN and DUFFY'S son described the incident to him and he was convinced
that it was a meteor. Mr. DUFFY also stated that some of the men from
Porcupine Road Camp (Alaska Road Commission) which is located due north of
Slana, visited the roadhouse on 30 March 1951 and when asked if any of the
men at the camp had seen or heard anything, stated that none had. Mr.
DUFFY, Sr stated that COBEN had served in the Navy during the war and was
considered by all who knew him as stable and reliable.
