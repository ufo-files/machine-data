* Subj: Sighting of Unidentified Flying Objects
b. (The Nashville Tennessean, 30 March 1950)
Two fishermen on Marrowbone Lake near Nashville, Tennessee
reported sighting between 6 and 12 unidentified flying objects at
about 0700 hours 29 March 1950. D. C. WHITESIDE and HOWARD T. WILLIAMS
told of the objects passing about 200 to 300 feet over their heads.
The men estimated that the objects - reported to be black, round,
heavy, and about five feet long - were traveling at least 500 miles
per hour.
CAA and USAF officials at Nashville disclaimed knowledge of
any aircraft in the area at the time.
c. (The Nashville Tennessean, 10 April 1950)
Eight employees at the Baptist Sunday School Board declared
they saw a flying saucer "wizz" over the Tennessee State Capitol
Building at Nashville, Tennessee just before 1200 9 April 1950.
The disc-shaped, shining object flying in a cloudless sky
was reported to have disappeared upward toward the north. Speed of
the object was a subject for disagreement; some said faster than an
airplane, others said slower.
2. No investigative action taken by this district.
