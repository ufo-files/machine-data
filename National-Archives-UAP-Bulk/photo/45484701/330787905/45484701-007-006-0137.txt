craft had four (4) concentric sections when viewed from underneath.

The outside section was narrower
than the others and like the center, did not rotate. MCLAUGHTIN said
the craft did not seem to have any exhaust or smoke, but that he did

He could see no slots or duct openings in the object. After approxi-

at an angle of 45 degrees and away from him at a tremendous speed.
MCLAUGHLIN estimated that it was out of sight in a matter of seconds,
but he could not estimate the speed at which it was traveling.
