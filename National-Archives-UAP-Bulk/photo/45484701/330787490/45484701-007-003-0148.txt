On 8 September 1950 at approximately 1,00

HAILS: On 8 September 1950 Mr. JOSEPH E. LATTA was
interviewed at his residence on Taylor Street, Del Paso Heights,
California and stated that at approximately 1400 hours on 8 September
1950 while he was working in his rice fields, which are located
approximately 27 miles north of Sacramento, California, he detected
a burning object falling very slowly through the air. LATTA asserted
that when he first noticed the object it was at an altitude of
approximately 200 feet, appeared to be about 10 feet in diameter and
was falling at very slow rate of speed. LATTA stated that the light
of the burning object was extremely bright and made it impossible to
determine any specific details relative to the shape other than the
fact that it appeared round to him. LATTA also stated that the object
left a trail of blue smoke as it descended.
