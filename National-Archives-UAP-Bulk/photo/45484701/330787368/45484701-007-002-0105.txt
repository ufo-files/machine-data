In accordance with AFCSI Letter No. 85, subject as above and
dated 12 August 1949, this District has been reporting on unconventional
aircraft and unidentified flying objects, including the so-called "Fly-
ing Discs".
Recently two newspaper articles indicating discontinuance of
investigation by the Air Force of "flying saucers" have been noted.
One of these articles appeared in the Los Angeles Times; the other in
the 31 December 1949 issue of the Air Force Times. The item from the
Air Force Times is quoted verbatim below:
"Investigation Ends for Flying Discs
Washington - Air Force announced this week the discontinuance
of a two year old special project for the investigation of
"flying saucer" incidents.
USAF based its decision on analysis that the flying objects
were the results of:
Misinterpretation of conventional objects
2. A mild form of mass hysteria
3. Or hoax
Since January 1948 some 375 incidents have been reported and
investigated."
This office is reporting and will continue to report on flying
saucer incidents in accordance with existing instructions in AFCSI Letter
No. 85. However, clarification is requested on current USAF policy re-
garding investigation of these incidents and the effect of any change
in policy on the above mentioned letter.
