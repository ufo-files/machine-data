SYNOPSIS
ROBERT F. SMITH, Lewiston, Idaho, stated he saw six unidentified flying objects
on 13 May 49, between 1100 and 1119 hours, Mountain Time; that he was travelling
East toward Boise, Idaho, by auto, when the objects were observed to his left,
approximately 9:50 o'clock;
, that the objects were within his sight about five
minutes; that five of the objects were in "y" formation, flying in a Southwesterly
direction, approximately eight to twelve or fifteen miles from his point of ob-
servation. He described the objects as being about the size of a B-29 aircraft,
silver on top and dull on bottom, and having the shape of a half-circle with a
point in the front, which was the middle of the arc. Persons living in the vicin-
ity of the location from where SMITH observed the flying objects were interviewed
but none had ever seen any unidentified flying objects. H. G. JEPSON, Pilot In-
structor and Trainer, Bradley Field, Boise, Idaho, stated that on 24 Apr 49, a
about 0900 hours, he was on a flight to Mountain Home, Idaho, in an L-13-B wit!
two passengers and that they observed flying objects about 1,000 feet above his
altitude, flying in the same direction as he but at a faster rate of speed. He
described these objects as being oval shape with a small point in the forward
section. No commercial or private flights were known to have been in the vicinity
where the objects were observed by SMITH; neither were any military airoraft
scheduled in that area.
No radar sightings or testing devices were in the area.
