CONFIDENTIAL
I told Sgt Buntz when I saw him that same day and that evening started
the flying saucer deal.
Sgt Bunts told me about Frank Scully and his book "Behind the Flying
Saucer", which I had read something about in the newspapers and in an
article in "True Magazine". He explained that he knew there were flying
saucers and that they had two of them at Wright Field with the sizes of
27' diameter and 99' diameter. He said that one craft had a broken porthole
in it and that the reason the three passenger's were dead was because of a
terrific heat field caused by friction had cracked the porthole and had
killed all inside. At Wright Field, he claimed the Air Force had closed
Project Saucer because it was a true fact and would be alarming to the
American public and that the Air Force reopened the project under the name
of Project Radiation and that the laboratory was situated on the top of
a hill in Wright Field. I listened to him on this subject and when I said
I doubted the story in some ways he told me it was fact because they had
proof. I asked hin what sort of proof could he have. He then said baoking
his statements that there was a man who came from this field by the name of
Davis, who was transferred to Wright Field and was put on Project Radiation.
He said that they (meaning Mr Mead and someone else I don't know and Sgt
Bunts) thought Davis would no doubt be assigned to that project and they
arranged for Davis to tell Kramer who works in Maintenance Electronics or
a building of that name; so that Kramer could transmit back to this radio
shop so Mr Mead and Sgt Buntz could know if there were really flying saucers
and such.
