"On 21 January 51, a veteran air line pilot, Larry W. VINTHER

straight wings set farther forward than a B-29's, bearing no
engine mountings, and having no exhaust glow. The mystery

underneath the fuselage.
As the Mid-Continent plane approached,
the strange craft blinked its lights on and off five or six
times.

other ship was traveling faster in the opposite direction.

pilot had just turned his head from watching the plane pass,
when there it was again going in the same direction and ap-
proximately 200 feet to portside. VINTHER checked the Offutt
Air Force Field and was informed that there were no jet planes
scheduled in the area.
