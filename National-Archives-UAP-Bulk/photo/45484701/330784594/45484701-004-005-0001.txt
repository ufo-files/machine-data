1. Reference is made to telephone conversation between Major
QUINTANILLA and SA FOLINO of this office on 13 November 1967,
concerning subject matter.
On 13 November 1967, Mr. ROBERT S. THOMPSON advised that Mr. DON
WAGNER, 263 South Claremont Street, Springfield, Ohio, advised him
that on 13 November 1967, at approximately 0400 hours, he had observed an
unidentified flying object (UFO) in the vicinity of Reed Park, Springfield,
Ohio. This object was approximately 30 feet in diameter and had an orange
glow to it. THOMPSON further advised that WAGNER told him that he had
shined a spotlight on the UFO and it had taken on a pinkish tint and came
towards him. When the object came towards him, WAGNER became frightened
and left the vicinity. WAGNER notified a lieutenant on the Springfield
Police Department of this incident.
3. The above information was furnished to Major QUINTANILLA via refer-
enced telephone conversation.
Major QUINTANILLA said that his office
would contact Mr. WAGNER first thing Tuesday morning, 14 November 1967,
and WAGNER was so advised.
