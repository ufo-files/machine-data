OSI Detachment 501, Cincinnati, Ohio, on 30 January 1964:
At approximately 2325 hours, 27 January 196h, she observed what
appeared to be a green flare in the sky north of Newton, Ohio. The
flare appeared to have fallen to the ground but she was unable to
determine the distance from her home to the flare. To the best of
her knowledge, no other persons in the area observed the flare.
OBERMEYER stated she had notified the Observatory at the University
of Cincinnati, the Luken Airport, and the U.S. Weather Bureau,
Cincinnati, Ohio, of the above information.
