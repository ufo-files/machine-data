highway in Warren, Minnesota, found this aerial object about
two (2) years ago
in the vicinity of Warren, Minnesota and
informant photographed it just an hour or so after it was found.
He stated that it contains a fuel tank, a three (3) tube radio,
and dry cells and that the opening at the rear of it appeared
to have vented hot gases, causing informant to believe that it
had been jet or rocket propelled. He disclosed that is containec
no directional steering and had been completely covered with
aluminum paint.
In examining this object, Informart found
nothing to indicate its place of manufacture.
He further stated

cincinnati, Ohio, and feels qualified to say that the main body
of this object was made in a press rather than by hand, which
indicated to him that it was a product of mass production.
He

Mr. SIREK who indicated that he still has the object in his
