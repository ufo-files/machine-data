Unidentified Aerial Object sited in the Vicinity of DuBois, Idaho
Director of Special Investigations
Headquarters, USAF
Washington 25, D. C.
UNCLASSIFED
1. This District has received information from the Special Agent
in Charge, Federal Bureau of Investigation, Butte, Montana, to the
eitect that a Mr. LORENZO HANSON and DARRELL HANSON had observed an
object, which appeared to be a flying missile, in the vicinity of
DuBois, Idaho, on 16 September 1950.
The informants stated that they
were hunting in the area of Medicine Lodge, Idaho, northwest of Dubois,
when their attention was drawn to a hissing or buzzing noise above
them. Looking up, they observed a plate-like object, dark brown in
color, approximately three feet in diameter, being propelled at an
altitude of approximately 400 feet from a southwesterly to a north-
easterly direction. The HANSONS advised that the object appeared to
be descending, and it was their opinion that it could have landed
behind some hills one or two miles from their observation point.
The
informants added that the missile appeared to have knobs or some kind
of appendages around the edge.
No smoke or gas of any kind was observed
from the object, which they lost site of behind the hills. The HANSONS
reported the siting of the object to the Butte Office, FBI, on 26 September
1950, and an immediate search of the area wherein the missile was pre-
sumed to have fallen was undertaken in conjunction with the Sheriff of
Idaho Falls, Idaho. Results of this search were negative.
2. No investigation into this matter is being conducted by
District Office No. 16. The above report is furnished for your
information.
UNCLASSIFIED
