has, in connection with their studies of winds aloft, launched Corner
Reflectors (Radar Targets). These targets do not bear any stamps, tags,
or other data which would serve to identify it for what it is.

quently, on a number of occasions, persons who have found these targets

The Air Weather Service does not desire the return of these
targets once they have been launched and served their purpose inasmuch
as they are of no intrinsic value.
3. Recently, one such target was sighted and recovered on a farm
in Eastern Maryland. A report was received that a "flying saucerl or
"disk" had been observed in flight and it had landed in this particular
locality.

dual who had sighted the object. This time and expense would undoubtedly

that it was a weather target and that if found it could be retained by the
finder. The targets themselves are susceptible to such stamps or markings
and it is urged that consideration be given to communicating directly with
the Air Weather Service in cornection with this suggestion.
