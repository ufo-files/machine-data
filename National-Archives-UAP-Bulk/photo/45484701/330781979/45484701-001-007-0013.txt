Statement of 2nd it ROBERT L. KUNZMAN - Cont 'd
24 Nov 48
Describe the tactics or maneuvers of the objects--such as, vertical ascent
or descent, horizontal, oscillating, fluttering, evasive, aggressive, erratic,
etc.
A. None of those describe it actually. They seemed to fly as I stated before:
West to Fast, thence Worth, with no apparent change of altitude. They were
not trying to attack nor trying to get away from us.
Q. Was there any evidence of exhaust?
A. No evidence of exhaust.
Q. What was their effect on clouds?
A.
There were no clouds in that particular area.
Were there any lights reflected or attached to the objects; were they
Tuminous?
A. No lights, There was no outstanding reflection.
Q. What was the means of support?
A. I was not close enough to see any visible means of support.
Q. What propulsion was used?
A. I coulân't tell.
Did the objects have fins or stabilizers?
I couldn't see.
Q. Any air ducts?
A. None visible.
Q. What was the speed of these objects?
A. I would not attempt to estimate the speed.
Q. Sound?
A. No sound.
%. was any radio antenna to be observed, i. e., any projections or ex-
Sensions that might presumably be construed as suchi
A. No.
Roll l Kunzman
