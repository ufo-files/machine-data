SYNOPSIS:
Staff Sergeant Marvin Fisher, AF 6 325 024, 141fth
Training Squadron (Saudi Arabian Training Mission), Dhahran Airfield, allegedly
snapped an unknown object, supposedly flying through space, (Incl #l).
DETAILS:
Information had reached this office that Sgt Fisher had in
his possesion, a picture, allegedly taken by himself, of an unknown object.
Contact was made with Fisher who stated in substance that the picture taking
episode, as to time or day and date, was obscure in his mind, however, he be-
lieved it to be between the 10th and 15th of April 1950, after darkness had
set in. Fisher went on to relate that he was exposing the last four (4) frames
of film remaining in his 35 MM camera in order to insert a fresh roll of color
film. The following evening, Fisher stated he developed this film, using dev-
eloper already mixed and on hand in the Photo Hobby Shop. Fisher further ad-
vised that after the negatives were dried, there appeared this one negative
bearing an unknown object. Fisher cannot account for the appearance of the
object on the developed negative, because when exposing the film, he stated
that he merely pointed the camera at a 15 degree level from the waist and
snapped the shutter, and to the best of his recollection, was not sighting
the camera at anything in particular. Fisher also stated that to the best
of his knowledge, he heard no sound or observed any illuminating flash or
light.
Fisher executed a sworn signed statement relative to the above •
