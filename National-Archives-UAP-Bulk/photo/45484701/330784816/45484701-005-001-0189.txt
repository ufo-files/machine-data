rolling and tumbling in the sky. She ran
to the newspaper office to notify others.
(2) THOMAS M. ROGERS, advertising salesman for
the TIMES AND DEMOCRAI, a veteran with
flying experience in B-29ts during the war,
stated that he noticed the object at ap-
proximately 1820 hours.
At this time, the
sky was blue, there were no clouds, and
the sun was setting. The object appeared
to have broken from a vapor trail and was
moving very slowly, like an object floating
in a glass of water. Mr. ROGERS estimated
the altitude of the object to be 15,000 to
20,000 feet. To him, it appeared yellow
in color, changing in shape from that of a
flat saucer to an oval-shaped object. The
object appeared to have come from the north
and was going southwest, down or toward the
horizon.
