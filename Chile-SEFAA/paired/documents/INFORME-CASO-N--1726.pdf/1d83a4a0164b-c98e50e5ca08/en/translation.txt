Case No. 1726 21122021

Date: December 21, 2021.

Approximate Time: 1:46 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: L. V., informed by F. C. Z.

Description: The informant reported the sighting of what would be
anomalies and that his wife had photographed them at 01:46 on December 21,
2021 in Iquique, in the Tarapacá Region.

The user indicated that the sighting lasted between 1 to 5 seconds, had
a "biological form," measured between 20 centimeters to 1 meter, and was
colored "like a type..."
"LED light focus". He concluded by testifying (sic): "Photo of my wife in Iquique in her room and street".

To support this, he submitted two JPG format photos. Despite the testimony, both show the interior of a room.

Based on the provided address, the case was georeferenced in the city of Iquique, in the Tarapacá Region.

Analysis of the information contained in the photos revealed they had been digitally altered.

As Figure 1 shows, both in this case and in Case 1737, the images have been manipulated.
Added is the same drawing, which appears alien to the scenes. Both reports
come from the same user.

Conclusion: According to the study of the report and forensic examinations of the images sent to SEFAA, it was determined that the user reported the presence of extraterrestrials that actually corresponded to digital drawings available for download on the Internet.

Additionally, it was verified that one of those images of a supposed extraterrestrial was reported by the same user in another case, but with a different background.
Therefore, the file was closed and classified as "False."
