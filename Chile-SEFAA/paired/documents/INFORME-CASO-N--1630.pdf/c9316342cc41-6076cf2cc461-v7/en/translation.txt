Case No. 1630 22082021

Date: August 12, 2021.

Approximate Time: 22:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: C. C., 32 years old.

Description: The informant reported having received a video of what he considered an aerial anomaly that was allegedly recorded around 22:00 on August 12, 2021, from "Puyuhuapi, Coyhaique, Chile."

The user described the reported object as oval-shaped, multicolored, "enormous," and at a distance he could not define. Based on what he saw in the video, he added that
This presented "lights" and "magnetic field."

Additionally, he explained that (sic): "The video was sent to me by my cousin, recorded with an iPhone 6 at night in Puyuhuapi. I consider it authentic in that it has no alterations."
"The recording and perception of the witnesses is an anomalous phenomenon in the sky."
Although he indicated that what was captured was "static", he said it had disappeared towards Puyuhuapi.
In three emails on August 23 and 27, and September 1, 2021, the informant was requested to provide the footage mentioned in their testimony.
and the necessary information to contact the person who made it. As of the writing of this
report, she sent nothing and did not contact the CEFAA again.

For statistical analysis purposes, the case location was taken as a site in the sector
known as Puyuhuapi in the city of Coyhaique, Aysén Region, General Carlos Ibáñez del
Campo. The location corresponded to the coordinates 45° 34’ 01’’ S and 72° 04’ 17’’ W. (Figure 1)

Figura 1

Images: Although requested on August 23 and 27 and September 1, they were not provided.
As of 2021, the user only provided their testimony.

Conclusion: Due to the lack of study material, the investigation file was closed according to numeral 2d (Study of cases that report images, audios, or videos) of the Technical Instruction Procedure, Research Times, Reception of Backgrounds, Preparation of Executive Report, and Archiving of CEFAA Reports.

The case was classified as "False" due to the lack of contact from the reporter.
