Enter the initial report of a sighting through the CEFAA web platform.
Summary: Flying lights in formation, occurred on April 18, 2018 in Puerto Montt.
Accompany the case with five images.

At 21:30 local time on April 18, 2018, while practicing night photography with his/her cell phone, the user took pictures of the sky and, upon careful inspection, noticed strange lights and formations that he/she hadn't seen with the naked eye, hence this was a fortuitous appearance.

Geometric analysis of the images reveals symmetry.
With regard to the focal center.

Investigation:

The technical analysis of the images indicates that what was photographed are the reflections of

surrounding lights on the lenses of the cell phone with which the photographs were taken.
