Upon studying the data contained in the video file, it was defined that it was
Captured at 15:57 hours on the reported date by a DJI Mini 2 drone with an FC7303 camera. This features an 83° field of view lens, capable of an f/2.8 aperture and focusing from one meter away.
From the nearly 30-second video clip, each frame was extracted and considered.

Their apparent speed was found to be consistent with their distance from the camera that captured them. The following Figure 4 shows one of the studied objects indicated with a blue arrow. The structures were white and low-density, consistent with the witness's report: "like a fuzz with well-defined movement and in a quick manner." As Figure 5 shows, between frames 486 and 575
It moved away from the drone, with slight variations in its altitude. It disappeared from the next frame because its color blended in with the sky.

Additionally, meteorological conditions were verified based on observations made from the Pichoy Airport (SCVD) around the time of the flight: horizontal visibility was at least 10 kilometers, with partial cloudiness at 701 meters and overcast at 1,219 meters altitude. Although the dominant winds came from the west.
Intensities of 3 to 4 knots (5 to 7 km/h) were reported around 16:00 hours from varying directions.

The details of the weather conditions prevailing at the site on that day and hour are included at the beginning of this report, under the "Meteorology" section.

The wind pattern described above explained the flight patterns and allowed the hypothesis to be raised that we were facing low-weight bodies being dragged by 3-knot breezes from various cardinal points.
This is consistent with seeds dispersed by anemocory. These are usually very small and therefore transported as atmospheric dust. Also, in addition to their size, they may have "wings", "hairs" or "feathers", which facilitate their dispersal by increasing the surface area of the air currents.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, and meteorological studies, it was concluded that what was reported were seeds carried by the wind.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained".
