Description: The informant reported filming two objects that she considered air anomalies at 12:40 on February 26, 2022, from the commune of El Quisco, in the Valparaíso Region.

The reported information was characterized as white spheres, the sizes of which were not specified, but it was indicated that they were two visible objects from 21 to 30 meters away.
seconds, always to the north of more than three witnesses.

The user concluded by writing (sic): "It was recorded from a terrace in a resort in the town, it was impressive as I started to look at it with other friends and it was phenomenal the movements."

The user's location at the time of the sighting was georeferenced to the coordinates 33° 24’ 40’’ South Latitude and 71° 41’ 50’’ West Longitude (Figure 1).

The examination of the information contained in the file defined that the following was used:
Samsung S5KGW3 camera of a Xiaomi cellular phone model 11 Lite 5GNE
(2109119DG), which features an optical system composed of 6 lenses.

From this footage, 902 frames were extracted and analyzed. They show two sets of bright, white objects composed of a variable number of points flying seemingly in sync. The first appearing on screen will be named Phenomenon 1, and the next one Phenomenon 2.

As shown in the following Figures 4 and 5, the geometric examination confirmed that the phenomena captured were reflections caused by light sources facing the camera lenses.

Figure 4. Origin of the first reported phenomenon.

Images: Along with the report, a thirty-second video was provided in an mp4 file format.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the
Geographical, forensic, and photographic studies concluded that what was reported were parasitic reflections or "flares" caused when recording lights in backlight. This light struck the optical system of the cell phone used and generated these optical objects in the camera's light sensor. As they only existed internally, they could not be seen directly.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
