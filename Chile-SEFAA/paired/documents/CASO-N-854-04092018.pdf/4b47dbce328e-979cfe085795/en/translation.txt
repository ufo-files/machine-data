It reports the sighting of luminous phenomena that it photographed with its cell phone.
Summary: In the Guanaco Hill (25° 48’54’’ S and 69°48’24’’ W), located within Taltal,
On August 27, 2018, he/she took photos at sunrise and did not perceive them at the time.

He/she mentions that this occurred while taking photos on Monday, August 27, 2018, and did not notice anything unusual while taking the photos, but only realized it after reviewing his/her cell phone.

The authenticity of the images is verified through ELA (Error Level Analysis) and CMF (Copy and Motion Forgery).

Investigation:
The images were captured with a Samsung SM-G610 cellphone at dawn on Monday, August 27. The image metadata matches the background described in the report.

The geometric analysis of the images shows symmetry in relation to the focal center of the photographs.

When zooming in on the objects, it is not possible to discern that there are volumes.

The technical analysis of the images allows us to indicate that the phenomena are
Conclusion:

Parasitic reflections from solar lighting.
