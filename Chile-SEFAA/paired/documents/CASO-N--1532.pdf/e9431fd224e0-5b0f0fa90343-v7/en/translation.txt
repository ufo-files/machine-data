Case No. 1532 29042021

Date: April 29, 2021.

Approximate Time: 19:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Autumn.

Witnesses: C., 36 years old.

Description: The informant, who only identified herself by a first name and did not provide an email, reported seeing "more than 10 lights aligned in the sky" at 19:00 (HL) on April 29, 2021, from the commune of La Pintana, Metropolitan Region of Santiago.

She described the reported sightings as circles, bright, aligned, and moving slowly and straight.
The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 33° 33’ 39’’ S and 70° 38’ 17’’ W (Figure 1), which offers a clear view towards the south of the Santiago basin.

Figure 1

A call was made and text messages were sent to the phone number provided as contact, with no response. Since there was no means to request further information and considering the characteristics and date of what was observed, it was determined that it was the same mass transit of satellites from the Starlink Project.
Reported by another user located north of the city and shown as "Group 1" in Figure 2.

Figure 2. Aerospace transits at 18:56 on April 29, 2021. An orange star marks the user's location. Yellow arrows show the direction and movement of the satellites. Cropped image, Group 2 is not shown in full.

According to the latitude of the user's location and the elevation azimuth
that could be seen, it was determined that the reported
corresponded to the first group of satellites.

Starlink is a project by the aerospace company SpaceX to create a constellation
of communication satellites.
They have been launched since 2018 and are placed in orbits at 550
kilometers in altitude.

Images: The informant provided no further information than her account.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of
the geographical and aerospace traffic studies, it was determined that
what was reported was the overflight of a formation of Starlink satellite
constellation satellites.

The case was closed, classified as "Explained."
