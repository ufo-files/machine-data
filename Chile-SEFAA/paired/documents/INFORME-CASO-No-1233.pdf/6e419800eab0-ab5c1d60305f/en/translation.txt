Case No. 1233 18082020

Date: 27 February 2010.

Approximate Time: 05:00 or 06:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: A. V., 17 years old.

Description: The informant reported seeing multiple anomalous aerial phenomena at 05:00 or 06:00 (LT) on 27 February 2010. He described what he saw as three white circular lights that allegedly formed an equilateral triangle around the Moon, "as if they were synchronized with it." He stated, "I believe it could be seen throughout Chile."
He ended his account by explaining that “...years later, I saw on TV a report, perhaps by Salfate, about sightings, and he was talking about what I saw. It was the most impressive OVNIs experience I've had, but I don't know why I didn't react to tell my family and that they also saw it. I just stayed fascinated watching it until I fell asleep.”

In emails on August 18, 20, and 27, 2020, the user was asked for any means that would allow the reported incident to be studied. By the time this report was written, no response was received.
Some.

Images: The informant did not provide study materials.

Conclusion: The case was closed because the witness did not provide images and because what was observed took place ten years ago, a time frame that makes studying a phenomenon almost impossible.

Considering the above, this decision was based on numeral 2e (Investigate cases older than one year only if they possess audiovisual materials) of the Technical Instruction Procedure, Research Times, Receipt of Backgrounds, Preparation of Executive Report and Archiving.
