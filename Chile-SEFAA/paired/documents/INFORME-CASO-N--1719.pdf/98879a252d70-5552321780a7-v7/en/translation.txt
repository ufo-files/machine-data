Case No. 1719 21122021

Date: December 21, 2021.

Approximate Time: 0:19 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: L. V., informed by F. C. Z.

Description: On December 21, 2021, the witness entered the SEFAA web platform
to report that his wife had seen and photographed an extraterrestrial being inside
the residence where they were staying in Iquique, in the Tarapacá Region, at 0:19
hours that same day.
The user stated that the sighting lasted between 1 to 5 seconds, had a
“biological form”, an approximate size of 20 centimeters to 1 meter, and was
silver in color. Along with his report, he uploaded a digital photograph in jpg
format to the web platform.

The case was georeferenced at the address provided by the user in Iquique, in
the Tarapacá Region.

Upon observing the photograph provided by the user, it was evident that it was
a photo montage.
When searching for the being in the image on the Internet, the same figure was found as an extraterrestrial being of a race called "Sectoid" from the fictional video game XCOM: Enemy Unknown (2013) (Figure 1).

Figure 1. The humanoid entity in the image provided by the user (left) and the extraterrestrial "Sectoid" from the video game XCOM: Enemy Unknown (right) are exactly the same.

The above left no doubt that the provided evidence medium was a photographic composition.
