Date: May 31, 2022.

Approximate Time: 17:58 local time.

Location: Las Cadenas, Valparaíso Region.

Witnesses: F. P. C., 31 years old.

Images: The user supplemented their report with an original video, which was analyzed frame by frame, examining each of its 408 frames.

Description: The informant reported what they considered an aerial anomaly that they saw and filmed at 17:58 (HL) on May 31, 2022, from the Las Cadenas sector, Valparaíso Region.
Valparaíso. He described it as an object with the shape of a cigar or black cigarette and 2 to 5
meters in size. He added that it was visible for 11 to 20 seconds, always to the north of two
witnesses.

Finally, he testified that (sic): “In this case, we were walking with my youngest son, to
buy at a nearby store from our house, my little son always likes to look at the sky, when I
looked up, he saw an object moving slowly, very large in the shape of a cigar, we looked at
it for a while and started recording, after a while, it slowly advanced and
"It disappeared, we kept walking and we didn't see it."

The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 32° 47' 32" S and 70° 37' 03" W. (Figure 1)

This location is in the eastern direction of the northern approach cone of the Arturo Merino Benítez (SCEL) airport in Santiago.

Figure 1

Analysis of the information contained in the metadata of the video allowed confirming its digital integrity and determined that it was captured on the reported date.
This recording was studied through each of its 408 frames.

Figure 2 combines two close-ups taken at frames 389 and 407, along with the shift pattern of the filmed material, which led to the hypothesis of encountering an aircraft in flight.

Figure 2

Based on the footage, the situation of potentially visible air traffic to the southwest was analyzed, detecting the presence of the Airbus 321-211 concluding the LAN1273 flight from Diego Aracena Airport (SCDA).

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that the reported event was the overflight of the Airbus 321 aircraft operating LAN flight 1273 between the city of Iquique and Santiago on May 31, 2022.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
