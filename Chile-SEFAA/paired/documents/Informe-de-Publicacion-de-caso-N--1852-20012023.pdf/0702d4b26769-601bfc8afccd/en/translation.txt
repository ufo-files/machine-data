Date: January 20, 2023.

Approximate Time: 11:00 local time.

Location: Costanera Avenue, Coquimbo, Coquimbo Region.

Witnesses: N. A. I. A., 20 years old.

Images: The user only provided their testimony.

Description: The informant reported having seen what they considered an
aerial anomaly at "11:00" local time on January 20, 2023, from Costanera
Avenue in Coquimbo, in the same-named region.

What was reported was described as a triangular object, assimilated to a
"light spot."
"Car", at a distance of 20 to 100 meters, which would have been visible to more than three observers always to the east.

"Object with triangle-shaped lights and one in the middle that moves slowly, tilts, and disappears by turning off its lights was approximately the size of a helicopter", the witness wrote in his report, along with which no backup file was attached.

The SEFAA sent the user three emails on January 23, 25, and 27, 2023, requesting that he specify where the sighting took place.
It was described and asked if there were images to study what happened. As of the writing of this report, there was no response nor were elements received for analysis.

For statistical purposes, a representative location was established for the reported place.

Conclusion: The investigation file generated with the report was closed due to the lack of an object of study and in accordance with items 2a (Minimum information and responsible party that warrants the initiation of an investigation) and 2c.
(User response to an email confirmation or a telephone call from the Investigator) and 2d (Investigation of cases reporting original images, audio or videos) of the Technical Instruction on Procedure, Research Times, Reception, Executive Report Processing and SEFAA Report Archive.

Case classified as "False."
