Case No. 1642 30082021

Date: August 28, 2021

Approximate Time: 19:59 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: I. R. A. D., 55 years old.

Description: The witness reported the presence of what she perceived as an
aerial anomaly for "approximately 2 hours" at 19:59 on August 28, 2021, and
to the west of her location in the city of Rancagua, in the O'Higgins Region.

The user described the information as "round" and "light" in color, "larger than
a plane" and moving very slowly.
He finished his report by writing (sic): "I was in my bedroom, walking down the hallway, I saw a light in the adjacent bedroom through the window. I went in and looked; it seemed to me that it could be a plane. I observed and had a reference star that was infinitely smaller, located vertically to the light I was seeing. I took that as a reference to see how it was moving, then I looked for my cell phone and recorded, on two occasions with a time difference, the last time I went to see it, it was no longer there."

In September 2021, the topography around Rancagua was studied, determining that when filming, the camera was pointed approximately towards the west. (Figure 3)

As shown by the celestial arrows in Figure 4, the daytime images allowed identifying the lower light of the footage as a bulb located near the user.

Since what is visible essentially depends on the image composition
performed by the phone's graphics processor for the capture, this study
focused on the rest of the audiovisual material.
In addition, three 4,128 x 3,096 pixel photographs taken after the reported sighting were received and used. They show the scene filmed in twilight light.

Figure 6

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and the forensic, geographic, and astronomical results, it was concluded that what was reported was the filming of the planet Venus from the city of Rancagua.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
