Multiple air anomalies were reported, observed at 10:50 (HL), on 23 December 2024.
What was reported was described as more than three white spheres appearing and disappearing in the northwest. No determination was made of the size of these objects.
But it was indicated that more than three people saw them for more than a minute.

Testimony (sic): "The formation of 18 objects is observed in a northwest direction.
Description of
Objects at some point remain static and continue to advance towards the user/userin:
"I lose sight of them several minutes later."

Along with the report, the files "IMG_20241223_105316.jpg" and "VID_20241223_105328.mp4" were entered.

The location of the person who reported was georeferenced at coordinates 35° 24' 18'' S, 71° 39' 38'' O, at 94 meters above sea level.

The forensic examinations of the photograph that supported the report confirmed that it was not digitally altered and that it was taken by the main camera of a phone.
Investigation: Xiaomi M2101K6G cellular phone at 10:53 (HL) on December 23, 2024. It is shown in Figure 1.

According to TVN #24 Central Red Maule news broadcast on December 23, 2024, a minor's funeral was held in the city of Talca, and it was determined that this occurred at the Parque las Rosas Cemetery, 1.2 kilometers from the camera (Figure 2).

The data regarding the wind conditions at that time were obtained from the automatic station in Panguilemo, located at 35.4° S and 71.6° W in Talca. The recorded data indicated that the winds were blowing from the south-southeast (150° to 160°) with an intensity of 6 to 8 knots (11 to 14 kilometers per hour).

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the photographic, geographic, forensic, meteorological studies, and those reported in the press,
Regionally, it was determined that what was reported corresponds to white balloons

Conclusion:

Released at the Las Rosas Cemetery for the funeral of a minor. Due to wind conditions, they were carried in the direction reported by the user.
