Case No. 1742 03012021

Date: December 25, 2021.

Approximate Time: 9:25 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: P. A. S. S.

Description: The informant reported the sighting of what he considered an
aerial anomaly at 9:25 a.m. on December 25, 2021 from the Pichiquillaipe
area, in the Los Lagos Region.

The user testified that (sic): “Witness 1 takes 3 photographs (Samsung J20) and
in the photo appears an object that is not visible to the naked eye. Witness 2,
takes 5
"Photos (Samsung A20) a few minutes later, a 10-15 minute difference, in 2 it
registers an object that is not visible to the naked eye."

A study location was defined in Pichiquillaipe corresponding to the coordinates
41° 31’ 37’’ South Latitude and 72° 46’ 01’’ West Longitude. (Figure 1)

Figure 1. User location in relation to the city of Puerto Montt and the aerodromes El

By examining the geometry of the four photos with the reported bright object, it was found that focal symmetry exists, determining that what was interpreted as an aerial anomaly was actually a parasitic reflection, or "flare," caused by photographing the Sun backlit.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and geographical, forensic,

There were more of them than in photographic cameras, so they could not be
