An Air Traffic Controller provided information regarding a flight contingency reported radially by the pilot of Lan Peru flight LXP2371.
According to the report, the crew observed a lighted area the size of medium cities, with white lights that they considered much too large to be the lighting of a fleet of ships. They indicated that "the largest group was 130 NM North of ASEPU and extended east-west for approximately 80 NM. There was another area of lighting to the South of ELASA, but it was alone."
Figures 1 and 2 illustrate the images from these testimonies.

The radio communication recordings between the various aircraft and the Oceanic Area Control Center on frequency 124.9 MHz were collected and analyzed. They were consistent with the reported images and with the hypothesis of an origin in oceanic fishing operations with the use of lighting.

According to the study of the radio report and the photographs taken by the pilot who reported the case and the information provided by the Chilean Navy, it was concluded
Conclusion: that what was reported corresponded to a high-seas fishing operation, probably by giant squid fishing vessels that use powerful lights at will to attract the targeted species.
