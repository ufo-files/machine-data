Season: Spring.

Witnesses: U. A. P. P., 35 years old.

Description: The witness reported having photographed what he considered an
aerial anomaly at 19:58 local time, November 7, 2021, from Ratmuncho Beach, in
Hualpén, Biobío Region.

What was observed was described as having the shape of a "saucer" and a silver
color, and it would have appeared in the west of the observer.

Given this, the hypothesis was raised of being in the presence of the
unperceived and involuntary capture of the flight of a bird, possibly some
kind of gull.

Consequently, the specialized advice of ornithologists from the University
of Chile, who are external advisors to SEFAA, was requested. One of them
indicated that although the image does not allow for greater precision, it is
not
I would discard that what was reported was a Cagüil, Capuchina, or Gavitín (Chroicocephalus maculipennis Lichtenstein). (Figure 8)

Depending on the stage of their reproductive cycle (from November to January), these birds change the appearance of their plumage, so that the feathers on their heads and throats darken during the summer in Chile, this is a gradual change. The photographs correspond to the beginning of this transitional period. (Figure 9)

Figure 8. Chroicocephalus maculipennis (Photography by Gerónimo Martín Alonso)
Figure 9. Chroicocephalus maculipennis (Photograph by Cláudio Dias Timm)

Another possibility of a white gull in the area is Leucophaeus pipixcan,
also known as the Franklin's gull, Pipizcan, Cagüil, or Caulle. By November,
these birds are in what is called the "resting plumage," with clear plumage.
(Figure 10)

Figure 10. Leucophaeus pipixcan (Photograph by Omar del Toro)

Images: The case originated from the first photograph taken from a series of
images sent by the user.
Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony; of the results of the geographical, forensic, and photographic studies; and of the information provided by the consulted advisors, it was concluded that what was reported corresponded to a bird in flight. It was estimated that it was a gull, possibly of the ones known as hooded gulls or Cagüil.
