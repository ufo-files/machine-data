azimuth that could be seen and what was photographed, it was
determined that the reported objects correspond to the first group of
satellites.

Starlink is a project by the aerospace company SpaceX for the
Creation of a constellation of communication satellites, which
are being launched since 2018 and are placed in orbits at 550
kilometers altitude.

Images: The user only provided his testimony.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results
of the geographical and aerospace traffic studies, it was determined
that what was reported was the overflight of a formation of Starlink
project satellites.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
