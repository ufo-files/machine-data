Case No. 1675 22092021

Date: August 15, 2021.

Approximate Time: 17:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: M. C., 45 years old.

Description: The witness reported that at 17:00 (LT) and for thirty minutes on
September 15, 2021, she saw what she considered eight "ufos" from the commune
of Pedro Aguirre Cerda, in the Santiago Metropolitan Region.

The user described what she saw as eight objects with a "circular ovoid" shape,
"white or shiny steel" in color at times, flying slowly. The movement
The general indicated was "south to north" towards "east direction".
Subsequently, he added that "I saw one that emitted a flash and disappeared".

He concluded his testimony by writing (sic): "[…] around 17:00 hours, my
A couple looks up at the sky and reports seeing UFOs flying, I look and see 8 balls flying and getting closer to each other, a park guard passes by and we also tell him about it, he sees them and says they don't look like either donuts or balloons, since
they maintained their position and approached, even forming orderly patterns. The
observe for approximately 30 minutes. Even when one of them blinked and disappeared, I saw it; no debris from something that had broken, like a balloon or drone, for example, fell, so I know what we saw and those things were not of this world.

The user was positioned at coordinates 33° 31’ 20’’ South Latitude and 70° 40’ 39’’ West Longitude. (Figure 1)

Figure 1

Given the testimony, the hypothesis was raised of being in front of the visualization of
metalized balloons in flight. Consequently, wind information at altitude was studied using data from the flight of a radiosonde launched from Santo Domingo Airport (SCSN). According to this information, between 200 and 1,500 meters of altitude, winds predominantly had a south-southeast direction. Their intensities fluctuated from 4 to 12 knots (7.4 to 22.2 km/h). (Figure 2)

Images: In an email dated September 30, 2021, the informant stated that they did not have any material to study their case.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and geographical and meteorological studies, it was established that there is a high probability that the reported sighting was metallic balloons reflecting sunlight. The described movement could have been caused by winds blowing from a southerly to southeasterly direction at speeds between 4 and 12 knots.
Case classified as "Probably Explained".
