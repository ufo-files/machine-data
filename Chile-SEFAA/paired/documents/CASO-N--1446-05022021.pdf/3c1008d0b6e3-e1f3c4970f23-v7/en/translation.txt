In the skies of the city of Talca, in the Seventh Region of Maule.

The reported sighting was described as approximately 50 objects forming a straight line, of a "light" color, "similar to supposed satellites sent by Musk." He added that he observed them as "a straight line of lights at different speeds from one another, which was losing luminous intensity as they advanced."

He concluded his report by stating (sic): "I need to know, please, if yesterday satellites were seen in space to improve internet."
That would be what I saw. Thanks for the space. In case that wasn't, that was...
"a fleet of UFOs without a doubt."
