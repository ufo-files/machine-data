By performing an enlargement, light handling and contrast of the original image and
By comparing it with one of the images sent in the second email, it was possible to identify a reflection and texture corresponding to the vehicle's dashboard.

It should be borne in mind that what was reported was not seen directly and that the photos were taken from inside a vehicle.

Images: The case was supported with JPG format photos.

Conclusion: According to the geographic, forensic, and photographic analysis, it was concluded that what was reported corresponds to reflections of the vehicle's windshield, as the reported image was taken from inside the vehicle.
The original photograph was compared with one sent in a second email by
the informant, confirming the existence of reflections from the windshield and
texture of the vehicle's dashboard.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
