Case No. 1249 25082020

Date: August 24, 2020.

Approximate Time: 20:15 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: b., 18 years old.

Description: An unidentified user reported through the committee's website having witnessed multiple anomalous aerial phenomena at 20:45 (HL) on August 24, 2020 from Saujil, Argentina.

He described what he saw as white circles, flying at a distance he could not specify. He stated that they were all "in a line, separated"
They were more than 200, the first time, then they passed again, they were "less." Everything would have lasted about 5 minutes.

It was confirmed that the city of Saujil is part of the Province of Catamarca, Argentine Republic. For this reason, this request for investigation fell outside the scope of work of CEFAA as it originated from an event that occurred abroad.

Catamarca is permanently in the GMT-3 time zone, so the reported sighting corresponded to the
23:45 on August 24th. When studying aerospace transit for that moment, it was established that the reported event corresponded to the massive overflight of satellites from the Starlink project by the American company SpaceX.

Images: None were provided.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the report and geographical and aerospace transit studies, it was concluded that the reported event corresponded to the overflight of satellites from the Starlink project by the SpaceX company.

Since receiving a report from an unidentified user...
He provided no means of contact, the case should be classified as "False" according to Chapter 4 (Classification of Reports) of the Technical Instruction, Research Times, Background Reception, Executive Report Preparation and Reports Archive CEFAA.

However, the case was closed as it is believed to have originated abroad. This is according to item 2b of Chapter 2 (Study of cases occurred or registered in national territory) of the CEFAA Technical Instruction.
Case classified as Foreign.
