Case No. 1355 14122020

Date: December 10, 2020.

Approximate Time: 20:00 hours.

Meteorology: N/A.

Season: Spring.

Witnesses: M. A.

Description: The informant reported the movement, for approximately 30 seconds, of a bright light around 20:00 hours on December 10, 2020 in the Paiguano Ravine, in the Fourth Region of Coquimbo.

He described the appearance of a "round and oval" luminous phenomenon, bright in color, moving from the east, in a
A straight line from south to north and that would have been observed by three people.

He stated that "(sic) 'We were hiking. Through some hills in the Elqui valley, when suddenly a round light appeared (I have a video recorded)."

Based on the previous account, a series of emails were sent on December 15, 16, and 17, 2020, requesting information and the video that the witness claimed to have filmed.

Awaiting the necessary data to study the report in detail, the approximate location of the sighting was georeferenced.
The witness was informed while trekking in an area with sparse vegetation and surrounding hill heights between 1,900 and 3,900 meters.

The coordinates of the indicated location are as follows: 30° 01’ 52’’ South Latitude and 70° 26’ 08’’ West Longitude.

Below are satellite images of the area and its location within Chile and South America.

Figure 1. Paiguano Canyon, surrounded by hills between 1,900 and 3,900 meters.

Figure 2. Geographical coordinates of Paiguano Canyon.
A response from the witness was expected until December 24th, in order to gather more background information to reconstruct what happened.

Images: No images were provided by the witness.

Conclusion: According to the limited information provided and the impossibility of contacting the witness, who did not respond to any of the three emails sent by this agency, the case was closed as "False."
