Date: January 29, 2021.

Season: Winter (Northern Hemisphere).

Witnesses: N. N. C., 56 years old.

Description: The informant reported the presence of what he considered air anomalies in a photograph taken at 06:10 (HL) on January 29, 2021, in Acacías-Dinamarca, Meta, Colombia.

He described the reported sighting as a gray oval, assuming it was static and far away. He added that he only perceived it.
The user was located at coordinates 3° 59’ 30’’ N and 73° 44’ 56’’ W (Figure 1). This corresponds to an area near the city of Acacías, Department of Meta in the Republic of Colombia. This determined that the case fell outside the scope of work of the Committee.

Figure 1

In his report, he concluded by writing (sic): "When enlarging the photo focusing on the moon, I see a strange object in the foreground."
The case report was based on a photographic file, from which the metadata information was analyzed. This confirmed that it was a digitally manipulated file, using the Picasa software. Picasa is an image editor and also a web tool for organizing, viewing, editing, and sharing digital photos. According to the user, the image was "cropped." In Acacías, the Moon was not visible at the reported time.
