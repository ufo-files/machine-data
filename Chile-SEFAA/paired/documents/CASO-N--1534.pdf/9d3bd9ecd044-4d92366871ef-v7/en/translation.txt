Case Number: 1534 29042021

Date: April 29, 2021.

Approximate Time: 18:55 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Autumn.

Witnesses: C. C., 22 years old.

Description: The witness reported that, for two minutes, at 18:55
(HL) on April 29, 2021, he saw and was able to photograph multiple
anomalous phenomena in flight from Alcohuaz, Coquimbo Region.

He indicated that he observed a row of white lights, from west to
east. Regarding the movement of what was reported, he added that
it was straight-line and at a constant speed.
The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 30° 13’ 26’’ S and 70° 29’ 37’’ W. (Figure 1). This corresponds to the locality of Alcohuaz, administratively part of Paihuano.

Figure 1
As shown in Figure 2, despite being embedded in a fully mountainous sector, the course of the Claro or Derecho River develops from south to north, so the user was able to see towards the southwest from their location.

The photographic study defined that along with stars, approximately twenty lights in straight-line motion were captured, with a displacement consistent with what was described. (Figure 3)

Based on the above, aerospace traffic was studied, detecting
The presence of at least two formations of 82 satellites of
the Starlink project. (Figure 4)

According to the latitude of the user's location, the elevation
azimuth that could be seen and what was photographed, it was
determined that the reported objects correspond to the first group of
satellites.

Starlink is a project by the aerospace company SpaceX to create a
constellation of communication satellites.
They have been launched since 2018 and are placed in orbits at 550
kilometers altitude.

Images: Three photographs in jpg file formats, attached below:

The three images that supported the report were perused through ELA (Error Level Analysis) and CMF (Copy and Motion Forgery) analysis, confirming they were not digitally altered. Analysis of the information contained in their metadata confirmed the photographs were taken between 18:56:54 and 18:57:15 on the reported date.
The main camera of a Samsung Galaxy S8 Plus (SM-G955F) cell phone was used.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the report and the geographic, photographic, and aerospace traffic studies, it was concluded that what was seen was a formation of twenty-two Starlink project satellites.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
