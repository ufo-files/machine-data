Case No. 1875 29032023

Date: March 29, 2023.

Approximate Time: 20:28 local time.

Location: Caleu, Metropolitan Region.

Witnesses: P.R.C., 55 years old.

Images: User provided 1 video recording.

Description: The reported sighting was described as an object in the shape of a white candle, with no specified size, but visible for almost one minute by more than three witnesses, flying from the north towards northeast.

The user concluded by stating (sic): "We saw lights moving slowly."
"North to east with the shape of a cigar and suddenly it disappeared. I tried to upload the video that weighs 42 MB and the page did not allow me to do it."

Although no audiovisual file was entered along with the report, a video titled "Luces caleu.mp4" was later provided for the study of the case.

The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 33° 00' 57" S, 70° 58' 52" W and 938 meters above sea level (Figure 1).

Figure 1
The analysis of the information contained in the metadata of the footage that supported the report defined that, although its original name was changed, it showed no signs of digital alteration and was captured at the reported date at a rate of 30 frames per second. It was studied by examining each of its 986 frames. Figure 2 shows the studied object as it was captured in frame 409 along with a zoom that inverted its colors.

                                       Figure 2
The air situation study at the time of filming verified the presence of two aircraft near the user. To the west was a Chilean Navy aircraft with identifier ALAGARTO on a flight operating from Viña del Mar (SCVM) airport in Concón. To the north, farther away, was the Airbus 321-211, registration CC-BEH, performing flight LXP319 of Sky Airline between La Serena and Santiago cities.

Conclusion:

Based on the analysis of the report and the results of geographical, forensic, photographic, and aerial and aerospace traffic studies, it was concluded that the reported incident originated from the overflight of satellites in the G5-10 mission of the Starlink project, belonging to the SpaceX company.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
