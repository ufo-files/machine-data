A report of what was considered normal flight was seen and filmed from the city of Valdivia.

The reported object was described as a shape resembling a cigar or a cigarette.
The object was white and had a size comparable to 20 to 100 meters. It was visible for 11 to 20 seconds, on a clear sky, flying from southeast to south as witnessed by two people.

Testimony (sic): "I looked out the window, and when I saw this object, I knew it wasn't a
