Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the user's testimony and the results of
the forensic, meteorological, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that,
given that the reported event occurred in the afternoon of August 11, 2021,
there is a high probability of what was reported.
It would correspond to the flight of an inert body dragged by the wind, whose
surface reflected the Sun.
