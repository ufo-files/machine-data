Case No. 1521 17042021

Date: April 15, 2021.

Approximate Time: 5:00, local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Autumn.

Witnesses: E. P. C., 32 years old.

Description: The informant reported having seen an anomalous aerial phenomenon approximately at 05:00 (HL) on April 15, 2021, from Quebrada Blanca, commune of Pica, Region of Tarapacá.

He described the reported phenomenon as 50 to 60 white stars that for about three minutes shone as they flew from west to east.

He concluded his report stating that (sic): "It was a phenomenon..."
"Stars or lights that appeared at one point and disappeared at another (even when a portal opened and disappeared at another location)."

Since the user indicated being a 'dumper' truck driver
A study site was georeferenced at the Teck mine, in the company's operation located in Quebrada Blanca, to the west of Collahuasi. This corresponded to the coordinates 20° 59’ 20’’ S and 68° 48’ 41’’ W. (Figure 1)

The air traffic situation was analyzed between 04:00 and 06:00 (HL) between the city of Calama to the south and the town of Camiña to the north. Although no flight with characteristics similar to those described (Figure 2) was detected, the passage of the Bombardier Challenger 604, registration LV-FWZ, was confirmed, which, after stabilizing at approximately 35,000 feet, followed the UL550 airway towards the San Fernando International Airport (SADF), near Buenos Aires, Argentina.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony and the
The results obtained from the geographical and air traffic studies, concluded that what was reported was the overflight of a Starlink satellite formation project in the process of deployment.
