Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of the forensic, photographic, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that what was reported is consistent with an inert object drifting, likely a balloon or a trash bag, given the distance the object was located, and its size did not allow for the precise determination of its nature.

Case classified as Explained.
