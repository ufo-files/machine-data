Case No. 1433 25012021

Date: December 31, 2020.

Approximate Time: 23:45 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: N. R. E., 27 years old.

Description: The witness reported the presence of what she considered multiple aerial anomalies captured on video, approximately at 23:45 (HL) on December 31, 2020, from a rural area in the commune of Casablanca, Valparaíso Region.

Although she explained that what she reported had been seen and filmed by someone else,
Third, without being able to appreciate it directly, the user described the information as five white circles. These would have appeared "around the Moon" to disappear from sight "into the night."
He added that (sic): "There were several and they appeared to move in any direction, they would stay still and then move again, the last ones went to the right."
Mrs. Rodríguez concluded her report stating that (sic): “[…]
They were waiting for the New Year in the plot they have in New World in the field, and they watched the sky and realized that some dots were circling the moon, they called me to see if I saw it from my house and I couldn't see them, and they sent me a video that they managed to capture something."

Since the case's intake, the user was emailed three times requesting the footage mentioned in their testimony, on January 28th, February 2nd, and July 14th, 2021. Although they responded to one of those messages on February 2nd, 2021,
The user did not contact again nor sent any study material of what they reported.

Waiting for the audiovisual material mentioned in the report, a georeferenced central location of the plots in the New World sector was identified: 33° 20’ 43’’ S and 71° 23’ 23’’ W. This location is approximately 3 kilometers southeast of the informant.
