An abnormal aerial phenomenon was reported at "07:10."

Summary:
On the hours of May 06, 2024, from Punta de Lobos.

What was reported was described as an object associated with the shape of a cigar or
havana, colorless, and for which no size estimation was made. It was indicated that
it was visible for 6 to 10 seconds, flying from north to south by two witnesses.

The user testified that they were observing the sky after sunset
“At 18:48 the ISS passed by”
North horizon (O to E), which I checked on the website to make sure it was what we saw. Confirmed. 20 minutes later, an object also appeared heading north, but moving from N to S. At first, I thought it was some kind of compressed starlink, but as it ascended, I could see that it was an object in the shape of a cylinder or cigar, which was almost translucent. That is, it wasn't...
It had lights, but only a low-intensity glow. I told my wife to look, and she also saw it as it passed over us. It quickly faded away among the stars. The place where it disappeared was near the south cross. No photos.”

No additional files were entered with the report.

The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 34° 25’ 42’’ S, 72° 01’ 58’’ W and 23 m.s.n.m.
Subsequently, the user sent the image included over the astronomical horizon of Figure 1, stating that the Sun set in the direction of the investigation: a palm tree indicated with an orange arrow and that what was reported "had appeared to the right of the bush to the right." It was verified that it was taken at 18:46 hours on May 06, 2024.

According to the information published by the Hydrographic Service, ...
The Naval Oceanographic Office (SHOA) for San Antonio, 101 km north, on May 6, 2024, sunset occurred at 18:01 (HL). The end of civil twilight, with the center of the Sun 6° below the horizon, was at 18:27 and at 18:57 the end of nautical twilight, with the solar center 12° below the horizon line.

What is listed as "N/A" was the Boeing 787-9 that performed the LAN842 flight between Mataveri Airport (Easter Island) and Arturo Merino Benítez Airport in Santiago. At that time, it was stabilized at 40,992 feet at a heading of 98° and a speed of 522 knots. Its white fuselage was illuminated by the Sun. At 20:34 (HL), it entered the continent over Quintero.

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the studies
The geographical, astronomical, and air/satellite traffic analyses concluded that,
although there was a possibility that the reported information was a fleeting reflection of
the LAN flight plane coming from Easter Island, it could also have been light reflected by
Starlink satellite 2451.

Due to the absence of images of the reported sighting, the investigation file was closed and
the case was classified as a "Relato" (Report) according to the protocol.
