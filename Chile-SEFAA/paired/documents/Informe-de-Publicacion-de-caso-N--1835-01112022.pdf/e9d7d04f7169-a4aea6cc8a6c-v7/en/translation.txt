Date: October 29, 2022.

Approximate Time: 8:35 a.m., local time.

Witnesses: F. P. C., 34 years old.
Description: The informant reported having seen and filmed what he considered an aerial anomaly at 20:35 local time, on 29 October 2022, from Escondida mine, in the Antofagasta Region.
What was reported was described as a white circle with a size of less than 5 centimeters, which was visible for more than one minute flying always northeast.
from more than three witnesses.

The user concluded their testimony stating that (sic): "Bright white circular object descending in circular motions until it appears to turn north and disappear." Along with the report, they submitted the file "WhatsApp Video 2022-10-29 at 20.39.55.mp4."

The user was contacted, who provided more precise details of the location from which they filmed and sent the original footage.

The original case video was examined, analyzing the information contained in the
digital file, confirming that the recording was captured at the moment
Reported. The filmed object was analyzed by extracting and studying each of its
2,435 frames.

As can be seen in Figure 3, frames 36 and 38, enlarged and with inverse coloration,
the flying object had a central body surrounded by what appeared to be five appendages
indicated with red arrows.

                                   Figure 3

Additionally, Figures 4 and 5 illustrate that with enlargement and direct coloration,
it displayed both red and green lights.
Figure 4

Figure 5

The visible silhouettes of the central bright body varied throughout the footage, both in terms of its position relative to the camera and the various degrees of pixelation in the images.

The aforementioned is consistent with the existence of an object with navigation or priority lights used in ships and aircraft, which are a red light mounted on the port side, or left side, and a green one on top.
starboard band, or right side. (Figure 6)

Figure 6

Both the displacement pattern and what was indicated above pointed to
the filmed object being a small aircraft from which only the navigation lights
were perceived, with the ability to maintain flight.

Images: The case was studied based on the footage contained in the file
"20221029_203416[1].mp4."

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony and geographical studies,
Forensic and photographic analysis concluded that what was seen, filmed, and reported was a drone in flight.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
