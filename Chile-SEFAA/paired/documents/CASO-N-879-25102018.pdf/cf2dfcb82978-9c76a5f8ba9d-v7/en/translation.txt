A person reports an anomalous sighting that they photographed on the coast of Quillaipe, commune of Puerto Montt, at 19:17 hours on 23 October 2018.
He describes it as an oval object of light blue color with a well-defined light blue streak. According to the user, it does not correspond to a sun ray as the sky was cloudy at the moment of taking the picture.

The images were perused to verify their authenticity through ELA (Error Level Analysis) and CMF (Copy and Motion Forgery).
Image has not been altered, moreover, metadata information is consistent with the reported date and location.

Investigation:
The user noticed the existence of the phenomenon while reviewing a photo taken with a Samsung Galaxy J7 Pro cell phone, with an f/1.7 aperture and a shutter speed of 1/1515s.

Optical analyses of luminance, error, and noise of the images allow
It indicates that the phenomenon does not have volume. Edge detection confirms the presence of the phenomenon as unique in the image.

The meteorological conditions at the time of the photograph are verified, indicating good visibility conditions existed.
