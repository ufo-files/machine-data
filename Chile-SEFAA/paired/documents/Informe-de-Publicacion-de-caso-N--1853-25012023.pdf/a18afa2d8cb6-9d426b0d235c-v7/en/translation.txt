Case No. 1853 25012023

Date: January 23, 2023.

Approximate Time: 7:15 a.m., local time.

Location: Lake Llanquihue, Los Bajos sector, Ensenada, Los Lagos Region.

Witnesses: I. A. R. A., 34 years old.

Images: Two JPG format photographs taken sequentially, one of which shows the reported phenomenon and is attached below:

Description: The informant reported what he perceived as an aerial abnormality in a photograph taken at 07:15 a.m., local time, on January 23.
From 2023, from the sector of Ensenada, Los Bajos, Frutillar, in the Region of Los Lagos.

The user described what was reported as an object with the shape of a cigar or a pure, black, and 2 to 5 meters in size, visible for 1 to 5 seconds always to the south of a witness.

“I was observing from the sector of Los Bajos in Frutillar rain falls that were occurring at that moment in front of the place where I was located in the Ensenada sector of Lake Llanquihue, visually I did not see anything abnormal, except for the”
The first photograph I took with my cell phone, when I saw the image, an object appeared that I did not see when I took the photograph, then I took several other photographs to see if the oval-shaped object, somewhat blurred, black, blurred, which could be due to a possible high speed of displacement, appeared again.

"That's what the witness wrote in their report, along with which the files 20230123_191540.jpg and 20230123_191624.jpg were entered."

The location of the informant was georeferenced at the time of taking the photographs.
Photographs at the geographic coordinates 41° 04' 23" South Latitude and 72° 56' 06" West Longitude, a beach adjacent to the Los Bajos pier on Lake Llanquihue, approximately 10.5 kilometers southwest of Frutillar Bajo.

The two images that supported the report were analyzed using computer programs, confirming that they were not digitally altered.
The analysis of the information contained in the files indicated that they were
Created between 19:15 and 19:16 local time, on the date reported by the main camera of a Samsung Galaxy A42S smartphone. Its photographic parameters were consistent with the technical capabilities of this device.

The following Figure 2 is an enlargement of the reported object, and Figure 3 shows the result of applying luminance filters (3a) and contrast enhancement and brightness adjustment (3b).

Figure 2. Enlargement of the reported object.

Figure 3
It follows that, rather than a solid object in the shape of a "pure" and
black object, what is present is something segmented, with different colorations
and slightly curved. Additionally, in Image 4b, two sectors consistent with hyaline
wings marked with yellow arcs can be seen.

Figure 4 shows that, along with signs of displacement, the volume contains two
differentiated sectors: one to the left that appears to be semi-spherical, separated
by a narrowing from another of greater dimension.
The above-described is similar to the body of an insect in flight seen from above,
with its anterior plane to the left of the image, so its movement
was from right to left (yellow arrow).
                                   Figure 4

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the
geographical, forensic, and photographic studies, it was concluded that what was captured was a
flying insect that crossed the scene from right to left.
Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained".
