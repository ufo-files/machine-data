Case No. 1219 12082020

Date: August 12, 2020.

Approximate Time: 01:49 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: M. C., 19 years old.

Description: The informant reported having witnessed an anomalous aerial phenomenon at 01:49 on August 12, 2020, from the city of Valparaíso, Region of the same name, always to the northeast of its location.

He described what he saw as a "circular body of yellow color that changed in size and light intensity," adding that
"It seems there was something else down there that gives the impression of
blurring the light in a fan shape" at both poles.

He indicated that the reported size was "variable, from medium to
large in relation to the stars."

A geographical study of the hills of Valparaíso was carried out, which
allowed georeferencing a study location near the user-reported address,
on Cerro Las Cañas.

From this location, the astronomical scene visible for an hour around
the reported moment was studied.
At the reported time, the Moon was emerging and Mars was also located to the east of the user. To the west, the brightest object on the horizon was Saturn, and most notably, Jupiter. Another point of light, located northwest, that might have caught the user's attention, could have been Altair or α-Aquilae, the brightest star in this constellation and one of the brightest in the sky.

Images: In an email of August 12, the user indicated not having any audiovisual media of what was reported.
Conclusion: The case was closed according to numeral 2d (Study of cases with audiovisual antecedents) of chapter 2 of the Technical Instruction Procedure, Research Times, Reception of Antecedents, Preparation of Executive Report and CEFAA Report Archive.

Case classified as Report.
