Case No. 1767 28032022

Date: January 6, 2022.

Approximate Time: 23:49 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: J. L. C. B., 43 years old.

Description: The informant reported seeing and filming what he considered an
aerial anomaly on January 6, 2022, from Futrono, in the Ranco Province,
in the Los Ríos Region.

The reported sighting was described as a saucer-like object with a color
similar to a strobe light. Its size was indicated as between 6 to 20 meters,
and it was added that
A object appeared from the southwest and disappeared towards the southeast after remaining in sight for more than a minute.

The user testified that (sic): "The object approached from the southwest, positioned itself in front, advanced towards the west, once it was in front of us, it retreated and moved away towards the west. All this happened in approximately 2 to 3 minutes I have a video (...)".

Along with the submission of the case video, the user indicated that the sighting occurred in the Quimán Bajo sector, approximately
At 2.5 kilometers east of Futrono. A study site was defined by the coordinates 40° 06’ 59’’ South Latitude and 72° 21’ 36’’ West Longitude, at 97 meters above sea level. (Figure 1)

Two photographs and one recording were received as case support, according to the user, captured using an Apple smartphone model iPhone 13. Analysis of the information stored in the images demonstrated
That they were not original. None of their files indicate the date and time of
the shots, nor does it specify the type of equipment.

Additionally, a 01:23-minute video was received in the "IMG_3235.MOV"
archive, created at 21:06 (HL) on March 29, 2022, combining live photos taken
by an iPhone 13. The file data indicates that the compiled material would have
been originated on January 6, 2022, at 23:49 hours.

Upon examining the aerospace traffic that had the potential to be observed
as it appears in the photos and recording of the case, it was verified that an
overflight from northwest to southeast of a compact formation of 49 satellites
belonging to the Starlink G4-5 mission and deployed
orbit by the company SpaceX at 18:49 hours Santiago de Chile time
on the same day, January 6, 2022.
In addition to the above, it was reported that these satellites were seen from Santiago. The
Figure 5 shows them in the photograph of report 1745 06012022 and Figure 6,
in frame 1042 of the footage of case 1754 12022022.

Images: As support for his report, the user provided two files.
Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the report and the geographical, photographic, and aerial/aerospace traffic studies associated with the reported incident, it was concluded that what was observed and filmed was the overflight of forty-nine satellites from the Starlink G4-5 mission of the American company SpaceX.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as
"Explained"
