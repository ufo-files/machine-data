According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of geographical studies,
Forensic, photographic, astronomical, and air traffic reports concluded that what was reported is of terrestrial origin and consistent with the recording of light generated by an LED lamp installed in a sanctuary on Quillayquen Hill. The conclusion does not rule out the possibility that depending on the time, the Sun's position, and atmospheric conditions, the solar panels of the antenna could also eventually...
