Description: The informant reported having seen multiple aerial phenomena at 21:30 (HL) on Tuesday, October 06, 2020 from the coastal sector of Las Cañas, Constitución, Region of Maule.

He described what he saw as a “Formation of luminous spheres in a line.”
"One after the other", moving from west to east, he then stated they were, "larger than an average star flying north to south".

Everything would have lasted about thirty seconds.

In a final contact, he indicated that "In terms of size at distance, I could say that these objects, or collectively, were 3 to 4 times the length of a cargo ship", and that "...I would dare to say that I was able to make out their entire span (10 to 15 lights in a line)".

Finally, he sent the following graphic interpretation of his sighting.
