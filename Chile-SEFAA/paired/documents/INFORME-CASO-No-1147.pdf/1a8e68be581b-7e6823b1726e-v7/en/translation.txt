Season: Summer.

Witness: C. G. C. O., 50 years old.

Description: The witness reported a personal experience she had during the summer of 1987, around 19:00 hours. She does not remember the date or month.

The witness stated that she was walking down Arauco Street, heading east, in the town of Quillota, V Region,
When suddenly he looked up at the sky and he would have observed "something
giant silver colored".

"It made no noise. It had no shape of a cloud, it was among the clouds, to be more exact, some cirrus. But I managed to distinguish some kind of portholes that framed some windows. ...It did not move, nor went away nor disappeared... I left!!!"

The location of the sighting was georeferenced, determining its Latitude of 32°89 South and Longitude of 71° 25´ West, in the commune of Quillota. The only study means is a drawing sent by the witness.
The witness stated that she did not take any photographs or videos.

Images: Only two drawings provided by the reporter. No photos or videos exist.

Conclusion: Only the account of a situation is available. There were no other witnesses nor any photos or videos to analyze.

In addition to the above, the observation dates from 1987, without a specific month or day established. The case was closed according to Chapter 2, items 2d and 2e, as the report does not include images and the time the event occurred.
