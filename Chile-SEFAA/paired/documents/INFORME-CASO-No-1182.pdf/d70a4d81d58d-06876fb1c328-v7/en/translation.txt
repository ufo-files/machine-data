At 400 meters, scattered cloudiness.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: M. V, 37 years old.

Description: The informant reported having observed a phenomenon
on July 17, 2020, approximately at 18:10 hours, from his residence in the commune of Ñuñoa, he observed an object moving across the Andes mountain range.

He described it as "flashing red, very large, and very clear."
I tried to record it for a very long time... my phone's resolution wasn't good and it recorded a small white ball. It wasn't doing much, just flickering and getting closer and closer.
As the mountain slowly descended, it became evident that it was not a peak, but a plain, as when it dipped slightly, it revealed a level terrain.
It was contradicted by mountains in the distance... suddenly
it disappeared.”

First, the meteorological conditions existing at that time in the Metropolitan Region and on the slopes of the precordillera facing Santiago were studied. Thus, information was obtained from Arturo Merino Benítez Airport and Tobalaba Airfield.

The prevailing weather conditions were scattered cloudiness at 490 meters (1,300 feet), so visibility was almost 10 kilometers.
Then, the two videos sent were analyzed, corroborating
that neither of them was altered and that they were created by a
cell phone iPhone 8 on July 17, at 18:12, local time.
The video was also georeferenced and verified to have been taken
from the location reported by the user.
The video detected a static light over the Andes, in
east direction and towards the pre-cordillera of the communes of Peñalolén
And The Queen.
According to what was observed, astronomical maps were consulted.
As of the report date. It should be mentioned that on July 14th, the planet Jupiter, the largest in the Solar System, reached its closest position to Earth, such that in the following days, in the southern hemisphere, it was possible to observe it with the naked eye, appearing over the Andes mountain range.

Images: Videos in MP4 format taken with an iPhone 8.

Conclusion: According to the analyses carried out, what the witness observed corresponded to the planet Jupiter that was located at that time.
On July 14, 2020, at one of the closest positions to our planet, allowing it to be observed with the naked eye towards the east, over the Andes mountain range.

The witness stated that the object suddenly disappeared. This was because as Jupiter rose, it was obscured by a scattered cloud located at approximately 490 meters in altitude, which is also visible in the two videos sent to CEFAA.
