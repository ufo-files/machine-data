The case concerns an aerial phenomenon discovered in a photograph taken on February 22, 2016, around 17:30, on the shore of the Lake.
Colbún. He described it as a plate, with light and dark coloration and horizontal movement.

While on vacation with his family at Lake Colbún, walking along the shore, the user described seeing...
Photographs taken of the riverbank are reviewed again two years later, and he sees strange objects in them.
The photograph was taken with a Samsung Galaxy J1 Ace cell phone. The metadata of the
Image indicated it was taken according to the user's report. The expertise conducted to verify its authenticity with an ELA (Error Level Analysis) and CMF (Copy and Movement Forgery) analysis indicated it had not been digitally altered.

Investigation:

The luminance analysis showed that the phenomenon was very distant from the camera, which explained why it was not detected at the time of its occurrence.
Photographed.

The edge analysis clearly identified the presence of the phenomenon and allowed its profile to be defined as that of a bird in flight. Confirmation was requested from the Wildlife Ecology Laboratory at the University of Chile (3/12/2018).

The technical analysis of the image that supported the account led to the conclusion that a bird in flight was photographed.
