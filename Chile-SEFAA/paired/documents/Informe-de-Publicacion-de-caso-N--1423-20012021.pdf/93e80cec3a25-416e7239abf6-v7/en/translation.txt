Case No. 1423 20012021

Date: January 20, 2021.

Approximate Time: 21:25 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: A. A. F., 54 years old.

Description: The informant reported seeing what he considered multiple air anomalies at 21:25 local time on January 20, 2021, from La Serena, in the Coquimbo Region,

The reported sighting was described as "protruding like a LED hose," "just a long line of lights," moving for thirty seconds from west to east.
in a clear sky when they were "seeing the first stars."

The witness concluded their report by stating what they had seen was "A long train of lights, traveling high." No additional files were attached to the report.

The location of the case was georeferenced at the coordinates 29° 56' 28" South Latitude and 71°15' 34" West Longitude, at 34 meters above sea level. (Figure 1)

With the records of the United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs
Exterior (UNOOSA), it was verified that the object under study was the deployment of
satellites. At 10:02 local time, on Wednesday, January 20, 2021, the company SpaceX carried out the fifth space launch of that year by placing 60 satellites into orbit from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at the Kennedy Space Center. Thus, it completed the seventeenth Starlink mission.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the report and the information provided by the user along with the examination of the information published by the United Nations Office.
The United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs (UNOOSA), confirmed that the object under study was the deployment of satellites for the seventeenth Starlink mission by SpaceX.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained".
