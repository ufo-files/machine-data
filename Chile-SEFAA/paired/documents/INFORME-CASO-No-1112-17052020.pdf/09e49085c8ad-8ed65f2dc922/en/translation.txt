Case No. 1112 17052020

Date: Saturday, May 16, 2020.

Approximate Time: 19:00 local time.

Weather: N/A

Season: Autumn.

Witnesses: S. C.

Description: The witness reported having seen an aerial phenomenon.
Anomalous from the commune of Santiago, Metropolitan Region.
He reported that, for about five seconds, he perceived a red light to the south of his
location that "appeared, dropped a bit, and disappeared."
He explained that he was in an apartment located on the 27th floor of a building and that the night was clear.
The user's location was georeferenced in the commune of Santiago at coordinates 33° 26' 09'' S, 70° 39' 32'' W and 621 meters above sea level.

Based on this information, air traffic that had overflown Santiago between 18:00 and 20:30 (HL) on May 16th and that could have been visible to the south of the witness was studied.

Only the passage of flight LAN1232 was detected, which took off at 20:05 from Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport (SCEL) in Santiago en route to the airport.
Miami International (KMIA, Wilcox Field), United States. It could
be seen by the witness from the southwest for approximately between
20:06 and 20:08 hours.
      The potential visible aerospace traffic was studied, without
detecting any visible to the naked eye during the twilight of the
reported date.

Images: The witness stated they do not have images or other
evidence of the reported phenomenon.

Conclusion: The case was closed based on point 2d (audiovisual
support media for the case) of the Technical Instructions.
CEFAA investigation procedures, as there are no study means that allow going beyond the reported account. Case closed for leaving the DGAC's field of action, classified as “Report”.
