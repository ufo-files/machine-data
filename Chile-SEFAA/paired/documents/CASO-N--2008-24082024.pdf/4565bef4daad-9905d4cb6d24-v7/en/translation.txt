Date: 24-08-2024 / 28-08-2024

Case Number: 2008

Report: An aerial anomaly was reported.

Time observed: "07:40" hours

Location: Paine, Santiago, Metropolitan Region

Classification: Explained
As of August 24, 2024.

The reported sighting was described as a white circular object, with a size comparable to 5 to 20 cm, visible for more than a minute, always to the northwest of a witness.

The witness user stated: "I was walking and looking at the stars, many satellites appeared passing over me, so I turned to look at the sky and suddenly I look towards the hills and see that there is a very bright light."
The description is of a bright object, which previously did not appear, seemed to be approaching and moving very little, almost imperceptibly, from top to bottom, then became stationary and did not move.
User: She moved, I returned walking towards the house, I didn't see her anymore because she stayed behind the hill, but I had a doubt and went back. And when I arrived at the point where...
"It was already not there. I took photos and one after the other, it was no longer there."
Along with the report, the following files were entered: "D6D8EBF5-B886-4BEA-8F84-F6E58837DB95.jpeg", "98909BF0-7605-42C5-8BA0-96DEB45372DF.jpeg", "2EDA57A3-10B7-4B95-9767-AEDFB2BB3EB6.jpeg", and "98A4DA7D-1F80-4B11-9107-91C67559A5DB.jpeg".

The user was requested to provide the original files of their photos, as it was verified in the four images that they lack basic photographic indications.
Research:

According to the user's testimony, a georeferenced study location of the user in the commune of Paine was identified, corresponding to the coordinates 33° 48’ 33” S, 70° 44’ 14” W and 400 m.s.n.m.

Investigation:

Additional precision was requested regarding the capture date, shutter speed, aperture, and ISO.  No response was received by the report authors.
The images that supported the report were perused, confirming they were not digitally altered. The examination of the information in their metadata determined they were captured by the main camera of a cellular phone, with no record of the capture date or time. The camera was configured with an image size of 3024 x 4032 pixels, with a resolution of 72 dpi, and stored in JPEG file types.
