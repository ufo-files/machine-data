He reported what he considered multiple aerial anomalies seen at 21:00 hours.
From August 07, 2025, from the sector of Pumol Alto, commune of Futrono, Region of Los Ríos.

Summary:
The user described the reported sighting as "more than three lights, star-like, appearing from the West (W) and disappearing to the West (W), of a size over 100 meters, seen by three people between 6 to 10 seconds."

The gentleman concluded his testimony by stating (sic): "They are lights that appear, move from west to east, grow larger, and after 8 or 15 seconds, they turn off, and they come from..."
"The same location and its movement do not follow any pattern, and they always emerge from the same place, travel a certain area, and then disappear in different locations."

No audiovisual file was uploaded with the report.

In emails dated August 8, 12, and 13, 2025, the user was contacted to provide original audiovisual material to study the reported information.
Greater accuracy regarding the location where the reported event occurred. No response was received by the report's editorial team.

Upon receiving the first of the aforementioned requests, the user indicated that he/she is unable to film what was seen (Figure 1).

Investigation:


For statistical purposes, a location was defined.

Figure 2 shows the astronomical horizon rise for the reported date and time, in accordance with the testimony, “...I looked to the west in the same direction where the sun sets.” At that moment, the Sun was approximately 34° below the horizon and at an azimuth of 262.5°.

Figure 3 demonstrates that there was a high concentration of satellites and other space vessels in that sector, among which it was verified that even those very close to
The horizon was illuminated by the Sun. Figure 4 shows this with the satellite Starlink 30444, which orbited at an apparent altitude of +15°.

Figure 4
According to the analysis of the testimony, the astronomical situation and the space transits, it was concluded that what was reported had the characteristics proper to
sunlit satellite overflight sighting under circumstances
That their observers were in darkness. Due to the altitudes of these movements, the above did not constitute any danger to air operations.

Conclusion:

In the absence of any image to study, the investigation file was closed in accordance with numeral 2d (Only cases reporting original images, audios or videos will be investigated) of the Technical Instruction on Procedure, Times.
