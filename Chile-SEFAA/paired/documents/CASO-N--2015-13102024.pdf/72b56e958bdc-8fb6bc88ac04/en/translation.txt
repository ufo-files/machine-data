An anomaly in the air was reported, present in photographs taken at "07:55" hours on 12 October 2024, from the Ritoque beach.
The information was described as a light, white, with a size of 5 to 20 cm,
seen for 21 to 30 seconds, flying in a sky with clouds, from southeast
to west by a witness. The reporter concluded by writing: "A bright light in the sky
that is noticed over the sea which then disappeared quickly towards the south."

Four photographic files were attached to the report: "IMG_0972.jpeg",
"IMG_0973.jpeg", "IMG_0974.jpeg" and "IMG_0975.jpeg". They are displayed in the first four figures.

Forensic examinations of the four images confirmed their integrity and determined that they were captured with an Apple smartphone, model iPhone.
IF, between 19:51 and 19:52 (HL) on October 12, 2024.

Figure 5 shows the astronomical horizon at the time the first photograph was taken.
The Sun was 0.3° above the horizon and at an azimuth of 260.5°. Although
invisible due to clouds, Venus was very close, at an azimuth of 266.8° and
an apparent altitude of 33.3°.

The review of air traffic situation defined the presence of the flight.
LAN482 coming from Mataveri Airport on Easter Island with a destination of Arturo Merino Benítez Airport in Santiago. As shown in Figure 6, this aircraft was to the right of the Sun, while the observed object appears slightly to the left.

Figure 6

In an email dated October 14th, it was indicated that what was reported was only perceived upon reviewing the photographs.

The geometric examination of the photographs determined that the object under study was
a reflection or flare (Figure 7). This was caused by photographing directly towards the
Sun, resulting in parasitic light incident on the lens curvature, creating this optical
aberration inside the camera. As it was not an object present in the scenes, it could
only be perceived in the photographs.
