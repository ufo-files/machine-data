                 He reported having seen what he considered an aerial anomaly at “03:35” hours
                 on the 28th of December, 2023, from the city of Valparaíso.
 Summary:
The reported sighting was described as a white light, ranging from 6 to 20 meters in size, and visible for over a minute, flying from west to east as observed by a witness.

The user concluded by writing (sic): "I saw a very bright and intermittent light above the building I live in (with cloudy weather) it stayed for several minutes until, out of curiosity, I went out to see and record it, and when I came out, it performed two more light flashes."
User/User: It left in 1 second very far, crossing towards a hill."

No file was entered in the report regarding what was reported.

The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 33° 02' 43" S and 71° 36' 46" W. (Figure 1)
