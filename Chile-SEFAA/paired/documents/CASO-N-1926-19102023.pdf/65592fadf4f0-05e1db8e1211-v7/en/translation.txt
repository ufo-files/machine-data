The witness reported what he considered to be aerial anomalies that he saw at "02:00" hours on 19 October 2023, approximately at kilometer 12 of the road to Cato.
Sector of Santa Raquel, Chillán, Region of Ñuble.
                 Summary:
                 He described the report as several star-like lights, 100 meters in size, seen by two people always to the southeast for more than a minute.

                 The user testified that (sic): “I have been observing this event for several years, and it always occurs from October 15th until mid-November. They are very large lights that always follow the same short route with intense lighting.
Sometimes they cross more than one at a time, these start to be seen more or less at
2:00 a.m. and remain like this for approximately 2 hours. These are found very close, we could say
in the constellation Crux (South Cross)."
