From Mostazal
It reported the sighting of what it described as a plate-sized object.
Summary: Indeterminate, silver in color on a straight flight path and would have been lost from sight as it disappeared into the clouds.

While conducting a site visit for a construction project, the user indicated that they thought they saw an object in the sky but it disappeared before they could identify it. They noticed more details about the phenomenon when reviewing photos and video from the day.
The photographs were analyzed using ELA (Error Level Analysis) and CMF (Copy and Motion Forgery) so it was determined that they were not digitally altered. It was verified that the technical characteristics of the footage were compatible with the equipment used to make them.

The location from which the photographs were taken was georeferenced.
It was located at 34° 1’ 12’’ S, 70° 37’ 48’’ W and 605 m.s.n.m. This is to the East of San Francisco de Mostazal. (Figure 1).
