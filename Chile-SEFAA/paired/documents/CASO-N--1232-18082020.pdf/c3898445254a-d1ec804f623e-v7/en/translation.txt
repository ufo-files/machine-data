Case No. 1232 18082020

Date: 4 December 2016.

Approximate Time: 1:30 a.m., local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Spring.

Witnesses: E. L., 38 years old.

Description: The informant reported having witnessed and filmed an anomalous aerial phenomenon at 01:30 (HL) on 4 December 2016 in the city of Bahía Blanca, Buenos Aires Province, Argentine Republic.

She described what she observed as circular, approximately 300 meters in diameter, and with a coloration of green, blue, and white.
He explained that the aerial phenomenon was several kilometers south of his
location and that it remained suspended over the Bahía Blanca Petrochemical Pole.
It would have lasted about 25 minutes, so he had time to get out of his car and
film it with his smartphone.

In an email dated August 19, 2020, the user confirmed that she was in the
territory of the Argentine Republic (Figure 1), so the reported case fell outside
the scope of work of the Committee, proceeding to
Close the investigation file.

Figure 1. User's location.

Images: The user states that while she had recorded a video of the reported event, she does not have this material other than stored on a YouTube server. It can be viewed at www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q5vxUy2KLKQ.

Conclusion: The Case was closed due to Chapter 1 (Investigation of reports within the controlled space and under the responsibility of the Republic of ...
(Chile) from the Technical Instruction Procedure, Investigation Times, Background Reception, Executive Report Preparation and CEFAA Report Archiving.

Case classified as "Foreign."

The user was informed of the closure of her case and was recommended to report the incident to the Aerospace Identification Center (CIAE) of the Argentine Air Force.
