Case No. 1627 21082021

Date: 7 July 2020.

Approximate Time: 19:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: J. S. I., 50 years old.

Description: The informant reported witnessing what he considered an aerial craft around 19:00 (LT) for approximately 40 minutes on Wednesday, 7 July 2020, from the Esmeralda sector, Colina commune, Santiago Metropolitan Region.

The user characterized what he reported as a white circle, approximately
At a distance of 30 meters and with a "circular motion, also from top to bottom, also from right to left." He added that this would have been to the northwest and 500 meters away from him, and that "they responded to our signals with a flashlight."

Directly related to the report, the witness added that (sic): "Everything started with an immense sensation of leaving the house to the patio and observing the sky and in this process a ship appears from south to north, with a straight direction, then"
It moves up and down, and then another straight line and disappears in front of my eyes."
