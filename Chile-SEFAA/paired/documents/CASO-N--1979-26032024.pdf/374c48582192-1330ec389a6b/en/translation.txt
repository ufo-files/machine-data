Investigation:

An analysis of the air traffic situation over the city at the time of the event was conducted.
A photographic capture found no presence at the sighting location.

The image that supported the report was perused through various forensic and photographic analyses that defined its integrity and was captured at 06:29:55 (HL) on March 26, 2024, by the rear camera of a cellular phone Apple, model iPhone 11.

The user provided the video recording IMG_3655.mov, whose forensic analysis verified its
Digital integrity and defined that it was obtained with the same previous camera, at 06:29:57 hours. It was analyzed in each of its 508 frames.

Figure 3 is a reversed color view of frame 51 of the recording, in which it is appreciated that, rather than a single compact volume, the studied object is a set of singular luminous elements, aligned and flying in a closed formation.
On Monday, March 25, the 23 G6-42 mission satellites for the Starlink project were launched into a low Earth orbit (LEO) from the Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral, United States.

Figure 3

The reported objects were identified as Starlink spacecraft 31464, 31349, 31479, 31446, 31477, 31495, 31445, 31469, 31475, 31455, 31485, 31282, 31480, 31500, 31474, 31504, 31447, 31502, 31489, 31409, 31487, 31386, and 31463.
According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, and aerial and space traffic studies, it was concluded that what was reported was the overflight of 23 satellites of the G6-42 mission of the Starlink project by the SpaceX company.
