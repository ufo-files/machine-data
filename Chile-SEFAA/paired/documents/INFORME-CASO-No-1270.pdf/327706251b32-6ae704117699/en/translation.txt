Case No. 1270 05092020

Date: December 10, 2009

Approximate Time: 15:00 hours.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Spring.

Witnesses: M. O., 46 years old.

Description: The reporting gentleman stated he had seen an anomalous aerial phenomenon approximately at 15:00 (HL) on Thursday, December 10, 2009, near Tongoy, Coquimbo Region.

He described what he saw as a metallic sphere, which seemed to be one-third the size of a commercial aircraft, flying straight from east to west and at "over 10,000 meters of
"altitude."

He explained that, on a trip between Los Vilos and La Serena, "I distinguished a small metallic sphere moving from east to west, I observed that the sunlight was shining on that side of the sphere, while the bus continued moving towards the north." A commercial airplane flying north to south crossed below the flight level of the reported phenomenon.

Subsequently, the user indicated that he was not sure if what he reported had occurred in September 2009 or in December 2008.
In light of the statements in the testimonies, on September 8, 2020, a series of images of objects with similar characteristics were sent to the user, asking if any of those figures resembled what they had witnessed.

In emails on September 10 and 15, they asked again if any of the images resembled what was reported. As of the writing of this report, no response had been received.

Images: Requested in emails on September 7 and 8
September 2020. By email of September 8, the user indicated that they had no record of the reported event.

Conclusion: Due to the absence of study materials for the reported event, the investigation folder was closed according to Chapter 2, numeral 2e (Investigation of cases older than one year only if they present audiovisual study materials) of the Technical Instruction Procedure, Investigation Times, Background Reception, Executive Report Preparation and CEFAA Report Archive.
The case was classified as “Report”.
