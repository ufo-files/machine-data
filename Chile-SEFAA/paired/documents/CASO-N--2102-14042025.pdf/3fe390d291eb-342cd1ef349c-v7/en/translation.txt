Report: Anomaly during flight

Location: Las Condes
Region: Metropolitana
Classification: Resolved

The reporter stated he observed what he considered an anomaly during flight at "05:57" on 13 April 2025 from the commune of Las Condes, Metropolitana Region.
From Santiago.

Summary:

The reported sighting was described as a white plate (no size specified), from which no direction of origin or disappearance was indicated, but it was visible for more than a minute by three witnesses.

The user concluded by stating (sic): "I was at my school's sports field, and my uncle told me there was something in the sky. We observed and saw a"
User Description:

"An object in the shape of a glowing plate. We were shocked. This object moved slowly and then stopped and ascended. It then disappeared."

Files submitted with the report:

"IMG_4318-1.mp4" and "IMG_4318.mp4" videos were uploaded. Later, original files "IMG_4318.mov" and "IMG_4319.mov" were received.

Throughout the two recordings of the case, the studied object appears as a flying object, far away, with a surface that reflects light, showing intense pulses of brightness. The appearance of its silhouette varies, particularly noticeable as the frames get closer (Figure 3).
The aerial scene review during the filming period did not show any aircraft flying directly over the camera location, but it did indicate that the Bell 429 GlobalRanger helicopter with registration CC-DVR was located to the southwest (Figure 4).

Figure 4. Bell 429, CC-DVR. Photo by Rogério A. Avelino.

Figure 5 illustrates the camera's location marked with a yellow marker and shows this craft at 18:01 (white arrow). At that time, it was advancing at 232 km/h.
towards the north (67°) at an altitude of 1,044 meters and was
practically 7 kilometers southwest of the user.

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that the footage had the characteristics of a distant flight by the camera of an aircraft reflecting light. The presence of a single Bell 429 GlobalRanger helicopter was confirmed.
