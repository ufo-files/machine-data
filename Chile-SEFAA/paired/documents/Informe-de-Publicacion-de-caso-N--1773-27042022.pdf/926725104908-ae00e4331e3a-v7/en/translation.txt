Case No. 1773 27042022

Date: April 27, 2022.

Approximate Time: 07:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Autumn.

Witnesses: F. A. U. D., 26 years old.

Description: The informant reported having filmed what he considered an
anomaly in flight at "07:00" on April 27, 2020 from the Concón Roundabout, in
the Valparaíso Region.

This object was described without specifying its shape or size, but it was
black in color. Although only perceived while editing a video, the user noted
There was a witness and what was reported appeared from the east and disappeared to the west of its location.

Testimony: "I was recording a story for Instagram at the roundabout of Concón, region of Valparaíso, when after recording and while editing the video, I noticed an unfamiliar object navigating towards the sea at great speed without a known form. I decided to create an account and report what happened."

Along with his report, the user uploaded a video file named "inbound33271559129660488883.mp4."
Based on what is visible in the footage, the capture location was identified as the bridge over the Aconcagua River that connects the northern part of the city of Concón and the Las Ramaditas sector, on route F30E. This location is near the Humedal Parque La Isla and is at the coordinates 32° 55' 12" South Latitude, 71° 30' 19" West Longitude, and 6 meters above sea level. (Figures 1 and 2)

Pending the availability of material of higher visual quality, the 421

The object described was detectable from frame 113 to 213 and particularly visible between frames 138 and 186.

According to information from the Hydrographic and Oceanographic Service of the Chilean Navy (SHOA), that day sunset occurred at 18:34 hours. Civil twilight ended at 18:58 hours and nautical twilight at 19:27 hours.

At the end of civil twilight, the center of the Sun is 6 degrees below the horizon.

On the other hand, at the end of nautical twilight, the center of the Sun is at
12 degrees below the horizon line and the sea horizon begins to disappear and
stars of first and second magnitude become visible. Given the visible illumination
in Figure 3 and that the user indicated they filmed at "07:00", it was estimated
that the shot was taken at
nautical twilight.
Considering the above and without a precise schedule, the air traffic visible between 18:00 and 20:00 hours, that is, between 21:00 and 23:00 UTC, was studied. During the period, two aircraft overflights were verified: a Cessna 172N Skyhawk from the Naval Air Club (Figure 4) and an aircraft from the Chilean Navy (Figure 5).

Images: Although they were requested via emails on April 28 and May 4 and 6, 2022, the user only provided their testimony and did not contact SEFAA again.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the reported testimony and the geographic, forensic, photographic, astronomical, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that what was considered an anomaly was the overflight of a Naval Aviation aircraft.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
