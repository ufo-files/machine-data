The user described the reported sighting as a "circle of white light that
It did not give off any light or illuminate the surroundings", with a diameter of 3 to 4 meters.
He added that the phenomenon was 100 to 150 meters away from the observer and that (sic) "it levitated 6 meters above the ground slowly following us.
"Parallel to us as we were traveling in the van", east of Route 5,
flying in a south to north direction.

He ended his testimony stating (sic): "[…] I noticed as I was the co-pilot a white light about 100 meters parallel to..."
We saw a white light that did not illuminate the surroundings that was coming.
"Parallel to us, I told my companion who was driving, and we stopped to look, he stood for a few seconds on the side."
