Examination of the metadata information of the original audiovisual material
It corroborated the digital integrity of the material and defined that what was studied was captured between 23:51 and 23:55 (HL) on Friday, September 29, 2023, using a Canon EOS R camera and a Canon EF 75-300mm f/4-5.6 lens. More details are found in the third section of this report.

Image 1922-3 (Figure 3) corresponds to a photograph taken at 23:55:55 on a tripod, in which the upper segment of a very powerful light beam, slightly thicker at its base than at its apex, was captured.
It originated beyond the horizon and pointed towards a luminous moving object. This element is the only one not present in the previous photo (1922-2) taken at 23:55:42.

Figure 4 shows that it was not a single luminous body. Its trace was consistent with a laser used as a pointer for astronomical observation.

It was also verified that within an hour, many satellites potentially visible crossed the visible horizon, describing arcs with different eccentricities, mostly from left to right.
According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, and aerial and aeroespacial transit studies, it was concluded that what was photographed was the overflight of the satellite with the NORAD identifier 49402.
