Following what is described in the Cloud Atlas of the World Meteorological Organization
Globally, the reported are orographic clouds of the lenticular type. They have the shape of a lens or an almond, often very elongated and with generally well-defined contours; sometimes they display irizations.

Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of geographical, forensic, and photographic studies, it was concluded that what was reported were clouds.
