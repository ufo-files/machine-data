Date: July 16, 2016.

Approximate Time: 21:15 local time.

                                N/A

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: J. M., 28 years old.

Description: The witness reported having witnessed an anomalous object, around 21:15 (HL) on July 16, 2016, near Lake Maracaibo, Zulia State, Venezuela. She described it as "oval" and gray metallic in color. She explained that it was approximately 300 meters from her and that
It should have been 4 meters in size. He added that what was reported would have appeared from "8°30' to 9°45' of north latitude and 71° and 73° of west longitude", disappearing in the Catatumbo River.

Based on the reported information, the locations of the points from which the reported phenomenon would have appeared were established at the coordinates 8° 30’ N, 71° W and 9° 45’, 73° W.

Images: They were requested in writing on July 14, 2020, but were not provided by the user.

Conclusion: Since it originated in a situation outside of their control, the investigation was closed.
In accordance with Chapter 1 and numeral 2b of Chapter 2 (Study of cases occurred or registered in national territory), after defining the mission and function of the Committee, the case was closed.
