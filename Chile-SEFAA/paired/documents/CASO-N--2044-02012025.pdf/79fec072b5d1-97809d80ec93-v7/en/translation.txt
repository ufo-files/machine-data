                 A consideration of an aerial anomaly was reported, observed in a photograph
Summary: Taken at "10:00" a.m. on December 31, 2024, en route to El Salvador.

A white triangle, estimated to be 5 to 20 centimeters in size, was described, visible for 21 to 30 seconds by a witness. No details were given regarding its appearance in flight or the direction it was last seen.

Description: The witness reported: "I was taking photographs..."
User: Nighttime flight to El Salvador using the Pro mode of the Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra. I didn't notice the object until I was reviewing the photos; the object is static as the picture was taken in Pro mode, ISO 1600, at 20 speed.

Along with the report, the photographic file "20241231_220521.jpg" was entered.

The photograph that supported the report was perused, confirming its integrity.
This digital image confirms it was captured at 22:05 (HL) on 31 December 2024 using the camera of a Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra smartphone.

The photo was taken with an aperture of f1.7 and a shutter speed of 15 seconds at a visual angle of 76.1°.  It was also determined that the image was processed using Adobe XMP Core 5.5.0 software, which appears to be part of the phone's functionality.
It is presented in Figure 1 alongside a zoom of what was studied.

In an email dated January 3rd, the person who took it mentioned that it was located on the route from Copiapó to El Salvador, pointing towards "Las Tres Marías," supported on a tripod, and using a 5-second timer to capture the image.

Access to the El Salvador camp is through Route C-13, which comes from Diego de Almagro, 54 km away; the route C-17 arrives from there.

Comparing the case image with the astronomical situation allowed the permanent astronomer of SEFAA to identify what was reported as the planet Jupiter (Figure 4).
The luminous silhouettes in the photograph were distorted both by effect of
The aberrations of the lens in wide-angle configuration, such as due to slight movements of the camera. The pseudo-triangular shape that objects in the image take on is attributable to a "coma" type aberration, common in images of the night sky.

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, astronomical, and air traffic studies, Conclusion:
The reported was the planet Jupiter, whose silhouette was strongly deformed as it was very far from the center of the photograph.
