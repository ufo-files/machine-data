Case No. 1632 23082021

Date: August 14, 2021.

Approximate Time: 21:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: C. M., 34 years old.

Description: The informant reported that "for a few seconds" he saw multiple
air anomalies around 21:00 (LT) on August 14, 2021, from Route 27, in the
Monjes de la Pacana sector, on International Route 27-CH, Region of
Antofagasta. (Figure 1)

                                  Figure 1
The user described the reported sighting as "circular lights, approximately one meter in size and white in color, that seemed to be 30 meters away, 'floating on the road.'" He then added that "It was four lights. Two of them approached very quickly (one first and the other later), and when they were almost on top, they turned off. The other two stayed several kilometers back, moving from left to right and vice versa."

The user concluded his testimony by writing (sic): "Due to problems with my
I was buried four kilometers inside the road during the mobilization. Without light, a companion and I headed to the road to ask for help. We stood at the side waiting for a vehicle to ask for help, but instead of vehicles, these lights appeared and approached very quickly from east to west as they took the curve, almost illuminating our faces, and then they turned off. Both had the same behavior. There was no sound to determine if it was a vehicle engine, drone, or something else.
In any case, the speed at which it was moving does not correspond to any of these. In the distance (I do not know if it was on the hill or in the sky), two other lights were seen moving quickly from side to side, but these did not approach.

According to the report and the indication of where the reported object was seen, a study location was defined at the coordinates 23° 04’ 10’’ South Latitude, 67° 28’ 26’’ West Longitude, and 4,550 meters above sea level. (Figure 2)
