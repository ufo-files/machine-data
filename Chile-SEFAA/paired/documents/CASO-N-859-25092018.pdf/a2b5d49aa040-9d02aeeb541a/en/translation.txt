The case of the sighting of two aerial phenomena was reported.
Upon reviewing a series of three burst photographs taken from the lookout of
### Ahu Tahai in Rapa Nui

Summary:
At the time of the shots, neither the photographer nor those pictured noticed anything abnormal, but upon reviewing the images, they noticed the presence of a semi-oval phenomenon above a Navy ship. The second phenomenon was only noticed upon reviewing the images with a zoom.

Description:
They were taking photographs as part of a Navy civic operation.
The date and location of the sightings are defined. The metadata information is consistent with what was reported in the case narrative.

The authenticity of the images was examined through ELA (Error Level Analysis) and CMF (Copy and Motion Forgery) analysis, confirming that they have not been altered.

The edge analysis clearly identified the presence of the two phenomena.
It also allows indicating that they fly at a different angle with respect to the camera,
so the volume of one of them appears larger than the other.

A luminance analysis confirmed the presence of volume and the size difference between the two objects present in the images.
Research:

On the other hand, the metadata of the three images showed that all of them
The photographs were taken with the same parameters in the camera. Therefore, the differences in the focus of objects are attributable to changes in their positions with respect to the photographic lens and not to the photographic equipment or the photographer.

General air traffic was verified, which corresponded to the normal with large-scale commercial aircraft. For further details, contact was made with
### Airport Manager at Mataveri International Airport Inquiring About Other Craft Operations

The airport manager at Mataveri International Airport asked about the existence of other craft that had operated.

The Chilean Navy was consulted to determine if the fleet present at the time of the report had operated flying machines with characteristics comparable to the silhouettes photographed.

When comparing images of birds present on the days in Easter Island to the photographs,
The present signs in the photographs led to the hypothesis of a biological origin, not necessarily of a unique type.

Anonymized photographs were sent to the Wildlife Ecology Laboratory (LEVS) of the Faculty of Forest Sciences and Nature Conservation at the University of Chile.

The two objects (A and B) were located near the photographic target, reason for their inclusion in the analysis.

It moved away to the right and in its third position it appeared to fly over the ship's bridge, but it actually approached the camera's target as its volume noticeably increased and also the blurring of its silhouette.

When it was very close to the camera, the distances traveled in the movements were shorter than what could be evaluated if they were next to the ship.
The flight patterns and the fact that they were not noticed, neither by the photographer nor by those who were photographed, except upon reviewing the images, are consistent with them being insects that flew very close to the photographer.

Upon zooming in at 400%, object B exhibits characteristics typical of an insect, with beating wings discernible. If object A were much farther away, it would have a silhouette comparable to a bird in flight.
The technical analysis of the images allows us to indicate that the detected alterations
Conclusion: correspond either to the flight, or of two insects, or of an insect and a bird.
