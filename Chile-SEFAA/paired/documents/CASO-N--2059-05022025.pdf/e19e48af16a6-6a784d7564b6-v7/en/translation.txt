                 A report of what was considered two aerial anomalies was made by someone.
Summary: He went to the SEFAA and noticed while reviewing a video taken at "01:30" hours on January 10, 2025.
The reported sightings were described as two circular, colorless objects, ranging in size from 6 to 20 meters. This was visible for 31 to 60 seconds, flying from the south to southeast in a clear sky, witnessed by more than three people.

Testimony concluded with the statement: "It can be seen in the video. It doesn't allow me to upload it completely. There are two ships, as if one is on top of the other. It could be seen with the naked eye. In the video, yes."
Along with the report, the file "Screenshot_2025-02-05-00-03-03-359_com.miui.videop.jpg" was entered. Subsequently, the video "VID-20241215-WA0024(1).mp4" was delivered, whose 3,050 frames were used as study material.

Based on what is visible and for statistical purposes, the camera was located on Puerto Saavedra beach, corresponding to the coordinates 38° 46' 40° S, 73° 23' 43'' W.
The recorded were the evolutions of two paragliders facing the Sun which was immediately at the edge of the scene. Figure 1 shows frame 2421, where, according to the geometry of the image, the Sun originated a parasitic reflection or flare housed in a concave light halo. This also repeated towards the end of the filming as evidenced by frame 2133 of Figure 2.
Even without appearing in the picture, its light caused concave and convex light halos upon touching the camera lens. They are shown with arrows in Figure 64. (Figure 3)

With frame 1644, as shown in Figure 5, it is verified that these are reflections caused by the incidence of light on the camera lens. This was also consistent with the user's testimony: "It can be seen in the video. It doesn't let me upload it in full. There are two ships, as if they are one on top of the other. It was visible in plain sight. In the video, yes."

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic studies, it was concluded that what was reported were reflections caused by recording towards the Sun.
