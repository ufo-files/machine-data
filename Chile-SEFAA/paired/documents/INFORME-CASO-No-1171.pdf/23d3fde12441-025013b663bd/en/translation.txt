Case No. 1171 29072020

Date: February 16, 2020.

Approximate Time: 5:03 am, local time.

Weather: N/A

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: N. P., 27 years old.

Description: The user initially identified as N. J., without providing an email, reported through the CEFAA computer platform having witnessed a series of airborne objects at 05:03 (HL) on February 16, 2020, from Valparaíso.

He indicated that what was reported appeared in his view from the "Constellation" area.
"From Orion" to disappear to the north, with slow motion, adding that "But they knew where they were heading." According to what he stated, the situation would have lasted for 20 minutes.

He described what he saw in the following two ways:
a) 20 long-sized saucers separated into two groups.
b) Around 30 white points.

He added that the sky was "half-dark clear at that hour." He concluded his account as follows: "It happened in a surprising and wonderful way. At a point. Where everything aligned."
"It's like a puzzle. We're not alone, we've always been."
(sic)

Due to the absence of email, on July 29, 2020, a call was made to the user at the contact number provided, which was found to belong to someone who claimed to be his mother. This person indicated that the user's name was N. P. and provided the phone number of what seemed to be his residence. When interviewed by phone, he stated that he had no record of the information provided. He also refused to describe the reported incident or the circumstances under which he allegedly witnessed it.
A representative location of Cerro San Roque in Valparaíso was georeferenced with coordinates 33° 04' 27'' S, 71° 35' 07'' W and 300 meters above sea level, from which the meteorological and astronomical situation was studied at dawn on February 16, 2020.

The meteorological study was carried out with observations taken from the Rodelillo Aerodrome (SCTB) in Valparaíso, which confirmed that the night was clear. In addition, the astronomical observations revealed...
The astronomical study allowed knowing how the celestial vault presented itself at the reported moment and determined that the stars of the Orion constellation became invisible starting at 02:45 (HL), invalidating the reported information.

The information provided to CEFAA regarding date and time does not allow corroborating that the reported event is accurate. Additionally, when reporting, the user provided false identification and contact details that would not allow communication with them.

What was described could be originated in aerospace traffic, which has
Regularly occurring in the visible sky from Valparaíso, but for which a minimum of precision must be achieved at the time of reporting the observation.

Images: The user indicated not having any.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of what was reported and the geographic and astronomical studies, it was concluded that the report is not studyable based on the information provided to the Committee, so the file was closed according to the numbers 2a (Identification allowing contact with the reporter and delivery of information).
The minimum that allows the initiation of the investigation of what is reported) and 2d (Investigation of cases that have images, audios or videos) of the Technical Instruction Procedure, Investigation Times, Reception of Backgrounds, Preparation of Executive Report and CEFAA Report Archive.
Case classified as "False".
