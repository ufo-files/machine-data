Date: January 8, 2021.

Witness: A., 60 years old.

Description: The informant reported seeing an anomalous aerial phenomenon at 09:50 (LT) on Friday, January 8, 2021, from Ciudad Lineal, Madrid, Spain.

He described the reported sighting as the shape of a "paraglider", dark and the size of a "helicopter". He added that he saw it flying at a distance of about two hundred meters, with horizontal movement, but
in different directions. He lost sight of it to the northeast of his
location.
