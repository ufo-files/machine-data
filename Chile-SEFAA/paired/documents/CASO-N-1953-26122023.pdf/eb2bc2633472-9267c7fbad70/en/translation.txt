It is necessary to point out that what was observed originally comes from a camera located on-site approximately 412 meters from the observation, likely positioned from the roof of a building near the coastline and that transmitted a live feed. To those images, optical distortions caused by the image processor effect that transmits the image to the Video Wall of the television set along with the characteristics
Regarding the quantity, size, luminance threshold, and refresh rate of the pixels of the Video Wall. Finally, to all the aforementioned, the optical degradation caused by the computers on the YouTube platform was added.

The above described not only resulted in the resulting frames but also in the overall degradation of the visual quality due to the cumulative effects of each component.
