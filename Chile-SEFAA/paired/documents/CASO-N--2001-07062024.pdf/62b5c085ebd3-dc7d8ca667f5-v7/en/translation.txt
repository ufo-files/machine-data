A multiple air abnormalities were reported seen at "03:00" hours on May 28, 2024 over the skies of Santiago.
What was reported was described as black spheres, less than 5 centimeters in size, visible to three witnesses, for more than a minute flying from southeast to east in a completely cloudy sky.

Testimony: "Aunt called my brother on the phone saying there were things in the sky (she lives 2 blocks away). When we went to look, several objects (like 7) came by."
With shapes between triangles and small spheres, aligned like a train. Then,

Description:   they stopped and 4 of them formed a symmetrical formation among themselves;
another user/a:   remained apart with 2 smaller objects by his/her side. Both formations
                 impressed being symmetrical and not random (as seen in the attached video).
                 Upon detailed observation, they were not just 7, but many, because
They had numerous "black points" that "flickered" around the objects that were in formation.

Along with the report, an edited video file was entered and then the original footage was received in the file "20240528_151256.mp4".

The 1,382 frames of the video "20240528_151256.mp4" constituted the study material that supported the report. The footage was digitally perused.
Noting that it was not visually altered and that its capture occurred on May 28, 2024 at 15:12 hours using the main camera of a Samsung Galaxy A04 (SM-A045M) cellular phone.
Figure 1 shows the frame 20240528_1512560064 with what was reported in the investigation: the interior of a red circle.

the absence of flights in the area.

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the forensic, photographic, geographical, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that what was observed was consistent with the sighting of a group of birds moving at a great height.

Conclusion:
Height. Moreover, the reported "triangle" that "passes at high speed" corresponded to an insect that crossed the field of vision very close to the camera. Case classified as Explained.
