Witnesses: P. G. A., 41 years old.

Description: The informant reported having witnessed and recorded a
Abnormal aerial phenomenon at 02:25 on December 22, 2020,
from the commune of La Florida, Santiago Metropolitan Region.

He described what he saw as a gray circle, approximately 50
meters in diameter and located 1 kilometer southeast of his position.
He stated that “before recording the object, it described a horizontal
spiral advance, followed by a horizontal straight-line motion.” The
reported object had “no positioning lights” and flew silently. He added
that “Finally, the object described a
"It turned to its left and upward, to completely disappear."

Then he explained (sic) "that although the night was clear, I noticed that
in the vicinity of the object there was something of a mist or light fog
(I say this because in the video there is something of a light projection,
as if it were a spotlight)."

In his report, the user indicated that he is an official with Latam.

The meteorological conditions prevailing were analyzed based on...
The observations carried out from the Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport (SCEL) in Santiago.

During the period close to the sighting, the atmosphere presented very good visibility conditions. Horizontal visibility was at least 10 kilometers, with winds coming from the south, and although relative humidity in the air increased, ambient temperature remained well above the dew point, so there were no conditions for the formation of fog. Based on the user's location, air traffic visible to the southeast was studied between 01:00 and 03:00 on December 22, 2020. Although eight flights moved during this period, none of them were visible to the user, looking east, southeast, or south from their position. At the time of filming, at Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport, the Airbus 320-232, registration CC-BAM, was completing the LAN175 flight from...
Iquique's Diego Aracena Aerodrome (SCDA). This occurred behind and below the visible horizon for the user.

Upon analyzing aerospace traffic, it was verified that the synchronous heliocentric orbit overflight of the Chinese vector Long March 8 Y1 was visible, which took off from the Wenchang launch site in southern China at 12:37 (Beijing Time) on December 22, 01:37 in Santiago. (Figure 4)

This propulsion system bears the international identification 2020-
092B and the NORAD code 47232.

These ships measure 50.3 meters in length and have two stages.
They can reach up to 700 kilometers in altitude and are powered by six
liquid hydrogen, liquid oxygen, and kerosene engines. In accordance with ISO 24113:2019 (Space Systems.
(Requirements related to the reduction of space debris) and the United Nations Committee on the Peaceful Uses of Outer Space, the change of momentum from the first to the second stage requires that prior to the re-entry of stage 1 into the atmosphere, any remaining fuel be disposed of. The ignition of this pulverized material was interpreted by the witness as a glowing cloud.

According to the report by the "China National Space Administration" (CNSA), on this inaugural flight, the rocket reached
512 kilometers of altitude to then deploy the satellite Gaofen 14.
