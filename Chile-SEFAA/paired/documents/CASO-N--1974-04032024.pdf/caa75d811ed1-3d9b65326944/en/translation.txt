The reported information was described as two red spheres, with no indication of their
sizes, but they were visible for 31 to 60 seconds flying from south to northwest by a witness.

The user concluded by stating (sic): "I wanted to add the video I recorded, but the form does
not allow it due to the size. Where can I send them?"
