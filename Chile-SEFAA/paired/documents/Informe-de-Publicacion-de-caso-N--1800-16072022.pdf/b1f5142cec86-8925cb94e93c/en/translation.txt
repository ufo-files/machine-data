Date: July 13, 2022.

Approximate Time: 9:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: C. A. B. Z., 46 years old.

Description: The informant reported the presence of what he considered air anomalies in a series of photographs he took at 9:00 a.m. on July 13, 2022, from the commune of Independencia, in the Santiago Metropolitan Region.

The reported phenomenon was described without specifying a shape, but indicating that it was moving in a peculiar manner.
The size ranged from 6 to 20 meters and exhibited color changes. The witness added that the phenomenon was visible for 21 to 30 seconds, always to the east.

The informant concluded his account by stating (sic): "It was only visible when I saw the photos, I ruled out camera failures, focus, etc. Since they were taken with different filters."

Along with his report, seven photographic files were provided, whose details are presented in the third section of this report.

I was alone, taking quick photos of the moon, but with the naked eye there was nothing,
all the photos were taken with the Galaxy cell phone and each photo with a different filter,
there were more people, but no one saw anything, only later did I realize.
"After I saw the photos."

Images: The report was supported by seven JPG format photographs. Two of these images were altered, but showed no signs of visual elements being incorporated or removed from the subject.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the account and the results of the studies.
Geographical, forensic, and photographic analysis concluded that what was reported were parasitic reflections caused by incident light on the camera lenses used, which made the light sensor imprint them on the images. Since these optical aberrations only existed within the equipment, they were not seen by the photographer.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
