A phenomenon was reported that was associated with an aerial anomaly seen at "07:25" hours on 06 May 2024, from the commune of Peñalolén.
The reported sighting was described as LED lights, ranging in size from 20 to 100 meters, visible for 21 to 30 seconds as they flew from west to east, witnessed by three individuals.

User description: "A row of bright lights moving from west to east, suddenly disappeared."

The report included the file "VID-20240506-WA0000.mp4".
The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 33° 28’ 28’’ S, 70° 32’ 40’’ W and 665 m.s.n.m.

Figure 1 shows what is visible in frame 13 of the footage after equalization. A light configuration consistent with the testimony and congruent with a dense satellite transit was evident.

Investigation:

Figure 1
From the Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at the Cape Canaveral Space Force Station, United States, the Starlink 6-57 mission was launched. This was the thirty-third Starlink mission of the year, consisting of V2-type spacecraft.

The presence of the following spacecraft listed according to their North American Aerospace Defense Command (NORAD) identifier was confirmed:
59667, 59669, 59670, 59672, 59665, 59658, 59659, 59662, 59657, 59660,
59656, 59655, 59664, and 59661.

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, and aerospace traffic studies, it was concluded that the reported event was the satellite overflight of spacecraft belonging to the Starlink G6-57 mission of the SpaceX company. Conclusion: The case was classified as Explained.
