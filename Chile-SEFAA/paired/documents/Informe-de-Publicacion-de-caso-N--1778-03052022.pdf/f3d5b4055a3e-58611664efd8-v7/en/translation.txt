Date: April 23, 2022.

Approximate Time: 3:29 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Autumn.

Witnesses: K. T. O. V., 39 years old.

Description: The informant reported having photographed what he considered four
anomalies in the sky at 3:29 a.m. on April 23, 2022, from the Tambillo Mine
industrial facilities in the Coquimbo Region.

No specific details were provided about their shape, color, or size, but they
were said to appear for 1 to 5 seconds to the east of a witness.
The user concluded by explaining that (sic): “I (...) was performing an inspection of HDPE pipes in the waste yard when I looked up at the night sky and noticed the moon appearing spectacularly. I took out my phone without a case to capture an image, made adjustments in pro mode to get a better shot, left it on a surface to avoid vibration, and took about 5 photos with different settings.

To my surprise, when I reviewed the images, I found this distortion.”
In one of the images, the shape of it caught my attention.
Continuing through the night, I approached the filtered tailings plant to verify the change in the feed pipe to the tailings filters, and when I photographed the change (23-04-2022 05:12), an object again appeared in the image, with the same hues as the previous one, but in the shape of a saucer. Normally, when I am on night shift, I make rounds within the plant, but on that particular night,
Given the clear sky, moon shape, and planet alignment, I prepared to try and photograph them."

The four images that supported the report were perused using computer programs, confirming that they were not digitally altered.
The analysis of the information contained within them determined that they were taken using the main camera of a Samsung Galaxy A51 smartphone between 03:28 and 05:12 hours on the reported date, and that the visible portions of the first three were edited using Microsoft Windows Photo.
On May 3, 2022, shortly before being reported.

These changes only had cosmetic effects, so the study of the reported continued.

Upon studying the geometries of the images, it was determined that in each one of
them, what was reported was parasitic reflections or "flares" caused by light beams
that struck the camera lens and originated when photographing directly at the Moon
and three illumination foci of the industrial installation that was depicted.
Images: As study material, the case includes four JPG format photographs shown above in this report.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the witnessed and the results of the geographical, forensic, and photographic studies, it was concluded that what was reported were reflections or "flares". These were due to parasitic incident light that entered the interior of the camera used and that originated when photographing directly at the Moon and taking a backlit image of three lighting sources.
Factory installation depicted. What was reported was not seen directly, as it only existed within the cell phone used.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
