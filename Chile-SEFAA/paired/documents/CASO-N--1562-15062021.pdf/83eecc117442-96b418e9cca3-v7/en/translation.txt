The witness reported what he considered an aerial anomaly, seen approximately at "21:30" hours on 15 June 2021, from Route 5 Sur, in the Santiago Metropolitana Region.
The reported object was described as a "rhombus-shaped" entity, "orange, luminescent", resembling a "brilliant, fiery-red metal object", at a distance of "200 meters" and with a size comparable to "10 meters", directly observed by a witness for "30 seconds". It was added that the object flew "slowly, no more than 100 km/h" from south-southeast to north-northwest in a partly cloudy sky.
It concluded by testifying that (sic): "I am driving on the 5 South road from north to south, around 21:30 hrs. on Tuesday, June 15, 2021, at approximately 100 Km/Hr."
Suddenly on the road, at the height of San Bernardo, about 50 meters to the left and about 200 meters high, comes from the northwest a luminous object.
User: Orange, in a rhombus shape, flying slowly. Passes over the road.
"It remained vertical along my path. I lost sight of it as it was coming on a motorcycle, and unfortunately I couldn't record it on any device. It's very likely that more people saw it, as it clearly contrasted with the night sky."

"No audiovisual file was entered with the report."

"For statistical purposes, a location was georeferenced according to the testimony."
In emails dated June 16th and 22nd, 2021, the user was contacted and requested to provide images to aid in studying and obtaining greater accuracy regarding the sighting location.

Figure 1

While awaiting images, an aerial examination was conducted, revealing no aircraft with an active transpondrer originating from the south of San Bernardo on the reported date. The closest aircraft coming from the south was the Pilatus PC-12 NG (Figure 2).
### Figure 2

As shown in Figure 3, the aircraft was near San Bernardo at 21:57 (HL). Shortly after, it landed at Arturo Merino Benítez Airport (SCEL).
