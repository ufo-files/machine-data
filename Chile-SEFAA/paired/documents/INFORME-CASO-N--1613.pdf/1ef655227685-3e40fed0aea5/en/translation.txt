Date: August 9, 2021.

Approximate Time: 13:50 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: G. Z., 60 years old.

Description: The informant reported seeing what he considered an aerial anomaly for 15 minutes at 15:50 (HL) on August 9, 2021 (due to a lapse, he initially gave the date as September 9, 2021, which was in the future at that time) from Puente Alto, in the Santiago Metropolitan Region.
The user described the reported information as a bright light that resembled a star, of undetermined size and "shiny silver" color, flying statically at a distance he could not calculate. He concluded his testimony by stating he saw: "A bright apparatus that was impossible to specify a shape, remained almost static in the sky."
