Case No. 1212 10082020

Date: April 21, 2020.

Approximate Time: 19:30 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Autumn.

Witnesses: V. R., 32 years old.

Description: The informant reported witnessing an anomalous aerial phenomenon between 19:00 and 19:30 (HL) on Tuesday, April 21, 2020, from Puerto Varas, Los Lagos Region. He described what he saw as having the shape of a boomerang and translucent coloration. He added that it flew silently and horizontally from southeast to northwest.
In an email, the user indicated that there could be an
hour error in the reported time and specified that the transparency of
the object he saw "seemed to bend light around it, in a way
deforming the vision of the sky above it. If there was a slight
tone change between the sky and the object (slightly grayer than the
dark sky)."

He added that he did not have audiovisual footage of the incident.

Images: In the email, he stated that he did not have audiovisual
footage of the incident.
Conclusion: The case was closed according to numeral 2d (Study of cases with audiovisual antecedents) of chapter 2 of the Technical Instruction Procedure, Research Times, Reception of Antecedents, Preparation of Executive Report and CEFAA Report Archive.

Case classified as Report.
