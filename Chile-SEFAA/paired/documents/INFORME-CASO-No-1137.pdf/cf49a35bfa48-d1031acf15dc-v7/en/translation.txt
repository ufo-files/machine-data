Season: Summer.

Witnesses: M. V. U. C., 34 years old.

Description: The witness reported the presence of an anomaly in flight in a photograph taken at 15:12 (HL) on January 10, 2020, on the shore of the Laguna del Laja, located in the national park of the same name in the Andean precordillera of the Biobío region.

In her report, the user indicated that they did not notice anything.
abnormal, only perceiving what was reported upon reviewing the photographs taken that day.

The image provided with the case report was perused through ELA (Error Level Analysis) and CMF (Copy and Motion Forgery) analysis, confirming it showed no digital alterations.

The geographical study of the case confirmed it was taken on the riverbank of the reported location, approximately at coordinates 37°22' 38'' S, 71°22' 11'' W.

The examination of the data within its metadata revealed...
that the photograph was taken at the reported time, using the main camera of a Huawei cellular phone, model Y6 (MRD-LX3), with a shutter speed of 1/702 s and the maximum aperture for the equipment: F 1.8.

Independent advice was sought from specialists in entomology at the National Museum of Natural History. (15 July 2020)

The advisors and entomologists reported that a flying insect was photographed. According to one of them, because of
Sub-spherical volume and some orange areas that he could distinguish in the image, would be a specimen of the Order Himenoptera, possibly a species of wasp. (22 July 2020)

Images: One archive photograph IMG_20200110_151222.jpg.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the reported narrative, the photographic study, and the independent results achieved by the two consulted entomological advisors, it was concluded that the photographed subject corresponded to a Himenoptera insect of the family.
Apidae in flight, a species of bumblebee (Bombus sp.) that could not be determined due to the characteristics of the available image.
