In addition to what was stated in the reported account, the technical analysis of the images suggests that the circular shape and its trail might be due to reflections on the windshield from the vehicle from which the recordings were made.
Conclusion: The reported luminous phenomena corresponded to reflections caused by the incidence of light on the car's windshield, dirt and imperfections on it, and the camera's lens system through which the images were taken.
