Case No. 1793 30062022

Date: January 15, 1984

Approximate Time: 21:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: S. G. D. S., 54 years old.

Description: The informant reported seeing what he considered an
aerial anomaly at approximately 21:00 on Sunday, January 15, 1984, from
the city of Antofagasta, in the same-named region.

What was reported was described as a white sphere between 20 and 100 meters
in size. This object would have moved from the east towards the north.
It was observed for 11 to 20 seconds by two witnesses.

The user concluded by writing (sic): "A bright sphere appeared suspended over the sea, approximately 1 km away, then rose almost instantaneously to approximately 100, then descended at the same speed to approximately 200 m, subsequently returned to the center and exited at a speed never seen before today towards the North."

The case only has one illustration of the witness, which is shown in the "Images" section of this report, later on.
According to the user's indication, a study location was taken in the center of the longitudinal axis of the roof of a building in the city of Antofagasta corresponding to the geographic coordinates 23° 40’ 32’’ Latitude
South, 70° 24' 15" West Longitude, at 184 meters above sea level and 994
meters from the coastline. (Figure 1)
