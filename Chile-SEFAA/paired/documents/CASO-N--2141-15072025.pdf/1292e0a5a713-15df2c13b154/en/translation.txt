Regarding air traffic, at the time and date of the video, two flights were detected: LAN043, originating from Santiago (SCEL) and destined for Valdivia (SCVD), and LXP222, flying from Puerto Montt (SCTE) to Concepción (SCIE).

Figure 4 shows that LAN043 was performed by the Airbus A320 with registration CC-COL, which was at an altitude of 34,983 feet (10,662 meters) and descending.
At a rate of 256 feet per minute and heading south (196°).

The individual aircraft identified as LXP 222 was the Airbus 320 registered CC-BHL,
at a lower altitude of 19,790 feet (6,032 meters) and advancing at 317 knots (587 km/h) with a heading of north (357°) (Figure 5).

Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographic, forensic, photographic, and air traffic studies, it was determined that the reported event corresponded to the intersection at altitude of two air traffic streams. These streams were separated vertically by 4,630 meters. It should be noted that, due to the atmospheric conditions at the time, only one of the aircraft generated a condensation trail.
