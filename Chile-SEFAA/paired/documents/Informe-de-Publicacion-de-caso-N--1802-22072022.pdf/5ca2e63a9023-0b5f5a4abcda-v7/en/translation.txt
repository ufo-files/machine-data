Case No. 1802 22072022

Date: 31 August 2021.

Approximate Time: 7:30 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: J. F. A., 58 years old.

Description: The informant reported the presence of what he considered air anomalies in a photograph he took at 7:30 a.m. on Tuesday, 31 August 2021, while traveling as a passenger on a commercial flight between Temuco and Santiago.

The user described them as two "saucers" ranging from 6 to 20 meters in diameter.
He characterized its coloration as “other.” He added that these objects were visible for 1 to 5 seconds, to the west, without indication of where he had lost sight of them.

He concluded by writing (sic) “It's a photo I took last year on a flight from Temuco to Santiago... while reviewing the photos today, it caught my attention... what do you think?”

Along with the report, he uploaded a photographic file in jpg format. (Figure 1)

Figure 1. The reported phenomenon is shown enclosed in a celestial circle.
Attached to an email on July 26, the user sent the original files of the five photos he took during his trip, which include the first image received by SEFAA. All were incorporated into the case study.
The photographer's location was georeferenced at coordinates 36° 23’ 18’’ South Latitude and 71° 54’ 40’’ West Longitude, at an elevation of 10.356 meters above sea level. (Figure 2)

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the
Geographical, forensic, and photographic studies determined that what was reported were parasitic reflections, or "flares," caused within the photographic equipment when capturing a sunset from an airplane in flight against the light.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
