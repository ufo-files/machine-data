Season: Winter.

Witnesses: S. M. G.

Description: The informant reported having seen, photographed, and filmed an anomalous aerial phenomenon around 07:30 (HL) on Saturday, 22 August 2020, from the city of Concepción,
Biobío Region.

He described what was reported as “a white figure that seemed like a combustion trail of something that was stationary, which wasn't there before, and started moving quickly from west to east at a steady speed, without altering its elongated shape throughout the space I could observe.”

Based on the user's account, its location was georeferenced. From this location, the meteorological conditions and possible air traffic visibility were studied.

The meteorological conditions were analyzed based on the
Observations carried out from the Carriel Sur (SCIE) Airport in Concepción, approximately 6 kilometers from the user.

According to this information, there were particularly favorable conditions for aerial observation. Between 07:00 and 08:00 on August 22, there was visibility of at least 10 kilometers, no clouds below 5,000 feet, and no cumulonimbus or significant meteorological phenomena. There were winds from the south-southwest with a speed of 8 to 7 knots.
ambient temperature that varied from 7°C to 8°C, always above
the dew point.

This was consistent with what was shown by the supporting audiovisual material for the reported narrative.

Upon studying the air traffic over Concepción, it was determined
that, although the city could have seen the landing of the Airbus A320-271N aircraft, registration CC-AWM, which performed the JAT231 flight by JetSmart Airlines from the Andrés Sabella Airport in Antofagasta, it did not correspond to the reported sighting.
According to both the user's location and the photos provided to the Committee, the Carriel Sur Airport was behind them.

To verify the hypothesis that what was reported was an aircraft that had flown to the east of the city of Concepción, a study of air traffic conditions was requested from an air traffic controller who is an advisor to the CEFAA.

The advisor reported that at the time in question, there was a virtually simultaneous overflight of two aircraft to the east of Concepción that were heading south.
Images: The user provided a total of eleven photos and one
MOV file in the archive.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the report, the geographic, forensic, and air traffic studies, and the independent air traffic investigations conducted by the consulted advisor, it was concluded that what was photographed corresponded to condensation trails caused by the passage of the LXP273 flight by Latam Airlines or SKU9389 by Sky Airlines.
