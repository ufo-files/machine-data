from 5 to 20 cm in size, visible for over a minute, flying from south to north.
The witness concluded by stating: "During a night watch shift at the community
camera hub, I noticed spherical lights appearing in front of the security camera,
located between Chacabuco Street and José Antonio Salinas Street. I recorded a
video of approximately 10 minutes from their appearance until they disappeared."
"In which I became aware of the presence of these spheres around 5 minutes before they disappeared from view."

The reported phenomenon had its first manifestations in frames 72 to 221, 842 to 1,504, and 1,848 to 1,876. Its last presence was verified in frame 13,814. It was observed as up to three white circles or ellipses, with apparent densities that varied according to the level of focus achieved by the camera, but which remained very close to it. (Figure 3)

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the
geographical, forensic, and photographic studies, it was concluded that what
was reported was the evolution of an arachnid in front of the security camera
while it was swaying in its web. Case classified as "Explained."
