The user enters the case through the CEFAA web platform with a report of a
Summary:   Nighttime aerial phenomenon in the port of San Antonio. Accompanies the report of 5 witnesses.
Videos. RPA flight.
It reports that while it was in the coastal sector of the San Antonio port, watching the entry of a vessel to the pier at dusk, a red light, like a flare, suddenly appeared in the sky.

In the same line, it comments that the object suddenly stopped and changed its color (green and yellow), beginning to move horizontally.
Subsequently, move in a pendulum form emitting a strobe light.

He adds that he made recordings with his cellular phone.

1. All material is sent to the external consultant with the most experience in
   filmings.

Investigation:

2. The image expert states that the motion and lighting pattern correspond to the
   flight of a RPA (Remotely Piloted Aircraft).
Conclusion: The filmed object corresponds to a RPA (Remotely Piloted Aircraft).
