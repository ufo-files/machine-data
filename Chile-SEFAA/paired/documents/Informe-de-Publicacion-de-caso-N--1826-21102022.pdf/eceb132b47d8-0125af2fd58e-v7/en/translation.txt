Case No. 1826 21102022

Date: March 20, 2022.

Approximate Time: 11:40 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: H. A. S. V., 49 years old.

Description: On Friday, October 21, 2022, the SEFAA received through its web platform a report submitted by the informant, aged 49, to report that he had captured a supposed anomalous aerial phenomenon while testing a photographic camera on March 20, 2022, at 10:39 hours, from his residence in Temuco, in the Region of La Araucanía.
The informant did not specify a shape or size for what was reported, but indicated
that it was colorless and was seen for between 1 to 5 seconds and heading east.

“Testing a camera in March 2022, I took several photos of the clouds (east) and
other objects, reviewing them now in October, I observe an anomaly among the
clouds that has 6 sides and linear edges. The sun was in a north orientation,”
the user wrote.

Along with his report, he uploaded the image file IMG_1981.JPG.
The location of the informant was georeferenced at the time the photograph was taken in a residential area of Villa Parque Estadio, in Temuco, in the Region of La Araucanía. (Figure 1)

The photograph that supported the report was perused using computer programs, confirming that it was not digitally altered. The examination of the information contained in the file determined that it was taken on the reported date, but at 11:40:02, not at 10:39 hours, although the above could be...
It had to do with a configuration error in the device's clock.

The information contained in the file also showed that the image was
obtained with a Canon photographic camera, model EOS Digital Rebel Xsi.

Upon examining the reported photograph and zooming in, a semi-transparent
hexagonal shape of orange color is noticed. (See figures 2 and 3).

     Figure 2. The image that backed up the case. The reported is indicated with the red arrow.
Figure 3. The reported image enlarged 300 times, where its hexagonal shape and the lines of that geometric figure are noted.

On Monday, October 24, 2022, an email was sent to the user, asking, among other things, if they had seen the reported phenomenon with the naked eye. In their response that same night, the informant stated they had not seen the reported phenomenon with the naked eye, but only after recently reviewing photos taken with that camera.
The above raised the thesis that what was reported was an optical artifact produced inside the camera that generated an image with that shape due to the camera's diaphragm. (See Figure 4)

Figure 4. The graph showing the diaphragm opening of photographic cameras displays the hexagonal shape of the same, which is the one appreciated in the sent image.

Being in that position facilitated the generation of the optical artifact within the
photographic camera.

Images: As support for the report, a 3.23 MB JPG photograph was received.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and geographical, forensic, and
photographic studies, it was concluded that the reported anomalous aerial phenomenon
in the image sent by the informant actually corresponded to a
A reflection or optical artifact generated within the photographic camera due to the incidence of solar rays on its diaphragm.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained".
