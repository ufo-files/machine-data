Case No. 1230 17082020

Date: July 17, 2020.

Approximate Time: 17:25 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: D. C., 25 years old.

Description: The informant reported having witnessed an anomalous aerial phenomenon at 17:25 (HL) on Friday, July 17, 2020, from the town of Nacimiento, Biobío Region. He indicated that what was reported was several kilometers away from his position, flying horizontally, perceived as a
A white light triangle that left a trail in its wake. He specified
that it was always found to the north of his location.

Awaiting the availability of study means, air traffic was analyzed
with an active transpondering that had flown north of the city of
Nacimiento between 17:00 and 18:30 (HL) on July 17 (21:00 and 22:30
UTC). The passage of four commercial flights was verified, with none
of them near Nacimiento at the reported time.

Without any graphic means that allowed the definition of both
silhouette of the aerial phenomenon and confirm the time it was visible, it
was not possible to validate if any of the aircraft described above could
have been the origin of the reported event.

Images: In an email on August 18, the user indicated not possessing any
means of studying the reported event.

Conclusion: The case was closed as the witness did not provide any images
nor confirmed the exact time of the observation. Considering the above,
the case was closed according to numeral 2d (Study of cases with
backgrounds).
(audiovisual) from Chapter 2 of the Technical Instruction Procedure,
Research Times, Background Reception, Executive Report Preparation
and CEFAA Report Archive.

Case classified as a Report.
