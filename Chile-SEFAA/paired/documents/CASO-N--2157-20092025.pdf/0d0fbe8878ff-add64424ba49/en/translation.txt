The user's location was georeferenced (Figure 1).
Based on what is visible and the geographical context, it was defined that the camera was pointing towards the southwest (Figure 2).

What is visible in the footage are three small white objects, apparently spherical and distant from the camera, flying slowly. Considering that it was testified that the filming was directed towards the southwest, overall they moved towards the south or south-southwest. Figure 3 includes frame 87 along with equal magnifications of the three objects under study.

This aircraft was unable to leave a visible trace of what was portrayed.

The meteorological conditions were studied using observations made from Arturo Merino Benítez Airport (SCEL). From the airport, it was indicated that between 12:00 and 13:00 (HL) there was a visibility of at least 10 kilometers; no clouds below 1,500 meters, cumulonimbus or weather phenomena.
A significant surface wind was observed with variable origins and intensities ranging from 2 to 3 knots (3.7 to 5.6 km/h). More detailed information between 12:00 and 14:00 hours is as follows:

The wind conditions at altitude were analyzed using data from the weather balloon issued by the Chilean Meteorological Directorate.
The Meteorological Station at Santo Domingo Airport (SCSN). The balloon sonde equipment that passed over the Metropolitan Region indicated that, at around 110 meters altitude, there were light winds, 2 knots, southerly-southeasterly (200°). At around 700 meters, they reached 5 knots and shifted to north-northeasterly, and near 1,000 meters, these winds intensified to 10 knots and were north-northwest (345°) (Figure 6).

Figure 7 confirmed that the Sun was at an azimuth of 28.5° and an apparent elevation of 51.7°. This was consistent with the illumination of what was observed and the cables that were captured.

This was consistent with what was captured being a free flight of balloons dragged by the winds at the time. This situation is similar to other cases studied previously by SEFAA, as shown in Figure 8, which
They were balloons filmed from the commune of Puente Alto, or as presented in Figure 9, balloons associated with a funeral in the city of Talca in December 2024.

According to the analysis of the testimony and as a result of geographical studies,
The forensic, photographic, astronomical, meteorological, and air traffic transit analyses concluded.
Conclusion: It is highly likely that what was reported were balloons dragged by the wind. There were no conditions that could imply a danger to the existing air operations at that time.
