Enter the case of a recording made with your cell phone of an aerial phenomenon.
A bright light was seen on the night of December 19, 2017 over the skies of Santiago. By the
Summary:
He/she declared that the perfect speed and alignment did not seem possible to be conventional air traffic.

While at a party in the San Cristóbal Hill sector, he/she saw in the horizon a formation of blinking white lights, and everyone told him/her that they were formation planes, but the user dismissed it due to the alignment and speed of these lights.
The initial analysis of the footage shows a set of white lights, which
appear to flicker in the distance and due to the filming speed, can be interpreted
as a single large object.

Backgrounds are sent to the CEFAA advisors.
Investigation:
The EMOL journalistic archive for December 2017 is investigated, finding a
publication on the 27th that reports the graduation of the
The 2014-2017 promotion of the Aviation School occurred in the night of the user's
recording.

Both the characteristics and opportunity of the recording, as well as the background
information gathered from the case, indicate that the reported aerial phenomenon
corresponded to the night flight of the Aviation School graduation ceremony in the year
2017.
