Location: White Beach Region: Coquimbo Classification: Resolved The user entered the case through the CEFAA web platform with a report of a
Associated Anomaly with Air Phenomena Photographs in Playa Blanca, IV

Summary:
Region. He accompanied the account with three photographs showing a dark, opaque spot associated with the Sun.

User description:

He observed the object once he opened the file on his computer.

Investigation:

The authenticity of the images was verified through ELA (Error Level Analysis) and CMF (Copy and Movement Forgery). Edge detection indicated the presence of an anomaly whose shape tends towards a circle.
It was determined that the main camera of the cellular phone, a Motorola MotoC model, was used, and its optical characteristics were verified.

The technical analysis of the images allowed to determine that the detected alterations corresponded to an electronic effect in the images, product of the (CMOS) sensor of the camera that the Motorola MotoC model cellular phone with which the photograph was taken had, and which was caused by light oversaturation.
