Case No. 1727 21122021

Date: December 21, 2021.

Approximate Time: 1:51 a.m., local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: L. V., informed by F. C. Z.

Description: The informant reported the sighting of what would be
anomalies that his wife had captured at 01:51 on December 21, 2021 in the city of Iquique, in the Region of Tarapacá.

The user claimed the sighting lasted between 1 to 5 seconds, that both
objects he reported had a "biological form," and measured between 20
In centimeters and one meter, they showed "color change". He ended up writing (sic): "Photo of my wife, Iquique, piece 2 more entities".

Along with the above, the user entered two photographs in JPG format. Based on the address he provided, the case was georeferenced at a residence in the city of Iquique, in the Region of Tarapacá.

The analysis of the information contained in the photographs demonstrated that they had been digitally altered.

Additionally, the images provided by the user to support the presence of
"Entities" contain drawings available for free download on internet sites for image sharing.

Figures 1 and 2 demonstrate this, as they are the same entities that appear in the photos sent by the informant.

Figure 1. Image extracted from the internet and used to compose one of the photos sent.

Figure 2. Image taken from the internet and used for one of the photographic compositions.

Originally, that entity is a character from the video game XCOM: Enemy Unknown (2012).

Conclusion: According to the study of the report and forensic examinations of the images sent to SEFAA, it was determined that the user sent for analysis two digital compositions made by adding available Internet elements of supposed extraterrestrials to two photos of the interior of a residence.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "False."
