Case No. 1896 30062023

Date: June 29, 2023.

Approximate Time: 20:50 local time.

Location: La Serena, Coquimbo Region.

Witnesses: M.N.C.

Images: User attached 4 photos and 4 videos.

Description: The reported sighting was described as a light that changed colors and was visible for more than a minute, always to the west of two witnesses and near another that appeared static. No specific sizes or distances were provided.

It concluded by stating (sic): "Good morning, along with greetings, I attach videos and
I take photographs which I will explain. I live in Serena at the end of four corners on the big hill, from there I see all of Serena and Coquimbo, on Thursday June 29/06/2023 at 8:50 pm on the horizon next to that star, a quiet light is seen that turns on and off, lasting approximately half an hour to 45 minutes static in the place (I don't know if it's the movement of the planet Earth that causes the reference star to descend to the horizon or the light rises over the star), giving very strong to weak pulses of light and then very
strong, along with this from north to south at a lower level (I attach a photo with the trajectory traced with blue pencil paste) a small red light (similar to a coal braze) is seen, which moves at a constant speed and slows down as it reaches the white land area, on that object the upper red light remains and below a bright white light appears that does not illuminate the ground and is intermittent, this object stops over that white land area, turns, and synchronizes with the other light.
It is on the horizon in its intermittence. These two lights, already synchronized, disappear as if one turns off a light bulb. In the longer video, both lights are visible, and when they disappear, in the photos, you can see that at second 00.52, that small light is advancing. Expecting a prompt response, he signs off. Mauricio Nehme. Dear, you couldn't upload the videos because they are larger than 50mb, to which email can I send them?"

All of the above pointed to the moving bright object being this plane. From the user's perspective, its movement was from left to right, horizontally and quickly, but with constant acceleration. The recorded differences in altitude and speed have amplitudes of 15 meters and 19 km/h, respectively.
On the other hand, as it moved diagonally, not only could its navigation and collision avoidance lights be associated with becoming fainter and fainter, but it also presented them at different angles with respect to the witness.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographic, forensic, astronomical, photographic, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that the reported sighting originated from the presence of Venus and the overflight of the Airbus A320-233 aircraft, which performed the LAN522 flight on June 29, 2023.
