Case No. 1387 03012021

Date: November 19, 2020.

Approximate Time: 11:30 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Spring.

Witnesses: J. R. R., 47 years old.

Description: The informant reported the presence of what he considered an
aerial anomaly at 11:30 a.m. on Thursday, November 19, 2020, in Puerto Aysén,
in the Aysén del General Carlos Ibáñez del Campo Region.

The user described the reported information as a small, white sphere that flew
rapidly from south to west for ten seconds. He concluded by saying
"An object appears in the vapor left by a plane crossing the city of Aysén."

In an email dated January 4, 2021, he added: "Good morning, I have the original video, I want to clear up what I recorded, it could be a bird, a fly or anything else, it's very fast even in slow motion."

The user's address was considered as their location at the time of filming, corresponding to the town of Puerto Aysén. (Figure 1)

Figure 1
Starting from that location, air traffic that could have been detected around the reported time was reviewed. As Figure 2 shows, two planes passed from south to north and west of Puerto Aysén between 11:17 and 11:30 hours:

• SKU003 flight by SkyAirlines between Puerto Montt and Punta Arenas, performed by an Airbus 320-251N aircraft. At 11:17 hours, it was traveling at 456 knots, stabilized at 39,000 feet and on a southerly course (180°).

• LAN9575 flight by LATAM from Santiago to Punta Arenas, a Boeing 787-8.
At 11:30 a.m., it was advancing at 495 knots, stabilized at 41,000 feet and on a southerly course (187°).

Although it is possible that the aircraft in the images was the LATAM Boeing, without greater precision on the time when the images were actually obtained, it was not possible to verify which of the two aircraft was filmed.

Despite repeated requests for the original footage of the incident, the only audiovisual material received is contained in the edited archive.
denoted as “received_174029667744404.mp4”.

frames. The following Figure 3 shows a representative selection of this

The meteorological conditions prevailing around the time indicated by the user were studied based on records from the station of the Chilean Meteorological Directorate (DMC) in Puerto Aysén, located 1.9 kilometers east of the user at the Cabo 1° Juan Román (SCAS) aerodrome.

Between 11:20 and 11:40 hours, the average temperature was 19 °C and winds blew at 9 knots from the south-southwest.
Although the low quality of the images did not allow for a full identification of the objects, the degree of blur in their silhouettes and the apparent high flight speed was consistent with them being small and passing close to the phone camera that recorded it.

Additionally, the user indicated that what was reported "appears in the vapor left by an airplane crossing the city of Aysén" and that the objects under study were very little visible despite being in static images (frames).
It is highly improbable that what was reported was perceived directly, as the cameraman's attention would be focused on the aircraft and its condensation trail.

On the other hand, based on the meteorological conditions and the flight pattern, it was considered highly probable that the first object was a bird or insect; and that the second possibly originated from a biological source, an insect, or a small object dragged by the wind.

Images: Due to the lack of other study material, the case was analyzed with
A video file contained in an edited and MP4 format.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, meteorological, photographic, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that there is a high probability that what was reported corresponded to the flight of a bird or a small insect that passed very close to the camera that captured the video that originated the case and that was interpreted as coming from the condensation trail of an airplane that flew over Puerto Aysén.
Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Probably Explained."
