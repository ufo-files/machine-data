Case No. 1769 30032022

Date: March 28, 2022.

Approximate Time: 21:45 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Autumn.

Witnesses: A. R. P.

Description: The informant reported having seen an aerial anomaly at 21:45 hours on March 28, 2022 from the commune of Pudahuel, in the Metropolitan Region of Santiago.

What was reported was described as a "pure" white object, with no information given about its size, but it was visible for 6 to 10 seconds flying from west to east.
The user concluded his report stating that (sic): "I was watering the patio of the house when around 21:45 I saw a long light, flying in a straight line from W to E, I saw it at an approximate angle of 45° from my position, towards the north. At first, I thought it could have been a Starlink train but it was compact, not a series of lights in formation; I also estimate it could have been some object reentering the atmosphere. I didn't manage to grab my cell phone to record the event."
The user's location was georeferenced in coordinates in the commune of Pudahuel, in the Santiago Metropolitan Region.
