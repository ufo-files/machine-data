Case No. 1723 21122021

Date: December 21, 2021.

Approximate Time: 00:48 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: L.V., informed by F. C. Z.

Description: The informant reported the sighting of what would be an
anomaly, and that he had photographed his wife at 00:48 on December 21,
2021 in the city of Iquique, in the Region of Tarapacá.

He indicated that the sighting lasted between 1 to 5 seconds, that what was
reported had a "biological form," measured between 20 centimeters and one
meter, and was
Silver. He concluded his testimony by writing (sic): "Photo of my wife in Iquique in her room" (as author, not as subject).

In addition to the above, he submitted a photograph in JPG format.

Based on the address provided by the user, the location of the case was georeferenced to a residence in Iquique, in the Region of Tarapacá.

The analysis of the information contained in the metadata of the sent photograph demonstrated that it had been digitally altered.
The supposed extraterrestrial present in the image sent appears in other photographs sent by the same informant and corresponds to a race called "Sectoid" from the fictional video game XCOM: Enemy Unknown (2012),
As seen in the comparison in Figure 1.

Figure 1. On the left, the photo sent by the user. On the right, the same entity in the video game XCOM: Enemy Unknown.

Conclusion: According to the study of the report and the forensic examinations of the image that the informant sent to SEFAA, it was determined that he reported the presence of an anomaly with a digital drawing that was created by adding to a photograph an element originally available in a video game.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "False."
