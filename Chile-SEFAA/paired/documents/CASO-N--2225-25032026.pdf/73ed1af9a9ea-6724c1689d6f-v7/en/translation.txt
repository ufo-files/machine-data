Although, due to the incomplete metadata of the case video, the reported date and time could not be verified, the provided date and time were used to consult air traffic. Thus, it was determined that what was seen in the video had no relation to the air activity typical of Santiago Airport.
According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that it is highly probable that what was filmed were free-flying balloons, dragged by a single mass of air. Case classified as Probably Explained
