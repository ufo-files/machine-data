Case Number: 1533 29042021

Date: April 29, 2021.

Approximate Time: 19:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Autumn.

Witnesses: J. P., 34 years old.

Description: The witness reported that, for one minute, at 19:00 (HL) on April 29, 2021, he saw and was able to film multiple anomalous phenomena in flight from Linares, Region of Maule.

He indicated that he observed more than 30 "luminescent spheres," of a color "brilliant white." He declared that they flew, from south to north, at an unknown altitude, at a high speed, and without producing sound.
In an email dated May 4, the user stated that what he saw was close to a 45° azimuth. He insisted that the movement was linear, from south to north and at an altitude lower than a commercial flight.

Figure 1 is frame 71 of the case video. Directly, everything present in the extracts is not completely visible without using very high-resolution screens. In this study, they were analyzed through the variations in luminance present in their pixels.
The support footage file for the report was perused, confirming it contains no signs of digital alteration. Analysis of the information in its metadata confirmed that the 1920 x 1080 pixel images were captured at 18:57 on the reported date.

The primary camera of a Samsung Galaxy A20S smartphone was used, recording at 24.82 frames per second.

The user's location was georeferenced using the coordinates...
35° 50’ 09’’ South and 71° 35’ 07’’ West. (Figure 1). This corresponds to a residential neighborhood north of the city of Linares.

                               Figure 1

From the footage, 199 frames were extracted and analyzed for sources of luminosity. In this case, luminance variations were subtle and not necessarily visible to the naked eye, but detectable with appropriate algorithms. As shown in Figure 2, it was determined that, along with stars, there were other sources of light present.
It captured a series of lights in apparently straight-line motion.

Figure 2. Frame 130 of the footage. Bright objects, corresponding to points of maximum luminance difference, are enclosed in circles.

The air traffic potentially visible to the south of Linares was studied around the time of the filming.

Figure 3 shows the presence of the Airbus 320-251N, registration CC-AZN, flying from south to north at 38,000 feet and 448 knots. This aircraft was conducting flight SKU434 by Sky Airline between Puerto.
Montt and Santiago. Took off from El Tepual Airport (SCTE) at 18:08 and landed at Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport (SCEL) at 19:32.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the report and the geographic, photographic, and aerospace traffic studies, it was concluded that what was seen was a formation of twenty-two Starlink project satellites.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
