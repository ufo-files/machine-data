An abnormal aerial sighting was reported on an unspecified date from Cerro Los Placeres in Valparaíso.
The reported sighting was described as an orange light, of which no size was associated, and that it was seen for more than a minute from the east by two witnesses. No direction was indicated where it was lost from sight.

The user testified that (sic): “I went out at night to look at the stars when suddenly I see an orange light that at first I thought was a plane but
Description: It was fixed and spinning, I called my sister and she also saw it.

User: I'm not sure what it was exactly, but I'll send you the video anyway, hoping it's visible, as it's from years ago and I'm not sure if it's the original since I made some copies. He says "file format not allowed, I'll try to send it via We Transfer."

No additional files were entered with the report.
The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 33° 02’ 23’’ S, 71° 35’ 31’’ W and 104 m.s.n.m.

Investigation:

Although contact was made via several emails, no response was obtained regarding the reported sighting date, nor was the corresponding audiovisual material received.

In the face of the lack of audiovisual media and a date that would allow for study,
Conclusion: The reported information, the investigation folder was closed according to the SEFAA investigation protocol, classifying the case as Relato.
