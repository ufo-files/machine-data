Case No. 1732 21122021

Date: December 21, 2021.

Approximate Time: 11:52 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: L. V., informed by F. C. Z.

Description: The informant reported the sighting of various aerial anomalies
that his wife had photographed at 11:52 on December 21, 2021 in the city of
Iquique, in the Tarapacá Region.

The user claimed the sighting lasted between 1 to 5 seconds, that the
phenomenon he was reporting was a sphere measuring 3 to 6 meters in diameter,
and that it had a
"new color". He concluded his testimony as follows (sic): "Photo recently
taken by my wife in her accommodation on the street above her house".

Along with his report, he submitted an image in jpg format, which he also included in
report No. 1734 21122021.

Based on the information provided by the informant, the case was georeferenced in a residential area of Iquique, in the Tarapacá Region.

The analysis of the information contained in the case photo demonstrated that
It is digitally altered and contains a drawing available for free download from image-sharing sites such as PNGWing. (Figure 1)
