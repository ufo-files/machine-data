Upon examining the information contained in their files, it was confirmed that they were captured between 00:14:15 and 00:25:25 hours on May 16, 2022, using the four main camera system of a cellular phone.

The fact that what was reported were optical artifacts present inside the camera was consistent with what the user stated in an email.
Electronic report of May 18, 2022. There is no means to corroborate that she could have actually seen something directly.

Images: The informant attached seven photographs to her report, most of which appeared to have been edited to some degree.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographic, forensic, and photographic studies, it was established that the reported sighting was of parasitic reflections, or "flares," that depicted the tracks of the
Cell phone lens used to capture a series of photos taken in backlight of the full moon on May 16, 2022.

Since these reflections only existed within the equipment used to capture the images, they could not be seen directly, nor were they an aerial anomaly.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
