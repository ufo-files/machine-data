Witnesses: M. E. S. P., 52 years old.

Description: The informant reported having seen in a live broadcast
Streaming what it considered an aerial anomaly over the city of Santiago at 22:48 hours on Thursday, September 8, 2022. The phenomenon would have been captured by a camera located in the Farellones winter center, in the
Santiago Metropolitan Area
The reported information described two white lights visible for 6 to 10 seconds, with no indication of size or direction they appeared or disappeared.

The user concluded by stating: "I always watch live YouTube in Farellones because there are always strange things to see in the night sky. Today was one of those."

No backup file of the reported incident was submitted.
