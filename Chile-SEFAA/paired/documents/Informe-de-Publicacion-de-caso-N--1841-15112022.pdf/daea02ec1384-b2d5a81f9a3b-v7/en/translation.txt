Case No. 1841 15112022

Date: November 15, 2022

Approximate Time: 21:30 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Spring.

Witnesses: S. F. C., 13 years old.

Description: The informant reported seeing and photographing what he considered an aerial anomaly at 21:30 local time on Sunday, November 15, 2022, from his home in the commune of El Bosque, Santiago, in the Metropolitan Region.

The reported information described an circular object that changed color and then
that no size estimation was given, but it was visible for more than one minute. The user added that it appeared from the north, but it was not indicated in what direction it was lost from sight.

Along with the information he provided through the SEFAA platform, the user wrote: “[…] we went outside to the patio and saw something that wasn't moving and we went to look for binoculars and it looked like a red circle with green lights in the middle.” Finally, in an email dated November 17, 2022,
He added that the object "moved but I could see it with binoculars and it moved side to side or up and down, sometimes the brightness increased or decreased, like at the time I saw it, I didn't take anything for granted, I just held them in my hand."

The three images that supported the report were analyzed using computer programs, confirming that they were not digitally altered. The examination of the information contained in the files determined that there were no digital alterations and that they were captured using the cameras of a phone.
Samsung Galaxy S20FE cellular phone between 21:28:06 and 21:32:18 on the reported date.
