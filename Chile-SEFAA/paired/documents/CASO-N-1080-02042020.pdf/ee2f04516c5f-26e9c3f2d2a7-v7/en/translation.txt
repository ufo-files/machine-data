It was filmed from a location approximately one kilometer northwest of the Preventive Detention Center of Puente Alto.
The following Figure 2 shows the reported object as it appears in the 1080-D_1 frame of the VID_20200401_234736967 video of the case footage.

The video that supported the report was digitally forensically examined, confirming that it was not
altered. The metadata information indicates that the capture was made on April 02, 2020 at 02:52:45 UTC, with an image size of 1920 x 1080 pixels, at 72 dpi and 24,735 frames per second, saved as an mp4 file. It has a duration of 0:04:43 seconds and was taken with the main camera of the mobile phone.

After extracting 7,011 frames, the frames 1080-D_336, 1080-D_4748, and 1080-D_4595 (Figure 3) were selected as the study subject.
It can be appreciated that what was reported had lights with color changes from red, green, and white. Its movement pattern was characteristic of an aircraft capable of static flight.
