 Summary:
A witness, flying in a clear sky, from northeast to north, reported a sighting in Temuco on February 08, 2025, at 22:18 local time.

The user testified that: “A flickering body followed by a white trail which disappears after approximately 5 minutes.”

The case was studied based on the three photographic files: “IMG_20250208_221841, IMG_20250208_222057, and IMG_20250208_222116”.
