                 Reported what was described as a white light, seen by more than three people for
Summary:        one minute, without movement that appears from the East (E) and disappears from
                 Northeast (NE).
The user concluded by testifying that (sic): “While driving up Calle Parque Central towards the EAST, we noticed a fixed light in the cordilleran sector. The brightness of the light caught our attention. We thought they might be hunting, but it's impossible given the height and the steep incline that would not be able to hold people with lights.”
The case was studied based on 1,688 frames from the video "20251102_220201.mp4" which was recovered from a Google Drive folder.

Georeferencing was used to pinpoint the user's location (Figure 1).

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical studies,
Conclusion: Forensical and photographic evidence concluded that the reported light was of terrestrial origin and therefore did not pose a risk to air operations.
