Case No. 1721 21122021

Date: December 21, 2021.

Approximate Time: 0:31 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: L. V., informed by F. C. Z.

Description: The informant reported the sighting of what would be three entities
or anomalies that his wife had photographed at 00:31 on December 21, 2021 in the
city of Iquique, in the Region of Tarapacá.

The user wrote that the sighting lasted between 1 to 5 seconds, that what was reported
had a "biological form" and possessed "color change."
"Photo of my wife in Iquique in her room," he indicated in the report.

Based on the given address, the location of the capture was identified in the city of Iquique.

The analysis of the information contained in the case photo demonstrated that it was digitally altered. In addition, as Figures 1 to 3 show, the supposed biological-form beings are drawings available as free exchange images on the internet. Furthermore, they have been used by the same user in other reports to SEFAA.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the account and forensic examinations of the image sent to SEFAA, it was determined that the user reported the presence of three alleged anomalies through a digital composition that added drawings to a photograph.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "False."
