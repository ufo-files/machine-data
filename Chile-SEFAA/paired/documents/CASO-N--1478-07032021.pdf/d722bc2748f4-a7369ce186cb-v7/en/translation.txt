In general terms, a clear sky was filmed with the Sun adjacent to the upper left edge to, according to the testimony, check "if my phone recorded in slow motion something that my eyes did not see." The review of each available frame determined the presence of two objects, analyzed below.
Object 1

It appears between frames 159 to 182 as a small, always black object, flying from behind a roof present in the lower right sector of the scene and moving towards the upper left vertex.

Figure 3 compiles some enlargements, which show that, though variable, the surface of its silhouette tended to appear as an elongated object.

All of the above allowed the object to be circumscribed to a biological origin.
The characteristics of its movement and morphology were consistent with those of an insect, with hyaline wings. The visual quality of the material did not allow for further detailed classification without risking bias.

Forensic and photographic analysis concluded that two objects were filmed: an insect in flight and a reflection resulting from filming facing the Sun. Due to the nature of what was identified, there was no relationship of an aeronautical nature.
