The images show an object flying between the camera and the Moon,
leaving a condensation trail behind it, which is characteristic of
a aeronautical photograph. A preliminary analysis pointed to the AMX258 flight by Aeroméxico. This was performed by the Boeing 737-852,
Registration XA-AMB, departed minutes before the take-off from the
Benito Juárez International Airport in Mexico City (MMMX) and landed
at the Miguel Hidalgo y Costilla International Airport in Guadalajara (MMGL).

Conclusion: Although the aircraft photographed was identified, as the
event occurred outside Chile's borders, the case was closed according
to Chapter 1 and Chapter 2, numeral 2b (Study of cases occurring or
registered within national territory).
