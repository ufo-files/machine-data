Case No. 1850 12122023

Date: January 11, 2023.

Approximate Time: 17:30 local time.

Weather: Cloudy sky.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: G. R. G. C., 50 years old.

Description: On Thursday, January 12, 2023, the SEFAA (Spanish Air and Space Phenomena Association) received, through its web platform, a case reported by the informant regarding the alleged presence of an anomalous aerial phenomenon in a photograph.

According to his testimony, around 17:30 on Wednesday, January 11, 2023, he was in a sector of Talagante, in the Region...
Santiago Metro, observing the cloudy sky along with other people and
their boss took a photo of the sky to capture the record.

"Upon seeing this image, I observed a black leaf-shaped object in the sky,
which caught our attention greatly as it was too high to be a leaf. She
shared the photo to the WhatsApp group for everyone to comment on the
image," the user wrote in their report.

He described the reported object as a black object, 5 to 20 centimeters in size,
It was not specified what form it was. Along with his report, he submitted the photographic file IMG-20230111-WA0007.jpg, which was sent to him via WhatsApp.

The user's location was georeferenced at the time the photograph was taken on Monseñor Larraín 1025 street in the commune of Talagante. (Figure 1)

Figure 1. The red dot shows the location of the witnesses at the time the photograph was taken, southwest of Santiago.

At 10:43 a.m. on Thursday, January 12, 2023, SEFAA sent an email to...
The informant asked to see a copy of the original image and inquired about the exact location where they were.

In his 10:52 a.m. response on the same day, the user clarified: "I understand that my colleague has already deleted the photo from her phone, but the one we received is the same she took (not a copy of a copy)," and he shared the specific location where they were in Talagante at the time of the photograph.
Despite not having a copy of the original, a look at the image sent
allows us to determine that, due to its similarity to many other images
received and examined by SEFAA, the apparently anomalous object captured
in the photograph is a bird moving to the right. (Figure 2)

Figure 2. The reported supposedly anomalous object is framed within a red circle.

In the zoom of Figure 3, the tail, body, and beak of the bird in flight are indicated.
Figure 3. The tail, body, and beak of the bird in flight are distinguishable in the photo sent.

It goes without saying that since its creation in October 1997, the former CEFAA and current SEFAA has received dozens of similar photos corresponding to birds in flight, such as the one in Case 827 24052018, attached below (Figure 4):

Figure 4. Bird flying upward and to the left, reported as a presumed anomalous aerial phenomenon in Case 827 24052018.
Images: As support for his report, the user sent a JPG format photograph, which he in turn received from a work colleague through WhatsApp, which is attached below:

Conclusion: Although the user did not send a copy of the original image, but rather the photograph he had received through WhatsApp, the characteristics of the same allowed for a quick determination that the reported anomalous aerial phenomenon actually corresponded to a bird in flight, descending and turning to the right.
The image is very similar to several others received by this office since its
creation in October 1997.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained".
