Case No. 1603 01082021

Date: June 21, 1989

Approximate Time: Between 11:00 and 12:30 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: A., 62 years old.

Description: The informant reported having witnessed a "ship," for "more than
5 seconds," at an unspecified time between 11:00 and 12:30 (HL) on June 21, 1989 from the city of Alban, Colombia.

According to the received testimony, what was reported would have been an object of
approximately 15 meters, in the shape of a "delta" and the color "gray-silver". It would have appeared in sight from the "zenith", "between a perfect circle", when "clouds open over our heads" to remain static at 30 meters distance from the user.

The testimony about the reported sighting concluded as follows (sic): "It happens in the midst of a group meditation... approx. 120 people. Only 3 of us saw it... A delta-shaped ship above us, in the middle of the clouds, a perfect circle opened."
"I raised my eyes to the sky when the medium said... 'And the man lifted up his eyes to the sky... And he saw that there was life...' Then I saw the ship above us, in silence, it did not emit any kind of sound. That day, my life changed."

According to the witness's statement, the event occurred in the municipality of Albán, department of Cundimarca, Province of Gualivá, in the Republic of Colombia. This placed the case outside the Committee's scope of work, as it occurred outside Chile's national borders.
