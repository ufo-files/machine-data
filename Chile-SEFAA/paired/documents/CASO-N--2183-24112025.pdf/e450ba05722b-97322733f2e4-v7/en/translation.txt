Date: 24-11-2025 / 02-12-2025

Case No: 2183

Report: Anomaly observed in the air

Location: Mejillones
Region: Antofagasta
Classification: Closed

The observer reported what he considered an aerial anomaly, seen at 20:58 hours on 23 November 2025, from kilometer 53 of Route 1, Region of Antofagasta.

Summary:
The user reported seeing a silver light, approximately 10 meters in size, directly and for 10 seconds, flying from north to east from a witness's perspective.

The gentleman testified that (sic): "yesterday, while traveling from north to south towards the city of Antofagasta, passing the toll booth, and about 53-800 kilometers further, he saw a flash of white and blue light."
"Description of a comet impacting behind the hills, for a moment I thought it might
have been an airplane, but there was no sign of fire or dust from the impact. Vehicles
that were ahead of me stopped on the side of the road to see what had happened. Unfortunately,
I couldn't take any photos or videos as it happened very quickly."

Following the user's indication in an email dated November 24, 2025.
Investigation: (Figure 1), the user's location was georeferenced at kilometer 53.8 of Route 1 (Figure 2).

On the other hand, the yellow sectors shown in Figure 4 are two restricted airspaces intended for institutional operations, and they are in the direction of what was reported. Although what was witnessed could have been consistent with the sighting of an air operation of this nature, due to the lack of images that would allow for the definitive fixing of the date, location, and direction of the event, the investigation had to be terminated.

Due to the lack of images or any other means to corroborate the time and location of what was under study, the case was closed according to numeral 2d (Only cases reporting original images, audios, or videos will be investigated) of the Technical Instruction on Procedure, Investigation Timelines, Reception, Processing of Executive Report, and Archiving of Reports SEFAA.
