Witnesses: I., 39 years old.

Description: The informant reported seeing multiple anomalous aerial phenomena at 23:15 on 4 February 2021 for one minute.
from the city of Purranque, in the Tenth Region of Lakes.

He described the reported sighting as white lights, flying in a line,
appearing in his view over the city of Purranque and disappearing
towards the Carrasco Population.

He concluded his account by stating (sic): "It was a display of lights
that reached a point and disappeared."

The geography of Purranque was studied, georeferencing the
user's location at the coordinates 40° 54’ 37’’ S and 73° 08’ 49’’ W (Figure 1). It was verified that Carrasco Population refers to
The urban extension of Purranque to the east of Route-5.

According to this, the displacement reported was from the
west to the east.

From that location, meteorological conditions, air traffic, and
aerospace conditions potentially visible around the reported date
were studied.

Meteorology was studied based on records made from the Carlos Hott Siebert – Cañal Bajo (SCJO) Airport in the city of Osorno (40º 36' 17’’ S, 73º 03' 40'' W).
This is located approximately 34.7 kilometers northeast of the user. According to these data, the night presented particularly favorable conditions to visualize the reported phenomenon: winds of 7 to 3 knots, visibility of at least 10 kilometers, no clouds below 5,000 feet, no cumulonimbus or significant weather phenomena. The detail of the available information between 21:00 and 24:00 on the reported day appears at the beginning of this report, under the "Meteorology" section.
A study was conducted on the traffic that could have flown over the vicinity
of the location between 21:00 and 23:00 (HL) on February 4th, confirming
that there was none that the witness could see and that met the characteristics
of their account.

Upon investigating aerospace traffic, it was verified that the overhead flight
of a compact formation of Starlink satellites and the remains of the Dragon-9
rocket that deployed them was visible. (Figure 2). In the following days, these
craft moved away, so their presence is not as noticeable.
Figure 2. Starlink satellite passage at 22:38 (HL) on February 4, 2021. An arrow indicates the direction and movement of the spacecraft, and a yellow star marks the witness's location.

Starlink is a project by the aerospace company SpaceX to create a constellation of communication satellites that have been launched since 2018 and are placed in orbits 550 kilometers high. Figure 3 shows the antennas of one of these satellites.
