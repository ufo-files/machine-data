Season: Autumn.

Witnesses: P. T. D., 13 years old.

Description: The informant reported having witnessed multiple
anomalous aerial phenomena around 23:00 (HL) on 17 April 2020,
approximately from kilometer 15 of route J25, commune of Teno,
Region of Maule.
They described them as “5 bright light balls similar to stars” and stated that they flew in a line from west to east, disappearing behind the mountain range. He further described them as: “There were 2 at each end and 3 in the center, they were in a line.”

A starting location was georeferenced at kilometer 15 of route J-25.

From this location, meteorology and air and aerospace traffic that could be visible during the reported time period were studied.
The meteorological conditions were analyzed based on data collected from the General Freire Airport (SCIC) in Curicó, approximately 22.8 km west of the user.

According to this information, at 23:00 (HL), the sky was overcast and there was at least 153 meters of vertical visibility, with no fog, as the ambient temperature (14°C) was significantly higher than the dew point (6°C), relative humidity was at 59%, and atmospheric pressure was 1016 hPa.
The detail of data between 22:00 and 24:00 (HL) on April 17th is shown in the "Meteorology" section above.

The analysis of air traffic between 22:00 and 24:00 on April 17th, 2020, defined that there was no flight visible from the study location with a flight pattern similar to the one reported.

When studying aerospace traffic, it was determined that several formations of satellites from the Starlink project flew overhead and could be seen from the Maule region. As stated...
The figure below, at 23:00 (HL), shows that the movements were particularly aligned with the informant's testimony.

Each green dot in the figure represents the position of an active satellite. Among them, the Starlink satellites that could be seen from the reporting area were: Starlink 1160, 1149, 1145, 1180, 1150, 1161, 1170, and 1153.

Images: The user only provided their testimony.

Conclusion: According to the studies of the testimony, weather conditions, and air and space traffic,
It concluded that what was observed was the passage of satellites from the Starlink project, belonging to the American company SpaceX.
