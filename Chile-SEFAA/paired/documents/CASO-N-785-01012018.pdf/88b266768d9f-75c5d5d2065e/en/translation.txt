CEFAA photo, stating that what was observed in both images correspond to parasitic reflections.

The technical analysis of the images and the conditions of the area at the time the photo was taken allow us to indicate that the detected alterations lead to the following conclusion:
They correspond to a parasitic luminous effect, product of the incidence of solar light on the optical elements of the cellular telephone used.
