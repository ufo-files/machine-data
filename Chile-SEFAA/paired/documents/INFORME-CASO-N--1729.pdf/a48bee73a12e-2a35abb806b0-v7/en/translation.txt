Case No. 1729 21122021

Date: December 21, 2021.

Approximate Time: 2:36 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: L. V., informed by F. C. Z.

Description: The informant reported the sighting of what would be two
anomalies, and that his wife had photographed them at 02:36 on December 21,
2021 in the city of Iquique, in the Region of Tarapacá.

The user wrote that the sighting lasted between 1 to 5 seconds, that the
entities were more than three, that they had a "biological form," and that they
measured
Between 20 centimeters and one meter and that had "a new color." He ended up pointing out (sic): "Photo of entities on the table by my wife."

Along with the above, a digital photograph in jpg format was entered.

The case was georeferenced in a residence in the city of Iquique, in the Region of Tarapacá.

The analysis of the information contained in the photograph demonstrated that it was digitally altered and contains drawings available for free download on Internet sites for exchanging images. Some of
They were used in other reports entered by the same user.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the report and forensic examinations of the
image sent to the SEFAA for study, it was determined that what the user reported
as the presence of "entities" actually corresponds to a
The composition that was created by adding available elements on the Internet of supposed extraterrestrials to a photograph taken inside a residence.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "False."
