Huape, commune of Chillán, Itata Province, Ñuble Region.

Summary:

The reported sighting was described as a light, resembling a car headlight, with an approximate size of "over 100 meters," visible in a clear sky, for more than a minute, flying silently from northwest to southeast as observed by a witness.

Testimony (sic): "I was looking out the window when suddenly it appeared."
"A light, enlarging and disappearing. Then he noticed the same light but smaller, both disappearing, continuing like this, but every time it appeared and reappeared, another light joined. This continued for approximately 40 minutes."

No file was uploaded with the report.

A location was georeferenced in a representative sector of El Huape.
The coordinates are 36° 36' 20" S, 72° 16' 01" W and 84 m.s.n.m. (Figure 1)
