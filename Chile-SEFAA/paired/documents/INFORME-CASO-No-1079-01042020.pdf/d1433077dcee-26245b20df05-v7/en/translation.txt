Description: The witness was in the commune of Lampa,
Metropolitan Region of Santiago, when they saw the overflight of
approximately fifteen "star-shaped" objects moving in a linear motion and regularly spaced from each other. He stated that he took photographs and a video sequence of what he saw and requested the opinion of CEFAA and if there was radar registration.

Images: Despite indicating that he had taken photographs and a video sequence of what he observed, the witness never provided CEFAA with anything more than the account of his sighting.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the reported account and the available data, the CEFAA determines that there is no evidence to confirm the existence of the reported objects.
Geographical, meteorological, and aerospace studies concluded that what was seen corresponded to the overflight of satellites belonging to the Starlink Project of the SpaceX company.
