Description: The witness reported the observation of a quartet
of aerial phenomena from the commune of Pudahuel, Region
Santiago Metro. He perceived them as white circles, the size of stars.

He stated that they moved in a linear motion, at a higher speed and altitude than commercial airplanes. He detailed that they flew "from west to east, alone or in pairs" and that at the same time "luminescent objects are detected with a north-to-south direction, disappearing into the stratosphere."

The meteorological conditions prevailing at the time of the reported observations were investigated with the observations made from the
Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport (SCEL) in Santiago. Between 04:00 and 07:00 (HL) on the day of the sighting, there were visibility conditions of at least 10 kilometers, with no clouds or possibility of fog.

The traffic that could have flown near the location between 05:00 and 07:00 (HL) on April 21, 2020 was studied, verifying that there was none that could be visible to the witness and that met the characteristics of their account.

Upon studying aerospace traffic, it was verified that they could have been
We have observed several steps of Starlink satellites.

Starlink is a project by the aerospace company SpaceX to create a constellation of communication satellites, which have been launched since 2018 and are placed in orbits at 550 kilometers altitude. At 05:00 (HL), the witness was able to observe a particularly notable satellite deployment.

It is noted that according to information extracted from the Heavens Above database, between 05:00 and 06:30 hours
There is a record of 137 satellites belonging to the Starlink-0 to Starlink-5 missions, which overflew the celestial vault visible from the Metropolitan Region.

Images: The witness only provided their account.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the reported account and the geographical, meteorological, and aeronautical and aerospace transit studies, it was concluded that what was seen corresponded to the overflight of satellites belonging to the Starlink Project of the SpaceX company.
