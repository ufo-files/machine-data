Case No. 1168 29072020

Date: July 22, 2020.

Approximate Time: 07:20 local time.

Weather:

                                 N/A

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: F. A. B. B., 29 years old.

Description: Mr. F. A. B. B. reported having seen an anomalous aerial phenomenon at 07:20 (LT) on July 22, 2020, from the city of Chillán, Region of Ñuble. He indicated that for one minute, he observed what he described as a circular white light, approximately 1,000 feet high, coming from the north of the city. He specified that “it was coming in..."
"Vertical direction from the coast to the mountain range very slowly and with great luminosity."

In three emails from July 29th to 31st, the user was requested to provide more information about what was reported and if there were any images of what was seen. Pending this, the air traffic over the vicinity of Chillán between 06:00 and 10:30 on July 22nd (02:00 and 04:30 UTC on the same day) was studied.

During this period, only the passage of flight JAT 295 was recorded.
JetSMART Airlines, between the Andrés Sabella Aerodrome (SCFA) in Antofagasta and the Carriel Sur Aerodrome (SCIE) in Concepción. Throughout its journey through the Region of Ñuble, it maintained an altitude over the ocean, flying from north to south parallel to the coast. Given its trajectory, this aircraft would have no connection to what was reported by the user.

Images: Written evidence requests were made on July 29, 30, and 31, but as of the date of this report, no response has been received from the user.
Conclusion: The case was closed according to numeral 2d (Study of cases with audiovisual antecedents) of chapter 2 of the Technical Instruction Procedure, Research Times, Reception of Antecedents, Preparation of Executive Report and CEFAA Report Archive.

The case was classified as a Relato (Report).
