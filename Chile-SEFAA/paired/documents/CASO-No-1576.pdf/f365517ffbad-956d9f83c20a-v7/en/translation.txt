Date: April 1961.

Approximate Time: 11:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Autumn.

Witnesses: T. R. C., 61 years old.

Description: The informant reported to the Committee that she saw an abnormal aerial phenomenon at 11:00 (LT) on an unspecified date in April 1961, from the city of Concepción, Biobío Region.

The reported object was described as a "shallow double metal plate", 10 meters in size, with several dazzling colors.
She added that the day was clear and that the reported object was 20 meters away and flew "in the shape of an L."

The user concluded her testimony by stating (sic): "I have seen it several times, just attach this photo."

The user's location was georeferenced to the south of the city of Concepción, at coordinates 36° 51’ 08’’ S and 73° 02’ 46’’ W. (Figure 1)
