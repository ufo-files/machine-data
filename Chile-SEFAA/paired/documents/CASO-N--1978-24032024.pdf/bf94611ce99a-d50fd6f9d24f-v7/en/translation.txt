Summary: A report described two bright, white objects, "less than 5 cm" in size, observed by more than one witness for over a minute, always to the west.
It was reported that (sic): "I was in an apartment facing the sea and the sunset was ending when I saw in the distance, just where the sun barely becomes visible, a static white glowing object. I started recording it and after a few seconds another one appeared slowly, a little smaller and less bright. They stayed like that for about 2 minutes until both disappeared into the... "
"They were suspended in the same place and very far away. My brother who lives in Mantagua also saw them."

The aircraft was at an altitude of 41,000 feet (12,500 m) and flying at 520 knots (963 km/h) to the east, on a course of 94° (Figure 3).
