Case No. 1382 02012021

Date: January 2, 2021.

Approximate Time: 14:05 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: J. R. A., 45 years old.

Description: The informant reported the presence for ten minutes of what
it considered air abnormalities at 14:05 on January 2, 2021 in the El Melón sector, in the commune of Nogales.

The user initially described what he saw as a black sphere, distant
approximately 8 kilometers from its location. From its flight pattern,
He indicated that it was static, in several senses and very fast. Additionally,
he stated that it had appeared from the east and disappeared in a "unknown"
direction.

He concluded his report stating that (sic): "Looking at the El Soldado mining
operation, located in the El Melón mountain range, I saw bright flashes pass
over the mountain range, I also saw a black sphere stand out in a white cloud,
which then disappeared after a few moments. I took photographs and recorded
a video; when reviewing the photographs, three spheres appear."
"White objects on the mountain range and in another photograph, there appears a kind of lead-colored vehicle levitating towards the same sector. On another occasion, I also saw the flashes and photographed and recorded a phenomenon a couple of kilometers further north, in the El Melón hill area; that was in December 2019."

As support for his report, the user provided two photographs and a video. Upon studying the information contained in the files, it was verified that the photos were taken with the main camera of a cell phone.
Samsung, model Galaxy A01, and edited between 14:01 and 14:09 on January 2, 2021.

This material does not have a record of the creation date and there is alteration in
the values recorded for the shutter speed and image resolution.

Upon examining them using computer programs, no indications of digital alteration directly associated with what was reported were found, which is why the case study continued.

The two photographs are presented in Figures 1 and 2.

Moreover, the video was recorded at 19:01 on December 9, 2020, that is, 24 days before the reported date for the case.

In an email dated January 6, 2022, the user specified the location of the shots and stated that the video was indeed recorded on December 9, 2020. Therefore, it did not correspond to what was reported, but rather to what was mentioned.
In the final sentence of his testimony: "On another occasion, I also saw the flashes and photographed and recorded a phenomenon a few kilometers further north, in the El Melón hill area, that was in December 2019." For this reason, he was not considered for the study of this case.

Given the hypothesis that what was captured in Figure 2 was a bird in flight, since
January 22, 2021, independent analysis by ornithologists from the University of Chile
has been requested four times, but as of the writing of this report, there has been no
response.

Regarding the possibility that what was described as "I see bright flashes passing
over the mountain range" could be ultra-light aircraft, on July 20, 2021, a request was
He wrote to representatives of paragliding clubs and aerial clubs.

One representative responded that for the Flypirámide Club, they are authorized to fly from the hills Polanco, Barrero, La Región, Carbón, and Manquehue, but they did not have operations on January 2, 2021. She added that the sector under study is not a flight zone for their group, as it has very strong and gusty winds.

For his part, a representative of the "Parapente Chile" Paragliding Club indicated
It is known that there are people who fly in the area, which is consistent with what was captured in Figure 1 and what the user reported.

Images: On January 4, 2021, the informant provided two photos and a video.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographic, forensic, photographic analyses, and the responses from representatives of light-aircraft clubs in the Metropolitan Region, it was concluded that the case originated as a result of the assimilation as a
abnormal flight of a bird and of the fleeting lights of ultralight aircraft.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained".
