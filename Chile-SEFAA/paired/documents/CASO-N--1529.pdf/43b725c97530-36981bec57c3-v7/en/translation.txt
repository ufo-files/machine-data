By comparing the relative position of each bright point with nearby geographical elements, it was defined that none presented their own movement.

Upon studying the sectors near the visible and luminous phenomena from the study location, buildings capable of generating the observed were found.
In the cone of the Osorno volcano, there is a winter sports center with a building whose reflective and conical metallic roof (Figure 6).

Figure 6. Origin of the luminous phenomenon in the Osorno volcano.

On the other hand, at the piedmont of the Calbuco volcano, there is an educational establishment at kilometer 12.2 of route V-613, with multiple buildings having reflective metallic roofs (Figure 7).

Figure 7. Origin of the luminous phenomenon in the Calbuco volcano.
Images: On May 12, 2021, the user submitted a video contained in a file in mp4 format.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, astronomical, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that the footage reported as aerial anomalies were reflections of metallic rooftops of buildings near the Osorno and Calbuco volcanoes.

Case closed and classified as "Explained."
