Case No. 1211 10082020

Date: 1996.

Approximate Time: 08:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: N/A.

Witnesses: P., 47 years old.

Description: The informant, who only identified himself by a first name, reported having witnessed an anomalous aerial phenomenon at 08:00 (HL) on an unspecified day in 1996, from Central Station, Santiago Metropolitan Region.

He indicated that, on three separate occasions, each 8 minutes apart, he saw three silver cylinders, ranging in size from 50 to 100 meters.
They would have flown in a straight line "from south to north at about 90 degrees to the horizon."

He concluded his account by stating that "at 9 in the morning, while looking out my window, I saw a silver cylinder pass by, which only materialized for about 4 seconds in a south to north direction, and then, after a few minutes, the phenomenon occurred again, repeating a third time, all at about 90 degrees from the horizon and perhaps at a distance of 3 kilometers from my location."

The user's location was georeferenced on the 12th, 13th, and 14th.
In August 2020, the CEFAA sent emails to request the individual's identity, the date and time of the incident, and to determine if they had any material to study their account.

Images: Requested via emails on August 12, 13, and 14, 2020. As of the date of this report, no response has been received.

Conclusion: The case was closed according to chapter 2, numeral 2e (Study of major cases over one year with audiovisual antecedents) of the Technical Instruction Procedure.
Research times, background reception, executive report preparation and CEFAA report archiving.

Case classified as FALSE.
