Case No. 1716 20122021

Date: December 19, 2021.

Approximate Time: 5:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Spring.

Witnesses: J. G. C., 59 years old.

Description: The informant reported the sighting of what he considered an
anomalous aerial phenomenon at 5:00 a.m. on December 19, 2021, from the
eastern sector of Santiago.

He stated that he observed "at a great height" and at 45 degrees above the horizon
a stationary light with variations in intensity of brightness that remained for
In 30 minutes or less, it disappeared in the direction of the mountain range
with rapid and zigzagging movements.

"I am a sky observer and I identify planets, satellites, and drones, and what
I observed does not correspond to any of them. I did not film or take any
photos," the user added, also stating that what was observed appeared from the
east and disappeared in the southeast.

He also described the phenomenon as a "light like the morning star" and
mentioned that two other people, whom he did not identify, were witnesses.
Based on the address provided by the witness, their location was georeferenced in the commune of Macul, in Santiago, in the Metropolitan Region, at the geographic coordinates 33° 28’ 25.1’’ South Latitude and 70° 34’ 40.6’’ West Longitude. (Figure 1)

Figure 1

Using the computer programs Stellarium and FlightRadar24, the air and space traffic was studied for the day and place indicated by the informant, but no matches were found.
As the user indicated not having taken records and not possessing any means of
evidence, it was not possible to study any material.

Images: The informant indicated not having taken any photographs or videos and,
therefore, not possessing any means of evidence.

Conclusion: As the user indicated that they had not recorded the reported phenomenon
using audiovisual instruments and that they do not possess any means of
evidence, there is no material to study.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as “Report”.
