Case No. 1718 21122021

Date: December 21, 2021.

Approximate Time: 0:08 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: L. V., informed by F. C. Z.

Description: The informant reported the alleged repeated contact between himself and his wife and supposed extraterrestrial beings and flying saucers, for which he sent four backup photographs.

According to his account, while his spouse was in the city of Iquique, in the Tarapacá Region, on December 21 at 0:08 hours he saw and photographed
Extraterrestrial entities inside the residence where he was staying and also saw flying saucers, which he also photographed outside the property.

He added that the sighting lasted between 1 to 5 seconds, had a "biological form" and was of "a new color."

The location where the photos were taken was georeferenced based on a direction provided by the user after specific inquiry, in Iquique.

The photos provided by the informant, who in turn had received them,
From their wife, they draw attention by suggesting that these are cropped and pasted images inside the residence occupied by the woman, with no apparent concern to avoid revealing it was a photographic montage.

Searching online for similar images, they found that the same figures are available on some websites. For instance, the entity of one of the photos sent by the informant is offered on the website pngegg.com for download and use. (Figure 1)
Figure 1. To the left, one of the photos sent by the user. To the right, a screenshot of the website “pngegg.com” from where the same humanoid figure can be downloaded.

In another of the photos sent by the informant, a being appears that actually corresponds to a race called “Sectoid” from the fantasy video game XCOM: Enemy Unknown, published in October 2012. (Figure 2)
Figure 2. On the left, one of the photos sent by the user. On the right, the same entity in a screenshot of the video game “XCOM: Enemy Unknown”.

Conclusion: Given the suspicion of an obvious and not very subtle montage, similar images were sought on the Internet, finding exact matches to those sent by the user in their report.
Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "False."
