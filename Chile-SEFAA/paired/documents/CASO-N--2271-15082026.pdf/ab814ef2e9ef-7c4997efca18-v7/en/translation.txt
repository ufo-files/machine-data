Location: Calama Region: Antofagasta Classification: Closed

He reported having witnessed what he considered aerial anomalies at 20:37 hours.
From August 14, 2026, from the city of Calama, Region of Antofagasta.
Summary:
It was described as more than three white lights, of sizes similar to "luminary points," that were seen for "1 hour," flying with "zigzag" movements and always to the east of more than three witnesses.

The user testified that (sic): "Several lights appear, move quickly, cross each other, brighten and dim, sometimes forming triangular formations, seen alongside planet Venus and the event disappeared."
"When they disappeared below the horizon."

"No audiovisual files or additional details about the sighting location were entered with the report."

"Although emails dated August 17, 24, and 25, 2026, requested the user to provide original audiovisual material for study, no response was received. In the end, the case only relied on witness testimony."
For statistical purposes, a study location was georeferenced near the address entered by the user on the reporting platform. This site is 3.2 kilometers northwest of the El Loa (SCCF) airport in Calama (Figure 1).
