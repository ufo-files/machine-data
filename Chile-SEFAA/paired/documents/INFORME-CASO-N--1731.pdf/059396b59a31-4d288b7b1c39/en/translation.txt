Case No. 1731 21122021

Date: December 21, 2021.

Approximate Time: 11:26 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: L. V., informed by F. C. Z.

Description: The informant reported the sighting of what would be an
anomaly that he had captured in a photograph his wife at 11:26 on December 21,
2021 in the city of Iquique, in the Region of Tarapacá.

The user claimed the sighting lasted between 1 to 5 seconds, that the
phenomenon he was reporting had a "biological" shape, and measured between 20
In centimeters and one meter, and it was silver in color. He concluded his testimony by writing (sic): "Recently taken photo at 12:28 my wife sends me". Along with the report, a jpg format photograph was included, which is the same one included in report No. 1734 21122021, also submitted by him. (Figure 1)

Figure 1

Based on the provided address, the case was georeferenced to a residence in Iquique, in the Tarapacá Region.
The analysis of the information contained in the photograph sent demonstrated that it was digitally altered. Additionally, it contains a drawing that is available to be downloaded for free from image-sharing websites on the Internet, such as pngable.com. (Figure 2)

Figure 2

Images: One JPG photograph, digitally edited, and attached below:

Conclusion: According to the study of the report and the forensic examination of the
The reported image, it was determined that the user sent to study a digital composition made by adding a drawing offered on the Internet to a photograph taken inside a property in Iquique.

Therefore, it was proceeded to the closure of the file and its classification as "False".
