Case No. 1675 22092021

Date: September 15, 2021.

Approximate Time: 17:00 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: M. C., 45 years old.

Description: The informant reported that at 17:00 (LT) and for thirty minutes on September 15, 2021, she saw what she considered to be eight "ufos" from the commune of Pedro Aguirre Cerda, in the Santiago Metropolitan Region.

The user described the observed objects as eight "oval-shaped" objects, "white or bright steel" at times, flying slowly. The movement was described as "unsteady."
The general indicated was "south to north" towards "east direction".
Subsequently, he added that "I saw one that emitted a flash and disappeared".

He concluded his testimony by writing (sic): "[…] around 17:00 hours, my
A couple looks up at the sky and reports seeing UFOs flying, I look and see 8 balls flying and getting closer to each other, a park guard passes by and we also tell him about it, he sees them and says they don't look like either donuts or balloons, since
they maintained their position and approached, even forming orderly patterns. The
observe for approximately 30 minutes, even when one of them blinked and
disappeared, I saw it, no debris from something that had broken, like a
balloon or drone, for example, therefore, I know what we saw and those things
were not of this world.
The observation location was georeferenced at coordinates 33° 31’ 20’’ S and 70° 40’ 39’’ W. (Figure 1)

Figure 1

Given the testimony, the hypothesis was raised of being faced with the visualization of
metalized balloons in flight. Consequently, wind information at altitude was studied using data from the flight of a radiosonde launched from Santo Domingo Airport (SCSN). According to this information, between 200 and 1,500 meters of altitude, winds predominantly had a south-southeast direction. Their intensities fluctuated from 4 to 12 knots (7.4 to 22.2 km/h).
