Witnesses: P. O. N., 21 years old.

Description: The informant reported seeing multiple aerial phenomena for two minutes at 22:30 (HL) on Thursday 4 February.
From 2021, from the Ponotro-El Durazno sector, in the commune of Cañete, in the Eighth Region of Biobío.

She described the reported sighting as a row of white lights that flew with "accelerated linear motion, from south to north towards east."

She concluded her report as follows (sic): "[…] we observed a row of lights moving towards the north and when we started shouting, the lights faded and I managed to take a picture."

The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 37°
51' 09'' S, 73° 25' 14'' W, corresponding to a rural area near the Peleco River, located 6 kilometers southwest of the Plaza de Armas in the city of Cañete. (Figure 1)

Figure 1

Based on that location, meteorological conditions, air and aerospace traffic potentially visible around the reported date were studied.

Meteorology was studied according to records made from the Carriel Sur Airport (SCIE) in the city of Concepción.
According to them, the night would have presented conditions that
allowed the visualization of the reported phenomenon: winds of 9 to
8 knots, visibility of at least 10 kilometers, with cloudiness at 600
feet.

The detail of the data between 21:00 and 24:00 of the reported day is
attached at the beginning of this report, under the section
"Meteorology".

The traffic that could have overflown the vicinity of the location
between 21:00 and 23:00 (HL) on February 4th was studied, finding
that there was none that could be seen by the witness.
It would fulfill the characteristics of its report.

Upon analyzing aerospace traffic, it was verified that the overhead flight of a compact formation of Starlink satellites and the remains of the Dragon-9 rocket that deployed them was visible. (Figure 2). In the following days, these vessels moved farther away, so their presence is not as noticeable.
