Case No. 1722 21122021

Date: December 21, 2021.

Approximate Time: 00:37 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: L.V., informed by F. C. Z.

Description: The informant reported the sighting of what would be an
anomaly, and that he had photographed his wife at 00:37 on December 21,
2021 in the city of Iquique, in the Region of Tarapacá.

He indicated that the sighting lasted between 1 to 5 seconds, that what was
reported had a "biological form" and was red in color. He added that the case
image
It was (sic): “Photo of my wife” (as author, not as subject).

Based on the address provided by the user, the location of the photo's capture in the city of Iquique was identified.

Analysis of the information contained in the case photo demonstrated that it had been digitally altered. To this is added what is visible upon zooming in, where one can see how the supposed red entity is superimposed over the fries, the table cover, and the sugar bowl that appear in the image. (Figure 1)
Figure 1. Detail of the case image that proves there was an overlap of the figure with the alleged anomaly on a conventional background.

Conclusion: According to the study of the narrative and forensic and photographic examinations of the image sent to SEFAA, it was determined that the user reported the presence of an anomaly with a digital drawing made by altering a photograph.
Therefore, the file was closed and classified as "False."
