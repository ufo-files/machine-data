Case No. 1730 21122021

Date: December 21, 2021.

Approximate Time: 2:40 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: F. C. Z., 55 years old, as informant.

Description: The informant reported the sighting of what would be an
anomaly that his wife had captured at 02:40 on December 21, 2021 in the city of Iquique, in the Region of Tarapacá.

The user stated that the sighting lasted between 1 to 5 seconds, and that they saw
Three circular phenomena, ranging from 2 to 5 meters in size and having "a new color." He ended up writing (sic): "UFOs,"

Along with the above, he entered the images shown in Figures 1 and 2.

The location of the capture was identified with coordinates corresponding to a residential area in Iquique.

The analysis of the information contained in the photo files of the case demonstrated that they had been digitally altered.
The first and third of the three images sent along with the report contain
drawings available for free download from websites such as pngwing.com (Figures 3 and 4), while the second image is available on the pixabay.com website (Figure 5).


Figure 3. Drawing used to compose the first of the images sent.

Images: The user provided three edited images in JPG format.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the report and the forensic examinations of the
images reported to SEFAA, it was determined that the user submitted three digital
compositions created by adding online drawings of supposed UFOs to three photos
taken in Iquique.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "False."
