The report, entered through the CEFAA web platform, describes a sighting photographed in the Cudico sector, Lican Ray, in the Los Ríos region. The phenomenon was not observed at the time of the photograph.
It indicates that, during the March 2018 holidays, in Lican Ray, it took some

user/a description:

photos of landscapes and, later, when seeing them on his/her computer, he/she noticed

something strange in one of them.

The photo is analyzed through ELA (Error Level Analysis) and CMF (Copy and Movement

Forgery), which indicate that the image is original.
Investigation: The photographic team for the shot is a Canon EOS REBEL T2i, of which the technical characteristics are reviewed. There is a possibility of optical aberrations as no type of shade was used.

It is estimated that the image shows a figure produced by parasitic lighting inside the optical system of the camera used.

The technical analysis of the images and the background of the report point to that
Conclusion: The photographed phenomenon is a parasitic reflection, product of the combination of the convex-plane optical system of the camera lenses.
