The camera was supported on a tripod, but no lens hood was used.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony and the geographic, forensic, photographic, and geometric studies, it was determined that what was photographed were parasitic reflections or "flares" caused by the incidence of light when photographing against the light.

What was reported was never seen, as it was only found in the resulting image from the photographic capture.
