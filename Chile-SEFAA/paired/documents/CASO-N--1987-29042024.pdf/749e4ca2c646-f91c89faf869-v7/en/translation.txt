Hondo, Argentina.
A "green light" was reported seen at "10:00" hours on January 15, 2024, from Termas de Río Hondo, Santiago del Estero.

The reported sighting was described as a green light seen for only 1 to 5 seconds, flying from northwest to southwest by a witness.

The user testified that: "It lasted a few seconds. The first thing I thought was..."
"That could have been a flare, but then I realized it wasn't."

No additional files were entered with the report.

It was verified that the sighting location corresponded to the Argentine Republic
Investigation: and that between 21:30 and 22:30 (HL), there was no overflight of any Chilean-registered aircraft that could have been seen heading southwest.

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographic analysis,
It was determined that what was reported did not occur in the controlled air space. Conclusion: Since it did not happen in the responsibility of the General Directorate of Civil Aeronautics or within national territory, the investigation file was closed, classifying the case as Foreign.
