Case No. 1728 21122021

Date: December 21, 2021.

Approximate Time: 2:05 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Summer.

Witnesses: L. V., informed by F. C. Z.

Description: The informant reported the sighting of what would be two
anomalies, and that his wife had photographed them at 02:05 on December 21,
2021 in the city of Iquique, in the Region of Tarapacá.

The user reported that the sighting lasted between 1 to 5 seconds, and that
two objects with a "biological form" were seen, with a size of approximately 20
In centimeters and one meter, and of "a new color." He ended up writing (sic): "Photo of my wife" (in the capacity of author of the images sent, not of the subject).

Along with the above, he entered the two photographs in jpg format.

Based on the provided address, the case was georeferenced to a residence in the city of Iquique, in the Region of Tarapacá.

The analysis of the information contained in the photographs demonstrated that they were digitally altered. Additionally, it was verified that they contain available drawings.
For free download on Internet sites for image sharing.

Figures 1 and 2 demonstrate this, as they correspond to the entities present in the two images sent.

It should be noted that the being in the right image corresponds to the "xenomorph" from the fictional movies in the Alien franchise.
Conclusion: According to the study of the report and the forensic examinations of the images sent to SEFAA, it was determined that the user supported his report with two photographs, which are compositions created by adding available elements on the Internet of alleged extraterrestrials to two photos of the interior of a residence.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "False."
