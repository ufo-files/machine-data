Conclusion:

Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of the forensic, photographic, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that the reported observation is consistent with the navigation lights present on the aircraft (Figure 5), the perceptible red color...
It corresponds to the Beacon Light (anti-collision lights); it should be noted that the green color is also available on the aircraft; the reported light maintains the same position throughout the recording; variations in intensity, form, and color are consistent with the effort the camera's image processor made to record in low light and contrast conditions.
