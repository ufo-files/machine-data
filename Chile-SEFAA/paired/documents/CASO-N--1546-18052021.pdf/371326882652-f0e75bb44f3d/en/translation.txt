The meteorological conditions around the time of recording were 6 knots (11 km/h) winds from the south-west (240°), visibility of at least 10 kilometers, and a surface temperature of 26°C.

Along with the stable wind regime, the movement pattern of the object under study was consistent with a free flight, susceptible to slight direction modifications due to turbulence caused by hills.
According to the geographical, meteorological, forensic, and photographic analysis, it can be estimated that based on the observed material and the wind conditions, as well as the digital study, what was reported was an inert object dragged by the wind, which did not pose a risk to air operations.
