Description: The informant reported seeing what she considered an
aerial anomaly at 20:00 on Friday, March 18, 2022, from the El Pabellón
area in the commune of Melipilla, in the Santiago Metropolitan Region.

The user described the reported sighting without specifying a shape, but she did specify that
It was black in color and measured between 2 to 5 meters. He specified that this was seen for more than a minute by more than three witnesses, but did not provide any geographical reference as to where it began or stopped being seen.

The witness concluded her testimony by stating (sic): "It was an object spinning near a cloud. At one point it appeared to stop and then continued moving until it disappeared."

According to the witness, the object would have flown over the hills in the sector of
Carmen Bajo, located to the northwest of her position and to the southeast of the Melipilla aerodrome. These hills have altitudes close to 800 meters above sea level.

The two photographs that supported the report were perused with computer programs, confirming that they were not digitally altered. The examination of the information contained within them revealed that they were captured with the main cameras of a Samsung Galaxy S21 (SM-G991B) smartphone at 20:07 hours on the reported date.
On the other hand, the information contained in the video file did not show signs of digital alteration.

The aforementioned raised the hypothesis of being faced with conventional air traffic, so the help of an air traffic controller, who is an external advisor to SEFAA, was requested. The specialist examined the radar records of the Santiago Area Control Center (ACCS) between 22:30 and 23:30 (UTC) and noted that what was reported was present in them at the time of the video.
According to their findings, there was an overflight of an aircraft to the southeast of the Melipilla aerodrome (SCMP).

Images: The user provided two photographs in jpg format and a video sequence in mp4 format, approximately one minute long.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, and air traffic studies, along with the information provided by the consulted advisors, it was concluded that what was reported was a
A plane was captured on the southeast of Melipilla airport, flying at 3,200 feet and 146 knots.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
