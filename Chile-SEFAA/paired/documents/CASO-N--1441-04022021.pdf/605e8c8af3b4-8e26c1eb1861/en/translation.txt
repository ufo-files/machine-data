Season: Summer.

Witnesses: A., 52 years old.

Description: The informant reported seeing what he qualified as multiple air anomalies for 15 seconds. This would have been observed at 22:35 (HL) on 4 February 2021 from the
City of Temuco, in the Ninth Region of La Araucanía.

The user described what was reported as "star lights," white,
flying in a straight line. He added that these phenomena appeared
to him "from coast to mountain range," disappearing from "west to
east."

He concluded his testimony stating that (sic): "We were outside the
house when this series of lights in a line plus 30 or 40 passed and
then turned off..."

The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 38° 44’ 45’’ S, 72° 35’ 00’’ W, corresponding to an urban sector.
Southeast of the city of Temuco (Figure 1)

Figure 1

Based on that location, meteorological, air traffic, and aerospace conditions potentially visible around the reported date were studied.

Meteorology was studied according to records made from the La Araucanía Airport (SCQP) in the city of Freire (38° 55' 33'' S, 72° 39' 05'' W).

According to them, the night presented particularly favorable conditions to visualize the reported phenomenon: winds from 4 to 2
knots, visibility of at least 10 kilometers, no clouds below 5,000 feet, no cumulonimbus or significant weather phenomena. The detail of the meteorological data between 21:00 and 24:00 on the reported day is included at the beginning of this report, under the "Meteorology" section.

The traffic that may have overflown the vicinity of the location between 21:00 and 23:00 (HL) on February 4th was studied, finding that there was none that could be seen by the witness and that met the characteristics of his account.
When investigating aerospace traffic, it was observed that a compact formation of Starlink satellites and the remains of the Dragon-9 rocket that deployed them were overhead (Figure 2). In the following days, these vessels moved away, so their presence is not as noticeable.

Figure 2. Overflight of Starlink satellites at 22:38 (HL) on February 4, 2021. A green arrow indicates the direction and movement of the vessels, and a red star marks the witness's location.
Starlink is a project by the aerospace company SpaceX for the creation of a constellation of communication satellites, which have been launched since 2018 and are placed in orbits at 550 kilometers altitude. Figure 3 shows the antennas of one of these.

Figure 3

This satellite constellation was sent into orbit on Thursday, February 4, 2021, with the seventeenth launch and mission 15 of the Starlink project from the Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) in
The Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida,
United States. (Figure 4)

Figure 4. Starlink Project Mission 15.

One hour and four minutes after launch, the vector deployed the satellites one after another, forming the aeroespacial formation witnessed by the witness.
