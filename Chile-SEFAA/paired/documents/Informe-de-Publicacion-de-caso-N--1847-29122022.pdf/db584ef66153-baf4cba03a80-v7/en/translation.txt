Witnesses: P. I. G. I., 52 years old.

Description: The informant reported having seen what he considered an anomaly.
aerial between 02:30 and 03:00, local time, on Thursday, December 29, 2022,
from the commune of Maipú, in the Santiago Metropolitan Region.

The reported information described an object that is a triangular shape, over 100 meters in size, white in color, and observed by three people. It appeared from the southwest and was no longer visible towards the northeast.

"A light that looked like a cloud, but when observed with binoculars, it was a triangle. But very far in the sky," the user wrote in their report.

In addition, they uploaded five JPG format files via the WhatsApp application.
The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 33° 32' 27" South Latitude and 70° 46' 51" West Longitude, at an elevation of 471 meters above sea level.

The site is located 9.2 kilometers west of the El Bosque Air Base Control Tower (SCBQ) and 16.5 kilometers south of the Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport (SCEL).

In an email dated January 12, 2023, the informant provided a total of eight photographs as support for their report. All were perused by experts.
computer programs, finding that they were not digitally altered.

The study of the information contained in the files defined that they were
captured using a Samsung Galaxy S10 (SM-G975F) cellular phone between
02:37:24 and 02:58:54 local time on the reported date.

The meteorological situation around the time of the observed sighting was
analyzed using observations from Arturo Merino Benítez Airport (SCEL). There
were particularly favorable conditions for
aerospace observation: visibility of at least 10 kilometers, without
presence of clouds below 5,000 feet nor cumulonimbus, and ambient
temperature above the dew point. More details of what was reported between
01:00 and 03:00 hours are found at the beginning of this report, under the
section "Meteorology".

The study of the air traffic potentially visible to the user during the period in which the case photos were taken only recorded the takeoff or landing of aircraft at Arturo Merino Benítez Airport. These were planes moving at low altitude to the northwest or north of the user and none could be seen as previously explained.

that at 1:43 a.m. on December 29, 2022, that is, 54 minutes before
the taking of the case photographs, the Chinese Academy of Technology of
The Launch Vehicles (CALT) launched a Long March 3B rocket
(CZ-3B) from the Xichang site in southeastern China, carrying

Images: The case featured eight photographs for study. Of this material, one image was found to have undergone digital alteration, cropping it to 1994 x 3545 pixels and modifying its pixel density registration to 1 x 1 per square inch. Therefore, it was not considered study material.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, meteorological, and air traffic studies.
In the field of aerospace, it was determined that the reported event was the deployment of the Chinese satellite Shiyan-10-02 by a Long March 3B vector on December 29, 2022.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
