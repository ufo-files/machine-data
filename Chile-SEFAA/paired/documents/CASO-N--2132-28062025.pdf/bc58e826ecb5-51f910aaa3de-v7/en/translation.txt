                 Reported what was described as two white lights, no size indicated, but visible for
                 more than a minute always southwest of
A witness, in the city of Punta Arenas, Chilean Magellan and Antarctic Region.

Testimony (sic): "The event began at 06:31:44 and ended at 07:15:19 (visible time in the video source, in the top left corner). The light initially appears over a ship visible at sea and moves in several directions at variable speeds. At certain moments it descends to align with"
In the area of the Cabo de Hornos Hotel, it is then observed over the sea again and later ascends to position itself very high, almost exiting the recording frame.

User description:

The object maintains erratic movements for several minutes. Finally, at 07:14:54, the light seems to split into two objects, which disappear a few moments later in the direction of the sea.
It seemed to me an unusual phenomenon due to the range of displacements, speed changes, the
height reached and the final division of the object.

Link of the video (it was public but I changed it to listed so only people with the link can see it)."

The case was studied based on the video hosted on the following website and from which no
original files were available (YouTube).
The camera location was georeferenced (Figure 1).

Investigation:

The footage was recorded with a security camera that logged the date and time of the recording, indicating it was done between 18:31:44 and 19:15:19 (HL) on June 27, 2025.

In the following sequence, it can be observed that what was reported moves, performing an overflight between the ship and the coastline (Figure 2).

Upon consulting air traffic in the area, it was determined that there were no flights with active transponders.

According to reports in the press, at the time the city was filmed, there were no flights in the area.
On the eve of an election, polling stations were guarded. The scene captured included the Armando Quezada Ocharán Industrial High School, located near the waterfront. The visible movement of the object under study occurred in that sector.

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, air traffic, and press studies published in Punta.
Sand, it was confirmed that what was reported occurred in the lead-up to an election event
Conclusion: and under circumstances where the polling stations were under guard and what was
              filmed was an overflight near one of these locations. The apparent existence of a
              navigation light and the movement pattern reported were consistent with an RPAS
              aircraft.
