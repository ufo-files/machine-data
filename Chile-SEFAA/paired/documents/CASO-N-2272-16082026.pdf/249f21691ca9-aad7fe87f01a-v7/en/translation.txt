The examination of the metadata information confirmed the digital integrity of the data
and established that the material was captured between 21:28 and 21:30 hours on the
reported date by the camera of a Samsung cellular phone, model Galaxy S22 Ultra (SM-
S908E).

The camera's location was georeferenced (Figure 1). This location is found
At 7 kilometers southwest of the General Bernardo O'Higgins (SCCH) airport in Chillán.

Figure 1

Based on what is visible in the images and the location of the studied area, it was determined that the filming aimed approximately between 255° and 275°.

The air situation analysis did not detect any aircraft with an active transpondrer heading southwest from Chillán that could have been filmed.

In general terms, the three are night recordings, without stars or the Moon.
Those that appear are luminous objects that show apparently linear displacements.

  Satellites that appear illuminated reflect sunlight as their orbits cross the illuminated cone, thus they can be seen by witnesses who are
They are found in the darkness. But, on the other hand, these ships orbit independently of each other and at different altitudes. From the perspectives of the witnesses, what is perceived are multiple bright points that fly quickly, which, if assumed as the same object, have very abrupt changes in direction, not assimilable to those made by planes. Derived from this, they are interpreted as air abnormalities.
