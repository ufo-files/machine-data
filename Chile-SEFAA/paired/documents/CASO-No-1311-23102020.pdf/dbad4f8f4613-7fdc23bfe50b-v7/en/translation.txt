Description: The informant reported having witnessed an anomalous phenomenon starting at 21:15 (HL) on Thursday, 22 October in the skies of the town of Nonguén, Concepción, Biobío Region.
He described what was reported as "a star but larger" with a color "yellow white."

"Small triangle but flashes of light at each point stronger than the rest, and a very small but very strong light. If light is like flashes of light legs, and it doesn't blink maintains its light but now at midnight the brightness increased the same but stronger (sic)", he added.

Regarding its motion, he indicated that (sic) "When it started up and down very fast, and then slow, then it slowly rose little by little."

On October 29, the user was asked to evaluate a description of
Mars' visible motion, without mentioning the latter. In an email on
November 1, he responded, "That's exactly as described."

To assess the hypothesis of an astronomical origin, three consulting
astronomers from the CEFAA of the University of Chile, the Metropolitan
University of Educational Sciences, and the Institute of Astrophysics of
the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile were consulted. (November 3)
Two of the astronomers consulted reported that what was seen corresponds to the planet Mars.

Additionally, one of them indicated that the planet was only 65.3 million kilometers from Earth, and with a surface illumination of nearly 100%, so it was very bright. He explained that the minimum distance between Earth and Mars is approximately 55.7 million kilometers, in this case, the minimum distance between Earth and Mars occurred on October 6, 2020, and was only
62.0 million kilometers. (3 November 2020)

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the user's testimonies,
geographical, meteorological, and astronomical studies, and the
information provided by the consulted astronomical advisors, it
was concluded that what was seen was the planet Mars.
