Date: 20-07-2020 / 11-09-2020

Case No: 1157

Report: Multiple anomalous aerial phenomenon witnessed for 30 minutes

Time: 22:30 (HL) on 20 July 2020

Location: Punta Arenas, Magallanes Region

Classification: Resolved
And the Chilean Antarctic.

Summary:

He described what he reported as three or more "bright white" spheres and a size he could not specify. He also stated he did not know the distance from his location, but indicated the phenomenon remained to the north, without movement, and with "lights orbiting around it."

He ended his report as follows (sic): "A white light caught my attention."
"Intermittent, static in the sky when a light passes by at great speed over the fixed, I took my camera and started recording the light moved slightly from its position for about 30 minutes. When reviewing the recording, I noticed that small red lights approached and then at some point, the second white light passed again at great speed over the static. I have a recording of those lights."
Once the report of this case was received in emails on July 21st, 28th, 30th, and again on September 10th, 2020, the user was requested to provide the footage he indicated having of the reported event. As of the writing of this report, there was no response from him.

Awaiting the study material.
It analyzed air traffic over Punta Arenas between 20:00 and 24:00 on the reported day. It was verified that there was no registered flight over national territory (Figure Investigation: 1).
Due to the absence of possible precedents with which to study the witnessed, the study folder had to be closed according to numeral 2d (Study of cases with audiovisual precedents) of chapter 2 of the Technical Instruction, Research Procedures, Reception of Background Information, Preparation of Executive Report and Archiving of Reports CEFAA.
