In an email dated June 26, the user was requested to provide more precise details of the location in Copiapó from where the video was filmed. As of the writing of this report, no response has been received. Consequently, and for statistical purposes, a central location was taken from the city's Plaza de Armas.

Figure 2: The geometric analysis of the footage determined that what was assumed to be an anomaly was, in fact, normal.
The aerial phenomenon was a parasitic reflection or flare, resulting from filming directly towards the Sun (Figure 2). This was consistent with the reported observation not being seen directly, as it was merely an optical aberration only present inside the camera.

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, and photographic studies, it was determined that the reported event was merely a reflection or flare.
