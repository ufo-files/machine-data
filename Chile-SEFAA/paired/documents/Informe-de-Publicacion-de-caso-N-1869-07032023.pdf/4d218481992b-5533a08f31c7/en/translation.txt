Furthermore, although the peripheral area of the volume has a consistency apparently cottony, but with a solid core compared to the previous one, there was no variation in its spatial orientation attributable to self-motion. This is consistent with seeds dispersed by anemocoria. These are usually very small and therefore transported as atmospheric dust, also, in addition to their size, they can have "wings", "hairs" or "plumes", which
It facilitates its dispersion by increasing the thrust surface of air currents.

Considering all the above, it was concluded that what was associated with anomalies was the involuntary recording of an insect in flight (first object) and the tracks left by the fleeting passage of two small, inanimate bodies dragged by the wind towards the northwest. Although the images did not allow for a full identification of the second object, it was considered that there is a high probability that, like the third, it was a seed.
Dispersed by the wind.

Conclusion:

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographic, forensic, and photographic studies, it was concluded that the report originated from the filming of a flying insect and two inanimate objects consistent with seeds carried by the wind.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
