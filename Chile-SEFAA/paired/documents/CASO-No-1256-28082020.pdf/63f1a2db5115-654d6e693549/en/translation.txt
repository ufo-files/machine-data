Case No. 1256 28082020

Date: September 19, 2019.

Approximate Time: Between 20:30 and 22:30 hours.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: J. I. T. B., 24 years old.

Description: The informant reported the sighting of an anomalous aerial phenomenon for 15 seconds, at some point between 20:30 and 22:30 (HL) on September 19, 2019 at Laguna El Encanto, in the Sixth Region of Libertador General Bernardo O'Higgins.

He described what he observed as a star or the flash of a
Bengala, white in color and approximately 2 meters long. He stated that this was found at 20 or 30 meters from him, and that "... it was like a bengala that stood out from the forest, it slowly descended diagonally to the ground and went out, lighting up the entire forest" (sic).

He concluded his report as follows: "... I saw the forest light up as if they were using spotlights like hunters do when they go 'spotting', I raised my gaze and saw a light moving at high speed."
It is difficult to describe, just as when in Star Wars the vehicles come from the speed of light and stop in space, in the same way this light appeared out of nowhere and comes quickly, stopping at a progressive braking and when it is completely suspended in the air it turns off like a joke that flies out of the fire."

In emails dated August 31st and November 3rd and 5th, 2020, the user was requested to provide audiovisual material to study their report.
