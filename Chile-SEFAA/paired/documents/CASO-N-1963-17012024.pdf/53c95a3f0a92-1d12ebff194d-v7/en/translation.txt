This location is approximately 4 kilometers southwest of the Viña del Mar (SCVM) aerodrome.

The report image was perused through ELA (Error Level Analysis) and CMF (Copy and Movement Forgery) analysis, confirming it was not digitally altered. Examination of the metadata information defined that
It was taken at the reported time, by the main dual cameras of an Apple cellular phone, model iPhone 12. It was also confirmed that the photo came from the composition performed by the graphics processor using two shots. More photographic details are found in the third section of this report.

Figure 4 shows that the geographical examination evidenced that what was reported was a parasitic reflection or flare caused by the incidence of parasitic light on its lens due to photographing out of focus from a lighting fixture. This was consistent with
that the user only perceived it in the photograph since this optical artifact only existed inside the camera.
