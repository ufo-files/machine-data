Summary: An observation was made from Reñaca, Viña del Mar on Tuesday, December 6, 2016, at
20:55 (HL)
They described what they saw as a bright, silvery-white source of light, resembling Venus moving at a moderate speed towards Southeast. They indicated that it appeared at 230° azimuth and about 60° altitude. They explained that its movement was zigzagging towards the east, permanently making right-angle direction changes.

User/s report:
It took approximately two minutes for the observation. He finally wrote that the phenomenon disappeared from view as it moved away in a southeastern direction, at about 125° azimuth, maintaining a constant altitude. He estimated that the phenomenon had an apparent magnitude of +0.5 on the Pogson scale.

In his report, the user did not indicate that there was any audiovisual record of what was described.
It was verified that, for the reported date, the civil twilight in the evening occurred at 21:13 (HL) and the astronomical twilight occurred at 22:26 (HL), which would allow for a sufficient level of darkness to visualize bright objects.
