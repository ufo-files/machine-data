He reported what he considered multiple aerial abnormalities, seen at 19:00 hours on 09 July 2025, from Villa Alemana, Valparaíso Region.
The user described what was reported as more than three white spheres, with no indication of their size, from where they appeared or disappeared, but they were visible in a cloudy sky for more than a minute by more than three witnesses.

The lady concluded by relating (sic): "Orbs or dancing lights in the sky. We thought they were a projection with lanterns but given the distance they covered, I don't think they were. We have no explanation."

Figure 14 illustrates the result of the geometric analysis of frame 246, which confirmed that the observed phenomenon was a parasitic reflection or flare caused by recording directly in front of a white light source.

Figure 14. Presence of a parasitic reflection in frame 246 of recording 2144-3.
This type of bright object are optical aberrations formed inside the camera by the incidence of parasitic light on the concave surface of the lens. They do not really exist and cannot be seen directly, so they could only be pointed out by whoever was observing them on the screen of the equipment used as a camera. This is consistent with them being targeted when they were no longer present in the image.
According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, and photographic studies, it was concluded that what was reported were lights projected onto a background of clouds. Additionally, a parasitic reflection or flare was filmed. None of the above represented a danger situation for air operations.
