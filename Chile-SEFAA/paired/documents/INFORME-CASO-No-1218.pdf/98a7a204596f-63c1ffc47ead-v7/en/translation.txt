Case No. 1218 11082020

Date: August 11, 2020.

Approximate Time: 12:39 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: C., 48 years old.

Description: The informant, who only identified himself by a first name, reported seeing an anomalous aerial phenomenon near the Sun at 12:39 (HL) on August 11, 2020, from Talcahuano, Biobío Region.

He described the reported event as a "spherical plasmoid" of color "plasma," without being able to gauge its size or distance from the observer.
He was located. Additionally, although he indicated that the object had remained static, he also testified that it had appeared from the west and disappeared towards the southwest of where he was located.

He added, "I checked it with infrared - its light is as strong as the sun's since they were no more than 20° apart." He concluded his report as follows: "While observing the sky, I noticed a bright object at the square of the static sun and without losing time, I took a picture with my back to the plasmoid and leaving...
"It was on the sun on my right shoulder and the sphere on my left shoulder, its light was the same as our star's; it only lasted something between 50 to 60 seconds, leaving a mark on a photo."

In emails from August 12th, 13th, and 14th, 2020, the user was requested to provide the graphic material they had written about to study the reported sighting. As of the writing of this report, no response was received.

Images: The witness did not provide any study materials.

Conclusion: Case closed according to numeral 2e (Investigation).
