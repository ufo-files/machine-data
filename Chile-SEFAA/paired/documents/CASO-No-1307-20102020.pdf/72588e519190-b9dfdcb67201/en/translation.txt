Witnesses: V. D. C. H.

Description: The informant reported having seen a series of
air anomalies in flight at 21:55 (HL) on October 20th.
2020, over the city of Puerto Montt, Region of Los Lagos.

He described what he saw as lights that looked like stars, white in color,
that moved in a straight line for five minutes from southwest to northeast
relative to his position.
He finished his report as follows: "I was outside in the courtyard smoking a cigarette and suddenly the stars started to move, then they aligned in a straight line and advanced to a point that was..."
they lost had about 8 and one was bigger and circular seemed
"The stars scared me, that's why I'm writing to you, please give me an answer
of what I saw."

The meteorological situation was studied at the time reported using observations collected from El Tepual (SCTE) airport in Puerto Montt (41º 26' 20'' S, 73º 05' 38'' W). According to this information, the night presented particularly favorable conditions for observing what was reported: visibility of at least 10 kilometers, no cloud presence below 5,000 feet, no
No cumulonimbus or significant weather phenomena were reported. The details of the previous statement are presented at the beginning of this report, under the "Meteorology" section.

The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 41° 28’ 30’’ S and 72° 57’ 11’’ W. (Figure 1)

Based on this location and the testimony, the direction of the reported phenomena was determined. (Figure 2)

Due to the lack of images to pinpoint the exact time of the sighting, the aerospace traffic that might have been present was analyzed.
Overflown Puerto Montt between 21:00 and 23:00 (HL) with a previously defined course. This allowed the identification of a formation of Starlink project satellites that transited between 21:44 and 22:04 (HL), some of which passed vertically over the city. (Figure 3)

Starlink is a project by the aerospace company SpaceX to create a constellation of communication satellites, which have been launched since 2018 and are placed in orbits 550 kilometers high.

The remains of a Falcon 9 vector were detected, along with the next 10...
Starlink satellites: 1028, 1469, 1694, 1021, 1019, 1685, 1012, 1687, 1748, and 1010.

Images: The user only provided the testimony of their report.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the narrative and the geographical, meteorological, and aerospace traffic studies, it was concluded that what was reported was the overflight of a formation of Starlink project satellites.
