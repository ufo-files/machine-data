An abnormal aerial sighting was reported at "05:25" hours on the 23rd of November, 2024.
The reported information described it as a light resembling a star with a size between 6 and 20 meters. It was visible for over a minute, always flying north from a witness's perspective, in a clear sky.

The witness who reported it testified that "The object was coming quickly from the East towards the North in a diagonal manner. When I woke up, I looked out the window and it was there."
"When I saw the object, it stopped, and I opened my Luminos Pro app to check if it was a satellite or not, it wasn't. So far, the object remains suspended in the air."

"The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 28° 34’ 33” S, 70° 45’ 26” W."

"An email dated November 25, 2024, was sent to request from..."
He reported that he would send original photographic or video files to allow the requested study. No response was received.

Due to the lack of audiovisual means to study, the folder was closed, classifying the case as a "Relato" (Report).
