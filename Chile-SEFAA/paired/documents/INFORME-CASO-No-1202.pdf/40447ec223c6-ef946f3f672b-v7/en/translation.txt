Season: Autumn.

Witnesses: J. T. N., 22 years old.

Description: The informant reported having witnessed an
anomalous aerial phenomenon at 17:53 (HL) on May 13, 2020
in the town of Chimbarongo, Region of Libertador Bernardo O'Higgins.

She indicated that, along with four other people, they observed
the movement of something leaving a trail in the sky: “it was
like a kind of smoke trail, like that of planes, moving forward”
She quickly saw a object near a hill that was in front. A discussion began that it could be a plane, but we also commented that it was different. I recorded a few seconds but then it disappeared and did not reappear. It even disappeared before perhaps passing behind the hill."

The witness's location was georeferenced and weather conditions in the area were also investigated, from the station located at General Freire Airport in Curicó, with the results indicated above under "Meteorology".
Those who observed the phenomenon did so under conditions of a
completely clear sky and with wide visibility.

Under those circumstances, flights of aircraft with transponders activated were investigated, obtaining the following data:

Between 13:51 UTC and 13:52 UTC on May 13, 2020, flights were recorded over the town of Chimbarongo, Province of Colchagua, Region of Libertador Bernardo O'Higgins:

1. Bell 407 helicopter, CC-AVF. From N to S. It was located to the W of
Chimbarongo at 13:48 UTC. 5.350 ft, 125 kts, heading 200°.

2. Aircraft NXX, from S to N. passed west of Chimbarongo, at
13:52 UTC, at 34.000 ft, 370 kts, heading 15°.

Given this situation, the ATC Advisor of CEFAA was consulted, who
He corroborated the presence of the plane by sending a radar image that showed the flight over Santa Cruz, which could be observed by the informant and his companions.

In parallel, the sent video was examined, verifying
which was filmed with an iPhone7 at the place and time indicated by the
Witness, discarding any alteration or editing of the same.

According to the geographical, meteorological, and air traffic study,
the witness, along with other people heard in the video sent to CEFAA,
observed the flight of an institutional plane.

According to the existing meteorological conditions at 17:53 local time,
the engines of the indicated aircraft would have produced a condensation trail,
leaving the trace in the sunset sky, a situation witnessed by the informant
along with the people accompanying her.
Images: MP4 video showing a condensation trail of an aircraft.

Conclusion: According to the analyses carried out, what the witness and her companions observed in the town of Chimbarongo, on the shores of the Estero of the same name, corresponded to the flight of an institutional aircraft heading towards southern Chile.
