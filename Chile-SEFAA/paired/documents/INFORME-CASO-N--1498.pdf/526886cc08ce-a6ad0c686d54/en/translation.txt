Description: The informant reported that for 30 minutes, at 02:09 (HL) on March 24, 2021, he/she saw what he/she considered to be multiple air anomalies, from the city of San Fernando del Valle de Catamarca, Argentina.

He/she described what he/she saw as a flickering light, in a circular shape, and red and blue colors. He/she added that, accompanied by other objects, the light moved in a straight line at a high speed.
The reported object moved from southwest to west.

He concluded his testimony by stating, "Then I take out my cell phone
or make several videos, the light moves away and disappears into the sky
but while this is happening, it remains stable, I feel observed. The
other lights move around until they finally move away. I have the
recording."
