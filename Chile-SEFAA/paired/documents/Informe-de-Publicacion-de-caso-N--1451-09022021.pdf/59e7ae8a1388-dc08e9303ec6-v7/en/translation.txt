Witnesses: J. B. V., 24 years old.

Description: The informant reported what he assumed to be an aerial anomaly
that he observed at 21:33 local time on February 9, 2021 from the commune
of Sierra Gorda, in the Region of Antofagasta.
The user described the reported sighting as an object in the shape of a “satellite,” yellow and bright, that was visible for approximately 4 minutes flying from south to east in a linear path, at what he indicated as “high, like an airplane.”

He added that “it looked like a star” and concluded by testifying that “it was like a satellite but at airplane altitude and the strangest thing is that it disappeared super fast at the moment of taking a picture.”

A study location was identified at the user's residence in the commune
from Sierra Gorda, at 1,626 meters above sea level. (Figure 1)

The two images that supported the report were perused using computer programs, confirming they were not digitally altered.
The examination of the information contained in the files determined that the photographs were taken with the cameras of a Xiaomi smartphone model Redmi Note 9S, at 21:27 local time, on the reported date.

The meteorological conditions prevailing at the time of the sightings were examined.
Photographic captures from observations made from the El Loa Airport (SCCF) in Calama. According to them, there were particularly favorable conditions for aerial observation: absence of cloud cover or temperature conditions that would favor the condensation of fog. A detail of this is attached at the beginning of this report, under the "Meteorology" section.

The conditions of the potentially visible traffic for the user were studied, determining that at the time the photographs were taken
Eventually, a visible flight by a Gulfstream V aircraft at 43,000 feet and 498 knots could be observed. It passed west of the witness on the potentially visible horizon, heading north, at an altitude higher than usual for commercial flights, and was not in close proximity to any other aircraft. Given the distance, it was highly improbable that what was observed was the IGS RADAR-5 spacecraft, according to the aerospace traffic analysis.
NORAD identifier 42073, crossed the horizon following the witnessed direction. Its solar panels could be momentarily visible due to the atmospheric conditions in Sierra Gorda.

This is a military reconnaissance orbiter from Japan's Satellite Intelligence Center, launched in 2017 from Tanegashima.

Images: The case featured two JPG format photographs as study media.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the report and the results of the studies.
Geographical, forensic, meteorological, and air and aerospace traffic data concluded that there is a high probability that the reported sighting originated from the Japanese satellite IGS Radar-5's overflight.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Probably Explained."
