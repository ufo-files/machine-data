An individual reported what they considered an aerial anomaly seen at 10:10 hours on 29 July 2025, from Andacollo, Elqui Province, Coquimbo Region.

Summary:
The reported object was described by the user as an amorphous, silver object, with a size comparable to 6 to 20 meters, seen for more than a minute, always to the west of two witnesses.

The gentleman concluded his testimony by stating (sic): "We were in Andacollo city center for work for 1 hour, we saw the sighting on the outskirts and it caught our attention as no one else saw it while we were in the city, in total, there are 3."
We expected it to leave or move, so we watched it for 25 minutes, observing only a rotation movement. We could not wait any longer and had to leave.

The user reported:

It was positioned almost in the center of the city, at an altitude of approximately 100 meters.

No audiovisual files were attached to the report, but later the videos "VID_20250729_101050272.mp4" were recovered.

When the first footage associated with the case began, the city of Andacollo was
overflown by the Airbus 321-211 aircraft conducting the LAN124 flight by Latam Airlines. It came from Santiago and was at 36,025 feet altitude on a north (4°) course. Furthermore, at the start of the second recording, there was an Airbus 320-271N to the southwest concluding the JAT133 JetSmart flight from Calama, waiting to land in La Serena.

Finally, the third video shows two aircraft advancing towards north of Andacollo. Figure 5 indicates they were the aircraft fulfilling the
flights LAN124 and JAT126 by JetSmart from La Serena and Calama.

Figure 5. Aerial scene at the beginning of recording 2146-3.

In the event that what was studied was some type of airship, it was verified that
no NOTAM (Notice to Airmen) was issued from La Florida (SCFL) Airport in La Serena
for the date of the recordings.

Directly consulted with the Communications Office of the Illustrious Municipality of
Andacollo, which reported that what was filmed is a balloon.
The meteorological phenomenon is captive of the Acatlán mine (Figure 6).

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical studies,
Conclusion: After forensic, photographic, and air traffic analysis, it was concluded that the reported sighting corresponds to a captive balloon.
