Case No. 1677 26092021

Date: August 7, 2021.

Approximate Time: 10:02 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: D. P., 33 years old.

Description: The informant reported that for "2 minutes," at 10:02 (HL) on
Saturday, August 7, 2021, he saw and filmed an abnormal aerial object flying
from an unspecified location in the commune of Providencia, in the Santiago
Metropolitan Region.

He described the reported sighting as an object with an octagonal shape, black
in color, and that
He would have reported a "cross in the center." This object would have flown straight from south to north at a distance of 600 meters and appeared "from a distance, a couple of meters in size."

The user concluded his testimony as follows (sic): "I was watching a fight between some eagles when I saw this object pass over the birds and I could see it next to other people, I consulted with friends who operate drones and they dismissed it as such, I have the video of the object."

In emails dated September 27th and 30th, and October 4th, 2021
The informant was contacted to provide the audiovisual material he indicated he possessed. Pending receipt of this material, an initial location was georeferenced at the coordinates 33° 26’ 01’’ South Latitude and 70° 37’ 07’’ West Longitude, a site near the intersection of Av. Eliodoro Yáñez and Av. Manuel Montt in Providencia, Santiago. (Figure 1)

Figure 1

From that location, the air traffic that could be visible was studied.
Between 09:00 and 11:00 hours, no detections were made of any objects matching
the reported description.

Images: Although requested in writing on September 27, 30, and October 4, 2021,
the user provided no additional information other than their report. They also
did not contact SEFAA again.
Conclusion: Due to the lack of a means to study the reported events and the absence of contact with the informant, the investigation file was closed according to number 2d (Investigation of cases reporting images, audio or videos" from the Technical Instruction Procedure, Investigation Times, Background Reception, Executive Report Preparation and Report Archive.

Case classified as "False".
