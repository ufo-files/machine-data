Investigation:

Figure 2

The forensic analysis of the images revealed that the photograph was taken at 05:57 (HL) on January 11th and the video at 06:11 (HL) on January 10th, 2021. Therefore, the user was requested to provide the entire
Video records that it was alleged to possess, which did not occur. Work was done from the moment of capturing the video of the case.

The review of air traffic for the reported date and in the visual in which they were taken did not reveal the presence of any that could have been captured.

There was atmospheric stability in the area. According to the data from the radiosonde at 12:00 UTC from the Santo Domingo station, it indicated the top of a
Thermal inversion around 1,000 meters. It was also noted that there was presence of humidity at low levels. Therefore, conditions existed for a small cloud formation without significant vertical development due to the thermal inversion.

The dark aspect of the cloud and its tone were the result of the contrast it had with the sky that was beginning to be illuminated by the Sun, which was located in the
Acimut 122,1° and 6,52° below the horizon. Solar rise occurred at 06:13 hours.

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, air traffic, astronomical, and meteorological studies

Conclusion:

It was concluded that what was reported was a cloud with little vertical development in a calm atmosphere. Case classified as Explained.
