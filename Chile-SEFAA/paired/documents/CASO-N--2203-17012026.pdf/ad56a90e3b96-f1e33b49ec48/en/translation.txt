The examination of the metadata information in the file confirmed its digital integrity
and determined that it was filmed from 02:10:27 (HL) on January 13, 2026, using the
rear camera of an Apple cell phone, model iPhone 14 Pro Max.
In general, bright objects were recorded moving along a course apparently close to or parallel to the horizon in a dark sky. The intensity of their glows varied, and at times as strong or stronger than the present stars. They are shown in Figure 1, apparently in the same location.

At the beginning of filming, there was a high density of satellites and other space vessels in that sector, most of them illuminated by the Sun (Figure 8).

Figure 8
Among them, the Starlink 47941 and Starlink 35142 satellites stood out, which were
illuminated by the Sun and whose orbits were consistent with what was present in the
images. Both were placed in orbit in different space launches in 2022.

They were at an altitude of approximately 519 kilometers and their structures did not
shine at the same time, but first the Starlink 4794 shone and then this one and later the
Starlink 3514.

Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of geographical, forensic, photographic, astronomical, and aerospace transit studies, it was concluded.
Conclusion: The reported lights were reflections of sunlight on the structures of the Starlink 4794 and Starlink 3514 satellites. Due to the altitude of their flights, they did not pose a danger to air operations.
