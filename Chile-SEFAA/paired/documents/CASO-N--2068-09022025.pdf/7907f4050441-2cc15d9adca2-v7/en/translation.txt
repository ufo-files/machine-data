The present in the case recordings is exemplified by Frame 30 of the video.
Recording 2068-3 shown in Figure 3. This was consistent visually with a first phase of the deployment of Starlink satellites. The review of visible space launches from El Tambo at the time of filming, confirmed the overflight of the 21 satellites of the G12-9 mission, launched on February 8 from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station, United States (Figure 4).
A closed formation was also photographed under similar conditions as reported (Figure 5).

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographic, forensic, photographic, and aerospace traffic studies, it was concluded that what was filmed was the overflight of 21 communication satellites of the Starlink G12-9 mission by the SpaceX company. Given the altitude of these flights, there was no condition that implied a danger to air operations.
