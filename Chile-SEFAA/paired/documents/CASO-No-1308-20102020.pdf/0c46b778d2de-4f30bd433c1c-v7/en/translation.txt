Season: Spring.

Witnesses: A. M., 17 years old.

Description: The informant reported having seen for ten minutes
a series of anomalous aerial phenomena at 21:30 (HL) on October
20, 2020, from the location of Melozal, Region of Maule.
He described what he saw as "20 or more white and bright stars, flying fast." "They made a sweep in the form of a rainbow and disappeared halfway through, never to be seen again," the witness stated, in a clear sky.

The user's location was georeferenced using coordinates of 35° 43’ 49’’ S and 71° 47’ 48’’ W. (Figure 1).

The meteorological situation at the reported time was studied using observations collected from the Carriel Sur Airport.
(SCIE) of Concepción (36° 46' 17" S, 73° 03' 45" W). According to this information, it was confirmed that the night presented particularly favorable conditions for the observation of what was reported: visibility of at least 10 kilometers, no cloud presence below 5,000 feet, no cumulonimbus or significant weather phenomena. The details of the above are shown at the beginning of this report.

Due to the lack of images to pinpoint the exact time of the sighting, the aerospace traffic in the air space was studied.
Visible from Melozal between 21:00 and 23:00 (HL), with the
described characteristics.

This allowed the identification of a formation of Starlink satellites that
transited between 21:44 and 22:04 (HL), part of which passed vertically over
the city. (Figure 2).

Figure 2. Starlink satellite transit at 21:29 on October 20, 2020. A red star marks the
user's location, while green and orange arrows show the paths of the two
detected formations.
Starlink is a project by the aerospace company SpaceX for the creation of a constellation of communication satellites, which have been launched since 2018 and are placed in orbits at 550 kilometers altitude.

Two large groups of Starlink satellites were detected, one flying from northwest to southeast and the other, more compact, from southwest to northeast. The reported phenomena belonged to the latter: 1755, 1697, 1678, 1687, 1025, 1531, 1743, 1749, 1648, 1741, 1754, 1733, 1746, 1747, 1017, 1753, 1744, 1730, 1737, 1714.

1650, 1671, 1682, 1659, 1007, 1729, 1644, 1676, 1699, and 1048.

Images: The user only provided the testimony of their report.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the narrative and the geographic, meteorological, and aerospace traffic studies, it was concluded that what was reported was the overflight of a formation of Starlink satellite project satellites.
