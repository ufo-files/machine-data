Conclusion: According to the results of the forensic, photographic, and
geometric studies, and what was found by an external consultant, it was
determined that what was perceived as air abnormalities was, in fact, a
distortion of the pixels due to lighting conditions.
In the supporting photographs of this case, the origin is optical and technological.

This is consistent with the reported testimony, as the presence of the phenomenon was only noticeable upon reviewing the photographs.

In all the photographs taken against the light, parasitic reflections or flares were imprinted, resulting from the incidence of sunlight on the lens surface, passing through the lens system and reaching the camera's light sensor.

Additionally, in one of the shots, another visual artifact was added.
of technological origin, attributable to the optics or electronics of the
phone used and not to something present in the depicted scene.
