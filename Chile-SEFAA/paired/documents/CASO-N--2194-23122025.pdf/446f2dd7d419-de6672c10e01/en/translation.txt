With the detection of the presence of the stars Hadar and Rigil Kentaurus, it was
possible to confirm the direction of what was observed, towards southeast (SE) (Figure 3).

From the present aerospace traffic, the Starlink-32804 satellite stands out, coded by the North American Aerospace Defense Command (NORAD) as 62846, and which was placed in orbit in 2025. It was located at an azimuth of 152° 30’, at an apparent altitude of 14° 40’ (Figure 4).

                                                                 Figure 4

This vessel was being illuminated by the Sun, which was below the horizon
At 28° 1' in azimuth 151° 5'. The Sun's position allowed its light to reflect off the satellite's structure, such that anyone observing from the surface would be in shadow, which was consistent with the reported observations.

Additionally, no air traffic with active transponders was detected that could have been captured in the case images.

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of geographical studies,
The forensic, photographic, astronomical, and air traffic investigations concluded that the reported event was consistent with the capture of satellite traffic. No mitigating factors were found that would imply a risk to air operations.
