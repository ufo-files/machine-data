Witnesses: D. M. C. H., 35 years old.

Description: The informant reported having seen what she considered an
aerial anomaly at 20:16 on 18 August 2022 from the city of Santa María, in the
Valparaíso Region.
The user described what was seen as an object with a "pure" shape, of a "car headlight" color, and with a size of 2 to 5 meters. This object was reportedly visible for 21 to 30 seconds, flying from the west and disappearing to the north of two witnesses.

The informant concluded by stating (sic): "The event occurred in Santa María V region. At first, I thought it was a plane due to the size and the light, but the object would light up too much at certain intervals and fly very low and not emitting."
"sound. In this area, planes constantly pass by, but this object was

The examination of the information contained in the video file archive, along
with the report, indicated that it was created at 20:42 hours on August 19, 2022.
Therefore, contact was made with the user through two emails requesting the
original file and more precise details of the location from where it was filmed.
This material was sent on August 23, 2022, allowing the location to be identified."
Sighting Moment and Location:

This audiovisual material was analyzed with computer programs, confirming that it was not digitally altered and was studied frame by frame.

The frames shown in Figure 4 evidenced that the object possessed its own lighting, which exhibited the characteristics of aerial navigation lights. There was a delay of 1/30 of a second between each of those images, which, along with the distance, the size of the object, and the existence of the windshield, made it difficult for witnesses to see and associate them with conventional air traffic.

The above led to the hypothesis of being in front of a conventional aircraft,
which is why the meteorological conditions and air traffic were studied around
the time and place where it was filmed.

Meteorology was analyzed based on observations made from Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport (SCEL) in Santiago. The night presented particularly favorable conditions for observation, given
that there was visibility of at least 10 kilometers and there were no clouds below the
5,000 feet or cumulonimbus. The relative humidity was 52% and the temperature
The environment was close to 11°C, with no conditions for the presence
of fog.
Details of environmental conditions between 19:00 and 21:00 hours of
That day is included at the beginning of this report, under the section
"Meteorology"
Moreover, the situation of potentially visible air traffic was analyzed.
From the location previously described between 20:00 and 20:31 hours on August 18. During that period, five aircraft overflew the area near Santa María.

Among them, the LATAM Airlines flight LAN591 from Cancún, which was approaching Santiago for landing, stood out. That Boeing 787 crossed west of the user at the time of the filming, at an altitude of approximately 3,800 meters and traveling at 516 km/h. Its trajectory placed it between 15 and 22 kilometers away, which would explain why its engines were not heard.
The object in flight was examined by bringing it closer, inverting its coloration, and increasing contrast to differentiate it from the black background of the sky. (Figure 5)

                                     Figure 5

Deformations occurred due to both its movement and that of the camera being held without support, as well as light passing through a curved and dirty windshield, where the light from streetlights struck the glass.

Despite all of this, the resulting silhouette is consistent with that of a
aircraft slightly moving diagonally away from the filming with an intermittent
lighting pattern.

An independent analysis was requested by an Air Operations Inspector from the
General Aviation Section (DSO), who is also one of the external advisors to
SEFAA.

The specialist reported that considering the distance, the light from public
lighting, the video capture from inside a vehicle through its windshield, the
visible size and the speed of the movement, the object appeared to be a
conventional aircraft.
The captured image possessed characteristics typical of an aircraft in flight.

Images: Alongside the report, a video file contained in the archive in MP4 format was entered. Later, on August 23, 2022, the original video was received, which was used to study the report.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the account, the results of the geographic, forensic, photographic, and air traffic studies, along with the input from the specialized consultation, it was concluded that the case originated from...
perception of the final phase of the flight LAN591 of LATAM Airlines between the city of Cancun and the Arturo Merino Benitez International Airport in Santiago.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
