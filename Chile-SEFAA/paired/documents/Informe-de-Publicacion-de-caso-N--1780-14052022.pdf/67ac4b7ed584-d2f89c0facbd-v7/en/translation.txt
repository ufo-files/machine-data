Case No. 1780 14052022

Date: May 12, 2022.

Approximate Time: 19:31 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Autumn.

Witnesses: J. P. G. M., 59 years old.

Description: The informant reported having seen what he considered an
aerial anomaly at 19:31 on May 12, 2022 from the commune
of Puente Alto, in the Metropolitan Region of Santiago.

What was reported was described as a light with color changes and a size
less than 5 centimeters. This had been seen for more than a minute.
The user ended up writing that (sic): “that night we were without electricity, it was already dark, I opened the door to see if any house had electricity and it was there that I saw it moving from right to left, it was an orange light and then it changed to white, it had no navigation lights or beacon that illuminated in any direction, it always moved horizontally before disappearing from my vision.”

Figure 4 compiles 600x magnifications of what was reported between frames 1872 and 1875 of the footage. They show that, as it moved from right to left, a red light came on or was visible on the object, consistent with a left/port navigation light.

The above and the movement pattern were consistent with the presence of a RPA or drone.
Images: As support for the case, a video was received in the VID-20220519-WA0006.mp4 archive.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that a RPA or drone aircraft was reported flying at 20:32 hours on May 12, 2022.

Therefore, the case was closed and classified as "Explained."
