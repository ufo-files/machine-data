Date: October 31, 2020

Approximate Time: 6:00 a.m.

Meteorology: N/A

Season: Spring

Witnesses: F. A., 32 years old.

Description: The informant reported seeing multiple anomalous aerial phenomena at 06:00 (HL) on October 31, 2020, from the town of Colliguay, Quilpué commune, Valparaíso Region.

He described what he saw as circular lights or objects, white in color and "the size of the brightest star." He explained that this group
The phenomenon flew from southeast to northeast, aligned.

He concluded his testimony by stating, "At 6 a.m. on October 31, after a beautiful full moon, hundreds of lights appeared in a row heading southeast. We were able to see hundreds of them for approximately 5 minutes, I managed to record them and take some photos where they can be appreciated..."

The user's location was georeferenced with coordinates 33° 10’ 29’’ S and 71° 09’ 45’’ W, and 465 meters above sea level (Figure 1).
