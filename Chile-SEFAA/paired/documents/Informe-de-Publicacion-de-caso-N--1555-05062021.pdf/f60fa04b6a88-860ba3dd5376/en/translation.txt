Case No. 1555 05062021

Date: June 5, 2021.

Approximate Time: 1:15 a.m., local time.

Location: Limache, Valparaíso Region.

Witnesses: J. G. K., 33 years old.

Images: The user only provided their testimony.

Description: The informant reported having witnessed what they considered an
aerial anomaly at approximately 1:15 a.m., local time, on June 5, 2021, from
Limache, in the Valparaíso Region.

The witness described the reported sighting as a "bright, very brief flash."
"It illuminated the entire firmament," "celestial or light blue," he assimilated that it covered "the entire night sky within my field of vision" in a southerly or southeast direction from his location. He added that "less than 10 minutes later, an airplane coming from Santiago (southeast) appeared, which must have seen the phenomenon."

"A few minutes later, after going out to the patio to smoke a cigarette, while looking at the starry night sky in a southerly or southeast direction (approximately in the direction of the city of Santiago), for 1 second or so."
"Less than the entire sky (or at least what was in my field of vision) lit up very intensely with a very bright blue or sky-blue glow. Less than 10 minutes later (I estimate between 5 and 10 minutes) an airplane appeared flying from Santiago, which must have witnessed the same phenomenon," he wrote in his report, along with which no images of what was seen were attached.

The user's location was georeferenced in the city of Limache, in a location near the Lliu Lliu sector, where hills rise in the south and southeast.

Given this, it was defined that what happened could have occurred before the declared time, around 1:00 a.m.

Upon examining the space traffic that overflew the user during the early morning of June 5, 2021, the passage of the remains of the satellite vector CZ-4C R/B (NORAD Identifier 32290) launched on November 11 was detected.
Launched in 2007 from the Taiyuan Satellite Launch Center in the People's Republic of China. Its current orbital parameters are:

The user's testimony indicated that the sighting would have lasted at most one second, so there was no way to corroborate any direction.
for the trajectory of what was seen. This made it impossible to validate that it was definitely this ship.
Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony and what was derived from the aerial and aerospace studies, it was determined that there is a possibility that what was perceived was a fleeting reflection of the CZ-4C R/B satellite.

It was also possible to define that there is no certainty regarding the moment of what was perceived or, since what was seen was a light pulse, neither of the direction its movement might have had. Due to the lack of audiovisual means, the investigation file was closed according to numeral 4d of the Instruction.
