Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the studies.
geographic, forensic, photographic, meteorological, and air traffic, it was concluded that it is highly probable that what was reported corresponded to a light object dragged
due to the wind. This, considering the wind conditions and the proximity of open terrain where debris accumulates.
