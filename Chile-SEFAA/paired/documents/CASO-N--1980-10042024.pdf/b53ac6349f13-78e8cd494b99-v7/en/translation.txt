Case No.:                    1980

Report:                     Abnormal aerial phenomenon in the shape of a "flying saucer"

Summary: An object considered an aerial abnormality in the shape of a "flying saucer" was reported, found in a photograph taken at "12:30" hours on January 7, 2023, in the Cajón del Maipo.

Location:       Cajón del Maipo
Region:         Metropolitana
Classification: Explained
The reported event would have been visible for 1 to 5 seconds by a witness. It was described as a saucer-shaped object, with no indication of its size, the location from which it was first or last seen, or its color, which was described as "other."

The user testified that while returning from a mountaineering expedition, they were taking pictures of the landscape with my camera, without seeing or hearing anything unusual.
When reviewing directly from the digital camera screen, after more than a month, and when zooming in on one of them, he found "a strange colored and shaped object, which did not seem to be part of this tree. Upon realizing this object in the shape of a 'flying saucer', I knew I was encountering something unknown, which left me in great wonder and speechless."
