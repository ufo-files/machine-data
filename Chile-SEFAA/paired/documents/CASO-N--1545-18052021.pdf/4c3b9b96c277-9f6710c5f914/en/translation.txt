A presence was reported that was considered an aerial abnormality from 22:00 to 23:00 on Sunday, 17 May 2021.
The user described what was reported as a "diamond green light and then suddenly red and it started moving." Regarding the coloration, he indicated it was "strong bright green and red." This would have disappeared around "02 in the morning more or less" and flown at approximately 4 kilometers from where he was.

This report was concluded with the following terms (sic): "I was smoking."

pixels, at 30 frames per second, with a resolution of 72 dpi. The video duration was 17.92 seconds, stored as an mp4 file, and captured by the main camera of a cell phone. It was analyzed by studying each of its frames.
