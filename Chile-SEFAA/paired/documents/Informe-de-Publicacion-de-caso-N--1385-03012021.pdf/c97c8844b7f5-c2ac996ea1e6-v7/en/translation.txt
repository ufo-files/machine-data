Case Number: #1385 03012021

Date: Mid-November 2019.

Approximate Time: 6:30 a.m., local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Spring.

Witnesses: V. D. G., 27 years old.
Description: The informant reported having seen what he considered 18 UFOs at 6:30 a.m. on an unspecified date "mid-November 2019" from the commune of Peñalolén, in the Santiago Metropolitan Region.
The user described the reported sighting as “around 18 UFOs (brilliant lights) aligned in a row in the same direction and speed,” flying with a
"a movement similar to that of a satellite but all perfectly aligned and in the same direction". He added that they moved in what he assimilated to a formation of three groups composed of 3, 5, and 5 objects.

The witness wrote in his report: "it was a 06:30 am, I was swimming in the pool for training, I was already finishing the training so I was floating recovering my breath, when I looked at the stars and saw three passing by, similar to satellites, so I thought, but then I saw..."
Another group near the first but this one had 5 stars and also in the same row, direction and speed perfectly aligned, a total of 4 groups passed. I couldn't take photos because I was swimming in the pool with just what I had on, no electronic devices, logically to not get them wet."

Based on the narrative, the study location was taken as the largest pool at the Chilean Army Telecommunications School, located
at the coordinates 33° 27' 45" South Latitude and 70° 31' 44" West Longitude.

Although there were no images of what was seen, according to the Technical Instruction on Procedures, Research Times, Reception, Processing of Executive Report, and Archiving of Reports SEFAA, the case would only be closed by classifying it as a "Narrative" based on the description and date of what was seen, leading to the hypothesis that one was facing a deployment.
Massive aerospace. For this reason, the space launches of November 2019 were examined in the register of objects launched into outer space maintained by the Office of the United Nations for Outer Space Affairs (UNOOSA).

          Table 1. Space launches in mid-November 2019.

11-11-2019       2019-074                 60        United States
         12-11-2019       2019-076                  6            China

According to this, the only one consistent with the number of lights seen by the
user is the mission 2019-074 or Starlink-1, which launched at 14:56 (UTC) on
Monday, 11 November from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral,
United States.
On this occasion, 60 satellites belonging to the Starlink project of the SpaceX company were deployed.

The deployment of these vessels began on the very same November 11, 2019, so in the following days they could still be observed close enough to be assimilated to what the user testified. The lack of an exact date did not allow for a deeper analysis of the reported information or for more precise identification.

Images: The user only provided the account of the reported information.
Conclusion: Although there are no images or an exact date of the reported event, the case was closed as "Narrative" based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical and aerospace traffic studies, which determined that there is a very high probability that the reported sighting was a partial viewing of the overflight of Starlink-1 satellites.
