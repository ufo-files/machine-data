The five photographs that supported the report were perused by
Analysis of ELA (Error Level Analysis) and CMF (Copy and Motion Forgery) revealed no digital alterations. It was also confirmed that the files lack all indications of the photographic parameters the camera of the equipment had, and the date they were captured. Although the files retained an indication that they originated from an Apple-manufactured device, it was not identified, its names were changed, and their formats were altered from the original "jpeg" to "jpg".
The metadata information of the video file examination also confirmed the loss of data at the moment of its capture, and shows that, along with its name, its original format was "mov" and it was converted to "mp4".

The search for the silhouettes of reported objects using edge search algorithms did not detect any in the first three photos. In the last two, this was more fruitless due to the window glass lighting up from the flash.

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographic analysis,
Forensic, photographic, astronomical, and air traffic data concluded that the available images were captured to the north-northeast and do not necessarily correspond to the reported time, as the Moon was to the west at 02:32 am on April 9, 2025.

Conclusion: Of all the available photos and videos, the recording presented the highest image quality, verifying that the luminous elements were consistent with a man-made object.
The present sightings were reflections on the window glass through which the video was taken.

No evidence of airborne elements or air operations was observed, so there would be no elements that could imply a risk to air navigation.
