Case No. 1266 30082020

Date: August 28, 2020.

Approximate Time: 23:34 hours.

Meteorology: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: J. G., 21 years old.

Description: The informant reported an anomalous aerial phenomenon
that she saw and filmed for three minutes at 23:34 (HL) on Friday,
August 28, 2020, in the town of Vilches Bajo, San Clemente, Region
of Maule.

She described the reported phenomenon as "round," "white, reddish
lights," and two centimeters in size. She specified that "there were 3
like them."
He observed lights in the sky that seemed to increase in brightness and then disappear.

He added that the phenomenon appeared from the east, remaining seemingly stationary and at a great distance. He concluded his account by stating, "The following night, on the 28th, we were on the same terrain and saw 3 star-like objects but larger, with a very peculiar light. I wanted to record them to see what they could be, and it recorded for about 3 minutes."

Upon receiving the report of this case via email,
Sent in late August and early September 2020, the user was requested to provide the footage she indicated having of the reported event. By the time this report was drafted, there was no response from her.

In the absence of study material, an initial location was defined within the Vilches Bajo area.

Using this, the astronomical situation at the reported time was studied.

At the reported hour, the most notable thing was the proximity of Jupiter, Saturn, and the Moon located to the north-northwest of the zenith. To the east,
It could be seen on Mars.

Images: The user provided no further information than their account.

Conclusion: Due to the absence of possible precedents with which to study the witnessed event, the study file was closed in accordance with numeral 2d (Study of cases with audiovisual precedents) of chapter 2 of the Technical Instruction Procedure, Research Times, Receipt of Backgrounds, Preparation of Executive Report and Archiving of CEFAA Reports.

The case was classified as "Account".
