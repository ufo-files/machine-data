Meteorological conditions were studied using records from the Eulogio Sánchez Airport (SCTB) in Peñalolén and data recorded by weather balloon flights launched from the Santo Domingo Airport (SCSN).

The observations from Peñalolén indicated that around the time of the image capture, there was a significant cloud cover.
The atmospheric conditions maintained visibility over 10 kilometers, with no clouds below 5,000 feet, cumulonimbus or significant weather phenomena. The dominant winds came from the southwest (210° to 220°) and varied between 3 and 6 knots (approximately 6 to 11 km/h). The ambient temperature ranged from 16°C to 18°C, the atmospheric humidity content went from 34% to 37%, and the pressure remained at 1,021 hPa. The original footage was received on September 4th. Analysis of the metadata

Analysis of air traffic conditions indicated the possibility of gliders in the
Penalolén mountain range area, thus
He requested information about the operations carried out from the Vitacura Municipal Airport (SCLC). The airport manager reported that four Pilatus B4 PC11 gliders were towed to an altitude of 3,200 meters in the San Ramón hill area. These were identified as the aircraft registered as CC-K16W, CC-AJQ, CC-K15W, and CC-ASG, all with white fuselages. (Figure 4)

Figure 4
According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, meteorological, and air traffic studies, it was confirmed that what was reported was the overflight of four Pilatus B4 PC11 aircraft from the Gliding Club, operating from Vitacura Municipal Airport.
