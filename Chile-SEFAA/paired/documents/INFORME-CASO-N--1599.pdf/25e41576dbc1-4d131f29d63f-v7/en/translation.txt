Description: The witness reported the presence for approximately three minutes of what
she considered an aerial anomaly, which she saw and filmed at 19:49 (HL) on March 28
In March 2021, from the commune of La Florida, in the Metropolitan Region of Santiago.

The witness described the object as a "concave diagonal with tail" shape, white in color. This object appeared approximately 2 cm in size, due to the distance it was flying, moving generally from north to south.

The user concluded their testimony by stating (sic): "I was on my night shift when I saw this image in the sky. At first, I thought it was a plane, but then I realized it was moving diagonally. I recorded it; I have a video."

The informant provided, as support for their report, six photographs and one
video. All of this material was examined, finding that it did not show signs of
digital alteration. Additionally, the analysis of the information contained
determined that these images were captured by the main camera of a Motorola
Moto G6 Play cell phone, and its metadata confirmed the reported date and time.
The location was georeferenced while filming at coordinates 33° 33’ 12’’ S,
70° 35’ 13’’ W. (Figure 2)

Figure 2
From this location, meteorological conditions and potentially visible air traffic
were analyzed based on the characteristics testified.

Based on what was visible and a geographical study, it was determined that before
it was lost from sight, the filmed object was located approximately southwest of
the user (Figure 3).
Figure 3. Scenario (a) and approximate direction of the recording (b).

The meteorology was studied using observations made from the Santiago International Airport (SCEL). According to these data, the atmosphere had conditions consistent with the case images: visibility from 10 kilometers, no cloud cover below 5,000 feet or presence of cumulonimbus. The winds became variable, with intensities between 3 and 2 knots.

Although the ambient temperature began to drop, it always remained very
above the dew point, so there were no favorable conditions for the condensation of fog or mist. The detail of the information is shown at the beginning of this report, under the "Meteorology" section.

Upon studying air traffic, it was verified that the Airbus A320-232, registration CC-AWC, was visible with the reported characteristics during its overflight (Figure 4).

as the flight JAT1212 began on the evening of March 28, 2021, between Santiago International Airport (SCEL) and the Andrés Sabella Aerodrome (SCFA) in Antofagasta (Figure 5).

According to the description in the witness statement and the fact that at
Recording the witness was sitting next to a window on the left side of a
bus traveling east to west, she only managed to capture the object in flight for
a brief moment before losing sight of it.

For that reason, part of the description given of it was based on an
interpretation and not necessarily on what was actually seen. This explains
that it assumed a general north-south course, not knowing that when she saw it
moving away "diagonally" from her, the object began to turn to set a course
north.
Images: The user provided six photographs in JPG files and an MP4 file containing a 36-second recording, captured at 1920 x 1080 pixels and 29,842 frames per second.

Conclusion: Based on the analysis of the testimony and the results of forensic, geographical, meteorological, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that the reported object was the Airbus A320-232 aircraft, which that day performed the JAT1212 flight between Santiago and Antofagasta.
