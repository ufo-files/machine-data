Date: December 5, 2021.

Approximate Time: 14:15 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Spring.

Witnesses: S. O. J. J., 48 years old.

Description: The witness reported the sighting of what he considered an
anomalous aerial phenomenon that he photographed and filmed at 14:15 hours on
December 5, 2021 from Bahía Blanca, in the Argentine Republic.

The user indicated that while he was in his home garden, he observed, along with
another person he did not identify, an object of white color in the sky, with
irregular movements.

"Its movement was changing, as it started moving slowly and then accelerated by changing direction, taking a southerly course until, due to nearby buildings, we lost sight of the mentioned object. Its shape was also changing, although its color remained white, which leads us to presume that it was not a drone, an aircraft or a star. Rather, it was an anomalous flying object," he wrote in his report.

He reaffirmed that the object was white and had an "amorphous" shape.
With his report, the user uploaded to the web platform eight digital photos in jpg format and two WhatsApp videos in mp4 format.
