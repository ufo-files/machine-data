The investigation of this case 2132 28062025 was concluded on July 21, 2025, and Mrs. González

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographical, forensic, photographic, air traffic, and press publications studies from Punta Arenas, it was confirmed that the reported event occurred in the lead-up to an election, under conditions where the polling stations were under guard, and what was filmed was an overflight near one of these locations. The apparent
The existence of a navigation light and the pattern of movement reported was consistent with an RPAS type aircraft.
