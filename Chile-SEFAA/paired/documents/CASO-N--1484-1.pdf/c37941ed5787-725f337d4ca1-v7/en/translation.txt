Witnesses: M. P. L., 38 years old.

Description: The witness reported having seen and filmed for approximately five minutes, at 21:15 on March 11, 2021, in San Pedro de La Paz, Concepción, Biobío Region.
He detailed that, to the north of his location, he perceived a white and bright light sphere, "like the Sun or Moon," at an "undetermined distance (like the distance of the Moon)."

He concluded his report stating that (sic) "Yesterday's was impressive, a light in the shape of a sphere above the sky that remained static for a few seconds on the horizon, then disappeared downwards (behind some buildings) and apparently then appears and makes a journey."

On May 18, the video mentioned in the report was received.
The user's location was georeferenced using the coordinates 36° 51’ 27’’ S and 73° 06’ 02’’ W (Figure 1). This location is in a residential area at an altitude of 100 m.s.n.m., approximately 9.7
Kilometers to the south-southwest of the Carriel Sur Airport (SCIE).

                              Figure 1

This result was corroborated by the user in an email on May 18, 2021.
From that location, meteorological and air traffic conditions were studied.
According to observations from Carriel Sur Airport (SCIE), the night presented particularly favorable conditions for the reported observation: visibility of at least 10 kilometers, with a 77% relative humidity and a temperature of 16°C, without dew present. Winds from the northwest were blowing, declining from 10 knots. There was no cloud cover below 5,000 feet, nor cumulonimbus clouds. The details of what occurred between 20:00 and 22:00 on March 11, 2021 are shown at the beginning of this report, under the "Meteorology" section.
The analysis of air traffic revealed the presence of the Airbus A320-232, registration CC-AWC (Figure 2), conducting JetSmart Airlines flight JAT230 between El Loa Airport (SCCF) in Calama and Carriel Sur Airport (SCIE) in Concepción.

                               Figure 2

At the reported time, the aircraft was approaching at 6,125 feet (1,867 meters) and 280 knots (518 km/h) to the north end of the Carriel Sur runway (Figure 3). Its landing lights were facing the user from the northeast. Minutes later, they seemed to disappear.
When the aircraft descended below its visual horizon.

Figure 3. Flight JAT230's situation at 21:15 on March 11, 2021.

Images: A 11.19-second video, contained in a mp4 file.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the reported testimony, images, and geographical, meteorological, and air traffic studies, it was concluded that the reported event corresponded to the final stage of the March 11, 2021 JAT230 flight, performed by the Airbus 320 with registration CC-AWC.
Case closed and classified as “Explained”.
