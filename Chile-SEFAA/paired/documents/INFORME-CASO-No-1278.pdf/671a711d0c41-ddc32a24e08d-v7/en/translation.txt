Case No. 1278 12092020

Date: September 12, 2020.

Approximate Time: 00:04 local time.

Weather: N/A.

Season: Winter.

Witnesses: L. C., 50 years old.

Description: The informant reported having witnessed two
anomalous aerial phenomena at 00:04 (HL) on September 12, 2020,
from Concepción, Biobío Region.

He described them as lights, intense white in color and 15 meters
in size. He added that they appeared approximately south (186°) of
his location. He concluded his testimony as follows:
terms: "... we saw a light very similar to that of a commercial plane, but it was strange that it didn't move, after 20 minutes another light identical with the same characteristics appears."
It is 0:15 minutes and they are still in the same place without movement."

Upon receiving the report of this case, by email on September 15, 2020, the user was asked to provide some means to study what was testified.
In anticipation of having material for analysis, it was defined a
Initial location situated within the Concepción area, at a place located approximately 3.4 kilometers southeast of the Control Tower of the Carriel Sur Airport (SCEI) of that city.

In its response email to the CEFAA request for study means, the user indicated that "...we realized that the lights belonged to a new LED electrical installation (light) on a hill in the area." Therefore, it retracted its report.

Images: Not provided by the user.
Conclusion: Based on the information provided by the user,
it was determined that what was initially reported as an aerial event
actually corresponded to the presence of an illuminated power line.

The case was classified as "of terrestrial origin."
