Case No. 844 01082018

Date: 31 July 2018.

Approximate Time: 20:30 local time.

Location: Cerrillos, Metropolitan Region.

Witnesses: B.C.

Images: N/A

Description: The user described the information as three triangular objects, with red and white lights, that appeared from “east to west” and remained stationary for an undetermined time, at a distance of one kilometer in a clear sky. She added that “For several days, these triangular objects have been seen.”
"Towards the coast and most recently towards the mountain range. Those from yesterday were 3 and placed in 3 points, east, center, and west."

Finally, the user testified that (sic): "For several days, we have observed these triangular flying objects in the sky, we think they are UFOs but they remain very still and it scares me."

No images were provided for the report as of July 31st.

The user's location was georeferenced at coordinates 33° 30' 53" S and 70° 43' 07" W, and 513 meters above sea level (Figure 1).
### Figure 1

The meteorological conditions were studied using observations made from the Arturo Merino Benítez (SCEL) Airport in Santiago, which confirmed that, around the time indicated by the user, there were particularly favorable conditions for aerial observation. Visibility was at least 10 kilometers, with no cloud cover below 10,000 feet and no presence of cumulonimbus clouds.

The analysis of the lighting and astronomical situation on the reported date revealed...
He confirmed that it was a nighttime scenario, without a moon. In fact, according to the information provided by the Hydrographic and Oceanographic Service of the Chilean Navy, the end of nautical twilight occurred at 19:00 (HL) and the rising of the moon was at 21:39 hours.

Figure 2 shows the horizon visible from the location mentioned by the user. The visible presence of the planets Venus, Jupiter, and Mars was verified, with their locations and degrees of visibility specified in Table 1.

Conclusion: According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the geographic, meteorological, and astronomical studies, it was found that what was reported could possibly be the planets Venus, Jupiter, and Mars. The absence of images did not allow for greater precision. Case classified as Probably Explained.
