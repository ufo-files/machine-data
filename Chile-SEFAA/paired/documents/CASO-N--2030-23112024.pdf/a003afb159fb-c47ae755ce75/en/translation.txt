Multiple air abnormalities were reported seen at "10:15" hours on 23 November 2024.
The reported information was described as more than three green lights, with a size of 21 cm to 1 meter, visible to two witnesses, flying from northeast to northwest, in a clear sky.

The reporter stated that he/she saw many lights in the same direction of verdant color, which he/she assimilated into "caravans." First, about seven with an interval of about five seconds between them. Then, farther away and with a spacing.
A temporal similar to the previous one, another group of about 6 more lights.

User description:

Regarding the flight direction of these objects, he added, "It's as if I were seeing the mountain range here in Chillán and the lights will travel towards Neuquén."

No audiovisual file was attached to the report, and later the files were received: "VID-20241123-WA0045, VID-20241123-WA0047, VID-..."
"20241123-WA0049, VID-20241123-WA0053, and VID-20241123-WA0055".

The camera's location was georeferenced at coordinates 36° 37’ 54’’ S, 72° 02’ 51’’ W and 152 m.s.n.m., approximately 5.6 kilometers south of the General Bernardo O'Higgins Airport in Chillán.

The videos supporting the report were perused, confirming they had not been visually altered. Since only the file "VID-20241123-" was available for analysis, it was not possible to verify potential alterations in the other videos.
Investigation: "WA0055.mp4", was moderately visible, was selected as study material, which was renamed 2030-5 and studied in each of its frames.

The forensic examination of this recording determined that it was made by the main camera of a cell phone, at 22:58 (HL) on November 23, 2024.

Air traffic conditions were consulted based on the metadata of all videos, identifying the flight SKU076 by Sky Airlines, originating from El Tepual Airport in Puerto Montt, as the material used for analysis.
It is destined for Santiago. Its flight level was 11,575 meters, its speed 735 km/h and its course north.

According to the analysis of the testimony and the results of the photographic, geographical, forensic and air traffic studies, it can be estimated that what was observed in the video VID-20241123-WA0055.mp4 corresponds to the navigation lights of the Sky Airline flight SKU076 between Puerto Montt and Santiago.
