A report of what was considered an aerial anomaly was made at "10:15" hours on 29 July 2024.
The reported information described an amorphous, black object with no indicated size, but visible for more than a minute flying always northwest from a witness, in a clear sky.

The user testified that “... where I live, planes from Santiago airport always pass by, so I am used to seeing them, so when I saw the object, I thought it was another plane, but it caught my attention that it looked 'like'..."
Description from "far away" or "very small" in relation to the planes I usually see, the user: I started observing and noticed that it wasn't moving, that's when I began taking pictures and recording it. It stayed in the same spot for about 20 minutes, I didn't see any lights, but it did seem to change color from black to silver, as if it were spinning on itself."

Along with the report, three photos were uploaded from the files.
"IMG_3645.jpeg", "IMG_3646.jpeg", and "IMG_3648.jpeg". Subsequently, four recordings were received in the files "IMG_3644.mov", "IMG_3647.mov", "IMG_3649.mov", and "IMG_3650.mov".

Based on what is visible in the recording and geographic elevation, it was defined that the filming was carried out at 123° (Figure 2). The extension of this visual leads to the southern head of the El Bosque Air Base (SCBQ).

The examination of what was recorded by the radars showed a DHC-6 Twin Otter aircraft of the Air Force operating at 10,700 feet at a course of 80°.
He also revealed the absence of air activity around the military airfield.
When consulted, the Air Force crew indicated that they did not observe objects
around the aircraft during its flight nor at the coordinates where it was filmed.

The meteorological conditions were studied based on observations made from the Arturo Merino Benítez Airport in Santiago. According to these observations, at the time of the filming, visibility was 6,000 meters.
The winds were weak, 0 to 5.6 km/h, and basically came from the north.
