This letter is in response to your Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request dated and received
November 17, 2020, by the U.S. Department of Agriculture's Forest Service Washington Office
FOIA Service Center and assigned tracking number 2021-FS-WO-00884-F. You requested
"records...of all reports of Unidentified Flying Objects (UFOs) received by the USFS either by
the public, or by USFS employees/personnel." Additionally, you specified records created after
the records you previously received in response to 2017-FS-WO-00542-F.
In response to your request 2021-FS-WO-00884-F, we conducted a search and found one (1)
responsive page: one (1) page is enclosed in part. Redactions were made pursuant to Exemption
6 of the FOIA, 5 U.S.C. 552(b)(6).
FOIA Exemption 6
Exemption 6 requires the Government to withhold all information about individuals in
"personnel and medical files and similar files," where disclosure of such information "would
constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy." Exemption 6 also involves a
balancing of the public's interest in disclosure against the individual's privacy interest.
Pursuant to FOIA Exemption 6, we have redacted a website comment poster's name and
reference to another third-party. These individuals have a clear privacy interest in the protection
of this information, and there is little or no public interest in its release. The individual privacy
rights outweigh the public's interest in this information.
The FOIA provides that requesters pay the reasonable costs for search, review, and duplication
of records. Pursuant to Title 7, Code of Federal Regulations, Subtitle A, Part 1, Subpart A,
Appendix A, there is no charge as the amount falls below the minimum required to collect fees.
This concludes the Forest Service's response to your FOIA request.
The FOIA provides you the right to appeal this response. Any appeal must be made in writing,
