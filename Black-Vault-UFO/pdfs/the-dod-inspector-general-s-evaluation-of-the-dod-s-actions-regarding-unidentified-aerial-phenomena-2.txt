disclosure of this information is likely to diminish the candor of agency deliberations in
the future;
• (b)(6), which pertains to information, the release of which would constitute a clearly
unwarranted invasion of personal privacy; and
• (b)(7)(E), which pertains to records or information compiled for law enforcement
purposes, the release of which would disclose techniques and procedures for law
enforcement investigations or prosecutions.
