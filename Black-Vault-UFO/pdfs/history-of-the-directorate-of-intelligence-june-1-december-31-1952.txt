The responsibilities of the Director of Intelligence are broad in
scope and entail such matters ass planning and executing functions to
ensure the prevention of strategic, tactical, or technological surprise
from any source; providing & sound basis for counsel upon air pre-
paredness; assuring adequate support for planning and conducting air
operations.
Io accomplish these responsibilities, delegation of authority for
certain accomplishments within these areas is necessarily nade to Deputy
Directors and comparable Chiefs under the supervision of the Director.
The specific accomplishments of these Deputy Directors and Chiefs are
expressed in Annexes I through V; these annexes are integral parts of
the "History of the Directorate of Intelligence", while this partieular
volume is intended primarily to reflect the activities of the immediate
