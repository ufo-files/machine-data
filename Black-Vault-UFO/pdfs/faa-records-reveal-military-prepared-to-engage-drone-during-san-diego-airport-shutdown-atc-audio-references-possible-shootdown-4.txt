J1. Summary - provide a brief summary for all MORs in this section that will provide enough information for QA to understand
what occurred. Include information about items that require additional information in the specific MOR you are reporting.


   1ST OBJECT @2054Z

   MULTIPLE TAXIING A/C REPORTED DRONE ABOUT 003 FEET TO 015 FEET DIRECTLY OVER THE RUNWAY
   DEPARTURE END BETWEEN B7 AND B10. ALL DEPARTURES STOPPED. TOWER WAS UNABLE TO GET VISUAL
   WITH THIS OBJECT BUT AT LEAST 7 PLUS A/C KEPT REPORTING IT. AT APPROXIMATELY 2130Z TOWER WAS ABLE
   TO GET A VISUAL WITH A SILVER OBJECT HOVERING NORTH OF RUNWAY OVER TAXIWAY F AFTER A UAL FLIGHT
   AT THE END REPORTED IT AT 010 TO 015 FEET. ASTREA POLICE HELICOPTER WAS ABLE TO GET A VISUAL WITH
   THE OBJECT AT 2140Z BUT UNABLE TO GET VIDEO.

   2ND OBJECT 2200Z

   SWA1562 REPORTED DRONE SILVER IN COLOR 1 MILE OFF DEPARTURE END AT 010 FEET NORTH OF THE
   RUNWAY. ANTOHER SWA FLIGHT ALSO REPORTED SAME OBJECT AT 008 FEET 8 MINUTES LATER. BUT THIS
   TIME THE TOWER LOST SIGHT OF OBJECT 1 SO THIS COULD BE POTENTIALLY BE THAT OBJECT. ASTREA
   RETURED BUT UNABLE TO GET VIDEO OF OBJECT.



   3RD OBJECT 2130Z

   NAVY NORTH ISLAND CALLED STATING THE MILITARY HAD AN UNATHORUIZED DRONE THEY WERE PREPARING
   TO ENGAGE.

   DEN, JSEN, ZEBRA, AND HPD NOTIFIED.



                                                              QA Findings

   An analysis of the event was completed by reviewing the MOR summary. Lat/Long data input by QA using position reports,
   multiple pilots both airborne and taxiing reported a UAS in the vicinity of SAN airport. Naval Air Station south of SAN also
   reported UAS activity during a one-hour time frame. Reported altitude was approximately 300- 1,500ft. J. Koble 9/8/2026
