                                                                              how a flood of digital classification has overwhelmed the federal

                                                                              a significant portion of that amount, while undermining trans-

                                                                              the solution—modernization of the declassification system. Senator
                                                                              Jerry Moran and I have introduced bipartisan legislation (S. 3733)
