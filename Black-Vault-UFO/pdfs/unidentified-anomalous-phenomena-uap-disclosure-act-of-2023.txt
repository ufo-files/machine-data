20                 (A) an intelligence agent whose identity
21             currently requires protection;

25             which has not been officially disclosed, the dis-

 4             the military defense, intelligence operations, or
 5             conduct of foreign relations of the United
 6             States, the disclosure of which would demon-
 7             strably and substantially impair the national se-
 8             curity of the United States;
 9             (2) the public disclosure of the unidentified
10      anomalous phenomena record would reveal the name
11      or identity of a living person who provided confiden-
12      tial information to the Federal Government and
13      would pose a substantial risk of harm to that per-
14      son;

16      anomalous phenomena record could reasonably be
17      expected to constitute an unwarranted invasion of
