Let me first welcome the Secretary-General back from his
pilgrimage for peace in the Middle East. Hundreds of thousands
have already fallen in the bloody conflict between Iran and Iraq.
All men and women of goodwill pray that the carnage can soon be
stopped. We pray that the Secretary-General proves to be not
only a pilgrim but also the architect of a lasting peace between
those two nations. Mr. Secretary-General, the United States
supports you. May God guide you in your labors ahead.

kind of pilgrimage. We come from every continent, every race,
and most religions to this great hall of hope where, in the name
of peace, we practice diplomacy. Now, diplomacy, of course, is a
subtle and nuanced craft - so much so that it is said that when ;
one of the most wily diplomats of the 19th century passed away,
other diplomats asked, on reports of his death, "What do you
