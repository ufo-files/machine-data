This is a final response to FOIA request 21-F-1322, July 21, 2021, to the Office of the Secretary

received your request from the Office of the Assistant to the Secretary of Defense for Privacy, Civil
Liberties and Transparency on October 3, 2024, requesting an additional review of the responsive
document provided to you. Your reopened request was assigned case number 21-FR-1322. Please use
this reference number when referring to your request.
The Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence & Security, All-domain
Anomaly Resolution Office, a component of OSD, and Defense Intelligence Agency conducted an
additional review of the records. The Initial Denial Authorities have determined that portions of the
two responsive pages are exempt from release pursuant to 5 U.S.C. § 552:
(b)(3), information specifically exempted by a statute establishing particular criteria for
withholding, applying 10 USC § 130b which pertains to personnel in overseas, sensitive or
routinely deployable units; and 5 U.S.C. 10 U.S.C. § 424 which pertains to organization or
any function of an organization, and certain information pertaining to, employees of the
Defense Intelligence Agency, the National Reconnaissance Office, and the National
Geospatial-Intelligence Agency; and
(b)(6), as disclosure of the information would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of
the personal privacy of individuals.
Please note that we have considered the foreseeable harm standard when reviewing records
and applying exemptions under the FOIA in the processing of this request. Additionally, in this
instance, fees for processing your request were below the threshold for requiring payment. Please
note that fees may be assessed on future requests.

have concerns about service received by our office, please contact a member of our Leadership
Team at 571-372-0498 or Toll Free at 866-574-4970.
