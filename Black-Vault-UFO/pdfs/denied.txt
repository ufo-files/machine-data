This responds to your request of 9 May 2019 appealing the National Security
Agency's (NSA) withholding decisions for documents responsive to your request for

105687, and the related document material. I have determined that the decision

The information that continues to be withheld by NSA has been determined to be
properly classified in accordance with E.O. 13526. The information denied meets the
criteria for classification as set forth in Section 1.4 subparagraphs (b), (c), and (d) and
remains classified TOP SECRET and SECRET, as provided in Section 1.2 of E.O.
13526.

concerning its activities. The withheld information remains protected pursuant to
Section 3.5 (c) of E.O. 13526, which provides for the withholding of information

Section 6, Public Law 86-36 (50 U.S. Code 3605, formerly 50 U.S. Code 402 note).
