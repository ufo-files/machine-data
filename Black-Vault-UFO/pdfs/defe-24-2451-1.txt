e   for an aerial object so I decided to look at it in more detail
    when the jet was out of sight, but when I did that and swung
    back to the object it was not there, neither was it anywhere
    else as I scanned the whole northern sky, left to right and
    up and down. Whatever it was it had either moved off at great
    speed, as I had only moved my binoculars off it for four seconds,
    or it had just disappeared. When I thought about it disappearing
    I wondered if the jet exhaust of the 747 had disturbed the air
    or atmosphere in some way that made the object appear from a
    previously invisible condition and that it was still there or
    nearby but I could not now see it.

         Now let me describe the object, which I do not consider to
        be a UFO as I did not see it move but rather an Unidentified
        Stationary Aerial Object. It appeared to be a dull metal box
        in the sky. I could not tell whether it was a house-sized object
        relatively near or a much larger object far away. As seen it
        was slightly smaller than the tailfin of the airliner. If it
        was near and yet beyond the jet it had the bulk of a three
        bedroom house. It seemed to me rectangular., a box with all edges
        and corners rounded, of a dull yet shiny, silver material with
        what appeared to be a dark stripe across the side facing me.
        I got the impression that the black stripe was a type of window
        and that the object was looking not at me but to the east (right)
        of me into an area within a few hundred metres of me. No matter
        how hard I try to describe it I always come back to a silver
        metal box with rounded edges and no protrusions just sitting
        there looking at something, not attached to anything, not near
        anything and undefinable as to true size and distance.

          Another interesting point is its posture. An aircraft in flight
        has a certain posture related to the intentions of its crew
        as to what the aircraft is about to do. A ground attack has
        a nose-down attitude and is travelling at speed. An aircraft
        about to land has a nose-up attitude and is travelling relatively
        slowly. A high flying heavy bomber settles early into a stable
        approach to its target. Paratroop dropping aircraft travel in
        groups by day on a steady course towards the dropping zone.
        In the case of this object it was static, comfortable in its
        posture and seemed to consider itself unseen and safe. It was
        not a balloon as we do have hot air balloons about, but only
        in the very early morning, and I know the shape, size and colour
        of Melbourne’s balloons. It was definitely there and it did
        not seem to move away at any definable speed, it just disappeared
        within four seconds.

          Now here is the strange confirmation of what I saw in
        Australia. Some nine months later I was watching a TV program
        made in America about UFOs when it showed a segment from Canada
        where a couple had been at their holiday cottage on the banks
        of a lake in Ontario. The husband had taken a cine film of the
        view from the verandah of the cottage and did not notice anything
        unusual until viewing his finished film which the program showed.
        There, above the water, in the sky in the distance was exactly
        the object I had seen, the same rounded silver box shape with
        the vague black band across the front but in a more horizontal
        posture. So I have seen a UFO in Australia which I have also
        seen on a film from Canada.
