Dear Mr. Greenewald:

    This letter constitutes a final response to your Freedom of Information Act request
for all documents relating to the United States Army Space and Missile Defense
Command’s correspondence between Dr. Travis Taylor and Mr. John Stratton, including
documentation provided to and correspondence with Dr. Taylor including the keywords
"Unidentified Aerial" and/or "Unidentified Flying" and/or "UAP" and/or "UFO" and/or
"Unidentified Spacecraft" and/or "Unidentified aircraft" And/or AAWSAP And/or
“Advanced Aerospace” and/or Skinwalker, and includes attachments to emails found.
The date range for this request is 1 January 2019 to 1 May 2021. You were previously
notified that your request was assigned the following tracking number: SMDC FA-22-
0005. You were also previously notified that certain records were referred to the
Department of the Navy for FOIA processing as such records were not records of the
Department of the Army.

    In regard to the request for classified information related to the above request,
certain information requested is denied in part as controlled information not subject to
disclosure in accordance with Exemption 3 (5 U.S.C. Section 552 (b)(3)), which allows
for the withholding of information prohibited from disclosure by another federal statute.
With respect to these withholdings, 10 U.S.C. Section 130 authorized the Secretary of
Defense to withhold from public disclosure any technical data with military or space
application in the possession of, or under the control of, the Department of Defense, if
such data may not be exported lawfully outside the United States without an approval,
authorization, or license under the Arms Export Control Act (22 U.S.C. Section 2751 et
seq.).

     Also, certain information is exempt from disclosure under Exemption 5 (5 U.S.C.
Section 552 (b)(5)). This exemption protects inter-agency or intra-agency
memorandums or letters which would not be available by law to a party other than in
litigation with the agency. Additionally, certain information is withheld under attorney
work product, attorney client and deliberative process privileges.

  Additionally, certain information which is exempt from the disclosure provisions of
FOIA is being withheld. The names, grades, and other personally identifying information

   Withholding this information constitutes a partial denial of your request. You may
appeal this determination, within sixty days of the date of this letter, through this office to
the Office of the Staff Judge Advocate, ATTN: SMDC-JA, P.O. Box 1500, Huntsville, AL
35807.

    We are also informing you that certain records and your request have been referred

letter, you must provide a statement of the reasons why you believe this initial decision
to be in error. The appeal envelope and letter must be clearly marked “Freedom of
Information Act Appeal.”
