Subject: Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) Request 2026-05893

This is an Air Traffic Organization, Mission Readiness Services, Western Service Center, partial
no-records response to your FOIA request received by the Federal Aviation Administration
(FAA) on September I 0, 2026, made under the provisions of Title 5 United States Code, Section
552 (5 U.S.C. § 552). You requested records regarding drone and balloon activity at San Diego
Airport Traffic Control Tower (SAN) on September 4, 2026.

A search for records was conducted at SAN and by the Quality Control Group (QCG). The
search revealed the following responsive records, which are contained on the enclosed compact
disc :

SAN

•   Audio re-recording from the Ground Control position, dated September 4, 2026
•   Audio re-recording from the Local Control position, dated September 4, 2026

QCG

•   Two Mandatory Occurrence Reports

Please be advised that voice recordings have an encoded time source on the right channel and
can be listened to on any media player by adjusting the balance to the full left position.

No records were found responsive to that portion of your request that sought transcripts, facility
logs, decisional documents, and FAA coordination with San Diego International Airport and/or
law enforcement, because the FAA did not create, maintain, or control these records.

There is no charge for this request because, under the "Media" fee category, you were not
charged for search and review time, or for duplication fees for fewer than I 00 pages.
