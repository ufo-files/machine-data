                                                 10 January 1967


          MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD

          SUBJECT:   Return of Wreckage of A-12 #125 to Area 51


               1. On 10 January 1967                      Deputy
          Chief, Security Staff, Area 51, advised telephonically
          that two flat-bed truck. loads of the wreckage of A-12
          #125 had been returned to Area 51 last night {9 Jan 1967).
         ~--------~1 advised that because of the size of the pieces
          it had been necessary to place same in boxes for return
          to the Area. An Area·51 helicopter escorted the trucks
          and assured that no one was following before the trucks
          turned into the Area. The whole operation was completed
          securely, without incident.

               2.             advised that it would be at least two
          more days before the remainder of the wreckage could be
          returned to the Area. He advised further that because of
          the terrain it would be most difficult to be assured that
          all of the wreckage had been recovered.
