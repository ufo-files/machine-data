Dear Mr. Greenewald

        This is in response to your July 17, 2013 Freedom of Information Act request for all
documentation supporting the theory that there were A-10s ejecting leftover flares instead of
there being a UFO sighting on March 13, 1997. In accordance with the Air Force Records
Disposition Schedule, Table 13-06, Rule 01.00 and Air Force Instruction 13-204 Volume 3,
Operational Data; these documents are not maintained beyond 6 months. The requested
documents have been destroyed therefore we were unable to view any logs corresponding to the
date mentioned.

         If you interpret this “no records” response as an adverse action, you may appeal our
decision by writing to the Secretary of the Air Force within 60 days from the date of this letter.
If no appeal is received, or if the appeal is postmarked after the conclusion of this 60-day period,
the appeal may be considered closed. Include your reasons for reconsideration along with a copy
of this letter. You may submit your appeal via our electronic system at
https://www.efoia.af.mil/palMain.aspx or mail to the Secretary of the Air Force, Thru: HQ
ACC/A6CK (FOIA), 180 Benedict Avenue, Suite 210, Joint Base Langley-Eustis AFB VA
23665-1993.
