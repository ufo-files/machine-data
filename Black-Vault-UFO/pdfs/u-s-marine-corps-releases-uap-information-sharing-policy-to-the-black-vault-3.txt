This responds to your April 14, 2024, request for records of the Marine Corps policy and
procedure for reporting information to the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO).
You also requested "adopted procedures" maintained from prior policies and procedures for
reporting information to AARO's predecessors. We received your request on April 15th and
assigned it file number 2024-USMCFOIA-001666.
We initiated a search of the files maintained by the office of the Deputy Commandant for
Plans, Policies, and Operations (PP&O), which provided the enclosed USMC General
Administration Message (GENADMIN), dated December 8, 2023. We have determined that the
record is releasable, though you will see that some information has been redacted. Specifically,
individuals' names, plus any information that might tend to identify them, have been withheld
pursuant to FOIA exemptions (b)(6) and (b)(7)(C), which prohibit disclosure of personal
information when an individual's privacy interest in it outweighs any public interest. In addition,
10 U.S.C. § 130b precludes the public disclosure of personally identifying information (e.g.,
name, rank, and duty address) of any DOD employee assigned to a sensitive unit. PP&O also
intormed us that they had no written reporting procedures prior to the introduction of the
GENADMIN and, therefore, possess no responsive records for that portion of your request.
If you consider this an adverse determination, you may submit an appeal by writing to the
Department of the Navy, Office of the Judge Advocate General (Code 14), 1322 Patterson Ave
SE, Suite 3000, Washington Navy Yard, DC 20374-5066 or, if you prefer, via e-mail to
Lieutenant Brandon Maitlen, USN at brandon.r.maitlen.mil@us.navy.mil and/or Ms. Wendy
Winston at wendy.a.winston.civ@us.navy.mil. Your appeal, if any, must include a statement
indicating why you believe it should be granted. It must be postmarked or timestamped within
90 calendar days from the date of this letter.

(SUA) WITHOUT PRIOR AUTHORIZATION OR COORDINATION.
3.A.5. (U) UAP INCURSION IS ANY UAP INCIDENT IN, ON, OR NEAR U.S.
MILITARY INSTALLATIONS, OPERATING AREAS, TRAINING AREAS, SUA, OR
PROXIMITY OPERATIONS, AND/OR OTHER AREAS OF INTEREST. OTHER AREAS OF
INTEREST INCLUDE BUT ARE NOT LIMITED TO U.S. CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE
AND NATIONAL DEFENSE EQUITIES OF ALLIED MILITARY AND INTELLIGENCE
COALITIONS (E.G., FIVE EYE).
3.A.6. (U) UAP ENGAGEMENT IS A KINETIC OR NON-KINETIC RESPONSE TO A
UAP, INTENDED TO DENY, DISRUPT, OR DESTROY THE PHENOMENON AND/OR ITS
OBJECT (S) •
3.A.7. (U) UAP INTERROGATION IS THE ELICITATION OF UAP LOCATION,

ACTIVE SENSING CAPABILITIES-INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO ELECTRO-
OPTICAL/IMAGERY, INFRARED/THERMAL, RADIOFREQUENCY/RADAR,
LIGHT/LASER/LIDAR/LADAR, ELECTROMAGNETIC, GRAVITATIONAL, AND
RADIOACTIVE MEANS.
3.A.8. (U) UAP ATTRIBUTION IS THE ASSESSED NATURAL OR ARTIFICIAL
SOURCE OF THE PHENOMENON AND INCLUDES SOLAR, WEATHER, TIDAL EVENTS,
U.S. GOVERNMENT, SCIENTIFIC, INDUSTRY, AND PRIVATE ACTIVITIES, AND
FOREIGN (ALLIED OR ADVERSARY) GOVERNMENT, SCIENTIFIC, INDUSTRY, AND
PRIVATE ACTIVITIES.
3.A.9. (U) UAP HAZARD IS A SAFETY RISK TO PERSONS, MATERIAL, OR
INFORMATION (E.G., FROM COLLISION) BY A UAP (S) THAT DO NOT
DEMONSTRATE HOSTILE INTENT.
