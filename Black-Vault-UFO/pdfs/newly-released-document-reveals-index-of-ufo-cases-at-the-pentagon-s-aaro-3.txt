This is a final response to your December 6, 2022 Freedom of Information Act (FOIA)
request, a copy of which is enclosed for your convenience. We received your request on
December 6, 2022, and assigned it FOIA case number 23-F-0204. We ask that you use this
number when referring to your request.

Office of the Secretary of Defense, conducted a search of their records systems and located a
document, totaling two pages, determined to be responsive to your request. It has been
determined that this document is appropriate for release in its entirety, without excision.
This constitutes a full grant of your request and closes your case file in this office. There
are no assessable fees associated with this response.
