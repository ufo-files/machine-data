PART B: SUMMARY OF THE WORKING GROUP’S DISCUSSIONS
The only information available to the Working Group was the report from the pilot of the B737,
photographs from the radar recordings, and reports from the appropriate ATC and operating
authorities.

have become more prevalemin the Sand SE of the country. Members took note of the AIS(M)
