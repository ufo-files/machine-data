WASHINGTON
TARY
SEP 2 7 1955
Dear Senator Kennedy:
In your recent referral to the Department of
Defense you requested a report in behalf of Mr. Charles J.
Quagliana relative to unidentified flying objects (UFO's).
The Air Force does not withhold or censor any in-
formation on UFO's. The investigations have always been
made available to bona fide news representatives and
scientific researchers. Press releases are issued as
warranted, and an annual report on the project is avail-
able to the public. A copy of this report is attached
for your information.
In 18 years of investigating over 9,000 reports
of UFO's, the evidence has proved almost conclusively
that reported aerial phenomena have been objects either
created and sent aloft by man, generated by atmospheric
conditions, or caused by celestial bodies or the residue
of meteoric activity.
The Air Force has lost one aircraft and pilot in
connection with a UFO incident. Captain Mantell was
asked to identify a UFO while on an aircraft ferrying
mission in 1952 over Godman Field in Kentucky. Because
his aircraft was not outfitted with the necessary oxygen
equipment for a high altitude flight, Captain Mantell
died of oxygen starvation while attempting to identify
the object, and his aircraft consequently crashed. A
copy of the Air Force evaluation of this case is also
attached for your further inforpation.
