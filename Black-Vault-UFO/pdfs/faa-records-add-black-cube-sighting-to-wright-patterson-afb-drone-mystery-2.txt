This letter responds to your Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request, 2025-04622, dated
July 11, 2025. Specifically, this letter responds to the portion of your request that sought
records of interagency coordination or notifications referencing drone activity or UAS
incursions in the vicinity of WPAFB between December 13, 2024, and January 31, 2025,
including any messages from the FAA's Domestic Events Network (DEN), System Operations
Security (SysOps), Air Traffic Security Coordinator (ATSC), or Air Traffic Organization
(ATO). The remaining portions of your request will be answered by another FAA office under
a separate cover.
A search was conducted by the Air Traffic Organization, System Operations Services, ATO
Security Directorate, however, a portion of the records located are being withheld under FOIA
Exemption 3. FOIA Exemption 3 permits the withholding of records specifically exempted
from disclosure by another Federal statute. In this instance, 10 USC § 130e states, in part, that
the Department of Defense (DOD) may exempt critical infrastructure security information
where such disclosure would reveal vulnerabilities that, if exploited, would likely result in the
significant disruption, destruction, or damage of or to DOD operations, property or facilities. In
a letter dated June 2, 2017, the DOD informed the FAA that DOD flight data constitutes DOD
Critical Infrastructure Information (DCRIT) and is therefore exempt from disclosure in
accordance with this statute.
If you owe fees for the processing of this request, an invoice containing the amount due and
payment instructions will be enclosed with this letter.

questions or need assistance, you may contact the FAA FOIA Public Liaison directly at (202)
267-7799 or by email to 9-afn-foia-public-liaison@faa.gov with "Public Liaison" in the subject
