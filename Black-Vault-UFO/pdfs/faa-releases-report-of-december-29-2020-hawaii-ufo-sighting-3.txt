Subject: Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) Request 2021-002372 WS
This is an Air Traffic Organization (ATO), Mission Support Services, Western Service Center
(WSC), partial denial, partial no records response to your FOIA request dated January 4, 2021,
made under the provisions of Title 5 United States Code (U.S.C), Section 552. You
subsequently narrowed the scope of your FOIA request on March 17, 2021. You requested
copies of Air Traffic Services data from any Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) Air Traffic
Control facility related to an unidentified flying object on December 29, 2020, near Honolulu,
Hawaii.
A search for records was conducted by the Honolulu Control Facility. This search revealed the
following records responsive to your request which have been saved to the enclosed compact
disc:
• One redacted copy of the Mandatory Occurrence Report on December 29, 2020.
We have withheld information under Exemption 6 of the FOIA, Title 5 U.S.C. Section 552
(b)(6) that protects personally identifiable information which, if released, would cause a
unwarranted invasion of personal privacy. We have determined that the personal privacy
interests involved outweigh any public interest in the release of this information. The withheld
information consists of personnel names and personal cell phone numbers, which have been
clearly marked on the enclosed records.
There were no records responsive to your request, which sought audio re-recordings and radar
data, because your request was received outside of the 45 day record retention period and this
incident was classified as a 45-day file. The FAA retention and destruction regulations are
outlined in FAA Order 7210.3BB, Facility Operation and Administration.
Your request qualifies for the "Media" fee category, whereby you are not charged for search
time, any review time, and the first 100 pages of documents. There were no fees incurred in
processing your request. An invoice is enclosed for your reference.
The undersigned and Clark Desing, Director, ATO, WSC, are responsible for this partial
denial, partial no records response. You may request reconsideration of this determination
through electronic mail at FOIA-Appeals@faa.gov or by writing to the address below:
