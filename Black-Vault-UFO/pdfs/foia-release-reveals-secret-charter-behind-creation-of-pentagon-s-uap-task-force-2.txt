Subj: (U) CHARTER FOR UNIDENTIFIED AERIAL PHENOMENON TASK FORCE
(U) This charter establishes the Congressionally mandated, Deputy Secretary of Defense directed
Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon Task Force. It provides the background giving rise to the Task
Force, defines the Task Force's objectives, focus areas, and roles and responsibilities, and also
sets forth the minimum coordination and reporting required of the Task Force.
1. (U) Purpose. The purpose of the Task Force is to create a single entity tasked with working
across the Department of Defense, and with the Intelligence Community (IC) and the
Interagency, to develop and execute plans to collect, exploit, and analyze operational and
scientific and technical data on unexplained Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon (UAP) currently
posing a threat to U.S. National Security. UAP are defined as any aerial phenomenon that
cannot immediately be identified.
2. (S/REL USA, FVEY)Mission. The mission of the Task Force is to detect, analyze, catalog,
consolidate and exploit advanced non-traditional aerospace vehicles currently posing an
operational threat to U.S. National Security and avoid strategic surprise.
