 SUBJECT: FREEDOM OF INFORMATION ACT REQUEST CASE NUMBER
          DON-NAVY -2021-005821

   This letter is in response to your Freedom oflnformation Act (FOIA) request submitted Office of
Naval Operations FOIA Office on April 21, 2021, in which you requested: "all records pertaining to
an incident on July 15, 2019, approximately between the times of I 0:30pm. - II :30pm Pacific off the
coast of San Diego, CA, wherein the USS OMAHA (LCS 12) encountered and observed a "possible
"UAS", spherical in shape, moving towards the surface of the water and then disappearing. If this
yields a "no records" response, the search be widened to around the same time frame of the same
incident. In addition, I ask that you include all ship and deck logs for the USS OMAHA, for the
dates of July 13, 2019 - July 18, 2019." Your request was referred to this office on April 28, 2021,
with assigned case number DON-NAVY -2021-005821 and assigned internal tracking number
2021-58.

   Your request was processed in accordance with the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. § 552),
Part 701 of Title 32 of the Code of Federal Regulations, and the Department of the Navy Freedom of
Information Act Program (SECNA VINST 5720.42G).

   For the purpose of assessing FOIA processing fees, you have been categorized as a "media"
requester, and as such, only duplication fees are applicable to your request. In this instance, there are
no fee charges for the processing of your request by this office.

   USS OMAHA (LCS 12) conducted a thorough search of the ship's spaces for responsive records.
For this class of ship, while underway, deck logs are digital voice recordings. These recordings are
periodically written to optical media and physically shipped to Naval History and Heritage Command
(NHHC) for archiving and freeing up space on ship hard drives. The ship searched for digital copies,
CDIDVD formatted logs and voice recordings and paper copies for any UAS incident occurring July
15, 2019 and deck logs from July 13 ~ 18, 2019, and concluded that the prior crew did not retain or
create records for July I - 18, 2019. NHHC conducted a thorough search of responsive records and
found none. Further, NHHC contacted the Federal Records Center (FRC) and were informed that
records for the specific dates did not exist in their archives. The loss of these records indicates their
system for record retention requires further attention. Nonetheless, NHHC was able to locate deck
logs for July 19 - 31, 2019, and provided to you in this release. Our review of the responsive records
reveal that they contain personal identifiers of third parties (such as names and/or social security

90 calendar days from the date of this letter. Please provide a letter requesting an appeal, with a

Information Act Appeal" or "Appeal of Records." You are encouraged (though not required) to
provide an explanation why you believe the redactions were inappropriate or our search was
inadequate. You may file your appeal by one of the two methods below:

         I. FOIA Online: This will work only if you set up an account on FOIA Online before
you make the request that you would like to appeal. To set up an account, go to FOIA Online
(www.foiaonline.gov). and click "Create Account" (a link located within the blue banner at the

"Save" (at the bottom left of the screen). With your account, you will have the ability to file an
appeal on FOIA Online to any request you submit on FOIA Online. To submit your appeal,
navigate to the "Search" tab and click on the "Search For" field. Enter your request tracking
number or a keyword. When the request is populated, locate the "Create Appeal" tab on the left
side of the screen. Complete the fields and "Save" and the system will automatically submit
your appeal.
