This letter is an intermediate response to your Freedom of Information Act (FOIA)
request, reference number DEVCOM GVSC FOIA FA-24-0006, dated December 14,
2023, wherein you requested a copy of the following 7 items:
1. All reports and evaluations, as stipulated in the schedule breakdown of the
GVSC CRADA with TTSA.
2. All e-mail communication about the GVSC CRADA with TTSA.
3. All letters, memos, and documents generated from the point the GVSC CRADA
with TTSA was signed to the final close out date of the GVSC CRADA with TTSA (i.e.
all records generated during the entire life of the CRADA).
Enclosed is the first release in response to your request, which consists of eight (8)
records previously provided as a discretionary release (259 pages), as well an
additional record (56 pages). After a thorough review of the records, we determined
portions of the released records are partially exempt from disclosure under Title 5
U.S.C.§ 552(b)(5) ("Exemption 5") and (b)(6) ("Exemption 6").
Exemption 5 protects against the release of predecisional and deliberative
information under the deliberative process privilege. One purpose of this privilege is to
protect the quality of agency decisions by encouraging frank and open discussions of
agency policy, however, these recommendations and opinions do not necessarily
represent the views of the Department of the Army. Revealing this information would
inhibit candor in the decision-making process, therefore, the information is exempt from
release.
Exemption 6 and Department of Defense policy protects Army personnel from an
unwarranted invasion of personal privacy. We removed the names, phone numbers,
and email addresses of personnel who are not general officers, are not division chiefs,
who do not normally work with the general public, or who are not federal employees.
