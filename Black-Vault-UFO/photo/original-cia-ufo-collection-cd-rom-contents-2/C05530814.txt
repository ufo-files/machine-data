166-SQUARE-MILE ISLAND. THE SIGHTINGS LASTED ABOUT 10 MINUTES, SOME

AIRCRAFT "AT A TERRIFIC SPEED" -- A LARGE ONE OUT FRONT, FOLLOWED BY
ABOUT FIVE OR SIX OTHERS.

GRENADA, SI LUCIA, AND MARTINIQUE.
