Honorable Margaret Chase Soith
 United States Senate
 Washington 25, D. C.
 Dear Senator Smith;
 Thank you for your letter of 2 January 1557,
 enclosing a letter from Mrs. Vincent J. Perry of Uo,cco.
 We will see to it that this letter is forwarded to
 the proper authorities for action.
 With kindest regards.
