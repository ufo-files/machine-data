MOSCOW AND SOVIET AND FOREIGN REPORTERS USED THE IV LINK-UP TO
SHOWER THE CREW WITH QUESTIONS.
THE SOYUZ IM-11 SPACE SHIP, CARRYING IWO SOVIET COSMONAUTS VIKIOR
AFANASYEV AND MUSA MANAROV, AND JAPANESE TELEVISION REPORTER
TOYEHIRO AKIYAMA, WAS LAUNCHED ON DECEMBER 2.

INTERNAIIONAL CREW JOINED GENNADIX MANAKOV AND GENNADIY STREKALOV
WHO ARE IN THEIR FIFTH MONTH IN ORBII.

THREE DAYS, BUT IS NOW FEELING ALL RIGHT.

BIOLOGICAL EXFERIMENT, WHICH IS PART OF A SPACE RESEARCH PROGRAMME.

"THE AIR OVER THE CARRIBEAN IS CLEAN AND BLUE.

FASTER THAN DURING HIS PREVIOUS FLIGHT.
