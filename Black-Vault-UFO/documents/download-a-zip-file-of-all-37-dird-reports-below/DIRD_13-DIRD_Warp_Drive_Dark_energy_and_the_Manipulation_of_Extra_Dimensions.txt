If one is to realistically entertain the notion of interstellar exploration in

these lengthy transit times would not make interstellar exploration impossible,
they would certainly dampen the enthusiasm of governments or private

theoretically they can exist as a valid solution to general relativity.

fabric of space in the immediate vicinity of a spacecraft. The basic idea is to
create an asymmetric bubble of space that is contracting in front of the
spacecraft while expanding behind it. Using this form of locomotion, the
spacecraft remains stationary inside this "warp bubble," and the movement of
space itself facilitates the relative motion of the spacecraft. The most
attractive feature of the warp drive is that the theory of relativity places no
known restrictions on the motion of space itself, thus allowing for a
convenient circumvention of the speed of light barrier.

profoundly alter the capacity to explore-and potentially to colonize-the

5. Extra Space Dimensions
In connection with the Casimir effect, extra dimensions provide a rich arena for one to

presence of the conducting plates imposes upon the quantum vacuum. The quantum

difference when compared to the quantum vacuum on the exterior region of the plates.
It is this pressure difference that causes the plates to attract.?
A very similar phenomenon to the Casimir effect can occur when the quantum vacuum

It was Riemann, with his development of differential geometry in the 19th century, who

provide an adequate description of nature. Improvements in physics led to Maxwell's

Nordstrom in 1914, who used a scalar potential for the gravitational field. Later Weyl

attempt involved an alteration of the geometry of spacetime in four dimensions. His

Einstein-Schrodinger non-symmetric field theory, which is widely regarded as the most
