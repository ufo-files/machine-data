9 p.m. last night and at once noticed what I took to be a rather
bright star low in the sky and that was changing colour like the

f'ew moments and then noticed how distinct and clear the colours red

it must be a helicopter, and went back in.     It continued to hover
f'or about another 5 minutes and then started going in various
evolutions fairly fast, I suppose it was really making circles and
then it went suddenly swiftly straight up and disappeared.     I
waited and watched f'or another 15 minutes but it did not return, it
is not there tonight.    The whole of' the time it was hovering it

heard no plane.
