  1.       Following receipt of Reference A, Operation ' Close

  2.        As requested by HQwLM, this report will outline , very

                                  METHOD
  3.         3CRU was directed at 2000 hours on Thursday 30 June 1983

  unit was organized into two shifts of 12 hours duration with
  sufficient technic ~ l staff on hand to provide a specialist for all

  by DOA memb ers .   Furthermore , nothing was seen on the TPS-43
  radar which correla ted with DOA sightings , so a team of three
  Air De f ence Controllers was despa tched to AACC Mascot to
  i nvestigate the matter in grea ter depth .
                                IlliSUIII'S

  doubt by Sunday 3 July 1983 tha t the unidentified objects
