Subject: Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) Request Number 2021-002114
This is in response to your FOIA request dated February 24, 2021, and your Appeal
2021-002133-520525 made under the provisions of Title 5 United States Code, Section 552.
You requested copies of certain records pertaining to an incident involving American
Airlines Flight 2292 that occurred on February 21, 2021, between 19:15 to 19:45 Greenwich
Mean Time (GMT), near Albuquerque, New Mexico.
A records search was conducted at the appropriate air traffic control facilities within the
Central Service Area. The search revealed records applicable to this request. However, we
are withholding a portion of an audio file from the Phoenix Tower, Local North Controller
Position. This file contains call sign and routing information related to the operation of
military aircraft in the National Airspace System under Exemption 3. There are no fees
associated with this request.
FOIA Exemption 3 provides that FOIA's disclosure requirements do "not apply to matters
that are specifically exempted from disclosure by statute." 5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(3). In this
instance, 10 U.S.C. § 130e states, in part, that the Secretary of Defense may exempt
Department of Defense (DoD) critical infrastructure security information where such
disclosure would reveal vulnerabilities that, if exploited, would likely result in the
significant disruption, destruction, or damage of or to DoD operations, property, or facilities.
In letters dated June 2, 2017, and November 8, 2021, the DoD informed the Federal
Aviation Administration (FAA) that information regarding the operation of DoD aircraft
constitutes DoD Critical Infrastructure Information and is, therefore, exempt from disclosure
in accordance with this statute.
Please be advised that the voice recordings have an encoded time source on the right channel
that can be heard on any media player by adjusting the balance to the full lett position.
