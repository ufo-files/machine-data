Belgian Air Force scrambled 2 F16 interceptors as a reaction to both visual and radar observation of a UFO

Catalog Entry: DEFE-24-1970-2
Special Comments: Belgian Air Force scrambled 2 F16 interceptors (Nos AL 17 and AL 23) from the base in Bevekom. Belgian AF found no evidence of any threat, and as a result they saw no requirement to inform any other countries or agencies.
