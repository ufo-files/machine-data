ACADEMY OF SCIENCES REVIEW OF COLORADO UNIVERSITY STUDY ON

UFO'S, POCKET NOVELS, E.G., PROJECT BLUE BOOK BY STEIGER

3. AT GRENADIAN REQUEST, MISOFFS MET WITH GAIRY, FRIDAY,
AND PERMRREP DOLLARD ON 30 NOVEMBER. GAIRY RECAPPED POINTS

IN FRIDAY'S SPEECH OF 29 NOVEMBER. HE INDICATED HE HAD CO-

AS OUTLINED IN 2, ABOVE, BUT WISHED US SUPPORT. HE PRODUCED
REVISED DRAFT RESOLUTION CALLING FOR SECRETARY GENERAL TO

CONDUCT SURVEY IN LIEU OF ESTABLISHMENT OF A SPECIAL COMMITTEE.
HE REQUESTED US POSITION ON AMENDED RESOLUTION. HE INDICATED

THAT ORIGINAL DRAFT RES WOULD NOT BE TABLED IF US WOULD
SUPPORT THE REVISED VERSION. GAIRY WAS CORDIAL AND EMPHASIZED

EVIDENCE OF US FRIENDSHIP.

MISOFF LATER INFORMED FRIDAY THAT US COULD NOT
REPEAT, NOT REPORT REVISED RES.

4. IN SPECIAL POLITICAL COMMITTEE MEETING AFTERNOON OF

30 NOVEMBER , FRIDAY, INTRODUCED REVISED DRAFT RESOLUTION,
TEEXT OF WHICH FOLLOWS IN PARA 7.

5. AMB. UPADHYAY (NEPAL) REQUESTED THAT COMMITTEE DELAY
FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF ITEM UNTIL 5 DECEMBER TO ALLOW TIME

TO STUDY RESOLUTION AND SEEK INSTRUCTIONS. IT WAS SO DECIDED AND
IN THE ABSENCE OF ANY ADDITIONAL SPEAKERS, THE MEETING WAS

ADJOURNED.

6. UKUN INFORMED US IT IS TOTALLY OPPOSED TO THE ITEM AND
HAS PRESENT INSTRUCTIONS FROM LONDON TO VOTE AGAINST ANY UFO
RESOLUTION. ADDITIONALLY UK MISSION INFORMED US THAT IN A

TELEPHONE CONVERSATION BETWEEN GAIRY AND AMB. RICHARD (UKUN)
ON THIS ITEM GAIRY SAID HE HAD US SUPPORT FOR TABLED RESOLUTION.
