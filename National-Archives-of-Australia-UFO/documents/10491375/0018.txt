west coast of Flinders Island (Tas). It was found on the beach at Parry's Bay,
opposite the norther end of Flinders Island Aerodzome. The date of finding
was 15 May 1983, and there is reason to believe the piece had not been on the
beach more than a few days.
The part has been identified as having come from a Cessna 182
aircraft between a certain range of serial munbers.
The part is an engine
cowl flap, for the control of airflow over the engine. It is 300mm long by
210mm wide, and 40mm at its deepest point. It had two side panels when first
Tound. The piece was once white (acrylie paint), and is made of aluminium
composition, now greatly eroded. The operating bolt, of steel, while heavily
corroded, appears to have failed on impact or in flight (1.e. not by corrosion).
