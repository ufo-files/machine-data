Phone call from Inspector Pox, D24 (13.30) 23.10.78.
A Senior Constable Campbell of Forrest (052/36-6372) had a report
from some children of an aireraft towing a glider in the Bazwon Downs
(Apollo Bay) area at about 5.30-6.00 pom. on Saturday 21 October.

that part of the State.
