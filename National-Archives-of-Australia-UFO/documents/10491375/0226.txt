ANALYSIS OF OIL SAMPLES RECOVERED FROM BASS STRAIT
During the search for the missing C182 aircraft, VH-DSJ, a
search aircraft sighted an oil slick in the waters of Bass Strait
within the prime probability area. Two water samples were obtained
from this slick and forwarded to your Laboratory together with a
sample of the type of oil used in the engine of the missing aircraft.
Please accept this memorandum as a formal request for an
analysis of the water samples to determine if the oil content is the
same as that used in the aircraft.
It is understood that the samples obtained from the oil slick
were of poor quality. Any advice you may care to offer on obtaining
better quality samples will be welcomed by our Search and Rescue
organisation for the briefing of future searches.
I.S. SMITH
for DIRECTOR
