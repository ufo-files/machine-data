Witnesses observe multiple Soft milky-white, round lights, from tennis-softball size, came from the plateau or Homestead 2 tree line; Saw a group of three blue lights and a group of three green lights. While Kavanaugh and Dietz were on the back porch of the trailer at Homestead 1 throughout the evening, they observed numerous soft, milky-white round lights that ranged in size from a tennis ball to a softball. The objects appeared sporadically and usually originated from the treel line near Homestead 2 or to the northwest by the mesa. At one time they observed three light blue lights just east of Dogrun 1 about one foot above the ground. The three lights were about five feet apart and lined up from north to south. Later they observed a set of three green softball-sized lights one foot above the ground. All of the lights were only observed for approximately one second each..
