Observed red lights leap forging each other

On the way to pull our security shift I observed a series of red lights. The last light in the line would go out and then appear in the front of the line. I observed this for no more than a few minutes and continued onto my post. The phenomenon I observed was also observed by the OP which had a closer look to whatever which appeared to be moving past it.
