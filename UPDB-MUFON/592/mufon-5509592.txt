{kt}It flew NE approx 1000ft very slow enough time for me to get my wife to look at it

July 4th, 2013 my wife and I had finished preparing for a party we were having on July 5th. We were sitting on our back porch. We have a lot of big trees. I was on the outer edge of our porch. Then I saw it. Slow, silent and low. Lower than I see single engine plane fly. My wifes view was obscured by the trees. I yelled "what the f**k is that". It was moving slow enough that we could get out of our chairs, walk to a better view and watch it disappear into the distance. The event lasted about 30 seconds. Im not saying it was alien, but unidentifiable. From what I saw as it moved away, there was a shuttle-like shape to it. However, it was flying level and the orange glow was one big circle. Not 2 or more thrusters. As I watched it I thought I saw a winged shape at the sides of the glow.
I must say that my wife tried to write it off as a meteor. "I have witness 2 meteor land", I told her.
I live about 2 miles from Willow Grove Air Base. Aside from drawing a picture, I don't know what else to say.
I didn't think of reporting this, but on the 6th I watched Alien Mysteries and the subject was the Bristol, PA sighting about the sparkles in the trees, so I figured I send it in.
