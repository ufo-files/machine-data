While lighting fireworks the craft flew over my neighbors house

On fourth of July 2010 me and my family were outside in our yard lighting off fireworks , i had lit a roman candle and proceeded to walk towards my neighbors yard . The candle started shooting in the air and my Father was standing next to me . about half way through the candle a red orb like craft with three yellow lights under it flew over our neighbors house . The craft did not make a sound and was flying pretty low . My dad then screamed out whoaaaaa !!! Did you see that? I said hell yea i saw that it wasnt a plane not that low and that quiet . The sighting gave me a chill and I know it was something not from this earth. After the incident i told everyone what we had saw and they basically thought we were nuts. But I know what we saw and my father was the only witness.
