{dr}Saw total of three orange/yellow glowing orbs move in straightline

Was with family at our cottage on Miner Lake in Waupaca County at about 9:15 PM when my daughter noticed what first looked like a Chinese lantern in southwest sky moving due north. I really didn't bother to look for it through the trees then she said there are two more. I immediately went out on the dock and noticed two brightly shaped orange/yellow orb like likes (both staggered) but moving at exactly the same speed and direction under the clouds in a straight north direction over our lake. They were below the cloud cover and I watched them for about two minutes moving without any noise whatsoever. They then just faded out and that was the last I saw it.
