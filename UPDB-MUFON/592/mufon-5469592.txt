{rjl} Triange of lights seen over LIE by exit 49 on service road - north side of expressway

I was on the LIE headed east and exited at #49 headed south.

I looked to my left because i had a feeling i might see something and I saw 3 lights in the form of a triangle on the other side of the highway. I am including a map of the location taken from google maps.

I looked to my left and saw the triange of lights, but then it banked and turned and as it did i lost sight of each light. Seems the lights were on the bottom of the craft and it was headed away from me. As it headed away it banked hard to the right, and as it did the lights went behind the ship and I no longer saw them.

I looked to my right and saw 2 helecopters, or 1 helecoptor and 1 small 2 seater plane.

I looked back had lost sight of the craft.

The whole experience lasted about 45 seconds.
