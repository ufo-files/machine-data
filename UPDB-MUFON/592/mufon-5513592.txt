{dr}Mass of strange lights

At 8:15pm on dec 19 2012 on a a very clear cold night ...I heard area dog barking like crazy...went outside to see... I went outside and quickly notice the sky in front of me and saw mass of white and red flickering (brighter then led lights) ... spread out ...but not in a straight line ...cross the whole sky...I called out my sister to come and see...while I went to get my ipad to film...my ipad could not film the mass of lights.....there was a med size plane that was far far behind them..just watching them....the mass of lights headed west crossing the I-10 fwy...then they all disappear .. The night had stars ....these mass of light were not plane lights or stars....
It had no sound...like the plane....Im sure with so many lights in the sky .....that if it was a plane it would have had sounds ...more louder then the plane.... They were not sky lanterns or helium balloons with color led lights inside them..... I tried those theories....

This is my fourth sightings of strange lights ...on Nov 14 2012 @7:34pm in the east south of Redlands .....some called me to see what they where seeing in the night sky...it was a circle ring of rainbow color blended lights ..inside the rainbow lights was a black dot...I told that person it was a planet....but it was the only object in the sky that stood out... Then next day I looked up ufo shape and colors and I came across a video that was just like what we saw....

In June 19 2012 @2:40pm clear day...I saw in the southeast of Redlands close to Colton ave..a smooth metallic oval object flying in different patterns up at a angle then a quick zig zag like a snake moves.... It was not a helium balloon ..the metallic is different...plus the size of it from the high of a distance was the same size as if..... I would see a airplane flying in the sky....why I would say that is because after seeing that object ...a airplane flew by more then a few minutes after seeing this...

On Oct 15 3:20pm. ....east south of Dearborn I and a few others we drawn to a strange object..that look like a cancer ribbon ...but it was black matellic and block looking..with a diamond reflecting light in the middle....this was very big it was hovering between two houses and the golden angel...that sits on top of the church....I don't know how big weather balloons can get...but this was big like a two story house and high above 8 story buliding...it was a raining day...and then it disappear......I ask another what they thought it was they said ufo...
