{dr}4 Lights traveling counterclockwise, coming together and then moving apart

I was taking an evening walk around 7:15 pm and for some reason, I looked up at the sky. At the time, I was directly in front of my driveway, looking away from my home. I saw four oval-rectangular-shaped objects in the sky that were moving counter-clockwise, coming together and then splitting apart. I had no idea what they were. My son saw two more in another part of the sky. My husband and daughter also saw them. They were moving in a circle and then meeting up in the middle. This went on for about 2 hours, until they faded. They were - at one point - very bright in the sky, and that day was partly cloudy. It even sprinkled around 8 pm. My daughter and I watched them until they faded away. 

I reported the same thing in May 2014. That first sighting occurred on a starry, clear night at around 11:30 pm. My husband and daughter also saw them in May 2014. (Case #56154, FI closed the case as Unknown-Other). I tried to take a picture and video both times, but nothing came through. I was in awe to be honest. I wasn't afraid. In fact, I'd love to see them again. I posted on another site over a week ago, and the post that followed mine included an event that mirrored my description (same type of sighting, same night, same time) and was in a town not too far from mine. I know we witnessed the same thing.
