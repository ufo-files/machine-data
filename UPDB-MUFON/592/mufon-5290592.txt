A small dull white circular light crossing the sky.

I was in my room at night with my blinds open, when i happen to look outside into the city. I captured movement above with both eyes, and when I looked, I saw a circular dull white light travelling in a straight line up in the sky. At first i thought it was an asteroid, but it couldn't have been, as there was no dust trail and the light just kept going. The event lasted only a few seconds, as sadly I was indoors and the light disappeared into behind some buildings in the distance. 
I hurried to do google searches, but nothing was similar to what I saw. Until this day I keep doing research and still can't find anything. 
I'm not sure what it was, and it's a shame I don't have any proof to confirm it, but it was interesting and very odd.
