{tjd} 5 bright lights travelling in a perfectly straight line

5 bright lights travelling overhead, in a straight line, travelling northeast. The three lights in the front of the line were very bright, and unblinking/unpulsating. They stayed consistent in their flight path and did not swerve or waver. The two lights in the back of the line were slightly different. The were dimmer than the other 3, and pulsated. Theses two appeared to move a bit more, weaving back and forth between each other. I barely noticed this occurence, as they were moving at a very steady speed, and only saw them for a short amount of time before they disappeared behind the treeline. The lights were very high up, possibly even outside of the atmosphere. Because of this, their shape and color was impossible to determine. Not the first time I've seen formations of 5 lights in this area.
