{cak} I only noticed it from the corner of my eye, a silvery streak traveling across the sky.

I saw a UFO yesterday while sitting at my kitchen table eating dinner around 7 pm in Downtown Los Angeles. I only noticed the object because I saw this bright silvery streak traveling across the sky. I thought it was strange because I knew it was not an airplane. It was obviously in the sky, but it was traveling at an upward pitch. There was a bright silvery streak and then it disappeared behind a building. And it wasn't a sky writer because the streak did not linger in the sky. It didn't occur to me until much later after I heard a plane flying overhead that what I had seen earlier could have been a UFO. It did not have a sound. I only noticed it because of the brightness of the streak. It was a very intense bright light.
