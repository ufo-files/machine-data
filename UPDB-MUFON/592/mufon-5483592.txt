{cak} Witness observed a stationary rectangular black harmonica shaped object with 10-11 multi-colored pulsating lights for about 10 minutes duration.

Witness (A) and his dog were taking there evening walk, while sitting on 1Praying Rock1 for 20 minutes at the NE end of Walker Lake, suddenly he observed a stationary rectangular harmonica shaped object. The object was located approximately 4000 feet away, to the SW over the end of the lake, at an altitude of about 300 feet on an inclination angle of 12 degrees. The object had 10 to 11 red/blue/green/white pulsating not blinking lights along the side facing the witness. The black rectangular object appeared to have square areas between the lights which provided the impression of a harmonica. The object length horizontally was half the length of a pencil at arms-length. After observing the object for 10 minutes witness (A) went back home, two blocks, fetched his girl friend to come see the object and she was able to observe the object also.
