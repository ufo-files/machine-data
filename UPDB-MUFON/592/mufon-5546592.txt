Two Slow Moving White Stars in the Morning Sunlight

I woke up this morning and went down stairs for a cigarette and coffee out on the back porch, here in Hobe Sound, today, 2/8/2010 at 9:50 am. I then looked west and looked up at an airplane and noticed there was a white balloon floating behind it. As the airplane moved on the white object moved much slower. I then noticed another white object and went to get my binoculars inside. When I came out they were still there and as I looked at them through the binoculars they got brighter from the sunlight. There then appeared a red/orange object between them that I could not see with the naked eye. My mother came out and asked what I was looking at and I pointed them out and she noticed them and went to work. There was a shuttle launch this morning and I wondered if that had anything to do with it. I looked back up and the trailing white round thing went back in the direction it came from (NE), and then I followed it through the binoculars. When I looked away and went back to find it, it was gone. I tried through the binoculars, but I could not find it. I then went on the computer to look for anybody else who witnessed this and there was nothing.
