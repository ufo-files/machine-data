{jpg} Strange Metalic Cube jolting across sky

I was sitting in my chair next to the bay window around 7:30 am drinking coffee. I noticed a reflection out of the corner of my eye. I turned to look and noticed a shiny metallic cube dancing in the sky. It was like nothing I have ever witnessed before. I was strangely nervous but wanted a better look. I grabbed my phone and headed out to my front porch. It was dancing in the sky very quickly from side to side. It took me a minute to find it in my phone. Finally I found it as it was hovering. It moved extremely quickly from side to side. Much faster than anything I have ever witnessed. Then it would stop and hover for a short amount of time. Then back to rapidly jolting side to side. I'm not sure how long it was there before I noticed it but I managed to film it for a little under two minutes. A final quick movement and it was gone. Completely gone. I went back inside and honestly was in a bit of shock. I continued to look in the sky for it but never have witnessed it again. After the initial shock wore off I thought to myself perhaps it was a drone. Logic tells me that is not possible. It was square in shape and completely metallic in color. I have never seen a complete square drone. Also it moved way to fast to be a drone. I would have most definitely witnessed it move off into the distance or land. It was gone in a flash. I absolutely believe in E.T's but also am a skeptic about false reports. I will maintain for my life what I witnessed was not of this earth.
