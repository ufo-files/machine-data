Star-like object follows Learjet, then changes direction and splits into 2 objects

I was outside of my office smoking a cigarette on 04-08-2021 at about 3:05pm PDT. I looked up in the sky and saw a Learjet flying from the east to the west. It's typical to see commercial planes fly in that direction, as I both live and work near LAX. The planes fly west over the city then turn east to come in to land. As I was seeing the Learjet, I noticed a bright, star-like object descend at fast speed from above the Learjet and began to follow the jet closely behind the tail. After about 5 or so seconds for following the Learjet, it made a directional adjustment, moving more towards the east than the jet's path. It was like the object lost interest in the jet. After moving in that direction, and after the Learjet was out of sight, the object stopped abruptly. It hovered there for a few seconds, then split into 2 separate objects. Both appeared the same size and shape and brilliance as the original one. After another moment of the two objects hovering together, they then moved off towards the east until they passed behind the office building on the other side of the parking lot. A coworker of mine was with me and saw them to. He doesn't want to be identified. I have previously seen these star-like UFOs before, but I've never seen one split into two before. It was remarkable and a bit startling.
