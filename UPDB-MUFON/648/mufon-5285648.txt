HUGE Plane-like object fell from sky at super high rate of speed

At 8:05PM on Sept 4, 2021 I was riding my bike East along the main road at the Los Angeles Equestrian Center and looked up to see an object larger than a plane appear to fall at an incredibly fast rate of speed. Eventually it was obscured by the trees and I awaited the sound of a crash but didn't hear any. I hopped on twitter and it seems around ~20 others saw the same thing. Sightings from Cathedral City all the way to the beach.
