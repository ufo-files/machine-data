{td} Large black triangle with four lights slowly drifted across the sky-11:21 outside Trenton NJ

I was laying on my hammock after getting home from a late shift at work, enjoying the weather and watching the stars. At first I thought I thought I saw watching a shooting star, but I quickly realized it was four lights moving in tandem, three in the shape of an equilateral triangle with one in the center. The lights appeared to be attached to an object, because I could see stars disappearing behind it. The object moved too slowly to be a star and made no sound. I live near the Trenton-Mercer Airport, but I know what planes look and sound like.
