Observed hovering reflective object for a few minutes, then another reflective object flew up to it and stayed in formation. a few more minutes they ascended into the sky.

two of my friends and I were outside when one of them noticed a strange reflective object in the sky. We thought it might be a silver helium balloon. After a few minutes of observing it we noticed it was not moving at all. Another similar looking object appeared and it flew up to the hovering one at a fairly high speed. The two objects hovered for a couple more minutes at the same distance from each other. Eventually they started slowly ascending until they became very faint and we could not see them. It was hard for me to judge the size and altitude of the objects, but my best guess would be over 5000 ft. I'm not sure what they could have been they did not look or act like helicopters or drones.
