It was just a bright light that following me

I was walking home from visiting friends at about 11pm when i was walking past a church near my turning when a looked up and saw between the steeple and an oak tree a very bright light at first i thought it was a star but a i carried on walking it began to follow me i was quite intrigued by this, i wasn't that scared because i was nearly home,i got in closed the door and thought no more about it and went to bed, i read for a bit turned the lights out and tried to sleep then to my astonishment the bright light was on the wall at the end of my bed filling the room with a blinding light,now I was scared!! then I heard a booming voice saying now do you believe in God.why it said that I haven't a clue I'm not particularly religious.Their was two other people in the house at the time and neither of them heard or saw anything.If anyone can make sense of this I will be much obliged.
