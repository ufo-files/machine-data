{kt}It was a bright sphere of light perhaps over 250 feet in diameter

I had just finished my shift as a cab driver. I was going home. I went to go onto I95 from Castor Ave in Philadelphia. It was just turning daylight. I saw a sphere of light that was huge. It seemed like it came out of the Delaware River which is near to I95 at that point. I sped up I95. It seemed to match my speed and stayed right with me. I saw it outside the passenger window. It also seemed to communicate to something inside me. It was just an eerie feeling. Anyway, I went to turn off Woodhaven Rd exit off of I95. At that point, it seemed to go higher in the sky and it just blinked off. It was like someone just flicked a light switch. I worked again that night and the next morning went to pick up the day driver. He was sick the day before; I picked him up on Castor Ave and was explaining to him what I had witnessed the previous morning; As if right on cue, the light came up the exact same way that it did the previous morning. It followed the exact same routine including blinking off at Woodhaven Rd exit. The day driver saw the same thing that I did. This happened in 1997. I was watching your show named the Buck's County Flap and it reminded me of that incident. You could possibly go there now at say 5AM in the morning and you might be able to see the same thing that I did. Why would it change its seemingly daily routine regardless of the time that has passed. I don't reside in Philadelphia anymore or I would look for myself. The other witness still resides in Philadelphia.
