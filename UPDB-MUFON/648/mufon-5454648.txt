{cak} Lights coming from SE to NE in fog

For started forming across Honey Lake out of nowhere. Highway 395 ran below our homepage approximately 1/4 mile. Got so thick highway was out of view. Lights came from my right to my left about eye level well above highway. Lights travelled in a straight line to a mountain across to my left and became stationary until 7 lights were all lined up in a row then one by one moved into either the mountain or into a valley on the other side of the mountain from my vantage point. Once the last disappeared into the mountain, for lifted within 10 minutes and everything was clear again.
