{rjl} Strange lights were seen in the Western Sky over Southern California

On the evening of June 25th btwn 10:30 pm and 11:00 pm I looked up and out my kitchen window and saw 3 huge bright yellow/orange balls of light hovering and pulsating over the Mountains to the West of my home. They were so big that I could not believe what I was seeing. I went outside onto my balcony and observed them. They hovered for about 5 minutes or more and then then moved down behind the mountains in the same formation that they appeared. The back side of the mountain was glowing for about 5 minutes and then about 12 to 15 smaller bright yellow/orange balls of light rose out of the mountain and hovered in the same place as the last set were. Some of the balls seemed to change their shape as they were pulsating. After about 5 to 10 minutes they went back down behind the mountains. The mountain glowed again for a few minutes and then it went dark. The whole experience lasted for about 30 minutes. It was extremely upsetting to see something like that. I was in total shock.
