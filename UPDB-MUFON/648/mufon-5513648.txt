{dr}A formation of lights in the sky below the cloud ceiling over southern Mecklenburg county.

At approximately 2030 on Friday December 14, I was driving south on I485 towards Matthews,NC from I 77 when I observed 11 lights in the sky to my 12 oclock. Initially, I thought it could be aircraft on approach to KCLT, but that did not seem correct. I had observed 11 separate lights, if the lights were aircraft on approach, then their separation was very close. Also, I do not know if there are any approaches from the south over the south eastern part of the county. What caught my attention was the fact there were 11 separate lights in somewhat close proximity to each other without any regular aircraft lighting (ie green,red, and white)and the fact that all 11 lights were a steady orange without any movement. 
I had estimated that the cloud ceiling was 4,000 ft, I went home and confirmed on Flightaware.com the ceiling to be 4,600 ft. Thus, the lights were most probably below the cloud ceiling. I was driving far to fast (65 - 70 mph) to make any further observations apart from the fact that the lights remained steady. As I485 began to curve east and subsequently slightly northward, the lights went slowly from my 12 to eventually 5 oclock. Bear in mind that the lights did not move; rather the direction of the road changed.

Currently, I am a resident of Florida and have been for many years but I visit family in Matthews, NC for the holidays. That being said, at the time I observed the lights in the sky, I had been driving for 7 hours. Also, my educational background is in aviation/aerospace and that is what was the first thing that led me to notice the unusual nature of the lights. Lastly, Ive included a map indicating where the lights may possibly have been relatively to where I was driving.
