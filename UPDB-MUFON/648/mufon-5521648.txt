Bright orange stationary light. Then moves to the right and disappears.

On the evening of January 23, 2009 at approximately 6:21pm I went outside to do some sky watching. I quickly noticed a bright light just above the horizon northeast of Dublin. I always have video camera in hand, so I started filming. The object was stationary for a few seconds and then suddenly started moving to the right in a straight line. I watched it through the trees as I was filming. It went behind the telephone pole and disappeared just on the other side of the pole.
