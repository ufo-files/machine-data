It was approx. 9;55 pm EST or so when my fiance and I stepped out on the front porch for a cigarette. Nice night, warm, clear sky. We had both lit up and we saw the same thing up in the sky just over our eye level - a streak of light. 

Both of us had the same reaction, saying what the. . . out loud. I stood up , stepped off the patio to get another look. It wasnt particularly bright, but it was certainly noticeable. 

The white lit object seemed "long" at first, and quite small, which leads me to believe it was very high up there. Sort of looked like a very fast moving star. However the light was not steady - I don't want to say that it blinked, because it wasnt blinking, but almost as if it was disappearing and reappearing in a different area of the sky. Though as it turned - which it did, several times - you could follow its projected path and guess where it would reappear. It seemed to circle over us twice or three times and then was gone. 

I didn't know what to think of the object as I viewed it. I had no explanation. In hindsight the woman thinks it could have been a firefly - fair, but they are typically lit up as yellow and hell, they certainly do not move so fast. Ive never seen something move so fast in the night sky.
