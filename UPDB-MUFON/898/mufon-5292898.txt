Looked like a star

Yesterday morning I was leaving my house and it was sunrise. I looked up and saw what at first I thought was a star or very bright light. Only thing is no other stars in the sky. This object was traveling south for a few miles then it went north a few miles at a very slow speed. Then it went east. All this took place for about an hour.
