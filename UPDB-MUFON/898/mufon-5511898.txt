{nd} I was looking up and noticed what looked like Mars. started moving down and over

I woke up one early morning, approx 4:30am, last month and went outside on my porch to smoke a cigarette. I couldnt help but notice how clear the sky was. I could see bright stars and notice one that was reddish color and figured it might be mars. It suddenly starting moving downward and to the right. at that point I ran inside to grab my cell phone and tried taking video of it. my phone isnt that great quality and after viewing the video it is only black. It moved downward and to the right and took about 1 minute total. after taking the video I couldnt find the object i was had seen move. it had vanished. I cant tell for sure what date the video is from because i think my cell phone, at the time, had the wrong date programmed. I since have received a new cell phone but the video was transferred to the new phone. I think it was either late January or early February. the object moved slowly with no jerking or sharp turns.
