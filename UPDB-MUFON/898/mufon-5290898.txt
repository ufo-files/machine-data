Shiny looking ball/disc at Sands Beach

Wednesday 25, November at Sands Beach, while surfing, I saw a fairly shiny/bright object hovering over the ocean and heading West towards the Airport and the mountains. The object seemed to follow a linear trajectory but not in a straight motion. It would move then stop, then move again then stop again. It is not the first time that these discs or "golf-balls" looking objects are flying over the coast of Goleta near the Airport.
