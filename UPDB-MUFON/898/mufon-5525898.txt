No sound

I went out on my porch to smoke a cigarette and because I am interested in the night sky (I have an 8" Dobsonian telescope) I was looking up. I noticed a solid bright starlike object moving from the north north west direction. I listened to see if there was any noise and there was none. I continued to watch it and it hesitated twice at a slower speed and then changed direction about 901 heading due east. As it moved in that direction it slowly dimmed until it was no longer visible.
