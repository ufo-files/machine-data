Two black triangular objects spotted.

Two or more black triangular objects spotted flying in formation, northbound. Flew directly over me during the initial first years of numerous UFO close encounters and sightings. The two craft were black with no apparent lights but can be seen in front of star light, or the background light of the upper atmosphere. I don't remember reporting this but here it is for record. I believe these two were military stealth aircraft (although not very stealthy at the time of sighting). I don't have record of what the exact time and date of the event was but I think it was in the fall of 2008.
