{cak} horizontal lights stationary, vertical beams - there were atleast 2- 1 to the west and 1 northwest

I was sitting on my back porch approx. 5:15 am 12/6 doing my normal am routine- coffee and a cigarette. I noticed a very bright pulsating horizontal object in the sky to the west. I saw an airplane to the southwest and knew it was not a plane, it was to big (long) I could see it producing various beams- straight beams, then it looked like it was creating a drawn star like image. I looked to the north west, more over lake Erie and there was another one- same patterns, except brighter. I got up, ran down cellar grabed the binoculars, went to my patio and they were still in the same place, the colors were red, blue, yellow, green.
It was not a star, I scoped those out as well... I thought someone else has got to see this, or everyone will think Im nuts. I ran upstairs to get my 17 yr old daughter out of bed, I told her what I saw and she said she wasnt getting up. I then went to my son Carsons room to get him out of bed, approx 5:20-5:25am. Took him a minute, but he got up came downstairs, we both went out on the patio, he took the binoculars and he saw the same thing. I texted my best friend and explained it to her and left my husband a voicemail.  I continued looking randomly for the next 30-35 minutes. I had gone to the basement to do laundry and when I came back up at approx. 6:00am- they were both gone... but, the small stars that were not remotely as bright remained. These objects did not move, they remained in the same spot the whole time. I got my son out of bed again and told him they were gone. He said "no way" he got up and looked again and saw the same thing... they were gone.
I instantly jumped on the computer to see if there were any sightings close to what I saw and there were some very similar pictures on line.
Then I found your website and am writing this now. I could kick myself for not taking any pictures, I was just to amazed.
