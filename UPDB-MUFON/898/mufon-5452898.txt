A triangle shaped object was hovering over my neighborhood

It was either the year 1999 or 2000. It was just after high school and I was living with my mother. I was with my boyfriend at the time and he was dropping me off at my house late at night. As we were getting out of his car I noticed the UFO up in the sky and it appeared to be directly over us. I pointed it out to my boyfriend and he also saw it. It was very low in the sky, maybe just over tree top level, and was hovering silently. The only visible part of it was the lights, three of them in an equilateral triangle formation. The lights never moved, flickered, or changed brightness. I assume the body of the object was black, because I could not see it, and in the shape of a triangle because of the lights. There was no sound. We stood for less than a minute in the front yard looking at it. I quickly became nervous and suggested we go inside the house before we get abducted. I remember going inside but I don't remember if I continued to watch it through the window or what I did afterward. I don't remember if my boyfriend came into the house with me or if he got in his car and left.
