I heard aircraft noise I was not used to, living close to NAS Joint Reserve Base in Fort Worth I've gotten used to various aircraft noises and this was one I did not recognize. So I opened my curtains and seen (3) V-22 Osprey tilt-rotor aircraft flying in Delta formation. Far beyond the formation I observed a dark colored object, very small from my perspective flying an odd flight pattern (mostly straight and ascending but not a true straight line.) At first I thought it might be a balloon due to the strange flight pattern, but it went beyond the cloud cover too quick to have been an inflatable object.
