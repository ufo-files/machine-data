16 bright white ORBS flying over house. Orbs shifting positions as changing directions

A string of ORBS over San Francisco caught on video tape! This is not the first time I have seen these type of things. I also have videos of other strings or ribbons of orbs flying over head just a few day prior.

I was lucky to have my new "IR video camera" and captured them flying over my house for a minute or so. Please see video. I will also post the video on You tube under my site UFOandUSOhunter.
