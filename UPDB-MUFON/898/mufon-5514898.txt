Received a witness report of an object - I saw the same thing and observed it and took photos and video. I pulled other witnesses together. We have video from two vantage points miles apart.

I am Alabama MUFON State Director located in Huntsville. I was heading to my office for an important meeting. I received a call around 8:50 am of a witness located 10-15 miles from me who saw an object. I looked in the direction and saw an object that appeared stationary in the clear blue sky and appeared extremely bright and then would dim only to become bright again. I took video and photos. I also saw three people enter the building and pointed it out to them as well. I had to attend the meeting (reluctantly), and when it was over around 50 minutes later - object was gone. When I got to my Redstone Arsenal office, my colleague reported seeing the same object from a different vantage point. She also found a link on YouTube of someone else who used their camera to get a shot. 

Here is a link to the clip in YouTube of my shot. http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aPrQOxODI4s
The other witness has his posted at http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GOorvLM7tpM. This witness is located approximately 8 miles towards the northwest. The object was 10 degrees from magnetic north relative to my position. 

My colleague saw it to my south. The witness who called me was 15-20+ miles away and to my southeast position over a mountain. Others are coming forward with considerable distance between us. 

Whatever this was, it remained stationary for over 25 minutes, was reflecting the light from the sun and would have been huge if truly seen over the distances involved here. 

In a call placed to the Huntsville airport tower, one of my Field Investigators was told that the Army alerted them to a flare drop exercise. While no aircraft were seen in the vicinity until much later than the event (helicopters), this does not appear as a flare that is of a known variety. 

It is unclear as to what this object was and it was witnessed by almost 8 people to date that are known. My photo taken with iPhone 4s. The other witness shot was with an android.
