I was a passenger in a friends car, mid day, and pulled onto cherrywood st and immediately witnessed the {large, metalic} disc shaped ufo hovering over Rushmore lake

Mid summer & mid day of 1988 in Jenison, mi, hovering low over Rushmore Lake. I was 12 years old & was a passenger in a car heading to my friends house when we witnessed the disc shaped ufo on a beautiful, sunny day.
 I had been skateboarding in my yard at our mini driveway skatepark with my brothers & a couple other friends. My older brothers friend gave me a ride to my friend Darin’s house on Rushmore St., directly behind Jenison Cemetery. As the driver pulled north onto Cherrywood st from Port Sheldon st, we immediately witnessed the ufo hovering over the homes & treetops directly over Rushmore Lake. I asked the driver if was seeing the same thing I was, & he said YES I AM. He slowed to a stop & we both stared silently & in disbelief of what we were looking at.
  The large, metallic, disc shaped craft was hovering over the lake. Just over the treetops from our vantage point. At minimum the length of a 747 passenger jet. We both looked at the object with equal parts confusion, disbelief, awe, & amazement at what followed. After possibly 1 minute of looking at the ufo hover motionless in the same spot over the lake & surrounding homes, the ufo quickly flew straight up to a higher elevation of 2000’-3000’. It then turned on a dime to an up/easterly direction with an immediate and immeasurable speed increase, disappearing into the upper atmosphere (space?) in the blink of an eye. 
 Then it was gone. No sound emission. No lights. No exhaust. No speed restriction. No sonic boom. No elevation restriction. No moving parts visible. Large, classic disc shape with bulging central mass. Minimum 100’ radius. Minimum 50’ height. Metallic/chrome exterior. No markings. 
 I have told my story to many people after that sighting. To this day I remember it perfectly. I have always wondered how many other people may have witnessed that same ufo & it’s majestic presence over the lake that warm summer day. If I am one of only two people who did gaze upon that craft, on that day, over that lake, so be it. 
 My story is now public for all to share and contemplate 🛸
