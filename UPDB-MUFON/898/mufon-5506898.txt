My wife was going to get some coffee when she stopped at the intersection of Thorton and Llano in Clovis, New Mexico. When she stopped at the stop sign, she noticed the saucer to the East heading west, then it just disappeared. She told me about it and I felt that it needed to be reported to someone. My wife said she was amazed that she finally saw one, she seems pretty excited now that she can be counted among those that have seen one. She told me that it was right there in plain sight then it simply flashed out, gone.
