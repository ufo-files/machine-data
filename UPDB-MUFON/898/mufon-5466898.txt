Cone shaped object flying silently to the NE from the SW, red reflection

I'm not sure what to make of this. Witnessed a silent conical or pyramidal shaped object at distance which was flying silently sw to ne. Not a helicopter, was flying high enough to go behind fluffy white clouds in a mainly blue sky and emerged again. Speed was not rapid, not plane speed and I think slower than a helicopter. Was too brief to get a full handle on distance. It appeared to be reflecting on the base, very red colour, this was not a strobe or aircraft light but more general sun reflection off metal. It was entirely unlike any other flying craft I have ever seen, but it's possible I caught a normal object at a weird angle, I do not believe so though. I grabbed my phone and tried to get pics, but was shooting through glass, not very clean glass. Only two seem to show the object but very unclear as I hadn't got time to focus, zoom and aim etc so I don't think they add much. Object went behind clouds again and did not reemerge, I guess 30 secs total sighting. Seen at home through bedroom window which was open, hence I know it was silent.
