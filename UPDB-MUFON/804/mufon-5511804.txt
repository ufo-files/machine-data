I left work at 820 pm and was halfway home. I was heading south on 1000E just north of 56 th street. I was watching two jets far away probably landing at Indpls Airport. Then to my right I saw What I thought to be a small plane coming toward me. That when it caught my attention because it had orange lights instead of red and green plus they were flashing really fast. I am still driving so it was hard to focus on it. By the time it got overhead I got my truck stopped and drivers window down. I stuck my head out and looked up at it and then it just went dark, lights out but with the full moon I could see it was round and it change direction. It was going NE and it instantly changed to South. I lost it after 3 to 4 seconds. Before I noticed it I had just a weird feeling and remember looking at clock on dash to make sure I had not dozed or anything and I was right on time then I saw it. I am a little paranoid now. When I watched it go dark and change directions and not be seen makes you wonder how many more are there and what do they want.
