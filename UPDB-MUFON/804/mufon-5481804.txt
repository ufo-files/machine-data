{dr}Saw a UFO with my parents

My father and I were outside our carport after going to my cousins wedding.my father was grilling steaks as we did every Saturday. When I noticed a very bright star over a patch of trees. I showed it to my father .that when it begin to start moving towards our Direction.my mother came out and noticed we were looking up and it stop directly above us. my mother ran in fear my father ran for a flashlight me I just stood in disbelief I saw a cigar Shape with what looked like portholes.and it was huge. My father shined the flashlight on it remember it is 9:30 at night. The object flash some light when my father shined and flipped the light on and off On his flashlight.my mother came out with a camera but for some reason it would not work. We watch for what seem like hours and slowly it started moving and all the sudden it shot straight up and was gone . we looked at each other in amazement .my father then said to me and mom we will never mention this to anyone or even talk about it to each other. This was June 16 1975 and he died in November of that year.he also said people would call us crazy and silent I remained for many many years. I was 11 years old when it happen now I am 52 years old and that night has haunted me all these years.just for the record my mother and I sit down before she past away and wrote down what happen an then gave them to each other. our description of what happen was almost identical. but now is the time to report it because we should that night. I know some will say it was a plane. But what I saw was not man made.
