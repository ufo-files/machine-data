7 UFO's moving single file July 3, 2011 11:00PM in Rochester Hills Michigan

We live in a suburban area. I went outside just before 11:00PM Sunday night. It was a calm, clear night. I locked my shed, went and turned on the garden hose so I could water the tomato plants, when I saw an orange red bright light moving slowly above the tree line. Because it was July 3rd and people were lighting fireworks, I thought maybe it was some kind of lantern or something. Then I saw another one following behind it. I watched them move slowly across the sky when 3 more appeared single file moving on the same route. The first 2 turned, single file, and started moving up & away from my vantage point. I ran inside and got my wife to come out to see them. See made it out in time to watch the first 2 slowly disappear from sight and the next 3 make the same turn and start to go up and away from us. She said "Are they planes or helicopters"? I said "They have no flashing lights & no sound ". All this time I was trying to get my cel phone's camera/video to work but I couldnt see anything in the viewer(stupid phone!) I then called my daughter in Iowa to tell her what we saw. I let her talk to her mother so she would't think her old man had flipped out. As my wife was telling her what we saw, 2 more appeared, traveling the same route. The last of the 7 was larger & brighter the the preceding 6. I ran into the house and got my hunting rifle with a 9X scope to look at the last 2 UFO's and all I could see were larger brighter orange red lights. My wife proceded to described the sighting to my daughter until the last 2 disappeared. I would estimate this sighting took a good 12-15 minutes in duration. I know with all the people out lighting fireworks, other people had to see these and hopefully they know how to operate there cell camera/video! I looked at my phone & the call to my daughter lists 11:01 PM and it lasted 8m 43sec. Then I remembered I had turned on the garden hose & proceded to water the plants & freak-out!
