Australian New Italy Encounter
 The female witness lived in the rural town of New Italy in New South Wales Australia an hour and half from Bryon bay. It was approx. 15 years ago (1997) while the witness was enjoying time in her swimming pool (around 9pm) when a horizontal string of bright yellow lights approx. 25 meters ( 751) long and 1As tall as an average tree about 10 meters1 descended behind some trees about 6 meters (181) away.
