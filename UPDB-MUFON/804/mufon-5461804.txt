{rjl} Strange craft

In 2011 I lived in New York outside of the city. It was winter and an ice storm took out the power for a large portion of the east cost. My friends and I being in 11th grade went out onto our friends boat to party, there were five of us in total. The area around us was completely black due to the power outage. We noticed an aircraft that was soundless and hovering over the tree line so we stopped partying and began commenting on how strange it was when it zipped off in a straight zig zag pattern. It disappeared for a minute or two before returning where it again hovered. It had about five white lights that changed shapes into a single blob like light before yet again changing back to multiple lights. It then zipped off a second time. It sped off in a zig zag pattern that was made by very quick straight bursts from the craft before disappearing completely. I do have video of it and the video also strangely starts to scroll and cut off before the video is over.. I have never been able to explain the event that we saw. I have wondered if being after September 11th and so close to NYC that it could have been an unknown military craft? That said, what we witnessed was not normal, is unexplainable and looked very high tech... the pictures that I’ve attached below are a screen shot of a cell phone video that I took of the video on my old camcorder.
