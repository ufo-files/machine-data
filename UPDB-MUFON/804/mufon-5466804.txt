{jpg} Translucent boomerang shaped caused air around it to be wavy like hot air above pavement.

I was sitting watching fireworks looking South towards Toledo Ohio when I observed wavy translucent boomerang or hammerhead shape dispersing the air around it like how heat rises up from hot pavement it was about the same height approximately 1500 as tethered paratroopers would deploy at. At first I wasn't sure if I was seeing anything or not until it got within about a 1/4 mile away when I observed a counter clockwise motion square mirror like light shimmer that outlined the shape this also showed some type of done section above this. It was completely silent and returned to translucency heading in a NE direction towards Lake Erie. I did not have time to get my phone out to get a picture or video due to not seeing anything until the translucency emerged.
