{rjl} Rapid movement of star like lights north of Las Vegas

In July,1973, my family were visiting Las Vegas, Nevada to go to the casinos. I was 14 years old and not able to go to casinos. I met 2 other guys at the Circus Circus hotel and we buddied up and ran around the Circus Circus chasing girls and such. Basic teenager stuff. After being out until midnight, I went back to the Westward Ho hotel we were staying at and found my family members were still out at the casinos. I decided to go sit outside by the pool. Las Vegas in July, even at night, is quite warm. So as I sat in the poolside chair to wait for either family to return, or some late night pool activity to happen, I was looking at the night sky and noticed these 3 stars to the north. My attention was captured when I saw one of these stars suddenly move sideways at a high rate of speed. Then, I saw the same star move back into place and the other 2 stars moved upward and stopped quickly. The next thing I observed was the stars did somewhat of a dance with each other at quick, straight angles and then they just bolted straight up and were gone. This was the first time i had ever witnessed such a thing. I was amazed as I knew a plane could not do what I had seen. This turned out to be the first of several UFO sightings I have seen since then.
