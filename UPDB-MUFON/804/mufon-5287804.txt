Thought it was satellite till it stopped

My husband and I were outside trying to enjoy the night sky, I was looking at satellites, and thought I saw another one when it suddenly stopped, I told my husband, and showed him the moving satellite to show that the thing stopped moving and you can see it was reflective, but then it’s like they were shining a light on us, not a beam or flood light. So I started waving, and my husband follows suit. After what seemed like a few mins the light turns of and it goes slow at first then zips off that in a few secs of watching its gone. I jokes that they think either we’re not strange enough or that I’m too strange. Probably confused at me waving who knows it was just odd cause it was as far as a satellite.
