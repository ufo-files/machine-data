{jd} I ascended a bright white light through the ceiling of my room

Asleep; bright white beam came through ceiling (over old portable closet) slanting to the floor. I awoke and with large doll in my right hand as I floated up the bright white light through the ceiling. Our home was just 3-4 houses from the original west fence of Davis-Monthan Field (just west of the original red and white checked water tower. This area is now the zoo, water ponds, park, etc. that was turned over to the City of Tucson approx. 1953/54. That is ALL I have memory of but it has NEVER left my memory to this day. This was my first encounter, but not my last. I will fill in another report on my second encounter.
