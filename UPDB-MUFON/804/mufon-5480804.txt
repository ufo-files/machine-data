{rjl} A bright star that just vanished.

I was walking the dogs like I always do about 9:45. I always scan the sky's when I'm out at night because I like spotting space junk. As I looked to the north there was a bright star that I didn't see the night before and while looking at it, it just vanished. I kept watching the area it was in and nothing showed up, no shadow, nothing. I thought maybe it was a meteor coming straight at me but it didn't have an orange, glow it was blue/white just like a star. The night was clear. I've never seen a star just go out like that. There's something going on in our sky's and you can bet your booty I'll be watching.
