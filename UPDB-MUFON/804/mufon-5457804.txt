{tjd} Witnessed orbs flying across the sky

I was in a pool laying on a float looking up at the sky watching planes fly by when I noticed a flight of 5 orbs fly across the sky. They changed formation from disorganized to an organized triangle to a slash (all in line) then back to a triangle and abruptly change direction from approx. 60 degrees to approx. 150 degrees according to the compass on my phone. There was no noise emitted by them that I could hear. They were a white dull grey color. They were round. They crossed the entire sky in approx 20 sec. by the time I lost them over a low roof line and I stood up they had started off in the 150 degree direction and were gone I didn't see them again after I. Tired to gain more ground to sight them they had either sped up or blinked up not sure which but they were not visible anymore.
