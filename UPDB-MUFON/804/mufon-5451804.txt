Silver Sphere over Power Line

Driving into Stamford Bridge on the A166 from Bridlington around 11am ish on a bright dry day myself and my Son saw a Football sized Silver Sphere hovering directly over a power line Pylon from a distance of approx 1/2 mile away, we pulled up approx 30 seconds later to get out and take a better look but when we looked again the Sphere had disappeared.
