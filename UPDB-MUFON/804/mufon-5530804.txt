Orange Oval

We were at a 4th of July party, watching fireworks outside. I went with my sister and nephew. There were about 20 of us and we all seen these weird objects that looked like: Orange Oval w/ flame coming from bottom. At first we thought they were planes or maybe someone was playing a tricks with something but there were too many of them. And they came over mtn (montage mtn) across the city of scranton then disappeared. Plus they were very slow. I also have some pictures of the objects. My nephew is 13 yrs old and he is interested in giving his opinion on this matter.
