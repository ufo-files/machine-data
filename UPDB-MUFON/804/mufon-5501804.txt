{dr}For the past week have observed several objects with blinking lights none appear to move

Outside standing on my deck at about 10:15p.m. I saw what at first was a star but seemed different from the rest of the stars. I got my bino's and looked at the object thru them I could see different blinking lights, blue red green orange and white or yellow. NO pattern to them and they on the object they seemed to move around on it at times. I looked around the sky and found several more objects that appeared to look like the first. I was in the military and have lived close to Shaw Air Force Base most of my life. What I saw was not an airplane or any helicopter that I know of. I have observed these objects every night for the past week have tired to take photo's but they are too distance for my camera's to make out. they appear to slowly drift across the sky. I don't know how long they last but one night I saw them at 3 a.m. They will most likely be back tonight, and I will continue to watch for them. I don't know what they are. And I wish that I had better camera's with which too take some good photo's.
