Illinois ball of light

I was traveling on interstate 70 in Illinois west bound mile marker 25.5 around 23:45. Northwest of the interstate I seen a brilliant white ball of light. I would say about one mile from me. The light was below cloud cover. But above the top by at least 100ft. It was traveling East to west parallel to the interstate. It appeared to be a sphere of light. I would say bigger than a small family sedan. It made two bright flashes then it was gone. It never changed shape or color. I heard nothing. I smelt nothing.
