{dr}Multiple orange lights hovering in the sky in Queens NY.

On or about 11pm on Friday night 7/27/12. I noticed what appeared to be 3 Orange lights in the sky. At first I thought they were airplanes but I looked around and noticed some airplanes (I leave near Laguardia Airport NY) they did not compare. The 3 lights then turned into 4, then 5 and finally 6. By this many other people had noticed and began looking on also in shock. Some lights appeared to pass others in height & distance and then came back to each other. This lasted for about 15 minutes when the lights totally faded out. It was very spooky and freaked us all out.
