Line of lights/orbs.

My son stepped out to check the sky around 6:30p.m. local time. He suddenly opened the door, and said "Dad, you gotta come out here, quick"! I stepped out, Andy said "Look up here"! There was a long line of nearly perfectly spaced lights/orbs, perhaps 100 or more, traveling single-file from southwest to northeast; most of them disappeared directly above our house. Several re-appeared, then disappeared again. There was no sound from any of them, no flashing lights, just a steady glow. The speed was fairly fast, but all appeared to be traveling the same speed, in a linear fashion. I've seen many things in my 57 years, but nothing like that before. We were unable to get photos, as it was fully dark at this time.
