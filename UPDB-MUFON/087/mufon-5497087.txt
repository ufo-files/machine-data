It flew directly over our neighborhood, then made an abrupt turn to the west, we observed the lights for a few more seconds before they suddenly disappeared. I'm sure other people had to have witnessed this event, as my neighborhood is next to Interstate 95. This is my first time ever seeing something like this, and to see it so clearly since it was flying so low was absolutely incredible.
