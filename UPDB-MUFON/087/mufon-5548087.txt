LIGHT SHOW TO ENTIRE SCHOOL CAMPUS.

While attending the culinary institute of america in 1985,me and my friend were studying in the dorm room in the main building facing the hudson river.We were three stories up with the windows open when we heard heard a crowd gathering ooohing,awing and oh my goshing.What we saw was a approx 30 to 45 ft long cylinder pause atop the hudson river approx 500 ft from the rivers edge,it had 5 lights same yellow color on,then changed from yellow to green to red to blue to violet,then the straight clyinder went from floating horiz,to slowly vertical.The shape went from one straight vertical shape to splitting to three seperate pcs all illuminating different lights.I proceeded to making different patterns with different lights.This went on for at least 30 min.After it finished we went running out of the building along with several hundred witnesses.it floated over the main building and across the campus 60 ft up silent floating charcoal gray shiny metallic cylinder approx 15 ft in diameter,30 to 40 ft long.we watched it float away.right after that airforce jets were in the air and private cesnas in the area as well.We know at least 100 fellow students saw the same thing we were stunned and nobody really talked about it.thinking somebody would asked questions later ,no one ever did
