{rjl} Two pulsing lights in southeast sky traveling easterly in formation no sound disappeared

On 8/25/17, at about 8:30 pm, driving east on Cottage ave at Russell Road in Albany. Looking southeast, saw two rose/red colored lights traveling east in line - moving slowly with no sound. The lights appeared to "pulse" rather than blink or "twinkle". filed by two different phones.
