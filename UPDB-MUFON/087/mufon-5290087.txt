2 unidentified lights in the sky

I was at work and I was looking up at the sky when I saw these 2 lights moving high up in the sky. They moved north and quickly turned directions towards the east in a way that seemed unreal. They seemed to just hover for a bit, then their lights got brighter and flashed. Then the first one disappeared and shortly after the second disappeared. It disappeared out of sight almost instantaneously.
