July 9 2016 caught this image while taking shots of air show practice runs.

I work at CFB Borden ontario,where they were having their 100th anniversary air show on July 11+12. On July 9th I was given permission to go up on the roof of our building to take pictures of the practice runs.I was using a Nikon d90 shooting at 4 1/2 frames per second.The Snow Birds made a fly by, but I was not able to keep them in frame for more than 3 or 4 shots and then they flew out of frame and the camera took one more shot(remember I was in rapid shoot mode) and then the camera simply shut down.No power at all.I just assumed my batteries had died and left.Upon arriving home I examined the camera and found it was workig fine.I reveiwed the shots I took but what I found on the very last frame before the camera shut down blew my mind.Don't know if this is some kind of camera anomilly but I don't think so.
