Would awake around three two nights before and the night this happened. Was awake and looked at the clock it said 5:00 am. Looked out my bedroom window and observed a satellite in the sky that I have seen many times before in the sky in the same area. Suddenly it started glowing lucent half green half yellow light that looked as though it was pulsating and swirling. Then it looked as if an object either emerged from the satellite or docked with it. The object had 6 lights 3 top 3 bottom that were congruent. After this object was completely visible then small orbs shot out of both ends of the satellite swirled around then were no longer visible. While this was on going I noticed 2 small orbs one red one white in my woods about 75 yards away. they were moving up and down in a random pattern. Then everything seemed to cease. I looked at the clock and it was 5:30 AM. Though it only seemed to take a couple of minutes for all this to happen. Thought about waking my wife but she was sleeping soundly so I let her sleep. Don't know if I went back to sleep but did get up at 6:30 AM.
