{rjl} Strange Light Swiggles through the Air

At sunset I walked out of my home and looked down my driveway to say hi to my neighbors and behind them I saw a strange oblong disk shaped thing the size or either a bowling ball to a small car that was glowing on the front or had a very bright light. It was hovering first then quickly ascended and descended south and then when up very quickly and then dropped toward the ground behind the trees. I stayed for a minute to see if it would take off and saw nothing.
