I was at lake shastina at the campgrounds closed for season enjoying the night sky when I witnessed this triangle shaped vehicle

I went to Lake Shastina to the campground which was officially closed for the season but still accessible by the public to enjoy the nite sky and hangout with my dog it's mid to late Sept to beginning of October. The mt shasta looms up on one side and the stars are everywhere it's around 9 to 10pm when I spotted this triangle shaped black object with oscillating lites moving across the nite sky so slow it appeared at first to be stationary yet moving as if a breath of air was pushing it forward. I remember the lites were oscillating red,green,orange,white rythemically. I observed windows like that of an old navel ship like that of the 1700s early 1800s yet saw no figures looking out.it went northeast and disappeared over the mountain, I shortly after heard a jet fly over going the same direction. Than silence. About 30-45 minutes later it reappeared from nowhere heading west and floated westward ever so slow for hours it seemed until it was so far away I no longer could see it because the darkness finally enveloped it. It has bothered me all these years I just want to know if anyone else ever saw this craft. That is the first ufo craft I have personally witnessed. But not the last.
