{cak} two lights. front light was not as bright as the one chasing it. disappeared after a few minutes. very odd movement. the one in front seemed to be trying to get away from the bigger brighter light.

I went outside at about midnight to have a smoke and was looking at the stars because the sky was very very clear. Then out of nowhere a light appeared to be moving up towards the ne from the nw. At first I thought it was a satellite but right behind it was a even brighter maybe even bigger, lighted object. It seemed to be following very closely to the smaller dimmer light. Like it was chasing after it. The smaller dimmer light would change direction and after about a second or two the bigger brighter light would follow in direction. This went on for two minutes then they just went dark. Lights were off. I knew what I seen was not normal. I sat there for about thirty more minutes to see if they would appear again. Nothing. Keep in mind, there was not a single cloud in the sky.
