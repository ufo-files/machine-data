Approx 10-20 seconds, one red UFO appears in the sky, mistaken for a planet until the other shows up.

Heading home from McDonalds in the backseat of my dad's car, I was looking at my cell phone, and I saw something bright enter my peripheral vision, looked up and saw a red light up high in the atmosphere, mind you the weather was clear, a bit cloudy and sunny. I mistook it for Venus at first and was about to tell my parents but thought that its weird that it is out at this time and then another red orb/light appears. I kind of just sat there with my jaw dropping at the sudden appearance of another light, and just as quickly as they came, they disappeared or blinked/faded out (sort of sucked inwards) and I immediately told my parents who seemed skeptical and did not witness what happened. I sort of felt sick after but that is possibly because of the cold weather we have been having recently.
