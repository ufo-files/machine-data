bright white light

I was at the Grand Canyon on spring break in 2006 with my uncle. It had been very hazy that day and into the night. It was my second trip to the Canyon, as I previously visited 3 years prior (that time it was clear and you could see every star in the sky, very spectacular). Anyway we were taking a walk around the south rim at around 9-10pm. You couldn't see many stars as moist as it was outside. All of the sudden a bright white light caught my eye off to the northeast. I initially thought it was a plane with its landing lights on, being as bright and obvious as it was. It looked to be maybe 80 to 100 miles away to the northeast. I watched it for a good 10-15 seconds, then, from a standstill, it darted off to the north at an unhumanly fast speed and quickly disappeared.
