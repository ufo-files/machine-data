3 UFOS Around Passenger Jet

On August 10th I was on my back porch messing with the camera on my new Samsung Galaxy S20 and checking out the features and taking pics of our garden and koi pond and some deck pics. I then heard a passenger Jet flying overhead while I was sitting on my bench swing and I leaned out from under my roof and took some pics hoping to get a p[ic of the plane. And then as the plane went out of sight I stood up and took a couple more pics as it was flying. The jetliner was my guess 35000 ft as it was way up there in the sky. As I was looking thru my gallery on my phone I zoomed in on the Plane and noticed in the 1st pic there were 3 UFOS in a triangle pattern around the jetliner. I couldn't believe what I had gotten on my camera. I didn't see anything until I zoomed in on the pictures. In another pic the UFO that was following the plane was now under the plane as you will see in my pics. It reminded me of my mother telling me a story about her as a stewardess for eastern airlines when they had a UFO fly next to her plane for over a 1/2 hour. This was before I was born. Her flight was documented with Project Blue Book.
