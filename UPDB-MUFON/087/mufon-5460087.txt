{rjl} Daytime bright sparkling stationary light

Driving on hwy 93 south from Lolo MT a very bright sparkling light in the sky ahead of me caught my attention. It was stationary, just at the bottom edge of a bank of storm clouds that were producing lightning. I watched as I was driving south for a couple of minutes. It just sat there, twinkling very brightly. Then it looked like it backed into the bottom edge of the cloud then disappeared. My first thought was that it was a plane coming in for a landing at Missoula airport, but then thought why would it just hover there? And why is the light so bright and sparkly? Hmmm...
