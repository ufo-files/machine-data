{nd} Red amoeba shaped with green lights circling

Around 10:30 pm at our home in Georgia, a light caught my eye throught a window and I went outside to see if it was an airplane or a star. It was a sparkling light and I saw it move slightly upward, then downward and then to the left. I woke up my husband to come see. It was sparkling red, blue, green and white but when I looked at it through binoculars, it is red and amoeba shaped, sometimes looking like a paisley shape going to a round shape. There are green lights around it but then it will just be a strip of green lights, like maybe it turned on its side. I have been watching it for about an hour and it is still there. I did see another one that was smaller, coming toward it and then it disappeared. At one time, I saw a slight white light falling out of it but only for a second so I don't know if that was actually a part of it. I saw an airplane pass under it by maybe a thousand feet (?) but other than that, nothing has come close to it. I keep going outside to see it but am getting a little scared of it for some reason. It is definately not of this world. I just checked and it is still there just sparkling.
