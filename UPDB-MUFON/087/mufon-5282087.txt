Small, spherical silver UFO flying in the southern Carrollwood area of Tampa

Today I was filling up the tank at [exact location details have been moved to Investigators report section/cms/LLL] and I saw a UFO. I am not a UFO skeptic even though I have never seen a UFO - it would always turn out to be some aircraft. I can usually identify small aircraft and helicopters and this was neither. At first I thought it was a shiny silver mylar balloon or a bunch of them. As it got closer I saw that it was too big to be balloons and its flight path was too steady - more like a helicopter in altitude, speed and direction and it crossed over the road diagonally. It was obscured a couple of times by an overhead structure but I was able to view it for about 3 minutes. It was small and somewhat spherical, a shiny silver - it appeared to be revolving as it moved and one side of it glinted steadily on and off very brightly like the sun was reflecting off of a part of it each time it came around - it wasn't like a marker light on an aircraft. The edges of the object were difficult to define and it looked too small for a person to be flying it. I would not go into such detail if I didn't suspect that it might actually have been something real. If it was real I could not say with full confidence but it was unlike anything I have seen with my own eyes before. I do feel as though I have seen something extraterrestrial.
