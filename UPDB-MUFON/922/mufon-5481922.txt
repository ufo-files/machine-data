{jpg} Noticed an unusual pulsating orange orb approach from the SW and continue overhead in NE direction. Object was luminous and silent. While there were clouds in the sky, the object was witnessed disappearing suddenly in a clear sky.

My wife Valentina, and our neighbor Chris, were sitting in our rooftop garden on July 30th, 2016, at approximately 1:15am, when Chris first noticed an unusually luminous object crossing the sky. Our roof has an unobstructed view of most of southern Manhattan as far north as Midtown. The object was observed by all three of us approaching from the SW, just left of the World Trade Center, heading on a straight course NE, and completely disappearing in an instant just south of the Kosciuzsko bridge (from our viewpoint). Being accustom to many planes, helicopters and commercial drones regularly flying overhead, we quickly came to realize this was something very unusual and out of he ordinary. It's size, color and velocity, in addition to being completely silent, was unlike anything else we have ever seen. As we watched the object continue to approach, we kept questioning what else it could be. Ultimately, we were left in a state of shock and excitement as we witnessed it disappear instantly amonget a completely clear patch of sky. Had it not been for this odd disappearing act, we would have likely written this sighting off. While it is difficult to say what it was exactly, it was most definitely not a plane, helicopter, drone, firework, missle/rocket, meteor or satellite.
