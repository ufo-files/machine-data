(GS) Bright gold orb in the sky above the city. Faded out and replaced by similar shape but lower. 2nd object faded out as well

I was driving home on interstate 80 when I noticed what I thought was a very bright planet above Sacramento. As I watched, the glowing yellow orb faded and disappeared. The first orb was immediately replaced by a second, lower object. This object glowed for about 2 minutes and then faded just like the first object.
