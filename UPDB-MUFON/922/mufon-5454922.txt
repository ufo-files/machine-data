{tjd} (50 lights were observed moving in a line, spaced evenly and moving in the same direction.{djs})Lights under Venus appear out of nowhere and move in a straight spaced out line. They disappear in the same spot to the North

While outside looking at the stars, two lights were observed moving slowly together in same direction and same speed, then disappeared. Then maybe 30 seconds later two more lights appeared from the same area moving together and in the same direction as the first lights, then disappeared in the same spot. Then a short time later while observing Venus (which was very bright in the western sky at this time) three more lights appeared under Venus in a triangle shape, then moved into a line formation and moved in a northern direction. Then more and more lights appeared and moved in the same direction then disappeared in the same spot. In all maybe 50 lights were observed moving in a line, spaced evenly and moving in the same direction. At one point there were two lights that were brighter and much closer together moving faster in the same direction and disappeared in the same spot as all the others. All lights were observed by three to four individuals; myself, my daughter, my adult son and his girlfriend at the same time.
