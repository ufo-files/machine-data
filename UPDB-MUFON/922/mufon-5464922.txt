{cak} Driving south three lights appeared to east and slowly passed by to west sped up and ascended

I was travelling south from Lancaster on rt 3 around 2100 with another person driving and we saw three lights on the east side of the road floating aproximately 500 ft above the ground below the clouds. It was two white lights and one bright green light, all not blinking and very bright, in the shape of a triangle. the object was traveling to the west slowly and smoothly, as we past the driver could no longer see and our perspective changed it to just one white light. once we were past it i was looking out the passenger window trying to track it and it sped up agressively going straight away from us and toward the horizon. when it was just about to be out of sight it turned upward and traveled in an arc ascending into the clouds and was gone for aproximately one minute. i then looked back at the horizon in that area and saw a series of flashing three lights, all white. it seemed to be the same area where the orignal object had disappeared and they flashed 5-10 times and then i lost sight because of trees.
