(GS) 5 pulsating red lights about 8ft in dia. traveled together past us then changed direction and parralled us on road, stopped when we did.

In March of 1978 myself and two co-workers were traveling on I-64 in Putnam county WV on our way home from work in Willow Island WV. We noticed a red light approaching us from the west and as it passed us we realized that it was actually 5 red lights, traveling in single file about 20 off the ground.Each light was about 5 in diameter and about 20 distance between them. When it passed us going east, it stopped and began to parallel us in the east bound lane while we were heading west. We stopped and pulled over to the side of the road and it stopped also. I climbed out of the back seat and searched around in the dark and found a piece of asphalt and I then ran out into the road onto the median and I threw the chunk at the lights. It fell harmlessly between the 1st and 2nd light. However, it must have seen it, because it pivoted towards the southeast and in an instant it was gone. It left without a sound, but left white and red traces of light when it took off. When I got home that night I was 3 hrs. late since I usually got home around 7:30 and it was close to 11:00 p.m. and my wife accused me of stopping at a strip bar which I didn't. My paycheck that was in my pocket was missing also. I mentioned this incident to the other two guys years later and they wouldnt talk about it. This is a true story and I have retold it to the best of my ability. I have a tiny object embedded in my skin behind my right knuckle on my right ring finger. It started as a black dot that I feel and I have tried to dig it out, but havent been able to dig on myself that deep. I have scars all over my body and I am a medical marvel. I have ate rat poison as a child, had a skull fracture and concussion at 5, hepatitis c survivor in remission, heart attack survivor, hit by lightning and survived, fell 50 ft and lived, etc... etc... someone has been looking out after me and I wake up with sores and scars on my chest....I have been to a hypnotist and she was not able to hypnotize me and she said I was the first she couldnt hypnotize... this is all true to the best of my recollection.
