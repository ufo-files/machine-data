{cak} Bright orangish light hovering but in pictures white and changed shape.

I was looking at the stars as I do every night and the object appeared in the northeastern part of the sky. I noticed it because it was bright. It was a bright orangish light/orb to the naked eye and when I took pictures it appeared as a white object that in each picture had a different shape. The object hovered for about 5 minutes growing brighter and brighter. It took off towards the east and was gone within a few seconds. I felt amazed because this is the third time I have seen this object in the last month. I took many pictures during the event I think the total is 13 pictures. In every picture it is a different shape. After it had left I went in and told my parents. I did not tell them during the event because I was afraid that if I went inside to tell them that it would be gone before they could get outside and see it as it was the previous two times that I saw it. I had no idea what the object was I just know that I have seen it twice before and that it was not a helicopter or an airplane. I am sending 10 pictures because that is the most that I can send.
