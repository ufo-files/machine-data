{rjl} I saw a boomerang/triangle shaped object.

Was standing on my balcony about 7:16pm having a cigarette looking south towards the sky as it was pretty much getting dark at that point. At 7:19pm I noticed some lights traveling east to west through the gaps in the tree and leaves. Basically traveling the path of I10. As I was waiting for a good opening through the leaves, I noticed perfectly spaced out round dim white lights that formed a boomerang shape. About 8-10 lights on each side. It just glided through the sky making not one sound. I tried pulling my phone out to get a pic but the flash was on and by the time I tried to adjust it, the object was gone. 
I was stunned that I just saw this thing. I've always been a believer, but now my own eyes finally saw something that was definitely strange. I've seen lights in the past and wondered, but nothing compared to this. The lights were so defined. Almost looked like dull led lights. 
As I kept staring in the sky afterward hoping to get another glimpse, at 7:22pm, I saw a commercial airline flying SE to NW which is usually normal. 
Hopefully someone else saw this too since a lot of people are still out and about at this time.
