Star like object that blinked out then reappeared several times in a line pattern.

On 11/16/2010 at about 6:00 PM I was driving through the Stephenville Texas City Park when I saw what appeared to be a bright star towards the south west. The star like object was in the direction of Dublin Texas, and slightly above the tree line. While I was looking towards the object, it blinked out. I then realized it was not a star. I brought my car to a stop and the object then reappeared, but in a slightly different location to the left. The object continued to turn off and on in a linear pattern moving from my right to my left. The object reappeared again in a few minutes but was lower and closer to the top of the tree line. I got my iphone 4 out and attempted to take video but the object stopped illuminating before I could activate the camera. I stepped out of my car and got my iphone ready to take video when the object reappeared for a third time. The first video attached below is what I caught on camera. The second video is of what appeared to be a military aircraft flying over about 15 minutes later. The military aircraft was coming from the Ft. Worth area (North) and was flying towards the Dublin area at a high rate of speed. The first 3 sightings happened from about 6:00 PM to about 6:05 PM. The military craft flew over at about 6:15 PM. I started to leave the park at about 6:30 at which time I got a glimpse of the object again, but only for a brief moment. I stayed for a few more minutes and did not see it again.  The object did not appear to be a conventional type aircraft. I did not hear any sound and could not make out a shape.
