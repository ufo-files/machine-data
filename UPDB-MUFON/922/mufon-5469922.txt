{jpg} Completely stationary white sphere hovering above the trees.

I was riding in my parents car through Issaquah, WA down I-90 eastbound. We noticed paragliders in front of the hillside. So we were looking up and noticed one of the objects in the sky was not like the others. It was perfectly round, like a ball. It was below the paragliders just above the trees. It was bright white, but did not appear to be emmiting it's own light, just reflecting the sunlight. We were on a straight-away so i watched it for about 30 seconds before i was able to get my phone out to record it. The entire time it did not move.
