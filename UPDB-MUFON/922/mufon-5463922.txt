{cak} Spotted a black, triangular craft with a white light at each point in the night sky.

I woke up at approximately 0300 hours or so and went outside to vape. Shortly after I left the building I glanced up at the sky. It was a very clear night and the stars were bright. Directly overhead, I spotted what, at first, appeared to be three dim white lights flying in a triangular pattern, at approximately 10-15,000 feet, in a southeasterly direction. As I watched, though, I realized the middle of the three lights was solid and blocked out the view of the stars above as it moved. This sighting lasted no more than 10-15 seconds and the craft flew over the Huachuca Mountains and out of sight.
