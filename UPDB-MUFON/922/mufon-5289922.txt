Small dot of light appeared, in sky at distance grew significantly in size into rectangle then square for a few minutes

This light was picked up on my dash cam on my way home from work. It first appeared as a small dot of light , similar to an aircraft light. Then almost immediately it brightened, and grew in size. It appeared to become square or rectangle and hovered below a cloud cover. It appeared to be somehow connected to the cloud. it was obviously very large due tot he distance up in the sky that it was from be. It was directly in the eastern sky, I would estimate a few miles or more form my location, traveling east on I-64 at the Huntington Mall exit. It lasted about 3 minutes and drifted right back up into the cloud that it seemed to come from.. 
My first reaction to it was, I wonder if the other drivers saw it. Certainly they did. They had to. it was too obvious not to have seen. I also thought to myself that I can't wait to get home to watch the 6:00pm local news to hear them report on all the people who saw the sighting. I was really amazed and disappointed when that didn't happen.
