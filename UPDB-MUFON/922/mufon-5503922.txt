WEBN Labor Day fireworks show

My name is "D" and I was at my girlfriends moms house (girlfriend - "A", mother-"L") located in Covington, Kentucky. We were all three watching the annual WEBN fireworks which occurs, I believe every Sunday prior to Labor Day. This was in 2010. The fireworks went off without a hitch. Right after the radio/song/announcement says, "open fire," the fireworks look like muskets being fired in that, there is a lot of black smoke clouds! I noticed a light that moved from right to left of the tv screen. I lost sight due to the camera focusing on the fireworks. I asked if either of them saw it which they said no. Thank you DVR. I rewound and played it over and pointed it out. They both shrieked. We played it over and over pausing the DVR and playing it in slow motion. We decided to all draw what we thought we seen. Everyone of us drew a bell shape I thought it was rotating and thought I seen three windows or lights. We were all so excited and scared. "L" saved it on DVR for me for along time because I wanted to upload it to the internet. I called fox 19 who covered it and they said a couple other people called in as well but that they thought it was a plane. I play it again and right at the end you see a plane moving slowly across the screen which means it's far off from the river where the show took place. Then I realize that no plane would fly so close to bridges let alone fly right through a fireworks show. She eventually deleted it with my permission though I was depressed. I eventually found it online, but it's bad quality. If you go to youtube and search "2010 WEBN fireworks" there is a. 31:12 video. At the 30:12 you see the start of the UFO on the right side of the screen, but the quality is so bad you only see it really in one frame. But if you all could get the real footage I promise you that it would be worth your while. I promise all three of us a educated professionals and I would not want to waste anyone's time maybe you could even call Cincinnati FOX 19 news and ask for the video.
