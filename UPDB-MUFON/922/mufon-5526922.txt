cluster of lights one of which circled the others once

Cluster of three star like lights about 45 degrees above horizon. At one point one of the lights circled the others. The clusters never moved as a group. The lights were just above the peak of our garage roof and never moved from that spot of it's northeast location. I never saw it go away, but rather we just got tired of looking at it. My husband and I watched this together.
