{cak} Object flying east to west, hovered and then moved south with an S shaped maneuver where it split into two.

A bright white light flying from east to west as the sun set around 7 pm. Thought initially it was a meteor due to the speed and the fact it was higher than the clouds. Traveled from above me to about 1/3 to the western horizon when it just faded out. I was disappointed that the meteor just fizzled out when all of a sudden, the object was at a standstill and very bright with multiple colors flashing from it. It hovered for a few mins at the same high altitude then changed to a very bright red. Soon after it darted in an S-shaped fashion to the south and perhaps a lower altitude, still very bright red when it stopped again. At this point, it split into 2 individual craft, both red in color. One flew east and lower, the other moved what seemed to be straight down where I lost sight of it behind a tree. Within in a few moments, the one light I could still see started to display multiple colors as it hovered, and then disappeared. At this point, I no longer saw either craft.
