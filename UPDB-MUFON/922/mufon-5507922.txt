{rjl} Hovering low, saw on east side of my house first, moved and stopped 3 times then ascended and gone.

This event happened in 91 or 92...I have never talked about it until just recently with my family, we live in Wise County in Texas, we also live on 5 acres in the country, myself and my two daughters were sitting outside on our back porch one night around 9:30ish playing with our new kittens only a couple months old, all the sudden from the corner of my eye on the east side of our house I see these 4 objects shaped like diamonds or kites whichever you want to choose, they were a very pretty red color, and one of them was bigger then the other 3, and they stay close together hovering low, maybe treetop, but they were not huge like a spaceship, they come around to directly face us {now facing south} stop and it almost seems like watching us for a few seconds, although I could not see inside the object it just felt like that, I guess you could say I was not believing what I was seeing, I tell my oldest daughter to run get the camera, when she comes back out they have moved over to the other side of our house now west, and just sit there a few seconds, she tried to take a picture but it did not work, I think she was just nervous and did not wind it because it was not a digital camera...then they move back south and go out a little ways onto the property that backs up to ours, still just hovering, then they spread out, sit there a few seconds, then ascend into the sky {never making a sound this whole time} and just like that they are gone...they never twirl, or spin just kind of move this way and that...left, right, up, down. I read about yall on youtube so came here to your site to tell this story, I always wondered if anyone else saw them that night and maybe reported it, I never did, and would not have known who to contact anyway, so here is my story, it may be to old to be of any interest to yall, but thought I would tell it anyway.
