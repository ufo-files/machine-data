Starlite light that shot off

Sitting on my deck this afternoon . No real clouds, perfectly blue skies. I suddenly noticed what appeared to be a star. I kept my focus for about 6 minutes. No movement whatsoever. Just twinkling like a star in the night sky. Suddenly it shot off to the right, further south and then it was gone.
