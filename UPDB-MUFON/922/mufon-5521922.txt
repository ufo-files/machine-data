Was outside by myself when I saw what looked like a bright red slightly pulsating star

I went outside in my back yard and saw, in the eastern sky just above the horizon, what looked like red star. I called inside to my 13 year old daughter and had her look at it too. She saw it the same as I did. 5 minutes later my neighbor also came out and I pointed it out to him and he saw the same thing. This whole time it never moved at all. Then I put a post onto facebook and while I was getting comments about it being Mars or the Sirius star, the red light sped off straight up just like a really fast rocket and disappeared.
