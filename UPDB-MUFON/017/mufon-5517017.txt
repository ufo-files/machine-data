{nd} Dark triangle shaped UFO flies overhead

My father and I were outside on 7/20/12 at approx. 9 pm. it was a clear night, all the stars were visible. we were in the backyard, which has multiple trees. we heard a hum-like airplane type sound, but it was not loud at all. we both looked up and saw a triangular shaped object flying south-southeast. it was a very low flying object. it appeared to have roughly 5 small white lights on the end of it. the best way that i can describe its shape is that of a stealth bomber. I am not sure if that is what it was or not, but it was too quiet to be any aircraft i have ever witnessed. the whole event lasted about 10 seconds as it flew across the sky and out of sight behind the treeline.
