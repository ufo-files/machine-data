Well I live in kechi,ks and was wondering if anyone had reported seeing an object in the early hrs on June 24th.  This was approximately 1:oo am , if looking straight up it would have been 5 to 10 degrees to the south and 15 degrees to the west. I was letting the dog out, and looking at the stars when I noticed this object, which I do not think was in our atmosphere. It appeared to be in space, but had to be very large. There were 3 orange to amber lights, very bright. I watched it for 5 min or so, then wondered if my eyes were playing tricks on me. So I watched for a few more minutes, then a smaller white light come out of it, flying straight north. with incredible speeds. It then made a u turn, and flew back to the 3 other lights. I am one of those skeptics from being in the military (usmc), knowing the gov and military have plenty of aircraft the public has no knowledge of. I believe that 99.99 percent of sightings is just unknown manned aircraft. But there is no way this could fall into that category. This was most definitely in space. And had to be extremely large.
