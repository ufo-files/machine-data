{jpg} Green glowing ORB larger than any aircraft moving at incredible speed over mountains

At exactly 12:08 a.m., 9/7/2018, I was sitting on my back porch enjoying the night sky & mountains towards Pikes Peak. From the direction of Cheyenne Mountain but at an elevation estimated to be 20,000 ft (I judged this because I know Pikes Peak is 14,000 ft high and this was considerably above it), a green glowing orb of a size that was way larger than any aircraft would appear in the sky visually at that distance from me, flew over the mountain range at an incredible speed and disappeared behind the west side area of Pikes Peak. It was a slightly arching kind of flight path. This took a total elapsed time of about 3 seconds! I had checked the time on my phone to make note of the time of occurrence. There was no sound, no trail and the only explanation I would have is that it reminded me of videos of a meteor entering our atmosphere however this was much lower and I never knew of a bright glowing green meteor before. I noticed no bright impact type light after it disappeared past the far side of Pikes Peak and I am guessing that it did not impact but kept flying at the same general altitude leaving my area.
