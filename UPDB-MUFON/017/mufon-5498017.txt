At least 40, solid white light objects, zig zagging and grouping headed out towards the coast.

My girlfriend and I were walking out the back door of the house to go to the store. As I walked up to stick my key in the door I realized it was very bright outside from the moon(the only reason I looked up). Right away I saw a single bright white light, I thought it might be a sattelite, I turned and said to my girlfriend, "hey do you see that?" Right as I said that I noticed multiple bright lights coming from the east towards the moon where I was looking, I screamed at my girlfriend "holy s**t, do you see those!" At first about three popped out behind cloud cover and the quickly grouped closer together, then about 5 after that came together, then multiple bunches of 3-5 with some single lights maneuvering around the sky, some were going straight, some were zig zagging and making quick turns to join with groups and so on, all while heading east to west out towards the pacific coast. After the group went by, a single two turbine jet flew past, possibly trying to follow or catch them, I don't know.
