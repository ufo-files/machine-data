Went out on my front porch about 9pm to water mums, looked up at sky because it was such a beautiful clear fall night. I noticed a VERY bright I thought was a star, changing colors, flashing white to blue to red to orange. I looked around at the sky, saw all the stars I'm familiar with, and constellations. The big dipper, orion all in place. So I figured I'd stick around and watch the flashing object. It was slightly wobbly and moving very slowly at a diagonal lower to the horizon from W/NW to north. There was a tall pine tree in the distance it was behind that for about 4 minutes and I could still see it flashing through the branches. Eventually, it went lower than a mountain in the distance and I lost sight of it. The whole sighting was approximately 50 minutes. I'm very oh, and an airplane flew right under it as I was observing it. I'm very familiar with the night sky, stars, and constellations, aircraft, I regularly watch the ISS, this was weird! My 9 year old watched with me she was actually freaked out! I tried to take a picture but all I had was my phone it did not come out:(
