Flashing grey cigar shaped object

I was driving and saw what looked like a plane. Because I have seen the same objects in the sky three times in the last few months, I know to look closer if it at first appears like a plane. I spotted the object and thought it was a plane, as it seemed to be at plane height, until I noticed it didn’t have a vertical tail wing or horizontal wings. It flashed white, then was a dark grey when it stopped flashing and then quickly disappeared into thin air. The sky was clear blue without a cloud.
