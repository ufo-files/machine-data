Two silver in color dish moving slowly across the highway between mountains.

Standing outside a friend home in eagle river Alaska, looking east, Two slow moving silver dish shaped UFOs slowly moved in view as they crossed from behind a mountain range, approx 50ft high across the road then back behind the mountains. I asked if my friend was seeing the same thing I was. She said yes. I don't believe my friend that was in her 40s at the time is still alive.
