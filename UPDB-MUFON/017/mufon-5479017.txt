{rjl} Green ball of fire/light slowly flies across sky, descends, then disappears

While driving through Dothan, AL en route to Montgomery, we saw a ball of green light, presumably fire, appear in the sky. It acted like a shooting star would, but in slow motion. It was descending, at an angle. It was much lower than a shooting stat would be, and it began with descending, so it wasn't shot up from the ground. It had a trail of white light then suddenly disappeared. Really weird.
