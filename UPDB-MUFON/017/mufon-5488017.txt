Bright unknown object in sky for half an hour.

I got into my car 8.30 am on Wednesday morning (12/10/15) and looked up at the sun rising from the east, it was still a little misty but to the right of the Sun was another really bright object almost like another Sun but maybe half the size. I drove of like any other morning but five minutes later when I was facing south again the said object was still there Bemused, I continued to drive but still observing when still in view, the object wasn't moving and remained the same. About twenty minutes into my journey whilst I was crossing the Kingston bridge in Glasgow I held my phone up to take some random pictures thinking it may be my only opportunity, I captured it about 4 times and continued on the last 10 minutes of my journey. I opened my shop at 9 am, told a friend who then went out to find a place to view it, he came back 5 minutes later to say it had disappeared, he then asked if I took any pics which I replied yes. I showed My friend who was also as bemused as myself as to what it could possibly be! The following day I checked the papers & Internet to see if there was other sightings, but nothing.
