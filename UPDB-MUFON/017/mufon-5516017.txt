On June 12, 2009 I awoke at 4:35 a.m.. I looked out my bedroom window and was shocked at what I saw. I told myself that I was going to be brave and watch this thing. I got my binocular out and watched it. I saw a silver saucer shape craft with red and white lights underneath it and a dome on top with small windows on the dome. The lights were bright. The craft was slanted towards the ground. It then went level (I feel like it sensed I was watching it) and moved slowly until it faded out. I was pretty scared but proud of myself that I kept watching it and not freak out. It was a clear night. I reported this sighting to Mr. Davenport. I found your site and wanted to report it to your group. This is my first real sighting of a craft but I have had some other strange things happen to me but do not want to talk about them on the net.
