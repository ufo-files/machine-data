Very large and bright object interested in me

I was taking out the trash at 6:23 PM when I looked up at the sky and noticed what looked like an LED light bar headed straight toward me, it’s descent angle was approximately around -20 degrees. It made no noise, and acted like it was headed to land, as it came toward me. I noticed it was huge, at least the size of a private jet or bigger. Then it stopped and stared right at me, as if it was spooked by my presence, almost like it was studying me. It had a a disc light all around it, super bright, this is the described LED light bar. It was so bright it burned through the cloud cover above. It also had a half circle dome on top, and another on the bottom. After a few minutes of studying at me, it leveled out. It then turned and darted off northbound, and disappeared.
