{rjl} witnessed 3 lights that first forned a line then changed into different types and sizes of triangles.

Saw 3 white/blue lights creating a line, then changed into different types of triangles and different sizes out by Ute HWY in between Lyons and Longmont. Changed direction as I did, and looked as if the bottom point of the triangle, was on the ground or hovering over a small body of water. On 1 of the 5 nights I've witnessed this, I was out of the car snapping pictures of the triangular object and it approached me,and all cellphone reception was lost until getting back on the Hwy. Notification popped up saying "no cell service available", while Spotify was still working. As I headed back into Longmont, the object continued hovering alongside the Hwy towards Platteville changing in different triangular formations. Lights would also blink on and off randomly.
