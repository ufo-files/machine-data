{kt}A light was seen in nightsky that made aprox 45degree turn towards atmospere and disapeared.

It was Halloween night(7pm), 2012. I was on our bay window looking out at he stars with my 2yr old and noticed what I thought was a star or planet because of its brightness(I just glanced at it and looked down to get my sons attention). When I looked back up, I noticed it was moving but there were no flashing lights on it. It seems to be flying towards the south, higher than a plane. I then thought it could be the ISS until it turned about 45 degrees (SE)& outwards, it appeared to fly further away from our atmosphere until it disappeared.
