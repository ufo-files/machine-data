{rjl} 2 orbs of flashing lights

Myself and wife were walking our dog and in park and walked onto top of park. We looked towards Pikes Peak and noticed to the southwest to orbs of flashing lights equal distance apart. We stopped and watched them for a second and I went to get my phone out and they shot off in opposite directions and disappeared.
