{dr}Rotating/flashing orb hovering in the North behind cloud cover, drifted slowly Northward.

Two of us were sitting in Rosicrucian Park in San Jose in the early afternoon relaxing in the park. We were looking at the sky, watching a bird and noticed a round object rotating/reflecting sunlight above cloud cover. Hovered and then drifted Northward. Was above nearby aircraft. Video obtained.

Eventually it traveled upward/northward and we lost sight of it.
