White glowing orb moving SE stopped above me and my wife, continued, then disappeared.

My wife and I exited a King Soopers on 120th & Colo. Blvd around 9:18pm. I looked up and saw a fantastic white orb moving SE. As I stared at it, it stopped, hovered, and then continued. I yelled at my wife to look at it. She saw the same thing. I knew it wasn’t Jupiter as the planet was south of our position. There was no noise. Just an unceasing bright white orb. I took out my phone and opened my star tracker app. I placed my camera on the orb. No star or constellation came up. It was not ISS. It then disappeared. Because it was dark, I could not ascertain its elevation. There were no other lights - nothing blinking. As it moved away from us, I took great pains to note that it was consistently a white orb all around. It was like a light emanated from it. 

I am a former state representative. So, I posted the experience on Facebook. I then learned that others saw the same thing moving from northwest to southeast. Also, later in the FB post, a Facebook friend placed a screenshot of a MUFON report from that same evening from Estes Park, which is northwest from my position, that described a substantially similar object. 

It’s eerie how it stopped in the sky when I looked up at it and just hovered for a few seconds before proceeding.
