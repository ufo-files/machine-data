Black Sphere

I was out doing some yard work in the back last night while i was walking to the front of the yard I happened to look up about what I would say is about maybe 500 to 800 feet up was a black sphere flying by in a straight line going west bound. The weather condition was windy I thought it might be bird at first but it was close enough that naked eye could make out it shape, no wings then i thought it was a ballon becasue of its shape but balloons don't fly in a straight line. I watched it for about 20 to 45 seconds before it disappered into the horizon. It was very quick moving about the air.
