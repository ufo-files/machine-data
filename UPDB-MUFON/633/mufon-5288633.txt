2 sphere shaped bright lights make fast right angle turns in sky

Facing west I observed an object in the distance sky flying in a northern direction. It was in the shape of a sideways V (like the stealth bomber but considerably larger). It was only observed after what appeared to be a flash from the center of the object. It moved quickly across the sky but faster then a commercial aircraft. Behind it appeared a smaller glowing sphere that sped forward to the main craft and made a sharp right angle flight change and passed behind the V shaped craft then disappeared.. A few seconds later another smaller glowing sphere appeared behind the craft and again sped up and made a sharp right angle turn but this object passed in front of the craft and disappeared. The speed of the spheres was exceptionally fast and there was no pause prior to the right single turn. There was no curve to the turns made by the spheres. The V shape craft continued in a northern flight path until it was past the tree line. It may have been observed by people traveling on the I-95 highway in the Vero Beach area. I reached out the our local airport as well as the FAA to file a report.
