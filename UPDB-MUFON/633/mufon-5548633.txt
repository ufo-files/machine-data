I thought it was a helicopter at first but no sound

I was walking at the Dublin football field and saw a light coming from the north east, first thought it was a helicopter but no sound then it started moving westerly and the light would go out and see a faint flickering and stop and illuminate and like it was hovering in one place.  I don't know long it would have stayed but I had go tell someone , Like always it was gone behind clouds and not to be seen again.
