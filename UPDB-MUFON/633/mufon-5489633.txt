{dr}Portal opened up in middle of night n beings observed me.

All I can remember is that it was about 2 or 3am and something made me feel really awake and I'm laying on my bed looking up at the ceiling because I couldn't go back to sleep and out of nowhere there was sort of what seemed like a portal on the ceiling right above my head and there were two beings looking down on me as if inspecting me. I couldn't move my body but I could move my head and eyes so I kept looking at them and they noticed I was looking back at them. From what I could see they were in what looked like an operating room peering down on me and then looking at each other in agreement by nodding as if to say to each other "this one might do just fine", after that all I could remember is this portal closing up and me waking up the next day. Best example I can give is as if someone in the first floor is looking up at the second floor of a hospital and being able to see through the floor and patient table from their bed in the first floor. After that night I was just left with many questions and no answers. I was afraid to sleep for the past two days after that but while it was happening my curiosity overwhelmed my fear because of the type of person that I am I like to observe n not run away from things unless I feel I'm threatened.
