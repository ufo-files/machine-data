{tjd} Photo of Northern Rocky Mountains after Major Snow storm

I took this picture of the Twin Peaks Mountain Range for a Dual monitor setup. When I checked out the photo on March 29, 2019, I found something flying in the very Middle of the picture! Since it made no noise, I didn't notice it in the air just flying along in a south facing direction. As you can see it doesn't look like a bird or a balloon or anything else for that matter that I know of. There are two Jet com-trails on either side of the photo, but I can't see any plane, just the trails.
