Orb over water.

Nov 26th around 7PM @ The Hapuna Westin.
My fiancé and I were in the hot tub looking at the stars.
Neither of us drink, smoke or take any form of drug.
We were using a star finder app to see constellations etc...
Then a large light appeared, we both thought it was a plane coming into landing as the light was bright, big and at the height of a plane descending to land.
But after a few minutes, we realized that it wasn't a plane. The light was not getting closer or moving and there was no sound.
I checked the star app. Jupiter and Saturn were the biggest and brightest visible according to the app, but in the wrong area of the sky.
At this point, I couldn't stop starring at it, and I felt a sense of peace and reunion, thinking about it sounds crazy. I felt as if it was sending emotion and understanding into my head.
The light proceeded to pulsate, change shape and glow; bright orb, to 7 pointed star, dim oval, etc... These changes lasted around 45 mins. 
At one point, it darted to the left. This scared my fiancé, as nothing he knows of could do that.
He studies meteors, and quickly ruled that out. As well as, planes, helicopters, shooting stars, a space station, stars, planets...
It then faded away.
If it was thick clouds covering it, we would have lost visuals of the stars too. But it was just that large orb that faded away.
For some reason, I didn't film it. I felt somewhat hypnotized looking at it and felt so much joy, like a reunion... He didn't film it, I think because he was scared.
The next day I met with Zach from Mufon HI, and we both explained what we saw.
