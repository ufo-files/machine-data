Triangle Shape Crossing The Sky

After come of a party at night, me and 2 witness lay down over the sidewalk, looking at the stars at clear sky, suddenly a giat V shape cross the sky from the top sky, had 5 point lights in formation and was visible just in few seconds, not for all the path. the other witness mencioned at the same time ''did you saw that'' and i take as a confirmation that our 3 saw the same thing.
