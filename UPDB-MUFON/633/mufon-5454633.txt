While taking scenic photographs in Estes Park near the hotel we were staying at, I snapped two photographs showing an object in the sky that moved from my right to the left. Interestingly, while taking the photos, I did not notice the object in my camera's view. I only noticed the object after, when I was flipping through the images. I didn't hear any sounds, or feel any vibrations other than the wind blowing. It should be noted that the Denver airport is approximately a 1.5 hour drive from where the photos were taken.
