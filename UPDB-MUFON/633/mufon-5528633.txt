Biright sperical orb that glided across the sky at a moderately fast pace.

I was walking my dog outside in my neighborhood, just listening to my Ipod and gazing up at the sky like I always do. I noticed to the left there appeared a spherical orb that I had not noticed before, gliding across the sky at a moderately fast pace. It had no tail or blinking light. It had the luminescence of the moon. It glided across the sky and was gone in less than three seconds. I first thought it was a shooting star, but I have seen shooting stars and it looked nothing like this object. I have no idea what made me look at the sky at the time I did. I think the object had already been on it's path and I noticed it while it continued it's northern glide towards Route 1. The orb just flew in a straight path. It had no stops or turns that I noticed. The speed of the object remained the same. This is the first time I have seen anything like this, so I freaked out and ran inside after swearing under my breath and contemplating what I just saw. During the 3 seconds I saw the object I was in a mesmerizing trance. Just following the object with my head until it continued it's northern path and was out of my sight.
