I was taking pictures of the Blue Moon on 7-31-15 when a black very Huge object swept quickly across the Moon. It was a very wide cylinder shape with a top sticking up in the middle. Almost like a boat with a single sail, but a bit rounded. It was larger to the naked eye than it is in my picture. Moved incredibly fast. It looked like it was 1/3 the length of the moon. Exciting, but freaked me out. Picture does not do it justice.
