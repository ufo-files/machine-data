Fast moving cylinder with no sound or vapor trail.

In front garden of my house. I was looking up towards a wasps nest near top corner of my house. Suddenly caught view of fast moving white/silver cylinder moving fast across the sky at height. What made me feel this was strange was I saw no wings, it emitted no sound and left no vapour trail. I tried focusing my phones camera on it but couldn't. It disappeared out of sight after a minute and I went in.
