{rjl} Second sighting of line of liggts in Kerman CA

The first sighting occurred on new years eve of 2014 but news stations shrugged off like it was just fireworks. Second sighting was by friends who said the same line of lights was back hovering or slowly moving in the sky. The lights remained for an unspecified amount of time before turning off one by one and disappearing completely. They said it caught their attention because it was odd for so many lights to be in an exact row without moving up or down. The outline of craft was not visible in dark. Everyone was scared since this was the second time the lights appeared.
