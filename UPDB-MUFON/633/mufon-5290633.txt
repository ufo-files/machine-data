2 bright, then dim lights side by side with red pulsing lights in the center

2 bright star like lights with glowing halo underneath first observed about 15 miles away. Thought it was a star and a satellite that was in close proximity. As I drove about 3 miles it got really close, the bright lights dimmed and could see that there was a red halo inbetween the 2 lights, it would stay constantly glowing however it would slowly get brighter than dimmer as would the 2 lights beside it. I got closer and it stopped travel and hovered about 200ft in the air. I could not believe what I was seeing and when I drove under it I pinned it home about 2 miles away. Came back with family about 2 minutes later and it was gone.
