5 am thought it was Jupiter it was bright then approached me.

Went outside on porch (woke up too early). Thought it was Jupiter and too lazy to bring out the telescope. Then it suddenly started approaching me. It was beautiful. As it got closer it looked kind of like the mason sign? It was no longer spherical but like an inverted triangle with a sphere in the middle and bright blue. I don't remember anything after that. My dog did would not go outside with me. All I remember is that it was beautiful and I was not scared but slightly freaking out as it approached me. I managed to get a few pics but no close ups at that point when it was close to me. The next thing I know is waking up in bed this afternoon.
