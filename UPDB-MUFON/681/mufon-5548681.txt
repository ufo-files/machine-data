flew across sky, no lights, silent

I was sitting outside in hot tub with friend. No alcohol involved. Sky was clear, no wind. at approx 0100 hrs my friend said, "whats that" and pointed to the NE. What I saw was a cylinder looking object, rather large but diff to say how big as I did not know what altitude it was at. There were no lights at all and the object made no sound. It flew in a nose to tail way as an airplane would with no wings. It went from the NE toward the SE. It flew in a straight path and appeared to be moving ratehr fast. We watched it until we lost sight of it. San Jose international airport is just west of where I first noted object but the airport does not allow landings after 0000 hrs. It flew across what would have been the normal approach path to the airport. i immediatly knew what I saw was not any aircraft I had ever seen before.
