Thought was just a satellite at first

Was about a half hour after sundown just happened to be looking at a small private plane when suddenly another light appeared The light was white then turned red so I thought it was a jet at first flying over the small plane. The light was on a steady trajectory like a satellite but was red. as it was crossing overhead the red turned to a green then to a very brilliant blue. The direction was traveling to the S.W. at this point from the East As I watched the blue light (no sound) it made a wide turn toward the N.W. and turned white again. I watched it as long as I could but lost it due to trees. It appeared to have been extremely high up and traveled like you might see a satellite travel except much larger, had colors and changed direction. I could not get any video and I doubt a phone would have recorded it as it appeared.
