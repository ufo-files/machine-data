Round metallic object

I observed a round metallic object with flat funnel shaped lower surface on which color lights have been flashing, flying noiseless and slowly in about 30 feet above the roof top of a house.
The object diameter was around 80 to 100  Feet and I could just see 1/2 of the object as it slowly disappeared over the rooftop behind the house, invisible then from my position.
Timeframe: Esther in 1977 or 1978 in late August.
