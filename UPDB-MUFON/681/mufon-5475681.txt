DISC THE SIZE OF A SMALL PLANE HOVERING ABOVE US

I WAS HAVING A FAMILY MEETING WHEN SUDDENLY WE SPOTTED A DISC THE SIZE OF A SMALL PLANE HOVERING ABOVE US, GLOWING SEVERAL COLORS BUT ALL OF THEM RETURNING TO A YELLOW-RED TONE, AT THE BEGGINING IT JUST SPINNED, WOBBLING AND GLOWING, THEN WITH NO SOUND IT MADE A BRIEF SLOW SPEED FLIGHT NORTH WEST BEFORE MAKING AN EXTREMELY FAST MOVE AND DESAPEARED.
