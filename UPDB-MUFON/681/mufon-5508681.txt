Bright star making erratic movements

I was leaving for work at about 9:20pm and as I went outside I noticed a bright star in the sky. Upon paying closer attention to it I realized that it was moving, travelling in a NNE/NE direction. At first I thought it must have been a satellite or something, but then it started moving from side to side and I was pretty sure that this wasn't a satellite. I went inside to look for a camera but when I came back out with one, it apeared to be gone, however I saw a faint moving light very far away in the East direction.
