Toplike object with legs seen in Coldwater, Michig

We were shooting skeet in the back of our trailer sales lot called "WH" East ofUS 12 in Coldwater, MI. I was suprised to see over the fence, 1/4 mile away, atop like craft with legs. It was off white and it was round like a top andnarrowed down like a funnel. About 30 ft. wide. It looked like it had 4 legsand it had lights, 1 on top and 3 lights I could see on the bottom. I guess thelights drew my attention.I was so shocked and couldnt believe a real UFO. I called my dad, andbrothers, and people also who were looking at a trailer and friends came tolook. We watched it for 10-12 minutes. All it did was hover above a tree. Mydad said he could see something moving around on the inside. He was using asmall telescope. I also noticed 3 U.S. Air Force planes circling for about 30minutes. The UFO all at once disappeared. We still cant believe what we saw,but we all know we saw a UFO.
