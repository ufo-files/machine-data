Glowing lights

I was driving south on I-39,It was 8:50 PM I noticed one glowing light and then four more came to light, they were orange in color. When they appeared they seem to just appear, kind of like some body was turning lights on with A dimmer switch. As soon as all lights lit they dissapeared. the lights were in A straight line . I thought it was very interesting.I had seen lights like this on 7/29/2008 in the same area but on the other side of the interstate. this area is about five miles south of the central wisconsin airport. I know for sure these where not an airplane. both times I saw the lights it was A clear sky and I was driving down the Interstate. I could not see anything but the lights.
