{nd} Two green balls of flaming round vehicles falling like a star

I walked out my back porch and viewed this with another witness and thought it might be an asteroid or rock made of copper falling from the sky. About a month later my son and I were driving home from Long Beach Ca and were just passing Garlock road on Hwy 14 and notice a flaming green ball with a fire trail coming in just like the two I had seen earlier that fall. It then stopped suddenly near the last chance canyon area off of Garlock road and became a saucer craft glowing green on bottom. This was witnessed by my son who could watch it from the passenger seat of the truck and I did not see it morph as he had into a saucer type craft. He said it slowing hovered down over the mountain and then did not see it anymore. The previous year last chance canyon was declared off limits to local miners by Senator Cranston and Diane Feinstein. After moving to Henderson Nv in 2005 and in the fall of 2005 I walked outside about 1 am and witnessed the same green flaming ball of light looking like a falling star and then going behind a mountain. So I had three separate viewings of the same phenomenon. I have witnessed 138 ufo's since 1979 and most showed a glowing red on bottom type of color but only one glowed green that my son saw. But all three viewings showed a green fireball coming in at a slant.
