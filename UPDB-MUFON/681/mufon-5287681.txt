UFO during graduation

This event happened during my high school graduation. Roughly May of 2011 in San Diego California. The school in question is Steele Canyon. During my principles graduation speech I felt a strong compulsion to look in the air behind me. Hovering in the air was a football shaped object that reminded me of a blimp, but none that I've ever seen before. The object had some kind of metal rod extending from on end to about half way across the bottom length of the object. The object was spinning clockwise the entire time it was in the air. It seemed stationary. It was made of some kind of metallic silver-like material. There were no lights or windows or anything, just a solid football shaped blimp. While watching this object I saw three rings of brown energy radiate from the object, like ripples in water. After the third ring disappeared the object began floating away past some nearby mountains and disappeared from sight.
