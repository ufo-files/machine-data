UFO disguised as a C-5 Glaxay

Making a left turn from Bellvue to Kipling-South, the same place where my left turn was mimicked by a UFO in April 2020, I saw a large aircraft about 200 feet above Kipling and C-470, above my residential neighborhood. I watched it as it turned north and followed the crest of the "hogback" until it was directly opposite my driving direction. It was slow and silent. It was flying about 30 mph, maybe slower since I was doing about 40 traveling the opposite direction. Once opposite me to the west, it dipped westerly below the hogback. I could no longer see it. I'm thinking at the time it's an odd place for a C-5 to be flying and flying so slowly, below stall speed (132 mph). There were no markings on the craft. If tET craft are capable of transforming into various shapes, why not an American aircraft? A Lockheed Martin facility is directly below where the craft diverted west. Perhaps they were signaling Lockheed, "If we can do you, you can do us." I filed another report in 2019 which included another winged aircraft in conjunction with two alien vehicles.
