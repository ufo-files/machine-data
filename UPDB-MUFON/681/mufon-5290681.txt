Triangle shape with 3 lights visible at points

We were having coffee outside on our patio in the dark looking for satellites when we noticed a small single prop plane that had just taken off from ( we think) a small regional airport ( Whiteman Airport) near Pacoima flying from the north-east headed south west. When it got almost straight over us, a triangular shaped craft appeared out of nowhere flying very fast from west to east and made crazy sharp 90 degree turns in four directions then continued on towards the east before just disappearing as it flew.
