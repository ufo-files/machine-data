Looked like white tic-tac

Object may have been high altitude balloon but did not find anything that shows a balloon in the area and with binoculars, could not see that it was shape of balloon.
The object did not move with wind or clouds. It was above cloud cover and clouds would pass but object did not move. We used binoculars against telephone pole and used wires above to use as guide to help stabilize and could see it was just white tic-tac shaped object hovering during entire time, (approx 15- 20 minutes). The object stayed in same place while small "wispy" clouds past by. I did call all the family to witness and everyone did see the same thing. I tried to get pictures with phone but such a small object, it did not turn out. (still have pics).
