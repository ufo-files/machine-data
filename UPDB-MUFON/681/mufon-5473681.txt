{cak} My husband and I saw two orbs of light fly over our house closely followed by two air force jets. We watched them until they flew out of sight.

My husband and I were standing outside about 10pm, we were talking and I heard a jet flying very low. I said, "boy he's flying low," at which time an air force jet came directly above my house and then we saw two large, glowing orbs that made no sound and were flying parallel of each other, and an air force jet was behind each one. We watched them until they went out of sight. I did some research and found out they had been seen the night before in the neighboring county.Fast forward a few months later and a friend of mine who works nightshift on a stripmining job posted a picture on Facebook of the two objects flying over his job. My husband and I were not at all afraid and we have talked about it alot. We have decided that we did see ufos, I don't think the government has any secret aircraft like that. Unfortunately, I didn't have my camera with me at the time as I was attending to a sick horse.
