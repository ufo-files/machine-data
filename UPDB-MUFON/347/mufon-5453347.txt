Looked like moving stars

On 4/27/20 at approx 21:05 I went outside in backyard facing the southeast direction and I was looking at the stars and crescent moon. I noticed a solid light in high in the sky moving silently from East to west. I was puzzled by no sound, no blinking lights so I thought maybe I was seeing a satellite. I called for my brother who also came out to view the sky to look for what I had just seen. Just then he noticed a “string” of solid lights arranged in single file, perfectly spaced, moving from northwest to southeast in the sky. They were high up similar to a jet that has reached its cruising altitude. No sound, no blinking lights....nothing!! We counted approximately 25-30. I called for my mother to come outside and she witnessed what we were seeing. Very disturbing....I’m still shaking by what I just witnessed. I have never seen anything like this.
