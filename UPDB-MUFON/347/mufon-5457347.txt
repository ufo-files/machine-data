{tjd} mysterious object seemed to pass over us.

On a back road in the huron national forest my father and I observed a glowing orb type vessel. It made no sound as it approched. We watched it get closer, from our possition the orb was about 75 to 80 degrees when the object begain to dim and brighten. as well as seem to get smaller and larger. as we watched it to moved to the otherside of the road. As it passed us. about 130 degrees after passing us the object got lower and brighter. Then shot up in the sky at a slight angle and within maybe a half second. the object was gone.
