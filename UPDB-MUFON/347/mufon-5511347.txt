Early morning observation

I was standing outside at work and I noticed 2 objects (white in color) in the sky on a clear night (no cloud cover) streak across the sky at a high rate of speed from the southeast. Both objects stopped dead in its tracks. Hovered for a couple seconds, decended, then rapidly ascended, moved on a straight path to the North. The objects hovered and split into what I counted was 4 smaller objects. The two main objects were stationary while the other 4 were moving with rapid omni-directional patterns. The 4 smaller object reconverged with the 2 objects and the 2 objects merged into one and turned into a bluish-white colored object. The shape of the object was more of an oblong shape but not a complete sphere. The object then rapidly ascended out of view.
