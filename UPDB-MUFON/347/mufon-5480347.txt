{cak} I was taking a picture of 2 airplanes flying around and caught this orb in the process

I was driving in Madison Heights Michigan on John R between 13 and 14 mile road. I noticed 3 airplanes flying which I thought was pretty close to each other. I took a picture of a single airplane and nothing out of the ordinary. I was able to stop at another red light when I took this picture of the other two airplanes. I then looked at the picture and noticed an orb above one of the airplanes. The most astonishing thing about it was the perfect circle shape with 3 dots inside which creates a perfect triangle if you connect the dots. I immediately thought of a orb like shape. After the sighting I must have looked at the picture about 100 times because I think I captured something out of the ordinary. After I looked at the picture I looked back up in the sky and saw nothing but the airplanes.
