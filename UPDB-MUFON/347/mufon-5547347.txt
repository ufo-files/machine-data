Big bright orb...again.

My daughter and I were coming out of our garage when we saw a very large and silent bright white glowing non pulsing, orb flying overhead going northwest to southeast. It flew towards Reno and when it hit this tiny puff of a cloud it disappeared. It was just beautiful. The time is not exact but I remember the sun was not down enough that we could see stars. We did see a low flying helicopter going over Reno for a nice contrast. This was the largest orb we've seen.
