{dr}HUGE DARK rectangular object with bright light in cloud cover.

I was waiting at the bus stop on North Camano Island, Terry's Corner. I was facing East and there were broken clouds over the mountains in the distance, when I saw a VERY LARGE Rectangular Light on a HUGE RECTANGULAR BLACK SHIP sticking through the clouds. As I was reaching for my cellphone to take a picture the ship moved into the cloud cover, and out of sight. I knew it was a UFO because I was trained as a lookout in the Navy to report one if I saw it. I felt disappointed that I couldn't get my cellphone out fast enough to get the picture.
