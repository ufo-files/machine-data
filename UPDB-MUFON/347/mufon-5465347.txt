{tjd} Like rectangular star of Bethlehem, soft light streaming from corners despite no clouds. I was walking, ran to neighbor's to tell him grab a camera, but it had descended behind mountain.

I went for an evening walk on the dark and empty road of my rural mountain. Stars were just appearing when I noticed a very large light. It looked like the moon popping out behind clouds, only it was rectangular and there were no clouds. It had moonbeams streaming from all four corners, soft ones like those that filter through trees on a full moon night. It reminded me of the way the star of Bethlehem is depicted, except for being an upright rectangle instead of a diamond shape. I remembered that there would be no moon and there were no clouds. At first I was just curious, noticed it was not moving and got excited. I wished I'd brought my cell, and began to run toward a nearby house where I knew the owner was a sky watcher, but realized his house was too far as the light had started descending, so I knocked on the nearest door of a neighbor. But when the occupant came out, the light had disappeared behind the mountain. I told him about it, then said goodnight and continued my walk. By then, the stars were brilliant in the moonless sky, as they frequently are here in the remote mountains of Northern California. I'm reporting it because I want to know if anyone else saw it.
