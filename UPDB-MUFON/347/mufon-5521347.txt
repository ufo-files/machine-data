UFO seen from cruise ship

While on our cruise to the cayman islands as we passed to the left of Cuba, our room was facing the gulf of mexico i was looking out our stateroom window and saw what appeared to be a object well off in the distance, it seemed to pulsate different colors, i tried to take a photo but couldn't get a good angle from window as it seemed to move slightly from view, i went from the 7th floor, and proceeded to the 10 floor, taking approx. 5 mins to get to that deck, i couldn't see the object any longer once i gained my bearing as it was pitch dark out there with it being about 1045pm in the middle of the atlantic. I do not know the size or true shape of the object as it was night and we were in the ocean. My wife says it just sort of turned itself off and she couldn't see it any longer , all the while i was making my way to the 10 floor(lido deck) to try and capture a photograph of the object. enclosing 2 photos one of the tv screen noting our approx location about 10 mins after the sighting. the other is a photo showing the line of sight to the possible ufo, and angle.
