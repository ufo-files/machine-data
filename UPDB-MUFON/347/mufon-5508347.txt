{nd} Red Lights over the Puget Sound

I had just got off work and was on the way home from a Mariners game with my two friends. After we drop my friend off we take a turn down 240th and start going west down this hill towards the water and I notice 4 red pulsing lights. Three are collected one above the other moving south slowly and at different speeds. One red light is by its self farther to the left. At the bottom of the hill we take a left on 16th and I notice that the red lights are starting to disperse. I begin thinking that by the time I get home I may not be able to see these lights because theyre moving so quickly. Before I know it two lights are tree level to the north and the lonely light is still to the west. We take a right and continue down the street for a while. When I look up to find the lights over our evergreen trees I see one flying above me (70 degree angle maybe). It was pretty shocking because I didn't think these lights were close enough to go from what seemed over the Sound to above me head in seconds. We took a left and went down Marine View Drive and I saw one more red light behind us in the northern sky. The light wasnt as bright which made me think it was getting father. Its annoying because my camera wouldnt pick up the light but it was such a exhilarating moment. Ive always wanted to say (I think) Ive seen UFOs. It was so cool and interesting.
