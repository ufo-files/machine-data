{rjl} Bright-white UFO, much nebulosity, zig-zag up and down

I had two UFO sightings from my house, one in September 2019 and one on December 27, 2019. The first was of a huge ball of bright-white light to the north. The brightness illuminated the clouds for 10-20 degrees around. 

This lasted about 5 minutes and the object executed geometric movements, both in altitude and azimuth. There was no sound. And then it disappeared. 

The second, last night, happened when I peeked outside with thoughts of doing some binocular astronomy. But a bright light, somewhat above the ecliptic, caught my eye. Again, it demonstrated some nebulosity. And then it grew closer...no sound, again. It then darted straight up and then back down. It went from east to west, up and down, in right-angle turns. It finally just disappeared. 

Also, back in September, a neighbor driving down the road by my place saw a bright-white object zoom down to just over the treeline. This person would probably be willing to discuss her encounter. 

I look forward to hearing from someone at MUFON regarding all this.
