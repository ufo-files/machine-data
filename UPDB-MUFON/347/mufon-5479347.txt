{jpg} Liquid metal in high winds it never moved, but drained my car battery and my dogs wew going wild.

We had just had a thunderstorm, and in the midwest you can see the winds whipping the clouds by. I had to walk my dog and when I went outside the dogs in the pen (4) they we're going nuts barking and growling. There is a large garage/barn about 100/150 feet from the house and in my line of sight. Above the barn there was a sphere just under the cloud line. It looked like liquid mercury. The clouds we're reflecting as they flew by. This sphere did not move at all. Eventually, the clouds covered it completely after about 15 minutes. My car was parked in front of the barn. When I went to go somewhere after I brought the dogs in my battery was dead. I don't believe in coincidences. The only thing I could think of doing was wave, so I did. There was no sound and I was not scared.
