{nd} Over two nights. Objects hovered and moved then turned off.

on feb 26 my aunt and I were coming over an over pass and noticed 3 yellowish lights hovering in the sky, maybe 2-3 miles in front of us, then it looked like they just turned off like a switch. The following night we were on our porch and saw 2 objects moving about the same distance away, and the same thing happened. A few minutes later I saw 4 objects an 2 were moving slowly then they all turned off abruptly. Each event was no more than 2 minutes long.
