Giant Craft Lined with Bright Lights Hovering Over Western Mass (October 24 2020)

My boyfriend and I were driving west on i90 on the mass pike when we both saw this giant "craft" hovering in the sky far off in the distance not moving at all for maybe 20 seconds before he said "hurry take a picture!". We probably saw it hovering unmoving for maybe a minute or so after I took the picture and before it just disappeared into nowhere. All of a sudden it was gone. At the time he described the lights on it as "multiple bulbs" and I first called them "rivetted". You can't see the individual bulbs in the picture because it was so far away. I've uploaded my pictures, time stamps, location stamps from my iphone. Those will tell the whole story much better than this. It wasn't until over a month later that I decided to look this up on the Mufon website when I came across someone's elses sighting on here of the same exact thing "giant cigar shaped sighting over mass". They're description describes the same exact object, 2 minutes before, maybe about 50 miles away along the same lattitude. The only thing I can understand is THEY didn't take a picture!!!!
