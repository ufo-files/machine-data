{nd} U shaped object flying straight. Metallic and transparent at the same time. Dark to light

I was laying down in the middle of a soccer field watching the sky when I noticed something out the corner of my eye. It began to be come more visible and I jumped up to get a better look at it. It flew a straight line path and was darker in the front and gradually got lighter in the back. It appeared to be both transparent and metallic but it is very hard to explain because I have never seen anything like it. It was dead silent and I only saw it by absolute sheer luck. It was huge and flying at less than 20,000 feet. If I had to guess it was over 500 feet wide and long. It not only appeared but also disappeared. It was incredibly stealthy and smooth.
