{rjl} metal object hovering with lights or object reflecting from the sun.

I was called by my co worker to come and see a metal object hoovering in the sky in the direction of the moon which you can see clearly today there is no cloud cover only a blue sky. I look up and couldn't believe it but the metal object looked like a small dot in the sky with lights or reflection from the sun. A fighter plane pass above doing mach 3 and you can definitely tell it was a fighter plane then another commercial airline plane passed underneath it the metal object was still hovering. I took several picture's but the object was so high the phone camera not strong enough to zoom in. I did manage to get one photo with an anomaly on it. After an hour I lost sight of it. I felt so excited..
