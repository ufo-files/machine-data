Walked outside and it caught my eye

On October 5th 2020 approx 8pm I went out into my back yard and noticed a light that somehow looked different than a usual aircraft light. As I watched it the object would get brighter then dim. The object would then change speed. Slower, faster then stop and then slowly speed up. There was no sound and no flashing lights at all. I went in the house and got my wife and 9 year old daughter that both witnessed it with me. As we watched the object it slowly went toward the northeast and not too fart past 90 degrees above our heads. Once it had gotten past us a little bit. Not even to the horizon or close to it it just vanished. I captured 1 minute of video on camera that I still have.
