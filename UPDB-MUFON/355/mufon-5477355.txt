{rjl} The object seemed to glow and hover as it changed direction. It also faded in and out in various places.

My son and I were driving towards our home when he noticed an object hovering. There was no noise and the object moved erratically after being noticed. I was convinced this couldn't be a balloon or an airplane as no such object can move like that. It seemed to fade in and out of different spots in the sky. It did this twice, moving in an opposing figure-eight with flairs at the ends where it faded. The object moved with such speed before disappearing. It faded out for a second time and didn't reappear. When we saw it we weren't sure what to, make of it but after we were sure it wasn't a weather balloon or an airplane.
