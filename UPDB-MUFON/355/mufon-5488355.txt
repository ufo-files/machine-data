I saw a bright orb like object in the sky and it moved very quickly.

It was around 8-9 o'clock at night. I was outside in my backyard and I was looking at the stars. After a little while of walking around, I looked at the sky again and a bright, orb like...thing was in the sky and it was so bright compared to the other stars. In fact, it pretty much made the stars appear to be dull. I was really confused and I was assuming it might of been a plane but I wasn't convinced at all. It was too big to be a plane or star. It seemed still but when I looked at it closely, I noticed that it was slowly moving. Kind of like a slight hovering movement. At this point I was really freaked out and a dull static noise began to grow in my ears. It was getting to the point where I looked away and rubbed my ears to soothe them (due to my autism, I have sensitive hearing). After the static died down(Maybe around 20 seconds or so, I looked back up at the sky and the orb was on the other side of my peripheral vision. I ended up getting really spooked, so I ran back inside. Unfortunately, I didn't have a camera or phone with me.
