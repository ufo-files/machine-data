{rjl}There were circular craft in the sky.

I was doing laundry on night. My laundry room is outside. In between doing loads I noticed something in the sky so I ran inside and grabed my phone to record it. I knew right away it was not regular aircraft because of its shape and the way it behaved in the sky. It was a circular craft blinking its lights. It made me feel excited and honoured in a way, because I was abke to witness it. If I wasnt with my two youngest daughters I would have tried to meditate to see if I could connect with anything. Unfortunately my youngest daughter is only 2 and it was around 10pm and her before time so could only record a few minutes.
