{nd} white light observed in field while in vehicle

AS PASSENGER IN VEHICLE OBSERVED WHITE/BLUE LIGHT APPOX 300 YARDS AWAY HOVERING IN AIR. WITNESS PASSED LIGHT THAN BACKED UP TO OBSERVE. OBJECT THAN BEGAN TO COME TOWARDS VEHICLE. WHEN LIGHT CAME WITHIN 100 YARDS WITNESS BECAME ALARMED AND LEFT AREA WITH LIGHT FOLLOWING THEM.LIGHT CAME NO CLOSER THAN APPOX 100 YARDS AND FOLLOWED VEHICLE FOR APPOX 2 MILES THAN DISAPEARED.LIGHT WAS NOT SEEN AGAIN.
