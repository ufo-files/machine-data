Bright light very slowlt moved closer while descending. Half hour later it had moved westerly, light flared out & began changing colours.

First observed the light/object about 5:52 pm on Dec 17/19. Took a vid or two as company arrived.
Observed again on Dec 22/19 beginning about 5:46 pm. Took the colour changing vid about 6:30 pm. While watching this I thought I might have seen a much smaller light drop down beside the original. Reviewing the vids the next day, noticed there seemed to be a smaller light mimicking the colour changing behaviour. There's possibly a similiar small 3rd light to the left. I've asked a couple ppl if they could identify if there's a gizmo that could do this. All were baffled.
Also took vid (all vid & pics are taken on a Note 8) around the same time on Dec 23/19. Also, there is a pic with 4 lights following a slightly curved line - I did not notice when I snapped the puc, was just looking to see what if anything would show up in the pic. I believe it was foggy that night.
Was busy on the 24th so no time to watch long but did get a vid or two in. It was overcast. Later left & stayed with family a couple nights.
Checked for the light/object at the same times on the 26th & 27th. No sighting. It is overcast/foggy.
I am not certain what flew almost directly overhead on Dec 15/19. I do know it was big & very quiet particularly for its size & proximity. I did vid it but was not in full control - its difficult to zoom in against a black sky with the pixelation through the cell. It does not depict what I thought I saw.
