{tjd} Orange glowing objects (7-8 total) in various flying formations

During PT one morning we witnessed 7 to 8 orange/red/white glowing objects coming from one horizon traveling straight over us. As they did, the group went from an almost circle with one in the middle to a V formation and then changed to a line and then changed to an almost W formation. At the time of day no aircraft was in the air and it is a no fly zone at the time. Was not after burners or flares! Just glowing spheres in intelligent formations.
