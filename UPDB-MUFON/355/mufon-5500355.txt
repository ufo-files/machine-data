{nd} Woke up and couldn't move, was pregnant w/ bed rest, problems went away

I kept waking up around two or three am and was unable to move. This continued for a couple of weeks. I was about three months pregnant and when I went to the doctor she said it might be poor circulation. I also had a problem bleeding so she recommended I stay in bed. One night I woke up and just like all the rest of the nights this happened I couldn't move again. This time I saw a three foot tall old man looking being, crossing in front of me slowly from left to right in front of my bed. He even walked like an old man and scooted slowly from foot to foot. I was terrified and my first thought was not alien. He did not look like anything I had seen before. I do not recall the rest of the night. After the experience I stopped bleeding and I am now super interested in physics and science. I feel as if I were woken up. My Son whom I was pregnant with is 9 now and he has experiences and so does my daughter. After the experience I have had many more. I have also started to remember past events that are similar in nature. I get the feeling that what ever I have dealt with is positive in nature although it scares me to death to think of seeing one again.
