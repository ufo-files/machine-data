Came in like a air plane on fire,with white sparks dripping off of it.

Came in First Dark.It appeared to be a air plane or jet on fire.It was headed like North when it suddenly changed 90 degrees and came towards me.It came within maybe a mile and a half.Leaned sorta side ways and eased down in some trees.While it eased down white sparks dripped off of it.Like hot steal or something.The woods seem lit up in that area through the woods,for maybe 10 seconds.It then came back up and poof a orange amber light came on.When the light went off we could see 6 lights or heat vents open and close along its outer edges.Like a furnace inside.While it was doing this display it moved away from us and then back toward us,it would also move to the right 100s of yards and then back the other way.One of us put a rifle scope on it and my friend said it was triangle shaped. It was in the area the next night same time but in a little different area doing the same thing.Moving around.It stayed in the area till January because some coon hunters witnessed it then.
