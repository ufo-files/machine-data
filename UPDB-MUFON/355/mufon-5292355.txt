An orange light moving in a random pattern

I spotted an orange light moving around the clear night sky. At first I thought it was from the street lights below the balcony I was on appearing in my vision when I looked up, but once I realised it was an actual light I went to get my phone to take pictures. When I returned I couldn't find it for a moment or so but when I did locate it, it had moved in the sky within the view of the moon and a really bright star which I guess was Venus. I took 3 photos in succession then checked my phone to see if I had got a good picture, which I have. Then when I looked back up to take more pictures I couldn't find it anywhere.
