Witness and friend observed a triangle-shaped object that suddenly disappeared.

WE WERE LEAVING KENNEDY GULCH HEADING NORTHWEST ON CO RD 97 TOWARD CONIFER AND WERE MAKING APPROXIMATELY 120 DEGREE TURN TO THE LEFT. MY FRIEND SAW SOMETHING IN THE SKY AND TOLD ME TO LOOK, I TURNED AND LOOKED AT A 60 DEGREE ANGLE UP TO MY LEFT AND SAW THREE LIGHTS IN A TRIANGULAR SHAPE. MY FRIEND THEN TURNED AN LOOKED BACK AT THE CRAFT AND HE SAW A VERY LARGE OBJECT IN THE SKY HOVERING OVER THE TREETOPS ON THE LEFT SIDE OF THE ROAD. IT THEN HOVERED ACROSS THE ROAD AND TURNED SLIGHTLY EXPOSING LARGE WHITE BLINDING LIGHTS ALONG WITH A STRANGE BLUE AND GREEN LIGHT ON THE FRONT OF THE CRAFT. WE WERE THEN EXPECTING TO SEE A LARGE EXPLOSION OR SEE THIS CRAFT ON THE GROUND AS SOON AS WE TURNED THE NEXT RIGHT HAND CORNER. AS WE TURNED THE CORNER THERE WAS ABSOLUTELY NO SIGHT OF THE CRAFT, WE HAVE ABSOLUTELY NO IDEA OR ANY EXPLANATION OF THE WHEREABOUTS OF THIS STRANGE CRAFT.
