{cak} Thick Disc Flying NW to W

Around 11:30am on July the 12th myself and my wife were driving on 77N (around Grant Ave.) headed towards downtown Cleveland when I glanced towards the sky and saw a thick disc like object flying NW to W. It was a white/grey color with the right last quarter of it being bright red. There didn't appear to be any lights emitting from it. I turned to my wife and asked what the object looked like to her. She said possibly a blimp. In my opinion it was way too flat and moving to fast to be a blimp. The object could have been flying over Newburg Heights, Tremont, or Ohio City. It looked very large from the distance I saw it from which was at least 1 mile away. I saw it for a solid 10 seconds before trees on the southbound side of 77 blocked my view.
