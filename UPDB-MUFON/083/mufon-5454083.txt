Unidentified flying object

At the date and time given, an unidentified flying object flew over my home garden and was witnessed and recorded on video by myself and my wife.
The object made no sound and glowed orange, although on the video it appears white. The object appeared larger when viewed live than it appears on the video recording.
The object appeared suddenly, flew around slowly, and then disappeared. The object was not an aeroplane or helicopter, and appeared too large to be a drone.
Duration of the sighting was 1 min 30 seconds, and was captured on video that I submit for your viewing and analysis.
