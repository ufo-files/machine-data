Cloaked object with cloud-like leading edge

While stargazing with my wife, waiting for our dog to do her business, outside our home, I noticed a very faint movement above us, that appeared to be very similarly colored as the clouds behind it or perhaps black with the city lights of Albuquerque reflecting off the surface. The clouds themselves were a mixture of airplane contrails and what appeared to be thin stratus clouds that were barely moving at all, appearing stationary.

The object was traveling from South to North, at the altitude and speed of an airplane leaving the nearby airport. It produced no sound or lights. However, it became apparent after several seconds that no object could actually be seen, only a slight distortion of the air with stars visible where the object should be.

As I looked more intently, I could see that the light was reflecting off of a cloud or mist that surrounded the leading edge of the object, in the shape of an elongated semicircle or soft "V" shape. It was close enough to see the swirling of the mist. As I watched, the mist shifted around the object like churned water would around the prow of a ship.

The object continued north, visible for between 30 and 60 seconds, before being obscured by trees. I tried the entire time to point it out to my wife, but her eyesight isn't the best, the object was very faint and so she could not see it.
