{cak} heard small plane while on my deck looked into beautiful clear sky felt compelled to look behind and saw this huge UFO

On August 31, 2015 between 1 and 2 PM I had taken my dog outside to go to the bathroom. We had gone back onto the deck when I heard the sound of a small plane. I looked up to see the plane but it was not in sight. I felt completely compelled to look backward from the sound of the small plane. When I did there was a huge beautiful shiny UFO. I was absolutely unable to take mhy eyes off of it. I'd say it was close to 100 feet across and almost as deep. There were no windows. It looked solid. It was a beautiful clear day with no clouds and the sun was shining bright. When I was finally able to look away I leaned back and tried to get my husband out to the deck to see. And when I looked back the UFO was gone. I felt content and quite happy for the rest of the day. It was so beautiful and so close to the ground and near the house.
