{nd} Observed light in cloud while walking around the bike path in park at night.

I was walking around City of Potenza park , as I was walking I looked up and observed the light sitting just under a small cloud , kept walking then looked up again and the outside edges shrunk inward and then it disappeared .

At first I thought it was a helicopter , but I didn't hear any noise . The feeling I had of the object was that it seemed at first a common thing , but then it seemed kind of odd and out of place . I lost sight of the object as the outside edges of the light shrunk inward and disappeared . It was a round light with sharp defined edges and kind of a dull shade , the light was not bright and it didn't shine down to the ground , it was just kind of THERE .
