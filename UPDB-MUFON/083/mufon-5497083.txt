{dr}Observed from a distance, as it came towards us we realized it wasnt a manmade flying device. Red orange ball of fire.moving straight and fast

We were in the yard of my girlfriends house. I was letting the dogs out while 3 others were out there for cigaretts. While talking I looked up to see a red-orange object coming towards us. It looked at first to be a plane or helicopter. We soon realized it wasn't. No man made flying vehicle glowed like this. It had no wings,blades or sounds. We did notice a very distant jet like sound after the object was almost out of view. The sky was crystal clear and the neighborhood was very quiet. We waited a minute to hear explosion but only silence followed. Alien craft or meteor? I think a meteor or comet would have a trail??? Something I'll not forget. Two of us have cellphone video which is not that great and was taken after best views were past us.
