{rjl} disk shaped object hovering over South Austin Texas

Walked outside to see this disk shape object hovering just over the tree lines. It would drop below for a few seconds then come back up as if it was riding waves in the ocean. It was clearly a disk-shaped object glowing white/yellow/orange around its rim. it was really far away from me and still a really good size so it had to be massive 3-4 time bigger than a 737 which we see all the time.

This is South Austin in between Manchaca and South First Street.

I sent Spectrum News, Fox7, and KXAN messages the next day and they said they got no other reports of it.
