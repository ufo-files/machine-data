dancing sky objects in appearance to distant stars observed multiple times multiple witnesses around reservation.

Self and several witnesses on numerous occasion would be outside in Fort Defiance Arizona and see what can only be described as being as the stars at night appear, as dots of light. These were not bright or anyway remarkable in appearance at all except that they would move. In direct coralation to one another there were usually only several lights that would in random lines, circles, turns and loops looks as though they were dancing with and around each other. It simply looked as though a few of the stars out the night sky decided to have some fun and more about the sky.
