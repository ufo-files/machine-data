{rjl} My camera picked up these objects that were not visible to the naked eye.

On February 20th at approximately 1410 I was leaving my house to walk my dog. Approaching my house was a squadron (5) Apache Helicopters. I pulled out my phone to snap a picture. The phone was a LG G4 with high end optics. As the choppers flew over my house I set the magnification to maximum as the aircraft were flying at approximately 1500-2000 feet and I wanted some good detail. This process took longer than I thought and I was only able to catch the two choppers that are in this picture. I thought cool, and went about my day. Later that evening I was showing the picture to my wife when I noticed something. In front of the lead helicopter I saw some dots. 6 of them in what I believe is a near to far flight pattern. When I magnified these even further with my phone I was unable to see any contrails or pixilated movement trails. I didn't see them at all in real time so this leads me to believe they were hovering at a very high altitude over the Yakima Training Center which is the direction the helicopters were going. This military complex is a well known hot spot for UFO activity. Any conventional aircraft would certainly be leaving contrails at that altitude as contrails are everywhere in the sky above Yakima. I took 3 more pictures of the same sky in the same weather to see if my camera had dirt or a flaw however there was nothing. I don't know if these are actual UFO's or not but I would love to see this picture washed through a computer to try to clean it up and see what they really are.
