{cak} very bright mostly white with colored lights off AFB.I know it wasnt our military or of our earth.

MY HUSBAND AND I AT THAT TIME WERE DRIVING HOME AFTER A DAY OF WORK ON THE BASE WHEN WE NOTION THIS OBJECT LOW IN THE SKY OFF THE DRIVERS SIDE.
IT CIGAR SHAPED VERY BRIGHT. WE WANTED TO GET A CLOSER LOOK AT IT BUT I DONT REMEMBER DOING THAT. I ONLY REMEBER I WAS BACK AT OUR APARTMENT. IT WAS VERY STRANGE AND I BLOCKED IT OUT OF MY MEMORY FOR YEARS. MY HUSBAND AND I NEVER TALKED ABOUT IT. I HAVE TOLD FAMILY AND MY SECOND HUSBAND ABOUT IT BUT I DONT THINK THEY BELIEVE ME. I WISH I COULD RECALL MORE.
