Observed a bright round, maybe oval white light shoot away and disappear in less than a second below the cloud cover

Just before 9pm on January 18th 2021 I went outside to my back porch to take out some garbage. As I was walking to my back gate something caught my eye in the sky. As I looked up I say a bright white oval shaped light streak away moving NW away from my neighborhood. It all happened in less than a second. I've never seen anything move that fast in my life. It made no sound. After my initial shock I replayed the event over and over in my mind trying to rationalize what I saw. But I can't. It was below the clouds, I was not a shooting start or falling satellite or burning space junk. It was just there, then it wasn't!
