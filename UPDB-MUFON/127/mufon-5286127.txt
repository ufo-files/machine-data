Gray dull metallic sideways teardrop shaped object with what appeared to be small antennae descending off in the distance.

This dull gray metallic looking object with small antennae made forward steady movement while descending approximately 1000-2500 feet away. It looked to us to be 30 or so feet long and had an almost small dirigible like motion. It was silent but very close to Logan Airport and in the flight paths of jets as they were taking off. My daughter thought it was a parachute but i did not see it as such, much more similar to a short helicopter fuselage with no rotors or sounds. It was fascinating and perplexing to watch and I baffled at what it could be so near a busy international airport.
