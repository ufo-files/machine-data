{nd} Looked up saw a bright light moving fast East to West straight line no sound at all. above treetops below clouds as turning to West to watch it was gone

I was out getting the cat in it was almost dark due to some dark clouds in the sky. My son came out to help because my cat is reluctant. As we are talking I looked up because I had heard about the Super Moon. Just as I did I saw a light, not real high in the sky. East Texas Pine Trees are up a ways, and I saw it coming from the East and traveling in a straight line. My mind was trying to reason what I was seeing and I was telling my son as I was watching it to look up, look up, look up, he was under the tree. As I backed up he came toward me and turned around and said oh my gosh there it is and then it was gone. Nothing there. There was not a sound. We're out in the country and it's quiet out there, if there was sound we would have heard it. It didn't seem that far up to me. My son said he saw two ends and 2 lights but wasn't sure if the one light was a reflection.
