Driving home under clear skies. Object appeared hovering and then slowly moving in straight path low in the sky to my right and then passed over me.

As I was driving home on Rt 140 N at 7:49 pm, I noticed an aircraft that appeared to be hovering over a field to my right (east). As I was near a small airfield, I assumed it was helicopter. Then the craft began moving slowly (less than 10mph) towards the road (Rt. 140). As it passed overhead, I slowed my car to a stop and looked up to see a solid, triangular craft with steady red lights on all 3 corners. The craft was dark gray/black , especially on all 3 corners. Then, as the craft silently passed overhead, the center of the craft showed small red lights in the middle of the triangle shape; the lights lit sequentially to form a circle. I then realized I was stopped in the dark in the middle of the road and saw headlights approaching from behind me. I pulled my car over to the shoulder and waited approximately 15 seconds for the car to pass. The headlights from the approaching vehicle made it difficult to see the sky, and i looked away from the sky for about 3 seconds as the car passed. I then looked to the sky where the craft had been and could find no trace of it in any direction.
