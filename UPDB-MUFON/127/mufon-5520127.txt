A small orb passed over my neighborhood

I saw a small orb that looks like a faded light blue star passing over my neighborhood at 9:51pm. It went NW. Its the second time it happens, but this time was just one, and it moved on a straight line. On New Years eve me and my friends witnessed around 12 of them, and since them its hard to not look to the sky at night. It is so faded its hard to see if youre not paying attention. Its hard to believe is some kind of balloon because the wind was in a different direction.
