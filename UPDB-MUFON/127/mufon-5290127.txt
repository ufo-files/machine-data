Pulsating orb shot through the sky

We were looking at the Orion constellation, and to the east was what looked like the planet Mars. It looked weird, like it was moving. Then we noticed it was pulsating and moving in a small circle. After a moment it shot off to the south east, and at least 4 silent aircraft came from the north headed south east less than 30 seconds later.
