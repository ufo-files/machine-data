Object in sky

My husband and I were sitting outside At our home starting around 9:30 at night watching for shooting stars and satellites. Around 10 pm I looked up toward the east And saw what I originally thought was a satellite but then it completely disappeared. About 15 seconds later a bright light came from that same area. It then started moving in a straight like pattern faster than any satellite I’ve ever seen. It would vanish completely the. Blink a very bright light. It wasn’t in a pattern thought. First time was around 20 seconds. Second was 26 then again at 39 seconds. It was extremely high to have been a plane or other aircraft. It would blink extremely bright almost like a picture was being taken then it just disappeared. We never seen it again.
