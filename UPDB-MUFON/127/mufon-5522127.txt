Floated around, no sound

It was about a quarter after 3, and i was out in the middle of nowhere with my grandmother in Idaho. I was just waking up, and she was outside looking at something. I asked her what she was looking at, and she motioned for me to look at the sky. It was fairly cool outside, so i decided that i would hang out of the window. What i saw amazed me. I saw a large disc-like object hovering in the night sky, too bright to be a star. All of a sudden, it quickly accelerated to unknown speeds, then stopped, and sort of started to do loops in the sky, then as i started to get out my digital camera, it flashed and disappeared.
