{cak} Several orbs of light emitting around Venus in the morning sky.

Observing morning sky. Venus was visible and quite brilliant. Over a period of several minutes bright orbs seemed to emit around Venus. Some sent straight out in several directions. A few split into several directions. And one or two seemed to circle Venus prior to shooting off into space. None appeared to move toward Earth.
