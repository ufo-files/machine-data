Confirmation of San Manuel, AZ report

I'm up in the northwest valley of Phoenix so I never actually saw the UFO that was reported. But about 10-15 minutes before the reported event I watched 3 fighters about 30 seconds apart in the sky over Luke Air Force Base. They were all headed south in the Casa Grande / Tuscon direction. It didn't appear to be a drill, all 3 had their afterburners firing (you could distinctly hear all 3 as they took off) until I lost sight of them. So they appeared to be headed somewhere in a hurry. Given the direction they were headed and the number of jets described in the report I'm almost positive these are the 3 fighter planes that were seen chasing down the object in San Manuel.
