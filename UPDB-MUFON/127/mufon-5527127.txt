orange lights forming triangle, then further orange lights

I was smoking in the garden, at home. At first, three orange lights forming a triangle were seen in the sky, they moved from West to South. They seemed to be orange-coloured bright stars with very large distances between them. While moving, the distances between them was continuously decreasing. At South horizont, they faded and disappared, one after the other. Then, a single orange light moved in the same route, in the same way. After it also disappeared, two orange lights went again and did the same. So, six lights were observed, 3+1+2. No noise was heared from the objects. I went back in the house and wrote dawn everything.
