Triangular UFO on Mt. Scio Road & Ridge Road/Fisheries college

Was a teenager in the car with my grandfather who had just picked me up from a friends house sometime between 9-10 pm. We were driving back to our home in the east end via Mt Scio Road when I looked out and saw a bunch of white lights in the sky. As we came up to turn onto Ridge Road it was turning in the air and proceeded to fly over the fisheries college on Ridge Road. I rolled down the window and heard no sound. It was triangular in shape and also had a red light on the bottom in the middle.I find it hard to approximate size or how far away it was but it didn't look to be too far above the building. It proceeded to fly over the road and begin to land in a grassy area (someone's yard i think?) across the street. There was at least one car ahead of us and I've always wondered if they witnessed it too. I picked 90 degrees above the horizon but I've never done anything with the horizon before so I'm unsure but I do know it came right over the building beside us and over the road.
