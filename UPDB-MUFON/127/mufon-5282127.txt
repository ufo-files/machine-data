Starlike object wavering through sky then abruptly changing direction before staying stationary and dissolving.

Was laying in my hammock when I saw what I thought was a satellite during the day, but it was extra bright. I then noticed it's path wasn't quite straight. After about a minute it immediately started in the opposite direction for about 10 seconds or so before staying stationary for a handful of minutes. It then just kind of dissolved and nothing else was noticeable in the sky.

I used to be an aircraft mechanic in the air force and this may have been a drone or something reflecting ridiculous amounts of light maybe, but it was still really cool and I like to play with the thought I saw something cool other than a drone.
