{rjl} Weird airplane shape

I was out on the back porch just playing on my iPhone. I noticed it around 12:00 day. I thought it was an airplane. Then I noticed the wings were at a very steep angle up. I am a student pilot so I can tell an airplane, helicopter, or glider when I see one. This was not any of those. It had a chrome-like appearance. It moved VERY slow with little to no noise at most maybe the quietest jet I ever heard. There were no engines on the wing. I felt odd. Like when you know something is wrong, but you don't know what. I lost sight of it when I went in to try to think of what it was. All I could think of is non-human. It looked like a Flying V.
