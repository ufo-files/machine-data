{nd} Walked outside looked east, saw a round domed object speeding through the sky

At 3:30 on in February 9th 2016, I stepped outside to smoke a cigarette and my 2yr old said she saw a "plane," note that she calls everything planes if it flies high in the sky, I proceed to look east and that's when I saw the object.
 It was a Large Silver Circular object that had what looked like a dome with 2 large lights or windows. The object flew from left to right, away from me. It made no sound.
 When I first saw it I knew 100% it was not a plane or a helicopter because we have heavy air traffic directly above our house. My first though was "oh my god that was a ufo I haveto report it!" The object continues its E to SE flight path until I could not longer see it because the the trees.
