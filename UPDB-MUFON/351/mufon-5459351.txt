{tjd} In yard. Glanced up saw craft slowly moving towards me. It stopped above me.

I was outside in patio area. It was dusk. I caught movement in my peripheral and looked up to see a craft moving above treetops between houses. NO SOUND. I stood and watched . It stopped behind my house, hovered. Then it apparently saw me and moved directly above me. I froze for a moment. I wasn't afraid. I then ran across the street where my Mother and Sister lived. Beat on the Door. My Sister came out quickly, I grabbed my Mom out of bed and virtually walked her across the street. They looked at it with me. Someone flicked a lighter, the craft flicked back. This went on for a few moments . It had yellow, white, reddish, and bluish lights. I then got nervous and said stop, stop,. All I could think is we dont know if they are using a code and we are unwittingly agreeing to something. There was NO noise I knew it wasn't a normal occurrence . I sent my Family home watched them go in their Door. I ran to the neighbors house. Knocked. She came out as well as two of her small Children. The craft is still hovering. I then saw it start to move across the street towards us. Instinctually I put my arm out to push the children back in the house. When I did that, it stopped, turned abruptly but slowly around. It moved slowly for about 200 ft then it was gone. Never a Sound !
