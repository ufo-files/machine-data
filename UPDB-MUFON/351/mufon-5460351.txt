{jpg} Strange lights, witnessed on more than one occasion

Unexplainable, strange lights in the south Western sky of Seattle, on more than one occasion. The lights are unlike any known aircraft I've seen in the night sky, and living in Seattle long enough, I know what a plane looks like going to or coming from the airport, especially at night. The objects seem to move and stay in sunch with each other. They will appear and disappear in the same spot, sometimes in a spot a fraction from where they were previously. Their flight pattern sometimes looks like geometric shapes, triangle, rectangle, etc but always in pattern. Looking at these lights through binoculars, they don't exhibit behavior of any known aircraft in the area, and on two occasions, most recent being 6/3/19, I witnessed what appeared to be a circular craft with bright white lights around the outside of the craft and red lights underneath. Also on 6/3/19 I saw a bright flash of light at the tree line these objects frequent and as I watched, it slowly rose above the tree line, and what appeared to be 3 bright white orbs, looking like they were moving in a circular motion, rotating left to right from my point of view, slowly rose in an angular motion and then disappeared. I honestly can't put into text the behavior exhibited by this craft, but it was like NOTHING I've ever seen. These craft have appeared multiple times, but never 2 nights in a row, or 3, etc.. there is always a few days or even weeks between times they appear. I could just be witnessing something natural, but these objects don't seem to be natural or man-made
