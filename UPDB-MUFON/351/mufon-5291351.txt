Starlike line of lights moving steadily

While camping at Jemex Mountain, just north of San Antonio Hot Springs in New Mexico on October 24th. We saw an interesting sight in the sky. Earlier in the night we saw a lot of satellites, but all the sudden we saw a line of lights, that moved so smoothly and steadily across the sky to a direction that we would not expect. All 5 of us witnessed the event and it puzzled all of us. We are avid campers, but have never come across anything like it.
