Ufo drone above manchester airport

Best watching this with full brightness on! Was out smoking on front door step. When i looked up i saw 2 lights coming from north to south, these lights were wierd they were flying next to each other in tandom they were flying behind the cloud so it wasnt clear all i could see was their lights. They had what looked like a triangle shaped light coming out of them sort of like a search light scanning left and right. Thought it looked wierd and was sure planes dont fly side by side like that plus there was no engine noise so i ran inside to grab my phone to film them. When i got back outside the 2 lights were gone but this small drone flew over my head from west to east at fairly low altitude id say about 100ft! No noise just a ball of light moving slowly, not sure if the 2 are connected but i see quite a few ufos near the airport and im guessing the airport knows they are there too
