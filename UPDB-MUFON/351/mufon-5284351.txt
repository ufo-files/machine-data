Light beams came through bedroom windows under a deck for prior month, craft seen by all of us, by me on 11/

All events occurred between July 1, 2021 and November 5, 2021. On 2 separate occasions later at night, my husband and his aunt saw a round craft high in the sky that hovered then took off upward until no longer visible. After that, for about a month almost every night, a bright beam of light came under the deck above our bedroom window and lit up the room. Then it came around the other side of the house and it up that window. We checked everywhere for intense sources of white light. There were none. The activity died down, but then Wednesday and Thursday of this week, a disk shaped object was stationary in the sky which was clear and cloudless. It was next to a star, but I did not recognize that configuration. All of a sudden, it started to move over me, then shot up. The next morning, I looked again. This time, it was in a completely different location and appeared quickly, moved above me again, and shot up and out of sight.
