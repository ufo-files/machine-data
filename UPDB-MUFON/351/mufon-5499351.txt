{nd} Likely occurred over Columbia River and or north into Washington state

I was outside observing morning stars; looking north I observed erratic star. Initially thought it might be a satellite but it move in a very brief straight line then abruptly shifted back then in a blink of an eye had moved a good distance to the side. Appeared to split into two objects; lost sighting as it jumped to lower elevation behind tree line obstructing view.
