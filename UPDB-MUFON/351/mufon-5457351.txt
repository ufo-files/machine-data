{tjd} Large star that became surround by 4 stars that blinked in pattern

I saw a star that was too large just above the horizon. I was debating if it was the right time of year for Venus to be visible just before dawn. Watched star for several minutes. Suddenly 4 smaller star-like lights appeared in a square around large one, all equidistant from it. After a second or two, they blink out, then blinked back on in a diamond formation around it. Blink off, square, on, back to a diamond. This happened 4or 5 times, then ALL lights including the center one vanished. I have tried to figure out what it might be. My first thought was maybe it was a new tower, but I viewed it from my yard, and it has never returned, and there is no new tower. The lights were bluish white, and looked like stars. I would have thought nothing of them if they had not blinked in pattern and then vanished all at once.
