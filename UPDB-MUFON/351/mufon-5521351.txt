Circular object above treeline hovering then stopped car to take a look, object then started moving away from our location

Me and a friend were driving from my house to another friends house at approx. 11 p.m. We started to come to a main road in which we saw a large circular shaped object above the treeline. As we started to stop the vehicle object then took off one direction and then changed direction at a very fast pace. When it changed direction it stayed at an angle and then slowed down speed and stayed moving above treeline. We then arrived at a friends house where another friend of ours came along with us to see if we could get another sighting. The last sighting we saw approx. an hour to two hours later was three objects in the sky with a formation that looked like Orions Belt and then they split off in opposite directions.
