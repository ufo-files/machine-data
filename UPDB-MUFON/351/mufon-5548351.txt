Massive Barbell UFO

I was outside looking in the same area of sky that I filmed my Easter UFO sighting and noticed a commercial jet leaving a contrail. When the plane passed directly overhead I noticed a large barbell-shaped object stationary in the sky. I noticed the object become smaller and then back to its original size. Then, it slid or glided- from a stationary postion about 1/3 the sky's distance- like on water- and back to its original position. This object was much higher than the haze at high altitude and much higher than air traffic. It was enormous! I could see the object's shading and it appeared to be white. I ran inside after a few minutes, grabbed my video camera and asked my girlfriend to come outside. She saw the object but could not focus on it. I filmed it but the UFO was much too high for my video camera to distinguish and it looks like a white blurry dot in the sky. It may still be there because the thin high clouds covered the object. I am going to keep an eye out.
