Four lights deployed in square

I went from my room to balcony. A few seconds after I spotted four ligths which were deployed in square. Every light was in one corner of the square. All thing was in size of aspirin or a penny. Then after a few seconds after I spotted it two lights which were placed in diagonal corners to each other dissapeared. Again after about 2 seconds more two another diagonal lights of square dissapeared. Square had no surface. There were only star-like lights. It was night. So I don't know either there were 4 lights (objects) or one object with unvisible surface and with 4 lights on it. All lights didn't dissapear in the same moment.

There were about 15 sightings in this area. I decided to report all of them. Some are more interesting to me and some less. The most amazing was on 1.6.2020 (it is also on the map with pictures). Our experiences are 100% real. I hope they will help you at research.
