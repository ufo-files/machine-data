{cak} Silently traveling out of the East, glowing and pulsing. Traveling WNW disappearing out of sight after about a minute.

A friend alerted me to a strange-looking glowing object moving from our left as we faced south at about 12:30 a.m. On October 5th, 2013. It moved silently and pulsed a reddish-orange-amber light. I thought it may have been a plane, but it had no blinking lights...it just glowed. My second thought was that it might be a meteor. My friend and I both asked each other repeatedly what the f it might be. We became a little excited and watched it until it disappeared out of sight. It was not visible from the rear, only the side or the bottom, whatever part we were seeing. Not a sound. (2
