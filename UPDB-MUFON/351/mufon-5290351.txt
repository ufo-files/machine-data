Fiery orange/reddish circular craft w/ metal like lid w/ black markings moving south. Greenish orbe appears, darts around, disappears & then returns.

Went outside to watch New Year’s fireworks, recognized clear night and brightness of moon. Immediately thereafter in the Northwest part of sky witness a fiery orange circular craft with a metal-like roof or top moving at a consistent speed south. Initial feeling of shock and awe came over me as I never seen anything quite like this in my life. No recollection of the craft making any type of sound. As it moved south, a greenish orbe appeared, darted around nearby, disappeared and then reappeared again. Lost sight of the fiery object as it continued its path south. Absolutely mind blowing experiencing!
