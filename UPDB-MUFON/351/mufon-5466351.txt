white oval shape, 3 black dots,no wings,no tail,silent,no lights

my wife and I saw a ufo Aug 12 at 8:50pm in the Woodland Caribou Provincial Park from inside our tent,our rain fly was off. It wasn't dark out yet and we didn't see any lights on it. The UFO was white,silent, and moved from west to east. My wife alerted me to it and she saw it for 3 seconds and I saw it for 2 seconds before it disappeared behind some trees ,it did not come out on the other side of them. It had 3 black windows on it or black dots, could be blimp looking but a little longer like maybe a 747 object with no wings,no tail,not as long and wider.....we didn't have time to get out our camera.We didn't see any other campers for 9 days before this happened. there were none for the next 2 days as we paddled south, there are also no cabins,stores,roads for at least 50 miles which would be back at Red Lake.After the UFO encounter I heard "the hum" for a few hours.I didn't know what it was until I heard it on youtube when we got home. I didn't sleep much that night. our story sounds similar to the Brockville, ON, encounter that happened on Aug 10TH 2018
