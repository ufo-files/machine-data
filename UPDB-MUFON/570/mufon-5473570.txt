{rjl} Lights in the sky at Grand Parkway(Hwy 99) @ I10

I was driving home on 9/18/2017, it was 8:20PM and I was on Grand Parkway heading north towards I10 @ Highland Knolls. I noticed 3 lights in the sky. There is no airport in the area. The flickered in and out, then one disappeared as another flicked into view. They appeared to be in unison. I originally thought it might have been flairs until I saw the last one move rapidly to the east at a 90 degree angle until it was out of sight beyond the tree line. I recorded the event with my cell phone.
