I was standing outside our home on the front porch smoking - I saw a pinky rose orange colored orb fly fast towards our house from west - I immediately called my husband to come quickly to see it - it was flying towards a north east straightline direction over the roof - I ran to get our camera (on our cell phone) and quickly returned back outside our house - I pointed the phone to the object and instantly the object turned its light of - I tried to take picture - but I-phone would not work. We watched it as it disappeared. Once the orb had disappeared we came back inside our house and after a few minutes our Iphone worked normally again. It was unusual because of the color and speed - we had seen something similar about a year ago when the same type of flying colored orb had hovered directly above our car - (that sighting caused us to pull off the road get out of our vehicle and we observed it shoot upwards but we were just amazed at the time, no bad feelings at all. Today however, this evening I felt a little scared after this sighting, even though we came back into our house, I felt a little unsettled. I kept thinking it is up there somewhere and can see us.  My husband said he feels curious and perplexed - questioning - trying to make sense of it. I think I am more unsettled from this event today, because my husband had a very bad nightmare last night and both these things together has spooked me a little.
