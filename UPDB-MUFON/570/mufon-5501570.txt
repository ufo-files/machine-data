{dr}Sitting outside in dark and object was observed I have never seen before.

I was sitting outside in the dark nearing midnight in late summer and I witnessed an object that was round in its shape like a sphere and appeared as it were on fire in color. This object gave no sound or smell nor had in conventional aircraft characteristics. It moved at a steady pace and never wavered in direction or altitude. This object was absolutely close enough to have heard any engine sounds or even rotor sounds including those rigged to run quietly. There was no way to detect it was even there unless you happen to see it. I have inquired about objects before to local authorities and was dismissed as nothing to report. This is not the first and as I have seen some pretty strange sightings that defies aircraft capabilities on several occasions I hope someone can figure out what these objects are.
