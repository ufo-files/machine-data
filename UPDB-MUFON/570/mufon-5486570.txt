{cak} Saw multiple, more than 10 pulsating lights traveling from north to south. They pulsed from white to reddish orange. Some changed direction and some ascended..

I went out to walk the dog before bed at about 10PM. I noticed lights moving toward me coming from the north moving south. They were high but fairly bright. They were moving at a fairly high rate of speed and seemed to be too fast for commercial aircraft. I opened the door and called for my wife Kris to come and see what I was seeing. We continued to watch as the passed over for about 20 minutes. At any one time we were watching 2 to 3 or more at times. Some just continued to move from North to south and some changed directions slightly at times. All seemed to be ascending as the flew away from us. They all pulsated from white to a reddish orange color. We did not see the actual objects or craft as they were high in the sky and it was dark out. They were certainly not commercial aircraft, starts, comets or any other known objects. We were both convinced these were UFO's and a lot of them.
