I was asked to report this through your website after speaking to Roger via Facebook Mufon Australia page.

Please note in the above answers I wrote missing time, as of the options given,this best suits the situation.

I live next to the Hawkesbury River in New South Wales, Australia. I like to hike and often make my way off the track to have a look for wildlife or Aboriginal carvings. Recently I have been leaving sound recorders out and picking them up a week or so later when i am in the area again. The sound recorder in question was placed out on the afternoon 22 August 2021 and collected on 7 September 2021. The device (Sony Icd- ax412f) was put approx 10 meters on the east side of the Midway ridge path, where it turns north (link below, via openstreetmaps as google maps does not have this track on its system). Coordinates roughly -33.535lat 151.257long. I plan to go there again over the next few days to place another recorder, scout the area, collect the exact coordinates and take photos

https://www.openstreetmap.org/?fbclid=IwAR1URChOp5Z9nUnlt5fwzHKkTfpcr7OZ6TGUISHQGksbBCY4_Fq0ojrsx0I#map=17/-33.53498/151.25622&layers=Y

The recorder was placed on the floor, propped up next to a tree with a small plastic 'sandwich' bag over the top to protect it from the rain. On collecting the device, i noticed that it was approx 30cm from where i had left it but assumed this was due to an animal or weather moving it, as there had been heavy rain during the time it was out. I didn't think anything of it, was in a bit of a rush, so didn't take any photographs, or spend time in the area. The area itself is a small track that leads from Little Wobby (to the South) to an area with a small creek called Rocky Ponds (to the North). From Little Wobby, where i live, it takes a good 2 hours at a decent pace to get to the area, this includes a tough walk up a ridge- this would be the closest residential area. To get to this area from the North (Woy Woy), I would guess it would take a good 4 hours on foot, some of the tracks to the North are accessible for bicycles but not all of it. There is a camping area approx 1 hour to the North, however Greater Sydney has been in Covid lockdown for 9 weeks and camping in this area is forbidden. The terrain itself is rocky bush land and scrub, i am happy to send you this pictures when i return to the area soon.
On returning home, I connected my laptop to the recorder and saw that there was almost 28 hours of audio collected. i took the first file and put it through a program called Wavepad which i downloaded for free online, this allows me to see the spikes of noise so i can move to the areas of interest. The file 200101-001 contained a few bird calls and the sound of rain throughout, this leads me to believe that, due to the weather, this was in the evening of the 23rd or morning of the 24th. The recorder was set to VOR which means the recorder kicks in when it hears something then stops when the noise finishes.
I started to look at the second file, and was scrolling through until I saw an anomaly at about the 2hr 30 mark, it seemed that there were deep sounds, listened to them and thought it was weird but nothing crazy, there was a further 10mins after this which i originally thought may be a mouse or something similar moving around the device, however having listened to it more, i think this is some kind of static. After this, comes a faint high pitched noise, that gets gradually louder, this noise is metallic in nature and definitely not a sound that you would hear in the bush. The sound is not constant in tone but almost comes in and out, it is punctuated with static as well. At this point, i made the only change to the file and that was to rename it start23020, as in - the strange stuff starts at 2'30.20s mark.
Looking at the following files - 200102_02 and 200103_01 these are both 6 hours long and contain the same noise constantly. 
The final file 200103-02 is 2hr 47. This also contains the noise, however the last portion (from 2hr 08m 37s) there is distinct change in audio that you can hear. I can only assume that the recorder went dead after this time as the batteries would have run out (the device had no power when i bought it home)
I checked that the device was working and it tested it, there is no problem and it has not malfunctioned.
