{cak} 3 silver bullet shaped objects in Hartford, CT

Was in car traveling south on Blue Hills Avenue and noticed 3 silver shaped objects in the sky in the east of me. I could not see their exact shape but could see reflective spot from the sun on their srface, and they resembled spheres turned on their sides like a bullet.There were 2 hovering a little ways apart from each other and at times appeared like they were moving, but not sure if that is the case because our car was also in motion. There was also 1 flying upward not to far away going in the opposite direction on an angle. Watched for about a mile until they were no longer in view. It was beatiful clear blue sky evening.
