Wobbling lights in sky

At approximately 11:45 pm on 7/11/2020 I let my dogs out and observed what I believed was a bright star to the south, south west. Upon watching it further it dimmed out completely and then turned slightly orange and pulsed back up to a very bright white. The objected wobbled a bit, varying in a small up and down motion. I also then found a similar light off to the east of it which was not as bright, but had the same wobble of movement up and down in the sky. I then saw to the west three small orange lights that were also wobbling and darting a bit. 

My husband was also a witness. Took a poor quality photo of the first two lights that were viewed and some poor video.
