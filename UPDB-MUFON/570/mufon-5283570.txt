Vessel the length of a football field w/rows of undulating lights that were bioluminescent in appearance glided above Mount Shasta

My friend and I along with probably 5 other people witnessed a very large row of lights appear from the West of the Mount Shasta summit. It moved towards the East passing directly in front of the mountain. It was very long, at least the size of a football field or longer. The lights seemed auric in nature with a bioluminescence of white and lighter hues of green and violet that undulated in a wave like motion as if it was propelling the ship. Directly above the lights we could see what looked like the hull of a ship, it was transparent or holographic in nature and at times when it caught the reflection of the moon I could make out the outline of the front of the ship. It moved gracefully as if it was floating or gliding along the ocean. It was not fast moving but more like watching a cruise ship go by in speed. As it made its way to the East of the mountain peak it slowly disintegrated from the front of the ship as if it was moving into a portal or a dimension and we were watching it slip behind a veil of holographic light. Before we saw the vessel my friend and I both remember the energy of the area shifting. I remember the air getting warmer and was more still and I know this seems crazy but I felt like they wanted us to know it was okay. There was a real sense of peace that overcame both of us. I also felt like I was connected to them - like a part of the biologically in some weird way. Maybe future versions of us type thing, hard to describe but I know what I felt.
