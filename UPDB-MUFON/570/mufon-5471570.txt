{rjl} Not abducted; Invited and 'willing passenger'

I was recruited by an organization associated with the U.S. government as well as other global governments while I was in college (1967-1971). One of the missions of this organization is to maintain an ongoing relationship with non-terrestrial species/organizations. Another mission is to detect, observe, and maintain unofficial communications with competing global organizations. For the next twenty-five years (until 1996), I was part of compartmentalized teams whose objectives were to support those missions already mentioned. For the next 5 years or so, I remained in an auxiliary status for call-up as needed.

I really wish that I could say I remember everything. However, due to some mandatory memory erasures, I only recall a fraction of the details. But, I still remember summaries and highlights of many more events. 

Now, for one of the more bizarre items is that part of me is second-born in this 'verse' ('uni' or 'multi' as you wish). I realize that one sentence can make you write off this report. However, if if does not, feel free to get in touch with me. 

The initial part of this report partially recounts my introduction to the organization I joined.
