{cak} 8 white orbs.

In [location removed] My Dad, my son and I were driving when my Dad noticed at South East to the Right of the Moon in broad daylight at 4:00pm - 4:30pm 8 star like orbs in a group were floating. Some looked to be in a triangle formation. Some were close as like touching. Some were flashing or flickering off the sun almost like communicating. They were to the right of the Moon in the day when the sun is setting, as like wanting to be seen by all. We tried to take pics with our cell phone but the orbs were to small to pick up on the camera. We pulled over got out of the truck and some people walking by also saw the 8 orbs in the sky. This was rare to see a small fleet of orbs.
