{rjl} Thought they were stars, but they moved up and down and sideways.

I was going to bed around 12:30 am, and my bed is near a window. Looked out and saw what I thought were two stars. But then they started moving. Once in a while, one would emit a red and green light. The other one was visible and then not. On and off like that. Looked out the other bedroom window and saw a lot more. Do not have camera or phone. I would have thought they were meteors, except they went up, down, and to the side. They did not appear to be moving together in any type of coordination. Once in a while, I'd see a red and green flash from one of them, and then it was gone, and back to looking like a star, albeit moving. I was in awe. Tried to wake my daughter up to see them, but she wouldn't wake up. They were still there when I finally gave up and went to sleep, approximately 45 minutes later.
