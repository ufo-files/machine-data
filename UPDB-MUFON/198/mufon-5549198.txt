Large white sphere over Texas

My husband and I were driving north on Dickerson and he noticed a flock of white birds that you can see one minute and dissapear the next. I looked to see the birds and didn't see them but, I saw a large white sphere in the sky that did not appear to be moving. It was not metallic or glowing, just large and white. I pointed it out and we had no idea what it was. I then saw the birds fly by and I could tell by the size of the birds that this object seemed very large. It was too low in the sky to be a planet and too big to be a ballon. I saw several planes fly over it and it seemed to be about the same size as a plane. I was able to see the object in the sky as we drove north on I-35 from Carrollton to Lake Dallas. I finally lost sight of it in Lake Dallas. It never appeared to move. Oddly, this object appeared 1 hour after sonic booms were heard from F-16 test flights of Lockhead Martin planes. About 3 hours later, we drove back to Carrollton and the object was gone.
