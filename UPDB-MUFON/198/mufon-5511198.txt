{cak} I was driving south. To the right and ahead of me a group of five or so, seemingly stationary. To the left a close cluster of seven to ten. As there left the area seven or so single ones passed overhead one at a time following the others .

I was driving on Beck Road heading south at about 9:45 pm on May 4, 2013. At Beck and Joy is when I first saw them. There were four or five to the right of Beck and ahead of me. At first I thought they were planes in holding pattern for Metro airport but there were so many of them and not plane like. They were "arcs",, kind of a  half round shape (the lights were anyway). To the left and ahead of me on Beck were a cluster of five? Six ? Seven? Of them . They were "Blue Angels" close but in no particular formation that I could see,,, but far too close to be planes waiting to land at the airport.  They were also not really moving or flying around like planes would have been. I drove slowly watching them and then pulled off the road close to Hanford. By that time the group and the cluster both had moved on. There were a couple of small "groups",, two or three together,, and then a line of maybe eight single flyers went directly overhead, with a good amount of space between them. (I was underneath their flight path). I took cell phone pictures of the last six. (There are two together in the first picture).  I could see a plane fairly close to one of the smaller groups at one point. Someone was definitely up there checking them out,,,, there were at least 4 other cars that had pulled over around me to see what they were and/or what they were going to do next if anything.
