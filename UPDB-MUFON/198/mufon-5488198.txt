{cak} silver round-oval object with ring gunmetal underside low altitude directional change then vanished while in clear view

Conditions were calm, clear, dry and sunny with few high altitude clouds. I was returning to work from lunch ~noon and heading east-bound on Route 46 in Parsippany, NJ. A silver object roughly to my left (~10 o'clock position/northeast) caught my attention as it was low in the sky. At first I thought it was a plane but quickly noticed it had no wings or protrusions. It moved slowly but 'purposefully' in a southwesterly direction (toward me); now thinking it was a blimp by its pattern of movement. At this point (~15sec) I noticed a ring-like structure about 1/2way down the circumference that appeared to be of darker color (gunmetal gray/lead) beneath. It reminded me of a classic 1950's portrayal of a flying saucer. I thought to myself 'this must be some kind of hoax' because it was so...perfect? The object then made a 45 degree/double-back and up turn (up and to the northeast) and vanished. It disappeared as if I unknowingly blinked my eyes...gone! No poof of smoke, haze or fairy dust for that matter...just gone. I looked around to other drivers and realized no one was even looking up. Most people were looking down like to a cell phone/device or gazing mindlessly out the windshield at the nothingness and traffic ahead. I checked online postings but found no similar events.
