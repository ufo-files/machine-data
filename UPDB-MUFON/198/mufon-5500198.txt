{cak} Massive orb flew through clouds at amazing speeds above Laramie, WY.

Me and my brother were driving home to Laramie, Wyoming after dropping my friend off at the Denver airport. When we were approaching Laramie on the outskirts of town a large orb shape about the same size as the moon appears in the sky shot through the clouds and disappeared over the horizon. This all happened in about 2-3 seconds, almost instantly, the object had to be moving at an insane speed. The object was round, and a bright white with a very light bluish tail. I just wanted to make this post incase anyone else had seen the same thing, it was unlike anything I've ever seen before.
