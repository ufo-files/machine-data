I was using digital Canon T4. I took numerous pix and kept 5 of them (see attached).On 03/13/2014, I started looking trough all other pix including 5 pix from 09/18/2013. I am including all 5 photographs from that day (all pix were taken within 20-30 min). I have enlarged the photo and object is very bright silver/white, triangle/diamond shape. Planes did not fly at that time due to severe weather. After doing more research object is right at O'Hare Int'l Airport area (right at the radar location). I have lived in this area for over 16 years and can point out the exact location based on this picture.
