stationary pulsating orb, pursued by helicopter and dimmed

My girlfriend and I came home from dinner after discussing my encounter earlier this week. When we looked up in the sky we noticed a very bright stationary orb. sky maps did not reveal any star or planet. We watched it for a few minutes and noticed a helicopter approaching the direction of the orb. It dimmed and the helicopter changed course. it illuminated again and the helicopter diverted course back to the object. this happen several times before the helicopter stopped circling and pursuing and left heading southwest. The object grew very bright and slowly moved away until it was not visible through the top of the tree line.
