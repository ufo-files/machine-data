Flashing red and white lights, shaped like a plane/boomerang

I was outside smoking a cigarette and I saw something that appeared to be a plane of some sort that was flashing red and white. It was very close to the ground and looked like it was literally right above State Line road (I live next to College & State Line Road). I was scared and started shaking. I almost went inside to tell my mom and dad, but I kept watching it for some reason. It kept getting closer with a somewhat fast speed for about 30 seconds and then the flashing stopped and it disappeared, I could no longer see it.
