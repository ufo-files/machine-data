I saw an unexplained creature.

I lived in the middle of no where at the time about 7 miles from an airforce base, out in the middle of the woods on 20 very wooded acres. I was standing outside talking on the phone about 10pm and my dog was asleep at my feet. I had a long driveway at the time and I saw a blur of light come speeding down the driveway. It stopped in front of me and we locked eyes and just stood there for a minute. My dog jumped up and started barking at it and it started very quickly shaking its head back and forth and it like out this ear piercing high pitched sound that made me drop my phone and cover my ears. It took off in a blur of light just like it came up. Just before that my father said he felt uncomfortable like something was watching him and he came inside the house. About 11pm my brother came home and said he saw a weird green light in the sky moving erratically and it took off and vanished. 

In the last we had airforce show up and ask to search the land in the middle of the night because "they had reports of a downed beacon".

Often saw weird lights out there in the woods and in the sky. 

I just now feel okay talking about it without feeling completely insane.
