Object appeared under chem-trails, traveling extremely fast,, turned at a 90 degree angle, traveled a few seconds and vanished.

I was on KY Hwy 52 traveling from Lancaster to Richmond. I noticed the object just before the Hwy 1295 turn to the left. I grabbed my cell phone and called my husband to describe the event to him. The object was under the criss cross pattern of the "com/chem" trails traveling from west to east before making a high speed right angle turn to the north and disappearing. There was an aircraft flying at a high altitude emitting no trail that I noticed shortly after the cigar shaped object disappeared. The object was silver in color perhaps due to the sun reflection. I wonder if anyone else observed this event? I did not feel a threat from the object probably because it happened so fast.
