{dr}I was a kid and in the backseat and my parents in front seat, and suddenly a big airplane type thing was hovering over our car and my Dad told me not to look at it, I looked up and seen several flashing bright colors, this happened on July 4.

I was a kid and I was sitting in backseat of car and my parents were in the front seat, suddenly a airplane or something hovered over our car while my Dad was driving, he told me not to look at it, but I did. This airplane like object was very close to the roof of our car and I looked at it and saw several bright flashing lights and it was kinda scary because I knew that airplanes never flew that low. The object hovered over our moving car for several long seconds where I was able to get a really good look at it. Then next thing I knew we arrived at our destination which was Arlin Field to join a group of people to watch the fireworks because it was the 4th of July. The entire time I thought this airplane object would return so the other people would also see it, but it never did.
