bright light travaling fast

June 3 about 4:25 am eastern tine 2 of us saw a bright object in the night sky travailing from northwest to southeast at a very high rate of speed bright white in color no noise at all no tail to indicate a meteor no flashing lights at all went in a strait line much faster than the satellite we were observing not much else to say we were standing out back looking at the sky before work. noticed it moving fast.thought at first it was a satellite.but to fast and to bright to be.we were very interested in finding out what it was.then we lost sight of it behind the roofs of the other houses.
