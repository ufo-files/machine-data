Looked like birds flying in a line

Looked up and saw what I first thought was a line of birds flying at night, but realized fast that they were glowing on their own. There was about 20 or so objects that were all the same shape, size, color and glow. They were triangle shaped, like an airplane, had a white glow from underneath that was not solid. A fuzzy outline, but you could still see the triangle shape. They went faster then a plane and they all faded into the dark at the same time. No noise of any kind. They were here and gone within 20 seconds.
