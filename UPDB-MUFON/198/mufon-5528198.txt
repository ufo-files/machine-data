Triangular UFO- lights on each angle- circular bluish light in the middle.

Summer of 2009, New Orleans area, Metairie to be exact. It was about 5:00PM Not even dark yet. I walked outside to sit on the deck to smoke a cig. Not much to look at around here, except for neighboring houses, so i usually look up at the sky, (cloud watching, or weather watching), When i then noticed a Triangular UFO w/ lights on each angle, and a circular looking bluish light/thing in the middle. It was flying ESE. I was amazed and couldnt keep my eyes off of it. It was flying WAAAY too high in the sky to be an airplane. It made no sound, and lost sight after about a minute due to a nearby tree.
