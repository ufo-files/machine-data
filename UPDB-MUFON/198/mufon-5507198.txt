{nd} Traveling North on I-75. observied 4 red/orange lights then 3 red/orange lights in the shape of a triangle hovering/decending close to the road.

I was traveling North on I-17 in Sunrise, Florida. As I was approaching the 4 story Chrysler/Jeep/Dodge dealership on my right, I first noticed 4 red/orange lights above the left side of the road. The lights were about 50 ft. above the ground. The lights were in the exact shape of the left half of an octagon. About 2 seconds later as I was still driving North, I noticed 3 of the same red/orange lights on the right hand side of the road. This group of the red/orange lights outlined the shape of a large triangle and it was hovering/descending south. The triangle formation was lower in altitude compared to the first formation. I drove right under the triangle formation as it was moving over my car.
