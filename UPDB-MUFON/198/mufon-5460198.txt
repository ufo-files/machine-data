{cak} ORBS HOVERING 5 FT OUTSIDE 2 STORY WINDOW, DESCENDED, FOLLOWED PATH THEN DISAPPEARED

::INFORMATION RECEIVED AND REPORT ENTERED BY GA SD

ESTIMATED TO BE SOMETIME IN SEPTEMBER, 2011, WITNESS WAS ASLEEP IN BED AND HEARD *IN THEIR OWN VOICE* THE PHRASE "WAKE UP". WITNESS WOKE AND OBSERVED BRIGHTLY WHITE LIGHT ILLUMINATING THE BEDROOM. FIRST THOUGHT WAS AN INTRUDER TRIPPED SECURITY LIGHTS BUT REMEMBERED THE BULBS WERE BLOWN AND NOT FUNCTIONAL. APPROACHED WINDOW AND FOUND 3 ORBS APPROXIMATELY THE SIZE OF BASKET BALLS HOVERING APPROXIMATELY 5 FEET OUTSIDE THE 2-STORY BEDROOM WINDOW. ORBS WERE EMITTING BRILLIANT WHITISH LIGHT. A FEW MOMENTS AFTER RECOVERING FROM ASTONISHMENT, WITNESS WOKE THEIR SPOUSE. SPOUSE WENT DOWNSTAIRS AND OUTSIDE. ORBS HAD DESCENDED TO NEAR GROUND LEVEL (DID NOT SPEAK WITH SPOUSE SO UNKNOWN ELEVATION) AND MOVING TOWARD NEIGHBORING HOUSE. SPOUSE STARTED CURSING AT AT LIGHTS WHICH IMMEDIATELY AND INSTANTLY DISAPPEARED .
