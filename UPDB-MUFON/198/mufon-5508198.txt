{nd} Multiple orange lights

Receved call from relative asking if i noticed orange lights in sky. Was just arriving home and got out of truck and looked east and noticed 5 orange "balls traveling at a slow and steady rate of speed from SW to NE. NO SOUND and Below cloud cover. There was a plane travelling ahead of this formation from E to W. Never saw anything like this! 3 other witnesses. They reported to me that they saw 10-15 total. All traveling in uniform and same direction. Best description available, "flare like" but with a definate flight pattern.
