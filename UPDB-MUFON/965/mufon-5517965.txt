{dr}Branson, MO UFO sighted Looked like a fireball.

I was picking my son up from work in Branson on HWY 248. I picked him up at 9 p.m. We were in an intersection next to the Cedar Ridge School. We were leaving his work when my youngest son said, "What is that?" He pointed to the sky. We seen an object in the sky, pretty close. I cant say for sure, but probably about a mile away. Maybe not even that. It looked like a red orb, glowing like a flame. It was hovering and moving slowly across the sky. I wasnt able to continue looking at it since I was driving but my son said it started moving up and down and back and forth. We were not able to continue to follow it because we were in an intersection. So we lost site of the object. I asked my sons if they had ever seen anything like that. They said, "No." We all described the same thing. This was definitely a UFO. We were all so shocked. My oldest son tried to take a picture, but it didn't turn out.
