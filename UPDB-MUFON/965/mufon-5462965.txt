{jpg} V shaped light formation, no sound as it passed over.

I observed a large V shaped light formation approaching slower than a plane. Altitude wasn't very high, maybe a few hundred feet. I first thought it was a military formation of helicopters due to speed and altitude. First noticed lights at about 1 mile away (using landmarks). It was moving north, absolutely no sound. Me and a coworker observed it together. Oddly enough, we never spoke about it to each other. 

Enormous if it was a single craft. Solid formation, no lights out of precision distance. All lights giving off equal brightness. 
Maybe 10-12 lights. 

If I had to guess, the complete formation spanned a couple hundred feet. Lights only in V, didn't know to look for triangular shape at the time. No moonlight, rural area in Central upper peninsula of Michigan. 
10:30 pm apx. 

Absolutely not any known plane, and absolutely not helicopters. I specifically was looking for helicopters, but only saw the lights. Zero sound, no other lights as it flew past us at about 35 degrees over horizon. 

It was nuts.
