{rjl} Bright white light faded and dimmed. The light went from white to reddish orange before it faded out completely.

I was in my backyard with my dogs. My friend and I were looking up at the stars. We noticed a bright light slowly moving from NW to NE. The light was like a star but much brighter. It got even brighter as it was moving along then started to fade out significantly. As is faded the color shifted from white to orangeish red. It then abruptly dissappeared and was not seen again. It was quite amazing to see and I am sure it was not a plane, helicopter or meteor. It was unlike anything I have seen in the sky before.
