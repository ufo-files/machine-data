{rjl} Red & Green, twinkling, starlight object seen in WNW skies, near my location at night.

On the date & location near 9mile/ Woodward, I was sitting on the porch of my apartment building. The night sky was clear & cloudless. I observed what appeared to be a star-like object low on the horizon in a WNW direction. I thought it was too low to be a star, even though I could see it was twinkling with my naked eye. I went in the house & grabbed 2 different pairs of binoculars & my Samsung Galaxy cell phone with an 8mp camera. I observed thru binoculars a disc shaped object, covered in red/ green pulsating lights. The object was mostly stationary, with an occasional side to side movement. The object(s)appeared to also divide into 2 & then merge back together. One of the divided parts looked like a ring that had pulsating red/ green lights rotating in a counter- clockwise spin. I used my cell to take several still photos, as best I could. I don't know what it was but I know it wasn't an airplane. Planes aren't stationary & don't move from side to side, or divide into two. I don't think it was a helicopter, because they only have a couple navigation lights. This thing was covered with lights. I observed this for about a half an hour. I was pretty excited about what I was seeing. Definitely never seen anything like it before. The cell photos show some definite shape changing going on, when they are zoomed in. The photos are available on request, if someone wants to analyze them. Pretty interesting.
