{tjd} Bright light which I mistook for a meteor because it was traveling so fast. Then it stopped and went 45 degrees SE

I walked outside and lit a cigarette. . I looked up and saw what I knew had to be a shooting star but it was much lower and brighter.  I got excited because I was for sure I would hear it hit because it was so low.  It was coming across the sky like a meteor. But then it stopped.  I moved around to get a better look.  It had stopped and was sitting from what I would guess about 10 miles from me now facing southeast.  Then it travelled straight away from me slower.  

I have seen things that looked questionable before but could eventually explain it away. This is one I can’t explain away.  It came in very fast then stopped. Then went about 45 degrees from its original course more south east.
