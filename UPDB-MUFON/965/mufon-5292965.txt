Giant ping pong ball Or egg

The object was less than one km away. About I saw it through binoculars it looked like it had texture to it. There was a blue light in woods behind us a few seconds before we saw it. It hovered for a moment and then moved over and behind my camp sit making no sound at all! All of the hair in my body stood up on end! My girlfriend is former military and a pilot. We both knew what we were seeing! The moon was not visible by us. I was looking at Jupiter moments before and the object appears 180 to degrees to the North . I never took my eye off of it it moved further to the North East and was no longer visible due to the thick Bush.
