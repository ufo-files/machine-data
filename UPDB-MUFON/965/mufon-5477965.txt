{rjl} Looked like a giant egg, white, glowing green outline around it; had tail of same color. Moved much slower than a falling star or meteor. Could have landed; too many trees to see for sure.

It was about 5 minutes after midnight and I was watching a movie seated in the living room. I had all the lights off except for the TV. I have a big picture window, and I was looking out the window when I saw it. At first I thought maybe it was a meteor, but since when are they green? It looked like a giant egg and was white, appeared to have a tail, and was glowing green all around it. It was moving slower than a meteor or falling star - seemingly deliberately slower. There are trees around that blocked my view once the object fell below a certain point, but it appeared that it could have been landing. The shape, the size of it - it seemed quite large - and the color - plus the way it was moving - these things are what struck me the most. I don't think I will ever forget it. I wish I could have gone out and investigated; maybe I would have if I could have seen exactly where it appeared to land.
