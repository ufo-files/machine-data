{cak} 2 Objects over Kingsbury Pond, Maine

My friend and I were on our way to Moosehead Lake in Maine when we pulled into a rest stop in Kingsbury. I had my new iPhone and was taking pictures and video of the pond and wind turbines... it was a beautiful day. My iPhone has a feature called slo-mo which I used for the first time. My friend and did not see or hear anything that day, it wasnt until the next day when I looked through the photos and video that I had taken that we noticed the objects. The photos were taken with the live feature and you can clearly see the two objects moving. The slo-mo video shows an object suddenly appearing and then slows down due to the slo-mo feature as it crosses across the skyline. I have attached the original photos and video, I have also attached two enlargements which clearly shows a silver diamond shaped object.
