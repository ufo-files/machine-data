{dr}I opened my back door once the sun has set and the temp cool down and look up above the power line and saw what I thought to be a star that was visible approximately 2 seconds then simply vanished

At approximately 6:30 p.m. on the 22nd of October 2016 I opened my back door just after the sun had set and the temperature had cooled down. The sky was a dark blue, had no cloud cover and no stars were visible yet. I felt compelled to look up into the sky above the power line and there was what I thought to be an airliner landing light shining in my direction. I live directly under a main flight path for Sky Harbor Airport so I am highly accustomed to seeing airliners with their landing lights flying overhead. 

However, within about 2 seconds the light simply vanished. I thought that maybe a plane's landing light head been turned off or had burned out. However there were no other lights to be seen, no strobes or pulsating lights, no object visible in the sky where the light had been and no sound. I stood and watched for about 5 minutes to see if it would reappear and it did not. I was confused as to what I had seen.

I believe that on this night or about the same time we were having the Orionids meteor shower so I questioned whether or not what I had seen could have been a meteor. But I have seen many meteor showers and this light left no trail and had no movement whatsoever. It was obviously not a meteor.
