Bright light around the Killing Station for the last two nights

My boys and I were standing outside infront of our house around 10pm,to 11pm when we notice a star like object around the powerplant called the killing station that sets on the ohio river around mile post 15 on state rt 52, we could see the object for about one or two minutes then it faded away or went behind a cloud that we couldnt see, whats weird is that I asked my boys what they saw and the oldest one said it was red the youngest said it was yellow and what I saw was a bright white, it didn't flash to me or the boys. we have live here for about 13 years, we see planes and jets like that all the time, and this was different. it was moving south to west,following the river but on the Kentucky side maybe over a mile distance from us or more, size cant say for sure.
the next night I seen the light one more time, it came on out of know where then went back off kinda fade off for about two or three seconds I knock on the house for the boys to come out but it was gone and never came back that we could see, thanks for your time.
