{rjl} Perfect line of hovering lights low to ground

I had just got into my car when I saw the lights out of my car window to the right. My boyfriend saw them also. They were in a perfectly straight line and did not move or change color. The lights remained the solid white the whole time. I took the first two pictures out of my car window sitting still and the next one when driving at a different angle. As I continued down the road towards the lights after three minutes and many twists and turns (the road was hilly and curvy) another orb appeared and the UFO was closer to the ground. It was extremely long and all of the lights were perfectly aligned. The lights shone even brighter when I saw them closer. I lost sight of the lights as I drove further down the road. The black line across the first two pictures is a power line. Also planes flew above the lights and around them but you could tell the difference between them because the planes moved and had flashing colored lights.
