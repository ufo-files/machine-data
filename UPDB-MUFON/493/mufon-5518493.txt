9 diamond shaped aircrafts flying close together.

Driving in car in Bellevue, near freeway, and noticed 2 bright lights in the sky, at first glance we thought they were helicopters or planes, but with a closer look we noticed they were diamond, or square shaped with 3 lights, 2 blinking (1 red, 1 blue) and one bright one that formed a sort of prong shape. After a while, we had seen at least 8, and noticed they were changing direction, they were all rather close together. Then, once we were off the freeway, at an empty intersection we saw a ninth one, flying very, very fast and very low, the lowest we had seen, just above the roofs of buildings. then as we were watching it abruptly came to a complete stop, hovered for a while, which was really freaky, because we were the only ones at the intersection, it was almost as if it saw us watching it. then immediately got back up to speed, and bolted out of sight. we didn't see anymore after that. We were so intrigued, that we called some friends and family to ask what they knew about this.
