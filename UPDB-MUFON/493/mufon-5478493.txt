MY LITTLE BROTHER CAME STORMING INTO MY ROOM SAYING,
"UFO! UFO!"
ME AND HIM BOTH RAN INTO HIS ROOM, WE ENTERED AND I SAW THAT MY OTHER LITTLE BROTHER WAS GAZING OUT OF THE BEDROOM WINDOW. ALL THREE OF US WERE PEERING OUT.
"WHERE!" I SAID,
SHORTLY AFTER, WE WITNESSED A GLOWING OBJECT FLY ACROSS OUR LOCAL PARK RATHER QUICKLY, IT FLASHED GREEN, RED AND BLUE LIGHTS. I THOUGHT TO MYSELF IT MUST BE A PLANE OF SOME-SORT MABEY? I WAS AWARE THE CRAFT DID NOT MAKE A NOISE, BUT THEN IT CROSSED PATHS WITH YET ANOTHER CRAFT. AT THIS POINT I KNEW NO AIRCRAFT OF THIS EARTH CROSSES A FLIGHT PATH OF ANOTHER AS CLOSELY AS THEY DID. THE SECONDARY CRAFT SEEMED TO FOLLOW THE OTHER, THEY SILENTLY DISSAPEARED INTO THE DISTANCE. 

I'VE NEVER SEEN ANYTHING SO ROMANTIC IN MY LIFE.
