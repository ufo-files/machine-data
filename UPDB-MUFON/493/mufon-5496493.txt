Pulsating star, increased in frequency and brightness until a white sphere emerged, then turned black

Family was stargazing at Beverly Swamp near the corner of Concession Road 8 and Westover road, near Millgrove and Puslinch, Ontario. Family was star gazing, saw a star, thought it was a pulsar. Over 2-3 minutes the pulsating became brighter and more frequent, until it became really bright and stopped. At its place was a bright white, intense, round object, the glow from the pulsating closed off behind it. This was facing north west at about 75-80 degrees from horizon. White light started heading down to earth and south-southwest direction. While it was moving it "powered off" and slowly floated down and away, was black, but visible (visible black circle was observable due to the orange haze of lights from nearby Cambridge, Ontario), then disappeared. Kind of cool to see it, thought it was a pulsar but became an object. Was with my wife, and our two teen kids, all 4 of us seen it. Lost sight as it went away toward the blackness between Cambridge and Brantford.
