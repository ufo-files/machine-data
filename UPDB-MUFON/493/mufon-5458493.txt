a silent white egg shaped object flew across the sky

me and my son were on a bike ride and decided to have a rest on a bench after cylcing up a hill. I looked up and saw what first appered to be an passenger plane but soon noticed that the object had no wings and wasnt making a noise and wasnt producing a contrail. Nothing but a silent bright white egg shaped object flying in a straight line across the sky. myself and my son watched the object until it flew out of view. I quickly got my phone out during this and managed to get a few pics and vids, the phones camera struggled to get an image the object seemed to reflect light was very odd.
