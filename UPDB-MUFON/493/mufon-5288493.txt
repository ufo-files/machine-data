Orb or spherical white/red reflective object witness for several seconds moving in a straight line trajectory

I was sat in the back garden of my in-laws house with some of my family. The sky was clear and blue, no clouds etc. 

Incidentally, we were talking about UFOs and the recent news releases from the US. I have an interest in the subject but until then had not seen anything myself that I could not explain. As we were talking, something in the sky in the left of my vision caught my eye and I looked up to see a white spherical object moving at a steady speed left-->right of my FOV, highish in the sky. It was difficult to estimate the altitude, but I'd say not much higher than a typical small plane would fly. The other people in the garden could not see it because the trees blocked their line of sight.

The object was reflective white, but occasionally seemed to blink red like it was glinting in the sun. It seemed to change shape, or at least look that way.  

I managed to record 2 short videos using my Samsung S8 camera phone with whatever settings it chose at the time. They are best played in slow motion frame-by-frame to see the object changing shape. In one frame it appears like there are two distinct sections (or objects?) to the object, the white and red one. I zoom in and out for scale, the trees were about 4 meters from me.

A map is included with my location and estimated FOV and trajectory of the object.
