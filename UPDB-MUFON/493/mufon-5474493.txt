{dr}3 capsule shaped shiny objects, silent, fast moving

Late afternoon walk on Norfolk Place in Sharon, Ma. We were looking at a large jet liner making a slow lazy turn, probably headed to Green in Providence, RI. 

At that moment, we witnessed 3 bright. shiny aluminum shaded objects fly by at incredible speed. They were capsule (like Nasa Mercury/Gemini) or somewhat bell shaped, moving East to West, silent, no emissions, at an incredible speed. They were visible perhaps for 2-3 seconds but were very clearly visible.

My companion and I looked at each other and both asked if we had seen "that"?

Although this event happened in 1977, it has always stayed with me. I can see it as clearly today as I did in 1977. 

To date I have only told three family members of this event but it has always haunted me.
