Several lights in tight formation moving across the sky.

Standing outside I looked up and saw this line of 15 or so lights, tight together, moving from West to East. They appeared to be together, as in attached to one vehicle but no lines or shapes were visible. I fumbled with my phone to get out and shoot video. I wanted my wife to see this too and ran to get her but by the time I got back,10 seconds or so, it was gone. It wasn't moving very fast so I should have easily seen it again without going out of sight. Now I really wish I just kept on with the video and showed it to the wife later. The video is about 4 seconds long but I had watched it for at least 20 seconds before I was able to get video rolling. Maybe this is explainable however I am always looking to the skies and have never seen anything like this nor can I think of an explanation.
