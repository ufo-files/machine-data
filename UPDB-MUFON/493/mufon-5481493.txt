{cak} 5 or more lights flew away in gem formation until out of existence

Myself and 3 other friends were walking into a building.
I noticed something in the sky when we were casually walking.
We tried to take pictures but they did not capture the occurrence.
We saw 5 or more lights that we thought were planes at first but it was before sunset and the lights were bright. They made a gem formation and flew away slowly until out of sight.
A couple months later we found a Facebook video of the same type of sighting but it was in a different state.

I called my mother for a regular conversation and someone or something else answered the phone. It sounded like transmission or interference followed by distorted breathing. I talked to my mother later and she claimed to never answer the phone and ignored the call.

Here is the Facebook video we found after the sighting. Note the video does not belong to any of us but it is the exact same thing we saw.
