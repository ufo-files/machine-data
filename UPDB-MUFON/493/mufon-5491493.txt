{cak} 2 bright stars/planet like lights stationary, one larger than the other.

On a Sunday night I was in bed watching the program 'naked & afraid' when I noticed in the west window next to the tv a very bright light. At first I thought it was the moon, then realized the moon wouldn't be at that elevation at approximately 2230, so I went outside (second story deck) for a better look and saw two glowing objects that looked like a planet (larger and brighter than stars) the first larger then the second which was behind and slightly to the left. It was a cloudy night and no other visible stars or the moon in the sky so I thought these must be ufos. I watched them for awhile (ten minutes) then lost interest. They were stationary, after about 30-45 minutes the smaller one faded out and I went back to watching tv. Eventually noticed the larger one was gone. The location was in the vicinity of Lockheed. A few days beforehand was the crash of that space shuttle and the next morning on the news they reported a large meteorite that stayed in the sky for about one and a half minutes, visible across the Carolinas and Georgia. I think the three events are somehow connected.
