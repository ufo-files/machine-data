After dinner at Tinys Restaurant, State St., Utica, NY., I entered my car along with a friend and drove south on State St toward his home on Kirkland St., when to my left I saw 3 red-orange bright lights with what looked like two small white lights at the outer edges of the red light. Jokingly I said to my friend, "look, ufos"...to which he replied, "they're helicoptors".. I pulled the car over, parked and got out. I heard no helicoptors motor noise, and said, "[Item Moved/CMS/nd] those aren't helicoptors. 
  The objects were traveling NE not in any line but random formation, and over very slowly and my viewpoint placed them south of, (to the right of) Munson Williams Proctor Museum that was nearly directly to my left ( east).
  I reached for my cell phone in my coat pocket and snapped two quick pictures, then as soon as I took the second picture, I looked down to my cell to switch it to video and they slowly but steadily faded out, all at once. You can see in the two pics that they are starting to fade, and tho the pics were taken immediately one after the other, they are just visible in the second pic. My friend, at first skeptical, could not believe that they were gone, asking several times, "where'd they go? Where [Item Moved/CMS/nd] did they go?"
