Two objects in formation

Driving north on Rt 15, just north of PA -MD border on a clear late afternoon, I observed two objects moving east to west, perpendicular to the highway. I first thought they were two military aircraft flying in formation, but they were much closer together than military aircraft fly. I estimate their altitude approx 30-40 degrees above the horizon. The trailing object was very reflective, and the leading object was dull black. The sun was located over my left shoulder, consistent with reflections on the object's surface. The brighter, trailing object caught up to the darker object, and the two were moving in very close proximity. Since I was driving, I had to look at the road; when next i looked up, there was only one object. After a few seconds; glance at the road, i looked up and both were gone. Visibility was excellent, with the sun behind me and the objects ahead of me, sky was clear and blue. I am familiar with seeing military aircraft in formation, and what first caught my attention was that these two objects were much too close together and did not resemble any aircraft i have ever seen
