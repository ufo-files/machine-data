{cak} Laying out on my front step, seen a small star like object emerging from the clouds. I watched this move from the north to the south east. It went over my field of vision behind a tree.

I was laying on my front step with my mother and listening to the radio, and I seen a star like object emerge from the sky behind cloud cover. It looked like a bright lit star, but I could be mistaken as it reflected light from the sun and had no marker lights like normal aircraft. The object changed its path slowly and moved southeast instead of south. Just as it changed direction, a airplane (I guess privately owned) came close and might have seen it. My best friend came just in time and added on as a third person (including myself and my mother) to see it. I watched till a high tree blocked it from sight. I walked to see it for longer but it was gone. The object had no sound and I cant say if anyone apart from us seen it but, this is a very busy street.3
