Back in 1992 in upstate NY Spotted very bright object in sky. Then noticed it darting across sky. Followed it while in car. Turned down street and then noticed Hugh triangular object hovering about a telephone pole height in sky. Drove up to object. Dark grey in appearance with a white light in the three corners. Got out of vehicle and stood smack in the middle of ship. Could hear a very low hum noise. It just hovered there. I tried to communicate but no response. There were houses around with lights on and I could see people in their Houses but it was like I was in another time zone. I was the only one witnessing this event. No one else around even had a clue as to what was happening. It was like time had stopped. Then ship darted to left then to right then it was gone out of site. Did not speak about this event for many years.Would like to have hypnosis to see if I can recall any other information about event. I cannot get this experience out of my mind
