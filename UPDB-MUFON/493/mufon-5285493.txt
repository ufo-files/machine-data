Disc shaped ufo hovers over city dump viewed from my kitchen window

I live on a hilltop just outside of [location removed]. The morning of October 20th, 2018 i was in my kitchen while gathering my things for work. It was approx. 6:35 am. The sun wasn't up yet fully and there was a beautiful blanket of fog resting on the fields out in front of my home. I took out my cellphone and captured two pictures to show my girlfriend who hadn't gotten up yet. As i looked out i saw a small bright line in the sky above the city dump that's on the opposite hill. the light was far too high in the sky to be a vehicle on the opposite hill and the sun wasn't up yet so i wasn't sure what i was seeing at first. The bright line had an opal rainbow-ness to it, like the ring around the total eclipse that we had pass over McMinnville a few years a ago.
