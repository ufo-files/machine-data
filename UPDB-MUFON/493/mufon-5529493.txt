Starlike Object in sky. Not moving.

I was driving down Hwy 70 in McEwen TN. I noticed a bright object in the sky that looked alot like a star. This object was not moving as if just hovering really high. I watched it for about 3 minutes and it just disappeared. I thought at first it might have been an airplane that the sun was casting a reflection off of, but then I noticed 1. it wasn't moving, and 2. it was between me and the sun. All I could think was what the heck is that. I lost sight of the object as it looked as if it just disappeared.
