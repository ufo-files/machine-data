I first spotted the object at about 10:20pm pacific standard time, on June 24, 2015, flying over the city of Monterey Park, just about 15 minutes east of Downtown Los Angeles. It appeared to be flying at about a 45 degree angle above my line of sight. It looked to be about a mile or two away from me. It had a bright orange, fiery appearance, as if it was flaming. It looked slightly more rectangular than round. I watched it for about 15 minutes as it faded away into a white or grey colored roundish object. It moved very slowly, not like a shooting star. It seemed to change direction slightly in a left/right/left motion. I tried videotaping it but was unable to successfully tape it. As of now, 12:06am,pst, I still haven't heard any news of the sighting.
