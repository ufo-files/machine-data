Much larger thanairlines traffic flying right past object

I was five stories up on a rooftop looking to the east horizon looking out towards Lake Michigan observing air traffic coming from the east. Out of nowhere this huge rapid flashing like star just appears before my eyes right in front of the flight path. When the closest plane approached, this object desends down to horizon height. After a second plane flew by it climbed to original spot. All of a sudden this green flourescent meteor like display falls from north to south, not breaking apart or appear like it was burning up in the atmosphere. Moments later the object just vanished like someone turned a light switch off.
