Light in sky witnessed on several occasions - I have photos

I own a property in Northern Ontario on a lake called Three Mile Lake near the town of Burks falls in the Township of Armour. On some evenings we sit at the firepit and relax and on a clear night the sky is filled with stars. I have seen satellites on many nights and some meteorites as well. I am well versed in Astronomy and aeronautics as an amateur. Occasionally I see commercial jets flying at well over 30,000 ft and you can see the lights on the planes and hear them softly. I also see jets flying lower as Toronto and Ottawa are a couple hundred KMs away and North bay is about 80kms north of us and I suspect they are on approach to land.  These planes you can see clearly. Please see map of area.

On a few occasions I have seen lights in the sky that do not match any known airplanes. There is only one bright white light (unlike a aircraft with a red/green and white light) and generally it travels North to South. There is no sound. On a few occasions the object changed directions. The most recent sighting, Friday May 28 2021, the object went North to South slowly then after a few mins just disappeared. I would say the object is maybe a thousand feet or so in the sky, its hard to tell. It is definitely not as high as a commercial jet. I have see prop planes in the day travel at this height or lower., so around 1000 ft. There is no sounds of propulsion, it travels slow, and on previous occasions it has changed directions, stopped in mid air, reversed direction. Drones don't fly that high and are not allowed to fly at night, as well. you can hear a drone. I can hear sounds from KMs away on that lake, and yet for these objects I hear no sound. I have taken a few photos; see attached. I believe I am a pretty credible person. I have a degree in business, a former Executive at Canadian Investment firms, I own and operate my own consulting firm, and am a part time Professor at Seneca College teaching several business classes. As well, on most occasions I am not alone. The last sighting on May 28 this year my daughter was with me and took one of the photos. 

If you blow up the photos you can see a light with a solid object silhouetted against the night sky. When I take the photos the object in question is center in the frame. One object from late July 2020 looks like a multi sided object, the object from May 1 2021 looks disc shaped, and the recent object from May 28 2021 looks kind of disc shaped particularly when you look at UFO 3; looks like it is on its side. This was taken with an Iphone, the rest with a Samsung. 

I am happy to talk to an investigator if they want to talk to me, I just don't want my name published.
