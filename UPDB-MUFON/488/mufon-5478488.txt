{rjl} An orb-like sighting at a hiking trail in Lake Mead National Recreation Area.

I was hiking with my family at the Historic Railroad Trail in Lake Mead NRA. It was 8 pm when we were on our way back to our car from hiking. It was around the beginning of Spring and there was still some daylight. I quickly turned around and decided to take just a few more pictures right before we left. That's when I noticed in my camera a bright round shape on the LCD screen while taking the picture. After looking at the 3 second preview shot on my camera, I was confused as to what I was looking at. So, then I looked at the actual view and it wasn't there anymore. Didn't think much of it, until I got in the car and zoomed in and compared it to another picture I took seconds right before. Attached are the picture taken from the sighting. The one without the orb was taken right before the picture with the orb.
