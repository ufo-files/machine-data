I was at a friends farm and we were outside having a cigarette, my friend pointed to a light in the sky off to the east and asked |what is that?" I told him that I thought it might be a planet, from that distance it looked like mars (a flashing reddish colour). I watched the light in the sky and noticed that it was moving. As it came closer to us, I could hear coyotes start to howl in that direction. I sent welcoming thoughts to it. I ran through the house to see if I could get a better look from the back deck. The object had moved considerably closer and was well above the tallest trees distance from the ground. I could not see the exact shape but there were lights that were flashing on it, they seemed to be around the bottom of the object. It hovered as it moved and I could not hear any sounds coming from it. And then the most amazing thing happened. It started to ascend and the lights became much more intense. It ascended to a very high altitude and when it reached its stopping point, it starting moving in a straight line going east. It was very high up and I could see it as a blinking white light until it was out of sight.
