{cak} Rectangular shaped craft different colored lights

July 19th 2019 10pm myself and my girlfriend were outside i was getting ready to leave we both heard a very loud noise like something breaking the sound barrier or entering the atmosphere. Then i looked up and there was a huge rectangular craft above us going from east to west at a steady speed just above the tree tops and it made absolutely no noise. I was in shock and unfortunately did not get it on video. It had a different color light on each corner & was completely silent when it flew over.
