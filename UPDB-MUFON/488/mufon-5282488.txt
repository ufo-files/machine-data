Star-like object

Was watching TV when a light catches my peripheral. I live near airports and an air force base, so I'm used to lights in the sky. I tend to look when they seem exceptionally bright, which this one did. I didn't pay much attention to it because the new Euphoria episode had me consumed, but after a few minutes, I'll say 5-10 minutes past and I notice the light is still there. So I pay a little more attention to it but time passes some more, I'm watching TV, another few minutes pass and it's still there. I get up with my video at this point because my show finished and I recorded 4-5 minutes of this object making strange, subtle movements. It's very windy tonight and the trees can make what I'm seeing confusing. I wanted to second-guess myself but the video is weird. I don't know anything that blinks and hovers in such a way. It's still there but it's moved beyond my peripheral vision. It's still very bright and I can't tell if it's closer or further away. It has moved from where I could see it out my south window but now it's moved more west to where my wall blocks my sight of it.
