I saw a silver/reflective object in the sky went to get camera and it was still in same position when i returned, took 2 pictures and the object disappeared behind clouds and was gone when clouds passed

I was in the garden about 19:00 to 19:30 it was a bright clear evening with scattered cloud, 0n the 13th of May 2015 I looked around the sky as i always do and noticed a reflective object to the North/East of my home location, this object appeared to be stationary and above cloud height (as can be seen by the cloud cover in the second picture) I went inside to get my camera and when I returned the object appeared to have not moved I took a picture and watched it for a short while and took another as cloud was just starting to cover the object when the cloud passed the object was gone, I then loaded the pictures on my computer and zoomed in as close as i could to the object, i can make out no wings or any discerning features that could point to an aircraft
