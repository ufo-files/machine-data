Bright Blue/White Basketball Shaped Object

This object had an extremely bright glow, it was a bluish white color. It was the shape of a basket ball and it was completely silent. When I saw it, it was hovering over a tree in the parking lot of my workplace. This tree was easily 30 feet tall. I viewed the object for about 3-4 seconds and then it suddenly zipped off towards a shopping center near my place of employment. There were no lamp posts or any other sources of light that could have made this object appear above the treeline.
