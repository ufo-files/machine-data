{jpg} Straight line of over 20 Starlike objects seemed to waterfall one after another

My wife woke up for work and had her normal morning cigarette. She woke me because she saw something strange. We witnessed over 20 Starlike objects very high up. They were travelling West to East and seemed to waterfall. They were always same distance apart and traveling same speed. You could faintly see them but only 2 at a time were brighter than the others. This lasted about 10 min and then nothing. There were also a few airplanes flying at the time to compare and you could tell that these objects were alot higher and traveling faster.
