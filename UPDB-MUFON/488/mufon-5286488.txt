I viewed it perfectly from the north pier in St. Joseph on lake Michigan. It was around 48 degrees up and approx. 30 degrees tilted down. Over water, hard to say exactly how far out it might of been say 10 to 15 miles.tt was. Gold with a half moon front swept back and rectangular main body. Where the half moon changed rectangular, it shot 3 white balls of light out, it hit what it shot at, i seen a yellow flame burn for approx. 3 seconds and went out. I didn't see anything but i saw the impact where it got hit.The next thing i remember, it was daylight and i was sitting on the pier, fishing. This all happen between two power plants that can be seen from that pier.
