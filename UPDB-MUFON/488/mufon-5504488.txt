{nd} I was at a red light updating Facebook status of being in rush hour traffic, did notice object in the sky.

I was on Silver Hill Road and Swann Lane, stopped at a red light across from the U.S. Census Bureau Headquarters in Suitland, Maryland with Rite Aid to my right. I was updating my Facebook status and snapped a picture from within my car (both hands over the steering wheel, on the dashboard) of the rush-hour traffic. I hit the "post" button and preceded straight towards Suitland Road and checked me Facebook status at the next red light across from Suitland High School (Silver Hill Road/Sunset Lane). 

This is when I discovered the object in the picture hovering low in the sky, later I noticed three odd ovals shaped like faces within the red spectrum of light in the image. I realized that I may have driven under the almost five lane wide craft.

Later, I make mapped the area to verify that there is no solid looking structure mid-air on Silver Hill Road and Swann Lane...the street view of the Rite Aid on Silver Hill Road daytime image confirmed my beliefs.
