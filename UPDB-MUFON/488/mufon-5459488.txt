{tjd} Orb like saucer

Driving from Flagstaff Arizona to Los Angeles California on I-40 Westbound. Noticed strange light object in my peripheral. Object was at altitude of a plane but would move from one side to the other. When you zoom in on the video you see the lights spinning and the rapid movements. For some reason the object would stop when we stopped multiple times on the side of the road. The object was directly above us. You can see semis and some vehicles passing by on the freeway. Multiple videos I do have but been weary of submitting due to my own safety but it is an event that I always replay in my head. The event started at around 3AM and continued for over 2-3 hours. Once the sun started coming up the object stopped and then shot off into nowhere. Video was taken in my digital camera.
