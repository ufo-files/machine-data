Strange red/orange object hoovering in the clear night sky

My wife and I were awoken to a bright orange/red in the light in the night sky hoovering for a few moments. This object resembled a Chevy Insignia. The object then started moving slowing left to right and down .I quickly grabbed my phone to capture anything in that vicinity the original object was no longer in sight . I did observe what appeared to be a fire ball thinking it was an asteroid which left a fire trail. This object then leveled appear to hoover then changed movements very quickly in unusually style manor. I was able to capture video and I am working to upload these to pc from my phone. The photos captures a bright long object appearing to have the capability to expand showing the segments, one photo reveals the fire trail when one object is descending. there is a white worm like object floating down appearing to take shape, however my favorite photo captures what appears to to 2 bright white stick figures shining a powerful light down in my direction. When I am able to download the video files I will post them along with what I viewed through my binoculars drawing.
