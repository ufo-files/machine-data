Silver, hole in the middle

My mother, sister, and I were hanging out on back patio and my Mom says "What is that?". And that is when we all looked at this thing is the sky, a silver disc with a hole in the middle. It was at first not very far off the ground maybe a couple hundred feet in air. It looked as though it was gliding or caught in a wind current like birds get. My Mom and I told *(Name deleted--CMS/sg), my sister, to get her iPhone. But we were in shock I guess. She finally gets phone and I notice it is moving towards front of house so we run through house while videoing. We can see the disc with our eyes that it has risen but the iPhone did not pick up the image. You can hear me saying there it is! But you see nothing on the screen. We have no idea what this was. But I have never seen anything like this before.
