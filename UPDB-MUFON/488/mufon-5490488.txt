{nd} 2 objects that appeared to be rectangular shaped, flashed and fluttered, slowly ascended.

My fiancee' and I had just walked out the backdoor and I noticed a 'flash' in the sky. They (there were 2), seemed to be about 200 feet high and rectangular shaped and moved in a 'fluttering' reflective-like fashion. The only time we could actually visibly see them was when they 'flashed'. It's hard to describe. They also moved very quickly away from each other- very fast! They also were moving to the east and higher up as they went. As for their general movements, they just seemed to 'flutter/reflect' around each other is the best way I can describe it. Also, the further east they went, the harder it was to see them, eventually just star like dots then they were gone. It also looked like each object gave off a sort of wispy, cloud like, whitish emission, but I can't be sure because it was so faint. They moved very fast around each other but slower as they rose up higher. I tried to focus in on them with binoculars and my fiancee' tried to take video of them but it was nearly impossible because they 'flashed' so quickly. We were able to see them from 2:00 p.m. until approx. 2:20p.m.
