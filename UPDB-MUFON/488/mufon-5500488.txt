{nd} Bright star-like object displaying various colors, small movements in same general sky location.

Witness (A) is a frequent observer of the night sky. On two evenings he observed a bright star-like object to the NNW at about 32 degrees inclination moving from side to side and up and down around the same general position in the sky.  The light exhibited various colors of white, yellow, red, green and the sightings lasted about 20 minutes.
