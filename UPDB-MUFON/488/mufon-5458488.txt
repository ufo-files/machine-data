{rjl} Object was observed assending from low elevation/ground then it stopped, hovered than left at a 90 degree path atgreat speed.

I was on a job site close to lake texoma and was facing north overlooking the lake watching a storm system moving across the lake when i observed a white oval object with what looked like a black band rise up from behind the treeline to what i would guess between 200 and 400 feet.It then hover for a second or two at which point it took off at great speed at 90 degrees from its origional flight path and headed west at which point it vanished from sight. I cried out in amazement at which point my wife replied did you see that as well, this is not my first sighting but it is the first i have chosen to report, several years ago while out with my telescope my family observed what i would describe as an ivisible/cloaked flying object.
