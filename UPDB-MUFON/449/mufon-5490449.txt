{cak} witnessed three objects descend elevation and speed, two where bright orange red which changed to a burning white like the other

Indianapolis, In. I was on the back patio having a cigarette around 11 p.m. Out of the corner of my eye towards the east, a bright shiny object caught my attention. At first I thought it was a plane, but the glow from the object was just as bright as burning mercury. I saw no flashing markers to identify it as a plane, so I got the attention of my fiancee and she came outside and witnessed with me what happened next. The object appeared to descend a little and decrease its speed and was heading in a north, north-eastern direction. At that time two more objects came into view of which where appearing as if they were burning a red-orange color and fell behind the first object creating a triangle formation. Seconds later the two objects changed color from yellow to a bright white like the first. At that point the objects seemed to cruise over the city maintaining altitude and formation. A few seconds later two of the objects just disappeared and the other one accelerated and ascended out of view. I didn't know who to turn to or to call for this matter. So, I found this website and wanted to post this to see if anyone else witnessed this as well.
