I was a police officer at the time of sighting,I was on patrol in the SE sect. of Mason City,the place I saw the object was on school practice field accross a river from where I had stopped my squad for a nature call. I got out of the car and I was just looking and up from the field this large object rose straight up but slowly approx. 10-15 mph it glided over the trees adjacent to the riverbank and almost directly above me and at a height of approx, 60-80 ft., the object looked metallic saucer shaped with a smaller dome on top there were multi-colored lights and they were flashing intermit but not extremely bright/somewhat subdued. There was a low pitched hum also not very loud and no air turbulance what so ever. I was quite frankly quite scared and very, very atonished! I didn't know what to do I thought of shooting at it then I thought it was best just observed rather than overtly violent toward it. The object continued its easward trek at the very slow speed and staying at about the same height until it disappeard over more trees and over a densely populated sub-division. I never saw the object again nor did I attempt to chase it in any way.
