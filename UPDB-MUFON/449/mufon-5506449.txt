{rjl} Bright star-like orb with flashing blue and red lights

My daughter from Shallott, NC called and asked me to got outside to see if I might be able to view what she was seeing in NC. She told me to look toward the Orion constellation which I did. When I looked at it through my binoculars I saw a very bright object that was flashing red and blue lights as an airplane or helicopter does. It did not move at all. I went into the house and came back out about 20 minutes later at which time it was gone.I have no idea what is was but I find it very strange that both myself in upstate NY and my daughter in NC could see the exact same thing at the same time.
