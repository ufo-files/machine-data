{rjl} 3 of us observed a bright, glowing orb directly above our heads traveling to the southwest

While camping in the Ashely National Forest (25-30 miles away from "Skinwalker Ranch"), set up to look at the sky in hopes of catching something strange. At about 10pm we noticed something right over our heads. It was easily the biggest and brightest thing in the sky. We thought plane at first, but with the lack of blinking lights and any sound at all we knew it wasnt. It was moving at a steady pace in mostly a straight line to the southwest, towards "Skinwalker Ranch". After shining our high powered lasers at the orb, it seemed to speed up and slowly dim itself out until no longer visible. The whole sighting lasted about 1 minute and 30 seconds.
