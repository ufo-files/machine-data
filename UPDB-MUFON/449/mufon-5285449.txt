Was not a shooting star

At the time described. Southwest just under the moon. I noticed a round object glowing white. Moving south. I looked for flashing lights like a plane. There were none. So I thought it may be a satellite. After 15 seconds of it moving south. It started to dim slightly. After dimming for a few seconds. It all of a sudden took off real fast to the west. Leaving a white streak behind it. It was moving southeast at a steady pace then shot off to the west. It was not a shooting star. It was however between me and the moon.
