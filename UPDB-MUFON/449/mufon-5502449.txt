Appeared to be a comet. Orange fuzzy fireball.

I was outside watching what I expected to be a great meteor shower, however only saw a few 'shooting stars'. I then came back into my house just to double check the area that I should be looking at was Polaris. I used the star app I have on my phone to find it in the night sky. As I moved my phone toward Polaris from North to South a light image caught my attention. It was a orange/yellow fuzzy in texture appearing to be a ball of fire. I just figured that it could be a comet but I am unsure. I then tracked the object as it headed south-east towards Toronto area. I lost sight of it after a few minutes. It was moving quite slowly and it also appeared to be semi if not within our atmosphere. It took roughly 5 - 10 minutes for me to lose complete sight of the object.
