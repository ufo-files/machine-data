{dr}Lights hovering over Pensacola Bay

Three perfectly circular, white lights in the formation of a triangle hovered over the Pensacola Bay. I saw them driving home from work on a night in the late spring or early summer in 2013, as I was going over the 3 mile bay bridge. There didn't seem to be any craft that they were attached to, and in the space between all three lights, I could see stars and the night sky...so they didn't seem to be attached, other than for the fact that they remained perfectly spaced. I saw them and thought they might have been lights of an aircraft, because the NAS was on the other side of the bay from where I was driving, and the lights would have been between me and the base; however, they began to spin as they hung in the air, as if they *were* attached to a craft that was spinning, because they stayed equally spaced from each other. Then, the lights shot into the sky, up, not toward any particular N/S/E/W direction. They were gone in the blink of an eye.
