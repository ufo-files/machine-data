{RJL} Cluster of UFO in a line

I was taking the garbage out when I looked up to the west. I seen a trail of, what appeared to be satellites, all in a line, moving west to east. Had to be about 20-50 of them. As I looked, I noticed the points of light would all start in one spot, travel west to east, then disappear in one spot. It was very very visible and extremely odd and I cannot think of what that might have been unless the government launched a bunch of satellites into the sky. My parents, who live about 50 miles southeast from me called me and told me to look out my back yard in the sky because they were seeing the same thing.
