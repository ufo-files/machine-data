{nd} BLACK OBJECT WITH LIGHTS MOVING SLOWLY CHANGED DIRECTION CAME CLOSER TO ME

MY HUSBAND AND I WERE ON OUR WAY TO TUPELO. WE HEADED DOWN HWY 45 SOUTH. WHEN WE WERE BETWEEN TUPELO AND SALTILLO I NOTICED 3 BRIGHT WHITE LIGHTS. I THOUGHT IT LOOKED DISC LIKE AT THAT POINT. I POINTED IT OUT TO MY HUSBAND THAT WAS DRIVING. WE SLOWED DOWN ANS BEGAN TO WATCH IT BECAUSE THE LIGHTS WERE SO BRIGHTLY GLOWING WHITE. IT WAS IN THE DISTANCE AT THIS POINT. IT QUICKLY CHANGED DIRECTION AND ALTITUDE. AS IT BEGAN COMING TOWARD THE HIGHWAY (EAST)WHERE WE WERE. IT APPEARED TO HAVE SOME SORT OF LIGHT "DROP" OUT OF IT. LIKE A RAINDROP OF LIGHT. WE TURNED TO EACH OTHER AND SAID, "DID YOU SEE THAT? ARE YOU SEEING THIS?" MY HUSBAND SAID WE NEED TO GET A PICTURE OF IT BECAUSE NO ONE WOULD BELIEVE THIS. WE SLOWED DOWN EVEN MOVE AND CAME TO STOP ON THE HIGHWAY (NOT ON THE SIDE OF THE HIGHWAY BECAUSE WE WERE SO CAPTIVATED BY THIS) AS IT PASSED OVERHEAD IN FRONT OF US. I DID NOT GRAB MY PHONE FOR A PICTURE BECAUSE I WAS AFRAID I WOULD MISS SOMETHING INCREDIBLE. AS IT GOT CLOSER WE COULD TELL IT HAD 4 LIGHTS IN FACT. 3 BRIGHT WHITE GLOWING LIGHTS. NOT BLINKING. AND 1 TINY RED LIGHT THAT WAS IN THE BOTTOM CENTER OF THE CRAFT. WE WERE ABLE TO MAKE OUT A BLACK STRUCTURE WITH A TRIANGLE SHAPE. WE DID NOT SEE WINGS ON THE OBJECT. IT JUST HAD THE TRIANGLE SHAPE. WE THOUGHT IF IT WERE AN AIRPLANE IT WOULD NOT HAVE BEEN ABLE TO TURN AROUND LIKE IT DID BEFORE, IT WOULD HAVE A BLINKING LIGHT ON IT SOMEWHERE AND WOULD NOT GO SO SLOWLY OVER US IT HAD SLOWED DOWN WHEN IT PASSED OVER US. THERE WAS NO NOISE LIKE A HELICOPTER. I DID THINK IT WAS SLOW FOR AN AIRPLANE. IT DISAPPEARED OFF TO THE EAST TOWARD BIRMINGHAM MORE QUICKLY THOUGH AFTER IT SLOWLY WENT ACROSS THE HIGHWAY. THE WHOLE EVENT MAY HAVE LASTED 5 MINUTES. I AM NOT SAYING THAT I SAW AN ALIEN BUT WE DID SEE SOMETHING! AFTER THE OBJECT WAS OUT OF SIGHT AND WE HEADED ON OUR WAY WE AGREED THAT IT WAS JUST LIKE SOMETHING YOU WOULD SEE ON TV. I HAVE NEVER REPORTED ANYTHING BEFORE AND PROBABLY WON'T AGAIN. I KNOW IT SOUNDS STRANGE BUT IT IS TRUE.
