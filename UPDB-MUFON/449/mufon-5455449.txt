Two bright lights with dim red light in middle

Driving home I saw two bright lights in the sky parallel to each other very close they stayed in place driving close too it I was nearly under it. Looking up I saw a long surf board shaped object with bright white lights on both ends reflecting off its metallic structure with a faint pulsating red light in the centre. When I returned with a camera it was still there faintly but thick cloud obscured it.
