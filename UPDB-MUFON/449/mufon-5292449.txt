Strange floating UFO high above Perris Gas Station 9 am.

My wife and I were filling up gas a few minutes before 9 A.M for a Road trip to Vegas on Saturday August 8 2020 when I noticed in the sky a reflective gold round shaped object with a cylinder coming down from the bottom. I immediately told my wife to look up as she was sitting in the car still, and we both observed it for a minute or so before I pulled out a phone to start recording it.The time stamp says i started recording at 9 am. I watched it float and hover incredibly high up and then it seemed to be able to move rapidly from side to side and up and down. I lost focus a few times but eventually was able to get a good shot of it zipping up and down and then to the right before it would disappear for a few moments and then come back into focus on the camera,..almost like a housefly how it moved rapidly. I filmed it for just over a minute before I got back in the car to start our trip. When I I was able to look at the video, I was able to zoom in somewhat to the speckle I had caught and was vaguely able to make out the round top and cylinder shaped tube jutting from the bottom of the top. I screenshot the closeups as well.
