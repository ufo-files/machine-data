{jpg} Blue-White light fluttered across the sky in a straight path but jerky, back and forth random motion.

Was outside changing a burnt out porch light at 22:22, looked nearly straight up at the stars and thought I saw one moving. As I focused my attention on it I noticed in was moving slightly south. It began to move east in a straight course, but sort of jumped around in a random, jerky motion north and south. No real pattern to this "fluttering" Looked as close as I could at it and it had a blueish tint to it, and shaped like 
a dart "-<" with the point heading towards the direction it was traveling. I also noticed a plane traveling south at the same time and this object at a higher altitude. Went to get my cellphone to record it after observing it for 2-3 minutes and it was gone.
