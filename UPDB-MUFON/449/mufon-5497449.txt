{nd} UFO Hovers near Chem spraying Jet then flies off as a glowing orb.

Last year I had been recording the daily chem-trails being sprayed each day before sunrise. Almost a year later I was watching these videos with some friends when I noticed a glowing object descend, change direction and then fly off. After slowing the video down and watching it several more times I began to notice the object appears in the upper right-hand area of the screen at 5 seconds in as a small, dark stationary object. The camera pans away from the object momentarily and then from the same area that the object left the screen it can again be seen descending and becoming more apparent. The object then suddenly begins to brighten and appears to change direction away from the camera and fades out of sight at what seemed to be a rapid pace. Being a strong believer in the UFO phenomena and a fan of watching the many UFO videos on the internet I must say I was rather elated at discovering I had actually captured an event such as this. Hopefully this footage will be of some use in the ongoing investigation in the UFO mystery.
