{nd} Triangle/Boomerang/Delta-Shape of 10 or more lights... silent gliding over Hollywood. This was no blimp or plane!

Last night, October 24th, myself and some friends decided to carve pumpkins and watch the good old classic British Horror Film Shaun Of The Dead...

At around 11:10pm the conversation moved outside as some of us smoke, so we all stepped out onto the balcony.
One of us pointed into the night sky and said - 
..."WTF is that!?"... We looked and the three of us who were outside ALL saw it glide across the sky for a good 30 seconds maybe... from left to right.

Through the LA smog layer, against the clear starry night we could see a jumbo-jet sized, silent, triangle/delta-shaped/boomerang with lights underneath it... Faint lights, at least 10 or so with one larger light at the front... none of them flashing - which by law you have to have the flashing beacons... this did not... heading in a Southerly direction... someone at LAX must have painted that or seen it from a plane... surely!?!?

We called the Whiteman Air Base and they confirmed theyd seen it also and that they had received three calls already.

This was no blimp or plane - it appeared to be fairly high up - but not of the 35k height of commercial air traffic... moving just as fast though... appeared to glide rapidly... silent... no flashing lights.
