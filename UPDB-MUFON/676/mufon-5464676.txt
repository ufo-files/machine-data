{jpg} 4 white triangles in blue sky and 2 domes in clouds

Photo was taken 15OCT18 re: Herd of Black Angus on Hwy 85 in WYOMING, 4 to 5 miles from crossing into SD. Being herded by dogs, cowboys on horseback and men and several vehicles. I'd say there were about 200 head of cattle, the calves were scared and bellowing, Mom's were trying to help them. Slowed me down, took me maybe 20 minutes to drive (very carefully) through / around the wandering and upset cattle, it was a beautiful day for driving.

Later on after checking into the Elk Ridge Resort cabin in Spearfish, North Dakota, I went out to dinner for a bison steak at the Steerfish restaurant. After dinner, I went back to the cabin, had a shower, put on my PJ's and sat down to look at the TV weather. As I was doing that, decided to take a look at the attached photo....that's when I saw the white lights in the sky and I enlarged the photo....!!!!

When you enlarge the photo and move around in the sky (upper right) you will see 4 white triangles that look like spacecraft vehicles, then move around to the left of triangle vehicles, in the clouds and there are 2 images (kind of in the middle of photo) that look like some sort of domes???
