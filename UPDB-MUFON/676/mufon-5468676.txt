{td} Translucent object, highly reflective, yet solid as well, changed shape.

I was leaning against the trunk of my car, at 1:25pm PST, facing eastward when I first noticed the object. It was moving slowly from my left, over the Walgreens building, approximately 35-40 degrees above the horizon. At first I thought it was a child's balloon or bubble, but it was translucent, and too large for a bubble. And it didn't pop or waver, and did not change directions.

The object continued drifting at a steady speed, from the NE gradually to the E. I put down my snack and took some photos using an iPhone 6. The sun was very bright. I could not make out what the object was, and used the phone to take approximately 1-1.5 minutes of video. 

The object seemed to rotate, or different aspects to it did, and it reflected the sun strongly at various points. It also seemed to change shape. It gradually drifted out of my sight line beyond the horizon, passing over different layers of cloud cover, after approximately 2.5 to 3 minutes of total viewing time.

I'm still not certain what the object was.
