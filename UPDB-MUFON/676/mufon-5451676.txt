Extremely Fast White ball of light

I was test flying a new drone i had purchased. I was in my front yard testing the controls and the video capabilities.  Everything went fine. I didn't see anything with my naked eye.  But when i reviewed the video i found what appears to be an extremely fast, white ball of light shoot into frame from the right.  It zooms in flying away from me, it descends, and changes direction coming back towards me. After that it appears to disappear. I've had people tell me it was a really fast bird, or sun glare but who really knows for sure.
