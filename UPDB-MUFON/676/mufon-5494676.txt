{cak} VERY LARGE AND SILENT WITH FOUR LIGHTS

MY FAMILY AND MY FRIENDS FAMILY WERE SITTING ON THE BEACH WATCHING THE SUNSET. HIS SAID LOOK AND POINTED TO THE SKY. WE LOOKED AND SAW AN OBJECT THAT HAD FOUR LIGHTS TWO AT THE TOP ABOUT 150' APART AND TWO AT THE BOTTOM ABOUT 300' APART. IT SLOWLY MOVED DOWN TOWARDS THE OCEAN SURFACE AND KEPT GOING INTO THE OCEAN WITHOUT MAKING A SOUND. I ASKED EVERYONE WHAT THEY SAW. EVERYONE SAW THE SAME OBJECT.
