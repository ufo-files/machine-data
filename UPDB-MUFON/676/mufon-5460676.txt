{rjl} 15 or 20 separate lights in the sky moving in a straight line across the sky

I stepped outside to smoke a cigarette around 11:35 at night and looked up at the sky as I always do because I live in the country and love looking at the stars at night. I seen 15 or 20 lights in the sky moving in a straight line across the sky. I watched it for about 2 minutes and they were moving pretty slowly. It was a perfect straight line but you could tell the lights were separate from each other. It was the most weirdest thing I have ever seen in the sky. I watched it until it went over the hill.
