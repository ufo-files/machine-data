Bright star-like object much larger than satellite followed by smaller point of light

At exactly 2041 hours EST, I along with two other witnesses observed two points of light bearing 300 degrees NW, one right behind the other. We were all seated outside when the two UAPs passed directly over us at an unknown altitude. The lead object was many times brighter than the second. The lead object was also many times brighter than a satellite or a star, (satellites are nearly impossible to spot from where we were situated because of our proximity to many sources of light pollution, including the moon at about 3/4 full and located to the southwest of these UAP). The second light was much less luminescent than the first - about the same as a star and far brighter than any satellite. The light was steady white with no color or anti-collision strobe as commonly seen on conventional aircraft. I estimated their course at 120 degrees SE with only a few degrees of separation between them. Both objects travelled at the same speed. I fumbled for my cell phone camera and was only able to capture them in a still photo before they passed behind a large grove of pine trees at the rear of our position.

I checked the historical position of the ISS for that exact time these UAPs were sighted, which indicates that the ISS position was directly over the Gulf of Mexico near the Texas/Mexican border and traveling NW to SE.
