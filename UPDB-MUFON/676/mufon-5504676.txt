{rjl} Rust orange lights emitting smaller orbs

My friend and I were driving around and I look up and see two very bright rust orange colored orbs in the sky. I stop the car because I have never seen anything like them before. They were very bright, very large (bigger than the stars), and orange. They did not pulse and by the time my friend looked up to see what was freaking me out the orb on the left faded away. While we were both watching, the orb on the right started emitting bright orange sparks off the bottom of it that seemed to fall to the earth (like little orbs with tails). It would stop giving off these sparks and then start again every 10 seconds or so. Eventually the bright orb stopped giving off sparks and started to fade but as it faded it moved back and forth in the sky and then a tail appeared on it and it disappeared.
