I have attached 2 videos and several still pictures. With use of an IPhone 13 Pro lens

I first saw a bright object in the sky when I woke up approximately 4am cst. I didn’t remember seeing it before, so it caught my attention.

I took a first video not really being sure if it was a planet or drone because it appeared stationary.

After monitoring for an hour, I came to the conclusion it was moving. A drift like move.

I was physically located between Tyndall Air Force Base to the East and Eglin Airforce Base to the West.

The object was first seen SE in the direction of Tyndall, but over the Gulf of Mexico.

When I stopped recording it was still drifting and was now S over the ocean. 

In several of the still photos, if you zoom in you will see several other lights around it. Those could be stars, I don’t know.

Footage was taken with an iPhone 13 Pro lens. All documentation was taken between 4am cst and 5:30am cst on 2/9/22.
