{td} white cigar stationary object that vanished and reappeared

at approximately at 1:50pm today, husband and I were 2.5 to 3miles east of Decatur on hwy 380. we noticed what a white cigar shaped object and thought it was a blimp advertising something. But noticed it was stationary, and after observing it for about 2min it vanished, only to reappear about 2 min later. When it reappeared it made a straight line to the north and a fairly decent speed and vanished again. No clouds in the sky and noticed small plane about 5min after object vanished so we know someone else had to witness this as well.
