I was driving OTR on a route through eastern Utah down to AZ. I always have my camera ready because I like to take pictures of the mountains and different rock formations. I came up to the formation "The Mexican Hat" I took out my camera and took a picture of the mountain because it looked kind of weird to me, like if a big machine had dug up the whole side of the mountain. I then took the second picture with the "Mexican Hat" in the middle of the frame. When I got home, I put the pictures on my computer. A couple of weeks later when I had time to look at them, I looked in the sky to the left above the mountain in the back, I increased the picture size to just above half way and there is the orb. I did not notice it when I took the picture but it is there now. Picture 18 was the first picture of the mountain, it is not in the picture. Picture 19 is the one with the orb. I locate it by looking at the antennas on the mountain and then going left to the third peak, then up halfway into the sky. I increase the picture by half, anymore and it will blend back in with the sky.
