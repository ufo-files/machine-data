disc shaped object at dusk

Observed a disc shaped dark object at dusk, lights present around the edge. hovering over the trees. The lights began moving in a circular motion after about a minute then the disc began to slowly elevate and began traveling in a NE direction toward the town of Auburn, California, I remember trying to follow the UFO down Barton Road. As I drove I saw several people standing in front of their houses pointing in the direction the UFO had traveled. I did nit stop and talk to any of them unfortunately
