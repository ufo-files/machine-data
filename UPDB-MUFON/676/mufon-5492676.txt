Took a few photos of Summerside and came across a UFO in one of my pictures.

It was a nice sunny day, perfect for a walk. I, as well as two of my friends, went down to the waterfront and noticed the tide was out. We decided to take a walk across the sea bed to take some photos of the shoreline. As we were leaving I had this feeling come over me to take one last picture of the lighthouse, so I quickly turned around and snapped the shot. We hadn't noticed the UFO at this time because it made no sound. I was about to show the pictures to a friend when I noticed an object in the sky above the lighthouse in the last picture I had taken. It looked as if this triangular-shaped object was either hovering or flying by. I uploaded the original photo along with a couple edited versions.
