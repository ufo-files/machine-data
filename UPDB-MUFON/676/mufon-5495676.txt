{dr}Saw a black, triangle shaped craft fly directly overhead with no sound emitted at all.

Me and a couple of friends had decided to go out and camp in a big field on the outskirts of our town. We arrived around 9pm, set up our stuff, and went for a walk around the area. Upon our return, we started hearing suspicious noises from the nearby woods, and fearing coyotes, which have been known to cause trouble in our area, we packed up our stuff and moved to the brighter lit area next to a nearby parking lot. Eventually, we fell asleep (probably around midnight or 1:00 am), but at about 3:00 am I suddenly woke up, and noticed the craft. It came in from the northwest, over the parking lot, flying in a straight line, like a plane. However, there was no noise at all, and it was flying very low, I definitely would have been able to hear it were it a normal aircraft. Physically, it was black, and triangle/chevron shaped, kinda similae to the infamous B2 bomber. It was very hard to make out though, so I can't really describe an exact shape. I probably wouldn't even have noticed it were it not for the lights, three of them if memory serves. One on each wingtip and one on the nose. I don't remember the exact position or color, as it was 3 am and I wasn't exactly functioning at maximum brain capacity, but I'm pretty sure all of them were red, maybe orange. I woke up my friends and tried to point it out to them, but before they could see the craft it flew behind the trees and out of sight. The rest of the night went pretty normal, we slept peacefully and went home safely, but I'm definitely keeping an eye out, especially after I saw another mufon report about a black triangular craft over I-459, in the same city.
