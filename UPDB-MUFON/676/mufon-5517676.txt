{ck} Looked like there was two suns in the sky

My mom and I were driving west on I-94 when it looked like there were two suns in the sky. One sun, the real sun was bigger, but there was a smaller one just as bright off to the left. It was the weirdest thing ever. I did get three pics of them. I expected to hear something about it on the news, but never did. Clearly other people had to see it, were driving down a highway. This is the first time I am talking about it.
