circular object passed overhead from ssw to nne direction

I was sitting in front yard of vacation home facing south and looking at jet contrails passing by on crystal clear day.a circular,silvery globe appeared under one contrail and proceeded overhead in NNE direction until it passed over the horizon. the object appeared to be almost as high as the commercial jets yet left no contrail and made no noise. it was traveling about 2-3 times faster than the jets and flew straight. I originally thought it may be a mylar ballon or weather ballon but it seemed too high and traveling too fast for those objects I had the impreesion it was under power not just floating by, but no noise after it passed and no contrail which confused me. I wondered if it is possible to see a satelitte in the daytime because i have seen them at night and that was the way it appeared to be moving to me i did not have time to get camera or binoculars
