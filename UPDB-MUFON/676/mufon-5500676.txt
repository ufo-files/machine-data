{rjl} (cfi) Red lights evenly spaced like a convoy, twenty to 100.

I got a call from a neighbor down the street that he was seeing lights that his daughter pointed out and I should look outside. I could not see anything as my trees obstructed my view so I walked to his house. When I got there I could see the lights in a row, on following the other taking the same path. My neighbor said at one point a group of 5 or so spun in a circle and that was when he was pretty sure they were not aircraft. I noticed that they did not blink like aircraft do just solid red lights. so I pulled out my cell phone and tried to video it. Real dark video but in some of the footage you can see 2 to 3 red dots in the sky. They moved from the west to the east then gained altitude then went north. This was while looking north from our location. I saw about twenty and my neighbor saw many more maybe around 100. I am uploading the very dark video as it was recorded. I am going to edit it later to try and brighten it up some to see if I can see any additional detail.
