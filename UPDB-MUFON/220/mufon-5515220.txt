{dr}5 glowing orange balls. 3 in a triangular formation 2 seperate to the right side. Spotted NE of Michigan/ Ohio border.

Standing outside in the driveway, sky was clear. Watched objects move around changing directions and then slowly flickered out. 

Right after last object flickered out what appeared to be a shooting star was seen in the exact same area.

We watched for a good 5 minutes between 3 people, we had a great view of the sky and was pretty amazing.

2 of us tried to get on our phone video recorders, but they are not really made for night recording so they were useless.
