{jpg} Bluish-white cigar shape blinking and changed directions.

My daughter and I were in her hot tub It was nighttime. I spotted the craft first. It moved East from behind a large cloud. It was a bluish white brightly lit craft that was blinking on and off. The shape was long and appeared to be thin. It suddenly changed directions and went back behind the cloud. I pointed it out and my daughter also saw it. It came back out and moved back to the spot it was originally in. The cloud it came out from then covered it and we saw it no longer.
