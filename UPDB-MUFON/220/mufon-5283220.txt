Hovered, keeping pace with us, then to space

In van with family to Los Angeles on hwy 101. Van filled with orange flowing light. Brother yells "IT'S A UFO!". I looked over, it's gone from site. I get up, go to window, look up and see orange dot moving same as us, but very high up. Orange dot speeds off into space. I yell, "IT IS A UFO!" My dad yells for everyone to sit the hell down and shut up. We do, and no one really talks about it. My dad was freaked out, and we had no choice but to do what he said.
