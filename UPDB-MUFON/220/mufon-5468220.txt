I dont know where it came from i just remember seeing it hovering or flying and it alpeared to be moving swiftly, it didnt hang around. When i witnessed this craft i immediately knew that what i was seeing was probably an extraterrestrial triangular craft. Cannot find a similar picture on the internet. I remember seeing red, green, yellow lights, alot of lughts underneath this craft, unlike anything ive ever seen. I figured that was its propulsion system.
