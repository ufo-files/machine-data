So I wasn't going to post here but then I saw another one and this time was able to get proof of what I saw. The first one was 3 months ago I was driving in the little city area of where I live and I was going up hill. I could see the Walmart and parking area and some thing bigh and bright in the sky that caught my eye. The sun was setting so some of the sky was darker. This was in the darker part of the sky. I watched the light and it was getting smaller. Then out of no where it just shot straight up and it was gone. It was a circle light. 

Last night I was at the lake and was looking at the moon. As i was about to pick up and go to bed I saw something in the sky. I grabbed my phone and took two pictures. I was going to try and get a video when I decided to look through my Telescope first just to see if maybe I could see it. I aligned my Telescope with the pointer and before I could get it straight, it was gone. I am glad I took the pictures first though. One is far away one is zoomed in. I had to get behind a tree because the moon was super bright. Both are very clear and can see it well. No planes were out either. No way this was a satellite... i don't know what it was, but I figured I would post here just in case it was from outer space.
