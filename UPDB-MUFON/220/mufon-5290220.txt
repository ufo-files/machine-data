Six specs of bright orange lights moved together in a straight path into a cloud

I have been a believer in UFO sightings for almost 10 years. I have deliberately spent hours on top of my terrace, longing for a glance at these kind of aerial phenomenon's which I only heard about . But it never happened. Finally when it happened, unintentionally, I wanted to shout to the world which ridiculed me over a decade on my faith in truth. Then I thought, What is the point? Even if I produce diamond absolute proofs, Some people will not believe, Some people want more proof, and frankly most people don't care. I want to share my experience with someone who cares, like MUFON.       
        
        I was working in my PC last night around 8:00PM. So I decided to have a time out and went to my balcony, which is facing the coast of Bay of Bengal, for relaxing. I am living in Chennai, which is a metro city in south India. So there is a considerable amount of natural light which usually hinders star gazing. I had to adjust my eyes for almost 20 -30minutes in darkness to observe any aerial phenomenon's such as meteorite showers. 
                                                       
       But the incident I am reporting here required no such eye adjustments. I noticed a cluster of 6 orange star like objects, which did not flickered, maintaining the same configuration and moving from the SE to S. I observed it for almost 4 minutes until it went inside a cloud. I tried to capture a video of that, but my phone camera was not that powerful. Although I took multiple photos by adjusting the camera settings in my smart phone. Luckily I were able to capture it in one of those photos. When I said this to my friends they suggested it might be Starlink or any other satellites. Then I googled about satellite travel paths and cross verified in an app called 'Satellite Tracker 'by Vito technology Inc. I got nothing matching with respect to what I witnessed in sky. 

       Today I searched for news articles about similar incidents. I stumbled across one similar story published in 'The Hindu'. In that story one family had a similar sighting in 2013. The link to that news story is (https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/chennai/flying-objects-sighted-in-city/article4834628.ece).
