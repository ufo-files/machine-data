I was stargazing as I always do. I was looking at the stars when this 'thing' appeared out of nowhere. It looked like nothing else in the sky and was so eerie, like nothing I have witnessed. Before I saw the UFO I was literally thinking about how cool it would be to see aliens, about 5 seconds later this thing showed up... I literally thought it was here for me and I was mortified. It was boomerang shaped, translucent (you could faintly see the outline and you could see the stars through it) but it was covered in white lights. It was just simply gliding across the sky with no noise being emitted. I was overwhelmed after I saw it. I lost sight because it went off into the distance where it was covered by trees.
