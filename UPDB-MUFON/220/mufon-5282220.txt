I was driving on I94 across ND and I noticed a bright ball of light In the sky ahead of me. I was driving west, towards Bismarck (approximately 40 miles east of Bismarck) when I noticed what seemed to be a huge meteor that was flying through the sky. I was trying to look and see if others on the interstate could see it but they seemed oblivious. And there weren’t any news stories about it that I saw. It was huge! And I had a hard time believing it was a meteor because of the color. The coloring was other worldly. It was gold, iridescent and shimmering. The shape was an egg like quality. And I’m positive it crashed or landed beyond where I could see. I’m sure it was at least 10-20 miles where it crashed beyond where I was driving but it came out of the sky almost on top of me. The whole thing took no more than a minute. It was almost blinding. I tried to film it but it was too fast. I didn’t know what to do so I called and reported it to the ND meteorological society. They didn’t care. I also called and reported it to the ND geological and atmospheric agency (or whatever it’s called). They brushed me off. It wasn’t until I just saw you post on next door that I found you!
