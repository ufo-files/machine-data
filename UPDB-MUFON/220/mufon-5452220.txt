Stopped at scenic view spot, saw flash, on camera ufo scene

We had been visiting Jerome, AZ and drove up to scenic overlook on hwy 89A above Jerome. When we exited the car, I walked up to wall at NE end of parking lot. I saw a flash of light ahead of me and asked I anyone saw it. I was only one out of 4 who did. My wife was taking a photo with her IPhone 7. When she looked at the photo, it showed what I thought I had seen. There is a flash near the wall, that moves to my right, then flashes higher above my right vision. Next frame shows a 4 sided flying object in only one frame of the “live frame” and then is gone. We have photo of it. I now believe we saw a flying ship due to the photo.
