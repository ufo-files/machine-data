Super bright still Venus-like object that disappeared

In North Portugal near Vila Real, i was in vacation on countryside. I have observed a bright still star-like object, like Venus at its best. I took about 5 seconds looking at it in the silent night and it did not move. I thought it was a bit akward at the time to see it so bright, so I searched my cellphone for Google Sky to help me identify it. When I looked again it was not there. I felt surprised. Definitely something was not right about that, i felt it was not human or natural.
