Saw disc like craft with either windows or lights all around the middle

I was using my astrobinoculars to observe the sky and saw a disc shaped object with either windows or lights all around the middle of object. It was hovering In place while at the same time turning over so that only the top/and or bottom outline and craft could be seen. Then would in place flip over again so that disc and lights/windows could be seen again. The second flip of the craft revealed that either top or bottom of craft had four rows of lights that the other side either the top or bottom was devoid of any lights. I have been seeing star like craft almost nightly but they just turn multi colors and flash. This is the first actual craft I've been able to witness. I will upload video of color changing lights I've seen, but unfortunately I couldn't get pics or video of craft as my camera does not zoom in that far.
