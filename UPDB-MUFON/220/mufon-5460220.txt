{cak} Blue Lights Moving In A Quick Check Mark Pattern Followed By An Odd Glowing Pattern

Driving home late at night after work I saw vibrant/bright blue lights moving in an usual way. I first saw them on Interstate 89 going southbound just passed Exit 16. As I rounded the turn I saw in the tree line (not in behind the tree line but in front of it in the clear area between the interstate and woods) a blue light. It was a what I would describe as "electric blue". It quickly darted down and then up in a check mark shape, starting with the left side of the check mark. The end of the check mark, the highest point, was above the tree line where the light disappeared quickly. It was not a light on flashing on the trees. It did this two more times immediately following the first, each check mark about 30 to 40 feet farther to the east than the last. They did not appear to be from a light store but physical objects that emitted their own light.

Still traveling on 89 south bound at about 80 mph, 5 minutes after the last check marks I saw one more like the first 3 appear in the tree line beside the interstate. Only one appeared this time however.

About where Interstate 89 is intersected by Stony Brook Road I saw what appeared to be multiple shapes in the trees and sky. Arranged in a somewhat haphazard pattern. They were roughly shaped like ovals/rectangles and in the same blue color as the check marks but not as vibrant and seemed more translucent. These moved together to the east for a short time (maybe 2 seconds total) before fading out.
