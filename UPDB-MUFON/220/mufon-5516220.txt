{dr}Three porthole type lights appeared then off and 6 lights appeared

Saw three floresent porthole type lights appear. they turned off like a light switch then three more came on to the west and three more came back on to the east to form a very perfect horizontal line. Very cemetrical. By my calculations with other observeres the lights spaned 1 mile and could have been over 100ft in diameter. I had over 20 wittneses. Cant forget or explain this sighting.
