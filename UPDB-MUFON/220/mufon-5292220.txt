Giant bird...possibly.

I was driving down 25th street heading to North Ave. in Bellwood IL when I saw a very large bird (possibly) from a distance flying over 25th. I tried seeing as much detail as possible. It looked large. Possibly black or dark brown. I saw it from the side and it seemed to have feathers and was flapping its wings. Its silhouette looked like one of an eagle but it looked much larger than an eagle. I asked my kids to take a look but they were reluctant because they were glued to their phones. When they looked up the bird was gone.
