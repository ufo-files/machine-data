It was 2:00 am and I heard a noise on my deck. I then received a telepathic
message to turn off my bedroom light and pretend to be asleep. Next I
heard foot steps in the hallway as the entity checked my walk-in closet
where my dog usually sleeps. But that night she was asleep in bed with me.
Then there was blackness. When I awoke in the morning, I had a screen memory that there had been a break-in the night before. It was a constant thought that kept occurring all morning. Several days later, I found a 6 inch by 5 inch patch of mud on the inside shoulder area of the bathrobe I wore that night. Immediately I wanted to save the bathrobe for possible evidence. Yet I was receiving intense telepathic messages to wash the robe
against my desire to not wash it. How did they know I found the mud patch?

I have been abducted several times in the recent past, and this encounter
has all the earmarks of another abduction. I have now learned that they
are able to "turn off" my dog. The screen memory of a break-in would
normally have caused me to call 911, yet I did nothing about it, because
there was nothing missing. How did they know that I found the mud patch
and manipulate me into washing the robe when I wanted to save it?
