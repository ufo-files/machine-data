3 bright orange light

July 24 2008 my family and I were sitting outside about 10:10pm. Up in the sky we seen this bright orange ball at first we thought it was a plane on fire. The more we looked at it, it was round and right over the tree line in the cloud. In a second it was gone, a few mins later it appeared to be at a different spot then gone again. About 10 mins later we seen 3 round balls in the sky really bright orange it stay for a few min then gone. This was something !
