{nd} Working in my yard. Wind blew. Saw an object in the sky. Grabbed my phone.

I was patching a tarp in my yard when suddenly the tarp blew over me and covered me. I got myself out of the tarp and looked up into the sky where I saw a disc shaped object about the size of a quarter.I stood and watched it moving in a way that was not consistent with a normal aircraft. I watched it for what seemed like a minute when I remembered that I had my cell phone on my work bench. I grabbed it and turned back to where I had last seen the object. I began recording and the light became very bright so I couldn't see what I was recording so I just held the phone up hoping I was getting the object on the camera. Finally, my eyes where watering so badly from the blinding light I had to stop the recording and put the phone down so I could rub my eyes. About 30 seconds after I stopped the recording and lost sight of the object, 2 military jets flew over traveling in the direction that I had last seen the object. While I was recording I didn't feel any fear, just excitement and hope that I was catching the object on video. I went back into the house and watched the video and realized that I had in fact caught the object. I showed the video to my wife and we downloaded it onto our computer so we could watch it frame by frame. When we did this we saw that near the end of the video there are 2 objects, one smaller and one larger.
