{cak} 10 to 12 lights in ne sky low pulsation and formation into various patterns

10 to 12 lights rose from horizon area around 7:45 pm in central ohio, I felt something behind me and turned around thinking it may be Chinese lanterns or large Christmas lights very high up. lights then formed into various patterns resembling tornadic swirls , orion the hunter, then a small hatchet shape appeared with the 12 lights forming the boxy top and then the long handle. that was the final formation before they progressively faded out one by one as they drifted upward in the sky to the north. the last one lingered as it seemed to understand we didn't want it to leave and we were truly focused and fascinated as the final light lingered and faded , lasting longer than the others. the lights seemed to possess an intelligence. the whole encounter lasted about 3 minutes as my boyfriend and I were canoeing on a small lake. we will never be the same. life altering experience.
