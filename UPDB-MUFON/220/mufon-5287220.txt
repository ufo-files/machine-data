Three bright orbs in a vertical straight line

While walking the dog on the eastern corner of limerick lane and Dublin Drive, saw three vertical bright orbs at twilight at E/NE high up in the sky, maybe 60-70 degrees above the horizon all in an exact vertical line. They were stationary in the sky, but then quickly quivered left and right 2 or 3 times and shot off higher off into the sky at a very fast rate is speed. Gone in about 2 seconds one the quivering began. Exact date unknown, summer of 2011 I believe?
