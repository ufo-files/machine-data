{cak} Bright ball with light beams coming off that moved from one place to another

Bright ball that appeared at first to be a star that caught my eye because it had a long light beam tail coming off the bottom of it, was close enough that you could see the light moving around the object, the light then traveled from being closer to the Dartmouth side of the harbour to the Halifax side of the harbour. After watching this object for approximately an hour, and taking several videos of it, close ups included, a plane from the Halifax side of the harbour that appeared to come from the navy base, approached object in a direct ascent from rooftop building height to approximately 1 mile directly in the air, in a 70 degree angle approximately, reached object and then left area in a straight line away from object faster than it approached.
