Bright, star-like lights moving close together, then apart then hovering.

Driving at 10am when my 4-year old daughter said "mommy...look at the bright stars in the sky." As soon as I looked where she pointed I saw 2 very bright, star-like lights that moved together initially, then parted a bit, then back together then just hovered. We lost sight of them for a few seconds but once I pulled over, my daughter spotted them again and we watched them for about 30 more seconds before they disappeared. After we lost sight, I noticed numerous planes racing toward that spot, then a few minutes later, a military-type helicopter flying in the general area.
