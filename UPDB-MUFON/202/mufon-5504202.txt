DRIVING ON I-40(GOING EAST)--MY SON BROUGHT MY ATTENTION TO THEM--OFOS--OBJECTS(PLURAL) WERE AHEAD OF US(1/2) MILE,MOVING TWARDS US AT ABOUT 200 FEET ALT.   THEY CIRCLED BEHIND US AND I GOT OUT MY PISTOL(45 AUTO.). I PULLED TO THE SIDE OF ROAD AND SUDDENLY FOUN MYSELF BACK ON HWY.(STILDRIVING). A TROCK APPEARED AHEAD(RUNNING LIGHTS ON).. THE UFOS BANKED AND WENT NORTH      THEY BANKED AS THO'AN AIRCRAFT
