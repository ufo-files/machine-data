Gave me a drink of water

Sometime around or on July 7, 2021, between 3am and 5am, I awoke in my own bed, to seeing my plastic green cup with water and a black blob floating in it, up to my mouth with the eyes of a grey looking over it, with a woman's voice telling me to drink. I just saw her eyes ant to the top of her head. It was half lit in m y room, she was a pale color, light grey, whitish. I was half away and gulped air I thought. Then here is the clincher, I fell asleep, and she said "there you go, I was hoping for you to fall asleep so easily". This is what made me suspicious, as she sounded like a medical professional, a woman,, then she said that I did as she wanted me to by falling asleep easily. Its this comment that made me think it was real, and not a dream. Also the cup was a new one from the kitchen, a bright green, that was lit up from behind. I had several in the sink. I do not dream in color, and seldom ever dream at all.
