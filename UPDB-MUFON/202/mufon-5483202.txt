{dr}2:30am outside observed bright sphere, changed shape took photos.

On May 31 2016, at 2 AM our toilet water tank cracked leaking water. Got up to shut water off and mop up water. When done at 2:30 went outside to shut pool water filter off. It seemed unusually light out so I looked up to see if the moon was out. I then looked up and saw a very large , bright pulsating, whitish,sphere in the Southern sky. It was 10 to 20 times bigger than anything in the night sky. It was lighter out than I expected.I was excited to try to get a picture of this object and ran in the house to get my wife's camera so I could photograph the object. The Object appeared a little smaller than when I first saw it about 1 minute later. I took over the next minute or 2 about 6 photos. The object changed shapes from round, to oval , to a cylinder shape(see attached photos) The object also appeared to get a little bigger again, then smaller and seemed to move to the right about 5 degrees. I looked into my photos to see if I captured the object then looked up again. It seemed to be getting darker out and the object seemed to blend in with the stars and disappeared.Went in the house and reviewed photos and zoomed in to get a more detailed view. Have printed several of the original photos and also the enlargements.
