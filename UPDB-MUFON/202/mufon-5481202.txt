{jpg} Flashing lights over Menifee, California

At approximately 945pm pst, August 30 2016, my girlfriend walked out of her home, we were headed to Sonic for drinks, and she called me to hurry outside and come look. Upon reaching her i saw several (40-50) flashing lights in the sky that loomed to be add far away as the stats in the night sky. They all moved independent from one another and seemed to be just hovering with no real direction. After about 5 minutes, they all converged into a small area together where they remained for a couple of minutes. Then the first, approximately, 10 began moving in a single file line followed by the rest in a cluttered group to the south east. We watched them move away to the south east until they faded from sight from being too far away.
