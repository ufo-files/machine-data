big, bright, fast satellite

What I saw was most likely a satellite. I was just finishing watering my plants about 10:15 PM PDT when I noticed a bright white light, about as bright as Jupiter, moving west to east across the northern sky. I watched it move, thinking it was too big, too bright, and too fast to be a satellite. I watched it from just past the middle of the northern sky until it went behind the trees on the northeastern horizon.
