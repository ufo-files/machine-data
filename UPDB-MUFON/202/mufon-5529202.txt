onboard uss compass island ag 153 during special operations on faintail watch. i saw in our wake approximately 30 ft from the faintail an object which was lit in a green color. the size was approximately 8 ft diameter and 12 feet long. the object was submerged and stayed with the ship for approximately one hr. mind you time and size are approximate since it has been since 1972. i reported the sighting to the boson on watch on in the bridge. he relayed my report to the officer on watch. the response that i got was forget that you saw anything and do not discuss.
