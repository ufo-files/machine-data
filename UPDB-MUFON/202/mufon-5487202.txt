Glowing football shaped orb.

This took place in the autumn of either 1980 or 1981. It was evening and I was sitting in our basement watching TV. I noticed that it was unusually bright outside compared with the normal light from the streetlight. I went out into the front yard and began to look around. After about one min I had a feeling of something moving to my left. I turned to look and at about 1 ft off the ground there was a football sized and shaped object that glowed a blue green color hovering near the base of the evergreen tree on the north side of our lot. When I looked at it it immediately made a straight ascending path to the north west with a slight curve to the west. It disappeared at about 30 deg to the horizon in the northwest sky. I have never heard of any similar sightings.
