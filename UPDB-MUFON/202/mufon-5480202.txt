Taking trash can out. Notice this glowing light in SW sky. Got binoculars out then small telescope. In telescope could see red on bottom white to yellow in middle then green to blue on top. Thought it could be a drone ppl buy and fly but noticed it had no flashing lights and was so stable. It finally moved so far away we lost on the horizon moving to fort worth. This object appeared to change shapes from a sphere to triangle all while changing color red being most dominant. Could see the layers of color as being described before. Was not a plane or drone. Was interesting to see and could not believe how long we watched it
