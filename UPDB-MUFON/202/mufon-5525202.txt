Large amber-ish light traveling quickly across the sky

I was standing with my friend outside of his house smoking a cigarette when I looked up and saw a large (dime to penny sized), extremely bright, silent, amber-ish light in the sky. At first it looked like it was coming towards us, then made a left turn and continued to travel across the sky until trees and houses blocked it from view. It was relatively low on the horizon, but definately above the clouds. There appeared to be a diamond/triangular/prism shape within the light but at the same time it seemed to be the shape of the light itself. The 2 of us agreed that there were no flashing lights or trail following behind, as well as moving silently through the sky. I was both shocked and amazed, as well as highly intrigued. Over the past few months my friends and I have seen all sorts of silent lights appearing and disappearing and moving through the skies, all of varied brightness, typically looking like stars. Sometimes stars that were stationary all night would start to move quickly and then disappear. Our general count is well over 160 lights/crafts from the beginning/middle of May 2011, to today, August 3 2011. However what I saw tonight was the biggest, brightest, most impressive UFO I've ever seen.
