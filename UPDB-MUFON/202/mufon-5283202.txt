Orb going straight up just west of La Plata, Maryland

While traveling east on Hawthorne Road (Route 225) and having just passed Bibury Lane I noticed a reddish colored Orb ascending into the sky. The sky was clear as the sun had just gone down. As I was driving down the hill towards Buford Place I saw a rising red orb/light above the houses to East of Valley Road, west of Japonica Place road, North of Homeport Landing Place. The orb/light started at the tree tops then continued for a constant speed straight up into the sky. As I noticed the light it disappeared/faded from view at about maybe 200ft in the air. The orb did not fizzle out like a bottle rocket/firework nor did it curve like a flare. My line of site was not blocked by trees nor was it raining nor was any fog present. I continued driving east into La Plata, Maryland as I was meeting family for dinner.
