{dr}Possible falling star, but appears to be below cloud coverage

I was driving west on US 60, about 1/2 east of Brandon Mall at 6;40am and notice a brillant white object fall directly down from the sky (possible falling star, because it seemed to be about that speed). But what had me concerned was that there was a heavy cloud coverage this morning from the morning rains. It seemed to be falling directly down and not across the sky like a shooting star. 
About 10 minutes later, the full moon came out from between the cloud coverage, so there were possible holes in the clouds to trick me to think it was below the clouds, but when I saw this, it was completely dark.
