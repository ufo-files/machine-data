Object crashed in the woods.

In the early morning hours of December 9th, 1964. witnesses in six states (including Ohio and Michigan) and Ontario saw a bright object streak across the sky, shedding debris along the way and finally crashing in a wooded area of kecks burg, Pennsylvania. Some reported a thump the alleged object crashed.
