{dr}I saw what looked like a star twinkling colors

I was out walking my dog and when I got a few duplexes down I noticed a star like orb in the south eastern part of the sky. It was pulsating colors of red green blue and some orange. the colors are what made me notice it and I had heard of people seeing this in the sky not to long ago. it stayed stationary and just pulsated the different colors constantly. It didn't have a particular patter that I could tell. I was so fascinated by it i put my dog inside and walked down my street to the corner to see if i could get a better look.
