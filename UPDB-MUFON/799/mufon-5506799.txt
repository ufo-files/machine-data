Strange blue orbs, inconsistent with reflection of land or water.

Alien week has us intrigued! I was playing on google maps, slowly searching in the Pacific Ocean. No real agenda, just curiosity. I came across three islands called sawatan, oneop, and lukunoch. All in the Micronesia area. At a closer look, there seems to be strange blue orbs around the island of Sawatan. The orbs seem to differ from other typical reflection such as sun spots. The orb that is located on the south end of the island seems to have circle like shape in the center! The other orbs do not display the same round shape in the center. This can be seen on google maps as of October 31, 2013. Very interested on your opinions and insight! Thank you, Ryan.
