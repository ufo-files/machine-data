UFO was on the horizon and then ascended up slowly, hovered and then shot incredibly fast from east to west (Over Blows Downs to West Dunstable).

On the 19th May 2018 at about 00:06 AM (6 past midnight). I was looking out of my landing window and noticed a fire like thing on the horizon, on top of Blows Downs Dunstable, England. (To begin with, I thought someone had started a fire on Blows Downs)

At about 00:07 AM the UFO started to ascend from its previous location just on top of Blows Downs up to just below cloud level. It then hovered there for about 5 seconds and then, at some speed, started to move quite fast from its hovering location above Blows Downs to West Dunstable going over the whole of Dunstable and my house in the process. Once it got to West Dunstable I could no longer see it anymore.

The UFO move from East Dunstable to West Dunstable and potentially beyond. The UFO had completely gone by 00:08 AM out of my view.

Description of UFO:

The UFO was a circular disc-shaped object and had several yellowly white lights on the bottom of it. Out of these several lights, one was in the middle of the UFO and the rest were in a circle surrounding the centre main light.

Location: Dunstable, England, United Kingdom
