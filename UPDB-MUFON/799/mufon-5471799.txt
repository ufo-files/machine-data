{dr}Bright green light in sky, went from mid-sky to western horizon in less than 2 seconds

While driving west on Beltway 8 near Ellington AFB at sunset, I noticed a green light hover in the sky, less than 1/2 mile away, for approximately 3 seconds, then it shot to the west horizon at an incredible rate of speed and disappeared at the west horizon. The object reached the horizon in less than two seconds. After a moment, I said "what was that?" My wife said, "you saw it too?". We discussed this at length and have no rational explanation for what we witnessed. Its not possible that a jet could have accelerated that quickly and reached the horizon that quickly.
