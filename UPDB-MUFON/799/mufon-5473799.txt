Came down fast on an angle. Hovered for a while then took off very fast

Was driving a friend home one night with 2 other friends. When I noticed a Bright light coming across the sky. Didn't know what it was or if I was even seeing anything so I asked everyone if they could see it to. When everyone looked they could to so I stopped the car and we all got out to look. It was a very dark night and I lived in the country so no street lights either. The object cam down to about a hundred feet or so above the trees Changing colours wile hovering for a while with slight movement. We all felt a state of awe and I felt like I was seeing something out of this world. Then took off straight away very fast and was gone. We were all very excited and not sure how long we stood in the middle of the road hoping it would come back.
