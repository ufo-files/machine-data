reddish orange light appears, maneuvers, disappears

We were waiting for fireworks to start. there were probably 2000 or so people there. we were looking southwest. my three friends who were with me and i saw a reddish orange sphere appear to the south. if i had to guess, i would say it was 10, 15 thousand ft high, probably 35 degrees off the horizon. it slowly moved toward us, maybe a few miles in 5 seconds. it stopped, grew brighter, more intense. it was about 5 or 6 times larger than a star. it stayed stationary for 20 or thirty seconds, varying in intensity. it then drifted away slowly, the direction it came from, about the same distance, then darted east. it moved 3 or 4 miles in less than a second, then seemed to fade away.
