{rjl} Lights in the shape of perfect triangle rotated as if it were a large object

At 9pm heading south towards Charlotte, I observed 3 lights in the sky in the shape of a very well formed triangle. I would've thought it were maybe aircraft but the entire object rotated as if the three lights were attached to a single large triangular object. Object rotated 1801 and I lost sight. This was observed on video.
