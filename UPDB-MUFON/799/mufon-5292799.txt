At about 8:30 pm I was looking out my bathroom window I noticed a light bouncing around in the sky. I grabbed my digit camera and tripod and set it up in my driveway to record the event. I then went and knocked on my neighbor's door and had him come out to see it. (not listed as a witness in this report) He was as perplexed as I was. He said it looked like it was over a park on 6th St. We chatted for about 15 min watching the light dance in the sky. I then decided to see if I could find exactly where it was. I grabbed my camera and tripod and drove over to get a closer look. I found the orb dancing above an apartment complex on N. 10th St. I pulled out my camera and tripod and set it up, the object was directly above me now. My camera had a hard time focusing on the object so I decided to grab my cell phone, only to realize that I had left it at home, I quickly drove home and grabbed it (none of the digital camera footage is worth sharing). When I returned I decided to go down 9th St. as it was much darker than 10th St. I got below the object and recorded it for over 30 min resting my cellphone on the sunroof (this video is almost 4GB's and I'm unable to add it to this report). At around 11:20 I decided to drive back home. I took two more short videos of the object from my bathroom window, one at 11:45 and the other at 12:30, before going to bed. I will look for the object again tonight and get better footage with something in the foreground.
