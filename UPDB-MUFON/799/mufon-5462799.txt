Out on my deck looking at the stars when I noticed several of them moving unlike planes or meteors.. Happens many different nights. Ongoing.

I was out on my front deck looking up at the stars when I noticed that three or four were moving around at different points in the sky. Planes fly quite often over my area and I am well acquainted with the sights and sounds of planes. These made no sound and moved back and forth,up and down and in circles at some points disappearing and reappearing.i have also noticed one that looks like a line of several lights and they blink one after another. It happens quite often since I first noticed them, always at night and sometimes through the early mornings. My husband has also started noticing them and some people that live in the area have also mentioned it. honestly, I was bit unnerved by it and when I hear a twig crack now in the woods I jump and run to the house. At no point have I ever completely lost sight of the objects except when I go back inside my house. Ther have been four witnesses including myself. Two I showed the lights to and then my husband noticed on his own. I also did some research and found a report quite similar and a video of a star like object that looks just like some of what I am seeing
