Metallic sphere picked up by quick moving cumulus

I was sitting in my solarium looking eastward.The object came out from behindthe building across the street - level of the 8th story.A child balloon.Interest- this is not the first time.The object floated south for appro.500 ft, turnedeastward and started climbing steadily. All the while, it kept flickering -dull surface to mirror surface.The sky was perfect. Suddenly a huge cloudformed over a distance of about 20 miles and came to pick up the sphere. At theprecise moment, I could not seen more since the sun bounced back on the biggerobject and my eyes were watering from the light. The whole incident lasted over10 minutes.
