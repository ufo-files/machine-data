Free falling, hovering, changed direction quickly and efficiently.

Walking the dogs in the neighborhood and noticed a red light falling fast. Kinda thought it was a plane going down then it stopped dead.. Went directly east and quickly changed direction and came west toward us. It was really windy here and it wasn't affected by the gusts at all. We kept thinking if it was a drone,it must be huge with a heckuva range.. Not sure exactly how to far it traveled but at least a great deal further than I've ever seen a drone capable of. It dropped below the tree line and never reappeared.
