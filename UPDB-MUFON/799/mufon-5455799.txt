{rjl} Blue-Green light moving in clockwise daisy pattern

Saw a very peculiar thing in the eastern night sky, around 8 pm Tuesday night about five miles north of Ripley TN. Was clear and cold (30's F) with tons of stars visible. A single bluish - green light, not blinking, was tracing a clockwise daisy shape in the sky. Repeated the same pattern for about twenty minutes (I was on the side of the road changing a flat tire). Unable to determine size or distance but was as bright as surrounding stars. Rural area with trees in all directions around highway 51 so I don't think it was a drone (my first guess). A plane was visible in the same area heading south with easily recognizable navigation lights. The light completely disappeared after about twenty minutes with no other anomalies. The file I attached is the pattern the light followed.
