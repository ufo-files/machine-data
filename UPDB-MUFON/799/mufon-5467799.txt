Dark Disc Shaped Objects Clustered and Separate Followed by what appeared to be Orange UFO's Coming out of the Sun

I was flying on a commercial airline from Recife, Brazil to Sao Paulo, Brazil. I started taking photos of the sun setting and noticed something unusual. So, I started to take photo after photo. When I enlarged and examined the photos later, I noticed dark disc-shaped objects, some clustered together and some separate. I also noticed lights in one of the photos. They were quite a distance from me. It seemed like they were waiting for something to happen. After a while, what appeared to be orange UFO's started coming out of the sun. I later researched orange UFO's and found they have been seen all over the world. I read nobody knows what they are or where they come from, but they seem to be controlled. I lost sight of the objects when my plane turned and we started to descend for landing.
