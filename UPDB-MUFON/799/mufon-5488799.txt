I was on my backdoor stoop giving my 2 dogs toilet break. The dogs were barking so loudly I tried to see them, it was nighttime. That is when I noticed the object. It was a very clear beautiful night.This was my second visual sighting of an unknown objest. The last time I was with my parents on the side porch. I was 7yrs old so when my dad said too look I did.
