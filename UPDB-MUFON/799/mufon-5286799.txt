Starlike but doesn’t look like a star - taken trying to view comet neowise

Trying to view and photo Comet Neowise using Iphone 11 max camera 7/20/20 at 6am. 

Photoing randomly roughly 5-6 shots where comet was supposed to roughly be at sunrise that morning. 

Looking at photos later in day. Saw stars in photos. Zooming in on photos to see if i may have picked up a trace of the comet, and ran across one star that looked odd. 

It wasn’t Venus. I have took pictures of Planets and stars before and none have ever came out like this one photo. It was not Jupiter or Saturn as they were to the south during the evenings. I also took photos of that conjunction event that didn’t generate the same image. 

It wasn’t neowise or a different comet, as i took photos of neowise at a later date.

Looks like a windshield that has been chipped by a rock. 

There was nor is any damage on my camera lens. No smudges no lens cover.

Know it was not a plane; could have been satellite or star, but not sure due to never seeing a star look like that, and satellites don’t normally orbit that low…

One photo is a screenshot of star-app using i took at 5:24am that morning where neowise was supposed to rise higher over horizon. 

Took photos in case I couldn’t see it with my eyes but camera might pick it up.

The last one is a photo if neowise along the blue ridge parkway that I finally was able to see on the same day July 18th 2020 at 10:30pm.

Wife suggested i send this in when i spotted this scanning the photos the next day, but was hesitant… thinking it was just a satellite or star. 

You never know though.
