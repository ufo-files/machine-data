As I was taking a photo of sunset from a sand dune overlooking the beach I observed a strange dark object next to the glare of the sun hovering motionless low in the sky above the ocean about half km away.

The sunsets in WA Australia are spectacular so I usually try to capture a gem with my iPhone 4G on my daily exercise walk/jog along the beach and sand dunes a mere half km down the road from my address at sunny Yanchep,60km N of Perth city.Coming down a steep 100 meter long sand dune I was in a hurry to get to the beach for a clear view of the setting sun, but as I neared the beach the sun was already half set so I stopped momentarily to take a quick shot before I lost it below the horizon. As I was looking into the glare of the sun I couldnt make out with clarity the nature of a dark, glowing object that seemed to be fixed low in the sky above the ocean and positioned near the top right of the sun (see photo). When I raced down to the beach for a closer view only a few seconds later, the object was gone! I still kick myself for not staying put on the dune to observe the object for longer and take some more shots. 
Two fishermen 200 meters along the beach on my right said they didn't notice anything strange in the sky, as they were watching their lines for signs of a catch. When I spoke to them about how I thought the obviously disc shaped object was tilted towards where they were fishing, they exhibited signs of anxiety when we discussed that they may have been the subject of the objects attention. When the photo is digitally analysed we will know what it was, or wasnt ! MI
