I was outside on my phone with a friend, i looked up because I heard a humming noise and witnessed a large triangle shaped craft with dim triangle shaped lights on the bottom, it had a red pulsating light in the middle of the craft and white lights on each tip of the triangle. It was roughly 500 to 800 ft above when it passed over me and was heading in a very slow but straight path northwest from my position. I watched the craft until it disappeared after roughly 5 minutes behind trees. I didn't really know what to think but I yelled for my girlfriend and she witnessed it's very slow movement as well.
