{dr}Observed a single light floating through the air(like a skipping stone on water) around 9:20 pm 9/5/2012.

Observed what seemed like a small light sort of skipping through the air like a stone skipping on a pond. I was standing on my back porch getting ready to lock the doors. I looked up at the midnight sky and observed this object for about 1 minute just "floating through the air". There was no doubt that this was no ordinary aircraft. It had no blinking lights, warning lights or sound.It continued on a NNW direction until I also witnessed 3 aircraft come from a SSE direction following or trying to catch up to this light. The aircraft that followed the light had aircraft sounds that I heard in the distance. The light continujed on until it disappeared into the night headed North.
