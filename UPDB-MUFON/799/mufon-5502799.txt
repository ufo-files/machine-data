{cak} I witnessed a dark object out of the corner of my eye. When I was observing it the object was hovering for a few moments and then turned to a ball shape and descended at a very rapid rate.

I was in the backyard and saw a large object very high in the sky. It first caught my attention in my peripheral vision. I turned to observe it thinking it may be a hawk or large bird. But it's movements where unlike any bird I have seen. And I grew up in the country. It hovered for about 3 minutes sometimes dropping its elevation rapidly a couple of times. It then became a sphere and dropped at an extremely rapid rate. My first thought was that it may be a bird hunting. But it's movements didn't seem natural too me. I have witnessed a few other sphere's at night East of my area that hover and the either ascend or descend rapidly out of sight.
