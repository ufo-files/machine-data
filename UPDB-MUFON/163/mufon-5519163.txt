I watched it for 3 weeks

3 weeks ago I saw 2 unkowns, one then the other, traveling west to east, then I was one unknown travel from north to south, all of which were beyond our atmosphere. Then I observed a light at about 1500-2000 ft travel from west to east. it was a lot brighter than any star, and I could easily tell that it was flying low. Then for the next three weeks I observed the same light staying stationary over Modesto from my house, it would show up at dark, and would usally leave after 10. I and my grandaughters saw it one night and I also have two pictures that I took.
