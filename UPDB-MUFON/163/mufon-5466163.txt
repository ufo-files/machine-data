{td} Sphere above house. Father-in-law observed through telescope. Lights on sides.

At about 7:30pm, we observed a shiny sphere above our house. Difficult to estimate distance but it appeared to be large. We called our father-in-law, who lives about 15 miles away and he could easily see it, suggesting it was fairly big. He's retired air force and has a nice telescope. He was able to get a very clear lock on it and he said it had lights around the sides and on the bottom. Appeared spherical, white and solid. We watched it for about an hour until the sunset. It moved slowly in a southeast direction.
