in my backyard bright red rose large spot of light stationary and then moved away

I was in my backyard June 13 2012 in north Phoenix Arizona at 9:05 PM Arizona time when I was smoking a cigarette on my back porch when I looked to the south from my house and saw a bright red rose large spot of light as bright as a police helicopter spot light from about approximately 2 miles or so at a 30 to 35 degree angle hovering briefly when I first saw it and then it began moving to the SE at a decent speed but not overly fast until it passed below the tree line along our back street that obscured my view of the light. I only saw this light for about 1 to 1 1/2 minutes at the most before I could no longer see it but I did have time to grab my binoculars that I have inside my back door for watching satellites and I viewed it until I could no longer see it but it was not a helicopter or plane as I am very familiar with those lights because I live within 5-7 miles from the Deer Valley airport and I watch the planes all of the time when I am in my backyard smoking. I just felt that I had to post this because it was very unusual and I wanted to put it on this site before the local news came on here tonight because Im sure others saw this with there being many small planes in the local air as well as many like myself who were on the ground that saw this thing. As far as how am I feeling at this time I would have to say a little anxiety from seeing something this odd. Thanks for the space to report such things, Witness in North Phoenix Arizona.
