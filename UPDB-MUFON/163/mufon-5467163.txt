{td} White star-like object moves slowly across sky; mechanical structure

After 3 years researching these objects, noticed a large number overhead since the 4th of July fireworks season, seeing 6-10 per night. Outside with 7x31 binocular with video camera capability and night vision. This object was white, a little larger than star-sized, first seen in the ENE (Az 73 deg, Elv 90), over my head, moving toward the SE (Last Az 163 deg, Elv 40 deg). As it moved out of sight below the tree line in the SE, I turned the infrared on and off (making trees white) so the object could be seen in comparison to the tree line. The object took a long time, slowly moving for 3 minutes across the sky. It also was too low for commercial satellite, about 2-5 miles altitude. This is evident by the detail in the video/photo with low power equipment. The object was not reflecting light, it had four bright circular lights, formed together into a square, which caused the object to look white as it moved through the sky to the naked eye, generating rather than reflecting its own light (see enlarged photo). The object had no visible solar panels. Sometimes these objects stop and hover, this one kept a steady pace. In a 90 minute period on this date, I observed 9 objects, videoed five. The others I flashed light at, hoping they would stop. Two did, and made a wide u-turn.
