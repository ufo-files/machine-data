Star-like bright blue orb lazily drifting downward & to the north-east

Around 10:30 PM in my hometown of Knoxville TN, i witnessed a very vividly bright blueish white orb (very similar to the north-star) lazily drifting downward & to the northeast in a slow, lackadaisical manner. It's behavioral pattern didn't appear random, as it did seem to be following a very linear path as if it were under intelligent control. Strangely enough, i recalled seeing two jets flying in very close proximity to each other on the EXACT same path earlier in that afternoon which i managed to snap a picture of on my iPhone (which i will attach) The pic is a little blurry, but you can make out the 2 jets if you just look closely. I apologize for that, but it's the best i could get. These two events could be completely unrelated although it did strike me as an odd coincidence.
