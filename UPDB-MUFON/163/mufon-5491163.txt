Two triangle shaped hovering objects

I witnessed two triangle shaped objects hovering over the experimental farm. I saw three white, unblinking lights in the triangle formation per craft. I could see an outline of the triangle shaped craft behind the lights. I could only see the bottom of the craft. One of the objects took of all of a sudden at lightning speed, and disappeared from view. The second object took off slowly above the nearby neighbourhood. Hovering above the buildings. Slowly. unblinking. Then it took off as well.
