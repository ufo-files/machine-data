{rjl} commercial aircraft size, translucent/glowing, disc, fast, flew 20 miles in 2 seconds, immediate 120 directional changes, disappearing, reappearing, hovering

I was looking in the sky over my dad's backyard(Orem, UT)doing yard work and looking for ufos I had seen one year before, not expecting to see anything. I saw a large disc shaped object that glowed and looked translucent white. It hovered for many minutes and then when a commercial jet was flying at the same altitude towards the object it dropped altitude, immediately changed direction 80 degrees, flew 1 mile in 1 second, flew upward with a 120 degree immediate direction change, flew back to the original location with an immediate acute angel change with speed. It hovered there for a while and disappeared then reappeared. I thought it flew faster and changed direction beyond anyting possible with aircraft on earth. I was shocked at how fast it was and could change direction so drastically and immediately. There were 5 witnesses to the event. I viewed it with binoculars and saw that it was transluscent and glowing white. It lastly flew in a linear direction over 20 miles in 2 seconds and then hovered over a large mountain for a while. I lost sight of it as I had to go about other work that day. The object reacted to the airliner and hovered in the same spot silently for over 20 minuts disappearing and reappearing at times while glowing white and transluscent. It was unbelieveable.
