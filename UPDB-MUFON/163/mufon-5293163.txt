18 ufos fly to a certain point and dissolve

At first I thought I was looking at an airplane flying from the north to the south and then changing direction to the east but then it disappeared. Then within a couple of seconds I saw another one do the same thing I watched this happen about 6 to 10 times and then I move my line of sight to the west and noticed that these things were in a line coming up and when they got to the exact same point as the other that disappeared it disappeared. It was like they went into an invisible doorway.
