{kt}Bright boomerang

I had just finished dinner and stepped out on my balcony. It was an exceptionally clear night with a lot of stars out. I saw a plane and constellations just before I saw what originally appeared to be a star moving. The object didn't have any indication lights and appeared to look like a boomerang on my camera lens. The object sped up and then changed direction a couple of times and each time the object changed altitude quite quickly as well. Once again the object totally stopped before taking off rapidly. It was still quite bright and then it simply faded out. I found it strange that it was totally silent and had a pinkish glow to it. Very interesting! It didn't appear to be anything like the Phoenix Lights my family and I witnessed during the late nineties, it seemed brighter and a different color and made faster directional changes.
