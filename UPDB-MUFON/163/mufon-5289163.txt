Spotted these tumbling red strobes in the shape of 2 triangles hooked together outside and over my truck while moving down the interstate highway.

While driving a tractor trailer out I-70 eastbound I noticed these red strobes or orbs shaped like 2 triangles hooked together. This was in April of 1997 and I am just now reporting it. As I observed this activity they tumbled along so to speak across the ground in my front view and slowly worked their way back to my rear view about a third of a mile away. I didn't know what it was and was about to forget about it when one of the strobes came slowly over the top of my truck right down the center and passing me at about 5 mph faster than I was going. Then to my left something caught my eye and it was another red strobe moving along and another at my right side moving along as if part of the same craft or whatever. I rolled my window down and stuck my head out the window going 70 mph and there was nothing in between the strobes or orbs but they seemed to be attached together. It seemed to rise up and then disappear after that. It was a bazaar situation and I would like to know if anyone anywhere has ever seen anything like this.
