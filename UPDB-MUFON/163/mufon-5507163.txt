{nd} Star like bright object hovering then moved directions

I was in my parked car with my boyfriend, talking. We were outside of my brothers house waiting for him to come home. While I was talking I noticed what I thought was a larger and brighter than usual "star". I even stopped and mentioned it to my boyfriend but he didn't think much of it. I couldnt stay focused in our conversation because I kept looking back at the bright star. I knew it wasnt an airplane because it was not moving it was just there but it was so bright and larger than a regular star. My boyfriend and I continued talking and maybe like 20 minutes passed when I looked back up and noticed the star had moved. It was lower than when I first saw it and it wasnt like a little lower it had moved enough for me to notice it right away. I pointed that out to my boyfriend but he really didn't think much of it and I don't think he had really looked at it anyway. I didn't actually see it move because I took my eyes off of it for a little bit. It was stationary or hovered in place. I ended up getting out of the car and going inside the house because my brother got home. I don't know what happened to it and I didn't go back to check on it, though I kept thinking about it and now wish I wouldve grabbed some benaculurs. I looked up sightings in my area and found a few stories of people reporting seeing the same thing these past few months.
