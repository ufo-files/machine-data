{nd} Observed directly overhead

Myself, my wife and two other people, were standing outside the house talking. Looked up and observed this huge, for lack of a better word, ship. It was so large I didn't even notice the shape. I would say almost rectangle, but the front and back were rounded. It was only about the distance of three trees above us. It moved so slow, I couldn't understand how it stayed up there. It didn't make a sound. We just stood there watching this thing slowly move across the sky, directly above us. I felt like I could almost touch it. I have a military background, and I knew right away, It was nothing we could have because it moved too slow. Nobody even thought to grab a camera, we just watched it slowly move across the sky and out of sight. It took a good 10 min. for it to go from west to east and out of site. It had lots of features on it, it was not smooth, none that I could actually identify, but they were so distinct. My wife saw lights, I didn't notice lights, but it was so clear, it didn't need lights to see it. The most amazing thing I ever saw, but it was five years ago. As far as I know, nobody suffered any ill effects.
