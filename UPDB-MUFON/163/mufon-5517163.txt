{flg} Taking pictures while visiting Mission, objects showed up in pictures only, not to naked eye.

Visiting Mission San Antonio de Padua on July 14,2012. Taking pictures with digital camera and personal cell phones. The Mission is located on the Fort Hunter Liggett Combat Training Base outside of Jolon, CA. We noticed some type of training manuevers going on in and around the base, one helicopter landing, but nothing that stood out as strange. The approx. time of the first photo is 1:35pm. I was taking a picture of the cross and bell of Mission and had positioned myself somewhat below and angling up. I took the picture. I did not see anything nor did I hear anything when I was preparing to take picture. I took 67 pictures in between the first one where the objects appear and the next picture where a single object appears. The second picture was taken when we were leaving the Mission/Fort Hunter Liggett. There is a Sheridan MFF1A1 Tank on display and we stopped so I could take a picture. The picture was taken at approx. 3:49pm. When I took the picture of the tank, I did not see anything nor did I hear anything unusual other than the sounds of cars entering and leaving the base. When I took both photos, there was nothing visibly to the naked eye in the sky above. No clouds, no birds, no aircraft from base. It was not until we had gotten home and I was looking at my pictures later in the evening that I noticed the objects. Both my spouse and I checked all of the pictures we had taken that day and the day prior to see if those objects appeared in any other pictures. They do not.
