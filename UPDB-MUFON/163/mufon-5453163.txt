When I was going to bed, I was standing by my window, I looked at the clock then I was struck by what seemed like lightning

I just got back from my honeymoon. I had to get up for work early the next morning, so I was going to bed. I was standing by my bedroom window, as I looked at my clock, it read 11:00pm. The very next second I was struck by the most intensely bright light I have ever observed. It seemed like a flash of lightning was striking me, there was no sound or pain or anything. A couple of seconds had passed, and I was standing in the same spot in my room, but paralyzed unable to move or speak. That was such a horrifying feeling, of standing yet suffering from paralysis. Something was speaking to me telepathically, and I remember being freaked out, saying go away, leave. As I said "leave" in my mind, not out loud, I was able to move again. I noticed the clock now read 2:00am, even though it seemed that only seconds had passed. I slowly moved to look out of the window to see what that was, and I saw a very long silvery gray tic tac shaped craft above the tree line. It moved like a snake slithering away in the sky, very strange movements for a long solid object. It was close to my house one second, and far gone the next second.
