Sphere like object that remained STATIONARY in the sky. Flashed red and green. Moved away then stopped again. Disappeared.

I was walking around outside saying happy-birthday to a friend. I look up in the sky to see a flashing red and green object. I thought it was a star since airplanes don't stay stationary while in the air. About ten seconds later it just shoots across the sky with tremendous speed about a kilometer North and then suddenly stops and becomes stationary again. I panic and run inside to call my mom. She walks outside and then the object starts moving away at the speed of an airplane. It then disappears behind a cloud and that was the last time I saw it. My mom does not believe me because I think it was a UFO. She only saw it going at the speed of an airplane and did not see it standing still in the sky.
