{cak} I saw blue lights over Rocky River, Ohio and they changed direction and ended up directly above me.

I was on my front porch at 1:45 am, the morning of Saturday, September 14th. I was getting ready to go to bed; since it rained all evening and it had just stopped, I decided to get some fresh air.

That's when I noticed a bluish light 1.5 miles away from my house, in the sky above Rocky River. I, grabbed my phone to shoot video and went out into the darkest part of my street to reduce glare from the streetlight in front of my house. 

As I was shooting video in my street, the light divided itself in two equal parts and came over the treetops across the street from my house. It almost seemed like the dual lights were shaking fists at me or trying to intimidate me. I slowly backed my way back down the street to my front yard and ended up shooting 6-8 minutes worth of video while the light entity was directly above my house and yard. I do not remember shooting the video. I do remember, just before that, hearing a man's dark laughter, on and off some type of weird music (like country and motown mixed together), and crowd laughter, in my head. It was as if I had entered a dream state.

I have very cloudy memory from then on. However, I do not remember shooting the video of the lights (6-8 minutes) when I was in my driveway and it was directly above me. The last thing I remember for those few minutes, is pointing my camera to the sky and pressing Record.

I knew the lights were leaving, as I became coherent and I was laying up my front stairs with my head rested on the front deck, looking into the clouds as the cloud system the light was in, passed to the south.
