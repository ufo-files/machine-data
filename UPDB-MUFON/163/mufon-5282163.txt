Bright tic tac shaped object moving

While repairing a busted waterline something told me to look up. When I did I and my friend saw a tic tac shaped object moving approximately 450-500’ above from South to North. The object made no sound at all and moved extremely slowly. I was going to get a video on my phone but didn’t get it on video. However I did get a photo before the object disappeared behind the hill across from my house.
