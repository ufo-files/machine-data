{rjl} Object with red, green & white lights moving at incredibale speed and quick directioal changes

My wife was on the deck at the rear of our home it was about 8:05 to 8:10 pm and the sky was cloudy. I stepped out the door and she said look up to the north, northwest and I saw an object that had red, green and white lights. At first glance I thought it was an airplane but as we watched, the lights did not strobe and there was no sound. All of a sudden this thing accelerated at incredible speeds (such as we have never seen before) and would stop in the sky again. then it made right angle turns, ascended and descended at those same incredible speeds. It would descend downward behind the roof tops where we would lose site of it and then a couple of minutes later it would shoot back in the sky at the incredible speed like being launched. it would stop in the sky and change directions at any and all directions at this incredible speed. This happened at least three times in about a 15 minute time span each time going further to the northwest and disappearing from site. We have seen strange objects in the sky before but nothing as shocking and amazing as this. My wife got a couple of pictures but it was as it was getting further away and are not very clear but in one you can see it as it accelerated.
