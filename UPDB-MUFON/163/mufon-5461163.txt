{rjl} Solid Gray Docs with Indentations Around Edge

When this sighting occurred, I was about 8 years old, outside playing with my BB gun, heard a very very low frequency pulsating sound when a shadow came over me from above. I looked up and saw the solid dark matte gray disc with Indentations around the edge resembling Windows but no glass. It was not much more than 100 ft above the ground moving slowly, not sure of direction because of surrounding hills. As the disc moved slowly away, I noticed it appeared to have a cylindrical component that spanned the top of the craft. I observed it for maybe 2 minutes then ran into the house to get my mother. I immediately ran back outside and it was gone.
