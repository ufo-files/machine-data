My neighbor started to say, "whats that?" when i saw it. Up in the sky an oblong craft...up and down...not across..it was coming straight down, not angled. It looked smooth bright white with a neon green haze around it. My neighbor saw it longer than i and said there were flames out the back. It just came straight down most likely landing in a farmers field across the street. It went out of view due to the home in front of us about 20 feet away. We did not go check for landing area, as we do not have any equipment to read radiation levels.the next day, both if us in our own separate homes, slept for an unusually long time and felt exhausted the entire next day.
