{cak} Tear-drop shaped bright light descending very fast and then disappeared

Driving on 128 Southbound, 9:30 PM EST, 11-16-15, noticed a white light with a tear drop shape descending from the sky very fast. Object "blinked out". The object appeared to leave a trail of some sort, but again, it "streaked" across the sky in a descending direction. Object was noticed to the south west. My first impression was that this was a meteor, a piece of space junk, a fireball, or firework. However, after considering all of the above, definitely not a firework or meteor. I never lost sight of the object as it appeared to have "winked out".
