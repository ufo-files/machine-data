{jpg} 1ST SAW DURING LIGHTENING STORM AROUND 9PM LOOKED LIKE STARS. THERE WERE 3 IN A TRIANGLE SHAPE & DIDNT MOVE UNTIL AROUND 6AM THEN TOOK OFF N DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS AT DIFFERENT SPEEDS

WE WERE AT A FRIENDS HOUSE 4 DINNER, LEFT FOR A CABIN WE RENTED AT UP THE CREEK NEAR TURKEY RUN STATE PARK. I 1ST NOTICED THESE WHILE WATCHING THE LIGHTENING STORM..(THOUGHT IT WAS WEIRD THET THESE "STARS" WERE OUT DURING STORM) WE WERE NOT EVEN A MILE AWAY FROM OUR FRIENDS HOUSE AT THIS POINT. WE TURNED LEFT ON SR. 59 HEADING TOWARDS THE CABIN AND PULLED OVER SO I COULD TAKE PICS.. BUT BOTH OUR PHONES WERE DEAD.. WE GOT TO THE CABIN.. THEY WERE STILL IN THE SKY..& LOOKED LIKE THE WERE MOVING ODDLY TWITCHING AND SHAKING OR (IDK CONVULSING?) I TRIED TO GET VIDEO AGAIN.. AND PHONES WERE STILL NOT CHARGED ENOUGH. THEN AROUND 5 OR 6 AM WE WENT 2 THE RESTROOMS AND THE OBJECTS WERE MOVING ACROSS THE SKY.. 1 HEADED NORTH.. I TURNED 2 SEE THE OTHER 2 OBJECTS.. MOVING SLOWLY.. 1 EAST FAR ABOVE US.. THE OTHER SOUTHEAST AND LOOKED CLOSER TO THE GROUND.. I LOOKED TO SEE THE 1 HEADING NORTH.. IT WAS GONE. THE 1 HEADING EST WAS NOW BARELY VISABLE.. AND THE 3RD OBJECT HEADING SOUTHEAST IS THE 1 WE WERE FINALLY ABLE TO CAPTURE ON VIDEO. IT STAYED UNTIL SUNRISE. THE ENTIRE NIGHT.. I WAS LOOKING UP AND WONDERING WTH? THE ENTIRE NIGHT OUR PHONES WOULD EITHER NOT CHARGE ENOUGH TO CAPTURE ANY VIDEO OR WOULD NOT RESPOND THEN RETURN TO THE HOME SCREEN. THE LIGHTENING STORM TOOK UP MOST OF THE EVENING, BUT I DONT REMEMBER IT RAINING.. I TOLD NY FIANCE THAT IT MUST BE HEAT LIGHTENING? AT 1ST..HE KEPT REASSURING ME (AND MAYBE HIMSELF) THAT WHAT WE WERE SEEING WERE DRONES.. BUT WHY WOULD ANYONE JEOPARDIZE AN EXPENSIVE "TOY" IN A LIGHTENING STORM?
