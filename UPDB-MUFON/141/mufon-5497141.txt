{dr|Nearly invisible craft hovering in front of cloud, could see visible light displacement, vanished suddenly

My mother and i were heading to wayne state University, traveling on I75 southbound. I looked up towards the clouds and thought everything was normal until i could see that something was displacing the light coming from the sun. The area of cloud it covered caused the cloud to appear fuzzy. I pointed this out to my mom, who could now also see it. Since this object was cloaked it was hard to see the exact shape, but it appeared to be a classic saucer shape with some kind of "tenticle" (this is in quotes because i really can't tell what this arm like apparatus was). At first i did just assume it was the cloud but at further inspection my mother and i could see that this was some kind of cloaked ufo. It appeared to be still and after 5 solid minutes of observation it took off. Given that this object was pretty much invisible, it was impossible to tell whuch direction it was going or how fast it was. Obviously we can't just assume this was an extra terrestrial vehicle, but i honestly believe it was and I felt privileged to have personally witnessed some of our galactic brothers/sisters traveling in our skies.
