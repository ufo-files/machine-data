{jpg} looked like a blinking satellite

was looking at venus/jupiter conjunction, a bright blinking light caught my eye, it seemed to be near the handle of the little dipper and then i couldnt see it, about a minute later i saw it again, moving in a straight line, and it appeared to be as far off as the stars, and it would inconsistently blink sometimes bright and sometimes a little duller, almost like it was an engine firing to propel it, it moved from nw to north right above the treeline but obviously far out in space, it was like a satellite but blinking in random pattern, all i could think of was the boeing x37b, possibly it was that and it was its engine firing at different times?
