{rjl} Rectangular Gray UFO Sighted Over Pittsburgh, PA. Date: 5-23-19 at 9:40am. Observed moving slowly over McKnight Rd/ US-19 near Babcock Blvd and i-279.

Rectangular Shaped Gray UFO Sighted on 5-23-2019, at approximately 9:40am while at the intersection of Braunlich Drive and S/McKnight Rd/ US-19 South, Pittsburgh, PA. There I sighted one light-gray colored horizontal cigar shaped UFO moving slowly westward or in a northwest direction, I observed the object as It appeared to pass just behind the hill-top summit of Ross Municipal Center Park where I lost sight of it.

When first observed, this UFO appeared to be located to the east of i-279 HOV and was perhaps one-half mile away from the intersection of Braunlich Drive and S/McKnight Rd here I was waiting for the traffic light to change. At first I thought it was a jetliner viewed from its side only to then realize that the object was too long in length and without conventional aircraft features. Over the following 90 seconds or so I watched this object gradually increase in both its length and height though mostly in its height. Its light-gray color gradually darkened to a very flat mid-gray color as it appeared to be moving closer and behind the hill-top summit of Ross Municipal Center Park. Once behind this summit I lost sight of the object. 

I thought it might have been a cylinder but saw no indication of any curvature nor rotation. The object appeared to be perfectly flat as if it were a sheet of rectangular card-stock with no visible side planes. Its outer contour edges were extremely sharp and clearly defined. All four 90 degree angled corners were perfectly symmetrical. Oddly, only the two-dimensions of length and height were visible. I saw no tilt nor turning of the object nor any side planes suggestive of a third-dimension. 

I think this UFO might have been viewed from slightly above my eye level but I'm not sure. I was quite baffled by its lack of any visible third-dimension. My drawing is a simulation modified using both Google Maps and Photoshop. I estimate that this UFO might have been 200 to 300 feet in length and possibly low to the ground over Babcock Blvd, US-19/ McKnight Rd and just east of i-279.
