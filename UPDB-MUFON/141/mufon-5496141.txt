{nd} Awsome to see something move that fast and scared at the same time

on Dec.24,2013 was out at mitani swa fora early morning duck hunt. Time was 4am clear skies and cold out. Was setting decoys at the point were two rivers come together. The platte river prouder river. I looked up to the west sky and I saw a disk shaped object moving out of the NW sky. It stopped moving in a path and hovered for a few seconds and then started to move in different directions. It moved up down forward backward left to right. It was changing colors from red to green blue yellow orange. I observed the object for about 15-20 min. Before the object left the sky I saw a white object come out of the sky from the SW as it was coming down the disk object moved towards the white object. Then the white object exploded like aa big firework shot from the sky towards the ground. There was no noise when that happened . The disk object then moved track to the NW and then disappeared at a unknown speed towards the west and was gone. I had my phone with me and I noticed that it was not working like it should. And after 3-8 min after the disk left my phone was working normal. I'm a retired law enforcement and I have seen some things in my life but nothing like I saw that night. I've been back to the same place several times and have never seen it again.
