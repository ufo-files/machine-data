Dark, black triangle shaped.

Walking home, overcast and dreary out. Felt like something was with us so we looked around, looked up and saw dark, triangle shaped object. I had no clue what it was but it was really freaky looking. I tried to Google its shape and got no response. Object moved freakishly slow through the sky just above the low overcast clouds. Eventually it moved far enough away to where we couldnt see it anymore through the clouds.
