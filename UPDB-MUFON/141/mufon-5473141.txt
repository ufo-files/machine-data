Pulsating orb

For a couple of months I had been witnessing these objects flying overhead that looked like iridium flares, but after analyzing the objects I came to the conclusion that they were indeed UFOs. Dimly lit lights moving slowly, performing these rapid, sudden bursts of blue light, blinking, almost intelligent like. No reflection of the sunlight through satellites behaves this way.
After these regular sightings, while I was working out outside, I felt the need to watch above me. I witnessed this orange pulsating light appear out of nowhere, flying directly overhead, in the troposphere, what seemed to be the same altitude as where planes fly at. It was no plane though, the object made no sound and flew slower than a plane, too big to be a drone. The only thing I could see of it was the pulsating orange light, that turned on and off, almost at the same rate of my air intake back at that specific time. Strange.
