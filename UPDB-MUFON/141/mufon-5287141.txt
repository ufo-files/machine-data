A light (thought it was a star) dimmed then brightened, then slowly began to move SW in a straight line, follwed by numerous other light phenomena

A minute or so after the first light disappeared behind the treeline, the same or another solid light moved a bit quicker from the same general area of the treetops from the SW to what appeared to be the North, before it seemed to lift away and disappear. After another couple minutes, noticed two bright blue-white flashes to the NW, spaced about 30 seconds apart. Just as the second flash faded, a much lower-flying red-orange solid light streaked overhead maybe only a mile or so up. It shot from behind the same treeline to the West toward an Eastern direction, appearing and disappearing from view in less than a second. No noises were seemed to be made by the objects, but shortly before the phenomena appeared a group of raccoons at the bottom of the yard started snarling and growling at something, possibly a coyote.
