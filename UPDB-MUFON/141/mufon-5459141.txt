Did anyone else see it? It was only around briefly, below cloud level, I saw out of the corner of my eye at first a white flash as I looked down for a while (as I look for UFOs sometimes and got too tired for a moment too keep looking up), when I saw the flash felt sour as I figured I missed a meteor (I was facing Tropicana from Flamingo as usual when I skywatch). And seconds later saw suddenly appear in flight what I immediately figured was a UFO (they don't have blinking lights), and had seen one at 9:28 PM the previous night (a red dot very high up that while in flight suddenly faded out). So this much closer one with two red lights very close together appeared from the left (roughly near the flash) moving right and was waiting to see if it was out cloud level, but it wasn't, and it suddenly stopped under a cloud, not an instant stop but seemed to go backwards slightly, it then reversed direction and appeared to lower out of sight, as if it was going to land. I wondered if it was a police drone, and after it quickly disappeared below tree level saw a police helicopter on the right appear with a spotlight, but it went towards me then straight up towards Tropicana and stayed on my right.

The UFO had no shape and that it disappeared so fast made me suspect it was a drone. It was moving very fast however, not super fast, but so fast I wonder if perhaps it was not a drone. I've never seen a drone in flight at night, so have nothing to compare it to. I encourage everyone to lie back in a lounge chair or mattress and watch from 9-12 PM.
