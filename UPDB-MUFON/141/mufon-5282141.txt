A Yellow light that got bright and dim and would blink out completely to reappear brighter than before.

Watering front yard around 8:15, looking east towards San bernardino national forest, and notice yellow light in the sky. Light got bright and dim, getting very very bright and big then dimming out till it was gone and then reappearing very bright again to another dim out. Event lasted 1 minute and 30 seconds. No noise, all animals and insects were quiet. A strange smell like a rotten garbage mixed with sulfer came and went 1 minute before event.
