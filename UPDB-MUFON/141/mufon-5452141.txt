EVENT 1: We observed white light going back and forth across the lake at a distance for several minutes. 
EVENT 2: We also observed a reddish-orange light in the distant sky to the West that came to within 100 feet of where they were sitting, hovering over a nearby hotel that he believes was the Bear Claw Inn. Witness was able to see that the light was coming from an oval object that was approximately 30 feet high and 12-15 feet in diameter. The witness states that the object was turned on its side and projected a reddish orange beam of light into the shallow water below the sea wall. The encounter lasted approximately 5 to 7 minutes and that the object departed back in the same direction that it arrived from.
