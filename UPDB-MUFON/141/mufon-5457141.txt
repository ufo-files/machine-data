It looked like a string of stars or lights, spaces of different lengths between each light. Perhaps a total of 15-17 lights. All the lights were moving with each other in a straight line It was much larger than 5-6 jets nose to tail. There was no sound, and only one light with any color, at the tail. I have never seen anything like it and WOW! It was something I will never forget. I was in a convertible and my phone was in the trunk. I don’t know if I could have captured it on video, since it was kind of far away.
