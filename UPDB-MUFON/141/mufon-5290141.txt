Thought it was a helicopter at first. Upon filming it changed shapes and color.

We were at home in Mishawaka Indiana. Looked out the window and saw it flickering and thought it was a helicopter. 
It was brighter and lower than anything else in the sky.
The object went from being a pulsating circle to a diamond shape. At times it was rounded on the top and then came to a point at the bottom. Sometimes it seemed to be exploding almost like fireworks. It would keep repeating this and move it would move slightly out of our camera view.  At times it seemed to have a tail and sometimes it seemed to rotate.
We were excited to see it and tried to eliminate what it might or might not be. We watched it for about 3 hours. Went to bed. We have probably an hour or more of video of it. We would like to find out what it is.
