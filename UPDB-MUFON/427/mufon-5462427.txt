{rjl} Group of us watching metors at night, noticed bright start all the sudden. Within 15 seconds it was hovering directly over us. Was not sold, bright white light and did not cast a shadow. Then shot off to the nw and returned about 5 mins later,

I was about 12 years and was outside our house with my brothers and sisters, dad, and some neighbors watching the metor shower. After awhile my father noticed a star the seemed to just show up and was flickering and was also brighter then the others. He said "do you guys see that really bright star" we all noticed it as well, it was odd in the way it was flickering with light.

It seemed like it was only about 15 or so seconds later it was directly above us! Within a few hundred feet I would say.
It was a very bright, intense white that seemed to be waving around the edges and not a solid shape. There was no sound, and it stayed above us for probably just a few seconds before heading to the Nw direction at a very high speed and leaving our sight. 

We were all in shock asking each other "what was that?" just really in shock of what we just saw.. About that Time it returned back from that direction, "but was at a higher altitude" was above stationary for a moment and then shot off to the south at a very fast speed and we never saw it again.
