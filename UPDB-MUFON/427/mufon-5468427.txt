{rjl} Two strange lights, odd behavior unlike regular stars

I was driving home from a concert, when I happened to look out of the window, and observed two strange orbs of light in close proximity to each other. My initial thought was that one or both might be an aircraft, but I them realized that both lights were stationary. I had an indescribable feeling when I noticed the lights; almost a feeling of dread. I lost sight of the object once I drove away.
