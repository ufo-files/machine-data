{nd} Hovering ufo that slowly changed direction

At approximately 12:30am my husband and I went out on our back deck to look at the stars. In the southeast, I noticed a star that was flickering and appeared to move slightly. I called my husband over to look at it and he remarked that it was flickering a lot. He sat down and I began looking at the other stars in the sky and they were not flickering like the other "star" I observed. At approximately 1:00am, my husband decided to go inside and ready himself for bed. I was curious about the odd star and sat in a chair and observed it for nearly 45 minutes. After watching it for 10 minutes, above a large white pine tree used as a point of reference, it became apparent it was not a star, but an object hovering and ver, very, slowly moving. The object moved first to the west but stayed in relatively the same area, but it would slowly migrated east, then down. Two airplanes flew nearby this hovering object and the object's lights became brighter. Over the course of nearly 45 minutes the objec ultimately ended up in the east. It was still hovering, but I became too tired to observe it further. It seemed like it was almost trying to look like a star by only moving very slowly.
