{cak} Five fast lights moving across the sky

I was in my car when I noticed three moving lights out of the corner of my eye. Noticing their apparent speed, lack of a tail, and lack of emission noises, I immediately grabbed my phone to take a video, in which I described everything I saw. In the middle of shooting the video, two additional lights appeared, separated by about thirty seconds, each moving more quickly than the original three. I thought they could be aircraft, but I heard no engine noises. I thought they could be meteors, but they didn't have tails. While I'm sure there are other logical explanations, I felt it necessary to report on the basis of being an "unidentified flying object".
