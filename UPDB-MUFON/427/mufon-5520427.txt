Redish-orange light or fireball moving at a high rate of speed.

On Saturday Feb. 25 at 8:52 pm, my wife and I had just stepped out of the pickup when I noticied something really bright in the sky. It came into Dublin from the southeast and continued traveling to the northwest. It looked like one fireball at first and then it would look like 2 fireballs side by side. They looked like they were spiining around each other or vibrating. It also appeared to have a fluttering movement to it. It was moving very fast. There was also a strong static feeling in the air. The flight path was straight but it was not smooth at all. It appeared to have blurry edges like it was vibrating. It made no sound. There is a mufon report from south Texas that same night at 8:30 pm. That one was moving north. We saw this object in north central Texas at 8:52 pm. They very well could be the same object as there was alot of similarities. We watched it for about 30 seconds and then it just disappeared.
