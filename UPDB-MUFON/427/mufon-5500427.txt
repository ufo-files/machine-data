Object hovered came towards the house then hovered above house and speed off.

The family came outside to see fireworks in front of the house. When I just happened to look up and began to notice a red glowing object with yellow light surrounding it moving slowly towards the house. There were no flashing lights that a plane has which made me curious. I grabbed our digital camera and tried to focus into the object and began taking pictures. The object was hard to focus it seemed like it was spinning. So object seemed to remain still so I went to grab my phone to take a video. When my wife noticed the object begin to spin wildly and move at high rate of speed and vanished. During the whole time the object was silent. After the object disappeared I felt like I had seen something I could not explain.
