Two stars following each other.

While looking up at the stars tonight, my wife and I witnessed two star looking objects moving accross the sky. They were about an inch apart at arms distant. The looked similar to satelites moving accros the sky. They were moving from South, South West towards the north east. When the two objects got under the Big Dipper, they slowly dissapeared.
