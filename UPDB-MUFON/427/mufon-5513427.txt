I took several pictures with my cell phone of the moon over the mountain when I was waiting at a stop light in Tustin, CA. I didn't see the object until I reviewed my pictures the following morning. Out of seven pictures that I took, the object is only in one of them. I never saw this object with my naked eyes, and I am stunned and confused by this picture. My wife was with me in the car at the time that I took the pictures with my cell phone. We were talking about how beautiful the moon looked over the mountain. I showed her the pictures on my cell phone the next morning and we both don't know what to think. I have all of the pictures on my phone and they are time stamped and dated.
