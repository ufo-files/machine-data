Plasma looking fried egg 2 in the sky

My son-in-law got my attention to come outside on back porch. He was pointing into the sky and asked me, What is that? He said that one had already flew by much faster, and the one we were observing looked plasma like. It was changing shape from round to oval to asymmetrical. The center was dark amber redish and as it moved away from the middle the color lightened from redish to orange then to dark yellow. It was totally unexplainable. We didn't even have time to grab phone. It did not make a noise.
