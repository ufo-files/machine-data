This picture captured by phone during my trip when I visited Thủy Xu1n, Huế, Vietnam on 1 Jan 2019. It was a foggy and raining day. Pretty hard to see the clear sky. I didn't see the object on the spot. I only notice the object on the next day when I browsing the picture on my phone. In the beginning, I was thought it could be my phone lens's dirt. When I zoom in I found out the shape is not a speck of dirt. It looks like a cylinder shape. At the bottom part seems to have a light. When I scroll to the next picture where taken at the same place and around the same time, the object basically appears to another side too. To double check, I asked my friend who has taken the same area to show me his picture. Surprisingly he has captured the same thing but just in a different spot. The picture I shared here is from my friend and mine as well. 

At the first glance, I assuming It could be sky lantern. But is pretty hard to fly the sky lantern in the windy & raining day. And the object seems bigger than sky lantern. If anyone happens to visit the same place and did take some pictures of the area from 3 pm to 4 pm on 1 Jan 2019. Please do check it out your picture. I really have no idea what it is.
