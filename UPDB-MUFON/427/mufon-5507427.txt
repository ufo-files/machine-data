I had just finished dinner and was looking out the window at the blue sky and saw a bright light over the nw part of the city in the downtown area. It was a bright bright light in the shape of a cigar about the size of a medium airplane, with no wings or tail. It was like neon light but brighter. I was going to get my binoculars but I had my glasses on and could see it quite clearly. I was staring at it and it was as if a cloud came in front of it, but no clouds were in the air and it disappeard for a couple of seconds then reappeared in the same position. As I watched, it stayed for maybe another minute and then a turbulence of air seemed to come from the north end of it and it disappeared.
