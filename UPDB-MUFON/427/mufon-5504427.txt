{nd} 9:15pm 3/4/14 North Las Vegas- 15+ orblike objects traveling across sky

around 9:15pm my husband I went outside to smoke and heard a jet in the sky, which is not uncommon for our area due to the close proximity to Nellis Air force base. When we looked up we saw around 15 to 20 dim lights traveling together, they went around the same speed as a plane and made no noise. They headed east towards to mountains and then we could hear another military jet in the sky. We walked down to the edge of our complex but the light were gone. We did observe around 7 to 8 blinking lights above the mountains, they were more scattered than the objects we seen a few minutes before. These ones were actually blinking and the ones earlier were faint lighting that did not blink and were more in a straight line. All night I could hear jets in the sky, which again is not uncommon for this area, lots of air traffic here. Don't know if they were having a military exercise last night, because sometimes they do. Or if this had something to do with the lights in the sky.
