A glowing, white light, approximately 1000ft above me coming from SE traveling N. Floated like a spec of dust, but had enough light to cast shadows

When going out at approximately midnight, November 1st, to pee in the grass, I noticed that something was casting my shadow against the wall near me. I look to my left (South) and notice an intensely glowing white light, roughly 2-6 miles away and between 500ft-1000ft altitude. It’s moving in a strange hovering manner, with the looseness of a dust particle falling. Except it was moving roughly horizontally, easterly. I felt very scared and worried, and stood watching it for about 30-40 seconds before going inside. I soon fell asleep, but 45 minutes after this happened my friend described very briefly seeing strange lights before they turned off through the ceiling window.
