I walked out side to get some air and the sun had set maybe 20 minutes earlier. As I looked south down the street I saw two perfectly round things about the size of large beach balls flying up the street.the best way I can describe them is they appeared to be a solid surface that looked like a soft light came from the inside. They we're orange like an orange dreamsicle popsicle.One followed the other when they got to the intersection by my house they turned 45 degree's away from me and flew over my neighbors house without ever making a sound.
