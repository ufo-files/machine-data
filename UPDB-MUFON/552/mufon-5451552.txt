Saw object hovering. When I locked eyes onto the object it began to move and appeared to turn into an orb and faded/disappeared.

I was watching a helicopter fly in the area looking for a missing person near the Sugarloaf area of Kyburz, Ca. After the helicopter left the area I looked up and thought I saw the helicopter hovering closer to me. This object was silent and clearly was not the helicopter. As I squinted my eyes while looking at the object it began to move to the NE. The object changed into an orb and then vanished. I thought about the book “Missing 411” and became a little unnerved about the incident and felt compelled to share. I want to remain anonymous.
