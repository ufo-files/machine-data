Orb of light hidding, changing size above the clouds. Just above me.

Well, I'm from Venezuela, it was 2 - 3 -am , I was with two friends outside a friends house, waiting him to open the gate, we was there for about 30 min, I was anxious to get inside because it was danger, so I turn my back I don't remember why, and I look up to the sky, the sky was clear, just one low cloud passing by, in that exact moment I saw an orb decending just above the cloud, and when the tiny light was close to the surfice of the cloud it turns into a big circle, it was color white or blue, no sound, then few seconds later the light become tiny again and start ascending and I lost it, i keep watching the cloud to see if anything happen but nothing happen then. All the encounter last about 10 seconds, it was very quick but I remember all the details, when it was on the surface of the cloud and becomes big, was amazing.

It was very close of me, just above me, I don't know why, I was thinking if they abduct me or something but I don't remember having a missing time or anything, I know I'm not getting an answer of what I saw but I want people know, I want to put this on record.
