long cigar shaped object without wings, windows or tail fins

My mother called me last night from Missouri telling me about an object that both she and my father saw in the sky about 5pm on 2/20/2008. She described it to me as a white long cigar shaped object that did not have wings or a tail fin like on a normal aircraft. When she saw the object she ran inside to get the binoculars and returned outside the object was still there in the sky moving slowly to the north. When she looked through the binoculars she said it did not have any windows, was flying lower than an air craft and did not have a jet stream after it as it flew. I sked her if perhaps it was a blimp and her reply to me was that it wasn't as round as a blimp and was flying higher than a blimp normally would. I then asked her if it was a space shuttle knowing that one was supposed to have landed sometime yesterday and she again siad it had no wings and no tail fin. I was just curious to report her sighting to someone and see if anyone could give us an explaination or if any other individuals had reported a similar sighting. Any information you could give me on this would be greatly appreciated.
