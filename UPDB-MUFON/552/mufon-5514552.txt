{dr}EIGHT COMET TYPE UFOS DESCENDED, ONE ERRACTIC MOVING ABOUT IN FRONT OF OTHERS, SPONTANEOUSLY ALL DISAPPEARED AT HIGH SPEED.

1. ON MY WAY HOME FROM WORK WITH MY HUSBAND, WAITING FOR THE LIGHT TO CHANGE,
2. MY HUSBAND POINTED TO THEM AND SAID ELAINE LOOK FIREWORKS? OR METEORS! I SAID NO WAY THEY ARE NOT MOVING OR DISAPPEARING, LOOK AT THE ONE IN FRONT 4. MOVING AROUNG ZIG ZAGING LIKE DECIDING WHERE TO GO, AND AT THAT MOMENT THE ALL TOOK OFF INTO THE NORTH DIRECTION AT HIGH SPEED, 
3. LIKE FIRE BALLS BUT WITH A COMET LOOK TO THEM, THE COLOR OF GLOWING FIRE. A AIRPLANE HAD BEEN PASSING NOT FAR OFF TO THE RIGHT NE SKY.
5. EXCITED, SCARED, BAFFLED AT WHAT WE JUST WITNESSED. I REMEMBERED LOOKING TO MY RIGHT TO THE LADY DRIVER AND TOLD HER TO LOOK AT WHAT WE WERE LOOKING AT, AND SHE ALSO COULDNT BELIEVE IT! SHE SAID WHAT IS THAT?
6. IT WAS GONE SO FAST, UNDESCRIBABLE.
