{cak} I saw a huge thunder cloud flash twice and then a white orb flew from over the top of the cloud in an arch then curve to the south east where it disappeared.

Between 11:00 and 11:30 on Sat. August 11th I left my house walking to 7-11. I was heading north down the street when I heard a car door shut behind me. When I turned around I saw two cars parked engines running and the lights on. As I watched them drive off I saw a Huge cloud to the south west flash twice and then a huge white orb shot out from over the top of the cloud. It arched over the sky from west to east and then turned south east and disappeared. It was a huge bright white orb. I didn't see any trail of light and it wasnt fuzzy at all. It just floated realy fast through the sky. At first I said to myself what the hell was that. And then I said I just saw a ufo. I cant explain what I saw. It made the hair stand up on my arms. I will never forget what I saw. I have watched the sky all of my life and watch shows about ufos but never saw anything like this.
