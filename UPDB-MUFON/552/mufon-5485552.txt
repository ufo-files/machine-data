{jpg} 4 to 5 glowing spheres

IT WAS 3 AM. I WAS ON GUARD DUTY WHEN I SAW 4 TO 5 GLOWING SPHERES COMING DOWN THE VALLEY ABOUT 25 TO 30 FEET AWAY FROM ME . I JUMPED BEHIND A TREE AND WATCH THEN FOR ABOUT 3 TO 4 MINS. THEY MOVED IN A STRAIGHT LINE THEN HOVERED AND WENT BACK THE SAME WAY THEY CAME. I WAS GOING TO TELL THE DI THAT WAS ON DUTY BUT I THOUGHT HE MIGHT THINK I WAS NUTS. THIS HAPPEN AT FT KNOX KY ON BASE BY FIRING RANGE. THE SPHERES HAD NO SOUND AT ALL. JUST GLOWING BRIGHT AND COULD NOT SEE THOUGH THEM SCARED THE HELL OUT OF ME.
