{dr}In May 1968, observed UFO along with grandparents and sister.

My father drove my grandparents, sister, and me outside of Austin Texas on R.R. 2222. We were going there to launch my model rocket. After parking on side of road, dad and I walked about 100 feet into a field to launch the rocket. There was a problem with the rocket and Dad was checking it out. My grandparents and sister called to us to look across the road, there is something in the air.I turned around and saw a saucer shaped object above the trees about halfway up the hill across the road. The object was silver in color and I could see a shadow on the trees below it. The object slowly seemed to go up the side if the hill. I turned around and told Dad he needed to look at the thing across the road. When I turned back to look again, the object had disappeared. My sister said later that the object had shot straight up and was gone in an instant. Even after fifty years, my sister and I still remember the incident.
