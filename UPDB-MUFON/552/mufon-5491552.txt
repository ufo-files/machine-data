{cak} Bright white orb SW of Albuquerque

strangely enough, I was watching the Siruis documentary on net flicks and out of the corner of my eye I saw a bright orb through my house window. I studied it silently and notice that the light got brighter!Feeling a little insecure about what I was seeing, I told my fianc1, "There's a bright light in the sky right over there." He got up and looked out the window and say the same orb that I did! from my view at this point there was two orbs; each dimming and illuminating at their own independent pace. Our window faces the SW Deseret past albuquerque called "pajarito" desert. My fianc1 quickly went out side to get a better view. I followed him some what nervous of the sighting. I have grown up seeing orbs all my life, especially the orange and red ones. The last two new years have brought these red orbs straight to my door basically. Anyway, we go outside and we are still able to see one bright white orb that was extremely bright. I quickly called my father who lives next door and had been wanting to see these orbs for himself. As I was on the phone with him, I was able to get a photo of the orb. It disappeared shortly after I took a photo.
