UFO over the beach in Valencia, Spain

It was very late at night on my fourth night in Spain. Possibly jet lagged, I was still awake and went to the beach since our Airbnb was right on the beach. I was staring up at the stars after just getting into an argument with some friends. I looked up in the sky and asked the universe to help me. I had been drinking that night and couldn1t tell if I was believing my own eyes or not, so I started videoing. You can hear the waves on the beach in the background.
