{cak} three circular-orb like objects that were in a triangular pattern

At about 7:35 PM, my sister and I were driving back from Brentwood after doing some shopping. We had just turned onto Old Peytonsville Road, near the Goose Creek Inn, when we noticed three circular-orb like objects that were in a triangular pattern. They were a yellow-orange color, and it looked like they were planes landing. I had made the comment that I had not seen planes fly like that ever.

We made it where we were staying, in the Ellington Park Development and started to unload the car. I had grabbed a box when I saw one of the objects once more. I got my sister's attention, and told her that it had returned. 

We stared in awe, and I decided to grab my phone and started filming. The object disappeared at the end of my video. During all of this, my sister asked, "Are you seeing this too?"

All I could do was reassure her that yes, I was seeing it too. We were in awe and rather confused about what we had just seen.
