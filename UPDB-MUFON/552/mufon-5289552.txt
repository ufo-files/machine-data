Historical group sighting, 1964

5 of us (3 names forgotten) camping at Stirling Lake, Davie, Fla., when 2 side by bright white lights approached from far across lake, we took it for an airplane. Lights slowed and hovered momentarily directly above us, maybe 500 to 1000 ft, then separated and quickly sped off in opposite directions-one north and one south. They disappeared below their horizons. Completely silent entire time. We were frightened and one guy fainted. Had a lasting effect on me and my brother (awestruck). 
I document this here and I describe it in a book I just published "Censoring Mars and the Madness of Sheep", chapter 2, "The UFO's"- (chapter 2 attached) (I was unaware of this forum). Thanks for the opportunity to finally share.  [name removed/cms/rjl]
