Looked like a large star / venus, but 5 times as large as those. It was bright pulsing white / silver looking into the sun. Appeared to be hovering. Vanished suddenly.

We were driving into the sun down Valin St from Trim road at 5:40pm. It was a clear and bright day and was hard to look out the window because of the sun. My wife noticed the object and pointed it out to myself and my daughter, and we saw it easily. It looked like a large, radiant star, hovering and pulsating white and silver. It was a strange experience, because the sun was so bright, yet this UFO stood out easily as we shielded our eyes from the sun. We watched the object for about 2 minutes, until it vanished instantly. There was a passenger jet also in the sky about 20 degrees right and 20 degrees up from the object, traveling west and leaving a contrail. The size of the passenger jet appeared much smaller than the UFO. Its body's reflection and contrail reflection were clearly visibly and much less intense than the UFO.
