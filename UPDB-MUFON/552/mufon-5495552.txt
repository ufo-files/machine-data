I was outside my job talking to a friend. My friend asked my brother and I if that was an airplane because it seemed to be moving awfully fast. We both looked up and thought he was talking about the planet next to the moon. And then my friend said, "No, that one." We looked south and saw, what seemed to be a very bright star or planet, moving at high velocity. Then it stopped mid air and hovered for a few seconds. My brother said "No, that isn't a plane." We stood there watching this light make sharp 90 degree turns in multiple directions. This lasted for about 10 seconds. I was a little bit in awe, when I realized I had to record this with my Samsung Galaxy S 4 phone. By the time I started recording it, it had stopped making its drastic maneuvers. It hovered in the air and made a course correction back to the southwest. What you see in the video is the final moments of the object. The object flew off at very high velocity back to the west. It seemed as if it was falling but wasn't.
