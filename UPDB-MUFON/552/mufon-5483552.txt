Flying fireballs

In Summer 2009 my brother, a few friends and me was at the skateplace near the Dutch and Belgish border to skate and bike .
(The place behind the basketball place with coordinates: 50155'6.37"N 61 6'28.59"E)
Then we saw many fireballs at the west sky.
They was very big and near so i am sure, that they was under our atmosphere and they was no comets.
The bright balls were very quick and flew from north-west to south-west.
We was watching them a while but then we went home cause at this time i was 15 years old and it was late.
It would be very interesting, when somebody have an explanation.
