Large low flying silent sophisticated very bright (red&green) lights aircraft never seen anything like this in my life before this event

I was in my backyard letting my dog have her last pee for the night at about 10.30pm on the 22nd April 2015 when a very bright object in the sky caught my eye as it became visible above my 1 story house. It was a rather large triangular shaped flying object with bright red and green lights on the underneath which is the only thing I could see...I couldn't see any height of the flying craft. It flew silently across the perth sky from a south westerly to a north westerly direction where it became out of sight due to it's descend which at the rate it was steadily flying and the direction I would think it would either land or submerge into the indian ocean close to Scarborough beach. I witnessed this for at least 1 1/2 minutes and was able to get someone from my house to check it out too. It was very close to earth and an incredible sight I can't stop thinking about it as I know there must have been other witnesses.
