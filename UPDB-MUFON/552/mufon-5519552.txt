Bright light drops from sky hovers for a second then slight zig zag flyaway

I was standing at my upper driveway.I was talking to a friend on the phone and was commenting on how bright the moon was. We had a brief interlude from clouds. It had just become visable (on the rise). The moon was to my left. I looked at what I thought was Venus or Jupiter (in front of me, looking over my house) and was commenting on how freakishly bright it was and then it just dropped. I was freaking out! It stopped then shot southwest then zig zagged to a western flight path and was gone.
