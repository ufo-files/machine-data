Very large shape in clouds, no colour evident but had staight lines, square like with corners, with white glow around

Walking to the gas station and looked up at the sky. The clouds were moving really fast and noticed a shape that was stationery that the clouds seemed to be going around. It had straight lines and appeared to be square like. A white light glowed from the edges. Tried to see evidence of it being spot lights from the ground causing it but no evidence of that. It was completely still, no noise and there for at least 5 minutes as that is how long I observed it. It didn't move so I continued on doing what I was doing. Didn't look for it after that. Having been sharing the photo with family today who seem to think it could be a ufo.
