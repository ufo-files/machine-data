Rapid zigzagging motiion

1955 Sighting observed in July of that year during our family outing to a newly opened Drive-in movie theater located at 244, old Montauk highway, in Bridgehampton, LI, NY. The object was an exceptionally brilliant starlike object (brighter than Saturn) that was first observed directly over the movie screen at an angle of approximately 40 degrees. We were facing the screen and that put us facing generally speaking, to the west. The object rapidly descended making 3 zigzagging, right angle movements first to the left, then right and then left again until it disappeared behind the screen (angle at time was approximately 30degrees). Entire sighting was over in between 1 to 1.5 seconds (estimated). It was impossible to estimate the speed and/or distance per se from observers that including myself who was 7 years old at the time, my sister who was about 3 and my parents. My sister and I were sitting in the front seat of our family car and my parents were in the back. I did not list my sister because she was too young so the only witnesses were myself and my parents (both deceased).
