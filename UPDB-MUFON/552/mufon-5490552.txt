{nd} firewatch in bootcamp saw ufo called others,about five witnesses

End of June or 1st week in July 1963,while standing roving patrol/firewatch.saw a ufo, very similar like in Movie Forbidden Planet.It was totally silent at altitude of about 4-500ft,could see various portholes with outlines of human like heads staring out.There were 4-5 other sailors that witnessed this event, I do not remember but believe that I logged it in my watch log. The object just kept in straight line until disappeared over horizon.Just watched it in wonder with others
