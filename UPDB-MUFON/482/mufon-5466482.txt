Observed UFO, car stalled, second smaller UFO, approached high speed, circled left, larger UFO ascended left very high speed

Driving to friends place, came over the hill and saw the UFO, as car came down the hill engine stalled. I tried to exit the car butt my wife was in a state so stayed. Suddenly a smaller bright orange UFO came at high speed and circled the larger one several times, then shot off at great in a NW direction. There was no sound, I exited the car and stood for about two and a half minutes. The UFO ascended slowly to about 1000ft then shot away at great speed looking like a slight streak in the sky. I would like to have got closer for a good look as I was curious. I served in the Military and had not seen anything like these objects. I still don't know what they were, but the large one looked very similar to a few I have seen in your reports. I remained calm during the entire sighting, but my wife was upset and fearful, hysterical in truth.
