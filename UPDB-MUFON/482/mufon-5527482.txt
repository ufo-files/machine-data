stationary object with flashing lights

last night at 10 pm i walked outside and noticed something bright with flashing lights in the sky notheast of the city. it caught my attention because it was really clear out and could see all the stars really well and this thing did not fit in. it was bright and flashing. i went in and got my high powered binoculars and could see it really was flashing red and blue. i can only guess that it was five miles away, approximately. it just stayed there and never moved. i watched it about 20 minutes and then i went in. a few hours later i went back out and it was gone. it was too far away to take a picture of it or break out the hd vidcam. this was certainly not an airplane or a star.
