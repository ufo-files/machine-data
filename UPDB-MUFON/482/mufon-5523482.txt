2 Orange glowing lights that hovered around and then sped off

I was driving north on Morrish Rd. in Swartz Creek in between Grand Blanc Rd. and Hill Rd. I saw 2 orange glowing lights in the sky. They were probably 500 ft in the air. They were obviously not any sort of known aircraft, so I pulled over. One of the lights quickly decended down and then went back upwards. They both traveled to the north at a very fast pace and were soon out of visual sight.
