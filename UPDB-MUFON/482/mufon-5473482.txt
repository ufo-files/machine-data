A static, silvery unequalateral triangle.

I was at school as a child of around six to nine years old; it was break-time - I think lunchtime - and I was in the playground. It was a fairly clear day with blue sky, but some minor cloud coverage. I just saw it up there; I don't remember how I noticed it. At first I thought it was a plane, but it was static. It simple hovered there, seemingly reflecting from its metallic surface, the light of the sun. I think I drew it to the attention of other children around me at the time.It may have been while I was looking away that the object disappeared; I looked up again and it was gone, but it leaves a very clear impression in my mind.
