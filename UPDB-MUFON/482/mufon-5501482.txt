{dr}Very Bright/Big Teal Object flew directly over my house!

While watching TV on June 30th just after midnight a family member began calling me to come outside onto the porch. She initially noticed a bright teal (blue/green) object through our TV room window. The extremely bright qualities of the craft caught her attention. When I arrived the object was moving in a southeast direction. It also like fluttered different colors, red and white, but always returned back to the teal color.It was very big and bright. It moved in a constant speed not far above the tree lines. I wasn't sure what to think, it was much bigger than a plane. The colors and size is what really freaked me out! They were so sharp. The object continued until I lost it behind the tree lines.

I documented this on a website forum community, minutes after the sighting. Please let me know if you would like the web address. I'm reporting now because the next day I found an article describing the exact object a few towns over. This wasn't normal, and I dunno if I should be excited or worried.
