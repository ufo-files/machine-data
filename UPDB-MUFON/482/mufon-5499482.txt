Looked like stars buzzing around the sun. Changed directions. Some bigger and brighter than others.

I went outside and looked at the sun at around 12:00 pm Sept 7, 2014. I noticed these bright orbs around the sun right away and called my Wife to come and verify. She say them right away. We blocked the sun out with our roof. Just before we were blinded by the sun, the roof protected our eyes and you could see these star like dots in the sky. Dozens of them flying around. Some would get brighter. They would ZIP away. From all directions. There was a lot of commercial airplane activity and it looked like some of the orbs would go and check out the planes. It was incredible. We have many videos of this event. This went on for over 3 hours. I called many people and they saw them as well. I contacted Nick Pope in the UK and he verified the anomalies from my video as well. We watched the orbs for over an hour then we had to go to hockey practice. When we came back, they were still in the sky. I was EXTREMELY excited. My Wife and kids were as well. We feel really fortunate to have witnessed this event. I will be looking again today. I left MUFON a phone message at the time this was happening.
