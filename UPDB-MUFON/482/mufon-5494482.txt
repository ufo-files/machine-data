The UFO photos I took on October 19th are not at all spectacular, at least not in a visual sense. Yet they are quite remarkable in another regard, in that I don't remember taking these photos. I also don't remember sorting through them, and deleting the dud photos from that day; the blurry ones and the black shots. So, as you can imagine, I was rather surprised to find these photos on my computer, with missing sequence numbers among the string of photos for that day. Along with the included photo samples from Oct 19th, I'm also including one from the day before; taken on Saturday, Oct 18th. This is a photo I refer to as "snake face." At the very least, it looks like a reptilian face in the clouds. The point is, I remember taking photos the day before, but not on the day after, Oct 19th. As I look at the other photos taken on Sunday, the 19th, it appears there is some sort of physics transaction going on. It looks to me like a "gather up and go" moment is taking place. Oh well, have a look and decide for yourself what you think is going on.
