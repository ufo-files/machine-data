Huge brilliant lights

Driving home at dark we’re on a new road that boarded Ft Knox, KY. Three huge brights started descending towards us. I thought it was an airplane though all we could see were these blinding lights. I said that I thought it was going to land on us. Then it was stationary over the road. Two more light turned on, on either side of the three. Then, as in one second, they simultaneously darted down to the side I’d the road and all lights and evidence of it was gone. In the daytime I looked at spot where they went down and it was a depression of weeds and tree saplings. It bordered on Ft Know property line. It wasn’t until years later I realized there was no sound. In fact I felt numb after seeing it.
