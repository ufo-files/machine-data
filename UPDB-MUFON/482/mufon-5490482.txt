{nd} Two silent balls of light directly overheard going South in Chicago.

CHICAGO 1957-58.
Playing ball in the Schoolyard, I noticed two bright objects (Silent,) moving in a Southern direction! Maybe travelling at 300 MPH., they continued to move until, at about 10% above ground level, they merged with about 3 other bright objects, whereupon they circled each other, changed colors, Green, reddish, and maneuvered that way for about 3-4 minutes, after which they just disappeared! I coaxed a friend to go with me to the nearest phone booth (Ancient history,) and I called either the weather Bureau or some other agency! The woman who answering the call took a minute to consult someone, then responded by saying that "There were NO reports of anything!" 

HENDERSON, NV., 2002-2003.
Early morning about 8:00 o'clock or so, I was waiting in my vehicle, in my Daughter's driveway, when I looked up and saw two round, silent objects, clearly distinguishable as round, with no Fuzziness whatsoever! Maybe 5000 feet, but fairly close to the ground! The startling thing about the event was that the 1st. white Object was CAMOUFLAGED, yes, CAMOUFLAGED on the underside like a Military Vehicle, going in a slightly arced Northwest direction! The 2nd. object trailed behind the 1st. Camouflaged object, as if watching the performance of the first object! This was just west of T's building, but still in the Henderson boundary! The trailing Disc was pure white, and had to be 20-25 feet in Diameter! 

Note.. It was wobbling and going quite slow! It had to be 20-25 feet in length, but since I was viewing it from the side, I only assumed that it was round! 

Then there was the Hard, stone-like object I actually "Ripped Off" a "Fleshy Outgrowth" from under my left armpit! I had never noticed an Outgrowth there before, so I was really surprised! This was in June of 2015, and I always washed my armpits, so I would have noticed something there earlier! Maybe another time I will go into a little more detail, but I am sure I said enough already!
