{dr}Bright light over park lake bath michigan

I was fishing with a friend at around 10 PM at park lake in bath michigan. All of a sudden a bright circle of light hit the water. It was around 30 feet in diameter. It looked like a helicopter search light but there was no sound. It slowly moved towards us for about 5 minutes. It started about 50 ft away from us and move to about 20 ft. We couldn't see anything above the light. Then we saw a flash of light above us and then again on the horizon east of us. About 5 minutes later a helicopter followed the same path that the lights headed.
