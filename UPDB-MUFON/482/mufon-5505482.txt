At least 12 red orb like lights hovering in treeline then ascending and making geometric patterns

I was tidying my Moms kitchen and went to straighten the covers on the 2 chairs by the window. I looked out the window and noticed 6 red lights above the mountain side facing north east. They were large enough that I could make out a shape. They were large orbs. I looked at the time as I thought it was oddly early to see such a sight. It was 10:15pm. The red orbs seemed to be placing themselves in to patterns. I thought it looked like constellation shapes. After I got over my surprise, I ran and got my husband from next door. Praying they wouldnt leave before I could get him a window. We ran to our sons bedroom window because the view from there was ideal. We also brought our telescope but decided that it was easier not to use it at that point. We saw at least another 6 to 8 again starting by hovering in the treeline, ascending into the sky at airplane level (maybe slightly closer), then purposely moving into various angles. When a new orb moved up another orb would flicker, move into another angle and then disappear. It was all very methodical and purposeful. No random movements at all. No smoke or light streaks, just simply vanishing. The orbs eventually stopped at 10:49pm. I have tried to look for the lights each night since but have not had the good fortune of seeing them again.
