{rjl} V shaped , numerous lights

On 10/18/19, at 2203 hrs while driving E/B off of the Verrazano bridge. I observed approximately 6 lights over Staten Island ( the south beach area) . I looked away to watch the road . When I looked again there were 10 lights , but in a V formation. I took a photo with my phone and continued E/B . I observed several people on the side of the road standing on the grass and some standing on their bumpers taking photos of the objects. Which made me feel somewhat relieved that I wasn’t seeing things!
