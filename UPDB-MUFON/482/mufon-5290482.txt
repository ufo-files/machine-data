Winnemucca Nevada Sighting

Traveling West bound along I-80 at Mile Marker 186 I saw a bright lumicent object about 40 degrees above horizon. The time was 4:45pm Pacific Time and I was on the phone (hands free) with my grandfather. The object was reflecting sunlight at sunset and at first I thought it was an airliner or maybe a Google Baloon, etc. As the minutes passed an the light receded it became aparent that the object was cylindrical in shape. I am guessing the distance is well over a mile from the vantage point but was very large. It was dark grey/black and appeared to be stationary form my vantage point. If it was moving it was slow. I lost visual when I exited I-80 in Winnemucca as the exit faces the wrong direction, I called my wife to view the object from our house as we have a vantage point on our second floor balcony that would allow a look through our telescope (4:52pm Eastern). When she went to investigate she could not see the object (within 2 minute of call). I arrive home at 4:58pm and was unable to reaquire visual and lighting levels were dropping fast. While we were talking back there was a brief flash in the area from an unknown source, low intensity but witnessed by both
