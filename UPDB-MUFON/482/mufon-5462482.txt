{cak} large bright light crossed from horizon to horizon in less than 10 seconds

I walked outside to check my horses in the pasture probably around 10 PM or later. It was a warm summer night and all was quiet. Suddenly a bright object appeared way up in the sky toward the west. It crossed over the sky in less thank 10 seconds,traveling straight across. I never saw an airplane do that. It was silent and quite bright. I kept staring at it to try to see a shape, but it moved so fast and only showed a bright white light. My horses didn't notice. I was concerned that if we built an airplane that could travel that fast then it could be annoying to us on earth to have huge items flying over us. But I was not frightened, just curious. It finally went over the east horizon and was gone.
