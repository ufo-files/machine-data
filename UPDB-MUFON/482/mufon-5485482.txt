{dr}8-10 Orbs/Spheres moving slowly

Walking up E. Montague to eat lunch, heard a prop plane fly over and I looked up and saw the plane and then what I thought was a flock of birds and then realized that the objects were moving too slowly to be a bird and too large. They then slowly rearranged their pattern in the sky and 3 came together to make a triangle and all rotated/changed at the same time and reflected the sun very brightly. I felt very calm and at peace, almost indifferent to what I was seeing. The objects faded/dissolved into the sky over about 7-10 seconds. I attempted to take video of the objects on my phone but they faded before I could get it recording.
