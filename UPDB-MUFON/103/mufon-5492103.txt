{jpg} First set of Orange orbe hovered over Atlantic Ocean and then fell to the ocean with no splash or impact into the ocean surface from my viewing point.The second set of six orbes hovered over the ocean and then suddenly disappeared.

When the sighting occurred, I was flying with my father in our single engine airplane and an instructor giving my father a check ride. We were flying inland a quarter mile from the shore of the Atlantic Ocean. When I looked at the ocean, I noticed six orange glowing orbes hovering over the ocean. The first set of orbes stayed stationary and then fell towards the ocean surface before disappearing. The second set of orbes appeared suddenly and then disappeared without any motion such as falling like the first set. I considered the orbes flares being shot from a fighter jet. I later found out that there was a fighter jet off the coast in a training session. Even though that seemed possible for the set of orbes that fell towards ocean, the second set of orbes is what made me wonder. They did not fall but stayed absolutely stationary before disappearing into thin air. My reaction was to remain calm and alert my father and the instructor. My father couldn't look because he was busy with the flight controls, but the instructor also saw the orbes. We had no earthly clue what we were witnessing. We lost sight of the last set of objects objects when they suddenly disappeared.
