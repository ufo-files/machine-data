Pulsating Light Above Signal Peak

Very large pulsating light hovering above Signal Peak on the edge of the Sacaton Indian Reservation. I have lived in my home over 20 years and spent many many night outside enjoying the sky. I have seen the signal lights on top of the foothill and this was easily four times the size and hovering over the mountain not stationary on the ground. It did not move and no sound at all during my observation of it. Not a plane or helocopter which I have seen and heard many times going over my home. This was very strange!
