{dr}Three Round Circular Objects Observed in Western Sky Moving South to North.

I witnessed an unusual aeronautical phenomena. Star gazing, while facing the west, directly above me moving from South to North was a bright glowing object with about the brightness of Jupiter in the night sky. It dimmed then brightened again and faded away as it moved north. Seconds behind it were two of the same objects side by side. They behaved in the same way. Whatever it was it was not natural and was flying in formation.
