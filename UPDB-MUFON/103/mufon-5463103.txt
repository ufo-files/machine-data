{rjl} UFO zoomed out of sky and hovered in front of me

This happened to me in 1957 when I was 4 years old. We lived in an isolated country home 20 miles from the nearest town. There was our home and a train depot across the street. The nearest neighbors lived about a mile in either direction. I was playing alone outside as I always had done. Suddenly an object zoomed down from the sky and hovered right in front of me. Being only 4 years old I didn't know what to do so I just stood there looking at it. I could hear what sounded like muffled whispers, but either I could not understand what they were saying or I have forgotten. After a short time (I can not remember how long this lasted) it zoomed back up into the sky and disappeared. I remember going into the house, walking into the living room and stamping my feet on the floor saying out loud "this must be a dream, it has to be a dream" But I was wide awake. I can't remember what happened next nor remember telling mom about it. At that age I knew nothing about UFO's. Our TV back then could only get 2 channels. All I ever watched were Saturday morning cartoons. I remember the object look similar to the shape of the Warner Brother's logo that was on during the cartoons. I suppose that is all my 4 year old mind could make any sense out of what I saw. I haven't seen any UFO's since then. It gives me some sense of relief to be able to report this to someone.
