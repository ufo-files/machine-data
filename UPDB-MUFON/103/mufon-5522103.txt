Randomly fluctuating red and white lights

I was looking out a kitchen window last evening when I noticed a strange light in the sky-off in the distance, that was bright white; I thought it might be an approaching plane, but it was just hovering. It then began to flash randomly, red, red, white,. white, red, white, etc. It went just red for a while. It moved abruptly, hovered, stopped, then changed direction. It went on for about 15 minutes. I thiought this cant be a plane, it is not moving like a helicopter. It then went over the tree line, but it was like it was floating up and down gently. It was off in the distance; it was hard to tell how far. It was really strange. I wasnt outside so I couldnt tell if it was making a noise or not, but planes flying by made noise that I could hear. This was not making audible sounds that I could tell. It flew in from the southwest, turned, and began heading in a northwesterly direction. Then it disappeared.
