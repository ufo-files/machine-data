I was driving down Hwy 47 towards St. Clair. It was 3:54am. It was nearly pitch black with nothing but farmland surrounding me. I noticed a very bright light in the sky moving from left to right back and forth. I heard no sound at all, and I would have to say it was brighter than any commercial aircraft that I have ever seen. I slowed down until I came to a stop. I started to video tape. I noticed it moving towards the road and then towards me. It did stay stationary in the sky for a few seconds once I had stopped, and I was able to see its features. It was a rounded rectangular shape, very bright and glowing. It was blinking. There was a transparent perimeter that I could see it was a very low glow, so it was still visible. There were windows. 
I started to feel anxiety. When it started moving in my direction, I took off very quickly. I noticed a strong burning smell once I came closer to it. It lasted about a minute. I think it descended because I saw a bright flash of light on my left drivers side close to the ground (50-54 second mark). When I arrived home and viewed the video, I noticed a 2nd craft in the sky on the upper left hand side of the sky at the time that the other craft descended past my door.
