Star-like object emitting red, green and blue light

Looked out kitchen window. I saw a star twinkling in the north west sky about 40 degrees above the horizon. I thought it was odd to be twinkling so brightly and quickly. After continuing to look at it, I noticed colors: red, green, blue and white. I grabbed some small binoculars and looked again, Same result. I asked my wife to look: she saw the same thing. The object never moved.
