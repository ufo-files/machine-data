Single row formation of multiple lights flying

This morning between 5:05 and 5:11 AM while taking my dog out to the bathroom I looked up and saw up to 50 different lights flying in a row moving from south to north. We have no obstructions blocking the sky view and it was a very clear dark sky.

We live in NM and looking East and up at about a 70 degree angle, toward the Sangre de Cristo mountains I could see a string of about 25 to 50, star like, lights flying very quickly from south to north toward Espanola or Truchas. 

The lights seemed to span the entire sky. The string of lights were nearly from one horizon to the next. As lights disappeared going north, more appeared in the south. A very long string of lights flying straight in one direction.

After going in and calling my husband he came out but they were gone. After they left there was the sound of a fighter air craft going over in the direction of the lights.
