{jpg} Object emmiting light switching between hovering and moving

I was at South Beach Miami at the time of the event around 830pm. I was laying on my beach towel looking up at the sky when I saw an object moving in a strange way. At first I thought it may be a plane because I was close to the airport, but it was not moving like a plane. It would hover, and then slowly move in any direction, sometimes in a circular type way. It was too far away to really tell the shape of the object. At first glance you would think it was a bright star. There was also a smaller and dimmer object near it that also appeared to be moving and hovering. I watched them for two hours hoping I would catch it fly away into night sky or something to affirm my suspicion. There were a couple times the object moved quite fast, but for a short distance. A little after 1030pm I had to leave because the cops were shooing people off the beach (it closes at 10). I started packing my things and I looked back up at the sky and the object was gone. I started walking toward the city and I saw the two objects moving toward the city as well at a fairly decent speed. I continued to follow the objects until they stopped to continue hovering. I had to leave because my phone was almost dead. I was able to catch a little video, but it is pretty bad quality.
