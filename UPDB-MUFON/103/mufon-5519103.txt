Filming plane flying over and noticed objects in playback

I was out at 5:49 filming a jet fly over at a high altitude, Filmed it for 31 seconds, I was looking at the playback the next day in slow motion when I saw these two orb, or sphere like objects shoot past plane at incredible speed. They were traveling so fast it is no wonder I did not see them fly by through the view finder. I down loaded and put each still frame of event in single movie and will submit it here. First part is uncut film of objects, and I have 50 still images in sequence of the event itself.
