Diamond shape making a whooping sound like a helicopter but no wind

I was parked on a dirt road east of area 5 at approx 0225 hours. while parked with the engine off I heard a whooping sound to the rear of my vehicle . I looked out the drivers side window and saw a diamond shaped craft hovering. The craft had a redish blue light located in the middle of the craft on the bottom. at this time I retreaved my issued M-16 and I pointed it at the craft. the craft then turned to its right and at a rapid speed disapeared into the night sky.
