3 bright white orbs in perfect triangle formation about large star size no noise moving west to north east

I was with a local astro club stargazing , there was about 10 people ,and we were in a members field , we had just noticed a few planets had come into view jupiter and Saturn and later mars ,this was about 23.30 - 23.30 GMT on sunday 22nd july 2018  I then saw 3 bright white orbs in a perfect triangle formation , the orbs were fairly big large star size , and it was moving from west to northeast at a slightly faster pace of a satilite , there was no noise and it did not flash or change colour , it then went into cloud and disappeared , we all saw it and none had seen anything like it before or could explain what it was , we were stunned but excited at what it could be ,most members don't believe in ufos etc they are hardcore stargazers and that's all , but we were all speechless .
