V shaped object hover out side of city

I was driving home........ And I looked up and saw a massive "v" shaped object hovering out side of the city....... There were 6 bright white on the bottom. 3 on each side of "V". It was a dark color black or drak grey...... I saw for at least 30 seconds it may have there longer..... As I was watching there wasnt any noise, just light traffic....... Then is just vanished...... After that I was still driveing and looking and I could see planes where the object was..i counted at least 10 or more planes and they were what looked like searching. When I got home the planes where still there and were there for like an hour ..... I wish I was not driving so I could filmed it and taken pictures...... But hands down the coolest thing I have seen..... I always did believe in that stuff..... But not now I am 100% positive....
