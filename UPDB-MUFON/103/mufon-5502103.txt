Blue neon ball came up from the treeline at 45 degrees hovered, did an erratic series of maneuvers , hovered again then dove straight down at an incredible speed.

I had worked late that night and my mother had come by to make me a late supper. After we had finished and she was leaving to go home I walked her to her car. We talked a bit and as we stood by the side of her car, we both saw this brilliant blue ball come over the treeline at a 45 degree angle. It was traveling north to south at an incredible speed. It then stopped, hovered for about 3 seconds and then did an erratic series of maneuvers that was almost to fast to follow. It then stopped, hovered for another 3 seconds and then burst straight down behind the treeline again. My mother and I just looked at each other stunned. I started to say over and over,"What was that?" Mom said," That was no airplane!" and she looked a little worried. We looked to the spot where we had seen the object and a small plane flew through the same area where the object had been seconds before. We watched another ten minutes but saw nothing further. The object was a brilliant blue and pulsated when it hovered.
