Object in sky makes massive sharp angle turns.

Outside on my porch on 09/11/10 at 925 pm, zip 02324. Looking up about 30 degrees above horizon south, south south west saw object in sky that appeared to be a plane in the distance. Object was traveling north, northeast at a steady rate when it suddenly cut 90 degrees "down" towards the ground, then again turned at a sharp 45 degrees to return north, northeast. it then disappeared from sight.
