Stop unusual vertical cloud observed ufo and Angel

Atop an unusual vertical cloud formation which initially gained my attention I saw a hovering craft like a side lying teardrop, sharp forward point to large pear shape rear. It was without wings, no rotors, no engines. The underneath was a deep red/brown color, darker out to the edges. The complete edge was white like a halo effect. The cloud top actually was an angelic figure holding its wings out in front like a defensive measure whilst holding out a sword like weapon in its right hand aimed at the unidentified craft. On my second picture the craft and angelic figure disappeared in an instant. The two pictures were about two seconds apart.
