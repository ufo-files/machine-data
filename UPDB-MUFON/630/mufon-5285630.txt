Craft casually flew by while taking video of hummingbird.

I was on my porch watching the hummingbird that comes on a daily basis and decided to take a video that day because he was extra vocal. Upon reviewing the video I noticed something in the sky that caught my eye. I paused the video and zoomed in to find a large Saturn like shaped object that I had never seen before. It was large, round, dark, except the bottom portion I could see was mostly a glowing green color. I also noticed another craft in a still shot, but was nowhere quite as detailed. This was my first sighting and with the detail of the green illuminated bottom, I’m sure this is either from another planet, or some black project craft that has not be released to the public.
