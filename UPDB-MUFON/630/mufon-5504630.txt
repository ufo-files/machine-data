{kt}Small spiraling black hole

Small spiraling "black hole", caused distortion of the space around it and was spinning like a whirlpool. Would move erratically at about the speed of a jet and then hover. After about ten minutes it started ascending into the clouds much faster than it was moving before and I lost sight of it. I was with my brother and my niece at the time and we all witnessed it.
