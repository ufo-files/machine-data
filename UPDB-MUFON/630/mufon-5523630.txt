Strange objects in the sky that disappeared and reappeared several times.

I was delivering pizzas on Sunday the 2nd of October in Flower Mound Tx driving north on FM 2499 at 12:18PM.It was a totally cloudless day in North Texas and nothing in the sky besides the normal DFW airport traffic which I am used to when over the horizon I see a group of objects,round in shape and highly reflective in the bright sun.They seemed to hover motionless many miles away.When suddenly as when a school of sardines turns to there sides in the ocean and change color,the objects seem to turn and disappear.I thought this was just my eyes playing a trick on me when I rolled up to the stop light at FM 2499 and FM 3040 a couple of seconds later, then the objects all in unison turned back reflecting the sun and becoming visible again.Awestruck I sat there and watched them move all in unison a 1/4 of a mile or so and then turn,all in unison again,and disappear once again.This repeated itself one more time and then after the last time they were visible,they were gone for good.It really shook me up,but the fear quickly faded away and now I am in wonder for what these objects were and where they came from.I tried to tell myself they were birds at first,because the way they moved gave them them the appearance of being living creatures,not metal objects.But this simply cannot be the case.These objects were perfectly round in shape disappeared into thin air all in unison.Its been almost 24 hours now and I still cant believe I actually saw this.
