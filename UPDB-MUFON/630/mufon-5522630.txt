I own a half mile airstrip with a pond next to it.This craft at about 5 mph at around treetop level followed the airstrip from north to south.

Noticed a dark grey triangle about one hundred feet across with different colored lights follow the 1/2 mile airstrip 900 feet from my house.The craft had no sound and moved in a very straight line until it got to the pond where it turned and continued out of sight at 4 pm Nov.1st.A red flashing light towards the middle bottom was flashing and around the craft were different colors that did not flash.The cat did a belly crawl inside the house for at least 10 minutes after the sighting.Also an orange ball the size of a beach ball followed the craft,about 500 feet behind in a straight line with the craft.The reason I saw the craft was the cat jumped up on the bay window sill and acted very differently which made me look in the direction of the cat.This is when I noticed movement coming from the north side of the airstrip.You could not miss the sighting once you looked out of my office window over the deck.When I first saw the triangle figure and the different colored lights around the craft and the way it moved just above the treetops there was no question at what I was looking at.There is nothing in our inventory of flying machines that would float at such a crawl and emit no sound at all.When the craft made a slow turn over the pond I lost sight of the craft because of the 50ft or so over the ground.
