I see at night 8-10 orange-yellow lights blinking sequentially move rapidly to the right then straight down then straight up then to the right again

My husband Caleb and I went to our local taco bell drive thru after he got off work late. We had our 9 month old and our 2 year old in the back asleep in their car seats. Right after we order our food I see a large orange-yellow light sitting still to the north just above the tree tops across the street. The light is the size of a large passenger plane when their descending to land. I actually thought it was a passenger plane descending at first which confused me because we don't live near a commercial airport. Then out of the one large light 8-10 slightly smaller lights the same color came out of it into straight line. (My husband sees it at this point.) The line moves rapidly to the NW then sharply moves straight down, then straight up. Moves rapidly to the west slightly more then disappears. The entire time the lights blinked sequentially. The entire event lasted around 8 seconds. The sky was dark with no clouds. We couldn't tell how large or how high it was or how far away it was. It happened very quickly. We couldn't tell how far it traveled before disappearing either.
