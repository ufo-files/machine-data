Baseball sized yellow light in sky moved from right to left in front of me as I was driving on Veterans Pkwy.

Driving home from grocery shopping about around 8pm central time moving east on Veterans Pkwy. Just below the beginning of the wind shield above the steering wheel in front saw a yellow light about the size of my fist or baseball that moved from my right to my left for a few seconds and disappeared. It was clear night. There were street lights (halogen lights) on Veterans Pkwy in my route. I thought may be it was the street light but i observed several street lamps after that and i did not see the same pattern of that ball of light moving from my right to left.
It all happened very fast. I don't remember the exact time but it has to be some time between 7:37pm when i made the last call to my wife from the grocery store and 8:29 when i randomly noticed time at home.
