cube shaped object viewed from airplane with dark vapor like trail

I was on Ryanair 605 from Budapest to Copenhangen on 18 May 2019. Noticed object out of plane window expelling a black vapor trail that eventually dissipated. The object was heading towards the plane on the right hand side. At first I assumed an aircraft was having difficulties in flight, but quickly realized it was something else and reached for my cell phone to take picture. During the fraction of a second it took me to lean forward, the object made a 90 degree turn away from the plane and, as it was directly across from me at that point, I noticed it actually was generating two black trails. I thumbed my phone to the picture app and when i looked back, it had again made a 90 degree turn and was pacing the plane. I managed to get three pictures of the object before it just disappeared.
