Took picture of clouds and 6 objects are in my image!

I went out on my deck to get a picture of the silky clouds flowing over the distance hills in front of the Great Continental Divide. When I went back inside and looked at the images there were 6 objects in the sky that I caught in the picture. I never saw these objects, never heard a sound. I took two pics back to back and the second pic does not have the 6 objects in it. There are however two objects in the second image that were no longer there after the two pictures were taken. I live at 9000 foot elevation in the Front Range mountains West of Colorado Springs. I was told there were no Military crafts up here when these were taken. I do not feel they were far from me, but since I never actually saw them, I am unsure how far away they may have been.
