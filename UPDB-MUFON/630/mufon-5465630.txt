a strong white light and stargate type that then dissolved into two parts and then disappeared

I describe an episode that happened in 2010 at home.
Given that, with my family, we do not live in civilian but military homes.
We were already in bed, my daughter was sleeping in the cradle, placed to the left on my side. Dampers turned down throughout the house, no lights on, only the TV, my husband and I were watching the door-to-door program.
I was on my left side when, with the tail of my eye, I saw a white mass of about 1 meter long and about 30/40 cm high. It floated above the wardrobe, did not touch the wall or the ceiling. It gave off no glow, although it was a shining white, almost neon and with a depth in its interior. It did not show absolutely.
I did not move, fearing I could provoke a reaction. My husband was still and the next morning, when I asked him, he told me he had not seen anything.
I remember exactly how long it was floating there to the point that it split into two and then crumbled and dissolved. I was not afraid. I looked to understand what I am ...
