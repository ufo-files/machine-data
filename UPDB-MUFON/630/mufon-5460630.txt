{rjl} I row of what looked like stars in a straight line with a glowing beam around them appeared slowly then moved west and disappeared.

My 2 friends and myself decided to go stargazing tonight at a field near by. After seeing a great shooting star and having an amazing night we were looking at the stars when we all took notice of a line of stars that appeared slowly out of nowhere. The star looking lights had a faint glowing beam encompassimg them in a straight row. We all pointed and were mesmerized. The line of light encompassed stars moved west until it disappeared in the light pollution.
