Around 8:00 PM last night (12-10-15) my mother and I were outside looking at the stars when we noticed what looked to be a sphere like object in the sky north of Olean, NY. In person, this thing was huge! It looked like the quintessential "flying saucer." This object appeared to be spinning with blinking red and yellow lights. This object hoovered in the air for about five minutes and then slowly drifted south. I attempted to get this on video but my phone does not record the best quality videos in the dark. However, I am attaching my video to this thread anyway. When observing the video you can see the circular outline of this object through three blinks lights that slowly form. Unfortunately, my phone was unable to capture the red blinking lights that were on the object as well. Please be aware that we were so startled by what we saw that there is some minor offensive language in the video. Thank you!
