I witnessed a UFO traveling West/Southwest shortly after sundown on June 13th, 2015. I saw a UFO right over the powerlines (which are approximately only 75 feet tall) which seemed to barely cross over that height across Roswell Road in Sandy Springs, Georgia. There was a visible glow around the aircraft for a split second as it crossed over the road. The color was a yellow/amber/streetlight color which embraced the structure and edging of the entire craft. There was a trail behind it as the craft's RAM (radar absorbent material) was temporarily disabled and the UFO became visible. This craft made a distinctly disgusting humming sound at a low/whizzing vibration. It is a really ominous sound and I heard someone inside this restaurant I was parking cars at inside drop their silverware on their plate loudly as I exclaimed "oh my...what the...". The aircraft disappeared as quickly as it came as it crossed over the other side of Roswell Road, just before Hilderbrand Dr.
