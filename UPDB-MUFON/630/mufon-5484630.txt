{cak} two to six objects in sky

Went outside and was looking around and saw a light above the trees that were about 2 to 3 blocks away. The light was steady the dimmed, then another next to it lite up and dimmed and brightened. I video taped this for a while. Looking through the camera I could see more than the two objects. Farther away I saw 5 then a 6th one show up and then a seventh show up. I thought they were being recorded but it did not show up probably because of distance. They had different colors blue red orange. My wife was with me watching as well. In my video the lights on the right are street lights. I went inside for a while then came back out and saw the two closer objects go up in the air higher and farther to the left then watched them fly to the ground fast! Then I figured it was over with and they landed. But I went back in to grab my video again and came out and they rose up and disappeared.
