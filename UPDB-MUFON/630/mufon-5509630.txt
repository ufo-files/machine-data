{kt}Dark Orange, Round Object Moving Quickly & Silently Through The Sky

The entire family was out watching fireworks from our back yard and a few minutes into the fireworks my father-in-law and I saw a strange, glowing object moving quit quickly in the sky. I then pointed it out to my husband who also witnessed the event. The object was dark orange in color and did not blink. The shape was either sphere or oval, I could not be certain. it seemed to be coming from the South and heading North along the Mississippi River. The event lasted about a minute and then went out of our sight. It seemed to cover a lot of airspace in a short amount of time. I would be very interested to see if anyone else in or around the Muscatine, Iowa area saw this object as well.
