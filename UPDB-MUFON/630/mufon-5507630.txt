{nd} Black ball moving fast over the mountains toward waldo canyon.

I was driving on cimmaron and highway 24 toward woodland park direction and was stop at a red light at the intersection of 24 and 21 street getting ready too turn left on 21 street. I seen this black ball that was moving at a steady speed but fast over the waldo burn scar area in Colorado springs, Colorado. It wasnt a balloon because it was moving straight then made a turn in a head wind coming over the mountains. We had wind gust up to 50 plus mph that day. I first notice this object because it was in my main line of sight. The first thing that came to my mind about this object was it was different than anything I seen before. This object was black and it was still day light outside. It seem to be under control and made a turning move over the mountains. I kept telling my daughter do you see that black ball moving in the sky. She said she seen it also. The object just vanish in the area.
