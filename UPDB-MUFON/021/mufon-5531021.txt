Round Amber/ Orange

Sitting on the deck with my brother, his girlfriend, county offical, son, mysself. My brother turn his head and said look at this, what is it. I walked to the rail and saw a glowing amber/ orange object, a few seconds later another one, and then another. No sound.They were the same distance apart and traveled the same speed. I walk to the drivway and saw the last one which turned to the East and after a few seconds shot off and was gone.The first 2 that we saw were no where in sight. About 15 mins later neighbors came over and we told them what we saw they saw the same thing, but said there where 5.We only saw the 3. All together there are 10 people that saw them that I know of. I'm pretty sure that anyone traveling on I96 would have seen them also, They were not that high up.
