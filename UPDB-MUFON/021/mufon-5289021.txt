slow moving silent triangular craft

we were facing southeast heading back into the house when we saw just above the treetops two bright white lights heading towards us very slowly. we assumed it was a small plane but there was no sound so we watched it approach. as it came closer there was a flashing white beacon between the two white lights. we often have small planes fly over our property at just above 500 ft and the object appeared to be at a similar altitude. the object flew directly over us extremely slowly. approximately half the speed of the small planes that fly over our property. before the object passed over us a third fixed white light became visible in the rear as bright as the front two lights. As the object passed over us we noted that the flashing beacon was in the center of the three other lights. we watched the craft pass directly over us and disappear behind the trees. All four lights remained visible after it passed over us, which we thought was odd since airplane headlights that we have seen before do not remain visible once the plane has passed over. we normally would have considered this a low and slow flying small plane, however we are reporting this as there was no sound from the craft and it passed directly over us low enough that the engine noise should have been quite loud.
