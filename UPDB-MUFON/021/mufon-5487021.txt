Bright light missing time

My two children and I were travelling from Poochera, South Australia on the A1 highway around 6.30pm I could see the sun setting but still light when all of a sudden the car filled with the brightest warm light. I thought it was a truck on high beam but when I looked in the rear view mirror the light was to bright for me to see anything so I repositioned my mirror down as it was blurring my vision. All of a sudden I saw two headlights coming towards me and it became pitch black. I asked the kids( ages 7 & 10) if they could see where the truck went as we were on a highway with no exits or off ramps. I stopped the car. We somehow lost time (7 hours) and have not spoken about it until today (4 years later) we ( my 2 children and I now 11 & 14) I don't do drugs and I drink on the odd occasion and am very sceptical about encounters from other planets. My kids and I have had strange things happen to us since. My son suffers unexplained nose bleeds a lot and hay reaver. I've been diognosed with RA since and I have become a recluse as has my daughter. We all feel someone is watching us a lot and we spot people watching us and get horrible nausea when they notice that we've spotted them. We all sleep together well but apart we don't sleep. My son sleepwalks a lot and we all talk in our sleep. My daughter and I suffer low self esteems and I don't trust a lot of people anymore. I'm not sure if it's all coincidence but my gut feelings are getting worse!
