{cak} the ufo hovered for a second then turned and took off

I was at the base of Weston tower in Manchester NH circa 1980 taking photos of the sunset when this object in the sky caught my eye. It hovered for a second then turned and took off. I was able to capture a couple quick pictures before it was gone.
