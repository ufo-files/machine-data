redish lights hovering around the skys on the 4th July weekend

It was July 4th Weekend. We live just outside city limits and was watching the city's fireworks display they have every year. Fireworks was in our front view. To my right I saw a redish/orange glow in the sky that was hovering, not moving. It dissappeared. I didn't say anything to anyone else. Then a few minutes later my sister said what's that light in the sky? There was another one of those lights right above us. It also dissappeared. Then after the fireworks ended, we saw these same lights come up from the horizon slowly one after another. After they got up to a certain point they vanished. Last year on the 4th we also saw some other kinda lights that were white and they were traveling in a group of 10-12 across the sky.
