Straight line flight with high speed and change of direction high over treetops and elevated ground

This ufo or uap was captured on 4-20-21 at 12;23 am on a home nest cam. The bright object moves out from behind a hill, at Lake Winnipesaukee directly over Weir's Beach area, Houses on the shore with lights on can also be seen.
  The object travels at high speed and comes to a stop. It then travels back behind the hill, reverses direction again, and continues along the same line and direction past the view of the camera at high speed. 
  The object can be seen in the reflection of the water, and also, at the top of the hill, light illuminates behind it showing the shape of the terrain. I believe these are important aspects of the evidence, excluding artificial creation i.e. by a flashlight or trick photography using glass. Yes, the camera is behind a window, but close examination will show that the glass was not reflecting a light from within the house (reason: light illuminates treeline behind hill). There was no activity inside the house at the time of the incident.
