Two star like lights moving together paralel.

I went to take the trash out about 8:15 on 9/8/09. As i turned around from the garbage cans i noticed a very bright light in the sky. I first thought it was a star. I then saw another bright light directly above it. They were vertical coming from the west. the lights were moving together and they looked to be a city block in length from each other. I said to myself "what the hell is that??!!!" I went and got my boyfriend. We both watched it. It just seemed to move and float right over our backyard and then turned and went to the north. I couldnt see it any longer because of the trees and house. I have lived near an airport all my life and never have witness anything like this in my life. Commercial jets have a flashing light, this had none. This was definately, unidentifiable.
