Hovered near the moon.

The best I can describe it is it was above and near the moon. If the moon was a clock it was at about 1 or 2 o'clock. It just sort of hovered and danced slightly in one direction and the other, 3 to 9 o'clock. I thought maybe it was the space station but I checked NASA's website and the station was only observable for under a minute and not until 5 hours later that night/early morning. I watched it for roughly 30 minutes in awe. I routinely see life flight helicopters and military aircraft in this area and it was much bigger in appearance and seemed to be much farther away. This was by far the brightest and oddest object I have ever seen in the sky.
