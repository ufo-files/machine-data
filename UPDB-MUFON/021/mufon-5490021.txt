{rjl} Appeared on photo with trail running contrary to cloud cover.

I was taking a photo of some very high cloud cover for my wife while we were standing on a driveway facing northwest over an empty field. Photo shows bright oval object glowing brightly with yellow-white blending to blue and a faint bluish trail following it. My wife was looking in the same direction but didn't see it. Took another shot in opposite direction to see if it was a speck of dust on the lense and it did not show anything.
