Saw 15 or more orbs appearing one at a time overhead moving from West to East In a similar direction

I was outside and looked overheard. I first noticed a bright light in the sky which was a plane. The plane was moving towards two orbs and then after the plane passed many orbs started to appear from a similar point in the sky and were moving from West to East over the night sky above Santa Rosa. Over a period of 10 minutes, I saw close to 20 of them. Very dim orbs that glowed white.
