saw a light hovering over the mountain

I witnessed a bright light hovering over the mountain , going up and down, up and down. Kept on disappearing and reappearing. I was sitting in my living room looking out my apartment window and saw it hovering over the mountain. At first I thought it was a star but it was way too bright. It looked to be yellowish orange and at times maybe silvery.  

I was totally numb struck and at the time I was on Facebook and asked others if they saw what I saw? This object hovered up and down for at least 16 minutes and then It disappeared. 

I couldn't see the exact date when I posted this incident on Facebook, but it's on there so I just put the date on here when I thought I might have seen it. 

In the past few months I have been witnessing a golden glow of a ball hovering over another mountain at times or even seeing some up in the sky. this isn't my first time encountering it or witnessing it. I always keep my eyes up in the sky.
