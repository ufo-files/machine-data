(GS) 10 + glowing orange orbs taking off seperately in the vicinity of chandler airport

Stopped to get food and an older gentleman was standing staring off in the distance shouting "what is that! what are those!!!" about 7 orange glowing orbs observed moving across the sky rising at a 45 degree angle, all in various positions not relating to each other. They were going higher and farther away. They were dim and seemed to flicker at the distance I guessed to be 5 miles away. Some of them floated close to each other and then there appeared to be only one. I looked around some trees blocking my view and observed 4 or 5 more taking off in 1 second intervals from the ground at a 45 degree angle in the direction of the other ones. The event was happening over near the chandler airport.
