After going to my mailbox to check my mail I turned to return to my house. I noticed a brighter and larger than normal light that seemed to pulse to the south that drew my attention. I at first thought it was just another plane that but it was significantly brighter, and had none of the usual flashing collision lights I was able to capture a few seconds of the flight, but ended it to call my wife to get her opinion. The object continued to head west in a direct path (with multiple aircraft clearly visible on varies flight paths around it). It seemed very odd to both of us that there was no collision lights visible however another aircraft on a similar heading had its lights clearly seen. After a couple minutes flight time the light disappeared to our southwest, which was another oddity due to it still being above any obstructions.
