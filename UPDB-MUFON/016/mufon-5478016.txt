{cak} Slow bright light like Venus very low, no sound.

I was outside on my pool deck observing the stars, especially Venus. Venus was to the west of my location, I had a feeling to look N.W. I see a star (what i though), it is moving really slow from N.W. to S.W. it looked exactly like a star, there was no noise, but it appeared to be quite lower than a star would appear. I tried filming & all I saw through the lens was black, I focused my camera on venus, to correct the focus but still no luck. I lost sight of the object towards the S.W. when it stopped. (When stopped you couldn't tell it wasn't a star) A bright light flashed about 50 or so feet below the object, then another to the left, & the light faded from it. It was miraculous, not my first encounter though.
