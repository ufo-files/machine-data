{cak} Traveling from Catalina to Florence, the road goes between to Mountains, the UFO went from the North mountain to the South mountain in the blink of an eye!

I lived in Tucson AZ from 1993 to 2003. I was working for the Arizona dept of Corrections in Florence AZ. To get there, you travel [location removed] This road goes between to mountains.
I first noticed something hanging above the N. mountain and as I got closer to going in between the object went from the right of me to the left of me over the S mountain. This all happened in less then a heart beat. It was on my right and then it was on my left.
There was nothing else in the sky, not even a cloud, and no one else on the road.
It then just disappeared

This was my first sighting.

My second sighting was in the early evening just out side of Florence, again on my way to work and I had a passenger another Corrections Office. We where using the back entry to Eyman complex, we exited state rd 79 onto Florence /Kelvin rd going east, for about half a mile, then turn left N. as we started up the road, there appeared a very bright and large light ahead of us. It was up in the area where we would turn left to get to work. The light was a little wider then the road and taller then any of the trees in the area. As we got closer, the light went out. So I sped up to see what it might be. it took about 2 min to get to where the light had been, there was nothing, nothing to the left, right, or up. This happened in July of 1998.

These where the only things I have seen, besides the lights over Phoenix, I did see them along with the 9 others I was with. We watched them for about 10 min and they did not move other then to blink.
