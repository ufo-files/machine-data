{nd} seven or more round lights in a row

pulled over to get something from back of car at 41 and 116 routes. Saw lights and grabbed camera. Only got last light as it disappeared. It showed again a few miles to East. got in car to drive South when it appeared. Stopped to get video and friends phone. Trying to figure out how to get video online but we are not tech savey. One light appeared and then a line of lights lit up and moved together. Each light disappeared one by one and then it appeared the same way again two more times within a five mile radius in various directions.I was shaken as I have never seen anything like that. I looked for it again and asked some folks at the gas station if they saw it over them and they laughed.
