{cak} low fire like lights scattered in a localized area that hovered for at least one minute. some tiny, some large, very beautiful. below the cloud line and above roof line of home

Driving home and the sky was filled with what appeared to be orange/red fireflies of all sizes behind a home (I thought it looked beautiful.) I thought it was power lines or a transformer. I looked again and saw even more objects lighting up the sky that were larger in size and higher in the sky that appeared to be burning and hovering im one area. I would venture to say they couldn't have been more than 100 feet above the homes though.

I called home and talked to my daughter for her to look outside, she saw them as well. Unfortunately I did not get any photos and no one I've asked saw what we did last night.

For a split second I had a moment of fear that something was falling from the sky because I could see it too clearly and that didn't feel safe. I couldn't stop my car but thought it was possibly a meteor shower or something along those lines but haven't been able to confirm.

As soon as I turned I could no longer see it.
