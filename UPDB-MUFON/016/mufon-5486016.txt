Multiple orange red orbs in lower night sky, intense in brightness, waning in intensity

I was travelling home after work one night and noticed in the night sky ahead of me at an a approximate 45 degree angle from the horizon 3 very bright orange/red orbs of light stationary in the night sky. I found a clearing where I could park my car and get out to observe the strange sight. 
When I first noticed the orbs I wasn't sure what they could be. Their colour was burning bright orange, very intense. Flares popped into my head briefly before I realised they weren't moving and stationary. 
There's was one object on its own, and two more closer together a short distance away. 

Within a few seconds each orb would dissipate in intensity till they were only a pin prick of light, barely observable. Again within a minute tops that pin prick would flare up into a bright orange glowing orb again. Each orb behaved this way, waning in and out, in and out. 

At one occasion two of the orbs formed a small formation, one directly over the other glowing intensely. They would fluctuate from bright orange to red in colour.

I was amazed and very puzzled by what I was seeing, especially when the two orbs seems to make that formation. I felt excited and also trying to rationalise what I was seeing. 

Could they be Chinese lanterns? Well, they were too bright to seem to be. And I don't understand how a Chinese lantern can fade away to almost nothing but a pinprick of light but then flare up to bright intensity yet again. 

I had the feeling these things were maybe a plasma phenomenon, though not sure why. They never exhibited extreme manoeuvres or darted about in the sky. 

Eventually they all just faded out one by one and weren't seen again. 

This video taken my a person in Arizona USA has captured what I seen, absolutely identically. The fade in and out in brightness, and sudden appearance of an orb in the night sky, not from the ground up as one would expect from Chinese lanterns
