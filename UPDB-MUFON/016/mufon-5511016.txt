(GS) I observed a bright shiny object moving at extremely high rate of speed, that disappeared into a cloud.

I was in the back yard by the pool, probably smoking. It was mostly sunny with a a few clouds, when I saw a shiny object moving at a rate of speed no plane could match. It was at about 19 to 20 thousand feet.? It was high enough that I couldnt make out a distinct shape. Closest I could come is saucer shape or something like it. . I thought it was a plane at first. BUT I have never seen any plane travel at that kind of speed and have never seen any plane reflect that much light off of it. This object went into a small cloud and didn't pass through. I should have been able to see him come out, which would have took less than a second as fast as it was going. Not sure what I saw, but I don't think it was any kind of plane
