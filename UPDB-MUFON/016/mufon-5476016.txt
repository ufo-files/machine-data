{nd} Cigar shaped Craft Reflective with Golden Sunrise and short wings

After getting off work I was crossing a large field to get home less than a block away when I looked up at the sky and saw a large very shiny metallic cigar shaped aircraft with very disproportionally short winds almost like a rocket would have for stabilizers. Traveling at extreme velocity in a South West ward direction. 8:00AM April 2nd 2017. No lights were present on the craft and it made no noise or sound. Did not appear to be any man made aircraft I have ever seen or heard of. I study Aviation and Fly DJI Phantom. I ran back to the house to get my Drone and by the time I got back outside the craft was gone. This craft appeared to be flying less than 300FT at extreme velocity and making no sound. I put the drone in the sky and searched for the craft, but could not find it. Assuming it had either vanished or flown away so fast I could not track it. I also tried to track it on Radar, but I found no trace of it. I contacted the local Airport which was less than 8 miles away and they had no aircraft in the area. Whatever this was, it was not one of ours.
