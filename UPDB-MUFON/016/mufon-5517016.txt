{nd} two spining domes connect by cylinder

I was in my bedroom when I noticed a odd and fuzzy looking object in the sky (south of my location in fullerton) close to fullerton airport.
I am an aerospace engineer and at first I assumed it was a helicopter hovering over the airport. However the flashing red lights on top and bottom of the vehicle and the shape of the object were odd. I grabbed my 10x50 binoculars and looked at the object. I could not believe what i saw...two triangular domes on top and bottom connected by a cylinder. It was greyish/silver color and was hovering and very slowly moving west. I watched it for ~20-30 minutes I took a picture but it was too far for my lens (75mm). Did not have enough time to set up my telescope and take a better pic.
