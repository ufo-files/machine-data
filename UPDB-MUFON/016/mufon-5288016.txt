“Cop blue light” orb.

Was driving NW on freeway. Saw a blue orb “cop blue light”, best color I can describe. I thought it was a drone at first. It was hovering on right side of the freeway approximately 1/16th of a mile to the right side of freeway, about 2 stories high on altitude. All of a sudden it shoots to the other side of the freeway, approximately same position of height and length but now it’s on left side of freeway. I pulled over because I now knew it wasn’t a drone. Seconds later it shoots lower altitude on left side of freeway. Then again to where it was originally at right side of freeway. Shoots again to where it was on left side of freeway. Then it descends lower and just hovers at a fast pace down the street on left side of freeway. Suddenly it shoots to the sky in a 45 degree angle and disappears in the sky. THIS IS NOT THE FIRST TIME I SEE THIS TYPE OF THINGS. ITS BEEN YEARS OF VARIOUS ORBS, ODD EXPERIENCES OF “DREAMS”, SEEING SHIPS SAUCER SHAPED WITH BLUE BALL FLOATING IN BACK
OF THEM. SEEN RED ONES. YELLOW AMBER ONES. I’m happy to discuss anonymously. It’s the most random feeling I get. Almost an urge of me wanting to go somewhere and then that’s when it happens. Dozens of times.
