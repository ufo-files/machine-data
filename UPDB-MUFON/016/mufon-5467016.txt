{cak} I and 2 friends

I was in eastern North Carolina at a private residence in the backyard conducting an investigation with hand-held audio equipment, digital 35 mm camera & video camera due to reported UFO activity in the area. The moon was full, but to the left of the moon was a bright, round object. We noticed the object, talked about how unusual it looked, unlike the surrounding stars, because it was much larger and appeared to be moving towards us. Two hours later we found ourselves standing in the exact same location, staring at the full moon, swaying back & forth and feeling dizzy & nauseous, but all of our equipment was on the ground and it was turned off. However, the video camera was set-up in a different location behind us and to our right, and it was still recording. None of us knew we had lost 2 hours of time until we returned to the owner's house and his wife asked us where the heck had we been! At the time we weren't sure what had happened and basically chalked it up to being tired because my friend & I had driven 3.5 hours to the 3rd friend's house and it was now 1:00 am. I and my friend left the residence for the drive back home, but as we drove about a mile up the road we both saw the most amazing, huge shooting star back in the direction from where we just came. We both yelled at the same time because of its size and closeness to the earth. Over the next few days we reviewed the audio from the video camera and whoa, seems we were abducted! However, we had absolutely no memory of the incident, until recently......

I am now having dreams & flashbacks about the event. The memories are very detailed and include seeing the object move towards us, running from the object, being taken aboard, walking thru shiny hallways covered in beautiful blue light and looking from within the object to the outside world. They took us to the dark side of the moon......

I have audio & video files from the event. The audio files are amazing!!!
