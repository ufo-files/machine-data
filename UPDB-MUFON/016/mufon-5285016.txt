Possible sighting

Summer 1965, going to Grandparents house in Carrizozo. Father stopped car with clear overlook of the canyon below. When I looked over the seat, I saw a large glowing object in the sky, it was motionless. In a few seconds I saw two jets come from the direction of White Sands missile range. They circled the object two or three times; and then they both fired two missiles. All four exploded around 100 yards from the object, but when they did you could see a glowing svear surrounding the object and then it took off in a northern direction, which was so fast you could not keep up with it.
