{rjl} I thought it was seeing Venus or Jupiter until it started moving slowly up and down.

I was getting off the bus coming from work. As I was walking away from the bus stop I notice this bright light in the sky. First I thought it was a star but when I saw it moving a little I then thought it was an airplane, but there were no blinking lights like airplanes are suppose to have on them and it was moving too slow to be a plane. What made me realize that this was no ordinary craft was when it started slowly moving up and down. Ascending and descending. Then a second one appeared out of no where like it uncloaked itself. This one was much smaller. It darted to the left, stopped and then disappeared. The larger one that looked like Venus slowing faded away. It was very interesting. A strange feeling came over me while I was seeing these objects. The feeling I got from these objects was like a message saying, "Hello! Can you see us." They wanted to be seen. I wish I had a video camera at the time. I'm sorry I was not able to catch it on video or take any pictures, but I just had to report it any way. I just had this need to tell someone.
