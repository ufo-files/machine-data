The family and I witnessed three batches of UFO's over Orchard Park, NY, in the early evening of July 24, 2016. The first batch consisted of 4 objects - one doughnut shaped, one cigar shaped, and two spherical orbs. The second batch consisted of 5 spherical orbs. The third batch is what I captured on this video and consisted of 3 spherical orbs. All objects appeared as dark solid objects and moved in an controlled trajectory.

Please pay close attention to the trailing two objects - one dark in front and one lighter. The lighter objects clearly accelerates to match darker object, trails back, then climbs in altitude. Again, movements look controlled.

These were not drones. From my vantage and relative position, I would estimate an altitude of 500-1000 feet. Hard to judge size or velocity, but these were very solid objects.

The doughnut-shaped UFO rotated in a random orientation.

I am kicking myself for not grabbing my phone sooner; I was in the pool and was just fixed on the objects for a few minutes before grabbing a phone.

Audio track is added to save you the pain of listening to kids screaming in the pool as they were watching.
