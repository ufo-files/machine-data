Orange transparent lights in formation

I was with a group of 5 friends outside of my vehicle on Saturday 14th December 12:15am in the three tuns area when one friend pointed out an unusual object darting frantically above us and then moved over the houses. It looked like an orange jellyfish texture rather than a craft that was dimly lit but was glowing. My friends joined to observe the object which then frightened us when failing to find logical reasoning. The object shot off at high speed and emitted a bright red light at its destination. This seemed like a guidance beacon as in less than a minute there were tens of the same objects moving in the same direction. These moved in formation like that of flocks of birds. 4 waves of objects passed in the same manner but at different altitudes, increasing numbers and slightly altered formation but V shaped generally. No noise was heard. Flashes followed in the clear night sky for an hour after seeing objects overhead. The flashes were white tailed streaks like shooting stars but were closer in the earths atmosphere and had a magnesium burn brightness to them. A helicopter was heard to be flying in the area but could not be seen. All activities seized on arrival of the helicopter.
