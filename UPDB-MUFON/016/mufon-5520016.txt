Green Shooting Star-Like Object Falls Straight Down

Around the date of Dec. 8 2011, I was driving with my mom into town to look for Xmas decorations. While driving on Harlansburg Rd (into Croton), a bright green light caught both of our eyes. It was to our right seemingly way out over a field. It basically looked like a shooting star but it seemed to come out of nowhere and it fell straight down. Now Ive seen shooting stars and am always watching the sky, and I have NEVER seen a green shooting star that zips straight down. We actually pulled over expecting to hear an explosion or see some kind of commotion in the area where it seemed to hit but nothing happened. Ive been seeing strange things in the area since last summer and am wondering if anyone else has or if Ive officially lost it.
