2 dark objects flying in a pattern descended

2 dark objects caught my eye because they descended so quickly and then moved in a straight pattern to the Northeast. Many aircraft fly over us from direction of the Philadelphia airport and then head North but these objects were flying in a strange direction. They were absolutely silent and moving with zero emissions but with haste. They moved as a pair. I was in my pool with my son and the event lasted less than 20 seconds so there was no time to get out and find my phone. Unique experience.
