I was on the terrace of my house and ended up capturing a photo with a strange light on the side.

I was on the terrace of my house taking pictures with my brother with my new phone, until after several photos a strange object appears next to my brother in the picture and when I noticed I was a little scared, I was half Night and a strange kind of dust was falling from the sky. It was the strangest night ever. Years later I saw the news in Brazil Talking about a light/object similar to the one I took ( the news of the object/light similar to the one I took was made in the same year that I took the photo ) and decided to make this report.
