Big red glowing orb traveled across the sky slowly. No blinking lights.

Before the 4th of July fireworks started, my family and I noticed a big red glowing orb, coming from north to south over Bloominging Indiana. It appeared to travel at an altitude of about 1 mile high. There were no blinking lights at any view point of its travel. It traveled in a straight line and was observed by hundreds of people looking to watch the fireworks show. Look for other sightings directly north and south of location. Indianapolis, Martinsville, Lake Monroe and Bedford.
