{rjl} On highway 575 south of Jasper, saw strange-looking clouds. Took pictures, but did not see huge UFO till later in pictures entering clouds.

On 29 October 2014, my wife and I were driving home from the North Georgia Mountains. At about 5:25 pm, we saw strange clouds forming over highway 575 south of Jasper. At that time I remembered reading about UFOs using clouds to hide or recharge their systems so, on a whim, I took three photos but forgot to look at them until now. It is important to note that we could not see the object in real time when the event actually occurred. It was only today, three weeks later, that I saw what appears to be a huge UFO, 800 to 1000 yards across, entering and hovering in the clouds. I took the pictures with my cell phone from the passenger seat of our car. The pictures are in sequence, taken several seconds apart. FYI, I am a retired Air Force Officer and Pilot who has flown all over the world, with a PhD in Business from a highly respected school.
