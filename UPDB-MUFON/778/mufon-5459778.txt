{rjl} 3 separate lights, orb-like in appearance approximately 5 minutes apart.

The first light seen was from the second floor window of my home. Looking south west I noticed a single red light in front of a tree line approximately a quarter of a mile away. In front of that tree line is a cow pasture. I figured the light was from a 4 wheeler corralling some loose cows.
 Only when the light rose above the tree line I realized it was not an atv. As it came closer, I could see the orb was yellow in the center, surrounded by red. 
 I ran downstairs and out the back doorway. It moved swiftly seemingly on a flight path headed north east until it disappeared into cloud cover. No sound or blinking lights.
 A few minutes, another orb, same appearance traveling in same direction. After the second one disappeared a third followed a minute or two later.
