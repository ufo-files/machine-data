{jpg} I witnessed 66 flying objects in a straight pattern single file

On Wednesday March 4th, I witnessed sixty-six flying objects moving in two paths in a straight manner from Southwest, below the constellation Orion, near the moon before disappearing. As soon as one disappears another one appeared in a similar place in the sky. About fifteen minutes later, I observed many craft simultaneously following each other, getting brighter and then completely disappearing, in pairs of four in a straight path. Some objects shone brighter than others and there was one which went in the exact opposite direction compared to the other sixty-five.
