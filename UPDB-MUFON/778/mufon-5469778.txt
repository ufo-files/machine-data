{cak} Beam of light from sky downward, but did not beam to ground. Approx 6 x's

I was outside smoking a cigarette approximately 11pm. Extremely windy night (20mph gusts) as cold front blew in. I saw a beam/flash out of corner of my eye toward NW. So I watched the area and it happened again. Same light beam, same area. It was like a spot light coming from behind some light clouds. I called for my husband to see it. He witnessed 2 more beams, about 10 or 15 seconds apart. Then just like that, we didn't see it again. First time in my life to see anything ufo related that I couldn't explain. I didn't see any aircraft to describe. The beam didn't go to the ground, from my view it was about 4 inches length up and down. There wasn't any sound, as the wind was unimaginable. I counted a total of 6 "flash/beams" Really cool!
