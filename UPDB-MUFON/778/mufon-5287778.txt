Noticed two hovering lights in the morning sky

Leaving for work a strange light caught my attention. My first thought it was an airplane or helicopter. However, after putting my things in my vehicle I looked back at the object and it hadn’t moved. I then took a photo with my phone. I then got in my vehicle to leave for work continually looking at the objects as I drove down the street. The objects never moved. I looked to cross traffic to make my turn and took one final look at the objects and now they were gone. I searched the sky and saw no signs of them anywhere.
