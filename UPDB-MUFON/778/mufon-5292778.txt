I think I saw a UFO shrouded by a cloud...

Well, I was outside smoking a cigarette with my husband on a Friday night... he had finished his cigarette before me, and went inside before I witnessed what I did...

We both saw a gigantic cloud, which seemed to just be going on it’s own path... faster than normal clouds, and there were literally NONE anywhere in the sky view ... my husband just thought whatever, “I’m too tired for this” and went inside... but if he had only stayed a couple of minutes... I will always never forgive myself for letting him ignore this and go inside.

When he left, I continued to watch it through the treetops near me, it looked like it got closer and then suddenly just ... gone. Completely gone. It made no sense, it was right there, you could SEE something was underneath the cloud... and then it was just gone... the way it just vanished is what confirmed that I was not just witnessing a renegade cloud, but something else... i even told my husband to go get a phone or camera but he really didn’t think anything of it at first... 

I wish he had seen what I did, I was clearly shaken up when I went back inside and told him what happened...
