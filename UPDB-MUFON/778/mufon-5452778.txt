Saw a orb like light while walking my dog

I was walking my dog and something caught my eye and I looked up and I saw a bright orb like light pulsating. It was moving west to east approx 200kmh easy . It was way too big for a satellite which I see every night. This was much lower and pulsar like...bright white light no other colour. It did a quick zigzag right to left ...very fast and stopped for maybe 7-9 seconds and kept going and before it disappeared over the mountain it zigzag one more time....when it went right to left it covered over a hundred yards in a fraction of a second and returned to its original path . No sound and didn’t come back. Been there over 9 years and first time I seen something here.
