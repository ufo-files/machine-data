Travelling faster than anything I ever noticed in sky.

Travelling on I-84 East, coming from grocery shopping, approaching Mt, Cobb exit, I, driving, asked my friend, Stella, to look at the flying craft ahead to our right. I mentioned it was flying too fast. As it came into view over the interstate, Stella agreed it couldn't be a jet. We watched as it continued over to Scranton's skies. No place to pull over and had no time to grab phone to take pic.
