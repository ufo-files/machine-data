{cak} train of craft above atmosphere

Multiple objects above atmosphere traveling across the sky. First thought was a meteor but the object did not behave like a meteor and was followed by another object at a regular interval. Regular interval followed by many more crafts. All in a perfect line across the sky. After each 10-12 bunch of lights, another craft would appear another 20 or so degrees north, as in a position of a drill sergeant commanding a line of troops. There were maybe thirty lights. My video is from my phone so not easy to make out, but on the computer with simple video adjustments, the craft are clearly seen crossing over Venus.
