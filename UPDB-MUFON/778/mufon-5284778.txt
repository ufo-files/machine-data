4 bright orange lights that appeared out of nowhere and moved across the sky in the same direction and then

Observed 1 bright orange light that appeared out of nowhere in the sky. The light began traveling very quickly from south to north. A second light then appeared in the same area and followed the same trajectory, followed in the same pattern by a third and a forth light. All contined from south to north moving faster that the air traffic in the night sky. When they reached a certain point each light blinked to a pinpoint and disappeared. No sound was made. Observed over Newtown PA. I am familiar with the air traffic in the area as there is an outer marker in Newtown and these lights did not follow any of the known flight paths. Also could hear the passing planes, but these lights were silent. The lights were a very bright orange and there were no blinking lights like you would see on an aircraft. They lights also seemed to be traveling faster than the aircraft in the area. I've worked in this area at night former over 20 years and have never seen lights like this. I thought at first they were "Chinese Lanterns" because if the bright orange flame like light, but they moved too fast and all had the same trajectory. I was able to take picture of two of the objects...in the picture they appear to be elongated, but to my vision they did not appear that way...also they were very orange.
