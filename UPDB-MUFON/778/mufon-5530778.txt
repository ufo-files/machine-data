I saw a UFO shaped like big glowing ball with a metal shaped pyramid at the bottom.

I was outside in my backyard, hanging clothes to dry. Then all of the sudden I heard roaring. Like a roar from a motor. I looked up and I saw a UFO very close. The distance was about 500 ft at the most.It was shaped like a ball on the top and at the bottom like a metal shaped pyramid. The ball glowed and I saw smoke coming out at the bottom. At first it moved slow and then it picked up speed. It took off very very fast. And then it disappeared. I got scared and I couldn't move.
