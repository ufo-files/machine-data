Glowing balloon like object hovering then slowly ascending

Saw two reddish glowing balloon shaped objects about 5 minutes apart that appeared from beyond a row of houses a little above treetops in the neighborhood a distance of about two miles. Objects slowly climbed to an altitude of about 300 ft and each illuminated for about 6 minutes and 15 seconds before disappearing. The path they took was exactly the same. Objects were bright enough to be a flare, but did not arc nor travel the same as a flare. Video was taken with a cell phone but is not distinguishable after the event.
