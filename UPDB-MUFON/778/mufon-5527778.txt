5 in a cluster, then 3 take off one direction and two in another

Riding 4 wheeler at the deer lease in San Diego, Texas. Saw bright orange lights, 3 oval shaped objects take off in one direction, two in another. Stopped riding the 4 wheeler and just watched them in the sky. Then lights go off, and come on again further out blinking simulataneously. I yelled to my friend who was in another location, he say two what described as "fireballs". Did not have my cell phone at the time.
