I was in bed sleeping beside my husband. Two daughters asleep in their rooms. Our family dog was in the house. The next morning and even now, 26 years later, I have a clear memory of that night. A bright light came into the bedroom window. 2 aliens placed me in a seat, like on a plane. They conveyed that I was safe and they were friendly. They were curious about humans and had me relax as they explored my memory of common events that humans experience. There may have been some physical manipulation and samples taken, though no puncture woulds were noted. Of note: an electrical substation is directly across a field from this home. No one believed me and I could not find a source to report this to at the time.
