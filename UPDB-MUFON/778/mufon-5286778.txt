White Cigar shaped craft with black spot where we thought was a wing (but not) flying NE over Ashtabula OH

We went outside to smoke and looked up to see a white with black "spot" cigar shaped craft flying about the same speed as a plane in a NE direction over Ashtabula coming from the area of West Ave and Hwy 20 intersection direction moving towards the general area of Main St and Jefferson Rd intersection. It was 8:52 when we took the video (which is not the best quality). That was within a minute or so of seeing it, as my daughter went in to get her phone. This craft made no noise, left no visible trail (as a plane would), and had no lights (as a plane would). It did not appear to be a plane. What we thought was a wing did not appear to be a wing. It seemed that no matter from what angle you looked, there wasn't a wing at all. It wasn't moving incredibly fast, but about the speed of a plane - or less. We first thought it could be a glider, but again, no wings. It flew at what appeared to be the distance a plane would be. There were no rapid movements, sounds, lights, or anything of that nature; Nothing that would make you look up because it was unusual. It was actually kind of inconspicuous unless you happened to notice, as did we. The sky was clear and there weren't any obstructions to our view, really. That's about all I can think to describe.
