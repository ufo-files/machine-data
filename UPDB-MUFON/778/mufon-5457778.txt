{tjd} Distant blinking light above NYC

In the late afternoon, in a cloudless sky over Chelsea in NYC, my sister and I both witnessed what seemed to be a very distant blinking light moving above the city. The blinking seemed to be random. I took video but it's very hard to see. The light moves from left to right, eventually disappearing behind a glass building. In the video, 43 seconds in, the light is visible just over the glass building's roofline. We assumed it was a satellite, but the blinking seemed odd.
