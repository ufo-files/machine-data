I was looking for the Jupiter Saturn conjunction

I was searching for the Jupiter Saturn conjunction in the western sky as I was instructed. This was way past sundown, so I wasn't expecting to see it. When I saw this light moving, I suspected it may be a UFO. I placed my face against our screened in porch to make sure that my eyes weren't wandering, making it appear as if the light was moving. When I realized it was moving, the path was somewhat erratic, similar to a satellite, but at times moving backwards and sideways, not very much, but certainly NOT the movements of a satellite or ISS. It wasn't moving very fast. It was cold outside, so I didn't want to stand outside too long, but it was still moving when I left. This was between 7:30 PM to 8 PM. It may be still out there. As much as I wanted to record it with my cellphone, I probably would not have been able to pick it up because it was moving past the branches, and it was very small. I have no idea how high it was, but it did resemble a satellite, just moving erratically.
