{cak} Unnatural speed, hovered then disappeared

We were watching planes come and go at the airport. Out of the NE a blue light flew to the airport at an unnaturally fast speed and came to an IMMEDIATE FULL stop and hovered over the airport. A couple of minutes later it took off again at an immediate unnatural speed and disappeared in the night sky. I’ve been around planes for over half of my life and I’ve never seen anything like that.
