(GS) 3 CYLINDERS UPRIGHT MOVING HORIZONTAL 2 FIRST TIME THEN 1 55 MINS. LATER

WE WERE SITTING IN MY DEN. MY FREIND SAYS WHAT THE HELL IS THAT LIGHT. THEN WE SAW A SECOND ONE NEXT TO IT, WE RAN OUTSIDE AND THEY CAM STRAIGHT OVER US HEADING EAST.THE FRONT WAS BRIGHT ORANGE GLOBE CENTER WITH BRIGHT WHITE ON BOTH SIDES.IT HAD WHAT APPEARED TO BE SOME TYPE OF FLAMES AROUND THE BOTTOM IN A CIRCLE WITH A BLACK SQUARE CENTER,AS IT PASSED THE BACK APPEARED TO BE REFLECTED LIKE A MIRROR. IT BLENDED IN BUT WE COULD MAKE OUT THE SHAPE. IT HAD A DOMED TOP BUT IT FLEW VERTICAL WITH THE FLAMES OUT THE BOTTOM. THE TWO HEADED EAST AT PERFECT HIGHT AND SPACING UNTIL THEY ARCHED UP AND POOF GONE.AT 24 HRS, HEREH COMES ANOTHER ONE BY ITSELF,SAME THING SAME HEADING AND GONE. MY 18 YEAR OLD GOT SO SCARED HE CRIED AND RAN INTI HIS BEDROOM.I WAS LIKE TOLD YOU ALL WE ARE NOT ALONE. THE OTHERS WERE RATTLED.
