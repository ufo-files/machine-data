{rjl} A Star like orb moving south through night sky

On the evening of June 21, 2014 at 9:28pm, my wife and I were finishing up a dinner date with a couple friends of ours. We were casually walking them to their car when our guest stated how good he could see the stars outside. Then he said, "What's that!?". My wife and I turned towards the North behind our house in the direction he was looking. We all were looking at a white-blue star-like orb that was moving relatively fast at a ultra high altitude in a south direction. My wife questioned,"Is that a plane?". I said,"It isn't flashing.". The star-like orb never flashed, didn't have a tail, never accelerated or slowed. As the object moved directly over head, it began to fade then vanish from sight. All three of us all saw the object and watched it move from 45 degrees off horizon to 95-100 degrees off horizon over approximately 30 seconds.
