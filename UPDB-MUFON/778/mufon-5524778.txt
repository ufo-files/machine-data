bright light in moutains

When on a spring bear hunt just northwest of Silver city N.M., i was awakened by somthing moving past my sleeping bag. Where we hunt you have to pack in everything you need, so we had no tent. We were at about 11,000 feet. Somthing had kicked dirt into my sleeping bag. I noticed that the ground was quite bright, brighter than day light. I sat up to see what it was, and noticed the light was coming from above. I tried to wake my dad, but could not speak or move. I sat there for what seemed to be an hour, but not sure how long. There was no sound, or wind. I was with my dad, cousin, and his dog, and they did not notice anthing in the night. We had horses, and mules, and they made no noise. In the morning I saw no signs of any of this, and we finished hunting. Now I know I have a vivid amagination, but I do not think I was dreaming. Not sure what happened, but it was real to me, and I remember it like it was yesterday. There are no roads, or electricity there.
