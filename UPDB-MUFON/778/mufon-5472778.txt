{cak} accidental photo of a star like object

The photo was directed at the trees in the foreground and I later noticed that this greenish object in the sky looked unusual when zooming in on it. I really paid little attention to it during the photograph as it was stationary and just looked like a star or planet. In retrospect, it was quite bright considering the amount of sunlight. I returned that way on the walking trail 15 minutes later and took a similar photo. The sun was lower and the object was not visible.
