Entity /s in my room

So this happened years ago, I was possibly around 10-12 years of age. Now things always happened in that house - more importantly, in the room I lived in. One night, I woke up in the middle of the night to see this figure beside my bed. I remember the fear I had, and the unsettling presence it gave off. I sat in bed for a good 5 min just watching this being. I knew it was looking at me, even though I could just see the shape I could tell it was studying me. After a while, I felt the presence move, I could not see movement, but I could feel that it was moving closer. At this time, I ran out of bed and into the hallway tying to make it to my parents bed. I froze at their door because I could see another being standing by the stairs. it was not moving, but just standing there watching. This is when I seen the being from by room with in an arms length from me (as I was just outside my bedroom door because my room and parents room were across from each other). The next thing I remember was a flash of light and waking up in my parents bed the next morning. I told my mom what happened and she did not believe me. I kept it secret most of my life because of the backlash I got from my parents. But even to this day, I get that same feeling once in a while, the feeling I had when the beings were watching me and I always wake up with strange bruises or scratches.
