8-10 blinking pattern lights moving in correlation with each other slowly

I work twice a week doing night audit at a small boutique hotel in West Hollywood, CA. I was at the front desk when my valet driver called me on the radio to come out side at around 1:15am.

I walked out to the front of the hotel. The Valet pointed to the sky and said what is that? It was a pretty clear night for LA and at first I thought he was just showing me stars or passing bye planes or a satilite. 

But I looked closer and I saw about 8-10 single slow moving lights/objects moving at a steady pace through the sky. The lights where white almost florecent and blinking in a pattern. Right away it reminded me of a bunch of synchronized swimmers moving in all different kinds of directions. At first I thought the lights might of been a outline to something larger but the longer I watched them the more they moved in all different directions. It looked choreographed. I have never seen any sightings before in my life and i do believe in UFOs but am sometimes skeptical. I see airplanes and helicopters all the time especially in Los Angeles, this was nothing like i have seen before, it was very weird. I went back in to get my camera phone but i got side tracked with a guest and bye the time i went back outside they had passed. 

A guest had also commented on it while he was waiting for his car and actually first noticed the objects
