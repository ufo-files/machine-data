{cak} 500'long blimp, no color, no sound, moved slow

I was sitting in my front room on my lap top when I noticed movement out of the corner of my eye. It was about 10 PM, I could not believe what I was seeing. I called my wife to come out on the back deck with me that overlooked the Spokane valley south of Northwood. The object drifted east and headed west about 500 above ground not making a sound! It was about 500'long, huge! The sky was clear that night. It had lights on the perimeter that chased around it uniformly counterclockwise like a Vegas hotel. The shape floated out of sight to the west behind trees.

I called my coworker on the swing shift and asked him to go out now onto the ramp at Spokane International Airport and look to the west where Beacon Hill is in clear line of sight. I told him Ito look for something large in the sky with lights on it at about mid hill level. He called back and said he saw nothing;

There is Felts Airfield at the base of the hill we were on to our west. The manager for the airfield lived about 5 houses from us. I asked him if he had any knowledge of this or ATC reports of it. He had no knowledge of it and no ATC reports of anything on that night.

I have been looking for the last 10-15 years for anything looking like this on the internet. Finally one day I was surfing on FaceBook and came across a picture/footage of THE VERY THING I SAW! I am attaching a screen capture of that. It was about the same level and height as the one in the picture. The only difference is the lights moved along the perimeter much faster. 

I have some level of satisfaction having vindicated in my mind actually seeing an exact image that someone else had seen of this object that was posted on Facebook. I had made comments on that posting that I too had seen this object but I can not find the post that shows the lights in motion.
