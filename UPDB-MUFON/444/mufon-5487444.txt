{dr}Madison, WI Solid bright white object moving east to west low on horizon. No blinking, no tail, no sound.

I was walking my dog just before 8:00 pm in our residential neighborhood. I was facing south when I saw a bright white object move steadily across the sky just above trees in the near distance. It moved from east to west and appeared to be descending slightly. It moved in a straight line for maybe three seconds before it was out of view. This object was closer than an airplane flying in the area. It did not blink or flash but was solid with no additional lights. I only saw light, no solid object around it. I do not think it was a falling star or meteorite, it had no tail or streak. The only other thing it could be is a drone with a bright LED light on it. This was over a neighborhood park, and as we approached it I thought maybe I would see someone but no one was there. This was on the west side of Madison, WI.
