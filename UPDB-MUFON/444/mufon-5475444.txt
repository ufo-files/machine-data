{nd} Saw 2 glowing orbs that vanished into thin airand 1 white .flashed light at us

My boyfriend and i were coming home from tavares, fl fireworks. We had the top off the jeep and as we were pulling into driveway i saw a strange glowing light and asked him do you see that light. He said what light i pointed and said that one over power lines. He then saw and said i dont know. We didnt even park jeep yet he stopped immediately left jeep running and we got out to see it. It slowly went over us in a sphere orange glow too big to be a star in sky and we watched it go past us overhead from north east to south west and just disappeared after about a minute. My boyfriend ran inside to grab a flashlight then flashed it in the sky all over. Then we saw a white glow moments later in the sky as high as a jet moving slow from south to north and he flashed his led spotlight at it. Then we got flashed two quick burts of light back. I freaked out and tried to lay down he stayed outside with his flashlight. Then few minutes later i hear him pounding on front living room window yelling for me to hurry outside. I got up and ran back outside to him and he was pointing up to look. I saw another orange glowing orb moving from east to west then curved toward and over the lake. He said it flashed those 2 quick beams of light again. I did not witness the flash of beams this time. I was so scared i came in again and stayed this time. He came back in after 10 minutes.
