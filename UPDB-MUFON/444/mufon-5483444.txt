{rjl} Triangular Orange/Purple Moving through the Sky

Driving northbound on M-24 the light first appeared towards the west at 11:00 pm, it seemed to be moving northbound a few degrees when it vanished. Only a few minutes or so later, still driving northbound, the light appeared directly behind the car. The light was moving northbound behind us, within a mile or so distance from the car. We turned west to go home and when we got out we could still see the light through the tree-line directly to the east. After a few more minutes it disappeared. We looked at it through binoculars and could make out a clear triangular shape. The object was glowing mostly orange but it would fade into purple. I hand drew a simple map of where the car was moving and where the object was in relation to us. The three stars show the points the object moved to. It began west of m-24, moved over it and slightly to the east before continuing northbound.
