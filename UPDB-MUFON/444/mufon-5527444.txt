Stepped on porch and observed a bright sphere shape above trees.

At 11:50 p.m. I stepped on the front porch to have a smoke under an umbrella. No stars were visible due to complete cloud cover and rain. I observed a shining oval/sphere shaped object hovering above some trees for about 20 second, then it disappeared. It seemed very close. This has been happening here for several months now, but this is the first time I have reported it. My brother has observed this with me before. I have no photos but will be on the ready from now on. This is getting interesting.
