It was a clear day about 4 to 5 pm I was heading east on a street named Pinellas Bayway. Stopped at a light and was looking at the red light when the UFO flew over over. It was about 30 to 50 feet in diameter being a round ball shape with a yellow color like the Sun but it was not glowing. It was moving at a slow speed estimated to be 100 mph or less. It appeared to be very low abut 300 feet in the air. There were no windows as the object appeared to be a solid ball shape.

The UFO suddenly changed color to silver metal similar to an airplane skin with no paint on it. Then it disappeared into thin air in a blink of an eye. It just vanished from sight.

The direction it was flying was almost the same flight path that planes take to Mac Dill AFB in Tampa to land. I live on this flight path and see planes coming in to land almost everyday.
