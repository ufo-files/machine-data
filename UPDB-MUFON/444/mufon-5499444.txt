{nd} cone-shaped with dome-like bottom

Taking a picture of the bright moon, and zooming in, the object is hovering in front of the moon with a dome-like bottom reflecting the moon's light. The "dark" side of the craft was shadowed and/or it could be argued that it was cemetrical as well.
