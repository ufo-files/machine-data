Blue/White pulsating light, stationary hovering just north of Pikes Peak at approximately 16,000 feet elevation

Living in view of Pikes Peak and Cheyenne Mountain, it is a daily (nightly) observation point for me. I am familiar with the multitude of aircraft flying along the mountains from the Air Force Academy. This exact object has been sighted by me several times at night, never consecutive nights and hovers at approximately 2,000 ft higher than the top of Pikes Peak. I'm sure of the altitude because of my knowledge of Pikes Peak. It doesn't move if watched for over an hour. No sound. It is not a star and isn't there when other stars are first visible and is much brighter and larger than any star or planet because it appears to be so close. Flight Radar showed absolutely nothing in the area commercially. I've attached 20 seconds of video (the date/time is incorrect as I didn't want to lose the recording opportunity by taking time to set it), Flight Radar and sky position screen capture. I'm thinking it is military because of such close proximity to Fort Carson and Peterson Air Force Base. What puzzles me is the alternating blue/white light, that it is stationary for a long period of time and I don't know of any helicopters flying at that altitude around here or with blue lights and I've watched for several years. I never see the lights leave as I stop watching after a while.
