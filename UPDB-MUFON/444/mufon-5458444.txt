{rjl} Two red objects merges

It was 10-15 yrs ago when I was in Winston co. Alabama . I stepped outside the house before going to bed. I glanced straight up and saw a red object moving across the sky. Then out of my peripheral vision I saw a smaller red object, going the same way. They were moving very fast and I watched them both. In a minute or two, the bigger object turned right, I looked at the smaller object again and he made what appeared to be on an intercept course turn across the sky towards the bigger object. I’m telling you that they were hauling a-s!!!! They both appeared to be above 20000’. 
 I thought nothing about these lights until I saw how quick the closing rate stared getting. It was one minute from the time I saw the second object enter my view that I saw it start a small course change to the right. Within three minutes both objects merged, then vanished. 
 I served 21yrs with the Army-27yrs with Jefferson county Sheriffs Dept. I know how to tell approx distances as i spent my last two years at Cairns Field working on secret to top secret( avionics and weapons systems, on the Cobra gunship) where flying was our way of life. I didn’t tell anyone!!!!!!
