{cak} Saw a silver cylinder shaped ufo right below the clouds.

I was taking a walk with my son and we saw a silver tube shaped object in the sky. It made no sound, if flew in a straight line, it passed through some clouds and kept going into the horizon, until it disappeared. We thought at first it was an airplane, but there were no wings and no tail, no sound and it flew slowly. It had no contrails, either. We weren't frightened by it, merely curious.
