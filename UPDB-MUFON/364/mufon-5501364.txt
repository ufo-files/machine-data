{dr}Fireball rising into sky making no noise. Bright glowing

Took the family to the Long Island Ducks Firework show. Driving home my wife says, now thats a UFO! We (self 49, wife, 45, and twin boys 8, ) all witnessed a bright rising glowing fireball in the eastern sky almost directly above us. We pulled over to try to get better view. I inadvertently took a picture of it quite off in the distance before video taping it. It moved erratically. Not a straight path. Very jagged like as it moved through the night. It was very bright, fireball is perfect. We watched it till it was on the horizon and out of sight. However when I took the picture, and zoom in on it, you can clearly see a dome top with intense flames emanating from under it with a clear dome and what looks like an appendage on top of it. The fact that the UFO was far away from us with the naked eye, the fact that when you enlarge it, there is amazing detail can only mean that this object was very large.
