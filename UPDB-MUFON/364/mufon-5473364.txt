{jpg} Total of 3 lights. Moving faster than I've seen satellites move but not by alot

Crystal clear evening outside my house having a cigarette. No exterior lights on so I could better view the stars. Straight above I viewed what I thought was a fast moving satellite (1st light) then saw another (2nd light) traveling same approximate speed directly at one another as if about to collide. 2nd light (moving south east) stopped and turned abruptly to the north for a short distance as a 3rd light faded in and then out very close to 2nd light. 2nd light seemed to be reacting to 3rd light as if to repel or to obstruct it making it fade away. I dont know what was going on other than they seemed to be reacting to one another.1st light kept traveling northwest and disappeared or I simply lost track of it as I focused on the 3rd lights behavior in fading away. 2nd light remained motionless and appeared to be Starlike other than a reddish pink translucent color shown to its right and a bright almost LED bright white shown to it left. Watched it for a half hour before my feet got cold and decided to go in. Took 4 pictures fully zoomed with no flash
