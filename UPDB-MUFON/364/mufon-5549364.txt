one object moving across a star lit sky in a line. stopped. then out of one, two moved in different directions

i was a boy scout [troop 4- st. gabriels church st.louis missouri} camping at s-bar-f camp in knob lick missouri, we stayed for a week, and on the third or fourth day we took canoes across sims lake to a beach where we spent the night. we had no tents, and layed on the beach to sleep. there were no lights, and it seemed like every star in the sky could be seen. i saw a very faint light moving across the sky, about the same brightness as a star. i pointed the light out to other kids until they saw it, then it stopped for several minutes, then it broke into two lights and moved away from each other like a y from the original line. by this time, most of the kids saw this, and we watched them until they could not be seen anymore. that's it. we also had daily f-15 flights at super-sonic speeds at very low altitudes above the lake, so low the sonic boom would leave ripples in the water. we would canoe out to the middle of the lake, and wait for them to fly by. ask anyone who went to s-f boy scout camp in the mid 1970's.
