Rectangular Object

On Friday, August 14, 2020 at approx. 5:10 pm I was traveling west on 520 towards Orlando. The area that I was driving was very bright with high visibility and the skies ahead were growing darker due to a forming storm. When I was about 1.5 miles from the merger of 520 and Hwy 50, I noticed a large, white (or lightly colored) rectangular object approx. 30 degrees above the horizon. 

The object appeared to be moving in a way that made it appear almost stationary which led me to believe that it may have been a blimp. As I came closer, I noticed that it had well-defined 90-degree edges and was slowly moving in a northern direction. I continued to scan the sky on the leading side of the object to find an aircraft possibly towing an advertisement banner. I could see no aircraft in the sky and quickly noticed that there was no discernable writing or images on the object. 
I estimated the object to be approx. 5 miles ahead of my location and roughly 180’ long and 130’ high. It continued to move to the north and then appeared to “narrow” as it seemed to be turning on a roll axis. [Imagine a sheet of paper being held up vertically and then rolling horizontally and becoming flat] The object continued to roll until it became a thin slit in the sky and eventually vanished from view. 

The whole sighting lasted nearly 2 minutes. I continued to Hwy 50 and eventually made my way to the University of Central Florida area. I continued to scan the skies, but I never saw the object again.
