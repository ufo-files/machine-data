Large dark stationery triangular shape just hovering in the air.

I was driving down Highway 690 West in Syracuse NY Dec. 18th at 20:00hrs. My husband was with me. As I am driving, this triangular shape caught my eye. About 300 to 500 feet in the air - just hovering silent. I asked my husband to confirm that I was seeing this dark triangular shape with soft yellow lights at each tip and a bright white light in the center. He saw it too. So we of course are trying to make this weird object that we've never seen on one of the most traveled highways we take. 
Maybe its a radio tower? 
Maybe its a cellphone tower?
Maybe its a really tall lamp post or possibly something atop a building?
After quickly realizing that none of those options were it we turned off the radio and I opened my window and looked up.
Silence except for the humming of car tires.
It look much bigger than I had originally thought also.

We drive this highway very often. I can confirm there is no tower, no wires or tall building where we saw this UFO.
