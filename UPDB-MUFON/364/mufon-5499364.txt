{nd} Bright round light in sky, emitting a fan shaped mist, then balloon shaped, then ascended and disappeared

I arrived at work just before 6 am. A very bright light in the sky caught my attention. It was so perfect that it looked like a whole in the sky. A strange mist seemed to come out of it, which almost looked like a searchlight through fog, although the sky was perfectly clear. The mist grew to a fan shape, and then into a balloon shape. The bright light ascended and then descended and then disappeared completely! I stood and watched for at least another 30 seconds as the mist completely disappeared as well. I did notice that the mist at first was very white, and then seemed to take on colors, and then faded.
