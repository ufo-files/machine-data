Bright white object traveling across the sky.

On 10-28-10 at approximately 1915 three of my friends and I observed a bright white light traveling slowly across the night sky from my driveway. The sky was clear. This object was just so bright and it looked too low in the sky to be a satellite. I have seen many satellites and this just did not behave like one. I live about 2 to 3 miles from the airport. Airplanes were clearly visible circling in the sky, and appeared to be much higher than this object. It was very quiet and lasted for approximately 30 seconds to 1 minute. The bright white light then shut off, and then there was a tiny red light, for about 2 seconds and then that shut off. It almost appeared as though this object just vanished. I lost sight of this object because the lights turned off and it was gone.
