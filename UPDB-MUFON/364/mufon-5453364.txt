long cigar object with 20 to 50 bright lights in a line.

On April 23, 2020 between 21:40 and 21:55 I observed a very long line of bright lights traveling horizontally across the sky from north to north east direction. It was a very clear starry night. I could not make out any distinct shape because the 20+ bright lights that appeared evenly spaced along the object prevented me from distinguishing its width but the evenly spaced lights in a row was at least 300 ft+ long.
It made no noise and my first thought was how could there be what looked like a long train with each car lit up travelling across the sky.
As the lights traveled across the sky horizontally above and I could faintly see what appeared to be 2 or 3 orbs evenly spaced under the line of bright lights faintly pulsating orange. It appeared to disappear east toward the coast.
