5 glowing Orange balls - like aircraft - 2 groups 3 and then 2

At approximately 11:25 3 objects forming a triangle in flight pattern flew over Kings Park NY from the Southwest to the Northeast. They were not like regular aircraft as they made no sound and were a glowing orange color - they were at a lower altitude as they were rather large. They flew in a path like jets but glowed orange. Within 1 minute another group of two flew in the same direction as the first three - toward Long Ialand Sound.
