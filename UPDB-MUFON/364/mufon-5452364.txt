Red Lights Glowing and Rising to Disappearing

I saw two red lights outside, went on patio and watched as they very very slowly moved towards me (left shoulder) first one looked like it was rising up slowly fading out, as it did, a faint light appeared approximately the same distance apart behind second light, a few moments later the second one seemed to do what the first one did, then the third one slowly faded out. All of this can be seen on the (not so good) video I'll include. The camera couldn't focus on the lights, I tried, but it wouldn't. I zoomed in and out trying to get it to, but it appears blurry mostly. They were clearer in person, hazy outside edges, but clear. This was my second time seeing these red lights, I saw the same three red lights similar to these maybe 7-8 years back in a west/south location here, as opposed to this north/west location. The following day I did a web-search to see if anyone saw them, and they were seen first in Oregon, then San Francisco and lastly here in LA. That makes me think they were outside of our atmosphere(?).
