WE WERE ON HIGHWAY 63 AND SAW A YELLOWISH GREEN BRIGHT GLOWING LIGHT TRAVELING ALONG THE RIVER. IT WAS GOING UPSTREAM ABOUT 30 TO 40 MPH AND TRAVELING OVER THE BRIDGES. WE STOPPED ON THE BRIDGE AND WATCHED IT FOLLOW THE CHICASAHAY RIVER, ABOUT 30 YARDS FROM US. IT WAS BIG, ABOUT 40 TO 50 FEET, IT WAS GOING OVER THE PINE TREES. THERE WERE NO BOATS IN THE WATER AT THAT TIME. WE HEARD NO NOISE OR SOUND COMING FROM IT.
