{cak} Ring of flames descending in trajectory towards the ground

I woke at 2:00am on July 31, 2015. I knew exactly what time it was because every time I wake up I look at my cell phone sitting next to my bed. I went to the bathroom and was sitting on the toilet and when I went to look up at the window (which is right next to me) I noticed something very strange that I have never seen in my life! It was a ring of flames that was burning very bright and it was descending towards the street right in front of my house. I rubbed my eyes quickly to make sure I was not seeing things. I discovered that what I was seeing was for real and it terrified me. Because it was moving ever closer and it was right above treetop level now and I could tell that the path it was on would put it only about 30 feet from where I was at if it was going to touch ground. Which seemed to be very shortly so after witnessing this strange sight for no more than 20seconds I was so afraid I ran into my hallway where there are no windows and stood shaking. And also praying to God that whatever I had just seen would not harm me. After about 10 minutes I got the courage to go to my bedroom and get my phone and call my sister as I was here alone and could not believe what I had just seen! I noticed it was now 2:20am. I was so terrified that I couldn't sleep or go near any windows until it was daylight. Remembering what I saw I'm not sure if there was anything in the center of the Flames because it was very dark all I saw were the ring of flames that were moving in a path towards earth it was easy to see this because the flames were shooting behind it similar to the way there is a kind of a streaming flame from a rocket behind it
