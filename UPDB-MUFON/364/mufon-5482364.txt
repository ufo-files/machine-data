{cak} Object(s) seen flash and streak

I was lying in bed and looking up into the sky through my window. Around 1:30 PM I noticed what appeared to be a flash that lasted about a second. At it's maximum brightness it appeared to be twice as bright as the two nearby stars. Next I noticed a streak in nearly the same location. This was around 1:45AM. It looked like it could have been a meteor. I noticed three more flashes in approximately the same location; one at around 1:50AM, another at around 1:55AM and another around 2:00AM. They all seemed to be in almost the exact same location. I tried to determine if the flashes were the result of reflected lights from the parking lot of the country club underneath my window but I was unable to reproduce the flashes or the streak by simply adjusting my head or my point of view and I could observe no other activity that would have accounted for either the flashes or the meteor-like streak.
