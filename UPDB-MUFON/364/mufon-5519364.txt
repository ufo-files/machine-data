JUST like a star...except it was moving. There were no differences between the UFO and other stars in the sky

I was in the train station in my neighborhood while I was checking out the stars. When, something in my peripheral catches my attention. I was watching a star, slowly move across the sky! I watched very closely so I wouldnt lose the UFO among the other stars. It then made one very abrupt direction change, but continued on its same course across the sky. At that very instant I knew it wasnt an air plane. In fact, I spotted at least 4 planes during the UFO sighting. I recognized that this UFO was way beyond normal altitude for our planes. It was literally, as if a star, a normal star, was moving with intelligence across the sky.

I called my mother to tell her what I saw. She tried to say I must have seen a plane. After getting off the phone, I see ANOTHER one. same deal, except this one does not make any abrupt changes in its flight pattern. It does however look exactly like the other UFO and stars in the sky.
