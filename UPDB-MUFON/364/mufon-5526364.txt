I saw a soundless translucent triangle with four blue lights fly over my neighborhood

I was letting my two dogs out in the back yard , I lit a cigarette and noticed something moving in the sky.I focused in a translucent triangle with four dim blue lights . I watched it fly from south to north.I was confused that it didn't make a sound.I could see a vapor trail as I watched it fly off into the distance, it wasn't moving very fast and it wasn't flying very high.I'm always looking up at the night sky and I can see why no one else noticed the triangle, it was stealth.
