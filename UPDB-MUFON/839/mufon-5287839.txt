Shape shifting blob like ufo with many nearby black dots.

From my wife: I was standing outside of Walgreen's parking lot when small/stationary craft was spotted. Craft appeared stationary at first but then began bobbing motion before twice accelerating left or right and coming to abrupt stop again. from distance it had the possible appearance of a large drone used for photography but apparent distance would have made said drone the size of a SUV. At least a dozen stationary black spheres hovered around larger craft although to the naked eye they were not visible as anything more than a dim rhythmic flashing. Tried to reposition for better view and craft zipped away at 45° angle into high clouds
