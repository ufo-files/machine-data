Orb hovering then shoots right, hovers, shoots left and down hovers then shoots off completely gone.

This happened twenty-one years ago when I was eight years old. My cousin and I were at his grandmother's house in Palm Desert, CA, she lived in the mountains (it was Christmas Eve). We were outside talking and it was already dark (my cousin was older than me, a teenager already). We both saw an orb in the sky, slightly above us but more north in the sky, not flashing or pulsing, just one steady bright light, it was just hovering in the sky. As we watched it, it shot to the right and then stopped and hovered, then it shot to the left and down and stopped and hovered. It then shot off to the right again so fast and was quickly gone. No traces, not a single sound. My cousin joked that it was Santa Claus (probably because I was so young), we have since both discussed the event and agree it was a UFO. I figured I would share just in case anyone else witnessed the same event.
