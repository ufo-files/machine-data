{rjl} Seen through binoculars traveling high over houses a triangle shaped craft with lights. Was seen by a grandfather and his grandchild ( drawing included)

I and my grand child were observing the stars outside our house when an object appeared over the house across the street at a high altitude. At first we thought it was a plane so my grandchild started looking through binoculars and realized it was in the shape of a triangle. It travelled form the NE to the SW at a fairly good pace. This was the same direction we had seen a fireball a few nights before. My Grand child was so impressed by what they saw that they sent me a drawing after going back home.
