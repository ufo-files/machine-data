Like a tiny little baby but with a jason type look

I couldn't BELIEVE what i was seeing. I made contact with the object i felt like i was better. then we saw a ralistic looking statue of a strange baby but with a jason like look. we snapped some photos(i lost the microchip but have the map).then we got out and here i am.
