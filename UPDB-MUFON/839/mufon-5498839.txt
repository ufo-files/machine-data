{cak} Subject looked like a commercial aircraft fuselage without wings and control surfaces.

I was in my back yard heading to my garage back door, when I looked to my right and noticed a reflective cylindrical object moving towards the south approximately 10,000ft. Airspeed appeared to be slower than a commercial aircraft for that altitude, this is what caught my attention. I listened for the sound delay, but it never came. I ran into the garage to grab my video camera with a 40X optical Zoom to get a better look, but I could not find it in my view finder. I never pushed record. I could still see it with my eye, but could never obtain a lock. It went behind some trees and I lost it.
