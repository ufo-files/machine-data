Flying Formation Of Circles In Shape Of An A

At 8pm EST my friend and fellow sky watcher called me and said he was going out to watch for a while so I decided to as well. I have a new gen 3 night scope and my old camcorder at about 8:25 I picked the scope up and the first place I pointed it to look through which was facing north looking North East 80 degrees I seen a formation of dull white circles in formation flying from North to south very fast. The first thing that hit me was this is the same thing I have seen before on a famous you tube video I mean a carbon copy of it. From the time I spotted it, till it was gone was around 20 seconds. When I first seen it I stood up to get a better look and so I could follow it south. It was moving very fast, and was not birds, the scope lets you see amazingly clear.
