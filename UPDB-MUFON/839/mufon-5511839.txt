{dr}Flickering light appeared to be coming at us, then disappeared.

Sitting on my back porch, I live on the edge of the San Gabriel mountain range, there is nothing behind my home but forest, there are very few lights in the area. I noticed as I was looking to the SW a flickering blue/white light that seemed to be coming towards us from estimated 5 miles away. It may have been going away from us, I could not tell, but it seemed to coming towards us headed in a north direction of travel. I noticed it and joked, "check out the UFO", I thought it was an aircraft, normal one, it stayed at aprox the same spot in the sky for about 5 seconds and it disappeared, quickly faded out. It did not go below the horizon, or at least it didn't seem to. The light was in the sky for longer than 5 seconds because I noticed it before that, once I focused on it and pointed it out, it was only visible for a few more seconds and was gone. Never seen anything like that before. I would guess its location aprox above mt Gleason. How I felt? I couldnt explain it away, its the first time Ive seen something and knew something was not right. Somebody else had to have seen it as well.
