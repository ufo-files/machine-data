{rjl} OBSERVED STEALTH BOMBERS CYCLING LIGHTS ON OFF

SITTING AT BASE OF DOME ROCK MTN.5 MILES WEST OF QUARTZITE AZ. ON MINING CLAIM AQUARIOUS AT DUSK WHEN AT EAST SIDE OF MTN. 3 OR 4 B-2 "SPIRIT" STEALTH BOMBERS CAME INTO VIEW. THEY HAD LARGE LIGHTS BELOW EACH WINGTIP THAT WERE CYCLING ON AND OFF SLOWLY. THEY WERE VERY LOW AND ABOUT ONE HALF TO 3/4 OF A MILE FROM US AND PLAINLY VISIBLE IN FADING LIGHT AS THE B-2 BOMBER. THEY MOVED VERY SLOWLY DOWN THE MTN. AND WE WERE ALMOST LEVEL WITH THEM AT THIS POINT LOOKING TOWARD I-10 THEY THEN TURNED TO THE LEFT AND TURNED LIGHTS OFF OVER THE HIGHWAY I-10 EAST TOWARDS PHOENIX. THE NEXT DAY WHILE IN TOWN FOR SUPPLIES HEARD ABOUT THE LIGHTS OVER PHOENIX EVENT. AND HAD A GOOD LAUGH ABOUT IT...!
