Object was travelling South to North in Chilliwack BC. Planes travel west and east here.

On July 28, 2015 at 9:45 PM I walked out my front door to have a cigarette. The sky was clear, and no clouds were visible. I looked up as I do every night, and I seen an object roughly 20,000 to 30,000 feet above me travelling from South to North {Washington State to Alaska}. There were no blinking lights, just 1 solid light that went from Dull to Bright 3 times, then the light just went out, and the object disappeared. Planes in this area travel East to West or West to East {Vancouver to Ontario or Ontario to Vancouver} there were no sounds from the object. I always here jets when they pass over our house 20-30 times per day.
