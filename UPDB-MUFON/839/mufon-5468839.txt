Delta shaped object, hovering over street. No noise, no flashing lights. Observed before view obscured by church spire. Object disappeared once past spire.

I was going back to my place of training after having lunch. As I walked down the street I looked above the spire of an old school where my training took place to see a delta shaped object, perfectly stationary. I did not know, in relation to it's actual position as to how close to the spire it was. I continued to watch, and I tried to work out if it was a helicopter or not. But, there was no noise, no flashing lights, and, it was the wrong shape to be a helicopter. As I continued to walk down the Street I lost view of the object behind the school building/spire. As I got to the area of skyline where the object was again, it had disappeared, but, I had kept my eye on the general area for any movement, but saw none.
