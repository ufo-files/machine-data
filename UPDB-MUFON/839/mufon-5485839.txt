3 ufo's while New Year 2016 fireworks were flashing

1. Where were you and what were you doing at the time?
We were in the garden looking at the fireworks around.

2. What made you first notice the object?
My wife told me that she saw object in the air while I was back in the house.

3. What did you think the object was when you first noticed it?
Non airplane because quite and no light blinking like an airplane.
The airport schedule was showing no airplane at that time on the website.

4. Describe the object and its actions and motions in detail.
There were several objects (3) , coming from south west and south east.
They were all heading north, same pace.

5. Describe your feelings, reactions and actions, during and after sighting the object.
We were quite excited (Myself, my wife and my son).

6. How did you lose sight of the object?
After few minutes when being far away.
