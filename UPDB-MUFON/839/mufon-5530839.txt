Why all the UFOs?

While I was watching TV I was occasionally looking out my living room window. One time when I got out of my chair looking outside I saw a brief image of a purple plane without any back wings. It promptly went behind a tree on my front lawn. It was only about 55 high. Then minutes later I saw in the south a tiny dot of white light traveling eastward. It would get big and bright then dim then tiny again...I just stopped watching it, because it greatly resembled the lights I filmed between 2003 to 2005.
