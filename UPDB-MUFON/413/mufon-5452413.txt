brightly colored drone-like object

I was on my ninth floor balcony just after sunset looking at the sky and ocean on a beautiful quiet night. I went inside but then I noticed a brightly colored glowing drone-like object coming towards my kitchen window. I thought it might be a drone but it was too fast and quiet and changing directions quickly in a v-shape pattern. I grabbed my cell phone to videotape it and the lights faded out and it darted around the front corner of my building and seemed to vanish. I ran to the front balcony and it was nowhere in site. I had the definite sense it was a UFO, not a drone or manmade object.
I thought I'd videotaped the object but what showed on my camera was just a still image of the object as it must have been moving away, and a second all-black image, which I've never seen before on any of my cameras. Unfortunately I didn't capture an image when the object was close to me, colorfully lit and somewhat magical in feeling.
