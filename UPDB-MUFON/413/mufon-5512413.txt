{nd} While I was sleeping a bright light shone through the window and paralyzed me

When I was 15 I was visiting cousins in Ashtabula, Ohio in the summer of 1967. On my first night there, I was sleeping alone in a separate room on the second floor. I had gone to sleep about 10pm. In the dead of night, appx 2am I was awoken by a bright light shining on me from the sky through the window. I found that I couldnt move. I was confused, but did not feel threatened. I didn't react at all, but went straight back to sleep.It felt as if I was being observed or examined, but also being communicated with. I remembered the event the next morning but for some reason did not say anything to anybody, until right now.
