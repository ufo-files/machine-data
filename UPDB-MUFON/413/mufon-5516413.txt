{cak} triangle of white circles moving quickly across sky then disappearing like it appeared

I was lying atop a building on my back because Im homeless and no one can see me sleeping up there. There wasnt a star in the sky that night, which was disappointing because there was a meteor shower taking place. The lights seemed to come from behind the clouds, or just appear from nowhere, on my right side of the sky, which was probably East at the time. It flew in a straight line at a fast speed, but not quite as fast as a shooting star. It disappeared the way it appeared after flying across about half the sky in my field of vision - pretty much the exact middle section, directly above me. 
I did see a quick white light flash in the distance a while after that, but by then Id taken my glasses off, so well assume that was a meteor.
