Woke up to a glowing bright blue metallic oval shaped ufo hovering 5 feet away from my window

I'm now in my later 20s, and I was 12 then. I was sleeping in the partially finished room that was to be a future bathroom of my house when a bright blue light woke me up around 3AM. I got out of bed and looked towards the large window and on the other side was an oval object, about 5 feet away, and brilliantly shrouded in a blueish white aura. It looked like a solid color but also metal, it's hard to explain. It just looked like pure energy or something.

I was petrified, I could do nothing but turn, and steadily walk to my actually bedroom, lie in my bed and immediately pass out.

I woke up in the unfinished bathroom the next morning. This was the first of many strange encounters and phenomenon I would experience in the coming years. A few of these events I believe involved greys which I will be submitting reports for separately
