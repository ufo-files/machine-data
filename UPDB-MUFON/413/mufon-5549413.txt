set up camcorder to film what I thought were rods, turned out to be something else.

I was over at a friends helping to finish some odds and ends on a deck we had just rebuilt a few days before when I decieded to play around with his camcorder and set it up on a tripod , pointed it up at an aprox. 65degree angle just looking past the edge of his roof some 20+ feet up and zoomed out to 40x and let her run for an hour adjusting for the sun every now and then. After that time I went into the house and started to download whatever I had on tape into the computer. After about 15 minutes my friend said we had to go so I stoped downloading and we went to do something else. It was a week later before I could spend some more time going over what I had downloaded and in that time my friend had taken his camcorded to a family reunion and had taped over what I had taped.I had started to go through it frame by frame because whatever I had taped was moving to fast to see well. Thats when I saw the two objects for the first time and my heart jumped. The objects were traveling in a east to west direction at a very high rate of speed. What ever the other objects were they to are traveling in pairs.You have to view the video frame by frame to realy see whats passing by.
