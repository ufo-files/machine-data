Oval shaped craft with red tail

My brother and I walked out of my friends house at exactly 1:30am on Tuesday April 22, 2008. Exactly two minutes into our walk we observed a bright white oval object with a redish-orange tail moving slowly above us in the western direction. The craft appeared to be the size of a dime at arms length. The craft made no discernable noise as it passed slowly overhead. Without warning it flashing a bright green color then all color ceased to reveal a smaller albeit same shaped black object that shot out of view over several rooftops.
