My friend Kim and I we're playing in the driveway I'm not sure what we were doing. but suddenly we found ourselves staring just across the street above the telephone pole this round Big Mac shape UFO with lights flashing and Windows around the center .. the lights seem to dim a little. We were just staring then I asked Kim if she is seeing what I am seeing and she said yes go get Mom.. she called my( foster momm)mom too. And I said no you go get her then we just stood there staring both of us was Motionless I don't think we moved a muscle and I remember telling myself don't ever doubt what you are seeing right now. Then I remember thinking how quiet it was it didn't make not one noise and I remember trying to look in the windows that seem to go clear around it but I couldn't see in. I was a little scared, then just as silently started going above the trailer park to the top of the hill then silently it shot off like a flash of light and was gone and me and my friend ran into the house I remember grabbing something to eat off the counter and my mom said where have you guys been and we said we were out in the driveway we just saw a UFO!! and we knew she wouldn't believe us and strangely we didn't even try to convince her we just ran to my room and it was many many years later that me and Kim even talked about it. Then we both now still to this day know that what we saw was not of this world.
