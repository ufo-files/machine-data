{tjd} Small and medium round lights.

Saw a long line of small and medium lights following one another in a straight line. Moved from the west to Switzerland in an upward line. As they reached a certain altitude they disappeared. Only lasted 1-2 minutes too fast to get our phones and record. Like nothing we've ever seen and definitely not a meteor shower,falling star or anything natural because it was going up not sideways or down.
