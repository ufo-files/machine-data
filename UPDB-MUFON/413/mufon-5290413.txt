White, silvery reflective object. Approx 500yrds south of my residence moving first north then west as I drove south to my residence from Bronte Tx.

I had just looked at the vehicle clock, noting to my passenger how long it took us to go get some snacks in a small town down the highway from my country residence. It was 3:37pm.  As we approached to about half a mile from the highway turn off to my residence I noticed a bright white circular object which appeared to be moving north as we traveled south. It was at an approximate angle of 30* through my front windshield just left of the highway about 500 yards further down the highway from the turn to the residence. I would guess it was around 800-1200ft above the ground. I pointed it out to my passenger and asked if he sees it. He responded yes and we both said almost at the same time, “What is it?” As we watched it abruptly turned west and when it did there was a reflective silvery gleam, and I could now see it had an oval shape with a flat bottom. It did not move west in a straight line. It made a swooping movement, first dropping then rising back to original height, as if making a “U” shape path.  As it moved it seemed to leave a trail that remained a constant length but vanished easily. I interpreted the trail at the time to an atmospheric reaction caused by the object. As it moved west in this swooping motion it got brighter, until after making three swooping motions it seemed to just vanish, or shot away so fast it looked like it vanished. We were both astounded.
