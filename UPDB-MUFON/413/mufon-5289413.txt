Three glowing lights observed NW of Bayswater, VIC, Australia

Three Glowing lights of differing colors witnessed by myself. One white, one orange, and one blue. Motion pulsated and zoomed in to and out of orbit on what seemed to be a direct intergalactic flight path. Observable for roughly one hour. Engaged contact and entered in to dialogue with SHIP commander on board the IFO with blue and orange lights.
