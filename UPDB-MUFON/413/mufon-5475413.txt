{nd} Saw a still silvery sphere.

At 7:30 in the evening of June 18th, 2017,I saw a silver spot as large as the planet Venus while leaving my neighborhood greenbelt for the sidewalk I was watching it for two minutes and a small dot with a con trail before the silver spot did a six second vanishing. The small dot with a con-trail remained between the Jemez River and the Zuni Mountains.
 The dot was about seven degrees lower than where the silver spot had been.
I was disbelieving until it disappeared over six seconds, and then I had to acknowledge that I was a witness.
