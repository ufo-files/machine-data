{nd} It's been for awhile now

I was at my apartment outside smoking a cigarette. What made me notice it was that it looked like a start but I know stars don't move the way this was. I thought it was a star. It was an orb glowing white, reddish orange, some with blue green color in it but the ones I normally are either all white or white and reddish orange. The orb would zig zag, ascend up quickly, it would make sharp direction change, and it would hover. I was intrigued/fascinated when I first seen them and still am. After the many incidents I just wanted friends and family to witness what I did but they thought I was crazy. Basically it just disappeared in the night sky because it was so high up.
