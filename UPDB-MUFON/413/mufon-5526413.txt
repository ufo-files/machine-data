Object hovered, moved toward us, then went back and up, into the clouds

On Saturday, March 13, at approximately 1:30 p.m., my brother and I observed a rather large silver colored round object hovering just below the clouds at the Ft. Harrison and Lafayette Avenue intersection area in Terre Haute, Indiana, which in the north/northwest part of Terre Haute. It had what appeared to be a large, black antenna like object on top of it. I saw what I thought was a red streak against black on the side of the object. This object hovered below the clouds, moved forward a bit, in the direction toward where my brother and I sat in my car watching it, then it started to move back and up, becoming smaller as it disappeared behind the thick, dark clouds.
