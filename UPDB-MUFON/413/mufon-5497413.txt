{nd} bright light with crescent trail

I was outside my home smoking a cigarette and I was looking south south-west at the stars and a strange object caught my eye. I yelled for my wife to come out side to see what I was seeing. My wife and kids came running out and saw the bright yellow/orange light that had a crescent shaped trail heading from west to east. We observed the object for about 20 seconds and then the object disappeared into thin air and then about a second later there was a flash where it had vanished. We stood there with our mouths open not believing what we had just witnessed.
