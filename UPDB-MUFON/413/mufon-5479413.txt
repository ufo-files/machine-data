my girlfriend went out to the back deck around 8pm last night, I turned off the porch light and jokingly said, its so clear maybe we will see a ufo!
just then she said what is that, I looked up and coming from the west, in a fairly slow flight, was an object, with all kinds of lights dancing around it, looking like it was throwing pulsating lights from it, it slowly came over the house, heading east, then it went straight up, looked like it was shapeshifting, the lights slowly went out, then we saw a whiteish, pink straight line, and it was gone, just like a zipper opened up in the sky , it was gone.
now the strange part,we were still looking up about a min later, I looked to the west and saw 2 more lights coming, these were kinda blinking like airplane lights, one was on the exact path of the ufo, the other was north a little bit, they passed over , that's when we werent sure if they were jets because all we heard was a faint low rumbling noise . if we have jets that make no sound, no telling what we have.  
the damnest thing I have ever seen, both of us now know that we are not alone . 
we were both so mesmerized we didn't even think to grab a camera or phone 
but I will see it plain as day for the rest of my life.
