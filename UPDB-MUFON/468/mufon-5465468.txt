A red bright object over ida mountain

an object with two or more exterior lights was seen by me and my boyfriend at october 2nd 2018 near the mount ida. it was completely noiseless. we weren't able to spot the exact shape but it had at least two bright headlights and was going straight from North to north east. a commercial plane was nearby (crossing from west to east) and as usual it had a continuously flashing light. that's how we noticed the difference between an airplane and a ufo.

after slowly going straight to north-east it suddenly dissapeared without making any noise or leaving any trace behind. just vanished...
