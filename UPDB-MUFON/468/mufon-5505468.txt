{cak} 2 witness at 2 locations, we saw a large round red light, with the suface of a disk reflecting slightly, watched for about 12 minutes then it winked out.

On a Thursday at 10:50pm my room mate called me from a nearby Applebys and had me go out to my driveway where I saw an approx. 30ft bright red light hovering in the sky about a mile away from both our POVs. Looking to be about 500ft altitude. Light stayed constant, no pulsing. My room mate was able to see top of a disk shape directly below the light as though the light was an upper part of this disk. We both stayed on the phone with each other til the light winked out. Warm night about 70 degrees. Nothing special about the area over which it was hovering, a populated part of town (actually right over a friends house as it looked) But I guess that usually the case.
