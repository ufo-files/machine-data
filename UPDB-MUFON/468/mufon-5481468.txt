West Europe coast summer 2016

Strange, dancing lights over early hour sky, by the seaside at a busy west Europe resort town.
Video recorded with a SONY Xperia Z5P phone's camera and has an impressive soundtrack as well.
Nearby moving and stationary light sources are to blame for the annoying light flares and could perhaps be obliterated, but also serve as landmarks for estimation of spatial geometry (daytime and nigh-time photos also provided). The camera zoom is used to observe the object's unbelievable form from a closer distance and follow its course. The ights are appearing during the first 1:30 and for the remaining two minutes the surroundings are recorded.
The incident was observed from the 5th floor of a busy hotel room overlooking the sea, nearby beach, distant lighthouse and sea-town. It coincided with the start of the pokemon go madness during Summer 2016 in Europe where people were actually searching everywhere with torches even at 02:00 AM.
The video represents the first 3,3 minutes of a 15 min recording that has not been altered, manipulated or otherwise changed. The trimming and extraction to pc file was performed with official Sony software. The same applies for the photos.
