Falcon 9 Booster Re-entry Televised from May 30 Launch at 3:22pm ET. UAP at +6.00 min to +6.04 min

Observe the television broadcast of the Falcon 9 First Stage Booster re-entry. Following booster separation and starting at +06.00 minutes to +06.04 minutes an object can be seen, in the camera tail view of the booster, moving at what appears to be a lower altitude, perhaps at or below cloud level. The object can be observed moving left to right in close visual proximity of the stabilizing fin on the right hand side of the screen. Using slow motion it appears that the object is at or below cloud level, very large and moving at an extreme velocity relative to the earth surface.
