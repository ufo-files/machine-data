{rjl} Silent object flying fast and lights that went from 'wing to wing'. Very smooth, fast with an abrupt turn to disappear.

Four neighbors were sitting in our street (no street lights). We heard a commercial jet fly high above which is a typical flight path for San Jose or San Fran airports. The jet was approximately 20k feet up. Below the jet and going in a similar direction (from south to north), we saw an object that had two white lights. The object was not visible but the lights were. The lights appeared to be on the tips and were not blinking. The lights then moved and traded places. The lights 'slid' from one end to the other at one point they crossed over eachother. It was very smooth and the object was going very fast. The lights immediately traded places again sliding across the object and ending at the left and right tips as it flew north. The object then abruptly yet smoothly turned NW and disappeared. This freaked us out as the object was silent and moved very quickly and smoothly. Once the steady lights moved and slid from left to right and right to left trading places, made it even more eery. The object suddenly veered right and dissappeared into thin air. It was a clear night, no clouds. This is my first report and I was accompanied by active two law enforcement people.
