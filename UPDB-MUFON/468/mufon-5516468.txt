(GS) 5 lights connected/moving together, moving very slowly in a monitoring like fashion. Light formation was : . :

August 11, 2011. 8:45 pm. Tarpon Springs, Florida. (Pinellas County)

Driving southbound on US 19. N, in Tarpon Springs just past MLK Blvd, middle lane. Companion and I noticed strange lights in the east. We were watching the eastern sky due to a thunderstorm in east Tampa, that had a great deal of lighting, but seemed like heat lighting from a distance. The lights in the sky we saw were in a formation like this : . : with nothing in between, but they moved together, and seemed to be on the same object based on movement. We could see the lighting from a storm in east Tampa in between the lights. We thought the size to be larger then most aircraft known in the area, if the lights were connected to the same object. The two sets of lights on the left and right flashed in rhythm It seemed to be over Lake Tarpon, and moving incredibly slow northward. We watched this while driving to Klosterman Road, then watched more while at the left turn light. Then watched as it turned to the east and began to move slowly towards the east lake area of Tarpon Springs/Trinity, at a slight angle. We did a U turn at Klosterman, and lost sight of it while heading north, the treed area around Anderson Park blocked our view. We continued north to Tarpon Ave, made a right on to E. Tarpon Ave/Keystone Road, where we turned right onto Lake Tarpon Ave, and drove to the dock area by Billys Tarpon Turtle. We were unable to see it after Anderson Park, and decided to go home and report it.
