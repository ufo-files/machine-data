{nd} Saw two round lights on one side of barn Came back and saw 3 round lights

It was 5:50AM Monday, Nov. 1, 2014, I was taking out my cat's garbage when I saw two round lights on the right side of my barn. My motion lights were not on, there was no noise,just these two rounded lights. When I returned from the garbage pickup spot, I looked down at the barn, this time there were three rounded lights on the opposite side of the barn. This particular spot, where the lights were, no human could possibly stand or set. Then the lights just turned off. I wasn't frightened but found it strange. These rounded lights just hovered on each side of the barn and turned off.
