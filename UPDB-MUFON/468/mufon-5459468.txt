Sixty years ago a friend, Alex and I witnessed a UFO hovering near a blacktop highway leading to Julian, California. It was around 9 p.m. at night. Alex grudgingly stopped the car so I could go to the other side of the highway and get a closer look at it. I watched the circular shaped crafter hover over the ground for about thirty minutes waving at it trying to get some form of communication. I was 19. Alex was 24. The UFO lit up the area. In the dome of the craft I saw a pulsating red light which slowly got brighter and darker. Around the UFO there were small portholes, I couldn't see inside. I have tried for years to make people aware of our findings. Please believe this actually happened. Later at home we both realized we had LOST THREE HOURS OF TIME. After an approximate 30 minutes the UFO went up straight then tilted to a 45 degree angle and sped off.
