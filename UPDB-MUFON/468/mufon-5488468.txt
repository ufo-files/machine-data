{nd} Noticed a cloud that looked strange and it absorbed into a solid sphere then before I knew it there may have been up to ten above me for hours.

I was smoking a cigarette on the front porch of my home and noticed odd movement in the clouds. It changed shape and looked very solid. Before the night was up I'd had ten or more above me and they chased each other and flew in the fastest I've ever seen an human aircraft go making sharp turns and pulsing off. The whole time I had a pulsating hum in my ears and felt oddly happy and content. I've had trouble sleeping since then and notice new marks on my body weekly.
