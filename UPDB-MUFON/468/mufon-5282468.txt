white, reflective sphere

seen above Gardnerville Nv- Feb 24 2022 ..... A 747 airbus fly's over town every day from Dubia to LAX usually around 12:00pm. But on Thursday Feb 24 2022 it was 15 minutes early which surprised me, so I was fixated on it for some reason. About 2 minutes behind it was a very shinny, white orb looking anomaly. When my eyes finally focused I realized it was a solid, white, reflective sphere. At first it was doing about the same speed as the airbus and then it slowed down to almost a hover and turned slightly to the east ( it was flying from the north-east to the south-west) after it slowed down it started picking up speed again. Not instant speed, gradual and then it started picking up speed very quickly and by the time I got the binoculars it was gone, vanished. The sun was almost right above it, about 5 degrees to the west of it so I could see the curve of the craft very clearly.
