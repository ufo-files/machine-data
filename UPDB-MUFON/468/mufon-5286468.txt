spheres and rings of light

I was at a ranch at night, between 10pm and midnight, watching television. Saw something bright outside, went to observe. Saw multiple spheres high up in the atmosphere, possibly in space? Unsure. Looked like pictures of supernovae with rings of light growing outward from the spheres. They all pulsated in random succession for several minutes. I went in the house to grab a camera and came back out. One final pulse of light, brighter than all the others, and the spheres/lights were just gone.
