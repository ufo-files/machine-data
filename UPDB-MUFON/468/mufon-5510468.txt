{cak} Satellite-like with a wobbly path

My wife and I both saw this light in the sky at the same time. We live in a city of 200K people so there is plenty of light pollution. It was a clear night and we saw what appeared to be a satellite. It was not blinking so I ruled out plane. It was moving a little faster than most satellites typically do. It was a very weak light as if a dim star but being able to see it in the middle of town it must have been brighter than it appeared. I was moving across the sky and then began to wobble making several "U" shaped deviations from the flight path and then began going straight again. I thought my eyes were playing tricks on me but my wife saw the same thing at the same time I did. It was visible for less than 1 minute and then faded away. As soon as it disappeared an airplane flying at relatively low altitude (landing at nearby airport) went by overhead. (2 witnesses)
