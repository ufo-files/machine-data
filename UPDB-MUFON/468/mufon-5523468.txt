UFO Over Lees Summit....Amazing Sight!

I was setting on my Deck in Lees Summit about 8-9pm on the 4th/Oct when I saw a HUGE V-Shaped craft flying south directly over me. I mean right above me which seemed like 500 feet or so. Its kinda hard to tell at night but this UFO was bigger than a NFL football field easily. It was going at an incredible slow pace and very low. It was so low I could hear a very slight HUM coming from it as its usually very quite outside at that time. I know many say they are quite but I could hear something. I could see many blue & white lights blinking at times but at times they were solid. I could also see what seemed to be 6 dark circles underneath (3 on each side) of the V-Shape what I believed to be the engines or turbines. Again very quite with a very slight hum. As it flew south it stopped and just hovered for a couple of minutes the turned to the left and went east for I say 5 miles then it turned again very abruptly in a very sharp L shaped turn. I knew no plan or helicopter can do that so it was a UFO---FOR SURE!. Then it slowly moved north at about 40-50 miles an hour until it it seemed like it was about 15 miles away then all of a sudden dropped down out of site. I will watch the skies every night around that time as I am not the only one who saw this. On a side note: This is the second time I have seen a UFO. The other time was in the late 60's early 70's in Mission Kansas. Are UFO's real? You bet they are! There are billions of stars and planets out there. Do you really think we are the only one with life on it? Anyone who thinks other wise are blind.
