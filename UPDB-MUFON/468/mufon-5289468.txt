Around 9 reddish orbs high up in sky, strange behavior

This is from years ago in the area of Fort Howard, MD. I witnessed about 9 reddish orbs very high up in the sky/in space. They at first appeared to be stationary, but upon closer examination I noticed they were sort of vibrating or pulsing. They were in a random formation that looked like a scatter plot. After watching them for about 3 min, they suddenly one by one faded while moving upwards at a diagonal angle.
