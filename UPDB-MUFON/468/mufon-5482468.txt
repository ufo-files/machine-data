{nd} Brilliant yellow-orange flying flame-like object

We were on the front porch watching the night sky and fireworks. We saw this object twice. The objects took the same path; about three minutes apart, approximately. The object was moving across the sky from a west direction to an east direction. It didn't move like a plane. We had been seeing planes that evening, but this didn't make any sounds, nor were there any trails behind it. It just appeared in front of us. We didn't know what the object was, because it didn't look like anything we had seen during the evening or ever. The object made a straight path. It didn't waiver or move erratically. It was this yellow-orange ball of light that flew silently. We felt astounded and confused over this sighting. The object made a sudden appearance and when it was going east, it just disappeared.
