Object moving extremely fast with the brightest red, green and blue lights.

Observed on the 20th of October 2019 around 2:30 AM on Kyrenia, Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus. The sky was clear without any clouds and so on..An object suddenly appeared in the night sky with extremely bright red, green and blue lights. I moved extremely fast from East to West and then made a 90 degree tight turn to North towards Turkey and disappeared in a second. The whole event took almost 2 seconds and the distance it covered is impossible to be reached by any means of transportation we have known so far! I am speechless. The colors of the craft looked like neon lights which are amazingly bright and made me feel a kind of joy and happiness. Just like a kid who saw colorful candies.
