4 ships silver and black with red lights

My 8 year old was lying on the trampolene w/a friend when they saw the triangle hover over them. They came in and were jumping up and down screaming and told us about the ships. They said there were 4 of them in a rectangular shape that broke apart into individual ships. This happened right above them they said. They said that there was a bigger light that one of the ships went to, one went off south, one went east and the other hovered over them and eventually went off towards the back of the house where there is woods and a river. They heard a noise with it, they described it as a booping sound. They are totally believeable and very insitent that it happened. This would be the second time in 2 years we have seen something like this. Another neighbor has seen something prior to this as well but will not report it for fear of ridicule.
