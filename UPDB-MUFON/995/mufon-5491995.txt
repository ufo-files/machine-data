{jpg} Bright star like lights, western pa

Driving home from local convenient store at 9:46 pm, I was on the phone with my daughter and noticed what looked like 2 big, bright stars in the sky, one seemed much brighter than the other, there was a gap between them and they were side by side and at one point I thought there was something in between them. As I turned onto my road heading home I noticed the star like light on the left seemed bigger and brighter. If the objects were moving they were so slight that I could hardly tell. There was nothing else viewable in the sky, no clouds, no other stars and no aircraft. The only reason I lost sight of it was because I arrived at home and tried to return to my conversation that I had started earlier on the phone.
