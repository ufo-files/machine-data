Saw a vague silhouette of something that was approx 300 feet long, seemed almost transparent being followed by a black helicopter

In my backyard just after dusk, I saw and heard a black helicopter with a noise different than the usual police helicopters here. It was following a small but bright red light approximately 10 helicopter lengths in front of it. Then a small but very blue light appeared approximately 300 feet in front of the red light. The helicopter and the 2 lights were traveling very slowly from south to north. There was still a little light as the sun was not completely set and as it traveled under a cloud I saw a very faint object silhouette between the red and blue lights. It appeared to be a craft. 
It was shaped somewhat like an armadillo with segmented triangular areas. These lights were attached to the craft. 
The helicopter and the object were approximately 1 mile away from me and maybe 2000 feet up and definitely not following the normal police helicopter flight path here in Mesa.
The whole sighting took less than 2 minutes and I lost line of sight as it moved north. I went around the house to my front yard but didn't see it again.
I never look for these things, have an open mind but didn't know what to think of this but thought I should report it to someone!
