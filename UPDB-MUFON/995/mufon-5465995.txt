{jpg} Object hovering as I passed on road. Triangle pattern of unblinking lights.

On my way to work I saw a lighted object hovering near a neighbors house. Object itself was vague due to darkness but at each of its 3 corners there was a different color light, burning steady. I slowed to a crawl trying to make our what was going on and assuming my neighbor had a mishap and that it was an air ambulance. I could hear it but it didn't have a chopper sound (no rotor noise) instead sounded like some sort of motor hum and a whooshing sort of sound for lack of better description. No other activity was observed at the neighbors' house. Passing by I turned around at the next intersection (appx. 1000 feet past it) and came right back but there was no longer any sign of it and no receding lights in the clear night sky. Strange.
