{rjl} Light pattern on ground.

In approximately August of 2003, my girlfriend and I were walking to Skylandia Park in Tahoe City CA at night. We frequently went there at night to sit on the dock and watch the stars. On this particular night, we were walking through the park side by side when a pattern of very bright light appeared on the ground directly in front of us. There were several circles of light about 1' to 2' in diameter that appeared and began to move erratically inside of a circle about 10' to 15' wide. There was nothing but a large pine tree above us. I looked up, but did not see anybody, nor did I see any objects. I have no idea where the light came from. There were no beams either. My girlfreind, who is now my ex wife, saw the exact same thing. The light was extremely bright. We both watched these circles of light dance around for about a minute or 2 and then they were gone. I have no explanation for what this could have been, but am hoping someone else may have experienced something similar.
