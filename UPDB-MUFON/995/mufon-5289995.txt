Large sparkling object hovering in place

My husband and I saw a bright, sparkling object in the sky below and to the left of Orion's left foot. It sparkled with red, green, blue with a white body.  At times it would dim and then get bright and start sparkling with all the different colors.

At first we thought it was a drone hovering over Snoqualmie Falls taking footage of the falls at night (that sometimes happens). It did not move in the sky. We ruled out helicopter as there was no sound. We checked to see if there was a bright star or planet; the brightest star in that general area is Aldebaran, but it is up above and to the right of Orion. We're out in the country and the light pollution is low. I was able to get some photos and video.

Not sure what it is.
