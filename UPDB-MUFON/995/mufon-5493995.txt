{cak} saw bright white objects moving from northwest to east

I was standing outside talking on the phone with a friend. I was talking about how nice of a night it was...so clear and calm and checking out the stars. At the time i was looking south/southeast and started to turn looking east than towards the north when i seen 5 white objects in the sky. At first i thought they were birds or bats. I then started to think,its so dark out and they were kinda high up to see them so clearly and just how white they were. There was no sounds. The objects were flying in a V formation and two of the objects started moving out of the formation, kinda falling behind and irratically moving in the sky for about 30 seconds before rejoining the formation. I lost sight of the objects as the kept traveling east. It had to have been at least a mile away towards the east when i was no longer able to see the objects. I was on the phone the whole time describing what i was seeing to my friend. I was and still am confused by what I had seen. Sense i was on my phone i was unable to get any pictures or video of the objects. This happened on Tuesday May 12, 2015 around 1130 pm. I live close to an air force base and see different kinds of aircraft throughout my lifetime, but what i have never seen anything like this before. I've been keeping an eye out the past few nights and have not seen the objects again.
