Light was outside over whole building

There was a light over the whole building - building is over 300 ft long - the bright light flooded into our windows and woke us up for about 20 minutes. Then the light began pulsating like a strobe light . This was all outside of the building not in our apts. At the same time another resident reported that the magnetic fire doors slammed shut , however , they could not be pushed open by hand. that is not how they are meant to function. Eventually they we opened. All of the electric clocks in residents apartments were off and some stoves (all are electric) still do not work. The light vanished abruptly. It was -14 degrees outside at the time.
