Clear blue sky, wearing contrast enhancing sunglasses, we see a white elongated object flying at high speed making a curved flight path

Laying in my hammock enjoying the quiet after a father's day brunch in our garden, I'm watching the sky noticing how clear it is. Then seeing a white object moving at seemingly high speed compared to the airplanes and even satellites we regularly see. I notice I can only see it distinctfully with my contrast enhancing sunglasses. My wife briefly sees it without and puts on her sunglasses too, she then is also capable of seeing it better. My son of 12 years is also shortly able to see it. It moves out of sight in eastly direction, my wife then shortly after sees it again coming from SE direction making a rather sharp turn to the East again. Shortly after it disappeared in the haze of what is probably a light cloud covering in the distance. As we have extensive experience in seeing and identifying all kinds of planes both civilian, commercial and military, as well as drones and satellites, we were quickly very convinced it was non of those. We live not very far from a military base and experience frequent fly-overs from fighter jets and military transportation. Those always move over our neighborhood in the direction of where this object was coming from or going too. Not too far from our neighborhood, there is both military stockage of nuclear armory and a research center about nuclear waste. From our house those are located in the same direction as the object was last seen.
