{rjl} It looked like a shooting star, but green and bigger. It was a ball of light falling.

I was at work. I went outside to smoke. I often look at the sky when outside at night. I looked left and noticed a green light, circular, and hazy. It fell like a shooting star, but straight down. I work near the airport and I see so many planes, and I know this was not a plane. The light was constant, not blinking, and it was a large ball of light, not little flashing lights like on a plane. It appeared to be glowing. It dropped out of sight quickly, just a few seconds. It fell straight down, and I said "wow" out loud. I looked around to see if I could see any other lights, or anything else, but did not.
