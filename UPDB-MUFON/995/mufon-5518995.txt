SAW TWO LIGHTS LOOKED LIKE FIRE FROM A CANDLE MOVINE EAST TO WEST AT CANNOMNGATE GOLF COURSE

iT WAS ABOUT 2:30 IN THE MORNING DECIDED TO GO TO McDonalds in Fairburn to get something to eat. Turned left off of Minix RD. onto Tyrone RD. saw two light hovering over golf course Told friend to pull over got out of van and watched light cross rd and move slowiny west to east. Watched lights for a couple of mins. One light shot off east to west very fast and went out of sight. The other light moved to the same spot in the sky and did the same.
