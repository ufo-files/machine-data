i was filming in 240FPS per second with my GOPRO and noticed an anomaly in the video when i got back home

myself and 2 friends where at a slate mine near Kingsbury Quebec, i had recently purchased a GOPRO hero 3 black and wanted to try out it's "slow-motion" capabilities so throughout the day we shot multiple shots of ourself fooling around etc etc. at the end of the day i got home and started looking at the footage, and very rudimentarily slowing it down by bringing down the percentage of the playback speed on my finalCUT. in one of the videos with the video at 3% speed i noticed a small blip come out of the horizon take an abrupt turn and fly away into the distance all in .2 sec (real time) i've cut the video to include this.
