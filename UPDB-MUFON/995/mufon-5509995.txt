{nd} Bright blue white light moving south east. No sound, could see air planes heading in to Philly to compare object

I was walking my daughter to my mothers house. We both saw a bright blue white object moving south east. There was no sound. We could see other aircraft near by to compare the object to. I was in the Civil Air Patrol and could clearly see this was not an aircraft. This was not landing lights, I know the difference between landing lights and what we saw. The light pulsed becoming darker blue, there seemed to be a distortion in the light then it just blinked out. I checked and could not find a satellite on the internet that could account for what we saw. This was not a meteor or at least it did not have a tail nor did it descend at an angle as I have witnessed meteors before. Not sure what was but it was not Venus, Mars or swamp gas. My real hope was it was simply a satellite.
