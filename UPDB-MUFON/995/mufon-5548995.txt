MULTIPLE WITNESS SIGHTING OF A DIAMOND SHAPED OBJECT SEPTEMBER OF 1999

THE WITNESS, A STATE TROOPER AT THE TIME SAW AN OBJECT FROM THE DECK OF HIS HOME. HE WAS ENTERTAINING GUESTS IN HIS HOME AT THE TIME. HIS BROTHER AND SEVERAL OTHER FRIENDS THAT WERE PRESENT SAW THE DIAMOND SHAPED OBJECT IN THE SKY FOR SEVERAL MINUTES. IT WAS TRAVELING IN A SOUTH TO NORTH DIRECTION. THE SKY WAS CLEAR AT 10 PM THE NIGHT THE SIGHTING TOOK PLACE. THE OBJECT APPEARED TO BE SPINNING, AT TIMES IT WOULD DESCEND AND AT OTHER TIMES ASCEND. THE OBJECT BECAME BRIGHTER AND GLOWING AT TIMES. DURATION OF THE SIGHTING WAS 5-10 MINUTES. THE OBJECT TRAVELED VERY SLOWLY ACROSS THE EVENING SKY. AT TIMES IT BECAME STAIONERY, THEN CONTINUED MOVING UNTIL IT WAS LOST FROM SIGHT.
