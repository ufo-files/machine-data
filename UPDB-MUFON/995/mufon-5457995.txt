{rjl} Big Bright Light

I turned around to face my wife at the front door from our driveway and I looked up and saw a big bright light, like landing lights on a plane. Right when I turned around it dimmed to almost where we couldn't see it. My wife and I watched it move from south to north in the sky. Its flight path was like a slithering snake.
