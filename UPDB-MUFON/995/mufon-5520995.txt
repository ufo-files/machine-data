2 white semi-airplane shaped objects came over Bakers mtn, a 3rd streaked between them they turned and followed, faster than possible

i was looking out of my front windows at bakers mtn, watching the clouds, looking at the tree colors, two objects that i first thought were airplanes came slowly over the mtn barely above the treetops, as they got closer, i stepped outside, i could see that they were solid white and strangely shaped for airplanes, when they got about 500ft from the ground they flew straight, no sounds at all, a 3rd craft, solid white, came from the east head-on course toward the first two, it was moving so fast i cant tell you its shape, it flew directly between the other two and up the side of the mtn, the first two did what i researched and found to be an Immelman turn maneuver, then pursued the other two again accelerating at an impossible rate making absolutely no sound, i didn't think about reporting it at the time, i just found that there have several sightings in this area and thought that this may help
