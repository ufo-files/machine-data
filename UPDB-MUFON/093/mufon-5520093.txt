Black round circle going south to north

I was standing in back yard with my dog when I noticed a black round object flying from south to north to my left. My initial thought was what is that? It was not an airplane it was not a helicopter and it was not a bird. It was moving at a significant speed with no sound and as it moved trees began to obstruct my view so I ran to get a better look and it appear for emit a light from inside the object (a dull white light briefly pulsed) it was not aircraft lighting I am sure of that. then it disappeared into the cloud cover. It was a rainy day and I am not sure what the cloud deck was but it was below cloud level. After about 20 seconds it disappeared into the clouds. My initial reaction was confusion, then curiosity. I only wish I had a camera in my hands.
