{rjl} Large Cone shape with flat top

The object was very large (estimated to be the size of an aircraft carrier) cone shape, dark gray in color. The object had a flat top along the entire craft. It stayed fixed in the sky at a distance from the witness who was driving NE on I-8 from Yuma towards Gila Bend. The witness' husband is a Marine stationed in Yuma. He works on F-35s. 

The object appeared to hover in the sky for approximately 40 minutes. The witness told me she looked away for only a few seconds and when she looked back, the object was gone. 

The witness is my daughter. She is not known to be dramatic, or to be a teller of lies. She saw something, we just are not sure what it was.
