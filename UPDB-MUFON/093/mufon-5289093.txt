Dark object

It was 7 March 2021 a little after 0700. I was heading to out to get breakfast as I turn on to state route 303 heading west I noticed a dark shape in the sky what look like a cigar or long oval shape I estimated to be over Valley City Ohio. The object was so big it took up most of the length of my windshield. A few minutes into my drive I was at a red light and watching the object trying to figure out what it could be ,when I saw move straight up and then just disappeared from view.
