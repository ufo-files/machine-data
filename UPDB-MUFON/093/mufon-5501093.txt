Two lights, one above the other hovered then went in separate directions. One light changed direction

At around 10.30-10.50 I spotted two flame like balls of light, one above the other. They hovered for a while like lanterns and they then went in different directions, one traveled to the left and I lost sight of it, the other hovered at a fair speed to the right and then changed direction fairly abruptly. It then hovered for a little more time.
