Saw at least 7 to 8 objects go from NW to N, one after another, about 3 seconds apart go from NW to N disappearing

I went outside to have a cigarette around 10 after 10pm and was looking towards NW sky. Saw a light moving at a steady speed, (not fast,no blinking lights)to the north. After watching about 5 seconds it grew brighter, then went back to brightness I observed at first,continuing to the N and gradually went out of site. I thought at first it could of been a plane, but there was no sound and as I said before, no blinking lights as an airplane or jet would of had. I probably would not of thought anything of it but about 3 seconds behind it was another one that did the same thing as the first one did. And about 3 seconds after that one, another one, then another, then another, I counted 7 or 8 in all, all turning brighter, then a little duller and headed towards north until not seen any longer. Pretty odd.
