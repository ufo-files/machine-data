spherical, white high altitude object, slow latitudinal descent over 90 mins

Observed a small, bright white, spherical object positioned at approx 280-290 degrees (N/NW) and approx 45-50 degrees latitude from my vantage point at 9000' with mountain range (false horizon). I photographed and filmed (cell phone) the object as well as using two astronomy phone apps, Sky Safari and Stellarium to see if I was (oddly) observing one of the large planets. I also observed it through a pair of Celestron 25x70 binoculars. Was able to observe a bright thin glow, first along the lower edge, then later along the left quadrant edge that appeared to be reflected sunlight. The brightness of the object, nor the left edge never waivered, fluctuated, or undulated in any way. The shape of the object also did not change during the entire observation. The object appeared to slowly descend (possibly following earth rotation) and was positioned at approx 20-30 degrees latitude. The longitudinal position never changed, holding consistently at approx 280-290 degrees (N/NW). I lost sight of the object after approx 90 minutes due to cloud cover.
