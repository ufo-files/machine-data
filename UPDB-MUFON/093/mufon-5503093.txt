{kt}grey blimp-like object descended quickly into the Christiana river, possible crash.

I am travelling on a bus with several other people on I-95 southbound. I looked out of the window near the exit to Bear/Glasgow and saw a grey blimp-like object rapidly descending with smoke trailing behind it. It landed in a marshy area on or near the Christiana river, made a splash and may have burnt some of the reeds. I tried to get a picture but due to the speed of the bus, I could not get a good image in time. Other people on the bus also saw this happen.
