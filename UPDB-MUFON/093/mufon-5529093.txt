bright blinking light that went away to reveal the shape of a stereotypical flying saucer

My boyfriend and I were sitting in my car facing the west, about 5 miles from Lake Michigan when a bright light caught his eye just above the horizon, to the left of the setting sun. He noticed the object first, then nudged me. The light was far brighter and larger than a plane or helicopter. I saw the light flash for just a second or two, and then the light stopped, revealing a grayish blue (just a shade darker than the sky) disc-like object. The disc shape hovered for a few seconds before going down into a group of clouds, where it changed color to match the clouds. From there, it travelled in a downward diagonal direction. We decided to drive to the shore to get a better look, and in doing that we lost sight of the disc.
