Concentric circle UFO

The UFO was stationary and distinctive in a clear night sky. It was too bright and too low in sky to be mistaken for a star or any celestial object. It made no noise and had no emissions. I have personally seen a confirmed sighting before so I was very careful and acutely observing it. It started hovering and moving slowly and in a way no object could. While 1.5 hours I saw it, there were 3 other people on a video call with me who all came to the same conclusion that it most certainly isn’t something natural or normal. During the time period, the object moved slowly and then suddenly moved faster in a zig zag manner. I am certain that the said object was indeed a UFO.
