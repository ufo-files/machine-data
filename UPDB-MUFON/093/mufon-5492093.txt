I was driving to work on a dark rainy night. As I went over a hill, I noted an "airplane" flying slowly right over some homes across the small valley. I assumed it was a smaller plane because there was a row of approx. 6-7 round white lights along the "side" in a row. I knew it was going to crash because it was so low in a hilly area. I drove around a curve in the road and was startled to see the object was now right over the road in front of me. This was a matter of seconds and it had to have been at least a mile away. It was still and about 20ft off the road, so I assumed it was a helicopter. I drove under it and stopped, opened my window, and looked up. It was not a plane or a helicopter. It made no noise, there was no downdraft. It was just dark with these lights. Nothing happened I just stared at it. I was worried about being late for work and eventually drove off with it still hovering over the road. Cannot believe I left, I was very calm, not afraid at all, it was very surreal. The next day I noted that power lines were going across the road in the approximate area. It was definitely an object and not identifiable. From a distance it seemed to be about the size of a small passenger plane, when I was under it, It went beyond the sides of the 2 lane road so I could not see the length due to rain and dark. It is still a mystery to me to this day, but I can still remember it all as though it were last month. No other cars were seen on the road before or after this incident.  did a sketch at the time that I am trying to locate.
