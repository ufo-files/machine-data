{tjd} 20 to 30 objects in “v” formation moving across sky

We were walking our dogs at night and stopped for some cars to drive by. Looked up and saw 20 to 30 objects moving across the sky in a “V” formation. The objects were in a loose formation with some of the objects not quite in line. It looked like birds flying in formation. We are near the port so my first thought was birds illuminated by the port. But they seemed to move a little faster than birds would. We watched them move across the sky and disappear behind the trees. As we walked down the street to see them pop out the other side of the trees they were gone. As we were looking for them a plane came out of the same direction as the objects but did not have any jet noise.
