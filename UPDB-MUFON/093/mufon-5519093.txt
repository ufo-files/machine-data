Saw three red UFOs on the Palmetto near the I-75 ramp.

I was on my way back home after attending a review session for my orgo exam. I was coming from sw 8th st and 85th ave near FIU. I got on the Palmetto North bound. After about 10 minutes driving on the highway I noticed 3 red lights in the sky ahead of me coming from the North East(My approximate location was between the I-75 entrance ramp and right before the Palmetto starts to curve facing east). In fact they were the only things that were in the sky at the time. At first impression was that they were planes of some sort but what got me curious and somewhat confused was that they were close to each heading the same direction (from north east to south west). I noticed that they were heading in my direction almost following along the Palmetto Highway heading south in a linear path. Oddly two of them were next to each other as the third orb was off to the side by its self. As they got closer I was trying to find any resemblance to a plane or helicopter. It had no flashing signal lights as all planes have to warn others their presences in the dark. The three objects instead were basically full red orbs. The red light the orbs emitted remained constant. No pulsating or flashing lights. They were pretty low too. Possibly under the clouds. They eventually passed over my car and after that I lost sight of them as I kept driving. The whole thing lasted less than a minute. I was eager to keep looking but I was driving on the highway and I did feel like pulling over as I was really amazed to find out what those three red orb were. I recently watched a youtube video of UFOs and I found a video that shows similar red orbs (although somewhere else). The clip that I will provide will give you and idea of what I saw.
