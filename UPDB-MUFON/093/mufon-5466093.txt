Basically, my family and I were at a night time Epcot celebration near Orlando Florida. I noticed some large glowing lights rather high in the sky. At first, I assumed these lights were from approaching airplane. When I looked again latter, I noticed that these lights had changed their formation. This is when I took out my Sony H5 bridge camera and zoomed in on the objects in an attempt to see what was really there. Unfortunately, most of the subsequent photos were basically a blur since the H5 was not designed for low light. I managed to capture 4 good photos which I am including for your review.

The objects remained stationary for several minutes which allowed some time for observation. There was no sound that could be associated with a plane or helicopter. The objects eventually moved off in a Northeast direction until they faded from sight. I do not have the editing software to see what was really there in the sky that night. I now own a much better camera with 4k video capability.

Also I wanted to know of anyone who might be interested in orbs. I have numerous videos depicting dozens of orbs flying around my Autistic daughter. These orbs are only visible on a camera.
