{jpg} circle of lights stationary in the sky for several hours

Three days ago, I was sitting on my balcony at around 5 am. I observed a circle of lights, gold in color, in the sky that were not moving. They did not blink, were very steady. The object remained there until daylight occurred and I could not see it. Two days ago, getting home from work, I noticed it again, but, this time, it was in a different portion of the sky and much lower and it actually lowered behind the building I was looking at it. Last night, again, the object was in the sky. I took a few pics of it with my cell phone camera, but one cannot see the image well. When enlarged, it looks like three round circles of mass with a blue color. Might be a weather balloon, but none has been reported in the area.
