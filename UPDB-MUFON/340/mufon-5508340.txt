My girlfriend and I were turning westbound on the bypass 4 129 on ramp when we promply saw 6 round glowing orange lights in what appeared to be two triangular shaped patterns moving together in a northeast heading. we were freaking out literally, and have seen the same thing in the same place once before, only it was a single light. time was approximately 10pm, and the patterns were moving against the wind, and completely silent. After about 3 minutes, the orange lights faded quickly very near to us as we jumped in the car to give chase, one remaining object was seen moving still in the same direction, but without the orange light, and possibly a reflected off white light. That was the last we saw of them that night. We have poor quality video and pictures. The ramp lights obscured the dark backdrop.
