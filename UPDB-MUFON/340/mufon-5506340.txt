Huge brilliant white triangle. Taller than the trees. I was literally next to it from a few tards away.

It was a summer evening about 9pm... I was fostering a new rescue dog & was walking her to the shop. I lived in a Park House (we were in charge of the parks & nature reserve where we lived on the Lancashire/Yorkshire border U.K.)
Opposite the park house we had a school. = I left the house & had my head down concentrating on my new rescue dog... Got half-way down the pathway to the shop when I raised my head & saw a HUGE brilliant white shimmering triangle (it was only yards away from me through the hedge!)
It was taller than the trees at the corner of the school field & the white light of it = it was shimmering...

I started to run to grab a camera from my house, but as I took those few steps I instantly knew that it would be gone, so I ran back...
As I did it just shot up into the sky on a perfect trajectory AND stopped absolutely dead!!! Then after a few 2nds it shot up again & stopped dead... Then it finally shot up again & by this time I could only faintly make out its shape in the night sky.
I was shaking like a leaf & still shook when telling anybody. The next day there was a huge water leak of the ground where I saw it

Some very strange things happened to me in the 2 weeks during this, of which I don't like to talk about.
