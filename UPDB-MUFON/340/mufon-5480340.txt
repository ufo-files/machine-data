There are new hangars at Andrews Air Force Base that go many stories down and have hydraulic operated clam shell roofs. They open to the sky and the floor opens, and these are launched straight up. you can tell these hangars by the absence of anything on the roof. and return like a meteor straight towards Andrews, glowing white and disappearing quickly. Ex-military, lived by an Air force base hear sonic booms, sometimes double, and never see a plane.
