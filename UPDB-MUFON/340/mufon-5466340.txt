It was August 16,2018 exactly 6pm as I looked at my IPhone. There was a bank of high white clouds approx. 10-15 miles away South East of me as I was driving down Avenue 42 . I looked at bank of clouds and started to notice two black saucer shape objects slowly come out of the clouds. They each had to be larger than 300ft due to the clouds being 10-15 miles away. They had no lights or other object protruding from them. I started to grab my cell phone and was gonna try and get a picture. I was driving at 40mph so it was hard to focus take picture and drive. They two objects started to slowly go back into the clouds as slowly as they appeared. I have one picture that shows as they both started going back into the clouds. I also turned the pic into black and white and you can Def. See what I am talking about.
