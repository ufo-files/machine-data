{cak} Shimmering object

I was in the yard around 2:00 pm and heard a jet overhead. I looked up, saw the jet and something caught my eye. It was beyond the jet, far into the atmosphere and it was an object that had the appearance of shimmering. I would not have seen it had it not been that I looked at the jet. The slight motion just to the left of the jet would not have been see otherwise. It seemed camouflaged in that the metal? was a shiny silver white in color and it blended into the atmosphere. The craft had a long body with a short arm off to the right at the top and then a longer arm that went completely across the body and was longer than the top arm, sort of like a cross. At the bottom of the long body was what appeared to be arms to the left and right (like you would draw a stick man's legs. Maybe it was a satellite of some sort, don't know but I do know that I had never seen anything like it before. I just disappeared into the atmosphere.
