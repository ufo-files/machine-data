{rjl} Spotted UFO over Keller Texas

I was swimming at the pool when I noticed a bright, pulsating, glowing orb flying towards our direction, coming from the North. It was higher than a plane, flying in a straight path and with the naked eye we could see pulsing lights of reddish orange and bluish white. We grabbed my cell phone, a Samsung 8 and began recording. I filmed until iI lost sight of it when it got near the sun. We watched the film and discovered that it was morphing into different shapes and had something black on it. It morphed into squares, triangles, bean shaped, diamonds, hexagon, circles, etc. About 10 minutes later a plane flew by and it was very clear that what we saw was not a plane or a drone. This is the second time in about 2 months that I see a UFO in the same vicinity during the day time.
