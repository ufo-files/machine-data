Blinking, pulsating light, changing color and direction very quickly

On the above date and approximate time, we observed a light in the sky while in the driveway of our home getting into our car. Our 5 year old child noticed it first and commented "Wow, what is that"!? Initially I thought he was staring at the moon as it was full and very bright directly in our view however, his mother pointed out that there was a flashing light changing colors which she thought was an aircraft. After a few seconds I focused on the light myself and noticed it to move very quickly to the left and then back to the right. In a manner inconsistent with any aircraft. We live about 5 miles south of Camp Pendleton and see many military aircraft in the skies on any given day, but they are loud and do not maneuver like what we witnessed. Unfortunately we were so intrigued with the encounter that we did not think to take photos or video.
