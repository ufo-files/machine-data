South of Pottstown, PA
Observed more than 30 "star like" lights traveling at around 135 degrees SE. Objects were traveling single file in a straight line and made no noise.
Objects were visible from Northern treeline traveling SE until losing visual due to light pollution.
Sighting was at around 8:45 PM and lasted long enough for me to go inside to get my phone to try to get video footage. Footage does not show objects due to poor camera capability.
