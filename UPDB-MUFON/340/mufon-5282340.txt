An accidental capture of an unknown anomaly in a photo while photographing desert geoglyphs.

To be perfectly honest, I didn't even see it. I was in the Lower Colorado River Valley doing some research on geoglyphs in the area. I was standing near one on the Arizona side of the river facing the California side. I was taking photos, doing my best, of the geoglyph that I was near. I didn't view my photos until later that evening and was surprised that I captured some sort of anomaly. While the rest of the photo is quite clear, this object is in the upper/center of the frame. It is weirdly pixelated and looks like an object traveling at great speed. Neither my brother nor I saw anything. We also heard nothing. 
I've posted this on a social media group for input. Of course there are skeptics and trolls, but I've received some good feedback as well, enough for me to reach out to you.
