USO Santa Barbara Ca. Witnessed a few times, only remember once that it actually shined a light on us.

We lived on the cliff in Isla Vista at 6613 Del Playa Dr. There were quite a few times that we saw this sort of orb underneath the water. All instances were looking east towards UCSB campus point that sticks out into the ocean. We saw the light going back and forth between campus point and the Channel Islands. We would watch it for quite some time as it made its way towards us going back and forth back and forth doing extreme maneuvers nothing we are capable of. There was one instance it worked its way all the way down the beach to us, roughly 1.5 miles, and it was high tide and we looked over the railing and it was a couple feet in diameter and it shined a very very bright light on us. I honestly don't remember what happened but it was just gone after that, no one knew if it went up or down under the water. I do not recall any of us ever talking about it until 4/5 years later I thought about it and asked my friend and she got the chills and turned pale and said holy sh%* I do remember that but haven't thought about it since.
