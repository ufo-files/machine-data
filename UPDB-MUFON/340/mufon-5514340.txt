Three orange orbs flyin north

I was dropping off a friend around a quarter after midnight when we spotted 3 orange lights traveling north very slowly. The speed of the objects was about the same as a car (around 70km/h). At first we thought it was a plane but they were way too low for a plane and I know for a fact that planes don't carry orange lights that don't blink. Two of the objects were flying next to eachother but the third one was flying lower and it seemed like it was very slightly changing directions and elevation. I managed to take out my phone camera and take a short video but the objects were gone north and I lost sight.
