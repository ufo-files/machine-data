{rjl} Black large triangle passing over my head

I was standing near Lummi shore when I noticed a object headed toward me from the south. It slowly passed overhead and contunied northward till I lost sighted of it. I could clearly see the shape as a triangle atleast the size of a basketball. No noise no exhaust. Green lights on both sides, at least 4 on the sides. I was amazed and confused. Told some other people they didn't believe me and no one else was around to see it.
