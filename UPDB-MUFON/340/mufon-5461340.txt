{cak} Seen from a commercial aircraft on flight from Bristol, England to Malaga Spain

I was sitting in a second seat next to a window, aft of the wing, on the starboard side. Our flight had begun its landing approach/descent thus my estimate of 20,000ft is based on the approximate duration of descent. I looked out the window, something I seldom do, and I noticed what I assumed was an shiny aircraft. It struck me as curious. because it appeared significantly closer than any other aircraft seen on any previous flight. As I continued to look at it, I then noticed it was trailing black smoke. By that I don't mean black smoke that appears as grey engine exhaust. I mean dense , black, opaque smoke being left as a black trail behind the craft. The craft had just begun emitting the trail when I noticed it because although it continued the black emission the entire time I saw it, there was only a very short trail when I first viewed it. As I looked at the craft, I realized that I really couldn't say with certainty that it was in fact a plane I had just assumed that it was on first glance. When I first saw it, the craft was below my plane and was quickly climbing at what I guess to be at least a 45 degree angle. Because it was climbing at such an acute angle, I was able to have a better idea about its speed as compared to what I might have guessed if we were passing in opposite directions at the same altitude. I can't say with certainty that it was flying at an unimaginable speed but it was an extremely fast climb. Concerning the crafts color, because I expected to see a plane I thought it had a silvery surface that was reflecting light but all I can say with certainty was that it was "bright white". I am certain the pilots my airplane also saw the craft.
