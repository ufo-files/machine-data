Multiple Orbs seen in Karnataka, India in the 1970s

I took the detailed note from my uncle of his encounter in 1979. He was living in a village called Mallenahalli, Tumkur district, Karnataka when the event occurred. He was in high school and in total he and my aunts have seen glowing UFOs multiple times (minimum 3 times). The incidence I am describing is his first encounter.

He came out of the house in the evening at around 7:00 pm. He was living in a farm house back then with a single house. Directly opposite to his house there is a farm with coconut trees, That day he saw a spherical glowing flowing object which was about 2 feet in diameter which was stationary on the trees. It stayed there for more than few minutes. He wanted to figure out what it is so he started approaching it. It suddenly vanished. 

There was a second incidence when similar such orb went past him at very high speed. The 3rd encounter is when he called others in the family and multiple people witnessed the event. All the time it was a glowing orb which was either reddish or whitist in colour. I always dismissed such things in the past. After the Nimits video was released I feel these can be real ETs.
