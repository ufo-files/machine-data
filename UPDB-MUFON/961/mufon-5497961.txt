Driving through my parent's neighborhood, spotted a bright round orange sphere hovering (no movement at all). Thought it might be a new radio tower top or something but it was brighter than a warning light and orange. I noticed it because there was nothing else in the sky (stars or planes in this area and it was pitch black with a ball of orange) It was over an area that does not have any tall structures. I asked my daughter to keep an eye on it as I was driving. She said it wasn't moving, I continued to drive down a hill, turned into the neighborhood that my parent's live and continued to watch it. I then pulled into driveway, got out of car, saw the object now moving toward the South East. Once it flew toward that direction, a plane in the further distance could be seen, flashing lights and all so I knew this wasn't a plane. Wish I could have snapped a shot with the plane behind it to compare. It then increased speed and was out of sight pretty quick. Seemed to be somewhat low because I could no longer see it once it took off in the distance.
