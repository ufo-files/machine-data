{nd} returning from morning golf viewed object in low meadow

driving south on rt. 169. 2 pm on a monday afternoon. saw luminous disc shape object hover over tree top in low meadow. wife at time also remarked what's that? looked like 25-30 ft. disc outlined in thin silver or metallic with gold plasma like interior. watched clearly for minute than trees intermittently blocked out. passed state of ct. highway workers who were raking and had their backs turned toward object..wanted to turn around to make them see object but decided to race to butts bridge road to packer road to see object over yaworski landfill(dump).when we got there i recognized tree it was over. disc was gone. tried to convince ourselves it was some sort of gas emitted from landfill. never told anyone but cpl law enforcement friends off the record...in 1989 these sightings weren't taken seriously.
