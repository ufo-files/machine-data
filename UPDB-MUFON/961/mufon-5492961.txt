{cak} Flew across the sky

I was sitting in the kitchen signing thank you cards and looked at the front door for no reason. I saw a shadow on the front porch pillar so I got up to go look. Nothing was there. It must have been the bush. But then lightning flashed and I looked up in the sky. Behold the UFO! All along I thought it was a UFO and nothing less. It was shaped like a triangle and had bright lights on all three corners. On the two sides the lights went across the whole object. There were no lights on the back, the other side, but there was a bright light in the center of the object. I couldn't tell how many lights there were, but there was at least the main four. All I saw was it moving across the sky going south south west. I felt excited when I saw it! It was the second time I've seen a UFO. I lost sight of it because it flew out of sight in my window.
