{jpg} Orange lights moving in groups of three.

I observed 3 circular orange lights flying SE to NW through the windshield of my parked car. At first I thought it was military helicopters but the lights didn't blink or flash, just consistent orange. They maintained a straight flight path and seemed to move slower than standard copters I previously observed. 
I turned off the engine and got out of the car. There was no sound from the objects. At this time three more objects became visible off to the SE. The flight looked as if someone fired flares in the sky. They ascended at an angle then flew in groups of 3. Very smooth, quiet and straight flight path.
