{rjl} Glowing Light in sky- Lake County, FL

Daughter and I were returning home from playing Pokemon Go, she pointed and said "mom, what is that glowing orange light in the sky? it's not a plane." We drove a bit further to a more open area to see if we could get a better look. It resembled a SpaceX launch..but, without the entire sky glowing, only the object itself had a pulsating glow. The object was stationary for a good 6-8 min before beginning to fall then fading away. There were none of the typical signs of objects such as a meteor or space debris entering the atmosphere. The lighting was not consistent of that of a drone. there were no blinking lights or sounds or movements which is typical of planes or helicopters. I have had family in all branches of the military as well as three family members who worked for NASA. We have really tried to logic this out and are baffled. Although we do not have any media, if any others in the area may have seen it, this post will confirm they aren't the only ones.
