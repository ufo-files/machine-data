{tjd} 4 large white lights

I was in a car with my aunt and uncle. My uncle was driving, my aunt was in the front passengers seat and I was sitting behind her, in the rear passengers seat. We were travelling south on I-25, and we were travelling between the first and second exit to Belen, NM. The night was very dark. To the west, over the mesa, I first saw what looked like an white ball of light disintegrate or explode, followed by a tail. I will describe it as a shooting star in reverse. A few seconds later I saw 4 large white lights, which looked like 2 sets of large headlights. The lights were close together. The object was not moving and the lights were facing east. There was also a dim glow underneath the large lights. It was large and looked menacing. As I was staring at the object, the 2 lights on the left turned to face the other 2 lights, while remaining close to the other 2 lights; so at this point, 2 lights were facing east while the other 2 turned to face north. The lights then turned back to face the same direction again. I stared at the object until I could not see it anymore. It never seemed to move at all. (I am attaching pictures I took with my cell phone. The object is accurately described in my text above. The pictures only appear to show a pinprick of light. I did not use a flash and we were travelling at a high rate of speed on the freeway. I will attach the original photos, and the photos after I zoomed in on them using my photo app.) The object looked large and menacing, the pictures are not indicative of the size I saw with my eyes.
