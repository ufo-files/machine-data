saw early hours of two mornings, too afraid to stay & scrutinize, have photo's

This was witnessed by my mother, who lives up in the mountains, she was up early in the morning, said the sun was just sitting on top of the mountain looking pretty, went to get her phone to take a photo then noticed a large bright orb like object a little way down to the left, there was a very bright pink colour around it, more to one side though, (i have the photo), my mother lives miles from the nearest farm, so she very afraid and ran inside, locked the doors and windows, and prayed. She then forwarded me the photo, on inspection of the photo i still see at the bottom of the mountain 3 little white lights, which she did not notice.
The following night my mother saw the object again but much smaller, still bright and with the bright pink, this time on the ground, (she took a photo).
Approximately 14 years ago, my brother was on the farm during the day with a few of the farmhands, they say a flying disk in the sky in broad daylight, they all took off with freight.
