Invisible UFO Appears As Cloud On Camera

We pulled over at the scenic overlook to take photos of Donner Lake. It was a perfectly clear day, with absolutely nothing in the sky, above or around the lake. After I snapped a panoramic photo, a perfect-looking cloud appeared on my camera screen on the photo. The cloud was not there. I have been looking for answers ever since.
