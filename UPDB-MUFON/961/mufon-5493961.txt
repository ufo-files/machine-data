{cak} Cyllender Orange glowing hovering above the Elizabeth River

When I left a building at 2am or so I looked to the sky over the Elizabeth river and hovering above it was a cylinder glowing orange and I thought it to be hot because of the color ---- it did nothing but hover there. I got in my car and drove away. That is all I have to say.
