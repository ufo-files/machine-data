{nd} There was a bright amber lights hovering over our home and then proceeded to move in a tri-angle pattern.

I was watching TV in the lower level of our home. My partner was up stairs in the living room lying down on the couch also watching TV. While watching TV, I was also talking to a friend of ours on the cordless telephone. Suddenly, my partner called down to me with urgency to come up to identify what he was witnessing. When I went up to see, there were three amber/orange bright lights slowly gliding in the sky in the form of a triangular pattern . There was a lead light and one on each side of it. The spacing between them appeared to be even spacing and they seemed to be synchronized in speed. My partner told me that when he first saw the first image it was hovering by its self and then joined by the other two glowing images. They appeared to be the same in size and from where we were standing circular in shape like that of a football. We quickly ran outside to get a better view. There was no sound or sent coming from the images. They slowly glided through the sky heading north of our home.
