Stargazing leads to UFO ( orbs ) flying over me.

It was 3am when i had checked the time on the microwave on my way to the restroom. After I was done using the restroom, I went outside and wanted to star gaze as the sky was extremely clear. The moon was on the west side and i was staring at it as well as the stars ( in all directions ). After about 10 mins, i stared at the stars directly above when me 3 ufo's ( of the orb type ) flew over me. 1 ufo was on on the left then like about 10 feet or so to its right was the 2nd one (these 2 were flying at the same speed and rate) and then the 3rd one was slightly falling behind but caught up to the one on the right (from behind ), then moved towards its side ( right side ) but stayed slightly behind and all of them were going at a steady speed. They were traveling east to west and about a few seconds later, all 3 just disappeared in front of my eyes. They were no buildings obstructing my view, they were no clouds that night. It was crystal clear. This isn't the first time i have seen them flyover my house. But it is the first time i've seen them disappear. The previous time, happened a few years ago ( same direction east to west ) but that time they were 2 ufos ( orb types as well ) and that time, they appeared before my eyes out of nowhere and flew over me and my house at a steady pace. There was no obstructions in my view. That time i was star gazing eastwards when they appeared in front of me. Both times they were not flying that high. I want to say they were probably about the height of a helicopter give or take. In both situations there was no sound.
