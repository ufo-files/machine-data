CONTRAIL - DONUTS ON A ROPE - INDISPUTABLE

AT APPROX. 7:30 P.M. THIS EVENING, AS I AND A FRIEND SAT ON OUR BACK DECK IN CONVERSATION , I LOOKED UP AND SAW THIS CONTRAIL IN THE SLIGHTLY NORTH AND WEST SKY ABOVE MY HOME. I RUDELY INTERRUPTED OUR CONVERSATION AND ASKED HER TO LOOK AT IT TOO, WHICH SHE DID , AS I EXPLAINED WHAT I SAW AND WHY IT WAS IMPORTANT. I HAVE READ , WATCHED VIDEOS AND TELEVISION SHOWS, AND IT WAS...DONUTS ON A ROPE . PERIOD - THERE IS NO OTHER WAY TO DESCRIBE IT . WHATEVER CAUSED IT WAS UNHEARD AND UNSEEN, WE HAD TYPICAL HIGH WINDS TODAY , 25-30 MILES AN HOUR, WITH GUSTS TO 35 MPH , BUT THIS WAS AT AN ALTITUDE THAT, WHILE CLEARLY VISIBLE AND FAIRLY LOW, WAS NOT QUITE AS SUBJECT TO THESE WIND SPEEDS . WE WATCHED AND THE WINDS DIMINISHED / DISSIPATED IT OVER 10-15 MINUTES, BUT IT WAS THE LARGEST , FATTEST AND MOST UNUSUAL CONTRAIL I 'VE EVER SEEN, AND I WATCH ( AND LISTEN TO ) PLANES MANY NIGHTS ( AND DAYS ) AS I GREW UP IN THE FLIGHT PATH OF THE AIRPORT AND LIVE JUST A VERY FEW MILES SOUTH AND EAST OF BOEING WICHITA. WE GET ALL KINDS OF PLANES ALL THE TIME, AND WE GET NICE STRAIGHT THIN JET CONTRAILS - THIS WAS NOTICABLY VERY DIFFERENT . WITH THE WINDS, WE HEARD NO SOUND AT GROUND LEVEL, THIS IS ALL I CAN REPORT . I WOULD JUST LIKE THIS NOTED. THANK YOU - KIM . VORNAUF
