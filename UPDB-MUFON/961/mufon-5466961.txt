{rjl} Large silent Gold orb flew in front of full moon blocked out upper 25 percent from view and winked out after passing moon

My wife and myself where watching TV in our pool and watching the full moon come up over our back yard. At 12:05 AM EST I looked at the moon and told my wife to look. A large bright gold ball was flying from West to East making no noise and had no other lights than the bright gold color. From first sighting to the end we only saw it for about 30 seconds. The planet Mars was bright and visible to the right of the moon from my view point. The orb was about 4 times the size of Mars and blocked the up quarter of the moon as it passed in front. About ten seconds after passing the moon the orb blinked out not to be seen again. Two high flights passed overhead in the next five minutes which appeared much higher and I could see there flashing lights and hear the jet engines. I also had two jets fly over to land at Greater Pittsburgh Airport within 5 minutes of the siting.
