{rjl} Looked like a Cube that twirled

My Daughters were both outside having a cigarette when they saw the object in the sky toward the west. They had no Idea what it was, but thought of it to be of an unusual citing of something in the sky. It appeared to be a cube that was spinning. The object would get dim then it would get real bright for a few seconds. Holly decided to start recording the object on her phone. They felt puzzled by what they saw. They called me to tell me about it. Holly kept the original recording and sent me a piece of the copied recording to my email. I have seen some things before but this had me excited when I watched it. I've never seen a better example of an apparent UFO in my life.
