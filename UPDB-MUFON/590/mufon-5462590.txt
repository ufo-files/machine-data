Started as bright light, became blinking light on dark craft, then saw tail of craft as it turned

On December 14th I was working in my second floor hotel room and heard a loud music outside and decided to see what was going on. I came out on to my deck, which faces N/NE and saw a car drive by with loud music playing. After it passed I notice a very bright light to my upper right (NE) and noticed that it was moving (Point A in the drawing). I thought it was an approaching plane, but it suddenly skipped forward to Point B in my drawing in an instant, then flew parallel to the ground at about 20 degrees above horizon and continued west. While moving I could not see the object, only the small blinking white light on it. Suddenly when it reached Point C, it turned 90 degrees and headed north and dissappeared. When it turn it tilted its body so I could see that it was rectangular with rounded edges, and had 4 bright white lights on its tail end. The lights were round and did not glow brightly bust just rather glowed, and I could only see evidence of tail lights when it turned. I did not see the object tilt flat again after the turn, but dissappeared instantly. No sound, just visuals and it was a quiet night, great sky visibility. It was moving fast and looked to be about a mile away.
