{rjl} At night in cloud cover 5 lights moved around in circle then all went to center then moved out and returned in circular pattern.

My adult daughter called me to come outside on the patio and look towards the east. Up in the sky between clouds were lights which were non-descript in shape except transparent white. There were 4 with one in the center. The 4 lights flashed in and out in a pattern then darted towards the center light. They touched the center light then darted back out and floated very fast in the round pattern again. These lights stayed in the same location while flashing and moving in a pattern.

 At first we thought it was the type of lights you see from events where they flash large beams in the sky. However, there was no beam coming from the ground! It was over North La Quinta or Indio area in Coachella Valley. After about an hour of observation we went back inside.

I tried taking photos with my cell but they didn't show anything clearly
so I didn't submit them.
