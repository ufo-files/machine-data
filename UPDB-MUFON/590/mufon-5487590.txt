{dr}5 lights that changed shape

While traveling south on I-76 heading south and near the Lowes exit I noticed that there were 5 lights in the air in the shape of an arch straight South. As I was watching them they changed shape and went into a triangle, while the green light stayed off to the side. They changed shapes a few more times before coming to a place to pull over and take video. As I stopped and looked up they were flying southwest and then they were gone.  they were nowhere to be seen after that.
