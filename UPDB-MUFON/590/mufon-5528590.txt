Three circles one inside the other with the outside being the largest

Everything at about 4to4;30 am I go for my walk. I always go up on our golf course because it dark and i can watch the sky. I have watched the sky since i wa in grade school. I am 73 years old. The three circles one inside the other were at the cup end of the big dipper and appeared to be behind the dipper. The odd thing was that thhey would appear and then disapear but didint blink or flash I should say the circles were made of disks forming the circles. After about five minutes i turned and walked away how long they were still there i donet know. The next morining at app. the same time They were at the same spot apperaing and diappearing as they did the morning before. The thrid morning wbich would be wednessday there was nothing there. I should say that there was a full moon the entire week. Sincerely *(Wit's name deleted--CMS/sg).
