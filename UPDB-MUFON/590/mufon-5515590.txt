(GS) Observed orb like object giving off light in a diamond/star like pattern

I was proceeding west on hwy 113 from hwy 66 towards St. Clair. As I rounded the bend before prices grove rd on hwy 113 observed a large object about the size of a basketball in my line of sight hovering the sky just past prices grove rd in the northwest direction. The light was very bright and reflecting the sun. The object after being observed for about 30 to 45 seconds then immediately shot straight up into the sky and disappeared.
