HELLO AND TY FOR RESPONDING.
MARCO ISLAND, FL
MARCH 29, 2013 - GOOD FRIDAY
MARCO ISLAND, FL
MARCH 29, 2013 - GOOD FRIDAY
11:00 - 11:30 to PAST MIDNIGHT
SOUTH END OF ISLAND

WHILE STANDING ON THE BALCONY LOOKING OUT TOWARD THE HI-RISE BUILDINGS & SKY AS I HAVE DONE FOR 15 PLUS YEARS IN FL, I NOTICED A DISTINCT DIFFERENCE WITH REFERENCE TO ONE OF THE RED LIGHTS ON THE AERIAL HI-RISES.... ONE SEEMED TO BE MOVING. FROM MY LOCATION I CAN SEE ONE DOZEN RED SAFETY LIGHTS/AERIALS ON THE HI-RISE ROOFS. INITIALLY THE RED LIGHT I SAW MOVING WAS THOUGHT TO BE A HELICOPTER OR DEDUCTED IT WAS SOME SORT OF DISTANT AIRCRAFT. I CONTINUED WATCHING AS IT TRAVELED EVER SO SLOWLY FROM THE SOUTHWEST TO THE SOUTHEAST, REALIZING THE MOVING RED LIGHT I WAS WATCHING WAS NOT A PLANE OR HELICOPTER, I BECAME INTERESTED. THE RED LIGHT LINGERED BEHIND ONE OF THE BUILDINGS FOR SEVERAL MINUTES AND THEN CONTINUED ON A STRAIGHT PATH OVER THE GULF FOR ABOUT FOUR-FIVE BLOCKS PROCEEDING SOUTHEAST. AS I CONTINUED TO WATCH, THE RED LIGHT DESCENDED EVER SO FAST (INTO THE GULF ?) AND IT WAS GONE! I IMMEDIATLEY WENT UPSTAIRS TO A NEIGHOR - MY LATE NIGHT WALKING PARTNER. I EXPLAINED WHAT I SAW. SHE GOT HER HUSBANDS BINOCLUARS, AND AGAIN, THE SAME REDLIGHT, OR A DIFFERENT ONE CAME BACK. AGAIN COMING FROM THE WEST TRAVELING TO THE EAST - AND AGAIN IT QUICKLY DESCENDED. IF IT WAS THE SAME ONE WHAT INCREDIBLE SPEED TO HAVE RETURNED. 5-10 MINUTES PASSED - I THOUGHT IT WAS OVER, BUT YET ANOTHER SIGHTING, AND CLOSER THIS TIME. THROUGH BINOCULARS, BRIGHT WHITE LIGHTS COULD BE SEEN AROUND /ON THE SIDE OF THE OBJECT VISIBLE. I FELT OVER-WHELMED, AMAZED, MY FEELINGS RE UFOS VALIDATED, BREATH-TAKING. 
I NEEDED AN ANSWER. I WAS TOUCHED.

CONTACTED BOTH FIRE / POLICE DEPARTMENTS. I WAS INFORMED THERE WAS A CALL FOR A BOAT IN DISTRESS - RED FLARE, AUTHORITIES FOUND NO BOAT IN DISTRESS. EVIDENTALLY SOMEONE SAW THAT RED LIGHT EITHER ASCENDING OR DESCENDING THINKING IT A DISTRESS SIGNAL. THAT WAS NO DISTRESS SIGNAL.
