{cak} White light emitting orb (sphere) moved east at high rate of speed (3 to 30 miles per sec) south to east in about 14 seconds

June 9 2017. Clear evening. Just before dusk. Looked up into the southern sky to view moon. I observed a white light emitting orb or sphere moving east at a high rate of speed estimated at 3 to 30 miles per second. No wings. No con trail. No heat signature. No sound. No deviation from direction. No wobble. No visible means of propulsion. Object covered approx 85 degrees of compass in about 14 seconds. Can only guess at size but flew over high thin clouds to the east just before it disappeared behind the building beside my place. Estimate altitude at 25000 ft. I would guess this object travelled approx 200 miles while I observed it. Guessing it was quite a bit larger than a jumbo jet... No doubt it was not anything manufactured on this planet. Bonifide UAP. Certainly an unidentified flying object. No doubt in my mind: extraterrestrial entity craft.
