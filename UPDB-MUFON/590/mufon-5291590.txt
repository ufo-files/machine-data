Spherical object hovering in the sky flashing red, blue, violet, and blue lights

I was outside with my dog in the backyard around 11PM, when I noticed a spherical object in the sky around 1000-2000 feet above the ground. The object was flashing lights in different shades of red, violet, and blue. The object looked like it was shaking or moving laterally and then vertically at rapid speeds while hovering. I stared at it for a couple minutes until I felt and eerie type of sensation, so I decided to go inside. I tried to look out my window after going inside, but could no longer locate the object.
