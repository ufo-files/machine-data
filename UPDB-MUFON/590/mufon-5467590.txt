July 2, 2018 at approximately 6:30 PM.

I was the driver with my son and wife in the back seat and they did not see anything due to where they were sitting.

When I pointed out the plane I caught a view of a oval shaped silver object to the right of the plane and at a much higher altitude than the plane. The object looked like it was just sitting there. I looked back at the plane to get my bearings and then looked back to where the object was and it was no longer there. I scanned the sky for it with no luck
