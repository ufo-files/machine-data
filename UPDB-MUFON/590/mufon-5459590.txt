{jpg} Triangular, transparent, silent craft slowly following a straight path.

After having dinner with a friend and our 4 combined kids, we walked across the parking lot to our cars. I always look at the sky, I love the stars, so I glanced up on our way out. I looked back down before I registered that something looked strange in the sky. I looked back up and saw a large, triangular, distorted section of the sky. The triangular shape was moving silently in a straight path. I could see the stars on the other side of the object, but I could see the distortion around the edges. My friend saw the same thing but dismissed it as some flying spider web messing up her vision. I did not see any spiderweb and was able to clearly see the triangular shape. The only thing I think it could be is maybe some kind of military craft as it was flying in the direction of Dyess AFB (about 47 miles from us as the crow flies). I don't know of any aircraft that we have that can be silent and move slowly like that, though.
