{nd} I worked for the forest service, on lookout. I watch the object for about two hours then it disappeared

I worked on the lookout tower in Southern Oregon. Another lookout named [Item Moved/CMS/nd] called me on the radio and told me to look in a specific direction and to tell him what I saw. The object looked like a ball bearing reflecting the light. if I remember right it was 10 to 15 miles away and looked about the size of your little fingernail. I watched it off and on for about two hours. I turned. my back and when I looked again it was gone. I was very curious but only George and I know about it because people would make fun of us. I know it was not a balloon horribly. It was in the middle of a deep forest at approximately 1 to 2000 feet in elevation. I have believed every since then.
