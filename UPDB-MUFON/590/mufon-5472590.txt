{jpg} Bird circling - no wing flap - accelerated very fast in a straight line due West

While attending a child's soccer game, I looked straight up and noticed a shiny mylar balloon float by at about 500ft - 1000ft. Observed it for a few seconds, looked down to watch the game, looked back up and it was gone, however in its place was a circling "bird" - same altitude. Noticed that it looked like a very large hawk yet there was no wing flapping. Just as I was about to look away, it accelerated EXTREMELY fast due west on a straight trajectory. It was out of line of sight in a matter of seconds. It made no sound. 

I would say it was a bird except for the fact that it took off like no bird I have ever seen.
