{jpg} 2 Disc Shaped UFOs on Valentine's

Driving South on Hwy 54 approx. 5 miles before reaching Kingdom City, I had caught a glimpse of light coming from my left side of view. The sky was clear. I spotted it, watched it for only a few minutes then hollered at my boyfriend to look. The trinagular disc shaped object moved at a continuous pace, tho not super fast. It looked as if the center was dispersing the only light seen. A whiteish light with a steady glow. As I looked back at the road I notices the 2nd object coming from the same direction as the first one was. The 2nd object looked the same but appeared a little bigger. It also looked as if it jumped as it came by. It was a steady path then it was as if it jumped forward. Both had what I call feet, for landing. We both clearly seen that. As the first one got farther away it looked as if it pulled in the sides on the triangular/saucer sides. Folded up. Like the wings of a bird. As we were reaching Kingdom City, the objects were harder to see and we lost sight of them as they headed Northeast. I was extremely anxious & giddy from the first view, and still am. My boyfriend was scared. He was uncomfortable by them. And remained that way.
