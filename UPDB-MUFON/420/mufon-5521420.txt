Black trident object appeared to me in a sunny day, it came, hovered and flew away

The object i saw was similar with a black trident object without engines for propulsion. It was large (more than airplane is). It appeared at the same speed than a airplane, even though I thought it was a strange airplane. Suddenly it begun to reduce the speed. And it started to hover. I remember i was playing a soccer with friends that morning. In the moment i had seen it, i was taking a rest... so, when the object started to hover, I tried to warn my friends, but i don't know what happened with my voice and i didn't say anything (i think i was in shock, or surprised). the object hovered for few seconds and turned the direction (not 1801) and started to acelerate using something similar to reverse gear. The speed achieved was so high that in few seconds i couldn't see it anymore.

I told to my friends what i had saw but nobody believed that story. Still today i have no idea what i have seen in that sunny morning.. I think it was a secret millitary project from U.S or other country.. i really don't know.. It was my only strange experience in my whole life. 

Please, if you know, or have already heard something similar with this, please tell me.
