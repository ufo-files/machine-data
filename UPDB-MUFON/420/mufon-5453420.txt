{tjd} Line of Lights over Peoria County

There appeared to be lights that looked like a string of beads connected equidistantly by an invisible string dragged slowly across the sky from west to East. There were 30-40 of them, very star like, and they just vanished before reaching the eastern horizon. They moved incredibly slowly in a perfectly straight line. There are supposed to be meteor showers, but these were incredibly bright and moving directly in a straight line very slowly with equal spacing and size. That’s not meteor shower behavior. We were celebrating my friend’s birthday out in the driveway and the lights were so bright that they interrupted our conversation. We started panicking because they were so bizarre.
