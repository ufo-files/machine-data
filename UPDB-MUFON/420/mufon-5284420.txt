Saw to sets of Very Bright Red lights moving thru the sky

Went out into my rear lani and noticed thru the window facing east 2 sets of bright red lights moving from the NE moving across the sky towards the SW. Each object had 2 very bright non flashing reds lights at the top and bottom and while it was too far away to see, there appeared to be some sort of connection between the red lights. One object was slightly above the other and seemed to be in formation. There was absolutely no noise or sounds of any kind from these objects. My house seems to be in the normal flight path of the local airport and Allegiant airlines jets come over often. As well as medical and sheriff's dept helicopters. The objects I saw were 100% not any of these. These objects were moving too fast to be ballons or drones. I checked the local weather and wind. Clear skies and winds 5mph or less. Also wanted to mention I used to be a Private pilot and Firefighter and Fire Inspector. And I have never seen any other objects like these before. Unfortunately I did not have time to take any photos.
