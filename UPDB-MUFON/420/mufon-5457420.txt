{rjl} Bright red orbs

Roommate had ran in after going outside to smoke a cigarette frantically telling us we had to go outside. We went out and saw 2 to 5 bright red orbs in the sky. They kept disappearing then reappearing. They also kept going in random directions, also seemed like they were flashing lights at each other. As they got closer they seemed to kinda be a triangle shape to me, but I don’t think the others noticed. We were amazed as they kept going and coming back in different patterns. I do want to mention the area we saw them looked to be right over the Indian resevervarion. They have completely disappeared now but we keep watching. There was no sound either.
