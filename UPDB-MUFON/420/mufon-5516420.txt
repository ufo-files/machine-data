{cak} Moving fluctuating light above Sebring/Berlin lake area

Me and two of my friends were watching the Perseus Meteor Shower (told to looking the WNW direction) when I noticed an erratically moving light in the NNE direction. It was moving far to erratically to be an airplane, so I kept my eye on it. It was moving all over the place but keeping around the same area going in a path seemingly in our direction. It would go fast at times and then just hover at others. But one thing for sure, it would not stay still a friend of mine compared its motions to a bumblebee. I felt entranced by the object being the first time i saw something like it before. The only reason I lost track of it was due to the cloud coverage. And like I said, last I saw it appeared to be heading south.
