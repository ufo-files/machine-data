{rjl} Fishing off shore with Wife observed light changing quick directions .

While on a fishing trip on Big Chief Lake in Hayward Wisc. my Wife and I noticed a very bright Star in the northern sky around 10 PM that night. We first thought it was nothing but we noticed that it moved very quickly to the North and few seconds it changed position to southwest and then dropped to the south in the sky. After half an hour of watching this flying around in all directions we started to get scared and quickly left and retired into our cabin. This happened in 1972 and no one ever believed us.
