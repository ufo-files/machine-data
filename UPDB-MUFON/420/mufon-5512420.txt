{nd} Dimly lit, oval-shaped object seen by drivers on highway; it was being pursued by helicopters.

I was with a friend in my car. He was driving south/south east on the main highway in Las Vegas, NV, the 95 south. He said: "What is that?," and I turned to look up through the windshield. There was a huge, oval shaped object that seemed almost dimly lit all around. It emitted a dim haze. It was farther away in the distance by a mountain range closely outside of the city area to the left of the highway. There were two black helicopters circling the object, shining spotlights on it. We watched this happen for four minutes until the object was behind us. I turned to look, and it changed position abruptly and with extreme speed. The helicopters re-positioned themselves to apparently attempt to shine their spotlights on it again. After about a minute of this, it was gone. The helicopters then hit each other with the spotlights as if the light was no longer blocked by the object. They then proceeded to shine spotlights on the mountain, and then they stopped and flew south east.
