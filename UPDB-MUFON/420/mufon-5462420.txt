{rjl} A large bright reddish pink object hovered in sky for almost a minute and then darted towards me suddenly

I was about 14 years old. I was in my backyard around 9pm listening to music on my Walkman and swinging on a rope swing hung from a tree. I don't recall how it came to my attention, but suddenly I noticed a very large pinkish/red light hovering in the sky. It was difficult to determine size because I couldn't tell how high up it was or how far away it was. From my perspective it was very large. The light from the object seemed to be contained within itself and although it was large, it was difficult to tell what shape it was. It was mostly a hovering self contained large mass of light. I stared at it for less than a minute feeling frozen, in shock and confused as I had never seen anything like it before or since. Suddenly the light went from hovering stationary to bolting directly towards me at a very fast speed. I had a surge of adrenaline and I quickly ran inside my house feeling extreme fear. Once in my house I stood even more confused but now curious. I cautiously went into my front yard to see if the light was still there, but I didn't see anything anywhere. I never heard any news about it or heard of any other witnesses. I am of sound mind. No mental illness and I was not on any drugs or medications. I have had multiple unexplained experiences throughout my life, although this was the only think I have ever witnessed or seen in the sky that wasn't later explained or that I could find another rational explanation for. I am unsure of exact date and time except that this happened around 1994 and it was between 9pm and 10pm.
