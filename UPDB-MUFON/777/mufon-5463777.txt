{rjl} Saw ufo on way home. Filmed and witnessed ufo disappear.

Driving back to town from Belleville,IL and saw the bright light in the skysouth east of Freeburg, IL. Filmed light for a bit and the light intensified quickly and then returned to original brightness. The object disappeared shortly after I started filming it. My wife thought it was a plane but it made no noise and then the light intensified quickly which I've never seen a plane do. It was exciting to see. I stopped recording because as you can hear in the video my daughter was getting restless in the car so I turned of my phone to talk to her. When I looked back a moment later toward the area the light was it was gone.
