I was joined co-workers at the Hyatt Place rooftop bar. The server offered us free drink specials in honor of the full moon. She said, "Isn't she gorgeous!" I turned to notice the moon and saw it looked partially obscured. I thought this was unusual as it is not yet eclipse season. I walked to the edge of the rooftop patio and took two pictures with my new Google Pixel3 XL phone. Afterward I was a bit overwhelmed and decided to leave. When I tried to use the Uber app my phone was not opening it properly. I asked the concierge for help and after entering information it still wouldn't function properly. I went back to the parking garage where my car was parked from work and tried calling friends to pick me up. One attempted to send and Uber but I was unable to ping my location. I walked back out on the street and after multiple tries my Uber app worked. My Uber receipt shows I arrived at home after 11pm, I have no way to account for the missing time.
