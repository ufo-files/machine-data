whitish/silverish orb

as i pulled into my parking space at work i looked to east. the sun was just comiing over the horizon. as i looked away something caught my eye. it was a small whitish or silver colored oval.it was hard to tell at the distance i was viewing it from. it stayed exactly still in the sky. i work within a quarter mile of hopkins international airport so i am very used to what planes of all sizes and helicopters look like at that distance. this was definately neither of those. i then thought it was a star or planet. but when i looked around i realized it was too bright to still be seeing a star, planet or the moon. it was the only point of light visible in the sky. i proceeded to watch it for approximately four minutes. at that point the object simply vanished from sight in an instant. almost like someone blew out a candle. being so close to the airport you learn the incoming and outgoing routes of the aircraft. the object was in a part of the sky where aircraft don't circle or come in for landing. the only reaction i had was that there was no way i was seeing what i was seeing.
