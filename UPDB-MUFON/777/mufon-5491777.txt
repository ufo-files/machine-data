Observed 3 red orbs / objects playing in the sky

A friend and I had gone to the local Park (centennial park) late one night after the bar, we had bought some off-sales and thought we would wind down at the park before going home. After about 20 min we noticed a single red light kinda dancing in the sky. It had no real direction. All we could see was a bright red light. After a few Min's we both noticed 2 more joining from the west. It was almost like they were playing in the sky, they would alternate doing flyby's and fly circles in the sky. We watched for a good 5 min, sitting still against the old swimming pool, then it was like a snap of a finger they were gone. We both were confused as to what happened, was nothing like we had ever scened. I knew in the back of my mind it was nothing human. We sat waiting wondering if they would return, about 10 - 15 min after they disappeared they returned doing the same thing. They came in about 3 s apart, same area. We watched for aprx 3 min, they would swirl and fly random patterns and passing (head on) each other close before one had disappeared, 5 s later the last two followed. The thing that burned into my mind was how FAST they came & left. They took off in a straight line from where we were sitting, almost at a 45' angle leaving a faint red line. To this day we still have no idea what they are, I started looking into it and appears that these red lights have been scene else where. I am an avid watcher of your show, and I am very happy to have found a place to report to. My friend can also vouch to what happened, I wish I had known about mufon earlier, I thought it would be best to report even tho it was so long ago. Thanks
