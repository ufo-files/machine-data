Meteor/Shooting Star/Fireball

At 455 pm, traveling southbound on Andrews Av, approaching Oakland Park Bl, I saw a fireball travel west to east. It was twelve noon, in front of me, and descended on an approximate 45 degree angle towards the Atlantic Ocean. It was very bright with a small tail. Possibly from the setting sun to the west?? More than likely it was a meteor, but a very interesting daylight sighting of some sort. Did anyone else see it?
