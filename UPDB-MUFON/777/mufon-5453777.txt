{cak} Observed 12-15 orbs moving across the sky for an hour

I watched 12-15 orbs moved across the sky for about an hour. They moved in a straight line about the same speed. They were about 1-3 minutes apart and event lasted for about an hour. All the orbs move about the same speed around the sky. All of orbs where a constant dimness of a bright white color with the exception of one orb. The one orb got brighter for 1-2 seconds before diming back down and then disappearing. All of the orbs seem to just disappear in the same spot about the same area. 
The number of orbs where probably much higher than the numbers I witness. I walked to the park to get away from street lights in between pictures.

They started from 307° NW moving and disappearing around to 354N°. Took about 10-15 seconds to point a to point b.

Last 3 pictures are of an airplane unless there was something else I did not see. I was hoping I could catch them both in a frame but not that lucky.
