5 shiny metallic objects flying north in triangle formation. Also 2 airliners in sky at same time

I was walking around the Heinz factory from the canteen to the stores. I looked up at an airliner that was flying past and noticed approx 5 shining objects flying high but leaving no vapor trail the objects where travelling slowly north three of them in a triangle formation. I phoned a friend to come out of the factory and see if he could identify them. The time stamp on my phone was 11:15. Neither my friend or I could identify them hence this report.
