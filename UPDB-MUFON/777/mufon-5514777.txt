(GS) Plane flew by a light in the sky, one of the objects shined a light at the other (Couldnt tell from which direction) and kept going

I was walking my dog, I have seen a lot of weird things when I walk my dog but I just cant pick it up because my iPhone wont pick up something so faint. I saw an orb just floating in the air, and I was watching a plane fly towards it. I the next thing I know there is a large light on one of the objects, like I said I couldnt tell which object was shining the light. For some reason the plane stayed in the same spot longer, but it didn't stop. It looked like it slowed down, but it didn't at the same time. Its very hard to explain, its like the plane was flying through peanut butter or mud, like it had drag. After the light shined for a few seconds the UFO just started to fly north, it was slower than the plane so at first I just assumed it was a helicopter but it was extremely high up, and I could be wrong but I don't think planes and helicopters can fly at the same height. They both just went on with their path, the plane heading West while the UFO went North. It made me sweat, and say outloud, "What the F***".
