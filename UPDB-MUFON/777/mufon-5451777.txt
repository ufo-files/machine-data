Floating Object with Orbs Surrounding it.

I was looking out of the window when I saw a small black object floating slowly NNW in direction, It was going over a small area of Colchester called Barnhall, I was observing from a small area called The Willows. I thought it was a bird at first, but it was slow and stayed relatively in the same area. I ran to another room and found a pair of binoculars and ran back to the first room. I looked at the object through them and saw a black object that looked like two oval stones, one on top of the other. They seemed to be rocking but not spinning. I then noticed higher to the left of this object at 11 o'clock, a smaller black 'orb', which was following the same path. I then noticed another orb to the right of the object approx 1:30 o'clock and another one at around 5 o'clock. They were all floating the same path NWW. I them observed the main object, descend below the 5 o'clock orb and the 5 o'clock orb change position to 7 o'clock. I then told my partner who looked through the binoculars and saw the same objects. When she returned the binoculars, the objects were now further away and becoming feint. I noticed also the main object had moved back to its original position and third orb had stayed at 7 o'clock. I watched until I could no longer see it. Total viewing was about 7 minutes
