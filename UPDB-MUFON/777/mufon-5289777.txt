white sphere in sky - far away - moving slowly

At start of our morning dog walk (7:40 am)- my wife noticed what looked like a star next to the moon in the SW sky (it was daytime - with no clouds). I commented you can't see starts during the day. It was about the size of what would have been a star at night. we watched it slowly move eastward. We pointed it out to 2 neighbors passing by who also agreed they saw it as well. It was moving way too slowly to be an airplane. Our dog walk took us under tree cover for about 8 minutes- when we got back to a clearing- it was still visible and had moved slightly more to the east. My wife got binoculars but even with them couldn't discern any distinct features. It appeared solid white- we both had to go inside to get ready for work.
The wind was moving slowly eastward that day as well- it may have been a weather balloon, however where we live- with a major airport and not far from a military base, I don't know if they would release weather balloons that close.
