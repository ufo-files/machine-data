{dr}Bright Orange Orb - No Sound - No Navigation Lights - Moving rapidly overhead - Approx. 2500 Feet Directly Above - 7:15pm MST

I observed a bright orange orb approaching from the South on a straight line trajectory to the North. Approximately 2500 feet in altitude. As the object past overhead, there were no navigation lights, and no sound form the object. The orb was moving quickly. The object was passing directly below the clouds and occasionally reflected it's orange bright glow on the clouds above. I grabbed my camera and snapped a photo but my flash was on and it distorted the picture. I did capture a picture of the object but as in most cases, it's not an ideal exposure. 

I've heard about these orb sightings and was shocked to see one myself tonight.
