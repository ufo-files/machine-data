{rjl} It was V-shaped.

I was ten years old when I first saw the lights in Phoenix night sky and my two mothers who by the way are a lesbian couple were asleep in their bedroom because it was bedtime. Anyway, I chosed to secretly stay up and see the night sky while my two female parents and my three older sisters were sleeping in their beds. Suddenly I saw a strange form of reddish or orange lights in the night sky, so I secretly tip-toed from my bedroom and slowly walked downstairs to the living room to get a better view of the lights in the night sky. Suddenly, I saw that the lights were part of a V-shaped object hovering over Phoenix. At first I was scare, but then I became amazed at the object in the night sky because it made me belief that we're not alone in this universe. But sadly both the lights and the object itself disappeared and I went back to bed.
