Orange light slowly moving through sky

We were waiting to watch the fireworks and noticed this bright orange light moving through the sky, to the east of me, moving from south to north. It was brighter than an airplane (and lower in the sky than one) and definitely not a firework. No noise to indicate it was a helicopter. So I am not sure what to think! It is an Unidentified Flying Object! If anyone knows what it is/was, let me know. Thanks.
