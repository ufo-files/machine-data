{dr}Saw an orange shape coming from S/SW in a straight line going N/NE

We were sitting on the porch in the evening when an we noticed a glowing object coming from the southwest in a straight line heading to the northeast. At first you always think it must be an airplane or helicopter but there were no flashing lights or noise. It was just a big orange light, not pulsating just a constant glow flying slowly across the sky in a straight line above the tree line but below wheres you normally see small aircraft. My kids and I watched the object until it finally was too far north and east to see. I was in the military and have been around and worked on planes flying at night, this did not look like any aircraft that Ive seen.
