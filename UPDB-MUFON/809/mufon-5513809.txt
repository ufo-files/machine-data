(GS) Saw hovering craft. It moved out of the way when I filmed. Black, triangle with wings.

I had seen it two weeks prior hovering over a house going 10mph. This time it was stopped and I took a few stills. It was off of Hwy 152 about 2-3 miles East of 435. It had triangular positioned lights and I was actually expecting it to be the supposed TR B3, but it had wings and the shape was more refined. I actually think its just a secret government craft, not alien. Ive seen UFOs in my life and I didn't get that overwhelming feeling in my mind like I am just an ant.
 Anyway, I was driving west on 152 from Zona Rosa and as soon as I got close, I pulled over to video it. It had started moving forward as I got out of the car and the object sped up as I filmed.I was really close, they saw me if they had eyes. My headlights were still on on my car. It was silent at first and then I heard a humming like a loud refrigerator and noises that resembled an aircraft, but sounded almost fake.
 As it passed me I noticed blinking lights in the back. Just watch the video.
