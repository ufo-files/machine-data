Black Triangle, 3 glowing orange lights outlining the object itself.

At approximately 23:00 hours London time on the 7th of September 1997 I observed a matt black triangular object traveling from East to West in a direct flight line from Gravesend towards the Isle of Grain, Kent.
I had just been called by friends to meet up at the Thames sea wall at hope point Cliffe, We would meet to star gaze, watch space junk and the space station "ISS" go over or very high flying jets every week or two, (I own a pair of stellar binoculars).
As I stepped out the back door I lit a cigarette and looked up to check the night sky.
At first I noticed a small orange glow coming from a 60 to 65 degree angel to the east, I walked into the garden (away from any light source) to get a better look, 
Almost in a direct line over my head was a black triangle emitting a sodium orange glow from above each corner, So outlining itself that I could tell it was matt black as the edges being illuminated did not reflect any colour or shine, 
I saw a very crisp outline of a perfect triangle, traveling east to west Flat leading edge side on (Not point first).
No sound, against a very dark clear night sky.
If the Triangle was about 200ft above me, The sides of the craft where between 27ft an 34ft.
I watched from my back garden for about 6 to 7 minutes as it seemed to drift along very smoothly,
A neighbours garage and tree blocking my view so I walked around the front out into the road to see the object again still on the same course but getting fainter until less than a minute later it faded away.

As A foot note: At the time I had been back an fourth to the sea wall on Cliffe marshes nearly every other day over the past week or two, As you go onto the marshes at Cliffe about 4 miles from Cliffewoods I had noticed that in a farmers field about 200yards from pond hill road some activity in a grazing field on the marshes, A group of people setting up a large radio antenna, the type that needs an tower at least 60ft high, with an old style caravan next to it (in a flat barren area)?
