Stephenville sighting from the air Jan 2008

I was on an American airlines flight from orange county to DFW in Jan 2008, sitting in first class 1D(window). AS we started our descent into Dallas, and probably 10 -15 min before we landed we were going over a town, which I later found was Stephenville. The elevation of the airplane was probably 10k-20k ft. Looking out the window and at an angle from the vertical of about 20 degrees below me was the layout of the town. At the edge of the southwest corner of the town there was an immense flying structure which was moving very slowly towards the NE at very low altitude (<500ft).. I estimated from my vantage point that the craft was about 2/3 the size of the town's footprint. AS I watched in amazement and thought to myself that everyone will see this object as it enters the town, it started to fade and became invisible over about 5 seconds, ie before it went across the town. All these years, I have not mentioned this to anyone because of the high strangeness, and given my seniority in a global eng/const company. I happened to see something about the stephenville sightings on TV and thought I'll add my statement, for what its worth. Note that I now live in Australia, so email best
