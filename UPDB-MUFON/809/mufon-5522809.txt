Two distinct objects giving off bright yellowish orange glow with a slightly off center red light

My roommate and I were standing outside of our apartment and noticed two objects flying towards our location from over the tree line. The objects were glowing the color of a burning cigarette butt with a slightly off center red light. The two objects slowly flew overhead at about the speed of a helicopter. After moving overhead they began to ascend in an arc shape up into the sky until out of sight. The overall experience lasted about 2 1/2-4 minutes in a highly populated area.
