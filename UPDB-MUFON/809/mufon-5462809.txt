A moving very bright white light stopped then turned blue

Woke up around 4.30 am. I was sleeping on the couch in the living room. I saw in my line of sight a very bright white light. I thought it may be an airliner as I live not too far from Heathrow.I got up to look because of its brightness, expecting it to turn but it didn't it just stopped. I had a new phone and tried to take some photos but I didn't do a very good job as I wasn't sure how to get a close up. I took some anyway and then enlarged them.i have three photos in one it looks like a chevron in another a disk and the last a ball. During this time the object turned bright blue.As it wasn't moving I lay back down on the couch and kept my eyes on it. I then fell asleep. My only thought while this was going on was. It can't be ?
