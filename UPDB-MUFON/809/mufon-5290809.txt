Ring shaped UFO flying over the park behind my house.

I saw something through my window that moved through the sky in a way that made me take another look. When I couldn’t figure out what it was, I began filming with my phone. It was a ring shaped object flying over the park behind my house. I would guess based on the distance that it was about 3 feet in diameter. My first guess was that it was a drone, but it was hollow in the middle. After researching, I could not find a single identifiable flying object of that shape or size.
