{jpg} WENT OUTSIDE TO HAVE A CIGARETTE THOUGHT I WAS LOOKING AT THE MOON IT WAS BRIGHT AS A STREETLIGHT, BUT IT WASN'T THE MOON

FILLED OUT AND FILED AT MUFON HEADQUARTERS WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE BY L. FLECHTNER 18809, ON WEDNESDAY MAY 2ND, 2018.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

I WENT OUTSIDE ON MY BACK PORCH TO HAVE A CIGARETTE AND LOOKED UP AT WHAT I THOUGHT WAS THE CRESCENT MOON, A BRIGHT WHITE/PALE YELLOW LIGHT IN THE SKY. I THEN REALIZED IT WAS NOT THE MOON. I SAW ABOUT THREE ORBS THE SAME BRIGHTNESS LEAVE THE LARGER LIGHT AND GO A SHORT DISTANCE FROM THE OBJECT AND THEN RETURN TO IT.I WATCHED IT FOR ABOUT EIGHT MINUTES THEN IT SLOWLY FADED OR WENT OUT. I COULDN'T SEE IT ANYMORE. I HAD SEEN THIS LIGHT BEFORE BUT DIDN'T HAVE MY PHONE WITH ME, THIS TIME I HAD MY GALAXY 7 ON ME AND TOOK PICTURES AND A 5 MINUTE VIDEO. I WENT INSIDE KIND OF EXCITED ABOUT WHAT I HAD SEEN SO I WENT OUT AGAIN AND I SAW A SECOND ORB/LIGHT. THIS ONE WAS REDISH AND A SMALLER CYLINDER SHAPE WITH RED TINT AT THE ENDS. IT HOVERED FOR ABOUT 1 1/2 MINUTES AND THEN MOVED SLOWLY AWAY. I COULDN'T SEE IT WITH MY NAKED EYES ANYMORE, BUT I COULD SEE IT THROUGH THE PHONE AFTER IT MOVED AWAY NORTHWEST. IT ROUGHLY APPEARED IN THE SAME SPOT AND AREA AS THE FIRST WHITE/PALE YELLOW LIGHT.
I WENT BACK IN THE HOUSE AND THOUGHT ABOUT IT AND THE CAME BACK OUTSIDE AND FILMED THE WHOLE NIGHT SKY TO SHOW WHAT IT REALLY LOOKS LIKE WITHOUT THE OBJECTS.I HAVE PICTURES AND VIDEO. I ALSO CAUGHT SOME ODD OBJECTS IN THE PICTURES. 
THE DATE WAS THE 19TH OR 20TH. IT WAS A CLEAR NIGHT, I DON'T REMEMBER SEEING THE REAL MOON, BUT THERE IS A BRIGHT STREET LIGHT NEARBY.
