It looked like a star, but maybe a bright pill shaped.

My girlfriend and I where simply in front of the fire at the cottage in Lanaudière. At aroud 7:30 pm, I noticed a star that was a bit brigter than usual. I felt like I have seen it mooved, just a bit. Later in the evening, It started to sig zag herratically, but seemingly within a predertermine diameter. I called my friend, living on a ranch some 300 km of there to look in the sky and they saw the same thing, wich ment whatever it was, it was very high in the sky. At some point, it turned red/white, and then white again. After this, it started zig zagging herratically in the sky at what seemed enormous speed and distances. Straight lines, right, left, then right then up, dow circles. I don't know what it was, but it was no plane, no satellite nor meteor. It was in the sky for at least two hours, but stable or somewhat stable most of the time. When it is still, it looks like when you see a planet or a star form a distance. It was making enough light that when a cloud passed before it, the light would be diffused. The speed and the mouvements where surreal.
