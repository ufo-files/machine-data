I saw a UFO while driving home.

When I was driving home from the Tremonton Rodeo at about 12:08 am, 8/28/21. I saw a big light in the sky near Farmington, Utah. At first, I thought the light was a plane, but it looked way bigger and it wasn't flashing any lights. There were about four bright lights on the exterior of the craft. The craft was all black. In Utah, we have a lot of sight-watching towers on mountains. I made sure that this was way above the mountains so we know that isn't a possibility. The craft moved very slowly in one direction and then slowly switched directions. Then all of a sudden it disappeared. I think that the craft shot up in less than a second. This all happened in about 3 minutes while I was driving home near Farmington, Utah.
