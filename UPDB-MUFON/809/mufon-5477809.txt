{rjl} It was a very bright orb that was going way too fast and was way too low to be an airplane.

I was letting my dog out to pee when my mother pointed out the UFO. I turned around and saw a very bright orb. At first, I thought it could have been an airplane, but the object was flying extremely fast- probably triple the amount of speed an airplane usually goes. It was also much brighter and flying much lower than an airplane. I could hear and feel a very low buzz as it passed. After this occurred and I could no longer see it, I felt very unnerved. I was confused for a good two minutes, as was my mother. I lost sight of it as it flew away and I was no longer able to see the object.
