Myself and a friend we're driving home to Fallon, NV from Reno we used a back road called sheckler cut off to skirt around the town to get home before midnight. We came to a stop sign and right there in front to the left of our car was a teardrop craft just hovering there I couldn't believe my eyes I turned to my friend and said do you see what I'm seeing? Then I proceeded to open the door because I wanted to go get near it check it out my friend was freaking out get in the car get in the car he's saying I said no I'm going to get near it maybe even get on top of the car so I can touch it he was screaming at me he was scared and while I was out there just about to get on top of the car hood to maybe touch it, it silently turn from a regular teardrop to a sideways teardrop and sped off towards Fallon naval air station I can't believe it it made no noise had no windows no lights no heat nothing it was just there I'll never forget it when we got home I told my husband and my daughter and my friend's wife and they didn't believe us my friend kept yelling at me to stop talking about it yes it did happen but he never wants to hear about it again I met up with him quite a few years later and talked about it and he freaked out all over again telling me not to talk about it that it hurts him to think about it. It's stuck with me so vividly that I knew I had seen a craft like that but but where? around 2016 I came across a video called flight of the navigator and I'm telling you it looks just like that but it was small it was like only able to maybe hold one or two small passengers not as big as the one on flight of the navigator that's the best description I have of it
