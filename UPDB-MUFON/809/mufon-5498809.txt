{nd} driving to work, noticed blinking lights like on a plane, but it was not like any plane I ever saw

I was driving to work at 445 am . It was very dark. I was on farm road 167. North of Springfield Mo. going south. Towards Springfield. It was a rural area, very hilly and curvy. I noticed the lights of what I thought was a plane. As it passed in front of me , left to right, in the sky . It was very strange. It seemed to be low to the ground. It was either reflective or lit from the inside. There was no physical structure , just a row of grey or lit squares. It had random blinking small lights. I slowed to almost a stop but went down hill and it went behind the trees to the right. I don't think it descended . I did down the hill. I could not regain the site of because of the trees. I was intrigued and puzzled. I had never seen anything like that. When I got to work , I made a note, "weird plane " So I could tell my friend when she called. She called me four hours after the sighting and I had actually forgotten until I looked at my note. That was odd.
