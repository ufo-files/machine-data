Saw cluster of star-like objects (planes) over 3 distinct lines in sky.

On November 26 maybe a fine chance the 25th, I was driving by car traveling north to south from Plainnview NY to Wantagh,NY on the Seaford Oyster Bay Expressway, I noticed a cluster(10?) of starlike objects and a little plane moving toward this cluster - it had a blinking red light, in the upper right hand corner of sky which I thought were airplanes and due to heavy Thanksgiving traffic but immediately noticed at least three vertical white line toward the center lower to lower right of my view. These straight lines were not illuminated, solid, white,and stationary which looked like white curtain pull wands.  I never saw solid (not blurred like jet lines) in the sky before this. I haven't a clue what this could have been. I was amazed but then lost sight a about 5 minutes earlier because I had to get off at exit 105. There was moderate traffic which I then tried to keep my eyes on the road and had no thought of reporting this, only to my boyfriend later that night, or getting out and taking a picture with my camera on my cell phone.
