{nd} Resembled a form like birds flying south. Had a point at its brightest and the rest dimmed as it went down.

Within 7 minutes saw 4 "shooting stars" and then saw this cone looking pointed thing slowly roam across the sky. The first point was very bright and as the wings flowed the rest of the objects dimmed. Lit up the majority of the sky. It was too large and didn't blink like an airplane would. As soon as I pointed it out to my mate, it disappeared right in front of us.
