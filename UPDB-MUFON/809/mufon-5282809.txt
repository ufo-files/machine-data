Small Orange Object Floating

Small Orange/Red/Yellow object, occasionally blinking silver, looked initially like a plane. We have a very large amount of aircraft traffic in this area. This did not proceed along a trajectory. It remained visible for over 30min. It occasionally wobbled quickly but generally meandered slowly across ~5 degrees of my field of vision. I viewed it from different parts of my house and, unlike the aircraft that typically pass overhead, it changed slightly in location leading me to conclude it was closer than I previously thought. I did not believe that I could have gotten a decent photo of the object with my cell phone due to previous failed night photography attempts and bug screens in the room. 
Aided by google maps and CAD tools, I estimate it to have been about 4200ft away placing it somewhere over the Danvers river between Salem and Beverly. Even if my distance measurement is incorrect, the line of vision would put it in view of a high traffic area with a bridge of considerable size, I have to think there would be additional witnesses in the area.
