3 balls of orange light traveling in a V formation being trailed not far behind by flashing strobes

It was 8:45pm when I was sitting on my deck when I saw three orange shaped orbes being trailed by flashing strobes. I went in the house to get my wife and kids to look at it. They too witnessed it. The whole formation was moving from east to west. I said to them what is all the flashing lights following the orange orbes. My wife and kids just brushed it off thinking it was some sort of military crafts but I did not think so. [item moved/CMS/nd] The movement of the objects were moving slower than a helicopter, jet or plane.
