{rjl} Glowing fluorescent green ball

I was driving home from work, I was approximately 1 mile from home light cloud cover. Just as I topped the hill the sky became fluorescent green ( brilliant ) then straight from the South came a giant fluorescent green ball ( or orb ) heading straight North. It was at tree top level, traveling at a tremendous speed, then the sky was the same fluorescent green and then same as nothing happened. No sound, exhaust, movement, other than straight flight, smell nothing. I mentioned this to a couple of my friends the next day and they too had seen the same thing only on different days. My son and I again seen this happen about a week later only off to the North of our position which was near where I was on my sighting. Later that night in a town 8 or so miles SW of my sighting ( Mountain View, several Blackhawk helicopters where spotted over the town. Later meaning between 10:00 pm and 1:00 am. Several people I have talked with spotted the same green ball in different positions in the area as well as towns 55 and 75 miles from my sighting. ( towns Green River Wy 55 miles, and Rock Springs Wy 75 miles to the East of Lyman. )
