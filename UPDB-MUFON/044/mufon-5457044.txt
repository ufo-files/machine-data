{rjl} Bright light in shy changed after flash from photo taken on phone.

At 11:30 pm I witnessed a bright orange light north west of my house over a lake. I pulled out my phone and attempted to take a photo of the light. When I took the photo my phone used the flash function and the light then went away. A moment later there was a flashing light that was much smaller the the original light and it was no longer orange but white and red. I continued to watch the flashing lights as the moved from north west to north east. I would estimate that the lights moved 7 miles in less then 45 seconds.
