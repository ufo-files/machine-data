{nd} UFO while driving.

This was strange because a few days prior to this incident my buddy was joking around to constantly keep my eyes in the sky. Well today, i did just that. I was driving down pacific avenue in parkland when i noticed two unusually bright lights on the horizon. I kept driving for a mile, just watching these two objects stay stationary. As i drove closer, the orbs became bigger and i noticed them pulsating. I saw a passenger plane flying in the same skyline, and before i knew it one object moved right and then boom it disappeared. The 2nd object just appeared to hover and it looked stationary. A few miles later i noticed passengers around me had noticed the object. The man to my right had his face in his windshield trying to get a good look. Just when i thought i was getting close enough to see if it was a helicopter it moved away from my direction at a very quick speed and disappeared.
