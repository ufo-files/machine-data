{cak} Glowing sphere hovering 20-30 ft away

Around 1993 I'm guessing .I was around 11 yrs old .late night church service had ended and I was the first one to go outside and the rest stayed inside to mingle .I was walking to the car and stopped .I turned around and looked back toward the church and on the left side of it in the cotton field hovered a sphere the size of a basketball .just above the cotton 20 ft away or so.I stood in amazement and in silence .until it decided to take off in a straight line and change direction in an instant and disappeared.my aunt and cousin that took me there that night told me it was an angel.I haven't a clue .years later when I was around 20 my brother gave me a call and told me he was driving at about twilight in Arcadia la,that he had a sphere in a field moving parralell but at a distance.
