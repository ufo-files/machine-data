{cak} South Central PA UFO sighting.

I live in a cabin in the woods, in a pretty rural area. This incident took place over a heavily wooded ridge. Went outside for a smoke around 2040 hrs., and I saw a very bright light approaching from due West like an aircraft. However, it looked like a "spiked star" to the naked eye (see my terrible sketch). After approaching my position in a linear fashion, it stopped....as if it were hovering.
It was completely silent at all times. Maybe 1000-3000 feet in altitude. Not sure of distance, perhaps 0.25-0.50 miles from my position.
To the naked eye, it appeared to be four lights in a cross configuration, with "spikes" protruding outwards like a Christmas Star, if you will. A couple of times, it looked like a German Cross (balkenkreuze). The four white lights blinked in a random manner.
After approaching, it hovered for approximately 20 minutes. During this time, it seemed to "scan" the area, from left to right, on a central axis...like an electric eye, if you will.. I waved, lit my phones flashlight to attract their attention, etc....
To my eye, it seemed to be surveying my area. I don't know.......
But after 20-25 minutes, it vaguely faded back into the cloud cover, in the same straightline path it arrived along.. Forgive my terrible iPhone6 photos...
