Circular flying object

On 5 June at approximately 1445 hrs. I was standing outside my office building on Minot AFB having a cigarette. It was a calm sunny day with a few puffy white clouds. I was looking west, and saw what I thought was a white bird, 40 degrees off horizon, flying in a gentle U shaped pattern from left to right. As I watched the object, it continued the U-shaped flight pattern, and went up through the bottom of a white cloud. I immediately realized it couldn't be a bird because the cloud was much farther away than I thought the object was. The object came up through the top of the cloud, and flew south for a moment into another cloud. My impression was that it must have been at least 50 feet across in size. The cloud seemed to be approximately 2 miles away. I strained to see the object, and glanced over to get my phone in order to film it. When I looked back, it was obscured by the second cloud, 5 degrees south, and I lost sight of it. I never saw the shape move. It was simply a circular object, and appeared to be the size of a very large star. The location was above, and beyond the west end of the runway on Minot AFB.
