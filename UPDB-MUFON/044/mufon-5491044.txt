{rjl} One witness and I observed an object in the air which seemed to be at a great distance but also gliding downwards as we studied it. It then seemed very different and further as it moved away on a straight path. It was a white then dull gray tube.

I was outside in the driveway with my boyfriend loading trash into his truck bed. I'm not sure why I noticed the object. I have poor vision without my glasses, however my boyfriend has better than 20/20 vision uncorrected. I asked him what the object in the sky was. He said it looked like an airplane without wings. The object seemed very high up as we tried to observe if it was an airplane. As we studied the object it seemed to glide downward in a random way as if carried by the wind like a bird. As we watched the object we actually began to consider if it was a bird, as the object now seemed to move in this random way and appeared to be white in color. Then it seemed impossible it was a bird because it was so high in the sky. We had also considered it was a drone but it was too high in the sky. Also the object now seemed too large to have ever even considered it a bird. It appeared muted gray with white lights on the back side which was now all we could see as it left in a direct or deliberate pattern as opposed to the random overhead gliding. The entire event last approximately 5-7 minutes. There was a sense of incredulity and interest. However there was this sense of disbelief as though our eyes were playing tricks on us, and we just couldn't figure out what it was no matter how we tried. Sounds ridiculous but it almost felt as though the overhead gliding was intentional and meant to confuse us greatly. After the random gliding directly overhead, it then moved in a straight line from SE to W. We watched the object until we could no longer see it as it approached the Gulf of Mexico.
