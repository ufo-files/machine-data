In the sky above Tesco Extra car park, I saw a white circular object moving at moderate speed directly overhead.

This white circular disc-like object moved at average speed (about as fast as a low-flying aeroplane) directly overhead above the car park. It was about 200 feet up in a partially cloudy sky and it moved from west to east. It went over the horizon and was visible all the time. Its shape was unlike any aircraft (or balloon) I had ever seen before. It was about the size of a commercial aeroplane. Two other people in the car park also saw the object, after I pointed it out to them. They confirmed the sighting.
