{rjl} Bright white light over Phoenix, OR

My wife and I were out on our back patio in Phoenix Oregon when we observed a white bright light in the sky over the tree line to the south towards Medford. This was around noon on Tuesday, June 18, 2019. It was definitely not a plane or helicopter, it was just a light. I tried to catch it on film but it barely showed up on camera. Anyway, it traveled at a brisk pace until it gota little past right over us, then it just hovered there in the same spot for about 2 minutes. When it started moving again, headed north, it got brighter and I was able to catch it on video for about 2 seconds before the light of the sun envelopes it and I wasn't able to track it anymore.
