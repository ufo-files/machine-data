Anomalous, amorphous low object

I was at my sisters, At the time she lived on the top floor (floor 11) of a block of flats in the Kirkstall area of Leeds, W.Yorks in the UK. I was gazing out of the window when I saw an unusual object moving slowly through the air below. The visible appearance of the object I can only describe as liquid, molten chrome in an amorphous, blob like shape. The motion of the object appeared as though it was unevenly weighted (imagine a knife being thrown in slow motion, where the handle is heavier than the blade.). It moved both up and down as it travelled in a southwest direction before it appeared to just blend into the background and become hard to discern.
The reason I did not report it at the time is because I was not aware of anyone I could report it to and last year,, when I heard about MUFON, I was unsure of the exact date. .However, thanks to Facebook reminding me of posts from previous years, I now know the exact date, as I posted a status about what I had seen shortly after it occurred.
