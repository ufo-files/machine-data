{nd} A bright circular light fell straight down from the sky; no tail observed; landing unknown

I was driving along I70 East around 11pm when I saw a really bright curicular light fall straight down from the sky. It did not change shape, had no tail, no noise, and left no evidence of it's path down. At first I thought shooting star but shooting star usually has a tail but this object never changed it's shape and kept it's circular appearnce before disappearing behind a wooded area with lots of tall trees.
