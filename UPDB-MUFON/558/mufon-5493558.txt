{dr}I was on a bike and other hovered kinda over me and followed me a short distance before I turned away peddling like crazy

I was 7or8 yrs old and I had just left the cow barn to go home for supper I was on my little red bike and I had to push it up the road away from the farm which is on a steep hill .I got to the top of the hill where there is a stop sign stopped to look for cars and catch my breath I happened to look up and there was a long cylinder like object silver of course with round looking widows in it just over the trees on the other side of the road I looked at it a little bit started peddling done the road I went around 200ft and stopped and looked up to my left and it had moved parrelel. To me I stood there a bit then continued down the road and very time I looked up it was beside me I started peddling like crazy road turned and it was gone when I got home and ran inside and told my mother she said there was no such thing but I remember it now like it happened yesterday.saw orbs on the farm yrs later
