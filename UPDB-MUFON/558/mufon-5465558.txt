Appeared to be following a passenger jet, then peeled off. It was catching up with plane before peeling off.

I was in my back garden feeding the birds, an aeroplane had caught my eye very high up and I pondered on where it was going and thought it seemed to be traveling faster across the sky than most other planes. It was them I saw another craft tracking along the plane's vapour trail, it was catching the plane up. I then remembered I had my phone in my pocket. It occurred to me this could end in the second craft making contact with the plane which worried me. However, before the craft caught up with the plane it peeled off south and disappeared from sight, the plane continued on its original flight direction.
