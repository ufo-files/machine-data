{cak}Transparent Boomerang

First noticed a star's light seem to bend and come back into place. I then noticed a craft with no lights and similar in shape to a boomerang or a triangle missing one of the legs, and as if it were in an invisibility cloak. The two wings were thick, and very similar to a boomerang. I watched the craft appear to float by at a height of a commercial plane or perhaps even higher. There was no noise. If it did fly at the altitude of a plane the craft could possibly have been the size of a football field or even a professional stadium. I noticed the craft from the north-northeast and fly over zenith and on toward the south-southwest. As it flew by the stars the craft appeared to bend the light, rather than block its cast. The experience lasted about 30 seconds
