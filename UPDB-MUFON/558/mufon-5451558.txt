2 very large silver coloured oval craft sitting together around 500 ft

I was walking my daughter to school, a lovey sunny morning not a cloud in the sky. I said to my daughter look at those stars, it is strange that 2 stars are shining so brightly side by side. then I though, stars are not silver they are more yellow in colour. my 13 year old daughter and I were watching trying to make out what they were.  After about 5 - 10mins, both craft shot straight up at such a speed, they went out of site in about 2 seconds. I have never forgot this and always wondered what I saw.
