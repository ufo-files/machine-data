Bright light dropping from the moon

My wife and I were in Kingman Arizona checking out some of their ancient ruins and Hoover dam in April and we were out on the patio of our motel room gazing at the full moon and sky on a crystal clear night. My wife spotted it first and brought my attention to it, it was a really bright clearly round white light dropping down from the direction of the moon deep in space it dropped for quite a distance it seemed like and then it just all of a sudden shot in the opposite direction after that and disappeared. It was an awesome sight. We both looked at each other and I ask her what in the world do you think that was? Her reply was it was not any type of a conventioal aircraft or a shooting star either because it just pulled a sharp left turn and it was gone without even slowing down.
