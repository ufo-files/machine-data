{dr}Lights moved fluidly in an east/southeat direction. Lights disappeared abruptly as if passing behind a curtain.

I was out walking my dog looking to the sky on occasion as I normally do. I happened to look up one last time before returning to my residence when I saw the lights of a disc shaped object in the sky to the east. It was moving in a more fluid manner than any plane or helicopter than I'd ever seen. Just as I was realizing what I was seeing it disappeared. The object did not appear to gain speed or change in elevation. The lights just seemed to slip behind a curtain in the sky. I was stunned and left with a heavy feeling in the pit of my stomach.
