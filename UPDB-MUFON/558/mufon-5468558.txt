{cak} Christmas eve saturday morning 2016, I was on I-10 approaching downtown houston in city limits. I'm not exactly sure on the time, but it was early morning, before 9...large crescent hoovering in hazy clouds multi lights

Driving in Houston, approaching downtown December 24 2016 saturday 7:30-8:30 in the morning. I first thought it was an airplane as i saw a bright white light. I noticed it didnt land. Sky was hazy. 2 minutes later, i saw the object still hoovering stationary, though it seemed to slightly move forward and retract into a cloud. Multi lights of red and blue maybe green. They didnt blink. I saw the front 1/3 of the crescent object as it creeped out of the cloud and in the cloud. Once in the cloud you could still see the faint lights. I got into downtown Houston and saw that the object was higher than the buildings and large. A vehicle next to me with a middle aged black man was staring at this object also, with his head slumped forward over the steering wheel, looking up at the object. 
I called my mother about what I saw. I was going to Eagle Pass Texas. I checked into a hotel for 2 days and reported to my dispatcher that I was sick. Felt like how you feel after going to a beach and getting sunburned. Not the skin, but overall warmth, weakness, fatigue, nausea.
