{rjl} Large Triangle heading west southwest toward and over St. Louis

Driving to work at approximately 6:00am on East bound I-64 at Caseyville IL. exit.When exiting the ramp I noticed an aircraft to the southeast appearing to be descending in a westerly direction. As I proceeded south on IL 157 the object was heading north towards me along the highway then it banked making a west southwest heading over the top of me. I could clearly make out the shape of the object at this point. It was a large triangle shaped object with bright white flashing lights.I then proceeded to follow it heading west on St. Clair Ave. towards East St. Louis and attempted to video the object with my cell phone. I was able to capture the flashing lights as it was still dark out. I then reported it to 911.
