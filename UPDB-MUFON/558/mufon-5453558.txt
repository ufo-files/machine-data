{rjl} 3 star like object moving across sky

I came outside to grab the garbage can and recycling bin. Looking up at the sky I notice that it was a particularly clear night.  Especially since the pollen count has been high. We had a recent rain though and the sky was quite nice.  Then I notice 3 star like objects. Except they were not stars. They were moving in a straight path. The first two were closer together almost lined up behind one another.  The third was further behind, a few degrees lower than the other two in sky. All three seemed to be moving at the same speed and direction. 
I watched until they passed the tree line and were no longer visible. This happened to be the same night another person nearby posted a sighting. At first I suspected that these might be star link satellites, but all the photographs I have see show dozens lined up near one another.
