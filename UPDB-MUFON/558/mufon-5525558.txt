Triangle

I am making this report for a gentleman who asked to remain anonymous. His story is as follows:

He's forty-nine years old and reports that in the summer of nineteen eighty-seven he saw a triangular outline which appeared to be a peer amid that had been almost flattened. There were lights on it and he said it reminded him of a city at night showing window lights. He said it was you which. He could not see around it, and it was 1000 feet up in his estimation. It covered an area approximately equal to one square city block. It was silently moving slowly. He Saw it in the area of Bonnie Avenue near Faro Park.
