Aprox. 7:00 P.M. standing on balcony of Apt. witnessed bright starlike object moving NW to SW. Magnitude was brighter of surrounding stars which were just beginning to show. Luminosity was constant. Aprox. 1min of visual. Brighter than most satellites Ive seen. Could be ISS.
