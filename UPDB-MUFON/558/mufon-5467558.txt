{dr}Orbs in triangular formation spread into larger triangles athen one darted away

I was watching fireworks with friends on the forth of July.

Upon their completion, at 21:46, a friend pointed NE stating, 1What is that?1

When we turned, three orbs were flying south in a perfect triangle when one of the orbs, the highest one in the sky, darted away at a massive speed in a southwest direction. I pulled out my phone and filled the remaining two orbs continuing their flight south.

My friend that initially saw the orbs said the were in a much tighter triangle and spread to the distance we saw after he pointed them out.

It was 85 degrees and partly cloudy, clouds far off in the distance.
