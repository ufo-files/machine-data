5 giant lights flew over the stadium above the buildings without a sound turning 90 degrees

A boomerang shaped outline was seen with 5 green lights in a triangle formation.
While walking my dog I turned to look at the night sky and noticed lights from above, it was in the middle of the city but this wasn't a plane since by the shape and lack of noise there was no possibility for any normal engine, it flew straight towards me down the stadium and then turned without slowing down or tilting to the west 90 degrees, it flew off away from the nearby river and disappeared over the buildings, the lights were dim but clearly noticeable, the outlines of the body were barely visible past the lights. Not sure of the altitude or size since it was above the buildings.
