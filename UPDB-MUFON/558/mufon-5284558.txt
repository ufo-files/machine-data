Flying circle

It was a clear morning in summer of 1964. I was walking to the Arts building at Duarte High School for my Summer School crafts class. I walked from Huntington Drive down Pops Road to an empty lot adjacent to the Arts building. I was about 20 feet from my desrination. I had an unsettling feeling so I stopped and looked around me. I saw a globe-shaprd object following the path I had walked, about three feet above the ground. It was about the size of a softball. It stopped a few inches away from me at waist level, and it kind of bounced for a few minutes, then flew back the way it came. It was glowing and had bands of electricity circling around it. It stayed in one spot, bouncing and down a few inches and had electricity moving around it's surface. I stood still and watched. I did not feel fear, I felt safe and curious. It stayed there next to me, bounced in the air and ran it's light show. When it was done examining me it took off in a westerly direction at about three feet above the ground. It was VERY fast. I continued on my way to class. I've only told a few people, this is the first time I've written about it. It only lasted a couple of minutes.
