Lights in the Sky

I was sleeping outside on my land in Steens MS on August 13. About 1000 or 1030I saw an intermittent bright light traveling across the sky directly above me atan extremely high rate of speed. It had a level trajectory and made no sound. It lit up three times before I lost sight of it from the tree line. I have noidea what the object was, but it didn't cause me concern. I went to sleep afterthat.
