{dr}Grey sphere captured on video.

I was at home in El Paso remotely checking in on cabin cameras up in the Sacramento Mountains of New Mexico. The location of cabin is approximately seven miles east of Cloudcroft, New Mexico off highway 82. 

It was cold at 36F, cloudy with low clouds (stratus) with no rain or snow falling. The time was 12:08 pm mountain time.

I had just switched on to this cam when I noticed a grey sphere moving right under or in cloud deck. It seemed somewhat translucent since it took on the color of grey like the clouds for the most part. It just caught my attention.

I immediately began recording as it was moving from a SE direction towards the south moving up and then down as it appeared to go behind a forested ridge and then back up and then downward before dissappearing behind a closer ridge. So the motions of this sphere like object were independent of cloud movement similar to like a roller coaster movement.

I enjoy the outdoors and the weather as a weather observer / storm spotter, in El Paso so I routinely go outside or check our cams up in the mountains when I want to see the conditions up there.

I dont know what is was or if its related to the cam itself. I remotely moved the cam to track it but its movement was different from that.
