Ufo sighting after a CE5 session

I had just done a CE5 session alone at noon. i went outside and there were alot of clouds but i still felt like i should take pictures. So i snapped a few pictures and when i went on to review them. I saw an egg shape ufo in the picture. clearly not part of cloud formation. Few minutes later all the sudden the cloud cleared for a few seconds and i saw a black triangle emerging from the cloud and disappearing just as fast. It was like pulling back the curtain and then another cloud came in and it vanished again.
