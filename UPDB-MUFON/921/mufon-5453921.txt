{cak} Bright Moving Light

It was a very cloudy morning. I could only see one star. I noticed one cloud formation that looked lit up and noticed what looked like the top of a full moon peeking out over the top of the cloud. Then it disappeared and next it was a pinprick of light and was moving. Then it disappeared again and suddenly reappeared, about the size of a baseball, but as a half of a circle shape. It continued to travel and completely lit up the cloud it traveled through/behind.

I started recording at 4:58 am and filmed for approximately 2 minutes.  During my observation it traveled from approximately 160° SE to approximately 185° SE. I kept zooming in and out to try to get some perspective as to distance. Filmed on iPhone 11 Pro, with and without zoom.
