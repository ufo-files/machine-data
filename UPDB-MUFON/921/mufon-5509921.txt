{nd} Orange Glowing Burst and small flashing objects in sky

I was outside in front of my home with my husband, just sitting in our driveway looking at the flashing/blinking lights in the sky as always. I was trying to tape the blinking objects in front of me in the sky, towards the right. When out of know where, on my left I saw A HUGE BRIGHT GLOWING BALL that just appeared and kind of descended in a downward position. After the glowing ball appeared I saw planes or helicopters or something in the sky. It was crazy..
