Flying objects/lights

I went to let my dogs outside around 10pm maybe a few minutes sooner. I looked up at the sky and noticed the stars but then realized some weren’t actually stars because they were a lot closer than they should be. I noticed they actually started moving. Then I noticed they were flashing red. It changed though because sometimes it’d be just red, then it’d be red/white, and some I believe even flashed green at times. You could only see the color flashes with your eye and not on camera. There were planes/helicopters that started to go by them to see what they were I guess. We live in a small area so we don’t get as many planes or helicopters like there were last night. The lights/objects would move up, down, side to side, and diagonally. It was unbelievable how they could be stationary and then just take off really fast. I’ve never seen something move that fast before.I grew up down around the Pittsburgh area and I would go with my mom to watch the planes take off and land at the airport by us. I’ve even been to shows where they fly jets in the sky and do tricks. Even then we never seen anything like this. I witnessed them for about two hours before I had to just go inside and my phone died. I went back out about an hour later and there were still at least two you could see off in the distance but they were a lot further away and harder to pick up on my camera. And when I was recording I kept trying to zoom in and out to try to get a closer and better look at them. It seemed like my camera didn’t want to focus at times though. You could definitely see more with your own eyes and not on camera. I do have videos of what I saw.
