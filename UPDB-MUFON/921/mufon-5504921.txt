{nd} Square object with orange and white lights

I was outside with friends and I saw a square object as I looked up. The object had one orange light on each corner that were very bright but not blinking. In-between each orange light was a white light. The white lights were blinking, but not as bright as the orange lights. The really strange thing was that one of the flat sides were pointing forward, not a corner.
