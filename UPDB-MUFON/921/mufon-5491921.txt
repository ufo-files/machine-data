{jpg} 3 lights moving in straight line, stopped to hover, started moving again, faded away.

I was on the second floor of my house, in my meditation room preparing to meditate. I was looking out my Southern facing window to see if I was going to catch any fireworks going off. I noticed 3 lights in a tight V formation, one red, blue, and green. At first I thought it was a plane but it was moving way to slow to be a plane. I next thought it might be a helicopter but if it were I should have been able to hear it, but all I heard was the occasional boom of a firework going off. I moved from the couch I was sitting on to the window to get a better look. I suddenly thought "if this is a UFO I want you to stop and hover", which it then did. At this point I started reaching back for my phone I'd left on the couch. As soon as I did the lights started moving forward again. I immediately thought "no, don't go. I'll stop reaching for my phone.", but it kept going slowly forward and started fading out. I felt as if it knew I wanted to get a picture or video of it and it didn't want me to.
