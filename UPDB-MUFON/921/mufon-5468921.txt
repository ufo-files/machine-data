Flying across the sky at a very quick pace, hovered, changed its direction slightly and then disappeared behind trees

I was looking out of my bedroom window at the time, watching the stars as it was a relatively cloudless night, when I noticed it out of the corner of my eye. At first, I thought it was a plane but it was moving much faster than any plane I had ever seen from that same location and it just almost felt different to a plane. Then, I thought it was a shooting star but then it froze and changed direction in an artificial type of way so I could tell it likely wasn't. It was similar in appearance to a star but slightly bigger and noticably brighter. I watched it until it disappeared behind the trees in the woods behind my back garden. I watched for a few more minutes to see if it would return but it did not.
