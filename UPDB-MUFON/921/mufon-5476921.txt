UFO

I driving on my way back from the hospital and went onto a highway towards home. It was about a fifteen minute drive but felt much longer that night. No vehicles in sight. Out of nowhere a bright yellow coloured light appeared in my rear view mirror nearly blinding me. I thought it was a vehicle behind me at first. I continued driving and slowed down a little, hoping it was pass me. That's when it started to speed up and that's when I realized it was above the car, maybe a 20 foot distance. I noticed it was a bright yellow orange colour and glowed. I could not make out the details as it was too bright. I sped up to get away from it but it continued at the same speed overhead. For fifteen minutes it remained this way. When I got closer to my destination, I went down a hill to get to my turn and slowed right down. The UFO then abruptely changed direction towards where I was headed to. Hovered above the property then the UFO glowing light went dim and disappeared into the darkness of the night.
