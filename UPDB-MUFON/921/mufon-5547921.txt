It was exactly like Bentwaters England. a close up with a slow U.F.O.

It was October of 1996. I was in the sixth grade and had been voted King of my class for Harvest Festival. Me and A guy who really is the meaning of Hillbilly. {Terrible Witness!!}. Were outside of my house as it had just turned dark. We had went to my mothers mustang to eat some cupcakes i could'nt sell at school.{Trying to raise money for my school.} When I noticed a red ball of light in the distance, I immediately thought it was a cellular tower, because they had just started setting those up out here. But my thoughts Quickly changed when the light started to come in our direction. I told my ex-friend. "Hey man look at this [pd/tg]." He got out of the car and then we both witnessed a small red ball of light that was not pulsing or anything just glowing. It was less than a football field away from us and very low to the ground. {Just above the powerlines.} It come toward us and then stopped and sat there for about 30 seconds. Then it took a left and slowly disappeared behind the trees. And I am VERY serious when I say I felt like I was being watched!! I was pretty sick to my stomach. We went inside my house quickly, and told my mother, And I've loved Aliens and U.F.O.'s ever since.
