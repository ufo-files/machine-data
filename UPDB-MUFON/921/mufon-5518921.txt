West to North reflective object in leipers fork, tn approximately 9:30 am on may 10th 2012

9:30 am 5/10/2012 I was driving North thru Liepers Fork, Tn. on my way to work, when a reflective object caught my eye just West of me over the Natchez Trace Parkway. The sun-glare showed that it was slightly banking possibly from an Eastward direction to North. I thought it ws a plane at first, but it was too low in the sky. Then I realized I couldnt see wings. I decided to try and speed up to get a better look and saw that it too was speeding up slowly then moved to a high rate of speed over the school. As a tall tree passed my view for half a second(I was driving 55 mph) the object was gone. There was no clouds in the sky and no obstructions. This is my first daytime sighting. From the time I saw the object to the time it vanished was approximately 15-20 seconds. I was perplexed at first, then just intrigued and wanting to see better detail, by the time the sighting was over I was in awe and excited. It definitely solidified the existence of these unknown objects for me.
