rectangular object with orange star-like light, no noise low in sky moved slowly, hovered,light faded without object moving. Happened twice.

Around 8:15 pm Sat. Feb. 4, 2012 rather low in the sky (below where most jets travel and above the treeline)in the north-eastern Oakland County sky an orange rectangular-shaped object slowly moved in a western direction and then stopped. It appeared to hover with the star-like orange light pulsating then died out without the object moving or falling from that position in the sky. However, it must have moved when object was unlit because, the object reappeared and repeated the cycle. Total experience lasted just a few minutes.
