Lights moving too slow, flashing strangely

Lights in the sky at first looked like a plane but began to flash strangely, too brightly and erratically. I noticed them and they seemed to respond to me noticing, slowing down and flashing really brightly. It could have been imagined, but they seemed to flash according to my thoughts, like responses to my internal questions. I wondered if anyone else noticed them, but couldnt tell. No other cars on the freeway seemed to slow down or respond to the sight. This was on Airport Rd, literally next to the airport, so that us probably why. But these lights, after slowing, flashing erratically, and then reversing direction, stopped and hovered without moving for at least 1 minute before I lost sight by driving out of view. They were very very low in the sky and I lost sight of them just by driving behind a shallow hill on the side of the road. Im positive this wasnt a plane or a helicopter; it produced no noise and acted strangely.
