Orb visible through camera phone only. Invisible to naked eye.Stationary in multiple shots.

Standing on the north side of King St west outside 498 noticed a bright waxing moon to the west of the CN Tower which was lit up with purple floodlights so decided to take a picture. Image revealed an orb to the east of tower at higher altitude. Orb appeared in multiple pictures in same position despite various camera positions and angles.

Approached a person having a cigarette and confirmed they were seeing the same thing as myself. Orb visible through Iphone on viewfinder and in images but completely invisible to naked eye.Recorded their contact information.

Very little clouds. Very bright waxing moon low over three story structures on south side of street. Moon lower than tower. Orb higher than tower.

Object appeared solid and spherical. More round than moon. Shadowed surface emphasized spherical shape. Brighter on lower right quadrant closest moon.

Clear photo enlarges well.

Seem to have lost several images in last 12 hours. Still have several.
