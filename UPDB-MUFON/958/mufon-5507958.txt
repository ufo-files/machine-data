{rjl} Hovering near airline headed towards LAX

I was driving north-bound on the 605 freeway. There is a flightpath followed by all incomming flights preparing to land at LAX. As I was driving I noticed a bright silver object hovering near an incomming passenger jet flying towards LAX, The object wasnt blocking that flight path, it was hovering a little higher than the incomming flights. Then I took a second to put my eyes back on the road where I was driving, then when I looked back at where the object was,it was gone. So I don't know what to think about it. 

When I first noticed it, I was thinking that it might possibly be another airline comming in for a landing, But a couple things seemed odd to me.

1. The shapes of other planes comming in where easily distinguishable, but this object looked like just a very bright light in the sky. There was plenty of sun out as it was only approx 4:15pm. It seemed smaller than other airlines and circular.

2. It wasnt moving at all. It seemed to just be hovering or frozen in place. Just beneath it was a large airline comming in towards LAX.

3. I looked away for just a second because I was driving and didn't want to cause a crash, then when I looked at it again, it was gone, and all I could see was that airline.
