{nd} Saw one then two red orbs hovering above field across from neighbors house late one night

Witnessed a red orb at tree top level one evening walking with a friend. We observed the object begin to move slowly away from us then the next moment we were standing there and there were two objects that moved slowly away from us until they disappeared to the south. As the objects moved they remained about 10-15 away from each other and moved together at same speed.
