Black, had a long structure, six other black structures attached with a tea bag shape attached to that

I was sitting in a car when suddenly I just see this black bizarre object hovering over the sky of Dhaka. The first thing I thought was: that has to be a UFO it doesn't look like any drone or cloud seeder at all. It looked like a line with three other lines coming off of the top and the same thing on the bottom, It looked like, on these six lines, that tea bags were hanging off. The bottom and top six were rotating and the object was going in a straight direction. Then it went into the clouds. I felt confused at this event and didn't know what it was.
