Glowing Orb

On my way home headed on Rt8 i observed in the southern sky a red-orange glowing orb. It was hovering in the sky an then i seen it moving. After i arrived home it was in the south sky still. From 1045 to 11 it moved to the east right across from my front door. For a couple hours i opened the door numerous times to check on it hovering in the sky with a wobble. I watched it eject a sparkling of light objects. 3 or 4 after 2 hours the orb was in the sky 1 minute then gone the next.
I felt like it had followed me home.
