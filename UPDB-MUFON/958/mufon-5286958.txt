2 Bright Lights; One large, smaller light below, to the side of larger light

I opened my window at noticed a very bright, LED bright orb in the sky that was brighter than any stars. There was one large orb with a secondary orb, smaller and at the bottom area of the larger one.
The most bizarre activity was all the coyotes that live in the valley were yipping. 
The orb lights disappeared shortly after my opening the window.  The disappearing reminded me of a camera shutter closing, consuming the light from the outside to the center. I could see some residual light that remained, highlighting the clouds for a few seconds afterward.
It never moved [when I saw it]. They were there then just disappeared, more like consumed by the darkness as if they were never there at all.
The oddest event was when the lights disappeared, all the coyotes stopped yipping. All of them, exactly at the same time as if a switch had been activated. The lights were gone, the coyotes became silent.
