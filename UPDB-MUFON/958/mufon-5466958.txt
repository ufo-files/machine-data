3 to 4 circled lights in the sky, in line, descending, disappearing

It was 5 AM and I was on my lunch break at work. My work partner and I were sitting at the lunch table outside enjoying the weather and suddenly I looked at the moon, and notice that to the left of the moon was 3 bright lights in a straight line. After about five minutes has passed, the lights started to Flicker and Dissent backwards.. gone !!
