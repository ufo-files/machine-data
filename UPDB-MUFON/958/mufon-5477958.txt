Observed like strong headlights, but no object that shot 3 light beams which flew straight that turned upwards

I was standing in front of dining area talking to my wife on the phone. I noticed a light gliding across the horizon. At first it looked like a helicopter but I noticed that there were no body of aircraft, only the light. It shot 3 wide light beams that flew forward than upwards total length of perhaps several hundred feet. Then it continued on it's path and I lost the sight of it. I was stunned and excited at the same time.
