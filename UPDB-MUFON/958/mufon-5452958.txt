Strange lights and unknown objects in the sky

My girlfriend and I have been seeing these objects in the sky for the past month on a fairly regular basis, from the deck of our apartment. They generally show up between the hours of 8 pm and 12 am CST. On this night it started around 9 pm and we recorded video and took pics till approximately 10 oclock.The interesting thing is, as you'll see, on the nights they appear the street light outside our apartment flashes off and on and has a greenish tint. The orbs appear to the west and north east of our building and everytime we zoom in something happens to my camera, as if it won't allow the camera to focus.
