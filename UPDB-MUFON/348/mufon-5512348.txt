{nd} It was 2 fly-bys one after another about 500 ft in parallel paths

I came out to talk to my husband while he was picking up after a project in our back yard when I saw 2 bright lights in the distant sky to the east north east. I was sure that it wasnt the average aircraft as the lights were brighter than any commercial plane Ive seen so, I brought it to my husbands attention and ran into the house to get my phone for recording purposes. After grabbing my phone, I headed to the front yard so I could have the longest segment of footage I could get. As it drew closer I noted how quiet it was and its strange rhombus shape. I followed their flight paths until I could see them no more. The aircraft seemed to come from Camp Swift, which is on Texas Highway 95 between Bastrop and Elgin. When you watch the video, note how quiet the first object is. I keep my eyes to the sky when I look toward my back yard as Ive seen strange things up there before.
