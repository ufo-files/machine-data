Many lights suddenly appeared rapidly, moved a short distance then disappeared

I had gone stargazing with my younger sister about 40minutes outside the city of saskatoon, it was about 4 in the morning when the cloud cover had been lifted for about an hour when we initially saw a group of 3 lights move from the west side of the sky to the east, then the group of three disappeared, but near the place it had disappeared, 1 had appeared then moved a short distance before disappearing, then a group of 2 did the same thing, then 1 again then 3 again then the lights continued in nonsensical patterns of 1s 2s and 3s for about a minute and then it stopped we kept our eyes peeled on the sky for the next 2 hours hoping to see the space x satilites or something explainable but they didn't come around again after that. I don't know what they were and they didn't look like anything my little bit of research showed hopefully this helps someone or brings us closer to properly understanding the phenomena that occurs over earth
