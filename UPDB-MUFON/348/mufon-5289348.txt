Sighting of a Bright star on the Eastern horizon with colored lights revolving around its center, then two bright Orbs coming from the same direction

This Sighting was fall of 1961 or 1962 at night after I had attended a night class at the Nelson art institute, another student asked if I would take her home, At that time I had 1957 Ford convertible and had the top down, it was a nice evening we were on a 2 lane highway when I noticed a bright star on the Eastern horizon as I was observing the star I noticed what looked like colored lights revolving around the center of this very bright star, I asked my passenger if she could see the colored lights going around the star she said yes and I pulled the car of the road and on to the shoulder where I turned off the car and got out and went around the front of the car to lean on the passenger side front fender, The girl got out and we were watching the star when two bright lights came from the direction of the star but at a lower altitude about tree top level, the lights where coming towards us from the East , we were on the west side of a lake called lake Jacomo on this 2 lane HI way called 71 hi way bypass and today is called MO. 291 as these lights approached I could see that they were moving very slow and had an inner glow which reflected on a outer skin that reveled a spherical shape, there were two of these globes that moved together with space of 25 feet between them, theses Globes were about 30 feet to 50 feet in Diameter and went directly overhead, about 200 feet without any sound and vert very slow and keep going west and disappeared, after I took the girl home I went to go home and found that I had gone in the wrong direction and had gone east when I should have gone west, when I arrived at home I noticed the time and only had time to turn around and go to work I had lost about 4 or 5 Hours. I told lots of my coworkers about me experience the night before one of my coworkers that lived in that area said that he had had a similar experience 2 weeks before. There was a talk show host (Walt Bodine)? that had a local program and he was a UFO fan, about 3 years after my encounter I was listening to his show when a caller called in and reported the exact same thing that happened to him, he worked in construction on grain elevators around Wichita Kansas He worked on the night shift and on his break he saw the bright star with the colored lights revolving around the center and the two globs that passed over him without a sound when he was telling his story you would think that we were reading from the same script. I have many other experience’s that I think that are related from before and now.
