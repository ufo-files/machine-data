{rjl} UFO spotted flying over Keller, TX

I was swimming at the pool with my friend when I noticed a white bright pulsating orb flying in a straight path in the distance. It was coming in our direction so we quickly grabbed my cell and began to record. With the naked eye we could see a trail at times and it was spinning as it was flying and it had reddish orange and bluish white lights and it glowed. When I looked at the film and paused it, we noticed that it was morphing the entire flight. It changed into a square, a triangle, a diamond, a bean, hexagon,a circle and it had something black on it that also morphed along with it. I recorded until I lost it when it got closer to the sun. It made no sound.
