Taking pictures of passing military helicopters saw object in back ground when I zoomed in.

Buddy and I was sitting on back porch when we heard a lot of noise, I said those are military helicopters and we both got up to take a look to see what they were.....I took out my phone so that I could get a picture of them. I photographed what appeared to be 4 black hawks helicopters flying to the SW. My buddy said where can they be going in that direction. We noticed that all 4 had refueling lines on them. We did not notice anything out of the ordinary. About 10 to 15 minutes later I scanned through the photos just to get a better look at the helicopters and in one photo I noticed an object in the back ground when I zoomed in that I did not see when I was taking the pictures. The object is not a bird or a plane and seems to have a field around it. I am not sure what it is but it is an interesting photo. My buddy also noted he did not see any other object at the time.
