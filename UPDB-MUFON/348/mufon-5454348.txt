{rjl} A hexagonish shape approximately 300 yds around spotted with all points of llight moving in a different direction than other stars.

Oneonta Al 35121
Stargazing 
Movement was the first thing that was noticed.
At first we had thought it was the stars moving as the world was spinning on further speculation we noticed the "stars" were staying as others were moving in a normal pattern going from the left to the right and occasionally going up to right.
At first we were just looking at one star moving and then we noticed the shape. We had thought we were crazy but when we had an officer come out we believe he thought we were crazy but when he looked for himself we noticed that he had changed like he seen something so he believed us.
We had lost sight when we had come back into the house after about 3 to 3 and 1/2 hours after seeing this object.
