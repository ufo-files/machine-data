There is an anomaly in this night-time photo. It's an hazy, irregular line in the sky.

12/25/21 -- 10:25:16 pm -- nearly overhead at 26.9762° N, 82.0906° W

In Florida, I took a photo of Orion. When I looked at the photo, I noticed the anomaly, a hazy line on the left side of the photo. I saw nothing except the photo. 

Orion was nearly overhead; the middle star of the belt was about ten degrees from the zenith toward the SSE. The three stars of the belt point 110 degrees East moving toward the bottom of the picture.

I submit two photos. "12-25-21.jpg" is the original photo. "12-25-21 close.jpg" is a blow-up of a section of that photo with the exposure increased and the contrast enhanced. This is the brightest part of the trail. Since the shutter speed was 1/4 second, it implies that the object -- if it is an object -- is blinking hundreds of times per second.
