Large white rectangle shape hovering above power lines

Driving west on Ranch Rd 2222 observed a massive opaque white rectangle (elongated cube) hovering above powerlines that approximately run between Jester Point and Spicewood Bend on the west side of Capital of Texas Highway. The object was observed for approximately 1minute as views from RR 2222 permitted. The observer was driving and the road has very few safe places to pull over. The object dissapeared by the time the observer could pull out their phone camera.

The object appeared to be approximately 1.5x as long as the distance between powerline towers and about as tall as the powerline towers. It hovered above the powerlines by a distance approximately equal to the height of the powerline towers. It did not move, change color or shape.
