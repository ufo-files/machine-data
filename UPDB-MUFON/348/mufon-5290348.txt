Glowing orange red orb drips object in sky

I know people are going to think fireworks because it was New Years Eve but this was no firework. About 11 30 am 3 of us sitting outside when I spot a glowing orange red object in the northern sky. It's moving east to west. It looks circular at first but then appears more cylinder. It's pulsated a bit but it almost seems it's pulsated more from the front side (in the direction it was moving). There are some clouds but oject was clearly seen underneath clouds. Objects seems to slow a bit and seems to hover as it gets closer to due north. Moving way to slow for aircraft. Then starts to fade.. At this time I'm not sure if it's fading out or moving behind a cloud because the background is so dark. However it eventually can't be seen. As soon as it disappears we are discussing between ourselves what we just witnessed, 30 seconds later the orb lights back up as bright as it was and starts dripping stuff. Like dripping fire with a smaller orb inside. Then everything fades away and doesn't come back. I'm not sure if you can put link's here but if you look up the youtube video, "orange red ufo over mesa, arizona caught dropping orbs!!", That's the exact title of the video. Well that's the exact color the oject was and it did the same exact thing as this one.. except ours only dropped the one time.
