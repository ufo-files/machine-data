(GS) An aircraft that was virtually silent with strange lights.

I was standing out back of my house when an aircraft that had the appearance of an airplane flew over. 

I was unaware of the object until I heard a peculiar sound like wind blowing above me. When I looked to see what the sound was, the aircraft was directly over me and was flying very low. 

It had the appearance of an airplane except that the lights were also strange. It had two solid red lights down the center of the bottom of the plane that did not flash. It did not have red and green lights like a normal plane but had white lights instead.

It flew like a regular aircraft except it was so quiet that if it had not been flying so low and directly above me, I wouldnt have even noticed that it was flying by. 

The aircraft startled me due to its quiet nature. I had no idea that it was there then in an instance it was directly overhead and at a very low altitude.

Within seconds the aircraft was too far away to be heard. I was eventually unable to see it because it was too far away and the view was blocked by the tree line.
