{cak} Sighting in Myrtle Beach SC

While sitting on our oceanfront balcony at our hotel in Mytle Beach,, myself, my husband and son all witnessed three orange red objects in the sky. They came over the hotel to our right which was much lower than ours. Two first--we watched them until they just blinked off...or went behind the clouds..the third one came from same direction, seemed a little brighter and just kept going overhead until we couldnt see it anymore from our vantage point. They made no sound, all came from same direction and went in same direction. The most noticable thing to me was their color and brightness, about the color of a lit cigarette at night. Strangest thing Ive ever seen!
