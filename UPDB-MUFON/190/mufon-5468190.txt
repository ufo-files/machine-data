{td} Observed 2 bright objects, one descended towards earth and vanished while the 2nd object began back & forth linear path

On May 31st 2018 at 12:48am, my girlfriend stepped outside to have a cigarette on the patio and noticed right away two very bright objects in the night sky, just above the treetops. They were approximately 30 feet apart. The first object then descended toward earth and vanished. After that, the 2nd object in the sky began to move east to west in a linear path, appearing behind the tree(s) and then back towards the street lamp. (daytime pictures are provided to show the distance and markers, as well as the original video taken by her iPad). The duration of the video is 9 minutes and 19 seconds. During this time, the object made several back and forth motions. The object did not appear to make a U-turn type motion, rather it changed direction rather quickly and in a constant motion. Towards the end of the sighting, the object started to move southwest and changed to a red/orange color. This is when the battery on her iPad had died and she continued to observe the object as it disappeared over the horizon. 

I did not witness the event but when I had woken for work shortly after the conclusion, I could see the excitement in her face and hear it in her voice. This event had caused her to become very excited, eager and left in amazement as she believes she had witnessed something very unique and perhaps something not of this earth.
