Asleep in bed frozen with fear

I was in Gouldhurst Kentstaying at a bed and breakfast, I was in bed around 11pm at night, I awoke to a bright light comming through the curtains, I heard a very strange noise nothing that I had heard before. I was frozen in my bed, not sure if it was fear or what but I could not move,
The whole thing only lasted for 2-3 minuets, however it is a memory that won't go away.
There was the a strange noise ubove that seemed to be hovering with a very bright light, just as soon as it started it was over, the craft moved at such a speed off into the distance, I still remember it today, it's something that won't go away has been with me all this time.
The woman I was with never saw or heard anything
