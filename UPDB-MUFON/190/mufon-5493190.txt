{nd} String of almost flickering red lights

In mid conversation, I noticed a string of very large looking, very bright, redish/orangish lights appear one after another totally in about 5, maybe 6. They appeared on after another, but quickly. From my left to right. Then they went out. Then what followed were random individual lights going on and then off in the area, but not the same spot. Then nothing. Just regular black night. I don't know how to describe it. It was fleeting. But bizarre.
