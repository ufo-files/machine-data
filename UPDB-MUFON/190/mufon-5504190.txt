{cak} Charlotte County FL, Rocket like object then jetted off north.

I was outside in the front yard walking my dog. Around 9:55pm. I noticed it because it looked like a rocket ship with a big flame going straight up. It looked like it was miles away. It seemed like it was ascending faster then our rockets, but I didn't think anything more until the big fire ball went out. It stopped ascending about a mile or two up. Then it started flying North towards me very fast. In seconds it covered hundreds of miles flying over my house. When it was above me out was a round object glowing a dark orange color. I was waiting for the sound to catch up to it and was expecting a loud boom, but it never made a sound. I was confused and tried to make sense of it, but I've never seen anything or heard of anything move that fast. I'm still scratching my head in amazement.
