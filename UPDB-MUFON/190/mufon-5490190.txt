{cak} extremely huge saucer shape w/lighting around center dark gray just hovering

just turned 12yrs, 10/12/1950. Nov.1950, I was in my back yard when I noticed a strange looking craft that I never saw before.I always liked building model planes but never had seen any thing of this nature before. I was intrigued by its size which spanned W.Liberty ave, approximately 5-6 lanes wide and beyond each side of the highway. I watched this object hovering in place for about 15-20 minutes, never making a sound of any type.When I had taken the trash out to the back yard & as I turned around to return to my house, that is when I noticed the craft hovering very close to the highway. After watching for that amount of time, I ran into the house to get my Dad to look at it also, but when we both got outside, it had disappeared.As years went on I realized what I had seen from other events I read about.I am now 75yrs old. At 12 years old,I did not read news papers, except for the comic section, so I am not sure if that incident made "THE PITTSBURGH PRESS" news paper. I certainly was amazed at the size, I knew this was not a blimp, I know/knew what they looked like!The time of day was dusk, just enough daylight to clearly make out the shape & coloring, still, to this day I am amazed as to what I saw. 

However,November, 1950, a major snow storm hit our area & shut down a big part of Pittsburgh with over 3 feet of snow. The evening I saw the craft, it was fairely warm, as I was not wearing a jacket.I moved from the area to a city about 35mi north called New kensington, where I also witnessed what was believed to be UFO's,several, do not remember how many, witnessed by thousands that day, in the 1970's doing what seemed to an aeriel show just shooting up-down in different directions at break neck speed. They looked more like white orbs, that late afternoon. The newspaper that ran the story was "THE DAILY DISPATCH" or "THE VALLEY NEWS". The paper had changed its name from the former to the later but not sure when. I do not have more detail on the 1970's incident, as my family saw this happen while on the way to a friends home to visit & as we told the story to our friends,they mentioned numerous airplanes flying in that direction.
