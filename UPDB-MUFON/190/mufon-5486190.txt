{dr}Blue light orb appeared suddenly, moving downward, blinked and disappeared. It left a blue tail that lasted only seconds.

My wife and I were travelling North on Highway 50 between Benson and McGee's Crossroads. It was very foggy that night, enough to obscure sight of clouds and most stars. The light/orb appeared streaking North/Northwest, blinked out and back, then it disappeared above the tree line ahead. My wife and I both asked excitedly "Did you see that?" at the same time. We tried to rationalize what it could be, saying that it was too slow to be a "shooting star" and too fast to be a plane/helicopter/drone at that distance. Also the fact that it was a bluish color light and tail that was left. The tail was only visible for a few seconds.
