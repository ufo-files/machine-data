Colorado UFO Disintegrated

At around 8pm me and my Mom saw a bright light in the sky that was moving slowly in a southwestern direction for a few seconds before I started recording a video. As soon as I started recording the video, the object disappeared in almost the blink of an eye, almost as if it knew I was recording but this could just be coincidence. I didn’t understand where the object went so I looked back at the footage and it shows that the object just disintegrated without moving, although the footage slows an apparition moving to the left which is the opposite direction to how the UFO was moving initially. Other things I noticed in the footage when I slowed it, is that it looks like the object is spinning or the light around the object is spinning around the object. I have no explanation for how the object disappeared from my camera and from the naked eye. I have included the original video that I zoomed and cropped, the video I slowed, and the video I slowed to see frame by frame with a bumped contrast.
