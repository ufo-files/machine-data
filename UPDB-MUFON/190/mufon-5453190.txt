Lights in formation moved through night sky

Sitting outside with family, one of our group called attention to lights moving across the night sky. We turned and I observed a set of lights in triangle formation with one additional light ahead of the apex, all moving silently across the sky. The lights lights were moving steadily from NNW to SSE and all appeared steady in brightness and similar in strength to weak starlight. There was no sound associated with the lights and no apparent trail behind. Soon after observation the lights disappeared one-by-one from leading edge to rear. I asked for the time and it was said to be 9:17 pm. One of our group tried to capture video on her phone but was unsuccessful due to the brevity of the sighting; however, some of our exclamations and comments at the time of observation were recorded contemporaneously.
