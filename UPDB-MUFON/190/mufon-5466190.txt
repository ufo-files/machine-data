{td} Star began to move then disappeared

Went to walk the dog before going to bed. Was on the phone with my girlfriend at the time. Sat down on the back deck to look at the stars while talking and waiting on dog to finish. Looked up at three stars in a triangular shape. The top point of the triangle then began to grow brighter and brighter until it was the brightest star in the sky briefly, not blinding bright like the sun, but certainly enough to stand out. It then began to move down as if between the other two stars forming the base of the triangle then simply disappeared. When it was gone, their was no point to finish the triangle of stars, so whatever it was that appeared to be a star certainly was not. I told my gjirlfrjend about this while we were still on the phone. When looking up at the night sky, this would have taken place between Cassiopeia and Andromeda as viewed from my house.
