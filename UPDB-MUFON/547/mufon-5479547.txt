{rjl} Was observed once before as a path of light onlly. Observed with infrared security camera. Not visible to naked eye. Glowing orb with flashing lights within. Basketball size with path of light above and below.

I noticed this phenomenon as I was about to go to bed at my house. I turned on my security camera monitor and noticed a bright orb with light path above and below the orb, lighting up a similar sized circle on my patio. This was the second time I had seen the light, but the first time I saw the orb. The orb was basketball sized and was glowing and pulsating with flashing brighter lights within that changed location as the orb pulsated. I went outside and looked all around. It was a very dark night. I live in the country with no lights within a mile. There was no noise and I could not see any light from the object, not even the light that was showing on my patio. The object and light was only showing up on my security camera equipped with infrared illuminators. I got out my night vision goggles to see if I could see it that way, but could not. I then took a picture of my security monitor with the object. Later I took another with video. I checked earlier video from the prior midnight and saw the orb hovering in front of my picture window. That is the video I posted on FB. I was in awe and curious for an explanation. I have not viewed more of my security footage to see how long the object stayed where it was.
