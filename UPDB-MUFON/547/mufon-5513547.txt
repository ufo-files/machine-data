{dr}Looked like a nova.

I and my g/f were walking up Aloma, towards the west (and the setting sun), when we observed a BRIGHT (not just bright, were talking "what the hell was that", brighter than the sun bright) object a few relative feet from the sun (as it was setting), that got intensely bright, faded to a pinpoint, went out, and came back a few more times before just plain going out completely. When it was fading, it looked like Venus in brightness and size. when it first lit up tho, it was almost 1/10th the size of the sun. No sound, contrails, sonic boom, etc. I believe it was something blowing up in space, or a nova (or even supernova- cant find anything online about one, though.) It lasted for a good 5 minutes.
