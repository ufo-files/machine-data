sighting ny 1991

in march of 1991 in Rochester NY, during a major ice storm that crippled the city and left huge areas out of power. My mother, my brother and i where driving to our childhood home, returning from my aunts home who had power. Driving north on the Lowden Point road, edge of Lake Ontario, Three bright lights flashed over the lake, glowing, changing color. Darting in a pattern over the lake to the north east, then in a circled flashed all accelerated at extremely fast to the east, then instantly gone.
 Growing up on Lake Ontario I knew of two other people to have seen lights seem to land in the lake in the 90s. One account was of a large orb flying under water in day light up next to a moving boat in Lake Ontario, the shape was like a dark shadow form the size of a small whale. it kept speed with the boat for a few seconds then vanished.
