On the Scarborough ME beach taking pictures with my family there was no moon, but when I looked at the digital picture there was a fairly large sphere in the sky above 2 family members.

When on the Scarborough beach at dusk on 11-20-2011 with my family I was taking digital pictures. There was some cloud cover and Jupiter was becoming quite visable to the left of us. I took a picture of my husband and son-in-law aiming the camera toward the right of the ocean. It was getting dark and we left the beach. When I arrived home about 15 minutes later I checked my pictures and saw a whitish, sort of glowing sphere in the picture over the ocean and the two family members. I had not seen the moon and looked at moon phases on the Internet learning that the moon would only be at 35% on the 20th and would not rise until much later. I have read many reports that cameras can "see" objects that the human eye cant perceive. (This was a third encounter with an unknown object for me; the other two being in Tecumseh, Michigan and Flint, Michigan.) There were seven family members on the beach to verify there was no moon. My husband and I have the picture. I don't have the capability to send it in this report, but I have dated the picture on my camera. I will be happy the share it with MUFON.
