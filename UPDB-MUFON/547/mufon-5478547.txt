I got to Oklahoma the evening right after the Moore tornado of Monday, May 20, 2013 for my first job in the wind industry. I showed up for work (as a subcontractor) at the wind farm in Minco, Grady County. Weather wasn't good and no work could be done as it was expected to get worst. I didn't see anything unusual other than the cloud formations and we could hear some thunder in the distance. Recently, while reviewing the image, noticed what I first thought was a helicopter but upon closer inspection couldn't make out either a main rotor or tail rotor. Showing the image to a buddy of mine, noticed a second and third objects in the image. It's unfortunate that back then my cellphone camera was only 5 Mp. I would like Mufon to review the image and comment on what you may find.
