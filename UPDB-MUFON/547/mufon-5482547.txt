{nd} Two Rod shaped objects moving opposite directions captured with my Trail Camera.

My trail camera was set to record video on the night of 2 July 2016. I usually have it set to still shots to capture deer eating my plants however I decided last night to try it in video mode. At 10:30pm on 2 July 2016 per the time stamp on the video it captured a video of two objects. Both were rod shaped. One moved from from right to left the other was higher than that one and appeared to have five small glowing lights. It moved in an upward manner from left to right. Neither was very large as can be seen on the screenshot from the video and the video itself. The object that moved from right to left can be seen in the video coming in from the right, rising and then making an arc towards the ground.
