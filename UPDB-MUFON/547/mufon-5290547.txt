SIGHTING 1: Approximately between 12:00 pm and 12:10 a.m. The largest size white ball of all sightings. Approximately 15 seconds in total from Full we

We were back in the car at this point, facing North/NW in order to keep watching the comet.
At approximately the time listed above, Katie looked full West out the driver's side window and saw about halfway up between mountain horizon to looking fully up (from my viewpoint) the fairly large white ball, a steady light. It's size was approximately how big Saturn looked in size and brightness that night. It was heading due East. Looked up and was able to see it out the moonroof so we both got out of the car. It was now going more South East by direction and then we both watched it literally curve and change it's trajectory and head straight up higher. It was higher than where Jupiter and Saturn were. The area where we lost it became significantly higher (at least 2-3xs taller/higher than it had been) before disappearing by a clump of small stars that I believe were in the Swan constellation.
