Fast moving elliptical near Meridianville AL

11 pm on Memorial Day my sister and I both in our early 50's were sitting out on the deck enjoying the cool night breeze. We live on an elevated lot that allows panoramic views west north and east with views far north to the tennesse mountains. I saw an eliptical bright white light but small from west to east , as soon as I yelled "look" to my sister it had left my range of vision because trees blocked my view but within enough time for my sister to react to me and she saw it shoot straight up and disappear. The event from beginning to end was over in a matter of seconds. We can see for miles and miles and this thing crossed the horizon and lifted out a view in seconds.
