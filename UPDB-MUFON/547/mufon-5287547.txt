Was setting in parking lot of Lowes when I observed the object

I had just got into my truck in the parking lot of Lowes off Ina Road when I noticed a long cigar shaped, bright silver object, hovering several thousand feet up in the sky. It was silver like a mirror surface with no sign of wings or a tail structure. I watched it for about a minute then looked to find my cellphone. When I looked back at the object it had vanished and there was not a trace of anything in the sky. There were no other aircraft in the sky at that time.
