Two lights in the sky between them a line which disappeared

My two friends and I were sitting in the neighbrhood chatting. I was looking to the sky when I first noticed two lights (like the stars) moving in the same direction and keeping the same distance between each other (looks like an object moving). There was also a line between the two points (not as bright as them) that we could barely see. I told my friends to look at it. When looking to the moving object we were calm. After that we were shocked, I had tears on my eyes that I couldn't explain (I am a boy, 21years old, and not the sensitive kind). We lost sight of it when it was hidden by a building.
