I then thought to myself "I wish I could see a sign that this is. Truly something extraordinary" no sonnet had this thought crossed my mind when one after another the two nearest street lamps to me flickered off one after another. After the object eclipsed the top of the hotel behind me out of view the two lamps flickered back on in the opposite order than when they went out. I was left with a very strong feeling that I was being contacted and the street light annomoly was an artifact to prove the existence of what I was witnessing. Very strange indeed
