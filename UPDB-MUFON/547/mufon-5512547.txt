Black rectangular shape; moving slowly; not quite floating

I was out on my balcony, and I saw a strange dark object in the sky. It seemed to be two black balloons; but I have been thinking a lot about balloons lately. Something made me look up; I generally don't look into the sky during daylight hours when its sunny. As I watched it; it must have descended because I was able to see it more clearly. It wasn't just a plain rectangle. It had sort of points, maybe 3 per long side of the rectangle. I was reminded of a stealth bomber of a different shape. There was no noise from the aircraft. It was moving very very slowly; not floating on the wind, more following a direct path. Other than the slow descension, it kept a steady altitude and did not veer or roll in any way. There were possibly aeroplanes earlier, before this aircraft , as there were jet streams (near vertical) in the sky. I thought that it was really pretty cool to see. I came inside to first make a quick sketch, then to look it up online and found a lot of similar sightings. I lost sight of the craft when it went past the top of the row of buildings I live in. In all honesty; before I went out onto my balcony for my morning fag; I had a strong feeling that I should go to the back of my building rather than the balcony. It was most interesting.
