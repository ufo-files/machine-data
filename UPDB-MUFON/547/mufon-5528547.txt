Driving south on I-15 and saw triangle ufos 2 times in different lacations within 10 mins of one and other.

I left school @ 7:00p.m. and was getting off I-215 and on to the I-15 on ramp heading south and was passing the 7200 South off ramp and saw 3 large red circular lights fading from on to off in a tri-lateral pattern just above the freeways lights. They were about 50 ft above the top of lights, and as I passed one of the street lights I not only saw the three red circular lights I saw the triangle shape (black/rust color). This craft was stationary and not moving. Now that my senses were heightened and eyes focused and scanning the skies the rest of the way home I started noticing all the aerial craft in the night skies. Roughly ten-fifteen minutes later I was coming up to 14600 South and noticed another set of lights in a triangle formation over the point of the mountain. I thought this looked much bigger and the large lights were white on each corner of the craft and one small blinking red light in the center of those three. So I thought that it was just a commercial air liner, but as I got closer to getting over the point of the mountain I could see a faint black outline of a triangle shaped bottom to pyramid topped craft becoming more crisp and clear that was moving eerily slow over the freeway heading East. It was @ foot hill height (so I'm guessing about ft). Traveling at roughly 15 mph. I tried to pull over but I was in the fast lane so I couldn't pull over safely fast enough in rush hour traffic. It was huge. I would say about 50 yrds in length or more and 30 yrds in height at least.............I WAS IN AWE.
