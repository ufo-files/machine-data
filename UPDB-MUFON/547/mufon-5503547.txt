{nd} Snowmobile riding with cousins when we saw craft above trees.

I am guessing I was around 10 years old when this happened. My Cousins and I decided to get the snowmobile out and ride between their property and ours which was next door to each other. There was 3 of us on 1 snowmobile I was driving and we would follow the property line on the North side of our property driving west. And then make a big loop and head back towards our house then go south to their house. And turn around and back and forth we would ride. On one of these trips as we were doing a turn in the field we saw a craft above the tree line. I remember saying something to my cousins and putting the snowmobile full throttle and being really scared. From that moment there is a blank until we were walking in my cousins house 2 or 3 hours later looking at the clock and it was eleven pm. I'm figuring we ate dinner at 6:00 because our parents went out to go to a Christmas party. So from 7:00 -7:30 pm is when we started riding. This is just one of the abductions and sightings I have had over the years. Would really like to be hypnotized so I would know the truth,I have so much missing time.
