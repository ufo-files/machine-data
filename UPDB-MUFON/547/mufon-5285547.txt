Large sedan sized UFO hovering directly above busy intersection

Was going on a beer run with roommate. We were completely sober at this point. We went less than .3 miles from our apartment, and on the way back, we both saw and heard something we couldn’t and still can’t identify (S College Rd & Randall Pkwy, 28403) hovering directly above us. It was only about 4 PM and it was very busy. The craft was only about 20-25 ft above us, and moving extremely slowly with no trail. We both heard whirring. It was black/silver/grey with lights on it. It was a spherical shape on the bottom, and had short wings and a curved top that slanted downwards towards the tip. It was about the size of a Toyota Corolla. We were so in shock that we called the police. I tried to follow it, and it must have seen me because as soon as I did, it went belly up towards us showing a flat, rectangular/spherical bottom, and made a sharp left turn and flew away over a Rooms To Go while ascending.
