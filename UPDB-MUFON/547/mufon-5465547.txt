Bright light hovered, moved and disappeared

I went outside in the garden for a cigarette with my sister. We noticed a bright light very high up in the sky. I took my phone out to check the flight app and there were no flights overhead. The bright light started to move slowly and so we followed it by moving into the garden. We saw it change shape almost triangular from oval. It was a clear night and then the light completely disappeared, there was no cloud. This left me feeling anxious and quite scared. It was also very high in the sky.
