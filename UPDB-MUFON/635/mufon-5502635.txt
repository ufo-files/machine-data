{cak} Observed 2 mirror like objects. few miles up in the sky. No clouds, blue skies. Both objects reflected like 2 mirrors. Objects hovered in 1 spot then started to move up, down, beside each other. Objects went straight up until out of sight.2 objects

1 I had just started navy ship fitter school in San Diego and was sitting on the ground outside of our welding shop eating lunch. I took a drink of my soda and while tilting my head back noticed the objects in the sky. I had no idea what the objects were because they were reflecting like 2 mirrors being aimed at us. The objects looked a few miles in the sky and stated to pulsate up and down beside each other. Both objects went straight up until out of sight. There were about 25 others guys sitting there with me who saw it. It was really the first time that I thought about UFO, but from that day on I believed in Ufos. The 2 objects just flew straight up until out of sight.
