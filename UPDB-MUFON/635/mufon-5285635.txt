Two white objects in sky

My daughter and I were driving down west on stone drive and I noticed a white object in the sky. ( MY daughter was driving) I thought it might have been a plane but usually, there's a tip that you can see but this one didn't have it. So I kept looking thinking it may have been a glare from looking up at the sky. As I was telling my daughter the object was moving south i noticed another white object but this one was round and not moving. That one puzzled me because the sky was mid to light blue with no clouds and there was a white object stationary. I kept looking and noticed as the first white object was moving closer to the second white object that the second one started moving towards the first one. The path was straight but diagonal down towards the first object, I thought might have ben a plane. It kept getting closer and closer and seemed like it just barely missed the first object by a few hundred feet. As I was watching all of this happening I kept yelling are you seeing this? Stop the car, we were at a red light..in traffic. My daughter tried to see it but the steering wheel was in the way and the traffic started moving again and I lost visual. The is the second time I've seen the white tic tact shaped objects in two years. (Never fails..when you need a good camera..you don't have one)
