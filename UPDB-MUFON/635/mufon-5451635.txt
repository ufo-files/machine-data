Rotating lights on an object hovering and moving slow

We were out on a walk through the neighborhood when We first observed the object in pure daylight no cloud cover coming from the south over the ocean we thought it was an airplane with the reflection of the sun as it approached slowly We noticed that it wasn’t reflection of sun but actual lights it got almost above us but at an angle. It was a lot closer at this point where we noticed the shape of the object , it was like three balls but triangle that had lights that were rotating around each section in a triangle form and they pulsates in and out , hints of other colors , such as green and reddish orange, but most dominantly white lights 
At this point a neighbor was walking by and asked if we we’re looking at a bird in the tree, we pointed out the object in the sky she noticed it , commented that it had lights and was rotating and then said , “well who knows with everything that has been going on” then walked off , clearly disturbed. We continued to watch the object at this oil town it started to ascend higher and my boyfriend took out his phone and tried to record a video but had difficulty focusing as it continued to move further and higher away from us. We watched it for a little bit longer then decided to walk fast back to the house to get binoculars for a closer look. As we walked home it continued to go further away and at one pint came back closer the went back further away. We saw it one last time as we approached the house, further away. We went inside to get the binoculars and went back outside but could no longer locate it. It was definitely in the key for a long amount of time as it took us at least 30 min to walk back to the house from the time of first sighting.
