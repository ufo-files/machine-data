{cak} Shooting-star-like light moving turns 90 degrees and shoots into space

I was walking out of my house looking at Orion's belt, the stars are bright, and to my left (south east), i see what i think at first is a shooting star(moving south-west). I keep my eyes on it and notice it fade. A second or so later i see the light on the same path illuminate, turn sharply and shoot off slightly tilted up in trajectory (towards the south east), dimming as it increased speed [away]. I was so impressed that i laughed out loud and jumped around a little in my drive-way. It was one of the coolest things i have witnessed, if not THE coolest.
