{dr}Flashing colored lights with no distinct shape

My husband and I were swimming in the pool late in the evening 11:30 and approximately 30 minutes later, my husband looked at the NE sky and noticed what appeared to be a planet and asked me if I noticed it - I told him that there was no planet in the NE this month. It seemed to sparkle and have alternating colors and we were unable to determine a distinct shape . It hovered in the sky for over 10 minutes a I got out of the pool to get my astronomy binoculars and gave them to my husband while he was in the pool, he was amazed that the colors of the lights DID change (white, red, blue, green) . My daughter came out and looked at the object, she saw the same thing, then I looked through the binoculars and witnessed the same alternating flashing lights on this hovering object. My husband then found his camcorder and was able to record this object. the object was still in the same spot at 2am. I wanted to report the object to 911 but, my husband told me knot to because we have a family member that works for the local municipal government.
