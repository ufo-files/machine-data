Appeared as a power line glistening in a street light or moonlight until I realized it was slowly moving and also much higher than a power line.

Slowly moved East at 3:54 am on Mother's Day. Thought it was a power line until I realized how high it was and that it wasn't stationary. Got a low quality video of it on my cellphone for about thirty seconds or so and attempted to pursue it. Was a clear starry morning and when I arrived at the Trans Canada Highway heading east it had disappeared. I found this odd considering there were absolutely no clouds and no fog (in the city). When I arrived at the lake to fish (about half an hour away) there was fog. When I first observed it it was almost gliding (it was travelling so slowly it was almost hovering). It was completely silent and in my opinion far too low to be an airplane. If I had to describe the shape I would say it was triangular (either an isosceles or scalene). Would have video but the 32 second clip I shot is 70MB which is too large to send on Yahoo! or Gmail.
