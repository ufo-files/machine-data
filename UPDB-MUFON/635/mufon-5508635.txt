{kt}Orange orb observed on two separate occasions by multiple people from the same vantage point in a single night

Dad came home and told us of an orange orb in the sky. We went to look and could not tell its distance or size. After a couple minutes it turned reddish and faded away. An hour or so later, we were outside again, admiring the night sky when it appeared again larger and orange. Within the space of a minute, the orb moved quickly in a Southerly direction, turned and traveled East, hooked around a star diagonally, and Northeast then turned reddish again, appeared to grow smaller and then disappear once again.
