Strange lights. Hovering.

The eyewitness who witnessed the UFO had gone up to his building terrace to smoke. It was at the time that he saw a strange object hovering above a mountain at 30 to 35 degrees from the axis of the mountain. Being a scientist himself, he thought to himself and tried to reason to himself that it was an insect, a satellite or a man-made drone. 

Having ruled out all unexplainable theories, he ran down to his room and took out his camera and tried his best to film the object. 

The video quality isn't that up to the mark. 

The incredible incident occurred above a mountain called the Lumsohpet Bneng (the Navel of the Sky (25.708661, 91.922749). The site is revered by the local populace following the traditional religion. The site is supposed to have a legend of a golden ladder which connected the Heavens with the Earth.
