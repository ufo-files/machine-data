Small object heading south over Casa Grande.

Spotted a small, white, highly reflective object moving south over Casa Grande South of Phoenix).
Object followed an arc bending South East.
Object darted left and right about its flight path moving hundreds of meters in fractions of a second.
2 passenger jets were also present. (One heading north towards Phoenix and the second heading east. They were moving a small fraction of the speed of the object and left contrails. The object itself left no contrails and traversed most of the sky in seconds.
