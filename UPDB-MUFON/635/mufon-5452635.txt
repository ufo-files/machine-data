3 of us saw a bright light moving quickly that appeared and disappeared 3 times and changed direction the last time..

3 of us were walking on a path by Tershishner creek west of Nordegg after dark, we all saw a light brighter than any star/planet/satellite moving very fast go out of view behind the trees. I saw another light that looked exactly the same 30 minutes later flash on briefly without a trajectory or moving very slow and turn off again. An hour later we saw it moving again and this time went from a lateral movement, then changed 90 degrees to moving down and the brightness varied as we kept an eye on it for a short time.
