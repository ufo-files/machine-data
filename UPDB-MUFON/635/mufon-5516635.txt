(gs) Observed orange lights.

The event happened on June 10, 2012 at 9:15 PM. I left work which is
llocated near 6th & University in Des moines, Iowa at 9PM and drove to
the post office at 2nd & University. As I turned into the driveway
of the post office, I noticed 3 to 5 orange round lights in the sky.
They were hovering in the sky and there was no sound. It was something of out the ordinary to see. There were no ohter cars in
the area at the time. I mailed some letters and turned north
to leave the post office. I still saw the lights to the north,
but this time they formed a triangle shape. I turned to the east and lost sight of them. The sighting was very strange. The lights
did not seem to be connected to anything solid and there was no
ssound. The lights were in a line and then appeared to be a triangle shape. The sighting made me feel uneasy.
