{dr}UFO flyover on my commute home

I was driving into my neighborhood on my way home from work and this flew silently over my car. Had a glowing 'X' shape in the bottom. I thought drone at first but it was bigger and had no blinking lights like a drone or a plane... and no sound. Smaller than a plane too. And no wings. Also thought Chinese Lantern because of the fire like glow but it was propelled, didn't float, and had a steady course west.
By the time I got my camera on and a clear view it was a distance away. But it was low flying.
