i went outside because i could not sleep, so i was looking at the stars to pass the time. i saw a peach-colored light far off and it was brighter than the stars so i thought it was Mars or something. i continued looking around and about a minute and a half to two minutes later, i looked at it again. it had gotten closer. in disbelief i stared until it was close enough to see 3 peach-ish lights, and a red light SLOWLY blinking (in the rear of the object). it got about 25 feet from the ground and slowly drifted over me, continuing diagonally to my right. i was frozen in place, goosebumps immediatly popped up on my arms in my shock/awe. there was no sound at all, and considering the distance above me, i expected something. as it went over my house i moved further out into my back yard, watching as it went over my house and continued diagonally to my right until i couldnt see it anymore. it was oval-shaped. almost like a mantaray without a tail. all i could think was, "no freaking way...i am NOT actually one of they few to witness this?! there is no way.." and then it was gone. (it never took off, just a slow drifting/hovering). i went out front about 20 minutes later hoping to see it coming back, i guess, but alas it was long gone. i couldnt even run inside to grab my ipod touch to take a picture or video. i was too afraid to miss anything, and honestly i was glued in place.
