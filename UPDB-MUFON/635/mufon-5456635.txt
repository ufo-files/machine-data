June, 1968 Pelham Ma. Me and my friend were 18. I drove up to his house at 2Am in the dead of night to drop him off. It was pitch black out with no stars in the sky. We sat in a 6 months old 67 Chevelle. We were facing east. In front of us sits is a mountain range. We were sitting in the car having a discussion about life.Out of nowhere, we observed through the windshield, a huge glowing white object over the crest of the mountain range (Quabbin Reservoir). It appeared out of nowhere (as if it was behind a "wall") and then vanished back into the night sky while continuously moving in a straight line from left to right. I asked my friend if he had just seen what I saw and he said yes. We were so shocked we didn't know what to do. 2 minutes later the horn in the car sounded. It was as if someone laid on the horn. I got out and opened the hood of the car and had to pull the wires from the horn to shut it off. We ran into his house, threw the blanket over us, shaking. I stayed over because there was no way i was getting back into that car and driving home. In the morning i plugged the wires back in to the horn and i never had a problem with the horn ever again.
