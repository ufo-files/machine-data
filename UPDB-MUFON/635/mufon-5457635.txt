Strange object spotted in the sky above Dempseytown area in Venango County, PA. It was a beautiful and warm evening on 10/8/19 without a cloud in the sky. I was helping my father-in-law with a building project at his home a few miles outside of our small town, and I saw a cigar shaped object sailing through the sky. I took a quick video with my iPhone and tried to zoom when I filmed it. I didn’t watch it or think of the video until later that day but I found when zoomed in, the shape of the object changed at the ~6 second mark. It changes direction and then turns back to course. Perhaps it’s a piece of airplane or satellite that was gliding high in the sky, but I felt it should be looked at.
