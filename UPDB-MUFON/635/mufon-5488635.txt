Vienna UFO sighting August, 2nd 2013

I looked to the sky when my witness said "Look up there, that looks like an UFO" and saw above the buildings (I am bad at guessing height so I cant tell how close it was exactly) maybe a few hundred meters far from us floating very slow to the left side (not sure if it was to the east or whatever direction, but from my angle it was to the west). Even a 13 year old boy was with us during a walk in Vienna who would see the object and immediately would say "thats a UFO, thats a UFO". When it disappeared behind the buildings we ran to still see it which we then did. We saw an object in a ball shaped orange and yellow and red kind of very bright light floating very slowly. I would say we watched this object for ca. 5 Minutes until we couldnt see it anymore due to the distance. It was definitely nothing like a ballon or plane as it didnt have any signal lights flashing as well as it didnt make any sounds and the height of floating was far to low for being a plane. I have good knowledge as I have been working as a Ramp Agent at Viennas Airport for more than 8 years and know what planes look like and how they fly and move in the air. We all had the same "wow effect" which came from our very inner human feelings. We werent scared but we all knew that what we saw was never something mankind. This definitely was a UFO.
Sadly to say we did not take any pictures as we didnt even think of taking our cell phones out to make a video or picture because we were so excited.
