(GS) Two red orbs flying side by side, traveling soutbound along the coast of long beach. Followed by two others one behind the other. Lead orbs faded to black, third orb accelerated quickly , slowed down and turned then faded to black. Final orb fades .

While going on an evening run with my daughter we stargazed as usual and I noticed two red craft flying in very close proximity to each other. Being a former special forces operator in the Marine Corp Ive seen many aircraft and these two caught my attention because they appeared to be flying in formation and there were no pulsating beacon lights. Just a constant red light on both, which is extremely unusual due to the Long Beach Airport being so close. We stopped running and began to walk as we watched these craft. The two craft in the front eventually faded to black before reaching the pier as they travelled southbound along the shoreline. The third craft behind them was the most peculiar as it abruptly accelerated for 1-2 seconds, banked hard west, slowed down and also faded to black. The fourth object faded to black soon after.
