{kt}3 white lights ascending above tree tops in straight line seconds apart..

I saw 3 bright twinkling lights ascending above the tree tops in a straight line...they ascended seconds apart.  When they were all in the sky they formed a triangle...constantly moving. I called my husband to see them, at this time the one to the left moved to form a straight line once more. He went to get a camera however they diappeared one at a time before we could get a picture. Were not sure if they went behind the clouds. There was thunder and lighting as this was going on. At first I thought it was fireworks but the lights went too high for fireworks This happened about 9:45pm on July 6, 2013.
