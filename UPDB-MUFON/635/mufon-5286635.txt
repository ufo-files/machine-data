Stationary colorful object in the sky

On June 22, 2021 I went outside to go pick up the kid. When I left I noticed a colorful rainbow like object in the sky South of me. I left and got the kid and when I returned it was still there. i went inside and told my wife about it and she came outside with me to look. When we first went out we saw the object South of us. We took a couple of still pictures and then we noticed that it disappeared and a couple seconds later reappeared below where it was about 8-12 inches from our point of view which was maybe 1 or 2 miles from us. The object stayed about 2 minutes then disappeared again and reappeared back where it was originally and we did not video tape that happening not thinking it could be a true UFO. While we were still watching it my wife started video taping it and we saw and recorded a green light separate from the object and move about 2 inches to the right from our point of view and shuddered then moved back in the middle where it had come from. We saw it do this 3 different times. When we came back in and watched the video we noticed on 2 of the recordings that there was a small white light moving around in the upper right side of the object, moving side to side and up and down towards and away from the object. We did not see that object at the time until we watched the recordings.
