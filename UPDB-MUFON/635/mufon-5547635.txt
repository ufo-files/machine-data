Star-Like UFO

I was going outside, I was on the deck, second story, with my binoculars, I was going to look at the moon before I took a shower. I looked outside, and didn't really see many stars, they were all small and faded, nor did I see the moon from the angle I was at, I was about to go around to the other side of the house when I saw a really bright object in the sky, it was a star like object, very bright, seemed far away, it may have been pulsating but it stayed pretty bright for a while. I saw an object like this before, it was moving and pulsating, this was reported to MUFON. When I looked at it through the binoculars, I thought I saw a shape inside of the light, I however; cannot be sure. I was able to take some low quality footage of this, the shaking is because I was having a hard time holding the camera still.
