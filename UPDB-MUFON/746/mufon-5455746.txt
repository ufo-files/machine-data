U.F.O. or Star Gate & 2 Blue Meteorites Reflections

February 27, 2019 very good night to search for stars and so much so that by doing long exposure tests, I managed to capture 2 meteorites ascending in parallel and each one turning on itself leaving two trails with a blue beam. I made several shots, taking the opportunity to choose a final shot. And my surprise was that it appeared between the 8 photos I made in particular in the 3rd, a blue object in the sky that I could appreciate slightly while doing the photograph. It was a brightness of great magnitude, appreciable to the naked eye with the eye while making the photographs with the remote shutter. Hard visible less than half a minute and simply stopped seeing. He did not make any movement, but he shined 20 times more than the Proción Star. In a sequence of 8 photos, with 20 seconds of difference between photography and photography, nothing else appeared in this one, in the others no trace of the object. Of course it is not like the flying objects used to see or the stars nowadays known, from my point of view when appearing a few seconds and then disappearing looking so bright and vibrant in shape that same energy resembles a Star Portal, since it left no trace of movement as meteorites, planes or UFOs leave in long exposure photography.
