{jpg} iss flies west to east over my house, this flew south to north

I was about to go in the house from sitting at my fire pit,, I often just stare at the stars. 4-29-2016 was cloudless in Rocky Mt, VA I glanced up and saw a bright object that appeared to be in orbit like the ISS appears,, however, the ISS flies from west to east over my house, this object flew from south to north directly over my head. I was able to view this for about 2 to 3 minutes. It never changed course or sped up, but disappeared from view as it got closer to the town lights. I came in the house immediately and checked the position off the ISS which to my surprise was over the ocean and not view-able from my location. The time was approximately 9:20 PM when I saw this object.
