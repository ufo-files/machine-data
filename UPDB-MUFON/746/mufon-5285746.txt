Boomerang lights shape shifted into a disc like craft.

I witnessed a boomerang/right angled craft in the sky. There were seven white lights, aligned in the boomerang shape. I had to have been the size of a football field. You could tell it was a solid object because you could see the stars get blocked out when the craft passed in front of them. Completely silent, not a single sound. I was with a friend and called him over to come look at this craft, taking ny eyes off of the UFO for a split second. When I looked up at the sky, I couldnt see the craft. So I decided to hangout in my car for about 30 minutes until, suddenly, the craft flew right over, heading north. It was about 200ft in the air. Same boomerang shape, absolutely massive and it isn't making a noise. The very interesting part about this whole experience is that the boomerang shape craft begins to spin in a counter clockwise rotation, then quickly starts to shape shift into a circular craft. I couldn't see a physical/defining shape to the craft. The seven bright lights converted from a boomerang shape to a circular shape, all within 3 seconds. It then instantly shot up vertically into the sky. Literally in a second. Completely silent. Never experienced anything like it in my life.
