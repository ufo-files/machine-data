{dr}UFO seen 7/4/2013

On the evening of July 4th 2013, as we watched fireworks from our windows we observed a row of lights over Ann Arbor as planes flew by and fireworks were on display. Lights were 9 in total and were in a curved vertical line, red and orange, stationary and not blinking. Gradually lights disappeared as if they were being turned off until one was left for a 1/2 minute then all were gone.
