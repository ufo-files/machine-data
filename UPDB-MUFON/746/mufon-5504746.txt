{rjl} 3 military helicopters surrounded a large object in the sky

My home sits on a mountain that is over a thousand feet about sea level. I look down into a valley where the Oconee Nuclear station sits. At 12:15 AM I heard a helicopter pass closely over my home. Around 12:30AM I heard another helicopter, but this time I got up and looked outside and saw three helicopters separating and moving into an attack formation. One stayed directly centered from my house while the other two flanked him on both sides. At this point they are spread out around the perimeter of the power plant. They hovered for a couple of minutes then the sky lit up in what appeared to be bright red triangles. This lasted 15 to 20 seconds then disappeared. The choppers quickly moved off.

I think the red triangles may have been a laser net projected from the helicopters. While these red lights were present I could see them reflect off a dark object that was in front of the helicopters. The object appeared to be at least half as large as the fenced in area of the nuclear station from my perspective. Although I could not see the entire object it appeared to be a huge deep disk shaped object.
