I was walking home from a friends house when I was half way when I couldnt help but look up, It was so big and about 500ft. or so high. It made no noise at all.
 I thought of running home to find a camera, but I didn't know if it would be gone! So all I
did was watch it. It moved slowly following the road I was on, Harvard.
 I knew it was a UFO not of this Earth, so I was intrigued.
I would say it had to be 1000ft. in diameter or so. As I said it was dark against the sky except for the four white lights on the bottom equally spaced on the edges, and one larger
light on what i assumed was the front. It continued in a straight line until it came to
Richmond Rd. intersection, and abruptly made a 90 degree turn to the South bit kept up
the same speed.
 I have to say, at that time there were nike missile silos right on the corner of Harvard and
Richmond roads. At first I could not believe what I seeing, but I sure was not imagining it.
I was really excited but disappointed I had no camera.
I watched the object continue South until I lost sight of it!
