Multiple UFOs ORBS sighted 1st Jan starting 3am

My apologies for the shaky camera work, I shot this on my canon eos 1100d in video mode around 5am 1/1/2014. 

My girlfriend and I were sitting on the balcony and noticed a strange red glowing orb in the northern sky around 3am, the orb shot out a orange flash of light which is what caught our attention, I grabbed my camera but it wasnt any good capturing the object in the night sky. Being curious we went out back with the camera to get a better view and noticed some more glowing orbs blue in colour zig zagging across the sky. We sat outside until 5am and saw about 13 of them over this period all moving in various directions across the sky. As the sun started to rise I finally could get some good video of these orbs that were hanging around, I took three videos in total. This one shows two orbs darting around, and the other shows a bell shaped object with four flashing colours. It was a lot clearer looking through the binoculars, I had a telescopic lens mounted on the 1100d which allowed me to get a good close up.

The best part is my girlfriend would give me crap about watching ufo hunters, (we have it on foxtel here in australia) and call me a ufo geek, now she has witnessed over a dozen in one go she is really freaked out.
