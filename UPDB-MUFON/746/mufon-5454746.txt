While driving west on I90, a triangular craft came from behind and kept ahead of our vehicle for about 12 minutes, then suddenly zoomed away at very high speed. Craft was triangular with three equal sides, but the corners were rounded. There were small white lights at each corner and a larger white light at the center. It was night and the body looked dark, was probably black in color.
