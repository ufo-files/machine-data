Standing outside my home to watch fireworks, 3 bright, sphere shaped lights flew forward towards our overhead, in a triangle formation. Then they got in a straight line and hovered for some moments before they disappeared. We could still very vaguely see them. It looked to me like they had an invisibility cloak. My phone couldn’t see them at this point, and my husband and I almost couldn’t either. It was a very faint outline of them. 
Then they disappeared altogether. 
Moments later they suddenly reappeared a bit lower and in a diff spot in the sky. 
The objects then started shooting fireworks out of them it seemed. 
Then they would disappear and reappear and shoot fireworks. They were def moving across the sky at this point. 
Eventually they moved away and disappeared.
