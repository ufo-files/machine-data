Two extremely bright white lights

My daughter (23 years old) and I saw two extremely bright lights just after nightfall (6:10 PM). The lights were side by side (almost like a stadium light would be), were white in color and did not have a halo or a beam. I know it wasn't a helicopter because there was no sound. It hovered above the tree tops on the hill to the North of our home for several seconds. It briefly appeared to move toward us and then stopped again. My daughter and I then drove toward it and turned around, but it was gone. We drove south which should have been perpendicular to the lights and which afforded us a very wide uninterrupted view of the night sky and it had simply vanished in a matter of seconds.
