Three orange red lights three time in s definite pattern

On the 14th of September 2020 on 102 North Ocean Blvd, North Myrtle Beach, SC facing the ocean (East) between 9pm and 11pm the latest I observed three orange red lights turn on one at a time from left to right with the first staying on then the second turned on then the third turned on and then all three turned off. This repeated afterwards a second and then a third time each time slightly left approximately 5 to 10 degrees north in the sky. This is over a 30 to 60 minute period approximately. On the 3rd appearance of the 3 lights in order I was frustrated at this point and happened to shine an extremely high power laser at the last pattern of the three light knowing full well this was not a plane as well as anything military having several high level security clearances. I went to sleep a little while afterwards but experienced something I have never felt before. I felt my self floating above my body being absolutely scarred. It was not like a dream but I really felt like I was was floating. At this point I fell a sleep, but will never forget this experience. I wish this to remain somewhat confidential as the high level nature of my previous work, but clearly this could not have been of human origin. Actual distance of lights is 1 to 3 miles out over the ocean, No possible human interaction would have generated the lights and pattern. Consistently tight spacing in sky so not flares 3 X 3 not accidental.
