Sitting in my hot tub, enjoying the night sky, I first saw the ISS on a pass from SW to NE. Beautiful site. Approx. 10 min later, I observed what appeared to be a "plain vanilla" satellite passing over our area. What caught my attention, was a bright white "pulse" every 10 or so seconds. A propulsion type burst perhaps? I am a 30 year veteran airline pilot and I always watch the sky. I've NEVER seen anything like this!
