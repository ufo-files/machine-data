{jpg} Small glowing object flew overhead at low elevation

At about 2:30 am, a smoke alarm in our home went off. We could not detect any smoke, nor could we shut off the alarm without taking out the batteries. We decided we should play it safe and try to take our children and leave the house for the night. Due to heavy snowfall, our car was parked at the bottom of a hill, approximately 1/4 mile away. I walked down the hill to try to bring the car up to the house, and about half way down a small (size of basketball) globe flew swiftly up the hill toward me, flew almost directly over me and then continued northward up the hill. Flying from south to north, it maintained a consistent tree-top altitude as it flew up the hill. The globe was a fiery red-orange color, and made no sound., I lost sight of it behind the trees as it continued up the mountainside.
