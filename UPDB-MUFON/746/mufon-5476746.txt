{dr}Very large silent unknown aircraft

I was on my way home on 1/11/17 at 1:20am driving on Harry S Truman Drive which is located in Largo, Md. I first spotted it about 700ft away and maybe 40ft high just above the tree lines. It was just hovering above a vastly wooded area. There were flashing lights what looked like on every corner of the object sometimes white green or red. While I was filming the video I noticed it started to decend a little more below the tree line as if it were trying to hide. I put my window down to get a better image and I noticed the sound that it made was not typical of any aircraft that I've ever known of, it was very quiet and had a machine type of sound no propellers or jet. I know it wasn't a helicopter. Another thing that blew me away after watching the video is I saw sporadic flashes of light hitting the wooded area very fast. After I made a u turn to try and get closer it was no longer visible, the lights were out and I could no longer hear the sound. Since a child I've always believed in UFO's but never thought that I would see one...what was it that I really was seeing.
