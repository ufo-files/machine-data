FIREBALL LOOKING OBJECT OBSERVED AT 4:42 PM ON DECEMBER 05, 2015 IN BROOMFIELD COLORADO WHILE DRIVING NORTH ON LOWELL BLVD FROM 120TH AVE. OBJECT APPEARED TO BE DESCENDING WITH TRAIL OF LIGHT THAT WAS WHITE/YELLOW/RED IN COLOR. AFTER OBSERVING THE OBJECT FOR SEVERAL MINUTES IT DID NOT APPEAR TO LOOSE ALTITUDE AT ALL AND LOOKED AS IF IT WAS HOVERING IN PLACE. AT APPROXIMATELY 4:48PM THE OBJECT APPEARED TO CHANGE DIRECTIONS AND THE TAIL OF LIGHT DISSIPATED. THE OBJECT NOW APPEARED TO LOOK LIKE A GLOWING BALL AT THIS TIME. THE OBJECT SLOWLY MOVED IN A NORTH WEST DIRECTION AND FINALLY OVER THE MOUNTAINS AT APPROXIMATELY 4:51PM. MY FIRST REACTION WHEN I INITIALLY NOTICED THE OBJECT WAS TO RECORD IT USING MY CELL PHONE. I ALSO CONTACTED MY MOTHER AND FATHER IN WESTMINSTER AT ABOUT 4:45 PM. THEY LIVE CLOSE TO THE AIRPORT IN WESTMINSTER AND I WANTED TO SEE IF THEY WOULD HAVE A BETTER VANTAGE POINT TO THE OBJECT. THEY WERE ABLE TO VISUALIZE IT AS WELL BUT STATED IT WAS NOT AN AIRPLANE. THEY WERE WITH ME ON THE PHONE AS THE OBJECT DISAPPEARED OVER THE MOUNTAINS
