{cak} 30 to 50 lights scattered across the sky heading west to east

Kid called to say go outside because stars are moving, went out and sen between 30 to 50 or more lights scattered across the center part of the sky traveling in an west to east direction. Some would stop and start moving again, others would grow brighter and dim. And also witnessed 3 appear in the same spot and started to head east. After one would get a certain distance away another would appear where that one started and start heading east then another appeared. That was the last one I seen and after that made it across the entire sky I couldn’t see anymore. Some traveled by themselves, so were paired up , also seen groups of 3, 4 and 5, the whole event lasted 10 to 15 minutes. I have never seen such a sight before and there wasn’t a sound coming from the sky. It was vey fasanating to witness
