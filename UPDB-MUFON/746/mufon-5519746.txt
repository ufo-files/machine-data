Red and Yellow Prisms in the Sky over Appalachia

My friend invited me to his brothers house for dinner. During dinner, he asked his brother if he still saw UFOs. I laughed, because I thought UFOs were stupid. His brother said not to laugh, because he saw them every night. I said Id stay and watch for them, kind of as a joke. But about 9 pm, after it was dark, I saw a red light appear on the horizon over a mountain far in the distance. I thought it may be a plane, but it barely moved. I thought perhaps a tower, but it moved far too much. Slowly, over about 15 minutes, 7 more appeared. At this time, his brother brought out a telescope. I looked at the dots through the telescope, and I saw red and yellow pulsing prism shapes. I was shocked and frightened. Ive never seen anything like that, and Ive believe in UFOs ever since. The scariest thing happened next. They formed a ring shape in the sky, and circled counter clockwise. Then, one by one, they faded from view. At one point, we saw one briefly chase an airplane. The most interesting thing about this is supposedly these lights can be seen every night [item moved/CMS/nd] from the mountain top.
