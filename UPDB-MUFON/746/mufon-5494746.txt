{cak} green orb hovering right above my basketball goal then shot off

Ran around the side of my house and seen a glowing green orb about the size of a large beach ball hovering right over top of my basketball goal for about 20 seconds. Then I shot off toward the sky and was gone in just the blink of an eye. You couldn't even follow it with your eye it was so fast. It for some reason put a feeling of adrenaline and dread in me when I saw it. I was in a full run and stopped dead in my tracks. It was the first time I saw a orb but it wasn't the last time. My sister saw it as well.
