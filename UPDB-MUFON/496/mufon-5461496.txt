2 long cigar shaped UFOs flying at extreme speed by plane

It was 9.30pm in South London and I was out in the garden filming planes with my 90x zoom Panasonic camcorder when I caught 2 cigar or rod shaped UFOs flying at great speed by a plane that was flying at approximately 1.500 M (4.500 feet) altitude. That same day I had already filmed a few other anomalies and orbs which appear a lot in the area which is on the flight path to Heathrow Airport. Upon visionnant the film later I spotted the 2 UFOs.
