{nd} spherical light that changed in color and intensity. flew in a wavy pattern before flying off.

I was at a campfire with 3 boys on Lovewell Pond in Fryeburg, ME. We were toasting marshmellows, when one of the boys said, "What is that in the sky flying all weird?" I didn't know what to make of it.  It was flying in an extreme wavy pattern, changing in color and brightness until it sped away at an impressive speed or faded away.
