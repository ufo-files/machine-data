{ck} Fourteen yellow objects with red aura observed for 2 to 3 minutes

1 I had watched the evening flight of bats, observing their aerial acrobatics as they feed on mosquitos, gnats, etc. Nightfall came, but the unseasonably cool breeze was so pleasant, I continued to site in my backyard, relaxing.

 At 8:40 p.m. I witnessed the first of a series of 14 luminous objects. Each was glowing yellow with a bright red field which was fan-like above it and diminished as the object moved away from me, going northward. There were several hundred yards between each with some variation. The linear flight pattern of all 14 objects required less than 3 minutes to move across and disappear in the northern sky. As each approached, it had the appearance of a large candle flame in the blackness of night.1
