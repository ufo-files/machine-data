{nd} Thought it was a satellite

I was outside around 9:15 pm talking on my cell with my old high school buddy. As he spoke I looked up and noticed a green faint light moving along a path in the sky. I remember my grandfather would tell me that they were satellites. So as we continued to talk this green "sattellite" came to a abrupt halt. And that's what stopped me from talking and I stared at this green thing alone in the cul de sac of my home. Then the size of the diameter increased to a big white circle. Like a flashlight had turned on, but no light was cast down. Just a big white circle of light. I froze! I got a little freaked out thinking is this my first UFO experience? Then the big circle of light reduced circumference and resumed the star like size it was before and it did this crazy zig zag "scribble", (like a laser light being moved really crazy, it was a laser though) then it just ZOOMED OFF! Like it punched the pedal to the metal!!! That what makes me believe it wasn't a drone. They way it shot off into space is exactly how it's displayed on UFO shows. And I NEVER go outside and talk on the phone. I had this feeling it was observing me for some strange reason then bolted.
