{cak} Orb moving and hovering

Noticed a small flash of light in the sky near 610 loop and I10 East freeway on the east side of Houston. I got out of my car and started recording. It seemed to hover and emit light while it was stationary but then the light would dissipate as it started to move and pick up speed. It did this numerous times and while the video is short I watched it move to WNW for about 10 min until it was out of view.
