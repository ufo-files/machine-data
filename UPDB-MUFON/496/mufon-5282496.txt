Sister and myself were waiting on the morning school bus just before sunrise,UFO came up the road shining a very bright light off to the side of the..

I remember seeing it right about where the trash cans were toward the upper part of the driveway. Stopped and hovered over us silently. It was circular, and had lights around the perimeter of it and one that shone down. It seemed to watch us for a minute and then disappeared extremely fast over the ridge. Like a blink fast. I'm not good at judging height distance, but would guess 150 to 200 ft above us. Witness by Matt & Mary Welch also Katherine Bryant a science teacher just a mile or so down the road.
