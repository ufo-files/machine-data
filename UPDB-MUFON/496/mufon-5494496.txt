{dr}many round lite objects in the sky, darting in different directions across the sky

I went out side for a smoke around 5am on April 26 2015, sat down on porch and noticed what a thought was a falling star shooting across the sky. Then another one same path, I said wow out loud. Then another, same path but ziged zagged across the same path. Then more and more started across the sky, no more than three lit at the same time. It seemed more like a game or something. I said to myself, I need a witness. I ran inside our apartment and woke my wife, she agreed at what I was seeing. Not as excited as I she went back to bed. This went on for nearly an hour, as the sky brightened up during sun rise I didn't notice them any longer. I had a ball witnessing this, and it was clear sky's during this event.
