Blinking starlike objects with firey flying object with directional change

Holy crap where do I begin???? I went back outside to see if I could see the blinking starlike objects and instead of one or two I found three this time. Two were about 45 degrees elevation the other one was about 30 degrees elevation in the West/ NorthWest. As I was looking at those I saw out of the corner of my eye something flash very brightly, when I looked straight up a saw what looked like debri falling into the atmosphere going from South to West. It had a long firey tail and I watched it fall closer to the Earth when it stopped suddenly at about 100-200 feet above land and turned North. As it went further away the light faded out and I lost sight. I swear Im not making this up. It was the weirdest thing Ive seen yet. I tried to get my camera phone to take a picture but it wouldnt unlock until the flying object had disappeared. Weirdest thing ever.... What is up with all this stuff happening out here?? Anyone figure it out yet or have theories????? I tried to get video and pictures of the blinking lights but Im not sure that my camera could pick it up.
