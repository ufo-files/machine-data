Bulb type light that seemed to be approaching the plane I was on before it disappears. Flashes across the screen

I was at the window seat on a plane going from Dublin to Fuerteventura when I noticed a bulb type light. I recorded it. After s few seconds I moved the camera to record the sun to show it was not the sun that I was recording. I went back and the light was gone. When watched closely, the light can be seen flashing across the screen. I had then and still have no idea what it was I was looking at. I was sitting beside a Grandmother with her approximately 8 - 10 year old grandson, who I showed the footage to and who were as dumbfounded as I was.I felt like I was filming something completely unique.
