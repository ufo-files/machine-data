Lights in sky above forest fire in Western Washington

I was watching news VIDEO from local TV station on wild fires in Western Washington last night (7/13/2021). The lights in the sky did NOT move in the video, but 3 lights blinked out at the end. I hit rewind and took still shots of the TV. The last two shots show when the 3 lights blinked out. One of the lights that blinked out gave a quick blue hue before blinking out. The video was about 30 seconds, so I do not know what happened next.
