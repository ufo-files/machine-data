Perfect metallic sphere could be mistaken for a balloon

As I was driving noticed a perfect sphere that seemed out of place flying over a dig site. I attempted to keep an eye on it until I could pull over to take video footage. I did not manage to capture footage at a low altitude as the craft ascended at speed towards a plane flying above. I did not entirely trust what I had witnessed so when I got the chance I started to film the area, I did not think I had captured any evidence until I played back my video footage and have a craft flying past so fast it almost looks like a flash of light until you slow the video down. This is the second time I have seen this craft.
