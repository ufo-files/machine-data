{jpg} triangular shaped formation of forward pointing chevron shaped objects appeared glowing through the clouds

I was getting some fresh air behind Fleetwoood's in Asheville, NC on a cold night. I was looking at the sky (I've had other UFO sightings so I keep my eyes up reflexively) when all of a sudden a triangular shaped formation of forward pointing chevron shaped objects appeared glowing through the clouds. There were five on the right side and four on the left - what would've been the third was missing from the formation. The had matching lines on them, like seams. It was odd that I could see these seams as the objects were as far away as moderately low flying planes. I saw them for about 5 seconds until they disappeared back into the clouds.
