I was driving north down shaver with a friend. My car radio reception went out. As we came to the intersection of edgebrook and fairmont, we saw cars stopped in the middle of the road and people out of their cars pointing up in the air. When I looked up I was shocked to see this massive ship to the northeast of the intersection it was over a shopping center off of almeda genoa and fairmont and was big enough to be over some of the neighborhood behind the shopping center. ..it had either 6 or 8 portals on the bottom of the craft and it had no lights that I could see we stood there for a few minutes ..I noticed a really small craft coming from the south-from the direction of Ellington air force base- I saw a small port open up- the ship went in, then clouds starting to form around the ship then it shot straight up at a pace that made it look like it disappeared. ..I saw the cloud dissipate and kind of follow the ship straight up..we all looked at each other in disbelief got in our cars and left ..I was very scared so scared I took my friend home went straight home and stayed in my room for a week...the object was slowly turning counterclockwise and made no noise just a swoosh as it took off...I swear that this happened..I don't like to talk about it because when I do people think Im lying the ship was very big I would guess 3000 feet in diameter I don't have a way to send you a picture but the ship looked kinda like the ships in V but the bottom looked like honeycomb cereal but no portal in the middle and they were deep portals...I hope others have come fourth and reported this to you as well...Im not sure of the date but the year was 91 or 92
