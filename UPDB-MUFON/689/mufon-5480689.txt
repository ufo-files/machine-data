I was playing basketball at the community park by my house. As I looked into the sky I saw one light appear out of no where above the church across the street. I am a firm believer in life of this planet so I payed close attention. A second object came out of the first one. The first object never moved the whole time. It stayed stationary. But the second object moved left and different speeds then started blue blinking. A third object appeared out of no where. The movement and way the lights varied was weird. I am not sure if there were more than 3 or if they just kept on moving and changing life light patterns because they would also disappear at moments. Eventually I went back to playing basketball and 10 mins later I look over and they were all gone. No noises or smells or nothing like that.
