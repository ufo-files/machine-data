{nd} observed multi objects

while driving coming to a three way stop, I observed what I thought were a group of planes in an odd formation. stopped my truck and watched for a few seconds, they were at about 45 or so degrees above horizon and at 1000 to 5000ft in altitude. After ruling out what I was not seeing, I started to film the objects with cell phone as most of them all moved together, then disappeared only to reappear in the sunlight, no sound and they did not float moving to the northwest than due west and slow. As they moved to the west I got back in my truck and headed half mile toward the interstate where they gained altitude slowly and disappeared. After reviewing my video I counted fourteen objects and you can only see the objects a few times due to the bright sunlight and not being able to see the phone screen. I'm most curious if more people saw this event, I should have tried to stop a passer by but I was so excited I failed to do so. signed by someone looking for answers.
