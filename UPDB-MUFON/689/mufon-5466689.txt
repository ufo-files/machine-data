{jpg} bright white hovering orb that appeared to be solid.

I was a passenger in a vehicle that was travelling in a northerly direction in the area of the southern NH-border/Nashua NH area when I observed a bright white, round, object in the sky. It was a sunny day with very few clouds in the sky, so the bright white object stood out against the blue sky. The object remained stationery the entire time I observed it (approx. 5 minutes). The hovering object did not appear to me to be a plane or a bird, so I took a photo of it with my iPhone 6S. After snapping the photo, I stopped observing the object.
