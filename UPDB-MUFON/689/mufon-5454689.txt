{cak} UFO sighting in Paramount, Ca

On 3/15/20 at approximately 8 PM PST, my neighbor and I were in front of my house when I noticed a very bright reddish/orange light in the sky east of me. It appeared to be about a mile east of me and at about 1000 feet in the air and below the clouds. Upon looking it appeared to be on fire and I pointed it out to my neighbor. At first we thought it maybe was a plane on firing and crashing. While watching, the fire appeared to stop. It seemed that when it was facing our direction, the object appeared on fire but when it spun the fire was no longer visible. The fire started again and the object started moving very strangely (up, down, side to side, diagonally, forward and backwards. We then realized that this was not normal and she tried to film it with her iPhone. We could see it clearly with our eyes but it would not show up on the camera. We had enough time to call her daughter who lives close by, but she could not see it from her house. This lasted about three minutes and then it left eastward extremely quickly. It went from being quarter sized in the sky to a pin prick in less than a second and disappeared.
