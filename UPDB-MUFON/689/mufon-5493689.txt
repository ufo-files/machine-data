a silver ball in the sky

I was walking my dog on the above evening there was very little cloud it was a crisp winters evening but with no frost i live close to the Derbyshire moors so there is little light pollution . As i walked along the road i saw what i thought to be a really bright star it was hard to differentiate how high it was because of lots of stars in the sky but i couldn't stop looking at it because of the brightness of it . As i got more or less beneath it a strange thing happened i had never seen anything like it a massive smoke ring appeared round it and the object shot straight up at great speed i didn't take my eyes of the object it looked like it was stationary but it was by now very small and when i looked away i could no longer see it amongst the star lit night then the smoke ring just dispersed i had no idea what it could have been but i know it was definitely not a plane or a helicopter or a balloon . I told my wife on my return about the encounter we are or were both very sceptical but then some 2 weeks later my wife accompanied me walking the dog about the same time of night 10 pm and we both saw a spherical looking object so bright come from behind a cloud and then go back again and till this day nobody will ever tell me i was wrong in what i saw then me and my wife saw
