{cak} Hovering ball

A woman told me about a UFO sighting she experienced in 2013, 2014 and 2015. She said she was having a cigarette on her porch in the late evening during the summer when a light showed up over the trees. She said it looked like a solar eclipse with a black center and a golden outline. She said she couldn't move until the ball left. It sat "staring" at her for a while before it "shot sideways" and was gone. This happened three separate times around the same time of the year. She said her son-in-law also saw it the last time.
