Light moving too fast to be man made

Around summer of 1982-83. Talking to GF on phone in mother’s 2nd floor BR, looking out open window. Two tall trees were behind house approx 150 yards and the light was beyond them but you could see the light moving and changing direction between them, using the trees as a reference. Do not recall wind, but windows were up on a clear night. Light was blinking/changing colors. Bigger and brighter than the typical star or planet seen in the sky. Changing colors, almost flashing/phasing. Called to my mother and brother to watch as the light was moving up/down and side to side by several feet….enough to know it was moving on its own. Suddenly, the light went from its peak height and dropped straight down with amazing speed….so fast that my mother screamed because she thought something was crashing. The light came to a very sudden and immediate stop, hovered for several seconds, and then slowly went up at a right angle towards the west. Interestingly, I recently had a realization that the view from the window was on a direct line with the Phipps Bend Nuclear Plant that began construction in the late 70s but was cancelled soon after Three Mile Island. There are still leftovers of the construction of the first cooling tower. Reviewing a map of the area confirms that my view was in a straight line to that area, approx 2.5 miles as the crow flies.
