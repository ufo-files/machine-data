{jpg} burger king parking lot bright beam and apparition sighting

I was sitting in my car after purchasing a meal from Burger King when I noticed a strange humming sound coming from behind a dumpster to my right. I remember looking at the clock and seeing that it was 1:23 a.m. I glanced again and within seconds I was overwhelmed with a bright white light. I remember hearing another strange noise, almost like a metallic heart beat. After what seemed like seconds passed by, the light disappeared and trailed off returning my vision to normal. I looked at the clock once again and it was 1:53 a.m., I could not understand what happened but I went back through the drive thru and ordered two more meals.
