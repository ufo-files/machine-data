Orange Cylinder Shape

Was on a balcony on 3rd floor of building and watching the sunset, when off in the distance I could make out what looked like a cloud moving the opposite direction that the wind was pushing the clouds. Above the horizon and the tree line, but I was 30 feet or more higher than the ground. It lasted about 7 minutes while the object moved slowly across the sky. to the northwest.
