{nd} Girlfriend [Item Moved/CMS/nd]. Traveling on 84 east. First observed extremely over bright star like object. Upon approach, a lit disc

I am not the primary witness. Approximately 8:45 on Monday December 11, my girlfriend was returning home from her nursing job. She was on rt 84 east bound in southbury. The event that follows took only approximately 30 seconds. Directly in front of her, at quite a distance, appeared a very bright star like object, at which point she grabbed her phone, as she said it was brighter than anything in the sky. As she continued to make attempts at pics, the object approached in much clearer view, moving over her as she moved under, at which point her phone had stopped working. Object was gone. As she continued on the highway toward home, she checked, and no picture existed. When she arrived home, they were there, on the phone.
