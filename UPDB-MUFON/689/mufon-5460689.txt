Line of lights

The sighting was a line of lights mostly in tiny pairs all following in a line in fixed position looking like window lights of an aircraft as you would see in the dark , some were a little dimmer than others and were travelling on a heading of approx 080 degrees over Guernsey and heading to pass a little south of Cherbourg , it is difficult to say how long the line was but I would estimate perhaps 1/2 mile , some distance behind the main formation were r 2 or3 stragglers which must have fallen behind , without my glasses on I first thought the line was a jet con trail but binoculars showed it to be a trail of lights.
