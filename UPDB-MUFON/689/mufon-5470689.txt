{jpg} black circle seeming to possibly be in distress

I was taking my dog for a walk and looked southwards and noticed an object headed roughly north. As it came level with me, it abruptly turned east heading north and east. It was flashing lights out of three large windows in the front and had a red light circling its edge. The flashes of light were not regular and it seemed to be having difficulty maintaining its altitude. It seemed to be in some kind of trouble. It passed out of sight over the visable horizon.
