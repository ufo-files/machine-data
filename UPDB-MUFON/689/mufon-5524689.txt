Taking video of strange cloud over river during storm

Was taking video from my living room 3rd floor window that faces east of strange cloud that was whiter and very low over river (the tree tops are only as tall as the building) and hoping to get lighting strikes, there was a storm at the time. When I reveiwed video I noticed this light appear forming into golden orb as its rising, dropping then disappearing in the lower left corner, reappearing again on left side and I also notice what seems to be 1 on left side of street light. I thought it was reflextion but noticed at one point it moves away from light a short distance. There seems to be alot of activity during cloudy and stormy days in this area so I have a few other pictures.
