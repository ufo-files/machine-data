It was 1968, around 10pm that lasted for about 5 min or less. A bright light from the space craft woke me. I got up and looked out the window. what I saw was a small craft dim in color there was a little entity looking at me not sure why, but I waved, and it waved back. The craft was oval shaped with what looked like a window for the top. After I waved and it waved back it took off through the breezeway and made a sudden turn to the right.
