{cak} lantern like objects flying by our building then out to sea

This sighting was not the first occurrence. The lantern-like orbs gave off a warm beautiful light that was calming. They would fly up close to us, then slowly fly off towards the ocean. They moved like birds - not like they were mechanically controlled. The video unfortunately does not capture the size, shape or what the light that was emanating from the inside of the object.
