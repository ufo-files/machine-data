I observed what I at first thought was Orion's Belt, however all three flew off at speed

On Christmas evening about seven thirty I was out walking my dog, it was cold and therefore frosty, the Stars were exceptionally bright. As I was looking at the stars, I looked at what looked like Orion's belt, but it was in the wrong place in the night sky. The thought had no sooner crossed my mind when all three " stars" started to move, one much faster than the other two, the front one turned sharply South and quickly moved off at speed, the others still travelling slower, then turned also and shot off very rapidly also to the south towards Edinburgh.
