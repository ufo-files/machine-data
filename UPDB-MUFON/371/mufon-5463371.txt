{cak} v formation of 5 discs hovering near ABM with nuclear warhead

Know this was a long time ago but... when exiting the woods above our homes at dusk in 1960, a group of about 6 of us, ages 10 - 14, were shocked to find a perfect V formation of 5 plain, dull silver discs hovering over us. No sound. No movement. After about 5 mins. they took off to the west without making sounds of any kind. The location where we were is on the edge of "first mountain" just west of NYC. Directly above us (about 1 mile) on the top of the mountain was a deeply buried nuclear ABM in a military outpost in the Watchung Mtns, which was part of NYC area defense system. (We often had disaster drills in school because of this.) We realized when talking about it, that it seemed that these discs were hovering above that nuclear warhead site. Have never forgotten it and can relate when your program discusses UFOs and nuclear sites.
