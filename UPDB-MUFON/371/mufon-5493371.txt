{jpg} I seen 2 sighting in the same week. On June 1st and on June 4th 2015. It was a red star like object hovering by the moon the first night it paulsated and dimly disappeared and last night it just hovered and then vanished.

I had 2 encounter sighting in the same week. On the night of June 1st, I was walking my niece home from my cousins. I walked out of the house and looked up at the full moon. I noticed that on the left side of the moon was this red star looking object just hovering there. I called for my cousin, but as soon as she came out it started swaying back and forth moving forward slowly. It then started to paulsate twice and dimmly disappear. On the night of June 4th, I was with my fiance and my best friend driving. I looked out the window to see what was a bright red star shaped object in the sky, once again. I had my friend pull over so she could see and again, it was gone. It seemed like it knew I was trying to show someone, but it just wanted to be seen by me.
