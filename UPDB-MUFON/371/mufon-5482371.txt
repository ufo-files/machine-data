{cak} Captured photo on iPhone of a singular triangle ufo, then a triangle formation of 3 ufos all within span of about 10 seconds.

July 8, 2016, while photographing the Atlantic Ocean, I inadvertently captured 4 daytime, no flash photos on my iPhone 6, one of a singular triangle ufo, then subsequent photo of a triangle formation of 3 ufos, and tow with traces of the ufos, all within span of about 10-12 seconds. There were a series of 4 photos taken from approx. the same location, and all have some indication of a ufo, though one was closer and clearer. Upon review of the photos taken on my iPhone, it was revealed that there was a capture of a black triangular shaped object right above the horizon line, which caused me to closer examine the other photos for consistency, but instead of the same image/distance, the other showed black "dots" in a triangle formation, but at a great distance EASTWARD from where the original triangle was spotted. None of the objects were visible or audible to my naked eye or ears when taking the photos, was a clear morning, few clouds, about 85 degrees Fahrenheit, 9:10 am eastern time, about a mile from NAS Mayport, looking east over the ocean. I have the untouched digital photos, as well as zoomed in screen shots I made in order to take a closer look. The first 4 attachments with original file names are untouched originals, and the following 3 (ufo1, ufo2, ufo3) are zoomed in to show detail.
