{tjd} Looked up in the sky and saw a glowing orb next to the moon

Walked out onto my balcony to have a cigarette and looked up in the sky and saw an obvious glowing circular orb way up in the sky on the left side of the moon which was in the west. At first I thought logically that it was a star until i watched it move from left to right above the clouds in front of the moon within a 5 minute period. Obviously there are no stars in front of the moon so i take out my phone and begin recording. What you see on the footage continues for 30 minutes. I walk inside to grab a drink and a cigarette and when i come out the orb is gone from the sky.
