Silver/grey sphere hovering in the sky then pulling back and shooting forward to disappear

I was driving to York, Pa in the morning when I say a silver/ grey sphere appear in the sky on 83S highway. The flying object was stationary for about 15 mins or so and then it moved back a good distance only to shoot forward in an unbelievable speed (for an aircraft) and disappear in the sky.
