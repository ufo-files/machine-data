{nd} bright light with a mist emitting, ascented then quick descent then disappears, mist remained a few seconds

Sitting in the back yard star gazing, saw a bright light, larger than any star, bright like a jet's headlights but only one and much too large not to hear any sound. It was suddenly above in the sky west ward, toward the pacific ocean. We watched the light, very bright, mist appeared to come from it, not like a jet with a stream in a line behind it but rather a round ball-shaped mist. There wasn't a cloud in the sky to explain such a thing. The light ascended, then descended slightly and suddenly went out leaving the mist visible for a few more seconds before it was no longer visible to my naked eye.
