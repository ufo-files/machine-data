(GS) The sound, a powerfull humm. Unlike anything Ive ever heard in the sky

I have to smoke outside! That okay because helps satisfy my addiction of looking at the sky. Anytime I hear an airplane or helicopter I watch it fly. Loved doing so since i can remember. This time I headed something that sounded alot different; looked up to where I thought the sound was coming from (nw), and sure enough there was 1 small light in the distance. First it was stationary; probably would have dismissed it if it hadnt started moving. The sound grew louder; not piercing but did seem to be moving with the light I was observing. Wasnt moving fast. Kinda like most helis Ive observed out here but did accel. It didn't fly directly over my head but passed at its closest at about a mile out. Dont really know how to describe the elevation but can say that it flew lower than most helicopters I see around here. I did try to pull out the iPhone to get a vid bot light was small and was shaking; damn thing freaked me out. I ran inside to tell the Mrs.; got mad for waking her up but couldnt get her to go out- ran back out and couldnt see the light in its direction I observed due to our tree lines but I still heard it- never forget that loud powerfull humm fading to the ne. Time recorded beginning at 5:05am fading out completely at around 5:35am.

Im hoping someone else hearing/seeing this reports it. Its not my first time experiencing this here but is my first report.
