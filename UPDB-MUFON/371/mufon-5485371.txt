{nd} photographed strange cloud formations and when I viewed photos I noticed an orb appeared in two of the photos

I did not see the orb when I took the pictures, I did not know it was there. So I could not observe any actions or sounds coming from the object. I could not tell if it was hovering or moving in any direction
