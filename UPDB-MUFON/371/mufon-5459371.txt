{tjd} Round White Glowing Orb

Small round white glowing orb moving at a very high rate of speed in a direct line from south to north/northeast. Clear cloudless afternoon approximately 1235pm in Tarrant County with nothing else but birds in the sky below. Appeared to be about 1/2-1 mile above the surface of the ground. Small and only remained in sight for 15-20 seconds but clearly not a plane as it was round with no fixed wings and was traveling faster than any commercial or military jet I have seen in the sky.
