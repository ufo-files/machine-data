{rjl} Red Circular Object in Northern Sky Heading West to East

I was walking my dog north on Fairview Avenue when we stopped at Lookout Park so she could urinate. I looked up in the sky and saw a circular object (about the size of a pea/aspirin at arms length) moving slowly in the sky from West to East. It was solid red in color and did not blink. As it neared the midway point of my view, another circular object of the same size, appeared above it (approximately three inches distance at arm's length), flashed white for a solid two seconds, then faded away. The red circular object continued on its Eastward path until it was out of sight.
