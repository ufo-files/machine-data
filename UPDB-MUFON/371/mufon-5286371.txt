Disappeared then reappeared

I observed what looked like a falling star, but was descending far too slow. It was moving slowly almost straight down before disappearing. It then reappeared to the left of where it disappeared, moved from left to right and streaked off before disappearing again. I would estimate it was several miles to the South. Altitude unknown.
