I was walking my dog at Gratwick Park in North Tonawanda and was admiring the sunset. I decided to take a picture on my Samsung 6 phone. I looked at the picture a few days later and I noticed an object. It's lower and to the right of the sun. This was over the Niagara River towards Grand Island, NY. It appears to be reflecting light from the sun and it gives off a haze through the clouds. There seems to be a pattern on it too. I can't figure it out so I thought I'd give someone who knows this stuff better a shot.
