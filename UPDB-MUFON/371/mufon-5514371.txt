(GS) Hovering, soundless object with pulsating white and red lights with no distinct flight pattern

Multiple low flying aircrafts were spotted with pulsating white and red lights. The aircrafts circled and appeared at times to be at house level or tree level, then very high in the atmosphere. It appeared to be at least 2, possibly 3 objects. At the lowest point, and closest, no noise could be heard from the aircraft. We followed the lights for a while until they disappeared completely.
