{cak} Several orange lights moving mainly east to west over downtown Seattle, not luminaria

Several, perhaps eight orange glowing objects moved over Seattle one after another over about fifteen minutes east to west. They were not luminaria, Chinese candle lantern balloons. They turned their lights on and off and performed maneuvers in the air and were moving too fast to be luminaria and luminaria cannot of course perform maneuvers. I called my friend to have him go out and take a look and he said one was doing loops in the sky. I didn't see that but I did see them move under control, changing direction and or altitude, hard to say. Meanwhile there was a pulsating white light directly south of downtown Seattle which was perfectly stationary and later moved east and disappeared to the east. My friend said they are probably drones and someone is having fun with us which is I guess the most likely explanation but I had never seen anything like this before and the white pulsating light south of Seattle was very eerie too. The pulsating light looked far further south than the orange glowing objects, maybe over Kent. Like I said the pulsating light disappeared eastward and the orange objects disappeared westward, just blinking on and off and then disappearing. It was very weird.
