Cigar shape, one large white light in center, two smaller white lights centerline, green strobe on tail. No wings or navigation lights

Initially appeared as orange light from Southern horizon. Gradually turned white as approached, when closer I could determine it’s cigar shape and lack of wings or navigation lights. As the object drew directly overhead I saw three “lights” on the underside, a large circular light in the center, almost as wide as the object and two smaller circular “lights” centerline, equidistant from each end. It also had a green strobe on the tail. My friend and I didn’t hear anything despite its low altitude. Judging size and distance are difficult, but it seemed the size of a 747 fuselage and maybe 3,000 to 4,000 ft above me. I kept this object in direct view from the horizon to 115 degrees overhead for 3-4 minutes. I would estimate it’s speed at between 200 to 300 mph.
