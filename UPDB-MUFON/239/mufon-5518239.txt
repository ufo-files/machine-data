Ufo would fly straight path, stop, make small circle, proceed on path. East to west.

I was sunbathing sitting about 45 degree angle and noticed a reflective object at what appeared to be an extreme elevation. The object traveled from south west to northeast. On its path, the object would stop,make a small circle path and then continue on the original path.I first thought the object was a satelite due to its apperant altitude. I was interested. The object disappared and about 2 minutes later 4 fighter jets flew from the south by NORAD northbound. Thats what made the whole thing seem strange.
