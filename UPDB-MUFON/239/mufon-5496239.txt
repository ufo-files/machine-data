Multiple fast moving objects recorded on camcoder.

Together with my mother we were tracing airplanes with flightradar24.com web site. At the same time I was recording them with using Sony HDR-CX305 camera. After couple of months I was going to erase videos from my hard drive. In one of videos I saw some strange fast moving objects. After that I started to analyse all the videos from that day. I recorded nearly 40 orbs/ufos at that time. Most of them were flying on the same direction, straight line, around 10x faster than plane.
