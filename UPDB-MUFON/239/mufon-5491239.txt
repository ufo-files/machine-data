Gentle glowing clouds moving around the sky faster than ever seen.

I was outside watching lightning hoping I would see a storm from the hotel balcony, when I decided to look into the opposite direction and I could see a soft puff of light moving around behind the clouds hovering above the sanctuary of truth in Pattaya, Thailand. I continued to watch and I noticed another 2 or 3 different sized puffs of light floating behind the clouds showing no particular pattern of movement yet they seemed to dissolve and re appear at different heights and directions. I called my husband to come and have a look and together we watched this amazing show of lights for another 25 minutes. At one point I got a little nervous and wanted to go inside as I felt one of the bigger lights got closer and larger in size. We didn't go back indoors instead we stood in amazement watching. After around 25 minutes they disappeared and to our shock so did the clouds they were dancing behind.
