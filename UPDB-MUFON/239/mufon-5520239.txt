The object appeared to originate from Mizar - the middle star on the handle of the Big Dipper, moving Westward to another star.

Sitting on the back deck watching the night sky. The object originated from Mizar, the second star in the handle of the Big Dipper. Initially appeared like a white-light pinhole that expanded to a flat line - like a dash. It moved from East to West across the night sky, expanding in length as it traveled then contracting to another pin-sized white dot - landing on a neighboring star. This was not a meteor. This behaved very differently.
