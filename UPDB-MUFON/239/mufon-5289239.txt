Triangular glowing green ship rapidly descends and instantly vanishes

Me and my husband had been on the way home a very late night at around 11:53 p.m. There was a slight green glow in the car and we saw a flying triangular object glowing green and descending rapidly. Initially I thought it must be a plane of some sort. At its speed and trajectory though, it seemed like it was going to crash. Fear gripped me that we were about to see something terrible happen in the fields nearby where the object seemed to be headed for. Then once it was only a few hundred feet up or so it just vanished in an instant. There was no light filling, fade out, fizzle, explosion, etc. No sign it was there a second before. Unfortunately the entire thing lasted less then a minute and I did not think to record as I did not assume it was unexplainable until it vanished.
