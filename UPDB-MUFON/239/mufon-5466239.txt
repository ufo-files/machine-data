{td} Observe 2 Chinook helicopter escorting 3 Orange orbs in tight formation heading west

At 11:05 pm ,I heard 2 helicopters flying over Eatonville and it sounded very low. But this sounded really loud and it shook the house. I ran outside and saw 2 Chinook helicopters in a staggered formation with orange orb flying side by side with the first helicopter. The 2nd helicopter was very close behind and it had the 2nd orb between the first and 2nd helicopter. The 3rd orb was flying next to the 2nd helicopter. They were all flying in a tight formation. As they flew over me I could see the helicopter navigation strobe light flash and that's when I saw the shape of the helicopter and I knew I was looking at a Chinook helicopter. I could see the blades rotating . The orbs looked reddish orange in color and was a solid color. They flew over me and I watched it heading west until they disappeared. It looked as if the helicopters were escorting the orbs.
