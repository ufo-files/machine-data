I went outside at about 3 am alone to see a meteor shower that was occur on 4/23/14. I was on my back porch and the sky was clear, but I couldn't find one shooting star after several minutes. Then I noticed an object high in the sky at the 1 o'clock position. It appeared triangular-shaped with green lights and a red light in the center. It was not big since it seemed far away. It looked strange and stationary, and next to it there was a brighter, white object that seems almost like a star, but it was too big and bright to be a star or planet. It fascinated me and keep saying OMG to myself because I knew I was witnessing a UFO. At one point the objects just disappeared for a few seconds like the lights were turned off. That portion of the sky was clear, so it wasn't clouds covering my view. And then I saw the objects re-appear in the same position. I went inside to get my camera, but it didn't have batteries, so I came back outside and watched some more. All together I probably observed the objects for 20 minutes or more. They were still in the sky when I finally quit watching.
