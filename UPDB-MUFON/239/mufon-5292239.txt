Hovering lights accelerated to west

My husband saw a unidentifiable, circular object hovering in the sky. The object was emitting a dim, orange colored light(similar to a candlelight) and two other white lights, one spinning around the edge of the object the other pointed down out of the ventral side. Both were also very dim. He called me in to take a look. This was strange, because he is highly skeptical of Ufo's. When I saw It I asked if It was a drone. It seemed to move slightly back and forth in a rocking motion. 
We watched it for roughly 10 minutes.
At first it just hovered over the tree line rocking back and forth. 
Then at about minute 8 it started moving slowly in and out of the tree tops in a SW direction. 
It passed directly behind a large tree. At this point we lost sight of it for a good 20-30 seconds. 
It then emerged, going at a much faster speed and going due West. It seemed to accelerate quite rapidly. To the pint where it was out of our visual range within about 10 seconds.
