{cak} Looked like a huge glowing red boomerang

My buddy and I were going to Watkins glen from Odessa around 10pm on June 20 2014 approximately. We saw a glowing red object moving above the tree line over the swamp so we pulled over n watched it for maybe 3 -5 mins. It moved over the swamp and across the road we were on and began to climb higher in the sky n in the blink of an eye there was a small flash n it was gone. I didn't know what to think I was shaking and nervous but at the same time excited.
