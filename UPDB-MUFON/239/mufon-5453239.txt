Very Bright Star-Like Object In NW Sky

Around 9:30 pm on April 24th I observed a very bright star-like object high in the sky to the NW of my house. The object was observed from my bedroom window. It was about 60 degrees above the horizon. It was stationary, but rotating. The object slowing followed an arc path and decended to tree top level around 11:00pm. It was still located to the NW, but further N than W. I estimated that it was more than 5 miles away from our house. After descending to tree level I lost sight of the object and could no longer observe it. My wife was with me at the time and also observed this object. We did not experience any odd after effects from this sighting.We have been seeing this object for several days now, and it is always in the same location demostrating the same behavior. No photos awere taken of the object
