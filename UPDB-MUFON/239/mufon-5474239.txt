DARK OBJECT APPEARS NEAR SHENZHEN

I SAW AN UFO DARK SHADOW - NOT MOVING APPEARS WHILE I WAS HEADING TO SHENZHEN AIRPORT FROM HONG KONG AT AROUND 515AM IN THE MORING. THE SKY WAS BLUE WITHOUT CLOUDS.
