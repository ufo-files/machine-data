(GS) glowing yellowish orb traveling low, west to east, over field behind house

Around 7:30pm tonight as I was taking my dog outside,spotted an orb glowing and hovering at low altitude (approx.200ft) over the horse field adjoining the backyard behind our house. The orb begin to move smoothly and slowly, gliding from west to east,maintaining a constant yellowish glow.Called for my husband to come outside and we watched it pass silently over the field about 100ft. in front of us, maintaining the same altitude as it headed off to the east and out of view behind the trees, as we live in a wooded/mountain area. Total viewing time was approx. 2 minutes.
