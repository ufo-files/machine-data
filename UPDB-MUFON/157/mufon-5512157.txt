(GS) Hovering object vanished from a cloudless sky.

Was driving east on Ann towards Jones and observed what at first appeared to be an odd shaped helicopter hovering slowly in varied directions. It was moving slowly, so didn't think much of it. Then I turned away for about 2 seconds and it was gone. No clouds in the sky to hide behind and it wouldve taken at least 5-6 seconds to fall out of view.
