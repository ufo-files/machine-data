Ufo sight in Brazil 2014 - daylight

Hi, first of all i would like to apologize for my english, i will translate in google...

on January 10, 2014 I was with my wife playing with my goddaughter on the playground in the building where I lived. while they played I sat on a bench and began to look at the sky. 

summer here in Brazil were approximately 7pm summer time. I noticed that there was something "pulsing" in the sky but could not see anything, just that light. I watched it for about 20 minutes so I decided to go up to my apartment and see with the camera which could be that.

I used a camera sony h-100 Zoom 42x, first took a picture and then started shooting. the object was too far away, the naked eye could only see the pulsating light but with the camera I noticed it was a metal object, at one end had a light beating. 

the object was exactly 90 degrees about me, it was bad to shoot because the zoom is at the most and I was shooting over my head, so the footage was blurry. it was hard to focus on the object, as was still day and could not see right in the camera display, and often had to zoom out to find the object in the sky. I filmed for 5 minutes and I got tired of holding the camera above my head and frustrated to not be able to focus on the object. I went down on the playground again and watched the object for another 15/20 minutes standing in the same position in the sky and then i can't see it again.
