Delta Wing Shape aircraft, huge, quiet right over Dodger Stadium

It was a hot & humid summer early evening, Dodger game was on, I lived in L.A.,on the shadows of Dodger Stadium with my parents and older brother, I was 12 or 13 years old at the time. On this particular night, I had invited a school classmate over for dinner to watch the Dodger game on T.V.
During a commercial break, we opened the door for some fresh air and to stretch the legs from sitting when I looked up and I yelled "look at that!" pointing towards the clear dark sky.

It was a Delta wing shape dark aircraft moving ever so slowly. The glare from the Dodger stadium lights, against the dark sky helped illuminate the aircraft shape as it glided across the sky right over Dodger stadium and right over our home. We were all in awe as we asked ourselves "what did we just witness?" The following morning at our neighborhood groceries store on the newspaper stand I saw a header that read "What was that?" I don't recall if it was the Herald Examiner or L.A. Times or other newspaper but I was relieved to know we weren't the only ones who saw this thing. Move forward to today, both my parents are no longer with us, and the two older brothers who were present are very vague with their recollection of this event. My schoolmate I haven't been able to find despite my efforts on social media, and the many searches of newspaper headers have been unsuccessful. I am hoping that someone attending Dodger Stadium that night that may have seen this thing can vouch for my recalling of this event.
