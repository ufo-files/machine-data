Silent craft flew over a military formation

I was at a holdover barracks at Ft Polk Louisiana in late October or early November 1967. We were having our morning formation. Over 100 men were in formation on the company street. Suddenly, an exceptionally large craft appeared over the company street. It was well over 100 feet wide. It flew over the company street at an altitude of about 100 feet and shined a light back and forth over the troop formation. It was absolutely silent and moved very slowly. It was certainly not a helicopter. When it got to the end of the company street, it just took off at an extremely high rate of speed and vanished. The whole event was less than 10 minutes.
