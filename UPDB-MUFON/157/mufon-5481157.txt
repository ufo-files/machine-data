{jpg} Bright white orb with halo around it

I was sitting in my bed, awake, reading something on my phone. All lights in the house and my bedroom were off. I saw a bright white softball shaped light to the left outside my bedroom window. About ten feet off the ground. I thought for a second that it was a person with a flashlight and was scared. But as it moved, it did not waver. It was moving in a straight line. Someone with a flashlight would be bouncing it a bit. This did not bounce or waver at all. It moved past my bedroom window and I jumped out of bed. Looked out a side window that faces my back yard. I watched the orb move across my back yard and disappear into the woods behind my house. It did not land. There was no sound. I felt restless and very anxious for about two hours afterwards before I could settle down to sleep.
