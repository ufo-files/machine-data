Slow moving telephone pole. Longer than a 737 estimate 900ft long

I saw a slow moving slender, silver cylinder that had a flat nose and had no wings, tail or vertical stabilizer. It was longer than a 737 and much slimmer. It made no noise. It appeared to be about one mile from my position at an altitude of about two to three thousand feet. It was moving from west to east and moved at an angular velocity of about 30 degrees in approximately five seconds. It looked like a silver telephone pole 
We live in a remote area and large and small passenger airplanes fly over frequently and if I can see them, I can hear the engines. This was an exception.
