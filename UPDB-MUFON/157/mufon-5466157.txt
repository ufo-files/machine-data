{td} Mirror like sphere

On a flight from Denver to Las Vegas, as we were entering the Vegas valley at approximately 5000 to 7000 feet I witnessed a chrome/mirror sphere below the plane. I noticed it when the object emerged from under the wing flying slightly diagonal to the plane and lost sight as it disappeared under the very front of the fuselage. I witnessed this object for about 5 seconds, as it was moving faster then the plane. It was either slightly wobbling or I was seeing reflections on its surface. Hard to determine the size and how far it was below the plane, if I guessed, I1d say 51 in diameter and within 1001 of the plane.
