lights in the sky

late after noon me and my brother was in the front yard we lived in succeess valley foot hills of the sierra,s looked up and saw 4 objects traveling east 3 side by side 1 back i noticed they were traveling fast to the mountains i did not think much of it i thought aircraft reflecting light but the sun was still high i watched till i heard a rooring sound back west 2 fighters that had to come from lemoore naval base when the got over head i could see they were chaseing the lights that then turnd 45 degrees and just shot over the mountains north east and just plain left the jets in the dust jets banked left but they were just not fast as the lights i did not know at that time till i joined the navy the jets where in after burners for me to have heard them so soon i estimated the light where traveling twice the speed of the jets never told any one not even my brother i have seen other strange things but those are things that jet pilots would probably not wont to know
