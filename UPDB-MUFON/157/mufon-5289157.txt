Camping Pictures

I was camping with my brother in law in the Appalachian mountains in virginia. It's private property and is around 10k acres of land. It was sunset and I took 2 pictures of the mountain range from a hill while we were riding atvs. I didnt notice the lights until after the trip when I was going through my pictures to make a camping video which I typically do and always post on youtube for family to see. I noticed in one picture a glowing light just above a tree, shortly after I noticed in a 2nd picuture it looked as though the light was on or near the ground. We found it strange since there are NO houses, buildings or towns in that direction. Also I was wondering if it was just a lens flair (pictures from an iPhone 10s max) but found it strage the light remained in the same area but looked as though it had moved lower in the later picture. We have gone back to this spot multiple times to see if we could spot or get something on camera again with no luck. I've even taken my kids there at sunsets and still no luck. We just found it to be extremely strange and very odd!
