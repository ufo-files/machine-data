Strange only white altering lights. Four altogether, No other colour of lights

Had observed a strange light while driving home and kept a watch on it but couldn't really make anything out as I was driving. Got into my driveway at the back of the house and had a proper look and know as someone who owns a telescope and watches the stars and planets that it was something I had not seen before. I have seen satellites, meteors and the ISS many times. I am also aware of the flight paths in my area. This was not on a regular flight path.
My neighbour pulled into his driveway and I mentioned it to him, he looked and seen the object and agreed that he hadn't seen anything like it before either. We watched as it went over our houses and disappeared. My neighbour went indoors but I went around to the front of the house and watched it as it disappeared from view to the south. 

My neighbour took a picture on his phone but I don't know if it would have shown much as the object was pretty high up.
