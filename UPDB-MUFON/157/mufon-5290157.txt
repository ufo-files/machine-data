Saw an H looking ufo

I was outside smoking when I saw a weird looking craft shaped like a capital H.
I watched it for about 40 minutes. It was above the river dart at about 5,000 feet. It moved up the river extremely slowly & completely silent. It had weird lights on it. Not like a planes nav lights. These were blue, green, pink, red, orange. 
The craft then made it's way down the river towards the Naval College at Dartmouth. 
I told my grandmother about it but she thought I was talking shit. As if i had had a few beers. 
It was 1985 & I was 15 yrs old. I am now 51.
I went to hospital complaining of knee swelling. They x-ray my left knee & ask why I have pieces of metal free floating around my left knee. I say I don't know & they cannot find any entry wounds on my left leg. They are still there to this day. I had an xray done about 7 yrs ago. What ate they and how did they get in my leg? 

Over to you guys.
