Over a cornfield

Seen this over a cornfield in Debert NS. Was meditating to see if I could make contact with ETs. I had previously witness a craft with Blue and Red rings of light like a gyro sphere rotating around a white pulsating light on this highway closer to New Glasgow and again driving from Yarmouth to Digby with a friend this time. It followed us this time. The first time I saw the craft was in 2011. I seen it above the tree line and thought it was a new tower of some sort. The second time I have seen it my friend told me that it was following us. I don’t feel in danger or anything. 
This time it didn’t have rings just blinking lights and one pulsating. We pulled the car over and ran across the twin highway to get a closer look. It just stayed hoovering over the cornfield. We got as close to the field without entering it. 
After that we got back in the car and continued towards Halifax and it followed us.
