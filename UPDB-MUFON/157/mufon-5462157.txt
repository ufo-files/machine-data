Bright Light

In Approx 1972-73, 4 of us witnessed a UFO bright light fly on an angle his behind a cloud, then horizontal hid again behind cloud for about 1-2 minutes the shot off up at an angle and disappeared. It was reported in local paper, Liverpool Echo, as people also saw it, I recon it was over Liverpool Airport. They said it was a satalite entering atmosphere. It 110% wasn't. It changed direction on 2 occasions after also stopping behind 2 clouds. We where all amazed
