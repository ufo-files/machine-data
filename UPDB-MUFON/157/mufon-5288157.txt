i was at work at U.S. Borax and saw white lights that appeared to be moving in a counter clockwise direction from south to north in a line.

I was working my regular night shift at a mine located in Boron Ca near Edwards Air Force Base on the morning of May 8th 2021. At which time, around 2:00 am, I observed small white lights just above the horizon in the west. At first I thought the base was conducting an airplane drill but as i watched it didn't look like planes. The lights where traveling south to north. They where equally spaced and moving at a slow pace. As they moved counterclockwise the ones to the north would disappear and more would just appear from the south. I couldn't see any definition of an object but it seemed like the lights may have been spinning around a round object. which could explain why the ones to the north where disappearing and then reappearing again form the south. It was a large space that the lights where taking up, maybe a 1/4 of a mile. Ive lived and worked in this part of Calif most of my life and have seen many many different military aircraft flown. But I've never seen anything as strange as what I saw this morning . Because of my job I couldn't stop my piece of equipment that I was operating, and eventually lost sight of the object as i had to travel deeper into the mine where I work. I spoke to one other coworker to see if he had seen them but he had not. I know what i saw.
