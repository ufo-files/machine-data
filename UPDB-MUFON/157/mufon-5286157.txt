Bright white light moving at high rate of speed from South to North across the western sky

Navy jets often train over the Skagit valley, we are used to their noise. Today we heard several of them hit their afterburners in sequence. When we looked up, we saw a bright white light moving very fast across the sky from South to North in the direction of Mt. Baker.
