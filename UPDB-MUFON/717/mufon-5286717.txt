Bean of Light NW/SE over WI

Tonight I was star-gazing (as usual) while taking my dog out before bed. We have little light pollution so it is a great opportunity to spot satellites while my dog goes potty. I was looking up at the big dipper pretty intently tonight because I had an odd sighting of a pulsating red light in the vicinity of that constellation a few nights ago (I reported this sighting as MUFON Sighting Report 116549). All the fireworks for the night were completed an hour or so beforehand, and I didn't hear any go off when I was outside tonight. First, I witnessed a satellite traveling overhead in a northerly trajectory. Immediately afterward, I noticed a very faint, but wide blue-white light that seemingly was bisecting the big dipper from the NW horizon to about 75 degrees overhead in the SE sky. It was faint, and seemed transparent. It was quite wide; about a pinky finger's width held out at arms length. It almost looked like a contrail from a plane, but at night. It was blueish white. I noticed it for about 30 seconds, and then it just blinked out. It reminded me of seeing a spotlight, except this one was over half of the entire diameter of the sky and was very thick. It didn't move. It was stationary until it blinked out. I would say that it appeared like seeing a flashlight beam with dust flying around...the beam is prominent. Immediately after this, I saw 3 meteors flash out in about a 5 minute period in the vicinity of where the beam was. It was quite odd. I have very good vision as [personal information moved to Investigators report section/ CMS/fgc] ... from a Top 5 research institution, so I am familiar with most celestial objects. This was quite odd. It was only present for about 30 seconds before it blinked out, almost as if someone turned the flashlight off.
