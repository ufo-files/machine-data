Triangular lights, which were not a star

I saw three lights in the sky from my solarium, and wasn't sure what I saw, so I got my glasses, and saw three triangular lights moving slowly across the sky. I tried to capture it on my phone, but it just appeared as one light, so it wasn't very helpful, but it was brighter than all the other lights from the stars. There is lots of air traffic around here, but I have never seen something like this before. I am injured, and can't move well, but I was compelled to dress and go outside, as it was so odd. When I went to look outside, I watched as the object was there, and then zipped away extremely quickly, and was gone. I questioned my own observation, as it was so anomalous. I don't think the data I captured on video is helpful, as it only appeared to be one point of light through the lens, but it was definitely three points of light by the naked eye. I didn't have a solid tripod to capture the event, so I can't show the movement with confidence that it wasn't my hand shaking, and my phone died just then. but it has 50%, battery, so I am not sure why it died. My phone could have just crashed. It was oddly coincidental though. I have seen meteors before, and it moved like that. However, I have never seen a meteor start from stationary, and be three points of light, and then streak across the sky. This was super odd. I'm not sure the media will be helpful, as it just appears as a point of light, but I can send it to you upon request. I'm tired, and need to sleep, but let me know if you want it.
