On thursday I was getting ready for bed when I say a fire type light from my back patio window. I went outside to see what it was. I was looking at a redish orange glow being distorted by clouds. I was trying to rationalize the event and said well maybe we have a red moon tonight for some reason. I was tired and just watched it for a while. I thought several times to go and get my camera, but knew that the battery would be low from earlier use with my kids. I kept convincing myself that it was a strange moon. The next day I was talking with an uncle and he said that we only had a quarter moon last night. I could have kicked myself. I realized that this was definatly not the moon. I really got suspicious the following day when I heard helicopters over my home. I went outside because they kept flying over. The helicopter was drab green with no markings. I was further surprised to see paratroopers doing loww jumps out of the back of the helicopter (long duel rotor). I ran inside and retrieved my camera snd staerted filming. I saw parachutes opening behind the trees. I had to pick up my kid from school so I stopped filming. I drove around the area that I thought the landed in and did not see them. I at this time stopped and left the area. I have never seen military trainnig in this area. I thought to myself is the series of the past two days coincidence or something else.
