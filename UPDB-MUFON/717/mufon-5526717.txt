15 to 20 lights in line up in the sky

In July 2006 around 9:00pm on a clear night my wife and i were driving on our way from BRENHAM,TX to BELLVILLE,TX on hwy 36 . As we where passing this town called Kenny,TX . I was getting on the over pass i started to see a strait line of light passing right over us going towards the west . The lights were flying so low as if their were helicopters. I mean their was about 15 to 20 lights as big as baseballs . I slowed down and put down my window but their was no noise . This is right in the middle of the woods so you could hear every single noise once i saw the lights i told my wife . That's when we started asking our self's what was those things up in the sky . This lights were so strait in line and so many i didn't know what to think about them . To this day we have never told anyone else about this light on hwy 36 .kept going south on hwy 36 towards bellville all the way we kept asking our self's about this mysterious lights . The thing is theirs no big airports around this area .
