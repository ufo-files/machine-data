{rjl} Two large objects side by side pulsating red/white lights hovered. when 1 moved away lights pulsated faster brighter. One went E the other SE

At 6:15 am I went to the kitchen and from my patio doors I seen two pulsating, strobe like lights in the Eastern sky. The lights flashed red and white and were above the tree line. The lights came closer growing in size and hovered side by side. The one on the left moved away from the other light and the lights of both objects pulsed faster and brighter. 
Then the light on the right went to the East and I watched until it was out of sight. The other light went south and I watched it move fast across the sky until it was out of sight. Neither one diverted from its path, in other words they did not move up or down but straight in a path. 
At first I thought of the lights that flashed in high towers but realized there are no towers there. I thought it might be a reflection but there was nothing in the house behind me that was lighting up and there are no windows behind me. 
The lights reminded me of strobe lights as they were pulsating very fast. They were too large and too bright to be airplane light and moved much faster. I live near an Air-Force base and an airport so I see planes, jets, and helicopters on an hourly basis. 
My only thought i they may have been large drones. It is something I've never witnessed before and I know what I saw but I don't know/understand what I seen. 
When the one light moved away from the other and the lights intensified in brightness and pulsating my first thought was they were communicating.
