UFO above the 10 Fwy just East of Cabazon

We were on the freeway back to Palm Springs after some Black Friday Christmas shopping in Cabazon randomly taking photos of the wind turbines and other desert scenery. We didn't notice anything anomalous at the time, but today I loaded my photos on to my computer from my camera. It wasn't until the pictures were on the computer screen that I noticed the photos contained an unusual looking object moving through the sky.
