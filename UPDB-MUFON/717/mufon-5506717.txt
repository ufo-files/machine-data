{cak} Bright light resembling a star, but bigger and brighter

My parents and I were on our road in the vehicle drinking out of town. My dad as looking up in the sky and sa a bright light, then noticed it was moving. I first thought it was a star. The object followed the vehicle, turned when we turned,sped up with us, when lost in the woods it held back a little but caught up with us when we were visible again. Then when my dad and I exited the vehicle, it hovered for several minutes while we went into the store, and was still there when we got out of the store. My dad and I were both freaked out that something would be following us and speeding up so quickly alongside it. We knew it had to be a UFO. I lost track of it when I entered another house.
