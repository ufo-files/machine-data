{cak} Reflective oval landed or hovered close to ground, left ground and we followed it in a vehicle as far as we could

In the summer of 1999 friend and I were outside. About 2200-2300. It was getting late. He pointed me to the directiin of something behind me. In the sky. Brilliant colors, some blinking, some pulsating, and the reflective object landed or hovered at ground level in a wooded area behind a cemetery in a strip of woods that were cleared to run powerlines. We watched it, amazed and confused and as it got to ground level, I immediately freaked out and started jumping up and down tapping him on the shoulder saying his name over and over and saying that is a UFO. We ran back to my house to tell my step dad, who was a shrewd businessman who didn't believe in anything he is not religious he doesn't believe in anything at all- I ran in the house and the dark dog started going crazy, and I said to come see outside, that there was a ufo. He was screaming at me and I kept telling him there was something outside. He was yelling at me in complete disbelief as he walked outside the side door to the house. He immediately stopped talking and watched as the object slowly ascended silently and began to move slowly away and he said get the keys to the truck. we got the keys and headed Northbound on a highway here and we followed it through four cities with others until we can no longer keep up. There were four of us in the vehicle.
