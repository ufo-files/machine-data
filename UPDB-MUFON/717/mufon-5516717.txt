{dr}White orbs (like pearls) going in and out of a huge cloud.

November of last year (2011) I was with my son at his lawyers office in San Jose, Ca. Sitting, looking out the windows, waiting for them to finish, I saw a huge cloud in the distance (one of many that day).  I noticed something was going in and out of the cloud, I thought birds, but in almost the same thought I realized at that distance birds probably wouldnt be visible. I saw what looked like giant pearls going in and out of this cloud. I watched as the "orbs" would leave the cloud, descend or ascend a bit and then go back into it. I went to the window and watched for 3 or 4 minutes until the trees outside the building blocked my view as the cloud slowly moved. The building across the street was a 2 story, we were on the 2nd floor of our building, and being on a busy street I knew there was no use going outside to try and see it again. I didn't say anything about what I saw until my son and I were in the car. I think he was too overwhelmed from his meeting (filing for divorce) because he had almost no reaction. When I saw my mother, I told her what I had seen and she was quite interested. Some time had passed when (at my mothers house) she called me into the room to show me a program she was watching. To my amazement it was showing another cloud with what looked like the same white orbs that someone had filmed. Before I saw the orbs, I thought life other than earth may exist, now I know we arent alone. I look to the sky a lot more now.
