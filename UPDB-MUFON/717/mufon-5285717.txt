Saw lights around a football-field-size disc over Hudson River on a summer night in 1984. It was hovering, then suddenly took off at a 15 degree angl

Driving along Rt. 9 North, saw car in front of me wavering and driving erratically, then pulling over and guy jumps out, looking up at sky to my left, over the Hudson River. 
I looked over the river then and saw lights glowing down from something very big, hovering. I got scared, locked car doors, sped up and as I did, I saw the orb lift silently up at an angle and ascend over the hill ahead, in the direction I was going. Didn't see it again, got home about 10 minutes later, called police number and said "I know there's an explanation for this, but I just saw.... (told him what I saw) and he said "well you're the 11th person to call in about this" I called back the next day to find out what it had been determined to be and the person answering said they had no record of my calling or any knowledge of what I was talking about.
