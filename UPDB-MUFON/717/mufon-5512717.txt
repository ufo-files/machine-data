{cak} I thought it was a really bright star at first until I noticed it moving. Small circles. Left then right. Strobe like flashing of red green blue and white

Williamsburg ky, I was laying in bed and I happened to look out the window and I saw what I thought was a really bright twinkling star. As I looked at it I noticed that it was moving but it wasnt moving normal or like anything I had ever seen. It gave me goose bumps. It was to the south of Williamsburg maybe over jellico mtn. I started noticing that I could actually make out the colors that it was flashing. Red blue green and white. Kinda reminded me of strobe lights used on emergent vehicles only with no real discernible patterns. I have been watching it for over an hour now and its about out of my sight. It is traveling southwest. Its like a big star. It was doing small circles and then it would move left or right. Its still there now however it seems like its getting even farther away. My first reaction was to get my video camera but its too far away to even focus. My camera thinks the cap is on the front of it so it doesnt pick anything up. I have been around the world and seen most things that fly and this did not fit the description of anything I have EVER seen.
