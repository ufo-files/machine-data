{rjl} Traveling west towards Clinton on County Line Rd. when I noticed a bright star-like object in the sky not but a couple hundred feet away

As I was traveling west to Clinton, I noticed a bright, star-like object in the sky to my left. It wasn’t a plane, helicopter, or drone. It was too bright and star-like. It just remained in that position then as I got further down the road I saw it coming closer, almost above the treetops, (descending at an angle) and I started to get nervous so I sped up. The road I was traveling on is dark with no lights and little to no cars traveling at night. I kept looking at it as I was driving and it seemed like it was coming closer and closer and I could start to make out a shape. A couple of miles down the road, I came to a highway intersection where I cross into Clinton. I stopped at the stoplight and looked out and couldn’t see it anymore.
