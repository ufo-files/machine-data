Very bright oval shaped object hovering just above ground, moved then disappeared.

I was walking on the roads that lead to the rice fields here in Japan with my camera, hoping that the pill shaped object that I saw earlier that day would return that night. At 10:30pm, I saw this very bright object hovering just above the rice fields, about 20 feet. I took this picture, showing the bright object, as it moved away, making two images of its form on the photo. I ran to its position, but it disappeared as I ran in its direction. I did not see it fly off, it just seemed to turn off its lights. It made no noise and appeared to be around 10 feet across. I circled it on the second photo.
