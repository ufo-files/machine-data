We saw an orange-reddish colored object going north

My husband and I were setting off fireworks in front of our home, many others in the neighborhood were as well. Suddenly my husband yelled for me to look up and pointed out something very odd, we saw a large orange-reddish colored 'orb' moving from south to north, below the clouds but above the mountain range. It was moving in very quick, but steady fashion. It was definatley not an airplane, falling star or a firework, impossible. We ran down the street so we could continue to view it, after just a few minutes it disappeared into the distance.
