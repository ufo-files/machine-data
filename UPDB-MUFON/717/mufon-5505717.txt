{rjl} Two red-orange light emitting orbs hovered a few miles north from Clear Lake Middle School. Both objects maintained equal distance and slowly gained altitude and went above clouds. Objects brightness did not change. Not a firework.

At approximately 11:16 I was going south on El Camino past Clear Lake Middle School when a light emitting red-orange orb was spotted hovering to my back left a few miles to the north before slowly moving NE at a slow speed. Driver of vehicle saw the same object. Objects brightness rapidly faded upon passing the intersection of El Camino and El Dorado heading south on El Camino until object was a pin point source and shortly thereafter, not visible. Called family member at 11:18pm who witnessed two red-orange orbs to the NE. Family member saw both objects maintaining equal distance from each other, slowly gained altitude, one going above clouds followed by the other a few seconds after (distance between objects did not change during siting). Objects brightness did not change when observed at a constant distance. Not a firework.
