(GS) Bright light falling from sky

Driving home at dusk on HWY W in Morgan County Missouri on April 14, 2013 it was very eye catching, how could you miss a giant brightly lighted object shaped like a tear drop/sphere falling from the sky? When I noticed it I knew it wasnt from a plane or some sort of missile, I felt intrigued
and scared of what it could be. It looked like it was on fire and moved downward with very little slant and very quickly with a trail behind it. when my friend and I stopped we took a picture and a short video and it then disappeared behind the clouds. Now all I can think about is what did we see and want some answers!
