Pulsating flying object spotted over houses flying south above N. Shamokin St.

I was walking east on E. Independence St, towards N. Shamokin St, shortly after dark with groceries. As I reached the intersection of E. Independence St. and N. Shamokin St., I started turning right to walk south on N. Shamokin St. As I turned, I noticed a very low flying object over the buildings across the street on N Shamokin St. It was flying south, and seemingly randomly pulsated white, green, and red. The object flew at a constant speed. It appeared around 50-100 feet above the buildings. It was so low that I quickly lost sight of the object behind the buildings. The sighting was so short that I was unable to video it on my phone in time.
