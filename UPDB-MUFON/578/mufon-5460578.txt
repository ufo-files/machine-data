Watched as 3 object- must have been well above 10k feet crossed from west to east. They were spaced perfectly in a line. No blinking lights. Watched from about 3 miles up old mission peninsula. These were not commercial or civilian aircraft. If they were in space, they were moving faster than any satellite I’ve seen with naked eye. Someone else saw the same thing in down town traverse city at same time and posted here. Very unusual sighting- never seen anything like it in my life. Tried to record, but my phones camera could not pick up the lights as it has poor night video. Objects remained in formation until they were over horizon- did not fade in brightness. Had to be metallic- light from noon on underside.
