A light in the sky I thought was a star or satalite but it started to move

I was on my roof trying to spot the Neowise comet when toward the top of the sky overhead coming from towards the south what I thought was a star or satellite until I saw it move in a weird turn. I watched it for a while fly in a not so straight line until trees blocked my view of it in the sky.The light on it was flickering like the light you would see from one of the planets or a close star, it was quite bright.
