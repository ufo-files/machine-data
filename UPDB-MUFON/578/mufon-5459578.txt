{jpg} Orb of light

A distinct orb of light that sort of wobbled as it crossed the sky slowly. I have a video of it, camera was zoomed in maximum so a bit wobbly but clear view of object. Also I have seen thousands of high flying jets in my life and this object caught my eye as odd when I first saw it, after a few seconds it obviously didn't track like a jet does and after a few minutes of listening carefully for the distinct jet engine sound of a high flying airliner there was nothing. I can't say for sure it was alien or not an aircraft but it didn't look right.
