amazingly fast object moving across sky

I was walking my 2 dogs in the yard this morning like i usually do. I notice a very fast object moving across the morning sky at an incredible speed,faster than any jet. It went horizontally from east to west at about 25-30 degrees elevation. I would estimate that It was as bright and the size of a cars halogen headlight at 150 yard. It lasted only a second and then it was gone. I looked down at my watch and it was 4:59am. It made no sound and left no trail.
