{dr}In observing anomalous clouds at bottom of field of view, final pix revealed much more

Driving in Cypress, CA, stopped at a signal. I originally observed an anomalous cloud which looked like an octopus with its arms facing upwards. I was thinking to myself how strange, when I noticd that at the end of each arm, there were other octopi forming. That convinced me they were not normal clouds, so I decided to capture them in photo (located to the upper left of the palm tree cluster behind the telephone pole bottom, right of the center. Unfortunately, the impact of the anomaly is not seen in the photo :(. However, once the pic was uploaded to my PC and magnified, there was MUCH MORE in the pic than I had noticed. I marked all the anomalies by number: #1 is what I call the skid-mark. #2 is a 3-D cube, #3 is the wet t-shirt (for lack of a better description), #4 is the flying pyramid, note what appears to be windows at the bottom, #5 untitled, plus others un-numbered/ un-titled.
