{nd} Odd sequencing randomly appearing and disappearing light activity observed for 40 minutes

Family traveling south of Breckenridge observed odd lights of immense size appearing and disappearing. Observed lights and stopped at hwy 112 intersection at Morton valley to record images. Observed lights coming on and off and sequencing at times for 30 minutes. Air Traffic came into area and lights stopped appearing. There was a lot of traffic going down the highway and the lights would appear in the sky well within their view.
