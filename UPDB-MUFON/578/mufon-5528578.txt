UFO Skeptic

I was driving into work and looked to my right over the trees, I noticed what I thought was an airplane. The object was traveling straight down. I thought it was an airplane that was going to crash. It was heading nose down like it was falling. The object disappeared over the trees. I was expecting to hear a boom and feel a tremble as a result of the "airplane" crashing. I looked to my left and saw the object on the other side of the highway ( the highway is 4 lanes, north and south)I couldn't fathom how the object was able to get from my right to my left as quickly as it did. I then looked above me an saw another oval shaped object. I thought it might have been a helicopter but I didn't see any propellers (dawn was breaking). I noticed a halogen light under the second object and lights all around it. The object traveled in a straight horizontal plane and immediately changed direction as if it was doing a nose dive. I have never seen maneuvering as these object displayed. I mean, the second object was flying straight and without a second thought or hesitation changed it's direction to completely vertical. I tried to catch the event on my cell phone. When I played the recording back, nothing was captured. I was completely freaked out especially because I don't believe in UFO's!
