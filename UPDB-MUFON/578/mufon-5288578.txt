Pulsating light source, hovering and bouncing

On 16 April at 2200 hours my dogs alerted to something. Thinking coyotes or a mountain lion, I exited the house to my back deck to see. I observed a bright point source of light bouncing up and down in a NE direction. My first thought was that it was someone walking up the hill on my property. But the light was pulsing from red to green to white, not sequentially but randomly. I could hear no aircraft or vehicles in the area. I live in an area with very little sound and light pollution. The point source stopped bouncing and remained fixed. Observing it through binoculars I could see the bright light cycling through the color changes. I could also see that it was airborne. It remained fixed in position until I retired for the night. My wife continued to observe it until 0200. She reported that the light started the up and down bouncing motion that I had observed. It was still there in the same location when she retired at 0200. The photo attached shows a white light source. If you zoom on it, the object looks triangular, but that could be pixel distortion. My conclusion...weird.. I don't know what could do that for 3 hours..
