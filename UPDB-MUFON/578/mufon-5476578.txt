The year was 2000. I was the passenger in a pick up truck. We were about mid way between Jamestown, New York and Altoona, Pennsylvanina. It was approximatly 8pm. It was a couple weeks before Thanksgiving and it was fairly cold outside. I was looking around because I was bored. I looked up and saw a strange shadow in the sky. I asked the driver to pull over. I got out of the truck and I saw it fully. It was very large and stationary. The underside was completely black except for four lights just inwards of the four points. It was diamond shaped. I could see it because the night sky around it was brighter than the bottom of it. I couldn't guess at the altitude. We got back in the truck and started traveling again. The craft seemed to be keeping pace with us. After awhile it just seemed to dissappear. I had no idea what the craft could be except a U.F.O. I didn't become upset because I got no indication that the craft knew that we were there.
