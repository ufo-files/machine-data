Very bright star like object appears and moves slowly then disappears

I was going to close the balcony door becouse it was getting colder outside and looked to the sky at that moment. First I noticed Mars but almost instantly I noticed a satelite that appeared to be moving from Mars. I called my son to bring the binoculars to look at the satelite. This satelite was rather difficult to spot becouse it was getting dark outside and Mars and Betelgouse were the only visible stars. Suddenly a very bright thing brigher than Mars! appeared near to Mars on the sky and moved very slowly. I and my 2 sons were amazed of this thing whith this much brightness this very slow movement in the sky.It looked similar to Mars in colour that is orange. I don't know what this was. After moving slowly for 2 and a half minute it just disappeared.
