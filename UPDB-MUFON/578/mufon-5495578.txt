As a child we were playing hide and seek in the saguallo hills of penuelas owned by our family..I ran to a hillside to hide and while hiding I heard a buzzing sound like there were bees hovering over me. I rolled to my back and there was a craft hovering over me with rotating lights directly above me. I was temporarily paralyzed snd I was in complete shock. I wanted to hide but could not move. The craft hovered for about 30 seconds then flew straight up into space in a flash. To this day I never shared my story because back in 1945 nothing like this never existed. I'm 77 years old and I wanted to share my story because I didn't want to take this to my grave and needed to share it. I asked my nephew to contact someone about this to see if they want me to share more info. I speak Spanish and broken English. Email in this form is my Son "F" and Nephew "A". Call if you have any questions. Thank you for your time.
