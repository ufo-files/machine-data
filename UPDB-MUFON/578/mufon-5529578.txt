Solid bright light moving slowly then gradually fades from sight

I was out with the family for an astronomy viewing event we scheduled with a company in Sedona. We were at a trail head just west of the town, in a desolate part so we could get a clear view of the sky. The gentlemen conducting the event was pointing over towards the eastern horizon when I just happened to look almost straight up. A solid moving light caught my eye. It was moving at a moderate pace in a SW direction. At first I thought it was a plane but it did not blink and it seemed to be at a higher than you would expect altitude for a plane. I then thought it must be a satellite. Even though it was much larger and brighter than any satellite I have ever seen. The size of the object was slightly larger than jupiter that night, and not as bright.  Best described as a solid yellow Just as I was leaning over to tell me wife, the object made a slight deviation from its course and faded out of sight, it did not go out abruptly but rather kinda faded quickly. There was no trail of an kind. I watched for it to show up on its path but nothing did. The entire viewing lasted 15 seconds at the most. We continued with our Astronomy tour and I was looking for the object to show back up. I scanned the sky for the next 2 hours and saw nothing.
