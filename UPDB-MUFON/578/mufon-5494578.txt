{rjl} Star-like UFO that had changing colors.

I let the dogs out & looked up to see the stars & I noticed that there was a very odd round pulsating orb in the night sky. It definitely was not a star (seemed quite lower than the stars that were out) it seemed to be changing different colors. It moved very slowly across the sky. I went back out to look at it about an hour after I first saw it & it had moved across into a different part of the sky (desendend a little). Went back inside for about 20 minutes & came back out to see it & it was gone.
