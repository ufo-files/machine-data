(GS) 6 red large lights hovering in pattern

We were walking our dog east and we noticed 6 red lights in North east direction above the foot hills of North Lake Forest Ca. The objections we in creating a pattern/line formation, then they shifted closer together. They were higher 1000 ft. Then a helicopter was flying towards them and they pulsated and 2 disapeared. Then the other 4 changed direction and pulsated and then vanished. Then the helicopter turned around and took off back towards the south west.
