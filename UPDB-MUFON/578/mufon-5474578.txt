Saw three UFOs in South Korea got two on film

1. I was driving home from work at 330am when I first noticed the object in the sky in the direction of my house.

2. I noticed it because it stood out. I knew it was not a star, planet, helicopter or plane. I got scared because I have frequent sleep paralysis which I blame on ET visitation. I felt like they were searching for me and realized I wasn't there.

3. I thought it was a UFO. It was too bright and low in the sky to be a star.

4. The main object (glowing white) appeared to move up and down, left and right as it was watching me and I felt like it responded to my thoughts. I saw a second object appear from the clouds that was cloaked. It had red and white flashing lights like it was pretending to be a plane. It made a turn above my house but was too fast to catch on video. I saw a third object which I got on film it was very large and it had different colored flashing lights. I have two videos of it.

5. I was already very tired from working at 4 pm till 3 am. I was really scared and nervous seeing the UFOS. I was also drawn to them, I could not stop looking at them. Like my mind and body said its ok, don't be afraid. My reactions were shock I think. I have been a believer but it was overwhelming seeing such incredible things for my first sighting. I went outside to get better video. I was really afraid of possible abduction. 

6. I did not break my sight. I watched them for two hours non stop until I was too tired and went to bed.
