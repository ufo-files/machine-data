Two dark round objects near the US. Marine Base

I was inspecting the roof of a house I was interested in purchasing & later that evening, while looking at my photographs of the damaged roof on the house, I noticed two dark round objects in my photo. They did not make a sound & I was not aware of them when taking the photo. They do not look like birds, they are too round. This was just a couple miles from the Twentynine Palms US. Marine Corps base. I was wondering if anyone else saw something that day.
