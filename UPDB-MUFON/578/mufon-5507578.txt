I was driving home from a convenient store with my coffee...I noticed a beautiful sunrise as I turned onto my street....then I noticed something in the sky below the cloud cover so i knew it was not a star. I also Knew it was not an airplane of any sort as it was hovering in one spot. I pulled into my driveway, got out of my car with my iphone to take a photo. It was still hovering in one spot. I took a photo of it and watched it for about another minute. Suddenly it streaked across the sky Northbound and disappeared from my view. Judging the distance it was a way from me, which was probably a few miles.....I am quite sure it jetted away at thousands of MPH. When I first observed the object I thought it might have been a balloon or something....then I felt strangely about the object. After it had jetted away I felt a little excited and a little scared. I had never really believed in UFO hype before. Now I believe our technology has increased by leaps and bounds or there are extraterrestrials.
