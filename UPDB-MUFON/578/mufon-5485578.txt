{jpg} Cloud looking ships

For our tenth wedding anniversary my husband and I decided to make the trek to Rachel, Nv since we both love sci-fi and have always wanted to go there. We were between the front and back gate when we saw these clouds so I whipped out my good camera to snap a few shots since the clouds looked cool but the more we look at them and the longer we watched them the more we have become convinced that they were unusual. I felt this huge need to share. I also live near an active air force base and have seen strange craft before but never ever like this.
