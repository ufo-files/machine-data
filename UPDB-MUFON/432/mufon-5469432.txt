{td}Triangle shaped object with 3 bright lights spun around and around under the clouds for several minutes. Saw it from multiple locations. Approached it driving up to it and stopped and watched it spin around and around for several minutes and vanish

I was about a block away from my home. Approaching the area via my vehicle when I noticed three bright lights in the sky. I recognized them as something I had seen in the sky earlier that night from miles away. Now I was right underneath them. It was a very large triangle shaped object. I took a short video on my iPhone but it doesn't capture the lights very well. You can faintly see the lights. They were large circles of light. The object spun around and around under the clouds for several minutes and then just vanished. So I drove him in utter shock and told my husband few minutes later what I saw. He had seen me stop but from the angle he was behind me did not see the lights. I have never seen anything like it again. It was a stormy night and I do see several planes in this area but I have never seen anything like this before.
