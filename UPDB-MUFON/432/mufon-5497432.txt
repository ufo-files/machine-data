I was at home smoking a cigarette with my mother on her back porch after eating dinner. I looked up in the sky to see what appeared at first glance a flying plane. But upon further observation, I was immediately assured it wasn't. It didn't travel in a straight line. It zig-zagged. Even did a loopdy-loop. All while maintaining the same radius in between 2 fixed stars. It had definite features of flapping wing like limbs. It also glowed as if on fire. My mother also confirmed it's unexpected route/path. At first, it brought deep fear with the immediate believe our galaxy was collapsing. Until I gathered myself&realized it was the only one doing so. I went inside to call a fellow colleague insight of his theory's on the matter, in which he recommended I report it.
