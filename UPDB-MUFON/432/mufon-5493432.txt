{jpg} I OBSERVED A TRIANGLE FOR 3 DAYS. DAY AND NIGHT.IT WAS THE SIZE OF A SMALL BB.IT WAS TRIANGULAR WITH 3 BRIGHT LIGHTS.

IM USUALLY OUT AND ABOUT LATE NIGHT EARLY MORNING HOURS.BEATING THE HEAT AND THE TRAFFIC. I OBSERVED THE TINY 3 LIGHT TRIANGLE AROUND 2AM MID MAY 1995. I DIDNT REALLY PAY MUCH ATTENTION TO IT THINKING IT WAS LIKELY A STEALTH BOMBER FROM NELLIS AS IVE SEEN THEM THROUGH THE YEARS. NEXT DAY WAS RUNNING AROUND TOWN LOOKED UP AND THERE IT IS AGAIN 3 BRIGHT LIGHTS THE SIZE OF A BB.I THOUGHT WOW THEY ARE STILL FLYING AROUND UP THERE. I WAS THINKING IF AIRLINERS FLY AROUND 45KFT THIS THING WAS LIKELY AROUND 120KFT.NEXT NIGHT I HAPPEN TO LOOK UP AGAIN AND SEE THAT IT SEEMS TO BE FOLLOWING WITH ME FROM ONE SIDE OF TOWN ALL THE WAY TO THE OTHER. THATS WHEN I STARTED GETTING CONCERNED.WHY WOULD THIS THING BE FOLLOWING ME AROUND.SO NEXT DAY I WENT AROUND TO A COUPLE FRIENDS HOMES AND ASKED WHAT THEY THOUGHT IT MIGHT BE. THEY WERE AS PERPLEXED AS I WAS. NEXT NIGHT EARLY MORNING HOURS I DECIDED I WAS GOING TO GET TO THE BOTTOM OF IT.SO I DROVE FROM THE RIO HEADED NW TOWARDS MT CHARLSTON.LONG STORY SHORT THIS BOOMERANG DID NOT LIKE ME POINTING IT OUT TO PEOPLE AND THE MORE I PAID ATTENTION TO IT THE MORE IT WAS DRAWN TO ME. FINALLY IT DECENDED DAWN TO ABOUT 500 FT AT 95/CHEYENNE AND LET ME KNOW.HEY WE ARE HERE AND THERES NO GETTING AWAY. I LOOKED DIRECTLY AT THE BOTTOM OF IT AS I WAS SPEEDING DOWN THE FREEWAY . IT WAS FLAT BLACK WITH ROUNDED EDGES SIMILAR TO A NERF BOOMERANG.IT PIVETED AND STARTED HEADING SOUTH ON CHEYENNE.I WAS THINKING MY BEST OPTION WOULD BE TO HEAD DOWN EAST ON CRAIG TO NELLIS AIR FORCE BASE LAS VEGAS BLVD. WE HAVE A STORAGE YARD OUT BY WERE THE NEW SPEEDWAY IS NOW. I TURNED LEFT ON LVBLVD HEADED OUT NORTH WERE ALL THE JUNK YARDS ARE AND SUDDENLY JUST LIKE YOU SEE IN THE MOVIES ALL MY GAGES ARE GOING CRAZY.I FINALLY MADE IT TO OUR STORAGE YARD AND THEN THE FUN BEGAN. WET FLAKY CRYSTAL CIRCLES,GLOWING PLASMA BALL WITH A LIGHT FEELING OVER CARS AND TRAILERS UNTIL IT FOUND ME.I WAS DEFINATELY BEING HUNTED.WEIRD THING WAS NELLIS AIRFORCE BASE SURLY HAD TO SEE THIS THING BUT DID NOTHING.  SORRY FOR THE BAD GRAMMAR GO TO HEAD TO WORK.
