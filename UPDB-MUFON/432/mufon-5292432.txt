Glowing red ball moving left to right and up and down

My husband and I got up early to see the Perseid meteor shower. We spotted a red glowing object go across the sky dipping in altitude then back up again and back and forth. We where able to get the first half on video (see attached) when it got the left side of the moon it was to bright to see. But it stopped and slowly went down under the moon and hovered for about 5 seconds, turned left to right again and shot off.
