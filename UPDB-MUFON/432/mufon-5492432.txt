{rjl} My husband and I saw 3 individual white lights in a triangle shape. They merged together then disappeared.

My husband was outside with the dog around 9:10 pm on this Tues. night. I was inside watching tv and he called for me to come out. He told me to look up in the sky to the south. There were 3 individual white, round,lights in the shape of a triangle. We watched for awhile as the one on the right moved to the one on the left then merged together. This light eventually moved up to the top light and merged together so there was one light. I went back inside to get my phone to take a picture and when I went back out, my husband said the light had just vanished. On several occasions after this, for the next several months we saw single white lights in the same direction. They hover,sometime move up or down, sometimes merge with another light, then disappear. We have talked about this a lot since then, as we have been mystified as to what we saw, and would like to know what it was. Our only conclusion was that we had seen a ufo.
