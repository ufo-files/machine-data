I was eating in my car by Housatonic river. 5/15/2016 7:42pm. Noticed orange glowing object. Thought it was street light reflected in car window. Got out of car with camera got still picture and video. Object descended from clouds. Leveled off under clouds. Flew in a slight arc from out of north west. Flew in southerly direction then ascended and disappeared into clouds. Very odd flight. Floated like butterfly on the wind. Moved very slow. Object was glowing orange with white necleous. Could see what looked lick solid structure inside intense glow. Inner object appeared to be more narrow at rear. This was not ball lightening.
