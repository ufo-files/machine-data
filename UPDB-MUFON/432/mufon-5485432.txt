{dr}first observed red-orange orb 75' away slowly rise from 8' up to 100' in neighbor's drway thru tree from my patio

Upper middle-class neighborhood, University Park, single family homes, I went out on my patio on a lovely starlit, quiet autumn night to have a smoke, sat in patio chair, lit cigarette, relaxed, then movement, straight out from my position, from a red-orange orb, size of a basketball, at about 8' off the ground in my neighbor’s driveway, about 100' away, caught my eye. I sat observing, at first thinking 'what in the world', as it slowly ascended, it would disappear when it would have a part of the large oak tree obstruct its view to me, then reappear as it continued to slowly rise. Several times I'd lose sight of it, behind tree limb, then it would reappear higher on same 'floating' trajectory, upwards, then approx. 100' up I lost sight of it. What did I just observe? And why? I got up, looked around, no-one to be seen or heard, went back to my world inside the house.
