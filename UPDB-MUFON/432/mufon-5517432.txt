{ck} Orbs of light seen from Carowinds Blvd (Charlotte - Fort Mill Area)

Tonight around 9:05 I was on Carowinds Blvd in Fort Mill, SC sitting at a red light heading towards Rt 77 in front of Carowinds Amusement Park.  Off in the distance I saw two sets of 6-7 yellow orbs of light off in the distance. My best guess is that they were somewhere over the Pineville area. I originally noticed it because on of the formations looked like the big dipper and I briefly thought thats what it was. The other formation was in the form of a circle. I initially thought they might be normal air traffic for the airport but the circle in particular puzzled me. As I continued to watch they start to slowly break formation rising to different altitudes at different rates of speed. Some disappeared, others kept rising. The only logical explanation I can think of is some sort of light being lifted by a helium ballon that seems doubtful. Im really posting just to see if anyone else saw this. I continued to drive and lost sight when I turned to get on the highway.
