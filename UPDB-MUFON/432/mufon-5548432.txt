Blurry ball of light drops from a cloud, gets brighter, and takes off

My girlfriend and I were driving and saw movements in speed and light with the sky that seemed unusual. A ball-like light dropped from one of the clouds fast and then stayed stationary as it became brighter and more fuzzy while it changed shape at the edges. You could see pieces of the cloud that broke off as it came down. We began to drive toward it and then it just became smaller until it was gone as if it sucked itself inside of a particle with a flash. We were in a sort of trance that might have been because of the driving and thought nothing of it until a few days later. We cannot remember if we were in New Mexico or Texas, but it was near the boarder.
