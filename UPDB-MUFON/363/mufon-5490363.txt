{cak] It looked like a shooting star but when I looked closer it didn't have a tail and stopped

I was sitting out back of the townhouse condos on the patio. It was around 9 - 10 PM. I was facing Westerly direction. I saw what looked like a shooting star going through the sky out of the corner of my eye. When I turned my head and looked closer toward it and it's path I immediately noticed it didn't have a tail like other shooting stars do from breaking up into the atmosphere etc. As soon as I saw this it slowed down and even stopped completely in it's path. My jaw dropped. It stood still in the horizon for what seemed like an instant. Then immediately took off, like something I've never seen before, for an object to appear to be a shooting star in the sky to stop like this, change direction and move like this was again like nothing I've ever seen. From the way I was facing and the way it moved stopped and continued it would have been flying north of me so north phoenix westbound until it stopped then moved north west. I lost site of it after it took off into the horizon.
