{rjl} silent black triangle moving at the speed of a helicopter - no noise - crazy

I was walking out to my car from my parents house, looked up at the stars and saw a black triangle blocking out the light from behind it. It was hard to see with the naked eye, but not impossible. Just hard to find. It moved in a straight line at the same height and speed as a helicopter. I tried to show my wife, but she couldnt see it in time, and then i lost it behind some trees; and that was it. The craziest part about it was that there was ZERO noise from the thing! 2 minutes after spotting it a helicopter flew by overhead.
