{jpg} Strange lights observed by hundreds in town

It was around 11pm and I was on my computer. I was on Facebook and began noticing a Facebook group for our town area giving a lot of notifications suddenly. I checked the page and noticed about five different posts of strange lights in the Saint Cloud area where I lived over a golf course. I watched the videos and kept seeing people commenting seeing they saw them as well. I told my mother and we headed outside to see if I could see them as well. I looked South east toward the direction of the golf course and sure enough, we saw 3/4 lights stationary and glowing. The objects stayed in place for 30 minutes before they began to move south and others on the Facebook page in other cities began reporting seeing them. They made no noise and were white, red, and orange in color. They were orb shaped. I have never seen anything like them. I was fascinated and terrified all at once that hundreds were seeing them. Eventually I lost sight of them but they continued according to other reports.
