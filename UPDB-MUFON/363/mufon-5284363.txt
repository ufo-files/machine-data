Multi colored lights on a object

My sister-in-law saw this last week from her Loveland home. she saw it again tonight and called my wife and told us where to look. we saw an object from our home in Hudson that was due North and a moderate altitude. I got my binoculars and they helped a bit I would say it was 30 miles away and I would guess 3000 feet. 

the object had lits that appeared to be in an oval pattern from my vantage point. there were white lights that went around the shape. there were also single fashing red, blue and green lights. The white lights were constant and the colored lights flashed at random times. 

the object moved north and lost it in the clouds a few times. I observed it for about 10 minutes through my binoculars but it was at such a distance the only thing I could see was the lights and the shape they formed.

The object was too far away to take a picture.
