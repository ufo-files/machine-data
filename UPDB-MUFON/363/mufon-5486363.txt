It was a small point like a star with two big white triangles in his sides. It quickly disappeared.

This happened in the Interbalnearia route at the Balneario La Tuna in Canelones, Uruguay. I was in the back side of my car when suddenly, I saw trough the window to the south when there was a big object rising from the horizon. I asked to my parents "What is that thing?". Both and I were impressed of that thing. It was a ball, smaller than a common star, and from the sides it had two big white triangles, I don1t know if they were lights or what... We stopped the car to appreciate that thing. It moved slowly, from the south to the north west in a straight line. We looked at that object for about 10 minutes. We got distracted for one second when suddenly it disappeared. It didn1t made any noise. We were surprised and interested on know what was that.
I dont have any photo or video. All what I know is what I remember.
