I woke up at 1am went to kitchen sink to get a drink of water and looked out the window, there were 2 horizontal rows of bright red lights about the size of large plate,maybe 12-14 in each row, there were also a white/green light on the top. I could not see the shape of the object because it was very dark out in the field behind our house. My husband had gotten up and I called him into the kitchen and pointed out the window, that's when we noticed round lights above the tree line about a quarter of a mile north of us, my husband went to the back door for a better look and counted 19 of them stretched out over half a mile, the object in the field was totally silent.for a reason I still don't understand we just went back to bed, I didn't even lay awake thinking about what I had just seen I went right to sleep. wish I had gotten some pictures
