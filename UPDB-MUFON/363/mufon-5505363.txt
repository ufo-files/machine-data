{cak} Two glowing objects, stationary, toying with airplanes.

It was the morning of christmas. I was sitting in my car parked, drinking a coffee. I had on sunglasses, which combined with my window tint, allowed me to see the sun clearly, without squinting. It was 0900 a.m.. I observed two stationary objects, very close to the sun. They were yellowish-red. After about a, minute, one would dissapear, and reappear in another section of the sky. Then, the other stationary object would dissapear, and reappear in a different spot. They seemed to be close to airplanes, which I could also see. It appeared the objects were playing with the airplanes. I watched for about ten minutes, then they both vanished.
