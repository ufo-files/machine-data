{dr}Lightning-like flashes in the same spot, no thunder.

I was at work on break sitting outside while in the eye of a storm at about 12:25 AM. Looking west over the highway I could see what I first thought was lighting, but it was flashing over and over again in a stationary position with no thunder a little over a mile away. It did this for about three minutes and abruptly stopped.
