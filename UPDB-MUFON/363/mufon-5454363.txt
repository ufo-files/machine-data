Clear orb changes flight path than at high speed continues flight, than crashes into sawmill and explodes

On April 23 2012 a bright clear orb came from the west and was just above the tree line out by the highway in front of the trailer court here in prince george bc.It came floating in a straight line,made no noise, than stopped above the trees line out by where the road and river is.The orb than at high speed flew across the road and river in a south west direction towards the lakeland sawmill .The orb in a split second flew into the sawmill and caused a massive explosion / fire and fatalities.Two other orbs on the same fight path from the west have flew by since the mill explosion and never changed direction like the first one that exploded into the mill.The second orb flew bye in 2018 and was about the size of a basket ball and few by just above the front tree line, like the first one ,it made no noise The third clear orb that went by flew a couple hundred feet above where the first two flew and was bigger about the size of a medicine ball.These three orbs all look like they were human made and controlled.The second and third orbs flew east past the prince george pulp mill and were flying lower than the smoke stacks at the pulp mill on the north side of stacks.The cause of the lakeland mill explosion was blamed on pine beetle tree dust build up in the mill operation area,The real cause was a clear orb that flew into the mill and caused the explosion.Reason for not reporting this sooner was scared,not sure if people would believe me .On feb 2020 in pg i finally made a report to the rcmp about the three separate incidents.I also mentioned to the rcmp that either the second orb or third one that i saw may have had something to do with the gas line explosion out by Shelly reserve awhile back?
