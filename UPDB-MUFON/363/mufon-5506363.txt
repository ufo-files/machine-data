My son and saw strange blue lights in shape of a boomerang over Anstey Hill N.E of Adelaide on Mon 18th Oct.

After driving my son back to his house in Vista, N,E Adelaide he stopped me just as I was about to drive away. He said that there were strange lights in the sky. At first I thought that he was joking however after getting out of my car and looking for myself I saw the following.
Above us over "Anstey Hill" approximatly 1-2kms away were a formation of bright blue lights (8-10 of)in the shape of a boomerang.We both laughed,unable to explain what we were seeing.At first I thought that it was a kite however when I relised that the width of the object was at least 30-50mtrs I ruled that out. The object then began to move away untill it looked like a single bright blue light occationally flickering. It moved slowly side to side approximatly 50mtrs then dived downwards and back up. It hovered for some time before moving in various motions again. At this point my son took pictures of the object on his mobile phone.We watched the object for a few minutes befor I left to return home (1.5kms away.St Agnes). I kept sight of the object all of the way to my house. Still curious, I returned in my car with my wife to try and follow the object.At this poit the object had moved further away going in a North East direction untill it was out of site. Up untill that night ive never witnessed any strange lights, objects or UFOs.I do believe that there are unexplained events happening.My son also took some camera film footage of the lights/object after I left him.His wife also witnessed the lights/object.I find myself viewing the night sky more often now. Regards.
