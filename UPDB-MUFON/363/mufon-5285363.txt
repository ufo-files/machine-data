At work 5 lights in a V formation flew over Jackson prison complex

While at work managing prison movement/chow lines I saw five glowing objects fly high over the prison. From the northeast fly to the southwest, three of the objects were in a diagonal line formation the other two on the right side were doing odd aerial movements perhaps dodging birds I don't know. They moved back and forth way too fast a split second later the two doing the aerial "dancing" converged with the other three to form a V shaped formation, a few seconds later they were gone, all happened in a matter of 5-10 seconds. Checked cameras and they were flying to high to catch on security cameras. There is an airport a few miles away however no blinking lights just glowing? No sounds. We hear helicopters and airplanes fly over us all the time, so just ODD.
