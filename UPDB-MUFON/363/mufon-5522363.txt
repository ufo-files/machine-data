Landing in forest and glowed on surounding trees

Three of us saw the object going down in forest glowing on tree tops. Called the police. Two officers arrived. The two officers, my brother, friend and myself went toward the area, crossed the bare field, then over the barbed wire fence and interred the forest. We hadnt gone but maybe I suppose a 100 feet when we all heard something coming toward us within the Forrest. The police had weapons drawn as I also did. It was a spooky feeling and the police nor us really felt safe to continue and backed out of the forest quickly. The police said they would have someone fly over the next day. That is the last we heard from them. Before that I had seen on occasion strange lights in the sky at night like moving stars but never reported anything like that until that night seeing something landing and the trees reflecting light! Then I knew for sure something had landed.
