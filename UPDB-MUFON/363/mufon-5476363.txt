Large object in the middle of the day, Nasca

I was returning from the town to the field in the middle of the day, then there was a large shadow over me, so I looked up and saw, perhaps at 300m high, an object, greater than 200m, I saw golden-yellow windows and other green, yellow and red lights.

The object was passing at low speed, and passed over the home next Majoro Hotel, so I run to search my camera, but when I found it, the ufo was no more in the sky. I called too a friend to see, but the object was no longer in the sky.

It was a very sunny day, as is almost all the year in Nasca.

In Nasca I saw ufos at least 3 times.
