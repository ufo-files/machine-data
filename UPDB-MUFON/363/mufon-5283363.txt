Overflight

My brother and I witnessed 2 tic tac shaped white, shimmering objects flying maybe 2 miles away and appearing to maybe 2-3000 feet above ground level. The craft were moving at an extreme rate of speed and made no sound. The objects traveled from west to east a d seemed to "sparkle" as they moved past and we could see the aft ends of the craft. There were absolutely no sounds or any evidence of wings/ control surfaces. My impression is that the objects were moving at supersonic speed. The event was experienced from the upper level parking at Navajo Lake State Park.
