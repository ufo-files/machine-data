{rjl} Three star-like objects with unusual flight paths and hovering, in the NE sky over the Atlantic Ocean at Hollywood Beach, Florida.

We were at Hollywood Beach in the evening, just to get some fresh air and relax by the ocean. My friend noticed and pointed out a star-like star-like object that approached us from the NE sky over the Atlantic Ocean, in a SW flight path, but suddenly stopped and hovered throughout the duration of time we were present at the beach and observed it. Otherwise we do not know how long it remained stationary.

 The second star-like object appeared approx. 5 minutes later, from the NE, close to the same area the first object appeared from. This object flew rapidly in a curved line path, West to Northwest, and disappeared.

 The third star-like object appeared shortly after the second object, by approx. only 2-3 later, materializing from the same NE location, and followed the flight path of the second object, but in an exaggerated, zig-zagging flight path to the extent that it seemed to fly as if it were a fish swimming through the water. It also seemed to have some brightening and dimming and/or pulsation of its light. It disappeared as it bent around the Earth's horizon. 

 I have seen satellites before, but never saw so many fly so close together, demonstrate any zig-zag flight path, pulsate in brightness, and/or suddenly stop and hover.
