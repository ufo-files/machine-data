I witnessed a real Unidentified Flying Object in the air after a high school football game in the 1970's in Sacramento, CA It was so breathtaking.

Norman and UFO-1974~

It was a clear Fall in Sacramento, California, at the Hughes Stadium after a Kennedy vs. Sacramento High football game in the late 70's (1974) when I saw my first UFO. The smooth cigar-shaped craft (300 feet or 100 yards equal to entire football field) hovered for a short period of time at a distance over the parking lot stadium. It had blue and red lights flickering from the sides. It was so obvious that some of the fans (3) looked up at the sky and watched it hummed, stopped, and suddenly disappeared in seconds. It rapidly accelerated to the South and disappeared in 3 seconds. It was approximately 2000 feet = half a mile
