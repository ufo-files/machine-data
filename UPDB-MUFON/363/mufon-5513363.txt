Was standing outside on porch of an apartment waiting for the New Year to
chime in to start our annual fireworks.  It was a few minutes before 12 AM
and we six adults and four children standing looking up to the sky waiting for the first fireworks. The glow appeared what seemed above our yard right where the guys were shooting fire works. It seemed to come a little
closer like it was watching. It did not seem to move for a long time but
just hoovered over. It was slowing shifting east but moving very slow.
It was in the sky what seemed to be at least 15 minutes or more. It just
faded out of sight. No sound, nothing really flashing just bright red glow.
We were laughing at it and someone suggested I call my brother who has 
written two UFO books but I was so freaked out I really thought It was silly because by the time he would get to the sight it would probably be gone. The children were getting a little scared so we just told them it was an airplane to make sure they did not worry. It was very puzzling and
we just sort of brushed it off and came inside. When I came home I tried to call my brother 2 times but the call did not go through. I tried to message him on facebook but my computer froze up. I finally messaged him
and let him know. When I checked by facebook page a friend and her husband
had also seen something wierd last night also. We are all adults and professional working class people who have no need to make up a story like this. It was definitely something different, not a plane of helicopter.
It was indeed a bright red glowing object. We watched until it seem to just fade out of sight.
