{rjl} Bright orange pulsating lights hovering all around us.

There was six of us lastnight driving in a van on Highway 93 South near Ely, Nevada around 9:00pm coming from Boise, Idaho to Las Vegas, NV. I was driving and my girlfriend next to me said she saw a dim light hovering and soon dissapearing. Being in rurual Nevada, right away I said "Keep your eyes open, I bet we can see some crazy stuff out here." Maybe a few minutes later bright orange lights began fading into the sky all in a sequence, then fading out. Some lit up all in a straight line making it seem like one massive craft. They were all around us in different parts of the sky and I was still driving. Everyone was screaming with excitement every time they saw a new light. It was a show! I felt such a rush of adrenaline while feeling both scared and really excited I also had goosebumps. I've never had a UFO experience before and this was definitely something of that nature. The lights seemed mechanical and not like fireworks or military flares. All while these lights were coming in and out of the sky there were several (maybe 10) smaller crafts with dimmer lights hovering very slow and disappearing. I tried flashing my lights a lot to see if I could get some sort of reaction from the lights but then the show stopped and we never saw anything else the rest of the drive. When we arrived in Vegas we looked up military flares and while looking very similar, these lights did not act like flares. The closest thing I can compare them to are the Marfa lights or the Phoenix lights. We did not capture any footage.
