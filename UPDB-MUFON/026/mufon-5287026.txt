Very large bright white round orb flying at an angle toward earth like a shooting star. Stopped and the made sharp left turn and was moving horizontal

Went out on deck to smoke (tobacco). Saw bright, white, round object falling at an angle in a Southwest direction. I thought it was a shooting star or meteorite and was pretty much in awe because I've never seen anything like lt.But then, to my amazement, it stopped falling downward and made a sharp left heading west. It would fly a little ways, lights out and then reappear and repeat the process. Did this 3 times before trees hid my vision. What really.got to me was how precise it's movement was. I thought to myself "there's no way that could be man made". Unfortunately I did not have my phone with me and didn't want to miss anything while retrieving it. I would sure like to know if anyone else has experienced something like this. Of course the first friend I told asked me what I'd been smoking. Oh well. I would draw you a picture but I don't think I could capture the awesomeness. I am no artist.
