Object/s silently flew over head in straight line and at high speed.

Around September 2008 at approx. 1.30am and in the Gleneagles area of Scotland I stopped in a secluded lay-by to "Answer the call of nature!" I had just delivered an item to the Gleneagles Hotel. Whilst standing, an object or objects flew overhead from behind me in a Southeasterly direction at high speed and approx. 500 hundred feet above me. The object/ objects were totally silent and appeared to be two rings side by side and bright "traffic light" green in colour. There did not appear to be any fuselage or structure that the rings were attached to or if they were joined to each other. Both rings were the same size as each other and appeared to be oriented in a verticle position. As the object/ objects were moving at high speed the event only lasted for approx. 5 seconds and disappeared behind hills that were in front of me. A mock-up is attached.
