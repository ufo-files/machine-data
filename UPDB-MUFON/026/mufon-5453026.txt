Four space ships flying in a strait line from Venus to 2 oclock from my position.

My wife [name removed/CMS] and I were enjoying the cool air on the patio at dusk. Four space ships appeared one after another, evenly spaced, from or behind Venus, heading in a strait line to 2 oclock from my position. Their light faded after they reached a position sunlight no longer reflected off the ships toward us. They were in space and appeared to be traveling faster than most sattelites as viewed from the ground. Visitors or ours!?
