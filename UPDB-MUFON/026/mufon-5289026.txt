I was driving my pickup truck at 3:00 am on highway 30 east going to Amboy, and saw a light and thought it was a helicopter because it was too low to be an airplane. I kept watching the light as I neared the town and when I was near the Park in Amboy the lights,
3 or 4 white lights, came over me at about 100' off the ground. I could not see a shape except the the distance of the lights on the object. It was silent and I lost sight of it as it went over the fields. It didn't seem to follow the road or any waterway. It stayed at treetop height about 100' the whole time. It traveled slow and steady at about 10 mph. I felt no heat, warmth or buzzing sensation when it went over me. It was just silent and slow.
