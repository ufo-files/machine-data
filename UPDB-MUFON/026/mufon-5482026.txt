{jpg} ISS was being followed and another craft blocked its path with a brilliant flash of light

I was on my patio at 3:58am watching the space station go by from west to east and noticed a smaller craft or satellite following the ISS and thought that it was a little to close for safety concerns. The object was not as bright as the ISS and was smaller. It was following at exactly the same speed and then I noticed a second object approaching from my right at a 90degree angle and was going straight for the first object behind the ISS. When it got in front of the first object it illuminated to be the brightest light in the night sky for about 2 seconds then I lost sight of all 3 lights because of a tree. When I repositioned my self for a better view the 2 objects in question were gone and I watched the ISS go toward the eastern horizon!
