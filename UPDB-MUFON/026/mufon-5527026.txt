It looks like the gates of Heaven

Me and my husband live in the UAE in Dubai. We were watching tv at our villa and saw that lightning had strated in Dubai. was standing outside filming the beautiful lightning, since this is the first time this year that we see lightning and hear thunder. It was so beautiful so i took a couple of video clips of this beautiful beast of power.

I took a couple of clips and the next day i wanted to send some clips to my mother who lives in another country, so before i sent them i downloaded them to my computer to see which ones are the best and i came across the only video that when the lighting striks you see a huge object in the sky that looks like the gates of heaven,it doesnt move or do anything as far as i can see and exactly a minute passes the lightning striks in the same place and nothing is seen.

It is not a building cause you see on of the tallest buildings lights flashing in the background near the lamp, and another tall building with blue lights on the right, they both look tiny compared to what is seen as the gates of Heaven.

I got really nervous and excited i even asked my husband to double check what i had seen and he too got excited. I don't know what it is exactly and would love to know if this is indeed a ufo.
