{rjl} UFO sighting South Texas

My uncle and I were in the backyard burning some brush. When I first noticed it was moving toward us very slow like hovering about 5-10mph. I thought it was a stealth bomber because the lack of noise and it's triangular shape. It was a black triangular object about 60 to 70 ft across hovering slow toward us then starts going south for about 500 meters then disappears for 5 minutes then we see an orb reappear. At first it's just hovering there erratically start zigzagging across the sky then it goes south towards the Gulf of Mexico and we lose sight of it. 10 minutes later we see 8 helicopters combing the area like they were looking for something. I wasn't scared when I first saw it but I started feeling dread towards the end.
