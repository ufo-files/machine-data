{nd} 2 reptile appearing entities

My daughter observed two humanoid entities in our backyard. She described them as "dragons" walking on two legs. The were reptile like in appearance and were either white in coloration or wearing white apparel. They walked upright on 2 legs - they did not have tails. They did not acknowledge her presense or communicate with her but simply walked behind our shed and then she did not see them again. She was playing on our swingset and it was the middle of the day on a clear sunny day.
