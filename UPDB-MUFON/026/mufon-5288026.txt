Orange orb

The encounter was very similar to my encounter in 2019. I walked out to put away my grill at 1030 pm. When I noticed one orange orb over Tussey Mountain in almost the same location as I did in 2019. I immediately went into my apartment to grab my phone, when I came back out it had dimmed to the brightness of a star. The orb the returned to it's original size and brightness. I stepped out into the yard to take a picture and it dimmed to to the size and brightness of a star again. I watched it for about two minutes then returned to my apartment. I went back outside approximately 5 minutes later and it was gone.
