Weird lights moving rapidly through the clouds whilst changing direction.

Walking the dog at 6am in Droitwich Spa when I noticed lights shining through the heavy cloud cover. At first I thought it was the northern light but think I live way to south for that plus these were more like a spotlight rapidly moving and dancing from side to side. Freeked me out a little so recorded them with my phone, have excellent footage.
Observed for a couple of mins but was already late for work so had to stop filming.
