unidentified object with lights caught on game camera

I have game camera's set out to watch wildlife and on October 7th 2011 at 8:45 PM a object with multiple lights was spotted on 1 of the camera's.. The video is 30 seconds long.. I have viewed this video multiple times and have counted up to 8 lights on it , it appears to spin at times.. As a hunter who has spent countless hours in the fields and timber , I have NEVER witnessed anything like this , in person or on my game camera's.. The object seemed to "float" down from above the camera , came close to landing , then banked to the left and appeared to gain altitude , later in the video it appeared again and started rising , gaining altitude.. There is a deer in the video that seemed bewildered but yet interested in the object.. I am an average person with NO experience with UFO's.. Video needs to be brightened to view the full effect of the sighting..
