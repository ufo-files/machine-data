Lights in sky

Was out looking up to see the ISS and noticed a lesser light beside it matching speed going northeast. At straight up the lessor light and another i thought was a star just took off to the right, south, going halfway across the sky and stopping. They just sat there like stars the rest of the time. The speed was about 3 times faster than the ISS. At the same time 2 others took off in the opp direction, and my friend said to just went out of sight. They accelerated to full speed in a split second and stopped instantly.
