{cak} Group of Flashing/Reflective Objects in Sky

While driving to my home in Collingswood, New Jersey from work in Philadelphia, I noticed a group (10-15) of bright/reflecting objects high in the sky to the West / South West. The date was June 19, 2018 at approximately 6:30 p.m. I was riding my scooter so I quickly pulled over and pulled out my phone to record video. There were also planes in the sky landing and taking off from Phila. Airport that clearly gave the objects scale and distinguished them as unique and strange. The objects were high in the sky, probably at or above cruising altitude for a passenger jet, and were slowly rising away to the West or South West. The objects would get brighter, reflecting the sun, and flash quickly, but it was clear that these were solid objects because even when the reflection or flash subsided I could still see the objects, which appeared to be white or silver in color. Shape was hard to discern but appeared to be a vertical cigar, cylinder, or elongated circle. I took 90 seconds of video on my phone but have had trouble transferring the video to a PC. I watched the objects for approximately five minutes and then drove home (about 1/2 mile away)and could still see the objects briefly from my lawn. I have never seen anything like these objects before or since. Because there were airplanes in the area, including flying through the video, the objects were clearly not airplanes. My guess is that the objects may have been balloons at very high altitude that were reflecting the sun, but these would have to have been very large balloons. I checked local news sources for the following few weeks and nothing was reported on the sighting, but I assume others must have seen it including airline pilots leaving and/or landing at Phila. Airport. I have been unable to get the video off my phone thus far, but if contacted by Mufon I would show it to an investigator and make a greater effort to download the video to another medium.
