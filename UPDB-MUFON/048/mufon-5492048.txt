July 1st, 2015, went outside between 8:20 thru 8:30 pm, first to have a cigarette, I noticed two bright stationary lights where the airplanes usually come in for approach , the one white light was significantly brighter than the 2nd, both were close together, appearing un-moving, no noise, I had the smoke, figured, they were helicopters, after about 5 minutes they didn't leave, I kept coming back out, they were not gone until sometime after 8:30, at 8:30 they were still there, around 9 they were gone. I did get one video, and two photo's, upon reviewing the video I notice a third light in the bottom of the screen, bottom right, near houses ,but it may just be the vantage point, I cannot tell if it is another one of the lights, a plane approach, or what, but it seems to flash a few times, weird. My best estimate on location would be between the western border of south mountain preserve in phoenix near ahwatukee foothills, and lavern, 52nd avenue and estrella, which is over the gila indian reservation, it did not appear to be real far, it did not seem to be north of south mountain, if at all, it seemed to be near the gila indian reservation, almost as if it was over the south mountain but just south of it.
