{cak} Morphing Daytime UFO Sighting

I was playing with neighbors on their driveway, and noticed a chrome saucer shaped object in the sky. I was excited, and ran to it because I was very enthusiastic about UFO's at the time. It looked like it either was hovering over the MN International Airport, or Bloomington Avenue (I was on Chicago). It looked to morph reality, going behind a tree, and not going the other way, like an illusion. I was playing with other kids at the time, and I don't know what they thought when they saw my excitement. It also had fuzzy edges.
