{rjl} Observed green fireball descending at a wide angle toward a river apparently landing in the water

While working as a cook at local restaurant, my head chef and i took out the trash at the close of our shift. The back deck area of this restaurant gives a great view of the northern section of Tarrytown. Specifically the hudson river. While working outside my chef suddenly exclaimed"*my name* look at that!" As i turned my head left i then noticed a green fireball shooting across the sky illuminating a small area of the dark part of the city. It emmited a redish/orange trail, but dit not show any debris, breaking off of it. I have seen meteors in the past but this seemed much larger, about a half, to a third of the size of a compact car; moved slower, glowed brighter and actually seemed to have landed in / around the hudson river. Possibly where Gory brook meets the hudson. I couldnt tell if it just stopped glowing (burned out if a meteor) and went into darkness, or if it collided with the water. It also seemed to move in kind of an arc rather than a strait line, but this may have just been my viewing angle. My observation of the object lasted about 7 seconds, not counting the few my chef saw it and told me to look. It seemed to have traveled from north east to south west at something like a 20 degree angle. My best guess was that it was traveling at over 100 mph but i cant be sure. I'm certainly not an expert my first thoughts were a mix of ufo, or just a meteor. But as i said ive seen shooting stars/ meteors before, always at high altitudes and yellowish in color. Also commercial airliners were visible after witnessing the object at much higher altitudes. Thought id report it somewhere, just in case. My chef jokingly said must be a ufo, i didnt really know what to think
