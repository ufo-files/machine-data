I walkded out of my buildings to the front porched for a smoke break, was prob 730-745 pm saturday the 12th 2013. It was already dark and the street im on has a couple of street lights out so you could see the sky little more clear than normally at night. I was smoking and just kinda spacing out looking up and i noticed an object coming from the south west. I figured was a plane because PDX is south east of my location, maybe ten or so miles away. Generally the air traffic travels oppsite path that the object was traveling so i watched it about 3-4 mins come from the south east over the top of the buildin and continue in north east direction. It did fairly close but the camera on my iphone like usual was being useless and had tough time keeping hand steady, fortunatly after it passed over head and was maybe a couple hundred feet away i was able to get a decent shot. It moved like how a car moves on ice, like there was no wind resistance slowin it down or drag. It made no noise and was solid object, didn't flicker or morph in any way. After passing over head it slowly blended in with sky and that was that. August 2007
