Extremely bright lights.

I was walking home along the river just after dark and suddenly noticed 3-5 very bright lights appear in front of me about 100 yards ahead.
I stopped thinking it was a cyclist or jogger approaching me or someone with a torch.
The lights went out and no body was around.
Yet they were weird looking, very bright and staggered in different levels and all close to the ground about 6-8 ft from the ground.
They disappeared and within a minute an extremely bright green light reapeared directly infrort of me.
I quickly slid out my camera phone and hit the red button and zoomed into it.
Then a red coloured light moved out towards it, intensifies itself with some amazing bright energy's making a very obscure noise before what looks like the green light merges into it and they vanished or disapeared or went invisible again.
I managed to get 25 seconds of this.
I've as yet no idea of what these were.
