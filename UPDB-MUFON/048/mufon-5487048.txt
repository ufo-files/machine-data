{cak} ufo appeared from behind hill thought it was a helicopter until it shot up into the sky at tremendous speed

was attending the Bufflo Party Convention in Eatonville Wa. 4th of july weekend 1970 which was a illegal rock festival I mentioned that because police helicopters had been buzzing the site for two days so when this object appeared from behind a hill I thought it was another police helicopter except there was no sound it moved slowly to the northern edge of site about 180 yards away an stopped it had whitish blue search lights but not like what search lights usually look like still thought it was a police helicopter until it shut off its search lights and shot up into the sky faster than I could move my head a friend of mine next to me said "did you see that??" I was totally flabbergasted. I watched the object disappear in to the stars.
I know this happened 45 years ago but I thought you might find it interesting and can add it to other sightings in that area during this period of time also this has been a ufo hotbed since the 60s a radio station attempted to communicate with ufos from this area during the early 60s found out later.
