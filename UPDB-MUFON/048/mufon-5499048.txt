{rjl} I looked straight up thru trees and saw what I thought was a star, except it was too early for any stars to be out yet. It stayed in that spot, but quite glowing & was just a ball in the sky, it then changed to two balls, then three then faded away

I was talking with my neighbor & happened to look straight up through the hole in the trees at what I originally thought was a star. It glowed bright like one, but then i realized it was still to early for any stars to be out yet. The sun was still rather high above the mountains. I kept my eye on it as I sent my daughter in to grab my phone to film it. It then changed into just a grayish/white ball in the sky. Then it changed to two balls in the sky then three. Finally it just disappeared. All this was about 15 mins total. I was unable to get anything on my phone. My daughter says she saw it. She called it an orb.
