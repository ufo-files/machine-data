{nd} Round object traveled in level flight, no contrail and was white to silver in color with bright spectral quality to center of object

I was taking my morning 4-mile walking exercise and was proceeding north. It was a bright sunny day with only a few cumulus clouds in the sky. Abruptly to the Northwest at about 35-degrees off the horizon, a moving object about the size of a beebee at arms length caught my eye. It was mostly whitish with a silvery center that seemed to glow. I presumed it was an aircraft that was reflecting the bright sunlight that was behind me. However, the object never changed shape in appearance as it traveled North-to-Northeast in level flight with no contrail. The object continued to appear round and whitish in color with a highly reflective center that seemed silvery. I stood still to watch it for about 2-minutes until it disappeared behind a tree line. At all times it flew above the few clouds that were in the sky and there were no other aircraft in my view. Air temperature at ground level was in the 50s at that time of day and winds were under 10 mph out of the Northeast that morning. I'd estimate that the object's distance from me would be over a mile since it was further away than the nearest clouds which were about a half-mile from me to the Northwest. What struck me as odd or unusual about the object was that it never changed it's apparent round shape or coloring. Over the next few days I made a point of observing aircraft in the sky and it was apparent to me that they significantly change their apparent shape and the amount of light they reflect when they are at altitude and flying in bright sunlight.
