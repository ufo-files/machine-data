As a amateur astronomer I saw the sky was clear, moonless night when I saw what I thought was a polar satellite. But it was going too fast. Thinking it must be a high flying plane, with sunlight reflecting but no, as soon as I was calculating it's speed, it abruptly stopped. I was in shock, as if it saw me looking at it. Then, like I said, it stayed still for about 5 seconds, then jiggled very fast like it was trying to escape from jello, for 5 seconds, then shot away from earth straight out into space, just like a bullet. I have told numerous people. That was NOT anything we have on earth. I believe it was searching for the right gravitational pull-out.
