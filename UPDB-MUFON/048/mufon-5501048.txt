{cak} My cousin's girlfriend was taking photos after a big storm had passed and this object kept appearing in the pictures.

These photos were taken during the day after a big storm had passed over lake Wentworth, NH in June of 2012. The person shooting the photos is the long time girlfriend of my second cousin. She told me that the objects were not visible to the eye but showed up after each photo was taken. She said she noticed the objects in the camera while shooting. We've known about these photos for a while but it wasn't until I was going over the photos with her last week that we noticed in the picture where the object becomes much darker and thinner that two orbs appear horizontally to the right of the object. This made the photo more convincing to me and we decided to submit it to mufon. The photographer of this photo made it very clear that there were no birds or insects in the immediate and general area while she was taking photos of the storm.
