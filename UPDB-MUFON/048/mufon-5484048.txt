{cak} There were 2 UFO's. One red, one green. Perfect Sphere's, no outer lights on either one. No sound.

This camera doesn't do this object any justice. It was a large, red perfect sphere that made zero noise. It was glowing like it had a force field around it. I couldn't see any interior or exterior lights. No discernible outlines or shapes. It started just east of my house around 500 feet up heading SE. I don't know how far SE it went, maybe 5-10 miles before it started to descend beneath the tree line. After it went down beneath the trees another one appeared right where the red one had first started. This one was green but I was only able to get a picture of it while it was moving. This one went a lot faster and I had no time to get a video. I hope you can bring this to someone so that they can clean it up for you. It was amazing and the third time in my life I have encountered UFO's. Later that same evening there was also a very large, slow, low flying plane that flew over our town. It seemed like a cargo plane to me. My oldest son and my wife were also there to witness the sightings.
