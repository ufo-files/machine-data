I went to Daniel Boones National Forest in Kentucky one day on April 11th 2006. I took a few pictures over a valley. Upon reviewing them I noticed an anomaly. I took 4 pictures no more than 10 seconds apart. Later at home I was reviewing all the pictures on my computer. I notice what looked like a water tower about a mile off in the distance. I thought "theres a water tower in a national forest?" so I looked for it in the next photo, to my surprise it wasnt there. Not in any of the other three photos. Only in the first pic I had taken. It was a beautiful day. The sun was shining & there wasnt a cloud in the sky. The photo shows a metallic orb about a mile or two in the distance ascending? descending? Hovering? above the tree canopy down in the valley. A rare treat indeed! Im thoroughly convince this is not an insect & indeed a Ufo. Another "metallic orb" just like the others that have been spotted worldwide. Anyway, its been a great conversation piece over the years. I figure I should post them for you to check out. I still have the original photos & the photos on cameras memory card.
