{nd} Large triangle shape flying extremely fast no lights very low no sound or trail

While outside me and a friend was testing out my new telescope trying to focus in on Saturn. We was out there for around an hour or so trying to figure it out. I was almost dialed in on saturn with the site scope when my friend quietly but urgently told me to look and nudged me. When I looked at what he was pointing at in the sky at first thinking a shooting star. But this was no shooting star. We saw a triangle shaped ship flying very fast and low. It was really like a glide with no noise no lights. It was only about 5 seconds but long enough for us both to tell it was something neither one of us was not supposed to see. It was almost like in a camoflauge/silhouette in the night sky. I'd say it was about 400ft wide and 300ft long. I can't say it is or is not related but I have had some weird physical and physiological events following this encounter.
