{cak} small translucent craft chased by a blacked-out military style helicopter

At about 10pm that evening,the wife and I had just stepped out front of our house to let the dogs out when we heard the sound of a helicopter approaching from the east.When the sound from the helicopter grew increasingly louder,we quickly realized that it was flying extremely low over our moderately populated neighborhood.We rushed out to the driveway to witness a "blacked-out"(it did have its red and green tail lights on) military style helicopter in close pursuit of what can only be described as a small(maybe half the size of the helicopter),quiet,translucent craft with what appeared to be a dimly lit orange off-center light.We were able to see the edges of this "craft",and can best describe it as maybe teardrop in shape.In about 30 seconds time,we watched these two vehicles move in a straight line trajectory,from east to west, across the night sky and out of our line of sight.My immediate thought was that what we had just witnessed was clearly an extraterrestrial vehicle being chased/followed by a military aircraft.As Im sure you can imagine, we were both in utter shock and amazement,and weve spent the last 24 hours scouring the news/internet for any info on this "event".So far,we havent seen anyone else report this "event",but I cant imagine that we were the only ones to have witnessed this happen due to how low and loud this helicopter was.
