{nd} I was seven yrs old, through my bedroom I window saw a round light outside, moving left, right, down and up behind the trees. It was about midnight.

The details are a little fuzzy and memory faded, but when I was seven yrs old living in Mabelvale Arkansas with Dad and Mom, I was asleep and then woke up to see a strange round light outside moving around behind the trees in the back of the property. The light was a soft white color and moved left to right and then dropped down and rose up again and vanished. I was scared and ran across the hall into my older sisters room for comfort. The next day I noticed a scab on the left side of my lower back. scar remains there after all these years, it is about 10 mm in size. I have only seen the scar in a mirror since I cant directly view this area of my back. I have no idea if this mark on my back has anything to do with the light I saw.
