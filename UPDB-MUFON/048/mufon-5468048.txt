{jpg} round diffused large pulsating light producing random on and off, angular high speed flight

U.F.O., co -witnessed 5.10.2018
  
 Burnsville, N.C., 28714                  Elevation here 3000 ft.
 Time; 10:15 to 10:25 p.m.,(2215-2225)  distance about 2-3 miles

 Narrative: while taking the dog out,saw an object in the sky South of the house, called Dona out to see it.
 
At an altitude of approximately 12,000 to 15,000 feet, was a large round light which pulsed and turned on and off at random intervals, the light was diffused and the object producing it could not be seen, only the light.
  
The object changed altitude abruptly and rapidly to about 1000 ft. lower at oblique angle then moved westerly about 1000 ft. the pulsing light varied in intensity and turned off and then on again, (e.g., on 10 seconds off 5 seconds, etc.), we watched this for fifteen minutes and finally it went dark and disappeared from view.
 
I am a pilot with fixed wing and helicopter ratings, she is a registered nurse and this is a true account of what we witnessed on that evening.
