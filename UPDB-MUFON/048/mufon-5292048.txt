6-7 ufos came in and out of fog and slowly traveler from west to east over Richmond area.

My girlfriend and I witnessed 6-7 ufos from her rooftop. She says 7 but i thought it was just 6. It was hard to tell how many because there was a bit of low clouds/fog and the ufos would get dim when passing through the cloud cover. They were all in formation flying from west to east over the Bay near the Richmond CA area. The first ufo had a reddish color but the rest were white. They were all orb shaped. We saw two airplanes fly near the ufos and we could tell they were airplanes and looked nothing like the orbs/ufos. That is what struck us as most odd at first but what was very unique was the second ufo in formation had what looked like a light beam or something lighter in color coming out of the bottom about 3 times the length of the orb then just stopping. My girlfriend is more skeptical than I am but we both agreed this was one of the weirdest things we had ever seen in the sky. We’re very curious if anyone else saw this. I took a photo but the orbs did not show up.
