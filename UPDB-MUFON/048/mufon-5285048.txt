Looked up at the sky and there was two lights moving.

One night i just needed to go sit by the water and relax at a park that I always went to since I was little. I sat on the bench looking straight ahead of me I saw two lights moving differently than a plane would. It got my attention so I kept looking, the light on the left began to move up and would fall down a little bit to even out with the light on the right. It kept doing this over and over like it was practicing or something. Then it starts to swirl around in circles not spinning then fall a little then back up again. I tried to look up the traffic control on my phone and my phone was blank like it was shut off. Then all of the sudden two big V shapes with lights came over my head and the two lights followed it. I looked down at my phone and had missed calls from my kids asking why I was taking so long.
