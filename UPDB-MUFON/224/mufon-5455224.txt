{rjl} Bright green football shape with green flash shooting down and back up.

My friend and I were driving dump trucks to a site to dump ash around 5am it was still dark out and I noticed a green light above a small hill top and asked if he saw it ,he said yes, we watched it for about 2 or 3 miles as we approached it you could see it was a football shape, as we watched it, it had what appeared to be a green flash shooting down to the top of the hill and then it shot back up to the green football shaped object, we lost site of the object as we drove away to the dump site but when we came back to the area about a half hour later as it was starting to get day light now it was still there and we observed it for another 10 or 15 minutes before we drove away but it remained in the same place till we couldn't see it anymore. We didn't have camera's at the time but I can remember it like it was yesterday as long as it was there other people had to have witnessed this total time we know it was there had to be at least an hour and it's a wide open area I'll attach Google pics of the area.
