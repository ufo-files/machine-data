{rjl} 3 white objects in sky.Far right object disappeared first, other 2 followed each other at close range, then separated before flying toward one another. Then vanished in air.

I was traveling to San Antonio from Yoakum, TX. Halfway between Yoakum,TX and Gonzales, TX, I was distracted by a bright white shape so i looked out my window and saw 3 white objects hovering in the sky. The far left and middle objects were extremely close, while the far right object was a greater distance away. My first thought was it may be an airplane but after looking closer I realized that there was no way because the objects were extremely too close together. The far right object disappeared instantly. The other 2 followed one other at close range for a few minutes before coming to a complete stop. They then separated quickly. They then instantly flew toward one other. Finally, they flew straight toward each other and once it looked like they collided, I saw a bright light flash straight up towards the sky. They were gone in an instant. I waited awhile to see if I saw them again but I did not. My mouth dropped open and I was shocked as to what I just saw. This sighting made me feel deeply confused and wanting an explanation to what I just saw. This sighting took place over acres of farmland in the country.
