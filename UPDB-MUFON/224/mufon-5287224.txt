Navy plane flies over object

While leaving the base heading to the truck gate I saw a object above the trees to my left. I was pulling over to get a picture. Before I was able to spot a navy jet flew over the object about 50 ft. The object then started rising. When I got out of the truck the object was gone. I was in the Air Force when I was younger and have seen all kind of aircraft. This was like nothing I’ve ever seen.
