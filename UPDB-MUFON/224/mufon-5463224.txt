{cak} Light hovering in woods north of house. Noticed once last week. Seen again tonight. Walked out into woods, nothing was seen. Occurred when we were young as well

At parents house. We took the dogs outside and my brother noted a light in the trees north of our house. The light hovered in the woods, not to high off the horizon, but could be seen through the tree branches as there are no leaves on the trees. The light appeared to be moving, not much from our position but enough to be blocked by a branch then reappear.
The week prior I had noticed a light north of our house before sunset. We also saw this light one Christmas over 5 years ago. My brother walked the dog and then we walked out into the woods to look for the light. By this time the light had disappeared from view. Will investigate the sky around the same time tomorrow to look for the light.
We were nervous and a bit creeped out when we saw the light. It was too big to be a plane or a star, and was not moving fast enough to be a shooting star.
