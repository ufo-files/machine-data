{cak} I observed a silent wingless craft with no windows in broad daylight for about 7 minutes. There were other commercial aircraft in the immediate area.

I saw a Wingless cigar shaped craft with no markings or windows in cloudless conditions over Vouliagmeni Athens Greece while taking my children to the playground immediately below Vouliagmeni suites hotel. I took 17 seconds of footage with an iPhone 7 and a few pictures as it left the area. It’s shape was distinctly cigar shaped and white metallic with no engines no wings and no windows and flying very slowly as it changed course and flew over the sea. No noise at all. It was only slightly higher in altitude than the descending commercial aircraft on approach to Athens international airport. It would easily be visible on radar from air traffic control and visible from approaching aircraft coming from the SE. It was about 10 meters long and perfectly symmetrical. Our position is west from the airport, maybe 20kms.
