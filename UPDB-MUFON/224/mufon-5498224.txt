{dr}I was in front yard and saw a saucer above me hovering. It then landed and they surrounded my house and took me. I was gone from 12pm until 10pm. They did surgery on me.

I was taken by at least two entities. I think they were wearing suits that were of a rouge red in color. One was taller than the other, slender with longer arms than we have. They came back a few times to at least four different homes over a period of years. My German shepherd bit one of them at the time of the second occurence. I was taken into a saucer and was subject to surgery. I didn't know about the surgery until years later when I was at the Drs. office having an exam. The doctor asked my mom who had done the surgery on me and mom said I had not had any surgery. They made me forget most of the abduction but I do remember that we were following a car on a mountain road that first night they took me. I was told telepathically that I would be okay then they took me home. I'm pretty sure they had taken my Grandmother also. At the second time My Mother was held frozen in place at the top of the stairs and her hair was standing out from her head like static electricity. She was yelling for my Grandmother to help her. She couldn't move for a few minutes. Last contact was in 2008 in my home in Florida. They did some sort of scanning of my body. It made me feel Euphoric.
