At dinner photos taken showed a red object shown on camera but not seen by the naked eye.

My husband and I were at dinner at Puerto Madero- Buenose Aires- steak and fish house in Cancun Mexico on 9/14/2012 about 8pm (loacal time). We sat outside, bay view. As usual I take a pictures of our view when we first sit down. Then again when I was done with my meal while my husband was still eating,so I took more pictures of our view- a photo had a red spot on a picture I took,which we could see on my camera, but with the naked eye I couldn't see the red spot in the same spot. So I pointed it out to my husband,he agrees, then he snaps a photo and the red spot moved upwards again we could see the spot on the camera but not with our naked eye. We take another photo and again the spot moved to the right. Now the employees of the restaurant is interested in what we are talking about and we tell them they too agree they cannot see the red spot with their naked eye but see it on my camera.I continue to take pictures,there are some that have nothing and there are a couple that have white spots.
