Moving star above Brisbane Australia.

My wife and I went outside to look for any residual meteors from the recent shower. We saw a star that seemed to be moving in all directions. I fixed the top of a palm tree in my periferal vision and this proved that it was indeed moving. The object was at an exremely high altitude and was changing in colour slowly, whilst moving in arcs and straight lines. Its movement was slow, similar to the speed of a satelite but it changed direction often and after watching it for an hour it is still above as I write this. Its movement seemed to be within a range of 10 degrees in all directions if that makes sense, if we are talking 360 degrees. I rang my brother who lives about 30 km from me and he can see it too.
