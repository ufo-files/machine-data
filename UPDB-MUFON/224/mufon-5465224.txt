Driving to work heading east on Gore Rd at Verterans... saw star like object on horizon

It was 515am and I had just grabbed my morning coffee before driving to work. I was heading east on Gore Rd at Veterans Memoral Parkwy when I noticed a bright light a few inches up and to the left of the horizon .. meh, not a big deal as I see stars and planets up there all the time on a clear morning. I bit weird his morning as it was cloudy and cold out...

As I drove another few minutes, the object became larger and brighter and moved more left from my perspective, until it was directly to my left -about 1km away and about 1000 feet up. I pulled over to the side, stopped the car, and put my window down to take a better look. This thing had lights all over it and was rectangle shaped. Just hovering there. Totally weird

I watched it for about 5 seconds and then it just started moving moving westward then it sped off in a NW direction and then it zoomed away and disappeared.
