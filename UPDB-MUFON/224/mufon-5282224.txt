Triangle craft appeared suddenly overhead

Me and two of my neighbors listed in report were out looking for a missing baby calf on their ranch at approximately 10pm. It was a pitch black night with almost zero moonlight.

The three of us were walking on foot through draws looking for the calf when we all met up on top of a hillside to talk. While talking we felt the ground start to vibrate a little bit. We thought that there were possibly cattle or something stampeding towards us so we shined our flash lights around. And did not see anything.

The dog then started acting odd & laid down whimpering a bit. When we noticed a sort of dull light above us. As we looked up directly above us we saw three green lines of light bars one at each corner of the triangle shaped object. It also had a bluefish white light orb in the direct center of the craft.

As I mentioned there was no moonlight so I could see not the outline of the craft from that only from its own lights. It was about 150-200 feet above us & appeared to be 50-80 feet long. 

The ground began to vibrate harder & Alex shined his light up at it. At that moment I thought to myself what the hell are you doing & started running as did they quickly behind me. We ran down across the draw up to another hill side by our fence line where we parked our trucks & looked back & watch the craft hover over the horizon & blink out in a bright flash & disappear.
