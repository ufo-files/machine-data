{cak} I saw a shadow, it was an outline of the front of a craft with the stars rippling in front as it moved and either disappeared, or became invisible.

I arrived home from work at about 11:39pm on Tuesday night, August 12th. I parked my car in the garage, then did a couple of other things, and paused in the driveway, looking at the sky, to see if I could observe any of the Perseid meteor shower. I saw stars and the moon, then, as I was looking West, I saw what appeared to be a ripple in the starlit sky, it was not directly above me, it was somewhat in front of me, to the west. I could make out just the front of a short snub nose with wings out to the sides. I couldn't perceive any other part of the craft, but the very front, because that is where the stars appeared to ripple along a dark, shadowy line. The rest of it looked like the normal, starry sky. I didn't hear anything. I watched it for about 20-30 seconds and it appeared to be moving slowly, blimp-like. Then, it either disappeared, or became completely invisible to me. I could not tell how large it was, or how far away, as it was barely perceptible.
