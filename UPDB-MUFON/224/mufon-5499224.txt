Walked across the street to talk to some neighbors, while talking I happen to look up at this tree and see a bright round looking light out of nowhere, as soon as I seen it, it shot off in a straight path over the top of the neighbors house. I started yelling "Did you see that!!! Did you that!!!" Running to the street to see if I could still see it, but it was gone..within 10 seconds. It looked as if it was maybe 2ft above her house. They didn't see a thing, that's how fast it happened. First thought it was a shooting star, but I have never ever seen a shooting star that hovered at the tree top then shot off over the top of a house. Scared the hell out of me!
