I went outside to have a smoke and observed a very bright star like object

I went outside to have a smoke around 21.00hrs and observed a very bright star like object above the house. It had originated I assume from the north and took a steady route towards the southeast. I called my mum out she witnessed the event also. I initally assumed it to be the ISS so i grabbed my tablet to track the station. The ISS was in fact over germany at the time. What struck me was the intense silver /white light it eminated, through my monocular it appared to be a tear drop shape on its side (pointy end to the rear of direction of flight). I am an avaition entusiast and dissmised my mums opinion that it was an aircraft. No noise or navigation lights were seen. it seemed too low to be a satalite and far too bright. Mars can be seen to the west at the moment and is very bright but this object out shone all. the object continued on its transverse across the azimuth until it dissapeared into the low cloud on the SE horizon.NB I was witness to a similar event about 3 years ago at around 4 in the morning but I had no App available to see if it was the ISS. I am referencing the station due to its similar apperance I would go as far as to say this was Lower/ brighter in the night sky. Could it be a low satalite or Rocket body?
