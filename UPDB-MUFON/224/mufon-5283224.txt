Hovering circle of 5 white lights. Slow rotation.

1989. Working as master control technician at local television station. I was required to check and document the stations tower lights every hour per FAA regulation. I checked the lights at the top of the hour at 11 PM. I saw a silent group of hovering/rotating lights passing the area of the top of the satellite tower. The lights moved slowly past the tower light and moved behind the hillside behind the station.
