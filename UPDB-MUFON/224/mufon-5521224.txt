Glowing object over South Lake Tahoe

Glowing, pulsating object over South Lake Tahoe as seen from Minden, Nevada in a valley to the east. Appeared as an unusually bright star with erratic pulsating. Through binoculars there appeared to be a red vertical ring that the pulsating light tracked around. Hovered relatively in place with slightly sporadic movements in all directions. Ascending and descending in slight movements. After about 40 minutes the object appeared to be moving away to the west or fading out of sight over the course of about a minute or so.
