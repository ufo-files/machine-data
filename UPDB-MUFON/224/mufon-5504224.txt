{cak} 30 far away.... 1 really close. dim light. silent.

Visiting friend in 1993.... on roof because he said strange things were seen.  Saw 30 far away objects at high speeds... acute,crazy paths.... all seemed to descend in nearby forest.... then an hour or so later... saw one that was twenty feet away hovering over barn in backyard. Dim spotlight went arond once and then it disappeared. We were freaked out. He had seen the far away ones before but not one that close. Dim dull light also was shone onto barn by the close one. Can elaborate if someone calls me. The thing that still haunts me about it is the fact that all of this was silent. No motors.... nothing. His house is literally in the middle of nowhere. No neighbors.
