unknown object that was smoking and had flame and crashed

I live in a small community in Costa Rica. I am 25 miles north west on San Ramon and 20 miles east of Fortuna. On Sunday, Oct. 31 I noticed high up in the sky a dark vertical line. I watched it descend and as it did I could see that the line was smoke and at the bottom was an abject. I got my camera and took photos of this object. As it got lower the smoke lessened and only after I looked at the photos and blew them up could I see that their was a flame source in this object. Eventually it went out and the object, which looked like a hot air balloon basket expanded on the bottom. I don't think this thing was a UFO. I think it was made of some kind of pliable material as once the flame went out the opening expanded. I have checked the papers and the news and their is no record of this thing. I'd like to send my photos and tell me if you can identify it. It was at least the size of a hot air balloon basket or larger. It came down from a great height. I have no idea where this thing landed. I was on the second story balcony and it disappeared behind the trees across the street. The trees are approx. 1,000 ft from where I was. I just need to know what I was looking at. I didn't think weather balloons had a propane tank on them. Other than that, I don't know what it could be. I'd appreciate if you could identify what I was seeing. Thank you.
