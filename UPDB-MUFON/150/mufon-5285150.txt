Stationary hovering object with bright lights maybe 75 feet above the ground. Approximate size of two cars.

My wife and I were out for a drive this evening in Fargo. We were going South on University approaching the Main Street intersection. When we were just north of main we both noticed what we initially thought was a life flight or helicopter hovering just east of the Tailgaters building. As we were approaching closer and crossing main we could clearly see the beam of light flashing from what we still thought was a helicopter to the ground. AS we approached and now were even/next/nearly underneath the object we realized it was not a helicopter. It made no noise at all. It was brightly lit, and it was literally just hovering what we said was 50-100 feet off the ground, we agreed probably about 75 feet off the ground. We both saw the object very clearly. It was a solid object, it definitely had a mass and volume. It was not shaped like anything I could imagine would be capable of flying. As we got even with and could now see it broad sided, it was longer than wide, had distinct different sections, and seemed to have a glowing dull but reddish lighted bottom. -Very hard to explain. As we were broadside to the object we both said that's not a helicopter, and it occurred to me what I was seeing and I began screaming my wife said... I don't know how to explain what we just saw, but to say, THIS WAS A VERY REAL OBJECT, and I do not believe what we just saw is made by man. This was LITERALLY out of the blue! We saw the object for probably 5 seconds straight, and it literally then just disappeared...
