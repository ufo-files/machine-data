{nd} White light, red lights.

About 2-3 times per week during the last 3 weeks, another adult and I have witnessed from our home, a moving bright, white light with with tiny red, dull lights flying around it about 500+ feet west of our home (approximate) and above the tree line, too high for it to be coming from someone's home. Sometimes the red lights go out and reappear in different areas and travel continuosly in various directions, too fast for any plane or helicoptor. On a couple of occassions we saw the red lights meet up with the bright light. The bright light moves slowly in random directions and also at times moves several feet up and down. At times it will drop qulckly. After about 45 minutes or so it just disappears. It usually starts between 8-9pm and is gone by 9:30p. When I first saw these lights, I was washing dishes and saw them out my kitchen window, At first I thought the bright light was a star but then realized the sky was overcast and there were no other stars visable. Also it was brighter and larger than the average star and it was moving. Then I saw the tiny red lights flying aroud near it. I was so fightened, I reported it to the local police. We have investigated in the area that looks like it may be coming from but can find nothing that would cause this. We've tried to take photos but they are too far away.
