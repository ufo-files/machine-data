While sitting on my back deck wearing binoculars searching for meteorites mostly. I noticed a movement upward and over to my right. The weather was calm, no moon, no wind. Stars were very bright. I watched this huge black mass block out the stars and go slow just above the tree line. As I looked with my binoculars up at the undercarriage i could make out what looked like pipes etc. (Just like looking up at the undercarriage of the space ships on star wars and alien.) I watched it move out of sight. I called the airport at Richard's Field in Massena and asked them if they had anything on radar? They told me no. But I knew it was most likely below radar.the ship had no noise as well but what looked like a slight link his which radiated from the undercarriage, enough so that I could make out the so called pipes underneath.not to be funny but it was shaped like a T-Bone Steak, that's all I could compare the shape to.
