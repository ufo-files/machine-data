{jpg} I woke up from sleep, went to my window and saw several colorful things moving through the sky at a speed and with angles not possible by anything I know

I was in Bradley, Maine. Off Main Street. It was very early in the morning/ late at night. I think somewhere around 230, I was deep asleep- and just woke up, not sure why. But I went to my window sill, it is big and I can sit on the railing of it- so I was. I noticed outside in the air above the treeline and very far up, lights moving, going around everywhere and near each other. sometimes looking like they were touching each other, I watched them for probably 2 hours, I kept thinking to go grab my phone to record, but I didn't? I also keep meaning to wake up early and check, but it's like it never enters my mind at those times anymore. My grandfather who lives on the same street as me has seen these lights a lot, and was so happy I finally saw them too, but also finds them to be creepy, so do I... I felt very tired, interested, but hypnotized. I kept saying to myself, grab your phone and record this, but I didn't and I still cant figure out why I didn't. Very strange behavior for me. I never lost sight, I eventually went back to my bed to sit down and think this is so weird, and I feel back asleep. When I woke up I ran off to go tell someone very close to me about it.
