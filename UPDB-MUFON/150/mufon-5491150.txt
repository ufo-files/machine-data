{rjl} Appeared to be a satellite until it stopped and changed directions

I routinely smoke a cigarette at night on my deck. While outside, I like to watch the stars and see if I can observe any shooting stars or satellites. I first observed the object approximately 4:15 am, and I thought it was a satellite, until it slowed down, came to a complete stop, then changed direction. Numerous times it changed direction, sped up, slowed down, stopped, then kept wiggling around the sky. It was in a high orbit, I'd guess about as high as the satellites I view regularly. Also, several times I saw it reflect the light from the rising sun.
