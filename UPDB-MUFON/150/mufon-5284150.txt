Invisible craft made air displacement sound as it flew by

I was in the front yard of my home when I heard a loud sound coming from the NE direction. It sounded like air being displaced by a semi large object. I physically tracked the sound with my head and eyes as it was unmistakably something there. It tracked from right to left and was easy to track with my ears which let me also track with my eyes. A little slight distortion behind it as it traveled. Almost like a trail of disturbed air. I have heard bullets zip by my head and the object was much slower and larger. Totally invisible craft. No engine noise. Only air displacement.
