I was watching footage from my home security camera as I do every morning. I noticed a bright streaking light headed down to the ground. At first I thought it may be fireworks, but it was -5 degrees out at the time, and this was the only streak of light. And I also did not hear any sounds of fireworks or flares at that time. Then I thought it could have been a shooting star, but it was way too close, and it would be traveling horizontally and not downwards. I lost track of the object after it disappeared off the screen. I11m not a huge conspiracy theorist, but this is definitely one of the strangest things I have seen and I can usually have a explanation for everything I see firsthand. I have shown my wife, and a couple close friends, and they all agreed that this is a weird event.
