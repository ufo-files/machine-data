Cylindrical object falling from Eastern Sky

While looking for Saturn/Jupiter in the sky, I saw something bright, which I thought was a star or planet. I took the picture and when I blew it up, it was a cylindrical mass falling from the sky. It left a trail behind which I could not see until I looked at the picture. When Ienlarged the picture further, it looked like a half a cylinder with a cored out middle. It was golden colored.
