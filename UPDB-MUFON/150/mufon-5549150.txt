Glowing sphere changing size and color

My wife came running into the house talking about a glowing ball above the field across from us. We went outside together to look. The moon was full or close to it and bright. This sphere thing was much brighter it looked to be about 2 miles away. It changed size and color. There was no pattern to this. At arms length away it was the size of the head of a pin, and then just like that it tripled in size. This continued the entire time it was visible. At the same time it kept changing color. I counted 10 or 11 different colors. It never went in any particular order as the colors changed. Before it vanished, it dipped to the right about 5mm at arms length, then to the left and up about 8mm. Then it disappeared.
