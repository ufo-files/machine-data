This happened in Southern Indiana--Floyd Co

EST/Central time zones-SO.IN, 1cigar shaped light appeared--following my dad's car. On n.e. horizon, dad stopped car--light stopped also--rolled down windows of car on passenger side--not a reflection. Dad started moving car--light followed and went behind some trees--2 lights emerged from trees--dad stopped car--lights stopped. Dad driving again, lights moving again. The 2 lights again went behind trees; 3 emerged and followed us (there were 5 of us in the car)almost until we got to New Albany. We were traveling on Old Vincennes Rd. from Pekin, In to New Albany. Summer nights were a little longer because of being right on the 2 time zones. It was early evening, summer, the sun had not gone down, but it was early dusk to twighlight. I was about 9 and can remember the event as though it happened yesterday. My sister remembers my dad mentioning something about the Airforce or the government, but I never saw glowing cigar shaped lights that stopped when our car stopped like that before or since. Were experiments being carried out? Were there other sightings in that area in that time frame? It was '67 or '68. I was 9 or 10. I will never forget it, but there was definitely no missing time. We first noticed it over the old, old graveyard on the road--just there suddenly. I thought it was a ufo right away--we had been hearing about them in the news. For awhile during my adult life, I thought maybe they were Ghosts(?) because of the proximity of the graveyard. My parents never talked about it, but I can still see those lights as though it happened yesterday--all 3 lights went behind trees on the horizon just before we got to the city limits of New Albany and never came out. We didn't see them leave except to go behind trees.
