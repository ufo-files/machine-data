Seen to fly in house through wallfinal report

I had dropped my daughter off came into the house. Approx 1925. As I walked into the kitchen I saw through an archway into the conservatory an orange ball no bigger than a us cent it was not glowing or giving off any light but solid. It was chest night about 5 feet from the floor. It moved from my right to left across the room and disappeared into the wall on my left. It was moving at a constant speed which to me appeared to be walking pace. It never deviated in speed heading or altitude. It gave off no noise or light. Behind the object are glass doors leading to our garden. It was dark outside and we are not overlooked nor can you see any road or other light source so it was not a reflection or light from outside. There was no reflection of it in the window that I could make out. I was perpendicular to the glass on seeing the object. Having observed it for no longer that it would take me to walk across that room between 3-5 seconds i ran into the conservatory. I examined the walls no marks no smells nothing to indicate anything untoward. The object was approx 10 feet on first sighting. 
I was left excited on what I had seen but increasingly anxious about what it was. I racked my brains to try and explain the phenomenon the next day at the same time I did the same thing checked to see if anything from the front of the house could have cause a reflection but I’m adamant that it could not. On a side not the dog refused to go into the conservatory or garden the filling day. Odd
