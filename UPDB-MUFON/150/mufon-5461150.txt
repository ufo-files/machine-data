{jpg} Glowing amber/orange cigar shaped object at lest 200 ft long

Leaving Motorola which is about 10 miles from the Ocean, at about 12 midnight I noticed this object in the sky while going home. It was big and cigar shaped like a blimp. I was silent no sound whatsoever. Moving at about 50 mph due west at approx 500 ft or more. I was doing my best to try to keep up with it. It was heading due west and never changed direction, altitude or speed. It was lit up in an amber to orange color. I do not know if blimps fly at night, but the Goodyear blimp is in a hanger that I know of. When it does fly I never saw the entire blimp light up or glow. I also have no idea if it was the blimp, why it would be traveling west. To the other coast. Eventually I lost sight of it.
