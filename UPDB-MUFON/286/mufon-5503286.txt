I am a sky watcher , i was looking at the stars tonight , the sky was finally clear, I noticed a bright light not moving, it caught my eye because I never saw a "Star" that big and bright , after looking at it for about a minute, it started to move to my Right across the sky not very fast just drifting, it then stopped and drifted to the left, i thought from my view it may be head in my direction appearing to drift left, then it stopped again for a few seconds maybe 10,then started moving right again and definitely away from me as the light was getting smaller, the light did not fade to black it just disappeared. I knew I it wasn't a plane, the light was way to bright, there were no flashing lights, and the way it moved right, then drifted left, then right again, with hovering stops in its movements, am I saying I saw a spaceship? No ,but I definatly saw an Unidentified Flying Object, it was not a satellite, i see them all the time ,they are very small and no where the brightness of this, and it wasn't an airplane or helicopter.
