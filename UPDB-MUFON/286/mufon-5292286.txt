Objetos in sky

Around 1967 in the part of the day, I who at the time should have some 
10 years old, along with a friend, sitting at the door of a house, we looked up at the sky and saw several objects parked as if they were silver coins in a circular formation they turned to the right they turned to the left 
They stopped disappearing and after a few moments appeared again and repeated the previous movements.
