{dr}Large moon hovering treetop high going straight until it took off into space. Very large object!

We were young and playing a game at night ( early 70s) . I would say at least 8 people and it came from my house area and passed slowly above us. It looked like a giant moon and we just stood there and watch it until it took off into space. None of us were scared, we did know what was happening. There were grown ups inside the house but we never said anything about it to anyone else. We still talk about it today
