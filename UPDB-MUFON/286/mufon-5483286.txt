{jpg} Was filming what I thought might be a tornado on South side of HWY 92. Suddenly two bright white discs flew toward tornado at amazing speed. when I panned the phone from east to west one was hovering close to us

I was with my girlfriend. she had picked me up from work in Omaha. We have about a 60 mile drive straight East on Hwy 92. Thunder storms were forecast. We passed through a small town called Treynor, Iowa. The view is unobstructed for miles on this hilly road. We were still traveling straight east and noticed that the dark clouds on our right hand side looked like a couple tornadoes were forming.I have been in several tornadoes and took my cell phone and started panning while driving. I thought I saw a couple bright white discs go across the hwy in front of us at a very fast speed. I didn't see the one hovering on the south side until I was looking at the video. But it is there, Twice. There may even be a few more zipping around in the clouds. Each time I took my eyes off the road and looked to my right I saw silver glints or bright white lights. There was no noise coming from the objects that I could hear with the windows rolled down and Tom Petty playing on the radio.I have all kinds of pictures of strange crafts. They are everywhere. You only need to be outside and look up to find them.
