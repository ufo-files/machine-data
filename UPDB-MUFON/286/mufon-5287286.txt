Flashing light/floating orb

Family and I were traveling from Zion to Reno. About 20 min after turning on 375 my wife wanted to photograph a joshua tree. After several minutes a circular object shining light suddenly appeared above us. I grabbed my cell phone from my car and took a video. We had no clue we were near area 51 and had never even heard of the ET highway and I am not a believer in aliens but all four of us are positive in what we saw and cannot explain it. We learned later when we arrived at our hotel where we were located.
