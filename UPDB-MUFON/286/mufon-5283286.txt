Light Following Airplane

I was driving down North Rutherford Blvd, headed south, with my girlfriend in the car. We suddenly saw two very bright lights in the sky to the east at a very low altitude. One looked like it was probably a plane, the other looked kind of like a candle flame and it appeared to be following the plane. We watched that for maybe two minutes, I could have turned left onto Halls Hill Pike, but with the recent snow did not feel comfortable driving down some backroads while gawking at the sky. There was a patch of trees immediately after the Halls Hill intersection that obscured our view for only a few seconds. After clearing the trees, the object we presumed to be a plane was flying overhead clearly coming into the local airport nearby. The other light was just gone.
