Red/Green ball flying diagonally at great speed

I was sitting in my car next to the River Mersey off a lane which leads to the cricket club. This was a point where I'd been meeting this girl before we went elsewhere.

The sky was dark and no cloud cover. The usual passengers planes were going over from and to Manchester airport. Also the police helicopter was in the distance. Slightly bored I was staring into the sky and that's when I noticed the object in the distance.

The object/ball was flashing green to red and visa versa every 10 seconds roughly. I disregarded it as being an aeroplane or helicopter due to it's movement. It was flying/hovering at great speed in a diagonal pattern across the sky ascending and descending but it wasn't constant. It would stop instantly at certain points.

At the time I was dumbstruck at what I was seeing. I turned away from watching it due to not wanting believe what I was seeing. I've only brought this up in conversations to this date.

I'm unsure if I took any footage at the time. I'll have to dig out my old phone!
