9 starlike lights moving in night sky over Louisville Ky

On 3/29/2020 at 9:46pm EST two witnesses observed three distinct events. The first of these events were 7 evenly spaced lights traveling from NW to SE at 90 degrees above the horizon. From our location the lights appeared from the NW, tracked towards us until they reached the zenith directly over us, then disappeared and did not reappear on the other side of the zenith. The second event was a light moving from N to S at approximately 70 degrees (at the time this was identified as the ISS). The third event was another more spaced series of lights (2 in total) that moved NW to NE at approximately 70 degrees. There were a few airplanes that also flew by in this timespan. Note that there were no airplanes in the sky during the events only before or after. The lights were too high to capture any image evidence.
