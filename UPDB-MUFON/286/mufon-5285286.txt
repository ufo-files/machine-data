Triangular black object fly across sky at very high speed

A sound like air, high speed fluttering air, low in pitch brought my attention to the sky. Looking up I watched a large triangular shape flying very fast across the sky, point first. No other sounds but that low, fluttering air sound. It was loud, and sounded powerful, but not bellowing. I've never heard the sound before, which is the reason I looked up in the first place. The speed was incredible, which I estimate because of the approximate height of somewhere up to 500 feet or so. Went straight across the sky, from due west to due east, Just below the clouds, which were low at the time.
