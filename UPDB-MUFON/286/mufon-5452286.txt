Blue orb seen in a 'Sun Dog', Halo on 08, June 2020 between 12:15 to 13:00 CST (-5 UTC) Vid and Stills taken.

I have been an amateur astronomer for over 50 yrs. I've spent the the past several years attempting astrophotography as a hobby. On 08 June 2020 at ~ 12:15 CST (-5 UTC) my daughter texted me a picture of a 'Sun Dog' or Halo that she took. The first thing I noticed was a blue orb at ~ the '7:00' position on the Halo. At first I thought it to be a reflection or a camera artifact. I went outside and took a picture and got the same blue orb in my pic. If it was a reflection or artifact it would be very coincidental being two different cameras and miles apart. I took pictures and videos and the videos are quite revealing and interested me enough to post here. I could accept that it may be an anomaly within the Halo but a camera artifact I'm pretty sure I ruled out. Anyway, I am posting the pics and vids and all theories are welcome.
