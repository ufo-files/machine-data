Metallic rod rising from water

In August 1970, when I was 16, a friend and I were on a 7 day backpacking trip through Rocky Mountain National Park. On approximately our 4th day we were camped on the edge of a small lake (Lake Nanita), we were fishing and goofing off when I saw an object that looked somewhat like an automobile antenna sticking up out of the water in the middle of the lake. It was shiny as it reflected the sunlight. It was approximately 50 ft away at this point. As I looked as this thing it began to move toward the west end of the lake, leaving a slight wake on the surface of the water. My friend and I ran around to where the thing appeared to be headed, but when it got approximately 10 ft from the shore and 15 ft from us it went straight down into and under the water. Soon after this we saw it again in the middle of the lake, sitting stationary. It stuck up out of the water approximately 3ft at it highest point. We never saw anything below the water. In all we watched it for 15-20 minutes then we left to go on a hike. It was not there when we returned. We saw this thing clearly, it was not a play of light or illusion but was clearly a solid object about 1/4 inch thick by 3ft long (out of the water). It clearly moved, slowly but steadily. There was no sound from it. Our sense at the time was that it submerged under the water because we has gotten to close to it. We never felt afraid or threatened, just very curious.
