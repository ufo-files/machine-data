{nd} Triangle shaped green glowing object with a solid light at each point. The solid lights were green and white.

While at a friends house in the mountain valley region of Burke County, we saw a strange triangle shaped object with three solid lights on each fixed point. Before the object appeared, several coyotes could be heard howling approx. 100 yards from our location. Once the object appeared, the howling stopped. I don't know if the coyotes stopped because of the object or if they were just done with howling. The object hovered for what seemed to be 10-15 mins. The object projected other green lights from it, illuminating the dark sky. After the object left, I checked my watch. We had all been standing there in awe for almost an hour and a half. I don't know how the time went by so fast, but it did and it didn't seem that long of an encounter. The object continued toward the mountains and disappeared over the trees.
