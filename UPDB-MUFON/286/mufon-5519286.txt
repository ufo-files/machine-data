Happened to look up and saw a flash like something flipped and reflected light

I happened to look up off my porch and see a flash like something flipped, then saw it keep moving thinking it was a satellite, but then it abruptly changed direction, speeded up then flew in an arch. I first saw it nearly straight up, slightly in the southwest, then it reversed and flew north, then slightly northeast going faster till it disappeared.
