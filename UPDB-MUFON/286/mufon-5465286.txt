{tjd} Saw 3 vertical lights fly above me on bike ride and disappear as if behind a wall.Very fast, top light was brightest and (still bright) but tapered in brightness level

I was riding my bike home from work on a clear night, heading north on Tremont st in Cedar Falls Iowa. What first made me notice the object was that i thought it was a shooting star so it caught my attention. I looked closer and it was a row of three vertical lights, all 3 bright..however, top one was brightest, middle less bright, and bottom was the least brightest. So i can't tell if the brightness could be all the same, and it appeared that way to me because of possibilities of shape, reflections, size, etc. The 3 lights seemed to travel consistently on top of each other in same motion and pattern. The lights did not fade off into the distance, but more so vanished behind a wall. It moved very fast and straight, then seemed to have went behind a wall (somewhat like a shield) just went black. At first I felt skeptical, then excited. I finished my ride home and called a close friend about it.
