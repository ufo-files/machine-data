{kt}it appeared to be like if there were too many stars in the night sky that didnt belong.

I was about 16 yrs old, just finishing up playing softball for a neighborhood team.My mom came and picked me up and we live about 3 iles from the park where I was at.As my mom is driving, I look up into the sky and see so many stars which was weird because with the city lights, you dont see many stars.So I took a second look and all of a sudden, the lights(stars) took off like light speed to the horizon, then shot straight into the air.It wasnt shooting stars because these lights were in unison..Needless to say, I didnt tell my mom because she wouldnt have believed me..I didnt tell anyone till about 3 yrs later, I confided in my older sister...never spoke about it again and i didnt know who to contact back then.I remember as if it happened yesterday.
