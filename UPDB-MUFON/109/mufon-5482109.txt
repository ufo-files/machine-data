{jpg} Standing in Driveway viewed the Phoenix Lights

I was initially in my house and a co-worker of mine messaged me that he sees the Phoenix lights in the Southern sky. So i walked out into my driveway and turned south and immediately saw 3 lights initially and then a 4th appeared after a minute of two (I have seen them before and reported them before). They were same as before bright glowing floating orbs in the sky at a 30-35 degree angle. I knew they weren't air traffic because I saw one helicopter and numerous aircraft flying underneath the objects, so that told me that they were stationary. The lights seemed to just turn off like every other time. I am currently an aircraft mechanic at Luke AFB and I personally know the flying schedules of the aircraft and I can assure you that there were no planes from Luke flying.
