{nd} Three lights at treetop height hovered for about five minutes then turned slowly south

I had just left home at 6:45 in morn on my way to meet friends. I was stopped on a corner facing north when I. Saw it just above tree level. It hovered there for about five min. It was dark so all I could see was three lights , red green and yellow. I thought it might be a tower but I live in the area and. Know there is no tower there. I watched it for about five minutes.. After five min it slowly turned east and then turned south going very slow. It was right over the highway when I had to turn west as a car had come behind me. I saw it for a few seconds from my car mirror but then lost track of it.    I wasn't frightened at the time, just more curious than anything.
