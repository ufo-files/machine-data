{cak} Fleet of UFO's near DFW International Airport

At appropimately 2:40PM today I was traveling on E. Southlake Blvd in Southlake, TX and happened to look in the Eastern sky in front of me. I saw several jets flying away from DFW International Airport and then noticed extremely high in the sky a fleet of a hundred or more silverish / whitish UFOs flying towards the SouthWest. They seemed to fly in formation with several of them flying in and out above and below. I stopped my car took out my iphone and took off my sunglasses to try and record them / take a photo. I could NOT see them so I put back on my sunglasses and there they were. I brought my iphone up to my eye and they were ALL gone just like that. They were not geese/ducks etc they appeared to be round and danced in the sky.
