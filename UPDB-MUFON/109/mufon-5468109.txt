In the 1990s 

i was leaving my house to go to my local club, which was , at the time, the Dunlop Sports and Social Club. it was about 20:30 in the evening a clear November night. Now this was the 7th nov and i dont know but the 5th was th previous Wednesday. Now I am aware that people will say 'you saw fireworks' this was not the case. As i said a bright clear night no fireworks wre going off, As I left the house I looked skywards, as I always do. I then saw whatI bekieved was a shooting star but this one stopped. Now i have seen our satelites, I know the difference. When it was trevelling initially it was like a shining shooting star but then it stopped and went reddish, then it shotleft, then right and left and right again. Then stopped. and then it slowly approached exactlty above where I stood. As I looked up, it looked like a germ under a microscope, with entrails wisping about. It stayed there for about a minute and all the while, I could hear some of neighbours talking, who hadn't seen or were awere of any of this. Now to make this even stranger, the next morning, the garden was full of what I would have thought were spiders webs. I have since come to believe these were 'Angel Hair' I have no reasonable explanation for this experience. I know it was close to Guy Fawkes night and the only date is 07/11 1980s ish, but as a regular skywatcher, I know what I saw. Contact me if you wish. [phone number removed/cms/dr]
