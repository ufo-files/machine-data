Lots of colored lights and siver

We were driving on the Altamont Pass coming from Tracy going home when all of a sudden it appears. At first thought it must be helicopter, as it got much closer could see it was not. There was no sound. My husband was driving and he sped up and it sped up, he slowed down and it slowed down. Other cars saw it also and were pointing at it. It followed us for a while and we were starting to get scared. My young kids were asleep in the back seat. Then as quickly as it appeared it took off. We did tell a few family and friends, but never reported bit, thought no one would believe us.
