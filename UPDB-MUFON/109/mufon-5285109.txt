Triangle shaped object to the right of the bright object in the picture.

Driving home from Jamestown NY on interstate 86 to Olean NY. We noticed a bright light in the southern sky. Was able to take 4 pictures of it and each picture changed shape and size.. To the right of the bright light there is a triangle shaped object way up in the atmosphere. Had to be 50 to 60 miles up. I could only see it after we zoomed in on it. By the time we arrived home it was gone.
