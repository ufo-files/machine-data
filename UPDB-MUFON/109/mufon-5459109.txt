{rjl} soundless black chevron/ancient arrow head shape with white aura caught in peripheral vision when scanning sky for planets.

I went out on my deck that overlooks a wooded stream to view the nightly planets and stars visible. here. The clouds were low but I could see stars and space between the clouds. Scanning the sky, I was aware of an unnatural dark geometric shape in my peripheral vision. I turned to look directly and the object and saw a black chevron or ancient arrowhead shape craft with a slight luminous white glow behind it. I could see this glow against the dark shape of the cloud it was beside. It had no lights or sound and appeared to be banking. It then immediately disappeared in that it seemed to have melted or evaporated. It had the strange sensation that it knew that I had spotted it and immediately cloaked or disappeared. I live near a flight path and airport and a plane came by minutes later. There were no planes or engine sounds at the time I saw the object. The sky was empty of all other aircraft. There had been a helicopter over head about an hour before that I could not see but certainly hear. We have those around only about once per every few months. I do not actively look for UFOs and have only seen zipping lights in the 70's and a star shape five years. I hope this report is helpful to MUFON..
