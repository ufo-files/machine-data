Hovering, big rectangle flash under light, then was across above the field

My mom and I were coming down the road about to turn into our driveway (we live in the country so no lights down the road or at the end of our driveway, we just had headlights from the car) when I looked over the trees before we turned and there was what I assumed was an airplane and a BIG rectangle flash of light and next thing I knew it was way off to the east, the same way our driveways goes, and it was going quickly over the field flashing a bunch of different colors. When it kind is stopped moving away, it was over a feild and a bunch of different colors flashed from it and it went in a few different directions before heading in the same direction it was heading in earlier. It looked like the lights/colors were moving around in a circular motion.
