{cak} V shape Lights chased by 3 fighter jets

Around 930pm, Sept 17, 2019, I observed 3 fighter jets, what seemed to be, doing regular manuevers in the area. All of a sudden, I noticed a light in the area of the jets which I assumed to be a flare. However, the light didn't stream down as usual but became much brighter and was holding in a stationary pattern. As the jets came closer to the light, it became much brighter and then 4 more lights lite up and the V Shape pattern of ights took off towards the East and veered to the NE as the 3 jets followed behind. The sighting was appoximately 3 to 4 minutes in duration.
