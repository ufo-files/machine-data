Hexagonal. Gold. Lines.

I was coming back from the park with my dog and it was a beautiful day with no clouds in the sky. I remember the exact time as the school kids were coming out of school so, 3.25pm. I was walking amongst the kids coming out and noticed this hexagon shape in the sky. It looked big but I could only guess the size as it was hard to say how high it was. 

I thought it was a kite at first but this thing was so still it was like it was absolutely motionless. It was hexaganol in shape with a dull gold colour to it. Each point of the shape had a line going to its opposite point. Bit like a Tic Tac To grid before putting any naughts or crosses in. No sould, totally motionless. I shouted at the kids to look but they were too busy talking and didn't bother.

I was the for around 10 minutes looking at it. I thought to myself, go home and get my camera. I did but, when i came back it was gone. This was something i have never seen before anywhere. Nothing can stay completely motionless at that height. This is the first time i have told anyone as i was questioning my mind as if i was seeing things. It happened, and it has stayed with me all these years.

Regards

Micheal Nuttall.
