me, my new wife, and mother outside of mothers home in dark clear sky saw two disc. objects hovering at a very high distance above the ground (miles high) then they first one then the other at a high rate of speed exited into deep space in the direction of a reverse capital J. The objects were large in size (dime size) and when they exited into deep space they went so fast that they went from their full size to a pin dot in about 2 seconds. This was all very real to all three of us and made us true believers that there is other beings than just on earth. The action and movement of these objects were way beyond human capacity.
