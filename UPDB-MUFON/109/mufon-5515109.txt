Observation lasted just 3-5 seconds

7pm I always walk my dog and I have to say, I am very much a sky watcher and even more so now as I saw a golden globe type object(No tail) move very quickly in the same place as the most recent sighting just a week ago. I quickly realized it was not a plane, there were no flashing lights. This was 4 lights in the form of a rectangle all the same distance apart. It's height I would say was the usual height of a regular passenger plane. But travelled much faster. The sighting lasted for around 3-5 seconds - maybe less. I have no idea what it was - It was there.....Then gone!
