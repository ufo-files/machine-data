Small blue flying thing came up to my face move back an forth around me an left

A beautiful blue object came right up to my face while I was at a playground at a drive in movie theatre in el monte California in Feb of 1985..i saw it coming from above the tree line an it was coming straight to me i felt like i knew it was there to see me an i to see it as well.it was eye level for a few seconds then it went back a few feet an dropped down a few inches came back up went from the left side of my face then to my right side an hovered infront of me for a total of 15 seconds or so ..the whole time it was going around my face I felt a hum thru my whole body it never made a sound I just felt it hum or maybe pulsate thru my whole body ..then it went about a foot or two away from me an it just disappeared an the humming or pulsing I felt was gone as well..
