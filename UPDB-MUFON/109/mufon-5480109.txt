{rjl} Small white light, slightly larger than mars appeared.

At or about 2005 Hrs. on Friday the 21st of October 2016 I observed a white ball of unknown size at an unknown altitude moving west to east with out a sound at a very rapid speed. I first observed the object at approximately the 12 O'clock position, I observed it for approximately 15 seconds when it disappeared over the horizon. I appeared to be on a straight flight path and might have been decending in altitude. The object flew along a line between Needles, CA. and Page, AZ.
