This event actually happened to my son-in-law. We were having lunch when he said he got some strange photographs. Earlier in the day, he was canoeing on Middle Waterton Lake, and was taking pictures of the scenery. Later, when he looked at the pictures, he saw a green orb in them. He had taken a series of eight photos, and in this series the orb first appeared just above the surface of the lake, then rose about 20 feet in the air, then descended again to the surface of the lake. We all looked closely at the photos, expecting it was a refraction in the lens or some other explainable phenomena, but it just couldn't be explained. He is a mechanical engineer, and even he is mystified. He has agreed to allow us to submit this report as his first reaction is that he captured photos of a UFO!
