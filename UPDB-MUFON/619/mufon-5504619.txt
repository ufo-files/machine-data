{kt}Round Orb

I was headed west bound on Interstate 10 on the 2nd from right lane (of 5 lane freeway). I first noticed a circle located on the upper-right portion of my car windshield, as I approached the 'S' jog in the freeway headed north. The size and brightness of the orb initially caught my eye and as I came out of the slight turn, I slowed to observe the orb. At first, I thought it was a reflection from my windshield; immediately, lowered my passenger window and watched it continue on a parallel tract with the freeway. This orb was just barely over the embankment of the freeway at approximately 20 to 30 percent grade from the freeway. Having seen this before on television, I am certain it was a UFO. Mixed feelings of awe and confused driving and observation saw it disappear behind the vegetation at the top of the escarpment. I kept checking the rear view mirror for the orb but never saw it again, having passed the site location.
