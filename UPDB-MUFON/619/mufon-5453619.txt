{rjl} Brighter than Bright white light

Driving East towards Olympia Washington on Hwy 8, between McCleary Washington and Ranch House BBQ I observed 2 hovering brighter than bright white lights one North and one South approimately two miles apart, one of the lights (Southern) looked to be pulling dull grey tic tack objects into the bottom of craft. Suddenly darted to the south faster than any human made object, then after aboout two miles abrubtly changed direction (90') to the east, made a thin black line that extended N/S as it headed east gaining approxamatly 1000' of elevation then made another seamingly impossible course change vertically, and disapeared into the clouds. I was so fixed on the southerly object that I didn't see where the north object had went, lost it. Kept driving. Dates are to the best of my recolection, I only chose to report this years later. Didn't take notes.
