{cak} We were VERY SURPRISED 2 see a craft.

A group of us were walking across campus heading EAST when we noticed a BRIGHT ORANGE-PINK Light coming towards us beneath the cloud bank.It was flying above the tree tops&was about the size of a golf ball traveling across the sky heading WEST.As we observed the STARCRAFT it suddenly changed course&seemed to be descending just North of us.The unidentified craft was moving slow enough that we had a conversation trying to FIGURE OUT what It could be.When it changed its direction&begin heading N/W.About 30seconds after the object changed course the bright glowing light went out,but we could still see the craft for a few seconds&then it DISAPPEARED-This was @8pm-
 About an hour later we found out that across campus at the same time of our sighting a couple, who were also out,Observed this glowing Salmon object,however their VANTAGE POINT was better than ours.They saw the craft turn off the bright light after it changed direction to N/W,but they didn't lose sight of it.Apparently the craft began to circle the University&surrounding area with its LIGHT OFF& when it arrived at the location where we first noticed the GLOWING OBJECT it turned on its SALMON COLORED light again- As the light was turned back on it again cruised towards the WESTERN sky.After a couple of minutes cruising at a low altitude& speed it began heading N/W& the craft again turned its light off &disappeared for the second time-Then there was a third pass following the same pattern & then it vanished-We have no idea what the object was or what the purpose of its flight pattern-ALL five witnesses got together to try and figure out what we observed on the evening April 7th 2013,but we are at a loss as to what the identity of the craft was or what its purposed flight was doing above our heads-Any information on the MYSTERIOUS Object would be greatly appreciated-
