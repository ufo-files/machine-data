Good afternoon. Today at 8 o'clock in the morning a few pictures were taken out of my house. NOT through the window! UFO at first did not see, did sunrise pictures from the 3rd floor of the house on the outskirts of the village Morgaushi in the Chuvash Republic, Russia. A few minutes later, when viewing the image, notice the point at the center of the sun on both frames. Phone shifted capture and point in the same place, so it's not a mote. We thought that maybe some mistake matrix because of the bright light, a glitch and did a couple of pictures of the sun - nothing like that no more. With an increase in the frame, the object looks interesting, with blue rays on the sides at the bottom. I do not know what it was, I do not want to guess, but maybe useful to you: what if you have an image of similar objects, and my photos will be useful to you.
