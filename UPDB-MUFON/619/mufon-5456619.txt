{jpg} A DC 3 shaped or cigar shaped UFO follows my coal truck for about 3/4 of a mile.

I was working at a open pit coal outside of Kemmerer Wyoming. I was driving a 50 ton coal truck on top of the West side of the property. I was driving about 18 miles per hour when I looked out of my right side window and observed a cigar shaped object going the same speed as I was. It looked like the front of a DC 3. I could see horizontal plates with rivets. It followed me for about 5 minutes. I went up a long hill to the top of the hill and I could see for about 15 miles and the object was nowhere to be found. So I stopped my truck,got out and looked in all directions and nothing was found.
