UFO was traveling North to South over Jasper, Georgia

While standing on the front porch my son(Michael) and his girlfriend(Stephanie) saw movement in the sky, at which point they called me to see it and what I saw was: At arms length the object was about 2 fingers wide and it was traveling at a slow to moderate rate of speed.. The object had a transparant glow to it which had about 8 lights on it that were very dim. It looked like a boomerang and was traveling north to south. My son saw it at about 12:00 in the sky and he said it was much larger (about 4-5 fingers wide at arms length). I saw it at about 2:00 in the sky and it was about 2 fingers wide. We watched it until it dissappeared near Ballground, Georgia. It made no sound at all.
