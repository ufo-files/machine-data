Seen a light blinking in and out as it traveled

Caught this just after 11pm coming in from the WSW traveling slightly NE turning E then turning SSE. My location is E behind BayCity N of the GolfCourse. I had trouble catching it. Either there were more than one, or it was hopping around the sky. I seen it light up and turn off as it traveled. When it lit up it would be in different spots. Out of 120 pictures this was the only one to capture it. It could be that I caught it in some of the other photos and can't see it due to it not being lit up.
