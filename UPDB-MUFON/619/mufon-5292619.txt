Saw a silver cube object overhead which seemly appeared to change shape

I was watering my garden in my back yard when I suddenly heard airplanes and helicopters in the area. I pulled out my phone and opened up the flightradar24 app to see if I could see what was in my area. I did not see any aircraft near my proximity so I looked up in the sky and saw what I thought at first to be a silver mylar balloon. It appeared to be changing shape from a cube to a triangle but it could have been the sun reflecting off it as it moved in a straight path from north to south. Suddenly an airplane flew underneath it so it had to be larger than a balloon but it was so steadily on it's path I wasn't sure. Then it stopped in its course directly above me for about 30 seconds. I took out my phone and snapped a picture of it. Then it continued on it's path south until it was out of my sight.
