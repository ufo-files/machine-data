{nd} 9 yrs old at cousins house looked straight up to see one white light stationary and one moving across the sky

I was at my my aunt and uncles house my cousins Lee and Michelle and my sister were in the front yard I was laying on the grass looking straight up the sky was very clear I saw to bright white lights one stationary and one moving across the sky. From the one moving came a smaller red light that went over to the stationary light and the a few seconds later it returned to the moving light. I remember even at nine I thought it was most likely some high tech that was secret because I was way into all that I knew and could identify them all Selfridge AFB was only thirty miles to the east. My father was a law enforcement professional also an air raid warden and then fallout officer we retrieved a couple of weather balloons so I knew about things even then. Ten years later I had a closer contact.
