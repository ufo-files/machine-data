Oval shaped with glowing ring

I was watching the D.C. bald eagle live cam when I noticed the strange object traveling from right to left in cam view. It appeared to tilt a little while traveling. It appeared as a glowing oval-shaped object with a a glowing outer ring. It went by fairly fast so had to use the DVR feature in the YouTube stream to take screen shots. 

I’ve included 3 screen shots of its movement from right to left.

I was not able to answer most questions on the sightings form due to witnessing on a live cam.
