{cak} black object that dropped fireballs that faded out

We were sitting on our deck at dusk and noticed the glow in the sky that we initially though was a paraglider, but did not have a parachute. We thought it was a paraglider because we had seen one earlier in the evening. It was a small black object, moved very slowly, and dropped what seemed to be fireballs that faded out. It moved far enough to the north of us that we couldn't see it any longer. It didn't last more than a couple of minutes, but enough time for my 9 year old to get into my house and grab my iphone to get a few seconds of video. It's VERY difficult to find but if you watch the video, at 13 seconds in we are able to fine "the black dot" and when he mentions it is dropping fireballs you can see that back dot illuminate and a small trail below the object if you zoom in all the way. We saw it in person so we know what it was, but it's possible that anyone else looking at this totally zoomed in video wouldn't have any idea what they are looking at.
