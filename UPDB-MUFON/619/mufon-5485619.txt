{jpg} TUESDAY THE WEEK OF CHRISTMAS 2015, WHILE OUTSIDE ON MY PARCH THAT FACES THE OCEAN, I SAW A MASSIVE CRAFT HOVERING 50 ' OVER THE OCEAN

FILLED OUT AT MUFON HQ WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER 18809, ON JANUARY 26, 2016
=========================================================================
IT WAS ABOUT 8:20 PM,
TUESDAY THE WEEK OF CHRISTMAS 2015, WHILE OUTSIDE ON MY PORCH THAT FACES THE OCEAN, I SAW A MASSIVE CRAFT HOVERING 50 ' OVER THE OCEAN. IT HAD A RED STROBE LIGHTS ON IT, BUT I COULDN'T MAKE OUT ITS SHAPE. IT WAS ABOUT A MILE AWAY. IT WAS SILENT. I WATCHED IT FOR 3 MINUTES UNTIL IT MOVED AWAY TOWARDS MYRTLE BEACH, NORTH. IT DIDN'T RISE UP, BUT STAYED AT THE SAME ELEVATION OVER THE WATER.

ANOTHER SIGHTING I HAD WAS ON LABOR DAY, SEPTEMBER,2003. THERE WERE THREE HUGH OBJECTS HOVERING OVER THE WATER, HARD TO SEE THE SHAPES, BUT THEY HAD PALE WHITE LIGHTS ON THEM. PEOPLE WERE SETTING OFF FIREWORKS AT THE TIME, BUT THE CRAFT SEEMED TO SCARE THE PEOPLE AWAY, AND HUNDREDS OF PEOPLE PACKED UP AND LEFT. THE NEXT DAY JETS WITH BOMBS FLEW OVER THE AREA LIKE THEY WERE LOOKING FOR SOMETHING.
