Silent red and blue lights, hovering, circling then dropping fast

We were sitting in the backyard hot tub, around midnight, when we saw bright blue and red lights in the sky. No actually craft was seen in the darkness. It came in toward our neighborhood, near the house, hovered silently, circled around silently and then dropped straight down at a very fast rate of speed. It then went away from our neighborhood toward the open space behind our house, where we lost sight of it over the neighbor houses. It wasn't a plane or a helicopter. My husband and I are in our eighties and we've seen many things in life. We aren't people that scare easily, but this definitely instilled a sense of fear, like we'd seen something out of the ordinary.
