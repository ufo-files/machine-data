I observed an object from aeroplane on sea below definitely not a boat because of it's behaviour

I was scanning the ground below me from the plane. We had just flown from land to sea and I saw some big wind turbines below, I noticed a small craft moving in a snake like fashion very quick between them and thought it was odd. It kept my interest so I swivilled to observed carefully. The craft was now moving away from the turbines towards the sandy looking coast line with blackened shrubbery after that. I watched as it made its way there but was puzzled because it left no wake on the water, none at all. Then it seemed to effortlessly went over the sandy beach and straight on ito the shrubs without stopping at all and disappeared. I have no explanation what so ever as to what I saw but I now believe it was hovering just above the water but from the plane looked like it was on the water. I was on a flight from Venice to Edinburgh.
