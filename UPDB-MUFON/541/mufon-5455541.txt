{tjd} Star Trek Enterprise

One evening, back in the late 1980s, I was visiting a farm neighbor with my two daughters. We were standing outside enjoying the evening when I looked up at the sky and saw the starship Enterprise in a break in the clouds. I said, "look at that!" to all of them and we watched as it sailed along in the clearing before going back into or above the clouds. There was no noise, nothing. The shape of it was the Enterprise. It was the Enterprise! The same shape, same color. We could see windows very distinctly around the wall of the body of the ship but there were more than on Star Trek's Enterprise. It had the size and shape of the Enterprise. I have searched for years trying to see if anyone else had ever seen it. Was it a new vehicle that some government had constructed? Has anyone else seen it other than on TV? Didn't own a camera or any type of recording equipment but I know what I saw.
