Cigar shaped bright blue/white object appeared then disappeared at a high rate of speed

It was 9:11 at night, we (wife and I) were on our deck. Our home is at 7600 ft (Santa Fe, NM) and has a full expanse view of the west. Off to our left (SW) a bright white hot blue flattened oval shaped object appears traveling at a high rate of speed in a slightly descending path. We watch the F-16 practice (out of Kirkland AFB), so we know relative speeds of a jet fighters, this was going much faster (600 mph?). The object "phased" in and out (of existence) as it traveled along its path (clear night, few clouds). The object passed behind a foothill across I-25, came out the other side, went behind another foothill (these foothill peaks are roughly at 7200-7800 ft), and never came out the other side. Total time: 5-7 seconds. This took place over the Galiesteo Basin (the area between Santa Fe and ABQ)...a few miles from our direct line of sight. It made no sound like a fighter jet. It was large, relative to the foothill's size...I would say 4-5 times the size of a semi tractor-trailer. Prior to the object appearing, dogs started barking in the community. After (about 10-15 minutes later) 4 police cars, lights and sirens, sped northbound on I-25 in the direction of the area. Dogs stopped barking. It wasn't a fighter jet with it's after burner on because there wasn't a plane "in front" of the object...the object was the "plane"...and a fighter jet would make a big sound and this was completely silent (at least from our distance we couldn't hear anything...typically jets make a lot of sound reflecting off of the mountains all around us...we live on a mountainside).
