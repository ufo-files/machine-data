Bright white beam just above road. getting out of vehicle. It's right above, maybe 10-15 ft.

The year was 1972. Time flies and memory fades. 
However, I have retained a certain amount of imagery from the incident, and so has my companion (witness).
We came across a bright beam of (pure) white light pointing this way and that on the surface of the road in front of us. We assumed it was a helicopter being as we had both recently returned from Vietnam. We stopped in front of where it hovered above the road and got out of the car. It appeared to be about 15-20 ft. above our heads. There was no sound, and no wind from helicopter prop-wash.
Suddenly we felt static and thought we may be electrocuted. That's when things got weird.
It has been a long while of telling myself it was a dream. I hadn't seen my companion in some time and when we got together recently he blurted out that we were abducted before I could say anything.
During the incident we also lost approximately five and a half hrs.
I just remember the white place and the little arm reaching across me which woke me up. I could not move, my eyes were open and I could see but, i could not move my eyes either. A little wrist reaching just under my chin was in my field of vision and I distinctly saw a metallic weave which appeared as if it was skin. I must have been lying down because there were several small [people] moving about who were perpendicular to me. Through the bright white haze I could see a red brick colored one standing farther back watching.
I have always been reluctant to talk about it with any other than my companion that evening. It happened but I sure get confused thinking about it.
