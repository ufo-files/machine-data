{dr}Flying objects spotted with dimming lights

On July 4th, 2012 I was outside with my family enjoying the evening before the late night fireworks started. The fireworks were due to start around 10:15-10:30. Around 8:30-9 pm, I had glanced in the sky and saw two flying objects that had an orangish/yellow appearance. They were circular but I could not identify what they were. I called my mother and father in law outside for them to help me identify. The objects would fly very fast, then slow down and hover in the sky, then pick up speed again. Out of nowhere three more appeared behind the first two, flying in the same kind of chaotic change of direction path. The objects eventually completely dimmed out, but we could still see the black outlines/shadow of the objects. I was so confused on what they were, I started researching my newspaper and Internet the next day to get some answers. I found over 100 reported sightings of the same thing I had witnessed; with the same details!
