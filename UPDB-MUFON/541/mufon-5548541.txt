Stationary bright intense multicolored lights -mostly white.green,blue,yellow and red

Traveling along Hwy 401 Eastbound in the Whitby Township, at about 7:00pm on Jan.24,2008.I turned off 401 in Whitby and headed south off Thickson Rd.I then turned east along Bloor Street to Oshawa township.I stopped at Best Buy parking lot to the back of building.I got out of my car with a analog camcorder and filmed over Lake Ontario.Within 40 seconds ,my camcorder loss power.I returned to the car and retrieved my digital camera.I took 10 pictures and 3 short video clips.My distance from the lake was approx.two miles away.This bright multicolored light stay stationary,over the lake about 2-3 miles away from the shorelone.A guess from my position in total maybe 5-7 miles away.Altitude at maybe between 3-5000 feet.After about 20 minutes,I then left due to low chilly temps.I went north into Oshawa to my home.As soon as I got out of my car at home in the driveway.I noticed a very low plane,with a bright light in the front, fly over my house.The direction the plane was heading was southeast.Same direction I just came from.I have one photo and a short clip to share in photographic evidence.I believe the clip has captured something else close over the lake that the naked eye did NOT see.
