{nd} Object SE with no trail near jet. Then went east and hovered approx 10 mins. Flashing yellowish light

Sitting outside 6:05 pm for bbq. Facing west as observed commercial jet approx 3k feet going south toward phoenix airport. Observed flashing object, approx 20k feet? It was blinking about every 3-5 seconds, all one color, yellow/ orange. It then headed north by east at constant speed and then hovered at 12 o'clock approx 10 minutes with same light
Got binoculars and observed a filament substance hanging below. It looked similar to a jellyfish and then disappeared. I am a private pilot and former USAF police and civilian police. I cannot identify what was seen.
