(GS) Ball of super bright white light just under Cassiopeia constellation appeared and dissapeared

While staring at Cassiopeia Monday night (not a cloud in the sky)a ball of white light, the size of venus or slightly brighter, appeared under the constellation for 3 or 4 seconds then quickly dimmed out; it was stationary. I waited to see if it may have been a Airplane turning but saw no other lights. Perhaps it was a falling star that was headed my direction which would give it the illusion of standing still.
