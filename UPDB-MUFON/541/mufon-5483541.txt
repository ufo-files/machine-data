{cak} Orange object. Smaller break off.

I was smoking and looked up to see a strange orange light. I didn't think much of it at first but when I looked back up it had become almost seahorse shaped. I started watching it closely at this point as it turned boomerang shaped.  As it changed shape it decreased in size and luminosity. Here and there I could see orbs flying away from it at various trajectories and speeds. It then turned into 3 dim Star-lite objects and eventually into something that you would think was a star or a satellite. It started getting brighter again and reformed into the boomerang/starfish shape again.  It repeated what happened before only faster this time until it was a dim star again and then completely disappeared. Was this a fleet? I've seen weird stuff in the sky before but nothing this odd or unsettling.
