3 glowing orbs appeared in front of car, Mom stopped car, what we thought was only a few seconds was a few hours later when we watched them leave.

I think I was 10 - 11, maybe 12 years old. It was about 50 years ago, somewhere between 1965 - 1968 in Vineland, NJ. My mother, sister and I were on our way to my Aunts house for our every Sunday family dinner 5:30 - 5:45pm. We were not far from her house. It was already dark so I think it was December or January. The street we were on had no street lights and was a somewhat wooded area.

Out of the darkness suddenly came 3, maybe 4 glowing orbs, very bright. I remember primary colors, right in front of our car, It seemed like they were 10 - 15 feet in front of the car, maybe even closer and maybe 6 - 10 feet off the ground. They were round, glowing, maybe the size of basketballs or larger maybe the size of garbage can lids.
I was sitting in the front passenger's seat, my sister was in the back seat. My mother immediately stopped the car and as soon as she did, I reached for the door handle to get out and take a closer look and she ordered me, "Don't you move!" We sat there for what we thought were only a few more seconds and then the orbs took off as fast as they had appeared. No sounds.
 
We continued to my Aunts house expecting to have dinner, as usual but they had already eaten, hours before and had been wondering where we were. We told them of our experience and none of us ever spoke of it again for another 35-40 years, but none of us ever forgot that evening! It wasn't something sane people talked about back then.
