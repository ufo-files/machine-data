string of at least 20 lights flying in formation, no sound, very high in the air

I went outside to lock down my country property at about 7:45 pm tonight (Feb. 24)
It is a very clear moonlit night. Felt compelled to look at the northern sky, which is not something I normally do. Saw a line of very dim lights moving slowly west to northeast. The lights were exact distance from each other. They seemed very high in the sky. There was no noise. Saw only 3 or 4 lights at first, then the string kept coming until at least 20 lights If I had to estimate I would say the string of lights covered at least 2 miles in length., all equal distance from each other.
My first thought was it was one of those giant UFO's people say they see, but I didn't notice any particular shape, just the string of lights. Could it be a group of drones doing research? If so, I have never seen anything like this before.
