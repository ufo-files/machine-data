(GS) Small starlike hovering light with rapid circular motions sometimes zipping left to right or clockwise/counterclockwise

1. I like to watch stars at night from time to time and I happened to notice the one zip upward and to the right. I was at home with my bf.
2. I noticed it was the only starlike object flickering brightly than the rest. 
3. I thought the object was a star or aircraft. 
4. It made fluent zips and dashes with purpose to the left and right then would hover for a 
few seconds then go in circular motions clockwise and counter clockwise. 
5. I was creeped out at first I have never seen anything make such movements before then the more I watched I became more intrigued and couldnt take my eyes off it.
6. I never lost sight its still hovering as I write this.
