Seen something run across the road.

I was just finishing up a 45 day hitch consulting for horizontal drilling at a Chevron job site. I took a nap on my office couch because I had a long drive. I woke up around midnight and signed out with the medic. It was mid winter, snowing quite a bit and I get about 3-4km down the road and I see something that I could feel had I very high level of sophistication sprinted across the road from 20ft on one side of the road to 20ft on the other side and fast. I got a huge hit of adrenaline and hit the gas I called my brother and said "Chris I don't know what I just seen but it was tall and it was white".. I was scared to death. I was sure I just saw something I wasn't supposed to. I couple months later I was at my brothers house and I watched the honourable Paul Hellyer"s disclosure on extra terrestrial's. He said something about "the tall whites" just seeing that sent shivers down my spine. Again I was scared to death, I showed my brother he couldn't believe it. It was a new video clip but I told him what I seen months earlier.
