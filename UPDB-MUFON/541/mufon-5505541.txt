{nd} hovering red light and then turned green as it moved and back to red

while star gazing using 15\70 binoculars we saw a abnormal light in the sky seemed to be about 5 to 8 miles away couldnt make out a definitive shape.52 degrees nw. red light was hovering and moving up and down then moved across the sky to 60 degrees nw, while moving it turned green. the object then returned back to its original spot we first saw it at. when changing direction it did not turn like a plane or helicopter it simply changed direction... we live in the san diego international airport flight path, when the object was in the sky we witnessed three airliners go off their flight path to avoid the object. one plane turned south west one turned north and the last plane seemed to ascend over the object. then the light descended and was out of view. there are 4 of us that all saw the same thing.
