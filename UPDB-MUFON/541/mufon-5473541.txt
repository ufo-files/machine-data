{rjl} Lying on my back outdoors clear night eyes wide open saw slow moving varied hundreds of ships do

Late at night clear sky lying on my back outdoors eyes wide open in relaxed state. Came into focus slow moving seemed like hundreds of varied sizes and shapes ships clearly defined. Took up the whole sky. Very high altitude. Very congested but like they were sight seeing or something. Going in different directions at different rates of slow speed. Semi rural area very dark night but clear and a moon. They were all the same light gold brownish like they were in a illuminated mist. But clear. No sound or lights.
