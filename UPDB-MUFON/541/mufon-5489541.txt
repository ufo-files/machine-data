{dr}egg shaped object in back yard hovered 5 ft off ground about 2 min then gone

About 6 am I went to let my dog out into the back yard. I opened the door and looked out to make sure there were no animals in the yard. When I looked directly in front of me about 30 feet away. I saw an object shaped like an egg. It was about 15 ft tall and it was about 5 ft wide at the bottom and about 12 ft at the top. It shimmered from the top to the bottom and I could see right through it to the trees behind it. It was hovering about 5 or 6 ft off the ground . My dog usually runs off the deck and around the yard. She stopped at the top of the deck and looked in the direction of the object. She did not move while the object was there. It did not have any lights or make any noise . I looked at it for 1 or 2 min and it just disappeared like someone shut off a light. The yard was still no movement in the trees or any of the plants.My dog ran off the deck and over to the location of the object and was sniffing the air. I went out to the spot but I did not see anything. I have been in this house for 30 years and have never seen anything like this before or since.
