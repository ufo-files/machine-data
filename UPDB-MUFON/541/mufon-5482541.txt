{nd} It was a spherical shaped yellowish/orangish object with what appeared to be a bright red dome. The whole thing just glowed as it made its way across the sky, in no particular kind of a hurry.

I was out on the back porch having a cigarette just scanning the sky back & forth watching fireworks.I heard the neighbors radio start to crackle like it was kinda off station, then I noticed the object in the sky to the South. I recall thinking it was an odd looking firework but, it kept moving across the sky. The object was spherical in shape glowing a yellow/orangish color, with a red glowing dome. It traveled from the South in a slight Northwest direction. I walked along the East side of the house so I could see where it was going. It came over the house & kept its Northwesterly direction.
