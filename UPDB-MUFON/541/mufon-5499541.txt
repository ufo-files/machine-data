{dr}Extremely fast speed and just disappeared before it got in front of the moon. No clouds were around.

I was walking to my car after leaving the grocery store. Right before I put the key in to unlock my car door, for some reason, I did a complete sigh and as my head cocked back I saw this object that was moving faster than any plane and then it just disappeared as if it was cloaking itself. The object had a matte white exterior with a red dome in the front of it and what looked like flames coming out of the back like a booster. Then again it looked like a trail of light. It was so fast that it happened for like a second. I just observed so I didn't feel or think anything until it just disappeared in mid air. I stared at the sky for a moment and waited to see if I could see it again but I never did. It made me feel that all my current problems were petty. I ruled out that is was not a plane,jet or a falling star due to it's immediate disappearance and how far up it was. I have never seen any plane or jet that high up in the sky that close to the moon.
