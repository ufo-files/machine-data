Super bright blue lights shining through dark clouds

Mom my daughter amd I went for a walk to enjoy the fall weather amd take pictures of the fall colors. Randomly taking pics of trees and what ever we could find including silhouettes of the tree on the cloudy sky. When we got back to the house she has me come look at a pic of the sky that has super bright light in dark clouds. I tried to be optimistic about it saying camera flare or glitch hut the next pic was hard to do that with. The light was not only very bright through dark clouds but the second pic had a small branch in the way of the light blocking some of the light. The only way that could happen if it was an actual light in the distance. We went back 15 minutes or so later taking pics and videos of every direction and couldn't find the light again it was just gone.
