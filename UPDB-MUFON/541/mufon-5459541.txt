I saw a bright white object with no other colors under it like you would see on an aircraft. The light came across the sky like a shooting star so I watched it but it slowed down then hovered and then after about 5 minutes the object then just went really bright and then just faded out into what would remind me a black hole in the sky. I thought it was a shooting star but not sure. My husband said it has to be a shooting star. My husband being a retired pilot says it could have been a test aircraft or a shooting star because he didn't see it. He also let me know that it was more than likely a shooting star because judging distance is impossible unless you are in the air. As far as the time it hovered he said it may have still been a shooting stars just extremely far off in the distance. Either way I don't agree because it slowed down it didn't keep going at speed and then it really looked like it was hovering. Again, I do not know for sure but I have an open mind unlike my husband.
