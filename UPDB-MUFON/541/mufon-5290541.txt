Spherical, grew in size, spinning orb

I saw a round white orb in the night sky which expanded outward very quickly in a big “poof.” It seemed to double or triple in size, then shrink down to original size. It did this a few times throughout. It jumped around, changing direction while spinning very rapidly in either direction back and forth. There was an intricate “pattern” to it: like the top of a circus tent spinning wildly. It had that teardrop type of shape with many “points” connected. It looked just like the orb that that Monterey airline pilot captured on video. *I don’t know how large it was; I put 30-100 in the questionnaire just as a guess.
