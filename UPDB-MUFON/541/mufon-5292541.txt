One large white light split in to several.

I was looking at the sky while having my last cigarette for the night, and noticed a very bright light appear in the Northern sky. I thought at first it may be an airplane but it only had the one bright white light. I thought, search helicopter. THEN, it split in to 4 in a diamond pattern. I said "HUH". Then it was 6. As I continued to watch it was suddenly 8 of them and all smaller than the original. They flew in odd formations, hovered several times, split off and 4 went out of sight toward what seemed like the Air Force Academy. This was the first of 3 separate, but similar incidents. There were 2 other witnesses from the last time, but of course they laughed at me saying they were UFOs, and I have no way to contact them if they did care. I was unaware of the existence of MUFON until recently, or I would have reported it then. They happened just about a month apart, the last one around May 2017. I have no photos nor videos, I am sorry to say. They looked to be saucer shaped but they weren't close enough to be sure of the shape or sizes. all 3 rimes it started as one big light, then split to smaller, they spread out, then regrouped in to the big one and disappeared as instantly as it appeared. I am open to speaking with someone further, if you have any more questions.
