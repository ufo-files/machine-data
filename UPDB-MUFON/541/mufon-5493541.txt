I am 46 years old and i reside in Port Arthur, Tx. In 1979 0r 1980 im thinking it was the month of December at around 8:30 pm after dark, my step father, [name removed/dr/CMS] and i were driving east on 25th street toward 9th ave to a local grocery store called Weingarten. And as we step out our car to my amazement was to most extraordinary thing i have ever witnessed. Hovering over a neighborhood of homes on the right side of the road was this circular object with multi colored lights dancing and blinking around it! And it definantly wasn't an airplane.
