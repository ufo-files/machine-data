Driving. Looked up and saw object right above me. It turned with me around roundabout then flew off ahead of me

I was driving home and was crossing over the A34 Puddleton Bypass on the A354, which I would join. As I came to the small roundabout, which which would take me onto the sliproad, I looked up through my wind screen and I saw a triangular craft maybe 100 ft above me. There were lights underneath it but I can't remember any pattern / configuration. As I drove clockwise around the roundabout the craft turned with me. As I drove down the sliproad the craft accelerated and moved off ahead of me until I could no longer see it. 

The craft was maybe 100ft across but I can't be sure, maybe it was similar in size to a Vulcan Bomber. 

The reasons I can't explain what the craft was are:

It was silent
It was very low
It was very slow when it was above me
It was triangular
It made a very tight clockwise turn in tandem with me around the roundabout

I don't remember feeling scared. It happened quite quickly so I just observed it and thought about it later.
