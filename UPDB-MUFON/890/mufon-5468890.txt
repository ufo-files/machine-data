{rjl} looked up and saw a silent ufo pass over me

I was walking my dog down my street and looked up and saw a big mass moving over me  it was moving from the southwest to the north east and reflecting the sun and made no noise as it passed over me It looked to be about the size of a football field and between 1 and 2 thousand feet up At first I thought it was the space station, but I have seen the space station fly over before and this thing looked much bigger I tried to make it out but the edges were extremely bright and fuzzy  after it passed over me, it ejected what looked like sparklers for just a second or two I watched it until it went out of site.
