{nd} Single lights at equal distances from one another all flying Northerly direction, no noise, only one light each, total of 7 lights, not close together but equadistant from each other.

March 10th, a fairly warm eveining at approx. 8pm my daughter, myself and a neighbor across the street observed 7 amber colored orbs flying from south to north more or less following the Chenango River. My daughter was out walking the neighbors dog. There was absolutely NO noise associated with the orbs. We did hear an airplane flying behind us which was later verified to be Delta Flight #1578 flying out of the Broome Co. airport. Our first thought was that they could be Drones. 
We live in a Complex of townhouses and condos situated across the street form one another which only allowed us to view 2 orbs in the sky at a time. They were flying equidistant from one another and there were a total of 7 orbs in all. I went into the house to grab my binoculars but by the time I got back outside the 7th orb was flying over and I never got to view them close-up. Before I came back outside my daughter witnessed one of the last orbs stopping and moving from side to side before continuing to fly north. 
My daughter reported the incident to the local Sherrifs Dept.. A deputy took our report and verified the Delta flight. He also called her a day later to let her know that a fellow employees brother had also seen the same lights two nights before we did, from the highway just south of our complex.
The Deputy also added that the Lockheed Martin Plant in Owego, New York conducts "Skunk/Snoopy" operations from its facility....but Owego is southwest of here. That isnt the direction the orbs came from.
He filed a police report and advised my daughter that Chenango County would be advised of the incident also.
