{nd} Saw an object I could not explain

About 11:00 PM, while sitting in my van, I saw an unusual object hovering at a very low altitude, not much higher than treetop level. Since this happened in Cleveland, Ohio, I knew no plane or blimp would be flying over a city at that low an altitude. The object started moving in my direction, and then all the lights turned off on the object. No pilot would turn off his lights over a large city. As the object passed about a thousand feet from me, I could see the outline of the shape, because streetlights provide quite a bit of light. It was similar to a blimp shape, but had no tail or undercarriage. Also, it was totally silent. At that distance I should have been able to hear an engine. Then the object turned on a straight row of 9 or 10 lights, on the bottom of the object. Blimps do not carry long rows of lights on their small undercarriage. The object looked much over a hundred feet long. It passed out of my sight because the trees blocked my view as it kept moving at a very low altitude. I worked with planes in the Navy, so I am familiar with nighttime operations of flying objects. This object was something I can't explain.
