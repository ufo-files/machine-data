{cak} Three orbs of orange/red light hovering/swaying/dancing/moving over hwy going West

Coming home after dinner, we saw three orange/red orbs moving. They seemed to be dancing in the sky. We were traveling north on Hwy 37 near Derby exit. They were about 500 ft in the air. The objects were odd and we had never seen anything like them. We wondered if they could have been drones. They moved up and down, a couple of the orbs got close to one another. Nothing else was in the sky that night and I believe we were the only car traveling at that time. We were curious but calm to observe such strange lights. We lost sight of them because we never stopped, but kept driving northward to our home.
