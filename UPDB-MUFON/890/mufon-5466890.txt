My family and I were outside looking at Mars.(It won't be this close for another 260+ years) I was looking at the brightest star in the sky in the SW direction, and noticed a light moving. I told my family to look at that light moving, and that it was probably a satalite because it looked too high to be a plane and it wasn't blinking. As we watched it move North, my husband asked if we had seen that?! I didn't know what he was talking about, and then I thought my eyes were playing tricks on me! It was "jumping" from the straight path to just above, and then back so fast! So quickly that I would have passed it off as just a trick of the eyes if my husband and two children hadn't been witnessing the same thing!Then, a short distance ahead of it's traveling path was a bright flash of light. Like as bright as a shooting star, but it wasn't. With that, it disappeared. It was incredible.
