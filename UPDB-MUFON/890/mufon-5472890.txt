{cak} UFO Hovering Over Home Neigborhood

First of all,lets give you a little background. I live near an old decommissioned World War air base. Free air shows every year in the fall, get to see the blue angels and all other types of awesome air crafts. Just so the skeptics don't think i could mistake our current aviation technology with something ET :) I don't live in the suburbs, there's farmland all around & open skies, not much treeline around my neighborhood. Driving home on my usual commute from work around 4/430, I always take the same route home & make a few pit stops on the last main drag before I turn into my neighborhood. Coming up on the last two signal lights before home, I catch myself staring at this reflective long disk like ship right above my neighborhood. I have never encountered an UFO, i wasnt a doubter before just never seen proof through my own eyes. its about a mile or mile and half drag down the road, the longer i observed it and got closer home it descended closer to the ground. At my last stoplight i looked down to stop at the light and to go glance at it again it was gone. No planes, no birds, no clouds in the sky to cover this up. i wasnt scared or anything, just curiosity. I dont really know what to think about this maybe i have been in touch with ET since it was so close to home. believe what you want. im a believer now....
