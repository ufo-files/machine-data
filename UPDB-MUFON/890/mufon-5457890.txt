{tjd} Black Diamond - Rectangle Shaped UFO

I was walking eastward and looked up and saw a dark diamond - rectangular shaped object slowly flying northwards.

While I've seen several orbs in the area, this is the first time seeing a solid object of this size. 

At first I thought it was a plastic bag, but it didn't act like one. There was no flapping or movement at all. It was an extremely calm morning with no wind or breeze. I also considered it was some kind of banner ad, but there was no plane whatsoever. A drone would be flying level and make noise. This was completely silent. 

I managed to record about six seconds of it with an iphone before it disappeared from my immediate sight.

I don't know yet if anyone else in the neighborhood saw it. 

I took a very close look at the video and was able to use software to optically zoom in. Its definitely a solid object. 

I then looked online to see if there had been reports from others sighting black diamond UFOs, and found several, including report #99733 from Springdale, Ohio.

They look very similar.
