Large bright white-lit round object traveling east

Approximately 9:30 p.m. on September 13, 2011 I took the dogs outside on my property. While walking back towards my house I noticed a bright, white-lit, round object appear over my head and travel in a straight line due east. The object disappeared just short of reaching the tree line. I immediately assumed it was a meteorite, but I realized that it had no tail. I then assumed it to be a satellite or the space station, but it was too large.
