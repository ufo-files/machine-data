{jpg} white object descending straight down

I was looking at the moon on August 23, 2015 at approximately 8:16 PM. I saw an object next to the moon but just slightly lower than the moon. At first the object appeared to be a bright star or planet. However, the object was in motion and descending straight downward. I attempted to take a picture but I was unsuccessful. There were too many tree branches in the way and I had to constantly walk in order to try and keep the object in sight. Within several seconds or so the object was too low in the horizon for me to see it. The shape of the object was vertically oblong. The object was white in color. I speculated that the object could have be a hot air ballon on fire, a small aircraft on fire or a potential meteorite. Since I was in a highly populated area at the time of the sighting there must have been many others who saw it.
