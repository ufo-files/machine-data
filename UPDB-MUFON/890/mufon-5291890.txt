crescent moon shaped craft

was in zilker ark attending a music gathering and was looking up at the stars cause it was a open night, and from the right of my view in the south east side of Austin i see a grey almost the color of the sky crescent moon shaped craft floating across the sky, at first i thought it was a Frisbee or bird, but i noticed it stayed the same speed and direction the entire time.
i watched it fly almost directly over us and it emitted no sound and had no lights, it looked exactly like a crescent moon flying round face forward, it also emitted a type of trail, but not really, i could see it around it as it moved through the sky but not after it left the area so it immediately followed it,
