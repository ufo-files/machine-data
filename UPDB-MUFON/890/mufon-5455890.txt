Not a satellite...

Outside observing the clear night sky, spotted what I thought was a satellite judging by the height, the speed, the lack of any external lights of the object other than a star like glow, coming from the east. Observed the object rather high up, travelling at speed westward, where it then changed direction quickly to travel south, then changed direction again to travel back westward, then it once again abruptly changed direction to head south, then changed direction to head westward again all at speed, and then the object remained stationary for some five minutes, before continuing in a westward direction, before disappearing from my sight. This was certainly no satellite or plane, of that I am utterly convinced!
