{rjl} intermittantly flashing orange yellow light flying straight from the nw straight to se.

We were finishing having dinner on the deck. It was still light in the NW. A friend notice an odd light coming from the NW. It was a very bright orange yellow light coming from the NW, towards the SE. We thought it was a meteor or ufo because it didn't look or act like a star, planet or airplane. The light flashed intermittantly, but not regularly. It took about 30 seconds to cross the sky, and pass out of sight, behind the trees.
