I observed this in the background of a television program aired on H2.

I was watching a t.v. show that was recorded onto my DVR when I observed the object come on screen and moved from one end to the other, behind the narrator. I had to rewind and watch it several times to believe what I just saw. The narrator has no idea what flew behind him. The program is called "10 things you don't know about Pablo Escabar". It originally aired on April 2, 2012 on the History channel's H2 station. The object comes into view at about 3 min 50-55 sec from the beginning of the program and enters from the left and exits to the right of the screen. The narrator is standing outside in front of what looks like a mountain range with a white cathedral or old stone building in the distant background to the left of the narrator David Eisenbach. There appears to be some distortion of the atmosphere behind the object on the video. It comes in at about 160 degrees and changes direction behind the narrator and exits the opposite side of the screen at about 10 degrees, using the approximate lowest altitude of the object as 0 degrees. If for some reason you have a hard time getting the footage I still have it on my DVR.
