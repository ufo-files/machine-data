{dr}9:45 pm 6/17/14.. we saw 2 lights overhead glowing, moved NE then gone.

At 9:45 pm, my girlfriend and I went out back to lock up for the night, I saw one light then my gf saw another. They looked like satellites but there were 2 and they were near each other. They moved NE from SW very high up. One glowed bright for a second then dimmed. They both slowly went away. I observed one through 50x binoculars but couldn't distinguish what the heck it was. I also saw a satellite zip past in the same relative direction. I love to sit out in the back yard at night and watch satellites and such so I know what is what usually. Aviation background. No alcohol or drugs were involved. Thank you very much for the opportunity to report these events. Something is here I know it. I with a witness saw the HB police helicopter chasing a bronze colored sphere in 1997 (on file with Mufon) in broad daylight and I know for a fact it wasn't us.
