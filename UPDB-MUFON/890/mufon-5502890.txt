It was a bluiesh wavering light approximately 30' wide, leaving a wavering light behind as it traveled.

On May 5, 2014, approx. 1939 hrs, I was walking my dog in the garden, when I noticed a distance away a light wavering shape object in the sky right over the main town of Fajardo, Puerto Rico. The object appeared from nowhere it went up about 200 feet from the ground and started hovering for about 5 seconds and then it shot downward at about 60 degree angle and it disappeared. The object disappeared into the ground where it was dark. I also noticed in the sky there was a dark cloud stationary with lights going on and off near where the object disappeared.
