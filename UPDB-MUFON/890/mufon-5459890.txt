Attenetion drawn to what look like moving star

I live some 2000 ft above sea level, in the country pretty much devoid of artificial light, on the evening of 27 June 2019, at 1758 hours I was on my rear porch (no lights) just on dusk when my attention was drawn to what I thought was the evening star. After a brief time watching the star and an airplane on it last run into Tullamarine Airport Melbourne which is 40 km to the south from where I was located. I saw that the star was moving in an easterly direction. Living in the flight path of the commercial airliners I am very familiar with the resonance of airplane lights. This was not a plane and it immediately alerted me to the fact that it was definitely worth a closer look. I have a pair of astronomical binoculars stored in the sunroom adjacent to the porch and I immediately recovered them and looked at the object adjusting the focal length. I had a clear unobstructed view on a cloudless winter night of the object. As the image became sharper I describe it as elongated oval shape and I approximated the distance from me at 10km. I looked at the plane which was 20 km due south of the object and back to the object and estimated the object to be somewhere 1.5 the length of the airliner on approach (say 125-150 meters in length and about 50 meters high). I immediately called my wife and she could see the object moving without the binoculars. I watch it for about another 30 seconds as it began moving faster away from our position. Having watch aircraft gain altitude I am familiar with the reduction in the light as the altitude and distance increases. This object began traveling at very high speed and within another 5 seconds, it simply vanished. I witnessed a similar event some four nights before to the north of my position at 1 am in the morning but did not have time to confirm with my binoculars be for it too disappeared.
