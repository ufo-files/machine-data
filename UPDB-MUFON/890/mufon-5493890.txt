{rjl} Bright Object Moving

At the time we saw this, we lived on Hobson Drive. When we drove to Hobson Rd. going out to Philadelphia Rd., we could see it on the right. We took a right on Philadelphia Rd., and we parked at a church on the left. We saw a couple of people on a back deck across the street pointing and looking at it in a smaller house (we don't know them and didn't stop by). The UFO is close to the mansion on the road (almost above it). We looked at it for a while in the car because we were kind of scared. Finally, my wife got her waterproof camera and got out of the car. There were no dark clouds in the sky at the time. When my wife and I got out of the car, the light turned into a cloud. We managed to film the cloud hoping the UFO would come out. It never did. We think the UFO changed or camouflaged itself into a cloud for disguise.
