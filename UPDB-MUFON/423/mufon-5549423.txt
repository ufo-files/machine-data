Three ballon like flying objects.

10-year-old daughter and I were passing football in the back yard. Daughter, looking west toward the Everglades, said, "dad look, someone lost their balloon." I turned to see three, what at first looked like balloons, traveling north at about 40 degrees above the horizon. Within seconds I could see that two of the objects were moving side-by-side, but the third was moving around in an "S" pattern below the other two, occasionally moving above and to the sides of the others. The balloon idea left my head when I realized that the air was nearly still and what wind there was from coming from the North. So the "balloons" were going against the wind and now were speeding up. The objects were black and since the available light was fading, so to were the objects as they moved further north. Much to my surprise, and my daughter, the bottom object suddenly began to glow. Ten seconds or so later we lost sight of all three. Best size estimate, and only a guess..All three were possibly 20 feet in diameter. I only wish that I had thought to grab my binoculars.
