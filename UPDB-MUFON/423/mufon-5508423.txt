I video taped this at Busch Gardens while watching the fireworks

I was at Busch Gardens in Williamsburg Va. taping the fireworks. Just as I turned on my camera phone I noticed a beam of light in the sky, but thought nothing of it. I did not know what I had videotaped until a couple of days later. What you see is exactly what I filmed. What it looks like is an unidentified craft that seems to be watching the fireworks. It moves at incredible speeds in all directions. It has what looks like a light beam coming from the craft. I have frozen the video at various points and the shape of the craft looks like a space capsule or shuttlecock. At various times it is moving so fast it is just a blur of lights. On two of the places I froze the video it takes a different shape like it is warped. Thats the best I can describe it. Like I said at the time I taped it I did not know what I was filming. Only after seeing it later I was in awe by what was on the video. I have tried to think of every other possibility other than a UFO that this could be. A ride at the park no someone pulling a prank I don't think so. I have no doubts this is a real UFO. There were other people there that were filming this also. I cant be the only one to have this on video. Aliens from outerspace cant be all that bad, evidently they like fireworks too! If I had known I was filming this I would have left my camera phone on longer. Let me know what you think and if you have ever seen anything like this before.
