star like object appeared and flew in a south eastern direction very fast

I was outside on my deck at around 7:15pm. The stars are just becomming visible and i look up as I always do because I love looking at the stars and constellations. As I am doing so I see a small star like pulsating light travel above the small cloud cover in an opening in the cloud. (moon still visible through clouds) I watched it for awhile. It had to be up higher in the atmosphered and travelled faster than any satellite I have seen. The light was mainly pulsating, no blink at all. I waited as it would have been visible through the other side of the cloud it went above (it would have taken approximately 6 seconds top to travel back in a clear area of view) but it never came out. I immediately went onto spaceweather.com (as i do everyday) and checked for flybys. the only satellite that would be visible would be at 7:39 pm and i observed the satellite while writing this sighting. 
I have seen 2 other objects in this area a few months a part. one an orange fiery colored pulsating energy looking orb about 500 feet just before christmas last year with 1 other witness accross town. 
I was also watching the leonid meteor shower not too long ago and witnessed the same thing i did tonight, but this one did many maneuvers and changed directions.
