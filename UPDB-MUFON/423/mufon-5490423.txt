I was a child (10-13 years old) and went to bed at night. I woke up for some reason, feeling something is up. A strong wind all of a sudden opened the window of my room. I remember sitting on my bed, watching and wondering. All of a sudden, I found myself downstairs. The windows in the living room were wide open too. I went to close the window, I looked up and saw the sky covered with a massive object. I could not see the sky. The UFO was massive and was all over the neighbourhood. I ran to close the back door in the kitchen, fearing that something may come into the house. As I ran towards the glass door in the kitchen, I saw a small slender human like being coming down the wall, approaching me. I closed the glass window; but the being walked through it and spoke to me telepathically, reassuring me I was not going to be hurt. We had a long conversation. The being told me a lot about where he/she came from and had an equipment (almost like a tablet with moving pictures in it that showed me the path and the mathematical formulas they used to determine their journey to get to earth). All communications were done telepathically. The being told me not to be afraid and that they will come back one day. I was so absorbed and attached to the being, I had a hard time saying goodbye. To this day (almost 30 years later), I have thought about the being every day. 
I had some other experiences later on but nothing compares to this first encounter.
