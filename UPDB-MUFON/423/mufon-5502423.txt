{rjl} two red/orange orbs or lights circling around each other..

I was walking out onto my front porch periodically last night to watch for meteor shower. Around 3am I walked out and looked to the north and saw two red lights circulating around one another and I knew immediately that this was something out of the ordinary. After they circled around each other about 4 times one went straight down behind the mountains and the other just blinked out. The hair on the back of my neck stood up and I went back inside the house. I now wish I had stayed out longer and maybe seen something more but at the time I had no desire to meet any E. T.s.. I would be interested to know if anyone else in my area had a similar sighting.
