{nd} Triangle ufo

In Rutland close to the hospital we saw a triangle craft maybe 2000 feet up right above us. Yellow tips, red blinking light in the center. Heading south west and turned south east towards New Hampshire. It did not bank like a plane does. It showed up again about 15 minutes later and followed the same path. I'm not sure if the bright flashes of white orbs that would flash 3 times in 4 different spots has anything to do with the triangle craft. Those appeared about 30 minutes after.
