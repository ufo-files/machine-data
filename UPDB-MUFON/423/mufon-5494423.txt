{dr}a blinking star red, blue and white. Motionless but descended below horizon

while at home I observed a star that seemed to be flickering in the southwest night sky, about 9:30 pm. The object was showing what looked like aircraft marker lights(red/green)but randomly. I assumed at first it was an aircraft. The object did not move but gradually descended below the Southern horizon-in about an hour. It appeared to be in orbit. I called several people, my brother being one. He was in the high desert at Acton CA. and could see it. A co-worker at Chino Airport in San Bernardino County could also see it. I did report it to the Altadena Sheriff's station. I also filmed it, both still and video. I am guessing the space station. But I do not know.
