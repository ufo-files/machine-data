Bright orb of light caught in sky while photographing a sun dog

I stopped to take a photo of a sun dog outside our building. For some reason I was compelled to take a succession of photos. The duration of the photo session was about 3 seconds.
When I reviewed the pictures I noticed a bright ball of light to the top right. I didn't see this ball of light while I was photographing. I thought it might have been Jupiter so I zoomed in. I noticed that the HAZE around the orb had dissapated, meaning it was emitting enough energy or heat to burn off any clouds around it, which debunked the star theory. Upon further review of the photos I noticed I had also captured a plane, which flew in just as this orb was coming into the shots. Then as I moved to the next photo I further noticed that this ball of light was at first pale and higher up. As I snapped the next two shots (and the plane flew into view) I realized this orb had moved straight down, and had become brighter.
The amount of time it took to take the photos was approximately 3 or 4 seconds, meaning this ball of light had travelled that quickly downward in that short amount of time. Attached are photos of this pattern, and the plane.
What makes this photo chilling is that this is almost directly over a bombing that happened 48 hrs later.
