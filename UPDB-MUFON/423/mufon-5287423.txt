My girlfriend and I were driving in Crown Point, IN on 231. We were heading east into town; when we were between Parish and Cline (see picture), we noticed a white cigar shaped object in the sky. We thought it was a blimp since it had no wings, but it didn't seem like a blimp. It was traveling north, very slowly in a straight line, and seemed to be at an only lower altitude, quite lower than a plane would fly. When we were stopped at the stop light at Cline and 231, we both observed a small light or orb moving rapidly around the cigar shaped craft. It appeared to make rapid and sporadic movement around the craft, almost how the light of a laser pointer would move. The orb would also seem to go in and out of visibility. We were unable to continue tracking the craft and orb, but we both realized that what we saw was something that doesn't seem to have a rational explanation.
