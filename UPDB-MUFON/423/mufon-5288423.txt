Series of lights cause explosion, pick up passenger.

Early on January 19th. i was travelling west towards redwood road. there was a bright white light that flew over my car at incredible speed. shortly after it flew past me i saw what appeared to be a red light, and then a beam of red light. then what appeared to be an explosion. bright enough to light the sky. i drove quickly towards the area ending up IN front of railroad tracks and seeing a fuel truck burning. at this time i was unable to see the object. police showed up and asked me to vacate the area. on my way home i saw a disc of white lights, not even 2 miles away from me, hovering over an area of dense forest. it appeared to shine a second beam, white in color that quickly picked up what appeared to be a tall white entity. id assume approximately 7-8 feet tall.

it is my belief that this disc whether intentionally or now, caused the explosion on its way to pick up this entity. after the pick up the disc sped off at a speed i couldn't track. heading north east. the area of forest is now closely monitored by "private security" after several homeless people vanished while camping in the area.
