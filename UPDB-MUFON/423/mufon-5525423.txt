[tg] Dark triangular figure, with 5 dull

I was watching a meteor shower with my sister and mom. We were looking out the window to the backyard because it was very cold, and all of a sudden we see this weird triangular UFO fly over us. It had 5 dull red "lights" underneath, was somewhat low and moving quickly. It was completely silent! All 3 of us saw it and were very excited that we were actually seeing this. We really wished we had a camera. It disappeared behind the roofs of other houses in the neighborhood. We tried to get a better view in our front yard, but it was gone. Definitely not a meteor or airplane!
