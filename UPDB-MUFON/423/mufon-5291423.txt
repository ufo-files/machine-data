Definitely not balloons!

Walked out of my garage and looked up to see multiple bright shiny orb like objects moving randomly about. They were at least 1 mile up in the sky, which was a clear blue sunny day. At first I thought that they may he balloons, but then I realized the size of them. They had to be as large as a compact car at the very least. I really couldn't tell if they were emitting light or reflecting the sunlight, whichever it was, they were bright. Most all of the objects then proceeded to come together to form a larger object, with 3 of them maintaining shape and position, which was in a triangular shape. The larger object, or objects began to wiggle and wobble morphing into different random shapes. At one point more all of sudden appeared. It was one of the strangest things I've ever witnessed. No sound was ever made or heard during the entire time either
