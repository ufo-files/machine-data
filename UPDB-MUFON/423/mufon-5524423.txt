Fuzzy rememberance of the event. Was a child. All I did was run.

Me and my sister were walking home. We turned around and witnessed a circular object in the sky with pulsing lights. My sister immediatly told me to run so we did. I only had eye contact with the object for a few seconds before we both ran to our house which was maybe 150ft away from where we were at the moment. I only recall feeling scared and confused. I never looked back to see if the object was still there so what it did next I can't say.
