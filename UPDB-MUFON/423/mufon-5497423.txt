{nd} me and my mom watched 2 what looked like stars gracefully pass over our street radiating orange light and then slowly move on

on december the 7 ---2014 I was in my room on the computer as my mother was on the phone talking to my sister shell while sitting on the couch--when all of a sudden I heard her say in a upset but kind of excited way I see something outside what is that----I could tell by her voice something was wrong --so I got up and went out on the front porch to see what was wrong expecting to see a rare animal or something --- when I all of a sudden looked up in the sky and saw heading towards our house the most beautiful but unusual looking lights ---they looked like a star you could say heading towards out house radiating bright orange light --all I could say was oh my god --oh my god ---- they made no noise whatsoever --they seemed to glide almost in a graceful way --one following the other ------they stopped at the end of dartmoore rd here and turned right on glendale as if they were almost following the street ----they were no higher than I would say 50 feet in the air ---one hovered for a few seconds over us and then just slowly moved on towards broadway ---- I never in my life seen anything like this ---all I can say is they looked like bright stars ----my mom and me just stared in disbelief and awe and wonder and shock ---it was eerie in a way because all was quiet ---they just headed off and seemed to slowly dissapear from site ----  they radiated orange light ---beautiful --graceful how they moved very slow ----- will never forget-- we reported this to the police department not long afterwards -- I am still in shock ----
