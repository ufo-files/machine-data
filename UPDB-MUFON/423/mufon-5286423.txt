A green blinking light at the horizon

At 1:30 in the morning, as I was outside and looking at the sky I saw an object small in size, moving at a fairly high speed. It was obvious that it was not an airplane due to its excessive speed. At first when I saw it, I immediately thought it was a falling star, but suddenly it turned green and started blinking while its speed fluctuated, but always having a steady course. I watched it for about 3 to 4 seconds, until it disappeared on the horizon.
