{cak} A very bright light traveled through the sky then disappeared instantly

I was smoking outside with my wife when I was looking up at the sky and saw a bright light, I initially thought it was a star, but it was about three times the size of a normal star and it quickly darted in a diagonal before disappearing completely, like a blink of the eye. Logically I was trying to come up with what it could have possibly been. I thought shooting star but I have seen shooting stars and they typically have a trail leading behind them, and they move more quickly and also fade away as they move further away. So I had no logical explanation of what I saw. I was in shock, I had heard people say that they have seen things in the sky and I quickly brush it off, but when you actually do see something that you can1t explain you feel a bit crazy. I had goosebumps and was shaking after my experience because I had never witnessed something like this before and never thought I would. It1s actually a bit scary when you actually think about it.
