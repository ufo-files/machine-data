{cak} Cloaked Boomrang UFO over Louisiana

It was Thanksgiving morning around 1 am and me and two other friends walked outside to smoke a cigarette. I noticed a shooting star off to my right and we started talking about aliens and the universe. So as we were star gazing, I had the best view of the sky, I noticed a big chunk of the sky was darker than the rest. When I starred directly into it, I noticed it was a silhouette in the shape of a triangle. I could also see stars on the object. It was directly above us, I started getting the chills and I tensed up. All I could get out of my mouth was "cloaking, cloaking!" My other two friends started looking up around this time. It started to move and every star that it went in front of another star that it covered was projected on the front of the craft like it didn't want to be seen. It had a cloaking device, As it hovered over the house, it did some type of glitch and the cloaking device that surrounded the whole ship lit up and it appeared to be a greenish blue light, and then it went completely black and took off, and I watched it fly away towards the west coast. When it was flying off I could make out two big weird light looking circles on each side of the boomerang. When it was black it looked like it was something similar to the phoenix lights craft.
