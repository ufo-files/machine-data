Large white ball like sphere Glowing brightly. No sound. Faster than the eye on approach and takeoff.

On my way to Mexico in the early 80s. Large bright white light approached Myself and three members of my family and Kept speed with vehicle. Object appeared to be 50 to 100 feet away and about 20 feet off the ground. Highway was elevated above new volcano eruption and all The black ground was illuminated by white object. Brightest light I have ever seen and also fastest object I have ever witnessed.The strange thing was no sound !
