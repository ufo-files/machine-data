On May 15, 2017, at 216pm PST (partly cloudy)on Las Vegas Blvd, in North Las Vegas. I have a Sony a350 DSLR,1/2000, iso 400, f8, at full 300mm, shooting specs. I was shooting the Nellis AFB airplanes coming in for a landing. At about plane 18, I kept looking North down the Blvd and spotted a light coming behind and out of the clouds. There were a few cloud banks in the area and I began to shoot, even if I couldn't see it, I still kept shooting, I managed to get 10 shots off in 10 or 15 seconds. I recorded a image on 9 out 10. I blew it up to about 200 percent and it looked like a cloud of circular smoke. I probably don't have the best equipment to say what it was. As the Last frame shows it covered some distance really quickly that's why I was determined to just shoot blindly.
