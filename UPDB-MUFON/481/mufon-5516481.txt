Trangle hovers above me and a friend

This event happened on July 2000, around 23 PM. I was in the garden, playing with a friend. Suddenly she exclaimed "look up! Look up!" so I did: there was a triangular-shaped object above the house. In a blink it was above our heads: it was black with many yellow/red little lights and a big spinning sphere in the center. The nature of this sphere was very peculiar: it looked like a washer, there was an orange liquid washing around a yellow liquid. I ran to my house, I was really, really frightened, but my friend stayed there looking at the object. Once at home I yelled "we must call the police and the journalists!" to my parents. After a while I calmed down.
The next day I talked with my friend about it and she said that the object went away as soon as I ran screaming.
It was a life-changing experience, and I still don't know what that object was.
