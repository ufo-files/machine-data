Hovering craft with flames shooting from top and bottom

I was working at a gas bar in 1973. Sitting at my desk near the window I noticed what I thought was a small cloud shimmering in the sunset. As the other clouds drifted off over the horizon this on small shimmering cloud hovered in one spot. I went out to get a better look and that's when it started shooting silvery flames from the top and bottom about 20 feet. It looked like a mirror the way both flames acted identically and for the same duration, twice pre second. I felt very intimidated alone and isolated. I called the local radio station and asked if anyone reported a UFO, he said he had received another call. I said look out your window and you will see it. He came back on the radio and announced he also had seen the UFO. That was enough for me I called home and told my parents the object just started moving towards our house to go out and wait for it. I closed the station as soon as I saw it move off and when I got home about 10 minutes later it was dark and the whole community was outside talking about it and my parents said it was a triangle shape with 3 bright lights underneath it, no sound. Moments later 3 helicopter's flew over it the same direction and altitude. I was interview by cjoh by phone and described what I saw. It was seen by at least 40 people in our area.
