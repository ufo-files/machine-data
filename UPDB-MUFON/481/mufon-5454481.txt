{rjl} UFO flying above a Walmart supercenter

One night, me and my son went to see my husband at work. I picked my baby up from his car seat and I saw something in the sky, something oddly shaped, something that didn't look like it belonged in the sky, something that looked like no one was meant to see it and yet it was there. So I tried to capture photos of it with little time I had. The aircraft was silent but moving fast. I took two photos of the aircraft before it disappeared in the night sky. In both photos, all that can be seen is 3 white lights in the shape of a triangle. The aircraft was a bit far from where my car was but the lights and shape of the aircraft were visible to me. The aircraft made no sounds, no beams of light or E B.E'S were seen, only the exterior lights could be seen. This aircraft flew over our workplace, Walmart Pearl City in Honolulu, Hawaii on May 23, 2019 at 8:06pm. As far as I know, I'm the only person that has seen this aircraft that night.
