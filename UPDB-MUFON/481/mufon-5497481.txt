{nd} Orange orbs

I first spotted three orange orbs in a straight line horizontally. I watched them as they hovered but eventually they appeared to be descending but it was so slow you couldn't tell if they were until I used the top of a truck as a reference point and noticed after several minutes they had descended ever so slightly. I went inside for a few minutes and when I came back outside they were still there in a line but it was obvious they had descended. I watched them for another minute until they all three faded to black. After about 10 minutes the orbs reappeared but they were no longer in a line one was at a higher elevation and off to the left of the lower one. They were both in a different location in the sky than originally spotted. They both appeared to descend very slowly below the horizon maintaining an exact distance from one another
