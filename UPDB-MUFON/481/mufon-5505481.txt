{nd} UFO approached, changed colors, made u-turn and left.

The most interesting thing about this event was that I had a vision of the UFO several weeks prior to the sighting. The vision was of the same UFO exploding over a forest and flattening all the trees in its path. I went out to a lake at three AM a few weeks later and I saw a small red dot in the sky that approached me. I squinted my eyes at it when it got somewhat close and it changed from red to a shade of orange. After that it made a u-turn and was gone.
