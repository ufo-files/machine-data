My truck was facing south on Nooney St. Poplar Bluff Mo. On my way to work on Aug 9 at 5:28. As I reached for the door handle I looked up and thought to myself wow the moon is bright, definitely full. Entering the truck I thought to myself the moon should be close to new moon phase now. So I looked up through the windshield to confirm.  Yes, thats the moon alright, and its full. A couple of minutes later (approx. 4 blocks) as I turned on to Main St. I had an unobstructed view of a perfect crescent moon. I continued driving to work another mile or so then I thought, wait, if I see the crescent moon now what was the light I thought was the full moon? I thought... there was no sound, there was no movement. Strange, very strange.
