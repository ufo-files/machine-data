Saw glowing flying disk in night sky over Tanzania.

I was on a safari tour of Tanzania and we camped by the Indian Ocean near Dar es Salaam. I was talking with a Dutch member of our group about ten at night when we saw a disk shaped craft in the sky. It was glowing an orangish-red color and traveling away from the earth. We observed it for about ten seconds when it instantly vanished. It was difficult to accurately measure the objects size,speed or distance from us.
