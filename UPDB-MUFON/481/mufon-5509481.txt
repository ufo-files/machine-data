(GS) 7 or 8 orange steady light in sky moving east to west and spaced 90 secones a part

orange red steady lights east to west  counted about 7 or 8 spaced about 90 seconds apart. lights appeared to be on front of vehicles as once past they faded out just like a car headlight fades out. if the crafts were the size of a commercial plane they would have been 4 miles distant at about 10,000 feet altitude and traveling at about 300 mph. if crafts were larger the distance would be greater and the speed greater. the wife and i observed them and went outside and there was no noise of aircraft or other. this event occured at the time of the "not guilty" verdict for Zimmerman. the night was clear with unlimited visibilitly and still some afterglow from sunset. about 10 minutes after the last orange light faded a commercial jet from Milwaukee Mitchel Field passed from north to south with flashing red and white brilliant lights as unmistakenly an aircraft.
our home has large picture windows facing east, south and west and the orange lights caught my eye as not being normal for aircraft.
