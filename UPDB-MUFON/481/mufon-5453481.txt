{rjl} Encounter at Main Avenue - [name removed/cms/rjl]

This was my sister's encounter, so some of the information added may not be quite accurate since I was not the person who had this experience. But I would have been in bed across the hall from her and in bed. 

She was about 9-11 years old. She was awakened and got up from her bed compelled to walk to look at a window. She saw a glowing reddish orb of some kind emitting bright light. She went to our father's bedside and attempted to wake him several times by shaking his arm and saying "Dad" "Dad". She recalls he wouldn't wake up; which would have been very strange indeed. She remembers walking back into the hall and there in the hall in front of her was a small grayish human-like entity. She just stood there and doesn't remember anything other than she guessed it disappeared and she went back to bed. She recalls some kind of foggishness I believe outside where the orb was. But it also could have been fog the area sometimes would get.

This took place a few miles behind the back gate of McClellan AFB. Our father was a pilot at McClellan at this time, and still on active duty.

I had my own experience and abduction inside the area of base housing at Luke AFB, Glendale, AZ at about the age of 8-10. 

During this incident we lived in a much more rural area than it is today. What is now the Sleep Train (old ARCO) Arena was not even built yet. We lived on a couple acres of land and had two horses on the property.

Other than my father not responding or my mother not waking up, another odd thing is that we had a large male Weimaraner dog that would have barked and wanted to defend the property and its occupants.. The dog was not doing anything that I recall from her story.
