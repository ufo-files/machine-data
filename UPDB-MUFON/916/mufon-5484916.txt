Landed 50 years ago on common opposite our house.  Mum and I definitely saw it.

I was a child at home with Mum, just the two of us at home that day. We heard a loud vibration and our windows felt like they were going to shatter. We looked out of the living room window across to the common opposite our house and saw a disc shaped object land. The sound it made was loud and humming. It had lights shining down and we were afraid so hid under the stairs, at Mum's request. After 5 minutes or so, we heard it take off in the same manner. Years later a mobile phone mast was put in the same place it had landed all those years ago. As a child I had lots of dreams about the UFO's after the event. There was also a large pylon opposite our house at the time.
