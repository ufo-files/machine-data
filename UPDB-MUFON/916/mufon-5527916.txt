Red Light followed by helicopter

We were alarmed by a helicopter that was flying very low over our house. I ran out onto my front porch with my two children just in time to see a red light fly over directly over our house with a helicopter close behind. They continued east until they both disappeared. The helicopter was very loud and didn't sound like a medical helicopter. It sounded more like a military helicopter because of it's size and sound. The helicopter had blinking white lights on it. The red light was just a red light. Nothing more. I have no idea what it was.
