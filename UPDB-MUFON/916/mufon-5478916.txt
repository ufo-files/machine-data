{dr}morphing orb that started in san jose treeline and ended up near me maybe about a mile and a half away about a mile or so up. the other two appeared briefly near twilight following each other as bright lights that went straight up.

On dec 28th in the morning, i looked out my window and something caught my eye. I watched as a light started to ascend what looked like san jose. I initially assumed it was a drone but the way it was pulsating made me want to record it. I watched it hover and ascend slowly moving toward me. After recording for a bit on my iPhone 6s, I looked at the vid to find it was a light orb morphing and twisting into various shapes. I checked it out for awhile live, recorded some more then left to go do something else. I went back after an hour and it was closer but higher in altitude. I recorded it again. Watched it till the sun started peaking up. That's when the other two suddenly appeared. hovered for a second or two, then they followed each other out of my view.
