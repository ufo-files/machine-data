(GS) Very short bright white object came towards me as I recorded the sky then quickly shot back the exact way it came maybe 3 seconds in all totally silent and on tape recorded.

This night I was recording the sky with my fathers older VHS recorder on a tri-pod and I had it facing the moon looking South SouthEast and set to record for a full 4 hours on a tape. This sighting took place within the first half of the tape and was totally silent. I then transfered to my tiny olympus fe360 so I could upload to youtube and show some zoom and edge detection and then to slow it down and zoom to see this object closer.I have the original tape.It was a very short fast sighting yet the sudden complete opposite direction change along with the speed and silence and the brightness of this object was simply amazing. To me this object seemed to be under control.
