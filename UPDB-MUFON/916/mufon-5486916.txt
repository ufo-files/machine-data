{rjl} Starlike object descended straight to earth in a vertical path

I was awakened at 5:30 a.m. by a wind-like sound...wasn't sure if it was wind blowing outside, as it was fairly dark, although there was a full moon. I looked out the window directly by my bed to the east to see if tree branches were blowing to indicate wind...there was a bright star-like light, to the east which dropped straight down to the ground. It was the size and brightness of venus. Not a meteor, as it fell straight down to earth. Hard to say how far away it was or where/if it landed on the ground. No idea what this could have been.
