Disc shaded, with dome top, Lights coming on and off, one red light always on.

I was in my room at 9:28pm I was having some trouble going to sleep, and I saw an odd shape in the sky so I get up looked out my window, it had two lights on both sides that where on the whole time, and one time all of the lights came on, all the lights were on the bottom,there was just enough light I could see the out line of the ship,it was disk shape with a dome top.I was pretty calm just frozen. I would have pictures but my sister wouldn't let me use her camera, the funny thing is she told me in the morning I should of came and got her camera. If you held a quarter up with one eye closed it would just barley cover it.
