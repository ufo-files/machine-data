{jpg} Two orange red lights moving slowly across the sky and eventually turning into small dark clouds that flew south out of sight.

Looked out my window shortly after 1 am and saw an orange red light moving slowly from west to east in the distance. At first glance it looked like aircraft lights, but it didn't seem quite right. The lights were moving slower than most airplanes I've seen and the lights were the wrong color. Also the lights didn't blink like I'm used to seeing on conventional aircraft. I observed it getting closer and was thinking it might be a helicopter considering the slow speed of it's approach and it also seemed to be slowing down to a hover. I grabbed a pair of binoculars and headed outside for a closer look. By the time I got around the front of my apartment, the object changed direction and was heading south. I looked at the object through the binoculars and under closer inspection it appeared the orange light was revolving on an axis. As the light got further away it seemed become surrounded by a dark cloud which eclipsed the light and made it almost unnoticeable. I watched the dark cloud which eventually turned into a lighter shade of grey as it headed south behind some buildings and out of sight. By this time another revolving orange light was noticed coming from the same direction and it too made the exact same turn and turned into a dark cloud that headed south and out of sight.
