Star Like Light Viewed Against Low Overcast Cloud Ceiling

While driving east on highway 285 into work the morning of the 19th in January, I saw a flickering yellow/white light in the southeast sky. It caught my attention when I saw how it stood out against the dark morning cloud cover. There was no breaks in the clouds and no rays of sun light were visible. I have worked in service in the aviation industry for almost 10 years and I have lived near Lackland AFB as well. Any lights I have seen before in the sky have been easily explained until that morning. This light resembled a single flickering “headlight” in the morning sky over southern Denver east of Littleton and I-25. There was no flashing red/blue/green beacon lights and no nav lights. The object hovered in place and was in sight for 10-20 seconds before it dimmed and disappeared into nothing. No breaks were made in the clouds and it never rose up into the clouds or fell down to the ground. It was like someone turned a dimmer switch slowly until the light was no longer visible. Since I was driving into work at the time I could not get a photo of it with my phone.
