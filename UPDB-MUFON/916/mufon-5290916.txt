2 white long lights and strange sound

Sunday, November 15th, I'm alone outside filming the sunrise. It's 6:40 am, on a rural road in a small town in Quebec, Canada. No car, nobody, no sound. My camera is on a tripod in the middle of the street. It' a little Nikon Coolpix. I'm zooming to have a nice shoot. I'm standing next to it. In front of me, a land and behind it, a wood. So, I'm standing in the middle of the road, looking in front of me, I heard a strange almost metallic sound and saw two long white lines flying horizontally in front of me. I'm about 150 feet from the two lights. They flew at about 12 to15 feet from the ground. I was surprised, shook my head and continued to watch the sunrise. I even forgot about the incident until I downloaded the videos from my camera to my computer. There is no mirror or glass in front of me to cause a reflection, nothing. And that sound...
