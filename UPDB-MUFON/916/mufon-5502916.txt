{nd} Flat Pulsating Diamond Shape slowly hovering across interstate than changed direction and sped away.

My wife and I were headed home from Nashville and were a few miles north of Murfreesboro when I noticed a strange light in the sky to the south-west. I believed it was a plane at first, then a helicopter due to it never seeming to move. I assumed I was following it by coincidence. As we started coming closer to it, my wife suggested it was a tower that had a malfunctioning light. Then it slowly crossed the interstate, heading north-east and then sped away, about 5 times faster than it had previously been hovering. The object itself, as it finally flew overhead, I could tell was very flat, but upright, and diamond-shaped. It glowed white and pulsates at a rather even rate. At random times a blue light flashed from its bottom area, but not in any regular pattern. I was excited by seeing it and it raised many questions for me. We lost sight of it due to driving down I-24 and the fact it randomly sped away at an angle behind us.
