{rjl} Hovering and erratic movement

We were outside in the field watching the Lyrid Meteor shower, looked towards the North, Northwest, and saw a bright light in which we first thought was a star, upon looking at it, it was changing colors from red, to blue, to orange to white, the it started moving from left to right, towards us and then away, small circles then it just seemed to be bouncing around, also during this time we seen a smaller orange object come from behind it headed in a south westerly direction. it stopped probably a few miles from the original object and was also dancing around in the sky. we watched it for a good 10-15 minutes, it was brighter than all the stars in the sky. we looked away towards the south west as a bright shooting start caught our eyes and when we looked back, both object were gone.
