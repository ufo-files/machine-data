{rjl} Alien encounter

One night I woke up about 3 am, I noticed 3 grey aliens at the foot of my bed my body was paralyzed and I was unable to move or speak. One of the aliens inserted a thin metal rod into my lower stomach area there was no pain but I was scared at the time I was only 10 years old. I never told anyone about this encounter except my wife.
