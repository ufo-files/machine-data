{cak} Our family witnessed a large, black, serpent like object rolling in the bay as going by. 6ft. hight 10ft wide 30ft long.

My family & I were going on a fishing trip one cool morning. As we drove through the channel in my fathers 18 ft. boat we noticed something in the water. My father first seen it.He acted startled & when we all seen it we were all afraid. We had to drive around the object for it was directly in the middle of the channel.My broth 9 years old & I 11 were frightened due to the fact that the object was larger than our boat & also our parents were scared too.The best i can describe the object is black in color, shiny & smooth like a dolphins skin. The object appeared to be rolling through the water like a snake, continually. We got around the object and my Father put the throttle down & we got out of there quick. We never spoke of the incident until a few years ago. We all seen the same thing but no one can explain it. This happened almost 25 years ago & just wanted to share our story. Are there any other sightings like this reported on lake erie?
