{rjl} 4 red and 4 white ovals white chased red.

AT MY HOME ON SILVER CREEK ROAD,SAUCON VALLEY.STANDING ON MT PORACH LOOKING OUT OVER LAND SCAPE.ABOUT 9PM CAUGHT OUT THE CORNER OF MY EYE SOME OBJECTS.8 PASSED ABOUT 1MILE IN FRONT OF ME.OVER FARM LAND,BY THE SAUCON VALLEY PIPELINE.4 RED IN FRONT FOLLOWED BY 4 WHITE. GUESS ABOUT 1 MILE APART.WHITE CHASING RED OBJECTS.RED WENT OVER THE TREE LINE.THEN WHITE.SAW MANY FLASHES.THEN WHITE OBJECTS FLY BACK OVER SAME TRACK BUT IN UPWARD 80 DEGREE ANGLE,VERY FAST.I MEAN SO FAST TOOK SECONDS TO DISAPEAR.AT THE TIME I WAS 15 YEARS OLD.IN 1968.NOW IM 62.TRIED TO TELL LOCAL COPS BUT THEY DID NOT BOTHER.I KNOW EVEN THEN THE DIFF. BETWEEN CHOPPER,PLANES,BALLONS.NO ELECTRICAL,CLEAR STARY NIGHT.LIVE IN WOODED AREA AT THAT TIME.WAY OFF MAIN ROADS BY 6 MILES. THIS THE FIRST TIME WRITTING THIS DOWN. NO PICS.TO FAST.
