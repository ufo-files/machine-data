2 lights hanging for half an hour.

I was out in my back yard having a cigarette when my youngest son pointed out 2 lights in the clouds.At first i thought it was search lights coming from the market area or even from the Blues festival till the thicker clouds partially covered the lights. So i was running all the possible causes in my head still thinking this should have a logical explanation till one of the lights took off heading east at high speeds crossing the horizon within seconds.The second light made a check mark movement as it also left at high speeds leaving me in a state of what i can only describe as euphoria. I didn't get any photos for the only camera i own is on my phone and phone cameras take crappy pictures at any distance.My wife also saw these.There was no sound.Also when they left, the one heading east went through a clear patch of sky but was still fuzzy like a lit up cloud.I think that's all of it but if i remember anything else i will post it.I am really excited with this citing i have seen strange things before like satellites chasing each other but this was my closest citing which from what i can tell was just above the lower cloud ceiling.
