Saw orange ball moving silently across sky, change direction then disappear.

Stepped out of my house, looked up and saw orange ball/orb moving silently across the night sky. At first I thought it was a plane, but the light didn't flash, and there was no noise at all. It tracked from the northwest, then changed direction due west, appeared to speed up then dimmed out. Took a few pics with my phone, but they aren't good enough to show any features. I have seen chinese lanterns in the sky, this was no chinese lantern. I have no explanation as to what this object is. Will definitely look up more often now.
