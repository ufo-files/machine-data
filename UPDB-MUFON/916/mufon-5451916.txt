Triangle shaped craft

Sitting out on a hotel balcony downtown Savannah over 10 stories high when an object came from the north at pretty high speed and stopped above and behind St. John's Babtist Cathedral . It had 3 bright lights lined up in a row . The left light was green , the next 2 lights were red . The green light blinked several times and the object made some very quick moves pretty much in all directions . Up, down , left , ECT . It then moved quickly North over the Savannah River , hovered for a few seconds , then dove at high speed straight for the river .
