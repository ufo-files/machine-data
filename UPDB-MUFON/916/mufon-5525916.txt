first one was hovering low colorful twinkling lights/second streaked across sky very fast

My wife let our 3 dogs out at 3:30am. At 3:45am she went to let them back in and noticed bright flashing colorful object in NE sky and woke me up to see it. I got up and grabbed the binoculars and my cigarretes and went outside to see. It was very low and bright flashing red & blue&green and white. It kept jumping or spining up and down and side to side slightly. Truly a beautiful sight to behold, the closest I've ever seen. It stayed in the same area moving slightly for a hour and a half. Then it started moving higher and further away till it just looked like a colorful bright star in the distance.I sat in a lawn chair drinking coffee and smoking for two hours till the sun was rising and lit the sky so I could'nt see it any longer. Around 5:30 am or so I was scaning the SE sky behind me and saw a bright light scream across the sky at a very fast rate of speed. I sat outside watching with amazement and goosebumps all over for two hours. My wife went back to bed after watching the first object for a hour or so. I've been watching the sky since I was a kid. I'm 47 this year and this is the first time I've been able to see one this close. I feel like a kid on Christmas morning. I'm up for the day now, I could'nt go back to sleep if I tried. I feel truly blessed to have had this experience!
