I saw a UFO above the ShopRite parking lot in West Nyack. It was three big orange lights in a row horizontally, moving noiselessly and southeast....so I stopped and looked at it....then the three orange lights moved to form a triangle...then I said out loud, "Holy sh*t!" and it immediately disappeared completely and all at once....only to reappear a bit ahead blinking like a 747. Noiselessly. And entirely too low that far from an airport. I watched it until it seemed to mysteriously fade into the light pollution of the Palisades Mall.
