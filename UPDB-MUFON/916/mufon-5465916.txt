{cak} large white object with a black object pointed up on the left side

I was headed on Freeway 8 East bound, On Sunday around 2:45 pm on 9/9/2018. I was approaching College Ave exit and I noticed a large white object in the sky at about 20,000 feet. It was just staying still, then I noticed to the left end of it there was a black fin standing pretty tall. Trying to get an idea how big this thing was, I was looking around and saw a airplane coming into San Diego airport to the south of me. It was much closer to me, so I look at it's size and then looked back at the object and the object was much farther away but it was four to five time bigger than the airplane. Then, the object made a 45 degree dive toward the ground in a blind of an eye and stop at about 5000 feet at the same time it made the move it always stayed level. It was unbelievable how fast it moved. I have never see anything move that fast. After that I was trying to watch it but I was also driving down the freeway. The object hovered for a bit, then it started to move slowly to the south. I then pulled off the freeway in La Mesa. Got out of the car, but at that time I could not see it anymore.
