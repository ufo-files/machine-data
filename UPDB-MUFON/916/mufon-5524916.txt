flight from north to south crossing paths with 2nd identical object flying from east to west

at the time of the sighting my girlfriend and I were camping 8 miles east of the town of Piedmont SD, east of the Black Hills of South Dakota. I awoke around 4am and went outside of our cabin (for some unknown reason) to have a look at the night sky, since I am an avid skywatcher. there from the porch I looked to the west when I saw an extremely bright light flying at approximately 1000 feet towards the south. this object flew at about the same velocity of a private airplane and was pulsating the same as an arc welder would (with the same light intensity) with intermitant flashings of faint red and blue lights... red on the left side and blue on the right side of the object. this sighting lasted several minutes when I noticed a 2nd identical object about to cross its path traveling from east to west. this scenario played out for sveral more minutes until the objects crossed paths about 3 0r 4 miles away from me. the object then faded from my view. this is not the first sighting of this kind I have had in fact, this is the 4th time I have awakened at this time of night, for the same inexplicable reason. it seems that I am being awakend from my sleep just to observe these kinds of objects. At first I thought it was wonderful but now I am concerned as to what reason this has been happening to me. As I mentioned above, this has been my 4th sighting of this type in about a two-year time frame.
