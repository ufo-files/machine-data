{cak} Large Dark craft blending in with night sky but still visable

My daughter was playing in a softball tournament in Clermont one weekend in November. Her last game was over around 8 and we were standing at our truck in the parking lot loading up equipment and talking when an object caught my attention because of its movement in the sky. It was a large shaped object that was dark almost blending in with the sky just maybe a shade darker but I could make out some square window like cutouts. It made no noise and it had no lights at all. It just floated across the sky above us and went further away until not visable any longer. When it first caught my eye I immediately told my husband to look. He said he could see something moving and could follow the movement but couldn't see the details like me cause his eyes are not good. But he did agree with me that he could see it moving and that it had made no noise and didn't have any lights. I knew it wasn't any kind of plane or anything there was just no way. It was long and I didn't see any wings. It was very weird. It wasn't until I was watching a UFO show on TV yesterday where the guy described seeing almost exactly what I had seen that I decided to report my sighting as well.
