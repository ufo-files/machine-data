{jpg} diamond object in sky with a definite pattern of black dots.

While leaving to go to work, I exited my development and noticed what seemed like a flock of birds in a strange pattern. I slowed the car to a stop and studied the object for several seconds. I could then determine that the object was staying still and was translucent with black dots forming a triangle/diamond pattern. It almost looked like a blue kite with black dots forming a perfect pattern similar to bowling pins. The larger part of the object was at least 30 of these dots on the front triangle and 10 dots on the back triangle. It seemed to be at least 1 to 2 miles away with very clear blues skies. I continued to stare until a driver behind me beeped and started to leave. I then could not find the object once I drove away.
