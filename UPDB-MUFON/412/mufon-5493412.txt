I got off work around 8:30 P.M. and made my way to exercise at the YMCA. At the time I was on a call with my mother from the time I left work until I arrived at the Y at about 9:00 P.M. I turned my car off and stepped out into the parking lot when I looked up into the clear night sky, and that is when I noticed the UFO. I told my mom "hold on a second" and continued to watch the UFO until it was too far off in the distance to clearly see. The UFO had a solid shape in the shape of a guitar pick and had nearly a hundred flickering lights on the belly of the ship. My sighting lasted about a minute or so before it was gone. I told my mom what I just witnessed and she called bologna. To this day I promise I know what I saw wasn't my imagination.
