{tjd} Visitor in the storm

Around 5pm I snapped two quick pictures of the storm overhead. Probably 10 secs apart or so. No rain, lightning or aircraft. The winds got really intense. Wasn't until a few days later I noticed the saucer. You have to zoom in to see it in detail. The first pic has a green line going left to right in the cloud, then a blob of green that almost looks like a face or something. Around that it almost looks like a ring the same color as the cloud surrounding it.

The second pic the saucer appears to be coming out of that same specific area. Has classic dark bottom and somewhat of a discharge around the bottom. Detail just isn't enough to clarify. This was taken on camera and not seen with naked eye. Nothing follows
