{nd} I took several pictures directly into the sun with my Samsung Galaxy Note 4 cellular phone consecutively. Upon returning home in viewing the film of the clouds in the Sky, I noticed one picture has a blue object I can't identify on my own

I was picking my daughter up from school and felt the urge to take photos of the sky directly above my vehicle and into the sun. I don't know why I had the urge to take some pictures immediately. Upon returning home, I was reviewing the photos I took that day and noticed that although taken rapidly in succession only one photo showed a blue object in front of the sun. I felt upon seeing the photo that I was looking at an object in the sky that I could not explain and the feeling that I must disseminate the information to anyone who might be able to offer an explanation which lead me to your site. I look at the photo every day, as if obsessed with finding out what the object could be. Now I have caught more types of unidentified objects in photos since this occurrence and feel a strange calling to capture this phenomenon on a daily basis. I feel like I have been chosen to capture the images.
