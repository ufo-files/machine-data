{dr}Two entities with lights in front of barn at night. When approached went back behind barn out of sight. Two craft took off from behind barn shortly after.

I am writing this for my friend who does not own a computer. This is a years long on-going thing at his farm in Georgia with many witnesses at times, and there were two occurrences last week.In the past he has gone to the sheriff, the GBI, and the military for help to no avail. He sees these bright white lights at night hovering with no noise. There is a smaller green light and an orange light to either side. An evening last week he was out in a pasture with his son and saw two figures with small lights in front of his barn. When he started walking up to them, they turned and went back behind the barn out of sight. Then two of these lights took off from behind the barn making no noise. There was no trace of the figures afterward so it was assumed they left with the lights.
He has a field camera that takes constant pictures when anything moves. During the day it took one picture of a silver disc about twenty feet wide and seven tall.
