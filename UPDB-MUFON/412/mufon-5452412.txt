Black/ silver rotating oval object in London

UFO seen in West Hampstead, London yesterday at around 7pm. It was silver/ black and oval donut shaped. It moved gracefully but purposefully and I watched it for about twenty mins before it disappeared from view (not suddenly). It was lower than a plane flies and higher than a drone. As it moved (fairly slowly) it seemed to rotate. I couldn’t work out if it was silver or black as it seemed to change and wobble somewhat. It’s the fifth time I have seen something like this.
