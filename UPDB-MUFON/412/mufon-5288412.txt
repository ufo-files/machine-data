Three (3) solid red lights forming a triangle; no sound; flew over West to South East

At approximately 930 p.m. on 4/19/21 we saw 3 red lights, equally spaced into a triangle, spacing remained fixed, fly over our property just outside of Bennettsville, SC. No sound. You could see the sky between the lights. Although it is difficult to gauge size against the night sky, I would estimate the lights to have been spaced approximately 100 feet apart. I would estimate the elevation to be approximately 500 feet, possibly up to 1000 ft. I did not have my phone with me to get video or photo, but I yelled for someone in the house to come out to see it, they saw it after it had passed over and was behind the tree line. If I had to guess what it was, the only possibility I can reach is synchronized drones; however, this is a very rural area and I cannot imagine who would have drones out there. The entire event lasted less than one minute.
