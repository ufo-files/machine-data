{cak} Glowing red light observed for 15 minutes

Date: Saturday, August 11, 2012
Time: Approximately 9:15 PM (CST)
Weather: low 60s, clear skies w/ some transparent clouds

I was among friends gathered for a BBQ, and at approximately 9:15 PM (Central Standard Time) one of them mentioned a glowing, slightly pulsating red object in the sky that he had observed for the last 15 minutes. I looked in the sky, about 30 degrees above the horizon, facing North/Northwest and saw the same thing. 

Being in Iowa City, we hear/observe helicopters on a daily basis, transporting patients to the UIHC, but this object had a single redish light that glowed and slowly pulsed, not flashed like a typical beacon light. It also never got smaller or larger in size, so it appeared to hover in the same position and we could not hear any noise. With this information we ruled out a helicopter approaching or departing the hospital.

Another person had the "Pocket Universe" app on his Android phone, so he started it up then pointed it to where the object was. It did not display any constellations in the North/Northwest direction where the object was at so we now ruled out any stars or planets, and since it was stationary, we ruled out shooting stars or satellites.

Five or more of us observed the object and when it disappeared (around 9:15 PM CST), it did not move, the light simply faded from the spot we observed it.

About 5 minutes after the object left, a hospital helicopter flew overheard, which we all heard and saw with its multiple lights, and at a much lower altitude and closer distance to us, than the other object.
