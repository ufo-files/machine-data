{cak} Dark triangular craft with orange lights

I saw a large dark grey craft flying North to South from my bedroom window. It had orange lights in a circular pattern on the belly of the craft and orange lights on the tips of the "wings" and front with a white flashing light on the bottom on the end. It was heading in the direction of the airport near me. It looked much to large to be a regular jet especially with it flying so low. It went a little slower then a plane would also. It was a little cloudy. It happened around 12:15 am EST.
