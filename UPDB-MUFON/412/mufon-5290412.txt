Sphere-Silver Ball

I am a 12 year Security Officer my post was at a papermill plant that day my shift started at 6am and I am on the south end of the mill at the security booth while I was on duty at approximately 10:30am I decided to perform a walking patrol as I started to walk down the contractors road, there was an eclipse happening that day from 1pm to 3pm...I looked up at the clear blue sky and happened to see an object that I thought at first was a balloon but the more I looked at it, it seemed to be a silver ball stationary in the sky. I flagged down several workers to show them, they got out of their trucks one of the workers took out his binoculars to get a better look then he handed me the binoculars, the object was no balloon as we at first thought because it had no people under a hot air filled balloon no motors or appendages and it made no sound the entire time this object remained motionless for approximately 15 minutes I decided to continue my patrol after a few more minutes the object then moved very very slowly away to east so slow it could not be of any craft known.
