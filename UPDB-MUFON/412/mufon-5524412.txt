Summer time in urban neighborhood, saw a diamond shaped object over homes and power lines

Object was diamond shaped and came straight down and then hovered over the houses then moved over to the middle of the street were it hovered for approx 20sec before going straight up. I was sitting on my neighbors steps and all of us and other neighbors saw it we all kind of just froze! I was a kid then and just recently remembered it. I asked some others who were there when we saw it and none remember. Some of my childhood friends however do recall seeing a object that I described later on in life.
