Video of UFO with several lights

I went onto my balcony to take a video of the sky because it was very gloomy and ominous. During that time I noticed what I thought was a dust particle on my camera lense. Went back to view my video for posting and noticed the “dust” particle moved unexplainably. Zooming into my video I notice lights on the object, it hovers, moves up and down, then accelerates into the downtown Houston area. Come out of the clouds, then disappears. My phone (which I used to record) had glitches, and was unresponsive following the incident.
