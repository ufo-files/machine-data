2 SPHERE-LIKE OBJECTS HOVERING/MOVING ..FLUTTERING/SHIMMERING LIGHT IN EAST SKY

AT APPROXIMATELY 4:30 A.M.ON SUNDAY, DECEMBER 27, 2020 i WAS DEALING WITH INSOMNIA AND HAPPENED TO LOOK OUT MY BEDROOM WINDOW. I SAW WHAT I THOUGHT WAS A SHOOTING STAR, BUT NOTICED THAT IT STAYED IN ONE AREA. I WATCHED IT FOR A WHILE AS IT MOVED SLIGHTLY IN DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS, THEN OBSERVED A SECOND "ORB" MAKING SIMILAR MOVEMENTS. BOTH OBJECTS FADED IN AND OUT AND MOVED IN SIMILAR PATTERNS FOR A PERIOD OF APPROXIMATELY 30 MINUTES... I TOOK MY IPHONE AND FILMED FOR 20 OR MORE MINUTES, UNTIL I GOT TIRED OF FILMING. i DID NOT OBSERVE WHEN THE OBJECTS CEASED TO BE SEEN... BOTH OBJECTS APPEARED TO BE FLUTTERING OR PULSATING AND MOVING IN A FAST CIRCULAR MOTION. THE OBJECTS EMITTED A BRIGHT WHITE AURA.
