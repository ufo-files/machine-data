{cak} Looked like a star in the sky. Yet it would move left to right, up and down

At approximately 2:15 am, I was standing out front of my job. And noticed a star??? That seemed to move in several directions. No great movement was noticed. But it was moving it would dip then lift, move left to right then right to left. I called a coworker from inside the store. And she also noticed the movement. Another coworker was reporting for her shift. Both myself and the first coworker asked her to take a look. She also noticed the movement. Then break time was over. When I went back outside during my lunch break at 4:30 the object was gone. I called to the first coworker to take a look. There was nothing there but open dark space. It seriously looked like a star high in the night sky when i first noticed it. Yet it was no longer there two hours later. There was nothing. I read earlier in the day on Yahoo. The the Military was launching three missiles on the east coast. And that NASA had spotted a star which they said was due to super nova. But no time line was given other that in out life time.
