While driving toward the east, we noticed a very bright red light that formed in a line that appeared to be diagonal to the horizon. It pulsed red, blue, purple, green, and white in a random pattern. We continued to see it south of us for several miles. Then, a much brighter white light appeared below and behind the first. It moved toward the colorful pulsing light. After only a few seconds of being followed, the colorful light accelerated quickly, turned to the north and rapidly moved out of sight. As soon as the colorful light started accelerating, the white light turned off.
