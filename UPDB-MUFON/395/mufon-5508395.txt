Bright glowing, red outer, orange center, globe about the size of a UPS van.

I was relaxing watching TV when a neighbor friend called me on the phone and told me to come outside quick, you need to see this. When I ran outside to see what was going on, I saw my friend running up the street toward my house. I thought he was chasing after some type of animal. He yelled look at that, what the hell is it. Then I saw it. It was diffently a UFO for sure. It was Hovering just above the trees right down the street where I lived. It was a round globe object, bright glowing red and orange in color, and looked to be about the size of a UPS van. There was no sound at all coming from this object. The exact time was 8:32pm and there was no wind or clowds in the sky. It was a nice clear night, it had just gotten dark and the Moon was nearly full. My friend said the object (UFO) had come from the southwest and then stoped and hovered, when I came out and seen it. The UFO was about a two hundred feet above the ground and like about 500 ft away. It hovered for about a minute as we were frozen in amasement watching it, and then I thought and said "oh @#$% we need to get a picture of this". At this time the UFO moved very quickly up and toward the west about two miles and stoped and hovered once again. I told my friend to keep an eye on it as I ran into my house for my camera, but then I remembered the batteries in it were dead and needed to be changed. As I grabed my camera, I also grabed a fresh pack of batteries and ran back outside. Low and behold the UFO was still hovering. By this time the UFO must have been hovering there for about two minutes. As I was in the process trying to change the batteries in the camera, the UFO moved ever so fast stright up into the stars and I was unable to get a picture of it. Oh my God what a sight this was to see, and yet no picture to show for it, [Item removed/CMS/RJL]. Oh well, Ill be ready next time.
