Looked like red

I heard a thumping noise on the roof (?), and went to ask if my housemate had heard it. He had, so I stepped out onto the porch to listen, and in the distance I saw a very bizarre light in the sky. (No, I havent been drinking. Nor am I on intoxicants of any kind, and I have a fellow witness!) With the naked eye it looked like just a pulsing light hovering above the treeline, making a few sharp darting movements. Through binoculars (Nikon 8x40 8.2) it was a ball/cluster of strobing LED looking red, amber, green (at least 15-20 individual lights, all flashing in no sequence or order). It moved left. Went below treeline for a moment, came up, moved right, went below treeline again, came back up farther to the right, moved a bit back left, went down below treeline and was gone.
