My son and I was watching TV and the outside and inside dogs started barking as if someone had pulled into the driveway. I looked outside and no one was there. They continued to go crazy so I looked outside my living room window and see what I thought might be a helicopter.I went out on the front porch and watch this object move slowly across the sky. I tell my son to come outside and see the lights because it is different than anything i have seen before.The lights seem steady with no blinking and it moved around slowly, then it disappears in mid air. There was never a sound and the dogs were barking and looking up at the object.I did not have any reaction to this event. I see helicopters and airplanes a lot and this was not any of them.I did't get to take a picture or video but this is not the first time i have seen strange objects in the air.
