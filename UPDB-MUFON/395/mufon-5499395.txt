{nd} Vacation in the rocky mountains. friends and i saw 3 ufos

I traveled to the rocky mountains to go camping with two friends (a couple) and we witnessed some strange lights in the sky. I took a couple pictures and captured 3 short clips from my cell phone. The videos are a little hard to see but you can see the blinking lights. we had set up our tents and made a fire. We saw what looked like a very low silent plane fly over head very low very silent. I noticed the colors on it seemed a little strange. I served several years in the military and I never saw the lights blink that fast in that motion. Well I never took my eyes off the object as it flew out toward the horizon. I finally got the courage to tell my friends I was still looking at it.I dont think at first they gave it much thought or paid much attention. They came over to where I was standing and were both amazed when they saw it bopping up and down like a balloon and also noticed the colors were strange. That one stayed i nthe sky for hours. During that time we noticed two more exact same type of craft fly over head and did the same thing. The last one the flew over was blikin very fast and changin colors and took off I only got a minute of video of that one.
