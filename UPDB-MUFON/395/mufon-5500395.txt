{nd} Odd things in the sky above Rexburg, Idaho

I went out to call in my cats, and I saw something odd in the sky. I went in the house and got my camera (Canon PowerShot A2200) and binoculars. I took 59 photographs. When I first saw it, I felt it was something out of the ordinary. The Sun was just setting, but there was still plenty of light outside. I watched it over half an hour, and it began to give me a strange feeling of unease. There seemed to be more than one light, or part of it1then it would all fade away completely. Then it would return. The last photograph that I took of it is, perhaps, the best: it shows one large object shining light onto clouds above it.

I do not have photographs of the most disturbing part of this. I was watching it with binoculars, when it began to take a different form: it began to look like an oval piece of the Moon, except it wasn1t the Moon: it was oval, then oval with a bright light at the base of it, then it all just faded away to nothing. As of this writing, it has not returned.
