{jpg} Saw odd con trail took photo saw object in photo next to con trail one day later

I was working on my car on 9-25-16 about 4:30 pm. I happened to look up and saw an odd Con trail looking like the doughnuts on a rope. I called my son who saw it as well. I decided to take a picture. I ended up taking several pictures. The next day I was looking at the photos and in one saw an object that at first I thought was an airplane. I worked in aerospace for 25 years on commercial and military aircraft. The object was not like any aircraft I have seen. I studied it for day or so and could not ID it and thought I should report it.
