Observed a object fly past on a clear blue sky early evening

I was outside of a music venue on July 3rd , 2010 prior to the show. It was a beautiful day, not a cloud in the sky. A airplane was travelling overhead and I was watching it as it looked cool being the only object in the sky. I saw out of the corner of my eye a smaller solid object travelling in the opposite direction that I could not identify. I was travelling faster than the airplane. I took out my cell phone, a flip phone at the time and snapped a picture. It kept travelling west and I lost it from view over the horizon.
