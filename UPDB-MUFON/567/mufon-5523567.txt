saw an object in the sky on october 4th over lees summit

i was looking out my laundry window facing east. i was talking on the phone and saw the object. it looked like the stealth bomber because of the shape. It was a large triangle with white and green lights pulsing. the object appeared to be on its side. so i walked to my back door to take a look outside and by then it was gone. i thought to myself, if it was the stealth i could hear it. since i have heard the stealth fly over many times before. there was no noise. i stood there for a moment and a helicopter flew by coming from southeast going northwest. i noticed the helicopter was much higher than the object. i have no idea what it was.
