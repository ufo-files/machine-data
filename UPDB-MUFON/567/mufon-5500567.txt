{cak} It looked like a wide penny and it was blue with sparks

I was outside my house at night near my mom and my cousin near the car, and while I was listening to their conversation and while they were talking I see something in the corner of my eye. When I look up I see a bright blue object shaped like a penny but wide and there was blue sparks coming out of it. After about 1.5 seconds go by after me staring at it, it descends sideways likes its about to crash then while I was staring at it descend it disappears quickly. At that moment I thought it was a UFO. And I kept what I saw to myself. I really didn't feel anything because this is the second time me see something like this in the sky, so I really didn't feel anything. Also I lost sight of the object as it quickly just disappeared.
