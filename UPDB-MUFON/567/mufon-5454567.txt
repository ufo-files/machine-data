{rjl} Very dim red lights flying very fast, made directional changes like a fast-moving bird. No sounds.

Walking dog just past midnight. Was looking up at stars, when noted very subtle dim red lights moving, thought it was a plane. But it moved very quickly (I'm in flight path of TF Green airport and Quonset ANG, so see plenty of planes flying over). It did a chicane or two while still proceeding very quickly from southeast to northwest, and I lost it. I'd describe it as bird-like in it's movement, like a fast hawk chasing something. After realizing it wasn't a plane, my first thought was it was maybe a drone, but it only had the dim red lights (i.e., no green) and there was absolutely no sound. It was either small-ish and relatively low, say 1000-2000 feet, or it was big and up high, I couldn't tell.

In all honesty, I can't imagine why a ufo would have lights on, so it's probably something explainable, but thought I'd submit in case anyone else in RI saw anything tonight.
