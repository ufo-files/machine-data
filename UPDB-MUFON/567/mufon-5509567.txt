{kt}Glowing Blue Disk Over Airport!

I was taking my friends home at about 10:30pm after a dinner party, when I looked south over the San Bernardino Airport. I saw what looked like a bright blue glowing disk. I hovered about 600ft over the northern-most hangars. As I watched, it vanished. Looked like it disintegrated but more like turned on invisibility. It became no longer visible. The whole event took no time at all. At least not enough time for me to get my camera.
