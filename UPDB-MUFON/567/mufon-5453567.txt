{rjl} Flashing bright light with dull read light in between.

While on the back porch facing East (090). Observed a flash of light at 11:00 above me. It was traveling in the 045 direction. It was 10 times brighter that Saturn. It would “Flash” in 2second intervals with a dull red light replacing it. Watched it for 15 seconds until loosing it to obstructions. Speed was about 3 times faster that an airliner at altitude.
