I saw a red circular/oval object (larger than a plane) going south and then abruptly stop. It changed direction and went NW and disappeared. Throughout sighting, it flashed gold light.

I was laying in bed looking out the window (which faces North)at 10:40pm CST on Fourth of July. There were no planes in the sky. All of a sudden, I saw this bright red circular/oval shaped object. It was if it appeared out of nowhere. It was headed south (straight to our house)and then abruptly stopped. It stopped for maybe 20-30 seconds and then changed direction and went NorthWest. It was at the altitude of a "normal" plane. Throughout the sighting, it would turn/flash gold and then become red again. It was not a plane - looked nothing like it. The entire sighting lasted about 2-3 minutes.
