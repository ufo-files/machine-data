A round bright white light then it made an abrupt right turn to reveal a tic-tac shape.

I was in my driveway walking my dog when I noticed a round very bright, white light to the North. I thought was this a helicopter but I didn't see any signs of that being the case. As I watched it coming closer I could see kind of an aura around it. Then it suddenly made an abrupt right turn and I could tell that it was actually tic-tac shaped. I estimate it's altitude to have been about 1600' msl. Then It slowly picked up speed and disappeared to the west. I've been flying for 43 years and have seen a few strange things but that one was kind of different. It had no sound, no position lights, no strobes or landing lights. It was also flying Southeast in a Northwest corridor at commercial aviation altitude, almost like playing chicken. It's good that at that time of night there is not too much traffic going into Indianapolis International. I'm a Mufon Field Investigator so I was and still am very curious.
