{jpg} My girlfriend and I saw 5 solid-orange glowing spheres moving in a straight line. Each sphere was 3 miles apart from the next. ​

On March 29, 2015 at approximately 8:35 pm I was in my car driving home and was about 200 yards from my house when I spotted a solid orange glowing sphere moving in the sky so I stopped and observed it for 10 seconds then I recorded it with my cell phone for about 30 seconds. This object was 100% solid orange in color and not blinking or flashing so I knew it wasn't a plane or helicopter because it didn't have any of the typical aviation-required red, green or white lighting on it. So at that point, my first thought was that it was a UFO. The official sunset for that date occurred at 7:38 PM CT so there was no daylight and it was dark outside with high cloud cover (no stars visible). This object appeared to be at an altitude of about 1,000 to 1,500 feet and was moving from the west to the east at a speed somewhere around 75 to 100 mph and was travelling along Minnesota State Highway 55. After it disappeared over the eastern horizon I drove my car to my house, parked it in the garage and went into the house. I immediately told my girlfriend what I had just seen so we immediately went outside onto the deck. Once we were outside, we immediately saw another solid orange glowing sphere on the western horizon, moving at the same speed, at the same elevation and taking the same identical path (from west to east) as the first sphere that I had seen a few minutes earlier from my car. This identical scenario occurred three more times for a total of five identical solid orange glowing spheres all within about a 15 minute period. All five objects appeared to be the same distance apart which I estimate at about 5 miles. These objects made no noise, no turns or maneuvers and I estimate the distance from our viewpoint (at the closest point looking South) to these objects at about 1 to 2 miles. I recorded three of them (each about 25 seconds in duration) on my cell phone but they only show up as a small white dot on the screen. Our viewing point was just off of Highway 55 (north side) about 4 1/2 miles west of Interstate I-494. During and after sighting the object, we did not experience any unusual feelings, reactions, actions or emotions.
