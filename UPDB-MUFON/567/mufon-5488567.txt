{jpg} Massive boomerang shaped UFO with 15-20 solid lights flying overhead in a north to southeast direction.

It was around 0850 pm on the 1st of October 2015. I walked out into the back yard to walk my dog and I looked up in the sky looking for the moon. I didn't see any moon but noticed a large number of lights moving across the night sky. There were about 15-20 dull white lights in a boomerang shape that were traversing the sky from a N to SE direction. The craft was difficult to see, almost translucent but the lights were unmistakable. The lights did not blink and the craft made no noise. From what I could tell the craft was massive. It would have been between 3-4 full moons from front to back and roughly the same from side to side. The sky was clear and it took about 45-60 seconds for the craft to disappear. It was flying towards the Atlantic ocean. I was awstruck as I saw the craft. I tried to get my wife to come out to see this thing but by the time she got there it was too far away to make out. We have airplanes and helicopters fly overhead quite often and this object could never be confused with either of these. 55 degrees
