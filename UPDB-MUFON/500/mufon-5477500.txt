{cak} watching plane while grilling, noticed shiney object in vapor trail hovering

I was at home, on the back porch, I had stepped outside to start grilling dinner, when I looked up at a passing plane, the plane was high at approximately 35,000 ft. flying to the north. when I noticed in the vapor trail a shiney object. I called for my father to come out to witness the object, but he was not able to see it. I grabbed a set of binoculars I keep next to the back door, and viewed the object. The craft was hovering over the North end of Hill AFB, I checked my clock to varify the time. It was 5:10 pm. As I looked at the object it flashed red, orange, and yellow colors and was itself a very reflective metallic color. The craft began to drift slowly to the East for about a minute, I handed the binoculars to my father and turned to the grill, to pull the meat, as I closed the lid on the grill I looked up, and the craft was gone. My father never did see the object.
