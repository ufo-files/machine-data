Silent transparent sphere with red interior lights and obscured inside

I was walking in the neighbourhood one summer night. The air was still and it was hot, but nothing seemed too out of the ordinary. While walking home northeast on Corkstown Road (towards Carling Avenue/Bayshore), I noticed something no more than eight metres (~26 feet) above the ground at the corner of Crystal Beach Drive. I thought it was maybe a balloon, drone, or bubble at first. I slowed my pace as I drew closer to get a better look, but I noticed it didn't behave or look quite like any of those things. I figured it might have been some trick of the light, maybe a car's rear headlights reflecting off objects in the environment and hitting my eyes in just the right way, I don't know, but no, it clearly wasn't that.

It was almost like a crystal ball, max. 2 feet in diameter There seemed to be two red interior lights in the bottom half of the sphere, they did not seem to glow or cast light outside of the sphere, and the red light was obscured (refracted?) by the transparent inside and not bright. The sphere floated silently, slowly and steady in the air, as if observing the area with deliberate intent.

When I got closer, the sphere ascended slowly over the houses on the right of me, and above the trees out of sight. I then went for a longer walk in a different direction than I originally planned, my heart racing and mind racking as I tried to work out what I saw. I didn't see it again, and haven't since.
