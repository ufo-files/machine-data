Strange view but exciting in a way of something rare and unique

It was around 19:00 (7pm)-afternoon, 29 September 2013. I was at my home in Durban, residing in Merebank, when i told my mum help me wash my hair outside.When i was outside washing my hair i did not notice anything until i washed my hair the second time,and towel drying my hair so i can then condition it. Just when i tilted my head back to wrap my hair i saw a bright light. It was a bright blue-green thick circle and in the circle was a shinny star,it was abit dim but from a distance i saw a star and the circle around it like couple minutes. I then told mum look whats that and then we called my aunt and the family who was at home to see it.The first thing that i thought it maybe cause of the rain over the weekend that its kind of a rainbow circle but only thing this was circled with a star in the centre and was different, so i then thought of it as a sign of something else because we did prayers that day so we took it abit of a religious way but then my brother mentioned what if its aliens like in the movie so i told him don't talk like that.Well,the emotion that i had was abit strange but in an excitment like wow that is beautiful,and thats when i called everyone to see it cause it was unique and i was all happy about it. As the clouds blocked it by seconds it went abit dull but still bright and i felt it should have stayed abit longer cause i enjoyed the view and was amazed what iv seen. my family too was amazed and wondered.. but they have said thats the first they ever seen something like that.
