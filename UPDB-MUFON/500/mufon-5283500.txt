Tall Thin Grey

Historical event.
In Dec 1964, I was only 9 years of age.
I was living with relatives in Sutherland, Sydney, Australia.
at 10 pm at night I went out the back yard to the then Out House
which how things were in outer Sydney in those days.
We lived on a rather large block of land, which had two houses
on it.There were also four large Willow trees mid way up the block.
I had just finished my business, came out and turned to hear
a rustling between the trees. i stopped, paused and noticed something
moving between the trees.
I paused, thinking it was a burglar, then I froze, Id never seen anything
like this before. I panicked, It put its long fingered hand out at me,
I had no idea what it was as a 9 year old.
I could not even yell.
The Image of did not register with me at all. so I spun around
and ran down the driveway, across the street and hid beside another
house.
I was a confused child not knowing what it was I could not
mention it to my foster parents.
Weeks later I told an uncle, and he said it sounded like
something not of this Earth.
I did not see a Craft, or other Aliens and no abduction took place.
The only side effect I can remember in Vomiting later with 
head aches.
Years later I was trying to out it together of why it was there
The only thing is that 12 miles away there was a Nuclear
site called Lucas Heights but it only manufactured medical
grade items.
I am still at a loss, was it an attempted abduction?
not sure, but the Entity was real.
