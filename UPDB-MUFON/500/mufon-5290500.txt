3 lights moving under cloudcover before sunset at unnatural speeds, hovering, disapearing and reappearing

On 2/20/20, at about 5pm( right before sunset) My family and myself were in the car driving to Corinth MS. While on the highway I observed what looked like moving stars in the sky, and I assumed it , was a trick of the clouds, or bc we were in a moving vehicle. Once we reached our destination, which was a video game store,my spouse who had been driving could then look and see what I was telling him about. We were parked but the 'stars' were still moving . We got out of the car and watched it along with my 20 yr old daughter for 15/20 minutes The cloud cover was thick, like a blanket and these 3 lights were under it. They moved very fast at times but were always relatively close to each other. Sometimes 2 would move, then all 3, then all would stop dead still. The brightness of one would fade and then blink out like a light bulb turned off only to pop back on. Only not in the same exact spot. They moved in relation to each other at very sharp angles but always coming back into a general triangle shape. My spouse was stunned as he is very practical and this was absolutely mind blowing, but my daughter was scared. I looked around to see if anyone else was watching it but saw no one except traffic. After a while my spouse saud we should stop watching it bc it was making us all very nervous and it was nealy dark so we finished our shopping . The first two pics are especially odd when zoomed in.
