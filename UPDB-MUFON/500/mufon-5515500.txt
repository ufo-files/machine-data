{cak} UFO Flying from NE to SW over Golden ,CO. at app. 1:45 am on 9-19-12

At Approximately 1:45 am on 9-19-12 my girlfriend was taking something to our trashcan outside, when she observed something flying from the NE to a SW direction Around Indiana and 6th ave. in Golden, CO.She immediately called out to me and I walked outside on to the porch.She pointed in an Easterly direction and said, "Whats That"! I thought she was pointing toward the yard and said,"Its probably a raccoon" to which she countered,"No, up there"!I looked in the direction she was pointing and noticed a rather large object with no lights on it moving through the sky.I really couldnt tell the color but it seemed reflective like the shiny side of a CD or DVD,and seemed to reflect mostly the dark of the night surrounding it,with occasional refractions of city lights.I think it was about 500ft up and about 1/2 mile distance from us and made no noise that we could hear (where we live its very quiet at this time of the morning).I would describe this craft as looking a bit like a stingray with a very small tail.It moved off in a A South Westerly direction by Green Mountain and just kind of blended back in to the sky(I think because its very dark to the SW of us with no more city light).
