{cak} Huge object Between the Moon and Earth

I stepped out in my backyard and looked at the beautiful moon that night when suddenly a very bright light appeared in the exact position my eyes were focused on towards the moon, i would say about 3 to 4 inches "separating 2 of my fingers, index finger on the moon, thumb stretched out to object". 

The object appeared suddenly, extremely bright and solid fireball in color, and moved very slow. I would compare the speed to a helicopter. Measuring again with my index finger and thumb, i would say it appeared and vanished at a slow speed at about 2 inches. It was not below our Atmosphere, seemed to be somewhere between the Moon and Earth, perhaps as far as halfway in between the two. 

Nasa conducting some sort of Space Tests? I first thought that perhaps it was the ISS, but later dismissed that after recalling seeing the ISS flyby years ago and comparing sizes of this object.
