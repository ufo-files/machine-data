I was in my hot tub when I noticed a bright light on the horizon. I thought it was a helicopter going along I-5 which I seen before. It wasn't moving fast enough for a helicopter and I realized it wasn't over the freeway it was over a electrical sub station on a road much closer to me. It wasn't moving very fast I would guess 15-20 mph. It was headed north then all of a sudden it started moving in my direction easterly. I couldn't tell the shape or size of the craft it was a moonless night and the only thing I could see was a bright white light in the front of the craft. When it was coming towards me about 400 ft. from me in an open grass field it shined a light to the ground for about 2 sec. It wasn't very high off the ground I would say about 20 to 30 feet up. It was coming straight at me. All I could see was just the bright light in front, I did not see any strobe lights anywhere on the craft. when it got close to me I could see a lighted rectangle on the bottom.I would guess it to be 20-30 by 10-15 ft. It was all lighted white except for 3 oval lights one Orange one Blue and one Green. There was a black small circle between the orange and the blue lights. I would estimate the diameter of the colored lights was 4- 5 ft. t flew directly over the top of me, The orange light looked like it was rotating. I didn't hear any sound but I had the jets on in the hot tub. The lights I believe were recessed into the craft because I didn't see the lights until it was almost over the top of me and when it flew away I couldn't see anything no lights , outline, nothing. The direction it was going there is a natural gas substation about 1/4 mile. I don't know if it was checking out our resources or just checking out the country side but it was both amazing and scary. I know it has been a while since I saw it , but it has been bothering me since.
