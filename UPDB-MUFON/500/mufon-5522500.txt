I saw a bright light coming from the NW toward my home.

I ran outside to see what it was, forgetting about my new camcorder of course, and watched as it flew straight towards me then over my house, barely clearing the treetops. It was shaped like a triangle, with red, green , and white lights flashing and pulsating all over the underside of it. It sounded like a hum/whir and moved steadily across the sky. It was absolutely breathtaking!
