{nd} Blue lights from a spinning UFO that glided across the sky

From Greensboro I was driving to Raleigh to meet a friend. It was my first time driving this route. At one point I found myself looking way up above me whilst on a narrow road surrounded by trees on both sides of me. There were these blue lights gliding across the sky. I was unable to stop because there was another car behind me. It appeared that the lights came from a hovering craft that spun - causing the lights to look like they were blinking. It was such a spectacular blue. A glowing blue. Because I could not take my eyes off the road just to completely stare at it.. from a distance when I looked up next it had disappeared curving west and into the lefthand side of the trees.
