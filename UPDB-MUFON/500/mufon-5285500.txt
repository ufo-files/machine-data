Bright lights noted moving north-to-south

We were attending a pool party at a friends' home in Sacramento, There were helicopters and water tankers shuttling out of what was formerly McClellan AFB to the fires in the hills and mountains to the east. I glanced up at one point, and noticed a couple of very bright lights close together moving in a southerly direction. At first I thought it was a high-flying aircraft, but they were definitely brighter than any lights I had ever seen on a plane. I also couldn't make out a fuselage, although there did seem to be something connecting the lights. The lights varied in brightness independently, at times both of them were brilliantly lit. The sky was quite clear that day with almost no clouds or smoke. It went over the sunshade we were sitting under and someone commented that it looked to have turned to the east.
