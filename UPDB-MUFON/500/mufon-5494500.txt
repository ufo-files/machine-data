{dr}Hovering glowing object 20ft above BC Van Nike Hercules Missle Base Mac Gregor Range NM

As a 16C Nike Hercules Fire Control crew member located at Mac Gregor Range in the NM desert about 40 miles from El Paso. I was on guard duty about 9pm in the evening trying to brush up on my radar skills, as my assigned duty was long range radar. I was tracking planes coming and going from El Paso International Airport as i have done on many occasions but this night was particularly slow so i decided to leave the BC Van as i opened the door above me hovering about 20-30 ft from the ground emitting no sound was a large glowing disk shaped object directly above me since i was on the top step of the van it's bottom was about 10-20 above me really that close.

I was so scared that i retreated to the van and the latch was a lever action with no lock, for what seemed like 20 mins or so i had held that handle down with all my strength, I'll tell if anybody or anything would of attempted to lift on that handle i think literally i would of had a heart attack. I was that scared since on that particular guard duty assignment you were only issued a stick the size of a billy club really just a cut down broom stick so really had no defense.

So after about 20 mins I looked at the radar to see if i could see anything since something that size would of blocked the screen completely. all i seen were the mountains on the scope so decided to take a look outside and it was gone. I never want to go through that again
