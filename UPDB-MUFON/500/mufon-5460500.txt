Black Triangle craft, silent. hovered then moved/rotated and slowly flew out of sight

Full Description of event/sighting: I was driving home from the store and
noticed an object hovering above some houses. The object was a black
triangle, three white lights on the bottom in each corner of the triangle,
and a flashing red light that appeared to be on the back of the craft.

Now I thought that with the flashing red light, it must be a helicopter.
So I pulled into my driveway, I quickly got out of my vehicle and observed
the object some more. It started to slowly move across the sky and as it
moved it rotated slowly, then it hit me... there was no sound! None!

I estimate the object to be about 500 meters away from me, and about 100
meters in the air. I felt a strange feeling of excitement and disbelief,
and I knew that the best thing I could do was to observe as much as I
could about this craft. It was about 10 meters on each side (an
equilateral triangle shape) 3 lights in each corner, on the bottom of the
craft, and a flashing red light on the back of the craft.

As it moved about 1 kilometer south, it was slowly rotating on it's z-axis as it was
moving horizontally, it also appeared to be somewhat fading or becoming transparent at
times. It then increased speed and moved East towards the Atlantic Ocean and was lost from
my view due to cloudy conditions. The craft was under the cloud level during the entire sighting..
