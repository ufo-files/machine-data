{rjl} 2 UFOs over Portage Island and Lummi Island

My husband was on the back porch, came rushing through the house to the front deck saying there were 2 orange UFOs. We were both looking south, one UFO was over Portage Island, another UFO was behind it over south Lummi Island, they were round, bright orange-red, slowly moving north. I quickly grabbed my sons camera off the table, it's right by the front door, made sure the the flash was off, and quickly took 2 pictures, right after the second picture both UFOs disappeared heading south. The camera was slow to take pictures because it was dark and trying to focus (I tried to focus on the UFO in front), so there is camera shake, but it does show how bright the UFOs were.
