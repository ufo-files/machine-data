{ck} I saw 2 yellowish oranges orbs of light that looked like they were pretty big.

I was at my home and it was 4:00 am. in the morning on 7/6/2012 and i got up with my husband as he was getting around for work. I just happened to look out our bedroom window because the full moon was so bright. And I saw these big yellowish orange orbs among the tree line. I called to my husband to come and see them. After he left for work I continued to watch them and they started moving they would go up and down and then side ways really fast. And they would dim and come back on again. I watched this until they disappeared which was a little after 5:00 am. and there were no airplanes or any other aircraft of any sort in the area of this. And this is not the first time we have seen something like this here. My husband and I both have seen things many times over the years since 1997. We were always afraid of the ridicule if we would have reported it. When we see these things here in the sky it scares me but yet Im fascinated by it.
