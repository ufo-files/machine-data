UFO over House Fire

I was in my apartment around 8 pm on Friday, December the 5th 2014. I heard sirens outside and then seen lights outside my window from some fire engines. After about 20 minutes I decide to take a look. I walk out of my apartment and down the street a bit to take a better look. Now to be clear, at the time I took the photo's I was focused on the fire and not what was above, so I did not see the UFO with my eyes. But I did take two photo's, both of which have the UFO in them, with the UFO in different places. To me, I cannot logically explain what is in the picture, and that's why I'm posting it here.
