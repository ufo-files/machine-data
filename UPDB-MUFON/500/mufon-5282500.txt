Observed 4-5 airplanes converge on sparkling light that changed direction; possible surface to air/AAA used.

Stepped out on my porch and observed a sparkling light moving south west across the night sky. It looked like a slow motion sparkler, the light intensifying when it sparkled. An aircraft identified by the anti-collision lights was moving opposite the direction of the sparkler, slowed, banked and chased the sparkler in the westerly direction. Lost sight of both aircraft over the porch roof. Went into the yard for a chance to continue to observe and the sparkler was heading in the opposite direction to the north east. One aircraft was following the sparkler but was out-paced, within approximately three minutes 4-5 other aircraft (identified by anti-collision lights) converged on the same heading as the sparkler and first aircraft in a normally quiet night sky. When the sparkler crossed the horizon relative my position I observed a solid yellowish white light move in an intercept direction to the sparkler at speed and roughly 35 degrees inclination from the south to the north, east of my position.
Addendum: one of our dogs was outside and began continuously barking maybe 10 minutes prior to my observations.
