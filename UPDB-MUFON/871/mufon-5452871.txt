Translucent white light tilted UFO that looked like a cloud, turned into very bright white star and then disappeared very fast

On May 11th, 2020, approximately 10:00 pm~ish, I was out walking my dog Bailey around Inland Shores Staats Lake in Keizer, OR. Off in the distance, facing south east I saw a tilted to the left UFO that was like a cloud shaped like UFO - I stared at it for like 10 secs & then it changed quickly by tilting right and then becoming a very bright white twinkle star and then very, very, very fast (faster than snapping fingers) it disappeared, in a flash. I was back to my house in the neighborhood by 10:23pm.
