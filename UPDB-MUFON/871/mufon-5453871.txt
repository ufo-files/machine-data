Sighting Report 2020041405:50

Sitting of six craft traveling in a line about 45 seconds to one minute apart, moving from NNW to SW. It was like six ISSs traveling in convoy, all on the same path. 

On my western states map this would be in the general direction of central Nevada.

Observation Location (GPS Coordinates 41* 59’ 55.3” North by 122* 29’ 10.3” West.

On the above morning. I was out on my south facing deck taking down my telescope after a late night session of observing the Moon and a string of planets. 

As I looked North of my location turned to put the scope into it’s case I noticed a bright light at a very high altitude Objects passed over possibly 50 miles north of my location and heigh enough to catch the light, as does the ISS, which is what I thought this was when I saw the first one. Then looking ahead I made out another light. This one was a dim orange. Four more passed in line, like a convoy, 45 seconds to one minute apart.
