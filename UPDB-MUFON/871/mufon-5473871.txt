{dr}Signing while camping at the Oceano Dunes

While laying down in my trailer looking out towards the ocean watching people getting stuck in the sand as they are coming in late to camp, I noticed a reddish yellow ball slowly come up from the water line. As I thought this was just a kid with another drone, I noticed that it kept climbing towards the sky in a slow zig zag motion, then stop, hover, then continue towards higher elevation till I could no longer see it. This first encounter was about 10 min long. So while I was still watching the sky for it to come back down, that's when I noticed 3 more come from the water which at that point I woke up my husband to witness this and to make sure its what I was looking at as well. While we were watching those 3 play/dance for 20 min, another one showed up and go across the sky, dive into the water and then back out again. After they were done playing, they continued to climb higher and higher and then just disappeared.

I kick myself for my phone not being charged to record any of this, but even if I was able to, I was too excited to record because we have always been fascinated and knew they existed just never saw one in person. I'm honored to be a part of the few to see them, and glad they felt comfortable with us to allow my husband and I to see them all.
