I am sending you a copy of a photo I have since the beginnings of 1980. It was taken by a photographer who was closely related to a friend of my mom, in spite the fact that I do not know who he is. The camera was placed at the Gurgel factory front yard, alongside Washington Luis Highway. The old car company was located in the city of Rio Claro, state of Sao Paulo, Brazil. I do not think this installation exists anymore, and I do not live nearby.
I have never shown it to anyone or made it public.
As I watch some TV shows about UFOs, I thought it was the time to show it to experts, so I decided to send you a copy of it. I do believe this photo is for real, and do not want to attract any media attention over me.
Actually, it would be better if you do not ever mention my name to anybody, because I have my concerns about men in black and this kind of thing.
