{cak} Two large orange lights floating in the sky

My girlfriend and I were out driving around my hometown when I was going up a hill and noticed this large orange light way up in the sky just hovering over an apartment building. It was so bright and large.... next thing I know I saw a second orange glowing light slightly smaller than the first, except this one wasn’t stationary. This smaller orange ball circled around the large one that was stationary and then suddenly disappeared into then air. Next appeared a blinking light towards the large orange ball which too seemed to be stationary. It didn’t move once. 

I finally pulled into a parking lot fast a parked for what seemed like a good 2 minutes. Within that two minutes, the large orange orb finally started to move slowly to the right... and I physically watched it just disappear. I was in such awe of what I was watching, that I didn’t even record this amazing event. 

Turns out while I was seeing this, my grandma who lives somewhat up town or up from downtown was also standing out on her deck and witnessed all this the same time I did... as well as my girlfriend, who was in the car with me.
