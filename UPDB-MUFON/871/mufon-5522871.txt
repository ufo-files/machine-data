Star like object hovering above us then slowly leaves in a South East direction.

Husband was outside and came to the door for me to come out, asked me if the star above us was moving? I looked up and it seemed to be just hovering and then it started to move very slowly in a South East direction. As we are watching this object basicly float away from us, and there was no sound, the next thing I hear is a helicopter coming in our direction. I told my husband that what ever it is, its being followed by the helicopter. As I was watching the object and the helicopter I couldnt but notice that the helicopter was keeping its distance from the object but following it at a safe distance and speed as the object.
