{jpg} Stationary , right above me, elelevation so high barely can discern colors rotating.

It was summer, I am pretty sure of that.. No school and warm nights to check out satilites rounding the earth. I was using my dog as a pillow. Looking intently for small moving objects, I noticed a tiny ring directly above me. I started focusing on it and I realized there were separate lights spinning like, how you catch a blade on a ceiling fan. Spining clockwise the lights were : Red, Blue, Yellow and Green.I have good eye sight. My family was military and I had a personal interest in flying machines. I felt excited and relaxed , it was so far away. The lights remained for about five minutes and then disappeared. I remember the incident like it was yesterday.
