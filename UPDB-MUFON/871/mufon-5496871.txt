Triangle with blue lined markings on bottom passed directly above my house.

About to step inside. Dark out. Decided to admire the stars for a second. Was looking directly above when I thought I saw part of a plane far above moving fast. After a second I noticed the triangle outline and knew what it was. Flew in a straight line fairly slowly. Looked identical to the outline of other triangular craft but had lightish blue lines or markings on the bottom. It was very exciting. Immediately I ran inside to grab a phone to tell someone. Passed directly over my house and I was at the door outside so after it was above the house.
