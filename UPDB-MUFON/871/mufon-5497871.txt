{cak} While standing on my deck observed 2 bright orange objects in sky hovering next to each other. They then got smaller as they ascended and disappeared.

Went out on my 2nd floor deck to have a cigarette after watching TV program at about 10 pm and was startled to see 2 huge, bright orange objects in the sky in front of me, roughly in the sky above the New York City area. Because the area is close to Newark airport my initial reaction was I thought 2 planes were on fire. Then I realized the objects were not moving but rather were hovering next to each other. There was no noise. After about 20 seconds of stunned disbelief, I called my wife out to the deck and she also observed the objects. At this time a plane was crossing directly under the objects en route to landing at Newark airport. The objects continued to hover for approximately 30 more seconds as the plane landing at Newark passed below and in front of them, all the while glowing a bright orange/red color. The objects then started to get smaller as they appeared to be rising into the sky. Within 5-10 seconds, as they got smaller and smaller, they just winked out of sight. The total time of observation was about 1 minute.
