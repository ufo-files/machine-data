{jpg} Black orb observed hovering, then erratic movement while flashing reddish orange

Went outside to lay on the chaise lounge and have a cigarette. While observing two birds soaring above me, gliding around in circles etc. While the birds gradually moved eastward and in front of a chem trail, I noticed a small black orb which I thought was conventional aircraft but as it caught my attention it flashed an orange burst, back to black again. A little more movement and then hovered, flashing again. All movement was restricted to a small area of sky, it wasn't moving in a forward path. I remember saying out loud "I see you!" and the obkect responded with another flash. I had to get a picture of it, even though it probably would be a crappy quality photo. I ran into the house, grabbed my digital camera, returned outside, by which time the object was gone. Total time of observation approximately two or three minutes.
