After a very strong thunderstorm, I left the houseat 2 AM. It was extremely windy outdoors, but the sky was very clear. As I wasdriving down our rural road along the ridge of a mountain, I noticed somethingin the sky which, upon initial observation, appeared to be a plane with itsinterior lights on. UPon closer observation, there were actually two rows ofbluish-green lights with a very bright strobe-like light in the center. Thething that struck me the most was that this object was stationary in the sky,although it seemed to be the height of an airplane. I got out of the car,something I NEVER do when driving alone (I usually have all my doors locked whenIm driving) to get a clearer look, and then really realized that this was noordinary thing I was looking at. Got into the car and tried calling my husband,but as I was dialling and watching, the thing made a right angle turn away fromme, shot out a spurt of light, and disappeared.
