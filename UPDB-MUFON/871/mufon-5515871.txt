{dr}Formation of 3 lights flew overhead, staying in lockstep with eachother

I was on the deck of my house with boyfriend and I watched as a formation of 3 white lights went flying overhead, staying in lockstep with each other, basically from south to north, going towards Lake Ontario, which is just about 500 feet north of my house. The lights were fairly small making them seem very high up. Soundless. I was slightly perplexed, but I knew it was out of the ordinary and lately weve met a neighbor who lives right on the lake who have been seeing orange orbs over the lake (which Ive heard of before as another friend living on the lake has reported the same thing). One friend went to look and saw the orbs just a few nights ago, I went to meet him and they were gone, but there is the feeling that they are allowing themselves to be seen more, thats what I thought when i saw the 3 going thru the sky last night.
