Saw colored lights in a strange configuration hovering then fly away

10/9/2021between 0130 and 0215 Greenfield/Rockford Minnesota North side of hwy 55 and North East of the Riverwood covenant church above a field maybe around half mile from the church or closer I saw a craft hovering above this field I would guess about 300-500 feet above ground there were green and blue and red and white lights on it in a strange configuration, it could have been a helicopter or a very very large drone but I didn’t hear any noise from turbines or rotors. I pulled over drove into the parking lot of the church to get a better look and it flew off towards the East
