Glowing plane like object

I stepped outside to let the dog out and heard a helicopter approaching from the west. I looked up and saw it was a black helicopter but above it seemed to be a glowing jet , pretty high but no contrails and it looked transparent but the nose had a slight glow. I figured it was because it was flying towards the sun but as it continued, it disappeared. I could barely hear the jet noise because of the helicopter but did hear a faint jet like emission.
