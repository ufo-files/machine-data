{jd} Moving star like object

I was standing out with my puppy in my backyard and gazing up at the beautiful stars out tonight and I noticed an object that was moving across the sky. It had no sound, it just flew over and away out of my sight. It was white and the same size as a star. I often see objects, but usually they blink. I am always seeing objects flying at night over the farm land around my property that have no noise and seem unusual. This one tonight was the first star like object and no blinking. That's why I decided I better report this one.
