{tjd} tiny lights taking path FROM south to North as I was looking at Venous at 7:30 pm

I was in Parma, Ohio coming out of store on State Rd which runs south to north. I looked up at very bright light which I assume was Venous. Then I noticed tiny light under Venous, one after another. At first I thought it was a plane, but realized another came after it following the same path. I saw about 9 lights or more, they just kept following each other with about the same distance between them. So from my perspective they were traveling at an angle, lower in the sky from left to right. (south to north) flying at an angle going up. All this was under Venous from the left of Venous going up at an angle towards the North.
