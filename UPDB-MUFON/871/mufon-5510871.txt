I just got done with an early morning bike ride. I was in my driveway looking up and observing the stars. Out of the western sky above I saw a solid light moving toward where the sunrise is and to the south. So it was moving southeast. It looked like it was moving among the stars. I watched it move across the sky. And within 2 or 3 seconds of me losing sight of it, I saw what appeared to be a shooting star, but it moved up and out of view. It made a trail going up instead of down like meteors do. It was moving very swiftly. It didn't rake long for it to cross the sky. Any questions please email me.
