Large silent triangle shape 3lights on the underside

Walking down bridlegate lane dusk time from elvaston castle Derby. I had 4 children with me aged from 6 months (in a pushchair) to 10 years. Eldest child looked east? and asked "what is that?" I looked in the direction and saw (this hard to describe in words) a large triangular object that seemed to blend into the surrounding area you wouldn't of noticed it if it wasn't pointed out which is amazing as it was very big. It was a metallic grey/dark colour with lights on the underside of it.it was perfectly still with no sound at all. It was just above the tree line which would of placed it in the shardlow road area alvaston Derby. The atmosphere was I can only describe as eerielie quiet with no one but us on the lane. I don't know why but all I thought was get the kids away as fast as I could.so I down played it and said it's nothing and walked away as fast as we could. I did not look back just kept looking forward. I recently asked the eldest child if they could remember this incident and they could not in fact all the children who are now adults have no memory of it.to be honest I wish I couldn't either as I would be happy Never to see anything like that again. I told my husband as soon as I got home and asked if he knew of any plane that could stay still and make no noise he said no. This has bothered me for many years and have told no one of this but since hearing of your organization I feel I can share my experience with someone who will not think I am mad.
