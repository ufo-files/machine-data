Animal Mutilation

There was no UFO involved in this incident, but Ive never seen an animal mutilated like this, so I thought Id report it. It looks to be a squirrel and appears as if something grabbed the head and pulled it hard enough to pull the entire inside of the animal out. The carcass is literally inside out. Some of the legs look long because part of the bones are still hanging from when the inside of the animal was ripped through the neck area. There was what might be the squirrels nest laying right next to it under our tree. So this could be a matter of a hawk attacking the squirrel in its nest, tearing the head of with such power that it pulled the entire insides of the squirrel through its neck, and the struggle was such that it knocked the nest from the tree.
