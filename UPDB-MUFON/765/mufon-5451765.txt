Appeared in broad daylight..got a very close look at it..remember it like it was yesterday.

Hovered just over the electric poles in broad daylight on a bright sunny summer afternoon. Within 40 to 50 yds of me. Very bright and shiny aluminum. Windshield clearly visible.. Hovered for 15 to 30 seconds...then eased off to my right slightly gaining altitude to clear trees then disappeared. Not a disc shape. It was rectangular with sloped front end where windscreen was located. Had a very pointed nose. It never made a sound...just appeared then disappeared . Got a good look at it. Something I will never forget.
