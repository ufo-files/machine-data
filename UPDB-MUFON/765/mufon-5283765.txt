Black rectangle with colored lights on bottom

My husband saw it first while he was outside in the driveway. He yelled to me to come out quickly. Flying overhead was a huge all black rectangle, shaped exactly like a shirt box. This was at approximately 3 pm. It was also flying extremely low, maybe a couple hundred feet off the ground. It was approximately 250' L x 100' W x 25' H and moved at an exceptionally slow rate of speed, that we couldn't fathom how something so large could stay up in the air. It had no signs of propulsion, no jets, no propellers, nothing. I grew up around planes and I know it should not be able to fly. Yet, it was. It had no protrusions from its surface. The only thing it did have is, on just the underside, it had different colored blinking lights. The lights were red, yellow, blue, white and maybe other colors, but don't recall. They weren't in a pattern, but randomly placed. They did not blink in any type of unison, but random blinking. It made a very loud constant sound, sort of what a blimp sounded like in the 1980's. I don't know what they sound like now. It was flying from NNW to SSE, which is not a flight path of any planes that normally fly over our property. It finally went behind the tree line and couldn't be seen anymore. Then we saw another one, off in the distance flying from SE to NW. It was far away, but you could still tell it was the same thing. Sorry we don't remember exact day and have no pictures, but thought we should pass it along anyways.
