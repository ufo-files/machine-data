{rjl} white reflective, planetary-like. Disappeared then reappeared further North.

I was laying on the ground and I was looking up, I noticed an object a little more South than directly above me. It was planetary like in that it reflected sunlight and was high above the atmosphere like Venus would look, but wasn't Venus given the place and time of day. I was watching it for several minutes, and possibly related was how clouds below it would evaporate in the sky. Then it disappeared. I continued to lay on the ground and the object reappeared further North, this time directly above me. It disappeared after a few minutes and didn't reappear. My phone was dead afterwards, I think it could be related.
