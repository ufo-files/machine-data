{dr}An object appeared while I was recording the moon and ejected other objects

I was at Sutter's Landing park in Sacramento getting video footage of the moon (and to look for UFOS) when I noticed an object in the sky that stayed stationary for about 20 minutes. At first I thought nothing of it because there are many planes and helicopters that fly over the area. When the object reappeared for a second time I noticed It had a different light pattern than the other helicopters and planes in the area so I decided to record it. For the first few minutes the object stayed in one place so I figured it may have been a drone or a quad copter but then it proceeded to eject a total of 3-4 smaller objects from the main unit all of varying brightness. I was very excited at the time as is very apparent from the colorful language in the video footage. I lost sight of the object as it slowly flew away in the opposite direction. 

In the video the object fades out a couple times due to recording through power lines. At the beginning of the video there was a police helicopter in the bottom right which appeared to be flying in the same direction as the object. I was only able to record one of the ejected objects because the rest were significantly dimmer and impossible to see with the camera. 

Sorry in advanced for the profanity in the video I was really excited at the time.
