Red and white lights attached to craft

Red and white lights attached in a ring. Ring had the diameter of about 40-60 ft. From my vantage point, looking west down 111, the ring was just past the power lines between mill Rd and 111A. (1 to 1.5 miles) Ring had 4-6 lights only, too big for standard aviation signaling lights. I was driving cross 111 and lost sight of it as I passed. I had a thought to pursue and investigate but thought better of it. 

At first I believed it was the same diameter as a cell phone circular tower. However, the only known tower in this area (near life storage), was not near the object, nor does it have these types of lights. No noise was observed due to being in the car.
