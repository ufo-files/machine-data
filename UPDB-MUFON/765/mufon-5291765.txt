Green Image viewed in Moon, Jupiter, Saturn Night Sky

Walking my dog at approximately 950 pm 09/24/20. Sky was crystal clear with perfect view of the Moon, Jupiter and Saturn Line up. Was wishing I had my telescope up and running. I decided to take a photo with my Iphone. I took four photos, One at 946 pm and another at 947 and 2 at 949 and 951 pm. I did not see anything appearing in my lens that was out of the ordinary with the first two. I went to take the third and fourth photo and i kept seeing a green boomerang type object moving through my view. I stopped and looked out at the sky but saw nothing. Went to take the photo again and there it was. I looked out again and saw nothing with my naked eye. Went to take a photo and it was there in the lens finder moving around. It seemed near to the moon and then away from it. I took a photo to see if it would appear on the photo as it was not visible to naked eye. It appeared in the photo. I then did a video and it appears in the video moving around. There were no streetlights in this area as it was near the lake and on the lake road.
I reviewed the photos when I got home and discovered that the photo at 946 shows the green image above the moon and Jupiter. The second photo at 947 pm shows it near the tree line and the second photo at 949 pm shows it in the tree line or tops. Third photo at 949 shows the image near the moon and fourth image at 951 shows the image in between Jupiter and the Moon
