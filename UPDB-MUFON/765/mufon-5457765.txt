{tjd} Most beautiful thing I ever saw in the sky.

Myself, my Dad and my Mom were riding around when we came to a curve and around the curve looked like a patrol car lights because they were so low. Once we got around the curve we noticed that it wasn't a patrol car and that it was about tree top level over a cattle pasture. At that time my Dad decided to exit the vehicle to try and get a better look at it, at that time myself and my mother started crying telling him not to get out. As it turned toward us very slow and started moving at a very very almost stationary speed, my Dad got back into the vehicle and we started driving up the road a little piece and stopped again. As the craft approached us, I looked out the back window as it was going over and it was the most beautiful thing I had ever seen in the sky, it had rolls of lights from the top to the bottom, larger to smaller, and as it came over the vehicle my Dad started cursing telling my Mom to unlock the doors where he could get out but she told him that she didn't lock the doors. After the craft went on over us the doors unlocked and my dad at that time got out of the vehicle and started watching it but the craft then sped away in a weird speed unlike any craft that we knew of at the time. At that time we drove off and turned around and tried to catch up to the craft to see if we could get another look at it but it was too fast. It was like it was trying to get away from us. I'll never forget it to this day because it was so beautiful and I can still remember it like it was yesterday.
