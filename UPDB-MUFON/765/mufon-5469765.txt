Strange, bright spherical/orb-like - recorded a few times in 2017 near a Hydro Corridor of Toronto.

Came out of house, getting ready for work, having coffee. Noticed bright orb-like sphere that looked out of place in skies, blending in with stars, in Hydro corridor near my house. It was too bright to be a star or planet. It looked almost like a drone or satellite, but almost lifeless and just sitting there in a high altitude.
