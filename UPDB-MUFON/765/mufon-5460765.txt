{rjl} Object appeared to be like a back to back pyramid with the bottom pyramid tip removed.

I was traveling west and noticed in front and above me an object that appered to be like two back to back pyramids hovering several hundred feet off the ground. The bottom pyramid portion looked like the tip was missing. I saw no hole on the bottom and did not see any kind of seems or windows or such. It was grey/silver in color. The object moved south a few hundred feet and hovered. Then it mived laterally north back to the original position and hovered. It did this one more time and then hovered for maybe 30 seconds or so and then shot straight up. It was out of sight in less than a couple seconds. The incident took place over about a 2 or 3 minute span of time. I was not on any medication except for my cholesterol meds taken the night before at bedtime.
