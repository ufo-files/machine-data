Smooth mirrored surface sphere approximately 10ft diameter hovered approx 7ft above lake surface. Observed for approx 30 sec, looked away for approx 5 sec to get a friend's attention, looked back and couldn't locate the orb again. I was very surprised to see it. I was not scared or nervous. I reaaly wanted my friends to see it, but was gone when I looked back to ooint it out. Object had a perfectly smooth mirror finish that reflected the environment. It was late in the day when sun low enough to cast a long reflection on the lake. I noticed the orb because the Sun's long reflection curved when it reflected off the orb. Then I was able to see the full size of the orb. I was the only person to see it in my group of people since it was gone when I looked back. The object remained still during The entire sighting. It was viewed from an elevated deck about 10-15ft above the object and 75-100ft away from it. The location of the sighting was viewed looking East at a narrow part of Phillips Lake, in Hancock County, near Dedham, Maine.
