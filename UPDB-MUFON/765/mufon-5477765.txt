My lady friend and I were driving North from Silver Lake, 9 PM about 20 miles East of Buffalo NY on our way to Lillydale NY to attend a workshop by Gregg Braden when we saw a glowing red object rise upcfrom the horizon from the NW. It rose up to overhead then hovered there. When a second one rose up from the same place and did the same. Then the two of them kind of danced around up there. Once turning sidewise to a thin rectangular shape. This lasted about two minutes before they faded away. I telepathically communicated love to them. Their size was 25 cent piece held at arms length. I am a former USAF navigator and thes was no aircaft and no flare. We were both very moved by the event.
