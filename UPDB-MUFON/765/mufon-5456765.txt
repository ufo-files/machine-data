{rjl} Black orb 2 millimeters in diameter, amber aura approximately 1 half inch in diameter, hovering 7 feet from my eyes

A single pitch black orb 2 millimeters in diameter, emitting an amber colored aura sphere approximately 1 half inch in diameter. It hovered perfectly still for approximately 2 minutes, 10 feet above the ground, 7 feet from my eyes. It was a very cloudy day, and windy, as the clouds were moving quickly. The grey and white clouds behind it gave me a clear sense of the aura’s amber color, as well as an accurate sense of depth perception. Upon first noticing the object, I assumed it was an insect, but there were no legs or wings, and it was too perfectly still for too long a time to be an insect so small on such a windy day. After starting perplexedly for a couple minutes, I glanced away for a fraction of a second and it took the opportunity to disappear.
