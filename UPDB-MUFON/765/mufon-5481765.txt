{dr}Several white beams moving very quickly caught on video in the afternoon

As reported to ASD: "I had placed a motion activated stealth camera outside in an effort to obtain photographs of strange animals which have been appearing on my property in the past several months and which remain unidentified. When I looked at the SD card retrieved from the camera on August 2, I saw several white beams of light moving very quickly across the screen. This was obtained outside in mid-afternoon next to a sign I have near my driveway. It should be noted that the camera reacts to motion and only if large animals set it off, so anything of light weight should not cause the camera to film anything. I submitted the video for analysis by MUFON in order to determine what these may be. I should also mention that I have seen a number of unexplained lights and UFOs in the sky over our property in the past."
