The sky initialy looked clear with stars clearly shining. Only when the object moved did it become visible.

I Stood at my back door of the house to have a smoke before i retired. I was star gazing on a cold clear night. for several seconds i was looking up and there was nothing to see but stars.All of a sudden it became clear that the sky above me wasnt stars and blackness but a craft.It looked as though it had cloaked into the background and was only visible when it moved slowly above me and the stars in the rest of the sky were fixed. There was no way for me to determin the hight it was at.I moved out onto the decking to follow its flight from south to north over my house and it excelorated from a slow speed to lightening fast in a second and vanished.There was no engin sound. Only the sound of a large mass moving through the air.
