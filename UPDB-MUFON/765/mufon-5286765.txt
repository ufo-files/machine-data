1993-94 ish playground missing time incident

In the first few months of this year it was raining and storming a lot, so we did not get a lot of recess time. The school was Montclair elementary, in Orange Park FL. The bell rings for recess, and we are released to the playground. something did not feel right, kind of off putting, like everyone came out in exact unison, and then, as soon as it had begun, the bell rang again, and the teachers said ok, recess is over, lets go back inside. I remember recess should have been one hour, and the entire hour was missing. every watch and clock was correct, in sync, functioning, one hour later. just about then out of the corner of my eye, i saw a triangular black craft, hovering above the treeline. it had three white spherical rear mounted dome things i assume was the forward propulsion, and a long nose. my first assumption, NASA has a new shuttle coming into use maybe....then i realize its not going anywhere really just hovering, and this is when it begins bobbing about erratically. it reappeared about a mile father, and to the right, then closer, slowed down, turned, and hauled more ass than a millennium falcon on steroids. i will never forget watching this thing fold space time around itself and literally just be somewhere else in the universe in a fraction of a second. my theory knowing element 115 exists and can is that this was a failed military attempt to recreate a ufo, being test piloted, but it moved so fast this thing caused missing time on a scale that affected an entire elementary school on recess.
