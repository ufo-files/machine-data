Noticed 2 bright balls of light in clear night sky changing in colour, size, shape, direction, speed, brightness and intensity

Was heading upstairs to bed on Feb 19 at 11:15 pm when out of the corner of my eye I noticed a bright fireball in the clear dark night sky and realized there were 2 ranging is colour, size,shape direction, brightness intensity. Nothing like I've ever experienced before it was NOT a plane, star, planet, meteor, Chinese lanterns, or drone and I was fumbling with my phone that was dying so I could take pics and multiple vids and screenshots of what i was seeing that did not look nearly as incredible as what i was seeing but gave a good idea. Went outside a few times but it was extremly cold and kinda didn't want to be seen... Even though it was pretty far away. Went back inside and called for my 21 year old to come see. i couldn't stop looking at them and went on for almost 3 hours hovering for long periods then changing shape colour speed and direction and intensity coming in and out of view and coming back to the same spot out my window as if putting on a show until clouds finally rolled in and could not see them anymore
