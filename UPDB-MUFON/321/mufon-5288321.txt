Object seen in sky yesterday

Yesterday morning between 830-845am I spotted an object in the sky moving from South to North. It was a sunny day with occasional cloud cover.
There was no humming,jet engines, or helicopter noise observed.

The object itself rotated in the sky as if you were kicking a soccer ball. Object rotated thru the sky in a forward motion as it made it's way northward.

Object was not above the cloud cover but below it. I observed it moving northward for a good 2 minutes as it rotated thru the sky.
Object appeared to be triangular shape. As it spun northward the sun would reflect off of it. When it was directly over my head I could clearly make out the triangular shape as it flew thru the sky..
