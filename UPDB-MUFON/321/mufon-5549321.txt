Black, hollow rectangle with lights

Three of us were driving home at dusk after dinner, and a passenger in the car exclaimed, "what is that in the sky?" I slowed the car down and we each saw a large, black rectangle with 3 lights on the top over Santa Fe. It didn't appear to be a vehicle, but looked more like a "square hole" because the interior of the square looked more like a "portal" rather than a vehicle. Strange. This is the first time I've ever experienced anything like this. We each went home and drew what we saw and our drawings were quite alike. In a matter of seconds, the object simply disappeared from sight and we were left wondering about our sanity.
