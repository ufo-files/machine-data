Rectangular object with many blocky lights and dark space between them moving as one object.

I was in the backyard at approximately 4:45 AM. This was either 11/23 or 11/24. Unsure. I looked up above my house and saw a very large rectangular object about 10-20 degrees West of my house moving from NE to SE in a steady path. It looked like it was made up of 40 or 50 smaller, blocky lights, somewhat irregularly shaped. I extended my hand and held my thumb and forefinger up in a wide C and it filled that space when it was probably 20 degrees south of my roof.

The morning was dark, clear and quiet. The object made no sound. It traveled steadily in that path until I couldn't see it anymore as it dissapeared from sight, no due to moving below the horizon by just got faint. It didn't appear like the Starlink satellite pictures I've seen. It appeared to be much closer but somewhat very far away in the black sky.
