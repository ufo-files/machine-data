round ball of light

The event took place while I was driving, my 17 year old son was with me he saw it too. It was a ball of light that fell at a 45 degree angle and then abruptly turned left at a 90 degree angle and just kinda darted all around before disipearring. We looked at each other and said, "Did you see that"? At first when it was desending I thought it was a meteor, then it turned, I knew then it was not a meteor! It happened really fast. It looked like the ball of light that decended over the wailing wall in Jerusalem and actually acted in the same manner.
