Reddish-orange light moving rapidly.

My fiance and I were sitting on the back deck. It was very quiet as it was late and no traffic. A light caugh my eye as it entered my view of the sky. It was reddish orange and moving rapidly. Not like a meteorite but faster than a plane. It was about 45 degrees from the horizon, moving from east to west, with a slightly downward trajectory. There was no sound at all. The light was steady, not blinking or fading. It appeared to be about the altitue of a plane. I watched it pass in front of the moon which was about 3/4 full and bright. It looked like a dark spot as it crossed. Trajectory was slightly erratic. like an ember from a campfire. It disappeared behind the trees overhead, but then I could see it again when it cleared, then disappeared for good behind other trees.

It appeared to be flying, not falling. The light was steady, not flashing or flaring, and there was no sound at all. Our reaction was; THAT was weird! What could that have been? It was like nothing else I've ever seen.
