Slow moving object at night, no sound, no navigation or strobe lights, climbed at incredible speed into space.speed

I was sitting on my back porch at night when I saw amber lights on the bottom of something that was moving very slow from east to due west at about 500 to 1000 feet above ground level. There was no sound of an engine of any kind and the color and shape was unknown. The amber lights were in the shape of a cross, the light was not focused into a beam but only a steady glow. It was moving too slow to be an airplane, so I thought maybe it was a hot air balloon, but there was no beacon, navigation lights or strobe lights which are required. the track of the object was on a diagonal to me on a due east to west direction. I was watching its movement for about ten minutes at this point and I new I would lose sight of it because of the porch roof so I stood up and stepped away from the porch. Just then, it turned about 45 degrees to the right which was coming directly towards my position. Still moving slowly and after about 30 seconds the object made another course correction to the left for another 30 seconds and then it turned to the right to its original track. After another minute or so, it got very interesting The lights went out and I saw what appeared I was looking at the exhaust end of a jet engine, but there was no flame. I saw a black circle with an outer ring of bright blue white light as it climbed at about a 50 degree incline at an incredible speed. The object got smaller and smaller until it was just a small dot and left the atmosphere in less than three seconds.
