{tjd} It passed directly over me, I all of a sudden fell asleep, woke to it disapating on its path.

This event occurred while I was lying on a bench on our deck. It was cool and clear that night. I was listening to the crickets and star gazing as I always have. I remember feeling like someone was watching me it was a kind of eery feeling. I kept looking at the stars pondering where we all come from. I do this almost every night and have for as long as I can remember. I closed my eyes for a minute as there was a gentle breeze. All of a sudden the breeze died and at the exact same time all of the crickets and frogs things fell silent I looked at my phone and it said 9:40 pm. I opened my eyes and saw the ship coming over the tree tops from my neighbors yard. The exterior to the ship was wavy and seemed opaquesh to translucent, and black at the same time like it was vaporish as well. It was completely silent and as it was coming directly in line, literally with my body. I continued to lay there and felt like this was meant to happen. I was not afraid, in fact my eyelids got very heavy. I sort of fell asleep when it was directly overhead and I was staring at the center light. There were 4 lights one at each of the corners and one dead center. When I came to, the ship was beginning to move directly above me along its same path. It ascended slightly and sped up and literally seemed to dissapated. This was the first of two experience. I had another one in Palm Desert, CA before this occurred.
