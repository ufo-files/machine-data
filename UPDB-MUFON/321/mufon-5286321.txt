Object was first thought to be a satellite that we were watching. No blinking lights. Solid blue white light. Made a sudden move straight up!

Light was a very bright non blinking blue- white light..moving form west side of Lee Lake and we all spotted it. It was NOT a plane. No sounds at all. No blinking lights at all. We all thought it was lower than sattelite we have seen in the past...then, as it was moving west to east...it suddenly changed direction and stayed going straight up until the the light faded very quickly!. We have never seen anything like it. It seemed to move very independent of any wind or or other influence. This was not a fire light Chinese lantern...
