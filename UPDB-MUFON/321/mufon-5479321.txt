{jpg} Taken into space. Put back 24 hours later

I was young and out riding my bike in the city park about one half mile from home in Beech Grove Indiana. I stopped to get off and walk my bike up a steep hill. But my arms and legs were stuck . I could not move them at all. I was scared. I looked around, nothing . I looked up and peed my pants. A 25 to 30 foot disc was 50 to 100 feet above me. No lights or sound. A round hole opened in the middle and a bright white beem came out on me. The next thing I remember was seeing the earth get smaller and 3 what you now call grays. 2 on one side and one on the other side. I heard them , but not with my ears. But inside my head. They said that they were my friend and that they will need my help someday. They would let me know when and what later. The next thing I knew, it was the next night and I was asleep in the grass at the top of the hill with my bike next to me. I have NEVER told anyone about this. I am now 58 and disabled.
