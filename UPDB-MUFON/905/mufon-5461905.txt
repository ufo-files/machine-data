{cak} Cigar-Shaped UFOs over Indiana, PA

I was out on a walk with my children, ages 1 and 7. As soon as we began our walk, my oldest and I noticed something in the sky. A cigar-shaped, bright white/chrome colored flying object in the brightly lit, blue, clear sky. There were a couple clouds, but none close. I listened to see if I heard noise since this is not the first time I have seen a UFO. We walked up a very big hill to continue our walk, and as we reached the top, we noticed the craft again, or another that looked just like the first. My daughter and I watched it for about 10 minutes before it was out of sight. This happened the day after i noticed a pulsating light in the same area, coming from inside the clouds, lasting several seconds approximately 2 pm. It lit up that section of the clouds even with the sun already shining on them.
