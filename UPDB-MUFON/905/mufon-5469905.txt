{jpg} bright white extremely high and fast moving cylinder shape that disappeared when I wasn't looking.

I was driving north along 1300 W and approaching 4100 S in Salt Lake County when I saw a white cylinder or cigar-shaped object extremely high off the ground. When I was stopped at the stoplight facing northbound at the aforementioned intersection the object was directly behind the telephone wires that crossed the street. The object caught my eye because of how white and quickly it appeared to be moving. After the light turned green and as I proceeded through the intersection I looked away from the object and when I looked back it was no longer there. There were no clouds in the sky and it was an exceptionally clear morning. I could not spot the object anywhere. I do not know if it flew away, or disappeared or something else.
