Unknown green disc that changed shape.

Headed to work January 5 2022 and was amazed by the Moon and how it appeared to be fake. It was around 6:15 pm that evening and I decided to snap a couple pics of the moon because it was so unusual. Every pic I took was blurry so I paid no mind to them. On about the 20th of January I decided to look back at those pics I saved and was surprised to see something else was captured in the photos. At first it was a green looking almost tower Chrystal shape with light surrounding it and I zoomed in and was amazed to see another object in the sky from these same moon photos. Looking through all photos I enhanced the pics and the green object was a different shape and took on the appearance of a disc shape with very noticeable lights below shinning downward. I love my pics and just thought it might be worthy of sharing. Thanks. I will load photos the first will be original and some will be enhanced.
