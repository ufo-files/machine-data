Glowing, pulsating, stationary orange orb

Myself and a group of fellow campers were sleeping in a fire lookout on top of a mountain at 7,300 feet elevation with Mt. Shasta in view. I woke up at 3 am, looked out the window and saw a glowing, pulsating, orange light on what I thought was the next hill over. I was alarmed at first, thinking it was a fire. I continued to watch it and realized it was not a fire and that it was actually hovering in front of the hill across the valley I watched it for a few minutes. I think it may have been quite large. It then suddenly disappeared without moving at all.
