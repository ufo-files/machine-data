A light went straight up then moved direction twice

I was smoking a cigarette out side my back door when I saw a light go straight up above the trees then it changed colour to three lights then stopped then moved to the left then went in a half round movement then went left again and I lost sight. This is not the first time I have seen something like this and I have reported this before. I lost sight over houses. And there was a humming noise soon after.
