In 1953( give or take a year) I came home on a late Sept. day. IT WAS ABOUT 55 DEGREES. i LOOK UP from my side walk =, ready to enter my parents house. And there where 2 silver saucers about 800 feet above our house, in a break of clouds. I look up what seem only 15 to 20 seconds, yet my mother looking out the windows says it was a long time. I told my mother it was only so many seconds, buy she said it was a long time. My neck was stiff looking up, so maybe it was a long time, I do not know. That was my one sighing... until 1973. I was a science and Math teacher in Honedale Catholic in late Oct. 1973.
