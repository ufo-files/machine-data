On the morning of July 11, 2015 my wife had mentioned that she had an awful nightmare, she felt that someone was in the bedroom with her and our grandson but she couldn't get away. She was screaming and struggling but to no avail. My grandson said that he heard her, woke up, and touched her. She became quite. The feeling was real and frightening. The next day I remembered that I had my game camera on the back deck pointed at a 45 degree angle. It was programmed to take 10 second video every 10 minutes and had been there on the 11th and 12th. While previewing the video and with the nightmare event forgotten, the videotape showed some clips that seem to be connected to the nightmare incidence. The attachment has a summary of clips on the morning of the 11th and a couple clips of the 12th which I can't explain. 
The clips on the 11th will show a beam of pulsating light coming from the sky entering into their bedroom through the glass doors during the night of the nighmare.
