{jpg} Pulsating lights seen regularly but this time it shot across the sky to another areatl

We are no strangers to seeing starlite lights in the sky that pulsates from red to blue to green. Usually we have one that is in the exact sane spot year round. We jokingly call them our aliens and go about our day. We have seen no more than 2 at once. At 2:05 am this morning, all that changed. We went to walk the dog and noticed "our aliens." But this time they were everywhere. Therr was one stationed in the NE, E, S, W, and NW and one overhead. We were looking at one particularly bright. Within second it shot across the sky to the left, going from NE to N. This was no plane. There is no place that could shoot that far across the sky to appear like the size of a star and move that fast. It ended up moving back towards the NE. We looked towards the NW and one appeared to be moving in a circular movement. Now I could wrote that off as eye strain but no way was this shooting across the sky eye strain. The area this was in had no clouds. We are on a dark mountain and see more than others in the city.
