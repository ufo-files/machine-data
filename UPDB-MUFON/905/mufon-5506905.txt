2 orbs floating in the air

I was with 2 friends and we had to be at school in the morning at 4:00am and decided not to sleep that night. we went to the service station at approx. 2am and got some food and continued to park the car in the carpark behind the service station. from there we never took notice to anything in the sky until my friend pointed out these two glowing red orbs in the eastern sky. we pondered and asked ourselves what are they? after 10 mins of just slowly moving up and down left to right and diagonally ONE of the orbs disappeared as if it were a dying light bulb and 1 minute later the other did the same.
