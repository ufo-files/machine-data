Bright star like zig zag once then flew a straight line as high as the other stars in the sky.

I went outside around 2100 hrs thursday, 15 july 2010, looking up at the sky, looking at the stars and counting the airplanes as I always do. It was clear and cool (76f). I suddenly noticed a bright star like object moving accross the sky in a easternly direction in a zig zag then continued a straight line. I hollered for both my boys 9 and 10 yrs of age to wittness the sight. I looked at the time on my cell phone to have an idea of what time i saw it. It was 2138hrs when the boys came out and viewed it with me. We watched together for about 1 minute as it then just dissapeared out of our sight due to the tall pines in our yard. I knew it couldn't be a plane with take off / landing gear lights cause planes don't fly backwards and zig zag high in the sky. It didn't appear to be a star / commet, cause they always move extremly fast in a straight line.
