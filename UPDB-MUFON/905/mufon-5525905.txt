silver in color about the size of a volkswagon

My wife and myself were returning from an ambulance run from KRMC hospital, coming out of Jackson Ky I was driving and looked out the drivers side window and saw a circular shaped silver object hovering in the air. The object had no wings, rotors, contrail or anything associated with an airplane. I could see nothing else around the object. The weather was warn about 88 degrees, sunny not a cloud in the sky. My wife didn't see the object due to being on the other side of the ambulance. We were about 1 mile out of Jackson near the wolverine area on highway 15 north.I am a Fire Officer EMT with the local fire dept and my wife is a firefighter paramedic. I have seen various aircraft over the years, I was also in the C.A.P and have been around aircraft for years, I could't identify this object.Myself and my wife are the only people who know what I saw. I wold like to remain anonymous as I feel I will be ridiculed for it.
