I saw a yellowish to orange light that appeared star like traveling low in the sky moving east to west while driving with my wife to a friends house.it was a totally clear sky with stars. I stopped at a stop sign then we saw a second light following ,thinking they were helicopters i turned the engine on the truck off to listen to the sound there was silence as they continued west. my wife wanted to go so i continued about a block to my friend house still watching the lights as i drove i went in to call my friends out to see the lights.
one was still there but further away and much higher in the sky {note i was traveling east all the while}
