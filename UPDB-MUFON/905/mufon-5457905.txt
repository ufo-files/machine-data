{rjl} Triangle Shaped Craft

I was on my patio using my new Gen 3, pvs-7 night vision goggles viewing satellites and stars, when at approx. 2130 hrs, I saw lights in the shape of a triangle, very high up. I am not a pilot, so I would guess 70,000 feet up, about twice the height of commercial aircraft, heading in a S/W direction. The lights were steady, not flashing, and were in a triangle shape. I would guess that there was approx. 12 lights total in the shape of a triangle. I did not hear any jet noise or any other noise at the time. I did not see any flashing lights on the craft. The total time viewed was approx 20 seconds, as my wife was with me and I handed her the goggles, but she could not find it. The next day, I found out that the Blue Angels were flying over Los Angeles on a sightseeing tour, prior to making their way to San Diego for an air show. The news reported the Blue Angels were flying through Los Angeles at approx. 1800 - 1900 hrs and my sighting was at 2130 hrs. I'm not sure if the Blue Angels refueled in Los Angeles and took off at the time I saw the lights, that is a possibility.If the Blue Angels were not in flight at that time, then I'm not sure what craft I saw, but was not a typical everyday aircraft. Another question I have is, do the Blue Angels fly in a tight formation, and in a triangle shape when traveling to another destinations. The lights on the craft were not a perfect triangle.
