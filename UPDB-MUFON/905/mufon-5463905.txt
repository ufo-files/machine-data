{cak} White orb descends over area Southwest of Bonham, TX

I was outside on the front porch facing West smoking a cigarette. For the second time this week, I observed a white orb appear in the same aread of the sky and, both descended quickly before seemingly disappearing in mid air. Both were large, bright, and solid white. Both left no trails behind or streaked across the sky as a meteor would. Both times the object was a spherical, glowing white orb that left no trail and faded away before nearing the treetops. Observed in the exact same area of the sky Southwest of Bonham, TX.

When I first noticed it, my immediate reaction was meteor. This was quickly replaced with the realization that it only resembled a meteor because it had a straight-line descent, but other than that, did not resemble a meteor because it descended much too slow.

The first time the object was just an interesting anomaly to me. Tonight, it seemed very eerie, as someone would have a low chance of seeing this twice in the same week in the same area of the sky when it was visible for a little over a second or two only. I don't believe in coincidences.
