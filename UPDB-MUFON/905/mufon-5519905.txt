5 daytime objects glowing moving slowly across the sky.

It was approx. 5pm and I was was laying on the couch talking on the phone looking through the back living room window through some trees out back and noticed lights through the trees in the sky. They were as bright as a planet is at night, I would say as bright as Venus even. They moved slowly in various directions. I took a video camera out to the front of the home and video taped a few minutes of the event, using a tripod as well for a brief time for a stable shot. The objects moved slowly east over the ocean until they were out of sight approx. 45 minutes later. There was other witnesses besides me and we all were asking what they were.
