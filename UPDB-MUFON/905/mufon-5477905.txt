Moving light with lit up clouds of its own around it, then casted a gas in the sky.

The light turned on and it was moving southeast form the west. It was a yellow-ish white light and it was steadily moving on a straight line. There was a cloud around it, it was round and the light was in the middle. The cloud was lit up and it was clearly something of the object itself because there were no clouds in the sky. Then it burst out something like cloud in front of it, it was shaped like a balloon. Then the light turned off, but the cloud around it and the burst cloud was still there. It was dark to see if there was any object in the middle. Then the clouds gradually disappeared. 

It is said that the object was SpaceX's Falcon 9 or a piece of it returning to the atmosphere, yet the things I've seen make that very unlikely.
