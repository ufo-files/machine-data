Looked like the moon moving fast.

I was sitting on my porch after work. I looked ahead and saw "the moon" I don't ever remember it being in that spot.It had a distinct face. ok whatever.. I watched it move enough for me to have to keep tilting my head back. At one point, I closed my eyes because I know the moon doesn't move that fast. I opened them and looked again and saw it shake. Then move up and a little to the right. I freaked out and came in the house.
