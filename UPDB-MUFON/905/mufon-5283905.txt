Cigar-shaped object

Seemed to be composed of several lights (4-5). Moved fairly slowly, deliberately from the south to the north before appearing got "dissolve." No clouds were apparent when it disappeared, and could see stars in the area last seen. Small aircraft were flying at the same time, but not nearby and not nearly as high. Seemed to be at a height equivalent with commercial airlines, but would be the size of four of them together. Partner described it as reminding him of a school bus. Tool video with iPhone which shows it as fuzzier than when I saw it with my naked eye. No erratic movement. I have seen starlink previously in the area several months back - was not even close in appearance to this, and starlink moved from west-east.
