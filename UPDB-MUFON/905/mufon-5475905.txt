{cak} classic ufo disc shape

approx. 4 mi distant. red firelike glow. start of forest fire. classic  ufo shape; was first on ground then rose up to treetop level and followed and paced myself and two partners in autofor approx. 4 or 5 miles to west end of red lake; then ascended to a high altitude and continued to pace us for approx. 30 miles to the village of red lake, mn. when I got to my home the disc was at a high altitude but was stationary. the two other persons that were with me on that occasion are currently alive and can verify this account. my feelings at that particular time was that was a genuine first hand sighting of a ufo.
