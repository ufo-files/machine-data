White object in the shape of a NASA Apollo capsule.

I was photographing an old barn at the south end of Malhuer National Wildlife Refuge. I did not see the UFO live. When my 35mm slide was developed, I noticed the object in the sky. It is to the south of the barn approximately 1/4 to 1/2 mile.

A few years ago, I scanned the slide and zoomed in on the UFO. I discovered a curved row of black dots extending out and below white UFO. A second group of black dots is above the UFO. This group is somewhat disorganized, not a smooth row.
