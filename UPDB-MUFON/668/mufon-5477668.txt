{dr}Strange lights in the sky. Moved very slow and didn't make any noise. Changed directions abruptly. Very close to ground.

I was taking out the trash at about seven at night, when I noticed some blinking lights in the sky. I didn't think much of it at first, but was curious when I noticed how slow it was moving. It was also silent. I followed it as it hovered over my house heading down the street. I couldn't really see it since it was dark out, but the lights were very clear. there were two or three blinking lights next to each other and they moved in unisen. I never got a photo because I didn't have my phone on me and I didn't want to run inside to grab it because I thought I might loose the object. It kept floating away slowly for quit some time before turning north. It headed towards the city, turning directions again to almost be heading north east. At this point it was very far away, but the lights were still clear as day. I hadn't realized I was in my neighbor's driveway because I'd been following it for so long. They opened the door and saw me, so I apologized and lost site of the object. The entire event was approximately five minutes long. I thought it might have been a drone, but again, it made no noise. Also it was much to big. There is no way a drone could have flown as far as it did, and the lights still be visible.
