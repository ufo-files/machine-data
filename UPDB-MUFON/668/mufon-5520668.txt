one single point of light flying at a nice pace then broke into five points then in to a V shape before clustering.

I had taken my then girlfriend out to the movies to see a Christmas vacation by national lampoon, we left the movie and made it back to her place, as I stood on waiting for her to find her keys I saw an object on my right that was a dull orange in color and was a single point of light, then separated into 5 points of light that flew in a v formation then clustered close together and flew away at a very rapid speed untill I couldnt see it anymore, all of this took place in less than 10 minutes. As it was flying away she turned and saw it and asked what it was and I told her I didn't know, thats my full account of what I saw now can somebody tell me what I saw please?....
