I saw a white shining craft moving east over Wichita, KS at 10:45 am central time.

I was walking my dog at 10:45 am with a sunny cloudless sky and saw a rectangular white, shining object moving slowly from west to east almost directly overhead. I watched as it noiselessly moved across the sky on a direct line to the east.  There were several jets flying at high altitudes in the sky with contrails, this had no contrail and the sunlight reflected off of it like glass. I estimate that it was flying at around 10,000 ft. or so and as it was gliding above me, I realized that it was not an aircraft that I had ever seen before. Read that someone saw a similar craft over Ballwin, MO today. Didn't have a camera or phone with me. Always considered the possiblity, but now know it is a reality.
