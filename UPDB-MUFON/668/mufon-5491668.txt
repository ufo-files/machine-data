{dr}A bright light came through my bedroom window. I was paralyzed. I crawled on the floor. My door opened and there they stood.

I was at my father's house. I visited there on weekends. I had a room to myself at the end of a small modular farm house. I remember waking to a bright light through my bedroom window. I remember how bright the light was and how it was the brightest light I had ever seen in my life. I wanted to move to the window but I literally could not move. I was able to hit the floor and fight my way crawling on the floor. Then I heard my door open and there they stood looking at me. There was three small human like creatures. The next thing I remember was it was morning and I was laying in bed. The strange thing that I remember was how I woke up in bed. I was laying on my back perfectly straight. The covers tucked neatly around me. Almost like the picture perfect sleeping position. This was not normal. 

I have kept this a secret for almost 30 years, only telling my wife. I grew up on a farm and I never knew anything about aliens or UFO's. After watching abduction stories and hearing the stories of others I realize what possibly could have happened that night. To be honest, it's bothered me for 30 years and I would like to know what really happened that night.
