{rjl} 2 and a half hour drive from river to home my brother and cousin sleeping BRIGHT light above us next we wake up 2 hrs later on dead end road

my brother,cousin,and me were coming back from jacks fork river it was a 2 and a half to 3 hour drive we were on hwy. 19 heading to Herman mo. 20 to 30 miles from Cuba i am driving come over the hill and right above us was the brightest light i have ever seen i stopped and it was above us i stopped then the light lite up the whole interior of the car i was so scared i have never felt terror like that i woke up by brother he started to freak out he told me to get the hell out of there but the light was so bright i could not see to go foreword so i backed up and it fallowed us i stopped and tried to wake my cousin he would not wake up i was so scared i punched him in the face twice trying to wake him but did not move my brother and i were flipping out crying and more scared then we have ever been in our life(the light was so bright i could see the pores on my brother and cousins face and dust floating around)i tried to move foreword but it was right in front of me.the next thing you know me and my brother wake up on a dead end road in the middle of the woods,with no memory of what had happens to us we got to Herman when the sun was coming up 5 to 6 hours after we left the river,(my cousin never woke up during this whole thing,and thinks me and my bro are crazy)for a couple of weeks i was having nightmares and me and my brother was talking about them when everything about the light came rushing back with all the feelings of terror we felt came rushing back we cried and relive everything all over again.we have no memory of the missing time.it has effected both of our lives in a negative way.
