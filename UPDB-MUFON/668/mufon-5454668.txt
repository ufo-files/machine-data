{cak} Observed bright light thought was aircraft East bound to turn south for KPAE never turned or a drone till after 20-30 min didn't land for battery swap

Observed bright whitish light around 8:50pm ish looking West out my sliding glass door, thought it was a heavy aircraft East bound to turn south for WEBVE INT to JUGBA for the ILS 16R Approach KPAE (Paine Field) never turned South then thought it was a drone doing some surveillance or something. It showed some characteristics of a drone, slight bobbing, slow moving, slight elevation changes ascending, descending, drifting North , Northwest but after 20-30 min waiting for it to land for a battery swap, it didn't land, just kept slightly drifting North and South. That's when I grabbed my monocular to try and get a better visual. Seemed very bright star like kept zooming in was pinkish rose colored kept getting rounder, reminded me of some kind of bio luminescence organism in the ocean till max zoomed in could see movement and like a grey ball shape and 2 lines moving and the interior moving almost bug looking like spider eyes.  Then tried taking photos through the monocular and video, don't have an attachment for phone so it was a little jittery trying to keep object in frame. As a helicopter pilot can't explain what i saw, first for me.
