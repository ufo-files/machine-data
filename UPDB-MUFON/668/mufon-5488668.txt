{jpg} Bright Star round shape position in clear night sky. Moved NW then stopped. Moved NW then disappeared.

Its September and I was entertaining friends visiting. I stepped out to get fresh air when I looked up to the stars. As I turned around from facing east to northwest, I seen a pair of bright stars in that I hadn't seen before. As I stared, the star on the left started to move to the right then it stopped. I ran inside to call them outside quickly to view that star. They came out, I pointed at the two still stars and thats when the one star moved back and then headed North. No sound, no additional lights. Just a star shape. It took only 7 to 8 seconds for it to move from Northwest to a Northernly direction as it dissapeared. My two witnesses were at awe that they had just seen something that did not match a plane or any drone I've seen to date.
