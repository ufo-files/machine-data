Racing across the sky, fastest thing I ever seen. Red-orange object.

Outside on deck smoking cigar. Looking east I see a red-orange object flying in a straight line level with ground at a low attitute at about 75-80 degrees above the horizon. Moving very fast, to the west, I saw it maybe for 1-2 seconds before it disappeared behind neighbors house. The object looked like a fire log with no flames, just glowing red hot. Thought it was a meteor at first, but just didn't look like one after I thought about it.
