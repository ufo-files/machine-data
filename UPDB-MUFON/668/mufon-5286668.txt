My 8 year old daughter and myself were chased near Oak Ridge Tn by a black triangle with small lights at each corner, and a round porthole like light

Halloween night in 1996 or 1997, I was a Paramedical doing insurance physicals for Equifax and other companies, and was looking for a patients address in Emory Gap in Harriman Tn and had my 8 year old daughter with me at the time. We turned onto Scenic Drive, and something above the trees caught our eyes so we stopped and looked up, there was a jet sized black triangle with 3 point lights and a round one in the middle hovering overhead without a sound! We of course freaked out and took off in fear of the thing, and it also took off staying directly overhead us at the treeline, Chasing us! It felt like forever but must've been about 5-10 minutes till we came to the first house on the road, turned into their driveway, and looked up, and it was gone
