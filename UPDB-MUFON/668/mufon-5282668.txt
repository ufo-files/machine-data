Round lite rose from ground squggled around and flew twds house with a tail -- then another smaller object appeared closer to house both took off

Ring doorbell camera captured event. Of round light translucent circle rising from field next door about 20 feet away. Three objects on the film. One round object went away up the hill and was less bright. The main object was very bright (Orangish) rose and squiggled around and came toward the camera with unusual movement and had a tail. Took off out of frame. Then a smaller more solid object appeared briefly which was very different in shape and brightness. It was more saucer shaped but on its side. The whole event lasted only about 5 seconds. I have the film and will try to attach it here if I can. I'm not good with that. 4 Days after a green rectangle of light appeared in the same spot. I also have the film of that event. The bright green rectangle hovered and then flew away as I arrived in my Ford Escort. I seemed to scare it away.
