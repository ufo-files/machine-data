Bullet or cylinder shape, very far away and must have been going very fast.

In back garden filming the object on an old iPhone. It was a cloudless evening and the object was travelling right to left as I faced North/North west. It left no trail and moved at a constant speed. It did not change shape or disperse in anyway. it looked like it could have been on the edge of the atmosphere and travelling with the curve of the earth. It was still visible so must have been inside our atmosphere.

For how far away it was/seemed it would have been massive and travelling at a very high speed, even though it seemed slow moving to me. travelled much faster than any planes i have seen up there and way faster. 

First feelings was that this was abnormal and a unique event, pressure change in my ears as I was watching, nervous feeling and feeling of wonder while filming. 

Object disappeared over the horizon.
