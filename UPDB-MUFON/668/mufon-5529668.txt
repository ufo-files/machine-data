Blue/red orb spotted curving, moving in an erratic pattern and then disappearing while heading inland.

Randomly looked out my window at 2:30 AM and spotted an orb like object that was brighter than any star in the sky, it was distinctive enough to catch my attention right away as it was moving left to right and then started heading inland towards the shore from the sea. The object appeared to be cycling between red and blue colors at a very high rate of speed. I watched the object with a pair of binoculars and it literally looked like some kind of energy field over the hull of some kind of large craft and then it slowly dissolved as it headed inland. The sky was very clear that night.
