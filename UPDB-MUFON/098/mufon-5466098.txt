Driving home from work approximately 1 am noticed a bright light hovering over Edwards AFB then being escorted by a Cessna from the south part of Edwards AFB to either a Boeing, Lockheed, or Northrop facility in east Palmdale ca where it then landed vertically on one of there facilities. While the triangle was being escorted you could clearly hear the Cessna plane but complete silence as the black triangle passed above me at approximately 500 ft.
