It looked like a star, but at first it was hovering before zooming off to the SE

I don't sleep well and often get up in the middle of the night and walk around outside. When I looked East, I noticed a "star" I hadn't seen before; then, I noted that it was hovering/moving ever so slowly over downtown Dade City. The object was just a very bright light. It stopped suddenly, just before getting to me, then it exploded into what looked like the star of bethlehem (ok, yes, I'm Baptist and it's the only thing I have in mind that relates to what it looked like)...once it exploded, it zoomed off to the SE at a rate of speed that was so fast that my eyes could barely keep up with it...then, it was gone.
