{cak} Sawtwo flying objects directly over Grand Junction, as seen from St. Mary's Hospital.

My mother and I were sitting in a hospital room on the 12th floor, SE corner. Mom saw what appeared to be 2 objects moving in the cloud layer over Grand Junction, south from around the CO National Monument, as I turned around to look, I saw them clearly, maybe a few miles out. At first they looked like black triangles, delta shape. As they got closer I could see a mirage type aura around them, with various reddish colors glowing or flashing. They were on a Northeast track, when it looked as if the 2 objects absorbed each other into one object. As they came apart, one craft went straight up vertically about 500 feet or less, then back down. The outline was clear, as it moved up, there was no tilt. As they continued to the NE, they appeared to have changed shape, I could see a definite round outline. About 15 seconds after this, they were gone. I would estimate their altitude at 10-15 thousand feet, based on their relationship to Grand Mesa, which is around 11,000 ft
