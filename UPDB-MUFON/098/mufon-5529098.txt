white cigar shaped object hovered over the hospital for 3 min (to my knowledge)

I was taking a delivery to Mana dr near the hospital. I was traveling down Bob Yunkin rd towards the south end of the hospital when I saw a white cylindrical object hovering about 60-100 feet over the hospital. At first I assumed that it was a helicopter or a plane but I decided to pull over to make sure. (when I pulled over I was just about on Appleby rd.) I studied the object for about 2 min. The object was white and cylindrical, it had discoloration like an off grey on both ends and a redish looking stipe in the middle, there were no wings,rutters, windows, tail sections, outboard engines, or any visible exhaust, nor did it make any sound. Having made the decision that I was not going to find out what this thing was I decided to go ahead and make my delivery. By the time I had reached Mana dr I had spent about 45 seconds- 1 min traveling when I stopped at my destination I looked where it had previously been and it was gone. I had decided that I would never see it again when (on my way into the building) I caught a glimse of it to the east atleast 30 miles away and a mile higher than before. PS. the object was about 30ft long and about 8-10ft wide.
