{rjl} Heard rain? Unable to shut window thought plant was in way thwn saw 3 aliens in my room

I was 9 yrs old I heard what I thought was heavy rain. I got up to close window. Something plant like was in the track so I left it and got back in bed. Moments later I opened my eyes I saw 3 white humanoids looking into my closet. Scared I pulled blanket over my head. I could hear them talking?! I lifted a small space to look and one of them bent diwn and swished his long white first finger side to side as if to say don't look. I covered my eye again and waited scared to death afraid to even look out again until the sun rose threw the window.
My window was closed then and there was no sign it had ever rained.
