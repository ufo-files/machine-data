{nd} Went to my truck and heard a buzzing noise I looked up and everything got really quiet and I saw three shinny spheres go into a cloud and never come out.

I have only told one person about this and it took years. Something was not right about that day, it' was just coming out of winter and it was still a little cold but the sun was shining and there were big clouds in the sky. I was leaving my house and walking to my truck and I remember thinking what a beautiful day then I heard a noise like a helicopter but not, it's the only thing I can relate it to. Then it got really quiet like everything just stopped, I opened my truck door and went to get in and that's when I saw three silver spheres off in the distance going into a cloud. I waited to see them come out the other side but they didn't. I am the type of person when I see something I want to be able to explain what I saw or when something brakes I want to know how to fix it but this I have no answer for. I feel like I am missing something and it's right in front of me but I can't figure out.
