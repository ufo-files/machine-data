{nd} 3 Bright lights in a row

Driving south to Mesquite on highway 318 and noticed as the sun was setting 3 bright lights on the horizon. We thought they were jets, but the stayed stationary and never moved. I watched them till the sun finally set. What got our attention the most was that the 3 lights were in a perfect row, staying stationary.
