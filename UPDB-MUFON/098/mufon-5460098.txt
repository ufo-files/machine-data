{rjl} Oval-shaped, silent craft observed while camping at Coldbrook Camprground

While camping at Coldbrook Campground in Wrightwood, CA (75R5+HR Falling Springs, California) myself and two others witnessed a silent, black, oval-shaped craft descend into the valley from over the mountain top from the direction of Falling springs / South Mt.Hawkins. The sighting occurred between the hour of 9 pm and 10 pm (uncertain of the specific time) and lasted for roughly 3-5 minutes by my estimation before we lost visual sight of the object. 

We were located just outside of the campground on soldier creek, facing due E - NE. The moon was nearly fully fully (about 75% illumination) and provided clear nighttime visibility without the need for flashlights. We could see a dark outline/shape of the object as it moved through the sky and passed above/behind the tree line.

Craft Description: Black and Non-reflective. Oval shaped, potentially 15-20 ft in diameter, with a single glowing orange light. The object did not have any additional lighting. The orange light appeared to be able to move around the oval independently, as when my friend Leah flashed her flashlight at the object the orange light immediately turned towards our direction at which point craft hovered in place, flickered its light at us, and then proceed to continue along its path . We were unable to hear any sounds or buzzing that would indicate that it was possibly drone. Additionally, all insect noise appeared to go silent from when we first saw it descend over the mountain top and over San Gabriel canyon road.

Additional information: I don't believe we had any missing time, although we were very turned around had difficulty finding the our direction and original path back to the campsite after the sighting. two items on our persons were also missing 1 x Flashlight (Austin), and 1 x Multi-tool (mine).
