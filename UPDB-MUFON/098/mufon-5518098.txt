encounter of entity

I was feeling scared and out of nowhere, pulled the covers over my head, Pulled them back down there is an alien to my left and suddenly I feel frozen or I cant move, like Im out of breath and then Im seeing a 4 dimensional square in my vision. Im watching the top right corner of the 4 dimensional squares and I can see an alien inside our house. Anyway.. the next thing I know Im screaming really loud and my parent bust in, no one is there. 

The next day my room is a disaster, all the clothes thrown and scattered very awkwardly across the floor in a way where you cant see the floor. My parents thought I did it and I was spanked for it, they don't know why it was like that. 

This was either a burglar or an encounter but the memory is very real, I am 100% positive that I wasnt dreaming when this happened to me. I urinated in bed for years after this memory
