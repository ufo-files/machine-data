a string of star like dots moving in formation.

i was in my backyard with my dog, while she was doing her thing i was staring at orion and other stars, i turned around with my head still staring at the sky and then out of the northwest portion of the sky about ten different lights moved across the sky toward the southeast. i had no idea what to think but i watch alot of ufo shows and this seemed very typical of this type of ufo, there were four dots a little gap then three more with three in a triangle formation behind the gap, none of them seemed to be stable, they would 'bounce' slightly. i lost sight after they flew over a large tree and the street light was too bright.
