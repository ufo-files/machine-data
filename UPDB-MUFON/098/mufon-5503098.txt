On my way home from a night shift job, after 8 a.m., I was looking up in the sky while driving. It didn't bother me, to look up, as a distraction. I was really looking up in the sky, day and night. The early 1990s to mid 90s, sorry I can’t remember the year. I drove south, looked up in the sky, I saw silver, grey oval craft. It came down to station above a white cloud hovered.
