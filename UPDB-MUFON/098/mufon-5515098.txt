8 orange lights in triangle formations

I was leaving a store, in Mississauga ontario canada, when I witnessed 3 bright orange lights in a triangle type formation, the were coming from the north, north of pearson int airport, on a southern heading, the planes were taking off and landing west to east. The light formation seemed to rotate, then 2 of the light asended into the clouds, while the 3rd remained in sight passing to the sw. I then noticed another single light from the north with same heading, followed by another 3 in a triangle pattern they followed the same path as the previous craft, then one more solo appeared, i watched them all till they were out of site, these objects were not planes there were bright orange balls that made no noise at all. I recorded 2 one minute long videos of the two formations.
