{dr}floating balls of light that disappeared

We were sitting on the back porch when we noticed three balls of light at first coming over thetree tops. We noticed as they moved across the sky that the numbers increased to a total of 8. The lights were pulsating and a couple appear to have small changes in direction as the moved across the sky. They appeared to increase in elevation slightly then evetually just disappeared. The incedent lasting about 8 minutes from the first sighting to the last one disappearing. Six lights were in the initial group and two more followed not long after the first few disappeared.
