sighting back around 1962. some of us playing ball and one says whats that in the sky we all looked up it was north of us a big light in the sky.   I looked at it for a period of about 1 hour and then my mother called me in home for bed. I said it to her 10 years ago and she said it was in the paper locally"strange lights seen over the northside of cork city" alittle later we had a powercut all over and while I was going to school at about 8in the morning a light shone down on me 3times from the sky. and I have had many experiences since then I am now nearly 60
