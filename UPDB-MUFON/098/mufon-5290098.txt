UFO in backyard early this morning

Got up in the night with my dogs. Made myself a sandwich. While in the kitchen, through the window, I noticed a red pulsing light in the sky. I opened the window, watching the object for approximately three to five minutes. I decided to go out on my back deck to take a look. We have a cell phone tower near the house. This object, however, was to the northeast of the tower. I could see the tower flashing white. This was not the tower. I went back in to get my cell phone. I'm attaching one of the videos. This video has not been edited in any fashion. The video shows our stationary solar porch lights. Also on the video, are two strings of solar lights that hang from trees in our back yard. These will show as various colors or blue. They are at the bottom of the screen. The red light in the center is what I saw that has no explanation. It moves, darts, changes shapes and size. I filmed a total of five videos. There was no sound at all. Of note, it was hard to determine proximity due to darkness and no real point of reference in the sky. This was in the sky at least two hours
