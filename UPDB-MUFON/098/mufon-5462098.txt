At about 10:00 p.m. I walked outside to have a cigarette on my back porch. I'm always watching the skies and was particularly looking for the star Called Alfard .. As I'm looking up I notice what seems to be a round orb of light glowing yellow and pink that just kind of seems to be slowly kind of going up-and-down and then back around but what was very strange to me was that when it stopped it almost seemed like it knew I was watching it because there was almost as feeling of knowing that we both have seen each other and in that second it completely disappeared almost as if going backwards into the night sky completely disappearing. We live near military bases and I personally have lived on Dugway proving grounds.
