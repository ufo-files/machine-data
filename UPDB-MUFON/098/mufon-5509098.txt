for the past month and a half, maybe two months I and my family have been watching strange lights that will appear out of nowhere. One is larger than the rest.(there are smaller lights that travel with it). Very bright like a star. It seems like its cloaked and when it appears thats when it moves. Each time Ive seen this it has been at different altitudes. for two weeks they were traveling south to southwest now they are traveling north to northwest. They do not pulsate or blink just a steady very bright glow.-It is the end of July and I am still watching these lights but at much higher altitude. I watch these travel higher up to a point where I cannot see them any longer. There is no sound.
