[dr}Line with two glowning ends.

I was outside near the top of some stairs and having a cigarette at 8 pm local time. I looked up (roughly 30 degrees above the horizon) towards the northeast. when I saw a line with some lights on the end. I wear glasses, so I took them off and rubbed my eyes and it was still there. 

The best way to describe it was a long light (like a cold cathode tube or florescent light with two glowing LED lights on the end. I noticed no blinking, no movement when I tilted my head or moved a down a few stairs. I grabbed my cellphone and was preparing to record what I was seeing. When I looked up, the object was gone. 

It could have been an airplane, but there was no movement, none of the usual indicator lights, nor any sound.
