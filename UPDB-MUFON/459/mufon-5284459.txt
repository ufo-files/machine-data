Very quick sighting in the night sky.

I was in a jacuzzi in the backyard that is in the ground. I was stargazing and while looking up I noticed an object come into my line of sight. It came from the south and right in front of me it turned fairly abruptly and headed west. It basically was heading due west but angling upward in a slight angle and then it just disappeared. It only lasted about 3 or 4 seconds. I had no time to think. The shape was like a boomerang and translucent but with a definitive shape. If you have ever watched the movie Predator and when they finally see that alien in the trees, that is the only way I can describe the object and how it looked in the night sky. I just want to know if there was any other person that might of seen something at the same time I did.
