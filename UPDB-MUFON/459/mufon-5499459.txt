I walked outside to use the phone and immediately saw lights flashing in the sky to the northeast. At first dismissed it as a plane but realized it wasn't moving and that the lights were far too many colors to be an aircraft's. I walked toward it out in the field and saw it was a dark dome type structure that appeared to be spinning counter clockwise with a band or ring of lights around it spinning in a clockwise direction. The lights were flashing sequentially, like Christmas lights. There seemed to be one large strobe type light pulsing near the bottom of the craft and multicolored smaller lights around it like the rings around Saturn. It was hovering and as I moved closer it started moving up and down as if it was bouncing and then went back to hovering. It repeated these movements several times and then went back to hovering. The horses in the field were acting agitated and running around in circles. This went on for an hour. Suddenly, a helicopter approached from the southeast directly headed for the craft. The craft moved straight up into the sky and the lights got extremely bright and began flashing rapidly and non sequentially and then the craft just disappeared like it was never there. The helicopter circled around the area a couple times. I got really nervous so I went back inside. I snapped several shots on my cell phone camera but it was too dark for them to show up.
