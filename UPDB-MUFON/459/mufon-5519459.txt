Daytime sighting of disc like UAV sighted in Normal Heights, San Diego.

On April 12, 2012 in the afternoon I was sitting outdoors on my patio. I heard a buzz or hum overhead. Witnessed a dark round disc like UAV object heading east away from my location, at an altitude of approximately 1500 ft at 45 mph. A cargo or military transport jet was also flying parallel at approximately 5000-10000 ft., slightly south and unusually low and out of normal flight paths. A police helicopter at a brisk speed was simultaneously flying at 800 ft heading west, in the opposite direction of the objects flight path, past my location. I observed the UAV for several miles until it was too distant to see.
