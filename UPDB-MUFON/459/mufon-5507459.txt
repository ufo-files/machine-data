{rjl} fading ufo over houston

after arriving home from work around 10:30 pm, i was walking across the parking lot when i noticed a dark rectangle shaped object above the building and between 2 clouds. It seemed to be about 500ft above me,mostly black color body with 2 visable dim/dark grey lights or domes on the bottom edge. it was moving silently in a SE direction, gave off no light, was fading away between the clouds but not in the clouds. i did not see the entire object, only the back corner as it faded. this event lasted about 5 seconds. i tried to rationalize what i saw by comparing it to planes,helicoptors,ballons,blimps,shadows,lights,large kites but nothing was the right match.
