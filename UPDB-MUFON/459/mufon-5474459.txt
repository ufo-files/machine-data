{dr}First a flashing light with occasional flashes that were really bright, second one slightly erratic movement with flashes

I was watching the meteor shower with my father. After about 45 minutes, I noticed a flash. At first, I thought it was a drone. Then I realized the height of the object, appearing to be the altitude of a satellite. I pointed it out to my dad and he was also perplexed. It kept flashing, moving slowly across the night sky. At some points it flashed brightly, like a strobe light. I got my mom and she also had no clue of what it was. We observed the object for about 10 minutes as its flashes got dimmer and dimmer until it was not view able anymore. My mother went inside and my dad and I talked about the sighting. About 15 minutes later, we saw another flash. We thought it was another plane but it was moving very fast, too fast to be one. It moved in a zigzag pattern until it was blocked by trees. It had appeared to be chasing a plane and it was about a mile high. Its flashes were nearly exactly like the flashes of the UFO we saw earlier. I have seen a UFO before in the area and now I have seen three.
