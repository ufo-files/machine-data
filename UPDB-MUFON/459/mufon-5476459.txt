{cak} triangular object moving from place to place

Rising early at 4:30AM in order to catch a 6:00 flight
at Monterey California, I observed a triangular object
(only observed by its 3 lights) moving to and fro in
the dark night. It appeared to be quite high up, but we had no clear reference to know. The "object" moved up and down and side to side in a
controlled manner.

It was not flitting around at high speed, so we "assumed"
that it was a military "drone". The object was visible for
an hour when it disappeared as the dawn arrived as we arrived
at the airport.
