A rectangle outlined

One day a couple weeks ago I was checking out a big jet high up in in my southern sky with my 10x50 binoculars, and just above the jet I saw a rectangle. Just a rectangle. But it looked like it was a lot higher up. Near the edge of space. and not near the jet. The jet continued on east while the strange rectangle moved slowly west until it went behind clouds. approx 6 or 7 minutes and it was gone. been too cold to watch for any more. Cant explain and buggs me. something hiding behind a giant mirror or reflector thats why we dont see anything in orbit, Thanks, chuck hart
