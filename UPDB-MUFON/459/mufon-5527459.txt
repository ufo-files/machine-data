Star-like object, moving randomly, seems to flicker - mostly white, a little red.

I was sitting in my back porch when I noticed what appeared to be a large star where none should be. I stared at it, as it appeared to be in an air traffic lane (2 planes were passing it at the time). It stayed relatively in one place, moving a little, randomly. It seemed to flicker, with red. After about 20 minutes, first one, and then another, similar light appeared, moving from roughly south to north, disappearing within a minute or two. After another maybe 5 minutes the first light moved a bit south and has been very slowly either getting smaller or moving away. It can still be seen.
