{cak} Blue triangle over Charleston WV

My British husband and I were driving through Charleston WV and on our way to KC,KS to pick up a puppy the following day. We were driving on I64 East at night looking off into the highway horizon. It was a clear night and out of nowhere a blue 3 light triangle appeared suddenly out of nowhere in one spot. I didn't see any nearby aircraft. At first I assumed it was an airplane and thought nothing of it...........until it moved. The way it moved was unlike anything I have ever seen even in advanced maneuvers. It suddenly moved down and over in a U shape at almost lightening speed then simply vanished into thin air. It didn't gradually fade away as if it were flying off into the distance like an airplane or helicopter.....nor can an airplane or helicopter move that particular way at that inhuman speed. Im not making a call on what it is or whether its alien or not. I just know what I saw and although that night I didn't think much of it because my focus was on my drive and my trip....when I got back to work and started investigating what I saw I discovered that triangles are one of the most commonly seen UFOs. Then I saw all the photos.....the videos, the stories. Whoa. So So my husband and I felt the need to report it. Were both 45 and Ive never believed one way or another in what they may be or may not be. Im simply reporting what we both saw that night.
