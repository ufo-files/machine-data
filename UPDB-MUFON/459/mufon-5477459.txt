{cak} Originally large silver orb; when closer, looked more like a type of contraption. Disappeared to a burst and reappeared

In High Point, on S. Main Street getting pizza at the Dominos. Family was sitting in car and noticed a bright light in sky; thought that it was the sun's rays.
Family was immediately startled, confused, but ultimately excited. Upon closer observation, it was found that there was a silver orb slowly crossing the sky. Family immediately began recording event on phone (have 3 min video of encounter); the UFO traveled into what looked like a hole in the sky, dispersing and then reappearing. 
A smaller white orb soon appeared as if responding to the larger UFO. Shortly thereafter, both orbs disappeared before our eyes.
