Bright white appeared round

I was getting mail and I looked at the sky as I usually do hoping to see what I saw. The sky was perfectly blue and cloudless. You could not miss it. It was bright against the blue background. I immediately knew I was looking at something unusual. I continued to watch expecting it to move, but it didn't. I collected my mail from the box and looked again. It hadn't moved. I walked slowly back to my house, about 40 yards and it was still in the same spot. I took out my phone and began to record a video (about 45 seconds). I had things to do so I went inside. When next I looked, about 30 minuets later, it had gone.
