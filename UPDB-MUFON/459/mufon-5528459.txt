4 Plasma Orbs

I was outside at my job and saw a strange glow in the sky. I was just off of route 378 in Bethlehem, Pa. I thought it was a Xmas decoration hovering over someones home to the west of where I was standing. I realized it was not that when it came floating towards me followed by two others. It was orange and redish and in a circular form though it was not a solid circle. They seemed low and close I would say at arms length I would have used a half dollar to cover them from visibility. I then looked to the west and saw another coming towards me but closer. I called into my coworkers who came outside and also observed this. the four objects were not flying or floating. The movement was very calm and peaceful, I cannot describe it. The four objects then seemed to hover for a bout 2-3 minutes and then proceede toward the east and hung there in a distance for 7-8 minutes and then just faded away.
