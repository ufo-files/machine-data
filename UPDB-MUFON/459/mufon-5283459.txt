small sphere, orange flames low altitude from W to E, no sound, trails, smoke, two, one after the other 10 minutes apart

I was outside when I saw a low altitude object, spherical in shape, covered in orange flames. It traveled at a constant speed in a consistent trajectory. There was no sound, no smoke, no emission trail, no other strange phenomena. It flickered some, perhaps extinguishing, but only for a second, then covered in orange flame again. It disappeared to the east behind trees. I called my daughter out to witness this object. We went back inside and I did some small chores. I was compelled by something, I don't know what, to return to my backyard. I did so, only to find another flaming sphere. It traveled the same speed, the same trajectory, and the same altitude as the other. No smoke, no sound, no emissions, some flickering, no strange phenomena. This occured about ten minutes after the first one. The second one was also witnessed by my daughter.  I took video with my phone. The resolution is not good, the flames are not visible. The shaking and erratic movement is me trying to keep the object in the frame. The object traveled unwaveringly in a straight trajectory and a constant velocity. What was so odd was the second occurrence. Had it just been one, something inexplicable, but, to have a second occurrence, identical to the first, is beyond happenstance.
