{ck} Object appeared from northeast cruising very slowly; stopped and hovered, bright orange flickered and pulsed, faded and abruptly moved south - first slow then accelerated

On July 4, 2012, in southern Las Vegas around 10:10 pm observed a bright orange pulsing/flickering object in the northeast sky. Object approached very slowly then hovered and continued flickering and shimmering. The was no sound associated with the objects movement. Object was bright orange with a fuzzy orange halo, appearing generally round. Estimated distance was approximately 1 mile away, about 30 degrees off the horizon. The object/halo when hovering was about the size of a marble in an outstretched arms length. Approach time from initial sighting was about 45 seconds, then object stopped and hovered in the sky. After about 45 seconds of hovering, the object began to fade and moved slowly southwesterly. After about 20 seconds of slow movement, the object abruptly changed direction and accelerated to the south and disappeared. Observed by three adults, each with scientific backgrounds. This was not a firework or flare, nor did it exhibit flight characteristics of any convention aircraft.
