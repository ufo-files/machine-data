This large object was hovering motionless over a major highway near a radio station, WEDG 103.3. When first observed, I believed it to be a commercial airliner on a landing flight path to Buffalo Niagara International Airport. But it never moved, we were traveling home on NYS I90 in Cheektowaga NY at the time near the Galleria Mall. Ive often seen jet airliners on landing paths in that area so I assumed thats what it was. But I was driving, and my wife disagreed with me right away! She snapped this picture of the object with out Sanyo Xacti cam. Its not the best camera in the world, but has provided us with many great pictures! There was a massive night time thruway repair going on at the same time. The nearby roadway was lit very brightly for the NYS DOT work crews. We were petrified, but this was not our first UFO sighting. It was the first we could share with a picture to prove it!
