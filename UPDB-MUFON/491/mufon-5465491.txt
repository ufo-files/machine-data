A hoovering object over Seylap Caddesi/Eskisehir

Me and my friend saw a hoovering object above Seylap caddesi in April 23rd 2018. It had a metallic colored surface and reflecting sunlight. It did not make any noise and hoovered above the street from NW to NE. The event lasted 5 to 7 minutes than the object slowly dissapeared. Tried to take a video but it was so hard without a tripod or a better camera.
