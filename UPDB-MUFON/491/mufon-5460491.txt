Was looking for Orion, and I saw three lights in a straight vertical line, then it turned and went horizontal, as it turned, I saw a string of 15 or so orbs tailing it, then they disappeared, and I watched the three lights travel across the sky, about the speed of a satellite, but they were not keeping in a straight line, but kind of bobbing and pulsating, I waited until they slowly went out of sight.
