{rjl} A bright light with a vapor like orb around it flying NE at approx 1500 feet

On Sunday, June 21 at approximately 2230 hours, my wife and I were sitting on our back patio underneath our deck enjoying the beautiful clear night at our lake. Our neighbors to the north of our house were all out in their backyards sitting by a fire pit. I decided to get up and go grab a drink when a light in the sky caught my attention. I immediately told my wife to stand up and look at it. It was the strangest thing I had ever seen in the night sky. A bright yellow orange light surrounded by an orb a vapor. I immediately ran into the house to grab my binoculars to try and get a better look at the object. In hindsight I wish I would've grabbed my camera. I yelled over at the neighbors to look up in the sky and they were all witness to the same thing I was seeing. The object flew north east and a medium to slow speed maintaining an even and steady altitude. as the object got farther away it seem too dim and slowly disappear as if it were being cloaked. The object did not make any noise at all it was completely silent. I am retired military of 22 years, a pilot, and I am in law-enforcement. I have never witnessed anything like this before
