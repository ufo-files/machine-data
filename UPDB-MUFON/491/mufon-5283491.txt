Metallic cylinder

I was sitting in backyard looking at cloud formations. I noticed a silvery metallic cylinder with rounded ends. I grabbed my phone and took 3 pictures. It is very small in the picture but I estimate it’s length to be 10-20 feet long and maybe 6-8 feet in diameter it was coming from the northeast moving about 20-30 miles per hour to the west. It disappeared from view as it approached the western horizon below the sun. At first thought it might be a Mylar balloon but I have never seen a balloon hold a stable heading and altitude and not roll, yaw or spin. It moved in an intentional sort of way. The wind speed that day was around 8-10 miles per hour blowing west. Temperature was around 80 degrees.
