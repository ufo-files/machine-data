Brief, blinking small object

While sitting outside in the dark enjoying the evening, we saw a small blinking green light go across the sky at a very high rate of speed. It first traveled toward the west, stopped abruptly, hovered for a few seconds, turned at a right angle toward the north, hovered again, then continued toward the northeast. It was blinking the entire time. Other than when it was hovering, it traveled at a very high rate of speed.
