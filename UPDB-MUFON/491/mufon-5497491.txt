{nd} Looked up and saw a narrow formation of lights in the shape of a triangle

I was going up to the airport to deliver drop off some packages for the aircraft. I was waiting at the gate for someone to notice me. After about 15 minutes of waiting, I glanced up and saw a very faint set of lights in the shape of a triangle. I could immediately tell that they belonged to an aircraft of some sort. I used to be a pilot. The first thing that struck me was that the object had no beacon lights what so ever. My first thought was it was a stealth fighter of some kind, however upon observing the aircraft, I noticed that the aircraft was too narrow to resemble any kind of fighter jet that I've seen. It didn't change directions, speed up or slow down, or pulsate at all. It just silently flew into the horizon. It was dark, so I couldn't make out a shape against the night sky, but judging by the lights, I would guess it was a triangle. I'm not entirely sure how far it was off the ground, but I can say that if it were a plane, it was flying notably low, especially for being in class B airspace (again, with no beacons). It was also flying perpendicular to the runway in front of me. I took a video of it, however the video is really just a black screen with me going crazy.
