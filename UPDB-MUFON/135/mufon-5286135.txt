Metallic object hovering in front of a trail cam

I have a trail cam situated at night to observe any animals that may wander into my backyard. On 3:03 am, July 30, the trail cam audio/video recorded an object hovering in front of the lens that looks exactly like a typical metallic UFO.

I think that it is very unusual to have a small metallic looking object hovering in front of the trail cam. It doesn't make sense. There was absolutely nothing to the front of the camera. There is a wood line about 50 yards in front of the camera and cannot be picked up by the camera.
