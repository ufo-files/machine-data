{jpg} CIGAR SHAPE WITH LEGS

I saw what looked like a cigar shaped object traveling east , very slowly. My girlfriend saw it first and alerted me. I stood up and looked in the direction she was pointing and I saw it.
The object looked like, well...it reminded me of the probe droid from Empire strikes back, it looked like it had legs hanging from the bottom only it was long shaped. It looked like a giant flea actually !
it travelled north, stopped for about 5 minutes and turned to travel north east. It kept going until it was out of sight. I tried keeping a visual with it with my binoculars for as long as I could until it was out of range.
