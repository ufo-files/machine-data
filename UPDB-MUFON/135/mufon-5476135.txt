Started out like fireworks or a fallling sparkler then a very big object appeared. It looked metallic and had flashing lights. It stopped directly above our heads and stayed there for some time. I

I first noticed the object while driving in a car. It started out looking like a giant sparkler that you would put on a birthday cake but it was falling, after that ended what looked like the flashing lights of a plane appeared out of the sparkles. It was still quite far away. We pulled over at a gas station to watch it as it kept getting closer. It moved very smoothly and never made a sound. It appeared to be coming right too us. Eventually it was right over our heads and stopped. It was so big it covered the entire sky I would estimate it to be maybe 4 football fields in size or about the size of an aircraft carrier and appeared to be about 50-100" directly above our heads. I could see metal and flashing lights and it hovered there for a time. I became disoriented and looked away, when I looked back it was gone and I asked my friends where it went and they pointed it out to me. It had moved about 2-5 km while I wasn't looking. After the event we turned on the radio and every local station was reporting calls from listeners reporting the event. The next day it was reported on the news that a Russian satellite had fallen from orbit the night before. THAT WAS A LIE!!
