While walking my dog this morning, I noticed an object in the sky between the trees above the houses. At first, dismissed it as a large advertising balloon that may have broke loose from a buisness. Then I thought it was a helicopter come strait towards me due to the object get lower in altitude. As I walked closer I thought maybe it's a drone from a real estate company. But it was about the size of a Volkswagen beetle and made no sound. At certain angles I could see flat/straight panels on the object. Silent, without sound or visible wings or propellers it flew from what looked like the center of my town over my neighborhood across the desert towards the estrella mountains.
