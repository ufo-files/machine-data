5 lights hovering North of Seattle

I am familiar with local flight patterns for planes arriving and departing so when I first saw these lights I immediately was puzzled because they were brighter than your average plane displaying landing lights and their formation wasn't in the normal path of most other planes arriving from the north. 

Also, in relation to the silhouetted trees one would have expected the lights to move toward the south if they were indeed planes arriving in from the north. 

The only movements I witnessed over the 5 minutes that they appeared was them, one by one, drifting off to the west as their lights faded.
