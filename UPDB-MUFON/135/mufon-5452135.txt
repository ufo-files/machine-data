cigar shaped UFO dropping an Orb into the ocean

This sighting happened around 1979-80. I was living at Silverstrand Beach in Oxnard, CA. I walked outside and glanced towards the sky and saw a cigar-shaped black UFO silently and slowly heading towards the ocean. I was so amazed and excited about what I was witnessing that I followed it until I reached the shoreline, which was about a block and a half from my home. I observed it traveling to the end of the jetty where it stopped and suddenly dropped a bright white glowing orb that disappeared into the ocean. Seeing that orb drop into the ocean scared me more than the sight of the cigar-shaped UFO. I ran back to my house, shared this sighting with my roommate, and called the police to report the sighting. I wish I had never made that call because they just laughed and did nothing.
