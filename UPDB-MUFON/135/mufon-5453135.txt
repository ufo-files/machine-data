Beam of light caught of security cams in front of house near where a circle in my grass is

From dusk to dawn on our security cams in the front of my house was a beam of light that pulsed and moved all night till morning. I have the video saved. That same night my crossed the street neighbor either died or had something happen to him. The paramedics came with a fire truck but the beam still stayed in the same spot. I went out to see what it was but couldnt even see the beam of light. Only on the cam is when you can see it. The next morning i reviewed the tapes and it was there all night. I also checked the cam for anything on the lens but nothing was there. When i went to the circle of grass the beam was on today 5-2-2020 the day after the event. My arm in a spot where i have a mark of something that wont go away started to hurt and buzz. Also the bridge of my nose did the same and so did my stomach. Also i had the sudden feeling i had to make a wooden marker like a totum poll in the circle in my front yard and i felt as if i was missing for a while. And i felt very confused and flustered.
