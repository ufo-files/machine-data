{cak} Observed unidentified object flying overhead. Large, lights, no sound.

At about 5:56pm tonight, my daughter called my attention to an anomaly in the sky. We were relaxing in our jacuzzi when she sighted the object. I immediately turned and saw a series of small white lights, that I initially assumed were Space X related, as we often see trails from launches they do in SoCal. I immediately realized this was different. We hear residual noise from launches and they angle up. This was the outline of something huge in white lights, appearing like dots. My 13 y/o daughter saw a pizza slice image. I saw a grouping of lights that looked like a long centipede. There was no noise at all. It manifested in her direct field of vision, then vanished as we were watching. I had my phone nearby and began recording. I couldn't see it in my screen as I recorded, but we both could see the object in the sky and you hear our audio. My daughter will play with the video and see if anything is visible with filters. The object was low, large and nothing I have seen before.

We reside near a Marine base and Palomar Airport. Military aircraft and executive aircraft fly over frequently. This was entirely different. It is Veteran's Day, so my first inclination was it may be a tribute of sorts, but we can find nothing online. Again. there were no sounds and it just vanished after cruising across or field of vision.

I was a flight attendant. My father was a pilot. I cannot explain what we saw. I am hopeful you can put the experience into perspective and perhaps confirm there was in fact some sort of military fly over that we witnessed. We are both happy to answer any additional questions. I had my daughter sketch what we / she saw and make her own personal notes.
