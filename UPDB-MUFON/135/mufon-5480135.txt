{rjl} V shaped boomerang gliding thru air, no sound, pretty fast, seemed transparent. Husband also saw

My husband and I were outside smoking at 10:10 pm. Looking up at sky I noticed a V shaped boomerang sailing across the sky. It looked very light colored around edges/shape but could not see body in between edges. I asked "what the hell is that?" He looked up and saw the object and said "I have no idea." It made no sound. Seemed to be moving much faster than a commercial plane. I commented that it seemed white or gray at the edges and husband said it reminded him of wonder woman's plane cause it seemed transparent. He got up to see if he could still see it, but could not. There were three planes that appeared soon after. The UFO was moving south and the planes came from the south, west and north. Not exactly at same time. Two were most definitely commercial planes. Dallas Executive airport is only about a mile from our house.
