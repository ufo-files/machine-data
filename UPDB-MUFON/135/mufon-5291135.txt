Two objects recorded in sky from aircraft.

Objects were discovered on film and video taken on a small charter flight from Ft Lauderdale to the Bahamas. There were four passengers and two crew. While in flight I was taking photos and videos to document the sun and clouds. I was never aware of nor did I ever witness any objects near my aircraft or anywhere in the sky with my naked eye. I did not review the footage until days later when going to post cloud in sun photos and discovered objects both an airplane shaped craft and a triangle shaped object with three dots. Both objects appear together and separately. Please watch in slow motion to see how objects come in and out of frame. Although the skies were overcast and windy the flight itself was smooth and devoid of turbulence. I am a very nervous flyer, but the 55 min flight was very easy.
