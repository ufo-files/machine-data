{rjl} For a sec or two sky went black ,day was bright and sunny, three people in house observed event, all at the same time sked wht was that.

While sitting inside the house in the kitchen, facing south, with the tv on but no lights on in any of the rooms of the house, at approximately 1:13 p.m. On March 17th, the whole house inside and the surrounding area outside of the house went black. It was like some huge object blocked out the sun for a second or two. No noise was heard. Two other people present in the house questioned what just had happened. One person was about thirty feet from me and the other person was in another room on the same floor of the house. We have lived in this house for over twenty years and have never experienced such an occurrence . We are under the flight path from O Hare airport but at that time we did not hear any planes. It was almost like having a quick seizure and blocking out only we did not loss consciousness . We all just experienced total blackness at the same time. We saw no object because of being in the house. But commenting about it afterward we agreed the blocking out of the light from the sun would have had to been made by something the size of a football field at least. I know it happened because of having two other people being with me at that time.
