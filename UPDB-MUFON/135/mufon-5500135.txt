{cak} Light in Sky Rose then fell in same path-not a firework or plane

During a small fireworks show off in the distance of our backyard, we saw an object appear in the sky out of nowhere. We saw red and blue lights on this object and quickly dismissed it as a plane. It rose halfway up the horizon, then suddenly stopped for 10 seconds then slowly sunk down then quickly shot backwards and disappeared from our sight. My dad, and mom and I all saw the same thing at the same time and commented on it. It was very strange.
