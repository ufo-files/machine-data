{nd} I was driving from Auburn TO Huntsville, saw hovering lights ahead, closer saw dark, silent, floating metal. People were out of their cars looking up, I kept driving, looked out window, it went straight up and disappeared.

I WAS DRIVING FROM AUBURN TO HUNTSVILLE, ALABAMA, I SAW BRIGHT LIGHTS IN THE SKY, ASSUMED IT WAS A FAR AWAY ADVERTISEMENT SIGN, THE CLOSER I GOT, I SAW THE DARK METAL FLOATING. I WAS VERY SCARED AND THOUGHT IT WOULD ATTACK ME. THEN I SAW 3 CARS PULLED OVER LOOKING UP AT IT SO I HIT THE GAS OUT OF FEAR. I ROLLED DOWN WINDOW TO SEE AND IT WAS COMPLETELY SILENT. AS I PASSED UNDER IT, I WATCHED IT GO STRAIGHT UP AND DISAPPEAR INTO THE SKY. I WAS SCARED AND LOOKING BACKWARDS AND UPWARDS THE ENTIRE DRIVE HOME.
