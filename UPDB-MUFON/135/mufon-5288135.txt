Line of lights evenly spaced where the lead light would stop and start again at the end

I received a call from by brother-in-law. He was panicked about lights in the sky to the northwest. He lives on the south side of Pueblo and I live in Pueblo West. I went outside and saw the following: about five lights, which were evenly spaced apart. The distance the lights were away could only mean the lights were coming from different objects. I first told my BIL that it was probably military planes until I saw the first light to the right stop emitting light and then move to the left at the start, left or further to the west. The lights continued to dot his about 10 times and then the lead light took off at an angle at a high rate of speed and disappeared.
I tried to video record the event on my cell phone but it only recorded for about 5 seconds. The only video showed where I was banging on the window to get my wife to come out. My wife also witnessed the lights for a short period of time.
I am a sergeant with the police force and have served in the army. I cannot say what it was other than it was something not from this world.
