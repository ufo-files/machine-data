{nd} I was driving near the above address at night when I saw three bright objects.

I was driving at the time of witness. I was on my way home from work when I noticed three objects in the sky. There was a plane to the right so I could tell it wasn't a plane. The objects didn't flicker or flash. They just remained at a constant brightness. They appeared to move but I really couldn't tell. I knew they weren't stars because they disappeared by the time I reached home and you could tell they were a lot closer. By the time I got home where I was going to further look at the object and they were gone so I am not sure where and exactly when they disappeared. I tried to take a picture but the lights didn't show up in the picture.
