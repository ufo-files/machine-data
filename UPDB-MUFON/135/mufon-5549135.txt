Red zig zag lightening bolt that retraced itself

My sister and I were driving Northbound on Lakeview Avenue, we had just passed the Kaiser Hospital Bldg. On the corner of my right eye, above in the sky I saw a red light go zig zag like it was tracing something. It was fast, like it was drawing a star, but more like Z. My sister mentioned as a red zig zag lightening bolt, which is good description of it. I saw it, and paused. Then my sister and I looked at each in shock. She saw it, but also heard a loud sound with people yelling. I heard nothing but normal street noise, but we saw the same thing in the Northeastern sky. After we saw it, we had to stop for a train on Lakeview and Orangethorpe. We can get a more accurate time by pulling my sister's cell phone records. She had called her boyfriend right after it happened. After I dropped of my sister off, and I called my husband. He told me to report it to Mufon and then the call dropped. I was not able to get service for a couple of minutes. I tried calling both my sister and husband. I have been having problems with my service since, which I normally don't. I am scared and can't believe what I saw and looked away in shock.
