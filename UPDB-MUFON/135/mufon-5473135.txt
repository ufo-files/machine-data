{cak} 4-5 Pulsating orange/red lights in fixed position over San Diego on Sept 28 2017 for 20 minutes

On Sept 28, 2017, starting around 8:20pm I saw a large bright orange stationary light from the window near my desk/computer in my home. The lights were so bright and stationary (and therefore very different from what I usually see from this window, i.e. normal airplane traffic and occasional helicopters) that I got in my car and drove to the top of a hill near my house. The lights then separated into several pulsating orange/red lights. The lights stayed in fixed position (i.e. did not move/float/dissipate for several minutes). I took few photos with my phone and then took a video. At first I thought they could be Chinese lanterns, or some science experiment from some university students (I live very near San Diego State University), but due to the fact that the same lights were seen in Tijuana, Mexico on the same night, it sort of rules out that logical explanation. I found two videos of these lights on YouTube for the same night (September 28, 2017), both looking up from the ground. One video is from San Diego, and one is from a beach in Tijuana, Mexico. My video timed itself as 8:31pm Pacific Time. I am unable to attach the video because it is too large, but I will attach 2 photos. I will hopefully attend next local MUFON meeting in San Diego to show my video.
