Large triangular {dome shaped abduction dome} object {with lights flashing and bright}

Large triangular object. Hovered over me and father while playing catch with baseball. 

We happened to look up and there it was. No sound. No warning. 

Above us was a dome shaped abduction dome. With lights flashing and bright. Was around 4pm. On a weekend i think. 

Father had developed short term memory loss. Not sure if was because of this incident or not. 

Was low enough to be able to shoot it with a rifle. Was long enough that it stretched beyond neighborhoods. 

We went inside i stood by patio door to observe it. Father looking for camera.

It began moving NE toward sun. Then it moved fast maybe super sonic. Then got to a point to almost went hyperspace like something out of a movie. And disappeared. I would ask my father if he remembers and began losing memory of it. 

Im starting to forget details about it. 
But i know i saw a UFO.
