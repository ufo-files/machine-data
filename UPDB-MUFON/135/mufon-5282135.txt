Reptile

I was walking home I stopped to have a cigarette in front of the La Crosse library. There are a bunch of trees behind me but they are trimmed to about 50 feet tall. I heard a bunch of ruckus going on behind me. Stood up looked around still heard it. Realized it was in the trees. Animals in La Crosse or Minnesota do not jump around and trees that high off the ground. So I scan the area. And out of the blue dropped a rep Tilian looking creature. Roughly 7 feet tall. Green eyes very reptilian looking. And the years that I lived in La Crosse Wisconsin there were many disappearances of people. Scared the living hell out of me. I lived two blocks away on Main Street.
