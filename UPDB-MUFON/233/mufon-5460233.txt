{cak} Bright yellow object

Object was visible early in the morning. It was low in the southeastern sky. Slightly above tree level it was a bright yellow color. At first I thought it was the planet Venus but it was too big and too low in the sky. Walked away for about 20 seconds with my dog and came back. I was perplexed to see that the object was gone. I am an amateur astronomer with a very good telescope. Planets don’t just disappear on a clear morning.
