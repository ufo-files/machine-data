A UFO landed when I was watching the stars.

I was stargazing alone on a hill when I heard a strange humming noise. I was surprised, and unsure of what to do. It was hard to describe how the UFO moved and what it did. I couldn't move or do anything, and I suddenly felt too tired to think. I feel asleep and woke up on the little hill I was on. They also stole all of my starwatching equiptment and my video camera. (I was recording what I saw for my science class.)
