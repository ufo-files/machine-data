Star like flashing light stayed in one place for 1 hour then began to move westward and disappeared

At about 9pm on May 5th, 2021, I went outside to my car for a cigarette break at work. Sun was still up and still blue in the sky. I noticed a "star", the first and only one out. It was in the western sky. Very bright. As the sun started to go down, it appeared to be flashing a faint red and blue with an unchanging white light. I watched it for an entire hour, with binoculars and it never moved or changed, only blinked. Then at about 9:54 pm, the "star" started moving at a slight NW direction, but more west. After a few seconds, it was out of view. I had my son on the phone the entire time, as I watched and described the light. I even had him write down the details as I gave them
