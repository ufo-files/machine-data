I was a passenger in the back seat of a vehicle when I noticed a white light or orb flying just above the tree tops going from west to east. it was close enough to the tree tops that I could see the light reflecting on the top of the trees. At first I thought it might be an airplane but it was too low. Then I thought maybe a helicopter but there was no noise and as it got closer I saw it was just an orb shape. In the video it looks like it is moving the same direction as the car but it flew right over us from right to left side of the car. Also, towards the end of the video clip I zoomed in with my phone camera so it looks like it was moving erratically but it was moving in a straight line. I took a snap shot of the orb in the video and then zoomed in on it and it looks like it is pulsating.
