Putting out recycling and a bright star slightly brightened and blinked out. No more then that, no less.

I was putting my recycling out at night and on my way back from the road, I looked up to the right in the sky and a whiter and brighter than normal star, slightly got brighter and blinked out. It was just no longer there, just darkness. I just froze mid step and stared for about 45 seconds. Immediately my right temple started to throb, gently and get warmer.I came in and reported this.
