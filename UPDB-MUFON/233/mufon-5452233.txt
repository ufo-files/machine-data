Object hovering over Air Force base.

I saw this object hovering over Arnold Air Force base while swimming in Woods Reservoir lake. It hovered in place over the base for at least 10 minutes. Then it travelled From horizon to horizon in under 4 minutes to the SE. It would Stop and hover and then move quickly. It emitted a very bright white light and it was pulsating it was super bright white. It was cigar shaped then either wobbled or changed shape Into an orb like those desktop kinetic orbital things people have. It was silent as far as I can tell. I tried my best to film it with my iphone but lose it a couple times.
