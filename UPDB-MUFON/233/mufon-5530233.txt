Very bright light of various colours.

I have severe sleep problems, so it's not strange for me to be up at silly times and just aimlessly wonder around or in most cases watch the sky.
I peered out my window for a quick look to seee if it was raining, all is dry and the sky still incredibly clear, as I was about to shut my blinds again I spotted a very bright white light just in the tree line opposite my house, it's changing to a various display of colours - white to green to red etc. The house opposite me's garden light came on and the light dissapeared behind the trees, dimming whilst it did so. When it reappeared it started ascending skywards about 1 fingers length above the tree line, where it has now been stationary for the past 20 minutes, it's showing no signs of moving, but it has moved significantly from where I first spotted it. I've checked the stars and they're all in the same position as when I first looked out, I've tried capturing it both on film and still photo, but my phone is not good enough or just unable to pick it up. There's been a number of lights over this area in the past month... Very spectacular.
