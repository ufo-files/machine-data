I was running for exercise on the extensive and uninhabited cross country trails across from my house. The land is a mix of prairie and forested area, but it is mostly open. I had ran about a mile and happened to look up and see two white orb/circular looking shapes in the sky. At first I thought they were drones, but they were moving like UFOs. I later looked on the internet to identify if they were drones and they didn't look anything like them. 
The objects were still in the sky hovering and then would move around again and stop. They were moving very fast and in a linear/constant motion. They did not speed or slow while moving, the velocity was constant.
My father was with me and I called out to him to look, but by the time he looked they were disappearing, moving backwards until I couldn't see them. My father never saw them. This all happened in broad daylight.
My feelings were in all 22 years of my existence, I've never seen something move so smooth or quickly/adeptly through the sky. I felt elated that I might have seen my first UFO. I already knew they existed because my friend from lower school had connections with the government and she knew they were real and told me. I didn't believe her, we were only 8 or 9 at the time and I thought she was trying to scare me. But later when her mother found out she had told she admonished the girl and I knew it must be the truth, that the girl really had heard it from someone in government.
