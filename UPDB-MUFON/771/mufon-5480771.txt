UFO came from behind the Eucalyptus trees .It was initially like fire ball but afterwards it was flying object with transparent walls without any noise

It was like fireball when I saw it coming from west the Eucalyptus trees behind the school building . I was in the school premises to pickup my younger brother when he came back from picnic. It was 9:30 pm .When the UFO came closer I noticed that it had no sound or any kind of engine noise.When it was flying above the school roof just may be 100 ft height I saw transparent walls of that object.There were some kind of Bulbs glowing slowly and then turning off.so it was kind of flashing but slow manner. Almost 7-8 minutes I saw it then slowly unlike airplane it was gone in to east direction.On the next day some students who werw studying on the terrace reported the same incident.It was published in local news paper also.
