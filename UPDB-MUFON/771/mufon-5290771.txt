5 dull white lights in delta formation

Standing on my apartment deck looking for satellites on a cloudless night, I saw 5 dull white lights in a delta formation starting at about 60 degrees and disappearing straight overhead being blocked by the deck roof. After a "what the.....?" I looked for a formation waver, there was none. Then I listened and there was no sound. Plans fly in the distance constantly, lining up to land at Sea-Tac and I can occasionally hear them, so I would have heard 5 planes flying directly over me. They were about he size of two fingers held at arms length and traveled 1.5 times faster than the ISS when it goes by...COOL!
