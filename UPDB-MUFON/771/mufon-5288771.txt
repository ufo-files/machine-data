Over 1 dozen small star-like objects in sky followed by what looked like a white, glowing paper-airplane

6:30 pm eastern time - I initially saw over a dozen little white dots flying around way up in the sky. They looked like stars, were moving with no particular path or pattern, and the sky was way too light and blue to see stars yet. Then from overhead (NW from where I had been standing on the beach) I noticed a huge, bright white light that looked almost like a caterpillar in shape (similar in size/shape to the EBANIS seen in Mexico but pure white light) - it descended in an arc over the ocean and as it got lower in the sky appeared to change shape into what looks like a paper airplane. At the beginning of my video you can see 1 of the little white dots to the left of the larger object. After the large object disappears from view into the horizon, I tape an airplane for contrast (timestamp 1:54), and then noticed another larger object in the sky - this one appeared above where I was standing (high up in altitude) and seemed to descend as well but not as drastically as the 1st large object. This object appeared more circular in shape. Apologies for the shaky video I kept forgetting I was recording and it was difficult to see if I was capturing the objects on camera with the sun being so bright still. Shorter video attached is of a military helicopter that flew by approx. 13 minutes after sighting
