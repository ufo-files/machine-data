Was outside smoking and talking with friends when a light appeared pulsating green as it flew over head it had no noise and was only about 200 feet above us.

We were sitting outside smokeing and talking when a friend of mine told me to turn around there was something in the shy moving left then right but still flying in a N.W. direction.As it got closer to us it was pulsating (Not flashing)a electric green color,We watched in amazement as it moved in very strange movements.We stood and watched it as it passed right over our heads,While still moving left to right but coming back to the path it was on.The strange part is it had no sound and was in no hurry,As low as it was we should of heard something,Im guessing it couldnt of been more than 200 feet over us.The shape was the shape of a triangle with one of the points cut off with 2 green lights on each side.We watched it till it was out of sight.It was moving slower than anything ive ever seen in the air.The strange part of the night was right after it went threw a jet left clinton field and was well below flight deck and could see the exhaust from the engines at night in the dark.We were discussing it when a few other people came up and said they had never seen one like it befor but there has been to many to count in the last 3 to 4 weeks.When you see something like this it makes you pay attention,And if you don't believe it will change your way of thinking.
