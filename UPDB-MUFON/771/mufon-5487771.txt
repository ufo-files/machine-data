I was 6. Went to sleep. Woke in hallway. Looked down hall saw alien pop head out. Dead bolt was locked.

It was 1982. I was 6 years old and was heading to bed, my mother, father and sister where in the house. Anyways I went to bed, my room was at the top of the stairs in or 2 story home. So I go to sleep like any other night, I wake up in the early morning hours and Iam laying at the front door of the condo, I start to freak out and start pounding on the door as it was still locked but as Iam doing this I get this overwhelming feeling that there is something in the hallway with me, that's when I turn my head down the hall and see this head pop around the corner. I go completely bannannas and continue to pound on the door, when my mother finally got to the door she was just as baffled as I was because the deadbolt was still locked... This memory is still very vivid to me... I have told people about it before, but I figured it's time to share here to get a blasting from the Internet... Maybe Iam crazy or maybe I was a kid with an active imagination. Who knows... Be nice to see if anyone else has experienced anything similar... This happened in Canada on Vancouver island...
