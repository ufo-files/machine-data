{rjl} Triangle craft with red lights on bottom

On a foggy night I went to let my dogs outside my back yard and looked back over my house and saw a triangler shaped craft hovering about 50 feet away from me in front of my next door neighbor's house, over the street. My house is across the street from the end of the Medows Field Airport runway, and I knew that this craft was in the flight path.
Because of the fog reflected the red light comming form the craft I could see the triangular shap that had three red lights , one each on the bottom corners of the triangle and there were also unlight circles in between the corners. There was also a larger half globe shape circle in the center, also unlight. 
I could see the bottom and one side of the craft. On the side I thought I saw two windows, because the fog relected the light back at the craft I don't think they were windows, but air ducts. the reason is I could see the inside upper part of the duct, and it didn't look like I was looking into a craft, but more of a duct like on aircraft. It was flat black and had no sound. If I had to guess I would say this is man made because of the ducts.
