{rjl} Group of strange lights appeared moved erratically and shot straight up and disappeared.

At around 8:30 pm EST May 20 2019 A buddy and myself where ending a day of fishing on High Rock Lake In Rowan county North Carolina. We where heading east toward the main channel from the Tamarac Marina and as we entered the main channel, my buddy makes a joke about the lights in the sky. Initially he said “ look it’s aliens” and we laughed it off. Both of us being lifelong friends and knowing we where both intrigued with aircraft we decided to kill the engine on the boat and watch, what we thought at the time to be helicopters in formation coming over the tree tops.( it was not uncommon as the local army reserves employ many helicopters) but as we watched the group of lights in the dusk sky skate around the edge of a large thunderstorm that had just passed. Three of the lights formed a triangle and seemed to pull into a tighter formation. A fourth light started to fly further toward the south and seemed to hold elevation. All of the sudden the group of three started to flicker and appeared to be climbing in elevation extremely fast until they seemed to just disappear into sky. The fourth light zig zagged in its movement and then it to stopped rapidly and shit straight up into the sky
