Multiple glowing objects disappear over Minot

We were in our backyard having a small fire and I happened to look up and saw what I thought was a satellite flying directly above me (small, circular white dot) in a straight line from west to East when it suddenly disappeared. Moments later a second object appeared near where the first object had been only this one was red, small and circular and flew from south to north before disappearing. The whole event lasted all of about 30 seconds. I have no footage unfortunately because I did not have my phone on me and it happened so fast.
