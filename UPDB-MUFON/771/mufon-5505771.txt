{rjl} Pulsating fireball Orb hovering in sky for 5 min. then blinked out.

At or around 8:30 pm on December 31 2013 Me and my wife were driving home from dinner and as we turned off of Chimney Rock Rd./Hwy 64 East in Edneyville onto Gilliam Mtn. Road we noticed a larger than usual star shaped object in the sky. Upon traveling directly towards the object on Gilliam Mtn. Rd. (approximately 1/4 mile) we observed the object was pulsating and appeared to be on fire. The colors changed from bright orange to red and the object at times moved away from our location and would stop and hover. Im not an aviator but would guess the objects altitude was between 5000 to 7000 feet as we could see the mountain on which we live and at its top is 3000 ft. we pulled in to a church parking lot and turned off our vehicle and got out. Standing in the parking lot we witnessed the object pulsate red and orange in a fiery glow as it hovered then slowly start moving away then would stop again. there was absolutely no sound coming from the object however there were several other aircraft visible in the sky and they all had flashing red/white navigational lights and moved in a consistent straight flight path that one would expect from conventional aircraft. After about 5 minute of the object pulsating and making slow irregular movements (all the time moving away from our location) the object blinked in and out of sight maybe three times the object then completely disappeared. the weather was clear. I did have my phone w/ camera but knew that it was pointless to video the object as the resolution would have made the video unintelligible.
