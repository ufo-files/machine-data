Red in color and no blinking lights

We were watching the fire works but you can see that the flashing stops and there is still something red flying thru the air and it does not burn out it is not part of the upper fire works displays.First one came thru the sky at about 9:00pm it was red and no blinking lights and did not follow the normal plane patterns and did not make noise. The second one came like a half hour later I showed my husband and we followed it thru the skies for a while until it disappeared and then a third one came thru like twenty minutes later or so.We could not figure out what the objects were. Then like an hour later there were two fly almost side by side. I called a neighbor to witness this and he said they were watching it from their back yard. When we were watching them it lasted for like five minutes maybe a little longer and then they just vanished into the sky. The neighbor, my husband and I all seen them.
