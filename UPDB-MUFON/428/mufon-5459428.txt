{tjd} Flying triangle with no sound, backlit by full moon.

I observed a flying triangle with no sound while sitting on my front porch. at first I only saw the lights, but it passed within a few degrees below and backlit by full the moon. It passed overhead banking toward and then over my neighbor's house. It appeared to hover between the area of the Beacon Metro North station and Bannerman's Island. It then continued following the river south. At no time was there engine or air noise. It disappeared from sight for approximately two minutes. It returned to the exact path over the Hudson River. I ran to get my cell phone and my wife to verify the lack of noise, but while I was inside it increased altitude and flew back over the Hudson and climbed into the darkness of the sky. It was similar in shape and I assume size to a stealth fighter, but made no sound. It also flew slowly. It was approx the same size as the moon looks from the ground. My wife said it was a plane, but did not see it as closely as I did. I waited on my porch the next night to see if it was a military plane and have my phone with me and fully charged the next night but it did not return. I will also be outside again tonight. -Also the sky was oddly free of ALL air traffic before and during the sighting. Cloud cover was light and sparse, air was humid but still clear. My neighbor has also spotted silent aircraft over our neighborhood when I told her the next day.
