{rjl} Repeated siting of three star like lights in a trianglular formation. Hovered. Quickly moved back and forth. Fly away quickly

I do not know the exact day or even year. I lived at the above address and I saw them from my bed at night. I'm guessing in the 1980's. I woke up and saw them out my window and would see three lights (star like) in a triangular formation. Thought they were just stars but they started to quiver and move slightly. I saw this more than once. I'm guessing 10 times?? Each time they were in the same place. Sometimes one would move back and forth very quickly. They might take off quickly. One might move away. Most of the time they seemed to quiver, hover, or move around them selves very quickly. I lost sight of them usually because I must have fallen back to sleep. I felt fascinated by them. Curious. I never told anybody about it. Not sure why. Perhaps I didn't believe what I saw either or that I dreamed it. I don't think I did in hind sight. I always felt like something was watching me from the moment I moved into the house. I never had that feeling before or really later in life. 

At least two times I remember being woken up by a VERY bright light shining into my window. It scared me a lot. Never knew what it was. Thought I might be dreaming it?? There is a hill outside my window and I thought maybe it was some teenagers on the hill shining a light into my window. But now that I think about it the light was way too bright. It filled the room with white light. I would curl up and close my eyes because it scared me so much. I never figured out what any of the above was, but I never forgot about it.
