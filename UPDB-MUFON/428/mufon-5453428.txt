{tjd} Twinkle-to Star like visual

While looking up to the stars (no moon) I observe the regular array of stars, a couple of larger bright er and a few smaller apparently farther away not quite as brilliant. Suddenly in the area of the smaller stars I saw what appeared to be a twinkle, and then a small brighter star appeared. The “star” flew southward at an extreme rate of speed, with no contrail. I only saw it for a few seconds and then it was gone. Within a minute or two I saw what appeared to be a satellite, traveling lower in the sky but in the same general direction. This object was traveling very fast also but probably not a quarter of the speed of the first object.
