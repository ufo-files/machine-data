{rjl} Bright white light traveling from west to east at high elevation and speed, also emits sparks’

I was standing in the door of my camper brushing my teeth when noticed a bright white light on the horizon. Initially it appeared to be ascending and I thought perhaps a rocket was launched from the coast. 
At about the time I realized it was approaching at very high speed I also noticed the light emit something that looked like an electrical spark, also white light, that traveled at even higher speed away from the object and roughly perpendicular to the trajectory path, but didn’t travel far before disappearing. 
I watched this bright white light travel in what appeared to be a straight line from west to east until it was out of sight and it emitted 3 ‘sparks’, one before passing virtually overhead and two after it passed by. There was no form, just white light. 
No sound whatsoever. 
It may have slowed down somewhat as it headed away from me, but still far faster than any plane I’ve seen. 
I’ve seen enough satellites to be certain it was not one. 
The main object was very bright, the emissions were not. 
The event happened within 5 minutes of 7:00AM and lasted for about 2 minutes.
