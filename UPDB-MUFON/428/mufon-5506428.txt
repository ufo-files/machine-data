Wow... seriously, you wont believe this, but as we were going to Yakima tonight to do some shopping - we saw a UFO! it was 8:29 PM, and my daughter, me, and my son were driving on I-82 toward Yakima, WA and just as we were nearing the Wapato Exit, my daughter said "Dad, UFO!" and I looked up just in time to see a wing shaped craft, sort of a smudgy Greyish color with a small red and blue light on it. It seemed as if we were driving faster than this UFO was coming toward us. It was only about 25 - 30 feet over our car as we passed under it, and as I turned to watch it go behind us...it was gone! I couldnt see it anymore. When it passed over us it did not make a sound, and as slow as it was going, and as large as it was - it was wider than the four lane highway we were driving on. Later, I thought how could it stay in the sky and move so slowly and quietly? You see something like this, so unexpected... why was it here? Where was it going, and where did it come from....?
