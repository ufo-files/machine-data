Group of approx. 15-20 orange colored ufos flying over Whittier, CA area

was turning onto Mar Vista street from La Cuarta and when I looked up i saw about 15-20 orange colored objects flying at what appeared at steady speed. I pulled over and got out my car and started staring. Other cars passed by and slowed down and i tried to take pictures but they didn't come out well. I kept just observing objects and some flew in odd patterns, two even almost seemed to touch then break away and fly in patterns that planes just arent capable of. I watched until I couldnt see them anymore. After that I called 911 and reported the incident, they told me they had received similar calls right before mine.
