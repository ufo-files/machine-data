There were thirteen aerial objects that stayed within an irregular yet consistent formation for 5 minutes. The objects disappeared and reappeared.

Thirteen individual aerial objects caught our eye while out for a walk on a clear and sunny day. There was not a single cloud in the sky. We noticed the group of angular, shiny objects that hovered very high in the sky for a total of about 7 or 8 minutes. They were not exactly triangular but their edges were straight and geometric. They appeared to increase and decrease their shine or reflectivity and at a couple of points completely vanished and reappeared in the exact same place. It literally looked like a star formation in the sky but it was only the afternoon. The formation then moved in the same pattern, keeping the 13 objects in the same pattern and it moved north east until it disappeared. Later in the week after my curiosity prompted me to watch some documentaries on UFOS, there were several other documented sightings of the exact same thing! It was an experience I will not forget. Would love to know if this is an isolated report in this area. I'd be happy to provide a sketch, I did not capture it on camera unfortunately.
