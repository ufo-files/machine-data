witness saw a bright light east of Orion. No twinkling. Intermittent lights flashed around the object. Lights would last less than a second. If she blinked she would miss it. Husband also present but did not see the same thing.

The witness said she saw at 3:00 a.m. aproximately a briht light in the vicinity of Orion. The light was steady, not twinkling.She thought it might be a planet. Intermittently around the stationary light would appear other lights flashing on and off one at a time. Described as disappearing and reappearing elsewhere. Witness sketched the constellation Orion from memory and placed the light above Sirius about fifteen degrees virtually directly above. This sighting lasted for about five minutes. Her husband witnessed the event as well although he did not see the same thing. He denied seeing the intermittent lights. The lights simply faded after a while. No special reaction was elicited from the witness other than wonder.
