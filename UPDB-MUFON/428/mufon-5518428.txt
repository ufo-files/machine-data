several lights (15) or more traveling from SW nnn

I was sitting in my backyard with my wife talking enjoying the evening weather when I said to her, look at all the planes coming in. There were 5 lights to begin with, then a bunch of lights (as many as 15) all at one time started to appear. I ran inside and got my camera and snapped a few shots. One photo we can count about 12 lights. I ran upstairs and got the video camera and by the time I got to the upstairs deck they started "burning out" or fading away. They were in the Southwest sky traveling East and upwards. This is the usual direction for planes on approach to Las Vegas from LA or Burbank. These were not planes because weve never seen that many and we can track the planes across the sky. These lights were going at an angle to the south or upwards. There were also way too many for airlines to be that close together. It made for a interesting night.
