{rjl} Formation of 8 star-like lights/objects moving in a straight-line formation

At first, we thought we were seeing the ISS or a satellite moving overhead as we sometimes see, but then noticed the bright light/object which we thought was the ISS or satellite was trailed by 6 more identical lights/objects that were evenly spaced and traveling in a straight line overhead. An eighth identical light/object was seen a little further behind the seventh, but followed the same straight path. All objects eventually disappeared as they traveled northwest.
