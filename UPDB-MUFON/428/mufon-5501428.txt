{dr}Green lights that either went in a circle or looked like it was flapping. It went straight over head and crossed over from horizon to horizon in 2 minutes or less.

My family and I were outside watching the fireworks. About 9:45 my mom, sister, aunt, and cousin saw something quickly fly over. We began to investigate to see if it might be a satellite on one of our stargazing apps on the iPhone. THere were none that were in that area. Then about 30 minutes later I was standing outside facing south and saw very clearly a green vessel or something similar flying over. I called my aunt over because she was outside. She saw it too. We watched it fly right over head and I followed it as it went as north as I could see. It was a Kelly green color with lights that looked like thy were on wings that were flapping or they were rotating in a circle. It made no noise. My aunt and I said "that is wild, that is NOT a satellite". But we did not know what it was.
