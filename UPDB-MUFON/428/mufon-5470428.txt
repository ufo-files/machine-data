{jpg} Looked like clouded star or airplane, then produced a cone tail or double tail.

I was sitting on my porch, noticed a light that looked like it was behind a cloud or food, but the night is completely clear here, at first I thought it was a plane, but then a plane flew by it undistorted. It moved slowly,and looked like it turned a bit before it let out either a cone shaped self reflecting vapor type trail, could have been two separate tails that were just at the right angle to make it look cone-shaped. I was flabbergasted and in disbelief for a while, even after it disappeared below the surrounding buildings. My camera wouldn't adjust to the low light enough to capture anything.
