8:35 pm AZ time, was outside with my coworker looking at the stars. I thought I saw a cloud moving but as my eye focused on it I noticed it was shaped like a boomerang, traveling kind of fast, & it seemed as though it was invisible. I know if its invisible I shouldn't see it but that's how my brain comprehended it. My coworker then said wow that's a space ship or something I then saw it ripple....no sounds, no lights. Maybe it's a spy plane but AZ it flew over it turned slightly & I no longer could see it. It never moved up or down it just moved; it scared me. I had an overwhelming feeling that I shouldn't have seen it.
