{cak} I seen 7 large objects solid red spheres flying too close to ground and too slow

I was going to work nightshift at the hospital. I was driving out in the country on a county road with no other cars around and no houses and few trees. Flatland. I seen 7 large objects flying too close to the ground in a line and they were red sphere shaped lights with no landing gear or wings or anything that resembled a plane. They were quiet and they were too slow moving and too low to the ground. They were wrong looking and my mind was trying to make sense of them but they were kind of pretty the way they flew. I was very curious.I said out loud to myself I wonder if their connected? Then the one on the end went up then down then back in line at the same time as the other one on up the line did. Then I wondered if they were answering me and I got scared. They continued on faster now. They went single file to the south with me behind them and we traveled that way until when they got over the city. Then they made a sharp turn to the right and up and disappeared in a flash of light. I got to work shaken up and excited and called the police and reported what I seen and the police were not interested. I know what I seen was not normal, whether it was man made from the airbase nearby or not from this world. I'm glad I got to see it and I'm more glad they didn't have time to stop and chat with me, whoever they were.
