{tjd} Solid green light hovers over Anna Marie Island, FL

In June 2016, my in-laws, family, and I were vacationing in Anna Marie Island, FL. We stayed in a beach house right on the beach and on the night of the sighting it was around 9pm possibly later, nighttime and dark. Looking out onto the ocean you couldn’t see much but the blackness of night with a few stars dimly lighting the beach/waves. There was a decent breeze coming off the ocean but nothing bothersome that would’ve sent us inside. There were about 9 family members out on the beachside patio- which faces the ocean. When a solid bright green sphere object came into my view out of the corner of my right eye. It was hovering below the cloud cover but farther up above the building lining. It first hovered then began a rapid aerial flight path too quick to be known aircraft. When it started shooting across the sky I yelled to my other family members who also saw it and we looked at each other and asked what it could be. We were all astonished at the sight of this green glowing sphere moving so rapidly across the sky. A true UFO. I first spotted it further off the coast then it flew in a path due East inland further into Florida state, past Anna Marie Island. We all wondered what we had just witnessed and I asked if there was an Air Force base nearby or not. No one knew. Neither did anyone else want to acknowledge what we all saw after that night. I have never told anyone this information about this sighting.
