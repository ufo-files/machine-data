FILLED OUT AT MUFON HQ BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR 18809 WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE ON 11/20/2019.
WITNESS IS 73 YEARS OLD AND ARTICULATE ABOUT THE SIGHTING.

IT WAS ABOUT 2:30 AM AND WE WERE SHRIMP FISHING ON A 72' FISHING BOAT, 70 MILES NW OFF THE FLORIDA KEYS WHEN A BRIGHT ORANGE LIGHT CAME OUT OF THE OCEAN, AND LIT UP THE BOAT.
I WAS AT THE HELM AND CREW MEMBERS RAN UP AND WANTED TO KNOW WHAT WAS GOING ON.
THE LIGHT CAME OUT OF THE OCEAN , HOVERED, TURNED BLUE, THEN TURNED WHITE LIKE A STAR AND SHOT UP OUT OF SIGHT.
WHEN THE LIGHT CAME OUT OF THE OCEAN IT LIT UP THE WHOLE SHIP
IT SEEMED TO BE ABOUT 100' IN DIAMETER AND HAD TURNED THE OCEAN ORANGE BEFORE IT SURFACED.
IT CAME OUT OF THE OCEAN AT AN 80 DEGREE ANGLE AND SHOT OFF FAST AS LIGHTING.
THE NEXT DAY AT ABOUT 2:30 IN THE AFTERNOON THE USS FORRESTAL (CV-59) CRUISED PAST US. THIS WAS UNUSUAL SINCE WE WERE IN ABOUT 90' OF WATER AT THAT TIME. I IDENTIFIED MYSELF TO THE SHIP BUT DID NOT MENTION SEEING THE USO/UFO.
