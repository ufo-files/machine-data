I was driving home from Bullhead city Az and observed 5 crafts flying down the Colorado river in formation then they stopped and hovered for several mins and then continued down the river.

At approx. 19 15 on 9-13-11 I was returning home from Bullhead City Az on Hwy 95 I was just passing the entrance to Home depot when I noticed 5 v shape crafts flying in formation coming down the Colorado river from the north. They were flying very slow and low only 50 feet or so from the river as they approached I stopped and watched them from the parking lot of Home depot on Hwy 95. They stopped in mid air and hovered for approx. 1.30 to 20 minutes they they started moving again the same direction they were they continued for another 200 yards then all of a sudden a blue white light flashed and they flew out of sight so fast I couldn't believe it.
