{cak} Was observing heat lightning

A friend and I went to Legacy Village to grab food when we observed heat lightning. So we started video tapping, because in Cleveland heat lightning is very rare. I saw the object as I was starting at the sky filming. It moved from right to left very quickly and was very shaky. When I saw the object, I felt as if I had seen a ghost. I decided to tell and show my friend the video once we got in line for our food and we both agreed that the light was moving way to fast though the gust of heat lightning clouds, and way to shaky to be an airplane. The light eventually just disappeared behind the clouds. So I decided to report it and see what you guys thought.
