Huge shimmering white pyramid triangle 4 yards length away from me. Taller than the tree's in the field.

I used to live in a park house, near the moors, nature reserve etc, but also a small school & fields opposite.
I used to foster rescue dogs & this one night I had a new one who was very timid, I took her for a walk going to the shop... crossed the road & walked down the path by the school field, but only concentrating on the dog so didn't look 'up' till halfway down the path.
When I did look up, I couldn't believe it = there was a HUGE shimmering pyramid triangle only a few yards away from me in the corner of the field (we were only seperated by the small hedge bushes)
It was so brillant white, so much that it was shimmering. (It was as tall as the tree's which were at that edge of the field.)
I started to run back to the house to get a camera, but then thought instantly "No, it'll be gone" so ran back.
It was there for about 20 more seconds, then it shot upwards in a straight directory into the sky, then it just stopped dead again & stayed there for about 5 seconds, then shot upwards again, stopped dead, then shot upwards again.
I visually tracked it in disbelief. Time was about 9.45 pm, late July 1998
Reaction during & afterwards = I was shaking like a leaf. I tried to tell people about it, but they thought I was crazy, so I stopped very quickly telling people.
I did notice that the next morning that where I saw the triangle = that the area of field where it was, was absolutely water-logged, inches of water above the grass (only in that area.)
