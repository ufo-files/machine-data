I saw a massive glowing globe at 4 Am It pulsated green, blue, red and white whilst leaving a trail. Moved slowly across the sky.

4 Am in Sydney, June the 11th, i was looking up at the sky and i saw a massive pulsating globe green/blue/red/white. It moved slowly across the sky in all sorts of directions. I watched it for about an hour and a half moving different directions. In this time i pulled out my binoculars and found it hard to focus on the object as it seem to have a trail. I pulled out the binoculars because i have seen this orb 2 other times in the past two months. The first time was 8 weeks ago, and the second 3 weeks ago, during the hours of 1am-5am. When iv seen this orb moving around the sky i have seen numerous of small white/red orbs which at first i thought were stars. Each time iv seen the orb, the sky will also be accompanied by these moving orbs in different directions, some very far away to be only seen as a dot, but i have seen numerous of them, even playing in the clouds. I told everyone the next day with a few weird reactions. I filmed one pulsating and showed my friends. The second time, as soon as i saw the the bright pulsating orb i grabbed my boyfriend and made him sit with me and watch the star. Not only did he see the orb move across the sky but numerous white ones moving around the sky. He was shocked and was sorry for not taking me seriously. This time on June the 11th at 4am i spotted the pulsating orb again! Grabbing my binoculars i only then saw the other detailed colors blue and red, which from a distance only looked like green and white with a trail. Almost like Christmas lights. No other small stars though. What is this and why is it becoming so frequent!
