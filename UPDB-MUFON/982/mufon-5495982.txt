I went bow hunting with my dad at a place that we hunted for years. We got into our stands in the dark.I was there for i think a short time. i then saw a light in the distance and it got closer i kept thinking it is a plain or helicopter.But then i started to think that i could not hear any noise the light kept getting closer still no noise.until it seemed like it got very close, almost like it was over me still no noise. then i do not remember it leaving. i do not think we saw any deer that morning and i do not think we really talked about the sighting that much either.I do not remember how long ago it was maybe 3-5 yrs ago.I ask my dad if he remember it and he has said he has but with some hesitation. he is 87 yrs old know and i'm not sure if he is just saying that to appease me. It was a bright round light that's all i saw. No noise at all.Very weird That is all that i remember. I did at the time think it was something other then a normal plain or helicopter and thought it might of been a helicopter with a noise suppresser or something like that because there was no sound and it seemed like it was directly over me. But it seems as thought i just shrugged it off. That is not like me. I do remember asking him if he saw the light when i came to his stand to get him and he said he did. He was in a stand that was 500 600 yrd's away from me. That is about all i remember. From what i remember the object was coming toward me the whole time i saw it, untill it was over me. Then nothing i do not know what happened to it. Never saw it again it never scared me to stop going there or have a care about it.i would go there buy myself in the dark no problem.
