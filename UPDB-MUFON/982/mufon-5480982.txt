{nd} Viewed series of orbs making incredible directional changes and changes in light intensities.

I decided to go out with some friends to Bricktown, a hot spot for people to hang out on Fridays/Saturdays in OKC. Upon walking into one of the bars I noticed something strange in the sky in the distance. Several orbs flying in all directions at incredible speeds (speeding up/slowing down). I have an extensive background in aviation as do all of my friends. None of us could explain what these were. Keep in mind, there was a massive low pressure system which had just moved in with several huge thunderstorms. Air was very turbulent and nobody was flying into Will Rogers World Airport due to the conditions. These were not planes.
