Three junior high students see three glowing orbs, appearing sequentially two dropped below horizon, the third hovered before disappearing behind hill

This form would not let me enter the year of this event which was 1965. In the fall of 1965 I left my house to catch the bus to go to school. I'd been listening to the radio and heard that a comet was going to be visible in the morning sky that day. When I stepped outside I looked straight up and sure enough there was the distinctive tail way up over my head. I went across the street to my friend John's house and told him about the comet. When we came back onto the street, our neighbor, Sherridee, was walking to the bus stop and we joined her and shared the comet story. After just a few minutes we all saw to our left, a fast moving bright light traveling parallel with the horizon then quickly dropped out of sight. It glowed like the sun and looked about the size of a nickel held at arms length. I noticed no noise or any other sensory impact. That object was immediately followed by another one that looked the same did the same thing. We all saw these thing and were walking and talking about them when we saw to our left another of these shining objects. This one was hovering above a small hill about a mile or so away. It remained there in place for perhaps a minute while we talked, the slowly dropped out of sight behind the hill. We continued to the bus stop and never discussed it amongst ourselves again. This event though changed my life. The world became a much more complicated place.
