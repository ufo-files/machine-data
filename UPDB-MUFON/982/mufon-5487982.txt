We were leaving a function just before midnight on 10/17/2015, in Springboro, Ohio and in the western sky, just above the trees, were 6-8 bright lights hovering. At first it looked like a couple helicopters just hovering, but then we noticed they were moving erratically, but in a cluster. They were moving, but still not gaining or losing altitude. They then turned orange/red, then disappeared. No smoke, they weren't Chinese lanterns, as they didn't go up. Several others saw it, and there is a video on Facebook, but nobody knows what it was? I thought possibly parachute flares, but there was no smoke and no noise and they just disappeared. I was with my brother and my 15 year old son and 16 year old daughter who also saw it. It lasted about 5 minutes. Probably a explanation of same, but nobody knows yet.
