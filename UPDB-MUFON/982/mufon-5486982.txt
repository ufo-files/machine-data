{rjl} A large, bright, green ball of light that fell quickly. Like a falling star.

I was driving home from a meeting down Fallen Timber Road in Elizabeth, Pennsylvania when I noticed this bright green light in my view. It fell from the sky, but I lost sight of it behind a house. It looked like a large star, but shined like a "glow stick green" in color. It fell what seemed to be straight down out of the sky and not like a shooting star shoots across the sky. It did not leave any type of trail in the sky. I was unsure of what it could be and was confused and anxious, but completely aware. I immediately came home and looked on the computer to try and find out what it could be.
