{jpg} sitting on the bus on my way to work, i observed the craft hovering, stationary in the distance

I was riding the bus on my way to work, i was looking through the side window to watch the sky and i first noticed the craft, silver and shiny, reflecting the sunlight. it was hovering, stationary in the sky a mile or so away. I can only describe it as spoon-shaped, like a spoon i was looking at with the protruding bowl shape facing me, with a long skinny handle shaped part sticking out to the right. I observed for at least 20 seconds before the bus turned and i lost sight, at least five seconds had passed by the time I was able to look at the same spot again, and by then it had disappeared.
