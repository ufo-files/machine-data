Large, high flying disc shaped aircraft flew north-east over me towards the ocean. One flame-like light in the middle of the bottom surface. Moving very fast and steadily in a straight line.

I was at a friends house in Brighton sitting in the backyard, I saw a very bright fire coloured light pretty far off in the distance. I had no idea what it was because of how strong the light was, it didn't cast light onto the ground but was powerful enough for me to easily see it from a distance. It went straight over us in a straight line , very quickly. As it went over us towards the beach, i saw a very distinct outline of a disc shape illuminated on the reflective bottom by the one bright light in the centre of the bottom.

The light flickered slightly, like a flame would but at the speed it was going i would imagine a flame would be trailing behind it. It was visibly going very fast even from far away. I was so excited i immediately grabbed my phone to picture it before it completely went out of view, unfortunately my phones camera wasn't focusing because of how small the light was by that point. After getting a couple shots it went out of view behind the house while flying over the beach.
