{cak} Appeared star-like

I witnessed what appeared to be a star like object making deliberate moves and sharp 90 degree direction changes. This object was observed during the Leonid meteor shower, but I can assure that this was no meteor. We watched the object jet across the night sky in back and forth up and down maneuvers for nearly 20 minutes before going back inside.
