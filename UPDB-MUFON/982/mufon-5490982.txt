{rjl} Orb that looked similar to a disco ball that changed directions

While sitting outside talking on the phone I noticed an usual object flying above the tree line. It looked similar to a disco ball starting out traveling West eventually it dropped below the tree line. Shortly thereafter it came back into heading back the way it came from. Eventually it was out of sight. I attempted to get photographs but only one came out. Randomly (maybe) one of the pictures came back black but had an orange orb looking item in the picture.  If you zoom into the star looking thing in the upper right hand corner of the photo named Castile sighting you will be able to see it's mysterious shape.
