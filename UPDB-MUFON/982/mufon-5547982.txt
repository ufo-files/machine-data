Bright Front White Light One small red light on side surrounded by several bright white lights

I was driving home on HWY411 with my daughters from the movies an I noticed a hugh bright white light. I asked both my daughters do you see that? What is that? The more I drove the closer I got to the light. I then saw that it was about 600feet or so in the air an it wasn't moving. It was just hovering there. I then rolled my window down an there was no sound. As I drove under it I then could see one smaller red light on the side surrounded by several very bright smaller white lights. The object than went away as quickly as it came. I was close enough to really see it for around a minute but could see it from a distance for atleast 5minutes or so. Sorry to say I didn't get any pictures. It was dark an I was on the HWY so wasn't safe.I felt excited when I got close enough to see. I have never seen anything quiet like this an don't know if I will again. After I was home for around 20minutes or so I got in my car an went back on the HWY to see if I could see it again, but of course I didn't. I even took the camara this time. Needless to say I will from here on out pack the camara in the car!!!!
