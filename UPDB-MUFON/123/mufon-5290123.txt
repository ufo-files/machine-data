Bright white light over Honolulu

There was a bright white light flying from the southwest direction of the ocean. The object hovered over downtown Honolulu for several minutes. Video was taken of it hovering over downtown. The object appeared circular with bright intense white light surrounding it. No wings were present and the object made no sound. My wife and I watched it hover over Punchbowl and then it flew northeast over the Mount Tantalus area.
