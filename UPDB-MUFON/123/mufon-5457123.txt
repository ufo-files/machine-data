{cak} abnormal shape object passing over the country

I was with my wife just getting out of a shop, to enter in our car, when my eye was attracted by a glowing star, the night was clear in that direction, and the light was strange : a bright point projecting two large beams, like a car in the distance , those beams seem to encounter a particularly foggy atmosphere, so the beam disappear/is stopped at a certain distance. 
The object is flying at a speed that can confound with a jet liner, at middle altitude. As We are close to the Geneva Airport ( 30 miles) , I first thought it could be a plane just airborne ( I use to fly from this airport, and the path when flying to the south is almost the same ) or circling ...
But the lights were really weird , so I told my wife to look and I took y smartphone ( Samsung galaxy S8) to take a video . I could film it for about 35 seconds, and then it reached a cloud barrier, I could see the lights glowing through the clouds, for a few seconds and then it was no more viewable . 
I forgot it , until midnight when a friend text me that a news paper : le progres ( from the city of lyon) published that some people reported the same observation over Lyon and Grenoble, 
I was surprised , because , The object seemed to be at least one mile over my place , and as it was glowing through the clouds, that confirmed me , it was flying at a quite low altitude . The other sightings being at the same moment and 70 miles from my place . The News paper added that satellites were just launched an hour before , but it does not seem to be a satellite
