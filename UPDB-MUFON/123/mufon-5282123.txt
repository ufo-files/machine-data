HUMAN MADE FLOAT PLANE HOVERING IN PLACE ABOVE THE TREETOPS

I was driving South on HWY 19 (just passed by the Lantzville Fire Department) at 10:20am when I noticed a float plane very close to the treeline on the lefthand side (nearest the water). As I got closer I was able to identify that the aircraft was HOVERING, COMPLETELY STATIONARY, about 10-20 meters (at the most), above the treeline. These trees are right up alongside the highway i was driving on, so the plane was literally 'right next to me'. It was nowhere near the water, and it was right in between two of the tallest trees, hovering in place. The propellers did not appear to be on, and no sound was coming out. The airplane was close enough to the ground that if I was able to get a longer look, I would probably have been able to see the person in the cockpit. It was definitely not moving at all and it was definitely NOT a helicopter. It was 100% a float plane. I almost drove off the road after seeing what I saw, so I was in a bit of shock, kept driving for about 20 seconds, and was able to turn around at the next intersection. I drove back the way I came, but there was nothing in the air anymore. I am hopeful someone else witnessed this event also, sees this report, and can confirm with me what I saw.
