MY FIRST IMPRESSION OF IT GOING EAST TO WEST WAS A SATELLITE BUT WHEN IT FADED AND STARTED COMING BACK, WEST TO EAST AND DID THE CRAZY LIGHT DEMONSTRATION,(THAT I HAVE HEARD ABOUT) I KNEW IT WAS THE REAL THING, (A UFO).!!!
