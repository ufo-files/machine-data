{jpg} Saucer directly above me and friend,saw belly of craft,flashed multi-colored lights in random then in sequence before leaving at very high rate of speed

Taking a walk with friend around block,just started to get dark out,noticed low thin clouds were still pretty white and moving rather fast from wind then saucer shaped craft was directly above us about 100-150' ,we remained silent with my arms to my side looking straight up at the crafts belly,it began to display multi-colored lights in a random order,I remember thinking that it looked "sloppy"or out of order then it put on a all out light show in sequences this time,outer perimeter lights moving to center then middle and back out to perimeter again,all sorts of beautiful sequences,I felt that it was showing off,it did a slight little wobble after light show stopped then took off in a straight smooth line to the east toward Hayward hills at a amazing speed,if I would have blinked I may have thought it just disappeared very incredibly fast,only after it was gone I looked to my left at my friend and only said.."that was a UFO huh."..and my friend just replied.."yes" and we continued walking around the block and met up with my friends older brother and his friends outside in front of there house and I asked them if they seen the UFO that flew over there way and they said yes but soon realized that they were joking with us,for some reason the friend that saw the craft with me was not as impressed by the event and I became uncomfortable even bringing it up to him as he would just say.."man Mike you're still talking about that?"..anyhow he does at least agnolig the event,me on the other hand cant stop thinking about it even after 30 years.
