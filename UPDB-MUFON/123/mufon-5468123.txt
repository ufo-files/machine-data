I was leaving the house at around 9 in the morning on june 4 2018. I live in a somewhat rural area. I just happened to look up, bright blue sky not a cloud in the sky. Noticed a white not shiny.just kind of an egg shell color. It had no wings,made no noise. It really did look like a tic tac. It seemed higher than commercial aircraft fly and it was moving fast. If I took my eyes off of it for a second and looked back up. Where you would expect to see normal aircraft. This thing was already hard to find again. I've seen b1 and b2s and different jets. Grissom arb is a 15 minute drive from here. I don't know what this was.
