Sighting contains 2 events.

Event 1: I observed what I believed to be two satellites (reddish-orange orbs looking like a typical satellite reflecting light) transiting on a similar SSW/NNE track. They were moving in a straight line sequentially and not gapped by much. I verified them through binoculars. I traced them both several times individually and together with a green laser visible at 20 miles causing the rear object to move laterally into a another trajectory and then up to beside the first object before it moved ahead of it and off. It continued to subtly move around within that line before they eventually moving out of sight. The first object stayed on its original flight path the entire time.

Event 2: Roughly 20 minutes later, I walked back onto the back patio and caught sight of 3 similarly colored objects directly overhead at 12 o'clock in the sky. 

Initially, I believed them to be lights on a fixed wing aircraft that would appear as described below, but through binoculars, there was no visible body to which the lights were affixed and yet approximately 20k ft. above and behind them was a jetliner visible with the naked eye and through binoculars:

- Reddish-orange, 3 of them (not moving relative to the others; moving in unison on a single flight path)

- Not blinking, entirely solid

- Not accompanied by any other lights blinking or otherwise.

- No visible body of an aircraft through naked eye or binoculars

Estimated altitude was around 10,000-15,000 ft. and there was another passenger jet above and south of it, southbound in the opposite direction. I heard that jet and verified it clearly with the naked eye and through binoculars. I would approximate its altitude at 35k feet. That jet was the only noise I could hear.

The accompanying video is a recreation that resembles what I saw but is not a perfect representation.
