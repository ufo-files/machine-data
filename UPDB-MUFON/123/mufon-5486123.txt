{nd} Classic dome shaped dark ufo pics and video describe it best.

I was at work time was close to 9 pm est. I had walked out back to smoke on a quick break. 
I was out side for only a few moments and noticed a formation of 2 planes, or jets, could of been helicopter flying in formation very close to one another. I thought to my self that was odd wonder what they are up to do to the speed of travel and tight formation. Their elevation was high I thought maybe if I took out my phone I could zoom in on my camera and maybe see a little better. As I took my phone out and pointed in their direction I happened to notice something dark over a building in the direction the jets/planes path was. I snapped a few photos and was amazed to see something in the sky over the building in the photo. I don't know what the object is. And at first thought is was a spec on my lens. I aimed the camera again zoomed in and could see the object again!!!!!! I couldn't believe what I was seeing and it was moving, almost dancing in the sky. So I snapped a few more pic. Thought to myself no one is ever going to believe this so I hurried up and switched to video. Began to record the object and was amazed to see it move in a erratic way. The object almost seem to disappear for a moment. Then after about 15 sec of recording in a flash of light shot off and up. I was amazed at what I saw and began shaking. I wasn't not shaking from the temp outside being 30 or so degrees outside I was in awe of what I had just witnessed.
