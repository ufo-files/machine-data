On way home from work witnessed hovering orb.

At approx 8:20pm myself and a coworker I was bringing home noticed an Orb in the sky. Thought at first it was a blimp or advertisement balloon for a car dealership. As we got closer we saw that it had three "legs" extended outward as if they were landing gear of some sort. We pulled into a parking lot and I noticed many other people standing around staring at it. My co worker entered the store and I began speaking with a woman that was also witnessing this "Orb". As I spoke with her she said, "Oh my god". I looked back and it was gone! She said it vanished. It didn't go up, down, east, west... it just vanished. It looked like a very large, small building size, pinball in the sky. All metallic reflecting the sky and sun.
