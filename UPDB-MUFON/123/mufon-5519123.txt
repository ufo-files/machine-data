Approximately 9-10 lights hovered briefly then flew off, very quickly, due east.

I was sitting in my living room watching television when I glanced out the window and saw approximately 7-9 very bright, very distinct reddish orange lights in a, roughly, round formation. I looked twice to make sure that it wasnt a reflection from lights inside the house. From the brief look I got through the window it appeared to be hovering, but I cannot be sure of this as I only looked for a few seconds. Though if it was moving, it was, at this point, moving very slowly. From the look I got through the window, Id approximate that it was less than a few hundred feet away and relatively low in the sky (if I had to guess Id say maybe 1,000-3,000 feet). I got up from my chair and went to the backdoor of the house just a few feet away. By the time I had opened the door it was just a single, extremely bright point of reddish pink light speeding due east and possibly gaining in altitude (though not much). It was just after 8pm and the sky was almost completely dark. There was one star visible just to the right of the object that was considerably dimmer. This did not look like a planet or star, it was far brighter. At no point did the object(s) make a sound. I never saw any of the typical white, red or green strobe lights you normally see on standard aircraft. The lights never pulsated at all, they were constant. I also never actually saw the shape/outline of a craft, only lights. The total sighting lasted about 30-40 seconds. It was really strange, and unlike anything Ive ever seen before.
