Driving home for the week end from work.

I was driving east on 166 HWY only a few mile from my home in Chetopa Ks, when a flash of light caught my attention to the north, I looked up into the sky and saw a large "Wedge or V" shaped craft very high, moving very fast to the north. At first I thought it was just a really big airplane, then I noticed that it did not leave any contrail or other exhaust behind it. I pulled over to the side of the road and watched,two or three times I noticed the sun light would really gleam of it, no more than a total of 4 or 5 minutes, the craft moved into a large cloud bank where I lost sight of it.
