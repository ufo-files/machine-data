Outside dwelling talking on cell phone, clear winter evening outside city with no light artifacts or interferences. Looked upwards, largest object in sky self illuminating. Additional plasma tubes were photographed in same area, almost nightly for two years. Following, on two occasions as I was sleeping felt a vacuum from under my body sucking me into the bed. Decided to take photographs inside my dwelling, late one evening, following the sightings and felt something blowing on my ear. I remained stationary and turned camera 180 degrees and captured what appears to be a goat/horse...very unsettling. Still experiencing physical contact weekly and photographs showing these plasma-like beaming lights going into and through solid objects. I am physically exhausted and the same eye the light came out of, I'm losing my eyesight.
