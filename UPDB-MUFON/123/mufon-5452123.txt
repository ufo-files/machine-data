Two craft hovering over house then took off in the blink of an eye leaving light trails

At this time I lived with my mom and was laying down in the bed which was up against a window. I could hear my mom washing dishes. While she was washing the dishes I could hear her say to her self what in the hell is that. I then heard her run through the house and open the front door at which time she screamed my name. I opened my eyes, jumped up and noticed lights coming through my window. We lived in a very rural place so my first thought was someone was in my yard so I grabbed my shot gun and ran to the front door. Upon arriving at the door and outside where my mom was standing. I noticed the lights were gone and my mom looked pale and kept saying they were making no noise. After about 5 mins of calming my mom down she told me that there were lights she noticed through the window so she ran to the door to get a better look. She said when she stepped outside there were two craft hovering over the house making no noise and had lights circling the bottom of them. Also they were so close she said she could see the metal of the craft. At this time is when she yells my name and I to could see the lights coming through my window. Then in the blink of and eye they took off heading north East at tree level leaving streaks of light behind them.
