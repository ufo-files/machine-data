{nd} Tandem lights streaked across the sky over Austin, Texas moving incredibly fast and without making a sound.

My girlfriend and I were standing on the top floor of an 8-story parking garage looking out over the Congress Street bridge in downtown Austin, Texas. At approximately 9 p.m. we saw two white lights moving at incredible speed from east to west just over downtown. At first I believed it was a low-flying military jet, but there was no sound and the two lights separated as they passed overhead. There was absolutely no sound, no blinking lights and they passed from horizon to horizon (as much as I could see) within a matter of seconds. The only possible explanation would be that they were tandem asteroids, but the flight path of these seem deliberate.Plus, asteroids close enough to fire up (creating the light) from the friction of entering Earth's atmosphere would most likely be heard.
