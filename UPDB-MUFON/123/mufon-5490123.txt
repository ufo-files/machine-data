{rjl} Saw with binoculars a starlike object moving from NW to NE direction with no visible aircraft lights change color & go vertical out of sight.

On the evening of Aug. 14. 2015, I was on my 2nd story rear deck. Watching the skies for the Perseid meteor showers. At around 10:00pm, I saw a bright object traveling from my NW direction to my NE direction. I saw no visible aircraft lights so I grabbed my binoculars & focused on the moving object. Still I saw no visible blinking lights, just a bright white light. As it got to my North direction, it seemed to change color to an orange/reddish color, the it shot up vertically in a NNE direction. I stayed focused on it but it was gone, faded from sight in about 1 to 1.5 seconds. It was incredible how fast it accelerated vertically. I immediately went inside to tell my wife who was watching tv what I had witnessed. It was way better than any of the meteor streaks I witnessed in the few nights I was out watching the sky. I felt I had to make an official report of that sighting.
