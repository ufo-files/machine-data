Satellite like bright yellow light in sky with red dot in center. Descended elevation and light dimmed. Then disappeared.

At approx. 7:45 PM on Trices lake, the sky was clear with almost a full moon. There was no noise at all. We saw a bright yellow light with a small red light in the center. It was above the tree line as we looked across the lake to the Southwest. The light was very bright and reflected strongly on the water. It was stationary as we stood on the pier facing it. We believed it was just a strange looking satellite. Watching the light and the sky for approximately 10 minutes, we noticed the light dimming to a point where the light was no longer reflecting on the water. Then, the light began to very slowly lower, turning a blueish color, below the tree line. We knew at this point that the object was not a satellite. The light completely disappeared below the tree line. Within a minute, the light rose above the tree line again with an orange light, but its location was slightly to the right of its previous position. It was still in a dimmer state and did not reflect on the water. It stayed above the trees for a couple minutes and then lowered again. It rose one more time, but this time the light was a dim blue color. It sank below the tree line again and disappeared from our site for good. Airplanes were flying in the distance with their blinking red and yellow lights. The object in question did not present any blinking lights.
