Just returning home from work at JFK airport when an unusual light caught my eye gliding at low altitude over Great South Bay the size n speed puzzled me. The object was heading west but as soon as I used binoculars it began to come towards me. I became slightly nervous and even more curious.Now the light appeared to be a pulsating orb orange yellowish and getting bigger!As my heart rate increased I actually became unnerved and wondered what is it and why is it coming at me.The speed was maybe 45mph? I was dumbfounded"what the hell is this?"I ran outside to my front yard and hovering silently overhead was a black triangle the size of Yankee stadium with 3 bright beams of light emitting from each corner coming together as one right at me!SILENT! The outer rim of craft had rectangular lights blinking on and off like a wheel within a wheel-Ezekiel's craft?mesmerized and frozen in place I watched with and without binoculars and observed a dark grey/black metallic surface with no noticeable plates or rivets. I believe there were silhouetted figures behind window/porthole structures. Childlike/kin?Then after hovering silently the craft ascended slowly at first but then accelerated to supersonic speeds,no sonic boom contrail or sounds at all and headed NW. I believe to have some missing time,felt like 8 mins but watch said over 20. The visions and nightmares have been a constant part of my life since. Even moved to California although I feel it was overall a positive experience I'm unable to live my life as I had previously.
