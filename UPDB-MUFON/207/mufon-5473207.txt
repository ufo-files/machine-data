{dr}Looked like colored sparklers on a styrophrome ball first one passes then two, one on top of the other

Falcon-ridge apartments witness saw a circular object described as a Styrofoam ball with sparklers of red, yellow, green, and blue attached all around it fly directly overhead from the NW to SE going towards Bush intercontinental airport. It was about the size of a large plane and was about 500-1000 ft in the air. It moved by silently and somewhat slowly as if it were a plane. Then two more of same description showed up coming from the same direction but they were very close together as if one was "piggy-backing" on the other. No report of sound, or anything else noticeable. Witness has never seen a UFO before but believes in them. She was not drinking or appeared to be under the influence of anything. she sounded rational and trustworthy. But she did not want her identity to be known.
