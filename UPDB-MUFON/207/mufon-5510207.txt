Photo Anomaly

I was around Horicon, WI back in 2011 taking photos of a Jesus Cross at a cemetery, and really didn't notice anything out of the ordinary. I took my photos, and finished the photo shoot. I was using a Nikon D80 and a 18-105mm VR lens.  I will have to find the original photos. This is a processed photo in color only, and some sharpening.  The EXIF info on the photo of the exact time and date is:

Upper Right Corner of photograph-- Triangle- at altitude below clouds.

May 21, 2011 6:13:21Pm 

Nikon D80/Nikon 18-105mm VR kit lens.
f/6.3 @ 1/200 sec. 

Photo was taken at 18mm. 

I am trying to locate the exact location of the shoot. I was mostly out driving around looking for interesting things to shoot. It was in the Horicon, WI area. 

To answer the questions:

1. Amateur Photoshoot
2. I didn't notice the "triangle" until post processing
3. It appears to me as a triangle shape. I say that because I know what they look like because I observed one close to the ground in upstate NY back in 1993. Big. No Sound. Hovered. 

4. did not notice the object while shooting the cross.

5. I saw it only in post processing. It is not dust on the sensor. Dust on the sensor appears as out of focus particles. This is not out of focus. 
I processed this photo using Adobe Lightroom 4. I have also tried observing the object in color and B&W. 

6. N/A -- Only saw it in post processing the photo.

This photo is possibly a bird, with wings tucked or something else. I don't know, but I just thought it was an interesting photograph. When you examine the object in the upper right corner there is another dot object trailing behind it and below. Birds usually travel in pairs, so that may be what it is or not. In high magnifications you will also note an Aura around the object. The second smaller round object has the aura too, but that might be an effect of the lens on the objects or maybe a heat or gravitational signature.  I only notice it on a 3:1 and higher magnification. I also removed my copyright, as I do not want any attention to me personally from the photograph.  

Enjoy.
