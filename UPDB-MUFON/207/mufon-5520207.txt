2 lights moving in the sky and then hovering, blue, orange, yellow, red lights

A friend of mine called and said she was coming by to pick me up as she and her family had been watching two objects in the sky for the last hour and she wanted to follow the objects; We drove down a State Rd and could never catch up with the 2 objects, they seemed to stay equal distance from us as we continued to drive; we pulled over on a side road and stopped and got out of vehicle and then the 2 objects seemed to hover over the same area but at a distance from us; I video taped the event with my phone and at this time is when we realized the objects had different color of lights. One of the objects was smaller than the other. The two objects always stayed fairly close to one another.
