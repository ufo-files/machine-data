First saw it SE looked like 3 lights together then it zoomed W to hover 5 degrees higher at a point in the southern sky.

I just woke up like I normally do on any given day around 4:40am. My window is at the foot of my bed and I have a good view of the stars/sky. I came back from the bathroom and looked out the window to see the stars. To my left (SE sky) I saw what looked like 3 lights together and thought it was a helicopter until it zoomed to my right and then up a bit to hover there in the southern sky. It was all one light by this time of various blues and predominately bright white. Every now and again the blue fuzzy portion would extend a little bit and then merge with the bright white light. There was no sound. Then it just winked out and was gone. I am very curious when I see this because this isn't my first sighting. I did try to film it but the sky was too dark for me to pick up that one light. It's just a pocket digital camera.
