3 bright lights over Portsmouth RI

I left my girl friends house aroun 11:00 Sunday Night 3/28/2011. I was heading south on main rd in tiverton ri, as I came ovever the top of the last hill before I get onto the highway, I could see 3 bright lights stationary over my home town, Portsmouth ri. Its a very clear night with unlimited visability. I quickly pulled into the parking lot of Little Bear lounge, I stopped and got out, I took My cell phone and i took a couple of shots of them. I observed the for about 10 minutes before the smaller of the 3 blinked out and was gone, the second one then started to move to the east before I lost it. The third and final one remained for a while longer before it moved away, as i pulled into my driveway I noticed the 3 rd one moving north over the Mount hope bridge, but it was no longer a bright white light, it appeared to have 3 or 4 red lights and the center one was blinking, then I lost sight of it.
