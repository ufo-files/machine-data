Slow moving bright white/orange object with red flashing lights

Saturday Jan. 8, 2022 at 9:10 p.m. This transpired over about 3 - 4 minutes. 
It was a clear night sky and I was letting the dog out. Standing on my porch looking south I saw this unusually bright light appear over my neighbors roof line off in the distance towards the airport. We live about 10 miles north of the Harrisburg International Airport, so we see lots of aircraft, civilian and military and have even seen a few civilian drones flying around. First I thought it was an airplane or helicopter, but the light was very bright whiteish/orange with some flashes of red lights what seemed to be towards the center of the object. The object was heading north in my general direction. As it was west of my direction about 40 - 45 degrees I could still see the same bright white/orange and flashing red from the side. At this point I estimate the object was about maybe 3,000 - 5,000 feet in elevation. The moon was out and in the first quarter and was in the western part of the sky. This object moved slowly, slower than any prop airplane or helicopter and there were no noises as it proceeded in a northerly direction. As the object was moving away from me (north) I could see the same bright white/orange red flashing lights from the back. Those lights appear to be eminating from all around the object. I didn’t want to take my eyes off the object, but decided to go to the door and yell to my wife to come out here now. She came out and saw the object which was north west of us still heading in a northerly direction. We watched it head over the blue mountains until it was out of sight.
