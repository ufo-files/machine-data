{cak} Back yard RING camera captures a luminous floating object

Our RING camera caught this object hovering in our back yard. RING camera infrared was able to see this luminous object but did not trigger the motion sensor. What is interesting about this video is the object fly's past the camera at a fast pace but it's no longer a straight line object, it appears square in the final video segment. The square image looks like a series of lines very well with organized shapes and alignment in relation to each other. This last image towards the end of the video is the most interesting part of this observation.
