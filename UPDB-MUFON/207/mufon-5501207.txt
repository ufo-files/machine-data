12-15 UFOs near Paris (Boulogne-Billancourt) 06/14/2014

This light has appeared strange to me because it was not blinking, it had a yellow-orange color and there was no noise given the speed at which it was moving. This caught my attention. The light disappeared up in the air and speeding.
I then scanned with a great attention the popular night sky in search of other UFOs. About 30 seconds later I saw 3 small orange dots appear that came from very far on my right (north) hovering about 8-10 miles away and between 500 m and 900 m in height. They went into the southbound then they accelerated soaring into the sky (40 1) to the southwest (above the Parc de St-Cloud). 3 lights flying together in formation and at the same speed. The training was then separated and the UFO took slightly different directions from each other by speeding up and disappeared in 2/3 seconds in the atmosphere.
After the passage of the first UFO, then I woke up my wife to be also witnessed the event.
Three Other sets of 2/3 UFO were then reproduced with a similar scenario.
I detected no noise, no breath, no reaction. No flickering, flashing lights, trail of condensation of water vapor (contrail) or otherwise.
