Flashing star

On July 13,09 went out to take new puppy outside. saw flashing star in the sky. Went in and put glasses on and watched star flashing. the bottom appeared to flash back on forth on the bottom. watched a few more minutes not believing what I saw. was telling spouse through door that a strange light was out there asking him to see. Went in over to couch to really talk spouse into coming. He was sleepy and did not come. went back out and light had vanished. Started crying as I was scared and knew nobody was going to believe me any how as I always talk about "aliens".
