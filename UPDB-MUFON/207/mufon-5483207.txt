See this URL for videos taken during the event by another party approximately 20 mile west of my location. [Link moved to FI Section/cms/tg]. Many other versions of this video on line.

My statement follows(I was unaware that anyone else had seen what I observed until the news story came out that you see in the video referenced above):

The object I saw was moving. It appeared to come out of the Wright-Pat area. It traveled northeast (turning slowly toward my place),s weeping path to the south, then west, turned around and proceeded to travel south on it's earlier flight path. As it was going west, then around to the south, I noticed a tiny red light stationary for a few seconds, then the red dot moved west, circled around & headed south toward the main object; then both were gone. I know the red object was not a cell tower as there was no blinking and the object was above a line of trees in the south. I have looked several times at the area I saw the initial object & the red object (over the trees) and did not see the red light since. The initial object appeared to be in the shape of an elongated triangle that was slightly tilted to my right. Red light on left edge, blue light on the right edge (slightly lower than the red light), and a green light at the bottom corner. I remember seeing a very light, white/gray vertical coloring on the middle of the object which changed color to red, orange/yellow, green, blue, light to dark violet. Just like a prism. There was no significant noise other than ambient noise from far away traffic. 

Went to the Sheriff's Dept. this morning. Spoke with a young Deputy & he hadn't heard of any "unusual" lights last Wed., but he did say that he has seen a couple of articles in the paper.  I re-read my experience & added a few things that I had forgotten; object stopping then rising upward a bit. If you want to pas this info on, go ahead and do so. I don't know where to start; re: making a report.
