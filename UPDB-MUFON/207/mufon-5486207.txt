{dr}Glowing orbs looked like fire then flew out of Forest up and over mountain dimming after rise above mountain

We saw them and they looked like fires at first glance but then they flew up at high speeds staying close to the forest. Flew up and over mountain top and off in to the sky one set was 3 in a Chevron formation becoming straight line formation heading to the east. Once they were clear of the mountain they became dimm very quickly. Object was glowing fireball orange with some red flares. Feelings were quick to rationalize as fireworks but lasted for minutes. Most were travel in a straight line but a few took quick turns then straight lines. Digital camera would not pick up the light. Distortion on camera when I zoomed. 2130 to 2140 mountain time.
