{td} Two bright Orbs randomly maneuvering then disappearing.

On 3/28/2018 as I was sitting in my van I saw two UFO Orbs over central Florida. As I first observed them as bright stars and they were randomly maneuvering in the sky for approximately 15 seconds and instantly disappeared. I reached for my phone and started to video the area in the sky that they appeared in hoping that they would re-appear. But I did not visually see them again as they vanished.

On 04/08/2018 I had some free time and I transferred it to my laptop and replayed the video I shot that day. I could not believe my eyes, a closer look showed me one of the Orbs faintly which I was unable to see when I was recording that area in the sky. I am posting the video and along with pictures highlighting one Orb. Viewing it on a larger screen will make it more visible.
