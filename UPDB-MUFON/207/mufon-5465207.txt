{rjl} Appears to the bottom right of moon in picture. Too bright for star or planet

I was at Appache Gold Casino in Globe Arizona. I was walking through the parking lot. I first noticed the object when I saw the moon was still real bright. I thought the object looked like a star or planet but I knew it was much too close to be either. The object did not move. I only watched it for a few minutes then walked in the casino. After I came back outside a few hours later it was gone. I took several picturea of the object and one of the pictures includes both the moon and the object within the frame.
