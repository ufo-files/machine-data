String of lights across the sky

I was standing on my deck to look at the stars at approximately 10:15 pm. The deck faces north. They was a string of lights, in the sky. They were moving west to north east, from the Southern Black Hills to the area over the Bad Lands The lights appeared like a moving string of clear Christmas lights. There was no noise. No flashing lights as with aircraft. Lights were not moving fast, airplane speed. When I walked onto the deck, the lights were already moving across the sky. I do not know for how long. I witnessed them for about 3-4 minutes. Holding my fingers in the air,, distance between lights was about 1.5". There was a definite last light. It was like a convoy of lights across the sky. The lights were all equally distance, and there were hundreds of them.
