Double black box on its axis connected by thick cable with cable extending from top box and bottom box each rotating opposite each other..

I was getting fuel when I looked into the sky at about 70 degrees mostly blue sky and then viewed the UAP. I watched the unit then decided to take a video. After about 8 minutes of watching and taking the video, I decided to get other witnesses, and then the UAP flew off as I got someone's attention. I believe the unit was about 2-3000' small but I could make out the detail. The UAP shape was of a square but on its axis, there were two units connected by a very thick cable. The two boxes rotated, the top rotated to the left and the bottom unit to the right. The UAP was stationary but wobbled, it reminded me of seaweed undulating under the water. The two units would converge together and flex out over and over all while rotating 360 degrees. The thick cable extended evenly from the top unit and the bottom with a slight curve. The top unit cable curved to the left and the bottom unit cable curved to the right. The black was non-reflective with the exception of one side, the reflection was not bright but subdued mostly like a dull metal, not a mirror. As soon as I got more witnesses to look up the unit disappeared.
