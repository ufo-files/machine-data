Bright orb that became egg shaped with windows across front with beings

Thought it was the moon and thinking it was extremely large and bright. Watched it for some time then realized it was getting larger and closer. Was fascinated and kept watching to see what it was. Thought it might be a helicopter but there was no sound. Went back inside apartment to find camcorder but the battery needed charging. Went back outside on my back patio and realized I could make out something other than a bright orb. It had windows across the front and appeared somewhat oval and egg shaped. It had a small projection on bottom towards the back . It was smooth and blended into the orb. Was not jutting out but appeared like a bulge on a hose. I was so fascinated by the shape and the fact it made no noise that when I looked up to the windows I could see beings looking down at me. That freaked me out and I went back inside and watched it move on up through the English Woods valley until gone. Around 15 minutes later the sky was filled with small planes and a helicopter for some time. The next day I called to see if there were any blimps or dirigibles in the air the night before but was informed there were none. Never talked to anyone about it because I figured they would think I had too much to drink. I don’t drink and never did drugs. I am 72 years old and was around 8 when I saw my first one through a telescope. No one believed me then.
