Glowing circular/disc spinning. Bright white light and it got brighter randomly.

I was heading home from a friends house early in the morning. What initially caught my eye was an airplane. Then I saw a bright spinning object brightly sitting in the sky. It appeared as if they were going to collide. The plane definitely saw it and circled around twice. I pulled over after they almost collided and took a video and a picture. I took a few videos that are all time and location stamped. I saw this object for 40+ minutes.
