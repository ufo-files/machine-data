{cak} Black triangle

A *huge* black, obtuse triangle that was only visible because it covered the stars in the night sky. It came from the South and was moving silently but quickly North. The tip which you would expect to be pointing North was pointing East towards the sea.
The sighting was witnessed by at least 12 people and static electricity was so abundant after the sighting that our hair stood on end and we shocked each other when we touched.
