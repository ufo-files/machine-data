{rjl} Fast moving object cover skyline in less then 2 minutes.

I went outside to smoke a cigarette. Facing to the east I can observe flight paths going into Logan International Airport (Boston, MA). I noticed in the corner of my eye at about a 55 degree angle a red light come into view. Attitude was quite high. The object then covered the entire skyline probably over 150 miles of skyline in under 1 min and 30 sec. I lost sight of it as it passed behind my neighbors house. There was no sound along with the light and the light was steady. As soon as a saw it I knew it was not an aircraft. The light never changed no mater angle the object was at. I am also confident that it was not a satellite because I never been able to see them because of the city lights off in the distance.
