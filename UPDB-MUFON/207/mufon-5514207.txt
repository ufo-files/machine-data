One light ascended, split into two lights, then both went into different directions and faded away.

Walking the dog as usual, I notice a strange light on the horizon. Usually no stars are visible along this area. At first I thought it was a helicopter or aeroplane, but I noticed something peculiar. It "split" into two lights in the sky and hovered next to each other momentarily. Then one starts going off to the left, then fades into the sky and the other object completely vanishes.
