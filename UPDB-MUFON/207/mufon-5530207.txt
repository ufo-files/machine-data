Omaha Air Show Stealth has two unknowns tailing.

I attended the Omaha Air Show on Offutt Air Force Base and used a Nikon Digital Camera to take pictures of the surprise appearance of a Stealth flown in not on the schedule. I didn't see the two unknowns until I downloaded the pictures from the camera to the computer. There is a third oddity on the right hand side of the picture and I had many of these dark blotches in different pictures always at roof line level or higher, some with other definitions indicating structure, possible legs, power source.
