{rjl} Orange Orbs - stationary, lit up and turned off in unusual pattern

We were on top deck of vacation house ocean front. (My wife and I had seen orange orbs in Charlotte NC on 9/16/2011 and I reported those to mufon as well). Just before lights appeared I said a silent prayer asking to see them again so that my step-son might see and believe. My belief that there is more angel than alien to them was confirmed when a minute after silent prayer three orange orbs lit up one at a time just above horizon over ocean. I immediately pointed and said "look!". My wife and step-son were present and saw them. It was night and they were parallel to the horizon. These lights faded and 5 more lights lit up one at a time perfectly perpendicular to the previous 3. They lit up starting at bottom to top with the last light slightly to the right of the vertical line of the other 4 lights. Best estimate of total height vertical lights covered comparable to a 20 / 30 story building but since it was over ocean could be as tall as a mile. In total the lights would create an upside down capital letter "T" with the one additional light at top to right. As quickly as they appeared they faded out in the same order. There was no movement. Just as the sighting that occurred in 2011 the enormous coincidence of talking about them and then the lights showing up have compelled me to report this sighting.
