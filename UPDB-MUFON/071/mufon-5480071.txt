{jpg} UFO seemed like a giant reflective piece of foil reflecting the eastern sun

I was driving south(west) on I-440 about 1 mile before the I40 interchange and 1 mile south of 67/167. There is marshland. The sun was coming up in the East on my left. I looked west to say something to my son who was sitting in the passenger seat. I saw something large reflecting the sun. I drive this way every morning to work. I have never seen anything looming over the marshland. It seemed about 1-2 miles away but im not sure. It was reflecting the morning sun. It looked like a giant square perpendicular to the ground. Not another aircraft. It just looked like a huge mirror or piece of copper or foil reflecting the sun sticking up out above the trees or the marshy area. I was driving about 70 miles an hour and passed it before I could determine what it really was.
