Tic tac like cylinder. Did not move along it’s length more it’s width moving forward. Slow and deliberate.

I was cleaning windows and saw it out of the corner of my eye. 
I observed it for a few seconds before filming it and noticed it’s shape was like a tic tac, no obvious tail section, it didn’t cross the sky along its length. It moved width ways forward. It was slow, silent and no con trail. 
I filmed it and several aircraft for comparison. I was pretty quick to get a film thinking no one will believe this. By comparison the aeroplanes you could see wings, that it was long and thin and left con trails where as this object didn’t fit with any of that, by comparison the regular planes moved quite quickly across the sky. 
I filmed it for 19seconds, and observed it for close to a minute before I lost sight of it.
