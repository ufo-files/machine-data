A triangle UFO flew low over my apartment complex at a slow speed then vanished after roughly 25 seconds

my friend and I were standing outside my apartment smoking a ciggerette when I saw an object out of the corner of my eye. At first I just assumed it was a plane because we have military and civilian aircraft fly over alot. We both looked up at the same time and saw a low flying object that appeared to be a distict triangle shape. It had three bright lights on each corner of the triangle, as well as a blinking red light in the middle of the triangle. As it went over us and into the distance, I could see more lights straight accross the back of the object which confirmed to me the triangle shape I was seeing. The object did not make alot of noise and its approach was almost completly silent. After about 20-25 seconds after we first saw the object, it vanished in an instant. amazingly enough, taking a picture was the last thing on my mind. I was so drawn in and amazed at what we were seeing. I have never seen anything like it before. My friend and I didn't even know what to say after it vanished besides, what the #$%& was that thing?
