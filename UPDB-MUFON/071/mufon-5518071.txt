Two witnesses observed a black triangular UFO with a hugh flourescent green light that changed into a cigar shape

Prior to the sighting I was awakened by the sound of a low flying helicopter ( both rotors could be heard and the noise reverberated through the bungalow).This is not that unusal as the Police have carried out searches and pursuits in the area useing this technique on several occassions.I listened to the sounds for a few minutes and as it did not subside I decided to see what I could on the way to the bathroom, I noted to time as 0315 hours.
I looked out from the rear lounge window it was a clear night with a few whispy Sirrus clouds and a bright sky lit by light pollution and moonlight.My attention was almost immediately drawn to a darkening of the sky to my left above the East wing of the bungalow, at this point I was aware that my wife had entered the room and was also looking out.Before I could make out what had appeared the object had travelled around 120 feet and carried out a right angle turn.The ufo now took a path accross the back garden parrallel with my viewpoint,I could now make out exactly what it was and being totaly amazed I said, "Wow what is that,what can you see?", my wife replied," A black triangle with a big green light".
It was a relief hearing this as I could hardly believe what I was seeing.
I would describe it as a black Isosceles triangle with a circular illuminated area underneath which represented about 50 per cent of the surface area,there was a small insignificant white light in the centre of the trailing edge.The object moved at a slow walking pace, 1-2 mph from left to right (East to West)at a distance of around 120 feet max and probably no more than 60 feet of the ground.There was no visible means of propulsion and appeared to be noiseless.The ufo was bigger than the flat of my hand at arms length and I would estimate it as beingaround 35 feet in lenth by 20 feet or more accross.All the time I was concious of the low flying helicopter which appeared to be right above the building and so out of sight.
I grabbed my camera from the lounge dresser and told my wife I was going outside to take photpgraphs, she said,"Don't go out it might beam you up", I realised she felt as I did that we were seeing an unworldy event.However I switched the camera on, nothing,the battery was dead, this is unusual as I had taken only one photo since the last charge.I cursed but grabbed my mobile phone/camera, the ufo was now moving out of sight behind the west wing roof so we ran into the bedroom,opened the curtains and the window to get a better view.My phone was taking an age to boot up, as we looked out the object changed shape before our eyes.I would now describe it as a blimp, my wife thought it more cigar shaped.This was hard to take in as logically this was impossible,I thought perhaps it was a side view and the ufo was banking,no lights were now visible and it continued to move out of sight,I ran into the kitchen at the front of the bungalow,I expected to see the object again or neighbours looking out,but nothing.
The sound of the helicopter was now fading,my wife said she had seen it fly off due South.
We felt we had witnessed something not of this world,that looked out of place with an aura, I aske myself why here it was almost like it was searching for something or wanted to be seen.
The following day my wife asked our neighbours if they had witnessed the events durring the night and although one had heard the helicopter and looked out the window he did not see anything,not surprising as they sleep in front of their property where as the sighting was over the rear gardens, my camera battery would not accept a recharge again.We fully expected the event to be reported in the media in the next few days, but no.Our neighbours thought we had been dreaming! I thought typical, people will think we are crazy.The events of that night continue to haunt us as we can't get an explaination.I have previously reported this sighting on the web at ufocasebook.com on 16/12/10,this did not provide any answers so we remain perplexed with more questions than answers.
