{nd} Glowing orange ball through sky then arcs and takes off.

My husband and I were sitting by our fire pit when I looked over to my right and saw something bright orange and round in the sky. I started over to look then started calling to him to open the fence so I could follow it. We jumped over the gate and saw what looked like a glowing orange ball floating across the sky. He went to get his phone and as he turned away it started to arc towards a star and within less than a minute it had disappeared completely from view.
