{cak} Dark Disc Shape with possible dome.extremely high under chemtrail.

I was on patio observing unusual chem trails when I observed a dark round object beneath one of the chem trails. At first I thought that it was a vulture, but it wasn't moving and it was too high in the sky. I saw it move in and out of the chem trail but for the most part it was just hovering around the trail cloud. I took a picture of it with my iPhone and enlarged it to get a better view. It appeard to be somewhat disc shaped. In a few minutes it just disappeared. Then a saw a small jet traveling at high speed in the direction of where the object had been. The jet continued North until it was out of sight.
