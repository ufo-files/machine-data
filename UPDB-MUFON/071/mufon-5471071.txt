Flashing circling red lights moving fast from my left to right. Changed direction very swiftly and drifted back and forth before I lost it

I was stargazing as the night sky was very clear. I was trying to notice satellites or planets and researched their morphology and location and how to spot them. I then suddenly thought something was moving and then chucked it to be something like a blinking star. But soon I found it move in a certain trajectory that involved an artificially accelerated pace. It was hard to track it for too long but it was shocking to see it change direction and hover up and down as it quit moving from my left to right. That's when I realized what I was seeing. It was a bunch of red lights in a disc shape with a certain self rotation and movement that involved displacement. I lost sight of the object after a car headed past in front of me as the lights were too bright and it was hard to find the object again.
