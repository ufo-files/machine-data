{rjl} object observed was a glowing orange orb, that hovered, moved erraticly then moved slowly away into the atmosphere

A friend of mine was in Antioch visiting a friend on Friday, March 14, 2014. While they were outside in the evening they observed this orange orb hovering above them. My friend immediately started filming it using her cell phone. They observed it for a little over 2mns before it went away. Her cell has smudges on it as you can see but the first few seconds of the video you can see the object clearly. She gave me a copy of it and as she was not sure what to do with it and told me to give to an appropriate authority. Because I know of you from watching a show that features your sightings I asked her for a copy of it to send to you.
