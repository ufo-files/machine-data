{cak} Bright light. Stationary like a star, then moved and stopped again. 5 times bigger than a star.

I was driving eastbound on College Ave. in Goshen,IN 46526 with my girlfriend on our way to get food. I noticed a "star" in the sky in the same direction we were traveling directly in front of us. I didn't take much notice and continued watching the road. Almost immediately after I focused on the road I noticed the star had moved slightly so I turned my focus back to the star, watched it a few seconds to see if it actually was moving. Sure enough it was moving but very slowly so I told my girlfriend "Hey look a shooting star." We both continued watching it waiting for it to burn out but it didn't. About 3 seconds after I told my girlfriend to look, it started picking up speed very quickly it was traveling in aproxamately the NW direction. We also noticed there was an aura around it with a small tail. Total duration was about 1 minuite. I thought it was a shooting star so I didn't get any video/audio/photo evidence.
