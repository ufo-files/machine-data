One bright reddish orange solid circle hovering over the treeline slowly moving parallel to the ground. Saw it for about 20-30 secs before it went behind the treeline. No sound, nothing blinking, no flickering. It was a solid, flat disc in the sky, the color of a home depot bucket, almost as if someone cut the bottom of a bucket and enlarged it. I must emphasize how it was like a flat shape in the sky, unlike anything i have ever seen before. Hard to describe how big or how far away it was but felt a bit like the size of the moon in the sky when its low. Both my wife and I saw it together and we both were stone-cold sober that night. 
Note: Strange things that happened the next day around 2:00 pm - tons of deer in the yard, probably like a dozen around and then a peregrine falcon almost hit me as I went into the house. It slammed into the glass door behind me as I was walking into the house. I turned and stared at it for about 30 seconds and then it flew away.
