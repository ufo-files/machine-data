Lights exploding in the sky

Thousands of light were crossing the sky we did observe the star link satellites but was not the interesting part. The sky looked alive with all the lights crossing the sky and were observed to what looked like were exploding. With flashes of green, blue, red and white flashes which would last 5-10 seconds. Other lights were flying parallel to the star link satellites but were observed to disappear with a bright flash of light.
I could not get video to work but can say with certainty I have not seen anything like this before in person or on any videos I have seen. It was very unnerving. Looked like missiles being shot out of orbit
