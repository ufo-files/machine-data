We were standing by our minivan in a town.

The date above is approximate. Lights appeared round with the appearance of a headlight that had just been in rain. Lights below seemed blocked off like seeing only a portion of them. This may have been from viewing them from the other side. The primary lights facing us were red then green or blue then red again. The bottom lights seemed similar, though hard to see. The vehicle had no apparent visible body perhaps due to the time at night. We only saw the lights moving very slowly, like they were drifting in very slow motion that was perfect without any sound or vibration at all.
