Husband was taking pictures of trees for trimming work needed and came running in the house and told me there is something in the sky. I was watching TV. He said it was moving. I ran to the window saw it and we both went on the balcony to watch it. We have both seen objects before this that were unusual and it was at night. This was the first time we saw something in the day. It was a bright shiny silver object in the sky - far in the distance. My husband said that it was moving. It appeared to be going bright than I could barely see it and it continue like that the entire time. We both looked at it with our binaculars. It looked like there was a red light that was moving on it. It stayed stationary for a while and then it traveled in a easterly course. I took a few pictures and can see something in one - if you zoom in close you can see it. I watched it for about 20 minutes until it moved out of my sight.
