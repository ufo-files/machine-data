rose from south sky, flew due north over my house, followed 90 sec later by similar object same path

What at first appeared to be a fireworks rocket launch south of our home rapidly became noticable as a flying object, very bright, almost like a fireball, proceeding in a straight line at a fixed altitude towards our viewpoint, then passing overhead slight West of our house. We were on a deck, south side of house, with good perspective to south and west and north. Object disappeared over ridgeline north of our location. 90 seconds later, a similiary (identical) object "rose" from the south and followed the same flight path an pattern.

Sighting immediately reported to local law enforcement, local newspaper and TV stations, with no indication of any other witnesses. There were three of us on the deck at the time and all agree on details of sightings.
