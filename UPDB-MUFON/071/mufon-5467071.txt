Reflective ball hovering and flying above small cornish town

It had just gone half 11 on a Sunday morning of the 6th May 2018.
My son was on his trampoline when he noticed what he thought was a bright star out during the middle of the day. The weather was perfectly clear and it was too late in the day to be a star or planet so i took notice straight away. My son showed me where it was and it was hovering just above the tree line in our garden though quite some distance away I would say. 
It stayed perfectly still for about 15 minutes then suddenly moved to the right and travelled some distance almost instantly north. It was now hovering at about the same height though in a north west direction.
Again it hovered for about 15 minutes before beginning to climb in altitude.
As it was climbing there was another period where it stopped for a while and hovered. At this point an air plane flew directly across its path, though it was too far to judge how far or close it was from the object in height.
I managed to get a few photos and it looks like a bright star in them, though if you zoom in enough it does appear that you can make out it was a metallic ball of some kind. I wanted to zoom in with the camera, but as the sun was so bright i could not make it out well enough on the camera screen to lock in on it and zoom in so i just took a zoomed out picture to make sure i captured it and thought id be able to zoom in at least on the computer afterwards as it was a high resolution camera. I tried videoing it via the same method but i did not manage to capture it.
