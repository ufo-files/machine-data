A strange, boomerang shaped amorphous object streaked across part of the sky

While stargazing, i looked S, and suddenly a streak appeared quickly, shaped from a streak, into what looked somewhat like a vertical seagull, and swept changing shapes. It disappeared extremely quickly, fast, slow then fast all in about four seconds. It looked golden, and when it transformed the shape it kind of looks like the star trek symbol. Very awe inducing! There was no trail, and this was not a meteor due to the speed and whimiscal way this swooped. And so low.
