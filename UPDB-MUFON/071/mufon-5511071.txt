Large Bright Teardrop shaped object drifted west towards vancouver island

Called out to the porch by my mother telling me something is in the sky, i ran up to the porch and observed a giant Teardrop shaped object Bright all around i assume from the reflection of the setting sun.. two planes flew laterally to it giving reference to its size... the planes paled in comparison to it, keeping in mind i stand on that porch and watch planes all the time, i may not be an air traffic controller, but i know what a plane looks like... Eventually it passed a tall treeline behind my house and out of view... I puttered around for a few minutes before deciding to race out behind the trees in my truck and try to capture a few pictures with my Rebel t3i. These are the pictures [Link moved to FI section/cms/ctg]. If you view in full res you will notice near the middle just above the trees is a red fleck, that is the object very far off in the distance. In the top mid right i just noticed, is a white dot, which is a star i am pretty sure because it stays pretty much stationary through all 3 photos. I did not notice it while i was taking the picture.
