3 Orange orbs of bright light shape shifting and hovering moving erratic.

3 orange orbs of light moving North West. Stopping abruptly and seeming to be blinding bright. I observed the bright light as it moved along the open blue sky in day light around 6 pm. I watched for several minutes with my brother and sister. We observed these lights with a strong pair of binoculars and they appeared to have light emitted from nowhere at all. They kept changing shape, size and in number. Moving very slowly and at times staying in one position hovering in different formations for 15-20 seconds. I observed them as they came together as one and disappeared going straight up into blue sky. 15 minutes later I walked out of my house and out of the east directly over my head flew a very large white colored disk with an orange light being emitted from the bottom. No signs of where light was coming from and was unlike any light I have ever seen before in intensity. No sound at all. Was traveling at rapid speed only visible for approximately 35-45 seconds as it moved across the sky. I clearly seen through my binoculars that it was very large and high. Under the cloud ceiling traveling faster than the speed of sound if I had to estimate. Again shot straight up and disappeared in an instant. I truly cannot identify what I seen and I have loved airplanes and can easily identify them. This was unexplainable. I seen all of this clear as day through binoculars with witnesses. Im not mistaken.
