{rjl} I saw approximately 10 White round lights

I work at a haunted hayride in the fall and in October 13, Saturday, I was waiting for a ride to come through my scene and I happen to look up as I was getting into position, and I see 10 White round lights. They are evenly spaced from each other, but it appeared to be just in the clouds because it looked fuzzy. No this was not due to alcohol. I see the lights moving from east to west. They just appeared out of nowhere. No sound! That’s what really irked me. Within seconds they were gone.
Fortunately another employee of the hayride who was in a different location witnessed the same thing I did. 

I want to add that a few years prior a few actors were freaking out asking me if I saw the lights? I had not that time, but I am glad I saw them this time. I will certainly be watching the skies this fall!
