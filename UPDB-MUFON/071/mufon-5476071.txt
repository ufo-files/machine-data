When I was almost 12 years old, living in Fulton Missouri, I was hanging laundry out to dry just behind our home. For some reason I felt the desire to look up and saw a silver thing in the sky. It was plain as day, a long cigar shaped thing just sitting in one spot. It did not move, and although it was completely silver there were areas that were a slightly darker silver and I took those to be windows. I thought I should call to my mother who was inside the house but then the thought came to me 'its just a satellite no need to call anyone'. So I didn't. I looked at it for a little while,continuing to hang the clothes out. But after the thought of it being a 'satellite' I seemed to lose interest in it and never looked up at it again. It wasn't a plane, it wasn't moving. There was absolutely no noise coming from it. It was not where the sun would affect my vision of it. It just WAS.
