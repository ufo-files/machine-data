{dr}It was a double looking luminescent triangle with 3-D circles under it.

I had seen something (which I will also fill out another report for) a few nights before in the same area. I had been thinking about this strange object. I happened to go to the Fresh and Easy grocery store in Burbank and as I pulled into the parking lot saw this odd object hovering east of where I was pulling in. It was luminescent, had almost a transparent quality, it almost looked as if it were made of clouds yet it was very geometrical. It had two very distinct lines with ball/sphere/circle shapes under it. I watched it and it just disappeared into the sky. It was broad daylight and very clearly unlike anything I have ever seen before. I have seen a lot of airplanes, military planes (Ive lived near more than one military installation) but this was almost etherial - it was as real as an object could be yet it had a transparent, glowing quality to it and the odd thing of having two very distinct lines one on top of the other like railings that formed a perfect triangle. The spheres were connected underneath the object. I tried to find my iphone to take video or shots of it but it was turned off and by the time it powered on it was gone.
