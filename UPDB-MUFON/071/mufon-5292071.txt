Maybe a 2 foot rod

Last Friday morning I was working the overnight shift at about 4:30 am my video doorbell camera sensor went off.  So I just figured it must be squirrel so I kept on working.  But the sensor went off four more times as I was working.  I was getting ready to leave work at about 5:40 am I opened up my ring app, and I was shocked to see what was causing the sensor go off.  When I went to a live view of the ring doorbell, and I could not believe what I was seeing.  It had been around my house for over an hour.  I did not know what to do with what I had captured, how to find out what it is. All that I know is that it had mass enough to set off the sensor.
