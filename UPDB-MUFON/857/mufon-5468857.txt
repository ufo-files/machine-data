Flying higher than the space station.

I went outside the front of my house for a cigarette, as usual I looked up and noticed a object moving very high up. It was white and moving forward. I was looking at a 45 degree angle above the ground. The object was higher than the space station and moving in a different direction, The space station passes over my house from west to east. this object moved North to South and at a faster cruising speed. after watching it for about 5 minutes the object turned East. I watched it for a further 5 minutes until it disappeared from view. It's not the first time I have seen something like this so it did not surprise me.
