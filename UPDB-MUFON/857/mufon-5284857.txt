Red and orange glowing globes at sunset

At the last part of sunset, two glowing orbs - one orange and one red - flew close to the ground, went behind a tree, and then dipped down into the horizon once they reappeared on the other side of the tree, coming from the south to a west/northwest direction. They flew well below visible clouds.
