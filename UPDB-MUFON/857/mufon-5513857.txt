{cak} Orange glowing lights spotted in Missouri

I was waiting on a friend near this church at 7pm on December 6th, 2012. There were orange glowing lights in the sky, as well as many flashing blueish "plane-like" lights. I have seen the orange ones before, the first time being in May 2008, and the second time being just a few weeks ago. These ones were much bigger, and rather than jitter and dance around the sky, they seemed to "swell", or kind of pulsate their light. Some of them were in pairs. Some of them were bunched together in weird formations. They moved around a bit, but I don't remember any particular pattern. There were less than 10 of the orange ones that I saw, but there were even more of these flickering, "star-like" lights, that looked like they could pass off as airplanes, but they didn't behave as such. There were quite a few of the blueish ones. There was a buzzing sound in the air, and kind of a "dreadful" feeling came over me, yet I was oddly calm. I was able to record a video of it.
