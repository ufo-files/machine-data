Fast-moving light in sky

Saturday evening January 30th, 2021, between approx. 6:20-6:25 (I placed an online food order at 6:16 and left the house minutes later), I walked out my front door and looked up toward the sky slightly SE from my position. I witnessed what appeared to be an incredibly fast-moving solid white light traveling S and either towards the Earth or along its gravitational curvature at a similar distance to observed satellites or perhaps somewhat closer to Earth. I stood still and observed the light/object travel roughly five-ten seconds between two neighboring trees and until it passed behind the southernmost tree and then continued down the driveway past the tree wherein the light/object was continuing its course. The light was bright without any observed flickering or deviation from its path. I've observed a satellite recently at a different point in the sky and traveling much more slowly than this. I've also been witness to plenty of "shooting stars," the duration of which was always much shorter than what I observed. I've tried looking into whether this was perhaps the ISS, but can't find any info about when or which direction it might've possibly been traveling over this general area. I assumed that's what this might've been, but what stuck out was the great speed it appeared to be traveling. I've been informed that the ISS typically takes about five minutes to cross the sky from end-to-end, but based on my observation, this light/object appeared to be traveling at least twice that speed. I don't remember whether I observed it until it passed behind the buildings across the street or looked away before and stepped into my car.

The attached photo was taken just now during the day from roughly the same position I observed the light/object, which was just above the right-most branches on the left tree traveling towards the tree on the right.
