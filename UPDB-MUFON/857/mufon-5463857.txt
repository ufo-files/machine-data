{rjl} Very unusual bright light appearing and reappearing while abruptly changing positions in the sky within 500 feet in the sky.

I was visiting a friend when we stood on the porch to get some air near 2:00 AM on May 21, 2011. We were in Green Valley (between Santa Clarita and Lancaster Ca) looking Northwest over Grass Mountain when we noticed a Very Bright Light hovering from behind the mountain within about one mile. The light abruptly moved to our right. After about fifteen seconds, the light abruptly went out. A few seconds later (5-10), the light re-appeared about fifteen degrees higher and abruptly moved to our left. This repeatedly went on for about two to three minutes before the light went out and never returned. My friend and I knew immediately that is was some kind of unconventional aerospace vehicle. I was disappointed when it ended and did not have access to a camera.
