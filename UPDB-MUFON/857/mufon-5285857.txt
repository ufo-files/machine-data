Cross-like object was outside my passenger window around 11pm near Mora.

Driving northbound on highway 518 in NM. I was driving 65 mph. (Cruise control). Approximate mile marker 15 or 16. Could be off. I saw a red light moving from my left to right in front of my vehicle. 600’ ahead, maybe? I thought it was awfully low for a plane. Only a red light. As it passed it split to two red lights. Then I thought it disappeared behind a hill. As I came up to where I thought I would be able to see it moving on, there was this object I could see out my passenger window. 100-200’ away in the air. It looked like a cross. It was moving, then stopped and slanted into a position where (I’m assuming is the bottom) I could see the shape. Like it was checking my car out. It was about 4 times the size of my car. The moon was shining so I had some light. On the “arms” it had 2 sets of green lights on each. Still driving, I tried to see through my mirrors. I could no longer see it.
