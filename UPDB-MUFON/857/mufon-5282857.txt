Three people saw a strange light in the sky

In period of half year I observed this phenomenon nightly. It was slowly moving in star like pattern. Periodically I would observe pulsations; spinning, rainbow like covers on zoom. House had 700-1600 mW/M2 RF and up to 1000 V/m EF spikes affecting my body heating up and painfully sensations in various parts of body which I attributed to high RF and EF. Nightly appearances were also witnessed by two visitors in my home and we interpreted this phenomenon as possibly drone or low orbiting satellite.
