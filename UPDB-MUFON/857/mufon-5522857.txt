White silvery disk hovering over Anaheim area then shot off towards Palos Verdes. Shortly fooled by fighter jets

we were bike riding home from Newport beach to Huntington beach along seashore. We saw a silvery bright object hovering in the sky sort of over the Anaheim area then it made some crazy moves then took off towards Palos Verdes. We tried to keep our eyes on it but then it was too far away. Then we saw two aircraft going in the same direction. Not sure what they were but they both gave off a short contrail and were going in the exact same direction as the object.
