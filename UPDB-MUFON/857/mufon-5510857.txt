{nd} I looked up and saw a large,bright stationary red light on the western horizon.

I had put the horses in their lot and started walking back to the barn when I looked up and saw a large, stationary, bright red light above the western horizon. I do not know how long it had been there since the trees had been in the way until I got closer to the barn.I stopped to watch it and called out to my friend who was helping me. We looked at it for a couple of minutes and then it disappeared as if it had been turned off.It was nearly as big as the moon.I have never seen anything like it. I am a stargazer and have seen odd things before, but nothing like that. I have no explanation for it.
