{cak} Ten lights hovering by Cheyenne Mountain Air Base

Ten lights in a row seen by myself and my friend. I was dropping her off at home and we were smoking cigarettes before she went into her house. We saw them from Fort Carson towards Cheyenne Mountain Air Base. The lights hovered and in a blink of an eye they were gone.
