Lights changing orbit

We were sitting outside at dark talking and my friend said look. I looked up towards the moon but kind of north of it , I saw a light. At first thought it was a plane until it suddenly changed orbit and went 90 degrees straight east and disappeared. I asked my friend if he saw it change direction and he stated that the object he saw was totally different, because his was by the moon and his disappeared also. Both were round white lights.
