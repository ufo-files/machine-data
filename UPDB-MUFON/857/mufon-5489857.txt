{jpg} Three objects emitted three lights apiece, one would shine lights then turn them off, then the middle would shine same then off, third also. Then all three shined together. Middle one kind of tilted a little, all hovered. Then all turned lights off.

Next day back about same time. Lights flew slowly south east. Turned all lights off. Later all on about ten miles south east then off again. Saw one craft in Colorado with three lights. It climbed up and down. Went down fast then stopped in air, at that time a noise of metallic bang. It hovered. Got bored and went about business. Could be man made hover craft. Neither sightings had high speed, lights did not rotate. Colorado lights during day. I guess so someone on ground could see it.
