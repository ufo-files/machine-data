{rjl} 1965 sighting by my brother, mother, and myself that lasted approximately 15 minutes.

My brother and I were awakened from deep sleep at around 3:10 a.m. on a weekday morning in November 1965. The sound that awoke us was a pulsating whine that can't be compared to anything I've heard since. My brother and I looked out our bedroom window to see a rotating disc 80 to 100 feet in diameter with a dome like structure atop the disc. The height of the UFO was about 1/4 the diameter, I estimate at 20 to 25 feet. The object was 250 to 300 yards out at approximately 30 degrees relative to our viewing it. It had lights showing yellow alternating with orangish red revolving around its lower base. The object itself was silver in color. It hovered for several minutes at low altitude. After observing in amazement, I ran down the hall to my mother's bedroom, shook her from a sound sleep, and told her to quickly come to my bedroom, there was something she had to see. She came in while tying her bathrobe, and audibly gasped when she saw what my brother and I had been viewing for several minutes. We decided to get in our VW bus and get as close a possible. I drove toward the hovering craft, and it seemed to notice our movement towards it and it immediately started to slowly ascend and move to the NE. I took the surface roads out of Valhalla (the name of our neighborhood), and was following it's path the best I could when it turned to the NW and descended over the foothills into a forested region that we could not access by roads existing at that time.The entire encounter spanned about 15 minutes. My mother, brother and I talked frequently about the vividness of the experience on that clear early morning in November 1965. My mother passed away in 1992. My brother and I discuss it still. By the way; my father retired from Northwest Airlines as a very senior 747 Captain (I have been a private pilot since 1974), and he and his crew were witnesses to an extended cigar tube shaped UFO, that maneuvered around their DC-6 cargo flight from Anchorage Alaska to Seattle WA. In 1959. It eventually left them at an incredible rate of speed that my father told me was impossible to be achieved by known technology.
