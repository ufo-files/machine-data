{cak} Bright Ball of Light pulsed twice before vanishing in the night. It felt telepathic.

I stepped outside my friends' garage, to the driveway and looked up to see a light passing over moving from the south toward the north. As I focused on it, wondering: plane? satellite?.. it gave a distinct single pulse burst which seemed to be directed at me. I contemplated calling to my friends but in another moment: it flashed again, a definite 'signal' which gave me a feeling of acknowledgement as it passed overhead. I turned to watch it cross the sky, but it faded out as it reached the center area up above. It left me with a good feeling. Mysterious, beautiful, and definitely intelligently guided. Although it lasted only a moment, I feel it will stay with me: another brief encounter with our 'neighbors' whom apparently are too busy to come hang out but occasionally like to give me the equivalent of a 'wink' or a 'high-five'.
