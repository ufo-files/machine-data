{rjl} Very dark oval vessel.

At approximately 9:20 PM on November 22, 2015 I was riding my bike home when I happened to look up at the sky. At first I thought it was a bat but upon further inspection it was an elongated black object traveling faster than an airplane and a very close to the ground traveling north to south. I made visual contact for about five seconds before the object turned south east and within a blink of an eye went left than straight up while leaving a white light trail behind only lasting a second. Still thinking this was a bat, I was very confused. It took me a minute to process that this was definitely not an animal but a machine. There is no possible way this was an animal or a human machine because of the maneuvers it took and its amount of power it generated in under a second. The only reason I was able to see this object is because of the overwhelming light pollution coming off from the city and the almost full moon making the sky appear blue rather than black. If these conditions weren't exactly like they were, I would have not been able to see the black object.
