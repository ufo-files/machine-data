2 diffferent crafts about 10 mins apart

I WAS ON THE DIRT ROAD LOCATED NEAR THE COMPANY CALLED AIR PRODUCTS. I NOTICE THREE LIGHTS IN THE SKY AT FIRST I THOUGHT IT WAS A PLANE. BUT I SOON CAME TO REALIZE IT WAS NOT. IT WAS TRIANGLE SHAPE WITH LIGHT ON THE POINTS IT WAS ABOUT A FOOTBALL FIELD ACROSS FROM POINT TO POINT. IT MOVED AT A SLOW SPEED VERY LOW IN THE SKY. IT MADE HARDLY IF ANY NOISE. AS IT FEW OVER ME HEADING TOWARDS MARION HIGH SCHOOL(WESTWARD)AND OVER THE MOUNTAIN I LOST SIGHT OF IT . I WAS VERY CURIOUS. AS I TURNED AROUND THAT IS WHEN I NOTICED A SECOND SET OF LIGHTS IN THE SAME SHAPE COMING TOWARD ME AGAIN. JUST AS THE 1ST ONE FLEW THE SAME PATH AS THE FIRST ONE. I DID THINK IT COULD HAVE BEEN A MILATRY PLANE THE STEALTH. BUT I DID MY RESEACH AND FOUND OUT THE NEAREST STEALTH HANGER WAS LOCATED IN LLLNOIS. I LIVED IN THIS AREA MY WHOLE LIFE AND THIS WAS ONE OF MOST UNEXPLAINED THING I HAVE EVER SEEN. NOW I CALL AIRPRODUCTS AREA 52 BECAUSE OF ALL THE STRANGE HAPPENINGS AROUND THAT AREA...
