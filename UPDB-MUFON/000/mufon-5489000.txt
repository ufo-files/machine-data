{cak} Star like object hovered in daylight then disappeared

I was watching the Georgia Tech - Notre Dame football game inside when I decided to go out on my balcony to smoke a cigarette. About half way through my cigarette I felt compelled to look up in the sky and that is when, about 80 to 85 degrees above the horizon I saw a star like object hovering in the sky. It was very small, like a faint star. However, this in the afternoon just before 2:00pm. There were no clouds in the sky. The object seemed to be in the direction of Santa Monica, roughly to the North-West. It was very hard to discern, and if you looked away it was hard to find it again. However, once i saw it I felt compelled to not look away. I observed the object for about 10 minutes or so, and at almost exactly 2:00 the object simply vanished. There was no flash, no fading out, nothing. It simply vanished. I tried my hardest to look for the object, thinking that I might have just lost focus of it. It was very small and, like I mentioned before, if you looked away it was hard to locate again. However, it simply vanished. When I first saw the object I became very excited. I felt like it was looking at me. Maybe that's why it vanished so quickly when I spotted it. Just to be clear, I live near LAX and I see aircraft flying over my apartment all the time. In fact, during my observation of the, a Boeing 737 with Southwest Airlines livery flew over my apartment. The difference between the two objects appearance and behavior were obvious. After the sighting I immediately came inside to leave this report. I am unaware of what the object could be. This is the 3rd or 4th sighting of an object exactly like this one I have had this past year.
