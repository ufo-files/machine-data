light crossed path of space station

Watching the International Space Station arrive 10° above SSW. As ISS approached 30 degrees a second fainter object appeared to cross the orbit of the ISS. The second object appeared to be moving at close to escape velocity when compared to the ISS. It arose from the SSW and out ran the ISS by a considerable margin but seemed to travel directly E while the ISS followed a course to the NE. The object also appeared to be at a higher altitude than the ISS.
