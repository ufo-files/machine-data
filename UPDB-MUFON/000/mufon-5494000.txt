We were all in our apartment jacuzzi maybe a little after 9pm, my cousin was in the pool with my son and as I'm watching them swim around I see some lights just appear in the sky, maybe between 40 and 60 lights. I see them moving and more randomly just appearing and fading away. I tell my girlfriend to look over and she jumps up yelling what's that. We get my cousin to look and another couple in the jacuzzi to look. We were all speechless and confused on what we just witnessed. The lights moved up and down,the pattern of the flight was something we have never seen before. The lights begun to get brighter. Almost looked like white led lights, we saw and watched the lights move from Southwest then to the south. As we were watching they all seemed to disappear all at the same time. Our first thought was Chinese lanterns, but these weren't amber colored and there was no wind to make the lights move the way they were moving. It was a night we'll never forget.
