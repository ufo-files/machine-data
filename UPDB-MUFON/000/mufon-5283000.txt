Flat white disk flying around downtown

Came home from working 3rd shift, let the dog out and looked up to see a flat white disk cruising at a high altitude. It zig zagged back n forth and continued to fly east. Let the dog in and told my family members to come look at it and the thing had gone in the opposite direction and made a good distance in a matter of minutes. Watched it for 15 mins til it flew off over the trees heading north west.
