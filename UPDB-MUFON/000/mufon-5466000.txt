I was visiting my father in FL. My dad woke me up to show me what he found on the security camera he put up in our house. I saw this glowing object on our dinning table. At first I thought they had a lit candle, but then it started moving flying around the kitchen hovering around the surface of the dinning table and other objects in that area. My dad thought it was a bug, I noticed a beam shining from the bottom of the flying object. It looked like it was scanning for something or just scanning. At one point It shrinks smaller and the propeller it had at the top of the object disappears. As soon as it shrinks smaller it flies up to the ceiling, and shoots back down and goes back to the original form it was in. Also I would have these strange dreams of someone coming to pick me up to go to work, but my work was not on earth. It seemed like i was in space. For the next week I kept having the same dream of being picked up but it would always be different scenario. I was in charge of something, at one point I was kicking strange looking rats. I was always tired in my awake time and sick sick sick, so sick I had to go to urgent care. They could not tell me what was going on. My cell phone would be fully charged and then its drained. I touched my cell phone only to charge it. I hope you guys can figure this out. I don't know if its a bug, a ghost or higher beings.
