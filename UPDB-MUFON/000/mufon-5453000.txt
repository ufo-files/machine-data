Bright green flash 3 blinking lights emerged

I was driving home from work had just gotten dark out and as 8 was getting off the exit in Pevely, mo where I live I saw this huge green flash of light in the southern sky. As I was looking up in the sky to see what it was remembering thinking never seen green lightning before. Anyway as I looked 3 lights blinking and changing colors from white to red to blue to orange and also green were moving slowly in different directions. I pulled over in a McDonald's parking lot and began filming with my cell phone. 1of the objects at 27 seconds into the video flies by from right to left at the top of the screen in the video at an amazing high speed. Another directly after comes from down near the ground and flies up at an angle. The last object is the rest of the video and it moved from the south to northwest at a slow pace then takes off at lightning speed. It was unbelievable but yet very exciting to see this although not my first experience with a sighting I had another sighting about 28 years ago when I was around 16 yrs old. Me and 2 friends laying in the bed of a pickup truck witnessed a huge black triangle slowly drift over us at a low altitude never made a sound.. I have been a uap believer ever since.
