Bright glowing circle, moved to east rapidly then back to west then disappeared in bright flash

I watched the object in the NW sky while I was warming up my scooter to go to work. It was moving slowly, then it disappeared and reappeared to the east of me. It then moved rapidly to the west and stopped in the sky to the north of me. The was a sudden large bright flashing the sky directly north of me and the object had vanished. It looked like a large star or aircraft light at first, but it was too large and bright and moved too quickly. It did not emit any sound, even after the flash. The entire event lasted about three minutes. I don't have photos, but am hoping someone else saw this as well. Kind regards.
