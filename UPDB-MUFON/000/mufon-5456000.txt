{cak} Triangle shaped with lights under and across back.

Triangle shaped with 6 white circular lights underneath. The lights didn’t shine down but were lit from within. It was stationary when I first saw it straight above my head. There was absolutely no sound at all. It started slowly forward at same altitude and as it moved forward, there were 3 lights across back side facing me. They were equal size - like brake lights - and 2 outside lights were white and middle was red. They were flashing, but not synchronized and sometimes all blinked at the same time. Watched until it disappeared behind trees. Lights underneath remained on the entire time.
