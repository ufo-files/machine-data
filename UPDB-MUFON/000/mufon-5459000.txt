{jpg} Strange pulsating light that flashed every few seconds

On Aug 3rd 2019 I received a phone call from a very nice woman that worked at Mufon by the name of Valerie. We talked about a previous video I submitted to Mufon and I told her that she had inspired me and I was going to go outside with my Nikon D5600/ Astro scope 9350 night vision equipment and see what I could find. Around 10pm PST I came across what I believed to be a satellite going North, typical speed nothing out of the ordinary that would make me believe this was anything else other than a satellite, but as I fallowed this object a much brighter object came from the opposite direction. This was about 4.40 into the video, as I begin to fallow this object it starts to pulsate, at one point it appears to speed up and then starts to flash some kind of light. This lasted for about 5 minutes until it flew behind my house I was in the front yard.
