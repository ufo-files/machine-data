10-12 white stars travelling in same direction one behind the other same distance apart

Was on Eglington Ave W in Toronto Ontario on March 5,2020 at 5:30am looked up and saw 10-12 ((stars)) moving in a line of one behind the other they were the same brightness same distance apart going the same speed at the same altitudes the light they emitted was constant and did not pulsate nor change color. From my position they appeared to be 2 feet apart ..?..and maintained that distance/speed/altitude perfectly as if they were attached by a string! My work partner standing beside me also saw them! We tried to take pictures with our cell phones but they were too far away and it was still too dark! We know what planes look like and are generally familiar with the altitude on the flight paths in Toronto and these were waaaay above like waaay above the flight traffic and the flight paths! Literally 10-12 stars moving in a one behind the other formation from NNW to SSE! Wife n I satellite watch , mostly in the summer , at the camp ground and it was exactly like that !.. BUT 10-12...?! TOGETHER?! In formation?! Nope. And well beyond regular airplane traffic altitudes and flight paths!
