Two craft flying from West to East Very high up one was four engine plane (contrails) the other was a larger grey triangle (no contrails)

I was out in the garden re-erecting an antenna and heard a loud rumble, looked up and saw two planes flying next to each other high up travelling from West to East. One was definitely a plane, it had contrails but the other did not look like a plane. it was triangular, was larger than the plane and had no contrails. With binos i could see the plane was grey and had four engines and the triangle was also grey with no features. I could see no lights or anything other than a featureless pointy triangle. I followed their path for a couple of minutes until they were lost in haze. the same sound was heard the day after later in the afternoon but as it was cloudy i saw nothing.
