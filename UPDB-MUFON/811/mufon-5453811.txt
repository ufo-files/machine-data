{cak} 3 crafts in low earth orbit traversing apart from each other.

We saw 3 objects traversing from east to west. All traveling same direction about 3 to 5 seconds apart, spread out from each other side to side. The objects were BIG or extremely close to ground, but, made no sound. I have observed the space station multiple times and these were MUCH bigger to the eye. They were not reflecting the sun like satellites would either. Though, the almost looked like they had faint hazy glow around them. At first I thought it was a balloon but quickly realized it was not as it would have been too dark to see one and these moved in a very straight line and traveled at a high rate of speed, which a balloon would not. They traveled at 2-3 times the speed that the space station would normally move. Did anyone else see these?
