Fleet of Fast moving objects in the sky

My 13 year old daughter and I witnessed 10-12 white lights high in the sky flying in a straight formation over the Simi Valley/ Santa Paula area. The formation was PERFECTLY straight and judging by how high they were they were very fast. We saw the event for 20-30 seconds before the lights vanished in order. There were planes and helicopters flying in the area but these were very distinct and unmistakably different. Hoping others saw this. We were shocked and excited.
