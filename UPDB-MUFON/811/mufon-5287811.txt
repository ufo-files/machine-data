7 to 9 semi-orbs flying SW to NE in a equally spaced line formation west of LAX.

Driving west on Slauson at Crenshaw saw 7 to 9 rose colored semi-orbs flying an equally spaced straight line formation one behind the other which caused me to pull over and stop my car on a side street. Objects were roughly 1/2 to 1 mile apart from each other following the same NE heading. Objects appeared to ascend from an area west of LAX to level off at 2,000 - 4,000 feet AGL at 9:40pm. Objects speed rapidly increased as they flew over directly over my head in silence. The solid rose colored light being emitted was not light from a diode or a filament. The light was plasma and it reacted to the forward motion of the object though the air like a candle flame in a breeze. Plasma appeared to be coming only from the bottom half of the object. It was only a semi-orb but too dark outside to see any structure. Objects were all identical in size, speed, spacing, and color.
