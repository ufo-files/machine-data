{jpg} I was stargazing and then witnessed a short streak of light with something leading it.

I was coming back from the gas station, and when I got back home I decided to just stand outside for a bit and look at the stars. Recently, I notice that the more I pay attention to the sky and what's in it, the more things appear in the sky. This was the first time ever seen something so clear in my life. As I was looking up, A bright flash of blue green white light streaked through the sky for about 3 seconds and then vanished like it was never there. I began crying because in my mind I was asking myself, "is there anyone else out there or am I wasting my time." Then, there it was! Right where I was looking in the sky. I wanted to stay outside longer and see what else I could witness, but I was overcome by fear just from hearing about abductions and people and children being taken by strange lights.. so I decided to go inside. When I came back outside about 2 minutes later, I tried to look for something else. Low and behold, I witnessed what looked like a star just making it's way across the sky. No bigger but no smaller than the stars surrounding it. I watched this for about 1 minute and then the feeling of fear, or being watched, took hold of me again. I began crying again but it wasn't tears from only fear, but also happiness in the fact that, wow.. there is no way that anything from THIS planet/dimension.
