I was driving from Mexico to Tuskegee to see a lazy friend of mine. I escaped across the border and I stole a laptop from a store and used a false email address to hide from the American police because I wasn't going back to Mexico, so anyway I was driving to Tuskegee when I saw these strange lights in the night sky and these lights were in the shape of a teardrop so I stop along the aside of the road to get a closer look when suddenly both my truck and wristwatch, both which I also stole as well, but anyway suddenly stopped working and then suddenly I heard some pulsating sounds coming from the ground so I went to see what it was. When I got to the source of the lights and sounds, I saw a ship in the form of a huge teardrop and then suddenly coming off the teardrop-shaped ship, I saw six blonde-haired humans that were all extremely beautiful and intelligent. From my point-of-view I saw that they're were taller than average humans and were all wearing the same white skin-tight suits, both three males and three females. Suddenly they saw me and slowly came towards me so I began to ran as fast as can and kept right on running until I turned around and saw the teardrop ship suddenly hovered above the trees and then it took off into the night sky at a very fast speed. After that all my truck and wristwatch suddenly. Suddenly to this day I still try to tell this tale even with a new laptop I stole and a new false address.
