Walked out of Walmart and immediately noticed an approaching aircraft.

I watched the aircraft approach from the WSW as I walked to the car and realized how slow it was moving. It never appeared to change altitude but as it passed over it changed to a NW direction until trees eventually blocked my view.
I probably watched for 3 minutes and as I looked around I saw 5-6 people also watching. It did have 3 lights on it but zero sound. There's no way a normal plane could be that quiet.
Silhouetted against a brighter than normal sky I could see the outline of a perfect black boomerang.
