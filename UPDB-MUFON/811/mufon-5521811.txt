White, cloud-like, soft white glow, vaguely bomber plane shape with no wings but with tail

As I got out of my car to go inside of my house my eyes caught something to the south that made me look. At first I didn't see anything but clear dark night sky. Suddenly my eyes caught a soft white glow moving behind the neighbors treetop. It was bizarre I had no idea what I was looking at. It was moving across the sky in a straight line. I almost couldnt see it but it had a soft white glow. I thought it was a fast moving cloud in the night sky but no other clouds are visible, clear skies. And I have never seen a cloud move that fast. The shape reminded me of a bomber but with no wings. It did have a very prominent "tail" like a bomber would. I heard no sounds from its direction. I watched it fly across the horizon for about 20 seconds and then it was gone behind the treeline, moving east.

It might sound crazy but it really looked like a ghostly image. I also thought it might be some kind of a cloud but it kept its shape the entire time I watched it. It could have been a smoke cloud from someones fire but Ive never seen smoke in such a shape or moving so fast and in a direct flight path when I felt no wind.
