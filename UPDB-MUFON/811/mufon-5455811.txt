Some kind of space suite but resembled human shape and size

While working in the back garden I had an overwhelming feeling I was being watched from above in front of me. I looked up but couldn’t see anything then all of a sudden my eyes focused on an object 75ft in front moving across the front of me very very slowly. I felt as if I couldn’t blink in case I wouldn’t be able to see it again. During this time of not blinking I observed a egg shaped object in upright position with some person sat in it wearing some kind of a suite. He or it seemed to be looking at me but was unmoving. It was almost transparent and blended in perfectly with the clouds. I knew that once I blinked I wouldn’t see it again. Once I blinked it was gone. I looked and looked but could not see it again. I would say it’s size was about the same as an helicopter cabin all glass fronted.
