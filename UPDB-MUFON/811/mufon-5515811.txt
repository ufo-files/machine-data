I woke up in the morning and went outside to smoke. When I looked up at the sky, I saw a very bright cigar shaped object. I knew it was moving very fast because it passed behind a pole at the top of a hill. I then ran in and got my camera and started to film. I was very shakey and tried to zoom in and then out to try to get the hill for referance. When I went to try to stabalize the camera, it was gone.
