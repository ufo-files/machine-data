Triangle object followed Shoreline then flew off at high velocity East (Towards Atlantic)

On June 19, 2011, Indialantic, FL. Around midnight, Triangular aircraft with Red/Orangish ball in center flow along the coast line Soutth->North for ~1.5minute. (Have old iPhone-3 crappy video with Audio of myself and partner discussing it as we recorded, video is available for analysis). Shortly after sighting you could hear and visibly see the afterburn takeoff of military jets from the near by Airforce Base, the object turned Eastward towards the Atlantic at its currently velocity, then accelerated eastward (Towards the Atlanic) and was out of sight within 15-20 seconds.

I've read some people describe this as a "TR3B". If anyone else saw this that night, please let me know!
