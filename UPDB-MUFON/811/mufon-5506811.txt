4 blue orbs in shape of diamond. about the size of small plane

My spouse and I were driving on a road on rose barracks, the ufo flew almost above our vehicle. There was no sound and it was no higher than 100 feet above the ground. It was dark, and we made out 4 blue orbs shaped in the pattern of a diamond. It appeared to be the size of a small plane. It did not make a sound. I followed it on a road near the woods, it seemed to fade away and we could not see it, then it would appear in a different location that was not normally possible for aircraft to maneuver. We saw it hovering about 200 feet into the woods and then the lights vanished. There seemed to be random blinking or flashing white strobes of light.
