Orb seen, that changed colour from white to red, then faded away

we live under the Heathrow flightpath, there is a plane every six seconds. We are used to seeing them. We were showing the kids the moon in the back yard and became aware of a very bright light. It was high, but not space high. It was 10 or so times the size of a star and super bright.

It flew over us in around 10 seconds, then changed to a bright red. Then either accelerated or faded out.

We were both stunned. The kids saw it too.

We all compared the planes and it was nothing like them, silent, eerie and so bright.

AWESOME!

We loved it.
