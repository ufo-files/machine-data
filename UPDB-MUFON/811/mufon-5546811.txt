Fast zig-zag star

I was out for a smoke and watching the skies as usual and saw what looked like a star... in motion. It was moving fast in a zig zag motion in the sky by the Big Dipper. I watched it for about 20 seconds and then it was gone. I thought at first that it was a sattilite, but they move in a straight line and this made two 90 deg. turns. I ran inside to tell my husband but it was gone by then.
