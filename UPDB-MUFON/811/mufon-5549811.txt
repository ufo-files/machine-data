They were White and Shinny. Looked like two sphere

Me and my mother were on the sofa watching TV when two shinny, metalic objectscaught my eye. I shouted to my mom and we both ran to the glass, sliding door.In the sky two spheres about a mile away rushed through the sky. My mother firstthaught it was a jet, but soon changed her mind, because the objects weresoaring at speeds close to 1,000 miles per hour! The objects danced in the skyas they soared east. soon they disappeared behind a few trees and I ran out toget a closer look. There was a strange but faint sound in the air. When Ifinally spoted them agian they were still dancing eastward, and soon they zoomedup into the clouds.
