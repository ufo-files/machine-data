Shaped like a dumbell.

On our deck observing the sky from dusk to darkness. Noticed an object traveling south to north shaped like a dumbbell, as wide as a commercial airliner but not as long, dull amber/gold colored, with no outstanding features. What we noticed is that the object was void of FAA lighting, nothing flashing. Even though the object was in a typical area that planes routinely took to Portland International airport from the south, the description was not the usual blinking red and white customarily seen and identified as common commercial aircraft. At first, I took exception to the sighting not thinking it was anything out of the ordinary, but it became apparent to me after witnessing that the object was NOT in accordance with FAA regs. I know that to be without exception that it was either a legitimately marked aircraft or not, the latter being the case. It maintained a steady course toward the north as any aircraft would under normal conditions and faded into the city lights 20 deg. above the horizon. Very perplexing.
