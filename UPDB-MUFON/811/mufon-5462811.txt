{tjd} While preparing to leave work for the evening I was alerted to a flying orb in the sky..pulsating then hovering away

On Sunday January 27,2019 at approximatley 6:17pm, and while preparing to leave work. I was located at the rear of my vehicle I was alerted to a flying orb like object hovering approximately 500 feet or better in the sky. The object pulsated and appeared to be on fire. I could observe and definitely tell it was not an airplane, helicopter, drone or any known conventional flying machine of public knowledge at least. After observing the object. I immediately grabbed my cellular phone camera and began to record the object as it propelled through the sky. The object lingered for approximately 2 minutes before disappearing into the night sky and then unknown. I was not afraid or alarmed at the time due to the object not appearing to be threating by nature just observational at best.
