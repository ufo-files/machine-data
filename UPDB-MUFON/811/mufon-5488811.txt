I was taking pictures of the Mexico sign. Object appeared in picture. Like huge angel.

My family and I were visiting the Chamizal National Memorial. It is located near the Mexico border. I took four pictures with my phone. I did not notice anything unusual. 
When we were about to leave, I scrolled through my pictures. In one of the 4 pictures, there was a distinctive object. It looked like a cross between a huge angel and a spacecraft body.
I showed it to my wife and we were both shocked. I am still in shock, and I have an extreme desire to know what it was.
I grew up around airplanes, my dad had his private pilot license and he flew as a crew member on various airforce aircraft. 
I'm also a combat vet having served in Iraqi Freedom. I have observed plenty of things in the sky to include other ufo. 

One of the 4 pictures shows a small black dot in the upper left frame. So if this was the same object, it must have traveled at light speed to get from a dot to a winged ufo in the few seconds it took me to take the 4 pictures. 
Please help. I am not sure if I will ever be the same because my picture looks like the strange objects I have seen on television that I thought were fakes.
