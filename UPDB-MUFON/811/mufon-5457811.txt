{tjd} Tiny glowing orb flew over our backyard as we watched

Upon hearing a hawk's cry, I went out to my backyard to see it and observed the hawk soaring over four blackbirds towards a tiny white glowing orb approaching from the east. The blackbirds flew out of sight as the hawk prepared to divebomb the object, then abruptly turned and flew off as the orb circled in a jerky fashion to avoid the hawk, and then glided on in a smooth trajectory towards the north. I called my husband, who came out and we both watched for a minute or so until it disappeared into the morning mists. It resembled one of the satellites that I've seen many times flying on a similar trajectory from S to N over our home at night. But the motion and the glowing - and the fact that it was visible during the day - were quite different.
