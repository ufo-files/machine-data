{jpg} 2 objects swirling above earth feed cut. then another object above earth feed cut again

I was on the NASA ISS live feed, about 5 minutes after watching these beautiful swirling portals/worm holes/twister like objects appeared above the earth and in seconds yet from one side of the earth to the other. I captured 1 screen shot before NASA cut the live feed. I continued to wait for the feed to come back hoping for another chance to see these objects, but they were gone when the feed came back up. However, about 10 minutes after the feed was up again, a different swirling object appeared, which I captured several screen shots of the object changing. Moreover, the feed changed and you can see part of the satellite in, with the camera lens (visible model and/or part numbers) in screen shots. You can see a bright light on satellite then a shadow coming closer and closer then the feed is cut. I placed the screen shots on a word doc. You can see the time stamps. I have questioned NASA and everyone that I could in regard, to these objects with no response. I feel these objects are very significant and need to be shared.
