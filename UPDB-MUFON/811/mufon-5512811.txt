{nd} Bright light traverling from west to east.

I was in front of my apartment having a cigarette when I noticed what looked like a comet. (resembled the comet halebop with the tail) It moved from the west over the everglades to the east towards the beach. I was looking north towards Palm Beach County. It was a clear night and stars were visible. The object was moving at a steady speed and then the trail disappeared but object remained. Then the trail reapeared and then the entire object disapeared for a few seconds, reapeared to the east with the trail and then it just vanished. I live near the airport so I know what airplanes look like. All I do know is that this was no airplane, no FAA lights, no noise. My feeling is that I just saw something that I couldnt explain nor identify. I was excited so I went back in the house to write down and draw a pic of what I just witnessed.
