Very similar to a satellite, but there were 20 plus in a row very close behind one another.

I looked up in cloudless night. Saw what I thought was a satellite, but looked different, a bit brighter. Much the same brightness of a bright star. It moved rather fast from direct west to east. From where I was standing to west approx 45 degrees west til disappeared at approx 45 degrees east. Here is the anomaly, it was followed directly behind and at same speed another one. At arms length out about 2” apart. Then another... counted over 20, my buddy came over when I yelled for him to look and he saw the last 6 or so. The objects just appeared and moved straight, not like moving in a set altitude but rather a straight line til they disappeared. No noise, just perfect same speed, same distance apart. The path was approx 47 degrees by 30 minutes going almost due east coming from the west. Our satellite tv, has a dish pointing almost due south, bell Canada, and off horizon 30 degrees. When we went back inside the signal was lost and a error of “ no signal”. Took few minutes to get back up and running. Never ever have I seen our tv go off line on a clear night for no reason. Can’t be satellites as why 20 plus all in unison. Just thought looked strange enough to warrant a report and perhaps might add to another sighting.
