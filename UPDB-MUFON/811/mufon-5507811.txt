{kt}Out window around 9 pm I saw 3 large orange glowing orbs slowly ascending arc, one at a time like being shot out

sitting in my living room i noticed a bright object outside my window around 9 pm. it was a large bright orange glowing orb moving slowly in an ascending arc high in the sky. i was thinking about the size and color being unusual as another one appeared... like it had been shot out of something because it appeared out of nowhere where i first noticed the first one. In the sky from my viewpoint it was larger than an aspirin but smaller than a dime... So much larger than a star or plane lights...and constant glow, not blinking . The bright orange color was very unusual. Then a third one appeared but it seemed more reddish orange.  They followed the same path and disappeared out of my view. They were moving slowly but the sighting was less than 2 minutes. I didn't hear any noise and I was thinking if it could be some sort of fireworks but my dog always barks at them and she didn't notice.
