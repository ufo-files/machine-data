Multiple orange lights in formation flying

During college I would go up to the observatory in Pullman Washington. On two occasions while hanging out outside of the observatory myself and some friends saw groups of orange lights flying from east to west. The lights would be in different formations each time. Sometimes it would just be random formations. Other times they would be in an arc formation. They were completely silent and were above the clouds. I would estimate at seeing 10 to 15 groups of lights over the course of 2 hours on both occasions.
