Solid triangular object passing very slowly and very low overhead disappearing over horizon

I was walking onto the Bolehill recreation ground to catch up with a group of friends whom were already on the playground section when i saw a red light out of the corner of my eye. When i looked up almost directly above me was a large black triangular solid object with a red light in the middle moving very slowly and very low from my left to my right. There was absolutely no sound at all which was puzzling as the object was so close to the ground. As it passed my field of vision from the left i noticed on what i considered to be the rear of the object an arrangement of what looked like three exhausts you might find on an F14 Jetfighter but in a triangular formation unlike any i have seen before and emitting a white light. These remained visible as the object continued overhead and disappeared into the distance. I was dumbstruck and didnt ask my friends if they had seen it too as i thought if they had they would have said something which none of them did.I headed straight home without telling anyone what i had seen and I have wondered what it was ever since.Also about half an hour before i saw this object i felt and heard a low humming sound in the same area that i witnessed this object.
