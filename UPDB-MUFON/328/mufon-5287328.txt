Running Humanoid

A couple of years ago while going on an late afternoon walk I saw pass next to me (about 1 yard away) a humanoid shape that was invisible and only shimmered for a fraction of a second. I realize that this is not much of a description of what occurred but after I saw this I turned to my wife that was walking along side me and she said "did you see that?".

I don't know why I waited this long to mention it but given what is going on these days I figured what the hell and what difference can it make anyways since we cant see them.
