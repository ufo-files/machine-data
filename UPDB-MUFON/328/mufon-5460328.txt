{jpg} Triangle shaped orange lights. Appeared to be slowly moving from a distance and then took off at amazing speeds.

My best friend and I began a late night walk on satellite beach, Florida. Around 1 am. We walked ways down the beach so the exact known location was unknown. The sky was very clear and the stars were stunning. As I was glancing into the sky I noticed a weird light I have never seen before. I immediately told my friend to check it out and he was just as curious as I was. It was a black triangle shape craft with orange circular lights on the bottom of the craft. It appeared hovering, moving slowly. Within a few seconds of staring at this object it instantly took off across the sky and disappeared. Once my friend and I saw the speed and weird flight patterns we knew it wasn’t anything we could identify and we both felt an intense state of fear. We both started running along the beach back to the house we were staying at. We kept talking about it and investigating as much as we could and hit every dead end we could. This was 3 years ago and to this day I still think about it everyday and search for answers. Patrick’s Air Force base is some miles from that. We suspected it could have been something the military was up too but there wasn’t anything else in the sky at the time that we could identify. We didn’t stick around to see if it was coming back.
