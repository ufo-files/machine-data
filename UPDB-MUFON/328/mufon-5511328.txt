3 bright lights, in triangle shape, suddenly each of them going separate directions, no sound.

Date is not noted by myself, but I know it was around this time of year, when leaves started to grow on the trees.
I never wrote this down, until now.
It was on an evening, late, could be 11-pm. Me and my wife to be, were going home from our weekly shooting event(rifle-club), on the Wednesday.
We were driving on the highway A-20, between Rotterdam and Gouda.
There were big white clouds in the dark sky.
I noticed a bright light in the sky in front of us. I said to my wife that is was strange to see a helicopter this late with so much light.
As the "helicopter" came closer and we moved on along the highway, we kept looking at 'this'.
Then I said to her,...'look'...there are 3 light's,...that must be a huge "helicopter".
...I thought is was a helicopter because the light's were vibrating due to the movement/oscillation of the rotor.
When the 3 light's where distinctively apart visible,
...they suddenly separated whit enormous speed....???
As fast as I could I stopped the car on the hard shoulder of the highway and we got out to see what happened.
The 3 light's went with up between and through the clouds, with no sound at all!!
Every plane or helicopter leaves a sound when ascending from under the clouds. But here...nothing..., there wasn't even a breeze from the wind.
Everything was just calm.
All 3 light's where going separate ways. One up to the north, one straight up and the other up to the south.
When going through the clouds,...we saw the clouds lighten up a little,..reflecting the light that was emitted.
And that was it!....
We never could explain what we have seen.

Our house is in the flightpath of Rotterdam-airport. We know how planes and helicopters sound-like on various height's.

No sound at all!!....just light's. Remarkable.
And with such ease and speed.
