{nd} Observed Glowing Red Light on the Horizon, fog approaching from coast.

My wife and I went out to take some recycling, I was thinking that I never see fireballs in the sky, not that what this was.I spotted what I first thought was maybe a red glowing firework, it was just above the tree line,it appeared to pulse that's why I thought maybe it's firework fading, but then it got stronger and I found that odd, I went and put my glasses on to take get a better look, it was much clearer that this was not a firework but more like a beacon, the only problem with that was there are no beacons that I have ever seen around here and the nearest airport is ten miles not to mention we hardly ever get any air traffic over here. I got my lower power binoculars and got a better glimpse, at one point it seemed like it was reflecting the glow below it, it started to get foggy. I went inside to get my cell phone to record it, when I came back it was still there, we both saw it, but it was too far and to weak to show up on the the cell camera. It also appeared to have moved to out left somewhat visible between trees, we both saw two glowing lights side by side separated by what would be about 4 fingers from out distance, the lights looked like to glowing red beady eyes to us it was very eerie and left us with a very uneasy feeling,we continued to try to observe it, but we lost sight of it. I turned on the police scanner to see if there were any reports of anything unusual in the sky but there was not, the whole sighting lasted roughly about 8 Mins. Hopefully someone else saw this, that is reading this and perhaps can explain better what it was.
