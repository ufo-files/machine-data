I witnessed 25+ lights travel mainly in groups of 3. From low in the southern sky to the east taking different paths and variants in speed.

March 27th 2020 at around 8pm GMT. After star gazing I realised the stars I was watching were in fact moving, They would fade into view from the SW travelling from low in the sky towards the SE moving up. The lights would come in groups of 3. Once one group had faded away another group of 3 would appear and take a similar route but never exactly the same. Some would come in pairs travelling one above another at different speeds. Then one light came down between 2 of the lights taking the original route and fade away. This did not look like a shooting star. I also saw a shooting star fly between the objects a few moments before. These objects would take a route I would describe as controlled or "free". Unlike a plane or shooting star that has a set trajectory. These objects Would slowly change direction. It lasted about 20 minutes in total.
