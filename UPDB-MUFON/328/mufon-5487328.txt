{nd} 100+ starlight objects moving in formation towards same direction.

Was outside with my mother attempting to witness the Taurid Meteor Shower. We thought we spotted a satellite as there is not a lot of light pollution in the area of our residence. However, we shortly began to notice that there were five other similar objects following the first one with a decent amount of space between them. Within a few minutes, more and more of these objects appeared from the horizon of ENE, mostly appearing with only one other object in a formation of sort. As these objects slowly moved they began to become more irrational as time passed. These objects began to make erratic movements while staying in formation and following the flight path. There were a few single objects and shaped formations as big as 10 of these objects, one of which looked like a box with a tail. These objects frequently changed colors colors, and combined formations all while staying on the same path towards what seemed to be the WNW.
