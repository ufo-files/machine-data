Large Black Triangle appeared from large cloud over house, sunny day, there were no other clouds in sky.

In June 2012 it was a warm sunny day, i was in my back Garden, i was looking around the garden and looking at the sky, i noticed a large cloud to the east over some house's next to me, i thought that's strange a cloud on a day like this, as there were no other clouds in the sky, the cloud was very dark around the edge of it, then a large black triangle stated to move from it, as if it was inside and was coming out for a look, i could see it very clear, it had 3 white light's on it's corner's and a large red light in the middle of it, it stayed there for about a minute, it made no nose at all,i could see it very clearly move slowly back into the cloud that it came from, the size of the Triangle was about 300 ft and about 500 ft away from me, i stayed there for at least 10 mins,waiting to see if it would appear again, but it never did, but the cloud stayed there for at least 30 mins. I then went inside to get my mobile phone and called my friend who worked at Merseyside police, i told him what i had just seen, he told me to draw a picture of it and he would speak to me later that day. I have told many people over the past few year's about what i seen that day and no one believed me except my friend from the police.
This is what i seen on this day in June 2012.
