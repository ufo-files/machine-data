{cak} what i thought was a bright star bagan to wobble, then ascend in the air and back, and move back and forth

I was sitting in my porch swing looking at the stars and enjoying the cool evening. I was facing a very bright star and as I watched it wobbled back and forth-returning to its beginning station. Then it began to jerk back to the right, and return. Next what I saw made me catch my breath. It ascended steadily, then returned to its station. There was then no doubt that it was moving in precise lines. I went inside and got my husband out of bed to witness this, not an easy task since his work day begins at 6 am. We watched it for sometime before he had to go back to bed. I watched for 10 minute , or so, intervals, going into the house on occasion because the mosquitos were bad. Around 12:30 I was too sleepy and uncomfortable to watch anymore and I went to bed. The next night I looked for the light but it wasn't there, so it wasn't a star or planet or else it would be there.
