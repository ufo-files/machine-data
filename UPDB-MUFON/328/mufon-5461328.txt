{c ak} 11 Minute Video of UFO on ISS Live

I was watching the ISS Live feed and was switching back and forth from the SD CAMERA LIVE to the HD CAMERA LIVE....As I was watching the HD cam, I started seeing an array of lights start to appear one by one out in the distance, far beyond the ISS. The lights were an orange-yellow color and they illuminated 1 by 1, about every 3 seconds. They were seemingly lighting up around a circular shaped object which was not visible. I hurried and flipped on my screen recorder app. And started recording. ( As I am viewing the ISS Live on my cellphone.) I then went back to the ISS app (now recording my screen) and the lights that formed the circle were no longer there, so I quickly switched to the SD CAMERA LIVE to see if anything were in that screen, and sure enough, there was. And I was able to capture a 13 minute video of a UFO that moves in several different directions, hovers, proceeds to move about. At :06 seconds into the video, the object moves upward from a hovering position. At :32 seconds, It rises up again. At 1:38, the object darts off at high speed to the right and out of frame. At 2:26, the object comes back into view from the left side of the screen, just as I was switching to check the HD camera.(you can see it move in from the bottom left just before I switch to HD.) I saw nothing but a black screen on HD, so I switched back to SD and the object is hovering in the middle of the screen again. It hovers there for several minutes , while making small, erratic, multidirectional movements. Then at 10min.38sec.(10:38) of the video, the object darts off at high speed to the left of the screen and out of frame, once again.
