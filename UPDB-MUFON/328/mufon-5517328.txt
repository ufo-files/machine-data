(gs) Strange pattern of lights viewed hovering while driving down a road.

I was riding with my mother to take my niece home when I happened to look out the passenger window when I saw lights. I always do this automatically since its normally an airplane or a helicopter. This didn't appear to be either though, as it appeared to not be moving at all and had a strange pattern of lights, almost like a triangle but with a line coming out the top. I couldnt stop thinking about it. When I first saw the object, I immediately thought it was a helicopter of some kind, or a plane, but when it was just hovering, I knew something was off. It appeared to not make a noise, since my mother didn't see it at all, but I couldnt tell because of the music in the car. I was a little panicky when I realized that it most likely wasnt an airplane or a helicopter, but kept it to myself. We lost sight of the object not very long afterwards since we were continuing to drive. On our way back, I did look for the object, but it was gone, no trace of it anywhere in the distance.
