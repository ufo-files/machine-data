{cak} Green red and orange flashing lights hovering almost like scanning the area

This morning around 3.30am I went and sat outside to stargaze whilst having a cup of tea as I was struggling to sleep. 
The sky was clear but to the North I spotted what I first thought was an airplane. But the colours were green and red mostly but with some orange. I assumed it was a plane until it flew across the sky then stopped, scaled back the path in which it flew, then abruptly flew up a few meters and it kept doing this over and over again as if it were looking for something or scanning the area. 
At first it was silent. After a while I noticed a hum as it drew closer. 
I could have watched it for the rest of the morning but my phone instantly died from having 85% battery life. And then my baby cried so I had to tend to her. 

The shape was triangular but it was all made up of lights. 
And honestly the atmosphere was eerily quite. Other than the hum.
