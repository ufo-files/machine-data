Giant Cross Structure Over Sun and green orb

We were coming home in Portland Oregon and we live on the Columbia River. My daughter wanted to take a picture of the sun because it was about to set. She had the Live Photo feature on her iPhone turned on when she was taking the picture. We noticed a cloud that looks like a dog and wanted to get it in the picture. We didn’t see anything unusual when looking with the naked eye. When we got inside she looked at the picture and noticed something black above the sun so she zoomed in and could see a giant cross structure above the sun. I held the picture to see the short video from the Live Photo feature and noticed that the structure did not move with the camera has she moved. But also noticed there was a green orb with a honey comb pattern and floating and making quick movements at the top right of the video. You can zoom in on the sun in the picture to the see the cross structure. You can also see the green orb on the picture. I also made a looped video of the short Live Photo it’s looped three times. You can see the cross structure doesn’t move with the camera and the green orb at the top right of the screen is moving in and out of frame. None of this was seen with the naked eye.
