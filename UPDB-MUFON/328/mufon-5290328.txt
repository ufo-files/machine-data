REDISH ORANGE LIGHT

JAN 2001 TIME 05.05PM  I WAS IN MY KITCHEN AT THIS TIME MAKING A CUP OF TEA , WHEN I NOTICED A BRIGHT ORANGEY RED LIGHT MOVING IN THE SKY , I THEN WENT OUT TO HAVE A BETTER LOOK AT IT ,I THEN REMEMBERED THAT I HAD SEEN THIS BEFORE WHEN I WAS 14 YEARS OLD , I THEN RUSHED UPSTAIRS TO MY FRIENDS ROOM AND SAID TO HIM TO LOOK OUT THE WINDOW AND TELL ME WHAT THAT IS , HIS ANSWER WAS I DONT HAVE A CLUE, SO WE BOTH THEN WENT DOWN THE STAIRS AND OUTSIDE TO HAVE A BETTER LOOK, AS WEE WAS LOOKING AT IT AS IT WENT OVER HEAD I SAID TO HIM CAN YOU HEAR ENY THING COMING OFF IT , TO THIS HE SAID NO NOTHING NOT A SOUND , I THEN RAN IN TO MY HOUSE TO GET MY PHONE FOR TO TRY AND GET A PHOTO OF IT , BUT BY THIS TIME I WAS TOLATE IT WAS GONE , A FRIEND OF WILLIAMS CALLED HIM THE NEXT DAY TO TELL HIM THAT THEY HAD SEEN IT ALSO , SO ALL I KNOW IS IT WAS JAN THE 1ST AT 05.05PM I SEEN IT IN THE SKY COMING FROM 317NW AND LEAVING FROM 370 S , AND ALL THIS LASTED FOR 2 MIN  NO LIGHT BEEMS NO TAIL NO SOUND NO FLASHING LIGHTS NO BALLOONS AND DEFINITELY NOT SWAMP GAS . SIG A.E.HARKINS PS. IM SORRY I DIDN'T GET A PHOTO OF THIS
