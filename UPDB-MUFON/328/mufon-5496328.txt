{dr}Series of for sets of lights, changing color and intensity circling around the city

received a text message asking if I had seen the UFO's over the city, Went outside with binoculars and I was able to locate 3 sets of lights, they seemed to circle each other occasionally hovering in place with the lights shifting from white to yellow to orange then red. Initially the lights looked like they could be from a plane but then did not follow color or flight patterns from airplanes. I called another friend about a mile away who also watched the lights with me for about 30 minutes. During that time a fourth set of lights appeared. Eventually the lights just dissipated.
