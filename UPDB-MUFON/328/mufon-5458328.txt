{jpg} Fireball/orange orb in sky

I went out on my deck around 11:40. Right away I noticed a fireball/orange orb. Definitely larger than a star and it was moving one way then the other and up down. Not sharply but like it didn't know where it wanted to go but then started to just move to the left. I was able to take a photo and a video. The photo was taken at 11:43 exactly. After the video I went inside to grab my boyfriend. He came outside and saw it. Seconds after it went left and down by a tree and just dissapeared. Im not sure how long the encounter lasted but I feel like a few minutes. It was like nothing I have ever seen before!
