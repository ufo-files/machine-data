(GS) Black brown bean object heading east over head-went to high elevation for plane landing at DIA

I was in the parking lot near the corner of Illif and Chambers in 80014 talking to my friend facing NNE and out of the corner of my left eye I saw an object and then looked directly up. It was about the size of 3 quarters side by side. black with swirls/stripes of dark brown. At first for the first few seconds it was a round ballon shape then looked like a butter bean. If you look at the side profile of a bean and it was heading from left to right, the left side/back of it slightly dipped down and picked up speed. I would imagine it was about 40-100 feet above us. We continued to watch it as it flew directly east slowly rising in altitude picking up speed very smoothly. I would guestimate about 15-20 sec later it rose smoothly and fast to a higher elevation. I wondered why and then saw an airplane come into my view traveling from south to north that I assume was landing at Denver International Airport. In my opinion it rose in elevation to miss the airplane. It was 2 quarters higher than the airplane at the time. The airplane passed behind some trees and the object continued its path into the eastern distance and disappeared into a little pin dot. I felt a surrealness come over me, an awakening once I first saw it slightly tilt back. I believe I saw an object I cannot explain other than as I have wrote here.
