@ Star like objects. White lights with blue, green, and red flickering in center. One to the east and one to the west of me.

I saw a bright object to the SE of me while standing on my front porch and one to the west. Object appears first to look like a star. But looks more like 2 stars one on top of another. But is larger and appears closer than stars. They have a green, blue and red light in the center which seems to flicker a pattern. When I woke up at sunrise the one to the west of me was still present. The other had gone.
