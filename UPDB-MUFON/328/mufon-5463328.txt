{cak} Triangular ufo, 3 red lights zigzaged 3 times then disappeared over horizon.

I was heading to the store around 7pm 1/7/2019 in Buena Park California, United States. I noticed movement to the east, looking towards Knotts Berry Farm. Above the horizon I noticed an object wobbling. It then zigzaged three times and then shot in an arc over the horizon and disappeared. This all happened in around eight seconds. Each of the zigzag movements covered a great distance. The object was triangular and had three very bright red orb shaped lights, one at each corner of the triangle. The triangle itself looked black. At one point the atmosphere around it looked disturbed like heat rising from the road. It did not move like a plane,and it wasn't a meteor. I immediately thought of it was a ufo. I was excited concerning the situation, and told two people immediately in my home. This is my first ufo sighting.
