I was watching a lone strange looking cloud. It looked very compact, oval, and was moving toward the east as it became larger and larger because of the proximity. It was a glowing pink although the sky did not show pink. All of a sudden a craft came out of the south end. It was triangular and a dark gray. It did not fly away...just stayed in the same position. I turned to go into the house for some reason. I was not afraid but I felt I must go in. I went in and was upset with myself and went back out. The cloud was gone and so was the UFO. This is not the first time I have seen a UFO in this area. I know we are close to several military bases so I try to remember that. But...this one made me feel strange and was strange.
