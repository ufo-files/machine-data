It was a bright light south east of the sun. It looked like a smaller version of the sun with a prism of light going through it.

I was getting off the 134 East at Figueroa in Eagle Rock, Ca. I was startled by the bright light in the sky with a prism of light going through it. I was called by a friend to ask if I was in the area and what direction to look. He stated that he had been looking at it for approximately an hour and couldn't figure out what it was. When I first saw it I thought it was the sun and then gasped out loud when I realized the sun was to the left of the object. It was quite a bit smaller than the sun, but at first glance I thought it was the sun because it was that bright.
