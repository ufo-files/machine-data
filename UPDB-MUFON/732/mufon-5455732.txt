{rjl} Five Reddish Orange objects in

While visiting my grandparents in Albuquerque, New Mexico, my mother, father, myself and grandparents were sitting outside their trailer. All of a sudden my mother jumped up and said "O my god George, what is that and pointed at the sky. The reddish orange saucers were flying over head at an extreme rate and were not in our view for long. They were moving north to south. My father was an Army Warrant Officer at that time and just said, Thats probably the military doing some experiments with aircraft. I have never forgotten that sighting. I always knew it was special. I was about 16 years old then and still in high school.
