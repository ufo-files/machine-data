Glowing spinning disc

My wife and I was sitting in the back garden just having a drink and a cigarette. I was looking up at the night sky as it was clearish kind of night with some clouds when I noticed a bright light coming towards our direction at a good rate of speed. I said to my wife look at that and she said go and get your phone, which I did and started to record but by the time I started recording the object was starting to go beyond my sight so I changed position so I could still see it in my camera. I zoomed in on the object and could clearly see it was a glowing disc pulsating as it was flying across the sky. I did think to myself could it be a satellite or something but it was flying below the clouds so it had to be something else. It definitely wasn’t a plane or helicopter there was no sound at all. I do see quite a few things flying over we’re we live quite often but this was definitely the best I have seen.
