(GS) In Allentown once again facing Eadt toward Dublin

So I just went outside to watch the meteor shower, which was pretty cool. But the really cool part was all the ufos flying everywhere. I brought out my dads night vision binoculars to see them better and there was groups of them just blinking away all over the place. They continued on just blinking on and off sometimes in triangular shapes other times in long straight lines (usually about 6-7 in a line). The blinking was a white blink. After about 10 minutes a few of the crafts in one of the larger groups became very bright glowing red/orange. Not nearly as bright as the one I saw about a week ago though but bright enough for my mom to scream holy s**t. lol They only got bright for a short time this time a couple of seconds at best. Not flares though from where the house is when they lite up bright they were very far apart from each other at different times and always around a group of 5-10 when they would do it. After about 20 minutes of watching them a bunch of jets came flying through and continued circling the area for around 45 minutes sometimes in the same area it seemed were these things were still blinking. I tried to get video but my wifes camera phone could not record anything from the distance we were from them. Second time we saw them at this time of night in about a week.
