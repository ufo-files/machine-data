Big orange ball of light that hovered above the tree line for approximately 3 minutes, flashed once and appeared to be sucked away.

My husabnd and I were sitting in the drive way in our car smoking a cigarette. He looked over to his right, south west direction and noticed it hovering there. He told me to look, so I did. It looked like a ball of orange light hovering about 500 feet away in the top of the tree line above the mountain. At first thought, it looked like the top of the trees were on fire but it wasnt. We watched it hover there for about 3 minutes. It flashed once and then my husband said it looked it went into "warpspeed". He said it disappeared quick into a small dot.
