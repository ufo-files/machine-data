{jpg} UFO directed light beam on group.

I was at a campout talking to some friends, while looking up at the sky. I noticed a light that seemed far away slowly move towards our position. At first I thought it was a helicopter, becuase of the flight pattern I was observing. But as it came closer there was no sound. I started getting excited and yelling to another group of friends that where in a tent but they just laughed and shrugged it off. When my attention turned back to the craft, it seemed so high up in the sky. Then instantly it was directly above me and 2 others, shinning a beam of light down directly at us. It couldn't have been more then 50'ft above us. We were mezmerized by the light, then all of a sudden it shut off. Once the light shut off the craft took off, straight up, really high, and then slowly made it's way south untill it dissappear ed from sight. The funny thing was right after the light went out there was a short bald man standing right beside us. My eyes went wide as I looked at my friends in disbelief. He came out of no where. He asked us if we had any smoked salmon in a russian accent. Turned out he was with another group camped there. It was all just so random though. We thought he was beamed down or something, none of us saw him walk up, we were so transfixed with the light above us. Anyway, I have had several strange encounters, and witnessed lots of other high strangeness in the skys since this event.
