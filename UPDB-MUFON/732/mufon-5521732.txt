2 Orange-Red Orbs flew from North to South, 1 climbed altitude very fast and disappeared, 1 hovered for 15 seconds then climbed altitude fast away.

We had 5 persons outside watching 2 red-orange orbs fly from the North(Tampa direction) to the South to us(Sarasota). They both flew a straight path 90% of the way seen, then 1 turned Eastward toward us. The 1 that flew straight shortly after the other turned climbed in altitude very fast and disappeared in about 3-5 seconds. The orb that turned flew toward us for about 10 seconds stopped and hovered for about 10-15 seconds and then it also climbed in altitude very fast and disappeared in about 5 seconds.
