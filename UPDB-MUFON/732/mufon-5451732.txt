A group of lights in the sky that hovered then flew off

I have a video. It was a group of lights from 3-6 that hovered for about 10 -15 minutes than flew off. They WERE NOT fireworks. They were far above and moved like controlled aircrafts of some sort. They seemed to be in formations in groups of three. I had no fear at all and felt very much at peace...excited definitely but no fear. 

Earlier in the day there was a strange hummingbird that kept coming and hovering above my head but it seemed different than typical hummingbirds and I even commented that it seemed different like it was another species or something. I remember thinking it looked somewhat fake. I have no idea if there is a connection between the two events but both were strange and happening the same day.
