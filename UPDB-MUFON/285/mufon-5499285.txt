{cak} Visible white dot roughly a little larger than a star would be at night appearing at noon on a cloudless day

Spotted the object in the sky at lunch time. I looked up to find an airliner flying over and spotted the object really far away and not moving. I was not the only witness to it. Others could spot the object as well and could even photograph and video the object. The object appeared to remain stationary for the duration. It was as if there was a star in the sky in the middle of the day time even though it was noon. The object was referenced against a building and could not determine any movement or distance away from the place being observed. Spotted a few other objects in the sky around the same area that looked metallic, but they only lasted roughly 2-3 seconds. The solid white dot appeared to not move. I thought the object was possibly a weather balloon but after about 20 minutes of observation, the object did not move and was not gaining in intensity or altitude.
