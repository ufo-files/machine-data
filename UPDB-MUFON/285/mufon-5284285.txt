Saw 8 lights slow down then float and disappear

Myself and my 19 yo son were sitting on the back porch when we seen a orange light that I thought at first might be a plane. But it didn't have blinking lights. Noticed a second one that looked like it was slowing down. Then it blinked out of sight. When down onto the yard to catch sight of the first on again then noticed 4 more. After recording that went inside to talk with family. Came back out and there were 2 more. All of them eventually blinked out. There was no noise. The lights were steady, going NW to SE. I used the compass on my phone.
