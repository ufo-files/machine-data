Glowing Hovering Object SE Direction Ejects Object Turns to 2 Turn Back to 1 Hover Then N

735/740PM on March 10th, 2022
On Patio after dinner and see bright object in between the trees 
SE direction miles out. At first I thought it was a plane coming directly towards me giving me the illusion it is one bright light, the light never blinked and only stayed the same hovering. All of a sudden after about 1 minute of me looking it., it ejected an object to the right making two smaller orb/objects. They were smaller now that they were separate. At this point, I was so in awe I couldn't move and slowly walked backwards, never taking my eyes off of the objects, they still hovered and the lights were still not blinking or making any sound. I knocked on the patio door a few times trying to get my boyfriends attention. He came out and he saw the two lights hovering and after another minute or so of us looking at them the right object went back into the left object and it traveled North rapidly. We walked around trying to follow it and then saw another 2 light objects side by side white/yellowish orbs with a tint or orange (Just like the other ones) moving rapidly N. Within minutes 3 Helicopters were up in the air, two around the fields behind our apartments circling and another headed in the Northern direction towards the lights
