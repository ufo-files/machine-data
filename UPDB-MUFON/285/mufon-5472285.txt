{rjl} Bright orb appeared in front of vehicle then vanished

I was leaving my neighborhood just after dark, as soon as I got to the main road I stopped at the stop sign and a bright object appeared right in front of my car about the size of a basketball. At first I thought it was someone shining a spot light at me from the field across the road, this lasted about 10 seconds before it vanished. I made my turn and started to feel strange about it. It then dawned on me that if it had been a spot light, the interior of my vehicle would have been illuminated, and it was not. It was an orb about the size of a basketball and bright blueish/white in color.
