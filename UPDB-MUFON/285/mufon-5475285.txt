{cak} What started as a shooting star quickly turned into a bright copper light that stairstepped down to horizon leaving a PERFECT copper stairstep trail then slowed down and lost sight behind neighbors house.

I was sitting in my work shed petting my cat on my lap when I saw a shooting star high in the sky through the open door of my shed. I grabbed my cat and ran outside to get a better look. I took about 5 steps watching the falling star when it quickly turned into a copper light moving fast horizontally and then straight down vertically then horizontally again. It did this 3 times stair stepping down to tree level and slowed way down to almost a stop as it went behind my neighbors house horizontally where I couldn't see it any more. I waited for it to reappear on the other side of the house, but it didn't. That's when I thought it had stopped or landed and realized just how close this thing is and fear came over me. I ran upstairs, woke my wife up and told her what happened. I was wondering what to do. Should I call 911? Instead I sat on my bed staring out the french doors at the last place I saw it and waited to see if this was possibly an invasion of some kind.

The speed with witch it moved across the sky and the stair stepping is technology I know we don't have on earth.

The copper trail was bright and remained visible for the entire event. Each horizontal and vertical segment looked perfect and the same length.

I was amazed at how the light looked so far away at first and in seconds it was here in my neighborhood.
