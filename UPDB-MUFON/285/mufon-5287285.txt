Three large bright lights above South Longmont

I was driving home with my friend when we turned on a southbound road in Longmont, CO. I saw three large, bright lights hovering in the distance over a visible tree line. I asked my friend if she saw it too, and she answered, "yes". I told her I thought there could be four but just saw three. I said this because the lights slowly dipped behind the tree tops and where at various levels. My friend said they looked stationary, but if you watched more than a few seconds they were in different positions, going lower, going higher, going under one another, and one moved in a directional path East after a minute or so. They were unlike anything in the sky. I told my friend they could be helicopters, but the number and size of them did not make sense. I saw airplanes nearby, and they were much smaller and consisted of more than one light. These... lights, were larger than all other lights in the area. They moved, and were a dull, yellow orange. We lost sight of them after trying to drive towards them. Also of note, the Longmont Airport was to our West. These lights did not head that way. I'm posting because I want to know if anyone else saw them, and/or got a better look. I've seen lights in the sky before, I've also flown aircraft before, but this was very curious to me.
