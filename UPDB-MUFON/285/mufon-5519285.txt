Photographed by accident while taking photos of a falcon.

I was taking pictures of my dachshund with my Nikon D3100. I had the camea set on sport mode and continuious shutter release (3fps). While taking pictures of the puppy, I noticed a falcon flying among the power lines above us, and decided to try and get a few pictures of it as well. I noticed nothing at the time, but when I got home and put the pictures on the computer and enlarged them, I noticed something strange. In the first picture that I will attach, you will see the two white objects directly behind the falcon.
