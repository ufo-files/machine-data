It was anywhere from around 6ft to around 15ft? orb shaped. Was solid ball of light

It was around 10:30pm to 11pm. I was giving my friend a ride home after work. We came up to an intersection and pulled into the left turning lane, apprx 4 cars back, and waited for the light to change. There were about 20 other vehicles at the intersection. While waiting, a glowing bright white orb came flying in, at great speed, and stopped and hovered about 100-200ft above the intersection. We both asked each other "What the heck is that!" as we got out of the car. About two dozen other people at the intersection also got out of their car. A few people were screaming "What is that!" and "Oh my God!". While we all were watching the object whisped to the left and up apprx 30ft, then down approx 30 ft, then right and up approx 30ft, then centered and hovered. It hovered for about 20 secs and then, with extreme speed, took off North. Everyone got back in their cars. We continued to wait for the light to change, it had changed green while we were all watching the object. While waiting, a black helicopter came flying by, about the same height above the intersection, chasing after the object. The helicopter was extremely slow compared to the object, there was no way it would catch up.
