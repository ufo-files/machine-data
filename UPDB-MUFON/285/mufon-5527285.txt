spinning multi colored orbs

my youngest child and I were on the patio of our apartments and all of a sudden in the field adjacent to us, I were mesmerized by some beautiful, spinning, multicolored orbs. These were not fireworks. One way to describe this would be watching some sort of energy, spinning and building up speed in a circular motion, horizontal to the ground, lifting and then lowering after becoming larger as they spun, and smaller around as it lowered again disappearing into the ground. This is so hard to describe, wish i could somehow make a moving diagram for all to see, It was beautiful..almost like a hologram, very colorful orbs. I always look to see if it will happen again, but nothing yet.
