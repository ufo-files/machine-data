(GS) Light shooting across southern california sky 11/15/2012 11:47pm Pac time

I was just getting home from my girlfirends house and I was getting out of my car at my parents house. Something told me to look up for a few minutes and see what you can see, out of no where from the North Western part of the sky I was looking at a beam of some sort of light shooting across the sky very fast making extremely sharp turns slowing down near a patch of clouds, I saw a flash relly quick and it shot back across the same area of sky making sharp and abrupt turns again then to disappear.
