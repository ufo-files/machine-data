Witnessed close up UFO while flying with father from Charlotte to Richmond Virginia.

While flying to Richmond Virgina from Charlotte North Carolina with my father we witnessed a UFO fly by our personally owned plane. The object was a ball of light which slowed to match our aircrafts speed and direction. When this happened all the electrical equipment in the vehicle turned off. After matching our course and speed for about 10 - 15 seconds the UFO accelerated rapidly until is disappeared from view moving slightly away from our aircrafts course by a few degrees. As soon as the UFO flew away from us everything electrical in our aircraft immediatly turned on again and operated as normal.
