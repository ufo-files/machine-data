STARLIKE AND FIREBALLS

I WAS OUTSIDE TAKING OUT MY TRASH AND I SAW 2 LIKE FIREBALLS COME PASS ME REALLY FAST LIKE ABOUT 500 FT AWAY FROM ME AND THE OTHER ONE PASSED OVER MY HEAD AND IT DISSAPEARED. I WENT AND GOT MY TELESCOPE AND BINOCULARS AND WENT TO MY BACK YARD. ALMOST EVERYDAY I HAVE SEEN STARLIKE LIGHTS THAT ALL OF SUDDEN START MOVING FROM SIDE TO ANOTHER AND JUST DISAPPEAR. THIS HAPPENS ABOUT 3-4 DIFFERENT TIMES SAME NIGHT. ITS LOOKS LIKE A STAR MOVING. I ALWAYS CALL MY SON AND WIFE TO SEE THEM. THEY SEE THE LIGHTS BUT THEY SAY ITS A PLAIN OR MY SON SAYS THEY ARE SATELLITES. I DONT HAVE ANY PICTURES OR VIDEO CAUSE THEY ARE TOO SMALL TO CAPTURE.
