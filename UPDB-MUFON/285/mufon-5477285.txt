{jpg} 3 lights in a large triangle shape

As I was driving home around 11:20 pm on Wed March 28, 2017, I noticed 3 large orbs in the shape of a triangle in the sky. The area I live is very tree heavy and the object kept disappearing amongst the trees. I drove in the direction closer towards them, which was near the downtown Minneapolis area. I followed them for about 15mins until I couldn't get a good view anymore. This was not the first time I've seen this shape before. They were huge, white/ light yellowish lights formed into a large,wide set triangle. It was very slow moving and in perfect uniform.
