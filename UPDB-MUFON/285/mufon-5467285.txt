{td} HUGE Hovering Orange Lights Over Ocean

My sister and I were on the balcony of our condominium. It started with one oval-shaped bright orange light. More ovals lit up in a perfect row, systematically, the same distance apart, and one by one. All in all, I think there five bright orange ovals and then it was GONE. It made no sound the entire time and then it was just GONE, also with no sound.

It was extremely large and about 1.5 to 2 miles out to see. It's hard to tell the scale of things over the vastness of the ocean, especially in the nighttime; but it was at least several football fields wide, could have even been a mile wide. All I can say is that it was ENORMOUS and awe-inspiring. I have no idea what this thing was, but it was definitely not of this world. It was not jets in formation. It was not something coming from out in space like a shooting star. 

We talked to other people on the beach later in the week. I just said, "were you outside Monday night at around 10:30/11?" And one woman answered, "No, I wasn't; but if you're talking about the aliens, my daughter and her two friends saw them. They came inside freaking out!" There were different exchanges like this with at least five different groups of people laying around us on the beach.

I know that aliens exist and that they come here, but I never thought that I would see anything like this in my lifetime. I am a songwriter and it was so inspiring, I actually wrote a parody of a popular Disney song to commemorate the experience. I would love to get it on youtube someday. Thanks for the forum to share these events. Never knew this site existed before! I also reported to the National UFO Reporting Center.
