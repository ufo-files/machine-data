{cak} My abduction story

When I was a kid, I was laying in my bed and something awoke me from my sleep. There was a bright light outside my window that was pulsating, I looked outside and can remember a bright light. Than I felt like I was instantly was apart from myself. I dont remember much of the event but i do remember being on a table, and feeling as tho I was going fast through the universe, feeling as thought there was like no time there in relation to earth. Than, the next thing I remember is being back in my bed frozen and couldnt move, and very scared. The years that followed i was scared to sleep, and having severe documented migraines for years. The doctors did an MRI on me and found out I had something that had to be removed in my head. Since the surgery about 3 years after the incident, i havn't had migraines. I do believe this incident sparked this issue.
