Oval Shaped Glowing Object

On April 12th 2020 ,at Ottapalam, Palakkad district, Kerala State, around 8:15 pm, I saw a white oval shaped craft move from North West to South East. I thought it was a plane but noticed that the standard red beacon lights weren't flashing, it wasn't moving very fast but when it was at the south east it was moving in a zig zag direction and again moving straight at the same altitude. I checked the flight radar app to see if there were any any aircrafts flying over my area or in kerala at that time , but there were none especially after the Covid-19 lockdown announcement.
Even checked the ISS tracker, it wasn't flying overhead at that time.
