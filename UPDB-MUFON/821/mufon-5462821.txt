{tjd} Orangeish red Glowing saucer shaped craft with white ring of light directly around craft

I was at my friend's house we were outside late at night looking at the stars, laying on a trampoline I noticed something stationary in the sky that was less than a mile from us it was saucer shaped with a dome on the very top direclty around the saucers material it was made from there was a white glow then completely around the craft there was an orangeish red glow. The craft hovered for about 15 minutes we were very afraid as we have never seen anything like it before. It darted from left to right, the craft instantly shot straight up in the air very quickly and disappeared right before our eyes. We stayed outside for an hour or more after that trying to find it again but could not find it.
