(GS) 10 or so lights in a group of

The first one we seen was over laplasance rd. Theres an outlet mall there along with a couple of gas stations and fast food restaurants. We were heading back from Toledo on North I-75 and seen 10 or so lights in a group of "v" formation. It was dark out & The lights were low and almost as soon as my wife and I seen them they began moving west and all but 3-4 lights turned off. The lights then started dimming until we couldnt see them. The object or objects in formation moved smoothly and did not climb in altitude. My wife couldnt believe it and felt scared and sick, I was nervous, but excited and wanted to see if we could see anything else. We got off on the next exit, front street, and was heading west, towards monroe st. When we seen another group of bright lights obstructed by the houses. I turned south going down Wadsworth and the lights were still being obstructed by the houses and were heading west. I turned west down E.5th and then south down laplasance. Thats when the lights again about ten but in an upside down "t" formation passed over very low. The lights were heading south and began accelerating. I followed them and they began heading west over the quarry.I turned west onto jones and the lights began turning off like the other and it began blinking. They dimmed out just like the other until we could not see them. I pulled over at Navarre field to see if we could see anything else. We didn't see anything so just drove off.
