Clear sky and no airplanes in the sky.. I saw an orb of light which did not blink and eventually disappeared as if it was never there.

In the early morning I was able to observe an object which had a glowing light was flying with a low speed over the Hague and was clearly observable amongst the stars. With 20 years of experience observing the sky, and having seen objects like this before, I had to report this observation. The most amazing thing about the observation was that the Lions giving orb actually suddenly disappeared without any logical reason. The sky was without clouds and filled with stars so there was no logical reason for me not to be able to observe its trajectory. After long evaluation I chose the report this phenomenon. It is surely not the most spectacular observation above The Netherlands but clearly is significant one.
