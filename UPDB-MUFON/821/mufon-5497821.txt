{rjl} Large neon green fireball, encapsulated by bright red, with white streak trail falling toward earth.

I was driving south at 2350, and witnessed a bright neon green fireball encapsulated in a bright red color with a white streak trailing behind. The object appeared almost firework like, although did not explode into color. It appeared as a falling star, but of much more vibrant colors. The object traveled from northeast to southwest toward the earth. It is unknown if the object actually struck earth as it fell behind the trees as I was driving. There was no noticeable noise, or electronic disturbance. The entire event lasted approximately 5 seconds. I was quite fascinated by the object.
