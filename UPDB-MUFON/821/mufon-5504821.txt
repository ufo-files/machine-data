{cak} Triangular shaped object with 3 lights hovering over the treetops

My mother, sister and myself were driving home north on hwy 17 leaving out of charleston SC.. As we were driving around midnight we saw a triangular like object with lights traveling north as well.. I was looking out from the right side passenger window when the object seemed to have stopped move ing and hovered in one spot for about a minute. We pulled off the side of the highway to get a better look.. At this time I have not taken my eyes off this object.. And out of no where the object disappeared.. It was a clear and cold night driving on the coast and I wanted to add that this object was very close too a cell tower as well.
