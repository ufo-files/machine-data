i saw up to twenty bright, orange lights that made geometric patterns

i was sitting in my van camped out in the desert about a mile south of quartzsite az. i looked out of my van into the sky and saw two orange lights above the town. i got out of the van and looked again and there was nothing there so i sat back down in my van. about five minutes past and then i heard the loud sound of a jet flying over me. i looked in the sky to see if i could see it and that was when i saw two orange lights hovering above town. they would go on for about five seconds and then disappear for about ten seconds. this happened three times and then all of a sudden, approximately eight of the lights appeared bunched up in a circular pattern for about five seconds and then went off for five seconds then approx. twenty lights appeared two at a time one on top of the other and marqee style lit up and then went out. watched for another hour or so and glimpsed two orange lights in the distance and then it was over. while all this was going on and right after the jet flyover , i observed two helicopters flying in sync going east and west across the valley back and forth, like they were looking for something approx. six or seven times
