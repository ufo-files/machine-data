Thought it was a drone.

Observed an object NW of my location that appeared to be a drone moving slowly from south to north in a cloudless morning sky. The object made no noise or quick movements. The object seemed to be very high altitude for a drone due to flight path being very near air traffic taking off and landing from a nearby airport. After observing through binoculars, the object did not appear to be a drone. Object did not look like anything I have ever seen in the sky. Difficult to describe, but had either lights or light reflections and really had no shape. Grabbed camera and got 1 good photo of object with 200mm lens. Object seemed to keep constant speed and direction until out of visible sight. Object seemed to be gaining altitude slowly from my perspective. Soon after the object was out of visible sight an airplane came by on a north to south path approaching the nearby airport but the airplane seemed to be a lot lower altitude than the unidentified object.
