Life changing!

I was at the Local Rancho Santa Margarita Lake in Orange County California.

On new years day, on 1/1/2017 at midnight to 1AM in the morning, I was going for a quick walk around the local lake, and I saw these "Lights" in the side of my vision, so then I looked and then I fully saw them, there was about 6 of these "Lights" at first, and they were going away from the local forest/mountains, they were flying away slowly, and THEY CHANGED DIRECTION RAPIDLY.

And after that, 2 more of the SAME lights came from the forest/mountains, But these 2 moved faster than the others, but a little off of the same path of the others.

When I first saw these "Lights" I thought they were just helicopters, but then I remembered that there were no Airports or Bases around Rancho Santa Margarita, and there's not a lot of helicopters nor airplanes that fly buy.

And when they got about a mile away from the forest/mountains they vanished from plain sight. the 2 others got about a half a mile further than the others and did the same.
