{rjl} Parked in convertible top down. Girl screamed. It was overhead. Moved North. Stopped. Disappeared in an instant.

My buddy and I were parked with two girls in my convertible with the top down on Cemetary road just Northeast of Ladysmith, WI. It was late, perhaps between 10:00 PM and 11:00 PM. One girl screamed and pointed at the object hoovering just above the tree top level and the tree tops were moving back and forth like a strong wind was whipping them. It was a misty night so the object seemed a little blurred but appeared to have dark spots on the hull like windows. We were young (I was 16). We were scared so I started the car and hit the gas heading into town where the local sheriff ran a drive-in restaurant. I believe he was called 'Woody' and the restaurant was called the 'Tinker Tot'. It's still there but closed for decades. We told the sheriff what had happened and another couple came up to us and stated that they had seen it rise from a swamp a little earlier while they were parked off the road. We all raced back to the sighting spot and found that it had moved North up the road were the road took a sharp turn left (West) The object was hovering again near a farm. At that time I counted 16 people observing it (most were kids). One person went into a farmer's house to call the Air Force and said the farmer and family were frantic. I remember the names of some of the kids but several have passed away by now. I'm 74 years old and should have reported it a long time ago. The incident was reported in the Ladysmith news and they asked what we were doing out by the cemetary.
