Rectangular transparent object spinning through sky

It's really quite difficult to describe it was a rectangular 'block' that appeared across the sky distorting the stars behind it giving us the impression it was rolling or tumbling across the sky. It was definitely transparent as we could see the stars behind it, although it had a dark outline. Once we noticed it was visible for about 5 seconds moving quickly. It's the strangest thing I've ever seen in my life and if it wasn't for my wife seeing it, I wouldn't have trusted my own eyes.
