Neon Blue lighted, rotating UFO, Mason, Ohio.

Looked like Drone, but way too fast, and had BRIGHT BLUE & some Green lights, so not airplane. Unusual strobe pattern and had to be going a few 100 MPH. No noise and almost looked like it boomeranged across the sky. Was not any more than a mile up. It was too fast and unusual to be a Drone. Location 1/2 hour from wright Patterson AF base, in a suburban subdivision near some woods & lake and 25 min from Cincinnati. 

I was parking the car when I saw it and drove up the street and ran out to try to capture it on camera. Unfortunately, my phones lock screen held me up from capturing it. From the time i got out and ran 100 ft to get around my neighbors house to try to see it again, it was gone. 
Within 30 seconds - 1 minute, it was from within a few thousand feet to gone. And the path was curved as well. It could have been boomerang or triangle, but it was rotating. With very bright neon blue lights and maybe some green.

Have seen a few other UFO's in the area in the past few years, at least 2 triangles with no flickering lights/ unusual on/off lights or 3 bright white lights. Also have seen a bunch of orbs one time in the sky, but that may have been AF exercise. Have also seen black dot cross the sky about 5 years back, around similar time to the orbs. 

Lot's of crazy s**t every now and then here in Northern Cincinnati region of Ohio. Especially with Right Patterson near here.
