silver sphere hovering over roof tops near our house

on approximately march 10th 2010 (unsure of actual time and date). me and my brother were standing on our drive way having a cigarette. when we noticed what at first we thought was a balloon. we observed a spherical object, it was stationary.which in itself was strange as it was quite windy that day. it had a bright silver/grey appearance, it looked the size of a basketball, it must have been larger in diameter than it looked to us due to the distance from us. it made no sound what so ever. me and my brother both looked at each other and looked back at the roof line where we both saw this object was and it had vanished without trace. to this day we have absolutely no idea what it was that we saw.
