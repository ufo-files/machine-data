Red light UFO sighted from the ground

While jogging I saw a red light in the sky travelling SW in line with the horizon. I've recorded it with my cell phone camera. It was looking like a solid red light, yet in the video it appears to be somewhat pulsating. I can only estimate it was flying from Warsaw direction towards the city of Lodz. The siting took place on Feb. 27th 2017 at 6:11 PM GMT+1 near the city of Lowicz (Lodzkie). I lost sight of the object while it flew behind the clouds.
