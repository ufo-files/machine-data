{cak} flashing light in the sky, every 10-12 seconds will little to no movement of the object.

I put my dogs out around 5:30am when I noticed a bright flash in the sky off to my right. I focused on the spot. I could not see any object in the sky. There were no clouds. Then around 10-12 seconds later it flashed again. It was a white light; like turning on a flashlight. Once it turned off, I could not spot any object. It seemed to be pretty high in the sky. This went on for a good 10-15 min. It would flash every 10-12 seconds. It seemed to change location when it flashed, but not by much. After about 10-min it seemed to slowly steer east. At that point I lost sight of the object. One last thing, when it flashed, it was brighter than any object in the sky.
