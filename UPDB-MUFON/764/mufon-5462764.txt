Looked like stars flying around each others

My brother was graduating from basic training from Fort Knox and it was a long drive, so my dad and I left during the night. Somewhere between 11p and 1a. My dad was handicapped, so I waited by the car and stated looking up at the stars. When he finally got to the car, I pointed them out so I knew I wasn't imagining things. His response dumbfounded me. He looked up and saw the lights dancing in the sky, and said "Yeah. They do that all the time."
