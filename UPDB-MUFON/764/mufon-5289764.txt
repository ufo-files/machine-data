Glowing orb moving erratically then shoots straight up at a very high speed.

I was a forward air controller in the Air Force and have 29 years in the aviation business including some pilot training. I opened the garage awaiting my wife to come home from her day as a flight attendant. I saw a glowing orb in the eastern sky. I took a picture but it was difficult since it was moving erratically. I took a video of it and hope the link I'm sending will open for you. It was taken on an I-phone. The orb went left to right, forward and back then shot straight up in the sky at a very high speed. It looked much larger than the picture shows...like a baseball. What's strange is in the still picture there are trees missing from the vantage point where I took the picture from my driveway. It makes no sense. You should also see houses by those trees and we can't explain the golden haze at the bottom of the picture that looks like tan grasses. that should be the top of a gray house. Also, we are very close to a highway and at that time of night there isn't much traffic. When I initially saw the object about 4 car horns were going off.
