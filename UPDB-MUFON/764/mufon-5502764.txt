Green and red ball of light that moved, changed direction and descended.

I was at a child's birthday party here in my town. Someone said that there was something on the sky, so everybody looked up (25 persons) and saw a very strong green and red ball of light that appeared between the moon and mars. The light appeared suddenly, from nowhere. It moved to northeast, made a turn and went northwest and began to descent until it disappeared behind the house.
