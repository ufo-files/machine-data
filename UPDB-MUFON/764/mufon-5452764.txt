Long Rectangular Glowing Object with Much Brighter Head Section

There was an unusual elongated rectangular glowing light that approached me while in my front yard. 
Then it moved in several directions. Initially it moved towards me and then behind a 30 foot tree and then back towards me again. It can be seen in the surveillance video in this sighting report. The estimated size of the object was 10' X 1' (long rectangle) with the head section much brighter than the rest of the object. Very strange object that appears to make abrupt circular movements and then hover above the trees and then back toward me several times.
