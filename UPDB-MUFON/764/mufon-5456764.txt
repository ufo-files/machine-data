{rjl} Unknown flying object with blue lights flying in sky.

I was a security officer working at a nuclear power plant in Mecklenburg County, North Carolina around 1981 to 1982. I was on patrol one night (about 1:00 am) when I saw an object silver in color with blue lights (flashing/pulsating) around the object. It was heading north over a large switch yard, low enough to determine it was not an airplane. I reported the object by radio and continued my patrol. During my career working there (1978-1996), I never saw the object again.
