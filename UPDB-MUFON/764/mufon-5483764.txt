{jpg} I was driving home from work on Quebec st and was looking north and saw the light.

Driving North on Quebec st, gray and rainy day, after work. Looking up at the clouds while driving looking North , I saw a green light streaking and descending at a shallow angle from West to East, then it started ascending at a shallow angle on a deviated path and then disappeared for a second then reappeared and then it went out , it all took about 4 seconds. The light was moving very fast. My father was in the passenger seat and I said "Look at that light moving!!"and he only saw it briefly,but I saw the whole thing.
