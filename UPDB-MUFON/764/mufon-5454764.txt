{jpg} UFO stuck in crater on moon. 7 very clear photos taken. Object very large.

These photos were taken through an 11 inch Celestron telescope through a 12.5mm Tele Vue eyepiece using a Nikon 1 camera. Total of 7 shots, very clear. Taken at zenith as moon passed over. Object appears to be tilted inward toward the center of the crater. You can see that some debris has been displaced by the object. It appears to have rear lights, but this could be a reflection of the sun off of prominences attached to the object facing out and away from the crater.
