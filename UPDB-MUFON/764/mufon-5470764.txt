{jpg} Saw rectangle in sky then a cylinder appeared with it; seemed misty around it. I was unnerved and went to my apt.

I was returning from my daughter's house to my apt. I parked and noticed an odd rectangular shape in the clear night sky way up, but not directly overhead. There were no stars out and no air traffic. I couldn't see blinking lights on it like on planes or helicopters. And it wasn't moving close or leaving. I stood watching it about 2 mins trying to figure out what it was. I stopped looking to go to my apt and let my hubby know I was home. About five minutes later I got curious to see if it was still there and went outside. It was there, still rectangular. I went to get my phone and took a picture, which was not as clear as what I could actually see. Then there was a sort of mist coming off it and a cylinder shape grew behind it. I photographed that too. I was like, "What the heck!" I stood there watching a few more minutes and then got unnerved being outside in the dark and alone, so I went back in the apt and didn't go back out at all. I also have a picture of it that includes my apt complex and the empty, dark night sky, but I couldn't access it from my photo gallery. I was able to include these two shots because they were enlarged previously in photoshop and easy to access.
