SINCE JUNE UNTIL NOW I AM SEEING LIGHTS ALMOST EVERY NIGHT IN THE SKY.
IN JUNE I STARTED SEEING A YELLOW LIGHT TREE LEVEL ACROSS THE STREET FROM MY APARTMENT. THE LIGHT COMES EVERY OTHER DAY OR SO AND LASTS FOR 2 MINUTES. SOMETIMES I SEE A TRIANGLE OF RED, GREEN AND WHITE LIGHTS. THEY SEEM TO BE 500' TO 1,000' HIGH. I'VE SEEN AVERY BRIGHT WHITE LIGHT, AND RED BLUE, GREEN AND YELLOW LIGHTS. ONE LOOKED LIKE A RECTANGLE BODY WITH RECTANGLE WINGS. THE ONLY FAST ONE IS THE YELLOW ONE, THE OTHERS ARE SLOWER. ONE LOOKED LIKE A SCORPION SHAPE, IT SEEMED TO HAVE A TAIL THAT CAME UP OVER IT. ONE BRIGHT WHITE ONE CAME DOWN AND SHINED LIGHT INTO MY MOTHERS BEDROOM. IT WAS ABOUT 100' AWAY AND 300' HIGH. THESE LIGHTS FLY JUST OVER THE TREES. ACROSS THE STREET WHERE THEY SEEM TO HOVER IS A BOWLING ALLEY AND A VACANT BUILDING AND RAILROAD TRACKS. I'M STILL SEEING THEM.
I WOULD LIKE SOME ANSWERS.
