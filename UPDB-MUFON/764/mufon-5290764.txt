6 silver, blinking white, orbs traveling in triangular formation passed overhead

Two witnesses, myself and my wife. My wife wishes to remain anonymous.

2020-12-05
13:42

Standing on the sidewalk at the north side of the T intersection of Twilight Dr and Wasach Dr, just east of the house at 1616 Twilight Dr, Longmont, CO 80504.

40.19502086311994, -105.06250977056096

87.35 feet (estimate calculated using Google Maps) east of me and at an altitude I estimate to be 450 AGL I saw 6 metallic silver obs in a triangular formation, flashing white 3 times a second, pass overhead traveling north to south over the tops of the houses on Wasach Dr. I first noticed them as they were above the park just north of 1948 Wasach and lost sight of them over 1936 Wasach.

Using Google Maps, they covered the space of 286.49 feet in 3 seconds (286.49 / 3 = 95.50 feet per second = 65.113636 mph). 

The sky was clear with no clouds. No sound was heard.

At first I thought it was a new type of drone but there was no frame to be seen. I could see blue sky between the orbs discounting a bird. I was wearing Blueblocker non-polarized sunglasses. My wife was with me and she briefly saw it as they vanished over 1936 Wasach. She was wearing grey-tinted polarized glasses and she also said they were silver but couldn’t identify them in the brief time she saw them.

When I first saw them I thought the sun was glinting off metal but then shortly realized they were blinking white. I was able to count the intervals in my head and then used the display on my Apple Watch to figure out it was 3 flashes per second. I was in workout mode at the time and my watch was showing me the hundredths of a second as a counter so it made it very easy.

For a moment over 1942 Wasach Dr the furthest orb in the grouping of 2 was obscured by the closest one due to my vantage point.
