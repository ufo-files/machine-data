Golden Disc Like Object Flies Effortlessly Through Sky in Riverside on Easter Evening

My wife and I were in our backyard at home in Riverside, California on Easter Day at 6:16 PM when my wife suddenly noticed an odd bright gold disc like object flying from the West at a fast rate of speed several thousand feet in the air and moving Eastward. Below it was another cylinder like object with green, red, and black that at first appeared to be carried by the bright golden disc but it flew and moved in parallel and was not attached. The object made no sound as it flew across the sky. With my phone already in hand, I quickly took 4 quick pictures (with 2 second motion videos) before it disappeared off to the East in a total time span of about 6 seconds. The motion videos attached have some background noise from a neighbor mowing his lawn, but no sound came from the objects themselves. For context, the area we are in is about 8 miles from March Air Reserve base and the within the flight path of commercial aircraft coming towards LAX and ONT Airports. We regularly see both military aircraft ( low altitudes after takeoff, C-17s, KC-135s, F-15s) from our home as well as commercial aircraft ( mid altitudes , decent path) but this object was like no other we had ever seen before as it seemed to fly effortlessly through the sky. For comparison sake, I also attached a picture of a commercial aircraft today at approximately the same time and location and angle to give an idea of the size of the object in comparison to the commercial aircraft. The pictures and motion videos attached are unedited copied directly from my phone.
