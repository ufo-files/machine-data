{jd} sitting on porch noticed light in sky looked through binoculars could see lights rotating

My wife and I were sitting on our front porch when I noticed a bright light hovering in sky between two stars. My wife said "It's just a star." but it was changing colors and hovering. I went and got my binoculars and you could clearly see a bright white main light with red, blue, green, and yellow lights rotating or flashing in sequence. We watched it for about thirty minutes and went inside. I went back out at about 11:20 and lights were gone. It was NOT an airplane or helicopter. I'm glad I saw it and even more happy my wife saw it also.
