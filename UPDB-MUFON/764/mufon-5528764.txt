Falling blue light, then the blue light flickered out like a firework .

I arrived home from work at around 0615. As i exited my vehicle in the driveway I turned around to look at the mailbox. A light caught my eye high in the sky over the tree tops of my neighbors yard. The light was bright and high in the sky. within seconds it was at the tree line then came within 5 to 10 feet of the top of my neighbors house and the blue light fizzled out like the left overs of a firework. No further incident after this.
