We were watching fireworks and a UFO at the same time.

We were at a Campground in our little town. They started the fireworks late this year so it was around 10:45pm. As we were watching them, my husband pointed out a bright red light off to the left of fireworks toward the South. I saw that it almost looked like an airplane except that it didn't have another clear light flashing and it wasn't moving in the sky at all. It also looked about double the size of an airplane's lights that I've ever seen at night. It stayed stationary for at least 10 minutes from the time we first noticed it. Then all of a sudden it quickly took off toward the Southeast, then stopped, then the red light started flashing, stopped, descended, stopped, then quickly descending at about a 45° angle and went behind a treeline that was in the distance. It look like it would've had to have landed. The photo attached is from my cellphone, Motorola Edge Plus, in telescopic view. In real life it was as red as the red in brake lights.
