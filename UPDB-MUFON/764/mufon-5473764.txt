{cak} Bright ball of light, possible flight pattern or more than one object in clear night sky

I had just arrived home, I approached my front door when my dad says he saw something in the sky just then. He came back out and described the event and the object. We both sat out in the dark. The skies were clear and other aircraft were in the airspace, including helicopters. With our eyes glued to the sky, we witness a quick small object with a short following trail pass at a high altitude north of us. I lost track of it almost immediately. We both exclaim, alerting my mother and grandfather. They join us and ten minutes later, a closer and brighter passing of the craft (or possibly another) occurs. It was a bright white circle in the sky with a bright white trail. Hard to tell the altitude or size of the object. I start to help unload the work truck and while I was storing things in the garage, my mother sees it again. No more sightings occurred after that. My father has been seeing these objects flying and zipping by over here some nights. Hope others that see this same event report it.
