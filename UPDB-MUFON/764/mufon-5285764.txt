Object flying at 70,000 to 80,000 ft flying north to south and made an abrupt 90 turn to the east

I was out walking my GSD dog just before bed at approx. 11pm and was looking up at the sky, it was a clear night and saw the object moving at 15,000 to 20,000 mph (estimated) and just as it got over head it made an abrupt left handed turn to the east. It was flying at 70,000 to 80,000 feet, the sun had set 3-4hrs before this so it wasn't catching reflection from sun light. Some back ground on me, I am a former Naval Enlisted Aircrewman that graduated #1 in my class and set the highest scholastic score ever in the history of the ASW "A" school with several yrs exp. as an observer, so I am trained in all identifying types of AC. I flew in P3C update III's(VP-45) back in the 90's and was deployed to Iceland and Europe during that time. I have thought about reporting this for yrs but didn't want people to think I was a tin foiled hat crazy person but finally decided to do so. Satellites while they fly very high they don't fly this low nor make abrupt 90deg turns in this case to the left towards the east. Anyway I thought you guys should know.
