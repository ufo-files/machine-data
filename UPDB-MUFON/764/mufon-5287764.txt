A light giving of what look like colored gas, or smoke

I was driving around 7:30 this past Sunday night, may 16th. I noticed what looked like a ball of light in the sky going up so I pulled over. As the ball of light ascended it was giving off what looked like two bright mists of blue and green. The ascension started slow then shot up and the light completely disappeared but left behind mist where it once was. I wondered Whether it could have been a firework, but it seemed pretty high and made no sound. What caused me to stop and pull over was the colors, it was bright. I stood parked for a while after to see if I could see the colors still in the mist but I could not see anything. I should mention we had a clear sky that evening . Unfortunately it happened so quick I did not have time to grab my phone and film.
