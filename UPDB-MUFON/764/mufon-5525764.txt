O-Balls

We were in the pool after watching fireworks, as we looked up to the sky we seen an orange ball of light flying southwest. about a minute and a half later it started fading out. in about a course of ten minutes, we seen four sites of the same ball of light go in the same direction but in a different place. later that night at about eleven thirty, we went back outside and saw another ball of light flying southwest again. after that, we seen it again. it flew southwest for about thirty seconds, disappeared. then re-appeared in the same place it appeared before in about a quarter of a second. we have nine people that witnessed this.
