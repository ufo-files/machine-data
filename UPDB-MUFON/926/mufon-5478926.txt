{dr}Meant to take pictures of moon, months later discovered UFO like lights in photo above moon

It was August 15t, 2016 at 10:52 pm, and I was taking a short walk to the gas station for some snacks. I was in a parking lot, with a basketball court right in the distance. I should note that I felt particularly good at that time, maybe even at the moment. The moon looked beautiful that night so I grabbed out my iPhone 5s camera and snapped 3 pictures. 2 were taken with no flash and one with the flash on. 4 months later I go back to inspect the photos when I noticed that there three odd looking lights in a shape of a saucer above the moon. When I snapped the pictures I wasn't aware of any ufos in the area. I didn't notice anything out of the ordinary. Only upon inspecting the photos did I notice the strange green lights in the sky above the moon. When you zoom in on the lights, it is almost as if you can see shapes of what might be the occupants inside. The photo wth the flash on is the one where you can almost clearly make out a saucer shape and the three lights. I speculated that the two without the flash show the "UFO" at more of a distance while the photo with the flash on shows the UFO more close up.
