{tjd} Bright, bluish, silvery, white orbs, at least 20, moving from west to east.

The objects were a bright silvery, blue, white color, blinking (possibly to communicate?), and were moving as a unit, when one changed direction, all changed direction. They seemed to fade out the further they got, possibly due to there being enough atmosphere between us and them, or they were truly fading out. All but two were evenly spaced, and the two that weren't evenly spaced were near the middle o the group of objects, and were much closer than the rest. Some of the stars in the sky weren't really stars either, as they began to move upon their own volition, and moving with the rest. After this event there seemed to be at least half as many stars in the sky. In my personal opinion, they seemed to almost be moving as though in a slingshot maneuver.
