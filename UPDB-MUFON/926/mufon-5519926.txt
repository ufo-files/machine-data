Black, helicopter shaped, with strange appendages, no noise, drone?

I was putting gas in my car at Tops gas station on Meadow st. Ithaca around 5pm on 3/16/12. Sky was totally clear, blue, sunny. I looked up towards south/west and noticed a tiny black odd shape, hovering, sometimes spinning. Seemed to have an appendage at times. It moved slowly west to east. I watched for about 3 minutes. No noise from it. It was not a helicopter. I stopped at another store in that area and came out about 15 minutes later. Two low flying jets were then flying in that area. I did not see the object at that time.
