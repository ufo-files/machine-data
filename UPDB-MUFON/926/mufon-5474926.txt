{rjl} soundless silverish white craft, tube or cigar/blimp shaped, moving in a straight line, no propulsion seen.

I was in the frontyard with a family member working on a car and we decided to have a break as it was a hot day. The object caught my eye because it was a clear day and the object was reflecting alot of light, whiteish in color. I thought it was an airplane as planes fly over all the time(the house is under the flight path of a few airports and bases) but the object wasnt making any noise which was odd, and there werent any wings or propellers. I noted it to a family member and they responded "its probably a plane" which i explained there werent wings or noise, and at the elevation it was those would be visible, they then said it was probably a helicopter, and i said it couldn't be because we would hear it, so I took out my phone and started recording, it was just white, going in a straight line northwest, with no visible markings, or wings or noises. Eventually i got a video of the craft and it disappeared behind the treeline after i stopped recording.
