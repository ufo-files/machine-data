Two Equilateral Triangle, Cloud-like objects in bright daylight over Wind Power Electricity Farm

A clear daylight sighting of UAP phenomena off the coast of Portslade, Sussex UK in May 2021. The aerial phenomena were sighted directly over the Rampion 1 Wind Farm, an offshore windfarm providing 400mw capacity of power to 350,000 homes in South East England.

The objects I took several still photos of appeared like extremely oddly shaped, triangular clouds that seemed to be largely stationary or slowly drifting in an otherwise entirely clear early summer sky. I could only observe some very high cirrus clouds many miles away to the west during my sighting. Otherwise the sky was clear and blue with several hours of daylight remaining.

The cloudlike, triangular objects (x2) were not aircraft contrails either as they were too low in the sky – a few thousand feet - with warm air rising and only light aircraft in the vicinity of Shoreham airport to the west.

I work as a science picture editor and photographic specialist and have never seen a regular equilateral triangle cloud such as the one in the foreground of the photos, let alone 2 such perfectly shaped clouds together. I have provided some pixel analysis of one of my photos and this displays the uniform solidity of the supposedly random, water vapour clouds.
