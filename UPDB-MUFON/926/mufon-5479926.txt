{dr}Belive we saw a UFO the same night we were watching the International Space Station pass.

On October 18, 2016 at approx. 7:25-7:30 PM my husband and I were in our hot tub watching the International Space Station pass over above us, travelling N, NW. A few seconds after we saw the ISS appear, we also noticed another large, bright light appear in the S, SE sky and it started moving toward the direction of the ISS path. It crossed the path of the ISS and continued to travel until it disappeared out of sight. It appeared to be higher and moving faster than the ISS.
