{cak} 2 sets of 2 ,, 2 red then 2 orange craft

Was on the porch facing NE and saw 2 objects with non flashing bright red lights traveling SE close together. One then headed E and the other continued SE. A minute later after 3 other witnesses came out a second pair headed in the same direction as the first another witness joined us to see what that was. We had binoculars and could see the outline of one of the craft. Clouds then rolled in and we saw nothing else. The event lasted from 8:21 pm eastern time to 8:25 pm eastern time. We had a total of 5 witnesses.
