we were driving in slow traffic when i noticed a very strange cloud unlike I had ever seen before. I took some pictures of it with my smart phone and then later, while showing them to my wife, zoomed in on the cloud to get a better look at it and that's when I saw the 2 objects. My boss Jesse was with me and saw the cloud and we both had a very odd feeling about it. I showed him the pictures and zoomed in for him to see and he was amazed. I don't know what the objects are but that was the strangest cloud I'd ever seen and felt as if though the two were connected. I could not see the objects until I zoomed in on the pictures so I don't know what they were actually doing, but I do believe they were a type of ufo.
