Large White Star-like light

Just saw a Mufon Sighting Report dated today from a person driving towards Lakeland and I wanted to confirm that I saw the same bright star-like light too. It was extraordinarily bright and no beacons could be seen. I saw it while driving east from downtown Tampa towards Lakeland. At the time it would have been located over eastern Tampa and it did not appear that it was moving very fast. Unfortunately, since I was driving, I had to keep my eye on the road and could not study it further.
