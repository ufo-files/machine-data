{cak} Clear sky, dusk, still light out, wind gusts. Bright white ORB UFO object moving silent overhead from the west to the east then fading out quickly. No sound was heard

I was looking out to the east from our back porch at a clear dusk sky, still light out, when I observed a white orb / ufo object moving overhead from the west to the east. The house roof line runs north/south, and the object was visible once it had crossed over the roof at 90 degrees straight up. I followed the movement for a few moments then went for a camera (DSLR) and returned when the object had gotten to about (estimation) 60 to 45 degrees from the horizon and started photographing the object with the DSLR camera. The object remained bright for at least a minute moving quickly over the city of Eugene, Oregon. This is not the first time this or these kinds of objects have crossed over the city of Eugene, Oregon.
