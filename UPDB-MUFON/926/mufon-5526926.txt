It finaly once again turned into a Pinkish Orange as it elevated higher and then it looked like a white faint star in the distance and then was out of visual site. Object was to far to be picked up by my cheap camera, only a faint dark spot. I phoned friend in Riverside and told them it's heading your way, before I could say more they spotted it immediatly, same black triangle with orangeish color.
