Shushing Entity

I woke in the middle of the night, approximately age 3 in the early 90’s, to my mom sitting up in bed, head in hands, swearing. Something shushed her, or I noticed it first I’m not sure, but I looked over and on the dresser was a creature. It was mostly in silhouette, but I can recall it was approximately 2.5-3’ tall while (presumably) seated. It had an oblong head that was wider than it was tall, and a squarish body. I got the impression of long fingers and, dark brown skin. My mom continued swearing, walked right by it and left the room while it continued to shush her. I followed. I didn’t speak about this for many years thinking it was a dream, but asked my mom if she remembered when I was a teenager and she does. She has seen it more than once and was upset because she thought it was following her. She claims I was present for an encounter with another creature, but I do not recall that incident. I did not include her information, but I am in touch with her.
