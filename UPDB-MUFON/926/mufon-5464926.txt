{cak} An orb was hovering in the sky and then slowly ascended above the cloudline

Driving eastbound on the NYS Thruway, Interstate 90, I was between exit 41 and 40 at about 7:10. I saw to the southeast, in front of me as I drove, an orb of light. It was, from my position siting in the driver's seat, about the size of a quarter in the sky, and at about 50 degrees above the horizon. I had passed the the eagle statue in Montezuma NWR already. It hovered for a few moments, not moving. It sort of flickered, but it did not blink or flash lights of any kind. I could not see any red/green lights. The orb's light reflected off the clouds. It slowly rose straight up into the sky, continuing to throw light on the clouds around it. It eventually disappeared behind/through the clouds. I kept watching but no other show of light appeared. 
As for my reactions and actions, I just kept driving and watching, perhaps a bit irresponsibly. I felt stunned, in awe, and tried to scrutinize the light to see if it was a plane, helicopter, and cellphone tower in an unexpected place. I drive this route home frequently, and I was surprised to see a light where I wasn't used to seeing it. If I had not heard of a similar siting before, I would not have considered it a potential UFO siting.
