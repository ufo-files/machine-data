{nd} Six bright orange objects w/white lights traveling in a pack. They stopped & hovered then went on their way about 10 mins later another 3 appeared did Same thing . The bright lights went out in twos first set the second set all went out at same time.

On march 24 th around 2:45 am I was sitting at my kitchen table in my apartment after getting in from work ( Im a cocktail waitress). I was on the phone with my boyfriend ( lives on west coast up bc of time difference ) when I notice 6 bright orange circular objects in the sky with white lights around them . After staring at the objects for a few seconds I told him I had to use my phone to tape what I was seeing bc I had no idea what was in the air . I then started to video record the event with my iPhone out of my 12 th floor apt window . The aircrafts did not seem to make any noise infact it was extremely quiet out my window which is odd for my location . I stopped recording the objects bc I wanted to call my boyfriend back incase something bad was about to happen . The objects all stayed together heading in same direction made a slight directional change in Unison. After about a minute or a little longer the lights on the objects went out two at a time . I sat back down at my table in disbelief and a bit frightened . About 15 minutes later 3 more appeared heading in same direction but this time I could see a helicopter in distance hovering as if they were watching the objects . I also saw a plane in distance this time . The three bright lights all went out at same time.
