a white cloud-like object that stayed in the sky for hours. Mistaken for a cloud.

I was looking out the bathroom window in the middle of the night..It was a white object, like a small cloud, above a tree. I thought it was a cloud. It looked like a sort of oval shape but I looked later and it was still there so it was not a a cloud. As I watched a line came across it from left to right (every fast) and it looked as if it just closed up. There was no trace of it after that. I saw it a few nights before also and I have seen it again.
I knew it was not a cloud and wondered about it. It just vanished after the line went through it (like a zipper).
