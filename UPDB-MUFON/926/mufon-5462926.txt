{jpg} Phoenix, AZ Two Fire Orange lights dropping smaller variously colored lights

My wife and I were out for a walk near Bethany Home and 19th Ave. Looking up as we were heading west, I saw to fire orange lights moving very slowly. It appeared as if it was dropping balls of light. It didn't seem like an airplane or helicopter. It (they?) made no sound. It was almost above us, to the east and we could see it drop the light. I remember one of them was blue. I'm sure that some were orange. They were not nearly as bright as the two lights. I had a feeling I should have taken my phone with us. I didn't. It faded out shortly after passing into the southeast. It vanished before it reached 1/4 to the horizon or 3/4 from the horizon.
