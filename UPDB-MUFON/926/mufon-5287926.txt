A strange UFO, clearly scene at dusk and very close

It was dusk and my girlfriend (at the time) and I were up on our apartment rooftop drinking some rolling rock beers. I had only drank half of my first Rolling Rock, I was not impaired and neither was she.

Looking southward we see what I can only describe as a large black mass the size of my fist if I were to hold my fist out in front of me. The black mass was in the south western sky and was not a cloud. It moved eastward slowly in a rolling, lumpy fashion. If you hold your fist out directly out in front of you as far away as you can, the lumpiness and size of your fist will resemble the size and shape of this object. 

From our rooftop (6 stories up) the object was just barely above our eyeline. I would guess the object was at least hovering at the height of a 9 or 10 story building. The object slowly moved eastward and then stopped directly south of us. This is when I noticed a small single light located directly below the object (maybe attached to the UFO). I believe it was blinking a white light.

The object was stationary for a moment and then it zipped off directly south at a very rapid speed. Completely silent. The movement southward was sudden and the it took approximately 2 seconds for this object to disappear completely as it zipped away southward. Attached is a map of where we were in relation to the UFO and where I believe it was/how it moved.
