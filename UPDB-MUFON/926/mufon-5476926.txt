Myself, my wife, daughter and her friend observed a Dull Black Triangle fly directly over our heads. A white light in every corner, one in the middle, and completely silent!

Myself, wife, daughter and one of her friends witnessed a huge dark, silent triangle fly over our heads. We saw it come from the west and move over our heads, disappearing to the east. It had white lights in each corner, one in the middle, and was the size of a football field. Completely silent, only a few hundred feet up. Moving very slowly, only a few miles an hour, it kept moving silently, finally disappearing out of our sight to the East. It was the strangest feeling I ever had, and something I will never forget!
