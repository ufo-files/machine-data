{dr}Arrowhead shaped craft with incredible speed flew over my head.

I was just a kid in Greenville North Carolina at the time. There are two thing I remember vividly about this sighting. This thing moved FAST and it was LOUD. It was so fast I didn't even hear it until it was over my head. It came at me from behind and I saw it just as I looked up to see where this amazingly loud roar came from. It all happened in an instant. It seemed to be only a couple hundred feet in the air. In an instant this thing was a little black slit on the horizon.
