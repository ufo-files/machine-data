{jpg} Looked like a star but didn't move, above Earth

Thought it was a star except it didn't move. Had white lights with a red blinking top, a tail below slightly off center on the left side. Got home to Richmond and it was still there. Looked again last night and it was still there only this time there were two more. The second one was more south of us and the third was to the right, West of us but further out. All appear to be in outer space, not moving but staying with the movement of Earth.

Too far out for my camera/cell phone.
