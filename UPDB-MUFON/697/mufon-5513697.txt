(GS) 2 orange lights hovering, disappearing, and reappearing in the sky

I was on my first break at work sitting outside smoking a cigarette and talking on the phone when I seen in front of me two bright orange lights in the sky. They werent blinding at all. I was freaking out on the phone because I thought Id never see one in my lifetime. The lights werent moving at all for a second or too and they were perfectly horizontal. The light orb that was behind the other one disappeared and appeared in front of the one that has been there. Then that one disappears and appears again in front of the one that just appeared. Then they both just disappear completely. They were perfectly still lights, not extremely bright, not off extremely far either, and they were in a perfect horizontal line. I could see it because the parking lot was pretty empty and it was already dark outside.
