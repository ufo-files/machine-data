Three rotating blue lights forming a circle of mist observed in night sky

I was walking home down my usual street route alone when I spotted 3 large blue lights in the sky. The lights were rotating slowly in sync around a circle of mist or cloud this suggesting what I was looking at was one whole object. The object was silent and apart from rotating did not appear to be moving in any direction. I stared at the lights for a couple of minutes and attempted to take photos on my phone, but these images came out unclear (phone was a cheap model with a poor camera). I then kept walking home while still looking over my shoulder at the object. About half way home after 5 mins from my first observation I realised the object was slowly moving across the sky. At this point I was at a street corner and again attempted to take photos with my phone, the best image I have supplied with this report. I continued to walk home while keeping an eye on the craft that was slowly moving. As I neared a park that is close to my home I could hear all the neighborhood dogs start barking even though it was a still silent night and the object was not making any noise. Once I got home I kept watching the object for another minute before it moved behind my house and disappeared into the distance. I messaged my brother at this point about what I had saw, the experience left me a bit shaken. The object I estimated was around 500 feet above the ground and its size was very large around 300-500 feet, similar size to that of a football stadium.
