My brother and I witnessed a UAP. It was not a plane,helicopter,satellite,balloon or drone. We weren’t drinking & don’t do drugs.I’m a ret police off.

I was visiting my brother in Pie Town, NM when he called me outside frantically. He pointed to the sky and I saw a white object hovering. I first thought it was a plane, however, two planes passed by with white and red blinking lights in a straight line movement. It was too high for it to be a helicopter or a drone. I watched the satellite video and it was not a satellite or weather balloon either. The object, first stationary then began to move up and down, sideways and diagonally at a speed way too fast for anything on this earth. I ran back inside and got my phone and started to video. There is no ambient light in Pie Town, so it’s just very black, but you can hear the audio on both videos. I was wondering if there is technology that can remove the blackness so we can see the object moving. We do not do drugs and were not drinking. I am a retired police officer.
