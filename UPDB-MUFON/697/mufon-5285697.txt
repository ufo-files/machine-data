Lights high up hovering

Saw august 31, and September 1st starting around 9-10. Watched for approx 30 minutes on the 31st and approx 5 hours on the 1st. One white bright light and one dimmer reddish light. Very slowly the red changed position over the course of the night. The white held approx position. Saw very small bright blue lights flying in formation and streaking by the white light. White and red light would make very small erratic movements (e.g. up, down, left, right, semicircles/circles, figure eights) seemingly in tandem or one then the other while maintaining approx position. Stayed in a general SSE direction from central Geauga county OH. Will be checking again tonight. Took a video on phone but it’s poor quality so it’s useless.
