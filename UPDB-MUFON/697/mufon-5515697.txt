(GS) star-like object, hovering, sequantial lights.

Two witnesses observed a hovering, starlike object in sky, for approximatey 1 hour. Every 10 seconds, the object moved counterclockwise, stopped, then sequence of lights on object lit-up, from left to right. Next, object then moved forward a litle bit, then stopped again. Object repeated this sequence over and over for the one hour duration.
