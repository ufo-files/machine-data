MY SELF AND 2 FRIENDS WERE COMING FROM A CHURCH FUNCTION>>> AS WE WERE WALKING HOME AT APP:9PM to 10PM OCT 1953 1954 ??>>> WE BECAME AWARE OF SOMETHING OVER OUR HEADS >>>AS WE LOOKED UP THERE WAS A WHITE DISC OVER HEAD>>>IT WAS BRIGHT LIKE A NEW MOON >>>THERE WAS ALSO A DIFFERENT INDENTATION ON THE BOTTOM OF THE DISC >>> IT WAS SILENT AND GILDING OVER OUR HEADS AND THE VARIOUS BUILDINGS HEADED TOWARD MONTROSE HARBOR AT LAKE MICHIGAN ON CHICAGO'S NORTH SIDE >>IT DISAPPEARED AS IT DESCENDED TOWARD THE HARBOR>>> WE CALLED THE AERONAUTICAL CIVIL SERVICE BOARD >>> AND WE THEN COULD HEAR A LIGHT PLANE FLY IN THE AREA >>>WE ASSUMED THEY SENT UP A SPOTTER PLANE??WE KNEW WHAT WE SAW!! A ABOUT A WEEK LATER DURING A SCIENCE CLASS IN SCHOOL >>>WE WERE REPORTING WHAT WE SAW >>>WHEN TO OUR SURPRISE A ANOTHER BOY WHO WAS NOT WITH US AND AT LEAST 2 TO 3 MILES AWAY ALSO SAID HE SAW THE SAME OBJECT>>AND DESCRIPTIVE IT IN THE SAME WAY AS I HAVE RELATED
WE ALL KNEW THAT THIS WAS A SAUCER WE WERE SEEING

THE NEXT SUMMER IN THE SAME AREA OF THE LAKE MICHIGAN MONTROSE HARBOR >>THERE WAS A EVENT IN THE LAKE AND AT MONTROSE HARBOR ENTRANCE A TIDAL WAVE 100 FT TALL >>>WHICH DO NOT HAPPEN IN LAKES >>> THIS EVENT SWEPT AT LEAST 30 PEOPLE OFF THE JETTY WHERE PEOPLE WERE FISHING >>> THERE WERE WITNESS TO THAT EVENT >>> AND FOR WEEKS THEY WERE FINDING BODIES >>> I ALWAYS THOUGHT THAT THESE 2 EVENTS WERE TIED TOGETHER SOME HOW !! AFTER WATCHING THE PROGRAM ON UFO IN LAKES IT SEEM TO MATCH >>> I AM SURE THE TIDAL WAVE EVENT WAS IN THE NEWSPAPER >>> I HAVE ALWAYS WANTED A PLACE TO BRING THIS TO THE RIGHT PEOPLE<<< THANK U FOR YOUR ATTENTION ON THIS SIGHTING R.
