Jellyfish-like paper thin UFO

On Delta Airline Flight DL392, at approximately 5pm as we flew over the Atlantic Ocean, I lifted my window to look outside and a jellyfish-like object floated past the plane towards the land. It was white but translucent and looked almost paper-like/paper-thin. I thought at first it was a strange drone but could not see clearly as it was slightly lower than the plane, so exact size was not possibly to calculate.
