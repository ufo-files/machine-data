{rjl} Black saucer Like object hovering through the rain clouds

I was driving nothbound on rout 15 through Thurmont MD when I saw a large black saucer like object zipping around in the clouds and then disappearing behind the clouds. When I parked in the parking lot at Food Lion my brother who is sitting in the passenger seat then alerted me that he saw it up in the sky above us out in the distance doing the same type of pattern in the clouds. That’s when I got out my video camera and recorded it doing the strange motion until it disappeared out of sight.
