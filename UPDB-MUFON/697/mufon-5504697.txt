{rjl} Very Slow Moving Orange Light, no known aircraft lighting flashes

On the telephone, looked in the sky to the south. Saw blur in orange. Put on glasses and became clear, but was still just an Orange light. Moved very slowly, too slow to be a small prop plan. There was no noise at all. There were no flashing Port or Starb Lights, there was only an Orange Light. It moved in front of me for 3 to 5 minutes and then it turned East toward the Atlantic Ocean shortly after the change in course it vanished.
