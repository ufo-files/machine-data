Descended down to 5 miles westerly, then abruptly leveled heading south.

The winter sky was clear and dark (no moon). I was in my back yard when a bright object descended southeast of me in a 45 degree slant toward the west, After 5 or 6 seconds it abruptly bounced into level flight bearing south for 3 or 4 seconds before dissapearing. The speed was always supersonic, but I heard no sound. This was visable above Fentress Air Force Base which is southeast from my house.
