{nd} We saw six to eight aircraft and have been seeing them since six o'clock pm. There were drone sized ones and airplane sized ones. One aircraft was spotted hovering over someone's house for about 3 minutes then continued to fly south east.

We were driving home from someone's house when around 6 o'clock my friends mom had spotted two aircraft and then I noticed three more aircraft flying very low. We kept driving and looking out the window when we passed through sun valley and saw a smaller aircraft hovering about someone's house so we pulled over and watched it for about 3 minutes until it started flying again. We continued driving and saw a much bigger one flying very slowly at a very low altitude. It flew directly over our car and you could clearly see the bottom of it. We kept driving and then once we got to Spanish springs we saw 3 more. One was going very fast, like a jet, and the other two were triangular shaped and got slow. They were all also flying at a very low altitude. We were very concerned and sort of terrified and anxious. When we lost sight of it, it was because of how low it was flying and it'd disappear behind houses.
