Around 7 pm, I walked outside to have a smoke and talk to friend that lives a few houses away from me. The next few minutes were a back and forth of "is it moving? Yeah, I think it is...wait no, its not...no wait, it looks like its movi--no, I guess not..." Suddenly, two more bright (slightly less brighter than the first object)appeared out of nowhere--they didn't appear to fly to this spot, they just suddenly lit up. The next few minutes were spent watching the three objects clearly interacting with each other. moving back and forth almost perfectly in sync. After seven or eight minutes, one of the two that appeared last took off to the ESE slowly and turned sharply to the S at a high rate of speed, followed moments later by the other object that it appeared with. The original object hung around and meandered for about five minutes more, before taking off from a dead stop to unbelievably fast speed. Amazing!
