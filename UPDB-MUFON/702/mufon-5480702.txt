{rjl} woken by loud vehicular sound, and taken by humanoid figure

Last night i was woken up by a loud vehicular sound coming from above my house, my husband who sleeps downstairs said he heard it too around the hour of 1 am, and immediately fell back asleep. When i woke up from the noise, i felt drugged. My vision was blurry, and i could only move very slow. Standing by my bed was a creature. It looked very human but with much longer facial features and appendages, big dark eyes with long very curled eyelashes, and different looking skin. At first i was very scared and tried to call out, but suddenly became very calm and wasnt afraid at all. I fell asleep, and awoke laying on a table, in a paper gown, still feeling very drugged, in front of either a screen or window, in a room that smelled sterilized and looked very clean, where i was saw beautiful images of space and the universe. It was all very calm and felt very peaceful. I fell back asleep, and was awoken, by my husband when he came up to say goodbye before work, feeling like i did when i woke up from a surgery a few months ago. My left eye felt like it was vibrating, and my vision was shaking with the vibration, but its fine now. I just generally feel very nauseous and lightheaded now.
