Very Bright light and no noise moving from the south towards the north east. Fluttering movement. Moving at a steady speed.

Around 10PM was taking a walk in the country next to the house. We live in a small village about 30km west of Montpellier. 

My wife and I noticed a very bright light moving across the sky. There were no running lights like an airplane and it had a tendancy to bob up and down in its movement which was somewhat steady in its flight path. There was also no noise coming from the object.

There was an airplane in the vincinity at the time which had a movement and light intensity nothing like te object we witnessed.
