{tjd} A white cylinder with a blue plasma glow came in at 30 degree angle and changed direction

A white cylinder shaped object came in from the east at about a 30 degree angle to the earth and changed direction slightly to the south (its left). The cylinder was about 5 or 6 times longer than wide. The front was well rounded and the back more cut off. It had a bluish flickering envelope some distance from the body of the craft. The blue flickering layer was more pronounced on the top and bottom of the craft. The cylinder had a solid white glow, like a billboard at night. That white light was not pulsing. 
The cylinder slowed down somewhat as it neared the horizon or tree line or ridge line. It was spotted while traveling on highway 377, traveling southwest about a mile before the railway crossing at Bluff Dale. There was no sound heard as I was in a truck driving at the time of the sighting. The object was straight ahead at the end of the viewing and at about 11 o'clock at first observed. The whole event lasted under 5 seconds. The area is hilly and has old mine pits. It is very rugged.
