We have observed multiple flying objects that change direction

We have seen on many occasions, these sphere/oval like craft flying high in the sky. They are usually semi reflective and change direction way too abruptly to be a plane. We observed them flying at incredible speeds and pulling 90-180 degree turns and then continuing flight at the same speed from before the turns. These things are usually seen every single night if it’s clear and disappear after 10-20 seconds.
