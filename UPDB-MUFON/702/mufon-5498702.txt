{nd} 3 white objects in triangle formation moving across sky

Me and my Son let our dog's out around 7:30-8Pm and I looked up into the sky and saw 3 white objects not blinking and not along any known flight path 90 degrees in the sky moving in a triangle formation from East to West. Also noticed a few more objects one moving from area near the 3 in triangle formation and it headed NE. Two others were moving in the same direction but much further apart and one shot across the sky from a Westward direction to a Southerly direction in a mere few seconds. I have seen satellites move across the sky but they go from West to East and they follow behind each other in this area. We heard no noise and saw no blinking lights just them as White objects like the stars themselves. We live near Chaumont,NY in Jefferson County, NY.
