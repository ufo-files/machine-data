Large silent transparent triangle shaped object

Observed a very large silent transparent triangle shaped object traveling directly south on a smooth linear route. Larger than any aircraft I’ve seen at that altitude. Estimating 3-4 wings spans of commercial jets in width. It did not change its course in direction, altitude or speed. Was observant of ambient noise, no other aircrafts in area at the time. Definitely was not birds flying in formation, drone, an owl or a eye floater. Did not take a photo or video, because I could barely see it with my own eyes. I almost had to look to the side of it and not directly at it to track it, as it appeared transparent. I observed it when it was directly overhead as I was smoking a cigarette and blowing the smoke out over my head while star gazing, it was not smoke from the cigarette. Tracked it for about 5-7 seconds until it was past my line of sight of the horizon.
