{rjl} Solid red light over lake with two smaller white lights moving toward it from SE/NE. All disappeared with a bright flash when they intersected.

My family was sitting down on the beach on 4th Lake making s'mores. We noticed a very bright red reflection on the water and followed it to see a large, bright red light in the sky. We had/have been vacationing in this exact spot every year of my life and for the majority of my parents' lives, so we were very familiar with the sky in this area and knew that this was unusual.
While we were discussing what it could be, we noticed two smaller, white lights moving almost in sync with one another toward the red light.
The instant they all met, there was a huge flash of light. They were all gone.
One of the strangest things about this was that there were six of us who witnessed this, yet none of us spoke about it for almost 10 years. I can't even remember the rest of what happened that night, but there was no talk of this incident for the longest time. Then one day a few years ago my dad randomly brought it up at dinner saying, "Does anyone remember that night we saw those lights in the Adirondacks?" Instantly we all remembered it, and the exact same way. 
Like I said, we go there every year, and every year I look out at that lake at night and remember that bright red light reflecting on the water; that will always stick with me.
