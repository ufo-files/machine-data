{ck} 3 start like objects dimmed out one at a time and another craft pulsing red

A friend and I were traveling home from dinner at approximately 10 p.m., heading south and when we approached a nearby intersection near my neighborhood my friend pointed out ahead from the front windshield and asked what the strange lights were. Before she even said anything I had already observed the 3 lights aligned in a triangle form in the southeastern sky. At first I thought they were stars since they werent moving and of a white color, but then they each brightened up a bit individually then dimmed out one at a time. Not too far away from that was another object pulsing red, but moving in the direction where the triangle was, and I observed closely to make sure it wasnt a plane, but after it traveled a short distance, it also vanished in mid air. The pulsing of it was different than a plane also. It was not cloudy in the sky, so it didn't go behind a cloud. We both agreed that we had witnessed something extraordinary that evening.
