{cak} Triangle in the Sky

I was driving south on I93 in the Derry area of NH when I saw a solid triangle in the sky heading West at a slow steady pace. It had blue'ish lights at each point and a light in the middle of the underside that turned on and off, but not at regular intervals. I lost sight of the craft due to driving. I was not able to get any pictures. I have see these types of crafts several times in the past.
