3 Lights Roll over each other through the sky like a bowling ball

In the sky I saw a plane in the background it was making a lot of noise and crossing the sky at a slow speed flying at a low - mid altitude, coming from behind me and above my head was 3 Large lights faded by the clouds but these lights was most surely in the clouds as they rolled over the top of one another it was like watching an invisible ball with 3 lights placed around the sphere rolling through the sky. They rolled towards the south before abruptly changing directions and heading east the entire event took around 10 seconds and in the final 2 seconds the lights rolled upwards in a south east direction and disappeared, the plane was still crossing the sky perhaps still within the first 30% of the the width of my view in of the sky the plane was visible for several more minutes. I wasn’t afraid I jumped multiple times with joy and told myself I knew it was true, it’s a relief to know the truth I’m not sure on my views on what happens after sightings as some experiences I’ve heard sound traumatic but the truth is slowly coming out
