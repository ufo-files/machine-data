Huge glowing light, goes out and either that or airplane seen leaving area

I could see out my window at 5:30AM a huge, glowing oblong or tear-shaped orb that I knew after a second couldn't be a star. My mind tried to identify it as a planet and a star lined up among other things that might be brighter than a star, then it vanished and I could see what looked like an airplane flying from around that area - unsure now whether that small craft was the glowing orb changed, came from the orb, or just a bystander airplane. I only saw a few minutes tops of this, I have no idea how long the huge glowing object was in the sky.
