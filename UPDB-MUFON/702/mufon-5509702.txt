{dr}Bright orange object sighted at dusk

At approximately 9:40 pm, Me, my wife and a neighbor were sitting in my backyard talking. I observed a very brilliant bright light in the sky. It seemed to follow a SW to NE path. It was sighted NW of my home. Object did not resemble any kind of aircraft. No normal navigation or flashing strobes. Just very bright object. Seen by all 3 of us. I saw same object 4 separate times over a 20 min time. Object followed same straight line path and area in the sky, maybe 15 to 20 miles away. Each time, I would loose sight of object in trees behind my house. I am a retired veteran with aviation experience and I felt concerned that I could not identify this object.
