Metallic oval sphere travelling through and vanishing in clouds

On Saturday, 11th of February at about 12:45pm I was on foot walking to our parked car which faced a South Easterly direction in Golf Road, Taumarunui when from the corner of my eye spotted an oval metallic object in the sky for about 10 to 15 seconds as it vanished in the long clouds, travelling in a straight line in almost the opposite direction as our car was facing (Northwest). I lost my capability in continuing to track it through the clouds. I was transfixed and knew it was something different and just didn1t want to lose sight of the object.

It was a bright sunny day with a few huge clouds over-head and I wore my polarized sunglasses and this assisted with my sighting in being able to distinguish between it being a plane or an unexplained craft.

The craft reflected the clouds from its shiny exterior and was silent. It was quite up high and was travelling at some speed.

This sighting has somehow stayed with me.
