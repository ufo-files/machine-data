{nd} Huge rectangular object, hoovering above a residential neighborhood

I was driving to work north on Semoran Blvd around 6:00-6:05am when I noticed what I first thought to be a low flying plane heading to Orlando International Airport (a few miles south) from my driver side window. What made me look closer was how low and big it was, first thing I did was to roll down the window and look for a fuselage, but there was none. It was a big Triangle object (150-200 feet wide and 20-25 feet high) and it was stationary hoovering above the residential neighborhood east of Semoran boulevard (between Curry Ford Rd and Lake Underhill Dr). The object had rounded edges and bright white lights at each corner, just before I lost it out of sight there was a fourth white light coming on from the center of the object. It was very low and very close, I would say 50 feet above the ground and no more than 1/4mile from where I was. The trees and 2 story buildings across the street were tall enough to block my view. I made a U turn at the traffic light on Semoran and Lake Underhill and got my camera ready but the light took forever and the object was gone by the time. I've never reported or had something to report before, but I'm really interested to see if anyone else saw or reported this. There is no way I'm the only one I saw it as it was in the middle of the City and it was big.
