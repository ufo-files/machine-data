2 Different Rapidly Pulsating Objects

Object 1 was located about 30 degrees above the horizon. It had rapidly pulsating red and green colors and even appeared to change shape from a dot to a bean like shape and then back to a dot. I viewed it in my binoculars and only saw light, no structure, but the light appeared to change shape. It also appear to be rotating. At one point I think I saw smaller dots of light around it, but I couldn't tell if the smaller dots originated or entered/exited the larger object, they just appeared and then disappeared. We witnessed that it abruptly moved in it's location in odd directions, but still seemed to stay in the same general place from when I first witnesses it.

Object 2 was located about 75 degrees above the horizon and was closer to me, but still very high in the night sky. It had a white/blue rapidly changing pulsating color. 

Both Object 1 and 2 looked only a little large than a star, maybe closer to the size of a planet but were both brighter and did not match up with any planets on my SkyView App. 
There was an airplane that flew over during the time I witnessed both objects, but I did not see nor hear any helicopters, nor balloons, or flying lanterns. The objects were remarkably distinguishable from all other lights in sky.
