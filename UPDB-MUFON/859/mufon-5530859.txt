Fireball like burning satellite. Split to 4. Disappeared. Reappeared as hovering lights.

My wife and I were in our driveway. She saw the incident too. I saw a fireball fall from the clouds (it was overcast). Ar first I thought it was a meteor. Then the fireball split into four then disappeared. A few seconds later, all 4 reappeared above the plane where they had vanished. They hovered for a few minutes moving back and forth from left to right and back again. They appeared to be about 5 miles away but no more than ten. They glowed yellow like a flame the whole time then disappeared behind the clouds again. I've never seen a plane act this way and never seen one glow like a fire.
