On a summer break, we go to my sister's vacation home up in Hills of Dehradun, Uttarkhand State in India. The vacation home has a large lawn and a porch area where we sit and do star gazing. I was sitting on the porch with my sister & son, all were star gazing, suddenly what at first appeared to be a slow moving satellite caught our attention that moment we lost electricity & this object zoomed in warp speed, and the lights came back!It was an unforgettable experience, very clear in my mind and I wonder what I saw, why did the electricity went off just the moment the object gained speed!!
