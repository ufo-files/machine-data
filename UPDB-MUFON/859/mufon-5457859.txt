My wife and I were sitting on our back covered porch looking over mountains and the lake. Out of nowhere a bright green object was shooting at us like a fiery Roman candle. It stopped no more than 200 feet and lit up bright green. Maybe 150-200 feet long cigar /rocket looking. Hovered above water for maybe 25-30 seconds. It then had propulsion like colors from rear end illuminate so bright and shot straight up and out of site within a mere few seconds.

This is as real as it gets. We are both educated and bright and we were in awe. We both were transfixed on it not saying a word. It then in seconds shot off in sky and we looked at each other in awe and described the exact same thing
