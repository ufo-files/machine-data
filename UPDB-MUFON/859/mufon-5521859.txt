Lights glowing over a mountain, that change intensity and number of light, they multiply

On dec26 at about 11 pm me and my girl friend and a friend went to up to mount Soledad, which is in la Jolla ca, we brought my telescope up there and pointed it to the north east direction where there is a mountain range approx, 15 miles north east of mount Soledad. This mountain is believe part of poway/romona but Im not sure exactly what the mountain is called. In the middle of this mountain is a canyon space that you can see right through and in this canyon area approx 100 ft about the canyon there are a row of light that point at a angle up above the mountain tops but it starts in this canyon. These lights are glowing orange and will change there glow from being very dim to all of a sudden very bright in a perfect row, when I look in my telescope I can see many more glowing lights circling each other and making triangular patterns. These lights have been there multiple nights. I have seen them twice with my telescope which is a Meade 4501. These lights seem to turn off and on at all different times, sometimes only 4 with be bright then only one then all 4 but what is weird is that they will have little glowing balls spinning around the original 4, these are not planes I have lived in San Diego my whole life and I actually live in the flight path, where Im looking it is approx 15 miles north of the flight path like I said these lights have been there for multiple nights so I def think we have a chance to investigate
