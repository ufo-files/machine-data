South sky of Toronto sometime betweeen 9:30pm and 11:00pm

I had my telescope set up for public viewing of Jupiter and Saturn. The Toronto Airport is right by, and planes are seen all night entering and exiting. As I was showing people Jupiter and Saturn, me and this lady noticed an object moving from southwest to south. We commented how it didn't really look like a plane. We kept watching it and it was moving at different speed than the planes would be. It didn't have the characteristics of the typical airplanes coming and going. It really did stand out to me and the lady. I showed her Saturn through the telescope and when we looked for it again, it was lost in the south sky. It did not have the same lights as the planes. 

I now heard of someone else sighting an orb in Toronto that night and someone else an orb south of Toronto, in New York state, earlier that day. After hearing about these reports, I felt I should also report. This could have been the same object.
