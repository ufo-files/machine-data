Fire balls that eventhough individual, kept close in pairs. In an curved trajectory travelled from south to North then portal

At 11pm June 10th 2011 I witnessed 6 fireball type orbs that came from SE in an arced trajectory. Arrived at exact same spot in the sky then pulsated growing & shrinking then turned from orange to white then it was as though each were shrunk through a hole in the sky. The orbs were big & around maybe 3/5miles ahead of me. I was with 2 friends who both tried to film the orbs on their phones but the phones switched themselves off & wouldn't turn back on until the last pen had been sucked through a hole in the sky. We became paranoid & the very next weekend my male friend & I were at my front door & as I unlocked it was hit by a white flash that knocked me along the small hallway & actually landed inside my bedroom which was off the hallway & to the right handside. I landed on the floor & my friend had rushed in to check I was ok, I couldn't hear him eventhough his lips were moving & when I'd come too could see him panic. The next again Saturday he was admitted to at Johns hospital in Livingston, West Lothian with a psychotic breakdown. It was conveniently called a drug induced psychotic breakdown eventhough he hadn't touched a drug since he was in his youth. My life has never been the same since that night & subsequent weeks, this is the first I've shared my experience. I'm haunted by whatever hit me(my solar plexus). My dad is ex parachute regiment(3para) in the British army & I'm fairly sure myself & my late sister have been claimed by them somehow. My sister passed away at only 46 after suffering long unexplained illness. Any information would be greatly appreciated. I need to make some sense out of these & other experiences which I'm meant to believe don't happen. 
Thanks so much for your time
