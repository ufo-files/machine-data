My brother and I were headed to the store it just got dark and as I was stopped at stop sign I noticed the light and pointed it out to my brother we then shut off the truck and got out to get a better look at it. It looked tuned shaped and had red lights spread out evenly which gave it the shape I believed it was. It looked to be about 2 telephone poles in length and maybe 10 to 20 ft in diameter and about 2 telephone poles above us with no sound what so ever. We watched until it wasn't visible.
