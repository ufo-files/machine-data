{jpg} Video is 3rd in a series for that particular evening

We were watching the neighborhood firework displays, when my friend became alarmed at a glowing red orb flying in our general direction. She alerted me, and when I looked up, I excitedly told her that was the exact look of something I saw in the sky earlier that year! A few moments later, it was either a 2nd one or the same one reappearing, and I ran for my cell phone/vid, while she ran inside for cover. Just then, the 3rd, and last appearance to us for the night came. I was lucky enough to catch it on my phone. 
Like I said, earlier that year, I had witnessed the exact same thing, only I was living in a different part of the state altogether. Never would I imagined I would ever see something like it again...and so soon! 
After both occurrences, the object just floated off into the distance, almost flickering out, then reappearing for a second or two before disappearing for good.
