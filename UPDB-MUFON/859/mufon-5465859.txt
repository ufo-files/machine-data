My partner and I woke to our dog barking upward at the sky out the baywindow, then we noticed what looked like a star w/pulsating xmas lights se of us. It seemed to be a solid object with our eyes(with pulsating red, white, bluish green lights). After a few minutes we started to video the sighting. The object seemed to hover and slowly change position.. When zooming in upon the video, the object looks more orb like, than what we witnessed. Also, during the event we saw 3-4 helicopters/planes go near the object the abruptly change direction nw. We both were mesmerized by the object and lost sight when checking our video of the UAP to see if we could get a better look at what it was. We both have seen other UAP1s (2 to be exact in the past), but nothing that we were able to get pics/video of. One was on Lake Erie(north of Perry, Ohio) when we were on our boat and another off rt. 90 between Geneva and Austinburg..
