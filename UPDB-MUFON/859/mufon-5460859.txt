{cak} I witnessed a huge cylinder hovering overhead slightly angled down

As i was driving in my car around 1pm on April 15th 2010 i saw a cylinder high up in the sky flying in a straight line. After losing sight of it for a minute or so i looked out my windshield and saw it hoovering over my car. It was massive. It was a grayish shiny but not polished large cylinder that was a little bigger on each end like a fuse. It sat there as if sitting on something invisible with no motion. As the light turned green i pulled over into a parking lot. At this time it was at a high altitude again and i watched it disappear at a 45 degree angle. Afterwards Ive experienced strange dreams and other experiences.
