{rjl} Diamond sitting in the sky.

This morning around 4:15am (Pitch black dark outside.I walked out of the house heading for work. As I am walking to my truck I looked west, up in the sky about 370-450 above a neighbors house (which is about 600-700 feet away from my house) and saw a very beautiful bright Diamond sitting stationary just hovering, no spinning or movement or sound. I new instantly It wasn't a star it was way to close, wasn't a plane drone or helicopter. It was perfectly quite. I tried to snap a few picture with my phone but it wouldn't focus for some reason so the shot I have is garbage, not worth uploading. I watched it for about 7 or 8 minutes just chilling in the sky but I was already running late to work so I really had to leave. It was still there as I was pulling out the driveway. I've been observing curious things in Daviess County skies for years now. This is my first time reporting. And will report more when I see them, As I seem to see them quite frequently.
