Saw this object while taking pictures of a high up partial rainbow

While taking pictures of hummingbirds in my backyard, I noticed a partial rainbow high in the sky (was unusual) so I was trying to focus on the rainbow when I saw a dark object to the right. I zoomed in as far as I could go and took some pictures of the object. Later when I downloaded the pictures to my computer I realized I had no idea what it was, zoomed in further and was more confused. The green colour was a surprise as well as the shape. It just looke d like a dot on my camera screen. The object hovered stationary and did not move and no noise was evident. It was there for approx. 10 minutes and one time while I was trying to focus on it was gone. It was very high in the sky, hard to estimate? Pictures taken with my Canon SLR lens EFS55-250mm zoomed to the max. I thought maybe it was a kite but it was so high and did not bob around like a kite might. I don't think a kite would be able to be seen with the naked eye that high?? If you could give me an idea I would appreciate it.
