Bright light felw over head, then dimmed and disappeared before leaving range of sight

Aerospace engineer. Was outside shaking a blanket at 19:05 - 19:10 when I saw a flight going overhead at altitude. Could hear the jet engines, saw two flashing lights, nothing abnormal. Came from the SW. Then from the NW (WNW) I saw a single bright light, slightly white-orangish. It was moving roughly the same speed as a plane, maybe a little faster. Watched it fly in a straight line overhead to the SE. It stayed just as bright until it got to about 50 deg elevation and then it dimmed and a few seconds later dimmed until it vanished completely. No blinking lights. No apparent noise (I could hear the other flight fading away before this light went overhead).

I checked flightradar24 and and planefinder.net, the first plane was DK1135 headed to Copenhagen (right time and heading). No other flights at that time.
