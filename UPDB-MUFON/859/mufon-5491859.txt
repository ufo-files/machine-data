I was bird watching on my back porch, facing east. glanced up and saw an object. not smooth. It was starlike, multiple points coming out from the center in all directions. It was close enough to see shape and form. I live on an island and from the direction I was looking the coast is about 4 miles away, the oject appeared to be over land. Once I became aware of it, I observed the object appeared bright white. It was in the full sun. The sun was behind me and the time was approximately 6:20.I kept staring, waiting for it to move so I could tell what it was. In the moments (about a minute and a half, too long lor any common thing to be stationary) watching, it never moved or wavered at all. I heard no sound. I live in close proximity to 3 major airports and am quite familiar with flight patterns and even helicoper patterns in my area. It's lack of movement was most striking. Once it sunk in that this is no ordinary object, I wanted to get my binoculars as I stood up to retrieve them the object moved (glided) slowly south and was obscured by a tree. I reached for the binoculars and ran into the yard to see if I could find it behind the tree but it was gone.
