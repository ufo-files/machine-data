3 orange lights north of wilkes- barre, pa. follow same path

looking north, 1st bright orange light came s-e down over distant mountain. slow steady turn in e. direction, then up toward n.[ large arc ] 1st light before going out of sight made very fast wiggling decent, then up & gone. last two lights moved in same path, but no movement before going out of sight.
