Taken on craft and shown their city on another planet.

Was 8 yrs old. Blond haired and blue eyed male and female in white took me with them on the ship. They spoke telepathically. They had a star map that was a holograph they moved with their fingers. They were having a slight argument in another language about something related to going to the City of Lights (what I called it). I was then placed in a transparent egg shaped vessel and "shot" off through the stars very fast. I was then in the observatory area just outside the city of lights where the male met with me and talked with me. Will detail this in the abduction report. After that, I do not remember getting home... but I "woke up" outside the house in the back yard. The house door was locked. My dad heard me and let me in the house after yelling at me for being outside. I told him what happened but he told me to never talk about it again. He was very angry.
