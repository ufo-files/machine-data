Wight ball w/ 6 pointed triangles on all sides

I was looking up from my coffee on the way to High school in the car when I thought a new star appeared. This star was not moving but was brighter than venus, it was about 30 degrees to the south of venus. it stayed where it was for about three minets and suddenly grew 6 triangles ( each the same diameter of the ball). After a few seconds the triangles collapsed on the ufo and it dissapeared. My reactions were not to be afraid and freak out, I just said " thats number two in my book" and spent a day as normal at school.
