I saw about 30 lights in the sky moving rapidly from west to East. They were equally spaced except the first 4 and the last 2.

I was on my deck at 0620 in the morning, sky was clear. I happened to look up and saw 2 lights moving from west to East. The first was accelerating and slowing slightly but seem to carry a consistent momentum. The second light was at a constant speed and following the first. Two other lights or objects appeared our of nowhere and fell into formation approximately twice the distance behind the first two. I then noticed about 30 more lights traveling rapidly from west to East all equally spaced and moving at the same rate in a line. The last two lights in the row were closer together than the rest. I would say they were half the distance of separation than the others. 
I watched the majority of the lights appear out of the darkness, all from the same point in the night sky. 
During this event a satellite crossed the sky from north to south, I could see one of the lights actually change course as if to avoid the satellite crossing it’s path. This would lead me to believe the lights or objects were at the same altitude as our satellites.
