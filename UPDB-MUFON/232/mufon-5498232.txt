{dr}Non-Airplane object, bright light, arc into sky

I read Case # 61011 and believe I saw the same thing. That case was spotted in PA, I am in CT. Like that case report, I was outside with my daughter looking for the Antares Rocket Launch, not knowing it had been scrubbed. When I saw this light I was sure I had seen the rocket! We had been watching different planes overhead, remarking on the different sizes and lights of each plane. All of those planes flew *overhead*. This light/object came from the horizon, and arced into the sky as if "taking off". It looked like white & fire, was small to my eye, and we kept saying it was moving slower than we thought the rocket would move, only to learn later it was not the rocket. I am confident this was not a plane - specifically because we had been observing planes so closely.
