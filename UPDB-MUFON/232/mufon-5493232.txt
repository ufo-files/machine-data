{nd} Observed three lights in NE sky

I was watching the hockey game and observed three lights in the sky towards the NE. They were orangeish in color, they remained at the same brightness for several minutes and then faded out one at a time. I went back into the house to get the dogs for there walk and when I went back outside two lights were visible. I called for my wife to come outside as she had done on the first sighting. Again they sustained their glow for a few minutes and faded away. I thought I observed smoke trails above lights but they were not descending, as if held aloft by something. I first thought they were fireworks but they hung in the air too long. Maybe military flares?
