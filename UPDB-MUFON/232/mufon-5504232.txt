{cak} observed diamond lights slowly approach then dim & disappear

As I was preparing our coffee for the next morning I was looking out the kitchen window. I was smoking a cigarette so the window was open. I often do this. The wind comes from the west so the smoke gets sucked out the kitchen window.
I often see and hear aircraft, i.e. planes and occasional helicopter. At 7:25 on Thursday 13th 2014 I saw a diamond cluster of light slowly approach from the east. I want to say less than a mile in the distance and at appx. 45 degrees with no sound, as it seemed to far off. Thinking it might be a helicopter, based on its slow speed, I wondered what may be happening here in town.
I thought police or news. They've been here before. Last winter two teens fell through the Budd Lake ice and drowned. ABC7 from NYC and NJ state troopers both had helicopters overhead.
As I watched the lights for two or three minutes on this night, they just suddenly dimmed out completely and didn't reappear. I checked the clock for the time, 7:25.
I observed the area of sky for a solid ten minutes before telling my wife. She thought I might say UFO. Although I've never been a witness before, She knows I'm a believer. This instance was very odd to me. I don't know if it was a genuine UFO sighting or not . I just want to document what I saw.
