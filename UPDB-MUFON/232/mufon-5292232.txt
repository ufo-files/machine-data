Cigar silver craft

First, I would like to say Im a 51 year old Registered Nurse and have ZERO to gain by lying. Furthermore, it took me several months of debate before I did this report. I feel it is a responsibility.
Southbound on 1st street, came to stop sign at intersection of hwy 47, At stop, proceeded to look both directions for oncoming traffic. At this point, I looked left again to make sure intersection was clear, I just happened to glance up. That is when I noted a large silver craft in the sky at my 9;00. It was approx 6-12 blocks away and appeared to be about the size of a van. I have always KNOWN there is other life out there, but nothing can prepare or calm you when you encounter something like this. After the first few seconds in ah, I quickly reached for my phone to get photo. When I looked back up, with phone in hand, the craft was GONE. Period. It could not of been more than one second, two at most, my eyes were off craft. I was only a few blocks from home, so I sped home, told my family and got my 19 year old daughter. We drove back to site and there were two dark colored helicopters and one or two, I think two jets that were circling in area of site. My daughter and I took photos of helicopters and jets. 
I hope this makes sense, if any questions feel free to email or call.
