I TRY TO IMPROVE MY PHOTOGRAPHIC SKILL-SET BY TAKING PHOTOS WHICH TEST MY ABILITIES SUCH AS FOCUS. FRAMING, SET-UP AND SPEED. WITH THE PHOTO IT DOESN'T MEAN MUCH EXCEPT TO SAY THAT WHAT I SAW WAS THERE AROUND THAT PLACE AND TIME. HERE IS TO HOPING THAT MAYBE SOMEONE WAS ALSO LOOKING AT THE MOON AND SAW SOMETHING.
