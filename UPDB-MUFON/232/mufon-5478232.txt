It looked like a star in the night sky.

I went outside to put out the rubbish and I noticed it was very cold and I looked up to see no clouds which is unusual in west London as for whatever reason you never get to see the stars. But I could see the stars as the night sky was very clear. I looked at them and admired them, then I noticed one in particular that looked very much like a star only it was changing colour. It flashed colours, orange, red, green, very fast, but there was a sequence to it, but it was too fast to remember. I thought this must be a plane or something but it did not move...it just stayed there in the sky looking like a star. I think it must have been very far away. It continued to change colour, white, gold white, orange, red, greenish bleu, but mostly it was gold, white flash overlaid by a pulse of red, then orange, back to red, and a hint of green. This stayed static in the sky for more than an hour. When I went outside to look again at 2.30am it had gone.
