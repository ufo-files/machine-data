Blue Dot starlike at noon cloudless sky. Jet exhaust flew by it. See photos.

Looking south in a cloudless sky at noon, I saw a metal object with wings and no exhaust.heading east. Next I saw a blue star like object next to a jet exhaust headed north slightly westerly. the exhaust kept spilling out but the object never moved. (SEE PHOTOS and note that the huge white light is the SUN.) Then the exhaust passed over me and I could not see the blue dot anymore. when I enlarged the photos the blue object appeared square with a square outline around it. I don't know if that was a distortion or what. When I enlarged it the sun didn't change shape so what the enlarged shape is would be up to an expert. It was very high in the sky and I heard no noise. Can a star be seen at exactly noon with a bright sun? I don't think this was any star.
