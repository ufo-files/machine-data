{cak} Bright blue pulsating orb anomoly rapidly changing direction

I was in my front yard when a brighter than normal "star" caught my attention in the southwestern night sky. It was cold and clear, with many stars visible. 

Having sighted and reported a similar appearing anomoly to MUFON several other times over the past two years, in the same approximate location, I decided to record it with my cell phone. I am also a certified MUFON Investigator for NJ and feel well qualified to identify a reportable sighting. 

In the past, I have seen a very similar orb that rapidly changed various colors while rapidly moving and changing direction. This anomoly appeared the same, except that it pulsated various intensities of blue, and no other color.

This anomoly remained in the same approximate position for at least the 20 minutes or so that I was outside observing it, and when I came into the house to file a sighting report. 

The object must have been traveling at thousands of MPH, in my opinion, in order to travel and suddenly change direction at the distance from which I observed it.
