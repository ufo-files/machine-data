{rjl} was on landing pattern foe Ohare airport but was not moving

First noticed when coming home from furniture shopping.Was heading east on st charles road when noticed.what struck me was lights on front crossed un like planes that would put theirs on about same area as they approached airport.As we got closer noticed it was not moving.so instead of turning where we would I continued another 1/4 mile.at this point we were under it.I got out of car and looked at it could make out cockpit with movement.made a humming sound and had green light in cockpit, my wife was screaming for me to get back in car but I didn't til it started to drift toward airport.when it did I lost it behind trees so I made u turn and went back to where I would have turned and went to top of bridge that went over rr yard.sky clear and gone.got home called police and they laughed but 1/2 hr later called back and wanted info for ufo center.got forms returned but never heard a word.I know there were more that saw because police said so.What I saw was like a flying wing ,I stood below and could make out someone in cockpit.but all my searching never shows any report in that area
