{rjl} We see an unusual light every night visiting us.

Every night for the past 2 weeks or so, there has been a glittering anomaly in our sky. It hovers for about an hour. The light gets very bright and changes colors from white to all rainbow colors. The light will all of a sudden flicker. It breaks into 2, 3 then 4 lights floating and then they combine back into one light and very slowly eventually floats away to the right then disappears. When my daughter called out to it with her mind to come closer , the light got extremely bright and came closer and then the light faded off.
