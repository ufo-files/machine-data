Kinda bright red light that flickered like a flame then vanished

I was walking my dog when I saw a kinda bright red light in the sky. It was moving at a kinda slow but steady pace, and half flickered between colours like a flame and half pulsed between the colours. After like 40 seconds it suddenly grew very bright, then dimmed to almost a star level, then grew very bright again, and then vanished.
