{rjl} V Shaped array of lights, moving fluidly through sky

I went outside my apartment after receiving a tornado warning alert on my phone at 8:27pm. Looking into the sky, there was a lot of cloud cover and it was very dark. While observing the sky I saw a formation of lights to the west, visible as a V would be typed on a page.. but composed of a central dot of light at the point followed by 6 additional lights on each side to form the "V" shape. After a second or two of looking at the object it started to move north for about 15 seconds until it disappeared behind the clouds to the north west. I would note that during the duration of the sighting the lights were faint from being partially obscured by light cloud cover at the time.
