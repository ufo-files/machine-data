{rjl} Vehicle hovering in front of me driving down road. Was absolutely not a plane

I was driving down the road North heading home. The road is long and goes straight for a few miles. I noticed blinking lights in the sky which I thought at first to be a light pole but it was moving slowly ahead of me. The object kept and exact path with the road and had two lights in the middle of the object that blinked simutaneously. The lights were a very bright white. I know this was not a plane since planes lights just do not blink like that. The object would go pitch black then light up again in intervals of about 4-5 seconds. I decided to follow it down the road more but all of a sudden it flew way ahead of me. I see weird occurrences in the skies out here every once in a while but this was the first time I was able to follow an object.
