Woke up to close window, then I saw triangle object hovering

I woke up when my alarm went off and I went to close the window because it was a cold morning and then when I looked out of the window I saw a triangle object with two circles pulsating underneath it hover over my neighbours house for about 15 seconds and then flew it off really fast in to the distance.
