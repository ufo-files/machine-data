At approximately 0415 January 7, 2020 noticed what look like an odd shaped light in the sky west of my apartment building. I watched for sometime and wandered if it was venus, but then I located venus, and was curious what this other item was. I watched long enough to note it was not moving, but stayed fixed. It seemed to have multiple lights Quite beautiful to see, from my vantage point it was almost cross shaped or angel shaped. It never moved. Airplanes flew by not noticing or taking alternate routes from Oakland International Airport. No sound, I was too far away. I considered trying to drive to get under it, but decided it was likely over water, or someplace I could not get to.
