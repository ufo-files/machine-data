{dr}Handful of orbs over lake union july 4

I was waiting for a fire work display to start.
I looked up to see if there was a plane flying as it does annualy (which there s.s) and looking for unidentified flying objects.
I thought it was a flare or helicopter.
Motionless and observant.
I dismissed the objects.
Objects were gone after display.

Note: I have since seen video from Lake Ontario, Moscow and California that shows hovering illumated orbs such as what I saw. I have also found video footage of the area prior to the firework display on youtube which clearly show these objects as similar to those in other videos.
