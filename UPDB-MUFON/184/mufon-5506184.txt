{cak} Straight beams of light that went from the sky to the ground

I was sitting in my car waiting for it to warm up when I saw beams of light in the distance. A couple of seconds later I saw a beam that was flashing (almost like lightning but straight up and down) and it would flash in the same spot every 30 or so seconds. It flashed for a few minutes and then It stopped. I was a little creeped out so I called my family out side. When they came out I got out of my car and then realized that these beams were all around the neighborhood.  They saw the lights and we didn't know what to really think of them. I left my moms to start driving home, a little up the street I had to turn left , once I turned left there were more beams everywhere, there were three really bright ones right in front of me, I stopped my car and tried to get my phone out of my pocket as fast as I could but when I looked up the three beams in the road were gone. Ii was sad I couldnt get a picture of them but kept on driving.... I made a right and once I did there were probably fifty beams in an open field from the sky to the ground.... Once I passed the field the beams of lights were gone.... I posted a status on my Facebook and one of my friends saw the same lights in the same area of town.
