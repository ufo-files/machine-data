5-8 red orbs approaching from NE swing to westerly direction

Originally sited 0.3 +/- mile south of parking. Orbs, (golf ball size) approx following Maine coastline traveling at approx 20-30 MPH evenly spaced (1,000+/- ft) following each other. We did not alert another couple in this small parking lot. While witness #2 was watching the grouping, I locked my eyes on a single unit, about third in line as they passed near us and a larger, barely visible circular image, translucent - almost invisible - like a jellyfish, appeared to be attached to the the red glowing orb at the bottom. While attempting to get clearer detail, the orb shot straight up diminishing to a red pinpoint in about 5 seconds. The leading 2-3 units, now passed me by, heading southwesterly resumed golf ball size. At their closest point the orb appeared to be baseball size. The "jellyfish" appeared to be 25+/- X larger. The trailing 2-3 orbs followed the path of those leading. Total time of event estimated at 3-5 minutes. 
Attached sketch made following morning (Aug 5, 2012 10-11 AM).
