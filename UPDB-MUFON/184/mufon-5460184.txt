I was outside walking my dog around the yard and looked up and could see the moon visible in the NE, Sun still up but was soon to set in a couple hours. Then I noticed what I first was a visible star...then I occasionally looked up at it, then all of a sudden it began to glow real bright, then the glow faded but it had an unusual sky around it. Kind of like a swirl, then it went to it's normal light, then moved to the NE/NW just a bit but faded away, like it went at an angle and out into space. Cant get it out of my mind.

But, this isn't my first encounter. When I was ten years old, I shared a bedroom with two brothers...one night I woke because I thought I heard someone walking around in the wash machine room which led upstairs. We had our room in the basement. I saw three greys...back then I had no idea what greys were...not until about 10 years ago. It was like they floated into the room, the right one was looking at my younger brother, and the middle one was glanced at the right one..the one on the left was looking at me and I got so scared, tried to scream but it was like my voice box was frozen, I looked at my brothers and they were sound asleep...next thing I know, it's early morning just as the sun came up. I awoke scared, and my heart still racing, and I was covered in sweat...no idea what happened, but to this day I still remember it...get this, my whole family has blue eyes, mine changed to green...what the heck?

Every now and then I still see the orbs or light that looks like a star...when my younger brother and I was stationed over in England (brother at Bentwaters, and I was at Greenham Common during the cold war); UFO's flew over our base. We had a gag order not to speak of it, or be dishonorably discharged.
