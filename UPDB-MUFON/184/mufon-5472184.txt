{rjl} Two orange glowing balls, travelling in the same direction

I was taking out the trash, and looked up two see an orange glowing ball, travelling slowly. I live near an airport, and am used to planes. I recognize the distinct red and green, and strobing white lights of airplanes. This was not a plane, a solid orange glowing ball. 

A solid glowing orange ball, like the cherry from a cigarette. I watched it move slowly, then I pulled out my phone and recorded a video (kept going out of focus) then another one came into my view. They both traveled at the same speed, perceived to be slow, in the same direction. The leading one stopped for just a few seconds. After about a minute and a half of video, I switched my phone to photo mode, during which time these two orange balls continued their course about 2x faster than they were going and disappeared over the horizon to the NE. Like I said, I live near an airport (Love Field) and they weren't travelling in a direction that made sense to be coming or going from that airport. No discernible noise. No assumptions as to what I saw, besides unusual. During all of this plenty of planes were visible coming and going from both Love Field and DFW Airport, these two orange balls looked nothing like those planes
