{cak} Spherical orbs travelling parallel above me and slightly in front of my. Then moved onto my right side. Spinning in a circle formation.

About 3 this morning, I am driving my truck from Carlisle when I see four full moon shaped objects spinning in a circle above to my right side. I carried on towards the A66 which is a left turn off the motorway. These lights seemed to be moving parallel to my truck, still spinning. At times they were obscured by cloud but when they were, light emitted from the cloud downwards. When I was able to park up. I filmed them. It took 4 goes to capture them even though they were clearly visible to the naked eye. One of the videos wouldn't play. I noticed at one point, two of the orbs shot off but shortly returned into formation with the other two. After filming I proceeded to drive and shortly after all four orbs disappeared totally.
