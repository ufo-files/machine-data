{nd} Orange ball moving east then hovering then coming toward my direction slowly then headed back to the east then vanished

I went outside to smoke a cigarette and look at my beautiful solar lights when I noticed an orange light between the trees moving to the east. I called my boyfriend out of the house and told him to hurry. We walked to the end of our short driveway to get a better look and it appeared to stop and then move toward us. Then it went back to moving east. It either vanished or moved behind some clouds. We seen this light for approximately 3 minutes or so. We thought it may of been one of those Chinese lanterns but it would of burned up and fell to the ground as others I have seen. I do not believe this was a lantern. Pretty cool to see for sure.
