Multiple bright lights moving across the night sky.

On Aug 24th at 9:34pm me and my daughter was outside and I was showing her that my new 6000 lumens flashlight had a strobe light so I turned it on and when I turned it off I noticed a light coming across the sky. It was bright made no sound and it went right over us but was very high up at first I thought it was a satellite but it was to low and big. After I couldn't see it anymore I turned my light back on and another one showed up that's when I was like what the hell is going on. So I kept turning on my light and turning it off and they kept showing up this went on for a hour or close to it it scared my daughter I called my wife out and she was freaked out to. They flew right over where we were some came in pairs one time there was 3 at one time one flashed a bright light at us and took off the last one I seen it was moving across the sky so I pointed my light at it and it stopped and did not move Intel I turned my light off. I did get a few videos with my cell phone I wish I had a better camera or video recorder. It was definitely an experience i will never forget I live out in the country so i don't believe it was drones or nothing like that. Have you heard of anything similar to what happened here thank you for your time.
