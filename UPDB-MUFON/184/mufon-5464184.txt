{tjd} Bright daytime object hovering over Pacific ocean south west of LAX Airport

I was biking south back home from end of the bike path in Pacific Palisades after a short 15 minute reading break when I noticed the fascinatingly bright object over the water in the distance between myself and Catalina island. I stopped, pulled out my phone and fumbled to get it to record the video. 

I managed to record about a minute of it before it blinked out of existence, with approximately 20 seconds of time lapsing before I started recording. 

The video at first was somewhat disappointing reviewing on my phone, however I looked at the uncompressed file in Premier and zoomed in 400x frame by frame and I'm satisfied it wasn't a complete let down. 

A few observational notes

1. It had structure - seemed to be a odd/lumpy shaped oval/potato, the infamous 'tic tac' video comes to mind

2. It was off shore, south west of what looked like LAX area or El Segundo

3. I'm 99.99999999999% sure this was NOT on the island, which is approx 45 miles from where I was. There was a good amount of marine layer between the object and the island which you can see in the video.
