Not close enough to see the object, only lots of lights that looked like it was on fire.

I was watching TV when I saw a bright object moving slowly through the sky from the east. Because of the orange-red glow it was emitting, I was concerned it was an airplane that had caught on fire. I ran outside to watch it to see if it was losing elevation and was going to plummet to the ground. It's movement was slower than a plane and there was no noise. When I first saw it, it was heading from East to West. It suddenly took a 90 degree turn and started heading due North. It abruptly stopped and hovered for a couple of minutes. I was SO excited, I ran into the house and upstairs to summon my husband to see it. He looked out the back window and saw it too. Then, it disappeared from sight in a the blink of an eye.
