First flare went off heading northwest, then unknown object heading northeast

It was training of our football team in Hamelspringe. Me and another player saw a flare heading west towards S1ntel forest. It went off a few seconds later.

Ten Minutes later, all of us saw a black triangular shaped object between Hamelspringe and Bakede heading northwest. It was to big to be something radio controled. It certainly was no helicopter because it was too low to make no sound at all. It then disappeared between the two villages.
