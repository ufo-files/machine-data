{dr}I asked them to show themselves and they did by lighting up directly above me and then shooting off...

I started meditating about a week ago, learning to try to project my thoughts etc.. Tonight I decided to sit outside and project my thought and see if they would reveal themselves. About half an hour into me staring up I kept repeating please show me. I was looking straight up leaning against the rails on my deck when it just happened. A bright blue object entered my vision from the left. Surprisingly close and bright.. Sooo bright! It came in slow out of no where. It moved in a straight line, slow like a helicopter, then it just shot off faster than anything I have ever seen. If I hadn't of been looking up at that specific spot in the sky at the right time I would have mistaken it for a shooting star. It had no tail no lights it made NO sound. I cannot even believe what I saw. It made me cry. They are real! The closest thing I have seen to my sighting was NASA footage where it shows them coming in and then shooting off. What I saw tonight was amazing.
