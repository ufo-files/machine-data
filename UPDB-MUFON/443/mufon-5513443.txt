Two reportings of same object.
December 20, 2012, 6pm. Driving south on Davis from North Tarrant Parkway in North Richland Hills. A plane is flying in same direction on east side of Davis. I look ahead and see a multi-colored sparkling object. It looked like a giant fireworks sparkler. It was flying somewhat low following the outline of the hills flying in a southerly direction. I took it for a helicopter at first except that it looked like a sparkler with all the lights. It took off at a high rate of speed as it reached Davis near 820 and shot off south. 
December 22, 2012, 10:25pm My husband and I are traveling north on 121 toward the 121/820/183 split in Hurst, Texas, exiting north onto the Davis exit. I see what looks like a helicopter sized object, and I initially thought it was a helicopter, until it flashed white forming a saucer shape, immediately went to a multi-colored sparkler of indeterminate shape, then white sparkles and it disappeared. This all happened very quickly. I took it for a helicopter because it was the size of a helicopter, but seemed to have more extra flashing lights then the ones normally seen around here so I watched it. It was flying just north of the highway split, flying east along 183 and disappeared.
