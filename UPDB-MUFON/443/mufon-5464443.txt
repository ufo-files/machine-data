{cak} The flying lights flew across in no particular formation and sped north-west.

I was at home and playing on my computer. I heard my parent and sibling calling my name. I ran over to see them clutching the window, staring at the sky. I pushed them aside and froze in shock at the sight of the flying lights. At first, I thought a war was about to break out, but my sibling was already dragging me to our porch on the second floor of our building. It was cold and I was in my pajamas, not to mention that I was still very shaken. I started to recording and mumbled out curses. I do apologize for them in the video. For my entire life, I've never believed in aliens and I couldn't explain anything with logic. My grammar even went out the window and I wanted to follow them! The lights were like Christmas lights at a Holiday Sale. They all individually flew straight and in no formation. We lost sight of the lights as they soared further and further away, fading behind houses and trees. I sat down with my family afterward and just had a miniature malfunction about how to describe it. Right now, I don't know what to think so I'm writing this so that I might be able to get some sort of answer. My sibling is also going to be writing one. Thank you for reading and I wish that any definition can be pulled from the water.
