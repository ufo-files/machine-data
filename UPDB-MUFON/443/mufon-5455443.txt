{jpg} Bright unusual light in the sky (and beam of lights that came down from the clouds {djs})

I observed a faint but distinct beam of lights that came down from the clouds to the ground while I was driving north on US 23. I thought the lights were light refraction caused by my windshield but I viewed them from my open window to be sure. The lights disappeared as I pass under the overpass at Whitmore Lake. I then shortly observed a very bright orange object over the Whitmore Lake area. I know it was not a planet or a star and it was far too low to be an airplane. It was very bright and at least cigar-shaped. I was driving so I could not observe as well as I had wished. The clouds were also distorting the object as well so it might have been shaped like a V or perhaps a checkmark. I quickly lost sight of the object though it did not appear to be moving.
