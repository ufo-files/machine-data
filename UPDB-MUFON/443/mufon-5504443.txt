{nd} Five red lights blinked on for 5 seconds then off

On March 01, 2014 I was outside under my back patio around 7:30PM. It was lightly raining and as I was watching the rain I was looking north east between my neighbors house and the empty lot next to him where I could see the northeast sky without any obstruction. As I was looking in that direction all of a sudden 5 evenly spaced bright red lights lit up for about five seconds then blinked off. This was just slightly above the rooftop level of the neighbors house 200 feet away and to the right of it where the empty lot is. It was about 15 degrees elevation and at a bearing from my back patio of 75 degrees. My impression was that it was no more then a block or two away just from the way the light intensity was and because it was drizzling rain at the time. The lights appeared to be in a horizontal line. In between the lights there was just darkness so as to appear to me that they were individual lights and not lights attached to an object. It did not appear to be moving but instead just appeared and disappeared like somebody turned on and off a light switch. The lights kind of reminded me of Christmas type lights but in the morning I used my binoculars to see if my neighbor had any lights on their upper patio deck which they did not.
