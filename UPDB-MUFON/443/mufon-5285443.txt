Orange ball separated into two balls

We saw an orange ball separate into two balls and two flying craft appeared and more minutes later in all directions. They had lights at times but would disappear and reappear in another area in a matter of a second or seconds. Several minutes later we heard the sound of what appeared to be fighter jets. At one point there appeared to be an explosion of an orange and yellow colored ball that formed a rectangle shape across the sky not like a fireball from an explosion. I got some of the craft on video but we were having problems with our phones and trying to keep up with the speed. It was happening fast but last a couple of hours at different times. It gets better about two minutes into the video. I have the video with audio and a few more seconds of footage but I'm not releasing it at this time only a 3:30 clip without audio.
