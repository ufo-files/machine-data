We noticed a stange green light in the sky at around 8:00PM

I was outside my house with a friend talking when suddenly I noticed a strange green light in the sky. When I first noticed the object I had no explanation as to what it was, just thought it was unknown. The object was a green light and it stayed stationary in the sky for a while before moving and leaving. I felt strange because I had never seen anything like that before so I decided to film the object. We lost sight of the object when it moved downwards behind buildings.
