As I was waiting for a bus I looked up and saw an unusual light in the sky

I work in the vicinity of JFK airport and I need to take two buses to get home. As I was waiting for my second bus I looked up, saying to my self thanks God I'm off tomorrow, and saw this unusual light in the sky that was flashing and standing still. I scan the sky try to see if I saw and aircraft near by, and I did saw an aircraft and another flashing light as well. I said to my self maybe is a start or satellite, but than it started to moved very randomly up and down left to right at different altitude and it did a sharp turn at a incredible speed.
