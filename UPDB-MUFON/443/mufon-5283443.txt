LOOKED LIKE A FIGHTER JET

I WAS DRIVING TO WORK WHEN I WAS GOING ACROSS THE CROSS TOWN FREEWAY HEADING SOUTH ON INTERSTATE 5. I LOOKED TOWARDS MY LEFT AND SAW SOMETHING FLYING VERY LOW THROUGH THE DOWNTOWN AREA NOT FAR FROM THE NEW COURT HOUSE. IT HAD MANY LIGHTS ON IT THAT SORT OF OUTLINED THE SHAPE. IT WAS MAYBE A FEW HUNDRED FEET OFF THE GROUND. IT WAS FLYING VERY SLOWLY. THE ONLY WAY TO DESCRIBE THE SHAPE, WAS IT LOOKED LIKE A FIGHTER JET FLYING VERY LOW THROUGH THE AREA. BUT IT WAS MOVING VERY SLOWLY. ALMOST LIKE IT WAS BARELY MOVING THROUGH THE AREA. I ALSO SAW SOME TYPE OF LIGHT AT THE FRONT OF THE OBJECT. SORT OF LIKE A HEADLIGHT. IT MADE A RIGHT TURN ( OR BANKED TURN I GUESS IS THE PROPER TERM ) AND THATS WHEN I COULD SEE A LITTLE MORE OF THE SHAPE. AS I SAID EARLIER, IT HAD A SHAPE OF A FIGHTER JET AND LOOKED TO BE ABOUT THE SAME SIZE GIVE OR TAKE A FEW FEET. LATER DURING THE DAY I CALLED A FEW AIR FORCE MILITARY BASES AND ASKED WHAT IS THE SLOWEST SPEED A FIGHT JET CAN GO TO STAY IN THE AIR. NO ONE SEEMD TO WANT TO GIVE ME A STRAIGHT ANSWER.
