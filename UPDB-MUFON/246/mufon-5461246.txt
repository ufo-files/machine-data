{rjl} Sighting of a light that moved horizontally, hovered then moved, picked up speed and changed course vertically then disappeared.

My older brother who was in his late 20's at the time, myself I was about 13 and my brothers friend were out fishing just after sunset when we saw a light slowly moving horizontally in the east over Toledo Bend Reservoir in East Texas near Hemphill. The light moved from the south to north, paused, continued moving, picked up speed and then at a rate of speed like nothing I've seen before changed direction and seemed to leave earth orbit and then disappear. I've seen many jet fighters and other types of aircraft as I am an aviation buff but never have I seen anything that could perform maneuvers like the light we saw in the sky that night. Soon after there were jets and helicopters flying over the lake and this lasted for over 24 hours. Some of the jets were doing extremely low maneuvers and high speed passes. There is a military base nearby at Fort Polk in Louisiana which just happens to be south of where we were fishing.
