{cak} 8 to 10 amber colored UFOs

I was working at Walmart in Washington IN for the day. We had finished work and were heading home to Anderson IN. We were heading west on highway 50/150 towards Vincennes about 8:00pm. I had happened to see a noticeably strange colored light. While observing the light more started appearing in the same area. I asked if my coworker was seeing what I was and he said it was strange and unusual. The lights would fade from dark to bright and back to dark. They would move laterally and horizontally in real erratic movements. I have never seen before or sense anything move the way these objects moved. They were not stars, comets, planes, helicopters,or meteors. They seemed to be about 1000 to 5000ft. They moved in strait liner movements with speed that We have never seen before. We were actually scared. They would fade "out" and fade back "in" what seemed like miles away from where they were just seconds ago. There was one at first and I think there was 8 to 10 in total. The sighting lasted for about 5 min. The whole time we were driving towards them and never seemed to get any closer. They were about 45 degrees on the horizon and moved from south to north about 25 degrees and ascended. The weirdest thing was the speed with which they moved in unnatural liner movements not like planes. I have worked outside all my life in construction and travel all over the country and have never seen anything like it before or since. The closest evidence I can relate to it are the Phoenix lights but they moved different and "faded" in and out of illumination.
