First of two sightings of lighted, triangle-shaped UFOs seen near my home

As I was driving home one evening after a theater practice, I happened to look up out of my windshield and see an unusual thing in the night sky. It was a group of lights, three large white ones forming a triangle with at least one smaller, blinking red light in the middle. I seem to recall red blinking lights on the sides as well. It was slowly hovering across a main road in my town, moving from the east to the west, from the more populated and busy center to the more remote outskirts. I was hypnotized by this UFO, slowing down my car to stare at it and even driving up over a curb. After moments of it slowly seeming to hover and rotate across the road, it began to move faster and went west, causing me to lose a good sight of it. I turned off onto a side street and drove in its direction, but could not catch up. The thing that interested me the most in this experience was the fact that I saw this in the middle of town, not that late at night, so I assume many people could have and probably did witness it. I immediately posted about the events on Facebook, which is why after over a month I still have a record of the date and approximate time.
