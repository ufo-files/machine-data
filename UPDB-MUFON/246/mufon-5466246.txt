{td} Object was bright white, changed shape from small round to long and flat.

My wife and I were out side looking at each other talking, from behind her I could see a light in the sky. I had her turn around and look thinking that it looked a little out of place and odd, being at sunset she agreed it looked out of place and then the light changed shape. It was bright white, looked rather reflective. I asked my wife to go inside and get my sister, which came out side to view the object and then it started moving and changing shape more. Once it started changing shape it also started dropping toward the horizon slowly and then changed shape entirely to a very long flat, what could have been disk-shaped object and traveled below the viewable horizon line. The whole event lasted less than 5 minutes. I tried to capture video but it did not turn out attached are photos I was able to get.
