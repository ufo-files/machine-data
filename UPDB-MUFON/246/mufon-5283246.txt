Gray oval flying extremely fast and high up

I was sitting on a lounge chair at the pool Ritz Carlton Key Biscaye Florida. Happen to look up at sky and notice a very large hazy gray oval very high up above stratus clouds moving at an extreme speed coming from the east from out over the ocean and crossing the Key heading west over Florida. 

I later calculated how long I observed the object and estimated it’s distance covered. We’re talking 35-45,000 MPH here. It was extremely high up I’d say 20,000 or more feet and very large. Judging by airplanes crossing in sky at similar distance I’d estimate 20-30 aircraft could fit in the oval. I observed it and followed it 8-10 seconds before losing it as it crossed over Southern Florida. 

I’m not a UFO enthusiast and I’ve never seen anything strange. I’m creditable and qualified as an engineer to know better and this object was not ordinary in an capacity. The size, elevation and rate of movement extreme by all measures. 

I was so upset for not reacting with my phone to take a photo, but this happen fast. 

Happy to speak with someone about this and provide more detail and color.
