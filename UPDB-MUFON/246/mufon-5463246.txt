{cak} Floating huge rectangle big as a football field with blinking lights about 500 feet above hwy

Driving home from night shift about 4amaround 1993 94 or 95 I stopped at a redlight looked up and saw a huge floating rectangle as big as a football field with lights about 500 feet up above the Hamilton Mall in Mayslanding Nj. It was stationary but started moving west slow then fast then disappeared within 70 seconds.
