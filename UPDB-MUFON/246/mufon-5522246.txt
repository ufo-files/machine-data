Black triangle with a light on each corner, rear lights blinked, light in middle on bottom was red.

Black triangle about 1000 feet in the sky flew right over me on highway 50 right before the Capital City freeway exit. It was flying in a southeast direction. Flight was slow, and in a straight path. When it was directly overhead I stuck my head out of my window and saw it against the night sky. Perfect triangle shape. One white light on each corner, a red light in the middle. The rear two lights blinked in a non random pattern, the rest of the lights were non blinking. Attached is a quick illustration of what I saw. My opinion is that it was a military blimp type object. It had no regular FAA anti collision lighting configuration.
