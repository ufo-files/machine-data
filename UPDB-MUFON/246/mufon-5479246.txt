{nd} on side of interstate

observed around 830pm on 12/07/15 on Interstate 95 southbound on the us highway 1 overpass in Daytona beach FL. before i was able to record i saw what looked like a shooting star. it fell downward looked like a orange ball of glowing light, lighting up the clouds around it, it also had a light trail. it then went straight upward and disappeared then reappeared around the same spot. i had stopped to record on my phone where i held the phone steady so the movement of the object is the object itself moving not the video. it glowed and moved around almost in circles hovering, then disappeared shortly after the camera automatically stopped recording due to full storage.
