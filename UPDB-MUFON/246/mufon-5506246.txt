{cak} Hovered above tree tops about a mile away. Began moving, then changed direction 2-3 times before quickly disappearing to SE

It was a seemingly usual night. I was on my way back from delivering a pizza going southbound on beacons view to el dorado. As I approached the stop sign I saw an unusual object in the sky above the treeline of friendswood. I sat at the stop sign for several mins to observe the object. Ellington air field is not far away and there is a private airstrip in friendswood so I was used to seeing planes and helicopters on a daily basis. However, this object did not behave like any plane or helicopter I had ever seen. The object seemed to be triangular in overall shape, perhaps the size of a football field. On the side there were somewhat bright yellow-ish lights, 4 if memory serves right. It hoovered stationary for about a min, then abruptly moved approximately 300-500 ft at a time, to several nearby locations, forming a kind of triangle flight pattern. It hoovered for several seconds at each location before moving very quickly out of sight to the SE. It happened relatively quickly. Im glad I stopped to witness this event, just wish I had a camera.
