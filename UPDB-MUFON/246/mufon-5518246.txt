bright reflective sphere over olympia

I just walked out the door and looked up and noticed something really bright and close. At first i thought i was looking at a satellite, then i noticed how bright and close it was. i watched it fly pass, not a super fast rate, but much faster then a plane. Plus it was still light out which showed the outline of the sphere for about 4 minutes, then ascended/vanished. Luckily a few minutes after i noticed a plane fly pass, which definitely allowed a comparison to the speed of the sphere to the jets slower pace.
