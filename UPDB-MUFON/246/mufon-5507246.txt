{cak} Orb lit up between 9pm 10pm, I believe over ocean for 1 minute blinked on then came on up the coast. No movement

June 2011, Myrtle beach SC. 21:00 to 22:00 I believe the time was. The night was in the last week in June 2011, cant remember the day. I was sitting on the deck during my vacation week looking at the ocean, night time and seen a few stars, some clouds. Instantly I witness an object blink on high above the ocean, at least 1/2 mile to a mile up. This object was very bright, it blinked on for 60-80 seconds without moving. Id say it was the size of a very bright star but bigger, much bigger, it was not pulsating,, just a bright lite. It then blinked off, about 2 minutes later the same thing happened, no motion whats so ever. This object did this three times I believe. I did not see any other objects, planes or choppers. This could not of been a military chopper or plane, Ive seen choppers hover over the oceans during my military career, this was nothing like that and it was way to high to be searching for subs, to close to the coast I believe. Ive never seen anything like this, a few years later I read a magazine and read a story about people seeing the same thing and objects,, I was like wow, this is crazy. I asked myself this question, did I see something unexplanable ? Ill never forget it.
