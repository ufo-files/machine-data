{nd} The object we saw was in our atmosphere, and had a dramatic breakup.

I was with my family getting ready to go on a fishing trip the following morning (Opening day for Walleye in NY). It was around 10 pm. A large intense bright light started to streak across the sky from left to right as I faced North with a trail of white light behind it. I knew it wasn't a shooting star, it was to large and the light was to intense. The intense light continued across the horizon unti it broke into 3 distinct, tailed objects. As the object continued, after breakup, the 3 objects turned from white to blue to red and then trickled out of sight, like a sparkler firework burning out.
