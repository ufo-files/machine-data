Massive Cylinder shaped craft! MASSIVE!

Clear skies, out side having a cigarette. Looking at the stars and noticed this massive silently moving cylinder shaped object moving away from me towards Portland in a East north east direction. No lights no sound. It seemed to reflect the city lights from McMinnville on its underside. This thing was HUGH. I'm not kidding. My guess on the size would be be two or three massive old tankers long and a couple if not three or so wide and tall or bigger! I have never seen anything this large in the sky. Completely silent. I live on a 50 plus acre farm about 10 miles west of McMinnville along HWY 18. People have to have seen this thing. I'm completely baffled as to what the hell this was. I do not have photo's. I went inside to get my binoculars and when I returned outside it was gone.
