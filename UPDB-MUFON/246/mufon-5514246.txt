(GS) 4-5 hovering round lights in crescent shape with 2-3 craft flying near them

I was driving in my car at about 25 mph beteween 6-7pm on Mon 11/5/12 at the time the objects were observed. They appeared to be about 5-10 miles SSW from my location on Bernal Ave in Pleasanton CA. This would possibly be in the Sunol-Fremont-Mission San Jose CA area. Hard to estimate as I am driving and there are trees and terrain changes. There appeared to be 4-6 spherical lights and they all appeared to be about the same size. They were in somewhat of a comma/reverse crescent pattern just hovering but there were perhaps 2-3 craft moving near them and their flight pattern was more like a helicopter. When I tried to get to a higher elevation to hopefully see better (about 5 mins later) they were no longer visible.
