{nd} Metallic object in sky, stationary, seemed to disappear & reappear without moving, occurred in early afternoon

My son & I were driving home from town(Casa Grande, AZ), was headed West on McCartney Rd between Trekell Rd & Pinal Ave. I observed a metallic, roughly rectangular object to the southwest. It was visible for approximately one minute, then disappeared. It reappeared after about twenty seconds, seemingly in the same spot. It was again visible for about a minute, then disappeared again for about twenty seconds. It reappeared again for about a minute, then seemed to disappear for good. This is the third time we have seen this object in the last month or so. On previous occasions, after disappearing, the object would reappear in another spot. This may very well be a man-made object, but if so, it is something I am unfamiliar with & have never seen anything like it in my nearly 40 years of life.
