{rjl} Stacked lights moved across the sky. Not normal aircraft lights. I'm a private pilot.

At 9:10 pm went outside to head to work. Looked up and noticed a dark triangular object moving across the sky from east to west. The object had two sets of lights stacked on top of each other like a double decker bus. One single red light was flashing below. As I watched the object made an abrupt turn to the south. I live near the traffic pattern for Atlanta int airport so I am used to seeing jets fly over pretty often but not on the heading mentioned. I am also a private pilot so I know the normal navigation lighting of aircraft.
