{dr}2 orange fireballs on identical path, approximate 30 seconds apart

Watching thunderstorms on my balcony. An orange ball flew overhead parallel to US 35. This was very different from the hundred of aircraft I see taking off or landing at DFW or Love field, which are also visible from my balcony. At first I thought it was a plane but within a couple of seconds I realized this was different. I went from that's weird, to I need to film this, so I turned on my iPhone 6S to video. My wife was with me and observed it as well. I lost sight of the object when it ascended above the cloud deck in the direction of DFW airport. I went inside to get a better camera when a second object appeared, identical to the first I tried to get the lens cap off, and focus the camera quickly but it disappeared before above the clouds before I could. This was distinctly different from the landing lights or other aviation lights I see all the time. I'm not sure the cell phone video does it justice but it's the only thing I had to quickly capture the object.
