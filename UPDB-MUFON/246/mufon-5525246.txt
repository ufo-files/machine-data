i was sitting outside on my porch talking to a friend.as i was talking i thought i saw a balloon in the distance,but quickly realized it was not flying up into the sky like a balloon would.its direction was from north to south towards my house.as it got closer i was realizing this is a circle.to me it reminded me of a basketball,but it was a dirty silver,grey,brown...like dirt color.the thing that most intrigued me was the way it was moving.not fast like an airplane ,but fairly slow and steady.at its closest point i asked my friend if he could see it.he looked up and could not see it.he has bad eyes.by this time i was frustrated because i wanted validation of what i was seeing.when the circle got approximately a block before my home not close to the ground,but in the sky still around a mile up it changed direction.as it started to go past the horizon of my house i saw on the very top of it...almost looked dead center of top..i was able to see a bright light on the top of it.i ran into my house and out the garage to the front of the house.i did not see it ,but in the direction it was going there was a bunch of helicopters in the distance.
