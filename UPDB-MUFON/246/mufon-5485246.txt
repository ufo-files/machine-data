{dr}UFO Spotted near airplanes (possibly AirForce One) or other world dignitaries.

I was driving my sons to their father home, when I noticed multiple low flying airplanes. I even said to my son, wow, look at those planes. The must be landing at PS airport. I made the comment that the airplane activity looked a little odd to me. Next to one of the planes which to me was clearly an airplane, was a smaller craft that looked sliver/grey that was "tailing it". It looked to be maybe half the size of the plane and had no wings. My son noticed it, and because I was driving at the time, kept an eye on it. The plane lowered to obviously land, but the smaller craft went upward as it landed. When I looked back up to see where it had gone, it was gone. No where in the sky. There are NO clouds, it's very clear in the desert today. 

When I got home I read the local news. It reported that today several Asian dignitaries landed to meet with President Obama in Palm Spings. [Link moved to FI Section/cms/tg]].

I'm not sure of what I saw, but it wasn't an airplane. Airplanes do not disappear. Maybe having these world leaders here has brought...something else to our valley?
