{dr}Smoking with coworkers and saw four ufos

I was smoking outside with three of my coworkers when I looked at the sky to my right and noticed two orangey/red orbs that resembled stars. When they started to moved and two others appeared I noticed they werent that. They pulsated between red and orange and moved in a weird pattern around each other a bit. I brought it to the attention of my coworkers and one of them said there was a plane crash in Shirley and that they must be helicopters. I told him they werent because helicopters didn't fly that high neither did they have orange lights on them. He then said it was possibly a plane. Then an actual airplane went by and my other coworker told him it cant be since that airplane that went by went quite a bit faster. I tried to get pictures but the light posts were reflecting off of my camera. They slowly started to disintegrate and after a good two mins and half the last one disintegrated. Before it disintegrated it was moving in a barely noticeable wave pattern. I know that these are ufos as Ive seen similar orbs in the last couple of months over the island and so have many other people. I find it weird I had just been telling my coworker about what had happened before about a month ago on the 4th of July and then this happened ten minutes later.
