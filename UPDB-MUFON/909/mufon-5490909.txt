I was driving N on the 710 freeway at approximately 815 pm. I noticed 2 jets coming from the west. I then called a friend in the area to come outside and look. While we were talking I followed the jet' s flight path forward and saw a very large hat shaped object. It was glowing very brightly. While I was describing its location to my friend it completely disappeared. He then spotted a glowing object and reported that it also disappeared. We continued to monitor the sky's in separate locations. Mine in Burbank, his in Buena Park. There was a lot of plane activity. They seemed to be crisp crossing. There were also crafts with odd lights flashing, one white then 2 orange. We spotted the same craft from our different locations. He saw more follow up activity then I. I live by Burbank airport. He lives by John Wayne. This was not usual activity. I first felt very excited, then I became increasingly uneasy. He reported having trouble calming down to sleep.
