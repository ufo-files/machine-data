{cak} VERY BRIGHT ORANGE ORB COMING IN MY DIRECTION. 5TH TIME WITNESSED SINCE JUNE, 2019.

I WAS SITTING WITH MY DOG OUT ON MY PATIO THAT FACES IN THE SOUTHERLY DIRECTION. IT WAS DARK AND I WAS WRITING A TEXT ON MY I-PHONE WHEN I LOOKED UP AND SAW A VERY BRIGHT ORANGE OBJECT APPEARING FROM THE WEST, TRAVELING EAST. IT PROCEEDED ALMOST DIRECTLY ABOVE ME AS I STOOD IN MY BACK YARD.

THIS IS THE 5TH TIME I HAVE SEEN A VERY SIMILAR BRIGHT ORANGE ORB HERE IN THE SKY SINCE JUNE 9, 2018--AND DURING THIS 5TH SIGHTING I WAS ACTUALLY COMPOSING A TEXT TO A FRIEND ABOUT EARLIER SIGHTINGS WHEN IT RE-APPEARED. VERY STRANGE COINCIDENCE!

FEELING AMAZED, I IMMEDIATELY JUMPED UP AND TOOK VIDEO WITH MY I-PHONE 8. THAT VIDEO IS APPROXIMATELY 1 MINUTE AND 45 SECONDS IN DURATION.

THE ORB DEFINITELY APPEARED TO THE NAKED EYE AS VERY BRIGHT AND VERY ORANGE IN COLOR IN THE SKY (AS IN ALL 5 PRIOR INSTANCES). AS YOU WILL NOTE ON THE VIDEO, IT VISUALLY RECORDED AS WHITE (WITH POSSIBLE FAINT ORANGE EDGES AT TIMES).

OBJECT SEEMED TO FLY AT SPEED OF A NORMAL AIRCRAFT AND IN A STRAIGHT LINE. I REALLY DON'T KNOW HOW FAR IT WAS FROM ME, BUT WAS PROBABLY AT ABOUT 35 DEGREES IN THE WESTERN SKY WHEN I FIRST OBSERVED IT.

I HAD EXPECTED THE OBJECT TO CONTINUE OVERHEAD FROM WEST TO EAST BUT ONCE THE OBJECT REACHED APPROXIMATELY 90 DEGREES OVERHEAD IT FADED AND DISAPPEARED.

INFORMATION FROM MY I-PHONE 8: OCTOBER 14, 2018 / 6:58 P.M. / VIDEO 1920X1080 / 56 MB / H.264 /30.01 FPS / 1:45. 

TO BEST OF MY KNOWLEDGE THE CAMERA WAS ZOOMED TO 6.9X, AS WAS SHOWN ON CAMERA SCREEN AT THE TIME.

AS MENTIONED, THIS WAS MY 5TH SIGHTING OF THIS VERY SIMILAR-LOOKING OBJECT. A FRIEND WAS WITH ME IN BACK YARD ON TWO INSTANCES. I HAVE ONE STILL PHOTO AND TWO MORE VIDEOS (THE FIRST VIDEO WAS OF THE OBJECT GOING DIRECTLY OVER MY HOUSE AND APPEARS AS VERY ORANGE AND MUCH LARGER THAN THE OBJECT I AM REPORTING HERE). SECOND VIDEO (TAKEN WITHOUT ZOOM) IS OF THE OBJECT NEARER TO THE SOUTHERN HORIZON, BUT IN THE 48 SECOND VIDEO APPEARS AS VERY ORANGE. I ALSO HAVE A STILL PHOTO OF THE OBJECT ON THE HORIZON.

FRIEND WHO OBSERVED THE OBJECT TWICE AGREES WITH ME--WE HAVE NEVER IN OUR MANY YEARS SEEN ANYTHING IN THE SKIES THAT RESEMBLED THIS BRIGHT ORANGE OBJECT.
