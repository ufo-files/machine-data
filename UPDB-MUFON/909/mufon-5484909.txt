{jpg} Flying craft shaped like a B-2 Bomber. Flew very low and very slow. Former USAF SSgt w/ Secret Security Clearance. Flew towards Camp Mackall -US Army Special Forces School.

Smoking a cigarette with my Aunt and noticed a strange pair of 2 red lights flying slowly in our direction. It made a slow 180 degree turn and revealed one white light far off on each corner and one static red light in the center. It made only a very slight noise as it appeared to be flying away from us and back towards Camp Mackall. A C-130 came through the area approximately 15 minutes later from Pope AFB (now Pope AAF). I have circled the globe (literally) in my Air Force career, and I have seen most every fixed wing aircraft and helicopter that exist. From the F-22 Raptor to the U-2 and everything in between. It totally blew me away.
