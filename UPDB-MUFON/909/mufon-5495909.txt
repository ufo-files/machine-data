{rjl} WE WERE DRIVING DOWN HIGHWAY 2 AND LOOKED UP IN THE SKY AND THERE IT WAS

MY FAMILY AND I WERE DRIVING DOWN HIGHWAY 2 TOWARDS THE WEST, AND I LOOKED UP TO SEE IF THERE WERE ANY PLANES IN THE SKY, A HABIT OF MY SINCE I WORK AT BOEING AND I ALWAYS CHECK THE SKY FOR AIRPLANES. I NOTICED THAT THE WAS A SILVER METALLIC OBJECT HIGH UP IN THE SKY AND QUICKLY TOLD MY GIRLFRIEND,' THAT'S NOT A PLANE, NO CHEM TRAILS, IT'S NOT MOVING, AND IT'S WAY TOO HIGH." SHE QUICKLY LOOKED AND CONCURRED,"OH YEAH, WOW". THE OBJECT WAS THERE HOVERING FOR 7-10 SECONDS, THEN VANISHED OUT OF THE SKY.
