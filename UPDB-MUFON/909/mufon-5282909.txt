Second night in a row, many craft hovering and pathing over Los Gatos

I went out again on Jan 25 around the same time - 7pm - as the previous night. From the moment I stepped outside of my house, I could see numerous silent hovering and pathing craft with lights. I walked around my neighborhood for over 1.5 hours observing and photographing these craft. They were everywhere. I managed to capture some interesting video and still photos that I will attempt to attach to this report.
