A white bright round object

I was in a hostal in birmingham england having a bbq with about 15 other people. One of tje lads present said wots that in the sky. We all looked up and several ppl said " i dont know" i looked but couldnt see anything. One off the staff said look to the left off the sun andrea, so i did and thats wen i saw the object. It was a round really bright object which was stationery at first. It then zoomed to the left and then took a sharp right to just before where i first saw it. Then took another sharp turn to the left again and disappeared out off our sight. The speed it went was surprising to all off us. I felt really weird thinking wot the heck was that. Within 2 mins off it disappearing ppl just started eating their food. I tried to talk in detail to everyone anout it but they just were not interested to talk about it. I was excited but scared and bewildered why noone found this as amazing as i did
