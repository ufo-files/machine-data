{rjl} 6 blue and 6 orange triangle shaped glowing objects moving north along the Hudson river

While at Riverfront Park in Troy.N.Y.,talking with a friend she was looking up and said there is something glowing up in the sky. I looked up then stood up and saw several unknown blue glowing triangle shaped objects in the sky moving North along the Hudson River. The blue glowing triangle shaped objects were followed by orange glowing triangle shaped objects along the same path and direction like they were in formation. The objects continued to move North until they were out of site. Other witness saw the objects on or near my location from a ship docked in the river.
