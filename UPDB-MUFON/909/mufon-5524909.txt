Bright white fireball in the sky

My fiance, sister and I where in my backyard taking a night dip in the pool due to the unusually hot weather. My fiance then grabbed my attention to a large white fireball that was emitting pieces out the back. This object was pretty far off in the distance, decending in a South, south west direction. The entire show lasted for around 4-6 seconds approximetely. I started to question myself and think of what it could have been. There was no noise, no sonic boom of anykind (at the rate it was going and for as large as it was, there is no doubt it would have made one). It was not up in the atmosphere, it was below and close! Not close to where it would cause a panic, but close enough to see the discharging of small white "Ambers" come from the back of it. I couldn't be sure of what it really was, but it was quite exciting to see it.
