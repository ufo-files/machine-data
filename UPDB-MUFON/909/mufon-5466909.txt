Black dot moving irregularly.

Spotted a black dot moving irregularly in a way that I don't believe typical aircraft can do. Previous to movements, I had suspected a helicopter. Spotted from office building in the city overlooking the bay. Was moving in and out of clouds. Two black helicopters were in the area shortly after, checked on WebTrak (and left Essedon Airport at similar time to sighting). Told my colleague to check it out but she couldn't see the dot in time and reckoned I was looking at smudge on the glass, then everyone laughed at me. Disappeared out of view of the window.
