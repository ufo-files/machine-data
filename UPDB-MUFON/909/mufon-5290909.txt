Bright light I thought it was two helicopters. But it was a helicopter in the back following the bright light!

I was driving north on Main St. And before I reached Speedway I noticed two lights. At first I thought they were helicopters. One waa following the the other trying to catch up. I. Reached the red light at speedway and main and I put my turning signal and reached for my phone in attempts to record. The first light was white diamond shaped almost it was not pulsating, no color change, no sound. The light behind it was a helicopter had red and green blinking lights. It was following this unidentified flying object, it was nothing but light, it then changed directly abruptly, heading east on speedway and at the same time it dimmed its light to a slight glow and speed up leaving the helicopter behind. I turned heading east in attempts to follow but I continued in a path that was diagonal heading southeast. I was able to see it as it crossed speedway blvd and 6th ave. But all I could see was a light and no noise. The helicopter continued to follow. I could no longer see the light as it had continued to dim its light until it went out of view. No video was caught as my phone had dropped to the floor.
