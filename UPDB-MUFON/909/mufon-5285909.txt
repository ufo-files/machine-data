Advanced military vehicle or something else

I saw this from the park on my lunch break. I was laying on the grass. Other people were there but I was the only one looking up. I haven’t seen anything like it before or since. I remember curved edges, not sharp. It darted across the sky in a zig-zagging way until it disappeared toward mountains. My coworkers said there is a military base somewhere in the area, so I assumed it was military, but I’ve been thinking about it since, and haven’t seen any military vehicles move that fast that far. I’m not even positive what year I saw it, but it was before 2015. I would like to know what it was. It moved sort of up and down in addition to horizontal.
