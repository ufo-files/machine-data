{nd} While driving I noticed a single, small, angular cloud in the sky that seemed to swirl. I saw the outside edge of a light blue oval shaped object. It all disappeared within about 3 seconds.

I was on my way to the gym, traveling east on Goodman Road in Olive Branch, Mississippi on May 6, 2014 around 9:30 a.m. I noticed that there was one single, small cloud in the sky that day directly in line with my car. I watched it and noticed that it wasn't round and fluffy, but angular. As I watched, it seemed to swirl and my first thought was that it was a whirlwind, but then I saw just the left side end and botton edge of an oval shape within the cloud. It was a light blue color. I kept watching and within about 3 seconds everything just dissipated. I thought it was odd that the cloud wasn't blown by the wind but just disappeared. I thought maybe it was smoke, but there were no other planes nearby. I also wondered why it was the only cloud in an otherwise cloudless day. I wondered how it could have disappeared so quickly. I continued to the gym and worked out. When I got home I told my husband about it and then documented it.
