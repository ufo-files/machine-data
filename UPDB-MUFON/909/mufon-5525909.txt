Circular craft of unknown size, pulsating red, green, blue, & yellow, slowly moved across sky.

I was walking my dogs before bed this morning. I saw a pulsating light to the n.e. moving so slow that it took atleast half hour to travel a distance that a helicopter could have done in under a minute. After I initially seen the object I took my dogs in Android got my wife. At this time I retrieved my telescope. I set it up and proceeded to watch the object. When looking through the telescope I could see what appeared to be a circular object with red, green, blue and yellow lights pulsating and changing colors in no particular pattern that I could tell. After 30 minutes or so it slowly left my view in a westward direction. Once this happend I preceeded inside, when I turned around and seen another identical object to the n.e. I also watched this object for about half Android hour. It was headed east but had a slight angle heading upward as it moved. Watched this object until trees blocked my view.
