Travelling home, my girlfriend and I witnessed a bright light in the sky directly in front and above us at a distance. There was a star above it, it appeared to be a star, but was brighter. It dropped downwards in a perfectly straight line at high speed

While driving home at approximately 9 pm on the island of Efate in Vanuatu, my girlfriend and I witnessed a bright light. Resembling a star, it dropped in a straight line downwards at a very high speed. 
My girlfriend looked at me, and I looked at her, it was an unusual sight. Our vehicle has no windscreen and therefore a very clear view. The light was below a star. I noticed it in the sky as one would notice a star, nothing more. It seemed brighter than a star which likely caught our attention initially. It then dropped vertically downwards at a speed that was surprising. 
It had no trail, my girlfriend described it as green but to me, was mostly white with maybe a very light blue or green hue. It disappeared below our vision of sight, this was the only sighting of it. It was not an easily explained light.
