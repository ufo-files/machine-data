{cak} Moved at 200 to 300 mph at 100 to150ft alt.going west to east about 200 to 300 ft in front of me

I WAS driving south #475 approaching Ohio turnpike overpass. I saw what I first thought was a plane about size of a Lear jet flying way too low across the Highway. It was impossible to not see it.It had 3 white lites one at each end and one in middle about 5 to 8 ft above the others. It did not have any strobe lights and did not make any sound that I could here.  After covering the 200 to 300ft to the point where it crossed the highway I turned my head to the left (east) To get a rear view of it..It was about a half mile away by then .I was extremly surprised by what I saw. I saw what appeared to be a rectangular panel of solid bright red lights made up of 6 to 8 smaller panels butted together. I was now quite certain I saw something that was no aircraft as we know them.I had to start watching where I was going and lost track of it.
