Right outside Seagraves Texas heading west I seen a metallic object moving what seemed to be slow, my eyes never left the object, and then within a blink of my eye disappeared, not a cloud in the sky.

Heading North to Lubbock from Seagraves Texas, I seen a shinny metallic disk or bullet shaped object at my 2 Oclock (east bound heading in the direction of Dallas) moving what seemed to be a slow speed,based on size and distance, I would guess speed of 400mph, it was hard to make out the shape because of distance, but certainly not a plane, balloon, helicopter, or birds, no contrails. Sighting lasted less than 2 minutes due to the object just disappeared, on a clear afternoon, not a cloud in the sky. I continued to watch the sky for 20 minutes with no reappearance of object.
