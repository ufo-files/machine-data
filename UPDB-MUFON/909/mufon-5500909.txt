{cak} saw globe-like light first stationary then ascend at amazing speed to S.E. then stop.   Then 3 or 4 fluttering type pink-red lights fly around it. Watched for 3 minutes or so before we could not see it.

On Monday evening July 14, 2014 I was standing in the backyard looking up to the eastern sky, it was about 9:00p.m. I saw two lights, and thinking these two stars look so close to each other. Now about with that thought the light on the right side started to ascend up and toward the south. I am watching in disbelief and tell my wife to come from the deck to my spot and look at this. At the moment the pace the light was moving was so fast I said to her this is a UFO and we both knew this was no from earth. The light continued to a stationary position in the S.E., high in the sky and then 3 or 4 pink to red lights started to fly around it in a jitterbug or fluttering type of movement. We watched for 3 or 4 minutes until it became to difficult to see as apparently it was moving away or becoming invisible in another manner.
