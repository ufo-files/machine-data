Large intense bright white light.

Observed a large bright light traveling southwest to northeast at about 8,000' altitude. Traveling at approximately 600mph. Had a consistent flashing light on the front. Very bright. Thought there might be a sonic boom coming due to speed. Much faster than other planes that have been observed. Passed overhead at approximately 85 degrees. Made no noise at all. Had no anti collision lights at all. I would have thought this was an airliner but with the speed, no anti collision lights and no noise I thought otherwise. The lighting was also very intense. Much more than aircraft landing lights. Checked my home ADS B receiver, ADSB Exchange and Flightaware . No aircraft within 20 miles of my location.
Weather at the time was 56 degrees, clear, no wind and visibility was unlimited.
This was not the ISS, a Satellite or a small drone.
