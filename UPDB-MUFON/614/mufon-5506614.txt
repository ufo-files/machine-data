I was amazed and confused because I don't know what we had seen. I took some pictures with my phone just to show some proof. The sky was clear with some clouds. The normal air traffic looked routine for the time of day. The planes in the sky were clear & there lights were easy to see. The objects flew into a low cloud until they were out of sight.
