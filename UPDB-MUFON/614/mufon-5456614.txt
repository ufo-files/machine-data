My son and I saw A ring of individual lights (maybe a dozen) appear out of nowhere in the sky above highway 36 between Tige Boats and the turnoff to 1750 maybe 3 times as high as a telephone pole just sitting there hovering. The lights weren’t casting a beam in any direction and were yellow but would pulse white.  We watched it for about seconds and then it disappeared. We can’t be the only ones who saw it. There were other cars on the highway and it was SO low!! 

added:
I would say a golf ball would have covered it at arms length. I dropped a pin on google maps as to my approximate location at the time of the sighting. It says latitude 32.3793 longitude 99.6559.

I was traveling on Highway 36 towards Cross Plains and it was DIRECTLY ABOVE the highway in the direction I was traveling. It was between my location and the turn off to 1750 and probably no more than 1/4 mile from me.
