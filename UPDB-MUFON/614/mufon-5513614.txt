{dr}Bright Bright reflective cigar shaped light that appeared to hover and progress at an extreme speed out of sight.

I had left home to go to town to run errands and after going thru the intersection was blinded by the reflected lighted object in the sky in front of myself and another car. I do not know who the person in front of me was, they drove a dark red compact car. It progressed out of site at a rate of speed that no airplane can travel and was gone from site.
