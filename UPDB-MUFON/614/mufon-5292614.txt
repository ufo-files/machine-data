Observed white pulsating light followed by a smaller, fainter light, move across the night sky

Five of us observed a white light moving slowly across the sky. It was followed, above and behind it at about the 10 o'clock position, by a smaller and fainter white light, which moved in tandem with it. 
We lost sight of the objects after about 30 seconds when our view was blocked by a tree. We picked it up again after it went by the tree and observed it for another 40 seconds or so when it then disappeared, just faded away. Although hard to tell, several of us felt that the objects were about the altitude of an airplane, maybe somewhat higher. There was no noise or sound whatsoever. When the larger white light emerged back into view from behind the tree, we could no longer see the smaller white light. 

You can't see the smaller light in the videos attached. However, if you expand the photo with the white porch railing in it, you can clearly see the second object.
