{dr}my friend and i saw a ufo/saucer while walking on the road

My friend and I were scouting his neighbor's house before halloween, because we were going to TP his yard. On the way back home, we took the road back to his house. Then, out of nowhere, a saucer shaped vehicle, flew up to above us, looking down at us, from probably a 20 degree angle. It was hard to gauge distance, but you could see lots of details, like the windows. The windows were yellow and opaque. You could not see in. It had a middle, gear or something, that rotated. It was very slender and smooth. The speed with which this thing came up to us and left us, was crazy. First there, then gone. Not really making turns, but just curving to where ever, it wanted to go. I asked Tracy about it years after it happened, probably abound 1997 or later and he completely forgot about it. I can never forget it. Not traumatic at all just utterly amazed. This is completely and utterly true and I do not care what people think. 
Also, it wasn't one of those wobbly things in youtube videos. This thing was straight out of a cutting edge sci-fi movie or something. Above my paygrade. Later.
