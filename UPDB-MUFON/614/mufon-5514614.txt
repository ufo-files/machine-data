{dr}Looking out windows to the east. First looked like shooting fireworks, but no explosion, then turned south

Watching TV. Object coming out of the horizon caught my eye. The first one was triangle with orange lights at each corner. Looked quite large, but I have no idea how far away it was. Other orange objects were coming from the same spot, but seemed to be single, not triangular. They went up and then went to the Southeast, disappearing into the clouds. They were orange lights,standing on end like an I. No flashing lights. 
I thought they were fireworks at first the way they came up from the horizon, but they never exploded and turned slowly. Still cant say that they werent fireworks, but I have never seen anything like this before.
I called the Three Rivers Police department and Dispatch in Centreville, but nothing was reported, they say.
My husband also witnessed this event.
