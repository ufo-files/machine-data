Triangle sighting in Newington, CT

As my wife and I were leaving the house at 8:50 pm I spotted a black triangle flying at treetop level at about a quarter mile north of our position along the Berlin Turnpike (US Route 5 and 15) and alerted her to it.
We were able to see it for about 6 to 10 seconds as it descended behind the trees / hill sharply turning north towards Cedar Mountain and were able to clearly see the underside of it. It had three white lights, one on each corner and a much smaller yellow/green strobe light in the center.
