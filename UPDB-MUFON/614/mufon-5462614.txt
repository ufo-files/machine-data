UFO hovering over a street in Berlin at forenoon

Went home to Berlin. As I arrived in my Street, I felt as if somebody would follow me. So I turned around, but nobody was there. Suddenly I got a strange feeling in my neck and head. So I looked upside in the sky and saw this UFO, it was long - shape like a cigar and had white and red lights at the side. It started to blink and hovered directly over my head. Suddenly I had tears in my eyes and I was happy beyond words. I beckon to this object. It blinked. Then - as I took my mobile phone to make a photo - it suddenly appears in a straight-line Path and so fast that I could not follow with my eyes anymore. It touched me extremely deeply, was like comming home - so it took me a few days to calm down. I was cying a lot but of joy. I'd like to mention, that - although we had New Year's Eve - the evening befor - I did not drank alcohol or any other substances that would have affected me in any way.
