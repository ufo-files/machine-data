{dr}Saw three or four grays in my bedroom

The craft casted a light in my room then they came through the wall and one stood at the foot of my bed it communicated to me that I should go back to sleep I closed my eyes but I opened them up again and there it was standing over me then it vanished I don't remember anything after it told me to close my eyes only that I opened them up a few second after it told me to go to sleep...... It seemed so real to this day I sometimes think it was all just a dream I have no evidence of the incident just my word
