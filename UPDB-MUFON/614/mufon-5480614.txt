{jpg} I spotted the Object through my NVG's and then through the GLID

I was stationed at Yakima Firing Center. I was supporting Night fire of 155mm SP Howitzers. We where sitting on OP 1 observing the impact area as the Battalion Range Safety. We would sit on OP 1 and report the impacts when asked by the FSO. I would use my GLID on the FIST-V to Lase the impact of the Shell and report the Polar Position to the FDC. During a break me and my assistant where sitting on top on the Track with the Hammer Head UP. Just watching the sky wearing NVG's while monitoring the radio. We observed a object coming from the south and watched it thinking it was a satellite until it stopped. It was about half way up from the horizon. I jumped down the rabbit hole and powered up the GLID and set it to illuminate and looking through the scope I found the object as it was moving very very slowly like drifting. I squeezed the cadillac's (Trigger) and it jumped 5-10 degrees from where it was and I switched the GLID to Designate and squeezed the triggers again the object moved from south to west I then continued to squeeze the triggers and lead the object and it followed the beam. During the observation/interaction the object changed direction as in 90 turns several times. The specialist that was my RTO/Driver was also observing the object outside the Track. I logged the incident in the Report I made and I was never contacted by anyone. Clear Night no cloud cover. Elevation 4500 feet. OP 1 Yakima Firing Center.
