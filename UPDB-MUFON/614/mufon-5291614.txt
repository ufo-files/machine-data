Multi-colored lighted object

At 8:30 pm until approximately 9:05 pm, I was putting out garbage pails when I noticed a rapidly changing, multi-colored stationary object in the wnw direction. The colors continually flashed, ranging from pink, green, blue and yellow. Towards the end of the sighting, it slowly started descending in a easterly direction to the nw. Exact distance unknown. I recorded 2 videos: first one is a bit shaky, but second one is not. Sighting was also witnessed in part by my wife.
