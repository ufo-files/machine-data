I was driving back to my boyfriends house and right before we got there we both saw something in the sky. It's a clear night too, there are absolutely no clouds anywhere around us. It was too low and too fast to be a plane, jet, helicopter, anything. But too high to be a drone. We couldn't see a shape but there was a blue-purple flamish cloud coming from behind it, but there was no trail. There was a gleaming light all the way around it. We were both speechless for a second then we both said it was a UFO and started looking around more. It disappeared about 3 or 4 seconds after we first saw it. My radio also had more static in it on my drive home.
