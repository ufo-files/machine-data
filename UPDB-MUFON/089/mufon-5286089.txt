White light and not a star

Facing north. Saw this come up from behind jet exhaust. Rapidly rose up. Then meandered to the left. Hung out a few minutes, then rapidly dimmed and winked out of sight.
Checked star map apps and kept watching it between my partner and I.
Could see the light through the clouds. Looked for familiar stars for comparison, but even Venus was not visible yet.
