{rjl} star like object high moving then zipping away

My mother and I were standing in the driveway, It was January 2010. I noticed a strange star, it stuck out immediately. It was holding still, as stars do and shined as such however it was a bit brighter. I pointed it out to her and she looked up and noticed it immediately.
