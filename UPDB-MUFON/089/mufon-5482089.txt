{jpg} Black triangle moving at a walking pace near AF Base

I saw a black triangle moving at a walking pace down the street. I was with my wife and 2 sisters and we all saw it. I pulled into some medical offices off the road to observe the object better. The object was see through and made absolutely no sound. It was about 4 car lengths on each of the triangular sides. I could not distinguish the thickness due to it being see through. I floated approx. 75 to 100 ft from the ground because I could see the dome that housed the lights but not the actual craft. It had 2 white lights and a red light.
