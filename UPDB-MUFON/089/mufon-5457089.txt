{cak} Orange lights

My wife and I saw two parallel rows of lights, each row containing 3-5 circular lights. Lights were bright orange. In between these two rows of lights I briefly observed a dark gray colored object appear. The dark gray object reminded me of what the exterior of a military aircraft looks like. I only saw this gray object in between the lights for about 3 seconds. 

The lights were bright and visible for approximately 3-5 minutes before dimming and no longer being visible. After all lights had disappeared, one more light gradually appeared, stayed visible for approximately a minute before dimming and vanishing like the lights before it. 

We began filming approximately 2 minutes after first we first observed the lights. More lights were present before we started filming. 

We used an IPhone X.
