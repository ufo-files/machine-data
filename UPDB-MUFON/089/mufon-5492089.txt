{jpg} Two lights, both brighter than stars and the nearest one larger and brighter.

I was walking with my other half and noticed two lights in the sky that looked unusually bright. One appeared to be a very bright star in the distance, and the other, a very large and very bright star with a hazy glow around it. We both stopped to observe further, thinking it was an airplane that would soon come into view, as we live in a neighborhood with a heavy flight pattern for airplanes. The two bright lights did not move or change direction. The bigger and brighter light appeared to be bobbing. I took out my iPhone to video it and when I looked at the video later on I noticed that the bigger bright light was pulsating and turned into a saturn-like ring. We continued to walk for 45 more minutes and the lights remained unchanged. Definitely not a star or airplane!
