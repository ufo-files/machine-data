Green continuous flashing light over car

My friend & I were on a roadtrip to Atlanta which took us through PA and WV. We stopped somewhere near the PA/WV border to get some gas & food and when we got back in the car I noticed some flashes of light in the distance, which I chalked up to being heat lightning. We drove from Rt 79 and took Rt 19 as a shortcut to Rt 77 and while we were driving this route through the mountains of WV at night (and of course with no other cars on the road!!!) we kept seeing these flashes of green light above us. My friend was driving at the time and we were discussing what this might be. She's more of the scientific mind who doesn't really buy into any of the extraterrestrial stuff but when we ruled out any radio towers or any other obvious light source even she started to wonder what this thing was. I was getting freaked out and it felt like the hair on the back of my neck was standing up every time this thing flashed. I got my cellphone out (although had NO idea who I was going to call!!) and there was no signal. We couldn't get even one radio station to come through. We travelled a while with the occasional flash above us and then it increased in frequency so much that even my level-headed friend was freaked out and started driving very fast so that we could get off that road as soon as possible. We never really figured out what the flash was. Maybe it was some static electricity thing or heat lightening. My stepmother suggested St Elmos fire but I can't remember exactly what she said that was. At any rate, when we drove back through that route on our return trip we did so during the DAYLIGHT. We also had no problems with receiving a cell phone signal or multiple radio stations to come in.
