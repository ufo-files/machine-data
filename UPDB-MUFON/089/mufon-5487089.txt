{cak} Saw 2 lights then 2 more then 2 more then then turned east.

My wife and I were driving down Route 132 North. I saw 2 lights and I asked my wife, I don't remember a tower being there. Then 2 more lights came on and then 2 more. Six altogether. It was moving from the North from the direction from WPAFB. 

The lights came towards us. They were 6 feet in diameter when they got close. It was 50 feet in the air and 50 feet away. It made no noise.  We stopped in the road. The craft moved to the East at about 5 mph. There was another vehicle behind us. I don't know if they reported the sighting or not.1
