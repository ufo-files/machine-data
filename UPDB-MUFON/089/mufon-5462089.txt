{jpg} 16 white lights in a V or boomerang shape traversed the sky in less than 30 seconds

Observation of approximately 16 white lights, brightness like flourscent bulbs, flew in a straight line path from NW to SE. Altitude was approximately 2000 feet. Speed was faster than commercial aircraft, possibly 5 times faster. Object(s) made no sound and gave off no emissions Three observations were made, initial one at 10:30pm, second at 11:00pm on 10/17/93 and third at 10:30pm on 10/18/93. There was no indication of any colored or strobe lights.
