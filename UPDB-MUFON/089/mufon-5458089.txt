Strange cloud

Whilst smoking outside my flat I noticed one cloud that looked like a rain cloud in a totally clear sky. I showed it to my gf as it looked so out of place. And I asked what the weather conditions are for just that one cloud to exist right there in a clear sky. For some reason I took a few pics of it. After going in I started playing around with the image not using filters just changing the exposure brightness hue etc using my phone. No gimmicks. As I did some very strange things appeared. You can see at the top of the altered images something that that looks like a boot. Which in other pictures shows a kind of vortex coming down from it into the cloud. Also a very demonic face appears in the cloud. In one of the original pics if you zoom in on the centre of the cloud you can see a dark disk shape I didn't see that at the time.
