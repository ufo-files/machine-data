{cak} Several lights hovering all changing direction randomly until lost over tree tops

Was leaving to work earlier than usual because i had a longer commute to a jobsite about 4:15 am, when i was about to drive onto a main road leaving my community i saw several lights hovering and changing form and position repeatedly. At first t thought they could be helicopters but one of the changes in movement was what i can describe as swirling downwards leaving a sort of a tail and then regaining its initial position rapidly, this was performed randomly by each one of them and that's when i thought it was strange and started to record video with my phone. In the video i was able to capture a portion of this movement before losing visual contact due to trees in the horizon.
