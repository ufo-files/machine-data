White orb type sphere shaped ball of light, with possibly a small section of red.

Standing in the backyard with my sister I noticed what looked like a star (one sixteenth of an inch to my naked eye) making short, fast zig-zag moves, mostly heading downward. There were many in the sky, at different distances & different travel lengths. Hours later, they were still in roughly the same position, while the moon had traveled it's normal path, making it obvious the unknown crafts were fairly stationary over a particular area.
