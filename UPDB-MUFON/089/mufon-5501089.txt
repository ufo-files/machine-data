My fianc1 and I were out walking the dog to where the dog owners usually meet. We were late and as a result only saw a few of our friends. They gradually left by about nine. We were hanging out with the dog she hula hooping me enjoying the moon, when I noticed a light moving quickly on the sky. Maybe 300 feet up, the perspective was kind of blurry she seems to think that they were much closer, I instantly said is this a UFO? We held hands, I was kind of frightened and ester later admitted the same. They moved quickly crossed the sky in maybe five seconds. Separated into two lights about an inch apart and disappeared. We were scared and in awe and feeling blessed and anxious when another of the dog owners came back to the park, a gentleman I don't know as well, as he keeps mostly to himself not speaking much to the other dog owners. As he came up we both got a bit nervous. So I asked if he had forgotten something. He said no and proceeded to awkwardly explain that he was there to see the moon. So I opened my heart and sat down to listen to him explain concepts of physics and light and perspective and the function of the cosmic fabric and God's creation. My heart moved quickly from anxiety to awe and inspiration and gratitude. When he left we stayed for a minute but ester was still frightened for the rest of the evening. We spent it painting. I.
