4 faint lights in a diamond formation extremely fast & very high altitude

First observed in the SW sky & very faint appearing to be at very high altitude.
Went directly overhead going NW presuming extremely fast since the entire sighting only lasted about 6 to 8 seconds from approx 15 dgrees SW to approx. 10-15 degrees NW. Near the end of the sighting one appeared to abruptly change direction before goingf out of sight.
 On Sept. 30th approx 10-12 years ago I was out camping with my wife when 
we both observed 3 objects in a V formation, again very high. I thought it a unique experience to observe 3 satellites so close together, and traveling in the same direction. Suddenly one of them completely stopped. As I watched the other two a second one also stopped dead. I watched the one that was still moving, but when I looked back to try to keep track of the other two, the first one must have also stopped, as I saw no more movement over the next ten minutes or so. Of couse, as soon as I saw the first one stop, I knew that they couldn't have been satellites after all.
