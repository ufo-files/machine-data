Three lights gathered into triangle formation and disappeared

I was driving and noticed a few red lights flying alone and thought they were planes. There were 3 different lights that appeared from various directions and they all gathered right in front of my view while driving north. The lights went into a triangle formation and in the middle a large red light appeared and the object disappeared. Two other witnesses were with me. It all happened in less than 2 mins
