Observed alone in bed. First thought coloured lights, then saw shape shifting orb

On January 10th, 2018 I was in bed watching Netflix when I noticed out my window colourful lights in the sky, possibly blue, green, red, and yellow. I thought it was an airplane but didn't notice any blinking. So I kept looking to try to figure out what I was looking at. I took out my phone and started recording. I zoomed in and noticed that it was a sphere that was breaking up into 3 smaller orbs that would all simultaneously rotate in a clockwise fashion and then come back together. This happened over and over for approx 2 hrs. I went back to bed and when I woke up and checked again 2 hrs later, it had moved north slightly. I was scared that if I kept filming or looking that "they would know" and come visit me, so I forced myself to go to bed. In the morning (7:30am) it was gone.
