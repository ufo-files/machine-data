{jpg} I thought it was a star until I got in the house and zoomed in looking for camera noise

1. I was in St. Louis Mo. taking pictures of Stars from 0400 to 0500. 2. I took a picture of Venus. I took another picture of what I thought was a star under Venus. 3/4. I thought the object was a star. I have no actions of the object to give. When in the house and zooming in on my pictures, it then became to me that I had photographed an object. It distinctly resembles a flying wing with sharp angles. 5. My feelings are of Astonishment.
6. I lost sight of the object by putting my camera up and walking into the house.
