{rjl} Red-orange lights in diamond shape; slowly faded away after ~30 seconds.

My friend and I were riding around the neighborhood on our skateboards, we then made a turn onto our street. I was in front of my friend, and i was focused on the ground. My friend-behind me- shouted at me to look in the sky, so I did. I looked into the sky and I saw 4 lights in a perfect diamond shape. These lights looked orange but kind of had a red tint to it. We saw these lights in between 2 trees, though the lights weren't actually "between the 2 trees" it looked like it was pretty close behind the trees. After about 30 seconds the lights slowly disappeared and we didn't see them any time after. My friend and I were very much in shock at the time of the event, and we didn't even think about pulling out our phones to record it.
