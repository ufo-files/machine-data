Object flew by slowly then took off 3 times and each time came by at a lower altitude until it hovered and i could see through port holes 3 figures

1. In my back yard as a child swimming in pool.. My brother and I both seen it pass over us slowly two times. Second time it was about a 100 feet off the ground and moving slowly over us then passed us and flew away out of site both times. My brother went in to grab some food .. the object came back and hovered over me about 50 feet off the ground and I could see figures moving and looking at me through the ships port holes..I felt calm and was not scared I remember waving up to them.. it hovered for about 20 to 30 seconds and I could see figures moving past port wholes and one figure stayed looking at me.. the ship was about 30 feet wide matted silver in color
2. Next year May or June at school while in the play ground i seen an silver object in the air.. it was there for a bit.. it was very high in the air and was the size of a pill .. i told the teacher on duty which she didn't believe me at first..but then all of a sudden before recess was over she told all the kids to go back in the school.. she was calm but stern to get is all back into the school..
I just want to know if anyone else in my area seen  all these events..I still think of these things till this day at 53 years old..
