{dr}It was very bright white ring around it with a fat cross in the middle

It was about 10:50pm I took my dog out before bed which I do every night and I always look up to see the stars. So as I was looking north east the night sky was beautiful and when I looked directly up and a little behind me I saw a bright object which at first I thought was a star but it was moving slowly so I thought it had to be a plane but I didn't see any lights flashing so I just kept looking straight up at it. It had a bright light around it with what I would call a cross but a fat cross inside of it. It kept traveling northeast and getting a little faster and light was getting dimmer and then gone. It had to go the speed of light. I got the chills and my eyes watered and I said thank you.
