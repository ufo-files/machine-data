Oval 2 tone yellow with 2 brown stripes joined by one lateral stripe

Driving south out of Sheffield towards junction 33 of M1 motorway about 1 mile from junction 33. Saw a yellow and brown shaped oval object in the sky about a mile away to the east on the other side of the motorway. It was stationary to begin with then moved slowly southwards about 500ft off the ground. I stopped on the hard shoulder to watch it and it changed to a sphere shape, yellow with a brown vertical horizontal cross and then accelerated away at great speed to the east down the valley until it disappeared from view in about 5 seconds. Friday 19th August 1988 at 1.30pm local time. I was amazed and wondered if anyone else saw it but no one else stopped. This is the only time I have reported this sighting to anyone other than close friends. 

I really wonder did anyone else see this event?

It is only after watching Mufon UFO hanger 1 files on Blaze TV that I decided to report the incident after 28 years.
