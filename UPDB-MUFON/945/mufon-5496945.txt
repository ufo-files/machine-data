{nd} I saw a large, geometric sphere with lights flashing at the corners.

I was lying in bed when I decided to look out the window, as I do most nights. One set of lights caught my attention; at first I thought it was a passenger plane, but after a minute I realized the object was stationary, or nearly so. I watched what appeared to be a geometric, spherical arrangement of lights change color, flash, pulse off and on. Occasionally the object seemed to move, but it was weird, it moved as if attached to a tether at its top most point, much like a tetherball. After a few minutes, I saw an actual passenger jet flying northwest to southeast, and could then compare the two objects. This verified that the first object was not a plane. I went outside to make sure I wasn't just seeing a reflection on my n bedroom window. The object was still there. (The lights, I'd estimate 10-50 points of light illuminated at any one time) moved, morphed, and gave the object the appearance of splitting into 2 objects from time to time, only to merge back together. The best explanation I can give of the UFO's appearance is that it looked a bit like a giant LED disco ball, with its movements and it's lights pulsing very organically. It looked, for lack of a better word, alive, nothing at all like the blinking of a video game arcade or advertising display. After about 20 minutes of watching the object, I finally made up my mind and went to sleep.
