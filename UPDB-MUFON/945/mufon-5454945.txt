{tjd} String of moving lights in a clear, night sky

We were up at 4:35 AM this morning looking at the sky and stars.

We observed a string of objects we thought were satellites moving from the northeast to the southwest in the same orbit, the same line, one after another. We observed this through a fixed sky light. The lights simply appeared in the sky at the same point, one after another approximately 3 seconds apart.

They were separated equal distance apart. From the time we first saw them until they ended we counted 14.

This happened is the span of 1-2 minutes. Very interesting.
