{jpg} Two very large orange glowing balls flying low to the ground

After the annual "Steam-o-rama" show in Windsor, PA in 2018, during the evening, two very large brightly glowing red orbs were flying left to right just above the barn, very slowly. One following the other. Once they flew to the end of the field, the first one dissipated and the other one did the same immediately afterwards. They were flying very low to the ground (tree level) and were less than 20 feet in front of my car as I was parked at the stop sign at Taylor Rd directly across the barn when I saw them. They were very large and flew very slowly.
