Strange light in window

Mt brother-in-law lives in Effort, PA which is close to Bucks County. His back-yard is facing the mountain and forest. Last Christmas he asked me about satillites. He said at night a spot light was shinning down in his bedroom window. Something woke him up and he saw it. The funny thing is that when he goes to the window the light fades but when he gets back in bed it gets bright. It happend at least 3 nights then he hung a blanket in his window. We were kidding it was a UFO. Then I learnd about the Bucks Co rash of sightings.
