Stars in cloudless blue summer sky.

My home is south facing, it is a clear sunny summer's day, and i am awaiting the sale of my house therefore i had plenty of time to relax in the garden. whilst sitting on a lounger facing west, the sight of a star in early afternoon was surprising, whilst observing the star,it became apparent that the star was moving south, this observation was approximately 5 mins in duration.

some weeks later, whilst sitting on the lounger facing the same direction as previous.
having seen the anomaly in the sky previously,i again looked in the direction where i had seen the star.....i was surprised to see the star again,however on this occasion,it had brought a buddy with him.
this time i saw the leading star travelling the same direction as previous,the following stat appeared to slow down / change direction for approx 20 secs, then proceeded to follow the leading star.
