{cak} Several stars moving than converge into one

I was at church on a wends day night. I had an unexplainable urge to leave. I told my mother that I had a headache and I wanted to go sit in the car. I went outside and sat in passenger seat if my mothers car. I was looking up at the stars. When I realized something wasn't right. The stars I was looking at we're moving. They were moving randomly as if battling. Than as if docking, at least five of them all converged on a single star that did not move. After that I blacked out and the next thing I saw was people walking out of the church. Church was over and I lost from a hour to an hour and a half of time.
