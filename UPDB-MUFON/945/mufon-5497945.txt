{cak} Bright white, turned red, dropped after holding, went back up 1/3 of original height, then dropped out of sight.

Walking with my 2 cats, one of which alerted me come outside. I kept a brisk pace and turned to see if they were keeping up with me. As I looked back I saw in the sky a bright light and thinking it was a helicopter. When it turned red I thought it maybe changing direction and showing a side view. But suddenly it started dropping straight down. Within a few seconds it looked like it bounced back up about 1/3 the original height, then dropped again and disappeared behind the landscape. It was too far away, too bright, too high in the sky, and too large to be a toy, especially the way it lost altitude, then regained, then dropped again. There was no sound, no smoke or mist, and no trail of light or smoke. I got in the car and drove toward the sighting, but I was miles away and saw nothing else, no lights in the sky afterwards. As I started driving I realized that light was too large to have been a toy.
