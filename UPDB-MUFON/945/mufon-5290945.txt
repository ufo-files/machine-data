Cruise in the Bahamas, middle of the ocean we saw orbs

I went on a cruise to the bahamas. We were on the balcony in our room. Miles and miles away from an island we saw three lights coming towards us. We didn’t think anything about it. Most airplanes have lights on them. This was in the shape of a triangle. It was directly above the ship. The lights then broke rank and were moving around each other. Almost in a playing manner. After a few seconds of this going on. The lead light took off. Faster than any known aircraft that I’m aware of. Shortly after the other two orbs followed it, just as fast.
