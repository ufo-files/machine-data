{cak} Balls of light that change shape and pulsate. Hover change direction follow ridge lines. See on a regular basis.

I was at home letting my dogs outside.I thought what I was seeing was stars but I noticed erratic movements and seen one of the lights following a ridge line. The objects in mention was balls of white light with rotating red and blue lights either on top or bottom. The balls also changed shape completely. There were three to four or these balls at different places around my home. They will dart and change direction in a instance. Some will disappear while others will stay in the same place for hours. My mom and father both witnessed this. We see them almost nightly. Last night I did see what appeared to be a drone flying around after the balls had disappeared. I do have video of the last two nights but I am not sure if they are good enough to turn in? Sometimes they do drop below the fog line.
