Dark triangular object moved slowly overhead then disappeared

Just before 2100, me and the kids felt a low rumbling in the house. At first we thought it was another extremely low flying c130 (of which numerous had come over in the previous year).
I rushed out front to see if I could see it, but saw nothing. I went out back and still saw nothing. I light a cigarette on the back porch, as the vibration continued to get more intense. I looked up again, and saw a very large dark shape moving VERY slowly over the house. It blocked out the stars, but was difficult to make out a shape over than roughly triangular. There were 3 red lights that seemed to pulsate rather than blink. It had a very tiny green light near the back half. It came to a stop directly over my house, from east heading west. It made no noise. It was still for about 30 seconds or so, then vanished. No "shot off, zoomed away, or ascended" just GONE. 
After I told the kids and my wife it was like a "stealth helicarrier" due to its size.

Anyway I msg'd a friend of mine about it the next day, then went on with my life. A few days ago , I heard about the Phoenix lights and thought that sounded like the same thing and got curious. I searched online to see if anyone had seen the same thing. I found a sight with alot of sightings were listed. I found one from cordele ga a few minutes after mine. It was put online the 19th of January and I have screenshot my conversation on the 14th with my friend.
