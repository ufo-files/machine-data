{dr}Reflective orb, followed by helicopter LENEXA KS

Was talking to my husband outside the city pool, getting ready to call the kids in to go home. I noticed a very bright flash just north of me, and instinctively followed the flash with my eyes to see the source of something bright enough on a sunny day to get my attention. I began watching a silver orb hover just above the tree line from where I could see, and after watching for around a minute I started to point it out to my husband. As soon as I lifted my finger in that direction, he said he was already watching it, he had seen it flash too. I kept my eyes locked on it, for a few minutes, until it seemingly vanished. I began standing on a table seeing if I could trace where it went, turning up unsuccessful, but then noticed a helicopter flying in from the southwest, toward the direction of where the orb had been.
