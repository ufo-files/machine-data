UFO event in Cordoba Argentina near the dam named river's mouth, a extrange objet suspended in sky with colour changing

I was in my house, I went to the backjard, something in the sky caught my attention, I saw a kind of star but it was brighter than normal so I turned on my Nikon P900 camera and made zoom and I notice that it was an UFO. It happened in Villa Dolores, province of Cordoba, Argentina, near here there is a dam, and a lot of people here have seen stranges things in the sky near the dam (river's mouth). The object was suspended in atmosphere, at a first sight it seemed to be a star, but with the 83x of zoom in my digital cammera the shape was similar to a coin with colour changing randomly. It changed direction to the left (SW) then of 45 min aproximatelly it disappeared from the sky.
