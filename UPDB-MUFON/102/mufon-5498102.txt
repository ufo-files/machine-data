{nd} Red light hovering over the lake

I had gone to the marina at Deer Creek State Park along Ohio 207 south of Pancoastburg, to clear my mind and to do some thinking about some things going on in my life, and I had been there for about an hour. I was listening to the local college football game on the radio when all of a sudden there was interference to the signal. I quickly looked up and out of the front of the car (I had just texted a friend on my cell phone) when I saw the light in the sky. I quickly got out of the car, which had me facing East and looked up at the object, which was about 700 to 800 feet directly above Deer Creek Lake. It was there for about 10 seconds, then shot south for a bit then straight up and then disappeared. Between the radio interference and the object, I quickly got back into my car and left the marina.
