{cak} Saw craft over tree tops. Two entities wavering in and out of dimension

It was either 11pm or so 9-2-13 or just after midnite 9-3-13. I heard noise outside. I went out and looked up. I thought, holy s**t these things are real! I saw about 100 feet away two entities that seemed to be emerging from what looked just like an old black and white tv flickering image; from that to a typical looking 4 ft alien feature.

I walked towards it and then suddenly they were gone and the craft was up over the tree tops. It went back and forth like it was pacing. It stopped briefly and I saw a beam of white light scan the street or house. I am not sure for what and it was about 2 seconds and then gone.

I do not want to talk about anything else. I am posting this because I think someone else saw it and I am hoping that they will post here also and I will know that I am not crazy.
