Two shiny objects

In the afternoon, I saw two shiny objects flying from south to north and abruptly turning east and going upward. They were shiny like aluminum and the sun reflected off of them. I tried taking a video of them but the camera didn’t seem to pick it up. They went behind the clouds. Then I only saw one which eventually disappeared above the airplane and i couldn’t see it anymore.
