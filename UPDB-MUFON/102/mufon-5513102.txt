(GS) Looked up and noticed it. Got so bright, i had to squint my eyes.

I was sitting on the back deck around 11pm. I looked up and noticed a bright object in the tree line. AT first I thought it was an airplane, but noticed there was no noise and it wasnt moving in any direction. As I was looking at it, I also noticed it getting brighter and brighter as it was hovering there. It started from a dull orange and gradually got brighter and brighter to a brilluant white. So bright that I had to squint my eyes. I took more pictures of it and when I looked down to see them on my phone, I looked up and it was gone. It all lasted about 15 minutes.
