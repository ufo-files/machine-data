Bright non constant flashing object seen in sky very irrational flight path

I went outside to smoke a cigarette & it was a beautiful clear crisp night. About a 1/4 moon & I was staring at Orions belt. The constellation was in the SW sky. I saw what looked like a bright star blink to the left of the belt & it caught my attention. Approximately 30 seconds later I saw another blink & it was slightly above the first one. At this point I was kind of puzzled & was waiting for another blink & my daughter yelled at me from in front of the house which would be to the north. She came running behind the house & I was still looking up waiting for it to blink or flash again so I went to the front of the house & she pointed the object out to me. We had no Idea that we were both outside until she yelled for me. The object looked like a bright star at that point & headed north east at a rapid rate. My location is about 45 minutes southeast of the Indianapolis airport & you could see planes coming & going. This object stood out. I’m also within 5 miles of the northwest edge of a military base & it did not originate from the base or the airport & flew the opposite way from both. While I was looking at the sky waiting for it to blink my daughter saw it stationary in the air & said when she moved it started moving. Thats when she yelled at me & in the seconds It took me to go around front it was moving rapidly compared to the airplanes in the background. I was in the military & have lived next to a base for several years. I can’t explain what I saw. The fact that my daughter her friend & I all saw the same thing behaving differently within a couple minutes not knowing we were all outside makes me question what it was. The two blinks or flashes I saw were odd but i would have wrote it off if she hadn’t came & had me go to the front of the house & see this thing fly off.
