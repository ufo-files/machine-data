{jpg} Triad of lights bright like stars and disappeared fast

We were out stargazing and watching planets through my telescope. It was clear sky, Jupiter, Saturn and Moon had presence in the sky. We were aligning our telescope towards Saturn in the east side and I suddenly noticed three bright stars like lights, just like Jupiter suddenly pop-up from the North. At first we thought they might be aircraft, but no, these were brighter than Jupiter. I showed it to my wife who was with me and two other friends who were also with us. We were so excited that we could not even think of pulling out our smartphones to take pics. The lights hovered and shot way high at a speed we have never witnessed. The lights then disappeared. We were stunned.
