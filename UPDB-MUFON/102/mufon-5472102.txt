Fast moving, dark object with four dull amber lights.

I was observing the clear night sky out in my back yard with binoculars and tracing what I think where two satellites (one about 10 minutes behind the other)on a fixed path SW to NE. On tracking the last one through the binoculars all of a sudden, hurtling in the opposite direction and at an altitude lower than the satellites, came a very fast moving dark object with three dull amber lights and a fourth one offset off the frontal leading apex. As it moved it tilted smoothly much in the way a fighter jet would, but it was definitely no jet as it was totally silent.
