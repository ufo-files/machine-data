Hovered over head shined light on us then left.

A friend and I were walking back from hunting to my parents home.We were about a half mile from home through a wooded area and this beam of light shined down on us.It made no noise.You could see kind of an outline around the light you could see the disc shape,My friend and I took a shot at it with shot guns.You could hear the buck shot hit the thing,it didn't move right away but when it did it was gone in a blink of your eye.The light was bright kind of yellowish and it was like a beam.That was the first time I had ever seen anything like that,it was Sept.1960 I was 13 yrs.old and I have had 5 more sightings sense then.
