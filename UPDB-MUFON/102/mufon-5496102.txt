{nd} Circled across sky twice with a glowing light at the bottom that faded in and out

On the evening of September 9th 2014 at approximately 8:16pm eastern standard time, I twice observed and object circle the sky. It moved extremely smooth and in a north west circular motion, made no sound, and had a single orange light on the bottom that faded in and out every 3 seconds or so. This object was roughly 300-500ft to the west of me. on its second pass,I was able to shoot video with my cell phone. I wasn't able to zoom, but if u look closely(may have to zoom video), u can see the object move from left to right about 200-300ft in the air. It was probably 7 to 8ft in diameter. I you have any questions, feel free to contact me. Thank you Jon - Louisville
