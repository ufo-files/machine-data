ROW OF GREEN LIGHTS/VERY LOW AND FAST/NO NOISE

I was returning home from Athens, Ga. traveling South on Hwy 441. I was just comming into Dublin, GA about 10:15 or 10:30, and had just past the elementary school in Dublin, Ga. It had been raining, but there was "open sky" no trees or buildings to block my veiw. I was slowing down as I was in a low speed zone, and looked to the right side of the windshield and happen to notice something moving through the air "very fast" and unusally low. At first I thought a plane was about to crash. I slowed down some more as it traveled across the air to the left of my car. I rolled my window down to see more clearly, and noticed there was "no noise"..no sound at all.. I saw only a row of green lights, I could see no shape other than the row of lights. Maybe six to eight of them, and from my veiw from the ground, they were about the size of a "mayonaise jar lid". They flashed "really fast." I came almost to a complete stop as I watched it, then all of a sudden the lights just disappeared and it was gone. I watched and nothing reappeared. This took place in just about 6 to 8 "SECONDS" !!  I called my husband to tell him what I had seen, I was still about 20 miles from our home in Rockledge, to see if maybe he could see anything from there. He didn't, but talked to a man a couple of days later that had seen the same thing, at about the same time, and he lives on Hwy. 199. We do not know his name.
