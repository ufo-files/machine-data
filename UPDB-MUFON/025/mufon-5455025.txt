{tjd} Two incidents same day

We were traveling on Atlantic Ave in Gloucester. This was an hour or two before sunset. Gina pointed out an object and I pulled over. Over the ocean but near the rocky shore/road was seen something hovering and moving slowly. It looked like two glowing car cigarette lighters, attached apparently but we could not see how. Several people were enjoying the shore looking here and there, but no one noticed what we saw.
An hour plus later, we were up the road at Good Harbor beach and much after the sun set, we were still there when it was completely dark. Stars appeared. Which was odd because the sky was mostly cloudy. Even more odd as we looked at each star, the star was a “double” or a pair. Many of them.
We took photos of both the ufo and the star pairs; none of which shows anything of what we both observed.
