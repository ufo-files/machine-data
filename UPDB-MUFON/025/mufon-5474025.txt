{jpg} Flying chevron with five lights visible.

It was between 11pm and 12am 8/24/2017 when I went outside my house to retrieve my reading glasses from my truck. As I was walking the approximate fifty feet back to the house, I looked up at the eastern sky, at approximately 75 degrees from horizon, to view the constellations and noticed a chevron shaped object with dim lights flying a straight line and constant speed from North to South. The distance, size, nor speed of the object could be determined. Our local airport (HRL) is within two miles of the house so I assumed this was an airplane, however the object did not have the standard aircraft marking lights and was too high to have taken off from the airport. The five lights on the bottom were as dim as the starlight behind it and, if not for the movement, I would not have noticed it at all. I observed the object for approximately one minute and thirty seconds until it was no longer visible.
