Starlike red orb with flat gold panel aft of orb

Observed a red- orange pointy orb with a flat opaque square panel aft of the orb. Once the object passed the orb light was obscured by the panel and appeared as a brighter gold light with good shape definition. No noise, no ill effects. Duration of sighting was about 20-30 seconds, southwest to northeast.
