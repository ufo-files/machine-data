{kt}3 red lights over 3 blue lights hovering, dropping, ascending, zigzagging.

In Scottsdale AZ visiting my cousin. Friday April 25, about 8-9 pm, eating dinner outside. Looked up to see stars, and noticed near where the sun had set, an object hovering for a few seconds. Then it took off in unusual patterns, up, down, sideways, etc. 4 people were with me looking at it, and they thought it could be a remote controlled plane. But I didn't think so, as it went up into a cloud at one point. It was rectangular, with 3 blue lights and 3 red lights (I think the red lights were on top but I'm not absolutely sure). I took pictures with my cell phone and videos as well, that did not turn out well due to the darkness. The lights did not blink, they were steady. I was excited to see it, and ran to the front yard to get a better look, with my nephew. He also saw it disappear into a cloud which made us think it was not a remote controlled plane. However, it last appeared going down to the ground (or at least low enough that houses blocked our view). We waited a few minutes but it did not reappear. It was in the SW sky, a little west of the flight path of jets flying into Sky Harbor. The total time we saw it fly was about 10 mins.
