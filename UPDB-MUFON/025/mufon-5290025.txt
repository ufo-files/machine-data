Tic tac ufo flying with commercial jet and changing flight path frequently

Randomly this morning I had walked down to my work van to grab some supplies while on a job and randomly looked up to the sky and noticed a commercial jet flying at about max altitude and noticed a white TicTac shaped object just below it. This morning was clear as day and was extremely visible from the ground. This object flew with the jet and randomly would change direction, slow down, then rapidly speed up for what had to have been my guess hundreds of miles keeping pace with the jet and also produced zero contrail nor emissions. I have attempted to come up with many different theories and have yet to come up with a reasonable explanation.
