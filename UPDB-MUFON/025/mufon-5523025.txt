pulsating red/orange object.

I had just put my son to bed at 2100hrs feb 15th 2011. Saying goodnight,I noticed his curtains were slightly open so I leaned over to close them correctly.As I pulled them together I noticed a very large red/orange light moving across the horizon much slower than most things in the sky.At first I just thought it was a plane or helicopter,closed the curtains and turned to return to my wife who was downstairs.I got to his bedroom door and it hit me that this was a strange colour of light for a helicopter or plane, plus it was travelling very slowly,nearly a kind of floaty sort of way.So I returned to the window for another look.After standing in the room for about 5 minutes watching the object pulsing,red in the centre and more orange on the edges, I was sure this was no plane or helicopter and with no noise.I live with hills all round me and you hear planes/helicopters before you see them.But no noise at all from the large light.Another thing is it was following power lines and easily turned with the change in direction of the power lines.The sudden change in direction really caught my attention as I have never seen any flying vehicle change direction so suddenly.The last ten minutes or so I headed outside to watch the object travel east following the power lines below it.I shouted from outside for my wife to come and take a look but she thought I was joking  and would not come out.I live in a small village and have not told any other person as they would think I was crazy,but needed to get it of my chest.I had never seen anything else before or since.
