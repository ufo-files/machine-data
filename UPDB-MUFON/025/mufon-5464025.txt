{jpg} Assisting in moving concret blocks from side of runway when Mich Black saw the orb moving from the south

On the 3 Dec.2018 three pilots were assisting the removal of the concrete blocks from the side of runway, it was dark (apx.9:30 pm.) When one of the pilots pointed out a moving orb, bright then any star in the night sky, and caused everyone to look. They watch as it moved from the south and as it past over head it made a sweeping right and headed East.
 The pilot that caught sight of the object had sighting three weeks ago which had the same light harmonics as the one that he was observing at that time. I told him and the two others that once you see one of these orbs you are likely to see more due to the special illumination features.
  This pilot had just finish his two year V.F.R. class. which is required all pilots who are actively flying.
