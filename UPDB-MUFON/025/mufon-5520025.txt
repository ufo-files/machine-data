two extremely bright stationary lights

Taking garbage out and looked up and saw two very bright lights much too low to be stars, and so bright that they caught my attention... so I ran and got my cell phone and took a quick picture and zoomed it and saw the first one was a craft of some sort and zoomed on the second one and it had the same shape but not as bright. The second one then turned into a cylinder and was multicolored. I live in the vicinity of Picatinny Arsenal and this is not the first time I saw something like this, they always seem to hover and very brightly, brighter than bright, pulsate. Then in an instant they are gone. No noise. At one point the bigger one on top had another blinking light that seemed to merge with it.
