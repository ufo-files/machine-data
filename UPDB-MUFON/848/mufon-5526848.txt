Saw object at approx. 15-20K ft. metallic and flashing like reflected light of sunset

Object flew from SE to NNW about the speed of a fast airplane. I first thought I was seeing a bird flapping its wings, but the speed and trajectory clearly were too straight and the light that at first seemed like the underside of a birds wing clearly became more of a metallic glint.

As it was near sunset (about one hour before), the "light" appeared like a dark-colored metallic object that flashed to bright (whitish color), but not so bright that it seemed any more than the suns reflected light.

The rate of the "on-off" of the light/reflection seemed to be about 4-5 times per second.

The speed and altitude, as subjectively compared to the many airplanes that overfly my house (I am 20 miles from a regional airport), was 15 thousand feet or so, no noise or contrail, about 500-700 knots (faster than all but military aircraft at that altitude in this area).

The size was smaller than a regional aircraft, such as a Canadair jet often seen in the area, perhaps 1/2 the size and from my viewpoint (southwest of the object which was about 40 degrees above the horizon) the objects side profile was saucer or cigar-shaped. 

This was the first time I have ever seem an object that I could not explain.

I was outside watching my children jumping on a trampoline. I was not on the trampoline and the children did not see the object, nor did I point it out to them, as my viewing frame was only about 5-10 seconds long before it passed my view due to being blocked by trees.
