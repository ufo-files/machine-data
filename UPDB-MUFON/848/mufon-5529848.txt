Star-like object slowly binking on and off

I was driving home from work early (I work night shift) so it was almost 2 am. I was in the middle of all the windmills near chalmers/Wolcott exit going north on I-65. I looked up in the sky and saw what I though was 2 stars very close to each other. But the one was moving at a slow but steady pace but blinking on and off kind of slowly as it was moving south east. It wasnt flashing lights like any kind of airplane, when it was off it completely disappeared until blinking on again. In the sky it appeared to me about the same size as a star but as I watched it I could tell that it was much closer. I watched it as long as I could until it was behind my car and I didn't want to drive off the road. 
  I felt pretty excited because I this is now my 4th ufo sighting in the last 4 months. I have since really started researching the history of UFOs and keeping an eye out for them, but only seem to them when I am not really looking. I know something is going on right under our noses and we all have the right to know what it is.
