Witnessed a very bright ball of light at approximately 150 feet above ground fly across neighborhood and vanish mid-flight - Very fast, and no noise.

On the evening of August 8th, I was meeting two friends for dinner at 8:30 pm. I parked my car in the parking lot of the restaurant and got out to walk in. It was a beautiful evening, still light out. I looked up in the sky to appreciate the evening and my eye caught site of a very bright object, or ball of light about a block or two away and approx. 150 feet in the air. It was silent and moving at incredible speed toward where I stood. My first thought was that I was witnessing a shooting star or some sort of fireworks (only there was no noise). The object/light continued past my location and within seconds vanished into thin air. It never lost altitude, and there was never any noise. The whole experience lasted probably 10 - 15 seconds. That's when I felt I had seen something incredible. I looked around to see if anyone else seemed to notice this site. No other witnesses were noted. I went into the restaurant and told my friends about this encounter. Also spoke with my family about it later that evening, but didn't want to appear to be a UFO fanatic. I did contact the news stations to see if others had reported anything similar (nothing noted). I also spoke with the electric company to see if there had been any transformer problems in the area that night. None were reported. I still believe I witnessed something very special that evening, but have no explanation for what it might have been.
