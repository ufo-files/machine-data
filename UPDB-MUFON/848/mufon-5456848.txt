Observed hoovering craft the size and shape of an aircraft carrier turned upside down with a flat bottom and a center light- revolving. I had seen amber lights in the sky and took note. I drove up to a light on the right side of the 4 lane highway highway. I stopped the car and my wife , my brother-in-law and I walked down a short embankment to the right of the highway and stood under this massive object and looked up. There was no highway traffic whatsoever. The revolving light was encased in what appeared to be double pane glass that measured approx 10 feet in depth below the object with a circumference of approx 50 feet. How long we observed is unknown and we said nothing to each other. Then I heard an elongated screetch like a large very large metal door closing and heard two bumps that reminded me of a ski boat going into gear from neutral. This object then moved and slowly shifted to the east and increased speed in steady increments from 1 mph to 10 to 30 to 50 to 100 to the speed of light
