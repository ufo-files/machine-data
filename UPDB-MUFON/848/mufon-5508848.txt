{cak} Travelling North on Rt 125, noticed object with 3 flashing lights then dissapeared

I was travelling north on Rt 125 from Barrington to Rochester near town line. It was approx 7:45-8pm, I noticed 3 lights connected by what appeared to be wings just above the treeline. This appeared to be just over the "Waste Management Landfill" area in Rochester NH. The lights would flash and they were extremely bright. At first I thought it was a tanker from Pease ANG base but then realized it was too low for that and no sound. The sequence of the lights were such that first the center light would be very bright then would dim and as the center light dimmed both lights to the right and left would get extremely bright. I lost sight of it as I turned a corner, so I turned around and tried to see it again but nothing...
