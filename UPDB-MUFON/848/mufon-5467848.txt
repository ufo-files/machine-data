{rjl} On a hazy and cloudy night the object appeared about 9:30pm and remained stationary about one hour then gradually moved away west and northwest

Surprisingly, this object appeared on a hazy and cloudy night. I observed it's shape very closely with my naked eye.This object changed shapes seemingly by a designed sequence of patterns. the most observed pattern being a solid white dot in a lower left position and being constant. In the upper right position and connected to the constant white dot, there flashed on and off, a larger black dot, with both dots surrounded by starlike appendages. This object at times had the appearance of a black-faced white cat or kitten with a white ball at it's feet. Two helicopters approached this object from the west and south of the object and were at the same altitude as they passed by it on the left and right also a airplane coming from the east flying low passed just under the object then swerved westward.These observations are truly amazing!
