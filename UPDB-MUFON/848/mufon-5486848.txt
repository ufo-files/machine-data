6 bright lights either one craft or six in formation

I work straight nights i went out for break at 1am i walked around the corner of the building and im kinda lost for words. There was six white non blinking lights in a horizontal row with a slight bow in the center. I dont know if it was one single craft or six in formation but as it got closer four (the two outside sets) of lights went out makeing it appear smaller a few seconds later a red blinker came on way over the top of the remaining two lights. Im familliar with planes in the night sky and area and iv not seen this before.. When i first observed the lights i ran back in the shop to get someone else to witness what i was seeing. With no one around i ran back out and continued watching it. From first sight i knew this was unusual i have only told my wife about this i instantly felt like people are going to think im crazy. I have no photos or video just my word and even that cant give justice to what i witnessed
