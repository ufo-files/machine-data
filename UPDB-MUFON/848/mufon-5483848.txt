{rjl} A moving star

It was about 9:00pm and I was taking my trash out. It was a warm night which wasn't normal from where I am. I was looking at the stars which I normally do, and saw a weird object moving. The night sky was beautiful, not a cloud in sight, no cars driving by, no helicopters or air planes out. Dead silent. I thought it was a helicopter but it was too quiet and too high up in the sky. There was no noise what so ever. No flashing lights, just a bright light. Small as a star per-say. This thing or object was small like a star and bright. It was noticeable for me too see without my glasses on, and it moved slow, and was by its self. It kept a constant speed for quite some time. Then while I was watching it, my eyes kind of shifted and I couldn't find it anymore. When I couldn't find it, I felt weird, not sure on what I saw in the night sky. I was outside for about 5 minuets or so, just feeling confused. I went inside my house and my sibling was wondering why I took so long.
