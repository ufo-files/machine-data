{dr}BRIGHT ORANGE LIGHT IN WESTERN SKY, CHANGES BRIGHTNESS, HOVERS THE FADES ONLY TO RETUN AND THE FLY OFF

I went outside to check on my pool filter and when returning to house I noticed a very bright orange light in the sky to the northwest. It made no sound and varied in brightness from very bright to very dim. It moved in an erratic somewhat circular motion then it seemed to fade away. I ran into the house get my wife and my camera but when we returned outside, no sign of the light could be seen. My wife went back inside but I stayed outside and decided to walk up my street toward where I had seen the light. I got in front of my neighbors house when the light reappeared from the north. It move through the sky to the original spot I had seen it. It remained there, not moving but changing in brightness as it had before. I took 1 picture of it using my old olympus digital camera D-580. The object appeared to rise in the sky , gaining altitude, then disappearing. The object made no sound.
