{cak} Three silent, increasingly bright flashes of light, lighting up the sky

Some of my friends and I saw what must have been this last night as well, in Vista, CA, sometime in the early nighttime, probably between 9 and 10pm. I was looking upwards towards the sky, while a friend was looking down at the ground. Both of us saw only the flashes of light, but even my friend looking at the ground saw the flashes (and looked up), showing it was similar to the intensity of lightning flashes. To me, looking upward, it looked like three flashes in a row, very quickly, with increasing brightness per flash, coming from a central point in the sky, but behind the roof of the house next to which we were standing so I couldnt see what that central point might be. Then, nothing. Didnt hear a sound (though there were several people with us in the open garage having lively conversations) and couldnt see anything in the sky afterwards. But the flashes were absolutely unmistakable; seemed to light up the entire sky, and at least three of us definitely saw it.

I believe this is the same event that was reported earlier today in Carlsbad, and wrote the above comment on that page as well (http://www.ufostalker.com/ufostalker/UFO+Sighting+in+Carlsbad+California+United+States+on+December+7th+2012/44403) but am reporting it here separately in case they are different events.
