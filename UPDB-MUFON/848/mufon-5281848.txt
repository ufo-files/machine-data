Glowing light viewed from my page window

I was flying home for my business trip in New York city. About 45 minutes to an hour into the flight I looked out my window and noticed when I thought was a plane at first until I saw The light pulsate. If I had to guess the distance when I first saw it it would have been about 5 miles from the plane and would have been the size of a golf ball. Because I did not know what it was I took out my phone and started taking pictures of it and zooming in to see what it was but could not identify it. At one point, probably 15 minutes in I noticed the distance was much greater but did not see it move. So it started it roughly 5 miles from the plane and easily move to a distance of 10 to 15 miles from the plane. This light stayed with us for about 30 minutes until our plane change directions that I could not see it anymore from our window. I also took two videos and several pictures but in the video you can definitely see this thing glowing and pulsating and different colors and what looks like rotating inside this ball.
