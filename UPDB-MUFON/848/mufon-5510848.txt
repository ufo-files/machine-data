{nd} Orange triangular object over Chicago night sky

we stepped out of our house (3 of us) and noticed an object overhead. it was 5/19/2013  21:05.  sky was clear, we could see the moon and a couple of stars. Difficult to determine how high the object was.
Object was triangular, with solid edge, orange in color. The angles of the triangle were rounded.  It was nearly directly overhead and traveling from slightly east heading northwest. We watched it for 6-7 seconds. There were no flashing lights, the object was very bright, with a haze/orb around it.  The haze had a spiked appearance.  There was no sound at all. path was straight, not erratic.  We took 2 snapshots with an iphone, and a very short video.  After seeing the object, we saw an airplane coming from the west. we could clearly hear the plane and see the flashing lights.  There was no sound and no flashing light on the object. 
We could not have been the only people in the Chicago area to see this...
All 3 of us saw the same thing.
