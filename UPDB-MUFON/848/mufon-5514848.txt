Observed a white circular object reflecting the sun. Moving fast, perfectly round.

Note: Submitted by Field Investigator.

On Sept. 2,2012, I was sitting outside in my backyard playing with my dog and as I sat back I noticed a white orb over top of my roof at a 60 degree angle west of where I was sitting. I looked twice to make sure nothing was wrong with my eyesight and realized that I was really seeing what I was seeing. I would describe it as white orb ( or disk) that was moving at a constant pace coming from the East and going towards the west. It was around 6:15 pm. The sighting lasted for not more than a minute until it disappeared behind the roof in front of my house.
It seemed like the sun was reflecting on its surface. I was sure it was not a plane or helicopter because of its perfectly round shape and also because of its speed. I was not sure if it could have been a weather balloon since there is a small airport not far from where I live and there is one across the river in Terrebonne.
I was so intrigued by what I saw that I looked on the internet for reports of UFO sightings and this is when I found your association so this is why I contacted you.
