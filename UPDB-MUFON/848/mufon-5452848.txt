Orb at the beach

My friend and I were at the beach one night in my small hometown in Florida going for a walk. When we first arrived we noticed an orange glowing orb off in the distance we thought was a weird star, maybe a planet. We sat down and the orb moved closer not far from us at all. It was quite big, and seemed to slowly get closer. We were a bit nervous to say the least, but we stood up and after a second it just disappeared. Not like our planes or anything, it had no sound and it left no trail of smoke or light to show it had been there. It seemed to simply disappear into the night like a snap of the fingers. I’ve always been a believer, but this just made it even more real to me.
