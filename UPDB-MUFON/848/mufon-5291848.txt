Saw on break in sky

At 410 am I was at work as a nurse that works night shift...my friend and I were outside looking at the night skies while having a smoke break like we always do. I looked up and saw an object coming from ne skies..there were clouds in the se area and dark skies in ne area when I saw a triangle shaped object with gray chevrons gliding across the sky towards se...there were no lights and no sound which I thought was strange..it seemed to be moving slowly...I told my friend to look up and he did but not before it glided behind some clouds and didn't appear after the clouds..I asked him if he saw it and he said what...I asked him for a pen so I could draw what I saw and showed him..it blended well in the night sky but was totally visible...the object had to be large for the size I saw that glided above the clouds and disappeared....I asked my friend how high are those clouds and he said about 10 - 15 thousand ft...birds don't fly that high...I told him I just saw a UFO and here we are...reporting what I saw.
