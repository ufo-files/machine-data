Orange spheres moved across sky in line. They disappeared and reappeared in various spots, lighting up and replicating themselves.

We were driving home on Pinetucky Rd when we saw orange spheres move across the sky together in a line that seemed to swish. We stopped the car to watch them. They disbanded and disappeared. Then they began to reappear one by one in different parts of the sky and started replicating themselves. When they lit up another light would appear then another. 3 times every time this process started. Then the objects would disappear and others would light up in other places. Sometimes the replicated objects were in a straight line, sometimes in an arc. This continued for a few minutes, but then just seemed to move farther away until they just appeared as glitter in the sky.
