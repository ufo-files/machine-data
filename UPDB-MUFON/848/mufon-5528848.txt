While using the bathroom at about 3:00 AM I had not turned on the bathroom light yet when I saw four small white orbs in a diamond shaped pattern rise fron the southwest at slightly above ground level and silently travel upward at about a 70 degree angle. They traveled in unison as if connected but I saw nothing between them. They rose to a point where my roof overhang caused them to be out of sight. They traveled silently and in a flat plane and just before I lost sight of them it appeared as if I was looking at their underside. They were baseball sized and seemed to be about thirty feet away initially and about seventy feet high as the rose in a north west direction. I saw a single orb very similar to these about three years ago and recently reported it. In both instances I felt a calm sense about what had just occurred. I then returned to bed as if nothing exciting had happened.
