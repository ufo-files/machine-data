Very bright light then surged extra bright white light dimmed to a orangish red. moved sw not flashing, solid colour

We were driving down sidney st. In our town, observed a bright white star like object in the sky. We thought it was Venus so we watched it, we observed the light surge to an even brighter white colour, extremely so that it caught our attention. We couldn't see a shape to the object due to the bright glow. We lost sight behind trees so we drove until we could see it again away from the trees. After it surged bright white it then dimmed to a red/orange colour. It was slowly moving SW still solid in shape and colour. We observed no flashing lights or wings on the object. As we were getting a better perspective it vanished into thin air as if it was never there. There were two people in the vehicle who both saw it.
