Fast flying blinking light

While standing outside at night, before midnight I looked up and saw what I assumed was an airplane very high up. I continued to watch bc for as far away as the plane appeared to be, it was moving very fast. Before I knew it, the object did a complete 360 and continued in the direction it came from. When the object was almost directly overhead, it made a complete stop, stayed in that position before either going further into space or disengaged its lights. It made no sound. No activity followed.
