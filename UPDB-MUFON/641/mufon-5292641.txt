Bright white spherical shaped, then star like quality hovering and moving closer to me

Got up at 4am to feed the baby and noticed a bright light in sky outside front door window. I observed it for a moment to see if it was moving. I went to make the bottle In the kitchen and came back to the door to observe again. I filmed 2 short movies one magnified the other in regular camera setting. The object was spherical and hovering, then had a starlike quality to it and seemed to be coming towards me. I faintly saw the outline of a figure in the center of the light but it was too fuzzy to know for sure. I can confidently say I saw the light but the image In the movie did not capture all that I saw with my eyes, such as the light beams coming from the sphere. I wished I stayed to observe more but felt I should feed the baby. I came out once more to clean the bottle and the light was still there. I jokingly said could you come back tomorrow and went to bed. Not sure what I was seeing but sure is interesting.
