While walking in the forest, I saw a UFO hovering over the trees. The object hung motionless, then flew away. I took four pictures.

Poland. A small town 1ywiec in a mountain valley in the south of the country. Surroundings of mountain Grojec. Forest nature reserve. On: 27/05/2018 between 17:00 and 18:00 PM, being on a walk in this area, I noticed a dark object hanging over the forest in the distance. At first I thought it was a rare big bird but I was curious that it hung motionless. I had a small compact photo camera with me, so I hurriedly took some pictures. After a while, the object moved very slowly to the right, disappearing behind the crown of trees. During the observation there was a gusty wind and thunder before the storm.
