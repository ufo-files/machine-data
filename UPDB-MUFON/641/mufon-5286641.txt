Light appearing during fireworks display

We were standing on Thaxton Road in Austin watching fireworks. I friend says "but what is that?" We saw a single point of white light about the size of Venus statutory in the sky. After a few minutes the light rose up to a higher altitude (straight up) and then stayed there for about 5 minutes. Then it came back down to about where it had started. After five minutes it went back up to the higher altitude and then it turned red. We were then called inside the house and that's all we saw. Just wondering if anyone else in Austin saw this.
