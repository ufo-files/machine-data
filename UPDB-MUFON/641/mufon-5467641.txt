Captured while taking a photo of a tornado

I was only young at the time but I remember seeing a tornado form and we were all pretty amazed that one was occurring in the U.K. I thought I1d never see one so I called my mother in and she started to take some photos of it, and upon looking at the photos later we discovered a strange shaped object next to the tornado with motion blur but the object was traveling faster than the tornado was spinning. The object on the photo was not debris as it was far from the ground at the time. I1ve kept this photo for a while but have not done anything about it, my mother has given me permission to use this photo as evidence it has not been altered in any way.
