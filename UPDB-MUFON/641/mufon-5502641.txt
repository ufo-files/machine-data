{cak} Strange objects over UWS NYC..

My apt located in NYC UWS on 28th floor with an unobstructed view of the entire east side skyline, and no window treatments. Just observing the beautiful night sky from the balcony I noticed 2 objects hovering in the sky just stationery at first. I photographed the objects then they moved closer to the building. After a bit they flew low and close to the building. No noise. Very big. Lots of lights. Living in the center of NYC in loo of the past horrors there is no way these objects were flying undetected. So no fear but this had been going on since 2009 and I had lived there since 2002. There were many strange objects in the skies and I would be curious to know if others have documented any events. I have many photos of whatever these flying things were. Some smaller odd shaped objects just flew in a circle all night long every night. Media to follow under separate cover.
