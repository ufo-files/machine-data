Three bright star like lights in single formation

Let my dog out around 0600 today the 11/02/22. 
As I often do on a clear morning or night I look up in the hopes of seeing something amazing. I observed what appeared to be three stars. They seemed to be in a straight formation. At first I thought it may have been starlink but there was no more lights in front of the three or behind. I followed the lights moving from west to east. They also moved very fast. The sighting took less than 10 seconds and from my perspective it seemed a great distance to travel. I either lost sight or they just faded into the blackness.
