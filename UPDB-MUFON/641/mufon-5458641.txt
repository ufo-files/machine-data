{tjd} Bright light, hovering stationary cylindrical object

On a clear cloudless day, my attention was garnered by what appeared a bright starlike emencely bright light. I caught it in my pereferal vision. I then directly fixed my sight on the light for approx 2 seconds. The light vanished and in its exact place was a metalic appearing cylindrical object that hovered silently in the sky. I kept my eye on it as a ran toward my house approx. 50 feet away to get my binoculars. I had to briefly enter the house to get the binoculars, when I returned outside it was gone.
