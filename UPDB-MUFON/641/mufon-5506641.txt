{cak} direct flight towards house, Non-typical flight pattern; coming towards house.

It was an unusual flight pattern for anything, including Health Net Helicopter, so I knew it was unusual from the moment I saw it. I yelled at my wife that it was coming right towards the house! We ran outside and noticed house quite it was, not a peep, we live in the country. We both watched the red object coming closer and then it stopped and just hovered or something, it just stopped. I yelled at my wife to get my binoculars and when she returned I got a glimpse of it and then it started moving to its right for a small distance and then vanished. Depending on its size, I am guessing it was within 3/4 of a mile from us, it was close. I called my older brother as we both like to star gaze and he told me about this site and thats why I am sharing this with you. 
Initially we thought it might be life flight and perhaps something happened outside in the neighborhood so when we opened the door and ran out on the porch and did not hear anything we knew it was very odd! 
Again it was not a typical flight pattern for our are and when it was coming straight towards the house it kind of freaked me out. When we ran to observe it, it stopped in its track! After a moment it kind of slid or shifted to its left and just hovered and then it kind of vanished. It is a clear sky this evening but there could have been a cloud or two out there that was not visible but overall, stars are out everywhere. 
My wife and I both were shocked and amazed at the experience! Wish I new more about what we saw and experienced?
