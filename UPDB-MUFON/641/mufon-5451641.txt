7/26/2020 6-6:30am timeframe

A yellow orange ball of light fell from the sky quickly at first with sparks, then slowed, then stopped, then faded after a minute. Looked like it was right over WPAFB. Checked a flight tracker and did not see a maker. 
Falling isnt the right word, it hovered. And appeared in front of cloud. Thought it was a shooting star or meteor at first, but it hovered at multiple levels before disappearing. Was sitting on the balcony with my boyfriend and we both saw it at the same time, in the eastern sky. Gave me chills.
