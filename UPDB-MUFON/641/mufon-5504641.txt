At Great Hill in Weymouth, Massachusetts looking over the city of Boston. Looking over to my right at 8:30 pm, saw a red light take off from land or water over near Hull. As this triangle got higher into the sky, it had begun to look like a dark triangle with red- orange lights at each corner (3). The triangle was upside down. The triangle had started straight up, then started to arc. As the triangle was descending, it hovered and then ascended again. This had happened 3 times. After this, the object was lost by slowly dimming out and it had look like to fly away from observation position.
