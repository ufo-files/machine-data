Observed a circular disk materialize or just appear above my head and then as soon as I noticed it, it moved at an incredible rate of speed.

I was taking my two dogs out to relieve themselves before bedtime at just after 11:00pm on Saturday, 5/22/21. My wife was with me but did not observe what I did or hear any sounds. As we were standing outside and talking, I observed from my vertical peripheral vision a circular bright red/orange disk, that appeared to be two dimensional, just appear, approximately 50-75 feet above my head. Almost like it just materialized from some other time or space. There was no movement that brought it into my vision that I could tell. As soon as I noticed it in the sky above my head, it moved from zero to mach speed, due East, without a sound. I lost sight as soon as it passed my roof line. The total distance of travel that I witnessed was 50-75 feet of travel at an incredible rate of speed with no sound.
