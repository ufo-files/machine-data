MULTIPLE OBJECTS IN STRAIGHT LINE MOVINE SOUTH-EAST FOR SEVERAL MINUTES

AT 5:30 AM IN SAN DIEGO, CA I ALONE WAS OUTSIDE UNDER A CLEAR SKY WITH THE USUAL CONSTELATIONS OF TIME AND SEASON. I NOTICED A LONG LINE OF STARS IN A STRAIGHT LINE, MAYBE 30 OR SO AND WITHIN A SECOND I REALIZED THAT THIS IS NO CONSTELATION. A SECOND AFTER THAT I REALIZED IT WAS MOVING SOUTH EAST TOWARD AND OVER MEXICO. 
I FOLLOWED THE LINE OF LIGHTS ACROSS THE SKY THAT STRECHED OUT OVER THE PACIFIC. AS THE LAST LIGHT IN LINE CONTINUED TO FOLLOW THE OTHERS, ANOTHER WOULD APPEAR AND JOIN THE LINE. ALL EQUIDISTANT AND EQUIL SPEED. THE FRONT OF THE LINE WAS SO FAR AWAY THAT I COULDNT TELL WHAT HAPPENED TO IT BUT IT WAS 100% CLEAR TO ME THAT THE LIGHTS AT THE BACK OF THE LINE SEEMED TO APEAR OUT OF NOWHERE. THIS PROCESSION CONTINUED ON FOR SEVERAL MINUTES. I TRIED TO USE MY PHONE TO FILM IT BUT THE RESOLUTION WAS TOO LOW AS I COULDNT EVEN GET THE FIXED STARS INTO FOCUS. THE NEW LIGHTS THAT WERE JOINING STOPPED APPEARING AND I FOLLOWED THE LAST ONE UNTIL IT VANISHED WHERE THE LEAD ENDED AND THAT WAS THAT. 
THERE WERE NO PLANES, HELO, OR ANY OF THE REGULAR CRAFT YOU WOULD SEE IN THE SKY. PLUS, IT WAS TOO EARLY IN THE MORNING FOR THE FLIGHT PATH AT LINDBERG FIELD TO BE MOVING AT THAT TIME OF DAY BECAUSE OF NOISE ORDINANCES. 
THESE LIGHTS DID NOT BLINK, SOLID WHITE JUST LIKE THE STARS BEHIND THEM BUT EVEN THE STARS TWINKLE A BIT. THESE DID NOT. 

PERSONAL NOTE: THIS IS THE FIRST TIME I HAVE REPORTED A UFO SIGHTING. IM 43 YEARS OLD AND HAVE SEEN PLENTY OF UNEXPLAINABLE LIGHTS IN THE SKY. THIS SHOOK ME TO THE CORE. I DIDNT EVEN TELL MY WIFE OR ANY OF MY FRIENDS. BUT THIS HAPPENED. I CANT EXPLAIN IT VERY WELL. IM JUST GLAD IT WASNT WAR BECAUSE THE THOUGHT CROSSED MY MIND. IT WAS SO OUT OF THE NORM THAT IVE SPENT THE LAST FEW MORNINGS OUTSIDE ON MY PATIO LOOKING FOR ANYTHING SIMILAR. NOTHING. I BELIEVE THAT THERE IS NO WAY IM THE ONY ONE WHO SAW THIS.
