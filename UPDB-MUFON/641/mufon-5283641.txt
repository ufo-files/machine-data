Triangular lights hovering over Washington Capitol Forest

While driving east on Hwy8 towards Olympia just east of McCleary WA, I saw to my right, very bright lights in a triangular pattern hovering over the forest. I continued to watch these lights as I drove until I could no longer see them. they were hovering around 100 to 200 feet above the ground. The lights were much much brighter than an aircraft and illuminated the forest beneath them. Additionally, The podcast I was listening to cut out for around 5 minutes during and after the sighting. I drive this route 4 times a week and have never lost service in that location before.
