{cak} Light, too close for falling star, descends in west. Projecting color trail that changes and ends in a flash.

I was driving thru country and needed gas. I stopped at a country store and began pumping fuel. Suddenly, I see what I initially think is a firework descend in the horizon. It was an object traveling in a westward direction and descending rapidly. It started off with kind of a pink glow that changed to green then at the end it had turned to a white globe then it dissiipated with a white flash. I knew at once that it was too close and too large to be a falling star or comet, thats why I initially thought it was a firework. I just stood there dumb founded for about five minutes, looked around too see if anyone else saw it it but to no avail. It reminded me of another UFO I saw several years before but that one had an immense green flash that covered the horizon. I had a very eerie feeling and actually felt a heat flash with nausea. I got in the car and continued toward town on the darkening highway. Theyre were very strange cloud formations in the sky or maybe it was just enhanced because of what I saw. At any rate, I found this anomaly serious enough to report it.
