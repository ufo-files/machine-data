{tjd} Taking random pictures of sky

While driving east on Interstate 10 the morning of a day in April of 2014 I was taking pictures of cloud formations and did not discover the lights that appeared in formation. I had lost this picture but found it in a collection of photos. I am now reporting this formation because this is the only time that I have ever seen anything like it. I am a former staff seargeant in the USAF. I worked in the missile field for eight years in SAC so I know a few things about planes and this was not any aircraft that I know of.
