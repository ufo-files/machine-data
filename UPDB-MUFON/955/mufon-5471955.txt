{jpg} I was at work when I got the sudden idea to go outside and look up.

On Tuesday November 28th 2017, I was at work and it was about an hour before we closed for the day. I was sitting at my desk scrolling through social media, when I started having thoughts about going outside and looking up. I got up and walked out the back door to the loading dock, and looked up at the moon which had been highly visible all day. Then I started to scan the rest of the sky over the top of the building when I noticed the object. A smile came to may face immediately, before I over excited myself I made sure to take a good look and make sure it wasn't just an airplane. There were a couple of planes in the sky that I didn't notice before I saw the object, with the comparison of the planes around it I could easily discern the object was not an airplane. I was able to capture two short videos on my cell phone before having to return to work. The object was still visible when I went inside, I wonder how long it was up there.
