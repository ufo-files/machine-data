I walked outside my backdoor into the backyard to light a cigarette for no apparent reason seeing that I smoke inside. These flashing orbs were everywhere facing towards my house coming from the south. I had no clue what I saw and didnt think anything of it but realized myself back inside my house smoking the same cigarette. My observations about this experience was that I was in a trance like state, walking along my garden and feeling the sudden urge to go outside to smoke a cigarette are unexplainable. But sometimes I walk along my garden in the daytime while smoking a cigarette admiring the garden. As soon as I went inside the phenomenon was over. This is extremely unusual, I do feel something was almost calling me outside and I was under some kind of spell like trance like I was possessed by some type of force.
