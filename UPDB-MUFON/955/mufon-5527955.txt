Triangular object with 3 lights

I was outside having a family bbq and I looked up towards the east and could see a triangular looking craft with 3 bright lights, it seemed to be not moving at all. We could see it rotating slightly every few minutes. Im not very good at measuring something from a distance but I could tell this thing was quite large. My video camera batter was dead I had my son go try to get a charge real quick on it, and I called up my mother to tell her to look outside to see if she could see it and by that time I looked up and it was gone, I never even saw it move or leave the sky, that really made me and my kids a little frightened
