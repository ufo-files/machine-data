{rjl} I saw a golden orb while driving in Arizona

Me and my sister where driving from L.A. to Phoenix, AZ it was night around maybe 11:30 pm or 12 am. I was driving and I could see the stars out of my peripheral looking up I noticed that the stars were white/silver but there was something else like an orb that was glowing like a star but it was slightly bigger and it was golden/orange. I noticed it but didn’t pay any mind to it while I was driving all of of sudden I saw it go straight down and wiggle a little and then it was gone. But it was staying up there like a star and I thought it was a star.
