{cak} We live very rural, a low flying bright white object making NO SOUND flew over our property. It was too low to be ISS or a Sattelites. We have never observed anything like it before.

At 10:03 pm 2016 in Rural NW Oregon, my husband and I had stepped onto our back deck to enjoy the stars.
While observing how lovely Mars looks, I turned around to see a low, Bright, Solid White round object, flying NE to SW. It was way too low to be ISS or a Sattelite.
It made no noise. Shortly after it moved out of sight, a large plane flew in the same direction at a high rate of speed, which we of course heard.

The whole event only took about 2 to 3 minutes, but it was very suspicious, and we have never seen anything like it before.
We lost sight of this object over our tree line. 
It was a strange feeling to see this, and am wondering what it possibly could be.
