Cloud from east horizon moving toward western horizon covering Earth.

Atlantic Ocean’s edge south Miami Beach [removed location]. Four of us were grilling freshly caught fish when, in an instant, a massive cloud-like formation began emerging from the eastern horizon stretching toward the western horizon as if encircling the entire Earth. It seemed as if it were the end of the world. Turning on the radio the deejay was asking the audience to stop jamming the lines so that he could get a line out to find out about this UP. It seemed as if there were many viewers all over Dade County. Walking back to the water’s edge, the formation seemed to evaporate and the only thing visible was a fluorescent green worm-like, kind of in a lopsided question mark light that recessed back out of the atmosphere. 
Today I posted to a FB “Growing up in Miami 60s 70s” group the details & got responses that others saw this. I know what I saw & it sure wasn’t a misfired rocket from Cape Kennedy as was the official statement. 
Wasn’t this a designated UP/UFO?
