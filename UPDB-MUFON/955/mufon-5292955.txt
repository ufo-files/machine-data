Unknown sighting

Several years ago I saw an object the was a little larger than a golf ball with a tail that was approximately 2 feet long. This object was perfectly cylindrical with a tail. I was driving approximately 80 mph on interstate I-80 in Nebraska as I was crossing the platte river near Ashland, NE. The object was on a strait line path from East to West. 
I researched the possibility of serval different possibilities and was unable to come up with any explanation for the event.
