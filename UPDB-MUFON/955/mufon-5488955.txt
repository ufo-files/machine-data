{rjl} saw a reddish orange slim line object in sky.

On Tuesday September 15th, I was at a meeting by the airport. There were several of us outside discussing things and I heard a plane getting ready to take off so I walked around the side of the building with my 3 year old daughter so she could watch a little 2 passenger plane take off. We watched it take off and fly towards the Waldron, Arkansas area. I walked back to the group and she wanted to see it again so I told her it would be back around sometime and we would watch for it. I kept watching for it but never saw it. About 10 minutes later, I saw this reddish orange slim line vertical thing in the sky. Slim like a pencil. It kind of resembled something on fire but it was very high up and towards the Fort Smith Arkansas area. I told my husband to look at it and he said that was the plane. I kept watching it and it wasn't moving at all. No blinking lights, nothing. He finally agreed that it wasn't moving either. There were 7 of us there and we all saw it. I kept watching it waiting for it to move or some closer or something but it didn't. So I turned my head back to the discussion for not even 20 seconds and when I looked back there was nothing there. Completely gone,no trail, lights, nothing.
