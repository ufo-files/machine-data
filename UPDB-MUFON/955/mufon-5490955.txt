{jpg} Moving Circle Lights Above Portland Oregon

Last night, I was laying on my bed by our 280 degree windows with my cat at the foot of my bed. My cat started looking behind me, and following something with his head, like he was playing with a light. I looked be hind me and there were giant circular lights shining through the clouds moving in circles. There were about 4 and they would individually move in a circle, then disappear, then move in a circle, then disappear. This pattern went on for about an hour. I went and woke my sister, and she saw it too.
