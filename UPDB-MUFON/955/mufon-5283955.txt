Hundreds flying in circles

At first I thought these were Elon Musk's because the starlight trail was in the area HOWEVER these did not go in a straight line, whatever these were did circles, went back and forth, flew up and down and there were hundreds. My neighbors and all my kids saw this. I've watched Elon Musk's starlight and these did not move like Elon's. I got an audio of all of us talking about it but couldn't get a video. They started to come from the west and east and then would fly back west and go up and down and then as sudden as it happened, it just ended.
