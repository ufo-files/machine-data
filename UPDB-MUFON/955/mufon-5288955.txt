Cluster of UFOs - Orlando, Fl.

My fiance and I were visiting Family in Orlando Florida earlier this month. On the morning of 3/13/21, we were chatting on the front yard of the house we were staying at, when suddenly I began scanning the sky for some reason and saw multiple lights moving in the same direction and then would appear and disappear. I ran inside to grab my cell phone and began recording. 

1st video: 8 seconds into the video you can see lights appearing below the 3rd power line from top to bottom.

2nd video: 2 seconds into the video, you can see a cluster of black dots moving the same direction, then they disappear and reappear as multiple white lights. There are also more lights above that cluster.

3rd video: 13 seconds into it, you can began to see white lights below the top power line on the video.

Throughout the videos you can see planes and birds (we were near Orlando Airport), and the lights were not moving like neither of them. They would also disappear and reappear not in the same place. At the end they disappeared and we were not able to see any more lights.
