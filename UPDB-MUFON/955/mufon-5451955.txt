Smallish black triangle hovering silently

My friend was driving us home from football practice on Highway 90- I was in the front passenger seat. 
We come around a right hand turn leading into a couple miles of straight road coming into Quincy. 
My friend says," hey what's that?"
I don't see what he's talking about so he points ahead and says, "THAT dude!" 
I see some lights, and assume it's a plane, but he says,"Nah, look at it, it's not moving!"
Sure enough, it isn't.. and we're heading straight towards it. 
As we get closer, it becomes very clear that this is no airplane, or anything either of us have ever seen. 
Eventually, we are almost directly under it. It still just hovering in the same spot.
We saw a dark arrowhead shape with a solid red light on each corner of the bottom face, and a flashing white light in the center.
It had no visible means of propulsion, and was not audible with my head out the window. 
I estimate it was 100-250 feet in the air, and 25-50 feet long. 
I tried to take a picture with my cell phone, but it was too dark/small/bad resolution to see anything.
As soon as we pass under it, I'm looking backwards with my head still out the window- 
I wondered if it would just stay there, or start moving to my right(south). 
The moment after I had that thought, it began floating south, which made me a bit nervous.
It kept slowly moving in that direction until it went over the treeline out of sight. 
For anyone who knows the area, it had been hovering directly over the house at the top of the hill where that guy is always waving at cars passing by.
