Unknown entity in Forrest

Both myself and my nephew were walking through the forest on the way back down from windy hill in Renfrewshire, my nephew had walked on infront so I decided to take a photo of him, after I had taken the photo there was a strange feeling as if someone was near us, however we didn't notice anyone. Later when we had returned home I decided to upload some photos to Facebook and I noticed either one or two, what look like small people peering out from the tree next to my nephew (on the left of the photo) We didn't notice them or it at the time and there was certainly nothing of that shape or colour in the area.
