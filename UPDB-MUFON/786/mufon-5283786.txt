Object first appeared as large light, then metallic

I was driving south on the Garden State Parkway towards Cape May, New Jersey no more than 20 miles south of the interchange with the Atlantic City Expressway. I'm not sure exactly where I was when I noticed to the south and just west of the road a large light, not like the kind of lights you see on planes, this was just a light and it was much larger (about the size of a pill) and did not blind, it just appeared to hover. The road turned slightly and the light became hidden by trees because it was fairly low. Then I come around another corner and instead of a light there was just a metallic looking aircraft hovering. It could have been circular or triangular but since I was looking at it from the side it just looked like a straight line. It could have been the back of a plane but it did not appear to move. I didn't notice the object when the light was in the sky. They both seemed far away, at least a mile away.
