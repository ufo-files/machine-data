{rjl] UFO caught after following me around Odessa, TX.

1. I was at UTPB running a camp (do not wish to mention which camp) since I work there (also do not wish to mention which department I work in). We had taken a bus to the local Cinergy in Odessa, TX and then we decided to go to Starbucks. On the way there I happened to look out of the window and saw an orb seemingly following the bus. After Starbucks and returning back to UTPB, the orb had still followed and stopped finally hovering over the main Mesa building. After taking several photos and videos, attached to this post, the orb finally went off to the right then backwards away from me and turned invisible and/or disappeared. 
2. I saw it out of the bus window and realized it was moving in a way that made it not a plane, star, or drone. Also its speed was ridiculous. 
3. I first thought the object was a plane until it ended up hovering above the Mesa building above UTPB, obviously making it not a plane. 
4. The object was a perfect ufo. It was a silver circle with a dome on top and even seemed to have a window after closely looking at the photo I took. The object mainly hovered but also moved in darting motions up and down and also forwards and backwards. It seemed to float around and just watch and observe. It was almost if it became agitated at me recording it and decided to leave. 
5. I was super excited to catch one! After my first experience I knew what I was looking for this time and made sure to watch it and record it. (No proof of this, but after I thought in my mind "Please move around so I stop feeling crazy" it started moving almost like it could hear me.)
6. I lost sight of it when it moved backwards away from me about tree line high and vanished into thin air.
