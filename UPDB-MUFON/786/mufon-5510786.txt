{nd} 3 orange lights in perfect triangle formation

I had stepped out back to put my dog on the chain so he could do his business. I look up and out of no where these orange lights appeared in a triangle formation, with perfect space between each light. When I seen it I froze, thinking of what all it could be, it couldnt have been an airplane because they don't fly that low, and there isnt an air port around here. If it was a Helicopter then I wouldve been able to hear It and there would have been flashing lights. It hovered there for about 10-20 seconds before moving east in still a perfect Shape. so I followed it and about 2 minutes passed before it just vanished in the blink of an eye. 
it was like nothing I have ever seen in my life, I was just frozen and I remember the first thing I thought was "get a camera and get evidence and footage. and just then my back porch light started flickering and went dead, but it was a brand new light that I had just put in about an Hour before. My dog was going insane and was barking and yelping like Ive never heard before. I waited for it for about another 30 minutes for it to re appear but never did. it was as silent as ever.
