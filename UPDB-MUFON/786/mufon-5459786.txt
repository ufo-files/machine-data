{rjl} Unknown craft caught in video/picture taken on vacation

Our last day of vacation 6/28/19 in Mammoth Lakes California, we agreed to have some breakfast and drive up to Lake Mary and take pictures of the waterfall looking down on Twin Lakes. After taking some pics I started to drive off but thought I would get out of the truck real quick since nobody was on the road at get a picture of my truck with Lake Mary in the background. I took a few pics and saw nothing flying around the vast area and mountain range surrounding us and one large landmark is above Crystal Lake called Crystal Crag at 10.403' a huge outcropping peak. 
When I was looking at the pictures on my Iphone XR Live Shots I saw one of the pictures with my Truck had a neon blue dot up in the sky on the right side and thought nothing of it, so I went back to the picture a zoomed in on it and the hair on my arms stood up, I could make out the wing shape of the craft with evenly spaced lights along the edges and others were symmetrical in distance from each other and I knew this was not a reflection it was a craft. 
Since this was taken with "Live Shot" mode I have a 3 second Video showing this craft bouncing up down backwards and forwards at extreme rate of speed without a flight path pretty much like a pinball or if you had a lazier pen shaking it against a wall. 
I shared some pictures with some air force friends and other military officials and they said I have one of the most dramatic footage they have ever seen. I talked with Peter Davenport today about the sighting and wants permission to share the pictures but I am not ready for people to make money off this amazing wittiness.
