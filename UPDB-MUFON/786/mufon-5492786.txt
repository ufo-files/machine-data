I returned home from getting my motorcycle inspected at 9:48 am 
When i got off my bike and removed my helmet a strange cem trail caught my eye alone with a rainbow circling the sun. I thought it looked pretty cool so I grabbed my cell phone and took a few pictures. I just aimed and hit the button twice. I changed my position so the brightness of the sun would be blocked out by the tree branches. and snapped off 5 more pictures one after the other. I tried to view what I took pictures of but the sunlight was to bright to see the screen so I went in the house to check them out. First photo shows a weird green orb. Second photo shows same orb but gray in color and in a different position. I thought it was weird considering i just pushed the button one after the other. Picture log shows they were taken in the same minute. The other five I took I didn't see anything weird till I e-mailed them to myself and looked at them on my computer.
