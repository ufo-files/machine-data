 He was traveling on M-15 toward I-75. He had just passed County Line Road (between Genesee and Oakland County), traveling South. All of a sudden a blue beam shot out of the sky at a 45 degree angle to the side of the road to his right. He was startled, but kept on driving, then saw two lights that he couldn't see through; when he tried to look at them they disappeared; he said he "kind of knew what it was; he knew it wasn't a drone". He kept traveling and saw a third bright light to his left, and another bolt of light, this time bright white, brighter than a lightening bolt (at Auten Rd.). He said it didn't light up the area. Further down the road another bolt of light, this time in the center of the road, very bright, in front of him at Oak Rd. Nothing else happened, no loss of power, no loss of time.
