{rjl} UFO STAR LIKE LIGHT MOVING THROUGH NORTHEN SKY BEING FOLLOWED BY A MILITARY JET OF SOME KIND.

I GO OUTSIDE EVERY NIGHT AROUND ELEVEN PM TO HAVE A SMOKE. I ALWAYS SKYWATCH FOR UFO'S. THIS UFO WAS STAR LIKE MOVING NORTH BEING FOLLOWED BY A MILITARY AIRCRAFT OF SOMEKIND. THE UFO LIGHT WOULD BLINK OUT AND COME ON AGAIN IN A DIFFERENT SPOT NOT FAR FROM WHERE IT WAS. THE MILITARY AIRCRAFT WOULD MAKE A COURSE CORRECTION TO GET BACK ON IT'S TRAIL. THIS HAPPENED 3 TIMES, AND FINALLY THE LAST TIME THE UFO LIGHT DID NOT COME BACK ON AND DISAPPEARED. THE AIRCRAFT JUST KEPT GOING NORTH EAST INTO CANADA. NEITHE OF THEM MADE ANY NOISE, AND I ALSO NOTICED THAT THE AIRCRAFT MADE SOME INCREDIBLE TURNS AT HIGH SPEEDS TO KEEP UP WITH THE UFO. WE HAVE 2 NUCLEAR AFB'S IN OUR STATE AND MANY SILOS. THIS IS NOT THE ONLY TIME I SEE STRANGE AIRCRAFT ABOVE MY HOUSE. I SEE ONE ABOUT ONCE A MONTH THAT MOVES AT INCREDIBLE SPEEDS WITH NO NOISE. I'VE ALSO NOTICED A ORANGE GLOW IN THE SOUTHERN SKY A FEW MONTHS BACK AROUND 3AM LIKE THE SUN WAS RISING. I HAVE NO EXPENSIVE CAMERA EQUIPMENT TO PICK UP THIS UFOS AND NIGHT. IT'S TOO DARK UP IN THE COUNTRY.
I ALSO SAW A TEAL COLORED ORB LAND NEXT TO A CELL TOWER WITH MY GIRLFRIEND COMING BACK FROM A MOVIE A FEW YEARS BACK. SHE WOULDN'T LET ME DO ANY INVESTIGATING AFTER IT LANDED AND WENT DARK. I'M SURE THEY ARE MONITORING CALLS OTHERWISE WHY WOULD THEY LAND THERE.
