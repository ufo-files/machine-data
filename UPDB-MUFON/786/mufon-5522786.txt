5 triangle shaped craft with 1 red and white light on each side, 1 bright light in front.

We saw 2 red lights in the distance with 1 white light far above it. We got out of the car and 3 flew above us at very low speeds and altitude. They were heading in toward Marfa (Marfa lights?)to the West. After it flew by and got in the distance a 4th craft appeared and they all turned to the left back in the direction they came from then toward For Davis to the North. A 5th craft flew directly above us, lower and slower. It was a dark triangle with 1 red and white light on each side and one bright white light in the front, like headlights. All of them made quiet sounds, like a really quiet jet but with a hum.
