{rjl} I was driving late one night and noticed a large bright ball of light shoot straight up into the air from my right. It shot really high up and disappeared.

I was driving up the road by my house to drop off my boyfriends badge at work at 12:15am. I noticed the object to my write because it was so bright. I was not sure what it was, but I wondered if it was a large firework. I thought this until there was no explosion and the giant ball of light shot very high up and completely disappeared. The object was bright white and looked large, at least 5 feet wide from my perspective. It appeared right above the tree lines as a large ball then shot directly upward extremely fast. The whole sighting lasted about 3-5 seconds in length. I was shocked. It caught me off guard and I swerved the car because it surprised me and it seemed so close. I lost sight of it when it disappeared high up in the sky. Afterward, I dropped off the badge at his workplace and completely forgot to mention it to him or anyone for at least a week. I then had recollection of the sighting a week or more later, which is very unlike me.
