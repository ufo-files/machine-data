I was driving home from work and talking with a friend on my cell phone. Just before I reached Exit 76 going south on I-85 in Salisbury, I seen this white orb shoot across the highway right in front of me almost like a comet would do. The difference is that for some reason I sensed an organic presence. The orb seemed to be controlled as the car I was riving was. My feeling upon seeing this is that there is increasing activity since I had seen almost the same identical thing a week ago. Once again I was on my way home from work. As I pulled into my driveway it shot horizontally directly over my back yard. This new orb flew straight across with a dissipating white trail. It seemed to disappear into the clouds.
