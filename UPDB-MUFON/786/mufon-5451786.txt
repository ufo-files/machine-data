something flew over me in a gas station and made a swooshing sound.

i stop to get gas at a gasoline station around 4 in the morning when I saw a light that look like a searchlight that illuminated the gas station  and pass over me and made a swooshing sound . when i look up to see it it had pass me and headed to the direction of the army base FT Buchanon.. I could not see it shape because it was very dark. I look at my watch and it read 4 am. I realize I had left San Juan around 12 midnight. I couldn't have taken 4 hours to drive to Bayamon from San Juan. It only takes 30 minutes.
