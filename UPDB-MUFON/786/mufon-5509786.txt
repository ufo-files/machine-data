{dr}saw 4 bright low flying objects, flew into formation along lake michigan

My wife and I were walking home after the fireworks display. Looked up to see low flying airplane approx. 300 feet in altitude, following closely behind were 3 bright red-orange objects and one white object. The first 3 then formed into a triangle ahead of the white object. I took out my cell phone and filmed the objects for 1 minute 7 seconds. The objects dissolved and disappeared. The objects were flying slowly from south to north, following the shoreline of Lake Michigan. They were approximately 300 feet in altitude. We viewed the objects for approximately 3 minutes. We knew immediately these were not airplanes, helicopters, balloons, fireworks or like anything we had ever seen before.
