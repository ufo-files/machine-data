It was my birthday, observed bright white ball of light

my wife and kids got me a telescope for my birthday, it had just gotten dark when I observed a bright ball of light making no sound coming directly towards us in the sky, we watched it for 5 minutes until I thought to start filming it, at that time the ball of light had made a slight left hand turn and that’s when I started to film it, again no sound whatsoever, I am a avid outdoorsman and I have never seen anything quite like that in my life. I am also a big history and military nerd, and have watched just about ever episode on all the latest well at least released military jets and drones.
