{rjl} Wide circular craft with close to 25 lights around the bottom.

I was coming home from a birthday party sitting in the passenger seat of my mothers car. We were stopped at a red light. I was looking at the clouds because the storm had finally stopped and I was admiring the cloud cover. I first noticed these lights appearing in he clouds that were becoming more distinct. The actual shape finally became visible but was still slightly covered by clouds. I immediately thought it was a UFO based on its shape and the way it traveled. It descended about 10ft until the body was visible. It was North compared to me and as it ascended the lights moved Northwest. After about three more seconds the ship completely disappeared. I was shocked. It wasn't my first odd experience. I said to my mom "holy crap are you seeing this, mom look." I was pointing in the direction but she was too late to look up. After I finished yelling she looked up and the craft was gone. I lost sight of the object because it ascended back into the cloud cover.
