On my way home and sighted three orange lights hovering in the sky changing formation.

At 12:33 AM on 08/01/12 I was on my way home from dropping my friend off. As I approached hwy 99 I seen three orange lights that formed a triangle. These lights were much closer and bigger than the stars and were slowly moving. I pulled the truck over and called my mother to tell her what I saw, but she just told me to hurry up and get home because it was late. I headed home and eventually lost sight of the lights because of the position of my home. I got home and decided it must have been something else and payed no mind to it. I logged onto my Twitter and posted a tweet about what had happened. One of my friends had told me that his older brother and his cousin had seen the same thing and were freaking out. Turns out his cousin had caught the whole thing on video. At this point im a little freaked out because my story completely matched up what was on footage. Heres the URL to the video. I really want to know if anyone else had reported this sighting. Should I report this to my local newspaper? Hope to get some feedback.
