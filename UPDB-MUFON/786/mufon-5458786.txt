Multiple sightings of our whole family

My family and I saw a pulsating hovering bright light pulsating many different colours hovering in the air around a mile away from us.

The same night my wife and I saw many different ‘star’ like lights flying through the sky in multiple altitudes although much higher than a plane would fly, we could actually see a plane flying well below the objects. 

Very strange encounter over garden wall with very tall very pale and skinny being, felt very frightened and very fearful. I stood in the doorway silent and didn’t move. 
Didn’t explain this sighting to my wife for 5 days as I was so shaken by this.
