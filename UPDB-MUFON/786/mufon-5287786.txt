Light Formations, seriously LOTS of lights

I love in the Columbia River Gorge. I saw a couple lights to start with, one followed the other in a row. I continued to watch them they were just past straight above me. They were not satellites or airplanes, I knew because the first light got bright and simply disappeared and the servings followed. I looked back towards the directing I first saw them and I saw more. Quite a few more. It looked like 5 lights in a V formation with a uncounted number of lights following beihind. I watched them and they did the same thing. Not all lights went out at the same time but they did all vanish. By this time my boyfriend came outside and I pointed at him and asked him what he thought because he is ex military and retains everything. There is a military base not to far away from here and I see helicopters and jets a lot. He watched this happen but it just seemed so unreal. A V shaped formation followed by more lights, another V formation and more lights. My neck hurt so much but we watched them for what seemed like forever but it was really about 20 minutes for myself and 15ish for him. I was so happy to see them. It was like arrows across the sky. It's not the first or lost time I'll see lights but this is the first one that just stood out to me.
