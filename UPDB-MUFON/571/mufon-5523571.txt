Tight formation of small planes

I also observed the "carnival ride" sighting in Lees Summit. The actual cause was a group of small planes flying in tight formation. Each plane had about 6 lights and made quite a sight. I live right next to the Lees Summit regional airport and the planes were landing and taking off from there.
