{rjl} I took this as an experimental aircraft, maybe ? Boise Stage Stop truck stop

Anonymous!!! 
It was the Sunday night after Thanksgiving 1999, I remember the year because we were hauling special contracts that year. Approximately 1 am in the morning Me and my now ex wife were leaving the truck stop to go back to our semi truck. For some reason something caught my eye toward the NE. As we got close to the truck it was closer. I stood and watched as she got into the truck. The object was triangular in shape and sort of resembled a stealth fighter, but clearly was not the same shape, Once it was nearly overhead a cockpit light came on and what i perceived as a human pilot waved at me. The anomaly about it was no sound at all, I could hear every truck idling in the lot but no sound from the craft. Second I could walk faster at this point than it traveled. Third no sign of any exhaust ports, No rivets. It was black, had beveled surfaces, and 5 rectangular lights along the underside leading edge of the wing. Had no tail ailerons. It was flying toward the National Guard air base in Boise as I was positioned NE of the base. there it disappeared or landed. I had always assumed it was some top secret aircraft that could or had overcome gravity. Flew using kinetic forces. I had asked on the CB and a few others were watching and thinking I was crazy for just standing there waving back. Because of my job at the time I was allowed to report any such incidents. Was the pilot human , i am convinced so but sure? cant say 100% since they had a helmet on.
