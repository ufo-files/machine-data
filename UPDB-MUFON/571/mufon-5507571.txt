{nd} saw a bright object that rapidly disappeared after a strange experince while driving

05/12/10 around 0845PM...driving in Central IL, followed by wife in separate vehicle...dark country hwy with no oncoming traffic- nothing unusual as I was driving on a curvy stretch of hwy until suddenly I did not feel in control of my vehicle, didn't feel as if I was still in vehicle, tried to tap the brakes and felt as if I was somehow hovering - could not reach the pedals...tried to steer my F150 into the ditch to avoid any potential accident but could not affect the steering wheel...uncertain of amount of time lapse but suddenly all was back to normal....could see that my wife was still following in her car.
