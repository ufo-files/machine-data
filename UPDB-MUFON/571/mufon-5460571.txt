{tjd} 3 bright lights plus several less bright lights in a line moving SW to NE

I was sitting in a lawn chair in my front yard in Marana, Arizona. It was Sunday, May 26, and the time was 8:40 pm. It was completely dark with no cloud cover. I was trying to find Orion when some motion in the sky caught my eye. I looked almost straight up and saw a LONG line of lights traveling in a straight line. There were at least 3 bright lights, each as bright as a bright star, and several other less bright lights that seemed to fade off and on in different locations along the line. I estimate the arc width of the line to be about six full moon widths. I ran to the front door stuck my head in and shouted for my wife to come see the sight. She rushed out and saw it as well. She tried to take a video with her phone camera, but nothing showed up. I looked at my watch and it said 8:41 pm. We watched the light line move toward the NW for maybe 2 minutes. Three bright lights remained visible the duration of the sighting, but the dimmer lights could not be seen after about a minute of observation. The apparent speed of the light line was about the same as that of a satellite we saw moving towards the north a couple minutes after the light line disappeared. No sound. I looked at the last 20 sightings list and note that several other people have reported the same thing, some from as far away as Michigan. Only other thing to note was that both me (outside) and my wife (inside) noticed a strong flash of light about a minute before I saw the light line..
