Bright orb flashed and vanished

My father in law and i were sitting in the hot-tub, listening to our radio. I had leaned forward to grab my beer, and remember asking him a question and as i sat back i looked at the sky and my vision fell in between ursa majors final star benetnach and the star seginus direct northeast perfectly in the middle of the two. There was a orb there which i took for a passing airplane. I continued watching it expecting an airplanes flashing lights only to be met with a bright flash/flicker and the object was gone. I abruptly sat up and asked if he had seen and he said he hadnt. I sat in shock and just asked what was that repeatedly. I have never seen anything like that in my life and hope someone else on this planet can confirm my sighting so i dont feel crazy.
