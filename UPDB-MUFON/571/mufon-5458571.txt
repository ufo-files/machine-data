{tjd} Star looking (because of brilliant shining surface), SW direction, flashes of colors on its cone shape,

Location: standing in street, border of San Marcos and Escondido, observed "star" in SW direction. It stayed stationary, same location, as I watched it for 15 minutes. I then got a friend to look at it, and she observed for about 3 minutes, and then it took off in SW direction until out of sight. We observed it with Binoculars and it had pulsating colors. Streaks/flashes of lights on its surface. As though, you were watching lightning in the sky, but it was "lightning" on the Surface of the cone shape "star" looking UFO. Colors: red, yellow, green, white,and very brilliant (not dull), lights. It would show a flash of color (light up, like lightning) and when the color wasn't flashing, the UFO looked white, and shiney, like a star. I would have thought that maybe it was a star, except for the flashing, random lights... I knew stars don't flash brilliant reds, greens, etc. and when it took off,( after my friend watched it for 3 minutes), that confirmed it was NOT a star.
