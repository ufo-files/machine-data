VISITATION BY 2 SMALL GRAYS AND ONE TALLER ONE

AWOKE IN BED ABOUT 2-3 AM COULD NOT MOVE BUT COULD CLEARLY SEE THEM STANDING BESIDE MY BED, TRIED TO CALL OUT TO MY WIFE BUT COULD NOT, I WAS FOR SURE NOT DREAMING I COULD SEE THE NIGHT LIGHT IN OUR ROOM AND ALL DETAILS IN THE ROOM NOT AT ALL LIKE A DREAM, THEN IT WAS LIKE I PASSED OUT, AWOKE IN THE AM AND ASKED MY WIFE IF SHE SAW ANYTHING SEE SAID NO, WHEN I TOLD HER SHE SAID IT WAS JUST A DREAM, I KNOW IT WAS NOT.
