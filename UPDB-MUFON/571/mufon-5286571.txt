Omni around Pacals Tomb 'temple of the Inscriptions' below hilltop seen and photographed from 1/2 mile away

Hi, I am Mark Knudson a film maker who visited the Palenque ruins in Chiapas, Mexico, and surrounding areas earlier this year. I am submitting this report for Antonio Martinez, who I met personally in Palenque, Mexico January 2021. Antonio lives in Palenque on a ranch across the valley from the Palenque ruins, where he took the attached photo. He told me of many sightings like this one in the area, and had recently purchased a smartphone to take photos. He says there are more photos or will be more photos, because he has seen these omnis in the area many times. For some reason, he said the omnis are often seen near Pacal's tomb at the Temple of the Inscriptions... a very famous pyramid that tourists go to see.

He does not have email or a phone number, speaks some English, but Spanish is his native language. He lives very primitive in a hut, yet is very intelligent and used to work for NASA in Texas awhile ago. His specialty is robotics. He wants me to submit this report for him, so today we are getting the process started. We communicate once or twice monthly via facebook and thats how I was able to get this photo from him. I told Ron James about this and he suggested I file this report. I would like to somehow have Antonio give more details for the questionairre.
