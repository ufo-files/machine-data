Light in the sky

This may be nothing but I did see a very strange light in the sky around 9pm onJuly 7. If it was a UFO I didn't see the object itself, but mainly the trail itleft. I was in a pretty rural area highway 407 in Argyle, TX. There arent manylights on the road so my view was pretty clear. When I first saw it I thoughtit was fireworks since it was only a few days after the 4th of July, but Irealized that it was much too high to be a firework, and it never exploded likea firework would, it just traveled in a downward path then dissappeared. I wastraveling East and the object appeared to be traveling North. I couldnt reallytell how far away it was because of its altitude, which I couldnt really telleither, but it was very high in the sky. It looked like a large flame with atrail behind it. The front of the flame which was the brightest part was sortof lime green, and the tail was a very bright orange. It only lasted about 15seconds and it traveled at a moderate pace at about a 45 degree angle and thegreen part of the flame sort of flared a little then dissappeared, the tailcould still be seen for a few seconds afterward. It was pretty uneventful, noaliens, just a flame. But I have no doubt in my mind that it was not a plane orfireworks. It could have been a meteor I suppose.
