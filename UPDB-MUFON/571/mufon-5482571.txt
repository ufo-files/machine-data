{jpg} Unexplained light in the night sky changing color and Direction very abruptly

I was sitting on a beach in Hawaii at about 10 o'clock at night, just looking up at the beautiful night sky as I do often, it was a clear night, not a cloud in the sky, at about 70 degrees in the horizon over the ocean I saw a green flashing light moving in the sky, I watched it for a few minutes just assuming it was an airplane, but when it started to change direction abruptly I started to film it, I started to become more and more shocked at what I was seeing, it was a green flashing light, not like any green I've ever seen on any airplane, it was moving in a western direction, then it started to move back to the east, again, these were very abrupt movements in the sky, as you watch the video it appears as if I'm moving the camera, but I am not, it is the object that is moving around so radically, as the object was heading east it all of a sudden switch to a red flashing light only it was flashing faster than the green was, again, this object was jetting around in the sky like nothing I've ever seen before!! The object then disappeared into the Eastern Sky.
