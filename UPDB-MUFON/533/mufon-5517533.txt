{flg} Slow moving orange fireball then disappeared

Was at the beach with over 200 people, the firework show had ended 20 minutes prior. I looked up into the sky and spotted a slow moving, what appeared to look like a fireball. I asked my sister and brother in law if they see it as well, which they did. We observed it for approx 6 to 6 minutes, on a somewhat straight path. It then just disappeared in a blink of an eyes time. Sky was very clear, no clouds. Everything was visible.
