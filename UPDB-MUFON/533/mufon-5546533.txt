Saw multi-colored flashing star-like object in night sky.

I was in my house and received a phone call from a friend telling me to go outside. I went outside and looked in the sky and there was what, at first, appeared to be a plane. It was flashing but the light kept changing colors. It did not move in the sky. We (my friend and I) got in my car and started driving towards it, soon realizing that its location was too far away to reach. We turned around and returned to my house. It was still in the sky (It was way too high to be a tower. It wasn't moving so it was not a plane. It was flashing different colored lights so it was not a star or meteor).
