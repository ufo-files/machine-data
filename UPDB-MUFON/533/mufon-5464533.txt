{cak} Delta shaped, pale yellow bottom light

Was out in the back yard as I was almost every night with a refractor telescope and binoculars. Two to two-thirty in the morning just looking around and noticed to the west that the stars were going out, disappearing. Watched for a few seconds and could tell something was moving to my left, silently, to the south, above Costa Mesa Freeway. Grabbed the binoculars and saw a totally black delta shaped object. As I watched it move and observed it some more, I started to faintly resolve a pale yellow, almost invisible light on the bottom with a sort of trellis effect, pale squares that broke up the light enough to see them. As I watched, a smaller object from the right front, it seems, either detached or came out of the other side and headed toward Huntington Beach. I followed it with the binoculars until I couldn't observe it over the roofline. Felt extremely excited as I have been watching airshows and knew a lot of types of aircraft and this wasn't anything like the advertised aircraft that the military exhibits. In my opinion the government and private industry have either come up with shielding for magnetism or someone else has. Like the SR-71, it existed even though the government said it didn't. 
Hoping you screen your people as I would hate to get picked up in the middle of the night and disappear. Thank you for providing this outlet. I thought of doing this over the years but now that I'm retired and semi-bored, it was time.
