{nd} Me and a friend were delivering newspapers and seen a fire ball come down and hover over the woods for 20 minutes

Me and a friend were delivering newspapers around 4:15 am.we were just starting the paper route and were on our bmx bikes in front of my house. Seen what we thought was a shooting star. Coming from the east. Fast and silent. It came to a complete stop over an area in re woods behind my house that for some reason never grew much bush or grass or whatever.it was like a big circle with no trees were we use to ride our bikes. That's where the red fire ball orb thing came down and was hovering right below tree level for about 15 minutes. Then it slowly started moving straight up. Then shot off to the west so fast and with no sound . It was as big as a house .but round and looked like a huge fire ball. Never seen anything like it again .and can't stop wondering and wanting to know what that was we saw that morning . I drive by that area all the time to remember. Somebody built a house on that exact place the fire ball was hovering . And it still looks like nothing grows there . I've been tempted to go speak to the owner of the house .but assume they would just think I was crazy and call the police on me...
