{rjl} Glowing egg shaped object possible missing time

My Wife Rita and I were coming home late one night, as we drove down our long winding driveway to the house.
We both saw this egg shaped object moving incredibly slow and with no noise over and behind the house. It appeared 30-50 Feet long because i could used the house as a point of reference. The height around the middle seemed like 25-30 ft.top to bottom. 

This object was glowing red-orange what I assume was the rear of object it had what looked like flames/heat static popping more around the end of this object. The colors were orange-red swirling with blue and yellow/white pops of static around mainly the back but i observed it come off the top middle of object as well. This trailing of heat/energy/mist fire came off maybe 10-15 ft off the object. 

This happened 20 years ago whats weird is my wife and I looked at each other that night and said did you see that without emotion?  Being super calm ,almost in trance, I pulled the truck up put it in park and calmly got out and we walked to the door and that's all I remember except waking up next morning in bed positioned like a mummy. We got up went about our business like nothing had ever happened. Strange is we just refereed to it like it was a normal experience. 

I'm a commercial/magazine photographer. Shot a few jobs for Time Magazine and got published as a stringer, I jump into action I tell stories with images, for the life of me to this day I cant get over our passive, like nothing happened attitude and never reported it until now.
I contacted Mufon because. two days a go I started remembering more details as if my mind was on some drug and its wearing off..My mind is now putting two other events together which until now, I could not imagine these could be related. That's what we experienced 20 years ago.. My wife Rita passed away suddenly in 2012 from ovarian cancer. Now its just me to tale this bizarre story.
