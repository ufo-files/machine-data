Large flame colored almost round object seen hovering above tree line in Exeter, NH. Watched it move around for about 20 minutes.

My mother is the person who saw this. She was driving home from work on route 27 in Exeter, NH heading towards Epping when she saw a large red-orange almost flame colored object rise up beyond some trees. The shape was like a circle with the top portion cut off. She said all around the top was grey fog/mist coming from it. It went from one side of the road to the other in the blink of an eye. It then stayed within view for about twenty minutes as she continued on her way home on route 125 N in Epping.
