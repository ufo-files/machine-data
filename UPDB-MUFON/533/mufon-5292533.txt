White light moving south to north and north to south.

My wife and I were sitting in our garden looking at the clouds and the sky . We watched a few planes fly over , everything was normal and then we spotted a white object fly from north to south at 21.10 , it only lasted a few seconds and then disappeared. We then saw the same white object fly south to north at 21.16 and it also just disappeared. 
At 21.43 a white object appeared from nowhere directly above us , quickly intensified to a very bright white and then just disappeared in a instant .
