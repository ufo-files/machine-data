{dr}Object presented upon zooming in on photo of moon

Pre-dawn (5:53) on December 3, 2018, there was a very bright moon and a planet/star close together. Since it was very interesting, I took a picture of the moon and a planet (assumed) on my iPhone. When I looked at the photo, I noticed another planetary body partially illuminated above both of them. But what really stood out when I zoomed in on the photo was the football-shaped object that partially covered the moon. At first I thought it to be a whisp of cloud, but there were no clouds. It was a very clear morning with no clouds in sight.

I never saw the object with my naked eye, never observed any movement nor heard any sound. But the very sharp geometric shape to the object is intriguing to me and I thought it might be of interest to the MUFON community.
