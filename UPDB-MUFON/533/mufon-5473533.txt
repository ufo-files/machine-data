{rjl} Red orange orb came from NW @ extream speed after I first saw it. Hovered for a few moments then left @ extream speed in the direction it came from.\ Vandenberg AFB

I. Was sitting on my balcony and saw a red light in the distance.as soon as I noticed it,it seemed to race towards me and stopped, hovered for a moment about 200yrds away over an open field and then shot away to the NW. the direction it came from, Vandenberg AFB. It was the early 70s and missle and rocket launchs were a regular occurrence as were weather balloons and the guppy air transport for the Apollo capsules.
