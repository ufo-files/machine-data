{cak} Triangular object with glowing blue and white light surrounding it. Hovered, flew straight, up, and vanished.

My best friend and I were driving back home from our friend's house in Millville, NJ. We got off at the exit with the Ramada.. and that's when we both seen this triangular shaped object with glowing white and blue lights surrounding it.. It was hovering over the Ramada.. Then it flew to the right and then up.. Then it just vanished. It was faster than a plane but slower than a shooting star. The whole thing lasted about 10 seconds. We automatically knew what it was. We turned and looked at each other with wide eyes. Freaked out a little. We called the friend we were just hanging out with and told him about it. Our hearts were racing. We were actually pretty excited about it.. freaked out, shocked, but excited. We still talk about it to this day.
