While watching fireworks with the family me and my wife first saw a brilliant glowing orb over Craig Field. Then the family saw about 30 brilliant glowing orbs that appeared to be coming from ground level about 2 miles away pass overhead from the NE to the SW. We saw these just over tree line and they were moving slowly and there was no noise. The objects seemed to be about 1000 feet away.
