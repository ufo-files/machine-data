{rjl} one main light split into six lights that hovered around like a swarm of nats

I was with a friend on his back porch star gazing when out of my periferal vision noticed a spherical orb of light, decently bigger than a planes lights. I asked my friend if he is seeing the same thing. We watched for almost six minuets when the light had split into what looked like seven smaller Lights. They shot back and forth like a swarm of small nats, or fruit flys. They appeared to have been serveying or observing something. Three more minutes went by, then all of a sudden all came back into one orb. There was this very unnerving electric/static feeling and I could not hear anything as if my ears were muted Or I was deaf. My friend experiencing the same symptoms. There was a very still moment in time as it bolted out of sight towards the north star exactly. The next few days I had very real and unnerving dreams of a fourth kind. I would wake drenched in sweat and very fast heart rate And marks to what looked like three small triangular marks on my neck and rib cage.
