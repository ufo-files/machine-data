Tall, thin Grey color with large head with black eyes

My mom was staying with us for a few days and on the 2nd night she woke me up at 5:00 am and told me that there was something just behind my barn with extremely bright lights. I got dressed and went out towards the barn and I definitely saw the light she was talking about. As I walked just behind the barn over a little hill there it was. A saucer type craft with the brightest light I have ever seen. My curiosity took me towards the craft when all the sudden a ramp came down to the ground. I was now about 20 feet from it when out of the craft came 2 Grey tall thin aliens with large heads with dark black eyes. They moved about 10 feet from the ramp and then started doing stuff to a deer that was dead lying there. I think they were probing it but I'm not sure. This went on for well over an hour and they even looked at me several times. After that they just walked back into the craft and it sped off into the sky.
