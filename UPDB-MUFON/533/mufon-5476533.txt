camping and followed a craft which landed in a valley . obscured by trees . two of us witnessed event . landed near pillons.

I and a friend camped for 2 nights in a local forest. Around midnight we saw a bright , soundless white light/orb flying slowly just about the tops of the fir trees . We started to follow it from a distance of 100m . It descended into a clearing where electricity pilons run . We watched through the trees from around 75m away on a higher elevation. There was a lot of light and some activity but it was hard to make out. We decided to go back to camp and not get any closer to it. Thinking back to it now. We did not take note of the fact of time . So unsure of any loss of time.
