{cak} 3 Blue lights moving over Brownsville Texas

Was sitting in the car with my adult daughter. She happened to look up and noticed three bluish-white lights in the sky. They were oval or football-shaped. The lights sometimes formed a triangular shape.They moved around abruptly, and sometimes followed each other, zig-zagging at times. They moved quickly, but smoothly, and did not move like planes or helicopters. They made no noise and stayed behind the clouds. They remained for at least five minutes and were still there when we left the area. No idea what they were. A family friend also saw them that same night in the same town (not sure of the area).
