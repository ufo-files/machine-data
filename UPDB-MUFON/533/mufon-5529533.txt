Black and white birds.

I was leaving for Banff to go camping that Saturday with my gf when my Mother who was seeing us off spotted what she thought were military show jets flying over the open Indian reserve across the street. The flew in perfect triangular formation like stunt jets but it became plain to see they were birds. We watch ed for about a minute then I decided to get my camera and film them hoping to zoom in on one to get a more detailed look. We thought they were very beautiful rare birds flying abnormally skilled and watched them circle from South to North until they disappeared as a result of being to far away and too high...
