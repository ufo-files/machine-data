Dark triangle with three rounded dark red dots

During the evening of July 15, 2015, I went on my balcony and looked at the stars for a few seconds. I immediately saw a silent dark triangle passing over my building with three rounded dark red dots at each corner. It went straight from South to North and I could see it no more than 5 seconds before it disappeared, hidden by the roof of my building.

I am not sure if the dots were faintly glowing or if they just reflected the lights of the city.
