I went outside to look at the stars because where I live there is no light pollution. I stared into the sky for a while and thought I found a star. I went inside to get my glasses to see it better and I came back to find what looked like the star I saw before, but there were several entities that looked all like bright stars flashing colors so fast. I watched 2 of the bright objects not move for a while which made me think they were stars. Then they began to move towards each other, then fly back and forth and then they slowly went up to each other and became one. When I went back inside I didn11t realize I was outside for almost two hours, which actually felt like I was just outside for about 20 minutes. The whole time I was watching these flying objects I felt as if I was in a trance. Their aircraft gave off a calm humming noise which didn11t seem to startle me which didn11t scare me off. Once I broke out of this 11trance11 I climbed down from a gate I guess I climbed to get a closer look. I even had a LED flashlight with me and would flash my light on and off towards the aircrafts to see if it would react. The only reaction the aircrafts gave off from the flash light was if they were moving they would stop after they saw the light. I literally looked away for one second and I looked back and they were all gone.
