{jpg} A triangle formation of flashing lights of multiple colors seen over a series of nights. Multiple witnesses

I first witnessed a light in the sky in Stoughton Wisconsin on May 28th 2018. It was stationary and flashing blue red and white. Then a second light which was white moved from the first object and across the sky in about 10 seconds. It was 9:30 at night. Then on June 4th I went outside to look at the sky and noticed a triangle formation of lights which were just above the cloud cover. I haven't looked to see how high the clouds were but the lights we're shining through the clouds and Illuminating the clouds different colors. The first time the light was white then it turned to Blue then it turned to purple and brightly lit up the clouds about 6 times in 30 seconds or so. My brother also witnessed it with me. After that I had my mom come out and look at them also. The next night I looked again but it was overcast. I went out again on June 6th and again saw the same formation of Lights flashing the same colors. I witnessed it again on June 8th but this time the triangle of Lights had moved in the sky to the north. This was about 11:30 at night. While watching one of the lights I saw an object come out of it and go across the sky. It was brightly flashing red lights. It went behind a tree and I never saw it come out. All three of the lights alternated between bright and dim during all of the sightings I have had. The lights always look to be about the size of stars, but I'm much lower in the sky. I haven't been able to find my binoculars or a telescope to get a better look.
