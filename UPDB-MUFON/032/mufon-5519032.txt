13 lights flying in a V formation going across the whole sky in four or five seconds like a shooting star

I went outside my apartment to smoke a cigarette. I looked up in the sky to look at the stars. I knew at first it was not a jet or plane because they were moving so fast and were so bright. They were traveling from south to north in an almost perfect V formation. I think they were all individual craft in formation because the V wasnt perfectly aligned. Chills went down my spine as I witnessed it .
