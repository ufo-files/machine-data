Witnessed by myself and husband large shaped disked object between two moving jet airplanes with contrails.

While driving on I-40 going towards Hickory,N.C. About 5 miles east of Statesville, N.C. We witness two jets flying with contrails, clear blue skys, no occlusions. A large comtrail came down out the the sky between the other two comtrails and suddenly presented a large shaped disk, very bright glowing and reflective. White to metal looking object. It was there approximately 6-8 seconds, then retracted back into the sky taking with it the comtrail that it produced. Like it was never there. Approximately 30 minutes prior we witnessed two small objects in front of our vehicle flying on both sides in apporimately same level of skyline and same discription of the larger disk object, but they were much smaller.
