{cak} Saw briliant light hover for 3 to 4 minutes than leave at high speed to the Southwest.

I was walking my dog a couple of streets from my house. It was 9:05 PM because I looked at my watch when I spotted the object. My dog, on a long leash, stopped to sniff some bushes and as a few stars were starting to appear in the sky I looked up. I noticed this very bright light about 80 degrees above the Eastern horizon. My first thought was that it was the space station, as I have seen it about this time of night three times this year. As I watched I expected it to move as the station moves, but there was no movement and it was brighter than I have ever seen the space station. I next thought it might be Venus, but even Venus is not this bright and it was in the wrong place. After 3 minutes of watching my dog wanted to go so we started walking. We had gone about 50 feet when she stopped again and I looked up to see the bright light, it was gone. I scanned the sky and picked it up traveling at a high rate of speed to the Southeast. It disappeared in about 2 seconds above a high cloud deck on the horizon. I was amazed that it was just as bright when it left as it was overhead. The illumination never varied so it was not reflection of the sun. I don't have any idea what it was but it was not an aircraft as it sat in one spot for at least four minutes and than streaked out of sight in seconds. I have seen many strange (explainable) things in the sky, but this one leaves me baffled.
