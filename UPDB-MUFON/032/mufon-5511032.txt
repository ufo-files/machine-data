We were out camping on a native reservation in Bruce County. We sitting by the camp fire when a Native who was fishing down by the river approximately 100-200 yards away came yelling saying to look up at the sky. I looked up over the field and saw 4 lights by then 2 of them had disappeared since the native had yelled to look at the lights. I quickly turned around and started calling over more people, in total maybe 8 people saw it from my point of view. This is a second time these lights were viewed over this area, but last time only one person saw it. I knew right away it was a ufo because the only airport around was 20km away and only have Cessna planes in there fleet and occasionally a Canadian coast guard helicopter which is bright orange flies over and an air rescue from the hospital. But there has never been more then 3 aircraft in the air at that altitude. The objects just disappeared 1 by one and soon they were all gone.
