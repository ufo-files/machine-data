Silver cigar shaped reflective

Driving to work down Ohio rd towards 121 (north) there was a silver cigar shaped object to the northwest. I noticed it when I stopped at a light and it was above the signal. At first I thought it was a plane but it was not moving and had no markings, paint, or wings. I watched it for a minute till I lost it behind a apartment building, when I passed the building the object was gone. I'm use to seeing airplanes living by dfw but this was gone in 5 seconds that I lost sight of it.
