{cak} Saw ufo fly over my house.... freaked me out.

I was outside at 10:30pm April 19, 2018. I witnessed an object flying over my head. It was rectangular in shape, and had one light on each corner. I focused my eyed, but it appeared blurry. My first thought was No one is going to believe me. I watched it till it left my view. About 2 minutes later an object that was as high as the stars was in the very same path and had a very bright light that was turning on and off in what seemed to be an untimed progression. 2 seconds on, 8 seconds off. 4 seconds on 3 seconds off, and so on... It appeared to be looking for whatever flew by minutes earlier. I want in... I know too much to turn back now.
