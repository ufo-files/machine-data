3 flying craft equal distance apart seamless and quiet

Witnessed 3 dark flying craft near Gibbs High School around 5pm on March 29th 2022 under cloudless blue skies. The 3 craft were dark possibly black or gray and non reflective, they appeared suddenly moved uniformly without smoke or sound made a succinct turn and disappeared into the distance visible for approximately two to three minutes. I was driving at the time and was not able to video or photograph this sighting.
