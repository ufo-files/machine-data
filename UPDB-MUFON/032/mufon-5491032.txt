{rjl} Orb-like structure glowing in sky that moved in a linear path, fairly fast, and disappeared out of sight.

I was driving east down Rt. 72 in Illinois when to my left, there was a glowing orb-like structure in the sky. The sky was still blue, with only the moon a bit visible. There was no chance of stars in the sky. The structure was a few miles above ground, but large enough to see the orb shape and bright light coming from it. It moved in a straight, linear path towards the ground, fairly fast for about 5 seconds and disappeared out of sight. I was shocked at first because I knew it had to be something other than a shooting star or plane. It was large and close enough to earth where it couldn't have been either. Very interesting sighting.
