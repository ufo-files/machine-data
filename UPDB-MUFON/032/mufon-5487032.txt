{cak} Equilateral Triangle shape, Silver matte color, black triangle in middle 1/3 size

I was visiting a family member and we were sitting on her back porch talking. I was facing west, she, east. Mid-afternoon, no clouds etc, no weather conditions other than Sunny.
While visiting with her, I noticed movement up in the sky and just watched as this silver triangle shaped craft with a dark (black) triangle in the middle, about a quarter or a third full size in the middle of it, just watched as it traversed from North to South in the matter of about a minute, maybe less. It appeared to be very high up and compared to watching a jet pass in the sky, this object must have been moving extremely fast to cover the range of my view of the sky so quickly.

I didn't want to interrupt while she was talking and once it'd passed, there wasn't much use in mentioning it to her I figured. I don't recall what the conversation was about but, it didn't seem worthwhile to mention it to her and cause any interruption.

That's pretty much it. I only recently mentioned it to a friend a few days ago who thought I ought to report it.

From what I could see, it had none of the features that I keep seeing on Hangar 1 or other programs I watch regularly on TV. The silver color of it was not a shiny silver but what I'd describe as a matte finish. It had no windows, doors or any other visible marking from my vantage point.

Other than seeing a triangle flying across the sky, there was just nothing of interest to it at all. It didn't change path, speed or direction.
