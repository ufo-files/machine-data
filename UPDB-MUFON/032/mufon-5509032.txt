{cak} flying star like object

Saturday morning, I went outside to water my plants. I looked up in the sky and seen like a blurry white orb hovering around, I watched it for about 2 minutes and it disappeared. A few minutes later I looked up again and there it was closer to be and looked like a floating star hovering around. I got my camera immediately and snapped a photo. Then it suddenly changed its path and made a sharp turn north. I live a few blocks south of the lake so im sure it headed over the lake. It disappeared from that point.
