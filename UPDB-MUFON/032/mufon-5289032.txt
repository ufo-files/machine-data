SPOTTED ORBITING HUGE OBJECT

Police and Private Pilot, watching the airliners come in from the south reroute and descending on 34 ILS path directly over the Salt Lake Valley. Looked up and saw a slow moving, very large football field (or longer) sized elongated triangular mother ship. Was definitely outside our atmosphere but moved much slower than the 18,000 mph Sats and ISS pass overs.

Several smaller ships came out of the front, hovered out in front a few miles away, then took off in northerly directions at literally three times as fast as the ISS travels. Three ships then faded out or disappeared. Definitely was huge and shimmering looking haul.
