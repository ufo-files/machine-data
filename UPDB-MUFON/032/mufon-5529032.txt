in clear blue sky a white upright cylindrical object with a dark spot in the center of the bottom was noticed

While driving east bound, up a short hill I had to lower my sun visor to block the sun as it was early morning and I was driving eastbound. I noticed the object in the sky to the left of the sun. At first I thought a small bit of bird [profanity deleted/cms/tg] was on my side glass. I double checked and there was nothing on my window. The object was the color of a white cloud but was shaped very differently. The bottom was squared off and the sides were straight. In the middle of the bottom there seemed to be a dark spot, I looked at this part of it more intensely to be sure. The top end kind of tapered off and had a wispyness to it. I stopped driving to take a closer look at it. The place where I stopped was next to a large tree. I was looking over my left shoulder to see the object. I kinda disregarded it after a while. Almost took a cellphone pic of it but decided it was too far away to get anything decent. It was about 10 miles away. As I pulled away from where I stopped, the large tree blocked my view of the object. I looked again to where it should have been and it was gone. I then looked forward and there it was, still about 10 miles away but now it moved 90 degrees clockwise through the sky. That startled me. I kept driving on and soon lost sight of the object.
