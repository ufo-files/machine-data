{cak} Flying Objects

This happened when I was around 12yrs old, My mother used to drive a bakery van, it was in the summer and it was a clear night, there was around 4-people on the back of the van ordering food and I was helping them up and down off the van. I looked up into the sky and seen several bright lights really high, thought they may have been stars thats how high they were. The thing is they were in a V formation, spaced out but in perfect line with each other one been at the front and the others in formation behind it, I told the other people and we all watched them for a while moving along then all of a sudden they all stopped still and stayed in the formation. After a few seconds a plane came from the other direction, no were near as high as the lights on the formation, regular airplane height, as the plane passed and kind of went out of sight, the formation started moving again till they went out of sight.?
