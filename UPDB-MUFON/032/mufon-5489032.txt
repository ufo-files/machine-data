{cak} Sept 5th-12:20 am- observed flashing lights while standing on porch,it was flashing red & white but did move in sky.

Noticed on Sept 5th, 2015 at 12:20 midnight while standing on porch saying goodbye to friends,and noticed over their car in a northeast direction, high in the sky was a flashing light with white & red lights.It was very high in the sky, but you could still see it flashing. I thought it was an airplane, but it didn't move. I observed for over 25 minutes and it only stayed in the same area of sky. I thought I saw it moved slightly up and down a couple of times, but it mostly stayed stationary. I didn't know what it could be, so I decided to check the next nite. The next nite I went out 8 pm and there it was again in the same direction, but the position was different, a little more north. It was the same flashing lights and stationary position. I observed this for 2 hrs. It happened again the next nite Sept 7th same lights but now it was in the northwest sky. I then took video with my cellphone and took some pics. On Sept 8th the happened again, the light in the west and stayed for hours. On Sept 9th the light moved back to the east. Sept 10-12 couldn't observe we had cloudy,rainy weather. Again on Sept 13th the light was back in the east. Same for Sept 14th, but on Sept 15th it was back in western sky, I took pics this time.
On Sept 16th, I observed 2 objects, one in the west and one in the east!! Same lights and position. Sept 17th-observed in western sky, I thought this was really getting weird so I took more video and pictures. Each time I took video & pics I tried to stop when planes were in the area. I reviewed the pics and you can see different shapes and colors on each one from the night of Sept 17th. My husband and daughter also observed. Today is Sept 18th and I will check the sky again tonight.
