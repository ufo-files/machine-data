2 spheres to solid tic tac alternating

Driving from California to Arizona we passed Yuma and a town of Felicity population 2, aka "official center of the world" (California side of border) then we saw a white tic tac object hovering perfectly still above the mountain range, and we watched it 20 minutes till we we had driven parallel to it, and another 30 minutes as we passed it and drove beyond view. Oddly, it never moved for 30 minutes then it took a fast path northward. It had altitude higher than most planes, and sometimes was a tic tac shape but other times 2 spears conjoined or near-conjoined. We have 2000 pictures and 3 videos. Here are some collages I made of the object using red and blue light screens to get varied perspective. We started seeing it while the sun was up and watched it over sunset, till the sun fell below the horizon, which is the moment it went north.
