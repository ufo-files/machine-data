We saw many at once come and go could not keep track of how many we saw but it was a lot my mom and my daughter also saw them all.

I first saw one on the highway in Madison and as soon as I pulled on my road in north haven ct at 11pm Tuesday night I saw one again. Then I got out of my car and told my mom to come outside to look and 2 more appeared in the sky. We stayed outside and had my daughter come out so she could see. More and more kept coming and going I lost count of how many we saw. I had to get my daughter in bed and it was also freezing so we stopped after about 25 minutes and went inside. The crafts had red white and blue lights that would pulsate. They were quiet and moving in all different directions some lower than others.
