I looked up and saw bright object within contrails in a wiggly and swirly pattern. I took photos and saw an extra craft in one picture south of object..

I went outside before work to check on the washing on the line. I looked up into the clear blue sky and observed a wiggly swirly contrail with a white starburst shaped orb along the trail. It seemed very high up. 
I knew that it would be impossible for a plane to make those directional movements so I took some photographs with my work samsung tablet. It was quite hard to catch the image in any detail. Later I showed my partner Wendy the photos and found to my surprise a bullet shaped object also in one of the photos. It was quite a distance from the first object and was dark in comparison. After taking my photos I needed to hurry to finish getting ready for work and the contrail and starburst object within it were fading away. When I left for work approx 15 minutes later, it had vanished.
