{cak} Took a picture and noticed lights in my photo.

I work for a construction company; I am a Safety Coordinator for construction project in Los Angeles California. Our project is located in Hollywood, CA. It was 1:46 am and I was sitting in my car at a Denny's Restaurant parking lot. On our project we have a tower crane; on the crane we have an American Flag that is being lit by a light that's attached to the crane just above the flag. I wanted a picture of the flag to show my friends. I rolled down my window, took the picture and thought nothing of it. Later on that day I took a closer look at the picture and noticed something strange in the top left quadrant of the photo. It was a very gloomy early Thursday morning; there were no visible stars out that night and I did not notice any airplanes nor helicopters while I was in the parking lot. I took the picture with my cell phone camera (IPhone 6 Plus). 
When I first noticed the objects in the picture I quickly got this anxious feeling (never thought I would catch anything on camera) and started to think if the objects could be stars, reflections of light, or even satellites. As I thought about it more I noticed that the objects look to be in some type of formation or "could this be one big object?". The following night I took my cell phone and attempted to take a picture of the brightest star I could see in the sky. I was not able to see the star on my cell phone screen; I knew then these objects in questions could not be stars. So I decided to send the pictures to the professionals (MUFON) so they can take a closer look with their software and try to explain these objects are real or just my imagination. 

Thank you.
