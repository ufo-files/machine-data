huge swarm of 30+ starlike lights flying south to north

I was at a friends house for a bonfire on a saturday night. while sitting with my girlfriend and chatting with friends i noticed 4 lights (satelite sized) that made a triangle flying in a straight path over the property we were on. I noticed it at first because i always enjoy watching the sky and thought it was a satelite but never seen them so close together so i knew it was something other-worldly. I had a feeling it was a ufo but you just never really expect to get such proof. all 4 lights stayed uniform as it flew south to north overhead of myself as well as several other witnesses. I yelled "look! what's that?" astonished. as everyone just watched for maybe 15 seconds as it kept going out of sight. about a minute later, in the same flight path and direction, a swarm of at least 30 single lights all bunched together came overhead. all i could do was watch in awe as this swarm of starlike lights buzzed silently flying south to north. i distinctively remember keeping my eye on two lights that were on the outer rim of the swarm, i watched one bounce over blocking my view of the other and then back again in less then a second. all the lights just kept going at a steady speed (about that of a satelite) and disappeared out of view. I felt like i had just seen something that I will probably never get to see again. I was upset that no one had any video proof, but was super estatic to have my own proof after observing the night sky and wondering what if for so long.
