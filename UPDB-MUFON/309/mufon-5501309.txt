{nd} saw ufo in sky during firework show on fourth of july

My parents, my brother his girlfriend, and I went out on Clear Lake in our boat to watch the firework show. We had to boat across the northern side of the lake to anchor off about quarter mile off shore from Library park in Lakeport. At around 9:35pm the firework show started, the beginning of the show was slow so I started to look around the horizon. When I looked towards the right of the fireworks I noticed the green and red object in the sky. At first I thought it was a satellite but I realized it was inside of our atmosphere. I alerted everyone about the object in the sky and we started to take pictures of it with my dad's digital camera. Unfortunately at the time the UFO was present there are no stars in the sky to contrast the object. It seemed as if the UFO was observing the fireworks, and it ascended and descended and it made sharp turns in the sky, as if it had anti-gravity abilities. Being a very firm believer in extra-terrestrial life whether it being intelligent or not, once I realized it was a UFO I was actually kind of joyed to see it since it's been a while since I've seen one. After about 10 minutes the UFO flew North more and descended behind the mountains. Also I have about 20 more pictures if you want them.
