Same Craft three nights in a row

The same craft, v shaped, came partially uncloaked. As it flew over the house from east to west. It had rounded smooth edges. It was streaking with a silky green light trailing off it's nose and wings. Behind it it left a 10 to 20 foot tail of the same shimmering fiery green light. The same green as what comes from the Northern Lights. 

The second night it did the same partial uncloaking but from South to North

The third nigh the ship did the same partial uncloaking, same color of light, and went from NW to SE.

Forming a Triangle over our house. A triangle when viewed from ground up or from sky down. But in the lateral view it would appear as a three different unconnected lines with each line a higher altitude than the previous.
