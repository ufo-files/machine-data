{jpg} Lights moving through trees maybe 20 feet altitude.

I was outside looking at Venus over the moon. Directly below that at probably 20 feet altitude I saw a red light followed by a white light maybe 10 to 15 feet apart moving through the trees in a northward direction. They moved from my left to right at probably ten miles per hour. They disappeared after about 10 seconds. I went back in the house and about 3 to 5 minutes later I went back out and again saw a red light in approximately the same place but maybe a little further away than I saw it before and it again moved from my left to right and disappeared after about 5 seconds. I live on a wooded hill and these lights were too high to be a person with lights and way too low to be a plane or helicopter even if the object was farther than it seemed. After the woods there is a swampy area but if i was seeing something that far away, the object would be in the swamp not above it.It was totally silent.
