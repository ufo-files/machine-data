{tjd} Bright Orb Over Sprain Brook Parkway

It was a hazy Saturday morning a few hours before it snowed so the sky was pretty white, like one big cloud. I was relatively close to Westchester Airport so I thought, "Perhaps it's a plane with really bright lights because it's so hazy." But shortly after I saw this extremely bright, LED-like orb, fairly high in the sky I saw a lower-flying airplane that had no visible lights and it was clearly a plane, so for comparison, what I was seeing over the Sprain Brook South was in stark contrast with the plane I'd just seen. It was over trees and transmission towers, but very high up and it didn't move. I only saw it for a few minutes before it disappeared. But I know that there are no airport towers over there, I know it couldn't have been anything like that. And perhaps this is unrelated, but the next time I drove on the Sprain a week or two later, that body of water that surrounds the golf course...perhaps the reservoir...was almost completely dried up, so much so that I don't believe it happened naturally. If it's manmade, perhaps they're draining it, but I found it very odd that the water appeared to be dwindling significantly so soon after my strange sighting.
