Boomerang craft with multiple lights

I observed a boomerang shaped craft with 3 or 4 dim lights on each side of the “wing” structure for about 7 sec. it was only observable because it was passing thru a light cloud cover that it illuminated. It was heading north from the south. No sound. Craft was dead black. I estimate it was west of the nearby town of Santa Clara Utah and would have overflown the Saint George airport on its path. Out of the cloud layer it was no longer visible.
