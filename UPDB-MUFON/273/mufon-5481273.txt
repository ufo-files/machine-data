2 bright orbs, following each other very slowly. One was bigger then the other. After 10 min they went down. It was far away, maybe direction Monchique

My husband went in home to tell me that I must see the lights in the sky. We live 4 years in Portugal and Portugal has a sky so bright with a lot of falling stars so beautiful. But until yesterday we never saw something strange like that.
We saw in the west above the mountains, 2 bright orbs. The were moving very very slowly. The smaller one follows the bigger one. Maybe 20 cm distance between the lights (were we were standing)
The orbs were far away, maybe direction Monchique, we try to put in on tape but it was so unclear.
We look trough a binocular and it was so beautiful and strange together. The orbs were so bright and glowing. They moved so slowly. They were bigger then a star. 
It was getting dark so I guess it was around 9 PM. 
After 10 min the orbs went down and we lost sight because of the mountains.
