{dr}OBSERVED YELLOW GLOWING SHERE LOW ON THE HORIZON, MOVING SLOWLY

ON THE NIGHT OF 4-29-2013 AT APPX. 8:45 PM I WAS WORKING IN FRONT OF MY GARAGE, I LOOKED TO THE EAST (270 DEGREES) AND SAW WHAT LOOKED LIKE A METEOR FALLING FROM THE SKY. THE BEST THING ABOUT THIS IS THAT IT DID ANOTHER PASS IN THE SAME SPEED AND DIRECTION APPX. 5 MINUTES LATER. THIS HAD ALSO OCCURRED ON 8-30-2008, OBSERVED BY A MOTORIST TRAVELING EAST BOUND ON I80 MUFON CASE # 12347.
