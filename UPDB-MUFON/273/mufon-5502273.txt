My family and I were Flying on Southwest Airlines Flight #3571 from NJ to Phx AZ. May 30th, 2014 Departing @ 1:40pm and arrived early @ 3:30pm in PHX. Not sure exactly what state we were flying over TX, NM or AZ... But I took some random pictures of the clouds from my middle seat looking down. When I took my first photo I noticed glowing disks in my picture My 1st thought was the spots on the window, So I quickly took another shot before we passed the same clouds and the glowing disk like objects her gone. But a few minuets before that My husband and I were looking at the clouds. My husband said. Look at those ugly dark clouds, But I quickly new It was a military plan silver grey in color leaving a black trail of smoke behind. Was flying toward our plan at a high speed then turned really fast enough to scare me.. headed East and it was shortly after that I took the random pictures and seen the glowing disks.
