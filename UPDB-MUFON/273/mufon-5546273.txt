I was having a smoke outside when viewing the planet mars. Just as I looked at the red planet an star like object appeared firstly as a dimly lit object in which the light grew in intensity and then faded out just as quickly. The object was visible for only 2 to 3 seconds and moving fairly slowly. It was traveling southwest to a northeasterly direction. no photos or videos of the object were taken.
