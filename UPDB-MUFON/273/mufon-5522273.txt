ufo sighting in blue springs/lees summit area

my mom and i were driving around 7 pm on adams dairy in blue springs, near I70 hwy. to our left in the area where blue springs and lees summit meet (around colbern rd i would guess) were a lot of very bright white lights flying low to the ground. we were close enough to know it was not a plane but far enough to not be able to make out the shape. it appeared to be a circle to me but i cant be sure. it was flying then hovering then flying in the opposite direction. it seemed to be flying back and fourth. as we watched the bright lights disappeared as the craft appeared to turn. i think we were looking at the bottom when we saw all the bright white lights. after it turned, the lights were gone and blinking lights of different colors appeared to outline the edge of the craft. my sister and her boyfriend were driving behind us and as we pulled up to the stoplight and were pointing at it also. then as we drove towards lees summit we saw it again when we were on 291 hwy. then it disappeaed.
