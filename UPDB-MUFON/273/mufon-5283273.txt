Hovering triangle

We we're driving from Hampton township after having dinner. We got on the 581 exit an was heading towards the 81 split to go north or south. We took south cause that's towards carlisle we're I reside. An from the corner of my eye I caught this object floating an moving slow across the night sky. I seen one light illuminating like purplish maroon the others I don't know what color it was. But they where on each corner of the object. Made no sound at all. I seen it then I tugged on my lady who was driving an said what is that? Now I've lived the military life an I know planes. So I thought I was a low lying plane. But when we came close to the off ramp an while turning one it I got more clearer an It was the size of 5 tractor trailers long. A black triangle craft with some kind of lights on the bottom corners. I slowly flew around and as it passed over the high way it zipped very quickly. And at this point I was hanging out the window. An slowed down as soon as it flew over the highway. An started slowly hovering over the other side of the highway going towards Hampton township.
