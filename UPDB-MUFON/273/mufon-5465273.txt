{tjd} Large triangle travelling South to North over Palm Springs Airport.

Came out from work on my break (with about 4 other coworkers who also saw this) at 1:15am to see large triangle shaped object emitting lights along the edges but not the leading edge, traveled from south to north over the Palm Springs Airport. Consistent speed, no directional change. I didn't hear any sound but my coworkers were also talking as we were watching. Wouldn't say it was moving fast but not too slow either, no hovering. Lights were not all consistent, noticed some dimming, others brightening, no pattern. Lost sight as it passed behind my view of our building.

Unfortunately I didn't pull out my camera, I could tell that with my line of sight I wouldn't have a lot of time to view the object so as it's my first experience seeing something like this I decided I'd rather view it through my eyes instead of a phone screen, trying to take it all in. Others have posted video of this event from the Los Angeles area and other parts of Southern California on Instagram and YouTube.
