(GS) looked like a shooting star but then broke up into 3 parts each changing direction directly after separation the flight path of all three made a T path all going in a different direction

My three brothers and i where outside in a 10 acre field about 9pm when we seen whet we thought was a shooting star but without a trail or tail and one of us said look at that shooting star travailing from east to west beyond the speed of any aircraft,when all the sudden the what seemed to be solid object split into three pieces one continued east to west,one went north and one south all keeping the same speed. they split at impossible speeds and never slowed and each traveled over the trees and out of sight!one of my brothers ran inside scared at what we just saw, all of us agreed that this wasnt a shooting star
