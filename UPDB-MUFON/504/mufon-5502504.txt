{rjl} Multiple orange/copper/natural lights appearing and disappearing over south side of Pheonix

I was in an airplane that had just taken off from PHX airport. I was looking out the window and noticed some lights to the south. They were over a cluster of hilly areas or just in front of them. There were about 5, maybe more. They would disappear and reappear in a different spot. Some didnt disappear at all. They just sat there. The configuration was random but it looked like some were higher in altitude than others. It lasted for a minute or more before they all disappeared and the plane was beyond visual range.
