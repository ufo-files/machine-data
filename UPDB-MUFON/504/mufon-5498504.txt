{jd} cluster of about 7 white floating dots in the sky that did not fit the pattern of movement or physical description of any plane, man made flying object, planet, star or flying animal known to this Earth.

Me and my girlfriend were doing laundry at a laundry mat at 35th avenue & Glendale ave. about 1:30pm on Oct. 16, 2014 when we noticed a cluster of about 7 white floating dots that had the appearance of stars or planets in the sky that did not fit the pattern of movement of any star or planet or fit nay physical description of any plane, man made flying object or flying animal known to this Earth. It was very weird the way they moved from a stationary position to a very slow horizontal movement then to a slow moving vertical and then back to a stationary/hovering position with bout 6 other white floating dots around the main dot with patterns of movement seen with purpose and non-random intelligence.
