Star-like appearance

My buddy and I were at Sole Uno (Spa & Wellness) in Rheinfelden, Switzerland. Outside in the swimming pool I looked up the sky (which I do often) and I saw a very bright star. When I pointed it out to my friend, it started to move (from left to right - North to East). It was too fast to be an airplane (afterwards we compared it with airplanes - there is an airport nearby in Basel) and also to bright. There was no sound, it was a bright white star-like light (Not white-red like airplanes). When we looked at it flying in a straight line from North to East, at the end (in the East) it suddenly with a very high velocity shoot straight up and we saw it getting smaller within 3 to 4 seconds, and then it was gone.
