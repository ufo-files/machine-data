Stationary, intermittent blinking light, like a star. Blinked every 30 seconds to 1 minute but did not move for half an hour. Near Orion's foot

I was in my conservatory looking up at the sky at around 2.30am. It was a brilliant night and I was looking out at the stars and thinking about life elsewhere in the universe.

I remembered a video I saw where a man claimed to be able to summon UFO's by thought and just out of curiosity and boredom I decided to give it a try, not expecting anything to happen. I put a thought out into the ether, saying if you're out there, give me a sign.

Within about a minute I saw a sudden flash, near the foot of Orion, like a star blinking into existence suddenly and then vanishing.

I watched the area for a while and then it happened again about 30 seconds later. I watched this phenomenon for around half an hour, the light suddenly appearing and vanishing in the same position in 30 second to one minute intervals.

Eventually I decided to go outside to get a better look, but then it stopped. 

It was definitely not a star as I am familiar with the constellations and it was in the vicinity of Orion and definitely not part of that constellation. 

It was not an aircraft, as I live near Bristol airport and I am familiar with the aircraft coming and going from there.

It was not a satellite or the space station or falling space debris as it was stationary for half an hour.

It could conceivably have been a drone, but who flys drones at 2.30 am?
