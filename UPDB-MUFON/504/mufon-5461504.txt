White orb

I first seen the orb slowly drifting north at approximately 2000ft at an initial distance of approximately 1 mile. The orb would fade away completely for several seconds every 10 seconds or so to then reappear at a position that did not correspond with its trajectory when visible. I estimate a speed of about 50 mph. 
The final time the orb appeared it was approximately 5 miles away. On this occasion however, the orb shot down about 500ft at such a speed that its movement appeared as a white line. The orb then disappeared and I didn’t see it again. 
I have experience of flying light aircraft but do not hold a license myself so I consider myself able to identify conventional aircraft.
