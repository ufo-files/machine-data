I jumped out of car waving my arms and jumping up and down in front of large amber ball of light(partner styed in the car, because he was soooo scared) 10min into it,I turned on the spotlight toward object (sending SOS),and it responded moving up and down,side to side, and diagonally and stopped in the same position with no sound. I did this around eight or more times, and it always would repond to me the same way (45mins) Within a second it went straight up out of sight leaving a long com trail.
