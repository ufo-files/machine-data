I saw a hovering, moving star and a light across the sky

I was outside smoking. I was star-gazing. I saw a hovering, moving star that was also moving towards me. I kept running inside and then back out because of fear. When closer, I could see that the star had a smaller star orbiting it very very quickly. After 20 or so minutes of observation, it also flashed red and green, and teleported around a few times. I also saw in a different part of the sky, a big white light that drew a short but thick line across the sky. I was then so freaked out that I ran inside and I'm now too scared to go out again.
