Followed 2crafts on way home in car walked to meadow to get better look to find 1 had come down to tree height

My then girlfriend and I were traveling home by car at around 22:00 when we spotted 2 craft high in the sky with rows of lights around the outside of wheels like fluorescent lights ,they were spinning slowly ,curious when we arrived home I decided to go down to the meadow and get a better look when I got there I found that one had come Down to tree height while the other was way up in th night sky still slowly rotating.the one that came down was still silently rotating I could only see the lights on the outside the centre off the craft seemed dark like the sky but hazy, suddenly a cube of pure light came out from the centre of the craft about the size of a small car ( the cube was like plasma but did not il1uminate anything and I still couldn't see the centre of the main craft ) this cube then shot off at great speed while I stood and watched from under the tree, it then returned stopped and re entered the craft ( at this point I was jumping up and down with excitement , I didn't feel threatened or scared at all ) I said to myself if the light came out again I would time it and it did , it took roughly 20 seconds to travel out and slowly go back inside the main craft , I then stupidly ran back to the house to tell them what I had seen and we watched both craft spin slowly off towards the Kent coast.this event was reported in the local newspaper at the time and was said to be a laser show held I think in Canterbury Kent about 20 miles away , but it certainly wasn't a laser show that was happening in front of my eyes at tree level , this event was seen by one of my neighbours who was watching from his bedroom window but he never wanted to talk about it and also a young girl of a friend who also saw the craft and cube of light zooming across the meadow towards London . I have looked at most things concerning ufo as now I'm a believer but have never seen another with the lights the same and the centre I would say cloaked , I would also say that it seemed to be searching or surveying while the other higher craft kept watch high above.
