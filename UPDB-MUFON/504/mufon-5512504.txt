{dr}5 pulsating red objects hovering and flying over the Columbia river

We receieved a call from our son in law who was in town. He told us to go outside and look up. we ran to the backyard and coming toward us were 5 objects at a very low elevation moving slowly up and then back down and then they were merging together. 3 of the 5 objects went straight up and into cloud cover where 2 were left behind moving from right to left to up and down until they just disappeared after approx 10 minutes.
