{cak} SAW TWO BOOMERANG SHAPED CRAFT GO OVERHEAD WITH TWO JETS CHASING THEM

FILLED OUT AT MUFON HEADQUARTERS WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE, BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER #18809 ON 3/15/201-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WITNESS SAW TWO GIGANTIC GRAY BOOMERANG SHAPED CRAFT. THEY WERE THE SIZE OF B 52 BOMBERS. FIRST ONE FLEW BY AND THEN ANOTHER ONE. THE WERE GOING IN AND OUT OF THE CLOUDS. THEY WERE A DULL GRAY IN COLOR. 2 1/2 MINUTES LATER 2 JETS CAME OVER APPARENTLY CHASING OR LOOKING FOR THE CRAFT. THE SECOND BOOMERANG CRAFT APPEARED TO HAVE A COCKPIT.
WITNESS HAS VIDEO TAPE OF THE BOOMERANGS.
WITNESS ALSO SAID HE WAS LOOKING AT THE MOON AND SAW A BOOMERANG ON THE MOON WITH SOME KIND OF PIPING NEAR IT.
