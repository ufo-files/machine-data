{dr}Driving home from work and saw the object hovering above the hills. By thie time I was cleared it floated behind the hills

It was around 3:50pm on 1-3-2019. I was on my way home from work and exiting the freeway. I noticed the large object in the air just above the hills. It was stationary - hovering. It was a large rectangular object in an off-white/ gray color with no lights. I kept driving headed in that direction and tried to keep sight of it as much as possible. Trees and buildings obstructed my view but it remained stationary. It was about five minutes. I pulled out my phone and camera to get some pics but by the time I got to a clear spot, it floated off to the north behind the hills and I saw the tail end duck behind there. 

At first thought, I thought it was a blip but it didn't have that rounded shape or the tail fins. It was also so low and close to the hills that I immediately thought of safety concerns for flying there. Behind the hills are nothing but homes and forests. No airports or landing strips exist on that side of the hills. 

The experience was exciting and I wished the roads went that way so I could attempt to follow it. Even as I write this, it still puts me into a state of awe with many questions. I know what I saw and it just defied logic given it's location and altitude.
