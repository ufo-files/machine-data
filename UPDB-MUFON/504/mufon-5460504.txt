{tjd} Captured a blue orb on camera and also captured distinct alien faces around the light pole

My 6 year old son and I were pulling into our neighborhood and noticed a blue hovering light in the distance. Once parked I ran into the yard and took a picture. It stayed hovering and illuminating blue for about 5 minutes. It faded out of sight.. almost as if the blue light just dimmed out into the darkness.. I looked at the photo afterwards and noticed that I not only got the blue orb in the distance but I captured a strange reflection on the light pole on my street corner.. You can see the blue orb in the top of the picture but more intriguing is the light from the pole.. I thought it was my eyes seeing what I wanted to see but after all these years, I still see many “Alien” faces in the light. Many different faces and expressions. The faces overlap but you can clearly see the details and definition in each of them.. Not sure what I captured, but I cannot explain the light images in the picture as just shadows or my imagination.. Look closely around the light pole..
