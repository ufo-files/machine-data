Observed big orange-white balls that disappeared instantly.

At 10:45pm my mother and I pulled into a parking lot just going for a drive as we knew that Jupiter and Saturn were supposed to be out in the sky tonight. We saw a huge orange-white star-like object and we assumed that this was Jupiter and Saturn at first. We drove closer, and at 10:48 we stopped the car and noticed that in the white and orange glow there was a smaller orb. We watched as they both disappeared instantly, didn't fade, outright disappeared.
