3 fireballs in a triangle

I was driving home from dropping my father of at the local pub, where upon driving past our hospital(NE) I noticed a glowing orange light, it seemed different so I slowed down to get a better look, at this point another similar light came on 60 degrees bellow (later to form a triangle). Being a big UFO enthusiast I decided to stop and get out of the car to get a better look, upon doing so a third light appeared completing the triangle, I also so noticed they did seem to be on fire, flickering and edges dancing. The formation stayed for about 1 min, the two that had previously appeared now disappeared slowly in reverse order. I noticed what appeared to be a helicopter now approaching from the NW towards the remaining object which then moved towards the helicopter, est 500m from the heli the object disappeared. As soon as I saw the second light appear I knew this was special.
