{cak} 2 yellow orange objects floated by my windows and turned direction.

This memory has just come back to me within the last month now. I can not remember the exact date but know it was warm out and no crops in the field. I live in a townhome and the only light on in my home was the light from the TV in bedroom. I was watching TV in bed and saw a light out of the corner of my eye. It looked like a Chinese lantern so I jumped out of bed and ran to patio door in living room. Was going to go out on deck to watch it go by. As I got to the door, I froze in spot as I saw another one coming right behind it about 15-20ft. I had so many thoughts going through my head at that time, but I knew I did not want the 2nd one to see me. I backed up in the dark hoping it would not see me. They both were about 3 feet out from the deck and about one foot higher than the railing. They were about 2-3feet high and kind of flat on top and rounded on the sides. As it went by, I looked under it to see if a candle was lit and it was a solid object. It glowed yellow orange and it would change to orange yellow but not to were I could see the color change. I think it saw me at that point as the 1st one was now at my neighbors deck and took a sharp right to go over the field and then to the back of the grade school. As watching them, I heard an airplane coming above and looked up and then back down and they were gone.
