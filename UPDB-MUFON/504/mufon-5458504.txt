{tjd} Central object with two much larger cylindrical objects on either side

I was watching a hawk above my house.  The object was in the background heading west when it caught my eye. I routinely see jets passing overhead at their cruising altitude. This object appeared to be moving much slower and with no contrails but ‘looked’ to be at an altitude of a cruising jet...(very high altitude). I got my 15 x 70 powerful binoculars and ran to the front of the house. Because of its apparent slow speed this object was still just moving over my house. I saw a central object, you can call it a fuselage, and on either side a huge cylindrical object. It looked like a small wing or support was coming off either side of the fuselage to support the round objects.  The supports did not extend beyond the huge round objects. I initially thought they were wings but I am wondering if they were just supports for the round objects.  When compared to a jet, the proportions of this object were unlike anything I have seen. I do think it was some sort of government aircraft. However this did not look like a conventional aircraft at all. I am aware that contrails may or may not be seen when a jet passes overhead.   I have never seen an object with these dimensions in the sky before. Thanks
