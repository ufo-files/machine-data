{rjl} Bright glowing objects, orange in front and white in back. Moving slowly from sw to ne, no sound at all. About 2500 feet high.(EST) Im a pilot, so thats a guess.

We were in our back yard, we were supposed to have a meteor shower that evening. We usually see a lot of satellites. We were looking toward the south, when we happened to notice a very bright orange/white object overhead...then we observed several more, almost in sequence, moving slowly from SW to NE. We watched for over 20 minutes as they passed over head, then disappeared. There was no sound at all. We have no explanation for what we witnessed. I am a private pilot and it was unlike anything Ive seen before. We checked the news that night to see if anyone had reported anything, nothing was mentioned.
