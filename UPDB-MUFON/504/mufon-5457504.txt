{cak} V - shape lights slowly moving over-head NW

Working at a “Crazy Eddie” retail store on Long Island, NY, back in Nov 1985, I was helping a customer load his car with a TV set. After heading back inside the store I looked out into the parking lot and noticed in the night sky 6-7 circular lights flying “very” slowly in a V-pattern going North West. I thought it might be a blimp but it was close to 9:00pm and it had no discerning lights like a blimp. And I thought it might be small planes but there were no engine noise or blinking lights. It simply looked odd to me. I went inside the store and had a few people from my dept come out to view it but it was traveling further North. Since it was slow in the store and we had time, my manager called a few places (local airports etc), to find out if any blimps were flying over the county. We were told no. Then we called the local newspaper and they said they “were” getting other calls about the sightings. And that’s it. The next day the story hit the paper and no one really didn’t pursue it further. But I know what I saw was not planes or a blimp. Just 6-7 lights slowly and silently moving quietly over Long Island. And that’s it. Very uneventful.
