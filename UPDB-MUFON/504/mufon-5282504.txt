Captured very quick movement and hovering with iphone

On this date and time I was taking pictures and videos with my wife and baby at bunker hill /graffiti hill along with the 1 highway. It was very bright outside and windy. I didn't notice anything at the time I was there other than I had a feeling of being watched and that I should film. A few weeks later I was looking at my pictures for posting on Instagram. The shot I took was of someone climbing the bunker and the moon in the background. I applied filters and noticed something moving at a high rate of speed. So I went back and viewed the video in the editor on my iphone and the photos just before and just after. The speed at which the object is travelling defies explanation. I initially thought it was a bird or drone. But the more I have looked at the video and pictures I have doubted this. I decided to report this in the hopes that someone has seen something similar and someone will go here to investigate further. 

I have blocked the face of the person to protect them as they had asked me not to film since he assumed it was not legal to climb the bunker. I do not know him and he did not mention seeing anything at the time I was there. 

The first is the video I shot. I slow it down frame by frame and screen shot it from my phone then allowed it to play at full speed. Next is a photo I shot immediately after. And another right after. You can see an object over the guy and the moon in the first photo. On the second photo the object is gone. I estimate the time between my film and the two photos taken to be 5-10 seconds after the end of the video I shot.
