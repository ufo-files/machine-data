{cak} Husband took picture of me working on roof. Unidentified object appeared in picture above my head

On 8/12/18 in Hohenwald TN at 345 in the afternoon my husband took two pictures of me working on our roof. He sent the pictures to his brother. His brother asked what the object was above my head. We dont know and have no explanation. It is in one picture and not the other.Thought maybe it was a guardian angel because light was around it...Felt good wasn't sure what it was in the picture until I got down from roof later. Did become extremely tired but probably from heat of the day.
After I got down from roof we heard shooting a distance away and heard what we thought was buckshot spraying the roof. Not sure one has anything to do with the other.
