{jpg} UFO Morphs Into Multiple UFOs

I was in my backyard at sunset when I noticed the first star in the sky. Then I realized it was the wrong star that is always first visible in the sky. I grabbed a video camera and zoomed in on the star-like object and noticed it was slowly moving, too fast though to be a star.

I kept the video camera on the UFO and it suddenly split into three UFOs, ejected two smaller and less visible UFOs. One of the objects left a small emission trail. The main object kept moving but now increased in speed. The smaller objects seemed to slow and move in a different direction.

About a minute later the main star-like UFO ejected two UFOs approximately the same size, but also slowed and began moving in a different direction. I then followed these two UFOs until they eventually disappeared. The main UFO also was gone.
