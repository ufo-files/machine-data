{cak} While observing fireworks, observed bright red object in sky pulsating then orange-yellow in middle. Object then vanished.

I was light my last fireworks with my son in an open field in a residential area when I looked in the sky (northwest) I observed a glowing bright red object. It then started pulsating to orange and yellow in the middle. I pointed out the object to my son and we both was amazed at it as it seemed to remain still. The lights then got dimmer and then it was gone. Once it was completely gone, my son thought that maybe it was an airplane but I pointed out to him the planes that were in the sky. It sort of looked like a worm hole or something like a portal as it also appeared to be spiraling.
