We were sitting out in my porch with windows all around. I saw these three yellow/gold lights traveling slowly past my condo. The color was what made me look more at it. It was traveling low in the sky. The three lights were connected somehow as one of the lower lights came forward as if to look at something and the other two lights remained steady. The top light was like the leader, holding them all together. It went slowly past us as if investigating the area. The space between the lights was maybe 200 feet. It remained shaped like a triangle the entire time. The lights would intensify and turn more golden than yellow. I felt they were doing a "drive by" looking into my window and listening to us.It kept going until it was out of sight, slowly. I felt no harm.
