{jpg} Driving home evening saw bright star that started to dip and sway but not move -- had lights under it shape of fat triangle with round circle of lights

I was driving home the evening of Thursday, December 21, 2017 coming off of Rt. 33 North to Leighton exit and was struck by how bright a star was (there were two stars next to each other but one was brighter than the other). I thought they were stars since they were not moving, I looked to see where the moon was and it was on the opposite side from them. As I was staring up at the brightest of the two stars the one to the right which was the very bright one started to dip or so that I could actually see the shape which was a fat triangle with rounded tips to the triangle instead of points. As I was watching this still trying to decipher what I was actually viewing (and it wasn't a plane not moving just a second of dip to see shape) it dipped to the other side then upright again. As it dipped for a few seconds I saw the underside to have three large circle of lights at each rounded tip. I then had to keep driving so I don't know what happened after that. It was not moving at all and was definitely very bright like a star but dipped and swayed to show shape of a fat triangle with rounded tips and at each tip circular set of lights. I felt that I looked for anything that it could be and it didn't fit any of those things -- not a plane, not a star, not a shooting star, not the moon
