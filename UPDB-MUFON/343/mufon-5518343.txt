Me and two other friends were on the way to a movie and saw a triangle craft hover in the distance and disapear suddenly

Me and two other friends were on our way to a movie in Maine coming from Massachusetts when my friend in the drivers seat noticed the object in the sky. His cousin in the back seat and I drew our attention to the object and watched it until it disappeared. It was in triangle form and had white lights on the outer edges and one blinking red light in the middle bottom part of the craft. We all assumed it was a UFO since we couldnt figure out what kind of craft it could be and my friend is a pilot so he would have known. We were pretty close to the movies and watching the time closely so we have an accurate time that we witnessed this. We just thought it was cool and decided to look on the internet to see if anyone else saw it and ended up finding this website.
