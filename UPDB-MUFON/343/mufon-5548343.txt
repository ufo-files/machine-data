Reflecting star like image in blue sky reflecting like a star

It was Tuesday 5-27-08 in Naples Florida. It was my day off and I was sitting on my patio looking at the sunny blue sky when I saw a star flashing light very high in the sky. I could not make out a shape it was flashing light like a mirror. I thought I was seeing things, I took video and my camera picked up nothing. The clouds rolled in over that spot I saw it for about 2 minutes and then the clouds rolled in. There has been what looks like a satelittes every night but they show up in different areas of the night sky and flash different colors. the colors kind of band around the spot and flash as well. I wonder if these things are related? I have been seeing strange things in the sky and never saw these things before. I lived on the east coast of Florida for 20 years and have lived here for 3 years and have seen more weird lights and such here than I have seen in all my 42 years I have lived.
