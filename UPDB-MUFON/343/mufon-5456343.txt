{rjl} At least ten slow blinking objects moved almost as though they were connected in a snake like pattern

Just before 7:30 pm on December 27th 2019 I stepped outside and immediately noticed multiple objects in the sky to the north west of my house. There was cloud cover so almost no stars were visible at the time. The objects all pulsed light in what appeared to be some sort of a pattern. The objects seemed to be connected by the way they moved. It stemmed to used a propulsion method like a snake through water, almost as though it was weightless. The objects made no noise at all, which seemed odd. We are located very close to Camp Lejeune, and are used to seeing military jets and helicopters, but this was unlike anything we had ever seen. After heading north west for about a minute and a half the objects turned course away from us and slowly moved out of sight.
