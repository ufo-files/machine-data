Triangle UFO space chase of 2011

In 2011 on Nov 1st. I seen multiple things. 

The first being a ufo over highway. The was a small airplane size craft. It was built of steel. Wobbly and hovering. It had 3 red dots. In each corner of its triangle shape. 
This was during the day light maybe at about 3pm pst. 

Later in the evening when the sun was setting I begin to look over into the sky and walk a few blocks. I noticed these two white lights. Very far apart from each other. It didn’t look normal like stars. 

I went inside the location ate my food. And when I came out side there a giant spaceship over the intersection. 

I could not tell what object what it was. But I started to run. In fear. 

As I got to back to where I came from. I was still looking back. As I ran. And I could now see this thing chasing me. 

Now. There was a space chase happening. There was two small crafts. Like the one I seen earlier In the day light. And it was chasing the giant space ship out of the USA   

The bigger space craft was better at this game and had a rocket. It keep turning the rocket on. For a boost to get away. 

I did not take pictures. But there are exact pictures of what I seen all over the internet. I have pictures of the crafts. And space chases.
