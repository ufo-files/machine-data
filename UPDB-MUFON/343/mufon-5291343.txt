Pulsing orbs within clouds observed after CE-5 meditation.

I had just finished the CE-5 meditation conducted live by Dr. Steven Greer and was performed by others at the same time all around the world. Approx 2 mins after finishing meditation i saw within the clouds 3 orbs that appeared in sequence in an upside down triangle formation. The bottom orb was deeper in the clouds and didn't light up as brightly as the other two. The sequence occurred twice in quick succession with about 2 seconds between the two. The sequence was- bottom orb would appear first then disappear with the top right orb then appearing and disappearing and then finally the last would do the same. Again this was a quick sequence and happened twice. Two sequences in total and each sequence was about a second long. These lights never returned. Both sequences maintained the same positioning of the the orbs and I cant conclude whether this was three objects or one larger object as the orbs were within the clouds. This is the first time I’ve seen anything like this and i couldn't explain what it was. Any concerts were at a different time. It didn't look like spotlights as the orbs appeared to come from within the clouds and not be a surface cast light. I have seen spotlights before. To me it’s unexplained and I wouldn’t report anything lightly.
