I work at a tattoo shop , went outside for a cigarette , and noticed a triangular craft flying from west to east. I was about 20 miles east of Buffalo , New York , right before Christmas of 2012. It was around 7-8pm , I don't know what day of the week it was. I looked up to the North Northwest and seen this craft that was flying by right above the shop , parallel with county road the shop is on. I could hear a slight humming / buzzing , I ran inside to get a friend , and she came out and witnessed it as well. It was triangular in shape , seemed like it was way too low , had 4-6 lights on it. As it flew by , I had a good clear view , it seemed like the back edge of it was a hologram or went through a blurry change with no hard line to define the back edge of the craft. Also when it did this another light seemed to appear on the back edge as well. I watched as it flew to the west and slowly went out of sight with no apparent rush. I went back inside and said , " I know I am not crazy " It just flew in a straight line as though it was kind of blurry and morphing , with a few lights on it and no wings or regular engine noise. Triangular in shape with some apparent outline , but blurry as well.
