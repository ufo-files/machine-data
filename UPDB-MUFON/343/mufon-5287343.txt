Driving home, saw unusual light.

On the way home from a friend's, about 1 block from her home I looked right then left before turning. Out of my periphery I noticed a light, I got excited thinking it was a comet, then kinda thought, why would I see a comet so brightly here. I was in a high light pollution area, so it didn't make sense. Then it desended in an arc to my left. It went behind an area with trees&homes so wasn't visible any longer. At first sight I thought a comet was headed in my direction had on& that's why I couldn't tell it was moving, but then it went & forward & desended to my left. I told myself it wasn't a big deal, but I did tell my husband about it a few days later. About a week after telling him he asked me about where I was, what time it was ect. He was curious b/c he heard a caller on a local radio show, I think garage logic, mention seeing a ufo in inver grove heights which is pretty close to where I was.
