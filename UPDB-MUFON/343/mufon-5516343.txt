{cak} large, starlike object hovered briefly, then shrank to a small dot

I stepped out of my front door (which faces south) at around 3:45am Saturday August 11, 2012 to have a cigarette. I immediately saw a very large, starlike object to the east just hovering. At first I thought it was Venus but quickly realized it was way too large and bright--my thumb at arms length didn't block it from my sight and it was about twice as bright as I have ever seen Venus. 

I stepped down off of my steps and realized that the moon, which was at waning gibbous and still quite bright, was only a short distance from the moon. I held out my hand at arms length and it was about the distance from my thumb to little finger tip with my fingers spread as far as I could. If it was that bright that close to the moon then I think it may have been bright enough to see in the daytime.

As I watched, it suddenly shrank it size/brightness until it was a small dot, about as bright as one of the dimmer stars in the sky (a little dipper star maybe). I could barely believe my own eyes.

Then a small cloud moved in front of the object and when the cloud moved on the object was gone. I watched for a little while longer but it didn't come back.
