7 lights in v type patern

We were traveling to work as Powerline Crew, my foreman, and myself. We came to where 56 drops into the Bosque River crossing at Valley Mills, TX. 
In the sky was what appeared to be 7 intensely bright white lights. Unwavering in intensity or heavenly position. 
My foreman tried to tell me that it was from artillery at nearby Ft. Hood, to our S about 25 miles away, but I shot him down. I had just left the Army for almost a decade as an Artilleryman, and seen many a Lum' round burning its way down. He didn't stop, and I lost sight. 
I saw the Phoenix lights years later, and that is EXACTLY what we saw.
