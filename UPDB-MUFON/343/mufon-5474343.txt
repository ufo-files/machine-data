{cak} Blinking lights in the Avis Pennsylvania sky numerous times

I noticed a blinking light in the sky after I got home from work. I put my dog out and something caught my eye. It was blinking so I watched it for about twenty minutes until it slowly moved towards two valleys.a couple of nights later I did the same thing got home from work,put the dog out. And the lights were back.but this time I had binoculars. That's when I saw the red and green lights blinking. Watched it until it vanished between the two valleys again. But this time it showed up on the mountain across the highway. I watched it until it disappeared.told my friend about it a and he saw it too.
