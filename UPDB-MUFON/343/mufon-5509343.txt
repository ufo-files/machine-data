[flg] Unusual RED \ ORANGE lights in the sky that caught my attention.

While almost to my house, I saw two Orange\Red lights in the sky. I told my wife and kids to see what it was. My wife said it was Two Helicopters. I told her, "Then where is the red and blue flashing lights?" I decided to pull over in an open area and got out of the car to look up. Both lights were pulsating and there was no sound or other flashing lights. These Orbs were so high, they looked like they were as high as a satellite path. They were too big to be a satellite and I myself have seen the Space Station size in the sky. They were bigger. Then at that time, both took different directions. from the Southwest, one went Northeast while the other went West. It was almost a clear night with a full moon. Both then disappeared. I called the local Police non-emergency phone line where they took my call and said "They will put it in there information."
