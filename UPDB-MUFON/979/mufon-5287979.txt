UFO Ball of Light w/ No Sound Above My House

I went into my backyard to let my dogs out. I looked up and saw a ball of light flying near my hone heading towards restricted airspace (Nellis Range). I am well versed with flying military and civilian planes having grown up on numerous military bases including SAC Alert bases. I ran inside to grab my phone because the object made no sounds at all and was simply a ball of light flying across the sky. I took 2 minutes of video footage before I couldn’t see the object anymore. 

This is my third experience seeing strange things in the sky that I can’t explain. I have a second video from several years ago of two objects without wings flying over my home area. I also witnessed in a different state a silver ball zig zag across the sky and then shoot straight up in the air in a blink of an eye in San Antonio which I also reported to Mufon. 

I have 3 college degrees and I am a very reputable stable person.
