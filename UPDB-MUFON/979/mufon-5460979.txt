{tjd} Octagon shaped flying object, with a dark gray/black matte surface and an intersecting grating beneath on the undercarriage area.

While driving to work, I saw an octagon shaped flying object in the sky, hovering above the treeline and frantically darting around like an insect. The object was the size of a Volkswagen beetle. At first, I thought it was some sort of drone or airplane but it was so large and flying in such an erratic pattern, I started to wonder what it could be. It also emitted a bright yellowish light from one side, almost like a searchlight. The light was strange, though, it didn't look like a direct beam of light and more like how a flashlight beam looks when it is shining through smoke, a kind of hazy, almost floating through the air like you can touch it. 

It continued to dart around, across the street and in the air. I pulled my car over to look at it and when I turned in my seat to look at the thing, it very quickly flew straight upward and flew out of sight into the sky.
