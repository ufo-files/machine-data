{td}2 balls of yellow to orange in color hooping each other before getting together and disappearing

Driving SE on Hwy 24 in Woodland Park, Colorado. We looked at this light that was at the right side of Pikes peak. At 1st I was thinking that was a flare, until the 2nd light show up separate from the 1st one and they were hovering not dropping or falling like a flare. Both lights were Hovering and Jumping to each other, each time getting closer to each other. At the point that on my point of view there were about 1 inch apart they both bright up and Disappears.
