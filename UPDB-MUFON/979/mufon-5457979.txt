Diamond shaped object with flashing lights pulsating left to right red and green

I got into bed at 4am Saturday night/Sunday morning when I noticed a weird light in the sky out the window, the closer I looked the more I realised it wasn’t an aeroplane, helicopter, balloon or anything of the sort because of the shape and the pulsating lights and I knew straight away it was a ufo, it hovered around the same area for as long as I was awake and fell asleep whilst still watching it so I couldn’t say if and how it flew off. Although once me and my friend had seen it we opened the window blind to see it clearer and it appeared to come closer to us almost as if it could see us which was when we realised it was looking straight at us no more than 50 feet in front of us but the size and shape of the object didn’t seem to change as it got closer, we just knew it was there in front of us then it would back away still keeping the same size
