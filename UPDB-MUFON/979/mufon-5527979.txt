Two stange sightings observed by 5+ people. First one is a similar object from a previous sighting. Odd bringht orbs in the sky.

Me and two other friends were leaving my house to go to a convenience store at 8 PM. It was completely dark outside, and we noticed an object that we have seen before in the sky. It appeared to be a bright orb floating about in the sky. Its light pulsated slowly at different intensities, and clearly was not a star. It moved to many different positions in the night sky. It observed no typical aerial movement attributed to planes of helicopters. It just floated around for hours! Long enough to have one of my buddies with a high-quality camera to come over and photograph and videotape it AFTER we came back from the convenience store. (These photos will be emailed to a MUFON investigator when I get them later today.) Meanwhile, about 5-10 minutes into our drive towards the Wawa in Charlotte Hall, we observed three of the same orbs in a triangular formation about halfway up the size of a cell phone tower like this one *(Link moved to Additional Evidence Section--CMS/sg) in the sky. These objects then exhibited the odd behavior of producing smaller red lights that, then floated up into the sky. After this, the objects disappeared one after another. (Another friend in another place in the county was observing the same object and reported them to disappear at the same time I saw my glow-boys disappear.) At least 5 people witnessed this event from different locations. When we returned to my house, we proceeded in photographing the initial object we saw. I did not lose sight of the object, as last time, and ended up just going to bed a few hours later. I can provide names and contact information of the other witnesses, as they would be glad to validate this claim.
