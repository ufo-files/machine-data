{dr}Lights in the sky heading north

I saw 2 non-pulsing lights heading straight north at 11:56 PM 06212012. They did not make a sound and the lights did not flash like a plane. We assumed they were satellites but I didn't think satellites moved north. Then I heard and saw a plane with red blinking lights coming from the east to the intercept area of 2 lights. When it got close to the position I saw a non pulsing light heading back south without sound. Satellites do not change direction. This was moving faster than any satellite I have ever seen. The red flashing lights and plane looked unusual too as it appeared the lights were really far apart given how faint the sound appeared. The non-blinking lights were moving much faster than the plane. Did any one else see this? I would like to know what this was.
