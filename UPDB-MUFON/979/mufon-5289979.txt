A spinning rectangle, flashing light, then dark, oriented vertically, moving from ocean surface to sky.

The object was at first below me, on the surface of the ocean(I was on 6th floor of Hilton Hotel in Ocean City, Maryland), I estimate about a quarter mile out to sea. It was spinning, alternating dark and reflecting the sun. So it was very bright, then very dark, which gave the impression that the object was spinning, but I couldn’t really see it spinning. The shape was vague, but seemed to be rectangular, maintaining a vertical orientation. In the few minutes I was watching, it rose from the surface of the ocean, below me, to above me, and began to shrink in size, which I assumed to be the object getting farther away. It disappeared into the sky above me, in the direction of Southeast, out to sea.
