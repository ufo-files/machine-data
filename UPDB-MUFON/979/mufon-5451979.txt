Sliver craft seen hovering then changing direction rapidly.

It was sometime during the summer of either my junior or senior year of high school. We were at baseball practice around 4-430 pm and our coach brought us in for a meeting. I was standing facing the hills while our coach had his back to the mountains. I was zoning out a bit while he gave us the usual pep talk. When all of a sudden something silvery/chrome shiny catches my attention. I focus and I see that it is kind of a dome/cylinder shaped object hovering in place. The craft made no noise whatsoever. The first thing that popped into my mind was that isnt a jet or any aircraft that we know of. I've always been into fighter jets and such so this really got my attention and I was zoned in. I kept staring at the craft and then it suddenly descended and made a (my right) left turn without making any noise or slowing down. In the blink of an eye it was gone. No sound or smoke or fumes left behind just silent.
