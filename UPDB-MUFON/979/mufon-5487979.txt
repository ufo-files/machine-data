{rjl} Sphere shaped object with lights or windows. A green like mist surrounds object.

I was in Daytona Beach Florida, staying at the Mayan Inn for the Labor day weekend holiday. Sunday morning 9/6/15, I went out onto the balcony and took 2 pictures of the sunrise on the last day of my trip. I did not realize I had captured any thing on my camera. I did not see anything as I sat on the balcony and looked at sunrise and ocean. So, the object was not visible to me. The next day I was looking through my pictures and noticed on the one picture something different and when I enlarged my picture I was shocked to see object and the clarity of the object that appears to have 4 windows or lights.
