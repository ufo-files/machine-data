{jpg} A Long cylinder with 3-4 round objects reflecting white light

I was preparing to go for a swim in my pool when I looked up and saw a strange crescent cloud formation. It seemed to have very defined edges so I picked up my Iphone camera and took a photo and emailed to my desktop computer to be further examined . I then went inside my house to view the photo I had just taken. When I down loaded and opened the photo attached to the email I noticed a separate object in the photo which I had not seen before. As I enlarged the photo, I could clearly see a cylinder object off to one side of the crescent shaped object. I circled the objects on the photo showing their position to each other. The photo also clearly shows 3-4 round areas reflecting white light from the cylinder. There was no movement or sound coming from either of the objects to the best of knowledge. I lost sight of the objects when I went inside the house.
