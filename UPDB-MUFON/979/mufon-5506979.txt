{dr}Spinning, Multi-colored Flashing lights, zig zagging then disappearing into sky behind hills

Driving home in dark on Hwy 280 going northbound in Palo Alto, CA after the Alpine Rd. exit. Saw spinning, multi-colored flashing lights, zig zagging then disappearing into sky behind hills. At first thought it was police car driving with lights flashing high up on a windy hill within view. Then it started spinning with rainbow colors, and we noticed it was up in the sky. Went from red-blue-white-purple-turquoise-yellow-orange-red. We were shocked and wondered if perhaps the lights were LEDs because they were so bright. We have seen several UFOs but nothing this spectacular. We watched it for about 2 minutes. I was driving at the time, so I had to look away, but my boyfriend was able to capture a cellphone video of it for only a split second before it disappeared. We got home and searched "rainbow ufo" and found many similar videos to what we saw on Youtube. I am trying to email the video to myself right now, it is taking too long to load. If anyone is interested we can send it.
