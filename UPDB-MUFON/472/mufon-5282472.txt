Bright light with changing directions

Leaving Allentown and heading south, I observed a bright white light that I thought was a shooting star but it had no trail and was visibly lower. The object travelled faster, slowed down, turned, and then disappeared. It had no blinking lights and was whitish yellow, and bright.
