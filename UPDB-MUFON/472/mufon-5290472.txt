Sighting Over St Mary’s College

As I passed the bridge beside the college towards the right, I saw a very bright fluorescent green light which appeared to be pulsating. Within a few seconds what appeared to be far away was right in front of my car. The green glow seemed to retract as it passed over my vehicle and I could see the white dome shape of the underside of this object. Even though I never stopped my vehicle it felt as though it was hovering over me and I was still. I pushed the gas pedal and didn’t stop until I was in front of my home. I was crying tears flooded my eyes and cheeks as I realized the fear and uncertainty of all logic was dissipating every second my mind recalled the image.
