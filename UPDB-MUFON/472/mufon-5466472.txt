{cak} Saw huge blue light in sky that was falling, it blew up, then the ship appeared, then shot off

I was in Marana, Arizona, and me and my friend were skating at around 11-12 at night. I was looking down at my phone when my friend told me to look up, and then something reflected off my phone. When i looked up, what i saw was what looked like a giant shooting star, but it lasted for at least a minute. It then blew up into blue light and flashed across the sky. We were very confused and freaked out, and then we heard a low hum. We looked up and saw a giant ship about 200 feet above us, probably less. It had lights on the side and a blackish exterior, but it wasnt a nice finish, it was very jagged, like scrap metal was welded together. It stayed there for a few minutes, and then shot off into space so fast we couldnt even see the acceleration.
