{rjl} The craft was absolutely massive; bigger than any plane I've ever seen. It was blacker than the sky behind it, and had one large white light at each point of the triangle.

I was standing outside with my sister and her friend at night. We were talking on the porch when we heard a high-pitched whirring sound. I looked up and screamed. Flying above, there was a MASSIVE triangle craft that was solid black in color. There was a white light at each point of the triangle. My parents came rushing out just in time to see it disappear over the mountain. Everything went silent, then there was a huge rush of wind blowing in the direction the craft went about 10 seconds after we lost sight of it. An hour later, I looked out my window to see a black helicopter flying over the mountain in the same path the craft did.
