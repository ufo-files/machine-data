Saw roughly 5 orange glowing orbs flying together

My girlfriend and I were on our last day of our vacation trip and we were hungry, but since it was 11 at night not many places were open so we went to Buffalo wild wings. As we were in the car about to get out I looked over her shoulder out her window and saw 5 orange glowing objects. As I said "what the heck is that" she turned to look at them aswell. They were moving all together but each going in a different direction. I quickly grabbed her binoculars she uses for birding to look at them, but by the time I did they were all gone. I'm an airplane mechanic and I know what an airplane looks like. They didn't look like airplanes.
