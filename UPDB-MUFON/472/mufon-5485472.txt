Slow triangle shaped, 3 blinking lights, seemed to disappear/reappear

I had been thinking about a previous encounter that had happened 10 years ago an hour or so earlier and had been watching a comic book series on my laptop. I opened the window to have a cigarette and saw three flashing lights in the shape of a triangle moving slowly north easterly. I recognized it immediately as a UFO. The blinking was slow and the craft was barely visible when not blinking yet it reappeared in irregular places as if it was projecting itself slightly each time. It had no constant light as you'd find on a plane nor did it make a sound (it was close enough to hear had it been "current" technology) and had a fuzzy aura around it when visible. I continued to watch it calmly and tried to communicate telepathically asking it to come closer as I had done once before. This time it didn't. I watched it disappear behind the houses to the North east of me. I wanted to run outside to continue watching it but wasn't suitably clothed. I felt as if the air was charged with static electricity. I live around 15 miles from an airport and there are planes passing in the night sky regularly on the same trajectory, this craft followed a different one, was much closer and again, made no sound. I wasn't surprised to see this as I've seen objects of varying description a number of times yet I was intrigued given the fact that I had been thinking about the same type of UFO earlier on.
