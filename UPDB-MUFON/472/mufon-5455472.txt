Orb, Unidentified Flying Object- March 27th,2019.       By: BorjaCaseroVelasco.©

March 27, 2019 at 21:15. We were in the Peak of the Stone Giant (El Peñagolosa), at 1813 meters high. During our photo shoot to the sky, we began to see a red light on the horizon that at 10 seconds began to fly towards the east and it did not take more than a minute in the air, when it began to descend very quickly and disappeared behind the mountains.
