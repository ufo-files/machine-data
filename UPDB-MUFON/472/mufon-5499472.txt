{nd} a huge orangish-yellow circle glowing at night right above the pine trees..moving slowly in the same direction of the trees...

I was approximately 11 to 13 years old and my step-father asked me to take the mail to put in the mailbox...remember it was late and already dark outside so was a little scared...ran to the mailbox...put the mail in and pulled the flag up and running back up to the house and was almost there when this HUGE light was to my left above the tall pine trees...was glowing orangish color and moving extremely slowly in the same direction of the pine trees...(the pine trees were planted in a straight line on the edge of the driveway)..can remember standing(almost frozen in place)staring at this thing and then it was like my mind kicked back in and I ran back into the house....never told anyone about this until years later when I met my husband and then to an administrator on the facebook Mufon site...who encouraged me to report it here. Don't recall thinking about what it could have been...just knew it was not right to see and remember being scared and running back into the house...did not lose sight of the object...when I finally could run....I did...
