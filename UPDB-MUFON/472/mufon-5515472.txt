Exact time mentioned above again right before I left for my evening walk, I looked up and there out of no where appeared the same looking star like object at around 80 degrees while I faced in a south, southeatern direction. I ran inside to get my video camera which I now bought and brought it outside, turned it on and tried to focus in on the object. I zoomed in as far as my camera would go and now the object did not appear star like but rather diamond shapped. The camera had a hard time focusing in and as I pressed the record button, the object blinked out and dissappeared. I kept observing for a few minutes more but it did not reappear. About 10 minutes later a Navy twin engine plane and some sort of jet which I couldnt make out what type it was were flying around low and high. Object did not reappear.
