Satellite observed to abruptly stop, move backwards than change directions

I was observing the stars with my husband, it was a clear night.  We saw a satellite and watched it move across the sky as normal. We then saw what we thought was a second satellite moving west to east in the northern sky. However it stopped for about 10 seconds, and reversed its path. I asked my husband if he had seen that and he said yes. The "satellite" then started to move in another direction (left, forward, right, reverse). It was at this point that we both were awestruck because there was no way that could have been a satellite, star, planet or passenger plane ( it was way too high in the sky and barely visible with the naked eye).
