{cak} Slow moving orb-like light, no flashing lights, low altitude. Moved east to west, turned abruptly, went dark

Was sitting on my front porch trying to listen for thunder from a distant storm and observed a slow moving, round light flying slowly from east to west for 2 - 3 minutes. Initially I thought it was a satellite, but realized it too low. There was no apparent beacon light, no engine sounds. Once it reached due south, it made a sudden sharp right 90 degree turn and the light was suddenly gone. I could no longer see the object.
