{rjl} flashing red light erratically changing directions

I happened to go outside around 7p ET. I saw a red light in the sky over the direction of Dallas, GA. At first I thought it was a helicopter because it was stationary but then it started moving erratically all over the sky. It appeared in the path of arrival patter for KATL but it wasn't a plane. It was shooting all over the sky. It wasn't a laser or a drone. I would say it moved miles between changing directions faster than any helicopter could. it moved up, down, left and right and below the tree line and back up. I can't estimate the speed but it was faster than any helicopter could move in such a short time. It was to the east, west and south of Jupiter which I know because it's super bright white in the sky.
