Historical. Small whitish ball

Biking down country road in KY next to my home. At turn around point I looked at sky before mounting bike saddle again. Saw coming across field a small whitish ball. It flew over me and headed straight ahead over another field. Disappeared. I watched until it was out of sight. I couldn’t believe what I was seeing. Never heard of a UFO the size of an orange flying broad daylight, silently across farm field. What was it?
