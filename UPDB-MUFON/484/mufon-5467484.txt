{jpg} A blue green orb-like glowing object appeared and zig-zagged downward as in an evasive maneuver and blinked out.

When traveling to Cleveland Ohio at about 8 to 9 o clock at night, we where traveling on the highway just outside Cleveland when we noticed a Ch-47 chinook helicopter traveling along side of us at speed. When it had overtaken us on the right side and flown a bit further ahead we noticed a second one off in the distance coming towards us. Right when they were within 500 feet of each other a bright blue green orb about the size of a small car suddenly appeared in between them and traveled off to the left in a sudden backwards 1z1 zig zag motion downwards and blinked out before it reached 50 feet above the ground. It felt as though the ch-47s were traveling with great urgency and the blue green orb was making evasive maneuvers to escape them. Once it appeared we felt a quick pang of fear as you would normally feel if something jumped out at you. I have never seen anything of that size move that fast before. I have considered every other plausible explanation for what it was from ball lightening to tracer fire and none of it makes any sense. It was a fairly clear night and The object did not originate at the helicopter but in the middle of the air between them. I have also never seen any tracer fire zig zag like that that fast.
