{jpg} chain of various objects

I observed a chain of 28 objects travel at a high altitude and of high speed in a NE direction. These objects were in a medium distance group of about 8 seconds apart.
I later observed on of these objects stop, enlarge in size about twice its size, split into two parts, travel at a slower rate of speed in opposite directions and then stop and remain until I lost sight of them as the sun rose.
I then observed two additional objects travel in an sw direction past these two. the two traveling SW were side by side
