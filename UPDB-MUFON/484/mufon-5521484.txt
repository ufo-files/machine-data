Flying along above a jet heading southwest out of DC

I was in a shopping center parking lot, the weather was sunny and clear. I noticed a jet flying overhead, heading southwest from Washington, DC. Directly above the jet was an object exactly keeping pace with it. The object was at least the size of the jet. It was too far above the jet to be in any way attached to it, and it was too close to be in any kind of approved flight path. The jet had a huge contrail, the object above it had none. I watched until the jet and the object flew out of sight.
