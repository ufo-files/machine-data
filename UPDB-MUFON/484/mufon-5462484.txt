{cak} 2 Objects were moving across the north sky slowly and disappeared.

I was taking astrophotography pictures of the Andromeda galaxy to the North around 9:35 PM EST. While photographing the galaxy for 60 second exposure, my eyes were scanning for a particular star near M31. As I was staring at this star, my eyes detected 2 objects moving that twinkled like stars. The longer i stared I could see the objects were diamond shaped. They slowly streaked through the sky and then the two vanished. One disappeared before the other. The objects never reappeared. Objects were not satellites as they typically do not move in pairs.
