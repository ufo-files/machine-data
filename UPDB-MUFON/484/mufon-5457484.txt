{rjl} UFO hovering next to us as we drove down highway

It was about 3am and we were driving through Ohio from Minnesota to New Jersey on Route 70. My husband was driving and I was in the passenger seat sleeping, while my sister and daughter were asleep in the back. My husband couldn't speak and hits me in the leg to wake me up to see a large cylinder maybe the size of a football stadium glowing and hovering next to us as we drive down the highway out the driver side. My husband was finally able to speak and said that it was following us for about 20 minutes. I excitedly woke up my sister and daughter who opened their eyes to see it as well. I was saying find the camera! The UFO must have realized we were all awake and aware of its presence. It stopped following next to us and moved directly behind us and then shot off into the sky and was gone in a split second. My sister couldn't even talk about it for 10 years without crying. We never really talked about anything in detail after that, other than that we witnessed it. PS... It was the middle of the night so not sure where on Route 70 we were, only that I believe we never passed through any major cities again after we were all awake.
