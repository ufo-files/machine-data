Glowing purple and green triangle.

I was lying in bed when I heard a loud humming noise from outside my window. I got out off bed and opened the blinds to look outside. I then noticed a bright glowing triangular shaped object low in sky above my neighbor's house. It was stationary and glowing very brightly. I quickly ran outside for a better look but the object had disappeared and there was no humming noise.
