{cak} Viewed orange light flickering like a candle moving slowly from North to South, then quickly changed direction back to the North.

I joined my husband on our back stoop for a cigarette. I was standing in the garage and he was outside. He looked over toward the front of the house and noticed a strange light. He beckoned me to come outside and take a look as he had no idea what it was that he was looking at. The light was a dark orange color and flickered slightly like a candle. It moved slowly toward us. I told him to take a picture and as soon as he pulled out his phone, the light started moving the opposite direction. It never looped out to turn around, just started going backward. It was dark out so none of the pictures turned out, but we just watched it move away from us until we couldn't see it anymore. It was very strange, we were almost in awe just trying to figure out if it could be a plane or something else.
