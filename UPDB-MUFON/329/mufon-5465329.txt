{tjd} Disc over downtown Deming NM June 1965

I have always wondered about this. My parents and I were traveling and stopped in Deming NM for the night in June 1965. We walked toward downtown for dinner and as we reached the center of town we noticed people on the street looking up and pointing. The number of witnesses was more than 3 but obviously I cannot verify them. It was a silver object that appeared to be disc shaped and was completely stationary. It did not appear to move or be tethered to the ground. I believe that relative to the ground where I was standing it was not a small object. When we went into a restaurant we heard the waitress say that they had called closest the Air Force base and were told it was a weather ballon. After dinner it was darker outside and the object was reflecting light. It was still there when we entered our hotel room and gone in the am. Could it have been a weather ballon given that it was stationary?
