I came home from work,was relaxing, there was to be a meteor shower late so I stayed up to watch. Sitting on my balcony I noticed a light from a good distance away moving very stealth like. Mind you last night/this morning was very clear out no clouds. The light looked like a descent sized star. Moving quickly and smooth. This was 1:30 approx 2 am. It would move then stop then to the left a bit then straight. I thought it was a drone the movement. In comparison. It seemed to get closer this went on for over an hour. I also did see a few meteors they looked closer. Then the object ascended up very quickly were the light was still very bright to see. But far. And it stopped and slowly moved over. Now this is why I'm writing this. There was a blueish circular tube that appeared like a cloud the middle was black. The object went into the middle. And then turned to two, or broke off the one light went through and disappeared leaving another light which went the opposite direction and then straight up until I could no longer see it. I tried to take photo. But did turn out. Has to be explanation. This is the most remarkable thing I've ever witnessed. I looked online to see if anyone else saw what I did. I'm not a crazy person. But I really do think I saw something extraordinary.
