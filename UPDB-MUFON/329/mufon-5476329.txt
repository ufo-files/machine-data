A picture of an UFO was taken by accident when my mother in law was taking pictures of the buildings in Toronto Downtown

Next day she was showing pictures of downtown buildings to her friends at the ESL program where she is studying English, and they noticed that there is was an strange object flying on the sky.

Picture is really clear and you can see something flying that looks like an space ship.
