I heard what I thought was fluttering or crackling in the tree behind me. Turned around to see orb take off from tree.

I was standing in my driveway talking to a friend who was sitting in her car with the door open. It was approximately 7;00 PM, it had been raining somewhat ( but not at the time of this event ) and there were thunderstorms in the area. I heard, from behind me, what I thought was a bird fluttering in the tree that kind of sounded like electrical crackling also. The tree was about 15 ft behind where I was standing. I turned around to look at what was causing the noise. As I turned a white ball came out of the tree and traveled away from us at a very great rate speed across the street over the house across the street and disappeared from sight. There was a definite curvature to its flight path as it flew away as it looked to avoid the trees across the street. The person who I was talking to also witnessed this event. I have never seen anything like it before or since. The entire event lasted no more than 15 seconds. I got a little excited and asked my friend if she had seen it and she said that she had and we wondered what the heck it was. At the time I wrote it off to ball lightning as I had heard it can occur when thunderstorms are around but was watching your today ( 4/19/12 ) show about orbs and it reminded me of this event.
