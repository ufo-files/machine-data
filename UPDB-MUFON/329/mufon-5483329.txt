{jpg} thought was satelite then noticed grey rectangle shaped bar which gave triangular appearance to front light followed closely by plane? white blinking lights

May 24th 2016, 9:15pm Norfolk Virginia. I was looking at stars and thought this was just another satellite until i noticed a grey rectangle bar close behind it giving it a triangular shape. Directly behind that, very close, was a plane? with white blinking lights. For a second it looked like one long object. White blinking object started going to the right of smaller front object, now they were side by side. Front object started to go left creating a larger gap between the 2. Blinking plane? headed left(as if following) and moved to the left side of smaller front object, now side by side again. Last i saw (trees were about to block view) front object was going right again creating more space between it and blinking object on its left. I've watched the skies for 30 years hoping to see something ~out of the ordinary~ and last night was it! :-) Total time 2 minutes.
