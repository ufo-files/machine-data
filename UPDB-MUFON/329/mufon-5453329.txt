Heard ufo take off

I was at San clemete state beach with 3 friends around 12 midnight having a bonfire along a small canyon running from hills down to the beach. The night was quiet when suddenly a loud loud sound occured . It resembled a noise like a slowly revolving large centrifugal engine of sorts I guess I would say to describe it when I first heard it, over the next 10 to 15 seconds the speed of the revolutions increase in speed exponentially until the sound was a high pitched whirring sound. Then suddenly the noise dissapeared. All 4 o us were running toward the beach as this was happening and by the time we reaches the beach thats when the noise was gone. I would say the noise was louder than any craft I have heard before taking off. It seemed to have come out of the ocean and take off into the distance at incredible speed. Although I only could tell this by sound.
