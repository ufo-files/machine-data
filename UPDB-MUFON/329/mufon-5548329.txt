blue lights triangular shaped objects "flip-flopping" above the trees

I was driving to work at approximately 10:15 PM on June 5, 2008, south bound on Van Buren Street towards La Palma in Anaheim, when all of a sudden, two flashing bluish triangular shaped objects caught my attention to the east of my vehicle, appearing to be just above the treetops. I could only see them for a few seconds, as they were traveling to the north, and I was headed south, but they almost appeared to be "flip-flopping" across the sky, or were flashing in such a way that they appeared to be flip-flopping. It was strange enough that I exclaimed , "What the %#@$^ is that???" to myself in the car. I did not pull over, as I was not in a good area, but was trying to see them in the rear view mirror. I could not see them any longer. It was all so fast I don't know whether I was seeing UFO's, or some kind of visual effect, but there are only basic streetlights in the area, and several run-down houses below the area where I saw these lights. When I thought about what this could have possibly been, I couldn't think of anything that would move so fast, or so erratically, and I have no idea what it was.
