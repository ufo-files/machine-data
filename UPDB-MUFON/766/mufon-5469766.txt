{cak} Three gigantic fireballs in the sky - one above, two below forming a vertical triangle shape

We were coming home from church one night with my parents and sister - all of us saw this. Almost as soon as we turn out on to the highway, across the road a little bit further down in the pastors sister's field there are three giant fireballs hovering close to their house but not completely over it, mainly over the field. There bottom two fireballs were just above the treeline. We were completely shocked but we were afraid to stop, so we drove past and went to get a closer look by coming up behind the field on a back road. We could see them nearly the entire time, they never changed position, just hovered there. Once we got on the backroad to the spot where they were supposed to be, they were gone. I've tried to do research over the years to find out what it was. I've never seen anything like it. We still bring it up from time to in family conversations to make sure we actually did see that and it wasn't just one of ours imagination. They fireballs were so huge someone else must have seen it. But I haven't spoken to anyone else about it in the area. I would love to know if anyone else has seen anything like this before.
