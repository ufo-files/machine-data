12 red yellow and green stationary objects over town for 5 hours

At approx 6 30 to 7 30 pm on Oct 14 with clear skies, my fiance and I saw up to 12 objects hovering in the sky for about 5 hours. they were red yellow and green. disappeared. no sound. should have been visible to entire town. on Oct 15 they returned at the same time. only 3 and very cloudy. one object moved around the others and had a rectangular shape and 3 lights on the back. 
on the 14th sighting was to the left of the moon and the 15 to the right of the moon. 
on the 15th while taking a video of the objects, when he looked at video the large orange light was not there to his eye!
