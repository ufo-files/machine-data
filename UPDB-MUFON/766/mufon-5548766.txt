i am 51 now.this event happened in Rumson NJ.i'm pretty sure it was june or july of 72.there is an apro report my parents gave with a drawing and description of the ufo as being as large as a 4 engine jet airplane.i found the report with help from a ufo website years ago and downloaded it,but the computer crashed and i lost the report.as i am aging i am getting memories of being abducted.we pulled into the parking lot behind the old high school.there was at least 25 people there...some crying..etc.etc.i remember looking at it after getting out of the car.it was so close i could have hit it with a stone...next thing i know i'm sitting in the car and asking my parents and brothers....where did it go/..it was like time passed...everyone was gone and the ufo was hovering over the oceanic bridge...traffic was stopped..people staring and then it just took off....very fast...we just said wow...went to relatives we were visting..we were up from florida on vacation...went to bed and almost forgot about it the next day,but my parents did make a report...i think with the asbury park press.any help finding this report would be apreciated...thanks...steven abel
