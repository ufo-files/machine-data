{rjl} Sighting of 3 circular light objects racing across the sky back and forth up and down like playing tag in the sky then exiting straight up

Approximately in the hot summer months, July or August of 1966 at around 5:00 pm (daylight hours in Southern California) my mother was swimming in our backyard and yelled out for help. I ran outside and she was pointing up in the sky and we witnessed 3 circular lights in the sky racing across the sky, stopping, abruptly changing direction, going back and forth, up and down defying inertia, almost like the 3 objects were playing tag in the sky. We watched them for 3 to 5 minutes in the northern sky, then all 3 at the same time went straight up until they were completely out of sight. I was 14 at the time and went inside and called the police. The police didn’t take me seriously. My own father didn’t take us seriously so we just kept this to ourselves but we knew what we saw. My mother has since passed away but we both knew we saw something not of this world!!!!
