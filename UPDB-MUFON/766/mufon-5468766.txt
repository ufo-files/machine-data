I went outside to smoke a sigarette. I looked at the stars as I always do. Movement caught my eye. A black triangle shaped craft flew almost directly over me. It flew at high speed. It had very faint orange lights on it's wingtips. The most unsettling thing was that it emmitted no sound at all. It came from a northerly direction. I lost sight of it when it passed over the roof of my home. I got scared and ran into the house.
