I was at my business in Little River, SC last night.1 I saw multiple lights above the tree line that "turned on" in succession, and then went out in the same manner.

Since these were very similar to what my wife had seen over our house in Longs, SC, a few months ago,1 I called her and told her.

After the phone call,1 the lights reappeared and again disappeared at a slightly different place over the trees.

I texted her that I had seen it again.1 When she received the text,1 it made her want to go outside and look at the sky.1

As she was walking through the back door, she heard a neighbor yell "holy cow! do you see that?" In the sky she saw a multiple orbs that instantaneously move from one side of the horizon to the other.1 Keep in mind that this happened 10 miles away from my location where I was almost at the exact same moment seeing what I saw.

The craft then moved to a very high altitude, directly over our house. At this point scared, my wife went inside and just asked me to come home.
