We were taking photos of the Capitol building and the Christmas Tree

We were taking pictures of the Canadian Embassy at 4th and Penn then aimed at the Capitol Building....then walked to 3rd and Penn and took more pictures. We did not see anything at the time with our naked eyes but as we were looking at our photos, we saw the lights in the sky. The photos represent about a 5 minute time span from the first photo to the last (1705-1710) In the later photos you can see spotlights framing the Capitol and thought that might be contributing to what we saw...but when you see the pattern and then zoom in, it’s just odd. We continued to take more photos of the Capitol from the same area but no other lights were seen in any other photos from multiple angles.
