{rjl} I observed a large object off in the distance from the 2nd story of my home. It wasnt anything that I have seen before & I live close to WPAFB.

I awoke around 2:30am. I went to the window and observed a large object hovering over the neighborhood. The lights were mesmerizing and I couldnt stop watching it. It seemed to hover for a few minutes before moving towards my house & I was on the 2nd floor and it appeared to be several hundred feet above the house and it was quite large & a dull black or gun metal grey color with many lights around it in a boomerang type shape. They were not your typical aircraft lights. There was no sound. As it left my line of sight I went to another window in the house just in time to see it go up & quickly disappear. Having lived in this area for over 50 years close to WPAFB I have seen a lot of various aircraft. This was unfamiliar to me.
