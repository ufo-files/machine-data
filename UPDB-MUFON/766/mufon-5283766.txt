Massive bright light seen by multiple witnesses who live almost an hour apart nearly simultaneously

I was driving home when directly in front of me in the sky a massive blue white object shot across the sky. It appeared diamond shaped, but given the speed that is possibly a trail from the object. My husband sitting next to me was not looking up and did not see it. Less than a minute later my phone rang. I ignored it in order to tell my husband what I had just witnessed. When I walked in my house a moment later my cousin who lives in a city an hour north texted me. She was the one who’s just called. Shockingly, she was excitedly describing seeing the same thing. She had been outside in her hot tub looking at the stars when the object flew overhead. Both she and her husband witnessed the item flying. What I saw appeared to be diamond shaped and blue-white, but what they witnessed seemed more round and blue-green. It’s impossible to imagine that these sightings are not related. I am reporting this because I have seen many shooting stars and various meteors, and I have never seen anything this fast fly straight across the sky before.
