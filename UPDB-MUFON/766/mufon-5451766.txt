Saw a translucent object in the sky hover before taking off

I was out prepping siding to paint it on the morning of 7/3/2020 at about 1030. I finished a section that was up on the deck area. I pivoted around which had me now facing north, and I stretched. It was then I noticed what to be a translucent diamond shaped object hovering at roughly 10000feet. I say 10000 feet because I see many single engine aircraft and helicopters fly over and this was roughly that altitude. The sky was fairly blue with some white clouds in the area, but this was in the blue field of the sky. A bird flew across the top of the trees, in my line of sight of this thing, and I knew my eyes were not playing tricks on me, the object remained.
I put my thumb up to it, and it extended at the longest section, just a bit past both sides of thumb. Where my thumb would block out a single engine aircraft or helicopter.
It then turned and took off to the NE, which would take it heading towards the Dayton area.
I did not have my phone on my person since I was doing work on the house.
