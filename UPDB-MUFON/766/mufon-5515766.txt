(GS) 15 to 20 round bright lights camedown from clouds in DNA formation

I was watching the end of the 2001 world series when my wife,my two sons and my brother in came to the door as they were out that night.When I saw there faces they all looked concerned. I ask them what was happaning and they pointed to the sky westward. Out of the clouds around 15-20 round bright lights came out in a DNA formation, It really looked like a invation. We could not believe what was happenning. While looking around my
block to see if anyone was looking at this I decided to knock on my niegbors door and told him to look up and he was in awe and shock. I decide to get my video recorder and I took videos of this lights doing trick up in the sky. They would scatter and then get back together. I called the TV stations and let them know I had footage and blew me off.
Next day on the Newspaper it said they were northern Lights, my niegbor showed me the newspaper. These things were not northern lights
I have analog video and I cant down load it here. Believe me I would not take the time to type this, I have better things to do.
