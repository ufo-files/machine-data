{rjl} glowing green orb

I work the night shift and I get out around 11:30. I stopped by a Wendys in Peabody to get some dinner and as I headed towards home, I saw a green glowing orb in the sky. It seemed reasonably close...possibly tree level. It was solid and no signs of movement. I wouldn't describe it as a craft upon first glance but the way it acted didn't seem as if it were naturally occuring. A few seconds after I saw it, the object seemed to have bolted full speed towards the sky. It was there one second and gone the next. I originally thought it could possibly be a meteorite or a shooting star as I have never had the pleasure to see any event in the sky before, however the more I thought about it, the more I thought had it been one of the two as an explanation, I would imagine the light would descend rather than ascend rapidly like it did.
