I looked up, and right above me was a golden mirror like orb. I watched it for a few minutes just sitting there wondering what it was, and when it was going to move, I was sure it was a balloon or something but it hovered there, perfectly still and round, and I could see the reflection of everything below like in a convex wide angle mirror and I turned down to my wife and kids and asked them if they saw that, and they looked and said yes, as I pointed up and then when I looked back up, it was getting smaller, until it was gone directly up above. It's very hard to tell the size or distance, or speeds. There were no noises we could hear, but it was an active summer afternoon in the neighborhood with lots of noises, we had a fenced in back yard, and I asked a few of the neighbors but they didn't notice anything.
