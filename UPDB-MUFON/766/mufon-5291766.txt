Triangle shaped lights in Kuala Lumpur sky

I was standing outside my building and than looked at the sky. It was cloudy but lights appeared in the sky above my building, one orange shape form with two blue lights and one orange and aside from that form was another orange light. I took a phone call and next time I looked in the sky, about 10 minutes later, nothing was there anymore. No sound and not moving shape.
