{jpg} Glowing Orange Sphere Hovering Over Windermere Florida

At 12:25am EST on 1/21/2018 while taking my dogs out I witnessed a bright red-orange orb hovering but moving slowly North-North West towards the city of Winter Garden. I ran inside to get my smartphone and tried to get it on video - but I couldn't do it fast enough. My wife also saw it as I rushed outside with my phone. The event lasted about 30 seconds - the light started dimming slowly as the orb moved away and a few seconds later it just disappeared completely. This is not the first or only time these red-orange orbs are observed in this area.
