{cak} 10 To 15 objects Triangel shaped and circuler shaped traveling in a line 1 behind another flashing lights low rate of speed

I was in my house on 12/09/2017 in Lexington county SC. at around 7pm when I realized I had forgotten to bring in the mail. When I went outside to get the mail I heard a humming rumbling noise that made me look up, and I seen a long trail of objects. there were Triangle shaped one's that were flashing red and blue lights and in between the Triangle shaped objects there were cylinder shaped reddish colored objects flashing a red light on and off. these object's were in a line one behind the other and they were in this order, A Triangle shaped one, then 3 cylinder shaped ones, then another Triangle shaped one, and 3 cylinder shaped one's and so on for a total of around 15 objects or so, they stretched out for a couple miles form what I could tell. it was very clear night and These Objects were flying relatively low and not moving very fast so I was able to get a pretty good look at them for about 8 minutes or so . I observed them flying in a straight line until I could not see them anymore because of the trees. the objects stayed at the same altitude the whole time that I could see them , I live very close to an Airport and am very familiar with planes and helicopters flying over my house and I can tell you these were not airplanes or Helicopters, I did go on line the next day and had seen a couple other people posted that they had witnessed the same thing I had witnessed with pretty much the same detail that the objects were flying in a straight line and they had seen 10 to 15 objects flashing red and blue lights! My reaction when I seen these Objects was complete excitement I could not believe what I was looking at I was not frightened at all it was an awesome sight. since this happened I have been going outside every night at 7pm and looking up at the sky hoping that they will return.
