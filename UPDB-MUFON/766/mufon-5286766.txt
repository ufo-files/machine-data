It was shaped a little like a space shuttle or airplane.

It was about the size of a shuttle and/or airplane. It was a whitish silver, with a sheen somewhere between matte and shiny. I don't remember any windows or windshields, and no doors or hatches. It had "tail-fins" kind of like a shuttle. I was driving down the black top road to turn onto our dirt road. It was motionless at tree top level until I got right up to it, and it took off. In the blink of an eye it eas a mile down the road south of me. No noise, No exhaust, no indication it saw me, except to go away from me. I went home and got my mother and friend and we saw it, but it took off, and we never saw it again.
