{nd} Large bright yellow orange orb turned into 4 small white lights then 2 lights then 1 light. Very small white and flickering.

I was driving down by Lake Michigan when I looked over the harbor/ boat dock when I noticed a noticeably large bright yellow/orange light moving quickly towards my direction in the sky. I slowed my vehicle as I noticed the object gradually getting smaller and duller. I pulled into the nearest parking lot and watched the object change to an object with 4 small bright white lights. At this time it was stationary over the boat harbor. The lights went from 4 to 2. They were still small and white. Pulsating rhythmically at times and flickering at others. This eventually changed to 1 light with the same pulsating/flickering. Object eventually moved North and disappeared. It was always in the sky. 

I was never afraid of this object, mostly interested.
