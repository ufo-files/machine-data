2 red lights with one fixed object beneath one in Sao Paulo Brazil

My wife and I saw two red lights in the morning clear sky in Sao Paulo Brazil on April 26th 2020. One reddish light had an object at a fixed distance. The object was bright white and glowing and was vertical. The one red light disappeared and the other with the object was visible for 20 minutes and continued to ascend until out of sight. Video and still captured the one with object but did not appear the same as the naked eye.
