Black Triangle UFO shot across the sky in broad daylight.

I was taking my 5 minute work break in the afternoon when this happened. I looked up to the sky because I was told a blimp was in town. The object apeared like a dark hawk or large bird just riding the upper currents of wind. It wasnt! No flapping wings, perfect triangle in a cloudless sky. In total disbelief I reach for my phone to get video, but i couldnt take my eyes off the ufo. I havent even looked at the video yet. This triangle ufo traversed the entire sky in a matter of about 15 seconds and vanished. This defied all logical reasoning. Im still coming to terms with what I witnessed. Ill check whats on my phone for posting, but the ufo was high in the sky and I seriously doubt it will show up. This happened about 4pm in Fayetteville, AR on June 1st 2012.
