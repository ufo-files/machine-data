Sitting out at beach deck late on a cold night. What I thought I saw was a meteorite entering the atmosphere over the ocean but it didn't fizzle out and it got a lot closer to me. I remember in my mind as it slowed to a stop trying to eliminate anything else it could be but nothing added up to what I was witnessing. I had to focus to get as many details as I could. When it had stopped or I guess "hovered," the fire had two distinct flames underneath a transparent type oval shape above it. The oval shape had many bright orange lines going in several directions. The flames stayed somewhat upwards other than when it made its decent. The flames did waver some as if it were falling a bit or maybe the wind was blowing it but it did not go out. It then moved back out in another direction of the ocean into the sky until I couldn't see it any longer. It didn't go super fast when it left. It left slowly but must have picked up speed heading away from me as it was gone pretty quickly. I then woke up my husband freaking out. It's only been a few days but this seems to be consuming me.
