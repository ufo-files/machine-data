UFO FLEET OVER RED SQUARE IN MOSCOW, RUSSIA

On February 21, 2018 I returned to Moscow for a quick business trip. Nighttime, between 10:00pm and 10:15pm (local time, UTC+3), despite of the freezing cold (-14 1C, ≈7 1F), I went to the Red Square, in the very heart of the city and nearby the Kremlin, to take some pictures before my departure the following day. The objects shown in the photos were not visible at the naked eye (or at least I did not see any of them until I watched the pictures on my cell the following day). Also that night I did not notice any other passerby pointing fingers or taking shots of something unusual in the sky. In some photos there are up to 10-11 objects that "descended" from the sky and then hovered on top of the Saint Basil's Cathedral (for reference, it's highest tower is 65 m high, circa 213 ft). Being on the side of the Kremlin, all this area is a 100% no-fly zone and under tight security measures. Some objects looks like single white orbs, others have a kind of "triangle shape" (with a orb of different color at each corner). Hereby attached I send you a copy of my video-presentation (posted on my channel on March 7, 2018), with full description of the events, some details extracted from my photos, plus location, directions, and some additional comments. Also I send you the n.11 (unedited) photos taken that night with my iPhone 5 and referred to in the video-presentation.
