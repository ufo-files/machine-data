{ck} Random blinking lights changing direction Super fast then taking off

I was riding in my car leaving my boyfriends house (he was with me but only saw the tail end of the event). The reason why I noticed it was because the blinking lights were very bright and they were very random. I first saw the lights near the center of the sky blinking in a very random fashion but they were still all in a group. Then it shot across the sky very fast an started blinking randomly again then just took off into the distance within a split second. There was no sound or anything. I was practically mesmerized for a bit before I told my boyfriend to look at it. I could really formulate any sentences cause the lights were so bright I didn't wanna look away.
