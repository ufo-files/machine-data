Solid Red Light - Central Austin

Solid red light in the sky heading from North East to the South West.

It appeared from the North East over the trees and headed South West at a constant speed. It was pretty far away. I could not see a shape, just a solid red glowing light. No other lights on the craft. I watched until it went out of sight.
