{nd} Weird object over a light pole and school before a storm

Was heading to see a movie with my brother. On the way it was lightning really frequently and I wanted to capture some of the lightning on my phone. After rolling my window down and putting my phone halfway outside of the door window, I waited to see if I could get some lightning on video. After catching it on video I remained holding my phone while I continued to drive. Hoping to catch another more intense flash. After I drove about a mile I turned my phone off so I could meet my brother at the theater. Upon reviewing the video footage when I got home I noticed right before a huge flash of lightning that some fast white object flew out of the dark over a light pole and descended down a little then vanished into the dark again. Was very quick moving. I had to slow my video down to 50 Percent to even get a good look. Please help me figure out what this may be. I highly doubt the light pole caused this object to appear in the video. However if it was something to do with camera malfunction because of the light I will accept that. I am open to opinions on this one. Not sure how fast it was moving or if it may of been a bat or bird.
