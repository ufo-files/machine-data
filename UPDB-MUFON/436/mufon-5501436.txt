{dr}2 objects, about 2 minutes apart, same direction, same angle, disappeared in a cloudless sky

Around 9:00 pm I went outside to watch the 4th of July fireworks. As I was looking upward, toward the southeast, i saw a red glowing light. At first I thought it was an airplane, since we are very close to the airport, only it did not appear to have the configuration or the strobe effect of any commercial aircraft that I am familiar with. The first one traveled in an inclined path to the northwest, and disappeared into a cloudless sky. A second object appeared about two minutes later, same characteristics, same angle of incline, and disappeared off into the northwest. I have pictures, although i don't think they are very good ones. I wanted to try taking pictures of fireworks, and got a glowing red orb instead....
