{cak} Very fast low light glowing orb or oval shape.

Having a smoke very late or very early around midnight or thereafter on March 16(17th). I always look at the stars and try to catch a glimpse of a satellite or shooting star. I noticed an extremely fast object coming from the south. It's direction was due north but with rapid altitude adjustments. Before it turned North east it rose at an approximate 30 deg climb. The shape (best as I could tell) was orbish or oval. It had a VERY faint glow like a transparent blue hue. As for elevation of this thing, I would say unknown due to the fact of it's speed and unknown size. There was no sound. My best guess as to approximate location of sighting would be in between the Sierra Nevada range near Arvin,heading north to the Woody area before turning easterly. But since I have no idea of altitude or size I cannot be sure. I witnessed this object for maybe 4-5 seconds before it was out of my sight. This object was faster than anything man made.
