There were 3 ufos, starlike, circular, one separated from the other two

My husband and I were walking home from an evening event, we were looking at the constellations and planets, I happened to turn around for no apparent reason and noted 3 circular spheres in a row, I showed my husband and as we were looking at them one separated from the other two and changed the pattern, they continued to fly noiselessly toward Port Charlotte Harbor. Then they were gone from view. Afterwards there were airplanes following their normal flight path, with red/white flashing lights but that is all. The ufos were silent, and if I didn't "happen" to turn around I would not have seen them.
