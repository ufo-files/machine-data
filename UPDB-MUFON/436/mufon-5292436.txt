While taking out the garbage I noticed this object that changed colors. It was slowly moving and then stopped. I saw the vehicular image at that point

I was taking out the garbage at about 10.30 pm. I looked up in the sky and saw a white pulsating object that was very bright. I looked down for a brief second, and the object changed to a bright red...glowing. I fiddled for my camera on my phone. I looked back up and that it when I shot two pictures of what was now some sort of vehicle. I had gotten much closer. The vehicle had a thick circular shape. I did not hear any noise. It actually got much closer before I could see that it was a vehicle. It seemed to be aware of my presence. After my photos, the vehicle simply disappeared...possibly very quickly
