Saw UFO with different colored lights fly directly overhead then out of sight. I was with three other people.

I was hanging out at a friends house. There was 4 of us all together. We were standing out in the front yard on a summer night. We then all watched as a UFO flew from the north over the house, then over our heads and south out of sight. The object did not change direction, flew silently (maybe a slight hum - difficult to remember). It did not change in elevation. It hovered at a consistent speed that was fairly slow -maybe 30 mph. The lights that were on the disk were multicolored, pulsating, and sequentially moving in a circular fashion. It was difficult to perceive if the lights were spinning, lighting sequentially, or if the disk itself was rotating. Maybe all three. There may have been a dome on the disk and there may have been windows (really difficult to remember). After the disk flew out of sight, we were all in complete awe of what we just saw. We spoke about it incessantly that night and some times after but slowly the conversation faded. I could explain in more detail about the specifics of memory or lack thereof of each witness. Currently however I am the only individual who openly testifies to what I experienced, but believe all 4 of us did.
