{cak} orange obs flying towards me, silently but fast observed by over a dozen people including army navy and airforce people

My nephew and I were listening to the radio in his car after pulling up to his mothers house. We got out the car and he looked up and asked me whats that in the sky? I am prior military so i looked up and immediately i knew it was nothing ive ever seen. I pull out my phone and started recording. At first it was one orb but then there were more. They were orange red orbs one after another flying towards us right over us and turning sharp changing direction. They made no noise and were pulsating. They were too low to be a plane and had no other identifiable markings, no wings or anything. There were over 12 people watching this. I was concerned but more excites. One was a prior army pilot,one a navy weapons specialist, n myself prior air force. None of us with over 30 combined years of military experience could explain what they were except that what flew over our heads was not of this world.i called the police to confirm that nothing was going on in the area and they said nothing was going on or reported. That same night i received word these orbs were seen in washington d.c. only a few minutes later but i cannot confirm that. There has been increasing number of sightings in my area.I was afraid that my video was going to get confiscated so i uploaded it online. If you want the link please contact me. A few people have seen my video and confirmed that what i recorded they have seen several times for years with no explanation and one that admitted he was afraid to say something because of fear of being ridiculed.
