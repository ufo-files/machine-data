Observed vessel moving silently just above power lines moving northward as I was driving southward

When first observed, I thought it was airplane that was going to crash because it was so low, but as it passed by moving northerly, noted it had no wings and was dark gray appearing. As it passed, I looked in my rearview mirror, still expecting a crash and it had disappeared. No signs of any crash, no lights noted coming from it but although it was dark out, I could see the color and that it appeared to be some sort of metal type material. I was on my way to work at that time and wide awake and not under the influence of anything.
