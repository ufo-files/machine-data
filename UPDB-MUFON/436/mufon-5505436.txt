{cak} Friend saw 1st, unusual orangy light moving from south toward us.

We were driving south on 8th ave.at Almaden, Eugene Or. when 1st. object was observed. It took only moments to realize it was no known terrestrial craft.We continued west to 11th ave to get a clearer view an then noticed 2 more objects with same apparent shape and color. while the first had turned west and faded 2 more appeared from south. while observing these a 4th appeared and shortly after a 5th. they all followed the same approximate path, coming from south then slowly turning to west. they appeared roundish and were all the same approximate color. they were moving slowly, about the speed of a private plane or slower, seemed to follow a smooth path with no apparent color or other changes. As I have seen many other unknowns(and photographed many) it was a treat to observe these and wasnt frightened, nor was there any apparent subconscious communication. I tried to video and then tried to photo objects with poor results. as it turned out the battery was nearly exhausted probably affecting photo quality. All 5 after heading west faded from sight.
Took several photos, but most were squiggly. I apologize for the quality of these.
