Bright red object, thought it was mars, VERY big looking star

Just got home, saw a bright red object in the sky, It had me baffled. I stood outside and looked at it for 5 whole long minutes, trying to figure out what it was, No way it was an airplane because it had no green nav light and I picked 4 points in the sky to see if it was moving in any direction and it wasnt. I thought it was Mars that just happened to be really big tonight, it was so bright and orangish/red and it was just up in the air there so hard to miss, so I went inside to grab my android phone with google sky maps on it turned it on and pointed it in the direction I saw it from my room, and nothing was listed, so I went back outside and it was totally gone. No noise, no sound, no light path or tail on the object, I just thought it was a really big bright star. Apparently it wasnt.
