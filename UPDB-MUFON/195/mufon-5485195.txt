25 degree Red light, quarter circled, approached me , left in an angle, like a check mark.

Close to 8 years ago, at night, the moon shined full. I saw the red light on the west above trees maybe 25-28 degrees from horizon, a few moments after it quarter circled me and stopped until north keeping the same distance 500-800feet, approached me until 50-55degs and then left in an angle of NNE, like a type of check mark. Recourse after 2 minutes in a different angle more to the feeble NNE and then disappeared after 3minutes or more, maybe 5mintes. I cannot be sure about it because it was in 2008. It was before 21hours and dark out there, stars in the black sky and for sure it was near an equinox. Now it is difficult to say if it quarter circled or the red light that took a course straight from the first moments. How I felt, like normal. There can be lies in there because I did not take notes back then. The speed seem to be constant, no hurry and no sound. But going away to the north it may have picked up some speed because the red light appeared miles away. How I felt after, like nothing happened. I was happy that I finally saw a light in the sky, especially after millions have seen some. Later, I just felt like composing a tune on the banjo to remember what I saw.
