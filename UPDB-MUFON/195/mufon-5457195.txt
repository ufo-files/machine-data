{jpg} Saw 3 objects far away, seemed large, no distinct shape reflected light

Was on the train (Hudson line) toward Poughkeepsie, sometime between Croton-Harmon and Cortlandt. Looked out the window and saw 3 objects in the sky across the river from me. They were definitely not on the window, as I could no longer see them after the train rounded a bend. They did not look like clouds, as they didn't reflect the sun the same way as the other clouds. At first I thought they were trails from planes, but they stayed stationary for at lesst 15 minutes. They looked like they reflected light to me, but I'm not sure. I tried to get photographs, but they were far away and the train was moving fast. I could only get photos of 2 of them in the same picture.
