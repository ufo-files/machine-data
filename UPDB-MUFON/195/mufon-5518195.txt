Very bright star-like light makes a u-turn over my house

Between 10pm and 11pm, I had finished a walk and stopped to check the mailbox before going inside. I glanced up and saw a very bright star-like light. Its size and brightness would be similar to Venus. At first I thought it was a helicopter searchlight. The object seemed to be flying at about the same height a helicopter would and at about the same speed. However, it was close enough that I would have been able to hear a helicopter and most likely see the shape of the body. I could only see the light and there was no sound. The light was bright and -for lack of a better word- twinkling. It was not like a searchlight beam. I watched it for about 15 seconds before I lost track of it behind the trees. The path the object took (from the point I first saw it) was a smooth u-shaped arc coming from and then returning to a northerly path.
