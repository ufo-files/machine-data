While scanning the sky in the direction of the sound I witness an orange orb appear from behind the tree line immediately pursued by a full throttle low flying helicopter.The object kept a straight path until out of view as did the helicopter crossing over the horizon.I immediately knew I was seeing something incredible. My initial reaction was pure terror and crying while running home to share my story.
