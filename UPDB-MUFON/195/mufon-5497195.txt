{rjl} White helicopter hovering up high with orbs at higher altitude circling erratically, getting in formation then shooting off in a straight line

I was at the warehouse dock directing a trailer into position. There was a helicopter hovering up high and slowly backing up. Thats when I noticed these 4 orbs at a much higher altitude hovering , then slowly began to rotate in a circle. Three of my employees came out to see what I was looking at and they also saw the orbs. The truck driver also witnessed the orbs. Suddenly the orbs started to separate out and in slowly moving north. Thats when they got in the formation in the picture moving faster. Then they formed in a single file and flew over the warehouse. The flying pattern was from southeast to the north. Our dock faces south. The helicopter had no markings whatsoever and was facing towards the orbs as it slowly backed away. We are near Hobby Airport and noticed that there were no planes taking off or landing except for this one helicopter. About 15 minutes after we lost sight of the orbs, we noticed airplane traffic as usual. One of my employees has a video that he shot with his phone which I can get if requested.
