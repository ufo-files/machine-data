Very very Bright White and long object in sky with contrail headed North West. At 0607 hours a very white and bright object was noticed in the sky. This was off Lisburn Road in Cumberland County Headed North West with a HUGE contrail behind it. It looked like it was coming into our atmosphere for the contrail was over 5 miles long and at least fifty feet fanning out to over 200 feet. This lasted from 0607 hours till 0611 hours. When the contrail broke off and object separated from contrail. The object had a flat front long bodied with a flat rear. VERY Bright Yellow light showing a clear object contour. Tried to pull off the road but with headlights on and stupid signs giving off glare could not film it. At 0614 hours the contrail was so large and then faded away. There is NO WAY that this was not on radar. The object had to be at least 90 to 120 feet in length. It did look like a damned very large missile. But with a missile there would be a lift off point. This was as if coming into our atmosphere and the way the contour broke off was like turning off the hose, it just stopped.
