{cak} large triangle ship hovering over house and street

Came out to go to school in college at the time and saw everyone across the street looking up. its was around 5:00pm and there was no sound, no birds, no air movement,and kid of a orange hue in the atmosphere. I saw the people across the street looking up and they pointed up for me to see. when I looked up I saw a Huge Silver White Triangle the size of about half the city block hovering over the street and our house. It was about 200 feet right above us. This was a metal object and silver. It was very clear. It sat there for about 20 minutes... at which time I ran in the house to get a camera but couldn't find it and ran back out to keep looking. The object shot upward with no sound and left a trail of white smoke straight up. never seen anything like this in my life and I was raised around airplanes. This was some kind of ship. I've seen them after this incident three other times. Now I have pictures of the last ones.but they were different types of ships.
