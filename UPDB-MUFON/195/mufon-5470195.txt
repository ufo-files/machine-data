{rjl} silver object

We had just finished playing tennis @ 11am when one of the players looked up and said "What is that?" We observed the object for about 5-10 minutes. At first I thought it was silver balloons, but they weren't gaining altitude or being moved by wind. And then, based on height and distance from us, it seemed too big to be balloons. It reminded us of this, but we couldn't see the movement caught in this video because of distance.
