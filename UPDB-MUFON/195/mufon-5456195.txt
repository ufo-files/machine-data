{jpg} pulsating, fixed position, then dissapeared

3:00 AM on January 6, 2020 after bathroom trip, climbed back into bed, rolled over on side and was gazing out N/E eight foot bedroom window. Suddenly a very large, white, bright as the sun UFO appeared out of the dark sky. It began to pulsate in large waves of white light for less than a minute, then vanished/disappeared like you would turn off a light. The sky was dark, no aircraft. no visible bright stars. I had no visible land mark orientation, however it was higher than tree top, not as high as the White Tank Mountain tops would be. I was shocked by the sudden appearance and in awe of it's beauty and somewhat mind numbed for a few minutes after it disappeared as I tried to process the event.
