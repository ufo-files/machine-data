{td} my x-husband & I on our farm went out to see stars..saw 4 in a perfect sqare bigger than stars.then they streaked across the sky so fast no sound left light trail

We lived on our farm in central-north PA, in the 70s in an area that is so quiet that you can hear snow falling before the storm arrives. My husband at the time and I often went outside at night, in summer months to see the brilliant display of stars. We saw something that puzzled us..four lights larger than stars in a perfect square..Suddenly, they came closer together as they streaked across the sky without a sound, so fast that each light left a light trail that was in a wavey pattern. We were stunned, ran inside and called neighbors, but no one saw the lights. We called the military the next day and they said that no planes etc were in our skies that night..they showed no other interest in the event, and without computers, I did not know how to report it. It has bothered me all these years later and I remember it as though it were yesterday. I am and have been a non-drinker and do not use drugs.

I could sketch it but have no other evidence. The color was white lights. The lights did not appear to be a part of a large object.
