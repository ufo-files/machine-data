Entirely quiet. Gave off silver flash and flew.

I was walking my dog when I saw a flash in the sky. It was completely silent, so I noticed it wasn't anything man-made, such as a plane, helicopter, blimp, etc. I looked up quickly to see a star-like object give a flash of silver light so bright, I thought it was almost a miniature sun. It flew off into the clouds a second later and I couldn't find it.
