Bright Lights hovering in a Triangle Shape over Everett

I saw three very large Bright lights hovering in the sky noticeable from North Broadway in Everett. I thought they were Helicopters hovering in search of someone on the ground until two more suddenly appeared. By the time I realized these were not helicopters the Five Bright lights went on the move in an intermittent slow, then fast pace, they moved southbound.... As I watched them leave in the distance I could see that only two remained. I was in my Vehicle following them so was unable to capture a video or photo.
