Possible UFO Sighting in Massachusetts

I was sitting in the parking lot in the car outside of the grocery store, waiting for my boyfriend to come out. At first I noticed I believe 1 (maybe 2 but not 100% sure) orangeish star-like light moving pretty fast across the sky. At first I thought it was a plane but it appeared a little bit different & a lot bigger then the light of a plane but didn’t think too much of it. 

Then I was sitting there still looking and I saw 2 more of the same orange/redish big lights appear and travel across the sky. I immediately opened the window to see if I could hear anything, like a plane going by or helicopter but it was completely silent as the lights traveled across the sky. They seemed to go horizontally straight (to my right looking forward), until they disappeared or were too far for me to see anymore. I took out my phone and recorded it, and as I was texting my friends about it ... I saw 2 more AGAIN. They were traveling at the same speed only these two were a little further apart. I recorded these as well. They moved in a very consistent speed, never stopping just traveling in a straight line, staying the same size until they were gone. This was definitely weird and 100% not an average plane or shooting star. I’ve never seen something move like that before. 

Also, after doing some of my own research .. I wanted to let you guys know that I did find a couple YouTube videos that resemble my video of the lights I saw. The videos were posted either the day of or the week of my experience so not sure if they are related.
