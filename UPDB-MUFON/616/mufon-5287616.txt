Two orbs moving around erratically, performing tight turns, hovering, changing colors in and around thunder clouds

I saw two orbs moving around erratically in and around thunder clouds during a storm. It looked as if they were scanning and searching for something. They would move very tight circles and then flutter about, hover, move again. All the time changing in brightness and color. For 90% of the time they were a solid white (with perhaps a blue tinge), but every so often they would turn into a deep red for maybe 4-3 seconds and then back to white for a prolonged period. One of them also strobed bright white light, then turned to a solid deep red and descended to the ground. I lost sight of it as it passed behind trees and buildings in the distance. I observed them flying around for 3-5 minutes, which seems way too long for ball lightening? I did some googling later and it seems most ball lightening can only last for 25 seconds at most, so that leaves me wonder what the hell these things were.
