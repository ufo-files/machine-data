Stationary UFO hovering in Orange County, CA

I went outside and was waiting for a prospective roommate to show up, when I observed in the sky a large, grey, stationary object. At first I thought perhaps it was a blimp, but it was not moving what-so-ever. I could kind of see colored flashing lights around it, but it didn't seem to be on the ship, rather than permeating around it. I kept staring, wondering what the heck it was, and it stayed stationary for quite a few minutes. When the prospective roommate showed up, I went to greet him, but also wanted to show him what I saw. When I took him back to the spot where I had seen the craft, it was gone. Not a trace of it. Tripped me the frick out!!
