{jpg} Three triangular crafts flew overhead

Three triangular shaped craft flew over head. The first flew from the NE to the SW. The second two flew the exact opposite direction. The sightings were approximately 15-20 minutes apart. They made no sound. The third object seemed to be traveling at a much greater speed that the first two. The objects made no sound and from what I remember they had 4 to 5 solid lights that Glowed on the bottom of the crafts.
