A white light going round shadowed by clouds in a huge circle over a school field

Was a while ago in Manchester United Kingdom. Our back garden was just near a old school field and woods behind that, mum was having a party and I looked out my bedroom window noticed this huge white light going round and round in a circle. I showed other people at party they saw it too. After the party I decided go outside see what it is. It was still there going round and round same place but no sound clouds covering it. I stood outside a while I didn’t think that long but when I came in it was 3.20am. Next day was nothing there. About a night later these light turn up flying back and forth over my window over the house back again stopped outside my window all different directions. I rang airforce and complained. I said “ well we either being invaded by aliens or you messing around in residential areas. They “ said you have to report to police not to us?” I said “ well I’m reporting it to you” soon after they left. Next day said on radio they was Chinese lights. They was not Chinese lights doing those manoeuvres outside my window going side to side flying over house and back again. Well my ex partner was USA military so I told him about it. He said “ they sound like drones” I said funny turning up after the white light the night before.
