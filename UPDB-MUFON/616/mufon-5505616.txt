{rjl} LARGE LIGHT

AT NIGHT I LESTEN TO MY POLICE AND AVIATION SCANNER. I HAVE IT LOCKED INTO AIR TRAFFICE CONTROLL IN KANSAS CITY. ON THE NIGHT OF SATERDAY DEC 28 AT AROUND 9PM AMERICAN FLIGHT 94 OR 911 SOMITHING LIKE THAT CALLED KANSAS CITY AIR TRAFFIC STATING THAT THERE WAS A LARGE BRIGHT LIGHT AROUND 40 MILES IN FRONT OF HIM AND REQUESTED IF THERE WAS ANY AIR CRAFT ON THERE RADAR IN THE TEXAS CORRIDOR. AIR TRAFFIC STATED NEG THERE WAS NONE. AT THAT TIME THE AMERICAN FLIGHT REQUESTED TO GO FROM 12000 FT TO 15000 FT THEN THE AMERICAN FLIGHT WAS TOLD TO GO TO A DIFFRENT FREC AND ALL TALK STOPPED ON THE SCANNER?. I NEW AT THAT TIME THAT THERE WAS SOMITHING NOT RIGHT AND THEY CHANGED TO A NEW FREQ WHERE NO ONE COULD HERE THEM. ON DEC 29 AT AROUND THE SAME TIME ANOTHER AMERICAN FLIGHT CALLED KANSAS CITY AND STATED ARE YOU GUYS STILL SEEING THOES LIGHTS IN THE ARKANSA CORRIDOR CAUS THERE BACK I HAVE BRIGHT LIGHT OUT IN FRONT OF ME HE REQUESTED TO GO FROM 9000 FT TO 1200 FT AFTER THAT ALL GOT QUIT THERE WAS NO MORE TRAFFIC TALK FROM HIM OR KANSAS.  SO ALL I CAN SAY IS SOMITHING IS GONING ON OVER TEXAS AND ARKANSA AT NIGHT WITH AIRTRAFFIC. AND THERE CHANGING THERE FREQ REAL FAST SO YOU CANT LESTEN IN ON ANY MORE TALK. I GOT THE FELLING THAT THE SECOND AMERICAN FLIGHT ON DEC 29 NEW ALL ABOUT THE LIGHTS THAT WHERE SEEN ON DEC 28 CAUS THE TONE OF HIS VOICE TO AIR TRAFFIC CONTROLL WAS AS IF THIS IS GETTING TO BE COMMON??. I LESTEN TO AIR TRAFFIC CONTROLL EVER NIGHT AND SO FARE THERE HAS NOT BEEN ANY MORE CHATTER ABOUT LIGHTS .
