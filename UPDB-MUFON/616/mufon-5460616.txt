{tjd} string of >15 white individual lights at varying distances from each other

A "string" or line of more than 15 individual white lights spaced at varying distances from each other, traveling at an altitude higher than commercial airplanes, with no sound, and speed a bit faster than airliners, 65-70 degrees above the horizon. Travelled from the NW to the NE sky in a constant altitude and path. gradually fading from sight in the distance. The leading point of the lights had a higher concentration in number of lights, with the tail end having four or five which were separated with greater distance. The length of time to travel across our sight was approximately 2 minutes. The position and line was horizontal and 10 degrees below the Little Dipper. In appearance, it seemed like a plane dragging a string of lights, but the length of the string is far greater than any airport runway could support. Also the lights were not grouped in any way that a "string of lights" might be placed. we attempted to take photos with the cell phone, however this failed. The altitude and speed seemed to be similar to that of the International Space Station, as we have seen this many times in the past.
