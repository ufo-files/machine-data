Strange moving light that circled backwards when noticed.

When driving on highway 55 at the intersection of #55 and #106 stopped for a break and to stretch. As we were standing there noticed a bright light moving across the sky. Thought it was a satellite at first. As we wathed it moved in an irregular manner, side to side, fast and slow. It stopped and remained motionless for a few seconds and then would continue on. We began to watch closely as it continued to do this a few more times. It then stopped for a longer period of time and then circled back towards us and appeared to be now moving to us. We became anxious and uneasy as it moved closer and got in the vehicle and drove away. This is a remote strech of highway along the edge of the boreal forest with many small communities along the way.
