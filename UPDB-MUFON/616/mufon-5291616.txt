Saw bright light in west sky.

I was woke up around 4:40 am October 3,2020. I looked outside & something was really bright so went outside & saw this bright object in the west sky right above the trees. So I started taking pictures. The light were not shining down on me but the object was really bright. I could see something at the right side on the bottom of the object. It was silver & looked like a old pickup grill. It look like it was going away so I started video. It changed to a circle & zoomed away. The videos aren’t very long but one you see bright circle & the next video you can see it zoom off.
