{cak} blue sparkling light resembling aircraft descends and

Blue sparkling light of size resembling typical aircraft viewed descending like "shooting star" from south side of freeway toward center of freeway while witness traveling eastbound on 202 just east of 101 overpass around 9:30 PM March 19, 2020 in Chandler, AZ (near Phoenix). Light appears to be no more than 1-2 miles away. Third such sighting by same witness in the area over past 6-12 months. Area south of 202 (where light originated) is mostly Indian reservation/open desert. Other two sightings also occurred on south side of 202 in Chandler near 101 overpass. All sightings occurred in early evening or later at night. Prior two sightings were of white/gold sparkling lights, also descending like shooting stars, but those did not appear to cross freeway (stayed on reservation area). Those lights also seemed to "disappear into thin air." Actual crafts were not apparent/visible. Did not appear to be typical planes because lights concealed entire craft (if any) and were not blinking. Military bases in vicinity.
