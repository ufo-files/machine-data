Rapid light streak across sky that changed direction

It was a cloudy night in the suburbs of Tokyo (In the Nerima City Ward - roughly near Kami Itabashi Station). From my apartment balcony I saw an orangey streak of light suddenly appear below the clouds. it roughly turned 90 degrees in an arc and disappeared. the light appeared to accelerate at high speed as it moved. the whole event was over within a couple of seconds.
