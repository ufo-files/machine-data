{ck} Starlight, red, circling eachother

At approx 10pm on July 4th I was outside viewing the fireworks when I saw what I thought was a glowing unexploded firework, but then it wasnt going away. It finally (about 20 seconds later) dissapeared behind the clouds. It traveled slowly across the sky, shortly followed by another red starlike light in the same direction and speed. I observed this for 3 more lights before I witnessed two coming into view at the same time, circling eachother for about 20 seconds before they also dissapeared. These were the only lights I viewed circling eachother, but in all I counted a total of 20 lights. I finally left our deck and came inside. After the 17th light I made my husband come outside to make sure I wasnt seeing things. He also saw them. They were not stopping when I came inside. Im not sure how many came by before I noticed them either. I am very freaked out right now. I know for a fact that they were not fireworks. I also know that they were not conventional airplanes, as 3 planes flew by when they were also going by. these planes went right by these "lights", they looked completely different, also I was able to hear the airplanes and when these lights went by there was no sound.
