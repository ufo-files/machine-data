Lights in sky

Lights in sky observed by my brother and myself 12 dec 2019 7:37 am looking north down rob avenue, in the sky, over the forth or even further over Dunfermline there was a bunch of lights scattered but moving west, flickering but also moving independently, not falling like a crash, certain points of light would dart down in a curbing fashion then appear further up, 6-9 points of light, some slightly bigger or brighter than others, looked more plane like from far away rather than star like but definitely not planes, they don’t move that way or so close together, if they were that bright close together they would not see each other and would crash, I’m very much into ufology but not so much as too make up a crappy story. My brother also witnessed it. We only managed to see it for about a minute in total but that was enough. I got footage but bad cam and street light in between.
