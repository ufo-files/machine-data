{dr}UFO appeared had unusual flight pattern then disappeared

I was standing outside my in-laws fence smoking a cigarette. I was looking at the stars just thinking when it just appeared at about the 10 o'clock position. At first I thought it was a plane as it continued straight, once it started to weave until about the 1 o'clock position then continued straight then shot off and the 2 o'clock position I was in awe trying to figure it out. The crafts altitude seemed to be higher than commercial flights but was lower that satellites. From where I was standing it was super bright and I couldn't tell the body of the craft but the light changed from orb to cylinder (like you flatten a balloon halfway). After it disappeared I went inside and told my wife because she asked what was wrong because I apparently looked "nervous"
