{dr}Three Orange lights to the North

We were just arriving home when we spotted two, bright Orange objects to the North of us. They were moving East slowly. We stopped the car and got out and tried to get some pictures. The Object that Eastern most began to flicker then blinked out then the one behind it did the same. We got back in the car and saw another one very low on the horizon. It was also moving in a West to East fashion. We got video of this one.
