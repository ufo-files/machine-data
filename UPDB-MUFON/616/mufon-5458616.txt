{tjd} Observed stationary object from ground level. Approximately 20,000+ feet elevation. Red,white lights bright to dim.

I observed a stationary object with red and white lights, very bright, which was high in the night sky, while I was facing somewhere between 228°-232°. My attention was drawn because the object seemed to be in the approach path for Indianapolis international airport, but did not move. Over time it seemed to dim it's light, disappear from view, the become bright again. This went on at least 30+ minutes. I interpreted the bright to dim to barely visible as possibly turning very slowly, with the brightly lit side turning away then reappearing after each full revolution. Eventually, the light dimmed and never returned to view. The object did seem to shoot quickly to one side and then immediate return to its original location, but it was difficult to tell if it really moved or if it dimmed and I was seeing a reflection off of a small, high altitude cloud.
