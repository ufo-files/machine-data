{nd} silver sphere that hovered then shot straight up on hwy 35 New Bransful Texas

I was making a delivery to San Antonio, Texas on Saturday, June 23, 2012 at approximately 4:40 pm. Traveling on Highway 35 near exit 175 marker when I noticed a silver like cone to my left. I didn't think anything of it so I rolled down the window assuming it was glare from the window. When I did I noticed it was still there. It was around 150 feet from the ground and wasnt moving. The color was like that of a milk truck, silver like. I watched it for about 5 minutes. It lowered a bit then shot straight up in the air. 

This was a scary feeling as I am a skeptic and I immediately called my wife to tell her what I had seen. I was confused because my brain couldnt register what I had seen.
