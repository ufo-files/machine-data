Not a Satellite!

Went out back on deck to look at the stars, looked southeast and noticed what looked like a normal satellite moving toward my direction. As I watched, it started to move faster and faster and when it should be directly over head, I just didn’t see it anymore.
I’ve seen Hundreds of satellites before, this one Started out at the normal slow speed from my perspective but then accelerated extremely fast. Then I lost sight of it. 
The whole thing only lasted 15 to 20 seconds.
