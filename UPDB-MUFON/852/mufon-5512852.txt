{nd} 7- 10 streaking objects from North horizon to South horizon.

In my front yard, taking dog out for a leak. 
7-10 streaking objects from the North. First appeared as if it were fireworks, but continued across the sky to directly overhead and on past 
the South horizon. Whole event took about 7 seconds to streak across the sky. No sound was detected. No smoke/con trails were left behind. Fire trails appeared behind the object (could be burning). If I held my hand an arms length from my eyes, the trail was about the width of my hand. Faster than commercial aircraft. Could be meteors? But path spanned from horizon to horizon. All appeared the same size through out the sighting.
Objects streaked out of sight beyond trees toward the south.
