{cak} orb of light flying casually. observed me though a beam or light.

I was at my grandma's house in Santa Rosa, CA. It was getting late, "around 12pm at night or so" and I was watching TV. I decided to go to bed so I walked back to my room. I turned on the lights and walked to the window to close the blinds. This is when I observed a flying orb like object. It was flying casually above houses just out side the court. I didn't know what to make of this of course and I rubbed my eyes and cheked to see if it was just a reflection in the window or some sort of helicopter. None of that appeared to be so. It was then I realized this was very real whatever it was. It was maybe a minute or less before the orb flew closer to my house maybe within 50ft. It must have seen me because a beam or light focused on my window and then fear set in immediately. The lights turned off in the room and an old radio from the 60s turned on at almost full volume. At this point I was beyond scared and wanted to run. I was yelling to myself in my mind to run but couldn't move. Something in me finally snapped and I took off running to the living room. I was shaking and hiding under a table for a very long time... By the time I got the courage to look out a window again the orb was gone. My room was back to normal when I went back... This happened around when I was 12 to 14. This was a very real experience to me that had been unexplained ever since... Was this orb man made? Was this an alient craft? Why did the lights turn out and radio turn on? Was it a threatening event? Or just a curious UFO? I have forever remembered this experience. I'm curious if anyone has experienced something similar.
