Fireball seen over Oceana Naval Air Station.

On Monday, 10/11/2021 at roughly 11:25pm, I was sitting in my living room when I saw a fireball streak across the sky from my living room window. I first saw the object coming from the area of Oceana Naval Air Station. It flew south below the cloud line at a very high rate of speed. There was no sound coming from the object that I noticed. There were no lights on in my home at the time, so it was not a reflection off of the window.
