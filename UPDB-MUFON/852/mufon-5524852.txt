my wife and i were filming the moon with our new camera when we saw this.

my wife and i were visiting our grandkids down the street. i had bought a new camcorder and i placed it on a tripod to get steady film of the moon. i wanted to see how much the camera would zoom in on the moon. we noticed the light ascending towards the moon. it reached its highest point then looked like it was falling towards the earth. noe of this could be seen with the naked eye.
