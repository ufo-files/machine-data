{cak} Ridged, spherical object

While traveling eastbound on I-90, I observed a white spherical object with some geometric raised patterns that appeared to be moving to the northeast. I was driving, so was only able to look at it safely for approximately 3-seconds before turning my attention back to the road. I immediately looked at the clock on my dash (11:13am) and looked for a geographical landmark which was the rest area on the western travel lane of I-90 (Framingham rest area). 

When I reached home, I went to google maps to try to estimate where the object was in relation to my estimated position on I-90 when I saw it. My best guess is in the Wayland Mass area of Heard Lake. There appears to be a number of golf courses in that general area. It is possible that others may have seen it.
