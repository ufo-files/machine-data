{rjl} At park, owl flew in front of car, landed 3 feet away. Took 4 photos. Found glowing rings when photos were printed.

After a day at a conference was over, my husband and I went to a nearby disk golf course to play a round before dark so I could unwind from the day. The park is Reed Canal Park As we were leaving this owl flew in front of our vehicle and sat staring at us. I took 4 photos of the owl. All the photos were taken within a minute. I took them using my I phone S5. The photos I forwarded should have the metadata attached. The flash was turned off at the time; I keep the flash off since it uses more battery, and I was in very close range to the owl. The gate post was only about 2-3 feet from the vehicle when we stopped.

Upon printing photos from the trip I noticed something unusual: strange double gold circles to the center right of the photos.

The owl did hold special meaning for me at the time this occurred, as did several other 11events11 during the conference.
