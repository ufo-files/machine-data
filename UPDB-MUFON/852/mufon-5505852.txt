{nd} v shaped craft flew right over me and my friends

I was in my early teens and my friends and I were playing ball. a low flying v shaped craft flew tree top high right over us. its never been a thing until the last maybe 10 years that the memories have bothered me. I just thought you should know there is something there! I never understood why we never said anything , and Im not sure there wasnt more. I just know what I saw.
