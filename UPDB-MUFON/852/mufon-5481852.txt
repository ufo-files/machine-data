{dr}Out for a drive, I noticed 3 light moving slowly over a field.

I was out driving around the country listening to music, just killing time before bed. I was starting to head back home into the city, heading south on flaugh rd off of leesburg rd, when I noticed 2 strange lights hovering above a corn field. I pulled to the side of the road as the object turned to its left and began to head west. As it turned, another light appeared as I was now to the rear of the object. The 3 lights formed a triangle and although it was too dark to make out a surface, I believe that it was one solid object since the 3rd light had been hidden before it made the turn. I turned down Arcola road and tried to keep up with the object but the tall tree line between fields was only allowing me glimpses. It was speeding up and was soon several miles ahead of me but it appeared to be moving to a higher altitude as well. It then turned south and went another mile or so before heading back east and that's when I finally lost it in the trees between yellow river road and bass road. I drove down bass to try and find it but it was gone.
