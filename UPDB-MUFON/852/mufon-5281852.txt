Squished sphere

We pulled into the parking lot to park on Broad street as a family to go to the Panther Pub. My 11 year old saw this silver sphere in the west/ northwest. It was small as it was far away . It took a second for me to see it because I thought she was pointing to a contrail. It was highly reflective so I thought it was a Mylar balloon. I then realized it was going into a strong wind and it didn’t twist, turn, and flip in the wind like a balloon. I took photos and videos. It was hard to track on my phone and look in the sky because it was bright out. I took a screenshot of the flight radar app that showed no planes. We then went inside to eat.
