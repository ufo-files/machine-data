{dr}I took photos with my camera of clouds and found the lights and outline later.

I was driving from Rolla, MO to Columbia, MO. I forgot what road/highway I was on at the time. When I looked at the photos later I noticed lights in the upper-right hand corner of one of them. I then looked at the rest and found two other photos with the lights in the same location. One of the photos was taken more than a minute after the first. The cloud positions had shifted but the lights still seemed the same distance away and in the same formation as the the photo taken a minute earlier. I put an overlay of the last photo (taken at 12:36pm) in Photoshop over the first (taken at 12:35pm) to measure the distance between the lights on the outline of the object. It seemed higher than the clouds in the photo. Also, during the ride, I blacked out and came to heading into oncoming traffic. I straightened the car back out, called a friend, and had him talk to me while I drove the rest of the trip. I did not witness the event beyond being there while I took the photos. I noticed the object in the sky while viewing the photos. When I first noticed the object in the photos I thought it was the flash on my camera. I tried to recreate the flash in photos and examined the shapes of the flash bulb casing (Kodak EasyShare M530 is the model number) but couldn't explain the lights and colored light down and to the left of them. You can see the lights in the-upper-right hand corner of the photos, which are digital copies of the original, with only date metadata changed (last opened, altered or what not), not image data as far as I'm aware.
