{dr}Observed what appeared to be a satellite traveling across the sky make a 90 degree turn and fly out of the earth's atmosphere in a matter of approx 5 seconds.

Observed what appeared to be a satellite traveling across the sky and when it reached the top of the night sky it accelerated in a 90 degree turn and flew off at an extremely high speed that took approx 3-5 seconds to clear the atmosphere out of sight. Middle of the Atlantic Ocean. I have never heard of another observation similar to this one.
