{rjl} Saw 4 orange lights in a V patter

A coworker and myself were behind our motel. I noticed 2 orange lights in the sky. Within seconds 2 more orange lights appears. They were in the shape of a V. They appeared to be flying directly away from us. The original diameter was twice that of a bright star. As they moved away it appeared they got smaller. The two on the right disappeared first, then the ones on the left did. There was cloud cover and I was not able to see any other stars. I did take a picture with my cellphone but I think I jerked when I took it as it appears there are two objects in it with a line connecting them.
