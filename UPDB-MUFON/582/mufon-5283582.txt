We both saw what we thought was a very bright star but it turned out to be a disc when we enlarged a photo of it

My neighbor and I had been walking our dogs together and as we stopped in front of my driveway, he decided to take a photo of my next-door neighbor's Christmas decorations.

As he was taking the photo, we noticed a very bright star in the sky above us. I said: "Wow, do you think that's Venus?" and he said "Maybe."

The photo he took was beautiful, so I asked him to text it to me. When I received it, I enlarged it and discovered that the star was actually a disc fully lit on the bottom encircled by a gray, metal-looking rim.
