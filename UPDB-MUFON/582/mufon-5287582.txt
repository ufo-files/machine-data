Stereotypical disc-shaped UFO flying past at high speed

As I was leaving work, I looked up in time to see a stereotypical, disc-shaped UFO fly past along Davis Drive in Morrisville, NC. I could not identify the object, but I know it wasn't a bird or plane as I see them every day (very close to an airport, RDU) and it was flying very fast at a low altitude, which would be atypical activity for a plane, bird, or drone. It flew in a straight line, very smoothly, like a cursor on a computer screen. My initial reaction was awe, followed by disbelief and uncertainty. I lost sight of the object as it passed the edge of the building. I tried to follow and see more of the object as I walked around the corner, but it was nowhere to be seen.
