{ck} reddish orange pulsing circle that went very slowly across the sky near downtown

I was on the way to Nashvilles downtown fireworks celebration. It was about an hour before the event. Were near the city at this point, so few stars are visible and only a few clouds. I look up and see a small pulsing orange light moving very slowly across the sky. It appeared to be fairly high. I look for running lights (like an airplane has) and couldnt see any. I watched as it moved very slowly across the sky and finally disappeared behind some taller buildings near the horizon. It was an odd shape, nearly completely round, which is strange for an aircraft of any kind. It was also moving very slowly for an aircraft, especially as I was facing West and the object was moving on a north/south path above my head. The light pulsed and had an irregular border. There were so many other noises I couldnt hear if any plane sounds were coming from the object. But, when I saw the descriptions here, I was shocked, because I saw the same object. Drone? Test craft? Im not sure. No one else looked up because they were busy setting up for the fireworks, attending to blankets and things.
