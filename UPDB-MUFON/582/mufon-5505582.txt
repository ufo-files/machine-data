{dr}Lights over Fort Myers

I was up on the roof of our rental condo with my telescope looking at Jupiter around 9pm with my brother. It was then when he pointed out a group of 5 or 6 objects that seemed to give off red/orange/green lights. The objects would change direction quickly and ascended very slowly. The lights would pulsate for a bit and then fizzled out completely. When we first saw them, I called down for my father because he is a pilot. I figured that he would have an explanation, but he could not come up with one. We attempted to video the lights, but by the time my dad got his camcorder, the lights were almost gone. My mother also witnessed the lights, and could not explain the lights either. She is the biggest skeptic that I know.
