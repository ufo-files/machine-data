The witness observed a vapor-like ring around the sun.

Walking home from grocery store noticed a chemtrail circle around the sun. Thought it interesting so took pictures with cell phone camera. The sun was so bright I couldn't tell by looking at my phone if I had pictures of the chemtrail or not until I got home a few minutes later. I took three pictures. In picture one the chemtrail is plainly visible around the sun as well as a plane making a trail. I'm not sure if the big white round flash is anything but there does appear to be something white barely visible behind the power line. In picture two the white object is clearly seen straight above the tree. In picture three the white object is now on the outside of the chemtrail and off to the left of the tree (the tree is in the bottom right of the photo). Picture three still has the plane visible. The picture I labeled "alternate" was taken by someone unknown to me. I saw the picture on a local facebook site. Apparently the person was also taking a picture of the chemtrail and had no interest in the white object. I posted a response to their picture but no one answered. I made two enlargements of the object.
