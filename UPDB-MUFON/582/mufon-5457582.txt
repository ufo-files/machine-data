{cak} triangle shape craft no sound at all clear sky

On park farm with my mate when i noticed a yellow pulsating light coming from the direction of peterborough, thought at first we thought could be police helicopter as we see and hear it regular above the estate, but when it got to just near us it was noiseless and the under surface was completely yellow which showed clearly it was triangle shaped craft, it was going very slowly and was no more than 500ft above us so it was visually prominent. I got my camera from my phone and took three pictures which are very strange although i was taking them as it passed by there are strange streaks of god knows what on the pictures.
both of us were really shaken up it was so strange and it was completely prominant no little light away in the distance it was low and very slow moving it was very large, we have no explanation at all of what it could be but we both agreed never seen anything like it no wings,fuselage or rotars perfectly triangular.
