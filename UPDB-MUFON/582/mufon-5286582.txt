Bright White flashing lights moved like fireflies

My wife spotted 8-10 bright flashing lights in the low sky to the southwest over the Gulf of Mexico in Destin, Florida while walking the dogs at about 8:45 pm on 07/08/21. 
She ran to get me, We both returned to the area where there was an unobstructed view where she saw them. We saw 8 to 10 stationary flashing lights in the distance in the sky several degrees above the horizon. 
These lights would flash regularly for 5 to 10 seconds & go out. They would return perhaps 5 seconds later either beside where they were to the left,right,above or below the former location. 
All of the lights were remaining in the same area from the north to south flashing on & shortly off & turning on in a close location to the left. Right. Diagonally, or up or down from where they were immediately before. They were about 20° above the horizon.
They were twinkling on & off regularly in brilliant white. The lights weren't in a straight line & individual locations were andomly moving around with no particular pattern. It was reminiscent of firefly behavior, though it was obvious that they weren't insects. 
After about 8;to 10 minutes, the lights would randomly turn off & disappear one by one until none remained. 
There were no visible normal aircraft in the area and no normal colored flashing aircraft beacon lights visible in the area during the sighting.
Nor was there any sound in the sky whatsoever. 
I am writing this report about 1 3/4 hours after the sighting took place. 
I am a former radio news reporter & now a retired attorney . I am making this report of what my wife & I both saw. 
We did not have phones with us & took no photos or videos.
