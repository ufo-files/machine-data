In South Amboy, NJ around 1980 -1982, I was with 2 friends at a park. We saw this floating craft in the distance that had us all believing we were seeing a UFO with lights spinning around the bottom like a propulsion system. We were totally convinced we were seeing a UFO. As the thing slowly got nearer and more above us we were shocked to see that it was nothing but a large blimp with advertising marquis of lights/letters flowing along the bottom like the marquis or news things you see along times square. Every time I see the reports of the Hudson Valley, NY and those areas like NJ on Ancient Aliens or the UFO shows on tv, I think of this incident/"encounter". I don't know if it's possible a this stage (many years later) to confirm the blimp usage in those areas but there was no doubt after the blimp got closer and more above that we were totally fooled. 
I do believe there must be intelligent life out in this enormous universe and hopefully I'm still alive when/if we do find out.
Mufon is a great organization . Keep it going..
