spinning bright object in sky

Louisville, Kentucky. April 16th at @ 7 pm. My friend and I were at an outdoor patio at a local bar meeting for drinks.. We had just sat down to order and I looked up at a clear blue sky to see an object that appeared to be VERY far away but extremely bright.. I alerted my friend to look at the moment that I could not recognize what object was. It seemed to spin showing bright white to silver and almost seemed diamond shaped. Going in a NE to SW direction. Object then moved backwards toward NE and then it seemed to move further up.. at this point we noticed bright Orange-red color, as if it were on fire.. Then object went in North direction up and gone. Disappeared. Lasted all of 15 minutes. We did not alert any other people.. we were in shock. No noise or sounds at all.
