So one night two summers ago. A friend and I were messing around in his car on some gravel roads since we were bored. As were going along this gravel road we passes a creek, and I happen to look out of my window and notice this white orb like object. I tell my friend to stop the car. We both look out at this object where we notice that it also has slowed down. That's when it when it changed color from white to yellow and started to come our direction fast. My friend gunned it. We ended up doing 120mph down this sketchy gravel road. We didn't see what happened to the light. We just went back to town. Ever since then I've been back to that area twice. I feel like i'm being watched, and I don't like it.
