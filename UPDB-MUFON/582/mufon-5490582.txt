{nd} Three different groups of round reddish orange objects with fuzzy edges moving silently under clear night skies.

I was getting out of my car on August 4,2015 at 9:51 PM EST and looked up and saw reddish orange objects coming toward me from the West headed East, I hurried to turn the my cell phone camera on and snapped a picture. As I walked to the other side of my car to let my dogs out, I noticed another group coming,again from the West and headed East,I ran up to the steps to the front door of my house and knocked on the door to get my husband outside and immediately ran back down and snapped another picture. They appeared to be in a some sort of formation. As we watched in total amazement,we saw another group coming from the West,headed East and I snapped my last picture. Before we knew it they were out of our sight.I was so in awe that I didn't think to count the objects as they passed silently under the clear night skies. There are also utility lines in the pictures that could not be avoided. This all happened in about a minute,based on the time stamp on the pictures. After looking at each of the pictures,three in total,there were a total of 11 objects. There were three in the first group,five in the second group and three in the third group. The second of the three pictures, in my opinion, is the most accurate. In the other two pictures they appear to be distorted with tails and that definitely was not what we observed. Also,when I enlarge the pictures the objects appear to be more oval than round,which,once again,is definitely not what we observed. I do regret that I didn't think to turn on the video on my cell phone. The photos are in the order that they were taken.
