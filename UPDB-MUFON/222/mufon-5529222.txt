small white reddish ball appeared on the horizon made a very large arc then disapeared in front of the big dipper.

Seattle, WA. My friend and I were on my front porch smoking a cigarette, looking at the planes flying over head, when I noticed a light that looked a lot like a satellite coming over the horizon. I thought it was weird because most of the time you can not see satellite's in Seattle. it had a white/reddish hue to it. It was moving quite fast, and instead of making a straight line like a satellite does, it made more of an arc towards The Big Dipper constellation. As it got to the middle portion of the Big dipper, it faded away. It was not a instant disappearance, there was a 2-3 second period where it had faded to about half of its original luminosity, then it slowly faded away. My friend and I were in awe, after it was gone.
