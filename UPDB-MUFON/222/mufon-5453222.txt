Traveling in a straight line

All the objects where traveling in tandem coming from the SW heading SE. About every ten objects where in pairs circling each other. We counted about 55 and the last 2 where changing places like they where racing each other.
We have been watching the skies for over 40 years and know the difference between meteors, satellites, and high fling jets. While this was happening there where at least 4 meteors and 2 satellites went past overhead. 
The objects where in view about 10 minutes. We have never seen anything like this before.
When I tried to video them my camera malfunctioned. After they had past it started working.
