{jpg} Bright light in sky up near the stars. About 6x larger and brighter than stars. Was moving way faster than a jet airplane. Disappeared over cloud cover.

On Wednesday June 24th at approx 11:15 pm I was outside of my home stargazing. I noticed something much bigger and brighter than the surrounding stars. It was moving north at a speed much faster than any jet plane I have ever seen. It then disappeared above the clouds and I then saw another bright light come out of the clouds moving at a slower speed then the first. The 2nd one had a light that was blinking on and off underneath. It looked like a panel of lights underneath. When the light blinked off, it was completely dark, no other lights observed.  I watched this one for about 8 secs before it also disappeared into the clouds. After about 10 mins later I noticed what looked like a military jet in the same vicinity as where the first light was observed but heading south. The next night (June 25th) I went outside again to observe the sky at approx. 2:15 am. Within 15 mins I saw the same blinking light heading west. I followed it for about 10 secs until it disappeared into the clouds. I know what airplanes in the night sky look like and this was not an airplane. Airplanes also have red/green flashing lights. This had a bright flashing panel of white light underneath it and it moved along with tremendous speed.
