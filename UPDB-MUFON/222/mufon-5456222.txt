{tjd} I noticed Four large very bright lights blinking alternating 1 and 3 then 2 and 4 repeatedly. It was hovering over large parking lot for school bus

As I approached the beginning of Rt 10 from the Providence Rt 6 connector, I spotted 4 large very bright lights alternating 1 and 3 then 2 and 4. It was hovering over a large school bus parking lot with an electrical grid at one end. Around the lot were football field type lights shining a little less brighter than the saucer craft. I slowed down to 20mph, went down the ramp while keeping my eyes on the object. When the craft lights were dimmed as the tinted glass of my windshield lined up with them, I finally saw the saucer shape with a smaller dome on top. It was hovering about 10 to 20 feet above the field lights. I was completely stopped just after the ramp and continued looking at it for a few minutes until I became afraid that they may become aware of me. At that point I slowly drove away. This bus parking lot was 3 lanes of highway and 2 lanes of railroad tracks away from me. I had a good long look at it close up.
