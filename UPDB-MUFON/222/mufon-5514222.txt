{cak} Seven glowing orange-red lights in a straight line

My wife was driving in a car with a friend and they both saw seven glowing orange-red objects, evenly spaced, moving slowly in a straight line, following each other in an easterly direction. As they came closer they began to blink out one at a time. She indicated that the lights were constant- no flickering or flashing.

She called me on a cell phone and asked me ifI could see them was well, as we live close to where she was driving. I went outside and looked towards the north and saw two orange-red pinpoints low in the sky, just above the horizon through some trees.

The objects were a saturated orange-red, similar to the color of a neon bulb.

My view of them was partially obscured by the trees but when visible they seemed bright and steady to me as well. As they drifted east I finally lost them in the trees. They were visible to me for approximately 10-15 seconds.
