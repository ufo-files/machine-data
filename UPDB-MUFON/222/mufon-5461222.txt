{rjl} Translucent sphere that meted a beam of light

Translucent sphere that meted a beam of light how's end came of slowly as tho it was controlled by, say a volume control. Slowly coming out from the sphere, extending onto the beam touched the ground. Them the end slowly began getting larger and larger until it was larger than the sphere itself. So now what was a straight beam looked more like a cone. Then the end of the cone shaped beam started becoming shorter, causing it to lift from the ground. I mean the beam had an end to it. And it was a sharp end. Or should I say, abrupt. It continued to slowly shorten, approaching the sphere until it came back, then going over the sphere like a lasso. After passing the sphere entirely it all faded away until it was gone. We, myself, my wife, her aunt and uncle watched this for about 17 minutes. I had time to go into the house to look for film, but I had none. Many people in Tucson should have seen it but we never heard of any reports.
