{cak} review of photograph taken of setting moon revealed an object; when enlarged object unidentifiable.

I frequently take pictures of the desert skies. I had taken several at the beginning of December and was reviewing some with the moon setting when I saw a small object in the sky in the photo. When I enlarged the object I could see the object was not a star and does not appear to be an airplane but is clearly three-dimensional. I was not aware of the objects presence when I took the picture of the setting moon. It has a very odd shape that I have never seen before.
