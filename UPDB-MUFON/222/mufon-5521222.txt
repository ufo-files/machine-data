Object moved west to east much slower than metoer, much faster than jet

On my way to work at 6:35 am I saw a bright light through my windshield. At first I thought it was a reflection of headlights behind me, but I had just checked my mirror and nothing was there. I instantly looked at the light and followed it with my eyes for approx. 2 seconds. I was still driving, so I checked the road ahead of me and looked back but the object was gone. The sun had not come up yet, but it was bright enough for me to see a few small clouds and contrails from commercial aircraft. This object left no visible contrail. I am 44 years old and have seen many shooting stars, jets, planes, etc. This object moved much slower than a shooting star but much faster than a jet. It appeared to be at an altitude similar to small aircraft. It moved west to east. This happened between Hastings and Elkton FL. on Friday Jan 20, 2012. I have always been a sky watcher, but I have never seen anything I couldnt explain until now. Hopefully this will back another persons observation.
