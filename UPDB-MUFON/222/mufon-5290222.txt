Yellow solid light ascending from foothills straight up then blinked out high altitude

I was parked at San Bernardino airport facing North at 3:00am. Suddenly I saw a yellow ball of light pop up from the distant foothills and begin climbing and coming towards the airport. I didn’t hear any air traffic over the tower radio. The ball of light continued to climb at a steady medium speed. I broke out my binoculars and could not see any other lights other than a yellow ball of light about the size of a landing light at the beginning but the light did not change other than get smaller as it rose higher. When it was about the size of a pea it suddenly blinked out. I saw this through my binoculars. No other lights were seen on the craft if it were an airplane the required blue red blinking lights would’ve been remaining, but there was nothing. It was very unusual. There are no landing pads or airports to the north especially in the foothills. The area it popped up from was near the San Manuel Indian Casino. No sound was heard.
