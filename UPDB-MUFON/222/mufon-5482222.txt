After that I pretty much brushed it off...until April. I was watching ufo youtube videos and it reminded of my videos. So I decided to do a frequent snapshot between both videos. After that I did zoom ins and contrast and lighting edits, and could not believe what I think I captured. The one in the front, you can see his eyes and the top of his head. The one in the rear, you can see his whole face. The other object was even weirder. Is was significantly bigger and seemed transparent in the snapshot. You could see one being sitting in the center in some sort of chair on a "laptop". You can also see computer screens mounted on the wall and floor. A door can also be seen. Listen, I don't know if this is some elaborate prank or what. I only know what I saw.
