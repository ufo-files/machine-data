On my way home the other night I saw a UFO in the sky, and stopped along the highway to film it:

Driving home from filming the sunset, I saw trianglular shaped object with orange lights. It was about 5:45. I first thought it was a UFO, because Id never seen anything like that in the sky before. As I started filming, the lights faded in and out, and also one on the right moved south. All three lights faded out, then faded back in, then, as I said, the one on the right moved south. Then, they all faded out. I was not scared, but rather intrigued. I still don't know if its military, passenger jets, or alien. 

I actually never lost sight of the object, as they started to fade out. But it was cold, and I was on the highway and didn't want the cops to give me a ticket for standing on the side of the busy highway.
