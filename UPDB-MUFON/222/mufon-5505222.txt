{rjl} bright golden light similar to a satillite but moved very fast and jerky

So at about 12am tonight I was outside with my dogs and was star gazing, as I always do because the sky is so clear and beautiful where I live. I thought at first it was a star, but then noticed that it was an odd golden light color. It appeared to be quite far away. I was staring at it because it was so bright, and I was wondering if it was a satillite. However, as I was looking, it began to move in a back and forth jerky motion, very fast. It covered a lot of ground as I watched it, and also went in several directions. It did not seem like it had any particular destination, it was just randomly moving. It was definitely not a satillite, as they are slower and not so..., jerky. I am curious, I thought it was interesting. My husband and my brother are avid aviation fans, and they did not know what it was, so I hope someone could fulfill my curiosity!
