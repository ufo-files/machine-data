Unexplainable stationary lights at distance. A sudden “orb” experience follows.

My daughter (7 years) and I (52 years/Father) had a strange experience with distant,,yet visually extraordinary round pulsating lights in the sky. We saw two of these lights towards the south at about 45 degrees first as we walked up a hill to our home. We stopped at the top of the hill and were absolutely stunned by the size and colors of a huge round light towards the NE. We could observe it well as it ws visible at about tree top level. Shortly after recording the extraordinary light towards the NE I panned 180 degrees to record the first 2 we had spotted just minuted prior when something (orb? I have no actual explanation for what there were/are) darted into our sight (recording) from seemingly thin-air. It darted left and right while dropping in altitude before there were flashes of light and it zoomed straight up and vanished. Two more “orbs” then enter our sight with one literally zooming by at what seemed to be merely just feet away. It was extraordinary and completely unexplainable by us. The “orbs” were not insects or bats or birds. They made absolutely no sounds and moved at unknown tremendous speed. Thanks.
