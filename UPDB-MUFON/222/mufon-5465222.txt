{tjd} Orb projecting light

I was at Spider Meadows off the Phelps Creek Trail. I was laying down in my tent with the door open. I observed some light flashes in the distance South of Spider Meadows. I thought it was dry lightning for a few minutes until I observed a white orb projecting a diffused beam toward the ground. It was about a half mile away and appeared to be looking for something. It was moving North towards my camp. It moved back and forth and came to a stop above the tree line about 80 degrees SW of me about 1001 up in the air. The orb appeared to be about 21 in diameter. It was just hovering making no noise. I became terrified and hid with my dog in my sleeping bag. After Five minutes I looked back up in the sky but it was gone. I talked to another camper the next morning that also had scene the orb. From the moment I first observed the orb itself until it stopped near my camp was about 3 minutes. I must have observed the flashes for 5 minuets before that. I was very scared and didn1t fall alseep until 4am. I packed up and cut my trip short Incase it came back the next night I was terrified of being abducted.
