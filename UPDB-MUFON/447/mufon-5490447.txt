{cak} Bright red stationary light

Around ten till eleven at night central time. Traveling south on meridian road north of state rd 6 I saw a very bright red orb in the sky. It was stationary. I went east on 900 north to get to my house. I got out of the car and walked down the street to see if it was still there. It was still hovering and there was another red orb underneath it. I looked over to the west and saw another red orb hovering. The two orbs to the south at about the same time dimmed. The started moving south and started blinking. I looked over to the orb in the east. It looked like it was moving towards me to the east. It went dark and into the clouds. It did not come out of the clouds and must have reversed direction. The cloud cover was half clouds to the west and all clear on the east. Bindery was north south. As the two red orbs blinked out they moved south then looked like planes leaving the area. I am a sky watcher we live on the midway airport flight path. I am use to planes looking very bright as they are miles out coming in. The size and the bright color of red they were lead me to believe they were not planes. When they all three dimmed out at the same time I thought maybe they were planes. I was wanting to see if anyone else saw.
