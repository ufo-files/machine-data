2 Objects, 1 white/1 red over Mpls Riots

One night during the riots I went out onto my back step, as I often do, and look up at the night sky. I saw 2 fixed lights in space. One was white, the other reddish orange. I thought they were satellites watching the riots and fires from space. I kept looking for a few seconds. The reddish orange light made a 90 degree turn. 
At the time, I thought wow, I didn’t know satellites could do that. The white light didn’t appear to move.
Later, as I thought about what I saw over the next few days, I realized that the other satellites and the space station I have seen flying over were moving. Then I realized that we probably don’t have the satellite technology to do what I saw that night.
