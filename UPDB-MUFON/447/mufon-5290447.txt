We were driving down the Long Island Expressway heading East & my husband was half joking/confused and said look a UFO. I looked up and there it was. It took me a while to get my camera out because I was shaking. The video is hard to tell it was foggy and we couldn’t pull over, but I have never seen anything so enormous and clear in my life. It was so large, and it had a circular dome on top, another on the bottom, and the whole middle was made up of a circular flat disc. It looked exactly like a ufo in the movies. There were lights around the disc, a bright light on top of the upper dome, and a red light on the bottom of the lower dome that seemed to explode and brighten periodically which may have been related to twice a strong fat light beam came through from the bottom then sucked right back up. The red light almost was explosive, not a regular blinking light & the beam almost looked like the whole bottom was just opening up I can’t explain it. And the movement was not like a plane just passing through it’s course. It remained level and steady along side me (I was filming from the passenger front seat) then it eventually drifted into the fog. I want to stress, I am not someone that has been a believer my whole life & now I have evidence. I am now a believer, because of what I saw. The video isn’t perfect, but you can especially see in the first few seconds the actually ufo disc/dome shape and for a quick second after 12 seconds into the video a light explosion shows a glimpse of the full shape. And in person it was the largest thing I’ve ever seen in the sky. I felt weird when it was happening. I was shaking, a feeling overcame me like “this is not normal”. Something unreal was in front of me. Not everyone will believe just from the video, but my eyes will never forget what I saw even before I was able to focus the camera!
