Round, possibly red with bright flames underneath.

I was turning from Wahconah Street onto North Street, when I saw something bright coming out of the south. It looked like it might have been coming from the direction of the Pittsfield airport. I quickly pulled over to watch it. I couldn't make out much of the shape, but it looked round and possibly red. Below it were billowing flames. As I watched it move (very quickly) over head directly north, the flames began to die down until they were just barely flickering, then dissipated completely.
