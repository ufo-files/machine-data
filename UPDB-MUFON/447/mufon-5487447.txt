{dr}I was testing my new Nikon P900 taking pictures of the moon. I didn't see anything until I developed the film and saw what seems to be ufo's orbiting close to the moon.

I was home and decided to test my new Nikon P900, that takes very close pictures of the moon. I didn't see any objects until I downloaded the pics on my computer. In some of the pictures, it is clear that there was something orbiting, maybe landing. As I zoomed closer, I could see up to 3 ufo's (?) on the dark space around the moon. As I take the pictures, they seem to move from one spot to another... there were at least 3 objects in the pictures. Even though I was still learning how to use the camera, it did move a bit, and I'm not sure if this is what caused the lights to appear... I would love to see if D'Antonio could take a look at the pics. I order to see all that appears, it is necessary to zoom on the moon (right side) and right bottom and left upright parts of the dark space around it. When seen with contrast, it becomes very clear.
