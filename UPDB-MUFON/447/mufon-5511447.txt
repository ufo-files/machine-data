As I Wes taking out the trash I stopped to admire the sky. I always try to do this if its a clear night. I rally enjoy looking at the night sky. As I was scanning the sky I noticed a pulsing light off to the west. I watched it for several minutes. It was a blue pulsating object that was moving very slowly. It made several position changes but always seemed to be moving to the west and northwest. I watched it for about an hour as it slowly moved away. It is still visable as I write this at 10:40 pm, but is so far away as to be very faint.
