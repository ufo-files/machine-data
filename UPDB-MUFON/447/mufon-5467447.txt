A very bright whiteish sphere like object with a blue hue going very fast toward the earth ( disappeared behind mountains)

I was driving out of Marbella toward Malaga on the highway when I noticed a very bright whitish sphere like object with what looked like a tail and a blueish hue travelling very very fast toward the earth, I saw it for a few seconds before it disappeared behind the mountains. Initially I thought it was a meteor which I have seen before but this looked different it was so very very bright and travelling incredibly fast like nothing I1ve seen before.
