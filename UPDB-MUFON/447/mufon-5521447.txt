Drive by sighting.

I was on my way to Mcdonalds on Fourth Plain (travelling west).(Around 9:38 or 10:00pm. Travelling on Davis Road, I looked out of my window (front) and notice above the tree line a orange orb sitting there. It wasnt moving or doing anything strange. At first I thought it was a shop light from someones house until it started to pulse and then it seemed to change(in an explosive way) into a much larger orb. Ive never seen anything so large in my life. At first I was amazed then a strong urge of fear set in. I was staring at it and a house got in my visual sight and when I passed the house it was gone.
