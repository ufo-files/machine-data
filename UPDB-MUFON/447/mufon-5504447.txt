It was approximately 22:20 hrs. I was looking out over the Atlantic Ocean and noticed a bright light. At first I thought it was aircraft making and approach headed west toward me. It looking like there were lights blinking. Then I noticed it wasn't moving , so I thought it might be a planet. Then I noticed a red light as well as a white light, so I got my binoculars and when I focused in on it I saw a green light as well. The lights were blinking in sequence. I watched it for approximately 20 minutes. I went inside and came back about a half hour later and it was gone.
