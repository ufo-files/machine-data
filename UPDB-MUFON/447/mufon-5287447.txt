Green ufo

I was driving to work, I used to work the third shift and I was stopped at a red light when I noticed a green light in my rear view mirror, I thought it was a helicopter but there was no sound at all then I looked again in the mirror and I was the whole street was flooded with the green light, so I looked up from my car window and there was a flying saucer just hovering with absolutely no sound. I watched in amazement, it felt like time froze and I just watched the craft hover over me and then pass me just going in a straight line , I kept watching it ...then it went past some wooded area and disappeared.
