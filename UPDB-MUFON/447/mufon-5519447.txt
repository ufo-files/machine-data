Shooting star dropped, slowed, then went up in unison with another star.

I was standing on my front porch on Friday night, looking up toward the north around 10:30. I saw what appeared to be a shooting star that moved slowly downward at about a 45 degree angle. It neared 2 stars and stopped for a second. It then went back up in unison with one other star and faded away.
