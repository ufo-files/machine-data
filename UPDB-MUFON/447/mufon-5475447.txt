{nd} Glowing orange orb with flamelike bright orange emanatinions. When overhead fade but object still visible

Watching fireworks over northwest Boise. Large orange orb emanating brighter orange was spotted by me. At least 4 other people saw it. At first I thought it was a Chinese lantern but realized it was moving about 100 mph or the speed of a small plane. When it was almost directly overhead light faded but round object was still visible against sky and moving at same speed. I lost it against the nearly full moon but others tracked it another 30 seconds. Finally we all had lost sight of it. It was visible for 1 minute glowing then not illuminated for 1 minute approximately.
