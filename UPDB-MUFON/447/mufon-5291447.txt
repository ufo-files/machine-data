Triangle shaped with V shaped lights

My daughter and I were sitting on the back deck at approximately 9:15 PM. The sky was clear and dark.  We observed what we first thought were large birds flying in a V formation. As the object moved overhead, we saw that the individual V shaped lights were glowing yellow/green (soft) and that it was actually lights on a large triangle shaped craft. The craft was dark like the night sky with a faint outline of the edge of the craft. There was one V shaped light at the point of the triangle and three more V shaped lights on the right edge and two V shaped lights on the left. The lights were actually more of a boomerang, flat V shape. It was completely silent as it moved from West to East almost directly above us. It appeared low and it moved in a straight line.
