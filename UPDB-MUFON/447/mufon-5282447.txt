Multiple orbs Video tape on iPhone 12

Watched several reddish orbs heading south towards Huntington Beach from Long Beach area. What appeared to be just off the coast. Starting around 9:00 pm first noticed three orbs kind of lined up in a row one following the other spread apart by approximately 1000 to 2000 feet. At first thought probably helicopters or possibly planes. However no sound made by objects . It is a clear night visibility is excellent. All the orbs traveled down coast then somewhere around the Huntington Beach pier they seemed to make a right toward Catalina island climbing in altitude quickly until they disappeared. I have four videos first one started at 9:02 pm last one started a 9:20 pm.
