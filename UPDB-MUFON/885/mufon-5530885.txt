Southern sky, moving south easterly orangish red object in the distant south just above the tree-line, no blinking lights visible, visible for 45 seconds.

As a took my wired haired fox terrier Cosmos across the road in front of my house I turned in the direction of the southern sky and saw a orangish-red object moving in the distant southern sky just above the treeline in a southeastardly direction. This object was a fourth the size as the one seen on July 4th 2010 because it was at a much farther distance. No visible blinking lights as seen through 10x30x50 zoom Bushnell binaculars. My wife and I were stargazing just prior to my taking our dog for a short walk. If I had continued to stargaze lying on a patio lounge chair I would have missed seeing this object because it was so low and distant in the southern sky. This is only the second orangish-red moving object I have seen in years of watching the night sky. Time of sighting was exactally 10:50 pm. same as on July 4th. coincidental???
