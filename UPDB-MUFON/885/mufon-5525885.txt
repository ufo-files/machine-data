Brilliant light traveling southeast, slowly dimmed and vanished

My daughter and I were sitting in the yard talking when this bright light came into my view around 55 degree elevation heading southeast, altitude about 1500 to 2000 feet. The object continued southeast, became very dim and vanished. The object traveled perpendicular to the Philadelphia landing pattern.My daughter photographed the object with her cell phone. The time of this event was 10:14, date: June 29, 2011. The sky was clear and the object made no audiable sound....
