{rjl} Saw object while coming out of work at night.

I walked out of the building where I worked and walked to my car. when I got to my car my back was turned to the building, at this point I felt as if there was something behind me. Immediately I told myself I was looking at an alien craft - I just felt this to be true. The thing just stopped, it didn't move. I stood there in a dark parking lot, alone, staring up at this thing and was never so terrified in my life. I thought I was going to be abducted. I stood frozen, I could not move. I remember feeling that if I did not try to get away it might take me but I just couldn't move. Right after I experienced the fear and had these thoughts, the thing just left.
