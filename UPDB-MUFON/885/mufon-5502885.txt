{nd} At first like satellite but then flew erratically changing directions quickly and super fast

When I was a young boy, me and a friend were spending the night in my parents front yard.  We were talking, looking at the stars, and trying to find constellations when we noticed a satellite streaming across the sky.  Soon then after, we saw what at first looked like another satellite; however, after approximately 5 seconds, the object stopped, and then began moving erratically. It would go left, then right, it appeared to get bigger, then smaller.  There was no sound, keep in mind it was small enough to look like a satellite.  This activity went on from this object lasted for approximately 10-15 minutes, then it shot away quickly to what I believe would have been the North.  Neither my friend, nor I as far as I know have ever spoke about this.  We were scared about what we saw, so much so, we decided to go sleep inside about 20 minutes after the object disappeared.
