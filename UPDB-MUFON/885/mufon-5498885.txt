{cak} over my house

I was having another round of insomnia so I was already awake at about 3am when I first heard it, I thought it was a helicopter at first so I got off my bed to close the window but I didn't see any light's like a normal chopper, which I thought was odd but didn't think much of. I laid back on my bed but the sound got stronger and louder, I still didn't see any lights or anything else for that matter, but the very loud sound was still there, I actually expected to also hear sirens but nothing like that at all. It was as though it was just hovered on top of my house, lasted about 2 hours, and not my husband nor my kids heard it, so weird because the sound was amazingly loud. I asked my in laws if they heard it since they live in the front house and she said no but my brother in law did, he said he also looked outside but saw nothing, only darkness and the loud sound. I have never heard anything like it in my life.
