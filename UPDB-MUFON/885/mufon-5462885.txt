We watched each other for less than a minute

I was gardening in Southampton UK and heard a rustle in the under growth a small human like creature was as shocked as I was to see it looked back and we made eye contact it climbed over a dead log and looked back at me we maintained eye contact for 20 seconds at least it turned away and disappeared into the undergrowth using the undergrowth to hide and I heard it scurrying away. 
I do a few hours gardening a month and the garden is huge by UK standards with a large area of woodland as part of it.
