Loud Hissing/Buzzing heard. Blue or White beam of light in backyard

He got his camera and went to the back door. In the yard he saw a beam of light. He went outside to our covered porch. HE TOOK 3 PHOTOS. The light appears blue but may have been white if the blue color was caused by a white light against a black sky. I can send photos of a normal night view of the backyard, if requested since it is NOT illuminated normally like a football field during a game!
