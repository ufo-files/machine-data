Triangle 5 large lights white to yellow no noise slow cruse towards SE

My neighborhood is loaded with very large trees, so our sky view is limited from our personal backyards. I used to work for an aviation company, therefore, I understand the blinking lights, etc. 

I've spent way too many hours at an airport strip for private owned aircraft. THIS AINT RIGHT!!!! It was a tight narrow/tall triangle

It was just above the tree tops, maybe 1/2 a tree higher. The lights were not the small lights of an aviation aircraft. They were much larger! I realize that when the aircraft is lower to the ground, the lights appear larger. This was LARGER THAN THAT!!! Not a single sound to be heard. It was crusing slow, at it's own pace. It was 5 lights in a triangle.

The lights were a "continous" on! Reminded of headlights, but BELOW the object, not in front of the object.

Then my neighbors 2 story house blocked my view.
I called my son on his cell, cause he was in town.

By the time I ran to the front yard, it didn't matter. Too many large tall trees.

My son jumped in his car and made a drive. He swears he NEVER saw it!

I KNOW WHAT I SAW!!!! I'VE NEVER MADE A REPORT IN MY LIFE BEFORE THIS!!!

It was traveling SW. This happened at 11:00 pm, or at least close enough, within minutes. Considering the time between tree blockage... I would have to say the sighting lasted maybe a minute, maybe less.

I've been watching the night sky for 20 years. I raised my 4 sons to watch the night sky's. This is my 1ST FREAK OUT!!!

I need to get a tape measure out and try to figure out the size accordingly.

I only saw light formation. I never saw an outline of the object. It is a full moon night. The full moon is in the SW region. But it did not illuminate the object.
