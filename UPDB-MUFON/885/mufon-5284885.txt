thought it was a really bright moon at first

I was in Ramona waiting for day break so that I could pick up ammo brass from people shooting there the day before . It was a very clear morning bight stars and a full moon . I was sitting on the hood of my truck with my coffee jug and cup , side are on and a m1 garand laying on the hood. the moon looked as if it was coming towards me but very slowly , getting bigger and bigger as it came for the west to east direction towards , started about 60 degrees up and descending very slow towards me . I remember the light was getting so bright as it crossed over highway 78 coming towards me and then I relized it was not the moon and I started to get scared a little . Im not sure of the time and date of the month of December that year but it was in December of 2002. I just took off my colt 45 and seat it net to my rifle on the hood of the truck , not believing what Im seeing out there all along by my self. It was huge well over 300 to 400 foot circle and very bright , and I could see as it got closer that there were patterns in the moon shape sphere that looked kinda man made with things moving around inside . I talked to my God at that point and asked him if it my time to go with him , then please just take me , it was so bright that I could see the brass on the ground that I was going to pick up when it got light that morning . The sphere stayed there about 10 or so minutes then when back to the west and over the horizon out of sight. I have only talked to one other person about what happened to me that morning and was thankful that im here today               (Name removed to Investigators Report Section/cms/LLL)
