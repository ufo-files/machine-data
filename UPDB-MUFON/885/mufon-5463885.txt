{rjl} oval revolving lights red in front multi color in back, moved quickly

Took dogs out at around 2am, stood on deck under tree and noticed flashing lights in the East coming toward me, the lights in front were spinning quickly in red sequence and the lights behind them were spinning the same but were multi colored. It was a large oval shape that moved very quick, called out to my husband who was asleep and did not call him again, in all of 30 seconds it went over our house and into the west, we have tall trees and could not see past that. I have never seen anything like this before.
