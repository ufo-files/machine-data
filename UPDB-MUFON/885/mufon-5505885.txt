{nd} six lights traveling in straight line with no sound

When I turned into my neighborhood, I noticed what looked like a star. However I noticed the light was moving. Then I noticed five other pink-Orange lights in a straight line in front of it. I rolled down window to see if I could hear any sound and there was no sound. Each light disappeared as it reached a large cloud and they never came back out.
