Seven bright objects in straight formation heading NE direction. No sound only movement.

On 6/19/20, at 0428 hours, I was walking north on Hartman Road and I saw seven bright objects at approximately a 30 degrees angle in straight formation heading northeast at a steady pace. The objects appeared to be very high in elevation or in outer space but were not stars since I photograph night skies and know what is observable.They were not shooting stars but appeared to look like them, all heading the same direction. I quickly got my cell phone camera (previously set for night photography), looked at my screen and noticed only four objects. I took the photo and saw that I had captured the four objects. I then noticed they were not in focus but the surrounding stars were, indicating movement. After taking that photo I saw that all the objects had disappeared, so I was unable to take any more.
