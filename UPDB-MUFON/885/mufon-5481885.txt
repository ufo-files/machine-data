{dr}Caught an unknown object in photo

On Saturday July 30, 2016, approximately at 7:23 pm I was returning home to Texas with my husbands driving along the 10 interstate toward Houston.At the time of the photos we were driving through Beaumont Texas. My husband was driving with myself in the passenger seat. I noticed the evening sky was particularly cloudy with rays of sun casting through them.I felt an importance for some reason to take random pictures of the sky and did not see anything in the sky of any interest just the clouds. 

After I took the pictures I went back to view what I took and it was there that I saw the round white object in the upper left corner of the second photo and in the first photo which was taken 15 seconds prior there was nothing. 

Like I said previously, when I took the pictures I did not see anything in the sky, it was only when I looked back through the picture did I see the object in the sky.

Could be just a random cloud but I find it interesting.
