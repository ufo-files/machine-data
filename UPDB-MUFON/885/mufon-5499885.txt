{jd} three lights in triangle shape

I was stationed at 29 palms in the marine corps. In September of 1966 around 10 pm a friend came into the barracks and said for me to come outside and see something strange. I went out , he pointed to the ne above a small mountain and there were 3 red lights blinking slowly . I said they must have built 3 new radio towers . He replied no stay here and watch them, after about 1 minute the object shot straight up about 1 mile stopped for a few seconds then shot back to original position , the lights change to orange then back to red. The object then shot to the right about a mile , paused then shot back to the original position out of nowhere came a 4th light that shot to the middle of the triangle then the object became real bright then dim then bright the it shot out of sight within 10 seconds'
