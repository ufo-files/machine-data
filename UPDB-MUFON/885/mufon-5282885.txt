A Morning Star in the Afternoon - Bright Star Light Object

Driving from Boston heading to Albany, NY on the later morning of 8 Jan. Saw an unusual bright star in the sky above the trees and slightly to the right. At first I thought it was a morning star but it was pretty sparkling. I asked my wife to take a look but when she did it disintegrated into the sky, as if pulling back. I did see planes after and it did not appear to resemble that. It was almost like the morning star in the early mornings except sparkling prominently in bright daylight.
