{tjd} About a 4 foot long flattened rounded cylinder

This object passed by the back of my house and I cought a glimpse for about 2 seconds and I was looking directly at it. The best shape I can describe is the shape of a Star Trek Photon torpedo. It was shiny two tone brown and beige or cream, was a glossy hard smooth finish, no other observible features. It went very fast, maybe 150 feet in 2 to 3 seconds, made absolutely no noise, travelled from South to North just outside of two trees about 75 feet from me in my back yard that is on a golf course. (no, it wasn't a golf club bag).
At first I thought DRONE, but it had no propellers or wings, and made no sounds and the speed may have been about 50 to 70 MPH. Did not accellerate or slow down or turn off its course. Left me with a distinct feeling (can't describe).
