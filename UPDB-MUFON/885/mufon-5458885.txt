{jpg} Saw a UFO outside my house!

Stepped outside with my fiancée to catch some fresh air during family late movie night and saw three lights stationary in the sky either over the river or nearby wind turbine and power lines that stretch across the mountain. What’s strange is it was extremely difficult to see it on video but it’s clear as in the photos.
