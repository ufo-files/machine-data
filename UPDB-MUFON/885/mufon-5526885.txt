craft hovering in a circular motion, light beaming downward, flying away

I was outside of my house, sitting on the side wall of my staircase. I heard a humming noice, low pitched, vibrating in deep frequency. I looked up and saw an oval disc the size of a football field, metallic sage greenish-gray, with 3 beams of light descending downward onto my neighbors apartment building. I knew immediately it was an UFO because this was not my first sighting. It was a coastal foggy, cloudy night. The craft hovered across street from my home for approximately 1 minute. Then the craft rotated very slowly in a circular motion, as the 3 beams of light jetted down. Then the craft moved in a sliding motion side ways, and speeded off (less than a second in time) upward, out of sight. I called the Santa Monica Airport and LAX to ask if someone saw what I saw. I called information and received a number and made a report. I was sent a form and was shocked to discover the questions as I knew someone or group had to have seen a similar sighting in order to ask and set up the questionaire as it was written. At first I was stunned, then afraid, then driven to tell my sighting to someone who would believe me.
