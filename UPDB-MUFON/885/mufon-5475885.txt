{cak} A bright light moving with a slight zig zag.

At about 9pm last night while our youngest daughter was eating dinner my wife and I heard a loud helicopter outside. As we are used to military helicopters flying over we ran outside to see if it was. When we saw it was only a regular one we sent our youngest back inside to finish eating. My wife then saw a light above her head moving at a slow but steady pace. It was a solid white light with no sound or no other blinking lights. We watched it as it flew what would say is possibly east occasionally making little side to side motions. We continued to watch it till it began to blink then disappeared. We then had an airplane fly over at about the same height and you could not only see the other blinking lights but hear it. My wife went inside and checked her phone which has an app that shows stars, planets, constellations and even the flight paths of planes and the only plane in our area at that time was the one we saw and heard.
