{rjl} bright lights in field randoming flashing colors described, object approached town and passed over and continued passed producing a low whir much different than a helicopter, it was a round object with very bright lights.

When I first saw it I was opening the window in the south side the house and saw some bright lights out in the field, so I watched it out the window and saw the colors of the lights, which were white, blue, green, red pulsating lights with two yellow blinking lights on the bottom. It was stationary when I first saw it and was the size of a beach ball and looked about 5 miles away, when it started approaching the town it increased in size to about the size of a small passenger plane and passed over town, this is when I saw the yellow lights, it also produced a whirring sound when it passed over town, a real low almost inaudible deep whir, this caused all of the dogs to start barking in town and set off a car alarm in town. It then continued to the NE and I ran through the house to see where it was headed and proceeded to watch it until I could not see it anymore.
