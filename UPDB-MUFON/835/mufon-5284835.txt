Triangle with 3 white peremiter lights and a cetnber red light.

I was letting my dogs in from outside, no barking when I heard what I thought was a jet engine but really low jet, lower octave of sound. I walked outside and followed the sound. As my ears followed the sound I looked above the trees southwest and saw VERY CLEARLY a triangle moving slowly in a non turning direction over the tree tops. I yelled to my wife inside the house to come out but by the time she arrived it was behind the treeline. We stood there for a minute and she could still hear the noise fading into the distance that I originally heard. I then saw it one more time THROUGH the trees, the red light, she couldn't see the lights the second time because I had to back up to see it, we live on the north side of Tucker Mt. I can easily say that if somebody was on I-93, they could probably have seen the same thing I did. The direction it was "gliding" was towards I-93. The relative movement against the tree line was definitely a lot slower than a helicopter I see around here. No doubt, triangle with 3 lights, red center.
