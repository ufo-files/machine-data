{kt}Heading w on 64 out of selmer observed metallic craft stationary to w/sw, it then appeared in a nw direction in roughly a couple of seconds, observed it for about a minute and a half, 2 lights ejected from each end in opposite spirals then disappeared

My wife, I and our two children were leaving selmer going west on 64 hwy approaching sulphur springs rd. my wife called my attention to the object which was shiny metallic in color but not reflective as it was sunny and clear, the object was cigar shaped and it was stationary in a west southwest direction referenced from 64 hwy, at an elevation of maybe 75-80 degrees off of the horizon, we all observed it for maybe 30 seconds when we thought it had vanished, I then observed it in a north northwest position from the highway same approx elevation about 2 seconds later we pulled over and watched it it remained stationary, after about another minute and a half 2 bright lights ejected from each end of it, the lights traveled in a spiral in opposite directions from each other and then disappeared, we continued to watch the object for approx another minute the it vanished and we left.
