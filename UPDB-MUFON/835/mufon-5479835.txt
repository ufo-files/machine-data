{dr}Slow moving/hovering Trapezoid solid shape UFO with red glowing lights that pulsated before changing formation and then flickering and disappearing one by one

I dropped my two children off at their fathers house and as I was getting them out of the car I noticed a large craft with six red glowing lights and a gray center slowly moving beneath the cloud cover. As I watched, I called for the children's father, who also came out to see it. The craft continued to move in a northeast direction, before the red lifts began to change formation to a triangle shape, then flickering and pulsating. One by one the lights disappeared and the entire soundless craft was gone. With no trace of it. The craft looked like it was the size of a football field! After a few moments we could hear helicopters chasing the direction of the craft we saw.
