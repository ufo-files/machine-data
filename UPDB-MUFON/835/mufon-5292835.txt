We have observed a bizarre hovering object in the sky close to Nevada border, in Kingston Range California dessert while camping

We have first observed the bizarre lights from the tent very early morning (4:36 AM), while it was still dark. Half asleep my first thought was it's a landing airplane that has all lights turned on and is making a final curve in the sky. The object was however projecting light to the sides and looked like a beaming line with a row of numerous lights in the middle of it, and there was no airport near. It was probably 10 minutes before I took the first picture. By then the shape started to change and moved a little to the right. It looked like opening/unfolding with rays of lights shining in different directions. The lights were pulsating, from very bright to almost invisible, in a regular interval of about 12 seconds. The object remained hovering in the same place for good 50 minutes. Cell phone we have been using to take pictures and videos was suddenly not working well - I wanted to check my pictures and my photo gallery was showing only 2 last pictures taken instead of hundreds taken before. Pictures appeared again after the object disappeared. Given the fact that the object was high up behind clouds, it appeared very large and very bright. We have stopped observing it at about 5:30 AM, a while later it was not visible anymore.
