{rjl} Red then orange-red glow with emission of sparks or balls of light, traveled east to west, then north

It was a stormy night. Lightening was flashing in the sky and rain fell intermittently. When I had seen the lights it was not raining. I was traveling from South Greensburg towards Youngwood on Route 119 nearing South Grande Blvd intersection. The object was coming from the East and did hover near the area of my vicinity. I pulled over by the Greensburg Sewage Plant to complete my observations.The object was a deep red glow which pulsated to an orange red glow and then back to red. From the end pointed towards the east balls of light emitted and disappeared. This emission happened twice. It was after the emission that it seemed to hover. After watching it for about four minutes it moved northward disappearing into the dark clouds. No sounds were detectable but I was near the highway. I had never seen a plane glow that color. No marker lights were flashing.
