{dr}Laser on tripod shined at 2 stars, stars shifted position repeatedly.

I was in my back yard at 8:30. A few weeks earlier, I was walking the dogs when out of the corner of my eye, I saw a star brighten. I thought I was seeing a super nova of a star or something, but instead, it was a star that suddenly moved out of position and beging to move eastwardly then ascend. It was trailed by a dimmer starlike object. I had seen two stars move and ascend, so I thought I might catch that kind of event by chance again or maybe I could force it by shining my green laser randomly at various stars. The laser is meant to be used as a star pointer, only when I pointed it at this particular star the star seemed to move. I then ran inside and placed the green laser on a tripod and aimed it at the starlike object , which was now stationary again and again it moved away from my laser light about a centimeter across the sky. I engaged it repeatedly and it shifted repeatedly at least eight times. I was afraid after a bit that that thing was going to swoop down and attack me for annoying it, but it didn't. I went back outside after about a half hour and it was no longer there. I was excited to have affected the UFO.
