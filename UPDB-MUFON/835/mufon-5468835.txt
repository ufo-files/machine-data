{jpg} My father and I saw strange light on ground and then lost 3 hours.

My father and I were driving from Louisville, KY to Corbin, KY to visit my brother. We got off I-75 at the Corbin exit and proceeded down 25E towards Corbin. We were supposed to meet my brother at 6:00 PM and have supper together. It was about 5:00PM when we left I-75. After driving toward Corbin for a short time (maybe 10 minutes), a very strange thing occurred. It happened like a memory. That is, it was like what happens when you are in an accident or other traumatic event. We both looked at each other in the car and said, "Did you see that?" at the same time. What I remember seeing was a large object with a door on it that opened down like a drawbridge and a bright red light coming out of it. That is all I remembered. However, we noticed that it was dark. When we arrived at my Brother's apartment in Corbin after a 15 minute drive, we were surprised to find that he had given up on us and had already ate. We then realized that it was after 9:00 PM. For some reason we did not put the two things together until later. We were just totally confused. I also discovered that I had a scar on my left forearm that is straight looks like a surgical scar. 
I have no answers as to what happened to the three hours we lost that evening.
