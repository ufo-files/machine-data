During blizzard and area blackout, I witnessed a large object with white lights and lots of faint light blue twinkle lights hovering.

During a late night blizzard and town blackout, I witnessed a large object coming towards my house down a county road. I thought it was a truck since I saw bright white lights and I was concerned that my husband, who was in the barn outside, was going to collide with it since he had to leave for work. So as I looked outside, I saw the object stop coming down the road but instead turned right into our field. At that time, I knew it wasn’t a vehicle because of the large ditch that couldn’t be crossed and the fencing that was in place. As I looked on, the object was hovering in the sky, about 100 ft from the ground, and I could see thousands of light blue twinkle lights all over it. The object was hovering near the neighbor’s house near our field and started to slowly move west when our barn began to block my view of it. I couldn’t go outside because of the blizzard, so went to another room to try and see it, but I couldn’t find it. I don’t know if it landed or sped away. I know it wasn’t anything from here. Nobody would have been flying outside, during a blackout, during a blizzard at 12am on my property. I never filed anything because my husband said I shouldn’t because I could get a bad stigma. I still remember exactly how it looked and all of the events that night; I was going to sketch it but thought that Id never forget it, so didn’t. I am 100% certain it was not from our planet.
