{cak} Big, bright blue ball of light shot across center of sky in about 6 seconds and disappeared with no trails whatsoever

I was talking with my uncle and old friend Holly in my livingroom. I was sitting on the futon facing the window and Holly was facing me. My uncle was playing Xbox so he hadn't been paying attention. Out of the corner of my eye I saw a bright object outside so I turned my head a bit to look and when I did I saw this huge, bright blue ball of light shoot across the center of the sky from North to South. It lasted about six seconds. I know it wasn't a plane or anything like that because of how big it was in comparison to stars and airplanes that I'd ever seen in the area, and we've had planes that fly pretty low. There was no blinking, no sound, nothing but this solid sphere. It left no trails of any kind as it traveled and as it got about a mile away in ground distance, it simply disappeared as if it just turned off. I'm not sure what it was but I've searched online for any reports of meteors or anything like that to debunk what I saw but I haven't found anything but other sightings of odd lights in neighboring states.
