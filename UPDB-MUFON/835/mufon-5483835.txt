Stick like UFO with blinking lights, maybe curved slightly towards bottom.

I went outside for a cigarette at 8pm (I know this time is accurate as I checked after.) At first I thought it was maybe a plane turning, as I live in an area that planes need to go by to reach Glasgow airport, but it remained vertical shaped, almost stick like, maybe curved slightly at the bottom with lights flashing occasionally, which is why I first assumed it was a plane. The light emitting from it was light blue-ish, but the material of the object looked metallic.
The sighting lasted about 2-3 mins on my end, but maybe someone else saw it for longer. I did manage to rush and grab my binoculars for a quick look, but I quickly lost it after taking my eyes off it for a moment. It certainly wasn't a plane though.
