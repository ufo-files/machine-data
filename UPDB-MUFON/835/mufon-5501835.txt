{dr}Multiple objects, triangles, saucer, ball of flame observed within 5 mile radius and seemed like we were chased. Most activity above TMI and then a housing developement

Driving on Rt 441 outside TMI with 2 friends. We were looking at aircraft cause there's an airport close by and notice maybe 6(?) triangle shaped flying objects. We say and watched them and it almost looked like a dog fight but you could tell they were friendly with each other, although the maneuvers they were making were odd for planes and then they seemed to get closer so we got in the car to leave and had trouble starting the engine. Then we left but they "followed" us. I droped one friend off, then the other. 2nd friend I dropped off we got out of car for cigarette n to talk about what we saw and got "buzzed" by what seemed to be a fire ball tge size of my Mazda. I got in my car and speed a mile home. When I got in my room I looked out my bedroom window and there was a very large, grey, metal flying saucer hovering above the development about 2 miles north of my house. I had an overwhelming sense of dread and started crying hysterically and praying and passed out.
