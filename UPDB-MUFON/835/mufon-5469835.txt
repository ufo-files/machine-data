{jpg} Looked like flock of seagulls swirling around and intermittently disappearing Into the sky

Observe what looked like a swirling flock of seagulls
swirling around each other, and intermittently one by one disappearing into the background of a clear blue sky. What struck me was the fact that they were way too high for gulls. For perspective, I also observe a plane, approaching from right to left toward the flock, and was able to see the gulls seemed much higher than the plane. As the plane got closer to the gulls position, they disappeared in ones and twos up into the sky.
 This occured on Saturday March 3.2018, at approximately 12 noon. Was coming out of my house, making my way to the car, and looked up because the sky was so clear blue..
When I saw this "flock", I was immediately aware of their disappearing and reappearing , and their altitude, which made me watch them till they disappeared into the sky.
Very strange indeed
