Saucer emits light in Dundee, Michigan - 1957

Witness Narrative:Duration 10 secondsWeather Clear and WarmMy cousin ____ and I were sitting on the screened in Porch of my God Mothershouse looking out at the wooded pasture across the road when this objectappeared to our left (south) moving in a path across the front of the house(house faces west) and disappeared behind the tops of the trees.The object was shaped like a saucer with shiny surfaces and emitting light fromthe bottom. It made no sound. It appeared to follow a straight path at approx20 mph.Because the pasture is in a gulley and the house is on a small hill the objectwas just above eye level so we could see the side and bottom as it passeddirectly in front of us. When it had disappeared we ran in the house and toldmy God Mother who was in her mid to late fifties at the time. She said we werejust seeing things and dismissed it at that.
