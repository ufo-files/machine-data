(GS) Object moved slowly from the north, stopped and a piece of the same color fell from it and burst into flames. Object then left going north

We stopped just outside of Fulton, NY, south of the city.

I looked into the sky which was very clear and full of stars.

I saw this orange cigar shaped object and I said to my friend look at this thing.

It moved slowly from the north, stopped and a piece of the same color ( glowing orange ) fell from it, burst into flames and then the object moved slowly off in the same direction it came from.

I was then and still am excited and curious as to what it was. My friend to this day still thinks it was nothing.

I am still puzzled by his thoughts of this event.
