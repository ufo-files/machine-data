V shaped, black with a light at each point.

I was driving down hwy 26 going west bound after work around 7:45pm. I got close to my exit North Plains/Cornelius and I noticed this ball of light in the distance. At first I thought it was a helicopter which I see many times when I'm coming home at night. As I got closer I thought it might be a star, I wasn't sure. Many times I see this ball of light and as I get closer I see it's a helicopter using search lights traffic reporting for the local news. I took the exit and continued to watch this ball of light as I waited for the stop light to change. As the light got closer it split into two lights parallel to each other. I continued to watch it and as it got closer the lights became three. I watched the lights and a V shaped object passed over the over pass, not large about the size of a large airplane. It was black and not triangular, like a V strip with a point of light at the tip and one on each tail. I drove down North Plains Road and stopped and watched it as it went toward Portland and it just got smaller in the distance. I guess my question is, did anyone else see this? You can contact me but this is pretty much all I saw.
