A ufo with 4 segments that changed into a ball and then back again.

My dad was lying on the couch and out the corner of his eye he saw something brighter than a star outside of our house. He woke me and my mother up to show us, I pulled out my phone and started recording. It was bright with lots of flashing color but it was also kind of transparent. It went from a ball shape to 4 segmented square shape then shifted into a cone shape, it kept randomly going into different shapes. After 30 seconds of filming on my phone, my battery was charged but it all of a sudden turned off my phone! Then, I got my moms phone and was able to record more. My parents and I watched it from 2:49am when my dad woke us up til 3:49am and it just was shrinking and expanding constantly until 3:49 and all of a sudden it faded into the sky and was gone.
