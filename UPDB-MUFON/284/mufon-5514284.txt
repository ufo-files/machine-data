(GS) Large glowing Orb with triangles covering the surface

Driving on I-90 just west of Jefferson Ohio exit Large orange glowing orb with black triangle shaped lines covering the surface. Very hard to miss little over one hundred yards hovering in a field just off the interstate for everyone to see. I did not stick around to investigate I just kept driving. I was not going to report this but last night I saw the same thing again out over lake Erie heading west. So obviously something is going on here in Ohio.
There were many cars on the road that night so I cant be the only one to have seen it.
