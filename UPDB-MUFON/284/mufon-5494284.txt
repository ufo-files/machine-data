{nd} I saw a cigar shaped craft silently low flying on 5/2/15 in New Orleans LA

On Saturday night 5/4/15, shortly after the Mayweather- Pacquiao fight, I stepped out on the porch to have a cigarette at exactly 11:15 PM. I immediately looked up because a shape caught my eye that I thought at first was a bird. I stepped out to the edge of the porch and saw a long, clear, almost cloaked, cigar shaped craft moving along a fixed line as if it was on a track. It was absolutely dead silent outside and this thing made no noise at all. I could see light reflecting off of it, but I never saw any colors,or navigation lights. It traveled in a straight line from where I was, in a direct line towards the city of New Orleans. It was above the trees but lower than 100 ft. and never changed altitude nor direction. Roughly the size of two limos side by side, I quickly stepped down to the front yard to continue watching this until it left my sight after 15 or so seconds. In the moment I was sort of paralyzed just watching it soar, but then realized what just happened. I immediately ran inside and started writing everything that I could remember down. This event has effected me profoundly and Im really interested to know if there are any other witnesses to it.
