{cak} Saw white red and blue moving object in sky that jumped around in same general area

I was on my front porch looking up at sky when I thought I saw a star but it was moving erratically. I WENT TO GET MY BINOCULARS AND A NEIGHBOR AND I BOTH WATCHED AS THE OBJECT WENT IN CIRCLES AND MADE CRAZY,FAST, SQUIGGLY MOVEMENTS AND STOPPED THEN MOVED IN STRANGE DIRECTIONS. TWO OTHERS CAME OUT AND ALSO SAW THE WHITE OBJECT WHICH ALSO FLASHED RED AND BLUE. I watched it for an hour. It was definitely not a star or plane because it was still then started bouncing around and wasn't going in a straight line. All of us were stunned.
