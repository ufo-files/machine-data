{cak} Glowing very bright light

I was sitting on my bed doing homework on my computer when a very bright light started shinning in my eyes through the window. When I got up to observe what it was, I could see two lights reflecting off of my car which was parked right in front of my window. I grabbed my camcorder and began to film the lights. One was the moon and the other was in front of it in the very top of the trees. I thought it may just be a reflection of the moon except it was extremely bright. Way more bright than the moon itself. After I videoed I ran to show my roommate and her boyfriend. They watched the video with amazement so we went to see if it was still there but the object had left.
