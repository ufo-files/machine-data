{nd} 3 fireballs in a triangular formation

3 bright orange fireballs seen over the Hershey, PA area. Stayed stationary the entire duration and did not lose altitude at any time, as aerial flares would do. Each of the lights would randomly flicker and "turn off" and then "turn back on" in the same position. Lasted about 6 minutes before theyd just seemed to turn off the lights one by one.
