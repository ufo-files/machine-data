{jpg} Multiple objects appear and disappear with video footage

I was grilling on my back deck just before nightfall when I felt a presence from above. I seen an object hovering around in all different directions but staying in its general area. I didn’t immediately take out my camera cause these types of sighting are a common occurrence and often just aggravate me since I have no explanation and they often fade away by the time you get your camera. But the first one stayed around 45 seconds so I pulled out my iPhone and started filming. Likely as so, as soon as I went to zoom in it disappeared as it was starting to move south west I believe. I zoomed back out cause I was focusing on the screen for the view and it was gone, hence my foul language...sorry! A few minutes later facing north what I thought was a star hovering got brighter and started moving southeast somewhat towards me before going below the tree line. In the video you can hear a plane but it is far west of the bright object. It just hovered along with no noise till it was gone.
