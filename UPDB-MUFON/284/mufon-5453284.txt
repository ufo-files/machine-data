Two star like lights flashed into view both disappeared, came back into view and flashed again before moving upwards.

Just up to the right of Orion's belt, the pot? There is a triangle formation of stars and tonight I was just gazing up there and a light flashed as it came into view, like it came out of nowhere. Then it seemed to disappear just as another light flashed and came into view just a little below where the first one did. The only reason I am reporting this sighting is because about three weeks ago up in that same area of stars, I saw a really big bright blue flash of light.
