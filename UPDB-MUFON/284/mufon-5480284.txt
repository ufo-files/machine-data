This happened years ago when i was on my way to work heading towards craftmaid cabinet factory in orwell ohio. I had been working there through a temp service for almost 3 months. I was on the highway and was approching a bridge ahead of the bridge i saw a big black triangle craft with white lights in each corner and one red in the center. It was big enough and close enough that it blocked the sky out as i approached it. it was just siting there hovering probably only about 100 feet above the trees and took up both sides of the highway. when i looked up as i drove under it the red light was right above me i have never seen anything like it. The weirdest part that bothers me to this day is that i could not remember where i was. I knew i was trying to get to work but all the sudden had no clue where i was and i had drove this route by memory for almost 90 days.
