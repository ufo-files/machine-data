cloud shaded cigar shaped object

I stepped out onto my back deck at 930 to let my dog out. My backyard has 20 ft pines along the back of my lot and right above them at a fairly low altitude, my guess around 10k ft and about 3 miles off a large cigar shaped object that must have been hundreds of feet long slowly moved across the horizon for about 30 seconds before completely evaporating into the night sky. The transition effect was like something out of a movie. It just disappeared as if dissolving into thin air. At first I thought it was a cloud but the object seemed to have actual form and purpose in its movement creeping west to east over the horizon. I heard no sound at all. Minutes later I witnessed a helicopter flying on a course from west to east that I have no problem describing as interceptive.
