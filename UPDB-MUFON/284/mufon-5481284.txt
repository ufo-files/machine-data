{rjl} Saw two small bright objects hovering very high then moving off

Sitting in backyard. Happened to look straight up during dinner and saw two very small bright objects, almost like stars, hovering very high up in the sky. They moved very slowly, almost like they were circling each other. Then one began to move south until it was out of view. The other stayed behind a wisp of cloud before it too moved off. Both disappeared behind neighboring house. Did not appear to be plane, helicopter, star, or drone. Possibly a weather balloon, but I've never seen one before. Felt very weird to watch them, as they looked like stars but there were no other stars in the area (the sun hadn't gone down yet), and they definitely were moving.
