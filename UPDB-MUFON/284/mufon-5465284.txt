{tjd} I was walking home on the coldest day of the year. I saw the underside of a gigantic metal disc that hummed faintly and slowly traveled east.

I was sixteen and walking home from my girlfriend's house when something caught my attention.
It was really cold with no wind, no moon, just a few stars.
I saw the bottom of cylindrical craft I estimated to be maybe 500 feet or more moving in an easterly direction at maybe 400 feet above.
It hummed faintly a moved slowly with no lights or visible propulsion.
I hurried home and told my Dad what I saw and he called a friend at Stratford Airport who said they didn't have any aircraft in the vicinity of the sighting.
