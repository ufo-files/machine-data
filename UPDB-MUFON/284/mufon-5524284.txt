Green light moves from NW to SE

Witness saw a green light( no tail)with a disk shape traveling downward at a 45 degree angle (NW-SE) on Sunday morning September 4th, 2011 at 6am. Witness entered the kitchen to check the time on the kitchen clock. It was 6am. A glowing, green light was see through the French door shears. According to the witness, this was the largest flying object (unknown) she had ever seen. There was no sound as the light (object) disappeared behind a hill to the East. The witness indicated that a softball held at arm1s length just might cover the object.
