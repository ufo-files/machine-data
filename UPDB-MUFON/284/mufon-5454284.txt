At 8:28 P.M. I got a call from my son in the same town. He told me to go outside and observe the lights at the base of the Big Dipper traveling South to North. We watched from multiple locations a constant line of lights isn succession which appeared to be red. There were to many to count and of to the east of the Dipper we saw multiple lights traveling East and West as one would go out of sight to the East one would immediately appear heading West. This went on for more than a half an hour. I called a neighbor who also witnessed it for a while after my wife and I went in and my son and neighbor said they saw something to seem to explode. To high to be picked up by cameras we had.
