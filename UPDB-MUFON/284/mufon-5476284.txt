I saw an aircraft carrier in the 1990s ... it looked just like the carl Vincent floated over me. I called my family out ant tried to report it but I was laughed at. Since then my cousin hung herself and I had to tell my children I will protect them.

In the 1970s I saw something. I was coming home from work. I caught buses so it was late. I had to walk across a park. I heard a rumbling sound above me and looked up. I saw a huge craft that later I could say was like an aircraft carrier slowly moving above me. It had like landing lights. Or portholes. At first I ducked because It seemed so low. I was frozen in terror then panicked and ran across the park and my family came out because I was screaming. I called the police but they laughed and no one investigated. My family didn't see the big thing. They were too busy looking at me because I was screaming. After standing in shock and then running across the park it had gone from view. It still haunts me because I know it was real. When I first saw the carl Vincent in the 1980s I thought of that night. It looked like the carl Vincent floating above me but I hadn't seen an aircraft carrier at the time.
