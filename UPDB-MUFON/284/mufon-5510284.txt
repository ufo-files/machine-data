(GS) HOVERING OVER A WEATHER LIGHT, LOOKED LIKE A CUSTER OF STARS, BUT IT WAS TO LOW IN THE SKY, MOVER SLOW THEN FASTER & DISSAPPEARED

I GOT UP IN THE MIDDLE OF THE NIGHT, I LIVE ON THE 7th FLOOR I KEPT MY BLINDS OPEN AT NIGHT TO LOOK AT THE SKY, I BEEN HERE A YEAR AND NEVER SEEN ANY THING BUT STARS, LAST NIGHT OR THIS THIS MORNING 06/14/13 I GOT UP TO USE THE BATHROOM, I NOTICE WHHAT I THOUGHT WAS A STAR, IT WAS BLINKING LIKE A CUSTER OF STAR, BUT IT WAS TO LOW IN THE SKY FOR A STAR. IT WAS HOVERING OVER ONE OF THOSE POLE FOR WEATHER OR POLICE STATION I DO NOT KNOW WHAT THAY ARE FOR, DURING THE DAY THE LIGHT FLASH WHITE AND AT NIGHT THAY FLASH RED, SO ABOVE IT WAS THIS CLUSTER OF LIGHTS THAT DIDNT MOVE , SO I ARRANGED MY PILLOW ON THE BED TO WATCH IT STARTED TO MOVE VERY SLOW, I COULD NOT BELIEVE MY EYES THEN A BIT FASTER, AND ALL AT ONCE GONE, THAT IS MY STORY.
