{nd} I Still Don't Know What it was

On my way back home 1/4 mile from the event I stepped on the sidewalk having 3 steps and turned around. Across the street about 70 feet from me and about 40 yards high I saw an metallic shiny object traveling East, as I was facing North. Standing in front of me was a tall girl who was looking up and down the street not seeing the object. The object resembled a helicopters body, but it had no propellers and it made no sound. It traveled at the speed of a helicopter or small plane. The body of it was pointy on the top and wide, oblong in the back. I watched it for around 20 seconds before continuing on my way home.

Thank you,

Thomas
