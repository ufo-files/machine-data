Large miles long glowing wormhole in night sky

My roommate and I were having a bonfire on our three acres of land when suddenly we looked up in the sky and saw a long, four sided, cube shaped glowing trail across the sky. My first thought was "this must be some kind of wormhole or energy contrail from some kind of craft." We looked up and noticed that the trail seemed to go on for miles, it was staying completely still and it wasnt dissapating. I believe we witnessed a wormhole event. As we have witnessed UFOs before in large clusters over our land and glowing skies after they passed overhead, I believe this event to be related.
