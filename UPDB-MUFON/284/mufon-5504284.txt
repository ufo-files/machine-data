Witnessed 3 bright looking stars in stationary triangle formation over ocean. As soon as we noticed that they were not stars all three of the lights slowly faded out over around 5 seconds and were gone.

Husband and I were sitting on back porch overlooking the sea of Cortez in Baja CA and noticed 3 bright looking stars in triangle formation over the water. At the same time we both commented that we hadn't noticed any constellations like that the past nights. As soon as we commented on that the 3 bright looking stars slowly faded out over a few seconds and were gone. They did not re appear and we realized that they were not stars.
