Very Intense Light at High Altitude, Descended, Maneuvered and then disappeared.

Sat in the back yard looking almost directly up into a cloudless night sky. I observed an airliner at altitude est 30-35 thousand feet coming from my left to right, as soon as the airliner had passed my position, a very very bright light appeared above and behind the aircraft, carried out an S shape manoeuvre and stopped for a few seconds then disappeared, just as another aircraft was coming on a reciprocal course at a slightly lower altitude.
Light did not reappear.
