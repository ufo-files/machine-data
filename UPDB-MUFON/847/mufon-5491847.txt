{dr}Super Black Cylinder inside Hovering Almost Clear Sphere

My mother and I got out of the car & thought we saw a drone or kids helicopter above the city. We walked towards it beginnt to assume it was a human Unmanned Aircraft then we realized there was a black rotating oblique cylinder rotating inside an almost clear perfect circle. It rotated every second inside the sphere. The sphere looked like it was somehow carrying this black object. It hovered while it ascended. It gave off the impression it was moving slow but somehow ended up Going from low down by the city all the way up past where jets fly until it was gone. It's coloring didn't change as it got higher. You could see the piercing black of it way beyond where you see a jet fly. The object inside of it rotated the whole time and it was silent. We both have never seen anything like it. It felt like a movie. The pictures submitted are the object drawn by my mother and I in seperate rooms. We almost drew the same thing without talking about it.
