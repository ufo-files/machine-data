{cak} Lights in formation

Night hike at Wright's Field - looked up and saw 10-15 lights flying in formation from west to east. They looked like tiny stars and one or two of these changed direction. One of the objects that changed direction seem to be interacting with one of the lights that was flying in a straight path. We also saw what looked like one of these tiny lights almost crashed into another one. We also noticed that the lights were moving at a very high rate of speed. The lights were not changing color and did not look like the lights from commercial aircraft. We did see some commercial jets and those lights were very different. The formation of lights was spread out and did not appear as a string of lights, but rather, appeared to be a long formation.
