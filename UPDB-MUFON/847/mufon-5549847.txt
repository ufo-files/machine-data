UFO Ocean City MD

At approximately 7pm 11/3/01 my wife and I heard a low flying jet aircraft circling our development. Upon going outside to see what was going on, we only found the moon and stars and the sound of the circling aircraft now in the distance. Then we noticed a pulsing whitish blue light the color of the new car xenon beams. The light was flying erratic and low in the sky (it reminded me of the way a small bird or sparrow flies) and did a large circle over the development. Then about 7:05pm the jet aircraft was back and following the light. The plane was low enough to actually see the aircraft illuminated by its green, red and white lights, looked like an F-16. Both airplane and the light flew northeast and went out of sight. 11/3/01 7:35 pm
