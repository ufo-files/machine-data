Bright glowing amber orb floating above Casper Mountain

I was up on Casper Mountain at Bear Trap Meadow testing night vision binoculars that I had just purchased. I was looking at some deer in the meadow when I saw the orb to the west. At first I thought it was an aircraft but there were no red/green lights, collision lights, blinking lights. It was solid amber colored, very bright, about 1000 feet up. It seemed to hover a little then continue on a straight path to the southeast. The video is from my nightvision binoculars. I apologize it isn't the best quality.
