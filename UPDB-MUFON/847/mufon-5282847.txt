Glowing orb that moved in all directions quickly then became idle and pulsed.

A multicolored orb caught my attention when I went outside to my truck to grab paperwork at 3:15 am on January 30 2022. Stopped and watched it for about 5 mins and thought it had to be a star reflecting oddly with the atmosphere, I then noticed it to move to the north a considerable distance. As I was leaning on my truck and had a silhouette of a tree as reference I could notice erratic movement's and that's when I went inside and grabbed my phone. I went back outside and struggled to find it again until it appeared to just be dormant and started to glow brighter, I started to film again and took a short video, picture, and screen shot of the time and date. It was odd and closer to the ground than any of the other visible stars normally in that direction at this time of the morning which I am often getting ready for the day. In the video the white specs that pop up and dissappear are not part of the sighting but a side affect of filming in the dark which I checked after I took the video. The lights seemed to either move closer and in all directions, it was also pulsing and would be barley visible at times while it was still. The movements were faster but fluid and not jumpy or terribly far from its original position.
