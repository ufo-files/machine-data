We saw a bright light at Moon Rise

Me and my girlfriend (I was driving) were moving East bound on Northwest Territories Highway 5 before Wood Buffalo National park to Fort Smith, NWT. We observed Moon Rise, shortly after Moon rise when the moon was above the horizon a strong light appeared to the right of the moon slightly below it. It was a large bright light glowing white in color. and looked far off in the distance. 

The sky was clear and dark and there were no planes visible or any other lights from civilization. 

I initially remarked to my Girlfriend to look at Jupiter. It looks very bright! We had been looking at Jupiter a few days before because it was prominent in the sky and it didn't seem like it was in the right patch of sky. She checked the SkyView application on her phone and Jupiter wasn't even close, it didn't identify the object as being anything. We kept driving east wondering what it was. The object was stationary and then it just vanished right out of existence and was gone. We both wondered what we saw.
