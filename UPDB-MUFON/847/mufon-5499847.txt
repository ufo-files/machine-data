I was cleaning my glasses due to a drizzle rain while riding my bicycle on the bike trail. Looked up to see if they were clear the object was in my line of sight. I was paralyzed with awe. I saw a disc shaped object then the object quicly took off fast. Noiseless and it had small unrecognizable letter style markings a lighter grey color. As it moved away a blue colored fire like burst could be seen on the opposite side of the direction of movement of the object.it went south and very high in the sky until i could not track it with my eyes anymore.
