Very bright light changing colors, moving jerky left to right like a puppet on a string

The light was very bright and would dim at times. Blue and pink colors were visible as well. I watched this through glass doors with framed panes so I could tell when the object went left, right, up, or down judged by the distance from the wood frame. It crossed over into a pane to the left the went up to the top row of 3 panes moving again to the left and back to the right. Eventually it moved up and out of sight. There was a full moon that night as well. The object hovered more than moved and seemed to be fairly high up in the sky. When it did move it had jerky motions like being on a string and jerked. I did not get up to get my phone to record because I was afraid I would lose sight of it and I was fascinated just watching it. I could not see a form, only light that was very bright.
