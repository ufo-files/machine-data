{dr}Silent glowing orange red object gliding through the night sky

Camping on site 36 at McGinnins campground in Holly, MI I saw an identical object, but did not have my camera. 
I watched and saw it again. Those 2 times it was moving from the south to the north west. My husband went in the camper, I kept watching and it suddenly appeared over the tree tops above our camper. I yelled it is back and he stepped out - the object stopped making no sound but the dogs at a near by camp site were howling like crazy! As I was filming, you can see the flight pattern with stopping, going, up down, etc. about 50 above the trees.
I am not sure if I saw 3 different or 1 object that returned as there was no more than 1 object together at a time.
I filmed it but the film does not give it justice - the object was glowing red orange, egg or coned shape.
We watched for it the night of 9-3-12 but were not able to see anything other than regular planes.
We lost sight of the object as it flew northwest and away from us.
