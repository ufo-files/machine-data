It was approx 1990. I was reading in my backyard and paused to look up looked up. This structure, shaped like a fat cigar, came across the sky..approx SE traveling NE. It was approx. 35-40 feet in length and maybe a. bit more.feet up. It was going about 20 mph.

There was no Sound. The cigar shape was bloated in the middle. It was matte grey with no marketing. Since this was 1990, Drones were not known to the general public. This did remind of a drone.

I apologize for no photos...It all occurred with no time to get my camara.

I didn't report this to the US program which takes care of these reports until about 3 months later. I talked to a female Air Force Officer in Washington D.C.

We had a big laugh about this but she took my report.
