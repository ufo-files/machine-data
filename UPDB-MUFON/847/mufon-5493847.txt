I was sat at home and a tubular object appeared in the room, dog barked, I watched as it became invisible and left.

I was in my front room watching TV, i looked up to the corner of my room to see a tubular object about 2 feet in diameter seemingly hovering just below the ceiling. On first sight I had no clue what I was looking at, but my dog was very nervous and scared of the object. The object seemed to have smaller disks attached to it, but it wasn't spinning or full of flashing lights, it was black in colour and it just sat there appearing to watch. It never made me feel scared of it just highly curious although as I say my dog was very uncomfortable. After I watched it and it seemed to watch me for what seemed like eternity but in reality was around 5-7mins it then shimmered and became see through, I could still see its outline but I could see the wall and ceiling behind it, this was very odd and it was about now that I got very nervous to what I was looking at and after a few moments in this condition it just seemed to lift and go through my ceiling, a solid object travelled through another solid object, not right. After it left I noticed some slight marks on my ceiling where it went through.
