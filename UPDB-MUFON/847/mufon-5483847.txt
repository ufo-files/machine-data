{rjl} Triangle clocked in cloud-like substance

We were driving to begin vacation. Received phone call from doctor's office to pick up med so we turned onto a road off 500 block of NE 27th in Moore, Oklahoma in order to turn around to go to pharmacy. While waiting to make turn we saw a triangle just "hanging" in the sky to our one o'clock. All other clouds in sky were thin cirrus type, stretching from horizon to horizon. Triangle appeared to be hiding in it's own cloud. See photo. We drove the 4 miles to pharmacy south on Eastern street. I watched out the window as my husband drove. It never moved nor changed it's appearance. As soon as we parked I got my Kindle out to take photographs. It was a bright sunny day. It was difficult to get the Kindle to shoot directly west since it was about 4:15 p.m. and though sun wasn't directly in view, it isn't easy to work a Kindle anyway photographing. Since it was so bright, I have included a photo where I upped the contrast and turned down the brightness. Other than that I didn't do anything else. After 30 minutes went by and nothing moved, I went inside pharmacy to get medicine. When I came out it was gone.
