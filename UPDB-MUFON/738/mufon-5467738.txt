{td} Object hovered east of PDX airport for 20mins before slowly moving infront on sun. I waited for a few minutes but object never became visable again.

On the morning of the sighting I was driving to portland airport PDX when I observed a object in the sky near the sun which appeared to be hovering in place. I have been doing this courier route daily for many years and have seen all types of aircraft, commercial and military none with this shape or behaviour. After a few minutes of observing I stopped to observe the object which was disc shaped and a mile or so away. Usually at this location i watch aircraft land or take off and sometimes aircraft look as if they are shaped different or moving slowly as they desend to the airport but this was definetly different. After roughly 20 minutes the object suddenly moved in front of the sun where i could not see. After waiting for about 10 minutes i got back on the road to work. I was unable to take pictures do to its proximity to the sun and my flip phone at the time had a horrible camera. I believe this was a ufo and was deliberately hiding in front of the sun in my opinion.
