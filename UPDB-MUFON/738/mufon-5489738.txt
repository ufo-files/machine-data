{jpg} I saw three hovering saucers in the AM sitting on my deck watching the sun rise

I was sitting on my deck drinking coffee waiting for the sun to rise on August 10, 2015 AM looking northeast from Grangeville Idaho toward the mountains of Kookia Idaho. As the sun was rising I saw one large saucer hovering over the town of Kooskia Idaho and then two other smaller saucers joined the big one hovering and reflecting the morning sunlight. Very bright with white/silver light. The smaller saucers would dart around the large saucer that stayed in one place. As the sun rose behind the mountains it reflected on the saucers and then they disappeared. They were right in the line of the sun and I couldn't see them anymore because I couldn't look directly into the sun. Maybe they were still there but couldn't see them because of the bright sunlight in my eyes. There was a big thunderstorm a few days later that started huge fires in the area around Kamiah idaho.
