{rjl} main huge light .Changed shape to triangle .Went back to one main light. Vanished when light went out.

Friend and I sat out on General store porch late at night talking. Noticed huge light on north side of Hahn's peak.{lived at base of this mountain in gold mining camp village named columbine cabins}. Light got huge and brilliant almost at top of mountain. Split into several smaller lights and formed a huge triangle on the top part of the whole mountain very quickly. Thought impossible to be any group because late at night, extremely dark, and very dangerous and impossible for anyone to run night or day at speed the lights formed the triangle. Had climbed the mountain before and talace rock very slippery and steep where the lights were at during the duration of event. After about 4 min. the light went back to center of the triangle forming a huge ball of light once again, stayed round and very bright for a minute or so and light went out quickly. Saw one of your hanger 1 shows on tv. Never knew who to report to before seeing your show.
