{jd} Large Bright glowing red object hovering then took off in seconds

When out in my backyard tonight to let my dog out when I observed a large bright red-orange object in the eastern sky. More red then orange. No sound. I believe I saw two but one was further away from the other. It was large, bright, flickering and seemed to be floating/hovering. I called my husband to come out and see (he was in the house) and when I did, the object started to move east and then took off in a matter of seconds. My hubby did see it and was like....what was that! Well it's not a plane or helicopter.
