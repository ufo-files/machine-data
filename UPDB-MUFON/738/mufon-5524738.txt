Two sets of 3 triangle lights of a halogen blue-whte color that trailed each other turning in an arcing motion then seperated, one dissapeared then the other disappeared . they were at about the height at which a large airplane flies.

I was returning from fishing in the Discovery Bay, CA area and received a call from my cousing at 8:34 PM. I was driving my truck when my cousing who was also driving called on the cell phone and told me to look at the sky to the north. I looked at the sky and was stunned because of the unusual shapes, brightness and speed of the lights in the sky. I pulled the truck over and stopped to get a better look. I took out my phone too get pictures. I took several pictures but the resolution was poor and I was not satisfied with the shots. We observed two sets of three triangle shaped lights that were in the shape of a triangle for 2 1/2 minutes before one group of three seperated and disappeared , then the other group of three followed and dissappeared. I have never witnessed anything like this before and decided to call 911. I gave them my report. I called my famliy to tell them what I saw because I was so impacted and excited about what I witnessed.
