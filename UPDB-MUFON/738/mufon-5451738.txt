bright white object in the air

I was outside my front door which faces West. I was observing my hummingbirds and when I followed a bird flying up that is when I notices this round object stationary in the sky. I was able to run to my garage and get my binoculars and I viewed it closer and it was like a blimp. at first I thought it was a blimp but it did not move nor did it have the same shape like the blimps we know. I followed it as it moved slowly toward my direction and it just gone!
