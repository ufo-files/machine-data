Woman in my dream and bedroom

I went to bed at my normal time on Sunday night around 830 pm. I fell asleep no issue which is somewhat uncommon for me. I don't remember falling asleep at all. While I was sleeping I did not dream at all, but then I had a dream suddenly out of nowhere. In my dream I was sleeping in my bed the same position as I always do. Every detail of the bedroom was EXACTLY the same as if I was awake. There was a woman kneeling next to the edge of the bed talking to me. But she was not speaking to me like in a regular conversation but instead she was speaking to me in my head. We spoke for approximately 15 to 20 minutes. I don't remember exactly what we were talking about but we for sure had an in depth conversation. I remember that she looked half human half not from this planet. And she was wearing what looked like a wig. Her hair was not real. Then I believe she told me I was at home and to wake up. When I woke I was in my bed laying in the same position and every detail of the room was still the same. Although when I woke up the woman was still kneeling at the edge of my bed looking at me. After a few seconds she said goodbye without speaking and disappeared. I was not afraid of the woman nor did I feel uncomfortable around here but felt calm. After the woman left I went back to sleep without any issues.

This is my most recent encounter with what I believe to be an extraterrestrial being. I have had several other encounters in the past 6 to 7 months that I would like to be able to share with you. I am looking for answers as to whether these are in fact extraterrestrial encounters or not. If possible can someone from your organization please reach out to me at your earliest convenience.
