Noticed odd

Sat at home in my bedroom. Out of my window (in 7 floors up in a tower block) I spotted something reasonably high up, but lower than aircraft. I initially thought it was a bit of cardboard lifted in the wind, due to its slightly odd shape. I kept my eye on it and realised it was a little weirder, seemingly spinning. Second instinct was a large balloon. That is what I think it probably is as it made no especially sharp turns, but I don't know. It was odd enough for me to film on my iPhone and submit here. I zoomed in to film, which may have caused some distortion on the footage. It just caught my eye and maybe if others saw it and caught it on camera it would back up those accounts. The object curved away into the distance, possibly simply moving with the wind. If only I had binoculars!
