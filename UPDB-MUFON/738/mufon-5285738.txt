UFO Caught By Security Officer

On June 10th, 2019, while working security for a sight I no longer work at, I caught this UFO on camera. At first, I thought it was a star. That is until I saw the star on this video moving around very erratically. 

I originally uploaded it to YouTube in hope that someone would see it. However, at the insistence of a friend, I am sending this video to you. 

The voice on the video is mine. At first, I was freaked out, but now looking back, I wish I could have seen more of this UFO.
