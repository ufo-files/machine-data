Two objects circling con trail from hypersonic vehicle

I noticed a con trail in the sky that seemed to be very similar to one photographed of a pulsed ram jet engine. It was odd that the trail was so fresh from horizon to horizon and had not dissipated. While lookng along the contrail i noticed two objects that were rotating the trail and circling each other in a clockwise motion.i watched the objects at a high rate of speed change direction twice before they were not visible over the easternmost horizon.
