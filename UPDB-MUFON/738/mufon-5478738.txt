Pulsating light of green and blue observed over houses. Rapid movement. I am still looking at it

I was outside my front door having come back from taking my partner to work. I looked over to check the car central locking had worked and just glanced up at the sky and saw a green light just above the houses, only a small dot of a light. It looked like the front or back of a plane or helicopter light, but as soon as i fixed on it, it shot up into the sky, it then made movements like the figure of 8 and then came back down rapidly. Its sequence seemed to be random. In making the figure of 8 it seems to flick between blue and green light in no set pattern.

I am feeling pretty nervous as i am still observing this object and it is now very high in the sky and is stationary.it is pulsating blue and green.

It is a cold cloudless night and i can see the stars. 

A few cars have driven past me but i dont have any witnesses to this event.
