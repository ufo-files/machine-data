Saw Bright white light being followed by smaller orageish light at same speed from west sky to east by south east sky

I was outside having a smoke and happened to look up at a magpie in the tree above me, I saw what looked like a star at first but noticed it was moving. So I went out on front patio to get a better look because I thought I saw a smaller light following it. And there were 2 lights, a bright star sized one and a smaller one that looked like a jet but with no blinking lights. I called my wife to come and check it out and she saw it immediately because of how they were both moving at the exact same speed in a strait line. We watched it until they both just instantly disappeared. My wife was saying did you see that, they both just vanished. I said ya, that was awesome. No vapour trial, no sound, and it was a calm and quiet and cool night. There should have been something. I came in right away and started to see if maybe it was a satellite or something that someone else has seen before. There were others on earlier dates that described the exact same thing. So now I am here telling you what we saw. We are not nuts, we both saw the same things at the same time. I work for Shell as an instrumentation mechanic and my wife is a nurse and instructor for the U of L.
