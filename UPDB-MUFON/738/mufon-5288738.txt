A Multi Colored Craft flashing and hovering in Video

I was living in Fowler,Ca in 2014 and it was after midnight I lived in the country surrounded by grape vineyards and I could see a large open night sky out in the distance a large flashing light caught my eye. So I video taped this craft through the telescope to get a closer look. To my surprise I saw what was a huge multi colored craft hovering had to be a good hour it looked like it changed shape as well. This was during the Summer in the middle of August so it was a beautiful clear starry night. it was far away and i didn't hear anything. After awhile i thought I had enough video and went to bed.I was the only Witness and the object was in the NE
