{rjl} several silver/gray orbs

Noticed them when I turned onto 28th street. Looked like they were attached to power lines. I continued to observed them as I traveled down 24th street. Each time I was able to look up the number and position of the objects was different. The size of the objects didn't really appear larger as I approached. 

They are just above and around of the mountain, You really have to zoom in to see them.

When I stopped to take my first picture I pointed them out to a lady walking her dog.

I also told the receptionist in my dentist's office when I arrived. They were gone after my appointment so I don't know how long they were there or how they left.
