Giant luminous and snaking fireball.

It appeared to be a fireball leaving a trail of sparks several hundred feet long behind it except that it moved up and down changing direction every 3 or 4 seconds definitely not a meteor. The trail would also linger for about 10 seconds. It could also disappear and reapear instantly and was joined by a second similar object before both finally went away. It appeared almost alive like a giant snake made of sparks flying thru the sky. It appeared to be centered around the south east face of Green Mountain in Lakewood. There was a chopper with a search light on in the air around the area about 5-10 minutes after the objects disappeared. My first job was at an airport and I've never seen anything like this before, very unusual.
