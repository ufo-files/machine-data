Told wife, let's follow it. We all got back in car. As I was backing out of driveway, told wife, I'll drive you tell me which way to go. I'm divorced now ,but ex will still agree with what we saw. Daughter still remembers also. Remember telling wife (was working in Naval Shipyard at time) the center fuselage was larger then aircraft carrier that was working on.
