Two triangula craft with white/blue lights.and three circular motors, one in each corner. Out of this world stuff. Beautifull to see

I don't smoke in the house so if i want a cigarette i go outside into the back garden.My garden is approx 30 meters long and has a raised railway embankment at the bottom of it.It was a clear night and as i looked up over the embankment i saw something that i can only describe as out of this world.Hovering in the night sky were two UFO's. Triangular in shape one just in front of the other, the rear one just to the left of the one in front.Thy were very illuminated with ice white light on the upper and lower sides of the craft and a vivid blue band of lights that separated the upper from the lower part's of the craft. On the underside of the two crafts i observed three circular power motors. They were glowing in a redish orange glow.I estimated the distance from myself and the craft to be around three hundred meters at the most.And around five hundred meters off the ground.I have never seen anything as beautiful in my life.Truly breath taking.Just above the blue band of lights i could see a line of windows but could not see inside them.As i watched them in the night sky they started to move in formation just to the right over my head. There was no noise what so ever.They moved slowly towards the west still at the same height.As they passed over head they looked more pyramid shaped as though they had sat up instead of lying flat.I then lost sight of them as they moved behind the house.If these craft were of our world then i would be proud of what we had engineered.But in all honesty,They were truly awe inspiring craft to behold. I feel very privileged to have seen them.Every evening now i keep an eye out for others.
