{cak} Started out as a metallic ball right above where we could see the sun, reflected light from it, changed shape several times to a cylinder like shape and such before disappearing

I was in my early days of high school, and our bus was on the way to the school. Me and practically the entire bus saw a metallic orb right above where we could see the sun rising though it could have only been a mile or two above ground, and began to morph into a vertical cylinder-shape with pointed ends, then switched to a horizontal shape, then was suddenly gone like it had darted away. We all saw it, about twelve of us. None of us knew what it was though me and two other people quickly decided it wasn't anything we'd ever seen before.
