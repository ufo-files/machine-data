saw a large v shaped object in the sky

i was leaving my neighbors house - going home. saw a light in the sky - thought it could be a star. stopped to see if it moved - turned out to be a plane ( we have many in our area) something to my left (in the sky) caught my eye - turned out to be (whatever) it was a huge v shaped vehicle with lighted windows on the outer perimeter (not all were lit). ran in my house to rouse my husband (he was in the shower) ran back to my neighbors to get my friend. by the time we got back out - the craft was out of sight. it did not move at a ridiculously high rate of speed (but then, i do not know how high it was). i just know that it was "huge" and moving from west to east with not all lights on. i would just like to know if anyone else saw something like this in the night sky on 12/27/2009.
