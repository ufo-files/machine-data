{dr}Multi-level craft that seemed to gradually appear that was V shaped with no wings then gradually disappeared as if it was clocking.

This event occurred in April 2013 at approximately 2 PM. Total duration of event was about 15 to 20 seconds. While traveling south on South Durango toward Blue Diamond I was stopped due to road construction and a red light on Pebble Avenue. My attention was drawn to the sky as I thought a commercial aircraft was making an approach to land at Las Vegas airport. The sky was clear in the area but scattered clouds were around. As I observed the craft it was approaching from the West and appeared to be appearing out of thin air until I could see the entire craft  It was a multi level craft (V shaped, no wings) approximately 200 yards in length. It appeared to have four or five levels. It was a dull dark gray color that seemed to absorb light. There was no reflective material on the craft. It was flying in a straight line due East. There was no sound eminating from the craft. After about eight seconds it began to disappear back in to thin air as if it was clocking until it disappeared. During the visual it maintained a constant speed as if approaching for a landing, but it was not on the same course or altitude as jets take when landing from the West. The craft began to appear from front to back and then disappear from front to back. I am only reporting this now because I was not sure I rally saw what I saw, but the details have vividly stuck with me since the sighting. As far as I know there were no other reports of this event.
