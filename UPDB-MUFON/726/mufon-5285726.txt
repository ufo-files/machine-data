Orange glowing ball of light, probably about 40 ft in diameter.

About 23:30 I was in bed watching TV, from my bed I can see quite a large portion of the sky. I noticed a large orange glowing ball of light zoom from my left to right, travelling north to south, very fast, it was gone and out of sight within 1 to 2 seconds, I jumped out of bed to see if I could see it on the horizon but it was gone. I had a distinct feeling like I would see it again, for some reason. It was about 200ft - 300ft in the air, my window was open and it made no noise at at all. One thing that stood out instantly was how smooth it seemed to be moving, as if it was in a vacuum, silky smooth, but very fast. I felt like a rush of adrenalin, as if I knew that was not something explainable. It would have travelled about 0.5 a mile to the west of Leeds Bradford Airport, so may be on their radar records. I would be really interested to know?
