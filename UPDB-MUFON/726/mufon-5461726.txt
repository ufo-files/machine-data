{rjl} Looked like a star but flattened on the edges, very much classic flying saucer shape. Lights radiating from bottom then it flew out.

I was looking to the north at the Big Dipper and noticed a star that looked different. I’m outside almost every night to cool off from hot flashes! I realized it wasn’t a star when lights radiated out of the bottom. Not really bright but def not a plane. White lights, and then the object moved left and then right. Not a severe move or big. It hovered above Taos Mountain on the Pueblo then it drew out. Flew out. Sorry don’t remeber the term. Lasted about 3 minutes. I couldn’t take my eyes off of it.
