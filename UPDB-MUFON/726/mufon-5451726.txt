slowly moving shiny sphere

Object first noticed to naked eye as a slowly moving white speck against a clear blue afternoon sky. Through 10X50 binoculars object appeared to be a shiny chromium blue sphere about half the size of a marble. On the west facing surface it showed a bright round highlight reflection of the sun. The highlight covered approx. 1/4 of the sphere's surface on the side facing the sun. This contributed to the appearance of the object as a solid shiny surfaced sphere. The object moved slowly, smoothly and silently in a westerly direction in a straight line, right to left in my field of vision. As it did so, the sun's highlight periodically grew smaller and then larger as if the object were changing shape slightly, going back and forth between a perfect sphere to a slight egg shape. Not being able to hold the binoculars perfectly still, I'm not positive this was the case reason for changes in the sun's highlight. After a minute or so, the object went behind the roof line of the patio I was sitting on. I got up to change position and then I couldn't find the object again.
