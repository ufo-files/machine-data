{rjl} me and a witnessed a large triangular shape craft with lights on the bottom of it float above us completely soundless

Me and one of my friends was leaving his house to walk to the store, as soon as we walked a few feet from his house I looked behind us and saw this thing coming our direction it was very large triangular shape and it had lights on the bottom of it it made no noise and it's like it was floating very low on a straight path. When it was directly above us I can remember like it was yesterday my friend said "what the heck is that"? I said I have no idea and floated on a straight path until we couldn't see it anymore.
