{jpg} Sphere floated over me, then performed aerial maneuvers that defy physics

I was standing in my driveway smoking when I felt very odd and my hair stood up. I heard dogs barking all over the place. I looked above me and noticed a red/orange sphere about 3,000 feet above me. I initially thought it was a satellite. I then realized it was far to low. I watched it glide over the town of Milo. Once it reached the edge of town, it dimmed down to the brightness of a star and started zig-zagging through the sky. I watched it then shoot faster than anything I have ever seen straight up. A black helicopter from nowhere appeared and circled Milo for 15 minutes. Once the helicopter left the area, it zoomed back by and flared up for a second as it travelled by at an amazing speed. I stood outside for 10 minutes processing what I just saw.
