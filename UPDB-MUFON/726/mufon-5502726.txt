{cak} Looked like a rapid fast moving star that stopped and hoovered.

It was either 1970 or 1971, I'm just not sure anymore since it's been over 40 years since we seen the UFO. We were parked at a park like young people do sometimes, we were in the car. I remember looking towards the south and noticed an object I thought was a star in the sky, but this star moved. I was amazed at the quick movement and how it flew from east to west in a matter of seconds then stopped in mid-air. I remember asking my boyfriend at the time, what is that? We watched it for several minutes so it seemed. It hoovered, it flew to the east within seconds, then it went up within seconds, then back down hoovering in the sky at each direction it flew. Then within a second it vanished, ascending, we didn't see it anymore. My boyfriend said "that was a UFO" and I totally agree to this day. A helicopter doesn't travel at speeds like that. If I could estimate the total length it flew in a second I would say 30 to 50 miles, then stopped in mid-air. Of course people think your nuts when you tell them this, but you know I don't care. We know what we saw that clear night long ago. And to this day I know it wasn't a helicopter, airplane or search light. I've tried looking in the Bible for similiar things because I want answers too. I really do not remember the feelings I had when I saw it but I wasn't scared at all just amazed. I've always wondered if anyone else ever reported a UFO at that same time. We did not report it, who would listen anyway. Thank you for listening.
