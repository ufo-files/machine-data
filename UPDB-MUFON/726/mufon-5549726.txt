Brief sighting during foggy weather of a gray disc

As I was driving east on highway 422, enroute to a conference with a Mufonrepresentative, approximately two miles from the Rt. 306 exit, a grey elipticalobject traveled across the freeway in front of me, moving north to south. Ionly ovserved it for a second or two, when it was directly in front of me andout about a quarter mile. It moved in a straight line about 200 feet off theground, dissapearing behind a wooded hill in the fog. Upon reaching the spotwhere I had seen it dissapear, the area was a small wooded valley, but nothingcould be seen to the south except fog shrouded trees. The object wassilhouetted against a distant hill as it moved south through the mist. Nosounds or other effects were noticed. It was reported to the Mufon personell Ilater talked to should there be another sighting that could be colaborated.
