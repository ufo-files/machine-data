Slow moving orange yellow glow

My family and I pulled up for a BBQ, when I looked up out of impulse and noticed a yellow orange glow moving north north east, almost over the ocean. I though It may have been a satellite because it didn't flash or pulse like a plane and looked lower than a plane, I also though it could of been a satellite because of the unblinking light but it moved too fast to be one as my step father pointed out. We stood there and watched it for a few minutes, it dissappeared once then reappeared. Then we went inside to our friends house.I tried to film it or take a photo on my iphone but the image turned out black possible because of the street light near us.
