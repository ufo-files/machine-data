I was driving. Looking for parking, when I looked up and saw a few twinkling lights hovering together. Very right. Reddish orange, but light. I pulled over and got out of the car to see better. More and more appeared, about 10-12 total I think. They kind of floated toward the left, not in a line or in a soecificformationbut definitely together. I watched for a minute or two, called my husband to see what he thought it might be. Then one by one they disappeared when they reached a certain point to the left where they were headed. It was silent. It was too coordinated to be lanterns but that's what they seemed like. Drones? I've never seen one. Why would so many drones be flying together? I have ever seenanything like these lights. Can't stop thinking about the event. It happened about 2 hours ago.
