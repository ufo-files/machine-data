About 1962 I would have been 10, sleeping outside with 2 friends. We were in a camper shell, looked up and this huge saucer shaped object was hovering over my friends house. It had an orange glow and panels or doors all around it.. Stayed for maybe 2min. the took off to the north, out of sight then came back over us just for a second then went up and gone very fast.
