At roughly 10:00 am I walked out to my barn to let our chickens out for the day. I walked out of the barn and looked at the sky as I was getting ready to leave for work. I noticed a very slow spinning triangular shaped object to the east. It had the same kind of reflectivity as mylar or chrome. It was only visible for about 5 seconds before disappearing into a cloud. I stood and waited for it to come out the other side but it never did. It didn't move like a cluster of balloons just a straight path into the cloud. It was so reflective I could barely make it out against the sky but I could see 3 distinct corner.s I can't give an accurate description of size as it was too far away and at cloud height. It was moving north to south. I didn't think it was any other object but a ufo. I wish I would have had time to snap a pic but I don't think you would have been able to make it out because of how shiny it was. After a few minutes of waiting I gave up and went on with my day.
