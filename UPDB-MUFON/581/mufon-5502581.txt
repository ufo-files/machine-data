Two orange-yellow balls of light

I was out last night watching a few fireworks with my kids near a building I work at and then two lights about 10 minutes apart went on a straight line path from South-West to North-East. I had my two kids with me and I said they were Chinese lanterns. I took three pictures of what I could but they disappeared from my sight once they headed North-East. They were just going over slowly and then going off in the distance.
