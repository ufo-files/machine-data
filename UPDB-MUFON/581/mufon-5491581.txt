{dr}Military Instalation - UFO following me

I was driving home when I looked up in the sky and saw a UFO about 1000ft in the sky. It was following me. When I first saw the UFO I knew it wasn't something normal. The flight patterns were anything we are capable of today. My husband was in the military and he taught me about military aircraft and ground vehicles. This UFO was something else. When I turned into my subdivision on base the object started descending until it was right on top of my car.i just stopped right there. I couldn't make out the UFO fully but it gave off the the brightest light. My car had stopped and I was frozen. What I remember next I being extremely scared. I raced home. When I was in my drive way I was scared that "they" were coming for me. But I made a dash for the house and once inside I locked the door and told my husband about it.
