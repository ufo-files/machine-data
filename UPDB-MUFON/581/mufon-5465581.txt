{dr}Saw extremely bright light for a few seconds then disappeared.

I was driving north in my car coming back from picking up dinner when I saw an extremely bright light hivering in one spot. I pointed it out to my wife and she saw it as well. I turned east and lost sight of it in a matter of 2 or 3 seconds when I turned north into my driveway. I kept looking around for the light when we noticed an airplane east of the location we saw the bright light. My wife stated that maybe it was the airplane we had seen but you could clearly see that what we saw was only a single light as opposed to the 2 white lights and the green and red blinking lights also on the airplane. Also the plane was at a whole different degree and traveling southeast and was not stationary. The 2 white airplane lights were also dull looking compared to the bright light we saw.
