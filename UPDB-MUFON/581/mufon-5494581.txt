{rjl} ORANGE LIGHT IN SKY

I was on my porch in Los Angeles and noticed a bright orange light in the West skies. I grabbed my phone to take photos and videos. It was perfectly between two trees in my view. I thought it was the moon but it was too low to be the moon. It had an amber color shadow and started descending and moving North West. It was weird. It looked bloody red, orange and black. It lasted about 4 minutes.
