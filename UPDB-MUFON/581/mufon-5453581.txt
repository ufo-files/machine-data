{rjl} 7 white dots in sky directly overhead traveling in straight line from S/W to N/E direction, no sound, appeared to be over 2000 ft high.

While working as a DOD contractor for Northrop Grumman at the National Training Center at Fort Irwin Ca. I was at one of the military motor pools on base when approx. 7 white dots or objects appeared directly overhead, they were against a clear blue sky around midday in July 2009. This was over the Garrison at The National Training Center at Fort Irwin, California. These dots or objects were in a perfect line traveling from S/W to N/E in direction. While watching them I saw two dots emerge out of one of the center dots each one traveling alongside (right and left) of the center dot they came out of, making a sort of cross shape. They continued in the same direction then after approx two seconds the two objects that came out of the middle object went back into the middle object and all of the objects continued across the sky in an N/E direction until out of sight which was only for a few more seconds. There were two US Army soldiers approx. ten feet from me when this happened. I yelled to them and said, "did you guys see that", while I was pointing up. They both responded by saying yes and following with, "you see some strange things out here".
