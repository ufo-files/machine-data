While observing a strange star-like object, I saw a disc shaped, gigantic ship with white & yellow lights beaming off the sides. The ship came straight down from way up high in the sky to my eye level. I rushed inside to get my camera

While staying at a hotel in North Carolina, I went outside to take my dogs for a walk. I was observing a strange, star-like object in the sky when I saw a very large disc-shaped ship come down, apparently, from that star. It had huge beaming yellow and white lights from all sides and it was descending very slowly in a straight path down until it was at my eye level. After watching this I went inside the hotel room, let my dogs in, got my camera and went back outside. I wanted a picture of it. To my dissapointment, it was gone. So I decided to take pictures of the strange star. When I looked at the pictures on my computer I was amazed that I had indeed taken a picture, unknowingly of what I believe to be that disc-shaped ship. Although, it was completely invisible to the naked eye, the camera was able to pick up on it. I went outside again and took more pictures and I was able to get 2 more photographs of it. Look in between the trees for a solid yellow, half-circle, 3 dimentional shape on the first picture. Please note that the digtal pictures attatched to this report are raw and have not been cropped or edited.
