3 foot bar of light appeared and set off motion security camera. 5 minutes later an entity appeared hovering on our deck setting off the camera.

On December 31st 2020 my wife and I were watching TV. We just put up motion activated security cameras we had got as a Christmas gift. We got a notification that something set off the camera. My wife looked and there was a 3 foot bar of light hovering in our backyard. We both thought it was weird but just kind of laughed and dismissed it. 5 minutes later we got another notification. This time something was on our back deck. You could see a face, it was weird. The camera was zoomed out and it looked like an animal, but something wasn't right. it just appeared without approaching, then even more strange it completely disappears after 3 seconds without moving. A few days after i was standing on my deck at night. I looked at where the shadow was in the video and where the object in question had to have been to create that shadow. It was right in the middle of my deck. There's no way it was a racoon or some kind of animal standing on a railing. I had be hovering or standing directly in the middle of the deck. This means the being was around 5 feet tall or was hovering. The shadow proves the whole thing. Theres no animal that stands upright 5 feet tall and vanishes without moving. I have seen ufo's several times but never any entities that i can remember. My wife forgot to save the video of the bar of light but we saved the entity appearing and disappearing.
