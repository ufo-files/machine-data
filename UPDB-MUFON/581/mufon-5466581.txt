{cak} Four or five lights took formation in a straight line

Me and my cousin was in my yard. I was watching for ufos.

When I first seen them I thought it was what I call a satellite. 
But then I see more and they are coming from different directions.
They join up at a fixed point where one more was waiting not moving at all.

When they got close to the one not moving. It started moving and they all fell in behind of it. 
They flew in a straight line for the most part. I kept my eyes on them until someone turned on my porch lights and blinded me.. there was four or five of them.
