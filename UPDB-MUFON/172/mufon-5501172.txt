{nd} Orange/Red orbs hovering and traveling east bound, turned into 1 orb and back into 2 orbs twice

Party of 3 saw 2 reddish/orange orbs coming from the southwest sky from back patio about 9:45pm. We were sitting on back patio talking when fiance spotted the 2 orbs. I immediately knew that this was some type of UFO as we live by 2 airports and a major police agency, we see all sizes of planes and helicopters at all times of the day and night. These orbs were like nothing I have ever seen before. They appeared out of the sky bright and clearly visible to the naked eye. The orbs hovered and changed direction frequently, one would hover above the other or move the side of the other. Twice the orbs became 1 orb and then split back into 2 orbs. The sighting lasted about 20 minutes. 1 orb then got smaller until disentigrating into the sky (almost looked like traveling higher into the atmosphere and at a fast speed) until no light could be seen. The remaining orb continued to travel in an easternly direction until also disentigrating, then moving fast higher into the atmosphere until it could no longer be seen. No sound, this area is surrounded by 2 airports (Chino and Ontario) and near a freeway, with a lot of helicopter activity due to major police agency near by. There was no sound, my fiance and I along with my nephew were mesmerized, we could not even run into the house to get our phones to record or take a picture. There was an airplane traveling west that had to have also seen the orbs, it should have also come up on the 2 airports airspace radars.
