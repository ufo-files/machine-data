{nd} driving north bound on street ans saw bright light in the sky

I went to a friends house and as I was driving back home I was going northbound on 29th street in McAllen Texas and as I was driving back I looked up about 60 degrees from my car front windshield and I saw a bright object with a star like feature at about 9:45 pm. The object maneuvered smooth and swiftly and descended going into a parabola motion and suddenly disappeared. As a result of that sight I felt excited and shocked thus had to concentrate on not getting into an accident.
