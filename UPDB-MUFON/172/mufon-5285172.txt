May have been a Stealth Bomber

Looking up into night sky observing stars when a large mass flew over my house. it looked like an aircraft shape but had no lights, made no sound, and was gray enough to stand out in the sky. It was moving slow enough that I could turn and track it from one end of our property to when it was out of sight.
