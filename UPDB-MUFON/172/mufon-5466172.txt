On August 18th this year (2018) i went to Carowinds theme park just outside of Charlotte, NC with my daughter. On the way into the park i was taking some pics of the roller coasters. The pics were taken just as the park was opening around 10 - 10:30am so no-one was riding any of the rides yet. A couple of days later i was showing my pics to some of my colleagues and noticed some very VERY strange images. I did NOT see anything other than the roller coasters while taking these pics. As i zoomed in on the pics, the first image that caught my eye was the cloaked cigar shapped image behind the clouds. As I zoomed in I begin to notice the white smaller objects around. The images I have attached are zoomed in screen shots. The window on my car was rolled up and we were in stop and go traffic. The second image shows what looks like an object begining to take off. Again when i first took these pics i could NOT see any of the objects described above. I am open to any feedback as to what you feel these might be.
