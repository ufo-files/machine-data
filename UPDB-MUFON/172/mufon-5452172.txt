8' TO 10' DIAMETER SILVER ORB SEEN OFF OF DECK

SILVER ORB TRAVELING APPROXIMATELY 90 MPH.L HEADED FROM EAST TO WEST IN THE WRIGHTWOOD CANYON. TRAVELING AS IF IT WAS ON A RAILROAD TRACK, STRAIGHT LINE, ALL OF THE SNOW COVERED TREE TOPS COULD BE SEEN REFLECTING ON THE BOTTOM OF THE SILVER ORB. 

MOST LIKELY WOULD NOT HAVE BEEN SEEN IF IT WASN'T FOR THE SNOW AND GREEN PINE TREE CONTRAST. THIS BECOMING MORE VISIBLE AS THE CRAFT TRAVELED ALONG ITS ROUTE. SEEMED TO BE SURVEYING THE AREA.
