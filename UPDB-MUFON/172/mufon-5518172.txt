pulstaing lights

was on the way to my mothers house to drop some things off when my wife noticed in the sky a huge ball of light moving toward our direction from the south west. when it got closer to us i could see that it had many different colored lights that ran around the craft. it was either triangular or the shape of a boomerang. the craft was between 500 and 1000 in the air. it came at our area from the south west and headed toward the north east. when it was over head i could hear what sounded like 5 or 6 jet engines and they made a loud roaring sound. my wife tried to grab the camera but it did not want to work. then my cb radio started going crazy getting louder as the thing went over us. the lights on our truck went dim and almost died out. when i noticed this in the air i tried to raise a friend of mine on the cb but could not get anyone.
