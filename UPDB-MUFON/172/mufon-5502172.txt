{dr}Dull red glowing object observered just above the treetops

My family returned home and I was removing some things from the trunk of the car when I noticed a star like object, at a high altitude, traveling from south to north. I immediately went into the house to retrieve my camera. After capturing photos of this object I sat on the front stairs awaiting any similar type object(s). Several minutes later I observed what appeared to be an almost cross shaped object slightly descend from the western sky. I quickly began to take pictures of this dull red object as it tracked across the sky seemingly just above the tree line going from west to northeast.
