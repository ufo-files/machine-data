walking on sidewalk on Boggsford road just happened to look up at the morning sky I observed a star like object with red and orange running lights. Not a cloud in the sky neither the moon or the sun or aircraft was visible. It was motionless. from 630 am to 715 am. I saw it as I looked south. Every now and then I get glimpse of the object amongst the clouds. I was excited and wanted to see more. So I continued on the sidewalk and ended up at the dead end of Kendrew and Bogssford road. Between the trees and the woods I continued to observe a glowing object. Then the clouds covered it. I have documented the 45 minute walk on my tape recorder.
