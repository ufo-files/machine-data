{nd} Upon waking up from her three dogs barking and acting up the witness stated at approximately 4:00am on 05/26/15 witness observed a hovering bluish green orb.

On 05/26/15 at approximately 4:00am the witness claimed to be awakened by her three inside pet dogs barking and acting up. The witness came to her front room and peered out a window from inside to observe a pulsating greenish blue orb in the South East direction that rose up above the treetops and hovered for approximately 5 minutes before changing it's color to violet and simply vanishing before her eyes. Upon the objects disappearance her dogs settled down. The area where the sighting occurred is densely wooded and on posted private property across the street from the witnesses house. About a week later after the incident one afternoon the witness was outside in her front yard with her dogs when they acted up again, she stated she and her dogs could sense a thickness in the air and a feeling like static electricity was in the atmosphere. She felt uneasy like someone was watching her so she and her dogs then went inside. As a MUFON Field Investigator I remain in contact with the witness as she lives nearby and would prefer to remain anonymous.
