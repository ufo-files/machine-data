{rjl} Saw a massive cigar with a small white light in the front and a large white light in the rear

My father and I just moved to Phoenix in 1989 from Indiana. It was around Christmas time and we were both sitting out in our yard enjoying the beautiful night weather. Then from the east, almost directly over us we saw a massive cigar shaped craft fly so low over head we could have hit it with a rock. It blocked out the stars. What caught our attention was the two super bright lights. A smaller on on the front, and a larger one on the rear. It didnt make a sound as it moved slowly over head. I jumped up and got right under it. It was now directly above us. It drifted off a little to the west then stopped. Then the large light staid stationary as.the little light moved and faced north. Then still not making a sound it went off to the north.
