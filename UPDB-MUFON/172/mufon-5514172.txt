{cak} VERY BRIGHT FLOURESENT GREEN OBJECT

I WAS OUTSIDE HAVING A CIGARETTE ON THE 20th OR 27th OF SEPTEMBER AT ABOUT 2:30 SOMETHING WHEN I LOOKED UP AND SAW A VERY BRIGHT FLOURESENT GREEN STREAK TRAVELING NORTH GOING SOUTH. IT LOOKED LIKE IT MIGHT BE A METEOR OR SOMETHING ELSE EXCEPT IT WAS TRAVELING WAY TOO SLOW AND IT WAS SO BRIGHT AND GREEN IT LIT UP THE WHOLE SKY. I CALLED THE SEATTLE P. I. A NUMBER OF TIMES AND I LEFT MESSAGES ASKING IF THEY HAD ANY INFORMATION ON IT AND I GOT NO REPLIES. I HAVE SEEN NUMEROUS SHOOTING STARS AND SOME FALLING SATELLITES IN MY LIFE AND NONE OF THEM LOOKED LIKE THIS. HAVE YOU OR ANY OF YOUR PEOPLE RECIEVED ANY REPORT OR ANYTHING ABOUT IT?
