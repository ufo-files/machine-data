(GS) At sunset hovering then moving away from my location slowly until it disapeared.

Relaxing at sunset the bright reflection of the sun off of this object caught my eye. I ran in to get my digital camera and when I got outside it was still in the same spot. I took a few pictures as I observed it start moving away slowly. It always maintained its level upright position. after a few minutes it disappeared. No sounds, no emissions. At first I thought it could be a weather balloon or a kids balloon that got away. After looking at my pictures and zooming in on the object I don't think its any kind of balloon. It seems like it has a hard clear transparent dome on top with a hard surface on the bottom. You can clearly see the sun reflecting off the top.
