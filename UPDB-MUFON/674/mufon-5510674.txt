{dr}observed reddish orb traveling east to west

My Grandson was driving to a friends house and as he was looking left for cars at an intersection noticed unusual object in the twilight sky. He thought it was fireworks. It was red and glowing and was moving east to west. Moving steadily. Looked at it puzzled and just watching it. There was a car behind him who honked and he drove off and lost sight of it. He took a picture of it on his i-phone.
