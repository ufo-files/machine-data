Grey triangular craft high acceleration skipping through the cloud cover

Grayish White Star Lake shaped object viewed from the deck in Spring Hill Tennessee moving at over 10 times the speed of an airplane . The object was viewed skipping like a saucer through the sky cloaking itself within the cloud cover. Object was first seen from the Northeast taking approximately 10 to 11 seconds to cross the sky to the southeast of my viewing distance . The star-like like shape craft was approximately half the size of an airplane. The craft had on and off flashes of light but only seen fluttering twice in the whole time it took to cross the sky.
