{rjl} lights over mine

I work at a lignite mine in East Texas, and I was working the night shift when I saw several lights fly down, hover, and appear to inspect our dragline equipment. The lights moved back and forth, hovered, and ejected smaller lights. The lights appeared around 5:24 AM in Tatum, TX over the Luminant Liberty mine. I've worked out here at various mines for over twenty years and I've never seen anything like it.
