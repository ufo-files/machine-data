Bright light object

I could observe a bright light in the sky. It looked quite large and unusual. It was in line with Venus and urananus. However the size and brightness seemed too much for a planet. It was stationary in the sky for for quite a number of hours. It eventually disappeared. 
It has returned on Monday 16th March. Where it was stationary for a number of hours then continued to travel west and disappeared from view. 
I tried taking photos but I didn’t have suitable lens. When viewed with binocular I could see triangle shapes forming a sphere. To camera it looks like a dot. I thought it was a drone but it was in the sky for hours in the same place before slowly moving out of sight. 
I’d be interested in ideas of what it is. I’m sure many other people in the area must of seen it
