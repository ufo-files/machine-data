{rjl} First saw bright light behind cloud cover.

The light I thought was the moon behind the clouds at first, then I realized it was not in the correct location. I then looked at the rest of the sky and saw a fast travelling small orb that came from the southwest. It was a solid amber circle/orb that traveled across the sky from the sw to the ne. Another orb followed this one on the exact same trajectory. I counted 10 of these orbs follow the exact same path in the sky at the exact same speed. I then looked back to the glowing large moon light in the sky, and it was gone, the clouds had dissipated and the light was no longer there. I then looked back to the southwest and saw more of the small orbs coming into sight. I tracked two that released smaller orbs that split off and headed south. In total there were about 15 of these that I counted in a span of 10 minutes. I have seen much lower flying UAPS directly over our home in this area over the past year as well. I did not have my phone or camera so I was not able to take any pictures or videos unfortunately.
