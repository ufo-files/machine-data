High flying Small Pulsing orb type object with speed and direction changes

This object was high in the sky. It initially looked as though it may be the sun’s reflection of a high flying plane. What was unusual about it was the path it took and the pulsating/flashing nature of the light. The object would have been tumbling if it were a reflection. It moved slowly and steadily before coming to a stop, then changing from a southerly direction to an eastern path. I tracked it for around 10minutes before it was too far away to see. This is the second sighting of this nature in this area with the same witness.
