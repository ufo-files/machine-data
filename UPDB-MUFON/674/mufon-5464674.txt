{jpg} Large, square or diamond shaped single bright light moving very quickly across distant horizon like shooting star and stop, disappear

I am a wedding videographer, and was shooting a wedding event at this location. As I was loading my gear to leave, out of the south door, I saw a large, square or diamond shaped single bright light moving very quickly across the distant horizon like a shooting star. The light began towards the south and headed northerly. The light was much bigger and strange shaped than a shooting star. The strangest thing about the light is that it seemed to move very quickly and suddenly freeze and disappear. I could see the cloud layer so I dont believe it just disappeared in the clouds. I am also a professional pilot and I am very familiar with aircraft lights at night. The whole event lasted about 2 seconds.
