{jpg} cigar shape,no sound,no contrail.no wings. planes in the sky at the same time.

I was on my deck out back of my house about 8 am Sunday Dec.8 2019. I looked up as i often do and saw a long white object moving from the north to the south. the first thing i thought was what the hell is that. It moved about the same speed as the planes that fly over but it was a lot lower. The planes about 5 to 6 miles up this thing about 2 miles up. It made no sound had no wings and left no contrail. I watched it for approx. 15-20 seconds before it faded off into the southern sky. I look back at the planes to make sure i could tell the difference. I had know doubt. even as high as they were i can still see the wings. FREAKY.
