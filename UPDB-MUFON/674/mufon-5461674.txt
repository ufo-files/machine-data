{tjd} 2 hovering objects in the sky

Noticed 2 circles in the sky looking north bother we’re traveling west the first one (left) stopped and stayed in the same place while the second (right) one slowly followed then headed north. The second one was making odd movements. Then the first one headed back towards east and then followed the second one heading north. Both eventually disappeared. In the photos there is 2 zoomed in pictures and there is a few of both of the objects in the same pictures and compared to the planes flying near them. Considering how one just stopped and stayed in the same spot and moving directions does not make me think it is a weather balloon...too circular for helicopters or planes and too high for a drone.
