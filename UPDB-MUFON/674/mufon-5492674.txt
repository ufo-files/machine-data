{rjl} Bright low flying orange orbs above the Stonewall Pride Festival in Wilton Manors FL.

Slow moving orange orbs were were seen by many people at the event moving fairly slow in the night sky above the buildings at the Stonewall Pride street festival. Many of us were looking up trying to figure out what we were looking at but noone could figure it out. There was no sound and the orbs were equidistant apart from one another and were flying at the same altitude in a straight line heading North/Northeast before diminishing one by one in the sky. There was no way these were flares because we saw them coming from a distance and they remained at the same altitude for the 10-15 minutes that we noticed them heading toward the crowd.
