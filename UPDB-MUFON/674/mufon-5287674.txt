Group of Spheres in Conical Formation

On 5/24 I witnessed a group of spherical objects. At first, they appeared to be a whispy cloud. As I got closer I saw a group of spheres that looked like a cluster of balloons but did not move like released balloons I've ever seen. Once I was almost underneath, I saw that there were two concentric rings rotating opposite of each other. About 6 minutes after seeing and questioning what it was, I decided to pull over to get a video but the only thing left in the sky were two other clouds that behaved normally and didn't dissapate.
