Traveling down HIghway 114 SW toward Grapevine (Direction of DFW Airport) Started with 3 then four lights and later 5. I though must be airplanes but they were brighter than any plane in the sky and no blinking lights. And they were too close to one another to be planes/helicopters without crashing. These things would phase in and out and in different formations slowly, gracefully. Some would disappear than suddenly more show up - I saw 5 at one time. They appear in a triangle formation straight lines, and mixed/random. It didnt appear to be drones (no flashing lights) and the lights pulsed on and off light like lights on dimmer switches.  The pulse on/off infrequent maybe just a few times each over the course of watching them. 

I watched this while I was driving through my front windshield for approx 8-10 minutes while traveling that highway from Roanoke direction on the way to a meeting. I was wondering if anyone else paid attention to this as there were many cars on the road. with me. I lost sight of this when the highway turns and the objects were out of my field of vision on the right side of the car. I did not stop the car to take pictures 1) my dashcam was flashing operational with its green light so i was hoping the video would come out.  2) I had a meeting to go to and didnt want to be late and since dashcam appeared functional no need to stop.

I was very dissappointed when I checked the flash memory on the dashcam as the last video entry was for July 30th, 2019 meaning it was no longer automatically recording properly since then.
