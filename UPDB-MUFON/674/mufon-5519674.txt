On the night of 2/21/08 during a total luna eclipse I saw an extremily large and very fast dark isosceles triangular craft coming from the East with illuminated areas in the back corners ( see dia.).

During the evening of 2/21/08 there was a total luma eclipse. At 7:55 I went to the south facing bay window to check the progress of the eclipse, which had just started. Out of the right side window of the bay I saw an extremely massive and very fast isososeles triangular craft coming towards me over Steele Hill from the east. I ran out as fast as I could to the ice covered walkway. At almost over head I locked in on the illuminated areas in the back corners ( see dia.) and then it went behind the pine trees. Because of the size and speed I assumed it must be only a few thousand feet. Puzzled, I came in and continued watghing TV. At 8:25 I once again checked on the eclipse, which was a little over half, and again a craft came over the hill with the same heading, but a bit to the right. As before I ran out and this time was able to pick on a nose strobe light and maybe, but not sure, one in the interior. Just over head before loosing it again in the pines I noticed the the sound, which was that of a multi engine jet, lagged the craft by 2 or 3 body lenths. Given this, I figured that it must be at least 20 to 30 thousand feet, making it very very much larger and faster than my earlier estimate. Each occurance lasted no more than 5 or 6 seconds. There were no further sightings that night or since. I have often thought about it and it has always bothered me as to just what I had seen that night. I am sure that it must be American man made. I really hope that this somehow leads to answering my questions as to just what I saw that night-- and I am sure of that I saw.
