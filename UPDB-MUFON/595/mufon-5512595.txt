(GS) White fireball-like object seen over southwest mountains of las vegas nv

I was driving on Hualipai heading west near Alta Drive and in the south west sky, just above the mountains I noticed a white fireball looking object. It looked like it was heading towards earth very slowly. I noticed two jets, the ones with the long trail heading towards the thing I saw...one was heading towards it going east to west and the other jet was heading towards it from west to east. It appeared to be by the Spring Mountains. I watched it as long as i could but then it disapeared behind buildings. I wanted to go and check it out more closely but I needed to get home. I am just very curious about it because of the meteor that was in Russia today and also the meteor that came close to Earth today.
