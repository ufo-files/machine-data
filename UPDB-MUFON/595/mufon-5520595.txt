glowing ember appearance with direct North path, once near us it hovered then proceeded, never zigzagged or fell

Walking,enjoying clear sky (no clouds or moon). Saw what appeared an ember (yellow,orange -- glowing)in the sky. Thought it was a meteor but it wasnt. Then thought perhaps it was a small plane on fire so we watched. 
As it came closer it was definitely not a plane --- no noise, plus it hovered over us for about 30 seconds then sped up in a direct Northern path. 

My other two friends had walked further down the street and talked to a lady walking a dog---she had also seen it.
