My wife and i were walking the dogs in the back yard, i always look up. I saw a round light moving relatively slow a mile or so directly overhead. I told my wife to look and she told me to take a video with my phone. I did but the quality isnt good. It took about 30 seconds to ascend to space while flying east or north east. It was obvious it was ascending to space. It was a silent steady round light that got dimmer as it went up until it was a tiny red dot and was no longer visible. Couldnt have been a clearer night.
