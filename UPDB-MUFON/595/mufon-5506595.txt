{kt}Red fiery objects

My wife and I were sitting in our back screened patio when my wife suddenly said "What is that?". We stood up and looked straight out towards our back yard and saw a round red glowing object coming from the east approximately 1000-2000 ft in the sky heading towards our house. We called our son to look at the object also and attempted to video record the objects on a phone and camcorder but somehow both the phone and camcorder only got a few seconds of video with only our voices able to be heard. Here is a link to one - [Link moved to FI section/cms/tg]

The object moved at roughly the same speed as a small plane but made no noise and came directly above our house and then seemed to gain altitude as it moved to the west. We noticed a second object flying the same path. When that object was just above the house, suddenly both objects just disappeared. 

My initial thought is perhaps they were chinese lanterns though they seemed to be moving too fast for that. The first one also ascended while the second one stayed at a lower altitude. The both seemed to disappear at the same time. 

I called the local police department to ask if anyone had reported a sighting and was told there were no reports. Suprisingly, 3 squad cars arrived at my home and I explained what had happened. I have a recording of the phone call made to the police department. I tried to be thorough in my evidence collection but was really disappointed when I found out that our video evidence wasnt there.
