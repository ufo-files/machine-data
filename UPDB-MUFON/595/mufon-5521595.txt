Pulsating bright blue light

I was in the front yard and my neice pointed out a bright pulsating blue light west of where we were standing. It did not move and it was the brightest light in the sky. We watched it for awhile and I brushed it off as being a star and it was possibly pulsating from distant clouds moving. It was dark so I am not exactly sure if there was any clouds in the distance. I went back inside and my neice said it faded away and was gone. I don't think cloud coverage is a factor anymore, because I can still see stars out in that direction and the clouds obviously would have covered the other stars, as well.
