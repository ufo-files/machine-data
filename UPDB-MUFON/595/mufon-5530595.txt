object appeared just over treeline moved slowly no sound fading lights

The first sighting was in march not sure of date close to the tenth but was at 503am. i had got up to use the bathroom and looked out the window looking west/sw i saw a bright yellowish colored object in the sky. I went into the kitchen to look out that window, it was still there and i noticed it sparkled and the lights faded in and out. There is a treeline approx 3 miles west of my house just west of st rd 1. This is where it looked like it was. As i watched for about 5 or ten mins the object slowly disappeared. It was on a wednesday. two weeks later on a wed at 504 am i got up same thing, to use the bathroom and saw it again. This time i went outside and stood and looked at it. It appeared to move in an almost straight path toward my house.As it got overhead i saw it had these yellowish light that didn't blink and almost appeared solid yellow. There was no noise. I live very close to a small airport in connersville on the fayette wayne county line. I hear airplanes ALL the time. There was absolutley no noise. The object passed over my house and just disappeared. Afterwards I experienced an extremely bad headache for a few hours. I saw this again over that same treeline in July. It disappeared very quickly and was around 530am on a tuesday morning. unsure of the date but will keep track of the dates much better from now on if i see it again..also the july sighting i woke up my husband to see this too.
