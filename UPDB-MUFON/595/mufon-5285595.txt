Moving Star like phenomena

Sitting outside of vehical in total darkness scanning sky, saw 2-3 airplanes passing then looked to my left & saw another light, about the size of the planes coming in, but this light began moving erratically. I pulled out cell & recorded this for nearly 3 minutes. It remained in one area but would zip back & forth, sometimes out of my view but then right back to same area if sky. It also would dissapear for a second then appear back again. I stopped to put charger on phone, then found it again there, this time after a couple more minutes recording, I tried using my zoom. At probably 6x or 8x, I saw then looked like a spinning disk that seemed to pulsate as it spun, moving back & forth, in & outward. The times it came head on toward my direction, I could see faint colors but definitely a spin, that would always dissipate then begin again. Over & over. After 30 minutes we left. From videos I could stop at a frame & use that picture to apply various filters, for interesting results that look like spirals. I do have all original video with the audio, but will post both without as its distracting! Freeze frame photos can post also, later today.
