{ck} Orange round orb floating slowly in sky

My friend and her family and I were watching fireworks go off for the fourth of July on the side of a hill that over looks the bay. While we were watching the fireworks I looked up in the sky and noticed a slow moving object floating above the treetops. It was an orange amber color, that was giving off a luminous glow. My friends father told us it was an airplane and that we shouldnt pay any mind to it. We noticed there were no flashing lights and watched it float across the sky. It went over the tree tops from north to south, it was floating very slowly. We turned around for a second to look at a firework that had gone off, we turned around and the orange object was gone. Nowhere to be found.
