Floating Grey Ball Type Object - Started Off As Bright Star Like Solid Light

I was stand on back porch and noticed bright white solid star like object appx 45 degrees in sky moving from west to South in front of me. I observed the object make a sudden but short lateral darting motion before object made sudden stop. After approximately 5 seconds object continued along lateral path toward my view of the moon which was behind light cloud cover. The object appeared to suddenly increase altitude and at this point I was aware of a second unlit ball/ cube shaped object following behind. It appeared to chase the illuminated object in a circular motion around my view of the moon. The illuminate object then dissapeared leaving only the unlit object in the sky, which also suddenly dissapeared a few seconds later. 
Managed to take a sequence of photographs of the incident using a digital camera in night mode as I knew from seeing similar objects previously, that the image quality would be brighter than if filming.
I also maged to stich the images together into a short video sequence.
