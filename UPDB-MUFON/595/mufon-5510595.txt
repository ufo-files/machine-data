{cak} While stopped at a gas station, I noticed a white rectangular shaped object in the sky

I was at a gas station with my wife to pick up something when I was just about to enter the vehicle and noticed a very white rectangular shaped object moving very slowly across the sky towards my wife and I with no sound or contrail left behind. Before I could remind myself to take a picture of it, it was already to the northwest side of where I had originally witnessed it and it was further away. I managed to take a picture just before I lost site of it.
