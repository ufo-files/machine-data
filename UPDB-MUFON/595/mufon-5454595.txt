White orbs in a triangle formation

On the 20th of March 2020, at about 8:30 pm, I observed about 30 white orbs flying in a triangle formation, in a NW/SW direction. I watched them fly over the houses for about 5 minutes, they made no sound has they passed over me. They seemed to be flying under the cloud cover, and at first I thought that they were geese, but they moved faster than geese and would say that they were flying at between 5-10 mph. Also that they were imitating a dull white light and no noise, as they passed over head, ruled out that they were geese.
