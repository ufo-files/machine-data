Witnessed multiple cigar/cocktail shaker shaped items in sky. 8pm not a cloud in the sky. Items came from east side by side then split apart. One turned back around and another was coming from east. At that time a plane headed south was flying over and the 2 objects crossed directly in front of it at different elevations. I had a witness but he chooses to remain unanimous. Earlier that day while me an wife were in pool there was a helicopter at extreme elevation circling for hours. The copter would swing out over Lake Michigan then back over land. It had about a 5-10 minute round trip. The whole day was odd and after i seen the objects i was ready to go home. I wish i had my phone on me but i left it inside
