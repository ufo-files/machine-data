I photographed an incredible event

On June 30th at around midnight I was taking a few pictures of the Moon with my telephone camera. After looking at the photos I noticed a light traveling what I thought to be in front of the Moon but now that I can see the light reflecting from the moon to the object the object was obviously behind the Moon when I zoom up on this object it clearly shows to have a Terrain type surface with spikes coming out Argos surface. I have six photographs of this little dot in the sky traveling across the moons position. Two of my photographs clearly show an enormous object with spikes on it and a clear reflection of The Moonlight on the surface of the object as it travels South by Southwest. This is something I have never seen I wasn't trying to photograph anything but I did and I look at it on the hour every hour I am in disbelief of what I see. When I try to send this photo to other family members phone if it loses some of its clarity
