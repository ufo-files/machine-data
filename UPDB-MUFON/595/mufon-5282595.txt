Hovering bright spot in a perfect blue sky. Stationary bright object slowly moving apparently without a precise direction

We were having a morning walk along the Tejo river, in Lisbon. It was 11:20AM when my eye was attracted by a bright spot emerging from a cloud. The spot seems to be fixed in the sky and for sure it was not a plane. I started following it and I noticed it was slowly moving away from the cloud on a horizontal line from left to right. I start filming it with my phone. There was a plane passing by (Lisbon Airport is not far) so I was able to confront this white spot with the silhouette of a flying airplane. After a while the white spot stoped and changed direction, from north to south. It was the only bright spot on the sky. I checked my astronomy application to check the position of any eventually stars, to make sure I was not watching some bright star. But there were no stars, no moon, nothing in the sky except that white spot. The observation took place for about 10 minutes. Last time I checked it was at 11:30. After that It disappeared. Unfortunately I did see it disappear, so I don't know exactly how it vanished. My wife was with me. She saw it too, and clearly she recognised it was slowly moving.
