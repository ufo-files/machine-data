{rjl} White circle w/ fluttering diving objects inside it

Before I retired in 2009, I was walking alone down an elevated street headed north in our mobile home park. I looked up at the end of the street and saw a circular object straight on, low in the sky, with what seemed to be like a pattern of birds fluttering and diving within the confines of the circle. They were white. They seemed to be flowing around one another, sort of like clothes do in a washing machine in the wash cycle. The object seemed to be a mile or more away, not too close. I stopped walking, looked at it for a minute or so, then walked on for maybe a minute looking down at the street. I looked up again and it was still there so I watched it for another minute. I then walked on for a minute or so, again looking down. When I looked up a third time, it was gone. I did not see it appear or disappear, no movement, no fade out. It was just stationary the whole time.

The alignment seemed to be hovering over Buchanan Airport in Concord, a local general aviation airfield. I did not see any conventional aircraft flying around in its vicinity, but that is usual because the airport has very little air traffic.

I started to carry my point-and-shoot camera after this sighting, but, to date, I've never seen any more sky objects to photo.
