Three cylindrical ufo’s flying in formation

Traveling On US 79 North just passed Fairmont, WV we observed 3 cylindrical, wingless, rudderless flying objects in the sky traveling North West at a slow rate. One object was out front a little further than the two following objects which were very close to each other in line. Sky very clear and sunny with sun reflecting off of the objects.
