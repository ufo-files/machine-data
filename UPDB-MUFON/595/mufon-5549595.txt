Sighting in Cass County

My husband and I were looking for our dog that had gotten hit by a car. As we were carrying him to the house that night, we looked up to the sky and two rows of bright orange lights appeared and disappeared. I screamed and then they reappeared in the same place and then disappeared again. It looked like there were two jets flying around them.
