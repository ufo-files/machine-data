{cak} Huge triangular craft silently hovered over Kirkwood, mo for at least 2min b4 it slowly left over the tree line, bright white light on Front of craft.

It took place during the summer of 2006 in Kirkwood Missouri real late (around 2am, maybe a little closer to 3am) when my friend and myself were in my car driving home from grabbing fast-food. We first noticed this very large bright white light off in the distance up in the sky straight ahead us while driving through the intersection of guyer & Essex but at first we could only see the light, it wasn't until we drove further down Essex that we started to be able to see that the bright light was attached to this huge giant craft, when we got to the end of Essex where the road intersects with another road called Dougherty​ Ferry, we were literally directly beneath it and I started talking a mile a minute to my friend who just sat there frozen looking up through my sunroof at it. This thing was the biggest thing I'd ever seen in my life!! It was triangular in shape, a dull dark greyish black color and it just hovered​ without moving or making any noise or sound whatsoever, and I kept repeating to my friend that it had to be either aliens/UFO or a secret government craft​ that the public doesn't know about, I kept saying how the only aircrafts known to man that could hover like that would be a helicopter and the stealth bomber thing..this thing didn't make any sounds & was way bigger than a stealth bomber..im not even joking when I say that this thing would probably take up half of the entire town of Kirkwood had it landed, it was just massive and we were able to just sit there in my car and look at it for about two minutes before it finally started to slowly move, and it kept moving away slowly until it was totally over the tree line & We could no longer see it. It was the most amazing and unexplainable thing I have ever witnessed in my life.
