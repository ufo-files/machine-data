I have spotted satellites reflecting the sun in the night sky from the comfort of the hot tub hundreds of times, this one was different. One night my wife Tracie and I were walking to the car and I happened to look up. The night sky was crystal clear and the stars were bright. I saw a moving pinpoint of light traveling in a Northerly direction. I pointed it out to Tracie and we both watched it for about 45 seconds, until the point of light made a dead left turn without slowing down at all. We watched for another 20 or so seconds until the point of light desappeared over the trees.
