{nd} My husband and I were driving I-16W at 9pm on a Wednesday night. My husband saw the object first and then asked me if I saw it. I looked out of the front windshield and leaned forward and tilted my head left, and looking up to the left.

I was in my car with my family in Savannah, GA driving on I-16w. We were just passing Chatham Parkway/Dean Forest road area. My husband was driving and he asked me if I saw the object in the sky. I looked for myself and saw a light in the sky. It was bright like a flouresent light. It was in the shape of an triangle. My husband and I were trying to figure out what the object was. It was not moving at all. It was not blinking. So, we did not think it was a plane. I continued to stare at it until it vanished. I continued to look in the sky and did not see it again. We were curious and wanted to know what it was and also we wanted to make sure that both of us saw it. We thought it was a cool experience but would really like to know what it was. We lost sight once it disappeared without a path.
