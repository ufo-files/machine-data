Bullet shaped object white turning blue green streaked on a downward curve, broke into two and disappeared.

Standing out front of our house facing West in Perkasie PA around 8PM. My boyfriend saw a white bullet shaped object and called it to my attention. When I looked up object and the white streak behind it changed into blue-green(much like the aurora borealis colors). The object was on a curved downward motion. It then split in two separate objects and completely disappeared. The whole sighting lasted only 10 seconds.
