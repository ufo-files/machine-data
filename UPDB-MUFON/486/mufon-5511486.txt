{nd} I have never seen anything like this. It was at night and for a few minuted I thought it was just a bird hovering.

I was talking with my friend by the North Packery channel parking area. I thought that what i saw was a pelican hovering over the dunes back and forth. This can be a very common sight, so I didn't pay much attention at first. From where we were standing the parking lot lights were the orange color HPS type, I thought the glow was just reflecting off the chest of the pelican. After a few minutes I looked back towards the dunes and thought a reflection couldnt be that bright. It was then I realized this was not a late night glowing,hovering pelican, but something even more strange! I whipped out my 5 year old beat up LG Smartphone, shot the required terrible movie images and then it slowly and silently rose to the northeast and vanished. It was a slow steady rise to the left and disappeared into the darkness, maybe a mile away. Blew my mind! I have perfect vision and was not doing any drugs or alcohol!
