Red and black orb spinning like a top

That day on June 23, 2007 I was headed to school heading north on foot I made a right hand turn on the corner of 7th and Normandie and I saw this thing above a building spinning like a wobbly top, you know when the top is loosing its momentum and it starts to wobble; Luckily that day I had my digital camera, I was taking art at the time at the local college which is why I had the camera that day, I only had a few seconds to react to pull the camera from my backpack, turning it on and snapping the two pictures by the time I had done that the object was gone. I've seen unconventional objects before but this was the first time I had a camera and I felt lucky to have captured it on digital film. The camera model is a Canon Powershot A95 5mp. The pictures don't do the object justice of what I saw that day especially the spinning movement characteristics of the object that I witness that day. Afterwards I took a look at the pictures on the first image it looks like a black sphere, on the second it looks like the object is going, for lack of better word, invisible. Looks like when I snapped the second image I captured the object turning invisible. I added arrows to indicate the location of the object on the pictures and cropped one of the pictures.
