{tjd} A very, slow moving, silent, flying, cylindrical object sighted above Clifton-Fine School playing field

A very, slow moving, silent, flying, cylindrical object sighted above Clifton-Fine School playing field (way upstate NY). I observed no wings, no windows, no markings, no contrail and absolutely no sound. Looked like a giant floating/flying cigarette - all white with a silverish hue at periphery. Approx 40' long by 10' diameter moving approx 2 mph. The most interesting feature was that as it moved over and away from me, in its fixed flight path, it "brightly flashed" at me from its aft end -- it was an extremely bright, haloed, camera like flash approx 3-5 seconds apart. Then it just disappeared into the SW horizon. It all seemed surreal and I was in awe.
