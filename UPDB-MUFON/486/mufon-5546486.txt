Three orange balls of light in triangular form.

We had just finished watching a firework show at the Iroqouis School area in Erie, Pa. We were driving up McClelland ave. (Also in Erie) around 10:25 p.m. EST. when I happened to look up into sky out the left side of my car and noticed a stationary object hovering about 100 yards off the ground about a mile away from us. I told everyone that there was an object over in the sky. Everyone thought it was a U.F.O., even me. I could still see the object at my house, but i had to go inside and go to bed. Before I went inside, it had started moving very slowly towards the ESE. I guessed it would have lasted about five minutes longer until it disappeared over the herizon after I went inside. 

Can anybody tell me if you saw the same thing or if it was real?
