My last ufo.
I went outside to my back garden as I heard my dog barking. Told him to calm down and to come in but he was looking up when he was barking. I looked and saw a tree but as I looked more closely to my dogs eyes, I noticed he was looking up in the sky and that is when I saw it.
It was bright, started off flashing different colours and must of been there for at 3 minutes. My feelings at the time was that it could be a plane or helicopter but have seen then before in the dark and this was no plane or helicopter I had seen before. It went from flashing green to yellow to blue. Then there was a flash and it had just disappeared. Have seen more in the last few years and have took some photos.
