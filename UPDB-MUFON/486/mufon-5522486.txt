Object in sky, watched it hover and zig zag some, then it left

I was looking out my window towards the south/south east sky when I noticed lights in the sky that seemed to be an airplane to me. After watching it for a few minutes I realized it was not an airplane or helicopter...this was approx 1:30 AM EST. As I watched it more, it would move up, move down, very quickly and precisely. It would hover, it was high up in the sky. After watching it for awhile I ran outside with a pair of binoculars to try to get a better visual on it. It seemed to be moving farther away from my sight. As I went outside to watch it I couldnt find it. So I went back inside to the window I was watching it from and it was still there, so after getting my bearings back down on where to look, I went back outside. As I looked up, it took off...as in, there was a streak of light, almost like a shooting start, but this was a straight path across the night sky. It went right across orions belt heading west. At that point I knew it was to be an unknown flying object.
I like to look up at the night sky, and on clear nights like these you can see a lot of the stars. This is not the first time I had observed a UFO in the sky. I was just laying in bed watching tv and looked out the window when I noticed the strange lights.
It appeared to me it was very high up in the sky, and after watching it make its maneuvers I realized it was not an airplane or helicopter. I was excited to see it in the sky and just watched it. This is not the first time I have seen objects in the sky that display this type of behavior.
