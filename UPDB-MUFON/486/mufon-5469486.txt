Looked like 2 craft firing at eachother

I was outside with my 2 dogs around midnight when I looked up at the sky as I always do. I noticed a white star like light moving in a straight line, at first I thought it was the ISS. Then above the same light another 1 appeared. The 1 above wasn't there when I saw the other 1, it came into view by brightening it's light, I noticed it was moving in the same direction as the other light but it was slightly left behind. Then I saw a silvery shimmering object leave the lower object travel in the direction of the upper object. When this happened the upper object seemed to change direction as if to avoid the object being "shot" at it. This happened 3 times before the lower object disappeared rapidly to the right, the upper object remained stationery for several seconds before its light dimmed and completely disappeared. I didn't notice any trail left by the object that sped off and the object that faded from view didn't appear again. I waited for around 15 minutes before going indoors but nothing else happened.
