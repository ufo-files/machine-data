{cak} Large Grey disk approx., 100 feet in diameter was traveling sw to ne silently without lights.

I had stepped outside to smoke a cigarette at approximately 2:30 or 3:00 in the morning and while standing there looking at the stars, I witnessed a large, grey disk float by overhead. It was about 100 feet or so above the trees and was traveling from SW to NE and made absolutely NO noise and had NO lights showing. It appeared to be around 100 feet or more in diameter and was barely lit up from the city lights below it. It appeared as a ghost, grey disk and just silently moved across the sky until it was out of sight behind the trees.
