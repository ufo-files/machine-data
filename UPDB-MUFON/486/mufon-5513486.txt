{nd} triangle object hovered in sky ,ascended then flew west .

On December 21,2012 at 2:45am I awaked to go to the bathroom, looked out my bedroom window and saw a triangular shaped object hovering over the treetops over a nearby stripmall. The object had white, orange and blue lights with a very bright beam of white light coming down from the center. It hoverd in a stationary position for about five minutes then ascended rapidly straight up and then shot quickly to the west. The object was silent the entire time.
