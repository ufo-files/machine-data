Hovering bright pale orange light

I woke up to go to the bathroom and I always leave my curtains open in my room. At approx 3am I saw a oval shaped light the was pale orange hovering NE of my house. I got up went to the rest room and the object was still there. I could here dogs barking in the distant. I had to work early that morning so I watched the object for about an hour and then I fell asleep. I’ve grew up near a airport and worked right next to a building and watched hundreds of planes and helicopters take off and land. This was nothing like I have seen before. It was silent and clearly too large to be a planet or a star. I’m a village educated woman who graduated top of my class. This did not appear to be any private or commercial craft.
