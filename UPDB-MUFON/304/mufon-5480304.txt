{cak} High speed object seen in 2nd video of the Blue Angels in San Francisco Bay.

This video was taken at about the same time as the Blue Angels video over San Francisco Bay seen in Case Number 79744, but taken from the Marin Headlands to the north across the San Francisco Bay and facing east. At about 3-4 seconds, a dark object moves quickly from the left to the right with an arcing flight path (starts in the lower left of the view, flies upwards to just above the altitude of the two Blue Angels jets, then arcs back downward toward San Francisco). Interestingly, the flight path of the unknown object in this video would bring the object to the area filmed in the Case Number 79744 video taken of the same Blue Angels jets. The object even appears to be on a slightly downward flight path as it reaches the filming location for Case Number 79744; this same slightly downward flight path was captured in the 79744 video posted yesterday, and the object was moving in the same relatively direction in both videos.
