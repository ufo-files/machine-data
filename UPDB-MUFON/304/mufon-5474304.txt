{cak} White orb observed in a very dark stormcloud.

As soon as I got home, I wrote my account to my two best friends, knowing that they wouldn't think I was crazy. Here is what I wrote: "Hey, I think I saw a UFO (or two) on my afternoon break today... I was having a smoke just before the storm and looking up, watching this big grey cloud move in. Then, I saw this white thing, flying towards me, going through the cloud in a straight line, sometimes disappearing into it, then emerging... I thought, "Uh oh, a plane is caught in that?" but, after about a minute, it swerved to the right at a 90-degree angle and flew through the cloud again! I thought, "No plane can do that!" It sort of bobbed and weaved in and out of the cloud, and zoomed off in the same direction. Since, I was under a shelter, I moved closer to the edge and looked up, but it was gone! But, then I saw a black thing, just as high as the clouds, moving in the same direction, but in a different area, and it was "tumbling"... I mean, it would flip over, stop, flip over, stop, again and again... That one looked more like a bird being blown away by the winds, but still, birds were born to fly, shouldn't they know better? Come to think of it, it didn't have any wings... It was in the shape of a shoe, that's what it reminded me of... A shiny dress shoe tumbling through the air, but able to right itself... I don't know if the white thing was some sort of meteorological phenomenon, but I'd like to know..."
