Fast, low flying shiny delta shaped object at LAX

I was pulling into the airport to pick up an employee and looked up to see an incredibly shiny and fast moving object that seemed to be flickering. At first I thought it was a glare, or my brain playing a trick on me, but have realized that I saw some type of phenomenon that I can't explain, and figured I would let MUFON know..The craft was not shaped like a perfect triangle, as it had additional angles to its outline. I can draw it if that helps. It seemed that whatever it was, had a cloaking malfunction as it appeared and disappeared multiple times over the course of roughly half a second and was moving at hundreds of miles an hour.
