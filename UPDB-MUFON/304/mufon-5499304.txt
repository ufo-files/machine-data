{cak} It was a grey circular shaped object that made absolutely no sound.

I just pulled into the house from my son1s baseball practice and we were unloading our gear from the truck when I noticed a strange object in the sky. At first it appeared to be hovering and I looked away for a split second to check on my son as he asked me what it was. At that point I returned my attention back to the object and told him that I was not sure. The object then began moving west at a very slow pace not making a sound. 

My son and I were just standing there watching in disbelief.When I realized I should be taking photos. So I pulled out my cell phone and snapped three photos of it. The object appeared to be 2500-3000ft in height and a distance of 1 to 1 of a mile away. There was cloud cover and it seemed well below it. My son and I watched it for about 3 min before it shot up at about a 75 to 80 degree angle and disappeared. It moved incredibly fast almost bullet like. What an incredible adrenalin filled moment that I got to share with my son.
