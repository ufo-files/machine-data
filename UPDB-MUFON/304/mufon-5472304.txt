I believe I was meant to see the craft

5.30 am walking dog, very short distance walked when I heard a humming sound, I looked up and the humming ceased. I stopped and watched as the craft went passed me almost overhead. it came to a halt , rotating lights blending together like a kalidescope. I watched it for a few moments transfixed but totally unafraid, then I found myself about a hundred yards from where I stopped in the direction I would have taken anyway.The craft then flew away I believe I was meant to see the craft by the sound coming from the craft because the sound ceased when I looked up at it..
