saw a bright that would change directions until it disappeared

In July of 2014, I was at the boardwalk in Belmar, NJ near midnight. Above me I noticed a bright light in the shape of a circle. It almost looked like how a star would look but only a little bigger and the glow was constant, not the flickering glow a star would have. At first glance, I thought it was a drone, but then after really looking at it, there was no possible way. The object was flying ridiculously high and able to freely fly miles down the coast and back in just a few seconds. After watching the glowing object fly up and down the coast for a few minutes. It stopped in front of me, a few miles away from the coast over the ocean. It was idle for a minute or two before flying straight up and as I watched the light, it got smaller and smaller before it was just gone. What I witnessed that night is still as clear to me as if it happened yesterday. It was definitely not a drone or plane. It was flying around freely at speeds that I don’t think are possible with our technology before flying straight up into the night sky and disappearing. After I lost sight of the object, I tried to rationally explain to myself what I just saw but after crossing each and every possibility off my list, I was stuck with more questions. Sometimes I replay what happened over and over again in my head but to this day can’t explain it.
