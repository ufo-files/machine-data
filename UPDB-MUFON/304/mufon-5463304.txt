Mt friends from the stars came to visit me...

1. At the time of the sighting I was sat outside on my rear balcony smoking a cigarette...

2. I initially noticed it as it was dark and so the pulsating light in the sky immediately caught my attention so I quickly went inside and grabbed my Samsung phone to video it as I knew nobody would believe me!

3. When I first noticed it, I thought it was some sort of spacecraft or entity from space or an alternate dimension.

4. As you can see from the video, it was an orb-shaped light in the sky which darted around the sky quickly and kept moving much closer to me and then back further away again... As it got closer it appeared much larger and to be semi-transparent/translucent.

5. It was very exciting to see for real with my very own eyes. I've seen similar videos before on youtube but to actually witness one first hand was a very exhilarating experience and I hope it/they will come back to see me again soon.

6. Eventually it disappeared again, just as quickly as it had originally appeared...
