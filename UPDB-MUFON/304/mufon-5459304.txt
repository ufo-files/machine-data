{cak} 20 min persistent lightning & orbs in video

Saturday night as I lay in bed with my back to the window a storm front moved in and my room was full of flashes of light. So I started recording over my shoulder with my iPhone on the reverse camera angle because of how unusual it was. But after a few minutes it didn’t stop. So I went out to the balcony and recorded two subsequent videos and then later on one in my bedroom window by just leaving the phone on the screen. It went on for 15-20 minutes and there wasn’t a sound. But when I went back to look at the videos quite often I could see vertical lines. When I would pause on the video and go back-and-forth through the flash you can clearly see moving beams of vertical light. I captured a ton of screenshots and in a handful of them you can clearly see the silhouette of an orb/sphere in the sky with lightning coming from either side of it. In one of the videos there is a flash that turns the entire sky green quickly followed by two small flashes in the upper right portion of the screen. The one video I’ve included is me playing it on my iPad and recording it on the iPhone slowly going back-and-forth during one of the flashes of light. That’s not lightning.
