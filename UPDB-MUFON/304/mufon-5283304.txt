Huge Mothership stops and meets up with smaller vessel then vessel departs back into eastern space then main ship continues on slowly!

Observed three lights (reflecting from the sun) traveling slowly northbound through the night sky in a two side by side and one behind a front one formation. Then observed a single light coming from eastern space slowly. Thought this will be interesting as it looked like they might collide. Then the large ship actually stopped to wait for west bound vessel to connect. Then ten seconds later the small vessel shot like a bullet back into eastern space and was gone. Then the large ship started slowly continuing north in and orbit around the earth.
