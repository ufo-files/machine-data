Luminescent Oval, vector, East North Easterly .No external features. In pursuit of small Cessna, believed Crashed.

Just came home from a wrestling match. I ran into the house to open the windows, because the house got too hot, so I ran upstairs to open window. Heard the sound of a cessna sized engine reving very high, but not going very fast. Seconds later, aperfect Grey oval, much larger that the size of a blimp, and'solidly fixed in space'flew silently behind the Cessna. The Oval was at least 70 yards long. flew about 500feet above me. On close inspection, the oval disapears, in cadence with the flickering of the whole body of the craft, then reapears. Seen my witnesses from the next mornning's Stockton Record, our Lodi News Sentinnel failed to report. Saw the Same Craft in successive years.72,73,74,75,76. The 1976 sighting was not reported by anyone. It was as though it was navigating by the confermation of the California Basin. I did a painting.
