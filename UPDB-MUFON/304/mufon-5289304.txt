Unexplainable Object observed flying the evening of August 2, 2020.

This object was observed flying in the sky on the eastern side of our house. In the video it appears white but to the eye it appeared to be a grey silver color. The object appeared to be shaped like an elongated tic tac or cigar shaped, no wings. The most alarming thing is that it didn't make a sound. Planes fly over here frequently, and when they fly as low as this object did they are normally so loud you can here them inside the house. This object also appeared to not be flying as fast as planes normally do. The video of it was taken on a Samsung Note 9. The sounds you here in the video is mostly the water running out of my fountain and into my pond. There's also the sound of my brothers fiance talking, and crickets chirping. The thing is flying without making a noise.
