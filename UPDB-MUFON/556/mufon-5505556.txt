{nd} Taking pictures of moon when I saw object in telephoto.

Moon was supposed to be big due to proximity to Earth. I took camera out and took some pictures. I saw the object and tried, hurrying, to get decent telephoto shots. Batteries went dead. I hurried into house to change batteries and upon return the moon was behind western ridge. I looked for object but didn't see it. My thoughts are that it may have been orbiting our moon. In photos object is bottom right of moon.
