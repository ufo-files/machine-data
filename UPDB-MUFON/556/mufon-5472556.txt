{jpg} Red Glowing Object Following Prop Plane

The witness observed a red-glowing object following a prop plane at a distance of 200 feet back and below the flight path of a landing prop plane. The plane was heading north. It descended to the north-east as it was landing. The glowing red object continued to the north. The elevation was estimated at 1,000 feet when first spotted, continuing at a speed estimated at 100 mph. The size of the object was approximately 20 feet in diameter. The witness observed the object as the plane crossed over 380/20 near east Waterloo. The event took place at 7:40 p.m. A similar glowing object was observed the very next day in the same area but was not observed following a plane.
