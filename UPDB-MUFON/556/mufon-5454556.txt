{rjl} Multiple objects traveling in line

While out cooking on the grill late on 3/21 I witness distant objects in the sky traveling in a line. All were exactly the same distance apart all the same brightness and all traveling the same path. This lasted for 10-15 minutes. I have watched satellites in the sky on many night here and have never seen them in this kind of course.
