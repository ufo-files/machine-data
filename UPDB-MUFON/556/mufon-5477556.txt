Blue light flying across sky. Joins 10 + other lights in moving constellation. All change coloursk

We were driving down Donald b Munro rd at exactly 7pm. I saw a blue light crossing the sky with what seemed like a fair amount of speed. It jumped up abruptly and then we noticed there were likely ten other objects hovering in a formation that looked like a slowly moving constellation. We pulled over and got out of the car.  The lights began to change colours from blue to green to white white and red. One of the lights appeared to get bigger and flashed a very bright white. 

The lights faded behind some clouds that had moved in and then were no longer visible
