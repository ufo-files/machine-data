3 orbs, different directions, got close to each other, got bright and shot out of atmosphere

Sitting with the third witness in her back yard, we looked to the sky as she was saying just the night before, she saw strange lights in the sky. Immediately we saw three orbs flying across the night sky very high and too fast to be an airplane. The orbs came from different directions and seem to be flying toward each other. Once together, the orbs stopped, got very bright and shot upward away from the ground and disappeared. This all happened while the pope was in the same city visiting the US in 2015. Witness 3 had other sightings of unidentified objects while in her yard. I have since had mysterious marking on my arm appear after dreaming of aliens being in my home with me.
