(GS) light in night sky performing zig-zag pattern

Just observing the night sky above a ridge-top on the north side of Coyote Lake, between 9:30 and 10pm. 

Light was performing a zig-zag pattern.

At first it look like one of many stars, but it was moving.

It was a pinpoint of light high in the night sky going first one way, then another, then another and so on in a zig-zag pattern.

I felt it was an unusual way for a light in the night sky to move. Doing a zig-zag pattern in the sky would involve sharp angle turns that normal aircraft could not do.

It seemed to get more distant and lost sight of it. Plus I was busy talking to other campers about other things.
