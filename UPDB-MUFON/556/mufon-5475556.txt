{dr}Three very bright lights in perfect triangle

I was sitting in my hot tub looked up towards the south I know it was to south as fly that route often and saw 3 bright stars, at first I thought WOW I never seen those before and watched them for about 1-2 minutes then one by one the dimmed and vanished . I thought they were stars or at least satellites , but they never moved or blinked (1-2) minutes , and they were bright very bright in the shape of a perfect triangle . They were brighter then 99% of all the other stars .... I never in my life seen anything like it .
