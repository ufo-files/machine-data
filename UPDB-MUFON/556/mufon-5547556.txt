We saw seven ovnis (UFO).

There was around 6 pm and my boyfriend and I were at the backyard of his house. He told me to look at the sky asking me what I thought all those flying objetcs could be. Then I looked, and the thing I saw was a straight line of seven shiny circle objects of small diameter from the distance they were, I can not calculate the altitude, but probably the same altitude that a small plane flys. In few seconds that straight line became a V shape line so fast I could not found out how, and then we lost them becose of the clouds, and did not see them again.
The reason I am writing to you is because 2 days ago I watched a show at discovery channel and the images I watched at tv are very similar with the thing I saw last year in person. I do not pretend to be investigated, I just know what I saw and wanted to share it with you if helps you in any way.
