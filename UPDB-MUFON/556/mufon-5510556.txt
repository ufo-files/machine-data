{cak} VERY bright starlike light moved west to east relatively quickly.

I was outside putting the garbage out when I observed a very bright star (or so I thought). It was a perfectly clear night on 6/3/13 at 10:30 at night. The light was easily the brighest star in the sky, even moreso than Id say when venus is at its brightest. I stood admiring it for a second when I realized it was moving. I watched it for about five minutes as it moved across the sky, moving west to east. As it progressed I saw it pass behind trees, in my line of sight and then appear on the other side of them as it made its progression. It continued in a straight line until it was out of sight on the eastern horizon. 

I don't know if there was any known asteroid or meteor but it didn't have a tail or a streak. It was just really bright and moved in a straight line. The light it gave off didn't blink or change from a steady white star-like light. After it passed to the east I noticed a plane coming from east towards the west (towards me) with the normal blinking lights on the wings etc but it was much higher up than what I saw moving eastward. Im a horrible judge at size and distance and how high up something is but the thing I saw was much lower when compared with that plane I saw afterward.

I wish I could have gotten a video of it but I left my phone in the house, not expecting Id need it to take the trash out.
