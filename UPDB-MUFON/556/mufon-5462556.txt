{cak} Loud bangs, dogs barking, three bright lights in sky

I was barely falling asleep and then I heard 8-9 loud booms that sounded like gun shots. Flashes of white light all 8-9 times. I was laying in bed and after the shots dogs were barking and the bed trembled. I looked out the back window and saw nothing but then came to my room and saw the three bright lights out the window...two were white and seemed to be trailing behind the bright reddish orange object in the sky. When I first noticed them I thought of them to be planes but they weren't moving and they weren't making any sound. The objects were just staying in place as if they were hovering and when I started to watch them closer they started heading towards my house and then all three made an abrupt turn away and then "flew" off I was absolutely stunned and in shock and honestly scared for my life. I was panicking so I went to find my dog and when I looked at the front door it was unlocked and my dog was hiding terrified and whining in the basement. I had video of it but when I woke up to look at the video more it was gone off my phone. Would be willing to sketch out sighting
