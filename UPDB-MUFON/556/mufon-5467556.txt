Lights in the sky like the ones seen in Arizona and Mexico. At 9:40 PM on July 4th 2018. while standing on my roof watching fireworks all over town I saw some bright lights in the western sky just above tree line moving in formation. The first one I saw seemed to just appear and I thought that it was some kind of airplane flying over downtown Denver for the fireworks show. Then another one just appeared and another all in a formation like ducks all three in a row moving slowly to the west it seemed. Then a 4th one appeared and lined up on the other side of the formation like fighter jets or an upside down L. Then they started a disappearing and reappearing act in different formations one of them dove towards the ground in a zig-zag motion I never saw it go back up. while the others one by one disappeared. I stayed on the roof for another 10 min. and didn't see them any more.
