Orange/reddish orb

The object was way to far to tell its distance, I would say much higher then a commercial airliner. 

My fiancée and I had just stepped outside when I looked up and noticed a very bright red/orange light far in the horizon coming our direction, very high in the sky and what seemed to be extremely fast. As it was coming our way I noticed that there was no possibility that it was a jetliner. We quickly pulled our phones and started recording this orb, the object would move insane change direction in ways I’ve never seen. Almost like a laser beam, just before I stopped recording it vanished, mind you it was a clear sky. No clouds that I could see.
