{rjl} Blue lights appearing like a constellation, they didn't move, then instantly went dark or invisible

I was taking out the trash behind my home. it is very dark where we store our garbage cans, away from city lights. For some reason, I decided to look at the sky. As I look directly to my south, there appeared what looked like a normal star constellation except all of the stars were noticeably blue. It looked odd and out of place. I asked my wife to come look. When she saw them, I asked her, "Why are they all blue". She said, "I don't know". At that moment all of the orbs(?) went dark or invisible, all of them. It seemed as if they all just blinked out of existence. My wife did witness this as well.
