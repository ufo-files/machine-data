Multi star pattern coming from east. staying in unchanging formation. stopped

Often, when the sky is clear, I look out bedroom window briefly before sleeping. This past Tue. at 11pm I looked out at a pretty clear sky and, after a min., saw a star pattern in the distant east that incl. 3 stars in a perfect line. As I continued to watch, I realized that the pattern of 9 stars (later aware of two smaller sattelites) was slowly moving straight across the sky toward me. I thought, Wow, I'm finally seeing something. I noticed that the pattern was not moving in a normal rotational direction as all other stars do. It came in a straight line, slowly and never changing formation. I quickly got my binocs.(7x35/8*)and after 30-35 min. I thought it was going to pass out of sight over my roof but it stopped just short of the roof overhang. I noticed that in addition to the 9 primary stars, there were 2 smaller, less bright sattelites that could only be seen with the binocs. During the next 25-30 min. saw the usual stars rotate towards the south but the pattern sat still. During the 1/2 hr. or so the pattern very slowly eked west (prob.rotation related). A little ways S.E. of me are the Fermi towers and I wondered if this UFO's observance had anything to do with them, hoping not. At about midnight, with nothing else happening, I went to bed. The following day I looked around the web, hoping to see some confirmation, but nothing. They were basically the same brightness as any star (way,way up there) and moving slowly so I understand why they weren't obvious. I wish I could show you the pattern because it included 2 perfect rows of 3 and a perfect triangle on the right end. None of this surprises me, because of my reading for the last 4 1/2 yrs. but it was a trip because it was the first sighting for me!
