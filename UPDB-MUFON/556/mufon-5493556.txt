{dr}Solid red light with flashing white strobe. Hovered and changed direction mid flight.

I was standing in my driveway and looked up in the sky as I do from time to time. At first I thought nothing of this object as it was a flashing light similar to any old airplane. But as I kept watching this object, I noticed the red light did not waver. Only the white light was strobing. Then, as I continued to watch this object, I noticed it was stationary and way too high (in my experience) to be a helicopter. Something felt strange about it. So I grabbed my iPhone and started filming it. The object began to move in a southern direction quickly. It then appeared to stall and hover holding it's position for a moment. It then changed direction heading back the direction it just came from. I had zoomed in to capture it better, but realized that there was no reference point. I zoomed out and caught the object again hovering or slowing WAY down. Then it moved some more headed north west. It slowed and shot a little forward and then again sped forward quicker. Headed north west. At that point the object continued off in that direction until it was out of sight. I wish I had a better camera near by. It was definitely unusual. I will attempt to post the video. But do not believe I can do so from my iPhone.
