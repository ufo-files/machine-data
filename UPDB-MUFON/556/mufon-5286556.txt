Pulsating color changing light.

On the 18th of November, 2019 at 6am I was outside my back yard when I noticed a small light on the sky. It was changing colors and was moving in different directions but between a small perimeter. I did not hear any sound. I took a short video of it on my iPhone but I had to zoom in all the way to see it better but the quality of the video is not soo good.
