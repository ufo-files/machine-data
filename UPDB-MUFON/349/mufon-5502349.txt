{nd} Three white lights in triangle moving North, then fading out one by one

I am a trained ex MUFON observer. I walked out to my driveway and was looking at the night sky. I noticed a triangle of lights that should not have been in this area. I then noticed that they were moving slowly to the North. All had the same white intensity. One by one they went out. I stayed and watched the area for five minutes and saw nothing. Having many years in the MUFON FI and ASD, I think I know what I am looking at. My possibilities include, three satellites, not likely' three aircraft; very unlikely; atmospheric sprites; very unlikely; and three unknowns. I felt that it was a little odd that I happen to be looking at this area and I saw this, however, it would not be the first time I have seen these. I have seen many over the years.
