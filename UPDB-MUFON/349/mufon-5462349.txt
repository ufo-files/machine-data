Up until that point, my grandfather hadn't done anything with his life except drinking and partying. That all changed after this event. 

Shortly after the event, my grandfather ended up getting a job with the U.S DOD. He heald an above top secret clearance. Travelled all over the U.S and the world for his job. He did not tell any of us what he did until his deathbed. He died at 65.
