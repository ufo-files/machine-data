[cak} Witnessed fireball with tall tongues of flame hovering for approximately 1-2 minutes. Object then slowly began to travel east. I gave chase as on foot as the object disappeared into distance.

Upon returning home early from a friend's house on New Years I was walking up my street when I noticed a fireball silently hovering in the sky at an undecipherable altitude. The base sphere was about the size of the tip of a pen-cap and had approximately 5-7 tall tongues of flame, roughly twice the height of the sphere below it wavering rapidly. My immediate reaction was to assume that I was about to witness a plane-crash, but as I observed, the object hovered rather than falling. I stood watching, trying to decide what it might be, but anything on fire like that would obviously crash, so the only remaining possibility was a UFO. After a minute or two the object very slowly began moving east. I pursued on foot. Despite its apparent crawling pace, the object quickly moved off into the distance, making me believe that it must have been at a very high altitude.
