{jpg} 8 objects traveling W to E coming together

Happened to look up and noticed 6 white star like objects in triangle formation (2 separate triangles of 3 lights) moving west to east quickly (not as fast as a meteor but much faster than the jets headed to LAX). About 5 secs into sighting 2 more objects came from the SW to join and at that point they started to form a single line and move closer to together. This is where I lost sight as they moved behind the house. Was in the backyard and ran out front to see if I could catch them but the street lights and traffic made that impossible.
