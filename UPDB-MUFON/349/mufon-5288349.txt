Bright orange orb that did it move like a plane or drone

Outside on deck on the second floor of my home around 10:30 when a bright orb (orangish color) appeared in the sky above treetops. I was outside with my teenage daughter who noticed it first and recorded. Another friend somewhat in the area noticed something very similar around 11:00 pm. 

It seemed as if it was dancing. It separated into two before the smaller one reabsorbed into larger one. The orb brightened and flashed and appeared as if it was getting closer. It disappeared and reappeared a couple times. Did not stick around too long to see more (went inside) because it was a bit creepy. Peered out the window one more time to see it still out by the treetops then disappear again. The video does not truly capture how bright and close it was. Have never seen anything like this. I do live on a flight path to O’Hare airport but I frequently watch airplanes and they never look like this or get this close. Hoping for some scientific explanation.
