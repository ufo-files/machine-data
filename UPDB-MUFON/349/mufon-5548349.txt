Red, shaped like an airplane pointing up. Did not move. Others saw it too.

Playing golf with my buddies, and all three of us saw this red object in the sky. It looked to be at about 10000 feet. It reminded me of an airplane, but it was not moving, it was pointing up, instead of horizontal to the horizon. Saw it for about 15 minutes, then the last time we looked up it was gone. Played golf again the next day, and a woman I've never met, who was playing golf at a different golf course in a neighboring town said she also so the red object in the sky when she was golfing 5/24 as well. It was easy to notice because the sky was blue with white clouds, and this object was vividly red, so it stood out against the sky.
