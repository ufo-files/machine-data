Orange lights on black crafts becoming grey bubbles

Three craft coming from the East going West in a straight line, keeping the same distance from each other and each with three bright orange lights in a triangle shape on the front. As the first got overhead the three lights went off and it changed shape, followed by the second and third doing the same within 2-3 seconds of each other. They all became giant gray bubbles with floating, moving sides much the same as the giant bubbles from a kid's blow bubble toy except that the surface of these crafts seemed to look more like a floating and blowing gray canvas tarp that you could almost see through. There was one orange light remaining inside each circling the interior in a perfect circle. The altitude would be about the same a traffic helicopter would stay at. The speed was 10 times that of a traffic helicopter. And there was absolutely NO sound.
