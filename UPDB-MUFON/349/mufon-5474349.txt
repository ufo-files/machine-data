{cak} 3 circular flying objects hovering over West Portland and Beaverton, seemed to be patrolling the area. Each flied in different ways

On August 16th 2017, we were in our friends house to look for her documents. She wasn't there because she had moved out of state a week ago. I loved her telescope so I to it to their 180 degree balcony and started stargazing at 9:37pm(western time) Portland, Oregon. After searching for 5 minutes, I looked up to get an eye break. Then, I noticed about 7 airplanes flying to the city airport and when I looked at a flight tracking app, I saw that they were all actual planes. Then I decided to go back to stargazing but noticed three additional objects. When I checked the radar again, I didn't see the objects documented and labeled so I thought they were military aircrafts but after gazing for some time, I noticed they weren't moving. That's when I took out my binoculars and looked closely. My jaw dropped when I saw the lights and the shape of 1 of the 3 Objects. There were no windows or cockpits and there were flashing lights with about 3-4 colors. The object was hovering like a helicopter. I observed the same thing with the other two objects. I called my dad and explained everything to him but he laughed at me and left after looking at them. I followed them for 3 extra minutes and saw all three of them line up in a straight line and then like a race, they took off in different directions at a very high speed. The other two disappeared behind the hills and I barely followed the third one which disappeared faster . I checked my tracker one more time and didn't see them. I also recorded three 2 minute long videos. I do believe I saw three unidentified flying disks.
