Daytime orb, Oahu, Hawaii, 8-10-101

I was on vacation this past week in Ohau, Hawaii. We visited the Arizona Memorial on August 10, 2010 between the hours of 8:00 and 9:00 a.m. The photos were taken between 8:30 and 9:00 a.m. on the boat returning from the memorial. I didn't see the object while I was shooting the pics and was both surprised and pleased to see it this evening while I was uploading the vacation pics from my camera to my computer.
