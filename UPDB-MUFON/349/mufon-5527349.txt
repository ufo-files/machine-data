2 bright stars ,top one brighter. vertically linear, moving slow downward.

I look at the sky very often to see if it is a good dark sky for looking. I have a scope and binoculars and just like to look up. I went out on my deck porch behind the house and just under the big dipper's top of the pot's star, I measured about 35 degrees with a compass (afterwards), I noticed 2 star like objects linear about 2 fingers apart top to bottom moving slowly downward for about 10 to 15 seconds and then dis appearing. they were brighter then the stars that were out at the time. I never seen any thing like that in the north sky. I know the airplanes pattern which is further east, but these objects were moving in the opposite direction of the planes flight path.
