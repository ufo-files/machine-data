{jpg} I saw 2 things that scared me half to death

I went out to have a smoke between 3-4 in the morning and I was talking to my friend on the phone when I saw a flash of light fall from the sky. I thought it could've just been a plane or something, but it looked like it was so close, I would've heard a crash if it was a heavy object like that. And it didn't just fall. It shoot down from the sky. Planes or anything else couldn't have fallen as fast as what I saw. I came back out for another smoke around 3:45 am and I started to see a small flashing light, yet again just thinking it was a planes light flashing. It was flying away from my house in the beginning and then once I told myself it was just a plane, it started hovering towards my house. I got scared once I saw that and I stood up, about to go into my house. I took one last look at it and it was above my neighbors house, still coming close and closer until I went inside. I felt so uneasy for the whole night that I couldn't get to sleep until around 6 am. I've never been a firm believer of aliens and UFOs because I was just messing around with conspiracy theories about aliens in middle school because they were all the hype. But after last night, I can now say that whatever that was, wasn't normal.
