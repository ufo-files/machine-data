Multiple Orange orbs moving north to south. Primarily in line however two of them “touched” and half-circled one another.

The lights were bright orange at first but as they went overhead and I began recording, they shut the light off. Gray-metallic objects could be seen when the lights shut off. All of them displayed unusual flight capabilities and once the lights were out, they quickly disappeared toward the south.
