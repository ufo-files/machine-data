At home watching the national news and heard about the event of the super moon. Went outside of home with smart phone to take picture of the moon. At the time of snapping the shot did not notice the lights at all. A few days later I wanted to look at it again in the phones gallery. That's when I noticed the redish orange lights at the bottom right. When I enlarged the photo it was certainly strange. It appeared not to be a plane or helicopter to me. What I see is two rectangler redish orange lights shrouded in clouds. It was as if you were looking up at the bottom of something. My feelings are mixed. I have always lived less than 5 miles from Tinker AFB all my life and have seen hundreds of plane/helicopters at night and they never looked like what was captured in the photo. Mostly just curious to what this could be. Really didn't lose sight of object, just have a picture.
