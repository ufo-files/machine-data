TRianGular Fire-Ball... Hovering then mimicks a plane

I observed it for closed to 7 minutes when it started heading westbound as it moved it seemed to dip down then go back up a little higher, it kept moving when all of the sudden a plane flew by between the object and my stand point. 

The unkown craft then all the sudden change from a amber ball and seemed to mimick the aircraft passing by, the unknown craft then started heading my way. (Note: I have seen plenty planes in my life to know, that the sudden change of direction for this craft wasnt typical of a jet liner) anyhow it headed my way and zoomed just about 1000ft just a few houses short of where i was and headed north/west.
