These spheres were in the 30,000 foot plus range - when i saw movement casually looking up i caught these craft in my field of vision.
I immediately knew these were NOT conventional aircraft and thy were NOT planets. They moved much like an aircraft - the shape was just different. Their movement was very uneventful - they just seemed to be on a straight path - They were NOT weather balloons. I have obeserved weather balloons in the past and these were not balloons. They were perfectly spherical at 30k plus. (Balloons deform in shape the higher the are in altitude). They looked like large pearls. In comparison to a 747 i would say that their width was 3-4 times as wide and every bit as long if not longer. The trailing sphere seemed to be half the size of the leading sphere. 

Because the event was so benign, it took me a few minutes to really digest what i saw. I keening looked at these objects until they were out of sight beyond the horizon.
