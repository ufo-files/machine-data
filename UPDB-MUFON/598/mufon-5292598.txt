Cylinder shape reflective craft

On lunch break I heard a rumbling noise quite loud which I pinpointed by sound to a high flying reflective shape which I could just about make out to appear cylinder shape, no wings or any discharge of fumes and no contrail, noise went immediately as I saw it, moving very slowly heading roughly north and appeared to be climbing up in the sky reflecting sunlight, I observed it for around a minute before getting my lunch It was still there when I went for lunch, there was an air liner roughly at the same time heading south leaving a contrail but the noise I heard seemed to loud to be coming from that and a different direction.
