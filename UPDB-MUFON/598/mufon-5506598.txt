I was just standing in my backyard one afternoon and looked up and saw a sliver craft moving from north to south. It was pretty high and had no wings, made no noise. I thought it might be a balloon but seemed to be moving under its own power in a straight line. It had a definite circular shape that I could see and it was metallic with a dome.
