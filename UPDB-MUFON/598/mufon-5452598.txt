Black Triangular Craft nearly hovering silently 500 feet in altitude directly overhead

I was in my West St. Paul apartment sometime between 2000 and 2200 when my Uncle calls my Mom and I out to the 3rd floor balcony looking South."You got to see this." When we step outside, we immediately see a black triangular craft with white lights on its corners, silently hovering less than 50 MPH, under 1,000 feet, travelling North, coming straight at us. As it approached, we could see features on its matte black hull, darkening the street light lit sky. We watched the craft fly towards us for about 2 minutes until it went out of site DIRECTLY over my apartment. By the time it was directly overhead, I would say it was at 500 ft, and going 15 MPH. It was the size of a large airplane. My Uncle and I went to the North side of the building to keep an eye on it but it was gone. Less than 30 minutes later, we watched (heard and saw blinking lights) 2-4 military helicopters about a half mile away traveling NE at a low altitude. This was 2 months after 9/11 and flight traffic was considerably less, 5 miles East of MSP International Airport.
