Orange Lights in the sky.

I stepped out onto my back patio at about 10:40 PM and saw several orange lights in the sky traveling from west to east. The lights appeared to be pulsating slightly. These lights were to the southwest of my home. I had a very limited field of view and was unsure how far away the lights were. I decided to get in my vehicle and drove to a shopping mall about 1 mile to the south me to see if I could see them again and get a better view. I parked in the north parking lot near the movie theaters and saw the lights to the north of me. I saw 2 groups of 2 orange lights here. They were still traveling west to east but this time, they turned toward me and went up at angle and disappeared or dissolved. There was no discernible shape to these lights or strobes, just orange lights. The sightings lasted about 30 minutes total and I saw around 30 lights total.
