{rjl} Object hovered over us for about 15 minutes, red and green lights moved toward each other, then object flew away silently.

In the summer of 1974, a friend and myself were driving along a farm road in White Pine, TN. We observed a dark object, possibly triangular, hovering over us at approx. 50 feet. It had two lights, one red, one green, at opposite ends of the craft. It was dead silent. We stopped the car and exited. It was around 9pm, after dusk, and very hard to define the shape. After about 15 minutes of observation, the lights slowly moved toward each other, forming on light, and the craft slowly moved upward and north. It crossed a low hill and descended again on the other side. We followed, but once on top of the hill saw nothing. Strangest thing was the silence. It made no sound at all. There was no feeling of fear, just an amazement that an object that big could hover and maneuver silently.
