Saw an oval or egg shaped black dot moving about the same speed of aeroplane, nearby to the airport.

I was on my phone in one of the bedrooms in my house then I saw a black egg or oval shaped dot in the distance, although not necessarily nearby there was an aeroplane directly below it.I saw this object out the corner of my eye, I wasn't looking for anything in the sky at all. When I first noticed the object I assumed it was a drone because of its movements, however the object was sighted nearby to the local airport thus making a drone illegal in this area and the object was far away and I could still clearly see it (drones are small). 

The object was egg or oval shaped and it was going as fast as an aeroplane during a landing, its moments were robotic and it didn't move diagonally at all. During the sighting I called over my brother, he was skeptical at first and dismissed it as a drone but when I explained to him that this was in restricted airspace for drones he began to consider the legitimacy of the object.

The object moved into dense cloud cover, just beforehand though I got a picture of the object
