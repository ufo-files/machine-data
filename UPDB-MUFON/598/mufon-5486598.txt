{nd} Feburary 2015 I witnessed a star move from the south west to the northeant in approximately an hour.

I was visiting my daughter in Edgewater Fl. I awoke and went outside for a smoke. I love to stargaze and while doing so I noticed a star or perhaps a sattelite moving through the trees. My attention was drawn to the star and 45 minutes to an hour later it was overhead heading out toward the Atlantic. The star started out as a regular sized star and progressively grew in size until it was smaller than an asperin but larger than a star.  A few days later I deviloped an infection in my navel and had to use antiobotic ointment in it for a week before it cleared up. I have never had anything like this happen to me before. I felt very curious and watched through the trees as the star steadily grew in size, it made no sound at all . I have seen many sattelites in my life because I grew up on the space coast, but this was certainly something new to me. I have seen many unusual things my life but not like this one. My family think that I dream up all this stuff but believe me I have not.
