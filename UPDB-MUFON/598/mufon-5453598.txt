Hovering swirling object about the size of 4 double decker bus put together. above electric pylons

cycling back from an evening out with my workmates at a local ranch in the country away from the town. Reached a small wooded area where my attention was taken by loud cracking noises coming from the power lines not far from me. The cables were giving off heavy sparks. I thought I'd only ever seen this in the winter upon a railway lines. A light caught my eye. I lifted my head above the pylons and saw a huge multi coloured object sitting stationary. Above was a dome also with multi lights. The object was tilted in my direction. I stood watching in amazement for about ten minutes. Suddenly I felt afraid and decided to ride as far as I could through the small wooded area back onto the main road towards my apartment. I looked back as I neared home and saw it no more. As I reached my apartment I put my pushbike inside the empty garage and closed the door and walked towards the backdoor of my block. As I looked above my apartment the object was hovering directly above my block. I was a little disturbed by now and just wanted to get inside. Weeks and months later 'things' appeared in my room, seeing entities and a glowing ball. Not long after this my bike disappeared from the garage? I took it was stolen?
