(GS) Red light ascending

At approximately 8:25 pm I was putting dogs out and noticed a red streak ascending to the ssw of my location. Object went straight up and disappeared.It appeared to wink on then wink back out again. It looked similar to a Fourth of July "mortar" dud, but just briefly winked on then immediately off. Had similar sighting night before which wife will file separately. I called my wife to the door, but due to a disability, she could not get there fast enough. Only lasted about a minute.
