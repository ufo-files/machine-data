{tjd} Large round flying vehicles

After sunset while sitting above the Pacific coast, I saw five rapidly moving round objects in the distance over the ocean. Quickly they headed towards the coast and my position, changing colors,speed and direction instataneously. When they changed direction, most times there was no speed change. They could reverse movement immediately. By the time they reached my position there were three, two had disappeared while taking off in different directions. They blinked out of sight almost immediately. The remaining three stopped directly in front of me and hovered, making no noise at all. They held perfectly still. Still changing colors. The light and color coming from them was contained inside of the spheres and did not radiate. They were approximately 30 yards from me and approximately 12'-15' in diameter. After a couple of minutes of observing each other they began to leave in the direction they had come from. Then they did their pattented speed and direction change and headed up and almost immediately were lost to view.
