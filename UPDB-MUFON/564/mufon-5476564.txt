{nd} Watched lights on Lake Michigan

Me and two friends was out on the rocky pier in New Buffalo, MI.. we was able to see the lights from Chicago, so just watching them we was able to see other lights that looked like planes at first but the they changed direction in a vertical way. Then we all started watching just the lights in the air above Chicago. That's when I decided to flick my lighter to signal them. Then one of the lights zoomed from Chicago to above us in seconds and had a spot light on us. We then crawled down into the rocks being scared. After a minute or two the object went farther east and we all ran from the rocks of the pier. The object was gone. we did see the object and looked like a big triangle an was like looking at an engine of a car with a cigarette lighter.
