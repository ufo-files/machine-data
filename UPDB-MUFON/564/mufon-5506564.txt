{kt}Strange looking funnel cloud emitting orb like structures then vanishing

I was attending a team meeting in Pennsauken,NJ that day which was 11/6/13. I left work around 3:30 pm and went across the Betsy Ross bridge to take 95 S towards Delaware county. While approaching Center City Philadelphia at approximately 3:40 pm I saw what looked like a funnel cloud forming over Center city so I started taking pictures as I was driving. The funnel cloud started to balloon into more of an upside down tear drop shape and what looked like a large orb started to emerge from behind it. The Orb hovered for a few seconds then appeared to moved toward 95 as it descended. I then noticed many smaller orbs descend to the same area. I continued to take pictures as the smaller orbs followed the large orb back into the funnel cloud and then the entire funnel cloud instantly shoot above the rest of the clouds leaving what looked to be some type of propulsion trail in the sky... The entire event was no more than 5 - 10 minutes.
