{rjl} Witnessed 2 huge fluorescent triangles flying overhead without a sound and disappear in zig zag motion at incredible speed

Moved very slow left to right then stopped mid air for about 30 seconds then zig zagged up and out of sight in split second. We were camping on a mountain top and girlfriend screamed and pointed up at it ran toward me. We watched it hover with no sound, they were huge! With a fluorescent orange color, two triangles.  They froze mid air for about 30 seconds then in a split second zig zagged upward and out of sight without a sound. We were stunned and could not believe what we saw much less the speed in which it moved. It did not blink, it was solid in color bright fluorescent redish orange.
