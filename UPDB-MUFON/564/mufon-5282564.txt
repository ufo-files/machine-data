Bright Venus like Orb

As I drove on county hwy C heading east into Rosellville, I noticed what believed to be Venus at first, but then I realized that it was completely overcast, with no other stars visible. I kept observing the object as I then turned South on country hwy M. My vision became mostly obscured for a bit because of the trees in town. As I left town to where there was open fields, I could see that the object was moving and darting around. I immediately pulled over to the side of the road to watch. It looked like it was just a few hundred feet over the treetops and under the cloud cover. It darted around left and right a distance of about 2 upraised fingers from my perspective. The object then suddenly shot straight up into the clouds. I did not see it any longer after that.
