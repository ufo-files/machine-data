Orange UFO seen near Johannesburg International Airport

Saturday 12 of December 2015, we were having a relaxing time at home in the back yard. The kids were swimming in the pool, at around 4pm I started the fire for the braai and was on my way back into the house. The kids started scramming very excitedly at me to look at the sky 1look there, daddy look there1 they shouted, i turned and saw in a easterly direction a orange ball of light in the sky descending, at first i thought it was a plane as the Johannesburg International Airport is in that direction,but it was too close to be from the airport it then stopped for about a minute, then moved slowly to the left, it stopped again in mid-air and hovered, after about 3-4 mins it ascended straight up into the air at amazing speed until it was out of sight. my wife, son and nephew also saw the light and its actions. i felt very confused about what i saw and took me sometime to decide to make this report, ive seen how people get treat when they report things like this.
