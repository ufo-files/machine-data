{nd} Black object in Eastern sky West of the Moon captured in two pictures.

I was outside taking pictures of the Sun with my cell phone from 5:00 PM till 5:15 PM and my husband came outside & suggested taking some of the Moon, so I did. I took four pictures. I took one zoomed in first so I could capture detail of the Moon, then zoomed out on my second one. I sat down in my chair outside and looked at the two pictures. I noticed a black object to the left of the Moon in the picture that was not zoomed in. I thought: How interesting it was that it was in one picture and not the other. Well, I got up from my chair and walked a few extra feet and took two more pictures. I did one zoomed in and one zoomed out. Then I sat back down in my chair outside and looked at them. The one that was not zoomed in, had the black object in it but the other did not. This time, the shape was different. It now had what appeared to be a streak behind it like it was moving. My first thought was: Hum! Wonder what it is & why did it not show up in either of my close up pictures? Well, my husband & I came inside and I plugged my cell phone to my computer and uploaded the pictures.
