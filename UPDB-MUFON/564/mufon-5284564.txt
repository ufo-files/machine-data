Two glowing silver orbs. Stationary, then took off. US jets arrived 5 minutes later.

We saw about 20 people looking at Crowder’s mountain. Then we observed two silver orb like objects that I first thought was a plane with sun reflecting off its body. But the first object was not moving. Then at least one more appeared. Our entire block was outside watching them. Then they shot off in another direction and were gone. Within 5-10 minutes two US jets flew towards the mountain like they were in pursuit. This was in 2003 or 2004... can’t recall exact date. Nothing can move as fast as they took off. We were extremely excited. Nothing ever was mentioned in news etc. We never saw them again. But their were entire families watching with us.
