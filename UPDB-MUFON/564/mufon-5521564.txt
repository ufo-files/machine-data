Third night of sighting. Predict more to come. Welcome your represenatives to help clarify this anomaly

At around the same time every evening for the last three days and on previous occasions the same glowing sphere makes a slow descent to horizon and when it gets close to the horizon a second glowing sphere pops up usually to the right of the object with the same pulsation glow of pinkish-red. Because of its higher intensity in color and brightness in comparisson to any other objects in the sky, I immediately was locked onto this object until it faded below the horizon and would periodically resurface until finally it did not do so again. I had a clear view of it for the entire duration of its flight. I would welcome any field investigators to join us in wittnessing this event. My digital camera would not show the target in the viewer, so unfortunately I don't have any images or footage. I am sure that it will appear again tomorrow and maybe again the next evening.
