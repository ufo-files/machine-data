{tjd} Four white lights a large distance apart moving same speed together

I was camping in jetty park, cape canaveral fl. I looked up to the sky facing east southeast. The area of sky was framed by trees on my right and left. There were a series of non blinking white lights, Four that I could see spread out in a slight arc all moving in tandem, slowly toward the east. I saw the lights blink out one by one. The distance between lights was as great as if looking at big dippers handle. Each light was a “handle” equidistant from the next light. Slow moving. Also was close to ocean so wave action noise cancelled any noise that may have been.
