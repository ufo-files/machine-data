I awoke on the morning of 9/27/16 and looked out the glass bedroom door, toward the mountains to the East, as I often do. I saw a shining " light" that reminded me of U.F.O. I saw three years ago. I got up, got my binoculars, and continued to watch it for 20 minutes. It hovered, then moved to a few different spots. I noticed that it was whitish and occasionally I saw a blue spot. I went to use the rest room, then came back to watch it for a few more minutes. Back in bed, I turned a couple of times, and noticed it moved West. I sat up to look at it hover. I went back to sleep. Later at around 8:00 a.m., I mentioned it to my wife, who said she, too saw it when she awoke to use the rest room, at about 1:15 a.m.
