(GS) VERY LARGE NON-REFLECTIVE CHARCOAL-COLORED OBJECT OBSERVED IN SKY MOVING FROM NORTH TO SOUTH

It was early evening sometime in the late nineties (1998 is our best recollection). My wife and I were standing outside in our driveway. I was talking to my brother on a cordless phone when my wife said, "Look up1what is that?" In complete amazement, I observed a dull charcoal-colored object in the sky moving slowly from north to south almost directly overhead. It was sort of like an odd parallelogram and was utterly silent. Neither of us could determine what its flying altitude was, so it was difficult to accurately judge its size. But we both agreed that if it was relatively low, then it was absolutely huge1and if it up very high, then it was incredibly huge! My wife and I watched in awe and wonder for about 30-40 seconds before she ran into the house to get a pair of binoculars to try to get a better look at the object. She was gone a little over a minute. During that time, I continued watching the object as it slowly traversed the sky while relating what I saw to my brother on the phone. By the time my wife got back with the binoculars, the object had flown directly south and disappeared behind a neighbors house.
