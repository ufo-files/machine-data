{jpg} I was driving home and saw a triangular shaped object at a distance, as I neared my residence the object was directly above me then moved away.

I was driving home from class around 6:50pm and the stars were really bright in the sky so I stayed looking for a moment when I noticed the object. It appeared to be triangular in shape with 3 lights. None of the lights flashed or were red or green like a plane or helicopter. They were steady lights. As I came down the hill to my neighborhood I could see the object to the north, and then briefly lost visibility. As I pulled into my driveway I knew I would be able to see the object again so I looked towards the left where the object last was, and it was directly above me. I honked the horn so my son's dad would come outside, and he observed the object as well and stated that it was making a noise like that of a plane. I thought it sounded louder then what a plane would. It also seemed to be moving rather quickly and changed directions very easily. I lost sight of the object as it moved towards the east and over some trees in the neighborhood.
