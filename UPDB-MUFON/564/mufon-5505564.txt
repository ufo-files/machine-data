{nd} Filming a Plane, didnt realize I caught something else?

In 2013 on a beautiful day in late September I took a short video with my iPhone of a Red hawk in N. Andover, MA. The Hawk was dropping leaves and debris from about a 1000 feet. What I didn't realize was that I caught something else that I didn't see until I watched the video on my computer screen. Youll see a commercial plane coming out of the top cloud at 26 seconds. Its heading from N. to S. coming out of a Cirrus cloud approx. 7 to 20 thousand feet strait up looking out my vehicles moonroof. It takes about 12 seconds for the plane to go from the top cloud to bottom cloud. Planes speed approx. 500/700 mph. Then out of the right side of the video comes what looks like a daytime satellite (Which Ive never seen or filmed before) going E. to W. Ive seen scores of night satellites none have been or seemed as bright as this day time UFO. This UFO also looks closer to the earth then the plane did and was traveling at 3x the speed of the plane. It only takes seconds for it to go the distance the plane did? It also looks like its arching downward a bit in the video. I didn't notice the second object/UFO until I downloaded the video to my computer other wise I would have taken longer footage of it. You may notice flickering of the leaves that the Red Hawk was dropping if you watch it a few times. After seeing the video I showed it to friends & family because I though it was unique and Im not quite sure what I videoed here. What do you think?
