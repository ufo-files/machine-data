{rjl} Most amazing thing I've ever seen

On patrol at Chateau Nouveau towards the front of the complex heading west. looked above to check blinking wall light when I noticed a bright light flying north. It stops suddenly in its tracks and starts reversing back south. It stops over the two story building in front of me before it drops down very low, 25-35ft above roof tops. I got a good view of the 4 bulbs, 2 on top 2 on bottom. Top two same always same color, bottom two always the same color. Bottom two spread further apart that the top two. Regular colors, top two yellow, bottom two green, beautiful blue glow. Did this with many different combinations really fast. Turns green and flys west towards the end of the building.
