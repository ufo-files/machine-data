Dark triangle with 3 white lights; one a thing each point of triangle. Silently.

Just parked my car and looked up as it was a clear starry night and I like looking up at the stars. A large, black triangle with 3 lights, one at each point of the triangle went overhead. The object was silent. We live on a flight path for Bristol airport but this object was flying perpendicular across the path. Lights didn't flash and remained solid. Even took place at 21:30 hours. Initially thought was the ISS but was too low and fast for that.
