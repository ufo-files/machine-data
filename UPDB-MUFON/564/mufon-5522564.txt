Myself and 3 others were traveling back from _______ in _____ after visiting a hotspot for ufos on the illinois/wisconsin border. We were on a backroad just coming out of a rolling hill valley when we saw it of to the right of the veicle. It was fairly cloudy that night so when we first saw it, we werent for sure what it quite was. It was passing in and out of clouds for a good minute. Then it showed itself fully out of the cloud cover, and we could definitively make out a perfect triangle, with 3 lights on the corners.
