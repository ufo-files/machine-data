I have a video and pictures. A few other random people saw the same things.

On Feb 25th at about 1:45 pm, I was driving west down broadway in placerville, CA. I looked to the south out the window as I often look to the sky's; and saw around 20-40 flashing, star like, objects during the daylight. I pulled over into a parking lot and was staring up at about 35 degrees to the southeast as were about 4 other people in cars. I asked a few of them if they see that up there and they said they did also. I then made my way to my destination about a mile west and once I arrived I took the video and pictures as these objects were still hovering and twinkling like stars. In the video on my cellphone, a few of them appear to move suddenly in and out of frame. I don't have a 4k computer or tv so I havent been able to see how good the quality really is. All I know is video doesn't do it justice. Witnessing this in person was very strange and I have never seen anything like this. Maybe it was just some balloons floating or some satellites from starlink, I really don't know.
