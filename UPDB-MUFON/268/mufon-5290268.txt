Blue sphere observed as though having risen from sea (English Channel)

Have reviewed old photos taken from July 2010, and have noticed this strange blue sphere through window of cross channel ferry heading from Calais to Dover (UK).
There appears to be a trail from the sphere, giving the impression that it may have emerged from the water. There are some corresponding strange disturbances in the surface of the water that may be the point of exit?
