On Tuesday February 9, at around 2:40 PM (20 minutes or so before the Chaser started on Channel 7), I was getting washing in off the clothesline, when I saw something to the south east, which caused me to do a double take,
I saw what looked to be a slate grey elliptical aircraft seemingly emerge from fog and rapidly disappear back into fog, just above the tree line - there are no zeppelin's or blimps active in this region of Australia and there was no fog or cloud of any description on Tuesday afternoon (here on the coast). The sighting lasted no more than twenty seconds and at first I decided it had been a trick of my eyes. I also felt an illogical sense of unease, a sense of foreboding, directly after this 'uncertain' sighting - which lingered for much of Tuesday afternoon, which was inexplicable, in itself. Earlier in the day, I had passed Jervis Bay and observed that the navy were conducting maneuvers off Huskisson and if the thing I glimpsed (fleetingly) that afternoon was an aircraft of some kind, it would be comparable in size to one of our smaller ships (presumably) - as I said, I was prepared to dismiss the incident as a trick of the eyes, but on Friday night, I took a look at Mufon (ostensibly to see if there were any tributes to Edgar Mitchell) and I started reading through some of the 'news reports' and read of an incident reported in America, which gave me cause to reflect that there was some similarity to what I thought I had seen. The American case involved a horse owner who was talking with his wife and two friends, outdoors near their horses, under cloudy skies - he observed the 'thing' passing through cloud and by the time his colleagues had turned to see it, it was gone, but then they saw it for themselves about half an hour later.
Reading that gave me cause to consider that my sighting may not have been a trick of the eyes after all?
