Large Object observed atop Superstition Mountain Peak

Standing at Hieroglyphics Trailhead near the waterfall area. Had been there for apx 3 hours. Looked up at the ridge several times. All of a sudden there is a large visible object atop a cliff that is situated way right of the floating rock. It was bright white, & very large. I had by phone in my hand so I pointed it right away while saying What is THAT! Do you see that. The trail is heavily traveled. My 7 year old daughter was with me & she says What IS THAT! 
I snapped 1 pic, then attempted to zoom & focus to get another. I had it in the camera & during the zoom went out of frame for a nano second then it was gone. I was shouting Its not there & looked up at mountain agin, it was gone. So i took 2nd shot then. I did not see it shoot up or dematerialize. 
Many people were there 1 family standing near me saw it as well but clearly freaked out & took off right away.
