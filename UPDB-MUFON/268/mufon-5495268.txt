{rjl} observed 4 white ligths parallel w/ one centered below blinking.

At 5 min. after midnight 3/25/15 I observed in the East sky while star gazing a set of four white lights parallel to each other with one white light below, centered, and blinking. The event lasted about 30 sec. and in that time I was able to view lights via my 10x50 binoculars. This did not reveal any structural basis to the lights, just lights. Then, suddenly, the lights disappeared. In the brief time it took for me to place my binoculars down and turn back to look, gone without a trace. No decent, no directional change, I got nothing. This is the primary reason for my report. Also, and important, there was no contrail present or related to this sighting. I do plenty of late night star gazing and know the difference between various space and air craft. They just don't vanish in a second. And last night it was easy to see aircraft leaving contrails in the wake.
