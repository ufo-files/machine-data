Bright Star like object traveling E/SE that seemed to fade out while turning reddish until it dissappeared

On July 28th we were out looking at stars when my wife and son started saying "Its moving, its moving". There was a very bright circular object moving East / Southeast at a speed like a plane or a bit faster than the space station. The object had no flashing lights, was star like only much much brighter sort of like the space station but not quite as large in size. Holding my straight out it traveled approx. the length of my hand x4 before slowly fading out and disappearing. 

The following night, the 29th at approx. 10 p.m. I went out to look at the stars I immediately spotted the exact same thing only a bit further North traveling a similar direction as the night before. I ran inside, grabbed my Leopold binoculars and told the wife and kids to come out. Everything we saw was the exact same as the night before but I was able to find it with my binoculars as it disappeared. Through my binoculars when it started turning red and fading out it appeared to be accelerating upward extremely fast until out of sight and gone.
