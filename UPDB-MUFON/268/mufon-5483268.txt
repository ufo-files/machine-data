{dr}It was just there, then beam of light, then gone. It happened all in about a minute.

This was hexagonal of not octagonal, two tiered with upper tier being smaller then the lower tier. This was a dull gun metal metallic color object with very bright pulsating lights. Lights were goldish white and very bright, the beam projected from the bottom tier of the object was a very deep violet color, and lasted literally maybe 2 seconds. After the object shot off almost in the blink of an eye leaving a neon color blue trail that shortly vanished with the object. The trail disappeared sort of like falling dominos.
