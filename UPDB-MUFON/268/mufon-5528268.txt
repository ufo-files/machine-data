Large stationary upright triangle with 3 white lights

Dec.22nd in Independence Mo. A friend and I had left a local shopping center and turned west on U.S. hwy 24, when my buddy asked me if I knew what the three white lights in the sky were. As I looked up I saw three bright white lights in a triangle formation,looking closer I saw a faint outline of triangular object in the sky. It was almost the same color as the nite sky (almost a matte black in finish). The object was approximately three football fields in width across and between 2 to 3 stories in height all sides were equal in size and slightly rounded. The object after we noticed it was stationary for about a minute and then started to bank left. In the blink of an eye it quickly flattened out and headed west following U.S. 24 hwy at a high rate of speed. I kept driving west and within about 20 to 30 seconds it was no longer in sight. There was no sound to the object what so ever. My friend and I are sill in complete shock and amazement at what we saw. I have been around aircraft since I was a child and keep up with the new aircraft technology. At this time there are No aircraft in the U.S or any other country with these capabilities or of this size.
