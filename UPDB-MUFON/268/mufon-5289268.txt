2 long tic-tac mint shape/missile objects

I was driving down the A41 grom Chester to Whiutchurch. The road was clear with very little traffic and visibility was excellent. I suddenly saw 2 bullet like white objects roughly around 6ft long flying in formation across the field to the right of me, about 20ft from the ground. The objects carried on over the road in front of me across the field to my left. I stopped my car to see if they where still visible.

As the objects flew over my car, my engine of my car which is a new jaguar and never had any problems, started juddering with all electrics being disturbed as though a wire had been shorted.

The objects had stopped and seemed to be distorting the air around them. The objects then sided together as though locked together and flew at an extremely rapid speed upwards, taking just a few seconds to disappear as though they had reached the heights of a passenger jet as a tiny spot in the sky over a time of just a few seconds.

The objects i would describe as entirely smooth with no doors, windows, fins etc,
They where long tubelike. Similar in shape to a cruise missile and reminded me of a very long stretched tic tac mint.
