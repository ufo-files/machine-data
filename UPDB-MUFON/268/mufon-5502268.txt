{dr}i observed multiple orbs in the sky

I was working in a celebrity gifting suite at a local radio stations concert. I had left the suite to go grab lunch in an open quad with food vendors. While I was walking to find something for lunch, I looked up and saw 2 glowing orbs floating and moving side to side. When i initially observed the objects I already knew they were not terrestrial aircraft. The way they maneuvered were uncommon of human airplanes or helicopters. At first I noticed 2 orbs, then at a closer glance I noticed 3 orbs in a triangular pattern. After about 5 minutes I noticed an additional 3 orbs floating off the top orb in the formation. Basically it was a very large triangular shape made up of 3 orbs, then the top of the triangle turned into another small triangle. I ended up losing sight of the object because I had to walk far away to another area. I felt very strongly that these were ufo's given that I have observed UFO's in the past.
