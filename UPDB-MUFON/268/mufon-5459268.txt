{cak} Blue beam of light came down from the sky during a lightning storm

I was watching the lightning storm while my girlfriend was in the bathroom in Spokane, WA. All of a sudden a beam of blue light shot down from the sky and pulsated once randomly. There is absolutely no possibility this was lightning or a person shining a light. This light was way up in the clouds and came down at a 45 degree angle behind an apartment complex and stayed there for approximately 20 seconds before disappearing. This was unlike any light I have ever seen before. The blue color was intense and the thickness of the beam of light was eerie. There's no way I'm the only one who saw this.
