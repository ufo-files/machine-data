{cak} I was on my second story balcony one day time sighting multiple orbs one nighttime sighting a solid rectangle

I was woken up around 10:00 a.m. By the sound of a helicopter passing back and forth over my apt. . I tried to go back to sleep but I couldn't so I went outside on my balcony with my ex wife . I could not see any helicopters , but I did see a small Cessna airplane flying low approach in our local airport . And as I was looking at the bottom of the plane , in the airspace between the plane and me about 30 white orbs appeared out of thin air . I kept asking my ex , what is that , what is that . They were all at the same altitude moving at the same slow speed . When all of a sudden a few of them changed colors . The one I was looking at turned red and I went from wonder to fear very quickly . I then went out side to follow them and just like that they disappeared . They were in a cluster formation the whole time they were visable . No sound at all . Another time me and my ex wife were on the balcony at night were we saw a long flat rectangle shape probe slowly flying barley above the roof tops . At first sight it looked Crome like a car bumper , but as it flew by we literally saw it slowly turn invisible like the predator . It made no noise , it had no wings , propellers , or jets . It was so close I could have hit it with a rock . Just a flat long rectangle .
