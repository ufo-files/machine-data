{cak} It was changing shape and color, dark gray, silver, black, molecular continuously changing

I was in a second story condo, looked out the kitchen window towards the undeveloped fields east of this new development, in the new eastern part of Aurora, Colorado. The newest development east. I saw the object which was ever changing shape and color like it was alive, just hovering off of the ground. I turned away to tell my Father to come and see this, then when I looked back it was gone! I know what I saw, it was a clear morning, and it was right there in the field. I believe it was definitely not of this World, and probably able to speed up the molecules of whatever kind of craft it was! So that I could not see it any longer. I ran outside and there was no sight of it anywhere. Again, clear cool morning, no clouds noticed. I think I felt compelled to look out of the window, just a conclusion I have reached over time. It simply disappeared and I think it may have still been there, I was just unable to see it as it speeded up the molecules or something, so that I could no longer see it. I have always kept this in the back of my mind, and am convinced that there are probably an endless number of different life forms out there, many of which have visited our small bubble in space (Earth)!
