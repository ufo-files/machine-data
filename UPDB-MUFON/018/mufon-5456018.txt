{cak} A unusually bright in the sky

We saw a very bright light in the sky. At the time Venus was visible so we assumed it to be that, however, about 5 seconds after observation the object made a lightning fast ‘circular’ movement before disappearing. The short trail was colored red, green and yellow. Nothing can move at that speed, and appeared to disappear quickly, as if it ‘dropped out our dimension’.
