i saw 4 glowing orange circles real low flashing randomly in a shape of a backwards L leaning

It was about 10pm or so..I had jus dropped off a friend of mine at his house. I drove down the street to go back home it is da last street beyond that is the river..I looked towards my right and I saw four glowing spheres/circles with an orange glow to them kinda dim not a real bright glow! It was in the shape of a backwards L leaning on its corner! I got the worst chills ever! I knew for a fact it was a ufo! It wasnt very high maybe 30 ft high and 30 to 40 yards from the st I was driving on! It lasted a few minutes then one at a time they slowly faded away! The next morning or night i really dnt recall. My father was watching the news and there had been a sighting in phoenix az of the very same orange spheres i had seen In mammoth..maybe the same night maybe a say later im not certain! Many years later I was telling a friend of mine this story in oracle az. When I had fonished telling him this story(and I put this on my family) there was a big flash the same orange color of the spheres I had saw! It was a huge flash! It made me feel very scared as if I was being heard...maybe even being warned not to tell my story again! At least thats what it felt like!
