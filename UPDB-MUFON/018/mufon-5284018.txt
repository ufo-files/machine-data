UFO sighted north of Glasgow, Scotland, UK

The UFO was hovering directly above the house, the humming noise woke us up, it had a huge bright white light radiating from it, a green light flashed once from it, I watched it for a minute then ran downstairs to get my camera. It was hovering high up at that point, possibly 2 - 3 km up. When I got my camera it began to move east, the path curved then started going north. I saw a cone-shaped beam of light come down from it, it did not touch the ground. When I was filming the camera was not working correctly & kept changing functions on its own. The humming noise and beeping coming from the UFO sounded digital and was echoed by my camera. My dog was upset by it. It traveled north at a constant speed then traveled too far to see. After a minute or so it returned further east than it had been at first. It was one light then split into lots of different lights, most were white but also a few small red ones. They were together in a group then moved into a V shape. I left the camera running on a tripod on my windowsill, it continued to move around, it seemed to come together as one light again, then traveled southwards and upwards. It seemed to go through a tunnel in the sky, the right end disappeared as it moved along to the south. It took ten - 15 seconds to disappear.
