{cak} light keeping pace with car on highway with no visible source

My dad,mom,gma,and i were in the car coming home from family get together in putnam county. it was about 2-230am and we hadnt seen another car for some time. dad was driving,i was in passenger seat and mom and gma were in back. i looked out the window and saw this light keeping pace with the car. it looked to be approx 18-24 inches across. when dad slowed it slowed. when he sped up it sped up. he turned off the headlights thinking it could be a reflection. no change. after about ten miles he finally pulled over and he and i got out of the car. the only thing we saw when we got out was a flash of light in the sky and it was gone. i did search and rescue for a while so i know it wasnt a spotlight. i saw the episode on hangar 1 about lights and stuff and wanted to report this.
