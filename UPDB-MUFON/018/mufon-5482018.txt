{jpg} Hazzy red and white intence light hovering see from Des Plaines IL and due west likely over Elk Grove VIllage

At 1230AM saw a blurry but intense light from my drive way just over the tree line in the due west direction. Likely few miles away possibly over Elk Grove Village or Schaumburg. The light was red and white but appeared to be rotating unlike an aircraft strobe. The object was very stable and did not appear to change altitude or direction, no movement at all was noted. Noticed the object due to it's fuzzy intense glow and unusual slow but steady alternating light. Did not think it was a aircraft thought it may have been a helicopter. The lights abruptly when out in the same position is was in after about 3min of observation. It may have quickly accelerated away from my location as there seemed to be a faint light that I could see higher and to the left and far into the distance but not curtain that was the case.
