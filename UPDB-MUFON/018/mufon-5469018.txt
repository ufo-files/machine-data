Observed star like yellow light in sky. It dimmed and reappeared about 20 degrees to the left. Dimmed/disappeared again and reappeared in same spot on the right. It expelled large amount of sparkling material on the bottom and disappeared and reappeared on the left again. April 18 (two nights later) I observed a similar yellow star like object traveling from south to north, zigzagging snakelike across the sky. It was travelling about twice as fast as commercial planes below it. It jumped from left to right without slowing down and I followed it out of sight to the north.
