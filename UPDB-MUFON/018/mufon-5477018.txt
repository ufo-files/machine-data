{cak} 2 points of light traveling silently at a high and steady rate of speed in a straight line from the SSE to the NNW across a clear starlit sky.

On this date and time I saw with my own eyes an un-explainable movement of 2 points of light traveling silently at a high and steady rate of speed in a straight line from the SSE to the NNW across a clear starlit sky. The two lights were faint red and moved across the sky to out of sight beyond the roofline of my house in approximately 8 seconds duration. The elevation was not discernable but appeared to be at the elevation that a jet would fly. I waited approximately 15 minutes in my driveway and kept looking up in the same area but did not see any other sightings and immediately wrote this record.
