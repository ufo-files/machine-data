It was just after midnight. I was laying in bed, just about to get up and go watch some TV as I was wide awake and couldn't sleep. When an object appeared in my hallway only 20 feet in front of me. He looked like a tall man wearing a black suit and hat. He then walked into our room, turned continued along the front of my bed where I saw the profile of him. he had a long oval head (tear drop shape), very thin with long fingers, skin tone was very white, he had round eyes and a small thin nose, and a mouth with no lips. He then turned walked up to the other side of the bed, then bent down over my husband, and jolted back up into a standing position while he looked over at me. I then waved and said "Hi, I'm over here now" He then turned and went straight though our bedroom wall. 
NOTE: 
1. My husband and I switched sides of the bed only 2 nights prior to this happening. 
2. My dog and I who were the only 2 awake at the time, were not afraid of it. Almost like a comfort feeling.
