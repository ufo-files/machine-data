Orange Orbs moving at extremely fast speeds. Instant shifting from infront to behind me. Hovering. Metalic discs.

Orange orbs appeared in the sky. They moved around the sky moving in all directions and seemed playful. They made zero sounds. They also stopped and hovered. They moved around the sky at extreme speeds. All of a sudden there where more orbs and they all seemed to be aware of each other. They seemed to have random formations. Around 7 orbs appeared randomly. My phone signal dropped. When the orbs stopped shining you can just about make out a disc like shape. As it was dark it appeared to be a dark disc, however I felt that the tone could have suggested a metalic/reflective colour. The most fascinating element to this story is as I told people about this experience, orangebflares began to appear of the next few nights, as though to debunk my observations. It felt as though the setting off of flares where strategic in order to debunk those people who witnessed these distinctive/intelligent discs/orbs. This experience changed my life.
