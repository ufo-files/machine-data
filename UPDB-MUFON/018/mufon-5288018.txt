small sphere moving from east to west

I was in the front yard mowing and noticed a small twin engine plane flying from east to west directly overhead at approximately 12 thousand feet just above or through the broken cloud deck, The plane was above the object I observed but not by much. As the plane flew towards the west I noticed a small dark sphere just below the plane but moving slower. If the pilot of the plane was paying attention he had to have seen the object. after the plane passed the sphere I continued to watch as the sphere continued on its course towards the west moving in and out of the broken cloud deck that was at or around 10 thousand feet. Occasionally when the object got into the sun it would reflect some of the sunlight shining on it. I continued to watch the sphere until it was out of sight.
