{tjd} Witnessed strange light in the sky

For several occasions I have witnessed a strange object that seems to hover in the sky, get larger, move in odd directions and then get smaller while sitting in my hot tub. This was witnessed by not only me but the other person in the hot tub as well. The last time this appeared I managed to get a photo with my cell phone and when review the photo you can clear see a star to the lower left of the object and the object big and not like anything I have ever seen before. The object has appeared twice before but I didn't get a decent photo of the object until the most recent. It seemed to hover in the sky, getting larger and larger like it was descending then moved in a westerly direction then seemed to get smaller and then suddenly disappear.
