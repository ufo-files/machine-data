My girlfriend and I were outside at 5 am. I was waiting for her to walk home from work. While sitting on my porch I noticed a bright white light moving steady and increasingly fast from west to east from horizon line to horizon line over my neighborhood. I told her to look and see if she could see it from where she was and she could see it too. By the time she had gotten home out had been long gone. There was also and Amber alert in Berlin to the surrounding areas in Wisconsin that kept going off on our phones which had originally woke me up. When I saw it I knew it couldn't have been a plane we're both very avid EAA fans and this thing was moving way to fast. A couple seconds later it was also followed by a satellite following almost the exact same flight path at not even a quarter of the speed. I checked NASAs flight plan schedule because I knew there was a launch coming up but the launch isn't scheduled until Tuesday later this week.
