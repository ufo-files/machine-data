{rjl} Fleet of star-like orbs (glowing or reflective?) above Commerce, Tx

I was training a client outside of my house and we went for a run. After we came back and as we were walking in the house, I noticed one very distinct spherical orb just hovering in the sky about a few hundred meters NW from the sun. I thought it was odd how it just seemed to sit still in the sky for so long (about 2 min) and pointed it out to my roommate who came outside and witnessed it also. We notice it starts to very slowly move more East towards the sun almost as if it were in a floating or hovering motion. Looked very reflective Luke an airplane but distinctly orb shaped. Sometimes would glow but could be the reflection.I know it wasn't a plane because there were a few in the area S of where we are as there is a small airport close by and it looked/moved nothing like any plane I've seen in the sky. My bf came outside to see and out of nowhere it seems two more appears and are almost in the shape of orions belt constellation. We witness these orbs hovering slowly and 4 more appear out of nowhere. There are now about 7 orbs in a triangular like formation, occasionally appearing and disappearing but in Tue same spots. We witness this for a good 5 minutes and then I sadly had to leave due to my client being on a time constraint. I try to capture it on my phone but for some reason I was not able to see them on camera. Not sure if it was their doing or that it was too far, considering my phone camera is actually not bad. Blew my mind but I did not need any more convincing as I have noticed weird stuff like this in the area, but mostly at night. And I had two other witnesses this time.
