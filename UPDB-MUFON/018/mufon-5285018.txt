Triangle with light around perimeter and round bubble dome lights on each corner viewed crossing overhead.

I was in my sisters convertable Mustang cruising around and around 7:40PM on Sept 31st 1 minute from my house I witnessed something extraordinary. 
I was at a 3 way stop sign and noticed a bright light just over the trees to my front left. Thinking it was a drone with super bright lights I slowly moved through the small intersection. As it moved slowly down the road, in front of me I noticed the size was much to large for a drone. Then I realized the shape was a triangle and not drone like. Then I knew I was seeing something that was not normal. It was probably 700 feet from me and very clear. I saw the greyish exterior and the light around the edge. The round lights on each point of the triangle had vertical ribs running top to bottom. To say the least I was a bit freaked out. It crossed over the field to the right of me. Then it just vanished. The lights out and object color blending in with the dark cloudy sky.
