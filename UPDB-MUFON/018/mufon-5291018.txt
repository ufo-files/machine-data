jelly-fish looking lights above Avalon

On Friday night at around 6pm, a massive shape came into formation in the sky about 50 degrees from sea level. the shape looked like a giant moon-jellyfish figure made up of lights. it was an oval-ish shape. then after about 5 minutes, a craft of some sort came out from the top of the light shape and went north. then, after about 2 more minutes, another craft separated from that one and they followed each other north.
