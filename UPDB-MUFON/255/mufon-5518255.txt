Spotted directly above moving west to east, dissapeared over horizon

object was round, giving off a silver light, it was noticed in the western sky as what appeared to be a shooting star descending from west to east, as the object grew closer we noticed it getting considerably brighter and brighter, until it was just as bright as the moon. many times brighter then the brightest star in the sky. As it passed overhead and continued east it appeared to 'wobble' up and down. The light was as bright coming towards us as it was going away from us, however began to fade as it gained distance until it was so far away it could not be seen anymore. Object moved from overhead to horizon in about 3.5 mins. No Strobes lights were seen. Object seemed to maintain a flight line that did not take altitude into account, object also followed directly west to east.
