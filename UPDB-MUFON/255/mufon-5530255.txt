11-13 orange/red objects appeared in sky.

Myself, father and daughter where sitting on outside deck when my father noticed an object in the sky. I turned to look and as i did i saw 1 then 3 then a whole line of objects, actually 2 lines not in formation moving very slowly from west to east approximately 1/2 mile from our house they were small so they must have been high in the sky. I thought that they were flares, but they never descended they just kept moving to the east. They moved slow enough for me to call my wife who also came out and saw them. They were'nt in a perfect line, they were staggered. I tried to take a pic with my cell but it didn't come out. The objects then disappeared behind a tree line. We recently moved in to house and couldn't find camera. My Dad called town police who came out to the house and i told them exactly what I saw. They said that there were other reports of the same things and that they were leaving my house to investigate at another.
