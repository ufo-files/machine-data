WEIRDS BLUE LIGHTS OVER THE SKY

I was with my daughter "Amy" (7 years old) outside of my home looking the atlantic ocean to take a picture of the sunset. And then "Amy" said to me: "Dad, look that strange light over the sky". When I saw the sky, there were three long blue lights in parallel line like a beam light crossing the sky. So I took immediately my cellphone to record that curious phenomenon from the balcony of my home. The video recorder from my cellphone worked fine the first 30 seconds but suddenly something odd happened and then stopped recording. It was like a electromagnetic field that blocks the function of the video recorder. At that moment I've notice that something bright crossed from one blue light and jumped into the other blue light. Minutes later those blue lights disappeared. I think that my video recorder was manipulated to block the displacement of this orb between those blue lights. I need to say that Never weird happened to my cellphone until that day when I was recording those blue lights.
