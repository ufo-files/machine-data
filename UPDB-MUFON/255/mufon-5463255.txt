Took photo from cruise ship balcony. No UFO image when photo taken. Saw images on iPhone afterwards.

1 - On Royal Caribbean Mariner of the Seas. We were in blue water and a day away from docking at Nassau. Took photo on December 4, 2018 at 5:18pm (according to my phone's documentation), from ship's balcony. The two white images (one appears structural and the other appears to be light shining on water but not as a beam) were not in the photo when I took the photo. I noticed the images days later at home when reviewing my cruise photos.

2 - Noticed object when I looked at my photos after my vacation. We were out on the ocean with nothing seen while taking photo other than the beautiful sunset and clouds.

3 - A buoy, then definitely not a buoy. Appeared to be above water.

4 - Since I didn't see it while taking the photo, I cannot state its actions or motions.

5 - After seeing the photo, it looks very unusual to me. I felt intrigued. Asked others what they thought it could be. Some thought it was in the water but I think it is hovering with light shining down onto the water.

6 - Never saw sight. Noticed after I was home from my vacation viewing my cruise photos.
