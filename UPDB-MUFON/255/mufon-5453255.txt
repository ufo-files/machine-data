10 objects moving from NW to SE all same size equal distance apart moving too fast to be airplanes, with no flashing lights like airplanes would.

They were all exactly the same size exactly the same equal distance apart from each other all moving in succession across the sky Over the neighborhood. when they got to a certain point, I was looking and watching very closely towards the Southeast and they seemed to disappear from my view.

I know this because I see them every day and I have never seen a group of airplanes flying equal distance apart all in succession in a straight line flying across my neighborhood.
