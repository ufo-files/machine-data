Home surveillance camera captured 2 UFOs hiding in the clouds the morning of Aug 1, 2021 at 7:43AM ET

My home surveillance camera captured 2 UFOs the morning of Aug 1, 2021 at 7:43AM ET. The crafts appeared to be traveling in the clouds, perhaps using it as cover.

The first craft appeared west of the camera as it traveled east bound in the camera field of view. During its flight path, the craft changed direction of travel multiple times and appears to stay stationary on multiple occasions. While the first craft is in view, a second craft appeared from the north west corner of the camera's field of view, traveling westward initially. Shortly afterwards, the 2nd craft changed its direction of travel on multiple occasions before it went out of view. These events occurred within 4:23 minutes window of time.
