(GS) A brillant oval shapped object descended into view. The light was so brillant it was difficult to see.

I was at a classic car event at the Ford Plant in mid October 1996.
A brillant oval shapped object dropped down through the clouds. The light
was so bright it was hard to see. It was the size of a Cessna and was
2-3 miles away. It remained stationary for 1 minute. It started to whobble
downward and then ascended back into the cloud coverage.
