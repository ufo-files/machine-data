15 red orbs travel in line over 5 mins. video available

A fleet of 15, silent, red orbs paraded over head in a long line. It took approximately 5 minutes to file by before passing into clouds that were at 30,000 ft that evening. While they passed by, I checked an airplane tracker app on my phone and there was nothing on the radar for our area given that most planes were grounded due to Covid. They moved at a comparable speed of a jet flying at 30,000 ft. spaced unevenly but following the same straight track and turning at the same point. They did not float or waver off course. I have several video clips at different lens strength still on my camera with some showing a possible black, ball shape behind the light but was unable to load them to this site. They were video'd in Windsor the same evening (approx. 250km from me)
