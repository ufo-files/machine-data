At first I thought it was a bright Star in the sky then it caught my attention by wavering from side to side and up and down. I videoed it on my cell phone then all if a sudden it streaked off downward and into the Eastern sky

While walking from my workshop to our house I noticed what I first thought was a very bright Star. It caught my attention and I started to look at it and then noticed this "Star" was not stationary but wavering back and forth South to North, East to West in a sort of circularly motion. I quickly went into the house and grabbed my Cell Phone hoping it would still be there when I came back outside. It was and I started to video the image resting my elbows on my stair rail to stay as steady as possible . I videoed for what seemed along time but probably only a couple minutes when all off a sudden the object just TOOK OFF to the East on a bit of a downward path and it was gone.
I have tried to E-mail what I have on video but without success but it is still on my phone. 
David Hay
