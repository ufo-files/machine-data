{nd} 3 white lights in a cloudless sky formed a triangle.

3 White, circular lights formed a perfect triangle in a cloudless sky. I first thought they were stars but they were bigger and brighter than the other stars I could see. Then I noticed they were slowly moving south and, as I was watching them, the front two lights seemed to disappear, as though behind a cloud. I looked for clouds but there was none and the last light slowly disappeared as well. I waited just in case I couldn't see a thin layer of clouds and thought it would pass and the lights would return; they did not. I came in the house for 5-10 minutes. Decided to go back out with my camera and saw a helicopter flying in circles and another aircraft, closer to Nellis Airforce Base, that was hovering. The other aircraft appeared to be at a higher elevation than the circling helicopter. The helicopter left but the other aircraft was motionless, then began to move North. As it moved North it appeared to be the size of a small plane and then it started hovering again.
