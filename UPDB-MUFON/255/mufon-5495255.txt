{rjl} A globular silver object emerged from a disc-shaped cloud

On Sunday, March 22nd at 11:30am (or so) MDT, I was traveling (by motorcycle) east on Hwy 160 toward Kayenta and Monument Valley. I noticed a large cloud to my left (see photos) that had two dark horizontal lines through it that seems too perfect and evenly spaced to be natural. The cloud was shaped such that it looked as though a large disc-shaped UFO may be hidden in it or generating it for cover. I watched it as I rode and stopped several times to photograph it. It was not changing shape like the other clouds around it. Shortly after I had stopped, taken a photo (arizona090.jpg) and started traveling again, I saw a silver globular object emerge quickly out of the top of the cloud and arc off to the left, moving very fast. The object appeared to be tumbling or spinning as it moved and left an irregular chemical trail that looked like a string of beads. I did not have time to stop and photograph the event but the chemical trail was long enough that, had it been an airplane, I would have seen it coming under the cloud and it would have shown in the photos. 

I felt as though I had just witnessed a UFO event. I was not expecting or imagining that I would see a UFO. I think it is significant that I had already been watching the cloud because of its unusual and persistent shape, then saw the object emerge from it.
