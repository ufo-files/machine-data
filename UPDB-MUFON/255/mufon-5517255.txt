(gs) Scouting campout

Taking a troop of boy scouts for a campout in woods on farm land. Group was out together playing game when suddenly in place of darkness the entire area was entirely lit as if it was daytime! It lasted only a few seconds but all were dumbfounded. Since we were in an area without lights there was no explanation for the sudden light. To this day we have wondered what that was.
