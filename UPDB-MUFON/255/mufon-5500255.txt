{cak} 6 to 10 bright pulsating lights flying east to west popping on and off like a light switch

i was coming home from the complex next door from me when i notice 6 bright lights flying very slow from east to west. as i stopped and tried to pull out my cell phone to take video my phone would not work. as i stood there watching the lights, more of the objects popped up one at a time just like turning on a light switch. i observed 6 to 10 objects moving very slow flying east to west. 2 of the objects stopped in mid air and hovered, it seemed like the 2 were waiting for the 3rd light that just popped in the sky, just like turning on a light switch. as the 3rd object caught up with the 2 that were hovering they then flew from east to west forming a triangle. as the lights flew to the west they suddenly disappeared just like turning off a light switch. as i stood there watching more of the lights popped on then flew to the west where the other lights popped off their lights went off at the same area where i saw the other lights went off at. i stood there hoping to see more of whatever i just saw when a helicopter circle the area along with 2 planes that i did not see but could hear because they were flying so high.
