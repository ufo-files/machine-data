I saw about 9 to 10 small crafts coming at me

At 230 am I went outside and I noticed several small dark objects flying towards me. Then were high in the sky but descended fast at a angle. I noticed they were flying similar to a flock of large birds. as they got closer I noticed they were silver or a dull chrome color and had no heads or wings.. they were not birds they were small crafts coming at me. they slowed down a little bit as the got closer. It was like they saw me and then they all stopped for about 10 seconds and hovered over me about 50 ft away. then one moved a bit closer they all moved with it like a school of fish .. thats when I ran back in to the house. I They saw me ... and when I started to go back inside they all flew over a large pine tree and turned and headed south west.. I got scared because they got really close to me and it seemed like they knew I was there. As I looked out the back window from inside the house I saw all of then shoot upwards then come down in a spiraling motion all single file spinning thats when I lost sight of them
