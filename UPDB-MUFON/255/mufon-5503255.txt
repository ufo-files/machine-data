{rjl} Witnessed 8 or 9 large black triangles, some with red-orange light in middle flying over my house. None were making any sound. They were moving very slowly

Tuesday evening at 8:15 p.m. My neighbor and his father were out in front of their house. I went outside to talk to him. He and his father had just seen 3 objects flying over. He said "look a UFO" We looked to the north west and saw 2 red-orange lights in the sky coming over the tree line. Behind the two lights was a large black triangle with no light. As they went over head with no sound what so ever, you could see that the red-orange light was in the center of the two other large black triangles. As we watched, the middle object with the red-orange light slowly went out. We watched the three objects slowly fly to the south east in a row until they were out of site. My neighbor ran home to get his phone so he could take a video just incase another one flew over. At that time I yelled to my husband to come outside. A couple of minutes later all three of us saw two more black triangles with red-orange lights flying in the same direction, again with no sound what so ever. My friend took a video of the last one coming over the tree line. About that same time we saw an airplane to the south of us flying in an easterly direction. You can hear the airplane in the background of his video. We saw no more after that.
