{dr}Wife and I observed three orange lights in triangular form move in sky and disappear into cloudcover.

My wife, Chrissy, and I were watching television. She got up and went to our sliding glass door to let one of dogs out and she began calling for me to come over by the door. She would not say what she was seeing, she just kept saying I had to come and see it. When I got there we both observed 3 orange lights/objects in the southern sky. They appeared to move together and moved in towards the southwest until disappearing. We could not tell if they disappeared behind the cloud clover which was not that thick but we they just disappeared. The incident lasted about 3 minutes and we each were able to take a picture.
