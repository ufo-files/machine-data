I was taking a walk and observed a bright red orb moving slowly across the sky, it stopped, remained still for a period of time and then took off in the opposite direction

I was taking a walk in my neighbourhood on October 31st 2016 with my dog. I live in a neighbourhood that's very small and there is a lot of space between the houses. Behind all the houses are a couple farms and then a valley. There is next to no light pollution in this valley or my neighbourhood. As I reached one of the largest gaps between houses in that neighbourhood, I saw a big glowing red orb of light. I didn't panic, I simply stood still and observed. The orb was slowly moving across the sky. I want to say it was headed west, but I am unsure. So it moved slowly for about 10 maybe 15 seconds. Then it stopped and remained still for maybe a solid 5 minutes. It felt like an eternity, so I will say five instead of 10 because it did feel like 10. I continued observing it when suddenly it flew spontaneously in the complete opposite direction and was gone within an instant. It was lightning fast. I just continued with my walk and tried to process what I had just witnessed. It was a very interesting event.
