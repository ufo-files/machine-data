{rjl} Black triangle seen hovering over buildings

A friend and I were driving, we had just gotten off of the freeway (I-60). I was looking out the window when I saw a black triangle hovering low over some buildings (I think it was an apartment complex). It was so low that it was only about 20 feet over the roof of the two story building. There was no sound that I could hear, and it's movement was almost like a glide. On each point of the craft it had white lights, and in the middle one big red ball that glowed.
