{cak} 9-915PM WESTERN SKY INTERSECTION BEACH AND ROSECRANS IN LA MIRADA, CA

TRAVELING FROM SANTA GERTRUDES BLVD ON ROSECRANS BLVD TOWARD BEACH BLVD IN LA MIRADA, LOS ANGELES CO., CA. LOOKED TO MY RIGHT WHILE PASSENGER IN REAR SEAT OF SUBURBAN-TRUCK AND NOTICED A LARGE ROUND ORANGE/REDDISH OBJECT IN SKY ABOUT 45 DEGREES FROM HORIZON AND ABOVE THE ROOF OF ROSEBEACH APARTMENT BUILDING ABOUT 10 DEGREES. THOUGH MAYBE A HELICOPTER AND TOLD SON TO LOOK - HE IS 26 - AND HE SAID IT LOOKED "WEIRD". WE WAITED AT STOP LIGHT AND TURNED LEFT ONTO BEACH BLVD. i WAS WATCHING IT MOST OF THE TIME - IT GOT WHITE LIKE A LIGHT BULB... THEN WE TURNED IMMEDIATELY RIGHT INTO POLLYS RESTAURANT PARKING LOT AND PARKED. EXITED TRUCK AND WENT TO FRONT OF RESTAURANT TO SEE IF IT WAS STILL THERE IN SKY, AND IT WAS, BUT APPEARED TO HAVE GROWN FROM DIME SIZED TO PENNY SIZED, AND WAS LIKE LOOKING AT SOMETHING ON THE GROUND IN THE DESERT ON A HOT DAY - HAD THAT SHIMMER TO IT AND WAS VERY RED/ORANGE LIKE IT WAS ON FIRE. IT MOVED TO OUR RIGHT ABOUT 5 DEGREES AND THEN APPEARED TO MOVE AWAY SLOWLY AND STARTED TO FADE A LITTLE - THEN IT MOVED QUICKLY TO OUT LEFT AND STOPPED STILL FOR ABOUT LESS THAN A MINUTE BUT MORE THAN 20 SECONDS - THEN IT LOOKED AS THOUGH IT WAS "DROPPED" AS IF BEING HELD IN PLACE AND THEN DROPPED, AND IT FELL RAPIDLY GAINING SPEED AT A LEFT ANGEL AND THEN DROPPING STRAIGHT DOWNWARD THE LIGHT FLUCTUATING/PULSATING RAPIDLY AND APPEARED TO GO OUT AND THEN GOT REALLY BRIGHT AND STOOD STILL IN SKY, AND THEN BEGAN TO DROP ONCE AGAIN AND FADED ONE MORE TIME BEFORE DISAPPEARING IN A CLOUDLESS SKY [WE LOOKED AND NO CLOUDS IN ANY DIRECTION THAT WE WERE ABLE TO SEE]. WHEN WE FIRST GOT OUT OF CAR WE HEARD A SOUND THAT SOUNDED LIKE A JET OFF IN DISTANCE - AND THERE IS THE LOS ANGELES LAX FLIGHT-PATTERN IN THE EASTERN SKY [COMPLETELY THE OPPOSITE PART OF THE SKY] AND HARD TO TELL IF THERE WERE PLANES IN FLIGHT LANE - BUT MOST LIKELY PLANES WERE IN FLIGHT PATTERN [ABOUT 30 MILES AWAY FROM WHERE WE WERE, AS THE CROW FLIES]. THE SOUND WAS VERY LOW IN THE FIRST PLACE AND THEN FADED AWAY AFTER ABOUT ONE MINUTE AFTER WE GOT OUT OF CAR IN PARKING LOT. oTHER THAN THAT SOUND AND THE FLIGHT PATTERN WE OBSERVED ABSOLUTELY NO OTHER PLANES OR ANY OTHER OBJECTS IN THE AIR IN THE WESTERN SKY - LOOKING DIRECTLY ABOVE US AND TO THE FAR LEFT AND FAR RIGHT - NOTHING IN SKY - NOT A CLOUD IN SIGHT.
