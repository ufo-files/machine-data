several small white objects surrounding 4 larger yellow orange glowing objects

This was reported to me by phone from my son on Saturday night, Oct. 1, 2011 at 8:32pm as he worked at a pipeline location.
I do not have all the details, but his location was in Carbon, Texas in area between Rizing Star, Deleon and Putnam in Eastland County. He witnessed several small blinking objects in the night sky at approximately 8:15pm, then 4 larger yellow orange object came in view with their lights/color fading in and out for lack of a better way of describing it. (He witnessed pretty much what was seen near the Brady/Brownwood area)except from his location it continued and kept coming back for at least 45 minutes. He stated the objects may have been 5 to 7 miles away south toward the horizon. He was able to identify 2 jets,as they scrambled overhead to the area of the objects, by their red blinking lights and roaring engines. We were hoping someone else saw the same event. When I saw the sighting seen from the Brady/Brownwood, TX area I thought I would pass along the information.
