2 amber Orbs hovering over Swan lake in Victoria BC on New Years 2017

Me and 2 friends were hanging out at swan lake park on New Years 2017 walking around the lake and hanging out on some of the Benches. 2 glowing pulsating amber orbs were hovering over the lake in the distance high up as if they were watching everyone on new years. These were not fireworks the fireworks were long over. There not Chinese laterns these things moved slightly Side to Side and and up and down. I live on my sailboat near the airport with a government helicopter base nearby. Im very well versed in Aircraft lighting and this was nothing I have ever seen before and was not a plane or Flare. It was both and Exciting and Spooky feeling like I am glad Im seeing this but I sure hope it does not decide to get closer and possibly pick us up. For approx 5 to 10 min the 2 orbs hung around and acted as if they were playing with each other. Both objects left as if they faded out or for lack of a better term Warped or Teleported out of there. They left at a speed not visable to my eye. I honestly could not tell you for sure what these 2 orbs were but I highly doubt they were anything human or any type of secret craft.
