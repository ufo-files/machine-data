(GS) My security camera picked up orb which I eventually recorded a portion.

It was a Wednesday evening and I had just got home and was eating my late supper and watching TV.
I have several security cameras around the perimeter of my house and something caught my eye in our backyard camera.
I was shocked when I noticed a glowing orb hovering as though it was looking into our windows.
The object did not trigger the recording feature of the camera and by the time I manually started the recording, 1/2 of the show was gone.
I was able to record part of the episode and am attaching the file along with this report.
The fact that this orb was looking into our windows astounded me.
It was though this orb was some kind of remote probe or something.
I have never seen anything like this before.
The color on the computer monitor appears to me to be a light pink with some kind of white string like light internal to the orb.
I lost sight of the object because it went out of camera view.
I went outside after this incident and there was nothing there.
