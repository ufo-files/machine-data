(GS) Slow flying craft begins to approach, then turns around.

Im sitting outside watching the sun set, and then I turn my head up towards a northeasterly direction. There is a craft with three extremely bright exterior lights slowly approaching towards my location. It was going slower than any aircraft I have seen in the area.

The reason I mostly discredit this craft as being an airplane is because of where it was flying. Every airplane in the area I have seen fly northwest to south east. This craft was flying in a completely opposite direction.

As far as sound, it made none. Every airplane I have seen in the area makes some sort of noise that is clearly audible, and this was the closest I have seen anything near my house (both in distance and height).

The craft then banked, and this was the final sign, as it seemed to "coast", the lights seemed to have never moved. There were lights all around this craft.

After it disappeared behind a mountain, a lot of aircraft appeared. Seemed at least 4/5 planes.
