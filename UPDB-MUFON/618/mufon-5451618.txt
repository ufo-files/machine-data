Blue Pulsating Object seen going South to North then hovered and returned back to the north

I was sitting on the deck at about 10:45 PM on July 7th. When I saw a blue pulsating light flying from North to South. I pointed it out to my wife and stepson. The object then stopped and hovered for a few seconds then went back in the direction it came from. We are in the landing pattern of SDF and this seemed to be lower than the planes that go over, This object did not make any sound and when it stopped hovering and returned back to the north, it was at full speed immediately. I have never seen a plane or helllicopter move as fast as it did, it was out of sight in just a few seconds.
