{jpg} Crescent shaped light descended, becoming obscured behind residential structure.

While jogging the morning of September 29, 2015 in Churchill County, city of Fallon, Nevada, I witnessed a bright, crescent shaped light descend from a 45 degree angle from my position within the sky. The light gave off a haze, like a street lamp. The light quickly descended behind a residential house, without a flash or sign of deceleration. No sound unusual sounds were present during this sighting. The house the light disappeared behind was approximately one-fifth a mile away. Unable to determine distance from observed light, or approximate landing area. An airport searchlight is located to the northeast of the sighting location. Witnessed the searchlight before and after sighting, and the searchlight was not the cause of the sighting. Cloud cover was minimal to non-existent this night. Witnessed Venus towards to the northeast of the sighting location.
