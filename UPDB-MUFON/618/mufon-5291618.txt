Square shaped object about 50 yards across in size with 4 lights evenly spaced on the bottom.

I was taking the trash out to the car so I could drive it down to the dumpster. This was a few minutes after 10. As I was approaching the car I caught a glimpse of something and looked up straight ahead and that's when I saw it. It was only a few hundred feet above tree top level just sitting there. It was a black(as far as I could tell at night could've been a dark grey) square shaped object with four greenish blue lights on the bottom. I would guess that it was at least 50 yards across. My adrenaline had kicked in at this point. I knew the object wasn't anything that was public knowledge. Then my second thought was that it wasn't even from this planet. It was silently hovering not making a movement and then there was this high pitched humming sound that I've never heard before and it was gone like something out of star trek. I completely lost my shit I was both scared and excited. The whole ordeal lasted about 30-40 seconds I managed to get one picture.
