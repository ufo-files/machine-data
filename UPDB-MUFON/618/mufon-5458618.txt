Glowing Orb that changed shape

Standing in the Salisbury Big Stop parking lot looking east I saw a complete circular/spherical red glowing orb. It was around 21:20 and pitch dark out, I was not familiar with the area so I shrugged it off and disregarded it as a strange lunar eclipse of sorts, or a local building. Entered the big stop to buy some drinks and use the washroom and I came out (21:28) to see the object has decended slightly (Eventually losing sight of it after about 5 minutes) and has gone from a circle to a half moon shape. At this point I took a photo with an iPhone 7 camera with a bit of zoom. Later I went on to Google Maps to see that there was no local features that could have blocked the light and caused the change of the objects light. Unfortunately I was unable to estimate the size or distance of the object, but I would roughly estimate it was at least 1km away. Didn't alert any of my coworkers to it so I know of no other witnesses.

Never seen anything like this in my life, the more I think of it the stranger it is.
