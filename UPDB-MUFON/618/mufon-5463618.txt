{dr}Stationary object for about 15 minutes during daylight

Observed the afternoon of August 24, 2018, a reflective object NE east of the observers (husband and wife). Wife left for a few minutes to retrieve a camera with zoom lens and object was still visible upon return. Object was viewed with the eye as s a white or reflective object in a stationary location. Husband took several photos of the object which was better observed with the zoom lens. The location is in the flight path of three airports and normal air traffic was observed though not near to the object. The object was stationary as clouds moved past the object. Eventually after several clouds passing within the time frame of approximately 15 minutes, a cloud moved in front of the object and once the cloud moved beyond the location, the object was gone.
