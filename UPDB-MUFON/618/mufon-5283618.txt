9 large metal orbs come up over treeline and direction towards observers

9 metal looking solid orbs, probably about 4 to 6ft in size, looked like just a huge metal marble, came up from behind the treeline on holly rd, as shown in coorinates given. There is a feild behind this treeline that is where they looked to have come from. One by one each orb comes up from behind the treeline, rises to about 5 or so feet above those trees and changes direction, a 90 degree angle in our direction, and started coming towards the direction of lake Takuya rd, where we were stopped at that stop sign, about to turn right onto holly rd. The orbs stayed at that level above the trees, one by one about 6 or so feet distance from orb to orb, following one after the other towards us, continuing that same direction as one would be if they were in the opposate direction driving up lake Takuya rd. There was a vehicle stopped behind us with i believe just the driver in the car. I believe they also witnessed this even. This lasted for about 5 to 7 minutes that we were there watching. The speed these orbs were going was pretty slow, maybe 3 or 4mph. No sound, nothing coming out of them.. nothing, just large metal.. marble looking orbs. Eventually i got the other witnesses attention after hysterically begging them to drive away. 
The other withness with me took a video on his phone, but since then has lost the phone.
