I go outside at night a lot , just to look up at the Stars, Moon, and any of the Planets that may be visible at that time. At approx-11:20p.m., I was outside looking up at the clear, crisp and cold night sky, as a moving object appeared from the South, and as it came directly overhead from South to North, almost directly overhead, it appeared the Object, which I had assumed to be a Satellite on a Polar Orbit- Started to what I can only describe as Taking Picture's, with a very, very BRIGHT FLASH, and continued to do so for the next few minutes almost until it was out of site- Then it stopped flashing for about 15 seconds, then started again. As the Object continued out of site it was still Flashing, a STROBE like Camera's Flash every few seconds. This was 1 of the strangest appearances of anything in the sky at night that I have ever seen.
