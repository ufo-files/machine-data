White object flying

At approximately 7-8 PM on Saturday, the 4th, 
I witnessed a white object flying past at approximately 35,000 feet NW in the sky.

As I continued to watch the object, it then flew into some clouds then appearing again. The white object then started burning as it descended. Three white pieces can be seen in the photos on the bottom right of the pictures as this object continued to burn. 

We have never witnessed anything like this!

I hope that this fascinating and mysterious event can be solved.
