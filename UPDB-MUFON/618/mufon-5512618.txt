TWO LARGE BRIGHT LIGHTS

Monday the 4th Feb 2013 I was driving out of our country village at 6.40AM taking my husband to his lift through two villages, before we drove out of our village, we were driving in a westerly direction and I noticed in the sky there were two very large lights very close to each other bigger than any planets we have seen in the night sky, as we live rural on a clear night we can see spectacular views of the night sky, stars and planets but these were bigger than anything we had seen before they were just stationary no noise quite high up.
At first I thought they were planets then realised that planets are not seen by the naked eye just one foot apart that close together, my husband was quite taken back in seeing them ,as I was driving I had to turn a bend they went out of view but it did worry me as I said to my husband I have to come back on my own which I felt quite uneasy about what we had seen, it was just daylight and that part of the sky was clear at the time from clouds, when I got back home about 20 minutes later I went outside to have a look at the sky,at the back and front of our house and saw nothing,
         
I have never ever seen anything like that before,I go out the same time every morning weekdays and again at night at 7pm the same route,

I still feel uneasy about this, any explanation would be welcomed.
