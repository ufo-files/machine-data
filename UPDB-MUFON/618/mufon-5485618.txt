{jpg} EARLY JANURARY THREE PEOPLE AND A DOG WERE TRAVELING NORTH TOWARDS LONE PINE WHEN THEY SAW 15 TO 30 REDISH ORBS

REPORT FILED AT MUFON HQ WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE, BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER 18809, ON JANUARY 26, 2016
========================================================================

EARLY JANUARY THREE PEOPLE AND A DOG WERE TRAVELING NORTH IN STOVE PIPE PASS, TOWARDS LONE PINE WHEN THEY SAW 15 TO 30 REDDISH ORBS. THE GROUP OF ORBS WERE IN THE SKY AND NOT IN ANY ORDER. THEY SEEMED TO BE GOING IN THE SAME DIRECTION AS THE CAR THE WITNESSES A, B AND C, WERE IN. THEY FINALLY PULLED THE CAR OVER TO WATCH THE ORBS AND THOUGHT THEY SAW A HUGH MOTHER SHIP WITH RED, GREEN, AND YELLOW LIGHTS ON IT. THIS SHIP WAS SEEN WITH BINOCULARS. AN WITNESS B HAD A VIDEO CAMERA AND TAPED SOME OF THE ACTION. WITNESS A HAS NOT SEEM ALL THE VIDEO, BUT THE OTHER WITNESS SAYS HE SAW IT LAND ON THE ROAD.
