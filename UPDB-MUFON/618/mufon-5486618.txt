Large bright ball, like a star but much larger and brighter. Then it vanished.

I was driving home from work at around 17:20 on Tuesday December 8th 2015. I was in the front passenger seat. We were heading West on Clark Dr. I turned in my seat to hand my child snacks in the back passenger seat. As I was turned I saw a big bright blue orb in the sky almost directly above the vehicle. It looked very like a planet, like Venus or Mars when they are visible only much larger and brighter. It was very high up and could not have been attached to anything like a building or other man made structure. I thought maybe it would move but it stayed perfectly where it was. I watched it as we drove about 4 blocks and as we turned right onto 1st Ave. I watched it once we turned. As soon as we passed the first tallish building on 1st Ave and it blocked my view of the sky, I tried to crane around and keep an eye on it. When we passed the building it was gone. I looked every direction for it and it was no where. As we got to Commercial Drive where there would be a better vantage point as it is higher elevation and has lower buildings, I got a good view in every direction of the sky and it was not there.
