{tjd} Cloud appears, object appears changes color, shape, and direction multiple times then disappears in cloud.

Around 9:35 Sunday March 24th while working as a security guard, I noticed a cloud come in from the ocean at a fast pace. There were no other clouds in the area from what I remember. This cloud stopped its progression over the downtown/Ventura Ave. area. From this cloud I saw a large oval shaped green light appear and it was moving south. I thought it was a plane moving at a low altitude as the light was much larger than normal planes that fly around here. 
The green oval shaped object then U-turned and started heading north and changed color and size. It was now Red and looked like 2 ovals stacked on top of each other or like an arrow shaped almost. Could not really make out the shape because it vanished from sight about a second after it turned red and grew in size.
It re-appeared a few seconds later as the green oval light again and abruptly turned East for a few seconds before U-turning and shooting into the cloud. 
After about a minute the cloud had resceded back to the ocean. 
Clouds remained over the ocean.
