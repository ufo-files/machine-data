{dr}Driving with my mom and saw them over the lake/mountains.

I was driving on the 15 freeway south through Lake Elsinore and saw (very clearly) three balls of light hovering over a nearby mountain/hill. My mom and I thought it was strange so we kept watching and the three lights zapped back together into one and then split apart again and at one point moved up and down and side to side way faster than any man made air craft would be capable of and it was most definitely not anything "night sky divers" would be capable of doing like the newspapers reported a day or two later. I've been sky diving before and you can't exactly zip back on up and side to side once you're in the air lol. They eventually zapped back together and flew in a curve up and behind the mountain/hill. My mom and I had never put much thought into aliens or UFOs before but when that happened, we just knew instantly that it was definitely not anything from our planet. Also, since we saw that we have both repeatedly experienced sleep paralysis. It's not fun.
