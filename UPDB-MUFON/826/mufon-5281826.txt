A red light caught my eye out the window. It moved unnaturally for a while then eventually made a curvy trail across the sky.

A red light that looked only slightly larger than a star was hovering on the skyline. I could not tell distance from me or size of object. I at first thought it was just a planet or a star but then when I acknowledged it it started pulsing brighter. Then it made a triangle shape with the path it follow then it went up higher and made another one. I went outside and watched it hover for awhile and then it slowly made its way across the sky in a semi-linear path. It appeared as though another red pulsing light was in the West but I only noticed it at the very end and didn’t get a good enough glimpse to describe.
