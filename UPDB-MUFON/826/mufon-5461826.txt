{rjl} CRAFT HOVERING ABOVE WATER

MARCH 1ST 2019,,,, WHILE HEADING OUT FISHING IN MY BOAT, MY FRIEND HIS DAD AND ME WERE RACING TO THE FISHING SPOT HEADED DUE WEST TOWARDS THE SOUTHERN TIP OF LOPEZ ISLAND.. WE OBSERVED A SHINY METALLIC RECTANGULAR OBJECT SITTING OR HOVERING ON THE WATER. AT ABOUT A MILE WE WERE QUESTIONING WHAT THE HELL IS IT. MY FRIEND SAYS I DUNNO. I SAID THE SAME, AND SO DID HIS DAD. THEN MY FRIEND SAYS OH IT'S A BARGE I THEN SAY WHERE'S THE FRIGGIN TUG. WE ALL THEN SAY I DUNNO. SO WE'RE STILL TRAVELLING AT ABOUT 25 TO 28 MPH. MAYBE WE WERE ABOUT A MILE AND HALF AWAY FROM IT THEN AFTER ALL THE QUESTIONS. WE WERE ABOUT 3/4 OF A MILE. SO THEN I SAY I'M GETTING THE HELL AWAY FROM IT I THEN PROCEED TO TURN LEFT TOWARDS OUR DESTINATION. ABOUT 5 DEGREES THAT TIME I THINK WE WERE ABOUT A HALF MILE FROM IT.,,, AS I DO THIS THE OBJECT PROCEEDS TO TURN LEFT TOWARDS US AND DISAPPEARED....... IT EITHER CLOAKED OR SANK INTO WATER THE OBJECT WAS ABOUT 3 IN. WIDE AT 3/4 OF A MILE. HAD A HIGH METALLIC SHINE FROM THE SUN HITTING IT ,,,,,,, VERY REFLECTIVE. AT THE TOP OF IT WERE 3 OR 4 DARK BLACK LINES THAT I PERCEIVE NOW TO BE VENTS OR WINDOWS. THERE WAS NO OTHER WATER CRAFT AND WE ALL QUESTIONED IT ALL THAT DAY AND AGREED AT THE END OF THE DAY THAT IT WAS [profanity removed/cms/rjl] WEIRD.
