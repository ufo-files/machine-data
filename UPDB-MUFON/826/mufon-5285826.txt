Starlight object that moves around and then hovers

For the past couple of weeks I have noticed starlight objects that hover and then start to move around. Today on August 27th I saw two of them one was really bright and it was to the South East. I retrieved my binoculars and saw that it was moving in multiple directions and then go back to the original spot it started. Then it started to change form and then revert back to the small star light form. I tried to get a better look and then it was gone two airplanes came in the area that were flying at a high altitude also. The second object that look like a star. Was actually to the Northeast and it was changing color and started to move around like the first object. I know it was two different objects because I thought the the second object was a star and but I paid attention to the first object because it was so bright and it looked so low around cloud level. I observed the second object. Then it started to elevate straight up over the clouds and then it disappeared. This has been going on for a while the first time I noticed it was about 4 weeks ago when I was driving on a dark road I saw an starlight object changing form and then it would change back to a bright starlike object. I don't have any photos but I will try to get a video with my wife's phone because my phone camera can't see the objects well.
