3 hovering lights

While deployed to Afghanistan in 2012 I was what people called the night battle captain. I over saw radio and chat traffic for the company that was ran through the battalion and brigade. On this night I had to go to the bathroom and so I left. The bathroom are outside maybe 30 ft from the work space I was in. As I walked out I saw 3 lights lined up vertically that were a orange redish color. It was no lume as it hovered and lume dosent hover it falls. One light would turn off and them when it came back on another would turn off. I observed this for about 2 mins when I went back in and told my coworker. He went out observed it and came back in and said yeah that's definitely not lume. I asked about the lights asking if someone shot of lume or if there was any other patrols going on. Others came over the net and confirmed they to had seen these odd lights. As we tried to figure it out the brigade battle captain came over the net and told us to disregard what we saw not to worry about it that it was lume and we didn't know what we saw or what we were talking about.
