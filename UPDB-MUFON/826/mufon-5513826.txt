(GS) Observed fast moving lights in western sky, went behind storm cloud then re-emerged and continued north to south path.

At 6:44 pm 12/9/2012 I observed 5 lights moving across the northwest sky heading south. They disappeared behind a large thunderhead and re-emerged to the south of the cloud. I snapped a photograph. Several slowed as if to allow the others to catch up. Before I could zoom on them, they were gone. Headed to the south, southwest.
