My wife and I were on our front porch wlooking at the stars. I noticed a dim light and thought is was a satellite. I then noticed there was another light slightly brighter moving parallel with what I thought was the satellite. The new light kept getting brighter and dimmer and both lights continued to move together never moving further from eacho other. We watched the lights as they slowly moved over the sky until they passed the tall wall on our patio. Neither of us have ever seen anything like this and cannot figure out what these objects were.
