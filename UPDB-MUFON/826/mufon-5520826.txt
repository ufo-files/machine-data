many small star sized lights

Williamston Mi, around 730 at night, girlfriend and I were on our way to the gym. Avid sky watcher, whenever I can I look to the stars. It was a pretty clear night, she was driving and I was staring out the front passenger window. I noticed two star like lights that were parallel to eachother moving at about the same speed as us. They were about 3-4 inches apart from ground level. They flashed at the same time, and moved at the same rate. When we came to a stop sign I pointed out the two lights to my girlfriend. Now the lights were directly in front of us. Suddenly another light appeared, now there is three flashing lights in a triangle formation all about three to four inches apart. As we continue to watch, the three lights flashed again, only when they flashed back on there was about six to ten lights in a cluster around the original three. As they flashed they separated from the cluster. Almost like they all flew in different directions. Eventually we lost sight of objects behind trees. Myself and my girlfriend both agreed that weve never seen anything like this, and its probably a once in a lifetime situations. It was pretty cool to say the least.
