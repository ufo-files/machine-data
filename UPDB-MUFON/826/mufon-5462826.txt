{tjd} 2 UFO's flying in echelon formation

I was on my way home from work on the 417 greenway about 2:30 in the afternoon. As I was approaching the 417 overpass, which crosses over 17-92 in Sanford Florida the road rises for the overpass that's when I saw the 2 UFO's flying in echelon formation heading south towards Orlando Florida/ The lower crafter was in the lead. The sky was a clear blue with no cloud cover/ What made them stand out was the size and brightness, they looked like two fluorescent light bulbs in the flight. They were just meandering along, not seeming to be in any hurry. If you hold your little finger up at arms length they would be about half as long as your little finger. Once they passed over the 417 a Southwest 735 coming out of Orlando International Airport passed right under them, they were a little longer than the Southwest flight. After the passenger plane passed they started to pick up speed and the trailing UFO did a 90 degree turn to the west and the leading UFO continued on south and both quickly flew out of view. I imagine that the entire episode was not longer than 2 or 3 minutes duration. The reason I never filed before was because I didn't know who to report it to, so I wrote it down and my journal and drew a picture of the objects. It wasn't until recently after listening to Stanton Friedman give a talk and knowing where to submit a report that I decided to file this with your organization. I've seen a number of UFO's in my life starting in 1965, but never as good as this one, my only regret is that I didn't have the sense of mind to take a picture of them, though I'm beginning to wonder if maybe the occupants had something to do with me failing to do that. I kick myself thinking about. I have no way of adding a sketch to this for I've never been very good with computers and such.
