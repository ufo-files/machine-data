{dr}Bright light hovering then moving overhead, no sound

My mom came to visit and we went outside to check on my animals because they were acting strange. The dogs were barking like they would with a stranger and my sheep were huddled in the corner of their pen. The light from the object was so bright I couldn't help but notice it. I didn't know what it was. I automatically knew what it wasn't. It wasn't a plane or a helicopter. It hovered in the air and made no sound. A helicopter flew directly over it and it remained stationary. The light was bright yellow and grew brighter. Then it began to ascend and the light dimmed. It then flew from the south east to north west and a fairly fast speed. When it was directly overhead I could make out a black outline and it appeared to be oval shaped. There were 2 more lights visible by this time and they were a blueish green color. Not as bright as the yellow light. None of the lights flickered or blinked. They were stationary. The whole time it moved, it made no sound at all. It was completely silent. Once it was out of sight, about 10 minutes later, the air outside smelled like lit matches or sulfur.
