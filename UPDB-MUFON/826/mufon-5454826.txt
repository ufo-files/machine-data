3 lights moving across the sky

My now deceased friend and I were young teens at the time. We were camping in the back yard when we saw 3 lights high in the night sky flying across the sky north to south at a high rate of speed. The weird thing is they were crossing back and forth between each other as they traversed north to south. The total time was less than 2 minutes for these objects to travel the clear sky I have seen satellites and these objects were significantly faster
