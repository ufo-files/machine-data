Transparent craft with shadows of standing persons inside.

I was around 7 years old. It was a late at night and dusk. Me and a friend are standing on the stairs to a neighboring house waiting for a friend to come out and play. As we wait, we see an extremely sharp white light coming from the valley. We stay and watch. It gets closer and flies over the neighboring houses across the road from were we stands. I see the craft. It was like it was transparent, or there were big windows where I could clearly see shadows of people standing in the windows. The windows was so big that I cold see the whole shadows of persons standing and watching out. 3 -5 persons. That was the last thing I remember that night. The next day at school, my friend asked me what we saw the night before. And I said I don't know. And it was the last time we talked about it.

It was only the front of the craft that had extremely bright light. The side had only light inside the craft.
