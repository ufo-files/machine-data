I glanced at the sky by chance and noticed a strangely high and larger circular UFO in the sky. The object reminded me of an animal cell. Not perfectly round, but a bright white light inside of a red orange outter orb, somewhat like the nucleus of an animal cell. The object started high but raised even higher to a point that was invisible to the naked eye. The object was lost from sight when it finally ascended too high to be seen.
