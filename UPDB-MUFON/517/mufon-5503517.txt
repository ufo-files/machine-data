I WAS A GUEST IN A VILLA IN MAZANDARAN NORTH OF IRAN ON SHORE OF CASPIAN SEA.I TRIED TO CALL MY HUSBAND IN CANADA WITH MY CELL PHONE BUT I DIDNT HAVE RECEPTOR I WENT TO THE TERRACE TO SEE IF IS WORKING OUTSIDE ,THE SKY WAS CLEAR AND I SAW THIS OBJECT WHICH WAS GLOWING ,I CALLED MY HOST FAMILLY TO COME OUTSIDE AND SEE IT.THE WHOLE FAMILY ,4 SONS THEIR WIVES AND THEIR DAGHTER AND MY HOST AND HIS WIFE AND THEIR 5 GRAND CHILDREN CAME OUT TO THE TERRACE.THEIR DAUGHTER WHO WAS AT THAT TIME IN LAST YEAR OF UNIVERSITY TOLD ME THIS IS A BIG UFO AND EVEN SHE HAD TAKEN ITS PICTURE WITH HER SMART PHONE WE ALL STAYED OUTSIDE AND TALK ABOUT UFOS. PEOPLE IN IRAN HAVE A NORMAL REACTION TO THIS SUBJECT AND BELEIVE HUMAN BEING IS NOT ALONE AS WE WERE TALKING THE OBJECT VANISHED AND AGAIN REAPPEARED BUT IN OPPOSITE SIDE OF WHERE IT WAS.IT WAS LIKE A SUN DISC WHO WAS IMPULSING FLARE OF LIGHTS .THEY TOLD ME WHEN THIS OBJECT IS AROUND CELL PHONES ARE NOT WORKING!PROPERLY MEANWHILE,I SAW AN AIRPLANE WAS GOING FROM SOUTH TO NORTH,CROSSING CASPIAN SEA TOWARD RUSSIA BUT THE CEILLING OF ITS FLY WAS WAY HIGHER THAN THIS OBJECT.I SAW THIS OBJECT QNE MORE TIME,IT WAS 3:OO IN THE MORNING .IT WAS STATIONED IN NE AND I COULD SEE IT FROM MY BEDROOM WINDOW ,I SCARED AND CLOSED THE WINDOW.BUT I COULDNT SLEEP LAST TIME I CHECKED ON IT WAS GONE IT WAS 5:00 AM
