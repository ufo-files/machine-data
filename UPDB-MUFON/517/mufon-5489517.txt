{dr}Was at river late at night, noticed small yellow orb, then changed into spaceship.

I was chilling at the river with my friend. I felt as if we were being watched but im always paranoid, I did notice some splashing in the water near us, but my friend claimed he didn't hear it (he's very unobservant). We were sitting close to a bridge and cars were passing by. Then at a moment where there were no cars, we both noticed a small yellow light hovering above the trees and began questioning what it was. Soon the orb turned bright white and began to expand as it rose up on a blue beam of light. As the shape became more defined it looked exactly like a space ship from the movies. It looked like a saucer from the front with blinking blue and white lights on the bottom where I assumed the door was. It hovered for a small moment then flew over us, I could tell it had a more winged shape and it had red lights on the bottom. I could feel it's vibrations and it's energy and as it flew over my head I thought I might be abducted.I'm glad I wasn't.
