{cak} Boomerang/triangle shaped object with weird light pattern not sure if it was a plane or not

My wife and I were on our way to take our son to wrestling practice and we saw some weird lights in the north/northwest traveling east/southeast, when it got closer I noticed there were 3 sets of lights that were somewhat smaller and each set were doubled up, these were a yellowish orange color and they were in the shape of a triangle with a pulsating red light towards the front of the craft. I pulled over and watched it fly over us and to me it looked like a boomerang my wife thinks she saw the back end of what would make a triangle. There was no “tail light” on the object you typically see with airplanes and there was no sound coming from it.
