{dr}observed group of lights while traveling E on Hwy 50

I was with my (2) cousins as we were traveling East on Hwy 50, in Rancho Cordova, when we observed a large group of lights approx. 1 mile in front of us. When I first saw them, I thought they were UFO's, but my cousins thought they were lights on the hills. But as they got closer to us, we saw they were moving upwards in direction and the shape of the group of lights was slowly changing. We could see that the lights were solid blue-white in color. We could not tell their shape, as it was late at night. The lights stayed solid as they were moving NW. When we saw they were not lights on the hills, we didn't know what they were and we all got scared and started screaming. We were headed in the opposite direction (East) when we lost sight of them.
