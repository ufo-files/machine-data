{rjl} Star-like object observed over duration of time

Joseph and I observed a strange object and phenomenon last night. It was a fairly clear night so we are stargazing. We both noticed an object we thought was a star or planet because it was brighter than anything else in the sky. I decided to take out my phone camera and zoom in and observe it via video because we both thought it we could see it moving just with out naked eye. I will include my videos and zoomed in screenshots I captured. The object was definitely moving in strange ways, it would appear to expand out and in on itself. It was almost like a pulsation in appearance. I also remarked that it seemed to have two “appendages or beams” that came out the bottom of the circular object. Sometimes it appeared circular and sometimes it looked oval shaped. There is some profanity/offensive language used in the video.
