3 spheres in a triangle

Taking my children to school
It was hovering in the sky and I noticed it motionless.
It was a triangular object comprising 3 glowing spheres. It hovered motionless for 15-20 minutes. It then raise up and shot of behind a cloud at speed.
I was in awe because it was not aeroplane. I thought it may have been a weather balloon but I knew it was a ufo when it took off with great speed.
It disappeared behind a cloud. I could not take my eyes off it. I remember I drew the object on an old piece of paper. I will never forget it! I realise it was many years ago but I feel any sightings may be of use to you.
