{dr}Three glowing objects moving west to east. Two with indistinguishable shape, one shaped like isosceles triangle.

As I was walking to my car I noticed a glowing object, much like a shooting star, but moving more slowly and glowing fire orange rather than white. The object moved from visible horizon (or tree-top) in the west, to visible horizon in the east. Filmed it with my phone. As I was phoning a friend, 1-2 minutes later, a second object shaped like an isosceles triangle followed the same path. This object had fire-orange lights on it's tail which revealed the silhouette of the entire object. The second object was near enough that I could have recognized a symbol painted on the bottom of it. I was unable to film the second object. Minutes later a third object identical to the first followed the same path, which I was able to film. None of the three objects created any sound. There is a large airport nearby and I'm very familiar with the characteristics of planes, satellites, and shooting stars. All three objects were lost as they fell beyond the horizon.
