{tjd} Captured images during a Astrophotography session on 2 different cameras

July 12 10:21 PM EST was outside my home using my 6in RC scope with a camera attached as well as a secondary camera attached. I was using the Star Vega for final focus prior to imaging the Veil Nebula. Both cameras were taking 30 exposures one through a Ritchey Chretien 6 inch scope with a Nikon D-750 and the other a Nikon D-3300 using a Nikkor 400mm lens. Both camera captured the same object below Vega. I was looking at Vega while the cameras were exposing multiple images when I saw a greenish spark with a slight glow trail. At first though it might be a lightning bug and continued on my image taking throughout the evening. Upon downloading the images I saw the strange object in both cameras frames.
Now this has happened before on Feb 19th 2017 where I sent the photographs to the American Meteor Societies upon their request. What I though was a meteor was not and the image was sent off to a professional astronomer. The conclusion was it was of unknown origin, lucky catch. 
I am writing you to see what you think, I have 3 photographs, one from 2017 and 2 from two days ago.
These images have been processed in Photoshop where they were converted to JPG, I have the original NEF files from both Nikons should these be wanted for further examination.
