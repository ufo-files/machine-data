They hid Infront of orion's belt and mimicked the stars. Till we saw them.

I live out in the country and was over at a friends house for a bit that night , it was getting close to 8 pm and was time for me to go home , so my friend said he would walk with me half way ,it was very nice out that night and was clear out in the sky , we were walking along the road and it was just starting to get darker outside , we got to a little opening in the road and trees I had my back turned at first and my friend was looking up in the sky , I was talking to him and he wasn't paying attention to what I was saying he was just looking up , he said to me "hey man look at those stars don't they seem way closer than the others , I turned around and looked up at the three bright lights in the sky they had to of been only like 175-200 feet in the air , they were solid white lights that where glowing ,they only stayed in one spot for about 3 seconds than the bottom right light shot backwards and flew off into the sky , you could actually see it getting further and further away but at a really fast speed , the other 2 stars lined up with one another and started to move slowly together to the left , it was also crazy to see that where the 3 stars where sitting directly behind them was the 3 stars or Orion's belt , almost like they where trying to hide Infront of them so that maybe we wouldn't notice them , the 2 stars that started to move to the left started to get blocked off by the trees so my friend and me started to run up the road abit , the front light shot starlight up into the sky and disappeared the back star than started to fly up at a 45 degree angle and just kept going until we couldn't see it anymore . While this was happening my friend and me both felt that they where watching us , we where frozen in shock at first we both had tears pouring from our eyes but couldn't blink or look away when we ran up the road when they started to move it was like I didn't even know I was running I wasent even looking where I was running something just pulled us to run after it after they started to move away . Me and him talked to each other about what we saw and agreed we seen the same thing and that it wasn't planes or anything like that , we both rushed back to my house and told my parents and my sister and her boyfriend , they all laughed at us and said it couldn't of happend , we have told other people and only some believe our story , since that day me and that same friend have seen unexplained things in the sky 2 more time together , and I've also seen something once with my girlfriend and once by myself all in the same general area . And always feel like I'm going to see something again .
