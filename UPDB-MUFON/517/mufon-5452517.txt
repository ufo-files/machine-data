Circular redish orange object, constant light until it passed behind high level wispy cloud. No flashes or change in brightness.

I was getting ready to relax in hot-tub after a hard day of work. I was only drinking vitamin water and had not turned on the tub jets when I looked up to admire the stars that have been more numerous since the lock down. While looking straight up and focusing on a group of stars I saw a larger orange/red circle moving and followed it as it quickly covered 60 degrees of my visibility from straight up , 90, to 30 degrees behind my neighbors tree. It came from WSW and was headed ENE. The time lapse made no sense to me. It was perhaps a second. The light had dimmed as it went behind a wisp of cloud as did the stars it blew in front of. This told me it was above the high wispy clouds. There was no sound as I had not turned on the tub and it was quiet out. So quiet that I heard the Miller moths hitting the porch light out front.
Many nights have been spent in the tub where I see planes, Military and civilian, cross the sky and they can take anywhere from 90 seconds to several minutes to cross that area of sky and I can see both the red and white flashing lights on these crafts. It did not follow any of the flight paths I normally see. This occurred at approximately 11:25 pm on the 26th of May. I have seen satellite's before and this seemed similar accept for the speed which still seems beyond everything I have seen.
