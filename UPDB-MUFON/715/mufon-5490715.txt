We were sightseeing in Morgantown WVA and we were checking out the view over Morgantown and took this picture of two objects in the sky. We didn't actually see them in the sky. It wasn't until we got home and looked at our pics and realized we had captured something. Though it's not the first time we've seen ufos in Morgantown. We saw one two summers ago doing crazy zig zagging and then we saw one a few years before that that were orangish boomerang shape just hovering then shoot into outer space. That first one. ..my husband and i saw and in unison remarked "what is that"? Thats when it shot into outer space. We both agreed we saw a ufo. The second one, we weren't really surprised. This is becoming such a common thing. We know others who have seen them.
