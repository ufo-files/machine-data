I saw 3 slow moving tringle shaped things flying slowly. Possibly drones.

I had just been at the Esso gas station cleaning my windshield. As I was leaving I saw the three objects. I got out of the car and shot this video with my smartphone.

At first I thought the objects were kites. But they were flying slowly.

They were triangle shaped.

They looked unusual. I thought they were drones. They kept flying and I lost sight of them as they were blocked by a large building.

I drove in the direction they went but didn't spot them again.
