Unseen object passed less than 50 feet in front of deck with barely audible wind noise. A/C worker 22 years and have never witnessed anything similar

After arriving home from work I nightly sit on the back deck of my home from 115am to approx. 215 nightly weather permitting. The deck is less than 15 feet from a river that is roughly 120 yards wide. I heard what at first thought was a helicopter approaching a few feet above the water but quickly realized the sound was barely audible and it passed directly in front of me at eye level with no shape visible and only a whisper of a noise. It passed by at a speed of roughly 50 mph with no wind moving the trees. Only the faint whooshing which is how I roughly gauged the speed of movement.  The sound quickly moved downriver maneuvering between the treeline and above the water.  Thats all the detail I can really provide.
