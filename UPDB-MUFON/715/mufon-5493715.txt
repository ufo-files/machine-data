{dr}4 bright orange round hovering spheres were observed in the sky

My 13-year-old daughter and her 2 friends and I were coming west bound on Mountain Road from the local Wendy's and we as soon as we made a right turn onto Mansion House Crossing Road, we all saw 4 round shaped lights in the sky to the left side of us above the trees. We were kind of scared and I kept asking the girls do you guys see this, and they said yes, and they were very scared, after maybe a minute or 2 they seemed to just disappear, they didn't fade out they were just gone, vanished.
