saw dark geometric object hovering above a building

Was riding the sktrain along the kingsway corridor, near Royal Oak station when we passed a building that had a large, black , what i can only describe as geometric, rigid edged craft hovering beside a building. It was not moving, except for a smaller piece that was separate from the craft, and under it, which slowly circled underneath it. It was a little square shaped. It was in the bright sunlit sky, and was definitely not a misidentification . We passed it by , and when we stopped the train at Royal Oak station,, i got off the train to look at it more closely from a distance. Dark grey or black. Kinda rectangular, but odd shaped,. Its edges where very angled and straight, and its body looked like a bunch of geometric shapes stuck together. Like a triangle inside a square. I was in a rush , so i did not approach it. It was still hovering when i left. This was either the day i posted, July 16th, or it was Aug. 14th or 23rd of that year. I know this because those wetre the dates i had a doctors appointment, which i was on my way to when i had the sighting. ASll the people on the train saw it, and i bet alot more too. I would love to hear if others reported anything in Burnaby BC, on or about those days. I could be off on the year, but the dates are good.
