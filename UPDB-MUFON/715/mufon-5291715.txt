Latest in Multiple sightings and encounters by myself and groups over several years

The most recent sighting was a week ago over the forest outside my bedroom window, with one other witness. We watched a craft that had 5 lights in a circlre around it hover in the sky for 40 minutes. The lights would change color and pulsate, and smaller lights, that looked like sparks, would shoot out and dart around it. It was about a mile away and just above the forest, in the area of a silver mine. While we were watching I tried to use my video camera to film it, it has I.R., but the camera would start malfunctioning and die as soon as I pointed it in that direction. We flashed a small LED light array at it a few times, then we saw a small plane fly over it going SW in the direction of an Army base, that stores chemical weapons. Soon after, a helicopter came from that direction and flew over the craft, and then circled back toward the base. A few mintues later the craft started slowly moving in that direction until it was out of view.
