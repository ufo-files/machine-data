Multiple orange lights floating. Appear instantly and faded away as they were moving.

On December 31st, 2009, 8:10pm I was walking in our neighborhood with my son watching sparatic fireworks. North view, East to West I noticed 10-15 lights orange in color. The lights did not blink, move fast or any type of action. There was two groups the first group had about 15-20 lights. I first thought of parachuters; There is a small airport not to far from us. However, I ruled that out when I noticed small craft flying in the same area. The small craft flying in our area is not unusual. The small air craft was closer than the lights to the ground, I noticed a few planes pass over the objects in the sky. Another reason the lights caught my attention, The small craft were moving at a faster rate than the lights. The first group of lights had appeared and the front part of the group was a triangular formation, the front part of the formation started to fade after about five minutes. When the first group started to fade a second group appeared. The new group of lights were about 30-40 lights, traveling the same path and same speed. I had stopped just to observe and called my wife to get our camera to film north of our house. She was not quick enough. There was a neighbor in his yard outside and I pointed out the lights to him. He stood there with me and watched the lights. We noted the time at about 8:14pm for the second group. The lights traveled east to west and after 5 or 10 minutes faded. I have seen meteor showers before and this was not a meteor shower. There is a constant flow of small craft in our area and this was not small craft.***
