Orange orb hovering over South Penticton over Skaha Lake, Penticton

I was walking my wife and dog and off to my right I spotted an orange orb hovering over the rooftops of houses. I asked my wife "What is that???" I said "It's not of this world." I had watery eyes because I knew what I was seeing was not an every day event. We observed it for about a minute and then it faded out for about 45 seconds to a minute. In the same place, it came right back again. It moved slightly to the left, then slightly to the right. Then, it appeared to come forward slightly. That was about another 45 seconds to a minute. Then it disappeared for another minute and then it came back again and lit up again. The same thing happened for another minute. Then it lit up and never came back. My wife and I went back to our car to get our camera, but it never reappeared. (This took place by Concordia Lutheran School.)
