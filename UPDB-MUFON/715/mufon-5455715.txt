Most interesting thing I’ve seen in a long time

Got out of my car with my teenage children and walked on bike path to see 8-10 light orbs moving in similar distances apart in a strait line going from S.W. to S.E fast enough it only took a few minutes or less to see the first light disappear and then the second one disappeared when it reached same spot as the first one. All the others followed the same pattern but the last one disappeared almost like it moved up a bit earlier than the others. No sound. It was very high up our cell phones couldn’t make out a picture or video.
