{rjl} 4 red glowing lights flying in a strange yet perfect formation just below the cloud line.

It was right after midnight when my room mates and I noticed 4 red glowing lights flying in a strange yet perfect formation just below the cloud line. At first we thought it was airplanes but it was too close to one another. They came into sight at the same time but the first one was speeding ahead and disappeared into the clouds. The last three, still in perfect formation, gradually followed suit, disappearing into the clouds as well. We were shocked and cant really explain what we just witnessed.
