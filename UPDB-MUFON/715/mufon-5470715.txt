{jpg} UFO over Hudson River

Driving south on Boulevard East in West New York, New Jersey. Noticed something come out of the clouds above the Hudson River between New Jersey and New York. My first thought when seeing the object was amazement. I could tell it was not conventional aircraft. It absolutely was not a balloon based on movement and size. The object moved slowly in a few directions (up, down, left & right). The object then went into the cloud.
