Erratic flight path then vanish.

At 9:20 my dog "Sassy" whines like clockwork to go pee. I took the dog outside & as always I found myself looking at the sky & the stars on a cloudless night. I do this all year but especially during hunting season to look for a high fog or ring around moon (no moon). I noticed what looked like a plane flying from Cleveland or somewhere up North. Just a small moving light like a plane on takeoff a long ways away. As my eyes focused on this dot "No biggie, just a plane" It zigged as it it fell out of the shy 100' I found this odd then it did it again, Then again, Then again. It had a odd "Wobble" like a very high pilot saying hello by tipping his wings at 30,000 feet. It was about to go behind my tree so I moved south east as this light turned dimmer & dimmer until gone as if turning off a lightbulb that takes 2 to 5 seconds to die. I was looking north, Towards Fredericktown from Howard but as stated it just seemed light a very long distance 747 without the flashing lights, Just a solid "Bulb". Exactly like a plane only it zigged or "Dropped" several times as it continued the same path dare I say upwards as climbing in elevation. As it dimmed it seemed to shrink in size as if a great climb in elevation in the last 1 to 2 seconds of flight. Ideally it should have went slower if climbing but during this time it might have sped up, not sure, couldn't really make sense of what I was seeing. it just did not register in my mind as making sense.
