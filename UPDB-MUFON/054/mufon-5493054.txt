{cak} Jumbo Jet With 2 Orbs following

I was at approximately 2000 ft elevation when I and 2 others saw a jumbo jet with 3 army helicopters following it, and 2 white orbs directly behind it (between jet and helicopters), they appeared to be at maximum 10,000 foot elevation, traveling from west to east, Observed for less than 2 minutes until out of view range.
