green light hovering and speeding up suddenly and changing directions abruptly

I was on my balcony and saw this green light hovering over the mountain. At first I thought it was a helicopter but when the object started speeding up and changing directions abruptly and in such a fashion that it would be impossible for any other aircraft to do it, then it got me curious. The whole event lasted for about 5 minute and the light vanished behind the mountain. I was just amazed by the movements of that object, whatever it was. I would say the size of the object was 20-30 feet in diameter. maybe. it was just green light.
