Star-like object, barely visible, very high

Star-like object flying towards the city of Warsaw. Vey high, barely visible. No sound at all.

It appeared right in the middle of the Big Dipper constellation (otherwise I wouldn't even notice it). I really had to strain my eyesight to see it. It was just like a dim star - only it was moving. Grey or orange in colour (I would say grey but I felt for a moment while staring in it, that it might've been orange). Altitude I can only estimate, that it was more than 40 000 ft.

Not a plane nor ISS (I checked both flightscanner and ISS live). And comets aren't moving that fast. I went inside to grab my binoculars and when I was back it was gone. What was it I have no idea.
