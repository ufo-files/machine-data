Stationary flashing lights recorded with a telescope from our backyard

We noticed these blinking lights of red, blue, green and white in the night sky for over a year now, so we bought a telescope and positioned it in our backyard on the one of the stationary blinking lights in the sky and took pictures and videos that are amazing! We shared these pictures and videos with our family and friends and posted it on my YouTube account.
