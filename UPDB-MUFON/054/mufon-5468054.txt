{jpg} Amateur photographer friend took this photo on take-off in my plane. Upper right is an object neither of us saw with our eyes.

We did not observe anything with the naked eye. An amateur photographer friend of mine was visiting me in Redding. I took him flying in my plane, a 4 seat, Piper Cherokee. A few seconds after take-off from the surface of Runway 34 at RDD, he took this photo, just to snap a pic of us on take-off. After he developed the photo, we noticed this disc-shaped object in the upper right airspace in the photo. Neither of us actually saw this during the flight, but the camera caught it. The blurry black thing near the nose of the plane is the prop, the camera catches it in an almost stopped position, but it is rotating at 2,700 RPM. Hence, for the object to be caught still, it could also be traveling at a very high speed. Snow-capped Mt. Shasta is seen to the North. It was a clear, sunny day. We didn't hear or see anything other than the usual sound of my airplane on a normal take-off (we were wearing noise-cancelling headsets though). My friend, a former chaplain guarantees (and knowing him, I believe him) that he did not "add" this or anything else, to the photo, and it is not photo shopped. I am wondering if it is a bug or smudge on the wind screen, but it appears to be in the distant sky. I am hoping you can debunk it for me.
