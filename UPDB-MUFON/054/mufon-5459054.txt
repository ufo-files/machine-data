{rjl} Orbs followed by military jets

There were 8 to 10 military jets circling NW of Willow Run Airport in the Superior Charter Twp area at a very slow speed, not uncommon since there is an airshow this weekend. The military jets were moving much slower than airplanes normally do coming in for approach to Willow Run. This was different, in that the military jets seem to be patrolling. When we took a closer look to the south we noticed 2 white orbs. At first we thought they might be bright stars, but the orbs were moving in a slow pattern from NW to SE. The military jets we layered at different altitudes obviously monitoring the orbs. The orbs picked up speed, shrunk to a smaller orb turning orange-ish Red, and accelerated south at an unknown speed. The military jets proceeded south in pursuit.
