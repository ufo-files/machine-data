Object appeared in photos taken by mine and my daughters cell phones. Pictures were taken after a bad rain storm. We only saw the object in our photos

My daughter and I were taking photos over Seneca Lake after a thunder storm in the evening. I took the first photo facing away from the lake, west, with my cell phone which was set at high definition. She took a photo right after me. Her cell phone was set for less definition. We didn't notice the orb until we looked at the pictures a few minutes later. It appeared in both photos. My picture also shows white dots that look to be set in some order and they also appear in close up to have a shadow behind them. When you compare my daughters photo to mine you can see that the orb is much higher in the picture. Whether it moved or she was just in a slightly different position than I was is the question. It could be a lense flare situation but I question that because it is so detailed in my picture and there is no reflective qualities around it.
