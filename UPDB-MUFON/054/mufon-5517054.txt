5 dim lights came across sky, turned into 3 lights,

in was in the backyard smoking with a friend and staring at the big dipper. sky was clear and i was looking at one star off to the left of the handle in particular. i noticed the 5 lights since it resembled a small close knit cluster of stars that i always admire but it was moving, and moving west through the dipper. i immediately pointed to my friend at which point we both witnessed the 5 lights get brighter, stop and then reduce to only 3. the 3 yellow orange lights then grouped closer together very quickly, then spread apart just as fast to form a very large triangle and take off in unison west. we ran down the backyard and into the back alley to see it soar off into the distance.
i was in awe, i always wanted to see something like this and i had a witness to share it with. we couldnt stop yelling in excitement.
