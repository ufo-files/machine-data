{cak} Bright pulsating light Southern Indiana

I went outside about 12:20 on 9/2 2013. I noticed a light to the northeast about 4 or 5 times the size of a star. It was moving slightly and pulsating what looked like white and yellow or orange light. I thought it might be a drone since I knew it wasnt a plane or helicopter. My wife saw it too and went to get her tablet to record video of the object. I also called my daughter to see it she also could not explain what we were seeing. It looked unlike anything we had ever seen in the sky. As I was recording the object my wife noticed another one to the east and at some point a third came into view between the other two but further off in the distance. When I zoomed in on the first object it looked like a diamond shape that changed to a square as well as changed colors and intensity. During the recordings a second light appeared which made me wonder if the light was the object or just a light on a bigger object. When both lights were visible it looked like it could have been a horse shoe shape with light on each end. It was hard to tell all I know is I never saw anything like it before. I watched for over two hours and they were still there when I went to bed at 2:45
