{rjl} a light blinking every 15 to 20 seconds

Using night vision goggles, I observed a light that was blinking every 15 t0 20 seconds, The light started from the bottom and illuminated up to a shape of a mushroom or like an umbrella. The light stayed stationary for about 45 minutes then disappeared. You could see a cloud shape before it lighted up so it was easy to located with the NV. After it disappeared the cloud shape was not visible. The night sky was clear of clouds.
