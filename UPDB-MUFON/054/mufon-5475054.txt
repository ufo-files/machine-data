{cak} Son and friend saw glowing orangish orbs called me outside I captured one on video

My son and his friend went outside to get his phone from his truck. They immediately saw a intensely bright glowing orange orb, it shot back up to clouds. After approx. 30 seconds reappeared over house next door, split into 3 orange orbs in a triangle formation. It became one and again with incredible speed shot up. They called on cell to tell me get outside! Now ! I ran out front door and as I was speaking to them I saw a very intense orangish/white orb of light behind them over golf course and we ran to fence to get a better look. They confirmed that's one, I grabbed my phone selected video and tried to focus on object. It looked much smaller on video screen and it took a bit to get it on screen. I was able to,but, as soon as I zoomed in object shot north and in a instant disappeared. For some reason I yelled. 
What we witnessed was like nothing we have ever seen before. There is no known object that can move at the speeds and directions witnessed. This event has had an impact and I wish it never happened. I waited to report, because, I was hoping to find any other plausible explanation. I filled out report only on what I witnessed.
