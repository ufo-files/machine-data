My name is [Name removed/cms/tg] and I live on a cliff overlooking the whole east side of Akron, Ohio and beyond. It was March 2, 2015 around 8:15pm. It was a beautiful star filled sky. I have 3 airports and several hospitals that use helicopters that I watch regularly and observe. This night i witnessed a beautiful orange light far off on the horizon. It intrigued me because the orange color was so unusually beautiful I wanted to see the aircraft fly over and see what it was. Thought it may be a new model helicopter. It was stationary which generally means its an aircraft flying towards me. Over several minutes of enjoying the view and stars I noticed that this beautiful orange light hasnt moved position. I could tell because of the cell towers blinking lights. This grabbed my curiousity and I turned around opened slider door and shut it and looked out to see the light as I was about to run to grab binoculars and it was gone. I ran back outside to the cliff to look for running lights or any light to justify what just happened. There wasnt any aircraft lights in the sky. I must admit I felt a scared feeling I never felt before.  I ran to my neighbors house and told them what I had just witnessed so if someone did report a ufo sighting i would have credibility. Couple of days later I googled ufo reports in ohio and there it was. A ufo was reported in Ravenna, Ohio around 8 pm and vanished on March 2.
