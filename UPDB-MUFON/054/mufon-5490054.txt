{rjl} Green ball of light above building then it just darted off into the sky

I was returning home from my girlfriend's house around 11:30pm. I was walking to my front door from my car when I looked up into the night sky to stargaze. That's when I saw a big green glowing ball of light above one of the apartment buildings. It stayed there hovering high above the building for about 20 seconds until it suddenly darted south and vanished into the night sky. It was definitely an unsettling feeling to say the lease.
