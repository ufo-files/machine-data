{jpg} It was a Dream: taken to restaurant for intelligence game-gave instruction on how I would remember and know it was real.

I was triggered to remember a dream in which me and others were taken to a local restaurant for intelligence/memory tests/games. I was told I would remember the visit to the restaurant because it was to be remodeled. And actually, when I was driving by one day, I did remember because of that (the restaurant was remodeled with a new look). I think someone was supposed to remember as part of the game. As far as the incident goes, I did not see any aliens, craft, or lights outside. I do remember sitting in the restaurant with about six other people and squares of different lights in a pattern on the wall going on and off. I can tell you what seat I was in and which wall had the pattern, I felt happy at that time like in a class. I can tell you that I was told how I would remember that it wasn't just a dream. And I can tell you that I still have some odd feeling like I am a winner at that game for remembering. I do feel odd for believing in it and maybe it was just a dream, however the night before, or days before, I can't remember any instances that would lead up to that type of dream, I don't even visit that restaurant hardly.
