Fast moving light vanishes

My boyfriend and I were standing in my back yard saying goodbye when he noticeda light fast moving across the night sky. When we first noticed it we ruled outthat it could not be a plane as it had no flashing lights, we thought perhaps itwas a shuttle or satelite in orbit, however after watching it move across thesky for 3-5 minutes it faded from sight. My boyfriend noted that it was as ifthe object moved up into space away from Earth.
