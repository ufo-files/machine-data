Was about 10:30pm Phoenix time. I had just finished watching a movie and it started to storm. I had been watching clips of UFO's forming in storms so that's what had glued my eyes to the skies, like I always do gazing above me to look for such things. A fireball or glowing object appeared out my 3rd floor bathroom window. It hovered there, this was the 1st time in 40 years or 8 years in Phoenix I had ever seen such a sight. I followed the ball outside in the storm. I filmed it dissapear. After that sighting a half hour later my girl & I witnessed more of the same crafts appearing then floating West. The crafts have appeared from 6-26-16 to 7-1-16 so far only in the evenings when the sun is down.
