{cak} Teardrop shaped orb hovered for a while then descended into distance

I was looking East toward the mountains during dusk at about 8:05pm and saw a bright, semi-teardrop shaped object/orb. The bottom part of it looked a little brighter than the top portion. At first I thought it was a small trail coming off of an airplane but a few seconds later, I noticed that it was still there. It was pretty high up, just below the clouds and looked much bigger and brighter than a star. It was glowing brighter than a star and certainly stood out. It stayed there for around 15 minutes, then slowly started to descend behind the mountains out of view.
