I was laying in my bed watching tv, I felt the urge to look at my window with the blinds closed, but saw bright lights through them. I opened them to see what was so bright, then I saw 3 bright white lights forming a perfect triangle with a dull red pulsating light in the center of the object. I got very excited, and had the mind set to grab my camera which has a video recorder, opened my back door and started shooting the video. It made no noise and moved very slowly across the sky along the tree line. This was approximately at 11:45 pm on January 5, 2012. The triangle object started to turn on a yaw axis,and continued to rotate counter close wise which showed multiple lights. It was very dark in color, such as black. It vanished in a split second.
