{rjl} hovering. brilliant white object...then departed straight up.

I have kept this to myself all my life until now. In the town of Cheshire there was a park on rt 10 called cheshire park. The park has or at the time had two seperate areas. In front near the road were ball fields and tennis courts. In back of this were woods with walking trails and a pond. It was evening, the lights were on and youth football was going on in the fields. I don't remember why but I was on the walking trail standing on a wooden bridge that crossed over the brook that either went into or came from the pond. It was dark and a clear night. Suddenly from my left abruptly came this round white object. It was not normal in several ways. It began to hover in one spot but would move to the left or right and hover there. The way it moved has never left my mind. It was suddenly in one spot then suddenly in another..there was no transition path...it was in one spot then in another. It did this for several minutes moving from one place to another..the suddely just shot straight up and disappeared. As far as sound goes there was none...if anything it made what I call a sound vacum...none. I do not believe it was more than 200 feet in the air.
