{jpg} Four lights, solid, white, craft appeared massive. Flew low over us, then cicled over town.

My son, a friend and I were working in my friend's garage when we heard a roar overhead. Like a jet but much louder. We went outside to look and saw a massive object with four lights in it's front. Hard to see the object but it was a single craft, not multiple. It flew very low at treetop level over us in a Northeasterly direction. Moving, in my opinion, too slow for any jet I've ever seen. It went out of sight then circled back and flew over the city at a higher elevation. It never moved fast but flew away very leisurely. The lights were equidistant. Similar to a "finger four" formation that military fighters use.
