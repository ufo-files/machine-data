{ck} They looked like Chinese Lanterns. But we actually started seeing them on July 3, 2012.

I was at my home on July 3, 2012 outside in my back yard. I have a large yard, so a lot of clearance overhead for me to see things in the sky. My mom had called me and said she saw something floating over the sky, so I looked up and immediately saw something. It looked like a Chinese Lantern, though there were 5-6 of them that followed shortly after. It was a white orb like object thats center was a glowing red. When the object was floating, it appeared to be shining a spotlight. I was convinced it was a Chinese Lantern, until I saw them again on July 4, 2012. They were moving from south to north in a straight line. At one point I saw 3 of them together, going south to north. There were over 10 of them within an hour. They never backtracked, just kept going north. I tried to get a video of one which Im attaching, as well as the pictures, but theyre not very clear. I basically set up camp out back waiting for more but they stopped at about 10:30 pm on July 4, 2012. The objects had no noise whatsoever. They appeared to be traveling about 40-50 mph.
