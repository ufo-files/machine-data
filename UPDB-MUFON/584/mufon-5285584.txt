At first I saw 1, then there was 5

Aleff and I were on our way to the grocery store in Greenville NC. Aleff was driving when I saw this light in the sky.
At first I thought it was a drone but I’d never seen a drone move in the pattern like this one was doing. The first one hovered but when it started moving had a yellow tail on it.
After that the next 3 came pretty fast and started doing circles and 1 came up to the first. 
Then i noticed that they were all in a spaced out line. 
I lost visual at the end of the road when we turned left going down E Fifth St.
