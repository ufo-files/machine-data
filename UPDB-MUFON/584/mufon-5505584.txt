{dr}Saw two clusters of oblong silver objects flying in one direction

I was riding in the passenger seat of my car, heading north on I-5 just before exit 206 in Washington. I was looking out the window and noticed two groups of silver objects moving in a straight southwestern line to the east side of the freeway. This was a bright clear day and the objects were highly reflective. I assumed they were aircraft but couldnt make out any clear shape or structure. They were oblong and seemed to be two-toned with division between top and bottom. There was a group of 5 five in an lop-sided V and a group of 4 clustered together in an irregular shape. They seemed to be fixed in relation to each other as though they were connected or in formation. They were moving at about the speed (and possibly altitude?) of a helicopter. They were closest to the freeway when I first noticed them. My view was blocked briefly by a bus. I caught sight of them again, and tried to take a picture but my cell phone froze. I looked down at my phone a second time to see if it had started working, and when I looked back they had disappeared. I would have expected to still be able to see them based on their speed and direction. Possibly they were only visible because of the reflected sunlight and I lost the angle.
