I was driving NW along route 2A outside of Westminster MA. I saw what I thought was a small, bright object emerge from a cloud. On first glance I thought I was looking at an aircraft. It was small and bright and appeared to have a chemtrail. However, as the object moved, the part that I thought to be the "chemtrail" moved with it, remaining consistent in size and shape and maving in tandem. For a split second the whole object glowed as brightly as the one end, then returned to its original configuration. It made me think of a transparent flashlight, except that the end giving off light did not produce a beam. My overall impression as regards size and distance was that the object was quite distant and definitely larger than a school bus.
