{kt}Very large fireball like object streaking through sky

At 7:44 pm Arizona time, I exited my apartment to the West. When I reached the parking lot I immediately turned to the left (South) and witnessed a very large glowing object streaking across the sky from South towards the Southwest. The object left a sizable trail and was the largest object I have ever seen streaking through the sky. It had some characteristics of the recent Russion meteor but the object I observed travelled slower and had a larger main body. It disappeared out of sight behind another building.
