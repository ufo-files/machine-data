{dr}Four dull white orbs of light in the SW direction floated and descended for 5-10 seconds before disappearing behind the clouds.

I was getting something out of the back of my truck and as I turned around to walk back into my house I noticed these orbs. Star-like in size with two in the middle somewhat close together and two (one on each side) flanking the outside, considerably farther apart. Dull and white in color they moved in unison and the best movement I could describe them as would be floating swiftly (but not with a direct path like an aircraft) as they descended. I witnessed this for 5-10 seconds before the disappeared behind the clouds.
