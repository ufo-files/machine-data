I was headed home after getting pizza with my 21 year old son. I happened to catch movement at about 20 degrees through my front windshield. I looked and saw a huge cigar shape object traveling rapidly across the sky. This object was much larger that a plane and seemed to be a very bright and shiny white. It was traveling faster than anything I had seen before and was traveling low enough that it disapeared from our view as it traveled behind trees.
