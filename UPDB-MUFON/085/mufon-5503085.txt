Aerial craft ascend and descend between 60 and 65 degree angle to ground leaving sporatic trails

My parents and i were driving from durban to johannesburg, and and we approached the shell ultra city in Escourt, i could see an object leaving trails of smoke in air. It left a solid cloud of smoke then vanished, and a few minutes later re-appeared and made a short trail again and then vanished from sight. When we stopped at the garage to fill up, i tried to take a photograph, but nothing appeared in the pic. I then went into the restroom, while my dad was at the car. He claims it flew past over the garage, and was "silver" in colour and was a plane. There was no engine noise to substantiate that it was a plane. The trial of smoke it left in the sky resembled a sewing needle, but it dissipated within 2-5min later. My sister whom travelled the same route with her fiancee the day before also claimed to have seen the same patterns being formed in the sky in the same facinity. This was yesterday and today (28/29April 2014)
