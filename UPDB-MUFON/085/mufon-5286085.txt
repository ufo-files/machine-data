Orange orbs

Witnessed over a dozen orange colored orbs in sky. Started with 4 orbs. 3 in the shape of a perfect triangle with the 4th in the center making look like a pyramid. Eventually changed formation and started fading out in distance while over a dozen others started travelling the same direction. At one point the orbs formed the perfect shape of the big dipper and held that formation for what seemed to be about 2 minutes before slowly moving SW and fading out again. During the time of the big dipper formation a helicopter or plane started approaching the formation which was followed by the lights leaving. During no other time were flashing lights observed. None came from the orange orbs at any point.
