{kt}Large Circle cloud ring over casino area

Was taking my son to ROTC Color Guard practice around 5:30PM and as we were heading south over the rail tracks to his high school we both noticed a very prominent circle shaped cloud to the southwest of our location. It was weird because it was slightly below the cloud layer and it looked liked something had shot up from it and caused a change in the cloud layer above. In my 49 years I had never seen this type of cloud before so I just wanted to report it and upload some pictures. I dropped my son off and then took a couple of pictures. I then decided to drive a few miles south to the AK-Chin casino area as it appeared to be right over their location by then. I drove there and took a few more pictures. You have to enlarge the pictures to see it better but it is there.
