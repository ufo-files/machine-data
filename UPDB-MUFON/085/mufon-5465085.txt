Star like object with bright light then nothing zig zagging silently in the sky

It's 5am I couldn't sleep. I opened the back door and let the dog out into the garden. I looked up to the stars ( I always do.) 
I saw what at first I thought was a really bright star but then it began to move. The light went off and when it reappeared a few seconds later it had moved slightly. I watched it for about five minutes it made no sound at all and seemed to be really high in the sky. While I was watching it a plane flying at a lower altitude flew beneath it and passed it in a second. It remained in the sky above me still make really odd movements. The whole thing probably lasted no more than about 10 minutes. I am totally baffled?
Also while I was watching it I saw a shooting star going from South to North, this sounds weird but I felt like it was watching it?
