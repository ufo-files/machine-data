4 Objects over lake ontario

I was heading out to put my bike into my dads van for the next day, when I noticed a car pulled over in front of my neighbors house. I went to investigate their car pulled over, and what they were doing. I assumed they were looking at the moon, so went to go look. 4 fireballs were in the sky, above where i assume lake Ontario, Hamilton area to be? 2 of the objects moved towards the Toronto area; both at different speeds , eventually catching up together and dissapearing at the same time. the other 2 hovered for a bit, appearing to be comets or some sort of burning up star far away. The two objects proceeded to come towards , then over us, slowly fading off at different speeds in what i guess to be Milton. There was no sound from any of the objects, where there were aircraft; which we could hear, (they did not appear to notice the objects)
