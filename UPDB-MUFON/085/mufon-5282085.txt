Black rectangular slow moving object.

This object moved apparently quite slowly, but with a consistent speed. We were driving northward on NV 167 on the west shoreline of Lake Mead when I noticed what I thought was one of those signs that airplanes tow through the sky over events, but there was no plane anywhere near the black rectangular object. I took a series of photos with my cell phone camera, hoping that one would come out clear enough to tell what it was, but the photos only make me wonder even more what in the heck it was. After about a minute moving northwesterly, it disappeared over a hill between lake Mead and Las Vegas.
