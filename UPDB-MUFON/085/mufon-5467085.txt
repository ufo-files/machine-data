{cak} One reddish orb to the right (east) two orangish-yellow orbs to the the left (one above the ot the other) equilateral triangle approx. 5 miles per side

I was in my bedroom watching TV. My TV is inches away from my north window. At about 10pm I saw a red orb and two yellow orbs out my window in the sky. I told my boyfriend to come look at the lights in the sky. He looked and said it might be a launch from Cape Canaveral so he went outside to watch. He came in and said it doesn't look right. We checked the local news stations to see if there was a launch and found nothing. The lights appeared to be over Cape Canaveral which is about 30mins from Palm Bay. They looked to be at least 5-10 miles apart in the shape of a "play" triangle. The red one was on the right and the two orange ones on the left, one above the other. The red one then faded away followed by the lower orange one then the top orange one. They never moved. Just appeared for few minutes then slowly disappeared. This is the first time I have seen lights in the sky that I can't explain. Please let me know if anyone else saw this.
