we were waiting for friends, but they were not home yet. next to their home was a cow pasture surrounded by stone walls, but had no gait, so we backed the car into it waiting for our friends to get home. it was not dark yet just at dusk. out of the corner of my eye I noticed movement at the top of the trees and spotted a vehicle about 10 feet long shaped just like a manturay with out the tail and more rounded fins. it descended to the back of the car and hovered there.Two head lights made it appear like a car was right behind us, which was impossible.A terrible fear came over me like a fight or flight syndrome, I feared for my life. the next thing I recall was a bright white beam over the car that lite up the whole pasture like broad daylight. I stuck my head out the window and looked up and it was gone in an instant. it was dark out now and the person I was with had a greenish, illuminous, sort of irridescent and glowing stain on his pants. He was so upset he went right home to change cloths. We realized it was late around 11pm so he brought me home. I'm getting older now and want to leave my story with you. Also after the event a calmness came over us, we discussed what happened and knew we witnessed something not from this earth.
