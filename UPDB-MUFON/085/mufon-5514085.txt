{nd} Noticed a bright light in the sky, when I approached, it began to change course and fly towards me.

At 5:15pm I got in my car to go to work. I noticed directly south of me a bright light in the sky. It looked like an extremely bright star but it was too early in the evening for stars and it was much larger than any other star Ive seen. I thought maybe it was a satellite and drove to work, not really giving it much thought. When I got home at approximately 11:15 this evening, I noticed the star was still there. I got out of my car and walked toward it, thinking maybe it was a light attached to the top of a telephone pole or something similar. I walked about 3 blocks until I could see it more clearly and could make out a circular shape with red lights on two of the sides of the circle. Suddenly, it began to move. First to (my) left, then it began flying directly towards me. I literally ran two blocks and ducked into a driveway between the wall of their house and their car. I could make it out much more clearly now, it was definitely not an airplane and it made a noise too... like a low hum, like a quiet airplane. After I ran into the driveway it changed course and began flying directly west.

It was interesting because tonight, unlike many nights weve had, has been such a clear night. No clouds in the sky and the stars and moon are brighter than usual. Its also interesting because I have never thought UfOs existed... until tonight. Im not crazy and I know what I saw and heard. When I got to my apartment I turned around to see if that bright light was still shining, and sure enough it was gone.
