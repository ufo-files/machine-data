I was on my balcony having a cigarette with my housemates, my house mate had seen an owl in the tree south of our house earlier in the day and was looking to see if it was still there, as he was focusing on the tree he noticed something move behind the tree and he said as he stared at it "what the **** IS THAT" we all looked and seen it instantly and it looked extremely large and moving extremely fast. It was moving in a straight line heading south had no lights on it what so ever nor any sound or anything, we all had goosebumps rush down our bodies in disbelief on what we were seeing, then after about 5-6 seconds of us staring at it, it just vanished. It didn't seem to teleport or anything like that, it just all of a sudden wasn't there.. could have gone through the clouds or something I'm not sure. we couldn't believe what we had just seen and stayed outside watching to see if it returned but we didn't see it again, my male housemate got a better look at it considering he seen it first, we both made our own drawing on what we seen but they differ.
