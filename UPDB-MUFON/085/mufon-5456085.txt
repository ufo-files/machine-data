{rjl} Saw small orange oval craft

I saw a small dark orange oval craft appear from behind a cloud to seemingly check out the two helium filled balloons released from my daughter’s b day party. About 300 yards in the air. Clear day, except small white cloud object came from. The object appeared to fly to the balloons, around them, and back behind the cloud. It was about the size of the balloons, but a little bigger and more oval than round. It had a dark orange color, not bright or glowing. My sister and brother n law saw the same object. About 5 years later, I was watching a tv show where people who have been abducted get hypnotized so they can find out information. This man with two older sons reveals, under hypnosis, that this tall insect like alien abducted him. When he was brought back to his home, this small orange craft was in his back yard. The alien’s “child” got out of it. It was at that point that my encounter with what I had observed made sense. I believe a young alien flew that craft, checked out those balloons, as any young entity might be curious, then hid. 
This is the first time I have reported this. I am tired of keeping it in.
