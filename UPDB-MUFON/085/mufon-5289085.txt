White objects moved in sync to the right, left, right, left, center and blinked out at once.

While eating at my 91 year old mother's , I was looking through the patio door window and suddenly saw more than 5 white objects appear at once. They moved in sync from left to right to left to right, etc. I went outside to get a better look. Clear blue sky. While moving, the objects appeared to be visibly blinking in and out as if they were implementing a cloaking device. They were doing this all at the same time. Finally, they all blinked out or totally vanished at once. I could not believe my eyes. I had my cell phone, but I have hardly ever used it to record videos and I did not get it out, as I thought the objects might disappear since they seemed to be cloaking themselves off and on. A guy was mowing his lawn on the other side of the fence, so it was making too much noise for us to hear the objects if they were making any kind of sound. I tried to point the objects out to my mother but she did not see those before they vanished. The whole time this happened was maybe a little bit more than a minute.
