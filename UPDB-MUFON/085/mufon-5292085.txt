The strange thing was that it came toward us rapidly then abruptly turned and headed North West out toward the town of Hominy at a high rate of speed, It seemed to head directly toward a small cloud then within minutes it had completely dissipated . This was witnessed by myself and my wife. I do not have a clue what it was but i could not identify it.
