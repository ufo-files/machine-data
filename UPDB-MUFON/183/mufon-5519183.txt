large orange ball with 2 small orange balls, creating own mist/fog as to cloak itself

I was smoking on my balcony facing southwards, something made me turn my head to the east, as i did i noticed a bright orange light in the distance, at first i didn't think much about what i saw and turned away to finish my cigarette, as i turned to go back inside the light caught my eye again, the light seemed to be rising very slowly into the air, the strange thing about it was this kind of mist or fog that was forming underneath the light, it was a kind of swirling twisting vortex that was growing underneath it, then suddenly the fog engulfed the light completely blocking the orange light out, it was a clear night and i could very clearly see funnel of fog continue to rise up and swirl against the starry night sky.
As the column of fog rose ever higher (around 200- 400 feet into the sky) I could see the orange light rising up inside the column and light escaping from the hole in the top of it, although the fog did a very good job of blocking almost all of the orange light from penetrating through the sides of the fog.
I remember thinking that it looked as though the orange light would surely pop out of the top of the fog...and sure enough it did, as it emerged it just hovered motionless in the sky, it had 1 large orange light with 2 smaller evenly sized lights either side of it but more towards the bottom of the larger light, the fog column had all but disappeared in a matter of seconds but the object looked as though it was steaming, but like cold steam coming downwards( like taking something cold from a freezer).
I made a mad dash inside to grab my phone from the coffee table and quickly managed to activate the camera and began to film the object, which to my amazement was heading straight towards me at a fairly rapid rate, and moved like no other aircraft that i have ever seen, I moved to the other end of my balcony to get a better view and the damn thing flew right over my block of flats, no more than about 100 feet off the ground !!.
As it glided over it was difficult to make out its actual shape, it almost seemed to be changing shape,underneath seemed to be some sort of rectangular orange box hanging, like you see under a zeppelin- with observation windows all around it emitting a dull orange light, but again it is hard to explain it exactly, it seemed to appear for a few seconds then disappear .
i would describe the front of the craft with the 3 lights on looking like a rolled up tube, almost like a giant n shape, but as i said it seemed as though it was kind of cloaked or able to change shape at will except for the front lights which could clearly be seen at all times, but there was definitely more to the craft than just lights.
it was not silent, it made a sound like a hissy dull muffled jet engine, but very quiet, it also gave off some sort of steam as it flew over and i could feel some warmf on my face and a faint warm/mechanical/engine/machine type smell- but no fumes at all.
I would like to point out that I do not drink alcohol or take drugs !!
What i saw has played on my mind ever since i witnessed it, although at the time i was not frightened at what i saw, I admit that once it starts to get dark i will not go outside alone, my family and friends although understanding and sympathetic- clearly think I am talking sh*t or making it up or mad.
The video i took is 8 seconds of blurry rubbish, but i managed to pull some stills from it and enlarge them- and they do show the shape of the lights and also some of the stills from underneath do show it about to pass over my block of flats, i also drew the object and have some maps charting the area, where it took off from and its flight path.
i also drew a picture of what it looked like as it created the fog column and rose from it, and some daytime photos of where i stood when i saw it and the roof it passed over, i will include as many images as you will allow me to upload.
I feel it worth noting that as a 14 year old me and 3 other school mates were sat on a bench in the park opposite where i live now and all 4 of us saw 5 huge silver spheres high in the sky that would flash,disappear an re-appear miles away in the summer sky, this show went on for a good 10 minutes and left us all gobsmacked, this was the only other time that i have witnessed any UFO activity, and i had put it out of my mind until this event.
