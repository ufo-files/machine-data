Long straight line that separated in middle then went back to line

A long straight line, first looked like cloud line from a plane but it was thicker and was a certain length. Moved faster than a plane but slower than shooting star and stayed in a perfect straight line. At one point it seemed to separate in the middle and there was a white "ball" in center then it went back together again. Moved to the NE.
