Blue orb - Daylight over water - stationary - picture,

These pictures were taken on Sept 26, 2011.
What you are seeing is the Branson Belle riverboat pulling into
the dock area of Big Cedar Lodge near Branson MO. I was,
at the time, a shuttle driver for the resort. The Belle coming into
our docks was VERY rare so I took a few minutes to take some
pictures. The camera was a small, hand-sized Canon which was
popular in those days. 
Picture number one shows the approach of the boat. Picture two shows the boat about 2 minutes later, but this time with a round, blue object at about the 2:30 position. Picture three is a closeup of the object. I did not see the blue orb when the picture was taken… only afterward when looking at the photos. Nothing more to report… no sound… no further sighting… no one else claimed to see anything (I asked). [ Item moved to F I Report {tjd} ]
