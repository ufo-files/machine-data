{jpg} We saw a black cigar/oval shaped ufo followed by lots of trails/streams of colored lights.

Me, my brother and his girlfriend saw about 50 blue, purple, and yellow light trails coming from what looked to be a long cigar shaped black object with a dim light on the front of it. The object was barely noticable almost like an outline but you could tell it was solid. It went over my house in one constant stream with no noise. It looked slightly tilted as if it was descending but when it got to the tree top that is about a two mile drive from my house all the trails disappeared and it looked as if the black object went down lower until we lost sight of it.
