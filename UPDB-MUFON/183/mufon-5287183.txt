Object flew over House at around 2000 to 3000 Ft.

I stepped outside to let dogs go to bathroom before going to bed and looked up and there was this dark long square shape with 4 dim white lights that was at the 4 corners of this object. It traveled in a southeastern direction never changing height until i could no longer see it. The object had no sound however lots higher up traveling in the same direction was a plane that you could hear the jet engines going. I have seen many airplanes taking off at night during my time in the military and this was was not like anything I had ever saw. It was huge and the 4 lights were dim, no red or green lights for landing lights and no front light, like on planes.
