{rjl} I was drawn to look up into the sky.

We came out of Denny's restaurant in Vernal, UT.  I was drawn to look up into the sky.  I saw one jet, and then another.  I still felt like I needed to look up. The moon was pretty much full, and I looked up and see a bright light in the sky moving east. I grabbed my cellphone and recorded the object.  It then faded away. Being 8 pm, I was thinking maybe a satellite too, but the object seemed to disappear.  I have a 26 second video. I also screen shooting the flight radar app with airplanes going the opposite direction. 
Later around 9:15 at home too I sat outside to watch the sky when clouds rolled in. During this time I saw a flash of green hit the clouds, and then about 3 minutes later light flashed in the trees in front of me. There was no electricity in the air either.
