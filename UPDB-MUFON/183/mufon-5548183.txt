Fast moving triangle shaped object flashed lights in a pattern

Last night at about 3:50 I woke and couldn't get back to sleep. I walked around the house and got a snack. Then just to have something to do I looked out the window of my room. I looked around the sky (as I usually do) and saw what looked like a star.......at first. I looked at it for a minute and saw that it was moving. I thought it was an airplane so I kept watching it. Then the object got closer where it's shape was clearer. Then it moved VERY fast around in a very random manner. I kept watching in amazing and curiousity. It was triangular shaped and had a bright light in the center with 3 lights on the side (one on each corner). The side lights started flashing in a pattern. It kept this up for a minute until small lights were heading to and from the object. The next thing it did was move in a side to side motion very fast. Then it stopped. All the lights shown bright. Then a row of light appeared off one of the corners. After this it made more flashing patterns with a seperate set of lights. All of the lights were bright and appeared a good distance from each other. Then moving around slowely and shown a semi-circle row of lights. These light didn't appear to be connected to it at all. Then it continued the flashing pattern for a about 20 seconds before flying away. It was pretty far up and away but I could see it CLEARLY. It flew away so fast that if I were to blink I would have missed it.
