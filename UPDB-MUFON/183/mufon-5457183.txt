{cak} looked like stars

While on a tow call I lay down to hook up car & took a moment to look at the stars on a very clear nice night. I noticed a circle formation of 6 stars never seen like this before, i could tell this was unique then suddenly one shot away like a bullet then another then the rest just took off in all directions. They were not in our atmosphere but in space. I was shaken by this observation & even called the Patrick AFB to see if they saw this, they did not say they did. I was a soldier & police officer in life & believe I witnessed alien spacecraft in formation. Shook me up. I am now 70 & watched a Stanton Friedman movie which moved me to contact you. We are not alone........
