Object with 2 rather bright clear white light with 2 randomly blinking red lights, on on bottom and 1 on the south side of object.

I 1st noticed a very bright clear white object in the western sky about 30 degrees to the horizon. At 1st I thought it was a planet like Venus. But it slowly grew brighter as it approached. At one point a jet passed by coming from the northwest and heading to RSW to land. At that point it slowly stopped approaching and seemed to recede a bit and slowly head south. Then I noticed an occasionally blinking red light on its south side and less frequently on the bottom. It then hoovered for a while then slowly descended until it was hidden by trees.
