There was a bright orange light, larger than a plane, lower than an airplane. It hovered for several minutes, and decreased in size until it disappeared.

Between 10:00 and 10:15 PM, 9/9/11, there was an orange UFO. It was larger than an airplane and lower than an airplane would fly. It was circular and orange. It came from the north and then hovered for a few minutes. It was quite bright. Then it looked like it was spinning before it started to shrink in size. I'm not sure why, but right before it started to shrink it was hard for me to look at. There were 4 of us in the parking lot who watched it. I never heard anything on the news, but there were many people around because it was about 15-20 minutes after the Paw Paw Wine Festival fireworks ended over Maple Lake.
