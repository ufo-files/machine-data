I have had many sightings the past four years; the more I review pics and think about possible UFOs the more I find. This is a pic I found from 3/10

This is my fifth MUFON report (prior reports: 105499, 100363, 95954 and 80116). The picture is from March 10 2018 when I believe my wife and I attended the annual Nashville Realtor Awards (aka Realtor Prom) in downtown Nashville at the Omni Hotel. I discovered it when reviewing old pics on 5/11/20. Although I often have an urge to take a picture of the sky, I do not remember anything about this occasion. I probably was dropping off the car with the valet or had just picked it up when the picture was snapped waiting on my wife. I have no recollection of seeing any image; or as seen in the short video I made on 5/12/20 of the live picture from my iPhone 8 (or 6 at the time) of the blue orb moving in an irregular pattern toward me. As previously related, I had been somewhat interested in the UFO phenomenon but had not paid that much attention until a multi-orb sighting in October 2016 in which my iPhone pictures were a joke relative to what I observed. Since then I have had many sightings, especially when walking our dogs early in the morning before gym time and going to work. As an aside I have not had any real emotions about what I have seen other than that was cool with the exception of one time returning home from a dog walk in which I looked up and saw what had to be a massive craft behind the clouds based upon the light outline with small orbs darting in and out of the clouds. On that occasion I felt sheer terror as I ran inside with dogs following closely behind. I may post a separate MUFON report for an intense sighting on 12/13/19 when I was in Memphis near the Central Station Hotel (highly recommended) when I had a thought about UFOs as I neared the hotel. After travelling a few blocks in my SUV a small bright light lifted off the ground near the corner of south Main and Butler, traveled maybe 300 feet upwards arching toward me, brightened intensely and then disappeared. I had enough wits about me to grab my camera, but by the time I snapped the pic the object had disappeared. There may be a triangular object in the pic, but it is not clear unlike what I saw. So, I do not read too much into all of this as it relates to me other than maybe I am now more attuned or receptive of what probably always has been here. My one caveat as I have spent some time researching the subject is that I find it disturbing that many phenomenon participants believe they have been told of some apocalyptic event for the earth. Perhaps because of the lock-down that resonates a bit with me even though I consider myself to be upbeat and hopeful.
