disc or sphere like. two flashing lights on ends, with sequential lights that went around the middle of the craft, circled , hovered low to ground and disappeared

i was at going from the gas station to school with a friend when i saw a funny light in the sky. i pointed it out and we both freaked out about how strange it was, it was flying low to the ground and circling like a boomerang would, it was large and most likely sphere or disc shaped you could not really see the body of the craft because it was dark. on each side of the craft was one flashing light, around the middle of the craft were sequential lights that went around it. it was circling near the school, no sound was audible, it then hovered approximately half a mile away, the lights dimmed out and i could no longer see it.
