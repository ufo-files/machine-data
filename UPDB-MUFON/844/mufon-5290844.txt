8 lights traveling in pairs N-S in illuminated pathway

Four pairs of lights (8 total) traveling quickly in a straight line from N-S in a faintly illuminated pathway or channel in the night sky. The path was straight, slightly wider than the lights themselves, and stretched from N-S for the duration of the sighting, then gradually faded.

Distance between the pairs of lights was greater than within the pairs, although the distance between the lights in the pairs narrowed/widened as they moved. 

First seen directly overhead and remained visible for 30-45 seconds before moving out of sight behind trees. Constant almost Venus-bright but dull-looking; no sparkling or blinking; no variation in intensity or color; no sound. Former USCG officer, I've been a "sky watcher" all my life and never seen anything remotely similar.
