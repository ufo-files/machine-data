{dr}Object moving southeast, came near cloud and cloud and object faded and disappeared

I was at Stephens Lake Park taking pictures of the sunset. I was looking at the sky and I noticed the object moving southeast.
The object was circular, with a dome on top and two rails on the bottom. I saw the object and it did not look like any object I was familiar with. I am prior military and I am familiar with current, known aircraft. It had 6 lights - 1 red, 1 green, 2 white and 2 yellow on the bottom. The cloud looked like a cloud but it wasnt there earlier. Part of the craft lifted up as it faded.
The object was moving slowly to the southeast. It came near a "cloud" and both the object and cloud faded and disappeared.
I ki8cked myself afterward because in my left hand I had my digital camera and I did not even think about turning it on, bringing it up and starting video of the object. Once I got home I checked what shots I had and there wasnt a cloud where I saw the object fade and disappear.
I did check with the local police if anyone had reported an unknown or strange object in the sky - there were no reports.
