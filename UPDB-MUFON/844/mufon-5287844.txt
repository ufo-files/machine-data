Bright White Object Appears, Drops Altitude Incredible Fast, Hovers and then Blinks out of Existance

It was about 6 A.M. in the morning. I was putting some items in our truck when I noticed a bright light in the distance (kind of looked like a very bright morning star) but it was lower than usual. I didn't think anything of it until I turned around and noticed it wasn't there anymore. So I decided to sit and watch and in a matter of a minute, I noticed a bright white light appear above where the original light was and it dropped in elevation incredibly quickly. I didn't have my phone out when it first appeared and dropped elevation because it all happened so fast, but I ended up getting it on film when the object stabilized itself, hovered for about 15 seconds before warping out of existence before my eyes.
