2 Bright objects

I was at home in sheffield, uk and went to my back door for a ciggaret. Outside my back door i have a pretty good view east over sheffield toward rotherham. I lit my ciggy and looked over sheffield and about 20 degrees above the horizon i saw a bright light hovering probably about 5 miles away. upon further inspection it looked like 2 bright oval objects very close together. I shouted my girlfriend to come and have a look. she came and saw the object for about 10 seconds, then the objects moved to the left, stoped then seemed to zoom off into the cloud behind it.
