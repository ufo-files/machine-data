{cak} addendum to Jan 22, 2018 sighting Antioch Calif

I had been visiting my friend on a Sunday in Pittsburg. I had been feeling somewhat agitated all day. I left his place at around 9 PM to head back to San Leandro.
I arrived at our office where I am often working on the computer. As I sat there I became aware of a shadowy figure in a brownish color, wearing loose fitting slacks moving from my right to my left. As I observed the movement I heard a voice about not to be alarmed but there was something to be done. Then I observed something circular move in towards me. I closed my eyes and in my minds eye I could see a white field with 2 screens. Within these screens were star fields in night vision colors of light green. There was nothing moving just a scene of stars. I felt oddly comfortable with it and promptly fell asleep. The next day I only felt an odd drained feeling. This is the first time there has been an observation by another that had a direct connection to one of my experiences.
