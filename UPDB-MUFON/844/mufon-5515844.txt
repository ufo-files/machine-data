Hovering & moving silver disc in Dublin Ireland

Noticed it in the sky. I reckon it was about the size of a eBoing 737. It was absolutely motionless for 30sec, then sped away vertically at about 10,000kph. It was a ring shaped but from certain angles it looked like a disk.
