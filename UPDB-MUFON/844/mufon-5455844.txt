Rows of lights

Three groups of lights crossed the sky, taking around one minute to travel out of view. Approximately fifteen ( 15 ) 'lights'were seen by myself, my girlfriend seeing around six ( 6 ).
They appeared to be quite high as even with binoculars I was unable to get any real detail other than white lights.
My assumption would have been that this was a drone pulling a row of lights but the speed and fact that a light plane or helicopter was some distance from our position and appeared to be considerably lower in altitude than the objects we saw, pretty much scuppers that idea.
