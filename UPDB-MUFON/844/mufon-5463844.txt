Lights captured in photo although not detected at time picture was taken

While taking some pictures of Kennedy Center for Performing Arts and surrounding buildings, unknowingly snapped 3 green, oblong, lights seemingly passing over the Saudi Arabian embassy. They appear to have 2 dark, vertical bands and appear to moving left to right, or Northwest to Southeast, and seem to have vertical blur as if they are quickly moving up and down. Only took this one shot as I did not notice the objects at the time and only discovered them while reviewing the pictures next morning.
