{nd} A bright light that hovered, then moved and dimmed out.

Ive observed quite a few of these in the last couple of weeks, I decided to try to record this one and got my camera out just in time to get the end.

The recording can be found here;

The object was stationary at first and changing in brightness, this is what first caught my eye. I noticed that it was much brighter than the stars and seemed to be much closer; I would guess and say it was between 400 and 1000 feet in altitude. The object than began moving, there was no sound like you would hear from a plane and no apparent FAA lights. It maintained a steady brightness as it moved to the south then dimmed out.
