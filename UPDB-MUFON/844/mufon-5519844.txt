My story starts [item moved/CMS/nd] when I was a young boy walking down our drive way to catch the buss for school. As I was walking I had a feeling something wasnt right. I looked to my left and saw an abject, or I should say a very bright light coming from the end of a blueberry field. I saw it once and then again about a 100 ft down the driveway. I had know idea what I was looking at at the time. The strange thing about looking at it the second time was the fact I have no memory of walking that 100ft. It was as if I was in 1 place and then another. The object looked like a very bright white ball with a blue light at its top moving counter clock wise and a green light at its bottom moving clock wise. I stared at it for a few more minutes and then it glowed brightly and was gone. This took place in the winter. By summer when the grass started growing, I remember the place where I saw the object, the grass didn't start to grow back for 5 or 6 years. All traces of the spot are gone now. Ive only toiled 1 other person of what I saw and it was my mom. [item moved/CMS/nd] Ive always wanted to tell someone else but people would have said I was nuts. The only thing I have to remember that day is a strange round mark on my right ankle. It never hurt and my mom said she never remembered me doing anything as a kid for that mark tobe there. I don't know if I was lucky to see it or in the right place at the right time. I feel a great sense of relief tell my story. Its hard to carry something like this for so long and not have a place or others to tell. All I can say is we arent alone.
