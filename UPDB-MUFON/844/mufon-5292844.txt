Did not see anything with the naked eye, but only saw it after I zoomed in the picture.

I was walking along the beaches area by 42 Hubbard Blvd in Toronto, Ontario with my father and looking out at the water. 

I stopped on the grassy area and took some pictures of the water. It was in the late afternoon and the sky and water looked idyllic and I remember a strong urge to take some photos. 

I did not see the object in the sky at all, and neither did my dad. I only noticed the object when I got home and zoomed in on the photos. 

Oddly enough, this has happened before with another photo I took - I just had a strong feeling/urge to take a photo "out of the blue" and when I zoomed in, I saw the object. 

I thought, this looks interesting and that maybe it's a drone, but I didn't see anything in the sky at the time and I think I would have noticed this object in the sky because at least in one of the photos it's quite noticeable.

I took the photos with a digital camera.
