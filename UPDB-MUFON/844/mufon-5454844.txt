I reported this UFO perhaps ten days to two weeks ago. It had been appearing in the night sky for about one week at that time. My son showed me enlarged photos he had taken of it on his phone, and there were viewing ports visible in the saucer shape. When I took pictures on several subsequent evenings, one photo showed a vertical cylindrical shape and the next night, the shape was like a bean. The important thing about this is that it has appeared every clear night. It looks like a large star, but it flashes a red and green light at times. For two weeks it never varied its position, but in the past week it has moved higher in the night sky and further south, apparently also further away. Several times it has moved up or down or from side to side at "impossible" speed, stopping immediately and then returning to it's previous position.I have not attached my clips because the object appears only as a tiny star until enlarged. I'm not sure whether that's possible on the computer.
