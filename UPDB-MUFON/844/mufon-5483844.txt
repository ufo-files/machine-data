It was behind cloud cover and then traveled quickly on a straight line path

I was at work and first saw a hawk in the sky so I took out my camera taking a shot of the hawk. Then this green orb emerged from the clouds below the hawk, and began traveling in a straight line path from the southeast to the northeast.The object appeared green in color with reflective smaller lights which changed position on the craft as it moved through the sky.I felt in shock during and then ill after this event. Once it made it past me in its flight path it disappeared just northeast of my position.
