It flew very, very slowly and wobbled from side to side. I noticed it in the night sky because it was slightly darker than sky line. It flew to an area that is well lit with flood lights, as it is fly line for commercial planes to and from SA airport. As it arrived to this area very slowly, it suddenly " turned on its lights" bright white and glowing. It hovered there for a minute and then rapidly flew to the North into cloud cover and I could no longer see it. I have seen several odd objects here on the NW side the last 2 weeks.
