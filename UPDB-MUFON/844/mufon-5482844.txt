{cak} Looked like they were playing with each other

Someone told me to come on here and write a report as what I witnessed years ago was 110% not man made. I watched 10-12 objects go in a NE flight pattern for roughly 45 minutes. It looked as though they were flying at an altitude that would've been well higher than any altitude our commercial or military jets could fly. On top of that I could see them go faster, slower, and stop on a dime. I watched this for roughly 45 minutes and it honestly looked like they were playing with each other. The formation stayed in a single file line and never deviated from the line. I was in the Marines and spent plenty of time on or near air bases. What was flying that night was nothing our boys have in the Arsenal. The altitude was far too high and the stopping and going is not something our aircraft are capable of. It seriously looked like the craft were playing with each other out of boredom.
