{rjl} Venus like light goes from airplane takeoff height, straight up, then hovers/moves.

Me & my aunt went outside to test the theory of "calling" U.F.O's once again, so we stood on the pool deck & "called". We noticed the object through some trees, as it looked like a helicopter or plane with very bright lights taking off, until we noticed it stayed stationary after moving for about 5 minutes. As i said, we thought this was a plane, or helicopter, but it had to be something else. When it first started taking off, it was behind some trees, it went straight up & hovered for a few minutes, the object then proceeded to move higher, & as we were filming, it moved south, then north, then stationary. Experimentally we asked the object to come close in which case we believe it did, as it became brighter & more visible. We felt humbled by the craft, & excited. We watched as a helicopter flew to the object with no lights on, except the red beacons. They helicopter seemed to almost run into the object, & after going to the said object, the helicopter flew west towards my direction, & passed over us. We also witnessed a few planes fly by/near the object & would turn their lights off, which is against air regulation, especially at low altitudes. We couldn't watch the object any longer due to a recent cold-front, So we headed inside, about 20 minutes later, we went out to see if it was still there, & it was gone. But we captured video & pictures.
