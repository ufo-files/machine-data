{rjl} Upright triangle shape, lighted on 2 loner sides with random red flashing lights

Around 11 pm over my back yard, I saw a dozen bright lights forming an up-pointing arrow shape (triangle with no bottom) hover then slowly move. It would hover for a minute, move then hover again. There were 6 bright white lights evenly spaced on both sides with red lights randomly blinking between the white lights, on the unlit bottom of the triangle where there were no white lights, and within the dark space between the lit sides. The red lights only flashed when the object was stationary/hovering. Only one red light blinked at a time. Four red lights would flash then the object would move to the south/southwest. Only the white lights remained lit when it moved. The whole sighting lasted about 10 minutes when it flew out of my view.
