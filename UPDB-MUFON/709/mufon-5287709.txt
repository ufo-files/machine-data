Three cameras capture UFO

Approximately 9:30 to 10:30 I observed a bright light NW my position while stargazing. Upon further scrutiny, I saw a white spike of light under the bottom of a donut shaped (on edge) object that was also brilliant white. I had another camera set up with a 400mm lens w/tripod, along with a DSLR w/300 lens. The object was stationary with no noise. It slowly went away from us (NW or WNW) very slowly.
