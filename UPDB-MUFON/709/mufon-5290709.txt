Vertical light

Is saw a short vertical light in the sky while driving and it continued to be visible for roughly 30 minutes. It was at sunrise with no clouds in the sky nor were there any stars. It moved very slightly while I was watching but I can’t be sure since I was driving and trying to record at the same time. I did stop once and get out of the vehicle in case it was a glare from the windshield but it was still there when I got out. I was trying to take pics and videos while driving but it was difficult. I did manage to capture several pics and I got it in a few videos also. I was moving rather fast with my camera so I had to track it in slow motion to get a good view.
