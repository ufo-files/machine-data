Two diamond UAPs observed over Phoenix Jan 15 2022

On January 15 2022 at 5:15pm I was walking in Granada Park in Phoenix and I spotted two diamond shape UAPs hovering below the clouds. No noise, no movements. Unable to determine the size but they seemed to be a mile or more away. I observed them for 11 minutes based on my camera records.
