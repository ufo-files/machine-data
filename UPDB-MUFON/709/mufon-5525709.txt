Bright ball of light ascended against the mountain and continued eastbound, pulsated and disappeared.

While by the high school sports complex,I observed a bright yellow ball of light to the west ascend into the air at about a 50 degree angle, like a plane taking off. The first thought that came to mind was that it was a plane because of the angle of ascent. I then changed my mind because saw now flashing red light, nor did I hear any sound whatsoever. I could tell that it was not yet at a high altitude because it was against the mountain, not the sky, at first. It climbed in a south-easterly direction. When it seemed to be at a steady altitude, it changed direction, heading east. It cleared the valley in a about 15 to 20 seconds, pulsated very slowly about three times, and disappeared. To say the least, I felt a rush of excitement and confusion at the same time.
