Flashing object over Florence

It was on 24.08.2018 at 10:04 am. We spent 3 days in Florence and in the second morning we went to the city to make some pictures. Whem I made the pictures I didn't realise that object on the picture. Some days before I started to cut a small film from the raw material and I saw this object on the video. I don't know if it was close to the roof or far away in the sky. At the first sight I thought that it is on the sky and sometimes hides behind the clouds... so I thought that it is a ufo... My friends told me that must be a chocolate paper falling down. It is too fast for me to be a paper, but maybe they're right... I don't know...
I made a small video with different speed and lights and I upload it. The text is hungarian... It means f.example: 5 times normal speed, 5 times normal speed higher contrast,... 50% speed... following the object etc...
