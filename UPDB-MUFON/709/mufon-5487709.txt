{jpg} 3 different pairs of flashing lights lasted around 5-10 minutes

At approx 3:15 AM Thursday September 24th 2015 I was leaving my garage and I saw 3 separate pairs of 3 lights flashing lights. I thought the objects were drones. I have video of one of the sets of lights. It had varied speeds slowing down and speeding up. It crossed the sky in a curved / wavy pattern. I did not feel any different than usual. I lost sight of 2 of the objects while recording the 3rd object that left my view behind some trees.
