{rjl} A silver orb. Reflective (like a giant flying mirrored Christmas ornament)

I was coming up on Montgomery Rd. from Walzem road toward New World..

As I crossed the intersection where Walzem rd. becomes Montgomery rd. I noticed a really large (considering its distance from me.. it had to be about the size of a 787 (if you took the length from nose to tail and use those points to make a circular profile..  that' was the sight I saw.. a big ass mirrored reflective hovering Christmas ball ornament... For a bout 10-15 seconds. Then my view was obstructed by trees and structures.. I got home about 1 minute or less and walked to the end of my block.. and it was gone... I tried to convince myself it was something else.. But I know damn well it wasn't a helicopter.. Military Special Operations 9 years.. (4 in the Army 5 in the Navy).. So I've seen choppers in all types of lighting and it wasn't any type of aircraft we have to date.. and it wasn't a weather balloon because it was gone too fast to be anything we have in the skies. In all directions nothing..  just disappeared.. 2 planes a a silver United Airlines or American Airlines and another private jet flew by and they both looked like planes, sounded like planes and would've maintained that appearance and plane type characteristics for miles.. far beyond the distance I saw the orb in the sky.  It was large as hell and was situated slightly under and to the left of the sun.. 
Montgomery Road at 830-9 am has the sun damn near dead ahead if heading the direction I came..
