Multiple lights that looked like star, some travelling in pairs keeping exact distance from each other horizontallly, both moving at same velocity

While walking the dog through a local park, was gazing at the stars as it is an extremely clear night. Noticed the first solid light, looked like a star, that was travelling in a straight path across the sky at a constant speed. Simply thought it was a satellite as there is reduced pollution due to the covid-19 lockdown. Then i witnessed another, then another, then it seemed as if i had seen around 4 or 5 exact same lights travelling in the same direction at the same speed. Began gettinf very curious at this point and the lights had my attention and my cousins attention. Then i began seeing the same lights, but travelling in pairs, keeping a constant distance apart and mirroring eachothers flight path and speed, also travelling in the same direction from north to east. Witnessed around 3- 4 more of these pairs of lights. I did want to record them but my camera literally couldnt pick up the lights.at one point a plane flew below a single light and it seemed to travel at the same speed and direction as the plane, but a lot higher above it. We compared how large and loud the plane was compared to these objects that looked like floating stars with no noise. No evidence but wanted to tell someone
