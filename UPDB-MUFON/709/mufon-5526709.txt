Dark object penetrates clouds...

I was walking north on Pottery Ave to my transit van pick up point (for reference of direction of sighting) when I checked my phone for the time. At which point I noticed movement in front of me, to the north, where the city of Bremerton is, which gives off a lot of light pollution. The citys lights can be seen on the clouds at night (early morning). Well, I saw that, as I looked up a dark curved object briefly come through the clouds. The clouds were an orange color and this object was completely black, it was not reflecting any light at all. As I watched (merely a few seconds)it went back into the clouds. This object was far to the north of me and looked to be of significant size. It did not completely clear the clouds so I only got to see a part of it.
