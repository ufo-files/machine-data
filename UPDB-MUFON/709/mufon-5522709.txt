7 year old spotted

My 7 year old said mommy look at that airplane it looks like it is going to land right here, on Centennial Olympic Pkway so as I am driving I turn to my left to look at what she was talking about and I said baby girl that is no airplane it is clear and hovering. I told her it was a spaceship and she started crying at once realizing that it was still, and she said what does that mean for us. I was driving and told my little girl to keep her eye on the object until I could pull over within 10 sec. I look back after stopping and it was totally out of sight. Keep in mind we are in a wide open spot...I know that what we saw was nothing humans made...It had two white lights and two red...flashing randomly.
