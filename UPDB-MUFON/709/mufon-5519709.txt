Observed UFO over CA side of Colorado River this date

I observed a disc shaped object over the California side of the Colorado River while on a walk this date. I left the RV at roughly 12:05 PM walking north to a marina and restaurant at shortly after noon. When rounding the marina at 12:22 PM, I noticed a silvery object over the CA side of the Colorado River moving slowly in a SW direction. It was at about 5-6,000 feet (as measured from the heights of the hills below them) and at a horizontal distance of about 1/2 mile. It did not have any wings. It did not have any emissions or make any noise that I could hear. I watched it for about 45 seconds. It was sliding to the SW; slowly ascending, and then accellerating at a rate that I could not begin to describe. It did have a top or upper layer. I returned home at 12:32 PM. I appear to be missing 8-10 minutes.
