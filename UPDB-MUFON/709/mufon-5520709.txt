Fireballs in sky changing direction quickly

Out walking my dog when my neighbor shouted from the street if I see what he is seeing. Towards downtown Hollywood, there was a large, bright (what looked to be on fire) ball. When it moved, it left a trail that is similar to a shooting star. The object would hover, then quickly climb diagonally, then to the side, then drop and shoot up - did this several times, in all directions. It finally appeared to move away quickly and then just disappeared. My neighbor said it started off as 3 balls of fire, but two were gone by the time I witnessed this. Definitely have never seen anything like this before. I grew up in Colorado Springs where there are tons of military happenings in the sky.
