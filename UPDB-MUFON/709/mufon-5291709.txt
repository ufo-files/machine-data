9/29/20 6:45 (MST) first saw bright light. Explosion...3 white lights dropped out...object headed south red glow followed by white glow

At 6:45 I witnessed a bright white light stationary light in the sky from Milcreek, UT 84124 looking at the east at about 65-70 degrees above me. It was stationary for at least 3-4 minutes. I went inside to get binoculars. As I spotted the light with the binoculars it looked like it had faded from the bright white light. It appeared to "explode" and a halo appeared and the white light disappeared. The halo appeared to be the size of a quarter as I viewed it...then the white light appeared to be falling below it...then a second light...then a third white light as it was falling away from the halo..this all happened in 30 to 45 seconds. I passed the binoculars to my girlfriend and she couldn't find it. I couldn't see the 3 lights with my naked eye. I had the binoculars back and scanned the sky...There was now a bright red light traveling south...with 3 dim white lights stacked below it. This was observed traveling south over a 15 minute duration.
