Disc was hovering right above a 50 foot tall tree disc shaped had writing on surface which I can only describe as a combination of Russian and heirogl

It was a cloudy rainy night but craft was below clouds I was driving down 23rd St. And noticed many people standing in the rain looking up so I pulled over got out and walked right under it and stood there for at least 5 minutes it never moved or made a sound. My best friend from high school was with me he was speechless and stunned a couple years later I brought it up to him and he acted like I lost my mind and said it never happened.
