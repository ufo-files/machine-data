Black Object

I was sitting in the stands at a football game. It was a sunny day. Some clouds but not in the area where it was. When I looked up, there was a huge gap of blue sky. I saw what I wondered was a black plane, I said out loud to my boyfriend, "Is that a plane?" and looked at him for a second, looked back, and it was gone. I knew that it couldn't have been an airplane because it would have been the fastest airplane in the world. No clouds for it go behind. If it were balloons, where did they go? I can follow airplanes. This was not an airplane.
