{nd} Pretty blue bright star moving horizontal above the tree line.

It was about four in the morning, I had just checked the time. I often look out my window at the sky at night. I am awake at night and sleep during the day. For some teson I randomly looked at the time on my phone and then looked out my window I noticed a bright bluish star that was brighter than the rest it didn't appear to be moving at first and then I noticed it was moving horizontal to the tree line. It faded out and was gone in about a minute. I am surprised I looked out the window when I did and caught it. It made me wonder how often this happens unoticed. It wasnt a flair, shooting star, or satalite. I have seen all of those before countless times. I am not sure what it was. Later this year my son and the kids at the bus stop saw something similar. That is what made me decide to report this. Lucky I had written about it in my journal.
