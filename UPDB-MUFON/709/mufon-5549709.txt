Orange, elongated object moving south

I had finished preparing a flower bed yup, it took me this long to finish andwas laying on the grass trying to recover becuse I was seriously overheated, soI was just laying there watching fireflies and shooting stars go by. I was aboutto go inside when I saw what i thought was a very large meteor burning up. I sawan orange flare suddenly appear in the sky but instead of burning up it sloweddown and I could see it was orange, glowing, and elongated. Kinda like acylinder or cigar. I yelled What the hell is that and I must have sounded upsetbecuse the woman with me looked up and asked if it as an airplane and I told herI saw it enter the atmosphere and if it was a plane wed only see lightsblinking. We watched it until it was too far away to see and it flew south in aslow slower then even a single prop plane curving zig zag. It just slowly wentfrom left to right as it went forward. My hearts still racing. Ive seen a lot ofstrange lights out here in the past, but this is the first time I actually saw ashape.
