Large, white, domed

My wife and I were walking along a lane in our village in Gloucestershire, UK, when we happened to notice, up in front of us, an object resembling a large, white, domed corkscrew with appendages at the base, moving swiftly and silently through the sky, from right to left, against the wind for a few seconds before it disappeared from view behind a small hill nearby. A moment later we heard a loud boom and local residents later reported that they had lost electricity for a minute or so at around that time. We thought initially that the object was a remote-control craft such as a drone but we realised later that drones are audible and this object was not only silent but also too large and moving too smoothly to be a drone.
