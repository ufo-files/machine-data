{jpg} Bright white long light hovering in the sky then disappears

As I was driving home from work around 6:12-15 I noticed an object in the sky. At first I didn’t think anything of it but then I noticed it was brilliantly white. I was driving north on county road 237 and was trying to pay attention to driving but my eyes were drawn back. The object appeared to stay stationary. The bright white light was in a straight line about 10 degrees from vertical. I also noticed there was more material to the object, it was gray and dark colored. Then all of a sudden I just asked my wife,”do you see that?” She was like see what. I pointed in the northwest direction of the object. She then said she saw it. We were both like what is that thing. She moved in her seat to get a better look, getting closer to the windshield. Then a tree came in between us and the object. When we passed the tree the object disappeared. We both expressed we had no idea what it was but we both had seen it. We described the same object. Then life goes on.
