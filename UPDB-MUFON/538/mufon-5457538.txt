{cak} Six lights seen

Whilst walking the dog on my own, at about 21:30 I noticed 6 lights in the sky, moving initially in an orchestrated way about 2 to 3 miles away, to the north, at a height of 500 feet. They were moving across the sky, covering a distance of approx a mile. After about 10 minutes the lights then were seen to move more erratically though they still seen to be dancing with each other which looked like they were intelligently controlled. I came back to the house where my wife also noticed them. I then went back out to the field, this time with my very powerful binoculars. I sat in watching them until 22:50 and during this time the lights were seen to disappear for a minute at a time but then reform. During this reformation the lights were seen to gather at very high speeds from different areas of the sky with the sky lighting up with a trail as they reformed. I texted my son to let him know, and he informed me that he and his friends also saw the display of lights.
