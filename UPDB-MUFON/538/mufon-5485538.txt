{jpg} thought it was a bright planet. it moved high, fast bright flickering...way to fast for a plane or satellite

Took dogs into backyard, looking at stars. Noticed very bright planet. It began to move very fast, too big and bright to be a jet! Realized it was huge and fast! Very bright!!! Ran inside, grabbed binoculars, ran out and it changed direction turning left of me then to the right...sudden clouds that were not noticed before. Through the binoculars it was very shiny, pulsating blue, gold lights, flickering. Like a planet had caught fire and was rushing around. I felt dizzy. I could easily see it through the light clouds. It was moving away very fast...suddenly, it blinked, then seemed to be a pinpoint, then was bright, then completely vanished...i was looking through binoculars during this time. I kept watching, nothing...there is constant air traffic near our house, but during the entire sighting there was nothing...no sounds at all, which struck me as very strange. Then everything is back to normal. I am texting this outside where I have been since I saw it.
