Black object similar with tail in the sky not seen with naked eye

I was working near downtown Chattanooga on January 5th 2021 on a "boom lift" about 60 feet in air painting a wall off U.S. 27.  The sun was bursting through so bright from behind the clouds and made a beautiful scene on downtown Chattanooga. I love taking pictures with my cell phone of nature and of the skies. So I pulled out my cell phone and took several pictures of the scene before getting back to painting. Later that evening I showed the pictures to my wife. She pointed out in one of the pictures, a black object that looked similar to a black wilted rose in the very middle of the sky. I had not noticed this when I took the picture and it was not seen on the other pictures that I took before and after it. We had no idea what it could be. I didn't think much of it , but my wife finally convinced me, after almost a year, to report it to Mufon. So I am doing just that and sending the picture of the object that day that still has not been identified.
