{cak} Saw creature crossing the road

I was a passenger in a car driving north on Morris Rd when I saw what looked to be tall, see-through Grey legs that moved backwards crossing the street. I was so fixated on the fact that they were backward moving and nothing I'd ever seen before that I didn't look up at the creature before it was gone. Myfriend did though and she said it had what looked like a fox head.
