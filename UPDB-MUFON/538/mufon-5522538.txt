amazing sighting over miami november 19, 2011

I was in the back seat of a car traveling on the 826 expressway in Miami,Fl. Night had just fallen. As i looked out the window i observed 4 lights in a triangle like formation hovering about a mile away at low altitude. As soon as i saw it i realized they were not airplanes beacause they were stationary and had no blinking red or blue lights. The objects suddenly disappered and reappeared in a different formation. They did this several times. It was amazing to watch. As we exited the freeway we pulled over to get a better view. After a few minutes the objects vanished and i saw a small plane circleling the area. I noticed there was a similar incident in Miami just days earlier posted on youtube.
