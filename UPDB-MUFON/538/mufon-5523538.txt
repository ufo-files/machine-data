me and 3 friends saw a triangular shaped object with 3 bright white lights in each corner hoovering over the Tappan Z bridge

Me and 3 other freinds were outside at night just having a conversation in our neighborhood when I noticed a formation of bright white lights in the formation of a triangle hoovering over the Tappan Zee Bridge on the Westchester side. We watched the object hoover and the lights seemed to get brighte. After about 10-12 minutes the object either flew away or gained too much ascension and distance from us to see the lights any longer. My boy freind and I have seen similiar objects in our area and in nyc earlier this year and several years before this incident took place.
