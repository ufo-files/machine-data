I was sleeping in my friend's backyard on his trampoline with three other people, and at about 1 AM EST, I spotted a moving, star-sized, object. being the smartest and most observant one of the group, I called the other three to look at what I told them was the ISS, but about 15 minutes later it stopped in its track. I was making sure I wasn't going crazy, so I asked my friend what He just saw, and sure enough, he said that He saw it stop too, but this is where it gets interesting. I asked my friend to point at what he was looking at, and it turned out that we were watching two completely different objects. 15 minutes after that, they started moving towards each other and stopped when they reached a certain point. At this point, my object emitted a red light that pulsed throughout the sky and my friend's object disappeared. in about another hour, my object was gone too. For the rest of the night my friends and I could feel things watching us, but we didn't look for evidence.
