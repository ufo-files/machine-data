Speed of light

I personally witnessed an event that I’ve never been able to explain nor have I encountered any such other accounts of similar nature having ever been reported. This is surprising as I always fully expected to hear more accounts exactly like mine, yet that never came about. I can’t wait any longer. On the early morning hours of January 30, 2002 while patrolling the parking lot of a hotel for which I was the night security officer, I happened to look to the western sky at approximately 30 degrees when I saw 3 lights streak toward me at a rate I can only compute as having been light speed. What I saw were 3 lights in parallel traveling toward me so quickly that they were just long streaks of light. There was no sound. It was a clear night, the moon was bright and just to the East behind me. Whatever this was, it was enormous. It is my own certifiable proof to myself that we are not alone in this universe. Unless something man made can travel at the speed of light, we have something/someone much more advanced with much more capabilities than we have, traveling in our part of the universe. I will leave out the instances just before and just after this sighting as to reveal them here would defy humanities comprehension of that which is believable surely labeling me as being crazy. I couldn’t believe it that night and still can’t believe it myself today even though I had personally had the first hand account to the event.
