UFO hovering above St. Josephs Hospital for no less than 25 minutes.

My sister from Florida a retired Nurse of Anesthesia and myself a retired Nursing Asst from Pennsylvania came out to see our sister who was placed on hospice. She was a patient at St. Josephs Hospital in Tacoma. We were getting ready to leave for the day. I went outside first to catch a cigarette before getting in the car for the drive back to my daughters home in Yelm. I sat in a bus stop shed across the street from the hospital entrance. I looked toward the hospital building and hovering high above the hospital somewhat to the left. I saw a disc shaped object. It was stationery. Made no sound. I watch this disc for 15 minutes . It never moved. My sister Helen came out of the building and saw me staring at the sky above the hospital. She asked whats wrong???? I told her to look above the hospital. Which she did, and was as baffled as I was. She too saw the disc. We watched it for another ten minutes or so. Thinking on my feet I pulled out my phone and snapped a few pics. I would like to send you the photo and also ask if there were any other reports in that area around that time. Please advise me on how to submit my picture.
