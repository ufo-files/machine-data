{dr}Red orb hovered over tree tops then flashed bright and shot into sky when tried to get a picture.

Walking on a golf course, saw a red orb a little ways in the distance hovering over the tree tops. It was so bright and still I thought it might have been a planet. When I pulled out my phone to see if it could have been Mars, it flashed once and shot straight up into the sky. I got a weird feeling like it knew I was watching it for a good half hour before it vanished into the sky. The time was around 5-6 pm eastern time.
