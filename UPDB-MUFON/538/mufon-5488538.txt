{jpg} I stopped to take foto of a fallen limb in the street. Arriving home showed husband and saw the object

Returning home at approximately 3:45pm I had my cell phone on its camera function as I had just stopped to take a pic of the clouds after a storm that had just blew through. It was monsoon and would occasionally storm complete with high winds and hail. On the side street before I turn onto my street there was a tree limb that had fallen during the storm. As I was driving, I only stopped for a moment to take a pic of the fallen branch. As I got home a few minutes later I showed my husband the photo and we both saw the object in the sky well above the neighborhood and although seemingly hazy as if cloaked by clouds it is clearly below the clouds.
