Orange Light

On my way home, back from work. I was heading west on Becker RD and made a right going north on Darwing blvd. On this street I noticed this oval shaped orange light. At first I didn't think it was anything other than a far away street orange light. Then it blinked 2 times, that caught my attention. At that moment it started moving to my right(east) very slowly, then it stopped for about 3 seconds and started going up slowly. 15 seconds later they light was off or it disappeared. I would like to mention that the skies were very clear and we had a full moon. The light re appeared not far away from where last seen and it kept going up, when it finish going up, it started heading east. I notice it had like an orange tail of light almost like a rocket. No blinking lights seen anywhere like any airplane or helicopter would have, but just a big bright orange light. I got out of my vehicle and did not hear any sound at all. This light went from my 10 o'clock to my 4 o'clock. Light just disappeared at that time.
