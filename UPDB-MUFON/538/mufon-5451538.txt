Green fireball type meteorite

At about 1am, looked outside window towards hill facing East and happened to catch a “shooting star” appearing to fall from the SE towards the East. Appeared to flare and disappear in fashion of meteorite, but the size/color (bright green)/and length of event (seemed to take longer than a second) were unusual. Went to other bedroom to tell family, and my father was already awake, and had seen the same. Unfortunately, being unprepared, we were not able to get documentation.
