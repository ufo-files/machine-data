Object moving west/northwest came to full stop and accelerated in opposite direction

I was getting a ride to work from my mother. It was sunny out, almost no clouds at all. While driving down Courthouse Road (approaching Jefferson Davis Highway) I noticed the glint of a metallic object in the sky, north of us and travelling in the same direction at between (about)18,000ft and (about)27,000ft altitude. I always try to identify any plane/helicopter I see, that being said, I focused on the object, thats when I noticed it didn't seem to have any wings. I thought "maybe it's a commercial plane banking in this direction." except there was no tail to the craft, so I was quite puzzled. After approximatley 20 seconds of watching the craft it suddenly stopped completely (my jaw dropped), thinking it was my eyes/brain creating an illusion of it stopping because the car had stopped at a light or something, I looked around only to find out that was not the case and that we were still cruising along. I then resumed watching the object/craft and it continued to hover for 3 to 5 seconds, at which time it doubled back on its previous course at two to three times the speed while ascending at a (visually at least) 5 degree climb. I kept watching it until the roof of the car blocked my line of sight. I was absolutley estatic about seeing what I had, so started watching the sky very closely and saw a formation of two Chinook helicopters approaching from the south at a maximum of 1500ft Alt. (NOT 15,000-I repeat 1500ft). When they got probably a football field and a half away from us, the two helicopter made a hard bank left in the direction that the object went I suspect they had just been told to try and tail the object due to them being in the area already (possibly while en route to Quantico).
