Taking pictures of the night sky and moon, took two pictures before taking the picture of this object

I was standing outside my back door...clear sky.. camera in hand as usual taking pictures of the moon and anything else that I can zoom into.I tried to zoom back into my second object but this other object was virtually in the same spot... at first I thought it must be a satellite so zoomed in...usually the Satellites are very bright with lights and spinning through the lens...very difficult to take a shot of...This particular object was very still it was to the right of the moon but all of a sudden it slightly turned and I thought what an odd shape almost upside down V shape lit up by the moon I thought...I zoomed in as far as I could and took a shot..Went to take a second and I couldn't see it anymore..I did scan for a few minutes more ..but nothing...
I have seen other strange things before.. moving in the night sky and not got a good clear shot...so mostly come out blurred..
I did not download the pictures til the 16th October 2016...
Whilst I was taking a look at them...I was really quite surprised as to what it was...
