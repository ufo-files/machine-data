3 starlike stars traveling in sequential formation on northern horizon going west to east

There were 15 of sitting around the fire looking at the stars when we noticed it looked like Orion’s Belt took off. We all observed in the northern horizon these 3 stars travel from west to east all in formation. My brother in law Eric and nephew look at it through the binoculars and it appeared as a cigar shaped object that had 3 large lights and possibilities of more lights that surrounded it and on the shape itself. We tried to get footage with our phones but it traveled to fast and was to dark to capture. We watched for approximately 10 minutes and lost the object behind a tree line and when we went to pick it back up past the tree line it was gone.
