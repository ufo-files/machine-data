Watched what appeared like B-2 Bomber with no lights move silently almost overhead at low altitude

This incident, with the consent of the witness, is being reported by Investigator Beverly Trout. Witness was on way home from work & incident occurred sometime during time span of 12:30-1:30 a.m., PROBABLY on July 6, 2011. Witness thought shape might indicate that it was the B-2 Bomber at what she considered low level, moving on a straight-line trajectory transit, during which it moved almost overhead her car - she remembers viewing it through her open sunroof. She also observed the object as it continued on its way and passed over a nearby housing development. Object was silent, and had no lights.
