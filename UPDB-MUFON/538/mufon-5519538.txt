3 faint starlike objects criss crossing path N->S and S->N across eastern hemisphere.

Was at home on the deck smoking. Porch lights on. 9 pm est -/+ 1 minute. Facing east and notice a faint light (i.e., very faint star) travelling a straight path from the NE sky (about 65 degrees vertical from horizon)towards the SE sky. Time of travel from spotting to horizon was about 40 sec. Next, about half a min later, spotted orange streak with hollow black center (shaped like a streaming firework with the center/core blacked out) travel from SW towards the path the first object took in the NE. Spot another faint light traveling from the SE towards the NE (about 45 degrees vertical from horizon) travel time approx. 30 sec from initial observation till lost behind cloud cover in the NE sky. Approx. 1 min later observed another faint light travelling from NE towards the SE (approx 50 degrees vertical from horizon) travel time from initial observation in the NE to SE horizon approx. 45 sec.
