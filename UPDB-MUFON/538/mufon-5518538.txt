Bright light over Stuarts Draft VA

Sitting on the front porch and over the tree line across the street we thought was a plane, but it stayed in one place hovering for about 3 min. It started bright and then the light got smaller and smaller. It didn't shoot across the sky (we sit outside in the evenings and do see quite a few shooting stars) this object didn't do that. It just hovered over the tree line. Then disappeared.
