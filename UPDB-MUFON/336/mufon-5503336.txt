{rjl} Black object passing over my head and landing

I was working on Andrews AFB in Md. A coworker and I were on the flight line waiting to recover one of our aircraft it was around 11 pm we were sitting in a tow tractor when a disc like object flew right by us so we both got out and were looking for it and it came back and hovered over us it did not have any nav lights but I gave off a blast of air when it was by us. Then we both see it land on the other side of the runway and then saw it pull into a hanger with no lights on then the doors on the hanger close very fast. A couple minutes later our aircraft lands and we ask our aircrew if the saw anything and they told us that they did not but were in a holding pattern. They asked us if any other aircraft had landed because the could not see anything coming in. We told them that there was no aircraft that landed. The next day my coworker and I decided to drive over to the side of the base were the disc landed but we were told we did not have access to that part of the base but it "was open to the hole base to drive up to the hanger" because I have driven to that hanger quite a few times with out any problems before the night before. We both were very confused on what was going on because all flight for the next month got canceled if it was going to have a return after 1030 pm. We both got taken off the night shift for that month also.
