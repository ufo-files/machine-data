Fishing looking at the sky , full moon and we seen a light just disappear for about 3 seconds then reappeared.off/on

1. My partner and I were fishing on the beach at Diggers Camp near Coffs Harbour at night full moon.
2. I noticed the light go off and looked at Jess and we both seen it reappear seconds later then disappear and reappear again. 
Then I noticed to more of the same lights close by. 
3. I knew as soon as I seen it that it was craft.
4. The objects were round lights that were staying mostly still but would disappear and reappear in a different spot but not far from were they disappeared. I was not scared and yelled to them "come down to the bay and say hello I know what is going on"
Then one of the craft the mother ship flashed us from left to right 8 lights around the craft , one light at a time and we counted them as they came on and went off. 
It was like they were showing us how big the craft was.
5. My body was tingling and I wasn't scared because I know about ufo's and how they operate and how craft move. We walked away and headed back up off the beach and the lights were still there. 
We returned after an hour but did not see any more craft or lights.
6. We left the beach but the main light from the craft was still there 
We just got a little bit paranoid we didn't want to be abducted.
