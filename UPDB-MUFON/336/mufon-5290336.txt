Lights and Sound

When I was a child I woke to what was a sound pulsating whine and my dog barking outside. When I looked out the window I saw green and red lights shining down from what ever hovered over my house. The next day my sister noticed something drawn on my neck and I noticed sores on my inner ankles. Another instance was when I was camping. I heard the familiar sound and hid my face under my sleeping bag. I saw the lights but did not open the tent to see outside. The years range between 1976-1978. I have never spoken publicly, but have confided in family members later in my life, for fear of stigma. I had heard of no one else giving any UFO accounts from my home city of Charleston SC during this time, so I chose to keep it to myself until recently.
