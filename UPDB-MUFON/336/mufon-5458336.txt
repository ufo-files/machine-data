{jpg} Two glowing yellow/orange orbs gliding above the treetops

I observed 2 glowing yellow/orange orbs gliding above the tree tops. They were traveling at what I would guess was about 5 or 6 miles per hour. (I feel as though if I had sped up to a fast jog I could have kept up with them for a while) One of the orbs was approximately 20 to 30 feet behind the other. They were not attached to each other and they were silent. I watched them as they traveled westward until I lost sight of them because of the density of the trees. I am not absolutely sure of the date but I'm guessing September 2nd in the evening. I did not have my phone with me so was not able to try to get a photo. It is when I described this episode to my sister and nephew today that they thought I should report it to the MUFON website.
