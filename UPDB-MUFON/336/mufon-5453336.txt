About 20 lights all moving in a straight line from the West to the South East.

I went out to smoke as I usually do and I usually look at Venus and the moon and they were close together tonight. Then I noticed some lights moving from between the moon and Venus. At first I saw five lights moving in a straight line. I was bewildered. Then a few seconds later, I noticed there were even more lights behind them, still coming in the same straight line. Just watched the news, and they say they were satellites launched by Spacex. Still, I will submit this just so you have it.
