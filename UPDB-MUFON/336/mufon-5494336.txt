{dr}A welcoming STAR as I entered home in New Mexico

I was driving from Texas and going home in New Mexico. Just as I arrived and went past the border traveling west, I observed a shining "star" high above the horizon. I thought it could be the evening star but I realized that it was still too bright in the evening for stars to appear. This object became brighter before it faded, cloaked and/or disappeared. I remember feeling as if being welcomed by "them". I had an elated feeling because of it. Then 5 minutes later it reappeared but much lower on the horizon and closer to some commercial jets that were traveling west. I could see their con-trails and they were very off in the distance. The "star" or "light" remained low in the sky and directly in front of me in the western sky. It remained another 3 minutes before it disappeared or I just lost track. I'm expert as observation of celestial craft... I believe it just disappeared.
