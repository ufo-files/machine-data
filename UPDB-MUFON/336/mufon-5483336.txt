{jpg} ME AND 2 FRIENDS VIEWED A UFO ABOVE TREETOP HOVERING IN A TRANCE STANDING ROUGHLY 10 FT APART - I FEEL THERE WAS COMMUNICATION WITH MY MIND I WAS TOO SCARED TO DEAL WITH COULD HAVE BEEN THERE 15 MIN OR UP TO 30 IT LEFT QUICKLY

i WAS IN 8TH GRADE COMING HOME WITH 2 GUYS I PLAY WITH BUT WERE NOT CLOSE FRIENDS WITH - i REMEMBER THAT IT WAS 8TH GRADE BASKETBALL PRACTICE WHICH PUTS THIS EVENT BETWEEN OCTOBER AND DECEMBER OF 1976. GENERALLY I WAS SCARED MOST OF MY LIFE ALWAYS LOOKING DOWN AS I WALKED WORRIED ABOUT WHAT MY FATHER WAS GOING TO DO WHEN I GOT HOME LATE. I DO NOT REMEMBER WHAT THE OTHER 2 GUYS WERE DOING BUT I REMEMBER THAT I FOUND MYSELF STANDING STILL LOOKING AT AN ANGLE OF AROUND 60 DEGREES THROUGH A TREE -NOT A THICK TREE EASY TO SEE THROUGH IT. THE UFO OR SHOULD I SAY SPACESHIP WAS DIRECTLY ABOVE THE TREE WHICH WAS AROUND 50 FT TALL. I NOTICED THAT IT HAD LIGHTS OF THE RED VARIETY MOVING AROUND ITS PERIMETER. I HAVE NO IDEA WHAT WAS GOING ON WITH THE OTHER 2 BUT I SENSED THEY WERE IN A TRANCE LIKE MYSELF. I FELT THAT MY MIND WAS VERY SLOWLY BEING ENGAGED IN SOME SORT OF THOUGHT EXCHANGE OR MAYBE THEY WERE TRYING TO ASK ME TO DO SOMETHING( WHICH IS MY SURMISE AFTER 30 YEARS OF THINKING ABOUT IT) AND I WAS GETTING MORE AND MORE SCARED AS TIME WENT BY. I ACTUALLY REMEMBER NOW THAT IN MY MIND I STARTED PLAYING THIS GAME WHERE I WAS PRETENDING TO NOT UNDERSTAND WHAT THEY WERE SAYING. I FELT A SENSITIVE LOVING COMMUNICATION NOT FORCING OR AGGRESSIVE AT ALL. THEN WHEN I FELT CALMER I BUILT UP A LITTLE MORE COURAGE AND STARTED REALLY LOOKING AT IT WITH MORE INTENT I TRIED TO LOOK INTO ITS WINDOWS FOR ANY ACTIVITY AND I MIGHT HAVE SEEN SOMETHING BUT NOT SURE. I THEN SAID TO MYSELF OK THEY ARE ABOUT TO LEAVE HOLD MY GAZE ON THE SHIP SO I CAN SEE WHERE IT GOES AND MAYBE EVEN FOLLOW IT -I KNOW FUNNY EVENTUALLY IT TOOK OFF IT WENT TO THE EDGE OF MY VISION IN ABOUT 1 SECOND OR LESS AND VEERED OFF TO THE RIGHT WITH A RED LIGHTED TRAIL. ALL 3 OF US SNAPPED OUT OF IT AND A POLICE CAR WAS RIGHT THERE AT THAT SAME MOMENT THE 2 GUYS WENT RUNNING TO THE COP CAR AND STARTED RECANTING THE STORY AND I RAN HOME WHICH HAPPENED TO BE 2 HOUSES AWAY AS I RAN I HEARD THE COP SAY YAA SOME PEOPLE UP THE STREET SAID THE SAME THING. WHATEVER WENT ON WITH MY COMMUNICATION IT TOLD ME DONT TALK ABOUT THIS AND BESIDES MY SISTER I TOLD NO ONE UNTIL ABOUT 20 OR SO YEARS LATER IT STARTED COMING UP IN CONVERSATION
