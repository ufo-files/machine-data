inwas between the ages of 6-9.I seen a light green light coming through the window and got up to look just then my step dad started yelling for everyone to get on the floor and stay away from the windows,of course I looked out.It was glowing green,circular,disk shaped object just hovering.My step dad started shooting at it with his rifle.I giggled because I knew,even at that age he couldn't hurt it.I felt a weird sense of happiness.It stayed there for a few seconds,even with my dad shooting at it and then shot off diagonally ,up towards the sky and south,then it was gone.A couple of days later I noticed something in my forefinger,it was a black line deep under the skin.I showed my mom and sister and they took me down and tried to dig it out with a needle.It was shiny and silver,once they got down far enough to see it.They picked at it for over an hour.You could hear that it was solid by the clicking noise it made as they tried to dig it out.I told them it was gone,just so they would  stop! The needle hurt.I noticed about a week later it was still there but told nobody because I knew they would try to get it out again.i heard my mom say to my aunt one day that she hoped that it didn't come from what ever was out over the house,and I knew it had to be.At times I can see a slight line still in my finger.I Would imagine there would be something on it,there were other people outside their homes.My sister still remembers it too,we recently discussed that night.The newspaper locally was the Messenger Index.
