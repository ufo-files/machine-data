(GS) Started when I was seven or eight years old, seems to have stopped about 12 years ago.

These events have occurred throughout my life, They started when I was about seven or eight years old, Bright balls of light circling my bed and a being sitting on my bed talking with me, always started with Sounds, humming or buzzing, than I would hear something like radio voices, no matter how hard I tried I could not understand what was being said. I started having strange dreams. As I attended Middle school I had a sighting of an object which started out bright star like and as it came closer it was shaped like a foot ball, a friend was with me, the sighting took place at night, Maybe Nov 1981, I reported this and was contacted by an Investigator who wanted to come to our home, My mom would not allow this, so I wrote my view of events and drew a picture and sent it to the investigator.
