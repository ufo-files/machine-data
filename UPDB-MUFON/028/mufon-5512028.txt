{dr}It was so large i thought i would hit it.in the same split second terror set in,i knew it wasnt supposed to be there.

It was approx 9 pm,i was driving to the local convience store. I was traveling at approx. 45mph. I came from a blind turn onto the crest of the big hill,steep grade with a wide deep valley. In a split second i saw this huge,gigantic bright oval shaped object with oval windows that were lit up very very bright. The object itsself seemed to be like a kind of mercury sort of like a solid liquid mass,just there not moving. My window was down,i don't remember hearing any noise at all. I felt terror and awe struck all at the same time. I hit the brakes on my vehicle, it seemed as if i may hit it. I quickly realized how big it was and i could drive under it to get away. It seemed to fill the whole valley. As i passed under it i felt more afraid so afraid i didn't look in my rearview mirror to see if it was still there as i went up the other side of the hill. I was trembeling so badly i could barely drive. When i arrived at the store the 2 women working tried to calm me. I was to afraid to tell what i had seen but to afraid not to. I stayed there about30 minutes. On my drive home i was so filled with anxiety i could barely breath. I arrived home about 11.00. I didn't sleep at all,trying to convince myself it wasnt there or it was something else. I know what i saw,i know what it was. Even now years later everytime i drive down that hill i can picture it . I feel alot of stress sometimes becuz of what i saw. Later that month i accidentally got some kind of bright moving orbs in a picture of the storm damage.
