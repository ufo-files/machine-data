{kt}Looked like a dull star, occasionally blinking purple or green

I was in my backyard, talking on the phone with a friend, and I saw a white light that looked like a dull star move across the sky, starting north west heading north east. I said something to her about it while I was on the phone with her. She saw it as it hit north east, went straight up into the sky and flash green and purple. Then it went down again in the same path, and went from north east to northwest again at a quick speed when I lost sight of it. My friend and I were disconnected from our conversation after some heavy static as the object was making its way northwest.
