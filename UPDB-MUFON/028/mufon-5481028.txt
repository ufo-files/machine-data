{nd} Flashing lights that disappeared and reappeared in different points of the clear night sky.

I was at a park with my friend, Kate. We were just talking when we observed a shooting star. Later on, we noticed eerie lights in the sky. They were small and they would disappear and then reappear at different points in the sky. This went of for about 5 minutes. The lights began south of us and departed north of us. I heard beeping sounds as it passed overhead.
