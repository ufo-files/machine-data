{rjl} A Large White/Clear Balloon Stationary in the E-SE Sky

We were boating in Hanson's Creek and one of us spotted this stationary large white object high in the E-SE sky. With the binoculars all 5 of us could make out that it was a balloon shape. The clouds passed in front of it so we could see it was not moving which we observed for about 2 hours. I couldn't find any information about it on the internet.
