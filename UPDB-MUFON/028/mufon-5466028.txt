Outside smoking then light appeared from sw direction. Stopped mid air then turned blood red in color stayed stationary for a while then moved straight then stopped again. Then went back the way it came

On the 3rd, went out for a smoke sat down facing west then noticed from SW direction a light coming.thought it was a helicoptor at first until it went completely stationary and turned into a blood red color and sat in the sky for a few minutes.then it went on further and I stood up and looked and it stopped again over some houses and went stationary again. I started to feel scared as I figured by then that this wasn't a plane or anything, and felt weary as it started coming back towards my house.as it moved it flashed green lights and changed color to a bright white orb as well and I got worried as it was coming closer towards my home.I lost sight of it as it flew over my and my neighbors home.
