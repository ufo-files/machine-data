8 year old daughter witnessed multiple tic-tac shaped objects moving in the area

On 3/4/2020 from 2:37 PM till 2:42 PM, my 8 year old daughter witnessed multiple (up to 6) tic-tac shaped objects flying into view. One tic-tac object shot to the left then down while the second tic-tac object stayed stationary. Then a third tic-tac object appeared then 3 more tic-tac objects. There may have been more but the tree line was obstructing her view. All stayed stationary for a moment then 4 shot out of view followed by last 2 tic-tac objects. She said she was at a neighbors playing outside when she noticed the objects and pointed them out to her friends. She was able to take pictures with her camera but they shot off before she could take a video.
