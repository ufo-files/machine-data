Witnessed a white oval shaped UFO low down and it moved in a straight line over the houses

Never had an interest in UFOs. Never even watched The X-Files. I always thought.... maybe.. we just don’t know. If it’s unidentified it’s just unidentified.

One night in 2016 (could have been May or June, I honestly can't remember), I’m standing out the back garden, in the dark, smoking (I no longer smoke).

I look up to my left, and I see a white oval shaped craft moving very slowly, and it disappeared over my neighbor's house. It was moving in a straight line. Looks like one of those tic-tac shaped UFOs, but more oval shaped...I think. I wish I had more time to look at it. Lasted about 10 seconds. I stood in stunned silence. And just watched it move slowly, and disappear over the house next door. No sound from it. Definitely wasn't a balloon or a lantern. It was like nothing I had ever seen before. It was a clear night and it was low enough that I could see that it a plain surface - no wings or anything. Just drifting slowly over the houses. It moved with purpose, in a straight line, like it was intelligently controlled - it wasn't like a balloon just drifting with the wind or anything like that.

I went inside and told my pal in New Jersey (he’s into the UFO thing). But I didn’t tell many others at all.
