(GS) Orangeish light split into 5 lights in triganle formation moving east

In my room with roommate at 1:29 getting ready for bed, my roommate called me over to the window and we saw a orange light shaped like orbs moving north stop near our house high in the sky but still large enough to see. It stopped above our neighborhood and split into five similar balls in a triangle formation. We then got his brother and went outside. The orbs rotated to face east and then moved off into the distance and out of sight. We all were a little shaken by what we saw but non of us were freaking out. We then contacted a friend who lived close by and he saw the same exact thing and was able to get a picture.
