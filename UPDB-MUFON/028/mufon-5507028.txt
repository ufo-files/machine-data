saw five orbs in the night sky, amber in colour.

I WAS IN THE KITCHEN ABOUT TO GO TO BED. DECIDED BEFORE I WENT UPSTAIRS TO BED TO LOOK
OUT OF FRENCH WINDOWS AND LOOK AT THE STARS.

WHEN I LOOKED UP I COULDNT BELIEVE WHAT I WAS SEEING. QUICKLY RUSHED UPSTAIRS TO MY BEDROOM TO LOOK OUT FROM BEDROOM WINDOW, THE OBJECTS WERE STILL THERE.

THEY WERE MOVING BACKWARDS AND FORWARDS UP AND DOWN. RUSHED BACK DOWNSTAIRS
FEELING EXCITED AND SCARED. PUT MY HAND OVER MY MOUTH IN ABSOLUTE SHOCK. 

ONE BY ONE I DISMISSED ALL THE THINGS THEY COULD HAVE BEEN, LANTERNS, BALLOONS, SPACE DEBRIS, WEATHER BALLOONS, MAYBE LIGHTS REFLECTED FROM AIRCRAFTS, COVERT AIRCRAFT. 
IVE NEVER SEEN ANYTHING LIKE THIESE OBJECTS IN THE SKY BEFORE.

THEY WERE STATIONARY, THEN ONE OF THE ORBS WOULD EITHER REVERSE, ANOTHER WOULD TRAVEL DOWN THEN UP, COME FORWARD THEY ALL DID THIS FOR SOME TIME THEN WOULD STOP.
AND REPEAT.

SHOCKED STUNNED ALL I COULD SAY WAS OH MY GOD REPEATEDLY. WITH MY HAND OVER MY MOUTH. I THINK I WAS IN A STATE OF SHOCK,

THEY SUDDENLY REVERSED VERY QUICKLY INTO THE NIGHT, IT TOOK A SECOND.
