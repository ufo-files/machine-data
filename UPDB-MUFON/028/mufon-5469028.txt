i am a star gazer living at Lansing in central lower Michigan and was enjoying an infrequent clear spring night. Since I was already looking up it noticed movement which turned out to be a ununiformed line of lights traveling from east to west at a very high rate of speed spanning the horizons in less than ten seconds. In a period of one and a half hours I observed 26 such groups. Some were also observed by my wife and adult son. The groups ranged in individual numbers of two to fifteen or more nd the lines changed shape as individual speeds of the lights changed as they passed by. Most of the lines were parrellel to the horizon running north to south end to end but one was vertical, east to west. At 45 degrees to the horizon they were white and not as bright but the groups straight above us appeared to be larger and reddish orange in color. 

I had no explanation because such movement is not of the natural world we live in. I become somewhat awed but not to excited as I have seen this phenomenon before several times as well as other types of unexplainable objects but never in this high of volume. I am making this repot in hopes that others had seen this and if they read this they might be inspired to write a repot as well. What's that chorus in the old Fleetwood Mac song, " it seems like a dream, they've got you hypnotized ?
The lights simply traveled out of view from the distance they traveled away from me. Thank you MUFON, I am sending a donation.
