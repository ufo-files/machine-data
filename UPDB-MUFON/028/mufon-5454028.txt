Looked like satelite at first

First one observed coming from south moving north east. Thought is was a satelite but got really bright and vanished. Then observed over 15 jaut appearing out of no where from the north moving in straight lines thru the handle of the big dipper, about 30 seconds apart then would vanish. The 4th or 5th one once at the handle of the big dipper sharply turned east thru the spoon part of the big dipper and speed out of sight. The last object grew really bright before vanishing, then disappeared b4 the cleared the horizon. They move at a slow steady pace, observable for at least 2 mintues each one. No air craft or lights seen in the area, just stars and moon light to the south east
