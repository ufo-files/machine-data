{jpg} Viewing of two OFO'S in evening sky over Hilton NY

I was leaving my daughters house on Christmas day evening. Her Street winds upward to a Main Street. As I was approaching the top of the street I happen to look out of the driver side window at the beautiful moon. In doing so I noticed a triangular shaped object in between and above two houses on my left. I thought it was a Christmas gift of some kind of a rocket or kite..but as I looked to the street level I saw no one outside. Then I thought it was a street lamp of some sort so as I rolled up to the top of the street it disappeared and almost in its place was a huge cigar shaped object with the whole bottom in huge brilliant orange and yellow flames! I pulled over immediately to the side of the road to my left as that is the way I was turning onto the main road heading to my home, and just watched it fly Northbound and asend as it dissappeard into the night sky over farm land. I looked around to see if anyone else was around as I was in shock! There was no one so I called my daughter hysterically as I drove shaking and weaving trying to still drive all the way home.
