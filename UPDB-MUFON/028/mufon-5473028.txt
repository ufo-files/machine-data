{rjl} White strobe very fast and intermittent. High air speed

While traveling southbound on hwy. 55 at approx. 6:20am I notice a strobe light at a high altitude to the east. It was an extremely fast pulse then stopped for a second and repeated. Not like a normal plane strobe. It moved south at a very high rate of speed, an was beyond the southern horizon in under 2 minutes. There was a plane at the same time to the south east, traveling south. The planes speed was much slower in comparison an took several minutes to travel out of sight. I saw no outline an it was not visible in between pulses.
