This has been ongoing incident for a many years with many objects in different areas of the sky and each time something different happens. At times it is round at other times the objects spins or changes shape. Sometimes the colors are extremely vibrant. There has been times where the colors flash sequential as though it was conveying a message. There have been times when three will pass over and head in different directions then one will come back and stop while the others one by one will fly across in front of it and the other will make a 90 degree turn in front of it. In this video a plane flies underneath the object.
