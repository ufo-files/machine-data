4 bright reddish lights, changing direction, altitude,as it hovered.followed by several helicopters searching the area.

My 23 yr.old son and I were on our way back from a neighborhood convenience store, and coming south on 137th st.towards 49th. As we came out of the housing and entered the open area surrounded by fields, at the same time my son and I both said "what is that!" In the sky above us, approx. 1/8th mile distant were 4 bright red lights with an orange-yellow glow in the centers.This was directly south of our position and we were heading towards it. It was configured like a triangle with the 4th light at the center of it.At first when I saw it through the trees, my first thought was that a plane had exploded and was coming down, these lights were so bright in intensity. We slowed way down as did the other traffic. I know there have to be other witnesses, as there was alot of other cars on the road, and 137th is a main connection to a main thoroughfare.My son told me to speed up and try to get closer. As I did this the triangle began to descend then rise back up. It did this several times as I tried to find a place to pull off. Once parked my son jumped out to get a better look, and I watched it and glanced to see if I could exit the truck also. As I got out my son said " It's gone, It just disappeared. I could'nt see it anywhere, so we got back in the truck and drove the 4 blocks back to my home and told my husband and brother-in-law what we had just seen.We were both pretty excited,as we have both witnessed "things" that did'nt seem natural in the sky before. And we were both aware of what we were witnessing.
