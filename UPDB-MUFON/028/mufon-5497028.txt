{de}on cruise ship from freeport bahamas to ft lauderdale fl. cylinder flew over with flames shooting out

Myself, my wife and my daughter were traveling by cruise ship from freeport bahamas to ft lauderdale fl in july 1998. About half way through the trip there was a cylindrical object shot across the sky from the south towards the north. At first I thought it may be something like the shuttles rocket boosters, but they wouldn't be shooting across the sky by themselves. As it crossed over the ship heading north there was what looked like a 50 foot trail of fire coming out the back of it. There was a very long smoke trail left by this object. The shuttle had taken off from canaveral a few days before this, but I don't think it could of been any piece of it because this thing was shooting across the water at about 50 feet off the surface and it never slowed down as it continued north along the atlantic ocean inside the devils triangle. After I finally lost view of the object as it got out of sight I felt strange til I got off the ship.
