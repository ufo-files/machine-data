{tjd} One bright light turned into three bright lights

I was sitting in my hot tub with the jets on looking at my phone when I noticed a really bright light to the left of me in the sky thinking that it was a plane. So I looked over and it was one single bright light, way brighter than any plane, it was lower than any plane would be because the closest airport that can land a plane of any decent size is 60 to 70 miles away. Also, the object didn’t have red or green flashing lights like I normally see from planes when I’m outside, just a single white light. Then that bright, solid, white light beamed directly forward and generated a second bright light, but the original bright light remained in place where I originally saw it. Then that second light, beamed forward exactly like the first time, and generated a third bright light, with the second light still in its original spot. This created three identical bright, solid, white lights that were lit up at the exact same time, where they remained motionless. I identified these lights at 345 degrees to the North, moving toward 350 degrees to the north. I was unable to take a picture as they were only visible for roughly 7 seconds, before I could attempt to get a picture. There is absolutely zero chance that what I saw was a plane or helicopter. The only possible explanation would be a shooting star possibly, but it was very bright and appeared closer than any shooting star I have seen before. However, there were two planes in a similar location. One traveling from the southeast to the northwest and one traveling from the southwest to the northeast.
