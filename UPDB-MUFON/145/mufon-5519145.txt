Beam of light from moon than what we guess was helicopter than what we guessed was something we could not explain but very bright and fast

around 11:30 pm eastern standard time new albany Indiana. We witnessed a single brite beam of light coming from one side of the moon. The tree line blocked the other direction so I am not sure if it went out the other side. As we looked up again around 12:30 to discuss the beam of light, again, it was gone. As I was disputing his explanation, now everyone is looking, a very bright light from nowhere, moved what seemed and arms length in distance, relatively speaking, the object was far away, than it disappeared as fast as it appeared. Now sure what to make of 3 strange events in one night within about an hours time.
