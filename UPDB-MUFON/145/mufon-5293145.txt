Object noticed in photograph taken in the vicinity of Cat Lake, British Columbia

I took a photo of an eagle over Cat Lake, near Squamish in British Columbia, Canada, on April 30th 2014. Some time later, while looking at the photo, I noticed a strange object I hadn't seen before. It appears metallic, with dark- and light-gray areas as well as strong lights on its upper half. I initially thought the "lights" were reflections from the sun above, but then I noticed that the very top is unlit. Also, the side facing the sun is dark, whereas the side opposite of the sun is lighter.
