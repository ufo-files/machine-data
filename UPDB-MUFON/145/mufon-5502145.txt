{dr}Observed dull metal disc in stationary pose above Ladd Hill Rd on Parrett Mountain, east of Newberg.

Driving South on Kramien Rd at approximately 6:45am toward Ladd Hill Rd on a clear, sunny morning, I noticed the disc hovering over the junction of Kramien and Ladd Hill roads. I drove to the junction and stopped directly under the disc and watched it for a few minutes. After occurring to me that this could be dangerous, as there were no other cars or people in the area, I turned onto Ladd Hill Road and left the area.
