{jpg} Bright flashing light that made several explosions

So me and a friend were just sitting down out back on the porch. It was about 12:40 and we were at his place in grand junction on patterson between 29 and 28 1/2 road and my friend jumped up and seen a light. I went to look and it was a huge bright light that made like an explosion but no noise. It keep moving fast all over the sky appearing and disappearing. It was to big to be a plane and it was moving way too fast. It couldn't have been a falling object because it would go back up and down. His son came out after a minute and witnessed a few "explosions of lights" with us. It kinda freaked me out but i wasn't afraid for my life or anything. It was very sudden and confused vand unclear on what it was exactly. Most positive it wasn't plane or anything i have ever seen. It was only a few miles away i believe. We lost sight of it because of some trees. My buddy tried to record but his phone didn't record.
