{jpg} UFO Streaks above Thunderbirds Demonstration at JB Andrews

While filming the first part of the Thunderbirds air demonstration in September of 2015, an object streaked from behind the clouds and through the top of the frame, making a noise as it did so. The object was not noticed until the next day when viewing the video. After getting a closer look, we saw that the object originated behind the cloud, then hops over cloud banks twice, then streaks upward and to the left above the aircraft into another cloud and beyond.

The shape went from a metallic looking egg shape in the clouds while "hopping", and then changed to what looks like a spherical object with triangular apparatus on the rear end while streaking through the air, then disappearing. At the end of the clip, you can see a point of light very quickly move from the tail of the C-5 towards the oncoming plane formation.
