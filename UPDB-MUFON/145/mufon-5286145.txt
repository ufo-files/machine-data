Unknown aircraft Not A plane

While patrolling on N Jackson street I noticed a flashing red and white light. At first I thought it was a satellite pole light for warning airplanes but I have never noticed one at that location because it was beside the elementary school. I then drove to a near by carwash to get a better view of the light . After stopping the car I noticed that it was not a satellite pole warning light because I could see a faint outline of it and that it was a triangle shape. That made me wonder what exactly I was looking at because it was not a plane or a helicopter but the same size or a little bigger. There was no noise and it was only about 500ft away . So I then got out of the car and turned on my cruiser spotlight and pointed it on the object That is when it began to move very slowly In a north direction. As I watched the object it was like a dark silver gray color there was no sound at all. It did have a whiteish yellow light that was dim on all three corners and there was a red light that was flashing in random spots on the object. the longer I watched the object the faster it seemed to go in the north direction. All I know is that when I seen the object it was not moving and when I turned on my spotlight and shinned it in that direction it began to slowly fly away then got faster . It was not a plane or helicopter. I don't know what it was.
