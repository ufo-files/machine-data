{dr}I observed 3 star like objects moving in an L formation.

I observed 3 star like objects moving in an L formation. The object at the tip of the leading part of the L moved ahead of the other 2 objects by a "short distance" before stopping and waiting for the other 2 to catch up, reformed into the L formation and then continued together for as long as I was able to observe them. To me they looked like at satellite moving across the sky at 12 o'clock high as I had to physically lift look up to see them. To my knowledge satellites don't travel in formation let alone stop in their tracks and wait for others to catch up. I have no clue what they were.
