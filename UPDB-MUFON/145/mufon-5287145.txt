Stepped outside and black sphere traveled overhead.

Stepping outside to see sun set and there was a black sphere that flew overhead in a constant and consistent manner. Possibly at around 6000 feet as I saw it pass through the lowest level clouds, though when I first saw it, it did look lower so maybe it was rising. Lowest layer of clouds was around 6000 feet. It also was traveling perpendicular to the wind direction as the wind at the time was not toward the west.

It left no trail, no lights, and made no noise. It didn't appear to be floating like a balloon, it appeared to be under its own power and was moving very quickly.

After it passed over the apartment buildings roof, it must have accelerated, because I ran to the side of the building (a distance of only 20 to 30 feet) and by that time it appeared to be on the horizon.

The distances, angles of observation, and time till I reacquired line of sight on the object didn't add up. It had to have moved faster after I lost sight of it. I believe in the last second of the video I took (once the video ends is when I started towards the side of the building) that you start to an acceleration.
