Appeared with no sound and was fast moving, disappeared then reappeared heading towards a nuclear power plant.

I was a truck driver working night shift and was just about to leave with a load. 
The object appeared from the west with no sound and very low, it headed east, i asked my colleague if he had seen it which he had and with that it appeared again heading out to the west then north towards the sellafield nuclear power plant. 
We were both speechless, we have spoke about it to others but they do not believe us, we know what we saw and it was not normal.
The object was delta shaped, it looked matt black with no sound or lights and about 20 metres in length.
As it headed north we lost sight of it.
