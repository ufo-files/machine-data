{cak} Saw black dimond shape below high clouds.Went in to get binoculars,came out and it was gone

I was trying to get weeding done before the sun hit the front of my house. My 4yr old grandson was out with me.He kept calling me to come look at bugs .I noticed the object out of the corner of my eye. I at first thought that it was a kite flying in Hotchkiss field. I then noted that it was not moving like a kite. It was just very very slowly moving up and it was not kite colored.It was black with no tail or side to side movement that the air current would cause, I was more curious that scared. I went in to get binoculars to verify what it was. It only took less than a min.When I went back out,it was gone.Then I felt a little scared.
