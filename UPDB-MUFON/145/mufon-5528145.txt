Black rectangular Vehicle with three headlights

At around 2AM my dog needed to go out. I went out to the fenced off back yard and was admiring the beautiful orange red "full moon" in back of my house I saw through the trees. I realized at this time the moon should be over my house. When I turned around there was the moon and when I turned back the orange/red "full moon" was gone. I noticed three head lights below tree top level coming through the woods at me about 200? feet away. The vehicle looked the size of a VW bug but was black and rectangular about four feet high? and 10 feet long? I was scared and ran inside to wake my then husband. He would not wake up. I looked outside standing on our deck but saw nothing.
