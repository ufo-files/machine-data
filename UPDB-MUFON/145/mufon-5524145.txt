starlike object in the sky which shimmered red,yellow,blue and green light. appeared stationary but appeared in different areas at different times

While looking at stars we noticed a bright light that appeared to shimmer and looked to be different colors with the naked eye. when observed with a pair of 10 x 50 wide angle binoculars the object was clearly shining red,yellow,blue and green light. It was stationary in the sky but each time we went out it was in a different place. we watched the object from about 9p.m. until around 1a.m. We go outside to have a cigarette as we don't smoke in our home so we would look for it each time we went out. Without a compas i can only estimate location but when we saw it was in the western,north western,northern and then again in the western sky. this was in a matter of about 4 or 5 hours and we never actually saw it moving.
