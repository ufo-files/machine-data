12 bright star like objects traveling fast in a straight line with exactly the same space between them

12 bright star like objects traveling fast in a straight line with exactly the same space between them. They were in directly straight formation and appeared very high in the sky directly straight up! One deviated from the flight pattern and made a quick turn and went back and circled the one behind it and returned to position in about 20 seconds. These 12 objects had no colored or blinking lights on them just appeared to be bright white like bright stars on the bottom. I caught these by chance while in my hot tub in the back yard and it was crystal clear completely. They came in from over the ocean from the Nw horizon and traveled exactly straight to the Se over the mountains. It took all 12 about 15 minutes to fly from one horizon to the other.
