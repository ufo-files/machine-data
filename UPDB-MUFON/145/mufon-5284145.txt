Round ball sighted

Tonight my brother spotted the same round ball of light that I previously saw last week. He took video of it. It’s far away so you have to zoom in. Also, last month I had a similar ball of light aabout the size of a tennis ball zoom over my car. It came out of nowhere and was gone as quick. I thought it was a shooting star but it was solid and bright and didn’t act like a shooting star. The orb we have both witnessed out in our yard zips through the sky and just disappears. The one I saw the week before south to north and zipped across and disappeared. The one he saw tonight came out of the west and flew over head him and turned and zipped upward and just disappeared before getting to where the stars are located. This is the best I can describe. They look like round pulsating orbs with white light. Interestingly they don’t frighten us. Years ago I stepped outside around 3 am and saw and orange one moving in a way no machine could and I dropped and crawled in the house and locked the doors. That one frightened me. We do have an army base here about 40 minutes from us.
