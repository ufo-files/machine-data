{dr}boomerang object flew over head rotating twisting 90 degees to right and back in a straight line heading south

I was getting ready to go to the work and when I was getting into my car I happened to look up. That's when I saw the craft. The craft is total white and glossy in appearance. It went silently through the sky. There was no windows or anything like that. the ship looked boomerang in shape but as it moved forward toward the south it would pivot 90 degrees to the right and then pivot back to its original position. I watched it fly over the club and over my condo I'm think the elevation was between 500-700 feet from the ground. The flight lasted for about 5-6 to six minutes. I wanted to follow it but at the rate of speed it was flying and the amount of time to get out of the parking lot the craft was long gone.
