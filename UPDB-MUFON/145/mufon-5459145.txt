{rjl} Noticed several reflective flying objects in group

I was floating in my pool staring at the sky at 2:10pm on a sunny day. I noticed an object far up in the sky that almost looked like a diamond or star but it was moving. About 1 minute later I saw a group of 2 followed by 1, the another one then 2 in a group and they would catch a reflection off the sun on their backside. The sun was behind them. I asked my husband to look in the sky. He saw them too. We live in an airport flight path and these objects were very far above the normal traffic of planes. They were headed north. I kept looking for more and there was another 2 a few minutes later. Then they were gone.
