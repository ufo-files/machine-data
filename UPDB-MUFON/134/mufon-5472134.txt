{rjl} U-shaped lights flying fast and steady

We were standing in our yard and looking at the sky at the stars (Pliedies and Orion) when we saw a U-shaped pattern of lights fly overhead. My husband said he saw a membrane after them, but all I saw was a U-shaped pattern of lights fly south by south-west and then disappear.
