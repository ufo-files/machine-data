A documented sighting of a very large UFO at a Naval Air Station

My daughter (14) was watching television when she heard a noise possibly out back of the house. She opened the back door and saw a very large UFO hovering in the sky. She told me the next day that it was in the direction of the US Navy base Johnsville Naval Air Station. This is where the astrnauts trained for space missions. I call ed the Officer Of TheDay at Johnsville and he confirmed that there was an event the night before. I called the Northampton Township police dept. and they confirmed an event that prompted approx 500 calls of a sighting. I called the local newspapers and they would not comment on this. I know that this is a long time ago, but these facts are documented.
