{jpg} I saw two objects in the west, one was triangular and the other was a single light. My son saw them after I called him and he said they were both triangular.

I went outside to put my car in the garage around 10:00 p.m.  When I walked back to the house, I saw two lights in the west. I could see that the top one was triangular and the one below it was a single light. I watched it for several minutes and then went in the house to call my con. While inside the house, my son said one of them went north (both were triangular) and the other moved east and hovered over his house. He took two pictures, but looking at it with the naked eye, it had more than three lights but only three showed up on the photo. Every once in a while a light flashed. It was still there when I went in the house after at least a half hour.
