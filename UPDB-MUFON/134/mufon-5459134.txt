{rjl} Bright oval flashing stationary light

Driving through country with trees ahead my stepson from Poland and I saw a very bright stationary white oval-looking light flashing on and off almost every five seconds, We were driving toward it. After about five minutes we were directly under it and lost sight of it. Once we pulled into our driveway and exited the car we looked up and it was completely gone. It should have been directly above us.

Three weeks later we stepped out of our house in the late evening and saw the same bright white light over the trees across the street to the southeast. My step son saw it with me. It immediately flashed off. It returned about fifteen minutes later directly south of us. It flashed on and then off and disappeared.

No sound and no movement. It simply flashes off and either reappears elsewhere in the sky or vanishes completely. Its an intensely bright light but no evidence of anything else. No sound and no movement. There are many regular jet aircraft that fly overhead from the SW to the NE on approach to Midway Chicago airport but this wasn't moving but completely stationary. I've been around aircraft and helicopters as I was in the Air Force but this behaved like none of them.

It was impossible to tell how distant it was or its altitude.

It seems to be a regular visitor to our skies so I will attempt to catch it on video if possible.
