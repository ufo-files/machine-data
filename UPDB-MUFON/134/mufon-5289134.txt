Large hovering oval shaped blue light/glow

Last week I was driving north on the taconic state parkway when I noticed to my left (west) a bright bluish purple oval shaped glow that was not coming from any source on the ground. I recorded it on my phone the best I could since I was driving. I saw for about 2 minutes and then the area was blocked by trees. Once that area of sky became visible again, the light was gone.
