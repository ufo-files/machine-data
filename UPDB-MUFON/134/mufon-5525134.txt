Orb-like UFO spotted over Golden Beach.

Two friends and I were at my house in Golden Beach, MD. We went outside to smoke a cigarette when we observed the UFO. I was unsure of what the object was at first, and came to the conclusion that it had to be some time of unidentified flying object that was under intelligent control. The object itself was a white-ish, blue-ish, orange-ish sphere/oval of light. It moved west above us, and moved very slowly. We could observe the object pulsating light and sometimes flickering. The three of us were dumbfounded upon realizing what we were seeing. The object eventually faded away in its western path.
