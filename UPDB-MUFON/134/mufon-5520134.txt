Red blinking light high up in the sky several nights in a row.

During the Christmas holidays it is my wifes & my yearly ritual to go to her sisters house for Christmas in north westen PA. They live on a farm that has been in my wifes brother-in-laws family for generations. My wifes niece & I are people of similar open minds. Shes in her early thirts where Im in my late 50s. She told me about the red blinking light that has been up in the night sky for quite some time. I went with her to view this red blinking light & sure enough it was there right outside of my sister in laws home. Its not up as high as a passenger jet but certainly higher then a helocopter would go. I thought maybe a huge radio tower although, a helocopter can certainly go higher then a radio tower. The approximate time was around 11 pm & very cold. About an hour later the object/light moved its position. I was at the same height but some distance from its original position. 

Now my wifes sister is having a gas company drill for natural gas on their property. Whether this has anything to do with the red light is anyones guess.

When I first viewed the object I was excited. I decided to see what would be there the next day during daylight. There was nothing there but clear blue sky.

The next night which would have been Christmas night the red light was back again in the same place. 

My niece lives in Mars & has told me see can see the light from her apartment. 

She also told me that there have been witnesses to orbs floating around in the past years in the same area.

The red blinking light does move to different positions but I have never witnessed it moving only after it has moved. Its is also completly silent.
