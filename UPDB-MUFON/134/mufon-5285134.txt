Large hovering craft

I observed a very large craft. First it was hiding in the clouds but as the sun began to burn off the clouds, the craft then switched to looking like a cloud. At one point the craft “realized” I was observing it, and I felt an intense conscious connection to it and I could not feel my body. As soon as I was connected to it, it began to move towards me. A very overwhelming thought that was not my own told me that I wasn’t in danger, and I felt no fear or malicious intent and almost like a drug I was calmed. I had the thought that maybe it was communicating telepathically and as soon as I thought that an eye appeared and bore into my soul and said “I see you”. Ive hesitated report this because the experience did feel highly personal and to be perfectly honest I feel like the experience was a gift. But I feel now that sharing my positive experience could help ease the stereotype of these kinds of encounters as being extremely frightening when it was in fact quite calming.
