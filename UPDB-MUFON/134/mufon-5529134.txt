Stationary, V-like objects (2) with flashing red/green/white lights

While taking my dogs outside for the last time of the night, I noticed an object in the sky that had flashing red and white lights. It was at a distance but still close enough to distinguish the different color lights. I asked my wife to come outside and see what she thought and she agreed with my assessment. Also, she noticed another similar object nearer the horizon. The direction of the objects was NE from our location. We observed the two objects, in stationary locations in the sky for more than a half an hour (they are still there as I write this). Objects look very similar in size and shape (V) and with pulsing red/green/white lights. One of the objects that appears to be higher in the sky (closer?) is in the direction of the Sequoyah Nuclear Plant. The objects/lights are definitely not from a plane and do not appear to be celestial as we (wife and myself) can find no other comparable objects in the sky. Will continue to monitor the objects as long as possible. Planes have flown through the sky (identifiable via their light patterns and their movement) and none seems to have approached or noticed the objects. Unfortunately, we do not have a telescope or a camera capable of taking night photos.
