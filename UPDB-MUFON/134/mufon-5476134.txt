{jpg} slow moving satellite looking object, sudden large flash then the object accelerated to a tremendous speed

I was walking my dogs in the backyard around 10:45 PM. I was star gazing as it was a very clear night. I observed an object moving from South to North that appeared to be a satellite. As I was observing the object it gave off a tremendous flash. Continuing in it's original straight line trajectory it began to accelerate exponentially and disappeared within a couple of seconds. I was startled and excited by the acceleration at what I assume was a very high altitude.
