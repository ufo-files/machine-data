{cak} saw 5 objects the first 3 were singles and the last 2 were together all were moving extremely fast covered the width of the sky in about 2 seconds

On the morning of the 15th of September I was star gazing with my telescope. I occasionally would stop and look around because there were many meteorites that morning. the first object I saw was moving north and south At an extremely fast rate and also was pulsating. in between pulses it was very hard to see. approximately 6 or 7 minutes later I saw another one. about 5 minutes after that I saw the third one the first three were all the same. all three were moving north to south extremely fast covering the width of the sky in approximately one and a half to two seconds. shortly after the third one maybe 2 3 4 minutes I saw the pair. the pair were moving north and south and were travelling faster than the first three but as they were moving they were moving side to side and slowing down and speeding up very quickly. about two-thirds across the sky one of them stopped and the other went so fast that it made a blinding streak of light vertically. after that I thought it was over but about 15 minutes later I saw three of them return from south to north one after the other none were together. I assume it was the same ones but I suppose it could have been different ones but I knew it was the same type of object because of the erratic way they were speeding up and slowing down.
