String of lights ascending the sky

I was at my daughters Christmas parade in town with many other people around. We were standing around waiting for the parade to begin when I noticed a strange light in the sky. I heard the parade band coming and looked in the opposite direction then back to find the light was climbing the sky in a straight line with a strand of pulsating lights. The lights continued to ascend for a couple minutes then was as though they went into a hole in the sky, the lights disappeared one by one as they came to a spot in the sky, literally looked as though it went into a hole in the sky. The parade band was getting ready to cross in front of me and I stopped the recording just before the lights disappeared. There are other’s looking at the same place in the sky and can be seen in the video looking towards where I am recording so they obviously seen it too. I told the children next to me that maybe it was Santa Claus but cannot find any explanation for what it could really be.
