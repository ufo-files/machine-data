{nd} Saw it when driving, hovered for 1 - 2 minutes then streaked very fast NE.

I was driving east I20/59 just past Bass Pro Shop in Leeds, looked up and saw a very bright, hovering, orb-like vessel that was stationary for one to two minutes. It suddenly streaked to the right NE very, very, very fast and went out of my visual range. That evening and for several nights after a family member observed low hovering but quiet black ops helicopters apparently searching for the object. I knew what it was as have seen multiple UFO's during lifetime and have had encounters, but it was still kind of scary.
