{rjl} it was a dark object that looked like an oddly shaped cloud in a completely clear sky

My father was driving on the 210-W freeway through Monrovia California when he noticed a dark object hovering in the sky. At first he thought it was a cloud so he pulled his phone to take pictures, thinking it was strange to see such a tiny cloud floating along by itself on a clear and windy day. He witnessed it for about 10-minutes during the drive, and when he went around a bend there was a hotel that blocked his view. When he came around the other side of the bend the object was just gone.

The object was just hovering there, not moving in any noticeable direction.
