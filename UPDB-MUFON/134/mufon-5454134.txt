{tjd} Triangle of White Lights and Red Strobe in Thetford VT

A large object moved from North, curving to the South-East, seemingly following the ompompanoosuc river. The object was accompanied by an engine sound so I first assumed it was a plane from the Post Mills Airport. However, after observing it move overhead I realized I couldn't see any green navigation lights or the regular strobe lights seen on an airplane.

The object consisted of three or four small white lights in the shape of a triangle. The center was a proportionally larger red light which flashed on and off with about a 1 to 2 second fade.

The object sounded unlike any prop or jet engine I've heard. Typically you hear a change in pitch change from doplar affect as the craft moves overhead, but this object seemed unchanging.
