{rjl} Stopped car on road

The item was described to be huge in size. It then, drifted off to the side of the road towards the airport, then a light from the airport connected with the Object and it is lights went out and the object disappeared. There was no other traffic on the road at the time. As soon as the object disappeared the cars GPS said they needed to make a U turn. Three people were in the car during this indecent.
