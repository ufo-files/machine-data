{dr}Near miss with airliner on approch to LAX. Dove and circled around the plane, then continued North then vanished. Very clear night

Looking into the north east sky traveling east, Airliners on approach to LAX in the sky's lined up. A object lit with orange and yellow lighting heading north in the sky towards an airliner on approach heading South West at the same altitude. I almost crashed watching what I thought was going to be a mid air collision. Just as it almost collided, the craft dove and circled under the airliner then continued north for 10 to 15 seconds then completely disappeared in a flash. Both my daughter and I witnessed this. We are aviation enthusiasts this craft was no airplane or helicopter. There were no red, green or white lights.
