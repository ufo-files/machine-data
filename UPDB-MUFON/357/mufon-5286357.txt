Six orbs in unison flight pattern

I was indoors when I got a sudden urge to go outside in the 100 degree heat and look up. I have never felt that way nor have I ever thought I saw any UFO's in my life. When I looked up from my front porch I saw six white orbs flying in slow formation heading East. I'm used to seeing planes fly and I can always see a plane's tail, wings and jet contrails. But these were orbs about the size of an asprin and white. They had no wings, no tail, no contrail, flying much slower than most air traffic I see from day to day. It gave me the chills because I had that random feeling I needed to go outside and look up, then when I did that exact thing I saw those objects. I wonder if anyone else in the area or on the path they were heading saw something similar?
