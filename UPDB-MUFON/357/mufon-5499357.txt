{nd} Bright orange light in the sky over Kelleys Island/Lake Erie

Around 12:20 AM on September 12th 2014 I was laying in bed getting ready to go to sleep when my boyfriend called to me from another room to come look out the window. I got up and went to where he was. When I looked out the window and across the lake towards Kelleys Island I saw an orange light that appeared to be mostly stationary and so bright it lit up the sky and clouds around it. At one point it faded out to nothing and less than a second later appeared higher and to the right of where it had been. After a while, maybe 5 minutes or so, it looked like it was slowly descending and then disappeared behind the island, like it was moving in a straight path to the North and out of our view. We continued to watch for a while afterwards but did not see it reappear.
