{cak} Bright light intense heat

1. Was laying in bed when I felt the house starting to shake. At first I thought it was an earthquake. A few seconds later this was followed by sounds similar to one would find on a video game as well as a bright intense light which penetrated my very thick curtains. Almost simultaneously, as whatever this was was flying by I felt like my skin was being burned. Lasted at most 2 minutes.
