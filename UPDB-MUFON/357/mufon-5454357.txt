{rjl} trail of lights from easter sky toward horizon

I woke up at about 4:45 AM and went outside for a cigarrette. My porch looks eastward toward the morning sky. I noticed a trail of very bright stars in a line, some on a parallel line dropping from about 35° toward the eastern horizon. They were very bright, a little brighter than the starlight and trailed slowly north as they dropped in a line, probably 30 or 40 of them, maybe more because I ran inside the house for my camera and I woke up my 14 year old daughter to see them as well. The came into view (seemingly popping through the atmosphere) at about 35° then headed slightly north and east toward horizon. I thought they were stars thrn noticed they were moving, not at a rapid pace. One light probably took about 20 seconds to reach the horizon. By the time my daughter came out there were only a few of them left. There was a last one that seemed to take a slightly different path, almost like it was wandering in and out of the flight path the others took. Wow! I have never seen anything like this! Ive seen lots of shooting stars burn up in the atmosphere and this was NOT that! My camera was not able to catch the, I tried but i dont think you can see any of them in the video.
