Tonight my wife and I witnessed a large bright ball of light pass over head in a NNE direction. We were sitting out on the deck enjoying the evening stars facing NE under our eve around 6:30-7pm. It appeared immediately above us from behind at the altitude of most civilian planes and military helicopters that are nearly always present (under 2000 ft). It was so bright she said wow and it caught our attention. It didn1t make any noise and moved at about 40-60 mph. I grabbed the binoculars and I could make out one large, wide oblong light with a fine line-like inner permitter, as if there was a solid object with a glow around it. There was also two vertical lines inside the perimeter connected to the bottom perimeter, but not reaching the top (see figure). No other lights were apparent. I watched it silently for a couple of seconds head into the Temecula sky area and had a hard time keeping the binoculars steady. I lowered the binoculars and as I found it in my vision again it quickly faded and went out. My wife said all she could see was a bright light which then shrunk to a red dot and blinked out. We thought we had seen yet ANOTHER UFO.
