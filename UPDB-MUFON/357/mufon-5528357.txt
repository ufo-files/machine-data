I saw a round glowing orb travel across the sky.

It was about 11:45pm on January 1st, 2011 and I was outside of my house smoking a cigarette, enjoying the clear night sky and all of a sudden an orange glowing orb appeared from behind the trees. It was travelling from West to East and seemed to have a steady speed and a fairly high altitude, (maybe about as high as a commercial airliner, I'm not sure). As it passed over me I noticed that there was no sound coming from it, at least none that I could hear. I moved to a side street to keep sight of it as it travelled. From the time I first saw it to when it disappeared into the sky it seemed to maintain a straight path. At an arms length, it looked about as big as an eraser on a pencil. It was a glowing orange orb but when it was coming toward me I noticed a slight white blinking and when it passed me and I was looking at the back of it I noticed a slight red blinking. The whole event took about five minutes as it travelled across the sky.
