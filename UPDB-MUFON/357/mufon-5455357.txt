IN 1972 WITNESS AND HIS GIRLFRIEND WERE SITTING OUTSIDE ON THE HOOD OF HIS CAR WHEN THEY SAW A SAUCER. THERE WAS A FULL MOON THAT NIGHT. THE SAUCER WAS ABOUT 400 YARDS AWAY AND ABOUT 200 FEET OFF THE GROUND. IT WAS SILVERISH AND HAD NO LIGHTS.
