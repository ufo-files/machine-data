{nd} Silent craft moving stealthily across the sky above the neighborhood

I was outside at around 2:30am Sunday morning, sitting in a chair on my lawn, my neck was craned to peer out of my skull at the dazzingly array of stars in the night sky. I make a habit out of watching the skies above my house and learning the movements of the deep cosmos at we revolve through time on this weird planet. I was sitting back with Sherlock, my tobacco pipe in my hand and I noticed slick movement against the relatively motionless backdrop. I thought it may be a comet, falling/shooting star, or whatever flashes across the skies at night, but it kept moving towards the house. I was semi-stunned because it was not normal and all I could do was point at the rectangular red lights on the bottom of what appeared to be a super stealthy rectangular tube floating silently across my neighborhood and house. The craft was not far from the ground and made absolutley no sound. It appeared to be a surviellance craft, man-made and deathly silent.
