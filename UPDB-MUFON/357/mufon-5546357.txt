VERY bright object moving first from NW to SE then changed flight path(down) and QUICKLY back to NW position where it

Went out to south porch of house to relieve myself after me and my partner got done watching a tv program. I looked up to the sky to enjoy the bright clear starlight night and as I changed my point of view from straight-up to NW view immediatly noticed a large bright star object moving from NW to SE, thinking it was possibly a satelite I called for my partner to witness what I saw, when she got from the couch to the south porch the object stopped, dropped straight down and behind a tree line. She was scared and went back inside, I was curious and walked to the south portion of the property and saw the same object moving slowly down then suddenly, in a flash, move back to the NW position, almost exactly where I first saw it. From there it seemed to change colors and move left, right and up, down for a short while before it eventualy dropped down out of sight.
