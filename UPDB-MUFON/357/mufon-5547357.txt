Dull orange coloured oblong object with wings flying fast in sky no sound.

Was outside my house in the garden with two friends watching the stars as it was a very very clear night when i noticed a dull orange oblong shaped object with wings moving very fast in the sky initially i thought it was a biplane but it was moving faster then expected of a biplane and there was no engine sound at all it looked to be fairly high up in the sky i instantly said "what the hell is that?" and my friends both saw it. it flew by for about 10-20 seconds and then disappeared.
