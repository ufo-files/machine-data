I saw a glowing, burnt orange solid orb, now glowing halation so it was lit from the inside the orb, if not the orb being the light itself

I was living in Liverpool at home, I was 16 years old, It was around bonfire night so, nothing special really to see lights in the sky.

I was watching TV in my bedroom with my back to the open, panoramic window, it was dark, around 8pm on a winter night, I was gazing frequently at the fireworks that were randomly going off now and then, I looked out and saw from my upper left corner a solid glowing orb, a sphere that made absolutely no sound, it moved in a continuous direction from my upper left from behind my house into the distance above the houses, moving steady, no jerking movements, no change in direction or elevation, the speed was very slow and continuous, maintaining a constant speed, I shouted to my parent who was i the house and kept shouting, I had time to run to him in the bathroom and he shouted at me because he'd cut himself shaving because of me, I asked him to come and look one last time, then I just ran back to my room, I had time still to see the object before it went over the next row of houses and disappeared into the horizon.

there was no halation from the object, so it wasn't glowing from the outside, there was no flickering or trail and no sound. I've seen many normal things is the sky from when I was a child, right till now, from Chinese lanterns, balloons and more that could maybe account or that I could even discredit myself, I am my own biggest critic, I have for the past 5 or 6 years had a growing interest in the phenomena, I see videos and accounts all over the internet, social media and YouTube, I look at most and think it's absolute bull, some are so obviously faked with bad CGI, camerawork or just people wanting attention. I am none of these, I just want to tell my brief story to get it out of me and into the open.
