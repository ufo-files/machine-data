{jpg} 3 objects entering atmosphere

On May the 11th or 12th between 9:00pm and 9:15pm I observed 3 objects enter the atmosphere. Each object had an atmospheric burn time of almost 2 1/2 seconds. Each objects burn was white like and identical in color. The first object came in at about a 45 degree angle, 2 and 3rd object came in one right after the other. None left a trail like a meteor. I was able to track the 3rd object after it entered the atmosphere because it blocked a star—which means it was still traveling in our atmosphere. There were no Lights on them. I live in Goodyear Arizona and am only a short distance from Luke AFB. I contacted them but got no return answer. I could not have been the only one to see this. At any rate there should have been a radar hit from Luke or the Phoenix airport. I have not read or heard anything about them at all. These are very small windows of time and date to check on and verify. Would you please find out if anyone else saw this or if there were radar hits? The only other thing might be due to Space Force vehicles, especially so close to Luke AFB. Please let me know if you find any answers.
