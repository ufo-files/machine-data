Three Ufos seen over North Phoenix during daytime

My partner and I were swimming in the pool at approximiately 4PM on June 3rd, 2012. The temperature was about 103 degrees and there were very few clouds in a very blue sky.  I was looking up at the sky and saw a bright orb travelling about 1/3 the speed of a commercial jet liner. I pointed out the orb to my partner who also observed the incident. The orb was travelling north. Two more orbs appeared one to the left and one to the right of the first orb. The first orb continued in the same trajectory at the same speed but the other two orbs pulsed and flashed on and off and seemed to go around the first orb. The two flashing orbs which moved about twice as fast as the first orb, seemed to cross very closely to one another and then continued to move ahead of the slower travelling first orb. Both continued to blink on and off until they disappeard. The first orb continued at the same velocity until it was out of sight below the tree line. I felt very excited and invigorated after the sighting which compelled me to look on youtube where I was able to witness several videos made in the last week or so with very similar objects pictured in New Mexico, Vancouver, Peoria, Arizona and California. I believe the objects that we saw were the same or similar as the recent sightings that I watched on youtube.
