Over 25 bright lights, all moving in a westward direction.

I take the dog out every morning before work. I generally look at the sky to see if it is cloudy, etc. This morning when I looked up, I immediately saw 2 lights, at first I thought they were jets, heading west/southwest in a parallel formation. After watching, I saw many others, all headed the same direction. I thought that to be odd due to the fact that most jets don't normally fly in the same flight path. I tried counting them and stopped at around 25 lights. There was no noise, there were a few clouds that I could see. I did not loose site of these objects, I just took the dog back in and got ready for work. Surely Ft. Campbell ( KY) or the Nashville airport would have picked this up on radar I thought. I don't believe I have ever seen this sort of thing in my entire life.
