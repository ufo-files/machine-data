{dr}Object in the clouds

I was on vacation in Montana in June 2014. I took some photos with my iphone at Spring Meadow Lake State Park in Helena, MT. I then downloaded them to my computer. I was looking at the pictures recently on my computer and I noticed a black dot in the middle of the clouds in the one horizontial photo. When I zoomed in it looked like a oval shaped object. The wierd thing is that I have another photo that I took veritcally directly after taking the first one and that black object is not in the photo. I also have some more photos of the area before and after and I don't see any obects in the sky. While I was actually there on vacation, I didn't notice the object. At the time I did not notice this object; I just noticed it today when I was deleating old photos on my computer.
