{nd} Gourd shaped ufo over Denver

Approximately 4pm Saturday 8/2/14 sitting on patio looked up into sky saw gourd shaped white object flying thru sky from SE heading to NW no sound, no blinking lights, just at a fast speed. Definitely not shape of usual airplane. Followed it with my eyes until it just disappeared. Then about 15-30 seconds later saw what looked like military jets flying in same direction as ufo. I began to wonder if it was a Drone?
