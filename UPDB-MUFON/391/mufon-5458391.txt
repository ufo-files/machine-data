{rjl} I first thought it was a plane with Dulles airport about an hour and a half away.

I saw a flashing light like you would normally see on planes in the sky at night. So I figured it was just another plane, same as usual with Dullas International Airport only about an hour and a half from me. Give or take depending on traffic. But I was surprised to the speed of this thing when I saw it fly down from above the tree's to hover about ten feet in front of me like it was checking me out. It was a deep dark grey. Not quite black, around 10 feet around with flashing lights on the bottom and a dome on top. I was outside having me a cigar. We don't smoke inside. This thing hovered there for a good minute and a half, maybe longer before it rose up and slowly hovered over the house. The entire time still looking at me, I could tell because it did have some kind of little square on the dome that was always pointed at me. I was assuming it may have been a window, making the beings inside tiny. Once it got on the other side of the house, it tore out of here so fast. I have never saw anything go so fast in my life. To go from just creeping along to that fast in a matter of seconds is unheard of in todays military forces. I wish I would have had a camera or my phone with me. Sorry guys.
