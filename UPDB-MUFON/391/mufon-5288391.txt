Slow moving object

We first saw the object moving over a farmers field while driving then stopped at our friends house and stayed outside to watch it as it slowly came closer moving as slow as 30mph. It had an almost pyramid shape to it with 3 lights 1 on each corner and the center seemed to almost absorb the light around it. After it flew almost directly over us it seemed to continue on its path towards either a
Akron/Canton Airport or Belden Village mall at this point we had run inside to get our friend to come out and see it, but by the time we had come back outside it was no longer in view. We attempted to contact local authorities about the object and received no answers and that no other people had reported seeing the object. Honestly it was very hard to judge the size of the object or its altitude due to the fact that we mainly saw it over a field, but it was either very large or flying very low, and I think it was flying low because of how fast it seemed to move and the amount of time we had been able to see it.
