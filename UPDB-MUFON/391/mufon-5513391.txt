UFO over Winnipeg, MB New Years Eve, 11:57pm.

I was watching a movie and noticed from the corner of my bright lights out my front window, but at first I thought it was New Years Eve fireworks. After I noticed the same light in the corner of my eye three times I got up to see what it was. It was not firework. It was approx. five glowing orange cicles. About the same color as street lights, a bit darker. I noticed the lights where moving N.E. out of my view from my front window so I went to my kitchen in the back of my house. I aquired the lights still moving N.E while forming a triangle and circling around each other but still moving. I started to lose sight of the lights so I went upstair to see if I could still see. I saw one light upstairs as it disappeared out of view. Thats what I saw at 11:57 pm New Years Eve. Also wanted you to know this is my first sighting of an object moving in the sky.
