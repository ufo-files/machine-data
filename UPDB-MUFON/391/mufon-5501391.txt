{dr}Four yellow/orange lights,no noise flying west to east singly in four minute intervals same path

At approx. 11:00 PM (7-4-14) over North New Jersey I spotted a yellow/orange light traveling from West to East. It made no noise and there were no blinking lights common to airplanes. It seemed to be traveling faster than airplanes since I see airplanes overhead often. Approx. three to four minutes later another similiar object appeared folowing the same path. Then another and another at about three to four minute intervals. A total of four similar objects flying alone in the same path. There was a unusual lack of air traffic at the time considering I live near three major airports. I have never seen lights like this, so I don't think these were airplane.
