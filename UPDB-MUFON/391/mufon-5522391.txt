Black triangle with 3 faint orange lights

I was outside smoking a cigarette Sunday evening when a large black triangle passed directly overhead. It was heading east/southeast across Tempe and Mesa. I was already looking up at the stars in the sky, otherwise i may have never noticed it. The craft made no sound and had 3 large orange lights on the bottom, one in each corner. The lights were faint, but took up much of the surface area of the craft. They didn't appear to actually "emit" light. Rather, they were just "there", if that makes any sense. I didn't see were the object came from, but i watched it traveling across mesa until i could no longer see it beyond the horizon. I couldnt have been the only one to see this since it was moving over such a highly populated area. Unfortunately, my cell phone was charging inside the house so i didn't get any photos or video. But, honestly, it all happened so fast, i don't think i could have recorded anything anyways. Hopefully there are others out there who saw this object too.
