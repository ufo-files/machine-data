{cak} Bright Gold Light in sky above ocean in Ocean City Maryland August 12, 2012 approximately 10pm

At around 10pm on the evening on August 12th, I notices a large golden light up in the sky above the ocean in Ocean city Md. The object was larger then a plane and almost looked like a star but was golden in color. at one point, it looked like a triangle but the majority of the time, it looked like lights. The object came down in in a "J" sort of shape like it was coming towards me and stopped. It shaked a bit back and forth. I did not stay long enough to see what happened after that because I was with a friend, who was not so interested. It definitely was not a plane or helicopter.
