{cak} Bright oval-like object came and hovered around me for some moments

I was walking back to home from my friend about ten years ago. It was around 9pm and I was about to call my mother for saying I'm sorry for staying out without telling her. Suddenly a bright light caught my attention, I looked up to the sky and an oval-like object which looked like pure light with smooth surface was charging towards me very fast. I didn't dare to move and when it was around ten meters from me, it slowed and came slowly close to me. It hovered and circled around me for a bit over half minute. It was about thirty to forty centimetres away from me and light from this light-like smooth object felt cold. I raised my hand and intended to touch it but when my hand almost reached it's surface, I heard this very high pitch voice I couldn't bear. The voice lasted for a couple of seconds before the object launched back to the sky, disappearing from sight in two seconds.
