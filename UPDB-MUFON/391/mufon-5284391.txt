Object with 3 orbs beside it forming a triangle at 3 am outside my window

At 3 am I was awake and noticed a stationary object outside my window. Normally a helicopter moves up or down, so I noticed it was strange. I looked out and saw a large object hovering outside my window over the roof of my adjacent building. It just sat there. No sound. 3 other small orb circles appeared to the right in a triangle form. The large object then scattered back and forth, to the left and the right, for several minutes. Eventually it slowly moved downward and out of my sight behind the building.
