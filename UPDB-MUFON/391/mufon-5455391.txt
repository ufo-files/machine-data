{cak} Translucent circular, slow moving, only front portion

Was in hot tub on back patio up against house.
Windless, clear night with some very light wispy clouds.
Noticed what I first thought to be a large plastic bag floating high in the sky, SSE to NNW, then thought it might have been a flock of geese.
Both of these thoughts were found to be bogus, a bag would have fluttered in shape and I've never seen geese fly in a perfect rounded formation for 15 seconds or more.
The shape did not change and continued on path until it faded out of sight.
It took about 15 seconds to go from straight overhead (where I was able to first see it), until it faded as it went north.
It looked like the leading edge of a perfect circle, almost like the wispy clouds in color and texture, but moved like it was just flying over me, very straight and never changing shape. 
I stood up as soon as I saw it and I could never see the other parts of the object, just the translucent leading edge.
Impossible to tell how high or how big it was but was about the size of a baseball held at arms length.
