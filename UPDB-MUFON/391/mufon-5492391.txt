A CIGAR SHAPED OBJECT DECENDING AT ABOUT 30 DEGREES.

IWAS DRIVING HGHY103 FROM HALIFAX TO HUBBARDS. AROUND FIVE ISLAND LAKE ON MY LEFT I SAW A CIGAR SHAPED OBJECTI THOUGHT WAS THE SIZE OF A TRACTOR TRAILER WITH FLAMES OF GREEN RED AND BLUE WITH BLACK SMOKE.ST.MARYS UNIVERSITY OBSERVATORY SAID IT WAS A FIREBALL AND IT WAS 60 MILES OF THE COAST OF NOVA SCOTIA GO FIGURE.
