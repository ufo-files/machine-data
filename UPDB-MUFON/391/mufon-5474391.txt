Three lights flying close to eachother

I was leaving work and stepped out the building, as I was walking to my car I noticed 3 bright lights in the sky. I quickly got my camera out, as at first it appeared as though the lights were stationary, and too bright to be stars. As I observed for a few seconds I could see them moving, as you can see in relation to the star in the background. They appear to follow each other, would could easily be 3 planes on a flight path. The fact that they were so close to each other, and initially appeared yo be hovering made me question the 3 planes theory. I filmed it for a little bit until they dissapeared out of my view. I was unsure, so didnt do anything about it, but thought its worth a second opinion.
