{jpg} Was not seen during taping of a rainbow above the sun when it's 90 degree hot on Thanksgiving day.

I was walking the dogs with my kids and noticed an unusual rainbow above the sun. Upon reviewing the clip two days later, I noticed this bright blue globe which I had no idea it was there at the time. About exactly a month later, I decided to videotape the sky randomly and experienced the same thing. Last month, it was more clear and moved more slowly. This time, it appeared abruptly and seemed to move a lot faster. I believe it's the same thing but with different behavior and can't be seen with my naked eyes.
