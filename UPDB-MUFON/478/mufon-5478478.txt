Saw 5 birdlike objects appear flying downwards in formation then flipping changing direction and flying off diagonally upwards and away out of sight!

On the way home from Mums with my daughter in the car I noticed 5 birdlike objects drop from the sky and turn showing the underside which was a shining silver before they flew off diagonally upwards towards the treetops. It was beautiful, precise and unearthly. I said to my daughter, "Woah what was that?". She answered a second later, "Yeah what was that?" I said, "I thought it was birds at first". She answered,"Aliens". We laughed. Later I felt excited but a little unnerved!
