{rjl} Heading South On Cotton Lane Saw three pair of lights staggered in the sky.

My wife and I were driving back from the grocery store (Cotton Lane and Greenway Road in Surprise, AZ) at roughly 8pm. I saw three sets of two lights that seemed to be just "hanging" or "suspended in the air south/southwest. It almost seemed as if I was staring at the amber roof lights of a construction vehicle that was extremely bright and very high on the side of a mountain. But the catch with all of that is, the direction I was looking had no mountains in it. That's what got my mind going. I noticed the lights immediately but didnt think anything of it at first. Then about a mile and a half down the road my wife saw it too then I started to wonder what was going on. As we drove the first set of lights just seemed to be shut off with a switch and then they came back on one at a time slowly. They hung in the sky for about 7 more minutes while we drove until one by one they eventually disappeared into the night sky. The reason why this was so late is because I am military and have been away from home for a year and finally got around to telling someone. This video on youtube [Link moved to FI section/cms/tg] I found is EXACTLY what I saw but it looks to be filmed from a different angle and there were two more sets of these that were disappearing and reappearing.
