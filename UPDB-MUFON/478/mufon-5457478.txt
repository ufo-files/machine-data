Multiple ufo’s in v-shape over Sousse

Multiple ufo’s flying incredibly fast across the nightsky in Sousse, Tunisia in 98. They were not airplanes, no way. I was 13, but I remember it very well. My family were there as well, but only I saw them because it happened very fast. They flew way faster than anything i can explain.
