{rjl} Strange metallic type orb seen crossing road in headlights of car.

Was driving car on Mosherville Road near Stone co gravel company. Heading west and a strange metallic orb small crossed road in my headlights. Was about 30-50ft away about 5ft above ground. Straight path and went directly over road and entered gravel pit property. Only reason I observed it was headlights reflected off its surface. Observed same type of orb on same road in November 2017 near same location.
Dont know what these orbs are but were only seen briefly and only because headlights were shining on it directly. Both times heading south directly over gravel pit property.

in case anyone else reports similar orbs in the area.
