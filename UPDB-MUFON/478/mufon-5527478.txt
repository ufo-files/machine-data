3 dim lights flying low and fast together traveling south across the sky. no sound or flashing lights. flying side by side

I went outside on the deck to finish my half-cigarette with my coffee. I was looking at the stars because they were so visible and the night sky was very clear. then, from the right side, i noticed three small lights moving together in a row. they looked like they were flying about as high as a small private airplane but at a faster rate of speed. As i watched them go by, i couldn't make them out to be a plane, shooting stars, a satellite, or anything that looked familiar. There were no flashing lights and they didn't block out any stars when they went by so i know they weren't part of one solid object. They looked like dim stars flying together. There was also no sound whatsoever. A very clear and quiet night (sunday around 9:50pm EST). The orbs were somewhat close to each other, equally spaced out, and flying in a line from north to south across the sky. the sighting lasted for about 7 seconds before the trees blocked my view. I was not drinking. I was not high. After the lights were out of sight, i went inside and told my family "I'm pretty sure i just saw a UFO or three," and explained what i saw. Of course they had to dismiss it as a plane or shooting star...
