{dr}Floating Orb near Midway Airport

Observed and Orange Orb flying fast from south to north then slow down and turn West then speed back up. The turn was slower than the straight line speed but still at an above normal speed. Gauged speed by noticing regular passenger jets from Midway and flying overhead in a east west or west east direction. The Orb flew the distance of the visible horizon within seconds. I saw the object while I was throwing out trash at work. I work mid nights and saw it around 3:00am. I just looked up at sky and noticed it. Pure chance. So I watched it until I couldn't see it anymore because of the distance which was about 15 seconds because it was cruising fast!
