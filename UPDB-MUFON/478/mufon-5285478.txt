Sporadic distances between each object but in a basic straight line

Observed about 25 objects all traveling in same direction. I think I came in the middle of this. First few I saw were closer together than those that followed. When last few were observed there was a few seconds of blank sky between them. The first few had some slight deviation in flight path suggesting they were not on an identical flight path. Hard to tell that with the stragglers. All were orangish yellow and seemed to be flickering a bit. There was no sound. Since they flew directly overhead, I had trouble keeping the iPhone steady until I laid down on my back. Some of the video was normal focal length; then I zoomed in. Objects did not seem at all like the video of SpaceX satellites, so I decided to report this anyway just in case, and I could not find out if the satellites emitted such a vibrant color, which further encouraged me to report. I remained “looking” for 20 minutes after the final object before coming in to do the report.
