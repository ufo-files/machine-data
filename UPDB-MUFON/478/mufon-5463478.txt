{cak} Triangle shaped craft with a cube top hovered then flew off

1. I was in my car, driving to work.
2. Two bright lights, which looked like an airplane coming down to land, caught my eye.
3. First, I thought it was a plane. Then, I thought it was a blimp. The object became clearer the closer I got. It was triangle shaped with a cube top. The cube had 4 bright white lights on the front and the bottom was covered in green lights with red lights in the center and at each point.
4. The object hovered stationary. It looked like a cube with 4 bright lights coming off it. I could only barely see the outline of the craft because it was so dark. It turned quickly and i could see the triangle bottom and all the bright lights.
5. I was shocked and wanted to take pictures but I was hesitant. I did get 2(bad) pictures.
6. It quickly turned east and sped off.
