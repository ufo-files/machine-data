{rjl} See through Cigar

I was on my back deck with company laying down watching the Stars. We saw some shooting stars. There was low clouds that were moving with a light breeze. We then see a cigar shaped craft fly through the clouds. There was no noise at all. The cigar shaped object looking to blend in with the clouds. It moved in a straight line North. I told my company and two other people witnessed object move through the clouds.
