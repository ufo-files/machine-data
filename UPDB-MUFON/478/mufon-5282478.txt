I saw at least 6-10 UFOs grouped together then fly off almost immediately after I made eye contact.

I walked outside onto my back porch heading over to my brothers house. After I closed the door i took a few steps & this cluster of stars just barely caught my attention out of the corner of my eye. It was a cloudy night & not alot of stars were out, so they were kind of easy to see. I thought nothing of it at first just a bunch of stars. I then got that eerie feeling on the back of my neck like I was being watched which is what ultimately made me turn to look. I then thought to myself that those stars look oddly grouped together just barely hovering over the roof & above the tree tops. Then before I even knew what I was looking at all but one zoomed off in crazy curved like directions. They flew away in almost a blink of an eye. It was almost like they disappeared into thin air. The one that got left behind slowly floated away into the moonlight & into the clouds. 

You could see the lights on the bottom of the crafts which made them look like stars. The way they flew away made my heart sink into my chest & especially seeing the one left behind just floating away. I got a perfect view of the one floating away in the moonlight. It was a silver sphere that glowed in the moonlight & I could see the dome on top & the metal structure underneath. It looked like they were trying to repair the lone craft left behind before I scared them away. There were a group of 6-10 of them. This hasn't been my only sighting of uap's.
