{tjd} Flashing lights bring on blue cigar shapped object

Went outside at 2:25 am to smoke a cigeratte on my pourch. I was looking above the mountain at the sky. When i seen a flash of light ontop of the trees. It was around 30 feet wide and 10 feet long. The color of the light was bright white and was shaped perfectly rectangular. Then 1 minute later it flashed the same size again in the same spot. I stood there for 2 more minutes but had to rush inside to use the bathroom. Came back outside 2 minutes later and was looking in the same spot. Then all at once a blue cigar shaped object streak across the sky for about 1 second or 2. which was above the lights i had seen earlier. It moved so fast it left a a streak behind it like a shooting star would.The blue color of it was bright blue to brighter blue.
