{dr}3 Unknown lights going in a half circle counterclockwise direction

I was on a smoke break across the street from where I work when I noticed a faint moving light. I first thought that it was a spotlight for a new store, but, as I looked around I then saw that it was overhead, and since the lights were over a cloud cover I could not make out any shape. (The lights seemed liked a light you make with a flashlight) As I watched them I saw that there were 3 lights which moved from the left to the right in a counterclockwise direction making a sort of half circle. The light on the left moved fast to the one on the right as they went around. When I was ready to cross the street I noticed that the lights were still overhead as I was going up the road like they were following me. The lights then went over the building as I went inside. I was curious as to what they were and thought it odd since I never saw anything unusual before and never heard of anything in our area. When I went back to work I told my co-worker that I wish she was with me to see it and to take a picture.
