Two lights appearing to be linked diagonally were moving in tandem

My friend and I were driving West between Concho, AZ and St. Johns, AZ. About halfway between, We stopped so my friend could pee. While stopped, we noticed 2 lights to the south moving parallel to the road, seeming to move together as if they were connected by a bar that appeared to be diagonal to the horizon. One light at each end. No blinking lights or anything that resembled an airplane. There was no sound. It seemed to be moving slowly. As it moved along side us, it would move forward then suddenly dart backward, both lights maintaining the same distance from each other diagonally. It kept doing this over and over. It would dart forward then dart backward about half the distance it had moved forward. It seemed to be very big, although how big, I couldnt say. It also seemed to be very low. It was right on top of the horizon from our perspective. It moved past us a little so we got back in the car and drove with it. It stayed along side us for probably about a minute while we drove then suddenly darted across the road in front of us and sped away so fast that within about a second it was no longer visible.
