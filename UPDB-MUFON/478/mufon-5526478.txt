Triangular formation, multiple flashing lights.

I was in Birmingham on vacation with my family, and suddenly I saw a triangle of multiple flashing lights, it looked to be heading North, but it sort of just slid across the sky, it didn't look to be flying, just sort of slowly gliding. I told my Wife to look but she couldn't see it. I had looked away to tell her to look more carefully, but when i looked back I couldn't see it anymore. I think I might have been seeing things, but I figure it's worth reporting. I've attached a picture of what I remember seeing.
