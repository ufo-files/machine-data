{dr}Bright white pulsating light that first drifted then stay suspended

I work on Main St. in West Orange, NJ. Just one hour ago I exited one of the buildings at my workplace and saw what I thought was a white plastic supermarket bag drifting along high up in the sky. I looked up at it because I knew it was not an aircraft, as it initially seemed to drift along. It looked like the wind was slowly taking it. I waited to see if it would drop because there is no wind today and thought it was riding on a high wind current, like hawks do. It then continued to steadily move and remained high in the sky. It was a white light that continuously turned on and off the entire time. I managed to round up 3 fellow employees and we all continued to watch it. I was thrilled and excited that I was able to get 3 witnesses to watch it with me in the courtyard at my workplace. We walked further backwards because it was being blocked by the building rooftop, so it apparently was moving further Southeast. Once we did this, it either drifted further away or disappeared as we could not see it anymore.
