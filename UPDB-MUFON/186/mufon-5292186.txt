Unknown looking craft suddenly appeared, then flew west

Sitting down after setting food out for outdoor critters. All of a sudden, dead center in the middle of my view of a clear blue sky, the craft was just there. Just appearing out of no where, no visible entrance into my frame of vision. Which caught my attention so I started filming as it flew a straight path west. Over time I’ve caught it a few times flying the same path in the same general time frame so I was under the impression it had a daily route. It appears white-ish with a dominant dark part down its center, dark on its top and bottom. Sometimes I think I see 2 small lights on the top of its sides.
