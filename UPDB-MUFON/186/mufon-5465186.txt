{rjl} on hill, in traffic. looked se and thee. it was checked mobile radio and two of my techs were seeing same.

Sitting in traffic on big hill on rte 55 east. Looked over to field side, driver side of window and saw this ship.i knew right away it wasnt from earth. It just sat there. Never moved. I was happy to be able to witness something so strange and so very large! I was also able to see it was grey, dull metallic with many what looked like bolts going down its side
These bolts were very prominent and large. 
I was on call so had my mobile radio on and heard two of my guys seeing the same thing as me. They were saying how big it was. The man working in the xbox said it was directly over him. I dont know where other man was, but he had a visual also. So i was glad two others saw it also. I am sure as traffic moved slowly on others in line probably saw it too. The man who it was hovering over said to his partner..."im getting out of here!" 
I lost sight of it when traffic moved on.
