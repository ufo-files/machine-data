me my 2 children and my nephew were traveling north to orlando fl.from ft.lauderdale on the fla turnpike going back to get xmas presents that we had forgotten bring with us. my 13 yr old daughter was in the passenger seat next to me and pointed out a bright light that was in the sky arching down toward the car. I didn't see it till it came up behind us and i saw it in my rearview mirror. it then flew to the passenger side of the car and we watched it land in a pasture by a stand of trees. I slowed down and pulled off the road. the thing landed and was so bright it lit up the landing area and trees with a glowing white-blue light. I was going to get out of the car and walk towards it but my daughter would not let me leave them. I could see a disk shape in the light and I know it was not an airplane. I agreed with her and got back on the turnpike. when i drove off the thing was still there. no other cars came by wile i was there.
