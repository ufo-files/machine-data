{dr}Bright reflective light or surface that then shrunk and then disappeared.

It was about 5:20pm and I was walking back to my house from the neighbors across the street, when I was looking in the direction of my house and deck on the right side, when this very bright reflective light caught my attention. At first I thought it was a plane simply reflecting the sun that was in the west.I noticed that it was very bright and in a circular shape with no wings or sound. Within about three seconds it shrunk down in size to a now reflective/bright dot. In about 2 seconds it just disappeared. I couldnt believe what I just witnessed.I ran inside and grabbed the binoculars to see if I could see it trailing away and saw absolutely nothing in the sky, in the entire area.
