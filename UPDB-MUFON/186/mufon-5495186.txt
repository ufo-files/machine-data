{rjl} As my husband was station in Fort Riley, Kansas one night while going out for the Air I notice 5 lights in the shape of a triangle. There was no sound it just hovered then took off heading southeast until out of my view.

My husband was station in Fort Riley Kansas and at the time we lived off post in Junction City. I went outside for air when I notice some lights ascending as if took off from the post Air field traveling southeast. There were five lights in the shape of triangle. The lights seem to be on the lining around this object but I could not see the center of the object. The lights were apparent but center seem to be almost transparent. It was in my view for a little over a minute until I could no longer view it over the trees. My husband pulled up minutes afterwards and I was so freaked out and didn't want him to leave for work the next day. My husband explain that could have been night military drills but I knew that it was not military. At the time I was in disbelief with what I had witness and was told not to discuss this with anyone by a family that was also military. I now know that lights were reported in Phoenix on the same night but not in Kansas.
