Red, rapidly moving light moving across the sky

I was possessed to sit outside in the cool night air, something I never do alone, and was staring up at the stars. To the north there was a spotlight shining into the sky, possibly from a nearby amusement park. I was trying to pick out the various constellations when I saw a very strange red orb move across the sky from south to north, directly into the constellation I had just identified: Cassiopeia. The light was orangey red, and seemed to have a kind of hazy aura to it. It moved very, very quickly. There was no tail or pulsation and it didn’t dim - just disappeared when it reached the constellation. I was overcome with emotion - almost a kind of sadness and grief. And I thought “well, it’s been a long time since I’ve seen a shooting star. Maybe I’m confused.” And within a few minutes, I saw a shooting star to the NE, which was white and obviously very different from the red object I had just witnessed. I know it’s odd, but I somewhat feel like someone was trying to prove a point.
