Two white cigar shaped craft observed at high altitude (30-40,000 Ft) travelling side by side movin from East to West. No sound and no wings.

I was outside on the deck with my family at dusk when my wife noted, "look at those planes" to my daughter. I moved to see them expecting to see military planes which fly over often from nearby Fort Carson and instead saw two cigar shaped craft with no wings flying side by side. We all watched them fly from East to West over the maontains at a very high altitude over the clouds. They flew in the same manner and appeared to be the same size as two 747's cruising at 30-40,000 Ft - except they lacked wings. They made absolutely no sound. Even when jets cruise over at 35,000 ft, you can hear them out here. We watched for about 45 seconds as they disappeared over the mountains. It fely very strange as they did not appear like any planes we had ever seen before.
