TV News channel captures disc flying over Hogback Mountain

I was watching the local (Greenville/Spartenburg South Carolina) 7 news around 12:10pm on 5/16/2020, the shot changed to the camera setup to lookout over Hogback Mountain in Spartanburg county. It was a live shot, and a red, glowing, disc shot across the screen. I rewound, and took video and photos of what was shown. I immediately sent a copy of the footage to the news channel and they got back to me saying they hadn't noticed it, but thought it was exciting.
