{dr}Observed while waiting at a red light

The weather was mostly overcast with breaks in the clouds. I was looking at the sky directly east when I saw a metallic object come from behind a cloud into blue sky then was obscured by a cloud again. I waited for it to appear again in the clear next to that cloud but didn't appear again. The first thing I noticed was, it seemed to be moving much faster than a plane. I walk out in the country a lot and I've seen jets flying out of Midway 45 miles away, small planes flying overhead and in the distance. It seemed like it moved so quickly and gracefully that I actually believed I was lucky to see it. It was silver maybe because the sun was reflecting off of it. It was above the clouds so I couldn't see a shape other than it was a solid object and it didn't look very big from where I was, but it was really fast. I was facing east and this thing traveled from my left to my right.
