A bright light flying over Des Moines in the early morning on June 4th. 2012.

My brother saw the news story about the UFO report from Mitchelville Iowa and remembered that he had seen a strange light a day or 2 earlier and took a picture with his cellphone. He was leaving for work when he saw it. He noticed the bright light and the movement. He didn't know but living near an airport we see a lot of aircraft at all hours. This was way too big and was pure white and too fast and quiet. An aircraft that big would have been slower and loud and the marker and warning lights would have been visible. It was just a white orb, really bright and it seemed round. He was confused as to what he saw. He has never been a believer in UFOs or life on other planets. He certainly didn't believe he would ever see such a thing but there it was. And he had the presents of mind to take a picture before it disappeared behind the horizon line.
