{rjl} Flying Saucer out for the meteor shower

In August of 1993, there was to be a huge meteor shower. My sibling and I were watching for meteors. He was standing on back of the deck looking over the house and yelled for me to come look as he sees a UFO. Ofcourse, I did not believe him but went and stood up on the seats at the back of the deck. I felt in aw and scared at the same time. It was a silver saucer just hovering a little beyond the house just over the treeline. The entire bottom had blue lights and I could see what I can only describe as rivets on the side of the UFO. It did not make any noise or movement. We watched it for a few minutes then ran inside for fear that they were watching us. We went back outside a short time later, maybe a couple minutes and it was gone. My sibling does not recall this encounter. I recall going to school the next day or so and talking to classmates to find my sibling and I were not the only ones who saw it. The classmates claimed seeing it 1 town over about 3hrs before our encounter.
