I was visiting my aunt and we were sitting on the back deck when she spotted odd uniformed glowing objects moving in the sky. When first viewing this, I dismissed it as birds but at a second glance it became apparent this was odd. There was a single light that emanated from these anomalies, and they were randomly "dancing in the sky", at what seemed treetop level and slightly higher; circling, moving in all directions in a very close pattern. Other objects of the same caliber would join in appearing and reappearing. They were transparent, resembling the characteristic makeup of a jellyfish. I was able to observe one close enough to see it did have a transparent structure outside of the light which was predominant. Internally it appeared to have channeled,blackish vein-like structures that I can only say looked like a gas. As a former Marine having served in intelligence, I am familiar with strategic moving vehicles, however, there was nothing I could see or imagine threatening in these close aerial movements. The objects simply disappeared before our eyes one,two,three at a time, maintaining the same close pattern with a few more anomalies joining late. The fantastic event lasted for about half an hour, only three or four more materialized in a fifteen minute time frame. I called the local police department around three O'clock to find out if there were any reports, they had received none at that time.
