(GS) Large starlike orb, bright orange/yellow in color, slowly moving N to SW

At approximately 7:15 p.m., November 14, I stepped outside our back door to see what the weather was like. I started to return inside when I noticed a large star-like, bright orange/yellow "orb" traveling N to SW. Observation only lasted about 5 seconds. It went behind a tree but I could see it as it passed between the bare branches. Then all of a sudden it disappeared, just as if a light switch was turned off. I kept watching but it never reappeared.  There was no sound.
