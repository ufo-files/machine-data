{jpg} A Star Craft appears in the West, Brightens, Changes Direction and Moves Northward

At 9:20PM a friend and I observe a star like craft in the western sky. I observed it appearing first then I exclaimed to my friend to "look up". We both observed this star craft move slowly and arc across the sky towards the north. It moved slowly at first even slowing down and/or stopping while hovering in the sky. It almost appeared to dance a bit in the fixed position but then would stop and begin moving again slowly towards the north of the "Ghost Ranch" outside of Abiquiu. It seemed to go almost due north and set between two other star crafts in the sky looking like three crafts in a row. The three stars hung in the sky but eventually dimmed out completely and couldn't be seen any longer.
