Flashes in the sky

At midnight on the 20th of Sept I saw a bright flash. about 60 Deg. North West of my position I kept looking in that direction and about 9 seconds later I saw some very tiny flashes in that same area and then a long plasma looking thing flashed across the area going from East to West about 2 Deg. long that lasted a millisecond about as fast, as a short blobby fat plasma looking lightning.
