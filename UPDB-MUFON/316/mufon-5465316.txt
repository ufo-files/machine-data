Star gazing and saw star like light blink twice brightly then once more but duller all in a stationery position

Was outside looking for satellites as l often do when I noticed a bright star like light that was near other stars that was brighter than the stars nearby that then disappeared and then reappeared about half a second later then disappeared again then reappeared for the 3rd time only noticeably duller. It then disappeared and I continued to look in the same spot for about 3 minutes with no other sighting. There were no clouds in the area and I know that the object was stationary to my naked eye. I looked around the area of the sighting for plane lights or satellites and none were visible. I can't explain what I saw and my first thought was i just saw a UFO.
