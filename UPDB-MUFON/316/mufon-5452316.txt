Space Craft coming out of Warp drive

As I stepped into my backyard on this night and looked to the south I observed what at first I thought was an explosion in space. The problem was that it did not dissipate, instead it formed a ring and in the center was a small light that looked like i was looking through this ring and seeing a star on the other side, this was wrong, after approximately 6 to 8 seconds the ring collapsed down to just the point of light, after about 2 seconds this point of light started heading east, then after 2 seconds the brightness more than doubled for 2 seconds then went back to its original brightness, and continued heading east until I could no longer see it.
