{dr}Orange/Red colored light over Linwood, Michigan

While at Driving north on Huron Road towards Linwood from Bay City just before first stop light in Linwood about 3 miles before Marathon on the left above the Power lines and Trees about 500 feet in the air was a large orange light hovering above the houses. I slowed down and watched it go about 300 feet higher in a matter of a second. I continued North about two or three miles turned around and went back as I came back up to the stop light on my way back towards Bay City (south) the orange/red light had almost neared the stop light heading North in my direction and as I neared it, it veered right (East) out in to the direction of the Bay over a large field and trees then it was gone from my site. The whole incident took place in a matter of 5 minutes at 3:17 am. The orange light did not blink or falter and moved with a smooth fluid motion, not erratic in any way and it did not go very fast. Almost like coasting. It had no sound as I slowed way down with my window down to listen, it was not a blinding light, you could clearly see it had a oval outline shaped very much like a street light but not as bright. It was a warm soft orange/red glow. Due to driving on the highway I was unable to get a picture or video. I travel the same road 7 nights a week. I have not seen this before but hope to catch it again. I put my camera in the car this morning!
