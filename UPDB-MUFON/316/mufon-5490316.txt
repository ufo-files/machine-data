Watching Perseids with parents - large shape overhead,thought it was a big bird at first

I was watching the Perseid meteor shower with my parents and saw a large (HUGE) chevron-shape made of small orange/red lights glide over our garden - I thought it was a bird at first and my parents did too, but no bird is that big. Went from East to West horizons and we wouldn't've seen it if it weren't for the meteor shower tonight as well. Very spooked at the moment, especially since I only just reported a childhood incident of mine today as well.
