As I was walking my Dog I noticed a helicopter fly over my head, in the highest point of my neighborhood. I could hear it through my headphones and got a great view of it. An hour later around 11 P.M I was smoking a cigarette, in the same spot I watched a meteor shower from a few days ago. Almost in the same direction I saw another shooting star, before I could think anything It shoots down incredibility low and fast. The plump behind it becomes very elongated and glows bright blue. It lowers to what I would guess as about a mile up and stops! I get a clear 3 second glance as it rotates from South to West. The actual object may have been round and cylindrical, but I KNOW the engine shot a bright green light, it looked like a rectangular jet. On what I'm guessing is the cabin I saw 2 lights, green and red. Which made me think it could have been military. But the speed, then hovering, then the direction change, and the meteorite like plumb made it impossible! the cabin looked almost like glass, like see through or maybe metal that had a slight glow. as I sew the cabin turn toward the West, I see the Green jets brighten but the object just disappeared. No plumb following it, no sound, It just vanishes. It was to quick for me to say if the lights on the cabin were blinking or not, and the body of the object was very hard to make out. I have no doubt what I saw was real, and crazy advanced. I'm attaching a crude hand drawn picture, the body may not be correct. But I know the rear of the object was definitely rectangular.
