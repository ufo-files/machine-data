{nd} Saw object while watching fireworks

I went outside to watch the neighborhood fireworks. As i was watching the fireworks i noticed an object much much higher in the sky than the fireworks. Airplanes wear flying by and they were definitely under this object. I stood there for over a half hour watching this object. I am almost positive it was not a star because this object had a white light and what seemed like a red light a bit further than the white light. The object seemed to move to my right and then back again. I couldn't tell for sure but sometimes i could almost make out black "wings" or a black shape around the lights. After watching this object for about 45 minutes i figured without a telescope i wasn't going to gain any further information. Today, July 6 th i came over to visit my parents. They live about a mile and a half from me. We were sitting outside and they were telling me about the fireworks they saw last night. I then said to my mother, " your probably going to think i am crazy but last night i saw something in the sky that wasn't an airplane". She almost jumped out of her chair and said "we all saw it too" they live on a dead end street and all the neighbors gather in my parents driveway to have a beer and tell stories. But when she said they all saw it i couldn't believe it. They said they also could almost make out a black shape around the lights. They said the lights they saw were red. They all said it also never moved. They saw the object about an hour after i saw it. They said around 10:45-11:00 that the object slowly flew over their house and there was no sound. They said they couldn't believe it and didn't want to tell anyone be ause people would think they were crazy. That's my story.
