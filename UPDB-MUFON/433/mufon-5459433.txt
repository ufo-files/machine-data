{tjd} Left work with an associate and saw a triangular craft

When leaving work (CravinVapes) in Charlotte, MI my coworker and I both witnessed a triangular craft with red lights right above us in the sky. The craft was very low maybe 100 ft above the tree line hovering. It made no sounds and stayed stationary in mid-air for about 2 minutes before very rapidly turning in a 360 degree motion from viewing north and spinning back into the same north direction it was facing. It started to pick up rapid speed much faster than any jet I have seen in person or on YouTube. It then shot straight up into the sky and disappeared. The craft was a dark black even though it was quite dark out and had constant red light. It was not blinking or pulsating and was clearly triangular. My coworker and I had a really eery feeling like it was watching us at it was unimaginably close. This happened at about 9:15 P.M. on July 12th 2019
