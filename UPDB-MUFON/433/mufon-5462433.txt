{rjl} Green orb with pruple heat signature

I was taking pre dawn long exposure pictures at Hilltop Park in Signal Hill in Ca. 5 second exposures. Didnt notice anything with the naked eye. Developed the pictures and noticed an object in two different pictures. The first picture shows in the distance towards the San Pedro Ca area a green orb that appears to be at a stand still then shoot in the sky and disappear and leave behind an upside down cone purple heat signature. I assumed it was how I edited the picture that showed the green and purple colors so I looked at the unedited photo and the same colors were there. I assumed that was the only picture until I edited another picture. I moved my camera maybe 40ft in another direction towards the Port of Long Beach Ca in a SW direction and captured the same object descending then stop. Again another 5 second exposure not seen with the naked eye. Object shows a purple heat signature coming down with a green light at the bottom and then a green orb appears. I have posted the image Unedited, edited and in black and white. Date and time stamp is wrong on Camera because it was a new camera and I had not set the date and time yet. I know this because later that day I went to a car show to take pictures and the car show is only on Saturdays once a month. Date was 02/03/2018 and the approx time was 6-7am
