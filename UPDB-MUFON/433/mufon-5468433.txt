{rjl} Comparatively large yellow orange light that did not move for 10+ minutes. Unlike typical air traffic. Looked and came inside and went out 90 minutes later and it was gone.

I was taking my dogs out to do their business at about 9:00pm and I looked to the west toward Eugene and was perplexed by a relatively large yellow orange light that did not appear to move for an observation of 10 minutes. I went back inside not knowing what it was or what it could be. I went out again with the dogs at 10:30-11:00 and the bright light was gone. Looked the next night and there was nothing in the objects location. Sighting happened the same day as an apparent daylight sighting in Medford, OR. (I know because I checked for internet news reports and saw one). Definitely strange and out of place for the night sky.
