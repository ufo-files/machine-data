{jpg} Intense brilliant green flying object observed flying over SR59/W. Main bridge while at stoplight, facing SB towards Longmere Dr.

I was returning rented movies and games to the Family Video on the west side of Kent; when finished, I left, traveling through Downtown to reach the Convenient gas station on W. Main. When I approached the stoplight to turn onto W. Main, my gaze was, for whatever reason, pushed over the bridge to my left. Immediately as I turned to look at the horizon over the bridge, an intensely colored green orb appeared, pausing briefly before rocketing southward at breakneck speed, disappearing suddenly into nothing and leaving behind a very short-lived green aura and what appeared to be sparking shrapnel, at which point, all traces of the object disappeared.
