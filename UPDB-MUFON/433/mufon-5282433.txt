2 circular rings of light flying parallel to I-75 south in No. Ky

I was driving north on I-75, near I-275 in No. Ky, at about 9pm, on 2/26/22, when 2 objects whizzed past me going south along the path of the expressway toward the Greater Cincinnati Int'l Airport. They were maybe 40-50 ft west of the southbound lanes, and about 150-200 ft high. They were circular, and all that I could see was a ring of bright, spinning white pulsating lights. They were 10-15ft in diameter, maybe more. They flew by very quickly and when I turned my head to see if they were still there, they were gone. I tried to see if there were drones that looked like this, but I couldn't find any. This is also a flight path for passenger jets.
