{nd} A bright (about -4 magnitude light) of red-orange color in Pegasus moving slowly

I was sitting in our house looking up out of our window when I noticed a bright reddish-orange light in the constellation Pegasus moving slowly toward the north. I ran out onto our deck and observed it for a short while to see if it was an aircraft going overhead. The light was unblinking and moving with no sound when it suddenly stopped moving when nearly straight up. Being an amateur astronomer I was intrigued and stumped as to what it might be knowing satellites do not stop I went into the house and grabbed my digital camera hoping to get a video of it. When I came back outside it started moving at a right angle toward the east and just winked out before I could get the camera set for a video shot. It was not an airplane and certainly not a satellite as they do not make right angle turns. It seemed to be very high up maybe a few miles. I have seen another object just like this - same color and appearance some years previous to this one only it was visible in daylight and stayed stationary for about half an hour before disappearing from view all of a sudden. It was not a star or planet as I am very familiar with them as an amateur astronomer.
