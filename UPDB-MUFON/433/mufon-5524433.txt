Triangular shaped craft. 5 bright lights on the bottom. (Newman,CA area)

I was with 5 people in a car coming back from fishing. We were out in the counrty near newman when all of a sudden I see a light falling from the sky. It looked like it was about to crash it was very low to the ground and made movements that a conventional aircraft is not able to do. The it came closer into few and we saw randomly blinking lights but you could tell it was a triangular shape. It the did flips and quickly hovered over a house and flashed down a bright beam of light. The lights came from 5 high powered lights on the bottom of the craft, I am a stargazer and i looked at the midnight sky all the time and I have never seen anything this close up. Then the object turned the lights off and quickly flew in the northern direction out of sight.
