{nd} Bright object that hovered then proceeded in a zig zag path to the east

I was outside looking at jupiter when I noticed a very bright object to the west. The object appeared to hover then proceed in a zig zag path to the east. It started out very white and bright then it changed to blue-white and appeared to get brighter. the object then appeared to ascend slowly getting dimmer as it continued to move in a zig zag pattern to the east. No sound or FAA lights observed. the object then went out or disappeared. Total time was about 5 min. Very simular to and object I witnessed last summer 7-29-2012 but the coler was white to blue-white not orange like the one this past summer. I thought at first it was a small plane but it made no sound, no FAA lights. I noticed it because it was about 2x as bright as jupiter which I was observing with my telescope.
