Bright flash in sky with a tail seen over Leesburg, VA

I was sitting on my couch watching TV and looked over towards my wife, who was sitting by the window, to talk to her. All of the sudden I stopped talking and was just staring out the window. I told her I just saw a bright flash in the sky (a very brilliant white/blue flash) and there was a tail as though it was streaking across the sky... but I never saw it reach the next window, as if it disappeared (or a flame went out if it was a fire of some sort). It only lasted for a few seconds and was sometime between 21:00 and 21:30 on October 31st. According to other reports of people seeing it (as heard on the radio), it was brightest over Leesburg, where there is a small airport, I might add, but could be seen by people who were 15 miles or so away. Some people did call it in to the FAA and from what I heard it is currently be reported by the FAA as a fireball in the sky. There should be better accounts out there than mine, but I wanted to make some of these UFO network sites aware of the incident, in case it warrants further investigating. I have no further evidence to submit.
