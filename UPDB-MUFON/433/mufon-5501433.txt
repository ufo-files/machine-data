{dr}witnessed UFO hovering over power station

I was driving home from my girlfriends house one night on Pearl Street in Tacoma Wa. As I headed down the road I saw to my right a hovering disc right above a small power station on the corner of Pearl and 26th street. I pulled over into a gas station just across the street and got out of my car. I stood looking up at it right across from me for several minutes as it just hovered there in one place. After a few minutes the craft silently moved over to pearl street. I jumped in my car and followed it fly on the path of the road for almost one mile it turned left and I followed it as it flew slowly down 11th street for a short time when it just accelerated at a very fast speed out of sight. I did call the police and the operator told me that they had several calls that night but nobody would file a report. She asked if I would and stupidly I agreed. When the officers arrived they pretty much laughed at me. I know exactly what I saw it was a flying disc so close I could have hit it with a rock. I was 23 years old at the time and now 59. This is something I think about often. Probably too often. I have never seen anything like this again. I tend to come on UFO sites from time to time hoping to find a similar sighting in Tacoma around that time when I witnessed mine. I haven't found that but maybe my experience will reassure someone else out there who shared my experience. Even though I live in San Diego now I still make it a point to visit that corner every time we visit relatives in Tacoma.
