{cak} 2 Fireballs over the 170 Hollywood free way, 500 over head, January 11, 2014 7:37pm

My son in law was driving, I was sitting in the back seat. My daughter who was up front noticed first. She said out loud, Hey you guys, do you see that? At this point we were coming up on the Sherman Way exit heading south on the 170 Hollywood freeway. The 2 fireballs were moving horizontally one above each other at 50 apart traveling in unison at a pretty fast rate of speed going the opposite direction that we were traveling at roughly 500 overhead as it passed us. There were only a few cars maybe 2 or three on the freeway with us at a considerable distance between us. As the 2 fire balls past us, I had a great view with no obstructions since I was in the back seat. As the 2 fireballs past us the fireball that was fly the lowest, left its flight pattern, it dropped between 25 to 50 from its original flight pattern and accelerated up ahead of the upper fire ball. At this point the 2 fireballs seem to have came together one above the other over THE 170 HOLLYWOOD FREEWAY and 5 FREEYWAY JUNCTION/MERGE where the traffic there was good, It hovered there for a bit, we finally lost visual sight. It was hovering over the merge/junction. description of the 2 fireballs: about 15 to 20 circular fireballs! The fireballs flames were compact, did not leave long flame trails whatsoever!!!
