{td} Huge Multiple Colored Object in front of us for 20 min, lots of orbs.

I was driving this evening on rt 65 north towards m32 near hillman and there was this pulsating multi colored object in front of us going in the same direction for 20 minutes. appeared as a bright star at first but it was odd as it was due north and low in sky maybe 5 miles ahead of us. As we were gaining on it I could see it was wider than tall. after about 15 minutes we were getting pretty close to it and could see all sorts of red and orange orbs zipping around it. The person I was with was getting kind of frightened by it as I was trying to speed up and get closer to it. I get a few miles from m32 and it just instantly vanishes. Have no idea what it was.
