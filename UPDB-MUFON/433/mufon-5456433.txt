6–8 Pulsating lights lined up diagonally

6-8 star-like white lights lined up in a perfect diagonal. Lights would dim then get brighter. Watched them do this individually for 3 minutes and then they all slowly dimmed out. We Witnessed this from Ontario Canada, and from our point of view it looked like they would have been above Michigan.
