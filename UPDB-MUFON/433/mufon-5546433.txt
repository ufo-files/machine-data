Black Triangle

I was at school in my sophmore math class and looked out the window facing west towards a trailer park across HWY 31 in Westfield Indiana and seen this triangle shaped object about 800 to 1500 feet off the ground. I immediately IM'ed my mom on FB and told her about it and she said for me to make sure it wasn't the stealth bomber. I couldn't disrupt my class so I kept my mouth shut as everyone whould have laughed. IT wasn't the stealth, it was going to slow then it was gone. I go to air shows and I have never seen anything like this except one time at night when my mom seen a triangle but I only seen the end of it going west. This thing today was going north over hwy 31 and I was close. I wonder if anyone else seen this. I keep up on my aircraft cause my Grandpa was a pilot in the AirForce and planes really interest me. IF this was one of our's, I want to fly it someday. I make fun of my mom and this UFO stuff but today kinda made me think..
