hovering bright object with five white lights

Driving East on hwy 96 at 6pm three occupants noticed a bright object hovering while displaying five white lights to the north of our vehicle. We estimate the object was hovering over Hillsboro Pike somewhere between grassland middle school and further north to the intersection of Old Hickory and hillsboro. Because it hovered in place without motion, we decided to redirect our van by turning off hwy 96 turning left to point north so we could pull of the road near gentry farm to simply observe it. We observed it for several more minutes then simply "glided" away to our right moving northeast, without any noticable acceleration. It didn't seem to turn upon moving so it maintained its visual impression upon us until it departed out of our field of vision.
