My wife and I witnessed a bright reddish light in the northern sky on the way home from dinner.

We were heading north on Riverside Dr. on our way home from dinner on Sat. Feb 25, 2012 at a distance of approximately 1/4 mile from our residence when we noticed a bright, reddish light in the northern sky. We drove into our driveway and parked. My wife watched the light while I went into our RV and retreived the camera. The light was gone when I met up with my wife. She stated that it had accellerated to the north and vanished.
