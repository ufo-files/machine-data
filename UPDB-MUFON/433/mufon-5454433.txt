{rjl} a long line of bright lights uniform in brightness and speed but not in spacing at least 50 lights

at 5:55 am on March 26 2020 i looked out my bedroom window to observe stars which in the sky and noticed a straight line of lights moving from the southwest to the northeast. all the lights were coming from a far distance and looking to the southwest i could not see the end of the line of lights! . 
some of the lights that were nearly directly above me were pulsating in tandem as to their forward speed but not extremely so . some of the lights were evenly spaced apart and some were not. i opened a window and could not hear any sound that so many flying things would have generated,if they were jets ,propeller of helicopter powered . there were no blinking lights on the white colored lights a few had a hint of pale red along with the white but not all of the lights did. after this very long train of lights had passed over and were gone a few minutes later a solitary light of equal size and brightness came along the same flight path as the others. to me this was a spectacular event to have viewed as it went on for at least five minutes and there were so many lights.   Ray Tonns
