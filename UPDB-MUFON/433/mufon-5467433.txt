{jpg} sighting of very large ufo

Outside my house @approx. 2a.m. With puppy, potty training.
Extremely large bright ship hovering to South, not the moon. Shiny, lights, as big as several football fields. Approached by 2 jets coming from west direction. Object moved in rapid upward motion,then blinked out. Jets then turned towards south, out of view. Me, dumbfounded. I have seen this one other time in 2007, but to the north/north east. Same time of night.I live a secluded rural location.
