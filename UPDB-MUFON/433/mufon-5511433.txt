{nd} Massive red/orange pulsating light in the tree line of signal point post falls

Approximately 11:15 pm, I went outside to smoke a cigarette on my front porch as I do every single night. From my porch I can clearly see the area where signal point sits. In fact the normal blinking towers are what I see on any clear night. I went outside and as I started to sit down I glanced up at the mountain as I always do and just below and to the left of the normal blinking signal light, there was a MASSIVE and extremely bright red light. It was way too large to be the normal things you might expect.. (headlights on a mountain road, an outdoor property light, camp fire in the distance) there is no way it was any of those. The size and distance would not match. I called for my husband and he tried to view it through binoculars but we couldnt make anything out other than what appeared to be a massive pulsating fireball. I watched it for probably 10 - 15 minutes total and it went from a very large and very bright glowing red to a dimmer orange and then began pulsating. I expected it to move as similar events took place last summer but it did not, it stayed where it was and took on the appearance of a glowing orange orb. After a few minutes of that, it dimmed even more, brightened up again a bit and then blinked out.
