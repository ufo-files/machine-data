{cak} pulsating stationary white light, flashing, ejected 3 red balls within seconds apart

9/15/2012 Family BBQ in the back yard approx.10:40 p.m. 89 degrees it was a hot night clear sky. When all of the sudden a stationary white flashing light appeared to the north east corner in the sky, pulsating. This made the family stand up and stare at the flashing light in the sky. We just stood there and observed it.
It released a small red ball of light and a second and then a third ball of light. These red balls were released from the bottom of the flashing circular white light. The red balls appear to look like a spark of fire, they dropped seconds apart and fell straight down in straight line down to earth.
Then the flashing white light turned off. Three feet over the white flashing light reappeared and then it shot of in a high speed in a horizontal direction to the south and disappeared. Soon after a helicopter was seen flying over head.
Everyone who experience this unknown sighting was in a shock state of mind, because we did not even react to get the cell phone and record this event. Cant believe we all had cell phones, but no one thought about recording.
IF THERE IS EVER A NEXT TIME, WE WILL BE READY to react and record :) NO JOKE!!
