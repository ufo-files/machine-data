{jpg} Observed about 30 steady, white, bright lights high in the sky, for about 15-20 minutes.

On 9/29/17, our neighbor called us outside to determine what was hovering in the sky. It is about 10:40 am. We counted 30 or more bright white things in the sky appearing to hover and very slowly descending ( getting slightly larger). Position was NW ranging from 60 to 90 degrees. Not a single cloud in the sky. Minutes later they appeared to be moving higher and more to the west. Then two military looking jet aircraft flew under them, helping us identifying them as being much higher than they first appeared. It seamed that within just a few minutes after that they quickly got higher and much less in numbers. Finally after about 10 minutes total the last four where straight west and about 90 degrees before we lost sight at about 10:55 am.
