{rjl} It was about 50 ft across and domed on top and bottom which I think it was two seperate cafts because it was different colors.

working in shop when 4year old nephew said come and see. we went out side and looked out at field and he said not there up there and when we looked up there it was about a hundred yards away just above the trees. It was woobling and headed east It was going down acrossed the highway. WE jumped in truck and when we got there it was in two sections about at 300 years. The kids wanted me to go down there but it was getting dark and I didn't want to leave them in the truck alone in case some the happened to me. We went back home and I got up early and went back but it was gone. I THINK it was two crafts hooked together because they were different colors the top was shining and the out dull colored. Me my wife and our three kids and to others seen it. It was late in 1982 and then we army helicopter around are shop as if they thought we had it. I kind sketch what we saw I will never forget it.
