{cak} Dark rectanglee object seen above Springdale Ohio

On 4/1/19 at 17:35 and in the area of 12120 Springfield Pike, my family and I observed a dark grey/black object in the sky. We were heading south on Springfield Pike and the object was to the south east. We continued heading south until we turned right on Glensprings and into the parking lot of 415 Glensprings. While in the parking lot, I was able to take two photos and one video. We continued to watch the object on what appeared to be a northernly flight path until it was out of sight. The object made no noise and did not have any lights.
