3 lights were moving in a triangle formation. Formation was rigid. Objects then disappeared one at a time.

I was leaving my house for groceries and while walking to my car, spotted 3 huge (compared to a plane flying overhead) lights in the sky, perfectly framed in the driveway between trees and a building. It was immediately abundantly clear due to the formation of the 3 that something abnormal was happening. I called my friend to explain to her what I was currently watching. After describing them to her and considering swapping to video on my phone, the first light disappeared. Within a few seconds of the first, the others disappeared, one by one.

I have absolutely no physical evidence and hindsight suggests I did the wrong thing when reacting. I have spoken with several friends and there is no explanation for what happened. Initially, I believed I felt ill from the idea that nobody would ever believe my account, but I still feel strange several hours after the incident. It was enough for my grandmother to detect over the phone without any knowledge of the event and encourage me to submit this report.
