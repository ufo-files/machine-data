Round sphere materializes out of thin air and travels northbound maybe 40 feet above a busy street

I was parked in the parking lot of my building. I exited my vehicle to have a cigarette. As I tried lighting it, I noticed a small flash maybe 40 feet above the ground. It was a beautiful day and no wind, so I rubbed my eye thinking something was in my eye. As I tried to light my cigarette again, I noticed another flash of light and immediately looked in the direction i saw it. I couldn't believe my eyes. First there was nothing, then very slowly emerged a chrome? Mirrored? Transparent? round object out of nowhere and proceeded to move north, moving slower than the traffic maybe 40 feet below the object, and continued north then on a 45 degree angle until it was gone. The entire scene took maybe a minute or so and has left me feeling very uncomfortable
