2 flying discs at Biggin Hill Airport.

I was in the terminal at Biggin Hill Airport (where I work) and was looking out onto the airfield. I saw two fast moving small disc like objects in front of the clouds. They were nothing like anything else I have seen fly and I have seen most things fly during my time. My brain tried to see them as birds but nothing about the way they flew fitted (no wings, too fast, too high, too big). They were white and shiny. Its difficult to say how big they were but my guess is somewhere between an armchair and a small car. After flying along in front of the clouds for a bit they turned left up into the clouds and disappeared. 

I was too embarrassed to tell everybody present what I had seen.
