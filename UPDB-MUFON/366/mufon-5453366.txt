unusual multiple light on the same pattern.

At 9:01 pm on April 25th I let my dog outside. I happened to look to the sky and and saw multiple lights, Estimate 20 - 30 star like light moving from the NW to the SE in a single file. The lights were all be moving in the same direction but were spaced equally. They crossed the sky in a matter of minutes and did not make any noise. The lights were all constant and spaced, not like a tail. There were no blinking or color changes. The line of lights did not leave any vapor trail. After observation I recorded the time & date and made sketch on paper as to what I saw.
