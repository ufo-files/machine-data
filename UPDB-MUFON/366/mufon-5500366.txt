Massive white cigar shaped craft observed whilst filming chemtrail spraying

I was outside filming one of the heaviest days of spraying I have ever witnessed, I turned to around to look at my photos on the digital camera when i saw a massive, possibly over 1000m long white cigar shaped craft come into view from behind the trees behind my house, it travelled very rapidly directly north where i lost it behind trees a few seconds later, I managed to capture an image of the craft but due to the short focal lenth i had my camera set to it focused more on my neighbours tv aerial than the craft, however i did find on checking the photos on the computer later that there appears to also be a silver orb like craft in the pictures as well...
