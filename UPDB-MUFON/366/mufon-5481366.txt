{rjl} 3 copper orbs about size of pea at arms length trailing sw-w over SLC airport area. Very bright, dissapeared in front of me.

I was walking home from store and witnessed 3 large shining, copper orange orbs trailing southwest towards the west over the area of the industrial area of salt lake just to the south of the great salt lake. The orbs were about the size of an aspirin at arms length and were space from what I can tell about a mile or so from me and about a mile or so away from each other in the air, they were at an altitude between maybe 1000-3000 ft. They made no sound and as I was watching the leading orb it dissapeared. The other two dissapeared when I went behind a building that obscured my vision for just a moment. These were very bright, and made no noise and what is strange is I saw this same thing occur with one orb on the 20th. No blinking lights to signal a plane, not a lantern, or anything else I've seen above the valley in the last 25 years.
