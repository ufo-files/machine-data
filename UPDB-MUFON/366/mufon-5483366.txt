{jpg} Lubbock Lights Orb spotted

Object was bright and stationary yet at a very low altitude likewise to helicopters and began to pulsate light and emit almost a fireball type of phenomenon. After parking the car and observing, witnesses observed the pulsating orb ascend slowly northeast towards what would seem to be Abernathy, Texas. The flight path of the object was considerably random with ascending and descending movements but what at a constant speed of about 50 knots ( maybe 70MPH ), as being a avid observer of these phenomenon it was another unmistakable Lubbock Lights experience. The lights were Red/orange and emitted a golden glow at random points and had vanished from sight within 5 minutes of the start of the observations.
