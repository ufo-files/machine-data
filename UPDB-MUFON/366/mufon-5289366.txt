Something around Mars

My father and I were watching Hale Bopp comet with a 3 in dobsonian telescope. Mars was almost strait above and I looked at Mars with the telescope. I saw what looked like a satellite going around Mars and disappeared behind Mars. This happened really quick and I knew that being that far away, what I saw was not something man made. We humans do not have anything that can move that fast and it had to be big cause one of our satellites could not be seen from Earth
