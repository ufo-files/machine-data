Large Flare like Orange/White pulsating light moving from right to left (E-W)

We were just having drinks in the back porch and when I got up to go to the toilet I noticed a large flare like Orange/White pulsating light moving slowly from the east to west. It didn't look like a plane as it wasn't moving very fast. I doubt it was a helicopter as there was no noise. I quickly went into the house to get my binoculars to have a closer look. Up close it was 2 white lights with an orange one in the middle pulsating slowly. The object then changed its path to the north and then disappeared. There were no observable aircraft in the area at the time and this object did not have any navigational lights.
