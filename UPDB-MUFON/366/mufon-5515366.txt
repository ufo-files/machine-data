{nd} White comet like. Flew up, across, and in circles in a pattern

We were driving North on McQueen Road in Chandler AZ towards Ray Road when our daughter saw the bright white comet like spheres flying North. Two continued while one flew straight up in the sky, quickly followed by another. Then three of them regrouped and began flying in circles, looking like comets with light trails behind them. Suddenly they flew off North toward the Superstition mountains and vanished. This was around 7:35 pm.
