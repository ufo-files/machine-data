{jpg} Bright flashes of light traveling through my house

I was up late gaming, (I had just bought a new PS4) and I stepped out on my back porch to have a smoke. It was a clear night and you could see quite a few stars being I lived out in the mountains away from city lights. I'd look for shooting stars or anything else and I saw a pulsating star traveling very fast in a curved path across the night sky over the mountains, I thought it was the ISS at first until when it was almost over the horizon it zig zagged very fast 3 times then vanished. I've never seen any star or plane do what the craft did i witnessed.
