{cak} triangled shaped lights traveling faster than normal with no sound

My wife and I were at a friends fish fry for the evening. We were sitting around a campfire. It was kind of cold out. I got up and went inside to the bathroom. When I returned to the fire I turned around to warm my backside. That's when I saw what I first thought was the Pleiades in the sky. But as soon as my eyes focused I saw that it was lights in a triangle shape and I said, What's That! pointing to the object. Everybody started to get up and look.(there were 6 of us). 5 of us saw it. It was traveling fast away and went over a tree in the distance and was gone. We stood there for the rest of the evening talking about it and wondering if anyone else saw it. Somebody had to of seen it besides us.
