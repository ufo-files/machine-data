{nd} Very large boomerang shaped craft flew over the Las Vegas Valley. Appeared to be very high elevation traveling west to east

Stepped out to the back yard and looked up and noticed a series of dull green light in a straight line pattern, then noticed another series of lights near the others and could clearly see they where connected by a solid boomerang shaped object, moving from west to east with a constant speed and direction. Very high and appeared to be very large ( over 500 ft long). Watched the object for 45 seconds before losing sight as it traveled SE.
