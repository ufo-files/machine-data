{cak} From my upstairs window I observed 6 to 8 pulsating red lights moving in a SE to SW Line formation at about 500+ knots

I was reading my tablet on my bed when I noticed a pulsating light out my South facing window. I reached for my glasses and noticed that there was more then one and the lights were pulsating. I assumed Military but heard nothing as far as rotary or turbine. There was no wind to interfere with sound. I went downstairs and got the other occupants of the house to come outside and see what I was seeing. All of us witnessed(3 persons) the lights moving East to West in the South. The entire event lasted about 10-12 mins. Outside there was no sound except traffic in the distance. Still no sound from the direction or from the objects. I have seen things before but nothing this blatant. I did some communications with friends and one suggested this may be Drones, since they are very quiet. But that is a possible explaination. I am not counting a military formation out.
