UFO descends several degrees in seconds

Tuesday November 14th, 2017 - 2nd Sighting during S1ance Session

Again, at 8:02pm I was outside facing north on the front balcony when as I looked upward towards the west I saw a brightly shining orb hovering at 45 degrees or approximately 3000 feet above and behind the clouds. All of a sudden the glowing object descended diagonally to my right (east) several degrees lower or hundreds of feet within 180 seconds. Moving clouds then covered the illuminated white beaming craft.
