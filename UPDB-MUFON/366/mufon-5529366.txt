While driving west on Hwy 6, saw out right window round red flashing object

While driving my truck west on hwy 6, about ten miles before Bremen, I saw a round red flashing object approximately 250 yards out my right window. I thought it was a tractor in a field. Then it changed color to green and I thought it was a stop light, but it was in a field. When I came even with it, it changed to a florescent blue and shot straight up in the sky. I've seen these before but never that close. I called my wife and told her to report it right away. Just in case. Contact me on my cell if you want. The company I drive for is out of Butler, Indiana, and I am often driving on this road.
