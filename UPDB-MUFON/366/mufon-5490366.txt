My brother and I witnessed the UFO over [location removed] on Aug 8, 1980. I was 11 y/o, my brother was 14.My brother and I shared a room together in our house on 1719 17th Loop on Kirtland Air Force Base. We were in bed at the time we witnessed the event. I was almost asleep when I was awaken by a very, very, incredible bright white bluish light that illuminated our entire house. At first I thought it might have been the Security Police were shining a very bright spotlight into our house. There wasn't any cars around and there were no aircraft in the air. It was totally silent, we knew it wasn't a helicopter, or any other aircraft. We had lived on Air Force Bases our entire lives. It was not an aircraft that we were familiar with at the time or since. It was so bright it lit up our entire yard, in fact it lit up the whole neighborhood with a white bluish tinted light. We could not look at the craft, the light was way too bright to look at. It was like staring into a lit strobe light that was constantly on. It seemed that it was hovering over our house. I became very frightened and pulled my covers to my bed over my head. The light was so bright I could still see it through my blankets. I also noticed my blankets were sparking with electricity. I am unsure if the static was natural or craft related. But I think it was the light that created it. I don't know how long the light was there. I don't remember falling asleep and the last thing I remember was I was very, very frightened. No one we talked to saw it or they wouldn't talk about it. Every since this has happened I feel something strange happened to us. I just don't know what, and have been bothered by this since it happened.
