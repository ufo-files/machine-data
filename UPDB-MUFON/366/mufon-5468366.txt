{rjl} Fast moving bright object flew across the sky, slowed down to a hover, made several sharp turns, then disappeared over the tree line.

I was standing outside talking with a co-worker when we both noticed a circular bright light moving really fast across the sky, moving from the South East to the West. We heard no sound. As it approached the west it slowed down as if it stopped, took a sharp turn to the South, then another sharp turn to the North. I ran out onto the street as I stumbled with my cell phone to capture a photo or video. All the images appeared to be blurred on the screen as I tried to capture some type of evidence. That's why the video I did capture was short. It appeared blurred on my screen but after reviewing one of the videos it actually focused on the object for a short time. This object shot out beams of light that reflected of the clouds above it. The object also flashed with multiple lights after it slowed down. 

Shortly after the object disappeared over the tree line two helicopters flew directly over head about 300-400 ft off the ground in the direction of the object. These helicopters were moving fast with blue flashing lights.
