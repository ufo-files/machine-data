Transparent object

I walked out of my house around 9pm on the night of April 20th 2021 waiting for my wife, who was returning from class @ college. I happened to look up because it was a fairly clear night and I enjoy looking @ the stars on clear nights. As I looked up their appeared to be some sort of disc shaped distortion of the stars directly above me. I initially thought it was just my eyes so I blinked them to see if it went away, but when I looked the distortion was still there. At that time I was able to make out more detail as well. I was able the defined edge of a perfect disc shape with another smaller circular shape in the exact middle of the larger one. The object was either completely transparent or was somehow projecting the sky above it onto the bottom. I was still unsure if I was really seeing what I was seeing, but then the object suddenly took off from a stationary position towards the NW without making any noise. 

I will admit that I still have trouble believing what I saw. But I decided to make this report because I saw what I saw and I hope your organization can help me figure out what the heck I saw.
