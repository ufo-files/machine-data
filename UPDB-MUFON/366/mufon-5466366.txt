{rjl} We witnessed this golden orb like light hovering above a hillside. It pulsated and the light grew bigger and then smaller. It moved around in various directions no sound heard like when a plane or helicopter is in the air. It then disappeared.

My wife 1st noticed the light in the sky above a hillside about a mile away. It glowed and pulsated and orange gold color. It slowly grew in size. I thought it might be fire but it was more golden in color. It moved about the sky slowly, hovering in place at times then disappear slowly. No sounds were heard like when a plane or helicopter is in the air.It was amazing and we could not figure out what it was at all. We do have a marine base, Camp Pendelton within 10 miles so we usually see planes and helicopters, but this was totally different and unexplainable.
