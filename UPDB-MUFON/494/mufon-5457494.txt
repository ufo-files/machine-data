{cak} Large round light about the size of Jupiter passed over

At 8:39 pm on October 9/2019 I noticed a bright white light coming from the NW going across the sky. Was so amazed that I got out my binoculars and watched as this round bright light silently went across the sky on a clear night. This round right light heading towards the SE, got about 3/4 across the sky when suddenly in front of object it looked to me like a vortex or void even blacker than the sky opened up. Then it seemed this light went into that vortex. To my surprise while still watching the void/vortex with my binoculars it started closing but not before I noticed a faint trail of light inside that void/vortex before it collapsed. It look as though whatever it was left a small trail of light inside that void/vortex before the void/vortex closed. 
I have seen many planes up here but this was definitely not a plane, it made no sound and was definitely a bright white light. There was no other objects beside the stars that I noticed in the sky during this event. What was so interesting was the void or vortex that opened that this object seemed to go into!
