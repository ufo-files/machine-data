{cak} Appeared, hovered with no sound shot up and flew away at impossible speed.

In the back yard playing with siblings.
It flew right into the yard and hovered right in front of us.
No clue

Object was a silver disc/saucer shaped UFO with no rivets. All one piece of metal. Made no sound and hovered about 10 feet off the ground for 5-7 minutes. About 10 feet from us. No signs of propulsion visible or audible. The object then ascended to about 50 feet and flew off to the west at impossible speed making now sound or wake. The object was the size of a large SUV. Only slight marks were made on the ground below. Some grass was disturbed. 

Feelings during the event were shock and not believing what I was seeing, but unable to move. Not because of it but because of the awe of what I was seeing. After it left I was scared but ok.  

It flew away so fast it was gone before I could blink.
