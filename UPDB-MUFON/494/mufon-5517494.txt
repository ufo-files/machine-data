Sitting on deck at home and noticed in eastern sky, a little distance above the treeline, five (5)solid green orbs, no other colors or flashing lights, evenly spaced apart and moving in a straight line slowly from the south to the north...no sound. The apparent distance and speed appeared same as the pattern of a typical jet. However, jets generally fly east-west or west-east, as well as marking/flashing lights. The sighting was observed, from south to north, approximately 15 minutes and out of our view.
