Transparent triangular glowing craft

It was getting later into the evening in the Summer, so the sun wasn’t beaming down brightly anymore, but it was still pretty light outside. I was sitting outside with my dad when I noticed something in the sky a similar color to the lanterns people send off. This is what I thought it was, until I noticed that there were many lights and they were all perfectly triangular to one another. The lights did not waver although there didn’t seem to be anything in between them, and they weren’t even flickering the way a normal lantern would. So I pointed this out to my dad and asked him what it was - we both watched it as it slowly hovered above a tree near a house across the street. Mind you, there were tall trees around and these houses weren’t too close together, so we weren’t directly in front of it, but we were close enough to tell that all these lights seemed to be the border of an aircraft that we couldn’t see or hear. Not long after I pointed it out, it went off into the other direction and completely disappeared. About a year later I went for a walk with my stepmom in the same neighborhood, only a few blocks down. Off to a distance, once again, I noticed the pattern in the sky and pointed it out. This situation went similarly to the other, only this time I was able to get a photo. Unfortunately, it is low quality.
