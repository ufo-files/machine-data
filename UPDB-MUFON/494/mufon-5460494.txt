Walking my dogs three years ago at 4:30 AM in the morning. I live in Johannesburg. It was a chilly cold morning. My route was to walk passed my local supermarket. I was not looking up in the sky rather watching how the dogs were doing. Suddenly from nowhere my attention was drawn up to the top of the supermarket building. In the first fraction of a second I thought a helicopter was landing. In the next fraction of a second I decided no this is a Jumbo aircraft. So by now a whole second had gone by and this craft was only 300 to 500 feet up and certainly not a Jumbo.

It was silent and by now I knew that it was not from this earth. It moved to my left at a fast speed and I would have been able to follow its path however trees got in my way. I was therefore not in a position to photograph it (no camera) so instead I took a photo of where I was standing, the next morning, and this is my identikit of what I saw. (you see the corner of the supermarket on the right hand side).
