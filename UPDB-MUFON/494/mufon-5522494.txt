Saw a reflective, red object at low altitude

I was driving along Gus Eckert Road on the way to work, driving north. I saw a red object at a low altitude (that is what made me notice it and its size). I thought was a banner or balloon, but is wasn1t moving and there was no airplane or helicopter around. About 10 minutes later I saw a helicopter flying in the sky (but it was actually moving and getting further) that was black. The object I saw had a reflection because of the sun. It looked shiny and not dull like the helicopter. It was not moving when I saw it.
