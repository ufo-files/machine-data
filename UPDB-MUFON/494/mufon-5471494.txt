{rjl} A glowing white object as far away as a jet but much larger descending at a 121 angle at the earth.

I was driving west down the highway, looking over to the south every once in a while to enjoy the glow of the sunrise when i notice the object between the clouds. It seemed pretty far away and it was falling at roughly 121 angle towards the ground. The object was far enough to end up behind the clouds after about 5 to 8 minutes of descending, clearing roughly 5 to 101 altitude in that time. It was a rounded arrowhead/diamond shape tip pointing to the ground and was a glowing white. No noticable features other than that and the lack of any kind of tail. It looked pretty big, which is why i thought it was a close weather baloon, but once in dropped behind the clouds i was certain it was larger than i originally thought. Once it dropped behind thr clouds, i lost sight of it.
