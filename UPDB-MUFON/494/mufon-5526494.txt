Airplane was following white orb

I saw an airplane (with the flashing lights) in the southern sky and in front of it at a close distance (maybe 1/2in looking through the binoculars, was a bright white orb flying at the same speed. My grandson and I watched it for maybe 2 minutes and then is just dimmed
slightly and dissapeared. The plane seemed to slow just a little and kept on flying. They were traveling South to east.
I was very excited as I had seen UFOs like this at James Gillilands Ranch in Troutlake WA.
