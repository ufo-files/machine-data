Triangle UFO with five lights

My current boyfriend and I at the time decided to go out and have a smoke. As I just lit my cigarette both of us at the same time happened to look above us. When I looked up. There was a large sized triangle craft. The craft was just above the tree top. The craft was black, if it was not for the clear nights sky you may have not even noticed it. On one side of the craft it had three amber coloured lights. And on the other it had two. For what reason to this day that I don’t understand we had the urge to look above us, I don’t know. It made not one noise. It was traveling at a quick speed. This sighting happened in a matter of seconds. For me to light my cigarette, look above me. See the craft and then out of pure shock I threw my cigarette and ran inside.
