{nd} Black cigar shaped object moving appxm 45 mph stopped at power lines.

At 8:05 pm PST a black cigar shaped UFO moved from South to North over Anadel State Park. Object emitted a vapor that disipated quickly from the tail of the craft. I was facing west as the object slowed to a hover over large power lines that run downhill. I can see inside my house the telivision was dimming and the cable reception was garbled. After the item was out of sight to the North a pair of helicopters flew over the house-one heading south and the other helicopter north after the object. The object was silent and pretty large and moved smoothly across the ridge in anadel state park.
