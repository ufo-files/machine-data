Woke up and decided to look out my 8th floor north facing window. Just as I was looking at the clear night sky over the nearby roof tops I saw this Blue-green glowing sphere shape object heading from east to west aprox 45 degree desending at a high rate of speed heading below my view behind the nearby apartment buildings, it appeared the size of a helicopter if 1/4 mile away. My cell phone displayed 3:26 AM. In the early evening summer around 1986 or 1987 I was driving to Falmouth Mass. and saw a similar blue-green object passing thru the clouds at a high rate of speed, at the time I was told the military reported it being a hot air ballon. My first impression of this object was how lucky to see this blue-green sphere again but then thought I would have preferred it to have been like some of the other never reported incidents I observed throughout my life which were I felt more interesting.
