{cak} One large craft and numerous smaller craft traveling out toward the Atlantic Ocean

I went out on the front porch to look at the moon with the lunar eclipse and there were numerous silent craft traveling in front of the moon. One was large and rest were smaller. I thought I was seeing things and went in to get my fiance' and when he came out his jaw dropped. I do not know how anyone could miss it - some times they stopped - they did not move in unison - the way they moved they were under separate control. The one large one gave the sight effect that is was huge and much bigger than the moon - below and to the lower right of the moon. I cannot even guestimate the size of any of the craft overall because it is dark outside. There were no lights, no noise - they just glided. The whole event lasted over 17-minutes! WOW!
