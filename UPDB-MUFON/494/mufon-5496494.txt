{cak} I saw what appeared to be four lights rotating and turning together through my telescope and they almost looked like they were glowing a redish orange color

I was getting my telescope ready for a night of stargazing around 6;00 pm ,I noticed a bright light and a dimmer one in the western sky ,way too early for any stars to be shinning that bright, I thought it might be an airplane light but an half hour passed it only got brighter and didn't move very much ( 2 finger width ) in the sky, so I grabbed my binoculars, the object almost looked like it was on fire ( glowing ) reddish-orange color and I then got my 6in Orion telescope and took a look that's when things got strange. I noticed the object was three to four orbs turning and rotating but keeping a rectangular formation. I was excited and ran to get my wife to look and make sure I wasn't seeing things and when she confirmed seeing exactly what I saw, I immediately got goose bumps. I watched it for about a half an hour and it faded into the distance.
