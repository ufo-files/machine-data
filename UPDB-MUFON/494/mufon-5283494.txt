Small stationary white object hovering in the sky

This was a small stationary white object hovering 20 degrees above the western horizon at 280 degrees West. Seen from Port Charlotte, Florida, at 5:28pm on November ninth, 2021. I observed it for about a minute until the thick lower clouds covered it. In the photo Port Charlotte A it can be seen slightly above the wire that touches the treetops, about halfway between the right tree top and the right border. On the photo Port Charlotte B, which is a zoom-in of Port Charlotte A, it can be seen above the wire a third of the way from the right border. 
Note the difference between the object and clouds. The object has a different shape and a more solid texture. It is oriented vertically, not horizontally. Its borders were sharp when I saw it.
