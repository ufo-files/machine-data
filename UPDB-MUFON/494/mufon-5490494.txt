{nd} Object hovered for 30 seconds, then darted across the sky at amazing speeds. +Sleep Paralysis.

I was leaving to go to my grandparents house when I was around 12 years old. While leaving at 10:30 I looked up through the backseat window and saw a orange orb hovering somewhat over the baseball fields across from the entrance to my neighbor hood where I was in the car when this happened. I watched this orb for a solid 30 seconds as the object hovered, and then all of a sudden accelerated are AMAZING speeds leaving a trail as if it were a commet in the sky. (0-3,000 mph) All of a sudden I realized that that object wasn't anything man-made and got a EXTREME sense of being paranoid like something was out to get me. The next day I slept in the middle of the day because I couldn't sleep the entire night. I kept my eyes open the entire time for some reason, because I was just resting. All of a sudden when I tried to get up, I couldn't. I was completely immobile, but at the same time strange things were happening around my room. I heard a weird "whomb" sound over and over like a machine was running keeping me still. Even though I didn't see anything, I knew that there was someone in the room with me. I don't know how to explain this, but my "6th" sense was going INSANE. I felt as if I was a toy to this 'creature' that was in my room with me. I looked this up and found it being a condition of sleep paralysis where victims seem to be immobile and feel and see weird figures in there room. Since I saw the UFO the night before, I have always blamed E.T.'s for my sleep paralysis and the orb.
