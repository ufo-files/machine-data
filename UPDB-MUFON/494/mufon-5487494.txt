{dr}Cloud Cloaked ship

It is around January 13, 2009. I was going to work on a very overcast day in Hazelton, WV. The skies were dark grey. As I approached my exit, I noticed a very strange cloud formation. In the entire dark grey clouded sky, there was another "cloud". This cloud was light grey to white. Perfectly cylindrical in shape except one end was larger than the rest. Never have I seen a cloud occur that was a different color and not in a weather related shape as the surrounding area. The grey clouds were rolling in a northwest to southeast fashion. The light grey to white cloud was pointing almost directly south. Hanging in the clouds without explanation. Perfectly straight. I finishied my drive to work while completely confused at this " cloud". I took a few pictures with a poor camera on a Motorola flip phone. I realize that it is a poor quality. In the 15 minutes that I observed this cloud, it never lost its basic shape. It just did not belong. Huge. Frankly I was too scared to report this earlier. Still makes me nervous to look at the pictures today. Even if the picture is blurred one can easily understand how strange these are. This was not an opening in the clouds. It was definitely another "cloud" formation in the main cloud based.
