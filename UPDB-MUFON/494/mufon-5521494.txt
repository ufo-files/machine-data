While driving down the road entering the Far Rockaway area I observed a sun like light in the sunset.

While driving down Hookcreek Blvd.(heading towards Rockaway Turnpike in Queens, NY.) I looked into the sky at the sunset and saw the sun and trails coming from it almost as it was on fire. While observing further I noticed that it wasnt the sun and it was slighty to the left and much smaller than the sun. As we continued driving it began to change color it became a yellow/orange almost as if it were on fire. Naturally we continued to watch the object thinking it might be an asteroid or something crashing to the Earth. After about an hour it slowly faded into a reddish/purple streak in the sky. I firmly believe this was a UFO based on my observations.
