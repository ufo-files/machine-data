Erratic moving blue light near the full moon

So the other night as I was trying to take a photo of the moon with my 100x Zoom lens on my Samsung Galaxy s20 Ultra I noticed a small blue object moving in different directions. Up, down, left, and right. I quickly tried to record with video but couldn't with that deep of a zoom. I switched over to camera mode and quickly snapped the shot. It just looked like a white light that could be mistaken for a star out of context. After the shot was taken the light disappeared. I decided to look again at the pic and lower the brightness and the exposure and what I saw blew my mind. It is very "saucer" shaped with what looks like blue radiation or exhaust of some sort behind it. Thoughts? The two photos below are the same, I just cropped the other one for a closer look.
