Firework UFO Wheaton, IL 2015

I was wearing a GoPro on July 4th, 2015 at Graf Park in Wheaton, IL. While watching this with my friend who is a man of God, we both seen a light that was hovering just beyond the fireworks. I didn't make a big deal until I started to see it move from left to right. Check around minute 13:00 in the video you'll hear me say something. I mentioned something to my friend and he couldn't make it out but definitely confirmed there was something in the sky that wasn't a firework. There was no sound other than the fireworks going off. Between fireworks was silence and people chatter. A few times I could make out red and green random pulsating lights. Towards the end of the show I noticed it moving south slowly then it took off straight west at a really fast pace. Now I own a drone and have never seen one take off a that far of a distance that fast! Unfortunately my GoPro didn't capture the UFO but you hear us talking about it through the clip.
