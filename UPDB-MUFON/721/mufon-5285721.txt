Brightest Blue Light

At approximately 11:00pm before going to bed I looked out the window and witnessed a few of my neighbors taking pictures of something in the sky. Some were jumping on cars / roofs to take a better look. I couldn't see what they were looking at from my front window so I went into my backyard (general direction they were pointing) to take a look. At first glance I noticed the brightest blue light. I climbed on my kids play set to get a better / higher view of this light. The moon was in a NW direction and this object was above it to the right. It was the brightest blue light in the sky. It was not a street light, stadium light and clearly above the horizon, but seemed possibly within the atmosphere? It was incredibly bright, bluish cast and the best way to describe would be a gigantic spotlight pointed in our direction. We observed this light, which did not move its position at all, and every now and then slightly pulsated. After an hour of observing, the object dimmed from a brilliant bluish white light to a warmer hue and significantly smaller size and eventually disappeared. In the moment, I downloaded a few sky / moon / tracker apps on my phone and could not line that object with anything. I've never seen any light this bright before. I included some pictures and videos below, including the video of me seeing it on my ring camera in the backyard.
