noticed the object in the sky in the distance which appeared big and stationary

I was at the back of my house and noticed that object along with another long object next to it. The other long object seemed to be emitting black smoke. I then ran inside for my camera. When I returned there was only one object left. I quickly started recording. I had no clue what the object was. I was very shocked and startled that it was just there in the sky stationary. It appeared almost round and dark. While recording I heard a helicopter flew past my house and I stopped recording. I went inside to put away the camera. I returned outside to see the object still there. It remained for about 1 minute and then disappeared within a could very slowly.
