(GS) Large slow moving triangle with five white lights. Moving approximately 20 mph and all lights flickered one tiime

I was driving between Harlem and Dearing Georgia. I noticed light in the sky and thought they looked different so I slowed down to take a closer look. The object I saw had 5 bright white lights in a perfect triangle pattern. The object was very low, approximately 500-1000 feet. It was moving so slowly that when I slowed down it appeared as though I was moving to it. When I was at my closest distance to it, all 5 lights flickered one time. The flickered, but it didn't seem like a flicker. It was more like a trembling of the lights. I didn't think to pull over to record it. I was working and had to get to my destination. When I first saw it, I had no clue what it was. Ive spent numerous hours in military aircraft to include many hours in the skies over Iraq, and Ive never seen a military aircraft that even comes close to resembling what I saw.
