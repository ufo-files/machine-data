{rjl} While preparing to land at Denver, metallic orb seen / reflecting light near Front Range air force base

While landing at Denver Airport I witnessed a metallic orb. The plane was heading west before circling back to Denver. We were only several hundred feet off the ground. To the north of the plane was Front Range Air Force Base. While I was looking at the base I saw a metallic orb gleaming very brightly. It was at the same elevation of the plane and appeared to be stationary.
