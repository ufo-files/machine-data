I was watching a video on hydrogen bomb testing and suddenly an apparition that looked like Frank the Pug showed up by my door. I could see it through my glass window. It gave me the chills at first, but I could feel that its presence was non-threatening. It then demanded my pizza and ate it all when a fellow being arrived and demanded more.
