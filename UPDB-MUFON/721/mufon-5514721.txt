{nd} My mother walked outside and seen a white tube-like structure hovering over the house.

Monday October 22, my mother walked outside around 9-10 pm to check for mail. She then turned back towards the house when she looked up and seen a white tube-like object hovering slightly above the house. The object was stationary; no lights, or sounds. My mother had thought it was my father doing some work on the roof, and so she did not mention anything. A similar sighting occured last thursday, October 18th. The object was seen by many across the county; the sightings were posted in the local newspaper the next day. However, my mother did not hear of this until she told me what she saw; then I told her.
