{cak} son took pictures of an indescribeable object.Object disappeared and reappeared at least 10 times. Each time it reappeared in a different spot.

On 8/8/17 @ approx. 7:45 pm in Hockessin, De., my son was looking up into a tree to try and get a ball down, which was stuck. As he was looking up he noticed a very bright object. The object disappeared. Suddenly it reappeared but in a different spot. This sequence happened at least 10 times. He took out his phone and shot some really interesting pictures. He was really scared. Note, other kids witnessed the event. They were all freaked out. The pictures seem to show a oval type object with a well defined rectangular, brightly lite center. It is definitely not a blimp. You can tell from the pictures that the object is in different positions. The pictures are really cool!!!
