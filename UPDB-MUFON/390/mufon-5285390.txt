Craft materialized and flew slowly/silently past me, above my garage and upward where it became smaller and a different shape then disappeared.

This whole interaction was about 10-12 seconds total. Was sitting in back yard before sunset in spot where I had done CE5 the night before for first time. I noticed from the corner of my eye an object come into view on my right about 15 FT off ground. Looked toward it and immediately thought it was a helium balloon loose from a kids party or something because of the shape and shiny metallic look. Immediately I questioned what I was seeing realizing it was not a balloon (no string and not randomly floating in wind) because it was gliding in a perfectly straight trajectory (avoiding obstacles), very slowly, silently and intentionally from my right to directly in front of me about 20 FT away. I realized it was not a balloon (in a few seconds) and I noticed the air around it looked wavy like when heat is rising from hot pavement. It changed from a balloon shape to an acorn shape then and the wavy air stopped. It kept it's trajectory but sped up a bit and lifted slightly to clear my garage (about 25 Ft high) and the power lines. At that point I realized I should be filming and jumped up taking about 10 steps to get within sight of it again (trees started to block my view), and started to film. In that short time the object had changed shape again to a metallic orb and was about 500 feet up in the air and much smaller and completely round/orb like. I got 3 seconds of video at this point and when I took a few more steps to keep filming it had disappeared into thin air. I ran to find it looking everywhere in the sky but it was gone. 
Side note: I would share my video but not publicly.
