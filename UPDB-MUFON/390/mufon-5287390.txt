Black Triangle Shape with Lights Corbin KY

My dad saw a craft he described as a triangle shape (looked like a sail on top, sail on bottom, with a solid black center) with lights around it, moving above the tree line "the speed of a blimp but silent" before descending behind them. At the same time, my mom was trying to use her Blink cameras to see how far the motion detection would work, using my dad as a subject, but she couldn't connect to the cloud. He came in and told her what he saw and she told him about the cameras. This was on Sunday night, after 11. We got my dad to draw what he saw.
