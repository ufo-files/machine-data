TRAVELING FROM NEW YORK I OBSERVED SOME STRANGE SHAPES AND IMAGES OF VARIOUS THINGS SO I DECIDED TO RECORD TO SEE THE OUTCOME OF THE FOOTAGE.THIS FOOTAGE WAS REVEIWED BY ME AFTER THE RECORDING, SO I DID NOT SEE THE PROJECTION UNTIL AFTER IT APPEARED ON FOOTAGE.THE CONTENTS OF THIS FOOTAGE CONSIST OF TWO HANDS IN THE SKY GRABBING A DISK LIKE IMAGE ON SEVERAL OCCASSIONS AND LETTING IT GO. THE HANDS APPEAR TO BE 10 TIMES THE SIZE OF THE SUN, THEN IT LIFTS THE OBJECT UP AS IF SPINNING A BALL ON ITS FINGER. THIS RECORDING WAS TYPED OFF MY CELLPHONE SO NO ALTERATIONS HAVE BEEN MADETO ENHANCE THIS FOOTAGE.
