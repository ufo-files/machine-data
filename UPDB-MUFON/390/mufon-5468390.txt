{rjl} Looked at Moon saw orb traveling NE. Alt. 30-60k vis.10mile

I just got done working on the house when my daughter looked at the moon and said Daddy what is that. it was about 8:30 and it appeared to be a glowing orb or sphere. I thought it might be the space station at first but it was too low in altitude and not moving as fast. It was moving Northeast at about 20 degrees it was bigger than Starlike but smaller than aspirin I would guess it was around 30 to 60 thousand feet in altitude. I could hear no noise. After it passed overhead a jet flew by heading about 75 degrees NE and altitude ranging from 10 to 20000 feet I could clearly see strobes navigation and here the jet engines from the airplane. I lost sight of the object has a past behind the trees it looked like the Jet(commercial) and object were on a intersecting course. I have noticed an increase of objects in the past three years in my area. I have been apprehensive to report because of my line of work. I just wish there was more disclosure to the public as to what's really going on in our sky's.
