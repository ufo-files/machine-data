2 Globs of light within Sphere shape that had no sound, they came from a star appearing object with colored lights that was revolving around it.

This happened in 1961 or 1962 after a night class at the Nelson art Institute I took a girl student that I meet in class to her home then went out to park at a lake called lake Jocomo, on the way there we saw a bright star with colored light revolving around the center of this star we were in a 1957 Ford convertable with the top down I pulled over to the side of the road and stopped and got out of the car to watch this star, then the next thing I know there are two bright lights coming from the same direction as the star when they came close enough to us we could see that they where large Globs of light About 50 feet to 75 feet in diameter, you could see that they were perfectly round with a glow in side, they came from a easterly direction and went right above us about 100 feet, very slow with no sound and keep going in a westerly direction after this I took the girl back to her home and left to go to My home, next thing that I remember is that I am 30 miles in the opposite direction that I should be going, when I got home it was time for me to go to work and I can't account for 4 or 5 hours I have more to add to this story that has happened in latter years that I think that is connected.
