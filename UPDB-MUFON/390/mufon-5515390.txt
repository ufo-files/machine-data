Witnessed an extremely bright object in the sky that was so bright you coulnt look at it straight on. Changed directions a bit and hovered a bit then slowly flew off. Intelligently flown.

About 10pm I was working on a car with my brother when he called me to look up into the sky. Saw an object that appeared to be a circular or oval shape. It was extremely bright at times. So bright you couldnt look at it head on. It was like a hot air balloon on fire. Very bright and intense, like a burning ball of gas.It was amber colored, sometimes different colors it twinkled. Not sure what it was. Traveled from the NW to the SW.
Chandged direction a few times, seemed to float around and hovered a bit. Then we lost sight of it as it ascended upward diagonally to the SW. Sighting lasted 30 sec. to a minute long. It was definatley intelligently controlled.
It was about 35 degrees above the horizon, about 5 to 10 km away. The sky was clear with a very mild wind. It was huge, about the size of a golf ball.
I was very excited. Freaked out. I was in awe wondering what it was. Had an uneasy feeling. Sighting was something that changes your life. It was definatley not man made.
I went to the police station and nobody reported any sightings. No mention in the local papers the next day.
