{cak} I noticed it while driving and pulled out my phone to take a photo and film it.

I was driving home from work on highway 169 northbound. I noticed it slowly descending in the western sky during sunset. It was falling slowly enough to catch my attention. I pulled out my phone and took a photo, then started recording. I recorded it for a little over two minutes. When I got home I showed it to my wife and she could not identify it. I posted it the next day on Facebook. It attracted lots of attention. Multiple people recommended I report it to this site. It may simply be a passing comet, however it's glow never dissipated and it did not give off a tail/trail behind it.
