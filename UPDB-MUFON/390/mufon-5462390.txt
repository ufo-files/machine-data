Yellow oval matte object with clear horizontal and vertical lines

1 - I was 8 yo, and I was in a small town named "Sonho Azul" a rural community from Mato Grosso, a Brazilian state. I was going to the WC. As in rural zones, the restroom is outside, at least in that time.

2 - I noticed a light object in the sky about the size of the moon (when it is in the middle of the sky), disappearing and reappearing in a straight line from right to left, in from northwest.

3 - I had no clue what it was, I never saw something like that and never had a similar view again.

4 - Yellow oval matte object with clear horizontal and vertical lines. The object was changing its place vanishing from the point it was and reappearing in a line almost simultaneously from northwest to west.

5- First I was curious, and was observing the object changing its place until it stopped changing its place just in front of me.

6- At that moment I had panic and go inside home ... The most regreting thing I did in my life. Hope sometime in life know what was it.
