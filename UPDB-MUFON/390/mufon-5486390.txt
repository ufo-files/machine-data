{rjl} Hwy 249 driving into Magnolia Texas and plainly saw UFO hovering in sky

I was driving home from work on 12-21-15 at around 8:30pm, and while I was on highway 249 just before crossing over the Magnolia City limits, my eyes were directed to the sky because of a very bright light that appreared to "wobble" or "shimmy" back and forth with a darker larger object next to it... I've never quite seen anything like this before, so I grabbed my cell phone and took a few photos... it was dark and I was driving so the pictures do not do justice to what I actually saw. I tried to keep looking at it but as soon as I drove under it, I couldn't see it any longer. It was over pretty quickly.
