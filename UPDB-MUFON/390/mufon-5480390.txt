{nd} I prayed to see three birds and instead saw a UFO that was dancing in the sky.

I was feeling dejected one day in my bed and prayed to God that I would see three birds as a positive sign. Instead of seeing the three birds I saw something better--I saw a UFO.
The UFO was metallic looking. It looked to be a bell shape as I remember. It had a shiny red.pink.green appearance and was also metallic. It appeared to dance in the air. I went to get my roommate to see it too but by then it was gone. I will never forget the dancing UFO.
