{nd} looked like a star that was moving

I was walking a dog at about 5:34am in Scottsdale AZ. Could not help but look up to admire the beautiful star-lit sky when I noticed one of these "stars" was moving. At first it was moving in a straight North direction...then it stopped, reversed direction for a few seconds before turning back to head North.  It would zig-zag left to right a few times and when it did the white color would change to a reddish-orange color but that would only last a second or two. This zig-zag/color change occured about 4 times before it stopped...stood still (totally resembling a star) and then shot off so fast straight North till it disappeared!  Entire episode lasted maybe 4-5 minutes.
