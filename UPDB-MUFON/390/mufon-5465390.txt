{tjd} Airplane traveling towards me disappears after being hit with enormous red light on bottom

I was taking a video of the sky with my camera, Nikon P900 when I spotted an airplane heading towards me in the North, traveling North to South. When zooming in it is hard to stay on target and hair got in my eyes so I had a moment when I was off of the airplane but then was locked back on it as it was traveling towards me. It was leaving a trail. It stopped leaving a trail and the bottom of the plane glowed Red which appeared too large for the size of the plane. The Red that lit up on the bottom of the airplane appeared to cover the entire bottom center area of the plane. Then it got wavy or blurry and it looked like a mirage, as if the heat in the air was distorting my view of it. I have filmed many airplanes over the last 9 months with this camera and I have never seen any of them distorted like this. Then the plane disappeared and a bubble or circle appeared where it had been. I remained looking for the airplane with my eyes and the camera before ending the video.
