UFO was stationary initially, hovering. Then moved to the left and to the right and eventually moved up and away until it was no longer visible.

It was aproximately 2 a.m. on July the 17th 2011 in Redmond, Washington. A friend and I were outside on a back porch. I remember noticing that there were no stars visible at the time except for one which was rather large and bright. After a few moments of I presumed it to be Venus because of its large size and brightness. It was bluish white. There were no aircraft flying around and it was quite silent. Then suddenly, I realized that the star was kind of wobbleing, moving to the left and to the right and changeing its altitude slightly; going up and down a few degrees. My friend and I watched for at least five minutes as the object suddenly appeared to display three or four, smaller, secondary lights that were basically orbiting the main, central light, but not in a fixed manner. The overall impression was that of a amorphous or shape-changing structure. I remember thinking that object could not have been more than a mile away because its lights illuminated the small clouds that were nearby. At one point the object was inside or behind the clouds but was still very visible because they were so bright. The object slowly moved up and away from us before vanishing completely. The objects location was seen approximately to the north-east of the new Virginia Mason hospital in Redmond. The object was initially fairly low in the sky; maybe 30-35 degrees from the horizon. Also the object made absolutely no sound.
