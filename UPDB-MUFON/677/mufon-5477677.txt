Two stationary large objets observed at about 150 metres while driving, disappeared without trace

I was driving from Pierrelatte north along the N7 to Montelimar. I saw two cylindrical objects above the tree line on about the same level as the hill top village of La Garde d'Ademar. I noticed them because they were not moving and because they were cylindrical. Also they were very large. Larger than a plane (light aircraft). One object was higher than the other, and positioned slightly more to the south. It made me think of planes refuelling in mid-air. But these were not plane shaped objets and there was no connecting tube visible. As I drove, I could not see the slightest movement from either object. Suddenly I went past a wooded area which shielded my view. This lasted about 30 seconds. When I was once again able to see past the trees, no objects were visible. I stopped the car another 30 seconds later when I came to a roundabout (i.e able to stop) and got out to have a good look, and see if I could see the objects were moving away in the distance. But nothing was visible at all. 
I was shocked to think it might have been a UFO. I certainly wasn't thinking about anything like that when driving. I checked the internet the next day to see if anything had been reported. A UFO sighting had been reporting for the previous week around Pierrelatte, but not that day. 

FYI Pierrelatte is the site of a Nuclear Power Station (Tricastin) and the largest Plutonium recycling plant in France. There is another nuclear power station north of Montelimar.
