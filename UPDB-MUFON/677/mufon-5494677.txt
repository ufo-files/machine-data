I was walking home and about two blocks from my home, I looked up into the sky and saw a bright fast travelling light that broke up into 4 bright fast travelling lights , then they disappeared. All happened within approximately 4 seconds. I was in shock but never took my eyes off of it. I told my friend to look but by the time she turned around, the lights had disappeared completely.
