{dr}UFO Sighting over Urbandale, Iowa 8/11/2013

8/11/13 

2 a.m. A few friends and I walked from the house we had been hanging out at to a nearby field to observe the Perseid meteor shower. After a few minutes of watching the meteors, someone pointed out a star-like object blinking and moving in a variety of patterns in an otherwise starless area of sky. I took out my video camera and started to film it. The object continued to blink and move about for several minutes. We all got paranoid and decided to leave (this is where the viable footage ends). On the way back to the house I noticed that an identical object had appeared next to the original one. They appeared to be communicating by blinking. The other witnesses claim to have witnessed up to four other ufos during this walk back to the house.

3 a.m.
We import the footage and enhance it/slow it down. The object in the video appears to changing color multiple times per second, all colors of the rainbow. A feeling of dread sweeps over us.

4 a.m. 

Two of the original witnesses were hanging out on the back porch. They said that a ufo came out of the clouds and hovered about. One of them says that the ufo moved in accordance with the direction of his eyes.

Myself and the witnesses of the back porch ufo spent the next two hours wide awake and frightened. After sunrise we were able to calm ourselves and go to sleep.
