{rjl} 20 red orange glowing objects flew overhead

I walked outside around 9:15PM on 07/26/2014 and saw 3-4 glowing orange red triangular? shaped objects moving smoothly and silently across the sky in a triangle formation. After they passed overhead another group of 4-5 moved across the sky in the same direction. I called my friend Bob who lives several blocks away to go outside and look at the sky as I watched a third group follow the same path as the others in his direction. While I talked to him he could see them pass overhead. I thought it was over when another group of lights moved across the sky in the same direction. After the last group flew over a single orange glowing light followed the others. I think there may have been at least of these lights.
