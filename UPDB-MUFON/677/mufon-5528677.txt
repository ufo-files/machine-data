Lemon Bay Satellites?

While star gazing as I do every night, I've noticed lights moving across the sky - as bright as a star, various directions. No unusual start-stop motions, but they do seem appear from nowhere and end the same way. They seem to flicker on, then off. Movement is in a straight line - but not a satellite or a plane. Wanted to report because it has been happening often; 2-3 times every night for the last 3 nights.
