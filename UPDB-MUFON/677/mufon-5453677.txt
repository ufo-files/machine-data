{rjl} Never seen anything like it before

A few houses on our block we’re having fires in our pits, and we observed lights appearing one after another after another appear out of thin air to the south of us. They were evenly spaced apart, moving in a straight line to the NE. So many. There appeared to be a large triangular craft also, completely transparent save the lights on each point. It was massive. Then the smaller ones continued to appear after, some moving unusually, some brightening and then dimming.
