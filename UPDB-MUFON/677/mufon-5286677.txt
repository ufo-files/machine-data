Started off as a stationary red light in the sky. Higher than a drone or helicopter.

While at my sister's home in Matteson, IL viewing fireworks on the fourth of July. I noticed a stationary red light high up in the sky to the west. Which could have made the lights be over the town of Frankfort, Tinley Park or Joilet. At times it looked to be a single red light at others it looked to be 3 red lights or three blueish/white lights. It sat stationary and unblinking at times. While at others it would blink. However when it moved was what made it extraordinarily unusual. It didn't so much fly as much as it seemed to zip to a different location. Not that I could estimate the speed it was traveling or the distance it covered. But it seemed as if it would be in one location. Then pulled to another location. Then pulled again to another location. It decended a couple times almost as if to be falling. Also it seemed to go away for several minutes only to reappear in approximately the same location. The total event lasted on and off between 30 minutes to an hour. Don't have much knowledge of drones. But again the height (thousands of feet) and movements were definitely not compatible with a helicopter or conventional aircraft. Myself and 2 or three other people witnessed this. Between 10 and 11 pm. I took video on my phone which isn't very clear mainly because of the distance of the lights
