drifting grey star dull stopped ,shooting star added and stopped next to other

April 1985 I was working corrosion control in my squadron, at 3 am I was done and went to the tarmac and climbed up on an airplane. I watched the stars and noticed a high altitude drifting balloon I thought, yet it drifted oddly and bounced back and forth, then stopped overhead, bobbing back and forth, it came from the north east. Then I saw a meteor coming from the southwest direction from the horizon to overhead in less than 3 seconds and stopped next to the other object, They oscillated together for a while, I got scared and ran back to the hanger.
