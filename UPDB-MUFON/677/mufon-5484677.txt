While taking a photograph of his friend in front of a mosque, upon review saw an alien grey type entity in photo

At exactly 5 pm on August 6, 2015 in front of the Mosque in the small rural village of Issoufari in Niger, the witness named Abdoul took a photo of his friend Nassar. Nassar is in a white shirt holding his shoes in photo. In front of him in the foreground of the photo approximately three feet from Abdoul is a three foot tall alien Grey type entity which seems to be vibrating as it stands in place (SEE PHOTO). The entity's legs are higher neae its shoulders while its arms are lower towards its abdomen and more centered on the torso.
