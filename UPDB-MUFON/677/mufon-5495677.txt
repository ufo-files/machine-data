{dr}observed 12 red orange non blinking lights moving across sky in random order without sound.

I was locking the patio door when I pushed back the curtain I saw a multitude of red lights in the sky(night).  I called my husband to come look.  We both counted the lights separately and both saw 12.  Red-orange colored round lights that did not blink.  The intensity did not change.  The pattern was a random one.  Two of the lights were very close together and then one of the two dropped down to the earth in a straight line.  No flashes or explosions were seen from this.   The lights moved in one direction going from the left to the right(we are facing south).  In a few minutes they were out of sight.  My Husband saw airplane lights to the right of this viewing.  We thought at first if might be a bunch of helicopters, but then the lack of sound and lack of blinking lights ruled that out. We were so amazed at the sight that we didn't think to try to take pictures!  We watched the lights till they were out of our site range.
