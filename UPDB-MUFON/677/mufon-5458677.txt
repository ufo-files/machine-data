{rjl} Converging lights

In roughly 1972-1974, when I was 8-10 years old, my parents and I lived in Buffalo, SC. It was very rural with a large, very dark sky. We witnessed many strange sightings, some of which were explainable - like NASA rocket launchings from Cape Canaveral, FL. But, one night, we observed 3 white lights that looked like very bright stars zoom together from different parts of the sky to form one light which paused then darted away in a different direction. My mom, dad and I were amazed. We all agreed on what we just saw. My guess is that the objects were around 20,000 ft in altitude. There was no sound.
