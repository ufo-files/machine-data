Oberved a Hovering disk that zigzaged 3 direction changes then took off in the blink of an eye.

My stepson and I were visiting a friend. Just south of Devonport Tasmania. As we were leaving I looked up to see a disk hovering. I told my stepson who looked at it as well. The disk was grey in colour slightly domed on top. It made an Instant move to to the left. Then back and above it first position. Then to the left again. After sitting in these positions for 1 or 2 seconds it would move again. The last move was SO fast it disappeared in front of our eyes. My stepson looked at me with his mouth wide open and asked me "What Was That" I replied I don't know its unexplained. We still talk about it when together and to close family and friends. In 1978 I was with my mother and sister at our family home in Devonport when my mother saw a strange object out to sea. My sister saw it but I didn't know what or where I was looking. I always wondered what it was. 2005 November I was on King Island Tasmania when an object was seen by many people and reported "crashing' into the ocean just of the shore of Curry. I believe that may have made the paper. I was sitting 1 Kilometer away when it happened and did not see it but started getting text messages from family asking me what was going on. I consider myself lucky to have seen that disk with my stepson. Because it was there we both seen it and can go on in life knowing They Are There!!! Thank you Mufon I need to tell this to someone.
