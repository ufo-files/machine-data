(gs) Saw Basketball SIzed glowing Orange ball in the sky moving very fast.

I was walking out on the back porch to go smoke a cigarette, when I looked above the tree tops, I saw this bright orange glowing ball flying in the sky lower than an airplane moving very NW. I called my husband and his friend out to the back yard to come see this. I noticed the object because how bright orange it was. I knew as soon as I seen it, it had to have been some type of UFO, because how low and fast it was moving. It was no way it had been a plane, as we see them fly over every night. The object did not dance, or disappear, but it just moving at a high speed in the same direction. I was actually very excited to see this object flying above us. I was just afraid of being abducted, as you never know. I lost site of the object was it was passing over near the trees. It only lasted about two minutes tops.
