I was between 8 to 12 years old. I was in the house making soap in a bottle to go outside and blow bubbles.the hydro had turned off. as I went outside I had noticed a u.f.o hovering above the gas station across the road where I had lived. there was a city bus stop right in front of my house at the time. and as the bus had drove by I did not even hear it. I don't remember if I was in a trance or not. but it felt like it.
