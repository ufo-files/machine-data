Very bright light over paoli, PA

I was driving to pick my wife up from the train station at 2:00PM on 11/15/11 and observed an incredibly bright light hovering in the distance. It looked like a car's highbeams would at night. I slowed down to observe it and noticed other drivers looking as well. I was able to observe it for a few minutes until I drove through a wooded area and lost it. It appeared to be at least 10x larger and brighter than any plane or star I have seen in the past.
