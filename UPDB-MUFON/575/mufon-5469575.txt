{rjl} Playing football with friend, both look up 2 bright lights diagonal to each other shoot across, red light trailing

Just friend and I. Playing football between two homes. 7:30ish at night and cold. While in the middle of tackling each other, we both look up see two white lights immediately above us (15-20 feet) with a red light trailing. It zoomed past in a parallel path over the roofs of all the homes on the street. The white lights were 10ish feet away from each other with a red light trailing maybe 20 feet behind. Very fast and absolutely silent. Friend and I instantly turn too each with scared expressions on our faces and then without saying a word bolt to our respective homes. 

In a weird way I don't remember the event as much as I remember my description of the event. I remember after seeing this I was petrified and described it to myself as a object that looked like a elongated shuriken. However it was dark so this description could be way off. I don't remember seeing the body of the craft but the lights and their positions made it seem like the shuriken description fit. I do remember with certainty the object had the two white lights and single red orb trailing.
