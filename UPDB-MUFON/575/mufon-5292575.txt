I REMEMBER IT LIKE IT WAS YESTERDAY, 11/11/1974 AT 11PM. WE WERE WORKING PICKING COTTON WITH A MACHINE TRYING TO GET DONE WHEN WE NOTICED A SHADOW IN THE SKY, IT BLOCKED OUT THE STARS. IT WAS HUGE AND SILENT. IT WAS TRAVELING AT ABOUT 20 MPH MAYBE 500' TO 1000' ABOVE US.
WE SAW THE DARK OUTLINE, IT WAS DARK COLORED, IT MAY HAVE HAD LIGHTS OR SMALL WINDOWS, COULDN'T TELL.
IT WAS A REALLY DARK SKY, NO MOON.
IT SEEMED TO BE COMING FROM SACREMENTO TO SAN DIEGO OR MAYBE EDWARDS AFB.
WE WERE ON HIGHWAY 152, NEAR TURNER ISLAND ROAD ON THE WEST SIDE IN A COTTON FIELD. 
I WAS IN THE NAVY ON THE AIRCRAFT CARRIER JOHN F KENNEDY, SO I KNEW IT WASN'T A KNOWN MILITARY CRAFT.
