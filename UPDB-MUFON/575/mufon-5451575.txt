Cigar spiraling with greenish lights

While working as outdoor security I saw a strange light appear traveling faster than anything I’ve seen. A cigar shaped object spiraling in on itself like a Mobius strip at the ends, leading into blackness. The middle had what looked like a gray or black straight rectangular support. And outside of that on both sides it looked almost like a chamber with glowing greenish color. Almost liquid like in the way it moved. It had to have been over 500 feet away but moved insanely quick. It was not like a shooting star and definitely not like any aircraft I’ve seen.
