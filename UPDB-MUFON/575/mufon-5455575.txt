{tjd} Noticed outside of bedroom window, over open soybean field. Very scary.

On 2/5/2020 at approximatley 2200 hrs, while laying in bed upstairs in the west facing bedroom, I was reading when I noticed a strange looking light coming from the air above a 20 acre soybean field just outside the window. The light appeared to be pulsating, fading in and out, and stationary. After getting out of bed and standing at the window, I then observed the object fall directly from the sky down to what i believe is under 500 ft. After dropping rapidly, the light then moved to the North in a direct path, and covered what i believe to be two miles or so, in a matter of a second. During my observation the light moved rapidly traveling North to South, and then back to the North very rapidly. During this time I could see other aircraft lights several thousand feet above the light, as there is a landing path for Charlotte Airport directly above our home. This was in fact not related and did not appear to be moving anything like an aircraft, as it covered so much ground in a speed that no airplane I know of could have traveled. The incident lasted approximatley 2 minutes. The last sighting of the light, was as it was headed back South and elevating out of sight. I did not observe a landing, nor did i observe the actual craft, only a pill or tic-tac shaped light, that appeared to be maybe 30-50 feet in length.
