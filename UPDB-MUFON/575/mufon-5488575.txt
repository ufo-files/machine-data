{jpg} BRIGHT SPLIT SECONDED WHITE FLASH WITH 2 SEC LIGHT GLOW.

AT 20:15 HRS I WAS AT WORK WALKING A PARKING PLATFORM WITH A GOOD VIEW OF THE SACRAMENTO SKY LINE.I WAS WACHING FLIGHT PATH OF PLANES FLYING NW OUT OF SAC INTERNATIONAL FLYING EAST OVER MTS,WHEN I THOUGHT I SAW A FLASH OF LIGHT FROM OTHER AIRCRAFT.I THEN SPOTTED A FAINT GREEN LIGHT MOVIN FROM NW TO SE,THEN I THOUGHT I SAW A FLASH OF LIGHT AGIN TO CORNER OF MY EYE,I LOST TRACK OF FAINT GREEN LIGHT WHITCH I THINK WAS A SATELLITE.I SAW LIGHT AGIN AND FIXATED ON THAT AREA OF SKY,ONLY TO TO SEE A BRIGHT SPLIT SEC FLASH OF WHITE LIGHT THEN IT WOULD GO OUT BLACK. THEN IT WOULD LIGHT BACK UP TO A LIGHT 2 SEC GLOW. WITH EACH FLASH IT WOULD MOVE.THEN SAW A 747 OUT OF S.F PASS BY GOING NORTH. ONEC PLANE PAST BY IT LIGHT UP FOR A SPLITE SEC AND STARTED TO MOVE INTO PLANES JET WASH AND TAILL 747.THE FLASHING LASTED 25 TO 30 MIN UNTILL IT LEFT BE HIND 747.
