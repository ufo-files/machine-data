I was traveling along 3300 North, coming from Burley and heading towards Murtaugh to drop off a relative at home. I was in the backseat and noticed a bright object moving over the South Hills. I wanted to try getting the attention of the other three people in the vehicle but the roads were rough die to the snowstorms, I didn't want to risk a crash and everyone else in the vehicle requires glasses (convenient excuse I know, but those are the facts). My initial thought was a regular aircraft that was reflecting light from the sunset. It traveled in a curve, coming roughly SSW and moved North, and suddenly stopped in the air. It was hard to gauge the distance but the craft had moved close enough for me to realize it was in the shape of a disc. The disc then flipped over at about a 150 degree angle before disappearing. I first noticed the craft at 5:03 pm, and the sighting lasted roughly 4 minutes.
