Camping in back yard with my father.

I was about 6 years old and it was around my birthday Aug 21 1996 I loved camping so my dad set up a little camp site in our back yard. And in the middle of the night I wake up terrified from a bright lights I wake up my dad and we both saw 10-15 circle like objects going left to right shooting across the sky coming to a halting stop then shooting upwards this went on for little over an hour and from that point on both me and my dad have no idea what happen. My mom found both of us on our kitchen floor laying down all over our sleeping bags and pillows are outside she wakes us up like why are you on the floor and my dad had no memory of why we were there or why he didn’t bring me to my bedroom for bed. 

That same year 1-2 days before Christmas I wake up with a pounding headache and a very high fever and I was diagnosed with tumor behind my right eye. And on my right hand middle finger I have a unexplained bump that was never there before. My sister(who was not part of this) also got that same bump about 2 years after on the same hand and same finger.

I am now turn 31 the bump is still there. I continue to get constant bad headaches l, random nose bleeds, bad dreams

My father got diagnosed with MS a few years after, lost his teeth and had multiple strokes after that night. And now lives in a nursing home. 

To this day me and my dad talk about it with each other trying to figure out what exactly happened that day. But we were always to afraid to report it.
