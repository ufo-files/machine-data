I was in my car coming back from grocery store. This object was in view out my front windshield. I had not idea what it was. I looked up and it looked like 3 large silver balls. It was daylight around 12 o'clock noon so I couldn't tell if they were lights or not. There seemed to be something around the top of them. Out the corner of my eye I could see something else darker off to the left side a good ways off. I was driving, so I had to look back down at my driving. I looked back up in a few seconds and it was gone. I looked out my side window and out my back window. It had disappeared!  I arrived home in about 3 min., I got out of the car and looked all over the sky but could not see it anywhere. This is the weirdest thing that has ever happened to me. I'm sure I won't forget it anytime soon and will keep asking around to see if anyone else in the area saw it.
