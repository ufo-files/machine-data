Bright light with pulsating plasma ball shoots off to the North

December 15, 2020
Kelly and I arrive today in Panama City Florida and sat in the hot tub this Tuesday evening. While watching the stars and a few satellites fly by I noticed a white dot that was roughly 15 miles to the east and perhaps 5 to 8000 feet AGL. Over and above Tyndall Air Force Base. The white dot was sitting still but above it attached to its right was a pulsating, glowing, bright baby blue plasma ball. All of the sudden the white dot shot north 0 to 1000 mph in one second. The dot continued out of sight behind the building where we were staying. It appeared as I would expect a typical anti-gravity anti-inertia flying device would from our US government or an alien race.
