Bright light traveled in 4 directions after stopping at each. No sound heard.

The observation occurred over Wahpeton, ND on a clear dark night in July 1974.
I observed a bright white light off and to my right. This light was traveling from my right (south) and towards me where it stopped directly in front or above of me. The light then traveled up or to the east away from me where it stopped again. The light then traveled to my left or to the north where it stopped again. The light then traveled from my left (North) to my right (South) and out of sight.
  At no time did I hear any sounds such as an airplane or helicopter coming from the area of the lights or see any light changes to the object such as aircraft's lighting.
