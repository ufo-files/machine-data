Triangular group of lights seen approximately at 11PM

Last Friday, July 2nd at approximately 11pm EST my wife and I saw a triangular group of lights flying at a steady pace from north to south in northern Indiana. It flew from horizon to horizon and we both commented about how it didn't look like any aircraft that we normally see. The lights were on steady, not blinking and all were white. I was looking for the usual alternating lights that you see on an airplane's wings but didn't see them nor did I see any sign of tail lights. There wasn't any sound that either of us could hear either.

Unfortunately I wasn't able to get any photos. The object was visible for approximately 30 seconds. Due to the light pollution in our area I wouldn't think that this was a group of satellites in polar orbit.
