{cak} Went to have a cigarette outside, looked up, and saw a light that shouldn't have been there.

Was outside taking a cigarette break when I saw something out of place in the night sky. It had just rained and no stars could be seen as there was lingering cloud coverage from the passing storm. The light source remained stationary for about an hour before the light vanished.

When I first saw it I thought it was a star. It was only after I realized I couldn't see any other stars that it hit me that what I was looking at was something else.

The object remained stationary the entire time.

Emotionally I was not phased by this. This is something I have seen regularly in the Pensacola area, but the first time I actually had my phone/camera on my person to get visual proof.
