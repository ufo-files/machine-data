{nd} red dot like lights in no order

The lights were about the size of a golf ball. Some were alone some in the form of a triangle and no sound admitted. The objects came along in a steam that lasted about 8 mins and were in small groups. Some objects had red lights only. some objects in pairs and others in triangle formation. I remember feeling scared and also interested. I tried to awake my neighbors no one answered. I then got scared I thought I was going to be abducted. I went inside my home and turned on the news hoping for an explanation. At first, I thought the lights were flares but they stayed in a forward motion and never wavered from a straight path. I lost sight of all the red orbs when they were about 100 feet from my range of sight. I was home when this happened at 215 North hickory Avenue. The fact that I was unable to arouse my neighbors to witness it with me is what bothers me the most. I had gone outside for a smoke. It was very scary and interesting at the same time. I thought I was crazy when no one would get up or answer their door to help me. now I feel I’m targeted because of what I saw.
