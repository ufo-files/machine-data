I noticed some lights in the sky moving as I was going out of a store parking lot. I stopped to film them for a few minutes but I was in a hurry and couldn1t stay long. I also could not tell if the lights were going to show up in the video because they were a good distance from me and I couldn1t see them well on my phone screen while filming. Sometimes five lights were visible, sometimes six. They went in different directions, some seemed to hover and some just moved horizontally back and forth from my view. One light went away from all the others and dropped down significantly lower. It is worth noting that the lights were in the direction of a small, local airport and I wondered if this could be a night jump for skydivers but was unable to find any information about that.
