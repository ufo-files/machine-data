{dr}Large Black Triangle Hovering Just Below Clouds Over Garage - No Sound

My partner and I were outside of our garage, talking with our neighbor, when my partner pointed to the sky and asked me, "What the hell is that?" Hovering just below the clouds, we observed a large solid black triangle, just hovering... no sound at all. It looked as though it may have had a round light, or dome in the center. We all had the distinct feeling it was watching us. It remained totally motionless... no movement at all. No visible means of any propulsion. A thunder storm was just beginning to develop and we were worried that the craft may get hit by lightning but it did not... it also continued to remain silent and motionless as the winds picked up. After about 15-20 minutes, the craft began to move slowly and silently, in a westerly direction, until it disappeared from view. Still totally silent.
