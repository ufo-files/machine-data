{jpg} 3rd Bend, OR Sighting Today - Similar/Different

Clear skies, sunny, moderate to light winds, air traffic. I was looking E in the same direction and general area as the previous two reports from earlier today: 101001 and 101002. I observed a similar orb-like object that was intermittently flashing a white-blue light. This object appeared closer than the previous two sightings and so I grabbed a pair of binoculars through which I could clearly make-out an object with defined, linear edges that was either shifting in shape or rotating while also flashing white-blue, brilliant lights. The object would flash in and out of view (appear, disappear, reappear) and when it did, it was always in a slightly different position. The duration the object would emit light v. not v. disappear all varied. I can say for an absolute fact that it was not a mylar balloon or anything similar. This was a moving, light-emitting/flashing and disappearing/reappearing object. I selected "square/rectangular" above because at several moments, it appeared in that shape.
