Bright orange pulsing orb viewed over Red Hill Brisbane

I was a passenger in my boyfriends car driving along Waterworks Road from the north towards Red Hill when he noticed a strange glowing object in the sky. I looked and immediately noticed that it was something weird- it didn't appear to be a plane or helicoper, although it looked like it was flying at low helicopter altitude. It was pulsing with bright orange light (no flashing lights like youd see on an aircraft). And appeared orb shaped. We attempted to follow it, and as we turned off the main road onto Windsor rd then down Victoria street, it changed direction so that it was ahead of us. We pulled over to the side of the road and then watched as it appeared to gain altitude until it disappeared.
I definitely got an otherworldly vibe from it! This was no ordinary aircraft.
