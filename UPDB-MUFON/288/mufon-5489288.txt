{nd} Greenish orb hovering above military bpat for 45 minutes. Sharply turned, dropped, ascended and moved unlike a man made craft

I was sitting on the boardwalk of Jaycee beach very late at night with a friend of mine. We noticed a boat on the horizon. At first we thought it was a cruise ship but realized it was shorter and the lights on the ship were varied. It was at this time I noticed a green glowing light above the ship. It looked like a dim star. It ascended a few hundred feet in less than a second. A few minutes later it dropped, and zig zagged it's way up again. It seemed to move with the ship until it was out of sight
