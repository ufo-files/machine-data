{tjd} my wife and I were out skywatching. While recording a particularly bright object an amazing 18 second video was created.

3/29/20 
10:20 p.m estd

Sunday night at our home in Clay County FL we were enjoying a night sky gazing in the back yard passing the binoculars between us.. We always try to move around and get the best view of whatever catches our eyes. While we watched another busy night proceed to take place we ended up on the side of the house. On this night it was a bright shine towards the east that was stealing the show. After gazing at the object that seemed to be floating in the air my wife pulls out the I phone to start recording and snap a few pics. I assumed that she was filming the bright object but i was wrong. Come to find out i couldn't have seen what she did if i tried all night long. The bright object was by itself the entire time we placed eyes on it. After returning back inside i realized exactly why my wife was so excited while recording.. This event happened so fast my naked eyes never seen the flashing light and or images that appear to be flashing in the video.
