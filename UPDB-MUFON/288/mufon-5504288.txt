{cak} I saw a series of low altitude lights in a sequence hovering above the ground at night. I took video.

It was at night. Probably somewhere about 9:00 pm. I was driving to the grocery store in Twentynine Palms, CA. As I got to the corner... I could see approx. 5 bright lights in the sky... like in a line. I thought that odd since that is not the norm. Then I thought to get my camera out. I filmed a little bit of it and then turned the corner and parked in the parking lot of the grocery store I was headed to. I grabbed my camera and aimed them at the lights. This time, more lights appeared. They went from straight lines to a formation line up. They stayed that way for a while and then they faded out one by one. As the lights were disappearing... I could have sworn I heard one or more airplane/helicopter in the area. I saw a flashing red light in the sky only. And it was very faint. The sky had been cloudy as usually you can see a lot of stars out. That's it!
