Three wavy triangles comprised of orange lines, a faint light and an area of blackness.

On the evening of the 2nd November 2021 I was closing my bedroom curtains when I noticed something in the sky. The object was west of my position at an elevation angle of approximately 45 degrees. It was a very clear, crisp evening and the stars were clearly visible. The object was comprised of three wavy triangles comprised of orange lines. Their shape and motion brought to mind a three masted schooner sailing on a calm sea, if the ship had orange lines for sails and you couldn't see the masts or hull. To the right of these was a faint light tracking in parallel to the orange triangles. Between the triangles and the light there seemed to be an area of perceived blackness. The objects direction of travel was from West to North East. It's speed was approximately the same as light aircraft I have observed over the years. When I lost sight of the object because it went out of sight beside the house, I went through to a rear bedroom to see it appear again. I waited for 5 or 6 minutes and it didn't appear. I estimated that this should have been enough time as I had judged it's speed. I have lived in this property for sixty years and have never seen any aircraft on this flight path.
