Large bright object headed into a wispy cloud and did not come out the other side

As reported to ASD by phone on 6/8/2020: I was outside in my back yard looking at the moon when I noticed a very slight wisp of a cloud over the moon.There were no other clouds in the sky and I thought that was odd. I then noticed a very bright large object headed from NW to S at less than 5,000 feet and at first I thought it was a plane but it had no red or green lights. It was all white, and larger than a commercial aircraft,and it was so bright that I could not see any structural features. I wanted to get my phone to take video but was afraid that it would be gone by the time I came back so I just watched it trying to figure out what it was. It then moved into the wispy cloud in front of the moon and did not come out the other side - it just disappeared into the cloud. At that point I realized that what I saw was not something of ours and that it might have been an extraterrestrial craft going into a portal or worm hole. I just wanted to tell someone about it and don't want any publicity.
