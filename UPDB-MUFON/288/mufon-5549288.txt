LANDED IN OUR POND IN THE AFTERNOON TUESDAY JUNE 27

FIVE OF OUR GRANDCHILDREN WERE FISHING IN OUR POND ABOUT 3:00 P.M. THE NEXT DAY WE DROVE AROUND THE POND TO SEE WHERE THEY HAD SEEN THE SPLASH, NOT KNOWING WHAT WE WOULD FIND AND WE WERE AMAZED TO FIND TRACKS LIKE TRUCK TRACKS THAT LED TO THE POND BUT BEFORE IT GOT TO THE POND THE TRACKS STOP AND THERE WAS A CIRCLE WHERE THE GRASS HAD BEEN LAID DOWN THEN IT MUST HAVE BEEN AIR BORNE BUT THE GRASS AT THE EDGE OF THE POND HAD BEEN BENT DOWN WHERE IT MUST HAVE PASSED AS IT SPLASHED IN THE POND. THE STRANGE THING ABOUT THE TRACKS WAS THAT THE GRASS LAID DOWN THE WRONG WAY FROM THE WAY GRASS NORMALLY FALLS DOWN WE FOLLOWED THE TRACKS UNTIL WE CAME TO WHERE THEY ENDED AND THERE WAS GRASS LAID DOWN IN AN ARC SHAPE. THIS HAPPENED IN OUR HAY FIELD THAT HADN'T BEEN HAYED YET. THE CHILDREN HAD NO IDEA WHAT THEY HAD SEEN BUT THEY WERE VERY UPSET.
