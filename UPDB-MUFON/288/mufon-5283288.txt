Flashing multicolored object

For the last month I saw an object for three times. First time it was on 17th of December when I set on the windowsill at home and looked at the sky. It was evening between 18:20-18:40. Firstly I saw an unclear object which flew counterclock-wise on the horizon. I realized that it’s possibly a terrestrial object. It might be some scout. Then I looked up at the sky and where I watched appeared dull flying and maneuvering object for a moment. It was in about 3-4 seconds. 

Second time I saw an object on December 28th and as before I set on windowsill and looked at the sky. On the horizon appeared «a terrestrial object» again flying on the horizon. And then when I looked more up suddenly from nowhere appeared a bright object iridescent in different colors and constantly flashing. It moved chaotically and while it flashed it appeared in another place. That was the way it moved. Sometimes it stopped and hovered in the sky. It wasn’t too bright (might be because of the city sky), but it flashed strongly and brightly. I observed it for about 5 minutes and then it disappeared by flashing. 

Third time I saw an object on January 4th 2022. The same as second time, but on the horizon before this object I didn’t see anyone. This object also flashed and iridescent in different colors. It was seen worse. I could even say barely. Maybe the reason is because it was too far and urban sky light also play it own role. I observed it for 3 minutes and then it disappeared. I couldn’t catch it on my camera not once. At the time when I took the camera the object stopped being visible.
