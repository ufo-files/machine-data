Craft shaped like helicopter.

The craft flew directly over us. Shaped like a helicopter with no rotors or wing/pods. Silent. 6 lights underneath. The two at the rear flashing. All white lights, no colors. Craft was gray or black and close enough to see its outline. The craft passed over us and continued west into the forrest. I am former military and am very familiar with traditional air frames. This was not any aircraft I have ever seen.
