{rjl} I saw a triangle in the sky along side of full moon and went vertically in the sky.

I was leaving my apartment and there was a full moon, if I recall correctly, January (a winter month in 1977 or 78), when I saw what looked like airplane white line in the sky. Except, it would have been 100 times as wide as an airplane's. It was a triangle and it was coming over a small mountain ridge. Instead of going across the sky like an airplane, it went directly vertically out of the sky and the rear of the triangle had a great deal of flashing lights in different colors. The next day,I went to visit my Father while he covered a Saturday morning shift at the County jail. I told him that I saw something last night that I have never seen before and told him what I saw. He picked up the Harrisburg newspaper and showed me where someone in the Harrisburg area saw the same thing and a drawn picture of it. I am looking for the article or information about that paper and to this date, 02/23/2019, I have had no success. It was something I never saw before and I can say to me it was a UFO. I have been encourage to write to you as a result of the special on the History Channel.
