SEVERAL PAIRS OF LIGHTS

Let my dog out around 4:30 and notice a string of light off in the distance. They seemed to be just APPEARING out of thin air. (I saw no cloud cover) They were moving slowly and fairly close together, starting from North of my house and heading up and to the East.
They then took their turn Disappearing.
After several seconds I attempted to record them and had no luck, just me asking what they are.
I have seen Missile and Rocket launches for decades, living in San Diego County. They have NEVER been in that area of the sky, tending to be more directly over my house, coming from the North West and heading South East.
