{cak} 9pm hovered, 10min in got bright changed red-orange 15 min in changes green 20 min in White 25 min heads west

July 16th 2015. A few mins before 9 I was laying in bed watching tv with my cat in my bedroom on the second floor of my house. The sun was starting to set. My cat went up to the window resting.I went to pet her and noticed a bright light in the Sky. Thought it was odd something so bright when no other stars were out. It started to get brighter.I noticed it didn't move for five min. So I grabbed my cellphone and started filming. During filming I got a little shaky and filmed the best I could. Called out to my aunt down stairs which you can hear in the video. The video lasted 3 mins because my phone died. A min or so after my phone died my aunt came up stairs and we both were surprised and a little shaken. The object got brighter and hovered. I told her keep watching it and I am going to grab my binoculars from the next room. As I looked through I noticed it was oval in shape and had three to four twinkling/pulsating lights. As we passed back and forth the binoculars, the white lights turned a red-orange color and stayed like that for about the minutes. Next brightened white and turned green-blue similar to an old Christmas light bulb. This lasted for about three mins as well before it flared back to white. After min 20 in the blink of an eye out shoot to my right (north). My aunt and I heard and saw a black helicopter with a red flashing light zoom from our left bottom corner of the window (south east). The helicopter disappeared from sight. The orb/light (s) moved further in the distance. By this time the sun was completely down. Noticed the orb/light get further away. A little after 9:30pm we couldn't see anything.
