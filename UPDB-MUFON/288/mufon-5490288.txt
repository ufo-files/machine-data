{cak} Bright amber sphere moving across sky, followed by blinking American aircraft. Straight path, continuously became larger. Blinking light seems to intercept amber light.

Quincy california, sitting outside on our deck. A friend brought to my attention something strange was happening in the sky. I've never seen anything like it. I though maybe it was a Satelite at first, but then I realized it was moving way too fast and it was a much brighter light. While we watched the amber sphere move across the sky a blinking light (aircraft) came up on it very swiftly and closed the gap within a minute or so. It also seemed as though the amber craft got closer to the ground as all of us could see multiple lights In it .Both the light and aircraft continued across the sky Until we could no longer see them.
