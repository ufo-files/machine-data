{rjl} Strange object in sky

My sister and I were coming home from our business. I had what looked like a very odd ball of fire catch my eye in the sky. It looked like a ball that was flat on one side. It looked like it was shooting fire off the sides. It would get bigger than smaller. It disappeared for about two minutes. Then it came back. My sister even took a video of it with her cell phone. That video does not catch even what we saw with our eyes.
