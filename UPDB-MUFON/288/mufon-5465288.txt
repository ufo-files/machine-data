Large bright light appeared in night sky.

I was on my back deck looking over the roof of my home at the night sky, when a large bright light suddenly came on, it looked like It was over the field at Esquimalt high school. The light was stationary, then silently the light moved slowly from NE to N approx 100 feet to the ball park. The light stopped, and then shot straight up until it was out of sight. No sound, no emissions.

I felt anxious as I could not get my head around what I saw. It really bothered me to know that I could not see anything in the sky prior to the light coming on.
