Fireball in the sky glidding over North Phoenix

As we were driving west on Thunderbird from 19th Ave, something caught my eye in the sky the flight pattern looked as if it was flying east over Thunderbird Ave. as we got closer I seen a 2nd Fireball appear, I rolled down the window and asked my daughter to get a clearer view see if it was a plane. We both say it looked like a fireball she continued to look at the object I proceeded to drive west on Thunderbird. Turned into my neighborhood looked for an opening in the trees and seen one Fireball heading North, I don't know what happened to the other Fireball. but the one I followed faded away into the distance. This object was fling closer than the planes. It did not appear to be big maybe the size of a volkswagen in the shape of a fireball. It seemed to be pulsating as well
