Huge, disc-like object, flying in a hilly area. 9/1963, no noise, lights all around & one underneath. Witnessed by myself & 2 others.

We were in a vehicle about 1 mile from my home, surrounded by rolling hills. We watched as long as we could see it, but couldn't follow because it disappeared behind a hill.  I was 16 or 17. There were no news stories or films. Reported at school, people laughed, told me it was the "Pepsi Plane". NOT A PLANE!!!  At that time, there were no technical items (cell phones, ipads, digital cameras, etc.)

I've lived with this for 60+ years, not knowing if I should even speak. UFOS have been seen since & videos seen on TV.

No Media was involved. It did not exist in 1963-64.
