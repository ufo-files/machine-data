Took off and left atmosphere

I lived in Thailand for 5 years. I enjoyed sitting on my balcony that had a panoramic view of the city. I was not under any flight path. I was across the street from the British and American embassy I actually saw several UFO's while living there. But this one stood out. It was the middle of the afternoon and I walked into my balcony with a cup of tea. I looked to my right and a metalic blue diamond shaped craft took off from what must have been the on nut area of the city and quickly went up and out of the visible atmosphere. What struck me was how normal an exercise it seemed to be. I actually thought to myself... They forgot to turn their cloaker on. 

I have several UFO's events from my time there and one possible abduction... But while I remember seeing the craft and light I have no memory of what happened. I awoke in my bed. But there was a little evidence, when I awoke I was holding a night mask over my eyes as if I'd been shielding my eyes from a bright light... Only it was still dark outside.
