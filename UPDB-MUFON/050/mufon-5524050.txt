5 LIGHTS IN STRAIGHT LINE THEN 1 BY 1 WENT STRAIGHT UP

My son called from 1 mile away, told us to look into the sky. we saw 5 red lights in a straight line. to close together to be airplanes. they were heading west bound one made a 45 degree turn south. they all at one point went straight up until lights disappeared. they next day our neighbor said they saw the same thing.
