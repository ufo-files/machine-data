{rjl} A round brushed silver saucer-like craft like the one in the movie

My friend and I were fishing and shooting doves at Lake Pleasant northwest of Phoenix Arizona on a Saturday afternoon when we heard what we thought were doves taking off from behind us. We put down our fishing poles and picked up our shotguns, turned around and saw to our surprise a 'flying saucer' just like the typical kind shown in the b-rated movies. It was a silver colored disc with tapered edges and a spherical dome on top and bottom. The craft kept rising and the sound got higher in frequency as it rose to a height of about 400 feet above the ground. My friend and I just stood there and could not move or raise our shotguns to maybe fire upon it. We were to scared to move at all. The craft then zoomed off in a southeast direction so fast it was out of sight in about two seconds flat. 
J.J, and I looked at each other and started jumping all around and yelling across the cove to the people and saw that three of them who were also very excited at seeing this object zip overhead. We put out shotguns and fishing poles in the truck and got the hell out of dodge as fast as we could. I was 19 at the time and J.J. was 17. We rarely spoke of this to anyone for fear of ridicule and being shamed. I have been listening to Coast to Coast since Art Bell and finally want to share this with all you folks. Thanks for your time. KK
