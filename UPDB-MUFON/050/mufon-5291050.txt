Witnessed a craft moving above the street.

At app. 9pm I was looking for my wife (who was gardening). I stepped onto the back deck and looked into the sky above my street. 
I observed a circular craft app. 50 to 100 ft across at an altitude of app. 80 to 100 ft above the roadway. 
I was frozen, just staring at the object that moved very slowly and silently in a straight line above the road. 
There was no lights or features that I could see. 
I ran into the house yelling for my wife but got no answer. 
I opened the front door and I could see the craft moving slowly in a straight line. 
There was a woman pushing a stroller right under the craft and vehicles driving by. I did not think to shout to them. 
My phone at the time was a blackberry and I did not get any pictures. 
I didn't know who to call. 
This was one of two craft that I seen up close. 
I have also seen two other objects (lights) at a distance.
