{jpg} Blinking, hovering lights for over an hour.

Watching T V, I always look out at the sky. This night I saw a hovering light down the valley, blinking red blue and green lights. It hung there for 20 minutes. I called my wife to look and she saw the same thing. It would dance around in a small area. Other very small lights at a good speed passed by it and at one point 3 of them headed toward it and faded away. We could see airplane lights pass by, but this was not one of them. We left the room for a minute, and when we returned there was another one, looking exactly the same, only bigger because it was closer, approximately less than a mile away, and hanging over the mountain across the valley right above the trees. The first one was still up there further down the valley. We went to get a phone to call my brother for verification, but he was not in position to see, and when returning to the window, the one above the trees was gone. Occasionally the cloud cover would part and we could see stars. This was nothing like them. It stayed in that area for over an hour, then gradually fade into an orange color as if it was moving away from us. This was nothing we have ever seen in our sky before. I believe it was a craft of some kind and the closer one definitely was also. I swear this to be the truth.
