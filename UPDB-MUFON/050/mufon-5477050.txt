My Uncle was personal bodyguard to Harry S Truman in 1947. They both went to Roswell at insistence of POTUS Truman in July 1947 after sneeking out of White House disguised as Press. My uncle said they met a Major Easley at crash site. Major Easley promised the President that nothing from the crash would remain.
