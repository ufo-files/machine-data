(GS) bright yellow/orange lights over sw valley

was at work at the phx sky harbor airport, looked south south west of phoenix near south mountain/estrella mountains saw two bright yellow lights much brighter than aircraft but did not seem flare like they were oriented side by side then faded, rebrightened dissappeared then brightened then dimmed and gone for 15 or so minutes

then the two lights reappeared same area maybe a little more east. this time the lights were at an angle from each other / like that but still side by side just one a little higher than the other. then after about 5 minutes dimmed and disappeared like the first set.

5 or ten minutes after those lights the lights reappeared only this time there were three sets, same color and intensity two right next to each other and one slightly higher and to the right of the other two dimmed brightened and dimmed again then changed direction in relation to each other then stayed at the same altitude this time they disappeared and did return the rest of the night..

Ive seen flares they dimmed like flares, but they did not drop, and flares do not usually move in a lateral direction.. or around each other there were no aircraft in the area except for normal commercial traffic whcih were at the time landing from west to east. 

if you were in the area of my vantage point the airport they would have been viewed west of the tv towers on south mountain, they appeared to be much further away than the towers, but in relation to them much brighter.
