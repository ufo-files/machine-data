{cak} First seen 10/16 seen again 10/17 at 6am

I went to lay down around 5:30-6 am in the morning when I turned over in bed towards the window there was an extremely bright light shining through. I thought it was a bit strange so I got up and investigated it and I noticed a craft of some sort just hovering there. There was also another smaller one just below it. The craft would sort of change in color and look. It had a reddish/orange glow and a white light on the bottom. The lights would change in brightness every so often. I was really creeped out so I called my parents into the room and they saw it too. It hovered around for about an hour then slowly ascended to where I couldn't see it anymore. I looked again tonight and it is back and it looks even closer. The white light on the bottom brightened up for a second then it dimmed a little. The crafts almost look like sail boats.
