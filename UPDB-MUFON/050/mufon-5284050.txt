Green orb morphing into a spaceship

Was taking photos of this amazing sunset with my wife yesterday Nov 22 at around 4pm and haven't noticed anything unusual with the naked eye, but after reviewing a couple of shots we noticed this bright green orb hovering low in the sky towards west and above power lines. We continued taking more shots and we saw that the orb was changing positions being now higher in the sky, but again, nothing was visible to naked eye, only on camera. When it reached ~45 degrees altitude we saw to our amazement that the orb has morphed into what appears to be a spaceship, something out of the Independence Day, the one Will Smith piloted towards mothership, "the big tamale". 
High elongated dome in the middle with lower rounded wings profile all around, with a slit at the back between them, like a horseshoe. It's also resembling a little bit to Millennium Falcon, but upside down. No noise and no stream jet. Couldn't find this shape anywhere in the UFO shape database online. Grey/greenish in colour and estimating it to be about 250-300 ft across. In the first three photos enclosed (ones with the green orb) can see clearly an airplane ascending from mid low center to top right, for size comparison. Couldn't hear a noise from the plane either, so it must have been at least 10 miles away.
Identical pictures of the orb were taken with both mine (Huawei P20) and my wife's (Google Pixel 4) phones, so there's very little chance for a trick of light.
