I was walking dogs and noticed A bright light in the sky. It was not moving then it moved very rapidly to the south than became stationary again. The distance was prob several miles to the south Then it left at very rapid speed to the South and then Disappered. I came inside and a friend of mine from Lummi Reservation .Sent me a message on face book that he had just seen something move in a southern direction at a High Rate of speed. He thought it was a falling star maybe. I went outside with biconlars and looked for it again. I saw a second object same size to the north and as I watched it it moved to the south at a very high rate of speed Looked triangle to me with white lights on top blue lights on bottom.
