On the morning of april 13th 2016 at 10:12 am I went out too my back yard to let my dog out for the morning here in Warwick.R.I. I was outside for just a few mins as I looked up at jet leaving 4 vapor trails maybe a 747 at about 30 thousand ft straight over my head on a clear sunny morning . I live on the east coast and I see most of the international flights coming from europe fly over us here in new england so I am always looking up when I go outside. I looked down at my dog for just a few seconds then I went to check out the plane again and as I look back up I see something just over the treeline in my backyard at about 500 feet.(The trees are aprox 20 ft from where I am standing at this moment and they are about 50 ft tall with no leaves on them yet) then this red orange almost straightened boomerang looking object was hovering with a slight wobble not making a sound. at first I thought it was a hawk riding the wind with his wings out but this was to wide to be a hawk and the I noticed a small bright spot on the middle on the outside edge of this thing like if the morning sun reflecting off it. so at this point I knew it wasn't a hawk so I watched this object for about 3 min trying to see if it was a mylar balloon or something like that but I could see it was too big to be that . besides it was hovering like riding the wind and not going up and still not making a sound ,so then I ran inside my house to grab my camera that took about 2 min and when I went back outside it was gone. and the feeling I had was anger because i couldn't get it on film and then I stayed outside for an hr to see if I see it again but I didn't.
