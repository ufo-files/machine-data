Me and my son were working at a haunted hayride in northeast pa when another worker in out scene area said quick look up what it that? The circular object with some kind of shaped appendages sticking off the sides made absolutley no noise and had no visible afterburners or glowing fuel source as it flew ahead of the jet as if being chased.
