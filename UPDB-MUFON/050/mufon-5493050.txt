{cak} Traingle with white light

At 515am I started my drive to work on Lummi Reservation at Silver Reef Casino. As I ready to leave my Driveway. I noticed a bright light coming form the East. I was stopped in my drive way. The Object stooped over head and a light came over head. I took off on my motorcycle to the west . It seemed to Follow me. I turned north and the object stayed with me it seemed. The light stayed on me as I turned  to the west again and increased speed to 50mph the light and Object stay with me. I increased my speed to 70mph and it stayed with me till I turned onto the driveway at work. As I stopped at work parking lot ..it was gone... No noise I could hear....Traingle shaped....Then it was gone. No ill effects.....
