At about 9:0pm on July 19th, 2020, I spotted an object in the sky heading NNE over the upper end of Moore Lake in North Littleton N.H.- traveling at a speed similar to watching the ISS pass overhead... this object was constantly emitting a bright reddish (maybe pinkish) color and about double the size of the brightest evening star. There were no other lights apparent - flashing or otherwise. I could not hear any sound typical of a passing jet. I called loudly to my housemate Gail, and she responded quickly enough to view it before it went behind the trees - seeming to be passing over Dalton and/or Gilman, VT which is North of us... We were under a thick overcast and I have no clue as to the cloud ceiling, but it was pretty low. I'd venture a guess of between 5 and 7k feet. Lastly, no visible con-trail. It looked quite like a red flare, but it's steady, straight line of travel indicated propulsion. I have served as an Emergency Management Director in a small town, and as a weather info contributor to our local grid, and I am intimately familiar with most all types of normal aerial phenomenon. I have posted a queery to a local Facebook page asking for response from any other observer in my area... we'll see what happens.
