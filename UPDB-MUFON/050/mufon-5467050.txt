{cak} Objects in the sky. Some stationary some moving

My wife first noticed an unusual object through bedroom window late evening. She woke me to point it out. We both went to the patio with binoculars. We saw a bright twinkling object that did not appear to be a star. Every once in awhile it would flash. Looking at it through the binoculars, it appeared to be rotating rapidly giving off red, green, blue, and white colors. We first noticed these objects in early April but still see them to this day. At the beginning we were quite excited to see them and would go out as soon as it was dark enough to see them. Now we watch them and wonder if we are being observed back. I'm no astronomer by any stretch but have tried to identify them with known stars or star patterns. I did some poor video of them and took a few still photos. As the days went by, the objects appeared to get smaller but are still identifiable. At various times they will disappear from one location only to see like objects to appear somewhere else. At present 4 or 5 of these objects, or similar, have formed a pattern in the NNE sky, again low on the horizon. Periodically as we are observing these lights, other star-like, but different, objects will enter our field of view from various directions, some changing direction, some appearing to pulse, some fading out while others continue until they are out of sight. I am very familiar with conventional aircraft and navigation light patterns. These are not conventional aircraft.
