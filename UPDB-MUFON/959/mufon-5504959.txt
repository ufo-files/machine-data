{nd} Multi-colored Orb in Sky approx. size of 'moon' in sky hovered over household traveling south to north

We were home at the time and we heard there was a meteorite shower in the sky that night. When we looked out we saw a multi-colored orb hovering in the Southern part of the Sky. It hovered for a awhile (approximately 2-3 minutes) then traveled to the northern part of the sky on the opposite side of our home and hovered there, it kept going back and forth a couple times hovering. I remember feeling very confused and vulnerable like we were being threatened. After that it traveled to the opposite side of the house when we looked out the window it was gone.
