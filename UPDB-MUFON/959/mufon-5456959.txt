{rjl} Brightest light in the night sky, was hovering and slight movements back and forth.

On November 21 around 10:45pm, I went outside to check the cloud cover in preparation for later viewing the expected unicorn meteor shower. The sky was mostly clear with only a few wispy clouds. I was looking at which stars I could see when I noticed straight ahead (East) there was an unusual bright “star” in the sky. I had a perfectly clear view with no obstructions as I was facing a large open farmland with trees in the far distance. It was the brightest object in the sky and I noticed it was moving. Mostly it appeared to be hovering and moving ever so slightly left and then right. I watched it for a few minutes and have never seen anything like it before. It looked like a circle or possibly disc shape. The light was a bright white color but sometimes showed a tinge of red or red-orange along the bottom edges of the light. After a couple of minutes, it seemed move forward (closer to me), which at that point I went inside to grab my partner to tell what I saw. When we looked out a window about 10 minutes later it was gone (so my partner did not see it).
