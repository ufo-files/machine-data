{nd} Oval shaped object that was really bright, hovering in the East sky of Illinois.

I was lying in my bed staring out the window when I saw a brought object in the sky at about 3am. The object was hovering. I couldn1t make out if it was a star or planet, but seemed a little too big for either. After about 15 mins, the bright object dimmed and disappeared. A few secs later, the object reappeared in the same exact spot. This happened about three times. Finally at exactly 4am, the object shot up into the sky in a matter of secs, leaving a thin light of light behind it. 

Also, if you looked closly and focued, you would see an odd sheped antenna on the top and tail end of it. I tried to take a picture, but my camera froze about 7 times and would not let me take a picture.
