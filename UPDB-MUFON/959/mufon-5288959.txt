Rectangular UFO up in the sky during the day

Los Angeles around 9:45am while driving on the 91 Fwy I happened to look up above the road into the sky and I saw a large white prominent rectangular object with slightly rounded corners that was upright like a monolith (it was flat looking not three dimensional) there were white/grayish clouds in the sky. The object was also somewhat white but clearly stood out it popped out from the background clouds. The fact that it was stood out so much was so striking, I knew immediately that it was some kind of unknown object or UFO. My after thought was that it seemed to be using a cloaking system. It appeared to be stationary, at the last minute before it passed over head out of my field of view (or I passed it) I saw an orange-yellow glint come off the top right hand corner of the object. I let out a big Whoa! That moment was an ah ha moment because I then knew the object was metal.  I see glints like this on airplanes all the time. The whole sighting lasted about 7 seconds.
