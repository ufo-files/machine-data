at midnight I witnessed 3 giant bright deep orange orbs of light hovering in the night sky. They were solid bright and did not flicker. they peaked over the houses west of 175 concord, wobbled almost in place and shifted to form a loose triangle shape, pointing down. the lowest orb forming the triangle then emitted a bright red crown of light, it looked like a fire in the sky. All 3 orbs started slowly moving east across the sky, I ran back inside to grab my partner, she threw on her shoes and followed me out. The orbs were still in the sky, but were picking up speed moving east, that's when I saw a 4th one appear in the distance ahead of the 3 that had formed the triangle. the 4th one seemed to shoot off east and up while the other 3 were still converging, then all 3 orbs began to gain altitude. Each orb seemed to rocket off in a different direction, within a matter of seconds, the orbs of bright orange light that had almost been as big as the moon in the night sky became as small as a pinpoint, disappearing behind the cloud line
