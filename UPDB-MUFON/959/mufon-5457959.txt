{rjl} Orange orb splits into multiple stationary orbs

Bright orange orb moves South above ocean and disappears. Around 10 minutes later the orb appears glowing brightly over the ocean roughly four to five miles out and remains stationary. A second orb appears and remains stationary. They are flowing bright in the sky. As one orb disappears another shows up remaining stationary. 

It was windy out, perfectly clear sky, yet the orbs remained stationary and never moved. 

Similar to a viral video from November of last 2018 from the outer banks of NC. 

Multiple witnesses and accounts on social media.
