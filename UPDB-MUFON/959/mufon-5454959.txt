Roughly 50 star-like lights appeared near zenith and travelled north-east over a 10 minute span

I stepped outside at 04h56 looked up and saw 2 star-like objects moving across near zenith then heading E-NE till out of sight from the horizon. I at first believed it to be 2 bright satellites in close proximity although travelling 3x faster than most satellites appear. I was thinking how odd this was as I had never seen 2 satellites that close to each other. Just then several more appeared equally spaced and followed the same trajectory. And then more, and more followed. I started to count but the end I counted approximately 49 objects although it appeared that the event started before I took notice of it. Most were equally spaced but a few had varying speeds and trajectory and would move close to the other objects then move away again. The entire event lasted 10 minutes that I observed. I took 3 videos with my phone but the quality is too poor to view anything. I was struck with fear and experienced flatulence during the event which I did not realize happened until later viewing the videos. I woke my wife partway through the event and she observed around 20 of the objects herself.
