{dr}3 lights in triangle pattern that brightened twice before slowly fading out.

I went outside around 9pm to check on my cat. I found him sitting under the bird feeder so I turned my attention to the stars. I was looking towards the South-Southwest at Saturn when I noticed what I thought was some extra stars that looked out of place. They were roughly around 0 magnitude. About as bright as the other brighter stars in the sky. As I tried to figure out what I was looking at, the "stars" brightened and slightly moved. I then realized that they wasnt stars but 3 lights in a triangle pattern. I said out loud to myself, "Are you kidding me?" They appeared to be hovering, but moved slightly. As I watched the lights, they brightened again Im guessing to around -7 magnitude and then slowly faded out. After the lights went out, I was unable to see anything. The whole thing probably lasted less than a minute, but its stuck with me ever since. Oddly, about an hour later, I developed a very bad headache. I don't know if this was related to the sighting or not but I never get headaches. And, this one never left till the following day.

Ive been into astronomy ever since I was a kid. I photograph and video lunar occultations, meteors, satellites, iridium flares and other astronomical objects. Ive seen planes, satellites, meteors, just about anything you can think of that would be in the night sky. But, I aint never seen anything like this.
