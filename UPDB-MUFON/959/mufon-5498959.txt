{cak} smoke break sighting

at approximately 830 pm we came out on our porch for a smoke break and as soon as we walked out the door onto the porch we saw a bright light, it was quiet, and very fast and I know it wasn't a plane or helicopter..it flew in a weird pattern and just disappeared..that's when we started to question what we had just seen and then sure enough another one appeared, but this one was awesome, it danced in the sky, turned a bright orange and pulsated, it gave off an orbe kind of a look, danced around, then just disappeared below some trees in the distance..it was awesome :) we felt like it was a special show just for our eyes only (a girl can dream can't she)
