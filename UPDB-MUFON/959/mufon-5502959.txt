{nd} while driving late at night witnessed 5 egg-shaped objects emitting while glowing light around house and above highway - 5 objects in a circle

Driving late one night after work I observed 5 bright lights which I first thought to be helicopters hovering near road. As I grew closer I noticed one was directly above rode but it was part of a group that was hovering above a house on the bluff next to the road. As I drove under the object I looked up through the windshield and could see the bottom half of the object that was not shrouded in a glowing white light, it was shiny and looked like I was looking up at the bottom of a giant silvery egg about 20-30 feet from end to end.
