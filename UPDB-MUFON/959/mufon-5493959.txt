{cak} across the canal from shoreline village saw two silver discs moving from se to nw then changed dir to ne disappeared in flash

Was in the park across the canal from shoreline village in long beach about hour before sunset. Saw two silver discs moving from se to nw. Had my camera and as I focused they suddenly changed direction toward ne an up. Then flashed out of view. Got one clear photo that I took showing 1 disc and the flash of the other disappearing. I felt amazed but totally forgot about it until days later when I got the film developed. I then forgot again until years later going thru old photos i found it again and scanned it in.
