Large pear shape black craft blotting out back ground stars flyin north silently at approx 200mphii

It was hot summer night at approx 10 pm Perth Australia , I was lying in a portable hammock outside admiring the clear view of the Milky Way. Just west of where I was I noticed the outline of a shape in the sky about the size of a 747 jet but it was shaped like a pear, narrow and rounded at the front and broadening out at the back, all the shape was rounded, no straight edges. It's shape was very clear as it was a pitch black silluette against a very clear starlit sky. There was no moon at that time. The vehicle was flying at approx 500 to 600 ft at about 200 mph and slowly accelerating heading due nth. The strangest thing was there was no lights, no sound and no emissions. The vehicle blotted out the stars as it flew across. I called to my wife and said hurry, come and check this out. She came out side and I pointed to the vehicle, she could see it too. We watched it fly nth until we couldn't see it no more. As it flew away it appeared to slowly accelerate.. My first thoughts on this was that it may have been a stealth bomber but there just wasn't any sound.
