{rjl} Small very bright oval shaped object directly above me. The object was stationary.

When I was at work, around 6pm I stared out my window and spotted a small highly reflective white or gray oval object directly above me, very high up into the sky. Relative to ground level, it appeared the size of a sesame seed. It was stationary. It was in the center of a very thin cloud or mist that was moving around it. After observing for about 3-4 minutes or so to see if it would move, I went to get my camera, when I returned 30 seconds later it was gone. 

I have no visual problems, and have never done drugs. I do not believe this object was a helicopter or plane. The sun was about an hour away from setting.
