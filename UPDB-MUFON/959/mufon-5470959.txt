{cak} No ufo,just grey aliens at a childhood slumber party. Arrived when all girls were asleep and walked among us.

I was approx six years old and had gone to a friends birthday slumber party. We were all tucked into our sleeping bags and chattering like girls do, there must have been 10 or so of us. My friend1s parents and younger brother were sleeping in their rooms down the hall. Our sleepingbags were located in the living room that looked onto the kitchen and feom the kitchen window is where I noticed the bright light flash, all of the girls were asleep and I had just remained awake enough to notice the light. Once my eyes adjusted, I saw 6-10+ grey alien looking figures starting to walk into the room without any sound. I tried to pretend to be asleep as they moved between our sleepingbags. If they noticed me, they did not show it. In a flash of an eye they were sucked back out the kitchen window and gone. I ran out of my sleeping bag and crawled into bed with Ashley1s parents and I spent the night there.
