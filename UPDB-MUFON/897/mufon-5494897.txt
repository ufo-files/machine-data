I was standing on my front porch around 2 am talking on the phone. I was looking at the ground when I noticed it got a little brighter outside, when I looked up there was a giant blue oval light in the sky, at first I thought it was maybe a street light or a house light in the distance but the light was too high in the sky for it to be anything on the ground. The light didn't move it just glowed and it looked a little bit smaller than the moon. It stayed for about 11 seconds then got a little darker and just disappeared all together. I was shocked when I first saw it I couldn't even speak to my friend on the phone she kept saying "hello" and I couldn't do anything but stare at this giant light. I tried to go to sleep after this but was just too creeped out.
