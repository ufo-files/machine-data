{nd} Green falling object Wednesday night, followed by 2 nights of saucer sightings.

On Wednesday July 25th 2012 me and my husband witnessed a green object fall out of the sky into a field about 1 mile from our home while driving in our car. The onject was like a fire ball. It seemed close, within 100 yards away.

The following two nights, Thursday July 25th and Friday July 26th 2012 we watched several hovering saucer shaped objects from our porch over the tree line. We were unable to determine how far away they were. Each object was a series of white lights. There was one big saucer shaped object which tilted showing the bottom of the object revealing bright colored lights in green, blue, and pink. Then there were two smaller saucers with only white lights around the edge.
