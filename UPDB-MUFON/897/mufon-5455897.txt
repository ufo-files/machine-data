{rjl} Strange close day time encounter with a silent moving saucer shaped UFO in Scranton, PA

My friend Andy and I were both around 13yrs old at the time, during one summer afternoon when we went for a bike ride to the Steamtown Mall. On our way back home, we stopped to take a break on a road/alley way that runs behind a few of the University of Scranton's buildings. We were sitting on a guide rail that runs along the top of a steep embankment, above the train tracks checking out the view and talking. As I was talking to my friend, I stopped mid sentence and said look, what is that? As I noticed the saucer shaped object that seemed to have just appeared out of nowhere, not too far off in the distance. We both stood up and watched in disbelief as this craft was silently heading towards us and descending. As it got closer I could see what I would describe as small round shaped windows or possibly lights that weren't turned on as it was daylight. They were symmetric in size and distance between one another and wrapped around the the entire saucer shaped craft at the equator. As the craft got closer I could tell it was rotating because I can see that the strip of windows around the equator were spinning around the craft in a clockwise fashion. At that point it was getting too close for comfort, as it was only about 50ft high and 100ft in front of us and getting closer, so we quickly jumped on our bikes and started peddling as fast as we can up the street. The craft followed right along side of us at the same speed, until we reached near the end of the road, where there's a tall pine tree. As the craft got close to that tree, it started moving upward and stopped right above the tree for a split second and then almost instantly shot up and toward the south east and was gone! It took off so fast, that other than the one second glimpse at the initial take off, you couldn't see it!

What's weird about this experience is that I can not remember the rest of the bike ride home after the encounter at all. The next thing I remember after seeing it take off, was my friend and I telling his mother about what we saw once we got back to his house. What's even stranger than that, is that I forgot all about the encounter until it randomly popped in my head one day about 7yrs or so later. I called my friend who I was with that day and was telling him what I remembered and asked if he remembered and he said no. So I told him to ask his mother about it. He asked her about it and I can hear her in the background repeating some of what I was just telling him. Then it finally came back to him and he was able to recall the encounter, stating some other details I didn't mention to him yet.
Needless to say he was just as freaked out as I was that he could forget something like that.
I will add more details about the craft sighting below:
-At no point during the encounter was there any sounds heard.
- looking up at the craft as it got close above our heads, I can see the bottom section of the craft was flat and not rotating. There was a few round lights (about 6" inch diameter) (not turned on) and a round door/entrance? (around 4'-5' foot in diameter) in the center of the bottom section of the craft, which was (roughly 30' foot in diameter).
- **Important Note on rotation of craft: As I mentioned above, the section of windows at the equator of the craft rotated around the craft in a clockwise fashion, the top section of the craft appeared to rotate in a counter clockwise direction. (So, the top section rotated counter clockwise, middle/equator section rotated clockwise and the bottom section did not rotate).
- Approximate date and time was July 24, 1998 at approx 1:30pm.
- Length of encounter was 1 min 30 sec to 2 minutes long.
- craft material was all metallic with a dull aluminum color.
