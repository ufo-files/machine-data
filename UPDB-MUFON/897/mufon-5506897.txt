{dr}silver ball that went from east to west with no sound

I was sitting in my living room watching tv, we have triple pane patio doors. A very shiny silver "ball" came into my view when i was just looking out the doors. I jumped up and ran to the door and went out on our patio. I watch it go from the east to the west, it dropped down then went right. There was no sound. It disappeared into a very dark cloud. The first thought was what the heck was that. The way it reflected light was weird because the sun wasnt out at the moment. All I could do was stand there and think I hope to God I am not the only one that just saw that. I started looking around to see if anyone else was standing outside. I came back in, sat down and thought, wow.. should I say something to someone? I have been checking every few minutes to see if it is back out there. I know it was 4 minutes for sure because I was waiting for a stupid life in a game that I play. My first reaction was I cant believe a ball can fly! My emotion was..that is really cool.. I lost sight of it because it went up into a cloud, after it went right.
