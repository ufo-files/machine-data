Large 200 Foot+ rectangular UAP moving NW.

5:40 pm 12/3/2021 
As we exited off 89 South. Exit 7 in NH. My daughter asked me what the line in the sky was. I looked to my left. I saw a huge rectangular object moving slowly to the NW. We got off the highway watching it right above the tree line. We parked in an open parking lot about 1/2 from the highway and watched it moving to the NW. HUGE.The stars were blocked out from the size of it. It slowed down then disappeared. The stars behind it reappeared. We were both shaking and said we just saw a UAP!!
