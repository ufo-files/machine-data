{kt}Massive Unidentified Craft

I was driving along Tollway 161southbound in the Dallas, TX area near the DFW airport in December of 2009, and I noticed a lot of vehicles pulling over to the side of the road so I slowed down to below 20mi an hour and I looked towards the east and saw a massive UFO. I pulled off to the side of the road for approximately 10-15 minutes and took a few pictures of the Craft with my cell phone camera, the object was very large about the size of a baseball field and Diamond shape it was very reflective and had multiple color lights around the edges it appeared as if it were in a transition from an invisibility cloaking technology to being visible to the naked eye it moved very slowly for approximately 10 minutes and then disappeared. Dozens of people were pulled over to view the object and after a few minutes my cell phone would not allow me to pull up my camcorder or outgoing calls then when the object disappeared my phone was back to normal. The photos I submitted will show everyone pulled off to the side and at first glance the photo looks like the sun but you will notice the sun in the west and the object has many different colors around the edges. I am only submitting this information now because at the time of the occurrence I was in the U.S Army and didn't want to compromise my security clearance.
