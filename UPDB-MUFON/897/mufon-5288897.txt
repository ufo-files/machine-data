2 objects in the night sky West of Tucson. Both objects only seen as series of orange lights.

My wife and I saw our first UFO’s on this March 16th at 10:30 facing the west in the night sky over Tucson but they lit-up then “faded-away” twice and were gone.

We saw what appeared to be 2 objects. One consisted of 5 Orange lights with a red outer glow in an Olympic Rings formation. This object appeared to be one craft. I was content to ignore it as an aircraft in the direction of Tucson Airport flight path when the 2nd object lit-up!

The 2nd object was a perfect line of 7-9 orange lights (also with a red outer hue) and illuminated about a mile North of the first lights and at about the same altitude.(rough guess 1,000 to 2,000 feet high) the 2nd object faded one light after another and the first did as well till both were gone. It was a cloudless night!

This all happened at roughly 10:30PM on 16 March 2021 and the total time of viewing was only 30 seconds from when my Wife first saw them to when me, her and our 17yr old son saw them fade away.

I am a trained observer/analyst and my former career in the USAF required that I could identify anything that flies from any country. These objects were observed at night in a cloudless sky through the front windshield of our vehicle as we were driving toward them. Also neither object had the FAA required Red/Green navigation lights of strobe beacon and were approximately 400-500 feet across for the first object and approximately 700-900 feet across for the 2nd object. 

Due to the darkness of the night sky no observable silhouettes could be discerned from our vantage point. The lights were about 3-4 miles ahead of us at a 45 degree angle above the mountain range to the West of Tucson city and just North of Mt Safford.

I am a Retired geospatial analyst with the USAF.
