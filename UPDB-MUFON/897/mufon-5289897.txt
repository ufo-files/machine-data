WHILE FILMING A MUSIC VIDEO WITH MICHAEL JACKSON THE ALIENS CAME TO HIS SET. I WAS THERE.

IN JUNE OF 1995 MICHAEL JACKSON FILMED A VIDEO IN FRAZIER PARK, CA. IT WAS AT THE START OF THE REDWOODS. HUGE TREES WERE STARTING THERE. THE PRODUCTION RAPPED AT ABOUT 3:30AM THAT MORNING. "MIKE" AND THE CREW WERE GONE OFF THE SET BY 4. SHORTLY AFTER I NOTICED A BRIGHT LIGHT OVER THE TREE TOPS. THE LIGHT WAS OVER THE SET WHERE WE HAD ANOTHER GUARD WATCH THE CAMERA AND SET. THE HOVERING LIGHT MADE NO SOUND. IT WAS BRIGHT BRIGHT. IT HOVERED BACK AND FORTH MAKING THE TREE TOPS MOVE AS IT HOVERED OVER THE TOPS OF THE TREES. AS IT HOVERED IT SHOT BEAMS OF LIGHTS TO THE GROUND. I THEN CALLED ON MY RADIO TO THE OTHER GUARD A COUPLE OF TIMES AND NO ANSWER. I THEN BECAME MAD HE DID NOT ANSWER. I THEN CURSED FOR HIM TO ANSWER AND HE ANSWERED. HIS ANSWER WAS OF A FRIGHTENED MAN "IS THAT YOU GUYS Fing WITH ME. I TOLD HIM NO EVERYONE RAPPED WERE GONE.  I THEN TOLD HIM TO GET IN THE CAR AND COME DOWN THE HILL TO THE PRODUCTION TRAILERS THAT WERE PARKED IN THE CAMP. HE GOT DOWN THE HILL AND I MADE HIM TURN AND LOOK BACK AT THE LIGHT. HE WAS VISIBLY SHAKING SCARED. HE TURNED AND LOOKED AND SAID ,"YAH THEY'RE THERE THEY'RE." THEN 3 SMALLER LIGHTS APPEARED. THEY WERE IN FORMATION. I FLASHED MY FLASH LIGHT 3 TIMES AT THEM A COUPLE OF TIMES. THE OTHER GUARD WAS BEGING ME TO STOP. THEY CAME CLOSER NOW TO US. I FLASHED MORE 1 2 3 ....1 2 3 THEN THEY GOT EVEN CLOSER. I FLASHED MORE AND ONE SHIP SHOT SPARKLE BALLS 1 2 3 SPARKLE BALLS DIRECTLY AT MY HEAD AND JUST BEFORE AT ABOUT 10 FEET FROM MY HEAD THEY DISIPATED. POOF POOF POOF GONE NEVER HIT ME. THEY DID IT 3 TIMES. IT WAS LIKE THEY TOLD ME WE SEE YOU...SINCE THIS EVENT I HAVE HAD NUMEROUS EXPERIENCES OF LIGHTS SHOWING UP. I WOULD ALSO LIKE TO KNOW WHAT THE SPARKLE BALLS WERE. I PRESENTLY LIVE IN NEW MEXICO AND THEY ARE AROUND. WHEN I AM IN THE GILA FOREST THEY COME. I WISH SOMEONE FROM YOUR ORGANIZATION WOULD LIKE TO SEE?.
