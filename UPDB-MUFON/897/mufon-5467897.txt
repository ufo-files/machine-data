{cak} Object slowly appeared and hovered for several minutes.

Sitting on my back deck in the early evening looking south and noticed this object seemly appeared slowly out of nowhere. It became clearer as I watched it and it looked like there was a disturbance of some type underneath? When I first saw it looked very fuzzy but eventually became visible as you can see in the photos. Under the craft it seemed almost like the distortion effect you would see looking down a hot road? I took a couple pictures with my phone (max zoom and normal) then realized I needed to get a better look at it after watching it hover for about 3 or 4 minutes. I ran into the house to get binoculars, ran back out and it was gone approx. 30 seconds later. 

Amazing!
