(GS) A bright white orb descending quickly down. All the way to earth, behind building. Large. Not a shooting star, as it went very low.

Waiting at stop light, facing North. Saw a large white light object descending quickly from up in sky, down to almost the ground, where I lost sight of it behind building. Went vertically down, not as any angle, like a shooting star would have, and which wold have burned out way higher in the atmosphere. This went all the way down, and was consistent in its glow the entire way down. It seemed like a solid object moving at a rapid speed. It never impacted the ground, or broke the speed of sound (no sonic boom), from what I could hear.
