Triangle shaped object in the sky

I was sitting inside when I saw a weird object in the sky. I thought it might be a helicopter or plane but it was in a triangle shape. Also it just hovered there and didn’t move. I have -2 vision (I think I was wearing glasses at the time) but my sister also saw and she has perfect vision. It looked like there were lights in but it was a bit far away. I was kinda confused how it wasn’t moving. It was a while ago so I don’t remember it exactly but it creeped me out. I don’t have any photos or videos as we looked outside, and as soon as we looked back it was gone.
