{cak} Eve of Sept 10, 10;30pm or so. We were going into our back yard when I saw in the sky 3 missled shaped lights, split into two and then one.

My husband and I came home the evening of September 10,2018 at roughly 10:30. We had just came back from a dance performance ( no alcohol or mind altering drugs were involved) We were entering our backyard walk-in Easterly, We live 10 miles from the Minneapolis, St Paul airport, so we see plenty of planes fly over as we are in one of the flight paths. I looked up and saw what appeared to be 3 very bright orange, missile shaped objects,it look like it split into 2 then 3 . That part of the story, I question my memory. It was traveling in a NE direction. I got the attention of my husband who saw the one flying object. It seemed to be going at a steady pace as were able to watch it together for about 30 seconds until the neighborhood trees obscured our view.
