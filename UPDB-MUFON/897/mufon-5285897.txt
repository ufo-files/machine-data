West Nashville Sighting 8/19/21 est. 9:15pm CST

On the night of Thursday August 19th at an estimated time of 9:15pm CST I was sitting on the back patio of my townhouse in West Nashville (Location removed to Investigators Report Section/cms/LLL) and observed two boomerang or crescent shape objects fly across the sky in unison. They appeared to be traveling at a moderate speed and were close to each other in a unison formation. They appeared to have an illumination of light and I noticed a black circle underneath the object. I would also like to include that within a minute before this I saw what looked like two diffent shooting stars flash across the sky from the horizon vertically and one from the horizon at a slight angle. The event lasted no more than a few seconds.
