Bright orbs flying in a triangle formation from west to east

Three of us witnessed about twelve Orbs flying in a triangle formation from west to east. The Orbs glowed red, yellow, and orange.They didn't make any noise. At one point a plane came out of the clouds and followed for a minute then changed route. They disapeared into the clouds to the west. I thought they might be lanterns but they where too big and moved too quickly.
