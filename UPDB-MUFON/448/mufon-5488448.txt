{nd} UFO shines bright light while hovering then moves in Disney world

This event occurred while was on vacation at Disney world. We were in the line for the seven dwarfs mine train, one of the new rides Disney released. This having being my second UFO sighting and report, I always look at the skies in hopes of seeing another light. The line was very long so I was not in the building to the ride yet. I was scanning the skies and didn't see anything, when I looked right above my head. I saw a bright blue light, almost like a helicopter spotlight right on top of me. At first I assumed it was the North Star because that is the closest thing that I could relate to it. As I'm watching this light or beam, I didn't think it was a UFO at all, more of like a star. As I'm watching the object, the light dims rapidly to a normal star size with a dim blue color. As the object hovers above me, it then moves rapidly across the sky in a smooth motion until it fades its color with the surrounding horizon. This is my second encounter like I said, but this will never be as dramatic as the first one.
