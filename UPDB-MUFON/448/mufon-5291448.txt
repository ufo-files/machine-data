Dozens of orb-like objects flying back and forth through a canyon

While camped out in the Coso Wilderness Friday October 16, 2020 we noticed these balls of light flying back and forth and up and down through a canyon less than a mile south of our camp. This is an extremely remote area and no one else was camped out. We observed these orbs occasionally shoot beams of light down on the hills around the canyon. They also hovered, flew vertically and then down very quickly then sometime fly in unison. At times they appeared to have connected and we could faintly see the flat top and sometime the bottom of whatever it was. They were basically level with us since we were camped a little higher than the canyon they were flying above.

We also noticed the following night how dark that canyon actually is, as there was no moon. On the night we watched the UFO's they had that whole canyon lit up with a glow. Also on the following morning we found some kind of hidden GPS antenna-type thing mounted in the rocks behind our camp and facing that canyon (Ive attached pictures). Not sure if it'd related in any way. We were near the China Lake Naval Weapons Center but I don't think these flying orbs were anything manmade.

We also tried to shoot video but our iPhones were not good enough to pick up the lights but it does give you our audio commentary.
