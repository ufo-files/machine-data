Back in 1986 Me and my husband and 3 children were going to Idaho from CA. on the way we went thru NV. it was about 3AM in the morning on a long stretch of road, children were asleep and we noticed lights coming at us from behind they were changing from red to white or orange looking color. At first we thought it was a helicopter so we slowed down to see why they were coming at us so low to the ground but when we slowed it stop and went from one side of the road to another then up in the air and back down the lights were changing and blinking and pulsating and we had rolled down the window so we could hear it but there was no sound we noticed it was not a helicopter at all but a round looking object with light around it at this point we just wanted to get away from it so we started to speed up and it was like following us the faster we went the faster it went we got scared because we knew we had the children with us so it was like chasing and we waited till we came to a store and pulled into the parking lot for help but it was closed at the time the object keep coming till it got about 1/2 a mile away and just shot straight up into the sky and disappeared. Never believed in UFOS until that time but I do now.
