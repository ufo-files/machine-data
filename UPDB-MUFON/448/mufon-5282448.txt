A column of approximately 12 lights gliding in a straight line

On returning from a walk we looked up in the sky and observed a column of very bright lights gliding silently overhead. The lights were organised in a straight line. I was unable to get take a video with my camera in time and the column of lights then disappeared out of the clear sky and into the clouds. It was not satallites. They were flying low. We have never seen anything like this before.
