Amber Lights and Craft

I observed a deep amber light approaching my house. It was on the east side of 31 and parallel 26. As it approached 100 west, approximately, I observed another pulsating amber light directly underneath the one and it continued gliding north, parallel 31. The deep amber light glided toward my home and hovered. I could see beautiful blues and greens and amber with a red light pulsating and it appeared to be rotating.A friend and I went to the west side of my home where high activity has been observed. I counted 4, 2 stationary at different points, and 2 were gliding south to north at low height. It appeared they were at 200 west ( Dixon Road ) or further west around 350 west. As I was watching 1 of them, it stopped midair and hovered low above a house, it dropped about 4 inches visually and rose slowly and continued north.It appeared like about 1/2 to 3/4 mile away from my position. They had the same color lights as previous craft, After a few minutes, they would return, north to south on or around 200 west, and when they approached 26, they would turn on a dime and go back north. It was my impression they were looking for something and reminded me of a hawk, scanning the ground while soaring looking for food. I have been watching high activity for quite awhile. I know they are not planes, flares or anything normal.
