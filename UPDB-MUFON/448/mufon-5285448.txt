Comet/Meteor Initial Observation Then Seemed to Change as if a Flying Object

The night of the 13th of September 2021 I was on a walk with my fiance walking along a canal. We were walking in the direction of facing south. In the night sky there was a bright light that appeared. It was red in color. Behind the red light was a long tail which was also bright red. The object was moving from west to a eastward direction. The glow of the bright red light almost looked like a comet or meteor. It was moving at a good speed for about 15 to 20 seconds. Then the tail of the object disappeared and only the bright light remained visable and still remained moving. Then the light disappeared from the sky. We both figured it was a meteor that burned through the atmosphere. About 10 seconds later the light reappeared moving in the same direction of west to east but at a much slower speed. Then the light seemed to slow down and change directions. The direction that the light headed was that of a southern direction. I watched the light for another 15 to 20 seconds, as it faded into the night sky. The light go smaller as it faded headed in the southern direction.
