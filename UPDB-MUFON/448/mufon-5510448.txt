UFO, Southampton, UK (03/06/2013 - 15:30pm)

This was not a balloon or human craft, it had no wings, and was from what we could tell; metallic as it was reflecting the light of the sun. This object moved progressively from North, to West and finally to South where it finally disappeared over my house. This was the second day in a row where we had observed a UFO fly over our house. The day before it was a bit later in the evening, possibly about 8.30pm-9.00pm and it was black and moved in the much the same way and disappeared in the same vicinity above the houses; unfortunately we did not get a video of this. These are amongst a number of amazing UFO sightings we have had above our very house including one where a military helicopter pursued the craft.
