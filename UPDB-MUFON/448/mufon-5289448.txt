It was like a Blake hole with triangles of light around the circumference

The image attached is what I believe a third party view would be. It was 22 beloe zero as I walked from my truck to the back barn , the sound of my steps were a high frequency squeak, the distance street lights shining straight up into space. About half way all about me the snow lit up like every flake a prism. This caused me to stop and look up, what I saw was a star less void. At hat point I do not know where the light was. It was pitch black, this startled me but I remained calm what seemed like only a second or two it was suddenly above me. Then I saw the triangles cycling in length which gave it the appearance of rotation. As i watched it became smaller vanishing into the Eastern sky. All of this in complete silence.
