Quarter size object in sky, very reflective moved upward diagonally up and out of view.

was outside on patio grilling, observing unusually clear sky when noticed very shiny round object about the size of a quarter appear in the sky. It moved slightly up and diagonally. The entire time it did this is got smaller in the sky (same shiny reflective appearance) I went in the house to get binoculars to view object better. Upon my returning to the patio object was no longer in view. Occurred in under 3 minutes. I scanned for object with binoculars but saw nothing. As a side note, I did see several planes right before and after seeing object. The object DWARFED them in the sky.
