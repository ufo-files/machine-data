Ball of Light, pulsating and moving

My parent was driving me home from work, around 8:30 pm, the sun was setting and the whole day it had been clear skies, no rain or clouds. The part of the sky I was looking at was still blue, with a star speckled here and there, there was on star that I saw which seemed oddly big and bright. I kept looking at it since it seemed too vibrant to be a star, especially considering it wasn't even dark out. As I was looking at it, it began to pulsate, like flash light, it would do it maybe 3 or 4 times and stop. At some point, I thought I saw it become a crescent-esque shape, I'm not completely sure because at closer look it didn't seem perfectly round. After a minute or two, it disappeared, I had pointed it out to my parent beforehand and they were in utter shock (as was I). We turned the corner, a treeline was to my left on my parents driver side, the light reappeard, this time it wasn't blinking or pulsating, just sitting behind the treeline. Eventually, we turned another corner and the thing was out of sight. I'd say maybe 6-7 minutes I saw a helicopter fly by. Helicopters have been flying over my area for the last two weeks almost nonstop. Also, I was too shocked and I didn't even think to take out my phone for a pic, kicking myself for it.
