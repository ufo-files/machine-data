{jpg} Light moved erratically then shot away.

My now wife and I were sitting on her front porch visiting and enjoying the evening. We were looking at the night sky when I noticed what I thought was a star getting brighter. The light then shot erratically around a patch of sky at an incredible rate, then shot off across the sky, again, at an incredible rate. We immediately asked each other if we had seen the same thing and then proceeded to recount what we had each seen. I have never seen anything like it before or since.
