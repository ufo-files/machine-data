{rjl} Two unknown triangular craft making a very loud, surreal pulsating sound

My friend and I were watching television inside when we began to hear a sound which slowly grew in volume until it could no longer be ignored or dismissed. Just like the person reported in this sighting - [Link moved to FI Section/cms/tg] - it sounded like a helicopter was landing on the roof, but then it got even louder. This was definitely no helicopter. The house was vibrating and I ran outside to see what the hell was going on. 

Opening the door and stepping outside felt like there was a distinct change in air pressure and that the air was electrified. The pulsing sound was unreal - like a million unbalanced washing machines in sync with each other. Looking up directly overhead into the black, cloudy, moonless, starless sky I could see three ordinary looking small, white, unblinking lights in an elongated triangle. I could see no silhouette whatsoever - no visible edge to this object, and therefor I had no idea if it was near and small or far away and large. There was a second identical triangle of lights to the west and at higher altitude, and both were moving very slowly (about 5 mph or less) to the northeast. My friend and I watched for a minute or so as they passed by until we finally saw the objects disappear behind a hill about 1/4 mile away. Only at this point, the pint where they disappeared behind an object of known altitude/distance could we estimate the size and altitude, which I would estimate to be approximately 40 feet in length and hovering/flying 120 feet in altitude.
