crudely folded brown tarp-looking UFO

At about 12:30 am oct 1st - I went out in the back yard to look for the aurora that had been forecast (it never showed) but I looked up and observed what looked like a giant dark brown trashbag or tarp that looked crudely folded into a wing or boomerang shape maybe 300' agl and about 100' long and maybe 30' wide. It was dark out but I could distinctly see it slowly moving to the NW heading into the wind that was blowing about 20mph from the NW. 
It had no lights but was faintly illuminated from the city lights. It made no sound. I'd say it was moving about 20 mph. I watched it for as long as I could. I continued straight until it was almost out of view then i observed it turn to the NE as it faded from view at about 30 degrees. Sky was clear but city light pollution was a factor. It was silent with no visible means of propulsion. I wouldn't have noticed it if I had not looked up immediately after exiting my back door. What luck! Very weird though. Not a typical UFO or UAS shape! I wanted to grab my phone and flashlight, but I didn't want to lose sight of it.
