{dr}green lights moved slowly south stopped and hovered motionless for 10 to 15 seconds then one by one took off at an amazing fast speed.

I was in Sherman Oaks CA in the hills facing Encino and the San Fernando Valley. My friend sent me outside on his terrace to see and photograph a huge spider web that had appeared on the railing. I took my camera and went out on the terrace and looked out the horizon to see several bright green lights slowly moving southbound behind the hills bordering Northridge. They stopped moving completely for about ten seconds and remained absolutely motionless .One by one from a position of absolute stillness..they took off into space at an unbelievable fast speed, i would say light speed. I knew they weren't planes,drones or helicopters because this was my fourth sighting,and there has been the same physical,mental and emotional feeling that I experienced each time prior.Plus they wouldn't have possibly been able to do what they were doing.
