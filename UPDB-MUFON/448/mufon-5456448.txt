{rjl} 12 lights in. Row

My husband and I headed south through Pontiac IL when I looked out the window and noticed a couple bright lights in the sky. Upon further inspection it was apparent that there were 12 very bright lights in a perfect row high up in the sky and perfectly spaced. It seemed to hover but stay in our view. There were airplanes around so we knew it wasn’t a plane and it wasn’t a star. We watched for 10 minutes and then turned to each other orly a minute and turned back and they were no where to be seen!
