2 lights traveling together

I took my rubbish to the bins & looked up enjoying the clear sky & the moon &’stars when I saw 2 nights which were orange & dull travelling close together but moving around one another in a way i can only describe as similar to migrating birds! I watched them until the disappeared only for a minute if that! They were very high up with no sound!
