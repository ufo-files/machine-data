Lights on edge of seeming disk like shape.

Was at a friends house in the back yard when one of the friends directed everyone’s attention to some lights in the sky. We all stopped to watch. It seemed as if these lights appeared out of nowhere from the left side and formed a sort of line but in a curved shape, then they would follow the line going towards the right and disappeared once they got to a certain point on the right side. To me and a few of the other people with me, it looked as if the lights were attached to a saucer shape that was rotating and possibly slightly tilted down on the side closest to us. The lights would also individually get brighter at random moments and then revert back to normal.
