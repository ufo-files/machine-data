Saw huge flame shooting from ground

I live on the outskirts of town. My home backs up to several acres of nothing but woods. No homes near the object sighting. I was getting up from bed to let my dog out the back to go to the bathroom when I reached for the patio door to open it. As soon as I looked otside I saw a huge ball of flame shoot upward from the ground about 10 ft. behind the trees which are about 400 ft. from my house. I had a very clear shot. It rose straight upward then made a complete turn toward the top of my house and it completely vanished. It was completely silent. By the time I hurried to the front of the house, it was gone. I know it definately was not a shooting star because it rose from the ground straight upward.
