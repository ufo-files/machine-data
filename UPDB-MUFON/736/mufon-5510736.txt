{dr} triangle object with five lights flashing in sequence from eat to west coming from the south.

My wife and I were going to church for worship practice the evening of January 24,2013 at 7:20 pm. we were just pulling in to the parking lot of the church when my wife shouted " Look at those lights! Oh my God!" I looked south to my right and saw the object. I said" Oh my God I cant believe it!" The object was hovering over a field about 100/150 feet away from us. When we first saw the object the lights were constant, all five of them, not blinking or anything, but after we observed the object for a few seconds all of the lights flashed in sequential order. When they flashed they were brighter than when they were constant. My wife and I were in a little unbelief at what we were seeing. At this point we pulled out of the church parking lot and headed east to follow the object. When we first saw it it was hovering but after it flashed its lights in order it started to kind of float to the east. By this time it was over a small housing edition. It was right over the trees. We lost sight of the object for no more than three to five seconds because we went down a hill and we lost sight of it behind the trees. When we got to where we could see again the object was completely gone!
