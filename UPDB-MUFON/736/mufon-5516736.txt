Yellow Light appeared above home visible for 5 seconds - dissappeared - travelling north

2 Aug 12
Petawawa, ON, Canada

Standing in Driveway of home before darkness at 9:10pm. Light appeared above at approx 8,000 to 12,000 ft. No noise. No trail. Yellow light, not white light from stars.

Light was clearly visible and moving north at a high rate of speed. Only visible for 5 sec. Dissapeared not that far away. Actually Dissappeared as simply as it appeared.

Clear sky above home. Clouds approx 5 km to the north. Light dissappeared before clouds. Did not enter clouds, just dissappeared.

Second strange occurance in the area. Petawawa is a large military community with a trg area to the north but also the Chauk River nuclear facility as well. Other occurance appeared there as well.
