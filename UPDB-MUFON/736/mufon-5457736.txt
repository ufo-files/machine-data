{rjl} Looked like search lights

I am a truck driver and was driving south on I-39 near Tonica Illinois when I first seen the lights. It was around 2:30 in the morning. At first I could see it out my windshield and it looked like 2 lights both going in a large oval swoop opposite each other. I stopped just north of Bloomington,IL and woke my husband up to check it out to make sure I wasn’t crazy. It was following along with us and at this point it was right outside my driver side window. That was at 3:10am. I continued through Bloomington and onto I74 east and when I got just about to Le Roy they just vanished. The last glimpse I had of them was in my mirror. It was a cloudy night and to describe what I saw the best I can would be it looked like 2 people were walking on the clouds looking for something with flashlights. But all I could see was the light below the clouds. It’s hard to describe. I do have a video but it’s not that great and was recorded just north of Bloomington when I stopped. (FYI it’s dark so make sure your screen light is up)
