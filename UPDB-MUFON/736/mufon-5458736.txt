Brilliant fluorescent white-silver light cross shape.

Around sunset my wife and I were sitting on our back patio having a coffee when my wife said “what’s that”?I looked to the wnw where I saw a brilliant silvery white cross shaped object about 20 degrees off the horizon. The west horizon had a solid dark cloud reaching from the south to north horizon and was stationary due to calm conditions at the time. The cloud bank went from horizon to approx. 30 deg. The cross stayed in one place for 2 minutes and then faded to nothing. This re-occurred about 5 minutes later with same results.The cross had horizontal arms reaching north-south and a longer vertical arm reaching towards the ground. There was no vertical arm upwards but it looked there was a a rounded dome shape on top. It was hard to determine its distance as it was over open farmland and made no noise, therefore it’s size is hard to determine. Peter
