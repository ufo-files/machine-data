Slowly travelling sphere of light which slowly dissapeared as it got closer to line of sight

My brother and I were star gazing in our backyard,which is near the outskirts of town. We had our telescope set looking due west, when my brother told me to turn and look at the eastern sky (he was scanning the night sky). We could see a glowing object slowly traversing the sky and is it got closer to us/line of view, it slowly started to fade out. The entire sighting took a good 20-30 seconds from the time it was first spotted until it faded away. Interestingly enough, it did not disappear over the horizon. It seemed to disappear quite a distance away from the horizon. There were no blinking lights, no flashes of light, just a glowing object that slowly floated by and then faded away. We were not scared, just felt like curious onlookers.
