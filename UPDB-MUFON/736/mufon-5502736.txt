I was laying on a bench in the middle of town staring at the sky when I saw over 30 sphere like objects in the night sky. I laid there for about 3 hours in awe just watching them go in and out of one another.they were doing figure eights and amazing circles in a concentrated part of the sky. No one ever believed me so I didn't report it. But I felt like they knew I was watching so they gave me an amazing show. They faded as the sun started to rise.
