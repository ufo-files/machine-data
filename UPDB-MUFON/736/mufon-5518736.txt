Five glowing orbs

I saw a group of five UFOs last night. They were like nothing else Ive ever seen. Strange yellow-orange glowing orbs. I was standing in my backyard about 10:30 p.m. near 71st and Glisan in Portland. When I saw the first one, it caught my eye because it was moving slowly from the west towards me. And I thought thats a weird helicopter. But there was no sound. It meandered north, then suddenly picked up speed and disappeared.

Then from the southwest, two more flying slowly one behind the other. The first one suddenly rose up and stopped, and the other one came up to it. Then they slowly followed the same path as the first one. Then two more, doing pretty much the same. They were not planes ... planes don't stop in mid-air. And not helicopters because they cant go as fast as these did when they accelerated. They had no flashing lights like normal aircraft. And there was no noise at all. Even with a high-up plane you can hear something. And these werent even that high up, probably 5,000 feet. I couldnt see any shapes, just a glowing lights.

Someone else reported seeing this from the nearby Fred Meyers parking lot last night.
