{rjl} Clear transparent bubble-like or glass sphere with object inside in the sky during daylight

I was sitting a table at a golf-course waiting for my tee time. I looked up in the sky, there was a mixture of sun and clouds. I saw a clear bubble-like object moving across the sky. I attempted to get my phone to video tape it but by the time I got it, it went into the clouds. My golf partner arrived and I looked up again and I saw it again and said Look! And she was not wearing sunglasses so the sun was in her eyes. again it went into the clouds. It seemed to be circling because I saw it again about halfway through it's circling and tried again to get my phone to video it, but it went into the clouds. Within about 5 minutes I saw 3 airplanes (not commercial) and a helicopter. I want on the gold course and kept looking up but didn't see it or other planes again. The object was like a glass sphere with an object in the middle of it.
