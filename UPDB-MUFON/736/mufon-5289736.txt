This incident was witnessed by me and my son on the evening of Saturday, March 26, 1983.
On this date, I was 44 years old and my son was 17 years old.
Which was our habit, we were heading for my car to drive to our local newsstand for the early edition of the Sunday papers.
Crossing the main street, in the direction of our car, I noticed a wide beam of light above the reservoir in our neighborhood.
I said to my son "Mark, what is that", but by the time he looked to where I was pointing, the beam of light had gone out.
It was at this point that I saw where the light originated from, as it slowly headed towards us, slightly to our left, I could see it's shape against the sky.
It was Chevon shaped, about the size of a football field, slowly flew over us, moving the slow speed of a blimp and with no sound.
It headed North, towards Westchester County.
We told no one at the time, except my wife, but the next day, it was front page of the local newspaper. A couple of years later, a book came out titled [removed book title] 
As mentioned, I told no one about this incident, but others have. 
I am telling about this sighting now because I am 82 years old, and before I lose this memory, I would like to tell those at MUFON what my son and I witnessed.
Thank you.
