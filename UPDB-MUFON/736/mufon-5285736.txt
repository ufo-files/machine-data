V Formation 5 Dome Crafts larger dome top and smaller dome bottom with Silver coating

It was a sunny summer day and we were out playing hide and seek. Noted 5 disc shape crafts in V formation (2-1-2) with domes top and bottom of each with pure silver craft.  The crafts appeared out West to my line of site from a 2 story view point (Townhouse) they hovered for almost 2 minutes in formation about 1/2 mile out and then in unison accelerated heading North about 1-2 miles out. They slowed to a stop ( lost view the last 2/3rds of the travel due to speed they hit in formation) they hovered in formation for about another 30 seconds approximately or longer and then accelerated vertical from 1/2 mile above ground level to many miles above the ground holding formation still and then another acceleration and went out of site about 5 seconds or less later straight up out of site period. All discs were silver in colour and the same size and had to be 300 feet or more in length each and height of 100 feet or more easily each.  My younger cousin was with me and she noted the same 5 discs that i did and watched them at both hover points as i did.  It is something you never forget ever in your life once you see them it changes your perspective on the universe and us on this planet being alone.
