{ck} I saw a flash of light that then disappeared in a straight line path into the sky.

I saw this too from Milwaukee, WI. I was standing on my deck on June, 25th at approximately 9:15 pm and looking up to the east, north east and saw a flash of light, at first I thought it was a star, because the flash was so high in the sky, a little over half way up on the horizon, but it was much brighter than a star. The flash only lasted a couple of seconds and I watched as the light continued in a straight line across the sky from the flash spot to the south, but it was going away from me so it was like it was going to the south east too. The light continued in a straight line and continued to fade in brightness until it finally disappeared. This all happened in less than a minute, probably took 30-45 seconds. I did not look away from the object until I could not see it anymore. The light of the object just faded away like it went into outer space. The light did not pulsate or flash more than the intial flash, it just faded away like it went out of site. I live about seven miles from the Milwaukee airport so I know what planes look like around here. They go directly over my house on takeoff and approach when using the north/south runways. There were multiple plains in the area at the time this happened so I was able to compare the light to them and it was nothing like a plane. It was way too high in the sky, and the flash and the speed that it disappeared were not how planes fly.
