I was with my daughter and 2 friends at Elitch Gardens, Denver, Colorado for the 4th of July fireworks. We were waiting for the show to start and at approx 9:45 pm we looked up and saw a ball of yellow/orange glowing light traveling West to East at about the same speed as an airplane. At first glance it appeared to be some sort of Chinese lantern because it was flickering like a candle, but I quickly realized that it was too high up to be a lantern. I watched it until it disappeared inside a cloud. About 5 minutes or so later, two more of these orbs came by. They too were going West to East as well. They were traveling in a straight line and were a pretty good distance apart. I also watched them until they went inside a cloud. I did not notice anything during the firework show as my attention was on the fireworks. After the show ended, we were on our way back to the car and I saw another one pass by. Same area of the sky as the other three, and traveling the same way. I am trying to figure out what they were. Was kind of baffled by it. I have never seen anything like it! It was fascinating but a little creepy at the same time. Have there been any other reports of these orbs in the Denver or Colorado area either on the 4th of July or the last week?
