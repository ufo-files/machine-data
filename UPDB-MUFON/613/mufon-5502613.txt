{rjl} After harvest lunch, 4 of us all saw it. VERY high rate of speed. Streaked across the southern horizon, then out of sight. No contrail, was close enough you could see it wasn't a plane.

We were harvesting South of Sprague, Wa. We had eaten lunch and had all climbed into the loaded wheat truck. The other truck driver and myself were sitting in the bed of the truck. The other two men were in the cab of the truck. Something caught my eye and I observed a silverish/glowing cigar shaped object streaking across the southern horizon. This thing was really moving. I pounded on the top of the truck and told the driver to stop. We all 4 saw it. We were all kind of dumbfounded. It was a mutual agreement that it WASN'T an airplane. The object was going faster than anything I had or have ever seen. The UFO went from east to west then turned north and out of our view. We talked about it for a bit then just went on about our business of harvesting. When I got home later that month there was an article in the Odessa record about it. Many had also seen it. I'm surprised this incident has never been documented.
