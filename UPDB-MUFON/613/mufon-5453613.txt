Took dog outside to go to relieve himself when I spotted a light that resembled the helicopters that fly over our neighborhood just about every night. Did not think much of it until I saw it pulsating and remaing stationary. (I just watched a video where a captain and first mate filmed, 28MAR20, nearly the exact same thing.) I tried to debunk it by comparing it to stars or other flying objects that take flight paths towards our airport or bases here in San Antonio. I really saw the pulsating once I started to record, so I wanted to zoom in to see if I could spot any other traits. (That is the only time the pulsating stopped.)
