Orange Triangles

I took my dog out in a very populated area of Nashville at 9:22pm. Shortly after I got outside, I saw two large orange triangles moving across the sky with another one following behind. It was a very constant rate, so fast that I knew if I ran inside to get my camera, they would be gone. I chose to stay so I could witness it as log as I could. It was very cloudy, but I don't think the clouds caused them to disappear, because all three suddenly disappeared even though the third one had kept his distance from the other two. I ant believe what I saw and I can't find anyone else posting anything on the internet.

Sorry, it's only what I saw, no pictures.
