orange color with moving blue lights

I was at the fashion square mall around christmas time with my wife, my 4 month old son and my coworker. We were getting into our car to go home when i happened to look up and right above the parking garage i saw this orange color egg shaped object with these blue lights moving around it. At first i thought it was a remote control toy,being so close to the holiday. I realised that it wasnt because it was pretty sizable and moving slow. I called out to my wife and coworker but by the time they looked it was already gone. It wasnt even dark at the time so i always wondered if anyone else saw this thing. Anyway for years i thought i was going crazy because no one believed me. Then about 6-7 years later,my wife friend was over at our home and she said she was driving downtown orlando the other night. She said she saw a flying object as she was driving home. She couldnt believe it because no one on the road seem to notice it. I then told her about my encounter i had 6-7 years earlier,as i described the object i saw her jaw just hit the ground. It was the exact object i saw those many years ago.
