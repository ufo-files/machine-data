circulating, yellowish light with red cone

First noticed light when in mother-in-laws driveway. Appeared to be a light moving in a repetitious path on some kind of aircraft. The central light was yellowish, but it had a bright red cone of light around it, not unlike a laser light show. The red cone did not extend much past the yellowish light and circulated with it. It travelled east to west for a few minutes and then began to head north at which time the lights faded. I would chock it up to a helicopter with a searchlight, but is was too high up ( just under the clouds) and its light was not circulating in a pattern consistent with a localized suspect search (we have police helicopters flying over our house all the time). I have never seen any aircraft with lights like I witnessed, nor circulating in the way that I saw, and we have many aircraft flying overhead or nearby.
