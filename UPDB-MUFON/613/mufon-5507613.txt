{nd} Four spheres about 500 feet or more above our courtyard: ran for camera, gone 15 seconds later.

I am writing to report a sighting of four ball shape objects this morning (9/16) at 8:01 am. I was finishing my walk with our dog, walking through our courtyard, when I saw four round spheres in a line. They were several hundred feet in the air, I would estimate 500 feet. They were in two sets of two and basically stationary while slowly rotating. One was reddish in color, the other three, gray. I tried taking a photo with my cellphone but could not focus, it wanted to focus on the contrails, much higher and behind. I ran the few steps inside my house to get my camera and tripod, but on my return 15 seconds later they were gone, disappearing from nearly directly overhead. I believe this would exclude the possibility of balloons. Skies were clear with a few contrails at much higher altitude, I noticed little or no wind. No witnesses besides myself were around. I swear to the truthfulness of this sighting. In the attached photo, I have used Photoshop to approximate the size, shape and location of the objects. The base photo was taken 5 minutes after the sighting. (On the attached form I stated "10 times that of a star" in apparent size, and also, "1/4 the size of a full moon," rather than "aspirin" as I chose here. I also indicated the actual size was larger than a basketball.)
