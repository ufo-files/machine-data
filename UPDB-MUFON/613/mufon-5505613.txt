{rjl} I saw lights in the sky from my window

I was in my room and at about 8pm I looked out my window and saw the lights. Their was about 5-6. Theyre usually airplanes that fly around the same area but these lights were more stationary. About 5-6 of them all near the same area. After a couple of minutes most of them became invisible, not appearing to have traveled anywhere before they were gone. About 2 stayed a little longer than the rest but a minute after the others disappeared, they did as well. As if nothing was there to begin with. I didn't know what to think. My brain tried to rationalize, maybe it had been stars? But they shined brighter and more orange than the stars that were viewable at that time. It was exciting, but a little scary as well because I didn't know what the lights were.

I heard about people seeing lights in California on New Years so when I first saw, thats what I thought, I think I may have seen the same thing. I seen something exactly the same January in 2009. I don't remember the exact date but the day they had the Super Bowl, so I cant forget that.
