2 fireball type obects over Fullerton-Placentia-Yorba Linda

Last nite at 6pm we were travelling west on the 91fwy. My wife said "what the hell are those" and i looked and saw a small plane and two bright lights. At first I thought they were passenger jets lining up for LAX. But they dropped sharply down so I pulled over at the St College exit to watch these UFOs Looked like fireballs with firetrails. One extinguished itself but the other one kept on. there was a helicopter circling (could have been a small plane) these UFOs. The one that disappeared, reappeared. They then moved sideways, stopped, hovered and then moved sideways again. Then after about 90 seconds they disappeared. The small plane/helicopter then flew overhead towards Costa Mesa/Newport Beach direction. It was surreal
