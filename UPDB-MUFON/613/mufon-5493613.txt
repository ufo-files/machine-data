Myself along with three other witnesses observed 10 red Lights hovering across the bay. These lights were in the shape of a triangle just like the red dots on a bowling alley. They were also directly across from the farm were I had observed a different kind of UFO in my youth(Case#65887). These Lights hovered for quite awhile. WE observed them for 45 minutes to an hour and left. They were still their when we left the parking lot. Their was no sound to be heard from the lights. When a light would fly away it didn't fly at warp speed it just flew away then another red light would come and take the place of the one that had just left. About a month or two latter somebody in Arizona filmed what appeared to be the same lights. We were trying to figure out if they were flares over the water but the formation was just to perfect. This area at times seems to have activity. A friend of mine told me of a sighting he and a group of about40 or50 people witnessed a craft come out of the sky, suck up a bunch of seawater and then take off. He viewed this from the same spot my friends and I Viewed the lights.
