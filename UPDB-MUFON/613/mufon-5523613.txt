Green Fireball with bright white fire trail, breaking up and exploding.

I was in my front yard, looking at the stars when I saw a bright green ball of light with a white firey tail.  Their was a gap between the ball and the bright trail. The trail behind the ball seemed to made of of pieces that were closely spaced but each making a trail. Then about five seconds into the sighting the ball looked like it brighten then exploded and went out.
I had a camera at the time but was only able to capture only a light that went out. Buy the time I got it turned on the event was over.
