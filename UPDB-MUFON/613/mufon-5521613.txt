Seven very dim red lights

My brother inlaw and I were sitting on the trampoline out the back of our house in cranbourne watching the stars for about half an hour. After our eyes had adjusted we could see dim stars that weren't visible befor. We noticed at about the same time nearly directly above us there were seven very dim red lights,they looked like very small red stars hovering for about a minute. We both made comments about them then five flew off to the north and two went to the south overtaking each other in a slightly zig zag pattern almost like they were playing. Both of us observed the two until we couldn't see them any more,not that they shot off they just got to dim, don't know what the other five did .They did get up to speed pretty quick. I don't know if we have anything that can hover that high up or why seven air craft would meet up like that then go separate ways. It is annoying because I don't think I will ever know what they were but I can be sure we both saw something strange that night just wish I had a definitive answer.
