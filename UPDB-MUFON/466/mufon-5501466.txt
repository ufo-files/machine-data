{dr}Sphere hovered above trees for 15 minutes or less. Witnessed by 3 people.

In June of 1961, my two brothers and I, ages 11,9, and 14 were playing baseball in a lot next to my parents house located in the Valley area of Albuquerque,N.M. We saw a red streak fly overhead at a phenomenal speed, no noise. We saw it go towards the west mesa about 10 miles from the valley due west and it turned back towards the east. We saw it come near our neighborhood, but it was about two blocks south of our house. We ran down the ditch towards the area where it came down. When we came out into the empty field, it was hovering above a big cottonwood tree. It was a large sphere,dull grey in color about 30' to 40' in diameter with a red blinking light on top and another on the bottom.It hummed like a muffled helicopter engine as it rotated. There were windows on the upper half of the sphere and it was rotating very slowly. My brothers and I were no more than 30' feet away from it and we were astonished to say the least. We started yelling at it and as we did, it moved to the northeast about 70 feet and stopped rotating. We could see figures peering out the windows on the craft and at that point, we decided to get away from there. It then took off due east and as it did, it turned red from it's velocity and was over the Sandia mountains in a matter of seconds, distance of about 20 miles from the Valley. We watched the 10pm news and several people saw it, but it was reported as an advertising balloon.I don't know if anyone was as close as we were.
