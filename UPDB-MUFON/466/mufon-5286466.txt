Driving towards work in the morning in palm coast florida

I was driving for work at 10:11 am at Palm Coast Pkwy stop crossing Cypress Point Pkwy i was looking up at the beautiful weather and i saw big eagle so i lean forward to take a glance of the bird then i see a oval silver grey white object hovering under the clouds . I already reported this a website UFOnetwork and Peter got my statement and he ask me to sketch it. So did draw and emailed it to him.
