Two lights move across sky, then go straight up and disappear.

I was standing on my front porch talking with my neighbor when we noticed two small lights moving across the sky slowly from west to east. The second light was following the first at a distance that was too close to be airplanes, matching it's speed exactly. After about a minute, the first light went straight up and disappeared, when the second one got to the same spot in the sky it went rtaight up and disappeared also.
