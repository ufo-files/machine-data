Light in sky close to moon

At about 8:45pm in my backyard I felt an inclination to look up and saw a light near the moon. It seemed odd to me because it wasn't fully dark yet but I thought it was Venus. I focused on it and asked for it to give me a sign that it was an ET (or equivalent, because I had seen Greer's film about CE5). I looked back down, spoke with my son, who saw the light too, and kept glancing up every so often. My son said, "it's moving!" Though I did not detect any movement (if it was moving it had to have been moving VERY slow). I almost got out the telescope because I was explaining to my son that you could see planets in the night sky as well as stars. I looked up a couple times and after the third time I looked up it had vanished. My son remarked that it had vanished as well.
