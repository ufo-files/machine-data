{dr}Saw 2 oval sets of lights 3 lights each, evenly spaced apart and hovering

My fiance went on deck, to be in warm night and view stars. Saw object, came in excited and told me. I ran out and saw same in disbelief. We have both seen UFO's before and are open to odd objects and are always looking in sky. We have never seen anything like this. The lights were NOT blinking and larger than a plane and hovered and moved parallel and then slowly descended and the size of each light within the oval was a tennis ball size. There were two ovals. They slowly descended and faded away. For him they appeared to be a white light on each end of the oval and red in middle and that formed an oval and there were two spaced about apart the size of their individual oval. For me it appeared that each had 3 lights. He took photo with cell camera and it came out one figure odd shaped. And the sky was filled with stars and the camera did not pick up stars. He knew instantly it was odd and not a plane. We were dumbfounded by shape and movement and size.
