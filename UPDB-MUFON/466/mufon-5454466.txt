{rjl} We saw a line of lights that appeared one by one in western sky. One would appear, travel toward the east and disappear .

On 3/25/20 at 8:30 pm I looked out our door toward the West and saw a bright star like light appear for about 4 seconds and then disappear. In about 3 seconds another appeared, following the same course. We saw about 30 of them total. All did the same thing, appeared for a few seconds and then disappeared. We stepped outside and couldn’t hear anything like a plane, or any other noise. We live in the country so it’s pretty quiet. Don’t know how many might have went over before we saw our first one. We looked back in the east and couldnt see anything. I did grab my phone and got a very grainy video of one of them.
