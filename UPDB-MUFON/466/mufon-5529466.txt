It was a brief sighting of the lighted object

Around 1:30 AM on October 23,2010 I was walking with my cousin down south pine st in Ishpeming MI we were heading north on South Pine St. as I Looked ahead I saw a round green light it appeared to be coming straight down as if to land slowly almost like it was floating at this point I thought it was a firework of sort then all of the sudden it shot up and in a northward direction at that point the action was almost like a shooting star but in an upward away movement and disappeared faster than it appeared. My Cousin and I turned to each other in amazement and were both saying the same thing "did you see that". We thought through all possibilities and the only thing we can come up with is a UFO.
