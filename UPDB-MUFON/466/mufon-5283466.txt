Whitish-gray irregular rectangle

While driving north on B highway about four miles north of Rogersville, MO, I observed a whitish-gray object, approximately 100 feet wide and 15 feet tall, hovering about 500 to 800 feet above the ground and about 1/4 mile in front of me. I first thought it was a piece of equipment on a cell-phone tower since it was close to that color, but it was situated horizontally instead of vertically as a piece of equipment would be. I observed it for from 5 to 10 seconds before I went down a hill with trees obstructing my view. Upon passing the trees, I looked for a cell-phone tower and the object, but saw neither. It apparently disappeared while out of my view.
