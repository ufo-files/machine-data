Hovering in plain sight

I was working at Tameside General Hospital as a subcontractor at the the time of the sighting. My job required me to walk around the hospital site for most of the day. The hospital happens to have one building that gifts a spectacular view of the city of Manchester, particularly at dusk and dawn.

I got called to one of the top floors of said building to carry out a small job. After leaving the ward I walked down the staircase (a good excuse to have a look at the view). It was early but I had a look out of the window and something that caught my eye. It was PITCH BLACK!

I grew up under a flight path, the hospital in question is in the flight path for Manchester airport and I regularly look at planes but something this black just jumped out at me.

I stopped and watched but it didn't move. I stayed and watched for possibly 2-3 minutes before a young family came down the staircase and I moved on. 

I got to my van and moved to the next job that was a few hundred yards down the road. At this point I was still confused at what I had seen. On the way to the next site there is a small traffic junction. I looked up and it was till there. I assumed it had been a balloon or a drone. When I arrived at the next site I glanced up wondering if it was still there? It was.

The 2 photographs where taken on the way to the 2nd site whilst at the traffic lights. The video I have was taken from the car park of the 2nd site.
