Just wondering if anyone else saw a stationary light in the south sky at 6 am over Dayton Ohio. First observed light when drink coffee outside. At first it looked like a star but it was flashing. I got out a set of binoculars (16x50) and then you could see 3 distinct lights white, blue, green. After watching for 10 minutes with binoculars I got out my telescope. With the telescope the object was a bright diamond white light in the center with green horizontal lights and blue/yellow vertical lights. My wife and daughter both watched the object with me. You could view it till dawn washed out the sky
