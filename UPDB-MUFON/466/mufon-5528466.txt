Rotating lights in the sky over Roseville, CA

My relative was heading home (driving) that evening about 9 p.m. from Roseville, CA heading south and noticed white lights rotating in the sky over Roseville that lasted for 10 minutes or so. Couldn't make out any shape of craft. There were probably 3 or 4 different lights. They just disappeared suddenly. My relative knew it was not a plane based on the odd movements of the lights.
