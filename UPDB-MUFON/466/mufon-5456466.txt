{cak} Object ascended straight up over mountains, hovered, began moving and stopping suddenly

I was coming off of interstate 81 south at around 8:30 or 9:00pm on December 20th 2019. I witnessed the object ascend directly straight up over the mountains as I was coming off the off ramp. The object began to hover. When I arrived home I got my mother to come out and witness the object. It moved rapidly across the sky and would stop suddenly and hover. The object glowed red, gold, and white seemingly randomly. As I went back in the house to have my father witness the object which took less than 10 seconds, the object was gone.
