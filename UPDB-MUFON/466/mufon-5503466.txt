{cak} Triangular orb sighting along I-25

In January of 2013 I was the general manager of a full service car wash and detail facility in Fort Collins. During that time I was living in north Denver and commuting to and from Fort Collins daily. On Jan the 17th 2013 I was traveling south bound on I-25 around 5:40 p.m. near the Longmont to Dacono stretch of highway when I noticed a formation of white/yellow lights off to the southeast. I have video to collaborate this event and the thing that gets me the most is in the video the formation is a red/orange and appears to have moving elements that I could not see with the naked eye. I can also say without doubt that was not the color I observed while filming these lights. The formation hung around for 30-40 mins and I finally lost sight behind some trees getting to my home. Once I was there the formation was gone. I traveled that interstate daily for almost 2 years and am quite accustomed to a large amount of air traffic heading in and out of DIA to the west of I-25. I have never seen anything like this before that night. I have never seen anything like it since. I have sat on this 2 part video for over a year mostly out of my own paranoia. After having several of my close friends watch this and pressure me to release it I have decided to do so.. Any help that can be given in the analysis of this video would be appreciated. I would also like to apologize for the coarse language from time to time in the videos as my excitement was getting the better of me.
