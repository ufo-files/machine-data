{rjl} triangle craft. moved quickly in all directions

I was at work, and was out smoking a cigarette. Just looked up to see if it was cloudy. Noticed a bright light that looked like a star except in was moving in jerky motions, back and forth, then stopping, then darting forward and then in reverse. As I watched I realised that there were atleast 5 or 6 more in the sky. Very high altitude, but you could tell they were triangle in shape as the night sky was a different shade than the object. Then I relized that as the objects moved, it appeared they were emitting very dull sparkes, that appeared to be filling in the sky. Watched on and off for an hour.
