AT APROX 2-215 AM I WAS TRAVELING EAST ON INTERSTATE RT 80.ABOUT A HALF MILE BEFORE THE LIGHT STREET EXIT I NOTICED WHAT I THOUGHJT WAS A NEW BILLBOARD SIGN ALONG THE HIGHWAY'BUT THOUGHT IT WAS TOO FAR BACK FOR PEOPLE TO EASILY SEE IT FROM THE ROAD'AND IT HAD AN AQUA MARINE COLORED BACK END THAT PULSED FROM RIGHT TO LEFT SLOWLY' THEN I NOTICED THAT IT WAS A GIANT TRIANGLE SHAPED CRAFT FLOATING.IT HAD VERY SHARP CORNERS AND NO LIGHTS ON THE UNDERSIDE.A SHAFT OF LIGHT CAME OUT OF BOTTOM TO THE GROUND FOR ABOUT 2 SECONDS THEN STOPPED.
