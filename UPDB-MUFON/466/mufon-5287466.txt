Swarm/flock of objects in the sky that started twinkling and dancing as they flew North

I was walking my dog on a perfectly clear sunny day, and I noticed what I thought was a flock of birds flying directly overhead. There were hundreds of light colored, oblong, solid objects (almost white) with no lights and no sound moving forward in the sky similar to a flock of birds. They were not flying in a straight line or in a V formation but were in a scattered formation with varying amounts of space between each object. I noticed one lagging behind that started catching up pretty quickly, and I thought to myself "that bird must be flapping its wings REALLY fast to catch up so quickly". As I kept staring at it, I realized that there were no wings and that they were not birds.
I finally started recording the objects on my phone after they had passed overhead. My dog was pulling on her leash and the video is nothing like what I actually saw in person. As they moved away from me, they started to look more like twinkling stars and stopped moving ahead uniformly but were now moving in swirls and appeared to be dancing lights in the sky as seen in the video. When they were initially passing overhead, I did not see twinkling or lights but just a steady white color. 
There were no planes in the area at the time of the sighting to provide a reference point for the altitude of the objects, but they were pretty high up in the sky. It appeared to me that they were flying from the direction of Richmond International Airport toward Hanover County Airport.
