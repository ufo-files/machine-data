{nd} 3 orange orange-red balls of light that always travel behind each other and then disappear. .

This is about the 5th time I have seen these lights all from different areas, this time I decided to take photos with my cell phone. I was driving and happen to notice in the sky of the small orange-red lights so I pulled into a parking lot of the school where they were going to be moving over. There were 3 in a row, they move slow with no sound and then it appears that this ball of light is starting to fizzle out and then they completely disappear. The other occasions I saw them around dusk and that time 6 travel in a line one behind each other as they always do. They do not fly high, maybe about 300 feet in the air. I have never seen only 1 of these lights, there is at least 2 or more every time I have seen them. About a year and a half ago is the first time I saw them, there were 3 of them I noticed moving over the Delaware River towards the Trenton Makes word takes Bridge. My mother pulled over as soon as we got off the bridge into Morrisville and I couldn't help but watch, as they were getting closer towards the bridge, they made a left turn and floated over Trenton and disappeared right before my eyes over the city. The bridge is not a high bridge so if they would have continued in the direction of the bridge they would have hit it. If you have questions please feel free to contact me.
