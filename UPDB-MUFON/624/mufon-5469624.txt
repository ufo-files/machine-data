{rjl} Basically hovering orb-like 2-3 times the distance of airplanes Then disappeared

Out back of my shop talking to an employee. Looking up like usual. I spotted it hovering above clouds & 2-3 times the distance of airplanes. It made a slight circular directional movement. I knew it was a UFO since it was not behaving like an airplane or other common aircraft. This went on about 1.5 minutes. Both myself & employee saw it. It was like an orb or a star. My employee mentioned airplanes to compare. I diverted my eye lock on the object to compare to the current aircraft in the vicinity. When I looked back out was gone. I searched the sky for several minutes & didn't see it again. We discussed that it was not a star or planet becoming visable because if it was we'd all be able to see it. I am confident it was not a satellite or space station as I've witnessed those before & it did not traverse the sky line.
