Bright white light about the size of a squash. The first light was the size of a bike light and the same size crossing the intersection.

I saw small light crossing the intersection. I thought it was a bike crossing the road with its light on. I slowed down as I approached the intersection with my car. Before I crossed the intersection the light disappeared. I looked across the raod expecting to see the biker on the other side of the road. I saw nothing. I thought WTH. Nothing no biker. I continued in co road 35 going home. About a 100 yards down the road I saw a white light the size of a squash hovering next to a house about five feet off the ground. I thought it was a flash light, so I thought nothing of it. The next day I talked to the people of the gouse and told them about the light. They were not outside yesterday at that time. They said they watched a light come down the expecting to see a car, but nothing came down the road. The small light was the same color as the bigger light.
