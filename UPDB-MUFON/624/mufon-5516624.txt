Bright Starlike object

Was outside calling my cat. As I looked over across the street a bright starlike object caught my attention that was due South. It rose up then stopped. I immidiately called my Dad, the object started to move at a fast speed going north. About a minute it was very bright as it came closer and as it passed overhead. My Dad looked through the binoculars and could see flashing lights to see if it was an airplane. I also looked through the binoculars and couldn't see flashing red,green lights as on a plane. It made no noise whatsover as it passed overhead moving now east. It dimmed down to a red-orange color and disappeared over the eastern horizon.
