{jpg} Over40 Objects seen this morning

6:20am 12/12/19 - My mind was just blown this morning......Standing outside just now and saw over 40 object traveling from northwest to southeast in our sky. I often see satellites fly over but when you see those there will only be ONE object. The size and brightness resembled a satellite.These flew at the same speed that a satellite does approx. 20,000 mph. I watched them for 5 minutes as they all flew directly over head. They were all equally spaced at several miles apart and then at the end they were closer together. They were all in a straight line but at the end some were off to the side but still going in the same direction, keeping up with the others.....It was quite a sight to see and I hope that others could see them. Tried to call news station but no answer....too early I guess
Objects are too small to film in the dark with cell phone....tried but did not capture on video
