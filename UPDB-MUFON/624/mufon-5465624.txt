{dr}Saw object in sky seemed to be stationary with beyond bright lights. To low for plane to high for drone

Coming down route 31 about 645pm Sunday evening on 9-23-18. Still daylight out at first sight. Saw the object which we thought was a plane with its bright lights on heading towards us. Until we realized the height it was flying. It seemed to hover at first then started to move. We continued on the road we still would see the object intermittently. As we approached the town of Paxton, we saw it again hovering over the tree line but higher. We pulled over to see if we could figure out what we were looking at, the object still stationary at this point, started to move in the direction in which we had just came. The object had one bright white light on top and a red light below that and 3 white lights underneath like the bottom of this object. It moved in a smooth pattern with no sound and at on point seemed to speed up then slow down. We watched it till it was out of sight. We were confused and curious about what we saw. Just trying to make sense out if it .
