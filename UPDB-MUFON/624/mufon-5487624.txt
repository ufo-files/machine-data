{jpg} Did not see a craft. [djs, ci (unexplained wounds)]

I was living alone at the time. I had a bird, dog, and cat. For several nights around the same time each night, I felt a strange feeling, my dog and cat became restless, and my bird (a Parakeet) would contiously squawk during each experience. Each night I felt a presence, but when I would get up to investigate, I saw nothing. The last night I experienced this phenomenon, I don't remember getting up to investigate, I simply awoke in the morning. While I was shaving that morning, I noticed 2 scars, white in color, on either side of my throat, almost as if I had been impaled through the neck. The scars of of uniform size, and approximately 1/4 of an inch in diameter. Since this experience, I have felt anxiety and a sense that someone is watching me. I have been nervous to come forward up to this point, but I spend many of my nights unable to sleep since this event occurred.
