Bright White Light hovering at night several nights in a row!

For the last 4 nights in a row my wife and I have noticed what seems to be a bright star in the night sky over Elko/Ryndon, NV
  It isn't a star. It stays what seems like perfecly still making it look like a star, but then it starts moving side to side and up and down.
  It starts in the east then moves to the south and then comes north closer to our property. It stays in the sky all night! 
  I know as a fact that this thing must require tremendous amounts of energy to stay in the sky as long as it does, and as bright as it is. 
  Sometimes it will do a swirling maneuver.
  We are not using any kinds of drugs or alcohol. Part of me feels like it can hear what my wife and I say to each other. More than once, as we were talking about it, it's almost like our conversation makes it move closer to us. I can not be certain how high up it is but it is up pretty high!
