Large very bright stationary light in the sky

@ 11:50 pm on 4/13/21 while walking my dog. I observed very very bright and large bright light low in the sky compared to the flight path of planes leaving and arriving a Philadelphia airport. I realized it wasn’t moving and there wasn’t any sound associated with the light. At the time I had stopped walking and I was just observing this light. I thought it may be a helicopter but the light was so bright and so large and not typical of the running lights on aircraft. Then after between 5 and 10 minutes the light was gone. It didn’t go up or down just disappeared as if a light switch was turned off. I noted the time waited about 10 minutes to see if any type of light would appear such as blinking lights as on the frequent planes that fly by. I saw the lights of a plane far off approaching it eventually flew over with its headlight and wing lights and saw how much higher the plane was and that I could hear it while it was far out in the distance. I’ve walked my dog for years very late at night. I live in a rural area and as I look south the sky is free from light pollution.
