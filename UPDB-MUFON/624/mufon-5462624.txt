{jpg} UFO Sighting

One afternoon I noticed a UFO traveling across Roswell Road (between Hilderbrand Dr. and Johnson Ferry and/or Mt. Vernon Hwy) in Sandy Springs, Georgia over the power lines pretty low to the ground and only a few hundred feet away. I saw a black mist trailing behind it as it looked to be breaking the speed of light with fuzzy edges surrounding the aircraft's housing. There was a dull darkened color to the UFO, and it only lasted a short time (like most encounters), and lasted long enough to cross over the width of the street. The aircraft vanished into thin air as quickly as it came.
