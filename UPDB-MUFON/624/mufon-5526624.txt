Black Silent Plane with V-Shaped lights

I was sitting outside looking up at the stars because I couldn't sleep when I say a V-shaped formation fly over my house. At first I thought it was birds but quickly realized it was a craft. It at first looked black with lights in the v shape on it's underside. It was completely silent and flying really low, seemed well below the clouds. It was heading East from the coast - as it was right above and over to the left, I couldn't tell if there was a craft under it because the lights seemed to distort. As it flew over the garage it was out of the line of sight. I was stunned for a second then i got up from the bench from where I was sitting and walked over the 10 feet to look where it was going and it was gone. Clearly, this was absolutely not any kind of optical illusion - it was a craft.
