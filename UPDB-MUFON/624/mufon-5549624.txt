New York Sighting 11004

came home from shopping around 630pm.....noted what i thought was a very brightstar high in southwestern sky.......thought it was a planet, but after observingit for a moment or two i realized that it was becoming brighterand seamed to shift in the sky. watched for a few minutes then went to retrievemy camera.....also observed small silent high flying lights.....with twopulsating red lights which seemed to be traveling two and from the larger brightstar....then i viewed which appeared to be a military plane flying highoverhead in the direction of the smaller object with the red lights.......itseemed to be tracking it.i watched for about 15 - 20 minutes and took some photos and a movie onmy digital camera......then i went inside as i needed to prepare formy evening plans.....as i was getting ready i looked out my second floorwindow a few time to see if the star was still there and it was.however, by 735pm the time i went out it had dissappeared totally.
