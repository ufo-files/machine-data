I was taking the garbage out to the dumpster outside in the parking lot at my apartment complex at 9:15pm. (September 14? not 100% sure of date) I looked up at the mountains and saw a glowing flaming object flying across the sky. West to East direction right over my head and over the town of Fernie B.C. It was lower then a plane would fly. At first I thought it was a meteor; however it kept flying a straight controlled path. It left a long white trail behind the flaming ball and was moving at a high rate of speed. By the time I got out my camera phone to record the object it was already out of sight, into the valley between the mountains. I was not surprised or scared, simply unable to identify what I saw. Said to myself: I think I just saw a UFO and laughed. I told my wife about it when I got inside. She suggested I report it to mufon.
