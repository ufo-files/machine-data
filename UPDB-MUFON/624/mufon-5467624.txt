{dr}Looked like 3 moons, a larger above, then a medium size, then a smaller. They all looked like moons, they were in the mountain range 5 miles north of Shurz, NV

This event happened in the fall of 1997. Approximate about 5 pm. I was heading south on highway 95, about 35 miles south of Fallon, NV and about 6 miles north of Shurz,NV. I was looking east to the mountain range, on the Walker River Reservation. I saw what looked like 3 moons, in three sizes, a large sized, about the size of our earth moon, then a medium sized about half the size of the larger one and the smaller one about half the size of the medium size one. They were all in line with the larger on the top, then the medium in the middle and then the smaller one on the bottom. They were just above the mountains. They looked like full moons. The objects never moved, they just stayed in place like the moon does. As I drove I called my family in Hawthorne to tell them what I was seeing at that moment. This went on for about 30 minutes, well past south of Shurz, NV about 3 miles south. When I decided to stop to see them, then they all disappeared. I mentioned it to a few people, but it sounded crazy to them. I had always wondered if any one had seen something similar to this after all these years. I felt like I saw something very extraordinary. Is there some site that I can check to see if any one has seen something like this. I appreciate your time in this.
