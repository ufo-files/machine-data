UFO Go not far from the church

I was walking with my little brother when we saw a craft pass gently over us.

My brother and I used our phone to take photos, but our phones became useless.

I strongly believe that we are not the only ones to see them, but on top of all that, there was a weird noise..

All this lasted a few minutes, but this things and parted towards the church and totally disappeared.
