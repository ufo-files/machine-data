{cak} event made me a believer. scared me bad

I'm 64 years old now. This happened when I was around 12 years old 
(around 1970). Some friends and I were camping out at a small city lake in Iowa Park texas when the other boys decided to ride their bikes to town and get some snacks. I decided to guard the camp, so I leaned back against the pup tent to watch the sky. Later I noticed 5 to 6 starlike lights (in perfect line one behind the other) screaming across the sky from the north at a unhumanly high rate of speed. No noise, no color changes and no shape. just starlike. Suddenly halfway across the sky all objects stopped. They didn't slow down, they STOPPED. The very last object then shot back to the north what looked like thousands of miles and "it" instantly stopped. Note: no slamming on the brakes and slowing down, it stopped! All objects stayed in their positions for approx. 30 seconds, then the far right object shot back in line instantly without slowing (as if they dropped something and went back to pick it up), Then all objects went south at an unbelievable speed and disappeared. It scared me bad and I'll go to my grave knowing there are "FAR" superior beings out there somewhere. IT MADE ME REALIZE HOW PRIMITIVE WE REALLY ARE.
