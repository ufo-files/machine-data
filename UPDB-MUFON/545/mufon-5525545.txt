silver craft shaped like a thin cigar or missile seen in Tucson 7-13-11

7-13-11 12:48pm I was driving West on Speedway Blvd. I first saw the unidentified craft after the intersection w/ Tucson Blvd. It was a silver craft in the sky shaped like a thin cigar or missile. It had no wings. At the front it had a stripe of orange metallic color. It didn1t have a 1nose1 like a plane- it was blunt. At the back end it looked either like a piece of silver metal flaring out that was brightly reflecting light or it was bright light flaring out the back. See my sketch craft.png. It was flying low, going south. I turned south on Campbell and just before I reached 6th st I lost track as it descended and the view was blocked by the buildings. It was farther West. It had been flying quite fast and didn/t slow down on the descent. Also, it's front didn't tip down. It stayed horizontal. At first I thought it might be headed for the airport, but it didn1t go that far south and was farther west. After looking at a map it might have landed in Sentinel Peak area.It looked very much like the craft I saw in this area back in May, which I also reported.My reaction was "It is broad daylight and there are a ton of cars around, is anyone else seeing this? It looks like the craft I saw before- but if it were a UFO wouldn't that be odd? There must be an explanation." I later did a search to find out if the University of Arizona was doing any aviation research, but no.
