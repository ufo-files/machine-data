{dr}It was like a ball of fire that had geometric laser like shape, constantly getting bigger and smaller.

Me and 2 others were at Lake Ray Roberts in Sanger, TX taking photos of the sky on a cloudy night. I looked off into the distance and saw a linear light akin to a laser floating over the dam. I looked away to focus on taking pictures when my friend yelled really loud and pointed at the object which begun to pulsate and change shape. The object rotated and increased and decreased in size and luminosity. The object then had two smaller fire ball like objects fly up towards it and caused it to increase in size. It then kept rotating and decreased in size till nothing was visible but a foggy/red residue trail. Of my friends took a crystal clear long exposure photo that racked the movement of the smaller objects into the larger one but the file became corrupted and code only be extracted as a coding file.
