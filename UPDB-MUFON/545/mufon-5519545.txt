saw five fireballs follow the same line spaced about one minute apart

I was returning from dinner to a relatives apartment complex and when we saw the first one we thought maybe it was part of the Russian craft that was scheduled to fall but then another fire ball came and three more following the same path. Nothing was ever mentioned in the news and I have never been able to find any explanation. I was between buildings so I could not see them for very long. It was very strange nothing was ever mentioned.
