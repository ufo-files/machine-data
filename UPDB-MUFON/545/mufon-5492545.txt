{rjl} observed mist/haze enshrouded object emiting intense white lite,moving from se to nw,passing diectly overhead

On 6/22/15,at 10:30 pm central,my wife and i were outside watching the very bright and intense northern lights. I looked to the south,and a bit east,and i saw what at first looked to me like a huge fuzzy comet nucleus.It surprised me,and i remember saying to her,what the hell is that thing?I then realized that it was moving in our direction,so i ran in the house and got my cheap binoculars.I found it with my binoculars,and it was very strange.It was just a huge,greenish,misty/foggy ball heading towards us.It was going to pass directly overhead.When it got to be at about an 80 degree angle[just before overhead] to me,i could suddenly see a very small,orange-ish colored orb that seemed to be leading this much larger fuzzy ball.When it got to be about 85 degrees to me,all of a sudden i could see straight up into this fuzzy green ball,and inside of it was an object,surrounded by fog or haze,and it was giving off an intense,brilliant white light,but the light was only shining to the northeast.These objects were heading north and a little west,and when it got to be 90 degrees to me,or directly overhead,the haze or mist seemed to clear somewhat,and i was looking straight up at this thing in the center of the mist or haze.I want to say the object in the center was a large,dark sphere,and on the leading edge,but on the northeast part,it was as if there was a port or a door or something that was open,and the most brilliant and intense white light was shining out of it,but it only was shining to the northeast.I say the light was shining out of a port or a door,because the lite came out in a wedge shape,and the lite had shadow in it from the edges of whatever it was shining out of,and also,the light was casting shadow on the edges of whatever the the object was that the light was shining out of.After the object got to be at about 100 degrees[just past overhead],the thing in it.By the time it got to be around 120 degrees of me,it was getting harder to see because of the intense northern lights.But,i did observe a second small orange orb come from inside the large fuzzy ball,and slowly make its way straight towards the small,leading orb.The 2nd orb merged with the lead orb for about 10 seconds,then it slowly made its way straight back to the large fuzzy ball.After that,the object was fully immersed in the northern lights,and even through my binoculars,which i used to observe this whole event,i finally lost sight of it.During this event,the sky was clear,the object made no noise,and it moved in a straight line,from south and a little east,to north and a little west.
