{nd} UFO Caught on Camera which makes Four Events Not Three in June 2015

During the first days of June 2015 I had, and reported, 3 sightings from the 2nd, 3rd and early morning 5th at 12:35am. Twelve hours later a friend and I went for a drive towards the directions where we had observed the celestial crafts the nights before. Since i was the passenger, I decided to take photo's of the area because of the natural beauty that is New Mexico. I asked my friend to slow down so i could take a picture of these unusual spires to the north of where we were. I took the snapshot. A few days ago on the first or second of August, I went to post some pictures on my facebook page. It was then that I noticed something in the one I took of the spires. I saw that there was something in the sky which I never noticed when i was taking the photo because I concentrated more on the subject; the spires. But there it is... a dark sphere which was traveling northward as the the others I had observed in June were traveling. This then would make it the 4th sighting and tells me that they were definitely observing me. I had seen this same craft a few years earlier in California which stopped right in front of me, downloaded information and whisked away and out the portal in the western sky.
