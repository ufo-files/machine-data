{nd} It was a bright craft that was vividly ascending and descending and then disappeared

I came home from work and like normal parked my car in my driveway and then as I always do looked up at the sky. I did a double take and noticed a hovering bright red craft. At first I thought it was my mind playing tricks on me but then I noticed how it was moving in a very peculiar manner. It would ascend and descend rapidly and move side to side. At one point there was an airplane in the distance that moved below it's path. I'm not sure if it's a drone or what but it was extremely bright and unlike any other drones I've seen. I wasn't scared but more stupefied. I've seen unusual things in the sky before but this really was unlike anything I'd seen before. Anyhow, once I came to my senses I pulled out my cellphone and took a video. The video is over two minutes and I couldn't email it easily from my phone to upload here but there's also a second video. The reason there's a second video is because I saw the object descend and disappear so I began walking back home but just looked back in case and it had re-appeared. This video is a lot shorter but I took footage as long as I was still able to see it. I've posted this video on a few of my social media outlets and everyone insists it's a drone but I really don't feel that to be true. Does anyone out there have any insight or have seen something similar?
