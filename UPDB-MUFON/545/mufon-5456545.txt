{cak} Witnessed Yellow/Orange Orbs, North to South overhead

My spouse & I had just got into our car, that's when I noticed the glowing orbs, off in the distance. (from the direction of Elemendorf Airforce Base, about 3.5 miles away) We got out of the car, and noticed they were coming towards us. We grabbed our cell phones, got a couple of pictures and a short video, as they passed over us. We just kept saying..."what is it!?" "What the hell is it!?" We got back into our car, and in a few minutes, they disappeared into the clouds overhead. We could not believe what just happened! What we just witnessed!
