Brief UFO sighting over golf course

We were in the car and the object was to our right over the golf course. My friend and I noticed it at the same time and started screaming. It was the size of half a football field and was hovering about feet above us and to the right of the road. The bottom the object was flat and a circular form. The entire object seemed to be spinning in a circle but the lights on the side stayed in place and flashed. By the time my friend and I looked at our other friends in the car and looked back it was gone.
