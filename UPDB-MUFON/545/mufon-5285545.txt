Unknown object(s) drops straws

It was a beautiful day with no clouds. I was in the garden and had some coffee when suddenly some straws flew around. I was wondering where it was coming from and looked around and above me, then I saw an unknown object. It's just a big drone, I thought to myself. I can't remember some significant features of the object. No trail, no noise. It flew in a straight line to the northeast.

Unfortunately, it took some time to take out my phone and make a video, so the object was now only a dot in the sky.
Later when I reviewed my footage I saw there are more objects, but it's hard to different it from birds flying around. Seems like they flew in formation.

There is a military base about 4 km away from my garden.
