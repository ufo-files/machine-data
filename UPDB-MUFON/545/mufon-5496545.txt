{rjl} Two silver orbs hovering over Doylestown, PA

I noticed my iphone was stuck in a horizontal position and i had turn on the portrait orientation lock to readjust it. I had a strange feeling and for some reason decided to look outside my window, which is when I saw the first silver orb in the sky, which I saw at 45 degree angle on the horizon, at 1:15am. I was intent on watching it, because I wanted to figure out what it was. It was far brighter than any star in the sky and it was a partly-cloudy night. Sometimes I would think that it was actually just a star, because of how perfectly still it would hover; then it would make a swift, small movement in one direction and hover still again. I watched this orb for 45 minutes until I noticed a second orb, almost directly above my property, about 5 degrees on the horizon. That is when I grabbed my camera and started filming the second closer object. My camera is facing out of my window, all the lights in my room are off, so there is no glare. The camera is on a tripod, so I dont move the camera at all after I focus and zoom in all the way; though I do bump the tripod at 3:58. At 3am I stopped filming and decided to go to bed. When stopped observing the objects, the second object was in about the same spot and the first object was farther away, about 55 degrees on the horizon.
