Bright light ,round

At sea in Atlantic, observed bright light following my aircraft carrier,(USS Lake Champlain). Kept looking at it as we were too far out for it to be a Helicopter, It did a quick circle and than at an incredible rate of speed disappeared over the horizon, which was 1000s of miles away. Then it shot right back and stopped above the ship. It made some really fancy quick circles, got the impression it was showing off. It did one more over the horizon and back at unbelievable speed then started to descend as it got brighter. then went straight up up up until it disappeared. Never told, as people unbelievers.
