{dr}Groupd of flashing lights moving in a seemingly random flight pattern.

I was sitting in my dorm room working on a paper. I had stopped to take a break and get some tea when I noticed that the moon was really bright. I stopped to admire it for a little bit as I'm going to college in the city and I'm from the country. I don't get to see the moon that often here, so I wanted to enjoy this. That's when I noticed something that looked like helicopter lights right next to the moon. As I watched, I noticed that there were a lot of different colors of lights and that there were more lights than those that are found on a helicopter. As I watched them, the lights moved back and forth across the sky. At one point, they moved toward the moon, flashing quickly. I thought I would see the shape of a plane or something over the moon, but there was nothing, and the lights appeared on the other side. They kind of flashed and blinked like a firework that fizzles out in the sky. There was no real shape, just a group of several flashing lights. None of them flashed at the same time either. I stood there watching it through the window of my dorm, afraid that if I went outside I wouldn't be able to see it. It moved randomly through the sky covering large distances in short amounts of time. This went on for about 10 minutes or so. I heard what sounded like rotors over my residence hall and the lights went out. A few seconds later, there was the shape of a helicopter over the moon. It was too dark so I couldn't see what color it was, but it headed in the direction of what ever it was that I saw.
