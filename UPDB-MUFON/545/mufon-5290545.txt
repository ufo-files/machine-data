I saw the tail end of a Triangle craft

I was called by a friend who lived a few miles away from me to go outside and take a look to the east and what do you see? I went out and saw a rectangle with 3, what appeared to be windows going from right to left, and where the 4th window would be was instead a large round light changing from Green, to white to red in an un-uniformed cycle. It seemed to be bouncing up and down and sideways. It was slowly moving away from me until it just seemed like it was a large glowing star in the sky. It was a large vehicle and I am sure one could easily stand upright in it if aboard. Never saw anything like it. Never made a sound.
