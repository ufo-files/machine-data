Alien Gray Sighting

Last night I was having a difficult time sleeping and after I drifted off for a while I awoke about 5:25am and realized I didn't take the trash out the night before. So I gathered my shoes and jacket headed outside to take the trash to the curb. For some reason I had this really uneasy feeling but I ignored it. After taking the trash out, i went to the front porch to smoke a cigarette. As I walked out I stood there and all of a sudden I saw movement out of my perifial and imeadiately looked at that area. I couldnt believe my eyes, because it looked like an alien gray trying to sneak across the street unoticed. I even walked off the porch to see more and it had to be hiding. No trace of it anymore. I finished my cigarette quickly and went back inside. This looked like the classic gray alien, the skin looked gray and no clothes, there is a street light right in the path it took to go across the road. Another interesting fact is that it's stride was different than what a human would look like. I took a picture of my viewpoint from the porch later that morning so you can see in the daylight how far away it was and the direction it traveled.
