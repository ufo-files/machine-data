Sitting in my spa outside at approximately 3 a.m. and saw a black triangle

It was approximately 3 a.m. and my wife and I were sitting in our backyard spa and I was looking up at the stars when I realized a large triangular portion of sky was blotting out the stars. I am a former pilot and estimate the craft to be at about 700' altitude. The triangular black, unlighted craft came in from the east and over the top of our spa and was headed west. West of where I live is McClellan AFB. It had no lights on, it was quiet with no hum or noise whatsoever and traveling in a westerly direction at approximately 30 knots. It maintained its altitude and direction during the time it came over the house. My wife wears glasses and that night she didn't have them on and she looked up but failed to recognize the distinct outline of the triangle or its movement toward the west. The triangle appeared at least 600' long and 300' wide. I was a helicopter pilot and stationed in Alabama, Colorado, Washington, Ethiopia and Vietnam and never once saw a UFO.
