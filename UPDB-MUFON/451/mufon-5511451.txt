{nd} Thought it was a bright star.....but it had sparkling colors

Thought at first it was a big bright star. Quickly realized it was sparkling. With binoculars many colors were easily seen flashing/blinking randomly. Lights were very vivid, somewhat comparable to LEDs. Was visible for about an hour. Object slowly faded leaving the impression that it was moving away although its position in the sky changed little if at all. We have never seen anything comparable or as unusual as this.
