{tjd} Oval Object, Grey-Metal Surface w/Dark Underside Flying in Straight Line

OBSERVER/OBSERVATION DETAILS: Partly Cloudy, scattered cumulus with 5000 foot deck. I am a trained NWS storm spotter and amateur astronomer familiar with the sky, meteorological conditions and flight patterns in the area on the video, which has been my daily view for the last 11 years. My location is also in flight paths of LUK, CVG and HAO. 

VANTAGE/RECORDING DETAILS: Looking to the N/NE at about 1:49 pm, an oval/rounded object with a dark bask and illuminated (lighter) top side was traveling E/SE (from left to right in video) at an angled pitch with leading edge elevated. Object at that level was traveling against the air flow at an indeterminate distance below cloud level, based on visual observation and known prevailing surface winds at that time. I observed through my first floor office window for a period of about 15-18 seconds, the last 9.6 seconds of which were filmed on a Galaxy Note 8 camera (1920x1080), zoomed in as much as I could follow. I followed as much as I could until it was out of view. Speed was constant, and no noise discernible and appeared to be fast for its size. No direction changes, no noticeable wing structures nor any exhaust trails in its wake. 

CONCLUSION: I have never observed a daylight object of this nature, much less, been able to record it. I suspect a drone of some sort, however, it lacks any of the features of any known drone I am aware of, even an Amazon drone (but Amazon only broke ground on the Cincinnati-area plant in May , 2019). All known Amazon drones have discernible structures on them to either propel and/or elevate them (wing structures, etc., to create lift).

Stills produced from the video and included at the end indicate an oval object, with a dark/black underside and lighter, silver-ish reflective topside. It also appears to be traveling and an angled pitch, up and away from the direction of travel.
