lights in the sky flying northeast in a loose formation changing positions red in color no sound

At approximatley 2140 I was on my way to work ,when I notice a couple red glowing lights above the tree line to my right. At first they looked like Antenna Warning lights at differant heights,but they werent blinking. As I continued to drive and watch 4 or 5 bright red lights came to view and they were flying in a loose flying formation,changing positions in the sky moving North east.I came to intersection and pulled over and continue to watch no sound was heard. Seconds later all but one disappeared and the one red light continued northeast.
