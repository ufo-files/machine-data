Open hearing jets overhead , observed rectangular object hovering.

At 3pm P.D.T. I heard many jet aircraft flying above my neighborhood.
 I went outside to see what was going on. I observed more than 8
aircraft flying above at a high rate of speed .There were all types
of aircraft.I paused and looked up and then saw a rectangular object with 
red sides . As I looked up the object appeared to be high in the air and
must have been large as the jets passed under "the object".
 As I stood in the driveway ,a mailtruck on its route pulled up next to
my box. I asked the mailman if he had a moment to look and see if he could see anything.As he handed me my mail he agreed to take a look . As we stood in the driveway to my house the mailman said," oh yes I see it" . We stood there together for several minutes and then the mailman said " wow, look , it appears to be going up higher" . At that time we both observed the red color on the side of the object to glow really bright. Then there appeared to be a yellow flash in the middle of the object and the whole thing became bright. I asked the mailman his name and he replied ," Phillip".
 Then as we observed the object it darted sideways and startled us.
 I became so nervous I asked the mailman his name again.
  He appeared to beginning to become nervous also . He said he was getting out of here .
 As he left he grabbed a cellphone he began talking in a foreign
language in a very "excited" manner .

     After that I went in to grab some binoculars ,came outside
but whatever it was I could not find. For several hours jets flew from every direction over the area.
