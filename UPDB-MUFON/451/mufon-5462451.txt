{cak} Triangular craft flying slowly Eastward

I was standing on the back porch, facing west, just prior to 4:00 am. I have been stargazing lately and wanted to see what was visible. I had the impression of being watched, and looked up over my shoulder to see a dark triangular shape silhouetted against the sky. It was an equilateral triangle with lights at each corner- two white and one red. The object was moving eastward slowly from nearly above my house, with the red light leading. The object appeared to be close, only a few times the height of the trees. There was no noise. I watched it move slowly from above me and over the house, in a straight line, away and behind the trees, and out of sight as the house blocked by view. While I watched it I felt disbelief, waiting for my eyes to make out a more mundane outline or to hear some noise. While I initially thought it hummed, when I continued to listen after it flew from sight, I realized the noise was from the highway. After it was gone, I realized that if it was a plane on a straight path, I would have seen it fly over me while I was looking up, so it's movements before I saw it couldn't have been normal flight movement. I continued to watch the sky for several minutes, hoping to see it again. When I was satisfied that it had gone, I immediately ran inside to search nearby flights. After a few hours of research, I was convinced that there was no flight nearby either in the direction or in visual range when the object flew over.
