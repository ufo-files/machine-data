{cak} orange sphere star-like hovering pulsating emitting rays of light

My boyfriends daughter and myself were driving home from the drugstore when all of a sudden we observed a glowing orange spherical bright light hovering to the right and a little bit lower than the crescent moon. We both observed it and were speechless as we both knew that it was not a plane as it hovered motionless, pulsating and emitting what appeared to be rays of light, especially from the bottom of the sphere. It was star-like, and we felt that it was nearer to us than the moon itself, and seemed to be no more than a couple of miles away. We raced home to go into the backyard to try and set up the telescope, but the trees blocked our view.
