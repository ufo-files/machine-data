2 Star-like objects in formation

I was on my deck calling in my dogs at 7:03pm EST. My deck faces south. I was observing the night sky. The moon was at 70 degrees and 10 degrees above the horizon. It was waning gibbous with 100% of the Moon's visible disk illuminate. The sky had a scattered layer of very thin clouds. Jupiter was visible at about 130 degrees and 25 above the horizon. The 2 star-like objects were at 170 degrees and about 20 degrees above the horizon. The objects were stacked one above the other. The sky was very clear in this part of the sky. The temperature was about 50 degrees and there was no wind. The objects were about 2/3rds the size and brightness and color as Jupiter. The top object was slightly to the right of the bottom object. I observed the objects slightly move towards the horizon. They did not appear to be attached but moved in formation. The distance between the objects was about the thickness of my thumb while extend my arm. I observed for less than a minute. I then went in my house and upstairs to the master bedroom to get to get my wife to observe the objects out of the southern window and they were no longer visible. I also went back down stairs with my wife and the objects were gone.
