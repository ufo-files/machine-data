{jpg} Pulsing Light looked to be floating then drop then float back up

I was at my house. I took the dogs out and I live next to Ft Hood and some helicopters were flying over my house (south to north) like they always do since there is a Ft Hood helicopter landing site across the road from my house on base. They helicopters flew to the north then turned sharp to the east. I then saw this weird light to the north. At first I thought it was just another commercial jet flying over. Then I noticed it wasn1t flashing then all the sudden it dropped about estimated one foot from where I was standing. Then floated up two foot. Then kept going north pulsing randomly and still dropping and floating as it went on. I watched it until it just totally disappeared. To the south of me at the same time to the south Ft Hood was shooting flares in the sky which are a orange yellow color. This floating light I saw was white.
