Light Prism Under the Moon NYE

New year's eve 2015/16 just after midnight, my (now ex) partner and I were with his family in Brisbane, Queensland. After saying goodbye to other guests out the front of the property, we noticed a shape appearing under the moon. When it began to change size and almost bounce up and down we got our phones out to record it. Being believers but having never seen - we were ecstatic. Obviously, we had been drinking on the night so we wanted to be sure we were really seeing what we thought we were (apologies for the language in the recording). 
The next day, and many days after, we've watched these videos back and still can't figure out what we had seen.
