Transparent Object traveling at a high rate of speed

I saw the contrails of an airliner coming over my house and I was looking at it trying to identify the plane. While watching it an object went past my field of view at a high rate of speed about 500 feet off the ground. I switched my focus to look at the object and I saw an oval object no longer than about 10 to 15 feet traveling in a straight line at a very high rate of speed. The object looked transparent and I could just see the outline of it as if the low angle of the almost setting sun was reflecting off the surface. It left a trail behind it about twice the length of the object that glowed an orange color just like a cloud does in the setting sun. There was no trace of the tail left after it passed. It made no sound at all which was surprising given the speed it was traveling, I would have expected to hear some sort of wind noise or something. I watched it for between 5 and 10 seconds until it disappeared below the tree line.
