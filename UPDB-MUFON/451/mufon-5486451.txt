I was an employee at a summer camp outside Fishkill,NY. It was about nine pm. Someone alerted several of my co workers. We went outside and saw the huge V shaped object slowly crossing . Almost directly over us. I really cannot cannot say how long it took it ti go out of site. But it was several minutes. I remember it made a low humming sound. I grew un near helecopter base and had never seen anything like that. It came over from south to north at very slow speed. Seemed to keep same hight until it went out of site. WE were all very unnerved by this.
