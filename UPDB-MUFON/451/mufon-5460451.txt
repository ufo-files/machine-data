{tjd} Triangle shape with rounded corners. 2 lights beaming forward on each

I was a MSgt stationed at Eglin AFB Florida working in the 58th Fighter Squadron as a production super with about 17 years working on fighter aircraft. Had just come off shift and parked my truck in the driveway. It was a beautiful night and i just looked up at the stars when I noticed a triangle shape craft rounded corners and with 4 lights beaming straight ahead, two on each wing. It was directly over my house and moving north at a very slow rate. Tree top level. Appeared to be about 30-40 feet in size. It had a pale orange glow about it. I originally thought it was a reflection from the sodium street lights, but the glow remained constant. I started looking for panel lines, fastners, nav lights, exhaust ports etc... thinking it might be some kind of experimental craft landing at one of the several auxiliary airfields on Eglin. It was absolutely smooth. I was able to observe this craft for about 30 seconds as it slowly moved north. I never talked about this to anyone for years since i had recently signed an NDA for a SCI program I had been read in on.
