Felt compelled to google earth my old home in Belgium. Saw the sky full of strange color and lights.

I was home in Indiana. I used google earth to look up my old home in Belgium. While visually reviewing my old neighborhood, I noticed a green hue in the clouds. I looked further and saw a green orb of light plus a long plume of green and yellow light within close proximity. I scrolled over toward the sun and clouds and saw an equilateral triangle of white white lights which resembled small orbs. It images seemed strikingly odd and out of place. I rotated my google earth location in a different direction within the same neighborhood and saw a dark gray circular silhouette behind the clouds but still visible against the backdrop.
