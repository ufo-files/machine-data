{tjd} Falling tumbling gold mass with flames

After leaving 7-11, I looked to my left, the north and saw what appeared to be a jumbled mass of gold material falling from the sky. It had periodically flames eruptings as it fell. It fell Slow enough, walking backwards I was still able to pass the Little Casears till it disappeared behind the roof line of a building no longer there.
I was so concerned that it was real, I called the fire dept to make a report,a nd they sent fire to Lagrange and Central thinking I made a report of a fire.
I explained I saw something falling from the sky and asked if a report of an objetc,t meteor etc had been made by others. 
I was never contacted by fire, police etc.even for making a questionable report. 
The object, despite being very shiny etc, still looked far off. I thought it could have been as far away as Sylvania Street of farther.
I did note the observation in a book, but lost that book after several moves..
I wish I had more memorry of date and time, but was single father going through a divorce.
