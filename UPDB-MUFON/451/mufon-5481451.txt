{cak} 2 Tandem ball of lights. Orbiting around each other, then sinking into the water in the bay area.

Walking down the the beach from bluff point rd. I looked up from my phone to see the 2 lights out above the water just hovering. Maybe 30-40 feet above the water. I stopped dead in my tracks and just watched them hover around each other for about a min. Then both orbs sank into the water as if there were no water there. And instantly the lights went out. I didn't turn my back to the ocean as I waited to try and see them again. But when I tried to move I couldn't feel my legs and my heart was beating out of my chest. I walked back to my house further up bluff point rd. Without turning my back to the ocean, mostly from fear.
