{cak} Green lights caught by camera in circular pattern, followed by white lights in line

I was playing with my Canon Rebel T3i, taking pictures down my street and had a long exposure turned on (just under 30 seconds) to capture lighting. I didn't see these lights by eye, but after opening the photos, we noticed these green orb with detached disc patterns that seemed to be on a perfect circular axis. The object was not moving in a circle and constantly lit, otherwise, it would have showed up as a line of light like a plane does. About an hour afterward, I went back out to see if the same thing would occur. I took 4 photos a few minutes apart and there was no obvious lighting to the eye. The first showed nothing out of the ordinary, the second had a line of lights that at first I thought could somehow be reflections of some sort, the last ones had the same, but with a couple of lights "turned off". These weren't reflections, as far as I can tell, but also weren't stars, and were completely stationary. These were also shot on a long-exposure. If anyone can tell me what these are, I'd be much obliged! I will take more this evening to see if there is any consistency.
