I saw one of these (something) in Chatsworth, CA.
At approximately 7pm, summer/fall, 1993,
I was at a friends house, went outside to smoke a cigarette. As I was about to light it I happened to notice something in the sky coming towards me. It was above me but not as high as a plane or even a helicopter would usually be. 
It was "flying" low. I kept my eyes on it the whole time it was visible to me. 
I was entranced because it was so unusually slow and low for its size. 
It was triangular, very big (like a football field size) and hovering/moving slowly. 
it was SO black it blocked out the night sky & the few stars above me as it cruised by. 
it seemed to be going about 15 to 20mph +\- .
It had 3 white lights, 1 on each point. I saw it as clearly as possible on a summer/fall evening. IT WAS ALSO EERILY QUIET. 
it seemed like it made everything quiet around the area until it passed over me. 
I think it was moving east to west in a straight line.
It looked like it was as big as the neighborhood.
I know what I saw was REAL. I can honestly say it was something I have never seen. 
Im not sure we have technology that can do that. 
I havent seen anything like it since that night.
I dont know what I saw. UFO? or secret tech? It was definitely surreal. 
I was stunned until it was out of sight.
...It happened so fast, about 10 to 15 seconds, but seemed like everything was in slow motion.
My cigarette was still dangling UNLIT on my lips as I ran to tell my wife and my friend what I saw and experienced. 
They say my face said it all. Like i was in shock. 
I probably was. I was so excited to tell someone what had just happened that I must've looked a little manic. 
They DO believe me. Im still wondering what it was that I witnessed. 
I just wanted to get that off my chest. thanks
