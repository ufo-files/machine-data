During Hurricane Florence

During a lapse in the rain there was lightening activity which I pointed my cell phone camera to the sky to catch. I approximately took 2 frames per second hoping to catch the lightening. When returning to my room I viewed the pictures taken on my laptop. Looking through them quickly I noticed a light in the foreground. Behind the bright light there was another faint one behind it. Investigating one picture and zooming in on the object to my surprise the single light which I thought I saw was a sequence of lights forming a disc shaped object. Going from one picture to another the object moved great distances in a very short period of time.
