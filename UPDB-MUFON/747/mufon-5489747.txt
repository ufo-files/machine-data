{jpg} Moving lights on and around My. St. Helens

Observed 3 distinct lights on the side of Mt St Helens viewed from my vehicle on top the Swift Dam working night security shift around 2:00am, I observed several light's making vertical and horizontal movements in kind of squiggly motion back and fourth and up and down the side of the mountain, I flashed my spotlight at stationary light & it appeared to flash back in response, I reported to Hydro control that I believed them to be unauthorized snowmobiles, & possibly needing assistance, I continued to watch for around ten more minutes, I then observed the two lights merge with the 3rd at the base of the Mt. Then shooting streight up over the mountain maybe 500 feet, hovering for 3-4 min and then blinking out and completley disappearing from view. The entire incident occured over a 20 to 30 minute time period. Many thousands of hours working in the mountains at night & never observed anything exibiting the characteristics these lights performed on and around the mountain. Lights had a slight blueish to white tint mildly pulsing. Possibly earth lights?
