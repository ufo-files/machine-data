massive triangle shape, spot light

opening eve of firearms deer season. in the middle of the huron national forest. around 930 pm noticed a large elongated triangle hovering above tree line. Maybe 20 feet above trees flying completly silent. triangle shape with multiple white lights outlining the craft. the head of the ship had a massive spot light pointing toward the earth. less than a quarter of a mile away, roughly the size of a bus, (very rough guess). watched it for around 10 seconds as it slowly flew out of my vision. will never forget what i saw.
