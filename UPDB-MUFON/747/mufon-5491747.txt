I was out for a walk before the storm on July 5th to clear my mind. I was a mile from home when I turned around to walk back. Then I noticed a hole in the storm clouds to the north. A's I looked I noticed a dark object near/under the hole. Thought it was a dark cloud but it drew my attention so I took a few photos with my phone. Then continued walking then I looked again. My dog stood behind me whimpering and I saw a flash of light with two straight rainbows on either side. Then the dark object was gone. The hole remained in the clouds and I watched the hole close and took pictures of it. As I stood there I felt like I had been heard and warned or rewarded. Not sure which yet.
