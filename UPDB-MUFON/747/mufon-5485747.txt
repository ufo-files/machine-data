{rjl} 3 yellow star-like orbs in a trangle formation that moved like a hummingbird then shot straight up through the atmosphere in the blink of an eye.

Hi I'm from Eau Claire, Wisconsin. I'm 24. Back in 2003, when I was in 6th grade living in Eau Claire, Wisconsin I was huge into astronomy at the time, and I was looking at the moon through my cheap ghetto telescope. I was a perfect night, no clouds, starry night, big moon.

Somewhere around 9:30-11:30 I saw the weirdest airplane I'd ever seen flying from the right heading left (East to West?) above what used to be the London Sqaure Mall. 3 yellow lights in a triangle formation, no blinking red dot. And I'm thinking "what a weird plane, I've never seen a helicopter like that either." and it did move normally like a plane at first (no sound), that's when it like... stopped flying like a plane, and started moving like a hummingbird, I'm not joking, the closest to how unnatural it moved; curved lines, full on reversing and dead stops/stationary hovering, I can only describe it as like a hummingbird. 

After maybe 10 or 20 seconds it just shoots straight up leaving a light trail like a shooting star, and disappears right through the atmosphere... straight up, in the blink of an eye. At -that- point I became ecstatic, I was in awe, my mouth was gaping, I had goosebumps, and I was hyperventilating! 

I tried telling my grandma what I saw but I was too excited to make any sense. None of my friends believed me at school through the following week. Other wordly life means a lot to me, this golden moment in my life has kept me from committing suicide before, I'd never lie about something like this so doubt me if you want to! I'd give SO MUCH to witness something as amazing as what I saw one more time!
