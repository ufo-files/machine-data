{cak} Multiple lights traveling together.

This evening around 9pm my son was leaving to go to his house. He ran back inside and said to hurry we needed to see these weird lights flying together. We all ran outside and watched as approximately 15 lights flying together and around each other flew over our neighborhood. The lights stopped at intervals and several would go off in different directions but always came back to the formation. Several went higher and stayed stationary as others circled around. We tried to video them but the video didn't turn out good enough to see. My husband did call a Sheriff Deputy friend of ours and asked if he saw them. He replied he was watching them also.
