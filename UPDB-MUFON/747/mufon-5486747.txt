Was watching TV and I heard sirens. I look out my back door thats when I noticed the light. I watched it for a few seconds then I started to record. I heard no sign of airplane or helicopter. The object slowly seemed to hover and move from left to right in a small circular pattern. The flickering in the video is from the craft passing behind tree branches that was about 25 feet in front of me. The light remained solid and constant brightness the entire time I viewed it. I then walked into the next room to get a better view since there was a tree blocking my view. It took less than 10 seconds to walk to the next room and the object had completely disappeared from the horizon. I walked back into the same room I originally saw the object and it was gone. It was a "wtf" moment and I'm still confused. I'm no ufo expert but I spend a lot of time looking into the void of space hoping for an encounter. I know what shooting stars, airplanes, helicopters, balloons, satellites etc look like in the night sky. This was totally unique and bizarre. I hope you can shine some light on my encounter either man made or extraterrestrial.
