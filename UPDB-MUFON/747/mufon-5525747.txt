UFO SIGHTING RIGHT AT THE END OF FIREWORKS DISPLAY

MY WIFE WENT OUT ON OUR BACK DECK TO WATCH THE FIREWORKS, SHE WAS WATCHING SOME GOING OFF IN THE DISTANCE AND NOTICED A CYLINDER SHAPED OBJECT WITH RED LIGHTS IN THE SKY IT WAS VERTICAL GOING EAST TO WEST, VIEW WAS BLOCKED BY BUILDING SHE DID NOT SEE IT ANYMORE. WHEN I CAME OUT SHE TOLD ME ABOUT IT. OUR TOWN FIREWORKS STARTED A SHORT TIME LATER AND I FILMED THE END OF THE GRAND FINALLY, WE SAW A RED BALL OF LIGHT COME UP FROM THE RIGHT GO ABOVE WHERE THE FIREWORKS WERE AND GO EAST TO WEST TURN AND WENT TOWARD THE NORTHWEST AND DISAPPEAR, IT WAS CYLINDER SHAPED AND BRILLIANT RED LIGHT THAT FADED WHEN IT JUST DISAPPEARED, IT ALL HAPPENED IN UNDER A MINUTE, I WAS SHAKING AND TRYING TO FILM AND ACCIDENTLY TURNED OF THE CAMERA OFF BUT I DID GET A FEW SECONDS OF THIS SIGHTING,
