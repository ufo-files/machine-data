{tjd}  CAME STRAIGHT DOWN, THEN STRAIGHT UP, NO SOUND, DISAPPEARED

DRIVING HOME HEADING SOUTH ON THE BLVD. I NOTICED A CRAFT COME DOWN FAST AND HAD A TRIANGULAR SHAPE WITH THREE STEADY WHITE LIGHTS ON THE TIPS OF THE TRIANGLE AND A RED BLINKING LIGHT IN THE MIDDLE. IT WAS SO UNUSUAL AND IN A DOWNWARD FAST MOTION TOWARDS THE CARS AROUND ME. ALL THE CARS AROUND SLOWED FROM 45 MPH TO 10 MPH AS IT CAME SHOOTING DOWN TOWARDS THE STREET, BUT JUST AS FAST AS IT CAME DOWN, IT SHOT UP AND DISAPPEARED WITHOUT A SOUND. IT WAS JUST GONE, IT JUST HAPPENED IN 10 SECONDS.
