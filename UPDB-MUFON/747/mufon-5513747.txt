(GS) 2 Torpedo Like Objects Hovering

I witnessed 2 torpedo like objects hovering. No sound or contrails and no prescence of any other planes. They were gray spheres and seemed to sometimes be metallic. I know it was not a plane and they did not appear to have wings.
