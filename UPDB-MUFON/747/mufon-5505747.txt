{rjl} Red-orange starlike orbs in every direction moving slowly and disappearing

I went outside around 1155 and there were two steady red-orange lights slowly moving towards the east. Watched them for about 30 seconds slowly move higher in the sky. Then they fizzled out. Then I saw two more rising up from the horizon so I ran inside to grab my boyfriend. Those lights did the same thing as the other two; moving up out of the horizon, then start to dim and then finally completely disappear. We turn around and look towards west southwest? and theres a whole bunch more. Like 5 or 6 close together. These ones seemed closer than the others. They kept popping up all over the skies and then disappearing. They were in every direction! Oh and one of them began falling back towards the earth in sort of an erratic way. ... I cant see them being flares because they came from every direction until about 1225. It was scary yet exciting. That was so bizarre!!
