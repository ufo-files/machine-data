Wife and I seen two large UFO's at dusk above Buffalo peaks in Buena Vista Co, between 1981 & 1988

This was in Buena Vista CO when we lived there. The ones I will tell you about are the two that we watched for four hours as we came home from town one evening as it was just getting dark, we lived three miles from town on five acres. As we came home we saw two big UFO1s above Buffalo peaks, that is north east of are house. They had six windows above six windows on the bottom, the bottom six windows the lights stayed on all the time, the top six windows the light went back and forth from one end to the other, the whole four hours that we watched them. 
They where approximately seven or eight hundred feet above the mountains. It is hard to judge ten to fifteen miles away, there where five or six small white blinking lights coming from each UFO, they where flittering around the mountains, I have no idea what they where doing, or looking for, we never did notice any of them land on the mountain.
After four hours all the white lights went to the mother ships, and then everything went black, about 5 or 6 seconds later we seen two lights appear straight across to the east and about 2000 high, they looked like two small moons side by side just setting there, then they went dark again and where gone.

They say they still see UFO1s in Buena Vista, and Salida Co, Salida is 2 5 miles South of Buena Vista.
