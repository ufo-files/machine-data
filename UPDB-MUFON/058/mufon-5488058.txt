{cak} Laying in bed awake for a few minutes, a very bright white light traveled across the scope of my window, in straight line North to South.

I had woken up between 3 and 3:30am, and was lying fully awake in bed facing my bedroom window; with curtains half way open; and the window open about 6 inches. I glanced toward the upper window pane and into view came a very bright white light, traveling from North to South. Only in my frame of view for about 5-10 seconds. It moved in a straight line. Not moving quickly...just steady. The light was constant. No streams of light coming down or out from it, just an oblong shape to the light. No defined edges. It made no sound. I wasn't scared. I just thought what was that? I knew what it wasn't. It wasn't a plane, or helicopter. I didn't even think to get up and go out to my front porch which faces South.
