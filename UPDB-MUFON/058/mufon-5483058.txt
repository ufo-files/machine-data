{nd} Star object that quickly became bright

I was returning from taking my dog for a night walk and about to close my door. A light caught my eye (a very bright star that was not previously present - we have a small telescope and are amateurs and like to view planets). I went to grab my camera to take a picture but the moment I did the Bright star disappeared. The next night it happened again - and I called for my daughter to come see it but it was too late. So I had her look up if our Space station is in our area (we know the difference)and no it was not. It was a star that became in seconds very bright then traveled to the South but immediately disappeared when I ran into the house to get my camera. My feelings are that it wanted me to see it but not have validation of it. I only lost sight because it immediately dimmed down when I went to get my camera and called my daughter to come outside. Then it disappeared.
