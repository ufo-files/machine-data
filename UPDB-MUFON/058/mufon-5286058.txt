Flashes, twinkles, a show if you will, and some amazing discs

8-2-21 Monday: 9 p.m. or so I had an instinct to step out back and look in one direction and witnessed 4 flashes, twinkles, blinking stars, one by one in different locations in the sky to our left. My wife immediately spotted another flash as she stepped out to see. Then she also witnessed the one I pointed out to her. Twinkle Bells I call them. 10:00 p.m. I witnessed two more twinkle bell phenomena, separate locations but in same direction. Turned to right looking into heart of triangle constellation another blinking star followed by a streak of light out of the corner of my eye and I see an amber streak of light slowly dissipating. (Tell my wife), go back outside. Scanning the skies, I find another smaller triangle star formation right overhead. While having that in my field of view two swift shadow discs sweep across my field of view and, in a swift arch pattern, were suddenly headed north from an east to west approach and were gone. In a boom-a-rang design flight pattern.
