UFO sighting by police officer on duty

I observed a red flashing light traveling at hundreds of miles per hour from the Northwest to the south east, over our local airport and change direction to go directly south. South of the airport, the object increased its altitude dramatically and continued south. The only other object I’ve seen fly in this manner has been a fighter jet. Fighter jets are a very rare sight in this area. The only thing that concerned me, was the fact this object emitted no sound. I was able to record the object on my squad car video camera.
