{cak} 40+ UFOs Heading North Above Opa Locka Airport

I mustered up my energy and ran inside my home to fetch my sister and my significant other so they could see this too. I thought I took too long, but when we came back outside, another line of 10 more UFOs appeared and following the same path. From 2 additional different directions, Southwest & Southeast in the sky, more UFOs started appearing and also heading in the same direction the inital 2 lines went. These other UFOs were a bit slower and more spaced out, but the spacing was so exact in between each one and felt extremely organized. These additional UFOs were slightly less dim than the first 20, but they kept appearing for about 10 minutes about 1 minute apart each before they all finally disappeared.
