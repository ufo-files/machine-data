{td}a blinking blueish white light, that jumped from one place to the next

home east of the airport
checking locks and windows at 2am
spotted a bright flashing light to my east
20 degrees down from my zenith
not helo not jet not sm plane not weather balloon.
it blinked. few seconds later blink again several degrees
from the starting point. then blink again several
degree else where. Then it blink near or at its original
position again. the pattern repeated twice and then stopped.
The space between the blinks was about a hands width.
total experience less than a minute.
flightradar24 shows nothing for miles around...

I grew up in this area, and many of the old timers have
seen strange things in the sky while sitting on their porches
or in their back yards. Not sure why Rochester is 
such a hot zone for them. So I guess I feel puzzled about it.
It was not a long experience, hardly worth mentioning it,
but I figure every small report when added up together 
may shed light on what these things are. Sadly, it happened so abruptly there was no time to get a pix or vid of it. I might be the only person on my street or in Rochester to see this event.

Weather is clear, 36, but feels like 29, visibility 10+m pressure 1027 mb wind 9mph moving north east.
