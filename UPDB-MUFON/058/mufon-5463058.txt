I took 3 random pics of the cloud trails in the sky, But I was blinded by the sun. I didn't see the object until I went inside out of the bright light. I didn't think too much until I actually zoomed in on the object then I noticed how odd it was and it was under the clouds not behind them like the sun was. Then I looked at the two pictures that I took first. They both had two jets flying in the sky towards the object! In the third pic, I had walked a few feet away so it was a slightly different angle. Possibly I got one of the jets in that picture too, but still not sure what the other spot is next to the pole. I would like someone to look at the pictures to get another opinion. It was very different looking at the pics on my laptop where I was able to zoom in so much more. I had just thought that was strange I had never seen the moon in the daytime look that bright, as it was about 11:30 AM. I showed a few people to get opinions, they all think it is odd at least. I've seen things in the sky before, but never had a picture, so I wonder if I should report it. Thanks from Canyon Lake, Texas. It is not letting me attach the pictures on my phone.
