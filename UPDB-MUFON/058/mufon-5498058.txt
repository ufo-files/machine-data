{nd} White orb high in sky moving from East to West. No smoke or sound.

On October 13th, 2014, at about 3:30 PM, I went outside the get my mail. When I started to walk back to my apartment, I felt an urge to look up in the sky. I did so, and immediately noticed a small white orb moving slowly high in the sky from East to West. It appeared to be going in a straight line. It made no noise and there was no smoke. It was a partly cloudy day, and I stared at the object as it moved closer toward the blazing Sun light. One thing that is interesting about this sight, is that I was in a location where a neighbor once told me that he has seen flying rods at night. My reaction to this site was incredible. I immediately started doing research on flying orbs. Something within myself tells me that this object was not man-made. It's almost as if a consciousness beckoned me to look up at the right time. I was so changed by this experience, that I have decided to purchase a good video camera.
