{kt}fireball ascending into sky

I had gone to dinner with my neighbors. We were all riding in the same vehicle. There were 3 of us (2 adults, 1 child). As we came back to town later that night, we all witnessed a huge fireball ascending very quickly from the ground into the sky. We all were stunned and were asking each other what had just happened. We all saw it right in front of us. I don't remember seeing a craft, just the huge fireball. There is now a pond were we saw this occur.
