T he neigbors yelled over the fence to look in the sky so the whole family went out and watched it for ages it was in the local paper but I can,t find it now

We were in side waiting for diner watching TV when the next door neighbours yelled out there is something in the sky, so we all went out to check it out I was the youngest in the family of seven, any way it was a cigar like structure just hovering in the air a couple miles from us a few thousand feet up it was very large you could see it very clear every body was trying to say what it could be but it wasn't a plane or balloon we thought it might be part of a rocket it was the days when men were been sent to the moon but it just hovered there for ages so it couldn't have been that any way the local paper the Daily Telegraph had a picture on the front page a day or so later saying unexplained object in the sky last night and then it just seemed to get hushed up and we never heard or read any thing about it since it was in the very late 60s or early 70s the picture the paper took was in the dark but we were watching it when it was still light in till it got to dark to see it properly, and till this day even with all the new types of secrete aircraft I still can't tell you what it was.
