Very small UFO hovered over while my brother, nieghbors and I played baseball. We noticed it chased for a little while until flying off.

I am 24 years old now(2010), when this event occured I was about 13 or 14 years old, brother and neighbors about the age of 9 or 10 years old. We all lived on Old Stage Rd. in Valley Grande, AL. We were playing baseball, across the street from my house, in the neighbor's yard. During the game I noticed the object hovering and made sure everyone saw it too. We had no clue what it was, so we walked closer to the object and it seemed to fly away from us, so we started to chase it, it seemed to play along with us and then it flew higher. The object was about 10 ft. in air(when spotted), small(no more than 3 ft. in diameter), grey and no sound came from the object. At one point it almost even seemed like we could catch the object. When it rose high enough that we could not catch it, we just stood in the yard staring at the object while it slowly rose and eventually disappeared. We ran in the house to tell the neighbor's mother of what happened and of course she most likely did not believe us. I have always believed this to be an unworldly object, because the whole event was unbelievable. The only ify part about it to me, is the object was small and I have never heard a sighting of a UFO that size. Curious if there has ever been another sighting similar to this one.
