{rjl} round, grey, penny sized object

I was walking toward my gate and that's when I saw a silver round object. It was about 4 miles away over Highway 191. I was on San Jose Road. I watched for 5 seconds and it abruptly moved very fast and sharply, straight in every direction just a short distance, it did not pause at all and it did not have to pick up speed when it went in the other directions, it stopped and disappeared.
