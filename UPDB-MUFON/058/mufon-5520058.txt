8 fireballs that came up over the tree lines perfectly straight! then moved to the left and just went out

i was on my porch last night around 1145pm in abingdon, virginia. i noticed how bright mars was, then after a few seconds over to the ne i seen the first fireball looking thing come up and i was like wow what is that, so when i got up and moved to the end of the porch i noticed another one coming up right behind it, by then i could tell it was not a plane or helicopter. i was mezmerised to say the least, and one right after another they kept coming 8 in all and in a perfect straight line. after the first one got up so high it just moved a little to the left and "went out" then the next one did the same...they all did it. it was really weird. i asked the local weather man if anyone had reported seeing these things, but he said no. i know im not crazy cause my husband seen them and i was so amazed i even woke up my neighbor so she could see them as well. i couldnt sleep worry about this all night. ive seen some unexplained things before but this really made me nervous.
