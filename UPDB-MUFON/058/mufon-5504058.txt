{rjl} i awoke to a bright light and an alien in my room and was promptly hypnotized and abducted

one night when i was 5 years old i awoke in my bed and saw it was daylight outside. i went to a baby sitters every day and sometimes my mom would over sleep and i was in no hurry to wake her and go to the 85 year old babysitters.when i drew back my curtains the ground was illuminated but the sky was still dark. confused i shut the curtain and sat back down in my bed. I looked over in my door way where a little grey alien stood. my 5 year old mind had no concept of what an alien was. i thought it was a gremlin of some kind because that was the frame of reference i had.I attempted to scream for my mom but nothing came out. then the alien was on my bed staring me in the face. he let me know non verbally he was not here to hurt me and that i would be all right.the next events are hazy but i remember standing in my kitchen with 3 or 4 all together and a horribly blinding light pouring in through my kitchen window. I woke up the next morning and asked my mom if she heard me calling for her. she replied no and asked what i wanted and just like that my mind was blank and i was no longer concerned.                                   About 15 years later i am lying down attempting to sleep, a phrase i heard on the television that evening" screaming in the dark" echoing in my head when it floods back. imagine your mates surprise when you shoot out of the bed sweating and yelling you were abducted by aliens. Imagine your own psychological battle with sanity after you dig up some unbelievable repressed memory.Where are our support groups? Why does no one care? Why are we reporting alien abductions on a @#$%ing website? 5000 years ago if you saw an alien and were abducted you were hailed as a prophet. now everybody just thinks you are crazy.
