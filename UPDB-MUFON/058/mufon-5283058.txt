I observed this white pulsating light in the NE sky, that abruptly went very dim and shot off in awkward trajectory. This was no star.

Around 9:40 pm, August 23, 2018, I experienced a mind boggling event that I cannot explain. That very night as I stepped outside to smoke a cigarette in the driveway, I observed a few planes taking off overhead my house and did a little stargazing as well. I noticed something odd when there was a white light, similar to a star, become abruptly brighter and start to slowly pulsate in the distance. At first I thought it may be an aircraft in the distance with a searchlight since there was an airport less than a mile from my house. Then, I became really puzzled and thought otherwise as the mysterious white light started to move in a swaying motion. This is the moment as I started to pull my phone out and record, the “object” went very dim and took an awkward but rapid ascend the the sky. I could still see the slightest of ambient light/outline of the object as it ascended. The story gets a lot creepier being that I saw this same object/white light a 2nd time only a mere 3-4 days after the first experience. It behaved very similar to the first time but this time, I saw the object move at an unearthly rate of speed. It seemed to be hovering at around 50,000 ft, and as I looked up 90 degrees in the sky at it, the object went dim and took off in an eastern direction until out of sight from moonlight. If I had to guess how fast it was moving, it definitely moved faster than any fighter jet I’ve seen. No noise, no trail… just an object that’s left me wondering for a while off what it was.
