Strange pulsating disc hovering above the tarmac at Melbourne Airport

Looking back over some photos my son took, before flying out of Melbourne Airport, we noticed a strange artefact in a few of the images.

I assumed it was some kind of camera glitch, but then we found our friends phone (different location) had pics with the same thing in them.

One of the pics was taken with an iPhone in Live mode, so there a few seconds of video - It appears to flicker, and looking closely it jumps around instantaneously.

Attached are the photos and video clip from both cameras.
