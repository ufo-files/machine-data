{cak} Large diamond or flying wing shape

While driving north on Rt 47 at approximately 8:30 PM, as I approached the intersection with Turnpike Rd. I observed two white landing lights on what I first thought was a large jet plane. I first thought it was flying away from me as I was driving north but its size did not diminish after driving about 1 mile. I thought if it was a large jet plane it was moving far too slowly. I was travelling at 45 mph so it should have been traveling much faster.Then I realized it was actually coming towards me not flying away. All I could see at this point were its two bright white lights and pulsating white 1tail light1 intermittently obscured by tall trees. At this point I pulled the car over and stopped while looking at it. I estimate its altitude at 500 to 1000 feet with a span of 100 to 200+ feet. As I sat pulled over, it slowly and noiselessly flew above me and continued south along Rt. 47. As it passed I stepped on the gas and took off north. When I reached the intersection of Rt 47 and Turnpike Rd. in just a few seconds, I looked back and saw it continuing south above Rt 47 at the same slow pace. From the rear angle I could not see the two landing lights but only the white pulsating 1tail light1 at the rear. Note: there was no vertical tail. From this angle the object looked like a giant diamond shaped kite deep black with its outlines glowing or shimmering. It's possible it was a B-2 Spirit Stealth Bomber, although it made no noise and did not display the typical red and green running lights most plane use while flying at night. I am very familiar with all kinds of aircraft and I am not sure what this was.
