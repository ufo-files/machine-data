{kt}Saw a craft hovering over a house right at treetop level.

I was driving in the passanger side of the car driving on a very busy road and I see in the trees a row of lights to the left of the car. When we came to a clearing in the trees, there was a huge black triangle with bright white lights at each point of the triangle with one red blinking light in the middle just hovering above this house making no noise at all. We stopped the car and opened the windows to see if we could hear anything and we heard nothing and we thought that was very weird because the mere size of this craft being as huge as it was should have been making some type of noise and it didn't move but we took off out of fear. It was one of the scariest moments of my life because I felt whatever was in the craft was watching us just like we were watching them.
