Silver cigar like shape with sun reflecting off end passed over head and conitnued in a southwest direction.

I was standing on the edge of our patio with a fly swatter. At this time of the year the stink bugs are so bad and trying to get into the house so I kill everyone I can. We have a white patio cover that seems to attract the bugs. As I was looking up at the rim of the patio cover this silver like object caught my eye. It looked to be a cigar shape on the front part but the back part was bright like it was reflecting the sun and made NO noise at all. As I watched it continue in the sky I could only see the back end an looked like a bright ball in the sky, however, I do not think it changed shape. It was traveling in a southwest direction from where I was standing. I jokingly told my husband I saw my first UFO and told him what I saw. He made the statement that it was the space station. It could not have been because it was to low. I didn't think much more about it but either the next evenning or 2 it came by again. I went and got my husband but by the time he got to the back yard all he could see was the bright light in the sky. That night the news station said the space could be seen about 8:30 that night but this came over between 7:50 and 8:10. 

My question is, is it possible for the space station to reflect an image like that low in the sky?
