Was taking my dogs out like i have done 100's of times. Thought i heard perhaps a plane approaching. I glanced up and noticed a sort of flattened dull grey sphere at some distance, could of been a mile, hovering. Initially i thought it was the plane coming directly at me, but noticed that it had stopped. As i took perhaps 10 steps closer, it casually drifted off to the left and behind some trees and carport and i lost sight. I know what a plane is. I dont think it was. Im completely uncertain of what it actually was.
