2 bright orange fireball like orbs flying parallel across the sky almost floating orbs pulsating, no noise

Was going on balcony with my wife to smoke and enjoy the nice night. As soon as I stepped on the balcony these two bright orange fireball like lights come over the condo building across from ours.I screamed to my wife to come quick and see. They were flying almost floating in parallel to each other. they kept the same distance but seemed like they were bouncing in the sky moving at a steady speed.I couldn't believe my eyes. These 2 orbs were abnormally bright (not the usual air plane lights)and my wife,who is a sceptic when it comes to UFOs, was freaking out. We both stayed there,flabbergasted at what we just witnessed. The lights kept flying straight right over our condo and then we lost sight of the orbs of light.
