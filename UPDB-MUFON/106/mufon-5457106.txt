{cak} String of lights

Facing south, bright lights caught my attention to the west. I look west and see a string of white lights in the sky. At first, I thought it might be geese but then realize there is no moon due to cloudiness and there was no noise. I noticed the equal distance between each solid, white light. There was over 50 lights in the sky. I go to the door to get my wife. She comes outside and instantly sees a continuous line of lights in the sky. They were all moving in unison. They angled up 60 to 70 degrees, as if on a fulcrum. The last light seen was about 5-10x the distance of the rest and was slightly brighter than the others.
