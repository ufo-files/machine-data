More than 40 lights moving in unison

At about 4:45 am on 06/17/20 I saw what appeared to be at least 100 lights in the sky moving in unison over Omaha in a northeastern direction. I yelled at my spouse to come see the lights. We both watched as these lights moved across the sky. They were white lights and the best description I can give as far as their formation would be that they looked like a line of braille. 
I saw the line of lights from the start and at first I thought it was a meteor. Then a trail of lights followed in perfect unison at varying spacing.
I just looked at a map and 45 minutes after we witnessed this, the lights should be about over Atlantic, IA and if they follow my best guess of direction they will eventually pass just north of Chicago. We live about 2 miles from the airport in Omaha so there is NO WAY their traffic controllers didn't see this! 
My best guess is that what we saw were the new Tesla internet satellites but that doesn't explain the formation or number of lights we saw.
