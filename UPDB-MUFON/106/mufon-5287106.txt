Two star-like objects from different directions joined together and changed direction

I was outside my house skywatching when I noticed two bright white objects coming from different directions towards each other - one was from the East, and the other from the West, then they almost touched each other, and both then moved off to the Northeast without stopping or slowing down. They were moving very fast. Did not look like satellites or planes.
