{nd} Star-like orb shooting across the sky

we were coming back from getting something to eat when we pulled up to the sidewalk and saw a very bright spherical light in the sky, too bright and low to be a plane. the light hovered for a moment and then shot off very quickly, but stayed visible longer than a shooting star and then disappeared before reaching our horizon.
