{cak} Same event as listed from Kingston, WA on same night...Chinese lanterns

was living in Poulsbo, WA at the time and was getting set up on the back deck for amateur astro-photography. I noticed the same glowing, hovering slowly drifting orange lights as those reported from Kingston, about 10 miles away, on the same night. As I was set up with my telescope for the night, with my scope and binoculars and I was able to to confirm that they were simply "Chinese lanterns" drifting with the wind that night.
