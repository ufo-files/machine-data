{cak} Frequent Sightings, Star-Like, Hovering, Sometimes Zig-Zag & Drop Straight Down

I have filed two previous reports: 56387 & 56462 (56462 includes various photos).
Have seen these star-like UFO's for years now, visible on any clear night totaling from 2 to 7 in number. They appear to be trying to cloak themselves as a star, but unlike stars, they hover up/down, side-to-side and don't move with the night sky. On a few occasions, I have seen them zig-zag across the sky and several times I've seen them drop to a mountainside near my property, but no sound of a crash. 
There is never sound involved and they will remain hovering for hours, and strobe bright white on top and bottom and swirling/pulse colors of red, blue/green in the middle that, through binoculars, seems to be a propulsion system. 
Have photographed many UFO's and orbs while at my cabin. I am submitting this report to include a video (one of several) that was filmed on Tuesday April 10, 2018. Video of another UFO was taken last night, April 14, 2018. Have included a picture of an orb caught on film during a family reunion held at my cabin. There was nothing wrong with the camera when the picture was taken and on several other pictures taken that same night, orbs were picked up, but not on all photos. 
Have been regularly checking checking MUFON's tracking site in the hope of hearing from others reporting something similar. I can't be the only one seeing these nightly visitors, although one neighbor did state that he sat on his porch most nights "to watch the UFO's". Other neighbors who don't live on the hilltop as I and the other neighbor do, don't see them, as their view is obliterated by heavy forest growth. I have had several friends and family members view them from my deck throughout the years.
Would love to know exactly why they are here on any given night! Would love to know WHY they have chosen this part of Idaho to frequent. My best guess, after speaking with those in the UFO field, is that it may be due to the rich minerals, the isolation, and the abundance of wildlife.
