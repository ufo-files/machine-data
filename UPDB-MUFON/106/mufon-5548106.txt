five white spheres in a row at high altitude

Around 10 a.m. my wife and I were sitting out in the back yard having coffee. I had just finished relating a dream. Interestingly, involving ET craft and interaction with a being. I stood to go inside when I noticed a white object high in the sky. The sky was totally clear above and to the west with a few clouds far to the east I immediately sensed that something seemed strange. I ran and got our binoculars. We both saw a white spherical object moving north. We joked that it must be a weather balloon. As we were looking and discussing we noticed four more. All identical in a row moving north. These objects were very high in the sky. They blinked in and out of visibility. We watched until the neighboring buildings blocked our line of sight.
