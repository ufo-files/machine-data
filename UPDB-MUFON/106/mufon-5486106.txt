Orb appeared while photography in progress from cruise ship

Several pictures were being taken from the stern of the Holland America Westerdam cruise ship. The ship had docked and our stateroom was at the stern of the ship in the middle. A galaxy edge was being used to photograph the area around the ship without incident. Several pictures of a similar shot were taken.

It was not until we were reviewing the pictures, did we observe what appeared to be a green ball with knobs around it, and a green haze. It was only present in one picture as we compared it to the other pictures being taken in the area. 

This photograph was taken from the balcony of the ship. While this aberration may be a glitch in the camera, I can assure you, that we have never seen anything like it in the hundreds of shots we have taken with the camera.

We showed the picture to some of the crew who said they too thought it looked like an orb of some sort...

I will be curious as to your findings.
