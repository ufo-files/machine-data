{nd} Object heading east, double trail, center possible square, slowed down then sped up then gone.

Driving south route 9 monmouth county nj. Saw object while driving but what made me notice was the double light trail which was pointing west while the entire object as a whole was going east. I knew it was not an airplane due to light trail and direction and there were also no colored flashing lights. I first said is this a meteor or something entering the atmosphere. It looked like a comet with a double tail the most which was not possible. The core of object was very high so details were tough but was either square or blended into a star shape. Once the object slowed down then sped up I realized it was something moving. I immediately said out loud that this is strange. It did not seem like a plane or helecopter at all the more I looked at it. It was not a meteor either because I se them all the time and they go quick. Once the double trail caught my eye and the object slowed down I realized it was something else. I even opened the side window to ensure it wasnt a reflection on my windshield from the street lights. By the time I grabbed my cell phone and opened up the camera app the object was already speeding up then it was gone. I was mad I didnt even get a shot at it. Also looked out the side window again and saw stars in the sky in the area with some clouds lower in the atmosphere. The object was speeding up right before I lost sight so my camera attempt made me not see the final way it left the area.
