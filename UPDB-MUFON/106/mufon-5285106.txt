Boomerang shaped with external lights

We were visiting the 2018 Calgary stampede. I had my canon 5d set up on a tripod taking pictures of the fireworks. When the event was over, I panned my camera over the city skyline to take some time lapse photography. The camera had sequenced 5 second exposures. In 2 of the many sequences I captured the UFO on camera before it disappeared. I have the pictures in RAW where you can see the incredible details of what I witnessed.
