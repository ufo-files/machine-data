Multicolor glowing fireball over forest, hovered over the treetop going very slowy East, disappeared. appeared again, then descended lower than the trees and I lost sight.

I was working, I was in a car when I noticed a light above the forest that shouldn't be there. It looked something like a street light, but it was a bit bigger and way above the actual street lights of the street I was facing. I stopped the car to have a better look. I saw an orange/yellow light that had reflections of other colours like blue/green. It was definitely not a normal type of light with those reflections. It was hovering, going slowing toward East, it disappeared for some seconds giving the feeling it was loosing intensity, then it appeared again just as before. I was completely amazed. It then started to gown down and I lost sight of it when it went lower than the treetop. I tried to record it with my cellphone but it didn't work, I was too excited and it was too dark for the quality of my camera. I stayed there for a few minutes waiting to see it again, but it didn't come back.
