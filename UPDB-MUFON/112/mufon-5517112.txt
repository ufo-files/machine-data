{dr}bright yellow light with green and pink lights on the sides

In the early morning hours of July 2,2012 I woke up around 4AM. When I couldnt fall asleep I decided to go outside to smoke a cigarette, that was at 4:17AM. When I sat down and looked around the yard I saw a bright light in the distance. The light was to the south of my house in Hope Valley, RI. I was able to see the light clearly although it seemed far away. My first thought was, thats a really bright and big star. Then I realized there were no other starts in the sky. It appeared to be a yellowish light. I watched more closely because I thought it was an airplane but then realized it wasnt moving at all. I sat and stared at this light trying to figure out what it was, It was a big yellow light in the middle and on one side of the yellow light was a pinkish colored light and on the right a greenish colored light. The two smaller lights did not remain on but rather faded in and out. I watched this for approximately 15 minutes and in that time the only movement was ever so slightly to the side then back again to its original position. I probably wouldnt have even noticed that if I hadnt been staring so closely. After watching it for about 15 minutes I went into the house. When I woke up again and went outside around 6 it was gone. I was the only one awake to witness this.
