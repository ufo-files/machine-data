{nd} round red light in early evening sky

I had gone outside to lock up my screen room. As I reached up to set alarm, I saw the round red
object seem to drop in sky. Then it moved up and down and finally out of view moving down past the house next to mine. I went into house to tell my wife. She went back out with me but we didn't
see anything else. I kept going back out hoping to see it again. The red round light seemed 
large however I have no idea how far away it was from my location.
