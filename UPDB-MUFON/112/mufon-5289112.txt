Bright light in sky, and projected disembodied voice.

This morning at around 7:30 AM my boyfriend and I were going for our morning walk. Upon our return home my boyfriend pointed out a strangely bright orange light emanating from behind a cloud. We observed it for around a minute, and the light itself was undulating growing brighter and quickly fading and returning again. There was a final bright flash, afterwards I heard a disembodied “voice” that came from within my own head. This voice sounded like extremely loud radio static with garbled sounds resembling words, then I saw the clear image of a dead snake which decayed into a skeleton almost like a time lapse, as if it were projected on my eyelids. With that the experience ended. I talked with my boyfriend directly after and he experienced the same thing. Since then we have both been experiencing piercing migraines, accompanied by the same “radio static” we had heard earlier, though the auditory sensation fades in and out.
