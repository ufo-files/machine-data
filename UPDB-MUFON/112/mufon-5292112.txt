Box-Shaped, rotating, multi-colored, glowing, fading spectrum colors (RGB, etc.) as the craft moved at high speed/altitude across afternoon sky

Strange, box-shaped object appeared from SW sky and moved across to the NE sky in a straight line, moving silently, rotating end over end. Multi-colored lights and hues emanating resembled a sort of "box kite." 5:30pm, September 4th, 1980.
Observed from SE Orlando, Conway area of Florida. 

Seem to give off a strange feeling, as if it were not of this earth. No sound. "Felt" like it was piloted or controlled by very advanced, super intelligent beings. Hard to describe, as I was only 12 years of age at the time of the sighting.
