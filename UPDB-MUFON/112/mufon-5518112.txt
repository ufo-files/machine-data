Felt an urge to go outside and look up, I saw a reddish bright orb shape in the sky almost like a planet, but larger, and coming closer to my location.

After I/we felt the urge to go outside of the cafe and look up i saw the red glowing and pulsing orb. I grew larger - much larger than any planet or star. It descended slowly. Then hovered for several seconds, maybe 30 or so. The pulsing of the red glowing looked occasionally like a flame. Lke you see in th tail end of rockets. Surprised and alone I ran back into the restaurant and pulled my friend out. He is a mechanic and photographer. I asked him to look at the strange thing. We got right back out and it was still there..ony it was going in a zigzag pattern. Extremely fast covering a lot of territory. 

Then it would hover a few seconds. Zigzag slowly. Come a little closer. (Get larger). Other cafe people looked and saw this going on. It played around or another minute or so. Then it shot out and up into space at an unimaginable speed disappearing into the myriad of other stars. My friend and I watched it maneuver for several minutes. We were spellbound.

It has not returned.
