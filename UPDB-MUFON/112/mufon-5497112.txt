{dr}Bright orange orbs in the southern sky. Then secondary small, white light made jagged pattern.

On Dec. 22, 2014, myself and one other person observed three orange orbs in the Southern sky. It was about 8:20 p.m. CST. I ran in to get the camera, and when I returned was able to photograph two of the orbs before they blinked out. I switched my camera to video mode, but didn't think it was able to record the activity. Later discovered that it did record one of the orange orbs blink on and then off after making a semi-circular pattern. A few seconds later, a small white light can be seen in the lower right area of the screen appear in view. It traveled a very fast, jagged path to about the center of the screen, then disappeared.
