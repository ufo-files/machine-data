I flew to Atlanta Airport from Los Angeles, aprox half way there I looked out the window & observed a cigar shaped object

I was flying to my sisters in Atlanta, Georgia for Thanksgiving Dec 19th 2011. As we were half way into the flight, aprox 11am to 12 noon, I looked out the window and observed a cigar shaped object that was aprox 5 to 8 miles away & seemed to be aprox 3 to 4 football fields long, silver outer shaped along with darker features inside of the shape. I rubbed my eyes, kept seeing it for 5 seconds, then I turned to the man sitting next to me and said "look, do you see that"? He looked out and said no! As I looked back out, it was gone!I was jut relaxing at the time, just looking out the window from time to time,It was so large, thats why I noticed it. I know when I saw it it was not of this world as I was in the U.S. Navy & worked on Aircraft & worked for the DOD and Government Contractors! It was stationary didn't seem to move. I was excited! and nervous!Lost sight when I turned to the man in the seat next to me. I really hope someone else reported the same type of object on the same day, time, and location. please direct me where to go to find out that info, thank you!
