{cak} A shooting star like object

I was in Karlsruhe, ND attending a bonfire when i was looking at the stars. I was looking up cause I had already seen a few shooting stars but one stuck out to me. I thought it was a shooting star when i seen it bolting accross the sky. But the object left a long yellow trail that vanished on its end to the middle in an almost syfy movie twist as if the object entered warp speed. I was shocked and alerted my friends that were around me and they were in awe as well. I lost sight of the object when it all of a sudden vanished into the darkness of the sky as if turning off a light switch.
