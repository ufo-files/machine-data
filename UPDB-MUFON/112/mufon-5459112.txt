On the morning of July 13, I observed from my kitchen window objects with very intense glows

On the morning of July 13, I observed from my kitchen window two objects qith very intense glows from the direction of the sunrise.
Both moved in the same direction and at the same speed, remaining equidistant and perpendicular to each other. They left no trace of condensation and seemed to emit a glow of their own. For the next two minutes I watched the two objects drive behind a mountain, and the first one seemed to land. The second continued to approach the mountain, when it was "straight" and drove toward the sunlight when I lost sight of it.
I was able to make two short shots of the objects, but unfortunately I did not capture the moment when the second object departed at great speed.
My house is on the edge of my small town (8,000 inhabitants), which in turn is just over 30km from Varginha, famous for several UFO cases.
