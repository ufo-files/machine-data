Spotted bright white object in sky returning from lunch break, decided to film it as it seemed to be pulsating and moving quickly

Was returning from lunch break walking back to work upon spotting an object in sky in near distance. At first glance thought it was a balloon as it seemed to moving in an upwards direction. The object was bright white in colour and almost seemed to be changing direction while ascending fairly rapidly. This is when I decided to get out my phone and start to film as was thinking perhaps maybe it was a drone. It was a clear day with no visible clouds as object began to fly in a fairly straight line overhead. There were seagulls in the same path and one is visible flying at lower altitude in same direction as object. Did not think anything of it until after viewing my video I was able to zoom in on object as used my iPhone 6. Can clearly see that the object seems to be pulsating white light and can see another object shoot across in opposite direction at 10 seconds mark in video frame. Second object seems to materialize out of thin air and is moving rapidly almost too fast too see.
