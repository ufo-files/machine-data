{cak} Observed two amber disks approaching which passed directly overhead.

My husband and I were returning home from a visit to our daughter who lives near Washington PA. After leaving Cecil PA on Rt 50, the road changes from two to four lanes. It ws at this point that I noticed two flame colored objects heading toward us. One passed directly overhead and I was able to see the underside which looked dull gray outside the flame color sort of like the edge of a fingernail. The second ship passed but was blocked from view by the edge of the moon roof. I felt we had seen UFOs so I quickly noted the time on our clock and watches. Thus occurred last Sunday, Nov. 18 at 5:35. We were traveling east toward Pittsburgh and the UFOs were traveling west bound. The lights were steady. I have never seen anything of that color in the sky. They were not traveling side by side but rather one behind the other slightly off to one side.

I was excited by the viewing but lost sight of them as we traveling along a highway and didn't think to stop and pull over. I think we were shocked at what we thought we saw.
