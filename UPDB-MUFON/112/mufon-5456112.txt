{jpg} Two really bright starlike/fireball objects in sky

Driving home on New year's I slowed down on beaver Rd because 2 extremely bright objects caught my eye. I stopped my car. I had a passenger in my front seat and we both looked at these objects to try to figure out what we were seeing. These were NOT any normal aircraft. They were hovering a few feet over the treeline about 6 ft apart. They were huge ball shaped bright yellowish white lights with an orange tint on the outter edge. They looked almost like balls of fire. Within seconds of us watching these lights my passenger attempted to open her camera on her phone which for some reason malfuncted and wouldn't operate. All of a sudden the lights moved completely horizontal to the left about 3ft and just disappeared. As soon as they disappeared my passengers camera opened. This whole event was over a 30second period.
