An impossible fractal appeared with an orbiting ball

My partner and i were driving south back home from buying groceries in the next town over (tiny town). When she said loudly, "whats that? I looked breifly and said it looked like trash in the air (i was driving) next she yelled NO LOOK AT IT!
For context im the grandson of a world renound chemist, to me at the time science was everything i refused to believe in anything outside of established science.
I looked closer, a fractal orb falling into and out of itsself hovered less than 100 yards to the east. Ive never been as scared as this. I hunt bears, this thing was far beyond what we are. I parked and tried to take pictures with my cell phone (brand new at the time) it was hard to take the pictures, it required will power to look away from this object. More unnerving i could sense it was looking at me. Being a hunter for over 30 years i got a scope to look through and as i raised it to my eye the object was no more. This thing was huge made no sound and was not necessarily a craft. Certainly not nuts and bolts.
My partner called it a glitch and hates when i bring it up, she took pictures but they were taken on a phone without lidar, and turned out blurry. Mine has lidar and captured the UFO but it distorted my picture it made it appear much farther off and smaller. The object in my pic is not what we observed.
