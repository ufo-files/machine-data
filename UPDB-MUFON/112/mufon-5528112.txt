Brief sighting of lights, could possibly be explained, behavior odd however

In my back yard there is apx. 300 or so feet of ground going strait back from my house, then a tree line about 60-80 ft. high. I had my living room blind open, saw lights which I thought were at first an aircraft. However, I noticed they were moving oddly; right to left, left to right, lights perfectly flat with the horizon, like running lights. I called relatives to tell them something odd was out there. I was scared but then looked again. If the object's lights were spread out flat with the horizon before, then they tilted to one side (I forget which side), and moved side to side, left then right, right then left as before, only with one side tilted! I closed the blinds then because I was upset. This was at dark so I could only make out lights. Looked uniform like the running lights you would see on aircraft wings. But the behavior was just odd! I couldn't make out any forward movement (ie towards the house)!
