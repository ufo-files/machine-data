{dr}Like a pulsar star,

At 920pm on the 22nd of march 2015 I was looking at the stars from my moutain home, at 5000 ft., when I saw a narrow beam of white light. I followed it with my eys to its sorce and saw that it was comming from a object that was blinking red with other faint colors. this beam of white was lazer like and looked like a thread comming from the scorce that must of been at lest 100 miles from me. What realy was weird was the fact this light beam was perfectly still, did not flicker, wobel and for a minute did not move. but when it did move, it moved in a perfet parale motion to its first position. The thing that has me in question is that I have never heard of a white light lazer? And being so far away I would expect it wobel and or flicker?
