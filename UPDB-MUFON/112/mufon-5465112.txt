{rjl} Cigar moving north then turned east; very slow and low altitude

I was leaving work, heading toward my car. This was late in the day, but with plenty of daylight left and no clouds. I noticed a dot or ball near the horizon to the south in the direction I was walking. I stopped to watch, and it didn't seem to be moving. Then it elongated horizontally, and very slowly moved east. I tried to keep watching it, but trees and buildings were in the way. I jumped in my car and drove over to where it had been, but I never saw it again. But I did see an ultralight aircraft fly right overhead when I got to where I estimate the UFO had been a few minutes prior.

I think the UFO was cigar shaped, but appeared as a hovering ball at first because I was looking at the end of it as it came toward me (heading north). Then as it turned east it appeared to elongate, because then I was getting a side view.

I was not frightened, and I was in disbelief that I was witnessing a UFO. I kept thinking surely it would turn out to be an airplane. But it was going far too slow to be an airplane, and did not even look like one.

This did take place near a small airport, and I have worked in the area for many years since and never saw anything like that ever again. Airplanes on approach look like airplanes, and move quickly (except when moving directly toward or away from you).
