{jpg} Triangle shape over Henderson Green Valley High School Area 3:50 am

I was driving home from work around 3:45am, turning on my street which is a rather large hill. As I came down the hill about halfway, I saw a giant, black triangle in the sky with three lights on each tip. Bright white, solid and unwavering. I just happened to look up because out of the corner of my eye, there was a patch of black in the sky that looked odd even with it being the night sky. The light pollution here is terrible, it's VERY easy to make out weird shapes. 

I had a very, very odd feeling and felt compelled to follow it. I saw it move horizontally in the southwest direction of me in a slow, hovering fashion. Trees started to obstruct the view, so I sped up o try and go to the direction I saw it hover and it was suddenly GONE. 

The entire time I followed it I just felt compelled to keep going until I could get close enough to maybe take video. But I didn't make it fast enough. What's horrifying is that once it became obstructed by trees, it was gone. I went to the area where those trees were and there was nothing in the sky at all. 

I live near an airport. This thing was larger than what a plane looks like when it's touching down. And you can hear large whooshing sounds when a plane is close to the ground, regardless of whether or not you're blasting music. I heard nothing from this craft at all.
