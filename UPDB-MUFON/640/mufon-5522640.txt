UFO - round glowing object in Atwater, CA - August 26, 2011 & November 16, 2011

I saw what I thought was a falling star on August 26, 2011 while celebrating my husbands birthday near Peggy Heller School. It glowed white/yellow and then stopped and hovered. After about three seconds it took off into another direction very rapidly toward Santa Fe/Merced. Only one of my other friends saw it too. It was a yellow sphere which turned blue when it changed direction. I work at Castle Airport and know that did not come from our airport.

I just googled UFO sighting in Atwater, CA today after I saw the same thing last night over Santa Fe going into Merced. This time it looked bigger and a lot closer and changed from yellow, to red, to orange, to blue and hovered over the road. Even after these sightings, I still don't believe in aliens. However you have to admit I saw some strange stuff.
