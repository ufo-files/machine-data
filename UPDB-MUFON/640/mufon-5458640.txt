Thought it was a satellite until it changed direction.

I was out on my back deck having a cigarette and looking at the stars and looking for satellites on a crystal clear night at 9:30 pm. 
I was watching what I thought was a satellite (bright blue as they ate) I was looking up to the East when I saw the object moving from the South to the North traveling as fast as a normal satellite would, I noticed it slowing down, my eyes were fixed on it when all of a sudden it turned about 17 degrees and made a U-turn and took off back to the south east at 2 to 3 times the speed of first observed. 
I ran in and told my wife what I had just seen, of course she rolled her eyes at me. 
This is not the first time I’ve made a report, I don’t know why I keep seeing these strange things, I guess because I’m always looking up at the night sky.
I can assure you that I am not an attention seeker or looking for fame, I am a 56 year old retired delivery man. 
Everything I have stated is 100% the truth. 
Thank you for your time.
