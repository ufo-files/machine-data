{nd} Ascent over Pacific Ocean

Having coffee on my deck at about 5:40 AM. I observed an object rising up some distance out over the ocean. The object was rising slowly and pulsating. It began to ascend at an increasing rate, and as it did it started pulsating faster. The glowing increased the higher it climbed. Then, in a sudden flash, it shot directly upward in an indescribable acceleration only to disappear.
