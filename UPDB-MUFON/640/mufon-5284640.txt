a bright burnt orange light traveling South to North

At 11pm on 10/08/21, while drinking a coffee and smoking a cigarette, a bright dull burnt orange appeared to the South traveling North toward where I was trying to relax; estimate distance at 5-10 miles. At approximately at the 10 second mark, the light became very bright, like collision light on an aircraft...at the 15 second mark, as it became its brightest, it completely disappeared like it was turning off the lamp or a room switch...gone! My watch read 11 pm; I went inside my home and immediately wrote down what I had just witnessed. I did the same thing on 01/26/18 when I also saw a circular object the was stationary, about 1-2 miles from me and a dirty yellow color with black around its edges and a black dot at its center. This object became transparent, a the only remaining visible part of it was the black dot which instantly turn off like an old CRT on a tv or early version of a computer monitor. Two things the same: 1) colors yellow and orange where both burnt or dull in color. 2) both objects? just turned their lights off. Like they were never there, but I saw them...both. At a 5-10 second sighting, even if I had a camera it was gone. The 2nd sighting had potential, but I never expected to see another object that night, so I did not have my phone with me. Maybe I should always take my phone with me lol. THANK YOU FOR ALLOWING ME TO REPORT MY SIGHTINGS.
