The craft looked like a bright teardrop with a gold/or copper tail

I saw the craft just outside my kitchen window when I got up for a drink, I rushed upstairs to my bedroom, which is above my kitchen to get a better view, as it was dark in February I had the bathroom light on which is next door to my bedroom but my bedroom was in darkness, the object was still there it did not move for 30 minutes, then it shot straight upwards into the sky, I awoke on my bathroom floor with no lights on at all in my home, I dont know how I got there but it puzzled me. I have a knee problem and a dent in my head just under my hair now and dont know if it is connected or not, dont know how this happened as my head was next to the small closed toilet door when I awoke on the floor in the dark. I Dont know if this is relevant but my father also had a strange encounter, he has passed now but my mother still describes how he fell into the house door frightened when he saw a star fall from the sky and get bigger as it came over our roof, he said it just vanished as if someone turned off a light switch. That was also in February, the night I was born 14/2/65, we lived in the house accross the road from the one I live in now and where I have seen the object. I live alone and have not told anyone as I am a Welfare right officer and dont want to lose my clients trust in me, but I need to get it off my chest as its puzzling me that my bathroom light was out and I was on the floor in the dark. I dont know how long I was on the floor for but I was.
