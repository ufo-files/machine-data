Two Red/Orange Wide Diamond Shaped Objects Flying

I was driving to Gastonia from Cherryville to pick up my husband from work. I was smoking a cigarette with the driver side window half down. It was 12:35am when I looked at the clock in the car, and had observed that no other car was on the stretch of highway I was on. Directly in front of me in the sky were two wide diamond shaped objects flying in my direction. They were side by side. Flying low, under the cloud cover. They were bright orange-red and glowing. No flashes, no pulsing....just a solid bright glow. I turned the radio off in my car and rolled the window all the way down. They made no sound. In fact, there was no sound at all! No crickets, no secadas, nothing. I felt drawn to them. Like I HAD to keep watching. They were not travelling fast. The object on the right just vanishing. One moment it was there then it was just gone. I pulled over onto a small gravel path with tall grass on both sides. I leaned forward and continued looking up. The object that was on the left seemed to stop and hover above my vehicle then it just vanished like the first one. I sat there stunned and quickly noted the time. It was a 3 minute time period that I had witnessed these objects. I immediately called my husband. My brain was rattled. I almost felt numb, not knowing what or even how to think.
