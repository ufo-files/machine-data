{dr}Was outside looking at Mars. Saw a light to the west, it was coming over houses on my side of street. I stopped over my house, wobbled back and forth then continued NE

It was around 8pm 9-21-2018. I was out looking at Mars when to my right I saw a bright white light coming over the houses on my side of the street. It was not going fast. It was not blinking and too large to be a plane. As it got over my house I was shocked that it just stopped! It went back and forth a couple of times, just above me, then continued east, turning slightly NE towards Nellis area. As it went back and forth it dimmed and brightened just a little. As it continued NE It slowly blinked out. Not because of distance, just disappeared. I was excited as I have had other sightings. I was just interested if any one else in the area had seen it. It was the closest I've ever seen one, which did not help in viewing any shape or occupant. I have no picture, or video. I was also excited as it seemed, to me, to be saying hello. Why else stop right above my house.
