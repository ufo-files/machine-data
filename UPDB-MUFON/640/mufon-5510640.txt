Star hovering at high altitude, tracking the fixed and backwards forwards. Intelligent thought provoking. Myself and brother witnessed for 4hours

Myself and my brother were in my shed making electronic music on a Friday night. Clear skies perfect weather. Approx 1am my brother called to me begging me to come check out this ufo. I was fixated on the music I was working on and didn't pay much attention. He soon gave up and rejoined me. Approx an hour and a half later I went outside to urinate. I looked up to see this orb tracking backwards and forwards and sort of floating and sometimes making decisive sharp monuvers. My brother and I watch this for hours till sun came up. It made us both feel very unusual. We have had many sightings but have seen nothing for quite sometime now
