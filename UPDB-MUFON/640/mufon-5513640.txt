2 white objects passed over silently and disappeared.

I thought I saw a shooting star, but then there were 2 of them travelling close together. I then thought it could be a bird due to flapping movements, but they were spinning, not flapping, and travelling far too fast. The objects were rotating fast, and moving close together, but not maintaining a set distance. Too close together to be planes, they flew over at night and passed over street lamps heading ahead of us, before changing direction to the left, and disappearing in to the cloud cover.
2 of my friends also noticed, one knew it was nothing she had seen before but maintains there is a non paranormal explanation, one believed it was 2 white birds but could not satisfactorily explain why they were going so fast and giving off a white light (it was dark).
