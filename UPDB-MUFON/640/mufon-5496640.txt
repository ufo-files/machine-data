Orange Glowing ORB

I am Not sure on the exact date or time but i know it was a nice, clear warm night of fall in 2014. It was already dark out between 9pm- 10pm. So i had decided to take a bike ride with a family member, So about 3 blocks away from my house we then biked up to the park which happened to be at the side of us. There was a church on the same block and some trees and we decided to stop and take a break. But she then asked me what that light was in the sky, So i turned around and looked. We both sat there on our bikes and stared watching this Big Orangy glowing Orb that seemed to fly just a bit higher then the trees about a block away or less. The orb moved in a straight path like it was slowly coming in our direction, We watched it for about 4 mins. It had no sound or anything. The object then looked like it ascended over the church and up higher where it then faded into a disappearance. We then waited to see is the object would have come back but we didn't see anything. We biked to the church to see if there was anything but nothing. After about half an hour there was no more activity.
