2 pulsating fireballs that suddenly disappear.

On the night of December 17, 2009 I was outside sky watching. Southwest from my house I noticed what looked to be 2 pulsating fireballs. They were stationary for about 30 seconds total. I started filming and was able to catch a few seconds of it on film. It made no noise and it never moved. It vanished suddenly and I never saw it again.
