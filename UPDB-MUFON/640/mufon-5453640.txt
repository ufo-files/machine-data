{rjl} Four different convoys moving NorthEast

Last night I notice a convoy of lights following the same flight path moving NorthEast. The objects were perfectly spaced out from another , and it was a group of six. A second convoy appeared following a different flight path, but still traveling North East. There were five objects in this convoy. A third convoy appeared traveling on a different flight path, following in a sequence. There were seven in this group. The fourth and final convoy followed the same flight path as the first. One object in this formation broke the sequence and accelerated towards the first object in the convoy and than follow it closely . These objects are moving quickly across the night sky from of the south west to the north east. These were no commercial or military aircraft.
