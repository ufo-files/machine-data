Two orbs colored redish-pink connected by some kind of short unlit walkway. Dumbbell shaped almost, slow moving.

Walking the dog, out of corner of my eye say it flying above the house in the sky appx 2-400 feet high. Dumbbell shaped object. Two balls or orbs glowing red/ink connected by a very short unliit walkway or platform. Straight flight path. I was in awe and my dog actually sat down and watched it with me. It made a low beep/humming sound followed by what sounded like a squeek, like a hinge needing oil. beep...beep...beep...beep...beep...squeak. Repeated constantly, as though it were coming from the object itself from in the air. Object continued on its path and went over the trees and I lost sight of it.
