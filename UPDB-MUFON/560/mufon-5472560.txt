{jpg} V shaped large stationary object with 5 large lights eluminating the North South and Median of Palisades parkway

I was driving south on Palisades parkway heading home
The lighted high way and the fact the lights were not moving but stationary
Unknown so I pulled to the side of the road and got out of my vehicle as did all the cars heading north and south.
I got out to see if there was any engine noise. There was none. I looked behind me to the car immediately behind . I noticed that the driver had also exited his vehicle. we looked across the median and noticed that other vehicle were also pulled off the highway and out of their cars. I then turned to the vehicle behind me and ask "what do you think it is?" He remarked "it's not from this world!!" I agreed! After a few minutes the object quietly banked to the right and disappeared.
