Observed a boomerang and a smaller unknown object and other possible objects

Was taking test shots with a new camera I obtained. I did not notice the objects while I was taking pictures. I only saw the objects after I uploaded the pictures to my computer. In one picture a large boomerang shaped object can be seen at a distance. Below the boomerang and at a lower altitude is another object a smaller orb shaped unknown object. A third object can be seen a flying a few miles east of the orb. And a possible fourth object also east of the orb flying at an even lower altitude. From the metadata the first picture was taken at 18:45:46. I took a second picture at 18:45:50 that shows the objects had moved east. This second picture taken 4 seconds after the first picture eliminates the possibility that the objects were dirt or debris on the lens. I don't recall hearing any unusual sounds. There are no reports from other people that I am aware of that saw similar objects. The pictures are fairly high resolution and were taken RAW. The Jpegs I am uploading have been run through Lightroom and highlights and shadows have been adjusted to better see the objects. I have the RAW unmodified picture files if you need them.
