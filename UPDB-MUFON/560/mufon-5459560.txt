Northeast Ohio, Seneca County, Clinton Township 4:29 am. When I got up, a light was so intense against the window shades, was flashing. I thought it was a utility truck, the illumination actually lit up my bedroom so i parted the louvers and saw two flashing lights in the slightly over cast sky. They flashed simultaneously, brilliantly, so intense that it was like someone was flashing strobe lights. The lights were seemingly silent, to my perspective was about golf ball sized as they were above the tree line of a distant field, approximately a mile or more away, above the tree line which has some good sized oak trees. I ask because, I was a little startled and I would like to know if anyone else saw these lights. I am 61 years old, not given to flights of fancy but I am the pragmatic type. I dont disbelieve in such phenomenon but I want to have facts not hysteria. I dont know what I saw but it was very, very interesting. I tried to get a better, closer look but within the seconds it took me to go from the bedroom to the front door, they were gone. I marked the elevation as 0 I dont know the degree of elevation.
