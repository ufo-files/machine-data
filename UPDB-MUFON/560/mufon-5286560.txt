Soft off white Light - flying a straight path

The ufo was a off white or similar to a soft white light. The ufo flew by above tree line but not as high as a jet it was very close. 
The ufo made no sound.. The ufo flew in a straight path northwest to east. 
Before the sighting I had been stargazing as I was headed into the house the ufo came from over my house i immediately started the video and yelled for my family who had already made there way in.
The ufo did not flicker or have any colorful lights on it. 
In the video you can see a flickering star showing through the tree line. The leaves and branches are obstructing the view of the star. The star in the video is in the east and stationary the ufo is traveling in that direction. 
The ufo does not make any sudden movement the sudden movement you see in video is caused by my moving the camera too much.
