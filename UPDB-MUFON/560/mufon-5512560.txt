     It was 11:00pm. but I saw a bright spotlight on a big cloud and I looked at it more closely.
     I thought it was a big white cloud with a spotlight on it.
     First it was a big white cloud, then the center of it Faded away, not dissipated like a cloud, then it was a doughnut-like cloud with a spotlight but the spotlight belonged to the cloud. The spotlight was coming from Inside the cloud. It was a large ball of light that circled the outside of the doughnut cloud ( like a cars headlight )only I didn't see any beam of light emitting from the cloud. When the ball of light reach to the back of the cloud it would disapper and reapper on the other side as though it circled around the outside of the whole cloud. (took a few sec.to come back on the other side).
