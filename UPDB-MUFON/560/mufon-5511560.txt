Myself and my friend witnessed a ufo then 4 jets!!!

I am in total disbelief at what i witnessed this evening, i was in my garden with my friend we were looking up at the stars when we saw an unidentified flying object... we tried to debunk what we were witnessing playing with the theories could it be a helicopter?. We dismissed this as the object was flying too high... Plane maybe?. I am now a firm believer that we are not alone.
