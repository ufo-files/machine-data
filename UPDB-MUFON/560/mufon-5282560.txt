Witnessed a bright shiny object that hovered, then moved horizontally and hovered again. Then slowly rose until no longer visible. Bright sunny day.

One afternoon, while reclining in my front yard, waiting for my kids school bus, something shiny caught my eye. I looked up and saw an object. The object was hovering (stationary). The middle (center) of the object had panels that were spinning rapidly and reflecting the sunlight. The object was a saucer in shape but taller. It sat stationary for several minutes, then slowly moved to my left and hovered again. Then it very slowly rose until it disappeared. There were no clouds.
