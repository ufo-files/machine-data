{kt}I saw lights turn clockwise & change color from red to green to white, repeatedly

At approximately 10:30 PM on November 7, 2013, I looked out my patio door to the North. Above a 2 story house on the next block were lights that looked the size of a frisbee. They were red at first, then rotated closkwise and changed to green, then rotated again and changed to white. The were red longer than green or white. At about 11:00 PM, I went outside toward the house but the lights seemed abostructed by trees so I went home. This was still happening when I went to bed at midnight. I never saw those lights before nor since. (I called my husband over to look at the lights & he saw them too.)
