{dr}Group of Ufos seen gliding across sky my me and wife

Coming home from dinner and driving south on Monroe st. My wife and I saw approximately.15 glowing orange spheres moving across the sky from west to east. they were silent and the group was followed by two more spheres identical to those in the group. the grouping seemed random and not an organized formation. the drivers around us were all looking up at the phenomena and some had rolled down their windows and had their heads outside to get a better look. The group formation never changed and there were no flashing lights or sound. They moved from one horizon to the next in about 3 minutes.2
