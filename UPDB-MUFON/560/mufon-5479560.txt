{cak} Black bow tie looking object zig zagged and looked like it was floating down before rising up and heading over mountains

Was walking my dogs near santa monica blvd and ogden just before sunset. Saw a strange black object at first I thought it was a helicopter but as it seemed to zig zag I determined it wasn't based on its movements (and a helicopter was elsewhere in the sky for comparison). I carefully watched as it seemed to be coming closer and lower, it had no lights and almost looked like a black plastic bag that was falling to earth changing shape as it did so. At one point I would describe it as a bow tie shape. I thought it might be a drone but it then seemed to rise up very high (a small aircraft flew below it at one point as it was rising up) I watched it as eventually disappeared into the sky heading above the mountains towards the north. The distance and height made me determine it was not a drone.
