{nd} WAITING FOR FIREWORKS TO START ON THE 4TH I SAW WHAT I THOUGHT WAS A PLANE MAKING A LOOP UNTIL I NOTICED NO WINGS & LEAVING A FIRE TRAIL

I WAS AT CENTENNIAL PARK ON HARRISON & BEVERLY WITH FRIENDS HAVING A PICNIC AND WAITING FOR THE FIREWORKS TO START. I WAS TAKING PICTURE OF THE OUR GROUP AND STARTED TO WALK AROUND WITH MY CAMERA. LOOKING UP I SAW WHAT I THOUGHT WAS A PLANE BANKING, THEN I REALIZED IT WAS MAKING A LOOP. I SNAPPED A FEW PICTURES OF THE OBJECT. AND WHEN I GOT HOME I WAS SHOCKED TO SEE THERE WERE NO WINGS ON THE OBJECT AND IT WAS LEAVING A FIRE TRAIL, I DON'T KNOW OF ANY PLANES THAT DO THAT. I LIVE BY KENNEDY SPACE CENTER FOR 5 YEARS AND SAW MANY ROCKETS TAKING OFF, BUT NONE ON AN ANGLE LIKE THIS ONE. THE OBJECT HAD 2 WHITE SECTIONS AND 2 THIN BROWN ONES.
