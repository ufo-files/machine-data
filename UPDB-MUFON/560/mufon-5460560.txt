{tjd} 3 strange lights in the sky moving w to e

Looked up at 10pm to see three unwavering white lights in a perfect row just under the Big Dipper in the northern sky. The lights were spaced evenly, indicating either three objects flying in a straight line formation or one VERY large craft. The lights moved slowly in a straight line from west to east until they eventually disappeared out of view to the eastern horizon. 2 other witnesses with me saw the same thing. None of us could offer any kind of rational explanation.
