Light went straight, then stopped, then rapidly raced around the sky with direct path changes, then stopped, then flew out of sight at high speed.

I am 66 years of age. When I was in my teens I saw a light in the sky that I thought was a satellite. It traveled slowly in a straight line until nearly directly above my parents and siblings and I when it stopped. It then proceeded to dash about the sky, it did not make turns, it would reach a point and cut directly back at an angle. It did this for a few minutes before it stopped again. roughly above us It then took off to the west so quickly that it was out of sight in about 2 seconds. All I ever saw was a light, which was about the size you would expect a satellite to be be back in the late sixties. There was no sound, no exhaust. There is no way that could of been a satellite, as to what it is.  I leave that up to you to decide. A couple of months later I was out in the backyard, alone when I saw a large red /orange orb come down slowly at a 45 degree angle, it went behind the hill on the left of the Yakima Gap (from Selah) there was no explosion and no report of anything in the next days paper. It could of been a meteorite, but I do not know. Since then, in the 50 years since I've seen nothing strange in the sky. Yet I still wonder, what did I see?
