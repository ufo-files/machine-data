{nd} Silver white cigar shaped, it blended in with the cloud behind it then turned normal silver/white after it passed

I was sitting at a red light in Russell Kentucky right next to the new Ironton bridge, when I noticed this large cigar shaped silver/white object moving slowly through the sky. I pointed at it and the guy in the truck next to me also pointed at it then we pointed at each other and continue to observe it for about a minute, it continued to move and then change direction and shot out of our sight.
