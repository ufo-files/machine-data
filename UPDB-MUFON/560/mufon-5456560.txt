{cak} Appeared on my phone video camera

Last Jan 7th, I went outside to take some pictures at night of the light snow in the cold air. I must have had my phone camera set on video without knowing it when I took my first picture. Later I discovered this 6-second video; it appeared on my phone and in my Google Photos account. I included the slow-motion edition, it's amazing. I have the still photos I took seconds after this video was accidentally shot.
No one I have spoken to can figure out how this happened, and I am becoming concerned because of the seemingly hostile nature of the video.
Months later, two bright discs appeared in a picture I took of a baby rabbit in front of a storage shed. At first, I thought they were reflections, but now I am not sure. Strangely, the discs stay relatively the same size in two separate pictures, and there is a reflection or glow on the shed behind the discs as if the discs are generating energy. 
Should this be reported to the military?  I have retired the phone/camera with the video stored in its memory.
