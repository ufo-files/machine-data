{dr}Two bright orange-white lights emitting orbs in the sky

Upon taking our puppy outside to go to the bathroom at approximately 9:41pm on Sunday May 29th, 2016 my fiance and I observed a bright white light with (what looked to be) an orange colored ring or aura around it in the sky from our backyard. The direction in which the light was observed was NW from our home and very quickly disappeared there after. We decided to sit an watch for a while to see if it came back... It did at approximately 9:52pm. The light first appeared bright white, but when it dimmed, had an orange glow, flickered, pulsated-like, then dove down quickly at an angle out of view from our horizon focal point. Over the course of about 3 hours, the light came and went but did not stay in view for very long. The light seemed to dim too much for video. However, I was able to take a few pictures in between observations (at full zoom on my iPhone 6s Plus). During that time, we witnessed the light change direction (from North to South and West to East), descend and ascend (from beyond the horizon out of view to above the horizon at about 10 degrees), emit orbs of light, disappear and re-appear, hover and sometimes follow a path or fly together with the orbs of light it had emitted.
