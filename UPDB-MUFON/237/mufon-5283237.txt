2 craft bobbing and weaving

2 craft observed by 2 people approx 11:15pm 12/24/21. The craft were boomerang shaped, lighted- one solid cream color, moving quickly from north to south within the clouds. They were seen bobbing and weaving as if playing a game in very close proximity to each other. Once they were further away, they appeared to fly side by side. No sound. No flashing lights. Clouds were low that night. Thought it was some sort of Christmas time light display but the lights were not seen again and there were no reports of such an event.
