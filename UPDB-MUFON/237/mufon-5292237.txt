cylinder very bright glowing ball at rear

I was outside front of my house looking for space station overfly, didn't see it, started back to house and noticed object almost above my head under 500ft going silently west to east very fast, had just enough time to see it clearly, cylinder shaped with thin pointed object in front lower portion of object but the most surprising was the very brilliant glowing ball yellow center reddish outline at the very end of object, no trailing flame at all passing by with no sound whatsoever not even trailing noise like you might get with supersonic flight, dead silence! lost sight when passing over my house, rushed to back yard but it it was gone out of my sight by then. Went by to fast to get photo. immediately drew picture of what I saw so I wouldn't forget details then I tried to recreate in adobe illustrator as best I could not easy to draw glowing ball, attached PDF file.
