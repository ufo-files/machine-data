Heard seagulls constantly talking, looked up, saw UFO, ran like hell.

Was playing out back in our yard, and i kept hearing seagulls doing their talking too each other. We lived close to water all around us so seagulls are a common site. But that day they were always talking like they do, i finally looked up to see what was going on and wow. It was big,cigar shaped. dirty gray color i do not recall windows or a door. What really struck me was it had blinking lights that were moving in a clockwise direction, and the reason i looked up, were the seagulls who were fling around the ship counterclockwise. After taking all this in i ran into the house.
