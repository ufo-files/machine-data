{rjl} Orange Triangle moving very fast, defied physics

I saw a UFO once in 2001. It was an orange triangle that looked too small to be a plane. It was in Syracuse during a Leonid meteor shower (a day early, we didn't see any meteors). The triangle had three dots on the corners, and what appeared to be lines at right angles patterning the bottom surface. It moved VERY fast, and gave the visual impression that it was only 100 or so yards off the ground, and the size of a kite. It moved fast, then did a rapid turn, rotated 360 degrees in a way that seemed too fast for physics to agree with, and then flew over the city. It was much higher than 100 yds, and probably larger, but it had the physics of something that was smaller, if that makes sense. I have not told anyone except close friends, but I wrote about it in a journal. I saw it with two other people, and was not using any substances/alcohol. I was 18 yrs old at the time
