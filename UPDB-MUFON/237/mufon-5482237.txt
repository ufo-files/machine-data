{rjl} We took our boat to bring a friend home. Returning we saw a disc shaped craft above us. We watched a light show for 5 minutes it then disappeared.

Our friend had to be home by 10 PM, or he would not be allowed to visit us for a week. So my sister and our friend and I jumped in our boat at 9:50 PM. We drove north to our friends home and got him home in time. Then we headed south to go back home. When we left his cove we saw the disc to the south and headed southeast around the small island. We came around the island and it was there hanging in the sky, no sound no movement. My sister cut the engine and we both crawled under the dashboard afraid. As our boat drifted almost underneath we looked up at the disc. It was very large and had long rectangular lights that went all the way around the entire perimeter of the craft. They were all the colors of the rainbow and when one would turn on the one behind would go dim,and then the next light would go on and the previous light would go dim. It appeared to be rotating,but it was the pattern of the lights going on and off repeatedly. The disc then disappeared and we drove into our dock,shocked and terrified, afraid of seeing something we might not have been supposed to see.
