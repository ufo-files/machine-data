{tjd} Hovering light with elevation change and lateral movement

When i first saw the light it was floating, it then dropped fairly quick in elevation down in a straight line, then I got my phone out to try and record while still watching my 4 year old swing. The light did not seem to be a drone and had no blinking lights whatsoever, only a solid white light. The weird thing is, if it was a drone, why would the light stay exactly the same when changing direction and move and then when hovering behind trees. I wish I had better video, but after it went behind the trees, I saw it hovering and then disappear. I could not figure out how far away it actually was but due to the faintness of the light, I would estimate it to be 1/2 to 3/4 miles away.
