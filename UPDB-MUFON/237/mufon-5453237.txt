line of lights

at around 9:53 pm i was taking the dog out. while he was doing his buisness i looked up to check out the stars and immediately i saw the big dipper along with a line of about 50 star like objects in a straight line traveling west to east. the white star like objects were at equal distance from each other. traveling at the same relative speed like they were being pulled by a tractor or all one structure. the distance between the trees in the back yard are about 120ft long and the star like lights were about 20-25 degrees above the tree line and just below the big dipper. I watched them for aprox. 3 or 4 mins until they disappeared at the eastern tree line of my yard. I went straight back inside the house and told my wife what i had seen.
