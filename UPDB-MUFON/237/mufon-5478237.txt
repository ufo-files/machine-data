First looked like v shaped light, later Rocket-like, duel smoke trails. Looking from 7th floor window. Two lights. Looked through with binoculars. Saw V shaped lights, not moving appearing to hover in place, but visually oriented diagonally. Both lowered behind a building. Minutes later several smoke trails appearing like missiles moved very slowly across our view between buildings, in several directions including, west, north-west, north, and north-east.
