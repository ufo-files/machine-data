{nd} Many unidentified objects followed the crowds movements, and through the city streets

I and friends, were gathered with hundreds of people to watch the huge, music synchronized downtown Indianapolis fireworks display on July 4, 2011. Toward the end of the display, it became apparent that these dull, dark red spheres were following each other in a linear, path fairly uniformly speed controlled, but with some individual variations in speed, and some with revolutions. These objects were perhaps less than 50 feet over the crowds, and followed the crowd movements through the downtown streets, fanning out continuously over a large grid. Many, many people noticed this. A friend's child was told by her former military father that this was a crowd control device---but privately, I was told that this was not the case. The objects size could have been similar to bowling balls, or they may have been closer to kids rideable rubber "kangaroo' balls. This phenomenon continued on certainly past the point that I had to disperse and go home to prepare for my next (work) day. I remember wondering whether I should be observed acknowledging these--(strength in numbers with my friends & others), or play dumb, which I believe I was frightened enough to do. I have not specifically seen the exact same phenomenon, since.
