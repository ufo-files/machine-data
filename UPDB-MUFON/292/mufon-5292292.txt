Weird Object {, had 2 flashing lights, and appeared rather large and moved rather slowly}, Spotted in Sky of South Lyon, MI - {djs}

On August 13th, my wife and I were sitting on our deck looking at the Perseid Meteor Shower, when we noticed a strange object moving East of us, and it was headed due South.
We live in South Lyon, a suburb about 40 miles west of Detroit, MI.

The Object had 2 flashing lights, and appeared rather large (was wider than 2 of our neighbors houses were wide), and moved rather slowly (slower than a plane).
There was also a third one that appeared overhead of us, alone and had a little bit of a rumble noise to it, and moved rather fast and toward the other 2 lights.

We want to know is this was a military jet, a huge drone, or something else. We are interested to know what it was as this was very strange, and definitely not like any drone or military aircraft that we have seen before.
