{jpg} Bright non-blinking light. No Strobe lights observed with binoculars. Appeared below 1000 feet.

Spouse observed a bright light outside the window of our house in Angel Fire NM. It was traveling in a straight line in the northeastern direction. She joked about it being a UFO. I then went to look at it and was alerted as it was not displaying the required aviation navigational lights. I looked at it through the binoculars to get a closer look. I could only see a solid white light with no strobe lights. It was moving away from us. It appeared below a thousand feet and was considerably brighter than the dark sky background.We both observed it go behind a single cloud and it never emerged. 
Having worked in aviation I was bewildered at the anomalous nature of what I was seeing. I went outside and observed several other civilian aircraft in the area about 5 minutes after the last sighting of the unknown object.they were easily identified with the required navigational lights and markings.
