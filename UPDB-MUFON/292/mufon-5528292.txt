Started out at as a Black Triangle flying towards the Airport, then its shape started to change, it then turned around and started heading in the opposite direction and turned into a solid black sphere shape went further up and hovered

I arrived at my bus stop on Greenville street, at 8:00 AM Jan 7 2011. I looked up and saw what I thought at first was a Military Aircraft because it was Black and Triangular shaped. It did not travel very fast for the military craft i thought it was. As i kept watching it, its shape started to shift. I thought maybe it was a bird or perhaps a balloon but it was way too high and then it turned around and headed the opposite direction. It sped up just a bit and took a black sphere form kept going in one direction then headed higher and stopped. It was hovering there for five minutes as I watched. The whole event lasted about 8 to ten minutes and then my bus came and I lost sight of it. This left me feeling uneasy beacause it was so clear.
