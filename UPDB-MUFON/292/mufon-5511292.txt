{dr}Bright starlike in eastern sky, random light pattern, ascending & descending randomly

I was on my back porch, noticed a shooting star & started scanning the sky. I first noticed the object because of the red, white, greenish blue light it was putting off. At first we thought it was a star doing something weird, my husband said "its moving" we quickly got a telephone pole in our sight to judge the "movement" & sure enough it was ascending & descending. It looked to be a sort of zig zag pattern at the beginning then it started up & down followed by back & forth again. After seeing the movement with the lighting pattern we decided it was not a plane nor any seemingly man made object. We watched it for 15 minutes, went inside for 5 and it was gone. This is the first time Ive ever encountered anything of this sort & it was amazing, whatever it was!
