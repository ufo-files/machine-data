{cak} multiple bright pulsating orbs

Getting gas at the BP gas station on the corner of Loomis rd and Layton ave, when spotted the first few orbs coming up from south-south west in no flight pattern appeared moving slowly towards the northwest about 5 were seen at the time before they disappeared. Then the next set of orbs were seen this time there were 5 or 6 that followed the first round of lights no sound was heard from them as they went northwest and just disappeared like the first set of orbs. All very bright orange slowly pulsating approaching and leaving with no sound whatsoever.
