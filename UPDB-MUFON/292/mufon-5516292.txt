{cak} Black streak through sky

I was outside at about 13:55 Mountain time in Westminster, CO, watching a Flight for Life helicopter traveling North to South about 500ft above ground and about 1/4 mile away. Right after the helicopter passed I noticed above there was a Black streak across the sky from south to north, too fast for any known aircraft, literally a streak. When it disappeared the power went out for about 30 seconds and came back on.
