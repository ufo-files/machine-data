I WAS A SOPHMORE IN HIGH SCHOOL AT THE TIME. THIS HAPPENED ON A WEEKEND IT WAS A SUNDAY, AS I WAS BACK IN SCHOOL THE NEXT DAY. MY BROTHER & I WERE SPENDING THE WEEKEND AT OUR COUSIN'S HOUSE.WE WERE EATING BREAKFAST WHEN ONE OF THE NEIGHBORS PHONED US. TOLD US TO COME OUTSIDE AND SEE THE STRANGE OBJECT OUTSIDE. WHEN WE WENT OUTSIDE THERE WERE APX 10 - 15 PEOPLE OUTSIDE LOOKING UP AT THE OBJECT. IT WAS CIRCULAR AS WE WERE LOOKING STRAIGHT UP AND SEEING THE BOTTOM OF IT. THERE WAS A BLACK CENTER WHICH COVERED APX 20% OF THE BOTTOM AREA AND THE REST OF THE BOTTOM GLOWED AN ULTRA-VIOLET BLUE.WE WATCHED THIS OBJECT FOR APX 30-45 MINUTES AND IT DID NOT MOVE AT ALL.AFTER APX 20 MINUTES OF OBSERVATION MOST ALL OF THE NEIGHBORS LEFT. THERE WERE 3 PEOPLE LEFT WITH US AT THAT TIME. THE CLOUD COVER WAS BROKEN AND MOVEING SOUTHEASE TO NORTHWEST AT APX 20-30 MPH WINDS. HOWEVER THE OBJECT DID NOT MOVE AT ALL. THERE WAS NO WIND WHERE WE WERE.THE BROKEN CLOUD COVER WAS APX 1500 TO MAYBE 3,000 FT ABOVE GROUND LEVEL. WE WERE ABOUT TO GO BACK INSIDE WHEN ONE OF THE NEIGHBORS SAID HE COULD HEAR AN AIRPLANE. IT TOOK US A MINUTE TO LOCATE IT AS IT WAS MOVING THRU THE CLOUD COVER, COMEING FROM THE SOUTHEASE. WE LOCATED THE PLANE BY LOCATING THE BLACK EXHAUST FROM THE TWO JET ENGINES. IT WAS A MILITARY AIRCRAFT PAINTED 100% CAMOUFLAGE. THERE WERE NO MARKINGS ON THE PLANE.   ( NOTE THE FOLLOWING PLEASE ) THE PLANE FLEW BY THE OBJECT AT A DISTANCE OF APX 500 - 1000 FT. WE COULD SEE THE OBJECT AND PLANE VERY CLEARLY. WHAT MAKES THE NEXT THING SO INTERESTING IS THIS. I UNDERSTAND THAT IF A JET PLANE WERE TO LOOSE 100% THRUST FROM THE ENGINES IT WOULD FALL LIKE A ROCK. THIS IS WHAT HAPPENED, WE COULD HEAR THE ENGINES AND SEE THE EXHAUST SMOKE VERY CLEARLY. HOWEVER WHEN THE PLANE GOT TO WITHIN APX 500 FT OF IT,IT WAS LIKE YOU HAD FLIPPED A LIGHT SWITCH. NO ENGINE NOISE OR EXHAUST SMOKE, ALL STOPPED. HOWEVER THE PLANE CONTINUED TO TRAVEL STRAIGHT ACROSS UNTIL IT REACHED APX THE SAME DISTANCE FROM THE OBJECT AND THEN, LIGHT SWITCH AGAIN, SMOKE AND ENGINE NOISE. THE PLANE TRAVLED APX 1/2 MILE MADE A 180% U-TURN AND PASSED ON THE OPPOSITE SIDE. SAME THING HAPPENS AGAIN. WHEN THE PLANE'S ENGINED STARTED THE PLANE FLEW BACK SOUTHEAST. AFTER THIS HAPPENED WE WENT BACK INSIDE FOR APX AN HOUR. WE THEN DESIDED TO GO BACK OUTSIDE AND SEE THE OBJECT AGAIN. AT THIS TIME THE SKY WAS COMPLETELY CLEARED. WE COULD NOT FIND THE OBJECT AT ONCE. MY BROTHER FOUND IT AS IT HAD MOVED ACROSS TOWN AND WAS APX 3-5 MILES AWAY FROM US.WE HAD A PERFECT SIDE VIEW OF THE OBJECT. IT WAS AN ELONGATED BELL SHAPE. FROM THE BOTTOM TO THE TOP OF THE OBJECT WAS APX AT LEAST 5-6 TIMES AS TALL AS THE BOTTOM WAS ACROSS. THE OBJECT SURFACE WAS A VERY SHINEY COPPER OR BRASS COLOR. THE NEXT DAY AT SCHOOL, DUE TO MEDIA COVERAGE, THE AIR FORCE ISSUED A STATEMENT THAT THE OBJECT WAS SIMPLY A WEATHER BALLOON. OF COURSE WE ALL KNOW THE US AIR FORCE WOULD NEVER LIE TO US !!!! THE PROBLEM I HAVE WITH THAT EXCUSE IS THE OBJECT WAS STATIONARY IN WIND FOR AT LEAST 45MIN TO ONE HOUR,PLANE LOST POWER TO ENGINES TWO TIMES AND DID NOT FALL, QUESTION, HOW IS THAT POSSIBLE? FAST FORWARD APX 2008 - 2012. I HAVE THOUGHT ABOUT THIS MANY TIMES IN THE PAST WITH NO REAL ANSWERS. HOWEVER TODAY IS DIFFERENT THE INTERNET IS A WONDERFULL ITEM.IN SCHOOU I TRIED REASEARCH TO IDENTIFY PLANE, NOTHING. THE AIRFORCE HAD 40 OF THOSE BOMBERS CONVERTED INTO RECONNANCE AIRCRAFT AND AT THAT TIME THEY WERE CONSIDERED TOP OF THE LINE SUPER SECRET.
