{jpg} Looked like a star in the daytime then about an hour later it split into two. The one that split turned into a pen shape and they both started moving. A third one started to fly around the 2 that separated. Definetely at high altitude.

Me and my family were driving from the Cove Palisades to Madras Oregon when I noticed a bright spot in the sky through my sunroof. We pulled over as soon as we could. I thought the object was a visible planet or star during the day at first or maybe a very small forming cloud. We know it was high in the sky because we drove about 15 miles and it was still in the same location and we could see it bright though a few cloud patches. It stayed in the same spot for about an hour everybody in my car was making sure to keep an eye on it, when we pulled in line to get coffee the object split into two. It seemed to all happen slowly. The object that separated from the original eventually turned into a pen shape and had two spots behind it. After the separation, 1 object came from an unknown area and flew around the 2 objects at high speed and made quick pinpoint turns and then disappeared. I watched as the pen shaped object left in another direction and the original object into another direction. I watched them as they slowly disappeared out of my vision because they became to small to see. If I was holding a bb at arms length that is how small it was at the beginning. They were not landing just moving into another region of airspace. I was a little scared after watching all of this and have been trying to come up with a reasonable explanation, but we can't and I am hoping somebody else had seen the same as we did in that area or anywhere else. The three pictures are what we saw at first.
