{cak} Starlike object seemed to follow dark clouds, flew higher than a plane and very fast.

Smoking a cigarette in yard. Thought I was looking at a star, till it started to move. Was excited to see my first actual ufo sighting. Lost sight when it flew straight up and left 3 perfect rings of clouds.
