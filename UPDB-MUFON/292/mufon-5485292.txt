{nd} One singular bright light unattached to an aircraft traveling in a direct linear flight path.

While on top of a local mountain, (Burkemont Mtn) one night, my friend and I saw a singular bright light traveling at least 400-500 MPH (I am radar and lidar certified and this was my speed estimate). At first, we thought the object my be some sort of spot light or shooting star. We thought this until we noticed the speed and no sound emitting from it. The light continued past the mountain never changing its path and went toward another mountain in the distance. The light was brighter than any light I have ever seen. I haven't told anyone expect my wife and that was just a few minutes ago. This was the first of three "odd" experiences. No pictures were able to be taken because of the brief light.
