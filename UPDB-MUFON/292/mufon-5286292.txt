Injured and lost time during believed abduction

I was outside on my 40 acre property trimming trees, as I have been doing for weeks when I felt like someone was watching me. It got so intense at one point that I stopped what I was doing and walked around my property to see if anyone had trespassed. There was no one. That lasted about 2 hours when I started to feel incredibly unwell. Very heavy and lethargic, a little dizzy and nauseous, so I decided to stop for the day. A huge storm came in that night, one of the worst I've ever seen here. I was up until about midnight, trying to comfort my dogs who are afraid of thunder. Finally I fell asleep and when I woke I looked outside and it appeared to be dawn. I looked at my watch and it said it was only approximately 1:30am, so I laid back down and went back to sleep. I awoke again and it was bright outside. I looked at my watch again and it said it was only 4:30am. It was far too bright to be 4:30 so I looked at another clock and it said 8:15am. I thought maybe my watch battery was dying, but its functioned perfectly for the past three days, since this all happened. I lost nearly four hours and can't explain it. I also woke up feeling like I'd been in a car accident. Whiplash, headaches, and today my lower back hurts so badly I cannot walk. Not once in my life have I ever claimed to be abducted, but I have absolutely no other explanation for any of this.
