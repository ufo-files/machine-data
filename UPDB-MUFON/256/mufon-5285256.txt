Bright orb suddenly appeared high in the clear sky, hovered then streaked away and disappeared

In Bali at the beach at sunset time when stars are just beginning to show, my husband and i saw an orb like like thing, really bright and clear - not fuzzy and sparkly like a shooting star - suddenly appear in the sky out of nowhere. It was really large in comparison.

It streaked away for a second, getting smaller as it did, then disappeared just like that. 

Amazing! Exciting!
