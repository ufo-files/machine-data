(GS) It was changing colors and powering up Blue thenpowering down red a few times.

I was walking my dog on the night of Nov.16th 2012 with my binoculars, that I always take with me,and was admiring the clear skies, the Milky Way and hoping to catch a glimpse of the meteor shower that was happening and saw a pulsating light changing colors and size. When I got home I got my old,4 mega pixel camera and when out on my porch to video it and take pics. For some reason the vid part refused to work so I took consecutive pictures and got it changing color and powering up as shown.I had just charged the battery to my camera just 2 days before but, the cameras battery died as I was getting some good shots and had to plug in the battery charger and wait until morning to see what I got.I was very excited and could not get to sleep.I have seen this type of orb several times before but it was usually gone when I got my camera.
