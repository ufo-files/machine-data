Ball shaped glowing object

At around 9PM I saw a flying ball-shaped, glowing object in east north-west direction making no noise at a distance of 50-60m passing. The object was flickering in white, yellow and blue without trail. The flight altitude was about 35m and the speed was fast (but not to fast). I saw it first passing the churches clock tower (height 36mm) in the east of my position, it was lower the the tower, then passing in NW direction behind a single tree (distance from the clock tower to the tree is approximative 100m), then i lost sight.
