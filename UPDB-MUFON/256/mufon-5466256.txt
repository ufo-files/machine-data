{td} triangular dark object about 3 feet long gliding for 1000 yards

I was in my front yard.Directly across the street I saw a dark triangular object illuminated by moonlight and streetlight gliding in the air just behind and as high as the streetlight which would be about 30-40 feet high.It was shaped like a giant moth.It was about 3 feet in diameter.It maintained a constant speed of about 20 mph.I could see the silouette of it silently gliding in a straight path heading SW for about 1000 yards before losing sight of it, and it did not flap any wings.I have searched the internet for night flying insects or birds that may have fit the description of this thing, but there are none that size.It was a spooky thing to witness.
