{tjd} I saw the UFO that is called the Heflin event on Aug 3 1965. Was on Brentford Ave. in Orange with neighbor we both saw it and it came right over us!!! spinning whitelight under it then shot off and away.

We were in the street playing ball on Brentford Ave in the city of Orange, when I noticed it because it was coming straight at us and we (three of my neighbors stopped and looked up.. it came up quickly and then like it noticed it stopped above us and was spinning. We looked up terrified I might add and saw that it had one large white light under the saucer shaped spinning disk and then two other lights around that, colored lights. We ran to the neighbors house and hid behind bushes because we didn't know what it was and we were scared. It spun for a moment and then quickly lighting fast shot out and away. We came out of hiding and I ran home and told my family and then we did read about it the next day. Found out that a man named Heflin had taken some picture of it that very same day, earlier time but I recognized the pictures as being the same one that we had stood under looking up at.
