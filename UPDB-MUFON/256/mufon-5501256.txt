{nd} two orange-red orbs

It was the 4th of July 2014, me and about 15 friends were having a cookout and shooting fireworks. All of a sudden we all saw two red-orange orbs flying overhead in the direction of the Greenville Downtown Airport. Less than two minutes later, two more red-orange orbs flew in the same path of the previous two orbs. At first we thought it was fireworks, but then the orbs never dissipated. The orbs continued moving in a straight line for over a few miles until out of sight. There were no sounds and everybodies reaction was kind of in shock and not sure what those orbs of light could be.
