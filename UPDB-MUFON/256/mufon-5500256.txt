beam of light striking the car

i was with a friend in her car, parked in a street that in the horizon the view was a mountain. suddenly the car radio started to work very bad and when we looked at the front of the car, there was a beam of blue light, like a laser, that was striking the car and another beam was striking a post street light. That beam came from that mountain, that was in several miles of distance.
The radio stoped working and the light of the post street made an explosion.
This as occured in 2002,at 3am and was very quick.
It was an event that i can not forget, and my only memory after that, was i talking about it to my friend, asking her wtf was that, and i can not remeber what happend after that night.
i didn't know, in that time, that we should be taking attention to the hours, and for that reason, i don't know how many time we lost in that moment.
I'm happy to finally have found this kind of internet page of yours, so that you can help me to understand what was that.
In that time, i reached for a portuguese organization, but never had the response to my e-mail.
