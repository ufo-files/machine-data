half moon (blimp) looking object stationary, a distance away from it orange satilite looking objects were apearing and flying around eachother then dissapearing

7am i walked out of work to go home. I observed a strange "mark" in the sky at the horizon as the sun was about to begin rising. I couldn't tell if the "mark" was an oval cloud reflecting the rising sun or a stationary object. About 10 minutes later to the far right of the "mark" a bright light appeared and seemed to "swim" around in the sky like a fish. It was a ball that seemed to glow orange then back to a bright light then disappear. For 20 minutes I witnessed this with 2 co-workers. At one point 2 lights appeared and seemed to interact with each other then disappear. The entire episode was about 30 minuets, the lights overall reappeared about 5 times and the phenomena gradually decreased as the larger "mark" drifted farther east until out of sight. Planes north of the phenomena from LAX gave a great control comparative during the observation. These were not commercial air craft, they simply looked like firefly's in the atmosphere.
