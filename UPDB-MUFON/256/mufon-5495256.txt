{rjl} From horizon, object zoomed upward along a perfect 901 vertical trajectory until visually obstructed by our porch

On March 26, 2015, at 5:20 a.m. (Pacific time), I saw a greenish-white dull glowing orb rise quickly from the eastern horizon. It zoomed upward on a perfectly straight 901 VERTICAL trajectory, until visually obstructed by the eaves of our porch.

Then, at 5:36 a.m. (Pacific) in the same eastern area, an identical greenish-white dull glowing orb zoomed quickly across the sky from North to South (left to right) at @ 101 along a perfectly straight HORIZONTAL trajectory, until visually obstructed by pine trees (we are in the mountains, elevation 6,50 feet).

The remarkable aspects of these sightings were the objects' color, their razor-straight paths, and speeds that were faster than aircraft but slower than meteorites or space debris.
