Thin green skinned humanoid with long black hair

When my brother an I were young living in a trailer on my grandparents farm, we would frequently be visited by a thin green skinned humanoid being named Kuka. Kuka was able to communicate with us and appeared friendly, but had a superior being that’s did not approve of the encounter.

When telling the story to an older relative who used to live in the trailer, they were visited by the same being (Kuka) and was able to call out its name before we even mentioned it. To me, this verified that I was not crazy and what I saw and experienced was, in fact, true.

Have there been other sightings of thin green skinned beings with long black hair?
