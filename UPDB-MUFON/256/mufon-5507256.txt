I asked him if it was moving, and he had confirmed that it was. It started out moving really slowly, but picked up pace fairly quickly. After a few more minutes of watching it, Sean went to get his girlfriend Chyenne from the house so she could see it. While he was gone, the object came to a dead stop out of nowhere. Sean had only been gone for a couple seconds before he returned with Chyenne. By the time we were all back together the object had moved from as far as the eye could see from one side of the sky to the other. As the object reached the horizon it made an abrupt turn to the left just before it had disappeared. Soon after the sighting, we began to hear strange noises In the woods behind his house.
