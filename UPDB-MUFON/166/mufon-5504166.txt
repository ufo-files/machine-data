Military exercise observed

This event occurred in Puerto Rico specifically in the Island of Vieques during a US Army reserve annual training. At the time I was a PFC E-3 to a Headquarters Company during the training everything looks normal the exercise was as plan and after completion 4 or 5 members of my platoon we decide to sit down and talk for a while, the night sky was really clear beautiful, and suddenly a sphere was ascending from the ground red orange color we stopped taking and observed what was happening, we thought was a kind of illumination flare normal for this kind of exercise but suddenly the object when gained altitude and stop, the object expanded in size and turned red in color and on a blink of an eye this object travelled north with incredible speed and dissapeared. We were quiet, speechless for about 10 seconds and I asked my fellow soldiers if we were in the same page on this on what we saw was really something out of the ordinary and everybody agreed. This definitely was an UFO not matter what, I decided to tell my story because it is time to let people know about the real existence of extraterrestrial and also that we are not the only ones in the vast universe.
