{dr}Three lights hovering. One very slowly falling. One grey craft.

There were three lights while I was driving from Lenexa to Lawrence, KS. One in the distance disappeared. There was one boomerang looking one. Another one looked like a meteor falling almost straight towards my car but very slowly with a vertical flame/light. On closer look, its structure was grey but still too small to see the shape. These hung in the sky for at least 10 minutes and then they were behind me. I don't necessarily believe in UFOs but these crafts seem to defy normal aircraft behavior and appearance.
