jacksonville bright energy balls blueish-white big about 300 of them in a clutter appearing went away making a boom sound

I was homeless, about 2am. eating food inside a dumpster behind a grocery store on a clear night . 20 to 30 minutes while eating a presence came on me that something was about to happen , suddenly above my head and around me these giant ball-like glowing white spheres were appearing for a second (like a camera flash) each one would make this loud boom sound noise as they appeared then disappeared . it was so loud i could feel the vibrations off each sphere as each one would appear thin disappear. i got out the dumpster from being scared. then suddenly it all stopped . still shocked what just happened i climbed back in the dumpster to begin to eat. then it happened again, so i got out then the event stopped .  this happened a few more times . then i figured whatever those spheres where , they were warning me or they didn't want me eating bad food inside that dumpster ,so i left . i couldn't figure out if they were angels , aliens in these sphere's , or people from area 51 controlling those sphere's. But whatever it was , they were trying to protect me from probably eating something bad that night . Boy, what a memorable experience. What was that? I wish someone could tell me to this day. what an incredible experience.
