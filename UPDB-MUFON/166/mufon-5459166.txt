{rjl} While riding bicycle I sensed someone looking down on me. I looked strait up into the sky and witnessed a dark red sphere shaped object.

While riding his bicycle home in the early A.M. hours he sensed a presence observing him; at 90 degrees above him he saw a dark red sphere with a fiery/brighter corona around it.
--------------------------------------------------------
The witness has estimated the actual size of the objects in both sightings were 31 thru 100 feet wide. Since both times they were directly overhead, he had no comparison to give a more accurate estimation as to their size. At first he wondered if he was looking at a planet but when it (they) took off at a high rate of speed over the horizon, faster than any plane he has seen traveling, they left a red tail behind that didn't linger. At this point he knew he wasn't looking at a planet.  He said he believed it (they) was in our atmosphere. He believes it flew at the same altitude a jetliner would have been and the object's width was wider that a jetliner is long from his position. The witness was wearing contact lenses. When he first saw the sphere it was stationary. He watched it for approximately 10 seconds before it took off in a straight path over the horizon both times in the direction the witness was traveling. 
--------------------------------------------------------
There was no evidence only a witness testimony.

WITNESS CREDIBILITY: The witness usually works as a restaurant support member. He was willing to cooperate and was calm. The interview took place on 08/13/2019 for 19 minutes. I drove out to look at the site the same day. I asked more questions on 0//15/2019 at 3:00 thru 3:13P.M. The witness said at one time he had an interest in witchcraft. He never practiced it but had read about it a little. He has had horrible dreams about aliens for years. He has seen a blue orb and his mother saw one outside of the house where he lived; it was translucent. The witnesses mother said the Virgin Mary was there and was praying for both the witness and herself. The mother thought it may be the Virgin Mary (because the translucent orb was blue).  Since he has seen these red spheres it aroused his curiosity. He does not have bad dreams about aliens any longer; he felt at peace. 
--------------------------------------------------------
I don't believe this was a man-made object. If it was it wasn't typical. The object elicited an awareness from the witness, and seemed to have wanted the witness to notice it. When it left it always went away in the same direction the witness was traveling. The witness also expressed that because of how it made him feel (having recognized its frequency) he felt it was the same object in both instances.
