{rjl} hovered over high school and or airport, was mirroring the area underneath it

I was in the car with my mother on our way to her friends house in Canyon City. I looked out the window and saw this large disc like thing in the sky, it looked like a giant mirror, hovering over Grant Union High School, which also included the local airport that is on the hill above the school. I asked my mom if she saw that and her reply was "what, I am busy driving, don't bother me". So I did not. I told friends about it but they gave me that "ya right" attitude so I just put in the back of my mind, not knowing where to report it till now.
