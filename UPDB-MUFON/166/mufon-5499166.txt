I was in an old friends large open backyard just after midnight in Gresham, OR September 21st 2014. I had just shown my non believer friend a few videos I created on his tablet, one about orbs and the other Starseeds. I had just shut the tablet off when I looked up behind him and suddenly out of no where appeared 3 glowing orange red ethereal craft, flying in a triangular formation, they almost looked like the ghosts of 3 old fighter planes. They were about 1000 ft above us cruising fairly fast S.E. and completely silent. I said to my friend, "look look look" he says "I'm seeing it oh my god" the suddenly disappeared as if into another dimension. Five minutes later we saw them again this time not over us but in front of us flying East to west. Fifteen minutes later we saw them again this time for a good 10 seconds cruising across the sky. Unfortunately we got no pictures or videos my phone was dead the first time and didn't expect to see more so soon! It was an incredible experience nonetheless. Not my first time seeing UFOs, but the first time I've seen anything like these. I've never even heard about these.
