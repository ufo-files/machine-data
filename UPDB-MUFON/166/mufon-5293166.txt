Very bright light, blinking very fast flew over my truck going East to West

I was driving to work. It was just after 0500. I was on Mt Pleasant Rd. going East toward Mayport. I was about here,(30°21'14.5"N 81°32'05.7"W). I observed a VERY bright light, much brighter than the street lights and blinking VERY fast, maybe 5 time a second, much faster than aircraft navigation lights. It was definitely above the street lights. It appeared to be moving toward me because it approached me faster than the street lights. There might have been two light side by side. It was hard to tell because it was so bright. It was hard to tell how low it was, but if it were an aircraft, I think I would have be able to here it. After it passed over my truck, I slowed down and looked behind me and I could see it in the distance. It was not nearly as bright, so I assume that the light were focused downward.
