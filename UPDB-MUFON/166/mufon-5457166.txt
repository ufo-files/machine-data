{cak} Silver metallic

I was outside with two customers when a bright silvery/white flashing light in the sky to my right caught my attention. I thought it may have been a helicopter sending a distress signal of some kind so I told my customers to look.
It was about 2:45 pm and it was bright and sunny with few visible clouds. The flashing light was so bright that we couldn't make out the type of craft that was emitting the light. It hovered in the same spot for several seconds (maybe 20) flashing it's bright light. Then it proceeded to move slowly North West. When it stopped flashing the light it became obvious that it was a shiny silver metallic triangle. More specifically a "V" shaped craft. The nose of the craft had two long solid arms connected to it, making a perfect "V" shape. 
We stood watching it slowly and silently move through the sky when suddenly it disappeared completely, right before our eyes. 
We were completely astonished and stood there in disbelief scanning the vast empty sky for any sign of it but could find none. It had simply vanished without a trace, without making a sound.
