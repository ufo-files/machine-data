{rjl} 3 flying objects with flashing lights, flying in an unusual pattern

While driving home I noticed two flying objects over some mountains, meandering from the northeast and heading east. When I arrived at my house, which is about a mile away from where I initially saw the flying objects, I saw they where over my property. I had my wife come outside and see. I thought there were only two, but she said there were at least three. It was hard to keep track of them as they would seemingly disappear and then reappear at an entirely different spot in the sky that didn't correlate with the speed they were traveling. The few seconds of video we caught doesn't capture any of the more spectacular things we witnessed. They either drifted away or disappeared entirely before we could further film them.
