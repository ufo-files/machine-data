{cak} 27 Objects in following single file in the same identical path spaced apart from each other

I observed 27 objects the same size traveling together, single file from NW to SE at 5:15 am. They were spaced equally apart from one another, with no strobe lights and no sound. 2 objects did travel alongside another and were the exception to the other 25. I also saw the same objects at the same time on 4/14/2020, but only counted 4 from when I looked up to the sky. The objects on that day were on the same directional path but were position slightly North of the objects today, There may have been more objects than 27, as they were visible as soon as I walked outside and started counting.

The photo is from one of the 27 objects today. The video was taken yesterday. I was filming one of the objects moving across the sky, and did not see all of the movements of the object in the video while filming until I watched it today.
