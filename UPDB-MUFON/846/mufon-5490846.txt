At about 1 am this morning (7-28-15} I was sitting in my back yard when I saw a dimly lit object come over a tree behind my house. At first it looked like a low flying small private plane with dim white lights on the wingtips, I quickly realized that a plane will have red and green wingtip marker lights; as it got closer I saw a third white light, The object was very dark against the dark sky. I could tell that there was something there, it appeared to be a triangle shape, but I am not certain of that. As it got closer I expected to hear the noise of a small plane engine but I realized that it was not making any noise at all and the object appeared the be slightly rotating. The object continued over the house and then I lost it. I was facing North and the object was traveling from NE to SW. The sighting lasted about 10 seconds and the object was moving slightly faster than a small private plane would. No colored lights were seen. I am an aviation enthusiast so I have an idea of what I am looking at when something is in the air. I have absolutely no idea what this was that I saw, I thought it might have been a drone, but again, absolutely no noise or colored marker lights. I checked the newspapers today for a possible story, but nothing. I thought that you might be able to help. Initial contact email only please.
