Massive red orb flew over my house

I was just standing in my drive way facing East and out of no where directly above me this massive red orb that at first I thought was a meteoroid of some sort because the way it visually appeared at first and the altitude it was at and the way it was traveling was similar. It did not move quite as fast as a shooting star nor comet/meteor because I have seen and caught those on camera and this was traveling about half the speed I would say of a comet but this thing then flew from over my house to East at an insane rate of speed and came to a halt out in the distance and just sat there and finally slowly dissipated to nothing. It absolutely terrified me. I ran inside to tell my girlfriend to come see and to grab my phone to try and film it and I luckily was able to catch the last little bit of the first event. I do apologize for the language in the video I’m sure you can tell I was shocked. Then about 20 minutes later while I was on the phone with my mom telling her about what I just saw it came back! This time it had appeared to the North East and travelled slowly this time towards the east and ended up basically where it had ended up before it disappeared the first time, It was very bright and had a glowing red aurora but no flames.
