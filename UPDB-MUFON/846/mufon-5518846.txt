What looked like white ball very small or very high in the sky moving very fast below the cloud level

Just saw from my back patio while gazing almost straight up in the sky, what looked like white ball either very small or very high in the sky moving very fast, but I think below the cloud level. Seemed to be above or higher in elevation than a near by vapor trail left by a passing jet, but below or lower than the highest clouds it passed in front of. The sky was pretty clear, with a few low level puffy clouds, and maybe just some very high elevation thin clouds. The object was a very small white light or something reflecting the sun. I would have thought it was a satellite moving across the sky, the way it was moving speed wise, and the straight line it moved in, but it was in the middle of the day, and Ive never seen anything move this fast, this high up during the day. Not a jet for sure. I lost sight of it when its path took it behind some trees in my back yard. Thats it!Ive just never seen anything like this during the day.
