{nd} Large bright ball shaped object

At the time of the sighting, I was going to my car to run the garbage down to the dumpster in our gated community.

I noticed the object immediately.

Large ball/circle shaped object. Very bright with faint red and green lights on either side. Very large object that at first I thought was a star until it came closer. This object actually passed overhead (Id say it was 500 or more feet above me. Then the object seemed to just disappear into thin air, moving north to south. 

I was stunned after realizing that it wasnt a plane, helicopter, comet or meteor. I had a sense of awe. I just stood there and watched it fly overhead. I couldnt believe my eyes.

The object just disappeared.
