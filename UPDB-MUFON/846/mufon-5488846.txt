9-24-15, 6:15 am, I went outside to look at the stars, on my driveway, in North Port, FL . I was watching what I thought was a satellite, moving across the sky. The angle was slightly to the west, not directly overhead. I noticed it in the far southwest, it moved in a straight line headed north, reaching the northwest. When it reached the NW it started moving in all different directions, and reversed course. It looked exactly like a satellite, faint white light, moving at satellite speed, across the sky. The satellite like object then disappeared. The object moved across the entire sky, my view of the horizon, in a matter of 30 seconds.
