I went out to my balcony, and looked up. I saw a silver disc just hovering, then a plane went right by this disc. The disc disappeared then re-appeared once the plane went by. The disc moved slightly to the south eas in a spinning motion, with a slight wobble. Another plane came and the same thing happened. After the first plane a second disc was approaching from the north towards the original disc then dissappeared. Then that disc after 15 minutes, came shooting across the sky just above the buildings in a north east direction. The first disc continued to move south east disappearing and re-appearing as planes went by. Afterwards both just vanished from the sky. I felt a strange feeling as I watched the disc, almost as if it knew I was watching. I felt calm, even when the second disc shot across above the buildings. I did not think it was a UFO at first, but as I stared I could see the disc spinning as it hovered. It was a bright and clear day.
