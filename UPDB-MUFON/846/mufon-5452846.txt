Scanning, pulsing light (mothership) moving above treetops - video shows other lights coming up to it from ground

On the evening of August 13th 2019, I was standing at the end of my driveway watching bats swoop in and out between the trees. It was after 10:00pm on a dark, clear, windless night. I noticed a bright white light suddenly pulse above the trees almost directly over the top of me. The pulse was a flash of white light with defined, square edges that went down to the ground. The light went out and I followed an undistinguishable grey shape move above me heading from approx. 240 degrees to 060 degrees in a straight line. The light was perhaps 30ft. above tree level.

The light then moved slowly east, pulsing every 5-10 seconds. I took out my iPhone, selected video, held the phone above my head and started to blindly record in the hopes of catching something.

The recording captures the pulsing light - and then multiple small lights or objects fluttering up and around the object previously overhead of me. 

My first impression was that it was a drone doing survey's. However, I have operated drones (I have a Commercial (CPL/IR) and Remote Pilot's license) and this object was not displaying any navigation lights or visual night tracking aids usually associated with drone operations.

It wasn't until I heard a story of a similar incident in the UK sometime during the 1980's and on reviewing the video that I thought this might be a credible sighting.
