Bright white object.

Ok, well, here i go. twice in as many months i have seen SOMETHING in the sky at night. The first time I kinda just blew it off but i saw what i believe is the same thing/kind of flying object & maybe it can be explained.
Now, I KNOW WHAT AIRPLANES LOOK LIKE in the night sky & sound like, this was not a plane. it was a white light that at first glance i thought to be a REALLY bright star or planet, until it started moving. the first time it passed over head & i fully expected to see the blinky plane lights & sounds that accompany them, no i did not. it was silent. it passed over with no sound & no change in the light, it remained fully bright coming & going. 
i saw it last night too, this time going west to east, first time north to south. but last night it was moving sloooooowly. i gauge the speed from where it shows up between the trees, planes have a pretty regular speed, this was slow. 
Both events were benign, i didn't freak out & my heart didn't race. once & i wouldn't be here posting this, but this happened twice. 
i don't know what it was, as i stated i know what airplanes look like, i see them every night, this was not a plane. being in the southeast lower part of Michigan, the tri-county area around Detroit, i am wondering if anyone saw it & if you know what it could be, im all for an earthly explanation. 
Thanks in advance.
