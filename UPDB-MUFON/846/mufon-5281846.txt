witness craft land and saw entities exit craft

On many occasions I was woken in my sleep to a humming noise and get out of bed and go to kitchen and look out window at the field across the street and witnessed a disc shaped craft coming from the north over the treetops descending towards the ground and landing having a door open and multiple entities exiting the aircraft onto the field. At this point I had become scared and went back to bed thinking I was just dreaming. This happened multiple times over roughly an eight-year period.
