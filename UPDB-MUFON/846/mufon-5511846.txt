{nd} 8 Circular Objects with Orange Lights Shine Down on Worcester, Massachusetts

On March 30, 2013 at around 1:00AM, I ran outside after hearing my children screaming. They were pointing up at the sky, and when I looked up at the sky I noticed 4 circular objects beaming orange lights off of them. I immediately thought they were UFOs because of the manner in which they hovered over us. They were close enough to appear large to us, but I really couldnt tell you just how big. Id say they appeared the width of a mini-van. 

As they hovered away, they got smaller until they disappeared. Another one then showed up and disappeared. Just then, 3 more showed up in the sky, making them 8 total. They were all coming from and going towards the same direction. The last three that showed up in the sky formed a triangle, to only then disappear before our eyes.

We were all very nervous about this encounter as it was something we could not explain.
