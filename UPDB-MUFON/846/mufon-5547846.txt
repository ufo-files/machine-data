Classic flying saucer shape with domed top.

My sighting took place while harvesting hay in a field north of Twin Bridge Rd. I was working for a farmer. He had returned to the barn to retrieve another hay wagon. Myself, my brother, and the farmers son were talking. They were sitting on the hay (stacked 5 high) talking down to me. I was leaned back talking to them when I noticed something out of my peripheral vision. I turned my head to see an object which was disc shaped and looked to be about 80 to 100 feet across. The object was not more than 200 feet in the air and traveling in a straight smooth line in a westerly direction. It didn't make a sound. At first it seemed to travel slow but as I stuttered and pointed (trying to get the other boys to look) it picked up speed. I could see a dome on its top as it was traveling away from us. I lost sight of the craft as it traveled west and trees obscured my view. Years later I saw the same object in a video and pictures dated Aug 6, 1997 of a sighting made in Mexico City.
