{rjl} Disc shaped flying object with red and white moving lights

At 4:00am my dog woke me up at this unusual time for her to go outside. I went outside and looked up to notice a flying saucer object with moving red and white lights. I rubbed my eyes in disbelief looked up again to see that it was there. I watched as it was aprox. 100feet above tree lines moving about 25mph NW for about 5 minutes before it was out of my view. There was red lights undernieth on the outside then next to it was white lights the lights were moving slow in a pattern. The dog did not bark as she was looking around the ground sniffing. The craft was making a loud but soft humming noise that almost sounded like a loud fan from an aircraft. The sound went loud and hummed fast for a few seconds then went to a soft hum and slow for a few seconds.It did this the whole time. The craft itself seemed to be aprox 100ft circumference. It was a clear night with lots of beautiful stars. I had never seen anything like this before and I was not afraid but just mesmerized. I will NEVER forget the noise or the visual of this craft.
