{cak}Heard jets flying fast-low. Stepped outside saw one silver round object, it cloaked itself then moved to another part of the sky. Then two more silver objects appeared out of nowhere to form a triangle. As objects cloaked 3 jets tried to catch them.

Approximately 7:15pm, me and my four children witnessed three unidentified silver objects in the sky. First we heard low flying jets, naturally my children wanted to see them. We stepped outdoors, and immediately saw a silver object hovering in the sky for a few seconds and then disappear. Soon after, two more silver objects appeared out of no where and made triangle. After a brief moment all three disappeared, then reappeared in a different part of the sky. All the while three jets scrambled to keep up with them. It looked almost like a cat and mouse game, with these objects appearing and disappearing, the whole thing lasted about 10 mins, and we also noticed that the clouds and Sun set were the strangest color and unusual. Sadly one of my kids broke the screen of my cell phone a few days ago, so I wasn't able to video this event! :( Hopefully someone else in the arklatex witnessed this and reported this.
