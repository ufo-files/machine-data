{nd} Hovered over Cincinnati. A orange orb flew into the translucent triangle shaped object. Then disappeared.

Story:
I was in a car coming home from getting food from a restaurant nearby. I saw an orange orb, which I immediately thought was a plane, and thought about how I had to travel for business the next day and was going to be on an air plane soon. This orb was the top of the triangle. I then shifted my focus to two more planes flying right behind the first, which were both the same size and color. I thought it was odd that the two orbs were both flying at the exact same speed, and then realized that all three orbs were traveling at the same speed. I then put two and two together and noticed the objects were forming a rigid triangle shape and were all traveling at the same speed and keeping the same distance. The second I came to the conclusion the orbs were in a triangle shape, they stopped together and just hovered over Cincinnati. I noticed how the object was completely translucent and that it seemed to be the size of the whole city (The large size could have came from my perspective). I was trying to take all of it in, as my friend took a right turn onto where I live. I had to travel up a hill and I was unable to see the object for about 15 seconds (Longest 15 seconds of my life). When we stopped at my house I was unable to see the object anymore. I thought it was over, but then I saw another of the exact same orb traveling very fast towards where the center of the object would have been. I ran up a hill (>1 minute maximum time) and by the time I reached the hill it was completely gone.

Object:
-Triangle shaped
-Initially with three glowing orange orbs
-Translucent
-Seemed near city size
-Fourth orb (exactly the same as the other three) flew into what looked like to be the center of the object

Flight Path:
-Triangle Flew South about airplane speed from North of Cincinnati
-Stopped over Cincinnati and hovered
-Fourth orb flew on the same flight path as the first 3 orb (Triangle structure)
-Fourth orb flew much faster than the original three orbs
-Fourth orb seemed to be flying in the middle line of the structure
-Disappeared
