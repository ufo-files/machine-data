Unknown object coming from the east at a high rate of speed; makes abrupt turn up.

Our son had a baseball game in Lincoln, NE. It was his first game and my wife recorded his first hit of the season on 27MAR21, around 1700 hours. On 1APR21, I was watching the clip she recorded because my son has some batting stance issues we had been working on, when I paused the clip by accident, exactly at the 19 second clip, I noticed a cylindrical object coming from the east just outside of the cloud pattern, as can be seen in the video. All of this happens within 1 second, from the 19 second to 20 second time frame. I rewound the frame by fractions of a second and saw there was nothing at the western edge of the cloud pattern. In the frame, it appeared to us that it was clearly cylindrical and appears to have an opening at the base of the object; for lack of articulation, it looked like a hole. When it went up, the object cants, and light is reflected off the top of the object, while the lower half has a shadow. Once again, it happened so fast, within a second, and I only noticed it while reviewing my son's hit, not actually during the game. It is hard to say whether or not it is glowing, so I would say no.
