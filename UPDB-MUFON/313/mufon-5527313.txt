I was standing outside and I saw in the sky a small orange spot. It flickered as a flame as it moved from one point in the sky to the other. It then flickered out. There was no associated sound that I as able to hear and there was no visible contrail. The best guess I could manage would be that it was the tailfire of a rocket. It was very strange. I also had my co-workers come out of the office and observe the object, which they did.
