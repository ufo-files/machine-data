Pulsating Ufo

Pulsating UFO was spotted, looked like this was a main craft and shortly after had smaller orbs moving around the UFO in a half circle then disappeared half circle to re appear to finish the other half of the circle in another location close to the UFO. Also observed was what looked like another UFO but this vanished. The main UFO then started to rise slowly to a very high altitude then moved from over the mountain area of La Concha in Marbella out to sea in a South West direction until it was out of sight.
