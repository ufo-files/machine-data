{rjl} bright daytime light

On the way home from a short trip the the grocery store a friend that was driving saw a bright light object moving some what rapidly. We were driving South on Kipling. I thought it was an airplane that had caught the reflection of the sun. Just as suddenly as it appeared the light went out. I expected to see the shape of an airplane but what we saw was a sort of fuzzy round object with no shape with a kind of dark redish color that was dull. The event lasted just about 5-6 seconds the object vanished.
