Gliding black triangle

I was minding my mums house for her. I let dogs out at 12pm and lit a cig. I looked up and seen large black triangle craft gliding towards me. I thought holy crap its 1 of them triangles i have read about. I looked down rubbed my eyes and pinched myself. I looked back up and it was still there.it slowly glided past me over the house. I went straight in the house and locked the doors and charged my phone up to start calling people. Later on another friend who i never mentioned it to posted a video on social media asking what is this and she thinks its aliens. I messaged her and told her i was so glad she posted that video as i now know i was not going crazy and it had just happened. This object was seen by well over 100 other people from 6pm (6 hours before i seen it) until sometime in the next day after sunrise.
I was laughed at an ridiculed by stupid people. 1 of them then seen this object on dec 3rd at 3pm start to materialise in the sky from being cloaked and took a picture and sent it me. I laughed at him and flung his comments back at him. Then i welcomed him to the crazy club.
