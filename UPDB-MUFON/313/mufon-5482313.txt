{cak} 3 flying in formation with abrupt stops and direction changes

My family (4 children) and my spouse were outside in our back yard when our oldest yelled "dad the shiny things are back" as we have seen them 3 days in a row now. So I walked out to see and sure enough there were three metallic objects flying in what seemed to be a formation. They would go in to a triangular shape then circle with the bottom one switching positions with the one on top. This went on for about ten minutes before one split from the group and shot off to the north disappearing while the other two remained doing their "dance" after a few minutes the third object returned and they resumed their maneuvers. We watched as planes flew by trying to dismiss them as planes but couldn't. They continued to fly within inches other each other rapidly switching directions and positions. After about 30 more minutes they stopped and hovered for a few seconds then started off to the south and then just disappeared altogether. We have been seeing this consistently over the last three days now and after speaking with others have determined that the way they are moving the gforce would tear somebody apart.
