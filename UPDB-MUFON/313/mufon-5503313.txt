I was experimenting with astrophotography in my back yard and noticed a stationary object within a long exposure containing star trails. Upon zooming in the object was disc shaped and appeared in only a handful of exposures and then disappeared. During an exposure a jet of some kind was flying at a very high altitude in the vicinity of the area. Upon importing the RAW photos into Lightroom and zooming in even further the object moves in one frame and then disappears entirely.
