(gs) It was a hovering metallic craft. It had no wings, no propellers. Within the blink of an eye was gone.

I was riding with my mother back home on Gray Highway. When we both noticed a metal craft in the sky, that was just hovering. It had no wings, no propellers, and had no motion. I first noticed it because the sun reflected brightly off of it. I thought it was a plane at first but then I noticed it wasnt moving. Once I noticed it wasnt moving, I kept watching it as we drove down the highway. I figured that it would come more in focus and I would realize it was just a helicopter. The longer I watched the more I realized this was not normal. I lost sight of the object in an instant. It was as if the craft was hovering and then immediately accerlated to an incredible speed. It was in the west. It must have taken a western flightpath. For how fast it apparently moved though, it could have moved in any direction.
