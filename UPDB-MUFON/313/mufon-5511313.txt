{dr}Bright red light came above treeline, 2 other objects followed then all disappeared.

We were setting up our telescope getting ready to see Saturn rise in Lisbon Maine at 9:00pm. We first observed a red stationary light in a round form pulsing.We immediately thought it was a UFO. It then started traveling north and was flashing and then disappeared. Soon after that we saw a very small pin of white light traveling quickly in the same direction. After that we saw another larger much larger red light rise from behind the tree line heading north for only a short distance and that too to just completely disappeared.After this sighting my mother was freaking out with joy and I was not that excited because I have seen things like this in the sky before.
