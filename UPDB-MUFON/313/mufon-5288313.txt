Glowing Yellow-Orange Orb/Object

My husband and I were out on our balcony on a Friday night when we looked North and saw an orange-yellow orb travel North to Northeast toward the Ocean. It lasted approximately 10-12 seconds. We both have perfect vision and are familiar with what lights from planes and helicopters look like. This bore no resemblance to something we've seen. It was very bright and had a slow and distinct path with no emissions or trail. Then, it completely disappeared. I'm compelled to report this 3 days later because I was reading articles on the Fox News app this morning and saw an article claim that there has been a spike in UFO reporting and it specified Florida as a hotbed of activity. Interesting times.
