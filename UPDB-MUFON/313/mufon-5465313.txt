{tjd} hovering glowing yellow like object for about 2 minutes then dove east/down and vanished.

I was driving with my friend to the gas station around 10PM. All of a sudden my friend said "look at that", and I looked to the east and there was this hovering glowing like object right infront of us. It seemed like there were 4 discs to the object but it was extremely large. We tried to follow it probably for less then 2 minutes and then it dove farther east and down and vanished. We were both too terrified to film with our phones/ had this eerie indescribable feeling. We even went to the golf course where the object would have landed with the direction it dove down into and there was nothing. Both felt really strange afterwards, I had ringing in my ear the rest of the night and didn't sleep.
