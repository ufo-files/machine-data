Was riding my bike to lake street, as I rounded the curve I could see the gas stations and some other buildings on lake and I94 Eden highway,
That's when I saw the disk, hovering above the gas station/dairy queen,
I stopped in the roadway ad was starring at the disk, the windows were spinning, they were black the disk was silver gray, no sound it hovered for some timetable 2 minutes then moved down toward the ground then moved away from me toward the north disk got smaller and went behind the tree line. From my point of view the disk was she size of the dairy green building, which was small about 100 feet across. Also never saw the bottom just edge on.
