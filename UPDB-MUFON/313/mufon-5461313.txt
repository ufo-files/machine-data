{cak} Rectangle UFO in the clouds in evening rush hour

I was driving home from work and I had just crossed the Ben Franking bridge into Camden, NJ. It was rush hour and there were cars at a stand still. I kept looking at the sky because it was raining pretty hard. The sky began to clear and I saw a rectangle UFO that appeared in the clouds. It was partially hidden in the clouds and was a dull dark gray color. I looked at it for a few seconds and was amazed! The UFO made no noise and it felt like I was in a silent movie. I looked down and I felt like I had missing time and I didn't know where I was. I don't know if anyone else saw the UFO but it was in plain sight.
