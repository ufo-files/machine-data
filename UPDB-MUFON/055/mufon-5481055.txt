I was on a flight from BWI to MIDway/ Chicago. The Pilot informed us that the flight would be of longer duration because of a massive storm east of Chicago. He was going to fly South, West then North to Chicago Midway. Sometimes I take pictures if the clouds are interesting or if I am bored. It was little bit of both this time. So I had my camera at the window snapping pictures when this object appeared moving in the same direction as the plane. It looked strange to me because it was bright and kind of fuzzy and it was moving rapidly. It looked odd. So, I took as many pictures as possible in the short time it was in view. I have a Canon EOS Rebel T5i 18 megapixel camera. 
The object look slightly cloud like and it disappeared behind clouds. Also, I had the impression it was moving faster than the plane. I have included the original pictures and my attempts to enhance the pictures to obtain more info about the object. I also have the flight information. United Flight 395
