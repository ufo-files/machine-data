{rjl} Orb more bright than Jupiter travelling NW to SE

During set up for Milky Way photography session, I observed a very bright orb rapidly traveling from NW to SE, a duration of approximately 1 minute. I was able to run into my house and get binoculars and see the object through them. It appeared very bright, around 3 x the apparent size of Jupiter and at least twice as bright viewed through 12x50 binoculars, with fuzzy halo around it. It seemed to slow towards the far SE point of my view.
Immediately after I lost sight, I noticed 3 aircraft (red and green wing lights) further to the south and west in a triangle formation. One then left the formation and flew into the direction that the bright orb had just traveled. I checked online flight trackers and there seems to be have no registered aircraft in the area at that time. 
Directions and line of sight radius noted in the diagram are approximate.
