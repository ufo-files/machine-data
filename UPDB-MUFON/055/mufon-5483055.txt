back garden sighting of a house sized molten orb around 100 to 150 ft high. Orb travelled in straight line illuminating rooftops.

I was opening the back door of the house to let the dog relieve himself before bed. The instant I opened the door was the moment I noticed the orb. It was roughly the size of a two story 3 to four bedroom house. It approached travelling slightly from the north west heading toward the coast. The object wast as plain as day in a clear sky. Roughly about 100 to 150 ft above me. It was orange in colour and appeared to have a molten fire like surface. Although this was a very subtle effect. I observed this ufo for around a minute as it glided slowly above the houses of my estate before it disappeared behind the rooftops. This was just the beginning of a wave of sightings that continue to this day. I have only seen this object the one time. But many other sightings of smaller and high altitude objects.
