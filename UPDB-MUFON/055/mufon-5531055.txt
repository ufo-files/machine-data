White light traversing the sky - dissapeared.

I'm a meteorologist so I frequently step outside to check the sky conditions - while doing so - I saw a bright white light roughly twice the size of the background stars behind it. It was traveling at the speed of a jet aircraft at high altitude (as witnessed from the ground) from northwest to southeast. At first I thought it was a plane - but it had no navigation lights and made no sound I could hear. The sky was clear and I had a good view of the object. It moved accross the sky away from me for aprox. 2 1/2 minutes - then turned red - and dissapeared as if it had simply blinked out of existance. I felt dissapointed that I didn't have an opportunity to see it in more detail.
