{kt}3 brights flashes followed by entity looking at me then 2 more flashes opening my eyes in a white hallway

I was laying in bed about to drift off to sleep when there were 3 extremely bright flashes through the window into the room. When my eyes adjusted there was an entity looking at me with a look like it had found something it was looking for. After that there were 2 more bright flashes and I opened my eyes to find that I was standing in a long white hallway. I could feel static in the air and it was also cold feeling. I remember feeling something touch my shoulder and I turned around to find another entity behind me in a room. It used a one of its hands to motion for me to follow it into the room. In the middle of the room there was a table and the entity told me to "take a rest" (although I never saw its mouth move) It pats on the table as if to tell me to sit down. I sit down and it forces me to lay down. All I remember from that point is the back of my head hitting the table and then opening my eyes and I'm back in my home. It was almost 6 in the morning when I looked over at the alarm clock next to my bed. I had looked at my watch next to the alarm clock and it said 4:15 My husband had not noticed that I was gone the entire time and I had a nosebleed.
