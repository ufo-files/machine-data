{rjl} A very large glowing ball with a tail and the second one looked the same but smaller

At about 7:30 P.M. I was in my patio on the phone with my daughter. I looked out my patio windows and saw one very large glowing ball with a tail and one smaller one. My daughter looked out her window and said she saw the same thing. We were both very excited knowing we were looking at something very strange. The smaller ball was traveling south with a tail behind it and we lost sight of it. The larger ball stayed stationary for a few minutes then started going straight up till no longer in our sight. Then I noticed a red large circle of looked like smoke. To the right of that where the smaller ball was there was a long red line. The larger ball glowed very bright and stayed stationary for about 4 or 5 minutes, then rose very slowly till out of sight.
