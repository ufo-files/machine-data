UFO Seen In The Neighborhood

Myself and 2 kids playing in the street one summer saw a UFO, which looked like 2 cereal bowls inverted with a blinking light. This was above a 2 story house on the corner and it was silver and blue. There were no windows or doors and no sound. It was across the street from us.
