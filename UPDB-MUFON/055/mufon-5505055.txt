{kt}black triangle, silent no lights just hovered over houses in a path like motion then disappeared.

It was summer of 2006. It was my birthday july 17. It was about 10 o clock pm I was out front hanging out with my friends talking and I looked up to the horizon area of the night sky and I noticed a black object. I realized it was a triangle because it blocked out the stars. It had no lights no sound. From my view in the sky it was about the size of a basketball. In reality it must have been about the size of baseball field. It was creepy. I looked at my friends but they were still talking to eachother so I got up and followed it with my head and it just kept going. It hovered as it passed by over the rooftops of the neighborhood houses maybe above power lines height. It was towards my grandmas house looking at it from my moms house. It started NW then it was looking towards the E and then last It was NE. Then I lost sight of it behind my view of the trees up ahead. It really changed me. I told my friends what I had just seen and they thought I was crazy.
