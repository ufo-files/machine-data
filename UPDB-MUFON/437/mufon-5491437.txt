{cak} A Powerful Force

On July 12, 2015; Humfried, Virginia between the hours of 0235 AM to 0300 AM, I decided to go back outside to check things out again. This time, I observed one more of the sightings creeping across the Northwest ceiling of the sky between 0240 to 0255, and then about one to three minutes later I saw a totally different phenomenon appearing out of the medium of the sky as if escaping one dimension and dashing into another, seemingly at a rate 10 times the speed of light or thought.

This occurrence greatly amazed me, and I hurried to ask a neighbor who was sitting in his car two houses down to see if he had seen what just happened in the sky, above our neighbors homes across the street.
