{rjl} Soccer ball,round but flat planes.maybe two lights, looked terrestrial but silent and tumbling through the air in straight line with no means of propulsion a few feet above telephone lines.

Came out of my house in Las Vegas,Nevada. I looked up and noticed a soccer ball sized polyhedron tumbling North through the air down Rancho Blvd. It was silent and had 2 tiny lights on it. It proceeded in a straight line just above the telephone lines with no observable means of propulsion. I was fascinated and watched until it left my view. I was wondering if anyone else had ever seen such an object which is why I am making this report
