{cak} BRIGHT FLASHING RED AND WHITE LIGHTS PORT WING WITH LITTLE CHANGE IN POSITION.EVENTUALLYOBJECT PASSED BENEATH OUR PLANE THEN EMITTED TWO LARGE FLASHES

WE WERE FLYING EAST OF CHICAGO ENROUTE TO NEW YORK. I OBSERVED ONE RED AND ONE WHITE LIGHT. THEY MOVED TOGETHER.RED LIGHT FLASHED REGULARLY BUT WHITE LIGHT WAS MUCH MORE ACTIVE .IT WAS LIKE A VERY POWERFUL STROBE AND MUCH BRIGHTER THAN THE RED ONE.I ASSUMED IT WAS A PLANE BUT VIDEOED IT ANYWAY.THE LIGHTS TRACKED OUR AIRCRAFT AND MATCHED OUR SPEED FOR ABOUT 25 MINUTES. LIGHTS STAYED MORE OR LESS IN LINE WITH OUR PORT WING TIP BUT AT AN UNDETERMINED DISTANCE. I WAS EXCITED BUT FRUSTRATED AS I COULDNT SEE ANYTHING THROUGH MY 
VIEWING SCREEN ON THE CAMERA.I SIMPLY POINTED AND VIDEOED AND HOPED FOR THE BEST.EVENTUALLY THE LIGHTS PASSED UNDER OUR AIRCRAFT.ABOUT TEN SECONDS AFTER LIGHTS HAD DISAPPEARED THERE WAS AN ENORMOUS WHITE FLASH THAT ILLUMINATED THE ENTIRE UNDER PORTION OF THE PORT WING.TEN SECONDS LATER THERE WAS ANOTHER HUGE WHITE FLASH.I THOUGHT AN ENGINE HAD BLOWN.
