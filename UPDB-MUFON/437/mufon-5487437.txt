{dr}Thought it was a star at first but then it started moving

I was at home north of Stephenville, TX in my front yard looking at stars. I thought it was a satellite at first because it was northeast of me moving towards southeast and looked much higher than a plane. Barely visible at first Unless you had good eyes. But then it stopped and was completely stationary. It looked just like a star. I watched it for a minute or two and then it started moving erratically. It seemed to do figure eights and half circles before reversing back around another half circle. Sometimes it seemed to fade out completely and sometimes it seemed much closer to me. It did not flash at any point, just once that I saw it went out for a couple of seconds and then came back on. I watched it for at least 15 minutes, and a storm was rolling in to the north east. It rained for a while but after the clouds cleared I went back out about an hour later and the thing was still there! Stil behaving the same. Eventually I had to go to bed because I had to work today, but the thing was still moving around to the south east when I went to bed. I was able to show it to my wife and also to my mother. I was fascinated by this as I often stargaze and had seldom seen anything this interesting.
