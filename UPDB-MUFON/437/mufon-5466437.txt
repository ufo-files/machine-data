{rjl}7 lights flew in formation, stopped, and absorbed each other only to break up once Again and fly off

I was at Watkins Glen International Speedway when a group of 7 lights came from the southwest in a tight formation. I thought they we drones as there were 7 of them, some were yellow, others white, others blue and one was orange. However, after about 10 seconds they started to act very irregular. The yellow one absorbed 2 other and made a LARGE orange light while the other lights circled around it. This lasted for about 5 seconds then they shot off to the South East at a speed and distance that makes it impossible for them to be drones. All 20 or so of us that saw them were convinced we just saw the real deal.
