{tjd} Very large cigar shaped object

On a Sunday morning in April or May of 2007, I was driving down Kraemer Blvd. in the city of Placentia, Orange County CA.  Two of my kids and I had just stopped to get breakfast at a drive thru--pretty normal morning. As I drove for on Kraemer Blvd. I saw a huge cigar shaped object hovering in the air. At first I thought it was some type of air balloon for a city festival but I realized I hadn't heard any advertisement about any festivals. In that moment I thought, "What the hell is that"!  I started to drive faster because I wanted to get a better angle to see the other side of it. However, when I made a left turn to get a better view, it simply vanished. Literally, it was gone. At this point I realized this thing was something "different". It's ability to take off with that amount of speed totally shocked me and the kids. I also thought that maybe it had the ability to camouflage it's self. All I know is that it was a very large cigar shaped object, it was a white color, it appeared to have some time of appendage on it, and it was gone in less then 3 to 4 minutes or less.  I have never forgotten it in all of these years. To note: I was the only person driving north bound on Kraemer that morning.
