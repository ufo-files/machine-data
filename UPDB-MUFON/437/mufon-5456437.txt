{rjl} FLEET OF UFOS OVER SOUTH SIDE OF Chicago headed toward Lake Michigan

Around 6:20 p.m. on December 22nd, 2019, I was leaving from a family member's house when I looked for the Southwest skies of Chicago and witnessed a straight line, fleet, of unidentified flying objects flying over a senior citizens building. I began to record and was able to capture two different moments where multiple unidentified flying objects Pier from over the senior citizens home and also created formations in the Western skies of Chicago as they headed Eastward toward lake Michigan.
