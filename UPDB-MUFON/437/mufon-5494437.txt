{dr}I witnessed a UFO hovering over a neighbor's garden near the USNA, felt sedated and saw it shoot off into space.

I was at home the night of this event, which was located directly next to the United States Naval Academy. It was around 8:30 PM in late Sept. 2007. As I sat outside on my third-floor balcony relaxing, I noticed something moving in the neighbor's garden/small vineyard that I could see clearly below. The plants seemed to be giving off an energy and moving but there was no wind. I stood up to see what was going on and suddenly I realized that there was something hovering over the garden that seemed to be camouflaged with it. The round object was about 25ft in diameter and seemed to have a surface that I can only describe as like that of a stingray. A mist seemed to emit from the underside of the disc. Suddenly I was dizzy and felt sedated. (Let me assure you that I had NOT been drinking, nor was I taking any kind of medicine or drug.) As I sat back down in this physical state, the object then rose to house top level then shot off into air so fast, that when I blinked it was so far up in the sky it moved off out of sight. I didn't know what to do and I was so tired that I just sat there staring in disbelief. The sedated feeling kind of made me second guess everything and made me think I had been seeing things, so I did nothing. It has been nagging at me ever since and wanted to finally tell my story.
