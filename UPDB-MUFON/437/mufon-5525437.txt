Extremely bright & shiny orangish-white cigar/saucer shaped object flying through blue sky

At approximately 7:50 p.m., my son and I were in our backyard when he happened to look up into the sky and noticed a very bright and shiny long white object flying in the blue sky. It was a good sized object (depending on distance) as related to an airplane that was also in the sky flying near the object. We used my phone to capture still images as well a video (attached)that shows the plane in the frame (the plane shows black against the blue sky). The object was traveling at a much slower speed than the plane. When zooming in on the still image (attached), the shape looks saucer like and has an orange glow in the center. There were no blinking lights.
