My brother and I had just sat on the bench in my front yard last night (7/26/12) around 9 PM. After just a few minutes, we happened to both see a light appear and go out toward the east. I live in Wesley, TX, and we were facing east toward Houston. It seemed to be over Chappell Hill, maybe closer. We continued to watch, but did not see it again.
