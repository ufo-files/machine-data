hovering and darting up/down side to side w/ flashing lights

My wife, son and I were sitting on our back porch and noticed what appeared to be a star-like object hovering over the tree line. It had red and blue llights that were blinking periodically in no particular pattern and was slowely moving up and down and left and right.  Through binoculars, the object appeared to be triangular-shaped, with 2 rows of lights on it. We observed it for approximately 1.5 hours before we went to bed.
