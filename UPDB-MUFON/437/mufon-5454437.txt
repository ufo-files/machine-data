{rjl} observed long sraight line of round bright lights traveling SW to NE

I was sitting in the outdoor hot tub facing west. A compartively large bright light approached from the SW head NE. It was followed by a long line of similar ights, however less bright, moving the same direction at basicaly the same speed. However the first light was going much faster and disappeared to the north. 
The lights in the line were in groups of one and two. I watched for a few moments then left the tub and called my girl friend , Shirley Beer at 6:16am. She got outside in time to see the last of the line of lights move out of view to the NE. She estimated seeing 12 before the last one was gone. The last light follwed some distance behind the rest. No sound, contrail ,discharge or blinking lights.. All together I saw at least 50 over a 8 to 10 minute span. This sighting was similiar to the previously submitted case # 105051.
