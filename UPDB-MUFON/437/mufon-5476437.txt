FILLED OUT AT MUFON HEADQUARTERS, WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE,BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER 18809, ON WEDNESDAY 17, 2017.

I WENT OUTSIDE TO HAVE A SMOKE AND LOOKED UP AND SAW THIS HUGH OBJECT. IT LOOKED LIKE A CHRISTIAN CROSS WITH BIG BALLS ON THE FOUR ENDS. IT HAD OVER TWO HUNDRED WINDOWS WITH LIGHT SHINING BRIGHTLY OUT OF THEM. THE WINDOWS WERE ALL OVER THE CRAFT. THE LARGE END BALLS WERE REDDISH IN COLOR. IT MADE NO NOISE AND MOVED VERY SLOWLY OVER MY HEAD, NO MORE THAN 60 MILES AN HOUR. I SAW THE BACK OF IT AS WELL. IT SEEMED TO BE ABOUT 2000 FEET HIGH AND 300 FEET OR MORE IN SIZE. IT WAS HEADING IN THE DIRECTION OF VENTURA, WEST.
I GREW UP IN THIS HOUSE, BUT NOW MY UNCLE OWNS IT AND I WAS VISITING HIM.
THE SIGHTING LASTED 5 MINUTES AND I LOST SIGHT OF IT BEHIND SOME TREES.
I THINK IT WAS EITHER FRIDAY OR SATURDAY, MAY 5TH OR 6TH.

I AM A CIVIL ENGINEER AND I HAVE NEVER SEEN ANYTHING LIKE THIS SINCE I WAS 14. WHEN I WAS 14, IN THE SAME HOUSE, I SAW SOMETHING VERY SIMILAR.
