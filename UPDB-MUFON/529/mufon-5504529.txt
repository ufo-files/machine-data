Alright, this all happened during Autumn with my three friends "J", "M", and "T". It was an interesting night already, I must admit that some "greens" may have been smoked (which I'm sure in the eyes of many would remove credibility from this sighting) but it is part of this account, and therefore must be documented. We were on my balcony facing the woods behind my apartment, [Link removed/cms/tg](North-west.) We were preparing to smoke a bit more green, and being the lesser-experienced and younger me, I accepted M's offer to give me the first hit on one condition (I had to let him slap me on the face.) Right after he slapped me these lights ascended from the forest.

  The two lights resembled a car's headlights as if it were driving straight toward someone. I assume there was a parent object since the lights moved as if there was a mirror between them (keep in mind that it was 1am so the lights were the only parts of the object that were visible.) These lights were blueish-white in hue. They also created a slight beam that actually came to a well defined stop (unlike most beams, which usually tend to fade into an end) which to me indicates a cloud or mist surrounding the object. The object hovered in one spot for quite some time as we looked up at it in awe. It actually hovered in a sideways, figure-8 fashion (symbol representing infinity.) At first I suggested to the others that it was a helicopter but this is not a logical explanation considering the fact that it created no sound. As we continued to watch I started to feel like it did not want us there and the only reason it stayed was to ensure that we did not follow it. We went back inside to get some food, after that M and I went back to look through the sliding glass door that lead to the balcony and the object was gone.
