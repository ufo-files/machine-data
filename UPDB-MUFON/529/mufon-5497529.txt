{dr}Glowing object off USS Yellowstone

Deck Department Seaman E-3 US Navy while heading due north on the USS Yellowstone, was in the look out watch station when he viewed a green glow just under the surface of the water, 2,000 yards dead ahead.
When he looked through his binoculars, he couldn't make out a definite shape, other than a green glow. 
Was very experienced and had never viewed anything like this.  He tried to radio over that there was an object dead ahead, no on one would respond or take him seriously. 
When they closed in to 1,000 yards, it appeared they were going to collide with the object that seemed to be just a few feet below the surface of the water. 
By the time they came upon the glowing object it was about 100 feet below the surface. His ship missed the object by 20-30 feet. 
No one on the ship seemed to notice this event, and the witness was beyond frustrated. No sound was heard during the event.
