17 star like objects in line

In Cayuga Ontario Canada, I witnessed 17 star sized and shaped objects slowly streaming across the sky inline SW to NE at 4am on May 25 2020. They were approximately 10 seconds apart but I now think they may have possibly been more satellites as seen in the video on the MUFON website, however I am uncertain if any were launched or would have been orbiting in my area. I also seen 2 similar objects minutes later at the same altitude orbiting in different directions.
