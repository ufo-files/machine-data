On Friday may12, 2017 I was leaving to go to the store. When I stepped outside I saw a bright red glowing light in the sky. For a split second I thought it was just a low flying airplane or maybe a helicopter. After a few seconds I realized it was neither. There was no engine noise and it was moving way too fast. I pulled out my iPhone and started recording. When I zoomed in it looked more like a yellowish/orange disc flying across the sky. There was pretty good detail. I zoomed out to show the whole sky and when I zoomed back in ithe object had clearly gained quite a bit of altitude. It continued on its path until it disappeared over the horizon. Only took about 90 seconds, maybe even faster than that. I was so amazed at what I was seeing that I didn't pay that much attention to it. All I know is that I try to watch all shuttle and rocket launches from Cape Canaveral and I can view these from behind my house so I have a pretty good idea of how long it takes those rockets to leave sight. This was waaaaaay faster. I have already reported this sighting and sent the video to a UFO group in Florida who quickly responded that they think I took a video of Venus or Jupiter...Lol! I have always been intrigued by astronomy and the possibility of life that exists other than how we know it. I can say with certainty that this was NOT a planet.
