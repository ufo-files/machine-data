perfectly huge blue round object.. The blue hue was darker then the blue sky.

I was driving s/e by Marina Del Rey, Ca. at approx 2 pm when I noticed a hugh blue round object floating s/w from behind a large cloud. At first I thought it was a weather balloon, but it was too large for a weather balloon. The object had no lights or windows or anything dangling under or around it. That object was so beautiful with it's blue hue and it's perfect circumference. I was 65 years old at that time and I never saw an object so large. It was traveling at a fast pace and was driving in the opposite direction and lost sight of it.
