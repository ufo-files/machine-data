{dr}Light falling from the sky. Changed from Blue to purple before disappearing behind tree line.

I left my home at 10 pm with my 2 children to pick up my wife from her friend's house. My oldest child was still awake, but my youngest was asleep. When I put my youngest child in his seat to buckle him up I thought he started to cry because of the cold. When I got in the driver's seat I realized he was actually laughing hysterically. I shook him and woke him up asking him what was funny and if he was okay. He glared at me and said nothing. I have never seen a look like that on his face before. I thought it was strange. He began laughing hysterically again so hard he was shaking. I tried to speak to him, but he just kept laughing. It started to scare me, almost like he was possessed. Right before I pulled over to wake him again I saw a blue light falling from the sky. I thought it was an airplane light at first, but it was not the controlled decent of an airplane. The light then changed from Blue to purple then disappeared behind the tree line. My oldest child got scared and began to cry. After I woke my youngest again, he gave me the same dirty look and then fell asleep again and started laughing. After I woke him up for the third time he looked upset and sad, he fell back asleep and didn't make a sound for the rest of the night. This experience has bothered me enough to report it and I have a gut feeling my child's behavior and the light are connected.
