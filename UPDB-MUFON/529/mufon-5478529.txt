First noticed low over city moving. We followed them until one was directly above us.

It was my girlfriends birthday, so on our way home, we crossed a bridge and I noticed 2 bright orange lights, low and I told her to stop, so we pulled over on the bridge. I thought they were typhoon aircraft with their afterburners on. She witnessed it too. As I opened my window, I noticed that they weren't moving and no noise. I told her to put her foot down and follow them. We ended up pulling over down welland road and would you believe, in front of a police officer who was talking to some young lad. We both got out the car and one of the orange lights had gone and I had noticed the altitude climb as it appeared smaller, though still bright. It hovered there for a good few minutes. Police officer also starred at it. A few minutes later, it headed west and its acceleration was incredible. My hair stood on end. We then went home in silence. I called my step dad as he is a member of the fighter control website to see if there has been any aircraft flying over this area we was at. It was a negative, nothing was flying. The day after, 2 pilots reported in the paper that two orange lights or ufo's had been spotted over the Isle of Wight. I believed these to possibly have been what we had seen.
