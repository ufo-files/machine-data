cylindrical white object travelling very fast

Witnessed from the ground a White colored cylinder traveling extremely fast over DFW . No wings, no contrail. Aircraft flying near the ufo was flying above it and approximate heading of 270 with contrail and clearly defined as an airplane. The UFO was travelling significantly faster, than the aircraft by at least 2 to 3 times faster. It appeared to actually be some kind of missile but may have been too big for that comparison.
