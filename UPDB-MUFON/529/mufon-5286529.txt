Glowing object(s) hovering above treeline, making sudden course changes, and quickly ascending and disappearing.

DATE: 2 July 2021, around 9pm; viewed from back deck of home in Lake St Louis, MO. We witnessed what we thought was a small aircraft that was flying very low (just above the treetops). However, it produced no sound, suddenly changed course, took a sharp turn to the left, very quickly ascended, and disappeared into the sky. It looked like it had fire coming out of the top and from the wings. This happened three times in a row, so we're not sure if it was three different objects, or the same one circling three times. I was able to get photos as it/they were ascending, right before it/they disappeared.

It was very eerie and unsettling.

This event recurred on July 4th, but the glowing objects arrived in groups of 4, then 3, another 3, and a final 3. I will submit a separate form for that sighting.
