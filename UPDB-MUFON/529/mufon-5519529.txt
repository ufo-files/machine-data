UFO in Santa Barbara, CA 4/9/12 8:50pm

On 4/9/12 around 8:50pm at night I was in my backyard watering plants and and looked up in the sky and saw a bright object and was wondering what star it was, and then all of a sudden it started moving. I called my wife outside as it was moving in a straight line and thought it was a satellite, and as we were watching it move in a straight direction for about 1 minute it all of a sudden it disappeared.  I tried to record it on my iPhone 4s but the video didn't come out very well, but I have included it anyway.
