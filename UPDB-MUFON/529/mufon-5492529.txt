{rjl} GLOWING OBJECT HEADING FROM SOUTH TO NORTH

WAS OUT SIDE SHOOTING DEEP SKY OBJECTS IN URSA MAJOR AND SAW THIS GLOWING OBJECT ABOUT THE SIZE OF THE FULL MOON. AIMED THE 102 MM SCOPE WITH DIGITAL CAMERA AT IT AND STARTED SHOOTING. IT MADE NO ENGINE NOISE. IN MY FINDERSCOPE I NOTICED AN AMBER LIGHT ON THE RIGHT SIDE AND A WHITE LIGHT ON THE LEFT THAT WERE NOT BLINKING. THE OBJECT LOOKED LIKE A VEHICLE WITH A CLOUD SURROUNDING IT.
