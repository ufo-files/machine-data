Three lights different colour, one silver light joined a red and green light from different direction then flew off in a formation

Me and my brother and friend were heading home from army cadets , it was mid summer and about nine I the evening, we saw a green light and red light in the sky traveling slow but steady , no noise , we then saw a silver whiite light join the lights from a different direction, then they all travelled off in formation . The next day it was reported in the Cambridge evening news paper , flying saucers over Cambridge. I have also seen other lights in the sky at different times in my life .
