{cak} Bright lights hovering in the woods late at night

Looking to the West through the woods from the back of my house, there were two lights that resembled headlights but were half way up the tree line, way too high for headlights on the next road over. The lights split up and one went slowly to the north about 200 yards and stopped, while the other light remained and was stationary. The light slowly hovered back over to the other light and then they both disappeared when they came together.
