One long led strip like object that was in a straight line. Alternating bright to dark, pulsating from left to right in the direction of line movement

We had stepped out on my patio to let my dog out and looked up to see a line or what appeared to be a led strip about the size of my pointer finger slowly moving and flashing in the direction it moved. Went light dark light dark light dark but would pulsate from front to back alternating from dark to light in the direction of the lines movement. At first we thought shooting star, but that happens within a flash and very quick movement along the sky. This lasted for 39 seconds and only traveled 15 feet in the night sky before slowing dissipating into the night sky right when my phone got pulled up to document.
