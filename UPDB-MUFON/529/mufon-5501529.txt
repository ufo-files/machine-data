{dr}Black Triangle flew over while driving just outside of Walla Walla

After taking my girlfriend for a little sunset drive in the foothills, on the way back to town just after getting on paved road I noticed what I thought was a plane in front of us in the sky, we thought it was weird cause it only had one red light flashing or pulsating. As it approached closer overhead it was a triangle with 2 bright white light on the back corners not flashing and as it passed over saw the 3rd white front light. I stopped and shut off the truck in the road(birch creek) heard no noise and a plane this close would be heard. my truck had the doors off and top off. grabbed my phone to shoot some video and it just slowly headed east over the mountains.
