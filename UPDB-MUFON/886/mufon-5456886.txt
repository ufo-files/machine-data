A group of 50 UFO’s unidentified, moving with uniform speed and direction in straight line

At 5:50am on November 25th, my spouse Matt got home from work and let dog out for a pee in Langley, BC. He looked South in the clear sky and witnessed around 50 unidentified objects that were star-like appearance that were coming from South/West direction moving towards North/East route. These objects were moving in a uniform speed with most of them being equally spaced apart, with a few flying in groups of 2 or 3 off, set from each other. But most of them flew mainly in single fashion and they were all on the exact same flight path. 
As a reference point, my wife saw a shooting star that moved in a different direction perpendicular to the group of UFO’s at the same time that the phenomenon of the UFO’s was happening. 
We tried to take videos on our iPhone XR and Sony camera but believe the objects were too far away to be picked up on camera.
The airplane was flying directly West from our location, towards Vancouver international airport. The airplane was lower then the unidentified objects and had regular plane light beacons.
