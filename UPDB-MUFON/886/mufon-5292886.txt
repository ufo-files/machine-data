Possible TR3B, large triangular aircraft

This was seen on a Monday night between 9pm and 1am in 2002 or 2003. All I can be sure of is that it was a Monday. I think a TR3B or something like that was seen flying north to south   along what would be highway 360 in Arlington and Grand Prairie. It was much bigger than say a 747 and flying only 50 to 66 percent the height of real commercial aircraft that take off and land at DFW. I grew up on Air Force bases and have either worked as a NASA/FAA contractor or airline employee for many years. This was not a regular military or commercial aircraft. I worked at Southwest Airlines when this was seen and told a manager who was a former F4 pilot about the large, triangular, silent aircraft. I would have run in to get my camera but figured it would be gone by the time I would get back outside. The thing was witnessed flying in a southerly direction for about 30 seconds. I only bring this sighting up now in case you have other reports of a Monday night sighting back in the early 2000’s.
