There were approximately 7-10 single lights observed travelling

On January 22, at 10.52pm in Auckland, NZ, we observed 7-10 single orange glowing round lights, (approximately the size of a small car, travelling at a steady, fast rate, relatively low altitude), appear from differing locations, which then veered off & disappeared into the same area (approx NE direction).
Also included are 2 photographs taken on November 2nd, at 2.51pm (NZ time) that show a moving triangular object. When zoomed in they look to be 3 diamond shaped objects in a triangular formation, plus another picture shows a single flat oval shape object, that don’t look to be a flock of birds.
