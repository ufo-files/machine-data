{jpg} Camping in Malibu canyon and witnessed the object over head very high and stationary and it moved west in the sky ( Australian pilot filmed what I saw)

The object moved and then stopped & then moved &estoppel & moved until I was blocked by rock formations .my son & brother in law & I were camping that night when I observed this object .i have seen a comet in south Chicago and it didn't move like a comet because this object stopped & and then moved . When we came back the next day the news had what the pilot had filmed and it was the same as I saw. If I were guessing I would think it was ultra high altitude . This night was one of the clearest nights I had experienced in the Malibu area .i believe it was jan .1 ,1979 evening
