{rjl} We watched and recorded object for half an hour or more, ate dinner, and it was gone when we came back outside.

My boyfriend and I stood outside watching what looked like a super bright star in the eest make small figure 8 patterns in the sky. He recorded it on his phone, and I went inside after about 20 minutes. This was around 7 or 7:30pm. Then, after 40 minutes or so he came inside, and we ate dinner. Around 9:20pm he went outside to lock up our shop and said the bright light had disappeared. The light was at least a mile away and about as high as a plane would fly, though it was difficult to tell because it was so large. It stayed in roughly the same spot for over an hour, and we're kicking ourselves that we didn't keep recording.
