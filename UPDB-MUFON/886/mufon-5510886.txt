{nd} This appeared to be a glowing red/orange orb.

I was just dropping off my sister and 2 nephews at their home at about 11:50 pm and was driving down the street when I noticed a red/orange orb floating maybe a hundred feet off the ground or so heading north. I spotted the object immediately after looking at the moon. I could only describe the object as a glowing ball although I must concede that it may be a chinese lantern or some other explainable phenomenon. I began feeling a little nervous and excited, but decided to follow the object after it left my field of view. The entire event lasted for less than 2 minutes but I managed to capture some video.
