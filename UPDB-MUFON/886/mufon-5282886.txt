Pulsating Orb-Like Star

I've observed this object and others like it each night it is clear over Newberg. These objects do not move and appear to remain in a geostationary orbit with the earth for the entire evening and reappear the following night. They are slightly larger than a star in the sky, but are brighter and pulsate rapidly often changing colors if you watch them in comparison to a normal star. Object 1 (see attached sketch) is the brightest and closest of the ones that I can see and I'm normally able to see 5-10 of these across the evening sky. Currently there are 2 others that are strategically located within the Orion Constellation and are located where the existing stars should be making them almost un-noticeable. One lies where the star Rigel should be located and the other is where the star Betelgeuse should be located within the Orion Constellation. I also witnessed one within the handle of the Big Dipper. From my observations, these objects do not make any sound and do not match up with planets or satellites. Based on my observations, these do eventually move, but I have not witnessed it and only notice when one isn't where I previously observed it over the series of a few weeks or months. I have been observing and witnessing these (when the evening skies are clear) over Newberg since July of 2021.
