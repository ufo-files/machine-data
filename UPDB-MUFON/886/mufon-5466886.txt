{rjlBright light traveling in straight line N by NE about 30 deg above horizon. Light slowly faded as it ascended into the sky and disappeared. Never reappeared. Clear night no clouds, no strobes and not a plane. light got smaller as it moved into space.

I was home at about 2246 hours and went out to smoke and was looking up at the sky and stars. Movement caught my eye and I observed a bright white light to the north about 30 degrees above the horizon several miles in the air. It was a clear night and there were no clouds or planes in the area. I observed the bright spherical light moving east and ascending into the air and moving away as it got smaller and smaller. The light sped up and appeared to be traveling into space at a high rate of speed. The light got dimmer and dimmer until the light disappeared into the sky and never reappeared. Total duration was about 10 to 20 seconds and moved an incredible distance in a short period of time. I thought at first, oh it's just another plane but immediately realized that it was moving very fast and there was no sound, strobes, or blinking red lights that you would normally see on planes from the ground. As I was looking at it ascend into the sky I felt a strong feeling of excitement and the hair on the back of my neck stood up, I wished it would come back. I was not close enough to my phone to record the incident. Right after the light disappearing I observed a small meteor entering earths atmosphere and burning out. The light was not moving as fast as a meteor and was a solid bright white light the ascended into space and the light slowly faded as it moved further away.
