{cak} Bright circular light appeared to be falling out of the sky.

It was about 7:20 PM NE of Decorah, IA when I was sitting in my living room facing East and I saw a bright glowing white light which was traveling in a southerly direction. It caught my attention because it did not blink and no other color lights appeared to be present. It may have been a plane or helicopter but it was much brighter than airplanes or helicopters I have seen in the past and it did not have blinking lights. It was also descending at a high rate of speed and was much too big to be a shooting star. I lost sight of it behind a ridge line but probably viewed it for 5 seconds.
