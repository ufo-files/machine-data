{nd} I walked outside with my sister and mother and my mother looked up and told us to look. Three bright orange red balls in the sky in a triangular pattern.

At around 10 minutes after 10pm Tuesday 12/24/13, I walked my mother and sister outside to their car as they were leaving the Christmas party at my house here in Austintown, Ohio. As my mother turned to get into her drivers side door, she looked up and exclaimed "Wow look at those". My sister and I turned around, and overhead of the house were three bright orange/red orbs moving from East to West directly. We had only 35 seconds to observe them before they went into a cloud bank. They were low, maybe 2,000-3,000 ft off the ground, so very observable and clearly seen. Another strange part was, after they disappeared into the clouds, my mother and sister left and I went back inside the house. I waited for about 5-10 minutes and went back outside, and looked up. The rather large cloud bank that they disappeared into had also disappeared and the sky was completely clear and dark with all the stars visible. However the objects were nowhere in sight either. I have attached a photo that is not from the event, but it shows exactly what we saw. The photo is really close to our observations.
