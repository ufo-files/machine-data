{dr}Bright Orange Sphere South of Lummi Island, South Portage Island, Dissapeared South

I was going to shut south facing window curtain, & saw a bright orange sphere through the trees. It was centered over Portage Is, Bellingham Bay, WA, but definitely south of Lummi Island. I ran over to the front of the house, looked out the front glass door with binoculars. The object was very bright orange, round in shape, seemed to be slowly moving northward. I asked son to come look at it, but as I was speaking to him, it faded out, seemed to be heading southward. 2nd time I saw this type of brilliant orange sphere, in the same area this year. I remember feeling like it's back, but not a pleasant feeling, & why is it here?
