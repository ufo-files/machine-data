{nd} Daughter spoted. Too high for hovering bird, wings too broad. Observed 3 min. then floated out of sight. bright sunlight.

Putting groceries in car at Sams Club. Daughter asked me what is that? I asked her, "what is what?", as she pointed into the sky. I saw nothing. Then I got close to her eye with my eye and followed her finger point to an object hovering above us.

At first I thought it was a kite because it was motionless. It was shaped similar to a bird/boomerang, had white-ish color on in the middle and an orange color all around the periphery.

It was basically motionless for about 2 minutes, then like a bird, it began to drift in the SW direction with increasing spead. As it got close to the overhead sun, it became hard to follow and we got in the car and drove home.

I approximate something close to 1.5 Miles in the sky.

It was bright and sunny without cloud cover.
