Black Triangle Sighting

I was in the garden at approx. 21:00 - 22:00.
For some reason, I looked up to see something moving across the sky, coming in my direction. 
Because it was close to New Years, I assumed it was a celebratory drone or blimp because it was moving slow. 
As it got closer, I realised it was a craft. 
It moved in a smooth gliding motion. 
I assumed it was military but as it went over my head, I realised that it did not make a sound.

It went right over my head. 
It had three round lights at each point and a hazy amber glow in the middle.
It then went over the roof and that was it.
I forgot about it for a while assuming it was military but I have never seen anything like it before or since.
After some research, I discovered that many people have witnessed these silent triangle crafts.
