I took a series of 9 pictures, 4 of them have a steak of light I cannot explain.

My ex-girlfriend and I were travelling to Nitinat Lake on the west coast of Vancouver Island to camp and fish. It was late in a very secluded area and we had been drinking for a while, anyway we were naked and some silly conversation had her getting ready to pee on the fire. I told her I was going to take pictures, these were the result. It wasn't until we looked at the pictures on the computer when we got back a few days later that we saw the streak of light going into the motor home and hovering above my laptop.
