{cak} Invisible to naked eye

While smoking a cigarette at work around 6am I took picture of the Moon. There were no stars out nor did I see a plane but it was a little cloudy. I did not see anything unusual at all till I looked at the photo and realized instantly something was there and I kept looking at the sky and could not see it.
