Star-like flying object with strange movement pattern.

I went out with my dog at approx. 00:06.
My dog just started to defecate, so I started looking around. I do it everytime, so he knows I'm not watching him poop. I noticed a movement in the sky - this "ufo" and two airplanes. The airplanes had the standard red blinking lights, but beside the "ufo" their lights seemed dim.
At first I thought it was a meteor of some kind, but it was moving too slow.

It started almost directly above my head, but in less then a minute it descended into maybe 35 degree angle, stopped and went for east. It was very bright, but when I squinted, it seemed the light is comming mainly from a circle in the midsection of the object. It appeared to be a sphere.

I must have been looking at it for longer than I thought, because I haven't noticed my dog was done and was watching me suspiciously from 5 meters away then where he defecated. My head was spinning (it is still, although mildly) and I felt a bit nauseated.

I wanted to pick up the poop my dog left in the grass, and when I looked back up, the object was very far away. He just disappeared gradually after that. 

I returned home, and found out I was out for 35 minutes. I thought the whole walk took 10 minutes max.
