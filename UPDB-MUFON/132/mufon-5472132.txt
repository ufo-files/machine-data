{rjl} Same as case number 88280. One light turned to three.

Driving through downtown kilmarnock and noticed a light in the air moving parallel to the ground at a unusually fast speed. I'm not very good at judging heights but it was in low atmosphere. The light began to split off into three single star shapes with tracers, continuing to move at a high rate of speed. The three stars dispersed into a top middle and lower track. Then vanished. This entire event only lasted about three seconds.
