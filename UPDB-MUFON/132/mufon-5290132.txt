Like a star that zig zags at high speed then stops instantly

I was swimming at night and star gazing with a friend. I saw a dull flashing white light. I said look there is a satellite. Then it stopped about a third of the way across the sky and hovered. then it got larger and brighter. It took off to the south east at a high rate of speed maybe 5 times as fast as a jet. then abruptly stopped and went west again at medium speed. then it stopped again and we could see that it has red orange and blue lights that flashed randomly. Then it went off to the south over a mountain ridge. I could not tell if it was getting lower or just going further away.
