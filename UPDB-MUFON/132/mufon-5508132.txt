{cak} Black mass moving across sky heading NE

Wife was standing outside and observed a dark object moving across the sky from the back on our house. I was in the shower at the time and wasnt able to observe the object. She was sure what the object was because she couldnt see any lights of hear sound. We do live West of the airport and a lot of planes fly over, but this object was dark with zero lights and sound. She said that it was a dark blimp looking object moving from the South and heading Northeast across the sky. It didn't have any lights or windows that she could see. The object moved out of sight behind trees that are located to the North of my property.
