{cak} At 5am i witnessed an upside down teardrop shaped object flashing multiple coloured lights and changing flight direction several times before zooming away.

I got up to use the restroom at 5a. It was still pretty dark out and upon climbing back into bed, I stared out my window into the sky and saw what appeared to be a very bright, multi-coloured star. I stared at it for about 5 seconds and quickly realized this "star" was moving slowly through the sky, flashing blue, red and white. Jolted out of my sleepiness I grabbed my binoculars and began following the flashy objects otherworldly path. During the 10 minutes or so that I watched it I observed this upside-down teardrop-shaped object change flight direction several times, up then down, backward-forward, dimming, brightening and flashing its blue, red and white lights. Eventually it flew out of my sight, disappearing behind some trees. I could still see the flickering of its lights but I had seen enough. All in all it was pretty eerie. The "UFO" was really high up, approx. 1/2-1 mile in the sky. It was definitely not any kind of airplane, helicopter or satellite. This thing was weird. I gotta say, Im not usually into all the hype of this "fire in the sky" stuff. I don't read UFO Stalker or DVR I Know What I Saw. I don't smoke pot or do hallucinogens (this year) so I know I witnessed something crazy. I should add, once I laid back down I looked up in the sky through the window on the other side of my bed. After gazing through my binoculars for awhile I saw a brightly lit object zoom through the sky at shooting-star speed. Unlike a shooting star it never burned out or dimmed, it just kept zooming throughout the twilight. Not sure if this is relevant but I live in d-town Riverside. Okay, the end.
