Have had many sightings in a 10 mile area photoes and video

My son and I were chasing lightning, and noticed a strange orange light to the east. We were approx 10 miles ne of langenburg. After watching for approx 3 mins, the orange orb seemed to split and kick out small white orbs, th1 would not move but would disappear and reappear.
After about 20 mins the white orbs went back into the orange orb and it went up into the thunder clouds... I have got video and photos of other sightings from this area... a lot of people have witnessed similar things in this 10 mile diameter...
The photos and video are not from this incident but from the same area...
