Orb-Like UFO Flies Haphazardly Amongst Firework Filled Sky

During a family event for the 4th of July, as my girlfriend records the fireworks from our patio...she catches what appears to be a ufo that is unsure which way to go. It zigs and zags around and appears to come down quite close to a local tree. She then films some fireworks and then goes back to that area and the ufo reappears briefly and takes off.
