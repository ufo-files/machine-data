I walked outside at 1700 on April 8, 2016 in Locust, NC. It was a sunny day with large bulky clouds and 70 degrees. Saw two buzzards circling and looked above them and noticed a thin white cigar shape quietly moving slowly between clouds. The direction was from north to south and lasted about 60 seconds before I lost visibility. Very strange because no sound was heard and no wings were seen. Usually a silver or metallic appearance is visible from a plane that low and wings should have been seen. If a pencil is held at arm length the eraser would provide an example of the size (width) of the shape seen this day.
