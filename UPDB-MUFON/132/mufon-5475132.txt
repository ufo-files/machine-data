UFO was hiding n moving with the clouds

I went out to my balcony, then there in the slow moving clouds looked like a saucer. I took a picture and it definitely looks like a UFO hiding in the clouds. This was on May 31st 2017. It lasted for about 15 minutes. There may have been another object in the cloud in front but I couldn't get a good enough picture of that cloud.
I noticed this because all the clouds were light in colour except these two clouds. I hesitated for a moment the ran inside for my phone to take a picture. I watch until the clouds moved out of my sight behind other buildings. I am sure it was a UFO.
