Ship with very bright green and yellow lights descended right over hwy and seemed to coast into fields and trees.

I was driving west on the interstate in a snowstorm, the roads were very bad, and saw very bright green and yellow lights coming from what appeared to be a craft with windows and human like movement within the circle or oval center. There were what appeared to be gray flat wings or saucer surrounding the carrier portion. My navigation showed 25 miles from the Maumee exit. I reached for my phone and knocked it down accidentally, so couldn't get a picture. The craft seemed to coast into a landing, or hovering, rolling into the field and what appeared to be trees. There was no way to stop because the roads where so bad. There was other traffic. I came out to where I thought it was a few days later and saw not traces.
