{nd} 3 hovering red spheres joined by a 4th one which seemed to glide in from the south

I opened the door to the patio to take my dog out and was looking down at her as I walked out and in my peripheral vision I immediately saw the largest of the three orbs. It was red like the brake lights of a car or like a stop light. There were two other orbs as well, smaller but still large size. None of them moved. There was no sound. Each had a large red aura around it. A fourth one then flew in from the south. It was white and seemed to glide in, first moving horizontally from the south to the north and then made a u-turn, then up to where the other three were. It's movement was very smooth and quick. It was a "I'm late but I made it!" type entry. When it stopped it was then red as well but it was the smallest of the four. They were there for a good 10 minutes. They were kind of low, like low cloud level. I took some pics with my cell phone but only white dots showed up. Then they started to fade out simultaneously until all four lights were gone. I didn't feel any fear at the time, just curiosity. I heard someone down the street yell "What is that?" so I think he saw it too.
