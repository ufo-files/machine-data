{cak} Overhead, daytime brightness grater than Venus

The expression “geosynchronous” may not even have been a word back then, but I knew enough to know that this was not normal because this “thing” was in a geosynchronous orbit, otherwise, it would not stay in the same place in the sky day after day. I finally got out my boy scout binoculars (which I have to this day) to get a better look at it. It was a shiny ring with a bar across its diameter.

After four or five days I saw two F-104 Starfighters take off from Westover Air Force Base in Chicopee, MA, fly over my house, circle around and execute a ninety degree climb towards the object. They soon disappeared from sight – I could not see them even with binoculars. After twenty minutes or so I saw them come back down and return to Westover. There were no reports in our local paper about the object or anywhere else that I could find. Just think, that event was visible to at least a hundred thousand adults in the immediate geographical area – no one reported it!
