Great Ufo Fleet in Clouds

It was years ago, in 1988 that I was working in Kastamonu, Turkey. While I was going to a eat something at a restaurant and as I have always been interested in nature and space, I immediately noticed the same style of clouds in the sky. There was no sun, it was a closed sky with clouds. All the sky was covered by such clouds all of which were the same shape as if they were measured and cut in the same shape. They were all square shaped, lines of the squares were softened like a pillow, in exactly same dimension going just side by side next to each other, as if they were offering an army parade, or as if I immediately thought "a Ufo parade". 

I showed them to the people around saying "Ohh.. Look at these clouds, how interesting they are... God... how can be such clouds ever..." to have a comment that they were "like Ufo" at least, but nobody confirmed my idea. 

I wanted to watch them all to the end, but because I thought people would think "This man is crazy", I went into restaurant to eat smt. :-(
