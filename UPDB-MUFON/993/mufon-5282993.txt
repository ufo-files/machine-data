Cheerio shaped orb

It was the evening I was out with two friends eating by the water and a really bright object in the sky caught our attention by appearing out of nowhere it popped out in the sky right in front of us as if someone had turned on the light in a room . The LAX airport is right in front of us so airplanes stand out right away. It slowly moved its way towards Long Beach which I believe was east to our location. This happens often in this area and I’ve always tried to make sense of it but this time this object was brighter than previous ones I’ve seen.
