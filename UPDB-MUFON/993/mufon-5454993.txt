Lightball UFO disappearing in its own residue energy cloud and appearing again

My husband and I went for a cigarette around 5 AM. We live in an apartment building in a town with population of 35k. 
First, we saw a line of lights coming from NW to SE. We observed about 15 satellite like lights, all flying at the same pace. I started to search for the answer online and found out we were looking at the SpaceX sattelites!
We then decided to have another cigarette and talk about the unusual satellite sightning when I noticed a bright lightball that appeared right above our building. I think it was flying rather high. It was brighter than Venus on the night sky. It looked like it was emitting its own light, because it was shining the whole time it was on the night sky, independently of the "satellite flare". We never saw anything this bright passing through the sky like this. My husband had phone with him so we recorded it. Firstly, we didn't see anything odd with the way it was flying, but on the second look, we noticed the light flickered. So we slowed down the video and zoomed in. Then we were able to see this UFO disappearing in something that looks like a residue energy cloud and then appearing againg a moment later.
