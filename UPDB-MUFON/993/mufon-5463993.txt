{cak} Orangish round lights sighted that came on dimmed another came one and a 3rd

On Nov.7 2018 I was driving on state route 23 south approaching South Bloomfield when I noticed a round ball of light out my driver side window it was about tree level and in the mild of a farm field drove past the farm market and saw another on or the sameone. I could not look for long I was in traffic. I did look at the sky in front of me and noted no clouds. I was approaching the 1st light in town and the light appeared again this time between the front windshield and driver's door area. When I approached the next light I asked my passenger if he thought it was a clear sky,he agreed it was clear sky. I told him what I had seen and the traffic light turned green and we moved slowly to the next light. We came to the 3rd traffic light we had 3 or 4 cars ahead of us. While talking about the light one appears across the street near the phone pole it lights up dims and a 2nd appeared the 1st is dimming out and a 3rd light comes on and it is clearly behind the phone pole at the cross sections. The are orange yellow white mix of color. Our traffic light turns green and we move forward and pull off in to a subdivision we get out the car I am looking to the west/ north and see nothing. 
We get back in the car head south again driving for about 6 minutes my passenger alerts me to the lights at the back of a farm field are the 3 lights at tree top level,heading westerly direction. We pull off again and the lights are gone.
To this day I have no idea of what the lights might be. I felt excited and in awe of the beauty of the color,I never felt any fear. The lights would dim out and reappear
