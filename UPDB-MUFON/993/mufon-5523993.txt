silver craft flying low near High Ridge Missouri

I was driving back from Big River Marina traveling down Hwy 30 when I noticed something odd looking in the sky. At first I though it was a street light but realized that there are no street lights where I was and it was too big. I kept watching it through the trees and when I came around the bend I got a better look at it through a clearing. I was silver shaped, oblong and flying fairly low. I thought it may have been a helicopter but it was traveling too fast. It went sideways and flew N/E very fast.
