I was visiting a friend , we were both in his garage , he went out side to urineate , 5 to 10 min passed . I got a little concerned why he was taking so long , so I walk to the side door of the garage and saw my friend standing there looking up , just staring up and not moving, so I walked out of the the garage to see what he was staring at , it was a object so big that my eyes couldn't see all of it . It was shaped like a shallow soup bowl ,the color was a dull silver metallic , little squares that could have been windows , a lot if lights some pulsing some not , I remember yellow , red , white and green lights . Then I looked down to see what my friend was doing , he was still staring up at the object, so I started to look up again at the object and at that moment it took of to the NE so fast it left light streaks like in the movies without any sound or wind. My friend must of been in shock I guess , he's still looking up , so I went back into the garage sat down , about 2 to 3 min latter my friend came back in , white as a ghost , didn't say anything till I told him I saw it to , he said he thought he was crazy , and we never talked about it again
