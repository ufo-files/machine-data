One large red light and smaller white light seen over Windsor, Ontario.

I was coming home from Leamington after Trick-or-treating(Our normal route was under construction), when I looked out the car window and saw 2 lights, one was red, and was on the left, and one was white, on the right, hovering over Windsor. I viewed it through at least 3/4ths of Plymouth Road, and for the last second or so I could see it, the lights had changed position. I lost sight of the object when I passed by some trees, and I noticed that the object had disappeared when I could see the position it should've been in. I can only assume that It took a path up, or towards the horizion(Either end), when I lost sight of it. Also, nothing else was in the area when I spotted it.
