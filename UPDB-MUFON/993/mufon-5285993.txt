Big Dipper Fell Apart!

Me and my friend were on a drive that we used to normally do. We stopped to check out the stars and the sky. My friend pointed out the Big Dipper; as we were looking at it, all the "stars" fell apart and were scattered just below where we originally saw it. Then as we were freaking out asking if we saw that, they all of a sudden go back into formation then all dissipated. Still to this day we have no idea what we witnessed... It looked exactly like the Big Dipper which makes us feel like were crazy but we both saw the same exact thing.
