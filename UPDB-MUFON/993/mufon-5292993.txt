Very large bright hovering object with fast erratic movements

[url moved/cms/rjl]

We can not rationalize what we saw. On July 19 at 4:30am as I was on my way to the restroom there was a very bright light coming from an odd shaped hovering object. My husband & daughter both said they could see a "U" in it along with "arms" that came down on the sides but met at the top forming a triangular shape. It looked like iridescent sparkling water was around it (this can be seen a little in video). There were 2 smaller ones spread out, one North & one West. I described the movement as quick swimming jolts or that of a humming bird. The smaller ones looked like dradle that the 4 sides opened & closed swimming in the sky. I first noticed this at 4:30 then they completely vanished about 6:30. The video is 30 min & we were able to download it to YouTube & I am included a sketch of what my husband & daughter observed for the shape.
