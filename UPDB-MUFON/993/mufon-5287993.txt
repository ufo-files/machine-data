Saw bright objects in sky that quickly changed direction and descended towards me

At about 10pm I took my dog out for a quick walk before bed when I noticed a strange moving light in the sky that appeared to split from another object it kind of fluttered it didn’t move in a straight line like airplanes or helicopters do then as I was watching it I noticed it changed direction and was descending towards me and my dog I reached for my phone intending to record it when I was overcome with fear as it was moving very fast and becoming brighter and headed in my direction I ran inside my house and locked the doors it took me about and hour to calm down and stop shaking from fear
