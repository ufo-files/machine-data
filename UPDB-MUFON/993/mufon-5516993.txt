{nd} Strange triangular formations

I was trying to see why my cellphone signal had completely lost service. Something told me to look east and I saw a strange triangle formation. The lights were not pulsing as regular military nor commercial aircraft. This area is common to have a lot of military crafts in the air, however the sky is clear of them tonight. Only these 3 strange orbs that rapidly pulse and then slow down. They kept changing from a triangular formation to a diagonal line. Every time I thought they were coming closer they would quickly move away east of my position. My cellphone, wireless internet and satelight television are also having disruptions.
