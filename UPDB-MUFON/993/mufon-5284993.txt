Unknown aerial phenomenon

A very, very, very large ball of different colored lights seen at angle over my house prior to complete darkness. At first glance, it appeared to be a star, however, zero were visible at that time. I live jogging distance from Austin Bergstrom Airport. No other planes or helicopters were in the air at that time (which was odd) and drones are not allowed in the area due to proximity of the airport.
