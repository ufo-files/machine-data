{cak} Fast in speed. Saucer like in appearance.

We were on a family vacation in south New Jersey. My 11 year old son was video taping the sun, from our balcony. Our rental home was next to a restaurant, playing live music, hence the loud music in video. The light green orb in the video was the reflection of the sun on the glass door. This is not the object in question. In the video, a black saucer like object came racing by in frame 15/16 seconds of the video length. If you stop the video at second 15, into 16, you will see it. My son saw the object as he was randomly taping. He felt amazed and excited. He came to get myself and husband after he saw it. He lost sight of the object after it sped away.
