{cak} Red/Orange glow 1-3 objects appear for 5 min then disappear over S. Phoenix

Have had multiple sitings of a red/orange glowing object in sky southwest of South Mountain Radio Tower for several weeks. 1-3 objects appear between 7:30 and 8:00 PM. They glow for no longer than 5 min then disappear. When they reappear they have shifted their location. This observations are made from Squaw Peak - mountain peak to the North of the sitting. On occasion, trails of smoke have been seen. No noise is noted.
