{jpg} At first considered that the light might be a helicopter, solid red, no strobe lights.

Looking out our back window I observed a large bright red light in the distance, was intrigued by this (as we are in coastal country NSW) - At first I considered the possibility that the light might be a hovering Navy helicopter (as Nirimba is near our location) or it might have been a police helicopter (though they are rare in our skies). 

I bought the light to the attention of my father and we both became intrigued by the lack of sound (on this clear cold night) as well as the absence of any strobe lights. I observed on the clock on our microwave oven in the near kitchen that it was 10:13PM. When I looked back at this very large bright red light, it began to descend slowly toward the state forest which lies beyond the Wool Road (St Georges basin) - in the direction of Huskisson. It appeared to lose brightness and size at it sank out of sight.

We began to ponder what it might have been, when a second (or possibly the same light) reappeared at around the same altitude and sat stationary as before. It sat in the air for approximately sixteen minutes - during this time, I phoned a relative in a near suburb (giving them some alarm, because of the hour) and asked them to go onto their veranda and look east to see if they could see the same thing. They were less than impressed at receiving the call so late in the night and came back from looking outdoors to report that they could see nothing for the smoke (as it is a cold night here and a lot of people have their wood fires burning). The second appearance of the red light disappeared (as though turned off) - no descent.
