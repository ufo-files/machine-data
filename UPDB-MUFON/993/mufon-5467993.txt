A UFO sighting close to the highway, looks like a menta ray with light beam under.

On July 24th 2017 at 14:00, we were driving on Hwy10 closed to Marieville near of Mt St-Gregoire, going south. What keep our attention was a beamlight in agricultural field besides the hwy. Then we saw a huge flying object with a laser or light beam analysing the soil at a distance of 1000 ft from the highway. The altitude of the object was around 100 ft above the ground. We cannot missed it. We were not alone to watch it, few cars stopped by to see the object. The shape looked like a menta ray with position lights at each end, the surface of the object was dark grey color in metal and under a turbine motor. The object moved very very slowly and then in one eye click, it flew away at highspeed and less in a second, it disappeared. In the front of it, we could see windows.
After the sighting, we feel very nervous and forgot to take pictures of the UFO.
