I observed a large Reddish Orange oval object hovering in the air just to the right side of the moon.  It was massive, pulsating and I took 3 pics.

October 2, 2020 at about 11:35pm I observed a massive Reddish Orange oval object in the air just to the upper right side of a full moon. It was pulsating, hovering and slightly moving from side to side.  I was able to take 3 quick pictures of the object with my camera phone, before I ran out of fear.  The object gave me an eerie feeling inside and I felt extremely distressed.  I ran into my apartment to gain my composure. Approximately 10 minutes later, I went back outside to see if the object was still there and it was gone. The flash on my camera phone reflects off the moon too the left but the object was so massive, you can still clearly see it to the above right side of the moon.
