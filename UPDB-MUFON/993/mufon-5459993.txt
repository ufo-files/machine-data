{tjd} Cloud or Light in a Tree at Night

This is two different events on the same day, but in different locations in my yard with two different game cams. I have the pictures in chronicle order, and the cams are set to take pictures every 30 minutes. The first phenomena is in the morning and the other at night. I have the before and after pictures to help orientate the phenomena. The one at 5:32 am looks like a cloud in a tree to me, but have never experienced this before. The other looks like an orb or light but looks too round to be a flying insect.
