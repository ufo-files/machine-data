(GS) Red/orange glowing orbs over I77 near Ft Jackson

Was Driving south on I77 near exit 9A/B and witnessed three glowing orbs (hovering) over the highway. They were formed in a triangular pattern with distance between (separate) them. I noticed a plane flying in the area to so it was definitely at a lower altitude. I merged on exit I kept watching, other cars were slowing down looking at them too. When I came off the exit I parked in a lot to see where they were and only saw one from my view which began to move in a West until out of view.
