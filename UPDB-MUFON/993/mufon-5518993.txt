i lay on the beach about a mile from my apartment, and i watched, as this shapeshifting cluster of what i initially assumed were balloons, traverse across the sky, and fade off into the distance. the moved one on top of the other, and rotated in the air. they seemed to reflect sunlight and be white as snow. the moved in a northwesterly direction until floating out of sight. they moved slowly like balloons floating through the air, but it changed its shape constantly and seemed like a very large array of very large balloons, if balloons they were..i am starting to doubt that they were balloons, given their apparent size and distance from me. they would have had to have been quite large. i also have never seen balloons roll one on top of the other giving the appearance of changing shape. but thats what this object or objects did.
