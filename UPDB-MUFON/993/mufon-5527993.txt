round orange lights

My husband noticed 3 orange lights in the distance, above the horizon, moving in strange back and forth patterns. I saw them too. About 2 miles away? Then they all hovered for a few seconds. One of the orange lights started moving straight up as if in a line, until it disappeared. The other two lights stayed low on the horizon and when the 1st vanished, the 2nd light ascended, following the path of the first straight up into the sky. then the 3rd followed, exactly the same path. It took about 1.5-2 minutes for each to ascend completely out of view. Then we walked another block and saw a 4th orange light hover, waver back and forth, then ascend in a straight line. We were excited and curious. Not scared.
