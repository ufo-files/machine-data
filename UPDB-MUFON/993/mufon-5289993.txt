We started calling them sky darts.

3 unknown objects. Observed objects with the naked eye and NVG (night vision goggles)

The objects would cover the entire visible night sky in seconds. Objects were flying at high speed, high altitude independently, and formation flight.

The objects were witnessed by ten or more personnel. They flew around traversing the night sky for over an hour. 

My occupation in the Army (MOS 13M) during the time frame in question was rocket and missile artillery. Earlier in the day and early evening, my unit was shooting short-range (14 kilometers) inherit rockets.

I come from a military family and my father worked for the Department of Defense. I grew up in Pensacola Fl. As a teenager, I volunteered at a small airport in Milton Fl. washing planes and refueling them so I could get free rides. I was also a member of the Civil Air Patrol. I as far back as can remember prided myself with military aircraft identification.

In hindsight, without evidence, I concluded the objects may have been F-22 Raptors. During the time of the sighting, they were in testing.
