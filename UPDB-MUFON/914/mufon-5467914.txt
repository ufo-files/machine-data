I saw, through binoculars, an object in the sky that was bright white & looked like a Ferris wheel. It was round & had spokes on it. It was brighter than any star & stationary in the southern sky. Then it would suddenly change form & become smaller pulsating lights of green, yellow, blue & red that would zip around erratically about the sky at impossible speeds, then it would return to appear as the original structure. I counted at least 20 of these objects in the sky, but all of the others were further away & these objects were seen everywhere except for in the Western sky. Does anyone have any idea what these things are? These objects have been seen by many people other than myself. I am going to view them through my telescope tonight.
