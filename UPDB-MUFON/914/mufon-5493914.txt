Fiery light

Noticed a randomly-flickering orange light which appeared to be a flare, or fire source, just under cloud layer. Originally thought it may have been a firework/meteorite. Was moving in a slow, straight path from SE to NE of my position. Flickering orange light went out abruptly and a black disc became very plainly visible in its place, against the lighter cloud cover. Disc continued on a straight path to the NE of my position. Made no noise. Flickering light did not return. Flickering light was visible for first 30sec - 1min30sec. Disc was visible for another 5min, then was too far away to notice. Lost sight of object over Lake Ontario, NE of Hamilton.
