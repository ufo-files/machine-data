5 white spheres in perfect straight line from SW to NE no sound - approx. 20,000' moving very fast

May 23, 2021 @ 2240 I witness 5 white spheres traveling from SW to NE at approx. 67 degrees. These white spheres made no sound and cast no light outside of the sphere. The area I saw them - the west and north sky was clear with cloud cover approaching slowly from the SE. Cloud cover elevation was approx. 20000 feet according to Epley airport. To my left is a Walnut tree that I saw the spheres passing behind the tree. The right side of the tree to the beginning of the approaching cloud cover left a view of 10 to 2 (using a clock face) These spheres traveled in a perfect straight line and all 5 were the same size. I started counting seconds when the last sphere cleared the tree to when it went behind the clouds and was approx. 8 seconds. I have 21 yrs of military experience and have never seen objects like this / travel at that speed. These were not Starlink Satellites that are out of our atmosphere -- these were in our atmosphere. This sighting lasted approx.15 seconds with 8 seconds of clear viewing.
