A small object in the sky emitting it's own light

I was walking my dog first thing in the morning and looked up in the sky to see a stationary object hovering in the air, at an unknown altitude, giving off it's own light. There was no appearance of a glint as if to give the impression it was reflective but gave off it's own glow. It appeared to have a hollow/dark center, but I could barely catch a glimpse of it. I brought my husband out and was able to catch 4 videos total of the object on my phone's camera. It was difficult to get a still shot zooming in from the range it was at, but I went in and out a couple times and it never moved. The object stayed in the air for at least an hour. It's unknown when the object appeared in the sky or when it actually left, but it did not move from that location visibly the whole hour I was in and out observing it. Four neighbors also saw it, but I do not have their addresses or names. They live in the neighborhood though.
