Myself, and two family members were at the backyard of the house about 11:07 pm, there was no clouds, just a clear sky. We had a fire pit that we all stood around, then I looked up and noticed a gray object shaped as a boomerang with dim lights underneath the belly of the craft. When I first noticed the flying object I thought it was strange there no noise like jet engines or propellers powering this object. When I first saw it I didn't know what to think, but my first thought was its shape. It was a shape of a boomerang and it was moving very quietly to the south, about 150 yards above us. Our feeling were confused we didn't know what we just saw and we freaked out about it, but we all agreed about its shape of a boomerang. It was moving south for about three minutes and finally in a blink of an eye it was gone into the sky. That's when we knew we encountered an UFO sighting.
