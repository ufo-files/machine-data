{kt}NOT ONE SINGLE EVENT. MANY OVER A DECADE WITH MULTIPLE WITNESSES

OUT OF SEVERAL EVENTS OVER MANY YEARS, THE ONE THAT BOTHERS ME TO THIS DAY STARTED OUT AS AN EVENING OF SPOTLIGHTING TO COUNT SPECIES OF NOCTURNAL CREATURES. MY PARTNER AND I HAD JUST RETURNED TO OUR COMPOUND AND WERE TALKING QUIETLY. THERE WAS A LITTLE LIGHT COMING IN FROM THE COMPOUND MERCURY VAPOR LIGHTS THROUGH THE FRONT OF THE BUILDING. ALL OF A SUDDEN THERE WAS LIGHT SO BRIGHT WE WERE BLINDED. ALL I CAN REMEMBER SEEING WAS THE INSIDE OF THE BUILDING LIT LIKE THE SUMMER SUN HAD BEEN DROPPED IN THE ROOM. THERE WAS NO SOUND, BUT A LOW VIBRATION WAS FELT BY BOTH OF US. THIS LASTED A FEW MINUTES THEN THE LIGHT JUST CUT OUT, LEAVING US BLIND. ALL THE LIGHTS IN THE COMPOUND ARE AUTO DUSK TILL DAWN AND NOT ONLY WERE THEY OUT BUT ALL THE LIGHTS IN AN AREA SPANNING 3/4 OF A MILE HAD THERE SENSORS SHUT BY THE BRIGHTNESS. WE HEARD NO SOUNDS OF JETS, HELICOPTERS OR ANY THING THAT COULD HAVE HOVERED OVER THE COMPOUND. AND SEEING AS A PORTION OF THE INDIVIDUALS ARE MILITARY PILOTS WE WOULD HAVE KNOWN IF IT WAS A MILITARY AIRCRAFT FLYING OVER A CLOSED SPACE.
