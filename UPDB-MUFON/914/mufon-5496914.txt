{nd} Roundish object hovered quietly, then 2 others appeared, one shot horizontally to the first object, then all 3 disappeared.

Woke up and peered out of the window next to my bed to look for birds as is my habit. It was early in the morning at first light; not really light outside but not dark either. I saw an object with flickering lights red, white, and green hovering over the tree line across the little valley looking toward Central Avenue. I thought it was a helicopter so didn't pay it much mind. But as I looked for birds, owls or hawks or anything, I noticed the thing did not move. I thought that was strange. If it was a helicopter I thought I would hear it so I cracked open the window to see if I could hear anything. There was no noise at all. It just stayed there, and I thought it strange since helicopters make noise and usually don't hover like that, plus I didn't see a tail. Then I saw two other objects maybe smaller than the first one. They were to the left (the east side) of the first object. One of the objects darted in a straight line horizontally to the first object. Then I grabbed my binoculars to see if I could see it better, but the screen was in the way. (It is a normal window and screen but when you put binoculars next to it the screen blurs the view. I could see the lights on the objects as if looking out of a window normally.) When I looked up from the window the things were gone. A moment later my husband came in and I told him what I saw. He was not in the room at the time, and when he came to the window the objects were already gone. I wished I had my camera at the time.
