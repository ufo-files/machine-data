{cak} Second UFO sighting in 2 weeks.

Spotted multiple objects again after my last report on October 29th 2014. This time again on Saturday evening around 9pm November 8th in my bedroom, windows wide open, perfectly clear night looked out and observed bright glowing red and white lights hovering up above the south west Novato/west marin mountains. Object hovered then ascended finally taking a straight path from the south west toward the south east. Stopping and hovering over Mt Burdel open space area. Objects appeared from the west and traveled over toward the first object hovering for a few min then blinking out or descending below or behind the mountain. Lights appeared on the actual Mt burdel open space area, first starting off as a bright white light intensifying into 2 separate lights then back into 1 then turning bright red. The lights seemed to dance upon the hill moving erratically changing direction abruptly and very strangely. This time I can hear other witnesses in the valley reacting to the lights in the sky. I specifically overheard someone saying wow look at that, wow there is another one. Shortly after that a jet did appear from the North west headed south looked like a big wing, large triangle or stealth plane? It was very different from commercial aircraft. There were horse ranchers on the hill at the exact same time I witnessed the lights as they must have noticed them as well. I have spoken with Marin Sherrifs Deputy and Marin open space park ranger who were both dispatched out on the Mt Burdel open spaced trail head the next morning. They did not mention receiving any reports however mentioned to me that there were ranchers out on the hill looking for a lost horse. Im attempting to contact the ranchers to question if they noticed the strange objects/lights. Something very strange going on over here in North Novato area. No idea what these thing are?
