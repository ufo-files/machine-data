{rjl} Pulsating array of 12 lights shaped like an arrow going S to N

I was in my backyard in Pismo Beach looking up at the sky about 9:55pm on Friday, April 21. 

I witnessed a group of about 12 lights shaped like an arrow ( -> ) pulsate about six times across the sky heading from South to North. It went from one horizon to the other. The individual lights were about four times as large as a typical star appears, but half as bright.

It appeared to be a light projection from something very high up in space to cast such a large and wide path without distortion to the arrow shape. The entire event and pulsation across the sky happened in under 5 seconds.
