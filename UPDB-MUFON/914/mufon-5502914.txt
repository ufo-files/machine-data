{nd} several orange lights over the ocean and then a stealth like craft flying above our heads

I was on vacation in Myrtle Beach, SC laying on the bed watching a movie when I noticed a bright orange light outside my window over the ocean. I was staying on the beach so my view was nothing but the ocean. I looked at the light for a few seconds and it turned into 3 bright orange/red lights. I immediately ran outside on the balcony to watch it more. It quickly disappeared but then more lights appeared shortly after in a triangular pattern. The lights would shift into several lights and then back into 1 before vanishing. This happened for about an hour. At first I was just looking straight out over the ocean looking for the orange/red lights but for some reason I looked up into the sky right above me and I saw a huge stealth like craft. It was a fluke that I actually even saw it cause it was almost invisible but I could make out the shape and lights on the bottom of the craft. At first it moved slowly and then in a matter of seconds it disappeared. It was shaped like a long boomerang with tons of lights on the bottom of it. I couldn't believe how close it was! After that the lights only appeared one more time and I caught video of it that time. I stayed up for hours though just watching the sky and noticed about 3 hours later a blue light in the sky that after a few seconds turned into 2 orange/red lights and just hovered over the water before disappearing.
