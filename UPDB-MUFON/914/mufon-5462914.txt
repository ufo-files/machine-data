{jpg } Green discs and continuous flashing white pinpoint lights

On this evening, I was in the kitchen and noticed flashes of light coming through my windows. I went out in the backyard and observed thousands of tiny bright white lights flashing throughout the sky. I grabbed my phone just in time to catch a few of the green disc-shaped objects with different flight patterns occasionally appearing in the middle of the white lights. They appeared to come closer and then retreat. This event lasted about 15 minutes and then abruptly stopped. Attached is the video I took. The bright white lights are easily viewed if the contrast is increased.
