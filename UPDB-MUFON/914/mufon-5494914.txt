{nd} I saw a very bright light go straight up and come straight down. It was a brilliant violet purple.

I was headed to the sushi restaurant "Umenoki " on 98th and Washington st. In Portland oregon. It was my meal break , so I was in a hurry . I pulled in the 98th st entrance and turned north towards the restaurant. Right before I pulled into my parking spot , I saw a very beautiful violet purple light shoot up into the sky and then fall back down. Similar to a firework , however it appeared to be more solid than not. I wish I could describe the color better , it was very bright and very beautiful . I couldn't tell how far up it went , I kept watching from the restaurant window but didn't see it again. I then finished eating and went back to work. The weather was a bit weird , I heard there was a funnel cloud the day before to the south of us. The sky was really dark towards the top and more "normal" night lighting below that. I thought it was very cool, I'm not sure what it was . My first thought was it was a new type of light beam they use to get attention to parking lots and such. However it didn't happen again , so I guess that is not what it was.
