{rjl} hovered then vanished

I was in Niagara Falls new York looking at the falls and the lighted buildings on Canada side. I first noticed it as a dull light in the sky, I assumed it was a building reflecting light. It was a purple light with a purple haze around it and a light being projected from the top. My reaction was confusion because it was no longer there. I lost sight when I looked at my friends to point it out to them and when i looked back it was gone
