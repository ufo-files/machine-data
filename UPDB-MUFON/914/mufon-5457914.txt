{rjl} Two very bright lights looked to break off from aircraft

I went out on my back porch while my son when to walk his dog. Looking westward, I noticed what appeared to be a very large and long aircraft which I thought to be an unusual plane. 
A brightly lit object flew from the end of it, and I feared that an engine or other part had broken off and there would be a crash.
I quickly flipped my phone to video record, the plane was no longer visible though it had only been seconds, but a second equally sized bright object had appeared near the original one. 
Both objects moved independently of each other, moving horizontally, crossing paths in opposite directions at one point, and at times appearing to have a short comet-like tail. One object vanished, then reappeared shortly after, and both zigzagged across the sky until they went below my line of sight.
