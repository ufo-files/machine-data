{rjl} Black triangular craft white lights flying low and slow

It was the night of July 10-15 in 2010 between 1am and 2. My boyfriend and I were on my back porch talking until he had to leave and I remained by myself. My backyard was fenced in and beyond that was owned farmland with cattle and a big pond.I sit out there regularly to enjoy the veiw.As Im staring at the stars I notice movement from the corner of my eyes and there it is. It appeared to have rose from a fog above the pond and heading straight at me.Moving less than 5 mph and very ominous.I was frozen. I could see white lights on the bottom and hear almost nothing. To this day I cant clearly explain what it sounded like. As if high and low pitches were being emitted continuously but at a very low volume. As it approached approximately 10-20 ft. directly over my head the pattern of the lights became noticable. Around 10 circular white lights in total. 4 very big lights formed horizontally down the middle of the craft and smaller ones on both sides toward the bottom. The craft was almost as big as our one story ranch house. It never stopped or changed speed.When it was no longer in veiw it was heading across the street toward a barnhouse. Immediately I went inside to get my phone and call my boyfriend. To my knowledge nobody else saw it that night and nothing came from it. It was a creepy experience but bearable.I didnt report it until eight years later because I never knew about these sitings.I cant beleive how many cases are similar to this and been happening for so long.
