My husband and I were having a nice bon fire when I noticed a light in the sky that was moving in a way I had never seen before. As I was in the military and did security for air fields I have seen many different kinds of air craft at night and can almost tell you what each are before they land just from the light placement on them. After noticing the odd light I pointed it out to my husband who was amazed at how it was moving, it was not like anything we had seen before with it's unusual flight path bobbing up and down and side to side at a rather quick pace. We watched it do this for around 6 minutes when what looked like 2 jets moved into the area and 1 Blackhawk. As these air craft moved into the area the light in the sky took off to the south east a great speed with the jets following behind and the Blackhawk looked like it was searching the area flying low... just above the tree tops.
