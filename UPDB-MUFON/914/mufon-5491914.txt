{dr}Witnessed multiple orange orbs during and after 4th of July fireworks.

While watching the last 15 minutes of the 4th of July fireworks from my driveway I saw an orangish red glowing orb slowly moving from the Northeast to the Southwest between the tree line. I pointed it out to my wife and she wasn't sure what it was. A few minutes another orb slowly traveled the same path between the tree line. After the fireworks were over we started to cleaning up our chairs and bringing them in the house. As I was in my house my wife bursts through the door and yelled " Come quick and take a look at this". As me, my sister, brother-in-law and wife ran outside I could see another orb moving across the tree line path. When my brother-in law reached for his phone to take a picture the orb moved fast to the west and disappeared from sight. I asked my wife what she saw and see said she saw 4 orangish orbs moving across the tree line. She was not sure how they were moving so close together.
