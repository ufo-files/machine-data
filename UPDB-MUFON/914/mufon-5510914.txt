Large triangle hovering over house (cant find GMT for time zone)

When i was about 11-12 years in 2001/02 cant remember the year my mother had a bad fall and we went to the hospital. At around midnight we got out of the taxi at our house and I it was a clear night as I remember. Being the only other person in I decided to sit and listen the radio mains powered with headphones on in my room. It must have been past midnight or 1.00am. Suddenly the radio station I was listening to disappeared like a hoover or microwave had been switched on next to me. I had the urge to run to my bedroom window I still don't know why I did this perhaps because I thought a helicopter might be passing over. I then looked up and seen the start of a low flying triangle passing at low altitude I don't remember hearing any noise. It had 3 big white lights at each tip and a larger pulsating red one in the middle of it. I watched it glide over other peoples homes before it west off into the distance while all I could see was the flashing red light in the far distance. I knew this was not a type of winged aircraft as It would have been dangerously low and the airspeed would have stalled the plane. It was too big to be a helicopter and considering they make noise what I seen didn't.
