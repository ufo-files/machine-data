lights across field

I saw thee headlights coming across the field. I though it was an airplane. It stopped by a clump of trees leaning to the left. The third ball of light shot up into the sky. It came back down level towards the ground. The colors were of sunset colors. And there was no water nearby. The middle light blinked, then the left one blinked. The last one blinked. It went straight up by the tree lines. As it was going upwards, it broke the branches on the tree. I went over to look at the tree and sure enough the branches were broken in an upwards position. And I havent seen anything since.
