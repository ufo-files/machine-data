Appeared seemingly out of thin air and then 'flew' over us, seconds later it disappeared (once again into thing air).

Laying on our backs stargazing and we see what we think is a streak (like a shooting star) appear out of no where. We see it come towards us, getting bigger as we watch it, and as soon as it seems to be coming right towards us it goes over our heads, we turned to continue watching it and it disappeared into the night. It didn't look like it faded or it didn't even diminish in size, it was the same size but just disappeared. 

The streak of light was a solid line, we could see the shadow/outline of a vessel with the light. 

The vessel was under the clouds the whole time and stayed the same size the whole time we could see it. It appeared to be at an angle moving straight forward. It crossed maybe 3/4 of the sky.
