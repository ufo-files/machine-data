8 red lights in a wedge formation

I was in my practice room at SUNY Purchase, when I notice out the windows(about a 45 degree angle from where I was sitting) 8 red/orange lights in a wedge/boomerang formation. They were moving slowly across the sky. I was reaching for my phone to take a video while keeping my eyes stuck on the lights. Before I could snap a video the lights dispersed in several directions, while seemingly spiraling away from each other. They then rapidly disappeared, the lights fading away. The sighting was at the peak of golden hour at sunset. It was clear blue skies.

I have provided a very rudimentary sketch of the formation shape, as well as a picture of the windows in the room to give an idea of my view.
