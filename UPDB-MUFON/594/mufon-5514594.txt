{dr}watched dozens of red lights traveling due south no sound no other lights they passed one at a time for over an hour

My daughter was almost 9 years old, and my wife was making a pinata in the front yard for her party that was planned for the following weekend. I was indoors watching TV when my wife called me outside. When I went outside i saw my wife looking up at the night sky and pointing at something. she then said that she had been watching alot of airplanes going across the sky and wanted to know why they were traveling like they were. I then looked up and saw for myself what seemed to me to be airplanes with only one marker light turned on; a red light. The lights were anywhere from 30 seconds to 2 minutes apart, but from the time i went outside to the last one i saw there were more than 35 that i counted. i do not know how many my wife saw and how many went by before she even noticed them. 
I am not easily convinced that these were anything other than airplanes, and i strongly suspect that they were possibly military in origin, ie a training flight for the local air guard or something similar.
With this in mind, i went inside, and got my binoculars to try and see the lights better. it was relatively easy to see the lights with my binoculars, but even at full magnification i was unable to tell any details at all other than it was a red, roughly spherical light that traveled across the sky over my house from north of me, to south of me at a rate not unlike commercial airplanes that ive seen. the oddest thing was that the lights/airplanes/whatever they were seemed to be completely silent.
