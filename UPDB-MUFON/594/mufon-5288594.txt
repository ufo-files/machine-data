12 or so balls of yellow light with a cylindrical object below.

Went out in the backyard between 9:30 and 10:00 pm to call my dog in. Observed yellow balls of light about the size of tennis balls that looked like they were on fire shooting up into the sky. Ran in the house and got my sister and my cell phone. Started videoing. Thought it might be Chinese lanterns or something but the balls made a square pattern and then one would go out and another would shoot up and join the others and make a triangle. Then one would fade off and go out and it would make a bigger arrow shape. When I uploaded the video to my desktop where I had a bigger screen, there is a cylindrical or barrel shaped object down below that was not visible with the naked eye.  Everyone is trying to tell me that these were starlink satellites but I don't believe it. They did not look or launch in the straight line of lights that you associate with those . Just looking for anyone who knows what this might be and if anything like it has been seen before.
