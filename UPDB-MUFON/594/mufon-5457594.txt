{cak} Lights like NC

I was working in the yard and I had heard a plane so I looked to the skies and when I turned to the east I noticed oddly spaced lights. No real pattern and I couldn't constantly see all the lights, as if certain ones were to turn on and off randomly. It was a good distance away, no clouds and my camera couldn't really pick it up at all. I had my husband come see and when he went inside to get the binoculars a jet took off from the direction of white sands missile range towards the lights. The lights then went more into the distance making nearly impossible to see. It was as if there was a few glittery spots in the sky after that. After that there was more air traffic and they were gone. I looked online to see if I could understand it more and came across a video from NC out in the water of the exact same thing. I confirmed with my husband to see if that's how he remembered them, he did. Again, my photo doesnt pick up much, you can see a couple of the lights in the jet smoke.
