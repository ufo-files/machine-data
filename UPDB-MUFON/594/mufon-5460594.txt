{tjd} Line of approx. 30 lights.

My friend and I were looking through binoculars from my back yard tonight May 26 and we saw approx, 30 separate lights flying in a straight line from SW to NE at an incredible rate of speed. I have about a 100 degree viewing radius vantage point and they traveled overhead and were completely out of sight in less than 2minutes possibly less time.
It was a straight line, some were brighter lights than others. They stayed in formation at all times. Absolutely no noise. They were configured as 3or 4 brighter lights then several dimmer lights and then brighter again to the end but there was no organization as to number of brighter versus dimmer. It went by so fast that it was impossible to count them by lumination parameters. It gave the impression the the brighter were larger craft. As I said there were at least 30 in the formation of a straight line. It could have been a greater number.
It was too fast to get a photo.
They flew directly over Albuquerque, NM high in the sky.
