Two star-type lights harrying a military jet at close range.

I was sitting in my car on Racecourse Road in York (with the engine off). The sound of a jet engine had been coming and going in this area of York for about 10 to 15 minutes, which is not especially uncommon, and seems to stem from military exercises in this part of North Yorkshire. I looked up, and a jet aircraft was about to pass overhead, coming from about the direction of the Mount and over the racecourse and/or racecourse stands, heading due south. It was too dark to see the body, but based on the sound it was a military jet. The sky was clear and dark, and stars were visible, and the lights on Racecourse Road were (unusually) turned off, which made the sky far clearer than normal. Two lights, both star-like in terms of size and colour, were flying along near to the jet, and taking erratic courses in the air. One was a lot nearer to the jet than the other. It was moving in a zig-zag manner, and kept approaching the jet and backing off. The closest it got seemed to be approximately one aircraft's length away. The movements of this light included a sudden reversal back along its own trajectory, back towards the jet. The other accompanying light was on the other side of the jet. This was also moving in an unpredictable pattern but was staying at a greater distance. I went to attract the attention of a family member who was near the car but who hadn't looked up. By the time we both looked up together the accompanying lights had gone, and the jet was simply continuing south, still on its original course.
