Two events, two days apart.

The first event occurred Friday at 153 AM (pictures provided). My husband went to the store and called to tell me something strange was hovering over the local 7/11. I went outside and I seen an red colored orb hovering in an odd spot in the sky (see pictures). It was cold so once my husband came home we watched for a while and went inside. 

We live on the space coast so we are used to rockets and air traffic. The next event, however, can not be written off as an airplane or other known aircraft . I’ve believe this was a UFO, as we have been witness to other events in other locations previously. Unfortunately it all happened so fast, I was not able to obtain any footage. 

Sunday night sometime between 1030-1115 my husband took a trip to the store. When he came home he ran inside and said quick come outside! I already knew it had to be another event and something inside me just knew if I went to the room to get my phone I would miss it. I ran outside and flying above us from the south was red-orange fireball. It looked like Friday’s event but larger, closer, and moving quickly and silently. 
The object passed over our heads to the north then became to elevate and veer off toward the beach east . The object disappeared once reaching a higher elevation, re appeared for a brief moment seeking to continue to fly north rather than east, then disappeared completely. When the object first flew over us, the distance could be compared to a mid-low flying plane. Relative to its elevation, its size was larger than a basketball and must have been quite large. It did not emit any sound nor did it have a trail behind it like a comet. It was one bright red-orange glowing ball/orb . It quickly flew to the n/ne before dissipating.
