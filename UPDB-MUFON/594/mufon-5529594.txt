Bright light/boomerang light in sky

I was traveling eastbound on Morse Road in Columbus, OH when I saw a very bright light ahead of us near the cloud line. I mentioned it to my husband and just a few moments later it changed into a boomerang shape, and then vanished from sight. I would guess that the entire incident lasted less than a minute, and I'm very curious if anybody else noticed this. The light never appeared to move or travel, it stayed stationary, changed shape and then disappeared.
