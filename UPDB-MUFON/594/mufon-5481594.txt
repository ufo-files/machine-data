{cak} Stopped at crossroads red light, glanced right, spotted UFO north, took phone picture, noticed a second in the shot, light changed, we left.

My son and I were running an errand. He was driving. I sat in the passenger side front seat. We stopped at a stoplight at a crossroads at Cornhusker and 36th. I glanced right and noticed a sphere moving smoothly and steadily eastward. It was pretty big and moving, and reflective, which is why I notied it. I examined it to see if it might be a plane, but that was not possible. I wondered if it might be a balloon but it was moving in too smooth a fashion and struck me as mechanical. I thought "drone" but saw no evidence of propeller or propulsion beyond what seemed a slight heat shimmer behind the object. I grabbed my iPhone 6 and took a shot or two as I told my son to look. He saw what I saw. We understood we were seeing a UFO of some kind but neither of us was especially upset or excited. We took it matter-of-factly and discussed it minimally. We had no clue what they could be. We saw that there were two objects moving in a steady, smooth way eastward but the following object seemed to adjust position, lowering itself, then speeding slightly, until their speeds once again synced up. When the light changed, we turned left and drove away from the objects.
