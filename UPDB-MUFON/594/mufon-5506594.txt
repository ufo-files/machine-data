{kt}Two orange orbs

Just pulled into work when I noticed a glowing orb type object moving slowly and silently toward the Gulf of Mexico. I work at a beach hotel. I ran up to the top of the garage, three stories high, and watched it continue moving out over the water until it disappeared. Then as turned to go into work, facing south east, I noticed another orange light moving the same way but it seemed to be coming from a different trajectory, but heading in the same direction as the other one until it too disappeared over the Gulf of Mexico.
