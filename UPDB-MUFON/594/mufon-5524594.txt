translucent orb simular color to sunset,although sun had set 5 to 10 minutes earlier

I was at a friends house waiting outside,I don't know why I looked to the west about 5 to 10 minutes after sunset.There was still plenty of light but the sun had set behind the trees a few minutes earlier and all of a sudden I saw an orange translucent orb(similar to setting sun but very much smaller) about tree top high going from north to south.It went behind trees and back into view and kept going at the same hight and speed.I was wondering in my mind what could it be,a mylar balloon,a large bubble of some type,I just could not figure it out,I was just so stunned.I watched it for several seconds,there was no traffic at all at the time in fact there was no sounds at all,no kids no dogs,no birds not even insects it's as if everything stopped for a few seconds,no sound at all,and no sound from the object,and no wind to push it if it was a balloon or bubble.A minute or two later my friend's sister came home from work and I asked her if she saw it,she looked at me funny and said nooo!I guess she thought I was crazy so I never told anyone else about it.I now carry a digital camera with me in hopes of another sighting.
