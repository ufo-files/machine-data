Standing on the porch watching the strange lightening (3 strikes at the same spot one after the other in rapid succession) I recorded the lightening. When reviewing the video saw the object appear from behind a tree that would be over Lake Texoma and shoot off to the north at an unknown speed out if the frame. I was shocked to have it on video but not surprised. This is the first time caught (purly by accident) on video but me and my family have had many sightings from large red orbs to shiny objects. We also hear sounds that I can only describe as the "senaca guns" (like large booms)Interesting point- the old Perrin Airforce base is about 15 miles from my house. There is talk of things going on there. They "train pilots" is whats said. I'm including the video I took from my cell and a screen shot of the object when paused at approximately 11 seconds into the video.Strange things happen out here. I live at the North end of the peninsula.
