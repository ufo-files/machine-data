{jd} fog light rain, clouds. Hovering object that is not of any known type

Approximately 9:40 I was traveling north on US131. I saw what appeared to be a very bright star to the north. As I got closer, it was apparent this is not an aircraft I have ever seen. 1. No flashing lights. 2. Row of 4 square intensely bright white lights close together in a row straight across. the free way curves around, but I was easily able to see it was not moving. I estimate altitude under 1 mile, approximately 1 mile east of US131 between Sand Lake and Pierson Exit. There is no military location, nor any item of significance to explain possible drone work, blimp etc. No outline was able to be seen due to foggy mist. lost view as object was on passenger side of vehicle. Passenger was completely enthralled. Claimed as we went past it at a range of maybe 1 mile, there was also an orange square window much dimmer. far too high to be a new tower, and there was plainly nothing under it. 2 Adult eyewitnesses. Lights appeared to be between 50 and 100 feet square.
