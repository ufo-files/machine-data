Hovering shining diamond shaped pear

It was shot today, 4, June, 2020. It shows a what looked like a silent, silver, vertical cylindrical object that is fatter in the middle and the base more narrow than the top. In person, it shined like it was made of chrome. It floated in one spot for about 15 minutes barely moving. No arms like a drone, no sound. The wind was blowing so not a balloon. Took photos, my turn to go into the store (cv19 rules), came out and it was gone. 12 other people saw it but none saw the object leave. It wasn't until tonight when I saw the second object in the corner of the 2and photo.
