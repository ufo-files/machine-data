{nd} Huge V shaped with at least 3 red orange lights. Flying north.

After working a late shift. I was at home getting ready to go to sleep at 2:15 am. I took the dogs outside to do their thing while having a smoke i suddenly looked directly up over my house and saw A classic V shaped UFO flying north in a straight line much like the stealth bomber would when doing fly overs for Air Force Games . But much bigger.It was huge about the size of a neighborhood block. I could make out a outline of a V with at least 3 big orange/red lights on the rear. no flashing lights. Totally silent. It glided North towards the Air Force Academy. Before it was totally gone from view it looked liked it just popped out of sight. It was the most amazing thing I have ever seen.
