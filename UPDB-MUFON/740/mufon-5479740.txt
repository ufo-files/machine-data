{nd} I saw 5 lights then 4 lights traveling from the West headed East. Not in a solid straight path, they mover around eachother. Then after 5 min they headed up and disappeared.

At 10pm, I was driving home from work. I turned down the street from my home and noticed an odd light in the sky (South of my home). I really couldn't get a good look because I was driving so I made a right turn so I could pull over and take a good look. When I turned the next corner I noticed 5 lights in the sky. I could tell they were not from a plane or helicopter. I parked and watched the lights move around. Headed in the East direction from the West. I don't remember seeing 1 of the lights disappear but I could only see 4 lights by the time I pulled out my phone to take a pic. The lights were moving slowly and not in a straight path. The light's moved around eachother. This lasted about 5 minutes. Then they headed up and disappeared, maybe into the clouds. I don't know how long these lights were in plain view, for I only noticed them when I turned the corner to look at the other light I noticed south of them.
