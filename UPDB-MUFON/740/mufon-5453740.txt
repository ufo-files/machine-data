{cak} Approximately 30+ objects passed over our house, in formation(s), for about 1/2 hour

We live in the country about 15 miles from town. Around 10 pm a friend from town called and told us to go outside and look to the west because they were seeing 'objects' in the sky south of town (our area).  The night was dark (moonless) and clear of cloud cover. I first noticed what I thought was a satellite passing over our house - white light, right speed. My wife immediately noticed another one right in line behind the first one. Then another one. And another one. All together, more than 30 of these passed right over our house. They kept the same approximate distance from each other - except that two passed over us right next to each other - almost like they were connected. All seemed to be going the same speed. Most of them passed over us within a 15 minute period, but it took another 15 minutes for all the last stragglers to pass over. Some of the first ones (6 or 8 of them) were on parallel paths, but all moving the same direction, west to east. The remainder that followed the first ones, followed the same linear path, one after another. They were definitely NOT airplanes. More like watching a formation of 30+ satellites. While our cell phones could photograph and take video of the stars, neither one of us could get pictures or video of the objects that passed over.
