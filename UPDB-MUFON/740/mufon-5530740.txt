Slowly moving over tree tops.

shortly before sunset that evening i was picking up my son from his friends house when i noticed a orange sphere moving slowly acouple hundred feet over the trees.I slowed down to observe what i thought was a helicopter moving south.Since there is a marine logistics base near us my first thought was it was military,but i quickly saw that it wasn't.It was absolutly silent and had no real shape to it or outline of an aircraft. I got out of my car and watched it slowly maneuver south/southeast,i got back in and continued on to pick up my son.On the way back[not even 5 minutes later] it was gone.
