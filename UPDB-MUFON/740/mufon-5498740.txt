{nd} It was three very bright white lights in a triangle shape.

I was in Vista California and my friend and I were driving up her driveway toward her house. I first noticed the object because it was very bright in the sky and caught my attention. At first I thought it was a plane but when I saw the three lights and how they moved together in the sky I realized it did not move in the same manner as a plane. They were three very bright white lights that formed a triangle shape. One light on the top and two on the bottom. At first it was moving upwards and toward the left then it suddenly changed direction and was going straight down. It was at that point that I saw a plane flying nearby it and comparing the airplane with the object it was very obviously not a plane. When I saw it I was amazed and transfixed on how bright white the lights were. I wanted to get a picture but by the time I was trying to get to my phone the object had gone down below the trees and buildings out of my sight.
