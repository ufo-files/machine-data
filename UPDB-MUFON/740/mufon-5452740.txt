Unidentified object changing directions in sky

I saw the big dipper and realized that I haven't played around with the super zoom feature on my new Samsung S20+. I pointed my camera with video up, moved away from the big dipper, and caught a glimpse of something moving in the sky. I held the camera as still as I could, I couldn't see this object with the naked eye. I noticed that it changed direction a couple of times, very fast. I took a still shot and also viewed the video in zoom and it appears to be spinning. The sounds in the background are likely the fire pit.
