Was driving west toward town, object appeared approx 20 ft above car. Paced vehile for 4/5 minutes.

I was driving toward home on state Hwy 12. Disc shaped object appeared about 20 ft above a street light. Sunroof was open & object hovered over and paced car for approximately 4/5 minutes then sped off to the NE toward the Rogers airport. It made no sound, but seemed to produce a powerful vibration. It was larger than the car, but smaller than a house. It seemed to follow the car and at one point was only about 6 ft above the car. It did not affect the car's performance.
