Saw three star like ufo in the sky. Over a walmart.

At Outback eating dinner. As we were leaving a man came in telling everyone there were UFO's in the sky. I joked with the hostess to no believe him. He insisted there were ufo's. We decided to go see for ourself. And sure enough as we drove to the walmart a little further down the road we saw three lights in the sky at first we thought it was cell phone tower lights. Then as we got closer we noticed they were free floating, pulsing like a star in the sky over the walmart on 192. People were standing around looking at it. Then helicopters came in and they just disappeared.
