{jpg} Nine lights in formation hovered then moved away together and disappeared

I was at the beach in Dana Point viewing the lunar eclipse with my husband and many other Dana Point residents were there, too. Because we were all looking up to see the moon, we all saw this low hovering formation of nine bright lights. The lights were in a formation then they moved slowly into a couple different formations--someone said it looked like they were trying to make constellations. After doing this for a few minutes, they moved off into the distance, into a small cluster. Then they got into a line and eventually were so small they couldn't be seen anymore. Strangest thing I've ever seen.
