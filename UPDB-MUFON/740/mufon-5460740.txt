{rjl} Bright flash of light that slowly fades. Happened 9x within a minute or so but could've been there for longer.

Was star gazing all night and about to go inside since the sun was coming up. I was going to take one last video of the birds chirping and how pretty the sky was looking when I noticed a bright flash of light in the sky above my house. I kept videoing and got 9 flashes total from what I could see. It was not a plane and im not sure what else it could be? The 7th flash actually stayed solid for a couple seconds and you can see the path it takes while a second flash goes right above it! I played the video in real time and then added in another video which is replaying the flashes again in succession right after one another. I slowed down some of the flashes so you could see in better detail. Especially the one that stayed solid and went in a line while another flashed above. Enjoy my video!
