Glowing ball of light floated in the sky below the clouds heading east at approximately 8:30 PM Monday 8/30/21

I was out on my deck Monday night, admittedly I was smoking weed so that may affect my credibility here, I was looking up as the stars were coming out, and I saw what looked to be a glowing ball of light moving across the sky below the clouds. I don't think it was a plane, because I saw what were clearly planes flying by before and after, and this didn't look or move like them. I also don't really think it was a satellite because it looked to be below the clouds. Whatever it was, it was moving east, and I eventually lost sight of it, it possibly went behind a cloud. No noise was coming from it either. I'm not saying this was definitely aliens, and it could have easily been something completely ordinary, but like I said, I really don't think it was a plane.
