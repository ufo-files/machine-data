{jpg} Huge triangle craft slowly passed over football field.

In 1999 I was coaching my son's football team. After the game we were taking the flag down and were looking straight up the pole. A huge triangle craft slowly floated over the football field at about 12 stories high.
The lights in the corners were 12 to 15 feet wide and round. As the craft passed by, the lights from the football field hit it and it appeared to start becoming transparent. I was totally enamored by it and my son was freaked out. 
The craft made no noise at all and if we weren't looking straight up the flagpole we would not have seen it or heard..it was dark outside at the time. The craft made no sound at all. I would estimate that it was 100 to 150 feet wide. And was traveling from the east to the west.
