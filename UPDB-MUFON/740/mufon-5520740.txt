White Orb moving west and then blinking red light and possible second object seen

Returning from church, I saw the orb in the sky again. I watched it for a while. As it was neared the point where it disappears, it blinked red again, this time it seemed to have been more intensified than before. I watched it and look away for a second and it was gone. About ten minutes later, I noticed another-or the same object- object the sky where the first object is usually seen. It later disappeared.
