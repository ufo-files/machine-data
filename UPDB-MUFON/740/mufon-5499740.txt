{jd} 3 bright light in triangular form , one solid craft , see metallic reflection on underbelly

Between the dates of Aug 15- Aug 25 2014 several objects were spotted..the first approx Aug 15- my husband,daughter andyself were standing outside watching the meteor shower.. At approx 10pm my husband spotted a large object in the northern sky , it was triangular in shape and had white lights at each point..the lights were bright and lit up the underbelly of the craft..so much so that you could see a metallic reflection...it moved eastward.. Going very slowly and it wasn't very high ( not as high as an airplane) and it was COMPLETELY SILENT..all 3 of us witnessed the craft and watched it move slowly across the sky until it disappeared behind the tree line..THEN a few mornings later at approx 615am while my daughter and son were standing outside to catch the school bus my daughter and son witnessed 2 oval shape crafts that were connected side by side ( they said it looked similar to the infinity symbol) they ran inside to get me ..I ran outside and witnessed the connected ovals and watched as they hovered then moved northwest .the craft was very large and silent.. It moved swiftly but strangely sideways.. As the craft continued north west it changed shape and went from 2 connected ovals to two connected slits before completely disappearing ...I can't describe the color but it looked textured in a scense and metallic like...my children were completely terrified and freaking out.this event lasted approx 3minutes...THEN a few nights ago my husband was outside at approx 10pm he screamed for me to come outside with my phone camera..I ran outside and looked up and directly over our heads, maybe 200ft was a single huge bright light..it moved north to south and it was pretty low..the light was bright but didn't seem to project light..it was like this bright burning white light contained within itself... It moved slowly straight over our heads then sped up quite a bit them the light simply dulled in and out then faded away..it disappeared..the sky was crystal clear each night of the 3 sightings...my daughter witnessed all 3 as well..and its the strangest thing..during these past 2 weeks we have been noticing these flashing strobing lights in the sky..at first I thought maybe lightening or fireworks or the cell phone tower close by..but the strobes and flashes vary in brightness..and they come from all directions.. We can't find or pinpoint where the lights are originating from..sometimes the strobes will be quick and so bright other times its more dull...the sky has been crystal clear...we are starting to get a little freaked out..what we've seen may very well be nothing out of the ordinary but to us its strange..we see planes, jets and helicopters all the time and even see the occasional hot air balloon..but this is different...words can't describe it..we are all just in amazement and a little fear
