{cak} While watching Fireworks noticed object come from west and circle area, then return to west and fly out of sight. Was very bright orange and pulsated bright red. Was circuler in shape.

It was circular in shape, although not a perfect circle. It glowed a very bright Orange which pulsated to a very bright Red aura around it.
