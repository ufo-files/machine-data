{tjd} recorded a fast moving light /object on our security camera

On the evening of December 22, 2018 I was monitoring our security cameras, while watching TV. I was monitoring the cameras, due to the recent visit of a coyote nosing around our chicken coops. At approximately 20:50 hours, a commercial came on the TV, I glanced at the monitor and saw a bright light streak across the monitor. The event was recorded on my security system.

It appears in the video at 20:50:00 in the upper right of the video and travels from right to left at a very high rate of speed. The camera is pointed to the south. The object moved from west to east. While watching the video in slow motion, frame by frame, the object appears to be pulsating. It disappeared into low clouds that were on the Southeast horizon.
