I saw the six craft in two rows of three from my kitchen window,the rows of three were touching eachother it appeared to me & one row was diagonally above the other

I woke up very early that august morning & for some reason I sat up in bed & got up right away,I went straight into the kitchen of my flat in worthing.Looking back I dont know why I got up right away & I didnt even go into the bathroom first as I normally would.I reached for the kettle & went to fill it up at the sink which was right by the window & that was when I saw them,my Jaw dropped because right in front of me directly above the rooftops of the houses behind my flat were six craft in two rows of three.They appeared to be extremely low in the sky & only just above the rooftops,I feel that they were very close to me indeed as the houses behind my flat were not very far away from my building.They were oval in shape & white/silvery in colour but what was most striking was the dazzling sparkle of each one,I can only assume that they had a highly polished mirror like finish on whatever they were made of that was sparkling in the bright sunshine.This was a very sunny morning with a virtually cloudless sky,which was unusual because the summer of 2012 was very cloudy & rainy.The two rows of three were seperated diagonally ie one row was above the other but diagonally off to the right & the three craft in each row were so close together that they appeared to be touching eachother.I could hear no sound atall & I plucked up the courage to go out on my balcony to see if there was any noise for sure,I could hear nothing & they were completely motionless just kind of hanging there.I didnt stay out on the balcony for long(I was living on the second floor)I came back in because I felt really uneasy/scared of what I was seeing.I first noticed the craft at 4.55am & I stood watching them for about fourty minutes until 5.35am I felt that I couldnt take my eyes off of them & I could not believe that this was really happening but it was I was wide awake & I even pinched myself several times to make sure that I was awake.I really wished that I had taken photos or woken my sons up to show them but I felt kind of rooted to the spot & I was also afraid that if I went away they might disappear.At 5.35am it suddenly occurred to me that they might be watching me,infact I became convinced of it & I felt suddenly very afraid & decided to go back to bed.This is something that I now really regret because I didnt see them go or anything...when I got up again at about 7.30am they were gone & have no idea now how long they stayed there or how they moved off etc,this I will always regret but I had become overcome with fear in the end & felt convinced that I was being watched.I did not report this incident at the time but I told my daughter & her husband about it also my two other younger sons,I think it rather scared them all.I have wondered & worried about this incident a lot since it happened & have now decided to inform you of it....I know that this did happen,I did see what I saw & I cant explain it.I know a fair bit about Aircraft as I was born into a family of Aviation enthusiasts & many people on my Dads side of the family were in past & are now employed in Aviation with De Havillands at Hatfield & Lockheed in the USA as well as the RAE at Farnborough etc.My dad had been in the RAF & was very interested in Aeroplanes...I was taken to Aerodromes & Air displays from a very young age & I know a fair bit about Airplane design & aviation & so i feel certain that what I saw were not man made craft,they wouldnt be considered aerodynamic I dont think.I cant believe that no one else in Worthing saw what I saw that morning even though it was very early,I just want to know what I saw & what or who it was & what do they want?I feel sure that I was drawn to my kitchen window that morning,thats how it felt to me then & it still does...looking back I was drawn to the kitchen window & I have a strange feeling that I was meant to see what I saw that morning & that bothers me very much.
