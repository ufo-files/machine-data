I was looking at the stars on Antero Reservoir when I thought I saw a comet enter the atmosphere. It looked like a bright white ball of light. I was waiting for it to break apart but it never did. It hovered for less than a minute and then shot due north then blinked out of sight. Then it flashed back in and out. Each time was a distance farther than the last. Finally it blinked completed out of sight. A few minutes after that there were helicopters escorting something huge that blackened out the stars. Finally, we saw the what I believe to be the new satellites go buy but I'm not sure. It was all too much to be coincidence. Does anyone know if any of the air bases were doing maneuvers? The first sighting I had I am sure was not of this world. The last two not sure. I did try to take video of the events but was too shaken up to get anything worth while.
