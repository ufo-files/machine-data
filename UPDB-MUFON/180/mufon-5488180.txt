{cak} Bright round light zoomed by the door, with a swooshing sound, made a right angle turn upwards when it got to the trees.

Sitting in the living room with the door open, about 8 pm talking to my late husband on the telephone and described to him what I saw. It was bright and round (About the size of a truck hubcap) I was also already looking out the door. Looked like a bright light to me. It zoomed by the open door and made a quick 95 degree turn upward and was gone. After I finished talking to my husband called our daughter and told her. Felt a little, not much, scared, but closed the door and kept watching, was happy when my husband got hom. It zoomed off to my left and then straight up when it got to the tree line. That's it, not much but will never forget.11
