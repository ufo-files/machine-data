Started in middle of night , was paralyzed and levitate above bed

I woke around 3 am to find that I was paralyzed and levitating above my mattress about 2 to 4 ft . I could move nothing but my eyes. I looked around but was unable to see anything that was in my range of vision.
 I layed that way and tryed to stay awake, but the next thing that I remember was that it was about 6:30 A.M. and that I was getting dressed for high school 
I looked out the glass doors and across the lake (in my front yard) in the trees but on or close to the ground i saw a round object that was glowing. It had colored bright lights of different colors hat were flashing and rotating at the same time.
Then, I next remember being at school in my 2nd or 3rd period math class.
 For at least 20 or so yrs. I never told anyone for fear of being ridiculed and not being believed
 In 2014 or 15, I found one of my neighbors online who I had not seen since the 1980's.
Somehow we got around to the subject of intelligence from somewhere other than earth. Then she said something about the "UFO" that she had seen on the way to the bus stop when she was young.
