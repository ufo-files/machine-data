{dr}6 ft diameter red-orange glowing ball sighted over marsh, rose, emitted two smaller spheres & the three flew off together.

Witness A approved the following statement: 1At about 7:30 pm one evening about six weeks ago, my friend and I were sitting in her sun room in her house in Prior Lake watching TV, when a bright circular object above the tall grass in a swampy area about 400 feet away attracted her attention. She called it to my attention. This object, about five or six feet in diameter, was rising up and moving to the left at an angle. As it rose it emitted two similar but smaller objects, each perhaps a foot and a half in diameter, which moved to each side of the first object, and the three flew off together in formation in a northeast direction until they were out of sight. The objects were all a red-orange color and not quite as bright as the full harvest moon.1
