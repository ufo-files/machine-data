UFO spotted in photograph a day after photo was taken.

I was on my drive-way in front of my house taking pictures of the setting sun. The sky looked particularly beautiful with the sun beams cutting through the clouds, so I took numerous pictures from different angles. It was only the day after while going through the photos on my computer, that I noticed the hat-shaped object in the sky. I never noticed an object in the sky while taking the pictures, and I don't remember being cognizant of getting any birds in the frame while shooting. In fact, I remember it being a clear and open sky - perfect for sunset pictures. Analyzing this photo now, I feel certain that it is something mysterious and unknown. I feel like I snapped a picture of something that was supposed to stay invisible.
