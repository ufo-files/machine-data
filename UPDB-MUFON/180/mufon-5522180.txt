A pulsating red/white light slowly moving overhead

I had just finished fishing on a private Lease 1/4 mile from City Limits. It lies in the flight path for Mid Continent Airport when winds are from the south. Needless to say, I know what aircraft look like, and the lights on civilian and military aircraft. I had made a campfire, was listening to music and chilling out by myself. The owner of the fishing lease (sandpit) was having a get together across the lake and about 20 feet in elevation above me. I was facing due east. For some reason I felt compelled to turn around and face the west. When i did I noticed this round sphere of light moving from west to east appx 500-1000 feet in the air.  It moved SLOWLY, maybe 10-20 mph judging by the distance I observed it in the time allotted. It moved directly above my head. As it was getting nearer to me, I became a bit concerned and decided to grab my Nikon D70 camera out of my trunk. I was a professional photographer in the past. I figured it was going to get me, at least I would leave evidence!! lol. Anyway, it paused directly overhead of me and I managed to get one Pic before it moved off. The object was pulsating red/white/red/white. It dissapeared above the tree line. I went out to the edge of the shore to locate the object but I was unable to see it any longer. What I found odd was the several people that were on the opposite shore and above my elevation, made no reaction to the object. I listened to hear if they spotted it, but, there was no indication that they say anything at all. Weird, but, they might not have been looking my direction. I do have a photo that I want to submit for analysis, which is the very reason I have decided to report this incident anyway. The object made absolutely NO noise, and was not traveling the direction that normal air traffic does common to the this area.
