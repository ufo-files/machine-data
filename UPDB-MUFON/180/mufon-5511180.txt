The red dots being faster than the white ones but the white ones having the ability to split. Night after night i witnessed these red and white dots out in space i even heard my mother tell me there was a report on the news about red dots over the great lakes somewhere around the same weeks i was seeing what i was seeing. I no longer saw the red dots after the orange orb came. Back to the orange orb, the orb was about 30 feet from the drive way and didn't shine light on other objects although it was very bright.
