I was outside just after 7pm and it's dark by then. Overhead we saw 2 starlike lights but brighter, side by side but seeming to be not together in any fashion. They seemed to be just too low to be an airplane and not hear any sound. They travelled east for several seconds then immediately turned southeast abruptly. It all lasted about 15 seconds or so. The light on the right had a little more movement than the left one. I have to say, I grew up in a military family. My dad was an airplane mechanic and i was very well aware of the capabilities of normal jets and other aircraft. I'm confident this wasn't a satellite and I'm confident this wasn't an aircraft. I know the only other possibility it could be would be possible 2 drones. But I'm still confident it was not that due to the lights being white and the distance they travelled and their speed.
