Lights in the southern sky.

We were pulling out of a parking lot, and I was looking south towards the airport for oncoming cars, when I saw yellow lights (colored like the reflectors on a road) moving left to right with lights arching down for about 2 seconds then disappear. As soon as the 1st set were done, then it repeated at about the same height, but this time right to left, with the same number of lights and for the same time duration. As soon as those were done it repeated again from left to right, and then stopped. They appear to be at some distance away south of town. During this event , I said to my daughter "did you see the lights?", and she said yes but didn't know what they were either?
