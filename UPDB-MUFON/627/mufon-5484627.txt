{cak} cigar shaped object over down town Cassville responding to my ring a cow bell to ring in the new year

tradionaly my husband and I celebrate the new year by ringing a large cow bell (real, handmade, and antique) to ring in the new year. As I was swing around in a circle with my cowbell clang away, when I was facing downtown Cassville, MO, I spotted a long narrow cigar shaped object hanging down by the point of the object. It was bright red in color and was aproxitmately 50 feet in length. It blinked its lights several times and disapeared into into the night after turning its lights off. It was the first time I had seen this object and I think we caught its attention with the noise of our bells.
