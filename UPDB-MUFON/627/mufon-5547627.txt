Bright LARGE white light, low flying, fast, possibly crashing?

My wife and I were on the road from Athens to J.J.'s flea market at around 6am. The road when it lets out from Athens is mlk jr drive and changes to 441(?) I think. Just before J.J's and after the Bulldog Inn motel is the stretch of road where we saw it. We saw a massive white light of a unusual shape at a high speed go over us and then down to the ground. I can't belive there wasn't a news report about it or something. It wasn't a meteor because of it's shape and size (if a meteor that big hit the ground they would have told us). Very weird stuff... we both saw it till it went lower than the tree line... we were shocked by it.
