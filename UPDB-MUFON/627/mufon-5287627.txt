I saw a white object ,tree top level ascending.

I was driving northbound on M 50 approximately 1 to 2 miles south of Napoleon Michigan. I saw a bright white object at Treetop level ascending at what I believe to be a 45° angle.It was a sunny partly cloudy day with good visibility. At first I thought it was a executive jet airplane however, there are no airports nearby to accommodate that type of aircraft. It’s speed in movement were very much like that of a small jet business plane however there were something about it that just didn’t seem right. As safely as I could I watched the object travel upwards and into a cloud. I kept looking for the object to emerge from the cloud but I never saw it after it went into the cloud.
it intrigued me so much I could not stop thinking about what it was or what it could be. If there were an airport close by I would not have given the object a second look. The closest airport to accommodate an airplane of that size would be approximately 25 miles West. When I first saw the object it was way too low in altitude to have taken off from that airport.
