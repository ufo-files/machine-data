Orbs of light hovering above head trough rain clouds

I was coming home from work, picked up my son from his nanny and we headed home. Close to home, we both noticed some lights in the clouds. At first I thought they were reflections from the ground. When we first noticed them there were 4 bright lights in the clods, circling eachother in one spot. While we walked further towards our home, the circling lights seemed to follow the path of the street we were walking on. As we stopped in front of the block of apartments where we live, they seemed to stop too. Some of my neighbours were there and they saw them too. They were like gigantic fireflies playing with eachother trough the clouds. Ocasionally you could see the lights were coming from above the coulds and not from the ground. I took my phone and did my best to film them. I had to use my phone cover to block the street light from interfering with the camera on my phone. After I filmed, I took some photos too and after that we went upstairs. I don't know for how long the lights remained in the sky, but they were there for at least 15 minutes. 
I don't know if it matters, but the folliwing day I had a dream about a partially destroyed planet on which the remaining people were searching for a way to prevent a cataclysm.
