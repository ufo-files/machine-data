orange lights

I had been walking my dog at about 7pm uk time and on return to my house i looked up in to the sky as i am an amatuer astronomer, and the skys were very clear, very cold, venus was bright. 
I noticed an object moving west to east, and at first i thought it was a satellite, it was a dim orange in colour, two aircraft were at high altitude at the same time. I didnt think much of it, then I noticed a second object on the same flight path, same colour, same speed same height, and I just thought its two satelleites, , then a third appeared, same height same speed, same flight path, i ran in the house and told my wife to come out and have a look., by the time she came out they were dimming. however when we were still outside and within 30 seconds. a fourth object in the same colour and same height came from the north on a southern flight path. then a fifth object came from the east at the same time as we were observing the fourth object, in the same direction as the first three objects. 
I regularly look at the sky because i am an amatuer astronomer , i have never seen anything like that before. my wife coudnt believe it, she thought they were going to crash into the aircraft
