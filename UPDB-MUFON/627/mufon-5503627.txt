{nd} Looked like a fireball traveling across the sky..

At appx 2100 my wife and I were standing in our back yard and saw what looked like a fireball in the sky. We see planes and helicopters out here all the time and this was neither. We first noticed it NW of our location and traveled towards the SE before it disappeared from sight. We witnessed it for appx two minutes before it was gone. It had a yellow to light orange glow to it. We've never seen anything like it. I didn't have my phone or anything to capture a photo of it. It seemed to fade away the further it got from us.
