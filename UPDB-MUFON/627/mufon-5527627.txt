Time & date approx. taken by ex-co-worker on his honey moon.

Unfortunatly no documented info at this time. Taken by an ex-co-worker on what was a cheap 35mm film camera. Upon their return from vacation, he shared photos from their honny moon. When this photo came up in my hand - I imediatly noticed the circle of lights but did not say anything at the time. I asked Joe what they were taking a photo of and he replied the whales in the forground, (some slight surface breaks -white-caps, in the lower right hand area of the photo). When I eventually pointed out the circle of lights he was surprised to see them - he and his wife were always looking for the whales in the photo. 
Location estimated to be due west off the coast of the ba ja peninsula on a cruise ship.
