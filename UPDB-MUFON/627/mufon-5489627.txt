{dr}Saw 3 disk shaped objects followed by 2 F86 Jets

I was on my family farm about 50 miles north of Washington DC and heard a whirling sound. I looked up and saw 3 silver shiny disk shaped objects being followed by 2 F 86 jets. They were flying toward me in a North direction and then turned East and disappeared out of sight. My dad was a pilot and I always loved seeing airplanes that often flew over our farm which is only a few miles from Camp David, Maryland.  This was about 60 years ago but the memory is as clear as if it happened yesterday.  I was very young but old enough to recognize F86 fighter jets and old enough to know that what I was seeing was very unusual.
