{cak} Taking pictures of a full moon we noticed an object moving in a manner that defies conventional logic.

Around 9:45 on Tues Jan 11th my neighbor and I were taking pictures of a beautiful full moon from our yard which is approximately a quarter mile from the Atlantic Ocean when we noticed a light that was visible with our cameras but not to the naked eye. The light had a cylindrical shape and was moving in ways that I've only seen humming birds move. After taking a few pictures and observing this object for about 5 or 6 minutes it disappeared with any sound what so ever. Having lived 15 mins south of Patrick Air Force Base I have seen many planes,helicopters etc. in the sky but nothing that moved in this manner as well as not making any sound.
