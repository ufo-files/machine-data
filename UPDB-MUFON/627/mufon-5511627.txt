{cak} Fast moving, abruptly changing direction

Multiple lights moving at a fast speed, stopping and immediately change direction. multiple lights moving together, like chasing each other, covering wide degrees of the sky for the distance. Observed outside and discussed by multiple related and unrelated witnessed (during a party where alcohol was consumed). No one could support a guess for what it was. No observable noise (although distance could have been a factor) Lasted about 10 minutes. Seemed to be outside of current human (publicly known) technology. Inertia would break apart the craft and incapacitate the pilot performing these maneuvers in current military aircraft.  This memory has stayed with me as I still have no idea what it could have been.
