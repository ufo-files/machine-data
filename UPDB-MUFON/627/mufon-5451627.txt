Observed 8-10 objects in infrared around 7pm July 5. Early morning July 6 abduction event. Visible hand shaped bruise on chest with 4 fingers

The evening of July 5th my friend and I went out to a local dry lake bed with powerful infrared goggles and lasers to look for anomalous objects in the sky. We spot at least one every time we go, but that night we saw 8-10 objects that exhibited extremely fast velocity, rapid directional change and intelligent response to laser. I performed a brief Ce5 protocol before hand, but it’s not necessary to see these types of high altitude objects. My friend who will remain unnamed and I are both trained observers, I’m a commercial drone pilot specializing in lidar and photogrammetry, my friend flies various passenger aircraft. I went to sleep around two am on July 6. When I awoke in the morning my chest was in a tremendous amount of pain and within the following 24 hours a hand Shepard bruise appeared. the hand appeared to have 3 fingers and a thumb and it was over the the site of a past chest surgery. I have included photos and measurements, and I am open to hypnotic memory recall. There is a family history of sightings and abductions, which led me to be a researcher in the first place. This is the first injury that seemed compelling enough to report.
