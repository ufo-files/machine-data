{dr}Saw some lights in the sky while fishing on the river

My brother and i decided to go out and go night fishing on 9/22/18 out on the coosa river. We put the boat in the water at brushy branch boat launch at about 8:15 pm. On our way up the river i looked back behind us to see this beautiful veiw of the almost full moon lighting up the water and thought it would be a good opportunity to take a picture. So i pull out my phone and snap 2 pictures to make sure i got a good shot and shoved my phone back in my pocket. Later on that night around 9:30 pm i glance up and see a very bright light in the sky that was shining like a star and pulsating, but looked way to close and large to fit into that category. So i point it out to my brother and we both sit and watch it for a few minutes wondering what it is, but it never moved so we just blew it off as a really bright star. A few minutes later i glance back up and it is nowhere to be found. "Crazy" i thought, but didn't think anything else about it. Around 10:00pm i decided i would post one of the pictures i had taken earlier to social media. That's when i discovered the 2 photos taken back to back(within a couple of seconds)were quite different. In one photo there is what looks like a star in the sky under the moon but in the second photo the star is gone and only a slight streak of light is seen. On the way back to the launch around 10:40 what looked like the most beautiful and brightest meteor i have ever seen streaked across the sky directly over us for around 5-6 seconds before burning up in the atmosphere. Could have all been coincidences. I for one honestly don't know.
