pulsating lights 112004

Several weeks ago I sent in a report about white stars with alternatingpulsating red, blue and sometimes green lights that several of us at work on thenight shift had been seeing since August. These were observed with our nakedeyes. Tonight someone had a pair of expensive, high-powered binoculars. Tommyand 5 of our adolescent customers looked through them and noticed the evenlypulsating lights and then told me about them. I went outside with one of thecustomers and looked at the star. It was very clear and easy to see. The starwas steady and almost a flourescent white. It had a red pulsating light in theupper left hand side and a blue pulsating light in the center below the star. These red and blue lights pulsated alternately very quickly. It seemed likeabout two cyclesl of alternating pulses per second. Also, as I stated in myfirst report, these stars DO NOT MOVE ACROSS THE HEAVENS with the normalconstallations and stars throughout the night. I go out several times duringthe night, and as the normal stars move across the sky, these stars with thepulsating lights are in the same spot every time I see them throughout thenight. Our skies are usually clear here in Yuma and the visibility is usuallyat 10,000 feet according to the weather station at the Marine base. Tonight Ijust observed one of these stars, but on many occasions there have been as manyas 5 at one time. I just wanted to make this update of my earlier report withthe added clarity seen with these great binoculars.
