Red Orbs of light

Understandably memory fades.I have been witness to three separate events.One very close. However this event was widely spotted across several counties and investigated by KWIX Radio out of Moberly Mo.
The exact year even escapes me now but it was early in the year,no leaves on the maple tree outside.1975-1977 Mid to late Winter.
Our house was situated 1/4 West of Audrain Co.Rd.989 (Range Line Rd.),1/8 Mile South of Audrain Co.Rd.946.
I had used the bathroom,located on the West side of the house,getting ready for bed. A habit was to always look out the bathroom window every night,see if any clouds are coming in and to make sure all is well.
I saw a red ball of light moving South to North at a leisurely pace.35 to 45 mph.I estimated it about over Hwy. 63 probably 9 miles to the North of Columbia.As I watched another light appeared,lined up neatly with good spacing behind the first,then directly came another,out of nowhere,this continued until there were 6 all running at the same altitude,equidistant from one another.I watched until I could no longer see them due to the limitation of the window.
The following morning KWIX radio had a couple of call in shows,Party Line and Trading Post.They were inundated with calls.The strange lights were seen apparently from all throughout the listening area.KWIX investigated the reports. According to KWIX the military had no flights of any kind in our area and the Airport at Columbia never had anything on radar.
I have never seen or heard anything more about it since that time.It was incredible.
