Star-like reddish orange light, south or southwest movement. Orange County, Ca.

Observed small star-like object reddish/orange glow, move in a steady straight path.
About 4 seconds and light disappeared into the starry clear night sky.
Light was unwavering in intensity over it's flight path. NO trail, no flames, smoke, noise, did not look like any meteor I've ever seen in my life. Too slow to be a meteor, way too fast at that extreme altitude to be conventional aircraft. 
Two hours later, had 2 more sightings - looked exactly the same, color, light intensity, size/ altitude/steady motion, steady color, each a few seconds, but the following two moved westward as opposed to S or SW as the first one did.
Judging as best I can on altitude it looked to be higher than the atmosphere.
Jaw dropped each sighting. Spooky feeling.
