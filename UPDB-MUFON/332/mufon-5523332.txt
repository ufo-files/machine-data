UFO Sighting in Hertfordshire

At the time I was in hospital. I took some air outside, noticing it was a particularly misty night. However the stars could still clearly be seen, as the visibility was not completely reduced. 

I noticed in the sky a set of yellow lights, not unlike those of a plane - yet they were in entirely the wrong formation for that of an aeroplane. The lights were arranged in a triangular formation. This was something I immediately could see as the lights were moving together and could be distinguished against the stars. I began to assume they belonged to one object. 

Another marked difference between that of a plane was that it was flying (or appeared to be flying) very low for a plane - that is, from what I could tell of its size from the distance between the lights, which I assumed were on the sides of the object. 

The way in which the object moved was the really striking thing. It moved very fast, yet smoothly, above my head and hovered in position above me about 0.2 km away. 
Then it disappeared after hovering for 5 seconds at most, via the same smooth and FAST movement but in a completely different direction. 

I felt a sense of calm at seeing it and didn't want to lose sight of it and definitely felt euphoric afterwards. I don't think it affected me in any way psychologically - I just felt privileged to have seen it.
