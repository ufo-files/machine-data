(gs) Object was noticed only after downloading photo from cell phone to computer.

While out shopping in the Lawrenceville/Princeton, NJ area on July 10, 2012, around 3pm, I was observing the beautiful clouds in the sky. I felt I had to take a photo of them. I snapped two photos with my cell phone camera: one at 3:04pm and another at 3:06pm both facing north. After downloading the photos to my computer, I noticed a strangely-shaped, dark object in front of one of the puffy white clouds on the first photo. The object is not on the second photo. I did not notice the object in the sky at the time, so I didn't observe any movement or sound. The camera screen was hard to see in the bright sunlight; I just aimed and snapped. It was a busy parking lot with lots of car and people activity. I have no idea what the object was.
