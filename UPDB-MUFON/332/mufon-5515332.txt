{nd} Space Shuttle Endeavor Piggy-back ride surrounded it

I was watching the live feed of the Space Shuttle Endeavors piggy-back ride to California on Saturday, September 22, 2012 at 10 am central time when a white pulsating orb and a HUGE black, then silver disk shaped object entered the screen. The lady announcer said, "Oh my God!!!"

The plane that was carrying the Endeavor immediately banked to the left, and I heard the engines de-celerate. Then the plane descended to the lowest level possible to miss the mountain it was going over. I have the entire event saved on my DVR. The object then re-appeared and followed the plane until the live feed was abruptly switched the ISS. 

Rememeber, this was supposed to be a live 3-hour event, that was abruptly cancelled.
