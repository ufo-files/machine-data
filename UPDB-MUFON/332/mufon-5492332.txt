{rjl} during Northern lights an orb was observed inan arch pattern going from South to North.

My husband daughter and I drove down a dark dirt road to observe the Northern lights on June 23rd 2015. We had been there for awhile when we all saw an orb coming from the South it was arching across the sky.It was much larger than a star and it had a soft glow to it. We watched it for at least 10 minutes if not more. It arched as far North as we could see. We were discussing what the heck it could have been. When we saw 2 small bright lights coming at us. They were moving up and down and side to side. One just disappeared but the other was coming towards us. It wasn't very far off the ground but it was hard to tell how far away it was. I said if it gets any closer that I am leaving.It to just disappeared. When I got home I went on Facebook and was commenting on the Northern Lights and people where talking about seeing an orb. People as far away as Grand Marie had seen it. And people in Lakeville where talking about the lights. So I'm definitely not alone in what we saw.
