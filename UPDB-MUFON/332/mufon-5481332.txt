My entire family viewed the craft hovering approx 50

On a summer night around dusk back in the early seventies my family and I were on our way to evening church service. As soon as we exited the back door we looked up and saw a saucer shaped craft hovering directly above our house. It was so close I could have thrown a stone and hit it. It was a metallic silver color with no weld or rivet marks. It made no noise or emitted any smoke. It was approx 30 - 40 ft in diameter.It had a glowing ring of light around the mid section. My parents, brother and I looked at it for several mins. My older sisters were terrified and ran in to the house. The craft slowly began to rise and we gathered all of our family to follow it. It moved slowly northward towards Mt. Doug. We followed it for 5-8 mins. It went behind the mountain and disappeared. That was the last we saw it. To this day neither of my sisters can recall the incident, yet my parents, brother and I can remember it vividly. The entire encounter was approx 15-20 mins. In my mind there was no doubt what it was.Over the years I have told the story to a few friends but have met mostly with disbelief and laughter. It has not affected my life in any way, good or bad. I recently saw on one of your programs an incident that was very similar with the craft hovering directly above a house. So , I thought I would share this with your group.
