{cak} Glowing hot disk shape object moving south to north and dropped a piece of like hot liquid metal.

I was sitting watching TV in living room when I noticed a bright glowing object through the trees in the first window, then I saw it go into view through the next window. By the time I saw it in the third window and saw it drop a piece of liquid then realized what it could and probably was, I had to get the dog down out of my lap, get up out of the chair and look out the window to see where it was going it was gone. Went out through the back garage but, it was still not visible. So I went back in and tried to see if there was any thing on the Mn Mufon site as to what it might be or a link to a web site where someone could see what might be falling from the skies that night but, no luck. In my best opinion I believe that it was something like a hot melting craft. You could see the heat that it had and when it dropped something straight down instead of like it falling to the rear while it was moving that sort of sealed the deal for me that it was something of interest. I had seen something similar to this back on August 19, 1993. I was thinking that at first it might of been a low falling meteorite or something but, when I saw the dripping come down out of it I knew that it couldn't be certain things that I was thinking it might be. I got up and told about it at the Mn Mufon monthly meeting
