{nd} contrail to the north heading directly west suddenly changed direction and headed directly east

Going home from town, the contrail (?) changing direction caught my eye. It began from the east moving west then did a deliberate turn back to the east. I told my husband, who also saw it, that it looked as though the pilot of a plane forgot something and turned around. He said it looked odd because he didn't think a plane could turn so abruptly. We lost sight when trees got in the way. I attached photo.
