3 lights in a line, similar to Orions Bet

On a clear summer night, I had set up at Friars Cragg with my Wife for a night of stargazing.
A little more than an hour into the evening we had turned to the south where we saw 3 (maybe 4) lights arranged in a horizontal line just above a hill line at the most southerly point of Derwent Water. Initially I was surprised because we had just been looking at Orion’s Belt to the North-North West and it looked, initially, similar. 
After a few seconds the lights started moving independently of each other, initially these were slow smooth manoeuvres, however they picked up in speed and became increasingly abrupt at fast speeds and at sharp angles of movement.
This happened for maybe a minute before the lights returned to their initial position as a horizontal line of lights before disappearing completely.
