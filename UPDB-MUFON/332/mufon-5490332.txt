{cak} Lights in the sky would glow bright orange and then multiply. Then would fade to small orbs and fly in formatio.

It was a quiet night in August 11th 2015. My friend "L" and I decided to take a country cruise since the stars were out with no clouds. We started the cruise at 9:35pm. Everything was uneventful for the first ten minutes. Then "L" stopped the car and said "Dude! Look at that what the f**k!?" I looked up in time to see 2 glowing yellow orange shapes in the sky then the orange glow would disappear and they would begin to imitate airplanes. We followed in pursuit of the two objects. For about 7 minutes we followed them while they blinked a very small white light. I must add there were airplanes in the sky at the same time and they would blink completely differently. The UFO's seemed to do a long blink followed by a type of strobe light blink that would go out and repeat on cue. Just about 7 minutes after they lit up like fire in the sky for the first time it happens again... Except this time we noticed a split and 2 UFO's turned to three and then the other one did the same and 3 turned to four. Compelety amazed by what we saw we had not thought about taking a picture or video yet. So I had "L" stop the car by an open field so I could get some shots from my IPhone. As soon as I pulled my phone out the lights started flowing on one again. This glow lasted longer then the others. Before it would only glow yellow red for 5 seconds but this time it held for about 15-20 seconds and I was able to take pictures and even have pictures showing it split into 2 making a total of 5 crafts in the sky. No sounds were observed and the lights soon all disappeared after I took the photo evidence. We drove home to examine the pictures and what we found can NOT be explained. All photos were taken while car sat motionless. Please contact me for more photos I was able to take 13 photos and 1 video
