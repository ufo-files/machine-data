{rjl} UFO was hovering, UFO was oval shaped. UFO changed colors from red to green in a very precise manner. No sound

My brother and I were sitting on our house balcony having a cigarette.  I happened to look up in the sky and noticed the UFO. At first I thought it was an airplane. The UFO was an oval shaped craft. The UFO started out as the color red and then changed to green in a very precise manner. The lights on the UFO never blinked or flashed, the colors just transitioned from red to green like a light switch. The colors were very bright. The UFO was hovering with no sound. It started to move slowly side to side as well as up and down. The UFO then started to rise higher and higher until it flew off and out of our sight. The sighting lasted about 5 minutes. I was very curious and interested to see what the UFO would do next. I wasn't scared or frightened. I was just very curious. After the sighting I did some research to find other UFO sightings in my area. I lost sight of the UFO when it flew off and it went out of sight.
