Starlike object, or maybe it was so far I couldn't see it's shape, it was shining just like a star but moving very fast and changing direction

I was sitting with my friend on a bench when I saw something in the sky. I first thought it was an airplane but it didn't look like it. It looked just like a star, it was very far so I don't know what is it's exact shape. It was first moving up but then it changed direction and started to go down but not in a acute angle.It was moving pretty fast for an object that far away. I was shocked and I showed it to my friend. She saw it too. That wasn't the first time I saw something like that. Last summer I was lying on my back in the pool. The nights were hot so I could swim at night. I was looking at the sky and I saw something. I thought it was a shooting star but then it changed direction very fast and started to go up. It was moving over the sky for a while and then it just dissapeared.
