heard strange noise,checked outside, saw ufo off in distance

I was laying in my bed at 11 pm on wednesday, june 6th 2012. I heard a strange un-explainable noise outside that did not sound like an airplane, but was in the sky. I heard it for about 2 minutes, then i got curious and walked to the back porch to see. Off in the distance to the left of me, i saw a UFO fly away. i Lost sight of it, because it went behind some tall trees. it was saucer shaped, with lights all around. it was spinning. it was hard to see, but im very sure that it wasnt a helicopter or airplane. the lights were white/yellow, and they blinked in sequence. i couldnt believe my eyes, and i was shaking. I started to cry, and for about an hour after that i was in a strange shocked-like state of mind.
