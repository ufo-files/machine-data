Saw giant glowing orb hovering above Wilkes-Barre PA

We were driving on I-81N just turning the bend to view the city of Wilkes-Barre Pa, when we noticed some bright flashing lights. When we were able to see the city, we saw a huge orb surrounded by orangey lights hovering over and sort of behind the city. It was night and very cloudy and stormy and the orb seemed to be shrouded in a haze. It only stayed lit up for a couple seconds and then it sort of shimmered and disappeared into the night sky. It was very big and unlike anything weve ever seen before. It almost looked like a planet or the moon rising but was too big and close looking to be that.
