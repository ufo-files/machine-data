Bright ball of light, Moved W to E. Imploded.

I was sitting on the patio talking to my neighbor, we witnessed a very large and bright white light. Moving west to east towards the Cascade mountains much faster than an aircraft. When it reached what appeared to be the foothills, the light imploded and it vanished. I am very familiar w/the air traffic in/out of SeaTac, this object was moving away from us, thus it was not landing lights. No strobes, no wings, no sound. There are 4 witnesses and we are all interested in knowing what this was. Robb in kent seems to have seen the same thing given his recent email on your site.
