{nd} Sighting during fireworks display

I was watching fireworks over a nearby elementary school when I looked higher up in the sky and noticed an orangish red circle moving across the sky in a straight line. The circle was definitely not blinking as a plane's lights would. It was unwavering and steady, as was it's path and speed. I watched the object as far as I could see, and once it faded away, another appeared in the same manner. There was a total of four in this exact path, then I noticed one past a grouping of trees a little further away, but still appeared to be the same type object, color, speed, everything. I looked forward to viewing fireworks that night because I knew there was a chance I might get to see something other than the obvious, I was excited I actually got to see something. I didn't feel much past the excitement of the sighting. I only lost sight of the circles when they traveled too far away.
