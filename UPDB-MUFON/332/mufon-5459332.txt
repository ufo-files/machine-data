About the size of a school bus... moving in non-erratic left to right straight pattern over single section of mountain side in East Tennessee. Had a mirror like surface. Seemed to cloak the pattern of mountain side behind it. It moved it straight lines back and forth in several close areas. My friend and I both saw it and immediately laughed and said what on earth is that. We were driving and turned around and went back to top hill to view. We sat there for about 15 minutes and just watched. It was the afternoon on a clear fall day. Completely bizarre and absolutely unlike any thing else either of us has witnessed.
