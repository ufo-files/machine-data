{nd} Witnessed a boomerang shaped object not far above the horizon it slowly disintegrated into nothingness and slowly started glowing orange again.

It was about 3:20 am and my friend an i went on a walk to a viewpoint to go look at the city lights. We first saw what looked like a house fire on the hill across the river, that fire got bigger and brighter. Then the fire slowly ascended far above the horizon and started to circle the skyline we flowed it walking around trying to get a better view through the trees it slowly went out of sight, we then turned around to look at the city view again. Thats when we saw a very large what almost looked like a crescent blood moon right over the tree line on our side of the river. The object was glowing bright orange red. It then slowly started to disintegrate one end of it became dim and appeared to break off in little pieces and fade away once almost completely gone it started to come back. Now back and even brighter we just sat there staring for a while after about ten minutes the whole thing just got dimmer and dimmer until we could only see the silhouette then it just became one with the clouds, gone..
