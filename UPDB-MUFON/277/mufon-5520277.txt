UFO fireball that changed direction

I was outside of Good Samaritan Hospital on Maxwell street, having a cigarette break with a nurse and a nurse tech. I was facing downtown they were facing the hospital. I was looking at my iPhone to find a link of a baby picture to show them when my cell phone went a bit crazy.. kind of like noise you see on a tv channel that isnt working! That was weird. One of my friends said " wow what is going on with your iPhone!" I looked up at him and I saw a fireball object hovering for a split second, then it moved to the left then up and to the right and then it fell in a trajectory fashion like a falling star... 
I quickly said "did you guys see that" as it disappeared behind the building. By the time they looked the big fireball looking thing disappeared to what seemed to be a trajectory decent to the north of lexington.
