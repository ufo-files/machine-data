a ball of light that looked like a star but was moving continously

I had first noticed it during near summer nights out of my window while I was lying in bed. At first, I thought it was a star and it moving was just my eyes playing tricks on me. The more I stared at it, the more it seemed to move. At one point it moved across a further span in the sky. When I realized it wasn't really a star I felt really happy knowing that I had just witnessed something that could've been an alien. I lost sight of it during cloudy nights and once a tree grew to where I can't see it anymore this year.
