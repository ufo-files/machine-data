20 foot craft hovering slowly above houses.

20 foot rectangular craft. Just after dark. I heard the distinct sound of an a.m. radio being tuned in. Looking in that direction I saw craft slowly flying above roof tops. As soon as I spotted it, the radio noises ceased and the craft came very slowly and silently right over my head. When it reached the roof area of the house I was standing at, it took off like a shot into the night sky. As it traveled off it shot two “comet like” torpedoes at the moon.
