Straight line of lights

My brother stepped out to smoke a cigarette and came back in after a minute telling me to come quick. I looked up NNW and saw a long line of white lights slowly moving SE but going directly overhead. Tried to take video on my phone but nothing showed up due to the darkness. We stood out there 15 minutes or more watching them until the line finally came to an end. Thought they were drones at first but there had to have been 50 or more of these things. DEFINITELY something out of the ordinary but there's a good bit of funky stuff in the skies around here. They didn't make much noise, just a faint hum you could barely hear.
