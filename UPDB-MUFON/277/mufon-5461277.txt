White star like object

I generally star gaze through my roof window. I observed a white star like object glow brightly in what appeared to be leaving a triangular object from the sky. This was shortly followed by at least 3 planes in pursuit and the bright light shone even brighter and then changed direction as the planes or whatever they were appeared to follow it. Within a minute it was gone.
