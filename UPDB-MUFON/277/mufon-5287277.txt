while camping at Kerr Lake- saw pulsing light in sky, recorded w/campcorder

While camping at Kerr Lake St Park, at 1pm my wife saw light/flash in the sky. It was in the distance. Almost directly north slowly moving very slowly eastward. I got camcorder and recorded as best as I could- (difficult to see moniitor on a bright day) called over 2 campers from next sight over who also confirmed. It blinked on and off- see video- just looked for "star" go frame by frame at 6/7 second mark. look at screen in center (left to right) and slightly toward top. Also frame by frame 38/39 second just to NW of center. This is not dust on lens- we were all also watching w/ naked eyes. 
I have not yet looked at all of my footage. Sorry its not better but I could not see monitor well due to sunny day.

as an aside, my wife and I also reported another sighting IN Cary, NC around Feb.
