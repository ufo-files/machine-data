{rjl} I had been looking at the sky and saw nothing until I put on glasses with photochromic lenses. Then I saw a shiny, black round object hovering above.

I was at my horse's pasture, just west of Castle Rock, CO, and had been looking west toward the mountains because the sky was orange and hazy due to a large mountain fire. I was there for about an hour, and it was dusk when I put on my eyeglasses that happen to have photochromic lenses. I was shocked to see an air ballon sized, shiny black object hovering less than 500 ft above ground. The object was roundish with irregular sides. Reminded me of a huge ink blot in the sky. No lights on it. I took my glasses off and couldn't see the object at all. I put them back on and there it was! My lenses were clean, nothing on them. I grabbed my cell phone and tried to take a photo. As I was doing that, the object started moving to the west at a fast pace and then went higher into the sky as it approached the foothills, and then disappeared. I feel like it was spying on me, shrouding, as I couldn't see it until I put my glasses on. The object didn't show up in the two photos that I took.
