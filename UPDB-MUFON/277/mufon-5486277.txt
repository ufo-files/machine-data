I was at my friends house around 15yrs ago waiting on my parents to pick me up. When my parents arrived I walked outside and something caught my eye. Flashing lights in the sky it was evening so it was dark outside, but I saw those lights flashing. So I looked at mom, an dad, and said look and they saw em to. The messed up thing is the UFO was hovering next to a highschool football game that was going on. I guess the UFO thought it was blending in with the stadium lights. But after a few minutes it was just gone poof gone. I've thought of this for a long time. Sometimes I'll ask my parents if they still remember just to make myself feel better. It was a real intense and creepy feeling when I saw it,my parents just shrug it off I think.
