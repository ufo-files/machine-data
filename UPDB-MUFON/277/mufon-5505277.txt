{rjl} red-orange glowing ball

While watching tv, noticed light in sky through closed sliding door. Went outside with my wife and we both saw what was a very bright glowing red-orange flickering object in the north sky at approx. a 45 degree angle. No noise present. Watched for about thirty seconds as it appeared to descend slightly. Then went very dim, and moved faster in a nw direction until we lost sight due to trees. At first thought it might be some sort of flare, but it accelerated after going dim with no breeze present.
