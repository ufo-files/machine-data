Bright solid lights observed in triangular shape formation

It was New Years night and my kids and I had been out watching the fireworks and had been recording fireworks videos. I first saw one object appear in the east and it immediately looked different than the normal plane or such, so I continued to watch it. I even said to my oldest son "what is that?". He said probably a plane, but as it got closer it seemed strange still , with no blinking lights and no sound. It passed but as soon as it did another appeared. It too seemed to follow same path. This happened several more times so by this time we grabbed our cameras and started video games. Then suddenly 3 appeared in a triangular shape formation never changing shape, height, or distance between the 3 points. It made no sound. They all seemed to be flying at a high rate of speed. After this passed there was 2 more single ones. Im estimating there were a total of 10.
