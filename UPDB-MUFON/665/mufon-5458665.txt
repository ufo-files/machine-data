So it was around 5 or so in the morning I was getting ready to go on a road trip. I went outside to load the car with two relatives that we’re vacationing in NY and something told me to look up at the sky and I saw four circular orbs in the sky two at the top and two at the bottom kinda like forming a square. When I saw it I pointed it out to the other two people who also saw it and about four seconds after I pointed at it more smaller circles came out of the four original ones who also became smaller. There must’ve been about 12 circles in total and they started dimming as the seconds went by until there was nothing. The whole experience was about 3 minutes or so. I tried taking a video but because the sky was dark the orbs didn’t show. However I did manage to take a picture.
