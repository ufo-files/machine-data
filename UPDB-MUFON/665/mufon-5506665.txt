It was a warm night in june or July of the summer of 1995. I was sitting at a park bench in Patterson park in Baltimore city. Im sitting there waiting on a friend, we were going out. As im sitting there waiting I hear a slight low hum. Looking around I see nothing. I light a cigarette then hum gets slightly louder. Its a small park, im the only one there. I look above me and there it is hovering approx. 500 ft directly above me. I sat there in augh!! Amazed at what I was seeing. It was circular/ oval, emitted a humming sound, bright lights circled the vehicle yellow/reds/orange. I knew it was a ufo as soon as I saw it. It just sat above me then lowered toward me some. I felt scared, worried, and unsure what would happen next. I didn't dare move. Then 3 air force jets came screaming in so fast and I know they were going as fast as they can. @ were in the lead while 1 lagged or hung back intentionally. The object upon their arrival shot straight up going in a sw direction, leaving a streak of light as it disappeared. Now looking back, I was probably about to be abducted if it werent for our boys in the air. I thank god for their presence to this day. This is the first time Ive reported this.
