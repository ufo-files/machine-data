Flashing pulsating light that hovered for approximately 45 seconds then started moving and disappeared rapidly.

On given night my wife saw a randomly pulsating/flashing light reddish/orange in color hovering. I turned around at the next street approximately 100 yards from where they saw it and I as well saw it briefly, but it was moving and disappeared rapidly. We turned around and started heading back in the direction we were heading and as we got to the intersection of Ijamsville Road and Route144, I looked back, and it was there again. I pulled to the side of the road and while we were all watching it, I called my mom we spoke for about 10 seconds and the phone beeped and went to the unlock screen (Motorola ATRIX 2on the AT&T network). Signal strength was full on mine and my wife’s phones. We called her back and the phone worked fine, and I explained to her what we were seeing. The object appeared identical and did pretty much the same thing that it did the first time. The entire incident was 20 minutes. There is no reporting of it and no aircraft were seen in the area
