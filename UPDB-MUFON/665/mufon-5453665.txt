3 lights flying in triangular formation

To the attention of the MUFON Team

Here is a summary of my experience:

I live south of Berlin in a town called Köpenick, I am often outside during the evening to smoke cigarettes. I often look at the stars in the evening as there are almost no planes or pollution in the sky during this coronavirus crisis . This day of April 19th is an especially nice day with no clouds whatsoever. 
I quickly noticed three lights flying in a triangular formation at an altitude a bit lower than commercial airliners. They were flying maybe two times quicker than normal planes, and I was surprised to see lights considering how high these "objects" were flying. 
I was unable to distinguish any shape, there was no sound.
If they were military jets but they would have made noise. 
It is possible they were military drones...but again I was surprised by the altitude and brightness of these objects. And if they were drones then they were flying very quickly! 
The object were too quick for me to be able to take any picture. In about 2-3 minutes they were out of sight. 
I also checked the flight tracking website to see if there were any planes in the area and the only ones nearby were stationary in the Schönfeld airport west of my home. 

I would really be interested if someone can maybe look into this a bit further and maybe get back to me.
