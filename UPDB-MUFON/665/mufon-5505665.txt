{rjl} Phoenix New Year 2014 UFO

Three adults and three kids in backyard of west Phoenix home. Noticed orange ball of light appearing from the North, traveling South. The light remained a spherical shape, approximately a golf-ball size - much larger than anything in the sky. The light would occasionally stop, change direction, then travel again in a southern direction. There was no noise, or any sounds coming from the area of the light. The light flickered a bit then disappeared at about 45 degrees from the southern horizon. No one in the yard had ever seen anything like it before.
