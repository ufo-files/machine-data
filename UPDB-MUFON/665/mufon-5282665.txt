There was a group of unexplained lights

My friend and I were in the Marine corps would often go to Joshua Tree to hang out. One evening we were hanging out in this one spot facing towards the base. All of a sudden we saw these lights, maybe 3-5, just sitting over the base. We were used to seeing flares so we laughed for a minute, because who would be sending off flares at that time…: they stayed still. We looked at each other and we’re like these aren’t flares. They must have stayed there for a few minutes. Then all of them vanished at one time.
