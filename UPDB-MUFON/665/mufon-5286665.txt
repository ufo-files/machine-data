There was a bright light in sky slowly moving west to east with a sudden direction change after stopping.

I witnessed after getting out of my car a bright light in sky. I was looking at first at a light lower in sky that seemed to be an airplane... usual flight pattern. Suddenly I also saw another object higher in sky that was moving in the opposite direction of the first one. The second one was much brighter. As I was watching this one, it suddenly stopped then it went 'down' in a 90 degree direction from it's original stop point. It then started to move in the totally opposite direction apparently going back to where it had started. It flew for a few seconds and increasing in speed. This was when I started to become upset/frightened and walked really quickly to the front door of my workplace. There was no other person there with me since I am the opening cook on this day. and the time was about 4:30 am. I've only seen one other unexplained object in the sky which almost made me drive off the street and into the sidewalk along with another driver who was in the next lane to me. I was also going to work that first time as well.. I work at a military base. I've never reported that first time officially, only have told a few of my friends. After this second event, I am finding myself looking up at the sky before I to go work and trying to research what it could be. i have a friend who also reported his experience and this is why I am now doing this too myself. I want to stay anonymous.
