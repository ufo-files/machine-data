Beam of light over the mark twain lake

Driving home from weekly game night sunday Feb 2 2020 i saw a beam over the mark twain lake area. I was coming across the bridge on highway 154 from Paris to perry Mo when i was a beam of light. It was blue green in color almost like the northern lights. It shined straight up and down from the sky to what appeared to be part of the lake it was only their for a second and was not able to tell what direction it was going ( ground to sky or sky to ground). From where I was on the bridge it was about as wide as my finger and about 1 to 1 and a half miles from me. I would estimate it was about 100 feet across. I didn't see a craft but that area of the sky was cloudy. This was not weather,heat lightning or any other natural phenomenon that I know to be common in our area
