I dont know if its ok that i'm writing this.
I was awake studying for my post phd exam.
I did what i used to do every night. I went out in front of the house and stand right beside the dry river that is just in front of the house.
That s when it happend , i saw a red light in distance, it came closer and closer. 
OMG i still get goosebumps when i think about it. I couldnt move at all, i mean i was just like statue staring to that thing. I am usually very brave compared to my friends and family but that was the most frightening event of my life.
It the started scattering a red light over the area, i mean everywhere was red.
After a minute it came up and just disappeard in a second, i mean the way it disappeard was even scary, like blinking.
I have never said this to anyone cause i think it will probably sound crazy.
I didnt know what to do, who to tell. I just found this site and reported what happened.
I also never went back to that spot for smoking !
Anyway, just writing about it here made me feel better already. Thank you.
