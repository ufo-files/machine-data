A Wight light that got brighter, before it got brighter you could make out the shape

I wish the remain anonymous due to the fact I am a defence contractor with MOD SC clearance. I live near BAe Warton Preston, Lancashire.

I was walking back to my car on Bank Lane after seeing an old acquaintance.
The first thing I noticed was the light travelling very slowly making a very low HZ hum noise, the light went out but I could make out the shape of a triangle. It was hard to say the exact altitude or size the vehicle was.

I am very well knowledgeable about civilian and air force jets, both fixed wing and rotating.

The object had no port or starboard lights and was pretty much silent apart from a slight low frequency homing.

When the light lit up again it was very bright and headed off at an extreme speed in a south west direction. It was out of sight within 1.5 to 2 seconds.

The questions below meant I had no time to capture the event however, I will re visit the same location ready tonight.
