{td} Abduction dream. Very lucid. 1st experience of its kind for me.

Awoke from a difficult sleep paralyzed to notice two insect-like humanoids. Each had a opaque, large, triangular eyes and from an aesthetic standpoint were hideous. They hung their arms in front of them dangling. Their legs appeared very muscular but emaciated otherwise. The skin had a rippled or more likely wrinkled appearance. I felt little fear surprisingly whether this was a dream or not. Both stared at me for an unknown period of time. After a while one of them, for some reason I felt as if he was 'outranked', crouched beside my bed while the other simply watched. I remember that within my mind I yelled at them and said, 'wait,' and then followed with 'help us. ' as in help humanity through this troubled era or something? Maybe I saw them as some technological Messiah or something. But upon stating that the smaller (by no means small) one looked over to the tall one. I have no memory of waking the next day. No activity since this date (the exact day is approximate) but time and month are reliable. Just thought I would share this.
