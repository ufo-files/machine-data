I saw an emerald colored green flashing craft crossing above the mid Hudson bridge. It traveled very fast to the ulster county side then abruptly moved straight up then came directly toward me. It stopped at 2 o-clock then hovered there for some minutes. Then I noticed a swirling of the light mist above me on two sides, Then a triangular craft with three dim white lights, traveled silently over the willow tree I was standing near at about the tree top level. I chose to stay with the hovering green craft which hovered for a couple more minutes then slowly moved off behind the tree line. While leaving the park I noticed a bright white object that looked like a bright star. It suddenly "went out" completely and did not return.
