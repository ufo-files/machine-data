moving star with light variations

It was the start of school for my son and he gets picked up by the bus very early in the morning. I was waiting outside in the driveway with him looking up at the stars. I asked him if he had ever seen the big or little dippers and he said no. I found one, (which one i don't know)and was looking for the other to show him. I was looking at these three stars in a row that curved like the handle of a dipper and before I could look for the pan part the first one started to grow brighter and brighter. It was like it was on a dimmer switch and someone turned it up relatively slowly. It got probably 10-15 times as bright as the other stars and then in a snap went back down to original brightness. Before I cound get the thought through my head that I had prob. seen a exploding star it started to move. It moved about 3-4 inches in the sky from its original position and then was gone. It took about 5-7 seconds to make that distance from my estimation. Whether it went out or moved out of sight I cannot say but it was gone and a space left dark where I had just seen a star. Im not saying it was E.T. but I am very courious to know what I saw. If there is an explaination I would be greatful for a reply. I did call the news weather team later that day and reported it - they had no clue and said no one else had made a report. They did say that that distance in the sky would be very, very great for an object to move. I know what I saw.
