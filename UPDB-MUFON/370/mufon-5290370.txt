Very oddly shaped grey craft slowly traversing the sky and turning.

While driving South down Whitworth Road at approx 1300 I happened to glance up and notice a very oddly shaped craft seem to drift across the road at an altidude of somewhere between 200-300 feet. It was moving like a balloon but was quite unlike any I have ever seen. Being a dark grey in colour and shaped like a blocky, asymmetrical boomerang, it appeared to slowly turn as it drifted across the sky. Being somewhat taken aback I pulled over and exited my car. It was silent and soon vanished over the rooftops heading in a roughly SE direction. I jumped back in my car and attempted to follow it at the next junction but by the time I pulled over again, it had disappeared. I attempted to follow it's heading on foot but alas, it had gone.
