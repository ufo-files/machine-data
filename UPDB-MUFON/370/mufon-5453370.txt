OBJECTS MOVING UP AND DOWN SIDE TO SIDE RAPIDLY WITH BRIGHT LIGHTS

SINCE 2/20/20, I HAVE SEEN OBJECTS IN THE SKY ABOVE WHERE I LIVE THAT APPEAR AT NIGHT AND HOVER OVER THE AREA ABOVE MY HOUSE, THEY HAVE LIGHTS ON THEM WHICH FLASH, PULSATE, CIRCLE AROUND THE OBJECT BRIGHTEN AND DIM AND TURN OFF COMPLETELY INTERMITTENTLY. IT SEEMS TO BE AROUND 2230 THE BRIGHTEST ONE PRESENT, ALWAYS IN THE SAME GENERAL POSITION, DESCENDS DOWN TO RIGHT ABOVE THE MOUNTAIN BY BLUE DIAMOND AND THEN APPEARS TO GO BEHIND THE MOUNTAIN. THERE ARE USUALLY AT LEAST 15-20 OF THEM , ALL AT DIFFERENT ELEVATIONS. SOME ARE ACTUALLY PRETTY HIGH, IT IS HARDER TO SEE THEM. BUT THEY MOVE IN WAYS TYPICAL AIRCRAFT DO NOT MOVE. THEY MAKE NO NOISE AND THEY DON'T FLY ONWARD TO A DESTINATION, THEY JUST APPEAR EVERY NIGHT AND STAY IN THE SKY FOR SEVERAL HOURS IF NOT THE WHOLE NIGHT(SOME OF THEM). THE BRIGHTEST ONE ALWAYS SEEMS TO RETIRE FOR THE EVENING AROUND 2230, THE REST OF THEM THAT ARE NOT EVER AS BRIGHT AND APPEAR TO BE FURTHER AWAY ARE USUALLY ALWAYS THERE WHEN IT IS DARK NOW. I HAVE A ROOFTOP PATIO AND HAVE BEEN OBSERVING THEM EVERY NIGHT NOW SINCE I FIRST SAW THEM. I KNOW FOR SURE THEY ARE NOT AIRPLANES BECAUSE LIKE I SAID TYPICAL AIRPLANES DON'T MOVE THAT WAY AND BECAUSE THEY DO NOT CONTINUE ON TO A DESTINATION, THEY JUST STAY THERE IN PLACE HOVERING. THESE OBJECTS ARE VERY CONCERNING , I WOULD LIKE TO KNOW WHY THEY ARE THERE AND WHAT THEY WANT. I WOULD REALLY JUST LIKE THEM TO GO AWAY BUT SINCE THAT DOESN'T SEEM TO BE HAPPENING, I WOULD LIKE TO KNOW WHAT THEY WANT AND IF THIS IS OUR MILITARY SOMEHOW WITH ADVANCED AIRCRAFT , IT IS PRETTY MESSED UP THAT THEY DON'T LET ANYONE KNOW ABOUT THIS AND LEAVE US TO BE CONCERNED AS TO WHAT THE HELL THE OBJECTS ARE AND WHAT THEY WANT. AT LEAST, IF IT WAS THE MILITARY, WE COULD UNDERSTAND THAT AND GO ON WITH OUR LIVES NOT BEING SO CONCERNED.
