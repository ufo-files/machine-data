{jpg} Witnessed object hovering over a ridge on the wasatch front for seven minutes.

I had gotten home early from work at 2:30 in the morning. My wife got up and we were talking together about finances around 3:00. My wife glanced out the window facing east during our discussion and said "Oh my god, I'm a crazy person." Joining her in looking outside my eyes were draw up to a ridge on the Wasatch front with a really bright light stationed just above the ridge. 

It was as bright as an arc welder as it illuminated a large circle of ridge around it allowing the scrub oak on the ridge side to cast shadows. After a few minutes of not being able to locate our camera I just watched with my wife as this bright light stayed there hovering at what seemed to be fifteen feet above the ridge. The center had more of a red like glow to it as the exterior was bright white. It was exciting to see as I had never seen something like this before. I knew it wasn't the radio transmitter station which was further down the ridge towards the university of Utah so I could not logically explain this light at three in the morning. 

It hung there for at least five or seven minutes like a balloon before it took off. It went straight up a hundred feet or so within a second before there was a slight flash and it was gone. I have tried to logically explain this one in my mind but helicopters and balloons don't take off like that. 

Our discussion about finances took a back seat to the experience as I watched the ridge for another fifteen minutes hoping to see something more. Tired and in need of rest we went to bed after.
