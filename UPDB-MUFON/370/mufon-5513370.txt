{dr}Large, star-like objects with a small trail behind them traveled NNW over Royal Palm Beach, Florida

I was cleaning up inside my home when my brother-in-law started knocking on the window from outside. I ignored the knocking but went outside after he started calling me and I heard my sister-in-law and other people making a bit of a ruckus. I went outside, they asked me to look in the sky, and I saw a small, redish object traveling north north west. After this, we saw about six or so more of them. I cant be sure how many were around before and after we went inside...

Being January 1st shortly after midnight, I first figured it was some type of firework or flare. However, these lights did not have those characteristics. And we agreed that the objects were moving too slowly to be flares. They moved across the sky for a very long distance. I don't know much about flares but what we saw was very similar to the following report later on in the evening from a location in the direction they were heading:

http://mufoncms.com/cgi-bin/report_handler.pl?req=view_long_desc&id=44882
http://www.mufoncms.com/files/44882_submitter_file1__Jan010159AM.mov
http://www.mufoncms.com/files/44882_submitter_file2__Jan010203AM.mov

The objects were going in and out of clouds but seemed to eventually go higher to a point where we could no longer seem them - probably due to clouds.

I certainly wont say these lights definitely were NOT flares because I don't know what the latest technologies are surrounding them. However, I wanted to submit this report in the event enough people saw them and can read my experience with them as a reference...
