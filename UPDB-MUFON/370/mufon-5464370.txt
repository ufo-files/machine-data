{rjl} Several red orb like spheres flying in a V-like formation in sets of 3.

After leaving my post one night at midnight, I approached an intersection and got into the turning lane. As I entered the turning lane, I witnessed the car turning in the adjacent turning lane peering outside of their window up at the sky. The car ahead of me in the same turning lane as myself also peered out of their window before turning. Once I came to a stop for my turn, to make a turn right, I witnessed what the others seemed to look at. Up in the sky, directly above the intersection stop lights, crossing from one side of the road near and above a Publix, then passing over a McDonalds across the street, before disappearing into the night, were between 6-9 glowing red spherical orbs. If I had to guess I would say the size of a basketball or slightly larger-considering height-distance. They flew in formation, without ever breaking or moving out of sequence, 3 per set, like a V-shape. I was able to take a few pictures with my cell phone, but it is low quality night pix. I will include them here. I made my turn once they disappeared. I considered the possibility that they could be drones, but dismissed this, because I did not see any groups of people nearby with remotes, the fact that it was night, the size and the fixed, unwavering mobility flying in formation.
