{nd} Hovering light with ascending and descending movements and a sharp fast right turn, lasted about 30 minutes

I was sitting on the porch watching fireworks when I pointed out to the 15/20 people here what appeared to be a bright light (appeared to be a star) we watched as it hovered in the same place for five minutes before the object began to move downward at a slow decent, after about 30 seconds it stopped and hovered again for about twenty minutes then made a very fast dive and stopped well short of the ground made a extremely fast eastward turn and streaked across our line of view and out of sight. Total sighting lasting about 30 minutes and cell video taken did not show the object. There was a storm that had just passed and the cloud ceiling was at about 1000 ft above sea level. There were Chinese lanterns in the sky all night so we could observe wind pattern which made the paper lanterns fly in an opposite direction, and light source in lanterns was obviously fire whereas the object was a constant brightness although it was seen by others to change colors (I am colorblind and did not notice the change). I watched the object the whole time as I thought it looked out of place with there being no stars due to the cloud cover.
