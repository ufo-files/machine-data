Diamond shaped white light over the ocean then over ridge top where it sat and went off and on for hours

On May 4th,2010 at 9:00pm,me and a fellow worker came out back of our grocery store that sits on the ocean to smoke a cig,when we noticed an extremely bright diamond shape light at low altitude,way to bright to be a star or aircraft,it just sat there for about 30 minutes,but i had to go back in the store to go to work and asked my fellow employee to let me know if it moved,about 15 minutes later he told me it was gone.We then went back outside to look for it and saw it sitting over the ridge top going off and coming back on for 1 hour and a half.When it would go off a dim red glow was visible,but it never moved,it was the whitest light we had ever seen,we went home around midnight, we both feel certain this was a UFO.
