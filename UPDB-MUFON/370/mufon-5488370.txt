{nd} It happened during a rocket launch with satellites aboard. Rocket was launched from Virginia.

A friend of mine and I decided to go outside work to watch the rocket launch that was taking satellites to space. It was being launched out of Virginia. We figured correctly that we should be able to see it. During the launch as the rocket was ascending, something caught my eye from the left. We were facing east, so this was north of me. I looked over and saw three giant lights in a triangle formation, moving very slowly and silently. It was dark so all I could see were the lights. I tapped my friend’s arm and pointed to it. It appeared to be no more than 1000 feet off the ground and huge. It moved very slowly and took over a minute before it was out of sight beyond the tree line. We both noted that there was absolutely no sound coming from it. We both figured it was some kind of unknown government aircraft. I did make a post on my Facebook page about our sighting, but nothing else. Currently I'm watching a show called UFO hunters, episode, Great Triangles, and realized they were describing exactly what we saw. This is what prompted me to contact your organization. With a little digging into my FB account, or reference the satellite launch you should be able to figure out the time and date it happened. I believe it was in October of 2013, and if I remember correctly the rocket had 29 satellites on board.
