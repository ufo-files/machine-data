{nd} Scoop mark on leg, subsequent discovery of

While staying at a friends home in Montana in June of 1993, awoke one day to discover classic scoop mark on left shin, five inches below the knee. Mark was half inch by three eights and oval in shape, color was taupe, surface of scoop was indented with a thin layer of taupe colored skin, showing a few wrinkles when touched. Doctor examined it and could not explain it, told me to watch it. It faded away with the skin thickening and leveling out in about a year. Forward thirteen years when Google earth became available. I looked for my friends property, which is a large ranch, and discovered a strange, bright object on the ground near the home, in the 1995 data base, two years after my scoop mark appearence. The ground near this object is covered in a series of circular marks, strung out like hop landings. This is the only year this object is seen on Google Earth. I feel there is a connection between this object and my experience. Both myself and my friend had UFO sighting experiences in the past, prior to my visit. I will try to attach the Goole Earth page image, it is quite interesting! It is twelve feet across.
