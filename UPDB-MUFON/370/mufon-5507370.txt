{cak} Shocking. Exciting

My 13-year old cousin and I were playing a game of basketball in his backyard at his residence in West Covina, California when he asks out of the blue: Whats that? pointing to the sky. At first I thought he was trying to fool me so I didn't actually turn around and look until suddenly, he threw the ball aside and pointed once again to the sky begging me to look. To my surprise, it was something I have never seen before; there were about fifteen orange spheres that were glowing a bit. More appeared and they vanished almost as soon as they came. I thought the objects were legitimate UFOs judging by their formation, there were 3 of them in a straight line very compact while a set of 7-8 resembled the formation of the Big Dipper and not so compact. The UFOs were very far from where I witnessed (unsure maybe over 20 miles) yet they managed to cover some serious distance. They hovered and some went their own paths but all eventually disappeared out of thin air. Luckily, 4 of the 15 family and friends watching the incident recorded the event. There were some mixed emotions among us; some excited and amazed while the kids confused and scared. The sighting lasted about 12 minutes, from first laying eyes on them to the last one vanishing. (15 witnesses)
