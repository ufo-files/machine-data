{nd} A red/orange light over tree tops heading SW, no sound, oval shaped . . wow

4-25-2013 I stepped outside to smoke a cigarette and noticed a red/orange light in the North East sky just above the tree tops. At first I thought it was airplane lights then realized there were no white lights and the light was not blinking. I thought maybe it was a drone since Dobbins Air Force Base is in the direction it came from but drones make noise. I ran inside to grab my binoculars and got a clear view of it. All I can type is, wow. As it continued South West the light just faded away.
An oval shape with a matte like exterior that reflected the red/orange just a little.
The light underneath was at the front of the of it.
The flight path was straight and steady. It took about one or two minutes to travel from where I first saw it till the light faded out . . . no sound at all.
