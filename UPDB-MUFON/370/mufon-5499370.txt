{nd} Two bright yellow-orange lights stationary in sky for 5-10 seconds. Reappearing in same place 10 minutes later. Also appeared in a different spot again after 10 minutes. All three sightings lasted only 5-10 seconds each.

Three of us were watching the sky up in the BWCA on a clear night, when we saw two large bright round yellow lights. They shone themselves for about 5-10 seconds and then shut off one after the other. Ten minutes later, three lights appeared in a different spot above the tree line. Then again, after about ten minutes they reappeared in the original space, but this time there were at least three bright lights or objects. Each time they only appeared for 5-10 seconds. They were a bright yellow-orange color and definitely bigger than an airplane or other known flying object.
