Saw three bright lights - middle of the day - over the desert (CA/NV area) while in flight

While flying in a plane from Houston Texas to Los Angeles, CA I saw three bright lights - middle of the day - over the desert (CA/NV area) while in flight. It was obvious that these lights hovered over the mountains / in the sky / did not appear to be moving, and were far too bright to stadium lights, or any other type of man made/ reflective building lights. It was over the desert, middle of no where - these lights were in the sky (Three in a triangle formation) I do not have specifics as to distance from the plane. I took photos. I wanted to ask the pilots what all they saw while flying, however, pilots were gone by the time I was exiting the plane.
