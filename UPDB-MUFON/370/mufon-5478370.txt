{jpg} Simply seen a bright light floating in my direction. It passed slightly over head then dissolved into thin air once it passed me.

I was walking in my driveway from my apartment to my car when I looked up and noticed a bright light in the sky coming my way. It was as bright as Venus. At first I thought it was an airplane so I got into my car and started the engine. I looked back to see it again but noticed that it was not blinking like an airplane. So I got out of my car, pulled out my cellphone and started recording it. I zoomed in and that's when I realized that it was "ring shaped". It was hollow in the middle, and made no sound what so ever. It continued in a straight path from west to north east but had a little wobble in it's movements. It was floating as high as an airplane would and as I looked around for other objects in the sky, I did notice a few airplanes. The airplanes were behaving as normal with their blinking lights and all. I had my camera fixed on the unidentified flying object the whole time until it flew out of sight. I shut off the camera, got back into my car and drove down the driveway. At that point, I could still see the object flying away in the north east direction. A few treetops got in the way of my view point but it did come into clear view and simply dissolved into thin air. It was not moving fast like a meteor or shooting star. It was slow paced. I uploaded the video to my youtube channel.
