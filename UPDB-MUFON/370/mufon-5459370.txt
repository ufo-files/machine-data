{tjd} Small, fast ufo sighted in Chattanooga area

My husband and I were running out to grab a quick bite to eat. It was around 9:15PM Friday night, July 19th. I was driving south on South Mack Smith Road in the East Ridge area of Chattanooga. 

In my peripheral vision I noticed a bright light to the west, traveling east. I saw what I assumed was a plane, we are near an airport, but noticed it reversed and starting traveling in the opposite direction VERY QUICKLY, something airplanes do not do. It also dissapeared and reappeared twice.

It was getting dark, we were unable to tell the shape of the craft, mainly saw the light. I'm not sure of the elevation, I put "0" as I had to make an entry. It looked higher than what airplanes travel.
