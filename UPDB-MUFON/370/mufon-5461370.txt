{cak} Four intensely bright star like objects weaving and darting

Noticed what appeared to be a small drone. No jet stream of sound. Shortly after drone flew past, there appeared 4 intensely bright stars/orbs. This is at 220pm, bright sunny day, blue sky. The 4 "stars" seemed to circle each other. Slowly Circling around each other. At one point, one orb remained stationary while another seemed to merge with it. Then they split again and continued to circle each other. Reminiscent of hawks flying in close circles, but intertwining flight paths. It honestly seemed playful in nature. Like playing the game of tag. The brightness got fainter and the orbs grew smaller as they rose higher. Then all four disappeared.
