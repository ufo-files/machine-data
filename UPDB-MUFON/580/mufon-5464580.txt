A BRIGHT ORANGE SPHERE EMITTING NO SOUND THAT LEFT ME FROZEN WHEN PASSED OVERHEAD, AND CERTAINLY NOT A CHINESE LANTERN!!

UPON RETURNING HOME FROM THE SHOP WITH MY 7 YEAR OLD DAUGHTER THAT SEPTEMBER EVENING, AS THE SUN HAD JUST SET, I NOTICED A BRIGHT ORANGE LIGHT IN THE DISTANCE HEADING OUR WAY. 

AT FIRST, I THOUGHT IT WAS ONE OF THOSE CHINESE LANTERNS BUT IT WAS MOVING QUITE RAPIDLY TO BE ONE OF THOSE!

THE OBJECT, THAT I COULD ONLY DESCRIBE AS AN ORANGE ORB OF SUBSTANTIAL SIZE WASN'T EMITTING ANY SOUND OR PULSATION. 

AS IT APPROACHED AND THEN PASSED STRAIGHT OVERHEAD, MY DAUGHTER AND I WERE LITERALLY MESMERISED BY IT, FROZEN ON OUR FEET. 

ONLY WHEN IT DISAPPEARED OVER THE ROW OF HOUSES BEHIND US THAT I SUDDENLY "WOKE UP" AND REMEMBERED I HAD MY MOBILE PHONE WITH ME AND MANAGED TO GET A COUPLE OF SHOTS AT IT. BY THAT TIME, IT WAS QUITE FAR AWAY, HIGHER UP IN THE SKY AND ACCELERATING.

I JUST HOPE YOU WILL SOMEHOW FIND THE PHOTOGRAPHS I HAVE ATTACHED USEFUL...
