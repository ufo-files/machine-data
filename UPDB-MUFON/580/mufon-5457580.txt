Bright diamond shape objects in the skies

I was walking with a friend along the beach when we stopped for a rest. I looked up the skies and saw hundreds if not thousands of diamond shape objects emitting a flickering blight lights hovering from inland towards the sea. They were from different sizes. Some bigger than others.
