{jpg} Object was detected by Ring security camera moving around the backyard.

This is our secondary home in Central Oregon and we were not present at the time of this event. We have Ring security cameras around the perimeter of the house. The east facing camera was activated at 0601 hrs. on the morning of December 14, 2019. At first I thought it might be a spider web across the camera. As I watched the object, it appeared to move slowly to the south behind a group of juniper trees that are located about 10-15 feet away from the camera that is on the eave of the house. It then moved about 20 feet in a north easterly direction toward the top of another juniper tree, remained there briefly and then very quickly shot upward out of view of the camera. I've never captured anything like this on our security cameras. Other security cameras came on around the house because they are configured to do so. I looked at the video from each and could not find any other evidence.
