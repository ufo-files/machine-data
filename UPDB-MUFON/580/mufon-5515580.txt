(GS) Three dim white lights that were rotating.

Me and husband had been out that Saturday night we were on the way home going down cone blvd. in Greensboro NC at about 1:20am Sunday morning. We had to stop at a stop light and when we did something caught my eye so i looked up in the sky and i saw three lights turning in a circle. the stop light turned green so i went and i was saying to my husband what is that i need to go back and see where those lights are coming from. so i did they had not moved. we stopped and looked for about 5 min. it kept doing the same thing over and over so i said lets go home. so we went home about three miles down the road when we got there you could still see the lights. at home we watched it for about 20 min. and it never moved it just kept rotating in a circle. the lights were not very bright they looked as if they were behind the clouds. you couldnt see a beam or anything that indicated were the light was coming from. this was the first time i have ever seen anything like this before.
