4 or 5 solid red lights in a row with a flashing white light on the rear

At 4:30 in the morning, I let my dog outside. I was standing at the door waiting for him to come back in when I saw 4 or 5 solid red lights in a row with a flashing white light on the rear. It was traveling faster than I have ever seen an airplane travel over my house and appeared a lot closer. It was traveling from the North West to the South East. I watched it until it was no longer visible because of the roof of my house. I checked with an online satellite website and found there was no aircraft in the immediate area. I printed a copy of the radar image and the flight number of the closest aircraft. The weather was 33 degrees F. with clear skies.
