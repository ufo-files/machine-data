Popped up before my eyes

I was alone in the living room on the 5th floor of a high-rise. Lights out and observing the dark sky. Suddenly, the light pops up and then another SW of it but further away. It did not move but did change shape. It went into two then back to one. Also, it went like a thin line once. My camera kept jumping as I zoomed.
