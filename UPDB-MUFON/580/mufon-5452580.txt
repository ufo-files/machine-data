I saw a spherical river object on a straight descent that travelled across the freeway until lost from view behind trees.

I was traveling westbound on I-270 in Worthington, Ohio from Westerville when I saw a silver spherical object traveling north. It was low on the horizon following a straight line descent. At first I thought it was miles away but as it descended, it crossed in front of Worthington Industries and that's when I realized it was much closer to me than I had initially thought. My wife was in the passenger seat although she did not see it. I asked her to look as we passed the area to see if she could see anyone flying a remotely controlled drone. She did not see anyone. I drove through the complex of buildings later in the day to see if there was a public park there where someone might fly remotely controlled craft and observed that the area was fenced off with no public admittance.
