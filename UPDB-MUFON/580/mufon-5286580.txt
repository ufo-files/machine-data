Three object entering the atmosphere at a steep angle and in direct line

I spend a considerable amount of time viewing satellites/ISS/space debris flyover on the back deck at my home. I live in a rural area miles away from the nearest town, so I have excellent conditions for viewing. I often use the Heavens-Above app on my phone to locate and view flyovers from satellites/ISS and various orbiting debris. Tonight while doing some viewing, I witnessed something I had never seen before. I saw what appeared to be a small meteor entering at a very steep angle. I am confident it was entering at a steep angle, because it left a very short trail before fading out. Within a few seconds of viewing this, a second object did the exact same thing in the exact same location. Within a few seconds, a third object did the exact same thing in the exact same location as the first two. If it was simply three pieces of space debris entering the atmosphere, they were coming in at nearly 90 degrees, in a straight line and equally distanced. I am 61 years old, a retired nuclear chemist. I have viewed several things over the years that I could not explain, but this truly has me baffled. I figured I would share it with you guys, and I hope someone else might have seen it and will report it.
