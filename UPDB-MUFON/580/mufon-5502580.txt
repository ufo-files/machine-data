{rjl} Red Star thought was Mars, So wrong..

My husband and I were standing outside smoking (around 1015pm), and we noticed a red/orange star so we thought. Well I kept telling him, it is moving very slow, It would move down and to the right. When my husband looked he said I was seeing things, but then it got very dim and small and disappeared. A few seconds later it was to the left of the star I was using to pinpoint it and was very bright and larger. We didn't understand what was happening and kept watching it. And it would all of a sudden be to the right of this star, get dim and reappear on the other side. We decided to take out binoculars to see if we could see anything (obviously we knew we couldn't) but we noticed that this red (star) had two glowing things by it that were a lot smaller then the main object. The funny thing is..this was the second night I noticed it, and the first night my husband finally believed me. During this, I was very uneasy and had a scared feeling going on, while my husband wanted to shine a flashlight at it, I told him no because I felt like whatever it was would then know we were watching it.
