(GS) Arrived at guard shack when I got back saw two objects rise

While at work on Patrol duty I went to the front guard shack and checked in when I came back outside and sat in the truck I observed two large objects glowing reddish orange ascend, one on the left height then the right one and once they got so far up the left object hovered until the right one was next to it and the both were hovering and then left one led the way going in a South Easterly direction quickly disappearing.
