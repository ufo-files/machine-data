{rjl} Two orange orbs, one ended up being a chopper

Was turning around on the service drive to go east on I-96 and head to work at 5:30am and saw these 2 lights. I pulled across into the parking lot of the strip mall to observe. There were 2 large lights, top one was twice as bright and a brilliant orange. Top light was hovering and then cut right and I could see a silver dot, then it disappeared (no blinking lights). The bottom light appeared to come towards where I was at and then also turned right to where I could make out blinking aviation lights and determined this one was a chopper. The orb and chopper appeared to be in the direction of Ann Arbor or Canton, from my perspective in Livonia.
