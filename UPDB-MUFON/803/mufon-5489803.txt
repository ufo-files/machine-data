{jpg} Watched triangle craft hover for several moments then fly over my car, it changed direction so quickly and vanished.

I was on my way home from dropping off a co-worker after work. I was driving down Rancier Ave. when I saw a strange light hovering a little past the park. At first I thought it could be a helicopter, since I live in Killeen,TX rite next to Ft Hood and there are always helicopters in the air. As I got closer I could tell it was a triangle shaped craft not a helicopter, I couldn't hear any noise from it. I pulled into the park to continue watching it. It stayed in the same place for about 15 minutes, then slowly moved over the park. I opened the sunroof in my car to watch it as it slowly flew rite over my head. Then it changed direction from heading west going parallel with Rancier to going South-West. A few seconds after it made the turn it vanished.
