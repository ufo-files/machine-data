Fast moving luminescent boomerang shaped object flying overhead from SE to NW then disappeared.

I was in downtown Oakland at the art murmur on friday night. I was walking back to my car when caught a bright flicker in the corner of my vision coming from above. I happened to look up. I saw what appeared to be a glowing boomerang shaped object flying from the southeast to the northwest at 90 degrees to my location almost straight up. It was moving at a very high rate of speed, approximately twice as fast as a commercial airliner. There was no sound. I observed the object for about 1 to 2 seconds then it disappeared. At first I thought it was a plane and it could have been something military but because it disappeared, it was 'unidentifiable'.
