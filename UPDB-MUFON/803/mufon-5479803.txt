{nd} White lenticular shaped craft appeared and disappeared in less than two seconds

My wife and I were waiting for the 16 bus to go home after being at the Grove. There was another, younger man waiting with us. Suddenly from out of thin air comes this white disc. It's flight path took it behind a telephone pole across the street, but it never came out the other side. The young gentleman asked me if I had seen it. I asked him what he saw, which was close to what I had seen, but he felt the color was more green, but agreed on the silence, speed and zero contrails or sparks. I have seen bolides many times and I guarantee it was no bolides.
