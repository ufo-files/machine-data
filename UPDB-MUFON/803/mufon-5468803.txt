Orbs of light hovering whilst morphing into more orbs and pulsating

I was at home (apartment level 3) at around 22:00 in December 2009,I had recently just bought a digital camera (Panasonic Lumix 12mp digital) and was in my room testing my new device when I peeked out from my curtains and saw the UFO(s). The UFO(s) were clear and bright and was bright red/orange in colour. I immediately moved to my kitchen window to observe and film it. I instantly knew that it wasn't a plane nor was it a helicopter because there were 3 orbs of light that was stationary yet hovering above the night skies. As I continued to record the object in question, the 3 orbs of light looked as if they absorbed each other or hovered closer to each other. A few seconds into filming, I momentarily lost sight of the object(s) due to shaky hands but caught sight of it again on video shortly after. The initial 3/4 orbs became 8-10 orbs, hovering around one another. Its worth noting that a larger orb/UFO was in the middle of all the other smaller red orbs, the orbs seem to hover and move around the bigger UFO. In terms of feeling or reaction, I can say that I was just in shock and amazed from viewing something so strange, because of all places that UFOs might appear, I never expected it to be in Malaysia. I've never heard of any legit UFO sightings in my country, I was just happy that I caught it on camera. Apart from momentarily losing sight of the object in the video only for a number of seconds (due to shaky hands as mentioned above), I didn't actually lose sight of the UFO entirely, I just ended filming so i could show my family members what I caught.
