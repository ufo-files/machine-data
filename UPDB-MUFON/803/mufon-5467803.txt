{rjl} Saw bright, diamond shape object flying around sky for several minutes.

My mom and I were in a parking lot, and above the bright lights, we noticed a bright white object, sometimes diamond-shaped, sometimes looked like a triangle. It flew in circles, ascended and then descended to above the trees, then up again. My mom had her phone and took the video. The object looked like it had a tail. It looked a little like a kite but the tail was not a string or thin, but it was thick, like another part of the object. It was already getting dark and it was too bright to be a kite. I think it had to be a UFO.
