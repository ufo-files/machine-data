{cak} Solid, white orb flying faster than a plane could in the same direction as our car.

We (my grandparents and I) were driving to Great Lakes mall, and the sky was just so beautiful I couldn't help looking at it almost the entire time. Imagine my surprise when I saw a tiny little white bubble thing floating between two clouds. It was moving in the same direction we were and completely solid in appearance. It couldn't have been a plane, planes from such a distance don't look like orbs. So it wasn't hard for me to assume the thing was a UFO at first sight. I remember feeling really hyper, and my heart started beating quite fast when I realised what it actually had to have been! The object traveled in the same direction we were until it disappeared behind another cloud at the end of the gap.
