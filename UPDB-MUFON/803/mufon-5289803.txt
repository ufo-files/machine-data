Bright ball of light ascended upward and stayed for 2 hours

NIGHT 1 of 3: Our car alarm went off, waking my son and I. Car was parked in locked garage; no sign of entry. I checked the ring camera and observed a bright ball of light in the night sky. When I looked out the window, I could not see the light even though I was looking at a live view on the camera. In reviewing the camera footage, the light ascended from the tree line to a higher point, where it stayed stationary for 2 hours then went up and off the screen. From 11:30 pm to 1:30 am
NIGHT 2: Neighbors car alarm went off at 4 am. No sign of tampering. Brief flash of the same light on the ring. 
NIGHT 3: Bright light again ascended from the tree line and stayed in the sky for approximately 2 hours then went up and out. From 2:20 am to 4:10 am
