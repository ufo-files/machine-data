Shaped like a rounded, less angular and more wide boomerang.

I was on a walk north on Marion with a beautiful clear night sky above. Saw a very large object ascend above the trees and houses from the NW. It moved absolutely silently over Marion road and swept on a continuous path to the SE. It appeared to be very large and about 0.5 mi to 1.0 mi away from me, not quite overhead. It was shaped like a rounded, less angular boomerang. It had many small, randomly positioned white lights, not bright. The effect of the lights caused the ufo to resemble delicate lace at times, with the body of the object seemingly invisible. The object moved to the SE and I tried to see its silhouette in the brighter sky in that direction, but it had disappeared. The whole sighting lasted about 10 to 12 seconds.
