Two fuzzy yellow-orange orbs moving at the same fixed speed and altitude.

At 8:25 pm I was looking out the kitchen window looking due north, when my attention was drawn to two unusual lights to the northwest, which had fixed speed, altitude, and proximity to each other, moving ESE with an estimated speed, and elevation of 180-220 knots at 1800-2300 feet AGL . Because I live five miles almost due south of Rocky Mountain Regional Airport (BJC), and 20 miles west of Denver International Airport (DIA), I am quite familiar with the approach and departure patterns of both of these airports. Both of these objects were in in close enough proximity to myself, and BJC that their AC, strobe and (or) landing lights should have clearly been discernable. No nav lights were visible, as well. 

I ran out the back door, still looking due north, raised my iPhone, and as I was attempting to swipe my phone into video mode, the two lights instantly and simultaneously vanished. The wind speed at my location was calm, with partly cloudy skies, and a cloud ceiling of about 15,000 feet. These objects made no audible sound, which is unusual, as I can currently hear the aircraft departing from DIA, and the one just recently observed moving from the direction of BJC. It is of my opinion that these two objects were not civilian operated drones, based upon airspeed and altitude.
