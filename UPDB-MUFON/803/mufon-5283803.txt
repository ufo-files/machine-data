Bright star like light object emitted 6 lights over water

It was roughly 2 O’clock in the morning. The room was dark and I was talking with a friend. 
My friend, T, noticed something in the sky and asked, ‘what is that?
I turned and peered out of my apartment window at a bright star like object, which was roughly 2 kilometres above sea level. I watched the object manoeuvre in a snake like pattern until it came to a halt. It hovered for approximately 10 seconds before emitting six (6) smaller yellow-orange objects, which began to descend down the sky in Helix formation toward the ocean. The lights disappeared behind a line of trees but appeared to be heading into the water.
