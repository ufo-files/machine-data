I was walking to a friend’s home. I just happened to look up over the trees.
It was coming down at a very steep angel. I thought it was a plane crashing. It was cigar shaped, it came down very fast, I heard a strange noise under the ground. I sounded to me like fox hounds yelping under my feet. Then the sound just traveled away back into the woods. I thought I should not tell anyone about this they wouldn't believe me. It has stuck with me ever since. It went down into the woods maybe a half mile to a mile away. I grew up near the airport. at that time, and I had never seen anything come down that steep.
