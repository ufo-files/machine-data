Orange glowing lights

I saw them at 12:40am. There were 6 glowing orange lights glide slowly past my window. They were all moving in a diagonal formation and were fairly close to the ground. These were not planes, helicopters, or lanterns. Ive SEEN lanterns, and these were not lanterns. They were also completely silent. I really wish I could have gotten a picture but I was too mesmerized to leave and get my camera, and I was sure theyd be gone by the time I found it anyway. They were gliding Eastward.
