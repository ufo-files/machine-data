Ufo/light sighting in the sky over San Mateo

Sitting in my back yard with my wife, looking up at the sky a giant craft flew quickly and silently over us, about 200 feet above. We looked in amazement as it headed east into the sky. Shocked, we watched it dissipate. over the course of the next week and continuing this day, there are lights in the sky. When I flash my flashlight up at it, It flashes back, it moves around not on a flat plane, more scattered. I know what it was, I have seen many many more in my lifetime. I was perplexed.
