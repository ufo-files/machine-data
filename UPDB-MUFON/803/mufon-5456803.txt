{tjd} Pulsating lights hovering in a normally dark sky

I walked outside our home and observed colorful pulsating lights in the sky to the east of our home. I called my wife out to see the phenomenon. We stood there for several minutes watching. The lights appeared to hover and one at a time the light seemed to fade out and then return. Eventually they flickered out and disappeared. This was the night before we moved to Colorado. The other lights in this video are our garage and a couple neighboring homes.
