{tjd} Objects in the distance

We were surprised to see 5-8 objects headed east in the distance. As we drove we kept an eye on them, even stopping the car and pulling over.
It looked like birds but they flew too high and fast. It looked like they were in formation but one or two would break away.
We had parked to watch them hoping an airliner would make a report.
Then they disappeared.
