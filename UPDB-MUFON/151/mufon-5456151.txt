{jpg} Dark object that emitted bright colors hovered in the sky slightly moving

A coworker of mine walked in saying there was a ufo outside. Another coworker and I went out to see for ourselves. And at first glance we thought he was just looking at an unusually bright star. But after further inspection we could see a dark mass just under the main light. I looked like the mass reflected a bit of the light around it. It would also brighten and dim randomly. While also randomly changing colors. It would periodically wobble back and forth. And remained in the sky after I went back inside. My coworker who stayed out longer told me it was still doing all the same things I saw while I was gone.
