{rjl} Black triangle

On my way to work, I thought I noticed an airplane or helicopter. As I got closer to it and drove past it I realized that it was a triangular craft with windows and humanoid entities inside of it. It had three red lights on each corner of the undercarriage and blue blinking light in the center. I was unable to stop since I did not want to be noticed by the craft and a car came up behind me. The beings appeared caucasion with light brown hair and had on grey uniforms. There were two of them and it looked like they were standing up behind each other.
