Triangle shaped almost tent like with rows of ultra bright lights running down each side. Also a row down the middle with a red light in the center. W

It was sitting there in a field as we were driving by going south on hwy 62. I couldn't see the exact structure but it rows of ultra bright lights running down each side giving it the resemblance of a big triangle almost tent like. It also had a row of lights running down the middle with a red light centered in it. We looked for it as we entered a tree line trying to see the lights through the trees. I immediately turned my vehicle around to get a better look with a clear unobstructed view. I turned left onto weed rd. and looked south directly were it had been and it was gone. Nothing but flat open field. The craft was big and the lights made it almost like daylight around it but so bright that you couldn't see the actual structure behind the glare. And in an instant it was gone. I had to ask my friend if he had seen what I just saw and he confirmed it.
