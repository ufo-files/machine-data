This was a multi coloured object flashing red green,white, and yellow. It definitely appeared to move randomly and the lights were extremely intense

I went out of my house to leave the rubbish bin at the end of my drive.It was around 7pm dark with a fairly cloud free starlit sky. My attention was drawn to an unusual multi coloured object which I at first thought must be some sort of aircraft in the northeastern sky.As I observed this anomaly it seemed to move vertically and laterally in random bursts whilst emitting intense bursts of very bright coloured light ranging from red,yellow,blue,green and white. I further observed through binos and confirmed at least that it wasn't an aircraft and it certainly was unlike anything I had ever seen in the night sky
