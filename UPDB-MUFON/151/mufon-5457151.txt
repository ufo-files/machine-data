{cak} very large, very slow, silent craft with patterns/lines like electornic circuit board on the bottom

Cold, clear, crisp night - no clouds or fog - at approx 10:30 p.m.  For some reason, I looked straight up and about 100-200 feet above me, moving very slowly (barely moving at all) toward the south, there was a very large rectangular craft (slight variations on the edges, so not a perfect rectangle). The craft was relatively flat on the bottom - I could not see the sides or top, because it was directly over me. It made absolutely no noise, and there were no lights on it that I could see. It was a very dark grey, non-reflective surface with design or patterns/lines like on an electronics circuit board on the underside.
