{dr}Random Flashing Lights

I was sitting in my hot tub at 7:55, on 2 Oct 2012. I noticed 2 flashing lights approaching me from the east north east at about 10 degrees above the horizon. I assumed that it was obviously either two planes or helicopters flying together in close proximatey. 

I watched for about 10 seconds or so and the 2 flashing lights became 5 flashing randomly in a straight "5 inch"" horizontal line. This 5 light flashing only lasted a few seconds and then turned back into 2 lights flashing. The object(s) turned slowly to the south and stayed headed south until I could no longer see them.

I submitted 3 previous UFO sightings to your organization and received no response on what the objects might have been. I hope to receive a response on this sighting.
