I was on my deck smoking a cigarette and I saw a dull white light to the north fly really fast like the speed of a shooting star from west to east then saw one from the ground level shoot straight up then another one from east to west. Thinking this is weird and definitely unusual as we see planes all the time and this isn't normal. I had my husband and son come out to watch and we witnessed for over 20 minutes these dull white lights go one direction to another some straight lines some diagonal up down. Truely a unreal sight. some would come from one direction stop mid air and change direction another one came from east and did a quick turn back east. We couldn't believe this was happening. we also saw a plane (we know it was a plane because it had flash lights and was moving slow like the planes do) in the air at one point in the middle of this and one of these white lights circled it one direction then circle back the other direction the shoot straight up I can not tell you how many unidentifiable objects we saw but we know it had to be several. Since this lasted so long I feel sure we can't be the only ones seeing this so I called and repored this to the local police whom called me back and were checking with the airport. I hope they will follow back up with me. What im certain of is they were not shooting stars they were not airplanes or drones. I'm so glad my husband and son witnessed this too. It was incredible to see. I'm in shock
