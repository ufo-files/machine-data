{rjl} Starlike going across sky @ various speeds and directions

As a 13 year old, while camping out in yard on clear summer night in 1966, could not put date and day as your form doesn't go back that far, I observed a starlike object moving from star to star in random directions, stopping and starting after being hidden in front of various stars for different periods of time. It took about an hour or so to go from horizon to horizon across the sky.
