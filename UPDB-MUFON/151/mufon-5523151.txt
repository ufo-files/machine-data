A hovering object w/good detail and two bright lights on the front.

I was driving home shortly after midnight going West on Harrison street. As I approached 42nd street I noticed 2 bright lights in the sky somewhat close to one another and that they were not moving as a plane normally does. As I passed 42nd street, I noticed that the object was still not moving. It was in the sky - I would say less than 500 feet above me. The wings were not like normal planes, instead they extended from the body a short distance and then they were vertical at the ends. I also noticed what I thought were small tiny greenish/blue flourescent lights that seemed to drop a small distance and then disappear. There were also lights on the bottom of the craft that were shaped like an arrow (<) that pointed towards what I assume was the front of the object. As I neared 48th street I kept turning around to view it and as I decided to get out of my car, it slowly headed east away from the direction I was in.
