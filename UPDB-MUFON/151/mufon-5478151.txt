{nd} Solid dark boomerang object that used cloud cover when passing the moon.

It had just rained in Los Angeles and being an editor, I like to hang out on the balcony while my project renders. It was a beautiful night and the moon was super bright, that's what caught my attention as well an odd moving cloud pattern that was moving quicker than the other clouds in the sky. As I viewed this and my eyes adjusted I could make out a huge boomerang shaped object gliding NW and using the cloud to mask it as it passed by the light of the moon. I yelled to my business partner to come take a look and he managed to catch a glimpse of it right as it passed the moon. At that point the cloud vanished just like that and I could no longer track the object. This was by far the most amazing thing I have ever seen and I started calling my friends to tell them what I saw. I think about it daily and decided to report this because I felt it is our responsibility to do so. I tried to take video but the object was too high and too dark for my camera phone to capture.
