Morrison CO bright fireball-like star hovering and approaching, then made hard right and slowly disappeared

Was standing in the cul-de-sac outside our house (near Littleton, CO) when we saw a giant orange ball in the sky looking south/southeast. Husband said "What is that?" and we watched it for a good 30 seconds before he went to get the neighbor. All 3 of us stood outside and watched this ball of gold and then it made a hard right (east) and slowly disappeared/dimmed/shrunk to nothingness.
