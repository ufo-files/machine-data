{dr}Long cigar shaped object with no clear markings,traveling very slow with no sound from south to north.

I was a Motocross event at the amateur track at Unadilla Motocross facility on May 11 2014 ( Mothersday). My Stepson was waiting to return to our pit area because he had a chain failure on the bike during his moto. As a result,I returned to our trailer in the pits before he got a tow back.As I was walking back toward our trailer,I noticed this object on the Western horizon moving very slow from the South to the North.When I reached our trailer,I continued to watch this thing for I believe maybe 3 to 4 minutes.And, I also remember thinking,"doesn't anyone else see this?" But,despite that,I neglected to even point it out to my friend just across the road.This is where my story gets strange,as if what I'm describing isn't strange to begin with.Despite the fact that I believe in the UFO pheom.,I'm not nutty about it.Thing is, I don't remember stopping watching it.I'm convinced I would have watched it until it disappeared out of sight.I'm convinced that I would have brought it to someones attention immediately.I'm convinced that I would have been extremely excited about it and told my stepson or anyone for that matter.Reguardless,I didn't.Fact is,I didn't tell anyone about it all day until about 9:00 that night when my wife and I were in the hot tub and I remembered and said to my wife,"Holy cow Babe you wont believe what I saw today."This thing was as long or longer than the body of an airliner but without wings.It was moving very slow.Slower than a blimp.I have seen several blimps at major league sporting events,and this thing didn't fly anything like a blimp.It was whiteish but didn't appear to be very clear in my focus at all.I made no sound.I recall it being approx. 1000' in the air if that.Seeing this object doesn't bother me,but,not recalling the incident until later in the evening does.
