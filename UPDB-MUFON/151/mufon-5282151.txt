I witnessed a white object moving fast from SE to NW. The object appeared to be tumbling through the air.

I am an amateur stargazer, so I was outside in a field that is attached to my driveway. It was 3/11/22 at approximately 12:30 a.m. While looking up with my naked eye, I saw what looked like a white, tumbling object moving from SE to NW in direction. I didn't see any lights coming from the object nor did the object make any audible sounds. I immediately took my iPhone out and tried to film what I saw. My phone screen was completely black so I couldn't tell if I had my phone oriented exactly right or not despite seeing it with my naked eye. I still have the recording that I took but you cannot see anything in the video.

After seeing this object, I ended up seeing and capturing at least three more unknown objects over the next two hours or so. On one of the video clips recorded through an infrared monocular, an unknown object appears and disappears very quickly. It looked like a type of lightning bolt (best way I could describe it). On another clip, also through an IR monocular, the camera recorded strange, loud beeping and clicking type of sounds despite nothing being audible to me at the time of recording.
