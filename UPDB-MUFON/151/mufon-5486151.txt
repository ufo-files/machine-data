Was traveling home from working in field combining barley. Saw a light in review mirror and saw light behind me. No other vehicles in area and was at night around midnight. I was 17 years old at time. Looked up in my windscreen and saw a disc shaped Ufo fly over my vehicle. It was very clear and not very high, about 50 feet above me.  I was close to my farm yard and it stopped hovering over our field. I could see it while I drove into the farm yard as it had just stopped and just hovered there. I parked and went into our house and woke my Dad and Mom. I asked if they wanted to see something strange as I was not really familiar with what it was. My Dad came out on the porch and we looked at it for awhile. I went and got my binoculars and looked at this object. It was very clear in the binoculars and was discredited shaped with a rim separating the top and bottom parts. The top part had portholes spaced around it and looked larger than the bottom part. A band separating these was slowly spinning going clockwise and you could see this motion very clearly. After about 5 minutes it slowly started moving toward the east. I asked Dad should we follow it so we got in my car and started following it. After about 5 miles it climbed higher about three times the height it had been. It started to spin very fast in place until it just looked like a blinking light. Suddenly it sped off out of site leaving a red light like when you move a glow stick very fast. The light it left was in a soft circle and it looked like it went very high or out of the atmosphere.
