{dr}Solid black large object moving very slowly and silently from NW to SE

In mid-January 1983 I was walking over to my friend's house. Typically I would walk through the lawn of St. John's Lutheran Church. As I crossed 5th Street SW from Main Street I happened to glance up. I remember the sky was absolutely clear. And I remember from the northwest something very large, black, and triangular was very slowly passing overhead. I was an avid aircraft model builder and several things struck me as very odd about what I was observing. First, the object appeared to be lower than any aircraft I had previously observed at that location because it seemed to stretch from directly over my head, over the church and to at least the house on the corner of 1st Avenue SW and 5th St SW. Second, it appeared to be flat, dark black in color and was a solid object. As it passed overhead it blocked out starlight. Third, it did not have any aircraft identification lights. I remember seeing a bluish light near my point of view, and a reddish light towards the far side, but no blinking lights or white landing lights that one would expect from a low-flying aircraft. And fourth, as the object slowly passed overhead, it made no noise (which should rule out a helicopter or propeller-powered aircraft) and after it passed overhead there was no sound whatsoever nor was there any visible means of propulsion (i.e. no rocket flame or engine glow). The object continued heading slowly east-southeast over Waukon. I've never seen anything like it before or since.
