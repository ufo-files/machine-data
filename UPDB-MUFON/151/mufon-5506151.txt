{rjl} Multiple UFO Orb groups over Virginia

Had been scanning sky prior to ice storm with night vision. After an initial 15 minutes of scanning I picked up on prominent objects in the foreground that were not there earlier (sky was increasingly clouding over in advance of an ice storm). As minutes went by they became unmistakably apparent. After careful tracking I realized they were in motion and spent a total of 45 minutes tracking their movement from a glide path left-right to stationary rest, to movement of orbs within their groups which are evidenced in the video. There is a rod flyby in the video at the end. Side note: I also caught a cloaked object (very large) moving through the sky at a much higher altitude...the night vision picked up its magnetic impulses disturbing the atmosphere and it moved SE to NW. Interestingly enough, there was absolutely no plane or helicopter activity in the sky the whole time the orbs were in view, which is extremely unusual in the Richmond area.
