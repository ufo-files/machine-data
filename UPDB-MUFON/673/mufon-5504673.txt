{rjl} hovering and crazy pattern light/lights

Was chillin with my brother in front of my house in my car around 530 when I noticed a light thru my sunroof in the sky and thought to myself that it was the north star, then I saw it moving towards my direction so I thought ooo maybe it's an airplane, since I see them flying over my house all the time. After about 5 minutes it was still in the same spot so I thought Ooooooook maybe I didn't see it move and it is a star. Now that light has my attention cause I was sure it was an airplane moving towards me but it seems to be staying in the same spot, so I tell my brother "hay, is that a helicopter or what cause it looks like it's moving towards us but at the same time staying in the same spot" so now my brother and I are starting at this light and as it starts to get darker we can see it better and now we're debating on if it was a helicopter cause it was flying with no direction, no! Its way to high to be a helicopter, its about as high as those airplanes that release chemical trails. It was moving at a very slow speed and then fast and then slow not going in any particular direction, almost like it was parallel parking in the sky, at around 7ish it was like 10 or more lights that just appeared and looked like stars but then they all seemed to move as a unit, I had to go get my gf from work around then so I was unable to keep watching but they seemed to stay in the same spot moving slowly then a rapid acceleration to a complete stop, if you looked up at the wrong time you would think it was just stars (to much detail to type but definitely a lot more happened)
