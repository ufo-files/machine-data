(GS) Unknown craft with rotating lights that would spin, dim, and then flash blocks of lights.

My wife and I were driving home on the 101 from Hollywood to Downey through downtown Los Angeles when my wife exclaimed with some alarm that there was something strange in the sky. Looking up I saw what appeared to be a saucer shaped object with rotating lights about its middle. It shocked me since it appeared to be a very classically styled "flying saucer" - very reminiscent of the ones in "Close Encounters." I saw the object fade to black, then light up in grid patterns of square and rectangular lights which would then strobe/flash as if to signal something. We lost sight of the object driving through the buildings near the 5 freeway intersection, then saw it again - this time hovering perfectly. If was flashing rectangular patterns at this point, then, once again it dimmed and began to display a rotary light display.

I researched online when we got home right now, and found this video on YouTube from a similar area showing almost exactly what we saw: http://youtu.be/MNsrB5281vM
