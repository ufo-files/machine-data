Flying glowing object inside a house caught on security camera on night vision mode

In the morning of October 1st 2020, we got 3 notifications on our cellphone for motion detected in my friend's condo. No one was in the house at that time. 
First event: 5:59am- camera detected a flying object in the left side moving upward and making a 180 degre turn and desend to passed in front of the camera
second event: 6:10am- camera detected an object on the far left, outside the camera angle but we can see the room being lighted by the object.
third event: 6:41am- camera detected the same flying object going downward.

All the windows were closed, door was locked and the blinds were shut down. There was no sign of birds, insects or sheet of paper.
