{jpg} we witnessed a triangular object with green lights exit a cloud and shoot upwards into another cloud and disappear.

It was 6 am on a job site and while talking with an employee from the corner of my eye I saw a light shoot from one cloud to another cloud and never reappear. My employee asked me if I saw it and I told him I had. He saw it very clearly and described a dark triangular shaped object with three very bright green lights that also projected pinkish beams from the rear of the object. There was a white aura around the front like it was pushing the atmosphere with its extremely high rate of speed. My employee was shaking for several minutes afterwards. I had another employee 300' away and after he approached us and heard us talking about it confirmed what we had seen by telling us he saw the same thing. He was not going to say anything about it to us until he knew we had seen it as well.
