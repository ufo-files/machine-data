All I can say that I saw was a light

I was riding home with my friend Jeff Farr in his pickup truck. He asked mewhat was that light in the sky behind us, and I looked behind us and saw abright light hovering in the sky behind us. It would bend as the road bendeduntil we got to my brothers apartment. At first, I thought it was Jesus Christcoming to us. I was on cloud nine, I was so excited. I had been on vacation in1986 to Acapulco, Mexico. We, my mother and sister and I were staying at thePrincess Hotel. It was around the same time of the sighting, around 330 am and Icouldnt sleep so I had gone down to one of the swimming pools and laid down on alawn chair and just started talking to the sky. Saying things like If anyonecan hear me show me a sign I mean you no harm, I want to meet your people Iwant to go to your planet. Nothing ever happened that morning. When we saw thelight two years later I just knew it was because I had summoned them to come. Iimmediately ran upstairs to get my brother to come and see this huge brightlight. Jeff stayed downstairs in the truck.
