big bright fireball looking object hovering between the island of st kitts and nevis then disappears

It was in the year 2010 around June, i was living on the beautiful island of nevis which is from the federation St kitts and Nevis in the Caribbean, i was on my cell phone talking with my girlfriend from Barbados when i heard my niece calling for me with a very scared voice so i quickly disconnected the call and rushed to her on the balcony to see what was the matter what she showed me that day was unbelievable it was huge it looked like a giant fireball not burning just glowing and floating in the air between the two islands and after about 30 seconds looking at it it was gone it just vanished  , what was most interesting to me was the way it vanished like looking at a magic trick or turning off the television it just disappeared. until now i really dont know how to explain what me and my niece saw that day but it was definitely something iv never seen before or never saw again.
