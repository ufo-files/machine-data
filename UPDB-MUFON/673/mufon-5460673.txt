I was a teenager at a campfire boys and girls camp. Another camper and I went out to the road waiting for someone to come and then we saw the ufos just in the trees sitting there. They were circular with lights on the bottom. We kept our eyes fixated on them and it was like we couldn’t move. Then the saucers went up and we ran away. We couldn’t believe what we had seen. She didn’t want to tell anyone but I told and no one believed us. I have lived with this for 20 years without telling too many people.
