The sphere shape got brighter and dark it was very slowly moving . Over 20 minutes it didn't move far.

I was leaving work in the Southwark area -I look up often while smoking. It was dark around 7pm I could see the red light from a crane down the road and I saw a few planes flying behind it in the distance. Then I noticed a circle light growing brighter. It was a slow pulse rather than a flash that lasted a few seconds it dimmed and brightened a few times . Over 20 minutes it barely moved but it was slowly drifting I only noticed it drifting because my view started to get obscured by a tree. I felt like I couldn't look away and after standing there for 15 minutes without smoking and looking up I was starting to get some unwanted on lookers and decided I should go. Another 5 minutes later not being able to look away it's dimmed and didn't brighten back up so I left to go home.
