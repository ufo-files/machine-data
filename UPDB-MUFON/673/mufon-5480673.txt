{rjl} RED LIGHT IN SKY FORTH TIME SEEING IT IN TWO MONTHS

AT ABOUT 11:30PM (PST) IN HOLLISTER CALIFORNIA I WAS OUT FRONT UF MY HOUSE TALKING TO MY FRIEND. I LOOKED UP AT THE SKY FACEING NW AND SAW A RED LIGHT STATIONARY IN THE CLEAR NIGHT SKY ABOUT 17DEG UP. THE OBJECT WAS AT ABOUT THE SAME ALTITUDE AS YOUR AVERAGE CESNA AIRPLANE. THIS IS THE FOURTH TIME SEEING THESE LIGHTS IN TWO MONTHS. LAST TIME I SAW THESE THINGS WAS ON 9-27-2016 BUT THAT TIME THERE WAS SIX OBJECTS MOVING LOW ON THE HORIZON FROM THE NORTH TO THE SOUTH EAST AT ABOUT 9:00PM. I KNOW WHAT NORMAL AIR TRAFFIC LOOKS LIKE AND HAVE BEEN USING A LARGE TELESCOPE FOR OVER 12 YEARS AND HAVE SPENT COUNTLESS HOURS WITH MY EYES TO THE SKY OBSERVING AND PHOTOGRAPHING SATELLITES,METEORS,PLANETS AND SUCH.I RAN AND GOT MY SONY HIGH 8 CAMCORDER WICH WAS ALREADY OUT IN THE LIVING ROOM AND QUICKLY GOT BACK OUTSIDE TO FILM IT. THE OBJECT TURNED OFF AFTER ABOUT TWO MINUTES. I STAYED OUTSIDE FOR ABOUT AN HOUR AFTER SEEING THIS THING TO SEE IF IT CAME BACK ON LIKE THEY NORMALY DO BUT SAW NOTHING ELSE. TIS WAS NOT A DRONE,LANTERN,PASSENGER JET,RC PLANE THE TIME THE LIGHT WENT OUT WAS ABOUT 11:42PM.
