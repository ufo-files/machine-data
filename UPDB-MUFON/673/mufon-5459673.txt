{rjl} Lights in formation hovering & watching fireworks

I was watching fireworks out my window. I live under a path that many airplanes take to get to O’Hare. For reference I was facing out toward the lake with O’Hare behind me. Many planes flew by that night, as well, but these lights appeared to hover in the distance. Sometimes I saw one or two and I wasn’t sure if I was seeing someone’s drone, but at one point the lights seemed to be in formation. The lights would go away when people stopped shooting off fireworks for a few minutes, and then often seemed to reappear once people resumed the fireworks. Sometimes I saw the lights and the planes at the same time.
