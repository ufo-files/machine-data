Objects falling from the sky

At approximately 2:45 pm I was driving with my daughters when I noticed something strange in the sky. There were three objects that appeared to be falling vertically from the sky. They had plumes of white behind them. We live near an international airport and Ft. Meade so we see planes all the time. They didn’t look like planes. I took pictures just as the larger one seem to eject something that changed directions. Even stranger is that after the object split from the larger free falling object...the sky just cleared like nothing was there. I have attached photos to help explain what we saw. You can see how there was one object...the second object starts to separate just as we pass the building...once we pass the building you can clearly see the split object heading in a different direction. It was strange.
