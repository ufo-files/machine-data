{jpg} Ruby red object flew south to north with no visible anti collision or directional lights. No sound. Airspeed approx. 150 MPH. Altitude of 1500-2000 feet.

Me, my wife and adult daughter had just left the movie theater in Fenton Missouri. As I was getting into my car I noticed a bright ruby red light flying in a northerly direction at 1500 to 2000 feet altitude with an airspeed of approximately 150 MPH. There were no directional nor any anti collision lights flashing.There was no sound coming from the object. It was silent. It flew at an altitude that a helicopter would fly at but no sound. The object flew out of sight towards the city of St.Louis. I was very astounded at what I witnessed as I have over 25 years experience as an aircraft mechanic working for a major defense contractor working in their experimental and flight operations. What I saw did not look or behave like any standard flying aircraft.The lighting for flying at night did not conform to FAA standards.
