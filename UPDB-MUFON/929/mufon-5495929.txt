{cak} Wife photographed star like object at first stationary then falling from view and disappearing.

On approximately Feb. 7th 2015, around midnight, my wife stepped outside of her mother's house to photograph the moon, which was full and exceptionally bright. I wasn't present. There was a apparently a bright star close to the moon, in a stationary position, and she didn't pay much attention to it at first. After taking a pic of the moon, she zoomed in and took another. She then lowered the camera to adjust the zoom, and noticed movement out of the corner of her eye. When she looked up, the star-like object was moving straight down. She raised the camera and took another picture, the last pic shows the path of the object, although there was some camera shake too. Then the object disappeared. If you zoom in on the stationary object, it appears to have four separate sections, in a diamond shape.
