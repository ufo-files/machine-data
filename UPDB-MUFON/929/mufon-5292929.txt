I watched 2 orbs fly slowly toward me.When they saw me both stopped on a dime, communicated clearly, &flew away at VERY high Gs.

I am a pilot's daughter-2nd generation pilot. I was walking my dog about 9pm-Rhodesian Ridgeback-more than 1/2 my height & bred to be fearless as their job in Africa is keeping lions away from farms. I immediately spotted 2 orbs coming toward me from the south,flying very slowly & silently, but it looked as though they were kids playing tag. One orb bumped the other from top, bottom, sides, etc. Each orb was engaging in bumping the other on all sides. I watched this activity in amazement for at least a couple of minutes. As I did, I noticed that my dog was watching them as well, & she began to walk tight circles around me, growling very low in her throat to protect me.(These dogs rarely bark, they depend on element of surprise.) As soon as the orbs got VERY close to me (within yds) they simultaneously stopped on a dime, I could palpably feel the presence of occupants & clearly heard in my head "Oops! We weren't supposed to let anyone see us!" Then they flew toward NW (silently again) so fast no human could have survived the G forces. It honestly reminded me of 2 kids playing tag on playground (until they saw me & flashed away. Any other reports of ET kids learning to fly?
