{jpg} Bright object light with no ignition trail, no sound, hovered then zoomed away into the stars.

2 other friends and I were in the Susanville meadow area taking a walk about dusk and going into night. One friend observed a light going across the sky and asked us what it was. We looked up and watched the object coming from west, brightly light with no trail of behind the object and not even a typical blinking light for a plane or helicopter. We figured it was a meteor but wasnt having a trail and appearing to hover across the sky. As the object kept hovering, the object stopped mid air and zoomed away into the stars still giving no sound. The object didnt dwindle or 'burn out' instead appeared to zoom away.
