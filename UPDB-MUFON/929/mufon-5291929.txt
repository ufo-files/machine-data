Resubmitting with pics Black diamond moved across our line of sight at times falling like a leaf

Resubmitting because pics did not load. This is my sixth MUFON report following my first intense sighting (that I remember) in late October 2016. On August 1, 2020, my wife, one daughter and I took our two dogs to Percy Warner Park (west Nashville, TN) for a walk. We parked at the PW golf course club house and took a trailhead into an interior loop. For those who know the location, we took a left on the road and walked a short distance to where the course is next to the road. The dogs wandered onto a tee box (maybe 7 or 8). At that moment the daughter said, “look a UFO.” I assumed she was kidding because she has been dismissive (24 y/o engineer who works in engineering in the automotive industry) of various photos I have shown until the Pentagon officially released the tic tac videos this year. There was a moderate overcast and a very faint drizzle (not perceptible when under the tree canopy). Maybe 250 feet in front of us at an altitude of 150-200 feet was a black diamond shaped object (or a cube with the corners rotated 20-30 degrees). It was four to five feet across. It sort of fluttered like a leaf while moving forward. I had enough wits to grab my camera (iPhone 8) and snap one picture once it passed a tree and had moved further away. The incident lasted 10 or 15 seconds before the object moved out of sight behind a row of trees.
