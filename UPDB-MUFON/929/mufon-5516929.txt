i was standing on my front porch watching fireworks when i looked over toward the eastern sky and saw one reddish orange "something flying across the sky. i had my husband come outside to see, a neighbor also came out and saw these also. within a minute or 2 another one came flying past. about another minute or so later a third one came. each one flew in the same line as the one before it. i watched each one and after a few minutes of watching they just seemed to fade out. there was no sound, no flashing lights.
