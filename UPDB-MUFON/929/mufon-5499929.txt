{dr}pulsating ball from white to green to red, moved several times very fast to right then stayed still last 2 hours

I first witnessed what I thought was a very bright star, until I seen it move down and then 90 degrees perfect turn to the right very fast. I had family camping out in a camper, 4 people, I started screaming for them to come outside that there was something strange in the sky. They all came out and we all witnessed the object, pulsing, hovering in the same place, and then it would shoot up or down very fast, and then always to the right and then back stationary. I sent 2 of them inside to get my binoculars, I then sat them on the mailbox to keep them steady, it was really big for being I would say about 3 to 5 miles away. It moved in the same pattern several times, and then would go back to hovering for 20 minutes or so. We then moved the binoculars to a fence post and began to take photos from a phone, on zoom. We could clearly see the entire object was changing color, white to green to red, could not tell if there was any pattern though, we all looked in the binoculars and we all seen the same thing. We tried to video it and the phone would not zoom on video though. So looking through binocular lens with a camera on a phone, we then filmed with another phone the image on the first phones screen. We had tears, we were kind of overwhelmed and a bit scared, it last for about 2 hours that we seen and viewed it, until we started loading the video on the laptop and it was gone, vanished.
