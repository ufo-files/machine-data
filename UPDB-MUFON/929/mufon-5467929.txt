{cak} Was driving in the country with friend-Came in a smear of lights-then hung above.

Going to a party with boyfriend.On a gravel road in Northern 
Iowa. Thought it was a small town in the distance-then thought it was football field lights in distance. Then it appeared above car. It was BIG-like a mothership-bigger than a football field.It hung above the car-no noise-it stopped the car. Had Bright white lights beaming down from the middle.At one point symbols appeared in a ring around the middle of the ship.When it left it took off it was like nothing we had ever seen.It zig zagged and was gone in seconds.I had a scoop mark on my ankle after that.We were in dumbfounded. Tried to tell people and did. Felt like I had a post hypnotic suggestion-not to. Wanted to report it but didn't know how or where.
