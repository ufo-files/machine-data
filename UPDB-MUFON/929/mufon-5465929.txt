Fuzzy white light moving in a circular clockwise and anti clockwise motion.It would also hover stationary and was joined by a second light

My wife and I were letting our dog out in the garden before we went to bed and noticed the rapidly moving light to the west. It was moving in a circular/elliptical motion both clockwise and anti clockwise, and would also stop suddenly and hover for a few seconds. It was joined by a second light a little further to the north, which appeared less bright and this moved erratically for a further couple of minutes before they both disappeared suddenly. We were both amazed and excited to see this event, and although our first reaction was they could be lasers or torch beams there were no beams visible being projected from the ground, and the lights were coming from behind the clouds.
