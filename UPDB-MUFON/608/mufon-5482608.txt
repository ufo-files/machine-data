We heard a low hum sound and found the sound coming from the sky and looked up and saw a large disk flying out from a large cumulus cloud.

I was only 13 years old at the time, and my friend was 14 years old. We were going swimming at Elliot Lake, lake at the time. We lived in neighbour 2 which is about 3 miles from the lake, but we knew a short cut which was a old logging trail to the lake and cut the distance in half. We were walkng down this trail with our towels around our necks, and talking and joking as we went. it was a bright sunny day, with large cumulus clouds here and there. We walked about 1/2 mile, when we heard the low hum, and we both looked up, and we saw the large silver disk shape UFO. It just came through the big cloud slowly on our right, and just hovered for about a minute, then it started moving slowly to our left into the next big cloud. It was massive, and it scared both of us really bad. We both looked at each other, and I yelled run, and we did...back home that is! It something I will never forget. We both told our parents, and they did not believe us. I just wanted you to know what I experienced. I wrote this because I have another UFO to report, and its when I am in the Canadian Military. They believe me now.
