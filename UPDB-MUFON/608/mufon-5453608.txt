{rjl} Extremely bright circular light

From our balcony on 54 street we noticed an extremely bright white light hovering to the left of the City Group building. 

The object was completely soundless and appeared rather low to be a star especially since it disappeared. We watched it fir several moments and went back inside. 

Later in the evening approximately 10 pm the object was gone.
