{dr}I saw the lights in the sky and was curious so I took a photo. Don't really know what they were.

I was on a boat in Miami inside the bay when I saw the lights. We were on a bay cruise before night time. I had no idea what they were so I took a photo. The lights didn't seem to be moving so I thought they might just be a reflection of the lights inside the boat but they were not. I momentarily forgot about it for a few minutes and then when I looked back, all of the objects were no longer there. I took the photo through the glass window and didn't look at the photo until much later. After enlarging the photo, It seemed there was a shape similar to a chevron like shape with the lights at the bottom. It was eerie after realizing that they could be something else other than an ordinary aircraft.
