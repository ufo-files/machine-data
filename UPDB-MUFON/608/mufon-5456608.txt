{jpg} 2 Red Lights with White in Middle Above NW Austin Arbor Walk

I was at the intersection Stonelake drive and Capital of Texas Highway on my bicycle when I look up to the east to see two red lights with a white light between hovering in the sky, just behind a little fluffy cloud.
I watched and noticed it did not move. When the turn light went green, I went through and stopped at the nearby bus stop. From there I continued to watch the thing in the sky.
The thing then zipped quietly from where it was to the west no far away where it hovered for a bit.
At the bus stop, I reached for my "smart" phone to try to get a pic, at which point, it zipped off without a sound.
