We were driving home and noticed low flying object moving slowly. The lights were red and orange in color and would alternate between each other in a V shape. We watched it then it was definitely noticeable that it was not a plane or anything of that sort with the lights just being one primary color. When we decided to stop to try to get a picture it had disappeared by that time. We got out of the car and shut the car off and couldn1t hear anything or see anything. It was the clearest night sky and could make out stars easily but not the object anymore. We were in disbelief but shock and so many different emotions into one.
