Large and very reflective. No wings, no tail fin.

I was walking down the path that runs adjacent in between Bunting Rd and Niagara St where the old railway tracks used to be. When I reached Linwell Rd I looked up at the sky and saw the sun reflecting off a chromed object slowly making it's way to the east less than 200 feet above the rooftops. I watched the object for a matter of seconds before it disappeared behind the trees in the area. I walked a kilometre down this path and when I approached Scott St I got a better look at the object as it crossed my path again. I saw it had a solid smooth and shiny surface, no visible wings and no tail fin or identifying marks and was in the shape of a cigar. It was moving far too slow to be a conventional aircraft and far too low. The object was silent and looked magnificent as the sun was just mirrored against it. It was approximately 30-50 feet long. I've never seen a UFO before but I never felt so at peace as when I adored this object. Again, I watched this object for about a minute before it disappeared from my line of sight behind trees and houses.
