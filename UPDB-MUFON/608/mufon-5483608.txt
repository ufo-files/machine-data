Shot over my head, changed direction rapidly and hovered

Taking my daily walk after my knee operation, I decided to walk in direction of the German Ems where I work. Suddenly noticed this white light shooting past above me. First I thought it was a shooting star but then it just stopped, started flashing, so I thought it was some kind of army jet. But then it shot like guessing 5 miles to the right within a second, stopped, cam back, shi[f]t to the left in the same was it did to the right, came back again, you could see an aircraft pass it and then notice the difference in size and especially the way it way it was lit! Then out of nowhere it shot upwards and was gone.

I live really close to the Frankfurt airport landing zone so I see aircrafts all day long. I work for the Ems so I know what a chopper looks like and can do. To that I'm a military child, so I also know how jets, military aircraft behave. This was way out of the range. Especially at the speed and then no sound? Wth?
