I moved to Tenerife 5 yrs ago, having worked all over the world including travelling deep sea, i have always watched the sky just as an interest to see things like shooting stars, i now live at the bottom of a mountain which i have just found out today 27 Jan is called Del Conde, when i finish work i always go out onto the veranda in the house as you can imagine it is very warm here, anyway i was looking at the mountain and all of a sudden from my left looking up came this beautiful bright glowing object halfway up the mountain it travelled in a horizontal line three quarters across the mountain then just disappeared, it was really bright orange on the left inthe middle like a golden yellow and bright orange on the right it was like looking at a floodlight but it gave no light off in front or behind it, i was so mesmerised i could not shout to my wife who was in the house or maybe this thing made sure i couls not shout i am not sure, anyway as soon as it disappeared i telephoned my son in england to let him know, people are sceptical when you tell them, but personnally i am not bothered as now i know i have seen the real thing. my feelings, elated and very thankful.
