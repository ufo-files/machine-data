{rjl} A bunch of planes. Then 2 stopped.

My dad and I were picking my mom up from work. We were parked in the parking lot for 15-20 min. All of a sudden we see exactly 5 planes in the sky. We thought it was weird (we live in the country.) So we were looking in the sky, they all had very pronounced jet streams. Then 2 of them stopped, the streams behind them disappeared, and they just hovered there. My dad and I were in shock. Then the 3 other planes approached the other 2 hovering metallic objects,And then one of the hovering objects was absorbed by the other metallic object. And then there was just that one metallic hovering object that sat there for approximately 10 min. One plane then flew away and there were only 2 planes left.Then we lost track of all of them. It was really insane. It was a clear sky. I almost forgot, my dad and I saw the crazy stuff, but my mom saw The single hovering metallic object.
