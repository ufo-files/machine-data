Cross like shape in 3D

I was working in a back garden of s customer of mine. I caught sight of something in the sky which looked as if it was approximately 200 feet above me. My customer was in the garden with me and I immediately pointed the object out to them. The response from my customer was, quote "maybe it's a UFO". We watched it move across the sky silently for around a minute. I felt very shocked but excited at the same time. The reason I'm telling this now is because on tv last night,27/06/2017 a program called UFO hunters was on the H2 channel. It was series 1 episode 3 and it featured what looked like the exact same UFO that I witnessed. There was a guy trying to decide wether the photographs were real or faked. I can 100% say that what I saw was very real but unfortunately could not see any symbols or letters on it because of the distance away from me. I took my mobile phone from my pocket to try to record it but was too late by the time the camera had tried to focus.
