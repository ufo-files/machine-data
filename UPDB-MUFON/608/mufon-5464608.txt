{jpg} after initial sighting, a beam of light surrounded and lifted me towards the sphere

I had been asleep in the back of my car in the parking lot of Curry Village in the Yosemite Valley. I don't remember getting up and out but found myself in a small clearing of large trees. I saw the sphere coming down towards me. It was covered with flames but the orange and yellow flames were coming off of it and curving back into it. The flames stopped. I could then see a metallic looking sphere with colored lights pulsating in a band around it's equator area. Suddenly there was a bright white light shining on me. I can remember the warmth of it and the feeling of total peace or security. I remember being lifted toward the sphere in the beam of light. My next memory was waking up in my car in the early morning and being filled with amazement and wondering what had happened. I drew 2 pictures of the object within a day or two of the experience. I have considered regression hypnosis many times since but haven't yet. I have told very few people and still have the drawings if you are interested. I knew that it was an alien ship after it stopped its' "flaming". I will have to dig up my sketches. I haven't looked at them for many years. Let me know if you are interested. Thank you, Karl
