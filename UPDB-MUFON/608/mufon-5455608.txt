{tjd} Eptaxian Induction (abduction)

On the date and time described I was inducted by a robotic humanoid which I later discovered they are called Eptaxians. They are made of liquid titanium and they move through wormholes. When I was abducted by this Eptaxian they must have needed my help because they were being harvested during my encounter which lasted 10 days. At one point everyone in the city completely dissapeared as I entered what they described to me as their "dead space". While in dead space time was very different, I spent 10 days there and woke up around the same time the encounter occured, time must be much faster in dead space. While I was being abducted I was able to subdue the enemy robotic forces that were harvesting them and helped them make it into dead space. Then I was eventually released.
