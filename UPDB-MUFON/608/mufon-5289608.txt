Spec mars rover picture

This is the first high-resolution, color image to be sent back by the Hazard Cameras (Hazcams) on the underside of NASA’s Perseverance Mars rover after its landing on Feb. 18, 2021. To the left of the center of the photo, in the sky line, is a spec. Now it is very small, but I'm having a hard time explaining it. I've never seen spec's of dust or debris show up in the sky line in any other photo's send back from the rover's. Even the pictures of "dust devils" don't show any bits as pronounced as this spec. Thought it might be worth looking.
