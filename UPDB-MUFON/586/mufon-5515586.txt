(GS) GROUP OF 3 LIGHTS / APPEARED TO BE IN FORMATION

I WAS WALKING SOUTHBOUND AT WORK IN THE OUTDOORS. A STREAK IN THE SKY ABOVE ME APPEARED TO BE A SHOOTING STAR. I SAW THE LIGHT TRAIL UNTIL IT REACHED A CERTAIN POINT. IT THEN APPEARED TO BE EITHER 3 YELLOW / ORANGE SOLID GLOWING ORBS IN A STRAIGHT LINE FORMATION, OR IT COULD HAVE BEEN A SINGLE OBJECT WITH 3 LARGE LIGHTS UNDERNEATH. BECAUSE THE LIGHTS WERE GLOWING, I COULD NOT DISCERN ANY OTHER SHAPE DUE TO THE EFFECTS OF THE LIGHTING. IT HAPPENED VERY BRIEFLY LIKE SEEING A SHOOTING STAR AND WAS OUT OF VISUAL RANGE DUE TO AN OBSTRUCTION. THE DISTANCE OF THE OBJECT ABOVE THE GROUND WOULD HAVE BEEN LOWER THAN COMMERCIAL FLIGHTS AND HIGHER THAN SMALL PRIVATE FLIGHTS OF AIRCRAFT. THE NIGHT WAS CLEAR AND CLOUDLESS WITH LITTLE TO NO WIND.
