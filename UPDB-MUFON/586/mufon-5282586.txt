I saw the UFO outside of my plane window as I was descending into CLT airport above Charlotte, NC.

As my plane was descending, I looked out my window and saw a light. At first I wondered if it was another plane or drone, but there were no lights flashing. There was a singular light with an aura coming off of it in a disc shape. The object didn't move, it was hovering above the city under cloud-cover. We passed the object and turned back in its direction, but when I looked for it again it had disappeared. It wasn't the moon, as that day the moon was in a quarter phase and had a red tint. The second image is a zoomed-in version of the first image. In the third image, I couldn't get my shutter speed fixed quickly enough, so there's a bit of a blur and fuzziness.
