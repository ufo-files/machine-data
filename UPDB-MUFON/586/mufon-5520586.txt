Saw white light or lights alternately appear and disappear quickly for a few seconds

I was driving north on North Main Street in Plains, PA at 6:15 pm on Feb. 20. I noticed a bright white light hovering over some houses in the distance, maybe about 1/4 mile away. It was dark and I had no depth perception. It could have been very close or very far away; I couldnt tell. At first, I thought it was an approaching aircraft, because to the north about 10 miles lies the Wilkes-Barre/Scranton Airport. The light disappeared and then quickly reappeared to the East. It stayed bright for a second, disappeared and then reappeared near its original spot. It stayed bright again for a second, disappeared and then reappeared to the West, all in quick succession. It then disappeared completely from sight. This all lasted about six seconds. I have never seen anything like this before. The light seemed to bounce back and forth across the sky and then just disappeared.
