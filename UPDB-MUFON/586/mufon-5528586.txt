No UFO just a continuous sound

Me and a friend were out at placerita canyon. off in the distance were the mountains, canyons, and a small road. the sound began at the ridge of the mountain and continued going down toward the canyon. then took a slight left and began to move toward us. the sound moved right on top of us, then in an instance it seemed as if the sound fully engulfed us. i looked up into the sky to see if maybe there was something but to my avail nothing. it was as if i wanted to see an object just to explain this sound. it lasted for a good 10 to 15 minutes then moved away from us to the main road. the sound stayed there at the road for a few minutes then suddenly stopped. no car head lights where around. the sound just stopped just as soon as it began.
