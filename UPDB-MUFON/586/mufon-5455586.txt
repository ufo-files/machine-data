{tjd} Sound was very low hum, more of a sense of what is that!

1st of 3 continuous days of oddities. Day 1: April 23, 2019, walked out north facing door onto front side walk and heard a hum. This is a rural area with Ponderosa Pine and sloping hills. We are at 2990 or so ft and Mt. Spokane is 5600 ft. North faces directly to Mt. Spokane. 
The hum stopped me in my tracks, "what is that", I followed the noise with my ears as it traveled, there was definatly something moving in the sky but I could not see anything with my eyes, just heard the hum. 
Our security camera was not recording for a week, so we thought....
Interestingly, the camera we thought was not working was infrared and because I was so perplexed by this I searched the dates 4/23 - 4/25 on the system and saw on 4/23 a strange moving object with red lights following it. The object when enlarged looked like a copter with an armed person on it but still with the red lights following it. I thought it might be the air force doing practice flights but since" I saw what I saw" on the 24th I wondered if the air force was tracking something, who knows, I'm totally weirded out by this whole thing. I felt in awe, not frightened but this is as close as I want to get to any of that. Although, now I am searching to see what other people have seen and experienced.
