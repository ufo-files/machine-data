{jpg} After taking some pictures, I went to look back at them and saw an object.

I was in the Wichita mountains with my mom and brother and as we were walking over a dam I took pictures from the dam. I went and looked back at the pictures to see how they looked and I noticed there was something in the picture that I didn’t see before or after taking it with the naked eye. There’s a military base around the Wichita Mountains so there were no flying objects like blimps and drones allowed in the area so I wasn’t sure what it was and I’m still not sure.
