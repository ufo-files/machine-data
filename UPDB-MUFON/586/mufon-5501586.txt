bright star light object travelling north to south at high speed.

15.30hrs 28th of June 2014 at home address I was in the back garden sun bathing the sky was clear no cloud cover at all I observed 4 black tip seagulls flying directly above me as I observed the birds wheeling I noticed a bright starlight like object travelling North to south at extremely high speed and at high altitude. The object was in my unimpeded view for approximately 6-7 seconds before disappearing from sight over the horizon. The object maintained a constant speed and direction for the duration of the sighting, there was no noise I could associate with the object.
