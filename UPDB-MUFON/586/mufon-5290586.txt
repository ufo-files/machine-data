Sudden appearance

Object appeared suddenly and had the appearance of a bright star. It didn’t show up in my cameras view finder. I could only see when I looked it. I pointed my camera in its direction and snapped several photos. It is only when the shots are blown up the object shows up. I didn’t get to excited about the initial view because it looked like a bright star and was stationary. It was only when I blew up the photo that my curiosity was peaked. There seemed to be a division down the middle of the object splitting it in to two half’s.
