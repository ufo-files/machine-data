Scary but strange, it hovered and moved and changed colours.

I was in the house and heard a rumbling noise and thought it was a plane that was low but when I looked out it did not look like a plane so I watched it a bit more. At first it was going round in circles then stopped suddenly and shot away then came back and hovered all this time it was keep changing colours, from white to red to green to blue. I was very scared yet excited I told my friends and family. It shot away again and then came back and hovered a bit again then vanished.
