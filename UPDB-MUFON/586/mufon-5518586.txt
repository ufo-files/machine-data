Outside at night-observed glowing orange light larger than star, then disappeared.

I was standing outside around 9:00 p.m. central time visiting with a neighbor. The neighbor spotted something in the sky and asked my opinion about what the object was. I looked up and told her that I didn't know. After discussing it we agreed that it was not an airplane. We witnessed only a singular orange light (perfectly round) moving faster than what we would have thought an airplane would have traveled. The light looked bigger than what an airplane light would look like. We watched it for about a minute or two and then the light started to get dimmer and then disappeared all together. The light traveled across the sky, at a pretty good pace, in a straight line. We were pretty stumped as to what it was and then started wondering if we had just witnessed a ufo!
