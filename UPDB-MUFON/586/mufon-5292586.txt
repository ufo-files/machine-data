Fast, rotating square lights

I used to live in Esbjerg, Denmark in the first half of 2014. My bedroom's window was looking southwest, in the direction of North Sea. I had a habit what was looking out the window for some minutes before I go to sleep. On that particular night (around 23:00) I was doing my usual "meditation stare" when something caught my attention around zenith. It looked like a square rotating around its center point, but only four white lights were visible on its four corners. Regarding the size: it looked slightly bigger than full moon. 
So it was rotating and moved about at a speed as an airliner would, when it sped up to an incredible speed in the direction of North Sea. It was beyond the horizon maximum in one second. After that I waited a bit but it didn't came back. The whole sighting was no more than some seconds.
The window was open and I didn't hear any noise.
