{jpg} Beach sighting of multiple craft hovering nearly stationary over the Atlantic.

On January 6th, 2019 I was with my girlfriend at Atlantic Dunes Park. It is a public beach in Delray Beach, Fl. We go to this exact location several times a month, as we both live in Delray, about 15 minutes from this park. I had never seen anything similar to this event prior and have yet to see it again. After we arrived at the beach and set up our chairs, I noticed a group of younger guys pointing at something over the water. I saw multiple lights/objects in the sky above the horizon. At first I thought they might be kites or drones used for sport fishing, but after reviewing my footage hundreds of times, I don't know what they are. I wanted to submit the footage to find out what these objects were. They appeared to be stationary and did not seem to move much, if it all. There was no sound, it just looked like stars in the horizon during the middle of the day. It was rather strange. We joked about them being aliens, but again I thought it was something that was probably easily explainable, but have never seen anything close to resembling what I saw that day again. The objects just sat there the entire time we were at the beach.
