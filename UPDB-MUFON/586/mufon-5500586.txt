My eyes caught (2) objects flying side by side @ very high speeds, these objects quickly became (1) object & as quick as that happened they split into (2) objects again only this time they were flying one on top of the other. Once these objects were above the tree line they disappeared. Now this event wouldn't have become anything to talk about if my wife hadn't found these (9) diamond shaped bruises on my back the very next day. I have not believed until this event had taken place.
