V shaped object appeared above me with lights then slowly dissipated into the clouds

I was sitting on a balcony facing the city, it was cloudy and in the Fall time around October 9 2019. I felt the urge to look up and saw a V-shaped object with lights along the V. It appeared for about five seconds, I stared at it disbelievingly. I felt no desire to say anything to the person sitting in front of me and before I knew it is had disappeared back into the clouds. I can still see those five seconds clearly in my head, everything else for that night is a loss. I know who I was with and where I was at, but the actual small details escape me, all except for the sighting of the V-shaped object. It felt surreal to me.
