{jd} 2 bright white lights seemingly attached to each other moving se to nw

I was on a gravel road by the railroad tracks at about 1 am in the last part of August in 2008. I saw many falling stars in the area while cat fishing. That night I got there about 1 am and got out of my truck to see the light of what I thought was a falling star brightening breaking apart and coming at my direction from the se and heading in a nw direction at about 500 feet above the ground. It came just above me and I saw that it was moving much to slow to be a falling star. It was 2 lights at this point and was separated by about 4 inches width from my perspective while just at 90 degrees above me. When it was just above me it disappeared and I was unable to regain sight of the object. The part that really made me pack up and leave was that the lights did not flash at all and the object made no noise. I was within 3000 feet of highway 3/46 and there was not even a car or truck in the area at the time to make any noise. completely silent even when the object(s) were within 500 feet. The lights were stationary in distance from each other as if it were one object and stayed in a straight path. Very bright. But I was unable to see features of the object or objects that were the source of the lights.
