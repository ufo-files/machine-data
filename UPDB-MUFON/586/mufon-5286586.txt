18 to 21” clear sphere w/ 5-8 pinpoints of light inside.

I was watering a tree next to my house, when my dog went nuts barking at something. I went to the fence to see what he was barking at, and saw what I first thought was a spot of light at the front if the house across the street. Upon closer inspection, I realized it was an orb, 18 to 21 inches in dia, dancing around the front window of my neighbor’s house. I say “dancing”, but it was movin* almost exactly like a mosquito flies. Bouncing, and darting right to left. After about 40 seconds, it went almost to the ground, bobbed abit, then shot straight up about 25ft, then took off NE like a bat out of hell in level flight about 300ft and vanished, making a single “pop” sound in the process. The pinpoints of light in this thing reminded me of led’s with the bluish tinge almost identical to 5500K color temperature. There was no discernable mount for the lights or wires, as the object was totally clear, like a big soap bubble would be. The light was reflected off the ground and the house. The pop was the only sound I heard, but my nextdoor neighbor said his ring doorbell picked up a really weird noise from it.
