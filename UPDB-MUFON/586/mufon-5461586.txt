When I was a child, 7-10, I saw a white light through my window. It was bright and unmoving. This took place in the early 1990's. 

I do not remember my exact age or the date it occured, but it was in Mobile, AL. It was after my bed time, so I estimate 10pm. 

I could see the light through my window and watched it for several minutes. It did not move, it did not change color, it did not do anything. It didn't even sway slightly the way a helicopter does when hovering. 

It was some distance away, however. It was a mile or so, if I had to guess. It was west-southwest of the house I lived in at the time. 

It scared me, as I felt it shouldn't be there. It was not a normal light, not a star or plane. 

I got up and told my step-dad and mother about it, but they sent me to bed. When I layed back down, the light was gone. 

I've decided to report this after coming across the TV show, "Hangar 1" and out of curiousity. I have to wonder if anyone else saw/reported it as well.
