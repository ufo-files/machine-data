{rjl} Very bright like a star and very high altitude. It was slowly traveling straight upward, then stopped and remained stationary

It was 8:45 pm and I walked out of my house to pick up my daughter. It was then that I noticed the object south and slightly east from my house. It was very bright and looked like a star, except that it was moving. What drew my attention to it was the fact that it was moving but much brighter than any airplane. It was bright white and the altitude was very high. A plane, if it could even reach that high altitude, would not be visible. Secondly was the direction, the path of travel was straight up, as in perpendicular to our planet, and have never known aircraft to fly directly upward like that. It travelled slowly upward for a few minutes and then stopped and remained stationary, as if to blend in with the Stars, which had the same brightness. I took a pic last night and sent it to my husband via text message today with a description of what I had seen from our house. A month ago he installed The Ring app/camera system on our porch which records any porch activity. When I left our house the cameras started recording which inadvertently picked up the object in the recording, and my husband sent me a snapshot of the object as well.
