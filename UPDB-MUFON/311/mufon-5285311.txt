Daylight UAP near home

My wife and I witnessed a daylight UAP while looking at locations to fly our DJI Mini 2 drone. We witnessed the object 3 times (or possibly 3 different objects at different times), but were only able to capture one video.

We have no idea what it is, but when viewing the video in it's original form we are unable to see what we saw with our eyes. When we full screen the video and add just a bit of zoom, the object becomes very apparent. Due to the direction and elevations changes, the object must be moving extremely fast as even though we don't know how far away we were from it, as you can tell from the video it's quite a distance. 

We are happy to answer any questions anyone may have, our goal is just to get some idea of what this may have been.
