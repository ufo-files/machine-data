{rjl} Multiple orange orbs in a 1V1 formation, the orbs switched position like bird flying in formation. When off in distance objects looked like orange ribbon.

It was after the University of Maryland lost in the third round of the NCAA tournament. I was taking my dog out so she could use the bathroom before we went to bed for the night. While in the backyard I heard a sound that sounded like a electronic Seagull. I was watching an airplane flying from the Northeast toward the East, it had his it's lights on as I have seen before. The aircraft was following the same path that I have seen aircraft fly before. 

I then noticed what I first thought was maybe a meteor burning up as it came into the atmosphere. But it was note falling it was flying. As the orbs passed overhead from the East to the West they started in a flying 1V1 formation. Then each orb started to orbit each other like birds. Into multiple levels and 1V1 formations. I started to look around to see if there was anyone else out back to see what was happening. As the orbs continued to move West they started to look like a ribbon.

The ribbon started to look like the space ribbon from the Star Trek Generations movie. 

As it moved farther away the Seagull sound stopped.
