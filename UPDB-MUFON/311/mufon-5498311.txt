{rjl} Hovering blinking lights over my residential then abrupt and incredibly fast take off to somewhere else

My wife and I were out walking our dog around our neighborhood. We walk four blocks and it loops us back to our house. It takes about 20 minutes or so. The other night we noticed a hovering aircraft with blinking lights. It was completely stationary in the sky. We were a little alarmed, but with our close proximity to the Air Force base we just assumed it was and probably is a military craft. What puzzled us was its abrupt take off at a high rate of speed and as to why it was over a residential neighborhood. We have seen similar things and so have our neighbors. We were very troubled by this experience. Could it be extra terrestrial, we can't really say. I've seen a lot of aircraft in my time but nothing like that. Because of the repeated incidences, I'll start bringing my phone and try to catch some video next time.
