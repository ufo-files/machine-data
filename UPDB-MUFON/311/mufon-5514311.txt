(GS) Object with red and blue lights with sequential pattern

I was driving home after working late and I was driving tbrough an area where there had been a previous report of a red object flying so I always try to stay vigilant in the area. As I am driving through a construction site, I noticed a blue and red object flying relatively low to the ground. It was flying from northeast to Southwest. 

At first, I though it could be a helicopter, but it made no sound and it was flying too low to be an airplane. It emitted a white light for about 3 seconds and continued flying. I though to myself that no airplane or helicopter would have just blue and red lights but I though it could be possible.

Then as I saw the side of the object as it flyed past me, I saw something that I am knew was not "standard". It had a panel of three square blue lights and it blinked in sequence from left to right. Like one, two, three, slight pause .. one, two, three. Thats when I knew it was a UFO.

I watched it for 5 minutes and I never saw the blinking sequence lights or the white light again. It was just red. It went behind some trees and I lost it. When it blinked in sequence, I felt like it knew I was watching. I hope to see it again.
