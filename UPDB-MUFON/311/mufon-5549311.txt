huge round glowing , no sound and low altitude

I was sitting on the Porch of the Fishermans Motel just east of Ore City Texas. I had my 9 month old daughter outside with me. When out of the treetops to our south a huge glowing object appeared. This object was as big as the Motel that we were staying in. It put off a glow as if lights were on but emitted none to the ground. The object as bright as ir seemed did not illuminate anything, maybe it was higher than it appeared to be. This object was going to the North in a somewhat quick pace, but not fast. It tracked right down the path of the Boat landing which gave me the opportunity to try to gauge its size , by way of the tree lines on both sides of the boat ramp access road. It completely encompassed the roadway and then some. This object made no sound. It continued North accross the lake and then dissappeared from my sight due to elevation problems. It was by far the stangest thing I had ever witnesed for the Mid 70"s time era. I felt in awe of this object by its eminsity and the unkown feeling of what had just happened
