Sighting happened in the Papa site area at F.E. Warren AFB. I was a security police Airman.

We were on regular vehicle patrol. On our way to do security checklist on a missile site. We drove around a corner and out of the corner of my eye I saw what I thought was a pair of helicopters hoovering over this field. I stopped the truck to get out and observe, so did my team member. They were probably 400 meters to out South East. It seemed like a minute or so they hovered there. Still thinking they have to be out helicopters, I grabbed my mini mag light and waved it at them. Then they slowly really slow started approaching us. At like 10 or 15 miles an hour or so. Holding a perfect tight side by side formation. As they got closer I could see the three lights each object had were not like helicopter lights, one was out front the other two in the rear making a triangle pattern. They slowly drifted over out heads about 100 feet high. After they passed us they increased speed and turned west and out of sight behind a hill. Right away we both looked at each other, I said to my team member did you here that, he said here what. Exactly I said. It made no noise. 

That's my story.
