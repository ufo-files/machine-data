{jpg} Hovering orb, then a second passing through it at high speed

I took this on Jan 28th 2017 at around 2:30pm with an iphone 7 while playing golf at the Okeeheelee golf course in West Palm Beach FL. I saw a floating chrome sphere moving very slowly and started filming. I only filmed for a short time as we were moving on since there were people waiting behind us. The object made no sound...it almost looked like a giant balloon, bigger than a car. After watching it on video I noticed that at the 6th second there was another orb passing through it from right to left at an incredible speed. I'm a student pilot and I'm fairly familiar with aircraft and I'm quite certain that these objects were no helicopters or planes.
