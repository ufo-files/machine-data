Bright white light that pulsated from red to blue

I viewed this unusual object in the sky while flying from Beijing to Detroit on May 2, 2015. I was seated on the left side of the aircraft and noticed behind the left wing was a very bright white light, pulsating from red to blue. It wasn't a plane because it stayed about the same distance from us for about 30 minutes, no blinking, very pronounced in the sky, it stayed back in the same position about the whole time. I lost sight at about 30 minutes and I didn't see it leave, it just became more distant very slowly. It seem to stay at the same distance for most of the time, then it seemed like we were making distance on it before it became too difficult to see. I pointed it out to my husband who agreed it was unusual. It was somewhere in Western Canada after we passed over Alaska.
