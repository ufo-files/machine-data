Unknown glowing translucent object sighted by myself and neighbor

At approx 12:15am 01.01.12, my neighbor and I where watching some fireworks around our neighborhood. I saw what appeared at first as an aerial comet coming out of the southwest sky. We waited for it to explode but it didn't. It proceeded on a southwest to northeast at a significant rate of speed, following this course until it disappeared over the horizon. No sound was heard. It was a clear sky and there was no contrail or silouette of an aircraft or aircraft ID lights. I was curious to know if anyone else had seen this.
