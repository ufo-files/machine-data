Three bright glowing lights in the shape of a triangle.

11:30 at night at my friends house Me and my friends go outside and we just happen to look up at the sky and see some planes go by but then this triangle shaped figure with three white bright glowing lights flying low in the sky going at least 30 mph goes by in a straight path. Me and my friends were just shocked because of how big it was compared to the planes and I thought it strange that the lights were big and bright/glowing. The figure when flying made a humming noise. I kept watching the figure until I couldn't see it anymore.
