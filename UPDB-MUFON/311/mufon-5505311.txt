{cak} Watched 2/ within seconds 2 more then 3 then 3 more orange lights moving horizontally from South to North then appearred to head East.

There I was, sitting in my living room with the front blinds open, when the wife said,"do you see these? I just saw 2 of these orange lights go over and there's 2 more". We went out in the driveway to get a better look at them when 2 more came frome the South on the same course, then 3, then 3 more. We knew they weren't planes, or drones, or Chinese lanterns. All did the same course at the same steady speed and appearred to be over the river. No sound, no vertical change in course, and no flickering. It lasted about 15 minutes. Amazing, exiting, and probably a once in a lifetime event.
