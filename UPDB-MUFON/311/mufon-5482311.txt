I was just going to bed,looked out of my window, everything was quite,calm and dark. I suddenly noticed movement above my head watched for about 2 seconds before I lost sight below the house roof opposite, this wing shape craft was travelling at incredible speed with no sound as the window was wide open and no visible light, travelling from West to east of Barnstaple, North Devon, England. Very light cloud cover with a lot of moon light. As this wing disappeared over the horizon I made a note of the time,1240am still in shock and not quite believing what I've just witnessed, thinking did I just make this up! Then within seconds the cloud that this wing fly over, lite up from the back an orange glow and then stopped. That showed me that what I had seen had happened. This is the second major sighting in 25 years in Devon. I'm still in shock of the incredible speed this craft was travelling at, I have nothing to compare it with, concord used to fly over Devon, it made this look like the slowest craft on the planet.
