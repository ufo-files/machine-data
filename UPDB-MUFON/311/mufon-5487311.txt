{nd} Glanced up at sky after getting out of car, saw craft going straight sideways and faded into sky.

Returning from Grocery Store,
Got out of car to walk in apartment, glanced at sky and saw object almost directly above, but very high up. At least several miles high. Object was boomerang shaped, but point of Boomerang was not pointing in direction of travel. Almost as if pointed object was moving sideways. I knew instantly it was a UFO. It was NOT of human origin. My reaction was "Holy ****, what the hell is that?!?" The object continued to move fast across the sky and faded away. Only witnessed object for about 20 seconds.
