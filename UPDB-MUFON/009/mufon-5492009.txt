I was laying out on the beach looking at the stars when one of the stars began to fly mimicking the path of a shooting star. It changed direction drastically and was flying 10 times faster than a plane or jet. As soon as it reached the clouds it disappeared. The aircraft was orange red and in the shape of an orb. This is the third time I have witnessed this type of aircraft. They look like the aircraft depicted in world war 2 used by the germans but the speed is far advanced possibly somehow close to the speed of light.
