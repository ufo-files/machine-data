{jpg} UFO observed flying at light speed

One evening in November, I took my dog outside for a walk to go restroom on his leash. While walking, I was observing the night sky and saw what I thought was the space station. A small star like object was slowly crossing the sky and appreared at the same orbit as the space station. However, as I watched this object it stopped, and then shot off into deep space at a tremendous speed. It even left a light trail behind it. I remember thinking to myself it appeared to be something out of a Star Trek movie.
