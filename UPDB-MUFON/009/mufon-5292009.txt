Seen orange flashing light hovering over trees

I was on my way to the baby sisters house to pick up my kids. I worked afternoons at the time so it was about 11:30pm.the baby sister lived a mile away from my house. I can see her house from my house.we live in the country so it’s nothing but fields.after work I stopped at home to let my dog out. Then I got into my car drove less then a quarter mile and seen an orange flashing light over the trees across from the baby sitters house. I didn’t think anything of it. I figured it was a tower for internet or cell phones.Then just before I pulled into the baby sitters house I thought to mySelf..wait them towers flash red not orange.so I continued to pull into the baby sitters drive way. They brought my kids out to my car . I told them what I had seen.they said hmm well that is strange. They continued to talk for a good 20 minutes. All the sudden the bright orange light comes shooting out of the corn field right next to the baby sitters house .The corn field was to the north of their house. It seemed as big as a car. It had no sound at all. It shot straight up about 100 to 200 feet then continued on an angle towards the East! I said see you guys seen that right? They all said oh yes we did and what the heck was that? I said good question but that’s the thing I seen on the way here!! It felt good that they had seen it to! As I was telling them that I had seen it on the way to their house.. they gave me a feeling as they didn’t believe me!! Well they seen it also!!!
