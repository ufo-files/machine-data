Circular craft spotted over Hamilton

Circular craft spotted over Hamilton. I first thought it was a drone but realised it was flying too fast. There was no sound. Thing was black matte colour, managed to get a photo of it from Almada Tower. When viewed with binoculars this was the saucer shape craft which appeared to wobble briefly though on the photo appears circular.
