{tjd} Video of orb in the sky changing direction

My daughter and I were on our way into town at around 9pm. Once we got on the road we noticed a huge orangish colored light that was significantly lower than the stars, and way to low for a plane. I’ve been seeing this light in the sky almost every night for about two years now. It’s almost a guarantee that it will be up there tomorrow as well, sometimes there’s two. The light looks very bright and is usually stationary giving off the illusion of a star, however it’s apparent that it is much closer than a star. Some nights on really cloudy nights, it’s the only light in the sky. At first I assumed it was Venus, but Venus doesn’t change direction. I sometimes think I’m going nuts, and assuming that it’s a ufo, when it’s actually just a bright star. However, on this night I captured the object moving across the road and changing direction. I had another video from last summer where I caught two of these things, but that video is lost on another broken device. Please tell me I’m not going nuts. I plan on trying to get more videos of this thing.
