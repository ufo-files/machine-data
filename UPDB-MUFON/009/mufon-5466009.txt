Bright orb over Lake

I was about to go to bed when i saw a bright flickering light over Lake Ontario. Northeast off to the right off downtown Toronto. Then I set up my camera on a typod with a telescopic lens 150 - 600. I have photos and videos of the orb which lasted for around one hour. 
All photos and videos taken from fourth floor of my condo out across the lake.
