{rjl} White orbs changing color, blinking, and behaving like sparklers

I was at my parent's house for a visit when I got out of my car and turned around to make sure they had checked the mail when I saw the UFO. On the drive to there house from my work I thought I had seen something in the sky but I was driving on a wooded road and could not see well. I first thought the light orbs to be drones as I had seen them at their house before. However, these did not appear the same. Several white light orbs would appear and disappear with at least two visible at all times. They would change color from white to red. I felt that I should record the event ans so I pulled out my phone and video recorded the orbs. I then realized that these were not drone when they began to look like white fireworks or sparklers in the sky but made no sound and left no smoke. After appearing like fireworks they briefly disappeared, approximately for ten seconds and reappeared as red orbs. I began to get scared and so I went inside the house. When I returned the lights were gone.
