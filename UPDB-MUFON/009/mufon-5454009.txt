{jpg} Web of [50] lights [in a grid like flight pattern] headed east over Lake Huron [djs]

A friend of mine called from about a mile away to go look into the sky. I went outside and saw more than 50 lights arranged in a grid like flight pattern flying west to east toward Lake Huron. 

When I tried to set up the camera to record the procession stopped, but one trailing behind made an abrupt change in direction and adjusted twice more before moving away behind a cloud.
