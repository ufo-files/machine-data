First spotted these on 11/15/21

I've been seeing the same two objects since 11/15/21. I reported to you after seeing them two nights in a row. I've seen them every night, except for two nights, when the cloud cover was too dense to see anything. The two seem to come every night in approximately the same spots. I can only see one clearly through my very little monocular - meant for bird watching. It looks a bit like a tire with square lights along the rubber and there appears to be a dome shape over the "hub cap" part. One night, it appeared to have a red lighted circle (like a shooting target) in the center, surrounded by a thin white light, surrounded by blue; it had a yellow, 6-8 spiked star over the center of the red circle, which made the blue lights turn green at times. It looked a bit like a super hero shield. I couldn't tell if this was the top or bottom of the object. I seem to see this around 5:42 nightly, although I'm not always able to check - or to stay out late enough to know when they actually go away. I did see the two regulars arrive at 5:25 pm on 11/24; there was also a third one that night and the one that sets higher up descended closer that night; they all seems to be equal distance from each other. 
ie: As I stand facing west, they align (left to right) south to north - almost at the same height. The one in the middle (the regular one that I can see best) does move very slowly north after awhile. The one to his left is the one that's usually higher and the one to his right only showed on 11/24.
