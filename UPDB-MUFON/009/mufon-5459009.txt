{jpg} 10 ft round cluster of about 8-10 very bright lights in a round ball with a beam of light coming from it.

Travelling down rt. 17 south near Hertford in N.E. N.C. around 2 am. Ball of light was behind me about the length of a football field, 300 ft, in distance about 10 ft in diameter about 20 ft above ground over roadway kept pace with me at 70 mph heading south toward Hertford. A beam of light was coming from it visible on my left shooting sse past me. After about 5 minutes the ball of light began gaining on me closing to approximately 100 ft behind me in a second or two, same position 20 ft above roadway. At this point I became alarmed at the speed it gained on me. It remained a minute or two and I picked up my cell phone to turn on the camera because whatever was going to happen I wanted to get it recorded. when I picked up my phone it immediately returned to its original position approximately 300 ft behind me, same 20 ft above road and remained there for around 4-5 minutes and then disapeared. A couple of minutes later it re-appeared behind me same distance and height from ground this time on my left with beam still projecting out ahead of me to my left. It remained there for about 3-4 minutes and then disapeared. That is the end of sighting.
