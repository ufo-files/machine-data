{rjl} UFO over tree in yard

I know this is along time ago, I saw a light shining on our gas tank in the back yard I looked out the side window and saw this just above a tree in our yard. It was real close to the house. It had red and blue lights under it flashing, I went to get my husband up and he would not come When I went back to the window shaking it was gone. I am telling you I saw this up close and have told several people over the years even my doctor. My mother in law said she saw a report in the paper that an older couple was driving home out in the country like we live and it stop their car. I am getting older and I just wanted to report this that I did see it and up close.
