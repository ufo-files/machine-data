The object was too fast to see. It appeared in a few frames of an iphone live photo that I took of some pretty clouds while waiting at a stoplight. 
1. I was on my way home from work on Aug. 3, 2018. While stopped at a traffic light, I noticed the clouds looked really pretty, so I took a picture so that I could paint the clouds later (I1m an artist). I did not see the object or hear anything out of the ordinary, but there was a lot of traffic.
2. I noticed the object today, Aug. 5, 2018, when I was looking at the frames of the live photo from my iphone.
3. I didn1t know what it was - but it was in only 4-5 frames of a 3-second clip, so it was very fast. 
4. It moved from the left of the frame, at least a block away, and exits up out of the top of the frame as it flies overhead, above the electical wires but below the clouds. When I enlarged the image, I could see that while it appears disk-like, in at least one frame, the shape looks a little more complex. It is a dark object, but there are a couple of reflections off the top, and maybe a couple of legs or something hanging down underneath. It flies in a straight path.
5. I thought there must be a reasonable explanation - bug on the window, military craft, something, but they don1t add up. I sent it to my daughter and she thought it was pretty inexplicable too, so I thought I should report it, in case it is something.
6. I did not see it at the time, so I don1t know what happened after it flew over the intersection.
