Me and my mate went camping in the local woods on a local mountain. We were lucid, just having a good time with the fire, cooking, ect. Late at night or early in the mourning I should say, we saw orbs seamlessly pop into existence. They looked like orange/red in hue, and when we noticed it happening they hovered, fluttered around and blinked in a repetitive motion as if it were a hand wave gesture or that we were recognizing each other. Astonished I managed to record some of it, but looking back at the recording, the orange/firey hue was lost in the recording. It only shows what I think to believe is the light that my phone was able to detect, not detectable by recordings, like my camera was only able to pick up some of its color on the color spectrum the 'thing' was emitting. I turn around and come back to realize whatever it was its gone now or can't bee seen. 

Later me and my friend were talking about it, and I believe what this is called 5th contact. I was just thinking to my self about how it could be right in-front of me and I'd never know because you can't dismiss the devil's proof, then suddenly I hear a ghoulish sounding laugh in my head like the thing was reading into my thoughts and taunting me. Nothing was there, but it's laugh was. My friend couldn't hear it, but he saw me leap out of fear. Yes I jumped and fell to the ground. This was all in one instance it felt like, you know like the sensation you feel when you think somethings going to happen and it suddenly does, this was that feeling exactly just I was hit with an ESP type laugh that left me startled. My friend couldn't hear it, but just asked me what happened when I fell.
