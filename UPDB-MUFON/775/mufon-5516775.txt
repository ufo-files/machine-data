I had not given this sighting another thought until I learned a pilot flying into KCI airport reported something he had seen. I drive into work early every morning between 4:50am-5:09am, which is the time I reach close to downtown KCMO. I will never forget what I saw. At first I thought I was just tired and imagined it. While driving on I-35 south (close to downtown KMCO) was a disc like shaped object. It was kinda of blue/white and it was indeed some sort of craft. It was just sitting in the sky/hovering. It then moved a bit to the right/flying, and then stopped and suddenly went vertical. I have never seen an airplane or helicopter make a move like that. It seemed impossible. It disappeared into the sky after moving very fast vertically. Very very scarey object-it was clearly not manmade that I could tell. What is going on.
