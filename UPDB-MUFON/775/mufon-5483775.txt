Ufo hoverd over the houses behind Heworth Metro Station

Me and my brother worked as cleaners at Heworth Metro Station and the night before we had seen a ufo streak across the sky towards South Shields, early in the morning my brother took several pictures behind the station, but never developed the film a week later. then he rang me and told me he thinks he has captured a ufo in one of the pictures. I was blown away when I seen the picture, it was exactly the same ufo we seen the night before, but this time the ufo is dark, against the sunlight, but the ufo at night glowed white.
The only thing my brother told me, that he did not see the object when he took the picture. so I assume the ufo was invisible to the naked eye.
