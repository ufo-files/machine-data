Saw light orb moving faster than the airplane I was on

I was on a flight from Honolulu to Atlanta, departing 4:30pm and arriving 7:00am. After the sun set I decided to observe the night sky. I saw stars and shooting stars - how nice!
Then I noticed a star shaped light, same brightness as all the other stars, no tail, no twinkle, it did not outshine any other star in the sky. If I was not remaining focused to the window I would have missed it. It was moving faster than my airplane! I blinked thinking what the heck is that, and nope it was still there, moving along. So maybe it was a satellite? But then it diverted from a straight line and moved down then up but still moving straight. It never went backwards. Overall it took about 5 minutes to move across my little window until I couldn’t see it anymore.
