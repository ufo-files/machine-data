{jpg} Saw info around 65 or 66 with aunt. Flew directly in front descending behind crop of large trees like it was landing.

Around 1965 or 66 @I was help feeding and counting cattle at my aunt's farm. It was southwest of Wilson, Oklahoma, I was 8-9 years old. I never told anyone till my aunt brought it up 7 years ago at our family reunion. I was so excited because I had always wondered did it really happen. I'd pretty much forgot about it by then. She described it exactly like I remember, she is now 89 I think, but great memory. Next day after police checked it out and only found an old washing machine with the squeegies. Nothing else!! Craft was very close, descending behind a group of trees. Probably 30-40 ft in air coming down at maybe 45 DEGREE angle and looked like it was going to land. Aunt Faye asked me if r saw it, I did. To this day we swear it's God truth, I swear the on my children and grandchildren!!! I have since told my family and that's it, don't want to be laughed at.
