{rjl} Lighted craft slowly moving south a quarter mile from my location. Military jet craft in pursuit

I observed a single large white lighted object from my car window as we were driving home in Union Kentucky June of 1988. The object was very bright in the sky so I took notice of it out my left side window. Once we got home I continued to watch it as it slowly moved across the sky. I watched it for about 10 minutes from the front yard until I noticed 3 jet fighter planes moving across the sky from the north on an intercept path. The white object seemed to take evasive action and flew south. When it did it also seemed to shoot out a green and a right lighted objects. All three objects now took different directions, one moving up across the sky, one moving down across the sky while the white object seemed to get smaller and smaller. I assume it was just moving very fast in a southerly direction. The fighter jets made it to the far south location of the white lighted object and all three objects were now gone. The jets circled and then flew back north from where they came.
