{rjl} My husband first spotted the 3 reddish lights appear over the school across the street from our house.

My husband & I were having a late dinner grilling out, when we noticed 3 bright red lights over the school building across the street to the east of our home traveling north at a high rate of speed. We ruled out them being aircraft, because they had no blinking green or red lights. We ran to the street to take a picture with my cellphone. I got 3 pictures before they were unable to view behind the taller section of the school building. We then walked down the street to the north to look for them. At that point, we could only see one and cars were coming, so we had to get out of the street.
