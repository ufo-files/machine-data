Pulsating orb

This is the second time in the past few months that I have seen this object. It appears star like at first but noticeably moves in a figure 8 pattern and other movements that would be impossible for any type of air craft to do. It will pulsate a white/blue light and then disappear completely but show up in another spot. My step dad is a pilot and can confirm this is unlike any object he’s ever seen and agrees about the strange movement being impossible for a normal aircraft. Also earlier today around 6 (right before the sun starts to go down) I thought I had seen it hovering and it appeared as the typical silver saucer. I have reported this last month and have a video of it but tonight I haven’t been able to get one.
