2 fireballs. Almost like chinese lanters, but not

Out enjoying the 4th of July doing fireworks in out road and I happened to look to the south and see 2 fireball looking circles in the sky. I had been watching fireworks in that part of the sky all night and these stood out. They never disappeared like a firework so I watched and realized to pull out my phone. They came from the south heading north initially and slowly veared towards the east. They both headed east at the same time. I wouldnt describe the lights as pulsing, more like a flame. A perfect circle flame. Red and orange, but my camera didnt get the color, just light.
Once they headed east we lost 1 behind a few tree lines but saw the other the whole time until it literally vanished into thin air. My last video got blurry, but you can clearly see it, and then its just gone. And thats what we saw. Sorry about us talking in the back.
