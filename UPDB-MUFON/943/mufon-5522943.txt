Silver Cigar with black band no contrail, higher than jet with contrail, object passed over the jet, could still see object, jet didnt block all of object

On 10/26/11 I was standing on a roof and a silver object caught my eye. Silver with a black band near / around the center, and as high as it was I thought that could be the wing that I couldnt see clearly, and no contrail at all. As I watched I noticed a passenger jet approaching following the curvature of the Earth with contrails streaming, giving it the appearance of climbing....to a point, so I figured I could now judge how high the silver object was. This silver object was travelling much faster that the jet approaching each other, it would seem due to the speed difference that the silver object was lower...but when the flight paths intersected the jet was under the object because I could still see 2/3rds of the silver object and all of the jet. So as Im throwing out expletives of amazement I noticed that the silver object was not getting lower in the sky...it was going higher, it went up and out, not the downward following the curvature as did the jet. The time was 3:31 pm, I went to an Aviation Map for real-time tracking, went to replay the flights over Orange County, N.Y. and it wouldnt do the replay for that time of day, and it was travelling North to South , the jet was South to North.
