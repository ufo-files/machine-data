(GS) Starlike in the line below orions belt, flashing crazy colors moving slow and randomly, 2 nights in a row

Me and a friend were outside my home up in the hills near Glendale community college in Glendale, California, two nights in a row now, when it gets dark and the stars come out, theres this bizzare starlike object, looks so different from all other stars in many ways: 1) its lower (closer to horizon) than all other stars, 2) its brighter and constantly changing colors randomly, red, green, blue, purple, 3) me and my friend were staring at it for a while, ten fifteen minutes straight and both came to the conclusion that it is not stationary, moving so slightly that u had to stare at it a while to notice the movement, goes left, stops, right, stops, towards us, stops, back, stops, all so slowly, always changing color. We pointed it out to a visitor arriving and he noticed movement as well. Just so bizzare, what is this please tell me? It has been two nights in a row now. Its still out there, anyone can see it. If u draw a line down towards the horizon starting from orions belt, its right along that line...
