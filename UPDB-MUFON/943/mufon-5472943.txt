Was in backyard looking for uranus with telescope black triangle shaped with circles on bottom flew west to east right over my head i was astonished

Oct 19 about 11pm was in backyard looking for uranus to view with telescope. Looked to west at bright star was about to look at when object flew right into my line of sight. I have researched this subject so i recognized it immediately. Object was a flying triangle with multiple round thing on the bottom. This is the 1st time ive ever had anything like this happen,and i felt like i couldnt have been the only person to see it. Selfridge ang base is 10 miles away and i frequently see aircraft from there flying over,but ive never seen any craft like this one.
