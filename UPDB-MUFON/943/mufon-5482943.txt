2 UFOs flying from side to side, against each other's directions then flew away diagonally

My father and I were laying down on the bench at our patio, it was very light drizzle and overcast skies. My father told me to look towards right below the clouds and I saw one light moving from side to side, seemed to be very far, approx 10 km or more away, then another light arrived from the clouds. I asked my dad "What are those things?" he replied "UFOs" , I asked "Like Extraterrestrial?" He said "Yes. No human craft known can flight at such speed.Nothing covers a distance of 5kms in less than a second. Keep watching." The lights seemed to go towards each other, as if playing "tag" for around 3 minutes, then suddenly flew diagonally towards the sky. No fear, just a sense of curiosity and wonder. I don't believe in ETs, but that was too much to ignore.
