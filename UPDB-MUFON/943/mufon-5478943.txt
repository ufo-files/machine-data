{dr}Hovering and zipping moving bright multicolored lights shaped like a saucer

I just laid down and was looking at the stars as I do regularly from my bed through a sliding glass door. I noticed an exceptionally bright (star) become much brighter and started to show colors. It then made several movements left and right and up and down. It then got closer and I got out of bed and grabbed my binoculars to see better. I then sat on the edge of the bed as my optics were attached to a tripod. I watched for another 5 minutes and woke up my wife who was sleeping soundly. I asked her to look and she agreed with what I saw. I could see a basic shape of disc. It had green and red lighting on either side of a large white light. It quit zigging and zagging and became stationary for another 15 minutes.....I could see the lights clearly with my optics. I decided to get some sleep and laid back down. I just knew this was no helo or airplane which I see every clear evening. This was the real thing..totally convinced here.
