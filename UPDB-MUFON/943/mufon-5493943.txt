{cak} Observed lights just standing still & thought there was a grand opening about a mile away.

My mother & I were out for a walk one evening "she is deceased now". When I noticed four lights & thought there might be a "grand opening of a store", but then noticed they were just not moving. We observed the lights to be at an angle exactly spaced apart, so we just stopped & stared. All of a sudden the first light took off coming our direction; then the 2nd light took off still spaced exactly apart.  Then the 3rd light & 4 lights did the same thing coming out direction. I knew they weren't airplanes as they didn't have the normal flashing lights that airplanes have. When they passed by us all you could hear was a "hum", they were traveling in the south like towards Tucson. Now I remember that there had been reports of "Lights over Phoenix" on the news so I attributed this event to be connected to that. It was the strangest thing I have ever seen. I didn't know of your organization till I happened to watch an episode just now on TV. I have always wanted to tell someone about what we saw but didn't know who. Anyway I'm 77 getting close to 78 and wanted to tell someone. Thank you for listening. Sincerely, [Name removed/cms/tg]. You can check the date I gave you if you look for articles about "The lights over Phoenix", I'm pretty sure is was in 1997 or 1998.
