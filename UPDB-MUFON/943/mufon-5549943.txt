A green triangle flew past a 3 story window.

A green triangle flashed by a 3 story window. My witness only saw the flashfrom his peripheral vision, as he was not facing the window. I saw the flashand the green triangle flew by in about 3 seconds. It appeared from the NW andflew to the SE direction and vanished. No interruption from the radio, noelectrical interruption, no affects whatsoever. The building is on the top of ahill and we were on the 3rd story.
