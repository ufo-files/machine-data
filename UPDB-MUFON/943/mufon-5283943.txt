2nd sighting of red/green lights

I saw something similar the first night after moving to Oregon.

Before this occurrence, sometime around 6:00pm I saw a red light out my window that looked like taillights going down the road. We don’t get many cars out here, so the ones that pass, I do notice. This was odd except the red light kept going straight at a fast pace where there the road only goes left or right. About 45 seconds after, there was a helicopter in the area and then a loud plane. 

I told my husband this at dinner and hopped on here to see if there were other sightings, and saw some reportings from tonight. He said we should pay attention as we drive back, as there might be weird things going on right now. 

Towards the end of our drive, I saw what looked like the top of a radio tower, except it slowly started to rise while hovering in place. It looked very similar shape/color wise to what I saw the first night I landed in Oregon. (I submitted a report for that as well.) I lost sight as we were driving, but just odd.
