3 glowing orange donut shaped craft spaced out ever 2 minutes.

Returning from dog walk at 8:35-8:40 pm 11/21/20. Was not wearing glasses at time. Noticed single orange, unblinking light at what appeared to be a "normal" airplane traveling height. Did not resemble airplane- only orange light, no tail/wing flashing lights. I ran inside to get glasses. Returned as it was passing overhead and could see that it was donut shaped: an orange ring . If I held my arm out and were to "grab it" with my fingertips, the object was a 1/4". Stayed in a steady, continuous path roughly east to west. From tree line in the east to tree line in the west, the objects cleared that distance in just about a minute. A couple minutes later, a second object appeared in the same easterly direction on virtually the same trajectory as the last. Same glowing orange donut appearance. My wife witnessed this one. Another couple minutes passed and a third object appeared but lower. I ran inside to find binoculars but couldn't. The orange of the third object was more intense possibly due to it's lower altitude. My wife described it as fiery but had the same donut shape as the others when seen from below. all three behaved the same way, traveling in a straight steady path. All three looked like a "headlight" when viewed from the front but lost that appearance when seen below. Other airplanes were clearly visible in the sky, all in stark contrast to these objects with blinking white or red lights. I couldn't hear a sound but attribute that to altitude. Generally, I was very excited durning the first two, finding the subject of UFO's both fascinating and scary. I began to feel uneasy at the third one, mostly due to my imagination and some deep seated fears about UFO's from my youth. I popped out a few times later in the next hour but saw nothing else.
