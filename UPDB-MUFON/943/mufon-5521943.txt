Witnessed bright orange light, slowly descended, changed color.

I was stargazing around a campfire with my brother when we witnessed a large orange glowing object to the east. It was a very clear night, the time was around midnight. My mother, who was in a nearby cabin, had a pair of binoculars. I watched the object through the binoculars for about 5 minutes. It hovered at first then slowly descended down. Just before it fell beyond the horizon it changed from bright orange to bright blue.
