I confirmed to him that I could see the object, it looked strange to me as well, not a plane or anything similar. The object was in a triangular shaped, made up of 8 lights. He tells me that he counted 9 lights. The lights were yellow in color, apart from the lights, there was no defined shape that I could see, partly because of the slight clouds in the sky, and 1light pollution1. The object was flying, but not in a conventionally way, that is to say as an aircraft flies, it was definitely moving, but at a slower speed, it was within our view for about 3 minutes or so before it was obscured by a building.

We do not undertake in any sorts of drugs or hallucinogenics and are of sound mind. My cousin does not believe in extraterrestrial life, I am open to the possibility and am very interested, this is the first time I have ever witnessed something like this.
