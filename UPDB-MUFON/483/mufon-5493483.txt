{jpg} observed around 15 pulsating orange objects for almost an hour period 5/29/15.

My Wife took the dog out ~ midnight and shortly after she yelled at me to come outside quickly. When I went out she pointed into the SW sky at two objects that were like flaming orbs that about 10 degrees apart above the horizon. The were flying at a steady rate and she said there were several more before it. The two objects appeared to be part of a larger object. As this object disappeared beyond our site another object appeared from the same southern direction traveling at about the same speed one after the other (no sounds) sometimes in pairs but almost identical in size although some of them seemed to be 'strobing' whereas others looked like they were pulsating. Shortly after we saw the last object a fog bank rolled in and we went back inside a little after 1AM EST. I went back out to check a few times and didn't see anymore but apparently the fog had also move on as well. We did see a few planes flying at cruising altitude and we are between some airports in the area so seeing a plane with it's landing and/or marker lights is normal for us as they'll int typically be decelerating for final approach around here. These weren't any planes that we've seen before and when they disappeared they appeared to pick up speed to a point that the light just became impossible to see.
