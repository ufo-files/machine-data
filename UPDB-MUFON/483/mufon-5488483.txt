{nd} a white light like no white ever seen before right above me and cousin that moved over us and then vanshished

Me and my cousin were out side on 12/25/1963 at around 6pm playing in the dark when all of a sudden a super bright white light(a white that I have never seen before) it was about 13 stories above us and moved over us about 5 miles an hour, the whole area was lit up like day light. We watched this light move over us it seem like a 60 seconds. it then vanished like someone turned off the light. we thought it was a meteor and that it crashed so we went to look up the hill but it was to dark and we could not see past a foot. We went back to the house and I turned on the TV to see what the news had to say, but nothing, so I then called the local radio station to see If they knew anything about a comet crashing which they said no. the crazy thing is that I remember looking at my watch seeing that it was 7pm and the time I called the radio station was around 8:30pm. It could no have taken a hour and a half to walk up the hill and back to the house. My cousin never remember the sighting, he died at 58 from a rare bone cancer that made him go from 5 feet 9 to 5 feet 2 and the looked like he was 90 years old. I have a feeling that our encounter has some missing time. This happened in Ansonia, Ct on 12/25/1963.
