Four spinning lights over World's of Fun in Kansas City, MO

While standing in line for a haunted house, I suddenly looked up to see spinning orbs in the sky. At first I thought it could be balloons, but my husband said they were moving too fast and the wind want blowing that direction. I didn't have a chance to see where they went. They were so fast.
