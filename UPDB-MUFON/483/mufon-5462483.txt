{cak} bright star like (space shuttle like)

I was out in front of my home after taking the trash out for pick-up. Walked back up our driveway and noticed my USA flag was tangled around the pole. When I looked up to untangle the line, I saw this object moving from south to north in a straight line. I thought it was to space station but it was moving way to fast for that. I watch the space station sometimes when I know it is coming over or the space shuttle when it did and remembered about how fast the moved across the sky. But this object moved from straight about my head to out of sight on a very clear day in just a few seconds. I went in the house and looked up to path of the space station and it was over the Atlantic somewhere. It made no sound nor sound barrier boom. Did anybody else see it or report it ? Do you know what it might of been ? 
Thank you.....Tom
