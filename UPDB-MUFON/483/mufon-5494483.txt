{dr}Noticed directly overhead; twin propeller plan flying perpendicular

I noticed a loud buzzing sound followed by chirps: a humming bird was flying in a large semilunar dips by me 3-4 times (seemed unusual, maybe I was close to a nest). I saw it fly off, I started looking for it and then looked up seeing a large cigar/cylindrical object directly overhead. I thought "wow, it's a blimp." I noticed a twin engine propeller plane flying perpendicular to the object. The object was moving fast went directly over the propeller plane on a southward course. I ran into the house and got my wife and girls to witness the event. It flew over a cloud, reappeared then faded into the distance. Less than five minutes later a commercial airliner appeared (wings clearly visible)--it was 1/2 the size and moving 1/3 to 1/5 the speed of the cylindrical shaped object.
