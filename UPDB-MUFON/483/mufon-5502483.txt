{rjl} 2 dim lights moving in tandem, the lights would get very bright and then dim. moving north, no pattern.

My husband and I were on back patio stargazing while we let our dog out. There appeared 2 dim lights flying or moving in tandem with one slightly trailing the other. One of the 2 objects would glow very bright while the other would stay dim. As we watched them cross the sky they both would intermittently disappear and reappear. There was no cloud cover to hide them. There was a plane a few moments later that we compared them to and the plane's lights didn't change like the object did. We tried to locate them but they simply disappeared. My husband kept dying he didn't want to believe it was a ufo but willingly admitted it was because whatever we saw scared him. We were both very sober at the time.
