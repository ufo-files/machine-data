{rjl} UFO Sighting?

I have thought about this quite a bit over the years but never had any idea of who I couild share this with or anyone who would know anything about this and take me seriously. What I saw happened about 45 years ago one night when I was about 12 or 13 in a suburb of Denver, back when we had clear nights. My older brother and I were laying in the grass in our front yard looking at the stars, just talking. He decided to go inside, naturally, and when he did I saw something just appear from nowhere. It looked exactly like a star, no smaller or larger, and it was moving in random, almost in a "beserk" way at incredible speed. It was moving all over the place from west to east. There was no sound, trail or anything. It looked like as if it were millions of miles away, as I said, just as bright and the same size of any star you see in the night sky. If I were to try to measure the distance it traveled with a ruler, say holding it over yourself, it moved about one foot, if that makes sense. It lasted maybe 10-15 seconds then was gone. I stayed outside for a few more minutes to see if I could see it again but it was gone. I ran inside to tell my family what I saw and of course no one believed me. Before and since I have seen satellites, including Skylab back in the day and the ISS, and this was no satellite. Can someone please help me? I just want to know, if possible, what I saw. Thank you
