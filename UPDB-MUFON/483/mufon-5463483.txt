My husband and I were sitting outside on the balcony relaxing and chatting when from a distance we noticed three objects coming out way. It traveled above us in a straight line possibly turning slightly in one direction. There were three of them, very bright (fluorescent) and large, one in front and two beside and back a bit and very large and very high, circular in shape. I descended the outside stairs and into the middle of the street, not taking my eyes off them, until they disappeared. I was in awe of it all. That night, I had a dream that I was standing at my bedroom window and there was a very large circular spacecraft with little windows all around it at the edges and it just hovered there in front of me. When I awoke the next morning it all seemed so real. I didn't report it at the time, but always wondered if anyone else saw them. I checked in the reports but can't see where anyone reported seeing them at the time. I know it was either late in 1968 or early 1969, as I had my baby that year in July and moved to this apt. in the fall of 1968. My husband also saw them, but stayed put on the balcony.
