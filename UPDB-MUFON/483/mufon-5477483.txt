{cak} Light in the night sky, as far away as a star, moved incredibly fast, changed direction multiple times before it vanished.

I was outside of my friends house this past summer, in Missoula Montana, listening to music, and I saw a very fast moving yellow/gold/white light. I first noticed the light because I was looking at the stars and saw movement, at first I thought it was a shooting star because of the light and trail. I called my friends attention to it. The light then descended rapidly, then changed direction 4 times before vanishing with some kind of warp drive, leaving behind a glowing trail of light in the sky! At first I we were astonished and in disbelief, then I became excited and I wanted to tell people, I only told 3 friends because I didn't think people would believe me. I lost sight of the object when it covered an incredible distance leaving behind a glowing trail vanishing beyond the horizon. I have seen planes, shooting stars, comets and satellites, I have never seen anything move like this, neither has my friend. My friend was a non-believer until this happened. I just want to know if anyone else saw this.
