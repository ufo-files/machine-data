At 02:02am I first observed the unidentified object in the sky. The object was emitting lights and it became more brighter by taking that time. After duration of 20 minuses or so it disappeared sunderlying and the light went up into the sky.

It was round about 2a.m. I got up from my work. Star-gazing has been my hobby since the time I grew up to know about terrestrial objects. Nothing goes missing from comets to meteorites, planets to eclipses. So as usual I was staring at the night sky. It was totally dark, no spots of clouds or moon was seen as far as I could see! Suddenly a bright object came near to my sight. I imagined it as some planet but was awestruck when the lights became brighter and nearer. It was just approximately miles away. It was clearly visible without any special equipments. The object was visible for not more than 20 to 25 mins and by 02:20am its lights became smaller and later it disappeared into the sky. I was able to see how it diminished and and there was no sign of any unusual effects or harms caused to human lives. It was a very unforgettable moment of my life and I wanted to share my experience with all of you through MUFON.
