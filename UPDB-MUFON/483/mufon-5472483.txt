It was approx 6AM and I just took the garbage out to the curb. As I was going back to the house, I noticed a large silver sphere just in the air. It didn't move, it just was stationary in mid air? I did not have a clue to what this thing was. That's when I thought to get my camera and video tape it. I hurried back in the house and on the way, I told my wife about it, that she should come out and see this thing,but she was doing something on the computer and was more occupied with that.
On video,it appeared as a energy source that was emitting from the object. But, when seen visually, it looked like a solid sphere?
After about half a minuet, the object just shot straight up. Like a bullet.
It appeared to be metallic. It was like a big silver ball? It seemed to be about, 3 to say 5 feet in diameter.
