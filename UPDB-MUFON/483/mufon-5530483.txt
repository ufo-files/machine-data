Red/orange UFO orb hovering over Calosahatchee River.

my girlfriend at the time and I had seen a orange/red orb. We had decided to take a walk that night as it was a cool and clear night. For some weird reason, we had no forms of technology on us at the time. It was weird for her to ever be without her cell phone. We walked down to the the river front near her house and what surprised us was an orange/red orb floating over the river about 200 yards from us. It looked like the sun. We watched it go from about the size of a large beach ball to what I would say was roughly 10 ft across. Completely silent. It is possible that it was moving closer and farther away from us, but at the time I swear we both agreed that it was stationary. The object was hovering very close to the water. After about 20 minutes of watching this object shrink and grow in size, we became less interested and went back to get binoculars and a camera. when we returned, the object was gone. I have been a private pilot for 10 years and have a pretty good idea of what belongs in the sky and this was nothing I had ever seen before.
