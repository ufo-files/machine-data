{dr}series of sideways v-shaped amber lights

I was awakened from sleep in my upstairs bedroom by the glow of bright amber light. I was between the ages of 10 and 15 at the time. I got up and looked out the north facing bedroom window. Up in the north by northeast sky I observed a series of sideways v shaped amber colored lights. The point of the v's was facing to the west. The lights blinked on and off in succession but they slowly dimmed so that several lights could be seen at a time in various stages of brightness to dimness to off. There were at least 6 to 8 lights visible all in a straight horizontal row. To my memory the lights merely hovered and from my vantage point did not make any sound. I went back to bed after about 10 minutes, The lights were still visible. I was startled and apprehensive but did not feel that I was in any danger. I covered my head with the covers and went back to sleep. I never reported this to anyone until about 20 years later. I believe I saw a newspaper article with a picture of the exact lights that I saw as a child.
