{jpg} Filmed with iPhone 2 lights blinking in sequence.

Was looking SE and noticed 1 lights spaced out that were blinking in sequence. I returned to house and got my phone and began filming the lights. I could hear a helicopter behind me and there were two fixed wing aircraft to the east of me. I recorded the lights for a minute or two as well as narrate what I was witnessing. I zoomed in and out trying to get something in the foreground for reference. The lights moved away from where they were and tilted slightly. After they were in the distance, I stopped recording. I have been in aviation since 1978 and have never seen anything like this. I showed it at work and no one could identify the lights either.
