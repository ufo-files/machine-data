We saw three triangular grouped lights start at one side of the horizon and move in formation to the other side of the horizon and disappear

My wife (Michelle) and I were sitting outside a timeshare we were staying at after having put our son to bed. I had one scotch and my wife had none. 

It was dark and much of the area is cleared where we were so we had a great view of the night sky. We noticed these lights. It was like a triangle shape composed of three lights. We could not make out any kind of an outline in the night sky. So there were three of these triangles slowly hovering at the edge of our visible horizon to the left. They were moving right very slowly when we noticed them. Before I could make a comment on it they went to the other side of our visible horizon in less than a second. The triangles stayed in perfect formation the whole time. My wife and I both looked at each other bewildered not knowing what to make of it. We gave up on trying to figure it out. The instant acceleration was inexplicable to cover such a vast distance. 

Since the last year I've been learning more about the efforts to report UAPs and want to do my part. Thank you all for taking these matters seriously for so long enduring the ridicule while searching for one of the ultimate truths of our time.
