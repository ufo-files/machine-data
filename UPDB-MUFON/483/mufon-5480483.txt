{nd} Large circular UFO with 20+ perimeter lights that blink in sequence observed while traveling south down HWY 99

While traveling south on highway 99 between Elk Grove and Lodi, I observed what I believe to be a large circular or disk shaped craft with 20+ sequentially blinking perimeter lights. Initially I thought I was looking dots of light in the clouds from a parking lot promotional spotlight, I tried and was unable to locate a source of the lights on the ground. I had to widen my focus to notice all of the dots of light, and their blinking pattern. The craft was so large that I am uncertain if it hovered or slowly moved south, because I was able to observe it for several minutes while traveling approximately 13 miles.
