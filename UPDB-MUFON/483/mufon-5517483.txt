{ck} Orange Orbs hoovering above homes in Northwest Sky

While cleaning up after lighting fireworks in our culdesac five of us saw what appeared to be 2 bright orange orb like objects moving rapidly across the sky from the west. They turned towards the north and it looked like possibly another orb was coming in from behind forming a boomerang shape. There also appeared to be an airplane following them. They hoovered for a short time and then moved at a very quick pace towards the north until we lost sight of them.
