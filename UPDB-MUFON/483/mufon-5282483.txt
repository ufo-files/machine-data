i was coming home after work around 8:30 in the winter! i lived on a farm between loveland and longmont a bout 4 miles east of I25.at at the time there wasn't much around but family farms not much in the way of lights.i toped the hill coming up to my drive way and turned in. the drive was a couple hundred ft in length.the craft were hovering above the barn at the farm to the south of the one i lived at .the barn was the same as the one where i lived. quite large and had loft. the loft at my place had a full basket ball court inside. 
the crafts were just hovering about ten feet above the roof! they were completely still and silent .they are oval in shape and about 40 to 50 long and about 1/3 the tall.i stoped my car and turned it off and stepped out by the door.i watched the for about a mimute or then the both moved up about twice the distance ,then the one on the right raised up a few more feet and shot straight up and out got sight in one second, the the second did the same! you could follow them up as the rose and got smaller and smaller! i got back in my car a when ao to the oust and told my mom! i was 17 or eighteen at the time. i wasn't scared or anything ,it was like watching a couple of trucks on the road! a couple nights later i saw them again only very high in the sky.the did the same thing again only way higher up ! i thank the powers that be for having seen this. i remember it like yesterday.i KNOW they are here! even if they were drones ,somebody had to make them!!
i fell very lucky!!!
