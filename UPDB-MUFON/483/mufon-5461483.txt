{tjd} Green light that went silently across sky

At about 4:15am, while walking my dog down Commerce Avenue, going NW/West, I saw a green light in the corner of my eye. I turned my head to see what it was and saw the object was a green point of light, round in shape, but not glowing or emitting light beyond itself. It went straight across the sky in a line that was almost due north, then curved down towards the ground. I heard no crash or explosion as it came down, but I did not see where it went down, as it dropped below my line of vision. The object was in the distance, so maybe 500 ft or so from me. It made no sound as it moved across the sky.
At first I thought it was a fire work as it had a slight trail, but there was no sound during (like the whining of a flare) or, before or after (like a pop of a Roman Candle-like fire work).
