Three of us just had dinner. We walked to the cars and to the East we saw flash of light. On the "Crest" in Albuquerque, NM we see a reflected light off something. It was many miles away, but the setting sun reflected off the surface causing us to see it. My friend got his binoculars and looked. He said it was a "crown". I took a look myself. The surface was like gold foil, it had blue, green type "window"s. but the biggest thing for me was how the surface rippled. Compared to the TV towers it was near, it was probably about 100ft in length. It was situated down on a ledge to the right of the towers. We observed this for about 40 minutes. We got in our cars and were going to head toward the "crest" but as we went around a curve it disappeared from the ledge. I didn't see it go. One minute it was there and the next it wasn't.
