Glowing orb stationary above the trees across the river from me

On a canoe trip on the French River, stopped to set up camp for the day. I was on a bedrock shoreline on the south side of the river, looking north. This is early afternoon on a clear sunny day. Just above the tree tops on the other side of the river I observed a glowing orb, similar in appearance to the sun, but not as bright. It wasn't uncomfortably bright to look at like the sun. I observed it for about 3 minutes and it did absolutely nothing. I looked away for a few seconds to do something and when I looked back it was gone. This was before cell phone cameras and I didn't have any way of recording what I saw.
