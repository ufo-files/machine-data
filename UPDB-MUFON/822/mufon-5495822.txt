Pancake Association UFO Sighting

I was on my way over to my pancake committee meeting at around 4am, todays meeting was going to be taken by the legendary Jimmy McSpaceMan and I couldn't be late. Suddenly I saw a glowing object coming from behind the moon. Quickly, it came down to ground level and landed in an unknown area (some sort of field possibly). A small 'person' appeared on the ground, with ginger skin and hair in some areas. The creature seemed able to transform as, suddenly, right before my eyes, they transformed into a leaf. They wandered around until they, eventually, got back into their transport vehicle which seemed to crumble and disintegrate right before my eyes.
