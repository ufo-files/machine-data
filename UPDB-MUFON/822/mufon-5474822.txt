At 7:40 AM on July 29,2017 I had just crossed the border from Oklahoma into Kansas. I noticed a very bright light in the sky to the northeast. I assumed it was sunlight reflecting off of an airplane, but while I was watching it, it suddenly vanished. I kept looking, thinking I would see the plane, but there was nothing there and it was a clear day with no clouds. The only way I could describe it would be that it was as bright or brighter than the brightest star in the sky at night, but it was broad daylight and there were no stars out. I pulled over and looked for a plane, but never saw anything. It was very confusing.
