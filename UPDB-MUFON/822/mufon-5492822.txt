{rjl} small craft 5feet above ground followed my car keeping 100 yard distance

Approx 3am. Driving on I-10 fwy at approx 70mph.No one else on hwy. Noticed lights in my rear following,keeping distance. Further inspection observed 2 lights,one on top of the other in the middle. Green and white lights. Smaller red lights attached to a wing. When i pulled over and looked out my door at it,it kept the 100 yard distance,just hovering.I could see the white light on the pavement in front of it. It seamed to communicate with me. I had spoken to some friends earlier that i would love to see a ufo. This thing somehow said"well you wanted to see a ufo,so here i am" in my head. I have never been so scared in my life! I spead off,getting off the next offramp. Never saw it again.
