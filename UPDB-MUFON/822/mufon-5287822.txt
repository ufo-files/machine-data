3 Cigar Shaped UFOs

While driving in Florida recently I noticed in the sky 3 cigar shaped UFOs. They were stacked on top of each other, not touching but close. The middle one appeared a white silvery appearance. The other 2 on top and bottom were shinier than the middle object and reflecting the clouds around it. They appeared stationary. It was a clear blue typical Florida day with some passing clouds.
