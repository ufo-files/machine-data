30-40 starlike objects flying silently in formation.

I witnessed 30-40 star like objects while in my garden at around 6:15am. The first object was 3 star like lights shaped triangular which immediately drew my attention then just behind was another one going at the same speed and keeping the same distance away followed by another the same distance and speed followed by another and another until 3-5 mins had gone by and roughly 30- 40 lights. The sky was dark and the came out of cloud cover into an open part around my house then went into cover again around 20 seconds after coming into view. No engine noise could be heard and the only light was the star like solid white light. No other colours or flashing lights were visible. A couple of mins later a plane appeared in the distance but seemed to be going in the opposite direction so is probably irrelevant but good to note I believe. This genuinely was the most amazing thing I've seen in the skies. I will note that I tried filming on my samsung s9 but couldn't seem to pick them up on the camera.
