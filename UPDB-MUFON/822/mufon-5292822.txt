I saw two orange globes meet then go opposite directions

I was driving home with my gf at the time from the Lummi Indian Reservation casino around 10pm in the winter of 2007. When I saw 2 orange globes in the sky, one closer to the horizon and the other high in the sky. We pulled over at this point. Then the globes traveled slowly in diagonal direction to meet each other briefly halfway. The horizon globe traveled in NE direction and the one from the sky traveled in SW direction to meet. Once they were side by side. The one traveling from the horizon flew straight up and the globe from high up in the sky flew straight down with no hesitation they were gone just like that.
