Took picture of cloud opening right after downpour

I was doing grounds work at school when it started raining so I drove the tractor to garage to wait out rain. It had been raining real hard for about 20-30 minutes and I was in the school garage waiting for rain to stop. The rain had slowed down and I noticed sunlight shining into the garage and looked out and notice one opening in the clouds where the sun was shinning through and took a picture of it, since it seemed odd. Later that evening I was telling my wife about it and showed her the picture when she noticed a dark spot in the picture. I downloaded the picture on my work pc and zoomed in on the image and cant tell if its a bird or something else. Since it was still raining I don't think it was a bird, but cant be sure. I never noticed anything in the sky when I took the picture and was surprised to see the object in the picture. I thought I would submit it in case there were any other people who may have seen something on that date. I included original picture and zoomed in picture.
