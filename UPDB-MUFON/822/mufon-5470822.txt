{rjl} 3 yellow/orange lights darted around each other like playing, then all 3 shot away in the same direction incredibly fast. I pulled over and watched. I will never forget what I saw.

I was going to college in myrtle beach, sc. It was around 3am when I left my apartment and headed towards the 24 hr McDonald's in Conway. I was driving down a road with golf courses on both sides of the road. I looked to my left and out of the driver window I saw 3 bright lights in the sky. They were very bright but seemed small or very far away. The lights seemed yo be in a triangle formation just hovering. When I noticed the lights I immediately pulled to the side of the road and watched from inside the car. I was transfixed. I was not fearful but incredibly confused and fascinated. The lights began darting around each other. Short, very fast movements. They looked as if they were birds playing. They darted around each other for several seconds before they simutaniously zipped away. I sat and watched for minutes before I left that spot. It looked like it came from directly over the ocean and they zipped away over the ocean of north myrtle beach. There were 3 bright lights in total but when they zoomed away it seemed like only one. I never reported it until now. I will never forget those lights and the way they moved and how they darted away that fast. I researched for days but could not find evidence of anyone else witnessing these lights. They darted around in all directions around each other, not just left or right but up and down and in all directions.
