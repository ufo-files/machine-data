{tjd} A very high intensity light Traveling south west at an unknown altitude and speed. That continually gained altitude and disappeared

I was at home and went out my back door.I just looked up and saw this extremely bright high intensity light that was moving in a southwesterly direction. I at first thought it was a jet traveling overhead until I saw 2 jets and heard them. The light was at a much higher altitude with it's intensity of light much brighter than the 2 jets moving by. The jets had on their green and red lights on and could be heard. The Light I saw was in view longer than the jets and I could no longer here the jets nor could I here the Object I was viewing. The object continued to gain altitude and seemed to get smaller but not lose it's intensity as it moved higher until it disappeared. It did not go out of view as a jet would. It appeared to leave the atmosphere. I was amazed when first seeing this and called to my wife to come see it but she took to long to come out and missed it. Afterwords I am just in awe at this observation.
