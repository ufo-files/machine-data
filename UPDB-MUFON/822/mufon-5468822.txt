{jpg} Mysterious Rock

I found a rock on the bank of the St Johns River near Mims in Brevard County Florida that is so strange I do know who other than MUFON to contact. This is not a UFO sighting so if you are not interested I certainly understand.

The rock is approximately 5 inches in length and shaped like a chicken leg (photo 1). When I tapped the rock to remove dirt it broke and inside is what appears to be a carbon rod (photos 2, 2a, 2b, 2c, 3, 3a). Also imbedded in the rock is what appears to be plastic (photo 4). The rock is a fine grain structure with many crystal inclusions (photo 5), most rock in this area is coquina shell or limestone.
