Firey object falling from the sky south of Westminster, Colorado.

I went outside and looked to the south and saw what I thought was a falling star but it was burning red and orange and getting brighter and looked like it was getting closer to the ground I called my brother to look as he was in the house we both ran out and saw the object split into 4 firey balls then it appeared they stopped falling and joined together then disappeared
