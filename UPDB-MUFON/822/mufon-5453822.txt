{cak} Over fifty “stars” following each other like a convoy across the sky

Last night my husband saw what we call “ floaters” going across the sky from SE to N. He then noticed one after another seeming to be evenly spaced stars following each other, he call his mother and then myself out to see what was happening. We were shocked to see “stars” all going one by one like a convoy accords the night sky. My mother in law doesn’t believe in UFO’s and even she was astonished as to what we were seeing. It was an organized line of lights. 2 of them lit up very very bright with a beam going ahead of it. There were a few planes with their usual flashing green white or red lights...we wondered if they were picking these flying objects up on radar. These were intelligent crafts all following each other in organized flight. To where I don’t know and why? I got the feeling they were all in a hurry to go somewhere or leaving somewhere quickly. It was amazing actually. This is for sure, very odd. They were not planes, not satellites, not helicopters, not drones. These were the same floaters we’ve seen before, but this time was the first times they were flying in perfect order, one by one.
