Bright white sphere shaped object appeared while traveling westbound on Route 22 at the Fullerton Bridge and traveled in line with the road. The object was large in diameter and as it got further away it diminished in size to about the size of viewable stars in the sky at the time eventually fading away into the horizon. The entire event was witnessed by a total of 3 individuals in the vehicle and presumably by others that were traveling on the road as well as it was quite noticeable for a period of time roughly 6 to 10 seconds. Unfortunately by the time we realized it did not have any markings, lights and was not an aircraft (helicopter, airplane, etc...) that we have over our area we were unable to get a picture.
