{cak} Witnesses saw three sets of lights form a boomerang shape above the tree line and move up and down and back and forth, than a fireball

Witness was walking with his girlfriend in garden by house and looking at the stars in a clear evening sky when they noticed to the east a group of white lights, about 30 to 50 degrees above the horizon. The lights were moving in a random pattern, then they came together in a triangle like shape, descended behind the trees, came back up above the trees, then went to the other side of the tree line and then went back down. His father joined them and saw the object with the white lights. As they turned to go in the house, they saw this orange fireball coming parallel to the horizon and approximately 30 degrees above the ground and then just disappeared.
