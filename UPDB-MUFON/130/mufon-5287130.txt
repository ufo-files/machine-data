UFO Chased by Military Jet Plane

I was on our rooftop deck and taking photos of the Las Vegas Strip, which lies about 18 miles to the southeast of our house. I then heard a military jet. We hear them all the time and I know that Nellis AFB is doing training all week. I turned towards the southwest above the Spring Mountains (which are only a few blocks west of our house) and saw 2 lights coming STRAIGHT towards our area. They were coming at a high rate of speed. I started up my video on my IPhone 12. I took footage of the incident, but they were traveling so fast, the video isn't very clear, but I am attaching it anyway for what it's worth. The jet noise is clearly heard, but the object it was chasing did NOT look like another plane and I didn't hear any sound coming from it at all. The light the jet was chasing pulsated and wavered in movement slightly back and forth in front of the jet. At the last moment, it zoomed to the north far ahead of the jet. Again, my video is not very good and the camera wasn't able to keep a focus on the lights because they were traveling so fast... but perhaps the noise and some still frames of this might be helpful.
