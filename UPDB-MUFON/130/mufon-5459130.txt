Followed and overtook train, returned in opposite direction at other side of train

Sky clear, full moon night on the snow covered and fairly flat Hardanger high mountain plateau, I was traveling on the night train en route from Bergen to Oslo. Due to full moon light reflecting off the snow, visibility of the barren terrain was very good (> 5km).

Two white-yellow lights/objects (which appeared spherical) came into view from higher altitude (above line of sight from train car window) and proceeded to follow the train (parallell trajectory) for 2-3 minutes, before crossing the train track some 200 meters ahead of locomotive to do the same manouver, but now in opposite trajectory on the other side of the train. No distinct outline or shape, just lights. Some variation of inter-object distance indicated two separate objects. However, they followed the same general trajectory. No sound heard from inside the train. Train car was fairly well sound shielded, as well as general noice inside a moving train, which may explain absence of sound.
