{rjl} Seen what I thought was a plane coming from the south west, as it got closer it didn't look so much like a regular plane and slightly bigger than a passenger jet with what appeared to be multi-winged, it made no sound at all.

Was out on my balcony looking up at the new batch of chem-trails when I noticed out of the south west an object I took to be a plane at first. I continued to observe as it came closer. When it was above I noticed it had what appeared to be multiple wings and it left no trail or made any sound. I came in and told my roommate what I had seen and noted the time, I then contacted this sight and left this report. Maybe it was just an experimental plane, have no idea but felt a record would be good in case someone else has seen it too.
