UFO sighting over Crow Mountain AR.

I was driving home one night when I saw three air planes flying overhead. (Planes fly very low over crow mountain). I started to watch the planes and almost ran my vehicle off the road. In that instance I reminded myself to pay attention to the road. This “voice” in my head abruptly said “no, watch the planes”. As I turned my attention back to the planes and slowed my vehicle to almost a stop, a solid blueish orb appeared out of nowhere. The instant I saw it my heart skipped a beat because I knew what I was witnessing. It “danced” around the sky above me. It then approached the planes perpendicular, stopped, then followed parallel with the planes. After a few seconds it stopped again right above me, and then shot off into the abyss in a matter of seconds. It happened to be a very clear night with no clouds. I fully believe that I witnessed a UFO! Furthermore I do not believe the voice I heard in my head was my own, it didn’t feel authentic to my person/psyche.
