Was called to front porch by oldest son who was observing an object. He stated that it was hovering and did not look like any aircraft he had ever seen. I took a look and saw what appeared to be silver tear drop shaped object hovering and apparently spinning slowly. As a Navy vet who was srationed on an aircraft carrier I knew this was not anything usual. My son hot his cannon digital SLR and took some photo. The photos show the object to be three distinct orbs connected by a spire. The object slowly moved off to the southwest against the prevailing wind.
