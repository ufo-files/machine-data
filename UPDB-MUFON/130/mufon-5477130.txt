{nd} Traveling home early morning on interstate. Saw simultaneous flashes from top of high rise bridge.

Traveling home from a short vacation late in the evening. My future wife and I were at the top of a overpass which we could see over the tree line and homes. Our location at the time was at the intersection i64 and i264 in Norfolk Va. In a very brief instant we saw several bright blue flashes far off in the distant. Appearing to be concentrated in the Virginia Beach , Chesapeake area. They appeared to look like large transparent blue domes of energy not solid but a well defined shape. Having seen a electric transformer explode before the color was the same. We reationalized that that was what we saw a massive power outage. A couple days past and I saw no reports of an outage in that area. But a curious story appeared in the paper. Many home owners awoke to totally empty swimming pools in the area we saw the phenomenon. Having owned a pool I now know you can't drain pool the sizes described in article in 5 to 6 hours. My memory of the incident itself is fresh but the exact time and date I'm sorry has failed me and my wife. The actual year was between 78 ,79. The missing pool water was reported in Virginia pilot newspaper a day or 2 after the incident. I'm not sure if you consider this worthy of your site but I would find interesting to know if any similar incidents occurred any where else. Thank you, DD
