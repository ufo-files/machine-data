{cak} White/Yellow Light Enters Atmosphere At Angle, Stops, Wobbles, Takes off at light speed

I was coming back from a late night dinner run from krystal burgers on western avenue, turned up pleasant ridge road, and proceeded to my house which I will say is in the plesant ridge road community. I pulled in my drive way after seeing my two cats waiting on me to let them in. I turned off my vehicle, approached the back of my car and said "hello my kittens" as I looked down at them, I don't know what compelled me to look but, then I turned my head up my driveway skyward and this is what I saw:

A light was all I could see, any shape or intricate detail obscured by its light and the darkness of the night sky. As soon as it entered my field of view, I was fixated. For the first few milliseconds, I thought what I was seeing was a meteor of some kind. Within about .5 seconds, my heart sank and my stomach dropped as I witnessed the light correct its entry course which looked like this -> /

It then came to a complete stop and leveled out like this -> _ and began to move rather twitchy and strangely in place, almost like a magnet would if surrounded by a case of opposite polarity. It then took off faster than anything I have ever seen before in my entire life. It moved so fast that the light I was looking at elongated into a cigar shape of white light and disappeared from my sight over the tree line. 

I was so stunned I actually screamed "holy f**king jesus god what the f**k is that" I believe were my exact words as I dropped my food and ran in the house. Almost as if it knew I was viewing it, as soon as I screamed it took off at the speed of light over the trees. 

My major regret about the whole incident is, I was so shocked and in utter compelling awe, by the time I got my hand in my pocket for my phone while screaming, it took off unfathomably fast. I feel as if the word "Light speed" hardly touches how fast this object moved out of my line of sight as soon as I yelled. 

I have never seen anything like that before in my entire life. I doubt I will ever again.
