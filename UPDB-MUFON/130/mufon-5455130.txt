{rjl} orange/amber lights - really bright, like stadium floodlights.

We were on the balcony of my friend's condo, looking out to sea. At first we saw two lights, one above the other. Gradually other lights appeared to form two vertical columns of three lights, with a triangle of three lights to the east. I thought I was witnessing helicopters out to see doing a rescue, or exercise. They appeared to be coming towards us and I kept waiting to hear the sound of helicopters - but they were silent. We kept asking each other "can you see this ?" "what IS this ?". Suddenly they were gone - like somebody flipped a light switch off. The next night at around the same time, we saw two lights, one above the other, exactly the same story - silently coming towards us out of the north, over the ocean. Then, lights out, gone.
