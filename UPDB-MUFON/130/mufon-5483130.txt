star like object changing directions and randomly disappearing and reappearing

Me and my mom were lying on the deck at our house at sunset and we saw the first few stars come out. While it was happening I questioned if my eyes were playing tricks on me because I was under the influence but my sober mom and my dad eventually did witness the event and had the same reaction as me. after about a minute the object passed by some trees and we couldn't see it anymore.
