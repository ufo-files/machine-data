It was late evening, I took dog out back, a clear moonless sky, no ambient interference from outdoor lights. I noticed two dull red ovine shaped disc's suddenly appear high in atmosphere, then they straight lined leaving no trail and getting smaller, as if leaving, at what seemed like great acceleration. This was 41 yrs ago, still fresh in memory. My wife and son were present in house when I rushed in and explained what I had seen. However they are no longer alive.
