Ufo photograph taken in 1950

This is a photograph taken in 1950 at ''Bodega Trapiche'', located in the province of Mendoza, Argentina. The kid in the photo is my father, to his left my grandmother, to his right a friend. At the moment the photo was taken they didn´t realize that there was a ''saucer'' shaped object behind them.
