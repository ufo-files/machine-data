{dr}There were 3 objects then two of them went underneath the larger object and they all became one

My friend and I were traveling east on I-10 (towards Phoenix) when we noticed 3 object to the south of us. They were like white lights and were moving around oddly. Then two of the objects went underneath the third object and it looked like the one object engulfed the other two. The shape then changed like a boomerang and there was a red glow to it. The whole process was about 3 minutes. One it changed to a boomerang shape it just hovered there. We then had to go north and lost light of the object. If you enlarge the object you will see the boomerang shape
