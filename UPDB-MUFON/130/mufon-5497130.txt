Giant white/blue fireball asteroid like falling in a straight downward trajectory from the sky, disappeared behind a hill but no sign of impact seen

12129114 at approximately 19:20pm (not exact on the time) in the Los Angeles County area - City of Diamond Bar / Brea - California.

Object was visualized by two witnesses in the same car (retired police officer and a health care provider)- for about 4 to 5 seconds while driving south bound on the 57 freeway, right where it crosses the 60 freeway. The object was seen from this spot, but appeared to be located approximately a mile or so ahead in the sky flying in a West to South1East trajectory in a downward crashing angle. The object appeared to be a giant shooting star at first traveling very very fast. the Fireball heading the asteroid(star) was bright and had a bluish hue to it. appeared to be about to crash around the area on the map with the green shading called: Firestone Scout Reservation (19001 Tonner Canyon Road, Brea, CA 92821).
It disappeared from our vision behind the silhouette of a hill.
We expected to hear a loud crash, explosion, see flames or feel the impact of the object crashing but there was no sign of fire/smoke/explosion or noise.

Neither witness was intoxicated or under the effects of any drugs/alcohol
