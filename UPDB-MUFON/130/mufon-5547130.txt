Incandescent--almost like a fireball--sphere flying north direction above West Harlem

I have a 180 degrees panoramic view of West Harlem in Manhattan from where I live. Around 11:30PM on August 7th, 2009, I decided to get up from my desk and take a break. The moment I approached my living room window to look to the horizon, I see this greenish/whiteish incandescent fireball flying horizontally above my building in direction north to West Harlem above Morningside Heights. However after two seconds the fireball suddenly--and instantaneously--disappeared without trace. The speed at which it was flying was similar to the speed of a helicopter in cruising mode.
