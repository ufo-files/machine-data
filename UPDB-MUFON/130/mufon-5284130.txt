Unearthly object in the sky

I first saw something in the sky with my roommate an hour before I took the video (the second time I saw it). Both of us tried to justify what it might have been, but couldn't identify it as any animal or thing we had seen before. The object was dark, almost blending into the sky and was very difficult to tell its distance from us or size in comparison to us. The first time, we chalked it up to just being odd, but still felt uneasy about its movements. An hour later, I went back outside to the patio of my apartment to watch the lunar eclipse/ red moon and saw the object in the sky again, but closer, which freaked me out. I ran to the door of my building and started taking a video of the black/dark thing and watched as a saucer-like object hovered and then effortlessly moved across the sky, without visible evidence of any device allowing it to do so. Once my building was obstructing my view of it, I ran inside shaking from adrenaline and utter shock.
