Bright light dropped at supersonic speed out of the sky and came to a dead stop several miles up. When it stopped, it got super bright, much larger and brighter than a star, and the light began to pulsate while it was stationary. This lasted about a minute. Then it started heading east. It would appear to get really dim and then brighten right back up again. The it stopped again for maybe 25-30 seconds made a sharp 90 degree turn then it headed due east towards Vermont in a straight line, still pulsating until I could no longer see it. At this point I called the non-emergency number to the Washington County Sherriff’s office and reported what I saw. I spoke to a female dispatcher and requested that the sighting be logged. I also contacted Mufon investigator Samuel A. Faldo and he asked me to file this report.
