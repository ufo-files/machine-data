After the sunset, it was a star like object falling down.

I was going out with my dog. When I saw the star like object for the first time I was getting down the stairs. The sky was clear, after the sunset. I think the object was a shining planet, like Venus or Mars. It was stuck in the sky. After a minute I was walking down the street, I turned back, saw again "the star" above and in few seconds it disappeard down the horizon like a teardrop. I was surprised.
