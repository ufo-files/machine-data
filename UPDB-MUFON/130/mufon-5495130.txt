{dr}one large starlike light and 2 small lights shot out to the right and then the big one shot straight up followed by the 2 small ones

I was at my family's camp in clearfield county Pa. on what they called the old Krise farm area on caledonia pike and my dad said go out and see if you can walk up to a deer by blinding it with a flashlight which we successfuly have done one time so I walked out to the edge of the cut grass looking over a field and noticed a couple deer far away to the south but quickly noticed a large star that I first thought wow that's a pretty big star. It was a quarter of the size of the moon. While I was looking at it wondering what it was 2 small lights shot out of the right side of it and hovered stationary at the same level for a few seconds and then the large star shot straight up and a split second after the 2 small lights did the same and at lightning speed and disappeared. I was 16 years old at the time. I went in the camp and told my dad and grandparents what I saw and they laughed and said I was seeing things. I didn't want them to think I was crazy so I just dropped it.I never doubted what I saw but did not tell them how certain I was about what I saw. I don't know if there were any more sightings in this time period or location but after watching your program yesterday I thought this might be helpful. Good luck in what you do and I am a fan. The time period is not exact. I only remember I was around 16 years old.                Sincerely, [Name removed/cms/tg]
