Slow moving fireball looking objectmoving west to east and semi-abruptly turned south.

My husband n I were in the front yard on are swing when my husband observed the circular shaped object which appeared to be a fireball slowly moving east .i looked up and saw it as well we continued to watch the object as it went behind the neighbors trees thats when I ran in the house to get the camera . My husband stayed out sideout and continued to watch .When I returned with the camera its motion turned south wards. I snapped a picture just before it went out of view. Being from Albuquerque I know what a hot air balloon looks like and this was not one. We couldnt believe what we were seeing and it was like nothing we have seen before.
