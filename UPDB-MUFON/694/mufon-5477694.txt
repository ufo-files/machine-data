WHILE WORKING AS A POLICE OFFICER IN GUAM, I GOT A CALL ABOUT BRIGHT LIGHTS OVER ALL THREE CEMETERIES

FILLED OUT AT MUFON HEADQUARTERS WITH WITNESS ON THE PHONE BY FIELD INVESTIGATOR L. FLECHTNER #18809 ON MARCH 2ND, 2017
---------------------------------------------------------------------

IT WAS ABOUT 1993, THE TYPHOON SEASON HAD JUST ENDED. THE SEASON RUNS FROM MAY TO OCTOBER. I WAS A CORPORAL IN THE GUAM POLICE FORCE AND WE GOT A CALL ABOUT CRAFT WITH MULTIPLE-CHANGING LIGHTS FLYING OVER ALL THREE GRAVEYARDS AT THE SAME TIME. THE CRAFT WERE SHINING A BRIGHT BEAM DOWN ON HE GRAVEYARDS. THE BEAM LOOKED LIKE A WHITE STICK WITH A BALL OF BRIGHT LIGHT ON THE END OF IT. 
I ARRIVED AT ONE OF THE GRAVEYARDS FIRST AND SAW THE BEAM JUST AS THE MULTIPLE-LIGHT CHANGING CRAFT SEEMED TO SHOOT OUT 20 MILES OVER THE OCEAN. THE BEAM WAS THEN OFF. THE OTHER OFFICERS REPORTED SIMILAR ACCOUNTS. THE LOCALS HAD CALLED IN THE LIGHTS. WE CONTACTED THE MILITARY BASES TO SEE IF THEY HAD ANY ACTIVITY IN THE AREA, THEY DIDN'T. A FEW SECONDS AFTER THE CRAFT WERE GONE, MILITARY HELICOPTERS FLEW OVER TO INVESTIGATE AND FOLLOW THE LIGHTS OUT TO SEA. THE CRAFT THEN DISAPPEARED.
THE LIGHTS WERE AS LARGE AS A MEDIUM HELICOPTER, AND A VERY BRIGHT WHITE WITH MULTIPLE CHANGING COLORS.
I WAS A FORMER NAVEL AIRCRAFT MECHANIC, SO I KNEW THESE CRAFT AND LIGHTS WERE NOT ANYTHING I HAD EVER SEEN BEFORE.
WE LOOKED FOR DAMAGE TO THE GRAVE SITES BUT SAW NOTHING AMISS.
