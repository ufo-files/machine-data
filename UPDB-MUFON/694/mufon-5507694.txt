{dr}Observed a star like object at dusk in eastern sky

While out walking our dogs, my wife first noticed several transparent clouds forming a ray like pattern parallel to one another about 25 degrees in the eastern sky. In the middle was what we thought to be Venus, but realized Venus was setting in the west. The object was bright, round, extremely distant, and stationary. The event lasted about 10 minutes. Unfortunately, we did not observed its disappearance.
