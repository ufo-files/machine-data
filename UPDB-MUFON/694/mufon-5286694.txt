Took a pic of a cigar shaped object in the sky

This pic was taken circa summer 2009 to summer 2010. My friend was a Directv installer. After every install, he would take a picture of his work. He didn't see the object in person when he took the photo. He only realized there was something in the photo after he got home to look through the day's photos he had taken. He noticed something in the background and zoomed in and it was a metal cigar-shaped looking object. It didn't look like an airplane, helicopter, hot air balloon, or anything else neither of us was familiar with. We have both looked at the photo and are not sure what it is. The pic was taken with an iPhone 3GS or iPhone 4. We have noticed when checking MUFON that there were other people in the state and even the local area that has seen cigar-shaped objects in the sky. If anyone has seen something similar please feel free to comment on this thread. There is a red circle drawn around the object for those who cannot see it. Thank you.
