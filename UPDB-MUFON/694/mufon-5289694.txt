Strange bright object filmed in night sky

On the evening in question I was laying in bed and heard a strange sound out my back door. I also notice that my dogs began to bark uncontrollably. I got up and looked outside, but didn't see anyone or anything that would explain the strange sound. We have security cameras in our back yard that I checked to see if they had captured the disturbance. On the video in the south sky you can see an object moving at a very high rate of speed from south to south east. The object appears bright white in the video. The object is oblong shaped with three symmetrical bumps on the top of it. The object had what appeared to be a cone shaped tail. The object appears to descend towards the ground when it goes out of sight of the camera. To the south west of our house is an airport. Rarely are there any flights at this airport after 10:00 pm. When we have seen planes taking off, circling or landing at the airport they are easy to define as airplanes. The speed at which this object was traveling was easily 10 times faster than any plane that has ever been seen near the airport. It is not uncommon to see fighter jets doing touch and goes at the airport, but they are easily identifiable as F-15's. This object had no resemblance to any aircraft I have ever seen or meteor or shooting star.
