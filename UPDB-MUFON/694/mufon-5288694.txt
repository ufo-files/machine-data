Star--like object which changed direction across the sky, possibly far away.

In the summer of 2008 (May-June), when I was 12. My mom, my cousin brother and I were hiking in the Himalayas in the state of Uttaranchal (now Uttarakhand). We were camping in a very small village by the name of « Duggalbitta », Chopta. We were settling in a tent on the mountainside, away from the former British resort and the shops. We weren’t very far, just a short walk away. 

We were lying on the ground, it was night time, must’ve been anywhere from 10-10:30 PM. It was a partially cloudy, but clear other than the clouds, and you could see mountains by the moonlight and the starlight. The sky was full of stars. We were camped at about 8,000 feet or 2,438.4 metres. 
When suddenly something caught my eye in the sky. A star, or what seemed to be a star was moving erratically back and forth, I thought that I was hallucinating (and I was going to exclude this account, but I didn’t when your list mentioned that autokinesis and autostasis happen during an inky-black sky not during a starry one). But there it was oscillating back and forth. I mentioned this to my mom, and my cousin, at first they didn’t see it, but then they did as well. They assumed that it was a satellite or a meteor. I thought that it was a UFO, because the « The History » channel’s « Ancient Aliens » program made me think so. This event lasted for about 5-10 minutes, and I tried to film it, but my camera was really old and decrepit. And unfortunately this event passed without filming.

I had a fear that aliens would come into my tent and abduct me because of those programs that I saw, but thankfully nothing of the sort happened, and I woke up the next day, ready for our hike.
