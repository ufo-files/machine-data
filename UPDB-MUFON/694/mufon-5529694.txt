I am a truck driver, I was driving home from Carlin Nv To Salt Lake City and stopped at the rest stop just east of wendover, Utah, at about 5:00 am, I always look at the stars when I stop just out of habit I saw a star that I had not seen before low in the southern sky, I whatched it and it started to move so I looked closer I watched for awhile it looked like it was trying to maintain its position kind like wobbling! then I asked it to give me a sighn that it could hear me, it blinked off then on real bright then back to were it was originally, it moved up and down and side to side then the clouds rolled in and I lost sight of it around 5:30 I have friends that say they saw one that morning about 2:00 am flashing red,blue and green and white lights they watched it for awhile as well it was moving upand down. it was high in the southern sky, we think that it is the same one that we saw last night that looked like a planet.
that night on 10-8-10 and 10-9-10 I drove up into the nearby mountains to get out of the clouds and lights of the city, I saw three of them in the northern sky far apart from one another, I whatched them as they moved around and blinked red, blue white and green lights, it was about 1:00-2:00 am I was listening to Ian Pundant on coast to coast am, I whatched them for about an hour and got tired and went home.

the one that was the most amazing was the one right above us that was just one white light, it was the one that looked like A planet, it was the closest to us, we looked at it with binoculars and could see windows on either side of it, oval in shape it was setting off center on a 45 degree angle it would move when we asked it to and it would change its light intensity from time to time.
