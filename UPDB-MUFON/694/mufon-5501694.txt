{rjl} Driving south on I 196 We saw 5 lights flashing in sky, They made a triangle

2)	Location: Between Glenn and South Haven while driving on I-196 south bound.
Date: Oct 23, 2011 Sunday @ 5:30pm
My girlfriend and I were returning for Ludington. While traveling south on I-196, we noticed 5 bright lights in the sky toward South Haven/Lake Michigan. The lights changed from white to red to blue then back to white. They then moved around and the lights seemed to strobe on and off. Then lights on two went out. We watched three. They moved around, became a triangle with the lights flashing and moving around as a triangle. They then became a straight line. Then two of the lights went out. Then the final one moved back and forth light flashing. Then in a blink of an eye there was nothing.
We observed this for between 3 & 4 minutes. We watched the sky all the way to our exit at South Haven. We then proceeded home east on M-43.
