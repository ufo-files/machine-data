{td} Strange object seen and caught on dashcam

Driving southbound on I-75, just south of Pinconning, headed to Flushing. (Dashcam shows coords 43.808237/-84.02103 our position). I noticed an object in the distance above the trees. It was dark against the sky, and seemed to be moving slowly east to west. It was far off in the distance. As we got closer, it stopped and hovered. We could make out more detail, but it was still hard to focus on. It looked square in shape, and seemed to turn a bit. I noticed people in other cars pointing at it as well. After a few minutes, as the freeway curved, the object was on my left and not in view of the camera. It looked to still be quite a ways off, and I still could not make out any detail as we passed by. I lost sight after several miles of trees blocking my view.
