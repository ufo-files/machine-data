{dr}Object seen above trees next to sidewalk then vanished.

In De Pere, WI, at the end of Sept, 2015 about 8:30 pm. It was dark. I took my dog out for her potty. We were on the sidewalk going back to our driveway. There was suddenly a large Orange oval shaped object over the tree next to driveway.It appeared lit from within, larger in front than in back, at least the part that I could see that was lit. It appeared to be smooth on the outside, no viable means of propulsion.It was about 1\2 as high as a news or police helicopter would fly. It came towards us a short way and then stopped and hovered over the tree closest to our driveway. It had small orange lights in a V, underneath starting in front and spreading towards the back. I could see the shadowy form of the object above and behind the lights. I watched it for probably 1-2 minutes. It was totally silent. It was a clear night, no clouds, stars were out but no moon. No airplanes or helicopters and no cars came by when it was there. We live on a quiet street.Suddenly it was just gone. No light, no sound, just vanished. It was so close, I had no doubt that I was seeing a UFO. Something I've never seen before. I was quite fascinated by it, not afraid. My dog and I just stood there watching it until it vanished. I felt calm and would have liked to watch it longer.
We just went inside and I thought about reporting it to authorities and then didn't, reluctant as they might not believe me. I did tell friends.
