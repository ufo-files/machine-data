Unidentified aerial phenomena hovering over Westminster and Arvada

Object was seen hovering and seemingly stationary in the area for about an hour with blue, red and white pulsating lights. Object then slowly moved westward and out of vision. What struck me as odd about the observation is that the object seemed to be stationary for quite awhile which seems to rule out a plane. Perhaps it was a helicopter but if so it seems like it would have to have been operating at a high altitude with little to no motion in any direction. Was not able to hear any engine or mechanical sounds from this distance.
