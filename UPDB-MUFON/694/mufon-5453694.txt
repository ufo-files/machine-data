I live on a flight path into Toronto from Europe/ England flights and when I got into my hot tub with my wife just after 9pm I started looking at the constellations noticing planes.
I start to pay more attention when I pondered what I was noticing wasn't normal. By this time 4 to 8 had passed over.
On Friday nights most flight are going in the opposite direction flying into Toronto not to Europe. Also there was only 1 white light and no red green navigational blinking lights.
Then I noticed the fast, silent flight paths, some in groups of 2 and 3 but most singular and not in a exactly the same path.
I first thought Airforce squadron as we are in their flight path between Trenton and Base Borden but that would have been SE to NW path not what I was seeing a SW to NE path and there was no sound or blinking nav lights.
I started counting and during one hot tub cycle of 20 minutes I counted 21.
That was enough for me, it got creeped out and went in the house.
