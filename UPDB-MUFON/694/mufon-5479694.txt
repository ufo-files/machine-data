white cigar type observed just below clouds, spotted it amongst clouds, made no sound, left trail towards end...

On October 3rd, I was walking over a park area, to a bus stop after visiting a family member, I'm always looking up and watching out for things, and on this occasion I saw a white cigar shaped object, spotted it just amongst the clouds, as though it was JUST below them.. the object made no sound, and it left left trail towards the end of the observation..
