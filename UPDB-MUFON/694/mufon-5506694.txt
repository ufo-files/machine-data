{cak} Newport Ky & Downtown Cincinnati Sighting

I have seen these on more than one occasion... I started seeing them around March of this year (2013) and at first I thought I was just seeing things. The first one I saw stayed in the same exact spot and just pulsated for exactly three hours, I have seen at least 30 since.

Today 11/5 driving home from work on 75 south I saw a bright light in the sky around 6:00pm it was very faint at first but as I kept going down the highway it got brighter and closer. I was stuck in traffic and I notice there were three total.. I watched in amazement as no one else around me paid any attention to it. 

From a far it looks like a big white light but to the naked eye and when flying over you, you can 100% tell its an aircraft of some kind. 

It gets even crazier... 

I have taken over four videos of great footage and up close shots, I watch them record and when I go to watch them theyre gone as if I never video taped anything at all. The first time it happen I let it go thinking it was my mistake but the second time it happen I threw change out the window. 

Its crazy that people shut their eyes to this and don't pay attention to their surroundings. 

I will submit the photos and videos I have been able to capture! (3 witnesses)
