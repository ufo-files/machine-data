{rjl} Falling and hovering decending slowly. 4 orbs then 1. Lights out then on then out.

Husband called me outside in a hurry to look. Saw 3 objects that were bright white and red and they were moving slowly Descending. They were spread apart and appeared to be floating. 2 lights went out. The third light continued to descend below the trees. I kept looking. It appeared to rise up above the tree line the light went back on for a few seconds and then out. We were both full of adrenaline.
