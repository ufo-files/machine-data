Large orange ball of light

I was Mikes Friendly Cab station, on the north side of Kokomo, looking to the southwest, about to get into my cab. (I drive a cab for a living) I saw a large, orange ball of light in the sky at about 40 degrees from horizon. It would take a quarter at arms length to cover it. It hovered in the same place for about 60 seconds, flared up and got brighter for a second or two, s and then dimmed in and out for a couple seconds. It did this a few times. The final time it started to get brighter, it began to move and suddenly then shot really fast to the east. I watched it until it was out of my sight.
