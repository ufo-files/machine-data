{kt}UFO with bright lights seen in western skies of CO last night

Observed bright lights in western sky--just above mountain range. With naked eye it looked as though it was 1 object and with binoculars it was likely two objects; 1 appearing bigger than another. The lights were magnificently bright and would change sequentially displaying all colors of the rainbow. I am going to see if our cameras caught anything but doubtful. I drew a picture of it--diamond shaped with diamond squares displaying bright colors--it seemed to be communicating with the smaller object next to it. It hovered for about 20 minutes then it moved south eventually disappearing. Definitely not a plane, star or planet. We got our kids out of bed so they could observe it. So cool!!!
