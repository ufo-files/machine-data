UFO boomerang shaped, mist colored on path, hovered then went to the right, then east

My husband was on the phone outside at approx 9:45 PM when spotted it above our house, he banged on the door for me to come out and pointed it out to me. It was boomerang shaped, the color was very hard to decipher I would describe it as being like a hard shear curtain color, hard mist... very hard to describe. It was moving very slow, and levitated in one place for about 5 seconds, moved to the right, then continued to move forward (which would be east from our home) then it was gone. Lost sight once it went east, feelings are indescribable, I was excited, in awe, but dumbfounded.
