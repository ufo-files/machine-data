{cak} 3 orange basketballs White Lake, MI 16March2019

At 10pm, I went into our three-season room to get a Coke from the fridge. I saw the lights and coming towards my house from the North. I ran outside into the yard to get a better look. At first, they looked like 3 helicopters flying in a staggered formation, but as they got closer, I heard no sound. It almost felt like they turned East, and faded/dimmed fairly quickly, at the very moment when I got my cell phone out to turn it on in order to take a photos.
