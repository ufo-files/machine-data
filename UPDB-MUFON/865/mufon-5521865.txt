bright light above sump

i was outside my house watching cars go by when i saw a light rise from the drainage sump diagonal from my residence. from my viewpoint it appeared to rise from the ground straight towards the sun. it was halfway up when it vanished. i thought it looked like a cobalt blue flare but it was moving too slow. i did not have any explanation for this sighting.
