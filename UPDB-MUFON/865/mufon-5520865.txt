Looked up & saw contrail

At 10:44 Am on Feb 6th, 2012 I walked out of my home looked up & saw this contrail. Having never seen another like it I grabbed my camera & got this shot. There was no sound associated or heard over the normal city noise of the morning . Davis-Monthan AFB is in the area as is Tucson International Airport, both have active military fighter aircraft stationed there . The direction of this contrail crossed both facilities approaches .
