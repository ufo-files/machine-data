{jpg} Three rings joined together in circular rotation

Around 7:15 pm we were cleaning windows when I noticed a small object in the distance while staring west. I pulled out my binoculars to get a better look and saw a 3 ring shaped object heading west. It was flying about the same speed as a single engine small plane. The object was spinning and the rings looked to blend in with every rotation to make one object. never seen anything like that before. lost sight after about 3 minutes due to tree line. I actually felt excited and can not remove the shape of the object from my mind. I am hoping someone else has see this today.
