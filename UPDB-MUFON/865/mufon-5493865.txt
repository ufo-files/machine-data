{rjl} I saw a UFO at Kmart.

At the time, I was 8 yrs old and with my mother at Kmart. We got out of the car and started walking through the full parking lot. While we was walking I looked up and there it was a black disc with red lights underneath it just hovering above the parking lot. I stopped and showed my mother and she saw it too. We hurriedly went into Kmart. I felt like it was trying to tell me to not be afraid. But my mother was scared. We stayed in for a couple of hours and when we finally came out it was gone.
