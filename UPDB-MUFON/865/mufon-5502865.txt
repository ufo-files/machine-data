My stepbrother and I where driving on a street about 2 blocks from my home. I caught a reflection from the corner of my eye from an object that I first thought was a jet. We passed a small patch of trees and the object was traveling in the total opposite direction in which we first saw it. The object was boomerang shape, maybe silver. It was hard to tell with the sun reflecting off of it. After we saw it we just looked at each other then I asked my stepbrother what he saw, and he confirmed what I also saw. I was excited in a way but also confused. After I asked him what he saw. We looked up again and we couldn't find it in the sky anywhere. I must add that when we saw it, it was moving slow.
