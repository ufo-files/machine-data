Very large elongated stationary cigar shape UFO observed for 10-15 minutes at midday.

Was at my workplace in Brossard, Quebec, Canada and getting fresh air during lunchtime when a very large object in the open sky was noticed due West from my location. UFO was approximately 5-8 miles (10 km) from where I was and possibly 500-800 feet in the air. Because of distance and size, I estimate the object to be at least 500+ feet in length and 50+ feet in height. Object was totally stationary for the period observed, 10-15 minutes. Color of UFO was a dull grey/tan non-reflective. 

I went back inside the building to fetch a camera but when I returned outside the object had gone.
