Fluorescent green disc near WA. State capitol building

A fluorescent bright green disc was hovering above the banks of the De Chutes river, with the state capitol building behind it. It ascended to the height of the second tree line, hovered for a moment, then proceeded north. I altered my position to get a better view, taking my eyes off of it for just a moment. When I looked back up, it was gone. Thought it was a drone, but could not find any drones on the internet that would cast such a hue (I have been involved in rc helis and drones for a number of years). Compared the size to sea gulls flying by, it was larger by at least 3 times
