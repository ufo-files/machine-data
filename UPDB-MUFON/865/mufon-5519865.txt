3 reddish lights in triangular form. Gliding from right to left

I was sitting on my couch... at 9:25pm. From there my husband looked outside through the balcony door and seen 3 distinct red lights in a triangle formation. We immediately jumped up and went out to our balcony, which is on the 3rd and top floor so we had a great view. 

They were very low, way lower than any plane would ever be... 3 distinct reddish lights in a triangle formation. They seemed to be fluttering or blinking randomly. 

I am not good with north or south directions, but it was heading from my left to my right... Like Shaws in N Attleboro on Robert Toner BLVD towards Sturdy hospital direction. I believe that to be from North to South. Me and my husband both witnessed this...

We watched it the entire time, maybe 2 minutes or so? and then they just seemed to vanish. 1 at a time. Right before it vanished it seemed to have moved further out into the sky... but it wasnt moving further out when it was going over us. 

It was very cloudy out so i couldnt see if there was a mass behind the lights. But the lights were clear as day!!

There was NO noise what-so-ever.

Damn me for charging my camera battery last night!!!!!!!
